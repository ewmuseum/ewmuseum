A Grand Saga for the Modern Age featuring Flamingos, Eunuchs and Dwarves

27 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-09-27 The wind screams furiously, blowing forbidding clouds of dust over a vast barren wasteland. A dark shadow is gradually casting itself over the rocky, desolate landscape as the setting sun moves slowly behind the peak of a cliff. The atmosphere is one of eerie, almost maddening isolation as it seems as if one could walk through here for miles and miles without coming across any signs of life at all, a feeling that is compounded by the fact that it’s nigh impossible to hear anything for the hideous screeching of the wind. A man with a very deep and official-sounding voice begins to narrate the segment, in a rather melodramatic tone… “In the year 2005 A.D, professional wrestling was rife with a seemingly unstoppable outbreak of endless kidnappings. Terror and uncertainty quickly became the rule of the day, with many wrestlers retiring early to become window cleaners for fear of being captured, bound, gagged and thrown into the back of a reinforced car trunk halfway through a match, never to be seen or heard from again. Others were not so clever, and ended up remaining in the wrestling business only to fall prey to this horrific outbreak of unspeakable evil; one of these many unfortunate casualties was Cereal Killer, who was kidnapped by the dastardly Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy during a tournament for the Cruiserweight Title and held captive in the Domicile of Darkness. However, in the face of such cruelty and horror, it was only a matter of time before one man stood up to the injustice! Sooner or a later a hero would inevitably come along, a man with the courage and tenacity to step forth and say, “HEY! You cant do that to Cereal Killer, that’s BULLSHIT! He’s my teammate for Super Series and if you don’t let him out, at least to just converse/talk strategy with his team mates, then we might lose! And anyways! besides that! what you’re doing qualifies as false imprisonment, which is illegal in most states nowadays. Perhaps YOU might not think he deserves to live his own life freely, in his own house away from the Domicile of Darkness, but I do, I mean come on, he’s a champion wrestler already! I LOVE Cereal Killer, and if my Papa lets me, I’m gonna marry him and have his babies!!” That man was Scotty Spalzcutoff, who - after learning that both he and the kidnapped Cereal Killer had been slated to represent XUW in an eight-man elimination tag team match against PRIME- set out in search of the Domicile of Darkness with a mission to rescue the steel-chair swinging, Death Match loving damsel in distress. This is his story.” The lonely, menacing atmosphere of the terrain seems entirely lost on our central protagonist, as Scotty suddenly bursts onto the scene. A scrawny, awkward looking little fellow wearing a red t-shirt, yellow cycling shorts, uggboots and a backpack, one cant help but notice he doesn’t quite LOOK the part of a hero as he skips merrily along a steep rocky track leading up to the top of a cliff while singing gaily to the tune of “Oh Susannah”… Scotty: Well I came here from Ohio with my banjo on my knee, Goin’ to the Domicile, my teammate there to see It rained all night the day I left, the weather it was dry The sun so hot I froze to death, Cereal Killer don’t you cry! Oh! Cereal Killer don’t you cry for me; I came here from Ohio, with my banjo on my knee! “And so he made the journey to the forbidding, barren wastelands where he knew the Domicile of Darkness was located. Here is where our hero came across his first problem: he was not sure where the actual mansion was, only that it had been built somewhere out here amongst this great inhospitable terrain of cliffs, canyons and mines. And so, here he was, prepared to climb every cliff and scour every mine in the off-percent chance of finding the Domicile, rescuing his teammate and saving the day…” Scotty must have already climbed a long way up the cliff before he had come into view of the camera, because he reaches the top fairly quickly. He wipes the sweat from his brow, takes off his backpack, unzips it, pulls out a bottle of chilled prune juice and sits down to have a proper look over the peak off the cliff. The view is incredible, with mile after mile of twisted, cold grey rock stretched out as far as the eye can see. Far off into the distance a thick white smoke can be just barely spotted, blowing cleanly up into the air as if from a fireplace chimney… Scotty: Aha! That must be the Domicile! Now all I’ve gotta do is follow the white cloud of smoke, and hopefully I’ll be there within a few days! Hang tight CK, I’m coming to rescue you if it k… Oh, GOD! I have to climb back down, don’t I? How boring is that? They need to put hot air balloons up here or something. Once you’ve climbed up to the top, you shouldn’t have to climb back down again, that’s just punishing people for making an effort and climbing to the top in the first place, its… CHRIST ON A BIKE!!! Scottys' blasphemous exclamation can be easily explained by the fact that he was suddenly picked up and carried away by a giant flamingo in mid sentence. The flamingo, about four times the size of the scrawny and weakly XUW superstar, and covered entirely in jet-black feathers, soars through the air at the speed of either a slow helicopter or a very, very fast blimp, take your pick. Scotty screams and flails about powerlessly with no hope of escaping the birds grasp, which could be a fortunate thing for him, as he’s now a long way up from the jagged rocks below… “And so Scotty was carried far off into the darkening wastelands by what was surely either a badly mutated bird or some kind of supernatural creature. Will this be the end of Scotty? Will he be eaten alive like some giant shellfish or algae? Or dropped to a certain gruesome death, his guts and pieces of his spleen splattered about on the sharp rocks below? Or is this all just piss and wind, and he’ll actually be fine in a few minutes? Find out after this very important message!” Cut to a commercial… “Aren’t YOU sick of paying good money for laxatives that only leave you feeling worse off than before you took them? Sick of having bouts of prolonged diarrhea marketed to you as a genuine, welcome alternative to constipation? Well I sure as heck was, until my friends told me to make the change to Lemoncleanse. A glass of Lemoncleanse a day is GAURENTEED to leave your bowels spick, span and sparkling with genuine Authentic Lemony Freshness! Lemoncleanse is the only laxative for me, as well as being a great refreshment when I’m feeling bloated on a hot summers day! Lemoncleanse: your innards will sing like drunken Pagan Gods!” As we return, Scotty is still being carried over the landscape by the giant black flamingo, however they seem to be much nearer to the ground now, and moving significantly slower than was the case when we last left off. Scotty has stopped struggling in a futile effort to escape, having come to terms with the fact that he probably isn’t going anywhere for quite a while, and has picked up again with the song he had been singing while hiking up the cliff… Scotty: I had a dream the other night, when everything was still I thought I saw THE PAIN a’comin’, oh yeah, down the hill! The buckwheat cake was in his mouth, the tear was in his eye Said I “I’ve come to save you, Cereal Killer don’t you cry” Oh! Cereal Killer don’t you cry for me; I came here from Ohio, with my banjo on my knee! “And so Scotty and the gigantic mutated/supernatural flamingo soared through the skies for hours, with Scotty singing heartily, stopping only sometimes in order to take a swig from his bottle of chilled prune juice. And before too long, despite being unable to speak each others language, a kind of unspoken camaraderie inevitably began to develop between XUW superstar and freakishly large bird; two outcasts in a world demanding perfection. The bird, originally having meant to simply drop Scotty from out of the sky and impale him on a jagged rock for sport, ended up opting instead to join him on his quest. So they flew over rocks and cliffs, until the flamingos giant wings finally began to tire, forcing them to make a sudden landing…" With Scotty now just a few feet above ground, the bird slows down just enough to drop him without causing any injury, before making a landing himself. They have flown past the most rugged, rockiest parts of the terrain and are now very close to the site where the cloud of smoke is coming from. However, as the source of the smoke comes into view, it becomes quite evident that this is not the huge gothic mansion the Domicile had been described as, but rather, a tiny hut made out of mud and straw, that looks like it would scarcely have room enough to fit a hermit. A hideous dwarf pokes his head out from a hole in the wall; his skin oily and blotched, with one disproportionately huge eyeball seeming to bulge out of its socket whenever he talks… Dwarf: Who goes there? Scotty: Scotty! Dwarf: Waddya want? Scotty: Well, I’m looking for the Domicile of Darkness. I’m on a quest to find and rescue Cereal Killer; a wrestler who was kidnapped by the evil and fearsome Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy, and is being held prisoner there against his will! This is my giant, possibly mutated possibly supernatural flamingo friend, who I’ve decided to call Gordon. He’s black. Anyway, if you could see fit to tell us where the Domicile of Darkness is, or… Dwarf: *GASP!* *RECOIL!* Did you say……. The Domicile of Darkness? Scotty: Well, yeah, but… Dwarf: ARE YOU CRAZY?! Scotty: Dude, I told you I was looking for the Domicile of Darkness as soon as I started telling you my story. The first thing I said after you said “Waddya want” was “Well, I’m looking for the Domicile of Darkness” and you didn’t recoil in horror then, in fact you didn’t react in any way whatsoever. Why is it th… Dwarf: SHUT UP! Listen, nobody wants to go to the Domicile of Darkness. When I was 16, I had a promising basketball career! I was a hot prospect; seven foot tall, had the grace and agility of a cat I did, and my dad sent me to the Domicile to train under the tutelage of Sirius Smythe. Thought it’d make a man out of me. AND NOW LOOK AT ME! They malnourished me and tortured me to the point where I became what you see before you right now! A hideous dwarf, relegated to working in the Cursed Underground Mines with tunnels leading to the backyard of the Domicile of Darkness, right next to my mud hut, see? Right over there! The dwarf pokes a hand through the hole in the wall of his hut, and points to a cave with a painted wooden sign hammered into the ground out front saying “WARNING: Cursed Underground Mines with tunnels leading to the backyard of the Domicile of Darkness”… Dwarf: And that’s why I wont help you! Please, promise me you'll NEVER GO THERE! IT WILL DESTROY YOUR LIFE AND LEAVE YOU AN EMOTIONAL, PHYSICAL AND SPIRITUAL WRECK!! PLEASE, FOR THE LOVE OF GOD, IF YOU NEVER LISTEN TO ANYTHING ELSE I SAY THEN LISTEN TO THIS!! You HAVE to turn ba… But the dwarf is too late, as Scotty and Gordon have already started running to the mine. Realizing he has just sent a rare majestic bird and an upcoming XUW superstar to their almost certain destruction, and perhaps knowing he himself has little left to lose in life (or maybe he’s just lonely), he chases and yells after them… Dwarf: WAIT!! I’ll help you! PLEASE WAIT!!! You don’t know the mines; you’re not safe without my guidance! “And so the wrestler, the dwarf and the flamingo braved the Cursed Underground Mines, venturing beneath the earth in search of tunnels leading to the backyard of the Domicile of Darkness. Will our heroes make it out alive? Or will they be crushed by rocks, or perhaps they'll run out of oxygen and suffocate before they make it to the other side? Don’t go anywhere, as you’ll find out right after this important message!” Cut to a commercial… “Here at Citrufresh, we have always subscribed to the time-tested and simple motif that a company can best be measured on how well it benefits the world around it. That’s why, despite priding ourselves on making premium quality, full-strength citrus-based laxatives at affordable prices, we are also the only leading laxative producer to never test our products on animals. Citrufresh has been around for four years, making it America’s official most trusted and best-loved laxative brand! Citrufresh settles the stomach and leaves you feeling elated and ready to face the day; scientists have proven that it’s three times more effective and ten times more comfortable than a herbal enema! So remember folks, for guaranteed excellence, always buy Citrufresh! As we return from the commercial, Scotty and friends have already ventured quite a long way down into the mine. The dwarf inexplicably has a lit torch now, which illuminates the mine somewhat, but everything is still quite dark, and it's very difficult to make out ones surroundings. He addresses Scotty and Gordon in almost a whisper… Dwarf: We have to be extremely quiet. They have college grads working off their tuition fees as guards in these very mines; they’re authorized to carry ice picks and tend to castrate intruders on site. Real vicious bastards they are, and that’s not the worst of it. The place is also swarming with psychopathic eunuch intruders lurking in search of potential victims. You must understand that many of these men were adventurers much like yourselves before they entered the mines; and so to be deprived of their virility in such a brutal and swift manner tormented them to the point where most of them were too ashamed to ever come out again, and have since developed a hatred and envy of the outside world compounded by their years of isolation here in these mineshafts. If we happen to come across one we will no doubt be force-fed our very bladders, gentlemen, mark my words. Scotty: Thanks for the advice. But Gordon and I aren’t really adventurers. Well Gordon might be, I really shouldnt speak for him, that’s mighty rude of me -sorry Gordon. But as for myself, I’m just a humble young chap looking for his tag team partner; I don’t like adventures and I certainly don’t go around looking for them, but I don’t really have much of a choice in this case. I have to get to the Domicile and rescue Cereal Killer, so that we can wax strategical and figure out how we’re going to beat those lame-o, knob-gobbling PRIMEate bastards at Super Series. “So Scotty and the Dwarf talked and talked; sharing their dreams, hopes and ambitions as they gradually made their way through the Cursed Gold Mine with tunnels leading to the backyard of the Domicile of Darkness, Gordon chirping in now and then with the occasional incomprehensible squawk. The two men began to really understand each other, and it seemed as though a friendship that would last throughout the ages was in the midst of forming, until their conversation was suddenly cut short…” Three demented eunuchs suddenly emerge from out of the shadows, staring down the wrestler, the dwarf and the flamingo with sadistic grins across their dimly lit faces. None of them are armed with anything, but they all look very raggedy and extremely insane… Demented Eunuch 1: TRESPASSER! Demented Eunuch 2: TRESSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSPAAAAAAASSSSSSSSSSSSSSSEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEERRRRRRRRRR!! Demented Eunuch 3: YOU MUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUST DIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!! What follows is an epic battle scene that is simply too breathtakingly awesome to possibly put into words. Suffice to say, one eunuch has his eyes scratched out by Gordon’s gigantic sharp claws, another is eaten alive feet-first by the dwarf and the third one trips over and shatters his skull on a rock just when he looks to have cornered the helpless and cowering Scotty. Battered from the ordeal, our three heroes continue to make their way through the mine, careful not to engage in anymore conversation for fear of drawing the attention of the college grads, or worse yet, more crazed eunuchs. Eventually the dwarf stops, and points to a small tunnel which has been dug in the stone wall of the mine, about five feet from the ground… Dwarf: There’s one. Scotty: Huh? Dwarf: There’s one of the tunnels that lead to the backyard of the Domicile of Darkness. And so Gordon gives Scotty and the dwarf a boost; Scotty being too physically weak to lift his own bodyweight and pull himself up into the tunnel and the dwarf being too short to reach it. The giant flamingo then somehow manages to fit into the tunnel himself, as the three slowly make their way up and out… “And with great haste our heroes climbed through the tiny mineshaft, never fleeting in their thirsty and tireless effort until they finally reached the outside world again. But what would await them on the other side? Would they have their heads hacked off no sooner than having poked them out of the ground by a furious, machete-wielding Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy? Or cracked with a nine iron by an oblivious Syrius Smythe as he swings wildly at the ball in the apparent privacy of his backyard golf course? Even if they managed to get their heads out of the mineshaft, or their entire bodies for that matter, surely the backyard of the Domicile of Darkness would be heavily fortified! Maybe they will be blown apart by landmines or blasted into confetti by drone machineguns? It’s very difficult to say, and if you go anywhere you might well miss it! So make absolutely sure to stay tuned during this crucial message break, and find out! Don’t be the one who gets left out of the dinner conversation, when all your friends and family are talking about what happened to Scotty and you’re the only one who doesn’t know! You will be rightly banished from your town after justifiably having the Official Mark of Exile burnt onto your face with a branding iron! It is a fate I would wish on not even the worst of men! We will be right back in a jiffy, so PLEASE, don’t go getting up and changing y…” The narrator is cut off in mid-sentance as we go to another add break… “Many other companies claim to produce premium quality citrus-based laxatives, but none have been able to duplicate the cleansing effect that Lemoncleanse undeniably produces. In a recent university study it was found that seven out of ten people prefer Lemoncleanse to Citrufresh, and that Lemoncleanse was by far the more trusted brand. This is because at Lemoncleanse, we value the safety of our customers above all else, and our products are subject to extensive testing before they are released to the public. Lemoncleanse has the strongest testing system of any leading laxative producer, and all of our products must meet a stringent set of standards before they are approved for release. So remember that if you’re serious about safety, and you’re serious about not wanting to have constipation anymore, then buy Lemoncleanse! Your innards will sing like drunken Pagan Gods!” As we return from the add break, Scotty, Gordon and the dwarf have all climbed out of the mineshaft and are dusting themselves off. It is nightfall, making it difficult to tell where the Domicile is, and they seem to be in the middle of a vast forest, bizarrely illuminated by faint, flickering streetlamps set up strategically to bounce a dull flashing light across the trees, which go on for as far as the eye can see… Scotty: This is the backyard? Dwarf: Yes. A giant impenetrable forest; no mortal man has ever yet passed through it uninvited and lived to tell the tale in a coherent manner –there have been a few trespassers who survived, but they were driven insane by the sheer trauma of the experience, to the point that when they returned home to tell their stories they couldn’t be understood, much less believed, and so most of them have since been forcefully immigrated to various mental institution throughout this great country of ours. To be fair, though, they do have a nice tidy garden out front of the Domicile. In fact the front yard in general is rather more pleasant than the backyard. This just pretty much goes on for miles and miles, with wild dogs and probably a few of Syrius’s opium-ridden foxhunting pals, who drop in occasionally to use the backyard for hunting expeditions. They’re absolutely stark raving mad, you know. “And so our heroes continued on their epic saga, now more determined than ever to locate the Domicile of Darkness and rescue Cereal Killer after having come so far. For miles they walked, discussing what they all would do when this adventure was over and done with, except for Gordon of course, who, again, was a mutated/supernatural black flamingo, and thus had no concept of the English language. And then, suddenly…” Scotty: IT’S THE DOMICILE!! Sure enough, far out into the distance, a well lit gothic mansion imposes itself over the terrain. Scotty slaps his thighs giddily and the dwarf does a breakdance, until suddenly the celebration is cut short, as two opium-ridden Foxhunters with posh accents and spiffy red hunting regalia burst into the scene on horseback… Foxhunter 1: I SAY, IT’S A BLOODY FOX!! Foxhunter 2: WHAT HO! ‘TIS INDEED! KILL HIM IN THE FACE I SAY!! A hunters horn rings loudly through the dimly lit forest, frightening Gordon so badly that he instinctively takes flight and leaves his friends far, far behind… Scotty: GOOOOOOOOOOOORDOOOOOOOOOOOOON!! DON’T LEAVE ME NOW, I COULDN’T STAND IT!!! Dwarf: Never mind that! Run, you fricken eejit!! Scotty and the dwarf bolt towards the mansion, which is still a fair way off into the distance- with the opium-ridden Foxhunters in hot pursuit, gratuitously honking their horns all the while. The dwarf takes advantage of his close proximity to the ground by picking up a large stone, quickly turning and throwing it full pelt into a Foxhunters face, knocking a substantially large chunk from the side of his head and rendering him pretty much dead before he even has time to fall off his horse. The second Foxhunter immediately halts, and wastes no time springing straight off his horse and frantically checking his fallen friends corpse to see if he had any opium or money with which to obtain some… Scotty: Gee whizickers! Thank heavens for that, I thought we were done for… Dwarf: KEEP RUNNING! The sound of the horns will have attracted the wild dogs! The dwarf was right, as four rotweilers, barking and foaming at the mouth, seem to jump out of nowhere and chase our heroes through the forest. His speed impeded by his short, stumpy legs, the dwarf falls behind and is caught by three of the dogs. His arms are powerful from his years working the mines, and he uses them to swing some heavy heymakers at the rabid, snarling beasts, but he is no match for them, and is quickly torn limb from limb and then devoured bones and all, as the last of the four rotweilers continues in pursuit of Scotty. Presuming he runs as fast as he can, he is still a good three or four minutes out from the Domicile and Cereal Killer, with the dog seemingly closing in on him with each passing moment… “Will our hero have his scrotum ripped off by a wild rottweiler dog? Will he reach the Domicile only to be shot in the face for trespassing? How will he ever be able to make it alone, having relied so heavily on help from his two new friends up until this point? Who knows? YOU will, if you stay tuned after these very important commercial messages to find out! Otherwise you WONT know and then you’ll be a worse person than someone who does. So stay tuned, be in the know, be cool, and die that little bit happier than you otherwise would have!” Cut to yet another ad break… “Here at Citrufresh, we don’t believe in smear campaigns, and that’s why we’re not about to start making up false statistics in order to bignote our product at the expense of other companies reputations. For four years –count them- FOUR FUCKING YEARS PEOPLE! we have been the leading manufacturer of laxatives, and have never been filed with a lawsuit or even received so much as a complaint letter regarding the safety of our product, this despite the fact that we don’t test our products on innocent animals unlike those sodding soulless bastards at Lemoncleanse. So don’t be suckered in by their self-inflating hype or their destructive lies! For guaranteed excellence, buy Citrufresh!” We return from the previous ad break with Scotty fleeing from the vicious rotweiler… and then we cut straight to another ad break… “USE Lemoncleanse! DON’T USE Citrufresh! If you use Citrufresh YOUR BOWELS WILL FALL OUT OF YOUR STOMACH and YOU’LL DIE!!!!!!!” We return from the final ad break with Scotty still fleeing from the vicious rottweiler, who is now almost nibbling at his heels, literally trailing just a few feet behind the terrified XUW wrestler. Scotty’s eyes are closed and he is quietly praying to himself, as he struggles to come to grips with his near-certain impending death… “And so Scotty, with no option left but to run, did exactly that, fleeing heroically from the dog in the hopes of somehow reaching the Domicile of Darkness without being chewed to pieces. And all looked lost for him, when suddenly, out of the sky…” Scotty: GORDON!! Gordon swoops down and grabs the dog by the scruff of the neck, carrying him up into the sky, over the treetops, past the Cursed Mines again and back to the place where Scotty began his journey, before dropping the rottweiler from out of the sky and impaling him on a jagged rock for sport. Scotty, meanwhile, sings merrily as he makes his way to the domicile, presumably with a reasonably safe path from here on out… Scotty: I’ll soon be at the Domicile Where I will find CK We’ll make love by the fireplace In a very hetero way And if I do not find him, I’m probably gonna cry Coz he’ll know that I didn’t come but he will not know why Oh! Cereal Killer don’t you cry for me; I came here from Ohio, with my banjo on my knee! The Domicile looks absolutely monstrous from up close, like some giant house-cum-cathedral-cum-prison. Scotty knocks on the back door. Once. Twice. Three times. He waits for a minute, then knocks again. No answer, and no immediate sign of life from inside… Scotty: I hope they’re home. HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEYAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA!!!! IT’S SCOTTYYYYY!!! LEMME IN! I’VE COME TO RES…. A slow, creaking noise cuts Scotty off in mid sentence, as someone is obviously opening the door. Scotty recoils, suddenly very nervous about having to deal with whatever’s on the other side… Scotty: Ummm…. Hello? The door is swung open, as Syrius Smythe, Jack Nobbledys’ ancient and cantankerous lawyer steps out in his black nightgown with an old ivory walking stick in hand… Sirius: What do YOU want? The frail but extremely agitated old man startles Scotty into taking a couple of steps back; he has just been woken up, and he isn’t pleased… Scotty: Well, um… I really don’t want to be a prick about this or anything, I know this is your house and a mans home is his castle and far be it for me to come in here and start telling you what to do, you know, in the privacy of your own home. But I’ve come to rescue Cereal Killer. Sirius: Go away! Scotty: Oh. Well can you at least let me in for a minute, just to talk to him? Sirius: No! Scotty: Oh, please? I’ve walked for miles! Sirius: Sod off! Scotty: I’ll be your friend?! Sirius: BAH! You don’t mean that! Scotty: I do! Sirius: Away with you! Scotty: Oh, come on! Do you have any idea what I’ve been through to get here? I’ve climbed mountains and scurried cliffs and fought eunuchs and dogs and killed some of your friends! This journey has been a profound existential awakening for me! Sirius: You're a goddamned fool and a lunatic! Scotty: PLEASE! JUST FOR A SECOND, then I'll leave I swear! Sirius swings his ivory cane at Scotty’s head; a swing so limp and pathetic that even if it connected it wouldn’t have done any real damage, but the even more pathetic Scotty Spalzcutoff makes sure to duck his head swiftly out of the way, reacting as if it were an aluminum baseball bat being swung by a muscle-bound twenty-something year old as opposed to an antique ivory walking stick being swung by a feeble, one hundred and-something year old. Scotty looks genuinely frightened, and has no idea what to say nor do at this point… Scotty: I hope I didn’t wake you up by the way. Sirius: I think it’s fairly obvious that you did! Scotty: Yeah, well, look, I’m REALLY sorry about that, and again, I didn’t want to come across as rude. And… Hey, look over there, it’s an antelope! Sirius: … Thinking he has Sirius distracted, Scotty tries to make a dash past the frail but cranky old lawyer and through the door… however, he is cut drastically short with a blow to the face from the walking stick before he even gets to the doorway, as Sirius didn’t fall for Scotty’s little trick for a second. He follows it up with another whack to the head, and then eventually another, though his withered old frame wont allow him to swing the stick as quickly or as hard as he would like. It has the desired effect, however, as Scotty turns and flees back through the yard and away from the Domicile… Sirius: That’s right, you keep running! Run all the way back to Ohio, you bloody fairy! Sirius straightens his nightgown and, feeling quite pleased with himself after having just beaten up and chased away an actual wrestler, he struts back into the Domicile like a peacock –a hundred year old peacock that is so frail it can barely stand up mind you- but a peacock nonetheless… “And so our hero bolted like a leopard away from the Domicile and all the way home, in a manner so graceful, so captivatingly impressive that old ladies would take photos, photos that they would one day show their five-year-old grandchildren and say, “See? Why cant you be more like this nice young chap here; this Scotty fellow. He displays the kind of selflessness and humility that people in our generation took for granted, characteristics that don’t even seem to exist anymore with your generation, what with all your ripped jeans and your hip hop music and your MTV and your dirty big sugar cubes laced with liquid LSD, and all of your filthy debauched rogering and buggery. Tsk tsk!”. And so his story would be retold throughout the ages, and his deeds would become the stuff of legends. The end!”
