The Violence of Truth

10 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-10 "INCOMING! EVERYONE, GET DOWN!" "Oh my God, we're going to die!" "Sarge is down! They got Sarge!" "DRAKE, WATCH OUT!" ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -(The sheets on the bed flung up ferociously as Drake stood right up, breathing heavily. His eyes were wide with fear, blood shot from another night of no sleep and bad dreams. Staring at the wall, he blacked out into unconsciousness and back into the real world, shaking the evil out of his head.)- "Jesus Christ..." -(Drake hadn't had a full night of sleep in years. Every night was the same dream, the same thing. He was always on the road, always in some scummy hotel room. Scummy was the only thing he could afford...who knew that professional wrestling would pay so poorly? This room was just like all the others: dim lights, chipping paint on the wall, a barely functioning black and white TV, and a bathroom that would make a pig puke. The place was a thing of beauty.)- -(Drake reached for the light, and as it turned on, Drake was sitting there in all his glory. The dirty bandages that completely covered his skin except his eyes, mouth, etc., were tight on him, revealing his built physique. They were tightly wrapped around his body, almost as if he was a mummy. It had been weeks since Drake had mustered up the strength to change them, and it showed with the off color white.)- -(As he turned to get off the bed, one of the bandages had caught on the corner of the bed. There was a loud rip, and as Drake looked down at his right arm, he saw part of his skin showing. The blackness of it made him squirm, the site of that charred skin still today made him want to puke. Grabbing a roll of bandage off the bedside table, he got out of bed and headed towards the bathroom.)- -(Opening the door to the bathroom, he turned on the lights to find something that was worse than he initially imagined. The shower had a stained lime green curtain to contrast against the nearly black, but formerly white, walls. The toilet...well...there was no toilet, just a piece of broken ceramic screwed into the ground. The mirror had a big crack right in the middle of it, and the sink was stained with a rainbow of colors of red and black. It was gross, it was disgusting, and it was what Drake was accustomed to.)- -(Looking across himself at the mirror, Drake shook his head in disgust. He used to be beautiful, a handsome young man...until he traveled over there. Until it happened. Until...)- "Get focused Drake, gotta fix this." -(Drake went back to work on the bandages, grabbing a pair of scissors from the sink top. He started cutting the bandage, ripping off strands of bandage and throwing it in the bathroom trash. The arm was now clean, the black skin looking bumpy and rough. Running his fingers across it, he shuddered and looked away. Going on with his work he unwound a long piece of bandage and wrapped it around the arm, covering it completely. Drake stuck it on there with a gooey material and then sealed it by running his finger over it. Re-bandaging: done.)- -(The room looked the same when he walked back in, which to Drake was always a disappointment. Drake had a knack for hoping things had magically changed when he saw them again. Mirrors were constant disappointment. Maybe, just maybe, Drake would finally look into the mirror and see the old Drake. Every time he looked for change, though, it was never there. Life was disappointment.)- -(The room began to feel claustrophobic, and Drake needed to get out in a bad, bad way. Standing up and stretching his whole body, he let out a yawn and pushed his arms to the ceiling. Next came the black trench coat, the black sunglasses, the black pants, the black boots, and the wide-brimmed black hat. Drake always covered himself in clothes when he went out to hide himself from the people. The people always crowded him and asked him questions, always in his face. If it wasn't congratulatory about being IWL World Champ, it was inquisitive about the scars. After a while, it became too much, and Drake could not handle it mentally. He was now ready to go and he left the room, grabbing the keys to his room, and heading out the door...)- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- I always told myself that night is the worst time to sleep, because that's when they'll kill you. I never sleep at night. I rarely sleep at day, but never at night. The night seems to haunt me, seems to move me to destruction. With that in mind, I had to get out of that hotel room. It was a reminder of what I am, and I hate reminders. I am a miserable mess, and that room is a miserable mess. If anything, I like to not think about myself. I just want to be someone else, you know? I left the hotel and I headed down the street, hopefully to a restaurant or a pub or something, anything, I just needed a bite to eat. At 60 years old, I need my nutrition, and with my body the way it is, I need a lot of it. The problem about going out in public is that people always ask you questions. It's always "Hey! Whats up with all those bandages?" or "Hey, aren't you Drake Coleman, IWL Champion?" A soldier is not trained to be famous; a soldier is trained for discipline or work. Even though the army abandoned me after my injuries, I still maintained the mentality. I eventually ended up in a small little diner called "Mary Beth's", another image of myself: dirty, scummy, and miserable. Walking up to the counter, I sat down and put my head down, looking at the counter blankly. "Here's a menu." The voice was sharp and cruel and stabbed me, the words seeming full of malicious intent. The menu slid in front of me, and I read it over. I already knew what I wanted, and proceeded to call her over. "Hamburger. No fries, no sides, just the hamburger..." She acknowledged my order by repeating the order to the chef, and I went back to looking down at my hands. I felt ignored, and I was used to that. People either ignored you or gawked at you if you're covered in bandages; there is no middle ground. At first, back after the injury in Nam, I wasn't used to it. It brought me to insanity; I was stuck in a world of pain in misery. Now, though, I'm only back there in tough times. The sanity never comes from attention or lack of attention; that's just a part of life now. Out of the corner of my eye, I could see a couple of young kids gawking at me, no older than 15. I wasn't sure if that was recognition or not, and to be honest, I really didn't care. Thankfully, one of them said something...it was getting awkward. "Are...are...are you..." "Drake Coleman. How do you do." Their eyes widened, and I felt a little warm inside. I never usually make people feel happy about themselves...usually just miserable. It was the best feeling I had since I beet 'Sinister' Steven Lee for the IWL World Title months ago. The awkward moment returned when they didn't say anything for a few moments. "Where's your belt?" one said, a little smirk on his face. "It's back in my hotel room; don't want it to get stolen." I flashed a smile from under my hat and received a few back. They didn't seem to mind the fact that I was covered in bandages. I'm assuming that's because they've seen me on TV, but it's still relieving. "Hear about CW?" The kid asked in such a tone, that I was curious and shocked. Had something changed? Had it been cancelled? I knew I was still in it...but...but what? "No, tell me." I said, intently waiting to hear the news. "Brackets are up. Devito bracket, Coleman vs. Captain Jack Morgan of LOW." I sat there quietly for a moment, a slight smile coming up on my face. It must not have been a moment, though, because my meal came, the kids left, and it still felt like a moment...but finally, here it was. I was a soldier, ready to prove my worth against the rest of my class. Suddenly, my memory had been jarred. I remembered that I did see the special on TV, the brackets drawing on the PTC network, everything. I had no idea who Captain Jack Morgan was, and to be honest, I still have yet to get a tape or a wrestling promo video or anything. Frankly, I'm quite worried about the match. I don't think I've ever been more unprepared for a match before in my life. Then, I remembered what all the pros had said on the prediction show right afterwards...I clenched my fist in anger. I wanted to punch something, destroy something. They were picking Morgan, over and over, ignorantly choosing him for his name...I had never been more disrespected before in my life. You do not judge a book by its cover, the same way you do not judge a soldier by his uniform...you judge the book by its content, and the soldier by his skill and precision and execution. Once I finished my meal, I left a twenty on the table and left. The twenty was more than enough for the meal, resulting in a generous tip for an undeserving waitress. It was of no concern to me, though...too much was on my mind. Back at home, I had a potential 3 people vying for my belt, with Chaos, Lee, and maybe an unknown opponent having a chance to win it, assuming Chaos doesn't win the main event at One Night in Olympus. Now, I had some opponent I needed to take care of... This was all too much, now. I started to head back to my hotel. The streets were quiet and dark, almost barren. A few people were out, but no one to make notice of. The air smelled thick with trash...this part of the city was disgusting. A champion shouldn't have to be in the slums all the time...but I knew it was where I belonged. I was an outcast, and no matter high up on the food chain you are, you are still an outcast. When I finally got back to my hotel room, I looked to my left, and then to my right. No one needed me, no one wanted me...it was all the same. I was either an outcast or a freak, and even though I was more than used to those conditions...I still wanted the old me... ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -(Trudging back into his room, Drake threw off his trench coat and onto the floor. Next came his hat, and then came a rush into the bathroom. The puking started to commence, Drake hunched over the sink blasting into it. He lifted his mouth off and cleaned off his bandages, shaking his head at himself again. Drake then went back into the main room and took off the rest of his clothes, leaving him in just his bandages.)- -(The TV flickered on to a nameless channel with a nameless, boring newscaster rifling off facts about a crime with no named thugs. Drake stared for a moment and then immediately lost interest, plopping on his back and closing his eyes. Instantly, he was immersed back into his dream, the same one he's had since the war...)- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- "HQ! HQ! We have been ambushed! Requesting back up! Requesting tank support! Requesting ANYTHING!" "Drake, quick, turn your fire onto those bushes, they're in there!" "Oh my God! We're gonna die! We're gonna die!" "They got Sarge!" "INCOMING! EVERYONE! GET DOWN!" "They're everywhere! God damn slit eyed..." "DRAKE, WATCH OUT!" ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -(Without fail, Drake had the same dream again. The voices kept on screaming at him, the flurried vision of gunfire and explosions in the jungle shook around him. Like every other time, it ended with the last conscious verbal warning he received before his injury. The infamous injury that brought him from one hell to another.)- -(Drake sat up again, and then immediately laid down. He was constantly in torment...he couldn't even escape his misery and insanity in his dreams. Stuck forever with his condition, he was forced to live with it.)- -(Suddenly, a phone call...)- *BURRIINNNGGG* -(Drake was not expecting this. A call this late? And, above all, who knew he was here? Why did they want him? Was there something wrong with these people? Have they no dignity?)- "Hello?" "Hello Drake. Heard about your tournament. Decided to give you a ring." -(The voice was recognizable, but at the moment incomprehensible. Your tournament? Wasn't a member of CW2. Give me a ring? Sounded like an enemy.)- "What do you want at...four AM?" "Just decided to warn you." There was a sense of satisfied anger in his tone. "The farther you get, the worse you will be punished when you return...its all an incentive to steal that title from you and to leave you a tatter of bandages." "I don't even want this title, you can have it." That was partly true. The title brought more troubles than it did positives. Though it showed his dominance, it brought him into the spotlight, the one spot he never wanted to be. "Ha! Regardless, you can't just give away, can you? Guess I'll have to pry it away from your cold, dead fingers, won't I?" *CLICK* -(The phone was dead, and Drake was fed up. He was not a favorite for anything, a target to get decimated by the IWLers, and now there was someone who wanted him dead. Drake was in a world of misery, and by closing his eyes, he only brought himself into a deeper pit of despair...)- -----([([([END FEED])])])----- "Somethings Telling you, To Wake up and salute, The Dangers of Obedience, The Violence of Truth" --The The
