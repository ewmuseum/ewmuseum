PTC WotY 2005 Hype

26 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mathematical proof that Karina Wolfenden is PTC Wrestler of the Year for 2005. Now, as good exam technique states: always show your workings. - Sinister Steven Lee (IWL) - I think he was also in SotB. Can't be sure, since he lost before the bits that mattered. - LeStatt Knight (GCW) - See above. Yup not even going to repeat myself for him. - Christian Zenith (Global) - He was in the Seven of the Best final... until he was eliminated, and I won it. - Emilio Rage (PRIME) - Kicked him so hard in the head to win the Global Title that he missed the rest of the year. - Tim Shipley (Core) - Nearly won Seven of the Best. But didn't. Because I did. - Brandon Youngblood (PRIME) - Passed out like a little girl before I could beat him in the Halo. - Angelo Deville (PRIME) - Pinned 1-2-3 in the Dual Halo. - Hoyt Williams (PRIME) - Was in Dual Halo. Didn't win it since, well, I did. - Peter Vetra (PRIME) - Beat without breaking a sweat. In fact, beads of sweat actually got sucked back in through pores, it was that easy. - Baltasar (PRIME) - When reading "Karina was PRIME Tag Team Champion" think "Beat Baltasar to be PRIME Tag Team Champion". - Ember (WWA) - Would have beaten him in the seven of the Best final... if he got that far. - Jesse Jamester (PRIME) - Wow. You'd think people who didn't win a PTC match all year wouldn't put themselves forward for this. Anyway, he lost to Zenith, who lost the SotB final, which was won by... any guesses? - James von Doom (UWF) - You're kidding, right? Now, let's put this all into the formula... Karina Wolfenden + 2005 = PRIME Universal Champion, Tag Team Champion, and Pulse Champion. Not to mention equalling the record winning streak of 10 matches... including surviving the whole roster of 30 to win the Dual Halo. Oh, and yeah, PRIME wrestler of the year for 2005 as well (which, let's face it, equals PTC WotY on that alone). Not forgetting being #1 for the 1st quarter of 2005 in The Mark's all-circles column. The above was also partly due to being PTC tag team champion, as well as winning the inaugural PTC Seven of the Best competition. That pretty much involved defeating Tsunami Oni - clean - then managing to actually stay in the ring as the 6 other winners got dumped over the top rope and down to the floor (including Shipley, who pretty much got shown up as the Core mid-carder he is at heart). Now, the prize for that was a PTC Global Title shot... which, of course, lead to a Global Title victory after kicking Emilio Rage so hard in the head that he was out for most of the year. Oops. To summarise the equation: Karina Wolfenden + 2005 = PTC WotY. And we can prove this by checking it against the formula 1+ 1 = 2 1 (Karina Wolfenden) + 1 (2005) = 2 (WotY) Therefore, as 1 + 1 = 2, Karina Wolfenden must = PTC WotY 2005. It's that simple. Unless you don't think 1 + 1 = 2?
