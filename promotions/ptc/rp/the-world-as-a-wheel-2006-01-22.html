The world as a wheel!

22 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-22 The world rages as a storm tossed sea swirls around a single masted vessel floating in the raging maelstrom of natures fury. In the vessel through the rain a single figure can be seen steering the vessel by a single rudder arm and in his right hand is a rope to the sail. He adjusts the sail and rudder to beat the waves and punishing forces of the sea he sails. Occasionally he looks up at the mast as the winds whip through as the sail whips about like a wet paper bag in a thunderstorm, Staring onwards into the nothingness that is this vast sea before him, his future unknown as he concentrates upon the dangers that he will begin facing. Quietly he thinks as he whispers into the wind as if someone will here him speaking. His words come in whispered tones sometimes seemingly laced with doubt and then rage. His anger seems to be quelled by the storm around him. Proving himself to his ancestors seems to be the most important thing to him. Knowing what the future holds is the hardest thing to predict. He has upheld his honour by fighting and hurting those that had come before him. Knowing he has to do what he can to survive the coming battle knowing only that this time luck has to be on his side. "The world is a wheel, an ever turning wheel where things come full circle. I have made it to round four in the GTT5 and in all hopes full circle will be to continue to round 5. The fight is going to be a massive one this time around. Four men in one ring, a torrent of devastation and all are fighting for everything they are worth. Yet I will not change my attack plan. I am going to attack and hurt each and every one of them. Now for once I am the true underdog. Each man has fought and has proven themselves over the years. The storm must be weathered and I must prevail." Gripping the wet ropes in his hand he pulls hard as the sail slightly turns and catches the winds full force. The sails pulling the vessel hard through the waves, with his free arm he grips the rudder steering it on its course. The destination unknown for this man and vessel as the winds continue to blow and the rain coming down in sheets upon the waves, as they turn and boil beneath them threatening to swallow them into the unforgiving past. Thinking to himself he wonders if his ancestors did this much like he is today. He ponders each and every piece of history and story he had ever been told. They were great fisherman and woodsman as well as great warriors, setting fear into their opponents whenever they were seen. He hoped that everyone who now sees him coming thinks and fear crosses their mind. "Was it something I said? Was it the fact that I decided to hurt my opponents because I enjoy it? Sets everyone against me that it does, I don’t care I have business to attend to, the business of conquering and crushing opponents. Each one of those three men has strengths and weaknesses. Each one is going to think I am an easy mark. Much to their dismay I am going to fight all the way. If I am to lose I am going to lose on my terms. Those terms are simple. Blood and broken bones are the simplest ways of stating the obvious. You three men are going to be the ultimate test and with this test I am going to prove something that has been ignored for a long time. Samuelsson should be taken very seriously. I am going to enjoy causing pain and devastation in you three men’s very souls." Suddenly the rain seems to stop as Samuelsson raises his eyes and looks at the sky as the clouds seem to break and leave nothing but the rough seas behind. Samuelsson grabs the rudder arm and steers hard then turns the vessel as the ship turns and heads for shore which is seen in the distance. As he flows over wave after wave the shore comes close and closer. Knowing what he seeks must be somewhere in the near future. He seeks the ultimate goal that he has set before himself. Standing he looks over the waves as they seem to begin to crash against the beach before him and the tall cliffs which disappear into the clouds. "Thor’s hammer the mighty cliff faces of the Norse conquerors that had come long before me. The descriptions they gave were right on to perfection. My ancestors have come here seeking answers and came in search of themselves. I came in search of something within myself. It is the drive to defeat the opponents I will face in the coming weeks. Why do I hesitate to let all the rage I am holding out? It is simple I do not wish to waste it before it is truly the time to release the pent up rage. I have all the aspects of my ancestors. The size, the muscle, the sheer want and need to fight onwards and excel, yet there is something missing. My opponents do not take me serious enough. They tend to hide behind false little pieces of tin and leather. Am I fearful of those of whom I face? Not in the least bit. I want to strive and fight onwards to take down my opponents. There is a war at hand and they do not open their own eyes to this plain and simple fact." Turning towards the cliffs he raises his hands to his mouth and yells loudly towards the cliff face. He yells out a single name which meant a lot of power to his people, a word for the gods to pay attention to and revere. The word would cause the Norse gods to kneel beneath the power that this word holds. "ODIN!" Turning to exactly due east he returns his hands to his lips. "ODIN!" Turning he leans his head to the side as some torches light up leading a path up the cliff face. Beginning to walk a full stride across the beach he makes a steady pace for himself as he makes his way through the brush to the path as a single cloaked man awaits him and motions up the path as Samuelsson nods and begins his ascent. "There is a destiny of a man in his many lifetimes. If he does not grace the halls of Valhalla he does not receive ultimate enlightenment nor does he reach his goals. I will speak of each man very soon. Chandler Tsonda, Saolok, and the Legend himself Angelo Deville, Each of these men bring with them a unique challenge to the ring. Each challenge for me is a different hurdle to get over. The challenges before me were preordained by the gods. Nobody even thought that Jorge Samuelsson the Viking conqueror would even make it this far in the tournament. I am on a roll and want to remain on this roll. If there is a fight to be found then I am going to be in the middle of it and will tear someone apart to achieve this goal. Even warriors such as myself must attain my goals. Did you ever believe I was going to lay down like a pathetic dog? No I will not accept defeat. I am going to hurt each and every one of you three men just to prove a point. You should have never looked past Samuelsson. Welcome to the world of the Vikings gentlemen this is a world that will forever haunt your nightmares and hunt you in every waking moment." Stopping he looks over at the robed figure accompanying him. Staring a hole through the man he slowly speaks. "Be gone I know my way. Do not accompany me or you will suffer my wrath. I am tired of your presence." Watching the man leave he turns around and looks down upon the beach and looks at the vessel he had left there. Turning his gaze towards the moon as it shines out over the harbour from which he sailed from and the torrent sea beyond. "The path of a man is much like the wheel of the world. Everything must come full circle and in this I will not be deterred. Your paths have crossed mine and our futures entwined within one moment. Is this the moment of reckoning? Will each of you step up to the fight and then return to only be beaten down by the man you did not take seriously?" Cracking his neck from side to side with violent motions he narrows his eyes and then begins to deliver a hearty laugh. "All of you are going to suffer my rage and anger. There will be a time when you are laying down and bleeding that you will think back and ask yourselves a simple question. When did I underestimate the man who defeated me in this match? When that answer comes to you, you will understand your folly in the whole situation." Turning he begins his march once again up the cliff face. Nobody but the torches accompany him as he takes each step with a steady confidence only seen him a few times. He knows with all of his being that this is a fight he is going to have to face and face it alone. There has been nobody he thinks and this time he will need nobody. Thinking to himself he continues to walk onwards into the unknown. "My path is set before me. This path doesn’t seem very clear yet I am going to fight onwards and upwards and show that there is a reason for my victories. There is a destiny that all men ignore and then there are a few who embrace the future in its entire scope. I don’t care about the money nor the petty crap between opponents. Concentrate on those who stand before you. Do not concentrate on just the one because someone is going to hurt you when you are not looking. Did you four surely think I wasn’t paying attention and not learning about each and everyone of you?" Walking along the path four men await at the top as Samuelsson doesn’t even look at each of them and he rubs his forehead and then stops. "Tsonda I don’t care what your issues are with Deville. You could be damned lovers for all I care and in the end it matters very little. Salaok you are quiet which has me intrigued. Do I like you? Not in the least. You are just another man in the long line of opponents thrown before me. You three are merely sacrifices for the gods. Remember if I move on one of you three must face me again. Is this a path you wish to take or learn to hate? Either way you are going to have to face me. None of you are going to tell me stories of your achievements and your past exploits. I don’t give a damn on how you have done in the past. You all grasp onto past glories as if they mean something to me. I hold onto my history as honour and guess what gentlemen. It isn’t something I dwell on. It is something I use to fuel my hatred for the three of you who have chosen to step in my way this time. Call your women for your conqueror will have use of them when he is done with you." Laughing a sadistic laugh he marches on past the men waiting for him and disappears into the darkness. The moon is brightest after the storm. Yet if there is no storm merely a man with nothing to lose then you have realized suddenly that there is no end to the pains he intends on delivering upon those who have overlooked him.
