IG31 Promo - Revenge

6 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler: "Hollywood" James von Doom Fed: GLOBAL It’s been a long time since the words James von Doom has been muttered anywhere without a laugh and a chuckle. It’s the same here as it is in the world of professional wrestling. He’s on the set of his latest movie, “All the Spaceships of Men” and its filming in the desert in New Mexico. This is the lousiest of locations for a really stupid astronauts and aliens movie that probably wont make any money anyway. The hot sun scorches the shifting sands and the movie crew as James is getting ready for the next shot. He is in full astronaut gear and is ready for his scene, but his actress co-star is nowhere to be found. Such is the way of a Hollywood movie set. It’s the same as it is in wrestling except for all the stars here have had botox, have their own servants, and are members of the cult, I mean Church of Scientology. James likes aliens and stuff, but he can’t stand their Xenu bullshit doctrine made up by some shitty writer who writes worse stuff than he ever did in high school English class. Its been like this for ten days now. His actress co-star, some bitch named Lindsay Lohan, keeps disappearing and then showing up to the set drunk rambling about Paris Hilton and Los Angeles. James has finally had enough of her crap. Not to mention he’s started up his PTC fighting and wrestling career again, which ahs made him doubly stressed over the past few weeks. His mind is wearing thin and he doesn’t have the time to deal with some clueless bimbo actress and her drinking binges. James turns to his left and sees the director sitting in his chair under a nicely covered green tent to keep the sun off. “Hey John!” James yells out at him. The director turns around. “When the hell are we going to resume shooting? I mean, it’s hot as fuck out here right now!” John, the director of the film, is an older man whose head is covered by a UCLA hat. He’s wearing a buttoned up green shirt, jeans, and a really expensive pair of sunglasses. He told James the night before that they cost one hundred dollars. James’ response to hearing that he paid that much for a pair of sunglasses wasn’t all too pretty, which has led to friction between the two on the set. John throws his arms up. “How the hell am I supposed to know?” he yells back at James in an angry tone. James glares at him with a really menacing look. It’s the kind of look that says “I want to kill you” but yet doesn’t actually lead you to kill the person. But, knowing James, he would probably kill this idiot. “You’re the director!” James fires back. “Do your fucking job you fucking overpaid Hollywood jackass!” James then turns and storms off, flipping of John as he does. James walks over to a box of big heavy crates and starts to kick them. Just then, two of the technical guys on the set come over to him. One is a balding fat guy with a ponytail, glasses, and a “I (heart) Ewoks” T-shirt on. The other guy is tall, skinny, has glasses, and is wearing a “You looked better on Myspace” T-shirt. Their names are Reggie and Tiny. Tiny is the fat guy, of course. James met them when he first arrived at this God forsaken hellhole to shoot this crappy movie that wont make any money three weeks ago. James stops kicking the crate and looks them over. “What the hell do you guys want?” he snarls. They look at him and seem a bit frightened. Reggie gulps as Tiny, the fat ass, answers his question. “You can’t kick those crates,” Tiny replies. “And why the hell not?” James shoots back angrily. Tiny and Reggie step back and then Tiny answers James’ question. “Because there’s very valuable equipment in there that relates to the movie,” Tiny replies. James raises an eyebrow after hearing that remark. He hasn’t seen anything of any remote importance on this set since Lindsay showed up not wearing any underwear and flashed the crew. That is the closest some of these uber-nerds will get to having actual sex in the next ten years, too, so James has had to deal with then trying to stalk Lindsay during some of the scenes as well. But that’s not what he’s thinking about now. He’s now curious as to what’s actually inside these crates that’s so valuable to the studio. James points to the crate and asks, “So whats inside of these, Tiny?” Tiny gulps and shakes his head. “We can’t tell you,” he replies. “And why the hell not?” James asks in an angry tone. “Because Lindsay and the director told us not to,” Tiny replies in fear of his life. James gets right in Tiny’s face, growling and snarling like some rabid animal on the hunt for its first kill of the day. He then looks Tiny over and then clinches his fist. “Uh-oh,” yelps Reggie as James coils his fist up and goes to hit Tiny. But just as his fist gets to Tiny’s face, James stops and a big, devious grin comes across his face. “I’m juts messing with you,” he replies with a laugh. James then points to the crates. “I bet it’s Lindsay’s revealing wardrobe, huh?” Tiny and Reggie nod their heads. James can only stop and laugh over this. “And why in the hell did they not want me to know what was in these?” he asks with a giggle. “Are Bill Clinton’s love stains all over them or something?” James starts laughing hysterically as Tiny quickly replies, “They didn’t want you to know because they thought you might think this is a porno movie or something.” James then suddenly stops laughing and raises an eyebrow again. “What the hell are you talking about now?” he replies to Tiny, now even more curious about the contents of the crates. He then laughs again for a moment and asks, “What? Is there some sort of Dominatrix shit or something like that in there?” Tiny and Reggie shake their heads in unison. “No,” Reggie replies. “But they are pretty revealing.” Tiny blushes and nods his head. “You see actual cleavage with some of these,” he says with a smile. James then starts to smirk and then looks at the crates. He sees a bottle of water sitting up on top. Since it’s over a hundred degrees outside at the moment and he’s in the middle of an endless desert substituting as an alien planet, he naturally grabs the bottle for himself to drink later. It’s pretty obvious that he’s plotting some sort of scheme at the moment. He strokes his chin, thinking up some sort of devious way to get back at the drunken bitch whose made his life as an actor miserable over the past three weeks. He could be back in GLOBAL right now kicking the crap out of somebody but instead he’s out here with the “Revenge of the Nerds” techie dorks, an asshole director, and an actress who drinks more than Andre the Giant, which is an accomplishment if you’ve heard the stories. After all of this, there has to be some sort of way to get back at Lindsay. And her bad acting isn’t helping matters either. Every single take in this film has had to be retaken at least twenty times because of her lack of acting skill. Oh, what a joy it will be for James to get back at her and in doing so, make the director’s life a living hell. But it has to be pulled off perfectly. And he’ll need some help, too. James turns to Reggie and Tiny. “Listen guys,” he says with a smile. “How has Lindsay treated you and the rest of the crew since we’ve been out here in Mexico?” “It’s New Mexico,” Reggie replies. “Whatever,” James says with a chuckle. “It’s still Mexico as far as I’m concerned. But listen, how has Lindsay been treating you and the rest of the crew?” Tiny looks angry and so does Reggie. James’ plan is already working perfectly and he hasn’t even figured out what it is yet. Tiny growls, “She treats like shit.” Reggie nods his head. “Yeah,” he replies. “She doesn’t acknowledge us, she calls us nerds, and she slapped Pointdexter in the head when he asked her if she wanted another bottle of water.” James’ eyes light up. He’s got it. The ultimate plan of revenge against the uber-bitch. It’s brilliant! It’s great! And it involves nudity! The nerds will love him for it and he will bask in the glory of the finished product of this plan for many years to come. James turns to Tiny and Reggie. “So what would you and the rest of the boys say if I told you that I had a way of getting back at her, but it involved you helping me out?” he asks them with a devious smile. Reggie and Tiny nod their heads. “We’ll do anything to get back at her for how she’s treated us,” Reggie replies. “We’re in.” James smiles and replies, “Good. Now let’s go get the rest of your buddies and tell them what’s going on. I’m going to need some tech gadgets for this. I have a plan that will both humiliate her and give you guys what you REALLY want to see.” “And what’s that?” Tiny asks, a bit puzzled as to what that might be. James wipes the sweat off of his brow and replies, “Lindsay Lohan naked.” James cackles evilly and motions for them to follow him. “Lets go inside my trailer to talk because it’s hotter than me out here.” Tiny looks at Reggie and shrugs his shoulders as they follow James to his trailer. When they get there, James climbs up the steps and opens the door. He then stops and turns to them. “Go find the rest of the crew,” he says with a sense of urgency. “We haven’t much time. I know that the bitch will come staggering in here any moment. Be back here in twenty minutes after you’ve found them all. I’ll plot the plan out and then we’ll figure out just exactly how we will do it. Also, bring the dress Lindsay will wear for her next scenes. It‘s the key part of our plan.” Tiny nods his head. “Got it,” he says as he and Reggie turn to leave. James then says, “Oh, and one more thing.” Tiny and Reggie stop for a moment as James says, “May the Force be with you.” He then giggles and goes inside his trailer, shutting the door behind him as Tiny and Reggie go off on their noble quest to find the rest of the nerds on the movie crew. Inside his trailer, as he waits for his nerd brethren to return, James is sitting on the couch thinking about absolutely nothing. The air conditioning is turned up on high and he’s still in his silly astronaut costume. Luckily his helmet isn’t on. The scene called for him to take it off, which was good for him because it felt like a fish bowl was stuck on his head or something. But now it is a moment of reflection for him. All of his hard work has led to this. Him sitting on a couch in his trailer on the set of his crappy astronaut movie plotting a way to make Lindsay Lohan naked. It doesn’t get any better than this. At least, for James it doesn’t. This has been the highlight of his life since he was last seen in the PTC. Since he left, his name has been dragged through the mud. The mere mention of the word von Doom is a punch line for jokes now all thanks to his dickhead, uber-nerd cousin Jason von Doom. Jason is GLOBAL’s Darth von Doom and has single handedly ruined the von Doom wrestling name like no one would ever think possible. Oh, what a joy it will be for him to spill Jason’s blood at WrestleCade in two weeks. James smiles as he opens up the bottle of water he grabbed off a crate and takes a swig. He can almost smell the crimson droplets that will spill out. How he’s longed for a good fight. In Hollywood, there’s no fighting, only drinking, and that gets boring after awhile. And the celebrities are dickheads, too. They’re all snobs who have nothing better to do than party and make asses out of themselves in front of the paparazzi. If there weren’t so many ambulance chasing lawyers there, James probably would be in jail for being the shit out of some dumbass celebrity. But he’s smarter than that. The dragon, the beast inside him that’s been caged for so long is waiting to be unleashed. The bloodlust and thirst for carnage has all been bottled up inside him for a year and a half. Ever since he left the UWF, all he’s done is act in shitty movies, each one of them more stupid and pointless than the next. James takes another swig of the water and smiles. It’s fighting time now. No time for this pussy bullshit where one guy fake fights the other on some movie set. This is real fights with real ass kicking going on. The PTC is in a state of upheaval at the moment and they need some new bad ass bastion of beating the living shit out of people to bring some excitement back to the sport. That is how James will get his message across. During the next Infinite Gauntlet, he’ll beat the shit out of some people and show that the von Doom name actually means something in this sport. His name has had little to no recognition from critics and wrestling fans alike. James throws the water bottle at the wall, splashing water all over the place. The bottle then falls to the floor, rolling for a second before stopping. A big dent is on the side of the bottle that hit the wall. “The fuckers,” he snarls as he crosses his arms, staring at the wall. It’s time to prove to the world that he still means a damn. Now, its fighting time. Time to beat somebody’s ass until they ain’t moving. But how can they take him seriously now? I mean, he’s movie actor for God’s sake. This very thought annoys the fuck out of James for a few minutes. With the whole David Arquette and Kevin Federline controversies that happened, how will everyone take James von Doom now that he’s just another Hollywood celebrity? “They’d better respect me,” James snarls as looks around his trailer. Just as he’s about to finish his thought, he hears a knock on his trailer door. “ENTER!” James yells as the door opens and all the nerds in the crew show up. They quickly enter and close the door behind them. James stands up and smiles. “Alright,” he says, rubbing his hands together. “Now that everybody’s here, where’s the dress?” “Right here,” Tiny replies as he walks up to the front of the nerds with Reggie to hand James the dress. James then smiles when he has his prize in his hand. “Good,” James says with a smile. “Now here’s what I have in mind. We’re going to rig up this dress…” James holds up the dress so everyone can see it. Some of them are already salivating at the mouth to see Lindsay Lohan in it. “So that it pulls right off of her and reveals her naked body for all of YOU to see.” Reggie then asks, “But how will she be naked? Won’t she be in underwear?” James shakes his head. “No, she won’t be in underwear,” he replies. “I guarantee it.” Tiny scratches his head, trying to put all of the pieces together. “But how?” he asks James. James smiles deviously. “Because I’m going to give her a bottle of beer before she goes into her trailer to change and promise to have sex with her after the scene is done,” he replies with an evil smirk. “Excellent,” Tiny says with a smile. “But how will we get her dress off of her?” Reggie asks. James replies, “That’s you guys’ end of the deal.” The nerdy tech guys start talking in a huddle and then come up with some sort of plan. “Won’t be a problem,” Tiny says as the huddle breaks up. “We’ll rig up two small remote controlled cutters that will go off at the same time and cut the two sides of her dress. Then, she’ll be naked and wont be able to do a damn thing about it.” James smiles like a proud chief happy with his tribe. “Good,” he says. “Now let’s make it happen." To which Tiny replies, “By the way, we are all members of the Doom. Assimilate or die. Resistance is futile.” All the nerds then give James the Star Trek Vulcan hand sign and then exit the trailer one by one. After the last one has left and the door is closed, James sits back down on his couch and shakes his head in disbelief. “What the hell am I? Lord of the Nerds?” he asks as he begins to contemplate this very thought. Luckily, he doesn’t have to think about this for long. About ten minutes later, Lindsay Lohan shows on the set drunk as hell. One of the crew members quickly runs to James’ trailer and knocks on the door. “YEAH!” James yells at whomever is at the door. “The techie opens the door and yells back, “Quick! Lindsay’s here on the set!” James quickly gets up and runs out the door of his trailer. The techie then points in the direction of Lindsay staggering around with an empty beer bottle in her hand. James smirks deviously. “Thanks,” he tells the tech guy as he then sneaks over to Lindsay and taps her on the shoulder. Lindsay turns around and is drunker than hell. Her eyes are bloodshot and glazed over. She reeks of alcohol, weed, and puke. She looks over at James in his spacesuit costume and asks, “What the hell do you want Buzz Aldrin?” James smiles and tries to sweet talk her. “Baby, I don’t want anything except for to get a little piece of your smoking hot body after we get done shooting the next scene,” he says with a grin. Lindsay thinks about it for a second. “Well,” she replies. “I haven’t had sex in twenty minutes, so yeah. Let’s do it.” James smiles deviously. Everything is going according to plan. “So keep your panties and bra off so we can just go at it faster,” he tells her. She smiles. “I’m going to fuck your brains out Buzz,” she says in a slurred sentence followed by a loud burp. James smiles and replies, “What I’m going to do to you is gonna blow your mind, baby.” Lindsay then giggles and stumbles over to her trailer just as Tiny and Reggie are exiting. Tiny tells her, “We just put your dress for the scene in the closet. Wardrobe just sent it over.” Lindsay glares at him and replies, “Thanks retard.” She then stumbles into the trailer and closes the door behind her. Tiny and Reggie quickly walk over to James and Reggie asks, “So, is it going down?” James gives them a thumbs up and they run off to go get some popcorn, cameras, and to tell the rest of the crew about what is going to happen. About ten minutes later, Lindsay is in her dress and she’s on the set with James, who now has his astronaut helmet in hand. This scene calls for them to walk through the alien desert to find some sort of settlement that may or may not exist. Lindsay stumbles around and gets in place as the asshole director John yells, “ACTION!” James then smiles at Lindsay, “Get ready to get BLOWN AWAY!” John then mutters, “But that isn’t the line.” Suddenly, Tiny pulls out a remote control and hits a big red button in the middle of it. The crew stares in awe as Lindsay’s dress comes flying off to the delight of the horny, droolling crowd of undersexed nerd crewmen just as the cameras to the scene are quickly shut off as John yells out "CUT!" at the top of his voice. The Klingon proverb states that revenge is a dish best served cold. In this case, it’s a matter of revenge being best served naked. For this, the nerds now worship James von Doom like a God. In the tabloids, the Lindsay Lohan versus James von Doom is heating up. And in the PTC, however, James’ revenge will be one best served with gold…PTC gold, the ultimate revenge from a man who never wins at anything he does.
