12 May 2005 The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes, Part III Nova cackles gleefully. "No, no, seriously...seriously...'We're here' is the most redundant exclamation I've ever heard...I mean, where the fuck else would we be?" Character Name: Nova Federation: PRIME The camera pans around a newsroom before settling on a man sitting calmly on a stool behind a marble table. He appears to be in his fifties, dressed in a tidy navy suit with a cornflower tie and powdery white comb-over. He looks intently into the camera before lifting three fingers. He drops one, then another, then gives a thumbs up. "Good evening," he says dramatically, using the kind of voice most men reserve for that intimidate-or-be-intimidated moment when they pick up the phone, "Brock Wolfson reporting for WKKP Fox 41, San Francisco. Our top story toni-JESUS FUCK!" A black sound boom crashes down onto the table top, causing the anchor to lean back and fall off his stool onto the polished floor of the newsroom. When he stands up, he's sans powdery white combover, his 'do now reduced to a grayish horseshoe. "What the hell was that?!" A voice off-camera expresses its sympathies. "Sorry. Butterfingers. Sorry, Brock." Brock takes his place at the stool. "Yeah, you better be glad this isn't live. You'd be fired already and on a plane headed to Barbados to film orangutan mating ritu...why is everyone laughing? Why the hell is everyone laughing?" An aide points to her head a few times and Brock's eyes widen as he reaches a hand up to his scalp. Someone scurries on-camera and grabs the toupee off the floor, handing it to him. He arranges it as best he can while trying to maintain composure. "It's not a toupee. It's just a supplement. Don't judge me, fuckers...are we gonna get rolling? Can we roll camera, please?" Brock stares into the camera, cracking his neck a few times before reassuming his intense glare. He holds up three fingers again and repeats the routine, finally giving the thumbs up to begin rolling. "Good evening. Brock Wolfson reporting for WKKP Fox 41. Our top story tonight...a man whose long-standing nickname-slash-double entendre of 'Rising Star' has perhaps never in his career been more appropriate than at this very moment. They're calling him PRIME's Wonder Boy. They're calling him the Poor Man's Karina Wolfenden. But now, more than ever...they're just calling him." Brock swivels around on his stool to face a different camera. "Nova's seven-month tenure with PRIME has been eventful to say the least. Two 5-Star Title reigns, the latter of which continues today, on top of a quietly amassed ten-and-two record second only to the inimitable K-Wolf, and it's no wonder that the internet smarks are predicting the PRIME-ate in a sweep for his upcoming PTC Extreme Title match. Or maybe it was his dark horse victory in the Royale to determine who would go on to face Global Wrestling's resident wrecking ball, the four-and-zero Shadow." Brock turns back to camera one, discreetly pushing up his hairpiece with a quick brushing motion. "The time Nova spent and continues to spend in PRIME's mid-upper card, on top of a sportman's attitude that's protected the sanctity of his record, has earned him a reputation as a work horse. So the question appears to be...can PRIME's Wonder Boy overcome a determined and currently flawless Shadow, or is the Rising Star due to peter out? More on the story as we inch closer to the Overkill Pay-Per-View, here in San Francisco in our very own Cow Palace. For WKKP Fox 41, I'm Brock Wolfson." The cameraman gives Brock a thumbs up. Brock nods and pulls a flask out of his coat inside pocket, taking a swig and gritting his teeth. "I'm not pathetic...I'm not pathetic...so who wants to grab a bite to eat?" "The Most Unlikely Candidate for Making History" by PTCfanman01 As the title of my weekly article would suggest, a gross opportunity has reared its head within the hallowed halls of Primetime Central...an opportunity to make history; and in a company with as much history under its belt as PTC, opportunities to do something that's never been done before are getting fewer and farther between. What opportunity, you ask (and it would be a good question)? The opportunity for PRIME to become the first member federation in PTC's history to capture the Global Title, Extreme Title, and Tag Team Titles within a five month period. Check your history books, fans. Storm, Jamester, Hessian, Almasy, LKK, and Rage. The last five Global Champs. Storm and Hessian were RPW members during their respective reigns. No one from RPW ever won the Extreme or Tag Team Titles. That eliminates them. XUW came close, no doubt. Joey Vendetti captured the Extreme Title and the Latch Key Kid won the Global Title...but LOW's Shadow Riders beat out the New Royal Legion. UWF had the Global Title won coming into this year, and they had two tag teams take home gold in the Masked Monkeys and Unholy Matrimony...but alas, no Extreme Title win. Finally, Jamester repped GW when he won the Global Title, but it would be nine months before another GW competitor took home gold...and even then, Shadow was in WMW when she won the Extreme Title. I end out of chronological order because Shadow is key to what I'm talking about here, fans...or, more specifically, one of the keys. The other is PRIME's Nova. By winning the contender's Royale, he inadvertently signed up for the responsibility of making history. If it isn't irony, it's at least unexpected. The deciding factor, the most important win for PRIME, doesn't involve Karina Wolfenden's shot at PTC's Global Title. It doesn't involve Boda's pending rematch for PRIME's Universal Title, or the fate of the Alias division that Emilio Rage popularized with his record-setting reign as its champion. It simply boils down to the interfed mid-card match taking place in mere days in the Cow Palace at Overkill. Nova, it's on your shoulders. PRIME is at the top of the mountain, and an accomplishment of this order would be the symbolic "cherry on top" for what has been nothing short of a full-blown transformation of the company. Everyone will be watching this match, from Bubba McCoy at the Global front office, to those superstars edged out in the contender's Royale, to the roster and staff of PRIME there at the event. To fail is to lose grasp of an opportunity that might never again rear its head. To succeed is to be written permanently into history books, or whatever passes for them in wrestling...I suppose perhaps internet chatrooms. You're the unlikely crusader, Nova. Make it happen. Nova slams a balled fist down on the table, knocking over a glass of milk onto his burned copy of Jesus Christ, Vampire Hunter ("The Power of Christ Impales You"). "Shit...," he mutters, shaking the CD before wiping it on his shirt. Ariel looks over at him from her own desk and raises a quizzical eyebrow. "What was that all about?" Nova runs a hand through his hair, scratching his scalp. "I just...I'm just a little irritated about the hype surrounding my match is all." "How so?" his wife asks, her eyes never leaving a spreadsheet sprawled across her desktop. "No one gave a shit about the contender's match," he replies, "Hell, I had to scout that one out myself and toss my name into the hat to even get into the damn thing...and no one cared about the Extreme Title match until someone started yammering about 'Ohhh, if Nova wins, PRIME pwnz(zero)or(zero) j(zero)(zero)!!!(one)!" "You know I hate it when you talk like you're typing in (one)(three)(three)(seven) speak, honey," Ariel points out astutely. "I'm sorry, but I'm a little peeved at the moment," Nova explains, "there's all this talk about 'making history' and 'achieving the triumvirate.' 'The responsibility is on me,' and all that bullshit. Where were they when I was worried about beating Storm, and Maxwell, Shaman, or Kuchinaga? Where was the 'massive responsibility' there? Where was the 'glory for PRIME'? No one even predicted that I'd take it." Ariel looks up from her work, her black-framed glasses sliding down her nose. "And everyone predicted you'd take this one...doesn't that make you feel good, to know how far you've come in such a short amount of time?" "Yeah, it does," Nova admits. "I hear things," Ariel continues, "some things, anyway. The critics are comparing you to Angelo Deville and Karina Wolfenden and the like...some are saying you're due for a Universal Title shot. In the last two months alone, your stock has shot way up. They're calling you a Main Event-calibur athlete now. You're making it, Chris." Nova gazes at his wife intently, one of those meaningful pauses where something special is shared non-verbally between two people. "Can we have sex, because I'm really loving you right now." "No," Ariel replies non-chalantly before pushing her glasses up her nose and staring back down at the spreadsheet. "Come on," Nova prods, a trace amount of whine detectable in his tone, "pleeease? What good is 'making it' if I can't even...*ahem*...'make it' with my own wife?" "Points for the double entendre, but no," she says coolly. "What?" Nova protests, "I worked hard on that double entendre! That was a sex-worthy double entendre! You should be tearing my clothes off right now and begging for anachronistic references or alliterative anecdotes!" "I really should," Ariel agrees, "I don't know what my problem is." "Not even worth a nipple slip?" Nova offers. The look Ariel shoots her husband describes, with admirable eloquence, the distinct absence of nipple slips that Nova can expect. Nova shrugs and gets up out of his chair. He opens the front door to their house and steps out onto the porch, pulling a Camel from his pack and plopping down contentedly into one of the rocking chairs. Well, at least confidence isn't as much of an issue this go-round. The contendership match solved that dilemma. I know I can compete on the level that PTC requires for success. While I don't know that I can beat Shadow, I believe that I can... ...and apparently a lot of other folks do, too. That gives me confidence as well. I know from my brief but memorable encounter with that testicle-hating "Destroy the world of Man, and erect a statue of Aileen Wuernos in every city" fem-Nazi that her confidence isn't an issue, either. ... Okay, that description was a little hyperbolic. But it serves a purpose, ya know? Shadow isn't concerned with PRIME's setting a record or not. She isn't worried about people predicting me to win it. She's only thinking about what she'll have to do to win, and I need to be careful with my own focus, or as soon as I climb into the ring, she'll hit me with her "Massive Chip on My Shoulder O' Doom~!" and it'll be over before you can say "Nothing shouts a feminist respect for genderless categorization of talent and ignorance of cheapened sexual pandering like fishnet stockings."
