Kill Jim: A Tale of a Thai Samurai

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Where’d he go? It’s flaming typical. I specifically told the little thing. Don’t go running off, you’ll get lost and it’ll take flaming ages to find you. But does he listen? Noooooo! And the flight is going to be here soon as well… I’m supposed to be going to Japan to find out about this Kyo Wishywashy bloke that I’m facing in the first round, but I’m not boarding that plane without him being with me. Well actually saying that I’m not boarding that plane anyway, stupid Rush Pro Wrestling and their crappy wages. I got a plane to Thailand instead, that’s quite close to Japan innit? I dunno, probably. They all look the same to me anyway! I’m in the queue to get my boarding pass. There are loads of people about. I get close to the desk, go to check my luggage, and the flaming duck has disappeared. It’s just typical. It’s so busy. I’ve been standing in this queue since forever. I can’t stand it… there’s this bloke in front of me that just stinks of matches. He’s one of those crazy American blokes that are obsessed with guns and stuff. You know the type, wearing their traditional hunting gear, knowing the calibre of 6745 different shotguns… what’s more alarming is the fact that he has this violin case right next to him. No idea what that’s all about. Oh for God’s sake, he has to turn around doesn’t he? The guy looks a complete mess. I thought it was bad enough having to see the ugly mug of this bloke. He turned around showing his wrinkled, demented, stubbletastic beard (stubbletastic is a made up word I know, but it’s the only one that makes sense damn it! He looks like Desperate Dan’s less-shave-happy friend for Christ’s sake!). At least that would have been bad enough if he didn’t smile. Blimey! I thought teeth like that only existed in the movies! Howdy Cletus! Jesus… “Howdy boy…” the guy said to me in a thick southern American accent, as if THAT was a shock… “…the name’s Cheese Steak Jim.” “Okay... Hi, I’m Dave,” This is all I need, the guy talking to me. As if I haven’t got enough on my mind already to be distracted by this bloke. In a week’s time I have to face a Japanese bloke that I’ve never flaming heard of and now I’ve lost my flaming duck! And no doubt this bloke wants to talk to me about his favourite guns. “Do you like guns?” He asks. I just don’t fucking believe it. “Not really, ma-” “I like guns… I really like Tommy Guns…” he says. Then he pats the violin case beside him and looks at me with a beaming grin on his face, “this is mah baybeh,” I really hope his ‘baybeh’ is a violin, the thing that’s supposed to be in a flaming violin case. How the hell would he get that past customs anyway? It’s typical, it really is. Well at least the bloke isn’t giving me a bunch of facts about his ‘baybeh’. “Thompson M1928A1, made famous by the gangsters of the 1920's and 30's.” Great… Where’s this flaming duck? I just sat him on top of all the cases, turned around and then he was gone. He’s not on the floor is he? “It's literally what made the 20's roar, mang!” Shut up! Jesus, doesn’t this bloke realise I couldn’t give a flying one what this bloke has to say about his flaming Tommy gun! I’m trying to find my duck! He’s not here… the duck is gone… I just don’t believe it. After all we’ve been through together these past few months, the good and the bad. We did everything together, me and ducky… ducky and me… we got lost in a desert together, I accidentally entered him into a wrestling match with a psychopath, the flop of us in every fed we went into… actually there wasn’t very much good was there? Maybe that’s why he’s gone? He couldn’t stand hanging around with EDH, loser Dave, guy that never wins, er… loser Dave. Let’s face it, I’m a failure. I’ve been in wrestling for the last 10 years and what do I have for it…? Nada… Okay well, I got an International title in a now defunct fed that nobody really cared of anyway, big deal. There are geezers in this tournament with real honour to their name, geezers with world titles, tournament championships and geezers with a million different belts in a dozen feds or more. But I have nothing… I’m a loser… it’s no wonder Ducky is ashamed of me. “They fire ‘bout 700 rounds per minute, ya know?” FOR GOD’S SAKE! I DON’T WANT TO HEAR ABOUT YOUR FLAMING TOMMY GUN! I’VE LOST MY DUCKY DAMMIT! Calm down Dave… it’s okay… he doesn’t realise you’re upset. Just speak nicely to him and things will be okay. “Sorry mate, I’m a bit busy looking for something.” I say nicely to him. “That’s alright mang. I hope I don’t lose mah Tommy gun. The ammo I can lose, they’re fine, I can get 20-30 round box magazines, or a 50/100 drum magazine.” “SHUT UP ABOUT YOUR FLAMING TOMMY GUN! I’M TRYING TO FIND MY DUCK!” Okay, I’ve gone and done it now… and the comment about the duck with it seems to be eliciting some pretty confused looks from everyone in the queue with us. I probably shouldn’t have shouted that loudly… “What…?” the idiot gun fanatic begins, “this one…?” And there it was... the symbol of everything my life has evolved around for the last few months; the small, plastic, yellow bath toy resting on the palm of this gunslinger’s hand. “DUCKY~!” I screamed at the top of my lungs before pouncing on the poor sod and swiping the duck off of his greasy hand. I can’t believe this bloke decided to steal a plastic bath toy. Oh my… he wasn’t going to use Ducky for target practice was he?! Oh man, I might have just saved Ducky’s life there and then. Thank God the dumb fuck decided to show the duck to me. My poor Ducky could have been gone for good! -----=====|=====----- I’ll never know the reasons for Ducky ending up in that guy’s hand, whether it was because the guy had tried to run off with it, or if it was Ducky trying to leave me. Ducky won’t tell me… maybe because it’s an inanimate object and its little beak can’t move, but I think it’s something else. The duck has seemed pretty distant recently. He hasn’t seemed his usual lively self. We’ll see, for now I have other things to think about, namely this Japanese geezer Kyo Wishywashy. It’s proving harder than it seems though. All these blokes look the same… 4ft tall, podgy yellow faces with a small moustache… even some of the ladies! Well anyway, I decided that I needed a break from this Jappy-search and went for a drink in this bar. So that’s where I am now. Ducky’s okay, I got the barman to pour some water into an ashtray and he’s happy floating there for a moment. Something did catch my eye on the way in though, a little poster showing the entertainment for tonight. Apparently in a minute we’re supposed to be having exotic juggling or something, sounds good to me. Hopefully will be seeing a nice looking bird with big jugs throwing stuff about making her boobs go flopping all about and stuff, it’ll be great. You know, I have a feeling this isn’t exactly a trendy bar. It’s really dark and smoky, the beer costs less than a packet of crisps in most pubs in good ol’ Blighty, the only people in the bar seem to be the older variant of the Thai blokes that all look the same, with the exception of a couple of tourists at the front who are typical perverted middle aged blokes and everyone seems to have their eyes fixated on the stage even though the show hasn’t started yet. Maybe it’s a good show… I dunno… Aw mate… I now know why this beer is cheap. It’s like watered down piss. I didn’t fly half-way across the globe to drink a variant of Foster’s for Christ’s sakes. Oh, the show is about to start… Ducky doesn’t seem too interested, but seeing the look on the faces of the people in the place I’m quite looking forward to this now. Hey! What a flaming rip off! I was expecting a blonde bird with big tits! I get a midget lady sitting on a chair, what the flaming hell! What’s exotic about this, it’s a Thai bird in a miniskirt sitting on a chair, oh and now she’s going to do the splits, and… WHOA! Hello there! That’s a nice brand of underwear you have there Miss Thai lady. The non-existent range really suits you! What’s the bucket of ping pong balls for? We’re not here to watch you play table tennis love! Where are you putting those… what the… she’s just put the ping pong ball… between her legs… into somewhere I don’t think those things were designed to go, it’s a perfect fit to be fair. Whoa! That thing just flew half-way across the room and hit some poor bloke in the eye! To be honest, I don’t think the lucky bugger minded too much! Now what is she doing… she’s leaning back really far, legs akimbo. Oh… my… god… now I know why they call it exotic JUGGLING. It sounds like someone’s micro-waving popcorn over there… pop… pop…. pop… Aw man, now that I stop and take it all in. I don’t know what it is, but there’s something about this girl. It’s just a small Thai lady that looks the same as all the other people in this place. But there’s something about the way she makes those ping pong balls move so gracefully… like spherical and sweaty ballet dancers, performing a hyperactive version of Swan Lake on trampolines… it’s a sight to behold, it really is. She’s amazing. I really need to talk to this girl. Oh wait the show is nearly over, a big finale? DUCK! Not you Ducky! Well, you better duck as well. She’s gone mad and is firing them across the room machine gun style. The crowd are sat there clapping and staring in sheer awe at the amazing display held in front of their very eyes. I couldn’t even bring myself to clap. The whole thing has me stunned on the spot. Well, here she comes, obviously off to buy something from the bar after a hard night’s squeezing. Come on Dave, say something. Say something witty, say something to impress her. What about one of those one-liners? Your body is like a spanner, every time I look at you my nuts tighten? No, don’t be stupid. She’s not a mechanic. She won’t possibly know technical tools such as spanners. I haven’t got a library card, but do you mind if I check you out? She probably doesn’t have books. Not sure if they have them in Thailand. Aw man, say something at least! Oh my word, she’s coming this way! Come on Dave, she’s getting a seat right next to you. Speak boy! Speak! Hey wait a minute… isn’t she Thailandish, or whatever they speak? She won’t speak any English. “Hi,” she says to the bar tender, that must be Thai for ‘bar tender’ or something, “can I have a beer please?” Whoa! I speak Thai. I always knew I was a genius, nobody ever believes me. I picked up Thailandish off of one flaming sentence! She waits intently as the bar tender goes to get her beer for her. As this happens she looks to her side and sees an ashtray of water with a plastic yellow duck floating on top. “That your duck?” she asks. “Er… Yeah…” “Cool” Who says ‘Yeah’, for God’s sake! You so stupid Dave! You’re such a nerd! Such a doofus! You suck! Now, say something cool. “Oh you speak English?” she asks. “Yeah, you?” “Well… duh!” Ha, ha, ha! She’s so funny. ‘Duh…’ ha, ha! “So how come you can speak English then? They don’t speak English in Thailand do they?” I ask her. “Well… I would like to say that it’s because I was born in America, brought up there for 15 years and then moved over here when my career in Hollywood went horribly wrong, but in all seriousness it’s because of some dumb ass that spent 15 minutes planning the storyline and didn’t stop to think for one minute about the fact that people from Thailand don’t speak English, so he’s decided to just slap me in here with some really lame excuse and expect the reader to flick through it as if it was perfectly acceptable for them to speak English.” “Eh…? You what, love?” “Never mind…” Okay, you’ve started talking to her. She’s a mentalist, but it’s okay. Now it’s time to impress her with your English charm and romanticism (flaming hell that’s a big word! – Ed)… say something to compliment her or something. Okay, she has that bucket of ping pong balls on the floor between her legs… “So… you have a nice set of balls there” I say, pointing between her legs. “Er… you’re not shocked in any way at all?” she says crossing her legs. Don’t know why she wants to cross her legs, but you know. “No… I think it’s all very impressive actually.” I say, assuring her that the show was a success. “Impressive? Well… I think its pretty average sized,” she says, obviously being modest about the whole situation. This girl is set for stardom, for Christ sakes! “Average, bah! It’s going to be huge!” I tell her. “Well… maybe later,” she says, winking at me. Am I in there? Am I? Did I pull?! I think I might have! I think I pulled! Hellfire and brimstone folks! I think I gone pulled mahself a Jezebel by God! Man I’m getting goofier than a pet coon, whatever the flaming hell that is! “Listen,” she says, moving towards me and leaning to my side, tilting her head to whisper something in my ear, an offer to go back to her place perhaps? Or does she just want to whisper sweet nothings in my ear? What’s up with sweet nothings anyway? Surely if someone was to whisper sweet nothing in someone’s ear not a whole lot would come out. “My lover has just been locked up. I need someone to help me. Will you come with me?” DAMMIT! And there was me thinking I’d pulled… I’m never going to flaming pull… “Well?” she asks, as I realise that as I was cursing the fact that she has a lover I completely ignored her question. “Er… well…” “Good!” she says grabbing my wrist and yanking me clean off my chair. It’s a good thing I managed to swipe Ducky from the ashtray as I went, I wasn’t going to lose that poor little mate twice in one day, that’s for sure. -----=====|=====----- I didn’t think about it too much. I don’t think about anything to much to be honest, but this geezer, who’s this Thai bird’s lover. He’s locked up. What’s he going to think when he was broken out of jail by his lover only to see me standing there next to her? Oh man. I bet he’s one of those skin heads as well. With a tattoo over the head, greatly overweight, flames on the outfit and the nickname The Beast from the East. This isn’t good. Hey wait a minute! All the people from this place are likely to be less than 4 feet flaming tall! I could ‘ave him, no problem! We’re going by motorbike. She rides the bike, I sit behind and Ducky sits in my jacket pocket holding on for dear life. At least he would be if he had any hands anyway. We stop outside a pretty large building, but not a prison by any stretch of the imagination. Maybe because they’re only little in this country they only have little prisons, I guess we’re about to find out. “Shh!” she says, as If I was talking anyway, “I’m going to climb through this conveniently open, but rather small window. You shine the torch so I can see what I’m doing.” “Er… okay…” I say as I shine the torch up her skir- I mean at the window. Damn, maybe I should have shone the light up her skirt. Too late now, she’s gotten in. That was quick! She’s just opened the front door to let me in. Okay then, too late to chicken out now. Now I get to see what a skin headed Thai bloke looks like. She could have put some new batteries in this flaming thing the light seems to be dimming out. Well shining the torch about this place I don’t see too much. I keep hearing some really creeping howling noises though, it’s freaking me out. Is it a full moon or something? Well, as we walk down the corridor between the two sets of cells, I daren’t look over there in case I make eye contact with one of the criminals. You can get some pretty tough blokes in these prisons you know. She suddenly stops in her tracks. We’ve obviously gotten to the right cell. “This is it she says as she gets down on one knee and takes out a key,” I tell you what this is one pretty small cell. It’s more like a cage really. You couldn’t even fit a 4 foot Thai bloke in her- Oh no… That was when it dawned on me… The Thai bird’s lover… … … …is a fucking Labrador. “Hey wait!” she says sounding really alarmed all of a sudden. “What…?” “This isn’t Butch! Where the hell is Butch!” All of a sudden I find myself blinded by a blinding light of blindness that’s really blinding and stuff. I would come up with a much more intelligent metaphor, but I’m currently trying to recover from temporary blindness, so bear with me here. “He checked out earlier today.” A black blurry blob says from the door to the room, “and I don’t appreciate you breaking into my Dog Pound.” “Dammit!” the Thai lady yells, “We’re too late! They’ve gotten to him first!” “Who’s gotten to him…?” I ask. “The Wishywashy tribe, they’re this really fucked up religious group. They all think they’re satanic samurais.” “That is pretty fucked up. Who’d want to be a satanic samurai?” I ask the Thai lady rhetorically. “I don’t know.” She says, replying to the question anyway, “The kind of person that would kill his own father, have samurai fights with floating orbs and turn down the offer of a demon to take over the world, I guess.” “You what?” “Never mind… we need to get going. The ceremony must be taking place tonight. We need to make our way to the temple before sun up.” “The temple…? They have a temple?” “No, The Golden Temple, the Thai restaurant up the road.” “Ah!” -----=====|=====----- This is actually a pretty flash place. We walk through the door and are greeted with a whole host of golden furniture. Golden tables, golden chairs, golden lamps even a golden desk-where-you-pay-and-stuff. When they said this was the Golden Temple, they weren’t shitting you, I can tell you. “Excuse me,” a guy with a mop says from the other end of the room, “we’re closed.” Okay, well the next bit is kind of hard to say. Mainly because I don’t speak Thailandish, however much I wanted to believe I could. I’m not going to even attempt to type what the Thai lady said, okay you twisted my leg. “ASAHKSDJGAHASOJU AOJIGAKLK ALJGASUAOGUAOUJGU~!” correct me if I didn’t quite pronounce it properly. What do you mean ‘am I pissed?’! Fuck knows what she said, but it seems to have pissed off the Thai bloke with the mop. He chucks the mop to one side, sticks his hands inside the bucket, and pulls out… a big arsed machete! The guy runs like an Arab declaring Jihad towards the poor little Asian beauty. I was about ready to step in there, but she doesn’t even seem scared. As the guy gets ever so closer, the lady calmly swishes her head to the side, swaying the hair down to one side of her face. She grins wickedly at the man, not a single worry about the peril it appears she’s in. Then as the guy brings back his arm, ready to drive the knife into her chest, in one swift motion she lunges forward and sends a palm strike to the guy’s chest before he could even move his arm at all. The guy is sent careering across the room, his head hitting the wall and him flopping to his untimely death, blood dripping slowly from the lips of his loose open mouth. From the opposite end of the room came a sound, the sound… of clapping. “Well, well, well.” A man says from the source of the clapping. It appears to be the Chinese version of Santa Claus. He has a really long white beard hanging down from his chin and wearing this red silken cloak thing which looks way too much like pyjamas. “OI!” I yell at the old geezer, “Pee pee! I thought you were killed by that bird that wore too much red lipstick!” “SILENCE!” the man shouts, “We will never die, for I am a bad old wise evil grandmaster cliché!” “You what, mate?” “Never mind… and my name is not Pee pee! It’s Mei Pei!” “Me Pee, Pee Pee, what’s the flaming difference…?” “Well there’s a Mei before the Pei instead of there being two Pe- OH SHUT UP FOOLISH BOY! For irritating me you shall now both die! MWAHAHAHAHAH! Beatrix Potter… Prepare to meet thy doom! MWAHAHAHA!” He claps once more and the moment he does so a flood of satanic samurai ninja geezers come bursting from behind the old man. The old man slowly walks out of the door as more and more satanic ninjas make their way through, the room getting ever the more crowded as time goes by. “Beatrix Potter? Didn’t you write Peter Rabbit?” “Don’t ask…” These guys are only 4 feet tall. They may have some whopping great swords, but I could still kick their arses. The lady looks alarmed all of a sudden, that isn’t a very good thing really is it? “That’s it matey!” I say, intimidating one of the ninjas, “you think you’re a double ‘ard bastard with that shiny sword eh? Well come and ‘ave a go, if you think you’re ‘ard enough!” The ninja turns his sword to one side, and before I could say ‘who turned out the lights?’ everything went completely black. You don’t think the little bastard managed to kill me, do you? Aw man. I’m fucking dead. How the hell am I supposed to beat that Kyo Wishywashy now? The light at the end of the tunnel… God… if I could grant one wish… could you please just send me back so I can win the GTT? Ppppplease? Hey, hang on. God looks a lot like that Thai bird, Beatrix… “It’s okay now.” She says, “We’re safe now.” “Okay,” I say as I lift my feet out of a pool of blood. Wait a second… pool of blood? Holy fuck! Looking around me, there’s an endless sea of dead bodies and blood all over the floor here. This kicks Kill Bill’s arse! “Hey,” she says to me playfully, “I bet you never thought you’d see this much blood and violence? Hey, to some people this would be a whole roleplay!” “A whole what…?” “Never mind…” Oh no… I don’t know if it was in the confusion. I don’t know if that flaming ninja did it after he knocked me out, but… “DUCKY~!” “Howdy boy…!” It had to be the fucking redneck… I should have known that idiot would have ended up here… Needless to say, the redneck has my duck again. The only bad thing is this time he took his Tommy gun out of his case and is holding the gun to the side of poor Ducky’s plastic cheek. “YOU BETTER NOT DO IT!” “Oh really?” he says sarcastically, “and what exactly are you going to do then, boy?” The smug bastard, there really isn’t anything I can do. What the hell is Beatrix doing? She looks up at me as she gets down on one knee and pretends to adjust her boots, as a smile begins to gleam across her round face she gives a wink. Am I in there? Oh wait, she has a plan. She grasps a knife from one of the fallen samurais, looks up to Jim sharply and launches the knife at him. Cheese Steak Jim panics and fires off in our direction with the Tommy Gun! The knife passes the gun, landing right underneath his chin and burying deep into his throat. Blood gurgles out of Jim’s mouth. He falls to his knees and directly to the floor, impaling the knife even further through his neck. After all of that there is the last sound, breaking the silence of his death, it sends shivers down my spine. A feint, breathless whisper… “Daaaaaaave…” And then… as if it couldn’t get any worse… TO BE CONTINUED… DUFF... DUFF... DUFF... DUFF, DUFF, DUFF, DUFF... (Thank you Eastenders)* * Taken from the British TV show Eastenders, the show sucks, but the end of episode music rules.
