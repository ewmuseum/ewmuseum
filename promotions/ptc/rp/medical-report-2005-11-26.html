Medical Report

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Medical Report Doctor: Dr. John Harrison Patient: Cyrus Skar Patient’s Condition: Multiple Personality Disorder Patient ID: G515273 Date: 27th November 2005 Notable Patient Details: Three personalities as found out in previous meetings. Cyrus is the dominant personality, there seems to be two others. One refuses to be named and seems to be referred to as ‘The Voice’. I’ve heard all three personalities speak through Cyrus’ tongue and I can honestly say ‘The Voice’ disturbs me the most. As far as I’m aware, ‘The Voice’ can only physically take control of Cyrus’s body when he is mentally weak, or stressed. This must be looked into. ‘The Voice’ seems to want to be the dominant personality but has never managed to take control of Cyrus’s body for long periods of time. It has even set Cyrus up for murder, its motives are questionable. The third personality has only recently arisen. It likes to be known as ‘Drake Kaos’. Mr. Kaos seems to be the most evil; for like of a better term, of the personalities. In my one meeting with Mr. Kaos I found him to have no morals or virtues. The tone in which he speaks does not scare me; though it commands me to listen and obey. I am bewildered as to how a tone of voice can show so much power. This personality seems to be getting stronger and stronger and has no problem in taking over Cyrus’s physical functions. This worries me due to the nature of Mr. Kaos and what he is capable of. Session: Mr. Skar and I discussed his condition once more, with any additions or new symptoms that had occurred since our last meeting. Mr. Skar informed me that his new personality, ‘Drake Kaos’ was causing him a lot of trouble. Fights have been started and ended abruptly by Mr. Kaos. Mr. Skar went on to say that he was having trouble stopping Mr. Kaos from taking over as the dominant personality, Mr. Skar fears that Mr. Kaos could become the dominant personality indefinitely if things continue the way they are doing. Mr. Skar then went on to tell me of an incident at a bus station. Mr. Kaos took control and threw, what he called ‘A disrespectful youth’ through a window. Mr. Skar felt like a bystander watching Mr. Kaos as he continued to bloody the fallen teenager. Nobody rang the Police in fear of their own safety. Mr. Skar wishes to put a stop to this madness, he felt a slight amount of remorse for the child as he hadn’t directly insulted anybody. He’d just spat on the floor. Mr. Skar requested any treatment I had an offer. Medication given (If any): Mr. Skar took away a new drug, still in it’s testing phases. Tramapoleethin. He signed a release incase of any side-effects and promised to report back to the lab every two days to make sure nothing severe happened to him. Next meeting: To be decided by Mr. Skar. Recommended to be within the next two weeks. End report.
