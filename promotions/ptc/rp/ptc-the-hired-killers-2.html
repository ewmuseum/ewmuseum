The Hired Killers

16 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-[Chapter 3: The Invasion]- Fathom arrives at London’s airport as he slams the car to a halt and jumps out. Circling the back in order to grab his bags, he steps in a small puddle that surprises him. He looks down expecting to find oil or another car-oriented liquid but soon realizes that his car is leaking green paint and it is coming from an open hole attached underneath his Honda Element. Fearing the worst, Fathom mouths out a profane word and pushes his right hand through his now-curly brown hair. He turns and begins to gaze around to see who were the perpetrators of this act but only finds many cars parked and off. Knowing that something is wrong, he puts his black bag over his shoulder and begins to walk away from his car as if nothing is wrong but still keeping an eye on his surroundings as to make sure that he is not being followed. He gets to the end of the parking lot where a shuttle bus is and begins to board it when he suddenly sees that a black Ford Explorer has turned its lights on and is starting its engine. Fathom walks and exits with the many people through the mid-exit on the bus as he finds himself in with a group of women watching as the bus departs towards the airport. Suddenly the Explorer peels out from its parking place and speeds off towards the bus, assuming that Fathom is actually on it although we know the truth. One of the women he is walking with turns and stares at him with a small smile on her face. Her baby blue eyes stare at him with a friendly affection as he begins to smile back. “Hello”, she says with a gleam in her eye. “I don’t believe that I’ve ever seen you in our group before.” Fathom puts his arm around her, realizing the body odor that extends from his body and then taking it off as he replies to her. “Well, that’s probably because I’ve never been part of this group before”, he says smiling. “I am just a man who is in need of a ride.” Her smile grows wider as she steps a little bit quicker so that she has caught up with the other girls and begins to talk to them in a whisper as one large giggle resonates from the crowd of about six. She turns back and looks at him, her cheeks growing a bright red from embarrassment. They all board a long white bus and take seats but nobody accesses the driver’s seat, which confuses Fathom. All sit in the back along the two rows of seats as Fathom is surrounded by all of the six women, staring straight at him with delighted smiles. One of the other women, a shorter one with brown hair but a nice body smiles as she runs her finger along his chest. “So then, is this your first time doing this?” she asks him with delight. Now Fathom isn’t sure what he is supposed to think. What kind of group is this? Where are they going? Are they supposed to be going? So many questions run through his mind but he finds himself with only one answer, the one answer that will not let them figure out that he doesn’t actually belong here. “No”, he replies, busting out his acting skills as he tries to act bashful. “I’ve never done this before.” They all begin to look around at each other with excited looks on their faces. The one who was a little embarrassed at first climbs over from the back section of seats and finds her way onto his lap to his surprise as he begins to put his arms around her with amusement. "I can't believe you've never been in an orgy before," says the woman on his lap. Fathom begins to smile with pure enjoyment when suddenly the van is jolted forward. He tosses the girl on his lap to the ground as all of the women begin to scream with terror. Turning around reveals the black Explorer now ramming the van as all of the women jump out of it and get away while he continues to stare out at the Explorer in front of him. Somebody jumps out of the passenger door carrying a gold belt. The tall man runs up to the passenger door of the van and points inside as Fathom turns and tries to exit but the man holds onto the door as hard as he can while the black Explorer backs up and hurries forward. Sensing danger, Fathom disappears into the van as the Explorer speeds off forward and runs into the van, sending it over the edge of the parking level down towards the ground as the man who was holding the door quickly runs over to look over the edge and watch it crash. He looks down with an approving smile on his face while flipping the gold belt over his shoulder. The man turns around and gives a thumbs up as the black Explorer pulls forward and allows the man to hop in. As the sirens begin to sound out in the front, the Explorer goes in reverse and then flees the scene to leave the parking lot desolate. That is until something begins to stir from behind a car a few spots away from the scene of the horrendous accident. Fathom pops up from behind a white Toyota Cressida breathing heavily as he puts his hands over his hips. He watches as the black Explorer speeds off but continues to be confused about exactly what went on. Fathom shakes his head before turning and looking down as the cops all surround the scene. -[Chapter 4: Surprise!]- Inside of the dark room lines all sorts of people around a table. There is minimal light and, in fact, the only light in the room is dimmed down and revealing no faces. A loud laughter is spurred from a leather chair that is turned away from the table, presumably the mastermind of the entire operation. Peering around the table, nobody can be seen but it is obvious that one of the men at the table was the perpatraitor of the act commited earlier as the gold belt seen at the crime is still securely placed around his arm. But all of a sudden, the laughter stops and the person in the chair begins to speak in a booming, manly voice. "I am glad, very glad. Our XWA United States champion took PTC's Extreme Champion out of contention and.... Haha, out of life." says the voice. "Now that will allow me to enter the PTC Extreme Championship contention and take the belt. Doesn't that sound good?" Suddenly every person around the table begin to laugh as the person in the chair turns the television on. Quickly flips a picture of the news as all of them quiet. "Watch this, this should be good", says the man in the chair. From the television, a news anchor sits in his chair and gleams a smile while reporting the accident. So amazing that one can smile over this but alas, this news anchor puts on his pearly whites. "News today", says the news anchor. "A van is tipped over off of an airport parking lot from six stories. Apparently somebody must have left it in park and left as nobody was found in the van." All of a sudden the silence gets a little more tenser as nobody speaks. Even though the owner cannot be seen, it is obvious that he is mad. Every single person around the table looks around at each other with worried looks gracing their facades before the booming voice suddenly explodes. "NOBODY WAS FOUD!?!" yells out the man. "Why wasn't anybody found! He was supposed to die!" Nobody speaks. They know that speaking could lead to being the next in Fathom's supposed position... Dead. Instead, the man with the XWA US Championship tries to speak. "Buh-boss..." he says. "I don't know wha-" He is cut off by the boss as the boss begins screaming. "I know you don't know nothing, that is why you are just a lowly wrestler!" he yells, slamming his hand down on the remote, flipping the channel. "I cannot believe you...." All of a sudden the man stops talking. In fact, everybody stops doing anything. The channel that has been turned to depicts the face of Fathom. It is a close up on Fathom's face while his curly brown hair drapes over the sides of his face. This is not the Fathom that we saw earlier in the day, instead this is the Fathom nobody wants to see, the pissed off one. We switch from the dimly lit room to where Fathom is at. Nothing can be seen but instead Fathom continues staring a hole straight through the television. "I have tried... and tried to get a hold of Argyle." he says, speaking through his gritted teeth. "And you know what I have got? Some little slut who continues to tell me that he is not there. Where is he? He isn't at PTC headquarters because that is where I am at." He extends his arms as the camera extends out. He is sitting on the floor of the office in front of a desk as it keeps extending until it shows the secretary sitting at her desk tied up with rope and duct tape over her mouth. Fathom continues looking at the camera and raising his hands in the air. "So instead", he continues. "Instead I will get my message to him another way. You see, this is how it is. I have been followed and attacked by the supposed "greatest organization in PTC history", the XWA. These guys have been coming at me left and right and you know who sent them? It was Mike Marvelous, the man who is too weak to come at me one-on-one. Instead he sends a bunch of lambs being sent to slaughter and one-by-one, I have proved to them that I am smarter... That I am faster.... And that I am stronger. And in the end, I walked out on top and walked out..." His smile grows a little bit bigger as he drops his arms down into his lap. He waits for a second as the camera zooms in closer until it is at his face. Suddenly he blows up with anger. "More pissed off!" he yells into the camera. "For Mike Marvelous, you are dealing with death now because death is the only thing that can set you free. Death is the only person that can help ease the pain you shall feel. Hope for death, pray for death, embrace him. For I shall no longer allow you to Fathom my words because words will not hurt you at this point because Fathom feels physical pain shall prove my point even better. End scene, end night, end... Mike Marvelous." The scene cuts to black with the woman squirming and trying to speak as Fathom yells 'shutup!' to her. Quickly we go back into the room where all of the men sit. Nobody speaks but the awkwardness is still there. "Great.", is the first word to come from behind the chair. "We haven't killed him, we just made him more pissed off. He is going to go in and kill Mike Marvelous and instead of having a worn-out Fathom after the match, we are going to have one out for our blood, I hope you are all happy!" The chair suddenly begins to spin around for us all to see the person's face but the scene quickly cuts to black as we are left wondering many things.
