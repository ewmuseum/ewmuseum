Anarchs in the IG

4 August 2005

Anarchs in the IG **All characters used in this rp have been used with the express permission of their respective handlers.** It's funny to think that all this used to be mine. I used to walk these halls and look through different eyes on the empire I attempted to build. The lawyers, public reps, paper pushers, and number crunchers. They all called me boss, and now they don't turn their heads long enough to give me a seconds thought. Inside this mask I'm nothing more than a name in a file, a personality in the ring, making the money that pays their cheques. Anarch Agencies. I forged and gambled this company on money I didn't have, in the end I created something that would last beyond my time. Now there's a new boss, now Johnny Lexicon is dead, now there's only me, The Marauder, to walk these halls. I can hear my compatriots down the hall already, and I can only assume that the cluster fuck cleverly disguised as a staff meeting was already well underway. The door stood slightly ajar and I pushed it open furthur; immedietly a blond woman spun around to face me, pretty and well toned. "Well look who decided to show up." She snapped and waved me into the room. Eva Van Lorne. Somehow I expected her to be standing, she was like that. Her brother, and co GCW Tag Team champion, Erik sat quietly by himself with empty chairs to his left and right. He was like that. The EVL Twins, Anarch picked them up on a GCW tour through Berlin, they seemed to take to the ring well. The table was long and flanked by heavy cushioned chairs. I silently moved to the nearest one and pulled it out to settle down. I look at all these people here, and I remember a time that they didn't exist. I was the first, I was the best, and now I'm nothing. "Yo man this is whak, if Jordan ain't even gonna show up what I gotta be here fo?" Lupus Feral, seated directly across from me, continues to stare. He knew, though I'd never give him the satisfaction of revealing myself, as much as he'd press me to do so. Lupus and his new manager Havoc had become Anarchs as a tag team, Havoc got injured and now he's pushing Lupus into that insane HATE promotion, the kid always had style in the ring, he just chokes when he gets the big shots. "We're here to discuss our new marketing strategy." Eva snaps with her usual candor. With a wrinkled nose Lupus settles himself back and shakes his head. "Where is Jordan?" Eva calls down the table to the last man left. Trey Tremendous. GCW's reigning United States and now defunct Hardcore champion. The man to put me down, the one who beat me for everything I had, the puppet on Jordan White's malevolent strings. He looks nothing like he did when he signed to Anarch Agencies. He's been kidnapped once, disappeared for a few weeks and came back different. He came back stronger than anybody I know. Including The Marauder. Trey shrugs and doesn't respond. "A constructive bunch we are." Eva sighs in exasperation and lifts a cell phone into view, dialing maddly before turning around pressing the device to her ear. Every pair of eyes turn to examin her pert behind, barely restrained by the white mini skirt she wears. I adjust the leather mask on my skull, the eye sockets cutting off just a touch of my peripheral vision, skewing my perception of the world without hindering my abilities to perform. Behind this mask I felt secure from them, the Anarchs, the world. The leather protected my identity, The Marauder protected Johnny Lexicon. "How'd that battle royale go, Lex?" Lupus gives me that wolfish grin. A chuckle. "How'd that next gen title shot go?" With a snort Lupus looks back to Eva who snaps her cell phone shut and puts a hand on her forehead to think. "What did Jordan want to talk to us about?" She muses. When it came to attempted predictions of Jordan White, the effort is more often than not as futile as it is exasperating. "Some how I doubt he had a pep talk and a prayer in mind." Lupus snickers. "Shyeah, ya never know." Trey produces. "Maybe he does have a prayer." "You mean a rant." The sycophantic prick. "Well, we're all here so he wanted something right?" Eva asks without giving time for a response. "Maybe he wanted to talk about one of us." "Or all of us." Erik says something for the first time, his german accent heavy. He stared at me sometimes too, always the quiet and unobtrusive shadow of his attention whore sister. He creeped me out. "Well, it looks like we're all here." A new voice enters the room. Standing in the open door is a tall black man wearing a black and white pin stripe suit with matching wide brimmed hat. His smile stretches his trimmed goatee ackwardly with just a touch of charisma. "And you are?" Eva cocks a hand on her hip. "Midas T. Gold." He lifts the hat from his head, revealing black corn rows that suit his broad face. Stepping inside he continues. "It's a pleasure to meet you Eva." He nods before looking around the room. Skeptical silence. "Alright... Well let me start by explaining why I'm here." "Might help." Trey cocks his head to the side. "My label has recently signed a deal with Anarch Agencies, Anarch Entertainment now owns all marketing rights to your images." "Excuse me?" Eva snaps. "I don't think I stuttered." He replies casually. "From this point on you are legally bound from appearing in any interviews, commercials, sponsorship deals, or movie cameos not produced or approved by Anarch Entertainment. No merchandise will be sold in your name unless produced or approved by Anarch Entertainment. In return, we've negotiated a hefty royalty fee." "Yo this is bull shit." Lupus stands up. "I own my own image. My contract says I own my own merchandise man." "What merchandise?" Trey laughs. "Fuck you man." Lupus retorts. It's said that every Anarch has a different contract, but none have ever seen another Anarch's contract to compare. None except for me and now Jordan White that is. And somebody has been feeding Lupus a line, that was never in his paper work. "This isn't ment to hinder your career." Midas consoles. "We're all Anarchs and it's time we started acting like it. You're wrestlers, not actors, not musicians, your sports entertainers. You leave the rest to me, and we'll make you all famous." "Shiit." Trey shakes his head and silences as abruptly as he'd spoken. "Which brings me to the topic of this meeting." "Wait a second, where's Jordan?" Eva pressures. "He couldn't make it, though I'm sure he wishes he could be here." Trey chuckles. "So you tellin me I gotta take orders from some advertising nerd?" Lupus continues to resist. "All you have to do is do what you're doing. You have a chance at a Next Generation title feud in HATE and it would be best for all of us if you focused on that. Trey, Eva, you have a United States title match coming up, have you stopped to consider the ramifications of this competition? "It's just business."Eva shoots a glance over to Trey, who in turn shoots a glance over at her. The tension had been there all night, but I wasn't going to say something first. This was the first time in two years an Anarch would fight an Anarch. We never claimed to be partners, a stable, a group of collaborated performers. We're just wrestlers wrestling, and our pay cheques helped ensure that the rest of us could continue to wrestle as long as we had the strength to do so. I should know, it's my brain child, but the tension is still there, would always be there. "And you." Somehow I felt the direction of this conversation was angling towards me. Midas was looking at me, as were the rest of the Anarchs. "You're not doing anything." "I have something in the works. Gauntlet. Infinite." I intend to win myself a little of what I once had. "You can't compete in the IG." Eva snaps suddenly. "Why not?" Midas asks. "I think it's perfect, we need all Anarchs in the field." "Because I'm competing in the IG!" "Maybe not the best decision with a United States title match. Cake. Eat it. Getting a little greedy lately, Eva?" She didn't respond, because like Lupus, and like probably most everybody else, she knows who is underneath the lies, the mask. There is history beneath The Marauder, there is a man who knows them, the business. "A little jealous aren't we, 'Marauder'?" "C'mon now let's settle down, if it's a feud you want save it for the cameras." Midas interjects. "Now I'm a business man, and I wouldn't take a project I don't think can make it big. I watched all of you, for a long long time, and I know we can make this thing work." Those leather eye sockets, almost like windows at times, settle on Midas as he prattles on. "We're not bound to work together, sometimes to bring out the best in each of us we need to compete with each other. And so just like Eva is competing with Trey and John... The Marauder, you'll all be competing in ratings. The better ratings you get the better bonus you receive." "You're kidding, right?" Eva asks. "This is a company and we have a business to run, in order to run this company all of our cogs need to perform." Midas paces a bit, pitching his lines. "And believe me, I know all of you can perform." I can't help but let the smile slip to my lips. All in all, it sounded like a deal I could probably live with. Which makes me look at Midas in a new light, it took a smooth talker. He has a vision for something beyond the five of us, to him we are employees. That puts him on the opposite side of the ring, and to a wrestler, there's only one side ever truly respected. "What kinda bonus' we talkin about?" Lupus crosses his arms reluctantly. "Five thousand dollars a month to the top rated Anarch." Midas swaggers. He had Lupus at five. "Five g's?" Lupus confirms. "Man, we worth so much you can drop five g's a month, on top a' what you already payin me?" Speculation. "This is big bussiness these days boys, and girl. The talented get rich and the talentess aren't Anarchs." Good line. "I gotta go." Trey says suddenly and climbs to his feet. The way he walks out of the room, hurried like he remembered he's late for something. "I think we're done here anyway." Midas compensates, settling his hat on his head. He looks from the EVL twins to myself, always that plastic smile in place. "Good luck lady and gentlemen. May the best Anarch win?" Heh, he's a sarcastic little fuck too. He leaves, and Eva stays. It was going to happen, inevitably we would be alone, the three of us. These two were the catalyst recruits of my demise. I should be angry, I could take vengeance, but... screw it. "So what is all this Johnny?" She asks, waving a hand at the mask. "Who are you fooling?" "The name's Marauder." I stand, taller than her. "And I have an Infinite Gauntlet match to train for." "I hope you perform better than you did at Dangerous Games." She says contritely. Pushing buttons. "I never did congratulate you on your performance, you went down on alot of big, tough guys. Should be proud." "You're not going to win!" She calls as I'm walking out the door. "Lestatt's going to be there too." For this I pause. "Good." I lie. "What does winning mean if I don't beat the best?"
