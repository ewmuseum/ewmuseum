From Here to Back Again: Ghosts of the Past

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 It has been a long time since I faced the injustices of a world that didn’t understand the person that stood before them. A long trek since I had last let my mouth salivate on greatness and placed myself amongst the angels. In the time that PCW fell, my wings were clipped and removed. My soul was tainted with sin and my body ravaged with wounds. Long ago people looked upon me as the future, the savor to a business and a company that seemed to be on its last leg. It was a task that I was more then pleased to be a part of, to help with. In the end though; success fell through my fingers…I missed greatness by the smallest fraction of a whole and my only friend was failure. People looked upon me differently; I was no longer the golden boy of PCW. I was no longer the great hope, I was another washed up nobody. I should have just died and given-up, but something inside of me fought. Something inside of me found an escape. It released me from the fear. Now I’m scared again. The night sky was painted with thousands of stars, each one glowing brightly amongst the black canvas that imprinted them. At the head of the army the moon rested as the general, the light source that took his command post each and every night when the admiral gave him the nod. A pale moon was pulling point tonight. Casting its silver hue down onto the simplistic street, giving the buildings a second form; shadows that danced upon the sidewalk as if trained ballet dancers were performing the steps. Like a black star in the night, he only reflected light and stood with no presence. He was hallowed and empty, donning a pair of faded blue jeans and a leather jacket pulled tightly to his chest. Matt Jackson smiled as he leaned against the dilapidated building, resting his back on a faded brick wall. Matt paid no mention to the riff-raft scurrying by him, like rats attempting to get cheese. Each one disgusted him more then the previous, with their tattered rags and unkempt hair. He stood impassively, viewing the events from a pair of somber blue eyes. The people before him disgusted him, but to revolt him and to change his style, something more meticulous would need to be done. Cars sped by his sanctuary, sounding horns and flashing blinding lights on the citizens of this hell. Hell was the only description this place could obtain, nothing more was expected from a place dominated by run down buildings. A place in which the screams of murder victims were the only chorus and gun fire completed the main verse. Off in the distance sirens would whistle and another death-giver would be sent to the final resting place. People continued to pass and cars blurred into one, providing a momentary lapse in the normal scenery. Matt had no idea the reason for his ending up at this spot, for some reason after the announcement had been made that he would be squaring off against former Global Champion Seymour Almasy and Lorenzo Guevera in the first round of the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament. We have something to worry about. The voice boomed out, reaching every corner of his thoughts. Two great competitors and you’re on the edge, look at yourself. You know we can’t beat them, it’s impossible for you to ever succeed. Matt tried to push the thoughts from his head, but they were attacking him with the force of a tank. He couldn’t explain the reason that he had come here in the instant that he had heard he was going to have to face one of the best that the PTC had to offer. Fear, could be the answer, but that was a cowardly answer, one that he didn’t thing…one that he didn’t want to believe, but the truth of the matter was that he was scared. You can’t beat them. Ever since PCW fell you’ve become a nobody. It had that smugness to it, like someone smoking a cigarette after sex. Yet your chance is right here, the one opportunity for you to get back greatness. I’ve offered you salvation and you are too blind to see it. You are willing to walk into the slaughter as if you were a blind lamb. I’m disgusted. Matt couldn’t block out the truth of the words. After PCW had folded, he had nothing. No one to turn to and no one to help him, but in that time the inner-fire within him and come and saved him. Now it was attempting to do the same. Giving him the chance of a life time, allowing him to beat the Final Fantasy, giving him the materia that he needed to do the thing that few before him had done, to place himself on a stand next to the likes of the current Global Champion Chris Storm…to no longer feign greatness, but to finally feel it, to be a part of something that he didn’t fully understand, but wanted to embrace. He paced around, glancing at his watch. The digital numbers blankly blinked at him and he blinked back. This was a choice he didn’t know if he could make. I don’t want to do this. It will hurt me and those around me. He told himself, hoping that he could convince himself it was true. Shut up! You know this is what you have to do. You know you can’t beat them without it. Matt felt the feelings in the pit of his stomach. He couldn’t know if the voice was right, but he knew deep down somewhere this was the thing he had to do. Without it he had no other hope to beat Seymour Almasy and Lorenzo Guevera. The fear of returning to real competition was worrying him; he didn’t want to once again feign greatness. He wanted to experience it in its full force, in its full being. I don’t know if I should do it. The risk is to much, it will destroy me. He screamed out in his brain. If I allow it to overrun me again it would be worse then a loss to them. It will leave me broken and alone, I’m not sure if that’s something I can live with. I can’t be the person that washes up faster then a wave on the ocean. You also don’t want to be a person who once again fails…without it you are going to fail. The voice boomed out its argument, filling Matt with self-doubt like never before. I’m trying to help you. I don’t want us to fail again. I want us to be the ones who stand at the final round, hands held high with nothing to regret. We will regret it; we are obtaining it through darkened means. Long since has Almasy stood and long since will he continue to stand. I can’t beat him. It’s impossible. Matt’s blue puddles began to tear up. He couldn’t believe this was happening. Never in all his days had he been so close to the breaking point and now here he stood. Second guessing himself, fearing Almasy. It disgusted him, but at this moment he had no other choice. To fail again would be a sin worse then suicide. He turned towards the building, watching it loom over him. Casting a shadow of mistrust upon him, he hated himself at this instant. You can beat him and you will. Once you get it, everything will be fine. It will once again be like the past and we will never fail again. People will no longer cast you in the shadow of Angelo Deville; people will cheer your name. Everything you want is in front of you. Just walk in and take it. The voice was dry and crackly now. It knew the argument was won. Matt was going to bend to it. Slowly he took a few steps towards the large wooden doors. Years had passed since he had last done this. Unhinged and broken the doors were a reminder of his breaking of wills. He had no other choice though. He had to beat Almasy. He had to do it, not for greatness, not to be out of Angelo Deville’s shadow. He had to do it for other purposes… …he had to free himself. I’m still not sure. Matt told himself. It’s the only way my dear angel. Get your wings back. The sinister argument couldn’t be disputed. Matt had lost. His will hadn’t held and he was going to do the only thing he knew how to. He had to win. Each step brought him closer into the blackness, the lightless world in which he had called home for so long. He pushed past the dusty fallen beams and entered the building. He had lost himself. ~><>…<><~ Success is the sole earthly judge of right and wrong. It was the one lingering thought on Matt Jackson’s mind as he laid in his bedroom. His eyes were fixed upon the ceiling and his mind was racing. In a matter of days everything was on the line and his only opportunity to keep himself from faded into the black waters was to beat Almasy and Lorenzo Guevera and stay afloat. Yet his soul was blemished and nervous. Matt hadn’t encountered real competition since his time in PCW. He had continued to wrestle, but real competition had not shown itself. He wrestled to the point in which he knew when he was going to win and match and when he was going to lose a match, the entire process didn’t please him, but it was the only way he could get by, but now he finally had the two things he craved the most. Competition and it. He continued to let his eyes hang on the powder white ceiling. His bedroom wasn’t extravagant, but it was well off. Blue carpet on the floor and white walls, with pictures of better times hanging from them. In the centre his bed rested and to the right of it a small oak nightstand sat, with various trinkets on it. The most notable was the digital alarm clock that was blinking the time in hues of red. Matt knew it was there, because he constantly looked over to it, knowing that he never had enough time. Days remained until he kicked off the cruiserweight tournament. He was excited originally, everything felt as if it would get back to normal. Now he wanted it all to disappear. He rolled over on his side and let his gaze rest on the clock. Seconds ticked off into minutes and it finally became clear to him that he had to do it. He had to enter into the pact with the devil. It was the only way he could overcome the great odds stacked against him. Not just to beat Almasy and Guevera, but he had to do it to outlast all the competition. The world seemed to come to a pause for him as he reached under his bed and produced a small box. Hands were shaking and tears were rolling out of his eyes. He set the box in front of him and rubbed the sweat from his brow. Are you sure we should do this? He asked himself, already knowing the answer to his question. Of course we should you fool. Matt opened the box, listening to the creek of the hinges as there rest was disturbed. His body was trembling and his heart felt like it was going to leap from his chest. He hadn’t seen the contents in the box forever: a needle, a rubber strap, and a spoon. He carefully removed the items from the box and set them down on the nightstand gently and took a deep breath, allowing the air to fill his lungs. Deep down in his pocket it sat and that was where his hand was darting. Once everything was laid out in front of him Matt positioned himself on the edge of his king sized bed. He always hated his bedroom. This was the room where he slept with Haley Hilton; this was the house that they lived in. Everything in here reminded him of her and how much he loved her. Then PCW crashed and burned and she left. The painful memories brought back tears, but he pushed them to the side. There were no more excuses, he had to do this. He tied the strap around his arm and flexed his tan muscles, getting his veins to jump up and stand in full attention. It was time, and he understood fully as the flame danced under the spoon, hitting its high and low marks like a trained dancer. He placed the needle into the liquid freedom and watched it run up the syringe. Some toppled out onto the floor, be he was shaken as if he were standing outside in the cold without a coat. There was no shield to protect him any longer. The needle passed through his skin and allowed it to run into his blood stream. To take away the doubt and to fill his heart with happiness, everything was falling into place now and his mind knew it had played its part perfectly. Salvation and the chance to obtain greatness were offered and the depressed black star was ready to accept. His mind was cackling as he removed the needle from his arm and laid back on the bed. I don’t know if I can beat them. Don’t quit on me now. You can beat them and you will beat them. I’ve ensured it. Nothing can hold you back anymore Matt. In the coming weeks, we are going to stand tall, topple people who are less then us. You are once again going to let me call the shots and control everything. It’s the only way you can get greatness. The voice lectured him, it was something that hadn’t happened since his time in the PWA. But it was correct. He needed it. ~><>…<><~ Hours had passed and Matt Jackson had still not moved a muscle. He let his eyes wander around his bedroom to the pictures of him and Haley, of happier time. Awards and titles that he had won on the grandest stage of them all – PCW, and through it all none of it meant anything, because he laid alone, he heard the darkness calling him back home. To return to the ghosts of the past, but he didn’t want too. He wanted to stay above water. At least his mind had gotten off his fear of Almasy for a while. He knew deep down he couldn’t beat him without the help of his alter-ego. No one knew about it, no one had the faintest idea that it was the device that had led him to victory all those times in PCW. Now here they were once again battling for position, for so long Matt had wanted to win without it. It wasn’t possible. He knew the knife was lying next to it. The one thing that could make the nightmares stop coming. It felt so cold in his hand though. Everything hurt so much though, the memories, the fear, and the worry. He wanted to cut it out and make it all go away. Matt slowly drug the blade over his skin, drawing a little more blood with each cut. What are you dong moron. People are going to know. Matt shrugged off the voice and cut more into his chest. He wanted to explain it away, but it felt so good. He was answering the call. “Matt what are you doing?” the female voice cut out through his thoughts. He looked up at the slender blonde standing in the door. “Haley…”
