19 April 2004 So much has been going on here lately, I'm not sure I can take it anymore. After I left the asylum, I didn't think it would ever come back, but it's here. It just crept back on me like a lion ready to pounce on its prey. The anger is ready, ready to pounce at any moment. I try to contain, it but there is nothing I can do. At any second it could just explode, and destroy everything around. Nothing is going right, but soon I will make sure it does. I open my eye and I had to suddenly close it. The pain hit me like a thousand nails dropping onto my eye. The pain is almost unbearable. Luckily one eye will still open.Why am I lieing on the floor? I try to stand up, but my leg is in so much pain I fall to the bed nearby. My mouth is so dry, as well as my lips. I lick them, but some sort of coating is over them. I manage to get to one fit and limp to the bathroom in the next room. I peer into the mirro and blood is stained on my face from my nose. My forehead is badly cut and is going to need stiches. My eye is black as the night sky, and my leg is straining to keep me up. What in the hell happened to me. Why in the hell am I busted open, my eye blackend? I limp my way back into the room and take a look around. Everything is ransacked. The night stands are overturned. The comforter is lying on the floor covered in blood, in which I was lying on. The matress is flipped over onto the wall. Everything in the room is destroyed, there is nothing left. "Lyndsi," I yelled at the top of my lungs. Nothing, nothing but silence. There was no one here, I could tell it. I haven't searched the rest of the house, but I could sense it. Where is everyone? I make my way out of the room and into the living area. Everything is destroyed like my bedroom. "Lyndsi!" I yell again. I knew she wasn't here, but I had to yell for her. I quickly ran to my daughter's room. It was like the rest of the house. Who was here? "Georgia!" I yelled, searching for my daughter. I look on the floor and notice my daughter's stuffed teddy bear. I kneel down, in pain, and pick the stuffed animal up. I stare at it, and suddenly a telephone rings. I quickly drop the animal and make it to the nearest phone as quick as possible. I look at the caller id, and it reads, "Unknown Caller." "Hello," I answer hesitantly. The other end of the phone is silent, the caller says nothing. I begin to hang up, but I hear a raspy mans voice. The man's voice chills my body as I know there is something wrong. "Finally, you are awake. You've been lying there for two days," the voice says. How did he know I was laying there? I was out for two days, I said to myself. "Who in the hell is this?" I said getting angered. "It doesn't matter who I am, but I know what does matter. We will get to that in a second. I am sure you want to know how I know how I knew you were lieing there for two days," the man says. "Yeah, I sure as hell do. What do you want?" I said getting frustrated with his game playing. "See there are cameras implanted all over your house. I will know your every move, so your not getting away. That's right, you can't leave. The house is armed with explosives and there is no way to get out," the man says with a small chuckle. "You are full of shit, this must be some sort of a joke," I said to him. "A joke? If this was a joke would your eye, forehead, and leg be in such pain? Would your house be this destroyed? What kind of sick as friends do you have that would destroy your home and you just for a few laughs? That's right, they wouldn't, this is no joke. If you don't believe me, open your front door," the man says. Please let this be a dream. What demented person would do something like this. Why would they choose me. What does he want? "Are you there? You've been sitting there for a while. It's OK, you will not be harmed as long as you comply with my every demand," the man says. "What do you want from me?" I asked starting to calm down, I have no other choice. "It's quite simple and just to make sure you comply I have your wife, Lyndsi, and your daughter, Georgia. In a moment you will be able to speak to both of them just to restasure you they are, indeed alive. What I want from you is of course the thing that makes the world go round, money," the man says. "If you harm my family..." I tried to say, before being cut-off. "You'll what, kill me? You will do nothing, but what I want. I know how much you love your wife and daughter. You aren't going to do anything," he said in confidence. He was right, there was nothing I could do. If he has my family and my house rigged with explosives and cameras I have nothing to, but what he wants. "See you will give me one million dollars, and I know you have it, so don't even try that. Once I receive the money the explosives will be disarmed and you will get your family back. Don't even bother calling the cops, because if you do I will know and your family will die," the man says. "How do you expect me to give you money? How in the hell am I going to leave? I have to go to the bank and get it," I said. "No, no you don't. See I am two steps ahead of you. You will go to your kitchen, and on the table is a laptop computer. I have it all set-up and all you have to do is type in your account number, and send the money to my account. Everything you need is on the computer. You have one hour for me to receive my money, and if not you will be visiting your family at the morge," the man says more serious than ever. That son of a bitch, if I ever catch him he will die. That's it, my anger is unleashed. The man will die, and he will not get shit. I can beat this man. "Let me talk to my wife," I said. There is a long silence on the phone and suddenly a women's voice is heard. "Honey," she says in tears. "Lyndsi!" I said yelling. "Please, just give him the money," she says quickly. "It's OK. I will get you two out of this. I love you," I said. "I love you too, just give him the money. He is going to kill us, he has two men here and they all have guns," she said. "Where are you?" I said. "We are..." she tries to say. Suddenly a slap is heard of the phone and Lyndsi's tears can be heard getting worse as she screams in pain. "He won't let me tell you," she said. OK, he just messed. That is one thing you don't do and that is hit my wife. I have never laid a hand on her, and no one else will either. This man is dieing. "I'll fucking kill you!," I yelled into the phone. The man thing snatches the phone from Lyndsi and puts it to his ear. "You won't do shit! I'll kill your wife, so don't threating me. I have your family not even five feet from me, so you are going to do what? Nothing, that's right. Now you have one fucking hour to give me my money!" the man says. The phone thing hangs up and I hang the receiver up. My body begins to tremble and my mind starts to run violently. Thoughts cross my mind, and anger. I punch the wall as hard as I can. The sheetrock crumbles beneath my firt. I pull it out and it is covered in white debris, and slowly it begins to bleed. "Son of a bitch!", I yell. I have to think. I have to do something. I am going to get my family back and this mother fucker is going down. ---- 21 April 2004 More than 20 minutes have past, but it seems like so much longer. No, that would be to easy I'm taking this guy down myself. I start up the car and go flying down the highway. The place isn't far from here, but it seems so damn far away. I guess since I am anticipating it, it is taking forever. Finally, I am here, it's time. I get out of my car and I make my way to the window, slowly. I peer through the corner of the window, and I see my wife and daughter tied to chair with duct tape around their mouth. "Good they are still alive," I said to outloud to myself. I scoot my head over just a little more and I can see a man standing beside them, and another man looking on his laptop computer. The man from the computer gets up and to reveal, it's my father.
