Sorrow vs AWS Man vs John Zero

13 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Quote of the Day (From the Upright Citizen’s Brigade) “Daddy, why does it make you cry every night when you put the barrel of the gun in your mouth?” (The scene opens up inside of the funkrageous living room of AWS Man (also known as Bill). What exactly makes it so funkrageous, I hear you asking? Well, sir, I’ll tell you. It’s not the furniture placement, though: a modest brown couch in the middle of the room, with a tope easy chair to its left and a burgundy leather recliner to the right, all facing a television set; the front door is over on the right, the kitchen entrance to the left, and the staircase is at the back of the room and to the right. The source of the funkrageousness also isn’t the walls being painted in stripes of neon green, mud brown, and something best describe as “blorange.” And it’s not the paintings on the wall, either, of such diverse subject matter as a red circle and X over a map of the state of South Carolina, and a giant flaming bag of poo with legs stomping on a group of teenagers. And also- you know, I lied. The room isn’t funkrageous at all. I just needed a hook, damn it! But now that you’re here, I might as well inform you that the room, which is actually pretty tidy and surprisingly devoid of mysterious odors, currently has two occupants. One is Brian Blade, a dour-looking man in his mid-30’s with a goatee, who is sitting in the recliner and watching the news. The other man, sitting in the easy chair, is the Nude; as his name might suggest, he is devoid of clothes. And also looking at porn magazines. So it’s probably best not to look at him too long. Suddenly, the door bursts in and in walks the star of this already downhill-headed promo, AWS Man (also known as Bill). For those who have not seen the Insane Commish before, a brief description is in order. He is wearing his constant attire of a black-and-white jumpsuit, a purple glove covering his right hand, steel-tipped army boots, and a paintball mask covering his face. The former WMW Great Lakes champion has short brown hair and a slight build, standing at 6’1”. He really doesn’t look too much like a wrestler … or a person you'd find living outside of an asylum, for that matter. AWS Man (also known as Bill) is holding his trusty spatula companion Pen in his right hand, and a bag stuffed to the brim with porn in the other.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hi, honey, I’m hoooooo-freakin’-oooome! Blade : Hey, what are you doing back here? I thought WMW Survivor wasn’t over for- AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hold on a freakin’ second, Blade, I haven’t finished my freakin’ entrance yet! (The Insane Commish sets down his bag of porn, which the Nude immediately leaps out of his chair and pounces on – another thing that’s probably good not to imagine, dear reader, is a naked guy pouncing … on anything – while AWS Man (also known as Bill) digs in his pocket and finally pulls out a device reminiscent of a tape recorder. He searches its surface for a moment, then pushes a button. The sound of polite applause and cheering feels the room. After a few seconds he switches it off.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : OK, you were freakin’ saying? (Blade stares at the Insane Commish for a moment and blinks before speaking.) Blade : Uh … what was that? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : That’s not what you freakin’ said before, I distinctly remember hearing something about Survivor … and leprechauns … and I think maybe Wayne freakin’ Gretzky… Blade : No, what is that tape recorder thing? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh, this little thing? Why, it’s my own freakin’ personal laughtrack. I figured it was about time a big wrestling star like me got one. Now it’ll be like I’m on TV all the freakin’ time! Blade : Well, since there’s a camera permanently set up in our house, you pretty much are on TV all the time! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : See, all the freakin’ more reason I needed one! How will people know I’m on TV if I don’t have appropriately timed crowd reaction freakin’ accompanying my every move?! (The Insane Commish presses the button for canned laughter. After the laughter dies down, he crosses his arms in smug satisfaction.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Point taken. Blade : AWS Man, the person you’re arguing with is supposed to say that; you can’t declare that I’ve taken the point! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh … Well, that’s a stupid freakin’ rule. How else am I ever going to convince anybody of something? Certainly not with my freakin’ arguments … Those are crap! (AWS Man (also known as Bill) once again plays the laughter as he takes a seat on the couch.) Blade : Great, you’ve been back a whole two minutes and you’re already driving me nuts. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, this place must’ve been pretty freakin’ boring around here without me. Nude : (Retaking his seat with a lap full of new porn) Not really. Blade and I joined a book club. That’s been fun … well, for me at least. Blade’s been pretty whiny about it, like usual. Blade : Hey, you periodically chucking copies of Jugs at me and telling me to have my book report done by noon tomorrow isn’t a book club! Nude : Sure it is. I have to admit, though, your reports were usually pretty well-written, and sometimes quite insightful. Blade : (Blushing) Well … OK, so there wasn’t a lot to do. What about you? How’d you like your time in the mall? (In case you’re wondering, AWS Man (also known as Bill) has just returned from several months playing Wrestling Midwest’s own version of Survivor, set in the WMW Mall in Cleveland. The game began with fifteen contestants, divided into three tribes and each located in a different store. During the course of the game the Insane Commish moved from Victoria’s Secret, to furniture store Ethan Allen, to the bookstore Barnes & Noble after the merge, only allowed to leave for WMW shows and Survivor-related activities. Somehow, despite having an intelligence level somewhere between the WWE’s Eugene and a piece of tuna (not the live kind), AWS Man (also known as Bill) managed to last all the way to the final two, along with rival turned somewhat alliance partner, and current WMW Great Lakes champion, Justin Evers. With the game over except for the final votes by the jury members, Evers and the Insane Commish were allowed to finally leave the mall.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : It was O-freakin’-K. Pen and Harold Hash were there to keep me company. Too bad poopiepants Justin Conspicuous won the final Immunity, or I could’ve taken Hash with me to the final. But even Justin turned out to not be such a freakin’ bad guy. So it wasn’t bad … except for the extreme porn shortage. Would you believe that the first two stores I lived in had no porn supplies of any sort?! (The Nude’s eyes widen in unfathomable horror.) Nude : No! (AWS Man (also known as Bill) also hits the “GASP!” button on the laughtrack, just for good measure.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : It’s freakin’ true! And even that bookstore, Shedds and Knobby, had very limited refined reading. Nude : So what did you do?! How did you train for matches … or relax from matches … or do any match related activity … or do non-match related activities?! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : It wasn’t freakin’ easy. Pen and I had to improvise. In Dancing Lunatic’s Mystery, we were able to construct elaborate freakin’ scenes using the mannequins. We had to use our imagination … but it was somewhat effective. Plus, Pen found himself a girlfriend there. He had a little bit of inanimate love with Mandy the Mannequin. Nude : Way to go, Pen! Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yes, freakin’ tragically it ended when we had to move on, and taking her with us would apparently have been theft of property or some phony luncheon meat charge like that. Freakin’ slavedrivers … Anyway, Ethan Embry was even worse. There we had to resort to … I think it’s called “memory.” Anyway, it was hard to do, and it hurt my brain a freakin’ lot after a while. Nude : Sounds rough. Well, good thing you’re back here now, where the porn abounds and … well, that’s pretty much all that happens here. But still, lots of porn! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : I know, I hope that stuff I picked up on the freakin’ way here is good. I’m going to need a lot of training videos to get ready for my match at Pus Plant. It’s about time the GLC comes back to me! Blade : Not to mention your chance at another Global shot right after that. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, not to freakin’ mention- AWHAA?! Blade : Uh … yeah, didn’t they tell you that in Survivor? You’ve got a triple threat match coming up the week after Blood Factory. The winner gets a Global title shot in August. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Is that the freakin’ belt I got a shot at before, but I was screwed out of it by the Omelettish? Blade : I think you mean Amish … and no, they had nothing to do with the match. Although you probably did lose it because you spent all the time you should’ve been training remembering some largely fabricated story about you and Pen hiding out in Amish country … God, you’re an idiot. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well … takes one to freakin’ be one! Wait, I guess that makes me one. Freak it! (The Insane Commish plays the canned laughter again.) Blade : I’m about to break that damn thing! (After a moment’s pause, AWS Man (also known as Bill) hits the “Uh-ohhhhhhh!” button on the laughtrack.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : But … but Blade, I just freakin’ wanted to impress you, since you do so much to help me out. (He hits another button, and the sound of an anvil dropping and then clunking a coyote or pussycat or duck or sometimes Martian on the head plays.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Whoops, that’s not the freakin’ one. Don’t they have the “Awwww-freakin’-www” sound on here? Nude : How did you even pronounce that? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : …I don’t know. Telepathy? (The Insane Commish hits a button for the impressed “Ooooohhhhh!” sound.) Blade : Enough! (The Insane Commish’s long-time friend/advisor/virtually babysitter jumps up and snatches the laughtrack out of AWS Man (also known as Bill)’s lap, throwing it on the ground and stomping it in a frenzy until it’s nothing more than a small pile of mangled machinery making a gurgling noise that sounds like the elusive “Awwwww” track. With obvious effort Blade ceases to kick the broken machine and retakes his seat, trying his best to look calm.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Fine … but I’ll have you know you owe Pen freakin’ fifty bucks for that thing. (Blade scoffs. You can tell from the distinct scoffing noise he makes.) Blade : Scoff! I’m not paying your spatula fifty bucks … I don’t know if I even have fifty bucks. Being your voice of reason doesn't pay a whole lot, you know. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well, I hope for your freakin’ sake you have the cash. Pen’s not new to the loan shark business and, well … he’s never liked you too much to begin with. If I were you, I’d get him the money … or find a way to get it to freakin’ him. (The Insane Commish slaps Pen against his palm several times in a menacing gesture.) Blade : (Nervous laugh) Um … right. So, don’t you at least want to know who’s in your PTC match? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Mmmmm … not freakin’ really. I’m not fighting a double of myself, am I? Blade : No, just like you never will, so stop asking! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Just making sure. I shouldn’t freakin’ have a problem, then. Promo done. Blade : (Sigh) Look, I know you think that it’s absolutely impossible for you to ever lose to any opponent who naturally must be worse than you … even though it’s happened before … quite a bit, actually … but you might have to say a bit more than that to convince the fans of PTC, especially the ones not familiar with me. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well who cares what they freakin’ think? If they don’t know about me, they must be such huge losers that if I went to a loser store to buy some losers and saw them, I’d go, “Eww, take them freakin’ back, they’re too loserish.” The freakin’ losers. Blade : …Yeah, be that as it may, I’m still telling you who you’re fighting, and you’ll damn well pay attention! Your opponents are Angelus Sorrow and John Zero. Zero is actually from GW. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : He works for the President?! Man, maybe he can freakin’ get somebody down here to fix our air conditioner. I don’t pay freakin’ taxes on years ending with "eleventy" so those fat cat bureaucrats can sit lolly-gagging around up in Washingtonia while my air conditioner goes haywire! Blade : One, I said GW, not George W. And two, our air conditioner isn’t even broken, so I don’t know what you’re babbling about. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Not broken, huh? OK … the freakin’ fat cats get lucky … this time. But our house will need painting soon, and old Curious George isn’t freakin’ going to get off so easy that time! Blade : Yeah, good luck with getting the federal government to do your household chores. But no, I’m talking about the GW that WMW is feuding with … the one that we’ve got a big interfed PPV against at the end of the month. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh yeah, that one. Locomotive works there, freakin’ right? He seems like a swell guy. I think he’s finally starting to come around to considering jumping to WMW. Blade : Oh really? What was your first clue, when Train threw you into the wall a couple Burnouts ago? Or how about when he smashed Matrix’s jeep after you guys jumped him at the last GW show? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well, you know you always hurt the freakin’ ones you love. And he wants to hurt us bad. Blade : Can’t argue with that. But yes, this is that same GW, and this match is going to be taking place a matter of days before Crossfire. I don’t believe that Zero is booked for a match against a WMW wrestler, but he’s still the enemy. And by defeating him, you’ll be sending a message to his entire federation. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah: “We Want Gore in 2004!” I saw that on a freakin’ bumper sticker the other day; and I agree, movies today don’t have enough gore in them. Or full-frontal nudity. Or Emilio Estevez. Blade : Ummm, no, I was thinking more along the lines of, “WMW’s best can beat GW’s best any day of the week.” AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Uh uh, not if they’re Shomer Shabbas. They don’t wrestle on freakin’ Sundays. Blade : …You don’t even know what that means. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Sure I do … Pen, what does it freakin’ mean? (Pen, the Insane Commish’s spatula, “speaks up” for the first time this promo.) Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : You don’t say? Wow, I thought it had something to do with freakin’ the Smurfs… Blade : OK, so if we can cut the gibberish short and focus on the match: your other opponent, Angelus Sorrow, has apparently left his fed LOW … Or maybe it’s a her. I really can’t tell with that name. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : I think it freakin’ sounds like a hab. Blade : Na, I’m pretty sure it’s an actual gender, I just don’t know which one. Anyway, whatever Sorrow is, I’m not too sure if it’s even in the match anymore. I think Sorrow has to find another PTC promotion to join to still be in the match, which I don’t think he or she has done yet. So you might wind up just having a singles match against Zero. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hey, I really don’t care if Angel Soft is in the freakin’ match or not. And Zip doesn’t scare me too much freakin’ either. From my wealth of information and research on him based on such aspects as his stupid name and the jobber fed that he wrestles for, I can tell he stands less of a chance against freakin’ me than those ninjas that jumped me last year and tried to break into my house. Blade : Are you talking about those little kids that you attacked on Halloween when they came here trick-or-treating? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Heh heh, freakin’ yeah. They’ll think twice about ringing my doorbell and challenging me with their freakin’ battlecry of death and threats of making me smell their feet again. Blade : Yes, I’m sure they will, if and when they ever wake up from their comas. Something tells me that Zero might be just a bit more capable than eight-year-olds in ninja costumes, though. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : You freakin’ think so? Well, I don’t know how likely that is … but even if so, it’s no problem. There’s no way any integer less than thirteen billion could take me down, let alone Nada. Even a negative number would be more intimidating, because they’ve got that little bar they can jab freakin’ you with. But what’s Zilch gonna do? Roll on me? Blade : I don’t think he’s actually- AWS Man (also known as Bill) : And I don’t pay you to “actually.” In fact … I don’t think at freakin’ all. Heh heh, what a classic joke. Blade : …You know, forget it. I’m sure you’ll have an easy time kicking the big zero around. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : I’m sure I will freakin’ too. Now, I can’t believe I’ve been sitting here a full ten minutes and there’s no freakin’ porn playing. What does a porn-obsessed wrestler in an incredibly spiffy outfit have to do to get some freakin’ service around here?! Nude : (Jumping up) Sorry about that! You want to see Missionary Position: Impossible or NYDP Blue? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : How about both? … AT THE SAME TIME! Nude : Nope, still not possible. Though we can try again tomorrow, like always. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Fine, then freakin’ put in Guess Who Came At Dinner. And now, looks like I’ll be busy training for the next couple hours. So to all out there watching me through the soul-stealing box, SOUTH CAROLINA FREAKIN’ SUCKS! (The scene fades out to AWS Man (also known as Bill) browsing through more hilariously titled porn found at http://xxx.aquasmack.com/. I couldn’t take credit for those. :D)
