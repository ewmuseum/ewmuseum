The Truth behind my Lies…

23 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-23 The last year of my life has been nothing but a horror story. Similar to something you might read in a book. But recently the tides are turning; slowly but they are still turning. As I sit here pondering my life, I wonder what I have actually accomplished. Nothing, there is not one thing in this world I can claim as an achievement. Hell I had the Alias Championship in what is known as the best federation in wrestling today, but that doesn’t mean anything. Trophies, plaques and awards mean nothing when you have no self respect, you have no self achievement, and you are empty inside. That is how I feel. My life has been a waste. It wasn’t until I met Melissa. The woman that made life wonderful for so many years and who has made my life a living hell the last year. She controlled me, acting as a puppet master with me being the puppet held on by stings. Happiness was easily glowing on the outside, but deep down inside we both were burning, fiery passion that made us love each other but at the same time have a deep… deep hatred. Life is filled with lessons. At least that is what I am told, over and over again. Take each lesson and make yourself a better person. Grow… Expand…. Become a better person… All these things have been told to me time and time again. All of these are different ways some one or another has told me how to cope with the different things in my life. What could I possibly take from the love of my life stabbing in my back? Resentment? Anger? Jealousy? Of all the things I could take from that whore, none were positive. None are what I want to remember the girl who made me happy for so long. She brought happiness to my life and I took it with a grain of salt. The day it was revoked from me, the day she placed me in jail… that is when it all changed. That is the day I lost respect for not only her, but myself as well. I however have changed, I have moved on. I have become a better person from that experience and look to carry it forth in all my future endeavors. But there still is one thing in my past that I can not surrender. There is still that one thing that lingers over my name… over my head… over my complete existence. Domestic Abuse charges? No…. GTT5? Yeah that kind of still pisses me off… but I got over that. I still have one thing to say, Fuck you Tony Stecchino. The time I spent in PRIME? Nope, not at all. Drug Addiction? I embrace that and consider it a blessing… so thank you Facey. GTT4? There we go. The real truth to my depression, the real truth to the way I have acted for years to come. I entered the world of professional wrestling, a starry eyed kid with hopes and dreams of making it big. Never once did I make it big. Hell! Look at my career to date. I have very few victories, I have very few accomplishments, I have very few of anything… of course that doesn’t count losses. But that’s for another story at another time. So I was part of the biggest federation to date since PCW. Yeah I had a hell of a time there, ups and downs. You know all the normal shit, part of the biggest stable in their history, Imperium. Became the Alias Champion, am one of the few that have ever beaten Brandon Youngblood. Yes, I have some accomplishments, but no matter what I had accomplished in PRIME, the legacy I created there was better. Goof… Idiot… Useless… I think it was Ignatius Liseaux that asked me why I even tried wrestling with today’s biggest and brightest stars. I think I simply responded with a fuck you have a nice day. But my hatred is not of PRIME the federation, my hatred is not of my co workers of PRIME. My hatred is for myself. I said I lied to the world, I said I never was a member of the Illuminati. That in itself was a lie to the world. I had taken the sacred vow only months before with my father, I became a full fledge member and then sprung it on my father, my true intentions in life. He would never have approved, he would have only be disgusted with the idea. I however continued onward, I took whatever he and fellow members had taught and used it in everyday life like they want, like they expect. But for him that wasn’t enough, for him I should not be showing it all to the world of wrestling. I should be like him a proud member of the Illuminati, but proud enough not to reveal it to the world. Why? Because I am supposed to be a better member of society, I am supposed to be something greater then the rest of you common folk. Well I gave into temptation, but I figured with my "gimmick" as the wrestling world called it I figured I would be able to make him proud and make myself happy. Well the only problem with that is the booking staff of PRIME lead by Motoki never understood it. My so called gimmick was actually my lifestyle. They need to come up with something else. At this point I was appalled by the Illuminati, had they not only not embraced me like I had expected but they actually lead to the death of my father. That’s right PRIME came to me with this depression character, they had this big idea to try and re-make WCW’s Raven. Well I went for it; they figured he will go for anything he isn’t succeeding with this bullshit gimmick. Once again PRIME was wrong; once again the gimmick they threw me into was my life story. The Illuminati society killed my father; I didn’t want to ever be associated with them ever again. PRIME had given me the opportunity to play it off as nothing had happened; it was an angle… just a work. I went with it, never once actually questioning my father’s death knowing that the Illuminati are much stronger then I could ever be. Their vows… their heritage… and their history are kept for the select few to know about it. I however was not one of those select few. A chance of freedom from the group was the one thing I wanted, and the one thing they wanted was me not pushing on the thought of them killing my father off. Well the time has come… I want to know what happened and it is time for the research to be done… the killer to be revealed. Depression was nothing… I over came it. Drug Addiction….Overcame it My Father’s Death… Never overcame it’s time for my check list to be complete. August 5,2004 All I can do is sit here. The words that this unknown man to me had just uttered left me in complete shock. The hurt… The anguish… The regret… That I’m currently feeling, out weight any other emotion I could fathom having right now. But I had to take it like a man; I had to be a trooper. Ten minutes ago I was sitting in my chair over looking different wrestling shows on TiVo when I received the news. Someone came knocking on my door. I wasn’t expecting any guest so I was hesitant to getting up but paused the most recent showing of WWF and made my way to the door. I would have looked thru the peep hole but I’m from the country and out here we don’t need those things. I slowly open the door and I’m face to face with a six foot tall state trooper with his hat on his head making him look closer to six foot six. "Good day officer." I must have had a quiver in my voice, I mean I don’t like cops and they don’t like me but there surely is no reason for them to be showing up at my doorstep. "I wish I could say the same in return sir, may I step in?" Not even waiting for a response the office makes his way into my home. "But of course officer. Would you like something to drink?" I was trying to be as nice as I could be just incase I had done something wrong. You know state troopers they can be ball busters if they want to be. "No thank you, I presume you are Mr. Peter Vetra." The officer looked down at his note pad when he read the name, he actually pronounced it correctly something of a feat many others were not capable of doing. "Yes sir, did I do something wrong?" "No sir, not at all but I however have some unfortunate news. If you could would you sit down?" The officer pointed over to the kitchen table. I took the seat as he recommended and sat there nervously awaiting this news he was going to hand out to me. I knew nothing good could come from this. I knew there was no chance he was about to talk to me about a speeding ticket. This was something serious. "Son my name is Sergeant Guy, and I have been sent to inform you of the unfortunate passing…. As he said that my heart started racing… who has passed away? Who was so serious that the Massachusetts State Police showed up at my door to let me know? Even worse how did they die, usually you would just receive a phone call. of your father. Mr. William Vetra." The office stopped for a minute to wait and see how the wrestler would take such news. I sat there quiet and glum for a moment, trying to take in all the information that was just released to me. I wanted to say something but I was unsure of what to say. I looked up and the officer could tell by the look in my eyes that I was grasping for some sort of closure. When? How?? But most importantly why? Officer Guy looked down at his note pad that he had out in front of him. He looked over a couple of notes and looked up at me who was in complete disarray from the news. "Peter we have very little facts to the case as of right now." Case? What did he mean by case? Was my father murdered? All these thoughts were running thru my mind over and over again. My brain was almost a revolving door that wouldn’t stop letting people in or out of the building. I built up the confidence to speak to the officer. "What do you mean? Are you trying to say my father was murdered?" I could sense hatred, resentment, and anger all coming to me as a big melting pot of emotions as I asked the question, I guess the only thing I could really do was a wait his answer. Officer Guy made it noticeable that he was struggling to decide which words he should use to explain this to me. I gave him a stern glare almost to signify to just tell me already the anticipation was more then I ever needed. "Well Peter, the truth was your father was murdered…." My head hit the kitchen table as he said that, my jaw probably would have hit the floor if it wasn’t for my forehead slamming against the oak table. So many thoughts racing around made me almost pass out. I held on though, it sure was tough. I looked up at the officer after picking my head up off the table and with a tear in my eye I was able to utter, "Do you know who did it?" Officer Guy had some compassion in his heart but he still had a job to do. He looked at the younger man, "Sorry…not yet. However we are on the trail and we would like to ask you some questions when you get a chance and are a little more composed." With that Officer Guy stood up from the kitchen table. All I could do was look up at him dumbfound not sure as if this was fact or fiction. It would take time to take it all in and it would be hours before I even fathomed the idea of talking to the cops. "Thank you for your time Mr. Vetra. I will leave my business card on the table and you can call me and we will talk when you are ready." I looked at the man knowing he hated this part of his job and shook his hand. Grateful he was able to give me the information in a professional manner still hurt and anguished but that was not his fault. "Will do sir, I will probably be in contact in a couple of hours once I get showered and digest this a little more." "Understandable, you have my number." With that Officer Guy let himself out. I sat there still in complete shock the world was crashing around me. My mentor was dead… My idol… But most importantly…. My Father…. Many suspects ran through my head and I knew that was the question the cops were going to ask me about. I however needed to do some research on my own; I need to do a little investigating before I revealed names or identities to the authorities. Of course all trails lead me to my father’s closets friends but also biggest foes. The Illumaniti. The group that made him who he was also knew his threat. They had wanted him out for the longest time, he was too big for the group while they were growing ever so slowly he was gaining more and more knowledge about the secret cult. This was the real reason I left the group. Not because I was a fraud but my fathers had warned me about the immediate danger that might be coming from me joining and making the group known world wide. If my father had only heeded his own warning he might still be here today. I need to find a resolution; I needed to find out who was to blame. With that those thoughts running through my head I knew I had no time to waste they might be making their way out of town right now. I ran up stairs took a quick shower and got dressed. I know I had told the cops I would call them but I threw his business card in my pocket and started on my own search.
