The Vermillion Facade (Part 3: Vindication)

23 March 2005

Date: 2005-03-23 Pain. The most powerful four-letter word in the English language. The things that caused pain, the things pain causes, and the hold pain can hold on a subconscious. Much would rather escape into the unknown of death than deal with the threat of pain. The was all the elderly man could think of as he took his breaths staring up at the ceiling. How much everything could be changed if he had played his cards differently, waiting for death to come and take him away as a crimson river began to flow into his eyes. His eyes began to feel as if they were being pushed back into his head by infinite toothpicks. The pain in which he was enduring almost drowned out the sound of a man approaching... Click... Click... Click... He appeared in pinkish blur... his features were distorted by pain and agony. The elderly man tried his best to turn that blur into something he could recognize... but that part of his brain was already gone, and the rest was fading fast. It was unfortunate he could still feel pain, and even more unfortunate that he could recognize the voice as it spoke to him... “Where is Dietrich?” The voice asked, ringing like the sound of death in the elderly man’s ears. He tried to move... but it was pointless, only seconds separated him from the other side. The man laid, hoping there wasn’t anything on the other side... because surely if there was Lucifer would claim his soul and make him a slave. But then again... that’s how he’d felt for years. “Where the fuck is Dietrich, Jacques?!” The interrogation was stated with a snarl. The sound of his voice could make even the mightiest men quiver... of course, this close to death nothing really mattered. He moved his lips, quietly replying with the little strength he had left. “US... Arms... Downtown. Christian... he has... suite... 12th Floor.” Jacques replied before his sight faded away into nothingness. He could no longer see, but he imagined the intensity of Christian Zenith’s face while the sound of reloading echoed off the bare walls. “If you see Jesse Jamester in hell... ask him how I did it. I’m sure it’ll be a good story.” BANG! Bum Bump... Bum... Bump... Bum... ... I can feel it Coming in the air tonight Oh Lord I’ve been waiting for this moment For all my life Oh Lord Date: December 20, 1990 Time: 22:46 Place: Zenith Corporation Building Location: Chicago, Illinois Darkness shrouded the streets of Chicago, Illinois as the half moon hung high over the metropolis covered in a blanket of white. The lights of the city were coming to a close, the only people walking the streets were the homeless and the dealers. It was a dangerous time of night for those outside, but the devious were not just contained to the outdoors. Inside a darkened skyscraper, foul play was still afoot. This was not the lifestyle for a 7 year old little boy... but it always seemed to come to him, even as a small child. Wearing a blue Chicago Cubs sweatshirt and a pair of stonewashed blue jeans, the child scampered around the confines of the tower. It was past his bedtime, but his sleeping pattern was delayed by several cans of Mountain Dew, that he had snuck out of the refrigerator in his father’s office while he was taking conference calls, similar to the one he was with now. But at night in the Zenith Corporation stronghold, there was always a mission around every darkened corner for young Christian. Christian was Batman and the dimly lit corridors were his Gothem as he chased down the bad guys and beat them into justice. As he certainly stalked The Joker, a light shone from behind a door and stuck the far hallway wall. A giant silhouette stood before Christian, his curious nature didn’t need convincing that the matter needed a thorough investigation. Little Christian tip toed his way down the hallway, as hushed voices from the inside became cleared as he drew near. “20 mil, cash and they’re all yours.” One of the voices said as Christian came to a halt outside the doorway. He could hear someone inside pick something up what sounded like a briefcase. Not being able to take it any longer, Christian pressed up against the door and cracked it open... eyeing the dealings going on within. Christian spotted ‘uncle’ Nikolaus Dietrich talking to a middle-aged African-American man. With a click, the African-American man opened up the briefcase... filled to the brim with stacks of green bills. “It’s all here.” The dark man replied before turning it towards Dietrich, who quickly picked up one of the stacks and ruffled through it with a smile. Dietrich closed the briefcase quickly before latching it shut once again. Nikolaus pushed 2 briefcases across the floor with his foot, stopping in front of man across from him. The African-American opened one of the up to reveal an AK47 with a smile. Christian looked in awe as it seemed like something straight out of a Batman comic. He role-played to himself as he watched the deal go down... “It’s not nice to play with guns Joker!” Whoops! Christian jumped in fear realizing what he had said aloud. Dietrich’s eyes widened as he jumped out of his chair and pulled out a Magnum, infuriated. Christian’s heart raced as he turned around and ran down the hallway while Nikolaus shoved the door open. The much larger and faster Dietrich began to take chase to the young Christian, who obviously witnessed and understood the entire illegal deal. If this were to get back to Christian’s father... his career would be over and his life ruined. Dietrich’s eyes burned with rage as he began to catch up to the little Christian, Magnum firmly in front of him ready to shoot to save his career. Suddenly, the 7 year old Zenith hit the ground, tumbling off balance down the hallway. Christian held his twisted ankle in pain as he rocked back and forth in the carpeted hallway. Dietrich had a maniacal smile across his face while he approached. Zenith’s eyes grew wide as he looked down the barrel of the Magnum, shivering in fear of his life. “You shouldn’t have done it Christian... putting your nose where it doesn’t belong. You know, you should mind your manners and let adults do business. But you can’t do that can you? Now... NOW you’re going to learn a lesson about what happens when you don’t leave adults alone.” Dietrich cocked his gun with a look of sheer focus and intensity. “DADDY!!!” Christian screamed as Dietrich pointed the gun at the 7 year old son of a billionaire. Suddenly, by some miracle the lights in the hallway flipped on... temporarily blinding both Zenith and Dietrich, whose eyes had grown accustomed to the darkness. Ulrich Zenith walked around the corner just in time to see Nikolaus Dietrich put a Magnum into his brown trench coat above his own son... who looked up at him quivering in fear. “What in the FUCK is going on here?” Ulrich screamed approaching Dietrich who looked much like a deer in headlights at the sight of his majority business owner. “This isn’t what it looks like Ulrich! We were playing a game!” That was all Dietrich could mutter before a fist hit him square in the nose, sending him onto the floor and Christian scrambling to safety. Dietrich covered his broken nose while he laid in a fetal position on the ground. Ulrich hovered over the man with a look of malice. “This business relationship is over. I’m tired of the late nights and questionable meetings. I’m tired of money disappearing along with weapons and nothing to show for it. If you get up and leave right now... I won’t beat you to your death.” With those words Nikolaus Dietrich ran out of the building in a panic, never to be heard from by again. That was... until Die Erntemaschine years later. Those were quiet times before Dietrich reappeared, now there seemed to be only one choice if Christian wanted those quiet business times to return. Wind tunnels were made of concrete and steel from the breeze off the Great Lakes. American flags flapped violently as they hung down from the skyscrapers, reaching up towards the moon which was partially covered by passing clouds. Christian looked down at the city below from on top of one tower amongst many. His black trench style suit jacket flapped in the wind but stayed together, latched by two gold buttons and connecting gold chain. His foot was perched up on a ledge as he watched people exit the doorway over 20 stories below him. The wind gusted as he backed away from the ledge with cruel intentions in mind. He adverted his attention on the door that led down into the building, his cadet blue eyes narrowed as he slowly made his way across the gravel rooftop. The stones crushed under his march, which were barely audible over the howling yet piercing winds. The visible breath of Christian Zenith danced into the sky while he slowly pushed open the door. Behind the door lied a narrow, bare concrete stairway... only illuminated by dim lights hanging loosely from the ceiling by wires. Closing the door quietly behind him with a click, Christian slowly snuck down the stairs. He shifted his weight between each black combat boots as he made his way down without making a sound. Each floor along the way had a door with a small glass window that Christian would duck under as he made his way by. The hallways seemed deserted as Zenith stealthily descended. 16th Floor... 15th Floor... 14th Floor, the names passed by him as he plotted his next move and every obstacle he could encounter along the way. With a sudden stop Christian peered through the small window on the 12th Floor door from a distance, concealing himself in the darkness. He reached out and grabbed the knob... but it wouldn’t turn. Zenith stepped aside the door with his back against the wall as he rose his arm into the air. The doorknob snapped off and clanked on the concrete stairs after Christian came down upon it with great force. With a deep breath, Christian pushed the door openly slowly... trying not to make a sound as he closed it with a click and made his way down the shiny marble hallway. Against the wall, Zenith slid slowly towards the upcoming corner... trying to stay out of the lights that were set to dim above him. The faint sound of a click halted Zenith in his tracks. Someone was joining him from around the corner, obviously not intending to give away their arrival. Zenith pressed his back hard up against the walk as he heard the sound of faint footsteps approaching. Christian drew his chrome Desert Eagle ‘Ares’ from the confines of his jacket as his adversary approached. They found him out... and there was only one way to get past them now, the element of surprise and the fear of pain and suffering. The footsteps ended just mere feet away from him... he could hear their heart pounding with anxiety. Bum Bump... Bum Bump... Bum Bump... “It’s time.” Christian said to himself before he slung around the corner and pointed his Desert Eagle out in front of him... “Freeze!” “Get on the floor!” I can feel it Coming in the air tonight Oh Lord I’ve been waiting for this moment For all my life Oh Lord Oh Lord Christian looked in shock as the barrel of ‘Ares’ was planted firmly against the forehead of Vivica J. Valentine. He felt the cold steel lift off his own forehead as Vivica smiled faintly and drew back her Colt Python away from Christian’s head. “What? Are you not going to get on the floor?” Vivica questioned sarcastically as Zenith reluctantly drew back the chrome Desert Eagle. Although Christian wouldn’t show it, a little part of him relaxed inside knowing Vivica was okay. Now... now he had no problems taking out Nikolaus Dietrich. “What in the fuck are you doing?” Zenith muttered under his breath as he looked around. “I’m escaping... isn’t it obvious?” Vivica responded as she swung a pair of stainless steel handcuffs from her index finger, “I can’t believe you came for me.” “I have my reasons...” Christian responded as he looked around, hoping no one had heard their conversation. Vivica’s brilliant cerulean blue eyes surveyed the scenery along with Christian as she planned their escape. Christian backed up into the hallway he was in and looked back and forth, seeing nothing blocking his way back to the door. Zenith turned around as suddenly the side door behind Vivica J. Valentine swung open and out popped Nikolaus Dietrich. As suddenly as he appeared he had Vivica in front of him, and a Samurai Sword against her throats. Vivica dropped the Colt Python in hopes of saving her own life as Christian drew ‘Ares’ quickly in response. Dietrich chuckled as Vivica began took a big gulp, rapidly breathing as she felt the cold sharp steel just millimeters away from her throat. Dietrich was too close to get a shot on without hitting Vivica... “Long time no see Christian... I knew it was only a matter of time until we met again.” Dietrich said as his white hair sparkled under the dim ceiling lights. Zenith could hear Vivica’s heartbeat as her eyes locked with Christian’s in panic...
