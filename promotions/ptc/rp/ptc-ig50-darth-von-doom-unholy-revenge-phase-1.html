Kenny Mercury versus Darth von Doom

27 September 2008

Unholy Revenge, Phase 1 - Evil Bongs and Zombie Robots (IG50 Promo #1 of 5) Title: Unholy Revenge, Phase 1 - Evil Bongs and Zombie Robots (IG50 Promo #1 of 5) Word Count: 1000 Exactly Wrestler: Darth von Doom The scene opens inside the Castle of Blood which is now the new official base of operations for the dreaded and feared Church of the Unholy. Darth von Doom has just received a fax from PTC management stating that he will be partaking in the biggest Infinite Gauntlet extravaganza of all time. Darth makes his way down a dusty old stairwell until he reaches the Castle’s dungeon where the Church leadership is waiting for him. The rest of the Church leadership council consists of the werewolf Wolfen, the Horny Mathematician, vampiric surgeon Dr. Acula, and serial killer C.G. Murder. Darth walks in on them discussing the latest Church plans for world domination as he walks in and sits down at the council table with them. Dr. Acula turns and looks at Darth. Dr. Acula: Where have you been? Darth holds up the fax he received from PTC Management. Darth: I have received word from the PTC that I will be participating in the greatest contest the world has ever seen since the . Dr. Acula: What are you talking about? Wolfen: *snarl* You’d better not be playing a joke on us. Darth looks at him strangely. Darth: I don’t joke around. I think you may have me mistaken for the Mathematician and his stupid theories that revolve around sexual organs and orgasmic pleasure. Horny Mathematician: But the universe is shaped like a giant penis. All theories point to the ejaculation of… Darth motions his hand and puts him in a Force Choke. Darth: I should’ve killed you a long time ago. Dr. Acula looks displeased at the moment. Dr. Acula: This arguing is only causing problems within our organization. Von Doom, release him. Darth: As you wish. Darth von Doom released the Force Choke on the Mathematician. C.G. Murder is not pleased by this at all. C.G. Murder: When can we finally start killing people? Wolfen: *snarl* I haven’t killed a no fur in weeks. When are we going to see some action! Dr. Acula, the defacto leader of the organization at the moment, sees that things are getting out of control and quickly need to be resolved. Dr. Acula: Guys, stop complaining. Once our plan to get revenge on our enemies if fully implemented, you guys will have plenty of people to kill. And the time for that will be very soon. C.G. Murder smiles happily. C.G. Murder: Good. That’s what I’ve been wanting to hear. Dr. Acula turns in Darth’s direction. Dr. Acula: Now Darth, what is this news that you have from the PTC? Darth: Well here’s the big news. The PTC will holding the Infinite Gauntlet 50 extravaganza soon and it will be a tournament to crown a new champion since the title as it stands now is currently vacant. This will be our first opportunity to regain some momentum on the world stage. We could really do something to create some anarchy at this event. Dr. Acula looks intrigued. Dr. Acula: That is a good point. Our contacts in the wrestling business are extensive so this is easily doable. Darth: Plus James has got us contracts to compete in EPW, Just Wrestling, HSW, SWF, HOW, LWF, and GWF. We have a complex network set up to get ourselves noticed in all of these places. Dr. Acula: So how are we going to make an impact in this Infinite Gauntlet thing? Any particularly demented ideas that you have in mind? Darth gives him a knowing look. Darth: I do have something in mind. Dr. Acula looks intrigued once more. Dr. Acula: So what’s the plan? * * * Twenty minutes later … Darth has brought Dr. Acula and the rest of the Church leadership to the Horny Mathematician’s basement lab to show them what the big plan is. Darth: Alright, now that we’re all here, I’m going to let the Mathematician inform you all about the newest monstrosities we have created to aide the Church in our plan for global domination. The Mathematician walks over to the back of the room where there is a bunch of stuff hidden under a big white covering by the Mathematician’s lab assistant Igor. Mathematician: After the original zombie attacks used by the Circus of Doom that failed, we saved some of the zombie corpses. So then we upgraded our army to being robots. When they were defeated we saved some of them as well. Wolfen does not seem pleased at the moment. Wolfen: *snarl* What does this have to do with anything? The Mathematician smirks deviously. Mathematician: It has everything to do with it. C.G. Murder: I hope this has killing involved in it somehow. Darth: It will. Trust me. Continue Mathematician and show them what we have in store for everyone. Mathematician now motions toward the stuff hidden under the white covering. Mathematician: Now for the big surprise. I now give to you the future of our grand plan for revenge…Zombie Robots! The Horny Mathematician removes the white covering to reveal hideous Zombie Robot monstrosities that look like the undead version of the Borg from Star Trek. The robotic zombie creatures start to move and are now looking for human flesh. Dr. Acula: But won’t they attack us? Mathematician: No. Darth put a failsafe on them so that they will only attack Holy entities so we all are good. Darth: When we unleash these creatures before the Infinite Gauntlet begins, our opponents will have no idea what to do. They’ll be too worried about family and friends to even think about dealing with. It’s the perfect distraction to keep them from paying attention to me. Wolfen: *growl* But you never win. Darth: I will this time. Wolfen: *snarl* We’ll see about that. I don’t think this plan will work at all. Darth: But just in case it doesn’t, we bought a back-up plan from a High Times Magazine ad. I now give you… He reaches for the table and holds up a giant bong. Darth: The Evil Bong! (To be continued)
