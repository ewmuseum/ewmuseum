"Core"

5 December 2005

Date: 2005-12-05 * * * * * "…you’re much more than just human!" Startled, his brow covered in a sticky sweat, Clinton Sage jumped out of his bed and stood trembling for a minute, collecting his thoughts. The nightmare had returned, after lying dormant for nearly a month. It made no sense to Clinton, because Harlan Raines’ final words were equally confusing as they were mysterious. When the serial killer was executed on August 1st, he had no ability to see Clinton Sage standing in the back of the viewing chamber, yet he looked directly at him through the glass, and both men locked eyes. The words shouldn’t haunt Clinton, but they do. The first month was restless, and a victory in the Infinite Gauntlet was immediately followed by a defeat at the hands of Rich Rollins. Clinton’s mind clearly wasn’t in the game. For so long, he had been the bad guy in wrestling – always manipulating his opponents as he saw fit for his own personal gain. It took someone who had killed nearly two-dozen men and women, someone sentenced to die by lethal injection, to break the stranglehold Clinton had on the wrestling world. If Clinton were such a horrible man, would he be considered anything less when compared to someone like Harlan Raines, who had existed on an entirely demonic level of existence? Standing there, Clinton Sage tried to gather his wandering thoughts. His sudden exit of the bed only stirred his companion, her head lifting off of the pillow towards Clinton. The dimly lit bedroom transformed her chestnut colored eyes into black voids. Her face, beautiful in daylight, seemed contorted by the darkened morning into something out of a horror film. "What’s the matter? A nightmare?" she asked, her voice soft and still heavy with sleep. He took a moment to respond, still baffled by the recurring horror, "Yeah. Listen, I’m going to grab a shower…why don’t you get ready, I’ve got an errand to run in a few hours." The girl smiled, and rolled over onto the side of the bed. She sat up, naked from head to toe, and playfully turned back towards Clinton, "I would do that, if I knew where you hid my clothes. Are you looking for round three?" It was a very beautiful smile. Slightly crooked, over-accentuating one dimple. Perfection is sometimes found in flaws. Clinton ignored her advances, and turned for the bathroom. He needed to shower, and hopefully wash away this feeling of desperation that has been covering him since his loss to Spartan a day earlier, "Your clothes are in the dresser, the second drawer. I’ll be out in 15…" He shut the bathroom door, and locked it. * * * * * I fully understand that the road I’m traveling isn’t the easiest, in fact, it might be the most archaic route possible. Considering the nature of today’s wrestling personas, I will undoubtedly be considered something of a throwback. Also, with my mask, an enigma. I’ve chosen to wear a mask for a reason, and for once, the primary concern isn’t to conceal my face, or who I actually am. In early September of this year, I started to have a recurring dream. It involved a masked man who defied the norm of what the wrestling business has become. It was a man who was clearly defined as a hero, even though he lacked a face. There was no shade of gray in his actions…what he stood for was the very essence of what should be good in this world. And in my dream, the fans roared. An endless roar, until I left that world and woke up in this one. For a month, I had this dream no less than twice a week. Honestly, I’m not a man of faith. But my lack of belief in a higher being is being tested day by day, because ever since I’ve been given this second chance at life, you could say that it has felt like someone – perhaps somebeing – has been guiding me along all the while, gently nudging me down a certain path. Fate, perhaps? I’m not sure. But I have turned that dream into a reality. I’ve cast my face aside, my name, and the previous life I’ve known…to become Spartan. To become not a man in a mask, but a hero clad in black. To become an ideal, in a sport where the ideals have been stretched so thin they have become as transparent as some of the so-called superstars the fans currently cheer for. * * * * * Clinton had been in the shower for hardly a minute when he heard the banging on the bathroom door, and the screaming coming from the other side of it. There was a reason he locked it, because he knew when what’s-her-name found her clothes in the second drawer, she would also find the underwear of countless other girls he had brought home from the nearby university. Women hate to be manipulated and used, and this one was no different. The sex was good, a way to temporarily relieve stress. At least the girl got to sleep with a rich celebrity. He ignored her expletive filled tirade, and continued to stand in the scalding hot water. His flesh was starting to turn a shade of slight red because of the intense heat, yet he could still do nothing but feel the chills roll down his spine. Every time he closed his eyes, he could see Harlan Raines staring back. Strapped to the gurney at Pennsylvania’s State Prison, with three tubes jabbing into his arms. Each one would administer a different stage of the lethal injection, and by the time the third liquid was being pumped, Harlan Raines should have been unconscious – his heart stopped, and soul on a journey to Hell. Yet, Harlan kept staring. "Wish I had your faults" Harlan said aloud, as the first poison entered his bloodstream. It was meant to sedate him and render him unconscious. Clinton stood in the burning water, paralyzed by the recurring nightmare, except never before had he endured it during his waking hours. It continued in his minds eye, "Nothing seems to phase you…" He remembered taking a step back from the execution chamber, with each pace moving away from the serial killer that had selected him specifically. He had been shaking his head in disapproval when Harlan’s final words were first uttered months ago, "Lies! You’re much more than just human!" A moment later, the face of Pennsylvania’s most notorious serial killer went slack, and his head tilted to the side, lifeless. His eyes remained open – perhaps staring from the great beyond – for 15 minutes until the chamber was deemed safe to enter, and a medical staff member had closed them. Clinton relived those 15 minutes once more, feeling the same unnatural inability to move he had experienced during the execution. People passed to his left and right, some brushing up against him, but he never removed his gaze from the vacant eyes of the killer. And then it was over, and the hot water suddenly felt hot again. The bathroom was filled with a thick layer of steam. He turned off the water, grabbed a towel, and stepped out of the shower. As he was toweling himself dry, Clinton Sage suddenly came to a realization that Harlan’s final words shouldn’t be the basis for his nightmares, but the foundation upon which his actions were erected. Clinton smiled, realizing what had to be done. Unlike Harlan Raines, death won’t shadow his actions. Only suffering. * * * * * When a casual wrestling fan thinks of his favorite superhero, he’s often left with a person who undoubtedly stands on the side of good. A hero. But lately, the genre has seemed to darken. Your so-called heroes have started to take on the qualities of the men they are supposedly trying to defend stop. They’ve become the anti-hero. The sub-genre has never applied to me, not even before I donned the black mask. How I received the scar on my back would actually have classified me as an antagonist, rather than a protagonist in the story of my life. But it’s also a constant reminder of the mistakes someone can make in their life. Never again, I said. And I turned my dream into a reality. The world of wrestling needs more men who will not turn a blind eye away from the rules and regulations of the sport. There doesn’t need to be a chairshot across the face of a heel in order to get the crowd to roar in approval. There doesn’t need to be a dark river running through the heart of a hero in order to make him understand the true nature of evil. In fact, who said a hero needs to have a face, anyhow? Villainy undeniably wears many faces, but the face of a hero is starkly numbered these days. There are fans out there that can count one or two, and some who can name five or six…but if you were to count the true heroes in the world of wrestling, you wouldn’t need more than two hands. Men like Clinton Sage have brought me to this wrestling ring. Men like Angelo Deville or Rich Rollins, who thrive on making the lives of the fans churn in and endless chorus of boo’s. Those men take pride in the defamation of the sport, of the character of their fellow wrestlers, and the intelligence of the common wrestling fan. In my dream, these men are silenced. But for every dream I’ve had, a repetitive nightmare has followed. I just hope that since one has come true, the other will not. There will be those who think of me as someone who is trying to pull the wool over the eyes of the common fan, or the common wrestler. Simply because the masked wrestler gimmick has been done to death, and in the end, someone has always popped out from underneath it and caused an audible gasp throughout the sport. Not this time. My honor will become my identity. My moves will soon sketch out my persona. Victories will define my character, and the honest route I traveled to achieve them. You will see no shades of gray when I step into the ring, only the brightest of white. There is too much darkness in this sport to do anything otherwise, and if I’m called a recluse or a idiot for believing I can succeed, then so be it. It’ll be the mask I wear, I suppose. And at GTT5, I’ll make my stance known, and start painting my masterpiece. Let’s see if my dream can truly become a reality. But then again, the unmasking of Spartan would make a potential match with Angelo Deville all the more interesting. * * * * * Hours after his revelation, Clinton Sage stood before the desk of [name withheld] R. Guyle, head director of Primetime Central Entertainment. The very man who had booked the GTT5 tournament, all the qualifier matches that had preceded it, and Clinton’s own match against Spartan. "Tell me who he is!" Clinton demanded, his arms defiantly crossed across his chest. R. Guyle sat in his high-backed leather chair, his fingers intertwined underneath his chin. He smiled, "Why should I? If I’m not mistaken, the reason he wears a mask is to make you wonder who he is. You’re an intelligent man, you should know that." Clinton clenched his jaw in frustration. Keeping control of his emotions, he stared around at the office of this Australian millionaire. Books adorned shelves against the back wall, and the wall opposite it was nothing but a large window overlooking the parking lot to the PTC business complex. It was collectively known as "The Forum" to all that visited, because of its strict guidelines about parking, about when and where and what cars were allowed in what spaces. Clinton didn’t care for these rules, and his Corvette was taking up two handicapped spaces on general priniciple. R. Guyle watched as Clinton studied the room, "See anything you like, because we’re done talking about Spartan." On R. Guyle’s desk was a lone book, Merlin by Robert De Boron. Clinton’s dark brown eyes studied the book for a few moments before he finally glanced up towards his superior, "Interesting choice, I never figured you for the mythical type." PTC’s administrator chuckled to himself, and then leaned forward and picked up the book. He flipped through a few pages before smiling at it, and once again returning it to the desk. "It was a gift from your latest foe, actually. When he walked into my office with his contract, he brought this book as a gift." "Spartan?" "Who else?" "If you won’t tell me who this coward is, then at least tell me where to find him." "He’s no coward, Clinton. You and I both know that." [name withheld] started, "But you’re obsession—" Interrupting, Clinton Sage slammed his fist down on the desk, scolding his boss, "OBSESSION? The man is hiding behind a mask, keeping his identity secret. I’ve asked around, and supposedly the boys in the back are telling me that he’s some patron of virtue, a relic of the good ole days of when good guys wore white, and bad guys wore black. YET HE HIDES BEHIND A MASK?" Hardly distressed, [name withheld] R. Guyle sat behind his desk and refolded his hands underneath his chin once more, "You’re taking this loss exceptionally hard, aren’t you?" Clinton leaned in, resting all of his weight atop the desk. He inched closer towards the GTT5 Tournament Administrator, his teeth visible through a predators grin, "I’ve lost before. I’ve thrown matches before. But this coward used my own finisher against me! It’s unacceptable! He’s ruined my chance to shine, to achieve that final cusp of success that’s eluded me these last few months. Fuck your Infinite Gauntlet and its little champions…give me the man behind the mask!" "Threats won’t get you anywhere." "Threats will get me his name." "Truthfully, Clinton…even I don’t know who he is. He came in as cloaked as when he wrestled you. His lawyer was present, some hotshot from Texas…and stated that his identity wasn’t needed to successfully sign a PTC contract. In fact, he actually requested the match against you. And that, sadly, is all I know of the man." "You lie." "Actually, I do know something else. He defeated you, and seemed to do it rather easily." Clinton Sage’s eyes flared open in anger, and he once again slammed his fists down across the desk of [name withheld] R. Guyle. This time, he tossed aside anything that wasn’t bolted to the desktop in one sweeping motion. Finally startled, R. Guyle jumped to his feet and quickly hid behind his chair. Clinton saw the fear in his eyes, and smiled at his employer, "I’ll be back. I’m going to find him, and I’m going to make sure you are telling the truth as well, asshole." Clinton Sage turned and exited the office, leaving [name withheld] R. Guyle standing behind his desk, amid a mess of notebooks, paperweights and a laptop sitting in a pile on the floor. Clinton knew it would be hours before [name withheld] noticed that Spartan’s gift was no longer in the office, and hopefully by then, he’ll have lifted a fingerprint from it and be on his way to pay the masked wrestler a visit. * * * * * Like brilliant paintings hanging in the museums around the world, my masterpiece will quite literally start on a blank canvas. With each victory and the momentum gained from it, I’ll paint a picture of what wrestling should be. If I’m successful, the fans packing the arenas around the country will not only forget that they don’t know who is painting in such definitive, broad strokes…they will only recognize the artwork itself. In the end, some may see a masterpiece, while some may see a failure. Who am I? If I achieve the dream I’ve set out to…the fans should soon forget I’m wearing a mask. Actions will define the essence of Spartan, not who is hidden under its guise. The art shall outshine the artist. * * * * *
