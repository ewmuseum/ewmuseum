Date: 2004-10-15 He'd been watching her for nearly twenty minutes. Transfixed eyes observing her sleek, stylised body, while lips were left just parted. Like staring at a magic eye puzzle, he tried to adjust his focus so he could look right into her... fathom her out. Karina Wolfenden had been releasing her frustrations for nearly twenty minutes. Her toned, flowing body vigorously throwing itself into each blow. Like trying to reach for the sun, she flung herself into the air and exploded her powerful legs out... pummelling the hanging bag. "Amazing," he exclaimed as she landed in a crouch, catching her breath. "Could do better," she critiqued, scraping the pink and blonde hair from her face. It had been 4 four days since Karina Wolfenden left the third round of the GTT victorious. Her thirst for an adrenaline rush finally quenched by evenly matched combat and nail-biting near falls, Kari didn't want to let the feeling evaporate off of her. She'd waited two long weeks to finally feel satisfied, and it was a sensation that she was going to keep, no matter how much her tiring body resisted. The split-second that the lactic burn had eased, she hopped back up, driving gloved fists into the bag. Never likely to be the hardest of punchers, what Karina lacked in force, she more than made up for in passion. Each punch ended with her knuckles, but began at the tips of her toes. Her whole body, from facial muscles to toned thighs, helped propel her hands towards their target, while perspiration was expelled from her rapidly-warming skin. All around her, the usual gym members gave her odd looks as they entered. To them, the building was almost as much a social gathering as a place to push their physical limits. Most were happy to leisurely jog on a treadmill, or half-heartedly lift the lightest set of weights available. The sight of the near-exhausted young woman, mixing violence with undeniable grace, wasn't 'appealing'. There was one member who didn't share that view, however. "Hey," he remarked to one of his friends, "see that?" After a slight bend of the legs, Karina exploded once more, flinging herself up foot after foot in an awe-inspiring vertical leap. As quick as her tiring and stiffening legs would allow, she peppered the bag with snapping kicks, as if she were running up the bag's worn surface. "Who?" the man enquired back. "The Matrix chick?" After landing in a crouch, Karina ignored the intense aching of her legs, and flung herself into a rapidly-rotating back-flip, flicking her right foot up in flight to skid off the bag. "Leave well alone, man," was the verdict. "If she's like that to a punch-bag, imagine if she finds ya cheatin'?" The more her body became sluggish and uncooperative, the more Karina fought it. She hated defeat, especially when she knew there was more she could have done to prevent it. As long as she was still conscious enough to realise she was aching, she'd keep going, pushing her body's limits as they drew closer. With muscles too pained to guide her arms properly, Karina instead pummelled the bag with wild, swinging blows... while her valiant kicks only just managed to hit the bottom of the bag, her legs no longer able to rise high enough. Karina could feel the limit approaching, the point of climax, and she prepared herself for it. Harder and faster her blows hit, sending her soaring closer. The feeling caused tingling in her extremities, before welling up deep inside her. Her breaths were deep, and panting. Eyes found it hard to stay open as they rolled back in their sockets. Cheeks took on a rosy tint, while the hairs on the back of her head stood to attention. Then, she didn't just hit the wall... she burst right through it. At that moment, Karina Wolfenden's body, stretched to its capabilities, gave up on her. The puppet-strings that energetically operated her were cut, and her body limply fell forward, hugging the badly-mistreated punch-bag for support. Karina had reached the point where physical exhaustion caused her body to remove her from command, in fear of its health... and she felt, for want of a better word, glorious. Sweat from her glistening forehead ran down the bag that she gripped, having just pounded it with every ounce of her being, using its gentle swing to help her wind down. Seeing her legs limply hanging under her, the man made nervous paces towards the recuperating K-Wolf. Behind him, the friend he'd attracted the attention of dismissed his advance with a playfully smiling shake of the head, before turning back to his weights and muttering, "not worth the risk..." "Er, hey there," went his slightly reserved introduction. "That was some work-out!" The tone in his voice wavered, showing he was wondering whether to sound jovial or smooth... the result was an unflattering mix of both. "Water," came her monotone reply as she remained slumped against the bag, her sweaty palm-prints and pattern of damp hair being imprinted on the red outer-layer. "Huh?" the man replied. "If you want me to speak," Karina explained, her words strained, almost breathless, "you can fetch me a water." Finally releasing her comforting grip, Karina just about managed to stiffen the aching legs underneath her enough to stagger towards one of the benches that criss-crossed the gym. Dropping limply down onto it, the K-Wolf grabbed a towel from her rucksack and buried her face deeply in it. In circular motions, she mopped her face, then dried around the back of her neck, before running it down her arms. By the time she'd finished, the small towel was near-sodden. The work-out may have been over, but its twisted enjoyment was not. Muscles which previously felt scorched now sensitively warmed her body internally, their tingling and throbbing helping truly bring her body to life. After tying her hair back, Karina slumped back against the wall, closed her eyes, then slowed her breathing. The afterglow. It was after pushing her body to its limits, that it was truly at its peak. The constant hum of mixed voices and equipment grinding separated out into distinct sounds. A dropping of a weight was like symbols crashing between Karina's ears, while each deep, heaving breath, echoed for seconds. At one with her surroundings, Karina heard the man's footsteps a second or so after she felt them run up her spine. Before he'd even gotten close enough, the K-Wolf reached out a gloved hand, expectantly. "Er, there you go," he remarked, a little taken aback as he handed her the bottle. Without opening her eyes, or even acknowledging him beyond the opened hand... let alone thanking him... Karina took the bottle and leisurely unscrewed the cap. Dropping her head back, Karina parted her lips just enough to let the slow, but steady trickle of quenching liquid revitalise her salty mouth and dehydrated body. When the bottle's contents had been reduced by about two thirds, she lowered it, then opened her eyes. "Thanks," she finally offered, her tone pondering why he was still standing over her. Looking up at his face, its expression mixed, she noticed a shift in his eyes, as if he were wondering where to look. Karina severely hoped it wasn't a case of what she was thinking. "So," he awkwardly tried to initiate a conversation with. "Big match coming up, or something?" It was. Damn. Damn. Damn. Damn. Damnity damn damn with a damned cherry on top. And damning sprinkles. "Huh?" she partially replied, deciding to give pleading ignorance a try. "You're Karina Wolfenden, right?" he beamed back, expecting her to be pleased that she'd been recognised... how wrong he could be. "I have you on a load of tapes back hom-... no, wait, that sounded dodgy. Did it sound dodgy? It sounded dodgy... I didn't mean it to sound, well, dodgy. Sorry." Well, she wasn't quite expecting that. Deciding that it was as good a time as any to split, Karina crammed the towel into her rucksack, before dropping the nearly-finished water on top of it. "That's okay," she assured him with, despite barely making eye-contact as she stood. "I have to get going, anyway." "I'm a wrestler too, ya know?" the young man then added, really showing that he didn't know Karina at all. "Well, not really... but I had a few try-outs with a few gyms. Last week, I did this WICKED Springboa-" "As I said," Karina cut him off with, sliding her rucksack over her shoulders and pulling the neckline of her vest back up, "I have to go." The guy wasn't bad-looking. Maybe a little too 'boy-band' for some, but he'd definitely not had to worry too much about being single. He worked out, had a pretty tight body, no blemishes, and enticing blue eyes. The way he dressed suited his frame, and the tightly cropped hair he sported suited his face structure too a tee. His personal hygiene had no flaws, his voice almost had an alluring quality to it, while his body language seemed to naturally veer towards the heroic. He brushed and flossed twice a day, and even shelled out for some electric nose-hair clippers. And he just got blanked. Really blanked. If he was any more blanked, he'd be... well, he'd of been REALLY blanked. Very aware of the brushing sound her long, baggy pants were making on the roughly textured carpet, Karina made her beeline for the exit... all too aware that he hadn't taken his eyes off her. Karina was even a little tempted to turn around, and maybe say goodbye, but that course of action remained only a mild temptation. "Hey!" he then called after her, not even forcing Wolfenden to break stride. "Wait!" Jogging up behind the rather curt young woman, the man weaved from side to side, only for the K-Wolf to subtly swerve to thwart his attempts. Looking as if he might actually be admitting defeat, the man stopped, sighed, and put his hands on his hips. Sensing him shake his head and walk off into one of the rooms along the hallway, Karina's lips broke out into a slight smile. The attention was kinda nice, in a way. Adjusting her rucksack, Karina carried on towards the exit, where a marbled grey sky could be seen through the glass, swelling and rumbling. Sighing at the sight of a wild storm brewing, prophesied by swirling leaves and zipped-up coats, Karina paused to retrieve a Nordic beanie hat from her bag, pulling it snugly onto her head so that only a tuft of pink emerged from the front. Then, after pulling on a tight, fleecy top, Karina readied herself to step into the growling outside world. "My car is parked just down the street if you need a ride somewhere," a familiar voice stunned Karina with as the plucky young man emerged from another doorway beside her. "You're very..." she began. "Persistent?" he enquired back, eyebrow cocked as a prompt. "Charming?" "In my way," Karina dryly quipped back, not giving him an inch. Her cold exterior and apathetic reactions had usually driven most off by this point... but this one seemed to stick. It was her tough, border-line rude, selection process. After all, if they could tolerate her long enough to find out anything about her, then they deserved a medal, let alone dinner with her. "You're not the most open of people," he smiled back, skirting around the K-Wolf's defences. "And you're not very good at taking a hint," came her matching reply. It was like a sword-fight. Strike and parry. Stood only a foot apart, both the same height, both looking into the other's startling eyes, and both standing against a backdrop of dark grey, ominous clouds. Let's call it a moment, shall we? "Wow," the young man exclaimed, stepping back, seemingly impressed at her hard exterior. "If you're this tough to people who're trying to be NICE, I wouldn't want to be Johnny Lexicon!" Karina's eyes narrowed, and head tilted slightly to the side. As much as she was beginning to find being around him a little less excruciating, the fact he knew little bits about her still seemed to make her ill at ease. "How did yo-" "As I said," the beaming young man revealed, "I'm trying to be a wrestler too, so I follow all the big tourneys." "So, think you can keep following them instead of me?" Karina winked as she breezed past him, picking her moment when someone entering the building had the door held open. Reacting quickly, the persistent young man caught the door as it fell closed behind Karina, then jogged out into the street behind her. Still wearing just a white vest and black shorts, he instantly felt the chill on the cooling sweat which still formed a thin film on his skin. A defiant roar of thunder ripped the sky, causing rain the plummet down with heavy splashes. "Who knows, maybe we'll meet again!" he called after Karina as she pulled her hat on tighter. "Or you could call me... maybe?" "Don't have your number," she replied, shrugging her shoulders and not looking back to where her young admirer was getting well and truly drenched. "Try your left pocket!" he answered with beaming confidence. Could nature have actually evolved a male with the patience and quick-wit to both keep - and put - up with Karina Wolfenden? Score one for Darwinism. With a bemused look on her freckled face, Karina paused, not caring about the rain that was cascading down from her hat, before riding the slope of her nose. Ripping off the glove that remained on her left hand from the work-out, Karina tentatively slid it inside the pocket, where the felt the frayed edge of a ripped piece of card. With a 'hmph' that gave away that Karina might actually be impressed, she retrieved it, to see a phone number scrawled. "And how do you know I won't just drop this down the drain?" she called back, to no reply. Finally turning her head to look over her shoulder, Karina just caught the sight of the gym's door completing it's final, short swings, before resting shut. For nearly ten seconds, Karina just stood... a mixture of confusion and mild annoyance blending awkwardly with the fact that she was, as much as she'd tried to resist it, charmed. Her walk home, through the pummelling rain and under the crackling sky, took a longer route than usual. Rather than running to the shelter of home and all its comforts, Karina let the cleansing rain be her muse and she strolled around, contemplating the afternoon's events. Yes, she had to say she found him attractive... in a conformist sort of way. And yes, the fact that he tried to match her word for word impressed her as much as it irritated. But... well... he was a wrestler. She didn't go there. Not with Gideon Poe, not with anyone. Six floors up in the elevator later, and Karina was home. Unzipping her sodden top, Karina tossed it straight into the laundry basket, before hanging her hat over the radiator to dry. Squelching steps took her further into the apartment, before her sneakers were kicked off, and soaked socks peeled from damp feet. "You have one new message," her answering machine announced as she instinctively slapped the button while passing. Grabbing a towel of an airer, Karina dried herself off, before draping it over her shoulders. "Hi, Kari," a male voice greeted her with, "it's Yorrick. Just phoning to say me and Sarah can't make it tonight. Her mother had another fight with her new boyfriend so she's over there playing peace-keeper... and I'd feel kinda bad heading off a hundred miles away. Another time? Anyway, call me back, I still need to know if you're up for Lapland again this Christmas. Take care." And there went her plans. Staring out through the floor-to-ceiling windows, Karina watched the rain lash against the panes, making it almost impossible to see out of them. The weather sucked, her plans had been nixed, and she'd only topped her energy levels up to brimming a half an hour ago. Karina wasn't about to waste the buzz she'd pushed her body so hard to initiate. "Ah, what the hell," she sighed, striding back to where she'd discarded her top. "Not like he could be worse than the last one..." Retrieving his number, Karina seemed to pause to reconsider, before completing her journey back to the phone. Retaining a little of her hesitancy, Karina punched in his number, and waited for a reply. One ring. "Maybe he's not in," she thought. Two rings. "I'll give him three," Kari pondered, "then he's missed his chance. Three rings. "Might as well leave a message..." Four rings. "Or maybe just forg-" "Hello?" he then answered, catching Karina off guard, and ushering in an awkward few seconds of silence. "Hello?" After taking a deep breath, Karina regained herself and spoke, typically true to form. "Congratulations," she dryly announced, not leaving a pause long enough for him to get a word in. "Your irritation and rather creepy persistence has paid off. Meet me at 8 o'clock, outside the gym. You seemingly know about me, so you have no excuses for not keeping me entertained. Oh, and delete this number from your phone after I hang up." Did he have any idea what he'd gotten himself in to? Heh. Maybe he knew all too well.
