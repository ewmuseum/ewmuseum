Brad and Johnny Go Hunting...Drunk

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 *Scene opens again to BigBadBrad and Johnny Hitchin, outside deep in an unknown forest in an unknown area. Brad is carrying a huge cooler so full of beer that the lid won’t close and it’s making the annoying “swish swish” noise cans in ice make. Johnny is carrying two rifles, one hanging on each arm. Both men are obviously drunk, stumbling around. Johnny stops abruptly, Brad slams into him hard, and one of the rifles falls to ground. Johnny leans the other rifle against a tree and Brad drops the cooler.* Johnny: This shall be our hunting spot! Brad: I don’t think we’re going to do much hunting, bro. Johnny: What? Why would you say such a thing? Brad: Your just so damn noisy, man. *Both men laugh loudly because of their drunkenness. Suddenly Brad stops and gets real quiet.* 3B: Shut up, man! Did you hear that? Johnny’s eyes widen, looking around: No, what was it? 3B: Hahaha, I don’t know man, here have a beer. *Johnny shrugs his shoulders and forgets what just happened as he opens a can and starts in. Brad grabs three cans out, making the lid shut all the way so he can sit down. Johnny sits down next to him, and they rest without realizing they are back to back.* Johnny: I wish my cooler had a back rest like yours does. Brad, looking puzzled: Yeah, this things pretty comfortable...........Wait - what? *Brad turns around quickly and realizes that they are leaning on each other. He busts up laughing as Johnny turns around and starts laughing after he realizes it also. They both finish off their cans and grab up their rifles. Johnny starts shooting automatically at absolutely nothing. Brad gets scared and shakes his head real fast.* 3B: No! No no no no no, Johnny! What the HELL are you shooting at?! Hitchin: I....uhh......it just felt good to fire the gun....... Brad: Oh hell man! How abut you go set these cans out there and we’ll see who can destroy the most. *Johnny nods his head and walks out a bit and sets them down. Brad pulls his gun up to get ready.* Johnny: Hold on, man, I’m not out of the way yet! *Brad pays no mind, and fires anyway. The bullet makes a thud sound and Johnny falls over. Brad jumps up off the cooler and falls twice before he finally gets to Johnny lying on the ground. He kneels next to Johnny and starts crying.* 3B: No man, I didn’t mean to! Brad shakes his fists to the sky as he yells: Damn you beer! Why must you taste so good and make things fun........yet cause so much damage? *Johnny starts laughing really hard and Brad looks down at him angrily.* 3B: How could you do that to me?! How could you do that to a drunk man?! Johnny, standing up: It was easy really. *Brad shakes his head and walks back to get more beer. He hands Johnny a can and grabs one for himself before they both sit back down on the cooler.* 3B: I think this beer really could help me out in the Survival match, dude. Hitchin: How so? You going to take a rifle and shoot someone? I don’t think that will go over very well...... 3B, laughing: No, dude! It makes me not understand what’s goin’ on, so I’ll just walk to the ring and start busting ass on those who stand before me! Hitchin: What about Reeves and the other two on your team? Going to knock them out, too? 3B:.......Well, if they got in my way......Haha, I’ll try not to. Johnny: How about we give up on this hunting stuff and head back before you really shoot me......Because your really drunk. 3B: Well, YOUR really drunk, too! Who’s gonna drive us back? Johnny: I don’t know, man. We’ll figure it out when we get there. *The scene fades to black again with BigBadBrad and Johnny Hitchin carrying the cooler and rifles out just like they came in - Stumbling all the way.*
