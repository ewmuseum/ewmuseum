Storm(c) v Apoc v Patti v Jesse

25 March 2004 ~A dark walk, or is it?~ It was dark inside that big bedroom. The cold settled on the bed that was sitting in the middle of the room. Beautiful colors to add to the nice bed sheets that were placed to cover the bed. Nothing was heard in the room, not even a mouse. As the smell from the outside passed threw the open window of the living room, a slight sound was heard in the other room. Sort of a ticking sound that kept getting louder and louder. The ticking, leading into the other room. Going in you could see a figure just sitting in a chair looking through the window to the outside. His eyes, locked at whatever there was to look at. In the bed was the woman that he fell in love with. She gave a soft sigh before rubbing her hand over the bed where she figured I was suppose to be. The woman rose up to show that she was Michele. She looked over to the far right to see a clock that read. “Five o’clock,” Michele said with a soft tired sign from her mouth. “Were are you Marc?” “I’m here baby.” I repeated as I continued to still look out the window. The cool breeze from the open window was brushing against my chest. The hairs stood high and my skin gave off a funny feeling of coldness at every piece of wind. Michele reached over to the table next to the bed and grabbed her white and blue night robe. Placing it on underneath the covers, Michele got up off the bed and walked over to the window with me. “So what are you thinking about Marc?” Michele responded with a concerned voice from her mouth. “Just thinking of the old days in my past.” I replied as I turned my head towards Michele. Her eyes glimmered in the night. Almost like a light helping me get home. I reached upward and took a hold of her neck. Pulling down to my level, I placed my lips on to hers. My mind went away as did my body to. Her lips felt so good that I could stay in them forever. I pulled away as Michele looked back down at me. “Well Marc, you try and not stay up all night.” Michele said as she headed off to bed to get some much needed rest. But for me, I couldn’t rest. That was the last thing on my mind. Sleep was a blessing if I could get it but right now all I could think about was the Global Title Match. After winning the FAW World Heavyweight Title, I didn’t think anything could get any tougher. I turned to the window again and took a deep breath when a little light came shining from outside. I pushed closer to the window to see if I could see it again but nothing. I pushed myself up from the chair that I was sitting at and walked over to Michele. She was out cold. I didn’t want to bother her as I grabbed my shirt and walked towards the bedroom door. My mind might be on the match but my inner body was still connected to Patti Anderson. Passing by the pictures, I turned towards the front door were my jacket was. Grabbing the black jacket, I placed my arms through each hole and pulled it on. Walking to the door, I twisted the door handle and opened the door. A really cold breeze hit me in the face hard. Almost like razors to the face as I walked through the door. The trees outside were rocking back and forth from the hard-breezed winds that have come from the storm. I looked to my right, looking for that little light that I saw in the bedroom window. Looking closely, passed the rocky road that went down passed my driveway, I saw the light again. This time I took off walking towards it. The night reminded me of the nights I’d walk trying to get me ready for battles. As I came up on the end of the road, I noticed that there was a lake across the street. Walking across the road, I went through the trees that stood tall in front of me. All types of noises came out in the wilderness. I looked around at the place. Taking in all the noises that came from the creatures that wondered in the place. As I got closer to the lake, I heard a noise come from deeper in the woods. “What’s there?” I said, eyeing the wilderness that stood in front of me. I walked forward a little more, closer to the bushes that were on the ground. As I got closer and closer, the noise grew even more loudly. “I said, what’s there?” I responded even louder. After about a couple of minutes, I took a step to the right and looked right around the other side. Standing there, standing tall and around what I knew best. There was Patti Anderson, Jesse Jamester, and Chris Storm all around the casket that held the name of Sara Johnson. “What the hell are you three doing here?” I screamed out. Bedazzled at the site of all this. I ran at the three of them and before I could get to the casket, the whole place went pitch black. All I could see was me and miles of darkness. I turned around and around looking for something. “WHAT IS GOING ON!” I screamed out, trying to figure out what is going on at all. Then the unthinkable happened. I heard a buzz go off and I shot up from a chair. Sitting next to a cold window, I looked around at where I was. Lying back in the bed was Michele. Sleeping away with the covers all on her. I on the other hand had sweat running down my chest. My heart rate was racing as I fixed my sitting position in the chair. I turned back to the window and looked out at it again. I tried to remember what was done in all of that. Was it a dream? Was it a part of me telling me something? I had to figure it out. “The clock is ticking.” I said to myself before leaning back against the chair. The night was young. I wasn’t scared at what I was going into, I could simply see that I was worried. Worried that everything would end. My life, it’s wrestling and wrestling is what we will do. ---- 27 March 2004 How in the world do you ever get over something that was so meant for you? How do you walk away from it and go on when it keeps coming back around day in and day out? The simple question that you ask never gets answered does it? That’s because the question that you are asking is wrong. The right question for a man to ask is when will he survive? For one man, Apocalypse, he looks forward to death. Not to the form of leaving the world in a horrific way. No, to leave the earth on a quiet day in the woods or a roomy house. That way he doesn’t feel the pain that his Sara felt when she died. But the true question that Apocalypse may ask himself is this. Am I going to heaven or hell? So the question for you is simple… Where are you going to after you die? “Michele, have you seen my FAW World Title?” I said as I walked into the room. My eyes swung to Michele who was walking out of the bathroom with nothing but a towel on. Her hair was wet. Like she hadn’t wiped it off at all. She held the towel together before closing the door behind her. “It isn’t in the truck?” She responded before walking to the counter that was next to the door. “Well Michele, if it was in the truck I wouldn’t be asking you would I?” Responding with a look of disgust. Michele swiftly turned around, her eyes locked on my ever move. I gave a soft grin to break the ice that was brought up before leaning down to the floor to look underneath the bed. Reaching under, I took hold of the suitcase that was there. Pulling it out and flopping it on the bed, I opened the suitcase to see right in front of my eyes, the FAW World Heavyweight Title. “There we go.” I responded, pulling it out. “I’ve been looking for you all morning.” Michele turned around towards me and confused look as she took out some clothing to put on for the long trip. “You and that title. Is that going to happen with the Global Title to?” Michele replied as she pulled out a set of pants with a black t-top shirt. “No Michele, it will be different. A lot different because it will mean so much more. Besides, I’m not going in there to win that damn title. I’m going in there for revenge and respect.” I said as I pushed myself up to my feet with the FAW World Title in my right hand. I began walking over to the door with the title to take it outside. Reaching for the door handle, the phone rang as I turned back around and walked over to the table to next to the bed. “Hello?” I asked as I held on to the title in my right hand still. “Yes sir, just wanted to let you know that you’re supposed to be out by eleven o’clock or we will charge you another fee.” The man on the other side said. His voice kind of cracked a little. Almost like he was scared of something. “Well sir, we are fixing to leave now. You can send over the clean up lady after that.” I responded before hanging up the phone. Michele turns around to give me a view of what she is wearing. It’s a nice red dress that goes down to her knees. Nice and tight on her butt, almost tighter then a car feels on a race track going around a corner doing one hundred and fifty miles an hour. My eyes went right for her face with a smile, almost letting her know how much I loved it. “You must really like it then?” Michele replied to the seductive eyes I had locked on her. “How could you not tell? You look sexier then any female could look. I almost feel like I should forget my career and just stay with you all day today.” I repeated with a grin that could be seen from miles away. I took the FAW Title and tossed it right back on to the bed. Stepping over towards Michele with my arms open wide, I took her in my hands and ran my hand right through her long blond hair. The smell that came from it was like wild flowers. Daisy’s, red roses, and blue bonnet’s were just a few smells that came to mind from the hair spray that she used. I took a look back before a knock was heard at the door. “Dang it,” I spoke out almost turning my head around. “I told that guy it would be a little bit before eleven.” I let Michele go before turning to my right and going for the door. Opening it up, there stood in the door a young lady with a message or something in her hand. “Hi sir, I don’t mean to bother you but this was dropped off here for you.” The lady responded before walking away. I looked over at Michele with a funny look on my face. No one knew we were here. I shut the door behind me and opened the note. “Time is ticking away for you?” It was confusing as to who would send this. ‘Ok, is this some joke or what?” “Baby, it’s got to be joke or do you think it might be that Patti Anderson girl getting deeper in your head?” Michele responded with a concerned look as well. “If that woman things she is going deeper in this head then she can try but will fail. After what she did at Extreme Beatings 4 with Sara’s casket, I want her to bring all she cane for the Global Title. Fact of the matter is that she will have to bury me next to Sara to get to slow down.” I spoke before walking over to the bed and grabbing the FAW Title. I also leaned down and took hold of the suitcase that was next to the bed. But I couldn’t stop thinking about who it could be. If it really was Patti then why not show it herself? I know she wants to get me off my game but why just a note. Michele grabbed the rest of her cloths as I opened the door to let her out. “And I know Jesse and Chris wouldn’t go this way to get me off my game. I have respect for Jesse, as he play’s this game like a man. Chris on the other hand, it really doesn’t matter if I win the title. I just want to prove to the world that he really lost that match to me when we first faced.” I said, walking out the door to the truck “Well Marc, you might be going into this match not worried about Patti but do you really think she can lose this match?” Michele asked, walking to the truck with her back to me. I stopped doing everything I was doing. The door shut and I just stood there thinking. Was I over looking a woman who had hurt my left shoulder, took me out of my game, and even placed me with in the bloody casket of my dead fiancée? I started to walk towards the truck, still thinking about what Michele had said. As I did, pushed the button on the key chain to pop the locks on the doors. “I stand by that Michele. The pain in my left arm is the one thing that will make sure she doesn’t win. There’s no way a female is going to over take me in this match. Over my dead body.” I said as I tossed the suitcase in the back. “Plus, it’s Chris Storm and Jesse Jamester I got to worry about. You heard the rumors going around on them to?” “About the match being down to them two?” Michele responds before opening the door to the truck and hopping in. I knew then Jesse wanted that title just as much as me. To feel the gold around your waist is something that we had only dreamed about. Though only one of us could win the thing. I opened my door and tossed a leg up in to the truck and hopped in. As I did, I took a look into the rearview mirror. “Everyone saying that I’m to old to win the big one. Well look at what I did at Extreme Beating,” I tap the world title on my lap before placing it in the back seating of the truck. “I can win the big one.” I looked over at Michele who was smiling. Ear to ear, it made me kind of happy. I put the key in the ignition and started the truck up for the long haul down the road. My mind couldn’t help but wonder on how the match might go. Would I hold up to the challenge or would I fail like the rumors state? It was a test that I was ready for but would I pass or fail is the question. I turned right at the rocky road and pulled away going south on highway 35 towards the next town. As I did, I looked out at the unknown that awaited me in the next few weeks. “Whether I win or not, I will have no fear going in to this match. Storm, Jesse, and Patti better bring there A game because I plan on bringing mine.” I said as I drove off into the sunset with one question still left to answer. Will the pressure of the title kill me…? ---- 28 March 2004 When odds are against you, do you run from the odds or do you fight back? In the four-way match for the PTC Global Title Match, we have Chris Storm, Jesse Jamester, and Patti Anderson all fighting to be the champion. All giving their all to go into the book for winning the most praised title in the business today. So the question is simple, were does Apocalypse stand? The road was long and dark. It felt like I had been driving for hours and the funny thing was, I had been. My eyes seemed like they wanted to shut down on me. I turned to my right to see Michele lying there on the chair with her eyes shut. She looked so beautiful lying there. The rain was coming down hard and had been all night long. I couldn’t hardly see a few feet in front of me. I pressed the wiper button to make them go fast. As I did, the noise somehow woke up a sleeping beauty in Michele. “Huh,” She said out from the sleep state that she was locked in. “It’s ok Michele. You can go back to sleep.” I repeated before placing my hand on her head. She didn’t listen as she opened her eyes for the first time in five hours. My eyes looked over at her to see the baby blue eyes shining out of her head. She wasn’t my Sara but she was the best thing in my life at the moment. “Man, I could use some coffee right now. I feel like I fell from the empire state building.” Michele said as she wiped the sleep from her blue eyes. The road was really getting to me and the rain wasn’t helping at all. I had been driving for almost seven hundred miles and it didn’t seem like it was going to let up soon. As I drove down the highway, I noticed a sign to the right that read, “Henderson 15 miles” in white bold letters. I blinked my eyes a few times before looking back over to Michele. “Well you want to stop for something to eat and drink?” I responded before looking back at the road. “Yes, I could really go for stretching my legs also. It feels like I’ve been laying here for hours.” Michele responded also as she rubbed the right leg a little bit to wake it up. I kept my eyes locked on the road and my thoughts sealed away from the torture that I was putting myself through. I kept doubting myself on how I was going to do in this match. I was going against three of the top wrestlers in the world. I was good but it took me this long to get here. Was I going to choke at the pressure or proclaim myself as one of the best wrestlers in the game to date? “You know I’ve been thinking Michele. Maybe it wouldn’t be better if you didn’t come to the arena for the match.” I said as I flicked the turn signal button and eased over to the right of the highway. Michele looked a little pissed off. The way you don’t want your woman to look. She pulled her hand away from her leg and looked at me right in the face. “What you mean it might be better if I don’t come to the arena?” Michele said in an angry voice that would even scare you for miles. “Well I’ve been doing some thinking and I don’t want you there if I lose. It’s hard enough going in there with my years on these cats besides Chris Storm.” I responded with a sad voice. My heart just felt like the love of the match went right out. I drove a little longer down the road before I saw the huge sign that signaled for the exit. I eased the truck over to get off when Michele unbuckled her strap to get prepared to stop at the restaurant. “You still worried about Patti getting to you baby?” Michele was the type that spoke the hard questions and this was a hard question for even me to answer. “I’m not scared of her if that’s what you mean. I’m just worried that I might be to old to finally get the title that I have been working on getting all my life.” I explained before slowing down the truck and pulling into the driveway to the eating-place. The place was packet and heavy lights covered the building. It was the only place to eat on the highway. The lights shined through the tinted windows of the truck as I pulled into a parking place several feet away from the front door. I shut the truck off and opened the door to fine that it was raining really hard. Michele opened the door also and ran like hell towards the front door. By the time we both got in, we were soaked from head to toe. “Dang, its raining cats and dogs out there.” Michele said as she pushed her now wet hair to the back of her head. I looked up at the hostess who gave a smile laugh at us as he walked over slowly to take us to our seats. “If you will come with me, I’ll take you to your seats.” The hostess said before turning to his left and walking down a long pathway to the table that they had open for us. Me and Michele took our seats as the waiter came over to us and sat down two glasses of water for us. “How can I help you two this morning?” The waiter asked before pulling out his book to write what we wanted. “Well I’ll take some coffee and a short stack of pancakes to. Also, can we get that coffee strong to?” Michele replied before leaning back and looking at the waiter at the same time. “Sure,” Responding to the question that was asked as he turned my way to take my order. I looked over the menu for something good but still my mind just couldn’t get off the fact that I was going in as the total underdog in this match. Besides Patti who I was going to make sure doesn’t win the match if it all kills me. I looked over the eggs menu before looking up and ordering. “I’ll take the two egg breakfast. Egg’s over easy and water to drink.” I gave back before grabbing the water that was sitting there for me and sipping on it. Michele was really looking tired as she sipped the coffee that was brought to her. She looked off to the side and through the window at the rain pouring down like snow. “You ever think about what it will be like after you retire Marc?” Michele let out before smiling at the window. How was I supposed to answer this? Sure I think, “Yes I think but that’s all in the future. Right now I got to focus on the Global Title Match that I have. I don’t even think I can pull off the victory but survive this.” Michele swung her head around at me faster then speedy Gonzales. She was what drove me to were I am right now. It was Sara but now Michele is my coach now. “Why do you say that Marc? You got experience at big matches. Plus you are the FAW World Champion. You can win this just as just as anyone else.” Her eyes, showing victory already. Michele just knew deep down that I could pull off a victory. “I keep running the match through my head. It ends the same way. Patti Anderson hitting the No Sex for You and getting the one, two, three.” I responded with a serious look on my face. I ran my hand through the little hair I had left. The restaurant was getting cold and I ran my hands over the arms that were open. The waiter then came with out food as he placed the dishes in front of us. “Here you go sir.” He walked away after setting down the plates full of food. “I just don’t know. I got to go against Jesse Jamester who is a future hall of famer in this business, Chris Storm who I’ve been looking forward to facing him against, and then you got Patti Anderson who I’d rather seen shot. Maybe it’s the nerves or something like that.” I said as I reached over and took a hold of the saltshaker. “You know what baby, no matter what you do in this match, I will be right here to protect you from the world of pain. You deserve this chance and have earned it. I think even your Sara would be proud.” Michele said as she took another sip of her coffee. Sara would be proud. Again Michele was right. It seemed like I was scared to announce that I might be a little scared. If I lose I become the FAW World Champion. Though if I win I become more. My goal is saved and a dream is made. I couldn’t turn down an opportunity like this. “Your right baby as usual. I just think I’m scared at what will happen.” I said towards Michele. Even as I said that, the thought of lying on the mat at the end of the match was my destiny. But that could change. But how? That is the question of all questions. I looked up am Michele who went to get up. “Sorry but got to use the ladies room.” Michele said. I watched her walk away. I looked down at my cakes and couldn’t help but think. As I did, the thought of the winner kept going through my mind. I needed the win, I really did. As I saw Michele walking back, a smile came over my face one more time. My mind went everywhere still. But that to me was a good thing. So the question is this. Who is better? Yes that is the question as Michele took her seat. Michele looked down at her food one more time before as the place started to get quiet. As it did, the scene started to fade with that one question still left to be answer. Would you run from the odd’s or take them on? That is the question…
