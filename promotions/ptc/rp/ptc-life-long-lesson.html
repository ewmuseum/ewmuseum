Life-Long Lesson

27 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme Rumble (Stevie Vertigo RP #1) **May 12th, 1980** [A five year old Stevie Vertigo walks down a hallway, along side an adult in a suit. The young Vertigo holds the older man's hand, as the two continue down the hallway. We can see numerous plaques along side the walls, as well as pictures of various musical artists from years past. Stevie and the older man reach an office, with a sign on the door saying "Steven Daniels." The older man opens the door, and the young Stevie Vertigo enters the office...] Stevie: Dad, can we go get some ice cream soon? Father: Sure, son, daddy just has to take care of some business, then we'll head over to the ROSEQUE CAFE, and get some ice cream. [Stevie's father takes a seat behind a large desk, and picks up the phone to the left. Stevie's father dials a number, then begins to speak...] Father: Hello? May I please speak to a Mr.... Geofferson? [Stevie's father pauses for a moment, then seems to get very angry as the seconds go by...] Father: Look, you slimy little piece of shit! You will do the autograph signings, and you will do them when I tell you, where I tell you, and how I tell you! Now, if you're not out of the fucking trailor in five minutes, I will PERSONALLY walk down to the K Mart outlet down on North Dixie Road, and beat the ever-living shit, right out of you, do you understand me? [Stevie's father, now very upset at the person on the other phone, waits a minute for the response, and the smashes the phone back on the receiver...] Father: Stevie my boy, come over here for a second. [Stevie places his stuffed animal back on the leather couch where he got it, and hurries over behind his father's desk. At this point, Stevie's father picks his son up, and begins to talk to him...] Father: Stevie, remember what I've been telling you? Stevie: About what, daddy? Father: About leaches, who will try at every chance to take you for all you're worth? Stevie: And the sons of bitches (in a cute, little kid-type tone) who do nothing, but expect the world in return? Father: There ya go son! And... Stevie: And dat, you half to do your business in any way that you half to, even though people might tell you it's wrong. [Stevie's father looks impressed at the young heir's early intelligence, and the father picks his son up, and looks him in the eyes...] Father: Stevie, you're gonna do something special one day. You have the look in your eyes, you're going to be someone, I can see it. I love you son. Stevie: Wuv you daddy (with a hug.) [The scene fades, and comes into a new screen...] **November 7th, 1990** [The scene comes in to a shot of Stevie's father once again, now older looking. We see Stevie's father step out of a limousine, and fix his suit. Following Stevie's father out of the limo. are two very large men, carrying a smaller man. Stevie's father proceeds to walk into a large mansion, followed by the large men, still carrying the smaller man...] Small Man: (Being carried by large men) Noooo!! Please, I have a family to support! I'll do anything, just please, let me go!! [Once all four men are in the house, the two large men throw the smaller man onto the wood floor...] Father: Ok you mother fucker, if you let out one more godamn peep, so help me god, this is going to be worse than what I had planned for you, you got it? Ok boys, take him to the billiards room, and let they guy have a seat. [The two large men do as Stevie's father say, and drag the now very scared man away, out of sight. Stevie's father then removes his jacket, and hands it to a near by butler. The father then rolls up his sleeves, and puts his hands to his mouth...] Father: Stevie!! Stevie boy! Come here for a moment, I want to show you something! [The now teen-aged Stevie hurries down the stairs, with some hip-hop oriented clothing on, including a pair of the NIKE pumps...] Stevie: Yeah dad? [Stevie's father puts his arm around his son, and then they begin to walk. As the two are walking, Stevie's father begins to talk...] Father: You've been around for fifteen years now son, you've learned all I have to teach you, and then some that you've learned by yourself, but now, I'm gonna SHOW you what us business men do, to people who don't want to co-operate. You've heard of The Halves Decreit, right son? Stevie: That new band out of Los Angeles? Yeah, they're cool. Father: Exactly, they are the new hot band taking the spotlight right? I discovered them, and I'm now managing them for Universal. This band is going to be huge,and they're going to make us a ton of money, but some people, don't want to sell this music because they find it TOO OFFENSIVE. And these people, well, they've been negotiated with on numerous occasions, yet, they still aren't moving, so I've brought one of these guys to our house today, and I'm going to let YOU in on the meeting. [Stevie and his father reach the billiard room, and they stand outside the door...] Father: Now, son, I am going to warn you right now, this isn't going to be pretty. In these types of meetings, words aren't used as much to negotiate, instead, I have to hurt these people, but you know what I always say, right? Stevie: Conduct business in whatever way possible, even if some people call it wrong. Father: That's right, now lets get this meeting started. [The two enter the billiard room, and we can see the two large men from before, standing on either side of the little man they brought in. Stevie takes a seat near the wall by the door, and his father approaches the man in the middle...] Father: Alright, Jerry, I have a couple of questions for you, answering them incorrectly, may result in some action, action that will hurt you, a hell of a lot more than it will hurt me. Now, you seem to have a problem with my new band, The Halves Decreit, and that you are refusing to sell any of their merchandise in your record store. Why is this? [Stevie's father is now in the face of the man...] Jerry: W- well, at first, a lot of people- [Stevie's father punches the man in the face very hard, but the man cannot fall to the floor, as the man on the right side sets his back on the chair...] Father: WHY ARE YOU REFUSING TO SELL?!?! I don't give a shit what others think, why do YOU refuse to sell the stuff?! Jerry: (Blood slowly dripping from the punched cheek of Jerry) I'm sorry, I'll sell the merchandise, I'll do anything, just please, let me go!! I'll sell the merchandise, I, I'll sell the shirts, just please- [As the man continues to plead with Stevie's father, we can see his father beginning to get very angry at the man's incoherent rambling. Stevie's father then goes to the corner of the room, and picks up a pool cue. Stevie's father then slowly, and calmly begins to unscrew the cue. Stevie's father then grasps the cue in his right hand, and turns around, bringing the cue down across the man's face, spilling blood instantly. Stevie's father continues to bludgeon the man, until the man is lifeless, and even the post-death twitches cease to occur. Stevie's father then picks up a towel off of the pool table, and cleans off the cue. The dad then calmly screws the stick back together, and wipes his hands...] Father: You wanna wine, and complain in my house, in my room, that's fine. Brian (talking to the man on the left) just go to the record store, and take ownership, James (talking to the man on the right) make sure this whole situation gets cleaned up, ok? Both Men: Yes Mr. Daniels. [The camera turns over to the teen-age Stevie, who doesn't appear at all shocked, even after his father leaves the room. Instead, we see almost an understaning-type look in the young Stevie's face. The scene fades and comes back in, to a shot of today's Stevie Vertigo, sitting on a porch, sipping a cool glass of lemonade, and puffing on a stogie. Stevie is dressed in a white, buttoned half way t-shirt, and pair of shorts, completed with a pair of Italian shoes, a Rolex, and an expensive looking pair od sun glasses. Stevie has his feet up, and it sitting in a porch chair, on his porch overlooking his enormous landscape of a back yard...] - STEVIE - (Grinning) Hello, Prime Time Central, and welcome, to my South Hollywood mansion, here in beautiful California. I know many of you may not know who I, or possibly, even may not CARE who I am, which is fine to me. [Stevie gets out of his chair, and walks to the railing of the porch, and leans on it...] - STEVIE - You see, I've grown up in the richest of living. The best schools, the fastest cars, the biggest allowance on the block, I've grown UP with it all handed to me, therefore, I have absolutely NO reason to care about the opinions of a few imbred, over rated pro. wrestlers. So why I am I here, why, in recent weeks, has this blue blooded, former All-American football star decided to compete, not only in Prime Time Central, but in PTC's Extreme Title Division? It's simple, I have something to show these supposedly "fearless" athletes residing in the Extreme Division. [Stevie puts out the cigar he was puffing on, and continues to talk...] - STEVIE - These guys think they are EXTREME. These guys think they are INSANE. They all think they know what it is like to put their lives on the line to take care of what has to be done, but I've got some news for all of you pathetic PTCers, none of you know SHIT. You do not know SHIT about dealing with destiny, none of you know SHIT about doing what's necessary to get what you desire. I was raised by the most cutthroat Record Executive in California, Steven Daniels Sr. I may have been given it all throughout my whole life, but I had to learn how to keep it. I was taught that if it meant someone gets a beating to get what I want, then so be it, if Joe-sick pack has to die for me to get what I want, then put one more death on the list. [Stevie pours a glass of expensive looking wine into a glass and takes a swig...] - STEVIE - Business is business, no remorse, no mercy. A lot of these guys competing in this division, they refuse to hit a man when they're down, these guys take it to a certain point, a shot with a piece of barbed wire, then they drop it, a kick in head with a chair, but only one. The difference between these guys and Stevie Vertigo, once a guy's down, I'm not just going to hit him, I'm going to pick up a brick, and brutalize him, I will take as many shots as necessary to finish the job. Stevie Vertigo is in Prime Time Central, and things are going to change, no more of this lilly-ass Extreme horse shit, it's about time a true Mental Case turned things up a notch, and I personally, can't think of any better candidate, than yours truly. So in the Extreme Battle Royal, when I make my PTC debut, everyone's going to see a new Extreme, a new wave of brutality, a new wave of blood will flow through PTC, and it's going to be wiped clean from MY hands. I'll be there, I wouldn't recommend it to the other competitors, but hey, what would the match be without any "challengers?" [The scene fades on a shot of Stevie, and the scene ends.]
