From the Gates of Heaven to the Bowels of Hell

10 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-10 The arrival of morning is announced as rays of sunlight begin to creep into the bedroom through the slats of the wooden blinds that cover the windows. A hand grabs at the warm comforter that covers the bed and pulls it up tight, nearly covering the two heads that now just peek out from underneath it. A groggy voice grumbles into the crisp morning air, sounding like a bear that has just awoke from a winters slumber. "Who's idea was it to leave the window open last night?" There is a hint of rhetoric in his voice, and we get the idea that he already knows the answer. The two forms under the comforter move closer together, attempting to use the tried and true 'body heat' method of getting warm. Kami Axtell snuggles up tight to her husband's body, pulling his arms around her as she releases an exaggerated shiver. "You're nice and warm as usual," she says before kissing her man on the chest. "Why is your body always so warm?" Jim Axtell allows a mischievous smirk to adorn his face, his eyes still shut from his nights sleep. "I believe you mean hot, right? Why am I so hot?" Kami gently slugs Jim in the gut, bringing forth a forced 'oomph' followed by a chuckle and a kiss on the top of her head. The two share a hearty embrace for several moments, enjoying the way they feel in each others arms compared to the cold world that awaits outside the covers. Finally Jim props his head up and peers over Kami's head toward the alarm clock. "It's seven o'clock already," he sighs. "Time flies when you're having fun." Kami rolls over and looks at the clock as well, something that seems redundant at this point but almost always seems to happen for some reason. She throws off the covers and sits up on the edge of the bed, rolling her head around to try and alleviate some soreness. Her short blonde hair, cut slightly over her shoulders, is mussed from the nights slumber. The set of Victoria's Secret PINK! cotton pajamas offer some protection from the morning chill, but she soon rises from the bed and shuffles over to crank the window shut. "Jaxon will be waking up soon," she says while making her way to the closet to find her robe. "We have to get him ready for school." While his wife begins her day, Jim wishes his could prolong the start of his a while longer. He sprawls out on the bed, still nestled warmly under the covers. Just as he finds a comfort level and sighs contently, the family's eighty-five pound German Shepherd bounds onto the bed and plops down on his chest. "OOH! Jesus, girl!" The dog lovingly licks the stubble on Jim's cheek, leaving a generous amount of slobber behind. "Okay, okay! Good morning to you, too!" Jim scratches the dogs side with one hand while gently wiping away some of the slobber with the other. After a few moments he pats the dog firmly on the side, making a thumping sound and signaling the end of his affection. The Shepherd takes the cue and hops down off the bed, running off to another part of the house. Jim takes another swipe at his face with his other hand, cleaning up the remnants of the dog's 'kisses' and wiping his hand on the comforter. Hopefully Kami didn't see that, or else he might be doing laundry for a month. Sitting up in the bed, Jim reaches high above his head to stretch as he yawns. The former professional wrestler is still in tip-top shape, obviously hitting the gym on a regular basis. Jim's tightly groomed brown hair is a mixture of skewed spikes and flat spots, and he could really use a shave. "Daddy!!" Jim's face immediately lights up as his son, Jaxon, comes running into the room in his favorite 'Go, Diego, Go!' pajamas. The three year old's curly blonde hair is in a wild tangle as Jim scoops him up off the floor and embraces him in a big hug. "Good morning, bear! How's my boy today?" "Otay." "Did you sleep good?" "Otay." "Daddy's glad. Are you ready for some breakfast?" "Otay!" The father can't help but chuckle. "You sure are easy. Let's try something other than an otay or no question. What do you want for breakfast?" "Pop-up's," says Jaxon, his eyes aglow. "Pop Tarts it is," declares Jim as he receives another hug from his son. Daddy palms Jaxon's head and scratches his fingers through the knotted curls. "I love you!" "I wuv you mo, daddy!" Jim sets Jaxon down on the floor starts to climb out of bed himself. "Why don't you go and see if mommy will turn the t.v. on for you in the living room?" Jaxon's blue eyes sparkle as he looks up at Jim. "Tickey Mouse?" "I'm sure mommy will let you watch Mickey Mouse." Jim watches lovingly as Jaxon dashes off into the living room. 'The Axphyxiator' stands up from the bed and looks at himself in the mirror that extends up from the solid oak dresser. Things are going pretty well for him right now, he thought. He has a wonderful wife and son. His family is living comfortably…not lavishly like he thought Kami might want and probably deserved…but comfortable nonetheless. His business is finally beginning to get some legs underneath it. And he is going to wrestle again, something that Jim Axtell has been missing for a while now. The closure of PWC came quite suddenly for him, and even though he had been the PWC Champion Jim never quite felt that he accomplished everything that he wanted to in professional wrestling. The arrival of Jaxon prompted Jim to put that career on hold so that he could be with his wife and new son, but the fire still burns and the clock doesn't stop ticking. After discussing things with Kami, it was decided that Jim would enter this tournament and see how things went. He would be an underdog for sure, with the talent and stakes involved, but that's just how Jim Axtell liked it. Jim looks himself in the face with determination and focus. "It's time to bring the noise once again, Jimmy boy." ********** After a nice, warm shower, Jim emerges from the bedroom dressed in a black hooded Full Contact Fighter sweatshirt and black warm-up pants. He saunters into the kitchen to find Kami, still bundled up in her robe, standing at the sink. Jim quickly walks up behind her and gooses her firm ass, prompting a nice reverse spinning punch to catch him in the arm. "Jim!" she growls under her breath as she checks to see where Jaxon is. "Your son is in the other room!" "He was watching the t.v., I checked first." He plants a kiss on her cheek, trying to smooth things over. "Is he all ready for school? I'll drop him off on the way to the gym." Kami dries her hands off and grabs some papers off the counter, stuffing them inside Jaxon's Thomas the Train backpack. "Yeah, he should be all ready. Here's his backpack, and I'll pick him up." "Okay, babe. I love you." Jim and Kami exchange a quick kiss on the lips before he turns and calls out for his son. "Jaxon, are you ready to go bye-bye to school?" The angelic voice responds from the living room with, you guessed it, "Otay!" Jim scoops up his son with one arm and the two Axtell men head out to the garage. ********** It didn't take long for Jim to drop his son off at school, and he made it to the gym by 9:00 a.m. It wasn't really a 'gym' per se, but a garage that he and his training partners had converted into a place they could work out. Complete with excercise and weight training equipment, a wrestling area, heavy and speed bags suspended from the ceiling, and even a small-scale boxing ring, the converted garage held everything needed to train. Some of Jim's training partners are already there as he enters through the side door. "Hey, hey!" bellows someone from across the gym. He stands six feet, two inches tall and weighs in at around three hundred forty pounds. He's not chiseled by any stretch of the imagination, but the muscle mass is clearly evident on his sturdy frame. Butch sports short dirty blonde hair with the same color goatee. "G'morning, Butch," Jim replies as he downs the last of his protein shake. "How are ya today?" Butch crosses the gym and extends his hand, shaking Jim's firmly. His grey sweatsuit has several wet spots on it, alluding to the fact that Butch has already been working out for some time. "I couldn't be any better even if I was you." Butch slaps Jim on the back and the two share a laugh. "You ready to get your ass kicked today?" Axtell smirks, then quickly grabs hold of Butch's wrist with one hand and twists his arm behind his back while controlling Butch's neck with the other hand. The burly man quickly taps Jim's leg, a big smile on his face. "You wait until we get those boxing gloves on today," warns Butch jovially, pointing a finger at Jim. "I'll be ready for you, Butchy." Axtell takes a look around the rest of the gym. "Where's Brian? I saw his car parked outside." Butch motions toward the bathroom. "He's in the office. I think he's starting a think tank in there or something." "Well, if Brian is the leader they won't get much accomplished," Jim jokes. The two men head over to the wrestling mat where Jim begins to stretch out. "So have you heard anything about this guy you're taking on in the first round of the tournament?" asks Butch. Jim does a few trunk-twists as he responds. "Not much, really. I guess he used to box or something, but other than that…." He finishes his sentence with a silent shrug of the shoulders. As Jim drops down to the mat to stretch his legs, Butch walks over to the water cooler to get a drink. "Shouldn't be much of a problem for you, Jimmy," says Butch after taking a pull from the flimsy paper cone filled with water. "If you could beat that freak of nature Penance, you can beat anyone." "I wouldn't say that, Butch," Jim cautions as he reaches for his feet. "Anything can happen. These tournaments are intense. They don't just let anyone in. I'm surprised I got in myself, actually, having been out of the game for so long. We'll see what happens." Axtell pops up to his feet. "Get Brian out here, it's time to get to work." Butch walks over and pounds on the bathroom door. ********** Jim sits on the end of a bench, leaning over with his elbows on his knees and a towel draped over his head. He had been training for two and a half hours and was pretty well spent. Butch and Brian had already left to get lunch, leaving Jim to rest. The rest period was about to come to a horrific end. Kami Axtell comes blasting through the door to the gym, a mixture of tears and mascara streaming down her face. "JIM!!!" 'The Axphyxiator' perks up and quickly rips the towel off his head, revealing a shocked and puzzled expression on his face. "Kami? What's going on?" he asks. His wife runs over and leaps into his arms. "They took him!!! THEY TOOK OUR BABY!!!" "What?" The words hit Jim like a baseball bat, leaving him dazed. Between sobbing and near-hyperventilation, Kami tries to explain. "I went to pick Jaxon up from school….and they said that two men…had just come and taken him. They just came right in the school and took him!" Kami buries her face in Jim's chest, sobbing uncontrollably. Jim still has that deer in the headlights look. He gently pulls Kami away from his chest and looks at her. "This is crazy! Who would do something like this…and why? I mean, are you sure someone just wasn't mistaken and Jaxon is still up at the school?" Mrs. Axtell digs into her coat pocket and hands Jim a note. "They left this." Slowly opening the paper, Jim's eyes can't believe what they're reading. "Mr. Axtell, in order to ensure the safe return of your son there is only one requirement. You must win the GTT6 tournament. We will contact you later with further details. Do not do anything foolish. Do not involve the police. The loss of a child can be tough to get over." He has to read it over several times as his wife just stands there in front of him, shock starting to set in. "Get our baby back, Jim." Her shaky, frightened voice is weak. Jim pulls his wife in close again, clutching her tightly in his arms. "I will, baby. I promise."
