At Any Cost Amsterdam, Netherlands Friday, February 2nd, 2007 In an act of desperation, Craig’s arms shoot straight up and block the sword closing in on his face. The weight of the beast above him was nearly unbearable, but he knew that he had to survive at any cost. The pressure in his chest tightens as he refuses to give an inch to the man looking to end his life. With the cold concrete pressing against his back he is literally between a rock and a hard place. Being flat on his back is not an enviable position for Craig, but he knew that he would have to just live with it. Slowly, he takes a deep breath in knowing that he has to do something fast or else it’s going to be all over for him. He can feel everything inside of him ready to burst out of him and instead of holding it in any longer, he releases it. A wave of fury and anger explodes through his arms and he can feel a strength inside of him he’s never felt before. Slowly, the sword nearly pressed against his face inches away from him as the people in the arena watch on in astonishment. The eyes of the mammoth beast start to widen as he can’t believe what’s happening to him. With each passing second, the sword slowly moves away from Craig allowing him a chance to fight back. With everyone in the arena in a state of shock Craig decides to takes his best shot as his right foot slams into the kneecap of his opponent. The force from his foot is enough to shatter his kneecap, causing the man to howl in a kind of pain that is inexplicable. The shattering of the kneecap echoes throughout the crowd and a pained grimace is on each person’s face. Craig rolls away from the behemoth and decides that he’s a pawn for nobody. Eyeing up his opponent, he catches a quick glance towards Daniel Santiago and his friend. The expressions they had before, Daniel with a gleeful look while his friend with one of despair and fright, had quickly changed. Now Daniel sat their fuming while the friend was breathing a sigh of relief. Craig didn’t know the relationship between the two nor did he care. All he cared about was the fact that Daniel had his daughter somewhere and he wanted her back. His eyes quickly go back towards the monster before him who has dropped to both of his knees. Craig, a few steps back, races towards the man and leaps into the air. His foot slams into the back of the monsters head, causing a sickening thud to echo throughout the stone arena, and the man drops to the ground seemingly unconscious. The fans just look in pure shock before they erupt into a chant. “Kill him!” they yell over and over again. He looks down at the sword on the ground that the monster had before and cautiously picks it up. Sensing this, the man known as Mammoth rolls onto his back, hoping to see what is going on. What he sees is one that frightens him as Craig stands over him with the sword pressed against his throat. The man gulps hard, knowing that this is it. Yet, Craig instead drops the sword and drops to his knees, wanting to let the man known something. “Next time, you won’t be so lucky,” Craig whispers before getting up and walking away from the beast. The fans in the audience immediately show their hatred of this, feeling like they’ve been robbed of their money. As Craig starts to walk towards the gate where he entered, he’s stopped by the security guards. “You’ve gotta finish him. Arena rules!” the guy yells at him and Craig looks at the man with contempt. His red eyes are burning bright as he looks him right in the eyes. “Move out of my way, now,” Craig utters with such a visceral tone behind it that the man scurries to open the door. Craig slowly turns around and looks up into the stands, his eyes landing upon Daniel. Today wouldn’t be the day he got his daughter back. This was a message he was sending to Daniel, letting him know that he an interesting player has just entered his game. This wouldn’t be the last time the two crossed paths though. It wasn’t even the beginning. It was just the next chapter for them. --- Dallas, Texas Thursday, February 8th, 2007 Slowly, Brady Williams’s eyes flickered open. As they did, a pain erupted in his head right between his eyes. He wanted to yell out because of the torturing pain, but he was able to suppress that notion as he just simply closed his eyes. The sun had just started to shine through the house and after a minute he knew that he had to clean up his mess before his wife woke up and found him there. Once again, he opens his eyes and starts to sit up on the couch. As he wipes the sleep from his eyes and fights to keep his eyes open, he notices his wife is sitting right there with a look of anger on her face. “Brady…,” she says in a harsh tone trying to find the words, but unable to do so. Instead, she sits their fuming as Brady looks on as if he has been caught in the headlights. Around him are beer bottles and an assortment of other liquor bottles he had consumed throughout the night prior to his passing out. All Brady can do is imagine the thoughts that are running through his wife’s head; none of them are good. He starts to get off the couch, but he is quickly stopped by his wife. “Stop. Don’t go anywhere. Don’t move,” she tells him softly, almost as if she was talking to a child, not wanting to wake up their daughter from her peaceful sleep. Instead, her soft brown eyes just stare at him, hoping for the proper words to come out of her mouth. Brady just sits there, looking at Jessica, and wondering if there is anything he can say to make this right. Yet, he knows how it looks. Jessica is smart enough to know that he did something stupid, which is why he started drinking last night. As he tries to think about last night his headache just gets bigger and bigger. Clearly, he’s hung-over and would much rather die than be in the situation he is in now. “What did you do?” she asks him, wanting to know the truth immediately. He looks at her almost like a deer caught in the headlights. Part of him knows that he can’t tell her the truth or else she would kill him. Yet, he also knows that he’s a horrible liar and she would catch him in his lie. As the memories start flooding back towards him of last night he cringes at the very thought of it. He had lost two thousand dollars; on a wrestling match. Brady had put two thousand dollars down on Karina Wolfenden winning the Extreme Title the previous night. Yet, it backfired on him for the second time in as many weeks. While she had come close, Karina would place second, she would ultimately be defeated by Dusk. This wasn’t his first time he had bet against Dusk and lost. That very thought made him sick to his stomach. “I was…,” he began to say, but felt the words get caught in his throat. He didn’t want to tell her, but knew he had to. He had dug his grave and now he had to sleep in it. “I placed a bet on the match last night. I placed a bet on it and I lost again.” Jessica stirred in her seat, obviously uncomfortable with this bit of news. The tension in the air was thick as the two just stared at each other. Before, Brady had felt anxious, but now he felt nothing. It was an odd feeling, yet it was there. He didn’t care about a thing in the world; he could care less if his wife was angry at him. “How much?” she asked him, a simple yet complex question. His eyes quickly darted away from Jessica and onto the coffee table in front of him where several liquor bottles were in various positions of drinking state. He wanted to answer the question, but at the same time he didn’t. Yet, the way he felt easily made the decision for him. “Two grand,” he told her and knew it was over. Whatever relationship they had before was just mere dust now. He looked over at his wife who just looked at him, but no longer with anger. This time, with tears in her eyes; he had broken her. She slowly gets up out of the chair and looks at him pure sadness in her eyes. The tears slowly stream down her pretty face as she builds up the strength to tell him one last thing. “I—I don’t know what’s wrong with you Brady. All of this gambling, it’s going to take everything away. It just took your marriage and your daughter away,” she whispers to him before wiping the tears out of her eyes. He looks up at her, with shock in his eyes. He thought it would be bad, but he was going to take their daughter away and that killed him more than anything. He tried to find the strength to fight back, but he couldn’t. Instead, depression sank in as he slowly leaned into the back of the couch. Jessica turns from her husband and heads towards the stairs. No longer can she find the love she once had for this man. This wasn’t the same man she had married. --- New York City, New York Wednesday, February 7th, 2007 The gold belt glitters in his hands as the cold sweat slowly rolls across his skin. As the sweat reaches his chin, it falls off and drops onto the stone floor below him. Yet, none of that exists to him as he looks at the Extreme Title in his hands. He had been crowned the final champion after defeating a competitor he respects, Karina Wolfenden. Never in a million years could he imagine winning a title prestigious as this. Forever he would be remembered. His body yearned for water as he sat on the wooden bench, drained after such a hellacious match. Never before in his life had he gone through such a grueling match and he could feel the pain over every inch of his body. Yet, he had done it. He had won the title and now he would move on to the Unification Match where he would have a chance at being the top champion in the world. It was something he had only dreamed of. Now, it was all a reality. Yet, part of him couldn’t even think about that. Instead, part of him was elsewhere, thinking about his daughter. Oh, how he wished that she was here with him to celebrate this. Instead, she was still in the grasp of Daniel Santiago. At the very thought of that he could feel his heart ready to explode out of his chest. It drove him crazy that he had been unable to rescue her yet, but he knew he had to be patient. He had yet to see her and Santiago struck him as a man that wouldn’t just give her up even if his life depended upon it. Daniel was a cocky man, a man of power, and he knew that his life was more valuable alive then dead. Death didn’t worry Daniel. Craig looks at the title for a moment, knowing that his career would go to an interesting place after all of this, but knew it had to remain secondary. Instead, he had only one focus on his mind as he dropped the belt in his leather bag. His daughter. He picked the bag up and slung it over his shoulder. Even though the belt only weighed a few pounds, he could already feel the pressure across his shoulders. It felt like a sack of bricks. Outside is a limousine ready to pick him up to take him to a private jet so he could make it back overseas for the next PRIME event. He coughs slightly, knowing that he needs rest as he walks out of the locker room. Yet, he can’t rest. Not yet. For things are just now getting interesting for him. --- Tokyo, Japan Friday, February 16th, 2007 Daniel Santiago walked out onto the balcony of his luxurious suite and was amazed by the beauty of the Tokyo sunset. With a cigar in his right hand he sits down in a leather chair underneath the covered pavilion so he can take it all in. Santiago is a man who values his power greatly and would do whatever it took to ensure that at the end of the day his clout was intact. Around the world, thousands of people were doing things in his name whether it be selling drugs, boosting cars, or any of the other various endeavors that he was controlling at that time. Sometimes even he forgot everything he had his hands in. His right hand twitched slightly and he looked down at it, noticing the cigar still sitting in his hand. He pulls it up and places it in his mouth before he lights it with a lighter that he takes out from his left pocket. As he sits there, wearing just a pair of cotton pajama bottoms, he thinks about the man he saw fight in his previous Arena event. The Arena was something that Daniel took pride in because of the mere simplicity of it all. Illegal fight clubs were notorious around the world yet they were broken up before they ever became anything serious. His fight club though was one that had been in business for a year now. The authorities refused to look into it because they knew if they did that then Daniel would control them before they even knew it. With the cigar in his mouth, he takes a puff, removes the cigar from his mouth, and slowly lets the smoke out. The man, who had been introduced as the Masked Avenger, interested him greatly. Not because of the horrible name he had coined, but because of the skills he displayed in the arena, skills that could be rather useful to him. Mammoth had been a staple of his organization since he started The Arena last year, and never before had Daniel seen him dismantled in such quick fashion. It had looked like the masked man was going to be quickly defeated, but a certain fire had exploded out of the fire before Mammoth had the chance to finish him off. The odd thing though, for Daniel, was that as the man was leaving he was certain that the masked man had looked back at him. That interested him greatly. This man, whoever he was, had gone there for one reason; for him. In that case, Daniel had something he wanted and he could use that to his advantage. With another puff of the cigar, he gets up out of his chair and goes back into his presidential suite. In the bed is a voluptuous woman fast asleep in the king size bed. Daniel smiles at this and walks over to the edge of the bed before snapping the comforter off of her naked body. Surprised by this, her blue eyes immediately shoot open and look at Daniel. The smile is no longer on Daniel’s face, replaced by one of sternness. “Get out,” he says to her in a rather harsh tone. The woman, unsure of what she has done, looks at Daniel with a puzzled look. However, he’s already moved away from her and moving on to what he needs to do for the evening; putting together another Arena event. The woman slowly gets up and walks to the bathroom so she can get ready to leave. Daniel was not one to enjoy companionship. The mere fact that the woman had fallen asleep in his bed angered him. He could feel that anger boiling. When he was angry, things happened. He wanted to take his anger out on somebody, and he had the perfect person. As impressed as he was with the mysterious man, he had also ruined is plans. He had wanted to show his… business associate what it was like to cross him, and instead of doing that he had shown Daniel up. He remembered how after the man had destroyed Mammoth the embarrassment he had been shown. Instead of snuffing out his business associate, he instead allowed him his freedom. Daniel knew he could get him at any possible time. That time was quickly coming up. Yet, it was this man that Daniel’s thoughts were consumed with. He wanted to hurt the man and use him to his own benefits. If there was anyone that could do that, it would be him. He walks over to one of the tables in the room where a cell phone is just resting there. As he picks it up, he sees that he’s missed several phone calls, but he ignores them all. Instead, he’s much more focused upon the task at hand. His fingers quickly scroll through his contacts and finds the particular number in mind. Jason White He clicks the dial button on the phone and places it to his ear, waiting for him to pick up the phone. The phone rings once. It rings again. Then, he picks up. “’ello?” the man on the other end answers with a rough accent as he picks up the phone. Daniel smiles slightly before responding to his dear friend. “Jason, long time no speak,” he responds, knowing that things are going to be taken care of now. “Now, Daniel, this better be good,” Jason responds, sounding as if he just woke up from a deep stupor. “Oh, trust me, it is,” he tells him and starts to tell him the story. Daniel needed a shakeup in his organization and Jason was just the man to do it. --- Amsterdam, Netherlands Friday, February 23rd, 2007 The phone call had come in exactly a week ago, informing him to make his way back to Amsterdam, Netherlands. Any other day, this would strike odd for Craig, but in this situation he knew that was how Santiago conducted his business. After the match had ended, he had been handed a cell phone by one of his associates, and was told that he would be contacted for the next Arena match if he was to be used. Craig just simply took the phone without question and when he arrived back in the states for his Extreme Title defense, he made sure that the GPS tracking services had been disconnected. It had been two weeks of hell for Craig, having to prove himself in the ring against the likes of Jason Snow after the draining match he had to win the Extreme Title. Now, he had to prove himself to a man only known to him as Jonathan Winters who had decided to strike a personal vendetta against him. But, he couldn’t focus upon that. Instead, he had other things to worry about. More importantly, the upcoming fight he was going to have inside of the Arena. The phone call had been succinct. Be there at the Arena on the 23rd of February or else. Craig didn’t like the threatening nature of the message, but he shrugged that off. Instead, he bottled it up inside for that moment when he would need it, just like when he went up against Mammoth weeks before. As he strolled into the backdoor entrance of the arena, he saw to it that his face was covered. He was far from ready to show his face off to Santiago and wanted to make sure of that. Within minutes, he was ready for the fight and was instantly brought out to the side entrance for his fight. Calling it a match would be too kind. Instead this was just what it was, a brutal brawl to the finish. The rules of the Arena were simple, but Craig refused to heed them. Instead, he did things his way just like he had done in the past. Slowly, he walked into the pit where he was greeted with massive boos from the crowd. This was a vast difference then when he stepped into the PRIME ring, where he was loved by the fans. “Introducing first, with a defeat over Mammoth last time, THE MASKED AVENGER!” the mysterious ring announcer screamed into the arena causing Craig to cringe once again under the black mask he wore. That nickname had been coined for him the last time he was there and he hated it then just as much as he hated it now. Wearing a pair of boots, he could still feel the unforgiving concrete underneath him. Across the way, another steel gate opened and out stepped a man who was nowhere close to as big as Mammoth was last time. Instead, this one looked rather dangerous with his speed and agility. Craig closed his eyes once, blocking out everything. He even missed the name of his opponent, but could care less. Then, he heard a bell signaling the beginning of the match and his eyes quickly popped open. Craig looked up into the stands and saw Santiago sitting up there once again at the very top of the arena in a room that reminded him of a skybox. Sitting next to him was the same man he had seen last time he competed in the arena, and once again this man looked terrified. Craig became interested as to why this man was being tortured in such a way and why he had become the guinea pig for the second time in a row. The thoughts rushed through his mind, but he quickly blocked them out as his eyes settled upon the man in front of him who was looking right back at him waiting for Craig to move. With thought in mind, Craig started to move towards the man, but was quickly dropped to the ground. Laying there in a daze, Craig wondered to himself what exactly had just happened. With quickness he had not seen to date, the man had run across the arena and leveled him with a stiff kick right to his midsection. The fans above were cheering Craig’s opponent fiercely. Determined not to be defeated, Craig got right back up and locked his eyes on his opponent once again. Craig started to move towards the man and once again, he did the same thing. This time though, Craig was able to catch his kick and slammed him to the ground. This caught the man off guard as he found himself flat on his back. Yet, the man didn’t stay down for long as he leaped right back up to his feet only be planted with a knee to his back courtesy of Craig. Instead of just letting him dictate the match, Craig pulled the man up off the ground and slammed his elbow into the man’s throat, causing a groan to be heard throughout the entire arena. Not caring about the people in the arena, Craig glances up at the skybox and still sees a smile on Santiago’s face. This doesn’t bother Craig though as he backs up and waits for the man to get back up to his feet. As he does so, Craig does what he knows. A superkick. The kick connects with the man and knocks him flat on his back. The fans look on in pure shock as they have seen another one of their stars completely destroyed by Craig. He looks down at his opponent and knows it’s over. He looks up again at Santiago who has disgust etched into his face. Yet, Craig doesn’t care about that at all. He’s one step closer to Santiago and he knows that. Slowly, Craig turns away from his opponent, wanting to be done with this. It was all part of Craig’s plan to show that he was in charge, not Santiago. However, Santiago refused to relinquish his power. Instead, he wanted to intensify it. “Stop right there!” Daniel yells out at Craig, refusing to let him just walk away. Yet, Craig blocks him out and focuses on just making it out of the arena. However, it’s not going to be that easy for Craig. It never is. “Stop him!” he yells out at the security guards who immediately pull out batons in an effort to stop Craig. He looks at them, wondering what to do, and he ends up doing the logical thing. He turns back around and looks up at Daniel, wanting to know what he wants. Daniel points down at Craig’s opponent and makes it perfectly clear to him. Daniel wants Craig to kill him. Craig looks up at him and then back at the guards, wondering what to do. Then, Daniel grabs the man sitting next to him and puts a gun to his head. “Do it or he dies,” he tells Craig. This means nothing to Craig as he walks over to the fallen opponent and looks down upon him. He is slowly starting to get back up, and Craig is caught in the middle of something he cares nothing about. Daniel is trying to prove a point to his business associate and yet Craig keeps ruining those plans of his. Craig looks back up at Daniel and simply shakes his no before walking back towards the security guards. Daniel then clicks the safety on the gun and cocks it so it’s loaded. Craig looks back up at him, wondering if he’ll truly do it. The fear in the other man’s eyes is evident, but Craig could really care less. Daniel looks at the man and then goes to pull the trigger. Boom! Yet, it isn’t the sound of a gunshot that echoes throughout the arena. Instead, it’s several doors being kicked in simultaneously. Craig looks around in shock, trying to figure out what is going on when he sees several police offers and agents storming into the arena. A smile creeps across Craig’s hidden face as he sees Daniel looking on frantically. He’s obviously panicked. Craig looks at him for a moment before heading towards the fighter’s entrance where he had come in earlier. The agents didn’t know about this entrance for a specific reason. So Craig could escape without being seen. With the guards trying to figure out what to do, he was invisible to them. It was exactly how he liked it. As he walked through the door, he knew Santiago would find his way out of this. Yet, he would never forget Craig, even if he didn’t know what his face looked like or what his name was. Daniel was now playing in a game that was unfamiliar to him, one that Craig found himself quite apt at. No longer was he a pawn. He was now the king. --- Dallas, Texas Saturday, March 10th, 2007 Brady reeks of desperation as he steps into the dank, musty bar. In the corner sits a man who is well dressed and suave looking.
