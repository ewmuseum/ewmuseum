Hype?

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 Dallas Reeves stands, holding a large sign that reads, simply, "HYPE". He has a confused look on his face. "I have to hype myself? Hype? What's the deal? I stand here, jump up and down, waving my arms about in a silly manner just to get people to notice me? Why don't I just light myself on fire?" He tosses the sign to the ground before him. "I don't need to hype myself. It's not that I'm too good for it, no, it's that people, certain people, and they know who they are, have already eyed me up and down, anticipating my presence. They already *know* that I've signed up for the GTT5. They've just been waiting." "Last year's GTT saw me drop Kenny Mercury and stop the formerly Unstoppable Eugene(~!), only to lose to Dave Harley. Well, I don't see Harley around any where. Instead, my first round opponent is some slab of meat with an alliterative name. Mitch Mitchum vs Dallas Reeves? Come now Argyle, you may as well have just given me the first round off. But we'll play it your way." "I unretired, and instead of being able to gently glide back into the routine of rolling Juggernaut-style over my opponents, I have to get acquainted with a new wrestling promotion in HATE, another hackneyed first round opponent in the GTT, and I still have yet to get my entourage together, let alone unpacked my wrestling boots." Reeves picks the sign back up and pushes it firmly into the ground. "Hype? I'm not going to stand here until everyone knows that Dallas Reeves has arrived in the GTT5. There's no sense. The smart cookies already know who I am and that I'm here. If you're not clever enough to know, you deserve to lose. Plain and simple. Mitch, get ready to feel like an idiot, although I get the feeling that you've been practicing all your life."
