Returning Just In Time

10 August 2004

Date: 2004-08-10 Sometimes it is better to take a few steps back and realize that it is better to look at things from a distance. Things can sometimes get complicated and the best thing you can do is just hope and pray that things work out the way you desire. July 27th, 2oo4 1:32 a.m. Industrial District Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania I was not really that sure what had happened, but the fact was that I was alone on a side street somewhere. I had almost no money, and was not really feeling that great. I had a splitting headache as I finally got to my feet and brushed myself off a little. I grabbed my head and felt a large lump on the back. It hurt to touch it, but then again so did my pride after the last Burnout and even though Fury and I lost I still had to accept the fact that I lost and as much as I hated the fact I had to tip my hat to Slayer and Epitome for doing something that I really did not expect them to do and that was beat a team that for all intents and purposes I did not believe could lose against a make shift team of that stature. I looked around and did not recognize any of the stuff anywhere. I scratched my head and that hurt also. I reached into my coat pocket and pulled open my secret stash that held all my personal items including my billfold and stuff of that nature although the Wrestling Midwest tag team belt was in the Hummer and I have no idea what had happened to it! I kinda remember leaving Burnout and stopping at a stop light on the outskirts of Cleveland but after that things are kinda fuzzy. But I needed to get to some where that had a phone, so I could call home. I needed to find out how the girls are and what has happened, if anything to my car. I looked and decided to go left, I walked down to the end of the alley and looked around and saw the dimly lit street was almost empty of any kind of activity. I really had no idea where I was so I just guessed as to which way I should proceed. I walked towards the right and followed the sounds of what appeared to be people talking. As I turned another corner the voices that I heard were actually those of what I thought were gang members but as I drew closer to the group they were not people at all but cats dressed in gang gear. I had to admit this was rather strange as I was not sure what to make of it. “Yo what the hell this is fur ball territory!” the first cat thug said as he angrily looked at me and then drew his claws. “Now wait a minute fella’s can we talk about this??” I asked as I started to take a few steps back. “Look at the man, he cowers like a small puppy when confronted!” another cat hissed. “Now wait a damn minute!” I said as I stepped towards the cats “You all need to stay off the catnip and leave all the big, bad trash talking to the humans!” It was bad enough to be trash talked by a bunch of carpet sniffing, mouse eating, pieces of walking fur but I was not about to stand around and let it go on for very long. I had taken enough verbal abuse over the tenure of my career in Wrestling Midwest and was not about to stand around and let anyone or anything else continue the trend. I hated the fact that I was not really sure what was going on; it seems like I am dreaming or something… End Dream Sequence As I started to open my eyes, I strained to pull myself to my feet. I looked around and rubbed the swollen and painful lump on the back of my head. I would have to say that I was jumped and then dumped from my ride; the fact was that I was in the middle of no where USA. I was sitting in an abandoned building somewhere I dusted my clothes off and walked to a door on the other side of the room. I was not really sure what was going on but I was going to find out and as soon as I got back to the home things were going to change. I slowly opened the door, as it slowly opened I could see that it was raining hard and fast. I reached back, grabbed the hood of my pullover and placed it over my head. I walked out into the rain and looked both ways down the alley and did not see my Hummer anywhere so I decided the best course of action was to get busy; and try to figure out where the hell I was and what I would need to do to get home. I made a turn and started down a street in the distance I could see a small diner or something. As I walked down the street my shoes sloshed through the water puddles it made my feet wet and soggy like I had water bottles in my shoes. The rain made it hard to see as my vision was slightly blurred I must have taken a shot too hard! I finally made it to the diner and opened the door the bell that hung over the doorway rang and announced my presence; some of the patrons looked and watched me briefly as I sat in a booth near the back. A waitress came to my table and placed a menu and glass of water in front of me! “I’ll be back in a few hun!” she said with a sweet smile and a twinkle in her eyes! I simply nodded and looked briefly at the menu and pushed it aside as I stared blankly out the window as the rain continued. The rain looked so calming, so tranquil as I watched it fall I wondered why I was where I am at this place and time. I really thought I would be dead because in all honesty I should not be alive. But as I stared out into the dark and rainy street I knew one thing that I was in fact alive or at least in some form. The waitress approached me ready to take my order. I looked up and smiled. She pulled out her pad and pen and looked at me. “What can I get ya sweetie?” I looked at her and smiled calmly. “Just a cup of coffee and a slice of apple pie if you have any?” “Yeah fresh baked apple pie.” She nodded as she wrote the order down. “You have a rough night?” She asked pointing towards the strained look on my face. I was not hiding my pain well. “You could say that, had my car stolen and have a nasty bump on my head.” I said as I rubbed it. She leaned over me and looked at it. She gasped at the site. “I am going to bring you some ice for that ok sweetie?” she asked. “Ok!” I said as I nodded. She was a sweet girl looking to help me the way she was. I appreciated it and in a lot of ways respect the fact that she wanted to help someone that she does not know. And the fact that in this day and age that someone cares is an amazing fact in itself. After a few moments she returned with the ice pack and handed it to me. “Now you hold that on your head and no arguing. I will be back with your order!” She said with a sly wink and then she disappeared behind the counter. All I have ever wanted was to do what I love doing and that is wrestle. Hell I am not eve sure where “here” is. The fact that I am totally have no idea where I am is kinda creepy. I looked up at the waitress as she brought me my order. I smiled and added some sugar and cream to the coffee and took a few sips of it and gazed out the window again. Wondering where I was and what had really happened to me. I looked back and saw the waitress was sitting across from me smiling slightly. “You seem like you’re carrying on a heavy burden, a lot from the heavy way your face looks.” She asked “My name is Kalista by the way!” “My name is Noah…Noah Hanson. Could you tell me something?” I asked as I stirred the coffee. “Where am I?” “Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania!” she replied I nearly dropped my cup at the announcement as I was in the opposite direction I was headed when I left Cleveland. “Are you sure?” I asked. “Well I have lived her all my life; so yeah I think I know where I am at!” she replied calmly “You ok?” “No, you see I was on my way home from Cleveland I was traveling to Kansas City and I am now in Pittsburgh! I have no idea how I got here and my Hummer is gone.” I replied as I felt like I was living a mystery novel. “You’re Noah Hanson?” she asked she got up and walked to the counter and grabbed a USA Today and handed it to me. I looked at the paper ands saw the headline… WMW star Noah Hanson missing after leaving show! I dropped the newspaper and gasped at what I saw as I looked at a picture of myself on the front page of the USA Today. It was almost cryptic as I looked at an article that described the scene of what appeared to be my Hummer in the bottom of a ravine. I lifted up my shirt and revealed a huge gash that had been sown shut by someone but I had no idea how it got there. But with the way the newspaper article described things I must have been attacked or car jacked or something hell I had no idea! But people that love me and are wondering where I am and maybe even thinking that I am dead. I was supposed to start training that new team that my friend had asked me to work with but hell I need to find a way home and then find out who did this! The lightning and thunder flashed and popped the sky loudly I looked up and for a minute I thought I saw someone across the street from the diner. I reached into my billfold and tossed down a twenty in haste. Kalista was looking as I got up and ran out the door. “Hey!” she yelled as she stepped into the doorway “You comin back??” “Depends on who that guy is!” I yelled back as I ran after the guy he dashed through the alley and leaped up to a fire escape. He looked down and then started going higher thinking he was home free. “Hey I bet you think that a big boy like me can’t get you now!” I screamed “Well here I come little man!” I took a few steps back and jumped but my hand slipped off and I went crashing into a pile of old crates. I got up angrily and ran to the front of the building watching to see if the elevator was used and when I saw that they were not I looked for the stairs and ran over to them. I opened the door slowly and could hear footsteps approaching quickly. I waited and then attacked knocking down the man. I grabbed him off the floor by his shirt collar and slammed him into the wall. He grimaced in pain. “Who the hell are you?” I yelled as I slammed into the wall a second time. “I will not ask you again!” The man cowered in fear and was shaking. “Look all I was told was to do a job and make sure that you were roughed up a bit after the show!” I looked at him irritably and then stepped back a bit. “Who hired you?” “Dunno?” he replied with a calm shrug of his shoulders. “All I know is that I received a small package with where to find you, how to do the job and the money to do it.” “Then how did my ride end up in a ravine!?” I asked with an annoyed scowl. “Had to be the guy we sold the truck too. But I had no idea that he would do anything to it he was just supposed to return it to your house nothing more, nothing less!” the man said as he cowered in fear. “I do have this though!” He said as he produced my cell phone. I snatched it from his hand and let him go. He ran out the door and disappeared from my view. All I knew that my life was turning back around and all I needed now was to contact my home and make sure everything was ok! I walked out of the building and the fact that it was still raining did not seem to bother me that much as I walked slowly back over to the diner and walked back to my seat and sat down and quickly downed the coffee and then took a bite of the pie. “You look like you’re in better spirits!?” Kalista asked as she looked at me. “You could say that there is a reversal of fortunes now that I have my phone back!” I said as I looked at the phone and checked the voice mail for messages. I listened to messages from Kasandra, Kristina and my cousin Alex I decided that I would not call anyone just yet. “You’re not calling anyone?” Kalista asked as she refilled my coffee cup. “Not just yet!” I said with a devilish grin. “I have a few ideas up my sleeve and I think this could work to my advantage.” Someone wanted me out the way and has done a great job of getting in my head; the trouble was who was behind all of this! That was something that I needed to figure out and the only place to start is to go to my truck and that means I need a car or something to get me there! I got up once again and drank the rest of the coffee and then started out the door again. Kalista walked over to me and gave me a kiss on the cheek. I looked oddly at her because I had never intended for anything like that to happen. “You be careful and go back to that women that loves you!” she said with a smile. I nodded as I walked off into the rain. I needed to find out who was behind all this and I needed to call in a favor so that I could get home. July 27th, 2004 5:45 a.m. Vinny’ Subs and Suds, Downtown Pittsburgh I saw the lights on and knew someone was there and opened the door. I was drenched from head to door and sounded like a wet dog as I sloshed through the place. The bartender looked at me oddly as I approached the bar. “What can I do for ya mack!?” the bartender asked as he placed a small napkin in front of me. “I need to know if Bobby is in the back.” I said as the bartender eyed me. “Who wants to know?” he asked as he picked up a phone and dialed a number. “Tell him Noah Hanson!” I replied “Boss a guy named Noah Hanson is here to see you!” the bartender said on the phone. After a couple of minutes Bobby appeared from the back. He had a huge smile on his face as he approached me. And when he got close enough he slugged me in the mouth and then grabbed and hugged me hard. “I owed ya that one ya punk!” he snidely remarked “For screwing my sister!” My eyes widened at the remark but alas he was right I screwed his sister and man was she hot I can almost see her beautiful face now! “That was along time ago Bobby!” I said as we walked to his private room complete with strip club type things, main stage, Island poles and a full bar in the back! “Nice place ya got here Bobby, all the comforts of home I see!” “Yeah I wanted to do something that made it stand out and what makes something stand out more than a full blown strip club in the back of the bar!” he said with a confident smile from ear to ear. “But something tells me that you’re here for business not pleasure?” I looked at Bobby and smiled as I shook my head. “I cannot really think of anything better my friend. But you are right I am here for a favor.” “Anything you need you got it my friend, just name it!” Bobby said enthusiastically. “I owe you ever more than you do me! It is the least I can do!” “All I am asking is for your private jet to fly me home tonight?” I asked with a quick pat on the back as I looked at one of the dancers on the main stage. “That’s all?” he asked as he seemed like I was going to ask for more. “Well I do not need to draw to much attention to my arrival I need to be able to slip in under everyone’s guard. Someone is trying to kill me and I need to be able to slip back in unnoticed!” I said as I downed my beer and wiped my mouth with the back of my hand. Bobby patted me hard on the back. “Believe me Noah your own mom won’t even recognize you when you get back to KC. Trust me!” I smirked as Bobby made a couple of phone calls to a disguise artist. He said that he had a good idea that would slip me in unnoticed. I really needed to get home because not only was I worried about the business but I was concerned for Kristina. She must be having such a hard time dealing with all of this. I could only imagine what she is going through and it pains me to know that she is going through all of this alone! But soon we will be reunited and that was a fact and after that I am going to go and take care of business and then I am returning to Wrestling Midwest and reclaim my rightful spot at the top of the fed! July 28th, 2004 9:22 a.m. Pittsburgh Airport, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania I felt ridiculous in this get up and all this makeup but Bobby said I looked like a million bucks. I felt like someone’s high priced call girl. I walked through the terminal and walked through the crowd and even got a few whistles as I made my way towards Bobby’s private Leer jet. His personal pilot was there to greet me and take my things. “We will be leaving shortly miss, please take your seat!” he asked as he turned and walked into the cockpit area and closed the door. “I am coming home babe…I am coming home!” I needed to get home, the sooner the better. I knew that the ppv was soon. But there was also the PTC Survivor ppv that I was hoping to be able to get involved in. I watched as the plane soared through the clouds like a giant silver bird. I was really eager to get home, I missed everyone so much. I decided to go to sleep as I hoped that when I awoke I would be home. July 28th, 2004 Noahs’ home, Kansas City, Kansas I started to wake up and looked out the window, seeing my more familiar home was very pleasing to my eyes. I grabbed my things, got up and walked towards the door. The pilot opened the door, letting it down slowly. “Hope you had a nice flight miss?” He smiled as he said like he was expecting something. I turned. Titling my head to the right slightly. “Hey, birddog go fly a kite!” I said as I turned sharply, as I then continued walking towards my house. I walked in the living room, dropping my stuff as I walked up the stairs to the master bedroom. I plopped down; almost feeling so much relaxed as the fact that being home was nothing short of a miracle in my opinion. I laid there on my stomach; I heard a gasp behind me. I rolled over, Kristina was standing there tears streaming down her face. I started to cry as I stood up. “I thought…you…could have died!!” she said as she cupped her hands to her face. “Not yet, babe!” I said as I walked to her and wrapped her in my arms, kissing her long and hard. “I missed you so…” I cut her off before she could finish, placing my finger on her lips. “I know!” I said with a reassuring hug. “I am back now though!” I was glad to be back and with things closing in fast I was eager to get back into wrestling shape, which would have to wait for a while as I had other things to do. “Thing is cuz, is that everyone is looking for you!” I looked up, seeing my cousin Alex was a reassuring thing also. I knew he had been taking care of things. “Who?” I said I walked towards Alex with my arm around Kristinas’ waist. Alex pulled out his notepad, lookin it up and down. “Well there was Brad Johnson, Fury and Adam Allocco from WMW.” “Yeah, they are my teammates probably just worried about me.” I replied as it was nice to know that my fellow WMWers were concerned. “Yeah, Johnson was wondering if you were still going to be able to do the PTC Survivor ppv?” Alex said as he looked at his pad, sticking it in his pocket when he finished. “Yeah, I am going to be just fine for that thing, which is bad news for everyone else.” I said with a calm, almost confident smirk. “My god you are back!” Kristina said with a gleeful smile. “Indeed I am!” I said with a wink. “And better than ever!” *end*
