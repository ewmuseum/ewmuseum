But that doesn’t stop dozens of people from standing outside of the newest San Francisco eatery, all waiting for a chance to see a famous face. Some are photographers and reporters that pay the rent covering events like this. Others are perfectly normal people, hoping to have a brush with celebrity to talk about around the water cooler or at their kid’s next soccer game. They all stand behind the velvet rope that protects the red carpet leading into the exclusive interior of… Planet Bollywood. Yes, Planet Bollywood. It’s hip, it’s happening, it’s Hindu. Founded by those actors and sometime wrestlers, the Calcutta Wrecking Crew, Planet Bollywood is THE place to get curry served with a side order of movie star glitz! Outs steps Tsunami Oni, UWF World champion and Hall of Famer, looking smashing in a gray silk suit with a collarless shirt. A cool breeze blow through his blue hair. Each federation is ending in one representative. And for the Universal Wrestling Federation, who else could they choose but the man that holds their highest honor, the Shogun of Air and Darkness, the Japanese Storm God...Tsunami Oni? So at the end, I’m going to be standing alone in that ring, just like I did at the Regal Rumble this past fall. And there will be another name to call Tsunami Oni… the Best.” That said, Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu continue their path to Planet Bollywood. An attractive Latina is calling the Sensation of Domination’s name. Recently photographs have been circulating of you with a tattooed female wrestler from North Carolina. Are they real or fake?” “What?” Her boobs?” says Shiatsu. “Totally fake.” “I meant the pictures,” Lolita clarifies. “Ohhhh…” says Shiatsu. “Those are real. See, she’s been laid up with injuries lately, so I went to visit her since I know all about TLC.” “TLC?” asks Lolita. “Tender Loving Care? I knew just the way to keep her mind off her owie. I just hope her boyfriend doesn’t find out. He’d really get pissed. Those Carolina boys are weird. You should see his brother with the body paint.” “Wow! He wears a filthy T-shirt that reads “smarkass.com” and seems rather agitated. “Tsunami tsucks!” he yells. “Tsunami tsucks!” The object of his ridicule stops, unable to escape the sound of his voice. Or the stench of his lack of hygiene. Tsunami Oni walks over to where the man is standing on the other side of the velvet rope. “Huh,” says Oni. “I haven’t heard that in a long time. Please, Mister… Smarkass, explain to me why I tsuck.” “Cuz you’re gonna be in Seven of the Best!” answers the smarkass. You’ve just been screwing around in the UWF, Seymour’s been all of the place, man! He’s an interfed legend! That’s why he should be in Seven of the Best, not you! Once inside the doors, they are greeted by a lovely hostess of Indian descent. “Ah, Mister Oni and Miss Shiatsu,” the hostess says with a melodic voice. “Your table is right over here.” She guides them past various mementoes from Indian films. They pass an original movie poster for the bizarre thriller “Taj Majalland Drive.” They walk around mannequins dressed in hip-hop fashions from the gangsta epic “Vishnu Jack City”. Finally, the hostess brings them to a table near a life-sized replica of the title character from “The Elephant Elephant,” the heart-warming tale of a circus elephant with elephantitis and the two animal trainers that loved him, played by the Calcutta Wrecking Crew. All around them, most of the tables are filled with celebrities, executives, and their various hanger-ons. Over by the bar, Ben Affleck stares into his empty glass like it was a metaphor for his career. At a table for two, Dustin Diamond, better known as Screech in “Saved by the Bell,” sits with a girl far too cute for him. And in a large booth in the corner, the legendary Rod Stewart holds court, surrounded by all the sycophants and supermodels a man of his stature deserves. He gives a small wave to Tsunami Oni, who responds with a polite nod. “Shall I take your coat, miss?” asks the hostess. “Thanks,” says Shiatsu, removing the faux-fur.
