False Idol

7 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-07 [The sweet sound of organ music slowly creeps around the church. But the music being played is not of the usual church variety. In fact, it sounds a lot like ‘Superstar II’ by Saliva – a song that WWA fans associate most with the leader of Devil’s Own, ‘The Extreme Machine’ Dave Harley. So far in the GTT tournament, Harley has decimated his opponents. Now he stands toe to toe with Tarrant, the last men standing in the Pyrenees Bracket. As the organ plays on, Dave Harley steps in to view. He is framed by the light of the beautiful stained-glass window behind him. He wears a ministerial robe and his hair is tied back. He smiles a knowing Dave Harley smile as he places a book on to the lectern.] Harley: Brothers and sisters, we are gathered here today at the Harleytarian Church of Extreme in honour of our inspirational leader…myself. [Harley leans over the lectern as he speaks.] Harley: For during the past few weeks, His Holiness has smote all those in PTC that stood before him. He overcame insurmountable odds to defeat Salaok and Dallas Reeves. [That at least was true. Salaok and Reeves were tough opponents. There were few who believed Harley could get as far as he had. But now in the Pyrenees Final Dave would have to defeat a man who had already eliminated a WWA member. In the first round, Tarrant eliminated Matt W.’s errand boy Khaled. Now Harley hoped to get a measure of revenge. Not for Khaled or the WWA, but for his own personal glory.] Harley: I, The Very Reverend of the Harleytarian Church, speak to you today as the sole surviving member of the WWA in this tournament. Muppet Man, Odium, Brian Calvin, Khaled…even members of Devil’s Own Desolation and Mr Darkness. None have achieved the heights that I have. None stand at the peak of their mountain like I stand atop the Pyrenees. [Harley may be standing on top of the Pyrenees in a figurative sense, but he couldn’t even locate the Pyrenees on a map. Still, he wouldn’t let his opponent know that…] Harley: And now, as Your Holy Leader, I must end the reign of one who claims to be indestructible. I must vanquish my final foe of the Pyrenees. Tarrant. [Harley utters his name with disgust. Since claiming Devil’s Own as his own and turning his back on his X Corp friends, Dave Harley’s ego has soared through the roof. Most recently, his struggle with former WWA World Champion Craven Moore has seen Harley unleash a megalomaniacal side we had yet to see. As Harley’s restraining order against Mal Somers slowly ticks away, we can only wonder what fury awaits the cocky Harley.] Harley: Tarrant, today’s sermon is directed at you. It is for your soul that we pray this fine morning. We at the Harleytarian Church know your past. We know that you align yourself with false Gods. You are a Champion of the Lord of Darkness. You channel His power in the fight against the Lord of Light. [Harley relaxes his grip on the lectern as he speaks these next words. Cool and confident – the usual Dave Harley.] Harley: Well Mr Tarrant, I must inform you that your Lord of Darkness is a false idol. For you see, there can be but one Lord of Darkness and that is I, the Master of Devil’s Own. And unlike those false idols you have dealt with before, I cannot be defeated. [Harley thinks back to how he won the leadership of Devil’s Own. How their first leader, Ian Tense, enslaved him. How he plotted with Leviathan to overthrow him. How he used the power of Devil’s Own to unleash his jealousy on WWA World Champion – and former partner – Mal Somers.] Harley: There is one major difference that separates us Tarrant. You claim that you will never rest until you have defeated the Gods and claimed their throne. Well Tarrant, I defeated the Devil himself. I banished him from the WWA and claimed his throne. The difference is I am a victor, just as I will be when we meet. [Harley breaks off his cold stare to open the book, which lies on the lectern in front of him.] Harley: Now let us read from the book of Harley 435; “He did rise up above his peers – a Champion of Champions. There He stood upon the mountain, ready to battle the false idol that opposed him. There they did meet in combat, and fought for forty days and forty nights. But His power was not diminished and verily he did smite the false idol and did sit upon His throne, the Devil Incarnate. Undefeatable. Indestructible. His Devil’s Own at his side…” Harley: Here endeth the lesson.
