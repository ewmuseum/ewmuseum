IG 67 RP THREAD

3 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Our scene opens with a close up shot of a denim jacket-covered back staring into a setting sun. Tints of oranges and yellows and purples dot the clouds, and it is beautiful. Like, really beautiful. A man’s long brown hair blows gently in the breeze, and we hear a soft voice echoing in the background.) Voice: They call it a one-horse town, but I just call it home. June 10th, 1986…down at the old VFW field is where I hit my first homerun in Little League, and everyone was so proud of me. (The man slowly begins to walk toward the sunset. To the far left is a very large flat-roofed building made of cedar. It contrasts the beautiful scene with trees and shrubbery and sunsets. We notice that the man is walking along a paved path that eventually curves toward the building. He is taking the path, like really taking the path. We see more of the figure and notice that he is wearing jeans and white sneakers, although the right side of his body is still out of the shot.) Voice: May 14th, 2005. District championship game when we were taking on our heated rivals…Owl High School. Fourth down and four to go, and the coach calls a reverse sweep to me. I took it six yards and got us the first, which led to the field goal that won us the game. Everyone was so proud of me. (The man keeps walking, and the voice continues.) Voice: July 3rd, 2009. People aren’t so proud of me. I mean, they appreciate what I do, but being a carpenter isn’t exactly heroic. It’s time for me to reclaim my fame…in the RING. That way, everyone in Glory Hole, North Dakota will be proud of me again. (The camera pans out to reveal a battery-operated drill in the man’s right hand as he approaches the building and walks through the opening to go inside.) Voice: My name’s Ferguson Schmurgeson. But you can call me the Glory Hole Kid. (We see a wooden sign attached to the building that reads “REST AREA” and a zoom into it then results in the camera fading to black.)
