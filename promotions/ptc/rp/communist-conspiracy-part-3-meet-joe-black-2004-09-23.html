Communist Conspiracy Part 3 - Meet Joe Black

23 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-23 No one can die - while he loves! -Tagline from the movie Meet Joe Black. “Who knows Comrade English, once you’ve completed this mission maybe you’ll get to meet the Grand Architect himself! Lenin is a very charismatic speaker.” For a brief second Red Ivan looked like a star-struck kindergartner at a Michael Jackson concert. “It’s just as well - I bet his ability to play table-tennis was more than a little impaired since, well, the chop.” George wondered if the Disney Duma defrosted Lenin’s head from time to time to keep him updated on current affairs. The ancient Limey had been cleaned up since his vile presence graced us last. When I say *cleaned* I mean in the loosest possible sense...the Russian Mafia had untied him from the Goofy chair and George had drained his colostomy bag. Why? Because when kicked over on the floor for the impromptu dental surgery, he’d been resting awkwardly on top of it - imagine having your bladder on the outside of your body, then imagine laying on it. The old git had nearly exploded in a torrent of piss. “Must you do that?” Ivan wrinkled up his nose at the whiff of salty brine. “What?” “That!” Side by side, the diminutive withered old Limey came up about as high as the huge Ex-KGB Director’s bellybutton, they certainly made an odd pair. What the Ruskie was referring to was George’s absent-minded drinking habit and it had nothing to do with alcohol...although the substance had been brewed. Granddad English unpursed his lips and withdrew the straw - follow it further down and you’ll see that the catheter led to his colostomy bag, “Better in than out I always say!” “I’ll take your word for it.” although while ‘between jobs’ (well, more a logical transition from KGB Director of the collapsed Soviet Union to Russian Mafia Don) Ivan did see a program on daytime TV where this hundred-and-two year old crone was touting the benefits of drinking your own urine. Apparently if you cupped it while still fresh, it was full of anti-toxicants or something. Maybe that was the answer to Granddad English’s tenacious grasp on life. Although if the answer to everlasting life was smelling like a public urinal, the Brit could keep it. “I remember back in the good old days -- about the time of the Black Death I think -- it was all the rage to drink your own piss. The plague was in the water see, so the only thing to drink was piss, that was why wine was invented.” “Ahh I see, to drink instead of the piss.” deduced Red Ivan. “Hahaha, don’t be so naïve. You’re Ex-KGB so work it out - why do you think supermarket wine all tastes like cock-juice eh? Remember if it tastes like beef and smells like bullshit it’s probably a cow.” Red Ivan made a mental note to cancel his invitation to next Tuesday’s getting-to-know-you wine tasting session organised by the Triads. He’d find another way to make new contacts. George replaced the tube in the socket located on the outside near his bladder. “You are full of knowledge Comrade English.” and many other things besides... “Err Ivan, why exactly are we here?” Granddad George was of course referring to the secret underground gymnasium that was part of the benefits package for the Mafia and Disney workers. Such was the secret nature of their current task (to recap, that would be succeeding in the tournament long enough to beat and then capture Doctor Curiosity as the head surgeon for Lenin’s head transplant) that the facility had been cleared, but for two guards on the door in pink and baby-blue Disney Store cardigans -- one of which was currently sharpening his razor blade by rubbing it up and down his tongue -- and three men who were peddling furiously on exercise bikes. “Isn’t it obvious? We are here today to make sure you succeed in your next match...” Suddenly the lights in the subterranean exercise centre began to dim; the three men on the bikes had slowed down, it seemed from exhaustion. Red Ivan didn’t look pleased. “PEDAL HARDER YOU BOURGEOIS DOGS!” A silent command was given and one of the Disney guards from the entrance, the one not sharpening a blade on his tongue, went over to the sweating fitness fanatics. He unfurled a whip and cracked it above their heads - a renewed effort resulted in pedals pushed at faster repetitions. As a result the lights resumed to their original brightness level. George English produced a monocle from the breast pocket of his sepia-tinted (or maybe piss stained) shirt and squinted in their direction, “Hey, isn’t that Donald Trump, Bill Gates and...” “...David Letterman. Yes. Now you know why they no longer make public appearances - each of our Disney operations are powered by the sweat and toil of greedy capitalist pigs hooked to dynamos. Ironic, no?” just for once Red Ivan would’ve liked someone else to share in the self proclaimed genius that were his social metaphors acted out through physical labour. For fucks sake: it was performance art! Ivan could’ve filmed it and won the Turner Prize. “I dunno, seems a bit queer to me. If I was a crime lord I’d have used fit nubile young wenches...” “You’re missing the point - it’s ironic. Exploiting fat cats and forcing them to work hard doing manual labour that provides power for the greater good of the whole.” “Still seems gay.” Just get in the ring!” Red Ivan pointed towards the wrestling ring. In the centre of the canvas was the familiar black mouse-ears logo; now as sinister as the Riddler’s question mark...but not quite as sinister as a green lycra-clad ginger-haired Jim Carrey. George English shuffled up to the bottom of the ring steps then turned forlornly back, “I can’t go up the stairs.” he moaned. “Why not?” “I’m old!” It was true. Like Daleks, when faced with steps, old geriatric pensioners were opposed with a barrier as insurmountable as any sea or rock-face. A mere couple of graduating inches turned into a jagged fall of death to those of the osteoporosis brittle boned club. What’s more it was a fear that would creep up on any elderly human - man or woman, that’s why bungalows had been invented. The stinking old shite wouldn’t budge from the bottom step. Red Ivan got on the phone. Ten minutes passed. “Go on, give it a go.” sighed the gigantic Russian. George English looked suspiciously at the overly-comfy chair. He had good reason to be suspicious of strange new objects... 1275 BC “What do you think it is?” The Trojans stood at the front gate of Troy looking up at the huge statue made of wood. “Looks like a ruddy big horse to me.” said George English. “I can see that, Socrates, but what do you think it *means*?” “Well it’s obviously a peace offering innit?” English and Odessa circled the construct testing the Grecian craftsmanship by kicking the cartwheels like a pair of conniving car salesmen. “I don’t like it, it doesn’t make sense - we’ve been at war with the Greeks for over fifty years. Why would they suddenly stop the siege, go home, and leave us a present? Something smells fishy if you ask me.” George went up on one foot and wafted his loincloth, “Actually that might be me, I think I just developed the first known case of clamidia.” Odessa moved upwind, “But why didn’t they just spend the same effort on making siege engines? What a waste of resources.” “Do you always hafta be so bloody suspicious? Granted, a giant naked carving of Aphrodite would’ve been more appreciable, but you know what a bunch of horse-shaggers those greasy Greeks are. When the Spartans ain’t fucking each other they’re doing their livestock up the wrong ‘un.” “Hey!” said the offended equine timber statue. Odessa’s ears pricked up, “Did you hear that?” “Hear what?” “That horse just said hey!” “Hah, maybe it’s hungry?” “I’m not making this up - I say we burn it right now.” “Yeah and that’ll offend the Greeks some more, just chill Odessa; let’s take it inside the city and see what the head honchos make of it.” “Good idea.” encouraged the great, great, great, great, Grandaddy of Mister Ed. Odessa chewed his bottom lip in thought, “Okay, here’s what we’re gonna do: we’ll call out all the citizens and burn it in front of the city as an act of defiance.” “Cool, a beach party and barbeque, I like it. Maybe I’ll score me some beach maidens - better find some toad puke to rub into me nads first though...” “...urgh!” Gasped Odessa as a javelin from on high pierced him through the chest cavity. Blood frothed up from the wound. English looked up at the sky and thanked his lucky stars that Zeus was a shite shot, the spear was obviously meant for him (no doubt for his hobby of prolifically spreading the clap amongst the God’s virgins on his sneaky night raids to the temple). He gave one abnormally-bearded cloud the finger. With his sudden promotion to gate captain, George came to a decision, “Okay boys we might as well haul this in - it’s just a present after all, what’s the worst that can happen...” Present Day Yeah, George had learnt his lesson well about suspicious objects. “JUST SIT IN THE GOD DAMN SEAT!” motivational speaking was just one of the many managerial skills Red Ivan had picked up along the way. Still with a look of distrust Granddad English sat down. The chair was pleasing to his bent back which was unsurprising really as geriatrics were exactly the target audience this device was aimed at. An electric motor hummed into life and George began his steady but slow ascent to the top of the ring steps. Five minutes and six feet later. The stair-lift shuddered to a stop completing its sedate journey to the top of the ring apron. The old man dismounted and weaved through the ropes like the barbed wire he’d had to negotiate at Verdun and Ypres…admittedly it was Allied barbed wire as he went AWOL for the seventeenth time in a row from the mustard-gassed hell of the frontline, but barbed wire is barbed wire right? “What do I do now?” asked George looking around the roped off alien environment. “We train you up to overcome your opponent. Just as Mother Russia will overcome the tyranny of Capitalism.” the rhetoric was a less than comforting thought. Last time George checked Russia had failed in that aim and the only other exponents of The State were Cuba, China and North Korea. “Did you find me an opponent nearer to my age this time?” unfortunately wrestling seemed to be a young man’s game. “Yes,” replied Ivan, “with some financial persuasion applied to the tournament’s organisers we managed to match you up with a jaded, bitter old legend.” Things were looking up, “How old is he?” “Fifty-nine.” “Fifty-nine? Is that it! He’s not even at retirement age.” “Rounding up it’s the nearest match to you we could find - there aren’t too many wrestlers nearing the venerable age of yourself Comrade English.” “Uh, how old am I again?” usually the answers to all of George’s questions (Who am I? What’s the meaning of life? Which creature is Goofy meant to be?) were found stitched into his underwear. Tragically a few weeks ago he’d had a bad case of the runs - it’d given his piles terrible jip. Granddad English hadn’t gotten around to checking down below yet, but, from the itching sensation around his crack, there was the suspicion that his abused Y-fronts had been burnt away by the Mount Vesuvius re-enactment. One of the many benefits of being old: you could crap yourself and no one would bat an eyelid. The Mafia Don just shrugged, the answer to that was lost in the sands of time. “OI, YOUSE CAN’T DO THIS TO ME - I KNOW ME CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS!!” their quiet contemplation had been broken. Boris carried Ian English by the scruff of his neck as he hauled the whining Limey in front of the squared circle. “I MEAN IT: I KNOW MARTIN LUTHER KING, HE’S JUST ONE PHONE CALL AWAY!! DON’T EVEN MAKE ME BRING MALCOM X INTO IT...” Then he was dumped unceremoniously on his arse. Like a rabid Jack Russell terrier Ian was up in a second and rolling up the sleeves of his punk rock leather jacket brewing for a ruckus...until he noticed the eight-foot-squared of deranged Communist that was Red Ivan. “Ah Ivan, sorry mate I didn’t realise...” past the monstrous Ruskie Ian spied his necrotic grandfather. “YOU! I bet this is your fault, wot ’ave you got me into this time Mumra?” “Now back in the good old days, people had respect for their elders...AND we had to walk to school bare foot through snow and rain, the drifts were bigger back then as well! Why I remember one time little Timmy got frostbite on his big toe and had to chew off his foot because amputation hadn’t been invented yet...did I mention the drifts were bigger...” “Look Ivan, wotever mess he’s got ’imself into, it’s nowt to do with me. Just shoot ’im, nobody’ll miss ’im. Tells you wot - gimme the gun and I’ll do it! I don’t even need a gun, I’ll just throttle ’im...” He dived forward but found himself once more revisiting the floor as Red Ivan stepped in the way stonewalling the obnoxious Limey with his barrel chest. The wooden boards were really starting to hurt Ian’s arse. George’s croaky laugh spurred his grandson back to his feet. “Calm down Comrade English Junior. I will explain all if you give me the chance...” Ian was inclined to listen (not one to fight people who were bigger, or younger, or stronger, or looking at him...) Red Ivan then went on to explain the Communist Conspiracy, the Disney façade, Lenin’s severed head and what Granddad George’s role in all of this was, “...so now do you see?” “You lost me when you got to the bit about John Lennon’s severed head.” “Not John Lennon - Lenin!” “He wasn’t the Beetle that got shot then?” “NO!” Ian narrowed his eyes, “Alright mate, keep yer furry ’at on, so wot do yer want with me eh?” “You are a great wrestler yes?” “I like to think so.” if we were using the term *wrestler* loosely... “Then it is decided! You will be the one to teach your Grandfather the ways of victory!” English glanced over his shoulder but the only route of escape was blocked by the burly Disney shop assistants. He let out a long-suffering sigh, “Okay, who’s the enemy?” “Bourgeois exploitation of the proletariat and the selfish greed of capitalism.” surely Red Ivan was evidence that too many doctrines and mantras were bad for your health. “Okaaay. Let me rephrase that. Who’s Skeletor’s opponent?” The whiffy codger chimed in, “Yeah, who is my opponent?” “An old veteran by the name of Joe Black.” “Let me guess,” said Ian, “his name is Joe and he’s black.” Obvious gimmicks were a necessary evil of the business. Boris had been silently sulking since his last rebuking by Ivan in the torture basement, but now he spoke up, “Just like your name is Ian and you are English, yes?” “Mere coincidence, besides I’m not black.” Ian replied, trying to distort the argument. “Boris has a point,” said Granddad. “Just shut yer mouth you old shite, the principle applies to you as well! Okay, think. So wot do we know about ’im? He’s old, well not as old as King Tut over there, but *oldish.* So he probably drinks piss from a colostomy bag...or the next best thing, a faggy beer like Coors Light. Being an old wrestler I’m willing to bet he tries to bore ’is opponents with tales of ’ammerlocks and the good old days...” “...yeah I hate it when people do that, why can’t they get with the times?” The other three men glared at the elderly hypocrite in the ring. “You sure this is a real tournament and not an internet stunt to see two old geezers fight each other, y’know, like bum fights? Wot’s the opponent’s name again?” “Joe Black.” repeated Red Ivan. “Wait-a-minute isn’t that the name of a movie?” now it was Ian’s turn to be stared at by the other men. Fortunately Boris came to his rescue by drawing the flak. “No, the correct title is *Meet Joe Black* it has Brad Pitt in it.” “Boris! You’ve been watching chick flicks again. I thought I had explicitly banned that after the *Pretty Woman* incident.” scorned Red Ivan to his underling. “I only watched it because it has that great actor from Mother Russia in it - I’m a big fan of Anthony Hopkins.” Nobody had the heart to break the truth to Boris. But George was confused, “I’m fighting Brad Pitt? They’re not going to turn me into soap are they?” “That’s a different film Granddad, in this film Joe Black is Death.” The world wasn’t ready for crap-smelling soap. “Pardon?” Ian raised his voice. “I said, Joe Black is Death.” “I’d prefer it if he was blind, that way I could trip him up.” Boris tried to explain, “No Comrade English, in the movie Joe Black is the Grim Reaper.” Red Ivan nodded approvingly: anyone who carried around a large sickle had to be a communist. Granddad George English on the other hand looked extremely worried; he’d been dodging the Reaper for most of his unnatural life. Plus last time they’d come face to face he’d won their game of chess by cheating when the super model wannabe had gone to the toilet. “Hang-on-a-minute, nobody said anything about fighting Death! Especially no black death,” -- it seemed like equal opportunities gone mad to George -- “how can I beat a guy who is already dead?” “Depends, if he were a zombie then you’d shoot him in the head...” Red Ivan still had memories from the last time the Soviet Biological Weapons Program forgot to padlock the cages. “Well,” began Boris, “in the film Joe Black falls in love with Anthony Hopkins’ daughter - while he is in love he cannot claim people’s lives.” “But I don’t have a daughter!” George was really panicking now. Then his eyes fell upon Ian, “YOU! You’ll have to do. You have to make Joe Black fall in love with you, it’s my only chance!” “Fack orf you old bastid, I ain’t saving yer wrinkly arse let alone engaging in homosexual necrophilia!” “I dunno, it could work.” Red Ivan was rubbing his chin, the premise gave him an idea for a new internet paysite. Ian snapped back, “You muppets, Joe Black is just some doddering old cunt, how hard can this be?” A heavy, musky, aroma descended upon the three men ringside. Much like a peeled onion it brought tears to the eyes. One by one they looked at the crypt keeper in the ring. George shrugged, “When you gotta go you gotta go.” Who were they kidding? The incontinent old man was the underdog; he had been his whole life. What chance did they stand? Lenin’s head would just have to be kept on ice a little longer than originally planned.
