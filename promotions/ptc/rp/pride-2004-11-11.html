Pride

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-11 Sebastian Moraco walked into the Starbucks dressed in blue jeans and a cream colored shirt. The place was moderately empty, but Sebastian didn’t walk in for coffee. No, he was there to see Sarah. He had talked with a few times after their dinner date two weeks ago, but hadn’t seen her since. He didn’t want to let her off with the impression that he wasn’t interested anymore. Truth is, he was, but with the tournament and other things going on it seemed more difficult for him have a normal life. Sebastian walked towards the counter watching Sarah walking back and forth working her hardest. In the back of his mind, he could still hear his mother’s words. Return home. Return home? He couldn’t return home. Not now. He couldn’t go back home empty handed. He couldn’t leave D. C. without finishing what he was put into. He was going to beat Doctor Curiosity and then he was going to win. After that, he was going to return home. On the other hand, he didn’t want to widen the gap between his parents and himself. He began to feel like maybe he should just call it quits. After all, it’s not like anyone is giving him a chance. Maybe that’s why he’s there to see Sarah. Sarah was the first, and only, person to make him feel at ease in D.C. She was the only thing that kept him calm during these tumultuous weeks. Maybe what Sebastian needed to calm him was a reason to stay in D.C. other than fighting. “What time are you getting off work?” Sebastian asked Sarah leaning on the counter. Sarah turned around a bit surprised. She hadn’t seen Sebastian for a good amount of days, despite talking with him. “In about two hours,” she responded. “You want to do something after work?” Sebastian asked. “I, uh…I can’t,” Sarah apologized. “I got a lot of work from medical school to catch up on. Between college and work, I hardly have enough time to work on my schoolwork.” “Then let’s make it a study date,” Sebastian suggested. “I may not be in medical school like you, but I might know some things.” Sarah smiled a little bit. “If you can tell me how to treat a broken jaw, you can help.” Sebastian thought about it for a moment. But, he couldn’t feign wisdom that much longer, so he confessed. “Give him some aspirin?” he asked moderately joking. “Ok, so maybe I am over my head here. But come on, just a couple of hours. I promise you’ll get your work done.” Sarah continued to do her work while trying to get Sebastian to leave. “No, I’m sorry I can’t. I have to finish up here.” “Something wrong?” “Yeah, I don’t think this is going to work out,” she explained. “What the study date?” “No us,” she revealed to him. “You seem like a nice guy. I mean you are a nice guy, but it’s more than that. We’re too different, from different places.” “It’d be boring as hell if we were all alike.” Sarah sighed. “You know, I know that. I heard about what happened at the gym.” “Is this because of that?” Sebastian asked. “You can’t possibly believe that’s how I am?!” “And why not?!” she whispered loudly. “You’re already about to break right now and we haven’t even done anything! You attacked a helpless man in public. How the hell do I know you won’t attack someone when you’re alone with them?” Sebastian rolled his eyes. He let his anger get the best of him with [name withheld], and now it seemed like it was going to cost him more. “I won’t. I’m not that kind of guy. That guy I attacked was on his last lifeline. We both know each other from long ago. He was trying to get into my life and business and I had enough of that.” “So, what you are saying is that you didn’t attack a stranger?” she asked. “You attacked someone you knew? That doesn’t make this any better.” “Look Sarah…” “Sebastian, just leave now,” Sarah begged. “Please.” Sebastian sighed. “Bye.” Sebastian walked out of there in angst and disgust. He didn’t know that attacking Jeff like that was going to cost him this much. In retrospect, maybe he should have waited for them to be alone. No, maybe he shouldn’t have attacked him at all. Jeff isn’t the enemy here. No yet. Doctor Curiosity is the enemy at hand. Sebastian walked out the door clenching his fist. The one thing he was sure of could give him a reason to stay in D. C, had left him. No, he had left it. He was solely responsible for Sarah’s fear. He attacked someone in public and it was only natural that Sarah was afraid. Sarah watched Sebastian leave the store. She sighed in relief. She knew that Sebastian probably wasn’t going to hurt her, but she couldn’t take that chance. She was right. They were both different people. They grew up in different parts of the world and their lifestyles could never intertwine. Awry [name withheld] limped into his apartment still feeling the effects of the sharpshooter. If Sebastian were to lock the move on Doctor Curiosity, then maybe he could win after all. That’s how Jeff feels at least. He may not be a wrestler, but he was still in very good shape. The submission move that Sebastian applied to him was devastating and after a day, he still could feel the effects of it. Jeff even cracked a smile when he realized that. Sebastian was full with anger and hostility. He had so much fire and determination in his eyes. Every word that people said about him losing to Doctor Curiosity seemed to fuel him. If Sebastian could continue to use the negatives as a positive and apply the move to the good doctor, he could come out victorious. Not only would he be victorious, but with 40-1 odds, everyone else would be very victorious as well. Much more than Sebastian could ever realize they would be. Sebastian was growing wiser with each round. He was growing more knowledgeable, wiser, stronger, faster, and alert. He could no longer be that naïve kid who first stepped into the ring with Inphino Blitz all those rounds ago. However, he still had a long way to go. His blind ambitions still made him totally oblivious to the truth. Jeff knew that. He also realized that Sebastian could not be made to keep the wool over his eyes for long. After the incident yesterday, Jeff had to be careful. Sebastian snapped. This time, it might be Jeff who snaps. “What the hell are you doing here?” Jeff scolded Ryan. Ryan Howell was laid out on the couch making out with a sexy blonde. She was in her mid twenties, about five feet five, one hundred thirty-five pounds, and had nice fun bags. Ryan Howell ignored him and continued to make merry with his new friend. Jeff threw his jacket on the hanger and limped his way towards his room. “Stupid fucking kid,” he muttered loudly. “So pathetic, he can’t even manage to get his own room.” Ryan pushed the blonde off him for a second. He heard Jeff and, more importantly, saw Jeff. “What is wrong with you, old man?” Ryan joked. “It looks like your arthritis is starting to kick in again. Or maybe you got assaulted? Don’t be jealous of me, man. It’s not my fault I can attract members of the opposite sex, unlike you.” Jeff turned around and walked, slightly, back towards Ryan. “Get the fuck out of here. I don’t care who your father is. You have no business here. We both know who your father is and there is no excuse for you living here. Get the fuck out, now!” Ryan just laughed and tongue-kissed the blonde to make Jeff feel awkward. “It’s so cute to watch you defend yourself. Now tell me what happened to your leg?” Jeff was about to tell him that Sebastian attacked him. That instinct quickly left him, however, as he knew that telling that to Ryan could make things worst. Jeff was man enough to leave Sebastian alone. However, he knew that if Ryan learned of Sebastian’s newfound anger, it could spell trouble. “I had an accident. I tripped and fell down some stairs. Now you know, and now get the fuck out of my place!” “If I didn’t know any better,” Ryan interrupted. “I would say you were lying. Didn’t you get attacked or something?” Ryan smirked at him and then continued to make out with his blonde friend. Jeff had enough. He picked up a vase and lunged it towards Ryan. Ryan Howell, despite his beers, saw the vase and ducked just in time for the vase to fly over his head. The blonde gasped, and both she and Ryan glared at Jeff as the vase shattered against the wall into many pieces. “What the fuck is your problem?” Ryan growled. “Yeah!” The blonde chimed in. Jeff took a deep breath and pointed towards the door. “Get the fuck out now, Ryan. You have no business being in D. C. You have no business being in my apartment. You have no business being here at all. You’re a fucking has-been who’s exploiting other people. Get out, now!” Ryan Howell got up from the couch, pulling the blonde with him. “Come baby, it seems we aren’t wanted here,” he said. “You better watch yourself, Jeff. I’m not in the mood to play. If you think what I’ve done in the past is bad, you haven’t seen anything yet.” “Just leave.” Ryan turned to his friend who was shaken up. “Don’t worry babe, that old man’s just delusional. I got a condo in town, and it’s just big enough for the two us.” Underestimation Sebastian walked back towards his suite that had been provided to him by the GTT Committee. He had been walking all night, not wanting to do anything with the public. He didn’t want to catch a bus or a cab. After the falling out he had with Sarah, he was still angry. He wasn’t just angry with her. He was angry with himself. He was angry with his parents. He was angry with Jeff. He was angry with the committee. He was angry with everyone. This had been a terrible week for him and it didn’t look like it was about to get better. As he continued to approach the suite and, next to it, the gym, he could catch glimpses of people staring at him and whispering. He overheard some of the bystanders whisper and point to him. Overhear them saying how he was the kid who attacked a helpless old man. Overhear them saying that he should be in jail. Overhear them saying that he is going to get what he deserves against Doctor Curiosity. Sebastian tried to filter out those comments from his mind, but their impact was just too profound. In a way they were right. He had no business attacking Jeff, no matter what the cause was. He was lucky that Jeff didn’t call the police on him, or anything of the sort. And with the odds 40-1 against him, he was lucky to even get one vote his way. Doctor Curiosity was going to be his most difficult challenge to date. Ironically, he was the last thing on his mind. Sebastian muttered slightly under his breath and clenched his fist as he approached the gym. The people who passed him all made sure they walked far away from him. He wasn’t the cute kid doing the impossible anymore. Now, he was seen as a wreck and a dangerous one at that. They all saw what he could do. What he had no problem doing. Sebastian grew angrier and angrier as he watched them look at him in disgust. He grew more in hatred as he could overhear them insulting him. He needed to get away from the streets. He was finally at the gym and walked inside. He didn’t even bother to change into his workout clothes. He didn’t bother to check in. He didn’t bother to clean the equipment before he used. He didn’t bother to stretch before he would begin to exercise. He just didn’t bother to care at all. He got onto the bench press machine and began to lift the weights. Just like that with no warm-ups. The gym was moderately empty tonight, but those were in the gym kept their distances. Sebastian kept lifting the weights over his chest, up and down. He was full of anger, and if he were to continue to be careless, he could be in bigger trouble. He tried to drain out the whispers of the crowd, but they were too much. That’s the kid who attacked that old man, isn’t it? [name withheld] had it coming. He was becoming a nuisance. He was becoming too involved with Sebastian’s life and routine. Everything Jeff promised him was becoming to be seen as a lie. Sebastian knew that, and he realized that there was much more to the GTT then Jeff was leading on. How could he attack an old man? That kid is a disgrace. He should be in jail. Jeff would not sink that low. Sebastian knew that Jeff would rather have them work out their differences then resort to the police. Sebastian never made intently attacked anyone. He attacked [name withheld] to prove a point. All these people are wrong. They’re judging him based on one bad mistake he did. Typical. He’s going to get what he deserves against Doctor Curiosity. Sebastian snarled and continued to pump the weights. Doctor Curiosity. All week, all he heard about Curiosity was how he could never be beat. He didn’t stand a chance. Doctor Curiosity was just too good for him. He was lucky to get this far. But now, his luck had run out. The Doctor was going to run circles around him. He was going to use his superior intellectual and devious mind to take the win and remove the four-leaf clover from Sebastian’s pocket. Yeah, that punk doesn’t stand a chance. Can you believe he’s a 40-1 underdog? He should be a 100-1 underdog. There’s no way he can beat a man like Doctor Curiosity. “Shut up,” Sebastian muttered as he continued to lift the weights. “Shut up. Shut up. Shut up. Just fucking shut up. I can beat Doctor Curiosity. I know I can. I can beat that overrated son of a bitch.” I don’t think it’s going to work out between us. “No,” Sebastian muttered. “I’m sorry Sarah. You have to believe me. I’m not that kind of guy. I would never hurt anyone.” You attacked someone you know. In public, no less! What makes me thing you won’t hurt anyone else? Sarah was right. Sebastian was right. The one thing Sebastian thought he had in D.C. that would convince him to stay had left him. No, actually he had left her. In his stupidity, and shortsightedness he had let his anger get the best of him. Now, not only did it cost him his reputation with the audiences, but also it cost him any chance at Sarah. Sebastian put the weight back down and sat up, exhausted. His anger and adrenaline could only take him so far. He sat on the bench catching his breath and wondering just what the hell he was going to do. There was nothing left for him in D. C. The committee had already sent him a reprimand and fined him for his attack. His mother had scolded him to return home. Sarah left him. His upcoming match with Doctor Curiosity was a joke. He wasn’t going to win. Sebastian sighed. There’s nothing left for him here, maybe he should just leave. Your father and I want you to return home immediately. Now that’s an idea. A problem Jeff popped open a bottle of Sam Adams and leaned back on his couch to watch some the late night news. But before he could enjoy himself, a knock at the door was heard. It was a loud and obnoxious knock. It was repetitive too. Jeff scoffed in disgust and slowly pulled himself up. He limped towards the door and opened the door. He didn’t have time to greet his visitors. Ross Howell and Jonathan Sams both invited themselves in and brushed past Jeff and took a seat by his dining table, opening their own bottles of Sam Adams. Jeff growled and forced his way to his unexpected and unwanted company. “So nice of you to drop in,” he greeted them sarcastically. “Cut the bullshit,” Ross snarled. “I understand that you ran my son out of here.” “Did he squeal to you?” Ross glared at him. “No, let’s just say I’m aware of these things. Like how you got that limp in your step, for instance.” Jeff looked down at his leg and took a seat. “It’s nothing.” Jonathan grabbed Jeff by his collar and pulled him off his seat. “Nothing? You stupid bitch, you almost ruined this for everyone. You are damn lucky no one decided to press charges and launch an investigation into the tournament. I’ve gotten hate mail from people all over the city asking why I got one of my competitors attacking helpless citizens.” Jeff yanked Jonathan’s arm away and gathered himself. “Don’t you put your hands on me. I was trying to make sure Sebastian realized where he was. He came this far, and I didn’t want him getting cocky or careless. Not this round. Not against Doctor Curiosity.” “Heh,” Ross chimed in. “The punk kid is always cocky. He never knows when to quit. I think our run with him is going to end this round.” “What’s the matter?” Jeff asked. “You don’t think he can beat Doctor Curiosity?” Jonathan shook his head. “Beat him? If he were to beat him, he would need a lot of luck, and help. We can provide the help. Remember last round, when our good friend Steve made sure things went our way? He can do the same with the good doctor. Just imagine winning on 40-1 odds. We are going to make a shitload of money.” “I don’t think it’s going to work,” Jeff chimed in. “The Doctor is a very careful man. He’ll expect something. We better hope Sebastian realizes how important this is.” “Like I said, our fun with him is over,” Ross reiterated. Jeff shook his head. “I can’t believe you’re giving up on him this easily. As his coach, I thought you would realize all the adversary he’s overcome.” “I realize that,” Ross explained. “But I am not talking about that. Yes, the doctor is a very formidable opponent. But there is one opponent here that is too much for him to overcome. Himself.” “What?” Jonathan slammed his half-empty bottle down. “There’s something’s that come up. I got word from my intern that someone was looking for our Sebastian. As it turns out, it was his parents. What is important now, is that my intern, just an hour ago, gave me Sebastian’s resignation letter. He’s quit the tournament.” Jeff spit the beer after hearing the news. Sebastian had quit? “He what?!” “That’s right,” Jonathan said handing him the letter. Jeff took the letter and read it. That was indeed Sebastian’s signature. He couldn’t believe it. Jeff stood up from the seat and reached for his coat. “Where are you going?” “To find Sebastian and talk some sense into him.” It’s Never Over Sebastian Moraco surveyed the room around him. Everything was clean and picked up. The bed was made. The towels were folded. The carpet had been vacuumed. All that was left was to finish packing up. He had handed the committee his resignation a little over an hour ago. Now, he was getting ready to catch his flight in three hours. Sebastian opened up his second and last suitcase, having already packed his first. He stared down at the empty suitcase wondering if he was doing the right thing. He looked down at the emptiness of it and felt that same kind of emptiness inside him. Was he really quitting? Or was he just doing the right thing and listening to his parents? Sebastian sighed and began to fold the rest of his clothes into his suitcase. Then a knock was heard on the door. Sebastian ignored it. However, that knock kept on coming loudly and repeatedly. Sebastian tossed his last shirt into the suitcase and went to open the door. Just as soon as he opened the door, Jeff limped into the room. “What the hell do you want?” Sebastian demanded. “You want me to really break your leg this time?” “Cut the crap,” Jeff replied. “I know you quit. Why the hell would you quit now?” “Because there is nothing left for me here.” “Nothing? What about pride and respect? You are in the fucking fifth round of this tournament. As an amateur, to make to the second round is unheard of. Yet, you made it to the fifth round against possibly the best fighter left! This is a hell of an opportunity.” “You want to talk about opportunity?” Sebastian asked. “Let’s talk about losing it. I lost my chance for the Olympics. I lost my chance for redemption. I lost my anger. I lost my chance with Sarah. I’m losing my parent’s respect. As far as I know, I’m losing my chances at the world championships. You can keep your pride and respect. There’s nothing left here.” “What about that second chance we talked about?” Jeff asked. “What about getting your spot back on the team? If you quit now, you will lose your chance forever.” “I already did. I don’t think any of the coaches are going to be calling me. You know it too.” “That’s not true,” Jeff lied. “They’re still scouting you. You can’t quit.” “Just get the hell out of here.” “I can’t.” “Get the fuck out now!” Sebastian screamed. Jeff shook his head. “I won’t let you quit.” “Get out now, or you will regret it.” “No…” Sebastian knocked him down with a hard right hand. He pulled him up from his collar and kneed him in the gut. “I told you to leave me alone,” he snarled as he dragged Jeff towards the door. “I know there is no such thing as the world championships for me. There is nothing left for me here. Now get the hell out.” Sebastian threw Jeff out of his suite and resumed to packing up his suitcase. He folded the last shirt he had and sighed. After all this, he was going to return home. He felt a lot of things. He was angry. He was sad. He was happy. He wanted to be back home, but he also didn’t want to quit. The truth is there was nothing left for him. Sebastian was about to zip up his bag until he saw something on the coffee table that caught his eye. It was a stack of newspapers, and the daily report that the committee gave to him. However, the thing that caught him off guard the most was the page on the fifth round. All the pairing were there, including his own. He stared at the section on his match and couldn’t look away at the subtitle. Doctor Curiosity Vs Sebastian Moraco The good doctor is expected to advance without too much trouble. Sebastian clenched his fists. That was enough. The committee didn’t give him a chance. His friends didn’t give him a chance. The media didn’t give him a chance. No one did. He was sick and tired of hearing how unbeatable the doctor was. He was sick and tired of hearing how the doctor was the best thing since sliced bread. He was sick and tired of hearing how he fluked his way into the bracket finals and now would lose to Doctor Curiosity. He was sick of hearing of Doctor Curiosity period. He picked up the report and ripped it into pieces. He then began to rampage through the other newspapers, ripping them up as he read about his match. His thoughts were becoming intertwined with the views of the people around him. Doctor Curiosity is a 40-1 favorite. Sebastian Moraco’s luck has finally run out. Doctor Curiosity expected to advance without too much trouble. Betting on Moraco will yield a huge payday, but don’t count your eggs before they hatch. You’re a dangerous man, Sebastian. Who the hell does he think he is attacking a helpless old man? He’s going to get what he deserves against Doctor Curiosity. He won’t beat Doctor Curiosity. It’s not going to work out between us. Sebastian stared at the ripped pieces of paper he left on the floor. There was one more page that needed his undivided attention. He picked up the paper and read the caption under it. “Sebastian Moraco has no chance of winning,” he read out loud. He smiled a bit and sighed. Rather than rip the paper up he just let it drop. He walked to his bed and picked up his suitcase. For a good moment, he stood there wondering what to do. Was he really going to quit? Or was this just a phase? He reached for the zipper and slowly began to close his suitcase. He looked at the page again. Again he repeated those words. “Sebastian Moraco has no chance of winning,” he repeated to himself. He lifted the suitcase off the bed. It was time for him to go home. Wasn’t it? Return Home. He couldn’t return home, could he? He managed to get to round five of the Prime Time Central’s GT Tournament. He managed to beat everyone who said he couldn’t beat. He was in the bracket finals. There was no way he could quit. Even if his attempts with Sarah failed, he couldn’t quit. Not now. Go home? Not a chance in hell. Sebastian turned the suitcase upside down and dumped his clothes back on the floor. He was not going to home. He wasn’t going to quit. Not now. He’s come too far to quit. If he did, he would prove everyone right that he couldn’t defeat Doctor Curiosity. Go home? Never. Sebastian picked up the report again and read it loud. This time he read it with gusto. “Sebastian Moraco has NO chance of winning,” he read aloud. He smiled and slowly ripped the page in half. He wasn’t going to quit. He wasn’t going to go home. He was going to stay. He was going to fight Doctor Curiosity and win. Doctor Curiosity Vs Sebastian Moraco I’m going to win.
