Is that new math, or what?

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 Christian Zenith is what? Yes, he's the current Global World Champion, but he's also one of the biggest sanctimonious assholes I've ever seen. He tries to use facts, and just like a politician, bends them and portrays them wrong. He's wrong on quite a few things. Let's go ahead and hit the real facts. Here we'll recreate some of the ones that he uses that can just be slashed to pieces in a heart beat. 1.Danielle Northsky has never defeated any members of Hunting Grounds, ever. Answer: Wrong bucko. The 2005 Mistress of the Mat, and a person who routinly scores higher then Zenith on buy rates and the top 10… Dawn MacFarlane was in the six person convergance for the Platinum, Cruiser and Television Titles. Are you that much of a moron you forgot who technically took Dawn's Cruiser Title from her and merged it into the Platinum Title. Dawn and I went toe to toe a few times that night, and in the end… it was me who won it. I won all three titles, and left with them. I'm the one who handed the other two belts to the Committee's rep, Brandi Sights after the match. Your selective memory is pretty poor there Zenith. Oh, let's not forget the tag team match between Shadow and Ambrosi, against me and Blaise McClancy. That match ended in a draw. What's that mean? I neither won nor lost there. So I actually have one thing over you… But you see Mr. Zenith: You're a living example of the moto that I live by. In adversity, such as all of the losses you showed the world that I've done… you learn and you do better. 2.Danielle Northsky has never successfully defended a GW Championship. You know, the white man has a word for this statement. Bullshit. I had one successful defense. And there were 5 other people in the ring, and three belts on the line. And in that match I beat Mike Biddle, the number one contender for the International title. I beat Dawn MacFarlane the Cruiserweight Champion. And I beat someone that you've never beaten… Emoticon. The Soul of the Poet has one of his few losses at my hands. I'll admit that I pinned Elia Smith, the TV champion to win the match. But that was not after securing the fact that both Dawn and Emoticon were not able to stop my pinfall attempt. I may be a rookie, but "The Irish Bastard" didn't train no dolt in the ring. I had the ring presense to take them out of the way, and then go for the kill. Then again, Mr. Zenith.. I'm not some little boy who thinks the size or quantity of my 'toys' define me as a person. But let me point out one thing there Mr. Zenith. While you've held your second Global World Title for over a month now, you've NEVER tried to defend it. So how do we know that you're going to not do worse then I did as the Platinum Champion there? 3.Danielle Northsky has never won the GW World Championship. Again, why does this matter? I've not been in Global for more then 5 pay per view cycles. But just last Evolution, after lossing the Platinum Title the pay per view before, the committee gave me a shot at gaining the contendership for your title. Yes, I lost to Jake Hix. Let me remind you though that anyone can beat anyone else at any time. But let's use Zenith Logic here. a. He, Zenith, lost to Emoticon at Devi's Night b. I beat Emoticon in the title convergance. c. He beat me. d. Hix will beat Zenith for the Global World Title when he gets a chance to gain that shot he earned. Isn't that how it goes Christian? By those rights, shouldn't you be worried that Jake Hix is coming down on your to take your precisious little toy away? 4.Danielle Northsky has a nice ass. I can't and won't refute that. Then again it sells more magazines then yours does. 5.Danielle Northsky likes to talk about her breasts to keep you from talking about her total incompetence in the ring. Oh, this is even more laughable. Mr. Zenith, if I'm so incompetant in the ring then why is it that the Committee gave me as shot at gaining a contendership for your ill gotten gain. But I'd prefer to fight fire with fire. Like, if you think I'm so incompetant, then why is it that you've offered me a spot in your 'elitist' group known as the Hunting Grounds, not once… but twice. Is it because of my ass? I mean, other then two muscle bound goons, the Hunting Grounds was you, Valentine, Shadow, Windsor and MacFarlane. A guy trying to surround himself with a bunch of girls? One of those girls supposedly his girlfriend. Yes, you bring up that I lost to Valentine. But that was… gosh, months ago. That's not last Evolution when I almost won a shot at your title. But here is one cold, hard fact. Zenith is the champion. But he didn't pin the existing champion to get it. But he is the top of Global. He is the champion. He has everything to loose. I've everything to gain. If I loose to Christian Zenith, it's a drop in the bucket. I'm the fucking underdog here. "Oh, poor Dani. You'll do better next time." But if he looses? Then he's lost to a girl, who is a model, with big boobs, and… they know that he's exactly what people like Emoticon and others have been saying about him. He's nothing but a paper weight champion. A facade. All false bravado. You're not Valentine. You're definitely not Shadow. You're by far not MacFarlane. You're just Christian Zenith. So what do I have to fear? And something else… Your math sucks. Just like your sanctimonious attitude and personality. Which were two of the reasons why I turned down your offer to join you.
