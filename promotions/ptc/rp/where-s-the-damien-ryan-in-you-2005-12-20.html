Where's the Damien Ryan in you?

20 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-20 We see all kinds of characters in wrestling today, may be it professional or in real life. There's unforgettable characters like The Ultimate Warrior, Stone Cold Steve Austin, Hulk Hogan, HBK, and the much more colorful like Papa Shango, Goldust, Jeff Jarret…there's so many. We see in our game there's chacaters like Ember, Tessa Windsor, Shanty Jones, Drake Coleman, AWS Man, Dallas Reeves, all popular names. A lot of thought goes into these characters. Then we see Damien Ryan, "Mr. I Don't Give a Fuck". His personality of the ultimate low on earth, scum…if he wasnt so good at wrestling, he's be nothing but a god damn deadbeat, I won't lie. I would never want to be his friend, and I wouldn't blame every single other person on earth if they felt the same way. Maybe that's why I like him so much. It's that uglier half of me, I'm so damn nice to everyone in real life, my family, girlfriend, friends, even just regular people I don't know. Some have seen that asshole side of me, but it's quite rare. But then there's that Damien Ryan in me, that low, shallow self that is held deep inside. That point where I just care anymore. The apathy that drives me inside because I can't take anymore of the bullshit that life throws in my path. So before we all see the superstar today, think to yourself…Where's the Damien Ryan in you? Where's that bastard self of yours hiding? Is it that part of you who cannot stand this crappy government in the United States, is it that part of you that cannot stand terrorists ruining your cities, that part of you that wants to tell your girlfriend or boyfriend to get over themselves? Give your parents the middle finger because they're pissing you off. Kick your friend because he's being an absolute prick. If he's being a Damien Ryan, why can't you be one. It seems like there are so many Damien Ryan's in this world…the real question is…do we annihilate all of them, or unleash them? ********************** [The screen opens up on the side of an interstate highway, the day is cold and murky. All we can see is clouds in the sky and the bare trees on each side of highway. Some cars drive by, on the side of the road is a navy blue Ford 150. We can see the front driver's side tire is flat. We see a man walking around with a spare and we can see it is HATE superstar Damien Ryan. He seems aggrivated that he needs to change his tire, on his pick-up no less. Suddenly we see a police cruiser put its blues on and pulls up behind the big pick-up. Ryan looks back while changing the tire, then rolls his eyes. A black police officer gets out of the cruiser and walks up to Ryan.] Officer: Everything under control? Ryan: Yes, everything is under control, do I look like somebody who needs help? Officer: I'm sorry if I bothered you, but yes, you are on the side of a highway changing a tire to a rather large pick up truck, I thought you could've used some help. Ryan: Well thanks, but I got this. [Ryan starts lifting the fork up and down to lift the truck. The truck starts rising, then all of a sudden the truck starts moving fowards a little and falls off the forklift and hard onto the pavement.] Ryan: Ah, fuck! [The officer smiles then moves towards the driver's side door and looks into the window. Then he smiles and laughs.] Officer: It could of helped if you had put the emergency brake on. [Ryan looks at him with a pissed off face.] Ryan: No shit, thanks a lot. All you police officers have a keen sense of sarcasm, I've found. [Ryan remembering the officer's attitude when he was arrested the other day.] Officer: Oh yeah, really? Well maybe you should watch your mouth. [Ryan stands up] Ryan: Oh yeah, [dusting his hands] Maybe you should shut yours. Officer: What the hell did you just say? [Ryan opens his car door and applies the e-brake. Then goes back to work on the forklift.] Officer: I'm serious about watching you're mouth, and this is your warning son, you can be easily arrested for harrassing a police officer. [Ryan gets up and kicks the spare tire and raises his voice.] Ryan: Let me tell you something, off-i-cer. I've been arrested already this week, thank you. One of your miserable colleages decided to make my night by dragging me in because I didn't have my license on me, when all he could have let me done was go home and get it to show him. I am an honest man, would I be telling you all this. Officer: Probably not. Ryan: Probably not is right, now please, you're holding me up, I have somewhere to be. Officer: I don't know if you'll get there with that attitude. Ryan: You know, probably not, all of you seem like you're the same. Maybe you should handcuff me and bring me down "downtown", "cook me and book me". You guys got nothing better to do but make sure you hide behind billboards to catch speeders, or dress as regular guys and sit around waiting for somebody to J-walk as you sit on a park bench eating Dunkin Donuts. Officer: Sterotyping….not bad, what else can you do, son? Can you do your ABC's? How about racial slurs, are you going to call me a nigger next? Ryan: If you piss me off enough. Officer: Christ, I have seen some really stubborn men in my life, man but you my friend are by far the worst? What's the matter, the old lady won't have sexy with you no more? She making you limp? Or is it that tool of yours isn't the size it used to be when you guys had to your honeymoon. Ryan: Sorry champ, we're not all savages and are hung like a moose. Officer: What did you just call me? Ryan: Do me a favor a leave? Officer: Maybe I just do myself the favor, I really didn't want to arrest anybody today, I'm not really in the mood. I came over here to help you, you prick. Ryan: Well then leave, go back to your pen, pig. Officer: Unbelieveable. Should I take my handcuffs out now, or should I commit police brutality on your ass first? [Ryan stands back up. Then chuckles.] Ryan: Come on now, you know who I am, you really think you beat a professional like me? Officer: A professional? Ryan: Yeah, come on. Officer: I'm sorry meatstick but I have no clue who the hell you are…I mean I did see some roadkill back a couple miles, but I didn't think roadkill came back to life. Ryan: You don't know Damien Ryan of HATE, PTC? Officer: Primetime Central? Hate Wrestling? Ryan: Yeah, Damien Ryan. Officer: I know Seymore Almasy, Digital Mortality, Dane Rennier…No Damien Ryan comes to mind. Ryan: You've to be….fucking….kidding….me. Officer: You know what, I don't like your mouth. I'm going to give you one last chance, I'm going to call in to check your plate, when I'm gone think about changing that loser attitude of yours. Ryan: I'm not a loser buddy, I'm the next winner of the PTC GTT5 tournament! Officer: Oh yeah, who you facing this round, champ? Ryan: Uhhh….Shanty Jones I think his name is? Officer: Shanty Jones? Ha! Isn't that the winner of last years GTT? Ryan: I don't know. Officer: Check your books nitwit, I think it is. Ryan: I didn't know that. Officer: I think I'm just going to let you off due to complete pity, you're going to lose, not only because he is better than you, but because you are a complete moron. Ryan: You know what…..why don't you just kiss my---^(^*&%(^$($^^&)*()&(^(* [the feed cuts out] ***************************** Ah, poor Damien Ryan….will he ever learn? Did he ever get to where he was going? Who knows, will he even make it to his match against Shanty Jones? Or will he be in court? Maybe this time they make him to go to anger management or order him to a psychiatrist. Is it good to unleash the Damien Ryan in yourself? It may at some times, probably best when you are alone and in your room, when all you have a is a pillow to punch. But here's a lesson for us all. Don't be like Damien Ryan, it seems like everyone is his punching pillow. He doesn't see anything but black and white, obviously he couldn't see the blue to realize he was talking to a police officer. So really is Damien Ryan a colorful characters, maybe not. Will he be remembered like Andre the Giant, Freddie Blassie, or Sabu? Or even Seymore Almasy, Jason Warr, or Brian Arson? Probably not. Damien Ryan is a fool, an absolute moron, and anybody can tell that. Psychologists will say he's stuck in Terrible Two Syndrome. He's as stubborn and as smart as a two year old. Makes sense, I wouldn't deny it. Do we all want to me like that? No. Hell, Damien Ryan will probably get coal in his stockings this year by Santa Clause. The poor guy is too busy being a hero, selfish and full of thorns. He's a miserable idiot who will never learn…hopefully from this we will. Where's the Damien Ryan in you?
