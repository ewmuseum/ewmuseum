Enter: The Forsaken One

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Fellas hide your wives! Hahahahahaha… I'm coming. Not that way, you sick freaks! Back on topic… Champions, hide your title belts! Why hide them? You can only keep them for so long until I win them. Bolt your chairs to the floor! I'll mutilate myself and you with them… He'll enjoy it. Every second of it. Don't piss on his brother's grave. You'll regret it. Everyone set your Timex watches, because in four days, one hour, and seventeen minutes, darkness will reign over the GTT tournament. [Fade to a scene of a man synchronizing his Rolex watch to match the date that GTT is taking place.] Fuck Rolex!!!! I said Timex!!! Goddammit!!! Why can't people get this shit right? What's so hard about the concept of synchronizing your Timex watch? Timex- It takes a lickin' and keeps on tickin'. What makes it so hard to understand the word Timex? Are you sacks of shit out there in corporate America too damn stupid to know the difference between a Timex and a Rolex? While I'm at it, I want to ask you all about your cigarette policies. If you know they're going to kill people, and you warn people that it will kill them, then why the fuck do you sell them? Corporate America, you suck! Ahem! Earth to announcer… Oh! Sorry, where was I? Oh yes… He's coming to GTT… I am the man upon whose shoulders society places their burdens upon. He's going to make an impact the likes of which has never been seen before. I am the man who will purge the world of its sins. SINNERS!!! YOU ARE ALL SINNERS!!! I am a martyr for society's dysfunction. He does it all for you, you ungrateful bastards! Is THIS how you repay him? By ignoring simple instructions to synchronize your TIMEX watches? Who the fuck said you could set your Rolexes?! Rolexes can burn in Hell!!! So can all you corporate assholes!!! Um… I'm a 'Face'. You can't be trashing people in my hype pieces or promos, dipshit. Oh… I didn't realize… I'm sorry. That's okay. I know you got carried away in the moment. Alright, finish your little speech or whatever. I am the Forsaken one. You forsook him! Oh, and by the way, 'forsook' is a word. According to the dictionary it means to leave altogether or abandon. Obviously, this is the past tense of forsake. Just a little information for all you high school dropouts out there. I am Axel fuckin' Action. He's Axel fuckin' Action. I will show you all why chicks go whack for Action Packed! [At that time, a movie posteresque picture fades in. It shows Axel Action in a 'knuckle cracking' position. It reads, 'Enter: The Forsaken One'.] THAT'S RIGHT! ENTER HIM!!! Wait, that sounded wrong… Ah, just shut up. You're making yourself sound dumb. [End Transmission.]
