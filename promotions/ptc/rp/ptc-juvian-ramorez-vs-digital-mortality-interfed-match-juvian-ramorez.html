Juvian Ramorez Vs Digital Mortality (Interfed Match)

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Puerto Rican wars Prologue What a week it has been for our enigma known as Juvian Ramorez, not only did he go through a lot of pain and suffering as he fought Swift Payne at Realm but he was also injured in a accident which was away from Lights Out Wrestling. However Swift Payne showed Juvian that he still has to improve if he is to carry on with this extreme title belt. What I mean by this is that swift took nearly all of Juvian’s offence and still never stayed down for the three count. It was only when Psymon interfered into the match that allowed Juvian to regain control of the match and find an ending. After that match happened last week Juvian left the arena straight away and placed him in isolation for twenty four hours to get over the emotional state that he was in, for those un-aware his mind set was taken off by the death of his late brother Marko. A little history for those who are unaware, Marko and Juvian both grew up around wrestling in Mexico, Tijuana to be more precise. Marko’s dream was always to make it into the profession wrestling business as a tag team with Juvian, however Juvian’s dreams wasn’t that strong as he had his music to focus on. But this all changed when the two brothers won a match when they were in their late teens. The emotion on Marko’s face was enough for Juvian to help his brother make it into the business. They travelled for years up and down the independent circuit stopping off at each and every show they could find hoping to get booked one more time until they found the right federation, but as they say for every success in life there should be some bad news and that would come a few years later when Marko was diagnosed with cancer. And last week in Puerto Rico Marko Javier Ramorez died in his sleep, not painfully but still he passed away, Juvian was told this immediately after the match. Looking forward to this weeks match Juvian now faces a battle with a Puerto Rican, however its his long time friend Digital Mortality. Now Juvian and Digi have had quite a past, at one time Digi gave Juvian some training lessons in another promotional federation called UWF. They have also squared off in a charity match over in a now bankrupt business called International wrestling alliance, where for the record Juvian Won. So this will be the first television taping between these two men, and its certainly going to be a battle and a half, and only realm is the place to see it. And how is our lights out wrestling extreme champion going to prepare for this long and crucial match, well we find him today just sat on his balcony of his hotel room. End Prologue As the cold November rain falls onto my palm its colder then it has ever been this year, the capsules of rain start to form a little puddle on my hand before I turn my hand over to watch the puddle form into a stream and poor onto the floor. One more drop falls onto my palm as I make a grimacing look on my face before once more allowing a puddle to form, the water runs through the cracks in my hands before once again falling to the floor. I have been sitting out here quite a while today; to be honest with you I have spent a lot of my time outside on this balcony this week, mostly its just clearing my thoughts of Marko. I won’t lie to you all, the loss of Marko has hit me very hard and I must try to re-focus for my match this week with Digi. With the loss of Marko really hit me hard as I was speaking on the phone to him just days before. I remember him talking to me about how I stole his finishing manoeuvre, you know the one I call deepest fate. You see I will admit I first saw Marko do that move and I had forgotten about it only till many weeks back when I was watching old tapes of our matches on the independent scene when I was looking for new moves to bring to my arsenal to beat Psymon with. And I saw it; he named the move “spinning fate” but I changed the name. After a long argument on the phone we decided that I would be allowed to use the move in return for him to get a shot at my extreme title. Knowing this would never happen at the time I agreed, now that promise that I made to him will forever be open, for Marko will never get that chance, he will never get the chance to face his older brother Juvian Ramorez one more time. On a personal note, I know that Marko wouldn’t stand a chance if he fought me today, I would beat him hands down but I have only him to thank for allowing me to use the move that would beat swift Payne last week. So that’s why when I hit that move later on in life I will forever dedicate my match to my long late and great brother. On a lighter note last week I celebrated samhian which to those un-aware is a celebration of us Wicca’s known to mud-bloods as Halloween. Now as I ever look into my future us Wicca’s don’t have a lot to celebrate but I like to celebrate when I can, samhian or the third feast is a massive celebration and it was the last meal that I would ever eat with my brother by my side. I remember the meal not being so nice because I am not a fan of meat but its tradition. I remember sitting side by side with my brother at an old oak table at his house opposite us where his family, his wife Tarja and his two kids Yalori and Jukka. Most of the convocation we had that day was talking about how late I was and that I had to rush off to the arena because I was late for realm. And to be honest the front office don’t like it when you are late, never mind the fact that it was my first main event of what I am sure is to be many but nevertheless it was my first so showing up late defiantly isn’t one of the best first impressions to make. So I shook my brother’s hand firmly with a mighty grip before embracing him into my chest with a loving hug. I hugged my two nephews before looking into Tarja’s eyes and giving her a loving kiss on the lips before heading out of the door for a three hour drive to Philadelphia. I have contemplated over a thousand times would he have still died if I stayed with him, but the answer always seems to point to yes, was there anything that could have been done, that answer always points to no as well. I hate being in a place where you cannot judge the outcome or do anything to predict what will happen. The lack of control in a situation is a nightmare thought and a nightmare feeling of what I felt when I received that phone call. Let me tell you just how I found out the news and you can think and hope to yourselves that one day you won’t go through this torture. I finished my match with swift and was walking backstage when I looked over to a member of the road crew who had a frown on his face, I looked at him more and he nodded to go backstage. I instantly thought that I had messed up in the ring and that the backstage party was extremely mad at me. I was nervous but this all soon ended when a young girl from the production team approached me with a cellular telephone and said that my brother had been rushed into hospital; I grabbed the phone sooner than light travelled and called Tarja up as quickly as my fingers could possibly work. As soon as I heard the tears fall from here eyes and hit the speaker of the telephone that’s when I know Marko had died. As her voice echoed down the phone I stood there frozen, for the brother that grew up with me had left me for “the greater place” I slowly dropped my arms and the phone down before a look of guilt hung over my face, I would remember all the times in the past where I had not taken pity on him, or when I had been mean to him in the past. When a person dies you always automatically think of all the bad things that happen in life. After a few moments of standing there I instantly gave the phone back to the young woman before heading off for my clothes in the locker room. A grabbed my things and told Adams that my brother had just died and I had to go visit him. He knew and saw the pain I was in and like any other friend allowed me to leave. I threw all my stuff into the hummer and sat behind the steering wheel. I couldn’t drive to begin with, don’t get me wrong I wanted to I wanted to so much that my brain wasn’t in gear properly and I just sat there. I remember that’s when the tears started to fall against my face, like a shower of my own, as I blinked It never stopped if anything more tears just kept pouring down, as I would wipe away them it would leave a red mark around my face before refilling with tears. I then turned the keys round and put the pedal down so hard I left a good twenty meter skid mark behind where I was parked, the tires screamed so loud that the two event security guards where thinking that somebody was stealing the car, but after they looked they say it was me and allowed me to go. I am not a fan of hospitals, I never have been and I don’t think I ever will be. But when I arrived there late that night the fact that I had a match next week never crossed my mind, the fact that I was a professional wrestler never crossed my mind, no…I was only thinking of one thing and that was that my brother was lied asleep forever in a room in this hospital and I was sure as hell going to see him. So I walked into the hospital and went right up to the desk where a woman would recognize me, I told here who I was looking for and she told me the second floor ward three. There was a lift but I was so hyped up I ran for the stairs and sprinted to the ward, I saw Tarja stood there outside of the hospital with her head in her hands crying, I knew that within a few more steps I would see my brother laid down for the last time. I walked further and like my thoughts I was right. He was laid there with his eyes closed right straight on his back like he had been knocked out. As I walked a few more steps Tarja turned round to look at me, I remembered I looked a mess, my hair and hands still had blood on them as I never had a shower after I left the match, she looked and me and then ran into my open arms as we embraced. My bloody hands squeezed more before I relaxed and told her I would have to go see him, I told her I would be back and that I must go. So I walked into his room. It was so quite, it was a un-normal feeling for me, as the last time I saw my brother we was joking and sharing a beer together and now he was silent and un-able to speak. I walked to the corner where there was a sink and towel and cleaned myself up, before heading back to his side. I looked on the desk where there was a letter marked with my name on it. It red and I quote. “Dear Brother, I know upon reading this letter that I will no longer be with you, I know you did well today, I am sorry I was unable to see you win but I am sure you did, I am lied here in bed with this nightmare feeling in my chest, I know what it is, its cancer. I have had it for a while now but I have not told anyone about it, so I guess its god’s way of telling me to not keep secretes from others. I am getting tired now so I should be going, know this Juvian I love you and I always will. Signed and dated. Marko Javier Ramorez.” I placed the letter down before quietly whispering into my brothers ears the final words of the lords prayer before exiting the room, I knew I couldn’t stay along time in that room as I would do nothing but sulk and cry and that’s not what Marko would have wanted. He would have wanted me to be strong and the only way I knew how to do this would be in the ring. You see myself and Marko are fighters, we always have been and we always will be, you see we cannot help who we are and we cannot change ourselves either. That’s the only way we know to show each other differences so it was then when I decided that I would dedicate my next match against Digital Mortality to my brother Marko. I know it wont be a easy task defeating a man that I grew up to admire in the ring but if that’s what I have to do to show my respect to my brother then that’s what I will do. I left the room and walked up to Tarja once again before kissing her softly before telling her it will be alright, I told her I was here to take care of here now, we embraced once more as I started to think of how I must now train for mortality. I know I will honour my brother well but how will I support Tarja and the family, I must now work extra hard but I am looking too far for my likings. Digital Mortality and I meet in two days time, will I be in a good state to wrestle?, I cannot predict fate, but I can predict a good match and that’s what we will have. For my brother this match is dedicated to you.
