The Unknown Forces

19 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-19 Tick. Tock. Tick. It was funny how time always went straggling by without a care in the world when you just wished it would speed up and pass right before your eyes, he thought, but if you were enjoying yourself, busy with something or preparing yourself for an important gathering of sorts for which you just needed a nice, long time of preparation, time would zoom past, seemingly just so it could look at you directly in the eye and laugh heartily. But what was even more disturbing to Danny Vista is that only three seconds had passed on the old fashioned grandfather clock that was sitting in front of him as oppose to a television since he had made this, what he thought to be ironically interesting, thought. "Danny, can you bring me up a coffee?" His response wasn’t even thought. Instead, it was a natural snap reaction that he instantly regretted. "No, the draw is on, get it yourself." Having thought about the harshness of his comment for a second, he quickly shouted out to her. "…Love you…" Though wanting to hear Laura’s response for his own safety, Vista had no choice but to ignore it as he heard his name called out on the Primetime Central radio station that was transmitting the GTT draw. "…And Danny Vista of the WWA with face…Ely Braxton of GW!" He jumped in surprise although, having no idea who his opponent was, he quickly sat down again and began to ponder. Is Braxton any good? Or, more importantly, better than him? Although he wanted to sit down and do nothing but think about the tournament and twiddle his thumbs for an hour, he was quickly taken out of his thinking chair with a start as something was quickly forced the letterbox at the front door, landing lightly on the dark red doormat that sat there, doing nothing to the point of being envied by Vista himself. He looked quickly down at the doormat, and he saw upon it a white envelope with a letter inside on yellow paper that was just visible through the envelope. The back of the envelope would usually be considered normal enough but, in the middle of it, the word "Danny" was rashly cut and pasted with letters from various newspapers. He thought it to be rather movie-like, but as he well knew this was reality. It worried him very slightly, but this was increased when he opened the letter and read the letters that were cut and pasted just as rashly inside. Danny Be careful in GTT. You would not want to go too far. That would be disastrous. Without reading the sheet of paper for a second time to make sure he wasn’t asleep or daydreaming, Vista forcibly pulled open his front door and frantically glanced outside at the road that travelled either way from his, looking for anybody looking remotely suspicious. He didn’t see anybody that looked worrying enough to have done this, but this wasn’t a case of shrugging it off. This was already serious. He practically leaped outside the front of his house and, without giving himself a logical explanation as to why he did so, Vista began to run down the left hand road away from his house, the fact that he had left his front door fully open not even getting a look in at the back of his mind. He could’ve continued running all day, and indeed he did for all of twenty minutes until rational thought came in and told him it was time to give up. He was not going to find the perpetrator for now, and indeed he had probably run straight past him some time ago. "I don’t have time for this." With a statement that summed it all up for him, Vista turned around with a few heavy sighs and took a few steps in the direction in which he came. I can never find what I want. It’s always happened to me, and for now I can’t envisage that fact ever changing, though I do wish it would. It has happened in a lot of important situations, and it always manages to knock me down when I’m on a perfect rise. The bad thing is, it isn’t one of those niggling annoying things that comes across as a slight annoyance every once in a while. It’s the worst feeling in the world. It strikes a chord of pain and fear into my heart each time it happens. It is the occurrence that can’t be helped no matter how hard I try. It is the Unknown Force. He began and ended his uneventful trek all the home, before realising he had left the front door of his house open, giving entrance to his home, possessions and soon-to-be wife, Laura. He walked into the house and straight into the lounge, in which he wasn’t particularly surprised to see a few of his possessions strewn across the floor in an undisciplined manner. He stood back, slightly gasping at what had been done, before walking around the downstairs part of the house and seeing no other damage apart from the living room. Slightly relieved, he called upstairs. "Laura?" As no answer came from above, his paranoia set in for a moment and he began to suspect and fear the worst had happened. "Laura!" After no answer came from the second, louder call, he immediately went up the stairs and began walking around the rooms. Nobody in the first room, and his fear increased significantly. Nobody in the second and third rooms, which also only added to his fears, now beginning to set in to the point of Danny muttering and snarling under his breath. He entered the fourth and final room, hoping to see her laying on the bed asleep like the angel that she was to him. No luck, and as soon as he saw the empty bedroom he shouted and cursed out loud, surprisingly with no immediate complaint from his neighbours, whom he thought would have easily heard the complaints. It struck again. The unknown force took the thing I valued most in my life. She was what kept me sane. She was what kept me alive, and she kept me going when it got tough. Now she is gone. This was the last straw, and it suddenly occurred to me that I would do anything to get her back. Anything. Without a single of the thoughts rushing around his head at the very moment being hesitant in any way, his natural reaction was to pick up the phone and call the police, which he did. When the response came that they would take approximately twenty minutes at least to get there, he cursed under his breath and sat on the end of the bed, taking in the rage that was coming instead of letting it out in short bursts. With the police on the way, he figured he had time to ponder the very letter that had started all this time. He read aloud the part that intrigued him the most to himself a few times, trying to figure out what exactly was meant by it. "You would not want to go too far." Could an associate of Braxton possibly have sent him a note that was trying to tell him to lose? "You would not want to go too far." No, he instantly thought. That would be stupid and Braxton was obviously a man with slight brains having entered GTT6. He would want to win fairly. Perhaps somebody didn’t want me to hurt him too bad because they didn’t want to see him hurt. "You would not want to go too far." Again, the first thought that came into his mind was a defiant ‘No’. The majority of people would barely know who he is, despite his undefeated run of 12 opening matches in the WWA , because this was a tournament featuring a lot of federations. It was insane to think that somebody would have been following him to tell him not to do anything too much, because he wouldn’t do such a thing. No, he reiterated to himself inside a head that had an overload of thoughts spinning around continuously. It was impossible. He didn’t know why anybody would do this to him, and if there was going to be anything more happening. As much as he hated to admit it, and it was a rare and extreme occasion when he did… He was scared. Scared of what might happen. It wasn’t that he was scared of what might happen to him, but to his fiancé Laura, for whom he realised would be screaming out in fear. Location: Back of car Time: 18.32 "DANNY!" She threw out her arms as dramatically as she could and cried out the name of the one she needed more than anything right now but, despite her cries of desperation, she knew he wasn’t going to come. "Shut up, voman. You make noise, we make gun bang." She sank back into her seat, her knees, arms, lips and entire body trembling with body whilst tears fell from her eyes. She was afraid. Location: The Vista Household Time: 18.55 "Mr Vista, are you able to tell us exactly when she went missing?" By now, the sadness and slow realization of the situation had passed, and the anger at anybody that was around had begun to set in. "Why yes, I kept a CCTV camera on her when I went out because I predicted this would happen." He knew he was being unreasonable, but right know he just didn’t care. He wanted her back, and he didn’t care how he did it. "There’s no need for sarcasm Mr. Vista, we’re here to try and help. Ridiculing won’t bring her back any faster." At this point, his rage had managed to erupt into a big bundle of fiery anger that he simply had to expel, and so he immediately ran out into the kitchen and couldn’t help but take out the steak knife from the cutlery drawer, and he held it in front of his nose, squinting to see the shiny, metallic blade that was in front of him. He wasn’t stupid, yet he had to take out his rage on something. He grabbed a pile of nearby magazines and, within instants of the policemen entering the kitchen following worried glances about his mental state at the moment, plunged it down carelessly into the magazines, ripping through them until the tip of the knife scraped the counter. He had fulfilled the wishful, daring dreams of anybody under deep stress, and it felt great. Momentarily. Within seconds, he had been tackled to the floor and leaped upon, and the same phrase was repeated in his ear, much to his annoyance. "Calm down, sir." ‘Get off me’, he thought, ‘or I shall calm you down.’ "Calm down, sir." ‘Bugger off.’ For five straight minutes the policemen just laid there, smothering Vista until he gave up squirming before picking him up to his feet and sitting him down on a stool in the kitchen. "We’re doing everything we can. Have a glass of water." Within the click of his fingers, the Sergeant got his assistant to put a glass of water in front of Vista, but he didn’t dare go any closer. Vista tried to reach his hand forward to take a sip, but the desperate side of him took over once again and clenched his hand, swinging it towards the glass and smashing it on the floor before stood up once again in rage, immediately to be leaped on by the policemen. "Get. My. Laura!" Something clicked inside me. A calming, soothing timer that kept me under control was suddenly swept off it’s feet, and my inner dark side was released. The danger of the situation at hand was so intense that I had to do what was necessary. I ignored the Unknown Force. I unleashed MY Unknown Force. The two were about to collide. To be continued
