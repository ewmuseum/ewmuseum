The Chronicles of Muppet III - The Aftermath

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Muppet Manor was a quiet house. With it's inhabitants away, it lay deserted, desolate, empty. Silence filled every corridor, it was around every corner, behind every closed door. The lush decorations, the expensive accessories, all purchased by King Muppet... it was all silent. The eye catching wallpaper, as loud as it might seem, was completely silent. Silence ruled, it dominated, along with it's right hand man, darkness. There was nothing that even resembled life remaining in this house. Well, apart from the Penguin of Death. Probably best to ignore that. Then the clock chimed twelve. The silence remained, the seconds slowly counting past. It wouldn't last. CRASH! The door burst open with a crash that shook the whole entrance hall. In came the Muppet Man and King Muppet, both looking a little worse for wear after the party earlier in the night. Scratch that... they looked like hell. King staggered, almost falling over, before steadying himself on the wall with his arm. He sighed, and his arm slipped down, taking him with it. Muppet just walked forwards, slowly, clutching his head. He wasn't looking where he was going, and tripped over the legs of King Muppet, falling face first to the ground. "Son of a..." he muttered, spitting on the carpet before getting up and dusting himself off. King got up, sitting on a nearby table and smashing an ornament without even really noticing. "We... officially... screwed that up," he said, quieter than usual. "Correction: YOU screwed that up!" "Yeah, and you going up to do karaoke was a brilliant idea..." "WHERE DID THAT SNAKE COME FROM?" "I dunno. No one knows where it went, either..." As you can see... it hadn't gone well. "You certainly didn't help things. What a great snake charmer you'd make..." Muppet said, his voice absolutely brimming with sarcasm of the highest order. He was definitely not in a good mood. "Hey! It's lucky that snake wasn't poisonous... I could be dead!" King shouted. He spoke the truth. "Oh, what a loss that would have been..." "You have no idea how much I do for you. Without me, you wouldn't have all this stuff. Without me, you wouldn't be the US champion. Without me, you wouldn't be in the GTT!" "I signed up for the GTT! You didn't even know what it was!" "Details, details..." King said quickly, shifting the subject. "What kind of behaviour is this? You have two important matches coming up!" "Wha... bu... this was your idea!" "Despicable, despicable..." he muttered, tutting and shaking his head, beginning to walk away. Muppet was left, mouthing some unformed words. He heard a voice from behind him, and almost jumped out of his skin. "He's a right handful, that Robert, isn't he?" It was Benjamin, the old man from the party. The Muppet Man was shocked. "You! Bu... wh... how... what are you doing here? How did you get past the security?" "Security?" Benjamin asked, still beaming. "What, you mean those sleeping dogs out there?" He didn't want to, but the Muppet Man slowly moved over to the window, and glanced outside. Sure enough, there were his 'guard dogs,' fast asleep. He cursed, leaning against the wall and looking down at the floor, feeling quite sick. "What are you doing here?" he asked Benjamin. It was legitimate question. "Well, you see, I'd lost all my money and had no place to go, so I thought I'd tag along with you." "You could have... asked us..." "But... I did!" ---- It was dark, gloomy, and pretty much deserted on the streets outside when the doors to the party burst open for the first time since the Muppet Man made his triumphant return with a truckload of beer. Out poured a herd of people, most too drunk to walk in a straight line, or even stand up properly. Among them was King Muppet, who was left crawling backwards out of the hall, a look of terror transfixed upon his face. The old man, Benjamin, also made his way out, and seeing King, helped him up to his feet. "Good to see you again, Phil. How are you? Did you have fun?" "Snake... it's after me... keep it away..." muttered King Muppet, that dazed looked still in his eyes. "I was wondering if you'd allow me to go with you tonight? I don't really have any money left, and well..." Benjamin began. No one quite knew what his problem was. "Snake... stay away... must... escape... snake..." "Oh, how nice of you!" he said, uplifted and very happy. "I'll get a taxi, hold on... TAXI! TAXI!" A taxi pulled over, and Benjamin got in, pulling the dazed King Muppet in with him. At this point, Muppet staggered out of the hall himself, and rushed in, seeing King Muppet. ---- "This is... just... too much..." Muppet said, breathing heavily. He turned, resting against the wall. "Well, I guess you can stay here for the night. You can have the guest room... it's just over there..." he pointed it out, getting up. Benjamin walked over, examining his quarters, and King made his way back over to Muppet. "So... what now?" "Now, we sleep. GTT round 2 is tomorrow," Muppet said, the realisation almost crushing him. Tomorrow, he had to face Scottywood. Tomorrow, he had to bring his A game to the table, or be beaten. Tomorrow, he had to win... or it would all be over. Tomorrow. "Your call, dude," replied King, looking forward to a good nights sleep. Muppet sighed, closing his eyes, and leaning backwards again. However, he didn't realise that he had taken a few steps to the right, and he fell right through the open door. King was unable to stop himself bursting out laughing, and Muppet slowly got back up having fallen down for the second time, cursing like a maniac. "Son of a..." he muttered, for the second time. "This isn't my day." No, today definitely wasn't his day. Tomorrow, though, would be a different story. It had to be. They say lightning never strikes twice, but if it did, then the Muppet Man was screwed. Tomorrow was make or break time. Tomorrow, he had to succeed. If he didn't, he wouldn't be able to live it down, not in this lifetime. All of this was suddenly dawning on him, and he didn't know what to do. He liked taking things in his stride, not having to worry about an upcoming wrestling match. Sadly, that was no longer possible, and he was slowly being crushed by doubt. He shook his head violently, trying to rid himself of these negative thoughts, but it was no good. They were almost overpowering, filling every nook, every cranny of his mind. Once again, he was desperate. Once again, he felt hopeless. He felt weak, almost powerless, with no hope in sight. He felt faint, and put his hand on the wall again, sudden feeling on the brink of puking. "You ok?" King asked, seeing Muppet leaning over, staring at the floor, yet unseeing. No reply came. King walked over to him, and patted him once, hard, on the back. "You ok?" he repeated. "I can't do it, Will," he said softly, meaning every word, but hating what he had to say. "I can't carry on. No... not tomorrow, not any day. I can't." "But..." King was speechless. This couldn't be happening. No, not now. "You can't give up. You're the Muppet Man, dammit! This is round two of the GTT... you can't go out in round two! I'm your manager, and I'm telling you... fuck that, I'm ordering you to do this!" "I can't, Will. It's not that I don't want to... you know me better than that. I just... can't." King couldn't say anything. He knew the feeling. He knew it painfully well. He knew that the Muppet Man was serious, and he knew that nothing he could do could change that. He sighed, turning to walk away. Benjamin stood there. "I'll take it from here," the old man said quietly, and King did not object. He just continued to walk. Benjamin sighed, muttering 'here goes nothing' to himself before walking over to the Muppet Man. "You know, Jeff has a point." "It's Will," Muppet said, finally bothering to correct the guy. "No, you're Andy." "I'm Andy... he's Will." "What, Toby?" "Will." "So, Toby is Will?" "Yes." "Are you sure it's not Bob?" "It's Will Bobson." "Bob Wilson?" "Will Bobson!" "Alright, alright..." The Muppet Man actually walked over to the wall and slammed his head particularly hard into it. Immediately, he turned and puked his guts out all over the carpet, before falling to his knees. "This is why I don't drink anymore," said Benjamin, sounding as optimistic and light hearted as ever. He tried to help Muppet up, but Muppet threw him away. "I need some aspirin..." he muttered, walking slowly into the kitchen. Benjamin followed behind, as Muppet rummaged around in the cupboard for some aspirin. "That won't do you any good." "Who asked you?" "Why do you lash out at me?" Benjamin asked, suddenly serious. Muppet faltered. "I... well..." "Because you hate yourself. You can't stand the fact that you're giving up, and you're not living the dream through until the end. Personally, I think you're right to do so. You're pathetic... you're acting like a child." "Maybe... maybe I don't give a shit. Maybe I just don't care anymore." "If you don't care... then you have every right to give up. I won't stop you. Now, if you'll excuse me, I'm going out to get some fresh air. Care to join me?" Benjamin asked rather sourly. Muppet didn't answer, so Benjamin shrugged, and made his way back outside. It was a beautiful night. Quite cold, granted, but beautiful nonetheless. He looked up at the glinting stars above, and smiled. Seating himself down on the grass, he began to reminisce about days long past, when he was still young. He heard a movement behind him, and didn't bother to turn as the Muppet Man walked up, and sat himself down next to him. "Maybe... maybe you're right," Muppet said shortly. "You have your whole life to live for, Andy. There's nothing that you can't do, nothing left that you can't accomplish... unlike me. I've thrown my life away. I've done nothing with my whole life, and now I have nothing left. No money, no place to stay, no one to care for me, or to care for..." "...Let me tell you a story," Muppet said, wanting to open up to someone, anyone. Benjamin said nothing, so he continued. "I'll keep it brief. A few years back, I was in a small independent wrestling federation with my partner, Will. We had a chance to win the tag team titles, and at the time, this was the biggest opportunity of our careers. Something was going on, though. I was going through an identity crisis. I needed something, anything... so I took the first thing that came my way. Weed." He paused, and Benjamin was still silent. He could tell that the old man knew exactly how he felt. "As I experimented... it opened doors. I found new... spiritual beliefs, I did research. I even started doing Acid. All the time, my hopes and opportunities were slipping away. Even Will was slipping away. I was becoming deluded. Suddenly, Will was no longer a friend... he was a threat. I had to do something... so I took him out. Through the next few years, I continued to take the drugs, and I continued on my path to destruction, until finally, my eyes were opened. I got rid of them all... but I was still deluded. It continued until... until Will came back." There was a silence. Slowly, Benjamin got to his feet. Muppet looked up, and caught his eye. He smiled. Muppet turned, looking up to the sky. He had to continue... he had to. This time, he wouldn't screw it up. This time, he wouldn't let it slip away. This time, he wouldn't give in to temptations. For once, finally... he would come out on top. "Night," Benjamin said, walking back into the house. Muppet sat there for a few minutes, staring up and watching the stars go by. Silence ruled Muppet Manor once again. Finally, he got to his feet, and began to walk back. He wasn't looking at where he was going, and suddenly tripped over something very solid. He heard a snarling. Two angry dogs backed him up against the wall, with nowhere to run. "Somebody... anybody... help..." *** "Where did that snake come from?" is a line taken from a British sitcom, Only Fools and Horses ***
