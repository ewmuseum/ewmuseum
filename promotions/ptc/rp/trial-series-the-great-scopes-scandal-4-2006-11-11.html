Trial Series: The Great Scopes Scandal (4)

11 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-11 A sculptured look of hybrid emotions remained planted on the face of Vangelus as he slipped the cellular device back into his pocket. Ceasing to hesitate even for a mere second, he snatched up his hat and jacket, equipping the both while simultaneously striving to make an anti-climatic exit. "Trouble in paradise?" asked Essex, seemingly amused by the frantic nature of his counterpart. "Tell the commissioner that I'll reschedule our appointment." Shift The crackling sounds of the gun caused Elisa to drop the phone. The sight of the masked figure caused her to crumble to her knees. The sight of the cold barrel centered directly between her eyes caused her to panic and weep. "Please…please don't kill me." she pleaded, stretching out her hands in added effort. "Why shouldn't I? After all the pain your little boyfriend has caused me, why shouldn't I kill the very woman who holds the key to his heart. Give me one good reason as to why I should spare you mercy." came the response of the figure, her voice muffled by the black ski-mask. "…Wh-who are you?" Elisa pondered, attempting to listen through the deep muffle in order to identify the voice in which she couldn't help but recognize. "All I wanted was a chance at happiness, but yet as a result of his selfishness, I was denied that. Now I have the chance at revenge, and I plan on taking it." A miscalculated look appeared on the face of Elisa. The voice was so familiar, yet she couldn't quite put her finger on it. "Tell me who yo-…" "Firing a bullet into his head would be too easy. No, I want to emotionally destroy him. You're everything to him…you're what he lives for. Without you, he is nothing. Without you, he's left to align himself with me as a tortured and battered soul who's chance of happiness dissolved at the hands of a selfish sibling." Sibling. That very word sealed it. There was no denying it now. "…Angie?" she asked, praying on the inside that she was indeed wrong. Shift Having assembled all of his belongings, Vangelus made his way to the door. Suddenly, he felt a firm tug at his arm, quickly shifting around to find his forward progress delayed by Essex's pulling of his arm. "And where do you think you're going?" Essex asked with an illuminate smile proudly crafted upon his face. With great authority, Vangelus snatched his arm from out of Morris' grasp and continued forward. "To save my soon to be wife, maybe? Just do what I told you an-…" Once more and again, Vangelus found his forward progress disrupted by Morris who, this time, stepped in front of him, blocking his exit. His still illuminate smile met an aggravated expression upon the face of Olsig who was clearly not in the mood for any of Essex's tactics. "Oh contrary, little critter. I'm not done talking to you." Shift "Who I am is not important. The manner in which I should go about in gaining revenge on my brother is the only relevant factor here." she replied, her words weaving together to provide concrete evidence. Elisa was correct in her theory…behind the mask was Angie Olsig, sister of Vangelus. "Why are you doing this to your own brother, Angie? What's possessed you to sink to such a level?" Elisa questioned, replacing the prior look of miscalculation with a look of worry, mixed with anger. "How dare you come at me with such an ignorant question!? What has he done to me!? LOOK AT ME!" Angie ripped off the mask, revealing the same batter, bruised, and scarred face in which she'd revealed to Vangelus in the previous week. "Look at my face. Know how that happened, sister-in-law? I was attacked. No why I was attacked, oh dear Elisa? Because of a crime HE committed. A crime in which he could have easily confessed to…one in which I literally begged him to confess to. But did he? No. And because of such selfishness, I've been made to suffer as well." she stated, slamming the mask unto the ground as her eyes flooded with rage. "He never meant to place you in the crossfires, Angie. He's your brother…he wouldn't do that to you, can't you see that?" "Are you that naive? Are you that drunken by love that you've become totally blind? He hates me Elisa. For years he's hated me." "No…he doesn't h-.." "HE DOES! If he truly loved me, he would've confessed and spared me the pain in which I've been made to endure. Here you are in your nice little penthouse, totally oblivious as to an outsiders life. An outsider like me. You have no idea how hard it is to constantly receive death threats and ransom letters, all because I wear the Olsig name in which he has stained. You don't know how hard it is to have a gun pointed at your head, set to trigger at any given time. You can't truly understand why I'm doing this simply because you don't understand what I've been through. He doesn't love me…he can't love me." Shift Attempting to remain steadfast, Vangelus calmly tried to shift to his left, but Essex shifted as well, continuing to block his path. Vangelus tried the right, but was only met with the same result. Finally he spoke up. "Look…I'm trying to stay calm. My wife is in serious danger…now just move out of my way. We can talk later, I prom-…" "No, we talk now." And in an act of frustration, Vangelus sent a cold right hook into the jaw of Essex, watching as the heralded attorney crumbled to the floor below like a chopped tree. With that distraction out of his way, Vangelus stepped over the fallen Morris Essex and continued on, offering his opposer four parting words. "Like I said…later." Gone. Pushing himself to one knee, Essex reached into his coat pocket and retrieved a cell phone of his own. With one touch, his desired number was dialed. "Aye Carlos…I stalled as long as I could…he's on his way." Shift Angie began to pace in small steps, displaying her discomfort with the present situation. She ran her hand through her jet black hair, hands becoming overcome with a sizable amount of sweat which nearly caused her to drop the gun. Following a brief moment of silence, Elisa broke the mold. "Way to contradict yourself, because right now I'm not seeing any love in what you're doing either. You play the victim role awfully well, but maybe, just maybe you're so close to the mirror that you've blinded yourself from reality. Maybe it's you who's to blame. I mean, look at you. You've got a gun, threatening an innocent soul in myself all because you want revenge on your brother. You didn't appreciate being caught in the crossfire, so why are you placing me there? Hypocrite…Hypo-.." Smack Angie sent a cold slap across her face, subsequently placing her finger into the face of Elisa. "Don't you dare try to turn this around on me! It's HIS fault and nobody but his!" With a new found desire to kill, Angie cocked the gun. "You've distracted me long enough. It's time to accomplish the task in which I've come here to accomplish." She placed the cold barrel into the face of Elisa. "Wait…before you pull, I've got to know one thing. It's clearly obvious that you didn't construct this plan on your own will, so who put you up to it?" Elisa queried. "Well, normally I'd respond to a question like that with the 'If I tell you, I'd have to kill you' line…but since I'm going to kill you anyway, I might as well fess up. Carlos Boss. Happy?" "But…Why?" A sadistic smiled crossed the face of Angie. "You mean to tell me that your own fiancée didn't tell you of the little threat Carlos gave him?" she asked with a smile that suggested trouble in paradise. "What threat?" "Carlos was the one who killed Esposito. He wanted Vangelus to pull the trigger on our uncle, but Vangelus refused. In doing so, Vangelus made enemies in both me and Carlos. Two minds were greater than one, we figured, so we aligned ourselves together." "So if Vangelus didn't kill Sharker…who did?" She cocked the gun one final time. "The very person who's about to do the same to you. Do me a favor, will you? Tell Ol' Uncle Sharker I said hello when you see him in hell." SMACK! The gun hit the ground and so did Angie. Elisa looked up to see her fiancée, Vangelus Olsig, standing proud and tall over the fallen Angie. "How dare you…" Olsig stated, face free of any and all emotion. "Van-…it's not wh-…" This time, Vangelus sent a straight kick into her already battered face. Subsequently, he yanked her up and pinned her against the wall, laying into her with ferocious right fists. "Please, Stop!" she cried, but he remained totally deaf to her pleas. With hands wrapped around her neck, attempting to strangle her, she continued to cry for mercy. "Please, Stop!" Vangelus tossed her to the ground, stomping on her with little regard to her fragile body. "Please, Stop!" He yanked her up once more, slamming her face first into the cold wall, watching as she crumbled to the ground. By now, blood was flowing from her cracked skull. Vangelus mounted her, laying into her with a series of right punches. BLAM! Vangelus fell to the ground, holding his bloody shoulder. A bullet had chipped him on the shoulder, and while it wasn't a life threatening shot, it was still enough to send him down to the ground. He looked up, and there stood Elisa with the gun in hand. "…Elisa?" A tear formed in her left eye. "I can't allow you to do this, Vangelus. Your future…our future…it's too bright to be wasted on someone like her." Grasping his chipped shoulder, he push himself up to one knee with a disdained look still present on his face. "But Elisa…she tried to kill us both. She murdered my uncle, for crying out loud. It's about time she get what's coming to her…she's nothing outside of evil." Vangelus cried out, attempting to jump back on the injured sibling, but Elisa immediately caught him and restrained him. "And if you kill her, you're no different. Murder is murder, Vangelus, no matter the motive." she calmly stated, attempting to soothe her companion. "I'm already an enemy of the country. One more dirty deed won't hurt anything." Vangelus replied, glancing over at the moaning Angie Olsig. "Don't…think like that. She's not the enemy." Angie responded, causing Vangelus to look on in bewilderment. "What do you mean?" he asked. "Carlos Boss put her up to it." "But…wh-…" "You know why…and now I know why." His efforts to hide Carlos Boss' threat from her had failed, akin to every secret he attempted to hide from her. He'd come to the conclusion that secrets simply could not be hidden from her. Somehow, someway, she'd discover them. It may occur the next day, the next month, or the next year, but someway…somehow, she'd discover them. A look of sorrow crossed his face. "Why didn't you tell me, Vangelus?" she asked. "I…I jus-…" "ARREST THAT MAN!" Both shifted their eyes to the open door where Morris Essex stood, surrounded by four police officers. "OH MY-…ANGIE!" He rushed over to the fallen Angie Olsig, cradling her in his arms and pushing himself to look as if he'd reached tears. Playing the role of a father who'd just lost his daughter, he screamed out at Vangelus. "WHAT'D YOU DO TO HER!" "Why is it of any concern to you, Essex? As a matter of fact, why are you here?" Vangelus asked, pushing himself to a full vertical position while circling around the grounded Essex. "A friend can care, can't he?" "Yes…a friend can. But you're no friend. Why are you really here?" Vangelus asked in a demanding sense. "Where's the gun. Why aren't you dead." Essex pondered, desperation evident on his face. "So you were behind this. That's why you attempted to keep me from leaving the office…to buy her more time to deliver the blow. Then you were going to charge in here with your little mob squad and convince them that I was the one that killed her, all the while Angie fled." "You can't prove it." "I don't have to prove it…you'll admit it." With that said, Vangelus headed over to his book case, snatching out a bible. He waltzed back over to the spot in which Essex sat, stretching the bible out before him. "Under oath…did you or did you not conspire to kill my wife and frame me with murder?" Morris Essex was so vulnerable. All he had going for him in life was his status as a heralded attorney. He'd buried his life so deep into his job, that he found himself completely unable to deceive it. In other words, he could not lie under oath. "I-I…" "Did you, or did you not." "I-I…" "Yes..or No." "HE'S BADGERING THE WITNESS, YOUR HONOR!" Insanity. That's the point Essex had reached. "We've seen enough." stated the head of the police squad as he stepped forward with handcuffs in hand. "Mr. Essex, you're under arrest. You have the right to remain silent…any and everything you say and can and will be used against you in the court of law…" locking the handcuffs around his wrists, the squad carried the kicking and screaming Essex away. A grueling battle, but Vangelus had won. Peace, all of the sudden, didn't seem so far out of reach. -- It occurred seven years ago. My best friend of twelve years was shot and killed in the middle of a bank robbery. I attended his funeral and sat there, listening to the reverend damn my friend to hell simply because he hadn't been baptized. I sat there and listened to the congregation shout their amens, agreeing with the reverend's sentencing of my friend. In the midst of it all, I got up and walked out of the church. From that day forward, I found my faith shaken. Even to this day, that memory runs rapid in my mind. It all cumulated in me totally dismissing the Christian religion all together. That, subsequently, led to the trial which landed me in this mess. Through faith in my new found religion, I found myself acquitted of the crimes charged against me…but the war had only begun. Now, however, with Essex defeated, I feel as if I've finally won the war. The internet reports have diminished and PRIME has assured me that they seek to keep me on the active roster. For the first time since that incident, I feel as if peace is totally reachable. Our flight was originally scheduled to take off a week from now, but me and Elisa left Bogotá the day after Essex's arrest. The sooner we abandoned such a horrid past, the better. Now I'm on my way to GTT, a tournament in which I look to make mine. I'll probably never be welcome in Bogotá again, but that's okay. Though GTT, I'll make the states my new home. I glance over at her, sleeping in the window seat of the plane. For the first time in quite some time, I envision a smile on her face. She and I both knew what the future held… An unreachable peace would finally be achieved. Fin. Authors Note: To fully understand these last two roleplays, you must first read the first two in the series, otherwise it probably wont make much sense. Enjoy.
