(IG23:Tigera) A question of honor

16 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Character: Tigera ( sorry forgot to put this here) Bring down your opponent by any means. Bring him down by deception, by craft, through uncertainty, through insecurity--Better to bring him down than to bring yourself down - Sun Tzu ~*~ It had been a difficult task, leaving her father's side to return to FUSE for her match against Cruz after all that had happened. In fact, she had argued against leaving for hours only relenting when her father resorted to calling her a coward. No one called Mya Yamomuko a coward. The bitter sting of his words still rested within her breast even as the Universal Title rested in her lap while she stared out the small window of the airplane. She could see Bangkok in the distance as the plane circled making ready for its final approach to deliver her back to home soil. Scowling, she looked down at the title wondering just what her father's reaction would be to this new prize. Something told her it would not be what she wanted it to be. Eiji never liked the idea of his only daughter wrestling. In fact, he had said as much one time stating he would have preferred she continued in the underground fighting circuits of Thailand. Honor is a strange and fickle entity in the Orient. He found honor in her fighting in the underground but fails to see it in wrestling. What truly defined honor? She had always thought it was defined by one's actions. Was it not honorable of her to rescue him when he had been in the hands of the Yakuza? Perhaps there had been no honor in her silence at the parole hearing of Yuko? Maybe seeking revenge for everything her family had suffered was not honorable? At this point in her life, she simply did not know. What she did know, however, is that she felt honor in winning the coveted belt resting on her lap. -Ding.- Golden pools drifted to the front of the cabin to see the seatbelt sign flashing. Mechanically, she placed her safety belt across her lap and waited. Soon, very soon, she would be with her father again and, together, they would begin the hunt to bring down those who had commited crimes against their family. ~*~ The silence in the car was deafening as Eiji navigated it through the streets of Bangkok toward their home. He had not been happy to see her prize draped over a slender shoulder and the fight that had followed was brutal. Mya sat with her jaw clamped tightly staring out her window. Her inner thoughts were filled with rage, sadness and confusion. Life with this man was like a roller-coaster out of control. Sometimes exhilarating, other times frightening and sometimes, just sometimes, it was down right infuriating. " Mya, I want you to quit." Her head snapped in his direction, those defiant amber eyes widening for a moment before slowly narrowing into mutinous slits. " No." " The honor of your family is at stake here. We have much to do and if you continue with that...facade you call a career then you will not be able to focus on our honor." " And what about my honor Papa? Is quitting honorable?" " There is an exception to every rule." " No Papa, there are only exceptions if you want there to be exceptions. You want me to soil my honor elsewhere to help restore your hon-" " Our." She jaw snapped shut at his interruption. Our? She just gave him a stony stare as everything that came boiling to the surface of her mind refused to spill past those tightly sealed lips. She knew to say the things she thought would be to dishonor him in his eyes. It was something that was never looked upon kindly and, as such, something she should avoid. But then again, how could one dishonor a man whom had already been dishonored by none other than his own actions? It was he whom had started this whole fiasco that caused all the pain and loss the Yamomuko family had suffered. It was his own act of cowardice that had started the vicious cycle. A cycle, he now needed her to help him end. All of these things were true and, still, she did not have the courage to cross that line. " You will quit and that is final. You have better things to do with your life." That was the final straw. " No." " No? You are my daughter and you will do as you are told." " I said no. I am a grown woman, Papa." His hands tightened on the steering wheel as he whipped the car into the driveway of their home and slammed on the brakes. He threw the door open and got out before speaking again as she climbed out in a calmer fashion. Almost too calm. " You would dishonor me by disobeying me," he snapped. " No, I can not dishonor a man who has dishonored himself by his own actions!" The moment the words left her mouth, she regretted it. Well, almost. Her gaze shifted over the top of the car to look at him. Eiji's face was crimson with the anger she had invoked by her words. Like lightning, his fist slammed down on the roof of the Volvo leaving a nasty dent. The action caused her to flinch. " Mya you will do as I say or so help me-" " So help you what!? What are you going to do Father? Cane me as you did when I was younger? Beat me? It doesn't matter what you do! I am NOT quitting. I do not need to be here every day, every hour do to as we must to regain your honor. I have my own code of honor to look after and I will be damned if I let you compromise that for the sake of your own goals!" His mouth opened only to clamp tightly shut. The driver side door slammed shut with a force that it caused the window to shatter. She said nothing. She merely watched as he stalked up the stone stairs leading to their home before looking back to the shattered glass. Oddly, she felt it symbolic of her own shattered life. A single defining action that led the complete destruction of an innocent soul. ~*~ Mya remained shut in her old bedroom refusing to come out and face Eiji once again. Instead, she merely lounged on her bed, fingers slowly tracing the contours of her prize in idle thought. This was not a life that anyone deserved to live. No one deserved to watch their brother die before their very eyes. No one deserved the anger of a grieving father blaming them for that same death. And, yet, this was the life she lived. It was those very events that had turned her into the woman she was today. The night of that dance she had gone to when she was fifteen had, inevitably, led to the journey she has taken thus far. It led her to learn to fight. It taught her to hunt and to kill. It taught her to shut down her emotions. Yes, it was that very night that been the spark which would lead her to the underground fighting and, ultimately, to wrestling. Wrestling. Those eyes lost their far away look as she looked to the belt she was still tracing with one question in mind. How much did this platinum encrusted leather strap mean to her? Everything. -Knock knock- The sound of knocking on her door drew her gaze to the frame. Even now, she refused to answer. " Mya. Come, let's discuss this." " There is nothing to discuss. I told you my decision and it is final." A loud thud shook the door frame causing her to jump slightly. But that was nothing compared to what would spew from her father's mouth afterward. " NOT AS LONG AS YOU LIVE UNDER MY ROOF!" Growling, she slapped the belt aside and jumped off her bed to storm across the expanse of her room. The door flew open as she came face to face with her father and screamed in kind. " THEN I WILL NO LONGER LIVE UNDER YOUR ROOF!" Having said that she slammed the door shut in his outraged face and began the process of packing. Within seconds, she had the top drawer of her dresser open, clothes flying into a growing pile on top of her bed. The sound of her door flying open and slamming off the wall in the next moment caused her to turn. There he was, in all his rage, stalking into her room with that bamboo sword. For an instant she was that young girl again, fearing the beating that was to come. The instant fled quickly. Calmly, she slid the drawer closed and faced him with chin held high. If he intended to beat her, he'd best be prepared for a fight. She was no child to be whipped into submission. Those days were gone a long time ago.... ~*~ " You are no longer my father." The words she spoke still drifted in constant stream through her mind as she placed the last of her luggage onto the hotel bed. They had been the last words she had spoken to the man whom had dared to try and beat her after all this time.The vision of the event was forever painted within her mind. He had been on the ground, staring up at her with a shocked and bewildered look as she stood over him with broken bamboo sword in hand. The sword she had wrestled from his grip and broken. The memory felt so surreal, almost as if it had not really happened. But it had... Wincing, she lay across the top of the bed and rested her head on crossed arms. He had managed to do some damage before she finally blacked out and fought back like the tigeress she was known to be. In the end who had really won? No one. This time they both lost something, faith in each other. Sighing she came to her feet and moved into the bathroom. Hands that trembled with the left over adrenalin turned the faucets allowing water to stream from the shower head as she started to undress. Each movement caused her body to groan in protest as she divested her being of her clothes. Once she was nude, those eyes looked into the mirror that was already fogging over. A hand lifted swiping away part of the mist before she turned to look at her back. Wide stripes of discolored skin could be seen. Bruises of varying shades of black, blue, and purple were rising from the usually smooth surface. Scowling she looked away and stepped into the shower hoping to wash the memory away, even if only for a little while. ~*~ Three days of silence. The phone by her bed had not made a single sound other than the wake up calls she had asked for. But today was different. The quiet of her hotel room was shattered by the sound of the phone ringing in the midst of her work out. -Ring ring, ring ring- She lifted her back from the floor for the final time and stared at the phone with curiosity. To answer it or not. Her lips screwed up into a look of distaste as she rolled to her kness and leaned over to answer it. " Hello?" " Mya.." It was her father. Immediately her fingers tightened on the receiver as she moved to place it back on the stand, but the sound of his voice pleading for her not to hang up caused her to pause. She sat there with the phone in hand and sighed heavily before bringing it back to her ear. " Mya. This is hard for me to say, but..." " But what?" " I'm sorry. You are right I have no right to dictate your life." Shock slammed through her form like a rocket. His words seemed to thunder in her ears so loudly that she barely heard his next words. " I just don't want to lose you too, even if it is to your career. I couldn't bare it. Not after Tsuko. Not after your mother. You are all I have left in the world. Please..forgive me." It was the second time in her entire existence this man has asked for her forgiveness. The question was, could she forgive him this time? Did she have the stamina to give him the forgiveness he sought knowing that he could resort back to his fuedal ways? Did she have a choice? Yes.
