TTD - Invincibleness~! v. Rodriguez Bros. v. SotBL

8 May 2004 Do I know you? Aside from her slap, that was the one question that circling through my head. If I had known her, it probably wasn't a relationship on good terms. Those eyes. I had never seen eyes like that before in my entire life. I had helped her up since we had fallen, and we started to walk down the street. We weren't going anywhere in particular, we were just walking. Her name was Lecia. I couldn't remember the name for the life of me, and I told her so. Suddenly, here eyes took a change in expression. They went from being cold and still to alive, and... wet? She wasn't crying. Rather, she shed a single tear. And we stopped walking for a second. "You don't remember me?" "I'm sorry. I don't remember anything before a certain period in my life." It's true. My whole being; everything I stand for, essentially drives me towards the fact that I need to know myself. John Locke stated that the most important component of personal identity is continuing consciousness. His theory insinuated that the body was only a vessel for the person, and a definition of a person, or "personhood", would be that the person could look upon his past and reflect on it. He said that one must be able to remember, reflect, and experience the memories one had. By definition of Locke's theory, I'm not the same person I was eight years ago. According to Locke, I'm the second person to use this body. Hell, I don't even know if I'm the second. For all I know, this could have been a recurring thing throughout my life. "We met just outside of Milwaukee," she started. "It was nineteen-ninety-six. You were seventeen, and I was just twelve. You had a short haircut, which made your face look so thin. And your eyes... your eyes were so empty. Void little circles on your tired face." "Why was I leaving Milwaukee? And why at such a young age? How did I meet you, and why?" Of course, I'd have questions. This is the me that I don't know. This is the me that I've been searching for all my life. And through Lecia, I might have found the one link that could finally make me whole. She laughed for a second before continuing, "We had both run away from home. My father was abusive, and you told me that yours was an alcoholic. I remember you told me that you had run away earlier, and lived with your aunt and uncle. I forgot where you said they lived, exactly. Your father had finally found out where you had been, so you packed your bags and left again. And you found me, at a bus stop, penniless and sick. "You've got to understand, Chivo, you were like this guardian angel. You took my hand, and you bought my something to eat. In a world where no one cares; in a world where you feel like you're the only one, fending for yourself against a cast of evil, you were like that shining light." My question meter began to fill up again. "Cast of evil?" "My town was a very religious town. Baptists, and Catholics, and Anglicans. Sure, they weren't all of the same religion, but they were all strong believers in their own faiths. Skipping a Sunday mass was like grabbing God by the balls, and spitting in his face. I was an outcast. My clothes were dirty; ragged. I had been bruised, and everyone looked at me as this trashy young whore. When you came along, they were appalled to see that I had continued doing business." "Did... Did I---" "No, God no. I was twelve for Christ's sake." Whew. Good. For a second there, I thought I had been a pedophile before I could give myself the chance to become one in good nature. I mean, I'm not even twenty yet! I need some time before I can stoop that low. She continued, "Anyway, from that day on, we were inseparable. We went everywhere together. You wanted to join the army. In fact, you were so determined to. You were so determined, that I shared in your determination. I guess, at the time, being so young, I just wanted someone to be with. It was hard being alone, and I couldn't bare living on the streets again. So, the next year, you enlisted." "Lecia," I interrupted her, because I had one question that I couldn't get off my mind. "How did we survive for that year? Where did we live? How did we eat?" "You said that you needed to do something to keep us going, so you did the one thing that you promised yourself you would never do. I forgot your exact reasoning, but it had something to do with your brothers, and maybe your father. But, I know you were running away from it." Running away from a job? What kind of job would I want to run away from? Money is money, isn't it? "What was the job, exactly?" "You were so adamant. You told me that you would do it only because we needed to survive. And, I remember when you enlisted, you told me that you would never wrestle again." Never wrestle? "Wait. Are you sure we know one another? Did you just say that I hated to wrestle?" "You didn't hate it. Hell, Chivo, you trained for a few years before we met. You told me it's what your brothers wanted to do. Like I said, I can't remember exactly why you wanted to get away from it, but you were sure that it was something you wanted to stay out of. You wanted to be a soldier. You wanted to be a leader." Well, that's not surprising. That's the reason I eventually took over the XWF. I needed that sense of power. It's that sense of pride. The reason I will be champion, and the point of being at the top of the UPWA. I want people to recognize me. I'm the leader of a locker room, and the poster boy for a federation. I like the idea of people wanting to be me, to be like me, and to be where I am. I like the fact that I'm a warrior, and that I'm fighting for my position. I am a soldier. My battlefield is the wrestling ring, and I'm the best the federation has to offer. I've cemented my place as a soldier, and a leader and very soon I will be one half of the PTC Tag Team Champions. I'm really no different than I was, I guess. Maybe I am the same person. Sorry Locke, point: Chivo. "Wait, you didn't answer my question. Sadly the dark half will eventually conquer the lighter half at this time; in a continuous cycle where light conquers dark then dark conquers light.] [The sun appears to set the sky on fire as it turns the clouds a pinkish red color and the horizon a glowing orange. The desert below the horizon seems to be a mirror of that image as though the desert is just an extension of the burning sky.] [Scanning back to the dark side we find a car parked some distance into the dark side of the desert, a figure apparently sits on the hood of the car. We slowly walk towards knowing the isolation of this place, we don't want to make a wrong move causing some sort of incident.] [The figure apparently stares off into the darkness rather then the marvelous sunset; now although this is disturbing to us, we don't worry for the figure seems calm and at ease staring off into the darkness.] [As we approach him, we notice the car is in fairly good condition, but we can't really tell what it is due to the darkness nor can we tell the color, but it if darker either black or blue.] [The man lays on the hood, with his head against the windshield he stares off. Taking a close look... We can recognize this man as UPWA's very own United States Champion Nicky Jam. His greasy dark colored hair is messed up on his head, very dirty looking and very almost alive on its own. His eyes are only half open, but yet the half that is open is very alive.] Nicky Jam: Its nice to get away from the world every once in a while... Even if there is no world left. I've been here for some time now, but I'm sure you can tell I am not the most social person ever. I find myself in my own world within the world, but now is a time a take to escape both... To concentrate on other things. [His eyes open wider watching the clouds overhead moving quickly together and apart, forming new and different images everytime for every different person.] Nicky Jam: Ironically, I am a person that considers thinking about business as an escape... Which might seem odd to most, but when you have nothing else and you actually semi-enjoy what you do; it in itself is an escape. [He pauses as he changes subjects... He sits up more now rather then laying down, he cracks his neck then continues.] Nicky Jam: Seems like the PTC wants to throw someone into the spotlight rather quickly, as Chivo and I have been placed in a battle where the winner will offically gain the surname of PTC Tag Team Champs... I suppose this was the chance I was begging for. But from what I've seen by Souls of The Black Lotus and Invicibleness, its not the competition I was looking for. [He grabs a pack of cigarettes from his pants and pounds on the back of them. Then as he speaks he removes one, puts it in his mouth, and lights it. He then places the lighter and pack to his side as he sits with his knees to his chest and arms around his knees, when he is not taking a drag.] Nicky Jam: They don't even know that they're in a death match, with less than a week before it goes down. Not that there's really a way to prepare for a match like this, but nonetheless the awareness of what they're about to be thrown into would be a plus. However, judging from their apparent intelligence there is no way I could expect any sort of intelligence from this opponents. [He leans back again while taking another drag, his body remains upright on the windshield; however his legs lay flat against the hood.] Nicky Jam: But I've wasted enough away time on this imbeciles and none of my other enemies that have chosen not to speak about me, therefore I choose not to speak of them... However; I do hope the competition they present is more formidable than the one I will get in the PTC Tag Team Title Tournament Finals, otherwise these next few days will be filled verbal beatings leading up to the physical domination. [He closes his eyes to blink or to plainly close his eyes and suddenly.] Nicky Jam: In bed I lie awake, watching the ghosts of this world flashing before my eyes and feeling my own demons all around me... Grabbing and pulling and scratching at me, yet I know their purpose is only to drag me in further to this world of fear, of nightmare. The ghosts of the cars, the shattered glass, her cut face... The sirens sounding, my pounding headache... All coming back as I relive my worst nightmare, the nightmare I still am trapped in, I am still dreaming now. They assault me over and over, ferociously they attempt to shatter my ear dreams with the screeching tires and shattered glass. They attempt to get me to listen to this so that way I might get caught in that moment and hear it forever more... Never be able to escape... However; I will escape, for I have my pride. Maliciously, they assault my eyes and try to get me to see her face torn with glass, her eyes closed by reapers, and my hands covered in her blood... Constantly they repeat this even if I shut my eyes, but I refuse to look at these images for I won't be turned to stone. I will not allow myself to be drawn in and become another victim of fear. And I am not afraid because I have my pride. And the demons they grab at my arms and chest, scraping and grabbing... Attempting to drag me down so this way they might consume me. Consume my hope, consume my confidence, and consume my reality... However, they fail to realize that they cannot consume me for I have seen and touched and heard reality; nothing will prove otherwise for I've been there thanks to my pride. Suddenly one of the demons hops upon me, I stay still knowing it cannot consume and take me if I stay strong... If have faith, if I have strength.The creature of darkness, this creature of this world hisses wildly before me. I stare straight at it, unafraid... I know you're not real! I know you're not real! I've touched whats real, I've felt whats real, and you are nothing, but a bad dream. Hearing my thoughts, the demon lets out a large maniacal laugh... The kind Satan could not duplicate if he wanted to; however I still do not give in to fear. I'm strong for you, I'm strong for you. He leans forward strangling my throat, waving his hand in front of my face, giggling as he does so. I follow his hand and I'm almost blinded by the light of it, I try to look, but I'm blinded by the light...I find my eyes closing, unable to breathe as he smothers me... The demon isn't real, he cannot kill me and he cannot defeat me for I have faith. I have reality. I have my pride. My breathing stops... I'm faking, I'm not dead. They're attack is weakening on me, I have won. For another night, I have won and hopefully tomorrow morning it'll be over... I'll wake up in heaven, in reality; not here, not anymore... I pray. Even if I wake up here again... I still have my pride. Death to all those who disbelieve! [CLICK...END FEED] ---- 11 May 2004 Another weight, and he continued to lift, descend, lift, descend. A puddle was forming beneath his head, and his eyes had begun to flicker as if he were falling victim to an epileptic seizure. Descend. One cough created a dozen more as his dry throat expressed it's need for water; any liquid to spare him of dehydration. His lips were chap. His face, ghastly. His hair, a mess. This man was determined. Nothing would stop him short of his goal, not even his own consciousness. The veins in his eyes were blood red. They were forced to work against their will. Pushing his eyes towards the boundaries of their sockets, as another weight was added. Lift, descend. Lift, descend. The very fact that his body hadn't shut down was a testament to the fact that he was both mentally and physically concentrated. He was a perfect specimen of a man. This was the kind of superhuman that children would look up to, and emulate in hopes of one day becoming supermen themselves. He was a wrestler, and every part of him resounded to all that witnessed his glory - "Champion. Champion. Champion." A description fit for a man like me. But this man is not me. He's not me, but I created him. With my own hands, I forged from a man more fit to be a monkey, a champion! As God, from the Earth, created man, I created this Beast. The bandages wrapped around his palms began to fall apart against the cold steel bar, constantly in his grip. A cloud of cigarette smoke danced over his strawberry eyes, and tears rained down his cheeks against his will. "That's enough for today, dude. Great work." The sigh, apparently of relief, was loud. Loud enough that I heard it above my Mini-Disc Player's full volume playback of Run DMC's "Run's House". Nicky Jam almost fainted once he put down the weights. "I would have spotted you," I said, "but this magazine had a really good article about how Winona Ryder single-handedly changed the face of the American legal system." It really was a good article, and I wish I could have been there to spot the poor guy. He looks like sh*t. "Did you know that the Microsoft MSNBC website had Winona's name spelt with a 'y'? W'y'nona Ryder. Sometimes you just have to question the integrity of some of these news websites. It's ridiculous." Nicky Jam didn't answer. His pale face looked almost transparent against the white workbench. His eyes were closed, but only enough that his body allowed them to be. The room was bright, and his eyelids were visibly shaking against the luminance of the fluorescent lights. "W...w...wat...er..." Hey, we've got some improvement! He's speaking. I pulled a water bottle out of his gym bag, and handed it to him. It was warm. But that's only because there wasn't enough room in the fridge for it. I mean, where was I going to put my food? What about my beer? I mean, I am the most celebrated wrestler in UPWA history. Nicky Jam is only my brother. I figure it would be better to teach him the hard way. If you're thirsty, and the water's hot - drink it. It's all a matter of need. If you're dying of thirst, you drink whatever you can. If you're naked, you cloth yourself with whatever is nearby. If you're sick, you keep working. I'm a Libra. It's all about weighing out the options. Like a set of scales. I put my hand on his shoulder, and sat next to him. "It looks like you're ready for this weekend." He nodded, while chugging the water. "Just picture it - the headlines! Nicky Jam and Chivo, the most successful tag team in PTC. You know, we should start coming up with some sick double team moves. Even some triple team ones. I can get Chezina to rock the ropes with us. What do you think?" "I think..." "I was thinking. After we win this weekend, we should probably reach a little higher. Picture it, Nicky Jam - Chivo and Nicky Jam, the greatest team in PTC, AND the tag team champions in PTC! We'd rock the body that rocks the party, in the tag team division! Can you imagine the headlines? The ratings?" "Y...yeah." "Listen, pal. Why don't you go shower? Afterwards, we can head off to a restaurant. Maybe get Shawnette, Stephen and 'Ferna, and grab some grub. My treat." "Really?" "Yeah man. For sure. You deserve it after all your hard work. PTC Tag Team Title Tournament Finals? We're going to light that b*tch up!" Finally, a smile crept onto Nicky Jam's face. He got up and walked off to the showers. With a chance at the now vacant UPWA World Title and the shot at winning the PTC Tag Team Titles, there's no way we could lose both. And it's true, we would light up PTC. I mean, who else would people pay to go see? Invicibleness? Souls of The Black Lotus? What worthless tag teams. Seriously now, tournament finals are supposed to be entertaining. Facing Invicibleness and Souls of The Black Lotus? Please. The Rodriguez Brothers are the reason to purchase your tickets to see the tournament finals. We both know it, and we know that everyone else knows it. Who's house? Rodriguez' house! At the rate that Nicky Jam's been progressing, it's sure to be a five star match. We've both been working like slaves so far. Training this guy is no joke. It's a tough deal. Apparently, he gets tired. He gets hurt, and requires food and sleep. I mean, I know that there's no one like me in this world, but I didn't think my brother would be so soft. All of those things are overrated. Once he learns that, he'll be completely ready. Maybe he'll be a worthy successor. But not yet. I've still ways to go. It's not use thinking about training now, though. We've got a table to book, and I'm paying. I check my wallet as I put on my coat, and realize I've no cash on me. Nicky Jam walks out of the shower with a towel around him, and I ask him what took him so long. "Just kidding, man. Take your time." "I'll be done in a second. Thanks for offering to take us out to dinner. I'm starving." "Yeah, man. No prob." I put a cigarette in my mouth, and light it. I offer one to Nicky Jam, and he shakes his head. "I'm just going to go get decent. I'll be back." Sure. I look at my wallet again, and turn to the locker room. "Say, Nicky Jam. Mind if I borrow a few bucks?" Who's House? Rodriguez' House!
