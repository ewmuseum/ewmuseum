Tequila Nights

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 “You dun look to good gringo.” “Fuck off.” Came a quick reply. Undeterred by the heavy worded foreigner, the elderly Mexican settles himself into the bench. “You wanna drink essay?” Johnny turns his eyes to peer at the stranger and then nods appreciatively, extending a hand to accept the small metal flask offered to him. Dressed in a pair of dirty jeans and a wrinkled white shirt, Lexicon savors the bitter taste with a satisfied sigh. “Thanks.” He says and passes it back. “You on yo way home gringo?” “Yeah, finally.” Johnny pushes himself into a straighter sitting position and clears his throat. The Mexican next to him lifts a shabby hat and settles it upon his head. The arid desert air permeated every inch of the semi crowded bus station, rendering even the most stoic of men weak. “How did you like Mehico hombre?” “It’s a long story…” Just the tone of his voice hinted at a painful memory in the making. “We ain’t got nuthin but time homes.” Johnny looks at the man again, his left eye brow raising ever so slightly. The crows feet around the Mexican’s eyes deepen as his lips stretch in a smile. Nodding a bit Johnny clears his throat again and takes the flask, after a long gulp he speaks. “Well it all started three days ago, I flew to Puerto Vallarta to celebrate a big win. I met this girl…” “Mamacita!” The elder chuckles. “Yeah… Anyways, I met this girl see, we got into the tequila and the next thing I know we’re driving down a dirt road in a stolen cadillac…” With tires barely clinging to the dirt road the ’67 Cadillac careened from one side to the other, a thick cloud of dust churning in it’s wake. The engine roared it’s fury into the crisp night air, broken only by the feminine laugh. With long black hair flying wildly in the air a tight mexican woman straddles Johnny in the drivers seat. Her top long ago abandoned and left for the desert. With one hand on the steering wheel and the other on her ass, Lexicon attempts to fuck and drive at the same time. “Oh Johnny, kiss me.” She utters huskily. “I’m trying to drive babe.” He responds with a grin, trying to look around her. “Kiss me Johnny!” She insists, both hands on his face as she pulls him into a kiss. HOOONK!! HOOOOOONK!! Headlights flood his vision. The honking and screaming of tires fill his ears, vertigo touches his stomach and he suddenly realizes he doing donuts on a dirt road. The tires skip along over rocks as the woman in his lap is dumped unceremoniously into the passenger seat. White knuckled fists grip the steering wheel. “Jesus Christ!” Johnny growls as he hears the ‘swoosh’ and ‘vroom’ of a cargo truck passing by, if he could only see how close his bumper had come to clipping the vehicle he’d have shit his pants. Finally the Cadillac stops and teeters on its axles precariously. Dust settles within the cab, both hands still wrapped around the wheel so tight they’ve left imprints, Johnny finally releases his withheld breath. Blinking a couple times he looks over to his passenger as she pushes herself into a sitting position, holding the back of her head and cursing in a language Johnny couldn’t understand. Throwing his door open, Johnny climbs out and runs his fingers through his hair, realizing the moment of panic had nearly sobered him completely. “You gonna be alright?” “I tink so.” She says, climbing across the cab to climb out the open door. With a thud Johnny kicks a flat tire with his shoe. “Where did you learn to drive?” The woman asks, crossing her arms across her chest to hide her naked breasts. “Shut up.” Johnny replies. Reaching around her he retrieves the keys from the ignition and turns away. “Dun you tell me to shut up.” Ignoring her angry voice, Lexicon opens the trunk and pulls aside the mat hiding the spare tire. “You could have got us killed!” She continues. Johnny chuckles. “What’s so funny?” His hands come up to either side, in one hand is a half rusted gas can and in the other a dirty gray shirt that may have been white at one time. “What is that?” She asks. “This is where the spare fucking tire is supposed to be!” Johnny’s voice raises steadily as he speaks. Slamming the trunk closed he drops a heavy fist onto it angrily. “Fucking gringo!” The chic erupts and breaks into spanish. Shaking his head and laughing again Johnny leans on the trunk and lowers his head into his hands. “Where’s the nearest town?” “What?” The woman turns back to him, breaking her tirade short. “A town, where’s the nearest town?” “I dunno.” “What do you mean you don’t know?” “I dunno I dunno, back way we come!” She points down the road. “Back the way we’ve come, yeah. We’ve only been driving for what.” Checks his watch. “Four damn hours!” “Is not my fault!” She spits at him. “Shut up already.” He throws the dirty shirt at her. “Put this on, we’re walking.” “Thas a long walk essay, that why you so tired?” The elderly Mexican sips his flask and hands it to Johnny again. “Oh no.” He sips the flask and winces. “No, see that was just the beginning.” “What happened next?” A young boy asks. Next to him was another boy, slightly older. Together they sat cross legged on the floor and looked up at Johnny. “Well, this mamacita didn’t want to walk, she didn’t want to leave the car at all. She insisted we wait for somebody to come.” “Back road like that, coulda been days meng, that what happened to joo?” “See I was thinking the same thing, but I couldn’t just leave her there in the middle of nowhere.” “What did you do?” A boy asks. “She only left me one choice.” Taking a final swig before handing the flask back. “I carried the bitch…” ”Put me down!” “Shut up already! Shut up!” The woman beats her fists against the wrestlers broad back to no avail, slung over his shoulder her legs flail about in the air as he trudges on determinedly. “Put me down!” She says one last time. Her lips part. Teeth bared. She bites down on his shoulder with a squeal. “Fuckin bitch!” Johnny swears and drops her right there. With a short scream she falls to the dirt and coughs, pushing herself onto her hands and knees. That dirty gray shirt now tied across her chest nearly comes undone as Johnny lifts her to her feet abruptly. Without a seconds hesitation she brings her hand around and smacks him across the face. “You’re really starting to piss me off.” Johnny says, his cheek already reddening. “Fuck joo.” She spits on the ground. “What is your problem?” “We go back to the car and wait, we could walk for days!” “We could wait for days, at least this way we’re covering ground.” “No, we go back.” She insists. Johnny is about to retort when a glimmer catches his eye. “I told joo!” “I’ll be damned.” He says as the car approaches. They step to the side of the road as a dirty work truck barrels down the road and finally stops in front of them. Three men sit in the cab with half grins as the girl steps up to the window. The greasy Mexican driver leans out and plants his lips against hers as the shotgun passenger climbs out. “No…” Johnny knew the score before the dirty rifle was even pointed in his direction. “This the gringo?” The driver asks. “Yes.” She spits at Johnny. “Why dun you go an get on joor knees essay.” The armed one says with a grin. “You fuckin bitch.” Johnny grumbles as he lowers to his right knee and raises his arms, slipping his hands behind his head. “Joo bettah hope ya got muney homes.” The armed man says, fishing Johnny’s wallet out of his back pocket. “Take it, just go.” “Jus go?” The man looks to his friends in the truck with a grin. “He’s tellin us what to do meng.” “C’mon, you don’t want to kill me.” “He’s big american wrestler, mucho deneiro.” Johnny winces a bit. “We take heem with us homes.” The driver says. The chuckle from the rifle bearing wet back was all the inentive Johnny needed to act, his right hand shoots down and grips the barrel of the rifle before his assailant even knew he’d acted. Johnny rises to his feet, his left hand swinging around for a viscous open palm strike when lights explode in his vision. Quicker than Johnny had given the man credit for he rams the butt of his rifle into Lexicon’s face. As Johnny’s reeling he drives it into the side of his head and nothing but inky darkness coalesces within his mind. “So thas what happened to hombre?” “Joo got taken meng.” A boy agrees with his elder. “I got taken alright.” “Thas pretty bad.” A newcomer to Johnny’s left comments. “That’s not even the bad part…”
