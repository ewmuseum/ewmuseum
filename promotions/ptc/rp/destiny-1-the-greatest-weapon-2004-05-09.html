Destiny 1: The Greatest Weapon

9 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-05-09 I’m so tired of being here. Surrounded by all the blind hype and selfish desires of the people. Their only desire is for me to win for them. They want me to win. They want me to fight. They want me to make money for them. I’m nothing more than a prostitute now. I’m whoring myself out for anyone who seems willing enough to take the chance. Jacob Stevens? He used me to pay his debt off. Karla? She tried to pay me to throw a match once before, and this time she’s trying to get me to sway to her side. Yet, where do I fit in all of this? Each day that passes by is just another day that I hate to fight more and more. However, I can’t do anything else. There’s never going to be a chance for me to do anything. This is it though. Once I’m done with the Cruiserweight Tournament, I’m finished. No more fighting. No more wrestling. No more debts. No more agendas. No more I wasn’t even sure I could get this far. Having not fought in months. Having not trained in months. I was worried when I had to face Alex Caine and Angel. Then I was worried about facing the Fool. After that, I was worried about Carys. Yet with her it was much more than that. She was an upstart; a fan favorite with heavy momentum. I was worried I would have hurt her, and then suffered the backlash. However, I managed to defeat her. She was incredibly quick in the ring but as the match wore on her inexperience in big matches became evident. One mistake is all it took to steal the match from her. She faulted in her own offense and after my Asylum Grounds was hit, she fell to the mat; easy pickings for the pin and victory. I cannot expect the same kind of luck against Seymour Almasy though. He’s a confusing enigma for me. His small frame leaves him vulnerable for heavy attacks, yet he’s quick. He’s a terrific talent who’s just a bit short on reality. I know not to overlook that though. His lack of reality has led him to become one of the more dominant wrestlers in the Prime Time Central Networks. He’s a former Global Champion. That means he was good enough to beat anyone the PTC threw at him at one point. He’s won his bracket, and I’ve won mine. I guess it’s destiny that we both now jus square off for the first time. It’s always been in my nature to test my skills against the best out there. It’s been no secret that Seymour has wanted to prove he could beat anyone. Now we both finally get our chance. It’s destiny that’s bought us here to the semifinals It’s destiny that only one of us should move on to face either the feisty Karina Wolfenden, or Ethan Knight. It’s a funny word….Destiny <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Previously… News of the Del Fuego gym burning down finally reached Syko at the Morir Sonando Casino. It was Karla’s plan all along. She never wanted to be friends with Syko. That part was obvious. She never intended to forgive Syko for disrespecting her. That part was obvious too. Her sole intent was to sway Syko away from his defenses and strike him hard where it hurt: His newfound freedom. Sure, Syko wasn’t entirely free. He was still fighting, but training the kids in an ironic twist of fate was his freedom. Now he lost that though. He could very well lose everything else as well. Ring! Ring! Syko pried Karla away from his lips and then pushed her to the ground. He wiped his lips clean in disgust and answered his silver-colored cell phone. Karla wiped her lips as well and seemed a bit agitated by Syko’s reaction. She quickly got over that as she shot Syko a smile raising to her feet. Syko was silent answering the phone. It seemed the whole while the other line was talking. And by the reaction on Syko’s face, it seemed that what they had to say was very important. Syko hung up the phone turning slowly towards Karla. “You want to kiss me after all?” Karla asked with such ego. “That’s why I am here, isn’t it?” Syko remarked. “Among other things…” Karla whispered back. “I know.” With that Syko reached into his back pocket and took out a 9mm pistol. Fully loaded. He pointed it straight at Karla’s face and walked slowly towards his prey. Karla was his prey now, much like Seymour Almasy will be soon. Karla’s once deceitfully-joyish face was now filled with terror. She stumbled back trying to find her call button, but it was nowhere in sight. “What are you doing Syko?!” She gasped, her lips trembling. While her evident fears would have charmed any man, it only made Syko worse. He chased her to the end of the room until she had no place to run. She was stuck between a concrete wall and a man with a gun pointing to her face. “Don’t shoot me, please!” Karla pleaded. She couldn’t scream. She could have tired, but she knew no one would hear her beyond the soundproof walls. Syko lifted the gun straight to her forehead until she was staring down the barrel of the gun. Tears rolled down her face as she began to whimper and plead for her life. “This is what you wanted…” Syko explained. “To lure me here and use me. Do you think you know who I am? Do you think you know what you’ve done?” Karla shook her head. “You don’t know. You have no idea what you just did. You have no idea who I am. I will never forgive you. I will never forget what you did last time and what you did here. You’re just a pathetic whore. A cum-guzzling redhead prostitute who’s success in life comes from picking on the weaker people.” Karla pleaded for Syko to stop it, one more time. “What an accomplishment that is. You have no idea what it really means to be strong. You have no idea about real success. You have no idea about people. You just use them like you use this casino. So don’t tell me you know who I am. Don’t tell me you know what I can do.” “Syko…please…” “Because you have no idea of what I can do to you..” -Click- “Until you realize that…this is me….” -Click- “And this is you…” -Bang- <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Karla stared down the barrel of the gun. Syko intended to shoot. Syko never shot. As Karla was seeing her life flash before her eyes, her security rushed into the room. She couldn’t scream or call for help on her intercom but she forgot about her other means: Cameras. The guards so what was going on and rushed to her aide. Just in time they were, as Karla whispered her last goodbyes. The Calvary arrived, and Karla was spared. Syko managed to escape the two guards who showed up at the room, and made his exit out of the casino. The guards would have given him chase, but Karla ordered them to stay put. She was still pretty shaken up from the ordeal. <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> “Why in the world did this have to happen?” Jacobs shook his head and shrugged. “Who are those people? Syko?” Syko looked up from his seat on the bench. “I told you already. Karla and I are two people who have crossed paths at the wrong time. She’s hell-bent on trying to destroy me, as I am hell-bent on making sure she doesn’t do that.” Jacobs sighed. “And because of that, the Gym is burnt to the ground.” “I’m sorry. That was never my intention. I never intended for my problems to reach you, or Jack, or anyone else in the gym.” “I know, Syko. Sometimes destiny is mankind’s cruelest enemy.” “Please don’t day that word again.” Syko requested as he rolled his eyes leaning back against the wall. “Why?” Jacob asked crossing his arms. “It’s a pet peeve of mine to believe that we don’t have any say in what happens to us. I refuse to believe that because of one mistake I do, everyone else has to suffer from it.” “It doesn’t always work like that…” Jacob tried to explain. “It has for me. I disobeyed someone’s advice and fears and she paid it with her life. Look at this situation. I’ve gotten involved with Karla, and you had your fingers cut off.” “Yeah, but this was different!” Jacob exclaimed. “This was because of my own fault! You had nothing to do with me gambling in their casino and losing half a million dollars! That was my fault!” “It’s still the same though. Once she knew you were connected to me, it only ignited her lust in trying to destroy me.” Jacob took a seat next to Syko on the bench inside the Police Department, where they were both waiting for detectives to interview them about the arson. “I’m upset that you feel that way. Besides…what fun could that be if you took on the world’s burden?” “Not too much fun.” Jacob laughed. “See? And shouldn’t you worry about the tournament? Yes, this is important. But arson, much like other crimes, is a job for the police to solve, not you.” “But I know who did it.” “We both do, Syko. But in this day and age, that means nothing. We’ll tell the police what we know. After that, it’s up to them to get the evidence and put two and two together.” “I think you’re right…” Syko agreed. “It’s best to leave the job to the professionals and just do what I can do. He believes he has supernatural powers that enable him to enhance his abilities. Despite his naiveness, he’s still an outstanding fighter. He’s had his, and is coming back towards them again. I know it was already mentioned about me and him having won two of the last major PTC sponsored events. You know what that means?” Jacob shook his head. “It means that we are one of the better fighters in all of Prime Time Central. At least we were at one point in our careers. Now we both have a chance to face each other to determine just who is the better man. This match isn’t personal. There’s no bad blood between us. It’s all just purely competition. He thinks he is better than me. That may be, but he’ll have to prove it.” “But I thought you didn’t want to fight anymore?” Jacob asked. “What made you change your mind?” Syko leaned forward resting his elbow on his knees. “I think it was ego. I’m not sure though. We both know I only started this tournament because of you. After your debt was paid off, I stayed for me. Up until I fought Carys, my only reason for staying was just because I didn’t want to leave the tournament without a proper dismissal. Yet, after my grueling match with her, I realized one thing. I never wanted to fight. I don’t ever want to fight. At the same time, I don’t want to quit either. I know my time to go away from here is slowly coming. So, rather than quit and leave the business on bad terms like I did long ago, I’ve decided to end my last run properly…” “You mean…” Syko nodded. “That’s right. Syko was thankful that no one was hurt. It was also helpful that Karla was a suspect for many other crimes, most of them more heinous than arson. However, she was never indicted, and when she was, she was freed on bail. Syko told him everything he knew about Karla and their relationship. Syko parked his car and shut the engines off to a full stop. I can’t believe I’m fighting Seymour Almasy soon, he thought. Syko sat in his car, inside the driveway, too lazy to move out. Maybe it wasn’t laziness. Maybe it was just his time to think. What makes you so great Seymour Almasy? What makes you a favorite in every encounter you are in? You’re not the most intelligible person here. Neither am I, yet I can tell what’s real and not. You refuse to accept reality for what it is. So what makes you such a terrific fighter? It certainly cannot be your small frame. You only stand at 5’ 8”. You only weight as much as I do, because I had to lose weight for this tournament. So what makes you so great? It’s not the titles you’ve won. It’s not the people you’ve beaten. It definitely isn’t the magic and supernatural abilities you think you possess. It sure as hell isn’t the Ultimate Weapon, either. No matter who wins. You better realize that in having someone to love, who loves you back, in Aerith, already makes you a winner. The match should mean nothing, because you’ve already won a far greater battle. Syko turned around and saw an attractive lady in her forties. She was evidently older than Syko, but seemed to have taken care of herself so she looked rather young for her age. She was dressed in casual clothing. “Can I help you madam?” “My name is Marissa Santos…” the lady explained. “I know this is pretty awkward for you. It is for me..” “I’ll say…” “Are you the person they call Syko Arsonist?” Syko chuckled. “Yes, I am. Do you want an autograph or something?” “No.” Marissa remarked. “Can you do me a favor though? Let me see your left cheek?” Syko turned his face in obedience. Marissa looked up and saw the scar she had been waiting her entire life to see. She was sure she had finally found her son. Then again, she was sure a lot of times before. But now, it was different. She couldn’t explain it, but she felt she had finally found her son after all this time. “That’s a pretty…nasty…scar…” Marissa said biting her lip to keep herself from crying. Syko furrowed his eyebrows at her, wondering why she would be so hurt. “Yes, it was I guess. I don’t mind it though. It gives me my uniqueness.” “How did you get it?” Syko shrugged. “I guess I fell down when I was little, I don’t know.” “What if I told you, you got it because your grandfather knocked you against the lamp, and the lamp cut your face?” “I’d say that you have a very active imagination…” Syko replied.
