The Big Shiny Sight Receptacle - Part I

21 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-21 WASHINGTON, D.C. - RENDEZVOUS POINT A DAY OR TWO AFTER BONG MIRACULOUSLY DEFEATED GORDO BROAD DAYLIGHT It was a picture-perfect day in Washington, D.C. The clear sky was an unbelievably deep shade of azure, and the unimpeded rays of the sun gently kissed the marble city, giving it a remarkable sheen. Today was the kind of day not even the most apathetic homebody could ignore -- it was so beautiful, and the weather was so perfect, that one just had to take a stroll through downtown. And, if one was truly inclined to partake of an invigorating constitutional on this afternoon, chances are that one would pass by a glamourous department store, with elegantly-dressed mannequins arranged neatly in the front windows for all the world to see. Did I really just say "invigorating constitutional?" Man, my mom was right... I am a loser. At any rate, the craftsmanship and detail of the mannqeuins were truly remarkable. In fact, there was one in particular, sporting a rather dashing tuxedo, that looked almost life-like... ...and for good reason. "I say, Shafted! How much longer do I have to hold still? My bloody arms are about to fall off!" That's right, it was Jimmy Bong, Agent Double-Oh-Nothing... pretending to be a mannequin modeling a tuxedo! It was an astonishing display of his versatility. "Quit complaining, Bong!" crackled the voice of Majorly Shafted over a miniscule two-way radio unit wedged in Bong's right ear. "Keep an eye on the alley across the street! Kolehnkov and Iva will be arriving at the agreed-upon rendezvous point any minute now!" Bong couldn't help but feel slightly indignant upon hearing the orders of Shafted. After all, Shafted was a man whose OHMCS status was officially listed as "equipment" due to the fact that he was genetically engineered to be an indestructible human shield. Nonetheless, Shafted's abilities as a human were every bit as effective as his abilities as a shield, and Bong decided not to protest further for the time being. Truth be told, this was certainly no time to argue. Bong's pursuit of Johannes Frederick Sebastian Billy-Bob Garfeld, the world's most fiendish fiend, had steadily grown ice-cold ever since the investigation had commenced. Nothing was going right for OHMCS -- they had been falsely led to believe that Garfeld was somehow involved with the GTT4 professional wrestling tournament, which Bong had been forced to compete in against his will. To make matters worse, the American government decided to shut down OHMCS foreign operations in Washington, D.C. on what seemed like a whim. The excuse offered by CIA Agent Walter Durnstrom, the man who served OHMCS their official "eviction," was that the United States was pursuing Garfeld and had the situation well in hand. That excuse didn't satisfy N, head of OHMCS. She sensed the Americans were trying to hide something, even if their intentions of putting a halt to Garfeld's aspirations of global domination were noble. It would have been simple enough to just sit back and let the Americans catch the evil madman, but at what cost? The Americans were definitely keeping a secret from the British, and the only chance OHMCS had of uncovering the truth was to find Garfeld before the CIA did. The odds of that happening looked depserately bleak, until OHMCS was contacted by a Russian intelligence agent named Dmitri Kolehnkov with startling new information. Apparently, Garfeld's fiendish plot to take over the world had something to do with a secret weapon left over from the Cold War. Exactly what that weapon was, Bong didn't know... but hopefully, his secret meeting with Kolehnkov, and Russian field agent Iva Gunnonmi (who had been rendered incapable of speaking any language except Bushman Swahili after a terrible accident in Africa), would shed some light on the situation. There were only two things Bong knew for sure: Garfeld was somewhere in Washington, and time was running out. "I certainly hope they get here soon," Bong mumbled to Shafted, who was clearly hiding somewhere out of sight. "I've been standing here for damn near twelve hours!" "Well, it's the best vantage point we have of the alley across the street," Shafted's voice replied over Bong's earpiece. "Best vantage point!? Then how come you get to hide in that five-star hotel across the street, while I have pretend to be a statue in this shop window!?" Bong complained through his teeth. A quick look across the street from the department store revealed that there was indeed a beautiful hotel, about thirty stories high. Several rooms overlooked the alley that Bong was keeping an eye on. "Why Bong," said Shafted, sounding almost pleased, "you know perfectly well that I can't be seen by the American government within city limits! This hotel room is the only safe place for me to observe the meeting spot!" "Well why couldn't I have hidden in the hotel room with you?" Bong whined. "At least then I would have been comfortable!" "Just think of it as endurance training," Shafted replied with a tone of amusement. "Yes, that's it... endurance training for your next match! After all, we can't have you getting eliminated from the tournament, otherwise we'll never find Garfeld before the Americans do." "What a load of poppycock," Bong mumbled, as a mother and her young child passed by on the sidewalk. The child stopped and looked through the window at Bong, while the mother kept walking ahead without noticing. "Looky, looky, Mommy!" cried the child. "That dummy is talking!" "HEY! I'm not a dummy, I happen to be very intelligent!" Bong shouted back. The child started bawling, and ran back toward the mother. "WAAAAAAH! The mean old dummy yelled at me, Mommy!" "I'm not old!" Bong retorted. "Shut up, Bong!" hissed Shafted. "You'll blow your cover!" "Forget it!" Bong snarled back. "I'm getting out of this place, before..." "Hang on," Shafted interjected. "I think I see them coming." "It's about bloody time," said Bong. "What's going on?" "They're getting into position," Shafted explained. "Kolehnkov has an envelope containing two data disks. He didn't say what was on them specifically, but he mentioned they contain vital information about Garfeld's plot. Once he places the envelope in a secure place, he and Iva will depart. That's when it will be your turn to act, Bong... retrieve the disks quickly. For all we know, we're being watched by the Americans... or even by Garfeld's men." "This all seems terribly unnecessary," Bong remarked. "Why on earth couldn't Kolehnkov have just given us this information when he met us face-to-face?" "We have to be as discreet as possible, Bong!" said Shafted. "If the Americans catch wind that we're in contact with the Russians, the political ramifications could be very unpleasant." "Hmph, very well then," sighed Bong. "What's going on now? I can't see them yet from my angle." "Kolehnkov is pulling the envelope out of his pocket," said Shafted. "Now he and Iva are talking. Well, Kolehnkov moreso than Iva... wait a second!" "What?" asked Bong. "What's going on?" "Kolehnkov!" Shafted gasped. "He's pulled a gun on Iva!" "Horrors!" yelped Bong. "What the bloody hell did he do that for!?" "I don't know, but I'm not waiting around to find out!" said Shafted. "I'm taking him down!" Bong looked across the street into the alley, and could make out two shadows cast against the wall. One shadow appeared to be a person holding a gun in its hand, and the other shadow was that of a taller, more slender person holding their arms up. A moment later, there was a BANG! that echoed along the street, and one of the shadows fell to the ground. "Got him!" said Shafted with relief. "Bong, get over there now!" "Right!" exclaimed Bong, who immediately leapt forward and crashed through the store window onto the sidewalk, taking everybody walking along the street by surprise. Bong stood upright, and brushed himself off. "I meant through the door, Bong," groaned Shafted. "Oh, so killing a man from a hotel room in broad daylight is all fine and dandy, but when I break through a window, it's a crime!?" asked Bong irritatedly, as he rushed across the street while several bystanders gave him odd looks. "Just make sure Iva is alright and the disks are secure," Shafted replied. Bong made his way down the alley and turned right around the corner at the end. He cringed at the sight of Kolehnkov's lifeless body laying on the ground, while Iva sat against the wall, looking stunned by the whole affair. "Iva! Are you alright?" asked Bong. Iva looked up at Bong and gave him what appeared to be a sarcastic glance, and clicked her tongue a few times. "Well excuse me for being concerned over the fact that you were almost killed!" Bong replied angrily. "And I'll thank you not to mention my mother and elephants in the same sentence ever again!" Bong looked down at the ground, and noticed a white envelope sitting next to Kolehnkov's body. He stooped down to pick it up, then immediately ripped it open. There were two red data disks inside. "I have the disks, Shafted!" said Bong happily. "They don't appear to be damaged." "Excellent," said Shafted. "Make sure you hang onto them. Iva didn't have authorization from her government to carry such high-level intelligence on her person, so you'll have to keep them until we get them back to OHMCS." "Right," said Bong, who looked down at Kolehnkov's body and shook his head sadly. "Well, I guess somebody had to die sometime. At least it was a minor character with little or no relevance to this story." "Bong, you're breaking the fourth wall again!" exclaimed Shafted. "Er, right, sorry," said Bong sheepishly. "You should probably do something about Kolehnkov's body, too," said Shafted. "We can't let the police find the body of a dead Russian intelligence officer, or else... well, the Americans are going to have a fit." "I have a perfect idea!" said Bong. "Iva, quick, help me reposition Kolehnkov's body before rigor mortis sets in..." Iva gave Bong a quizzical look, but shrugged and got up to help. A few minutes later, Bong was back in front of the department store, looking inside the shattered window where he had been standing all day. In his old place now stood the stiffened body of Dmitri Kolehnkov, wearing Bong's tuxedo. Oddly enough, Bong was still wearing a tuxedo of his own. "It's a good thing I always wear a tuxedo under my tuxedo," he smiled to himself. Bong looked in his coat pocket, where the two data disks were being kept well-hidden. "Better get these back to OHMCS so we can get some answers." Bong turned around and walked across the street to the hotel, just as the sun was setting over Washington, D.C. He was hoping that the data on these disks would finally answer the questions he had been facing for so long. What was Garfeld's plan? What was this "weapon" Kolehnkov had been talking about? But now there was yet another question: Why did Kolehnkov try to kill Iva? The answers would come in time... and they would start coming very, very soon. TO BE CONTINUED...
