Calling Upon The Devil

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 *In the beginning good always overpowered the evils of all man’s sins.. But in time the nations grew weak and our cities fell to slums while evil stood strong. In the dusts of hell lurked the blackest of hates, for he whom they feared awaited them… Now many many lifetimes later, lay destroyed, beaten, beaten down, only the corpses of rebels and ashes of dreams and blood stained streets. It has been written, “Those who have the youth, have the future.” So come now, children of the beast, be strong and shout with the Devil.* Sometimes a figure so powerful and so ruthless emerges from the depths of hell, that no one man could ever stop him, let alone contain him. As the years and times have persisted the underworld has produced many evils, likes the universe has never seen. Caligula, Genghis Kahn, Hitler, Saddam Hussein, George W. Bush. Others who do not make it to the throne of world power sit in the reeds near the bank of the river. Never jumping at the wrong opportunity and making themselves known, while keeping the dark secrets of power hidden. Truth be told, they like to watch and enjoy looking at moments of weakness and analyzing their victim. Only Satan himself knows exactly when and where they are going to strike. No human being alive could ever predict the animosity writhed deep within. Others have forced themselves to the for front and crushing all the competition in their wake. But No one has ever realized that the true power is those who lie in wait. The smart man. The man who knows every crevice of the brain of his foe. The smart man can taste fear in the eyes of his opponent and gets inside his mind to drive him insane. Psychology is the best medicine. An evil man who can pear into your mind and exploit you is pure genius. Its an Eternal Nightmare. A boat is seen washing in the waves off the coast of Maine… A mans standing just about six foot six stands in the bow. It’s dark and the sea is pretty rough, fog and rain blanket the coast. The man is just a shadow in the night. The boat creeps closer and closer to the shore through the heavy surf, the man stands fast and strong, unmoved by the killer hands of the sea reaching up for him, he doesn’t even acknowledge them. The cries of a thousand dead sailors scream at him in the wind, yet they go unnoticed. His hair soaked by the rain and heavy fog blows hard in the wind. Wet and heavy the long death black locks of hair are being pulled by the thousand dead sailors wanting more company. But he knows they can not pull him in. They were once mortal, they hold nothing over him. The boat rises to the crest of a 22 foot wave ready to crash on the rock littered beach. The man unmoved, his gaze focused on the dark horizon in front of him. The wave slopes forward, the boat unsettled, could tip over any second. The man’s gaze is unbroken. Ten feet from the beach, five feet from the beach. The boat crashes in an explosion of wood splinters and cold, Atlantic sea water. The man takes this in stride and steps on the beach as the hands of the Atlantic push up his back. The wave rushes in thirty feet to the shore. The man makes a steady walk toward the dry land, the waves begin to rush back out, thousand hands from thousand dead sailors do not wish for him to escape. They need the company, they yearn for some one new to add to their prestigious list. The hands can not grasp him, he is untouchable, soon he has escaped to the dry land. His gaze still on the horizon. Looking toward the south. His steps continue in that direction. He reaches the tall thick grass of the beach in the dunes. He kneels down there for a bit of a rest before he continues. His eyes ice blue, so cold they are almost white, but there is something else behind those eyes. Souls of his victims? He believes nothing of the occult, only his true inhibitions. It’s still too dark to make out what he looks like. He is just a shadow among the living. “Years ago…” he speaks, but trails off thinking. “Years ago I turned to a rage, felt the evil inside of me. I tried to contain it, but soon realized it was my destiny and that I should no longer deny my true father. My true father ordered me to kill my family. I did. I was arrested, set to be executed that night, but escaped. There is no jail cell in no country that could ever hold me.” As the story was told, the man was 13 at the time and lived in Northeast India with his family. He grew restless one night, his eyes became demonic, his voice had grown deeper and in the single one room hut that his family lived in he killed his mother. His father. His grandmother. His grandfather. His little sister. His older brother. Not with weapons, but his bare hands. There were no signs of a struggle, they seemed willing to be sacrificed. All their hearts were removed while they slept. He was immediately arrested by the town guards, beaten and thrown into a three foot by three foot cell. He was set for execution and when they arrived back at his cell. He was gone. No signs of anything broken. He was just gone. “I went to Japan for a few years, they tried to execute me a hundred times. But they were always unsuccessful. You can’t kill a man who is already dead on the inside. But full of life in his heart!” After he had killed his family and loved ripping hearts out of people, just for the shear enjoyment of it. There was nothing better. Especially when he started to eat it right in front of them before they died. He did that two of his executioners. The Japanese guardsmen just ran away. He was a monster. Though no one could ever see his face. He was always a shadow even in lit room. “I was an outcast of my family and my people. I would be made fun of and tortured. Though I enjoyed the pain too much and soon the beatings stopped. It was sad. So I became a shadow in the world of the living. By then I no longer considered myself part of the human race and began to hate them too much. I wanted to kill them all. I killed a lot, but I haven’t killed nearly enough.” Violence is what the man lives for. Waking people up in the night by ripping out their still beating heart has earned him the name The Eternal Nightmare. He can be caught, but some how he always avoids execution. He either disappears with out a trace or he kills guards or executioner. He could be the worlds greatest magician, but with a killing side. “I just want to fight, I love the violence of life. It’s the only good quality a human has left. But I will not rest until all the humans are laid to rest. For I am The Eternal Nightmare. Sweet dreams.” The man stands up after enough rest and begins walking south again. He is just a dark image in the dark sky. He disappears into the fog and rain. There is a stir in the sheets, Stecchino sits up in a cold sweat. He looks around the room and he looks to the open window to his left. It wasn’t open before. Could he be in here? Stecchino gets up out of bed and puts on a robe. “Tigam?” he whispers into the blackness. “Tigam, are you here?” There is nothing except the wind blowing in through the window. Tigam is a master at hiding in the shadows, he could be anywhere. Stecchino looks around some more, trying to find the possible intruder in his home. Why would he dream about him being in Maine, when he is resting in Rome? Why would Balsala play with his mind like that? Why would he be here. He is the hired assassin. Stecchino walks out into the living room. The front door is wide open with a piece of paper floating around the area of the front door, he walks over and picks it up. Tony, Please forgive me for entering your home uninvited, I had to make sure you were capable of dealing with me. I looked back into the dark regions of your dreams and conscience and you are a disgusting man. I will do the job and allow you to gain what you so desire. Please leave my bounty in your mailbox tomorrow and I will see you Friday night. Best Regards, “The Eternal Nightmare” Tigam Balsala. Stecchino has a evil smile spread across his face as he looks out into the darkness where his hired gun left. Balsala could possibly be more evil and more dangerous than Stecchino is. But to gain what you want, you have to sometimes have to stop playing with fire and play with something a bit bigger. An atomic bomb that has a hair trigger.
