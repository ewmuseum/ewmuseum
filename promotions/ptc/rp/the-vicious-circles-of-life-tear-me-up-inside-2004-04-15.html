The Vicious Circles of Life, tear Me Up Inside

15 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-15 (Special thanks to Justin Evers for use of his Catch phrase) It’s been an unseasonably warm spring in and around the Toronto area, a fact that has few people complaining, and even fewer remaining indoors. Barbecues have been unearthed from their long winter sleep, and the smell of freshly cooked hamburger fills the air…provided you’re some place where the smog doesn’t choke out everything else. The warm air makes for perfect riding weather, either that or perfect for driving with your tops down and windows rolled all the way open. It’s in that exact position that Dawn McFarlane can be found; Roaring down the empty stretch of highway outside of her estate, with her mid-length black hair snapping out behind her in the wind. There’s not mistaking her state of mind really, the megawatt smile on her face betraying the thoughts bubbling just beneath the surface…happy would be the wrong word right now. Dawn McFarlane is Exultant. “I finally did it!” She exclaimed to the road, “I’ve finally broken that black halo that’s been hanging around my head!” Months of failure and self doubt, months of loss after loss, of only being able to prove herself in a tag match or a three way. Not a singles win since Darwin’s Revenge 8 months ago…and a spiraling depression of drugs and abuse to go with the losses. The smile fades slowly from Dawn’s face as she thinks back to her months with Marcus, the endless nights with no light at the end of the tunnel….and then in the space of only a few weeks it seemed as though her whole life was turning around. First it had been Corvus, coming for her out of the darkness like an avenging angle, breaking her free of Marcus’ oppressive yoke and lifting her to her feet, forcing her to really look at what she had done and the nothing she had become. Then PTC had come, Brad Johnson encouraging her to accompany Fury and represent WMW in the coming Cruiserweight tournament. In his own way forcing her to step out from the protective casing she’d built around herself and look at the world around her, really look for the first time in a long while. Her mind had a field day with it of course, telling her the things people were going to think, the things people were going to say…her will had almost failed her then, and she’d almost backed out of competing before the round one brackets were even fully set. Again Corvus had saved her, pushing her along and driving her to succeed…filling in for the ambition she seemed to have lost. In her own way Tessa Windsor had fueled it too…showing Dawn that it WAS possible to succeed, that it was possible to reach out for your dreams without assuming failure. And she had won. Dark Horse, they were calling her now. Sleeper and Surprise were all words that she was starting to hear after her first round win, after she had defied the predictions of her peers and advanced. And of course…Upset. The smile broke onto her face again, the words of her peers and comrades flooding over her as she heeled her dark blue corvette coup into a sharp brake turn, the wheels squealing in protest as they shed black skin onto the road. She grinned at her reflection in the rear view mirror, finally liking the woman she saw looking back at her. Dawn ran a hand through her black hair, noting with some amusement that she had allowed it to grow out a little again, the frosted blue tips now hanging just past her shoulders in a shaggy cascade. Her eyes were different too, bright emerald eyes that reflected two things so long lost that she had begun to forget what they even felt like. Hope. Ambition. Hope that she was not as far gone as she’d originally thought, and ambition to succeed, to push herself to continue the winning streak she considered to be just starting. With a final wink at her reflection her drove her foot back onto the gas and peeled off again, this time in the direction of home. ******************************************** The snails pace with which she crept into the drive stood in sharp contrast to the earlier speed she’d been enjoying. The only real sound a faint purr from the engine and the sound of gravel crunching under the weight of her tires. She eased the car into the garage and closed the door herself, wincing slightly at the crash it made as it met with the floor. She wasn’t the only one who had succeeded her most recent match, and Corvus had the wounds to prove it, slumbering a deep healing sleep in the master bedroom they shared. Her hand drifted up to the bandage on her own forehead, fingers gently touching the rough exterior that protected the most recent addition to her list of wounds from pro wrestling: Chair to the head. She shook her head suddenly, trying once more to let her good mood slide in over the anger she felt from that strike. Dawn slipped into the mudroom and removed her boots, gently closing the door behind her and heading to the kitchen in the back of the house. The suns last rays crept through the porch door and windows, glistening on the tiled floor with a warm buttery glow. She tip-toed across the floor to the fridge, stomach not so gently reminding her that it had been many hours since she had last eaten…when the phone rang. Dawn jumped a good foot in the air with a small yelp, then clamped her hands over mouth in annoyance at her own outburst. She snagged the phone off of its cradle before it could ring again, “Hello?” “Dawn?” An all to familiar voice was on the other end of the phone, rich tones carrying all the way from wherever he was to make her stomach clench in on itself. “Justin? Is that you?” “None other.” Came the slightly smug reply. She hung up suddenly, slamming the receiver back into it’s cradle with such force that for a moment she was afraid she’d knocked the phone clear off the wall. It rang again though, quickly dashing that fear as she snatched it up again, “Y’know,” Came Justin’s voice, “I generally don’t call people back when they hang up like that. You should be falttered.” Her mouth pressed into a firm line and she folded her free arm across her chest, “Oh yeah? And I suppose I should be grateful for the fact that you handcuffed me to the ring and drove that chair into my head too. Silence. ‘Ha’ she thought, ‘Chew on that a while.’ “That was business and you know it. I couldn’t just let what you did stand…besides, you showed me yours first.” Her face flushed at the mention of the chair she’d swung into HIS forehead just two weeks before, “Yeah well that was business too…” she returned mockingly. “Then we don’t need to worry about it right? You hit me, I hit you and we’re all fine and happy now.” “What do you want Justin? I have things to do.” His voice softened almost imperceptibly, “I was calling to check up on you. Since Marcus has been calling me trying to get in on what Top Shelf has got, I guess he isn’t hanging around with you anymore.” Dawn closed her eyes and leaned against the warm texture of the wallpaper behind her, closing her eyes and massaging her temples with her free hand. ‘No he’s not here. Corvus would kill him if he so much as set foot on these grounds again.” ‘Well good…” came the slightly gruff reply. She sighed, “Look Justin, you know what happened in that 6 man tag wasn’t personal, I needed out and there wasn’t any other way to do it that would keep my pride intact.” “You coulda just said good-bye, I didn’t need the chair in my face to emphasize the point ya know.” “What was I supposed to do? Dis wanted me out and I wasn’t even part of the team anymore…Corvus gave me that and I had to stick up for him. I wasn’t going to just let you guys have your way with him in the middle of the ring.” Silence again. Then, “He’s really got you eh? Somebody finally managed to get under the Lioness’ skin.” She blushed this time, a fiery red that crept up her neck and into her cheeks, “If you want to put it that way then yes, he’s really ‘Got me.’ Things have really changed for me since Corvus walked into my life Justin, and changed for the better.” “Better then while we were working together?” “Justin we based our association on revenge, pure and simple. We both wanted to hurt Fury and we made it happen. I’m not going to deny that I like you, or that there was something between us…but things are different for me now.” “Well as long as I don’t have to see you all bruised up anymore then I guess that’s a step in the right direction. You know if you’d told me what was going on…I would ended that guy for you.” Dawn closed her eyes again, “I know Justin.” “Well…I guess that’s all I wanted to say then. Congrats on your win, and good luck in the second round. You better take this tournament home for us.” A slight smirk crept to the corner of her mouth, “Yeah, trust me I intend to.” “You can’t slouch here. Karina Wolfenden is one of the toughest ladies this sport has to offer…stay focused.” Her turn for silence. “I guess that’s it for now then. I’ll see around Dawn…probably in the ring.” Click. Dial tone. She stared at the phone for a long moment before replacing it on it’s cradle. ‘Fuck you too Justin,” she said softly. “As in Justin Evers?” A voice rumbled from the doorway, again making Dawn jump in surprise. Corvus was leaning against the door frame, his arms folded across his expansive chest and a blank expression on his face. “What the hell did that Top Shelf lackey want with you?” Dawn shook her head and crossed the floor towards him, resting one hand on his arm, “To wish me good luck…and to clear some of the air between us.” ‘Clear the air?” “Yeah. We know we’re on different sides now, I think we just both needed to make that clear.” Corvus gazes at her impassively for a moment before shrugging his enormous shoulders. “Alright.” Dawn forces a smile onto her face, “You up for another round of sparring? I need to keep in shape if I’m going to compete against the K-Wolf…but I’ll understand if your wounds are too much for you.” Corvus snorts, “My wounds are never too much. Let’s do this thing.”
