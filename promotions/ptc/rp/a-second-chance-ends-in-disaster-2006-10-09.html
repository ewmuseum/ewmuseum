A second chance ends in disaster?

9 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-09 September 16, 2006 10:22 PM Location: Unknown A scene flashed into focus showing the finish of a wrestling match. "One…. Two….. Three…." The ref finished his count and raised the hand of the winner as the shot widened. It immediately became clearer as the shot widened that the match itself was taking place in a grungy gym with about thirty or so spectators watching. A small cheer from the crowd was overheard as the defeated wrestler sat up and began to roll out of the ring and let the victorious celebrate. The defeated wrestler landed on his feet as he rolled out of the ring and as soon as his feet touched the floor you could tell that the man was a broken individual. This person looked very familiar, but also looked very aged; as if this were some shot taken from the future. But it wasn’t the future, it was the present and the defeated wrestler made his walk to the back as not just a defeated wrestler in the match but also a defeated man in life. The man reached the curtain and then inevitably the locker room that lied behind it. On the other side of the curtain the man was met by applause from his fellow peers as he made his way towards the back of the locker room. "You can put people over like no one else!" One wrestler yelled as the man walked through the locker room where he could sit down and take a long deep breath. "Hey kid, you did good out there tonight," a raspy voice rang out from the doorway in which the wrestlers would normally use to leave out the back door of the gym. The defeated man finally raised his face up so that all could easily recognize his facial features. The man was none other than Semore Smoke, about three years removed from the primetime fame he shared with many other top pro wrestlers before the collapse of what was the wrestling juggernaut known as WrestleOnline; the interfed that held such federations as PCW, NTICW, PWA, and PWC. "Yeah, well… you should know as well as anyone that I’ve done much better in front of exponentially larger crowds," Semore bitterly said as he unwrapped the tape from his wrists. "I miss those crowds, that atmosphere… But those times weren’t for me. At least they weren’t for the person I was." "You say that kid, but you were one of the best at the time," the voice answered back as the figure of a much older man came into view. The older man sat down next to Semore and patted Semore on the back as he sat down. "Yeah, the keyword there is ‘were’," Smoke responded as he turned his face away from the older man. "I’ve been in this gym for what now… a year? Two years!? I live in the fucking storage close on the second floor! I’m not what I was, I’m a shadow of what I was a mere flicker of light of what was once Semore Smoke. The drugs took my life away from me, the demons that haunted me for years finally got a hold of me." "That may be true kid, but if I didn’t believe you had something in you. I wouldn’t have hired you for this small time promotion and wrestling school. I wouldn’t have given you a place to stay on the second floor and I sure as hell wouldn’t have asked you to train any of my wrestlers," it became apparent that the older man is the owner of the gym and the small town wrestling organization that runs shows out of it. "Mr. Wachter, I have nothing but the utmost respect for what you did two years ago," Semore said as he turned his head back to stare Wachter in the eyes. "If it wasn’t for you, I’d probably be living in a cardboard box down the street in some alley or even worse, found dead in some gutter. And I guess I should appreciate what I have here, the past is the past and no matter how good it might have been, the present could always be much worse than what it actually is." Mr. Wachter stood up and patted Semore Smoke’s head, which under the dull light looked a dark shade of gray. A color more than likely from the years of heartache and suffering that Smoke had gone through. "You’re thirty years old kid, far from being an old man. Yet you look much older than your age and even the wiser from society’s tough sanctions against you through the years." Mr. Wachter began to make his way towards the doorway he entered from. "Yeah, but I’m not wise enough to get back where I was. In the past three years I haven’t seen any opportunity to make a return, even to an interfed I helped build from the ground up, the PTC," Semore said as Mr. Wachter walked away. Semore stood up as well after unwrapping his wrists and began to gather what gear he had and placed it into his gym bag. "I guess I’ll be here forever, something that won’t be too bad as soon as I accept it." Mr. Wachter was near the doorway when he suddenly stopped in his tracks and turned around to face Semore as Semore finished his sentence. "I know that what I have here isn’t great, but it sure as hell is better than what you could have. I know you know that, but hearing it might get you out of your funk, which you might need to do before I give you this letter. It came in the gym mailbox for you earlier today." Semore picked up his gym bag and slung it over his back, "Mail? I wasn’t expecting anything." "I think that’s what makes this letter all the more important, you weren’t expecting it." "Where is it? What is it?" Semore asked as he walked up to the elder gym owner. "Right here, in my pocket and I’m not exactly sure what it is," Mr. Wachter dug into his pants pocket and pulled out a letter that had the Primetime Central emblem in the upper left hand corner of the envelope. "I figured you might be bitter tonight so I thought I’d pick my spot before throwing you the bone." The old man smiled as Semore dropped his bag onto the dungy floor and took a hold of the letter with both trembling hands. "What do you think it is?" Semore asked with anticipation as he began to carefully pull apart the envelope. "I don’t know, but what I do know is that it’s your ticket out of here. Sometimes it takes time for opportunities to fall into your lap. I would say that this one literally did in the form of a letter, take whatever it says and run with it and don’t look back." Semore pulled the folded piece of paper from the envelope and trembled with anticipation as he unfolded to read the text within it. Mr. Semore Smoke, Semore, you’re a tough guy to find. If it wasn’t for the records the police department and detox clinic kept on you I don’t think this letter would have ever made it to you. I’m sure that wondering why we’ve contacted you now and being the prompt person that I am I’m sure you’re just waiting for me to get to the point. Well here’s the point, we’ve got another GTT coming up, I’m sure you remember those from your golden years. I want this tournament to have a lot of flavor in it, and that flavor will come from the addition of several of our veteran wrestlers from several eras of PTC. I’m informally asking that you join us in this tournament as a Free Agent, a tournament that if you win could solidify your status in the wrestling industry and quite possibly catapult you back to the big time success you knew in NTICW and PCW. If you’re interested, don’t worry about replying back to this letter, just show up at the PTC HQ on September 23. We’ll go through everything else there in person. I hope to see you there Semore, Your Friend, [name withheld] F. Semore finished reading the letter and looked at the face of the man who has given him everything he’s needed over the last two years. "It looks like I’ve got my opportunity." "It certainly looks that way kid… it certainly looks that way." -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- September 23, 2006 1:45 PM Location: PTC HQ Semore Smoke stepped out of his teal 1999 Nissan Altima, which looked to be in bad condition, probably from the years of little to no maintenance due to Semore Smoke’s budget. Another sign of Semore Smoke’s budget was his attire for his big meeting at PTC Headquarters. Semore, wearing an old dark blue PCW shirt and brown cargo pants looked as if he was headed to the Vans Warped Tour show instead of a business meeting. Semore looked up at the office building that stood in front of him and let out a long sigh. "Well, here I am. I guess there’s no turning back now," Semore said to himself as he locked his car and walked towards the front entrance of the building. Semore reached the revolving door at the entrance of the building and stepped through it feeling the cool rush of air that welcomed his face. He stepped into the lobby and stopped in his tracks to admire the PTC logo that sat up on the back wall above the receptionist desk. "It’s been a long time since I had this feeling. Let’s just hope that this isn’t a waste of my time in the end." Semore continued walking forward staring around the almost empty lobby that only consisted of himself and the receptionist. Semore took time to look over the receptionist who had long blonde hair about shoulder length and what could easily be compared to the face of a movie star sitting snug in between the strands of hair that shared a middle part. "Hi, is there any way I can assist you?" The receptionist asked as she looked up from her paperwork lying on her desk. "Yes, I’m here for a meeting that involves myself and the upcoming GTT," Semore said as he searched the side cargo pockets of his brown cargo pants. Semore found what he was looking for after seconds of searching and he pulled out the letter that he had received about a week earlier concerning the GTT tournament. "Name please?" The receptionist asked as she checked the appointment book sitting in front of her. "Semore Smoke," Smoke said with confidence to try and boast his appearance to the young receptionist. "Ah, yes, they’re expecting you. Well, just go over to the elevators towards the left there and take them to the fifteenth floor. When you get to the fifteenth floor you’ll turn left as you get out of the elevator and you’re looking for the first office on the right," the receptionist said while pointing towards the lobby elevators. "Thank you. Might I add that you’re probably the most gorgeous receptionist they’ve ever had here," Semore said trying to use his charm to possibly procure a date later in the evening. "That’s flattering sir, but by your looks I’d say I’m probably a bit young for you and I’m currently already seeing someone. Thanks for the compliment though none the less. Maybe we’ll see each other in the future and can at least share a cup of coffee," the receptionist said with a smile on her face as she looked back down at the paperwork in front of her. "You should probably get going for your meeting, they don’t like to be kept waiting." "Ok, thanks again. I’ll probably take you up on that cup of coffee sometime," Semore said as he started making his way towards the lobby elevators. Smoke pushed the call button and waited for the elevator to make it’s decent down to the lobby level, looking at his reflection on the golden elevator door in front of him. Had he really aged so badly that he was mistaken for much older than he actually was? Probably not, the young woman had to be no older than twenty-two and maybe she just thought Semore was in his mid-thirties, it was a possible and respectable assumption. The elevator finally reached the lobby and Semore stepped into the empty elevator. Smoke pushed the number fifteen on the dial and waited patiently as the door closed and the elevator began its ascent to the fifteenth floor. Music by Kenny G piped into the elevator through the speakers overhead had Semore immediately felt relaxed throughout his body, almost intoxicating him after the twenty-hour drive it took to make it to the PTC HQ location. The door to the elevator opened and Semore stepped out into the busy administration hallway of the building. Very authoritative looking personal walked back and forth in the busy hallway and it took all of Semore’s cunningness to make it from one side of the hallway to the other without bumping into someone. Semore finally made it across to the right hand of the hallway so he could easily locate the office he was supposed to have his meeting in. Semore took a left from the elevators as the receptionist told him and soon enough he found the first office on the right. Semore looked into the open doorway and noticed a face that he couldn’t recognize sitting behind the desk. Semore knocked on the door and the man behind the desk looked up and motioned for Semore to go ahead and enter the office. "Come on in, don’t mind the paperwork here on the desk. Go ahead and have a seat," the man said as Semore entered the office and sat down in one of the two leather chairs sitting in front of the desk. "You finally made it Semore, [name withheld] had really nice things to say about you so I’m certainly glad I could meet you in person," the man continued as Semore looked at the man with a puzzled look. Semore reached forward to shake the man’s hand, which had been extended when Semore entered the office. "I’m a little confused, I was under the impression that I would be meeting with [name withheld] and not someone else," Semore said as he sat back into his seat with the letter ready in his hand just in case it was needed. "Yeah I’m sorry about that, [name withheld] is very busy today as the applications for the tournament have just started to come in for the tournament," the man soon responded with. "Oh, by the way, my name is John, John Davis. I’m here as one of [name withheld]’ trusted executives, helping with the welcoming process for several of our veteran free agents. I’m sure you know how busy things can get when the GTT comes around every year so I’m guessing you won’t have anymore questions regarding the situation." "Yeah, I know. So, why exactly was I called for a meeting here if all I needed to do was apply?" Semore asked as he folded the letter back up and stuck it back into his pocket. "We sent letters out to several of the old veterans to see if they would make the trip out for a meeting. In a sense we wanted to make sure that the wrestlers would be interested in having a chance at the big time again," John said as he rummaged through the paperwork on his desk. "Here, take this. It’s the application for GTT6." John handed Semore a standard wrestling application. "Am I guaranteed a spot in the tournament at least for showing up? I can understand that you wanted to make sure I wouldn’t just no show the tournament since I haven’t been on the radar in three years, but do I get anything for coming out this way?" Semore asked as he eyed over the application, reading every bit of detail that he’d have to fill out. "Unfortunately no, we’ll be doing a draft on the PTC Radio Show that’s set to air September thirtieth. You will have a very good chance of being drafted by the people conducting the draft but we can’t guarantee you a spot in the tournament," John said as he leaned back in his chair. "Besides that and the application though, that’s pretty much everything you need to know for now. You can find the information for the radio show later this week either on our website or in the newspapers and the application can be faxed, mailed, or personally delivered back here." "So let me get this straight. I made the twenty-hour drive out here just to get an application for a non-guaranteed spot," Semore looked visibly agitated. "I’m getting no compensation for my time or effort here. But, I guess I can understand why you want someone to come out this way to make sure. It’s just a waste of both yours and mine time in the end if all I needed was a simple application." "I can understand your aggravation and I think it’s just as much a waste as you do," John said as he tried to sympathize with Semore. "But since we have had problems in the past with people no-showing the tournament after being accepted we wanted to make sure that the veteran free agents were actually serious about this." "Yeah, I know. I’ve watched the tournament for the past couple of years now and I’ve found it very frustrating as a fan to see matches have no-shows by wrestlers," Semore said as he started to understand the need for meetings like this. "I can only bet it’s a hundred times more frustrating for someone like yourself. But I’m here for the meeting and you can bet I won’t be no-showing at all, I’ve got way too much to gain from my placing in this tournament." "That’s the spirit, we actually counted on this reaction from you," John said as he stood up. "We figured you’d be one of the more desperate guys we contacted, which means you’re one of the hungrier competitors. That means for exciting television in the future." Semore stood up as well and put out his hand to shake John’s. "You can bet I’m hungry, I miss that limelight, I miss that glow from the spotlight and the feeling an entertainer gets when tens of thousands of fans stand on their feet cheering." "I look forward to watching you work, just remember, that radio show is next Saturday, don’t miss it." John shook Semore’s hand and Semore immediately turned around to walk out the office doorway. "If you get drafted we’ll contact you via the phone number you have on your application to let you know where you’ll be flown to for competition." "I’ll be listening, and I’ll be waiting for the announcement that will completely turn my world around." Semore exited the office and made his way back towards the elevators with a grin from ear to ear on his face…. -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- September 30, 2006 12:53 PM Location: Semore’s Pad The scene opened with both Semore Smoke and Mr. Wachter sitting at a table in what looked to be a run down storage closet, quite possibly Semore’s pad that sit’s above Mr. Wachter’s gym. "And as the sixty-seventh pick of the draft I’ll take Semore Smoke," the voice over the radio rang out as Semore pumped his fist. "You’re excited for going that late in the draft kid?" Asked the aging gym owner Mr. Wachter. "Sure it might have taken over two hours to hear my name called but I’m just glad I got drafted," Semore said as he wrote down his name next to the number sixty-seven on a piece of paper that was littered with sixty-six other wrestlers’ names. After jotting his name down Semore moved his hand towards the radio and turned it off. "Kid! You’re not gonna listen to see who the rest of the picks are? Or see if they’ll announce the first round matches at the end of the show?" Mr. Wachter looked very nervous and concerned over the fact that Semore was so confident to just throw that knowledge away. "Yeah, I know the bulk of my bracket by how and from what I can see there’s not a lot of need for me to worry. I’ve got Trashcan Man and Violence Jack in my bracket, both of which I know I can beat and in the case of Jack I beat when we were in PCW. Along with last years GTT winner, Rich Rollins, who even though he won, he’s just a chump compared to me," Semore said as he circled names on the master draft list he was keeping as the draft went on. "Plus old man, it’s almost one o’clock in the morning which means you need to get some rest." "I think you’re taking your bracket light kid, you aren’t as well off as you think you are. But whatever kid, chances are you’ll probably be paired against someone else who wasn’t drafted yet and you won’t know what to do with yourself once the match is announced," Mr. Wachter said trying to play with Semore’s emotions to have him turn the radio back on. Mr. Wachter stood up after finishing that last statement and went to put one his jacket. "But, you are right with one thing, I should head off to get some rest tonight and I would suggest you do the same. We’ve got a lot of work to do tomorrow before you leave for the big time again." "Work!? What work?" Semore looked confused now as he stood up to help Mr. Wachter with his jacket. "There’s no work to do tomorrow besides getting myself packed and ready to go." "You’re not ready kid. I might have picked you up from the streets two years ago but you’re not ready to just jump back into the fray. These other kids here are nothing compared to the talent you’ll see in that tournament," Mr. Wachter said as he walked across the dungy floor of Semore Smoke’s pad. Semore let out a sigh as he realized he wouldn’t change Mr. Wachter’s mind on the matter. "Fine, we’ll do some training starting tomorrow before I leave if that’ll make you happy." "It will kid… it’ll mean the world to me," Mr. Wachter said as he walked through the narrow door and into the darkness. -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- October 1, 2006 5:42 AM Location: Semore’s Pad / Mr. Wachter’s Gym "Wake up kid! Time to get ready for the big dance!" Mr. Wachter came storming up the stairs to Semore’s pad above, attempting to wake Semore along the way. "Come on kid, we’ve got some training to get out of the way this morning!" Semore rolled over on his cot that sits perpendicular to the back wall of the small storage closet sitting above the almost ancient gym owned by Mr. Wachter. "What? It’s five forty-five in the morning, what kind of training do you have planned this early? It’s not like we’re making Rocky VI here Mick." Semore sat up in his bed shaking his head as the older gentlemen walked across to the bed. "Last week you said you were hungry kid, hungry for action, hungry for respect," Mr. Wachter said as he pulled Semore out of his bed and onto his feet. "Well, we’re gonna test that hunger this morning." Semore grabs a pair of pants and a shirt from a pile of clothes sitting on the floor not too far from his bed and put them on. Mr. Wachter then beckoned for Semore to follow him out the door and down the stairs to the gym below. Wachter led Semore to the main hall of the gym where he pointed out a table set up within the wrestling ring. "You see that table, on it you’ll find a couple of different plates full of food," Wachter said as Semore rubbed his eyes to get a better look. "Your training will consist of you downing all that food, if you’re truly hungry and motivated to win then you’ll have no problem doing what I say needs to be done to win." "This has got to be a joke," Semore said as he walked closer to the ring. "You’re kidding me, we’re up this early just so I can eat a shit load of food?" Wachter walked up to stand next to Semore and pointed at the ring again to emphasize his point. "There’s no joke here kid, you said you were hungry, let’s see how hungry you really are." Semore walked towards the ring as the old man finished his sentence and stepped into the ring to stare at the food up close. On the table sat a pile of fried chicken legs and next to that plate sat a plate full of hotdogs with an equal amount of hotdog buns next to that on another plate. "Looks like I’m participating in some sort of eating competition." Wachter climbed into the ring with Semore and looked anxious over whether or not the wrestler could actually down all the food. "That’s essentially what it is kid, it’s about fifty chicken legs and a little over fifty hotdogs and buns," Wachter said as he pushed Semore closer to the table. "If you can eat all of either of those, then you’ll be downing championship amounts of food." "This shit will put me out of shape, not in shape. I’m hungry for competition, don’t get me wrong, but this is taking it a little overboard don’t you think?" Semore became inclined to ask as he let out a yawn or two. "It might be overboard son, but if you’re not up to this, then how are you gonna beat any of those big brutes in the tournament?" Semore knew that Wachter had a point, even if it was a crazy point in the end. Semore stared over at the food quietly as he tried to strategize how he would be able to get through this test without losing more in the end with a bad diet. "Kid, think of it this way. Think of it like, the bladder of a mongoose," Wachter said with a serious face. "You gotta have the bladder of a mongoose to compete out there!" "Don’t you mean the eye of the tiger?" Semore asked trying to suppress a few laughs. "No, that shit only works in the movies. A mongoose has to have a good bladder to eat all those snakes. Well the snakes are the competitors you’ll be facing, if you can’t eat them all up with your hunger, then you’ll nothing more than a lowly rat!" Wachter proved himself to be serious in the matter and Semore just simply shrugged. "Ok, so bladder of a mongoose it is I suppose." Semore stepped closer to the table so the food was within reach and as soon as he was about to pick a piece of chicken off the table a loud knocking sound was overheard coming from the front entrance. "I wonder who the hell that could be," Wachter said as he turned to face the front entrance. Almost on cue as if they heard Wachter’s comment a loud voice of authority rang through the door informing the two men inside who they were. "It’s the police, we just have a few questions to ask an individual who lives at this address!" Wachter looked immediately at Semore and Semore looked almost as confused as Wachter did as he shrugged with no clue of why they were there. Wachter then turned back to the door and stepped out of the ring and started to make his way to the front entrance. Semore looked at the food and then to the door and decided to follow Wachter to the door to find out what all the commotion was about. Even though Semore left the ring after Wachter, he made it to the door at about the same time Wachter did and was able to greet a police officer on the other side as the door was unlocked and opened. "Good morning sirs, sorry for intruding so early but there is an urgent matter that we need to address involving Mr. Semore Smoke," the officer said as he stepped through the door and into the main hall. "I’m Semore Smoke," Semore said as stared past the officer and out the door to see about three more officers standing outside. "What can I do to help you?" "I’m afraid we’ll have to ask you to come downtown," the officer said as he grabbed Semore’s arm. Semore looked immediately to Wachter with a look of innocence and Wachter stepped up to talk in Semore’s defense. "What exactly is the matter here? The kid and I were just getting ready to get some training out of the way." The officer pulled a photo out of his pocket and placed it out for Semore to see. "Do you know this woman?" Semore looked closely at the photograph and noticed that it was of the young receptionist he met at the PTC Headquarters. "Yeah, I know the woman. She works at the wrestling organization that I’m contracted to wrestle for in a few weeks. Why, what’s the matter?" The officer put the photo back into his pocket. "That woman’s dead sir, she was found dead almost a week ago in her apartment and a lead has put us here in the federal investigation. The FBI has taken over the investigation since it stretches across several states and we’re here as part of the Rocky Mount police department to offer whatever aid we can." Semore looked visibly shaken by the turn of events, almost as if he couldn’t believe what was going on. "Agent Ryan is outside and waiting for you to come with us Mr. Smoke," the officer stated as he pulled on Semore’s arm. Semore looked over at Wachter with a helpless look. "I guess I gotta go and see what’s going on with this. Don’t worry Mr. Wachter, I didn’t do anything so I should be back soon," Semore said as he followed the officer out the door. "I’ll be back for the training as soon as I can." "Don’t rush it kid, this is serious. I hope for your sake you’re being honest. It’d be a shame for you to go down for something like this after coming so close to success." Wachter looked out the door and watched as the officer opened the back door to one of the squad cars sitting outside and Semore sat inside. The officer closed the door and then entered the front passenger side door, signaling to the other cars that they were ready to leave. The other cars started up and led off to the police station with the squad car holding Semore Smoke closely behind… TO BE CONTINUED…
