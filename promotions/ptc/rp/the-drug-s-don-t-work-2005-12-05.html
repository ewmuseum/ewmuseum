The Drug's Don't Work

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Would this endless torment ever stop? Why am I being made to experience my worst hell? I need a glass of water, why can’t I move? Why can’t I feel my body? This isn’t right! This isn’t how it’s supposed to work! What’s going on with me? Cyrus tried to focus himself on one thing and one thing alone. Moving his index finger on his right hand. He sat staring intensely at his digit, yet no matter how hard he thought about it easing through the air; it would not. He tried to move his lips to call for help but was unable to. He felt so useless, like a bystander to his own dead body. Yet; he knew he wasn’t dead; for he was still breathing. This was just another chapter in his personal hell. Maybe if he could utter a word, a scream; any audible sound, somebody might hear him. He tried to move his lips, but they wouldn’t budge. His head was spinning, the room a blur of colours. He tried to focus his eyes but the vivid images just got worse. The banging in his mind wouldn’t end, wouldn’t ease up for a second. He pleaded for it to stop; he prayed that it would all be over soon. He couldn’t take this, not now. Then he passed out. ------- The gentle song of the robin outside woke Cyrus into a semi-conscious state. With a great deal of effort he managed to prise open his weary, bloodshot eyes. As he started to come round he remembered last night. His memory seemed foggy, unclear as he thought back over the nights events. Something went wrong and he couldn’t think what. He moved his fingers first, to check everything was working properly now. He let out a sigh of relief as they moved up and down. He stretched to get out of bed and felt a sharp shooting pain up his spine. ‘What the hell was that?’ Cyrus thought to himself. ‘What is wrong? I don’t feel like myself at all.’ He slowly eased his way out from under the sweat-drenched bed covers and was barely able to stand. His legs felt like jelly as he stood in front of the mirror, staring into his own depressed eyes. "I could tell you what is wrong Cyrus, if you’d like that is." Those words came from within Cyrus; though they were definitely not his own. It was one of his other personalities known only as ‘The Voice’. The Voice sounded like Cyrus in some ways, yet it had a sly edge to it. "What do you mean? I’m just ill, that’s all; a little flu maybe." Though both of them knew that this was not the case. "Oh my Cyrus, it must be that crazy avian flu! I’ve never seen anybody have a night like you did yesterday," the Voice laughed to itself but Cyrus didn’t move, he stood staring into the abyss of his own blue eyes, seemingly in a world of his own. "Are you listening to me? Fine, it’s your own loss. I won’t tell you why you were so ill last night." Cyrus still stood completely still, unnerved by the Voice’s suggestion. He wasn’t thinking straight right now, he needed to get out and clear his head, try and find some rationality in this whole situation. "I’ll speak to you later." Cyrus’s voice was solemn, almost a complete monotone. He seemed almost in a trance gazing into his disjointed reflection, wondering why. The Voice didn’t argue, he knew what was going on. There was no point arguing. There was no point even giving an opinion or information to Cyrus. He would find out on his own. ----- The cold water felt good against Cyrus’s skin as he splashed it upon his face, cooling him and cleansing him. He took a towel from off the rail and dabbed his face dry, his eyes now returning to their normal piercing blue. His hand reached for the cupboard and pulled out his pills. A jolt of pain shot up his spine again, his muscles twitching his head throbbing. He fell to the floor clutching his head, as the paralysis crept through his body, clambering up his legs towards his torso. Cyrus grasped for something to pull himself up, his hand finally managing to latch onto the towel rail. Struggling, he clutched for the door, throwing himself into the main room where the phone lay, just out of reach. He could feel his body numbing from below the waist, this was it. One final attack; he threw his arms towards the phone and knocked it off the desk. He lay breathing heavily, exhausted from the physical exertion he’d just partaken in. He moved his fingers one last time, struggling for the operator’s number before he lost the ability to cry for help. He dialled the number, before it reached his head. "Hello, can I help you? Hello? Sir? Madam?" Then the phone line went dead. The operator cut off the call; it was probably just another prank call. They seemed to get that a lot; he thought the cliental were classier these days. At one time they’d never have dared in-case of being thrown out, but now it was all about money. Ah yes money. Wasn’t that why people did things nowadays? He went back to reading the latest issue of ‘Heat’ magazine. He had money to earn too. ----- Cyrus’s eyes began to focus, slowly but surely. The vivid blur, the sensory deprivation; it all seemed to be gone; at least for the time being. As he focused more he began to recognize his surroundings. The familiar wall charts documenting the human body, the cabinets and cabinets filled with experimental drugs. Yes, Cyrus knew where he’d been taken to, the very place that had gotten him into this mess in the first place. "Ah, it’s nice to see that you’re awake Mr. Skar. It is under strange circumstances we meet, but I must introduce myself. I am David Hamilton and I’m sure as I say this you’re probably starting to remember. Unfortunately I’m under strict orders to keep you in the dark. I do apologize." As this was being said to Cyrus his mind was whirring, cogs in his brain turning as he tried to piece together the leftovers from the jigsaw that was his life. He’d definitely met Mr. Hamilton before, but he couldn’t think why. "I’m confused, why am I here? And who are you really? Why do I recognize you?" Cyrus tried to readjust his eyes as a he found a bright light being shone into his face. "It seems some of the drugs we’ve been giving you, clashed with the drugs your Doctor prescribed. That is strange; we’d tested them for weeks before letting you take them. Nothing that can’t be rectified though." Hamilton let out a smirk as his mind began to calculate his next moves. "You didn’t answer any of my questions." "Now, now Mr. Skar. I’m under a contract; I don’t believe I can answer of your questions. Somebody thinks you’re a threat it seems. All I do is give you a few extra tablets a week to make sure you’re not at your best and all is good. Though it seems the first patch has had a few, teething problems; shall we say." Hamilton let out a burst of laughter at his own joke. Cyrus angered at his situation; tried to get up from where he’d been sat and realized he was restrained with leather straps. "The thing is Mr. Skar, I can’t have you remembering this; otherwise I could get in a lot of trouble. So I’m afraid I’m going to have to give you a little injection now. It won’t hurt much and then all the bad thoughts and feelings will go away. I promise." Hamilton began walking over to Cyrus, testing the needle in his hand as he did so. He walked right up by the side of Cyrus and bent down so that Cyrus could feel his breath caressing his face, his power over the monster. "The problem is Mr. Hamilton; you’re not dealing with Cyrus anymore. You’re dealing with Mr. Kaos and that isn’t a situation you want to be in." Hamilton staggered back a few steps on hearing this new voice coming from Cyrus’s mouth. "But, I thought, I thought that the drugs were controlling you?" came the stuttered response from Hamilton’s trembling lips. "Unfortunately for you, no they’re not. Nobody can control me." As the last syllable left Drake’s mouth he began to break free from the restrains. First he ripped out his left arm, then his right and untied his legs. He rose to his full height and looked down on the now petrified Hamilton. "Please don’t hurt me. I’ll give you anything you want, money? I heard you’re hard for cash at the moment Cyrus? That’s why you entered the GTT right?" Sweat had started to drop from Hamilton’s brown; Drake walked over to one of the medicine cabinets and without even trying knocked it to the floor, causing pills to skid over the tiled surface. "You think I’m influenced by money? Why would I need money when I have power? People fear my name, people respect what I am. I don’t need your pathetic prosthetic bribes. You think I want to know who’s trying to mess up my chance of winning the GTT? I don’t care, because nobody can change my fate. I am destined to do what I want to who I want. I think you need a demonstration Mr. Hamilton." Hamilton found himself unable to speak; he tried to hard to make a sound as he opened his mouth again and again like a goldfish. Eventually, he managed to get three words out. Unfortunately for him, not what Drake Kaos was hoping for. "I am….sorry." Quivering, Hamilton was backed into a corner by the behemoth before him. Drake stalked his pray and loved every second of it, he let his senses savour everything around him. He let his eyes observe how pathetic Hamilton looked. He let his nose smell how awful Hamilton smelt and he let his ears hear how weak Hamilton’s pleas were. Drake lived for moments like this and now he’d gotten a taste for it again he wasn’t going to stop. Without warning David Hamilton landed on the pavement outside covered in fragments of the window that once kept people from entering the very building he’d just left. Drake calmly walked out of the cream building and into the sunlight. His eyes adjusted accordingly and he began to walk back towards town. He’d need to book a new hotel room now, though that wouldn’t be a problem. His influence could get him anything he wanted. He loved it. "There’s one problem Drake. We need to find out who took the contract out on Cyrus. It’s surely somebody in the GTT, which would be the only plausible answer." The Voice had decided it was time to make a special guest appearance; it had somebody new to torment and manipulate. It loved it. "You think I care about who took the contract out? It doesn’t matter to me. I sorted that problem back at the private hospital. Even if it was one of the punks from the GTT, it won’t matter. Cyrus Skar may not be mentally capable to compete, but I am under no stress whatsoever. I am going to show everyone the new monster. Drake Kaos is going to dominate and there’s nothing you, or anyone else can do to stop me. Drake carried on walking towards the buzz of the big city, how he loathed other human beings. How he loathed the Voice. It thought it knew so much, yet it knew nothing. It thought that Drake was going to take its side. Drake was a loner, he needed nobodies help. "What about Valentine Burst? Are you not worried about our first opponent at all? Cyrus has been drugged up for weeks. Whether you feel fit or not doesn’t matter, his body won’t take it." Drake paused for a second and let out a slight laugh. The Voice was amusing him now. "What you need to remember is that I need nobody. Cyrus is weak, I am not. I will defeat Burst with ease. He is emotionally distraught; I am everything he wishes he could be. I will feel no emotion when I destroy him in the ring. My body may not be at one hundred percent, but my mind certainly is and that is where it counts." The Voice was starting to get a little spooked by Kaos. It didn’t understand him like it did Cyrus. It couldn’t manipulate him quite as easily as it wanted to, but it’d wait. It’d bide its time and wait for the opportune moment and when it came. Well, let’s just say Drake Kaos would be a forgotten name; the Voice deserved this more than either of them. It’d get credit for this if it meant causing a little harm, a little trouble maybe even a little bloodshed. There was a price to pay for success and the Voice was prepared to pay it…
