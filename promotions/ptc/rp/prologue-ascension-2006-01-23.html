Prologue: Ascension

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-23 "We should hurry. We shouldn’t linger here." The man spoke with a nervous hush in his voice, his eyes canvassing the street with tireless vigilance. He pulled his dark overcoat tight around his neck, trying to shut out the cold breeze whipping through the main drag. Despite the warmth of his attire, he couldn’t shake the chill that had penetrated his body. But he knew it wasn’t the air that had chilled him. Beside him, his friend was struggling to light a cigarette, blowing off his counterpart’s concerns with a frustrated grunt. "Damnit," is the only audible word he manages, finally getting the cigarette to puff to life. The first man spins on his heel, quickly surveying the street. A cold, bitter mist fell over the Entertainment District, filling the air with a faint neon luminescence. Again he nervously pokes at his friend, his voice trembling slightly with trepidation. "Let’s just get back to the car," he mutters, his squinting eyes still darting in and out of the shadows. His friend turned sharply, a plume of smoke rising from his face. He shook the water from his sopping black hair, his face contorted with aggravation. "What the hell is wrong with you? We’re going…" The first man took a deep breath. "You haven’t heard, then?" he responded in a grave voice. "Heard what?" his friend answered with a chuckle, turning to head down the street towards the garage where their car was parked. The first man followed closely, inching nearer to his friend. "There is a demon lurking in these streets," he stated flatly, in a voice that was barely a breath. "A demon?" came the incredulous response, his friend stopping in mid-stride. The first man pushed past him, nervously turning to beckon him onward. "I think you had too much sake tonight." "It’s not the confounded sake," the first man spouted. "I know a man who has seen it! A man somehow cheated the roulette tables last week, and he made off with nearly two million yen. But he didn’t make it far. When they heard his screams, they rushed outside to find him. He was curled into a ball, tears running down his face… tears of terror. Above him was a massive shadow, its features masked save his burning red eyes. The demon let out a terrifying yell, and it is said he has the voice of the devil himself. As the shadow turned to flee, the ground shook from its footsteps. It is said that anyone who displays greed, lust, or gluttony in these streets will be punished for their sins by this demon…" His friend smirked. "Greed? Lust? I defy you to find anything but in these streets," he said with a dismissive wave of his hand. "Stories; spread by the owners of the casino to frighten potential thieves. Come on, let’s go." They took three steps before stopping dead in their tracks. Neither of them could be sure they’d seen it, but together they turned to peer down one of the many anonymous alleys nearby. It had been but a fleeting shadow, moving too quickly and silently to be human. But it was massive, bellowing out from the gloom of the alley to eclipse the both of them. Wide-eyed and suddenly paralyzed with a creeping apprehension, the two men couldn’t help but stand perfectly still, not daring to draw breath, peering hard in a vain attempt to pierce the darkness. Then they saw it; very faint at first, but intensifying steadily into a full-on blaze. Two small orbs, flaming red in the murky alley air; they had the unmistakable appearance of human eyes, but burned with an inhuman malice that filled both men with dread. The burning red eyes fixed them in an unwavering stare, watching them with malevolent persistence. Then, from behind, a car rounded a corner, briefly illuminating the forbidding alley. There before the two men was the unmistakable form of a demon, a monstrous specter rimmed in shadow. It was disguised as human, but was undeniably born of another world; the brief glimpse they had seen was enough to convince both men beyond doubt that this shadow was spawn from hell. And, just as quickly as it had appeared, the specter was gone. Another car rounded the corner, and upon splashing its lights over the alley it was clear that the demon was gone. Yet, for both men, the red flaming eyes burned on. Finally able to blink, the two men realized with terror that the flaming eyes had been burned into their vision, and burned on even with their eyes shut tight. Turning quickly the two men bolted down the street, not stopping until they reached their car and had sped far from the Entertainment District. God, I love that… Kimbusa smiled with satisfaction. It was a slow night, and Shindo had suggested he spend the evening familiarizing himself with the territory. He had spent the first few hours scouting most of the favorite local nightspots, but had quickly given into boredom. He soon found himself with a new method of entertaining himself; lurking in the shadows, haunting the passersby and listening to the growing rumors of his presence. Even this had grown tiresome, though the sight of terrified patrons running for their lives still hadn’t lost its novelty. Nonetheless, Kimbusa had turned and, realizing where he was, inexplicably began heading down a familiar path. As he approached his destination, he recalled the events of the evening. It was then that he realized how monumental the events of that evening had been. The rush hadn’t subsided; in fact, it hadn’t weakened in the slightest. It had been three days since his first assignment, and still Kimbusa could not shake adrenaline high he’d hit that night. Nor did he want to; it was the greatest feeling he’d ever experienced in his life, and he planned to do everything within his power to maintain it. Perhaps that was the reason he found himself standing before that same door again. Propping his five-hundred pound frame against a shadowy wall, Kimbusa peered across the alley from beneath the brim of his fedora. A thin line of white smoke rose from the glowing tip of his cigarette, which he had taken up smoking more for effect than anything. During his short time in the Entertainment District, he’d learned first hand just how mean the streets could be. But his reputation as Shindo’s newest and most deadly weapon was spreading rapidly, and combined with the increasing whispers of the deranged renegade sumo roaming the night, the streets trembled with fear. For a five hundred pound man, he had maintained a low profile, to the point that some doubted such a man existed. It was with his little hide-and-seek games that he hoped to slowly convince people that he did, in fact, exist… and he was always watching. And yet, despite his rapid ascent through the underworld, a single figure remained fixed in the back of his mind. The woman in the red dress. She had been so enchanting, so captivating, that he had finally given in to the urge to find her again. Just a week ago, he had been timid and uncertain when entering the casino; this time, he walked to the door without hesitation, turned the knob, and slipped inside. The casino was alive with activity, teeming with boisterous, impeccably dressed high-rollers from across the city. Kimbusa had since learned that this was but one of many such casinos in the Entertainment District, but this was the most prestigious. All the wealthiest businessmen and most powerful politicians in Tokyo stopped here weekly, a place where they could throw away a few thousand yen, find the company of a young lady, or indulge their addictions. It was operated by a man named Tony Zaccanelli, an Italian-American with a well documented mean streak. Zaccanelli was in Shindo’s pocket, but Shindo spoke of him with both pride and suspicion. Zaccanelli wasn’t seen on the casino floor often, leaving the day-to-day operation to the older Japanese gentlemen Kimbusa had met on the previous week’s drug delivery. The room still impressed Kimbusa; curiously built inside a decrepit condemned building, the casino was brilliantly designed. It was open and spacious enough to inspire awe in the casual observer; and yet, as Kimbusa was quick to note, it was designed so that one could not see the face of anyone more than a few feet away. Perfectly positioned walls, mirrors, plants and statues obstructed all lines of sight, so that the only thing a quick glimpse could provide was a view of a priceless work of art, but not one single face. But it wasn’t faces Kimbusa had come to see; it was the red dress. Scanning the room quickly, Kimbusa caught the odd glimpse of a scandalous blouse or an obscenely pricey jacket, but not a glimpse of red. The patrons tended to favor darker tones; it enabled them to better blend into the crowd. Kimbusa had quickly caught on, and as such was wearing a black suit and tie under his black leather trenchcoat and fedora; and though a five hundred pound man finds it difficult to blend into a diminutive crowd, the patrons managed to keep their attention focused on the tables, despite the chill that would run down their spines as the massive man passed by. So it was with some surprise that he actually heard his name. "Kimbusa! Kimbusa, come here!" The voice was subdued yet welcoming, beckoning him into the labyrinth of tables in the center of the room. Scanning the room, Kimbusa’s eyes met a smiling face a few feet away, standing behind an empty baccarat table. The man wore an exquisite tuxedo and motioned forward with his hand. Kimbusa had never seen him before, and could not imagine how the man had learned his name, nor how he had summoned the courage to actually welcome his company. So it was with a bemused grin that Kimbusa slowly slipped over to the table, taking a seat across from the dealer. "They phoned ahead, said you might wish to play," the dealer said smoothly, showing not the slightest twinge of fear. He slid the shoe across the table, obviously offering Kimbusa a game. Confused, Kimbusa shook his head. "I’m afraid I have no money," he muttered, still eyeing the dealer suspiciously. "And the table is empty." The dealer was unfazed, offering Kimbusa a pleasant, accommodating smile. "Your credit is perfectly good here, sir," the dealer offered. His eyes then turned up behind Kimbusa with a glimmer. "And I believe you will find the competition to be quite… firm." Turning, a flutter entered Kimbusa’s heart. There before him was the same stunning red dress, filled by the same breathtaking figure. The young Japanese woman took a seat across the table from Kimbusa, meeting him with a sultry stare. "You came back," she cooed, pulling a thin cigarette from her bag. The dealer quickly offered her a light, and she pulled a puff of smoke with a seductive motion of her lips. Before he could respond, another voice appeared behind him. "What will you have, sir?" Kimbusa turned to find a young waiter standing behind him, arms tucked behind his back. "A bottle of sake for myself and the lady," came Kimbusa’s gruff reply. The waiter spun and disappeared, while the dealer quickly tossed four cards onto the table. Kimbusa locked eyes with the woman in the red dress once more, and she broke the stare only briefly for a discreet look at her cards. "My name is-" She quickly cut Kimbusa off, her voice smooth as silk yet strong and confident. "You are the demon they speak of," she whispered nonchalantly. The answer took Kimbusa off guard, but she pressed on. "You have struck fear into the hearts of these men, yet you command their respect and they serve you unquestioningly. But for me, I believe I will just call you Kimbusa." Kimbusa raised an impressed eyebrow, as a warm bottle of sake was suddenly placed before him. His lady companion was served first, then a glass was poured for him. She raised her low-rimmed glass to Kimbusa and took a sip, her gleaming eyes never breaking contact with the fearsome monster across from her. She displayed no fear or apprehension in his presence, but once again seemed to be projecting an unwavering magnetism. "And what shall I call you, then?" Kimbusa asked, is confidence growing as he surveyed his situation. This was his first true visit to the casino, and already he was receiving treatment reserved only for the most powerful of patrons. Tossing a few chips into the center of the table, she gave him a challenging smile. After a pause, Kimbusa matched her bet and tuned over his cards. "Seven." "Eight," came the reply. Surprised, Kimbusa studied the cards with wide eyes. "I am Ruby," came the reply as she expertly stacked her chips with slender fingers. Pausing to examine the situation a little closer, Kimbusa realized just how far they’d gone to make him feel welcome. They had a private table waiting with the establishment’s finest dealer, had promptly brought him a perfectly warmed bottle of sake (a 1984, an excellent vintage). Even his chair at the table was noticeably wider than the others, no doubt placed there specifically for his use. And now here was this woman, clearly the most radiant and sought after beauty in the entire Entertainment District, choosing to sit at the baccarat table with him. That’s assuming she had a choice at all… Kimbusa made up his mind that something else was going on, but for the moment he would enjoy the moment. He had been given everything he desired readily and without question. Well… almost everything. It was then that Kimbusa decided to see just how far their generosity extended. Dropping his eyes to the table, Kimbusa spoke in the flattest voice he could manage. "Come with me," he said, standing from his seat. It took Ruby by surprise; she maintained her cool façade, but appeared to be at a sudden loss for words. "And where are we going?" Kimbusa nonchalantly tossed a thousand yen chip at the dealer, not that it was his to begin with, nor did he intend to bother cashing in. "I thought you might show me around town," he replied with the cockiest undertone he was capable of, deliberately letting his newfound royal status shine through. Yet Ruby was unimpressed. She stood, flashing a nonchalant wink at the dealer, who could only grin knowingly in response. Kimbusa motioned towards the door, but Ruby’s eyes made it clear that she was only humoring him. In an instant, he knew his attempt to get her out of the casino had quickly earned some attention. "Sir, Mr. Zaccanelli would like to speak with you. Immediately," came a stern yet polite voice from behind him. Glancing over his shoulder, he found three tuxedoed security guards standing at the ready, the lead guard motioning toward the back of the casino. Frustrated by their interruption, Kimbusa opened his mouth in protest. But once again, Ruby’s expression made it clear that he had no choice in the matter. "Sorry, handsome. Looks like we’ll have to wait for another time," she said with a seductive stare. She walked past him, making sure to brush against his massive chest as she moved by. Kimbusa turned, still taken by her flawless figure. As he watched her leave, he felt an odd wave of anger sweep over him, taking hold of his senses. Suddenly he found himself prepared to strike down the guards, to destroy them for costing him his prize. Even the dealer would have fallen, has he listened to the voice at his shoulder. None can stand against you! Who are they to deny you your desires? You are Kimbusa, you can take anything you want! Kimbusa’s face twisted with rage, unwilling to admit defeat. They had given him everything, and yet the one thing he came for was walking away from him. Nobody walks away from you… Shaking his head violently, Kimbusa cleared the animal impulse from his mind. He had lost control for a moment, as if some primal instinct had taken over, urging him to sieze the woman in the red dress and make her fulfill his desires. But despite his obvious influence, he felt he might regret the aftermath of any battle that might take place. So I’ll wait… but she will be mine. Kimbusa pushed past the table, heading away from Ruby to the back of the casino. He caught the occasional veiled glance from the patrons, as they strained to get a look at him while trying at all costs to remain inconspicuous. He kept his eyes facing forward, locked on the subtle back door where they were taking him. Mr. Zaccanelli wanted to see him? Shindo said Mr. Zaccanelli paid little attention to the people in his casino, hoping to recognize as few faces as possible. A visit with the casino boss was no doubt a dubious honor. The first guard pushed past him and opened the door, motioning for him to pass through first. Kimbusa didn’t hesitate; he knew these men feared and respected him, though he detected a distinct air of resentment as well. In a few short weeks Kimbusa had become the most highly regarded man in Shindo’s organization, a high distinction to achieve for sure. But Kimbusa knew, despite their bitterness, none would dare stand against him. Unless, of course, this Zaccanelli had something else in mind. Entering the room, Kimbusa found himself in a brightly lit encasement, a well decorated if slightly claustrophobic compartment hidden away in an inconspicuous corner of the casino. A modest desk adorned with a pair of priceless kitana blades was centered in the room; flanking the desk were two large bodyguards, their expressionless faces hidden behind thick, dark sunglasses. Standing behind the desk, sipping a brandy glass, was Zaccanelli. Short in stature, Zaccanelli was clad in a gaudy purple suit, giving Kimbusa the image of a stereotypical nineteen forties Italian mobster, more concerned with looking threatening than actually being so. His black hair was slicked back and he was clean shaven, but his face was scarred and rough. Despite his guff appearance, he offered Kimbusa a smile that was at least halfway genuine. "Kimbusa, come in. It’s good to finally meet you!" he exclaimed in a high-pitched, heavily accented voice. The door closed softly behind him, after all three of the guards slipped in after him. That’s three behind me, two in front of me… and Zaccanelli, Kimbusa added with a snicker to himself. Despite being ridiculously outnumbered, Kimbusa sensed an overwhelming feeling of fear in the room. Whatever was about to happen, the guards were obviously afraid of it. Zaccanelli circled round the desk and extended his hand to Kimbusa. Kimbusa was accustomed to bowing, but felt no particular need to show respect to this greasy low-life. He begrudgingly accepted Zaccanelli’s hand and shook it firmly, his dark black eyes surveying the tiny room. "What do you think of my establishment?" Zaccanelli inquired proudly, pointing to the front wall. The wall was made of two-way mirrors, allowing those inside to keep an eye on the action on the casino floor. Kimbusa made sure he didn’t sound too interested in the conversation, and in truth his mind was still fixed on Ruby. "Fine, fine." Zaccanelli seemed concerned by Kimbusa’s detachment. "I trust the service you received tonight was… satisfactory?" he asked with a raised eyebrow. "For the most part," Kimbusa responded, flashing another frustrated glare back at the guard who had interrupted him. The guard gulped and adjusted his tie, but kept his attention focused forward. "What is it you wished to speak with me about?" Kimbusa didn’t know what this character wanted, but he was certain it wasn’t worth his time. But, with sudden and alarming sharpness, Zaccanelli’s tone became fiercely intense. "Well, now that we’ve got all that bullshit out of the way, you and me got a fuckin’ problem, you slanty-eyed piece of shit.," Zaccanelli spouted. Zaccanelli stood inches from Kimbusa’s face, though he stood a good five inches shorter. Surprisingly, Kimbusa didn’t sense himself growing angry at the man, despite his egregious assault. For some reason, the guards stealing Ruby from him infuriated him to such an extent that this small little would-be gangster barely registered. Kimbusa retained his smug shortness. "What problem is that?" he asked flatly, shooting a quick glance at each bodyguard flanking the desk. The one on the left had noticeably begun to sweat. "Ruby. You seem to have taken quite a liking to her, and she seems to have taken quite a liking to you. And that’s too fuckin’ bad for you, because Ruby’s my fuckin’ girl. Her job is to get the regulaz to drop a few extra bucks at the tables, not to fuckin’ converse with ugly freaks of nature like you," Zaccanelli spouted, without taking a breath. His voice was stiff and confident; he was clearly used to intimidating anyone who came on to his girl a little too strong. Unfortunately for him, he’d never faced a man the likes of Kimbusa before. "So here’s the fuckin’ deal, listen the fuck up. You come back here on business from Sindo only. You don’t come here to gamble, you don’t so much as take a shit in my joint. And if you ever so much as look at Ruby again, I’ll-" Kimbusa cut him off. "Here is what will happen now," he said, his voice low and frightfully grim. All five bodyguards began to stir, each reaching slowly for their jacket, but looking as if they may crumble from fear at any moment. "You’re going to take an extended vacation. It seems your mother has come down with an illness, and being such a caring man, you’ve rushed home to tend to her. In the meantime, I’ll oversee your business for you. And I will make certain to take care of Ruby… personally. I am sure you will be comforted to know that she will be looked after in your absence." Zaccanelli was aghast with fury, and he flung his jacket off in his rage. "I beg your fuckin’ pardon?! You’re tryin’ to fuckin’ muscle me out of my own place? You just signed your own fuckin’ trip to the meat locker, asshole. Razo, take this piece of shit to the fuckin’ alley." Kimbusa didn’t need to turn around to know what happened next. Razo, apparently the first guard who had approached him, froze on the spot. His hand was tucked in his jacket, his fingers wrapped around the grip of his gun, but he found not the energy to produce it. He, like the others, had quietly heard the rumors, and they were instantly certain that this man before them was the demon they had been told about. None had seen first hand what this man was capable of, but they were certain that any move against him would prove fatal. Even with Zaccanelli growing red with fury, his voice venomous and intense, they knew the most dangerous man in the room was the five hundred pound behemoth towering over them. Zaccanelli was incensed, stomping his feet as he demanded action from his petrified guards. "What the fuck is wrong with you assholes?! Someone just shoot this sorry fuck!" As he shouted the words, his face grew redder yet, veins bulging in his thick neck, pulsing with ferocity. Kimbusa remained perfectly still, an intense and unwavering stare still locked on Zaccanelli. Still the guards remained motionless. Zaccanelli kept fuming, but suddenly appeared to grow weak. Even as he continued slinging obscenities, storming about the room berating his guards and threatening Kimbusa with every spare breath, Zaccanelli’s hold on the situation was clearly slipping away. They had warned him of the dangers of confronting this mysterious beast, but Zaccanelli had refused to be intimidated. But now, caught in the inhuman gaze of Shindo’s monster, Zaccanelli appeared to be melting down before their eyes. "Nobody comes into my place and muscles me! This is my fuckin’ town, I own this fuckin’ town!" he continued to shout, his voice growing weaker with every word. "You’re dead, you hear me?! DEAD!" And with that, Zaccanelli collapsed to the floor, his hand grasping his left shoulder with a sudden wail of pain. And still the guards refused to move, too terrified to help their employer and face the wrath of the demon. Kimbusa tried to hold back a chuckle, but couldn’t help but laugh at the man’s weakness. "Help him to a hospital," Kimbusa ordered as he stared down at the writhing man on the floor. Instantly two guards rushed past him, and even as they pulled Zaccanelli off the floor, they felt feeble in the shadow of Kimbusa. The two guards moved to the back of the room and opened a hidden door, pushing their way outside and disappearing with their gasping employer. The remaining three guards, the two in the sunglasses and Razo, remained stunned. Kimbusa turned to Razo and offered him a sadistic smile. "Well then… Looks like I’ll be in charge from now on. You can go about your duties… Razo." Wide-eyed and breathless, Razo actually dropped to a knee and bowed before Kimbusa, and never raised his eyes even as he turned and slipped back out to the casino floor. He hadn’t intended to do it; he had no clue how to run a casino, nor how to oversee any sort of illegal activity of this scope. But it had been an amusing distraction, and certainly was a welcome release after the overwhelming frustration he’d suffered only moments before. But a moment’s reflection brought him to the conclusion he’d been toying with ever since Zaccanelli’s explosion; he owned this casino now… and everything in it. He had just seized control of the most prestigious underground casino in Tokyo without moving a muscle, or even raising his voice. Who needed a gun when he could fell anyone who opposed him on reputation alone? His mere presence on the streets had set the underworld trembling, and a few short weeks were all it took to achieve legendary status. He still operated in shadow; he had fallen in love with the Yakuza for the anonymity they provided him, and that had not changed. And yet only now had he realized the extent of his power. Few had seen him; fewer still knew his name. But these men had known precisely who they were dealing with the moment he walked into the room. And without the slightest effort, he had sent one of the underworld’s most powerful men into a massive heart attack. For all Kimbusa knew, the man could be dead already. Oh well, he thought to himself. This could be fun.
