Date: 2006-01-10 Your not saving me, I exhale. My vision was blurry, as my mind stumbles onto the realisation that he has found me. Again. "But Steven, I have in so many ways." His piercing voice cuts through the darkness, as I struggle to see. Each breath felt like Everest to a mountaineer, as I began to be welcomed back to the real world. A sharp pain in my ribs grabs my attention. I thought I had been careful these past three years. I thought I had evaded him for good. Maybe little Steven shouldn’t think anymore. "Everything you have ever achieved, everything you have ever done…I was the one who let it happen." My head tilted back, making me groan in pain. I was carved from the same mould as him. Yet I despise his very existence. "I made you son." Fuck you Kevin, I exhale. "For the last time." He steps out of the darkness, and slaps me with the back of his hand. I could taste the iron on my mouth as the blood pours. "Call me Dad." … When I opened my eyes, I didn’t know if the nightmare was beginning or ending. I tried to fathom a world where I could live just like the Walton’s on the TV. Where they loved you, and everything turned out all right in the end. In my tender 16 years on this earth, I realised that my fantasy world would never come true. That I would never be truly loved. As long as he lived anyway. I grabbed my jeans and pulled them on with haste, as I looked rampantly for my lost pair of converse. However hard I looked, all I could see was a mountain of clothes and rubbish. 7:34am, I had to get a move on. I picked up a creased grey shirt, as I pulled it over my head while I walked to my bedroom door. When I emerged from the t-shirt, he appeared. "Where you going?" I hated his voice. No where, I shrugged . Lies. He let out a slight laugh, before lashing out a fist to my startled jaw, sending me flying back to my slum of a bedroom floor. "Don’t lie to me boy." He said as calm as ever. I rubbed my jaw with resentment, as I began to rise. He met me with a kick to the ribs, making me sound in pain. I could hear a rib crack as he stands above my writhing body. "I didn’t say to get up either." Die. Die. Die you fucking scum. He walked around my room; in his boxer shorts and white vest, thinking he was a god. I could imagine his fucked up mind, sounding out the same old soundtrack of how he is better then everyone else. Kevin the Great. The Almighty Kevin Murphy. The big man Kevin Murphy. Kicking around his only son because he can. "Your not going school today, It can wait." My only sanctuary against this crime to humanity. And he even took that away from me. "You have to call in the factory and tell John I’m not coming in today. Just give him a bullshit excuse about me being too ill. He will understand more if you go in person." Big man Kevin Murphy can’t even call in sick to his shitty job, huh? Probably my most stupid comment to date. Punch to cranium. Another to my nose. Repeat. "What have I fucking told you." He grabbed me by my now blood-soaked t-shirt. "Call me Dad." He pushes me down to the ground, as I hold my broken face in pain. I couldn’t show it though. I wouldn’t give him the satisfaction. He walks out of my room, muttering more orders. "Change your shirt before you go too. You look a fucking mess." And that was the daily social interaction in the loving Murphy household. … I never liked lots of noise. Especially when your brain has just been beaten senseless. I doubted some paracetomol could help this headache. The engines of the machines rattled the whole warehouse, as I stepped through the mass of men working with their ear muffs and bored faces. Each pressing a button, Pulling a lever. A job a monkey could perform, for a generation of labour monkeys. I imagined what it would be like to work in one of these places, when I was a little kid. Watching my dad come home; disgruntled, from a wasted brain packaging the latest toy, kitchen appliance, whatever. I actually was intrigued. And asked the obvious question to my dad. His answer? "Shut the fuck up." Didn’t expect it to come out as top answer on Family Fortunes, but whatever. I made up my own version of factory life, even if I have never experienced it. Walking into the factory for the first time actually confirmed my mindset. Testicle Numbingly Boredom. I actually began to sympathize for Kevin. But then again, he just a complete wanker without this job. Hello? I say weakly, knocking on the boss’ door. No answer. Knock. Knock. Again, no answer. I shrugged to myself as I turned around, to walk into a giant of a man. His overbearing presence astounded me, as I looked up at him with wide eyes. "What do you want?" He bellowed out, looking upon me. Er…I’m Kevin Murphy’s Son. I said unconvincingly. "Good for you. But you haven’t stated why your outside of my office." Cheeky bastard, didn’t let me finish. Well, Kevin aint’ coming in today. He is ill. My voice remained calm, even though I have to say I was scared shitless of this man mountain. "Not comin’ in eh?" He made a nonchalant face my way. "Maybe he shouldn’t send his boy to send his bad news in future." Then a brainwave. He isn’t even sick. I blurted it out. Like a tourettes tick that couldn’t be helped. I got the big mans attention this time. "You kidding me right?" he boomed, this time with every ounce of meaning in his voice. ‘Fraid not. This felt better then good. "Why are you telling me this?" He quizzed, puzzled by Kevin’s own son’s Judas treatment. I didn’t say a word. No explanation needed. Just a solitary point to my bruised and battered face. "Listen kid, you stick around here for a second. I need to make a few calls." And for the first time in my life, I finally got one victory of the Almighty Kevin Murphy. Kevin the Great. It was better then good. … "You think you could get this far in that tournament of yours without me?" His words where spat out with venom, as I looked up with disgust. Disgusting. How could he of been part of making me? "I told you about that mole "Frank", I posted you those leaflets to enter this tournament. I orchestrated this so you will get fucking far in this tournament. And what do I get? You fucking a whore in a school toilet during a fucking AA meeting or whatever you do." What do you want? My words are calm. I know this pisses him off the most. The fact that I don’t give a shit about what he is saying. And he laughs at me. "I’m giving you the best option you have got going for your life son." I really thought I was the only one on drugs too. "I’m focusing you." Liar. "I’m preparing you." Fucking liar. "And ultimately…I’m saving you." I spit my blood filled saliva at his face, hitting him square in the eyes. I could feel his rage building, as he wipes his brow. Your still a twisted fuck. I throw my words at him. He raises his fist and pummels my temple with one sharp, vicious blow. My eyes voluntarily shut, as my head jolts back into a sideward position. I hear the muttering of words before I pass into the darkness of unconsciousness‘. "Its not twisted…its love."
