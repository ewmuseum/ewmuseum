"Time To Get Ready"

24 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-24 *Sucess? Is it really worth it? I thought to myself as I seen my friend Larry Lighting put into the a stretcher. He was a man I trained with in Fort Knocks Pro Wrestling looking for a chance to make it. Instead Joshua "Hot Body" Johnson sends 2 big goons out there to injure him. This was his big chane to prove how good he was and get out of that no name fed. He wanted to head to Japan for a bit and wrestler before coming back to The States. He was the kind of guy you want to see succeed in well anything. I wasn't able to go with Larry Lighting to the hospital that night so I drove home with my mind wondering in a million places. I had a big WMW pay per view to worry about where I could very well be wrestling 2 mataches in one night. My 1st match against Dawn McFarlane which is no easy task. If I manage to get by her it would either be Justin Evers or Shadow again no easy task. On the next burnot I'd be facing Justin Evers in an I Quit Match. The kind of match that settles grudges but this one could go on for a long time. I was the number one contender for Shadow's Ace Suprior Championship so even if I don't win I still will enter the ring with both of them. GTT4 I faced a man named Moraless and don't know too much about the guy besides the fact that he works for UWF. What does this matter to me right now? Not a thing... I put family and friends ahead of my career. Right now all that matters is the well being of my friend Larry Lighting. I just needed to get home and see if I could even attempt what went down tonight aside. I needed to focus but right now that seems impossible. I pull up into my drive away and walk into the house. I walked around the house and found Valkyrie lying in bed sound asleep. I decide not to wake her and just let her sleep. I close the door softly and I just crash on the sofa for awhile. I sit there wondering how am I going to pull this off. I can't concentrate on anything and this is argueably the biggest time in my young career.* Fred Grest- Screw It...... *I turn on the tv and start watching an old 3 Stoogers episode. Its up louder then I thought it was because as soon as I turn it on you here a loud racket. A bunch of shit just fell on Curly ofcourse what else would it be. I turn it down but it has already woke up Valkyrie. My hot ass girlfriend comes out of the bedroom not looking so hot. Well do you look great waking up in the middle of the night because some dumbass had the tv too loud?* Valkyrie- How was the MWF Show Sweetie? *I look at her with a blank stare and she realizes that he didnt win. I doubt she could tell what went down though.* Fred Grest- He got screwed over by Joshua Johnson. Valkyrie- What happened?! Fred Grest- He sent a two guys out there pushed Larry off the top rope and now he's in the hospital. Valkyrie- Thats fucked up Fred Grest- Yes it was... Just go back to bed I just need to think. Valkyrie- Alright sweetie *She left and went back to bed I sat there for awhile watching the 3 Stooges. I laughed a few times but I was pissed as hell. I stepped outside for a breath of fresh air and sat on the steps. I thought about what had happen today and I wondered why I do what I do. Why am I choosing to be the hero? Why do I want to be the good guy? Because its who I am. A man is walking across the street and recongizes who I am. He comes towards me with an uinsure look on his face.* Man- Are you Fred Grest? Fred Grest- Yes I am........ how may I help you? Man- I'd like to thank you. Fred Grest- Why is that? Man- For giving my son someone to look up too. Fred Grest- No thanks needed I am just being myelf. Man- Ever since my son got to meet you its all he's been talking about? Fred Grest- Whose your son? Man- Paul Nuemann *Fred Grest smiles as he remembers the whole "THE MOTHERFUCKING BEST!" line* Fred Grest- What are you doing out this late? Man- My car died... Fred Grest- Want a lift? Man- No thanks I need the walk. Fred Grest- I can understand that be careful Man- Thanks I have to get going. Fred Grest- Take care and tell your son I said hello. Man- Will do. Fred Grest- Oh and tell him I am going to win at GTT4 for him. Man- Alright that'll make him happy. *As the man walked away I knew I couldn't lose. I knew I had to suceeded in this business. If I am the role model now I had to become a major star in this business. Not to satisfy my own ego but to be the guy you can look up too. I am pretty clean cut as you can get into this business and will continue to be. I always said I was the best and now its time to prove it. If you are going to be looked up too why not be the best at what you do. The good guy always win huh? We'll see about that.* Fred Grest- Time to get ready *I walked back into the house and the 1st thing to do is get some sleep. A tired man won't be able to do much.*
