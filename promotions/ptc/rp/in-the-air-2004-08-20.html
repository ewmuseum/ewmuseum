In the Air!?

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-20 If you don’t stand up for what you think is right, then you’re wrong. ----Yogi Berra [Wesley Williams IV, otherwise known as Fnord in the wrestling community, is sitting comfortably in his personal jet as if starts to descent toward the earth. The captain of the jet comes on the speaker system.] Captain: We will be landing in Houston, Texas shortly, Mr. Williams. I hope the flight was pleasant and I’ll see you when we land. [Fnord leans back in his seat and closes his eyes as the jet starts to land.] Fnord: All the way from Japan back home to Houston… I gotta talk to my stupid ass uncle and then get back to wherever the hell this PTC IPPV is in order to kick some ass for the home team… [The jet softly lands on the airstrip in Houston, Texas and rolls to a stop. The brakes are applied and the pilot steps out of the cockpit. The pilot is none other than Fnord’s half-brother, the ex-wrestler once known as Joshua Justice. Wearing a red, white, and blue suit, Joshua smiles at Fnord as he opens the exit door of the jet. He extends the stairs and steps out of the jet. Fnord follows shortly behind him. Joshua puts the stairs back up and closes the door. The co-pilot drives the jet away. Joshua puts his arm around Fnord and gives him a light punch on the shoulder.] Joshua: So, you only come to see me when you need to talk to our uncle? Fnord: Nah, you could come visit me anytime. Hell, I would love it if you made an appearance in 4WW to help me out! [They start to walk towards a limo as their conversation continues.] Joshua: I would love to, but actually, I’m thinking of running for governor of Texas. Fnord: What! [Fnord stops him in his tracks and they go face to face.] Fnord: What the hell are you talking about? Joshua: It’s true. Uncle Dan still works as my PR Agent and he thinks it might be a good step. Fnord: Hell, if Arnold can do it, why not you? [Fnord and Joshua hug with sappy smiles on their faces. All of a sudden, an earthquake starts! Everything starts to shake violently. The sun seems to drop and everything goes black.] Voice: We are having a tad bit of turbulence and will be in tiptop shape shortly. [Fnord opens his eyes to see that obnoxious screaming people on a regular plane surround him. He rolls his eyes as he bangs his head back against the seat.] Fnord: Why me…? [Fnord is about to rise to his feet to calm everyone down, but thinks better of it. All of a sudden, a Jerms action figure comes flying through the air and hits Fnord right in the face! The action figure bounces off his face and lands in his lap. Fnord picks up the toy of Jerms and chuckles out loud. The obnoxious overly round woman to his left looks at him like he’s crazy.] Woman: Aren’t you scared!? [Fnord looks over to the bouncing woman, she’s bouncing because the plane is still bouncing, and shakes his head.] Fnord: What are you… 350… 400? Woman: Excuse me!? [Fnord pokes her with the Jerms toy.] Fnord: Pounds woman! Pounds! [The woman frowns and gives a raspberry to Fnord as she vibrates along with the plane. A flight attendant with her drink cart comes running/rolling by very quickly. Fnord extends his arm and stops them from moving any farther. The flight attendant yells out a passionate ‘thank you’ but Fnord doesn’t even acknowledge her. He grabs a few of the small bottles of alcohol with his free hand. After getting a handful, he moves his ‘blocking’ arm out of the way. The flight attendant and the beverage cart go flying down the isle, while Fnord points and laughs.] Woman: That wasn’t very nice. [Fnord chugs one of the small bottles of alcohol and then glances towards the bouncing and jiggling fat woman.] Fnord: Listen here, fatty, I’m not here to be nice. I’m hear to get down to Houston to talk to my uncle about some stupid crap to find out who the hell my father and grandfather was. Woman: That sounds like a lot… Fnord: Of course it sounds like a lot! Don’t be an idiot! I also gotta show up at some stupid ass PPV for the PTC thing. Hell, I’ve been told my teammates… they all suck! [The woman covers her eyes as the plane starts to plummet down perpendicular to the ground!] Woman: Oh my GAWD! Fnord: That’s right! Think about it, Jerms, Mike Marvel… whoops… Biddle, and Dark Angel… dammit… Darek DeAngelo, and me in the ring together! Is Ian mad! [He opens another small bottle of alcohol and before even pouring the alcohol into his mouth, it flies out of the bottle and hits him in the face.] Fnord: Now, that’s what I call service! [He throws the small bottle behind him and it hits some little kid in the head. He was already crying and hurt, so it doesn’t matter. The oxygen masks fall out of the compartment above the passengers’ heads.] Fnord: I can call him Ian, because I’m one of his favorites! [The fat woman grabs the oxygen mask and tightly stretches it over her fat melon-head. She looks over to Fnord, who is still downing… or would that be upping now… the alcohol.] Fnord: He loves me! So, he picked me to represent 4WW in this huge match. This is to show which federation is the most dominant. I don’t even know why that is a question! We so rock! [All of a sudden, everything goes black. The motion of the plane’s wheels hitting the pavement wakens Fnord. He sits straight up startled.] Fnord: A dream IN a dream? That’s fucked up! [Fnord looks to his left and the fat woman is sitting there munching away on a cheeseburger she must have had hidden in her purse or something. She’s shoving it in her mouth, having the ketchup and mustard spray out from the bottom of the burger all over herself. She doesn’t seem to mind. The plane rolls to a stop and the pilot announces that they have landed in Houston and can now depart the plane.] Fnord: It’s about damn time! [Fnord steps out of his seat at the same exact time that everyone else does. Everyone else goes to their overhead compartments to get their carry-ons. Fnord, on the other hand, brought no carry-ons! He ducks and covers and dodges and swerves until he almost gets to the door. He stands up straight, thinking that he has gotten through without a scratch. Fnord turns around and heads towards the exit. Out of nowhere, the bathroom door slam open, crashing into Fnord’s face.] Fnord: DAMMIT! [The door closes and a flight attendant looks at him with a smirk. It’s the same flight attendant that he dreamed went flying down the isle with no help from Fnord! She helps him up, but you can see a smirk behind her kindness.] Flight Attendant: I’m so sorry sir. Fnord: Just get me the hell out of this plane! [Fnord brushes the flight attendant away and makes for the door exiting the plane. He turns back to view the crowd. They are all trying to squeeze through each other, but it’s not working. Fnord lets out a chuckle and then steps out of the door. To his surprise, the step down is about a foot deep. Fnord tumbles forward and hits his head on the ground. Everything goes black.] [When Fnord comes to, he is sitting in a chair. He tries to move, but he finds out that his arms, legs, and body are strapped down to the chair. He goes to close his eyes, but he finds out that his eyes are clamped open! He can’t close his eyes and he’s strapped to some strange chair.] Fnord: What the… [A huge screen, about the size of half a theater screen pops on. A few voices can be heard from behind Fnord, but they are hidden in the dark and they are talking too quietly to hear. A picture of Fnord comes on the huge screen. He’s standing with a stern look on his face and holding a gun, pointed to the ground. A deep and dark voice comes over a speaker system, which makes Fnord jump.] Voice: Fnord. Wesley Williams IV’s life spun out of control his whole life. He started to find out who he truly was in one deadly day in a wash of blood and a hail of gunfire. Did his life spin even more out of control or did it finally spin into control? [Fnord starts to struggle some, but it has no effect.] Voice: Williams had been leading the life of quiet desperation as Fnord—boring girlfriend, dead-end wrestling career, surfing for Internet porn on a daily basis. But when Dis Cordia plucked him out of his humdrum existence, she showed him possibilities unimagined: He was Wesley Williams The Fourth (IV), heir to a vast fortune and powers he didn’t even know he had. [Fnord stops struggling and actually starts to pay attention.] Fnord: Where’s the popcorn? [The voice ignores him, but the muttering people in the dark continue.] Voice: The scales finally dropped from Wesley Williams’ eyes, and he saw the hidden clockwork of the real power behind the throne. A vast ‘Fraternity’ was running the world, and had been since 1986, when they teamed up to destroy all the real ‘heroes’ and do-gooders. Fnord: Let’s get on with the show already! This is boring! Voice: Wesley was taken under the wing of Dis Cordia and The Professor, and embraced his life as the new Fnord. He could steal, kill, or fuck whoever he wanted, all without repercussion. He was born again, baptized in blood and fire, as Fnord. But then his real past started to show up. Truths came out that contradicted other truths. An Angel blessed his grandfather… [Fnord starts to squirm and wiggle in the chair.] Voice: Which path will he take? Will he listen to the rest of his uncle’s story about his grandfather and choose the path of righteousness? [The screen goes black. After a short time, another picture pops on. This one you can tell that Fnord doesn’t like. This looks like and older and slightly larger version of Fnord.] Voice: Wesley Williams The Third… Fnord’s father… The world’s deadliest assassin is dead, the victim of assassination himself. But while he was alive, he lived up to his name—frequently, and without remorse. He was known just as Death. [Fnord stares on, this being the first thing he has ever learned about this father.] Voice: Death killed for money, for sport, to advance his own cause. He killed for fun. The riches he gained brought him the Epicurean lifestyle he so greatly desired. He bathed in the finest champagne. He devoured the finest caviar. He bedded the finest women—and sometimes, the finest men. [Fnord’s face goes completely white with this. His eyes shoot back and forth.] Fnord: This is bullshit! End this crap now! Voice: So dangerous a foe was he, that when Death met his end, he was shot through the head with a gun from two miles away. No one would dare get closer. But Death passed on his legacy—and his killer instinct—to his illegitimate son, Wesley Williams IV. [The screen turns black as a single tear falls from Fnord’s left eye. It rolls down his cheek and away when a picture of one of his partners for the PTC IPPV match comes on the screen. This is Mike Biddle.] Voice: No one knows how this walking, steaming shit-pile came to life. Many say it formed in the world’s sewers, the result of a mystic spell, or perhaps science gone horribly awry. Suffice it to say that this shit-head is part of the 4WW army, perhaps the most vile creature among a horrifically vile crew. He is a mere foot soldier in the 4WW crew, used as muscle. [Fnord giggles at this.] Fnord: You’re damn right! Voice: He can make someone have diarrhea by just listening to him talk. But he can pound an enemy with the force of a freight train, then slip away silky smooth. Shit happens. Just pray he never happens to you. [Fnord slightly shakes head, not knowing what that was all about. Next on the screen comes Jerms. Jerms is in one of his stupid-ass poses.] Voice: Meet the Down’s Syndrome copy of the world’s greatest wrestler. Jerms is the product of an experiment to create a superstar. Part of the experiment worked: The body is there. The brain… not so much. [Fnord gets a huge smile on his face.] Voice: Jerms is massively technical with his skills, but dumb as a bag of hammers. He is the concept of the child with the gun, writ large. He’ll yank your arm off with a simple arm bar, and not even realize he’s done anything wrong… or was that right? [Fnord nods his head in agreement.] Voice: Jerms has all the opposite qualities of a new puppy—he’s not friendly, loyal isn’t a word in his vocabulary, and he couldn’t care less about anyone other than himself. Unfortunately, he does have all the toilet training of a new puppy. What if the most technically sound wrestler in the universe had brain damage? His name is Jerms. [Fnord rolls his eyes in disgust.] Fnord: Jerms, did you make this shit up? HA! [A picture of Darek DeAngelo pops up on the screen and right away, Fnord’s face turns into a frown.] Voice: Once, he damned souls, now he saves them. The man who was Dark Angel is turning into a devout Christian, the most pious of men. But what happens when he expects Heaven, but finds… Nothing. [Fnord’s eyes turn from anger to interest.] Voice: No God. No Heaven. No afterlife. None of what he has been promised for living a devout life. Just a void. And the void will leave a void in his heart. Let’s hope that never happens. Let’s hope that Darek never sees the truth. If that ever happened, it would create a man without a conscience. He will live life completely without a moral compass. With the fact that there is no eternal consequence to his action, he will life for the day and that is all. Kind of like how Dark Angel was. Dark Angel did everything you’ve ever thought about in your darkest moments. Every whim he came up with, he carted to it. And his whims tended toward the dark end of the spectrum. [Fnord yells out.] Fnord: But now he’s a punk-ass bitch! [There is a pause.] Voice: If Dark Angel wanted to eat, fuck or ill something, he did it, without thought. He was a creature of pure id. Now, after spending years embracing the dark, he has turned to the light. What happens if he finds out that the light doesn’t exist? Then he will plunge even deeper into the darkness. Fnord: Now, THAT would be awesome, but he’s still a punk. [The screen goes black. One word pops on the screen.] CORE [And then everything goes black.] [Fnord opens his eyes and he is staring up at the ceiling. He sits up on the couch he was laying on. It’s a very nice couch. Fnord starts to look around the room. He sees pictures that he recognizes. Above a fireplace, he sees an old picture of ‘The Cowboy’ Daniel Garrett, back when his uncle was the DWW World Champion. The picture above the fireplace shows him with his title belt. Fnord stands up, feels a little dizzy and sits back down.] Fnord: ANYONE HOME?! [Fnord’s half-brother Joshua Justice steps through the entrance to the kitchen. He raises his glass of orange juice to his mouth and takes in a gulp. He looks over to his half-brother Fnord and shakes his head.] Justice: Oh, shut-up already. Fnord: Yo, bro! How the hell did I get here? Justice: They called me from the airport. After making an ass of yourself, you tripped and knocked yourself unconscious. Then tons of fat people walked all over you, kicking your head as they walked by. Fnord: No wonder I’m dizzy. Fat people suck. [Justice walks over and takes a seat next to Fnord.] Justice: Why were you coming all the way here anyway? Fnord: I need to talk to our uncle. He’s got more information for me about my grandfather and my father! [Justice pauses and takes another gulp of his orange juice. He sets the glass on the table and looks towards Fnord.] Justice: I have some bad news. Garrett’s going to be in Japan. He’s on tour with 4WW. Fnord: WHAT! Justice: That’s not all. I guess you haven’t been watching the boob tube. You have a match in Japan. Fnord: WHAT! Justice: That’s right. You are in a triple threat match. Fnord: With who? Chris West and Lord Byron? Justice: Nope, Jerms and ICE. Fnord: Well, at least it’s the main event. Justice: Nope. The East Wind Title match will be the main event. Fnord: WHAT! This so sucks. I gotta beat some asses on the PTC IPPV too! Justice: The PTC what? [Fnord puts his head down in his hands to try to ready himself to stand up. He gets steady and steps up to his feet. He walks towards the kitchen. Justice picks up his orange juice and follows him.] Fnord: I don’t know. It’s some big team thing. I gotta team up with Jerms—a loser, Mike Biddle—a bigger loser, and Darek, a.k.a Dark Angel—the man that doesn’t trust me… and I don’t trust him. Justice: Sounds like fun. Fnord: You could say that. So, there were like three other teams in this match… Then one dropped out… Then another disappeared… So, alls that are left is someone named Core or something. Justice: Who’s in that? Fnord: Um… some fat guy named Munson… Some retard named Nilsson or something… Then two other guys I never heard of. Smiley and Matt Epic… They sound gay. Justice: Don’t be prejudice. Fnord: Don’t be gay. Justice: You suck at arguing. Fnord: You just suck. [Justice smacks Fnord on the back of the head.] Justice: Anyway, I already booked you a flight back to Japan. You gotta get outta here and then to Japan and then to the PTC IPPV. Go. Fnord: I don’t want to. Justice: No no no no no no no no no no no, DO IT. Fnord: Okay, that was lame. Justice: No no no no no no no no no no, DO IT. Fnord: Shut up! [Fnord black out yet again. When he wakes back up, he is sitting on a plane. It starts to land as the pilot makes an announcement.] Pilot (overhead): We are now landing in Japan. Thank you for flying… [Fnord slams his head on his headrest as everything fades to black.] So, it’s Darek, Biddle, Jerms and I against Core first… Then we take on the other losers. People that have to get past their own first matches. People like… Noah Hanson… Cyrus Skar… Josh… Asesino… Kenny Mercury… Those are names that I’ve wanted to rumble with in the past. Those are names of superstars in PTC that think they are the ‘shit’ and that pissed me off. That pisses me off just enough to beat the living hell out of them. I got nothing to lose. I have everything to gain. Can you see how PTC will treat me and the other 4WW superstars when we stand on top of the PTC Survival? Yeah… that would be something. No… That WILL be something. 4WW is coming out on top of this so-called ‘Survival’ that we are involved in. Core will be an easy win. Then when the other punks are worn out and tired… That’s when 4WW will get our second wind. You other fools don’t know me. You don’t know that I specialize in Iron Man matches. They are what I do best. I always get pumped up and do serious damage. And that’s what’s going to happen… those are my two favorite words… SERIOUS DAMAGE! 4WW… we are going to cause… Serous… Damage… he he he he he he he he he he HE HE HE HE HE HE HE!
