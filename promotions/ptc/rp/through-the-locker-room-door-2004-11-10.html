Through the locker room door

10 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-10 A young man stepped out into the chill November morning and inhaled the frosty air deeply. The smile on his face was so broad as to almost split it. He quietly closed the front door of the mansion and walked in the direction of town. Neighbours looked in askance at him as he strutted down the street, his head held high and his eyes glazed over with happiness. This was the kind of small town where everyone knew everyone else, but that day, Craig Goat was a stranger to them. The locals were used to seeing a shy young man leave the mansion, hunched over with the worries of his world pressed firmly down on his shoulders. A man who darted from place to place, always in a hurry, always with some errand to complete. But the man who they saw today was a different man. He seemed… happy. Craig flashed his ID and a smile at the guard and entered the mostly empty building, at this time of the day; there would be only cleaners and the person he was here to see at the Arena. As he walked through the labyrinth of corridors, the theme to ‘Saturday Night Fever’ started to play in his mind. Strutting in time to his internal jukebox, almost led along by his groin, he decided that today would be the best day of his life. Reaching his destination, he rapped on a door identical to all the others except for the large ‘Keep Out – Seriously’ sign nailed to it. A few moments passed before it was wrenched open and almost immediately slammed shut. “Nurse! Let me in. I’ve come to bring you home.” He wished he’d brought a hat, forget about John Travolta, he decided that this situation would be more akin to Richard Gere in ‘An Officer and a Gentleman.’ After a considerable pause, Craig thought he heard the reply, “Oh fuck off Craig,” but surely he had misheard? He knocked on the door again. “Come on Nurse, let me in.” No reply. Craig’s brow creased in confusion. Nurse Esme Matilda Wretched had always been unpredictable, but this shunning of him was something he definitely hadn’t foreseen. With a sigh of resignation, he sank to his haunches, resting his back against the cold stone wall. He could almost feel Nurse’s rage emanating through the wall. What had he done wrong now? Since he had met her a year ago, Craig had done everything in his power to make Nurse Wretched, his beloved Esme, happy. He’d committed his own parents to an asylum, handed her the keys to their house and let her replace the priceless antiques with tat. He woke earlier than he would have liked and brewed her coffee to perfection every morning. He had hand washed her underwear twice weekly, perhaps the only chore he enjoyed. He cooked and cleaned for her. He’d been her sparring partner in the ring, taking beating after beating on a daily basis to help her in her bid to become the top wrestler in XUDF. He’d stalked her opponents and sabotaged their chances against her without hesitation. He’d bribed officials for her. He undertook dangerous trips to South America to buy the illegal sedatives she injected her opponents with. He celebrated her victories with her and been there as her whipping boy on the rare occasions she lost a match. He’d been her confidant throughout everything she’d gone through during her titanic rise in XUDF, and all he had ever asked for in return was the chance to be near her, to be able to love her even though it had become clear very early on that his adoration would never be returned. Compared to recent events, all that could be considered the nicer parts of the last year. He didn’t know why Nurse was so angry with him, but he was pretty sure it had something to do with GTT4. Ever since Nurse Wretched had forced him into entering the tournament, things had gotten complicated and more twisted then ever. GTT4 had been nothing but pain and hardship for him, despite his surprise victories over Jonathan Borealis, Jenna Ryder, Flyin’ Brian Calvin and one of the favourites, Seymour Almasy, the Final Fantasy himself. He would gladly have given up each one for just a modicum of the irregular stability that had been. But no, Nurse had decided to finally make good on her long ago promise to turn him into a champion, a pet wrestler she could be proud of. Craig cringed to himself when he realised that his life was indeed a cliché; the phrase “Be careful of what you wish for, it might just come true” being the one in mind. Had he really once begged her to remake him in her image? The muscles in his thighs began to cramp, and the cold of the wall at his back and the floor beneath him began to seep through to his bones. Weary and impatient, he reached around to bang on the door again. “I’m not going away ‘til you talk to me Esme. Just tell me what’s wrong and I’ll do everything I can to fix it.” The door opened suddenly. Nurse stood there, looking dishevelled with her hair hanging in greasy clumps – living in an Arena locker room did have some major drawbacks. “You know what’s wrong Craig.” As quickly as the door had opened, it was slammed shut again. Infuriated, Craig banged his head against the wall. Why did women do this? Why did they assume men were mind readers when millions of years of history and precedence had proved that they barely knew their own thoughts, never mind the thoughts of others. With a sigh, Craig scanned his memory for anything he may have done to upset Nurse, but came up blank. She had been angry, furious even when she stormed out of Goat Mansion shortly before his match with Seymour Almasy. At the time, Craig was caught firmly between the wills of the two women in his life; his mother, recently released from the insane asylum, had wanted him to quit wrestling and return to being the geeky sickly child she had known prior to meeting Nurse. She on the other hand had wanted Craig to continue on the path to glory she had set before him. Craig’s hesitation in choosing had caused Nurse to storm out saying she’d only return if he grew some balls. Well, he had balls now! He’d managed to work around his mother and pummel Almasy to get to the bracket finals of the tournament, so what on Earth could be bothering her? Was she on her period? On the other side of the locker room door, Nurse sat on the bare bench and sobbed quietly. She hoped Craig would figure out what was making her feel like this, because she sure as hell didn’t know! When it came to emotions, Nurse was used only to the more violent ones such as rage, fury and hatred. This crying and the accompanying sadness were alien to her and as such angered. Nurse was used to being on control; in control of her own life and that of everyone around her. She had become a nurse originally to feed her need for power over others, and it had grown from there. She had always felt nothing but contempt for the depressed patients that had passed through her ward, believing their wailing and suicide attempts to be nothing more than weakness that they should be able to extricate themselves from easily if they actually tried. So sitting alone with tears rolling down her face was a nightmare scenario for her. Silently, she cursed GTT4. This tournament was supposed to be the making of her as a manager and top trainer; instead, it had turned her into a snivelling mess such as Craig had once been. She longed to let him in to comfort her, but couldn’t bring herself to let him see her so weak… so human. The first threads of a plan began to form in Craig’s mind. He would lure Nurse out of her isolation with the strongest tool available; her own ego. He moved sideways a little so that he was now sitting directly outside the door and tentatively called out to her. “Esme? Please talk to me. I need you. I can’t do this without you!” Inside, Nurse hesitated briefly before replying, “No Craig, you don’t need me – not anymore.” “But I do! I can’t continue in the tournament without you. I still have a lot to learn that only you can teach me. I’ve come this far because of you. Can’t you see that?” Nurse snorted, “Really? Then how come you managed to defeat Almasy without me? I’m redundant now.” As she spoke the words, she realised just how true they were. Craig had done something on his own! For the first time since she had known him, he had taken some initiative without her having to bully, push or hypnotise him (don’t ask!). His dependence on her had always been a constant, and now it was quickly diminishing. Was this the reason for her melancholy? Nurse felt truly alone. For as far back as she could remember she had always had people relying on her, her parents, her patients and Craig. That she abused the trust they placed in her was inconsequential, the fact that they all had needed her in some way was the very heart of self esteem. “You’re not redundant! I only won the match because of you. You are the strength that drives me. When I stepped in that ring, it was your voice I heard in my head instructing me, supporting me and telling me I could do it.” “Ha! You must be mistaken. I don’t say things like that. I might tell you to stop acting like a piece of shit baby and get on with it, but I doubt I’d get all nice about it. Maybe it was your mother’s voice you heard.” Craig sensed his plan was working. He could almost hear the swish of the rod and the reel spin as Nurse fished for compliments. “No, it was you I heard, I may have toned down the actual comments though! I’m facing Jimmy Bong in a matter of days. You have to help me.” “Oh for Chrissake! I never thought I’d see the day when you of all people would patronise me! Bong is not a challenge. Anyone could beat him.” “Well then tell me why four other champion wrestlers have failed to do that? Come on Nurse, it’s a miracle I’m still in this tournament.” Nurse smiled. That was true. Craig wouldn’t have made it past Arena bouncers if it hadn’t been for her. She’d created this incarnation of Craig… but had she gone too far? Had she given him the confidence and strength to continue on his own or could he really still need her guidance? “If you don’t help me, I swear I’ll drop out.” “I won’t let you do that Craig! If Bong wins, it has to be because you fucked up… and I don’t allow those to happen.” Now it was Craig’s turn to smile. Nurse might be all for cheating to gain a victory, but her twisted moral code meant she despised it when a wrestler sailed through a match due to a no-show. Before she could commit to rejoining Craig, there was one important question Nurse had left to ask. “You’ve gotten rid of your mother haven’t you? I assume you wouldn’t be stupid enough to come for me if you hadn’t.” Craig cursed to himself, he had hoped this wouldn’t come up. “Well, I guess the answer is yes and no, but more yes then no. I’ll explain on the way back.” Seemingly satisfied with this, Nurse made her decision. Craig was relieved when he heard movement from inside; water splashing in a wash basin and the rustling of paper towels. With her face scrubbed clean of the tears, Nurse opened the door to her protégée. Before she could protest, he grabbed her in a bone crunching hug and kissed her with a loud smack on the cheek. Nurse pulled her arm back as far as she could and let her fist fly into his face. Staring down at his unconscious body, she muttered, “No need to be insolent and take advantage, just because I love you.” … … “Oh my God!” she screamed with dismay, “I love him??”
