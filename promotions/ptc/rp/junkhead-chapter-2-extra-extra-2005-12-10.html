Junkhead - Chapter 2: "Extra! Extra!"

10 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-10 "Only after disaster can we be resurrected." – Tyler Durden Pathetic might be the word I would use, but it doesn’t quite have the humiliation that Davis Branxton should feel along with it. No, Davis Leonard Branxton deserves a word much more pathetic than the word Pathetic. He’s totally lost now. Completely. He lays on rough unfinished wooden floors of an abandoned Starbucks coffeehouse in uptown Manhattan. It’s cold so Davis is making sure that he keeps warm by wrapping himself up in the daily news. It’s best to get that very day’s paper because it’s fresh and crispy; less likely to have been rained or pissed on. It’s warmer. Davis has been trying to get to sleep for one hour forty-three minutes and twelve seconds approximately, but can’t seem to lull himself enough. He shivers. Davis wants a pill, but he also wants to ration them. He’s doing a poor job of it though. He needs something else to calm him and take his mind, what little of it is left, off of the cold. The first thing he tries is singing to himself. He starts out loudly, belting out his old entrance themes over the years starting with "Life’s Sweet Drug" by Slash’s Snakepit and running the gauntlet through "The Perfect Drug" by Nine Inch Nails, "Bleed American" by Jimmy Eat World, "Junkhead" by Alice In Chains, and "Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns N’ Roses. Once he gets to GNR he’s barely even humming anything anymore. He blinks. Hard. Next Davis does some push-ups to tire himself out. He makes it to fifteen and gives up. His body isn’t nearly what it used to be. He wants another pill- NO! He can’t go back, no, not now. Ration. There has to be another way to get to sleep. He starts to read his blanket: the sports section. Initially nothing interests him. The Philadelphia Eagles suck without two people Davis hasn’t heard of, McNabb and T.O. It’s been a long time since he followed sports let alone football. His father, who Davis hasn’t seen in years, used to take a young Davis to Knicks games back in the 70s and 80s. The two of them used to hate the Boston Celtics because they would always beat the Knicks in the playoffs. "God damn Larry Bird, Dave," his father would say. He rarely called him Dave. "If he wasn’t around it would be a whole other story; a happy story." Davis doesn’t care about sports though, not anymore. He now cares about one thing: the fix. Until one thing does catch his attention. Someone he knows. Someone he hasn’t seen in years. Someone who has lived a quality life in athletics and used to always bring a smile to Davis’ face. That someone is dead. He was found in a motel room at around 10:02 am by a colleague who wanted to get breakfast, the paper says. The cause of death at this time is unknown, but an autopsy is likely to be held. This someone is- was Jewish and so will be buried within two days here in New York City. This dead man, this friend of Davis, this athlete was a professional wrestler. Performing on the independent circuit for over two years he was scheduled to perform in the large PTC tournament GTT5. He was a champion in multiple promotions, but was mainly known for his outstanding submission and technical skills as a cruiserweight wrestler. His persona was about as original as they come and while he didn’t spend much time in many federations he did make his mark on some and certainly on many people. He will be missed. Davis utters this person’s name in the darkness. "Ilan…" The newspaper says that the first RPW Cruiserweight Champion ‘The Jewserweight’ Ilan Levin is dead. Davis sits up quickly and yells into nothing but an old coffeehouse. He imagines all of New York hearing him and joining. This doesn’t happen so instead he rips the paper. He tears his blanket into pieces until his arms hurt. "Why?" he shouts. "Why? I need to know why?" He isn’t asking God. He isn’t asking anyone. He just needs to ask. And Davis runs out of the coffeehouse. Tears are starting to well in his eyes, but it’s too cold for tears and they do not fall. His cheeks are as dry as everyone else’s. He rushes into the street looking for someone, anyone. A man in a big puffy coat is walking down the sidewalk. Davis sees him and the two make eye contact, but the man breaks it quickly and begins to walk a little faster, a little farther away from the drug addict he sees coming towards him. Davis tackles this man in the big puffy coat right to the ground. Perhaps the big puffy coat lessens the fall and he isn’t hurt, but that probably isn’t true. "I need something from you!" Davis screams. The man is terribly scared. The coat is blue. Davis punches this man in the face three times. Hard. The punches are solid and trained. He goes through this man’s pockets and retrieves what he wants: coins. Davis gets up and leaves the man in the big puffy coat lying there on the sidewalk. The man in the big coat will call the police in twenty minutes and he will now feel a slight twinge of fear every time he steps onto the streets of Manhattan. Davis looks back at this man on the sidewalk. He wants to say something, but doesn’t speak a word. He just moves onward. Every second that passes he feels more sober; his eyes look stronger and more focused. He looks much more himself. The pay phone is close by and Davis runs to it. Seven digits later and he goes where he needs to go. "Hello," a lovely woman’s voice begins, "You’ve reached the offices Global Championship Wrestling Enterprises, but no one is in right now. If you could leave your name, your number, and your reason for calling we would greatly appreciate it and will get back to you as soon as we can. Thank you!" One, two, three, beep! "Caldy!" Davis sobs. "It’s… it’s Twizzy… I- I just read about Ilan. In the paper. Ilan’s dead! Caldy, Ilan’s dead!" He cries for five seconds and then starts again. "Caldy, I read that he was going to be in GTT5. I need to- Can I take his place? Ilan’s. I want to take Ilan’s place. Ilan’s dead. Ilan!" He bangs the receiver against the pay phone and then shouts into the darkness, into the sky, into the whole God Damn world. "He was my brother!"
