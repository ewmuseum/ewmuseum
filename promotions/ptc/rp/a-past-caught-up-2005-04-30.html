A Past Caught Up

30 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-30 “Is this the place?” It felt cold and warm at the same time. The mist was rising still and the dark alley corner wasn’t what I expected. It was empty at first sight but once I looked again, I saw a few odd looking people. One, rolled up in a blanket next to a trash can that looked to have been there for years. Holes in the metal were cars or other objects had come in to contact with it. It was a place that a twenty-two year old girl didn’t need to be. The smell stayed in my nose, making me hold it a few times. The feeling inside made me grab my stomach, almost feeling like something inside was trying to eat it’s way out. The image of Mark laying on the floor in my arms kept going through my head. I just couldn’t believe it. Walking along the brick wall of the building, trying to walk slow and out of the lights, I heard a noise behind me. “Hello?” Speaking out a little behind me. No answer at all. My heart began to beat a little faster. My pales poured with sweat, making me wipe them on my short shorts that I was wearing. I felt cold in them, though my chest wasn’t any better. Wearing a short sleeved shirt that showed the red strap bra that I was wearing underneath. Like a fool that I knew I was, I began to step forward towards the noise. Watching the pot holes that the street had in them. This was my first time being in a place like this. It was new and it was scary. Even then cold made me freaky inside, almost making em piss in my own pants. Getting closer to the noise, walking passed the cars and trucks that were parked next to the street, I could see a little light. Glowing down onto the street from a fence holder, I could see several things. A barrel that was setting up next to a pitch fork. My eyes locked on the pitch fork, almost wondering why and how it ever got in a place like this. I just wanted to get out of here. I felt so frighten that you could of said boo and I would of jumped. My heart still raced, like it was in the Daytona Five Hundred. The only problem is, I can’t seem to leave the place I was at. I got to the side walk again, right next to the light where the noise was heard. “Hello…”, I said once again. This time hearing a little voice coming out from behind me. “Silence.” The rough voice said. The voice sounded familiar. Almost a reflection of the voice on the phone. I began to turn around, wondering who the man that could of killed Mark looked like. His eyes were white, a little brown in the center, with the rest of his face covered in darkness. He looked like a giant. Not with his height, no but with his weight. Looking to be ten tons more then me. “Who’s there?” I responded with authority. “I SAID SILENCE!” His voice rattled my ears. Pain shot through them like a gun shot to my heart, echoing through to my eardrum. His eyes, smooth and glossy at first sight but the more I saw through them I could see the evil they held. My heart began to rush even faster. “Well hello Miss Smith,” he rattled towards me with his rough voice. “ I beat you’re wondering what is going on?” “Yes…”, unsure to anything right now I replied. “You see Miss Smith, I picked you out for a reason. A reason that you have no earthly idea about.” He said, standing a little was away from the lights on the street. Picked me out? What on earth could I have done to be picked out so Mark could be murdered in the same room as me? I was a young innocent girl with her life ahead of her. I swallowed my saliva, trying not to let out the rush of fear that I held inside. “Several years ago your uncle forgot to do something for us and now you will fulfill that promise.” His voice said, walking towards me in a slowly rustic walk. A promise. My uncle. What did he mean? Did my uncle do somethink he wasn’t supposed to do in the past or was it the other way around? His eyes and face came a little closer, reviling the red colored cheeks that he had. His lips were big, almost blowing them out there for the sight of my eyes. “You see Elia, you will fulfill that favor in a way that your uncle couldn’t. And if you don’t,” he stepped out from the light fully, reviling a face that I hadn’t seen in years. “You will never see your uncle again.” He was built strong. His arms ripping through the blue suit that he was wearing, almost looking like a comic figure in an all evil city. His grin was still there and my mind seemed to wonder, remembering the first time I ever saw him. ******************* November 10th, 1989 “Uncle,” I screamed out loud. Eight years old and the house was my playground. Running down the oak wood floor that lead to the doorway to my uncle’s little office, I saw him looking at an old trophy on his awards table. “There you are,” he said, turning around and grabbing me with his two arms and lifting me up to his chest. “I saw you fight the other night. Daddy said you are dangerous for me to watch though.” Lowering my bottom lip in disappointment. He gave a small smile, trying to make me feel secure at what he feels is right and wrong. He turned around to the trophy one more time, looking and staring at it harshly with his brown eyes. “What trophy is that Uncle?” I said with my little voice. “That’s my trophy for winning the IWW Heavyweight Title Tourney. I beat the amazing Ryan Williams in the finals.” I was to young to even think about who that was or what a Heavyweight Title was. Grabbing the title with my tinny hand, my uncle pulled it away, making me almost knock over a picture of him. “Uncle, who is that with you there?” Looking at the picture. The man in it had short hair. Deep dark coloring to his eyes and a evil body language mad my uncle’s eyes widen up to a size I had never seen. He gazed in the picture with his eyes while I looked over to it myself. “That’s someone…”, he said, not finishing at all. *********************** My memory finished, letting me not only remember who this was but where I saw him. I felt so confused but he was the man in the picture. Who was he I never knew but he and my uncle knew one another some how. I took a step back, trying to get a little more distance as he fully emerged from the darkness. “I know you now,” turning an innocent face that I had to an angry face that could kill. “Do you really?” He asked, showing every line in his face and body in the light. He finally got to me, stopping right in front of me with his bad breath. He wasn’t almost the same as the picture showed him expect for a few changes. His size seem to be different. Humping down a few inches with his back, it almost seemed that he had a screwed up disc or something in it. A scar held the shape on his forehead, seeming to be old and ancient looking. “Yes I do. You’re the man that was standing beside my uncle in a picture several years ago in a wrestling ring.” My strong words brought out. “But what does this have to do with me?” “It has everything to do with you because if I’m not mistaken, you call your uncle father right?” He replied, taking my shoulder with his left hand and rubbing it slightly. “Yes he is like a father to me.” Unsure still of the reasoning. He stopped with my reply, pausing like the wind that was once blowing all around us in this cold dark night. My heart paused as well for a few moments, but like all things, it never stayed that way. The mystery man brought his fingers together and snapped them swiftly, making my heart rattle once again, and rush forward once more. I turned around, right to a door that was on the other side of the light. It was rusted out, almost like it hadn’t been opened in years. Then a sound that hurt my ears more then anything, like fingernails scraping on a choke board, the door came open. My body began to sweat more, wondering what was going to revel out of it. Out walked a tall, bulked up man, with arms that were the size of my entire body. His hand pushed a wheel chair, though I wasn’t interested in it only but what it was in it. “UNCLE!” Nothing shocked me more this night then the sight of my uncle. Tied up with rope with his hands on the back of the chair. I wanted to run to him but my feet refused. Knowing something I didn’t know, I turned to the man with an angry voice, ready to rip his head off. “LET HIM GO NOW PLEASE!” Begging with my entire heart for him to agree. “Now, now, now Miss Elia, why would I do that? Though I have a better idea.” He said, again stopping right in front of me. “You fight Drake Coleman this round right?” Drake Coleman? What did this have to do with the Cruiserweight Title Two Tournament? I closed my eyes, trying to think of all this as a dream but my mind didn’t let me. It wasn;t a dream and for once, my Uncle relied on me instead of it being the other way around. “Whatever it is mister, let my uncle go and I’ll do whatever you want me to do.” Giving up my life and will for a man that has gave me everything. He shook his head. Bringing his index finger up to his lips to single for silence. The cold air had come back, blowing right into my face, making me turn towards the doorway that my uncle was at before. “LOOK AT ME!” He said, grabbing my jaw with a powerful grip. All I could to was surrender to his demands. I looked at him, everything wanting me to be able to be strong but knowing I was a weak little girl inside. “You have two choice you can make and they are rather simple. You see, fifteen years ago, your Uncle made me lose some money and you are going to help me get it back.” letting his hand be placed on my shoulder again as he responded. “It’s rather simple little girl.” The night lit up, blasting the sky with a short of lighting, almost like fourth of July is with fireworks. I kept looking at him, frustration in my mind and anger in my soul, almost a rage of passion toward what he has put me through. “We need you to do one thing for us and one thing only and that is win. If you win then you get your Uncle back. But if you lose, then you can figure out what happens to him right?” He said, taking a step back from my face. All I had to do was win? It sounded so easy but yet at the same time it sounded so hard. I just couldn’t understand what my Uncle could of done to this man that made all this happy. This was supposed to be the happiest time of my life yet I felt so helpless. But as fast as I lowered my head and came back up, was the time it took him to vanish. I looked around, twisting and turning in the middle of the street looking for him. Nothing was there. Where could he of gone? I took a long breath, almost releaved in a way but at the same time, my soul was taken. I dropped to my knees, splashing in the water that was under me. The rain was starting to come down, crashing across my torn down body like knifes. My heart was broken and my life was turned upside down. I felt like crying but yet I couldn’t. I looked upwards, knowing that I was for once in my life, counted on to be good and it was someone other then myself.
