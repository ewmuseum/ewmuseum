Hitching a Ride

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 A nice red convertible car came right beside me and stopped. The window start to roll down. There was a really hot girl in the front seat with some sunglasses on. She looked to be about 19 years old. She took her sunglasses off and I smiled at her. She smiled back. She then motioned me to come inside of the car. I opened the car, and hopped into the front seat. She rolled the window back up and turned around, in the way of Chicago. She started going as I sat there. "So, what's your name cutie?" she asked me. "Jackson. Jackson Kable." "From EPW?" "If your talking about Evolution Pro Wrestling, then yes, I am him." I said. "Oh my god. Your my favourite. Your winning streak is just so awesome. I really hope your can get past round 1 for PTC Survival with EPW, and maybe win the whole thing." "Yeah, I hope we do to." We had already entered Chicago and were going down a street. This was a really nice girl that let me come into her car so she could take me to my house. She turned a right and then a left. She stopped and parked right in front of a Wal-Mart. She unbuckled her seat belt and then opened the door to get out of her car. I unbuckled my seat belt to and then got out of the car. She ocked the doors, and both of us walked into Wal-Mart. "So, why are we in here?" I asked her. "I need to get a few things." "By the way, what is your name?" "Kristen. Some of my friends call me Kristy for short, but I don't really like that." "Ok, well lets meet right here in like 30 minutes. I need to get a few things to." I walked away from her and went for the toys section. Yes, I know what some of you are thinking. Toys are for kids, but I have to check if there was something that I want that was there. I got into the toys section, and went straight for the action figures. I looked at the whole wall of action figures. Suddenly, there were boxes for action figurs for EPW. I looked if they had me yet. They did, they also had Ace, Chapel, and Ricky Summers. I grabbed mine off the wall. It looked exactly like me, and it also came with a chair as a weapon. I took my action figure and started walking towards the electronics. I started looking at the new realeases for the cds. I grabbed the new Papa Roach cd, and the Beastie Boys cd. I walked towards the aisle that I had to pay for the cds. I put the cds down on a little scanner so the man could scan them. He put them in a bag, and then put the receit. I took the bag, and started walking towards the aisle so I could pay for my action figure. I put the box of it onto the scanner. The lady looked at the figure in the box, and then looked at me. "You look awfully familiar to this action figure." "Maybe because this is me." "Your Jackson Kable?" "Of course I am." The lady scanned the box and then put it into a bigger bag. I took it and went back to the spot where I said me and Kristy could meet. I waited there for about 10 minutes and finally came with 3 little bags in her hands. She smiled at me and then looked at the only 2 bags I had in my hands. We started walking out of Wal-Mart and back to where she had parked. We got into the car, and she started to drive. "What did you get?" she asked me. "I got a little wrestling action figure and two cds." "Aren't you a little old for action figures?" "I got my action figure from EPW." "Oh. So, where do you live, so I can drop you off?." "Columbia street." She turned and started going towards Columbia street. She went down Columbia street and stopped in the middle of the street, but at the sidewalk. I unbuckled my seat belt, and got out of the car. Right before she left, I started talking. "Hey, I want to thank you for the ride. Maybe, do you think, you would want to go for spper with me tonight?" "Sure." "Ok, can I pick you up at 7:00?" "Ok. See you later." She drove away, as I smiled really big. I started wlaking down the sidewalk towards my house. She parked like 5 houses away from mine. I walked up the walkway going to my door, and then went into my house. I shut the door behind me, and then chucked the stuff I bought at Wal-Mart onto the couch that was pretty much beside the door. I sat down on a chair. *Scene Fades*
