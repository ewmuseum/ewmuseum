PTC — Roleplay

Jurgen Raymann vs Elia Smith vs Emilio Dervante

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, this is Jurgens first RP for this match. I've noticed that I use a different style than most of the PTCers around here, but that won't be a problem... I hope so. European Championship Football V.I.P. The camera fades in. Wallace: Hello, folks. I guess you know what time it is. Yes, that’s right. Another action packed interview with Jurgen Raymann. Well…. Action packed. It will certainly be unusual. Anyway, I’m here in Portugal, for the European Football Championship and to interview Jurgen of course. Wallace knocks on the door. A butler opens the door. Jurgen: Who is it? James: It’s Wallace Coleman from the WWA, sir. Jurgen: Ah good, let him in! Wallace looks up at James with a weird look on his face…but enters the Skybox nonetheless. Wallace: Hi Jurgen, why the butler? Jurgen: To get me my drinks, of course, I’m still recuperating from what Jack did to me, but we’ll get to that! Have a seat! Wallace sits down. Wallace: What are you doing here, Jurgen? The Netherlands don’t have to play tonight, do they? Jurgen: No! They’re just practising. I come here some times to just clear my mind and relax and enjoy the benefits of a butler. James, a coke and some Mars bars for Wallace here. Wallace: Do you have doobies as well? Jurgen: Not funny. James delivers the goods. Wallace: Thanks. Anyway, shall we talk about last Fiesta? Jurgen: We’ll get to that. First things first, Wallace. Anna! A masseuse comes in. Jurgen: The feet need some work, baby. Rhonda sits down and starts massaging Jurgen’s feet. Jurgen: Ah, that’s better! Wallace’s jaw is still dropping. Jurgen: What? Hey, Mr. Darkness has a Butler working for him as well. Only he gets paid nothing and just walks around. Might as well do something useful with him, but we’ll get to that. Let’s get back to Fiesta. Wallace: Yes. We were witnessing what was terribly awfull for you. Jack DDTed you of the stage. Jurgen: Yeah, I hate him. Wallace: Also at the Fiesta, we saw a shocking turn of events! Jurgen: Yeah, I know! I lost! Now get over it! I can’t win all the time! Wallace: I meant that Dave and Ian signed their match. Jurgen: Oh that! Hey, there’s that Ian Tense guy again, he must be really something! Yeah, well, it wasn’t a surprise that he signed the match. We all know that Dave Harley steals all the Happy Meals. But Ian is making a big mistake. You see, I know what he’s thinking. He’s thinking, if I fight that Happy Meal Thief, that’ll increase my chances for a shot at the World Title, but in that he is mistaken. In return, he loses in a stretcher match against the Monkey Man. Wallace: Some harsh words, but speaking of that match! You’re in a match as well! You will face off with Elia Smith and Emilio Dervante in a crossfed match! Jurgen: Ah yes, a PTC match. Anybody knows what that is? Wallace mouth drops down of Jurgen’s Stupidity. Jurgen: What? Wallace: Prime Time Central is the interfed. WWA is part of it! Jurgen: I knew that. Wallace: Sure you did. Jurgen: Indeed, me and my brain have never heard about the PTC, but I still know what is was. Not bad for a former stoned little f*ck! Wallace: But you didn’t knew it! Jurgen: Yeah, Wallace. I know, I knew that I know that I knew what PTC is, so you don’t need to tell me okay? Wallace: ….? Jurgen: Hey! I know I’m Dutch and English is not my Primary Language, but I’m trying like to hell to pronounce the words right! So don’t be such an ass when I say something false! Wallace mouth falls open again. Wallace: That’s not what I meant. Jurgen: Then why did you start about it? Anyway, well, you see, Wallace. If you look at tonight’s match. You will obviously see the winner. Portugal versus Greece. Coach: Of course, I am a loyal Greece Fan! Jurgen: My condolences, but we’ll get to that. Wallace: Huh? Jurgen: Anyhoo, you could see Portugal as the favourite to win that match because of their longtime experience. And Greece is the underdog, but performing nicely this year. In other words, Elia or Emillio is Portugal and I’m Greece. Now, because of their experience they might gain the upper hand in the early going of the match, as Portugal is going to do, but than somewhere during the match, Greece, or me, will get one back and the tide starts to turn my way. And in the end, quite embarrassingly I might add, Greece will draw level, but it shall not stop there with me! Wallace: No? Jurgen: Hell no! I will go one step further and beat the Elia and Emillio to win my first PTC match! See, I’m not dumb! Wallace: Eh…… Jurgen: Hey! Wallace: What? Jurgen: If your not gonna interview me then why the hell are you here? Wallace; Uhm… Jurgen: I know! You’re here because you can watch the EC for free, aren’t you? You sneaky little bastard! Wallace: Busted…… Jurgen: Busted? What the hell are you talking about? Wallace: You accussed me of sneakyness, so I’m busted. Jurgen: I didn’t bust you! I just told you that you were a sneaky little bastard! Wallace: Why that? Jurgen: Why what? Wallace: but… Jurgen: But what? What are you saying to me? I can’t speak Kazilionisian you know? Wallace: Kazilionisian? Jurgen: Yeah, the language of dumb, sneaky and annoying little bastards! Wallace: Hey! You’re not that smart either! Jurgen: Take that back! I’m handicapped! How dare you! Insulting a handycapped man, how desperate can you be! Wallace: But… Jurgen: Shut up! No buts! Wallace: But… Jurgen: But what? Wallace: The interview? Jurgen: Ow yeah, I forgot. Ask away then. Wallace: What do you think about the fact your facing a woman? Jurgen(with a scottish accent): If a woman wants a slap, damnit I’ll give it to her. But before I forget. Top of the mornin’ to ya Wallace, I’m going to kiss the blarney stone, then I’m going to find me pot ‘o gold. While I sing cockels and mockels, a live, a live oh. Wallace: You know that Irish say that, not the Scots? Jurgen: You didn’t say that. Tell me, you didn’t just say that! Wallace: I’ve heard that…….. *gets interrupted* Jurgen: It doesn’t matter what you’ve heard! The millions…… And Millions of Raymann fans over the world now that Jurgen will lay the Smackdown on Elia and Emilio! And that’s the bottom line, because Jurgen Raymann said so! Wallace: That sounds familiar, really it does. But hell, you imitate everything and everyone, so that isn’t a surprise. Jurgen: I guess so. Wallace: Well, I’m out of questions. So I’m going to leave. Jurgen: Doesn’t matter. I’m glad you’re going, the Simpsons will be on TV soon. Wallace: Then I’ll go then, see you again soon. Jurgen: See ya. Wallace leaves, as the camera fades out.
