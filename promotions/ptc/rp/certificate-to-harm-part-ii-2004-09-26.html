Certificate To Harm - Part II

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 A DARK ALLEY SOMEWHERE A DAY OR TWO AFTER THE EVENTS OF PART I LATE AT NIGHT OR EARLY IN THE MORNING DEPENDING ON YOUR VIEW O'CLOCK There was a slight chill in the night air. Billows of steam snaked their way upward through a sewer grate, located in the middle of a dank alley set between a wall of faded yellow limestone and another wall of red brick. A white street light from the end of the alley cast a long shadow across the dark cobblestone on the ground. A couple of greasy little rats scurried to and fro around a green dumpster and a couple of metal trash cans, their squeaks echoing off the walls. It wasn't exactly the best place Jimmy Bong had in mind for a meeting spot. "Ugh, this place smells terrible," Bong remarked, wrinkling his nose in disgust. He stood at the entrance to the alleyway alongside his human shield, Majorly Shafted (who miraculously showed no signs of having taken a missile blast to the chest as he did previously). Yes, Bong was wearing a tuxedo like he always did. At least Shafted demonstrated some variety in his wardrobe -- he was clad in a brown leather jacket, blue jeans, white t-shirt, and a pair of Doc Martens. "It may smell terrible, but it's inconspicuous," said Shafted. "What time do you have, Double-Oh-Nothing?" Bong looked at his watch. "It's just a minute or two past Late At Night Or Early In The Morning Depending On Your View O'Clock. We're a shade early for the meeting, so our courier won't be showing up for another ten minutes or so." "That's some watch," Shafted said to himself quietly. The two OHMCS operatives (well, techncially speaking, one OHMCS operative and one piece of OHMCS equipment) quietly walked into the alley, carefully glancing around to make sure they weren't being followed. After all, if Luna Morbid really had been hired by Garfeld to kill Bong, they very well couldn't take any chances. Bong sighed and leaned back against the red brick wall. As soon as the OHMCS courier arrived, he thought, he would have the means to protect himself. Shafted reached into his jeans pocket and procured a pack of Camels and a cheap orange Bic lighter. He opened up the pack, and frowned as he looked into it and saw it was empty. "Bloody hell," he said, dejected. "I really could use a smoke right now." Bong's gaze slowly tracked along the ground. He stopped, as he spotted a couple of unused cigarettes lying in front of the dumpster. "Er, well," he started, "I suppose you could have one of those on the ground over there, seeing how they haven't been consumed. Although I don't know if you're desperate enough to..." Before Bong could finish, Shafted darted over to the dumpster and plucked one of the cigarettes off the ground, and immediately put it into his mouth without hesitation. He light it up, and took a long drag, letting out a deep sigh as he exhaled a puff of smoke that crept up into the air. "I guess you are then," said Bong, scratching his head. Shafted smiled. "You'll have to forgive my behavior, but I'm rather attached to my cigarettes. I'm not allowed to smoke in U's laboratory of course, so it's a rough going for me on some days. Nasty habit, I know, but that's addiction for you." Bong nodded slowly, as Shafted walked over and joined him now in leaning back against the wall, inhaling deeply once again. "Well," said Shafted, peering through the sewer steam up towards the clear, starry sky, "you have quite a task, don't you Double-Oh-Nothing? Save the world from Garfeld, the world's most fiendish fiend." "That's right," said Bong gravely. He frowned a bit as he thought about the gravity of the job at hand. Shafted turned his head to look at the secret agent and continued speaking. "I hear that Garfeld is your archenemy," he said. "Forgive me for saying so, but I find that rather odd. You have a reputation for never doing anything right..." "And rightly so, as I really haven't done anything right," Bong interjected. "Er, yes," Shafted continued. "At any rate, I was curious. You've never succesfully completed a mission during your entire tenure in OHMCS, so I would imagine that you don't really have any enemies at all. After all, if I were a terrorist, I certainly wouldn't mind having a bungling dolt working for British intelligence." Bong smiled. "Why, that's the nicest thing anyone has ever said to me!" he said genuinely. "You're... welcome, I guess," Shafted said, arching an eyebrow at Bong strangely. "But anyway, my point is that I just don't see how someone like you would make enemies with anybody, let alone Garfeld. How in the world did you become archenemies with him?" Bong sighed, and stared blankly at the ground. "Well," he said after a few moments, "it wasn't always that way. We used to be good friends, dating way back to when we were in preparatory school. Oh, we'd scamper about the streets of London, looking for any opportunity to cause mischief. We had some great times... throwing rocks at the Buckingham Palace guards... trying to break out of our jail cell when they eventually took us in... deciding what story to tell our parents when they came to pick us up... jolly good times. He had a younger sister, named Cassandra. My goodness, she was the most beautiful creature I'd ever seen. Shimmering brown locks that went down to her waist. Dazzling blue eyes, like rubies..." "Rubies are red, Bong," said Shafted. "Oh. Blast, what's blue then?" asked Bong. "Sapphires." "Right, so she had dazzling blue eyes like sapphires, silky smooth skin... I tell you Shafted, she was stunning. I remember so clearly... it was the year before Garfeld and I would be off to University, and I had asked Cassandra to be my date at the school formal. She agreed, and soon the magical night came. We were having a splendid time, until... until..." Bong stopped. "Until what?" asked Shafted. "Until... I accidentally spilled a cup of water on her," Bong said quietly, as if it pained him to relive the event with his words. "It was her favorite dress, and quite expensive too. She became furious with me, and ran crying to her brother, who swore revenge." Shafted seemed astounded by this. "But... she did know that water just dries out and doesn't really cause any harm to fabric, didn't she?" Bong's eyes widened with surprise. "Are you serious!? I wish I'd known that so I could have told her! Oh, the pain that might have been avoided..." Shafted scratched his head. "So... bascially what you're saying is, you spilled water on Garfeld's sister, so now he's your archenemy because of that?" Bong nodded. "That's a stupid reason for anyone to become someone's archenemy," Shafted said bluntly. Bong smiled and shrugged. "What can I say? I'm a stupid man!" "Touché," muttered Shafted, looking away. "Life wasn't the same for any of us after that night," Bong lamented. "It's quite strange the way things turned out. I was planning to enroll in the London Academy of Evil and study to become an evil madman bent on conquering the world. Garfeld was going to enlist with the military and eventually become an agent with British intelligence. But Garfeld was so disillusioned by what happened, that he changed his mind and went to L.A.E. I couldn't bear to be anywhere near him, so I ended up joining the military instead. So here we are, I'm a secret agent, and Garfeld is the world's most fiendish fiend. Amazing how the world works, doesn't it?" Bong looked at Shafted, who wasn't answering. Shafted was instead devoting his attention to staring at a hobo that was seated against a wall down the alley, apparently sleeping. "Who's that?" Bong whispered. "I don't know," Shafted whispered back. "I didn't notice him when we arrived, but it seems like he's been there for a while... wait! The courier! That's it!" "Of course!" Bong exclaimed quietly. "Leave it to Beaver... I mean, leave it to me! I'll take care of this." Shafted shook his head at Bong, as he puffed on his cigarette once more. Bong strolled over to the man sleeping on the ground, who looked quite dirty and was dressed only in tatters and rags. There was a wrinkly brown paper bag on the ground next to him. Bong leaned down in front of him and gently shook his shoulder. The hobo snorted a bit, then woke up and looked right at Bong. His eyes widened with surprise, and he jumped up to his feet. "¡Perdón, señor!" he exclaimed. Bong looked at the spanish-speaking hobo oddly, then smiled and spoke very slowly and loudly. "Uh... yes... could you please tell me where the courier is?" The man looked at Bong strangely. "Soy el mensajero de Londres." "Soy? I don't eat soy, and I don't see what it has to do with MENSA or doing laundry," replied Bong. "Now please, can you tell me where the courier from OHMCS is?" Shafted covered his face with his right hand. "Bong, that man is the courier!" he shouted. "Si," said the man. "Have you lost it, Shafted?" asked Bong. "This man is talking about laundry and soy!" "He's speaking Spanish! He just said, 'I am the messenger from London' in Spanish!" "Oh," said Bong, turning back to the hobo. "Well, I guess things are alright then." He held out his hand. "My name is Bong. Jimmy Bo... why the bloody hell do I always forget to say my first name first? Oh well. Jimmy Bong." "Mucho gusto," replied the spanish-speaking courier from London. "Nice to meet you, Mr. Gusto!" said Bong cheerfully. Shafted sighed loudly and shook his head. He dropped his cigarette to the ground and rubbed out the flame with the ball of his foot. "Perhaps you'd better let me take over, Double-Oh-Nothing. I can speak spanish quite well." "A human shield and a translator!" said Bong. "U is a scientific genius!" Bong stepped aside as Shafted came in to converse with the courier, who emptied out the contents of his paper bag. There was a minature tranquilizer pistol, a pack of cigarettes, a roll of athletic tape, and a shiny new pair of black shoes. Bong looked at the equipment curiously, as Shafted and the courier continued talking. "Well, according to our friend here," said Shafted, motioning to each item in turn, "these items are some of the latest, directly from OHMCS headquarters in London. This," he held up the tranquilizer pistol, "is the newest model in our line of sedation weaponry. This pistol is easy to conceal in your pants or coat pocket." Shafted handed the pistol to Bong, who looked rather dejected. "I wish I could have a pistol with bullets for once," he sighed. "Stupid certificate to harm only." "This roll of tape here," Shafted held up the athletic tape now, "is actually a low-grade form of plastique explosive. You have to be careful with it, because it's engineered to explode upon heavy impact. It doesn't do much structural damage, so you can blow in a door with it or anything... but you can, for example, wrap it around your knuckles. When you punch your opponent in the face, it combusts and increases the strength of the blow exponentially! Just be careful not to use the entire roll at once, otherwise you're liable to blow someone's head off." "Use it all at once, blow someone's head off, got it," Bong repeated... well, sort-of repeated. Shafted yawned a bit. "Oh my excuse me for that. Anyway, where was I? Ah... these shoes should come in quite handy. They're outfitted with small rocket boosters in the heels, and they should give you an extra boost in case you need to perform some sort of high-flying moves in the ring or something. I think the courier said they were fueled by your own foot odor, but we probably lost something in the translation there." "If that's true though, I'll have an unlimited supply of fuel!" said Bong. "Right," said Shafted. "At any rate, all you have to do is jump, and the shoes kick in for a split-second while you're in the air. That brings me to these," he held up the pack of cigarettes now. "But I don't smoke," Bong started. "That doesn't matter," Shafted explained. "These are a special brand of cigarette... if somebody smokes one of these, they'll be knocked unconscious for at least an hour! Here, take them." Bong took the pack of cigarettes from Shafted's hand, and looked inside. "Hang on," he said. "There's a couple of cigarettes missing from here!" He pulled one cigarette from the pack and examined it. It looked oddly familiar... "I say, Shafted," Bong said. "This looks remarkably similar to the cigarette that you smoked just a few minutes ago." Bong looked up, and saw that Shafted was now passed out cold on the ground. "And that would be why," he said. "Well, I guess I'll just wait around for about an hour or so until he comes around." "I'm afraid you won't be around long enough for that to happen, Señor Bong," said the man dressed as a spanish hobo. "Come again?" asked Bong. The man smiled a wretched smile, as he reached into his tattered coat and pulled out a fully loaded Walther PPK, and pointed it directly at Bong. "What the bloody hell is going on here!?" Bong gasped. "Your idiocy truly is legendary, Señor Bong," said the man. "I knew you were thick-headed, but I find it hard to imagine that the guise of an OHMCS courier who could only speak spanish didn't immediately tip you off that something was wrong." "How's that?" "People from London typically speak English." "Oh, right, that thing," said Bong. "I'll have to remember that next time. I'm not very observant, you see." "I could tell," said the man, not surprised. "It's a lesson learned too late, I'm afraid. Mr. Garfeld will be most pleased when I report back to him and inform him that I killed his archenemy, the infamous Jimmy Bong, Agent Double-Oh-Nothing!" "Garfeld!? Why, you diabolical cad! You work for him! You're one of his evil henchmen!" Bong exclaimed in horror. "Well done, Mr. Bong," the henchman quipped sarcastically. "It only took you until after I revealed that fact to you to figure it out. Bong started to slowly back down the alley, his mind racing. Then he remembered... the tranquilizer gun! It was in his coat pocket! If only he could draw it fast enough... he would need some sort of brilliant diversion. He pointed to a spot behind the henchman. "Look out behind you!" he yelled. "Huh?" The man whipped around, buying Bong enough time to draw his pistol... aim... pull the trigger... ...and all that shot out was a suction cup dart! "WHAT!?" Bong shouted. "You've got to be joking!" The henchman cackled. "You stupid fool! Do you really think I would put actual weapons in your hands? I assure you, the real courier has already been... 'disposed' of, and I destroyed all of the equipment he brought along with him. The so-called 'equipment' you have now is completely fake..." "Oh yeah? Well take THIS!" said Bong, hurling the roll of athletic tape towards his soon-to-be killer. The roll bounced off of the man's face and fell to the ground, doing absolutely nothing. "I just got done telling you that your equipment is completely fake!" he barked, irritated. "Weren't you listening!?" "Huh? What?" asked Bong. "I'm sorry, I wasn't paying attention. Anyway, where the bloody hell is the real courier?" "It makes no difference, now does it?" the henchman sneered. "Getting rid of him was a simple task, to be honest. It seems the courier is a smoker like your friend lying on the ground there, so I was easily able to get him out of the way with one of those special cigarettes, just as I was able to temporarily put your precious 'human shield' out of order. But anyway, I've had quite enough talk for one evening. This is the end of the line, Señor Bong. Good night." "Oh, good night then," Bong said with a smile. "Shall we have tea tomorrow afternoon?" "No Señor Bong, I'm afraid you won't be able to make it... you have a permanent appointment at the cemetary. Adios, amigo." The man raised his gun and clicked off the safety, his finger on the trigger. "Curses," muttered Bong. "I hate this part." Bong closed his eyes. Then, there was a loud BANG! that rang into the night. A man fell to the ground. But it wasn't Jimmy Bong. He opened his eyes, and saw that Garfeld's evil henchman was now slumped on the ground, grasping his arm and moaning in pain. Bong looked around the alley frantically, wondering who or where his savior was. Then, his eye caught something strange about the dumpster... there was a smoking gun barrel portruding from the side! "Wow, what are the odds?" thought Bong aloud. The lid of the dumpster swung open. A man wearing a World War II flying helmet, goggles, and scarf poked his head out. "Direct hit!" he declared. "Served him right, the bloody bastard." "Who are you?" Bong asked the man. "Ah, Agent Bong!" said the man cheerfully. "Dreadfully sorry about my tardiness, but I seem to have been drugged by that spanish-speaking fellow that tried to shoot you just now. I arrived here just a little over an hour ago, just sitting around and minding my own business. I wanted a smoke, but unfortunately I didn't have any of my cigarettes with me. Then, out of nowhere came this stranger, who was more than happy to offer me one of his. The next thing I know, I'm feeling incredibly sleepy, and the last thing I remember is being dragged over to this dumpster and thrown in!" "So you were 'disposed' of," said Bong thoughtfully. "In a very literal sense, yes!" the man guffawed. "But anyway, it was quite lucky that he threw me into this particular dumpster, because it just happens to be a newly-developed camoflauged urban operations vehicle. It's outfitted with all sorts of equipment, including that gun that just spared your life a moment ago, Double-Oh-Nothing!" "How splendid!" said Bong with glee. "Indeed, although I'm afraid that nasty fellow destroyed all of the equipment I brought along for you," the man sighed. "It seems as if you'll have to fight Morbid on your own." "But," Bong stammered, "but she's trying to kill me! How the bloody hell am I supposed to defend myself?" "Oh, I don't know," mused the man. "You could just wrestle, I suppose? After all, you did defeat the UWF World Champion." "I don't even know how I did that!" said Bong. "Look, can't I just have that dumpster tank instead, with all those weapons and whatnot, so I can just blow Morbid to kingdom come?" "Come now, Bong, I can't do that! You're not licensed to kill! You're certified to harm!" "Stupid certificate," Bong pouted. And so, after going through all of that, Bong was nowhere closer to being able to defend himself against Luna Morbid as he had been to begin with. Isn't in funny how after taking a long, roundabout road, you end up where you started once again? Makes you wonder what the hell this entire story was even written for.
