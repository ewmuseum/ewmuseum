Date: 2005-11-29 July 17th, 2003 Charismatic, Up-and-Coming Boxer Missing, Whereabouts Still Unknown By Justino Smalls White Brooklyn, New York, July 17 – The stakes could not have been higher for yesterday’s scheduled bout between the undefeated middleweight champion of the world, Paul ‘KO’ Sanders and his challenger Shaun ‘The Steel’ Styles. It was truly a tale that could not have been better written. Since early 2001, Paul Sanders has captured the middleweight championship and has defended it on a regularly basis, unifying titles all around the world. He held the boxing competition at his grasp until a training session turned the scene upside down. Shaun Styles, found in an almost clandestine gym in Bed-Stuy (Bedford-Stuyvesant for those of you not down with Brooklyn lingo), was hired as Sanders’ sparring partner to prepare for his next match. The usual media frenzy accompanied this training session but the frenzy soon would turn into a riot as Shaun Styles knocked Paul Sanders down. The champion’s reaction to the swift right hook that left him on the canvas was enough to intimidate the most hardened of characters. It took the entire professional team of trainers to break up the chaos that ensued out of the middleweight champion who was reported ripping off his gloves and going after the considerably smaller opponent. After the ‘shot heard ‘round the world’ Shaun Styles was quickly signed by boxing agents for a few exhibition bouts in which he proved to be deadly. Quickly, he rose up to the top of the boxing ladder where Paul Sanders would jump at this seemingly sweet opportunity. A contract was written up for the championship bout in a matter of hours. Well, that brings us to today, the day after the scheduled fight, but there’s only one problem. Shaun Styles never showed up for his chance to become the undisputed middleweight champion. He was last spotted talking to his long-time friend, Jade Taylor, who was also positioned as his manager for this newfound fame. That was five days before his championship shot. No one has seen or heard from him since. After researching this mysterious character’s past it was uncovered that Shaun Styles was formerly an amateur boxer but was exiled from the sport after revealing several mafia ties. In particular, he had a notorious reputation as a friend of the mob boss Don Laproni and has been tied to fixing several of his past fights. Where is he now? No one knows for sure…but for one reason or another it has been reported that Don Laproni has put out a contract for his head. Shaun Styles stuck a thumbtack on the top of this newspaper clipping and tagged it to his peeling wall. He glanced at it for what felt like hours, looking it over with his baby blue ices that have turned an ice cold color over the past years. It's All Coming Back
