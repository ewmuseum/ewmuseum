Lights Out

11 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-11 Her name was Dee Dee Rodriguez, and to a lot of people, she was the bee’s knees. Only 18 years of age, and she’s already been blessed with fame and fortune both here and in Japan. In America her fame’s risen through the wrestling world as DDR, the high energy girl next door that can fly like the wind and is faster than speeding bullets. In Japan, however, he fame comes not from wrestling, but from her mastery of the Bemani craze Dance Dance Revolution. It was imprinted into her at birth that she would become a living legend in the popular arcade game, and from her excellence she’s received profitable endorsement deals, modeling work, and TV and printed exposure. She was quite possibly bigger in Japan than she was in America, or anywhere else in the world for her wrestling ability. But despite the zero-filled paychecks and the adoring fans around the world, when she looked at herself in the mirror, she was still little Dee Dee, the middle child in the massive Rodriguez household. She woke up the same as she did before she became world renown, still brushes her teeth the same way, and still plays DDR at the local arcade every weekend or so with her friends. There was no time for the money to go to her head because she used it for her family, buying a new house so their large family could have their own space and privacy. Her fans never made her ego grow out of proportion, because she now had hundreds of friends all over the world that if she saw one on the street, she’d greet them with a smile and a hug. She was a class act, no doubt about that. So when the brown haired beauty found Xian Phillipse standing on her doorstep in the pouring rain at near midnight, she could tell that something was seriously wrong with him. "Oh my god," her hand went to her mouth as concern swept over her face. "What happened?" XP stood there staring at her with empty eyes, his face a sunken wreck of sorrow. His blonde hair matted around his forehead, beads bouncing off his leather jacket. "I saw Angel." Her heart jumped so hard that her entire body jerked back. With eyes wide her jaw dropped in shock. "No way… Angel?? But that’s impossible!" "Tell me something I don’t know." The Puerto Rican/Filipino girl stepped to the side, letting Xian slip through into the house. He stood there as she closed the door, looking back to see her leaning against the door with her arms folded over her pink tank top. "You think I’m crazy… but I’m not." "I never said that. I know you good enough to know you don’t joke around when it comes to Angel." Seriousness was the one frame of mind that XP hardly got into. Life was too short to be serious all the time, but when he became serious and focused, he was a scary person to be around. He didn’t think of anything but the topic at hand, and he wouldn’t let anything or anyone distract him or get in his way. The mechanized mannerisms that came over him was like another person possessing him. DDR’s gaze shifted downward to her rainbow socked toes, uncomfortable to look him in the eye. XP knew how she felt, and turned around to take off his jacket. "Let me get you something from Che’s room to wear. He’s out at his girlfriend’s for the night, so he won’t mind." She pulled up her baggy black sweats before bounding up the stairs to her brother’s bedroom. Xian stood there alone, hearing music playing deeper into the house, while upstairs someone was watching TV. Another person was on the phone, chatting in Spanish. Light poured into the dark hallway where XP stood, an older woman’s voice coming from the kitchen speaking in Tagalog. There were people all around him in that house, but for the moment, he felt completely alone. He hadn’t felt like that in years… since the last time he had seen Angel. DDR came back downstairs with some gray sweats and a white sleeveless tee. "Here, you can change in my room. I’ll go make you some coffee." She rushed into the kitchen, Xian watching her with a dead gaze. Then as she disappeared around the corner, he sighed, walking towards the stairwell to his left and heading downstairs. He had already dripped on the plush red carpet enough; DDR’s parents wouldn’t like their carpet getting any more soggy. Love Lost/Lust Laden It was 2 weeks since the last time he had seen Angel. He had not been to school since that Saturday when he rode with her home on the subway. Her brother was supposed to pick her up at the platform and walk her the rest of the way, but from what XP was told by the teachers, she never left that platform. "Her brother was involved in some shady dealings… drugs and the like. He had crossed the wrong people and they thought it’d be fair if they went to give him a warning. They came as her brother showed up at the station, and after some heated words, they pulled guns on each other. Angel… was caught in the crossfire. She took two shots to the chest and one in the thigh, one of the chest shots piercing her heart and killing her. I’m so sorry, Xian." He had tried to contact the family afterwards, but they seemed to want nothing to do with him. They wouldn’t answer his calls, and when someone did pick up, they’d pretend no one by that name ever lived there. XP knew that Angel’s family didn’t like him, but to allow their hatred to carry on this far even after their only daughter had been gunned down in an altercation of her brother’s doing… it drove XP to the pinnacle of desperation. So there he stood, in the last place he had seen Angel… Harrison and 22nd station. He was at the edge of the platform, his boots mere inches from the red safety line. There were only a few people around, and they were either waiting for trains or leaving the station. The security guard was loafing in his glass box near the front of the station, reading a paper instead of watching for turnstile hoppers. XP knew that if he had decided to jump off now, then no one would be there to stop him in time. All he had to do was wait for a train… His suicidal ambitions didn’t have long to wait, as soon the 6:45 began to rumble towards the platform. It was now or never for Xian Phillipse. For all his training, all the hard work he’s put into learning martial arts to protect himself and the ones he loved… he still couldn’t protect Angel. If he couldn’t protect others, how could he even begin to protect himself? It was all in vain, an elaborate lie to make him feel like everything was secure. But it wasn’t secure; life was dangerous, no matter how much you tried to prepare for it. It only took one ember to start an inferno. Now that his life was complete hell, he might as well make his way there personally and put his foot in Lucifer’s ass for taking away the one person he loved. He took a few steps back from the safety line, so he could have a running start. The train was almost there, a few passengers approaching the platform edge to board. One stood next to him, a girl listening to her walkman and rocking her head to the beat. The lights peered out from around the corner, the speedy metal snake on approach ready to grind XP under it’s steel belly. And just as he took the first two steps, death denied him at the front door. Someone suddenly tackled him to the ground, wrapping their arms around him and grounding him. He looked from the top of the person’s head to the train that pulled into the station, brakes whining to a halt. Exhaling in defeat, he looked down at the person in the black fleece who had thwarted his attempts at ending it all. And to much of his surprise, a 15 year old girl with dark brown pigtails looked up at him angrily. "Are you crazy?!" She pulled off her headphones and draped them around her neck. "You could’ve gotten yourself killed!" XP stared at her for a moment before coming to his feet, brushing off the back of his jeans. "That’s the point." It was now or never for Xian Phillipse. If he could not die now, well then… never again. He walked off in a huff, leaving the girl sitting there on her knees. "Hey, so what, no Thanks’?" she said, jumping up and running after him. Ever since that day, Xian Phillipse made sure no one was one step ahead of him… not even Dee Dee Rodriguez, the fastest woman in a wrestling ring. -=-=-=- "She said her name was Demona, and at first she looked and acted nothing like Angel. But it was only until later that everything clicked together." Xian took long drag from the blue mug, setting the Folger’s down and looking at DDR. The two of them sat what appeared to be a shrine to Japanese Anime and music, but to a select few, it was DDR’s room. Pictures and posters wallpapered the room, her king size bed covered with white comforter with the large, adorable face of Hello Kitty on the front. Sitting on the ground leaning against the edge of the bed, XP leaned his head back and stared up at the ceiling. "It was her smile…" he uttered, eyes half closed. "I’ll never forget that smile." "So you’re telling me you met this girl at Barnes and Noble, she said her name was Demona, and you two sat there and talked for a while. About what?" "Nothing, really. We just chatted about the basics. Where I’m from, why I’m in DC, where she’s from… it was like we were old friends catching up on things." DDR leaned back on her bed, hugging her Badtz Maru doll. "So then what?" "She looked at my scarf and said she liked it, and I told her it was a special gift from someone I’d lost. So that got her nervous and she ran out of the store. I figure she’s gone for good, so I make my way to the metro. So I’m standing there minding my business when a train pulls up. Right before it pulls off, someone gets off and kisses me." "Kisses you?" her brow rose with interest. He nodded, taking another sip of coffee. "Kisses me… and it’s a kiss that I haven’t had in a long time. I don’t remember it until it’s happening, and then all the memories come flooding back to me. So the girl backs up and gets on the train… and I’m looking at her and I see it’s Demona. But she doesn’t look like Demona anymore… the hair, eyes, and clothes are the same, but her face… it didn’t belong to the girl I had just met." "It looked like Angel?" "Demona didn’t smile the whole time I spoke with her, but when I saw her on the train and saw that smile, I recognized her right away. And those eyes… I’ve stared into those eyes long enough to know Angel when I see her." "So what… did you see Angel, or did you see an angel?" You never knew Bizzy was in the room until she spoke up. DDR and XP looked to the left, where the cyberpunk was sitting at DDR’s desk using her computer. She turned around, pulling up the sleeves of her black hoodie. "That’s something think about right there." XP shook his head. "I didn’t SEE an ANGEL. I SAW Angel. There’s a difference." "Not necessarily," she said. She turned back around to continue checking her Myspace. "When you take into account the number of angelic encounters, most people would think that they’re seeing someone they thought was dead, like a loved one coming back to keep them from harm." "Keep from harm? What harm are you in?" DDR asked with anxiousness. XP shrugged his shoulders, which prompted her to push further. "Okay, you’re in GTT right? Who’s your next opponent?" "Ember." Bizzy scoffed. "Well we can rule out any harm being received there." "Yeah, I doubt Angel would return from the dead to tell me to be careful about fighting HIM." Even in his serious mood, XP couldn’t help but roll his eyes at that thought. "I can see if it was Seymour Almasy or Snow… or even Plucky or Mentok the Mindtaker… but even I’d be a little skeptical about receiving celestial visits of warning about Ember." "That reminds me to write Hanna-Barbara a letter of disparagement," Bizzy mumbled, clicking ‘yes’ to add Dethklok as a friend. "But I digress." DDR threw the black plush penguin aside, scooting up and crossing her legs underneath her. "So if Angel wasn’t manifesting herself to warn XP about Ember, then what? Why did she come back?" "Well that depends on what she said to him." Bizzy said, glancing over her shoulder at Xian. "What’d she say, huh?" XP thought about those words again, a calm look coming over him. "‘Keep me in your heart forever… okay?’ Those were the last words I heard from her." DDR and Bizzy sat there thinking for a moment, their minds both divided by conflicting interpretations of that phrase. XP stood up, finishing off the last bit of coffee before looking at them both. "Tough one, isn’t it?" "Yes." Bizzy and DDR shared their response, looking at each other sharply before looking back at XP. Shrugging his shoulders, he walked over to a poster of Trigun and stared at it. "But then again, we’re assuming we’re dealing with an ethereal image. How’s about we look at it from a realistic standpoint. What do we know as far as facts?" "We know that you told us that she was killed about 3 years ago. So that means either someone lied to you, or you’re lying to us." Bizzy raised an eyebrow at DDR. It was very uncharacteristic of her to accuse someone of anything, let alone her best friends. But DDR looked dead on at XP, searching his eyes for an answer. Bizzy turned around and watched as XP looked her in the eye and frowned. "You were there a couple weeks after it happened, D. You saw what condition I was in. You kept me from killing myself, for Pete’s sake." She nodded, looking down at her comforter and wringing a handful of it. "Yeah, I know… so I know you’re not lying to us about her. So that means someone lied to you… but why would someone lie about Angel being killed?" "Maybe someone didn’t want you dating her." Bizzy turned back to the computer screen to groups. "Didn’t you say her family hated you?" "Yeah, but c’mon… no family would go as far as saying their daughter was dead to keep me from her." "Vana’s parents did." Another point goes to Bizzy. DDR’s mouth dropped as she looked at her. "No way!" "Yep, they had X-to-the-P a mixture of emotions. At first he was sad, ‘cause you know… someone dying sucks. Then after that first 5 minutes, I tell you’ll never see a man that happy unless you tell a dickless man that he’s suddenly got a salami roll in his pants." Folding his arms, XP shook his head with a sigh. "That was the worst April Fool’s joke ever. If only she didn’t answer the door the day I came for the funeral… I could’ve killed her there if her parents didn’t show up and yell ‘April’s Fool!’ all in my ear. But besides the wacky ass Zell family… no OTHER family would go that far, especially a lower class Hispanic family from the Bronx." "Well then I guess we’re out of options. There’s no plausible reason for her to return from the dead as an apparition," DDR held up a second finger to join the first point made, "and there’s no logical reason for her entire family to have faked her death to keep her from you." "You could go out on a limb and cry conspiracy. That’s what Hanson Love did." "Who?" DDR wasn’t filled in on that bit of info yet. "My… something or other. He’s a family member somewhere along the lines. But I was at his place earlier with my mom and his girlfriend. The whole way there his girlfriend’s going off on all these crazy ass theories that she and Hanson made up about the government. They said that Arnold Schwarzenegger was trying to take over the world in Hitler fashion, how 9/11 was allowed to happen so that Bush could have a war like his father had the Gulf War and so the US could get their hands on the oil, and how North Korea’s nuclear accolades would be the start of World War 3." "No way; that’s nuts." DDR shook her head, laying back on her bed and crossing her legs at the ankles. "No way the Terminator could take over the world." "Well all of that wasn’t what worried Hanson and her so much. It was the whole thought of nukes dropping on DC that had him pissing his pants. That’s pretty much why he hung himself." "I thought he was in a car accident?" She shook her head at XP. "Nope. She told the family that, but she told us what really happened. So we get to the house, and like there’s nothing there at all. Whole place looks torn up, like someone had been looking for the Holy Ark in there. Now she’s flipping out, my mom’s trying to calm her down, and I’m talking to the cops, asking them what’s going on. They said they found like 2 keys of cocaine in the car after the accident took place, so they got a warrant to check the apartment." "But I thought there wasn’t a car accident?" "Exactly." She pointed at DDR, who caught the point of the story. "There wasn’t a car accident. But it’s strange that now the cops are saying there’s a car accident. I think Hanson and his woman were involved in some dirty shit, and didn’t want his family to know, so she probably wove together a mixture of lies and truth while leaving details out to really leave us confused and fucked in the dark. I haven’t thought about it all yet… I came over here when I heard that XP was trippin’ balls talking about seeing Angel." "There’s too much to really think about right now. We could sit here all night wondering what’s going on with Angel or Hanson or Rumsfeld or Bin Laden," XP walked over to the corner of the bed, looking at Bizzy and DDR. "Tomorrow we’ll just go to Barnes and Noble and ask about her. Then we’ll get some answers." "Word," DDR sat up and came on her knees with a affectionate look for XP. "And if we get there and find out that you only made this all up in your head… believe you me that I won’t hesitate to beat your ass in the bookstore for making us worry tonight." Giving her a smile back, he tapped her on the chin with his fist. Bizzy turned around and cracked her knuckles, stretching her arms back behind her head. "Well I’m done. What now?" "Honestly," he began, slipping his hands in his pockets, "I could go for a drink." Bizzy and XP looked at DDR, who sighed and hopped off the bed, heading for the door. "I’ll call my brother. He can get us some beers or something." "Shoot for whiskey," XP watched as she closed the door behind her. As soon as it was shut he found Bizzy’s arms around his neck. His gaze turned from the door to her, and when they locked eyes she planted a soft kiss on him. "I hope you’re all right," she hissed, standing close to him and laying her head on his chest. XP nodded, patting her on the head and staring up at the ceiling. "Yeah… I’m fine…" That kiss was on his mind. He had been without Angel for 3 years now, and this day was the first time that he actually thought about her. He hadn’t forgotten about her… he just never thought about her deeply. Ever since the time he heard she was dead, he buried himself in his training, putting every extra moment not in school, eating, or sleeping into advancing himself in the ways of combat. But now that she had become a major focus in his mind again… now that her lips were fresh on his memory again… every other girl’s kiss couldn’t compare. DDR managed to find a bit of whiskey, and along with the beer the three of them drank away the pressures of life. Like one ember can set afire an inferno… so does one drop spark a frenzy of drunkenness, where inhibitions are lost and the carnal desires of the flesh dictate logic. Angel became a fleeting memory, a sorrow that XP wished to put on the backburner while focusing on the immediate fun. In the midst of drinking, though, while Bizzy and DDR attempted an intoxicated round of Dance Dance Revolution on her personal arcade machine… a thought came into his mind. Something he had said to Angel one night, after her brother threatened to beat him up if he didn’t stop seeing her. She wanted to end things, but Xian had looked into those creamy caramel eyes and smiled. "As long as I keep you in my heart, there’s no one that will stand in my way." Then he thought about the last thing she said to him, in NYC and now in DC 3 years later. "Keep me in your heart forever… okay?" If losing her was his motivation to fortify his combat craft… then he will surely dominate the world with his ability should he only keep her in his heart, keep her love on his mind. The power of their love can stop tidal waves, lull earthquakes, and reduce tornadoes to mere gusts of winds. Just as a small ember can be the precursor a conflagration… one kiss will cause Xian Phillipse to set the world on fire with his talent, to go right to the top of the business in a blaze of glory. Ember had the same ambitions, not to mention every other person in the tournament. And week after week, the fire of competition were being consumed by the stronger, the agile, the determined… and greater flames were rising up against others of their kind, only to consume them and rise on. Xian would not be extinguished by Ember, or anyone else in this competition. To him, Ember was just a mere matchstick struck from his past, waiting to be blown out. And right before XP passed out, with the laughter of teenaged girls echoing in his ears, he muttered one thing blearily to himself with a half smile. "Lights out." The Ember gets doused here and now. Sorry… that’s just Hard Luck.
