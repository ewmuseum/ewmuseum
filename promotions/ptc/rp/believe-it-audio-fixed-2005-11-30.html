Believe it. (Audio Fixed)

Date: 2005-11-30 (Typical New Eden Flag) Link: Hear this Statement! [A STATEMENT RELEASED VIA RADIO FROM RILEYVILLE, NEW EDEN] "Wek hto Sovereignty my friends, and thank you for listening to my publicly broadcasted message regarding the upcoming Global Title Tournament of which I have been entered. For those of you who do not know me, I am Kasidy Williams Drake, prince of the sovereign isle of New Eden, the garden which has been rebuilt and prospered under the rule of my mentor, the great Emperor Calvin Ulysses Riley, a benevolent leader, who shall lead this nation, as well as this world into a new Genesis and rebirthing as he, the Father would have it done. Amen. But come to preach to you sinners the glory that New Eden shall have done, I have not. Not now at least. My fans across the wor ld who may remember me as Smiley from Core Wrestling have demanded that I, the Edenic Prince, release a statement in some form regarding my participation in the Global Title Tournament – a honor that most professional wrestlers spend their entire careers striving to qualify for. Because I share my mentor’s benevolence, I shall heed their cries and speak on the upcoming bout – my first wrestling tour since I departed from Core Entertainment some time ago. So. The common theme going on throughout this tournament is that of hype. Like dogs hearing a dinner bell, the self-centered and sorely overrated champions of Primetime Central’s past have once again crawled out from under their quickly-crumbling careers to claim superiority and politic their way to the GTT cup, and the infamous guaranteed Global Championship contention that will follow. However, I wish for these combatants to know, that true talent and force, the likes of myself, do not need to politic their way to the top and shall not. The greats will fall from the onset of the first round, just as they always do, but when I claim the Global Title Tournament Cup, I want to make it clear that it will not be because of some fluke, or anything of the sort – it shall be because I am simply the better. I’m stronger, faster, more intelligent, and much more well-liked than the pathetic likes of Seymour Almasy and the like. During my seclusion upon our sacred isle, I have mastered new tequniques, holds, and impactful strategies than he would ever attempt in his dizziest daydreams. I mean not to bloat my peers. I respect all of you and what you have done – but that very statement is restricted by the paste tense. I have God and the support of an entire nation by my side. You have historic reigns for championship titles, most of which, no longer exist – and whilst your streaks and championship reigns may have come and gone, God is forever. This is power that you cannot buy. I apologize for the briefness of this message – there shall be more after I defeat my opponent in the first round, but I think few words are needed for what the world is about to experience. Trust me. And do not fear the Sovereignty – we only hurt to make this world a better place. Wek hto Sovereignty my friends. And believe the hype. Believe it…"
