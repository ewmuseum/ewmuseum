I don't need hype.

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 Another tournament, I thought to myself. I hadn't spent much time around this circle of federations, nor did I know any of the talent. But none of that was truly important. It usually worked out the same way. I'd come in, stir the pot a bit, then leave a trail of victims. I could imagine the other talents watching tapes of their opposing competitors. Trying to find something to use against their opponents; and know what they were in for. Not me. Everyone was the same. Whether they were a seasoned veteran, rookie, or world champion, everyone knew my name. Everyone knew when Sean Galen came around, there wasn't much chance of anyone else winning. Hoping to draw a decent competitor, I was thrown Jayce Bradley. A name without a face. Simple enough, he wouldn't win. For in reality, it was nothing but the truth. Jayce drew a legend, and hopefully he’d bring a pen and paper. Time to reopen the clinic. Dozens of titles, respect, and fear. It's what you can expect with Sean Galen. Not only a hell of a showing, but talent that will leave you in awe and expectations for more. It's tough being me, I won't lie. But I love everything about me. Cocky, conceited, arrogant. Simple words used easily to describe me. But still the greatest thing to stand before the masses. Watching me is like bumping a line of blow. I'll leave your heart racing, and begging for more. Simple enough, you can expect me to win. But I'll only leave you with a taste. Hype's for everyone else. With Sean Galen, there's only one phrase that needs to longer in your brain. No hype needed.
