The Odds Have Changed

19 October 2004

Date: 2004-10-19 “It’s a beauty, isn’t it?” Jonathan Sams exclaimed. Sams, as was learned before, was a member of the GTT Committee and responsible for keeping Sebastian in the tournament. Standing next to him wearing match suits were Mr. Melcher and Ross Howell. The three stood together side by side, standing proudly, looking like three big tycoons. Where did they stand? They stood inside the first floor of the Coliseum looking up at the huge liquid screen display. There, playing on replay was the recap of the previous rounds of the Primetime Central’s GT Tournament. Several of the most impressive highlights were featured on the screen, including upset wins of Shadow over Tsunami Oni and Sebastian over Digital Mortality. “That’s our kid.” Melcher pointed out. “I knew I molded him into a very strong fighter.” “But not a bright one.” Ross chimed in. “Does he really think he’ll get what he wants from all of this.”? “Probably.” Melcher replied. “It doesn’t matter, once he sets his mind on something it’s like he’s got blinders. Totally oblivious to the truth.” Overhead the screen depicted the round three results before a flashy presentation revealed the round four pairings with matching portraits for the competitors’ names. These portraits were taken early in the first round of the tournament, and each had a unique distinction to them. Deville’s looked like he was about to kill the photographer if he didn’t hurry up. But of all the portraits, perhaps none looked as wild as Karina Wolfenden’s with her pink and black hair, or as strange as Doctor Curiosity’s, or as innocent as Elia Smith. “There we go, gentlemen.” Jonathan pointed towards the screen. “Rockies bracket, semifinal match number two: Elia Smith Vs Sebastian Moraco.” “That girl doesn’t even look like a fighter.” Melcher sighed. “She made it this far? There’s no way Sebastian is going to made an underdog.” “That’s why Steve is going to make sure of that.” Ross explained. “Once Sebastian suffers and injury, his stock value is going to drop like ESCO.” Jonathan turned out feeling uneasy about the entire plan. “You sure this Steve guy knows what he’s doing? The idiot could break his damn leg, and then all of our plans are ruined.” “No, he knows better.” Ross assured him. “All Sebastian is going to have is a slight accident, and then it’s up to Martin to change the odds.” Daily Routine A beige-colored faded coverall decorated Steve Spinerall. Steve tipped the brim of his cap just slightly above his eyebrows politely nodding to those passing by him as he mopped the cream-colored ceramic floor. He dipped the mop into the cadillac and pushed it forward; the wheels of the bucket screeching as it rolled down the hallway. This janitor role he was playing was a façade. The tattered and faded clothes he wore were a lie. Steve was far too wealthy to be employed in such a job, but for the greater scheme of things he had to be. He was the Howell’s eyes into the GTT, more specifically keeping Sebastian Moraco in check. Not that Ross didn’t trust [name withheld] to keep Sebastian under control, but because he felt it wasn’t too precautious to have another security blanket. Steve’s main role was to make sure that nothing happened to Sebastian to make sure his creditability in the tournament remained in tact. He was also to make sure that nothing would keep Sebastian out of the tournament even engaging in small conversation pretending to be interested in his success. Which he was to a point. Today, however, he wasn’t here to make sure Sebastian was all right. He was going to make sure Sebastian wasn’t all right. Who better to make sure Sebastian had an accident, then the one person who practically knew his entire schedule? Steve had been monitoring for Sebastian since he first arrived in D. C. and could tell you his entire daily routine like it was water. What he had for breakfast, what time he takes naps, his workout routine, anything you could think of Steve knew. And it was that knowledge that would help Steve even up the odds so to speak. Steve looked up ahead and saw Sebastian walking out of the breakfast lounge in denim jeans and a white polo shirt. “Right on schedule.” Steve smirked. His knowledge of Sebastian’s routine was almost dangerous, he could predict his next move without question. “Good morning, Steve.” Sebastian greeted him. “Good day, young sir.” Steve replied. There was something different about Sebastian this morning, Steve thought. Sebastian was always an amiable person, but this time he seemed a little joyous. Apparently something good had happened to Sebastian last night. “The floor’s looking real good, Steve.” Sebastian complimented him. “Thanks Sebastian.” Steve replied. His response was too formal, with him hating to do this cleaning work to make his presence seem less conspicuous. “Good luck against your next opponent. Elia Smith, right? I think you can beat her.” Sebastian nodded. “I think so too. However, she isn’t exactly on my mind right now. I’ll worry about her when the time comes.” Unexpected Training Tuesday. Tuesday’s were Sebastian’s days off from his workout routine. Every Tuesday morning, he would take a slight job around the headquarters to not let up on his training, and then would take the rest of the day off exploring new areas of the city. When he returned to his suite, he would spend the majority of the night looking at scouting reports about his next opponent that [name withheld] would leave with him. Tonight, however was a little different. No, today was a little different. Sebastian woke up this morning, had his routine jog, and continued with his schedule much like Steve had anticipated. Sebastian packed his camera and camcorder and walked around the city, looking for any souvenirs or views he could take a picture of to show to his parents, who were unable to take many trips. The day had been mostly uneventful until he met Sarah at her workplace. Sebastian met Sarah Monday afternoon in the GTT Gym, and had a dinner date of sorts with her later that night. The entire evening kept Sebastian in a mood he had nearly forgotten about: happy. The GTT, [name withheld], and Elia Smith were all put on hold as Sebastian finally found something in D. C. he could enjoy. Sebastian learned that Sarah worked as a waitress in downtown, trying to make errands meet for her college. Her degree? It was medicine, she was studying to be a nurse, and later on a full fledged doctor. Sebastian spent the majority of the afternoon with her, being her escort around the city, even if he was the tourist and not she. He even accompanied her to the community college she was attending. Now the afternoon was over and after another dinner with Sarah, Sebastian found himself back in his suite. It was Tuesday evening, and that meant he was going to be watching footage of Elia Smith’s past matches. However, that wasn’t going to happen tonight, at least not totally. Sebastian had already watched Elia Smith’s matches before. He had access to all the matches on tape, much like the rest of the field had, and even watched Elia’s last round match in person. He was impressed with her abilities, but more so was impressed at how fragile she looked. She was so frail, yet strong. He knew he couldn’t underestimate her, but still didn’t feel nervous about the match up. Nope, tonight he wasn’t going to study any more tapes. He had already seen Elia Smith wearing tight clothes too many times. Tonight, he was going to study something else: learn new moves. Sebastian was still very spotty in his wrestling technique making quite a few noticeable mistakes. He had just started learning to wrestle as a professional wrestler merely days before the first round and still had a lot of room to improve. That’s where tonight’s training comes in. What better way to learn professional moves, than to watch old footages of old school World Wrestling Entertainment. Yeah, I went there. Sebastian put the DVD inside the, well, DVD player and watched some old matches. He had grown fond of Bret Hart’s matches, even if he learned that his career in WWE didn’t end on good terms. Tonight’s match was Bret Hart Vs Mr. Perfect, for the intercontinental title at Summer Slam. Even if he didn’t learn any moves he could mimic, Sebastian was going to at least enjoy watching the show. A trap is set Steve woke up pretty early this morning to fold his plan into action. He knew Sebastian woke up around 7 am every morning and had to be sure that he had enough time to make everything right. He knew Sebastian was going to go out for a jog, and then come back to the breakfast lounge before walking down the very same hallway he was in towards the suites for his shower. Steve spent the entire morning, waxing the floor and stairs making them not only unusually shiny but slippery as well. He put up a sign on the hallway to make everything seem in place. He had already seen Sebastian leave for his jog and knew he only had half an hour at the most to prepare. The floor was ready, all that he needed was bait. He had the bait. He was going to fill up his cleaning cart with as much junk as he could. The cart was getting pretty heavy, with broomsticks, handles, and buckets all barely overshadowing the edges. Steve tried to push the cart and found it very strenuous. “Shit, that’s heavy.” He murmured budging the cart a little bit. “Still needs more though, can’t take anything for granted.” He added a few more essentials to the cart and then pushed with all his might towards the stairs going down. He stopped a few yards away, taking deep breaths. Hell, he was going to need Sebastian’s help after all. However, it isn’t the weight of the cart that was going to be the culprit. Oh no, on the bottom level, the shelf was a bit unstable and a broomstick stuck out. It was meant to trip Sebastian and send him tumbling down the stairs, with the weight of the cart landing on his leg, in theory. In Motion Ross Howell answered the ringing cell phone in his pocket. “Hello?” “…” “Quite a bit early, isn’t it Steve?” Ross asked. “So, what do you want? Is our punk kid injured yet?” “…” “Not yet, huh?” Ross exclaimed. “Well you better hurry up. I’ll call Martin and tell him to get the odds ready.” “…” “Yeah, you just make sure you don’t overdo it this time. I remember what you did to that other kid. We don’t need any mistakes like that.” “…” “Hey, I’m the most feared lawyer in New York, sure this is D. C., but I’m sure things won’t get that bad. Now hurry up and finish the job.” Ross hung up as he drove his BMW around the corner. He picked up his cell phone again and dialed Martin Booker, his Las Vegas booker. “Martin, time to wake up.” Ross bellowed. “…” “Steve just called. He’s moments away from making Elia Smith the favorite in the match.” “…” “Oh I trust him, I don’t think he’s going to make any drastic mistakes.” “…” “Yeah, everything is in motion. You just make sure you handle your end of the plan.” “…” “That’s right, this kid is going to earn us some serious retirement pay.” “….” “Goodbye, Mr. Booker.” Ross hung up and looked into the rearview mirror. He wasn’t looking at himself, or at the cars behind him, but at the shady figured slouched back in the back seat of the car. The figure was cloaked in darkness, with a baseball cap covering his eyes. Ross smiled. “Well, are you ready to meet up with [name withheld]?” “Yeah…” the figure replied nonchalantly. Stock Value…Dropped Steve smiled as he saw Sebastian walk out of the breakfast lounge, right on cue. He quickly turned back towards the cart, pushing it as much as he could, not making a scene. A few bystanders walk by, but none of them offered him any help. Sure, it was bad manners of them, but he liked it that way. He needed it to be that way. “Jesus, Steve.” Sebastian remarked. “You sure you got enough stuff in there?” Steve sighed. “Yeah man, they’re having me relocate the cleaning gear locker, so I got to move all this stuff.” Sebastian walked towards the front end of the cart, on top of the stairs, just like Steve wanted. “Where are you taking this?” “Down the stairs, around the corner, towards the other stairs.” Steve explained. “After the stairs, it should be easy though.” Sebastian grabbed the handles and pulled back on the cart. “Alright, just push with me.” “Thanks.” “Hey, no problem.” Sebastian remarked. “Everyone should be helpful, I believe.” The two managed to push the cart towards the top two steps of the stairs. Sebastian carefully stepped down the stairs and kept the cart up as much as he could. He looked a bit unbalanced, and this was Steve’s chance. He kept both hands in sight, on the cart, but with his foot, kicked the broomstick on the bottom shelf. Just as he did that, Sebastian let go with one hand to hold on to the rail. That split second of unbalance was enough to cost him as the broom hit him in the knee and Sebastian lost his footing. Steve watched, biting his tongue, as Sebastian tumbled down the stairs with the cart following behind like a mammoth. Sebastian landed on the bottom floor, severely shaken up, but that wasn’t the end of it. The cart followed behind and it landed with a sickening thud on top of his right leg, which already had landed in an awkward position. Sebastian screamed out in pain, trying to get the cart off him, but not even an adrenaline rush was going to help him. Steve quickly ran down the stairs, apologizing like a madman, all the way down. He could see Sebastian was in pain, and knew that the plan had worked. Steve pulled the cart off of Sebastian as much as he could. Finally, after struggling for a bit, he managed to get the cart completely off him. He had to survey the damage now. Sebastian rolled up his pant leg and saw his right leg black and blue with bruises. “Fuck…” he murmured under his breath in pain. Steve looked at the leg. Yeah, the plan had worked. Sebastian was no longer going to be a favorite going into the match, and the winnings were going to be more plentiful. Yet, his leg looked mutilated. Had he gone too far? Sure, Elia was now going to be the favorite, but was it going to be too much? “Can you move?” Steve asked. “A little bit.” Sebastian replied grimacing. “I think I should be okay though, after a few days.” “What about your match?” “I’ll worry about that when the time comes.” A Surprise Visit Jeff, still sleepy, walked lazily towards the door of his apartment. The ringing had been going on forever, and whoever it was, didn’t feel like letting up. “All right already!” Jeff shouted. “I’ll answer the fucking door!” Jeff opened the door and saw a smirking Ross Howell on the other side. “What do you want?” Jeff asked. “To let you know that Steve went on with the plan.” Ross explained. “By this time, Sebastian should be in a world of hurt, and the underdog against Elia Smith.” “Sounds like good news.” Jeff replied. “It also sounds like news you could have called me with.” Ross nodded. “Actually, I came by to reacquaint you with an old friend.” Ross stepped inside and made way for the mystery figure to walk inside the room. [name withheld] gasped. “What the hell is he doing here?” The shady figure looked up at [name withheld], slightly pushing his baseball cap up, and smiled. GTT Fans meet Ryan Howell.
