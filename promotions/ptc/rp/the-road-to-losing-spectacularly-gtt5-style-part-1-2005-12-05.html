The Road to Losing … SPECTACULARLY!! GTT5 Style Part 1

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Fatbadger isn’t a competitive person. He doesn’t really care for winning. He doesn’t get caught up in hype or training. That isn’t to say he isn’t in shape, or that he’s not the most gullible person you might possibly meet, but it really doesn’t all add up to professional wrestler, yet he‘s had his share of accomplishment. That is, perhaps, what makes him so unique in this sport. Fatbadger holds an air of infantile innocence that is continually offset by his matching intelligence, always ensuring a consistant record of getting nowhere with memebers of the opposite sex. He only cares about two things: his family, the Boomer Sooners, and where he’s gonna grab his next bag of Funyuns. In fact, that’s basically all his normal day revolves around: sitting on the couch in the Boomer Sooner locker room, eating his favorite onion-based snack and sucking down some Mountain Dew Code Red. Damn, that’s just boring … and pathetic. It’d be nice if he got pissed or sad or happy about things more often, but usually he just shrugs most things off with an unwitting ease. Sure, he gets scared quite easily … and way too often … usually over not much at all, but man, that gets old. I mean jeez. What a pussy. Anyways, enough talking about what a loser Fatbadger is … when we can just look at him. Badger walks toward the PTC Arena Dome of DoooooooooM! where the first round of GTT5 is being held. He’s wearing a brown backpack over a black jacket, his long dark hair blowing around them. His XUW Intercontinental Title sits over his right shoulder. He approaches an annoyed looking security guard with a clip board next to a large overhead door at the back of the building. Fatty smiles and struts up to him obliviously. Fatbadger: Hey dude, I’m-- Security Guard: I don’t really care who the hell you are! I’ve had freaks coming in here all day, half of you bastards aren’t even on the list. A bunch of party crashers! Trying to take any open spot that might open up during this thing. Well listen up: it’s an exclusive tournament and YOU are not getting in! Fatbadger: But I’m Fatbadger! Security Guard: What the FUCK is a fat badger?!! Fatbadger: Actually … this is perfect dude. This gives me a viable reason for me to like, list off my qualifications and accomplishments and stuff without having to, like, have to say it out loud to myself or like, think it all out in my mind. Everybody in a big tournament like this has gotta like, do that at one point! So sit back dude! Security Guard: Why not? I could us a nap. He sips on his coffee. Badger digs through his backpack. Fatbadger: Ok, well, where to begin dude. Crap! I should have practiced this. My note cards are all out of order and stuff. Where’s my laser pointer?!~!! Ok, I’m ready. He clears his throat. Fatbadger: Alright, well as you can see -- Security Guard: OH GOD I GOTTA SHIT!! The guard grabs the back of his pants and quickly waddles in the door behind him and down the hallway. Badger looks disappointed, but at least thinks to grab the door before it closes. Fatbadger: Dude! Should I just wait out here or …? The guard doesn’t look back. Badger’s bottom lip starts to quiver as he slowly puts away his presentation materials and goes inside the building. He walks down a dark hallway. Fatbadger: Now I’m gonna have to like, talk out loud to myself about my history and stuff. Alright then, I guess like, it all started back about four years aaaAAAAAHHH-- Fatbadger is suddenly jumped by three men dressed in black and wearing masks. Two of the men are well built while the third appears shorter and much stockier. He kind of scuttles in behind them. By their posture, they slightly resemble Badger’s XUW teammates, the Boomer Sooners. They knock him to the floor of the dark hallway and point guns at his head. They actually appear to be something like Super Soakers crudely painted black, not really resembling real guns much at all. One of the attackers steps forward. Attacker 1: Take that … FATRICK J. MCBADGERSON!! Badger scratches his head, as that would be how either of his friends Saij or Dave Kipfer of the Boomer Sooners would greet him. Fatbadger: How … how did you know to call me that? Attacker 2: Real smooth … DAVEY BOY MYSTERIOUSON!! HUZZAH!! Attacker 1: Sorry about that … SAIJWICK HU INCOGNITOBERRY!!! Fat Attacker: By god! JR’s pants are making extra gravy in these hot tights! It’s pretty obvious that these three are the poorly disguised Boomer Sooners: Saij, Dave Kipfer, and Jim Ross (yes, that Jim Ross). Fatbadger: You three dudes are a mysterious pair. What do you want? Attacker 1: What do we want?! We want to know if you know ANYthing about your first round opponent in this GTT5? About … Ragnarok? Fatbadger: Well yeah, he’s like, uh, religious and stuff. Attacker 2: You bet your farkin keester he is. He used to LIVE in a church! That’s how religious he is! Fatbadger: Whoa dude. Attacker 2: Yeah, and he’s got God on his side, that’s for sure. He was once the only survivor of a huge fire. Fatbadger: God must love that dude! Attacker 2: Indeed. This whole building could burn down, and he’d probably walk out of the ashes with new boots on! Fatbadger: I’m … not quite sure what that means dude. Attacker 2: Me neither, but it sounded good in my head. Fatbadger: Is that all you dudes wanted? To like, know if I knew about Ragnarok? Attacker 1: We want to know what your goals are here! Fatbadger: Oh, well, obviously, to like, lose … SPECTACULARLY!! But like, more than that to get somewhat far in the tournament, and then lose in a spectacular fashion, adding to the overall spectacularness of it all. And like, ya know, more than that, I was once XUW’s most lovable jobber, and in the future, I’d like to, ya know, maybe be known as PTC’s most lovable jobber. So I need to like, get some more losses over here and stuff. The attackers huddle and whisper a bit before turning back toward Badger. Attacker 2: Fark! That’s a better answer than we thought you’d have. Fatbadger: Why’d you dudes want to know? Fat Attacker: By god! We’ll ask the questions!! He shoves the obviously plastic water gun in Badger’s face. Fatbadger: Sweet Buddah! Please don’t kill me! Attacker 1: Back off JR-Not. We were just worried Fatty, that you wouldn’t have a thought out purpose for this tourney, and that we’d have to come and threaten to kill you if you didn’t win your first round match. Fatbadger: Please don’t kill me!! Oh man, I peed a little. Fat Attacker: By god! That’s more than a little! Attacker 1: So I guess that mean we really don’t have to be here. Attacker 2: Wrong! That means he farkin needs us more than ever! Sure he’s got a goal, but he’s still missing what every PTC tournament entrant needs … He motions the three of them. Attacker 2: … guns and some masked psychos!! You can’t get anywhere in PTC without them! Attacker 2 poses with his gun. Badger cowers in fear on the floor. Attacker 1: What about that guy you told me about that won the GTT4 last year … Doctor Curiosity! He didn’t have either of those. Attacker 2: I thought of that, so just in case, that’s why we brought a chemistry set as backup. The Fat Attacker pulls a large boxed chemistry set from his pants. Fat Attacker: By god! JR has almost perfected the Viagra© suppository! Attacker 1: That’s … disturbing. Attacker 2: Shut up man, I’m a majority investor in that study. Attacker 1: Shall we wrap this up? Attacker 2: Indeed. Attacker 1: May I? Attacker 2: Be my guest. Attacker 1: Thank you. Attacker 1 lunges at the cowering Fatbadger and stands over him, waving the painted water bazooka in his face. Badger’s eyes grow wide and start to fill with tears. Attacker 1: NOW LISTEN UP YOU GOOFY BASTARD! YOU’RE GONNA WIN YOUR FIRST ROUND MATCH DAMNIT OR WE’RE GONNA KILL YOU FOR DAYS AND DAYS!!! Fatbadger: NOOOOOOOO! NOT DAYS AND DAYS!! NOT DEAD!! DEAD FOR DAYS!! Attacker 2: Psssst. We can alter that last part, remember? Attacker 1: Oh, yeah. Thanks. IF YOU DON’T WIN YOUR MATCH AGAINST RAGNAROK … YOU’RE GONNA BE SORRY!! REEEEEEEAL SORRY!! WE’LL MAKE SURE THAT YOU DON’T EVER SEE FUNYUNS OR CODE RED EVER AGAIN!! Fatbadger: WAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHH!! He curls into a ball and rolls back and forth, weeping. Attacker 1: Uh, are we sure that that new last part won’t also kill him? The attackers all shrug. Attacker 2: Well, while we’re here … let’s go put super glue on Almasy’s toilet seat! Attacker 1: Nice. Attacker 2: You got the super glue JR-Not? Fat Attacker: By god! It’s also down in JR’s pants with that bottle of liquid E-Z Poop we mixed into security guard's coffee! Attacker 2: Oh fark! We were supposed to mix coffee in with that?! The three attackers walk off down the hallway. Badger’s weeping eventually quiets down, and he picks himself up and dusts himself off with a determined look on his face. Fatbadger: Now I MUST win this match! Even though I had planned on doing that in the first place … those dastardly masked strangers CONVINCED ME FURTHER THROUGH THEIR TACTICS OF FEAR AND INTIMIDATION!! I must win for FUNYUNS and CODE RED!!! Badger poses triumphantly. Fatbadger: Oh, and all that jobber stuff that mentioned before. I must defeat Ragnarok!!! He poses again and then looks around sheepishly. Fatbadger: Ah crap, I’m talking to myself! Well then, I guess I better go clean the pee out of my pants.
