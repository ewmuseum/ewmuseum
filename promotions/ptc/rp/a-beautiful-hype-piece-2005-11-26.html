A beautiful hype piece…

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. The apparently Grand Daddy of all PTC events. And, let's just be honest, in the fifth incarnation of this biggest who's who rush to suicide there are a lot of interesting names involved. You got Angelo Deville, a considered favorite by many on name recognition alone. But hell…it's the Devil's Don…and the last time he actively took part in a wrestling match he managed to pin his own shoulders to the mat in an attempt to escape my "pussy bitch ass faggot completely unrealistic" modified katahajime (ironically…judo does not exist…it is all an illusion). So…when it comes to taking stock in such claims…I pass on the notion. That said, who the fuck am I kiddin'? Here I am…on the waiver wire and not a person will sniff me. I really guess not beating Hoyt was the end all be all. I made a vow to myself that I would go silent if I lost to him…but… Let's just say… Nah…I won't say. I leave that for informative dirt sheet writers like Dave Meltzer to explain to you. KEEP THAT CREDIT CARD NEARBY…THAT TORCH NEWSLETTER IS UP FOR RENEWAL ANY TIME NOW! Sigh. I received an automatic birth into this thing through placement as a PTC Number One on their last top ten list. But…truth be told…it's been downhill since then. I'd usually be the first to tell you such groundbreaking tales like "I'M THE GREATEST~!" and that old chestnut "I'M BETTER THAN YOU BECAUSE *insert reason*!" It's predictable…and quite laughable. But it is efficient, and it works in some cases. How do you think some has-been like Seymour Almasy stands looking at himself in the mirror? "I'm great! I'M AWESOME! I KICK ASS!" Hey…whatever forces the blade away from your wrist. Or the bottle away from your lips. Trash talk is overrated because it is trash. I'm sure Gary Payton could spin some yarn on Chris Paul about how much of an epic badass he is, but that doesn't exactly translate on the floor. It's 2005…and while Payton was good in his day, I'd take Chris Paul against his old has-been title-pissing-away ass any day of the week. It's about talent…what you bring to the ring. And while talking can be beautiful…it's hollow. I hate how people try to mask their flaws through talk. Myself included. I'm an indignant asshole who can't stay with an organization for a long period of time because I am a cancer. I went toe to toe with someone who does drugs claiming I was trying to destroy him to teach him a lesson…ironically lost was the concept that I was a recovering alcoholic. I got a bitchy wife who I stand for whatever reason…and every single week it feels like we patch shit up and everything is sunny and then the next day shit comes falling from the sky again and she's bitching about me training, or how I don't take out the trash…or whatever. My beautiful send off from PRIME involved a six foot eight bearded preacher man kicking my ass into oblivion and stamping me out of the equation…even though for the better part of six months I told everyone that I was an unstoppable wrestling machine. I lost a submission match to Carson Nash…losing the one true World Championship I've ever held. Carson who? Ex-fuckin'-actly. For every achievement I have, I have five other failures that dwarf it. For every beat down I gave Karina Wolfenden there are double that amount that Angelo Deville beat me black and blue. For every Five Star Championship success there are fWo Internet Championship failures…or failures to even go for the Evolutionary Championship in the Squared Circle. For every Jolt Triple Crown Championship…there is the PRIME Universal Championship…the fWo World Championship…the tSC Championship. One out of four is twenty five percent positive, and if it isn't above seventy percent it really ain't worth a shit. Sure…I beat Nova at Colossus Two. But leading up to that I lost to Dave Gibson…someone who is certainly in the Almasy camp of talking to himself in the morning in the bathroom mirror. I'm flawed. And while I can put on a five star match with anyone in wrestling…that doesn't exactly mean I win. There is an adage about a wounded animal…how it is the most dangerous or whatever. Fuck that. My career is on LIFE SUPPORT! You don't see someone who is deadlocked in a coma as dangerous. A fucking pillow over the mouth will kill them. That's me. Not some super human figure of awesome. Not this shining tower of professional wrestling. I'm Brandon Youngblood…and unlike everyone else here…I don't give a shit if I win or lose. As long as I place, I'll take it as a moral victory. I'm not a comedian. I can't list off accomplishment after accomplishment. I'm a former alcoholic who is probably going to die before his fifties because of his lifestyle choice. I'm not a legend from yesterday or a hero from today. When it ends, I'll be some guy that will pop to mind only in respect to them. "Hey…you remember that great submission match Deville had Kyle Trueblood?" "Hey…what about that beat down Hoyt laid down on that Youngblack chump." "Mike Randalls real coming out party in tSC was against that…that one guy…shit…who cares!" I can't list thousands of degrees that I have in my possession from Pwn Community College. I'm a guy who loves to wrestle…who is more than likely in a very apathetic mood because even when I climb that mountain and get as high as I can…the thing that knocks me back down to the bottom rips away a huge chunk of my soul. You want a hype piece? My name is Brandon Youngblood and I'll try. There…that make you happy? I’d blame the bourbon…but I never was one to say some profound Hemingway bullshit. I’ll leave that task to everyone else.
