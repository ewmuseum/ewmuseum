Date: 2005-11-28 A black screen. The twinkling ivory tones of Sam Spence reminded the mind of a time when a toothless man represented unspoken integrity. A Facendian voice boomed over this darkness… "In the history of men, there has always been the need for the greatest story. A tale of valor, a sweet sonnet of regret, harkening songs of courage, shameful odes of death. The greatest story does not need the greatest man, nor one of pure heart, godliness and sanctity." "Sometimes it is the sinners that teach us the follies of man. Lucifer's fall, Job's anger, Tony Danza's accent…" "In the days of yore, a man who delved into his emotions was a renaissance poet; more likely to assblast a man named Cornelius than to pillage with the vikings, thrusting their oar into the maiden seas…" "…but there is one to remind us what it is to be a man…what it is to be a hero…" Slowly, the screen brightened under the visage of a shaking handheld video camera that couldn't be more than three feet off the ground. In the echoing emptiness of a warehouse, a long-haired dark brown skinned Mexian was tied, bound and gagged to a chair. Tears were streaming down his face, which flickered perilously close to a raging blowtorch. The man who held it watched the flames dance in his electric blue eyes, a cigarette dangling from his lips; not ashed once and halfway done. Leaning down, the man smirked as the Mexican's nose started forming snot bubbles. "If we are to search for the prophecy of Biaakazatron you shall face the fires of hell, my friend." The man with the blowtoch hadn't shaved in days, a dirty blonde 5 o'clock shadow well-formed, his hair a spiky mess. The Mexican looked to the roof in prayer, hoping God would take his soul before his flesh seared. In the background, a man in a silver jumpsuit scrached his balls… …then sniffed said hand. "Do you solemnly swear to fight by my side, for the fathers of Biaakazatron, for the maidens of the Bunny Ranch and to get Steve and I beer whenever I ask?" "BUUUUUUUUUUUUD." The Mexican shook his head vigorously, for years he had feared this man would kill him without the blink of an eye. The man smirked and looked towards the stranger in the silver jumpsuit, "What about you, the one they call BEEF?" Beef responded with a pelvic thrust, "OH YEAH. I'm in so deep Jenna Jameson *might* feel it." The cigarette smoking man looked around nodding in approval, "So it begins…but first…let's get some fish tacos." And so the man stood, turning off the blowtoch and spinning on a dime away from the Mexican who sat back in shuddering relief. Beef followed the man, the camera fell to the ground as the Mexican screamed under the gag for someone to untie him. A midget was viewed from the camera from the ground-up, he shrugged back at the Mexican, "HUNGRY." They all exited, the Mexican left alone to suffer under the sounds of his own muffled screams.
