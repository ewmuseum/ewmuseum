The Past Haunts

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 *** October 12 1995; The Past *** "Alright kid, today is the day. You ready?" That question came from a voice, so deep and full of sadistic emotions, towards a young kid sitting behind the wheel of a car in the driver’s seat. Three men and a young kid, somewhere in his puberty, are sitting in a car. The three men looked big and muscular and two of them were black, with one of the black men talking to the kid. The other man was white. "This is your real test. Today we’re going to look if you’re the man or if you’re a real big pussy!" Said the other black man. Even though these words should sound a lot more sadistic, scary and even insulting, the voice of this man was actually more calming. The kid nodded, looking confident from the outside, but was almost pissing his pants from the inside. He tried to show as less fear as possible, but he was scared as hell, not knowing what would happen once the unthinkable happened. "So, are you scared?" The black man with the deep voice asked the kid. The kid just nodded back just a little bit, again trying to show as less fear as possible. "Ain’t we all scared?" Asked the man back to the kid. "You must be crazy not to be scared…" "Come on, Matt, he’s not ready. Look at him, he’s going to take a big shit in his pants any minute now and shake in fear! Once we get back to the car, he’ll freeze and sit behind the wheel motionless!" The white man said to the black man, known as Matt. "Silence!" The man known as Matt yelled back. "If I say he’s ready, then he is ready! I found this kid and we hung out together for the last few months, he even knew how to drive a car in just two weeks thanks to me! He is ready to do this!" It was silent for a couple of seconds. The three men and the kid stared blankly into the deep. Thinking about what would happen next, how it would happen and probably what next after it all happened. "Alright men, today is finally the day." Matt started again, trying to convince his boys, but with a voice like that, it probably made them feel even more nervous for whatever was coming up. "Even though we did plan all of this just a month ago, we will succeed. I don’t need a big plan to succeed, my strategy is simple and that is to succeed and that happens by all means necessary. After today, we will leave the country and split whatever we have fairly between us four. Everybody gets the same amount of money as he deserves, do we all agree?" The three others nodded yes. Then the two black men and the white man lock up their weapons. The white and black guy carry shotguns, as Matt grabs his pistol. The kid looks on to the three men as they prepare for something. All three men proceed to pull pantyhoses over their heads. "Alright men. We’re ready to go!" Matt said. He was the one that was ready the most. "Okay TJ, you keep the engine running. We’ll do the biggest part. Just sit here and wait patiently." Matt said to the kid, named TJ. TJ nodded, and had his hands wrapped tightly around the wheel. Though, he was anxious for what was coming up. The three men stared out the window, and they looked at a local bank in the city. It was a pretty big bank as well, and they were probably planning to do a bank robbery. Matt grins as they see a guard walk out of the building, probably taking a break. The guard looks around him and proceeds to walk away slowly from the bank. With the bank open and without protection, the three men saw an opportunity. "Wait for it…Wait for it…" Matt said silently. The kid held the wheel tight, licking his lips, as the other two men hold their guns tight. The guard turned around the corner slowly, as all three men started to grin. "Is it time?" The black guy asked. "Oh yes, it’s time. You know what to do TJ. Sit tight and let us do all the hard work. We’re counting on you!" Matt said with his deep voice. TJ was barely moving, but still able to nod. He never was this scared before in his life. "Alright men, time to get rich!" Matt yelled. The three men jumped out of the car and started running up towards the bank, with no police or many people in sight. The black guy holds the door open, as the white guy and Matt storm into the bank. From here on out, gun shots and people screaming are heard. TJ just stares at the bank, trembling in fear. Maybe the white guy was right and maybe TJ will freeze whenever the three men return. You can hear people yelling as a minute goes by, which seems a week to TJ. Just then, the alarm can be heard of the bank, followed up by a gun shot from a shotgun. TJ freezes and with eyes widened, he looks in front of him out of the window. After another minute passes, the three men return and run out of the bank with all carrying a bag with money in it, as some dollar bills escape the bag and fly through the air and land on the ground, the three men run towards the car, with the white man having blood on his shoes and pants. "Alright TJ, start the car! We must get the fuck out of here!" Matt yelled as the three men entered the car. "And why the fuck did you shoot that guy!?" "Because he was hitting the alarm button! I had to shoot the bastard!" The white guy yelled back. "Next time, if you’re going to shoot somebody, do that BEFORE he can hit the button!" Matt yelled back angry. "And why aren’t you starting the fucking car, TJ!? We’ve got to go, move it!" TJ sat behind the wheel like a zombie, completely frozen with a dead look in his face. He wasn’t moving or responding to whatever Matt yelled. "Fuck! I told you this piece of shit wasn’t ready! He’s frozen completely!" The white guy yelled. "Oh shit, it’s the cops! I can see the sirenes!" The black guy screamed, starting to show fear as well, with a bag of money on his lap. Matt has seen enough and takes matters in his own hands. He pushes his bag of money on top of the black guy and gets out of the car. He opens the door to the driver’s seat and pulls TJ out of it onto the pavement next to the car. TJ lands hard on the ground, but is still completely frozen as he starts to look towards Matt. Matt looks back and shakes his head. "Maybe I had to high expectations of you. I thought you could do it, but you disappointed me. Nobody disappointed me without regretting it, but you’re in luck. I’ll see you one day, if you’re still alive." Matt said to TJ, looking him straight in the eyes. Matt jumped in the driver’s seat and closed the door and started the engine. The other two men kept yelling to Matt to move out, which Matt eventually did. The car just started to drive away, as police started arriving. As the car with the three criminals was almost exiting the street, they were stopped by two police cars. The car with the criminals crashed against the two police cars and were stopped dead in its tracks. You could see Matt hitting the wheel with his fist, as police officers started to surround the car of the three men with guns pointed at them. The three criminals threw their weapons out of the car, as police officers opened the car doors and pulled all three criminals out. The police officers pushed them against the car and told them their rights as the officers handcuffed them. TJ was still sitting on the ground, looking on as Matt and the other two men were taken into custody as other police officers ran into the bank. As TJ sat there, a police officer approached TJ. He stood next to TJ, as TJ looked up. "You okay, kid? Were you hit by those criminals or something, or were you just sitting here for fun?" The cop said, laughing. "Here, let me help you up." The officer helped TJ up as Matt and the two criminals cursed and yelled in the background. TJ looked the police officer straight in the eyes. "Thank you for helping me up. I’ll take it from here. I’m going home, where I belonged in the first place." TJ said to the officer, his first words since the bank robbery. TJ was finally moving and acting normally, atleast from the outside. "That sounds like a good idea, but you have to come with us. You’re a witness of this crime scene and we have to ask you some questions. Don’t worry, it won’t take too long, we just want you to ask some questions about what happened here, as you were one of the few witnesses here on the street. Matt Glaze and his gang are very dangerous and was wanted for a long time. So is it okay if you came along?" The police officer asked TJ. "I’d rather not, but if I have to do just that to get home, than I will." TJ said as he nodded. He then proceeded to look towards Matt, where police officers had trouble of pushing Matt into the car. Matt looked TJ straight in the eyes with a sadistic and angry look in his face. Even though Matt was far away, TJ could see what Matt was saying to him. "I’ll be coming for you, you have been warned…" *** October 13 2006; The Present *** [It’s finally that time of year where the big, annual tournament is being held. The tournament many wrestlers are waiting for. This is a tournament where wrestlers and legacies are made. This is the place where you need to be to open the eyes of the public. If you ever wanted fame in wrestling, this tournament is it. This tournament is called the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and with past succeses, it reached number six, making it the GTT6.] [One man who waited for the tournament was Sledge. He waited patiently until he had a second shot in the big tournament. Once again Sledge is coming into the tournament, trying to make a name for himself, since his previous stint did not leave that big of an impression. But this time he has an even bigger mission. And that is to wave the UWF flag high in the air, as UWF is closing its doors this December.] [The UWF had a fantastic and memorable run where Sledge gained his first taste of fame, even though he never was a big part of the UWF, nonetheless, he was a part of the UWF family and a proud member of the UWF roster. He will carry the UWF name into the GTT6 and will represent his home fed proudly, before it closes down.] [But the question remains, will Sledge have a better run this year and make it memorable to others? Who knows, but Sledge is excited about entering the GTT tournament once more.] [The scene opens at a local diner. It rains outside, as you can hear the rain drop. We can see two man sitting at a table. One of the two men is Sledge, with a hamburger and some fries in front of him and a bottle of Heineken beer. The other man, sitting on the other side of the table, has black hair and a lighter skin colour than Sledge. He’s pretty muscled as well. He’s got a plate of fries and a big steak in front of him, with a glass of Cola next to it. This man is known as Lance, a friend of Sledge. Sledge stares out of the window, thinking about stuff, as Lance eats some of his fries.] Lance: "What’s up, dude? You haven’t touched your food yet. Eat up, you could use the energy for the tournament! You want to win, don’t you? Better eat that stuff before it ends up in my stomach!" Sledge: "If you even lay a finger on my food, I’ll break it. Twice." Lance: "Bitter, aren’t we?" Sledge: "Maybe, but I don’t know. I just have a strange feeling that something is wrong. Not with me physically, but there is something coming up I’m not prepared for, but whatever it is, I need to get over it quickly. I want to take the GTT6 by storm." Lance: "Hey man, you will. You’re facing somebody who’s been out of the game for too long. He must be rusty. What’s his name, Sean Smoke?" Sledge: "Semore Smoke. And yes, he may be rusty, but I’m not going to take any chances. I’ll take him down and move to the next round. But he carried a name with him before his wrestling career went downhill. He’s quite the veteran. But I’m not really worried about him, it’s something else I just can’t seem to put my finger on." Lance: "I see, that’s weird. But if it helps, I met your old friend today. He said he will come to visit you later this or next week. He said that he still needed to take care of some business before he meets up with you." Sledge: "Friend? What friend?" Lance: "Err…He said his name was Matthew Glaze. Yeah, I’m pretty sure that’s it!" [Sledge’s eyes open up wide. He looks towards Lance as he’s just seen a ghost.] Sledge: "Matthew Glaze!?" [Sledge starts to look around him, looking for Matthew. He acts like he’s about to get killed and is acting all paranoid. Nobody is there at the diner except some customers and it’s dark outside, not to forget it’s raining so it’s hard to see whatever is outside.] Lance: "Yeah, he was a pretty nice guy. He had a scary voice though. But after a little talk, I found out he was a cool guy. He said he knew you very good and that you two go a long way back. He said that you haven’t seen each other for eleven years and now that he is in town, he wants to meet up. He looked pretty old though, somewhere in his forties." Sledge: "I’m not surprised by that. But do you know why he hasn’t come up to me personally?" Lance: "Oh, he said that he didn’t want to ruin your concentration for your first round match in the GTT6. He knew you entered the tournament and that this tournament means much to you. He wanted for you to get through the first round, so he didn’t want to bother you with a visit. He’s rooting for you to win as well." Sledge: "Lance, he already ruined my concentration by just being in town. That’s just some fucked up news to know. Don’t you find it strange that he talked to you? Don’t you find it strange that he knew that you knew me?" Lance: "Actually I don’t. Maybe he saw us in the gym, but never had the guts to talk to you. You’re more famous than you think, dude." Sledge: "Maybe I am, but that isn’t the point. Look Lance, Matt is a dangerous man. He may be old, but he’s dangerous. He’s got more connections than the damn president! You don’t know Matt. He may act nice, but I know him. I want you to stay away from him, and better lock yourself up at your place until the coast is clear. You never know what Matt is planning to do next and I’m not taking any chances!" Lance: "Dude, just chill down. I’m sure this Matt guy ain’t that bad, whatever he’s done. I’ll be fine, and so will you. Just chill and eat your hamburger." [Lance continues to eat his fries as Sledge looks at him dumbfounded. Just then, Sledge jumps up from his seat and raises his voice.] Sledge: "Look man, you’ve got to listen to me. You don’t know Matt. He might be watching us right now as we just sit here. We might be in danger, the both of us. He may give me space to battle in the GTT6 tournament, but that won’t last long. You just don’t know him well enough!" Lance: "Look man, you can yell all you want! We’ll both be fine, just sit down and eat your food. This Matt might be dangerous, but you can beat him up if you wanted to. He doesn’t look that tough. Just push this Matt guy out of your mind and start focussing on your upcoming matches. Lets take down one guy at a time, beginning with this Semore Smoke guy." [Sledge sits down, looking at Lance. Lance takes a drink from his glass of Cola.] Sledge: "Yeah, he gave me time for the first round. Maybe you’re right. Maybe I should focus on Semore first. Even Matt wanted me to get through the first round, then he probably meant it. He wants me to beat Semore Smoke, so let’s do that first." [Sledge looks out of the window, staring into the deep as the rain continues to drop. Lance is grabbing a fork and a knife to cut his steak.] Lance: "Yeah, that’s it dude. Calm down and beat the shit out of Semore Smoke in GTT6. This Matt Glaze comes later. But ummm…We’ve been talking all this time and you haven’t touched your food yet. Are you gonna finish that?" Sledge: "Yeah, don’t you worry…I will finish it…" [Lance shrugs his shoulders as the scene fades to black…] To Be Continued…
