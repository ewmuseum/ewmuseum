A Day In The Life

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 “Hello.” The phone calls had interrupted the packing of the Flying Vergomovs. They were no longer members of the Lights Out Wrestling roster. Things just hadn’t worked out. Constantino Zergolov held the phone up to his ear as Igor Arefiev looked on, eager to know if it was for him. The Flying Vergomovs were standing in their open plan living room. It was littered with brown boxes left, right and centre. Igor was standing over the coffee table with a suitcase lying open in front of him. On the remaining six inches of the table lay the Flying Vergomovs wrestling attire folded neatly. The contrasting colours of the red and blue were anomalies in another wise monotone apartment. “Hello. Is the Flying Vergomov there?” questioned the voice that awaited Constantino. The voice was female. She spoke very well and had a slight English sound to her voice. “Which one?” questioned Zergolov. The tone of his voice was not good. He didn’t seem too happy to be receiving the phone call. On second glance, it appeared Igor was in the same bitter mood. Something, apart from the contract termination was wrong. “There is more than one Flying Vergomov?” The voice is surprised by this development. She takes a second. Constantino can hear rustling of papers. Then there is silence. “Yeah, we are a tag team,” adds Constantino, fed up with the ‘long’ two seconds of silence. Igor looks on puzzled. He can only hear one end of the conversation. He looks down and the suitcase, and then at the costume. “Did a Flying Vergomov apply for the PTC cw2 tournament?” questions the woman as she returns to the conversation with Constantino. Her tone of voice makes her sound hopeful as though she is guessing. “Yeah,” is Constantino’s quick reply. “They did?” “Yeah,” Constantino nods but obviously doesn’t need too. Igor looks at him as he nods. Zergolov smiles a nervous embarrassed smile at his tag partner as he realises he was nodding to a person on the other end of the phone. Arefiev shakes his head in a moment of mixed shame and confusion. Zergolov shrugs. Zergolov can almost feel what the phone call is about. He can sense it. He’s going to get the play-in. Constantino Zergolov is going to appear in a singles tournament and not be overshadowed by Igor Arefiev. Constantino looked down to the brown that lay at his feet and rose to his shins. “Wrestling Awards” was scrawled on the box that was filled with newspaper sheets. Some awards shone brightly back at the Russian superstar. “Global Wrestling Summer Awards 2004. Best Rookie.” It wasn’t Constantino. It was Igor Arefiev. “MVP of the night. Master of the Mat 2004.” It wasn’t Constantino. It was Igor Arefiev. “Semi Finalist. HSW Total J Cup 2004.” You guessed it. It was Igor’s award. Constantino eyes his tag team partner. No awards that the Flying Vergomovs had won in the wrestling business belonged to Constantino and Constantino only. Igor had won a few singles accolades. Constantino had won none. “Is that Mr. Arefiev?” questioned the voice on the other end of the phone. Her voice seemed panicked, rushed and just a little out of breath. Constantino is silent. His eyes lock on the awards in the brown box. Then he eyes his tag team partner, folding the wrestling attire into the suitcase. “Fuck you!” snaps Constantino Zergolov. He clicks the hang up button on the phone and tosses it into the nearest armchair. The cursing has drawn Igor’s attention, who looks over at his best friend. “Who was that?” questions Arefiev as he stands to his feet and looks at Constantino first, then the phone and then back at his tag partner. “No one…” snaps Zergolov as he bounds past. ----------------------------------------------------------- No one understands it. Yeah, we are a team, but I can take that guy. It’s only Igor. I’m bigger than him. I’m stronger than him. I won all the awards in Warsaw and Moscow. Who was the first inductee to the Flying Vergomovs? Me. By two minutes, by still me! I am the highlight of this tag team, not Igor Arefiev. ----------------------------------------------------------- Constantino Zergolov stood across the room from his best friend and tag team partner. “What has he got that I don’t have? What makes him the bigger star of this tag team? What makes him the Shawn Michaels of the Flying Vergomovs?” Nothing. Constantino could not decide what it was. There was no factor that made Igor the bigger star; in fact he was an inch shorter. “Con, are you to help me pack?” And with that, Constantino remembered. It didn’t matter. He had just told the woman from PTC to fuck herself and their contracts with Lights Out Wrestling were gone. The Flying Vergomovs were dead weight. The Flying Vergomovs were flying home to Warsaw in the morning. ----------------------------------------------------------- I can’t get the plane. It felt so good to think it. Constantino Zergolov. PTC cw2 entrant. Screw Igor and his little Total J Cup showing. This is all mine! ----------------------------------------------------------- Igor Arefiev surveyed the remains of the flat; that he and his best friend had shared on their whirl wind tour of wrestling. The past year had been amazing. The bumps hurt and the chair shots were sore the morning after but the experience… The experience… Well it was… over. As much as it pained Igor to realise he would never be back there, it was true. He would never get back there. “Goodbye,” he mumbled. It had been easily the best year of his life, and there was no way it could continue. He turned, faced the door and walked out, leaving his keys on the telephone table. The Flying Vergomovs are dead. ----------------------------------------------------------- “I can’t go!” The screeching voice of a joyful Constantino Zergolov filled the air outside the Vergomovs’ old apartment. Igor Arefiev looked confused and puzzled as his tag team partner ran towards him. “I can’t go!” screamed Constantino. “Don’t bullshit me about being afraid of flying!” snapped Igor. He was not ready for any shit about leaving. Their wrestling careers were over. They should deal with it. He was. But he knew that Constantino never would. Igor knew his best friend inside out, and he knew Constantino would try everything to stay. “I’m not…” Constantino had stopped in front of Igor and was now out of breath. He panted for a while and then looked at his best friend. “c…w……2! I’m fighting!” “Seriously?” Constantino could only nod. He must have ran the whole city he looked so tired. “Yeah…” Constantino stood to his feet. His breath was more relaxed and normal. “I am taking over for some guy. I get to face Tsunami Oni….” “You may as well fly. Oni will destroy you. Just give up.” “No!” snaps Zergolov shaking his head. “I am fighting for my career and that means I will do anything I have to do… Trust me, not even Oni will stop me from getting someone to sign me up. Not even Oni..."
