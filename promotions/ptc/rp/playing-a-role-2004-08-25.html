Playing a Role

25 August 2004

Date: 2004-08-25 All he could think about was the tape. It could've almost qualified as a trance. Train was nearly standing as he leaned himself back against the high stool in his little dressing room. Train had felt the small tug on his skin as the adhesive of the strip of black take had first made contact a few inches below his right wrist and he'd started to pull, unrolling it along the short length from there, over the wrist to his hand. LL Cool J with is tag team partner Timberland pumped "Head Sprung" in the background through a decent little boom box with expanded bass Train had found in the room when he first arrived, the bouncing beat meant to amp Train up. Train had burned the cd the other day figuring he'd need something to get him in the zone...but now it was a non factor.... Train couldn't hear a thing. All he could think about was the tape. It was a little odd, because there'd been plenty to think about earlier. It had been a hell of a long day. Fact is the length of it started yesterday. It had been a long trip from Dallas to New York City. The Giovanni Rims people wanted to get started on their ad campaign for their designer chrome rims featuring the GW Platinum Champion started post haste. It was rather inconvenient considering it was the day before the PTC Survivor's Series event. That was going to be the biggest interpromotional event Train had ever taken part in. Even the "Crossfire" pay-per-view and it's it's hellacious main event, Team WMW vs. Team GW in a Elimination Hell in a Cell match hadn't been as big as what this was going to be...and that had been huge. But Train hadn't complained. What for? First off the company sent the ticket, first class all the way to NYC, not a dime out of his pocket. All those cats who complain about airline food had obviously never had the lobster. And with butter sause that featured just a hint of garlic what was to complain about? And the other cats that said they got too nervous to fly obviously did have have the 4 or 5 complimentary Crown and Cokes to the edge off on their side. Second, as per the contract, a photo shoot that would last about 2 or 3 hours where he'd likely get to spend the equivalent amount of timing running game on some fine ass models equaled a quick $25,000 dollars in his bank account. Naw, nothing to complain about. The whole thing had been just like he'd figured it would be. Train was hardly a giant, especially in this business. About every opponent he'd ever had was taller than him, but he'd never been in a room where every woman had been taller than him at 5'10". He wasn't sure if it had been all them or the fact that they were all wearing high heels, which essentially put them on their tip toes. But then again, with tight dresses of ever shade gripping their curvacious bodies, which came in about every color as well, who the hell cared? It damn sure wasn't Train. All some guys would likely see would be a reason to be a little insecure. All Train could see was several opportunities to go mountain climbing. As far as women went these girls were as tall as the Rockies. The second Train stepped in the room and all their heads turned to him with their beaming smiles and their bodies speaking the subtle language of attraction... turning their bodies to whatever angle they believed best showed off their features... brushing their hair back away from their faces... giving hugs when only handshakes would have been required... small, what they believed was unseen nibiling on their lower lips... Train knew, even if he had to by a grappling hook and some rope he had some peaks to climb. The whole thing had been easy. Just like he figured he spent most of his time conversating with the girls as the photographer set up for his shots in the large studio loft they had set up in. The area by the back drop glimmered with some serious hardware as Giovanni had set up a whole line of their best stuff. Train had to do a double take, checking dubs, dub dueces and 24"'s he hadn't even seen before. In between rolls of film the PA's took their job pretty seriously, shinging up the Platinum Title belt to a high gloss. Train had shined it pretty often since he'd had the belt, but he'd gotten a chuckle out of the process from them as they took one of those big, high speed deals to the belt. At first it had worried Train and he determined the first time he saw it that a scratch on the metal was gonna mean a boot in someone's ass, but it hadn't come to that in any way shape or form. The damn thing looked good. He determined then he'd have to head down to the Auto Zone when he had some free time he'd invest in one of his own. Models who spend so much time complaining how hard a job the shit was were full of shit. All Train had to do was stand there and do him. Hold the belt. Hold the chicks. Mean mug. It was simple. Hell they only shot for 2 hours before breaking 2 more hours for lunch. He spent half that time wining and dining 2 models. And it was ironic that they hadn't been too hungry in the resteraunt because the other hour he spent in the back of the complimentary limo that had been driving them around all the wanted to do was eat. Train had always considered himself a patron of the disadvantaged. He'd served both of them, Brizillian and Japanese as best he could recall, like a buffet...he gave them all they could eat. And then, to save the poor limo driver and his company the high cost of maintenance, Train gave them a complimentary test of their shocks. Train had been worried for a moment, not havign the right equipment for the job, but NY models were pretty smart and they carried all the proper pack of tools with them in their purses. The rest of the day was a breeze. Before he headed off the the airport the photographer let Train see the shot they wanted to use. It was Train stood straight on, his left boot up on one of the 24" bad boys. His massive torso and shoulder leaning somewhat forward with his muscular arms crossed at the wrist over his knee. Train clutched the fruit of his labor, the GW Platinum Championship tightly it's leather strap in hi right hand, letting it hang down prominently in front of him. The bulk of his dreadlocks cascade down off his shoulders as he chilled the air with an ice cold glance into the camera. The girls took their spot too, standing behind him in their black bathing suits their arms extended as they gripped his shoulders and arms. His upper body was uncovered revealing every ripple of muscle and the tatoos stood out boldly... The Ornate "S" he sported on each forearm and as well as the band with the cross in the middle that wrapped around his right bicept, the name of his mentor and trainer, written in cursive beneath the cross gave a firm testament to a life he lived long before wrestling came a long. They let him know the intent of the ad too. He dug it... apparently it would say "There are two ways you can get Platinum for yourself..." at the top of the ad. "Be smart... Just buy Giovanni Platinum." at the bottom. Definitely a productive day. The redeye out of New York hadn't been quite as nice as first class on the way their. It was only business class and the steak was a little tough, but train muddled through. However Train still found no reason to complain. Actually he found himself appreciating the step down. The fact of the matter was business class put him in the frame of mind he needed to be the more he thought about it. Jet was speeding off to his destination, straight to the city where history could well be made for him. The Survivor Series venue was only a few hours from where he hung in midair. Business class was perfect. Cuz that's exactly what this shit was gonna be... Business. This was going to give him the exposure he wanted. This was the kind of event that could take you from being your run of the mill "Superstar" to the fucking legend. Assuming you played the shit right. Being a world champion? It was an honor and all, but there are plenty of cats out there who can claim they been there, done that. Oh, but being THE Global Champion? There weren't too many cats out there that could talk all that. Of course more than all that... It was the biggest stage he could think of to do what he really wanted to do... The ultimate stage available to show up every other chump team GW who had the nerve to think they were more important than him. It wasn't like he liked it. Normally if he wanted to show these cats up the proper method of operation would be to put a foot in their ass. But in the Survivors Series he had to be a team player. And he would be...however being a team player didn't mean he had to sit back and play some back up role like he knew they had picked him to do. It didn't mean he just had to sit back and play second fiddle to the World and International Champions. Naw.... fuck all that. Train was gonna play his part for sure. No doubt Bubba McCoy wanted this to be a show case for his big boys. To show all the talent he had sporting the big gold in GW. He didn't have the balls to tell Train to his face, but he knew this was suppsosed to be their showcase. Train hoped it wouldn't be too embarrassing for anybody when he spent the entire match suplexing muthafuckas out of their boots, rattling more skulls and eliminating more sons of bitches than Leinad Soma and the all mighty Josh could ever conjure up in their best wet dream. Oh yeah, Train would play his part alright. HIS part. Train felt the agression boiling up in him the way it always did. It was the fuel had always driven him. Pushed him to limits he didn't even know he could reach. It was a good thing....for the most part. Unfortunately it made it hard to sleep. He couldn't manage more than a combined 3 or 4 hourse on the flight. The ension in his body always stirring him out of his slumber. Train could see why the called the flight the redeye as he glanced in the rear view mirror of his rented Escalade on the way to the hotel. Train could've taken a later flight, but he didn't want take any chances with hitting delays and not getting here on time. Not for this. It was too big to take the chance. It wasn't long before Train found himself in his large suite just staring up at the cieling from his queen size bed. His body was tired, but the agression... it had reached a high boil now. It was tired, but at the same time it was ready to go to war. Finally a braintorm. A quick call to the front desk of the Hyatt Regency and ten minutes later there was a knock at the door. Train answered it in the plush, terry cloth robe provided with the room. The petite redhead, dressed in her formfitting white pants and white Hyatt Regency polo shirt flashed her pretty smile at him as he opened up. She was small but she had no problem man handling the large case she carried into the room. Train offered to help, but it became obviously he likely would have just slowed the process down as she opened the case and pulled out her portable massage table in a quick flash. Amy was a smart girl. She relayed her trek through the technical school system on her way to her chiropractor degree and how this let her earn the cash to pay for it. She also had good taste. She recounted to Train the events of the past 4 Evolutions she'd viewed on HBO as she rubbed the warm, aromatic oils on Train's wide back, kneeding it like it were dough. Teh tension was definitely curbed as she worked her skills to their utmost, Train cracking jokes with her and flirting ever so subtlely as she did her job. Finally the curiousity had gotten to her. She inquired as to whether he had the Platinum Title with him. He let her no the belt was like the American Express card, he never left home without it. He didn't even bother to make her ask. He just hopped his oiled body off the table, covered only by a pair of boxers and headed over to his bag. One quick zip and a pull and there it was. She played it timid at first, asking permission to touch. simply slipped around behind her and snapped the belt into place around her slender waist. He thought even at it's tightest it might slide off her, but it actually rested rather comfortably on her sexy hips. She giggled, stating she was the champ now. Now that she had the belt he'd have to fight her for it. Train grinned. He moved back over to the bag and produced a small video camera. She looked puzzled as he turned it on and inquired what the camera was for. He smiled at her and replied that if he was goign to get into his fight in his hotel room with a little white girl that worked at the hotel he wanted proof she started it... "My Name ain't Kobe." he said grinning slyly at her. She could only giggle and shake her head at the less than politically correct comment. She grinned coyly and told the camera man she'd always been a troublemaker. A few more seconds of charm and the fight was on. Train had the opportunity to work on his mat based work, folding her up in every way imaginable and a few that weren't. However she wasn't just a defensive fighter, several time taking control of the match, exploding with her own brand of offense. Train was the professional, but like any good amatuer wrestler, she knew how to ride her opponent. Finally Train had to go UFC/MMA on her, pinning her to the mat and pounding her until she finally had to tap it out. An hour later it was all over. She conceded the belt back to Train and gave him a peck on the cheek as his real reward before packing her things and making an exit. Another quick phone call to the desk and the wake up call was set. It's just what Train had needed. Every guy knows... there's nothing like a good fight to take the tension right out of you. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The trip to the arena had been pretty smooth, no problems finding the place with the little map the hotel provided for him. Train was no stranger big crowds and arenas. No stranger to the spotlight. Even so, even he had to do a double take as he looked out at the oceans of cars in the lots and parking decks surrounding the arena. He couldn't help but notices the mass of humanity as the fans poured into the huge venue prior to the event. Yeah...it was primet time. Time to shine. The second Train stepped into the building the little PTC workers swarmed him, rattling off a million things they claimed he needed to know. The rules, the schedule. All Train wanted to know was where his dressing room was. By that time the pack of PA's had dwindled down to just one. The kid pointed him him down the hallway... "Team GW's dressing room is down that way to the left." he said. Train looked down the hallway.... It was begining to boil again. Here it goes. Time for him to be the good little GW boy...tow the company line... be Mr. Teamplayer.... Train reached into his pocket and produced a bill bearing the likeness of Ben Franklin. Train wanted his own dressing room. He didn't care where it was. What it looked like. He wanted his own space. The kid stared at the bill for a moment before sliding it out of Train's hand and into his pocket. He lead Train down a side hallway, the space cluttered with equipment and boxes. They finally arrived at the door at the end. The kid pushed it open revealing a small room. It's only piece of furniture was a tall stool and it's only luxury was a wide counter with a mirror above it, lined through lightbulbs that provide the only light. A medium sized boom box sat on the counter along with a little dust. Train quickly produced a $50 and holds it up. The kid grabs at it, but isn't quick enough to avoid having it snatched away. Train quickly informs him it's only his after he coves to get him for his match. The kid nodded and was quickly dismissed. Train stepped into the room... The tape encircled his fist. The crowd had been audible in the room before the music drowned them out, but still none of it meant anything now. Train could only focus on the task at hand. It was bubble, bubble, toil and trouble inside Train as he taped up. This was the only thing he coud focus on because it had to be perfect. When his fists weren't taped up right he knew every shot he delivered would sting a little. Oh, but when it was done right? He felt nothing? Not an ache, not a pain. When it was done right... He could pound. and pound... and Pound.... and POUND.... He'd never feel it. When it was right. He could throw shots all night and never stop. He'd never met the people he was about to put in a world of hurt, but he was still sending them on the trip. Train tore off the last piece of tape and smoothed out the frayed end. He stood and slowly moved to the mirrored. He stared coldly into his own reflection...all he felt was cold. He was almost ready. Train reared back and suddenly... his fist slammed into the mirror. Teh the cracks spread like a virus through the length of it before it all came apart, falling to the ground and any other surface available in an explosion of razor sharp glitter. The comotion settled, every shard finally coming to a rest. Train pulled up his fist and looked. Not a cut. Not a scratch. Not a hint of pain. Perfect.... There was suddenly a thumping at the door. "You're up man!" yelled the kid from the other side door. Train quickly turned and snatched to the Platinum Championship out of his back and hoisted over his shoulder. "Time to play the role." Train said to himself. Train whipped the door open and slid the 50 dollar bill into the chest pocket of the kid's shirt as he exited, storming down the hallway, every step echoing with one thing... un adulterated... unmidigated... Agression. It's Showtime.
