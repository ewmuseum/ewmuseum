Magical Hype

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Every year something comes along, something unexpected, something out of the ordinary, something that will make you stand up and say wow. That someone this year is Rich Rollins. But then GTT5 has a habit of doing that. GTT5 some of the best wrestling talent on the planet come together to face each other, to find out who is better, to see who is the best of the best. Finally we come to something that strikes true in ever tournament, there’s always someone that sneaks up with out anyone noticing and goes far. A lot of people say that this year that could be Ember. Ember who is he; everyone seems to like him, why what on earth has he done? To quote some teeny bobber from a well known website out there had this to say about Ember, "Ember is too damn good to be called an underdog…" Its obvious to me that this guy has serious mental problems or is lets blind. Now the guy he is facing in round one, none other than 4WW’s Andi the Magnificent. The magician, The Wizard the man that has participated in not one GTT but two, In his very first one as the person he was before he found his magical gift, ‘Ice Cold Andi’ he caused a upset then by beating a guy everyone thought would win a man called Scorpion of CCW. So Andi doesn’t have what it takes, well we shall see cause this man has done more, he thought against the people that said ‘Magic don’t belong in Wrestling.’ He won that battle. It took him just Ten weeks to gain a shot at Four Winds main title. One thing you can say about the Warlock is that he stands tall; he has proven beyond a doubt that when the odds are stacked against him, he performs better. So watch out for the true underdog Andi the Magnificent.
