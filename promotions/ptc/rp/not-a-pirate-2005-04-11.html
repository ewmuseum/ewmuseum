Not a Pirate?

11 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-11 JACK, YOU'RE NOT A PIRATE [Captain Jack Morgan, the "Killer Klown from Outer Space," and James von Doom are in a tavern in Tortuga that caters to their (well, besides James) 'culture' group. They are in the Leaky Penis, which also doubles as Madame Bissou's Little Whorehouse on the Island. The three of them are sitting at the bar drinking. Jack is drinking a Mug full of Rum, James is ironically drinking a Captain Morgan, and the KKFOS is drinking Blood on the Rocks. It's actually real blood, too. That's all the clown can drink. He needs it to survive in our atmosphere. They chug down their drinks and start laughing after setting their mugs down. James is wearing a silvery business suit, Jack has on his Captain Jack Sparrow costume, and the clown is in his usual clown suit.] Jack: "HA HA HA! Are we having fun yet, mates?" [The KKFOS nods his head. James smiles.] JV: "Yeah, mate. We are." KKFOS: "Drinkiando!" [James laughs it off.] JV: "You know, you're really fun Jack." [Jack gets excited.] Jack: "Really?" [James nods his head.] JV: "Yeah. Despite the fact that you're not a REAL pirate, you are a really fun guy to..." [Jack stops him right there.] Jack: "Ho...hold on there a minute, mare. 'Not a real pirate?'" [Everyone in the bar turns and gives James an angry look. He gulps and hesitantly looks around. He tugs on his collar slightly and looks to be sweating a little.] JV: "Yeah...you heard me, Jack." [Jack glares at him.] Jack: "How can you say that? I AM REAL PIRATE!" [James brushes it off.] JV: "Yeah, and I'm Brad Pitt." [James smacks him in the back of the head.] JV: "Dude, get back to reality here with the rest of us! You're NOT a pirate, you never will be a pirate, and your days of sailing the high seas are over you stupid, incompetent idiot! Hell, you and [motioning toward the KKFOS] that idiot in the clown suit [KKFOS growls in background] can't even win a Tag Title Shot in the PTC Tag Team Division, so what the hell makes you think that you even have a chance at surviving on the high seas let alone winning that Cruiserweight Tournament I entered you into?" [Jack looks befuddled.] Jack: "Cruiserweight tournament...?" [James slams his mug on the counter of the bar.] JV: "I'm out of here, dude." [James gets up from his stool at the bar and walks out. But before leaving, he turns and makes a mocking gesture toward Jack.] JV: "GARR~!" [James then turns and exits the tavern through the door. Jack turns and looks at the KKFOS.] Jack: "Am I really a pirate?" [The KKFOS incessantly nods his head.] KKFOS: "Piratiando!" [Jack then turns and motions for the bartender.] Jack: "Get me some more rum." Bartender: "Yes, Jack. Right away." [Jack is staring off into space in disbelief.] Jack: "Am I really..." [He acts like he's almost believing what James says.] Jack: "Not a pirate?" [Fade out.] * * * AM I REALLY NOT A PIRATE? A FEW HOURS LATER... [Jack is still sitting at the bar with a melancholy look on his face. There are five empty mugs of Rum sitting in front of him on the counter.] Jack: "Pirate..." [Jack belches loudly as the clown downs a midget appetizer to his right.] Jack: "BBBBBUUUUUURRRRPPPPPP!!!" [Jack shakes his hand.] Jack: "I'm not a pirate...I'm just a miscreant in a Halloween costume!" [The KKFOS shakes his head.] KKFOS: "Shando mihata zango nudai amni ata." [The bartender nods his head.] Bartender: "He's right, Jack. There's no need for you to get all worked up over this. You're a real pirate." [Jack looks up at him.] Jack: "Really?" [The bartender nods his head.] Bartender: "Yes. No need to worry about what that jerk said. Just go do some pirating to work off some steam." Jack: "Go pirating to work off some steam?" [The bartender nods his head.] Bartender: "Yeah, sure. Go rape and pillage some of the neighboring towns, steal some gold from Cortez, and go off and be a pirate!" Jack: "Uhhhh...ok." [Jack gets up from his stool and stumbles toward the bar entrance. The KKFOS moves his large, flabby arms in the air in a waving motion to get Jack's attention.] KKFOS: "Jackio! Jackio!" [Jack turns and groggily looks toward the clown sitting at the bar.] Jack: "What is it, mate?" KKFOS: "Wheraindo yousa glowring?" [Jack turns and looks to the fat, ugly pirate standing directly to his left.] Jack: "What the hell did he say, mate?" [The pirate shrugs his shoulders.] Pirate: "Dunno, mate. I don't understand Chinese." [Jack turns and gives the pirate a look.] Jack: "Chinese?" [The pirate nods his head.] Jack: "Yeah, man. He's speaking Chinese." [Jack turns and gives the clown a funny look.] Jack: "Chinese? Is he really speaking Chinese?" [The KKFOS shakes his head from the bar after downing another mug of Blood on the Rocks.] Jack: "Then what language are you speaking then, mate?" KKFOS: "Ashtriando." [Jack looks at the pirate standing next to him.] Jack: "Ashtray? Did he say he is speaking Ashtray?" [The pirate shrugs his shoulders. Jack gets mad and walks out the door as the KKFOS passes at the bar.] [Fade out.] * * * OUT TO PROVE THE DOUBTS WRONG A FEW MINUTES LATER... [Jack is roaming the cobblestone streets by the docks as the fog rolls in from the sea. He stumbles around and almost walks into a light pole. Jack looks around, confused and disoriented.] Jack: "What..." [Jack looks around.] Jack: "Where am I?" [Jack looks up into the misty fog that has covered the Port of Tortuga in the wee hours of the night.] Jack: "Am I really here? Am I really a pirate?" [Jack looks around at his pirate garb.] Jack: "I may be a pirate, I may not be but I'll show him. I'll show him and everybody else just how much of a pirate I am. I am not a fool in a Halloween costume. I am not just a pirate! Sure, I grew up in New York City and I didn't get this way until... [memory lapse] I don't know when, but dammit von Doom! I am a pirate! But I could be something greater but NO! [shakes his head] You had to go and ruin it with your big mouth! But I'm going to show you! I could be somebody! I could be a contender! I could be the next big star! But I never will be if I don't show you how much of pirate I really am!" [Jack storms off down the street, his feet clumping against the cobblestone as he power walks his way down to the wharf.] [Fade out.] * * * A KILL TO BE MADE... [At the same time Jack is storming off, James von Doom is standing in the shadowy corner of an alleyway in Tortuga with his laptop computer. The computer is an HP TC-1100 tablet laptop PC. Text can be entered via a stylus or a detachable keyboard that comes with it. James' prep for whomever he has been hired to kill is a PNY Attaché on a USB flash drive. James is looking at the intel data on his potential kill.] JV: "Interesting. I wonder where that idiot ran off to now?" [James is looking down at his laptop when some fatass pirate comes strolling over.] Fat Pirate: "Hey, that's a nice laptop you have there." [James gives him a look.] JV: "Really?" [The Fat Pirate nods his head.] Fat Pirate: "If you give it to me, I'll let you live you little SCUMBAG." [The Pirate tries to grab James' laptop but he pulls out his Heckler and Koch (HK) USP and plugs him with three shots to the sternum like it was nothing. The Fat Pirate falls over in a heap. His carcass hits the ground with a thud and James looks down at the fat, bloodied body like nothing happened at all before shaking his head.] JV: "I hate gluttony." [The camera then zooms in on James' fallen laptop, which is lying on the ground, and shows who his victim is on this little mission. The picture on the laptop is none other than Captain Jack Morgan. James grabs his laptop off the pavement and closes it. He puts on his Silhouette 8586 sunglasses and brushes off his suit.] JV: "Sorry Jack, but I always finish the job." [James then strolls out of the alleyway and looks around for a moment before walking off into the fog and out of view.] [Fade out.] * * * SHOULD I KILL MY FRIEND? AN HOUR LATER... [The scene opens in the wharf section. James has tracked Jack down and has been tailing him for three or four blocks. James has been hired to kill his friend by an unknown source for an undisclosed sum. James is very thorough in his trade. He's one of the best there is and a cold-blooded killer to boot. But there is one slight problem. James has never killed a friend before. James stops in the shadows behind a bait shop as Jack stumbles drunkenly toward a vessel called 'We Got Crabs' and boards it. There's a light on below decks and whole lot of noise coming from the ship. The fog makes it hard to see what's going on, so James pulls off his sunglasses and puts them up. The light above James swings from the sea breeze rolling in on this gloomy night.] JV: "The shadows may hide me and my motives but Jack, my man, I'm sorry." [James pulls out his Heckler and Koch (HK) USP and unloads his old cartridge before pulling out a new one. He reloads his deadly weapon of choice. He arms it and then conceals it back where he had it before.] JV: "Now, I must decide." [The weight of the world seems to be on James' shoulders, as well as a very large chip that's been sitting there since he was damn near decapitated in the ring back in 2003 by a gang of goons hired by a once trusted friend and James' ass kissing replacement. James holds his hands out in front of him.] JV: [looking at his left hand] "Should I kill my friend..." [James then looks at his right hand.] JV: "Or should I let him live?" [James looks at each of his hands slightly for a few seconds, alternating between them before getting an evil grin on his face.] JV: "That's it Jack. Time to die, fucker." [Fade Out.] * * * SOME KILLS..BUT NOT THE RIGHT ONES MOMENTS LATER... [James comes out of the shadows and strolls casually down to the wharf casually as to not arouse suspicion. He heads down the pier to the 'We Got Crabs' and quickly looks around before boarding the ship. James then peers down below deck and hears a bunch of pirates partying.] JV: "Fuck..." [James climbs down below deck and peers around. Jack then notices him and stumbles over.] Jack: "Hello mate!" [Jack belches and then puts his arm around James.] Jack: "And welcome to the party!" [James looks at him funny.] JV: "Yeah...I can see that." Jack: "Now, these are my mates." [Jack motions around.] Jack: "We be getting ready to set sail, mates." [James looks around. There are twelve gnarly, nasty, ugly, and mean looking pirates drunk as hell standing and sitting right in front of him on or around a table covered with food. He scans them all quickly with his eyes, trying to figure out how armed they are and which ones are the most dangerous. The fattest one, the one holding the giant keg of Beer, speaks first.] Fat Pirate: "Yeah. We be setting sail but not you Jack." [Jack looks shocked. He removes his arm from around James.] Jack: "Oh really?" [The fat pirate nods his head.] Fat Pirate: "Yes mate. You and your [motioning toward James] landlubber little friend here aren't going anywhere." [The pirates laugh it off. Jack is pissed.] Jack: "Says who? You fat boy?" Fat Pirate: "YEAH!" [The fat pirate then socks Jack in the jaw with his giant fist. Jack turns and falls to the deck unconscious.] JV: "JACK!" [James quickly grabs Jack and puts his over his shoulder. The pirates laugh it off as James carries Jack up to the top deck and then off the ship onto the pier where he sets him down. James then turns and looks angrily toward the ship. Below deck, the pirates are still partying as evil dramatic music plays and James is back standing at the end of the room looking in on them. The fat pirate notices him.] Fat Pirate: "HEY! I THOUGHT WE TOLD YOU TO LEAVE!" [James smiles evilly.] JV: "Oh really? You assholes told me to leave?" Fat Pirate: "Yeah! You and you're drunken friend. So what're you doing back here?" [James' smile quickly fades away.] JV: "Collecting souls for Davey Jones' locker." Fat Pirate: "Huh?" [James then pulls out his Heckler and Koch (HK) USP and starts plugging the pirates as the view stays trained on him. He kills all of them within a matter of moments like it was nothing. The gunshots echo throughout the wharf area as James runs out of ammunition. He stops to reload before looking down at the carcasses all over the room and realizing that all the pirates are dead. James smiles hideously before exiting the ship like nothing happened. Once on the top deck, he looks around quickly before leaving the ship fast. He retrieves Jack by picking him up and putting his arm around him to help him out of there. He then tries waking him up.] JV: "Come on Jack! Wake up!" [Jack groggily comes back into reality.] Jack: [looking around] "Huh? What happened? Where's the ship?" JV: [moving as fast as he can] "We gotta move buddy. We gotta get out of here!" Jack: "Uhhhh...ok." [Jack has no idea what is going on as James carries him off into the shadows of Tortuga's back alleys before the police arrive as the scene fades out.] [Cut.]
