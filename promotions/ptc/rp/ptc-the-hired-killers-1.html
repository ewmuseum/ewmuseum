The Hired Killers

11 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-[Prologue]- It was only a short while ago that Fathom secured his dream and gained revenge on Tristan Belmont in a horrific match that saw Fathom come out on top and win the PTC Extreme Championship. Since this time, Fathom has found himself on the top of the proverbial mountain in PrimeTime Central as that one win has secured his spot in the hallowed halls of PTC, placing his name near those such as Maelgrim, Donny Diamond and the Bum Boozler... well, near some good names I guess you could say. But soon, even with all of the adulation and great amounts of attention received, people began to get jealous of him. Evil people who have set out for one goal and that is to try and rid the PTC of Fathom and, in turn, handing over the belt to the next challenger Mike Marvelous. Only few people know of these plans and none of them involve Fathom or PTC owner Argyle. For they have snuck into the world of PTC under hidden names and other identities as to not be revealed and enter through a federation that has quickly made a namesake for themselves here, known as XWA, or as the longer term, the Xtreme Wrestling Alliance. Will Fathom survive the onslaught of the XWA? Or will he become nothing but a mere name spread into the dirt and allow Mike Marvelous to be the next PTC Extreme Champion? Only time can truly tell. -[Chapter One: Innocence]- All of the PTC cameras are now off of their hottest star. He steps into the dimly-lit showers with nothing more on than a white towel as sweat continues to drip off of his tanned body as it gets entangled through the hair that is on his chest. Still feigning for air after the marathon match with an EWA superstar that went on for an amazing thirty-seven minutes, Fathom disrobes the towel off of him and turns the water on as the first few sprinkles of lukewarm water hits his body and soothes the aches and pains suffered minutes ago outside in the ring. He grasps the small metal bin that is positioned midway up the showers rod and holds his hands in there for many seconds as he fidgets around for something until it reappears with a bar of soap. With this, he begins lathering himself up while raising his head into the air and allowing the water to hit his neck where an apparent cut, no longer then a mere two inches, continues streaming blood as it drips down his body, the blood becoming watered down into a very faint red as Fathom continues with the "Norman Bates" shower. His eyes are closed as he continues to wash himself with the soap. He lets the waters soothing sounds play through his ears while it hits his body and rotates his neck to loosen it up with the water hitting it with a massaging feeling. The dim lights are not too hard on his eyes as no apparent light tries to bleed in through his eyelids and makes for an overly enjoyable moment that is tarred when Fathom hears something hit off of the wall. Quickly, Fathom's eyes open and he begins to look around confused as there doesn't appear to be anyone inside of the room. This is truly confusing to him as he could have swore he heard a noise but instead, he goes back to washing himself as his brown dreadlocks slowly transcend down his back and look like real hair once again. They will need to be attended to before the naps that lock them together lose their hold. Fathom knows this but the intensity of the match has injured him so much that he just wants to have a restful moment that will allow his injuries to heal themselves up. But this good moment is once again ruined as another sound reverberates off of the walls and into Fathom's eardrums as he opens his eyes and looks around. Finally figuring that someone is around and messing with him, he drops the soap back into the bin and lets the water wash the remains of it from his body as he begins to call out to find who is there. "Hello?", he asks, trying to get a reply but nothing comes. "Hello?" There is no answer and it makes him slightly unhappy. Somebody is ruining a truly great moment and even if this is some sort of rib, it shouldn't be done right after as long a match as he just had, there is some lines that just can't be crossed. He decides that he should now use his detective skills to figure out who this man is as he grabs onto the faucet and turns it clock-wise to turn the water off and grabs his towel, wrapping it around his naked body and walking back into the locker rooms and out of the dimly-lit area. The dressing room is silent, so silent that you could drop a pin. But there is something about this silence that worries him. It is just after a huge show by the EWA and nobody is around? Especially around the locker room of one of their top stars? This shouldn't be happening and he knows it. Still in his towel, he steps through the empty room towards the door as he quickly reaches the handle and tries to pull it open but something has jammed the door. Being pretty sure that he didn't do this, he turns around and looks inside of the room before running over to his locker and dropping the towel as he picks up his underwear and shorts, simultaneously putting them on before dropping down and picking up his wrestling boots to get them back on. Now partly clothed, he stands to his full 6'6" stature and stares around the awkwardly silent room as if to find something inside, hoping for an answer to all of this when he once again hears that sound of metallic hitting the wall inside of the shower. He slowly begins to go along the wall towards the entrance to the shower as he looks inside to see a man standing against the wall tapping a coin against the wall. Smiling, he notices Fathom looking in and stands up to great him. "Well hello", says the man as he begins to walk towards Fathom. "I was wondering when you'd notice me." Fathom brings his head up and to the left as he stares at the man curiously as if he recognizes him. Of course, he does, this man is a wrestler but from where? Fathom stands his ground by stepping into the shower with the other man as now the two stand along the tiled floor staring at each other. "That's right", he says, now remembering. "Luke, from the XWA, right? What are you doing in the EWA?" The man smiles, realizing that Fathom recognizes him. Fathom is still confused however as the EWA is in England and the XWA is in Florida, so what is going on? Luke takes another step so that he is now in front of Fathom. He puts his hand on Fathom's bare left shoulder with a smile on his face. "I'm glad you recognize me, such a big star you are.", he says, his smiling face showing Fathom that there is no harm. "I just came to tell you that the PTC has made your match with Four Winds Wrestling's Mike Marvelous and it will be on the next edition of WWA Fiesta. I just thought I'd come down here and pass that along to you." Fathom continues to stare dumbfounded as that truly didn't answer the question he just asked. Luke's hands begins to squeeze down a little harder on Fathom's shoulder, so much so that it actually begins to hurt as Fathom tries to swing his arm up to knock Luke's arm away from his shoulder but Luke intercepts Fathom's hand and slaps it away as a sneer appears on his face. Everybody has known about Fathom's hurt shoulder since the day it occured in 360 Wrestling from the result of a horrible bump during his match with Pola Bare one night and Luke is capitalizing on this situation. "What... the... hell?", asks Fathom, continuing to try and struggle out. Fathom finally uses both arms in an upward swinging motion to break Luke's grip. Luke stumbles back as fire begins to fill Fathom's eyes, quickly he jumps and spears Luke to the ground and begins to deliver left and rights to Luke's face which is now wearing the crimson mask as Fathom yells at him. "What the hell do you want from me? Huh?" he asks through heat grunts. "What?" Luke continues to try and block as Fathom stands up and grabs the 6'3" red-haired man and throws his head into the tiled wall of the shower as Luke grabs onto his head as he falls to the ground unconscious. Fathom steps over his body and runs out to the dressing room where he picks up his black "EWA" shirt and charges at the door to break the top part open as he notices that a rope was tied onto the latch on the other side and wrapped around a production truck to keep him in but this doesn't worry him right now as he leaps through the opening in the door and looks around as two hefty looking men point over at him and begin to run in a sense towards him but he is able to run a little faster as he makes his way back into the arena as he notices that the last match is still going on. He can hear the fat footsteps of the men behind him as he quickly runs into the crowd and escapes through them although it is hard not to notice where he is as all of the fans make it a point to show where he is at by all screaming at whatever point he is at but nonetheless is able to escape through one of the ports into the hallways of the arena. -[Chapter Two: Be Aware]- Fathom finds himself walking through the corridors of the building with paranoia piercing his brain, causing him to continue to look back a lot to see if anybody is behind him but sees nobody. He turns back and locates a phone that is nearby a concession stand as he runs over to it and dials numbers, forgetting to put the money in. The operator helpfully points this out to him as he locates some english pounds in his pockets and drops them into the slit for them in the receiver as he impatiently waits for an answer until it finally comes in the voice of a PrimeTime Central assistant. "Hello, Primetime Central?" she says, perkily. "How may I help you?" Fathom pants, out of breath as he tries to locate his voice. Through breathes, he tries to speak to her. "Argyle", he says. "I need... to talk... to Argyle." There is a short silence as the sound of typing fills the receiver of the phone. Fathom waits but very impatiently as he peers around to try and spot any of these XWA cronies, still not sure why they are doing this to him. "Argyle isn't in right now, he is at a meeting but he will be back in about an hour. Would you like to leave a message?", she asks, still sounding very happy. Fathom continues to breath heavy as he finally spots the two rotund men exitting into the corridors near him as he speaks into the phone with a near silent voice. "Tell Argyle that the XWA, the new PTC fed, they are trying to kill me. Tell him and get him to get me back into the U.S. as soon as possible.", he says before hanging up on the woman and trying to casually walk away. He begins towards the mounds of people in the concession stand only to disappear, leaving the men to look around for him. But all wonder is quickly gone when a hord of fans begin cheering, pinpointing exactly where he is at. The fat men's feet quickly shuffle through the crowd as Fathom notices a door and makes a quick exit, flying around fans and dodging objects as if he were Clinton Portis in a mad dash for the end zone. Once out the door, he peers around for his car as he realizes that time could slowly be ticking down until the men catch up to him. He now has figured out the downside to valet parking because of this but soon remembers his alarm as he pulls his keys from his left pocket and begins running down the stairs hitting his alarm but it isn't until he hits the first rows of cars that a loud beeping noise comes from a green Honda Element thats lights flash off and on as he views it and then hits the 'unlock' button to get it to stop sounding as to not draw attention from the people already looking for him. After running and dodging around cars, he finally gets to his which is very close to the back and, coincidentally, the exit of the parking lot as Fathom darts to the front, drivers-side door and slides in, looking around as he is out of breath. He looks around and sees that the two fat men are still standing up at the top of the stairs dumbfounded as Fathom begins to stick the key into the ignition but stops before actually sliding it in as he begins to pound the edge of the key against the ignition's metallic surrounding trying to grasp the entire situation. "My god", he says, confused beyond belief. "What the hell is going on?" And then it hits him what is going on when he recollects his upcoming match with Mike Marvelous. 4WW's answer to competition, little at that. Could Mike have sent these XWA brutes after him in an attempt to maim Fathom before the match? Could Marvelous have been too scared to go one-on-one with the Inspiration? "It's settled then", says Fathom, now growing angry. "First I go and destroy these XWA punks and then I will injure Marvelous beyond all fixing. He wants a Lion's Den match? Then just how will it feel when the lion eats him alive." Fathom sticks the key into the ignition and starts the car as he speeds off out through the exit as a closer look shows that a bucket is stuck to the back of Fathom's car and leaking green paint out of it as another car, a 2000 Lexis, exits along with Fathom's.
