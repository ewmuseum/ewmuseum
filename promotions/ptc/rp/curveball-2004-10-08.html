Curveball

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 "Don't think about it" His black Nike clad feet kept pounding the ground below him, step after step in seemingly continuous motion, a small whirlwind of dust left in his tracks; a miniscule natural disaster for the ants and other insects who probably saw his passing as the end of their World, their very own hurricane Jeanne blowing through on the heels of a celestial visitation. Nothing else mattered except for the constant momentum of his running form; a fine line between complete loss of balance as his body was thrown forward only to be saved at the last moment in a never ending cycle as he pushed for more speed. He had to be beyond good, beyond better, beyond exceptio ... "Don't think about it" Each time his feet landed on the hard surface of the asphalt his muscled calves tensed, contracting as they absorbed the impact. Being gifted, blessed, with natural grace and ability had always been an advantage but not one that was taken lightly. What the Gods had given, man improved on. Hour after hour, day after day, months turning into years, a regime of training had taken the raw physicality and turned it into the closest thing to perfection that there could be this side of the after-life. A toned body with strength enough to make an Olympic weightlifter jealous; the agility of a gymnast and the stamina of a long distance runner. All this and more had helped make him what he was, all this and more had helped prepare him for what was to come. A World class wrestler about to face another World class wres ... "Don't think about it" With a rhythm that could have been set by the mind of the best Swiss watchmaker, his arms swung back and forth as they helped the overall motion of his body. Propelled through the air, his feet now seemingly only skimming the ground, he picked up speed as his arms churned away in counter-measure to his legs. His stomach muscles were taut under the tight white t-shirt that covered his upper body, the definition of the defined six-pack clearly visible as the sweat soaked material clung to his skin like a needy lover. His chest expanded as oxygen, badly needed, filled his lungs in rapid breaths to fuel the effort that the sprint was taking on his body; worthy of a one hundred meter champion he pushed himself ever onwards, further and faster, reaching for the finish ... reaching for the white tape ... reaching for the final, the titl ... "Don't think about it" A dark brown ponytail, the hair tightly bound with only little wisps escaping the constraints, bounced down along his back swinging from side to side across his spine in time with every stride. Faint traces of the original auburn colour shone through the hair now darkened with moisture, nature becoming the perfect hair dye. Green eyes, normally so piercing with their vibrant intensity, almost peer out from under hooded lids, clouded and haunted. Small tight lines crease the surface of the tanned and bronzed skin at the outside corner of the eyes, out of place on the youthful face that is normally only broken by a carefree smile. The frown above the eyes broke the forehead neatly into three areas, just as markedly out of place. Yet every second brought another breath, every breath brought another stride, and with every stride more distance was put between the runner and that which he ran from. Eyes focussed on nothing but the horizon he ran on and on, pushing his body to it's limits and beyond, knowing as he did so that for as long as he ran his totality would be consumed by that one task. Nothing else mattered, nothing else would intrude, thoughts of the future would be firmly locked away behind the momentum of his body and the sound of his own heart beating. As the further he ran the more distance he put between himself and the one thing that he didn't want to ... couldn't ... face. As long as he ran he didn't have to think about the future, didn't have to think about tomorrow, didn't have to think about ... "Don't think about it" The words came out of his mouth like bullets from a revolver. Fast and furious. Each word broken down into its component syllables and spat out from between clenched teeth as if the very act of saying them would somehow ensure that the desire behind them was fulfilled. A vain hope that somehow a self-fulfilling prophecy was achievable. So instead of thinking, instead of allowing himself the chance to think, he ran. Jay Phoenix ran ... and he kept on running. As the familiar white-bricked building that he called home came into view it appeared that he was not going to stop, but instead propel himself bodily into it ... the immovable object meeting the unstoppable force. Mercifully however the meeting of flesh and brick at speed did not come to pass as at the last possible moment Phoenix stopped running, all forward momentum halted as he leant over, panting, as he reached out and placed one hand flat against the wall of his house, trying to catch his breath. As his breath came back under control, his panting lessening, he reached to a buckle at his waist and unclasped it. Reaching up to his shoulders he pulled at two straps there and breathed a heavy sigh of relief as a small rucksack dropped from his back to land on the ground at his feet. A groan escaped his mouth as he stretched his body, arching his back as cartilage audibly popped. The aches and pains in his protesting body in recompense for the punishment that the arduous ten mile run had put him through were a welcome diversion from the one thought that had been haunting his every waking ... and quite a few sleeping ... moment. Ever since he had soundly beaten the man known as Tex Chatanooga to progress in the tournament; ever since he had found out that he would be facing ... "Don't think abou ..." "Talking to yourself Jay? Not the best way to prepare for the third round of the tournament, but considering the fact that you are facing Dave Hurst, a man not exactly known for his tight grasp on reality, perhaps a little insanity of your own IS the way to go!" Standing up straight and spinning around in surprise and shock, Phoenix glared at the grinning face of the man who had interrupted what had become a personal mantra that day. Oblivious to his transgression Rick James, Phoenix's boyhood friend, continued to laugh to himself. "Seriously though Jay, it is good to see that you are taking this upcoming match seriously and stepping up the training. No point taking Dave lightl..." Rick stated, being cut off abruptly as Phoenix reached down and lifted the rucksack up in one hand. His forearm muscles tensed as he threw the rucksack towards Rick who automatically reached out his hands to catch it. "Shut up Rick!" Phoenix growled at his friend before storming past him and vanishing around the corner of the house. Grabbing the rucksack as it hit him, Rick was bent nearly double as the air exploded from his body in a loud splutter of shock. Sitting down on his backside, legs splayed open, Rick held onto the bag as he gasped like a fish out of water, trying to get his breathing under control. Leaning over the bag he held it tightly as his complexion returned to it's normal colour, the red cheeks fading as he realised that he could breathe again. A look of perplexity played over his face as he stared down at the small rucksack that had knocked him off his feet. Still seated on the ground he opened the top of the bag and glanced inside, his face incredulous as he discovered the contents. With some effort he held the rucksack upside-down and watched, eyes wide open, as a rain of rocks fell from within to land in a loose pile in front of him. "Un-frikkin-believable" he muttered to himself as he discarded the now empty rucksack. Getting back to his feet Rick dusted of his hands, wiping them on the front of his faded blue Levis. Giving a disgusted kick at the pile of rocks, his gaze wandered to the corner where Phoenix had disappeared before he made his own way around the wall himself. "Hey Jay have you taken up geology or have you REALLY lost it now? I mean I can understand that you like to try and figure out how your opponents think, but c'mon man even Hurst doesn't take his pet rocks out for a jog!" Rick called out as he turned the corner. "I told you to shut up Rick!" Phoenix panted from his prone position on the grass a few feet in front of Rick. With his knees bent Phoenix brought his upper body up so that his chest nearly hit his own knees as he performed sit-up after sit-up, the effort of crunching his abdominal muscles very evident on his pained face. A look of concern crept across Rick's face as he hunkered down beside Phoenix, his feet flat on the ground as he bent his legs almost as if he was sitting on an invisible stool. Running his hand through his longish blonde hair Rick composed himself, trying to get his thoughts in order as he watched Phoenix perform his crunches with a fury. "So Jay, wanna tell me what is bugging you?" Rick asked gently. Without answering Phoenix suddenly sat fully upright before spinning around and laying flat on the ground. Rick had to move fast to get out of the way, Phoenix's feet nearly catching him in the face as he threw his body into rapid fire press-ups. Grunting with effort Phoenix looked like a man on a mission, a perpetual motion machine, as he brought his face down to within an inches of the ground before pushing himself back up to his arms full extension again. "Nothing Rick, just drop it" he gasped, short of breath. "Don't give me that crap Jay I know you far too well!" retorted Rick as he reached out to lay a restraining hand on Phoenix's back, stopping him in mid press-up. With a groan Phoenix lay prone on the ground, his breathing heavy, before rolling onto his back and resting with his right forearm over his face. With his eyes masked from the sunlight streaming down on his sweat streaked visage Phoenix allowed his breathing to return to normal. "So ... are you going to talk to me?" Rick prompted gently. With a low sigh Phoenix uncovered his face as he levered himself up from the ground. Brushing some loose strands of damp hair away from his face he looked into the concerned face of his friend before turning away hurriedly, seemingly wiping grass and dirt away from his legs. "There is nothing to talk about Rick" he said with his face still partially turned away. Walking back towards the house he followed one wall around the corner and out of sight. With an incredulous look on his face Rick watched him walk away before coming to his senses and following him, his face beginning to show signs of anger. Turning the corner himself he watched as Phoenix grabbed a set of protective gloves from the base of a chain hung kick bag. Taking a deep breath Phoenix began to unload combinations of punches at the bag, the chain at the top and bottom stopping it from moving too far, but the sheer force of his blows still causing it to move as much as the chain's leeway allowed with each blow. "This is crap Jay, you don't act like this and you certainly don't talk to me like this when there is nothing wrong!" Rick accused his friend as his voice raised a couple of decibels. "Well maybe I just don't want to talk about it right now Rick ... maybe I don't want to think about it right now either" Phoenix replied between two vicious punches to the bag, not taking his eyes of his target. "Well 'maybe' I don't give a damn what you want right now Jay, because there is sure as Hell something bothering you and I know you too well remember ... I KNOW that you bottle things up inside" stormed Rick as he moved around the kick bag so that he could stare at his friend; his voice softened, anger draining away as he saw the forlorn expression on Phoenix's face. "It is stupid Rick, ok, it is stupid and I FEEL stupid for thinking this way which is why I don't want to talk about it!" Phoenix suddenly blurted out, his eyes blazing with anger that seemed to be more directed at himself than at Rick. "The only thing stupid here Jay is you NOT telling me what is going on ... I am not a damn mind-reader you know so how am I meant to understand this?" he pointed out, arms folded across his chest as he stood definitely firm. "You are not going to just let this go are you Rick?" Phoenix asked quietly between punches. "Nope, so you may as well save us both some time and just tell me" Rick replied with a small smile. Not looking directly at his friend, instead focussing his gaze on the canvas of the kick-bag, Phoenix settled into a rhythm of punching, his hands weaving a continuous pattern of blows as he started to speak ... half to himself and halt to Rick. Almost as if floodgates had opened words tumbled out of his mouth, seemingly beyond his conscious ability to control. "It's a curveball Rick, it is as simple and confusing as that, life has thrown me a curveball. A few months ago I announced I was taking a break from active competition ... maybe even announced my retirement because I wasn't sure that I would ever come back ... and instead threw myself into reopening my own fed, the PWW. I wanted it to be a place where I could leave my mark, a place where I could help bring in the next generation of wrestlers" he started as his voice took on an almost musical tone as he kept in time with the punching. "I mean I had done pretty much everything I had wanted to do in my career, I had wrestled for some of the biggest and best federations around, I had won titles around the World, I was known and respected in the industry and my by peers ..." his voice faded for a few seconds as he channelled his emotion into a series of particularly hard blows, causing Rick to take a small step backwards. Oblivious to his surroundings now Phoenix continued. " ... or so I thought. I took a closer look at my so-called glory days though, I took a walk down memory lane, leaving the rose-coloured glasses behind me, and do you know what I saw? I didn't see the titles, I didn't see the successes ... I saw the failures. I saw me leaving PWC with a knee injury and a smiling Ron patting me on the back as he said goodbye ... I saw my original stint in PCW where Ron waved me out of the door, grinning all the time ... I saw my second stint in PCW where I wrestled against a retarded teddy bear and a failed sumo wrestler while others took the glory road of the World title. I saw the times that I had wasted,” he continued. Rick swallowed heavily, about to interrupt and then thinking better of it and instead waiting quietly as the out rush of emotion from Phoenix continued. “I saw everything that I had once taken pride in in a different light and suddenly none of it mattered in comparison to the milestones that stood out in stark relief in front of my eyes … my failings in both the PCW and the PWC … my failings in running the PWW … and my failing at the greatest hurdle I had ever reached. The inaugural GTT” Phoenix’s voice faded out as his punches became nothing more than repetitive motion, no real force or emotion behind them. “C’mon Jay none of those things were your fault, especially not against Vamp …” Rick tried to point out; cut off as Phoenix interrupted. “Fault or not they all haunted me though, especially the GTT, especially getting so close to real greatness, so close to making history but instead only being remembered … if remembered at all … as the man who came second, the man who nearly won, the man who got beaten by the cheater! Someone even pointed out that for me to lose against a cheater must have hurt … and it did … it DID!” Phoenix almost snarled through gritted teeth. “So is that what this is all about then Jay … you are worried about failing again, about losing this match … afraid that Dave will beat you?” Rick asked gently. For the first time in the conversation Phoenix stopped moving completely, his punch stopped in mid-flow, looking almost as if someone has taken a photo of the moment and framed it for posterity. For a few seconds neither Rick nor Phoenix himself moved, barely even breathing. Then the silence … and moment … was broken in a very unexpected fashion; Phoenix burst out laughing. “God I needed that Rick, thanks!” he said as he got his laughter under control, leaning forward to finally rest his weary body, both hands placed on the kick-bag as he took a deep breath. “I think that you have got things a little confused somewhere Rick. I said that life had thrown me a curveball and because of that I looked back on everything that I had done previously, and some of that was to think about every time I had failed … but those failings are not the be all and end all of who I was, they are simply part of who grew to be … who I AM!” Phoenix pointed out, wiping sweat from his eyes with the back of his gloved hand. “Every time I was knocked down I got back up again, every time I got knocked of the mountain I simply climbed it once more. I reached a crossroads in my life when I took that break and I decided that there was only two things that I could do. Quit or fight. I chose to fight. I got back in the ring at the WrestleGod tournament and I walked away from it undefeated … not the champion but not the loser either” he continued. “ … and you were there when I decided to take Argyle’s invitation and enter the GTT. Four years after I stumbled at the final hurdle, four years after I went past one hundred and twenty six other men, four years after Vampir Nosferatu STOLE the title of inaugural champion from me … I got to try again, I got a second chance!” Phoenix’s voice raised in tone as emotion began to shine through. “… and you ask if I am worried or afraid that Dave is going to beat me … that Dave is going to take my chance away from me? That never even entered my head Rick, I didn’t even consider it for one single, solitary second. On his best day Dave is a good wrestler, sometimes even a great wrestler” a flicker of his trademark smile played across Phoenix’s face. “ … but even on his best day he wouldn’t have a chance against me one on one in the ring. That is MY home Rick, that is MY World, and inside the ropes he is nothing but a visitor on a day pass, soon to leave. All ego aside he may be good, he may even be exceptional, but he simply isn’t good enough … not to beat me anyway!” Phoenix trailed off with a sigh, resting his head against the kick-bag and wrapping his arms around it as if it is all that lies between him standing and falling over. “I don’t understand Jay, what IS the problem then?” Rick asked in obvious confusion. “It is simple Rick, in order to achieve my dream of going all the way in this tournament, in order to get the redemption that I hunger for I have to destroy Dave’s own dreams” Phoenix said softly, beginning to punch the bag again with strike after strike … the intensity of the blows increasing with each and every punch. “ … I have to do the ONE thing that I never thought that I would have to do, the one thing that I am not sure that I even WANT to do … I have to beat the one sort of wrestler I have never faced before” he muttered, almost inaudibly causing Rick to lean forward to hear the words as they trailed off. “What is that Jay … what IS the problem?” Rick asked just as quietly. Instead of answering Phoenix suddenly threw his head back and gave a guttural scream, emptying all of his despair, frustration and emotion out in one shriek. At the same time he drew back his arm and threw a last punch at the kick-bag, enough force behind it to snap the chain the held the bag aloft. As it went flying to the ground … barely missing Rick who stepped back in shock … Phoenix suddenly looked directly at his oldest friend, staring into his pale blues eyes, his own piercing green eyes filled with pain, clouded with moisture as he replied in a choked voice. “To win … and I WILL win Rick … I have to beat my friend!”
