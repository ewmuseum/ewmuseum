Date: 2004-09-16 "James, you made bail." The officer spoke in a forceful, but cold and detached tone as he grumbled his words in Train's direction. Train had been sitting in the holding tank of the Dallas/Ft. Worth Police Department since late last night. It had given him plenty of time to think. To remember all the things he'd forgotten about this life...or, more accurately, "The Life". That was the euphamism men who lived outside the boundaries of the law used to describe there livelihoods. It occured to him that since walking away from the street game he'd been battling a serious case of forgetfulness about who and what he really was. Of course, it might not have been forgetfulness as much as wishful thinking. He didnt' always show it, but Train was an idealist at heart. Wrestling was supposed to be an escape for all this, but it had become more and more obvious to him over the last few weeks that fairly tale escape from it all was just a pipe dream. McNasty. The son of a bitch had pushed too hard. He'd pushed so hard in fact that it knocked Train out of his dream state and right back into The Life. Turned him fromt the man he wanted to become back to the one he'd been. It had been unwise for McNasty and unlucky for Train. Now Train was forced to sit here reminiscing on memories he'd been happy to forget. The holding cells. Didn't matter what precinct they were in, they were all the same... wreaking off urine, b.o. and just a hint of vomit thrown in for ambiance. The sound of metal grinding on metal as the cell door slides shut behind you...the loud, metallic pop as the lock snaps shut. Train knew the "Iron Circle" wasn't going to be pretty for anyone. 20 cars, formed into a circle, all headlights facing the wide open space in the center of the circle. All somewhere outside the arena...didn't matter where... Just as long as it meant there'd be no interference....no one, or not enough people to stop Train from ending it once and for all. There would be 2 canvases... one made of concrete or asphalt, the other of steel. Either way, there would be no soft landings...for anyone. That's why he'd suggested it. McNasty had been running his mouth...talking trash. Train had tried to tell him not to, but apparently dumping a man in the back of a garbage truck and trying to crush him doesn't work as well as it used to. Train understood why to. Working inside the cofines of an arena offer alll the creature comforts... Security... Trainers... EMT's... You stay there long enough it starts to seem like that's the way the world works. Like, if something bad goes down, there'll always be somebody around to save your ass. Train may have forgotten a few things over the past few weeks, but he never forgot the truth... the stuff he learned on the streets that made him the man he was and was well on his way to returning to. It's easy to talk shit when there are people around to break up the fight that's gonna happen once you open your mouth... You're only a man when you can still talk the shit when it's just you and the muthafucka you're talking about...when there's no one call it to a nice, safe end...when's not just your ass, but your life on the line. McNasty had thought it was all good... Train knew he'd agree to it with his big "King of Hardcore" monicer...like a million other suburbanite chumps who thought they were bad cuz they've gotten in there shots in a couple of bar brawls. McNasty thought he was ready. Train knew he wasn't. Yeah he won the match and all. Sent Train straight through a windshield... He wasnt' sure how long he'd been out when he woke up, but it hadn't been long. McNasty was stupid enough to still be there. Celebrating... hanging on to his belt... hugging his bitch... like it was all good... as if the shit was over... Stupid. Even after everything Train told him he still hadn't gotten it. McNasty really thought that shit had all be over a belt. Over a peice of shiny metal attatched to a leather strap. This holding cell had been no night at the comedy club, but everytime he thought about it Train felt like rolling on the floor, laughing his head off... hell if he thought anyone had mopped the bitch within the last month he might have done it. It hadn't been about titles and rankings... It was about one thing... Respect. Train hd come up making sure that when the name Train came out of people's mouths it sent a shiver down there spine as they said it. Last time Train could remember being disrespected an any shape form or fashion echoed back to the days when his voice was changing and he was nursing some peach fuzz on his chin. He had forgotten how much it pissed him off... and he didn't care to remember. And it wasn't just McNasty. A lot of bastards in GW were under the impression he they could just run their mouths and run Train's name into the dirt. It was time for a reality check. He'd learned a long time ago nothing snaps reality back into focus quicker than tearing out someone's heart. And the real heart? It wasn't in a person's chest... it was in their homes... the people they cared about.... It was an ugly reality Train had made a lot of people face... It's the people who love that pay for your mistakes. It took McNasty a second to even notice his bitch wasn't behind him. Once he figured it out all Train had to do was asks nicely to get what he wanted. It tends to work that way when you have your hand wrapped around a man's girl's throat... Life is funny that way. He put up the normal tough guy barrage... ran his mouth about how he was gonna get Train... see him burn in hell... blah, blah, blah... But he still got in the trunk. It was a lessoned learned for McNasty. Don't just prepare from one battle when your opponent plans on fighting a war. For a couple of hours after it was done Train had felt weighed down by what happened next. Memories of the man he wanted to become... for his family... for Isaiah, the man who trained him... for himself... The fell like anvils from the sky in a Road Runner cartoon. The pounded him for a bit... haunted him... For a while the only thing he could think about was what McNasty must have felt. What it must've felt like when all the light disappeared after the trunk lid slammed shut. How cold it must've been in the trunk of that car, as it had been soaking wet from the pouding rain that poured all through the match. Train could only imagine the way his heart probably skipped a beat when he heard the engine turn over...and rev up... and suddenly fly into gear. The rest...he did his best not to think about it... bouncing around in the trunk with the spare tire and all the other miscellaneous crap back there as it went flying off the top deck of the parking garage. After that.. wasn't much to imagine... as soon as the car hit, landing on it's top, on top of all those other cars? He couldn't have really felt much...until he woke up in the hospital that is. But it had only lasted a bit though... just as long as it took Train to take that shower, iron his clothes, smoke a blunt, get dressed, jump in the car and head through the doors of the club. Which brought Train back to the thing he did his best to forget. The damn cops. Didn't matter where they always found the most inconvenient moment to stick their nose into your business. He and the two ladies he'd found to celebrate the moment with him had been well into their third bottle of Cristal when the cops showed up... It had been awhile so the sensation of the cuffs was unpleasant even though there was a time where he'd become accustomed to them. Next stop? The holding cell... Train had also forgotten that cops weren't into accepting credit cards for bail since you were supposedly a criminal when you were in there. It had been a long night in Jail. Another thing he'd forgotten was how hard the damn benches were...but that's alright... his ability to deal had been bread into him now. He adjusted quickly... the lawyers assured him they'd get the cash for bail the moment the banks opened the next morning. As Train exited the cell past the pack of losers that had been huddled in there with him he glanced at the cops watch... 8:15 a.m. The lawyers were right on time. Train walked down the hallway as the locked door at the end of it slid open for him. Train knew he'd rather forget all this, but the Attempted Murder charges the prick D.A. was trying to slam him with because of the McNasty thing weren't just going to go away so he couldn't forget... But that was ok... he'd beat this bullshit and in the end? It would help him remember who is in life... "The Life". He was the boss. He was the one to be feared. He was the one who'd demand respect. And get it... by any means neccessary. He was Train.
