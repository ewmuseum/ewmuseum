20 March 2005 Character: Shaman Fed: WWA Sitting in front of a burning fire place a man sitting and contemplating, His mind is wondering why he has returned to try and go after the prize that he had within his grasp before and failed to grasp it. Thinking he opens a book and thumbs through the pages. He decided to speak before he reads each section with a response. Slowly he turns his head and looks at the fire. Shaman: “I have come to this conclusion about the WWA and for the PTC Extreme title. I am returning to the ring once again to fight for the PTC Extreme title. Yes I am the WWA’s hardcore legend. Only champion to have it for the first time in the WWA,Am I striving to be the first or the second to make my impact known by winning a championship through the PTC. My fellow WWAer Ember is fighting for the Global Championship. He will do well if he doesn’t allow his ego to get in the way. Will I give up my quest for the PTC Extreme title? Not in the slightest. I have many people to face in the ring during this major rumble. Anything and everything goes.” Shaman runs his hand over the book stroking the binding as if it is a mere pet. He grins and raises his eyes. Shaman: “This book represents the war within for me. I read it and I study it and turn its teachings into my own understandings. I am willing to present each and everyone of you in the ring with me the teachings of pain. I like pain and I like blood. You shall understand each meaning through the hits of my fists or the various objects around your being. Now I will discuss each of you in short responses much like each of you seem to do about me. Let me open this up first.” Shaman unfolds a piece of paper and places a letter face up on the desk as the camera pans over to it and displays it. ************************************************************************* To those involved, Shaman of the WWA we are glad you decided to join us in the PTC Extreme Title Division battle royale. You were the first to sign up less than a few minutes of the listing. We greatly appreciate the effort and look forward to your time in the event. Best of luck to you and we hope you enjoy the event as much as the fans will. Here is a full list of the combatants in the event. 1. Shaman - WWA 2. Chris Storm - UWF 3. James von Doom - UWF 4. Kyo Kushinaga - 4WW 5. Blind Rage - UWF 6. Anthony Palmer-LOW 7. Peter Vetra - PRIME 8. Tony Stecchino- CCWA 9. "The Dark Messiah" Peter Maxwell - GCW 10. Kris CarMicheal - HSW. 11. Nova - PRIME 12. Kris Krimzon - CCWA 13. Wesley Williams - 4WW 14. Lyger – CCWA Sincerely, The PTC Extreme Division Staff and crew. ************************************************************************* Shaman folds the letter up and slips it into his back pocket as he stands and walks thumbing through the book before him. Quietly he raises his head with no emotion upon his face. He shakes his head slightly. Shaman: “Each and everyman in this event have their own reasons for being there. I have one simple and straight to the point reason. I want another shot at the PTC Extreme Division title. I am not here for pretty stories or to outshow the other man. Straight to the point I am and I am going straight to the point with this book describing my mind and actions and how I will interpret this book into my actions in the coming weeks. Am I scared about this event no. I am about as relaxed as any man can be. I am looking forward to making people bleed as well as bleeding myself. I feel alive when I bleed and hurt. In fact the rage that lives within will be released. I do not hide who I am I just come to the fight and fight. Enough of this. My book before me is by Sun Tzu it is called The Art of War. I am giving my own interpretations on this and each meaning. I have chosen a specific section which I feel is relevant to the task at hand.” Shaman grins as he looks at the book and drops his finger down to a specific line and begins to read aloud. Shaman: “Sun Tzu said: In the practical art of war, the best thing of all is to take the enemy's country whole and intact; to shatter and destroy it is not so good. So, too, it is better to recapture an army entire than to destroy it, to capture a regiment, a detachment or a company entire than to destroy them.” Shaman rubs his chin and grins as he leans back and smiles. Tapping the book he contemplates at which he wishes to interpret this phrasing. He shakes his head and then sits down as he sets the book down on the table. Shaman: “My interpretation of this may be a bit difficult for some to understand. The capture I understand here is to capture the Extreme Title belt. The fight and destruction would only mean the defeat of my opponents in the ring. Maybe just beating them stupid and embarrassing them in the ring will be the capturing aspect of who they are. Just one time is all it takes to knock a self inspired champion or former champion off their little throne.” Shaman pulls a highlight marker out of his pocket and marks the top section as he obviously thinks it is finished in this portion. Shaman: “Hence to fight and conquer in all your battles is not supreme excellence; supreme excellence consists in breaking the enemy's resistance without fighting. Thus the highest form of generalship is to balk the enemy's plans; the next best is to prevent the junction of the enemy's forces; the next in order is to attack the enemy's army in the field; and the worst policy of all is to besiege walled cities.” Laughing at this he flips the page and grins as he makes a note on the side of the page after he pulls a pencil out from his pocket as well. Shaman: “Man I feel like I am in history class. Anybody knows that laying siege to a walled city drains resources and causes more losses of your troops than needed. My take on this excerpt is simple. Take Chris Storm as an example. He is a great wrestler going a little out of his element here. Yet before him I face a man as good as I am who I have not had the pleasure in facing. Plant the seed of doubt within his head and then his ego will get the best of him. He feels he is superior to everyone in the ring. That will be the downfall of many of my opponents in this match. The superiority feeling will cause them to be hurt and maimed as well as defeated. Will they begin to understand that they aren’t invincible and get down to business of surviving or will they be blind and hurt themselves in the end by getting defeated by someone they overlooked?” Getting a serious expression he stares at the next verse. Pursing his lips he turns his head and rubs his temple seemingly realizing something not thought of before. He rubs his thumb and forefingers together he turns his head and looks at the fire and then shakes his head as he reads. Shaman: “The rule is, not to besiege walled cities if it can possibly be avoided. The preparation of mantlets, movable shelters, and various implements of war, will take up three whole months; and the piling up of mounds over against the walls will take three months more. The general, unable to control his irritation, will launch his men to the assault like swarming ants, with the result that one-third of his men are slain, while the town still remains untaken. Such are the disastrous effects of a siege.” The realization sets in for Shaman as he looks up at a poster depicting his last match for the PTC Extreme Title. He turns his head and then remembers something else he had recently done. Shaman: “This certain verse causes me a bit of humbleness. I took the PTC headlong in my last few matches. I have fought hard and well, But in the end fell short. I fought Shadow whom I believe is current PTC Extreme Champion. How I would love that rematch and look for the hard fight. Our last meeting fell short and I must begin the fight anew. Secondly I fought in the big match against several opponents for the PTC Extreme Title. Obviously I came up short. Will results be different this time. I am mostly hoping to make a difference. There is a title I want to bring home to the WWA.” Shaman reaches to his belt and pulls out a knife that looks much like a raptors claw. Not a small raptor more like a tyrannosaurus rex. He looks at the knife lovingly as if it is an extension of who he is and he turns and picks his finger nails with it as he reads the next section. Shaman: “Therefore the skillful leader subdues the enemy's troops without any fighting; he captures their cities without laying siege to them; he overthrows their kingdom without lengthy operations in the field. With his forces intact he will dispute the mastery of the Empire, and thus, without losing a man, his triumph will be complete. This is the method of attacking by stratagem.” Shaman looks up as he looks at pictures and posters on the wall each representing each federation involved in the PTC Extreme Division Battle Royal. His eyes narrow as he looks again at the piece of paper he is pulling from his pocket. Opening it he pays close attention to how many people from specific federations are involved. Some are represented by more than one. He was the first to sign he realizes so in a way he is a marked man by stepping up to bat first. He folds the paper and continues to read on. Shaman: “It is the rule in war, if our forces are ten to the enemy's one, to surround him; if five to one, to attack him; if twice as numerous, to divide our army into two. If equally matched, we can offer battle; if slightly inferior in numbers, we can avoid the enemy; if quite unequal in every way, we can flee from him. Hence, though an obstinate fight may be made by a small force, in the end it must be captured by the larger force. Now the general is the bulwark of the State; if the bulwark is complete at all points; the State will be strong; if the bulwark is defective, the State will be weak. There are three ways in which a ruler can bring misfortune upon his army here are the ways represented by each a number. (1) By commanding the army to advance or to retreat, being ignorant of the fact that it cannot obey. This is called hobbling the army. (2) By attempting to govern an army in the same way as he administers a kingdom, being ignorant of the conditions which obtain in an army. This causes restlessness in the soldier's minds. (3) By employing the officers of his army without discrimination, through ignorance of the military principle of adaptation to circumstances. This shakes the confidence of the soldiers.” Shaman smiles as he throws his knife at the posters on the wall and hits the one of CCWA and he stares as he looks back down at his list and then raises his head again. Shaman: “Each federation head is a general in their own right. They choose the men they send to combat. Each man needs to represent the fight. Some will retreat and some will conquer all. I am a massive man and I come alone in this fight for the WWA. CCWA sends several men to weigh the odds in there favor. So they look to outnumber the rest of us. This will prove to be there undoing because it all comes down to strategy. There will be temporary uneasy alliances in the ring and all in all there will be betrayal in the end. When all is said and done when there is one man standing the victor. The PTC will crown the number one contender. The soldiers are each wrestler in the match. The generals are the fed heads. No man in this match is doing this for the greater cause that I am attempting. I am seeking this victory for the fans and to bring home a piece of gold from the PTC for the WWA and its fans.” Shaman stands and reaches up and pulls the knife out of the CCWA’s poster and rubs the blades edge between his fingertips and then lays the knife down onto the pages of the book as he begins to read again. Shaman: “But when the army is restless and distrustful, trouble is sure to come from the other feudal princes. This is simply bringing anarchy into the army, and flinging victory away. Thus we may know that there are five essentials for victory: One He will win who knows when to fight and when not to fight. Two He will win who knows how to handle both superior and inferior forces. Three He will win whose army is animated by the same spirit throughout all its ranks. Four He will win who, prepared himself, waits to take the enemy unprepared. Five He will win who has military capacity and is not interfered with by the sovereign.” Shaman smiling as he picks up his knife and holds the folded letter from the PTC staff. He grins as he looks at the two held in each hand. He turns his head and stares at the fire burning and crackling nearby as he speaks in a low steady voice showing no emotion or concern. Shaman: “Our leaders have let us become the commanders. Some still have their hands in the pot though. Trying to grasp at straws for greatness and yet they have decided to let those of us go alone due to our experience and expertise in the ring. They have chosen not to fight and to allow us to fight. Facing your opponent in the ring has been an up and down battles. There are some of us in this match who have never faced each other before and there are those of us who have crossed paths. This is a war in the greatest of all schemes. It is a proving ground where the best of the best come to fight. We are all on this list and there is a prize to be earned at the end.” Shaman flips the page with the tip of the knife as he lays the folded paper down on the surface near the book. He suddenly slams the knife down into the folded paper as he looks at the book and begins to read the last section. Shaman: “Hence the saying: If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles. If you know yourself but not the enemy, for every victory gained you will also suffer a defeat. If you know neither the enemy nor yourself, you will succumb in every battle.” Shaman closed the book slowly and stood back to his feet and flexed his massive muscled form. Staring down at the closed book he raises his eyes as the fire had slowly died down and become a burning hot red pile of embers. He stares into the dark red burning of the logs as he speaks. Shaman: “The world of extreme is a dark place gentlemen. I have had my wins as well as losses. I have had my victories and defeats. Do not think in the slightest I am not studying my opponents to the fullest. I am fighting you all in this and I am not going to give you a quarter. I do not cower from the future or the past. I prepare for the here and now. The fight of the day is in the here and now. Do you all not see the path we are all facing. It is one of blood and pain. I am not going to play in the slightest event. I am going to fight hard and fast. I am going to fight for a title that I want to take home with me. I am done playing the games of others. The battle begins now as we stand ready to fight for everything that is before us. A chance at a coveted prize and a chance to make history against one of the better PTC champions. Welcome to hell boys we are going to be fighting for everything we are.” Shaman turns and walks away as the camera pans down to the closed book and the knife driven down into the folded paper as the fire burns on through the night only dimply lightning the area. ---- 24 March 2005 Character: Shaman Federation: WWA Preperations for a fight!! Shaman is standing near a window as he stares out at the world beyond. A glass near him is sweating perspiration as the ice cracks and crinkles as it shifts in the glass. He reaches down and grabs the glass and brings it to his lips as he drinks it slowly watching the world outside. He begins to speak slowly as he wants a point made it seems. He turns his head towards the camera and begins shake his head. Shaman: “Before I go on and speak about the PTC event I am involved in I think there will be only a fair way to express my distaste for what’s happening in the WWA. First there is Dave Harley our cowardly Champion. Hiding behind Devils Own in fact he was avoiding everyone on Meltdown including the man he is facing at Rage in the Cage Mal Somers. Now we have Leviathan who uses his strength as a weapon, But forgets that others carry that ability as well. Yet he has his hands full with Tracer Bullet his sworn enemy at the moment. Then we come to my favourite yet less than honourable Joey Edwards. The man I face at Rage in the Cage for the Extreme Title. In nonetheless a pit of my creation and a little device added in we like to call a cage over the top. This means Joey Edwards gets to face me in an environment that I thrive in. I am going to hurt you Joey. Rest assured I intend on taking you to the limits.” Shaman turns and walks to the side as he steps in front of the desk where The Art of War by Sun Tzu sits upon the desk as he turns and looks at the camera. Shaman: “Now the PTC Extreme Division Battle Royale has a lot of talent in it. I am going to have to fight like I have never fought before. These men are going to have to face me as well. I wonder if any of them are going to be too cocky for their own good. Not everyone and everything is going to stop me from attacking them mercilessly. There is a fight coming and I intend on being in the middle of it. Do I want to walk away and concentrate on Joey Edwards only? No it isn’t that simple. I’d like to say I am simply going to enter the ring and eliminate opponent after opponent, But that is not how it is going to happen. I have to be vigilante in my actions in the ring and follow through. I am one of the largest skilled men in the WWA and in the Event I will be one of the biggest if not the biggest. I am a Technical Powerhouse. I can incorporate Technical skills as well as Power moves in my distinction. Yet there is one surprise I like to keep in the match. That is the fact I can play dirty pool with the best of them in the ring and I can and will go extreme.” Smiling he turns and raises the glass to his mouth again drinking out of it. He swirls the ice in the glass before he sets it down as he stands back watching and waiting for something unseen. Shaman turns and opens the book to a specific page and grins as he speaks again. Shaman: “Back to Sun Tzu it seems it is much needed for me to create a balance in chaos. The coming match will be dangerous in its highest form. I want this match and I will fight hard to win it. I am not sure if any of the guys I am facing are taking me serious. It matters very little to me. I have a lot of opponents to face and stratagem needs to be placed in a very capable fashion. Do I have a plan this hopefully will help me set the guideline of combat.” Shaman motions his hand as he begins to read the book. Shaman: “VARIATION IN TACTICS” Turning the pages he opens to a new chapter and thumbs down through the pages as he comes to a certain point and presses his thumb down hard and holds the place. Shaman: “Sun Tzu said: In war, the general receives his commands from the sovereign, collects his army and concentrates his forces.” Frowning he looks around. Shaman: “Obviously that one is useless to me since I am the only member of the WWA involved in this specific match.” Shaman turns the page and begins to read again. Shaman: “When in difficult country, do not encamp. In country where high roads intersect, join hands with your allies. Do not linger in dangerously isolated positions. In hemmed-in situations, you must resort to stratagem. In desperate position, you must fight.” Shaman taps the page and thinks about who he has to face and then blinks and then looks around. Shaman: “Temporary truces can be made to achieve a grater goal. I am sure I am a major target in this match up. Nobody wants a large man in this match at the end. I am sure that even if they get to that point they will gang up upon me or I will have earned temporary allies until then.” Stretching he reads again as he turns his head to look out the window he then turns and continues to read. Shaman: “There are roads which must not be followed, armies which must be not attacked, towns which must be besieged, positions which must not be contested, commands of the sovereign which must not be obeyed. The general who thoroughly understands the advantages that accompany variation of tactics knows how to handle his troops. The general who does not understand these, may be well acquainted with the configuration of the country, yet he will not be able to turn his knowledge to practical account.” Shaman reaches up and rubs his chin as he looks at the book. Shaman: “So basically I have to pick and choose my fights and not run in head strong.” Shaman nods his head in understanding and looks around as he observes the books writings as he reads on. Shaman: “So, the student of war who is unversed in the art of war of varying his plans, even though he be acquainted with the Five Advantages, will fail to make the best use of his men. Hence in the wise leader's plans, considerations of advantage and of disadvantage will be blended together. If our expectation of advantage be tempered in this way, we may succeed in accomplishing the essential part of our schemes. If, on the other hand, in the midst of difficulties we are always ready to seize an advantage, we may extricate ourselves from misfortune.” Shaman thinks for a moment as he looks at the book and speaks his mind. Shaman: “Control the situation and if I lose control take advanatage of someone else’s misfortune and shoot for victory.” Shaman just continues to read again. Shaman: “Reduce the hostile chiefs by inflicting damage on them; and make trouble for them, and keep them constantly engaged; hold out specious allurements, and make them rush to any given point. The art of war teaches us to rely not on the likelihood of the enemy's not coming, but on our own readiness to receive him; not on the chance of his not attacking, but rather on the fact that we have made our position unassailable.” Shaman ponders this for the moment and then looks about as he rubs his cheek and then purses his brow as he looks at the book. Shaman: “Keep them engaged isn’t that the point of a battle royal to defeat and eliminate your opponent. Keep them always looking over their shoulder for another person to attack them. Keep them preoccupied to better your own personal position within the match?” Shaman frowns and looks at the book again. Shaman: “There are five dangerous faults which may affect a general: One Recklessness, which leads to destruction; Two cowardice, which leads to capture; Three a hasty temper, which can be provoked by insults; Four a delicacy of honor which is sensitive to shame; Five over-solicitude for his men, which exposes him to worry and trouble. These are the five besetting sins of a general, ruinous to the conduct of war. When an army is overthrown and its leader slain, the cause will surely be found among these five dangerous faults. Let them be a subject of meditation.” Shaman simply closes the book and walks away from it over to the window. He reaches forward and opens the window as a low rumble is heard in the distance. He breathes in a deep breath as he watches the clouds darken in the distance. He knows there is a storm rising and ultimately in the end it will pass. He wants to be at the forefront of this storm he thinks to himself. He speaks as the wind blows through the window. Shaman: “The time is now and the war has begun. The war of words is nearly over and the physical war is just about to begin. This is a battle for the ages pitting all the very best of the federations against each other. Win or lose everyone will have made their mark. I want this for the WWA and its fans. I wish to bring a title home for them from the PTC. Someone needs to break the mold and show that they have what it takes in the WWA to win a championship. There are doubters and those that claim there is a conspiracy. I say there isn’t I say there has been a problem of taking the reigns and running with them. I am not one for stories I am one for straight to the point and understanding.” Shaman smiles as he sees a bolt of lightning in the distance flash. He notices the rain begin to fall and he looks about as he closes the window slowly. Shaman: “The storm is here gentlemen. Seek shelter or face the raging might that is the storm. There is a force of nature rolling about each of us. We all are willing to do everything in our power. Yet there is a specific balance which must be made. There can only be one victor in this match. I am addressing each and every one of you men in this match. Will you bring out the ultimate fight in everyone or will you cower from the fact that you know not who or what you face. A man can be extreme without showing it. A man can be a hero without actually saving someone’s life. A man can be a champion without even holding a championship. Will you fight for the prize or brush everyone off falling just short of the ultimate goal. I want the title shot gentlemen. I want my second shot at Shadow. Stop me if you can. If you can’t stay out of my way as I stride to victory and get my rematch.” Shaman turns and walks away fom the window and disappears into the darkness as the camera focuses on the book and then simply fades to static.
