Date: 2004-08-24 ‘Luke, it is your Destiny. Join Me.’ - Darth Vader pitching his idea for a Skywalker And Son franchise. * * * Jimmy Blackwood was just an average guy scraping out an uneventful existence in a dead end job at the airport kiosk. See with an average IQ he had earned average grades at his average high school, which in turn had got him a place at an average college. Normality for James T. Blackwood was regular standard typical uneventful middling ordinariness. Since his birth it had been preordained by Destiny that he would go to the average college with similarly average students and learn the social skills to become an average family man while obtaining an average education that would lead to an average nine to five job...Water Distribution Technician, or meter man. It was honest modest and, most importantly, average work. In short he was to become another member of Middle America. Let’s give the magic eight-ball a shake and look ahead. His future - like most Yanks - was Big Macs, elasticated pants and ten minutes of fame on the Jerry Springer show. It’d all end when he shuffled off this mortal coil with a typical heart attack on the toilet (the straining death once reserved for prestigiously obese megastars, like Elvis Presley, now the most popular way to go with eight out of ten Americans). That was his Destiny. It was just as sure as a bear shat in the woods, the Pope was catholic, white men can’t jump and black men can’t swim. However, there was only one problem: six months ago Jimmy had dropped out of college. In doing so he’d broken a chain of predetermined events in his life that were lined out before him and which periodically fell into place like a metaphysical domino rally. Jimmy Blackwood was one of the rare few who had kicked Destiny right in the balls (metaphorically speaking). Even with metaphorical testicles that has gotta hurt. If everything had gone to plan then at this point in time Jimmy shouldn’t have been employed in a dead-end kiosk at the airport, but instead experiencing college life and on track to a lifetime of the mundane. He should’ve been in the halls doing what other kids his age were doing; namely drinking, drug abuse and very little else. Granted, an unlucky few may have learnt something along the way but they were mostly the exception. Today of all days, Jimmy’s attendance at Average College was important: for the next domino in the rally of his existence was ready to fall and herald a catalyst for a life-changing event. This date with Destiny was actually a date with Bessie. Today - at 4:35pm - in the jock’s locker-room, he was supposed to have an intimate encounter with Bessie the Cow; the football team’s beloved mascot. This contrasting and less-than-average event in Jimmy Blackwood’s hitherto dull life would be so shameful as to insure that he’d most certainly keep his head down and lead an average existence meekly reading water meters for the rest of his life. It was vitally important that this was his Destiny. * * * Fifteen miles away in a college locker room Bessie the Cow let out a sorrowful ‘MOO,’ the bovine mascot yearning for some good animal loving... * * * Why was it was important that Jimmy should be embarrassed and humiliated into leading an average life? Well that’s because he was ‘special.’ Not special in a prodigal way, not special in a Dustin Hoffman Rainman-count-the-matchsticks-at-a-glance way, nor special in an Albert Einstein invent the atom bomb and leave the human race in the shadow of nuclear annihilation way. He was special in a more, well, psychopathic talk to the fairies way. Having escaped his fate as ‘Mr Average’ Jimmy had been left to his own devices and to make his own way in the world. It gave him a chance to listen to the voices who lived at the end of his bed. The job at the airport kiosk may have been dead-end with very few prospects but it did give him a disposable income and plenty of time to read the magazines he sold. He liked to read Guns & Ammo. He also found he liked to buy guns: big guns, small guns, shiny guns, laser sighted semi-automatic magazine fed Kevlar-tipped bullet spraying guns. Not good for the rest of us. * * * In an alternate universe where the domino rally had fell according to plan, Bessie couldn’t help but be disappointed with the lack of foreplay involved in her latest sexual encounter with Jimmy (although it wasn’t her first; those jocks on the football team would often shag her if they weren’t bumming each other)... * * * Not good because now Jimmy was working with the public rather than skulking around driveways reading meters. He liked guns because they gave him the one thing he desired most - respect. One example of this was when he crept out of the trailer one night, taking great care not to disturb his single parent mother, and went to a bar on the edge of town. It was a real spit and sawdust joint where he’d never been before and nobody knew him; some motherfucker smelling of oil and grease bumped into him spilling his drink. The guy looked down on little Jimmy and had laughed - for he’d nothing to fear when being the size of a blubbery walrus and wasn’t about to pay the little punk for a new drink. But Jimmy produced the gun from the baggy knitted jumper his mother had promised him he would grow into. Even a guy that size had to show him some respect. Needless to say the drink spilling mo-fo immediately apologised - profusely even - and offered to buy him another drink. Hell, he bought all his drinks for the night...just as long as Jimmy didn’t shoot him. Now that was respect. It was like his scrawny ass was King Arthur and the gun was Excalibur pulled from the stone. Okay, so maybe shoving a Magnum Desert Eagle in the chap’s face helped and yes; it was probably fear and not respect that led to such a benevolent gesture, but let’s not split hairs - Jimmy had felt powerful although he’d never spent a day of his life in the gym or gulped handfuls of roids like the people Ian English would have to face in the Survivor matches. He’d gotten R - E - S - P - E - C - T as the song went. That night the voices at the end of Jimmy’s bed had told him it was what he rightly deserved. Since then he’d shoved the oversized hand cannon into three more obnoxious faces, people who deserved it, people who’d wronged against him, morons afflicted by a sickening rudeness. He even slept with the damn thing (not in a sexual intercourse type way though, the kid was ‘special’ but not dumb). In fact you could say James T. Blackwood was a damn hero: he upheld the morals of society and become an avenging vigilante of politeness for the good of society. Well that’s what the voices told him. Damn psycho! The dead-end job of checkout assistant at the airport kiosk did little to engage him mentally or physically (unlike the fatefully assigned job of water meter reader) which was a dangerous thing. He now had plenty of time to fantasize as he stood there day-after-day looking out over people who were going places. Jimmy didn’t mind though, for he knew that soon his time would come and then he’d be the one going places. He usually fantasized about sniping someone from his counter just because they’d chosen salt and vinegar potato chips over cheese and onion. It was the power over life and death decisions upon the unsuspecting public that gave him a hard-on. Savory snack choice and selection of flavour was such a trivial issue. But not so in Jimmy’s kingdom. BANG. Jimmy could end your life. In this kiosk he chose who lived and died, Jimmy was God. Unfortunately his congregation numbered zero, word obviously hadn’t gotten about. But that would come eventually, once he got the ball rolling (employed a few missionaries to knock on doors), the voices had assured him of that. At the moment he had more pressing concerns: the airport janitor. Now there was a man who was definitely rude and disrespectful! Every time the janitor walked by Jimmy’s kingdom he’d give him an aloof glance. The guy stunk of shit and made a living from stabbing other people’s crap with a blunted spoon when the toilets got blocked...how dare he have the audacity to give HIM the evils!? It wasn’t damn polite for sure and maybe sometime next week Jimbo would shove a gun in Harold’s sour old face and earn his ‘respect.’ Just thinking of that pompous old fart had raised his blood pressure. Just recently Jimmy had started taking a gun to work with him, keeping it in a holster under his shirt. No one suspected he had it, not even airport security; he was average old Jimmy in his dead-end job after all. If only the fools knew. In times such as these when he worked himself up into a state, the feel of nickel-plated steel pressed cold against his skin was reassuring. Truth was freakboy got turned on by it and even now he put his hand up the standard issue blue-uniform shirt of the airport so he could caress the barrel, slowly sliding his finger down to the curved trigger. Oh yes, in Jimmy’s kiosk kingdom he would be definitely shown respect and politeness. “OI MUPPET!” Jimmy looked up startled at the bellow, he hadn’t heard anyone come in. “Yeah that’s right I’m talkin’ to youse boy-wonder! Get yer ’and outta that shirt and twiddle with the dials on that bloody radio rather than caress yer hairy nipples; see me lug’oles aint as big as yers and I can’t ‘ear wot the fack is being said.” Slightly embarrassed at being caught off guard with his hand up his top he automatically turned up the volume for the abusive guy. Then Jimmy started to sweat, his head pounded with the rhythm of his heart, anymore stress and he’d have probably turned green - for this bastard had just been rude to Jimmy in his own kingdom and like a bitch Jimmy had taken it. The guy had no manners. He had been disrespectful. The voices weren’t best pleased. In the background a red digital clock on the wall changed to 4:35pm...that preordained fateful time for James T. Blackwood. The next minute of his life was going to be a life changing experience. You see Bessie the Cow at this moment in time may have been stood-up, but Destiny could fuck with you in more ways than one. Reality wasn’t like the movies where you could take Destiny into your own hands, the illusion of choice was just that - an illusion. All that Hollywood bollocks with Luke Skywalker declining to join Vader in the family business of galactic ass-whupping, or a brain-damaged Rocky Balboa beating up Mr T after a brief run on the beach with Apollo, just wouldn’t have happened in the real world. So not even George Lucas would be able to help Jimmy escape his Destiny. Well not unless he was a Jedi, but even Jimbo wasn’t that ‘special.’ And so the next domino was about to tumble in order to get Jimmy’s life back on the determined track… Of course the main protagonist of this story didn’t believe in Destiny...but he believed in Fate. Although it meant an entirely different thing to Ian English - the Yanks were fat and they ate put the two words together and voila, you have Fate. On reflection it wasn’t the same as the concise Oxford Dictionary definition. Like most people English wrongly assumed that he chose his own Destiny, when in fact it was the other way around: Destiny had chosen him. His destiny was to fuck up other people’s destinies, a job that suited him just fine. He’d make sure he carried out that task to it’s fullest at that PTC Interfed PPV he’d somehow gotten roped into. Not that the Limey was consciously aware of this, he cocked his head to one side and listened to the radio. “...and now for the final news story: a man who tried to rob a sperm bank in a neighbouring state yesterday is still at large after representatives from PRIME Entertainment confirmed that Tony Rolo was on a promotional shoot at the time of the incident. The impostor had signed himself in as the federation’s Global Champion...” So English was still in the clear then. He chewed his lip in contemplation; the misfortune of that escapade was something best forgotten about, God knows he didn’t need the title of wank robber on his resume. Especially not when he was being forced into a team that comprised of his two biggest critics, Rolo and Troy. “...we’ve asked a criminal profiler, who in the past has worked on some high profile cases for the FBI, what type of desperate wretch would try to rob a sperm bank? In his personal opinion the individual must have been raised in a hippy commune and will have serious life issues resulting from a lack of contact in meaningful personal relationships...” English flung the playboy mag down on the counter: the closest thing he'd got to a long term relationship (he'd have to remember to send his hand a Valentines Day card next year), “Price this up fer us ’tard.” Retard? Jimmy was fuming: nothing in this world hurts quite like the truth. “...and his use of a banana in the attempted robbery could have been a moral substitute for a small penis...” Now Ian English was fuming! He stared at the radio in disbelief. There was an atmosphere building up in the little kiosk, like ozone before a thunderstorm you could feel it, almost taste it. (Or was that Ian’s 80’s Hai Karate deodorant?) The only other person in the vicinity, a little old lady, shuffled away from the celebrity gossip magazines and out of the small shop away from the impending danger. “...it might even be a tiny penis, or maybe he doesn’t have one at all; we could speculate it was lost in some kind of freak accident involving farm machinery...” “Arrrghhh!” would they shut up already about his cock!? CRASH!!! The newsreader’s voice was replaced by static. Jimmy Blackwood couldn’t believe it; the Australian or Irishman or whatever he was had leant across the counter and smashed his radio! He’d only just paid twenty dollars last week to get the radio modified so it would pick up on the bandwidth of the police frequency. Adrenalin coursed through Jimmy’s weedy body, all he could hear was thump, thump, thump of blood being pumped directly into his head...well at least the sound spared him from Ian’s latest rant. “Aint it fackin’ enough that I’ve been forced out of retirement and on to a team of natural born losers? That I ’ave the indignity of facing talent deficient muppets from Ladies Only Wrestling? Further still that I’m at risk of being crushed to death by Mr Ten Chins himself: Adam “Who ate all the pies?” Allocco and the rest of the losers from Noah’s Wrestling MidWest ark - the only place to be if you want to see the morons lose two by two? That I hafta stand across from a piss poor bunch of WWA Korn tribute band wannabe freaks that drink piss, smell of piss and have their own aromatic accessory range of Armani products such as ‘eau de piss.’ Oh no, obviously it aint because now they’re bloody debating the size of me todger on local radio, I mean wot the fack is wrong with...” Jimmy reached back into his shirt and felt the holster - he was gonna teach this rude foreign bastard some manors. “SHUT THE FUCK UP BITCH!” It wasn’t very often that Ian English got interrupted. Puzzled, he looked down at the boy behind the counter. Instead he found himself looking down the barrel of a gun. PRIME’s Only Hope Of Winning The Intefed Tournament narrowed his eyes, “Did that butt-monkey Joey Troy put youse up to this eh? Wot? You want me to scream like a lil’ girly and beg fer me life?” actually Jimmy conceded that would be nice. “I bet if youse pulled that trigger a flag with “BANG Ur Ded” unfurls don’t it?” English put two fingers on the Desert Eagle and pushed it away from his face. The foreign cunt was making life hard for Jimmy. Obviously he was some bad-ass who was used to being around guns (from Jimmy’s limited experience the mere sight of a real gun usually caused people to break down and crap their pants). He was probably a cop then...or a Mountie...or a member of the IRA...well someone with a strange accent who wasn’t afraid of guns. In a desperate bid for respect Jimmy turned the gun back on his customer. The actual truth of the situation was somewhat different from Jimmy’s prespective though: see Ian English had grown up in England (unsurprisingly) a country known for it’s strictly over-the-top gun laws. Outside of TV land this was the first real gun that he’d ever been on the business end of...not that he believed it was real for a second. Well, for maybe for a brief instance the thought had crossed his mind that it may have been the genuine article but Ian had seen Diehard: it took at least five bullets to kill you right? “DON’T FUCKING MOVE OR I’LL BLOW YOU A NEW...” “WOAH, WOAH THERE!! You’ll BLOW me!?” Jimmy wasn’t sounding as cool or as menacing as he’d hoped. “NO - I’LL BLOW YOU AW...” “Blow! You just said it again fag - you must know Troy!!” “IF YOU’LL JUST LET ME FINISH WHAT I WANT TO SAY!” The fraught exchange of raised voices from within the kiosk had gathered a group of onlookers on the other side of the glass frontage. Alerted by the little old lady airport security were also in attendance looking on - none seemed willing to enter the sphere of danger that was Jimmy’s kiosk kingdom, instead SWAT had been called for. “So wot then kid? Youse want to bang me?” Ian actually found the situation quite amusing. “WHAT DO YOU MEAN? SPEAK ENGLISH!!” “Well guns go bang don’t they” “WELL YES, I GUESS I WANT TO BANG YOU!” Hook, line and sinker: the Limey reeled him in. “I knew it...QUEEEERRR!!” he bellowed at the top of his voice. The damn foreign shit had tricked him. English grinned from ear to ear like the Cheshire Cat…that was until he noticed a group of ninja’s outside with SWAT daubed in big white letters on the back of bullet-proof armour. Suddenly the seriousness of the situation dawned on the Limey as he realised the gun was real, the kid was a psycho and the string vest he was wearing probably wouldn’t afford protection against one bullet, let alone five. In fact the bad guys usually got shot repeatedly and suffered a grisly come-uppance. English gulped and clenched his arse cheeks realising he was long overdue. “THAT’S IT WISEGUY I’M PULLING THE TRIGGER!!” “NOOO! Ahem, I mean ‘no’ - let’s not be hasty mate...Jammy? Jeremy?” Ian theatrically squinted at Jimmy’s name badge attached to his shirt pocket. “IT’S...I mean, it’s Jimmy.” He followed the Limey’s gaze down to his name badge just to check the airport had spelt his name right. >POW< …well more of a thump: it was Ian English and not Batman after all. “’AVE SOME O’THAT YOU MUPPET!!” the bastard from the British Isles gloated. The red digital clock on the wall behind Jimmy changed to 4:36. His Destiny had been corrected. BAAANG!!! As Jimmy hit the floor the Magnum went off firing into the roof and spraying polystyrene ceiling tiles all over the kiosk like snow. SWAT rapidly charged in jumping Jimmy faster than a family of hicks on a sow at a barn dance. To English the world had just exploded, half deafened, half trampled, it took his brain a couple of seconds to process what had just transpired. Snowflakes fell out of his spiked red hair as he stood up shell shocked. “The fackin’ gun was real!” he exclaimed to nobody in particular. A SWAT officer looked up from the ground where they were restraining Jimmy, “Yes Sir, you’re a real hero!” Ian fainted. * * * When he awoke Ian English was on a cloud. As AWS Man (also known as Bill) would say, it was a ‘freakin’ big cloud that stretched all the way to infinity and back. Ahead was a golden gate with a huge guy leaning against it. Saint Peter. Bollocks. Ian must’ve copped a bullet, probably a ricochet. Not that it mattered, dead was dead and he had more immediate concerns to address; even though he’d known that one day he’d have to face judgement he’d never managed to think up a satisfactory justification for all the bad shit he’d done. He didn’t doubt that his lame excuses wouldn’t be accepted although he did have one cunning strategy still up his sleeve: forgiveness. If he said sorry and repented to the ways of the Lord then they should let him in anyway. All he had to be was genuinely sorry. Hmm...easier said than done. He approached the gate knowing that more so than ever before in his life (well death) that it was imperative to lie his arse off. “Jah, vot do yoo vunt puny man?” It wasn’t Saint Peter but Arnold Schwarzenegger. The Limey was bewildered to say the least. “Err, wot happened to the other geezer; the two thousand year old bouncer with a big white beard?” “Ay terminated him.” “No way mate, you terminated a Disciple? Chap, I gotta say I’m genuinely impressed by that.” and Ian really was. Killing thousands of henchman and the odd Predator was one thing, but a bonfide Saint? He doubted if Jesse Ventura could have done that. “No, nut reuhly: he voz too old und saggy to be a door greater...unlike me.” the Austrian began to flex through his white toga, the Englishman had horrible flashbacks of having to share a locker room with Tony Rolo wearing his too tight Rolomania tee-shirts. “Well nice talking to you geez…I’ll just be going through here then.” but a huge arm barred his way. Arnie didn’t budge from leaning against the gate, “Yoo nowh da routine skinny - so vot iss yor eggscuse?” Arse, English would have to pull something lame out of the bag - he doubted he could blame all his actions on Tony Rolo and Joey Troy. “Ahh well, see, the thing is...I did it all in the name of entertainment?” Arnie let out a hearty laugh that reverberated around the clouds, “Vong answer weakling! Yoo fink I did nuht try that vun efen afta such crimes to humanity as Kindergarten Cop und Twins?” Shite, the roided up lummox had a point. It was time for a different approach, “How ’bout if I was like really, really, reeeally sorry mate?” “I believe yor sincer...sincerit...uh, I believe yoo little man, but I canuht let yoo in: yor nuht Amewican.” Ian couldn’t believe his ears, “Wot bullshite man! A fackin’ Yank? Daaaymn right I aint! Hey wait one friggin’ second - neither are you!!” The reply came with a wry smile, “Hey, I’m a gud actor.” “This is discrimination Captain Meathead, I wanna talk to yer bleedin’ boss!” Arnold shrugged his boulders for shoulders nonchalantly; seconds later a puff of smoke appeared before the Limey - he was going to finally meet God. The Man emerged coughing from the lingering haze of the cheap effect...although there was no glimpse of a big white gown and sandals. It’s fair to say Ian wasn’t impressed with what he saw. “Yer’ve gotta be kiddin’ me, this muppet ain’t God!” “There is no jokicating up here son and I ahm not pulling your arm when I statify that I ahm God.” George Dubya Bush consulted the big tome of entrants before him, “Ah do not see yor name on the list - if yor not a fella Merrikun then you cahn’t come into Heathen.” “You mean Heaven? Look muppet wot about me constitutional rights eh?” “Discrimination is mandatory in the United States of Hefun, anyway, Eeon, firstifily yor name sounds like the noise made by a donkey and secondistically you have no Merrikun constipational rights; I thort I asked Colon to bomb all you Eng-raqis in our war against terrosity...” English reflected that even in heaven he couldn’t escape the obese stupidity of the Yanks, it was his curse. He wasn’t angry though, in his life he’d come to expect such things. Although the situation wasn’t helped by the fact that he was trying to argue with the two most illiterate politicians this side of Gengis Khan’s dynasty. “...as punification, I syntax you to an eternity amongst similar small-minded peoples suffering an endless torture of heat and sweat.” “I’m going to Hell?” Arnie laughed, “No stickman, yor goink to Texas!” “NOOOOOO!!!” A trap door opened in the clouds beneath Ian’s heavily booted feet and he fell through. “I’ll be baaaack...” cried the tumbling Englishman. “Hey dat is my line!” * * * Ian woke with a start, only a few seconds had passed and Jimmy was still struggling on the floor. He picked himself up from the floor and ventured closer to the boy who could’ve ended his self-important life. “Cunt.” English booted Jimmy in the ribs once, then twice, then...SWAT looked up at him. He held his arms up in innocence, as he’d done so many times before to numerous referees in the past, then backed up to the exit. But not before doing an Artful Dodger and swiping his porno mag and a six pack of lager off the counter on his way out. Time to wreck some more destinies. * * * Meanwhile the domino rally of fate that was Jimmy Blackwood’s life was back on the rails thanks to Ian’s intervention. It seems Jimmy had only managed to postpone his Destiny - thanks to the fine correctional facilities of the local penitentiary he was now being trained as a water distribution technician...but not before a romantic encounter in the showers with Bobby the Bull, leader of the white Arian supremacists and most definitely not the bovine mascot of the football team.
