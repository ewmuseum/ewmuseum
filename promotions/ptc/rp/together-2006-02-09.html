Together

9 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-02-09 Shanty Jones stared at the door. This day was insane enough without another interruption. "Ve didn’t order any room service," called Doctor Curiosity. Shanty looked around to see what Paul would make of it, but the escaped prisoner was suddenly nowhere to be seen. The knocks at the door repeated. "I said zat ve didn’t order anything!" "Good," came the deep voice, accompanied by the sound of the electronic lock being deactivated. "The Ripper neglected to bring any food." With that, he pushed the door and walked in to the room. Jack stood closer to seven feet tall than six, and little of his body looked as is it had gone to waste. He wore a black matt coat that stretched almost to the floor, and the rest of his attire was of a similar colour. "Who are you?" asked Shanty. "Zis is ze Ripper! Also known as Jack sometzing or other." "What is he, some sort of heavy metal singer?" "Alas not. He is like you and I. He, too, is a professional wrestler." This seemed to amuse The Ripper. One corner of his mouth twitched upwards, and his eyes gleamed at the other inhabitants of the room. He surveyed his surroundings, seeming to count in his head as he saw the comatose Artemis, the nervous Doctor and the plain bemused Shanty Jones. "There appears to be someone missing from our scene," said The Ripper, his voice deceptively light. "Come out, come out, wherever you are…" "There’s nobody else here," said Shanty. "Jah! Ve are all zat zere is," affirmed the doctor. "Then why is your brother in the bathroom?" The Ripper’s cracked smile showed again. "Paul, you can come out now." The door slowly creaked open, and Paul Goodman came out, his smile echoing that of the man in black. "Good guess," mumbled the doctor’s brother. The four men stood in the room, but the entire space was dominated by The Ripper. "So then," asked Shanty. "Do you want to introduce yourself?" His confidence was returning. Whilst The Ripper seemed to have a certain presence about him, and he was undoubtedly a tremendous physical specimen, what good would be afraid do? Shan had lived through more than almost anyone alive, and one more muscle-bound wrestle wouldn’t make a difference. "No." "No what?" said Shanty. "No, The Ripper is not one for introductions. Perhaps if you had observed the world around you more over the past few years then you wouldn’t be on the back foot, always asking questions." Shan frowned. "You might be some big shot wrestler, or someone famous, but you could be the Devil himself for all I care. Either tell me why you’re here, or get the hell out of my room. I’ve had enough with the mystical babble coming from everyone these days. I thought I was done with all that…" "You are, for the time being," replied Jack. "Steven was, after all, the last of the dead, was he not?" "How… how do you know about that?" "The Ripper knows a lot about you, Shanty Jones. He knows of your birth and your death. He knows of all your past transgressions and your atonement." The Ripper’s voice was increasing in tone as he started advancing towards Shan. "So ask not ‘how?’, but rather ask the ‘why?’ of the matter." "Vhy are you…" started Doctor Curiosity, but he was silenced by one large raised arm. "You were not being spoken to, little curious one." Paul looked over towards Shanty. "Have you seen this man before?" he asked. Shan shook his head briefly, before turning again to study The Ripper’s face. No flicker of recognition appeared. He’d never even heard of this man before now. "Interesting…" said The Ripper. "The Ripper considers that this discussion might continue along more preferable lines without the interlopers." "Jah!" said Curiosity. "He meant you," said Shanty. "Oh," replied the doctor, slinking towards the door. "Take your brother with you," added Jack. Paul turned to face Shanty again. "I’ll be nearby. If there’s trouble I’ll be back in seconds." "Thanks." Curiosity reached the door and turned the handle. As he left the room, Paul looked up towards The Ripper and something unspoken seemed to pass towards them, before Paul’s eyes suddenly bulged wide in their sockets. He turned quickly, looking straight at Shanty again. "Don’t trust him! He wants something from you! He knows more than you think! He knows more than I do!" "That will be enough," said The Ripper, pushing Paul Goodman the last bit of the way and then closing the door. It was only then that it registered for Shanty. Paul hadn’t actually spoken during the last couple of minutes. It had all been in his head. *** Along a corridor, down eight flights of stairs, through the door and out on to the road, the two police cars were still keeping up their surveillance. Next to them was the third car, an old, innocuous black car, and in it sat a man who was in many ways the key to future events. He ran one hand slowly through his tangled hair and coughed into his other. Examining the contents of his hand, he found that he was still bleeding, somewhere. It was to be expected, he supposed. He had no idea how long he’d been in the Domicile, the lair of The Ripper. It had seemed like a lifetime ago that he himself would take to the ring. Oh how the mighty… thought Cereal Killer. Oh how the mighty have fallen. *** "What’s going on? Why do you want to speak with me alone, and… and how did you get them to agree to leave, just like that? Something’s not right here." Shanty Jones was worried. The world was getting stranger and stranger, devolving to a point where he couldn’t be sure if anything was real at all or if it was all just a dream. The Ripper spoke, and the words broke the solipsistic spell that was hanging over Shan. "They are gone now. Why continue the masquerade?" "How did Paul just speak into my head?" "Enough with the games, Shanty. The Ripper tires of them. You will tell me what you know of Paul Goodman, and of his work when you were together." "His work? We were in prison! He had connections and I did the occasional job for him, mostly intimidation. He really came to my rescue, because… no! Why am I telling you this? What’s happening?" Shanty’s usual calm, stoic demeanour had been utterly ruined. He had lost control, but couldn’t see any way of getting it back. "You must know of Paul’s work in the prison. You’re the only link." "Link?" "You do not know it. All this time, placing everything in the right space and time, and you missed what was going on right under your nose." The Ripper sounded both resigned and slightly surprised. "I had a mission for my life," said Shanty, his confidence coming back to him as he spoke of events that he knew had happened. "The Ripper is aware of this." "I had a mission in my life, even though I didn’t want it. I killed the men from my own squad, and then I had to be God’s executioner in the final instance. I was the one who had to send them to Heaven or to Hell. Who are you to come here and tell me that I didn’t see things that were going on in front of me? Who are you to seek to control everything?" "The yin to your yang." Shanty paused. Now he was getting somewhere. This man needed something from him, and that gave him power. "So, you think us alike in some way?" "In many ways, Shanty Jones. You have spent your life trying to do deeds according to a certain code of honour, or of respect. You have been the one to cast judgement on people, and shape the world to become more like your image. You have never flinched from anything or anyone, and you stand here, on the brink of fame, because of that. Think of The Ripper as being the same as you. Except coming from the other direction." "So you’re my enemy now?" "No more so than darkness is the enemy of light. They are both simply what they are. They oppose and they can conflict, but it would be unwise to prescribe an emotion to such forces." "Why are you here, Jack? To tell me how important I am?" "No, The Ripper is here for the same reason that Paul Goodman is here." Jack walked closer again to Shanty, and stared him in the eye. "I’m here for information." "How did you do that?" yelled Shanty, reeling against a nearby chest of drawers and knocking a piece of slate from the top of it, letting it tumble over and over, to land on the carpeted floor, a soft cushion for an artefact. "The Ripper cannot believe that he didn’t tell you." "Tell me what?" demanded Shanty. "The Ripper cannot believe that your good friend, Paul Goodman, never told you what he was really doing all the time you lived together." "And that was?" "Running a factory." *** "Do you trust zis Ripper man, Eegor?" asked Doctor Curiosity, waiting in the lobby of the hotel. "Yes and no. On the one hand, from what I know of him he’s an evil son of a bitch, but he’s got some sort of whacked out notion of honour about him. It’s hard to understand, or to explain," replied Paul. "So is that why we’re down here now?" "We’re down here now because he wants us to meet someone." "Why?" "I have no idea, but I just have this unstoppable urge to go and find out." The two men strode out of the lobby and towards the parking lot, before Paul spotted the black car parked on the roadside. "It’s that one." "How do you know?" "I just know." They arrived at the car, and Paul hunched down at the side to look through the window. Inside sat a man who looked on the verge of death. His skin was so pallid as to be almost translucent, showing through purple veins and tissue underneath that had seen better days. As the man turned his head, slowly, to look at them, they saw that only one eye was focusing, and the other just swam in his head, less useful than an appendix. "Should ve open ze door?" suggested Curiosity. Paul nodded, and tugged at the handle. The door swung upon on well oiled hinges, and they could see the full extent of the malnutrition that had wasted away Cereal Killer’s body. His arms were so thin that either of the others could have encircled them with a thumb and forefinger. His waist looked more suited to that of a supermodel, and his legs looked like old bones wrapped in ill-fitting jeans. In short, he was a mess. "His name is Frank," said Paul, pointing to a name badge that had been pinned to CK’s chest. "Do you recognise him?" "No, not at all." Cereal Killer looked up at the pair and tried to focus. Nothing had been working like it used to, and his arms and legs didn’t seem to want to move. He tried to speak, to ask them for mercy, or for help, but his lips had decided to rebel too. He mumbled something, but it was nowhere near a word. He was too far gone. "Did he try to say something?" asked Paul. "No. He’s just, you know… mentally deficient. He is von of zose people who you call ‘retarded’. He must have learning disabilities or something." CK heard the words being spoken and tried to protest, but his body would not respond. And so, inside himself as a prisoner again, his last little piece of resistance was broken down. He would be a blank canvas. A surface on which an artist could create. *** "You don’t mean that," said Shanty. "The Ripper does not waste time with idle talk or gossip. Please believe that every word that you hear is the honest truth. You have been grossly misled, Shanty Jones." "What do you know about the factories?" "The same that you know, which is why The Ripper was surprised by your ignorance. The factories were started up in Iraq due to the ready supply of prisoners and people who would not be missed if they were to vanish. Similar systems were attempted in prisons back in the US, but it’s a lot harder to do, because even the most hated murderer has people writing to them all the time. The war was… was such a convenient way to find a new supply of people." "Are you saying that they invaded Iraq to start the factories?" "No. The war was always going to happen. There were too many reasons for it to go ahead. Some were honest and a lot were… less so. The factories were not the prime reason for it all, but they were a reason." "You’ve seen what they do to people?" "Yes. The Ripper has seen it many times over. The genetic splicing, the attempts at radiological mutations, the torture, the butchery… The Ripper has seen it all." "There was nothing like that back inside." "Technology has moved on, Shanty. Just because you saw no enormous needles when you were in prison doesn’t mean that there weren’t experiments going on, or that some of them weren’t carried out on you." "No! I’m still myself. I haven’t had my life torn away like the people I saw back…" "How do you think that Paul spoke directly into your brain? You are not dealing with magicians here, Shanty." "NO!" Shan let out a burst of frustration and anger, balling his one hand into a fist and slamming it into the other. He felt almost like a cliché of anguish, of impotent angst, but in those situations there are few genuine alternatives. "Perhaps The Ripper should leave you to converse with your good friend Paul Goodman again. The two of you must be dying to get reacquainted." Shanty said nothing. His mind raced again, trying to piece things together. How did The Ripper know so much about this? Was it true what he was saying about Paul?" "The Ripper will now take his leave. This has been most… enlightening." Shanty nodded. He could feel that things were just a little out of his grasp, but if he could have some solitude, some peace and quiet then he could work it out. "It has been interesting to watch you as time has gone by," said Jack as he stepped out of the door. "The Ripper was disappointed that you didn’t recognise him… initially." The door clicked and Shanty heard a soft beep that meant that the lock was now engaged. He felt so close to uncovering it, to discovering why everything was as it was. Why was The Ripper so interested in Paul Goodman, and how did he know so much about the factories? Why was he disappointed that Shanty hadn’t recognised him initially? Initially. It hit him all at once, like a terrible thunderbolt of intuition. He ran to the door and flung it open, but The Ripper was gone, and he knew at once that he would not see the man again until the time was destined. To think, he’d had the man he wanted here all along, and he left knowing that Shanty would work it out. Initially. Jack Nobbledy. Jayenne Industries. J N Industries. *** "So what do we do with him?" asked Curiosity. "The keys are in the car. They’re in the ignition. We’re supposed to take care of him." "Why?" "I don’t know," said Paul. "I don’t understand the game… I just know that we have to play it." "What about Shanty?" "He’ll be fine," replied Paul. "He’s a tough bastard, and he’s got more strength than he knows. Jack wouldn’t end a game this quickly, so he won’t be harmed. We can meet him again after his match with Masquerade, and try to end this once and for all. "And until then?" "Well, until then," said Paul, a smile coming back to his face. "Until then just lay back and enjoy the ride." *** I will not be a pawn. I was not raised to be subservient to others, to play in their little games. I was not taught the injustice of life and the importance of being a leader only to become part of some sick tug of war. I am not the bridge or the link or the join between factories run by the sick-minded and the evil. I am not as weak or as easily manipulated as they think I am. So I will show it to them. I will prove that I am more than they bargained for, that God put me here for more than one reason, and to accomplish more than one task. This will be my masterpiece, a searing display that captivates and enraptures all around. Paul and Jack will be caught up in the swirl, as will anyone else who stands in my way… and then they will all be crushed. Masquerade is only the next, is only going to be an example of what I am capable of. If that doesn’t make them sit up and take notice then they will be forced to when I stop playing along and come for them. First Masquerade, then Paul Goodman and then Jack Nobbledy. I will know the truth, and I will see their empires crumble to dust. And then, perhaps, I will be allowed my seat in Heaven.
