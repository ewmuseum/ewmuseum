Pizza Time!

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 Quote of the Day (I just found this hilarious. This guy’s certainly quick on the uptake.) “To this day I still do not understand the point of the quotes. They never play a part of the rp and seem to be there to get a laugh.” (The scene opens in your standard children’s pizza parlor. You know, Chuck E. Cheese, Showbiz Pizza, that kind of place. Actually, I think those two were part of the same exact chain, just with different names. Lousy giant mice, and their corrupt mob front franchises… The restaurant/game room/seventh layer of Hell is packed to the brim with screaming kids: screaming because they’re running, screaming because they’re crying over something, screaming because they want their food, screaming because kids are annoying. It is not a quiet, relaxing place for adults: especially not for one particular adult who is currently suffering from a massive hangover brought on by a long night of drinking with the apartment pool boy. The mother of the monstrosity known as Unstoppable Eugene slumps over her greasy linoleum table covered with the remains of equally greasy pizza, massaging her forehead and trying to drone out the piercing noise of children’s laughter with occasional swigs from an unmarked flask in her purse. The monstrosity himself is currently wearing a ridiculously small cone birthday hat while giggling and trouncing around in the ball pit, sending waves of multi-colored plastic balls and children flying with each gargantuan leap. The constant tide of balls caused by his movements makes it hard for the other children to keep their footing, or even to get into the pit at all. Soon the children’s cries of terror, as well as Eugene’s tremendous guffawing, draw the attention of the establishment’s management. A smarmy-looking man in a blazer and tie approaches the ball pit and attempts to get Eugene’s attention.) Manager : Excuse me, sir? … Sir, hello! … Sir! (Not used to being called anything but his name, or for that matter addressed by anyone but his beloved mother, Eugene continues obliviously jumping around the pit, nearly crushing a floundering toddler beneath a mighty boot. With more yells, and frantic waving of his arms, the manager finally manages to secure Eugene’s attention as he turns back around.) Manager : Hello! I’m sorry, sir, but if you could please exit the ball pit- Unstoppable Eugene~! : BALLS ARE FUN! Manager : …Yes, they are fun, which is why we have them: for our younger customers. The ball pit is not meant for adults, especially not of your stature. One of the children could get hurt! Unstoppable Eugene~! : …BALLS FUN! Manager : Sir, please, if you do not stop mocking me and vacate the ball pit this very- SIR! (The conversation over in his mind with the establishment of the inarguable premise “Balls fun,” Eugene has dived back under the top layer of balls and is now swimming underneath them, a process that results in many cries of pain and likely internal bleeding from the kids unable to escape his path. By the time Eugene emerges from the balls, the token Big Purple Mascot Thing for this particular restaurant has joined the manager and is conversing with him. A few kids are trailing after him with foam bats in their hands, who the mascot chases away with a growl. The costumed man steps up to the netting that surrounds the pit from floor to ceiling and gestures to get Eugene’s attention.) Mascot : (In a thick Brooklyn accent) Hey, youse wanna get the hell oudda there, ya friggin’ dumbass? Dere’s kids in dere! Youse come out now, or I’s gonna drag ya outside myself and put a beatin’ on ya. (Eugene’s eyes light up when he sees the cuddly-looking character, obviously either not having heard or comprehended the man’s words.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : MISTER MUNCH!!! Mascot : Hey, buddy, youse trying to get us sued? Dat’s copyrighted intellectual proper- HEY! (Eugene clearly doesn’t care much for the explanation of patent infringement, as he bounds forward and bursts straight through the netting and tackles the purple creature to the floor. He grasps the pseudo Mr. Munch in a bearhug, squeezing the costume inward and crushing his ribs.) Mascot : Urgh … So dis is it for me … Tell da family boss I went out doing what I loved… (The manager and other restaurant personnel attempt to pry Eugene from the mascot’s probably lifeless body, but to no avail.) Manager : Would someone call the cops?! This man is clearly out of his mind on steroids and plastic fumes! (By this point, Eugene’s mother has finally noticed the situation with her son, and runs over to where the employees are about to jab Eugene with tasers that seem slightly odd for children’s pizza parlor personnel to carry.) Eugene’s Mother : Wait, no! Leave him alone! That’s my son! (The manager gives Eugene’s mother a skeptical, and somewhat leering look.) Manager : Miss, you’re … this man’s mother? Eugene’s Mother : He’s not a man, he’s a disabled sixteen-year-old boy! Manager : …He is? Eugene’s Mother : Didn’t you notice he was acting like a child? Manager : I thought he was just whacked out on drugs. … You’d be surprised how often that happens here. Eugene’s Mother : We were in the middle of celebrating his birthday, which I’ll have you know you just ruined. Manager : But he attacked one of our mascots, who might be dead now! Eugene’s Mother : (Glancing down at the man that her son is still squeezing tight) Oh, he’ll be fine. Just passed out from oxygen deprivation, I’m sure. (Nudges Eugene with her foot) C’mon, Eugene, let’s go have some pizza. (Eugene dutifully lets go and stands to walk off with his mother. Upon his departure, a swarm of foam bat-armed children pounce upon the downed mascot. The manager quickly runs around to stand in the way of the mother and her son.) Manager : Wait! Your son was endangering other children … He broke our netting … He assaulted our employees! I’m sorry, but I’m going to have to ask you to leave. (Eugene’s mother stands staring at the man for several moments, calmly at first, but growing more incensed with every moment. Finally, she instructs her son to cover his ears and steps forward, shoving her face into the manager’s.) Eugene’s Mother : Listen, buddy, I don’t know you think you are, or who you think you’re dealing with, but I for one am not going to be pushed around by you. I paid good money for this shithole of a pizza place, and we’re not leaving until I get my money’s worth. So my son’s a little different. So what? Would you turn away a little crippled boy at the door, huh? Would you say, “I’m sorry, Little Jimmy, I know you’re dying of cystic fibrosis and your last wish was to come here and meet one of our piss-ass mascots, but wheeling your death bed in here would just be too much of an inconvenience to the other children”? Would you do that?! Manager : Well, I, I- Eugene’s Mother : Of course you wouldn’t, shithead! (Eugene’s now very imposing mother grabs the man by his tie and jerks him closer, tightening it around his neck with every emphasized word she says.) Eugene’s Mother : My son is a very special little boy, and he doesn’t deserve this kind of treatment because of his condition. So, if you don’t mind, we’re going to go back to our table, continue to celebrate my son’s special day, and enjoy some of your fine establishment’s pizza, which will be on the house, of course. And if we encounter any problem with that, well … neither I nor my son can be held accountable for what he’ll do if he doesn’t get his way. Are we quite clear? (The manager swallows heavily, which is easier said than done with his tie constricted so tightly around his neck, and glances up at Eugene’s hulking figure. Even the fact that Eugene is currently playing pattycake with an imaginary friend doesn’t make the idea of his wrath any less frightening. The man slowly nods and chokes out a response.) Manager : Your pizza will be right out. (Eugene’s mother smiles and slowly releases the tie, wiping her hand off on her blouse as if it was soiled by touching a part of the man’s outfit.) Eugene’s Mother : Thanks so much for the courteous service. Eugene, this way. (The largest GTT4 entrant follows his mother back to their table and falls into one of the chairs, promptly breaking it under his weight. Unfazed, Eugene stands and sits down in another seat, this time with more care. While they wait for their complementary pizza to arrive, Eugene’s mother embarks upon the tortuous task of attempting to converse with her son.) Eugene’s Mother : So, Eugene, honey, are you enjoying your birthday party? Unstoppable Eugene~! : PARTIES FUN! Eugene’s Mother : They certainly are. And you know that you’ll get a birthday party every time you advance a round in the GTT4, right? Unstoppable Eugene~! : EVERY TIME?! Eugene’s Mother : Yep. In fact, it looks like you’ll be having lots of birthdays within the next few weeks as long as you keep making Mommy proud and winning your matches. Unstoppable Eugene~! : YAYYY! EUGENE IS AGING AT AN ACK-SELL-UR-ATED RATE! Eugene’s Mother : Ummm … Not really, Eugene. Just don’t think about why you’re having so many birthday parties and just enjoy them, OK, sweetie? Unstoppable Eugene~! : OKAY, MOMMY. Eugene’s Mother : Good. Your next opponent shouldn’t give you any more trouble than that last loser you faced. His name’s Desolation; you remember that promo we watched by him? The one where he thought he was talking to you, as if you could carry on a semi-coherent conversation? Remember the video by the stupid man, Eugene? Unstoppable Eugene~! : (Proudly) NO! Eugene’s Mother : …Oh, right, what was I thinking? Of course you don’t. Remembering something that happened more than thirty seconds ago isn’t exactly your strong suit. Ah well, you didn’t miss much. All he did was point out that you’re a favorite to win this tournament, which is obvious to anyone just by looking at you, and keep using the word “hegemony,” which I got the distinct impression he didn’t even know the meaning of. At least, it didn’t make sense in a lot of the contexts he used it in. I don’t know if it was that day’s entry on his Word of the Day calendar or what, but somebody’s seriously struggling for material when they say hegemony ten times within a five minute promo. I suppose I could retaliate by picking a random word myself, such as, I don’t know, “meatloaf,” and using it over and over again in a desperate attempt to apply it to the match … but really, what would be the point? Unstoppable Eugene~! : I LIKE MEATLOAF! DESECRATION PROBABLY LIKES IT TOO! I WONDER IF HE TASTES LIKE MEATLOAF! IF MEATLOAF FOUGHT TURKEY IN A WRESTLING MATCH, I BET MEATLOAF WOULD WIN BY INTERFERENCE FROM SALISBURY STEAK! MEAT- Eugene’s Mother : That’s enough, Eugene! But thank you for proving that even you can cut an equally entertaining and coherent promo as this idiot that you’re fighting in the tournament. This man annoys me. Hurt him for Mommy, Eugene. Hurt him badly. Unstoppable Eugene~! : OKAY, MOMMY! I WILL GRIND HIM LIKE MEATLOAF! Eugene’s Mother : There’s a good boy. Oh good, here comes our food. Now eat up, honey, so you can get even bigger and stronger and crush all who oppose you – just like Jesus would do. (The scene fades out as Eugene takes to the task of shoveling whole pizzas at a time into his mouth.)
