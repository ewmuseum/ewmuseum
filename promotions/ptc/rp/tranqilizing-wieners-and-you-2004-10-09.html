Tranqilizing Wieners and You...

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 GTT4…the only thing Dallas Reeves is concerned with right now. He has recently arrived here, in the playland known as the Canadian Prairies, after learning that his home federation of the NGW is closing its doors in a few weeks. The usually upbeat and light-hearted Reeves now sees his current status as both a blessing and a bring-down. Without any other competitions to hold his attention, he can focus wholly on the GTT4 and on Unstoppable Eugene(~!). However, at the same token, he is unsure of his employment once his participation in the GTT4 is done with. That is not enough of a dilemma to sink the heart of a man as amiable as Reeves; indeed nothing in his life is going correctly right now. He still hasn’t heard from his best friend John Hitchin, who has since left the NGW to start up his own wrestling promotion, not extending an invite to Reeves. Jessika, Dallas’ on-again off-again girlfriend, has yet to return from her photo shoot in Cuba, and Reeves is beginning to wonder. On top of that, Reeves’ manager “Alberta” Jack B. Carrington has dragged him out to this farm in Mountmartre, Saskatchewan, the homestead of “Pepsi” Joe Trudeau, in order to be subjected to some interesting training techniques. Within the confines of the musky barn, Dallas Reeves kneels, bare-chested, bathed in the rays of what little sunlight sneaks between the gaps in the barnboards. There’s a black bandana over his eyes and tied amongst his long blond hair. The barn is silent. For a few moments. But then, a commotion erupts, as his burly bodyguard Beastor Brandt scurries in, lifting Reeves to his feet from behind. Ah, the madness that is a “Pepsi” Joe Trudeau work-out. Reeves starts to think back to his first days of training with good ol’ “Pepsi” Joe…but the only thing that he can think of is that new damn Pepsi commercial…with the dog that wears the damn Pepsi Cola sweater. Dallas shakes his head, wondering why his mind won’t stay in one place. Slowly moving in after Beastor, is a seemingly scared Francis Calrissian. Dallas: Frankie…what are you doing here? You know a “Pepsi” Joe work-out is only for men, and those that wish to become one. Dallas chuckles as he looks at Beastor, whom is still scratching his head, wondering what Dallas finds funny about what he just said…or maybe it’s the lice in his scalp. Frankie: I know…but I was thinking maybe this time, I could try and be a man! Dallas: Oh? Frankie: Yes! I was thinking that maybe I could put down my dolls for a day, and really show you what I’m made of! Dallas nods his head at Frankie’s enthusiasm. Frankie gets a certain light in his face, one that shows that he may finally be ready to step up to the big time. And Beastor…well, he’s checking his own body odour…reminiscent of Booger from Revenge of the Nerds. Reeves cringes at the site, and focuses back on Frankie. “Pepsi” Joe: Don’t be ridiculous, Calrissian! Reeves is gonna mop the floor with you, just like we had planned out! The trio in the barn look around, trying to discover the location in which the familiar voice is coming from. Beastor starts to give out mini-yelps as he believes the barn to be haunted. Dallas: Don’t worry Beastor…not every barn in the Mountmartre region of Saskatchewan has a ghost in it…only the majority…and it’s mainly spirits of livestock, not of grumpy old wrestling trainers. “Pepsi” Joe: Grumpy?! I may be old, but I’m certainly not…err…ok, I see your point! Dallas finally sees Trudeau, sitting up on one of the railings in the top of the barn. Beastor panics, and pulls a move right out of Donkey Kong, picking up a random barrel, and hurdling it up at “Pepsi” Joe…to no avail as it almost hits Dallas instead. Dallas turns around, shocked. Dallas: What the eff?! Seriously…I mean, who throws a barrel these days! Beastor bows his head as he takes a few steps back, ashamed that his primal methods didn’t serve him well this time. BOCK-BA-GAWK! Beastor raises his head at once, as the sound of a live chicken brings Heaven to his state of being. He licks his lips as he slowly starts to turn around. Looking out the barn door, in the not so distant backdrop, Beastor sees a group of chickens, and he begins his charge towards them. Beastor goes right out of the barn, leaving the others behind. Dallas looks to Frankie. Dallas: I was wondering how long that was going to take…usually he’s done it once or twice by this time in the day. Frankie: You’re not wearing a watch, how do you know what time of day it is?! “Pepsi” Joe: Enough babbling! Get me down from here, and let’s get on with this! Frankie: I got it! Frankie’s continued enthusiasm impresses Dallas as Calrissian moves towards a large ladder, one that can reach Joe’s position…or disposition if you will. Frankie is a little too enthused as he trips over his own two feet, and faceplants the ground. Dallas shakes his head, laughing at the young Calrissian. Frankie gets up to his feet, and walks the rest of the way, not even looking at Reeves. He positions the ladder, and “Pepsi” Joe comes down. Frankie finally turns towards Dallas, who’s got a frightened look on his face now. Dallas: Ok…time out! I am not wrestling Frankie…especially now that he’s got a cow patty covering his face! “Pepsi” Joe: Boy! Do you want to win the match against Eugene or not? Dallas: I don’t see how that question relates to the pie on his face! Frankie just stands there shocked, as much as he wants to break down and cry, cow pie on his face and all, it’s training time with Trudeau…it’s time to be a man for once in his life. “Pepsi” Joe shakes his head. “Pepsi” Joe: Don’t think of it as a turd patty…Eugene wears a mask does he not? Just think of it as a more authentic Eugene…more than what a plain old Frankie could provide on his own. Dallas: Excuse me? If Eugene smells like that, then I don’t even want to be in the same ring has he…that’s just… “Pepsi” Joe: Quiet! The smell isn’t important, what’s important is that you make it to the next round of the GTT! Dallas: Yeah I guess so… “Pepsi” Joe: Good, now strap these around your wrists! Trudeau throws a seemingly tampered-with pack of Fletchers Ballpark Franks at Reeves. He catches them, and then looks at Trudeau with a morbidly confused look on his face. Dallas: Gunh?! What exactly do these have to do with anything? Trudeau crosses his arms, and widens his eyes as if he were telling Dallas to stop with the questions, and do as he says. Dallas stares at the wieners, and then just shakes his head. Dallas: I won’t do it…that’s just sick…what’d you do to these things anyway? Reeves throws the franks to the ground. He stares at them for a second, then looks up at Frankie who continues to stare at them. Dallas: Tell you what Frankie, you impress me here…and they’re yours. Frankie: Really?! You’d really let me have them! “Pepsi” Joe: Not on your life boy! If Reeves won’t use them, then I’ve got myself some dinner tonight! You…can just…eat grass with the rest of the animals here. Frankie: Really?! That is so cool! Dallas: Anyway…Joe… “Pepsi” Joe: I thought I told you to call me Sensei… Dallas: Uh…no you didn’t…or at least I don’t recall any demand of the sort. “Pepsi” Joe: Oh…well, then I would like it if you called me Sensei from now on, it adds a little bit of mystery to this whole training program that I’m putting you through. Dallas: So am I gonna learn some karate or perhaps some sort of Brazilian shoot fighting of some sort…I mean come on, if you want me to start calling you Sensei, then I wanna learn some cool stuff here…instead of fighting Turd Calrissian over there. “Pepsi” Joe: Tomorrow…we will start the intense training…with some Joe-jitsu and a highly explosive afternoon of Tai-Chi. Dallas: You’ve gotta be kidding me…what does all of this have to do with my match against Eugene. I mean…he’s mentally challenged isn’t he? I shouldn’t even be getting in that ring with him, I’m going to hurt the poor guy. Frankie: Actually Dally…he’s a sixteen-year-old with gigantism… Dallas looks over at Frankie about to get angry with him for calling him Dally. He takes a deep breath, but is interrupted as Beastor makes his way back inside of the barn. In his arms, he holds a chicken, almost as if it were a baby. Reeves is astonished. Dallas: Well will you look at that. Beastor actually caught the chicken, and he’s not trying to eat it! A revelation indeed… BOCK-BA-GAWK! Beastor smiles as the chicken sounds his opinion. Nodding his head in approval, Beastor lets out a grumble. Beastor: Mis-ter…Bock-ba-Gawk… Everyone stands, wide-eyed…staring at Beastor. He just stares back at them, wondering what they’re all staring at. Beastor pets the chicken on the head, before playing with its gobbler with his fingers. Dallas: My lord! Beastor, what did you just say?! Everyone staring at each other wide-eyed, Beastor feels as if he’s trapped in some way. The other 3 just start to smile, as what could’ve very well been the first words ever out of Beastor’s mouth have just come out. Beastor panics, throwing Mr. Bock-ba-Gawk at the trio. He lunges, grabs the pack of wieners off of the ground, and then runs towards the exit. In typical Hollywood-chase fashion, he tips a couple barrels over behind him, to trip up his pursuers. The two other men and Frankie just stare at the wild man as he leaves their sight. Dallas: What the heck was that all about? “Pepsi” Joe: Him taking my damn dinner! That’s what that was about! He’s not the one I wanted to eat those! Dallas: What? “Pepsi” Joe: They popped full of… Trudeau stops himself before revealing the contents of that tampered package. Dallas puts his hands on his hips, raising an eyebrow as his earlier suspicions seem to be true. Dallas: Well well well…it seems we have a scheme on our hands here. What’s going on here…Joe? “Pepsi” Joe: I think we…uh…should be focusing on your beating of a sixteen-year-old here shouldn't we? I mean…it is the next match in the GTT for you…and it’s the only time that you’ll be able to give a little rapscallion a butt-whoopin’ like this, and not get in trouble for it. Dallas: Frankie, get him! Frankie starts to move towards Joe, as his “mask” has hardened, and looks like nothing more than a brown clay…with pieces of grass in it. Joe looks somewhat frightened now as the stinky, “masked” Calrissian starts to move towards him. “Pepsi” Joe: Ok! I give up! I put a horse tranquilizer in each of the wieners! I wanted to see the effects of what it’d do to a person if they ate one or two! I knew Frankie wouldn’t be able to resist eating them if you had them on your wrist…he was gonna be a guinea pig! Frankie: Hey! I happen to like guinea pigs! Dallas: What? Nevermind…you were hoping that it’d work…and then you were gonna feed that man-child Eugene some of them before the match! Make him all groggy for the match…and I was going to win because of that! You mean today’s training is for nothing? I can’t believe this, you out of all people…cheating to help me win! “Pepsi” Joe: No…the training isn’t for nothing, I just wanted to see it happen…that’s all! It didn’t even occur to me about Eugene, I just figure we could use a night or two without Frankie that’s all, he’s quite the handful on the farm. Frankie: Hey! I’m standing right here you know! Why do you say things you know will hurt me?! Frankie starts to tear up, screaming at the top of his lungs as his arms wave back and forth over his head. He charges out of the barn. Dallas: Well…so much for my training partner. “Pepsi” Joe: Nevermind him…if you can beat an old legend such as myself…then you can whoop the “Elephant Man.” Dallas: He’s got gigantism…not elephantiasis. Besides, I’m not going to hit you, that’d be like hitting my Grandmother. “Pepsi” Joe: Don’t give me that boy! Come on…I’ve taken bigger hits from breaking news on channel 5. Dallas: But still, I’m not gonna… “Pepsi” Joe: Just do it! Dallas throws a right cross into the side of Trudeau’s jaw. Joe drops like a sack of potatoes. Out before he hits the ground, “Pepsi” Joe looks so peaceful on the ground. Dallas shakes off his hand as he looks to the barn door opening. Dallas: I’ve gotta find Beastor… Dallas leaves the barn. The sun shines into his eyes, momentarily blinding him until he puts his arm up to block it. He looks around for the Beastor. Looking from left to right, he thinks it’s hopeless, until he sees an arm coming out from behind a tree. Dallas moves towards the arm, stepping through all of the muck associated with the farm. Coming up to the tree, he goes around to the backside of it, and of course, there’s Beastor, hibernating like a bear against the tree. Dallas shakes his head, looking at the pieces of uncooked hotdog wiener stuck in Beastor’s beard. Dallas: You poor…misunderstood hairy man. Sleep well my friend. Dallas stands up, as he looks around the farm, so peaceful…deep in the heart of Canada’s flatlands. He starts to walk around the farm, thinking about his match with the Unstoppable Eugene (~!). Today’s training session didn’t really accomplish anything…but “Pepsi” Joe got his share out of it, hopefully Dallas didn’t inflict any permanent damage to the old man. Beastor…well, he spoke what is to be believed his first words today. Even if it wasn’t complete…bock-ba-gawk has got to be in some dictionary somewhere around the world. And Frankie…well, he just couldn’t cut it today. Apart from falling in cow feces, he wept like a little schoolgirl, he’s nowhere in site. Reeves has the GTT on his mind, advancing to the next round…after defeating that man-child…he sees nothing stopping him. Eugene will just be a mere stepping stone to the next round of the GTT. Frankie: Dallas! Mr. Carrington went to town! He left a note! Dallas looks to the house, seeing a cleaned up Frankie on the porch. Frankie: There’s also a phone message from the Hitch! Frankie seems excited. Dallas just smiles as he starts to make his way back to the house. To check on the message from his best friend, the first contact since their match. Dallas moves forth as the scene fades to black. The words “Dallas Reeves vs Unstoppable Eugene” appear in gold lettering, then fade out. This promo has been released through Dudesy Productions. MMIV
