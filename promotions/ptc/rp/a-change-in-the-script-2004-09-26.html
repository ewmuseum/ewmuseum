A Change in the Script

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Jerms sat in the plush recliner situated perfectly in front of the large mirror in his dressing room. Puffing on a cigar he leaned back in the chair and let out a sigh of relieve. Jerms: This is the live! I am so fantastic! He took one more drag from the cigar and flicked it across the room. What did he care, people were paid to clean up after him. After all, he was a big movie star now. There was a loud knock on the door. Jerms: Busy! The knock came again. Jerms rubbed his eyes in frustration, and then jolted to the door in a mad rage. He flung the door open only to find an equally angry producer standing before him. Jerms: Can I help you? Ed, the Producer, stepped towards Jerms and came eye to eye with him. Producer: You listen to me you little arrogant bastard, you work for me. ME! When I say get on set, that doesn’t mean arrive when you feel like, it means get on set NOW! Jerms doesn’t respond to the Producer and begins to scratch his head. He rolls his eyes and walks out the door to the set. The Producer follows him still chewing him out. Producer: And another thing, did I smell cigar smoke in that room? What have I told you about smoking in that dressing room? It’s a fire hazard, and against the rules. Do you want me to have a heart attack, or better yet shoot you? Jerms throws his hands up in the air in disgust as he continues to walk towards the set. Jerms: God Ed, why are you always breaking my balls? Don’t you ever forget that I am the Technical Spectacle! Producer: Oh shut up you twit! Jerms arrives on set and is quickly rushed to wardrobe, and then to make up. The stage has already been constructed and is ready for shooting as Jerms is about to shoot a dramatic face-off scene in the Jungles of Guadalcanal. A few moments pass and then Jerms is finally ready. Producer: And ACTION! The Jungle was hot and muggy. You could barely see your own hand in front of your face. Corporal Rusty Davis lay deep down in the swamp in stealth. He had to get out of this Jungle and steal a boot. Loud gunfire was heard as a platoon of about seven men ran by him. Corporal Davis held his breath and didn’t move, praying he wouldn’t be cut. Voice from the Platoon: When we find that Benedict Arnold we get back the Diamonds and we end his life! Corporal Davis was a good man, and never could have imagined doing something as awful as he had just done. He let Greed overtake him while on a routine platoon through the Jungle were his former squad was held up until help was available. The platoon encountered heavy enemy fire as they attempted to take out a Generator in an open field which supplied the underground enemy troops with electricity. As they completed the mission, they happened to stumble on a rare diamond that was worth millions which had to have belonged to a high ranking official. The Platoon planned on selling the diamond upon their return home and splitting the profits. Corporal Davis, needless to say, didn’t like that idea and decided to go A-WALL. Corporal: Trust yourself Rusty, everything will work it self out. Well get back to the mainland, sell this damn thing, and head for an Island far away from here. More gunfire erupted from down river. This frightened the Corporal because he had no clue what was going on. He was utterly alone. He began to feel a massive amount of guilt and regret for his actions and wish he would’ve never acted on instinct. He gathered his composure and was amazed at how quickly his feelings on the situation had changed. Corporal: I can’t turn back now, I’m in to deep. I have to finish what I have started. Time to move out. He stood up with his rifle in hand. He looked all around him to make sure the coast was clear as he stepped out of the mud and onto the bank. He continued to creep along the bank as a heavy fog surrounded him. He began to cough. Corporal: This isn’t fog…it’s….it’s a smoke grenade. His eyes began to water and he fell to his knees. He began to roll until he was completely out of the smoke. Lying out in an open field, he rubbed his eyes some more and attempted to sit up when he heard a gun being cocked right behind his head. He froze in fear. Voice: Don’t you dare move a muscle you Benedict Arnold. Corporal (Trembling): Calm down Martinson, it was a mistake. I am so sorry… He began to sob at his former platoon leader’s feet. Martinson: Not this time boy. It’s time to meet your maker. Corporal (Screaming): WAIT!! Let me at least give back the diamond… He reached into his pocket and took out the diamond. He slowly turned around to face the elderly platoon leader and slowly moved his hand up to Martinson’s hand. Martinson reached for it when all of a sudden the Corporal took out the back of his knee with a swift kick. Martinson fell to his knees and the Corporal punched him in the face. The set lights came on, and the fog machine stopped. Producer: CUT!! CUT!!! Jerms what the hell are you doing? You just messed up the whole scene! Jerms: I was improving! The actor playing Martinson began to roll around in sheer agony, screaming. Actor: My leg, it’s broken. Oh God why? Stage hands rushed to his aid as Jerms walked off set. The Producer began to scream at the top of his lungs at Jerms. Producer: Oh, you’ve done it this time Jerms! You’re out of this business for good! Jerms: I’ll be in my dressing room! -The End- -Begin Soliloquy- Do you have any idea what you’ve gotten yourself into Persia? Well let me tell you. First of welcome to my thoughts. Here, I tell it like it is. You made a big mistake entering this tournament. Soon, you will regret it. I hate Wrestling, and I hate Wrestlers. What does this mean you ask? I hate you! You are a talentless loser who has aligned himself with the Evil Empire that is the Four Winds Wrestling. You will realize why I am the Technical Spectacle, and why I am the greatest actor to ever grace his presence in Hollywood. One more thing, GO SEE MY MOVIES! Good night! -End Soliloquy-
