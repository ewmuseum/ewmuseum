Sin City ...

22 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-22 Slowly my eyes began to flutter open. A sharp pain went down my neck, and I tried lifting my hand up to massage it out, but there was a small problem. My hands had been chained together in front of me. Chains connected from a set of steel wraps around my wrist to the walls on the side of me. I tugged at them, but there was no use, these chains weren’t to be broken. I could feel blood caked to the side of my face, and it was mixed with tears and more than likely sweat. Everything was still a bit hazy so I couldn’t quite tell where I was. I struggled to lift my head up, and look forward. That’s when I noticed that I was not alone in this large dark room. It took me awhile to shake the cobwebs out and regain my vision, as I looked forward three figures sat in front of me, each wearing a different colored cloak that covered their faces. All I could tell was that they were people, and they remained very silent and very still as they watched me finally awake from my slumber. Things began getting clearer and clearer the more time that passed. The room was only dimly lit by candles that were scattered around. The place seemed like some kind of ancient temple or cathedral of some kind, with all sorts of statues, religious paintings, and monuments hidden in the shadows. I began examining my surroundings, wondering where I was, but probably should have first been thinking how the hell I got there. My thoughts were interrupted by a booming voice, it came from one of the three who sat in front of me … but the voice didn’t seem quite human, almost ghostly in fact. “Do you know where you are Roberto?” I jerked my head in the direction of the people seated in front of me. The fact that they knew my name didn’t help matters, as now my curiosity was even further peaked. I didn’t answer right away, I was in bit of awe. I squinted my eyes slightly trying to focus better on them, but their faces were unreadable. “Did you not hear me Roberto ? Do you know where you are ?” “N..No.” They knew the answer to that question, I could barely remember what happened after I left the bar let alone where the hell I was. I lowered my head, because it began to be too much of a chore to hold it up any longer. I just continued to take in deep breaths, calming myself. “You probably do not remember last night. You were in an accident Roberto, a horrible accident. You crashed into a tree not far from the bar you were at, and went flying through the windshield.” “How did I survive ?” I expected an immediate response, but to my surprise there wasn’t one. This fact frightened me, as I forced my head up so I could look at them. I tried to hide the worry in my voice, but I could not. “HOW ?” “You didn’t Roberto.” My bottom lip began to quiver and once again I could feel tears begin to swell up. My first reaction was that someone was playing a sick game on me. They knew I would be vulnerable right now, with my nightmares and worries about everything lately … but then … no one knew any of that. Every muscle in my body tightened up, as the events of the night flashed through my very eyes. Then I remembered something … why I had swerved. “DID SHE LIVE !?” “Who Roberto?” “Cindy … did she live ? I swerved to miss her, I don’t know why she was in the middle of the street, but she was. Please tell me she is alright!” “Roberto … you know that Cindy died long ago in Spain. You were merely dreaming when you saw her.” “NO ! She was there I swear it ! I saw her …” I couldn’t say anything else at that moment, as the tears began falling down my cheeks. I fought them back with everything I had but I just couldn’t, my body was driving itself now. Things just couldn’t be happening like this … I couldn’t be dead, I was standing right here ! I was dreaming, or on drugs, or sleeping … yeah SLEEPING ! “Roberto you are at a crossroads right now. Your death was unexpected to us, and we had not fully prepared. You see everyone reaches us at one point or another. I am the gate keeper. Behind me are two sets of doors … one leads to Heaven and one leads to Hell. The question is Roberto, which do you belong in ?” I couldn’t answer that question, there was no way I could. There has been a lot that’s happened to me that wouldn’t help my cause. My stare fell to the ground, all but letting them know exactly the way I felt. I gritted my teeth, and tightened up trying my best to cope with everything around me. “Very well Roberto … I will tell you what we know. You have done some horrible things in your life. You have betrayed the ones that have cared about you, you have tried to cripple your bestfriend, you took the lives of your wife and child, and you even led your younger brother to death. Do you really understand the kind of devastation that you have caused ? Do you understand the anguish that these people felt ? I do Roberto … and there is only one thing that will make you understand as well.” “What ?” “Roberto for you to understand your sins … you must revisit them. You must look into your soul and search in the shadows. To repent your sins you must take responsibility for it all. So you tell me … do you wish to feel that pain again ? or simply find your way to Satan and his hell ?” Was it really something to think about ? relive the hardest parts of my life, or an eternity of Hell … there wasn’t a choice to make here. “Show me my sins…” The one in the middle looked to his right and nodded at his partner who had on a black robe. The one in the black robe stood up from his seat and stepped from his spot, walking towards me. He seemed to almost glide towards me, not making a sound as he approached. He looked down at my battered body, then slowly revealed his face. My eyes looked up his … and my mouth dropped. “Lexicon ?” “The afterworld is what you make it Roberto … I am but a messenger. Are you ready ?” I merely shook my head. Lexicon then closed his eyes and placed his right hand on my forehead. A wind began to pick up into the room, and soon a flash of white light blinded me, and we weren’t in the dark room anymore. Lexicon and I stood next to each other, at the side of a road, a road that I knew all too well. “Roberto … your life of hatred and evil began here. This is the night that changed what you thought about life and about god forever … this was your first real sin.” I said nothing but turned my attention to a set of headlights that began coming from the west. I knew it immediately, and the beat of my heart began to pick up. Another car came speeding around the bend to the opposite direction, I couldn’t allow this to happen again. I quickly moved to the center of the road, putting my hands up. “NO STOP!” It was as if they didn’t even see me … the cars tried avoiding each other but crashed head on, with me in the middle. The cars were barely recognizable after they collided. I was unharmed though, physically anyways. I looked over at Lexicon, who simply gave an unreadable mask. I turned my gaze over to one of the cars and ran to the driver side door. I reached out and ripped the door from the hinges, tossing it to the side. I reached in, and pulled out the woman who had went head first into the wind shield. My entire body began to shake as I dropped to the pavement holding her in my arms. Tears began flowing as I held her close to my chest, rocking back and forth. I didn’t even notice that Lexicon was standing right behind me now. He reached a hand out and placed it on my shivering shoulder. “You cannot interfere here Roberto … this is the night that YOU decided you would have too much to drink and attempt to drive home. No one told you to drive because you should have known what kind of consequences come with such actions. You hold Cindy in your arms now … you killed her, and your baby.” “Starr …” “It’s time to go Roberto … “ “No, Let me …” He didn’t let me finish as he placed his hand on my head and the flash of white light blinded me again. When I regained my vision I was still seated, as if I were cradling Cindy … but she wasn’t there. Snow was all around me, in fact I was sitting in some inches of it. I looked up expecting Lexicon to be there … but instead I saw a man I hadn’t seen in awhile. David Sandy, aka RaZiel sat on a bench. His presence startled me and I pushed myself to my feet. RaZiel sat there, not even acknowledging me standing near him. He seemed to be in deep thought about something. I missed him … he helped me through some very tough times in my life. He was the only one there for me when I thought that living wasn’t worth it anymore. “He did help you a great deal Roberto …” I jumped a bit and looked behind me as Lexicon approached. “Yes he did … but he also lied to me.” “He knew how you’d react to him being in love with your ex-fiance’ so of course he’d be wary. He knows you better than anyone Roberto, that is why on this day … your actions were inexcusable.” I was a bit confused, but looked as lexicon pointed towards a figure that was walking towards the bench RaZiel was sitting in. The figure was me, but yet not quite me. My face was blank as I approached, and my eyes weren’t the normal blue … but darker. Immediately I knew what was to happen. “NO ! That’s not ME Lexicon !” “Sure it is, look at him … at YOU.” “That’s HIM … that’s LeStatt…” I turned again just in time to see the silver of the bat slide from “MY” trench coat, and come up and down on RaZiel’s head. Blood oozed from RaZiel’s unconscious body, and that sick bastard smiled. In a rage a rushed him, but I went right through his body falling into the snow behind him. All I could do was watch the attacker walk away. “That wasn’t me … you have to believe me.” “You still aren’t learning Roberto. You and LeStatt Knight are one and the same. You have used the mask of LeStatt for years to cover up your darkest fears and your hatred for those around you. You have allowed your dark side to control you when you can’t afford to most ! You must learn to control it, and sadly it doesn’t seem like you can.” “He is too powerful! He knows everything about me, my weaknesses, my strengths !” “HE IS YOU ! YOU have the power to destroy him … YOU have the power to control him!” “I CAN’T !” Everything grew quiet now. Lexicon just stared at me, probably in disappointment. This was all just too much to handle that I was about to crack, I couldn’t take watching all of this, all of the sins I have committed. “One more then … ONE more sin and perhaps you can fully understand yourself.” The bright light flashed before my eyes and I was kneeling in front of a tombstone. A steady rain fell down on top of my head, and ran down my face. My long hair stuck to the sides of my face, hiding most of my features. I read the names on the Tombstones outloud … “Cindy and Starr Abitua.” A voice behind me came then, and it wasn’t Lexicon’s. This voice was one that I longed to hear, a voice that could soothe me no matter what. A small hand came down on my shoulder, and I turned my head to see Mary standing behind me. A very concerned look was on her face. “Knightie … I dunno why you brought me here.” I can’t explain how the words came out, but it was almost as if they were programmed. I began telling Mary about my family, the family that I had killed. The rain hid my tears this time, although I am sure she could tell. I poured my heart out to her … then as the rain came down as hard as ever I told her the words I wanted to tell her for a long time. “Mary … I love you.” To my surprise she backed away from me, not saying anything. Then she cupped her face into her hands and began to sob. She turned her back to me, and I wanted to reach out to her and comfort her, but didn’t know how. “Mary … I’m …I …” “You’re sorry right ?” A deep voice came from behind me, and I spun around to see Lexicon. I quickly turned back to Mary but she was gone, so I turned my attention back to Lex. “I don’t understand, this was the night when Mary and I began the only relationship that I hold dear to me now.” “No Roberto, what you did was drag Mary into your life. You brought her into a life that you cannot control. You forced her hand by telling her you loved her, if you truly cared for her you wouldn’t have put her in that position.” “No ! This you are wrong about ! I Love Mary and she loves me. We will be together forev…” “FOREVER!? Isn’t that what you said about Cindy and Wylie as well ? No Roberto you don’t know about true love, nor can you keep a promise as “FOREVER”. You have proven time and time again that those who get close to you regret it … why pull her into that !? this my friend … is your greatest sin !” Everything in my body was empty … was this my life ? was I really meant to be empty and alone? I dropped to my knees and dug my face into my hands, trying to make sense of it all. There is no way I could believe this was it … it couldn’t be … “NO !” When I raised my head up I was at the beginning again. My hands chained up and staring at the three who brought me here. “End of the line Roberto … You have proven to us what needs to happen.” I looked around the room as the candles that lit it began to flicker. Then in unison all of the candles fell over, and quickly the flames began to spread. They grew and grew until they covered everything. I fought to rid myself of my chains but it did not work … I screamed and growled as my efforts were in vein. Then a voice … “Welcome … to your nightmare.” I knew that voice … it was impossible to forget. A small opening in the flames came about and through it walked to the one CREATURE that I really feared. “LESTATT !” “yes Roberto … It is me. You see This world works in mysterious ways, and now I AM YOUR HELL.” “No … you can’t ! I control you !” “No Roberto … your life is now mine.” I tried rushing him but got brought back violently by the chains that bound me. His laughter began to ring through my ears … it felt as if my ear drums were going to burst. I fell to my knees grasping at my ears, trying to get the laughter out. But it just kept getting louder and more hysterical. Until I couldn’t take it anymore … “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” A flash of light and a loud boom came as I sat up in bed. Sweat poured down my body, as I looked around not quite sure what hell happened. I looked out my bedroom window, and rain began pouring down. My entire body was shaking … “You okay Knightie ?” “MARY !” I grabbed onto her with everything I had, not wanting to let go. The things I had seen and felt … I couldn’t believe that’s how it would end. Perhaps things needed to change …
