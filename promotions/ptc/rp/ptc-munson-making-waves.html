IG 31 RP - Making Waves

7 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrstler: "Big Bear" Munson Monsoon Fed: PRIME The sound of whispering was overheard as the scene began to take form. The camera opened up in the elevator lobby of an office building. Standing at the elevator was a large, burly figure. He was not Fabio, but he was thick and strong. Big, too. The elevator dinged as his eyes watched the numbers descend from the upper levels. A slim and sexy brunette steps up next to him, wearing a skirt suit. She glanced up at the man. He had been looking in her general direction, although sensing her attention he looked to the opposite side. He wore a brown suit that was much too small for him. The pants ended just above his ankles. He wore a pair of black-plastic frame glasses. His hair was brown, medium in length. His eyes were brown. He had a handle bar mustache. “BIG BEAR~!” A middle-aged man, balding on the top of his head approached the man in the brown suit and whispered to him. He extended a handshake to the expressionless figure, glancing over to the woman who was smirking a bit at the situational humor. “Hello Fred. How are you?” He yanked at his friend’s collar to the other side. “Look chumpfink; keep your cake hole closed! If anybody finds out… thatIwrestle…I’ll be toast before you can jam the copier in Yuniss’ ass! And we both know that’s a three second operation, Fred.” The two stood upright, the woman’s eyes stopped looking at them and then looked forward as the golden doors opened. Ding. ------------------------------------ Big Bear was sitting at his cubicle. He was writing frantically, scribbling things down so fast his tongue was falling out of his mouth. He stopped as drool hit the paper and he made the ‘ewwww’ face. He sat back, cleaning his personal space. As he ran a hand through his hair, he opened up the second drawer of his metal desk. He pulled out a letter. It was short, brief, and to the point. Prime Time Central Infinite Gauntlet 31 Open Invitational All PTC affiliate Members Welcome! Bring you’re A game. Big Bear scanned the room, knowing what it said. He has read it thirty-seven times this morning. He has read for the past three nights, fifty more times than the night prior. His dream was in his grasp. All this hobby talk needed to stop. This was his chance to make a name for himself. Make that saucy brunette quit thinking of him as some desk jockey and the show-stopping, jaw-dropping Lumberjack from Bear Creek, Alaska. He sat at his desk, holding his head in his hand. The phone rang, causing him to lift his head in shock. Was this a sign? If it was, he better pick up the phone before the sign passed him by. “Michael Munson…” His eyes shifted from side to side, listening carefully. He nodded as he curled his upper lip under the lower. “Yes sir. On my way, sir.” Big Bear hung up the phone, now holding onto another letter. This one read a little different. Dear Munson Monsoon, You’re recent performance in the ring has captured the eyes and hearts of many internet wrestling fans. In fact of all the talent we have scouted from your designated market, Big Bear was the strongest contender and fan favorite out there. At PRIME the level of competition is unparalelled. PRIME is the industries top company, offering an all-star roster that has no weak spots at all. If you are interested in pursuing a career in the spotlight of the wrestling world, entertaining fans all over the Earth, and getting your chance at gold – call PRIME now. Your Future is Here, PRIME He stuffed it under his jacket, into the breast pocket. For the past few months, Mike Munson has been examining his life. The professional phone-call desk jockey was not exciting him. The hobby life as Big Bear Munson Monsoon was getting old. He was working hard for the fans, they loved him for it. His heart and confidence were empty though. He has no awards, belts, or pats on the back for giving his mind, body, and soul to the sport he so loved. But here was the only chance he had ever been given to entertain the type of people he always wanted to electrify – strangers, Japanese people, and midgets too. Munson stood up and walked down the hallway. ------------------------------------- The scene came into focus from behind Big Bear, seated in front of a large oak desk. Behind it sat a mid-sized man. He had grey hair, a white mustache, and a blue tie to match his suit. “Look, Mike. The numbers are down. We’ve just lost our CFO. We’re really in a bad spot. A raise just isn’t in the cards right now.” Big Bear leaned back, folding his hands together and letting out a deep breath. “Mister Royce, I’m going to be perfectly honest. I don’t want this job. In fact, this job is better suited for some janitorial master…like Tommy here.” “Tommy is my daughter, Lois. Put her picture down.” Big Bear cleared his throat, setting Mrs. Royce’s portrait back on the mantle. She was pretty rough around the edges. “She’s got your eyes…” Big Bear was standing, overlooking the city from his boss’ window. He stuffed his hands in his pockets. More than fifteen jillion ways to tell his boss his time was up ran through his mind, but he couldn’t pick just one. “So you like wrestling?” Big Bear stopped in his tracks, turning slightly to see Mr. Royce displaying a picture of himself and Mongo McMichael. Mike walked over to the wall, taking a deep breath and looking his boss in the eye with all the courage he could muster up. “Sir…Mister Royce…I’m going to be leaving Royce, Royce, Royce & Royce Pretzel Company. I just can’t do this to myself anymore.” “What? You don’t love pretzels?” “No sir, I do! It’s certainly not even a debate when it comes to pretzels over other baked snacks, it just so happens that my heart is somewhere else. Ever since I left Alaska, I had a dream. A dream to succeed and conquer the one thing I have ever wanted.” “Not pretzels, I take it…” “In that very ring. The wrestling ring, sir. The one place where I am myself. The one place where I exterminate the likes of a Chainz, or Nova-“ Mr. Royce gasped, checking around for any members of FU. “-Sonny Silv-“ Mr. Royce cupped his hand over big Bear’s mouth. “Look, son. I know two things; Pretzels and who not to talk shit about. If it’s one group you don’t want messing with you, or you’re pretzels, it’s FU. Now please, stop licking my hand!” Mr. Royce wiped his hands on Big Bear’s sleeve. Big Bear licked his lips. “Mr. Royce, sir; I have only a few dreams. One of them happens to be that I get to bring whatever fight those assholes deserve to their doorstep. No matter how hard it is, no matter how tough it gets; I’m not giving in to some subconscious fear of failure. I’m not making the money here, and that’s fine. But for the same amount, I can be entertaining thousands…millions…septillions of fans! PRIMEates! PTC has thousands alone! True, blue-collar, hard-workin’ rednecks who understand what its like to be an underdog!” “Like a gutter ball that you throw right off the bat ‘cause you’re shitty bowling shoes have holes in the back so bad it feels like a dough knife is slicing into your heel. Suddenly, that gutter ball bounces out – clearing the entire rack of pins! Bowling analogies are just one of my many skills; the other is head butting people so hard they think I have a bowling ball for a head.” Mr. Royce gave the blank stare. “Mister Royce, the point is – I’m taking this opportunity. I’m going to PRIME. I’m going to Infinite Gauntlet 31. I’m going to go celebrate the fact that we are all alive, and as long as we are I’ll be doing whatever I can to keep our most prestigious titles safe from the scum of this world – like the PTC Unified belt and the PRIME Universal, Intense, and 5 Star Championships. My goal will not just to be a champion, but to be the man the women and children adore; the man that, in the face of the fiercest of competition, people expect to do what is right. I will conquer this sport with grace, with honor…honor and dignity. That is my destiny, sir.” Another blank stare. Munson got the picture as he shuffled his feet and headed for the door. “Michael…” Munson stopped and peered over his right shoulder. Mr. Royce was right behind him, holding a check. Big Bear turned around all the way. He knew Mr. Royce was a kind man, but not that kind. Big Bear looked at the check then back at Mr. Royce. He nodded as he patted Mike’s shoulder. “Just do one thing for me, son.” Big Bear raised an eyebrow and opened his ears. This was going to be good, for sure. “When you get the chance…remind FU where they sit on the food chain next to bears.” They both shared an inner chuckle, but this was serious MAN~! talk. No laughing. Only a nod, handshake, and exit from Munson. Munson Monsoon: PRIME’s freshest face, PTC’s newest participant, and the wrestling world’s first charismatic internet hero would need to be stepping things up. He was entering his first open challenge in Prime Time Central. The winner of Infinite Gauntlet 31 gets a shot at the newly Unified belt somewhere down the line. That wasn’t set in stone. What was is the fact that everyone involved in this melee will be putting out more than they can handle, both physically and mentally. Big Bear was making his march to his PRIME debut. He was making his way to the first PTC event of his career, also. He has entered in the TEAM Supershow IV FREE FOR ALL~! Title Elimination match, too. The bottom line was; Big Bear was not afraid of making waves in this pool. In fact, he hopes that everyone has followed his advice so far. Big Bear hates saying things twice.
