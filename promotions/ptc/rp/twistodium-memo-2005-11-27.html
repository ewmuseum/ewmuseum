Twistodium: Memo

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 From the desk of Roland P. Morris DEA… Dear members of Congress, It has come to my attention that many performers and athletes use common steroids to increase their ability in their chosen profession. These people often can keep themselves in check with these legalized drugs, but often times it can get out of hand. Sometimes it is the specific performer’s misuse of the drug or sometimes it is that the drug itself is too powerful and hazardous for human beings to use. Currently there is a steroid on the market that is hurting many people because of its high addictive qualities, its cause of dementia, its rapid power, and other side effects. This drug is the steroid Twistodium produced by the billion-dollar organization Drugs N’ Stuff Inc. My recommendation is that it be recalled from stores immediately and indefinitely suspended from retail sale. The company Drugs N’ Stuff obviously did not test this drug harshly enough or if they did they chose to ignore their results. We in the DEA have found that Twistodium causes its users to become extremely dependent on the drug to even get through the day. They develop memory loss and in some cases hallucinate in the present as well as past memories. These users even develop a strange obsession and fixation on the farm animal the goat. Their fascination is completely unexplainable, but is most definitely derived from using the steroid. It must be known that these side effects exist. We must do what we can to protect the public from such a terrible and powerful steroid. Thank you for your time and please vote wisely. Sincerely, Roland P. Morris
