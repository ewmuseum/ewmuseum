preternatural selection : point A (an outline)

3 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2010-01-03 + Alias, professional wrestler and man on the run, is heading to pick up his car with Karina Wolfenden, who recently put AD's Halo on him, which should conceivably keep him from getting killed. K-Wolf tells Alias to keep an eye on Angelo's old right hand man Able Ichabod and his close friend, Riggs McQueen, as they might look to turn in Alias and kill K-Wolf on the drop of a hat. Soon afterwards, Russel David is on his way to find hospital records of Jackson Chase, not knowing the fail safe plan hidden within such a record, and that Nixon Monroe, who Riggs claims owes him money after their time spent together in prison, disappeared in the first fifteen minutes of running from the fire. On there way to the car though, Able wants to stop by and get the good doctor who might be able to help Alias... so that he doesn't slow them down. The good doctor has been shot dead and Sheffield has left is calling card. + Russel David visits a hospital in Memphis but is knocked unconscious, and awakes to find himself being questioned by his Nixon Monroe (knows more about Jackson Chase then any of the others except for Alias might expect), who informs David that what there here for is not proof on Jackson Chase being Alias, but for a certain Black Wisdom that was placed here by he and Jackson Chase in 1999. + We pick up on a car chase scene of epic proportions that ends with Riggs ramping up the L.A. Rivers concrete sides and THROUGH a dude with a shotgun. Able is all "Damn am I glad you came into work today, when you didn't even have to." Later, Alias calls Monet Samuel, who is safe at the Jacob's household and just wanted to let him know that he is safe and nothing but reports of an EAR(?!) were found at her home, they get in an argument that goes back to Alias coming back into Monet's life thanks to her disappeared father. + Flashback to Alias and Nixon Monroe in a nightclub, but upon hearing that Alias has pissed off many a nasty bastard and he has an answer that would allow him to become a pro wrestler again, Nixon tells Alias about the 'Iron Ira', something sleek jet-powered zepplin, suspected of bringing mysterious goods into port, and upon which both Christopher Sheffield and Bruce Shannahan are rumored to have disappeared on. Alias learns that Nixon has been working for the government as an informer and as well as the Sect of Black Wisdom as a mole, but is allowed no contact with his family. Alias tells Nixon of when he was tortured by Shannahan and the brainwashing he left behind. + While driving to Alias also discovers that the good doctor had found a laundry list of things wrong with Alias and the belt, including something that the Original Pulp Hero doesn't recognize. It seems that Sheffield planted a tracker on Alias and has been tracking him and sending others to attack him at his own amusement. Alias gets a call, from Ivy McGuinness. Also Able has found papers on the good doctors desk that notes that he has prostate cancer. + Russel David and Nixon Monroe pay a visit to the HQ of Avaritia Enterprises where he meets Zephyr Coney (a possible future love interest... for Alias), a young tearaway whom Nixon had returned to her wealthy parents on several previous occasions. Zephyr reveals that she has stayed at a clinic named the Gerasene Institute (in Austin) as she's kind of a Paris Hilton. + In Vegas, K-Wolf, with Nixon Monroe, places a bet with the manager of the Loaded Hand, one Bona Fortuna, that Alias will win GTT7. + In Austin, Russel and Nixon visit the Institute, where they encounter Alias and company, and deduces that Sheffield isn't working for the Black Wisdom, but by persons unknown of equal danger. Alias then reveals that he sent David after the Jackson Chase information as a way to throw a hail mary at Violence Jack who it happens... will protect him as long as they can lure his father, Citizen into a trade. Father for son. It is questioned where Riggs is, and Ichabod notes that the kid needed to unwind so he went and saw a movie. + McQueen, having just seen Up In The Air at the Draft House. He writes something down on a piece of paper and throws it into a waste-bin. + Meanwhile Alias is then told, by Nixon, that Sheffield works for a group going by the name of W.A.S.T.E. (which stands for "We Await Sudden Terrorist Extremists", because damn it I'm in this division final for a reason) and that Nixon is going to have to turn on Alias's Black Wisdom brainwashing when he goes deep just in case he might try something in the run for Citizen. Karina argues against it but the Halo secures that Alias is the best for the job in this. Russel David is ushered to safety, and is told he will be given information on the long lost wrestling federation known as Legion for his troubles. The Devil's Men take Alias to track down Citizen with K-Wolf as well. Alias isn't going anywhere without her, no matter what his condition. + Alias is put into his world of contentment, where he is "The Alias" Christopher Sheffield, and his best friend is Vince Jacobs. He is married to Monet, has a beautiful daughter Izzy and has just won GTT7. "Helter Skelter" by The Paranoids is his theme music. Still even with his happy marriage and wonderful life, he celebrates with Karina Wolfenden. Everything is not as it seems, and it's already cracking at the seems, he is not content. + Alias and The Devil's Men discover links between a notorious loan shark named Quinn Fiction, and Citizen's latest siting. Citizen is a regular pulp hero. After a visit from Grace Fortnight, a female companion of Citizen, Vox and the Devil's Men travel to Austin in search of Fiction. + Through one of the last connections that Alias has, that doesn't want to kill him, he discovers that Quinn Fiction is holed up in an old jazz bar. Alias visits the site and encounters Fiction. A lone trumpet player is playing a muted post horn which has one loop. Fiction is an ecentric and has Citizen bound in the basement, having had his people catch him on one of his latest escapades against the Fat Tony crime family. Again with the Fat Tony, thinks Alias, and he also wonders how a man like this can have 'men' of his own. They decide that Alias can have him if he wins a staring contest against Quinn Fiction. Not just any staring contest though, but one which melds minds and twists reality into a Dali-painting of insanity inducing intensity. + As the world shakes, we once again go inside Alias's head... and by his love for Karina, Sheffield finds that this world of greatness surronding him, his father, Monet, Izzy, his son, every... it isn't real. It never was. It fades to white. + This led to Alias coming to... and blinking. Fiction smiles, he has won. He gets a bullet between the eyes for his troubles. Alias turns to discover he had been set up, Sheffield had sent the man with one ear Alias's location and now that man was going to kill Alias. Boom. Only he gets his head blown off. There stands Riggs... and beside him... Christopher Sheffield holding onto the Citizen mask. W.A.S.T.E. is mail distribution service cum black-ops government agency (the wonder of 200 years of beurocracy) and he had been used by the authorities as an unofficial assassin, effectively immune from the law (who better then a man who no long existed), and who employed Riggs, and permitted Fiction to capture Citizen. + We get a peak of what happened to Sheffield, as he and Alias sit in a car with Brian Sheffield, before Brian puts a bag over his head, opens the door, Alias throws a blanket over him and they kick him out of the car, "Don't forget the gasoline." + A: "Why do you think my ring gear had flames licking at my legs for the first five years after I returned. That night haunted me. It still does." We're back in New Orleans. Suddenly Riggs head explodes, and it's Able. "He was like a son, like a brother... guess I should have should have seen this coming." He shoots at Sheffield, but it rockets off the helmet. So there's a stand-off. "Hit the road Jack." A: "My father..." IA: "Probably isn't even in the basement." CS: "I wouldn't say he's a smart man, since he's made the decision to point a pistol at me, but he is astute." A: "You, Ichabod... if you stay here he'll kill you." CS: "I will." IA: "Better now then later." + Alias jumps into the passenger side, K-Wolf is in the back, Monroe is driving. K-Wolf rubs his neck, "Karina..." "Don't smile so dumb, you gave me a heart attack, Bigby." "What happened in there?" "Natural selection..." A flash within the dark and empty jazz club brightened the windows for a split second before they where once again dark... before they then disappeared into the night as the car sped towards Miami. "Natural selection always wins."
