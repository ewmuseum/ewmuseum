Date: 2005-11-26 Santa Barbara News-Press Arson Back At It Again 11/15/05 by Timothy Dougan SANTA BARBARA - An all too familiar occurrence gripped the town of Santa Barbara, California yesterday as local authorities stormed the home of famed professional wrestler, Brian Arson, for the third time in the past six years. Arson, more notoriously known to authorities on the West Coast with his involvement in a southwestern narcotics network, was taken into custody by local Santa Barbara police. Details on the current situation are still unfolding as detectives investigate Arson’s home and continue their questioning. For those living in the region around the towns of Santa Barbara and Ventura, the raid came as nothing new as illegal activity surrounding the Arson household has become somewhat of the norm in recent years. Many recall a few years ago when Arson was taken into custody, believed to be linked to the murder of Pablo Marinez. Marinez was found dead, yards away from a van carrying hundreds of kilos of cocaine. Authorities believed that Arson had a hand in a drug ring that was being closely investigated by the FBI, but there was not enough evidence to connect Arson to the crime, and he was released shortly thereafter. A month later Arson joined the Dominant Wrestling Organization but left after nearly two weeks when the federation shut down. Arson’s latest venture started when he joined Core Wrestling, coming off a long hiatus after the closure of Rush Pro Wrestling. His stay there was ended abruptly after his father, James K. Arson, chief partner of the well-known Arson and Smith law firm located in downtown Santa Barbara, died this past September after a massive stroke. Arson has not fallen into the hands of the law until now. According to many online wrestling web sites, Arson was expected to enter the famous Golden Turnbuckle Tournament hosted by Primetime Central in December, but now it seems he may have bigger issues on hand. What started off as a promising career just over a decade ago has turned into a downward spiral off wrong turns and mistakes for the now thirty-year old, retired wrestler. Arson has declined to speak to the press thus far, but surely there is much left to be written in the ongoing saga of this infamous Santa Barbara patron.
