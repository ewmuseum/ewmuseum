An Orgy Of Questions...

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Distractions My life has been full of them. I could have been anything my dad said. The same song and dance you get when you are a child but like many Americans all I saw were money signs. They were large. They would get me nice houses, cars, women, and security. I thought the easiest way was to work hard at what I could do. I started out as a cargo pilot with my expertise in flying but that got me very little. I married into a made family hell bent on becoming the drug czars of New York and there I was with several late car payments and a need for success. Nothing occurred at that time but what I did do was meet my ex-wife who I won’t give the courtesy of naming. Instead of asking her family if they needed some help I asked her if she was hungry. I was distracted… From there I was thrust into their lifestyle and everything I dreamt of owning and doing began to come true. I was an outsider to them being half Mexican and half Caucasian but they liked my results. I could weasel in and out of countries with their money bring back their drugs and I was getting paid well. It was a dream come true. I had several nice cars and I had a wonderful house and I wife that I loved. Unfortunately everything can fall apart quickly when your wife begins doing the drugs and her family blames it on you. They do not even look in the mirror and realize I am doing what they asked. They just assumed I was giving her these drugs and that I was doing it with her because of my nationality. Do not get confused with my latest action with doing coke because that was the only time I had ever been hit by that wall. I was as clean as I could be back then other then the usual drink when I could no longer handle her begging for coke. We had Angelo while she was using and I was said that he was a miracle baby because he turned out ok. No defects or addictions and I loved him as much as I ever thought I could love someone. This was a good distraction. I needed him but even more he needed me because his mom was never there. It was not to be though because she became worse and her family became more enraged with me and then several years later the marriage was over and I was squeezed out of my son’s life. Now I have taken him back and have once again given myself distractions. I have left the cartel and taken their money so I could take my son from my bitch of an ex-wife whose family is made and very connected. I have done all of this to find happiness. Golden Turnbuckle Tournament? That is what I rest my life upon. How can I concentrate on such an important event when my life is extremely volatile? I cannot answer that question but you can rest assure that I am not going to give up because my stress level has exploded into dangerous realms. I have a lot of money that I have taken from Juan but when it is goes away what do I have? Distractions… I will have absolutely nothing to help my son live well. I cannot get a nine to five where I sit in an office because I am easy prey there. I have to be able to move often and quickly when any danger might turn its head towards my grizzled face. Wrestling is that industry and if I cannot succeed in this tournament I may as well hand deliver my son back home and let the gunshots begin. It is do or die not ‘this is fun no matter the result.’ If you think like that you are bound to fail. I cannot afford to fail just I cannot afford using credit cards or shaving. I need to be like a ghost and if I am seen I am not recognizable. None of you can understand the loneliness and despair that I have gone through. You cannot even fathom how difficult it is to look over your shoulder ten times a minute just waiting for that final breath. It is inevitable, isn’t it? No distractions well deter me from this dream. No distractions will make me give up and run away from it all. I am multi-tasking and I plan on one thing and one thing only: victory. I will escape, I HAVE TOO! I will survive, MY SON NEEDS ME. I have something to live for now and I won’t let anything get in my way. Must Truck On… ==== Enemy Lines Juan is seen pacing outside next to his pool. His hands are clenched into fists and sweat drips from every part of his body. He has a sneer on his face that goes well with how red it has become. He is muttering to himself but it doesn’t even sound like words. A bigger man dressed in all black with guns sticking from his waist stands behind Juan staring at him with a mean look on his face. Juan stops pacing and turns around and quickly wiping the sweet from his hair onto his lavish shorts. “How did this happen?” Juan asks angrily to the big man. The big man steps forward, “he delivered the shipment and then left with the money and did not come back,” he says. Juan sighs and shakes his head, “I know that you buffoon. I mean how did that fool find a backbone?” He says. The big man shrugs, “I have no idea, sir,” he says. Juan begins pacing again as he punches his hands towards his sides, “how much did he take from me, FROM ME!” he exclaims. “Over five hundred thousand dollars, boss,” the big man says. Juan growls and stops his pacing again and stares at the big man again with vengeance in his eyes, “get him,” he says. “Dead or alive?” Juan laughs, “I want that mother fucking traitor tortured to death. I want his eyes tacked to my wall,” he says crazily. The big man grunts, “that can be done,” he says seriously. Juan looks at him and raises his eyebrow, “well what are you waiting for?” he asks impatiently. “Do you need me to teach you how to kill or how to find that goddamn gnat?” “No sir. I just don’t know where you want me to start looking for him. We have the man power to go to several locations but it would be nice to have those known instead of running around and wasting resources,” the big burly man says. Juan scoffs, “very well,” he says annoyed. “I have all the info about Gonzalo inside and believe me when I say that if you need to question his family there are no limitations on what you can do to them,” he says angrily as he storms off towards his mansion. The big man takes the cue and follows him toward it with a serious and; also, mean look on his face as he readies himself for violent actions. The Hit is On… ==== Escaping Towards Paradise “This is a sweet car, dad,” the happy voice of Angelo Rey says as he jumps joyfully up and down on his seat with a big grin upon his face. Gonzalo looks over for a second and laughs, “thanks, it took some maneuvers to get the right price,” he says. He looks back on the road and concentrates on his driving but constantly looks in his rearview mirror and to what cars on his driver’s side. His paranoia is never-ending but it is better to be careful then cocky. “Where are we going?” Gonzalo’s son asks curiously. Gonzalo does not respond right away and keeps his eyes on the road. He sighs as he thinks to himself, “there really isn’t…” Angelo interrupts Gonzalo, “we going to see grandpa?” he asks. Gonzalo stops at a red light and mumbles to himself, “uh…I have not spoken to grandpa in a two years,” he says. “Why?” Gonzalo sighs and slumps his shoulders, “I have been busy I guess. I really don’t have an excuse, son, but if you want I will try to get a hold of him,” Gonzalo says. His son smiles and nods, “yea he always gave the coolest presents,” he says. Gonzalo laughs, “I am surprised you even remember,” he says. “So where is grandpa?” Angelo asks with a big smile chiseled on his young face. Gonzalo shrugs and begins driving again as they pass a welcome to Ohio sign. Gonzalo lets out a deep breath when he sees that and seems to relax a bit. Getting out of New York was a remedy for that stress because the longer he stayed there the bigger the chance was that he was going to get caught or pinpointed by numerous people. As soon as they get into Ohio a McDonald’s is seen on the right hand side. Gonzalo gives it a distasted look but then again he knew Angelo had to be hungry and they did sell salads so what the hell. He pulls in the nice car to the McDonalds. “Yay!” Angelo screams. Gonzalo nods, “thought you may be hungry,” he says. Angelo nods, “I am, dad. But don’t you need to get to your wrestling thing?” he asks. Gonzalo shrugs as he puts the car in park and turns off the Mercedes engine. “I have to get to DC and you are correct, we are going the wrong way,” he says nonchalantly. “Then…” Gonzalo puts his hand up and laughs, “just go grab yourself some food,” he hands his son some food. “I am going to go make a phone call at the pay phone outside and then we will head towards our paradise,” he says. “TOO VICTORY!” Angelo yells as he opens the door and jogs towards the McDonalds entrance. Gonzalo Rey stares towards his son as his son enters the McDonalds and he smiles knowingly, “I have missed so much but never again,” he says. Gonzalo opens his door and steps out quickly and begins walking towards the pay phone outside the McDonalds. He takes his wallet out and flips it open. He digs around for a bit and then comes back out with a piece of paper with a phone number written on it. He sighs a few times seemingly trying to find the nerve to call this number. He takes a few quarters out of his pocket and slowly puts them in the coin slot and then stares at the number again with a distraught look on his face. He shakes his head and then begins dialing the number. RING… RING… RING…. “Hello?” Gonzalo pauses as if he has no idea what to say and he begins sweating just like when he was first escaping; his nerves becoming crazy. ”HELLO?” the voice asks again louder. Gonzalo takes a deep breath and exhales it quickly, “hey dad,” he says quietly. ”Gonzalo?” Gonzalo’s dad asks with surprise in his voice. “Where the fuck are you are, what the hell have you been doing?” he asks with a worried tone to his voice. Gonzalo frowns at the phone, “look I don’t want to rehash this shit. I just wanted to call and tell you that I am alright,” he says. ”You took Angelo didn’t you?” his dad asks. ”What are you talking about?” Gonzalo asks. ”Received a phone call from your ex-wife’s family and it seems you let your son write a letter and he told her pretty much everything. He is not a smart one,” his dad says seriously. Gonzalo sighs, “I couldn’t say no. I have my son though and that is the way it should have always been. I am just finally doing it,” he says forcefully. ”Gonzalo, how can you raise a child when you are a criminal?” Gonzalo seethes into the phone, “what the hell are you talking about?” ”Do you think I have been deaf and blind? I know what you have been doing and it sickens me to think that I raised a son that would do such immoral things,” his father says angrily. ”I have changed,” Gonzalo replies. ”By running away?” Gonzalo frowns again and grits his teeth trying not to explode into the phone. “I am not running away. I am trying to find a better life and even if I did make wrong decisions in the past I am trying to make up for them now dammit!” he says. ”So your employer will understand this?” his dad asks. ”I do what I please,” Gonzalo says angrily. ”I highly doubt that. I am aware of how the cartels run business and it is a business you are in till you die,” he says seriously. Gonzalo laughs trying to brush it off yet still surprised that his father knows so much about what has gone on. “What are you getting at?” ”I am telling you that you are a idiot, son. You have now put your son into the middle of this and that is more important than your falling-down world.” Gonzalo pauses and nods, “I know what danger I have put him in. It doesn’t matter though because I know that his life will be better with me and I promised him it would be a lengthy one, dad. I will not break it. I will survive no matter if you believe it,” Gonzalo says emotionally. ”I am extremely worried for you but you got yourself into this mess by breaking the rules to become what you perceived as successful,” his father says. Gonzalo looks around and sees that his son is paying for his food, “I need your help,” he says slowly. ”Anything.” ”Do you still have the place in Virginia?” he asks quickly as he looks into the McDonalds. His son is still waiting on his food but it is fast so it should be any minute now he thinks. ”Yes.” ”Then I am going there and I think you should hide yourself, dad. The cartel will do anything for vengeance. Now tell me the keys are still hidden in the same spot and I will be gone. You wont have to worry about me anymore,” Gonzalo says with hope in his voice. ”Everything is the same but this wont keep me away. I will find a way to see you again but for now I am going to get in my plane and go on a small vacation. Good luck son and I will most definitely see you again,” his father says. Gonzalo sighs and hangs up the phone just in time for Angelo to walk out with his bag of McDonalds. Gonzalo slaps him on the back with a smile and they both walk towards the car. They get in and Gonzalo turns the key and they head off going towards 95 south towards DC and the match but more importantly Virginia and the hideout. Angelo begins eating his food happily and it doesn’t take him long to finish it. Gonzalo smiles and then it fades it away just as quickly as it had appeared. He grabs his son’s cup of soda and takes a small sip and hands it back to him. “Where we going now?” his son asks. “We are going to stay at grandpa’s country house in Virginia but on the way there we are going to DC. Enough of this driving without a destination. It is time to make decisions and I have,” he says seriously. Angelo nods, “cool, I get to see you wrestle!” he exclaims. Gonzalo nods and then sighs, “do you think we are running away?” he asks out of nowhere. “That is the point, right dad?” he asks. Gonzalo smiles at his son and then looks back at the road. Fade ==== Running Away That’s the point, right dad? It isn’t the point at all… This is running away but it isn’t the point to what I do. I had to leave where I was to do what I am doing now. I am not running away from what I have done because I am well aware of what is probably going to happen. At least I will be protecting my son and hopefully the two evils will cross themselves out and leave me relatively invisible. A missing man who they will assume is dead as well. It is hard to say I am not being selfish. I am being selfish but in my mind it is the own way for everyone to find his or her paradise. It doesn’t have to do with those I despise it has to do with my son and me. I have put him in harms way by taking him but any time I left they would go looking for my family…. any family. They are not kind people and hence everyone would be disemboweled. I am running towards DC and if I have to run to Baltimore next then so be it. I have created a stop and move lifestyle for us but in the long run it will all work out. I am looking at this as a glass half full situation. There is no reason to be pessimistic any longer because I have already been through hell. The hardest part though is escaping it and through the fire and gunshots. I will bust through it all though and disappear with Angelo towards our piece of heaven. This business of wrestling is about pride now. My abilities have begun to whither over the past few years. I have been stuck in the same place for so long that I had become stagnant. It was me going through the motions and not even wrestling as much as I wanted to. My talents went untapped and now I take a look at myself and realize I am not as young as I use to be. I have to do this and since entering the tourney I have felt rejuvenated of course that could also be the adrenaline rushes I get daily by being fearful for our lives. Again I have been selfish. I have the money I need to live after ripping off the cartel. We could go out of the country with this type of money and live rather well. Of course when I think of that I think of squandering opportunities. I think of al those times I ran from my dreams and took the short cut to make a lot of money. I just can’t do it. I have to continue to wrestle because I have to see if I really can compete with these supposed legends. This is about me but then again, whose son wants a father that gives up? None. So here I am putting my gear on in DC with no intent on losing, no intent on running, and no intent on giving up. Brian Calvin can take his cheap pops from the crowd and can shake little children’s hands all he wants. It really does not matter to me. I do not care if I get no response because the only thing I want to hear is my name after I dispose of another hyped up peasant. I am running away from a life but running towards you all. I will not stop and in the process your goals will be mine and your pride will become mine. That is the point to all this nonsense. Running away is a must but giving up is not in my vocabulary! End
