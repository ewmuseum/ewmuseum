Extreme Division Match

23 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
23 April 2004 More or Less... At the present moment, it is five o'clock, ante-meridian. The black morning outside the house of a man who just returned from the hospital is just that, black. The sun, if it is even up at all, is now tucked behind the large black pillows that have blanketed the sky after a night of many storms. And the storms that rocked the large Italian Villa embedded into the rocky coast of New Jersey on a cliff overlooking the ocean were nothing short of spectacular, with lightning and thunder that made the night seem like it was day. But... it didn't wake Joey... Joseph Marciano Vendetti. A name that isn't very familiar with members of Primetime Central. A name that, if you heard it at the PTC headquarters, wouldn't mean a thing, because no one knows Joey Vendetti... yet. UUUUGGGHHHH.... The moan reverberates out the large window that protrudes from the side of the gigantic house. The rain has finally stopped, so the sound carries completely through the air. The scene fades now, and we open back up inside the large, illustrious home of Joey Vendetti. It's still dark after a long painful night of trying to get some shuteye, and Joey is awake, for the moment. Joey is shown lying in his large bed with the silk sheets and light comforter, looking out the window and grasping what must be his lower back in pain. Because Joey remembers that night... XUW Reborn. The night when Joey's stable, the Skeletons of Society, were set to establish themselves as the most dominant force in XUW today. And the Skeletons managed to pull it off thanks to a lucky toss by Bubba Bailey. The boot smacked The Fallen Fae in the side of the head and Joey capitalized with the brass knucks and picked up the pin. And for the night, the war with the Army of Darkness was over.... or was it? OOOOOOOOOOOOOOUUUUUGH! As Raven X locked Joey's own submissions hold on him tightly, Joey looked up into the lights... "Am I in heaven?" Joey thought. But that couldn't be the case... because of Joey's resent towards the society that shunned him, he has done many things that definitely wouldn't earn him a spot behind the pearly gates. Joey sat under those hot lights, feeling the mass amounts of pain surging through his back and knees, and his neck of course from straining to see the lights. Raven X had gotten Joey back... using the same move that Joey used all those months ago to cripple Raven X... Isn't payback a bitch? Joey moans again and clutches at his back. The sharp pains that rip through his spine nightly are nothing short of his very own personal version of hell. At the present moment, five o'five ante-meridian, Joey is experiencing one of his more difficult nights. Trying to get any measure of rest this morning has been difficult, but will not be impossible. Because no pain is impossible to battle through. And Joey knows this... Oh God... help me... As Joey begs to God for mercy, it must feel very ironic. This is the same God that Joey curses night after night, day after day, because of the way Joey's life had to be. It's not easy being Joey. Sure, everyone has a few hardships in their life. Everyone has things that they must deal with on a day to day basis. Everyone has a reason to cry, but why do they hide it? Why is it that all those horrible things that happen to people day to day are tucked away somewhere deep down inside their soul? Because then no one can see the pain... but everyone can see Joey's pain... it's written all over his face. Papa... Joey tosses and turns under his covers, dreaming while still awake. How is it possible? To dream and still be awake... Well that's when things happen in your life that seem like some sort of horrible dream, and you continuously hope to wake from them, but deep down, as the misery washes over you, you realize that you can't. Joey has lived with this feeling everyday for the past year and half, almost two years now. All of his personal problems were broadcasted across the air waves of XUW, and he hasn't had any sort of relief from them until now. He has had the sweetest three weeks off that anyone has ever had, other than the sharp pains in his lower back. It is now five ten ante-meridian when we hear it... RING!!!! RING!!!! RING!!!! Joey moans and sits up in bed, popping his neck to either side and then stands from the bed, clutching at his back. He sees his crutch mounted on the wall by a hook and begins to reach for it, but at the last second he pulls back, unable to make himself take it and not fight through the pain like he has done successfully for three nights now. Joey throws on a robe and a click is heard as a wash of light falls over the room from the table lamp on the mini table next to Joey's bed which houses the phone. Joey grabs the phone up to answer it, and lets out a heavy sigh of pain as he stoops over to grab the phone and then stands back up. Hello... From the where?... Primetime Central... Hmmm.... Joey grabs at his head. How could he have forgotten? He placed his name on the injury leave list upon leaving the XUW Reborn arena, but he had somehow forgotten to have the PTC remove him from the list. I have a match... next week... who against?.... Moraless... terrific. Was he the one who... yes... yes he was... thank you ma'am... Click... and with that the stage is set. Joey has just accepted his match with Moraless, a member of the Universal Wrestling Federation. Joey rubs away what little sleep he has from his eyes and slowly makes his way out of the master bedroom and down a long winding hallway. Small bits and pieces of light are beginning to stream through the windows of the hallway as he marches down. The pitter patter of his feet echo and re-echo off the walls of the small corridor that leads to his charming den. A house like this is a house that some people only dream about. But for Joey Vendetti, fine things like this are a reality, especially because of what his line of work used to be, and in some ways, what his line of work still is. Joey has finally reaches his den area, but for one reason or another passes it right up and heads on down the hallway. This house is unlike many others, in that the one corridor allows the other rooms to branch off into different areas of the house. After about three doors, the self-proclaimed Italian Stallion turns right into a large kitchen area, complete with chandelier. He marches up to the coffee pot and turns it on, beginning to brew. He will need his fair share of caffeine to calm his nerves if he is to prepare for a match with Moraless, who must be a well accomplished man in the UWF to... Wait... didn't Moraless have a number one contender's spot... Joey shakes his head, not really caring to answer his own question, and continues with the brewing of his coffee. For a moment, Joey stands silent until the phone in the kitchen rings. Joey rolls his eyes and marches over, clutching at his back, and grabs the phone up but then turns on the speaker phone and sets the receiver down. Hello... Voice: HELLOOOOOO JOEY! Joey sighs and walks back to his coffee. His Image Consultant slash manager, known as Marti Pfuffkin is now on the horn, ready to speak with Joey. But why so early? Joey: Hello Marti... it's a little early for you to be calling... it's five fifteen am. Marti: Ante-meridian indeed Joseph! How have you been Joey!? I haven't gotten to talk to you in a granny's age! From the tone of Marti's voice, and the pitch with which he uses it, the impression could be gathered that Marti Pfuffkin was playing on a different team. Though no one is quite sure yet. Joey: Yeah Marti... I know. Marti: So what are you doing Joey? Getting up early enough to make your coffee still, as I can hear. You really should think about getting a new coffee maker, the one you have is absolutely horrible! It's dreadful! Joey: I know Marti... is there a reason you called at the crack of dawn or did you just want to throw me further down into a hole? Marti: Well Joey, I talked to Saij, the new XUW commissioner, huzzah! Haha! Joey: Ok... Marti: And he is willing to negotiate a fresh new contract for you because I brought him a big ole bottle o'code red! Joey: Uhm... Marti, I think I'm still a bit tender from the attack from... Raven... Marti: Oh Joey, you're the Italian Stallion! The One Man Mafia! That big ole sexy Italian that graces that ring every night and kicks some major booty! No one is like you Joey. Joey: You can say that again. Marti: No one is like you Joey! Ooh! I made a funny! Joey: Marti, I can't negotiate any kind of contract this week, I have a match in the PTC. Marti: Oooh... Primetime Central? That's great Joey! Tomorrow we'll go pick out a new outfit and get you a new hat! That ratty old porkpie hat is just rotting away in the corner since you have been spending your days in bed. Joey: Whatever... I don't have time for all that. My knee is killing me, my back won't stop shooting those pains, and I've got a match next week because I screwed up and forgot to take my name off the PTC list after Raven X put me out... Joey kicks his leg around to pop his knee and then pops his neck as well. Marti: Well that's all well and good... who are you gonna be fighting Josie? Joey: Don't call me that. I'm facing a guy named Moraless, he faced DDT a while back and got layed out. Shouldn't be too difficult... or else it wouldn't be if my knee wasn't killing me. Joey shakes his knee out again and yawns. Marti: Well how bad is it Joey? Maybe we should get you to a doctor. Joey trying for the save...: NO!... I mean, no, that's ok Marti, I'll just work it out. Marti is silent for a moment, long enough for Joey too pull up his robe and look down at his knee. Not a bump... Not a bruise... Not even the slightest discoloration... Joey sighs and rubs his back. Perhaps the pain he experiences in the night isn't as bad as he makes it out to be. Marti: So what do we know about Moraless? Joey: His real name is John Edwards, he's got a brother that wrestles in some other nobody federation. He lost to DDT. That's it. That's all I need to know. If I can get in that ring it won't be a problem. Marti: What do you mean? Joey has quickly considered at this point what he has just said... IF That seems to be the million dollar word for Joey. But for a man with a past like Joey's, and for a man who has done so much damage as Joey, why would there be an "if"? Is Joey worried about further injury? No... "Stop sounding weak Joey", he thought to himself, "You know how Papa would feel about that." Joey nods to himself and continues on. Joey: I just mean... well... Suppose my knee gives out on me over the weekend. I need to get back to XUW. This PTC thing doesn't even bother me in the least. I haven't even given it thought since I heard I had the match. Lies! Joey continues to lie to himself and to others, knowing full well that his pain will be broadcast to the world eventually. Why not sooner rather than later? Marti: I understand Joey, but you're a big boy, you'll be ok I bet. If DDT could beat him, you can. I'm looking him up right now. He really has issues dealing with those failures... Joey glances down at his knee and closes his eyes. Joey: Yeah... what a wuss... Marti: Well I'm glad you are at least going to get some more experience Joey. You've never faced this man before and you're going to do great! I just know it! DING! Joey: Marti, I have to go, my coffee's ready... Marti: Ok Joey, as soon as your wounds heal and you defeat Moraless, we'll get started getting you back into XUW so you can get ready for Stable Wars! Joey: Yeah... Joey takes out a mug and pours the coffee into it. The steam rises high in the air and Joey gets a big wiff of it and smiles, but only for a moment. Small moments of happiness occur few and far between for Joey Vendetti, so he must make sure to savor them all. Marti: Well you enjoy your coffee now. Oh Joey, by the way, which one is bothering you more? The fact that you're lying to yourself, or the fact that you're lying to me? Joey jerks his head toward the speakerphone. Joey: What? Marti: I said does your back or your knee bother you more? It is at this moment that Joey Vendetti decides to stop lying to himself. Rather than hunching over, Joey Vendetti stands erect now, stretching out his lower back, showing no indication of pain. A frown appears on his face as he glances at the machine. Joey: Neither... I have to go Marti. Marti: Ok, good luck Joey, you really think you're ready for this? Joey clicks the phone off before he answers and then pops his neck and looks down into his coffee. The reflection that now stares back at him is one that he isn't used to. Joey Vendetti is now looking into the face of a coward. Joey: More or Less... Joey takes a sip of the coffee as darkness floods the scene... L'Estremità... ---- 25 April 2004 Are You Ready... Primetime Central. Two words, four syllables, one place for all the greatest wrestlers from all the greatest wrestling federations in the world to come together and compete to answer the ultimate question... "Who's the Man?" Sometimes it might not even be a man. Primetime Central is a place where two men who have never even heard each others' names before could meet in the ring and compete until one of the two competitors is pinned to the mat, made to submit, or bloodied to a hideous, twisted pulp. Two men who have never seen each other before, two men who have no idea what the other has done or wishes to do, could meet in a ring and demolish one another for the enjoyment of the fans.... sometimes to the disdain of the fans, but that comes with the business. But somehow, fate and Primetime Central have dealt one man a cruel hand in this wicked game. Joseph Marciano Vendetti is that man. But, why is it a cruel hand that has been dealt to him? One would think that any man would love to prove his dominance over any other man, but Joey Vendetti has a hidden agenda.... a reason that he doesn't wish to return to the ring. After all, the hardest medicine to take is your own. Light comes to the scene. What we see is a ring that sits in the middle of a very large room, almost like a warehouse. In the center of the ring is a "PTC" logo, bold and brash, and the rest of this particular room is shrouded in darkness. Several days have passed now since Joey Vendetti learned of his match with Moraless from the Universal Wrestling Federation. Several days have passed since Joey Vendetti, one of the many faces of XUW's premier list of stars, learned that he had forgotten to remove his name from the Primetime Central List of contenders... from the list of contenders in the Extreme Title Division. And after several days, Joey finds himself in a Primetime Central Warehouse, where a ring waits for someone to wrestle in it, and suddenly, a large "thump" sound is heard as the lights in the training room come on. The lights spill all over the floor, covering the ring and the huge area of concrete floor that surrounds it. Joey Vendetti walks into the room through a huge pair of double doors that lets the sunlight outside spill into the room. After staring into the room for a moment, Joey steps in and looks around, glaring into the rafters and then at the ring. Dressed in gray sweatpants and a black "XUW" t-shirt with the green XUW logo, and having his training bag over his left shoulder, Joey appears to be ready to do what he was born to do... wrestle. Joey walks to the ring, and two steel folding chairs have been sat next to it. He sets his bag down in one of the seats and stares into the ring, taking a step towards it, then taking the same step backwards... odd... Joey: Well here I am, ready for my first match as a member of the Primetime Central Extreme Title Division. It didn't occur to me, until just after I got the phone call from PTC headquarters, just what I was getting myself into here. A match in the Extreme title division. A match between myself, and the number one contender, will take place a little less than a week from now. I just.... I can't imagine what it must be like.... Joey smiles and climbs up onto the ring apron. To have nothing to lose and everything to gain. Joey takes a look around the massive warehouse. His problems must be.... small compared to this. Joey begins to take a step through the ropes and into the ring, but for some reason just stops and stands back up. I may have come off as a little cocky when everyone first happened to hear my voice and saw me for the first time, here less than a week before I face off against Moraless. I said that, just because DDT had his way with Moraless, that I would have no problem with him. Well, before my opponent throws his words my way, or to his friends, let me set the record straight. I don't mean to say I'll win the match, only because DDT defeated you, no. I'm going to defeat you because of who I am. Joey smiles. Every wrestler has wrestled someone before and heard them say that they were God's gift to wrestling, or that they would have no problem walking over their opponent and not even break a sweat. Everyone has wrestled their share of men who are cocky, and weak-minded. But before you think that I'm cocky, I think it's important you realize just who I am. Moraless... When I heard that name, I knew who you were. I knew you were the man who took on DDT in an Extreme Title Division match. I knew that you were from the Universal Wrestling Federation. I knew that you gave DDT a real fight... but that was DDT friend. I have had my share of matches with Damien D. Teri as well, and I've always come out on top of them. Not once has DDT known a victory over me, Joey Vendetti, a graduate of a developmental federation of XUW, XUDF. Each and every time I step into the ring with DDT, I obliterate him, and grind him into the mat, and leave my name in his blood. What does this tell you Moraless? Of course the obvious, "I'll do the same to you", but what else? What do you think about when I tell you about the things I do? I don't even need to ask that Moraless. You seem like a smart man, I bet you know where I'm going. Joey hops down from the apron, rather than step into the ring. See, I have faced DDT many times, after leaving him in a pool of his own blood. But you Moraless, you get just one shot at Joey Vendetti. So after I do what I always do, and leave you in a pool of your own blood, you won't be able to come back and do anything about it. No. You, the number one contender to the Extreme Title, will have lost to a man that is deep in the list, a man who at this point hasn't even wrestled a match in Primetime Central. And you'll never be able to do anything about it Moraless. Joey smiles again and looks into the ring. But that sounds very stereotypical doesn't it? "I'll beat you and you'll never beat me". It all sounds like something that wrestlers say all the time. But the thing you need to realize before you get yourself somewhere that you'll never be able to get yourself out of is, this isn't a job for me, this is just a professional version of my lifestyle. A version of my lifestyle that I get a paycheck for. So when I tell you, Moraless, that I'm going to leave you in a pool of your own blood, I mean that very literally. And what is my lifestyle? Joey walks to the steps next to the ring and slowly walks up them, looking around the room. He reaches the apron and slowly starts to walk around the apron. Well Moraless, my lifestyle is that of a puppet. For my whole life I have had someone who would point their finger, and Joey Vendetti would destroy. First, it was my own father, Geopetto Joseph Chonci Vendetti, otherwise known as Papa. Then after that, my own brother Luigi Vendetti. Then after that, Don Giamo, but now he's gone... But when they would point their fingers, I had no sort of choice in the matter. I would be the one to destroy whatever their finger led to. Why? Because I was conditioned that way. Some of the kids in the neighborhood played soccer and baseball just for the fun of the game. Some kids could walk off the field, find their fathers and mothers, and expect to hear, "You did good Johnny, we love you" and blah blah blah. When Joey Vendetti walked off the field, he would have pushups to do, or maybe run some laps. That's what happens when you're not the star player. My father told me that was why I was doing it at least, but that wasn't the real reason... Joey takes a step into the ring, and as his other foot touches the mat, he lifts it back up, sighs, and stands on the apron. When I was twelve years old, my Papa had two friends over to the house, Don Giamo and Venchenzo Raimundo. I had no idea why they were there, but my Papa made me come into the den and perform for them, like a windup doll. I did pushups, I ran in place, I flexed my muscles, and each of the men applauded, telling Papa that he had such a handsome and strong young boy. Yeah... I ate up every minute of it, not knowing that my heartless father had just sold my own life away, for about three hundred grand. Working out and all the punishment just got worse as time went by, especially between sixteen and eighteen. Those were the three hardest years of my life. But at that time, I wasn't much smaller than I am now, because I was always in the gym, but not by choice. One day my father brought me to an XUDF ring, a developmental federation of XUW, and I got my big break there. Papa said it was to become famous, to show the world that the Vendetti family wasn't just some other family, that we meant something. He always stressed pride in my family... "La famiglia prima di tutto l'altrimenti" Even after we moved from Verona where I was born to here in America, we were still a close family. And when I joined XUDF my father pushed me to new limits, encouraging me to be better than ever man I wrestled. Encouraging me to not only beat these men, but make sure that they're never the same again. But not long after winning my first title in XUDF, my Papa passed away at a young age from a heart problem... and I never knew why... until I met Don Giamo, one of the men who had come to my house so long ago. I started working for Don Giamo in the same way that I worked for Papa. Don Giamo trained me to be better, but Don Giamo's philosophy was different from Papa's... Don Giamo said that win or lose, the man I wrestled should be crippled and left paralyzed on the mat. I graduated XUDF under Don Giamo's supervision and entered XUW, where my reign of terror continued. It wasn't until then that I realized exactly what I was... A Puppet. My father had sold me as a child, and trained and conditioned me to be in fine shape when I began to work for Don Giamo. All those years of pushups and situps and football, baseball, soccer, and wrestling had all been just to get me ready to work for this man. Don Giamo owned me... but I took care of that quickly... Joey stares into the camera and smiles. Don't you see Moraless? It's the only way I know how to do it. The only thing I understand is violence. The only thing I understand is completely ruining someone's life after I wrestle them. So when I issue that stereotypical threat, "I'll beat you and make you bleed", I don't say that hoping that it will happen, I say that knowing that it will happen. You're no different than any other man I've ever met Moraless. Trying to make his own way in life, trying to make a name for himself, trying to build himself up to be something really spectacular one day. You don't have any personal business with me Moraless, but when you and I step into the ring, I'm going to make it personal. Suddenly, the double doors creak in the corner. Joey looks up to the door and hops down from the apron. Through the door walks a man who appears to be about 275 pounds or so, and about five inches taller than Joey. He walks into the room wearing a "Skeletons of Society: Not Sending Out for Help, But You Might Want To" t-shirt and a pair of black sweats. This man is Overkill, another member of Joey Vendetti's stable, the Skeletons of Society. Overkill and Joey Vendetti have been friends for a long time, but that's understandable when for so long before that they were enemies. The two men met in a different federation, the Championship Wrestling League. They won tag team gold and had good times together until Joey's father relocated him to XUDF. Joey dropped his half of the tag team titles and left Overkill alone in CWL. Overkill vowed revenge and followed Joey into XUDF. There, a heated rivalry ensued until they met, along with The Longhorn, in a cage match where the Longhorn escaped into XUW and Joseph Marciano Vendetti became the XUDF North American Champion. So the hatred between the two men lasted only a while, until Overkill entered XUW and joined Joey once again to fight for a common cause. Joey Vendetti and Overkill, both being the truest form of an outcast, were together again in the Skeletons of Society, the very same group that defeated the legendary Army of Darkness in the War Games match at Reborn... the same match where Joey Vendetti took a heavy dose of his own medicine. That dose of medicine may have ruined Joey for the rest of his days. Overkill marches up to Joey Vendetti and sets his bag down next to Joey's. Joey and Overkill share a hug and then Overkill backs up and looks at Joey. Overkill: Well you've never looked better, I can say that much. Joey: Gee thanks. Overkill: No problem... so this is Primetime Central? Joey: Yes... I got a call just a few mornings ago telling me that I'm facing that Moraless guy that DDT fought on that one Mayhem. Overkill: Ha, DDT, what a joke. It wasn't too long ago that DDT and the rest of the Army of Darkness learned who was really the dominant group in XUW. Recalling the events of that monumental war games match, Joey shudders. Joey: Yeah. I remember. Overkill: So you wanted to train and get back in shape so you can waste this guy? Joey: Yeah, I thought I'd get used to some of my moves again and maybe teach myself a few more. Overkill: Ok, well let's get started. Overkill takes a step towards the ring and Joey stops him with a hand. Joey: Wait a second. Did Marti call you a few days ago about me signing a new contract with XUW? Overkill: Yeah, he called and said he'd try to get you a bigger deal with a couple of title shots in it or something, what's your point? Joey: I was wondering if I should sign it already... or if maybe it was too early for me to be back in XUW... you know, because of my back and stuff... Overkill: We need you back Joey, Stable Wars is coming up and me and Bubba won't be able to do this alone. Joey: Yeah... yeah I guess you're right. My back feels a lot better, but uhm... my knees are still bothering me quite a bit. Overkill: Still sore huh? Oh well... you ready to get back in the ring? Joey: I'm about to wrestle Moraless aren't I? Overkill: I meant now, to train. Joey: No, not yet... uhm... how's Gwen? Overkill: ... She's fine. Are you... Joey interrupting: And little April, Overkill junior, how's she doing? Overkill: She's fine Joey. I think we need to ge... Joey interrupting again: You know I was thinking. If we won the Stable Wars tournament, I thought maybe I'd take a shot at the World Title. Overkill: ...what? Joey: Yeah, I mean, lesser men have gotten shots before. That screwjob Wildlife even got a few shots at the World Title. Why shouldn't I get a shot? You could keep your Hardcore Title and then go for the Intercontinental Title too, and then Bubba and our fourth guy could try the tag team titles! Overkill: Look, whatever Joey, but I don't have all day and we need to get to... Joey: Maybe we don't even need to train today. Overkill sighs and walks up the steps and steps through the ropes into the ring. Overkill runs to one of the ropes and bounces off them and into the other ropes. He repeats this process several times and then stops in the center of the ring and starts to throw a few punches at the air. Joey takes a big breath and steps up each steel step and walks onto the apron. Joey swings his legs ever-so-slowly through the ropes. Once he steps into the ring, Overkill is already climbing each turnbuckle and getting the feel of it again. Joey pops his neck a little bit and then takes a step forward....... and steps on the side of his foot. His leg bolts sideways and he quickly regains his balance and throws his arms out to his side. Overkill stares at Joey as the Italian Stallion takes an unusually long breath. Overkill: Joey, are you ok? Joey: Yeah... fine... let's get started... The two of them move toward each other and Joey makes an attempted lockup. Overkill ducks under and shoves Joey into the ropes. On the return, Overkill tries a huge dropkick but Joey stops in his tracks and puts his hands up. Overkill gets back to his feet and takes a step towards Joey, but then stops and cocks his head to the side. Joey mutters... ashamed... Joey: I'm not ready... Darkness falls on the scene, leaving Overkill and Joey Vendetti standing in the ring. Joey Vendetti is left wondering whether or not he'll be ready for this huge match with Moraless, an opponent he's never faced... it should be just as any other time before, but for one reason or another... it's not the same L'Estremità...
