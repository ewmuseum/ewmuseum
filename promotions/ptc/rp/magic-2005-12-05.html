Magic

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Three minutes ago The chill evening breeze did its best to bite into whatever exposed flesh that it could find on the figure as he made his way through the dimly lit cityscape. The rush of oncoming people, moving quickly and blindly as if they were all part of one hive mind, parted and moved around him without, seemingly, conscious choice. Like a tsunami careening inexorably forward, parting only when met with a cliff-face, the crowd rushed around him; a subconscious need to avoid contact at all costs. The end of the working day combined with the upcoming holiday season, the capitalist need to spend foremost in all minds, led to an influx of people all trying to get to their destination at the same time. The streets were filled and emotions were high as bodies jostled into one another and voices rang out in anger. The press of human flesh never reached the figure that moved against the tide, the sharp retorts of anger were bitten off, swallowed whole, before they could be uttered; in this insane rush of humanity in motion was a single oasis of calm. The unstoppable force met the immovable object. Only the lights that were sparsely placed along the sidewalk and the shop front signs, neon shadows trailing behind them, which proclaimed all manner of goods for sale within light the street itself, illuminating the moving mass of people. Billowing along like a living shroud only a single column of stillness stood out as a pillar of dark stability. Head bowed against both the chill wind and the people moving around him, the man known as Ember stood with his arms wrapped around his chest, seemingly oblivious to the pale blond hair that whipped around and across his face. An ankle length leather jacket was held tightly against him protection against the cold that pervaded the night air and the presence of so many people. Invading his senses, the stench of humanity assailing his nostrils as the cacophony of meaningless conversation assaulted his ears, he stood still and silent as people barely gave him a second glance before changing their path to avoid him completely. A small circle of emptiness, no more than two or three inches, surrounded his body, a miracle brought to life. Reaching up to his face Ember pulled the black scarf tighter, ensuring that it covered him up to just below the eyes and allowing his free flowing hair to form a curtain in front of the remainder of his face, and took a couple of faltering steps forward. Oblivious to the people in front of him, parting like the Red Sea to his Moses, he kept moving step after faltering step. If it had been possible to see clearly through the dim light, if it had been possible to see his face through the hair that seemed to wrap his visage in a tender embrace, if it had been possible to see his eyes through the mask that covered them, if any of that had been possible then it would have been possible to gaze into the supposed windows to his soul. And see nothing staring back. Four hours ago Whistling happily, if somewhat tunelessly, to himself Ember strolled along the city street. With his hands shoved deep into his pockets and a black scarf wrapped around his face, both things to fight the chill of the wintry air, he looked like nothing more than a shambling mound of blackness; this image only alleviated by the waves of shockingly pale blonde hair that flowed freely down past his shoulders, nearly touching his waist. While not normally a morning person, or even an afternoon person if truth were told, as he was more comfortable with the comfortable embrace that the night brought to him, Ember had found that he couldn’t stay indoors. From the moment he awoke he had been restless and after grabbing a quick shower he found himself walking along the streets of the city, almost empty apart from him. Just how he liked it. He couldn’t quite put his finger on it but something coursed through his body, a feeling of anticipation or expectation mixed with something else, something unfamiliar. An edge that he couldn’t name. An edge that filled him with a nervous energy and put a bounce in his step nonetheless. Exuberance filled him and a chuckle nearly escaped his mouth but he swallowed it before it could get free. The last few weeks and months had been nothing short of a success for him, and he ruminated on just how much had changed in such a short space of time. Only a year ago he was nothing, he was less than nothing, and yet in that time he had risen to prominence in an industry he had watched from afar before, an industry that he had always been jealous off but knew that he wouldn’t be able to compete in. Not while his erstwhile brother held dominance … Shaking of the one thought that could, if he allowed it, darken his mood Ember came to a crossroads and with a shrug of his shoulders he turned left and continued walking. Such a small choice, such a meaningless decision. Left or right. Right or left. Hardly noticing where his feet were taking him Ember lost himself deep in thought once more. One year. That is all that it had taken for him to go from nothing and no one to the number one contender to the World Wrestling Alliance’s championship title. One year is all that it had taken for him to go from a rookie within the WWA to one of its most feared competitors, dominating the industry with his partner, Khaled. One year is all that it had taken for him to go from a complete unknown within the organisation known as Primetime Central to a mainstay of tournaments such as Seven of the Best and the Infinite Gauntlet. One year since his life had begun, since his ‘birth’. Underneath the scarf that hid the mask, and underneath the mask that hid his face, Ember grinned as joy, fierce pride, flooded his soul and coursed through his veins. Life was good. Turning another corner Ember almost skidded to a halt, barely stopping himself from careening into a large crowd off people. About twenty people of varying ages were gathered, standing in a semi-circle, around one man. Dressed immaculately in tight fitting blue jeans, with a beige turtleneck jumper under a denim jacket, the man seemingly had the impromptu audience enthralled as he caused a deck of playing cards to dance between his hands. Stifled ‘oohs’ and ‘ahs’ sounded out from the people, their mouths open in wonder, as the cards seemingly moved with a life of their own. "Some people seem to believe that there is no such thing as magic in this world" the man said, his eyes gleaming as a smile lit up his handsome, almost beautiful face, "but I prefer to think that those people are just blind to what is really in front of their eyes." Three cards were kept in his left hand as the remainder of the pack was placed into his jean pocket. With a flourish of his wrist the cards leapt into the air one by one and then vanished. Walking over to a young woman, in her early twenties, the man smiled as he reached out tenderly to her hair and pulled the three cards from within the folds of tumbling auburn. "I mean just look into these beautiful eyes and there is proof of magic" he said as the young woman blushed. The crowd applauded enthusiastically as the young man stepped back into the centre of the circle and laid the three cards face down on a small table that was setup there. With a quick movement he laid them out side by side and then brushed one hand across their backs. As the crowd watched each of the cards vanished from view and then with another flourish he threw them into the air where they seemed to hang motionless for a fraction of a second before falling gently to the table again. Another quick movement and he had them laid out once more. "So if you believe only what your eyes can see then you are missing out half of what this World has to show you" he continued with another smile, "you cannot see love, but how many of us would deny that it exists?" All around the audience smiles were appearing on people’s faces, young couples reached out to hold one another’s hands and the older folk amongst them nodded knowingly. At the back of the crowd only Ember stood motionless, his head cocked to one side as he watched intently the reaction that the man is evoking. "… and who here would deny the truth that the heart knows, the truth of love?" Ember sighed, softly. He had to admit, as he watched the intent look on the faces that enveloped the man in the centre of the circle in the warm stares, that the kid was good. In fact he was more than good. The inflection of the voice, the tilt of the head, the sky blue eyes and the perfect smile. He had it all going for him. If he asked them to eat of out his hand I bet that they would Ember thought to himself, the knowing grin hidden deep within the folds of the scarf that covered his mask from view. "As well all know the symbol of love is the heart, red as blood and as pure as snow" the man stated with another smile as he ran a hand over the three cards and as it passed over them they were flipped over to reveal that there was the Queen of Hearts in the middle and a joker on each side. "As always, love is surrounded by those that would stop it from flourishing." The man’s hands continued to move over the cards as his tongue moved just as dexterously over his words. The cards flipped back and forth between face up and face down, and each time that they did the Queen was in a different position. First the centre, then the left, then back to the centre. As fast as his hands moved his tongue moved faster. "Each of us knows that there is such a thing as magic, each of us knows that there is such a thing as love" he continued softly, a twinkle in his eyes as he gazed around him knowing that he had them. Knowing that he had them right where he wanted them. "Each of us has a chance, a single chance mind you, to become magicians ourselves and capture that love and set it free from those that would deny it. Kill it." With all three cards face down the young man beckoned tenderly to the auburn haired woman from whom he had pulled the cards. Looking around her, again blushing, she stepped slowly forward to stand in front of the small table. Gazing in apparent confusion at the man in front of her she titled her head to one side, confused. "Find love" he stated simply, indicating the three cards in front of him "find love and set it free. Understanding lit up her eyes as she reached out one hand and let it hover over the cards, looking uncertain as she tried to decide which one to go for. "Trust your heart" the young man said simply, smiling sweetly at the woman. In the middle Ember thought to himself as if you don’t already know that. Whilst everyone else had been fixated on the young man’s words and hands Ember had been watching his eyes. He had seen his glance linger for a fraction of a second on the woman and seen her answering gaze. Two strangers in a crowd though they appeared to be Ember knew their game. Again he smiled and stood still and silent as he watched it unfold in front of him. Hesitantly, slowly, the woman reached out and placed her hand over the card in the middle of the three and with a reassuring nod from the man she flipped it over. A gasp of appreciation ran through the audience as the Queen of Hearts was revealed lying there. The man stood up and took the woman’s hand in his own, bowing over it as he brushed his lips against the smooth skin. Looking up around the crowd he slowly and deliberately pulled out a roll of ten-dollar notes and handed one of them to the woman. "Love never goes unrewarded my dear," he said with a small smile, "and the reward for finding love here is a small gesture only". A ripple of excitement flowed through the audience as the young man placed the roll of notes on top of the table and looked around him. "Are there any other seekers of magic, seekers of love, amongst you?" He asked with a twinkle in his eyes. "As you have seen it is easy to find love if you look hard enough, and so to ensure that I do not starve tonight I will simply ask that each of you searchers for the truth wager five dollars against my ten. If you find love, as this young woman did, then you go away all the better." Ember watched and nodded knowingly to himself as he noticed the man’s eyes tighten slightly as the audience listened to his words. Nicely done, the bait is laid down gently and now he just has to reel them in Ember thought to himself. A flush of disdain ran through him as he noticed the look of greed on more than one face. "… in fact I will even give the first try away for free" the man stated as he sat down again. An older man, brown hair balding over an overweight face, stepped forward quickly. Pushing past another couple who also seemed ready to step forward he looked down onto the table as the young man smiled innocently back up at him. Hook, line and sinker Ember thought as he watched the man’s hands slide over the cards, nodding in appreciation as he considered the skilful approach of the hustler. "Find love and you will earn your reward my friend" he said as he gestured at the cards. Almost without pausing the fat man reached out with chubby fingers and flipped over the leftmost card, a crow of delight escaping his mouth as the Queen of Hearts lay revealed. The young man leant back and clapped his hands enthusiastically. "Well done my friend it seems that the Lady looks down on you with kindness" he said as he handed him a crisp ten-dollar note. "Now, who’s next?" Ember stood back as the people around him moved forward, each one wanting to try their luck and feed their avarice. Lies and money become the White man’s God Ember thought to himself as he watched man after man, woman after woman, line up to take their turn at finding Lady Love. One or two actually did and the smiling young man gladly handed over the ten-dollar bill note to each of them. More often than not, however, it was one of the jokers that was turned up while the Queen of Hearts was turned up just after by a smiling, and apologetic, young man. Taking the five-dollar note that was held out to him and tucking it away out of sight. "He is winning six or seven times to every one that he loses" Ember mused to himself as off in the distance sirens could be faintly heard. The young man glanced up, briefly, evidently also hearing the sound. "My friends it is nearly time for me to go, but I think that the Lady would like one more chance to bestow her favour on one of you" the young man said as he looked around him. An older woman, probably in her fifties, reached into her purse and pulled out a single five dollar note and started to step forward, but before she could get to the table Ember stepped past her and laid a hundred dollar bill down. "Wh … what?" the young man stuttered, his composure briefly lost as he glanced from the note to the figure swathed completely in black in front of him. Taking a quick breath he forced the smile back onto his face, but there was no emotion in his eyes. "Well my friend it appears that all that hair has masked your eyes. This is a hundred dollar bill, not a five," he stated with a forced laugh as he tried, in vain, to peer through the hair and past the scarf to see the face behind. "I know" Ember said, his voice barely above a sibilant whisper as he indicated down to the cards, "my one hundred against your ten." The young man looked like he was ready to argue but then greed flashed in his eyes and he started to move his hands over the cards, sliding them back and forth. The crowd pulled in closer, their faces full of expectation as they watched the cards movement turn into a blur. With a sudden movement the young man sat back and, with a large grin on his face, was still. "So my friend, do you think that you know where the Lady is?" he asked with a small smile. Without moving, without evening breathing. Ember stood silent. Seemingly staring down at the table seconds turned into minutes as he stared at the three cards below him and then his shoulders started to shake. As the crowd watched the smile began to fade from the young man’s face and a frown appeared on his forehead. Starting slowly and softly, but building in both volume and intensity, Ember began to laugh. "Oh yes, my friend I do indeed know where the Lady is" he said, cutting off the laugh so completely that some of the crowd stepped back in shock. With a movement that made the previous card shuffling seem like it was in slow motion, Ember reached over and grabbed the young man’s left wrist, hauling him to his feet. "Don’t bother" Ember said nonchantly, his grip vicelike, as the young man struggled to get free. With his free hand Ember reached down and, one by one, he flipped the cards over. There was a gasp of shock from those that could see the cards, a gasp that Ember himself mimicked as people craned to see what was on view. Three jokers. "Imagine that, it seems that your magic has deserted you, doesn’t it …" Ember stated, then paused for a second, "… what is your name anyway?" he continued as he tightened his grip. "And … Andrew" the young man, now known as Andrew, whimpered through clenched teeth as the crowd started to mutter. "Oh ho, it seems like the natives are getting restless doesn’t it Andy old bean?" Ember quipped. Kicking over the small table with one foot Ember looked over his shoulder and addressed the crowd. "Whoever lost any money now is your chance to take it back" he stated simply and laughed as he watched everyone begin to bend down and scurry on hands and knees for the notes that were fluttering around in the slight breeze. Without turning his head he brought his other hand up and caught Andrew’s hand as it came flying towards his the back of his head, fist clenched. "How did you do that?" Andrew Ember whispered as he leant his face in close to the younger man’s ear, his tongue flicking out to lick the lobe gently. As Andrew froze in shock Ember twisted his wrist and pulled his arm down slightly, reaching out to catch the Queen of Hearts as it fluttered down out of the young man’s sleeve. "Fancy that" Ember quipped again. "Want to see another trick Andrew?" Releasing Andrew with one quick move Ember brought his left hand up in front of him and seemed to concentrate on it. As Andrew’s eyes widened a small ball of flame appeared in the palm of Ember’s left hand. Holding the card above it Ember concentrated and then released the Queen of Hearts which began to float in mid-air. "… how?" gasped Andrew through a dry throat. "I told you Andy … magic." Ember said without looking at the man. "For my next trick, how to make a group of people vanish." With a wry chuckle Ember suddenly thrust his hand into the air and an explosion of flames rushed up to envelop the card before spiralling above his head. With screams of fright and panic the crowd began to run in all directions until all that was left was Ember, a crouching Andrew and the auburn haired girl who had first ‘won’ the challenge. "Now then unless you think that you can make a rabbit appear out of your ass I think that it is time that you took your girlfriend and got the Hell out of here" Ember said coldly as he looked down in scorn at the man below him. "… and if I ever see you around here again, with your little ‘magic’ tricks, the card won’t be the last thing to go up in flames." The words had hardly left his mouth before Andrew had grabbed the girl by the arm and run off into the street ahead. With a chuckle Ember reached down and picked up the hundred-dollar bill that he had placed on the table, holding it in front of his masked eyes. Without moving his hand the note vanished from view and another laugh escaped his mouth. "Magnificent." One hour ago Closing the door behind him Ember started to take his jacket off as another chuckle escaped his mouth. All afternoon, and into the evening, his good mood had stayed with him ever since he had put the fear of the Gods into that second rate street magician. With the jacket half on and half off, the chuckle still reverberating through his throat, he noticed something out of the corner of his eye. Reaching down he picked up the envelope that had been slipped under his door and stared at the single word that was on the front of the otherwise plain paper. His name. Gently peeling it open Ember reached inside and pulled out a small rectangular card. His shoulder’s relaxed as he saw the three lettered logo that proclaimed that the sender of the card was the PTC and another small laugh escaped him. "Must be the invitation to take part in the Infinite Gauntlet again." Ember said to himself as he pulled the card completely out and let the envelope float down to the ground. His whole body suddenly froze, the words stuck in his throat as his breathing completely stopped with one sudden indrawn breath as the rest of the text on the card became visible. "… no" came the whispered, almost choked, word from his throat as he leant against the wall, sliding down it until he came to rest in a crouch with his body bent over his knees, foetal like. The card fell from his senseless fingers and lay in front of him, the black and heavy type clearly visible in front of his unseeing eyes. Ember, You are formerly acknowledged as a confirmed entrant of the 2005 edition of Primetime Central’s GTT. Good luck. The PTC. The high pitched noise echoed throughout the hallway, spilling out into the rest of the apartment complex where residents stopped what they were doing and looked around them in shock. It carried up and on for seconds that turned into minutes and through it all Ember lay there oblivious to the sound, oblivious to his surroundings, oblivious to himself. Oblivious, even, to the fact that the sound that rang out hollowly around the building was his own scream. Now As the late night crowd moved around him, making sure to not get close enough to touch, the unseeing and unknowing Ember stopped his shambling forward motion and suddenly fell to his knees. Throwing his head backwards he reached up and pulled the scarf down from around his face, exposing the mask and the mirrored lenses that covered his eyes, to view. With a deep breath, ignoring the people who had come to a dead stop and were staring at him in a mixture of fear and fascinated horror, he began to keen. One heart-wrenching word of complete loss. "Nooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo!"
