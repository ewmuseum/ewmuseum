28 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a tribute to my undying love for my loyal fanbase, I’ve decided to take the legwork out of your voting experience. Granted, it’s not a galling task, but with your busy schedules and all the time devoured by giggling over my hype pieces, not to mention watching in awe my other numerable exploits, it’s an unnecessary inconvenience, especially considering that you’re all writing pretty much the same thing. Therefore, I’ve decided to save you the hassle by issuing a ballot template. Simply open a PM addressed to Argyle, enter the subject as “PTC WOTY 2005 VOTE”, copy the proceeding text into the body of the message and read along, following the instructions as you go, then submit.
