Chris Storm: IG 23: Cocaine on the Carpet

18 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It had been a long time since he had bothered to step into a Primetime Central ring, a LONG time. Despite being a former Global Champion, the King of PTC if it were, that ring was filled with bad memories. Ones that, for a time, he never wanted to remember. He could see himself being held outside the ring while his hard-earned title was taken from him and placed around the waist of another man. He saw a piss-poor performance (maybe one of his worst) in the center of the ring at Super Series in what should have been a five-star match. Instead it was shame and mockery, a cheap display of half-assing talent. And there was the first time he had ever stepped into the ring only to lose to a man you knew you deserved to beat. Stolen! Like a thief in the night, the match was taken from him and there was nothing he could do about it. In his sleep, Chris could not help but toss and turn. It was late and he needed sleep, but his body seemed to reject the entire idea. It seemed no use. Whenever he closed his eyes he became enraged. He became enraged by the memories that played before him like a bad movie. He could see what was going to happen, yet was powerless to stop it. Waking from his bed, the former Last Great Technical Wrestler rose and made his way into the kitchen of his small New York City apartment. It was a common ritual for him. On nights that he had trouble sleeping, from whatever reason, he could go into his kitchen and stare out the main window that overlooked Central Park. The sights of the city always relaxed him and allowed him to clear his thoughts. Tonight he had too much on his mind, it all made his head feel like it was being split open. He had an important decision he was agonizing over. In a brief moment of mental lapse he had decided to throw his name into the pot for PTC’s Infinite Gauntlet and try his hand at a bit more Primetime Central success. But now he questioned that decision. Why should he go back? Did he need the money? Did his ego need to be fed that badly that he would step into the place of shame once more, only to allow himself to be publicly ostracized? Perhaps it was for revenge against those who had did him wrong. Or maybe he just wanted another chance to put a stomping on Seymour Almasy, the little fucker. That was most likely the case. After all, if there was one thing Chris Storm did better than others, it was making Seymour look bad. His blood boiled whenever he thought about Almasy. The thought of him actually living so close made it impossibly to enjoy viewing New York tonight. He had to close the window; he couldn’t stand it any longer. In the past he had always managed to play Seymour off cool. No matter the situation Storm knew he could keep a calm head and one-up PTC’s poster child. And the more Storm remained cool about Almasy, the more Seymour seemed to be irritated. After all, what was worst than losing to a man like Storm? Losing one of the most important things you had ever held to him. No, not the Global title, although he had lost that to Storm as well. This, like many stories, was about a woman. A woman that two men loved but only one would have. Take a guess which one won. It wasn’t the Final Fantasy. Now it is no secret that throughout the tenure of Primetime Central that Tessa Windsor was Chris Storm’s girl. Despite how they fought and even exchanged blows, one constant remained; they always got back together. It was the ultimate love/hate relationship. It was one that transcended not just through television, but into the real world. Fans of Rush Pro remembered the duo for their long rivalry over the Mid-South Championship; but what those same fans never saw was that when the cameras stopped rolling, the two would enter their vehicle and drive off together to enjoy family bliss. Chris looked over to his phone, contemplating calling Tessa. He knew it was late but he just wanted to hear her sweet voice. After the long and hard road the two had been down, she still managed to smooth his flaming heart. But just as Chris reached for the phone his other half grabbed hold of him, it was too late to be calling after all. But something happened. It always does. Rush Pro closed and they both needed work. She found one place and he found another, it was just the way the business worked. Sure, they called each other when they could. But the distance between the two clearly put a strain on their relationship. Chris had found work in the one place he swore he would never be, the Universal Wrestling Federation. Surprisingly (or not to some), he fit in like a glove. Success was his and he grabbed it firmly. But the more success he found, the further he seemed to have been dragged from her. “Daddy!” a frightened voiced cried in the night. Chris’ head turned toward the spare bedroom, almost groaning. The whole parenting bit had always been a pain on him, given his lifestyle. Regardless, it was an excuse to try and block out the current flow of thoughts running through his head. That was when HE decided to make a move, he being Seymour Almasy. He was cocky, sure, and overall a douche. Or at least that is what Chris has told himself several times. The strife between the former Schmoopies was enough for him; Seymour saw a window open and took it. Marching into the room, Chris’ eyes turned toward the bed where, wrapped under warm blankets, a small child sat upright. Upon seeing Chris, the child instantly held out its arms to be picked up. ‘Another nightmare’ Chris thought to himself. This had been the third one of the week, with the nightmares seemingly getting worst. Chris lifted the child up and brushed the back of his head, “it’ll be alright” he cooed to the child, trying to get him to calm down. Maybe Chris Storm should have seen it coming. After all, Tessa had always been a well-sought after woman. But even in his wildest of dreams had he never believed that she would agree to date Almasy (or anyone besides himself for that matter). To Chris, the two were not still actively dating despite the fact that their last encounter had not been on a positive note, nor had they spoken to each other in over two months. A little thing like that shouldn’t matter, right? The worst part of it all? The company Chris worked for, the UWF, had decided the whole situation would make for a great television angle. It should have come as no surprise that Chris Storm would reject the idea furiously. But the company pushed the idea along anyway and soon, day by day, Chris started to hate Seymour even more. He projected all of his disdain to Seymour, while ignoring the fact that Tessa had played an equal part of the whole thing. It took a little over half an hour to put the child back to sleep before Chris could return to the kitchen. The series of events had still yet to stop playing, no matter what he tried to distract himself with. It was moments like these that he wished he owned a television. At least then monotonous late-night television would be sure to end his insomnia. Sadly, he couldn’t stop. It was like passing by an accident on the highway and having to slow down for a closer look. And the closer Chris examined his own thoughts, the angrier he would become. He wanted to fuck him up, badly. This was quickly moving out of the professional territory and into personal. The fuse finally reached the explosion point when the UWF had booked a match between the two. It would be Chris Storm taking on Seymour Almasy in a classic rematch of their famed Global title match. All it was missing was a dumb piece of shit in an ugly mask. There was just one small problem. Chris Storm had no intent to “wrestle”. So when his music hit he stormed down to the ring (no pun intended), and processed to try and beat the piss out of Almasy. The fighting had gotten so out of hand that by the time security could pull the two part, Chris had a broken nose and Almasy’s face was visibly bruised and beaten. This was the same mental processes that had led to his determination floundering. He had allowed himself to become so enraged that logic had been lost to him, much like with many of his career decisions. Grabbing his gym bag from nearby, Chris tossed it onto the table and unzipped it. Thankfully for Chris the entire situation crumbled and once against Tessa left Almasy for Storm after Seymour’s personal life started to fall to pieces at the hands of Rich Rollins. The series of events that left Almasy an emotional shell had put Storm in a much better light. It had to be another humiliating blow to Seymour. First he had lost his coveted title to a match who he believed to be far less deserved and then he loses his woman to a violent sociopath. Almasy never made any public statement about any of it; the brutal fight with Storm, the break up between himself and Tessa, and constant mocking from Chris. He ignored all of it and soon Chris did too. He had had much more important issues to deal with after all. The sheet of paper lay in front of him, mocking him. It was a dare. “Sign me, you know you want to.” It was calling out his manhood. “Prove you’re not a fluke, not a loser like they say you are.” But he ignored it. He felt he had nothing to prove to them. His accomplishments spoke for themselves. He was a former Global Champion. He’s been a champion in every Primetime Central league he’s taken part in. He’s even beaten some of their best. Since May 10th of last year he’d been content to avoid any spotlight and gladly do what he loves, wrestle. Chris Storm would be damned if he let some figment of his imagination control him like that. He knew the paper couldn’t talk; that it was all in his head. Pushing the paper aside, he turned his head and scoffed. But all it took was the right jab and the paper would get the best of him. “I bet he wanted to fuck her like you never could.” Sadly though, Chris never forgets nor does he forgive. To him, Almasy tried to steal his Tessa away. And if there is one thing people know from dealing with Storm, you do not mess with his Tessa. Chris grabbed a pen off the counter and hunched over the table, peering at the contract before him. “Fuck it, I’m going to rip his face off once and for all.” And so he signed away his name to that which is called the Infinite Gauntlet. --------------------------------------------------------------- Chris Storm felt lighter, as if someone had suddenly taken a large weight off his shoulders. For the first time in nearly a month he had a great night of sleep. As soon as his head hit the pillow, he was out like a light. It was the type of sleep that made you feel like you could take on the world, which is what Chris needed as he mailed off the contract to compete in the twenty-third round of the Infinite Gauntlet. At his side was the child from last night, his own flesh and blood. Chris looked down at the young boy, no more than five, who shared his name. The child happily totted a booksack as he walked beside his dad. The two cut a corner around an old brick building and started heading to where a multitude of parents and children were gathering. As they approached the cozy little building, a sign hung over the door that read “Kinder Care”. Outside the building there were smiling teachers, greeting each child as they parted from their respected parents. Approaching the building, Chris turned to his son and kneeled down beside him to look him in the face. “Do you have everything you need?” he asked, concerned. The child just nodded with a smile. “Good, I’ll be back to pick you up in a few hours.” And with that he patted him on the head and kissed him on the forehead to send him off. The teacher happily accepted young Chris (the child) into the building as Dad headed off to the gym in order to train. As he headed off in the direction of the gym, Chris felt a bit uncomfortable. He had never been use to doing the whole “dad” thing before. Even though he had spent many years with Tessa and her two children, he had always been hands off when it came to the children; leaving Tessa to pick up much of the slack. Funny how that worked, Chris thought to himself, as he was now the one abandoned in the role of parental figure. Though it had not been that long ago, to Chris it felt like it had been a decade since the child’s mother had walked into his life. At first he had dismissed her, like he had with all the other women claiming to have had his child. But alas, it would not be so this time. He had fucked up. The worst part of it all was that Chris had seen neither hide nor hair of the woman, Jennifer, since she had dumped the child with Chris. Oh sure, at first she seemed like the shy and quiet type, but as soon as she her little fingers got a hold of his credit card, she was gone like a flash. It would be days on end before Chris would even heard from her; even though to Chris that was no necessarily a bad thing. He hated the dumb bitch, and hated himself even more for ever getting plastered on booze and drugs to knock her up. Before he knew it he had arrived at the gym. It was the same gym he had frequented so many times before, and would surly visit many more times to come. Ralph, the owner, was a short and cubby fellow but had always been incredibly inviting to Chris. With just a phone call ahead of time, Chris could get Ralph to hold a ring for him for private use. Sure, the ring was a piece of crap and was actually for boxing, but Chris kind of dug the old feel to it; duck tape was stretched across the mat, the pads had rips and holes in them, and taking a drop on the canvas felt like dropping onto concrete in the middle of a New York winter. Sliding his gym bag into the corner, Chris rolled under the bottom rope and came up to his feet. After bouncing off the ropes a few times to warm up, he stopped over in the corner where he bag was to grab a sip of water from his bottle. It felt weird; here he was at the New York Athletics training for a match in PTC again. He never thought he would, honestly. When he uses to have matches regularly with Primetime Central, he would come regularly to train. He even had a special training regiment just for those matches. He laughed, “What a fucking joke.” It all felt so stupid to him now, trying to change things just because there was a different audience. He had tried for so long to fit in, when it just seemed like he never would. Instead he always came off as the leader of the Clique. Part of him wanted to go through the old training ritual, after all, why break tradition? Of course the other part wanted to call up the Primetime offices and cancel his appearance. He didn’t need the bullshit that may never well follow his appearance. He certainly didn’t want it. But then again, there was that chance to lock horns with Seymour Almasy and truly fuck up his world. That was what truly mattered to Chris. It was enough to make him lower his head and enter between those ropes. He would look every PTC fan in the face and do what he came to do, regardless if they wanted him there or not. So…you want to be a Rock Superstar? Chris paused, turning to his bag. “Hello?” Shortly after answering his cell phone, Chris Storm wished he hadn’t. It was Jennifer, and from the sounds of it she might have been drunk. “You need me to meet you? What’s the matter, maxed out another credit card?” The venom couldn’t help but flow from his lips. Just hearing that woman’s voice made him cringe. This is what they meant by saying you could pay the rest of your life for a mistake. It should have come as no surprise that the woman was clearly upset by Chris’ harsh, though true, words. “Meet you? I’m busy,” a slight pause, “no, I’m not with Tessa.” He tried to argue with her but like an old crow, she just kept going on and on. “Fine, where do you want to meet? The one on Twelfth Street? Fine.” And with that he slapped his phone shut and threw it into the bag. He was now pissed. A perfectly good chance to train, wasted by that wretched whore. Scooping up his bag, Chris rolled out between the ropes and heading on out. ---------------------------------------------------------------- There was a small café over on Twelfth Street that had the best blends of teas in all of New York City. The place was frequented quiet often by people passing by, though despite its fine selection of assorted teas, it never got a strong flow of customers. It was a quiet place, one of those you would refer to as a well-kept secret of the city. That’s why Chris Storm loved it. It was a place he could go that wasn’t crowded and he could replenish his body. Though he sorely did not look like a tea drinker, Storm was actually quite the regular for Chan’s. A nice, old lady brought out a hot cup of oolong tea while Chris waited for Jennifer, but before he could enjoy his first sip, she arrived. Under her arms were a plethora of bags, each with a different designer label on it. There was Gucci, Coach, and many others Chris didn’t recognize but assumed cost a good penny. When she finally got close enough for him to get a good look at her, Chris noticed that she looked horrible, as if she hadn’t slept in three weeks. Despite the large aviator sunglasses covering her eyes, Chris could still see twice as many bags under her eyes then were in her arms. Her hair, despite what looks like it may have been her best attempts, was wild and matted. Even her clothes were wrinkled and dirty. To put it bluntly, Chris thought she looked like crap. A far cry from her vixen days of youth. As if the two were absolute best friends, she came over to his side of the table and gave him a peck on the cheek before taking her seat. “Oh tea!” she giggled as she reached for his cup. Chris’ hand cut her off, pulling the cup closer to him. Jennifer just laughed and gave him a smile. “So…” It was at this point that the left side of Chris’ brain had turned to the right and said “what the fuck is wrong with this bitch?” The right side would have responded but it was too busy trying to figure out why we even agreed to meet with her in the first fucking place. This was how aneurysms happen. Unfortunately Chris wasn’t that lucky. “So, what?” he replied, trying to figure out just what she was getting at. Jennifer giggled again and playfully slapped the back of Chris’ hand. Jennifer moved her chair closer to him but Chris just responded by pushing his further away from hers, while still holding onto his tea. Jennifer tried to move closer again, but for a second time Chris kept his distance from her. “Oh you tease,” she said, “stop.” Chris had no plans of stopping or being anywhere near her if he could help it. If it weren’t for the little five year-old in daycare right now, he would have absolutely nothing to do with her. “What’s the matter?” Chris didn’t bother to answer her though; instead he turned his head off to the side and sneered. Adjusting in his seat, Chris pulled out his wallet and handed over yet another of his credit cards to her. He wanted this little meeting to end. Sure enough, upon seeing the plastic Jennifer went on the attack and snatched it out of his hand. Like a deer she was off, no doubt to another shopping mall or some bullshit. In the distance she muttered off a ‘thanks’ to which Chris simply ignored. He hated the entire situation. One on hand he had the option on what just happened; giving her money and keeping her out of his life for extended periods of time. On the other, however, she could take him to court for child support. Given how loony some judges are, Chris didn’t want to bother with option two. The damage to his wallet would almost be as bad as the damage from the publicity. At least this way he kept her at a few thousand a month and he only had to see her whenever she managed to max out another card. Despite the delicate flavors of the oolong tea, Chris still had a bad taste in his mouth. Deciding he needed a bit more physical stress relief, he decided to head back to the gym to see if he could get ANY training in before he had to pick little Chris up from the day care center. So as he walked, he thought about how he had even managed to get himself into the situation in the first place. It certainly wasn’t a point in his life that he was proud of. That short vacation to Amsterdam had cost him a great deal of money, his career, nearly his life, and many future headaches. But he couldn’t blame anyone but himself, he knew this. It was his decision to use the drugs. It was his decision to get drunk. And it was his decision to have unprotected sex with Jennifer (and consequently another woman at the same time.) Now he had to pay the price for his reckless youth. At least one thought kept him going. He knew that no matter how bad his own personal life had become, or would become, that Seymour’s would be worst. If in Infinite Gauntlet he had a chance to make it even a bit tougher, he gladly would. That drove Chris Storm to better himself. It forced him to give that extra inch when his body and minds tells him to stop. With his two first and assortment of suplexes, he would pound Seymour into the mat for no good reason other than to show him that no one messes with HIS Tessa. Damn it felt good to be a prick sometimes. ---------------------------------------------------------------- The lights of the city were illuminated, causing the city to look like a million fireflies. In Chris’ apartment, the Ironman was getting ready for another night of sleep. He had just tucked his son into bed, hoping that the child would not be plagued by another night terror, and was heading to his own bed. As he cut across the kitchen to head to his bedroom, there was a knock on the door. Part of him wished that at the door it would be Tessa, but he knew it would be impossible. She was in Las Vegas for the GCW PPV Deadly Games (cheap plug). Maybe he could get lucky it would it just be a late night vacuum cleaner salesmen, if they still existed. The door then shot up before Chris even had a chance to open it. No surprise it was Jennifer came busting through the door as if she lived there, or if Chris even liked her. She threw down her bags and walked over to the couch, plopping down on the brown cushions. At least this time she looked a little more presentable. Patting the cushion beside her, she encouraged Chris to take a seat beside her. Chris just stood where he was; content to stay away from her. Jennifer have him a coy smile, motioning for him to come over to him, but Chris just shook his head. Trying to be intriguing, she unbuttons the top on her shirt. Not wanting that behavior to escalate any further, Chris put a stop to it, “don’t bother. If you came here for sexual advances than you can forget it.” She was clearly put off, but not enough to give her the clue that she wasn’t wanted there. “Hey you remember that night?” she said, lying over on the couch to face him. Snarling, Chris replied “I try not to.” Jennifer rolled over, her eyes lighting up, and she went for one of her bags. “I think I have something that will remind you.” For some reason, that statement sent chills down Chris’ spine. He just knew the girl was going to do something incredibly stupid. Sure enough she did not disappoint. “Is that what I think it is?” he shouted, nearly jumping out of his skin. In her hands was a small plastic package that was filled with a white powder. Jennifer brought the package over toward Chris but the former Last Great Technical Wrestler kept moving away from her, as if the package would leap out of her hands and attack him. Placing the package on the table, she started to undo the plastic wrap that bound it into a tightly packed cube. Seeing her starting to open it, Chris snapped “Get that shit out of here!” Jennifer ignored his demand to stop and placed a razor on the table next to the powder. “Come on, you know you want to.” “What are you, fucking nuts?” Chris pointed his hand over toward the guest bedroom door. “Bringing drugs into here when your child is in the next room!” She clearly did not see the problem with that, “so?” he replied, “he’s asleep.” Using the razor to separate a portion of the cocaine from the pile, Jennifer started to shape it into a thin line. Chris couldn’t take it any longer. He may have his many vices, but he wouldn’t stand for this. With one sweep of his hand the drugs were sent flying off the table and crashing into the carpet, painting part of it white. Jennifer’s eyes became enlarged. In a surprise fit of rage she leapt forward, both arms extended, at Storm. “You fucking bitch!” she screamed, trying to claw at him. It was easy for Chris to toss her to the side, where she crashed into the floor. Turning to face him, Jennifer was crying. “It’s because of that slut, it’s it?” SMACK! For those wondering, that was the sound of Chris straight up back-handing Jennifer, knocking her back down to the floor. Unfortunately it couldn’t have happened at a worst time, as the child had walked into the room at that point to see Chris strike his mom. “Get out of my apartment, NOW!” Chris ordered, not noticed the child yet. Jennifer knew it wouldn’t be swayed by her, so she picked herself off the floor, rubbing her cheek where she had been smacked. With a snuffed retort, “fine”, Jennifer grabbed her bags and exited out of the apartment. Chris turned to try to finally head back to his bedroom when he finally noticed the child. ‘Fuck’, he thought to himself, wondering just how long his son had been there. The tears rolling down the boy’s face showed that it had been too long. Chris kneeled down to comfort him, but the boy ran back into his room and dove underneath the blankets. Mentally berating himself, Chris didn’t know if he should try and explain the situation to the child or to give up and go to sleep himself. He wanted to just do the second, but that annoying voice in the back of his head kept insisting on the other. Cautiously he entered the room, keeping his eye on the younger Chris. The boy was cowering under the blankets in the dark; the only source of light was the Thomas the Train nightlight in the corner that gave a small radius of illumination. Chris sat down on the corner of the bed and slowly moved his hand to the boy, which turned out to be a bad idea, as the child backed away, scared to be hit like his mother. “I’m sorry” he told the boy, moving closer on the bed. “You never should have seen that, and I never should have lashed out like that.” The boy’s head poked out from under the blanket, “do you hate mommy?” Ouch. How was he supposed to respond to that? “Yeah, you’re mom’s a gold digging bitch?” Somehow that didn’t feel like the best explanation. “You’re mom and I have a very strange relationship, one that we are still trying to figure out. And sometime that causes us to have problems, like what you saw.” That was so dodging the question. “But it doesn’t give me the right to do what I did. I’m sorry.” Luckily the child started to feel better after having Chris try to explain his side. The small neon numbers of the boy’s Power Ranger alarm clock flashed [11:35 P.M.] in bright green letters. “You need to go to bed, ok? We’ll talk more about this in the morning if you want.” The child nodded and pulled the blanket back over him. Chris kissed the boy on the forehead and headed out of the room. Entering the living room again, his looked around with a sigh. His carpet was coated with cocaine. He had no time to train for the Infinite Gauntlet. Tessa was thousands of miles away. His son was most likely scared of him now. It was late at night and before he could even go to sleep himself he had to clean up the illegal drugs off the floor. (Can’t go leaving those just lying around, right?) By the time he was able to crawl into his own bed, Chris felt like shit warmed over. He was tired and just wanted sleep. Wrapping his sheets around his body, Chris closed his eyes and nodded off to slumber. Tessa walked behind him, telling him to keep moving. He’s trying to tell her to slow down, that he needs a moment, but she keeps rushing him. Why? What is the hurry? “You need to keep moving! Faster! You have to keep going!” He turned himself around to confront her but it’s too late. She’s gone. Chris looked around, trying to see where she could have went, but it is no use. He’s found himself alone down an empty stone hall. A faint voice called out to him; “You have to make them love you.” Then, as if someone had cranked up the volume, Chris is bombarded by waves of cheers. Looking toward the end of the hall where he was being pushed, there is a velvet black curtain. The noise is radiating from the fabric, getting louder with every step Chris took. As he approached the curtain, it felt as if the noise was actually in his head. Peering out from behind the curtain was an entire arena of fans. As soon as they noticed the Perfect Storm the entire place fell dead silent. “STORM BLOWS! STORM BLOWS! STORM BLOWS! STORM BLOWS!” The shock wave from the chant slammed against Chris, causing him to stumble back and soon fall. Still, relentlessly, the waves kept crashing against him. He was effortless to try and stop from rolling back. Before he knew it he was blown right down the hallway and came at a stop flat on his back. Gazing up, he saw a pair of crystal blue eyes looking down at him. “Failed again? You’re such a loser.” Chris cringed at her words like sharp knives to the chest. It couldn’t get any worst, it wouldn’t get any worst. It got much worst. From behind her came another figure, casual in his strut. His arm looped with hers and soon Chris could see the strands of white hair falling over his shoulder. Seymour Almasy looked down at Storm and smirked; “having fun lying down again? Honestly Chris, don’t you have anything better to do than humiliate yourself again?” Chris shot up to his feet, muttering a few choice curse words. He took a swing, aiming for the chin, but fell a mile short. Tessa and Almasy just laughed as Storm fell on his face again. Before Chris could even try to get back to his feet, the two strolled off together, arm in arm. When he could finally get back to his feet he found himself being pounded by those vicious words again. “STORM BLOWS! STORM BLOWS! STORM BLOWS! STORM BLOWS!” Chris Storm shot up in his bed, sweat soaking his forehead. A nightmare, it was just a nightmare. He tried to calm himself but his body continued to shake. Why did it feel so real? He wanted to call out, for someone to help him. But no one was there. Unlike his son, there would be no one to enter the room and comfort him, telling him that everything would be alright. Turning to his night stand, he picked up the phone. “Tessa? Hey, sorry to bother you…Yeah, I know it’s late, I’m sorry…No, nothing wrong. I just wanted to hear your voice…Forget about it, pretend like I never called. I’m sorry. I’ll just talk to you later.” He wanted to ask her if she was with Almasy. To know if the two of them had been mocking him behind his back. He knew deep down it was foolish. She cared about him and would never do anything like that to hurt him. But still…it didn’t make Chris want to pound Seymour’s face into hamburger any less. Fuck the little white haired bitch, Chris was going to fuck up his world. GTT6? Forget about it, his swansong ends here with me.
