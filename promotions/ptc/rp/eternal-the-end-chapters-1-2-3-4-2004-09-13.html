Eternal: The End (Chapters 1,2,3,4)

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-13 Eternal: The End Prologue It was said that we were born in the world of kings, and raised from the ashes of despair. That people from past generations had said we were the frost in the cold winter nights, and the fire from the flames of fury. Told that we were born in the shadows of the night, separated only by the greed of the gods. We were the result of an experiment known to mortals as evolution. Some say evolution had stopped. We say they are wrong. It never stopped, and we are testament to that fact. For everything in this world comes in pairs. Where there are boys, there will be girls. Where good is present, so will evil reign. So it shouldn’t be all that surprising when it was known that where there are mortals, there are the immortals. However, not all immortals know about their dark gift, the gift that the heavens present to them in the form of pure eternity. Where the gift to the mortals is the ability to die, and yet the gift to the immortals is the chance to live. They can still feel pain and die, but not through the medium of time and aging. The gods play the game in such evil ways, creating opposites in the world of death. They thought they had hindered our lives, hoping that we will one day beg to die. Yet we smiled back in their faces. Instead choosing to live in the shadows, observing life from a distance. We are the guardians of time. But still we remained contented. Hoping that one day eternity will no longer be a friend, but an enemy. I know. You all must be wondering, that there must be some form of end. That the life of immortals can never go on forever, for there is no such thing as forever. That whatever began will have to finish. You are right. That’s why I am here. At the very brink of damnation, looking at the world through my eyes with a different perspective. It isn’t as beautiful anymore. Maybe I’m different from the rest, now that I’ve finally accomplished what I’ve searched for my whole life. A sister that was hidden from sight, but had always been there all along. There is no longer the need to wander aimlessly in the timeless medium of eternity, everything’s perfect now. The jigsaw of eternal memories is finally complete. It is now over. So you ask me why am I still here? You already figured the answer. I know, I haven’t kept my word. And that is why I am here for one last time. I promised to paint you a stunning picture of eternity. I will, I already began. Now I will finish what I have started. I will conclude the beginning. I will finally show you the end. Chapter One: Detachment Detachment. That seems to be the story of my life. Drowning myself into the memories of the past, somehow it detaches me completely from the pains of reality. Isolated within the confinements of my own imaginative world, desperation running through the very blood in my veins, enriching it with unwanted emotion. The world had shrunk in my eyes, placing me with only a narrow gap to view the constraints of time. Believe me, it wasn’t always like that. Time can both be a blessing, and a curse. For me, it was the latter. Time changes all things. It created wars out of jealousy, feuds out of hate, even fear out of pain. It is something magical in an almost evil way, its significance hidden in the eyes of most others. Throughout the years and generations I’ve searched, trying to seek another while I still seek a cure for the pain that I’ve endured since the day my mother passed on to the after life. And while I tried to heal my sorrow, I seek a medium to do so. The world of fighting, the game of sport. It provided me with a space to take out my frustrations, helping me drown my sorrows. Easing my anger at the injustice of my life. And soon, what I’ve worked so hard in the past for in the sport, I will finally get to finish it off. GTT Tournament, where the great will be separated from the good. First Round, and Hell Itself from the WWA will be the first victim. He will be the beginning of the end. You know what, forget I ever said all that. You wouldn’t understand anyway. You may think that I have all the time in the world to correct the wrongs into rights. And I don’t deny you for thinking that. You are right. Most things can be changed and corrected if you have enough time for redemption. But think of it this way. Maybe, just maybe, some things can never be redeemed even with eternity in your hands. I used to feel like that, the feeling of utter despair. Not even knowing which direction to look at. For years I’ve spent finding a sister I never knew, never seen except for in my dreams. As a matter of fact, I was even beginning to doubt that she ever existed, and that all that my mother had me believe was all a lie. But a sudden hope had arisen out of the depths of sorrow, shining a glimmer of light directly in my eyes. I finally had something to hold onto. It may not be much, but it is still something to grasp. If only my memories serve me right. Yet still I hold my doubts. Impatient of the outcome, knowing what it will finally achieve. Maybe the generations of trying had finally paid off within a split second of drowning myself into pieces of memories that I never experienced before. I guess with time we all will know. It’s always about time. Only time will tell where the story ends. Chapter Two: Strangers Snap. Kasper Sky’s frame of mind clicked back into place, bringing him back to the limits of reality. Away from his imaginations and dreams. He seemed more and more detached lately, his pale face not affected my emotion, but deep inside, he was burning with the destruction of feelings. He shook his head vigorously, trying to clear his mind of his previous thoughts. Flinging back his head to remove a flop of the pure white hair from his eyes, he shoved his hands into the pocket of his black jeans, hiding the silver rings on his fingers. “The years have tainted this building,” he muttered under his breath, observing the brick walls of the buildings that passed him by, “with its age, these walls have seen it all throughout the generations.” Kasper shifted his eyes down, hiding them from sight. He wanted to be lost in the eyes of mortals, but tonight there was nowhere to hide. He was walking all alone on the streets, passing dark alleys as he walked down the sidewalk, noticing the cracks of the concrete that he was stepping on. “In a week, I will finally be competing in the fourth edition of the GTT Tournament, and this time there will be no mistakes.” Kasper spoke sternly to himself, clenching his fists. “First round and I will be facing Hell Itself from the WWA. I’ve heard good things about him, but yet I don’t fear him. I don’t fear anyone, and I will get the task at hand done. He will be the first step on the track to glory.” He whispered to himself again, under his breath, just audible to his ears. He seemed intent on focusing on the tournament, trying to rid his mind of other thoughts. And then he heard someone whistling to a tune. A girl. It was distinctly familiar, but yet he couldn’t remember what it was. But somehow, the tune sent a shiver down his spine. For a quick moment, he tried searching into the library of his memories, but yet he couldn’t quite grasp and trigger something. Surprisingly, to him at least, it frustrated him. He looked up, and his eyes scanned the immediate surroundings, paralyzing his body of any other movements. “Looking for something?” a soft voice whispered from behind him, “or are you looking for someone?” she finished it off with a slight pause. Kasper spun around in a slight movement, taken aback that his usually reliable instincts have failed him. He looked, and he took a step back. A small one, but yet quite noticeable from the look of the girl’s reaction. She smiled right in his face, her crystal blue eyes almost similar to his. She seemed amused by Kasper’s lack of communication. “Guess some of us in this world aren’t really that fond of speech huh?” she spoke with a tinge of sarcasm in her tone, direct and quick, obvious that she wasn’t expecting a reply. Kasper frowned. “And where did you come from?” he managed to blurt out. She gave a short laugh, looking up towards the heavens in amusement. Kasper noticed her total black outfit, blending her in into the surroundings with ease. Especially at the spot where they were standing right now, hidden by the shadows. Somehow, the thought of her knowing all this entered his mind. She couldn’t have planned everything, could she? It was too perfect, he thought. Her footsteps interrupted his thoughts again, and his blue eyes followed her steps as she walked into an alley that he passed a couple of minutes ago, positioning herself against the wall. She was still in his line of sight, so he didn’t bother moving. Her blonde hair gleamed slightly in the dim light, her features now clearly seen. She was attractive, he observed. “You passed me just now,” she shot back, “but obviously you didn’t notice,” she smiled sweetly as she spoke. Kasper Sky kept his silence, trying to figure out this girl’s intentions. He didn’t know her, but she looked like someone from his past. But still he couldn’t remember her. “Don’t worry, you have never met me before,” the girl replied, as if reading his thoughts. She crossed her arms, creasing her black jacket slightly. “She is your sister. Your search is finally complete.” The girl spoke, short but straight to the point. Kasper looked up in a vigorous movement, and her eyes met his. He was completely shocked. How could she have known this? The answer is simple, she couldn’t. “What do you mean?” he enquired in a rather harsh tone. The girl looked up again, this time in exasperation. “You know what I mean. All those years that you’ve endured, have finally come to an end.” She whispered back, a smile playing on her lips. “This is the end, Kasper Sky.” Chapter Three: Emotions The bell chimed as Athena Alexandria walked through the door of the convenience store, startling her slightly. She blushed as she saw the girl at the counter giggling, and managed a smile that was returned with another. “Hi…” Athena stammered out the words, “a quiet night at work huh,” she managed to blurt out, trying to create small conversation. The girl smiled at her again. “Yeah, it’s a pretty empty night…not that many people are out. Especially at this hour.” She replied back, while straightening her white blouse. Athena managed to steal a glance at the clock. “Half past one,” she muttered under her breath, “that leaves me about fifteen minutes before Kasper gets here.” “You seem to be in a cheerful mood tonight. Looking forward to something later?” the girl interrupted her thoughts with a teasing tone. Athena blushed again, and looked away. After thinking about it, somehow she wanted to confide in the girl, to ask of her opinion. She looked around, making sure that no one else was in the store. The girl noticed and leaned forward against the counter, as Athena approached. “You know, I seem to be in a dilemma…” Athena whispered, stammering a bit, not wanting to look straight into the girl’s eyes. “Tell me about it.” The girl’s reply was to the point, and it calmed Athena down a bit. She managed a small smile, while tapping her fingers on the counter. “Well, I really like this guy. He’s a really good friend of mine, but I’m confused. I don’t know what his feelings are towards me…” Athena replied back, in a soft tone, while burying her face in her hands. The girl gave out a small laugh, as Athena peeked out of her hands to stare at her face. She pouted her lips. “That’s easy.” The girl replied, ignoring Athena’s annoyance. “Well?” Athena enquired impatiently, still staring at the girl’s face. The girl grinned at Athena, pausing for a couple of seconds, apparently thinking of an answer. She crossed her arms, a playful smile on her lips. “Well, tell him. Make your feelings clear, and see what he says. That’s the only thing you can do right now, and that is to make the first step forward.” She spoke with enthusiasm, leaning forward and placing her face right in front of Athena. Athena kept silent for about a minute, thinking it over in her mind. “Maybe you’re right,” Athena whispered, heaving a sigh, “maybe I should just be honest with him, and see what he says.” “There you go, problem solved. Easy.” The girl teased while standing up straight again. “Thanks for the advice, I really appreciate it. Hopefully this will work out for the better. Cross fingers.” Athena spoke with hesitation, straightening herself up. She smiled at the girl behind the counter, whispering a soft thanks to her once again, and started heading for the door. The girl grinned, amused. “She must be so busy thinking that she didn’t even buy whatever she came to buy,” she muttered to herself, shaking her head. Athena didn’t catch her words. She stood outside the convenience store, and looked in both directions. No one was in sight. Quickly, she glanced at her watch. “Kasper’s late again,” she grumbled to herself, sighing loudly. “Well, he said he was going to come from the hotel. Maybe I’ll just walk in that direction, and meet him halfway.” She thought to herself decidedly. Athena turned and started walking in the direction of the hotel, alone on the street, as her steps created a thud each time it made contact with the concrete. Chapter Four: Rising Tensions Kasper Sky stared directly in the girl’s crystal blue eyes, not breaking contact. He clenched his fists, but the girl was unconcerned. “Just admit it Kasper Sky, everything points to that fact,” she went on, raising her tone, “there is no escaping it anymore.” “She is your sister.” The girl shouted, her echoes bouncing from wall to wall, ringing in Kasper’s ears. Kasper looked down slowly, trying to take whatever the girl was saying inside his head. Once again, he delved inside his memories, detaching himself from reality, trying to find the answer to the mystery of his life. “Kasper, let’s play a game.” Krystal said playfully, tossing her blonde hair around by shaking her head. Kasper smiled back at his younger sister, and looked into her deep brown eyes. “Whatever you want, Krystal.” He replied, sitting himself beside her in the living room of their house. “But keep it quiet, mother’s asleep.” Kasper continued, a stern warning to his sister, while trying to keep a straight face at the same time. Krystal giggled. “Ok. Let’s play this question game. What is your favorite word?” Krystal turned towards Kasper, and asked him, an innocent expression on her face. “Eternal.” Kasper replied, while Krystal wrinkled her face. She didn’t understand the word. She was much too young to understand. “It means forever.” Kasper smiled back into Krystal’s face, annoying her even more. She punched him playfully, causing him to rub his hand in pain. “Well… what’s yours then?” Kasper shot back. Krystal looked at him again, and smiled ever so sweetly. “My teacher taught me a new big word in school today.” Krystal whispered, sticking out her tongue at her older brother. “Captivation.” She said, a glimmer seen in her eyes. Kasper snapped back to the world of reality, shaking his head, trying to rid and clear his thoughts again. Krystal, his sister that he only had faint memories of, now lost within the constraints of time. “You see Kasper, that was only one part of your memories. Fit the other part in, and you will have a complete jigsaw piece.” The girl continued in a stern voice, capitalizing on Kasper’s current weak state of mind. “Go on, Kasper. Finish up your jigsaw of eternal memories. Finish what you started, show the world where it finally ends.” She whispered, smiling sadly into his blue eyes. Kasper closed his eyes, drowning himself once again. “You’re always stressing yourself over your memories, Kasper.” Kasper turned over and looked at Athena, after hearing her say those words. She had broken a long silence, while he had wandered deep into his dreams, trying hard to find a solution to all his problems. “That’s the only thing I have left, Athena. My memories.” Kasper whispered back, a touch of emotion in his voice. Athena turned quiet, her smile disappearing fast. Her eyes glazed slightly, welling up with tears. She couldn’t bear to see Kasper this way. Kasper noticed her reaction, and decided to call time on his thoughts. He turned his head towards Athena again, and smiled, trying to comfort her. It was in vain. And Kasper knew it. But he couldn’t think of anything else. He looked around the hotel room, trying to think of something. He finally did. “Let’s play a game.” Athena’s eyes widened, and gleamed. She loved games. “Sure.” She replied back, short but to the point. “What is your favorite word?” Kasper asked, a curious look on his face. Athena laughed, noticing the innocence in Kasper’s expression. “You first.” “Eternal.” Athena laughed again. Kasper’s favorite word seemed just about right. “Now how about yours?” Kasper whispered to Athena, annoyed at her laughter. Athena paused but for a second, thinking about her answer. “Captivation.” “Kasper?” Athena enquired in a soft voice, noticing Kasper Sky standing a couple of feet in front of her, his head down. The interruption from Athena brought Kasper crashing back down to reality, and slowed his reaction a bit. He turned in a sudden movement, and faced Athena, his face grim and without emotion. “We’ll meet again sometime soon, Kasper Sky. That I promise you.” The mystery girl whispered as Kasper turned away, and slipped into the dark shadows of the alley, oblivious to Kasper’s killer instincts. “Are you alright?” Athena pressed further, clearly not noticing the girl that was standing in front of Kasper just a couple of seconds ago. Athena was worried. Kasper ignored her question, gradually realizing where he was again. He turned his head, to see if the mystery girl was still there. The girl was gone, and he looked down, still thinking of her words over and over again in his head. “I should be thinking about the GTT tournament because that is what I’ve been constantly walking towards my whole life, or my opponent Hell Itself from the WWA who stands in my way, or even my entire life in general. But I am not.” Kasper whispered, facing Athena again. “Then what are you thinking about then?” Athena replied curtly, impatience overcoming her as she crossed her arms. Kasper Sky looked up at Athena Alexandria, his crystal blue eyes gleaming slightly. “You.” TO BE CONTINUED
