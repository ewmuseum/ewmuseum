Piece by Piece: Part Three

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 Sleep. More specifically, the sleep of one who has ingested large quantities of alcohol the evening before. It can be restless, yet at the same time, it protects one from the symptoms of hangovers that arise when a person wakes up. For Seymour Almasy, that headache is compounded by worry and preparation in regards to round two of GTT4. Enjoy those precious few moments of peaceful sleep, Seymour. From here on out, you won’t be getting much of it. ----------- “Seymour? Seymour? SEYMOUR!!” The exhortation was accompanied by loud banging, which was enough to jar Seymour Almasy awake. Even before his eyes opened, he could sense the massive headache was forming. To say he didn’t feel well would have been an understatement. Still, he heard the constant screaming and banging, and decided that shutting whoever it was up would be an important first step to mitigating his headache. Seymour Almasy: Alright, alright, I’ll be there in a second… Groggily, the PTC Global Champion threw back the covers. Several things were odd. First of all, there were a pair of panties resting next to him. Secondly, there was another pillow next to him. Third, two cups from something called the Gaia rested on his nightstand. He looked from one object to the next, to the next, and could only come to one conclusion Seymour Almasy: Shit, I must have had one hell of a night. “Seymour? Are you in there?” It was way too early to try to remember what happened, Seymour decided. All that he knew was that his first vocal effort to get the infernal noise to stop had failed, and so he would now make another. Seymour Almasy: I SAID I’ll be there in a minute! He shook his head out, attempting to wake up enough to make a movement towards the door of the hotel that he was staying in. He tried. He tried some more. He didn’t succeed. “SEYMOUR!! SEYMOUR!!!” Seymour Almasy: Mother FUCKER… By this point, it was entirely clear to Seymour that whoever the fuck it was that was banging on his door and screaming loud enough to wake the dead couldn’t hear him, and would thus continue to bang and shriek until Almasy answered the door. “SEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEYMMMMOOOOOOOOOOOOOUUUUURRR!!” Therefore, his first move was to get to his feet, and put on a pair of pants, as he was naked (fourth thing that was odd, for those counting at home). Sufficiently clothed to answer the door, he crossed the room to the door, and promptly realized something important. Seymour Almasy: I’m in my apartment. Wait a minute, I’m not supposed to be here…so what the hell am I doing here? By this time, Seymour was conscious enough to realize that, since this WAS his apartment, he had to make his way through several rooms. To a sober Seymour, this would be a simple task, but for the disoriented, hungover Almasy, it took far longer than it should have. Finally though, Seymour managed to make his way to the apartment door, and ceased the banging by opening it. Seymour Almasy: Who the hell are you? Before Seymour stood a somewhat mousy looking man. His dress shirt bore the insignia of Primetime Central, and in his hands, he held a bag. Seymour’s bag, to be exact. PTC Man: I work for Primetime Central. Trying to work my way up the company, but for now… Seymour Almasy: You do the grunt work. PTC Man: Err, yes. You left this at the arena last night. We’ve been looking all over creation for you. What happened? Almasy looked at the errand boy, and decided to be perfectly and bluntly honest. Seymour Almasy: Yeah, I kinda had a wild night. PTC Man: Really? I’m kinda surprised. That’s the sort of thing I’d expect from Darek, not you. Seymour Almasy: What do you mean? PTC Man: He seems to have a thing for hot women. Blood too, although I don’t know if the two are combined. All speculation, of course. The look of horror on Almasy’s face is clearly evident. He decides that it is high time that the conversation ends, before any more of DeAngleo’s “habits” can be brought to the fore. Seymour Almasy: Riiiight…can I just have my bag? PTC Man: Oh, of course. Sorry to wake you! Before I go though, take this. It’s a flyer for the Appalachian second round bracket. We’re posting them up all over. Handing over the duffel bag and the flyer, the PTC Man vacates the apartment, leaving Almasy to close the door, and attempt to begin to piece together everything. Seymour Almasy: Okay, first order of business, get the image of DeAngelo having sex out of my head. He chuckled to himself. Darek, despite any fetishes he may or may not have had, was a persistent opponent, one of the few men Almasy had not managed to pin to the canvas or force to submit. The fact that he was freaking huge didn’t make Seymour feel any better, either. His discomfort, however, would not be shown. Seymour Almasy: Hmm, nice flyer. They got the good UWF promotional shot of me. The flyer is a well-produced piece. Reading “GTT4: Appalachian Bracket Second Round”, it goes on to list what are presumably the two top matches of the round. Seymour Almasy: “All-Star Championship Wrestling superstar and Master of All-Styles Jamar Gordo takes on the King of the Indies, formerly of Rush Pro Wrestling, Violence Jack!” Heh, that’ll definitely be a match to watch. Despite the fact that I think the latter’s insane and the former’s a failed Hollywood actor, they both can go…alright then, let’s get to the important part. Beaming, Almasy reads out the caption for his match. Seymour Almasy: “Two of Primetime Central’s best! Reigning PTC Global Champion Seymour Almasy of the UWF takes on Darek “Dark Angel” DeAngleo from 4WW!” Bah, they gave the Gordo and Jack caption more effort than THAT… Minor complaints aside, the flyer fed Almasy’s ego. He knew that he was one of the best in the tournament, and PTC as a whole. Things like this simply reaffirmed that. It was his match that was the biggest, in the spot of honor. His match that was being used to promote the bracket. Just the way he liked it. Seymour Almasy: Alright then, I‘m the biggest name in the bracket. Everything is as it should be. Now, before I can worry about the evil demonic psycho who wants to kill me, I have to figure out what the hell happened last night. I beat Ash, got dressed to go clubbing, went down to this club…the Gaia, I think. Well, that explains the cups. I met this woman there…her name was…you know, I don’t think I ever found out. No one ever accused Seymour of having the most fluid thought transitions in the world. The tournament takes a temporary leave of absence from his mind, as he makes room to think of exactly what the hell happened. The Final Fantasy blushes slightly, as his memories of the events of last night begin to come back to him, slowly but surely. Seymour Almasy: Well, I’ve made out, and probably gone farther, with a girl who’s name I don’t know. Brilliant. Absolutely brilliant. The still somewhat sick Seymour collapses to the couch in his living room. Thankfully, his headache is starting to subside, but the twin feelings of worry and dread begin to take its place. Seymour Almasy: And to top off everything ELSE that’s going on in my life, I’ve got a guy the size of a small Third World nation who’s confident he can beat me. Undoubtedly, that Childress fuck who owns Four Winds Wrestling has probably reminded him of the glory that he’ll get when he beats me. Hell, maybe Childress has put a bounty on my head, anything to avoid facing me himself… Sore memories attack Seymour’s thoughts. In the buildup to the match between himself, DeAngelo, and Windsor, he had challenged the man behind the madness, 4WW’s owner, Ian Childress. Childress had refused the contest, and Seymour’s dislike of 4WW and its superstars was cemented. Seymour Almasy: Bunch of pansies in 4WW. Their vaunted Patti Anderson’s already out of the tournament. This leaves them with the waste of oxygen that is Kris “PWNED by Me” Kanton, this Robb Hardy guy, yet another of those damned McClancies, and Darek. Gee…I think I see why Childress would back DeAngelo. He’s the only one with any talent. Not that it matters much, really. Once I beat DeAngelo, Childress and the rest of his company can go crawl back under the rock they came from. Christ, 4WW annoys me almost as much as the DWO did, and they’re dead. Maybe when DeAngelo goes, 4WW can imitate DWO’s career path and implode. With his angry rant finished, Seymour continues scanning himself and the apartment, trying to piece together more of what happened. Seymour Almasy: I could have sworn she said that she wanted me to go back to her place. Ah well, we must have changed our minds. I wasn’t too coherent after that drink…that bartender in the Gaia wasn’t kidding about that thing. Rummaging through the duffel bag, Almasy located all of the things he would need for round two of GTT4. His white hair dye lay in a bottle on the bottom of the bag, and was taken out and put on the nearby coffee table. The Divine Blade Imperial II, resting in its scabbard, was also removed, along with the faux armor Almasy wore over his legs. With the bag nearly emptied, Seymour pulled the final item out of it, a small mirror. Holding the mirror in front of him, Almasy sees his weary and sick visage, in addition to several long scratches on his upper torso. Seymour Almasy: Well, whoever she was, she probably liked to be in control. He chuckled to himself, before rising, and returning to the proverbial scene of the crime. His bedroom was much cleaner than he would have expected, and save for the panties on his bed, and the leftover cup, he never would have known anyone was in his room. Seymour Almasy: Still…I wonder why the hell we ended up here? Maybe it was closer, lord knows she was trashed enough before I even met her. Nice girl though. Pretty, too. And intelligent… Almasy shook his head. No getting involved with women again. It always only led to trouble. Seymour Almasy: For all I know, we did it last night with no protection, and now I’ve got a child on the way. I sincerely hope not, I’m probably the only person less fit than my parents to raise a child. Well, except maybe DeAngleo. I wouldn’t wish that psychopath as a father on anyone. Gracelessly, Seymour fell back down to the bed. The worry and dread were now subsiding. However, nausea was taking their place. Seymour Almasy: Darek’s obsessed with recognition, obsessed with fame. Even now that he’s become the 4WW North Wind Champion, he’s not content. He knows that he’s not the best, not recognized as a top star. And he knows that the best way to get it what he craves is to go straight through Seymour Almasy. For all of his other faults, he’s certainly nobody’s fool. But to last through a regular match without me pinning you is one thing. There was a third person in that ring who defeated us. In a tournament, in a one on one match, Darek…there is no third competitor. There is only you and I. You seem to love to paint the picture of how in that match, I failed to get the job done. And yet, you never look at the flip-side of that statement. You ALSO failed in your goal. You failed to defeat me. So what makes you think you can get the job done now? Seymour sighed out of a combination of exasperation and exhaustion. He was desperately trying to fight back the nauseous feeling that was overtaking him. It wasn’t a battle that he seemed likely to win. Seymour Almasy: You can’t, Darek. No matter how good you are in Four Winds, no matter how big you are or how strong you are, you will not be able to topple me. You see, a match is like a poem, or a short story. A self contained work. But a tournament is like a novel. Each match is another chapter. And my tournament work, Darek, is simply epic. I made my reputation by beating six competitors in the Primetime Invitational Tournament. Solidified it by beating six more in PTC’s Cruiserweight Tournament. But now, Darek, I don’t need to create a reputation. You do. I don’t need to solidify a reputation. You do. What I am here for is to make absolutely and utterly sure that fifty years from now, when a new generation looks back on us, that my name will be spoken of as the greatest ever. Above Chryste. Above Homo/Phobic. Above Tristan Belmont, Chris Storm, Hessian…above every single Primetime Central modern-day ‘legend’. And if you honestly think that I’m going to allow a delusional, clumsy oaf like you to ruin that for me, you are sadly, sadly mistaken…urp… The passion of the moment, combined with the speaking, forces Seymour to run to the bathroom, thankfully attached to the apartment’s bedroom. For several moments, the unmistakable sounds of vomiting can be heard. Mercifully, the harsh sounds end with the flushing of a toilet and the running of a sink. Seymour then emerges, a towel in his hands, white skin turned even paler. Seymour Almasy: Alright, let’s hope THAT doesn’t happen when I’m fighting DeAngleo. I’m either going to need to get better at drinking, or stop. Option number one seems like a good idea…heh… Walking back over to the bed, Seymour picked the panties up. They were black, thong-cut, and felt absolutely wonderful in his hands. Just then, he noticed a flash of white, and found himself reaching into the panties, coming out with a folded piece of paper. Seymour Almasy: Yevon…just when I thought my day couldn’t get any stranger. I hope this wasn’t in these last night… The Global Champion opened the paper, revealing a note written in a sloppy, inconsistent handwriting. Seymour Almasy: This must have been written last night, while she was still drunk. What the hell does this say…? Squinting, and trying to make his way through the bad handwriting, he slowly began to read the note aloud. Seymour Almasy: “Thank you….for one of the greatest knits--no nights…of my life.” Aww, how sweet. “When you wake up, I won’t be here.” Well, I figured that out pretty quickly, yes. “Good luck in your tournament. When you’re done, if you’d like to see me…” Hmm, it’s an address. An address in TORONTO?! What the hell? That fact alone gave Almasy pause. If she wanted to see him, then why the hell would she have decided to go to Toronto? But if she didn’t want to see him, why did she give him her address? Seymour Almasy: She’s toying with me. I don’t know if she wants me to go or not. Ah well, it’s not like I don’t have time to make up my mind. He sighed. The fact that he was even considering such folly was evidence enough that yes, Seymour was STILL quite swayed by a pretty face. Thankfully, he could put those thoughts away. He had a tournament he could think about instead. Seymour Almasy: Let’s see, in the past two days I’ve kicked a prostitute out of my house, beaten Ash, gotten totally fucking wasted, met this really cool girl, and woke up alone in my apartment. Next to that, none of DeAngleo’s bizarre freaky supernatural shit can phase me. *RING! RING!!* The sound of the telephone jarred Seymour from his thoughts. He didn’t even need to pick it up to know who was on the other end. Seymour Almasy: Man…I really don’t want to deal with this right now. As he looked around the bedroom, he noted that none of his ex-wife’s personal effects were in the room. Seymour Almasy: She must have gotten everything out already… At Terror in Tokyo, the UWF’s most recent pay-per-view event, Seymour turned on his wife for an opportunity to destroy his hated nemesis, the Flinders bracket’s own Tsunami Oni. Needless to say, Laura was not pleased at all. Over the course of the past few weeks, she had removed her things from the apartment, when Seymour was not in. Finally, it seemed as if she had finished the job. Seymour Almasy: Ah well. Good riddance. Rarely have words been said with less conviction. The phone continued to ring, as Almasy struggled to come to grips with exactly what had occurred. Seymour Almasy: The stupid plan fell apart. The Knightmare Brigade is no more. I could have just gone into UWF with her and gotten back at Tsunami Oni. It was a stupid idea. Live and learn, I guess. There wasn’t remorse, per se. As much as he thought his plan was bad, he didn’t really want to be back with Laura. That fact surprised him greatly. Before he could think any more on that, though, the answering machine picked up, and the voice rang through the apartment bedroom. Voice: Seymour, it’s your attorney, Mr. Fields. I know you’ve got your tournament, but you’re going to need to get back to me within the next week or so. Laura’s finally officially filed for divorce… Seymour Almasy: It’s about time. Voice: …and her list of requests is here. Nothing too extravagant, lucky for you, but some things we’ll need to go over. Talk to you soon, bye. The recording stopped, and Seymour nodded. He had expected this day to come for weeks, and it was finally at hand. Seymour Almasy: I don’t even really know why I don’t want her back. I guess it’s partly because I’m stubborn…partly because I see her as a symbol of what I used to be. I depended on her too much. Never really lived. Now I am. Hopefully though, we can both act like adults, and get through this with a minimum of pain. After all, it had already been painful enough to turn on his wife in front of millions on pay-per-view. More painful than any Shooting Star Cheat, Blackbreaker, Whirlwind Romance, or Soprano could ever be. But he had put that behind him now. That was in the past. The present was Darek DeAngleo, the here and now was GTT4. Seymour Almasy: I can never have nothing on my mind, can I? Even now, that I’m trying to live a regular life, I’ve still got things hanging over my head. This woman….my divorce…and I’ve got to try to keep my career straight through all of it. Yevon, I’m twenty-three fucking years old. I’m too young for this shit. It was times like this that made him think why he even wrestled. His life had been enough of a mess without having to worry about three-hundred pound psychos like DeAngleo who wanted to make their name on burying him six feet under. Seymour Almasy: It’s pretty sad that wrestling’s my escape right now. A sport in which I take beatings that would cripple an average human being is my salvation from what could be a messy divorce. Laura can have pretty much anything she wants. I was an asshole…I’ll admit that. But please don’t let this be one of those long drawn out battles. I can’t take it. Just can’t. The taste of vomit was strong in his mouth. He had been so high last night, after winning his match and meeting the woman who’s name he didn’t even know. With the morning, however, had come real life. And, just like it had when he believed himself to be a fantasy hero, real life kicked Seymour’s ass every single time. Had Real Life been his opponent in GTT4, Almasy would have been out in the first round. It wouldn’t even have been close. Seymour Almasy: But I’m going to keep on fighting. It’s all that I know how to do. For a few glorious hours this weekend, I can stop worrying about mystery women in Toronto. I can stop worrying about divorce. And I can do what I love. As seriously as I take what I do, as important as it to me to win…when it all boils down to it, I’m in the ring because I love it. You don’t take the amount of abuse I do inside those ropes without loving it. Darek DeAngleo’s going to be the second man in this tournament to find out just how much I love it. For now, however, Almasy was done thinking. He carefully placed the panties and the note on his nightstand, next to the two Gaia cups. Slowly, he crawled back into the warmth and safety of his bed, throwing the covers back over his tired and sick body. Just before his head hit’s the pillow, he speaks the final words he will say for at least nine hours. Seymour Almasy: Goodnight, world. His eyes closed. It would take some time for sleep to come. But while he waited for it, several things were clear in his mind. The story of Seymour Almasy was inexorably linked to the GTT4 tournament. However, unlike the tournament, his story wouldn’t be coming to an end any time soon. Whether or not he wanted to admit it, Darek DeAngleo could end his run in the tournament. But that wouldn’t end his story. Win or lose, the story of Seymour Almasy would continue on. Whether his tournament would, well… That would be known soon enough.
