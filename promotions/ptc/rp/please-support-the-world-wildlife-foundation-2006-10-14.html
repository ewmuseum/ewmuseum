Date: 2006-10-14 -- Thanksgiving, 2005 -- Location: The Zell Estate. "I’m telling you… this is the last place I wanna be right now. You know how much SHIT I’m gonna get because I quit WWA? She’s probably already thinking of ways to castrate me." Red lights were never long enough when you wanted them to be, but when you were in a hurry, they took all the time in the world to change. The blonde teen in the black corvette sat there for a while with his foot on the brake. His body and mind were one in the decision that they didn’t want to go forward. "Yeah, you’re probably right. I mean this is a good way for her to finally dump me, and then I’ll be rid of her dumb ass once and for all. It really goes against my plans… cause I wanted to at least do her once or twice before I got rid of her… but hell, never look a gift break up in the mouth when you’re trying to get away from a clingy, obsessive, greedy airhead." The horn blaring behind him forced his hand. Damn family and friends trying to get to dinner on time. The turkey wasn’t going anywhere; the beheading, gutting, stuffing, and baking made sure to that. The car surged forward reaching 30mph. That didn’t please the car none behind him either, the teen gaining another sound of the horn. He extended his arm out the window and waved them by, watching the red sedan speed around him and on their way to happy holiday cheer. No such thing existed in the forthcoming moments that awaited him. "Well all I know is that once Easy VZ is out of the picture, then I don’t have to worry about her parents anymore. I’ve had enough of the Zells to last me the rest of my life. So let’s do this thing… one night only, and it’s sayonara, suckas!" The car pulled up to a gated entrance, where an intercom was posted at the side of the road. Rolling his window down, he pressed the button. "HIIIIIIIIII!!" It seemed like the static fuzz and the distortion only elevated the annoying tone of her voice. "Yo, open the gate. I’m starving." "You got it, honey!" Cringing, he rolled up his window and sighed as the gates began to open. "Yeah… I know. It doesn’t matter how you hear her voice, she makes you wanna die." Xian Phillipse was quite fond of Tricky, obviously. Lowering the trip hop booming through his car stereo, he pulled around through the half mile of woods to reach the luxurious Zell manor. It was only 3 floors, but the way it was built made it seem like a movie star lived there. Mrs. Zell had always made it a job of hers to make sure her home was in the pinnacle of design and fashion. It was her only job, actually… aside from looking good, drinking, smoking pot, having sex, and semi-raising her daughter. Parking right at the front door, XP sat there for a moment thinking. "This is it. No turning back now. Well… I could, but she’d run me down like a gazelle or something." He leaned his elbow against the door, holding the cell phone to his ear. "Yeah, sucks for her. She’ll be embarrassed considering her grandfather’s supposed to be here or something. Heard he was a bit shot bureaucrat or something… like that’s supposed to be important to me. I mean you could be G-Dub Bush and I’d still treat you like every other retard I run into." The speaker made a good point, which made XP smirk. "Yes, I do see the correlation there. But I’ll get with you later, ok? Tell your mom to save me some venison. Peace out, gangsta." Folding his phone up, he exhaled deeply before shutting the car off and stepping out. He looked semi-formal, dressed in black slacks with a long sleeved white shirt and black tie. The formality of the outfit ended there, as he wore a pair of black Chuck Taylors, and his tie wasn’t tightened around his neck and his top button undone… and his shirt wasn’t tucked. Yep… XP knew how to dress for dinner. The frosted glass doors opened before he could reach the walkway, and around 100 pounds of rich girl came bolting out and pouncing onto XP. "XIIIIIII-AAAAAHHHHHN!!" She wrapped her arms and legs around him, kissing him right on the lips. Good thing she had her eyes closed, cause he rolled his eyes right then and there. When she leaned back, he smiled hard, as if the jig in fact was NOT up. "How’s my big strong wrestling champ?" "Ah, couldn’t be better, I guess." He let go of her waist to give her the hint he wanted her down, but she clung hard, like a fame and fortune parasite. He stood there with his arms down by his sides as she held on for dear life. "Good! Mommy and daddy are waiting for you inside. And Big Daddy will be here any moment!" Nodding politely, XP started walking towards the door, hoping she’d get the point then. Still, the pixie held on hard, as if she was drowning and he was a life saver. "Yeah… well… I’d like to get inside and sit down, ok?" "Ok!" And she still held on. "Well… you gonna let go or what?" "Oh! Sorry babe!" She dropped down onto her tan sandal heels and backed up. "You like my dress? I bought it today so I’d look good for you!" She twirled around in the low cut cocktail dress, flashing bits of under-aged temptation. There were certain bits that XP would miss should he sever his relationship with one Vana Delilah Zell… and those naughty bits where definitely the most important parts. If it weren’t for her vanity, greediness, promiscuity, and overall annoying nature… she’d be the perfect 16 year old girlfriend. And since XP was making so much money with WWA, the Zells ‘overlooked’ the fact that XP was 18. -- Monday, 2006 -- Location: The Bontecou home "Okay, dude… you mind just skipping to the good part?" Roughly a year late, Xian Phillipse was stretched out on a black futon with his arms behind his head relaxing. Huddled up next to him was a lithe, pale skinned long haired brunette wearing a pair of black running shorts with white trim and a black tank top with black emo-styled glasses. Dressed in a blue tee with fitting blue jeans, the two of them sat listening to the various artists that Winamp decided to throw into their ears. At the moment, it was Nightmares on Wax’s ‘Jorge’. XP glanced down at the bottom of her head with a wrinkled brow. "You asked me to tell the story, so I’m telling you the story." "But I remember that part… I was on the phone for most of that part." "And I thank you for relaying to me the entire phone conversation we had that night. A photographic memory works well, don’t it?" She sat up, leaning back against the wall. "Just get to the point, bitch. When Senator Halifax showed up…" "Well… he shows up, and…" -- Thanksgiving, 2005 -- Location: The Zell Estate. "Is that your heap parked in front of the house?" This old relic was everything that embodied politics: old-fashioned, gray haired, prejudiced, snobby and overly-serious white bread. XP stared eye to eye with Cornelius Scrooge Halifax., the rugged southern politician who didn’t seem intimidated by the teen’s forwardness and refusal to cow-tow to those in authority or higher status. XP nodded, "I wouldn’t call it that." "I would," he retorted. Vana stepped in before things could get any worse. "Big Daddy, this is my boyfriend. Go on, shake his hand!" Snorting, he leaned his head back to look down on XP. "So… this is the Phillipse boy you told me about. How do you pronounce your first name, son?" As many times as XP has heard this, it was more irritating to hear it from this patronizing old fart. "Like Zion… with a Z. You know… like Greater Mount Zion Tabernacle choir… or some shit like that." Sr. gave a short nod, looking at him as if he were still trying to figure the outspoken lad out. "Xian Phillipse… sounds like one of those Earth-child hippy names. You’re not a hippy, are you boy?" "Eh, I’ve got no beef with Mother Nature," he shrugged his shoulders casually, slipping his hands into his pockets. "As long as she doesn’t get PMS on me and fuck us all up with earthquakes and tornadoes and blizzards in the summer and stuff like that." It was at that moment that Vanessa and Vanderbilt came into the living room, thus putting this struggle between old and young to a halt. A moment more and the two would be wrestling each other to the ground. Of course… XP would win, though. "Big Daddy!" Vana’s mom rushed over and gave her father a big kiss on the cheek, hugging him tightly. Vana joined in, wanting to feel the love. Vanderbilt stood behind XP with a glass of wine, adjusting his tie before leaning in and shaking his hand. "How you doing, sir?" The levels of kiss-ass in his voice couldn’t be measured on any scale. "Fine, now that I’m here with my two little girls!" He hugged them tighter, getting a giggle out of the two blondes. For that moment it was like XP didn’t even exist to them… which was something he really didn’t mind… made it easier to sneak out. Vanderbilt placed a hand on Xian’s shoulder, causing him to lose all focus in ignoring everyone around him. "Hello, Xian. Doing well?" "Well as well can be, I reckon." XP watched as Vanessa took the wien glass from her husband and downed the whole thing from brim to stem. "It’s always good to see you, Xian. You know how welcome you are here in our home." She leaned in and hugged him around the neck, placing a little kiss on his cheek close to his lips. She very well could be a spy, because she was living two different lives right in plain sight; the first being the rich housewife, while the other being the sex-crazed nympho. In this household, if you didn’t have a penis, you just loved Xian Phillipse to death… and right now the other two cock carrying members of the house had nothing but ill will noted in the back of their heads for X to the P. -- Monday, 2006 -- Location: The Bontecou home "Hey yo, Bizzy… why am I telling this story again?" The raven haired vixen folded her arms across her chest, looking up at the ceiling. "Because Vana’s grandfather, Senator Halifax, is running for president… wanted to know what your impression of him was." XP sat up and looked at her confused. "Is that it? Shit… thought you wanted to hear about how I dumped Vana and her whole family flipped out on me, like psycho shit for real, Biz." "I don’t care about that. I just wanted to know what he was like." Bijou Bontecou’s frankness was legendary in high school, along with everyone who knew her. Even her parents would tell stories of the past, when a young adorable Bizzy would tell it like it is without any remorse for hurting anyone’s feelings. While she didn’t feel the whole night’s details were important, Xian thought they were relevant. Of course he was the type of person that if he told a story, he told the prologue and epilogue to it as well, so you understood the full extent of what was being said. "The bitch threw a CARVING KNIFE at my FACE!" That was indeed true. He could still hear her shrieking voice in his mind to this day. Sometimes it woke him up at night, while in the midst of dreams he’d hear her voice screaming out through his mind and jolting him awake. He always checked the room afterwards, just to make sure she really wasn’t there with a hook or a icepick looking to finish the job. Believe you me… Vana Zell was definitely that crazy. "Meh, mere trivialities of life, I say. But from what you said, it sounds like Halifax is a traditional shitty politician. He won’t be getting my vote." She pulled her knees up to her chest, allowing her to inspect her black toenails. "So, what’s your plan for GTT? You know it’s coming up real soon." He scooted up next to her on the bed, folding his hands in his lap. "The same thing I do every match, Pinky. PROCEED TO BEAT HIS ASS!" "You make it sound so easy," the installed monotonous pitch in her voice made sarcasm extra potent and stinging. You just had to learn to deal with it, and enjoy it with a somewhat dark sense of humor. "You’re fighting that environmentalist, aren’t you?" He shrugged his shoulders as Goldfrapp and Esthero’s ‘Superheroes’ came to play. "Captain Planet? He can’t fuck with me. What, is he gonna tell me to get some toothpaste? Colgate for the Juggernaut… " It was those inside jokes that made the corners of Bizzy’s mouth twitch slightly. She called that a smirk. "Ain’t that a bitch." But all jokes aside, "Nah, but I’m probably just gonna deal with it like any other guy I wrestle, ya know? He’s just another guy that’ll step into the ring with no idea of what I’m going to do to him. I mean, who really remembers me besides the dicks in WWA?" "I would barely remember you if I wasn’t your best friend." This was true. If you weren’t one of the few people, things, or ideas in Bizzy’s circle of life, you were forgotten by the next day… less if you just aren’t important. She wasn’t self-centered like that… she just didn’t have time to think about everything under the sun unless it came across her path. Xian was just like that in a way, except he sometimes counted in the outside factors. It was little things like that which kept them close friends, as is usually the case with everyone who has a close friend or two. "Yeah I did some research on this Wildlife character… and if I didn’t know any better I’d swear he was an endorsed character by the WWF." That got XP’s attention. "You mean McMah--" "Shut the fuck up." Grinning, he knew she was too sharp not to know that was coming her way. "Heh, almost zapped your dumb ass with that one." "No, shut up for a minute, really." She sat straight up, her neck craning as she stared up at the ceiling. XP watched as she sat like that for a minute perfectly still. Then she crawled over XP to lower the volume knob on her speakers. It was times when Bizzy would linger over him in tight and closely cropped attire that the thin line between friends and fuckers began to disappear. The only thing that could keep him from standing at attention was wrestling… so he thought about the beating he’d be giving Wildlife. He could always come to the match wielding an aerosol can and a plastic rings from six-packs. Nah… when he thought about it, X to the P was earth-friendly as well. He just wasn’t as adamant about it as this nut-job. When Xian wanted to help out, he’d recycle and make sure no one littered around him… not become an earthen enforcer with the ability to leap tall redwoods in a single bound. Part of the reason why XP left wrestling was because of guys like Wildlife… guys who wanted to become these out of proportion gimmicks and become more of a showman than an athlete. GTT was the perfect forum for his return; it’s the Olympics of Wrestling, fore Pete’s sake. Men from all over the wrestling world come together to compete for one prize, the honor of their name. But as soon as XP saw the quality of people he’d be facing… he knew it’d be just another freakshow… and he’d have to take each freak out one by one. Bizzy climbed off the bed and sighed. "Damn it… she’s doing it again." Xian got up and followed her down the loft stairs, which led to the second floor of the home. They walked barefoot on the grass colored carpet downstairs and into the living room, where they found an older dark haired woman sitting Indian style in front of a miniature Buddhist bell and a stick of incense burning amidst a container of sand and gravel. "Oh Mother Earth we thank you for taking another one of your children into your comforting bosom. For it is from the earth that we are born, and to the earth we shall return when we are dusted off this mortal coil." Her palms were pressed together in prayer fashion, her crimped dark brown hair pulled back in a ponytail with a black scarf tied over. XP and Bizzy stood there watching as the barefoot woman in jeans and frilly white hippy dress continued on. "I thank you for the gift of life that you’ve given my blood, and we are grateful for it to be taken away as well, as it means we are to be inserted back into the life-stream and return as something greater in your wondrous plan." Now was the time that XP needed to ask a question. "Uh… what’s she doing?" "Death toll." Bizzy muttered softly, folding her arms across her chest. "She keeps count of everyone that dies in her life, and does this ceremony." "Oh," He took it rather lightly, despite the fact that keeping a death count of your life was rather… morbid. It was uncharacteristic of her… but then again, Bizzy’s mom was a cornucopia of contradictions and complexities. That was definitely passed down from mother to daughter. She took a small metal rod and rapped it against the bell, the sound chiming off through the room. After a moment of silence, she exhaled as if she had just finished with some form of gratifying labor and stood up. It was only then did she notice Bizzy and XP standing there. "Oh, hey sunshine." Yes, she meant Bizzy. Ironic, no? She smiled and walked over to them gracefully, hugging both her and Xian lovingly. "Who’s the bell toll for this time, mother?" Sometimes it was hard to tell they were mother and daughter, but then there were times when they were almost the same person. Her mom was so carefree and loving, where Bizzy was only carefree and bitter. Bizzy’s mother placed her hands on her shoulders, rubbing them softly. "Oh, honey… do you remember Hanson Love?" "No." she didn’t even have to think about it; no one of that name registered. "Well he was my sister’s aunt’s brother’s cousin by second marriage." "So what does that make him?" inquiring minds wanted to know. She placed her finger to her chin and thought about it for a moment. "… I’m not sure… but he was still family, so I had to honor his death." She kissed both of them on the forehead and walked towards the kitchen. As awkward a moment as that was, XP was used to it. He had been welcomed into Bizzy’s home since the first time her mom met him. She said that he had a kind soul, and a warrior’s heart… whatever that was supposed to entail. She said once that she thought of him as part of her own tribe. Bizzy was part Cherokee from her mom’s side, from the Shakori tribe around North and South Carolina. Strangely enough, her mother’s last name was Love. Full name… Summer Love. Don’t ask any questions… she never answered them. Xian looked to the Biz, a little unsure of what to do. "So… uh… you wanna mourn or something?" She shook her head, her face saying that was a bad idea. "Nah… let’s go get drunk." Sounds like a good plan to X to the P. Both he and Bizzy knew that for everything there was a reason, be it big or small. You eat because you’re hungry, you drink because you want to get drunk. Wildlife said he wrestles for the earth, for Mother Nature. How does winning a match help the ecosystem? A pin fall definitely doesn’t emit helpful gasses to patch up the hole in the ozone. He was just another deluded wannabe do-gooder that couldn’t realize the truth… that Mother Nature has been a bitch for centuries, whoring herself out to its inhabitants so that man can live. We rape her of trees, drink her dry of clean water, and covet her natural fruits and resources for our own selfish gains. It’s a game that man’s been playing since we can remember… a game that she’s been losing. Wildlife is just another loss on the record of Man v Nature. Way to take one for the team, kid. As sad as it was for XP to think about the planet’s plight like that… there was just nothing he could do about it himself. One man can’t save the world… but one man can do his best, and make a difference around himself first. Set an example and lead others to follow… so instead of trying to wrestle for yourself and saying the Earth appreciates a victory over one man… go clean up on the highway, bitch. Let XP proceed to winning GTT so he can donate a portion of his winnings to the World Wildlife Foundation. Won't be much help to the environment though… the charity'll have to change its focus in order to help Wildlife in his time of need after Xian beats him into a coma. The Wildlife won’t survive… that’s just hard luck.
