The End nears....[DWO HW Title rp#1,1/6 of The Final Saga]

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kaiowas Cable Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 52 Posted: Mon Apr 07, 2003 5:59 pm Post subject: The End nears....[DWO HW Title rp#1,1/6 of The Final Saga] King? That is what he has become.... A legend in his own right it has all come upon him at this one strenuous time. Kaiowas is the Lord of the Ktulu.... Not only did he have to wake up to the striking reality that he had passed a whole month in an unconscious state but he also was struck by even more surprises as soon as he re-entered the world of the living. Kaiowas' mind has been a fuzzy blur of information and a deadly mix of emotions. Pride flows alongside bloodlust and megalomania in his veins. His nerves fill his body with sensations he has never before had to experience. His brain gets him high on the thoughts of the future, then it crushes his hopes in swift bouts of depression and doubt. Kaiowas is going through the motions for what lies ahead and he knows he must or he will fail. The Ktulu Tribe that he has now come into rulership over depend on him for their survival. There is not much else important to Kaiowas than that.... The people that he lived with so many years ago in the Amazon Rainforest before he moved to America to join the DWO. Although it isn't just all for the Tribe.... When a man is wronged he strikes back, but when a servant is wronged by his master..... He strikes back with all the force that he can muster. That is what Kaiowas must do. He has long toiled for this moment, this chance, this opportunity to strike back at his former deity. The man that enslaved him with the promises of power, who turned him to his will and who nearly lead to the destruction of Kaiowas' life. And all along this "god" had been lying to Kaiowas.... lying through his giant teeth. The hatred Kaiowas feels for the manipulative evil twisted "Lord of Vengeance" Pultra grows daily. The respect he once held for a god that treated his Tribe well has fallen away like a veil. The truth was that Pultra was a fake god..... he was a fallen Angel from Heaven who had turned all the Tribes of the Amazon to his will. Kaiowas knew the truth and had opposed him, forcing conflict. Now Kaiowas stands defiantly in he and his Tribe' new home in the familiar Iowa Forest.... His mind racing with thoughts after just being proclaimed "Lord of the Ktulu".... *** King? Is that who I am? How can it be that I am Lord of the Ktulu? I have but saved them from the tyrant deity.... Is it now also my duty to make them survive? Why do they trust me so? Why do they respect me so? Why? *** [Kaiowas' thoughts ring true in his mind, but soon the mood changes within him and the other more confident natured side of his personality bursts in over the top of the frail doubting attitude...] *** King? Why not a king? I am the heroic saviour that lead them from certain defeat! I am their saviour from that wretched god.... I opened their eyes, I lead them from him! I see it now! This is my way, my destiny, my path.... Like my destiny to overthrow the evil bastard impostor. I am to rule the Ktulu! I am to bring them to victory.... The power that I will wield will be immense.... You cannot begin to understand how much power this brings to me! Power over men? That is but the beginning! Power over nature? That I will leech from that wretched Slayer who told me the truth about Pultra, the one sent to destroy him..... His reward to me shall be sweet. My betrayal shall yield me everything I need.... for now. Pultra.... that name even sickens me now even though I stand many leagues from where I must face him.... The sound of the name evokes hatred and anger.... The anger of battle, that heated passion of killing and victory... I must have it once more... I must destroy Pultra to know the taste of victory and to lavish in his eternal demise! His corpse shall be my stepping stone, my link to the world above and beyond this blue and green orb. I shall have powers equal to many a spiritual being... I shall rival the powers of Heaven itself! And I shall bring the Ktulu to greatness and me.....? I myself shall not stop! I will relentlessly destroy those who oppose me.... They shall feel the wrath of the Tribal Warrior.... of the Angel of Retribution.... of the LORD OF THE KTULU! *** [Reality] Cerra: Kaiowas? [Kaiowas shakes himself out of his raging reverie and brings himself away from the power hungry thoughts pulsing through his flesh and mind. His soul's fire is burning deep within him, scorching everything else, torching him, setting alight his will to destroy the falsity that is Pultra.... But shaking it aside, he forces himself to inhabit the world of mortals... for not too much longer. Standing as he was, on his high hilltop in the centre of the Iowa Forests, he drags his attention back to the present and back to reality. Having just awoken from a deep and long sleep, lasting thirty whole days, the Southern American twenty something Tribal Warrior had exited his tent to find his world had been once again turned upside down... Returning to a new place than where you fell asleep is one thing... when the places are thousands of miles apart is another. When you awake as something you were not before, you are also shaken and shocked. But when, to your surprise, all these things pop up all on the same day, the same hour and the same minute, the shock to the system is immense. Kaiowas has woken up miles from the Amazon Jungle to find that a month has passed and that he has been duly appointed as the sole leader and Lord of the Tribe he once stumbled upon on his travels to find his roots in the Amazon. Add to the the shock to find the place that you lived in America while wrestling in the DWO has been turned upside down and converted into an efficient defensive location. Kaiowas is standing gaping in awe in reality, but in his mind the possiblities this development hands him are being run by his sub consciousness. The power greatly appeals to him. But standing now, looking over the new village being constructed, he must talk to his closest friends in this Tribe. The three of them stand around him, shocked as he is, they thought he was dead and that they would have to give up hope on their leader. Around him stand Cerra, the influential Elder, Ferrus the leader of the Tribal Warriors, and Sett, a young hunter with great stealth abilities.] Cerra: Kaiowas? We have not prepared for your return..... We thought you dead... We worked to shield ourselves but now I feel this may prove useful for your next offensive.... [The next offensive... In polite terms... Even now Kaiowas could feel the blood boiling inside of him, the pure intangible feeling of hatred and detest towards Pultra.... for Kaiowas this "offensive" could not come any quicker.... However Cerra, the old grey seer of the Ktulu, knew this and had attempted to avoid evoking this physical and mental mix of emotional passion and ruthless aggression. Trying to edge away from an early outburst Kaiowas slows his heart rate with a few Tribal methods he picked up years ago. Slowly and coldly he speaks to his old ally...] Kaiowas: Cerra... I know.... I know that this shall make it even harder for Pultra to survive.... This Tribe has sealed the fate of the tyrant.... Now all that is left to be done is for I to deliver the sentence.... I will take pride in ridding this world of the foul liar, I shall take great pleasure in tearing out his pulsating heart and stopping it with my own hands. I will choke him of his life's essence until he is on the brink of death and then push him over the precipice to death..... [Kaiowas trails off into silence with his vow of vengeance. After a few seconds the six foot four chesnut brown brazilian pushes back his long dreadlocks in an action of revitalisation and he looks out over the once dense forest. For a mile in every direction the Ktulu have felled trees to clear space for living and to use the logs as the main resource. Kaiowas sees the sweat and blood that has been put into this, the effort and the work that has gone towards it all. He sees that it is good and smiles. Not a nice smile, but more of a brooding thoughtful smile, meaning to say: I have what I need Pultra.... Your wait will not be long...] Ferrus: Tribal Warrior? I feel we need to explain things more thoroughly... [The ever present ever reliable Ferrus is a colossus. Standing in his full Tribal Warrior regalia with beaded necklace and his weapon of choice, a nasty stone maul, Ferrus towers over all the men present. Strong as both a bull and an ox combined, Ferrus could destroy a pillar with his bare hands. His loyalty to Kaiowas, his former training partner, is the one thing that brought him here, to follow the man who had so much honour so that maybe one day he himself would be honourable. Little did he realise that all the Ktulu respected the quiet giant for his patience and solid work ethic...] Kaiowas: Perhaps we could have a tour..... Fetch Lecood... if he followed us... [Lecood, the brazen old Hunter, and the fastest sixty year old this world must have ever seen. Now wizened and knowledgable, Lecood still activley hunted and trained the younger hunters. Sett himself had been the protegee of the old man for many a year, but no-one had been ever able to match or better the old man's battle tactics and outrageous lightning quick speed. A few flashes of sunlight from his huge customised wrist blades and you knew that his target had fallen to some sickening move to slice and puncture their skin. Kaiowas knew him as more than a hunter, nearly every Tribal Warrior who valued speed as a weapon had gone to him at one point to train and to learn the basics of his techniques. Kaiowas had always respected this man for his cunning and battle readiness. And so Kaiowas would call upon him to do him the honour of showing him the whole village. Kaiowas was also in need of a few pointers on speed in battle... he would soon hold them for very important.] Sett: Yes of course! Lecood was one of the most outspoken to follow your plans.... I will take you to him.... [Sett outstretches him arm in invitation to Kaiowas to step down the hillside first. Kaiowas acknowledges, and he and his Tribal Staff step cautiously down the hill. Kaiowas is still feeling the effects of not standing for a whole month and stumbles a little as he scales the familiar ground. Sett hurries down the green grassy slope hoping to appease his new leader by being efficient, but Kaiowas' eyes are elsewhere searching the village in awe of what has happened in just thirty days.] Kaiowas: Sett! Is this all to oppose Pultra? All because I told you to? [Sett stops and pauses thinking over the question...] Sett: No Kaiowas..... Not because you told us.... Because it's true. [Both men silently mull over what has been said and an unfamiliar voice breaks the tranquil moment.] Lecood: Massimillio! You old evil bastard! [Kaiowas looks up and sees the man, the old gray man. But he doesn't just see that, he sees the warrior phsyique, the cunning of the hunter. On his wrists he sees the long blades attached by metal bands that have caused many to fall to their demise. It is Lecood, and he is as he always was...] Kaiowas: Greetings Lecood.... I have no time for idle talk... I need your ear and your brain. Walk with me. [Lecood nods silently and both men look at Sett, who understandingly nods and turns leaving. Kaiowas returns his vision to Lecood and walks to him, when he reaches him he continues walking, pacing towards the outskirts of the whole encampment.... Lecood catches on and quickly follows. As they pace towards the outer wooden wall that rises six metres into the air they pass by many new buildings and many Tribe members. Kaiowas however seems disinterested with them right now... He seems determined and calculating as he speaks with Lecood.] Kaiowas: Lecood..... The time shortens until I must face him. I need your ear, to pour my questions into, and your brain to answer them. I need you to train me more, I need you to build my battle attitude, I need you..... Lecood... to help me defeat Pultra.... and the son of Satan, Donny Diamond. I know you would gladly be at my side when I place my swift and hard strikes to the points of the body you once told me most vulnerable, but I feel you can help me so much more here.... [Lecood raises his brazen hand to his roughened face feeling the weathered skin there give way to his fingers. The skin wrinkles and is loose, Lecood is old and he knows it. Breathing in he begins to speak slowly and confidently.] Lecood: Massimillio, I honour your wishes, but do you not think that two great fighters against one fallen Angel is a much more matched fight? I know you are stubborn and that this is your fight and that you must face Donny alone.... I know they are both your mortal enemies... but won't you at least need a distraction for Pultra.... [The men halt as they reach the perimeter fence and Kaiowas is about to continue the argument when he spots something... His face turns puzzled and he turns to Lecood.] Kaiowas: Do the Hunters return before nightfall in Iowa? [Looking confused Lecood replies.] Lecood: No... Never. Kaiowas: Arm yourself then! Someone is opening the gates! [It was true... A man stood at the gates tugging at the thick rope holding it shut. With one last pull the rope comes away and the huge wooden gates begin to creak and swing inwards opening the whole new village to attack. Kaiowas and Lecood are already covering the half kilometre between them and the gate as the first assailants pile through the widening gap of the gate. Kaiowas and Lecood ready their weapons as they up the pace towards the beseigers. They are spurred on by the sight of the enemy. Kaiowas brings his staff to bear while Lecood crosses his wrist blades attached to his lower arms in preparation for a headlong rush. As they get closer and closer more and more enemies plough through the now fully open gate. At this range Kaiowas can make out what they are. In total there were about twenty attackers all up of all sorts of different creatures. Huge ogre like beasts lead the charge, backed up by man sized black animals which looked as if they comprised of charred mud, a couple of huge spiders, the height of men, also scuttled into view. The man who opened the gate himself stepped aside as the creatures rushed past. Then strangely his body began to convulse and melt, everything becoming liquid and formless all at once. Then it all seemed to re-order itself to form a totally new being.... Within a few seconds a robed human with dark eyes stood laughing evilly...] Kaiowas: Pultra has sent these bastards to kill us off before we even get the chance to attack him! Raise the alarm! [As Kaiowas shouts this at a nearby sentry tower, the horns go up everywhere about the camp, each blast follwing another. The deep bellowing of the thick horns calls all those capable to arms. Soon this place would swarm with the greates warriors the Ktulu had to offer, but before the enemy could do some real damage to the camp, Kaiowas and Lecood had to hold the fort..... alone. Raising his staff preparing to attack Kaiowas bellows the Ktulu warcry at the top of his lungs, and a second later the sixty year old hunter Lecood screams it in unison. The beasts do not tire or stop in their tracks however and the two groups of warriors clash at full speed.] Kaiowas: OWWWWWWEERRRRRRRR!! Lecood: OWWWWWWEERRRRRRRR!! [A huge sickening thud is heard over the cries of battle as both men execute their charging blows on their opponents. Holding the staff in the middle with both his hands Kaiowas lands his first swift smooth double strikes on the pair of grotesque ogres pelting towards him. As the speed of the charge brings them nearly past him he jumps and strenghtens his grip, letting the staff thud into the thick skulls of both the huge beasts. One goes down screaming in agony as Kaiowas' strike brings forth a thick flow of blood. The other staggers back stunned having survived the near skull crushing blow. Lecood on the other hand had faired even better in his first flurry of fast blows. As he had his sharpened wrist blades close to his chest he unleashed them like slingshots as he met the enemy. Aimed at just the right level, he spit the throats of two attackers, and then spinning away again, arms outstretched he caught the dazed ogre under the chin with a swift deathblow. Quickly he removes the blade from the dead beast and the two men dig in for a lengthy and tough encounter.] Kaiowas: (over the cries of battle) Stay close! Do not let us be seperated! Back to back fighting! [Wordlessly Lecood acknowledged the order and he slid with every slice and stroke closer and closer to Kaiowas. Kaiowas was himself holding his own in the mass of the melee. The staff was a great weapon for such situations. Fending off blow after blow, then forcing forwards landing sickeningly hard strikes to temples and kneecaps. Skulls were being smashed, noses broken and legs were giving way all to Kaiowas' honed skill and bloodlust. The bloodlust was flowing now, really pulsating through his body. Pushing and forcing its way into every thought and every muscle in Kaiowas' body. Every strike had more and more spite and hatred behind it. He knew Pultra had sent this party to destroy him and he was going to send it back in pieces to put the fear of the Tribe into that foul beast! But right now it wasn't going too well. As planned Lecood had arrived at Kaiowas' shoulder and fought there, but both men were tiring, Lecood due to his age, and Kaiowas due to the fact that he had slept for a month. Cuts and bruises were appearing on both men as they battled relentlessly with the foes. Soon Lecood found it too hard to fight back to back and signaled to Kaiowas he was to move away. Mere seconds after Lecood took those few fatal steps away from Kaiowas, a sort of flare hit the mid afternoon blue sky. It flew up into the sky then hurtled back down to the ground. A cry went up... that of Lecood!] Lecood: Noo!! Ahhh! [Realising what had happened automatically Kaiowas was enraged! How dare they use magic to beat us in our own homes!] Kaiowas: Lecood no! [Everything was starting to get very very blurry for Kaiowas, blood was dripping into his eyes from a wound an inch above his eyebrow. It was impairing his sight and making him feel ill. His blows became more clumsy and slow, his movements staggered and fatigued. Kaiowas was losing it and was soon knocked to the floor by a huge spider. The creatures piled onto the prone Tribal Warrior who was barely hanging onto consciousness nevermind his life.] Kaiowas: NOOOO!!!!!!! [Suddenly a swoop is heard as another body enters the melee and mass of bodies and sweat. Blades strikes are heard and cries are gurgled from the cut throats of dying beasts. The new defender clears the ground around Kaiowas and through bleary eyes Kaiowas looks up from his prone position at his saviour.... His pointed ears and pale face matched with the long cloak and huge black collar stare down at him. He wears a type of protective mask, but you can still see the features clearly. Kaiowas exhales in shock.... It's a vampire....... in the daytime? Quickly looking away from the prone Kaiowas, the vampire notices something and fleets away as quickly as he arrived....] Ferrus: Tribal Warriors! CHARGE!!! [By great luck the rest of the Ktulu had organised themselves and caught up on the battle near the gate. The anger and aggression in Ferrus' voice gave Kaiowas hope. The crunch and thud of the two opposing groups clashing on the green pastures and the alien cries of weird beasts and spiders were soon to be heard. Suddenly an order was passed between the beasts and soon they were in full flight, now no longer more than six in number. Many of the Tribal Warriors raced after them making sure they left by the gate. Kaiowas was helped to a sitting position by Ferrus and opened his eyes. Kaiowas looked hazily towards the gates as he saw the enemy fleeing. Suddenly, unexpectedly a black shadow darted out from the other side of the gate. It was Rula! Kaiowas' old jaguar pet. The pet who was more loyal to her god than her master. Rula was more than a jaguar, she was Pultra's one link to Kaiowas, the one way to keep tracks of his once wayward servant, and now she served him against Kaiowas.] Kaiowas: Rula? [Growling at the pursuing Tribal Warriors, Rula pads forward allowing the beasts to escape. The Warriors stop in their tracks ready for the order to attack...] Ferrus: Attack the fiend! Kaiowas: Hold! Do not strike her down.... I will do that soon enough! Let them go.... [Retreating backwards the Warriors allow the beasts to escape along with their saviour Rula. As soon as they are gone they close the gates locking them tight... Kaiowas now struggles to his feet, staggers and is held up by Ferrus.] Kaiowas: Lecood? Where is Lecood? [Realising the truth and spotting the prone form of his old friend Kaiowas yells out in anguish and rushes over to the body, breaking free of Ferrus' caring grasp. Dropping to his knees when he finds the old man charred and burnt. Removing Lecood's wrist blades Kaiowas carefully sets them aside. Feeling desperately for a pulse Kaiowas searches for all the signs but on failing to he bursts into a fit of lamenting anger. Lecood is dead.... and Kaiowas' voice is heard all throughout the village.] _________________ DWO World Champion and Most Improved 2002, PTC no1 january 8th and 14th and no7 too!
