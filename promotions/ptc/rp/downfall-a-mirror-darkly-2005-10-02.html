Downfall: A Mirror Darkly

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-10-02 The constant throaty purr of Dawn’s Corvette Coup had been the only sound for several kilometers, and the almost silence was starting to grate on Alex’s nerves and patience. Since their trip to ‘Gother Thern Thou’ Dawn’s sullen conversation had evaporated into sullen silence, leaving Alex to try and coax some kind of conversation out of her moody companion. She had been trying for the better part of four hours. Folding her arms across her chest she grumped, letting out an annoyed sigh as she did. “You know if you’re angry with me, giving me the silent treatment isn’t going to help anything.” “I’m not angry with you.” Dawn’s reply sounded distant, her eyes not leaving the road ahead. “Then why the silent treatment? You’ve been totally quiet since we left that clothing store, and you didn’t even BUY that bustier the girl was pushing on you. What gives?” Dawn hesitated, searching for a way she could explain what she was feeling to Alex…and coming up with the same giant pile of nothing she’d been coming up with for the better part of four hours. “I’m just not feeling well.” It was a cop out response and they both knew it, but Dawn was hoping it would give her enough space to think about coming up with a real answer for herself…eventually. “You don’t seriously expect me to buy that do you?” So much for space to think. Dawn sighed, bringing one hand up to gently massage the bridge of her nose. “Look I’m not mad at you okay? I’m just feeling really off right now and I don’t really know how to explain exactly what I’m feeling to you. I’m not even sure I WANT to talk about what’s going through my head right now, some things are better off handled by yourself.” “You know, this is the kind of thing that makes it hard for you to talk to Lia.” Alex peered at Dawn intensely, hoping to draw some insight from her reaction. “If you’re always pulling into yourself when you have a problem, how can you expect yourself to be honest with anyone when the situation demands it?” Dawn growled in annoyance, jerking the Corvette to one side to pass a slow driver that had the gall to be in *her* lane. “I’m not pulling into myself, I just don’t know how to explain what I’m feeling alright? Hell I’m not even sure I know WHAT I’m feeling, never mind how to tell anyone else.” “Well how about you try me? You can’t figure out how to say it if you don’t even try.” Dawn reached up and undid the silver clasp on her black silk choker, tossing it to one side and taking her eyes off the road for a moment to look at Alex. The other girl couldn’t meet Dawn’s eyes however, her own eyes riveted to the smallish series of scars around Dawn’s pale neck. “I had to look in a mirror today…” Dawn said slowly, “…and I didn’t much like what I saw. So maybe I have a hard time talking to other people, but just maybe people don’t always want to hear what I have to say.” Alex brought her hands up to her mouth in shock, finally tearing her eyes away to look up at Dawn. “Did you…I mean was that…did you try to…” She fumbled with the words, unsure how exactly to voice the question on her mind. Thankfully it turned out she didn’t have to. “Yes.” Dawn turned back to the road, settling both hands shakily back on the steering wheel. “I thought that was it for me, that there was nothing left.” “But why?” Alex couldn’t keep the shock out of her voice, her eyes drifting once again to the scars on Dawn’s neck. “You’ve got everything in the world, what would make you think you didn’t?” “Everything?” Dawn’s voice was scornful, and she was unable to keep a small sneer from her face. “Sure I had the estate, my bike and a decent amount of money…but that was all of it, just material things. The only guy who had ever given a damn about me had just left me on public television for another woman, I had suffered two of the biggest failures of my career and I had nowhere else to go. I had no friends, no family and no hope.” Alex swallowed, mustering up the courage for her next question. “Was it really…really that bad? I mean…you’re still here right?” Dawn nodded tersely, “It seemed that bad at the time. I just didn’t realize until I had almost totally gone through with it how much I didn’t want to die just yet…although the blinding pain certainly helped that realization.” Alex was silent for a long moment, suddenly unsure of what to say. “Uhm…who did your boyfriend leave you for?” “Lia.” **************************************************************************************** The rest of the car ride home had been done in silence, with Dawn stubbornly refusing to answer anymore probing questions from Alex, eventually causing the younger girl to lapse into a sullen silence of her own. When the car finally came to a stop inside the Estate’s garage, it was with great relief that Dawn watched Alex unbuckle herself and disappear into the mudroom. She sighed and leaned forward to rest her head against the steering wheel, squeezing her eyes shut and gripping the wheel so tightly that the fiberglass shaft it was bolted to groaned in protest. After a moment of quiet contemplation she released the wheel and unbuckled her own seatbelt, slipping out of the car and slamming the door shut. Her reflection in the windshield gazed back up at her, reaching one self conscious hand up to where she’d replaced her choker, gently running her fingertips along the smooth surface. Tearing her eyes away from the reflection, Dawn swept past the front of the car and into the estate, shutting the door behind her and tossing her duster onto the nearest coat hook. A small mirror hung on the wall near the washing machine, giving Dawn pause at the haggard expression staring grimly back at her. Turning sharply on her heel she strode down the hallway, determined to reach the privacy of her own bedroom. Three small mirrors hung at varying intervals in the hallway, having been put there long ago by one of her agents to give the house “better Feng Shui” but for now she hurried past them, not wanting to catch sight of her own reflection. She knew the choker was a way of hiding a part of herself from the people around her, but what would anyone think of her if they discovered she’d tried to hang herself in the estates garage? She had a hard enough time dealing with sympathy, the outright pity she had seen in Alex’s eyes made her nearly sick to her stomach. Her reflection breezed by as she passed one of the estates many windows, a blur of black and blonde that she studiously ignored as she swept up the central staircase towards the single room fourth floor. She took each step two at a time, keeping her head low and not looking up for fear of seeing that same fear reflected back from her own eyes. “Where did I get so many goddamn mirrors and windows?” Dawn growled as her sock feet hit the third floor landing. “You’d think I was a ***ing narcissist the way this house is set up.” Her pace quickened again as she left the third floor behind, bounding impatiently up the steps with a growing sense of dread. Her reflection was showing her things she could not and did not want to see, like some deranged stalker it could follow her anywhere she dared to go, always peering back at her with those haunted eyes. She hit the fourth floor landing at a run, darting inside her room and slamming the door shut, quickly fumbling the lock mere moments after. She leaned back against the door, her heart pounding against her breast as she fought to catch her breath. “This is crazy…” She muttered breathlessly, trying to avoid the skin crawling sensation her reflection had given her. “I need some sleep; try to clear my head some.” Tugging her shirt off over her head she tossed it into the laundry basket, reaching back to unclip her bra before tossing it into the same location. She stepped lightly out of her cargo pants, leaving them in a pile on the floor as she walked to her dresser, pulling a simple black t-shirt out that she normally wore for sleeping. With a sigh she turned away from the dresser and headed to bed, stretching her arms up over her head…and then pausing, frozen in place as though her muscles had frozen of their own accord. Slowly her head swiveled to the right, emerald eyes wide with fear. From the floor length mirror in the corner of the room, her reflection stared back at her…but it wasn’t entirely her own. The woman in the mirror was strawberry blonde, where Dawn’s hair had been dyed black with only a few streaks of its natural colour still to be seen. Her neck was naked and unscarred, where Dawn’s was rough with hanging scars when it wasn’t hidden beneath a choker. Finally she was dressed in a manner that was both alien and terribly familiar; White wrestling boots with silver roses on the sides adorned her feet, attached to white kneepads by a pair of blue shin guards that would give limited protection to the wearer while kicking. A pair of blue shorts with white stripes covered her lower half, the letters WMW up the right thigh and RPW up the left. Her stomach was bare, her breasts and shoulders covered by a blue and white halter top like shirt, the final touches being a pair of fingerless blue gloves on her hands. Dawn stared at the strange reflection in shock, letting her arms slowly fall to her sides…and watching as the reflection did the same. She swallowed hard and advanced toward the mirror, fighting back an instinct to run as the reflection seemed to advance towards her. She reached out a shaking hand to the mirror, seeming to touch her fingertips to those of the woman in the mirror…before taking a step back and shaking her head in shock. When she looked up again it was all she could do not to scream; The reflection hadn’t stepped back, but rather when she had stepped back…it had stepped forward. Dawn took another step back and the reflection folded her arms under her breasts, shaking her head slowly in disappointment. “Since when did you turn into such a wilting rose?” Dawn’s jaw hit the floor in shock, and her mouth worked for several long moments before she could force any sound out. “You…you can talk?” The reflection rolled her eyes and smirked a little, shaking her head again. “Of course I can talk, I’ve never had a problem talking before.” “But you’ve never talked to me before…” The reflection looked down at herself and then back up at Dawn, raising one slender eyebrow. “Have I ever looked like this before either? You think that *maybe* things are just a little different right now?” It was Dawn’s turn to shake her head, although for her it was done out of sheer amazement. “Who ARE you?” It was the reflections turn to stare in amazement for a moment, her mouth hanging open just slightly. “You’re joking right? Let’s see…blue and white wrestling gear, check. Strawberry blonde hair and green eyes, check. WMW and RPW on my tights, check. Same general height and appearance…” The reflection cocked her head to the side, looking Dawn up and down with a critical eye. “…although I’d say I’m a fair sight thinner then you are. You’re still eating right aren’t you?” Dawn held up her hands in frustration, trying to stem the outpouring of sarcastic commentary. “Wait wait…so what you’re trying to say is that you’re…you’re…” “You.” Came the triumphant response, chased by a small smile. “Or me, more appropriately. Since I’m basically you…only about two years ago really, before you started really fucking things up.” Dawn bristled, folding her arms under her breasts and glaring. “Hold on just a minute here. Fucking things up? How exactly have I been fucking things up? At least I have a career in a good promotion, you’re still languishing in WMW hell.” The reflection glared back, putting her hands on her hips. “For one, I’m not anywhere; I’m just a representation of who you were. And for two, ouch, that was dirty pool.” Dawn snorted. “I’m just telling it like it is. If you’re really me, then you *know* that I…we…couldn’t wait to get out of there by the end of things.” “Of course I do, but this isn’t about me you know.” The reflection started towards Dawn, shaking her head for what seemed like the thirtieth time to Dawn. “This is about the fact that you’ve turned our life into a mockery of what it was.” Dawn stubbornly refused to budge, letting the reflection start a slow circle around her. “You still haven’t given me any evidence to back that up you know.” “Alright, how about the drugs? The alchohol? All it took was one little loss and you jumped right into the bottle, Christ it was like you were *looking* for an excuse.” “You know DAMN well that’s not true. It wasn’t just any loss you know, that was my first real chance to make it big.” “And you did! You blew past Tessa in the first round and made a very good first impression on PTC. So you lost in the second round, big deal, you lost to Karina Fucking Wolfenden. If everyone jumped into a bottle after they lost to her we’d have a HUGE collection of drunken wrestlers on our hands.” Dawn clenched her hands in anger, stubbornly sticking her chin out. “I made a mistake okay, it was a stupid thing to do and I know that.” “Oh no, that doesn’t even BEGIN to account for the stupidity. I don’t think I even NEED to remind you of what came next, the kind of things Marcus made you do.” “You bitch…” Dawn hissed, fighting back tears at the very thought. “And then you jumped right into bed with your “Hero” Corvus…brilliant.” The reflection rolled her eyes, tossing her hands in the air in frustration. “He loved me…” “No he didn’t, and you didn’t love him either. You needed a hero and he was there, you may have ‘thought’ you were in love, but then how did that turn out in the end?” “That wasn’t my fault…” Dawn forced out, her throat constricting, “He was an ass…” “Well yeah…but you slept with him.” Dawn spun on her heel and slapped her reflection hard across the cheek, snapping the other “girls” head to one side. The reflection paused, running her tongue over her teeth and turning her head slowly back to Dawn. “You don’t get to do that again.” Dawn growled, stepping up into her reflections face. “I’ll do whatever I want.” The reflection grinned, taking a step back and holding her hands out to the side. “And that’s the other thing, where the hell do you get off thinking you can just say anything you want to anyone at any time? You think nobody else around here matters but you?” “What the hell do you know?! I give people as much leeway as they give me.” “Oh bullshit.” The reflection flopped onto Dawn’s bed, sitting on the edge and resting her chin on her fists. “What about poor Emoticon? The guy is head over heels in love with you and you can’t even give him the time of day.” “I DON’T LOVE HIM!” Dawn snarled, taking a menacing step towards the bed. “Is it so fucking hard for people to accept that I don’t feel that way about him? Maybe we could be friends if he could accept that maybe it can’t BE any other way.” The reflection pursed her lips, looking as thought she was mulling over Dawn’s response. “Okay fair, I suppose I can see where you’re coming from on THAT one…but how does that excuse Lia?” “If you’re going to suggest I don’t really feel for her…” Dawn took another step, raising one threatening fist. “Whoa whoa…” The reflection held up her hands, “I’m not suggesting anything of the sort and you damn well know it…but we both know you’re not being entirely honest with her don’t we?” Dawn faltered, her arm hanging down to her side. “I…I’ve tried…” “Oh yeah, you’ve tried really hard. You know if you had told her you loved her, really loved her for who she was, and that you’d love her no matter what choice she made…maybe she wouldn’t have left. But all you could think about was winning that title…which you lost I might add, to a girl barely half your size.” Dawn hugged her arms to her torso, shivering as though suddenly cold. “I…I wanted to tell her, I wanted so badly…” The reflection cocked her head to one side. “So what stopped you? That fear of failure you’re so keenly aware of? Guess what? You failed.” Dawn flinched as though struck, taking a step back from the figure on the bed. The reflection wouldn’t stop though, pushing herself up from the bed. “You were so busy trying to keep your own goddamn feelings protected that you sacrificed hers just to protect you.” “I know, I KNOW!” Dawn screamed, grabbing the sides of her head and shaking it back and forth, she sank down to her knees, tears flowing down her face. “Then you need to STOP what you’re doing and fucking well FOCUS on the things that are important. You keep dwelling like this, fearing failure so goddamn much…and you’re going to MAKE yourself fail. And maybe next time you won’t get a second chance…” Dawn looked up at herself, her eyes brimming. “S-second chance?” “She’s coming back right? Well then you better make sure you don’t blow it this time.” “DAWN!” A familiar voice came from the other side of the door, startling Dawn and snapping her head to the side. “Y-yes?” “Dawn it’s me Alex, are you okay in there? I…I heard screaming?” Dawn looked around the darkened room, finding herself alone once again, the mirror in the corner showing nothing more then her shivering self in a heap on the floor. “I…I don’t know…” She whispered to herself. “Am I…okay?”
