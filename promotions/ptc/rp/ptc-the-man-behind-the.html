The terror lines from a topographic disaster on an otherwise pretty blond's face formed like fault lines on a new Earth. Craning her neck backward, she glanced at the cruel hand of scientific experimentation gone wrong that currently ravaged her city's streets. With mouth agape, she couldn't help but stare at the impending doom as she ran away as fast as her skirt would allow. When she woke up this morning for another regular day at the office...where Mel would probably attempt (and fail) yet again to pick up on her during the mid-morning water cooler break...she never could have imagined that THIS would be here to greet her. "RAWWWWWRGH!" An off-white venom-like spew fell from the monstrosity, covering a nearby lemon-yellow Hummer H3. The scent of the giant creature's emission was grossly familiar to the woman. Was that...was that dogwood blooms? The stench was overpowering, such to the point that the woman clutched her throat and began to gag. Caught in the sheer awesomeness of the thing, she once again opened her mouth to scream. It was futile. The pressure on her throat was too great, and not a sound came out. A giant rubbery arm reached out to grab her. However, instead of a hand, the end of the appendage featured a veiny blob of sticky mass. It was followed by another arm with an almost identical blob that proceeded to sandwich the innocent woman in between the loose-skinned orbs. The stretchy arms began to lift up the damsel in distress in order to gain a closer look. Since the creature had only one eye, its vision was impaired slightly and it just had to know the answer to its burning internal question of existance.
