The Lowest Point

20 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-20 A Month Ago I sat behind the counter of my new job… my new life… a video store clerk. I knew this isn't where I belonged but I simply could not hack it in Global Wrestling or Wrestling Midwest. My 1st real shot at the big time and I couldn't do it not even winning a single title in either promotion. It was most definitely time to walk away after the beatings I took at various well known Prime Time Central Superstars. Leaning back on the computer chair looking around not seeing a soul in the store I sigh and lose myself at what could have been. What could have been if I had any talent besides being the comic relief. Replaying various matches out in my head against the likes of Syberus, Justin Evers, Leinad Soma, Dawn McFarlane, and Shadow everyone considered an important of there respected promotions and all ending the same way….. My shoulder flat against the canvas after a fatal mistake. However, I have shown promise which is probably why I still have the itch. I had two very big fluke moments where I manage to get the better of AWS Man and Tessa Windsor but that was along time ago. I suddenly hear the bell ring and a customer walks inside the video store. A kid about the same age as I was when I started training. He's a regular here his name is Brad Miller… he thinks my name is Adam Lyons. I lower my head and sigh yet again as I remember all the free time I have had from being away from this sport that I love. So much free time that I used a name from a cartoon simply because I don't want to be recognized yet I haven't changed a thing about my appearance. I still have the same long black and red hair that I did when I was global and even wear some of my old wrestling t-shirts. Brad comes up to me and hands me the movie he wants. I grab the scanner and almost scan it however I see the tape is a porn. I glance up at Brad smirking softly. "You're not old enough for this?" Brad nods knowing he can't do this but will attempt to convince me anyway. "Come on man, Its just a porn what's the worst that could happen." "I could lose my job over it that's the worst that could happen. I'm not getting canned over your constant need to whack off." "Come on do you really want to work here all your life anyway" Brad says trying to convince me but not realizing how insulting that sounded. "Put it back and go get something more age appropriate for you. I'm sure you know where the kiddie section is you walked right past it." I say pissed off with the kid. The kid tilts his head for a moment and smiles brightly. "And if I don't you'll Freddy Kick me? Well, it'd probably be the 1st time that you won a match with that." I glared at the kid intensely wanting to knock his head off badly but not being able too because that's not a part of my job description anymore. I look away for a moment and take a deep breath. "Yes…. I was The Original Pranksta… but that was along time ago." He keeps smiling as he looks at me. "You really sucked back then and even more so now. Well, at the very least now you have a job you can't screw up." I shake my head surprised by the kid bluntness of what he thinks of me. "Kid, what was your childhood dream?" "I'm plan on going college about to go into business." He replies. "That's not what I asked. I asked what was your childhood dream." I respond with a now cold blank stare into his eyes. He lowers his head knowing where this is going "I wanted to be a wide out for the Detroit Lions." "I've lived my childhood dream. I dared to follow it and I didn't make it. I believe that's much more admirable then the guy who didn't even make an attempt." I respond in a monotone voice trying to convince myself as well. He looks back up at me. "Maybe so but I came to the realization a long time ago I couldn't do it. You….. You had nothing to fall back on. I'm a realist I'll end up telling dreamers like you what to do one day." He storms out immediately after words and I can do nothing but the door pushed open violently and closed equally so. I turn around and open the door behind me and lean in. "Hey Frank." Frank turns around in his computer chair and looks up at me. "Yes Fred?" I smile. "I quit…. I'm going to give my old job one last chance." "What makes you think this time will be any different?" He asks. "There is an old saying….. Even a blind squirrel can find a nut every once in awhile. I'd rather try one last time then spend the rest of my days wondering. The ink on my arm means nothing if I am content at staying at the lowest point." He raises his left forearm to show him the tattoo that reads hope.
