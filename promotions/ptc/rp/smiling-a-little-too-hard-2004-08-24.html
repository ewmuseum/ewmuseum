Smiling a little too hard?

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 OOC: HOLY FUCK! I made it with ONE fucking minute to spare. It's midnight on the dot! [13 - Truths and Consequences - “Smiling a little TOO much!” ] Breathe in. Breathe out. Inhale. Exhale. Is this some kind of a joke? Is this what our lives are supposed to be? A bunch of bags of water sent to wander aimlessly around the world damned to repeat this stupid process over and over again? If there is some kind of higher being... some kind of God... why’d he make us have to breathe? Why’d he make us have to see, and feel, and see? Why’d make our hearts have to pump? why did he make us have hair? And what about farting? What’s the deal with that? How does expelling methane gas into the world fit into the glorious master plan? Breathe in. Breathe out. Inhale. Exhale. This same meaningless process over and over again is what we have to experience from our sunrise to our sunset. And the only time you get to stop is when you’re dead. Isn’t that grand? I guess that’s why I don’t get religion. When you’re sitting atop the roof of your apartment building in the rain, with your feet dangling over the edge wondering why you have a tail and claws, it’s kinda hard to believe in this alleged “Man Upstairs.” Caught your attention, have I? You either think I’m crazy or you’re scared of me now. Heh ... I don’t really care which it is, so don’t bother to fill me in. Yes, I have a tail. No I’m not crazy, and yes, you probably should be afraid of me. The real kicker, though? Not only am I a human being, but a doctor at that! No, not a medical doctor you yuppies – a master of chemistry and biology. I’m a bit of a scientist, you see... Let me tell you something. If you’re a teenager reading this, and you ever have your consoler give you the “Popularity isn’t everything, just keep your grades up and you’ll have a great life” speech, disregard what that bitch says. That’s all bullshit. Bullshit, I tell you. Even with a 3.0 from high school through college, the only thing I ever managed to get based on my brilliance was some lousy job at that crackerjack factory they dare to call a laboratory downtown. GoodAid Pharmaceuticals. I haven’t introduced myself properly, have I? My name is Kasidy Drake. I am the developer and the only user of the Prometheus Serum – a formula which I devised with the aim of it becoming the world’s first one hundred percent healthy and side effect free steroid. If successful, I could have made millions. Maybe even billions. But like I said, I’m sitting atop a roof with a tail in the rain. Obviously it didn’t work. Long story short, I’ve yet to be able to successfully crunch the numbers. Not yet anyway. Why do I keep doing this to myself? I could have easily used rats as the test subject. Could have used some willing test subject who wanted a few bucks thrown his way... and yet I keep putting myself at risk. What do I hope to gain by putting my existence on the line every time I pump my veins full of this juice? Why can’t I just be happy with the way I am? That’s a stupid question to ask myself. Of course I’m not going to be happy with myself. In my normal form I’m a scrawny little nerd – bald, stick-figure like, and practically wearing a sign on my forehead reading: “Beat the fuck out of me please!” For so long I’ve been weak. This... serum, however... it gives me a chance to be strong. I cannot count the amount of tragedies in my life that could have been avoided if I weren’t so weak... if I weren’t so passive ... if I didn’t let society shove itself up my ass. There are so many people I could have saved ... so many crying nights that could have been avoided... This serum. It’s killing me. Not just figuratively, but literally. With every injection, I become a little bit stronger, but once it’s gone I’m that much weaker. It’s like being on the ground and gaining wings to touch heaven .... and then falling back down. Falling. Falling. Straight to hell. But nobody can bully me around when I’m like this ... nobody can tell me that I’m not strong enough to take control of my life. Nobody will control me ever again. If I could just get this formula right ... then.... then I’d be strong enough. But what’s the point, self? The damage has already been done to your life. Everything that made you happy has been stolen away from you long before you started doing this to yourself ... lives have been lost ... the people who really cared about you have been turned into whispers and memories. You’re all alone in the world. Sitting on a rooftop in the rain... Is it really too late self? Is it really too late to save them? To save her? If there’s only a tiny miniscule possibility that you can save them ... make them, and yourself truly happy again ... if you can bring the one person that you love back ... then isn’t it worth all of this? And who are we trying to save, self? Stupid question again, self. Every time I close my eyes I see her. She’s tall. Petite. Long, celestially-soft dark brown hair. Troubled but warm brown eyes that tell a sad story. And a smile. That warm, white smile that somehow didn’t fit her. Didn’t fit her because it was happy, and the rest of her was sad. She put up a brave front, but even though her smile could tell tales, her eyes couldn’t. I have those same brown eyes, anyway – I know how they work. I know that they can’t tell lies. Everyday that I looked up into her beautiful face, it was like watching an angel cry. That’s why I tried to never look her in the eyes. Tried to keep my eyes locked on that “Don’t worry, I’m happy” smile, but every once in a while they’d creep up to that “Please save me” glint in those abysses of eyes. I loved her. She never would have gotten together with him if it hadn’t been for me. Butch, I mean. That man. That bastard. That overgrown snake. I still remember him. I still remember the first day that I met him. I couldn’t have been a day over six. My mind was still filled with dreams, instead of the demons that haunt me now. She and Butch were the exact same height: six feet on the dot. They stood toe to toe, and yet... somehow he made himself so much stronger than her... He broke her. Shattered her soul. Took her away from me... My mother. My mother. My. Mother. Back then... I was too weak. I couldn’t save her from him. I couldn’t save him from himself. I just had to sit and watch. And get hurt. Again. And. Again. So tell me self... if this formula means we can save them... save her at least... then isn’t it worth this? Maybe self. Maybe. It stopped raining. I was ... myself again. Weak. Feeble. I grabbed my pencil from my pocket and went back inside. Right back to re-writing this formula... ...so that I can be strong. Again.
