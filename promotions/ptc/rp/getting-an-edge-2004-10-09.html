Getting an Edge

9 October 2004

Date: 2004-10-09 ‘Only a few days to go…’ After refocusing his mind and finding new inspiration to continue on with the tournament, Brian Calvin began to go through his usual preparations for the flight out of Denver. He was extremely superstitious when packing his clothes and his other necessities; everything always had to be in the EXACT same place each time so he would have no problems at hotel getting ready for his pre-match ritual. This ritual consisted of: taking a cold shower, shaving and brushing his teeth, icing his knees and any other sore spots he might have been suffering from for at LEAST a half an hour, changing into his sweats, ordering room service off of the hotel menu, and going to bed. He did the same thing, same order, before every match. He was always alone; not even his wife accompanied him on the match. He never watched television, never read a book or magazine, and never talked on the telephone. His room was completely silent, and Brian simply used the time to think about his match. No one really knew exactly why Brian stuck with this peculiar system. Brian himself didn’t really know, but it was his system, and it seemed to work well enough. ‘Something is missing…’ But even though Brian’s over-anal methods had been working for him thus far, he couldn’t help but feeling that his other competitors were getting a bit of an edge. Although he was accomplishing his desired goal using his superstitious system, which was basically just finding a way to remain focused on his opponent, Brian would have welcomed something or someone that would help him pass the time a little bit during the night. Television and reading were out of the question; he would never be focused or mentally prepared if he was glued to a TV screen or had his head in some magazine. He would have enjoyed some sort of companionship; a manager of some sort, maybe, someone who he could have hung out with and talked about his match or just wrestling in general. His wife was definitely out of the question; she could never focus on anything for more then a few minutes, let alone focus on wrestling. A lot of the other wrestlers used managers, and Brian was beginning to think that it wasn’t such a bad idea. He could have used a great wrestling mind to aid him in the tournament; someone to give him a second perspective on his opponent and his approach. Of course, he would never be able to find himself a capable enough manager on such short notice, so the idea would have to wait at least a few more weeks. Ring! Ring! Brian threw his suitcase onto the bed and hurried towards the living room to answer his telephone. He had been expecting a phone call from his wife, Rachel, at anytime; when she visited her parents in Colorado Springs she often gave him a phone call around 9:00, just before she was going to head off to bed. He picked up the phone with a smile on his face, anxious to hear his wife’s familiar, sweet, soothing voice on the end of the other line. “Hello?” Brian answered. “Brian!? Brian, please, you’ll never guess what happened… Oh god!” He heard Rachel break down into sobs. Brian’s face immediately went white. His first thoughts went to his kids; he couldn’t imagine his wife being hysterical like that if the problem wasn’t something with his two boys, Ryan or Cade… He took a deep breath, trying to calm himself down. His heart was going a million miles a minute as he listened to the sobbing voice of his wife on the other end of the line, but he didn’t want to panic. “Rachel, calm down.” He tried to be strong with his tone. “What’s the matter? Rachel?” Her sobbing continued. “Rachel? Is… Is it the kids? What happened? Talk to me, Rachel!” Finally his wife stopped her sobbing, and he heard her blow her nose on the other end of the line. “It’s not the kids, Brian, it’s my parents! My mom… has been paying some guy for sex… she’s been cheating on my dad for the past three week with some young punk from out of town…” She blew her nose again. “Dad found out last night when he answered the phone for my mom, and it was that guy… Oh Brian, it’s horrible…” Brian sighed, completely relieved. ‘Thank God…’ It wasn’t that Brian wasn’t upset by the news, because he was, but this was much less the shock that he had been preparing himself for. Rachel loved her parents dearly, Brian knew that, but he also wasn’t overwhelmingly surprised. A major reason that Brian had avoided his own parents for twenty years was because they didn’t approve of his marriage to Rachel, an act that had greatly angered him. Their reason for disapproval was because Rachel’s parents were, well… they were basically known as the town scum around the town where Brian and Rachel grew up. It was a fair assessment to make, unfortunately. Rachel’s father had been arrested multiple times for crimes as extreme as breaking and entering to stuff as minor as stealing a toothbrush from a store. And Rachel’s mother was known, simply enough, as the town whore. Of course, Rachel was completely innocent; by all means she was a diamond in the rough. It was a miracle, at least to Brian, that she had survived her horrible home life and turned out as the sweet angel that she was. Rachel’s own weakness was her blind eye she turned when it came to her parents. Every time her dad got thrown back in jail she would bail him out, convinced when he told her that he was going to change. And every time that she heard a rumor that her mom was sleeping with this or that guy she would vehemently defend her mom’s honor. Brian had always wanted to tell his wife that it would have been a better idea just to cut her parents lose for good, but he knew she never would. This was why the news she was relaying to Brian at the moment, including her reaction, really didn’t surprise him all that much. He felt badly, but that was only because he knew all the anguish and despair Rachel was going to go through trying to convince herself that her mom was perfect and never would do just a horrible thing to her dad. “Dad left the house this morning in a fit… The kids are all worked up, Brian, they don’t know what’s going on! Mom claims the guy tricked her into giving him money, that she really just felt sorry for him, but I don’t know what to think…” She whimpered helplessly. Brian shook his head. “What can I do, Rachel?” He asked. “Would you just come down here and talk to this guy, Brian? I know you don’t think highly of my parents, not many people do… But please, for my sake, come down and talk to the guy mom’s been… been cheating with. See if you can find out what’s really been going on…” He sighed inwardly. This was not what he had intended to be doing just a few days before the Round Three was supposed to begin. That was for DAMN sure. Rachel’s mom’s house was only about an hour and a half away, but still, he needed to focus on other things. Somehow he had to figure out a way to say no… “Rachel, I really want to help out you and your mom, but I’ve still got to pack. My plane leaves tomorrow, I’m only like half ready…” Rachel whimpered, “I don’t want to have to beg you, Brian, but I need this. I need to find out if my mom’s been telling the truth. She is still claiming that this guy tricked her into having sex with him and to lend him money … She’s saying she was manipulated, and I just want to know if she’s lying or not.” ‘Some young guy tricked Rachel’s sixty-four year old mother into having sex with him, AND convinced her to pay him afterwards… Right. A likely story,’ he thought to himself. She continued, “I know you don’t have high thoughts of my mom and dad, but they’re still my parents, Brian. I can’t change that fact. I just need to know…. For my own sanity.” Brian began to rub his temples, sighing to himself. This obviously was a big deal to Rachel, and she had been there for him a million times when he was in need. Plus, maybe if he went down to Colorado Springs, checked things over, and finally found out that Rachel’s mom was the lying whore he thought she was, maybe then his wife would finally be able to come to terms with her parents. Maybe then she’d find the strength to finally be able to cut them loose. “Okay, Rachel, I’ll be there as soon as I can.” ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The trip down to Colorado Springs was easier then expected, and by the time he had gotten there his wife had cooled down quite a bit. Rachel’s mother was still stubbornly sticking to her story that she had been manipulated by the guy, and then gave him three hundred dollars because she felt ‘sorry’ for him. What was a little more likely was that the guy had probably offered to sleep with Rachel’s mom if she gave him three hundred dollars, and that was what Brian was going to go find out. Rachel had convinced her mom to give Brian the address to the place where Brian could find the guy, and then asked for a description. His mother-in-law had used these characteristics: short, skinny, black-hair, dark complexion, and Hispanic. Before he decided to jump back into the car to go see this guy, he pulled his wife off to the side to double check exactly what it was that she wanted him to find out. She said, “Get the story. Just find out what really happened. My mom’s probably bullshitting me… But I need to know. There’s a pretty big difference between paying a male whore for sex or being manipulated into the act. Neither is right, but I have to know the truth.” He turned to head towards the door, and Rachel grabbed his arm. “Oh, and Brian? I don’t like to encourage violence, but I wouldn’t really be upset if you kicked this guy’s ass.” ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ And so Brian was off. The address really wasn’t very difficult to find; apparently the guy lived in a run down house about fifteen minutes out of town. Brian was going about fifteen over the speed limit all the way there, because he was in a pretty big rush. He wanted to get back to his home as quickly as possible so he could finish packing and begin focusing on his Round Three opponent, Craig Goat. Running around doing chores for his wife really didn’t appeal to him at the moment. Eventually Brian finally did reach the house, and his mother-in-law wasn’t lying when she said the guy lived in a run down house. Hell, a more appropriate term for the place would have been to call it a run down ‘shack’. The siding on the house was cracking and obviously uncared for. There were shutters on the windows, but quite a few were missing and some looked like they were hanging by a thread. A porch led up to the main entrance of the ‘shack’, but the front door was completely ripped off and was lying in the middle of the yard. Only a screen door remained on the house, but that was half open anyway. As Brian began to walk up the stairs to the porch he could feel the rotten wood groaning under his body weight. Brian pulled open the screen door and stepped inside, cautiously. The inside was definitely not in any better shape; the carpeted floor was a complete mess, stained black in multiple spots. The drywall in the house was cracking BIG time, and in a few spots there were even complete holes. Dirt littered the floor, along with pizza boxes, porno magazines, and really old looking food. There was a single tattered couch in the corner, and it was facing a small television. When Brian peered closer he noticed the television had been kicked in. The house in general was tiny, and Brian didn’t understand where the guy he was looking for could have been. From the looks of the outside, Brian didn’t even think there was another room in the house. Cough, cough. Brian turned towards the sound. It was coming from the couch in the corner, but that couldn’t be right, there wasn’t anything there except for the couch. It was then he noticed that there was a small space between the wall and the furniture. He pushed his way through the garbage dump, making a beeline for the couch. He eventually reached the stained, sagging piece of furniture, and he bent over and pulled it away from the wall. What he saw made his draw drop. Sleeping on the floor with his shirt off and a pair of old jeans on, curled up next to the wall, was pro-wrestling sensation Gerrard Heart. Heart had had a popular following in the wrestling business but had been conspicuously absent from the ring for the past three years. Gerrard had wrestled in numerous federations; including the World Wrestling Alliance, Brian’s own company, but he had mysteriously fallen off the map about three years ago in the prime of his career. Finding him sleeping in a shit-hole like this was more then a surprise to Brian. Brian stepped forward and nudged the sleeping Mexican with his foot. Heart stirred, but he didn’t wake up. Brian stepped back and stared, forgetting the reason that he had come to the house in the first place in his surprise. Heart was a legend among Mexican high-flyers; his name had been gold in the business since he had first entered a ring. He tried to remember where he had heard Heart was wrestling last; he believed it was a small company based in New York, if his memory was correct. Wherever he had been, Heart had always been VERY popular with the fans. He had been most famous as a wrestler for his high-flying style, his insane antics, and his sexual fascination with – Ah, shit. His sexual fascination with the elderly. Now Brian remembered the reason he was there. He gave Gerrard Heart another kick to the ribs, this one a bit harder. The sleepy Mexican coughed again and rolled over onto his side, finally opening his eyes. “Not now, muchacho…” he mumbled stupidly. “We don’t need no stinkin’ batches…” He rolled back over. “Get up, Heart!” Brian yelled, leaning down and beginning to shake him out of his sleep. Finally Gerrard sat up, rubbing his eyes and glaring daggers back at Brian. “What do you want, amigo? I already paid your boss his damn money. Why are you hombres still bothering me?” Heart began to scratch at his ass, not fully woken up yet even thought it was 1:00 in the afternoon. Brian took a long look at Gerrard. “Tell me this: why is Gerrard Heart, one of the greatest Mexican wrestlers our era, living in a damn shack in Colorado Springs and having sex with my mother-in-law?” Gerrard stared back at Brian, searching his eyes. He then began to grin. “Hey man, pussy is pussy. I give the ladies what they ask for!” The Masked Mexican scrambled to his feet and hopped over the couch. “Now, if you’ll excuse me, I have to go drain the main vein.” He tip-toed to the bathroom and shut the door. Brian followed him, not ready to lay off the questions. “What are you doing here, Heart? Why aren’t you still wrestling?” Brian shouted through the closed door. Gerrard answered back, “I owed some people some money, hot shot. Not everyone has a fairy tale life like you, Calvin.” Heart knew who Brian Calvin was? But how? “How do you know who I am?” Brian fired. “Take it easy, my man,” Gerrard shouted back. “I haven’t been living under a rock for the past three years.” He paused. “Well, actually, I kind of have been. But I still manage to get out and check up on the business every once and awhile. I hear you’ve been doing well in the WWA, eh? Not bad.” Brian heard a flush and Gerrard stepped out from the bathroom, zipping up his pants. Brian asked, “You’re still following the WWA?” Gerrard nodded. “Of course I have been. I wasn’t planning on leaving the business forever, you know. Just until I got back on my feet.” He wiped his hands on his jeans. “I’ve recently been looking for a way back into the business. Been saving up money, biding my time a little until I can get back to the top.” He pointed to the dresser where a pile of money was carelessly being kept on top, out in the open. “By whoring yourself to old women?” Brian asked sarcastically. Gerrard became defensive. “Hey, that’s like killing two birds with a stone, man. I get satisfaction AND I get paid. What’s better then that?” Brian sighed and shook his head. He didn’t know why he was going to ask this, but he was. Gerrard could have been the great wrestling mind that he might need as a manager for the rest of the tournament. “Listen, Gerrard. I may have a way for you to get back into the business. Come with me back to my house in Denver. I’ll get you some decent clothes, and you can fly out with me tomorrow to Washington, D.C. I’m going to be wrestling in the Gtt4 tournament, and I could use a capable manager to help me out.” Brian brought up his hands, not wanting to offend him by using the term ‘manager’. A lot of wrestlers didn’t like that idea. “Just until you get your name back out there, Gerrard. Very temporary, just to help me out with the rest of the tournament. Then you can go out on your own again.” Gerrard glared up at Brian again, as if searching to find out if Brian was being serious or not. But Brian was being VERY serious. He had given it careful consideration, and he thought that Gerrard could really help him out in that way. Brian had wanted a person who knew a lot about pro wrestling to accompany him for the rest of the tournament; someone who he could talk to during his pre-night routines, someone to give him a second view on an opponent, someone who could give him advice on a style he should wrestle on a particular night. There weren’t many other capable people that weren’t already working who could give a good second perspective like Gerrard Heart could. That was for sure. Gerrard asked, a bit sheepishly, “But what about the whole, you know, me-screwing-your-mother-in-law thing…” Brian shook his head, “I’m not mad. My wife is upset, but I just think it’s kind of disgusting.” He smiles. “You’d have to hide in the car when I’m at her mom’s house, but my wife’s not coming back to Denver for a few more days. We’ll be able to get you a plane ticket and then take off; she’ll never know you were the one who violated her, umm… yeah.” Gerrard grinned, and he extended his hand. “I like it, Calvin! You won’t regret this, buddy. It’s a deal!” Brian gratefully shook it, “Awesome, man. I could really use another perspective. Let’s get going, and I’ll fill you in on my opponent in the tournament on the way to Denver.” The masked Mexican nodded. “Great! Let me just go get the money I made whoring myself out to Denver’s elderly.” He went back to his dresser, and as he walked he started singing softly. “Guess who’s back… back again… G-Rod’s back… Tell a friend…” Brian nodded approvingly and began to walk to the car, thinking to himself. The only thing he really had left to worry about was his wife and her mom. He had decided that he would try to ease his wife’s pain and lie; he’d tell her that her mom really didn’t pay for sex with Gerrard, no matter how much he disliked his mother-in-law. Maybe that kind gesture would show Rachel’s mom that she really needed to pull herself together and starting acting halfway decent. After that he would sneak Gerrard back home with him, finish packing his things, and get ready for the tournament. ‘What luck…’ Brian really had gotten lucky to find Gerrard Heart. No longer would the other wrestlers in the tournament have an edge over him. He now had own of the best wrestling minds to give him a second perspective on his future opponents and of the tournament in general, someone to really aid him, which he would need if he succeeded to the higher rounds. Finally, it was Brian who had the advantage! ‘Of course, there was still the fact that Gerrard’s sexual preference happened to be women in their late sixties… Yeah… They were going to have to work on that.
