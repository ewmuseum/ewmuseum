Mistaken Identity

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 “It’s a conspiracy.” “They are trying to hold us back, it’s a damn conspiracy.” “We have to defeat this conspiracy again.” Those were words that used to come out of Bailey’s and Danyon’s, The New Royal Legion, mouths quite often back in the day but they have not been heard in quite some time from the two of them. Instead, Bailey and Danyon have enjoyed relative quiet period in regards to the conspiracy and the conspirators. However, with their biggest match to date in the PTC quickly approaching and the return of Webster as the Commissioner of XUW (who they originally believed was in on the conspiracy the whole time), one has to wonder just how long until the conspiracy against the legendary New Royal Legion returns……. ** The scene opens up at what has now become a local landmark in downtown XUWville, the Howard Johnson Hotel. The hotel has become infamous in the XUW circle because it is the home to the New Royal Legion and their cult like fans, the Legionnaires. Regularly, the Legionnaires can be found perched out front of the hotel always having a party or waiting to catch a glimpse of their heroes Bailey and Danyon. Now, not all one million of the Legionnaires are stationed in the hotel parking lot. (Correction, there are actually one million and one Legionnaires in the world but only one million of them are humans as the other one is a penguin.) Still, there are a large number of them that call the hotel parking lot home but given that the Legionnaires are a truly global cult they live all over the world. Today, the Legionnaires are outside enjoying the summer sun and lounging beside the pool that is in the parking lot. They have good reason to be cheerful today as things with the New Royal Legion are going well. Bailey is in the semi finals of the World Title Tournament in XUW and will be facing off against Dante in a weeks time to get into the final later that nite. Also, the New Royal Legion will be fighting soon on the PTC PPV Survival against the Shadow Riders from LOW for the PTC Tag Titles. The New Royal Legion have been waiting months to finally get a shot at the PTC Tag Titles. They were crowned the #1 contender’s to the titles back in May but have yet to fight for the titles as the champions at the time, the Souls of the Black Lotus dropped the belt which caused a bit of turmoil in the tag division scene. But now, finally, the New Royal Legion will be getting their chance to fight for the titles and crown themselves champions of the PTC. The scene switches from the outside of the Howard Johnson Hotel to the inside of the aptly New Royal Legion Suite which is located on the 15th floor of the hotel. This is the home of the New Royal Legion as well as the very first Legionnaire, Larry the Legionnaire. Also known to stay in the room a few times is the former GIWF champion and former owner of UWF, The Bastard Al Crud as Al is an honourary Legionnaire. However, today it is only the New Royal Legion themselves that are in the hotel suite. Bailey is standing near the fridge in the kitchen area of the suite while Danyon is standing over on the balcony of the room. Bailey opens up the fridge door and looks inside the fridge. He shifts a few beers bottles, most of which are Al Crud’s and sees a bottle of water in the back. He pulls it out and opens it up and takes a quick drink. He looks back in the fridge and sees another bottle of water there too. Bailey: Hey Danyon, do you want some water. I actually found two of them in the back of the fridge behind Al’s beer. Danyon walks back from the balcony. Danyon: Yeah, I’ll have one. Bailey reaches back in and grabs the other bottle of water and tosses it towards Danyon and he catches it. Danyon looks at the bottle before opening it and taking a swig. Danyon: I wonder how long this has been in the fridge. Bailey: I don’t know and it’s not as if water can go bad, so it shouldn’t really matter. Danyon: Water can go bad. I mean there is an expiry date on these bottles why else would they put it on if it couldn’t go bad? Bailey: But how does water go bad? Danyon: It could get stale I suppose. Bailey: Stale water? Danyon: Yeah, stale water. That’s when it can get diseases in it and you have to be careful to drink it. You know like malaria………..and heh probably ironically if we caught it legionnaire’s disease. Yes, it’s true that New Royal Legion’s cult following call themselves the Legionnaires, which also happens to be bacteria that can be found in stale water and can cause death in the some circumstances. However, obviously they call themselves the Legionnaires because of the Legion in the name and nothing to do with the disease. Bailey: That might be true Danyon but how would the water get stale and get those diseases if the bottle is never opened? Danyon: Hmmm…..that’s a good question. Bailey: See, that’s my point, why does bottled water have expiry dates on it. Danyon: I guess it can be one of those unsolved mysteries….. Just then the door opens up and in walks Larry the Legionnaire with his sidekick, Stripes the Penguin. Yes, that’s right, a penguin. Why a penguin? Well, a few months ago Larry inexplicably decided that he was from Antarctica and that he was also a professional wrestler, neither of which is true. Larry is actually from Toronto and has been the New Royal Legion’s lawyer in the past. He has wrestled a few matches in the past but he was forced to wrestle in most of those occasions by either Bailey or Danyon so he’s still not a real professional wrestler. Still, that has not stopped him from he has been claiming lately that he is the best wrestler ever from Antarctica. Strangely enough, since he is the only wrestler ever to claim he was from Antarctica he is the best wrestler from Antarctica even with his dismal in ring career record. Anyways, somewhere along the way, Larry decided that he needed a fellow Antarctican to accompany him wherever he went so he could feel more like at “home”, so he bought a penguin because most native Antarctican’s are animals not humans. And owning a penguin is a much more sensible than say a leopard seal or at least it is to Larry. Nevertheless, Larry is standing in the doorway without his normal parka and mittens and toque on – those of course being the normal clothing that an Antarctican wears. He is wearing just plain old khakis and a sweater. Stripes the Penguin is obviously not wearing any clothing because he is a penguin. Bailey: Whoa, Larry, where is the parka today? Larry: It’s way too hot out there for that parka. It must be about 25 degrees Celsius out there today. I really can’t stand that heat. Give me negative 30 degrees and that’s more like it. The colder the better is what I say. Danyon: It’s about time that you came to your senses and realize that you probably shouldn’t be wearing all that clothing in the warm weather. Larry: But that’s what all Antarctican’s wear. It’s our national clothing. I’m proud of my culture and my heritage. Many other cultures wear traditional clothing, so why can’t I? Bailey: Yes, many other cultures do wear traditional clothing but I doubt you’d see someone from an African tribe wearing their traditional clothing in Antarctica. They’d freeze to death if they did. Larry: So? I’m making sure I’m not going to freeze to death here in XUWville. Bailey: But you could sweat to death with all that clothing on. Larry: That’s why I took that parka off. I was finding it a bit too warm with it on. Sheesh. Larry walks over to the fridge and opens the door as Stripes the Penguin follows him to the fridge. Larry reaches in and pulls out a small fish and tosses it towards Stripes who snatches it in the air. Larry takes a step towards Stripes and pats him on the head, congratulating him on catching the fish. Larry: That’s my penguin. Good work. Larry looks up at Bailey and Danyon. Larry: Oh, speaking of my parka, I remember I have something in the pocket I had been meaning to ask you guys about. Danyon: What’s that? Larry: It had to deal with the PTC PPV Survival. I thought you said you were wrestling on that PPV. Bailey and Danyon are confused by Larry’s statement because as far as they know they are wrestling on the PPV. Danyon: We are on the PPV Larry. What are you talking about? Larry: Oh. Well, I was looking at a flyer the other day for the card that they were handing out over at the XUW Arena and it didn’t have you guys on the official card. Danyon: Huh? What are you going on about? I think wearing that parka all day long in that heat finally affected your brain as we are on that PPV. Larry: I know that. I thought that too. But then I saw the official card on a pamphlet and then I saw the official press release and it didn’t have the New Royal Legion listed on the card. Bailey: What the hell? Show me that flyer. Larry: Okay, just a sec. I have to run down to the 14th floor to Stripes’ room as that is where my parka is. Bailey: Fine, hurry up and go and get it. Larry turns and leaves the room and goes down to the 14th floor which is where Stripes the Penguin’s room is. Yes, Stripes the Penguin has his own room in the hotel. They had a room renovated on the 14th floor so it resembles what life is like on Antarctica for him. Every now and then Larry the Legionnaire, believing he is from Antarctica, likes to stay in the room so he can feel closer to “home”. Meanwhile as Larry is out of the room, Bailey and Danyon (and Stripes as well) stand there in silence waiting for Larry to come back. Bailey looks over at Danyon who just kind of shrugs as if to say he has no clue what is going on. Bailey looks back over at Stripes the Penguin who looks at Bailey very curiously. Stripes sticks his wing out and up in the air as if to give Bailey a high five. Bailey looks over at Danyon a bit confused as to why a penguin would give him a high five but Danyon just motions him to do it. Bailey reluctantly, as he hasn’t really taken to Stripes as many of the others have, gives him a high five. A smirk comes over Danyon’s face as Stripes walks over to him and gives him a high five as well. Stripes then waddles back towards the kitchen area and waits by the fridge door. Just then Larry flies back through the front doors with a piece of paper in his hand. Bailey: That was quick enough. Larry: Well, I’m pretty good at climbing stairs from all the walking on snowshoes down in Antarctica. Anyways, here’s that pamphlet I was telling you about. See look at the first match on the card. Bailey walks over and grabs the pamphlet from Larry’s hand. He reads it over and shakes his head…… Bailey: Well, I’ll be damned……. Danyon: What do you mean, he’s right? Bailey hands the pamphlet to Danyon. Danyon reads it over and shakes his head as well. Danyon: The New Foreign Legion of XUW vs. The Shadow Riders of LOW for the Tag Team Titles. The New Foreign Legion? Foreign? What the hell is going on here? Bailey: I am not 100% positive but do you know what I think this means? Larry: This means that people are going to call you guys the NFL now for short instead of the NRL and then you’ll be mistaken for the National Football League. Bailey: No, I somehow don’t think we’ll be mistaken for the National Football League………. Just then there was a knock on the door to the suite. Bailey walks over and opens up the door and standing there are two overly large men with football jerseys on. Dude #1: Hey man, is this the headquarters of the NFL? Is the commissioner here? Bailey: Ummm…..no this is not the headquarters of the NFL. This is the home of XUW’s NRL. The New Royal Legion, the greatest tag team of all time. This is not the NFL. Dude #2: But like why is there a referee over near that fridge? The dude points towards the fridge located in the room. Bailey: A referee near the fridge? What are you talking about? Bailey turns around looks in the direction of the fridge and he sees Stripes the Penguin standing there. Bailey looks back at the two dudes standing in the doorway and the guy still gesturing towards the fridge. Bailey then looks back at the fridge and Stripes. Bailey: Ummm…..that is not a referee by the fridge. Dude #2: It so is man. He’s wearing a referee’s shirt. He’s dressed in white and black stripes like a referee. Bailey: That’s not a referee. That’s not even a human being. That’s a penguin, he is black and white. Dude #1: Whoa, why is there a penguin in the home of the NFL? Dude #2: Maybe that’s the mascot of one of the teams. Dude #1: Oh yeah. You’re right. I think I remember reading something about that. At this point in time, Bailey is getting noticeably upset at the two football fans standing at the door. Bailey: Listen this is not the home of the NFL. That is not a mascot for one a team. That’s a real penguin from Antarctica and this is the home of the NRL. The New Royal Legion. The home of the NFL is in New York. Okay, you got that? And before they have time to answer Bailey he shuts the door in their face, obviously flustered by them. He walks back to where Danyon and Larry were standing. Larry: What did they want? Were they just Legionnaires? It is a very common occurrence that Legionnaires from the parking lot come up to try and visit and see their cult heroes Bailey and Danyon. Bailey: No. They somehow thought this was the home of the NFL…….but I told them that it was in New York City. Larry: New York City? I thought that Fox Sports was the home of the NFL. Bailey: I’m not even going to dignify that statement with an answer. Bailey shakes his head and looks over at Danyon. Bailey: Listen you know what I think this means. I think this might mean that the conspiracy has reared its ugly head once again. It’s been a very long time since we saw it. But if you think about it, it makes sense. It’s been what, 4 months since we became the #1 contender’s to the PTC Tag Titles and are only receiving our tag title shot now. All because of these small technicalities or so they keep telling us. And when we finally get our tag title shot, they screw up our name getting it wrong. Danyon: That is an interesting thought, Bailey. It makes sense that the conspirators would focus all their energy on us in the PTC Tat Title tournaments since we have pretty much done everything there is to do in XUW. We’ve beaten the conspiracy time and time again in XUW so it makes sense that they focus their attention somewhere else and somehow else try to screw us over. Bailey: Plus, the fact that we haven’t wrestled a tag match in XUW for a little while now so it’s hard to conspire against The New Royal Legion when we aren’t fighting for the XUW Tag Titles as often as we used to. Although, I am going for the World Title now on this coming Monday, so that makes it very interesting that the conspiracy is coming back when I have my biggest match in XUW to date as a singles wrestler and we have our biggest match in the PTC to date. It’s all very convenient. Danyon: And don’t forget that Webster has returned as the Commissioner of XUW and he was an original conspirator. This all adds up. It always happens that as soon as you think you have defeated the conspiracy once and for all and then it comes back. This thing never seems to go away. It really does haunt us day and night. Our whole history in XUW can be traced around this conspiracy against us. But we got to win the PTC Tag Titles, we have to beat the Shadow Riders in order to beat the conspiracy once again much like all the other times. Bailey nods in agreement at what Danyon said. Bailey: It’s time to finally prove to the conspirators and to the PTC and most importantly to the Shadow Riders just how good we can be. It’s time for the conspiracy to end. Bailey looks at the pamphlet where it wrongly states their name as the New Foreign Legion and crumples it up into a paper ball and tosses it in the direction of the waste paper basket. Both Bailey and Danyon have a look of determination on their faces as they realize that there is another obstacle in their way of attaining the PTC Tag Titles that they have long coveted. The obstacle they now know of is of course the dreaded conspiracy that has haunted them since day one in XUW, when they first teamed up over two years ago. The scene slowly fades to black.
