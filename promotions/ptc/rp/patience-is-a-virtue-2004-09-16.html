Patience is a Virtue

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 (The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which says . . . “Life may end but legends never die.”. The scene opens to a view from high above the crowded strip of Las Vegas during the night time hours. The streets however are still flooded with the lights coming from the cars below and the brightly flashing neon signs of the various casinos. There is however no sound penetrating the air from the traffic below. Instead, the air remains strangely quiet and undisturbed. Suddenly the sound of a voice cuts through the air causing the camera to jolt in surprise.) Voice: Welcome to Sin City. (The camera quickly spins, stopping on a shot of “Superstar” Eric Russo. Russo stands leaning against a wall next to a rusted metal door that leads back into the interior of the building. He is wearing a silken black shirt which is partly open over a pair of black dress slacks. A pair of clear sunglasses rest on the bridge of his nose and gleam as they reflect the neon lights. Russo now pushes himself off the wall and begins to walk towards the camera as he continues speaking.) Russo: You know, a lot of cameramen would refuse to come onto an open roof with a Shadow Rider. It’s a well know fact that the company we keep tends to be a little accident prone. Cameraman: I’m not wor . . . (The Cameraman is cut off as Russo explodes.) Russo: Did I ask you to talk!? (Russo stops directly in front of the camera as he continues.) Russo: Are you being paid to talk!? Do you think I asked you to come up here so I could have a conversation with you!? (Knowing better then to answer back, the cameraman just looks on in silence.) Russo: I’m paying you to film me, so shut up and do what your being paid for. (Without hesitation, the cameraman quickly focuses in on Russo’s face as he looks deep into the camera with an icy stare and begins to speak.) Russo: It is said by some that patience is a virtue*. What is not said though is that patience can also be a curse. Take me for example. (The camera begins following Russo as he begins pacing from right to left. He continues speaking.) Russo: I’ve sat back and watched quietly as men with far less talent then I were given opportunities that should have been mine. I’ve watched as men like Mike Meyhu and Tristan Belmont were handed title after title on a silver platter in the LOW, a federation that was built on my sweat . . . my blood. (At this point Russo walks over to the stone ledge of the building as the camera follows him. He takes a seat with his back to the street below and continues speaking.) Russo: I’m done with that waiting now though. I’m going out and I’m gonna take what I want. I did it on Realm with the LOW World Title, I’m gonna do it when we face the New Royal Legion for the PTC Tag Titles, and I’m gonna do it when I step in the ring with Jason Starr for the GTT. (Russo stands once again and begins pacing in front of the brightly lit skyline behind him as he continues talking.) Russo: Now some my pass my comments off a cockiness. They may push them aside as nothing more then the ramblings of an oversized ego. I assure you however that is not the case. It is simply fact. (Russo pauses in his tracks and turns his attention directly to the camera before continuing.) Russo: The fact is that I’ve paid my dues and now it’s my turn. It’s my time to step into the spotlight. It’s time for me to show the entire world exactly what Eric Russo is capable of. I’m not gonna let my time be taken away from me. I’ve worked too hard for too long to allow that. (A strand of Russo’s hair falls into his face and he brushes it aside using his hand to smooth it down. He then continues.) Russo: When I put my name on the dotted line for this tournament, I knew that I would have a long hard road ahead of me. A road filled with many trials and tribulations. A road that would test my ability as well as my patience. (Russo now turns to his back to the camera as he looks out over the city of New York. The camera quickly moves around to Russo’s side and gets a profile shot of his face. Russo continues.) Russo: I’ve learned to deal with that though. Hell, I’ve made a career out of it. The important thing though is that I’m still standing. (Russo turns his attention back to the lens of the camera. He looks deep into it as he continues.) Russo: Long after all of the others have faded away into the past, I’m still here. I’m still fighting and clawing my way through anything that is placed in front of me. The GTT will be no different. (In almost an instant, Russo’s demeanor changes to one of a quiet reflection as he begins pacing back and forth once again.) Russo: Even as I say that though, I feel a slight sense of remorse. A remorse for the fact that I know that to make my mark in this tournament, I can show no mercy. I cannot allow myself to stop until my worth has truly been proved. That is an unfortunate reality that my opponents will come to know all too well. (Russo walks back to the stone ledge of the building where he once again takes a seat. He then hangs his head down as he continues.) Russo: The person that I feel the most sympathy towards however is Jason Starr. Starr is unfortunately the first who will bow down before me. Being that he is the first however, he will also be an example. While the others will suffer, Jason Starr will experience so much more. (Russo shakes his head in sympathy for what is about to occur as he continues.) Russo: He will be an example of what 16 years of being held down, being pushed pack, being denied opportunity after opportunity can do to a man. Jason Starr will be an example of what true pain is. (Russo now raises his head and begins to get to his feet as he speaks.) Russo: Patience may be a virtue but for Jason Starr it will also be his death sentence. Because he just like the rest is about to become nothing but another falling starr. (Russo looks directly into the camera as he speaks.) Russo: Oh and that’s not ego talking, that’s reality. (With that the cameraman calls out from behind the camera.) Cameraman: “That’s a wrap.” (Angered, Russo responds.) Russo: “No shit you jackass.” (Russo then smacks the camera away from his face as the screen cuts back to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which states “Life may end but legends never die.”. The screen now fades to black.) * “Patience is a virtue” (Chinese Proverbs)
