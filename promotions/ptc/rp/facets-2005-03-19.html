Facets

19 March 2005

Date: 2005-03-19 Allow me to introduce myself to all of you fine gentlemen, and not to forget a lady too. My name is Ember. Well ok obviously that is not my name, as even I find it ludicrous to believe that any parents would sit with their little books of baby names, preparing for the birth of their beloved child, and sit and debate the value of such a name. “Mackenzie is a nice one, it means son of the light or the son of the handsome one” “Oh that is lovely darling, and you are so handsome after all … but what if it is a girl?” “Well we could call her Sabia, the sweet one, just like you my dear” “Oh you are such a flatterer you know … by Jove though I think that I have it!” “You do? Tell me my turtle dove!” “Ember, we should call the first born of my loins Ember!” “Perfect!” Why do I feel that that is not the most likely of happenings, ey? Anyway, as you have surmised … well actually as I have blatantly told you … my name is not actually Ember, but that is the name by which you may address me, if you have the need to address me at all. Feel free of course to make the obvious and oh so stereotypical puns and plays on my name during your foray into finding ways to demean me. A near dear fire. Flames that are burnt out. Ashes. Sparks. … history? Trust me you won’t be the first and you won’t be the last to think that my name actually means anything about me at all. It is simply something that I choose to represent me in the field of glorious battle that I seem to find myself in. … and apologies for any sarcasm that you find, I could say that it isn’t meant but then I would be lying. By nature my tongue is twisted and my thoughts inherently biased. I prefer to think the worst of everyone and that way I cannot be disappointed. Though I am willing for someone to prove me wrong on that, as I am sure that as low an opinion that I have on my peers in this little contest they can still surprise me and bury themselves further into the gutter. So anyway like I said my name of choice is Ember, my home of the moment is the WWA and for God only knows why I find myself here and now in this little tournament called the Seven of the Best. I have to admit that at the start I was looking forward to this as I assumed that it was going to be held in a dark club somewhere with canes of willow of whip of blackest leather. I even had my little safe word already chosen and on the tip of my tongue for when the dominatrix’s ministrations became too much for my reddened … but oh so pleasurable … body. Silly me though for thinking that I had been fortunate enough to find myself in the gentle … or not so hopefully … hands of a sado-masochistic foray. Instead I find out that I get to pit myself against thirteen other wrestlers and beat the living Hell out of people in order to get a pithy little accolade like ‘the best’. … actually when you put it that way it isn’t too different really, just a pity that there is only the one filly in the stable so to speak. I don’t really bend the other way you see so it won’t be half as much fun with all those guys, but I suppose that I can only hope that Karina makes it to the end so that I can try out my little word on her. It is ‘flagellate’ in case you were wondering Kari dear … though of course I hope that you make me sweat before I even think about using it. So now that you know who I am, you know where I come from and you know that I … just like you … am here to take part in this little thing called Seven of the Best. To be honest I am not sure why the powers that be in the WWA put me forward for this. The ego-maniacal side of me … and yes it is actually the larger side, and I happen to think my best side so warn me if you want a photo and I will turn that way for you … thinks that it is because they know that I am the best that they have to offer. Though in a place that ranks the broken down Shadow, the misguided Dave Harley and turncoat that is Joshua Taro Freedom among their best I could read that as more of an insult than praise. I could but I won’t. You see … whether you want to take this as ego or not, I don’t really care … the truth is that I AM the best that they have to offer. … but another part of me thinks that they have put me here to be the sacrificial lamb to the slaughter. I have made no friends in the WWA since I began my journey there just three short months ago you see. I suppose when you walk in and beat their legends, beat their champions, beat their heroes one after another without breaking sweat it is bad enough. When you constantly point out that fact, and laugh about it, week after week there is a chance that it may annoy them ever so slightly. Oh well, woe is me for rocking the boat that is the HMS WWA. It isn’t my fault that they don’t seem to have a single wrestler that is able to pin me cleanly. Ten matches so far and not a single clean loss. Yes, yes, yes … I did say ‘clean’ loss, well spotted at the back, give yourself an extra five merits. The WWA have resorted into putting crooked referees on my case, referees who don’t see a foot on the rope … who make a three count finish on two … for example. So forgive me when I actually look this gift horse in the mouth expecting to get bitten. I don’t think that the WWA are being all too altruistic when they give me the shot at this tournament you know. I mean when they told me that I had this chance I was eager and confident … at least to their faces. I wasn’t going to be anything less now was I? I told them that it would be no problem, that I would go through the ranks of this tournament just as easily as I went through the ranks of their own federation. … just between you and me though, and keep this to yourself, there is a part of me that is not so confident. Have you SEEN the entrant list for this thing? Oh yes, of course you have, silly me! It reads like a ‘who’s who’ of wrestling … and if there WAS such a book I know that the entry for Ember would be a little smaller than anyone else’s in Seven of the Best. I mean that it is bad enough that I am facing a man like Scott Monroe to start with … sorry what was that? Why? Sorry I thought that was obvious, I don’t know a damn thing about him. I mean I thought that I had the whole mysterious and unknown angle sown up, in fact I had thought that I had trade-marked it, but Monroe is someone that I can find nothing about. The little press release the PTC sent out lets me know his name and where he works … and not much else. The publicity stuff that I have seen from him so far … well actually I haven’t seen ANYTHING from him so far. My heat skipped a little beat in girlish anticipation, like my first time at second base, when I realised that something had appeared with his name on it, but when I looked into it a little more I realised that it may have mentioned Scott Monroe on the cover but inside it was nothing more than a movie that Peter Jackson may have farted one night in his sleep. … I wasn’t sure if it was an acid-fuelled trip into the Bible or a day in Manhattan. Sometimes it is really hard to tell. The one thing that I know that it wasn’t was anything that lets me know about Scott himself, about how he feels about this tournament, about how he feels about me! Oh well though, Scooter is the least of my worries, I have bigger things to dwell upon. I mean how on Earth do I figure out who the other six people are that will face me after the first round? Fourteen people enter, and just like Noah and his Ark two by two they face each other … hey Scooter if you are watching maybe you could use that in your next instalment and do a play on Raiders of the Lost Ark … until there are only seven left. Then those seven get greased up, naked and slide into a ring full of jelly … oh ok, they don’t actually but it WOULD be a lot more fun … … then those seven enter the ring at one time and fight to throw each other over the top until there is only one. There can be only one you see, and damn it if I left my dodgy Scottish accent in my other full latex body suit. Oh well I will just have to go with what I have. … and do you know what that is? Of course you don’t because I haven’t told you yet. What I have is the desire to be the best that I can be, what I have is the spirit to go into this and give one hundred percent, what I have … the one thing that will make the difference I think … is the ability to know my own limitations, to think outside the box, and realise that this is NOT just about me, it is about me and thirteen other people who ALL want to win this thing. The biggest difference is that I can see all the sides to this, I can see that Karina … Oni … Shipley … Adams … anyone actually, has the chance to win this thing. I, of course want to win it, I hope to win it, and I know that I have the ability to win it, but I also know one other thing. I know the truth. Ah … now I have your interest I see! I have heard it said that the truth hurts. I have heard it said that truth is stranger than fiction. Fourteen people, one winner. Fourteen people and thirteen egos running wild with promises of victory, and boasts of glory. Fourteen people who are so blind to the truth that they can only see one side to the tale, one facet of the whole. Fourteen people and only one person who will tell the truth. Only one person who will admit to their limitations and to their concerns, only one person who will admit that the scope of this tournament … the level of competition in it … scares the absolute shit out of them. Only one person who seems to realise that WHAT the truth actually is. Me. You see it isn’t what Karina says … it isn’t what Oni says … it isn’t what Monroe says … it isn’t even what I say. You see there are three sides … three facets … to the gem that is the truth. There is your side, there is my side. … and there is the truth. Scooter my friend, this last is just for you because as the Bible says, “Ye shall know the truth and it shall set ye free” ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ “So, are you finished yet?” he asked quietly, an edge of impatience evident in his tone. Jimmy Dixon was an image of perfection as he stood staring at the back of his brother’s head. While still only seventeen years old he was built like the stereotypical Greek God, a benefit of his’ father’s genes, standing at just a shade over six feet in height his physique was obviously well toned, visible through the tight fitting Gap t-shirt that covered his torso. A mop of light blonde hair fell over his forehead, tousled as though from sleep. A small smile played at the edges of his mouth, the humor lighting up his pale blue eyes and making them sparkle, as he watched his brother’s shoulders stiffen. “Like chalk and cheese” the thought ran unbidden through his mind, eliciting another smile from him as he could almost palpably feel the frustration emanating from his older brother’s body. Though only six years older than Jimmy, Robert Dixon could easily have passed for his father … well at least he could have if their father had been prematurely bald with thinning brown hair and an obvious paunch from one too many visits to the Colonel’s favourite chicken restaurant instead of the gym. “Not quite Jimmy, and I never will be if you keep asking me that every five minutes.” he said as he looked over his shoulder. “You know that my work cannot be rushed and this is more delicate than usual,” Robert continued, his brown eyes peering over the rims of his black-framed glasses, his lips tightly pressed together as lines creased his forehead in either frustration or concentration. Jimmy smiled as he watched his brother turn back to his work, his grin becoming bigger as he listened to the half-formed words that Robert muttered under his breath. Jimmy may not have been able to make them out but he knew roughly what his brother was saying. Something uncomplimentary to Jimmy himself no doubt. Leaning over his brother’s shoulder, resting a hand gently on the back of the chair that formed a barrier between the two men, Jimmy’s eyes widened slightly as he caught sight of what Robert was working on. "What is it Bobby?" he breathed, his voice barely a whisper, subconsciously holding his breath as he gazed in awe at the gemstone held carefully in his brother's fingers. Glinting red, and sending refracted sparkles of crimson across the desk in front of Robert, the gemstone almost seemed to shine with an internal light of it's own making instead of reflecting that of the lamp overhead. For a second the familial resemblance was unmistakable as both men stared in unabashed awe at the stone, neither one seemingly breathing. “One of the rarest stones known to man Jimmy, a Siberian red diamond, that is what it is” Robert breathed to his brother, not taking his eyes of the stone that he held. While he was acknowledged as one of the seven best gemmologist in the United States, if not the World, Robert Dixon realised that his area of specialisation was not one that would have him at the top of many peoples list of experts to call upon when something special … something priceless … was discovered. There were plenty more high-profile specialists who could have been called upon to identify and assess the stone that he held, but he also realised that he had an advantage over those people. In the first instance he was one of the few men to have ever worked with one of the famous Siberian Red’s before, and secondly … and more importantly for his clients … he WASN’T high profile. His workshop was small and mundane, very much like his life itself, and as such he wasn’t likely to be a target for anyone wanting to steal the gem. …and with something like a Siberian Red, one of only seven stones like it ever found, theft was foremost in the owner’s mind. “There are only seven of these stones in the known World Jimmy” Robert started to say but was interrupted by his brother’s raucous laugh. “… and they trusted YOU with one of them? What is so special about it anyway?” Jimmy barked out with a good-natured smile on his face. The amount of respect he held for his brother knew no bounds, but he was irascible in making fun of him anyway. Robert’s head flicked around as he glared at his younger brother, a look of irritation being quickly replaced by a small smile. “Yes they did Jimmy … and as for what makes it special, it is simply that it is a perfectly formed tetradecahedron …” seeing the blank look on his brother’s face Robert gave another smile as he held the gem up between them, and slowly spun it around in his fingers, “ … you see, it has fourteen facets, fourteen sides. Basic math Jimmy” “How much is it worth?” Jimmy asked quietly, his eyes fixated to the stone as his mind played over the facts that had brought him to his brother’s workshop that night. A gambling debt had been called in two days previously, and the bookie was one of those men that you just didn’t want to argue with … or at least you didn’t want to if you valued parts of your anatomy leg your legs. Jimmy had managed to get most of the money together, even going so far as to take a loan out on his treasured Corvette … something else that would have to be sorted out sooner rather than later or the loan shark would be the next one calling on him in the late hours … but he still found himself nearly two hundred dollars short. So while he knew that Robert wouldn’t approve, he had nowhere else to turn. “Worth? … it is priceless Jimmy, I don’t think that you could put a figure on this” Robert exclaimed as his eyebrows came together in a frown, “… oh I get it, how much do you need this time?” he asked with a sigh. Hearing the tone of resignation in his brother’s voice Jimmy was slightly abashed, but he knew that Bobby would come through for him. Ever since their parents had been killed in a car crash nearly five years previously Bobby had been a father figure to Jimmy. Bobby had been the one who had made sure that the bills were paid, that Jimmy was fed and clothed. He had put his life on hold to make sure that Jimmy was taken care of … in fact Jimmy and his career were Bobby’s whole life. Bobby was the one person that Jimmy knew he could turn too when he needed, the one person that loved him unconditionally. The one person that Jimmy himself loved. “Just two hundred Bobby, but I promise this is the last time and I will get it back to you in a few days!” Jimmy said as he stared through the gem, directly into the gaze of his brother. Through the red hue of the diamond their eyes met … and locked … and … / FLIP \ “So, are you finished yet?” Robert Dixon clenched his teeth in frustration, wincing as he felt the pain of the abscess on one jaw that had been nagging him for a few days now. ‘Nothing like the pain that the damn stomach ulcer gives me’ he thought to himself as, right on cue, his stomach clenched in sympathy. For the last couple of years, nearer to three, he had found that he had suffered from an ulcer that just wouldn’t go away. His doctor had told him to try to get rid of stress in his life … but they didn’t know his brother. As his stomach gave him another acid edged poke he realised that he had another pain. A pain in the ass by the name of Jimmy. Thinking of his brother breathing down his neck like a vampire waiting for lunch, Robert frowned in aggravation. He had barely been in the workshop for fifteen minutes and already he was prowling like a caged lion. ‘All muscle and action, instinctual in every fibre of his body the great lummox’ though Robert as he glanced askance at his brother. A flash of jealousy fired across his face too quickly for Jimmy to see as Robert took in the athletic frame that his brother had been gifted with. Not for him the abuse and jokes from his peers about being too short or too fat, not for him the cries of ‘four-eyes’, not for him the grief that was a prematurely receding hairline. “Like chalk and cheese” the thought ran unbidden through Robert’s mind as he lamented the fact that Jimmy should have been blessed with a body like that. Though if the choice had been between the body of Jimmy or his own brains and aptitude with gems Robert realised that he wouldn’t trade … though sometimes it was a close call. Only six years difference in their ages and the last little bitch that Jimmy had brought home, the last slut that had spread her legs in his bed no doubt, had looked at Robert and assumed that he was Jimmy’s father. Called him Mr Dixon for God’s sake! “Not quite Jimmy, and I never will be if you keep asking me that every five minutes.” he said as he looked over his shoulder. ‘That is if you even know what five minutes really is with your MTV attention span you idiot’ Robert thought to himself, gritting his teeth … as he had so many times in the last few years … to stop from shouting the words at his brother’s face. “You know that my work cannot be rushed and this is more delicate than usual,” Robert continued, his brown eyes peering over the rims of his black-framed glasses, his lips tightly pressed together as lines creased his forehead in frustration. A tight, hot feeling seemed to pierce his head right between his eyes and Robert winced as he realised that on top of everything else he had a migraine coming on … yet another side-effect of too much time around his brother. Even as the words came out of his mouth he realised that they would wash right over Jimmy like water of a duck’s back. Jimmy wouldn’t realise how delicate or specialised his work was. Unless it involved two men pounding on each other in the middle of a ring in the name of sports entertainment, or women in tight t-shirts with the word ‘Hooters’ on them, it didn’t factor into Jimmy’s little world. As he turned his back to his brother, looking down at his workbench, Robert could feel the heat of the inane grin that spread across Jimmy’s face. “Stupid fuckin’ idiot, how the Hell did we ever come from the same womb?” Robert muttered to himself, just too low to be heard he knew. “…wouldn’t surprise me if he had climbed out of an abortion bucket and suck his greedy mouth to mom’s tit” A flash of anger hit Robert as he felt the chair move as Jimmy leant on it. A few more seconds and he may have been working on the stone and his concentration would have been ruined. He could have ruined days of work, ruined a priceless stone, simply because his retard of a brother couldn’t keep his hand to himself. "What is it Bobby?" The irritation that Robert normally felt when he heard himself called by the name Bobby … a name that only Jimmy called him, seemingly oblivious to the fact that he himself always referred to himself as Robert unfailingly, seemingly oblivious to the fact that it was a name that Robert hated … faded quickly by the pride he felt in the stone before him. The awe in his brother’s voice was unmistakable and Robert was gratified that even someone as moronic as his brother could see the perfection in front of him. As Jimmy stared at the stone in wonder Robert didn’t realise that for that one second they looked so much like each other that it was impossible not to notice. The one instant where Robert could have visibly seen the blood that ran through them both played out in their matching visages was lost forever. “One of the rarest stones known to man Jimmy, a Siberian red diamond, that is what it is” Robert breathed to his brother, not taking his eyes of the stone that he held. He realised that Jimmy wouldn’t have a clue what he meant … he sometimes wondered if Jimmy had a clue about anything other than working out and jacking off over the latest Playboy special … but pride in his own knowledge forced him to say the words anyway. Jimmy may not recognise the fact but Robert was extremely proud of his status as one of the industry’s top gemmologist and the fact that he had been entrusted with one of the World’s seven rarest … seven best … stones filled him with pride. “There are only seven of these stones in the known World Jimmy” Robert started to say but was interrupted by his brother’s raucous laugh. “… and they trusted YOU with one of them? What is so special about it anyway?” Jimmy barked out. Robert’s head flicked around as he glared at his younger brother. ‘Un-fuckin’ believable’ he thought to himself, ‘ he is looking at something that most of the World will never get to see and he doesn’t realise it. A look of irritation played over his face as he took in the mocking laughter, the oh so mocking grin that spread from ear to ear on Jimmy’s face and he felt his face burn with embarrassment and anger. He had been looking after this kid for over a third of his spoiled life, he had wiped his ass for him, he had held him in the night when he pissed his pants at imagined ghosts, he had done EVERYTHING for the little bastard and here he was laughing at him in his own workshop. Taking a breath to calm himself Robert consciously changed the look of irritation to a small smile as he looked at this brother’s face. “Yes they did Jimmy … and as for what makes it special, it is simply that it is a perfectly formed tetradecahedron …” he said, smiling as he realised that Jimmy didn’t even know what the word meant and with a sense of satisfaction he continued, spinning the gem hypnotically in front of his face “… you see, it has fourteen facets, fourteen sides. Basic math Jimmy” he finished with a sarcastic flourish. “How much is it worth?” The words actually stunned Robert for a second, as the value of the gem was secondary to him. The beauty of it, the uniqueness of it, the fact that it was something pure, something perfect, something that was HIS and his alone was all that mattered to him. To put dollar signs onto it not only cheapened it somehow but demeaned the hours he had put in polishing it, cleaning it, perfecting it. ‘Worth?’ he thought bitterly to himself, ‘why would he want to know how much it …’ “Worth? … it is priceless Jimmy, I don’t think that you could put a figure on this” Robert exclaimed as his eyebrows came together in a frown, suddenly realising exactly why Jimmy asked that question, suddenly realising exactly why Jimmy had deigned to visit him at work “… oh I get it, how much do you need this time?” he asked with a sigh, feeling the bile rise in his gullet and a fresh wave of pain overtake his stomach. Robert nearly smirked as he finally saw a look of embarrassment play over the face of his younger brother. Not content with stealing the life from out of him … working every hour that he could to pay the bills to make sure that his wastrel of a brother got what he wanted, keeping a falling down house barely habitable just so that he had a roof over his head, simply so that the little money that their parents had left kept coming in. Damn them to Hell for writing a will that forced Robert to take care of his brother until his twenty first birthday. Damn them to Hell for dying and turning his life into a living Hell for another three years until his rightful inheritance would finally be his and he could get Jimmy out of his life once and for all. Damn them to Hell for dying before he could finish collage, and forcing him to run up debts that forced him to keep Jimmy around and the money from the will. Damn them to Hell for dying and leaving him! “Just two hundred Bobby, but I promise this is the last time and I will get it back to you in a few days!” Jimmy said as he stared through the gem, directly into the gaze of his brother. Through the red hue of the diamond their eyes met … and locked … and … The scream of agony was cut off almost before it began, and Robert wiped the trail of blood from his face barely conscious of the fact that it was there. His left hand went limp and his fingers released their hold on the electric drill that was customarily used to get rid of imperfections from the gemstones that were his life. A drill tip that could take fractions of a millimetre of the toughest of diamonds. The whine of the small engine that powered the drill cut-off as his fingers let go of the small button on the drill’s body and Robert barely blinked as he watched Jimmy’s lifeless carcass collapse to the ground at his feet. His right eye stared up at his older brother in frozen shock, uncomprehending of what could have happened to him while his left eye oozed out over the lid to drip down his cheek, the three thousand dollar drill sticking out of it in a mockery of a flag at salute. An eye was no match for a tip that could pierce a diamond, and bone was no resistance for it either as it made it’s way directly into Jimmy’s brain. In less than a second Jimmy’s life had been snatched away from him, stolen by his brother’s hand … is de-facto father’s hand … and his brother’s tool. One moment he had been gazing through a red filled World staring at his brother’s eyes and then his gaze was filled with red … and pain … and nothing. Standing up Robert stepped over the lifeless form of Jimmy and reached for his jacket, flicking the Siberian Diamond into the eye and grinning as his gaze was filled with refracted red. Three more years until his inheritance would have been such a long time to wait after all, and as Jimmy had said, what was it worth in the end? It was worth a life of his own. Finally. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The darkness was near absolute. A flicker at the corner of the eye and it became apparent that there was a palpable difference in the darkness, and the realisation came that if there was something to compare that darkness too then it had to be that there was something other than darkness there too. A small tendril, barely a breath of life in a near dead corpse, of smoke threaded it’s way up through the darkness like fingers reaching desperately for purchase as they feel life slipping away from them. As the saying goes there is no smoke without fire, but in this case it was so very nearly wrong. Fire is living and breathing, fire is glorious and majestic, fire is elemental and immortal. This then was not … could not be …fire. At the base of the darkness, just barely registering as colour against the blackness … more a different shad of blackness itself rather than something completely different … something small, something inestimably tiny … seemed to writhe. A memory of what was once fire. The recollection of a glorious past. The yearning to be again what once it was. A remnant of flame among the ashes. A spark. … an ember. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ To be continued in part two: "The Amazing Spider, Man"
