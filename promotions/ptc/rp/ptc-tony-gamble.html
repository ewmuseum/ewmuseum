5 July 2009

5 July 2009 (The crowd goes wild when he walks out onto the stage, the flamboyant shirt sparkling under the overhead lights of the arena.) (Ladies and gentlemen, Tony Gamble has entered the building.) Gamble: What the hell, this isn't Letterman! (The crowd boos, upset at the little man's reaction.) Gamble: Wah! Wah! Wah! You didn't think I wanted to come out here and see you, did you? Aw, that's sweet. Now go die! (Fade to black.) ---- 6 July 2009 (The light on top of the camera blinked to life, setting us free from our black prison of darkness.) "Is it on?" (Tony Gamble stood in front of the camera, slightly pacing in a semi-circle around the black stool set up in front of a navy blue PRIME backdrop.) Voice (Off screen): The light is on, so I guess so. (Tony Gamble turns to look toward the camera, throwing his arms out to his side at shoulder level.) Gamble: You guess so? I didn't hire you to guess so. Voice (Off screen): Hire me? You're not paying me to do this crap. Gamble: That's beside the point. Lets focus on what is important here... (Tony pats himself on the chest with his left hand.) Gamble: Me. When I heard that they were going to give away a spot to whoever wins this Infinite Gauntlet, battle royale, royal rumble, last man standing, free for all match, I figured who better than Gamble to waltz in under the radar and snatch it from the grasp of some Doc C rip-off. Seriously, apart from that guy from Ghostbusters, do you honestly think anyone stands a chance to win this thing? No. And do you know why I say no? No, of course you don't, because you can't read my mind. That, and you're all a bunch of idiots that have nothing better to do than to sit down in front of the television with a bag of cheetos in one hand and yourself in the other. I know, I'm hot, I get it, but do you honestly believe that playing with yourself is going to help you win a match like this? Of course you do, because you're an idiot. (Having not taken a breath in like ten minutes, Tony takes this moment to hold up a photo.) Gamble: As you can see, I'm not alone in my assumption. Knowing that these two guys are experts on the subject, how could I not agree with them. Now, I would love to sit here and tell you how I am confident that this is going to be so easy, how I doubt I'll even manage to break a sweat against you losers, but I'm not going to waste my time telling you something you already know. And I know that you know this because I am that awesome. So, take my word for it and just accept it as truth, because if there is one thing that I am not... (Tony Gamble smiles, which isn't really that hard when you only have to worry about one half of your face, and gives the camera a thumbs up.) Gamble: Its a Liar. (We now let you go back to your pitch black prisons.)
