Final Fantasy I

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-05-23 The Finals. Ordinarily, this would be where Seymour justified his quest, and told of his desire to save the world by beating an evil end boss. But Seymour no longer completely believes in the delusions, and Karina Wolfenden is not evil. About the only connection that Seymour COULD make is that Karina looks like she belongs in Final Fantasy X-2 with her flashy outfit. Even though he had no true motivation save pride, this match was his most important. It was potentially his last. Almasy vs. Wolfenden. PTC’s old guard versus its new. One time, for all time. ----- The Almasy home was abuzz with activity. As Seymour rushed to and fro packing a gigantic suitcase, Laura sat at a table with a giant stack of papers, reading each one carefully. Seymour desperately began trying to close the suitcase, much to Laura’s amusement. Laura Winters: You know, we CAN get you another suitcase if that one’s not big enough. Seymour Almasy: Well, yes. But I want to fit them all in THIS one! What are you doing, anyway? Laura Winters: Just checking over your contracts. You signed with half of the federations in existence, just making sure your commitments are up. Seymour gave a look of mock hurt. He blinked slowly, before addressing the woman he loved. Seymour Almasy: You mean, you don’t have faith in my ability to have negotiated my contracts? Laura Winters: In a word, Seymour, no. I love you dearly, but I don’t trust you with anything more technical than starting up the PlayStation 2. Seymour Almasy: Oh, it’s okay. You’re probably right though. Thanks for checking. Laura Winters: No problem at all, sweetie. I’ll take care of all of the business, you just worry about Karina. Karina was more than enough to worry about. Seymour had already dispatched one of RPW’s many entries in the tournament in the form of Matt Jackson in the first round. But this was different. Wolfenden was one of the franchise players of the fledgling promotion, a competitor known for a devil-may-care attitude and the ability to take every single chance in the book. Seymour Almasy: Yeah… Laura Winters: What’s wrong, Seymour? Seymour Almasy: I don’t know…I just expected the end to be more glorious somehow. Even now that I realize that the world’s not just one big RPG…it still makes me feel good when I defeat evil people with nefarious objectives. Karina has none. Laura Winters: Most people don’t, Seymour, but your line of work seems to bring them out of the woodwork. Seymour Almasy: You know, you’re right. I don’t see evil god worshipping psychos on the streets every day. Laura Winters: Not at all, and that’s a pretty good thing. Seymour Almasy: Agreed. The Final Fantasy shrugged his shoulders a bit. Wolfenden was, by all accounts, a normal person. A trust fund baby who loved her extreme sports, and treated wrestling as a hobby. Despite all of this, she was lethal. Lethal enough for her team with Tessa Windsor to be dubbed Hardcore Candy. A woman that had retired the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. Laura Winters: You’re worried, aren’t you? Seymour Almasy: Laura, I get worried when I fought El Turtle in UWF. Of course I’m worried now! Laura Winters: It is your way, Seymour. Seymour Almasy: It makes me focus more. If I think of every single opponent I face as being capable of beating me, it keeps me on top of my skills. It prevents me from letting up. And against Karina…I can’t afford to let up for a second. Her style brings the attack from everywhere, whether I’m in the ring, out of the ring, on the top rope…or whether she’s in any of those positions. Laura Winters: You know…I think a lot of your reluctance to do this stems from the fact that, really, she’s a lot like you. Seymour was caught off-guard. He never once used the word ‘reluctance’. Yet, on some level, he WAS extremely reluctant to step into the ring on 4WW’s StormFront against the K-Wolf. Seymour Almasy: What do you mean, Laura? Laura Winters: Face it, Seymour. She’s been trying to deny what she is for a long time. She doesn’t especially like coming from the background that she does, save for the fact that it gives her the money she needs to live and enjoy her activities. You’re also from a similar background. Despite your mother being a jerk and leaving you home alone all the time, she and your father provided you with whatever you wanted. Seymour Almasy: I guess that would explain all the game systems. The remark was made somewhat sheepishly, a bit of embarrassment on his face, but there was still much that Seymour didn’t understand. Seymour Almasy: But why is she embarrassed about her upbringing? Having a well to do family isn’t a bad thing, is it? Laura Winters: Well, if you’re a typical person, no. You and Karina aren’t. Yours was due to a psychological condition…but Karina just can’t tolerate it. Can’t tolerate the typical middle to upper class person. I know what she means, and why. I came from that environment. Seymour Almasy: What’s it like, Laura? Aerith could only smile at Seymour’s innocence. It was ironic. Had he been normal, he would have seen what wealthy people were like, the snobbery and the gossip. In a lot of ways, his mental affliction had saved him, had saved his personality from annihilation. Laura Winters: I’m not going to lie to you. The creature comforts are quite nice, but the people are just disgusting. Seymour Almasy: In what way? Laura Winters: In the way that they automatically assume that owning a BMW or a Lexus makes them better than everyone else. In the way that they so arrogantly believe money DOES buy happiness. Seymour Almasy: Oh… Laura Winters: I know, it’s kind of odd if you’ve never been through it, but money changes these people. It makes them lesser human beings. Seymour Almasy: Money doesn’t buy happiness. It can’t. Laura Winters: Although, it CAN make happy occasions happier… Despite still having his usual innocence, Seymour knew exactly where his wife was going. She had been able to talk about little but their official wedding ever since they had decided to have it. Seymour Almasy: Laura…I thought I told you I wanted a small, understated affair. Laura Winters: Oh, don’t worry. I only invited one hundred people. Seymour Almasy: One…HUNDRED?! THAT’S NOT SMALL, AERITH! TWENTY PEOPLE IS SMALL! Laura Winters: But twenty people would barely cover my parents, grandparents, siblings, and first cousins! Seymour Almasy: But Grandpa Cid’s the only person from my family coming! Laura Winters: Don’t worry, I took that into account. Besides, he’s the best man, so he doesn’t sit with everyone else. Good thing too, or it would look really silly to have seating divided by family with just one person on one side of the aisle. Seymour Almasy: Indeed. It was an easy choice. My grandfather’s been my best friend for most of my life. Laura Winters: And he’s pretty much financed this wedding. Seymour Almasy: He WHAT?! Laura, we have more than enough money to do this ourselves… Laura Winters: You’d think so…Seymour, when you were living in the shack in the middle of Manhattan after you left home, he called me and asked me to take care of you. When I agreed, he kind of gave me five million dollars to help. You learn something new every day, huh Seymour? Seymour Almasy: He…I had no idea Grandpa had that much money! Laura Winters: Runs in your father’s side of the family. They’ve been wealthy ever since the 1800s. And when your father died, he split his estate between you, your mom, and your grandfather. Realistically, you’re worth probably around $10 million right now. Of course, we’ve been keeping it in a bank for you. Lord knows what you would have done $10 million dollars as your former self… Seymour Almasy: Trip to Japan. Strippers in Final Fantasy costumes. The look on Laura’s face could have melted steel, before Almasy quickly clarified his point. Seymour Almasy: BEFORE we got together, Laura! BEFORE! Laura Winters: Oh! Okay. Seymour Almasy: Why did you never tell me? Laura Winters: Because, I handled all the financial aspects of our relationship. You compete for the thrill of it and the glory, not the money. You’re not exactly making the biggest money in the world, but you’ve been doing okay. Combination of that and dividends from some investments I’ve made has been enough to keep us with this apartment and the beach house. Besides, you don’t drive, so I’m the only one who needs a car. Oh, and the official got back to me, we’ll stop by the city offices later to pick up your chocobo license. Seymour Almasy: Woohoo! They’re calling it a chocobo, not an ostrich? Laura Winters: Yeah. The kid working in the office is a big Final Fantasy fan. Small world, isn’t it? Seymour Almasy: Yes, but it’ll be nice to be able to ride Boco legally….hey, wait a minute. How the hell did we get from the wedding to Boco? Laura Winters: You know, I don’t really think I want to remember. Seymour Almasy: Okay then, let’s just go back to the wedding. Laura Winters: It’s going to be wonderful. I have such a nice place picked out for the ceremony and the reception. While I’m at it, would you like chicken or sea bass? Seymour Almasy: Sea bass. Chicken reminds me of chocobo. I can’t eat something that I scratch behind the ears of! The former Aerith Heartilly laughed, shaking her head in disbelief. Laura Winters: Sea bass it is then. Seymour Almasy: You know…it’s strange. The quest isn’t yet over, but the ending is pretty much confirmed. Our wedding. Laura Winters: Yes, that’s one advantage of life not being an RPG. You don’t die when you lose. Seymour Almasy: True. Very true. But still…it’s an interesting feeling. I’m descending into the Northern Crater, fighting my way through Ultimecia’s Castle… Laura shook her head again. Here was the long string of Final Fantasy analogies… Seymour Almasy: Battling through Memoria and the guts of Sin, entering the Farplane Abyss…oh, and conquering the Temple of Chaos for any old school fans. Laura Winters: You’re leveled up as much as you can be… Seymour Almasy: Sadly, I have no Knights of the Round materia to help me…but back to the Temple of Chaos…the more I think about it, the more I see myself in an FF1 style time loop. Laura Winters: That’s understandable, considering the situation. Seymour Almasy: Last year, just a few months later than this, I married you, and then fought Roulette in the finals of the Primetime Invitational Tournament. This year, I fight Karina Wolfenden in the finals of the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament, and then marry you. For the second straight year, I will be married and fight in the finals of a major tournament against a female competitor in the space of a week. Laura Winters: That IS kind of freaky… Seymour Almasy: Ah well. At least I don’t have to worry about Karina trying to break my arm with every submission move known to man. I don’t think she ever bothered to learn what a submission hold is. Laura Winters: She’s never had to, but then again, you probably don’t know what a cross arm breaker is, but can perform a back-mounted arm scissor sleeper hold combination. Seymour Almasy: Ah, the Fallen One! I guess you’re right, in that respect we’re similar. We’re not classic mat wrestlers. Neither of us really looks at this as life or death…well, I used to, but not anymore. We sort of ARE alike. Laura Winters: The major difference of course being she can kick you in the head really, really hard. Seymour Almasy: Pah! I have the Nap Shot, Venom Shot, Wither Shot, Jecht Shot, and the ULTIMATE Jecht Shot for good measure! What do her kicks worry me? Laura Winters: Umm, if one of them hits, you’ll be in deep trouble? Seymour sighed. All that bravado, gone to waste… Seymour Almasy: Well, yes. That just means that I’ll have to make sure that they either don’t hit, or hit me in an area less vital than my head, kneecap, or groin. Laura Winters: That would be a good strategy, yes. Seymour Almasy: Really, I want to use the relative strength advantage that I’ll have in this, but to hit her with anything damaging, I sadly need to catch her. Which, as you’re about to tell me so it’s really not necessary to say anymore, is far easier said than done. Laura Winters: Yep, that pretty much covers it. Seymour Almasy: I was somehow hoping you’d be more supportive… The tugging at Laura’s heart strings succeeded in making her come closer, and wrap her arms around her husband tightly. She pressed her body against his, and rested her head on his shoulder, whispering lightly into his ear. Laura Winters: But we promised to always be honest with each other… Seymour Almasy: I know, I know, you just make it sound worse than it is… The Final Fantasy teasingly ran a hand through her hair, causing his lover to squirm against him. Moments like this reminded Seymour of the true reward of his quest. Titles, glory, and championship belts all paled in comparison to the woman he held in his arm, the woman that he would soon officially be married to, and he would have that fact proclaimed to the four corners of the earth. Laura Winters: I know you’ll be okay…but it’s not an easy match by any stretch of the imagination. Seymour Almasy: I know…Rush Pro Wrestling’s become all the rage in PTC, and she’s one of the top competitors there. She held one of their championships for a very long time, so she certainly is capable. Laura Winters: But you are more capable! Seymour Almasy: I hope so, Laura, and thanks for the belated vote of confidence. Laura Winters: Don’t mention it, sweetie… For several moments, Almasy wondered why he had even gone through with the tournament once he had decided he would likely be done with competing soon. As much as he loved being in the ring, he loved being at home more. More specifically, he loved being with Aerith. Seymour Almasy: I honestly can’t say this is the most important match of my career. For so many weeks leading up to this…I wanted to go out on top. But now…it all seems so insignificant. Laura Winters: It’s not insignificant, Seymour. For the first time in your life, though, you have your life in perspective. The matches in the ring are important to you because you don’t like to lose. No one does. But at the same time, whether or not you beat Karina Wolfenden, the world doesn’t end. Your legacy isn’t at risk of being tarnished. Years from now, fans will still know who you are. Seymour Almasy: But I want to win. I’ve told myself every match is life and death…which makes for a great way to be motivated. Now, though…it’s much harder to find. The Final Fantasy promptly plopped down upon the couch in the living room next to his suitcase, eyes focused on his wife. Laura looked back to the pile of papers that she had to go through, but shook her head. Those could wait. Laura Winters: Seymour, motivation’s not difficult to find. Seymour Almasy: Yes it is! His wife pouted slightly, batting her eyelashes at him. Laura Winters: Please win? For me? The color drained instantly from the Final Fantasy’s face. She had caught him terribly off guard, and they both knew it. Seymour Almasy: I…I… of course I will sweetie! Laura Winters: See? Motivation isn’t so difficult after all, now is it? Her usual smirk followed. Laura’s immediate impulse was to drag her husband to their bedroom and give him her fondest good luck wishes, but for now, she kept her desire in check. Besides, Seymour had to finish packing the suitcase… Seymour Almasy: Stupid freaking suitcase… …a task that seemed much more difficult than it should have been. Seymour Almasy: Why did we have to make it so we don’t come home in between the match and the wedding again? The Final Fantasy was practically sitting on the suitcase in a futile attempt to zip the suitcase closed. Laura giggled, and playfully smacked him on the shoulder. Laura Winters: Because, we wanted to get married as soon as possible because we love each other? Seymour Almasy: Oh yes, that! Of course! Almasy pulled her into another hug. She was right earlier. Everything HAD been put into perspective for him. Karina Wolfenden acted off of emotion in the ring. She could truly care less for titles…she simply wished to have fun. To Seymour, the thought was ludicrous, until he too realized that, for the most part, he had enjoyed every second of his career. Seymour Almasy: I’m sad it’ll be over…but at the same time, I’m ready. Laura Winters: You’ve done a lot, Seymour. You’ve won almost every title in the UWF, fought honorably for GW and LOW, been the only man so far since the Global Title was reinstated to defend it successfully… Seymour Almasy: And most importantly to me, I have brought some measure of happiness to spectators around the world. I cannot thank them enough. People who knew I was delusional…and yet cheered me in my efforts. Laura Winters: Through you they lived, Seymour. People live dreary lives. They get up, eat their meals, and go to work. In entertainment they seek release. Some find it as you do in slaying mythical dragons. Others watch movies, and still others come out to what you call the Battle Arena. Sitting in the crowd, they forget their problems, a bad day at work, things at home…and they cheer for their favorite competitor. Seymour Almasy: I’m flattered that so many of them cheered for me. Laura Winters: You’re quite likable. You don’t wrong people for the most part. In the end…wrestling is populated by lots of people who have no qualms about violating rules. True, many people cheer for the anti-hero, but for the most part, they want to see the good guy prevail. Seymour Almasy: Which for the fans must present a problem with this contest. Karina and I are both well liked. Laura Winters: You each have your home fanbases. RPW fans’ll cheer for her, but the shows being held in 4WW, which has connections to UWF. It’ll probably be a favorable crowd for you. Seymour Almasy: Excellent. Almasy smiled. The task wasn’t any easier at all, but fans in his corner certainly helped. They would provide more of the motivation that the Final Fantasy so sorely needed. Laura Winters: She’s lived more than you, you know. She’s quite young, and yet, she’s seen more, experienced more than you could probably ever imagine. Seymour Almasy: How are you so positive? Laura Winters: Just look at her move names. Move names tell a lot about the wrestler, about the person. For Karina…the Mayday Riot. The Whirlwind Romance. Things that indicate experience. She lives life to the fullest. She’s been snowboarding on some of the greatest slopes in the world. She’s seen more of the world than you have. She travels outside of wrestling. Seymour Almasy: So? Laura Winters: Fair enough question. She paused to collect her thoughts, to try to express her feelings. It was difficult to do so, but Karina Wolfenden was a dangerous competitor. Behind the cute appearance and pink outfit beat the heart of someone who would never give up, never give in. Laura Winters: I’d think you of all people would agree that we are shaped by our experiences, right? Seymour Almasy: Of course! I know I am. Laura Winters: Karina’s been stalked from federation to federation by Magnum Wolf, a man who seeks to bury her. She’s used to going up against impossible odds. Used to feeling despair the likes of which you can only imagine, before coming back to prove her detractors wrong. Seymour Almasy: I know…damn it all. Laura Winters: What’s wrong? Seymour Almasy: It IS like fighting a somewhat more sane copy of me. Someone who can take an amount of damage that no one can possibly conceive, and yet she goes on. She manages to survive to be victorious. Laura Winters: And that of course begs the question, how do you beat yourself? Seymour Almasy: Technical wrestling. Which I can’t do. Almasy hung his head. Suddenly, it seemed so hopeless. Karina Wolfenden was the perfect opponent for Seymour. Someone just like him. Someone light enough that he could pound at with everything in his playbook and yet not defeat. Laura Winters: Seymour, please…don’t worry. Once more, she embraced her husband, this time cradling his face in her hands, and delicately kissing him on the lips. Laura Winters: I know I said I wanted you to win for me earlier…but I was just teasing. Of course I’d love for you to win, but in reality, it does not matter. I will love you if you win, if you lose. We will be married, and live happily ever after. Seymour Almasy: Is that the end then, Laura? Laura pulled his face up to meet hers, kissing him once again. It was a long, slow kiss, one meant to shatter the doubts that he held in his mind and heart. Laura Winters: No, Seymour. This is not the end. This is the beginning.. The second the referee’s hand strikes the mat for the third time, the Final Fantasy is over… Seymour Almasy: I know, Lau-- Laura Winters: But the sequel begins. A sequel in which we are both blissfully happy with each other. Seymour Almasy: You know, I think I’m going to like Final Fantasy II… She prevented him from speaking any more with another kiss. Soon, the final battle would occur, and the credits would roll. And with that, the story of one of Primetime Central’s greatest talents would end, not with a bang, but with a proverbial whimper. Just the way the new Seymour liked it.
