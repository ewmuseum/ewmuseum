Shadowmane Public Access Pimp Joined: 10 Feb 2003 Posts: 19 Location: Cirque de Soleil Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 2:13 pm Post subject: [Gothic Knights] Tag Team Clusterfuck #3: Road To Debut “They say freak when you’re singled out…” Eddie stands in front of the tombstone of his brother again. He has a black trench coat on with his wrestling gear underneath. After days of preparation, he felt he and Tommy were finally ready to take on the big guns. Eddie knelt down and ran his hand down the cold face of the tombstone. “So what do you think, Fritz,” he asked. “Do you think we have a chance?” He didn’t expect an answer. There were many possibilities. Tommy could get hurt again. He could get hurt. They could be involved in another accident before they got there. He didn’t even know much about the competition. Who were these guys they were going to face? Damn, he thought, am I just fooling myself about all of this? Tommy needs me now. He can’t do this alone at this point. We’ve put too much work into the preparation, but God, I got butterflies in my stomach the size of eagles. What if I’m not up to it? I’ve been feeling the effects of the bumps more than I used to. I think I’m getting old. He scowled and stood up, looking around the cemetery. His soul was filled with desolation as he thought about the lives of all that had passed before him… the legacies that were left behind, or squandered. He wondered if his would be as great as he thought it could be. He wondered if he had what it takes to be a champion again. He had enjoyed the run as a tag champion with Fritz, but he never thought about what he could do alone… that is until a few days ago, when Tommy had come in late. He had decided to go ahead without Tommy, working out with Stan alone. He found that it had a certain appeal, going out there and working for himself. But he knew he was a team player at this point. He knew that without each other, he and Tommy wouldn’t be able to break back into the industry. They just weren’t strong enough without the bond. It was a symbiosis that could be weaned away slowly, but it was at the point now where it would be destructive. The Gothic Knights, which is what they had been christened by Ernie, were a team. Tommy was to take up his Crusader persona, and Eddie was to relive his Gargoyle persona. Crusade. Yes, that’s what it was they were launching, he thought to himself, a crusade to rid themselves of the demons that had been holding them back. We are fighting ourselves inwardly as much as we will be fighting our opponents on the outside. I will win this battle. It’s the only way. I vow, right here and right now, to win this battle. Eddie Bane will accomplish what he has set out to do. He looked down at the tombstone again. Fritz’ death had held him back. No, he had been holding back because of Fritz’ death. Even in the ring with Stan at the gym, he was holding back. What was it that was causing this? He knew, but he couldn’t admit it. He had tied himself to Fritz like he was now tying himself to Tommy. Damn… this was beginning to look like a pattern, he thought. I’ll be damned if I’m going to let this go on for too awful long. Its time I put my money where my mouth is. Tommy can hold his own… now I have to learn to do the same. I will continue on, even if Tommy calls it quits. I’ve worked too hard for this. The beginning of a smile began to play across his lips. Finally, he had come to a solid decision in his life. He was Eddie Bane. He was the one in control of what he did. It was his life and no one else’s. It was high time he started living it his own way. The cocoon was breaking open… the Gargoyle was being re-birthed. The son was going down and the night was upon him. The stone would become flesh, and the flesh would destroy all around it. He smiled again and slammed his fist into the ground. “Nothing will get in my way, ever again,” he said. He turned and walked away from the tombstone, not looking back. Fritz was gone… dead. He was alive, and he had something to prove. He had been way too soft… no more. He jumped in his car and spun out, leaving the memories in the past. It was a new day… well, no… a new night, and he was on his own crusade. Yes, he was a knight, and his errand was awaiting him. “The red… it filters through…” Tommy sat in the Dark Talon bar and dance club in his corner seat, watching the interaction of the men and women on the dance floor. He was so deep in thought, that he hadn’t even tasted the beer that was in his hand. In fact, the beer was slowly getting hot as he continued to hold it. The waitress would pass by from time to time, asking him if he was ok. He would just nod and continue to stare, letting the dance music put him into a trance-like state. He wasn’t meditating on things guys usually meditate on. The women weren’t on his mind, and neither were sports. Well, one sport was on his mind… wrestling. He had worked himself into a frenzy the last few times he was out there in the ring with Eddie. They had a chemistry that wouldn’t quit. He knew they would be able to light up an arena if they were just pushed right by the promoters. However, right now, he just wanted to get over with the fans at the PTC Pay-Per View. He had become the crusader again, just like before… only this time was different. He wouldn’t be crusading to rid the league of the dregs of society. His new persona could care less about that. Instead, his crusade was going to be for the hearts and minds of those who understood where he was coming from. Those who didn’t, were probably just too stupid, or just didn’t care. Fuck ‘em. What have they done for me? Nothing. I’m the one who has to get in that ring and put my body on the line. I’m the one that has to take the hard hits and then come back for more. All they do is sit their asses on the seats and take up space. True, its their asses on the seats that pays his salary, but who gives a fuck… certainly not him. And what about these people out on the dance floor? Did they even care what he was going through? Did they even know who he was? It doesn’t matter. I’m more talented than most of them anyway, and I’ve got more heart. He finally realized he had a beer in his hand, and took a sip, almost gagging. He spit it back out. “Damn,” he said, then turned and searched for the waitress. “Waitress! I need another beer!” The waitress turned and looked at him, then smiled and headed for the bar. At least he had said something instead of sitting there, brooding all night long. But he would pay for that warm beer. She returned with a cold beer, giving it to him and taking the bottle with the warm beer in it. Tommy thanked her, paying for the beer out of his pocket. He drank it half down, then began studying the people on the floor again. What about the guys you’re going to meet in the ring, he thought to himself. They’re going to give you as much of a beating as Stan and his guys, if not more. Are you prepared to put your body through that again? Are you ready to hit the big leagues again? Indy’s were one thing, but in the big time, you have to work hard, sometimes night in and night out without a break. Can I handle that? Of course I can. Otherwise, I wouldn’t be sitting here right now, enjoying this beer. He took another swig of the beer, finishing it off with a satisfied sigh. “So lay down the threat is real…” On the other side of the bar, at the entrance, Stan Otterman walked in, looking around. When he saw Tommy sitting in the corner booth, he couldn’t help but smile to himself. Tommy had been doing well in the ring, dealing with the problems he and his boys were sending at him, but how would he handle himself out of the ring. How much had he been drinking tonight, wondered Stan? He would just have to see. He moved over to a corner of the bar where he saw four of his old wrestling buddies. “Fellas, I need your help,” he said as he shook their hands. “I got $100 for each of you if you do it.” They all perked up, wondering what it was Stan wanted them to do for $100 a piece. When he explained, they all smiled to themselves. They were going to enjoy this. Stan stepped back to the doorway, to watch as the guys made their way towards Tommy, cracking their knuckles and smiling ruefully. As they approached, Stan walked up to Art and handed him five Ben Franklins. “There’s about to be some action,” he said. “I set it up to test Tommy.” “Damnit,” said Art. “I just had that table fixed.” “Thanks Art,” said Stan, as the guys moved in on Tommy. Art shook his head and motioned for his bouncers to stay back. “Its alright. Happens all the time.” “Hey, punk,” said the leader of the group to Tommy. “Are you too high and mighty to hang out with the rest of society?” “Yeah,” said another one. “You sit back here all the time brooding to yourself and making love to your beer. Maybe we need to teach you that there’s actually life outside your little bubble.” “Fuck off,” said Tommy. The leader threw the table away from Tommy as the others began to surround him. Tommy took the moment to rush the leader and crack him over the head with the beer bottle, shattering it into pieces. The leader went down, holding his head where a gash had been cut into it. The others rushed him, but he caught them off-balance, sending them into the wall, the tables, and out onto the dance floor. They stood back up and rushed in, but Tommy took them down one by one. The first, he connected with a superkick. The guy hit the floor sprawled out and didn’t get back up. The next guy came at him from behind, but didn’t get far as Tommy hit him with a mule kick to the groin. The guy toppled over, clutching his crotch. Tommy then turned to the last of the four, a big, burly guy who hadn’t gotten involved yet. The guy smiled at him and rushed in, putting him in a bear hug. Tommy immediately hit the guy in the ears, causing him to release the hold and grab his ears, staggering backward. Tommy hit him with a superkick, sending him flying back into the bar. They guy looked up with a pissed look. “Oh shit,” said Tommy, as the guy cracked his knuckles and headed for him again. At the entrance, Eddie walked in and looked around. The first thing he saw was Tommy kick the big guy, then the big guy shake off the kick. “Damn,” he said, as he rushed the big guy from behind, hitting him with a spear to the back. They slammed into the opposite wall, barely missing Tommy as they went by. They big man slumped and hit the ground as Eddie got back up. “You ok,” he asked Tommy. “Yeah, thanks,” Tommy replied. He turned and delivered a spinning-wheel kick to the guy whose crotch he had hit, who had been getting up to attack again. “Everything’s fine now.” “What happened,” asked Eddie? “Why did they attack you?” “Seems they didn’t like the fact that I was sitting back here brooding,” said Tommy. “Actually, I was just over here thinking. Ask the waitress, I only drank one beer.” “I believe you, Tommy,” said Eddie. “Lets get out of here before they wake up.” Tommy nodded and they made their way to the door. Stan stepped out just as they approached it and began clapping. “Good work, both of you,” he said. “You passed the test, Tommy.” Tommy looked from Stan to Eddie and back, getting a pissed look. “You had those guys attack me? You son of a bitch!” Tommy took a swing at Stan, but Stan ducked out of the way and began laughing. Eddie grabbed Tommy and pushed him towards the door. “Its alright, Tommy,” he said. “I’ll deal with him. You just go out to the car and cool off.” Tommy shrugged Eddie away, pointing at Stan. “You had no right to do that, you bastard.” He then turned and walked out of the bar. Eddie turned back to Stan. “He’s right, you know. You didn’t have to do that.” “I wanted to see what he had in him,” said Stan. “I also wanted to see if he was drinking again. Obviously he wasn’t.” “Stan, you’re a bastard,” said Eddie. “Yeah, but I’m your bastard,” said Stan. “Look, I already paid for the damage. I finally have Tommy in the mood to fight now, which is what he needed. Consider it a free lesson, Eddie.” Eddie gave Stan a crooked smile and a harrumph. “Whatever, Stan,” he said. “We’ll see you in the gym tomorrow to sort this out.” Eddie turned and walked out the door as Stan began chuckling to himself. “When his sight goes red again…” The next day, Stan was in the gym working with a student in the main ring. He was showing him how to hit the ropes right, and take a fall. Over in the corner, Ernie was with the rest of the students, working out. Suddenly, the doors to the gym burst open and Eddie and Tommy rush the main ring. Eddie takes the student and throws him out of the ring, while Tommy nails Stan with a dropkick, sending him hard to the mat. Eddie then turns around and pulls Stan up by the hair, turning him around and holding his arms. Tommy nails Stan with a superkick, and he goes limp in Eddie’s grip. Eddie then turns him around and powerbombs him as Tommy mounts the top turnbuckle. Eddie stands up and points at Tommy, who sails high into the air, spinning and flipping, landing hard on Stan. The two then high five and exit the ring. Ernie comes rushing over to the ring as they exit. “Get out! Get out now! What the hell are you two thinking, coming in here while we’re running a class and attacking Stan like that?” “Ernie,” said Tommy. “We’re just getting a little payback for what he did last night. If you don’t know, ask him. He got what was coming to him. “Tommy,” said Ernie. “I don’t know what’s going on between you guys, but if you’re going to settle it, settle it in an arena somewhere, or at the bar. I’m not going to have this in my training facility. Now, you guys better leave before I call the cops.” “Suit yourself, Ernie,” said Eddie. “Just remember, Stan had us jumped last night. If he knows what’s good for him, he’ll steer clear of us.” Ernie looked at Stan sprawled out in the ring, then at the two pupils. He shook his head and began chuckling. “I think you two are ready now,” he said. “Take the day off. I’ll have a talk with Stan. You looked beautiful in there, and if I wasn’t so pissed at you, I’d go share a beer with you. Now get out, before I change my mind.” Tommy and Eddie smile and turn, leaving Ernie and all of the students gawking at their handy work.
