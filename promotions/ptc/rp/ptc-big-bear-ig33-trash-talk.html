He’s from Massachusetts for cryin’ out loud! You know what kind of people come from there, right Mom? Yeah, exactly! NO-GOOD-ASS-PIRATES! You said it that time, Mom. Not me. The new reign, the new regime of the Infinite Gauntlet. The Big Bear administration, ready and willing to crush skulls and beat some ass in order to preserve the glory and greatness that is the IG Championship. I will be damned if Hunchblack, von Doom, Silver, Tchu, or even Hall gets to touch that belt before I pry it from Snow’s cold grip. The fact is simple and sweet – nobody’s safe from the Big Bear and the Big Bear gold rush. ---- 8 July 2007 IG 33: BIG BEAR, SERIOUSFACE!
