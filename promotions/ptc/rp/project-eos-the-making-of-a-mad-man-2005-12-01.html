Project Eos: The Making of a Mad Man

"But everyone knows who you are Mr. Gilchrist." "No, they just think they do." # Welcome to Aurora. Picture a small, peaceful, almost too quiet town – the kind of town you see in Hollywood movies where nothing could go wrong and you’d have a small glimpse of what Aurora really is. If the streets of America were truly paved with gold, Aurora would be the most tell-tell sign of that. Not that the streets of Aurora are actually gold – hell, they’re filled with as many potholes as New York – but the city life has led many to start on their new dreams. But for all its schooling systems, city life, crime rate, and beautiful scenery, Aurora is most famous for being a hoax town. Maybe it’s the fact that it’s surrounded by nature on every front – a sea to the east, mountain ranges towards the north, and forests to the west and south – or maybe it’s that the town gets little excitement from anything else. Whatever the reason, no town in America, on Earth, has ever had as many tabloid tales of urban myths and legends. However, all the changed in the latter decade as experts and myth busters from all over began to slowly and methodically dissect each of Aurora’s supposed monsters. Aurora slowly began to lose its mystique and a once great tourist attraction became just another town. Like so many towns in North Carolina, or Florida, it just became another pretty smalltime town to raise your family. But all that would change in the ending weeks of November. Unbeknownst to them thousands of miles away in an unmapped jungle Dr. Colin Gilchrist was resurrecting an old secret from the grave. A secret he bought with him to Aurora, along with his science, his research, his assistants, salvation, mystique, and a new sense of awe to a town lacking it. Dr. Gilchrist bought with him GTT5. Now the town had a representative in the biggest tournament in the world. Everyone was bound to gain something from this, from the stores selling jerseys, to the mayor raking in revenues from tourist attractions. Even Dr. Gilchrist had something to gain from this. The GTT is, was, and always be the mold that breaks every competition. With Dr. Gilchrist as their representative, Aurorians were ready to tell their tale of victory. And much like any story, there were two sides to this one… # In the outer edges of Aurora, Dr. Gilchrist has fashioned his latest research center. Inside the iron defenses of a an advanced security system, solid steel walls, and endless terrain for miles, Dr. Gilchrist makes advances in science that no one should be able to. Inside, he oversees his assistants and workers like a slave driver wielding a cat-o-nine tails. But sometimes, even Dr. Gilchrist must change roles and be the one doing the explaining. The automatic doors opened as Dr. Gilchrist led his guests through the main hall, a section of his lab where only the most privileged had access to. With Colin Gilchrist, were his best friend and most trusted assistant, Demetri Vieri, and Mr. Lee from the Department of Defense. While this was Gilchrist’s house, it was Mr. Lee who seemed more at home. "You have quite a research center, Dr. Gilchrist." Mr. Lee said, making observations. "I don’t think that even NASA has this much money in their pockets." Demetri chuckled and Colin quickly motioned him to stop. "Which is precisely why I’m here," Mr. Lee said, as they continued to walk through the research center. "Mr. Lee, I assure you everything is under control." Dr. Gilchrist replied. "Yes, everything should be as you requested within a matter of months," Demetri chimed in, unaware that his best participation in this might be to stay quiet. Mr. Lee had a stern, almost rude look to him. "You want a couple of more months? You promised to have results soon. It’s been two years now." "I-" Dr. Gilchrist began, but was quickly cut off. "The United States Government doesn’t simply donate money on promises. No, we make up a story and then feed it to the public." Mr. Lee said. "Just give me more time and more money, Mr. Lee." Dr. Gilchrist asked, "And I assure you, you will see the results of our project soon." Mr. Lee shook his head. "You’ve had enough time and money to make good on your research. I’ve been tracking you down the minute you graduated college, Dr. Gilchrist." "What are you getting at?" Colin asked, perturbed. "You haven’t exactly been lazy," Mr. Lee explained. "You have all this for a reason. It’s obvious that you have used the resources we gave you, but it weren’t for us. But all that is going to change. The United States Government will be pulling back all funds, resources, and manpower from our failed relationship. When I get back to the Pentagon, all evidence of our relationship will be erased." "What if we had something to show you?" Demetri blurted out. "What if we could guarantee you a remarkable find? Something that might even surpass what you’ve been looking for?" "Demetri!" Colin shouted. "This can’t be right, Colin." Demetri replied. "You know we have done too much to just let this pass away." Mr. Lee let out a half smile. It was only a half smile because Mr. Lee rarely ever smiles, the kind of guy who has no humor he’s aware of. "There is something. Word around the Pentagon is that you were involved in a pretty interesting adventure down in the Biminy jungles. In fact, we hear nearly two dozen specially trained marines were murdered. There might be a way you can save your money there. What exactly did you find there?" Dr. Gilchrist shook his head. "We didn’t find anything. It was just a waste of time." Demetri rubbed his fingers together. He didn’t know what Colin was up to. He knew they found something in the jungle. He knew all too well what is was that they found. Mr. Lee sighed. "Very well, it was a pleasure doing business-" "Wait…" Dr. Gilchrist replied, interrupting. "We didn’t find anything there, but we still have something that could change your mind." # To understand Dr. Gilchrist, you must go back to the beginning. Not the beginning of his birth, or his youth, or even his upbringing. There’s nothing remarkable about his past. There’s no sob story of being orphaned, losing his parents, killing them, being addicted to video games, or even tales of an ex girlfriend who betrayed him with his brother. In fact, he didn’t even have a brother. So, clearly the story will never go the way of an old flame returning to try to squeeze more money out of him. No, Colin Gilchrist’s story is as common as they come. There was nothing in his past that would explain why he would become such a vile and deranged man. His parents were loving. He experienced no kind of adolescent setback. He never got dumped. He never got bullied around. Somehow, he just reverted to a kind of senseless and remorseless man. It was almost as if he went through with his own activism – a phenomenon that occurs once in a hundred generations: reverting back to your primitive self. The beginning of this story begins in college. In John Hopkins University, the most acclaimed medical school in the nation, Colin Gilchrist was doing very well for himself. Soon, he was going to add romance to his wheel of success. Studying medicine herself, to be a pediatrician, Veronica LeBlanc was the most beautiful woman he had ever seen. He wasn’t a dud himself. He expressed the kind of stereotypical look that most woman find attractive in a man. He was tall, had a great smile, nice eyes, was in very good shape, and his black locks just bounced from his head as if he were a walking shampoo commercial. There were many college coeds who looked to him for adventure, and he greatly obliged to them. But his heart wasn’t in them, at least not as much as his warrior, and that there lies the problem. Veronica was never attracted to him. At least, not the kind of way Colin hoped she could be. The way she would eventually be. Veronica always dismissed his advances for many reasons, but never did she tell him the truth. She was scared of him. Colin wasn’t the friendliest person in the world. He had a violent temper that preceded him. Even more damning than that was the rumor of his excessive alone time. He would spend hours each night alone in the University’s lab. There certainly wasn’t anything charming about him. He lived almost like a recluse and his good looks only hid a fraction of what he really was. # "This had better be remarkable," Mr. Lee said. "It is." Colin replied as he inputted a security code opening a pair of gargantuan doors. Mr. Lee had been led to a part of the research center that not even he knew about. The main overseer centers were already high-class and very privileged for just anyone to go into. This area was even more secure than those and the fact that not even he knew about it made Mr. Lee a bit anxious of what he was to find. As they walked through the gargantuan doors, they came upon a majestic room. The ceiling was nearly ten stories above them but the most remarkable feature was that the room almost had no floor. In fact, the room was actually a giant covering for a pool double the size of any Olympics pool. Mr. Lee walked ahead to the security rail. He leaned over to look down at the shady waters. "What is this?" Mr. Lee asked. "This is one of our pet projects," Demetri explained. "But rather than tell you about, what say we show him to you?" Dr. Gilchrist asked. Mr. Lee nodded. "Well gentlemen, impress me." "Just pay attention to the waters, Mr. Lee." Dr. Gilchrist said. Colin took out a remote control from his coat pocket and pressed a red button. Mr. Lee looked down into the waters and saw a small flash appear in the water. The suddenly a large shadow loomed underneath and slowly moved farther away into the pool. The shadow swam farther from them, but it was obviously surfacing. "Mind telling me what this is?" "A picture is worth a thousand words," Colin answered him. The looming shadow eventually grew enormous and in the far end of the pool, Mr. Lee saw a serpent-like head break through the surface of the water. "T-that’s-" Mr. Lee didn’t finish his thoughts as he saw the long neck of the serpent rise above the water. Eventually, the creature’s entire back was surfacing to the water. Mr. Lee watched in awe as this remarkable sea creature swam by him so close that he could clearly see the flippers at his side and the long tail-finned tail it swung behind itself. "Is that…" "We call him, Nessie." Demetri answered. "It was only right. After all, his name means so much to so many people." "This is remarkable," Mr. Lee said. "Is it real?" "About as real as you and me," Colin answered. "We found him in an underwater cavern in Scotland. You would think with all the time those experts there spend trying to prove he doesn’t exist, they would have found him." "Her." "Her?" Mr. Lee asked. Colin nodded. "Nessie is a female." Mr. Lee breathed a sigh of satisfaction. "I think you may have just earned yourself more time, Dr. Gilchrist." "There’s more." Mr. Lee raised his eyebrows. Exposing the Loch ness monster to him was an extraordinary find in itself. What on earth could Dr. Gilchrist have that could surpass even that? Demetri, too, looked at the doctor suspiciously. Even after all these years, and knowing how truly deranged his friend was, Demetri was still surprised by Colin’s actions. "There’s something else you should see." # Eventually, Veronica LeBlanc began to fall for Colin Gilchrist. She finally began to see that his mind, his inner self, and everything else about him were as good as his looks. But she never once questioned why she began to fall for him. Not after her closest friends all urged her that she was doing something wrong. They didn’t trust Colin. Colin didn’t trust them either. Colin wasn’t alone in his conquest. Demetri, too, began to win over the love of a woman he long yearned for: Lisa Mellows. In their senior years at John Hopkins University, both relationships took a giant leap forward. Demetri was so in love with Lisa, he asked her to marry him. She said yes and midway through their senior year, they got married. It was certainly good news for both of them and even so for Colin and Veronica. For Colin, Demetri marrying the woman he wanted was nothing less than a sure sign that he was going to enjoy life with Veronica. For Veronica, it bought to her a new kind of happiness. Her stubborn determination to stay with Colin had deserted her of nearly all her friends. She was rapidly becoming the kind of person she once feared Colin to be. She was easily agitated, enraged, and often vengeful. She had gone a complete transformation in a matter of months. By summer, things were only going to get worst. Without even the consent or guidance of her parents, Veronica saw her life as a lost cost. And in, like a true knight, swept in Colin Gilchrist. He assured her that she had everything in the world to live for. She was so much better than anyone who had deserted her. Their desertion, he said, was a sign that they were never her friends. Veronica knew it. She loved him then more than ever. So, it was only natural that she said yes to his proposal. As she graduated John Hopkins University, with a Masters in Laboratory Medicine with emphasis on Health Sciences, she became Mrs. Veronica Gilchrist. # Demetri walked closed to Colin as Mr. Lee trailed a few feet behind them. "What’s going on here, Colin?" Demetri whispered. "You know we can’t let this guy go out alive." Colin replied. "Yes, but we can still get the money." Demetri said. "We need a lot of money." "More than he can give us?" "It’s not just the money. Everything is falling apart." "Everything?" "Everything." "So what are we doing?" "We’re going to introduce Mr. Lee to him." Behind them, Mr. Lee cleared his throat. "What’s going on?" Demetri answered. "All is fine, Mr. Lee. We should get arrive at our destination behind these doors." They walked through another heavily fortified set of doors and this time walked into a damp room. Well, Mr. Lee did. As they entered the room, Colin and Demetri both exited the room and left Mr. Lee alone. The room was more desolate than it was dark. There were obvious signs of something being restrained in there. Mr. Lee looked around and noticed that he wasn’t accompanied. "What the hell is going on?" Mr. Lee shouted, demanding to know answers. Above him, a thin line of yellow lights turned on slightly illuminating the room. In the distance, he could make out a crumpled over figure of some sort. The speakers also came to life with the laughing of Dr. Gilchrist. "Dr. Gilchrist, you are a mad man if you think this is going to-" "I don’t think this is going to do anything, Mr. Lee." Colin spoke on the PA system. "I know it will do something. You see, you were right about me. I have used all the resources you gave me. I have made some interesting advances. But they were all just for me." "You sonofabitch!" "Don’t act so surprised. You knew it all along. That’s why you came here. You came here to stop all my resources and steal anything of importance to you. That’s the real reason you came here, isn’t it?" "How did.." "How did I know? You embarrass me with such questions. I’ve been nothing less than spectacular during our business relationship. As a man, I’ve overcome nearly every obstacle on every level. And now, you’re trying to take it away from me? You have no idea just how much I have, or how little I have." "What are you going to do? What can you possibly do?" "I’m losing a lot these days. All you have seen today will eventually go away in a few months. My income is slowly deteriorating and my family has no idea of how soon we’ll be unable to afford many of our belongings. That’s why I’m in the GTT5. The exposure and revenue I’ll rake from that competition will be like a life saver tossed at me." "You’re going to waste all the resources the government has given you on some stupid tournament?" "Yes." "You’re insane. What kind of an idiot joins that kind of thing?" "This kind of idiot, Mr. Lee. I don’t think it’s in your best interest to make threats now. I’ve already told you all you need to know and more. And now, I’m going to tell you more. Remember when you asked me if I found anything in the Biminy jungles? I did find something. It was really extraordinary." "I knew it." "You don’t know the half of it. There were others there that found it, too. But one of them managed to capture it like I did. In fact, they were all murdered. But there was one who survived, one whom I chose to save. I found it in the best interest to combine both of our efforts." "What are you getting at?" "I’m telling you that our super soldier project is more along than you think. That lone soldier that survived the mayhem in Biminy? He was volunteered for our cause." Mr. Lee turned his attention away from the sky box above him and towards a ruffling noise in the room. The shadowy figure he had seen in the distance had moved. He quickly spun around trying to catch it again and saw it at the other end of the room, slowly making its way towards him. "What the hell have you done, Dr. Gilchrist?" Mr. Lee shouted, frightened. "I’ve turned that soldier, Alexander Jackson, into a primitive being. He’s no longer human, Mr. Lee. But he is very obedient." Mr. Lee turned around to see the once Marine, Jackson staggering next to him, as if he were just beginning to learn to walk. There was nothing frightening about his appearance. His skin was a bit pale, but the most telling signs were on his face. His lips were almost blue, from a lack of oxygen, his eyes were glazed over, and his teeth were unusually sharp for a human. Mr. Lee noticed just how different this young kid was, when he grabbed Mr. Lee by the neck with one hand and threw him several meters across the room. "Y-You won’t get away with this. They’re expecting me back at the Pentagon. You have no idea how much shit you are in." Dr. Gilchrist laughed hysterically on the loud speaker. "No idea? It is you who do not have any idea what shit he is in. You think I’m some kind of bumbling idiot who still plays with bath toys? You think I’m some kind cartoon doctor? You have no idea who I am. You label me, try to assure others that you know my real identity, but you don’t know anything about me. I’m not who you think I am. I’ve got news for, Mr. Lee. You won’t be missed. You see, the Pentagon is not expecting you back. They asked me to make sure of it." Jackson reached down for Mr. Lee, who valiantly tried to fight back. But in one swift move, the mindless Jackson broke Mr. Lee’s left arm and held him by the neck far above the ground. He snarled and showed his newly acquired teeth. "There is one more thing you need to know about your new friend, Mr. Lee. That is, he has a developed a taste for human flesh." Mr. Lee screamed as he was helpless to stop Jackson from biting a huge chunk out of his neck. Jackson let the body of Mr. Lee drop the floor and watched as his prey quivered on the floor with blood gushing from his neck. But rather than wait for his prey to finish dying, Jackson crumpled down and began to eat Mr. Lee alive. # With the ordeal with Mr. Lee behind them, Colin and Demetri were left to discuss matters in Colin’s office. "Are you ready for GTT5?" Demetri asked. "No." Colin replied, quickly. "It’s more than just being ready. It’s about being able to execute. It’s about being able to be focused." "I never thought you would actually get into that tournament," Demetri said. "You’re in good shape, but the GTT is more than just being in shape, it’s about being on an entirely different level." "And you don’t think I’ve accomplished that?" "In what way? This isn’t some kind of random event. There are people there who defy physics." "And I haven’t?" Colin asked. You saw with your very eyes what I do to people." "But you said you weren’t ready," Demetri said. "How are you going to handle it?" "I’ve already thought it through," Colin said. "We both know I’m not in any shape to win this thing, but our friend is." "Him?" Demetri asked. "He’s still very unstable. His emotional factor is only monitored at 70 percent. There’s no telling what could go wrong with him." "And through him, I’m going to win GTT5. I’ve already made arrangements to wrestle under a mask and outfit. If anything goes wrong, there’s always a way to fix those problems." "Indeed." "He will be wrestling under a name I’ve decided to use: The Savior." # The first round of the GTT5 was finally underway. In a few hours, half of the matches in each of the four brackets would get started. The atmosphere was certainly loud and electric at the GTT5’s fourth bracket arena, where Dr. Gilchrist was set to do battle with Sam Wolack, representing GCW. Of all the avid fans, there was one of particular notice. He was a short, stout man with short brown hair. He was dressed rather sharply, but acted as if he were in jeans and a t-shirt. He was one of the columnists for the Aurora Post, and wrote under the pseudonym, the Masked Slammer. He quickly made his way through the crowd when he says Aurora’s hope, Dr. Gilchrist. "Mr. Gilchrist!" the Masked Slammer shouted, getting his attention. "What is it?" "I just wanted to get a few words from you, before you have to leave for your match," The Masked Slammer explained. "All right. But make it brief." "This is all off the record," the Masked Slammer replied. "The GTT5 is a big event and people don’t need to be reminded about it. I just want to know what you are feeling right now. As you go get ready to do battle with Sam Wolack, what’s going on in your mind?" Others were now being attracted t the doctor like moths to a flame. One by one they began to recognize him and began to acknowledge him. Sure, he wasn’t as famous or as well known to the GTT crowd as was Seymour Almasy, Angelo Deville, or Doctor Curiosity. He wasn’t successful…yet. Dr, Gilchrist saw the attention he was receiving and behind his false smile, he planned just how fruitful this attention would be. He saw before him the glowing eyes of youths wanting to be in the tournament, a sense of lust from women who just want to ride a real winner, and the hope of a man who assured an entire town that Dr. Gilchrist was going to win. He saw all that, but in his mind he saw nothing but a bunch of dumb, sheepish, and ugly people. The GTT was only the beginning of his real conquest. It was only the beginning of his real destiny. He found his destiny in the jungles of the Biminy and GTT5 was going to be a start. "Well…" the Masked Slammer said, pleading for Colin to hurry up. Colin cleared his throat. "What’s going on in my mind? Well, now isn’t that something. Well, everyone knows how big this is, at least they think they do. I used to think I knew how big this was and how ready I was. I was wrong. This was bigger than I expected. But, I’m still ready for it. What’s going on through my mind is how I’m going to not only beat Sam Wolack, but everyone else in this year’s tournament. The only thing going on in my mind? Total Victory." The crowd surrounding him cheered, and a few jeers were mixed in, most of them girls wearing "The Deville was here" T-shirts. Dr. Gilchrist raised his hand in acknowledge, as if he just made some kind of historic civil rights speech. Once again he smiled at them, the same fake smile he always had. If only…if only his story were as fake… # Carl Weathers, a black fat security guard, cursed under his breath. He was always given the duties of making the boiler room rounds under the GTT5 arena. As one of the new, less experienced cops on the force, he was often assigned to the less creditable jobs. He cursed more as he heard the thunderous crowd above him. The GTT5 was underway, no doubt with explosive matches, and here he was walking through the hot, humid, noisy, and damp place of the boiler room. Carl was just about to leave the room, when he noticed a door opened. It was the door to one of the gage calibration rooms. He sighed and walked back to check the opened door. As he walked inside he began to see that the room was a bit out of place. Some of the pipes had dents in them and all the reports and manuals were scattered around the floor. Carl quickly hovered his hand over his pistol, there was a struggle here. He swooped in low around the corner and saw a pair of legs on the floor. He summoned all his courage and leaped forward. He screamed. In front of him was a young man, viciously murdered. His body was bent at an unusual position, his arm was bent backwards, and his head was barely attached to his neck. In fact, his head leaned towards one side in a sickening image. Carl quickly reached for his radio. "Central!" Carl shouted, his radio experiencing static." "Central! Come in! This is Rover 12.
