Prelude of Omina Part 1

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 “Hello! I’m Mina de Margo, age 24.” Inphino’s manager was focused in the camera with a black backdrop looming over her in the distance. The black and red haired woman bowed, a bit of cleavage showing from the narrow slit allowed in the collar of her red blouse. Next, Mina was standing near a traditional Japanese sliding doorway made with tan oak and white paper screens. In a pair of sweats and white tank top, she cradled the silver cell phone to her ear as she pressed the free one to the paper. “I don’t know…” she whispered careful not to be heard. “I think… Inphino might have actually lost his mind.” Various images flash across the screen, showing Mina de Margo in corporate attire in hues of black and red and white, from pants to skirts to dresses; always with the black framed glasses on her face, perched at the narrow end of her nose teetering. She’s shown sitting at a desk going through files, followed by another of her giving a presentation in front of a room of suits. The next shows her with the sleeves of her white blouse rolled up as she shows a young Japanese woman how she sets things to scale; her hand forming a box to view through. The near blonde haired woman nodded with a ditzy smile while Mina explained whatever details she deemed important. “Mina de Margo over the course of time,” informed a bold narrator that interjected on this slideshow, “has risen to success in the Japanese entertainment industry, all thanks to one man.” Cutting back to Mina in front of the black background, she looked upwards pondering the answer to a pitched question. “Inphino Blitz did really good work. I was impressed with him the minute he stepped on the scene. But of course he wouldn’t be in the position he’s in today without me.” Nothing but pearly whites lined up behind the smiling lips of Mina. She was proud of the work she did… maybe even a bit too proud of the matter that she made his career. Money power and success seemed to blind those from the truth of the matter. BRRRINNNNG! Rolling her eyes with a sigh, Mina crept away from the sliding door into the main hallway. The phone continued to wail out for her until she turned left around the corner and was smack dab in front of the table where the phone rested. “Hang on,” she told the cell phone caller as she took up the main line. “Hello?” Her brow wrinkled as she listened to the speaker. Then she said hold on, walking back around the corner to the sliding door where she was hunched before. Rapping her knuckle against one of the posts, she slid the door open a crack to peer in. “Phone,” she announced, staring intently at Inphino. Blitz sat in the middle of a barren room with a black acoustic guitar in hand. He plucked at the strings lightly, eyes closed and hair hanging around his face tangled. When Mina spoke, he slowly glanced up through black and white locks. “Wh’ is it?” he grunted under his breath. With a huff she shifted her weight to her left side. “It’s Jamar Gordo, one of the dudes in the tournament.” He stared at her for a short moment before his head lowered back to his guitar. “Hell he want?” Mina shrugged. “Didn’t ask. You taking the call or what?” She watched him sit there in silence for a while before he started strumming at the strings again. Rolling her eyes, she slid the door back shut and walked back around the corner to the phone. “Yeah uh… can I take a message? He’s a little busy right now.” Producing a pen from the draw built into the small table, she began to copy the message down for Inphino. “Glad to… see you putting …value upon this… competition. You are… a real… pureblood athlete… in today’s day of actors…” She stood up straight, cell phone ready to hit the right ear as she tried to hang up the left. “Yeah, okay thanks… yeah he’ll get it… yeah don’t worry about it… okay yeah, BYE.” Slamming the receiver down on its black charger, she sighed before taking up the other phone. “Sorry, had another call. But what were you saying?” With her original conversation picked up again, she glanced back in the direction that Inphino was in. “No, I know he’s like… not like everyone else and all, but it’s just been lately that he’s been… acting strange… er. “After OSE’s demise, it was my idea to move Inphino into other areas.” Mina explained things as a mute reel played showing Mina walking around in front of filming cameras in a black suit pointing things out to the crew, Inphino standing nearby in a black suit with black shades. He looked generally uninterested in the whole thing as Mina pushed the opposing emotion, her entire heart resonating on her face as she glowed while giving orders for how a scene should be filmed. “I said to him first that maybe he should try movies. Normally when a wrestler moves out of the industry he turns into an action star because he’s a big dude. But I didn’t want Inphino to become another action star. I wanted him to be an actor that had some definition besides what’s in his abs.” Mina stood before Inphino now, a script in her hand folded back to display a page. She shifted to stand close at his side, holding the stapled book up to his view to read. Inphino stood with his arms folded across his chest, eyes tired and careless. He glanced down at the script before looking up at the people passing by. A few fans began to take notice and waved in his direction. Giving a short nod with no expression, he turned his back and started to walk away without saying a word to Mina. She looked up to see him walking away, and then noticed the people and rushed off to shoo them away herself. Back to the scene of Mina at the backdrop… “If I had to describe myself in one word… it’d be… savvy.” Mina was shown alongside Blitz yet again. This time Mina was in a long flowing red dress with matching heels, her hair pulled back in a ponytail while Inphino was in black slacks with a white dress shirt untucked with the lapels flared out over his black jacket. Mina stepped forward to greet the other suits at that dinner party, giving chic kisses on the cheek before leading them towards Inphino, who hadn’t moved an inch the whole time. “Aside from movies, I moved a bit into music. Inphino’s very musically inclined; a lot of people don’t know that. He can play violin and guitar, and he’s learning the drums right now. I’m hoping that sometime in the near future that he’ll release a CD of the work he’s put together with other artists. I hope to use that to market my own music label, and then merge that into the film industry and start my own entertainment company.” Inphino soon found himself alone as Mina went on to shmooze with the neckties and the stockings. Walking over to the bar slowly, he sat down and raised his finger to the bartender. Right away the red vested attendant placed a black cup of sake in front of him, but before he could take the bottle away Inphino took it from him and placed it on the left of him. Shrugging his shoulders, the barkeep went back on to attend other customers. Inphino was fine as long as he had his one friend right there by his side… And in the home of Inphino Blitz, Mina de Margo was back in front of the door perched with a wary ear to the door. “What… is he DOING?” she uttered under her breath with her phone cupped around her mouth and ear. “I think he’s just sitting there with the door open to the yard… but it sounds like he’s… TALKING to someone.” To be Continued
