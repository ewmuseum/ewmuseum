A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 7

24 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-24 The last couple of days had been some of the best in his life. In their lives. They spent as much time together as humanly possible, nothing would ruin their happiness. It still felt like the gods had finally found it to reward him with good fortune, something he had never been able to have. Every color, every feeling, every touch seemed to be amplified but his new found optimism. For once in his life he was not thinking about how long it would last like this, but he kept thinking about what he could do to make her happy. Her happiness was the most important thing in his life now. He would work overtime to obtain it, because if she was happy, he was happy. Their relationship was unique, special and Digital was sure that he would never find anyone as perfect for him as Veronica. He was already thirty-one years old and he wasn’t getting younger each year. Most of his life had been spent on wrestling and becoming the best professional wrestler there is, by any means necessary. One of the reasons he knew she was the one, besides the way she made him feel deep down inside, was because she was one of the few people that knew him to the core. When one has some level of fame, one wonders if the person that you meet is just interested in money or fame, but he knew that this was not the case with Veronica. She genuinely loved him and the feelings were reciprocated by Digital. This girl not only knew the Digital Mortality that everyone knew. The determined, give it his all attitude that characterized him and separated him from most of the other wrestlers in the business. She knew and accepted the man who had done horrific things in the past but was trying to change his ways. She also knew Antonio Vargas, the man behind the name, and the fame. Digital took the glass with the champagne from the table and took a sip from it. He watched as Veronica talked with her sister. She had gotten much better after her head had suffered a bad impact against the edge of a marble wall. That moment had started the whole thing with the father. He did it unintentionally, but he did it anyways. Christian and Burton were there, both talking, probably about music. That was probably one of the things that they had in common, both had metal hearts. Digital was sat down on the chair besides the table that held the food. Veronica’s mother, after hearing the news of their engagement had decided to invite them over to her house and have a small gathering. She saw Digital sitting by himself and she sat down by his side. She looked at her daughter and smiled. She was happy that at least someone from her family was happy, and she was sure that Digital made Vero happy. "She looks happy. Happier than I have ever seen her. It is all thanks to you." At that moment Veronica looked over to Digital and her mother and smiled. Every time she smiled something inside of Digital kept telling him that his heart had been right, that his heart had taken him to the woman of his dreams. After the smile, she turned back towards her sister and they both started giggling. They were probably talking about Digital at that very moment. "All that I want in this life is to make your daughter happy, Raquel. It doesn’t matter what it takes. I will make it my mission in life." At this, Raquel had to laugh. If there was something that her thirty five years of marriage had taught her about, it was happiness… or the lack thereof. Alberto had spent most of his life on the search for salvation for himself and every other that went to his church… that paid the penance. He had spent almost no time though, keeping the family that he had at home together. She knew what the sacrifices of marriage entailed. Digital and Veronica were still young, still in the part of the relationship were nothing the other does is wrong. It would change, she knew. It would change with time and it would be hard for them to get it all back together before it fell completely apart. "I don’t mean to pry but can I ask you a question?" "Sure, ask what you want," she looked at his eyes trying to find something… "Why did you stay with him? Why didn’t you leave him long ago when he first started to make your lives hard?" She sighed deeply. It was probably a question that she had asked herself many times before. A question that Veronica had probably asked before. She always gave the same answer, to herself and to everyone else. Although she didn’t know if she was lying to herself. "I still love Alberto. You have to understand our history. In order for us to get married we had to run away together. My mother wanted nothing to do with Alberto back in the day. She always told me how the decision I made was bad, how the marriage wouldn’t last. I promised myself that no matter what I would make it work. It is hard… it has been hard throughout the years but I had been able to keep it together… until now. There are lines that can’t be crossed. He crossed them when he put his hands on my two daughters." Digital understood. If there was something that would make him make a life changing decision it was someone putting their hands on his fiancée. It was an awful feeling when a loved one is hurt and you are not there to shield and protect them from said evil. "I an understand that, but you knew that your daughters were unhappy and Christian had a confrontation with him about the church he led. Didn’t you think that at that moment it was time to say that it was over?" "I know what-" At that moment Digital could smell the alcohol coming from the front door as it was opened in a furious way. Alberto stood in the entrance to the house, his hair was unkempt and the shirt he used had beer stains all over. The eyes were not those of a man, but of a monster. The eyes seemed like those of a man possessed by something out of this world. He walked hurriedly towards Digital and stood his ground. Digital waited for him to make the first move. He could see madness in his eyes. He hesitated. As drunk as he was, instinct told him that he would get nowhere by attacking Digital. He could see that his ‘enemy’ was bigger and a much better fighter. His tongue was his strength, but alcohol had crippled it. He looked at his wife and his look made her turn away in tears. He decided to prey on someone weaker than him. He moved towards his daughters, before Digital had made his move Burton and Christian were protecting Adriana. He got in front of them and looked towards Veronica. "I don’t want you marrying that man! He is not right for you in my eyes and in the eyes of God!" Before she could answer him, he attacked her with surprising quickness. His slap sent her to the ground before any of the three men got the change of holding him. After the slap he was restrained by Burton and Christian while Digital was on the ground attending to Veronica. Adriana and Raquel were both crying huddled next to each other. Digital tried to help her up, but she pushed him back. He stepped back with a look of shock in his face. At that moment he felt like he had done something wrong. He feared that she would blame him for not stopping her father. It was his fault. He should have tackled him as soon as he came through that door. He never brought anything good with him. All these thoughts passed through Digital’s head through that second. After that, Veronica had stood up and prostrated herself in front of her father and slapped him herself. She looked bigger than all of the people there at that moment. Her eyes were red with anger, as she stared down her father. "Who the hell do you think you are to tell me who I can marry or who I cannot? You come here with that father attitude, but for the last twenty years of my life you have been no father to me. You cared more about that church of your than you did us. You exorcised Christian’s room because you thought he was possessed, you almost had your way with my career and you almost sent Adriana to that school of your church that only teaches how to criticize others for their beliefs. You have been no father to me." Burton, Christian and Digital were all stunned at this outburst. She had always been the only one who protected him from all the criticism he got from the other family members. She had always been daddy’s little girl in his own mind and in everyone’s mind. "I will marry him because I love him. If that means breaking all ties with you then so be it. I won’t leave him because of you. You have done enough already to keep me unhappy, I won’t let you ruin my life." She was defending him. At that moment he understood. She had to step up and show that she could live her own life. She had to show everyone that she was not with Digital because she needed to be protected but because she loved him. She didn’t need him, she wanted him. No words were spoken but the tension could still be felt in the air. Her father gave her a look so full of contempt that she almost started to cry. She was strong though and was able to hold her ground. He moved towards the door, rage in his eyes. He couldn’t believe what was going on. "You all are possessed and the demon is right in front of you all! That man, your so called loved one, is the devil in disguise. That monster hurts other people for a job, what more proof do you want? It is so clear! All our problems started when this devil arrived. It is testing us like he tested Christ. Don’t fall for his tricks!" His words fell on deaf ears. His powers of manipulation had disappeared. He looked at all the faces and could not believe that everyone just stood there and did nothing about the demon in their midst. He was the only sane one in the room and he had to do something, for the sake of his family. He promised himself something right then and there. He would never sleep until he had rid his family of the devil that controlled them. It was his mission to save them. Evil could try to take his family away from him but not for long, because he was going to try his hardest. Another day though. He may have won this battle but they would lose the war. "Ok, hagan lo que les de la gana. Pero un día de estos se darán cuenta que ese hombre es un demonio." He left. Christian went over to his mother and sister and tried to console them. Their faces were wet from all the tears that had streamed down their faces. Burton could do nothing else except to sit down in a chair and drink his champagne while he processed things in his head. Veronica could do nothing more and she just hugged Digital and held to him tightly. All the strength she had seemed to have been drained from her body. She couldn’t believe what had just happened. Her father had simply gone completely crazy. "I’m sorry. I’m sorry that I couldn’t stop him before he hit you. I was so-" She interrupted him before he could finish. "Don’t worry. I think I needed it. It was time for me to stand up to him. His slap made me realize that I needed to do it. Your presence here also gave me the strength to do it. If you hadn’t been here, I would have just cried on the floor while he then started to beat up my sister or my mother." "For a second there I thought you were mad at me. When you pushed me, it really surprised me. Have you been getting training from someone behind my back?" They both laughed. It was a nervous laugh though. "Thank you, Digi. Thank you for everything. You have changed my life. You have put things in motion that should have happened years ago. You are special. I love you." "I love you too." They kissed. It felt good, to be loved. Digital walked over towards Burton who was still sitting there staring into oblivion. Digital sat down besides him and tapped him on the shoulder. "You okay?" "I don’t know… is it just me or did we somehow walk into a nuthouse without knowing?" He had no idea. "You have been around me too long to think of this as crazy. We have gone through some really strange places you and I. This is just life. This has taught me something. I know what I won’t do when I become a father. I wouldn’t want my children to think of me as everyone thinks of Alberto. At least we got it out of the way now. It would have been bad if he had arrived at the wedding and ruined it." "What makes you think he won’t go?" He was right. Burton was always right. He would not stop until he saw that his daughter had not married Digital. He would never let the "devil" marry his daughter. It didn’t matter to Digital though. It didn’t matter to him if some god himself came down and tried to stop him. He had found something that not everybody could find. He found the love of his life. He had always thought that she had died two years ago, but she was here right now. She was about to marry him as well. The last couple of days had been some of the best in his life, and the days to come would be much better.
