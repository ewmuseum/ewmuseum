Unnecessary Exposition

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 --------------- Qwest Field Seattle, WA 4:28 PM --------------- A cup of beer splashes to the ground, the plastic cracks and pops, the beer splashes on the back of the heads of two men painted in dark blue and neonish green. Huge Seahawk fanatics. They both turn around, equally pissed. Oh shit! They quickly turn back to the field and begin to cheer with 70,000 other Seahawk fans. Stecchino: They’ll choke -- They always do. The same fans turn back around, they shoot Tony a not so nice look. Tony raises his eyebrows and shrugs them both off. You know they always do you stupid son of a bitch. They were eight and two in 1999 and they lost five out of six and backed into the playoffs and lost to an old Dan Marino and the Dolphins. Shit I was three when they last won a playoff game I think… Tony motions for the beer man. Stecchino: Two more. The beer man shakes him off. BeerMan: No beer, just soda. Stecchino: You’re kidding. BeerMan: We stop at halftime, don’t want fights. Tony waves his hand. The beer man continues on his way. I want a beer and I’d fight for it. Tony kicks the seat in front of him. The Seahawk fan turns around for the third time. Stecchino: It’s going to Overtime, what more do you want? Watch the coin toss. The Seahawk fan turns around. Fucking Seahawks and their fucking fans. Stecchino: Why can’t they hurry up and just lose already. --------------- 4:46 PM --------------- Stecchino: Jesus Christ not again! MAKE A FIELD GOAL! Tony rubs his hands through his hair, annoyed. Everyone else is jumping up and down, enjoying the fact that Jay Feely has just missed his third opportunity to win the game and have the Giants move into a tie for the top seed in the NFC. Why am I cheering for the Giants to win, they need to lose so the Eagles can sneak into the playoffs. But I hate the Seahawks so much! The football rolls around in the north end zone all by itself. Jay Feely walks over to the Giants sideline all by himself. Tony’s cell phone rings. Tony carefully pulls it out of his pocket. Popcorn flies through the air. Tony brings his cell phone to his ear. Stecchino: It’s Stecchino -- GTT Five, yeah I know -- Who… No, Stevie, I can’t hear ya, the Giants missed the field goal -- where? Doesn’t matter, it’s not like we are going to base the winner off the words we say or type on a screen, nothing geeky like that, it’s all about the in ring performance -- Who? Vetra… Yeah I know about him, dude I know says he sucks, not worth the space he takes up on the roster -- Won't give up the source. No way. Not ever. Tony hangs up the phone. Stecchino: Stop cheering, it’s not like you’re gonna win the Super Bowl, shit. Everyone is still cheering as the Seahawks offense takes the field. No one heard a single word Tony said. Not like it was important anyway. --------------- 5:01 PM --------------- Josh Brown kicks the ball. It flips end over end, getting closer to the goal posts. The ball begins to hook left. Qwest Field grows in anxiousness. The ball hits the netting behind the end zone. The referees look at each other for a split second and then raise their arms. The crowd in Qwest Field erupts. The two Seahawk fans in front of Tony jump up and down. Tony with a mighty shove, sends them both tumbling over the rail and down on to the field. Everyone else is too excited to notice. God, I hate Seahawk fans, I wish a comet would hit the stadium at a game I’m not present at. I hope they lose to Philadelphia by ninety-seven, stupid Seahawks. Tony begins to fight through the people trying to get to the stairs to get to the concourse. Gross, they’re touching me. --------------- Tony’s Condo Seattle, WA 6:45 PM --------------- Stecchino: This is it, make sure they know that, not ever again after this, this is the last one. A woman jots notes quickly down in a notebook. Stecchino: Oh and make sure GW is ready for contract negotiations. Why can’t I just leave with good health and not come back and take all of this punishment again. The gold Yeah, but that only goes so far. Glory You can never have enough of that, that’s why I’m doing this again. The door closes. The woman is gone. Tony walks toward the door. Maybe it’s better to walk away fully after this. Actually, you can never walk away like John Stockton or Karl Malone. Always go out on top and never go out with people questioning you, your critics will win. Good point. I know this would be my last PTC participation unless I won, then - - Tony locks the door. You can’t trust Maverick Reno. You can never trust anyone, but Mav seems OK now, he’s calmed down. He is now a family man. Tony begins to walk away from the door. Stecchino: Am I crazy to keep a running dialogue with myself? Possibly, but who cares. Just seems like unnecessary exposition. You’d think that now, wouldn’t you? You’re thinking it too, moron. Son of a bitch. Tony walks into his bedroom and closes the door.
