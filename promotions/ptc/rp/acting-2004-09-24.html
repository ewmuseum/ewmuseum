Acting

24 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-24 "That was f*ckin cool!" The teenager, here known as Brad Duncan, was screaming at the top of his lungs from his second level seat in the confines of the huge arena. While millions of other fans jeered until they were blue in the face, Brad Duncan was ecstatic at the display of violence just seen in the center of the Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling ring. Nothing excited Brad more than a good fight, except that is, for a good massacre. Brad had ordered the tickets from the XUW website and they came in the mail just two days before he and his girlfriend Rochelle Breen were set to see it. As Brad screamed at the very top of his lungs, he could still remember getting the tickets at just the right time to cheer him up after a long day at school and a failed test or two. Nothing cheered Brad up more than the concept of going to see his favorite grapplers going at it live. "I don't know why you like that stuff so much Brad. It's stupid and you know it's fake." Rochelle had said with a hint of fatigue in her voice from trying to convince Brad to sell the tickets. "No way Rochelle!" Brad exclaimed, "There's no way I'll sell these tickets. For one, I paid twenty-eight bucks a piece for them, and two I heard that wrestling actually isn't fake at all! I think they might just tell the media that so they won't cancel it. Otherwise they wouldn't allow guys to walk around and zap each other with tasers and stuff." Rochelle sighed... She knew she was fighting a losing battle when she saw the look on Brad's face. He really wanted to go see this thing. She really had no idea why, but she knew that as his girlfriend, she was obligated to go with him. A little quality time probably wouldn't hurt them at all. Those wrestlers were fairly handsome after all. "Still Brad, some of the stories they put on are just like the soap operas I watch. It's either a sob story or something like 'I never knew my father' or whatever. It just seems kind of pointless to me." Rochelle argued. "Oh I know, it's not the stories I like, I just like the wrestling. Like, you remember who my favorite wrestler is right?" Brad asked. "Bailey?" "Yes!" Brad had answered quickly with excitement. Bailey, one half of XUW's New Royal Legion, was what most in XUW would consider a hero. And the night of the show he would get a chance to test his valor. "Who's he facing?" Rochelle had asked apathetically. "Oh he's facing that prick Joey Vendetti. I can't believe Vendetti. He's such an idiot. He clubs Dave over the back of the head and then Joey and this guy shove Dave into the trunk of this car and--" Rochelle shot Brad a callous look and shook her head as he stopped talking. "Ok, so some of the stuff seems a little unbelievable. But isn't it wild to think that maybe it could be real?" Brad asked. "I guess..." Rochelle lied. "You know, Joey's done a lot of things on XUW television that seem pretty bad. I just always wonder how it was that he never got arrested." "Like what?" Rochelle asked. "Well I mean not but about two months ago Joey was beating the life out of his tag team partner and stuffing him in a bodybag. The guy's wife ran out to the ring, the mother of Joey's godson, and Joey slapped her around too." Brad said. "Oh, so this is the stuff that excites you, spousal abuse... I feel safe." Rochelle said jokingly. Brad grinned and took Rochelle in his arms and got nose to nose with her. "I love you, you know I'd never hit you. And besides, she wasn't Joey's wife, she was Overkill's." Brad smiled as Rochelle chuckled. "And you don't have to worry about 'spousal' abuse for a while Rochelle, we're only seventeen." He had said. But the scene was totally different as Rochelle and Brad sat in their seats and prepared for a night of Xtreme Ultimo action. As far as Rochelle was concerned, this was all nothing but fake, and as far as Brad was concerned it was his only escape from the real world. He could believe it was real if he wanted to. I mean, the storyline about Joey Vendetti being a trained former Mafia hitman, that could very well be real as far as Brad was concerned. But somehow in the back of his mind he would always question such things. But as far as Bailey went, there was no question as to whether Joey Vendetti was for real. As the capacity crowd roared, Joey tossed Bailey into the ropes and hit him hard under the jaw with a move that he had dubbed the "East Side Smack" which was just a standard superkick. Bailey was the last thing that was on Joey Vendetti's mind though. Joey had about a million things to think about that had nothing to do with the man whose jaw he had probably just dislocated. Whether he was thinking about his massive match with his archrival Dave Kipfer that surely would be signed, or the fact that he had actually kidnapped said archrival is something that can still be left to our imaginations. But one thing probably dwelled in his mind more than once during his match with Bailey... The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. The tournament would be the fourth of its kind, and Joey had thrown his name into the hat to compete. As far as Joey Vendetti was concerned, he was on a hot streak. And as he pinned Bailey after a double underhook piledriver that he had dubbed the "Family Ties", Joey started to become a bit more confident. Joey Vendetti began to believe that the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament was definitely was within his "range of victory" and he knew that he could quite possibly take it. As "Square Dance" by Eminem hit, Joey stood up in the middle of the ring and had his hand raised by the referee. This defeat of Bailey, a legend in XUW, came only weeks after Joey's victory over former PTC Global Champion Saij. Joey knew that he was on his way. He knew that he was for real. And most of all, he knew that he would do anything to get there. In an entirely different section than Brad and Rochelle were seated, a young boy gazed up at his father who was holding a beer and cheering at the top of his lungs. "I'd rather have one of them Italians win a match than that there filthy Canadian feller! He tain't good f'nothin!" the father said in a severely drunken tone. The young boy, who wasn't even tall enough to see over the man in front of him sighed with disappointment. He hadn't even seen the match take place. All he knew was that Joey Spaghetti was wrestling one of Dad's beers and that the delicious dish had won out in the end. All he had ever hoped for was that he could bond with his dad. He was only seven years old, but severely underdeveloped, so without being able to see the match, he had nothing he could talk to his father about. Chris knew his dad thought that wrestling was real, but to Chris, this was absolutely crazy. Chris had once heard his mother yell at his father about how much time he was spending watching wrestling. "I wanna see that feller get what's comin' to 'im!" he remembered his father had once said. "You're a fool Cletus!" his lovely mother had argued back. They yelled at each other into the night about how she thought wrestling was fake, and how he thought she was nearly retarded for thinking so. How could a chair shot be fake? How could a DDT be fake? How be hate be fake? Those who have followed Joey Vendetti's wrestling career know his very name has a hateful connotation laced between the letters. Whether someone wanted to get personal with Joey Vendetti or not, it always happened. Years in the family had trained Joey to get under a man's skin in the worst way... persistance. Joey will never give up no matter how many times the "good guy" gets him down. He insisted that even his most ridiculous statement was true and if anyone decided to think otherwise, then Joey would simply beat them into submission with his same "I told you so" smirk on his face. Some people would argue that professional wrestlers are some of the greatest actors in the world, but there's a point where it just does not seem like acting anymore. Sometimes, the acting that the professional wrestlers put on in the ring seems almost real. And for people like Brad Duncan and Cletus Brown, the realism of each situation was overwhelming. Joey stepped through the ropes and gathered his trenchcoat and hat as the crowd booed him with insane volume. He sneered at them and started his long march up the ramp. Joey knew better than anybody else that sometimes the hardest part of this was listening to the crowd. But after a while, Joey Vendetti just decided to stop caring. As he marched up the ramp and the fans threw their trash at him, the same thoughts that crossed his mind in the ring ran through his head. Joey had a chance to win the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. He had a bye out of the first round, and now found himself against "Misfit" a member of the UWF, a federation that XUW has an unspoken rivalry with. However, this wasn't something that Joey cared about. He didn't care whether he "brought home a win for the team". The only person Joey would win for was himself. He had no one to answer to now, and he had no "team" to bring home a win for. For as he sold the limp and lifeless body of his best friend Overkill to the Raimondo family, he sold the last of his long-time stable "The Skeletons of Society". It seemed almost ironic that Joey should face a man called Misfit. After all, for the longest time Joey thought of himself as a misfit. But soon Joey learned that all the people who had anything to say about him were right, and if Joey was a misfit, it was because he had chosen that life. No one was a Misfit for no reason. No one was "picked on" by society. Joey had chosen to be an outcast. Joey didn't know a whole lot about Misfit. But he could probably make an educated guess as to why his name was "Misfit" in the first place. Joey walked into the backstage area where the tech crew started to dim his music. He dropped his stuff off next to them and ran his fingers through his hair as he thought again about the GTT4, and how it would be the greatest test in his short history. He glanced around at the scene around him and wondered if someone in this GTT4 was going to take him seriously. It seemed that throughout his career no matter how much Joey Vendetti accomplished, there was always that guy that stood there and smirked at him, sure that he could joust with Joey and win without a problem. Joey thought this was ironic, because normally the man who stood with a smirk was a man who had never stepped into the ring with Joey Vendetti. Anyone who has ever set foot in the ring with Joseph Marciano Vendetti knows that he is for real. And while the question of whether wrestling is fake may be a recurring concept in the mind of Brad and Cletus, it never crosses the mind of anyone who has ever personally known our above mentioned mafioso. As Joey walked towards the meet and greet room that he was scheduled to linger in, Brad and Rochelle marched out of the arena in a totally different part of the hallway. The look on Brad's face at that very moment was indescribable. He had found a couple of backstage passes that someone had dropped on the floor. He was a pretty intelligent boy after all, and knew that if he didn't use them quickly that someone would surely claim them and he'd never get his chance to meet Bailey. "Where's the room where we can meet them!?" Brad asked one of the guards impatiently. "Down the hall, it's the third door on the right, the label on the door should say 'meet and greet'" the guard answered politely. Brad nodded his head and snatched Rochelle's hand before he quickly drug her down the hallway, and as they passed an intersection with another backstage hallway, a large burly man was walking from the intersecting hallway and turning to head down the same hallways as Brad and Rochelle. Brad was too excited about the prospect of meeting his favorite XUW stars to even care what the man's name was, but to this point he's been known as Cletus. He held his son high on his shoulders as they marched around the backstage area with two backstage passes hung around Cletus' neck. So soon, the XUW meet and greet room would meet Brad, Rochelle, Cletus, and Chris. In the XUW meet and greet room, Joey Vendetti waited patiently for anyone who had bought one of the six backstage passes. Two people were already in the room, but they were too busy chatting it up with Fatbadger to really even notice Joey sitting in the opposite corner of the room in a steel chair. Joey's breath had calmed since his stint in the ring and he was clearing the sweat from his face with a large towel as the foursome whom we met earlier strolled into the room. Cletus was the first man who caught Joey's eye. After all, Cletus was a mountain of a man, and Joey was certain that he was about to deal with one of those trailer park types who just "loooooooove their wrasslin'!" and from the looks of the other two, a couple who had stolen a pair of passes. Joey stood to his feet as all four approached him. He knew that if anyone else had been in the room, and had these four been in their right mind, they might not have approached him at all. But Joey sighed, and Joey pulled a pen out of his boot, and Joey prepared to endure what he was forced to endure by threat of losing his job. "You're Joey Vendetti!" Brad barked. The obvious was something that annoyed Joey quite frequently here in XUW... "Yeah, you kids want an autograph or what?" Joey asked. He rolled his eyes as each of them patted themselves down for something that Joey could sign. These four were just not people that mattered to Joey. He cared about getting into GTT4 and dominating his way to the top and proving all his doubters wrong. More than anything, Joey wanted to prove that he was real. Brad pulled what appeared to be a grade card out of his pocket and shoved it at Joey. "I can't believe you beat Bailey tonight! You won't get so lucky against Dave Kipfer at the Road Rage Four Pay Per View! I know Dave's coming back for you, even if you did throw him in that..." Brad started. Joey held up his hand and signed the piece of paper and shoved it back at Brad. "Look, I don't gotta be nice to ya, I just gotta sign your shit. Now take your little girlfriend and go talk to somebody who cares. I got more important things to worry about than your frigg'n opinion." Joey said blankly as Cletus laughed out loud and handed Joey his backstage passes. Brad looked at Joey in shock and dropped his jaw. Rochelle simply rolled her eyes and glanced at her loving boyfriend. "Brad, it's his job to act like an asshole. That's all he is, an actor. Right Joey?" Rochelle asked. Joey finished signing the backstage passes and looked up at Rochelle. He smirked at her for a moment and was about to answer until Cletus jumped in. "Wrasslin's real yea little punk girl! Git outta here!" Cletus demanded. Joey laughed and as Rochelle was about to retort, she was stopped by the sound of Chris' small voice. "But Daddy... Mommy thinks like that girl does, she says it's not real..." Chris began. "You shut your hole boy!" Cletus said angrily. This brought a smile to Joey's face. While Joey knew that his job was real, the media sometimes reassured the fans that it wasn't. While Joey knew that the perks of his job were taking out all his anger in quick explosive blows, these people didn't seem to know it. Joey knew what he would do to Misfit to get into the next round of the GTT4... but half of this foursome thought it would be fake. Joey looked up at Chris, then over at Rochelle before speaking. "Hey guys, I'm in this Golden Turnbuckle Tournament thing. I'm wrestlin' a guy named Misfit in the first round. I 'eard he's pretty good, but I'm gonna beat him." Joey said as Rochelle chuckled. Joey looked into the eyes of the young and doubtful teenage girl and a serious look spread across his face. "It will be the greatest display of 'acting' that you'll ever see.... ever." Joey said. Joey brushed past the fans and walked out of the room, leaving them all with an autograph, and one or two of them with fear. "That was f*ckin' cool!" Brad said as Joey walked away.
