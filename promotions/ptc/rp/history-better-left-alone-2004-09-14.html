History better left alone

14 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-14 1. ALEX GREY Needs... Wants... Desires... We all have them. We all lust after them, strive to achieve them, to make them our own. But perhaps the most frustrating time is when one has needs, wants, and desires... And is unable to achieve them. This has been the last year of my life... My back nearly broken beyond repair by my most hated Enemy. My Title stolen as if to add insult to injury, to give the bastard a memento of what should have been our last match. Instead of certain victory I was left with all the time in the world to think of what could have been. Every thought was another iron bar that made up my prison, unable to even move I lay in bed and plotted my revenge. My pain numbed not by drugs but by thoughts of what I would do when I was able to get my hands around his throat again. Slowly, ever so slowly I fought my way back into top physical condition, countless hours of therapy, doctors, nurses, they were just faces, people trying to do their jobs and nothing more, how much did they really care if I got back on my feet again? When you can walk and you take such day to day things for granted what do you care for a person who doesn't have those luxuries? It's simple...you don't You don't give it a second thought. Tonight I sit on the edge of my bed alone and ponder these things. I ponder the mistakes I’ve made, too numerous to count, my own personal burdens so persistent that there's no where to go to escape them, they occupy my mind during my waking hours and visit me in my dreams, not even drugs can offer me the peaceful blackness of dreamless sleep. There's only one thing that could offer me such sweet release...but I’m not ready to leave this life just yet. Let me get revenge first...then maybe I'll punch my one way ticket and reside in the room that hell has surely prepared for me. And then maybe I’ll finally get a good nights sleep... Laying down I let darkness slowly creep over me. I wonder what pictures my subconscious will paint in my mind tonight. --What Dreams may Come-- Soft moans... The room illuminated only by a pool of moonlight, I can feel her warmth surrounding me, gripping me. She grinds her body against mine punctuating the quiet of the night with a sharp gasp or cry. Her body is perfect, pale white skin glows in the moonlight, outlined by the perfect darkness of night. "Alex..." She whispers my name. I look up at her puzzled, while I can see her body above me I can't quite make out her facial features. "Oh Alex..." She rocks harder against me our bodies slipping and sliding, her breath coming faster. Something isn't right... A barely perceptible warning bell rings in the back of my mind, I raise my hand to reach for her face... Only to discover I can't move. "Oh god...Alex..." Her body trembles. "I've been waiting for...so...long" To my growing horror I find I can't move a single muscle. "So...long...for this..." She throws her head back and screams, it sends a tremor through me and my body spasms, I open my mouth to speak but a barely perceptible groan is all that comes out. "Starting...to figure it out...now...aren't you Alex?" she shivers on top of me "Something...wrong...isn't it?" Horribly wrong...I want to reach up and shove her off me, something isn't right about this but I can't for the life of me figure out what it is! "Can't move can you...it happens when...when you're...in shock...oh!" another tremor wracks her body "Sometimes...when you're in...shock...you need a good jumpstart to...get moving again..." Without warning she rakes her nails down my chest rending and tearing skin, something in me breaks and I scream. "There we go...Alex...wasn't that nice?" She holds her hands up in the moonlight, my eyes widen as I see abnormally long nails glistening with blood...my blood Body straining for any movement that it can purchase I find again I can only produce a small twitch. "Ohhh Alex...what's the matter? Not having fun?" her voice mocks me from behind the mask of shadows. "I am...and I can't tell you how long I’ve waited for this" Leaning down she runs her tongue over the bloody tracks in my skin, lapping up the blood, then she gazes into my eyes, and for the first time I can see her face... I feel a scream welling up inside of me... "What's the matter Alex?" Her face... "Don't like what you see?" Once it was the beautiful face of an aspiring actress...now deformed beyond belief. It's what happens when your face collides with a steering wheel at seventy miles an hour. "Don't like it?" her grotesque visage twists into a pout, one eye socket is empty and the other eye hangs from the socket in a useless lump of jelly, only a few teeth remain in her mouth, some jagged pieces remain, perfect white teeth, her nose crushed to one side and her left cheek split open, I can see what remains of her tongue trying to moisten her dried cracked lips. "You SHOULD like it!" her voice screeches "Because YOU made me this way!" I can taste bile in the back of my throat...the stench of her decaying body is overwhelming. She lunges and buries her jagged teeth into my shoulder, I feel the skin break The scream building inside of me finally escapes, a long wail issues from my throat, a wail I never thought myself capable of making. The last coherent thought that flows through my head before darkness takes me... "My fault!" My Fault... I did this to her... --Waking up never felt so...bad-- Knocking... I'm staring at the ceiling, bile in the back of my throat, chest heaving. I'm awake this much I can tell, dimly I hear the knocking and wonder if my scream carried over from my dream and woke one of my neighbors, Christ I hope not. "Alex!" More Knocking. "Alex it's Martin! Open up!" Sunlight streams through the open window, my excursion into hell seems to have lasted the whole night... "Alex! You there? Wake up!" Martin's voice is relentless and about as welcome as a hangover after a night of drinking, sitting up I swing my feet over the edge of the bed, still dressed in the same clothing I wore yesterday I gather my thoughts as I head for the door, there's a note of excitement in Martin's voice...this much I can tell even though the door separates us, he's gotten himself worked up about something, as I reach for the door I pray it doesn't involve the police or buried bodies...god knows I’ve got enough trouble with bodies not staying buried. "Alex you..." Martin's knocking on the door even as I open it "You...look like shit" he finishes as he gets a good look at me "Did you sleep at all last night?" I nod and motion for him to get in before I decide to slam the door, he accepts my invitation and enters, after the momentary puzzled look at my appearance his face breaks into a grin. "Alex I've got some great news for you!" he beams I sit down on the couch and wait for this ground breaking news, I can still hear the echoes of my scream in my head...what the hell could he possibly have to tell me? "This is what we've been waiting for Alex!" Martin says shifting his weight quickly from one foot to the other "I've got something you're going to want" he says opening a folder and taking out several papers, I can tell right away it's a contract and that's enough to make me want to throw him out of my apartment right then and there, but Martin isn't thrown off by my sudden glare of recognition, if anything his smile has broadened. "I know what you're thinking...why the hell should you sign it?" A nod lets him know he's reading my mind "Steven Caldera..." Martin grins "That's one reason" Steven Caldera...God damn, how many times did I fantasize about meeting that man in a dark alley? The bastard who'd almost ended my career by forcing me to fight in a 5 man battle royal...with a fucking broken arm! I'd be lying if I said I didn't want to get my hands on that man, but it would also be a lie to say I’d sign a contract to get at him...Fuck Caldera. "Another reason..." Martin reminds me of a game show host revealing what's behind each door "Fame Alex...this tournament is huge! This is a chance to reinvent yourself, to prove to everyone we're a force to be reckoned with, you can perform on a bigger stage then ever before...and I can help you make it possible...just look at the contract!" he says dumping the papers into my lap. With a sigh I pick the papers up and try to maneuver through the legal bullshit, but like always I find myself growing bored...Martin always handled the legal work...I think it gave him a boner to twist the contracts around, find loopholes and play god with careers...he'd done it with mine all too often, and if it hadn't been for the wealth it had accumulated me I probably would have wrapped my hands around his neck and snapped it a long time ago. GTT4...sounded like the next hot title for a consol racing game. "Fame Alex! Recognition...you could finally..." "Quiet!" I hissed up at him "Settle down Martin...you've got yourself so worked up I’m afraid you're going to piss on my carpet...and don't think I wont beat you with a newspaper and rub your nose in it if you do..." Martin isn't deflated in the least by my comments, he's staring at me with that same shit eating grin on his face...he knows something I don't. "Alex..." he begins but I cut him off again "Spill it Smalls...what do you know that I don't?" His next two words hit me like a physical blow "Lestatt Knight" Those two words change my mind "Knight...in this Tourney?" Martin nodded I close my eyes, I'd never been a believer in god...but someone had heard my silent prayer last night...Maybe the devil had appealed and granted me my wish. "Lestatt Knight..." The same son of a bitch who'd almost broken my back... "Lestatt fucking Knight..." "That's right" Martin smiled "You wont face him straight away...you both have a bracket to struggle through first...but think about it Alex! Doesn't a possible shot at Knight make this whole thing worth it?" "Where do I sign?" I want this... "I thought you'd never ask" Martin flips to the back and shows me where to place my name, this is the only part of the contract I understand...the only part I need to I desire this... I sign on the dotted line, Martin's grinning so broadly now I’m surprised his head doesn't fall off, he gathers up the papers briefly and leaves me with a folder, information on my first opponent. Annihilation... “First round is important Alex...this is where it all starts or ends” “Get out...” “What?” Martin looks surprise “Get the fuck out Martin...I don’t want your advice...you’ve done your part...now I do mine, and you know better then telling me how to do my job” Martin doesn’t need any further convincing, throwing his things together he casts a glare over his shoulder as he leaves, but he knows just as well as I do that it wont faze me...he leaves me alone...alone staring Annihilation in the face. –Facing Certain Annihilation– Annihilation... Kevin Thompson... He has a name... It’s funny these names, these names that hide behind the wrestling persona, what kind of a man is Kevin? Is there a face behind his name? Or are Annihilation one and the same? Does Kevin know anything about me? Does he lay awake at night wondering? Does he lay awake knowing that every time he steps into the ring it could be his last? Or did that stop troubling him long ago? It doesn’t bother me any more...I’ve cheated death and I think death likes letting me think I’ve gotten the best of her, but one day she’ll come for me, and I’ll feel her cold touch...she’ll fill a space inside of me that’s been so empty for so long. But not now... Not right now Kevin... No right now I’m feeling...stressed...it feels good, it gives me just the right combination of pressure and adrenaline... Right now Kevin I feel like I could break your nose...watch the blood flow...lick it from your face and see the fear in your eyes, when you realize that this tourney? This tourney Kevin...it isn’t fame...it isn’t popularity...it’s my chance for revenge...it’s my obsession. I need this... I want this... I desire this... And I WILL make it mine... Just try and stop me Kevin. Just try and stop me.
