This Can't Be The End…

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 "Son of a bitch!" A wet white ball of snow struck Brian in the back of the head as he walked a few yards away from his older cousin. Suddenly a volley of snowballs came careening down upon him from behind the evergreen trees to his rear. Brian covered his head in annoyance as he had very little patience when it came to children, especially his cousin’s. "Damnit, Jeff!" He shouted over the shower of snow. "Get your God damn kids under control!" Brian waved the ax he was carrying in anger; arms open wide as he hoisted it up in the air. "Chill down, Brian, no need to get all riled up, they’re just kids." "Yeah, well… I have a fuckin’ ax and a short temper." ********** Two weeks had passed since the raid of Arson’s house had brought him back into the national limelight. The authorities had whisked him away while the retired wrestler grinned in arrogance. He was clean. He was always clean. The Santa Barbara police released him the next day, and he parted as if leaving family and friends after Thanksgiving dinner. It had been close this time. Closer than Brian was comfortable with, had they been there a split second sooner his hopes of becoming the GTT5 champion would have been crushed before the tournament had even begun. Of course, Brian seemed to always be once step ahead of most people, let alone the poor excuse for a police force known as the SBPD. But to play it safe, he decided it was time to get away from the West Coast. As if someone had read his mind, his cousin called days later, inviting him to spend Christmas with his family in Connecticut. The unexpected call wasn’t what Brian had in mind, and he had to decide whether visiting his cousin and his devil children was worth leaving Santa Barbara. In the end, he realized that the situation could work out to his advantage, as Connecticut would be a safe place for him to live for the next month. Perhaps it was arriving at the Bradley International Airport in Hartford and seeing his cousin and his wife with their five children, four of which were under the age of twelve, that he started having second thoughts. He could only imagine how long the December would be, having to deal with a bunch of pre-adolescent kids causing havoc and running a muck across his cousin’s farm. Brian had shunned himself away from the rest of his family because every time he spent any significant amount of time with them it seemed to turn out in a screaming match of the ages, leaving Brian deathly close to having a brain hemorrhage. He remembered having to deal with his uncle this past September after the passing of his father. He had nearly been thrown out of the house after he’d left his uncle’s teenage son alone and inebriated down on the Ohio State campus. His cousin Jeff was about ten years older than him and already had five kids, the oldest one, his son Michael, was about to turn seventeen. According to Jeff, Michael had been causing a multitude of problems at home and with authorities. Maybe that was why he took a liking to Brian the very first time they’d met almost eight years ago. In each family, there seemed to be someone infatuated with the fact that Brian was a professional wrestler and perhaps that was why all of his cousin’s outcasted him; he was corrupting their children. Brian figured the only reason Jeff had invited him for Christmas was that his wife, Shelly, had seen him on the news and felt some sort of sympathy towards him. She was such a warm and caring woman that even Brian was surprised that he took a liking to her. No one in the Arson family, it seemed, had a good taste in women. Jeff had been lucky and struck gold. ********** And so now Brian was stuck in Connecticut, on his cousin’s farm, with his children, trying to pick out a Christmas tree deep in the backwoods of the property. The ground was covered in a few inches of snow as the state had received a bit of a storm the previous morning. The sky was a light gray, the sun lighting it from up above, but the day was dreary and weather reports said another storm would be moving in around midnight. Brian knew he lived in southern California for a reason. Jeff had given Brian the honor of carrying the ax because he had wanted his cousin to pick out a tree that he liked, but now he wasn’t so sure that that had been a great idea as Brian threatened to kill his children. "Brian, how about you let me carry that thing for now?" Jeff insisted, motioning for the ax. "Fine, I don’t care, just keep these devil children out of my reach or I might snap one of their necks." Michael, who had been leading the group a few yards ahead of Brian, began to laugh and turned back to them, but his dad gave him an icy look that shut him up almost instantly. "See, I’m glad at least someone gets my sense of humor around here!" Brian trudged on and caught up to Michael who looked like he was having about as much fun as he was. He towered over his cousin’s son and the young man looked up at him in awe. "Don’t you just love Christmas? It makes me want to stab a kitten," Brian said with a held in breath so that his older cousin didn’t hear. "I don’t know how much longer I can take listening to Mr. Rogers over here and your crazy brothers and sisters." "Yeah, well, at least you don’t have to live with them, so don’t be complaining to me about it," Michael shot back at Brian, who was taken by surprise at the snap in his voice. "Whoa there, Mikey, don’t start going off at me because you can’t stand your family, I’ve been in your shoes and I’ve had to deal with plenty of this shit, so I’m not feeling any sympathy for ya, bud." "That’s just great, isn’t it? You’re not stuck here though; you should just fuckin’ go then and do whatever the hell it is you do! There’s a high school about three miles away, plenty of teenagers to go sell some crack to." "You son of a bitch!" Brian said in hushed tones, about to strike Michael, but he kept his composure in front of Jeff. Seconds later Brian was smiling and Michael was even more confused. "I knew I liked you, buddy, you got that Arson vigor in you! How would you like to come see my opening match in the GTT5, I know you’ve heard about that shit? Your parents tell me you’ve been following my career since the start," Brian frowned, "…kinda sad really, but whatever keeps you entertained!" "Are you serious?!" Michael’s eyes opened wide at the thought of being able to see Brian wrestle in a match for the first time. "I can’t believe they actually let you into that tournament after what happened these past few weeks." "Neither can I, Mikey, neither can I, but if you knew Argyle like I do… well… actually, you’d still be wondering why the hell he let me in." Brian thought about that for a moment, "Come to think of it, there has to be a catch, there’s no way that son of a bitch would let me into this thing." "Maybe he wants to see you embarrass yourself, since you’ve done a pretty good job of that in the past," Michael chimed in. "Who’s your first round opponent, anyway?" "Boy, I really am about this close to ripping your nuts off and shoving them down your throat," he shot Michael a nasty look but then smiled once again. "Who am I facing? Uh… his name is Christian Montiero or something like that, nothing special, I haven’t even given him much thought to be honest. "Aren’t you going to look into him at all? What if he’s a monster?" Michael inquired. "No, probably not. He’s from the XUW, and I figured they exhausted all their talent with those other guys, umm… Uber Judge, I think, and fatbadger? Wow, is that really their top-carders?" Brian thought aloud. "I have no idea. There can’t be much room left on the roster for anyone else good, in fact, I couldn’t even find the guy on the official roster, maybe they just brought him up from the Ohio Valley. Wouldn’t that—" Before Brian could finish another snowball had nailed him directly in the back of the head. He turned to see Michael’s little sisters and brothers laughing at him from in front of a snow bank. "That’s it!" Before anyone could stop him, Brian had picked up a handful of snow and pounded it into a ball. He wound up and launched a snowball right into the face of Jeff’s nine-year old daughter, knocking her flat on her ass. "Oh my God!" Jeff cried out and rushed over to his baby. "Yeah, HA HA HA, look who’s laughing now you little shit! I warned you! Who wants it next!" All of the kids ran in fear behind the giant snow pile as Brian’s intimidating figure loomed over them. "I WILL EAT YOUR SOUL AND BURN DOWN YOUR FARM!" About as soon as Brian said that he turned around and walked back to Michael with a horrible grin across his face. "I don’t suppose we’ll have to worry about that again." Brian smiled as Michael still stood in shock at what he just witnessed. Even he couldn’t believe the short fuse attached to Brian’s temper. "So do you want to come to the first round of GTT5, or not?" Brian asked and Michael nodded sheepishly. "Excellent." Brian shoved his hands back into his winter jacket and began to walk off back to the house, which was hundreds of yards away, and Michael began to follow. "Oh, and by the way," Brian put on a fake smile. "Don’t ever mention anything about me and drugs again or I’ll fucking kill you." ********** The role that Brian Arson would play in GTT5 may seem rather unclear to those unfamiliar to his career, but to those veterans that knew him or had worked with him, his presence would more than likely go overlooked. He had been in the business for years and had wrestled and been taught by the best, including Belmont, K-Wolf, Chris Sloboda, Magnum Wolf, and even his one-time mentor, Xavier Kannon. He had observed, up close and afar, techniques he’d never be able to learn elsewhere. In RPW Magnum Wolf had instilled in him the cheating mentality; do anything to win. It had always been there but Wolf had nurtured it and sprung him loose into being the ultimate heel. But then, as if on cue, everything came crashing down and Rush Pro Wrestling was no more. Since then Brian had never been the same. While his peers went on to wrestle in great federations such as PRIME and GCW, Brian became dormant, retired, forgotten about, and he receded into the back of the Primetime Central vaults as another notch on the wrestling history belt. They didn’t question his ability to wrestle in the tournament because they just didn’t care. To put it simply, Brian Arson was nothing out of the ordinary, and in a bracket that included Angelo Deville and the new faces of Primetime Central stardom, he was overshadowed. He had reached the ripe old age of thirty this year; he wasn’t a kid anymore. For many, thirty years was far too late to achieve anything significant. But there was one thing Brian wanted even more than the GTT5 championship, and that was to go toe-to-toe with Angelo Deville. While he had been in RPW with Kannon and Wolf, Deville was not there, and Brian, although Deville was younger than he was, always saw him as the one man he’d never have a chance to face. With the brackets set up as they are now, Brian’s dreams of being able to take on Deville looked like an achievable goal. He knew his one-time mentor, Kannon, would be laughing at the thought of him having a chance against Mr. Deville, but this was the one thing that could spring him Brian back to life. Losing first round to the likes of Montiero would simply crush his hopes of ever returning to the spot he had once attained. Arson wasn’t going to let some punk from XUW keep him from getting what he wanted. Doing well in this tournament would not only boost Brian’s own morale, but may also earn him the respect he had lost from many of his peers throughout the years, and perhaps allow people, particularly the ones like Michael and anyone else that looked up to him, to start believing in him again. Staying with his cousins may not have been the best of choices, but the distractions there would be far less than anything he would have to deal with back home. More distractions were the last things he needed, and he could deal with family. It’d been a long time since he’d had any of his own. ********** "So… how’d picking out a Christmas tree go today?" Shelly said with remarkable enthusiasm. The entire family sat around the dinner table completely quiet, the younger ones watching Brian in fear, as he scooped food from the china dishes onto his plate. Michael looked at his father who put on a fake smile and rubbed his daughter’s shoulder. An enormous welt had developed over her right eye. "Well," Jeff began, "I thought everything was going well, except for the fact that Brian over here could quite possibly be legally insane—" "Oh, shut it, Jeff!" Brian barked across the table with mashed potatoes crammed into his mouth. "Look at my baby girl!" "She can deal with it. Just think of it as a learning experience!" "She’s nine years old!" "Isn’t this dinner just lovely?" Jeff’s wife blurted out. "I’m so glad you could come to visit for the month Brian!" "God damnit, Shelly! Brian’s a mad man!" Jeff yelled. "Well, you’re not so perfect yourself there, Jeff!" Shelly replied, beginning to get more upset. "Just because Brian lost control of his temper for a second, doesn’t mean he’s crazy!" "Oh give me a break!" Brian didn’t quite understand how all of a sudden the fight turned from him to Jeff and his wife, but he didn’t seem to care and rather watched in enjoyment at the two quarreling. He cut off a large piece of parmesan chicken and shoved it into his mouth with a delighted smile. "I can’t believe you, Shelly!" Jeff was still screaming. "This chicken is delicious!" Brian said. "Why thank you, Brian, I’m glad somebody likes my cooking," she said with a smile and then instantly glared at her husband. "You’re unbelievable! Shelly, I don’t understand what the hell is wrong with you, I really don’t!" "Jeff!" Shelly yelled, beginning to get teary-eyed. "You know what, I’m just not hungry anymore!" She kicked out her chair and threw her napkin down on the table and rushed off upstairs to her bedroom. "Oh, baby! C’mon!" Jeff scooted his chair out as well and went clambering after her. With both of their parents gone, the younger children left the table and went back to watching TV in the basement, leaving Michael and Brian at the dinner table alone. Several minutes went by while they ate quietly and then Brian finally piped up. "So… that was a good dinner, don’t you think? Your mother’s certainly got a knack for the kitchen." "Yeah, she’s not bad," Michael mumbled while taking a sip of water. Brian’s idea of as little distractions as possible wasn’t turning out quite the way he had expected. Staying at a hotel had crossed his mind several times, but he liked Michael and thought maybe he could finally get close to someone in his family so that he wouldn’t be shut off completely. Unfortunately, tonight he really didn’t feel like rekindling a bond with his newfound friend, and he stood up from the table. "You got any Jack around here?" Nope, definitely not tonight. ********** The hours were going by quickly, and with them, so were Brian’s wits. Never before had he been so nervous for an event in his entire life, especially one linked to wrestling. For the first time since his father’s funeral he was beginning to feel something. There was something about the GTT5, the most prestigious tournament in wrestling, that made people feel different about themselves. It was more than just a chance to make or break yourself, more than just a chance to get noticed, and more than just a sense of pride in competing. When you enter the GTT, it’s an entirely different experience from any other tournaments you’ve ever entered. You’re stamping a place in wrestling history when you go the distance. A win for Brian would mean everything. Going down into the mud, being stomped on and forgotten about and then returning with stronger resilience than ever before. It’s more than just pride… it’s vengeance; against everyone who has down trodden and spit on him in the past. Everyone’s who has laughed at the joke of a man, who can’t even keep himself out of the hands of the police, who had a chance to really be something but went and fucked it up right when he’s at the top; the man known as Brian Arson. It begins with you, Montiero. When you step into the ring, you won’t understand the immense amount of pain I’m going to inflict on you, the years of vengeance in the making, bottled up, only to be unleashed at this very moment. Perhaps, my last chance. I will not be stopped here, Mr. Montiero, no not yet. I have unfinished business to take care of, and you; I cannot have you stepping in the way. You’re a mere obstacle that will be thrown aside because it’s my time to step back into the spotlight. I will not be overlooked… I will not be overshadowed… This could be my very last chance.
