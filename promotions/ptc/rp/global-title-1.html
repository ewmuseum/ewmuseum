Global Title #1

12 April 2003

Jomary Affiliated Pimp Joined: 08 Apr 2003 Posts: 1 Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 9:15 am Post subject: Global Title #1 Can you believe it? Jomary managed to get in the Global Title Division in her first PTC appearance! Give her some props, she KNEW she was going to make an impact and d**n, she did. So where's our star-to-be at right now.... at promo time? Why, she's at the one thing out in the Minnesota streets again. She is wearing a blue top with matching shorts to go along with a pair of white socks rolled down to the top of her tan hiking boots. Why hiking boots? I don’t know and knowing her bitchy attitude, I’m not really about to go and ask her! Her hair is being worn down, some of it in her face due to the slight breeze around and she also has a backpack over her right shoulder. She takes a couple small steps then stops and looks upwards. After a moment she looks back down, her head at eye-level, and starts to speak. Jomary: There's always someone or something out there that makes you want to think. That certain 'something' that gets SO frustrating you want to pound your fist into his/it's face.... something where you would just want to drop whatever it was that you were doing and run over to him/it and just flat out deck him/it. That certain 'whatever' that just pisses you off one time too much. Do you stupid fuck heads out there even know what I'm talking about? If you don't I can give some good examples: the PTC Global Title Match---that should do. I get a shot next week ---in this dump called New Era. They just so fuck up in their retarded myopic thinking. They're 'all that,' they're “all man,” they don’t lose, she claims. They are who everyone comes to see, who everyone cheers for, who's all PTC ever had. I don't think so. I hope it finally gets into their heads that they’re not as important to this inter-fed as their drug-fried brains thought. They finally will be introduced to this, the real world, and find out why they haven't been in main events and why they aren't on promotional posters for cards and why they don't get the big firework shows. I’ve heard what Angel has said. Her little dopey interview was a good way for me to realize just how wasted the television airwaves are these days. I’m telling you, I’d rather see Billy Mays hocking another invention, be it Orange Clean or that stupid weed whacker thing he’s got, than have to sit through some more rambling by them.” Global Division? Damn! You need an Iron BRAIN in order to sit through a droning of those people. No ordinary brain would allow idleness during such stupid interviews. What’s sad is that it’s not like they were talking FOREVER—just about 10 seconds really. Trust me, the time I spent watching them? Hey, those are 10 seconds of my life I wish I had back. Bullshit if I ever heard anything worse than what they’ve said. Like I've never seen this before in any other federations. Playing the whole “I have more experience than you” card. Come on...is this for real? If experience meant jack shit in a match, I’d be well into my grave as we speak. I’ve won so many matches coming in as the “green rookie” against the “old, grizzled veteran” that I’ve lost count. Something that should be taken of note considering my knowledge and straight A’s, even in classes like calculus and advanced calculus. Besides, if we want to go on experience, you know what there guys? I’ve been going strong in this business for over one freaking year! You’ve never heard of me? Big whoopee-ding-dong! BITE MY CLIT!! I’VE NEVER HEARD OF YOU EITHER!! Dudes get real.... heck, DROP DEAD! You haven’t done anything special of yet because if you had, I’d have heard of you by now. I’d be looking forward to facing you as much as I would a debate over abortion. (Whispering) By the way, that’s a good thing. It would mean I actually cared about you. (Normal voice now) Instead, I look forward to facing you in this first ever Wheel of Misfortune match as much as I do watching someone receive an anal-scooping. Think about it— when you speak, that means that you’re hoping thousands of dick suckers out there really are hanging on each word you say. First off, like they care or know who you are for that matter, secondly, you’re all boobs. Granted, when you fall down the stairs or add two and two and get 16, it's something that qualifies as publishable material in the 'PTC Guide to Being Smart,' but here in my world, Jomary's New Era, it's something that you’re doing to attract attention. Those are loser comments. Those are 'I haven't won in over a month' comments. You can try to win this match; I don’t really give a flying fuck or a rat’s ass or any other cliché you can come up with. I’m going out there and try not to lose. If I win, sweet deal, more power to me earlier in my career. But if I don’t get the win and I’m NOT pinned, guess what? I’m STILL HERE!! Don’t be surprised if you’re fighting yourself the whole time while I sit on a turnbuckle and suck back a Pepsi, a Coca-Cola, or Evian. Just remember, losing is BAD! (In a motherly tone) Can you do that? You can? GOOD FOR YOU! YAY!! (Claps her hands a couple times and returns to her normal voice again) You don't want to try to get another win without me helping out? Wow people, you really are a fat-filled colon-brain aren't you? Then I’d have to do the dirty work, which would be okay then. I’d just go up to you, apply the Jomary's Superkick right away and make the cover. No fancy moves, no high spots, nothing for the ignorant fans to get excited about, just a quick, simple win so I can get on with my life. You want to entertain the fans? You want to give yourself a chance? (Holds her hands out at her sides with an expression of guilt on her face) OOPS! (She puts her hands down and loses the guilt look) Get bent! (Starts smirking, trying to hold in laughter that is brewing in her, but she fail and starts laughing) Not like you could beat ME anyways. She laughs a bit harder now but quickly settles down and stops. At this point she starts brushing some hair out of her face, those random strands continue to blow in it due to the aforementioned breeze. Jomary: I'm telling you, I swear if and when I get my hands on you, I'm going to beat you down and make you wish you were able to have access to a time machine so you could go back to now, before this match-up, when you still have your eyes, nose and ears. But okay now--while people are being dumb and checking their pockets for cash they don't have--I'd like to look at just how cool my life is. Now check this out goons: I, Jomary am the only person in this federation right now, today, that is 100% athlete with 100% brains. If anyone out there thinks they really can shake a stick at me, I say go out and win the Xtreme and Women’s Titles and THEN knock on my door. Better yet, send me some e-mail (Holds her right hand to the right side of her mouth as she turns to one side and whispers) I don't like dealing with marrow-less losers face to face. (Stands back up straight putting her hand down) Hey, it's not that I don't like scrubby hicks that aren't me; it's just that I don't like them. But I think it's best I be going right now---I got more to say, oh yes I do, but hey, why waste breath when it's not 100% necessary? Jomary readjusts her hair again as well as her backpack and starts walking down the street. Seeing she has no more to say--we fade out. Simple as that! The fans in the arena are electric, after just witnessing a grueling match between two up-and-commers. The fans are on their feet as they wait for one of their favorite superstars to appear or another match to begin. The camera cuts to the entrance ramp as purple strobe lights begin to circle around the arena. Seconds later *Gold-Lust* begins to blast over the sound system and Jomary's video begins to play over the tron. The fans start to cheer and scream. The camera pans over them, highlighting some Jomary signs and posters. The camera cuts back to the ramp as Jomary and Chezina burst through the black curtain. They jump along the stage before starting down the ramp. They slap fans hands as they walk past before reaching the ring. Jomary slides in and Chezina jumps over the ropes and up onto the turnbuckle. Jomary jumps onto the ropes and raises her free hand. Jomary jumps down before getting two mics and handing one to Chezina. Chezina: Well it's great to be here in San Diego! The fans scream at the mention of their city and Chezina smiles at his cheep pop. Jomary: Well we're here to talk about two things..... Chezina: One relates to our matches at New Era.... Jomary: The other is about last house show. Chezina: You guys all saw the last house show, right? The fans start screaming their replies. Chezina: Maybe I'll show you again, just to refresh your memory. A video of last house show is played over the screen, showing Chezina hitting the Chezina Bottom on El Locon. The image fades. Chezina: You see, Locon, you mess with Chezina and you got your ass kicked. You mess with a Rodriguez Brother you...get your ass kicked. On New Era I have a match with Chivo as my partner and you had better believe I'll be ready for all the top teams in PTC. Of course in the end I'll watch the ref raise our hands in victory, but if there is even a hint that you are getting any 'help' I'll have to bring my little self out and 'lend a hand'. You think you've been in tough matches before? You haven't seen anything yet. Chezina stares into the camera, a smile on his face, before Jomary begins to talk. Jomary: So now we come to my match at New Era. My encounter with the top wrestlers in all the PTC federations in the Wheel of Misfortune Match. Now it seems to me, and I am just speaking from a woman's point of view, that you don't like women. Seems to me that you don't like my sex at all. The audience starts to cheer and laugh. Jomary: You walk out, you say this and that, about being smart and being this and that, but you don't prove it. I was watching your little scenes back in the locker room and I did notice some of you say you were smart a few times but then you just went ahead and messed that whole premice up by making a mistake. Wrestling against Jomary? The audience starts chanting 'Idiots.' Chezina: No no idiots isn't the word for it. Oh wait.... yes it is. Jomary: But let's not dwell on the fact that they are.... idiots. Let's concentrate on the fact that at New Era I will become the Global Champion. Chezina: When you diss me you diss every single fan that comes to these arenas to watch you...well not you personally. Jomary: So tie yourselves up real well, cause at New Era we're taking out the trash. The audience being to cheer again and Chezina waits to talk. Chezina: Now onto your 'top teams', and I use the term loosely. Tag Team Clusterfuck. Jomary: Now I know that we've never met before, well if you can call it that, but I hate to tell you, friends, that the fate you will suffer will continue to happen everytime you get in the ring with me. You may give me a good match, but you will still lose. You think you can beat me? You have got another thing coming. Chezina: Now I did notice that some people think they are extreme....hmmm. Wonder if that'll help you at New Era, or all of you will prove my point that the only way to be extreme is by competing in the XWF all others are just pure imitators. Chezina smiles at his last statement as *Gold-Lust* hits. Chezina's video begins to roll over the screen as they hand their mics to the ring helpers. They jump up onto turnbuckles and raise their hands for the cheering crowd. They jump down and climb out of the ring. They walk up the ramp, slapping a few more hands. As they get to the curtain, they raise their hands and walk backwards, until they are no longer seen.
