Fanboi

Date: 2005-11-28 The wind blew across flat plains, a chill in the winter night. But one small Ohio household had a warm fire, burning cozily in the grate. Across from this fire sat a young man, pushing his glasses up on his nose. "Is it on?" The scene jolted as the camera nodded "yes," up and down. He looked over to the side. "What do I say?" The response to this question was an irritated sigh. "It’s a say anything segment, okay? You introduce yourself, and then you talk about GTT5, okay?" "Anything I want?" "Anything you want, skipper." The youth excitedly pushed his glasses up again, turning a myopic gaze to the camera. "I’m Butch Hawkins, I’m twenty-three, and I reckon I’m the biggest PTC wrestlin’ fan here in Boyett." "What’s the first round matchup you’re most looking forward to?" "Deville and Rollins, no question ‘bout it. That’s gonna be the match that everybody’s watchin’." "No kidding. Anybody else you got your eye on?" "Lessee…" The young man shifted his weight, and the wicker chair creaked in protest. "O’Shea and Uber Judge definitely. Ummm… I’m watchin’ Almasy too." "Ohhh…" said the producer, "An Almasy fan, huh?" The young man instantly sat up, straight as a ramrod. "Almasy? Hell naw! I just know how it is with him and PTC…" "What do you mean?" Butch leaned conspiratorially closer to the camera. "You know how it is with Seymour. They probly put him in a special bracket or somethin’ so he can win it all." Abject silence reigned, as the camera man and producer shared a look that could only be described with the acronym "WTF?!" "I’m not gonna touch that one with a ten foot pole. I like my job." The producer sighed. "What about all the women in GTT5? Pretty good showing for them this year, huh?" Butch shrugged. "Guess so. I ain’t studied up on ‘em much. I’ve never liked the whole idea of women wrestlin’, specially not them women like’s on Global Wrestlin’. You know, the Amazons that look like they could tear the arm off a brass statue with their bare hands." It was at this point that Charles Cooper was beginning to wonder exactly what he’d gotten into by taking this job. "There are a good few women, though… Eva VanLorne, Michiru Kaiou, Butterfly Hamada, Doreen Pepperpot, Teresa Tomas, Amy Silveira, Tessa Windsor… but you’re right, the largest contingency of women did come from Global… Dawn McFarlane, Danielle Northsky, Elia Smith, Shadow." "Dawn’s good." Butch produced a toothpick seemingly from nowhere, and began chewing it. "And the rest of them?" "Hell, I don’t know. I already told you I don’t much study on women’s wrestling." Cooper blinked. "Ooookay, then. What PTC promotion is your favorite?" Butch shifted his weight. "You know, I don’t actually watch any of their stuff anymore. I used to like PTC a lot more, like over the summer and stuff." "So what DO you watch?" "Mostly WMW… You know, I go to shows here an’ there, cuz they’re so close to Boyett, and when they got the boot from PTC, I just didn’t have much heart to keep watchin’ anymore. But I tell you, I can’t keep m’self away from GTT5." "WMW, huh? So you know all about not only Dawn, but Shadow too?" Butch snorted. "Lemme tell you… Shadow ain’t got NOTHIN’ on anybody out there. Bet she’ll go out first round." "First round? She was the only one from WMW to make it to the bracket finals last year." "Yeah? So what. Bet she’s got soft." "Held two titles, only lost one match." Butch shook his head. "Yeah, and what’s that say about Global? Everybody knows that she left WMW cuz she couldn’t hack it with the competition there. They got the hardest competition in wrestlin’ today, far as I’m concerned. She quit cuz she knew she couldn’t get to the top there, and now she goes to Global, and beats a lot of people there… they must really suck." Cooper raised his eyebrow. "It could also back up her claims of favoritism, you know." Butch violently shook his head. "That ain’t it, it just ain’t. Y’all can go now, I don’t have nothin’ else to say if you’re gonna sit here and say bullshit like that. Shoulda known better than to talk to somebody from PTC." At that point, Cooper and his camera-man were more than happy to pack up their things and get out of the house. There was only so much you could say to a rabid WMW fan, after all, before things just turned into one big gay joke. Out on the porch, the camera-man lit up a cigarette. On second thought, he passed it to Charles Cooper, who accepted the offer gratefully, and took a long drag off of it, breathing the nicotine-scented smoke into the cool night air. He shook his head, and commented, his New-Yawkah accent coming out much more clearly now that he was annoyed. "You see if I ever fuckin’ waste my time in Ohio again… Who’s fuckin’ idea was this anyway?…"
