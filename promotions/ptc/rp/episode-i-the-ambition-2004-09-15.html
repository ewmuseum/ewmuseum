Episode I: The Ambition

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 Teh K0rey Dr0ck Experience Episode I: The Ambition From the moment I entered this business, I always walked around the corridors of buildings not knowing what to expect. I would drift in and out of off-limit zones not knowing or understanding what made them so different from everywhere. I'd slip and I'd slide my way along through office areas, promotional departments and executive stations never really taking grasp of what was expected. In the life of a typical human, we live and most of the time, we ultimately fail. We have a goal, a star that we want to chase... but when opportunity knocks, we're never around to answer the door. I'd walk around these various places and I'd look at the expression on these peoples faces. Sure, some of them would be overly estatic... like a fat kid finding a cupcake with sprinkles on top. However, for the vast majority of things... it was the forlorn expressions that seemed to catch my attention. A look, a vacant stare that suggested that these people weren't doing what they wanted to do. It was as though their dreams, their hopes... they'd just vanished. They'd got up and walked away... drifting on into the abyss of the human mind. I wanted to walk up to these people and try to offer them some kind of encouragement, some kind of hope that would suggest their dreams and their ambitions were not wasted. That, their goal was always going to be around... as long as they could live to see it. It was at that point that I had to hold myself back from walking into each of their lives with these hopes and these dreams... these words that would suggest that nothing ever truely comes to an end on that scale. I was a man... almost a man like these men and women. My hope and my desire, the will to succeed was there but the success that was supposed to follow hung in the balance. Like a man free-falling through the skies, my ambition was the very same. The story of my career began in the offices of an organizations building. I don't remember the name or anything but it was a short time after I'd discovered my opponent in the first round of this huge tournament I'd applied for. Despite not having any real experience outside of the training ring, the organizers decided that it would be interesting to see how far I could get... or perhaps, how easily I'll be destroyed. Rather than think about the negative though, I kept trying to tell myself that I could get through the first round. I kept telling myself... that nothing was impossible. I remember sitting in the office of one of the executives, looking around to soak in all of the atmosphere. The entire place was fitted with oak structures, the cabinets, the desk, the chairs and shelves. It was almost like walking back in time, to a period where such things were true delicacies. Of course, the modern equipment scattered around the business area was enough to throw me out of my dream-like state and back into the real world. I glanced across at the various trophies sitting on the shelves, the various decanters and bottles of old-aged whiskey that lines the panel-less front of the cabinets. Eventually, I turned to look straight ahead, all the way down to the desk at the back of the room. A middle-aged man was sat, a broad grin spread across his face. It was both a sign of expectancy and politeness, an attempt to coax the less-informed individual that this place was some kind of happy-family. I strolled over to the desk and sat down in the opposing chair when gestured to do so. I expected the man to stand up and perhaps shake my hand... but alas, that formality didn't come to pass. "So, who are you and what exactly do you want?" the gentleman asked, his hands clasped together whilst his elbows lay upon the table. He looked rather relaxed, extremely calm at the very least. I had a feeling that either this guy didn't know who I was or he simply didn't bother to consult any kind of schedule. I tried to hide the disappointment of being nothing more than another face coming through... but, I couldn't. Even if I wasn't important enough to be known, at least the guy could have taken a look at who was next to come in. Hell, even my family doctor made more of an effort to look at the appointment sheet before I entered the office. Noticing that I wasn't looking too enthusiastic with the lack of an introduction... or knowledge of who I was, the gentleman rapidly brushed through a pile of papers and stopped when he reached an appropriate point. "Ah-ha..." he began again, his voice trailing off before coming back with a bang, "... I know you, Mr Drock. Well, not in that way... but I remember the name at the very least. Aren't you the fellow who wants to jump from the training room into the big leagues?" My dejected face wasn't so dejected anymore. Sure, there was still the hole that came with not being recognized when I walked into the office... but at least this guy knew a little about Korey Drock. At least he knew what I was trying to accomplish. A hint of smile coming to my face, I nodded and answered in the politest way that I could, "... Yes, that would be me. I've entered a big competition known as the GTT in an attempt to burst through the ceiling of this great business." My voice was enthusiastic even though I was a pessimistic character at heart. My enthusiastic tone of voice was welcomed by the gentleman, he seemed to appreciate it more than I would have. "So, you've entered a tournament with some of the biggest names in our business... with the sole hope of being able to get so much coverage, that you'll be able to jump into the bigger side of the picture without having to put in the years of work and effort. The short-cut to the bigger paydays is what I like to think of a move like that. A cheap route to success... beating a handful of guys." the gentleman responded, his smile still spread across his face. I couldn't tell whether he found amusement in the plans that I had. I couldn't even tell if he was just trying to get a reaction from me. Like everyone else, I didn't appreciate the attack at my dream... the attack at my desire... the attack at my sole ambition. Against my own judgement, I arose from the chair that had contained me and looked across the table at the gentlemen. "If I only wanted a little coverage, don't you think I would have joined something that was vastly international? Don't you think I would have tried to compete somewhere that has links to every step of this? An Olympic contest, perhaps? Some kind of worldwide competition to test myself. No, instead, I climb the first rung of the ladder with ambition and a pocket full of tricks. Yes, I want to be the man who is able to win this competition... I want to be the one who is allowed to stand at the finish line with no equal. Have you never had something that you wanted to do so badly? Something that you felt was absolutely needed? Like the little boy who will walk to school breathing the oxygen that he so desperately needs... like the little girl who reads the teenage magazines for the crushes that she fanatically desires, this isn't about running to the top of the ladder. If it was just about getting there... I wouldn't have the ambition, I wouldn't have the desire to get there. I wouldn't have spent the last four years of my life working toward something... something like this." I said, the true meaning behind my voice being impossible to describe in simply words alone. I'm a passionate man, I'll admit that much... because if I didn't I'd only be lying to myself. I'd be spitting in my own face if I couldn't come to terms with something like that. Does it matter if I win or lose? Will I continue to ascend if I am embarressed in my first professional encounter? Of course. The gentleman looked surprised at the way that my outburst had come across. Like many of us, when attacked, it's natural to react in a way that allows physical violence to enter the picture. When the meaning for your entry in something is undermined and abused, the only way forward for most people is to launch a physical attack. The gentleman had expected nothing more than such an attack... and for the first few seconds, I didn't expect anything different from myself. I stood there, the silence hanging over the room like a sharpened blade. I put my left hand over my eyes and sat back down, looking down at the floor as the outburst began to settle. "... People like yourself, they're not uncommon and yet, they're not common. Most people in your position would have done something to regret... something to symbolise the true explosion of anger, the erruption of an otherwise dormant volcano. Yet, unknown to both me... and probably yourself, you refused to go to physical retaliation. Instead, you stood up for yourself and defended the decision that you've made. You stood up and embraced the true meaning of what you're getting yourself into. Perhaps a mistake? Perhaps you don't fully understand what you're letting yourself get into. Personally, I don't even know if you're completely sure about who you're supposed to be facing in this first round encounter." the gentleman responded to my rant. As much as I didn't like to have my dreams and my desires trampled upon, he had a point about my entry into this contest. Unlike a lot of the people who were going to be looking for a win, I didn't know anything about anyone... I didn't even know anyone that was taking part. For all I knew, my actual opponent could be some kind of seventeen foot giant. The gentleman got up from his own seat and walked around the table, forcing me to turn in the chair to follow his movement. The man walked over to one of the cabinets and pulled a decanter filled with whiskey down. He opened one of the cabinet panels shortly thereafter and pulled out a couple of small glasses. "Care for a drink?" he asked, giving the whiskey decanter a gentle shake. "No... thank you." I responded, not wanting to indulge in such a horrific element of society. The gentleman shrugged his shoulders and pushed the spare glass to the side, removing the top from the whiskey decanter and pouring himself quarter of glass. Placing the decanter top down, he raised his glass and smelt around the rim. It was a typical behavioural element of a drinker, the apparent method of strength detection in the drink... or something to that cause. As I was not such a drinker, I could not actual verify the meaning of his actions. Regardless, he soon gulped the liquid down and slammed the glass back onto the table. With a slight chuckle, the gentleman backed off and moved toward one of the many shelves in the room. Upon each level was a collection of trophies and medals. I didn't take into account what they were for... but figured that they must have made him some kind of important figure. "Mr. Drock, whenever somebody comes into my office... be it for business or some kind of personal element, I always make sure they take a lot at something. Something that usually either spurs them onto doing something worthwhile with themself... or breaks them down into a hundred and one pieces. At the moment, I'm standing by a small portion of the trophies that I have acquired from being in this business. Quite intimidating, don't you think?" he asked, his tone of voice still rather happy. I looked over to the trophy count and ran over the potential number in my mind. He was right, being in a room filled with such personal accolades was rather intimidating. I nodded my head in response, prompting the gentleman to continue, "Intimidation is but a small element of victory against anyone. Your opponent, an individual known as Misfit... he is quite the intimidating type, isn't he? He's an individual who could send a chill running down your spine. After all, just as I have these trophies to display an element of my own personal skill... he will have various scalps to display the elements of his own personal skill. Do you understand what I'm saying?" "I think I'm understanding what you're trying to say... but as with many things in life, something like that is a lot easier to say than it is to do. Intimidation is only a concept in life, it's not a rule that anyone has to follow. However, I know plenty of people who assure me that such a trivial thing means nothing but when push came to shove, they could do nothing but stand in awe of their greatness. They could do nothing but stand and stare at the accomplishments... be amazed by the immense level of recognition." I responded, confident that I knew the point that was trying to come through. "Intimidation is just as much a concept as ambition, my dear boy. You must be sure of yourself... you must be sure of what you're going through if you want to even start looking at the other side of the first round. You can stand here and spill out everything about what you intend to do. You could bring in diagrams and graphs, every little piece of paper instructing me on the things that you're going to do. Hell, you could even bring in a barber shop quartet and get them to sing such information to me. What does it mean to be? Nothing. What should these mean to you? Nothing. If you truely want to make a go of something, you need to have the ambition here... in your heart." he said, his voice getting a little more serious but the same happy gesture being displayed. After saying 'here', the gentleman clenched his right hand into a fist and placed it over his heart, nodding his head at the same time. His words had given a silver lining to the meaning of his message, that single motion being the only thing that I needed to see. Following my dream, trying to accomplish my desire... it wasn't about just having a mindset to accomplish something. If you really wanted to prove to somebody that you had the ambition to get to the top, if you REALLY wanted to prove that you had it inside you... you needed to perform when the time comes. I smirked at everything that had happened, everything that had been said in just one meeting with this individual. To be completely honest, I still hadn't been properly introduced... but for some reason, that didn't seem to matter anymore. Just as the trophies had become insignificant upon his command so had the identity of the person that I was talking to. If I was going to defeat Misfit, if I was going to progress to the second stage if this tournament, I would need to have all of my ambition ready for battle. I would need to have a level of ambition so great... that NOTHING would be able to derail me.
