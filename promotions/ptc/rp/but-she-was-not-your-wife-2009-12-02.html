But She Was (Not Your Wife)

2 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2009-12-02 If you can keep your head when all about you Are losing theirs and blaming it on you, If you can trust yourself when all men doubt you, But make allowance for their doubting too… As he ran, Alias cursed his pack a day cigarette habit. The same habit that, no doubt, had allowed him to get through fifteen years of hard times, was now taking inche upon inche between them and him. One coffin nail at a time. He cursed each and every fat cigar he had every smoked; they where now railroad spikes. Still he had to let slip that devil-ish grin at least a little, at the recent attempt at a forced sabbatical from his cancer sticks. Those months without a smoke just might have added a few more feet to his current pace. His lungs burned, and Alias really had to wonder how long his left knee would hold up. Still he ran and he ran, through the streets of East L.A. Blessed be the years of in-ring conditioning and the iron will that was all his own. God damn the bullets at his back, though. At very least the west wind was also there at his back, and almost like an otherworldly force it helped keep his eyes six feet high as opposed to six feet low. If you can wait and not be tired by waiting, Or, being lied about, don't deal in lies, Or, being hated, don't give way to hating, And yet don't look too good, nor talk too wise… The shifting vibration of the cell phone shuffled it towards the edge of the nightstand. Karina Wolfenden’s head stirred her echoing dreams into the ether, before she eyed the phone, unimpressed. As it teetered half way over the edge, Karina snatched it up and mustered simply: “Yup.” The Pulp Hero pulverized the pavement beneath his feet. The old off-white Adidas that he wore, were holding up reasonably well. The same thing could be said about the Original Pulp Hero himself, in this race against the reaper, as he explained his situation in detail to the Wolf. There was a pregnant pause in the conversation, which had to do more with Alias evading a man who was charging at him with a large sword (which might have simply been an even larger knife, but that was neither here nor there) then anything else. Half asleep, Karina shouted into the phone. “I want you to go to a bar called the Reservoir Lounge…” She then gave the Big Bad directions to said bar, hoping he would receive the full information of his ‘bling tag’ as it sounded like he was dealing with a few problems at the moment. Rubbing the crusted sleep from her eye she remember the second piece of information connected to the Res… and quietly cursed the Devil for being kind of a dick in this regard. If you can dream - and not make dreams your master, If you can think - and not make thoughts your aim, If you can meet with triumph and disaster And treat those two imposters just the same, If you can bear to hear the truth you've spoken Twisted by knaves to make a trap for fools, Or watch the things you gave your life to broken, And stoop and build 'em up with wornout tools… A man walks into a bar. Bwahahaha. Well suffice to say it was a meaner looking bar, then the one that would usually precede a punch line, and the man walking in looked even meaner then said establishment. Save for the laundry list of violence inflicted upon his person, that follows: Three facial lascerations. Possible cornia damage on the left eye. Five separate knife wounds in the right arm and left arm. One gun shot to the back right leg. Bullet went in, didn’t come out. Alias shambled through the door, and almost fell against the bar. Even the most hardened of patrons stopped and stared. What matter of man, was this? He almost looked as if he had crawled back from the dead and walked through a firing squad to do so. Suffice to say, a place at the bar was given to him. Looking up at the man behind the bar, a man who looked not unlike a certain guitarist for the Chicago rock band ‘Machines That Think’, Alias sputtered: “I’ll have a Cosmopolitan.” The bartender eyed Alias, up and down, with a small grin now. “You sure?” Alias was. “Sure thing, princess.” When a man such as Angelo Deville creates a safehouse for those connected enough to know what to ask for, every detail is thought out. Down to the off chance of the ghost of Deville getting a chance to have a good laugh a the toughest bastards possible ordering the gayest drink he could think of for safety. The entirety of the Reservoir Lounge erupted in laughter. The eyes of the Pulp Hero scanned the patronage as they joined in, all seemingly chuckling along at the walking dead who had ordered a girl’s cocktail. There was the large Japanese man with the lotus tattooed on the up-side of his bald dome. The no-necked son of a bitch with the coke bottle glasses and cleft palate, but also with what seemed like 300-odd pounds of muscle to back it up. The atypical Harley-Davidson rider, the bastard son of Willie Nelson and Hank Williams Sr, with an extra ton of blue collar horse shit for flavor, and the man who looked to be either a government agent or Mad Men extra. It was a diverse, dangerous looking crowd and Alias would take every single of ‘em on, if that were the way that this was going to be. First though, he had a date with the floor. As Alias blacked out from both internal injuries, loss of blood and exhaustion, he thought it’d be swell if he didn’t break his nose when he hit the hardwood floor. When he hit the hardwood… a pop was heard. He had broken his nose. The bartender let out another chuckle. If you can make one heap of all your winnings And risk it on one turn of pitch-and-toss, And lose, and start again at your beginnings And never breathe a word about your loss, If you can force your heart and nerve and sinew To serve your turn long after they are gone, And so hold on when there is nothing in you Except the Will, which says to them: "Hold on" “Bigby?” Alias would later recall seeing Karina, backlit by basement lighting, as angelic. He wouldn’t tell her this, but he’d remember the moment just the same. “Shut your mouth, bitch.” Now this was not Alias’s reply, incase you were wondering, but instead the man standing over Alias’s head. See, given what went down between Karina and Angelo, before he ‘disappeared’, she was not exactly popular with his people. The Original Pulp Hero instinctively shot his arm up and grabbed the man by the throat. A short outburst of yelling from all sides soon followed, like a cavalcade of shocked dominos, before Alias was hit in the stomach (or more specifically the bandaged Black title around his mid-section) causing him to release his grip. “Glad you’re awake, asshole. Another move like that though, and you’re out on your ass to fend for yourself.” To be perfectly honest, Alias would much rather not have to fend for himself. Even though his current situation wasn’t exactly five star medical care (the only reason a cockroach wasn’t crawling across his open wounds was best the lights where on) and the man who had stitched up his deeper cuts was back-alley doctor at best (though at least he had used 85 proof moonshine to clean the sewing equipment), this place was as safe as Vox Nihili could hope for at the moment. Though there where untrusting looks towards Ms. Wolfenden, no one had dared touch her because before her and Deville ‘ended’, he had put a Halo on her, meaning she wasn't to be touched and everyone on his payroll would be held responsible if she was. Even when it ended, and Deville probably wanted her dead himself, he didn't lift it. As it goes, unless they hear Deville say to lift the halo, or see a body after his apparent death, they might wanna assume they're still bound to it. If you can talk with crowds and keep your virtue, Or walk with kings - nor lose the common touch, If neither foes nor loving friends can hurt you, If all men count with you, but none too much... Closing his eyes to recover with Karina by his side, Alias longed for the closest second to recovery, when he could once again sit-up with a minimal amount of pain as opposed to a wall of screaming nerve-endings, all sending the same signal to STOP whatever the hell it was that he was up to. “Did I ever tell you about the time when I gave my brother a black eye no more then a day before my cousins wedding?” Karina said quietly, leaning forward in her steel chair. “hah… ah… No.” A loud thud came from the bar upstairs and then the breaking of glass. It wasn’t long before gunfire broke out and wouldn’t you know it… in came the crowd that was there when Alias first awoke. What was happening upstairs wasn’t an escalating bar fight, no it seemed that those looking for Alias… had found him. “Kari, we’re here to get you out.” The words were forced, but had the Halo to back them up. Luckily that’s all the extra weight they needed to worry about, however. “Your friend is on his own.” Karina Wolfenden stood hard and fast beside Alias, as she knew the state he was win would inevitably leave him open to all the worst that was coming to him. As Angelo’s old boys approached her, to take her away by force if necessary, The Wolf motioned to the Big Bad. “I’m moving the Halo to Christopher Sheffield, then.” Alias looked at Karina with surprise, and she only winked at her friend. The man at the front of the pack, with an angular stout face, and graying closely cropped hair, sneered at the Wolf. “Would you really like that to be done?” K-Wolf nodded without a second thought. On the up-tic of the nod, the grey haired man caught K-Wolf with a flush right, sending her to one knee. “So it’s done. Deville may have never lifted the Halo on you, but he would have wanted that.” The gray-haired man turned away from K-Wolf and motioned for the other two former foot soldiers of Deville to grab Alias and help him to his feet. The Pulp Hero refused to move any further then a standing base, until Karina herself got back to her feet. As smoke drifted down from upstairs, and the heat of the escalating fight found it’s way down the stairs along with it the back-alley doctor (at his best) rushed down to get to safety. Soon the Big Bad and the Wolf were brought by the Devil’s old boys through an old rum runners underground passage way, and out under the building they escaped, emerging from the other end, blocks away. Alias looked back to see the Reservoir Lounge burn, and like his past, the building now fell apart behind him. He would never look back again. If you can fill the unforgiving minute With sixty seconds' worth of distance run - Yours is the Earth and everything that's in it, And - which is more - you'll be a Man my son. 'It really wasn't that pretty, but she was (not your wife)' is a lyric from "Rise Up With Fists!" by Jenny Lewis and The Watson Twins off of 2004 album Rabbit Fur Coat. "If" is a short poem written by Rudyard Kipling.
