Respect

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 Voice Over: The GTT…nothing more needs to be said in order to hype up such an event. The name itself carries with it all the weight of fame and legend that it could ever achieve. With every year the GTT comes and goes and another champion is crowned. This year will be no different. 128 men and women have enterted the honored and respected tournament to find out just who is the champion for this year. Another thing hasn't changed this year either. The fact that people are blowing their loads over a handful of wrestlers. They are dying with excitement to see matches like Angelo Deville vs Rich Rollins. They can't wait to see if Seymour Almasy will put together one more huge tournament run. The standard fair when it comes to a huge tournament such as this. But there are 128 wrestlers in this tournament and everyone of them would die to be the champion. Will give it their all to be the one holding the plaque and the bragging rights that come with being known as one of the best ever. One such man happens to be a champion in a major company. That man looks to make an impact in this tournament not only for himself but for the company in which he champions. Voice over ends and the black screen opens up to a brightly lit backstage area of some sort where we find Buddy Kingfisher, dressed in an expensive suit, wearing the Core Legacy Championship over his right shoulder and a serious look on his face. Buddy: Hello I'm Buddy Kingfisher. As you can see I'm currently the Legacy Champion of the federation known around the world as Core. Now I step into this tournament, one with a long tradition of great competition, and I'm known as a "dark horse". I'm the kind of guy that no one notices or cares about at the outset of this thing cause I'm not one of the tournament favorites. I'm not Angelo Deville, Seymour Almasy, Rich Rollins, Paddy O'Shea, or whatever other name that comes to mind when people start making their pre-tournament predictions. I'm just the Legacy Champion of Core Wrestling. An unknown to many in the Primetime Central community. The under dog, you could say, as I walk into a tournament full of men and women with far more experience and wrestling knowledge than I have. Still there is one thing that we all share as we come into this thing. It is even bigger than the dream and hope of winning it all. Lets face it we all want to win but 127 of us are going to go home having not accomplished that goal so the odds aren't exactly in the favor of anyone here. No the one thing we all share is the belief that when all is said and done we will have gained something more important in this sport than anything else. It is the driving force behind my entering the tournament and it will be the fuel I use to propel me through the tournament. One thing, one goal, one truly burning desire. Respect.
