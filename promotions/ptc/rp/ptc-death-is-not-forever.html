Death Is Not Forever...

4 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“They’ve all abandoned you.” In his heart he knew this was true. His dream world has long since been abandoned by all its inhabitants. Dreams would continue without him, but he would still fade. A figurehead all along. He glared at the woman who blasphemed him (despite the truth in her words) and snarled. “They will return,” he said with conviction, he had begun to believe his own lies, “I am too strong to let this happen and we both know it.” He wore a blue cloak. Although calling it blue would be an insult to the people who made it. It was a solid cloth, but it shimmered as if it wasn’t even there. It seemed like a mirage against the wearers body. “But it is true. You’ve been dead for too long, resigning yourself to this… this… falsehood of a kingdom has left you weak.” She sat down next to him and held his hand, her nails were black and sharp but she was careful with them. She left no mark on his skin. “Lover, it is time to abandon this before it becomes the true death of you.” He scoffed, “I’ve already felt death twice times before. Once by my own hand to become what I am now, a King amongst the Gods, imbued with the power of humanities dreams and nightmares… then I tasted death by the hand of that burnt excuse for a demon that I once called an ally.” He spat, “That was not my choice.” He stood, letting her arm fall off his shoulder. The movement dragged the hood of his cloak back and off of his head. His hair matched the garment and it too seemed like it was flickering against the almost endless dark of his realm. “I no longer fear death. I can exist in spite of it.” Now it was the woman’s turn to be angry, she stood with a flourish and turned her love to face her. “You are being foolish and stubborn. You are no longer the Prince of Dreams as you once were, your power is failing you. When this Kingdom has faded away you will have nothing left, and you will fade with it!” She was less careful with her nails, gripping his arm tight and drawing blood beneath his raiment. He barely noticed. “Then what will you be? A wasted and forgotten icon, never again to walk on the Earth, the Heavens, or anything in between. You are a god, not a mortal, you do not have the luck that they have. You will not die and be judged, you will just be gone.” She let go, and for a second her true concern was known, “And I will be alone.” “Would that be such a bad thing? You have toiled away at my side for long and faced much darkness because of me. Would I really be missed that much?” She nodded, her eyes clouding over slightly, “When I’m here, with you, I am sane. I can speak normally and comprehend without problem… but down there…” He understood. She lost her sanity by just setting foot on that planet. It was a chaotic place, full of violence and pain. She was blessed, or cursed, with second sight. She could feel every scream of anguish and see every single future. It had driven her quite insane, but when she was in his realm he could keep her grounded and lucid. He made the rules here. No others. “I’m sorry, mon amour.” He held her hand in his, “My mind doesn’t rest, not even after a year of death… nearly two years of exile.” “Has it been that long?” He sighed to himself. It had. He gave up the mortal coil and retired to the world of his own making almost two years to the day. He didn’t regret the decision, but he felt the pang deep in his heart. Since he left he had become forgotten, and his realm was proof. It was once the garish, glittering, pride of the Inbetween. No God had a more extravagant realm. He housed all of existences dreams and nightmares within its endless bounds. He was king of the fantasy creations. It had been six years since he first took the mantel and scepter of his position from his predecessor. When he received the realm it was just as bad as it was now, it decayed from years of ill use. Nightmares were loose and running through the real world. It took almost all of his strength then, and it lead to his first death. When he returned he was stronger, he understood what the position truly meant, he acted out and traveled through dreams and nightmares equally. Always being the mediator between Good and Evil but never making a decision on his own standing in the great cosmic struggle. It was in death that he became his strongest, after being cast to hell by Clyde years ago he rose again. His body was but a vessel, he didn’t need it to exist. He only needed it to walk fully through the waking world, but he could exist in dreams and memories. But without the memories, without the peoples dreams-… he was nothing. He would grow weaker and weaker and his empire would crumble and turn to the sand it was made out of. He knew it was all his fault. He stood there contemplating the fact, rolling it over in his head. He was not a full god, in fact only a quarter of that type of blood flowed through his veins. He could not be eternal, he had to rely on the power of existence to pump his heart. Without it… he didn’t want to think that thought. “I grew to complacent. I was fine staying here. I grew fat with my own power.” He closed his eyes tight, looking towards the ground. He wasn’t fighting tears, sadness was a weak emotion, but instead trying to fight off the pain of the realization. “I was happy just existing. I was cocky, this is all my fault. How could I have thought I would have had enough of a legacy to sustain myself?” “You couldn’t have known…” “I should have known!” He exploded at her, she took a step back and braced herself against the black marble wall of the throne room, “I should have known that humanity was too fickle to rely on. Useless wastes of flesh and organs. How could I have thought they would have the attention span to remember me or the imagination to sustain me?” He walked to the center of the room. There he could stand and see the Earth. He could have seen any planet from any place in his kingdom, but he always enjoyed the symbolism of making everything viewable only from the center of the throne room. The image of Earth he saw was distorted, it looked like a hologram shown through a television with bad reception. His powers were waning drastically. “They let television destroy their minds. Humanity lacks imagination, they lack creativity.” His mouth contorted into a snarl, “They lack the ability to even think for themselves, letting their pathetic idols shape the way they live. If it wasn’t for Hollywood these people wouldn’t even know how to diet.” Turning his head, he stared one last time at the planet projected on the obsidian. “If it wasn’t for nature they wouldn’t even know how to die properly. These are the people that I owe existence to? These are my brothers?” He paused. For a second his face drained of all emotion, then just as quick as it emptied it filled. Flame reaches his eyes and he lashed out. With a punch he shattered the obsidian into thousands of tiny pieces, the image of Earth fizzled off and dissipated. “I will not accept this fate.” His hand bled, it dripped amongst the broken stone. The red and the black contrasted in a sickly beautiful way. “Lover, what will you do?” She was genuinely curious. He was too weak to surpass death and regain a human form again. “There seems to be very little left that you could do.” “I know. There are but a handful of options, and you are right. I could not regain a form that would allow me to exist amongst reality. No. That is not what I plan to do.” She was confused. That was the only option. It was a brutal catch twenty-two. In order to regain his past strength he had to draw his power from the dreams and memories of humanity and the rest of existence. In order to do that he had to ‘gently’ remind them of his existence, convince them to dream again… but for all that it would take a great amount of power. He can no longer travel in the real world, so the option is lacking. “Than what?” He clenched his fist, the blood squeezed out through his fingers and flowed heavy, he didn’t even feel it. “I will have to borrow power from elsewhere…” He gritted his teeth, the next thing he was to say hurt him more than any death, more than any blow he had ever received. “I will seek a meeting with God himself. Without dreams his kingdom is nothing.” She gasped loudly, covering her mouth with her petite hand. “You are speaking madness.” “What else can I do?” He walked towards her, his footsteps echoed loudly as if all other sound had been suddenly sucked from the world except for his feet meeting the floor, “Would you have me disappear from this realm? Have my kingdom fall apart and let any jackyl or scavenger take it over for their own devices?” He scoffed, “How about I take the mantle and hand it over to that one eyed bastard Odin, maybe I can curry favor with him and his ravens? Or Anansi? Or maybe I could travel on the surface of the sun itself and ask help from Ra and his people.” She was still taken aback, “But we would not be well received in the kingdom of God. He holds grudges and he holds them long.” “Yes. I know. Lucifer Morningstar can attest to that.” He couldn’t help but be amused by thinking about the fallen angel and his plight. “But I cannot so easily give away the keys to my kingdom as he could. I give both of them power. Dreamers populate heaven, nightmares populate hell. Without this realm they both decay… and when God decays what is left for them?” “But why do you care? You just spoke about how useless they have become. Would it be terrible for humanity to be finished? For the Earth to return back to its roots. The trees could grow back through those monolithic skyscrapers and new Gods would be created. New Gods, maybe Gods who can help the world instead of simply being bemused by it.” For the second time in as long as he could remember he smiled, “Ah. Now that is the fire that I first saw in you, and the reason I fell in love with you with such force. Maybe someday one will try to commit genocide against the Gods in order for a fresh start, but that one will not be me.” After a slight hesitation he added “Well… at least not this time.” He put the hood of his cloak back up and retrieved his lovers for her, putting it onto her shoulders reverently. It was made of an animal that would never exist again and existed only once in order to make the cloak, the white fur shimmered as vibrantly as his own blue cloak. “I would ask that you stay here where it is less risky and where you would enjoy sanity for at least a touch of time… but I have known you and loved you for too long to think that you would listen to me.” She stood on her toes and kissed him lightly on the lips, “Then let me thank you for understanding me so well. Maybe I will be of some use… but I doubt it highly, lover.” She would lose her delicate grip on sanity the minute she left this realm. There were few places in the Inbetween that still paid tribute to the Lord of Dreams. Whether it was for true reasons or not, they were soon to enter very dangerous places that would not be kind to either of them. “Whether this is for folly or success… I hope when I return to this realm it will be whole again,” he turned to Talena and held her hand tight, “I hope that we will still be whole also.” She leaned towards him and rested her head on his shoulder, her eyes were closed. She meant to enjoy the last few seconds of lucid thought, she thought of her love with him and their future… but she didn’t worry. She trusted him. “And when I return… I will once again be known as DarkStar.”
