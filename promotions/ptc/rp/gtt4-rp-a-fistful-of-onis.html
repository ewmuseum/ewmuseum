Scottywood Peter Vetra Title: A Fistful of Onis Featuring: Tsunami Oni Date: 15th Sep 2004 Ever notice how super-heroes always have mementos from their past around? Like Batman. He's got that giant penny and dinosaur in his cave. And Superman keeps the rocket he was sent to Earth in at his Fortress of Solitude. Hell, the Flash has a whole freakin' museum. Spider-Man ain't got nothin,' but that's 'cause he fights fat guys with bowl cuts and stunt men with fishbowls on their heads. Sure, Aunt May is a relic, but not exactly one you can show off. No wonder it took him so long to score with Mary Jane. There was a point to this somewhere... Oh yeah. Although Tsunami Oni is nobody's idea of a superhero, he too keeps mementos from his various battles and schemes. In fact, he is looking them over at this very moment. There is a warehouse in a rough part of San Francisco. A very rough part. OK, it's in Oakland. Here is where we find the Vivaldi of Violence, Tsunami Oni. He has come on the eve of the GTT4 to inspire himself. To be reminded of the many accomplishments he has... accomplished. To be inspired to further glories. And to get a chuckle as he remembers all the nasty things he has done to fellow human beings. He stops in front of a bust with a horned mask on it. Although he no longer wears them, Oni is proud of his masks. This one in particular was the one worn during the UWF's Contest of Champions, where he ended Lacuna Debris' winning streak and became Champion of Champions. And he didn't event cheat during that match. Well, he didn't cheat A LOT. Cut the guy some slack. Ah, and there is a bunny suit. This was one of the suits worn by PeTA members as they assaulted Seymour Almasy for his rabbit-slaying ways.Gee, I wonder who brought them to the building? Maybe a certain Devil from the Deep Blue Sea? Some things couldn't be brought to the warehouse, Like Oni's orbiting laser cannon. However, there is a lovely photo of the cannon being used to melt the dress off the Statue of Liberty. Oh look, there's the radioactive skull of the Loch Ness Monster, acquired after Oni dropped a nuclear bomb on her to piss off Joshua Dunn. And no Tsunami Oni collection would be complete without a TV showing Oni delivering the Flying Monkey Bomb to Moraless on a constant loop. Gazing at his collection, Oni stops at a large glass case. Inside is his imperfect duplicate, Bizarro Oni. No, not a sculpture of him, the actual idiot. Bizarro Oni: Let me in Let me in! Me promise to be bad! Oni: And it seemed like sch an excellent idea at the time. But Oni's most prized possession isn't on display here. Rather, it is sitting right next to him, licking its scaly reptilian penis. No, not Dick Cheney. It's Tsunami Oni's pet Komodo dragon, Rodzilla. A creature that has left his mark on the UWF, usually in the form of scarring after he latches his teeth on some poor yutz's crotch. Oni: Ah, Rodzilla. We have been through so very much together... Rodzilla responds with whatever noise Komodo dragons make. Maybe he sounds like Scooby-Doo or Astro from "The Jetsons." Yeah, let's go with that. Oni: When I look at you I can't help but think... damn, I wish I could reach my genitals with my mouth. But then I think about that day so long ago that our bond was forged, a bond that remains strong to his very day... Suddenly, a mist blows in, obscuring all from a view. This means... FLASHBACK TIME! There is a small town in Baja California. Gee, that's fun to say. Baja. Baja. You know what else in fun to say? Cabo. And Tijuana. And Cancun. A lot of places in Mexico has fun names to say. Hey, have you ever noticed the more useful the organ the more boring the name. Brain, heart, lungs... those names suck. But appendix! That's a name with panache! OK, focus... This small town goes by the name of El Abismo. It is a dry, dusty place of old buildings and older vendettas. Not the place for underage coeds to get their drink on. In fact, the only bar is a seedy dive called Fat Pablo's. Inside, Fat Pablo is serving spirits to some of the shadiest characters this side of Haliburton. It's a typical day of drinking and glaring silently. The best glarer is a big man with an eye patch named One-Eyed Hector. Damn, he can glare with that one eye. The good times are broken up when the door bursts open and a small, weaselly man runs in. This is Weaselly Miguel. Weaselly Miguel: Amigos! Amigos! I got big news! Fat Pablo: Why did you call us "amigos?" Couldn't you just say "friends?" Or "buddies?" Weaselly Miguel: But we're Mexicans. In Mexico. Fat Pablo: Yeah, but everything else we're saying is in English. Weaselly Miguel: Hey! You're right! We are speaking English! Fat Pablo: So quit throwing in random Spanish words to sound authentic. It's annoying. Everybody know we're supposed to be speaking Spanish. But then most of the people couldn't understand us. Weaselly Miguel: What people? Fat Pablo: You know, those people. Weaselly Miguel: Where? Fat Pablo: You can't see them, but there out there. Weaselly Miguel: Oh... Fat Pablo: So what's your big news? Weaselly Miguel: I just saw the biggest Japanese guy right here in El Abismo! Fat Pablo: How big was he? Weaselly Miguel: Big! He must be five-eleven! Fat Pablo: That's it? Weaselly Miguel: Well, he's Japanese, man! They don't get that tall! Fat Pablo: How many Japanese guys do you know? Weaselly Miguel: Well, there's that Mr. Miyagi dude... Fat Pablo: You're an idiot. Weaselly Miguel: Listen to me, man! He wasn't just big! He was horny! Fat Pablo: So send him to the brothel. Voluptuous Esmeralda will treat him right. Weaselly Miguel: No! I mean he had horns! On his head! Fat Pablo: What? One-Eyed Hector: Hey! Keep it down! We're trying to silently glare over here! Weaselly Miguel: He had horns! Like some sort of devil! And he was headed this way! Gah! There he is! With a yelp, Weaselly Miguel dives under a nearby table. The door to the bar is flung open and there stands a man, impressively backlit by the desert sun. he is dressed in a pancho and cowboy hat, which is a neat trick considering he is wearing a mask with really big horns. He clenches a hand-rolled cigarette in his teeth. He scans the room, gazing at everyone... Fat Pablo, One-Eyed Hector, Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto, Randomly Giggling Jorge, and Weaselly Miguel, still cowering under a table. Confidently, he walks to the bar and takes a seat. Fat Pablo: Ain't seen you before, stranger. Oni ('cause it's Tsunami Oni, in case you couldn't figure it out): I am not from around here. Fat Pablo: Where you from, then? Oni: The Deep Blue Sea. Fat Pablo: Uhhh.. OK... you want a drink, mister? Oni: Do you have sake? Fat Pablo: I have tequila. Oni: Is it like sake? Fat Pablo: Sure. Reaching for a glass and a bottle, Fat Pablo pours Tsunami Oni a shot. The Vivaldi of Violence knocks it back. Oni: I do not believe this. Fat Pablo: You don't like tequila? Oni: No, I can not believe I have stooped to ripping off "The Three Amigos" for material. Fat Pablo: It happens to all of us. Think of it as a fromage. Oni: Fromage? Fat Pablo: You, know a tribute. Oni: That would be "homage." Fat Pablo: Oh. What's "fromage" then? Oni: It would be French for "cheese." Fat Pablo: I got it. Maybe it would be a cheesy tribute, eh? Oni says nothing. Fat Pablo: Uh... need another belt? Oni: After this line of conversation, yes. Fat Pablo pours the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea another shot. Fat Pablo: So why are you in El Abismo, stranger? Oni: I am looking for the one called... Dirty Sanchez. At the mention of that name, a chill settles over the bar. Fat Pablo looks nervous. The patron glare harder. Weaselly Miguel cowers more... weaselly. One-Eyed Hector gets out of his seat and walks over to the bar, Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto and Randomly Giggling Jorge a step behind him. He leans in right by Tsunami Oni. One-Eyed Hector: Hey, stranger. That's not a name you say too loud if you want to stay healthy! Randomly Giggling Jorge: Hee hee hee! Tsunami Oni turns and stares at One-Eyed Hector right in the eyes. Well, eye and patch. Fat Pablo is polishing a glass, which is what bartenders do whenever there's about to be trouble. Oni takes out his cigarette and blows into the face of Randomly Giggling Jorge. But instead of secondhand smoke, Randomly Giggling Jorge gets a faceful of EVIL BLUE MIST~! Randomly Giggling Jorge : Hee hee- AHHHHHHHHHH!!! Before the other two can react, Oni takes his cigarette had puts out on One-Eyed Hector's patch! One-Eyed Hector: My God ! My God! Sweet Jesus, I'm blind! I'm blind! Wait a minute... I'm already blind in that eye. What the hell? One-Eyed Hector looks back at Tsunami Oni just in time to see the tequila bottle coming right at him! Glass and skull collide and One-Eyed Hector falls to the floor. Fat Pablo: You gonna pay for that, stranger? Oni: No. Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto: Oh yes he is! Grinning a grin made up primarily of bloody sockets and a few yellow stubs of teeth, Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto draws out a big knife. Tsunami Oni grins back and draws out a katana from beneath his pancho. As Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto stares dumbfounded, Oni's leg shoots up and nail him in the mouth. The last of Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto;s teeth fall the the floor. Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto: My geeg! My geeg! As Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto bends over to gather his "geeg," Tsunami Oni conks him on the head with the hilt of his katana. Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto joins his teeth on the floor. Tsunami Oni notices Randomly Giggling Jorge recovering from the EVIL BLUE MIST~. So Oni decides it time to get off of his bar stool. And then use said bar stool to knock Randomly Giggling Umberto silly. The Vivaldi of Violence stares down at the carnage as he hides his katana back under his pancho. He turns to Fat Pablo. Tsunami Oni: Tell Dirty Sanchez to be on guard. Tsunami Oni has... an issue to discuss. And lose some weight. That is just not healthy. Turning dramatically, Tsunami Oni starts walking out the bar. He stops when he sees Weaselly Miguel still cowering under the table. Oni kneels down and grins at him. Oni: BOO! Weaselly Miguel: AH! Weaselly Miguel faints dead away. Still grinning, Tsunami Oni stands and walks out of the bar. As he exits, he lets out a fiendish cackle. Oni: NYAHAHAHAHAHA!!! After leaving Fat Pablo's bar, Tsunami Oni walks over to a taco truck to get his grub on. Mmmm... taco truck. How can food made in a vehicle built in 1936 taste so good? Well, Oni enjoys his food and continues his quest for the elusive dirty Sanchez. He walking along the road when he sees a pretty young woman coming the other way. He politely tips his hat to her. She smiles and her big brown eyes glitter like... big brown shiny rocks. Hey, you know any brown jewels? So shaddup. This Hallmark moment is interrupted by a truck screeching to a halt next to them. In the back is One-Eyed Hector with a machine gun! He fires away as Randomly Giggling Jorge feeds the ammo belt and giggles randomly. Randomly Giggling Jorge: Hee hee hee! Bullets are flying everywhere! But Tsunami Oni dodge the hail of lead death! He's safe but then he notices the pretty young woman is in the line of fire! Oni tackles her just as bullets explode around her! Hey, he may be a supevillain, but he ain't scum. One-Eyed Hector stops firing, mainly because the machine gun is empty. Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto looks at the carnage from the driver's seat. Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto: You miffed! One-Eyed Hector: What do you mean I missed? I shot a hundred bullets at him! Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto: Loog at da baggard! Do you gee any bwoog? Sure enough, Tsunami Oni has no wounds on him. One-Eyed Hector: What? I missed? Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto: You miffed! And you were leff fan free feet away! One-Eyed Hector: Well, I only got one eye. Totally jacks up my depth perception. Tsunami Oni gets up off of the pretty young woman. Oni: Are you all right? Pretty young woman: I'm fine. Butt! Oni: But what? KLANG~! The butt of the machine hits Tsunami Oni right on his noggin. He falls into oblivion and back onto the pretty young woman. One-Eyed Hector: Ha! That got him! Let's go tell Dirty Sanchez! Randomly Giggling Jorge: Hee hee hee! Tsunami Oni comes to enough to see the truck drive off before he is consumed by darkness once more... ...and then he comes to again. But now he is no longer lying in some street on top of a pretty young woman. Instead he seems to be on a bed in a doctor's office, dresses in a patient's gown. And the pretty young woman is wearing a lab coat with a name tag that reads "Dr. Alejandra Valencia." Alejandra: Ah, you've joined the rest of us, stranger. Oni: My head... Alejandra: Yes, I got quite a bump there. Tenderly, Tsunami Oni touches his head. There's a bandage there over his mask. Just go with it. Sure enough, he can feel a bump there of Elmer Fudd-like proportions. But he also feels pain... somewhere else. Oni: Tell me. Was I shot? Alejandra: No, all the bullets missed. Oni: But I fell other pain. Alejandra: Where is this pain? Oni: It is in my... rectum. Alejandra flashes him a dazzling smile. Alejandra: I can explain that. You see, I'm a general practitioner here. That's all that's need in El Abismo. But my dream is to be a more specialized doctor. Oni: What sort of specialization? Alejandra: A proctologist! Oni: A proctologist? Alejandra: Yes! No part of the body hold as much fascination to me as the posterior! I swear I could stare at an anus all day. That pink star. That rosebud of flesh. The gateway to the magical tunnels of the bowels! Oni: I see... Alejandra's eyes glisten in a way that's more than a little disturbing. Alejandra: So when you were unconscious, I had to examine you. Oni: You had to... Alejandra: Yes! And what a treasure your buttocks are! So firm, so smooth... Not like the hairy flabby glutes that the men of El Abismo have! I was awestruck! I felt like Howard Carter unearthing the tomb of King Tut! Oni: I can imagine... Alejandra: So you understand I had to do more than look, I had to touch! And touch I did! All over your sooth and taught buttocks. And of course, the was you wonderful anus! Oni: Of course... Alejandra: And, of course, I had to probe it's mysterious depths! In the name of science. Oni: Science, sure... Alejandra: I was able to fit for finger up there! And with a little practice, I'm sure I could insert my whole fist! Oni: Well, let's not be hasty... Alejandra: Oh no! In fact, I have another idea! Oni: Great... Like a kid on Christmas, Alejandra scampers to the corner and returns with an archaic looking device. Alejandra: This is a a genuine 18th century enema machine! I'm a collector. Oni: Of course... Alejandra: And as a reward for saving my life, I would be honored to use it on you! Oni: A reward... Alejandra: Now some parts are somewhat rusty so I hope you've had a recent tetanus shot and.. hey! Tsunami Oni has heard enough and is running out of the office with his clothes under one arm. Alejandra looks over just in time to see his back going out the door, his gown flapping in the breeze. Alejandra: *sigh* Will I ever see such a perfect posterior again? Tsunami Oni and his perfect posterior duck into an alley. There he dresses, much do the delight of housewives hanging their laundry off of fire escapes. After dressing, he exits the alley, Turning a corner, he sees a familiar group glaring silently at him. Oni: Oh, damn. Randomly Giggling Jorge: Hee hee hee. One-Eyed Hector: You're dead now, gringo! Oni: Gringo? I will let you know I am from Japan! One-Eyed Hector: Hmmm, didn't think of that. Hey, Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto! Can a Japanese guy be called a gringo? Poor Dental Hygiene Umberto: I dunno. He loog preggry whibe. Just then, a woman's voice calls out behind the three thugs. Woman's voice: Out of my way, needledicks! The needledicks part and there stands a stunning Latina in tight jeans and a leather vest. Just a leather vest, no shirt. And it's unbuttoned. How hot is she? Imagine a threesome between Jennifer Lopez, Salma Hayek, and Penelope Cruz. Yeah, that's nice. Hold on, still imagining. Imagining just a little loner. Harder. Faster. Harder. Faster. And... done. Anyhoo, she's really hot. And on a leash is a large Komodo dragon. Both Latina and lizard look Tsunami Oni like he's a piece of meat. Woman: So you're the one that's been causing all this trouble with my boys. Oni: I did not want any trouble. I only wished to find the one called Dirty Sanchez. Woman: Then, you're in luck. Because I am Dirty Sanchez! Oni: You? Dirty Sanchez: You bet your ass. Know tell me, why have you been hunting for me? Oni: Answer me this, have you ever been to a Starbucks on top of an Aztec pyramid deep in in jungles of the Yucatan? Dirty Sanchez: Maybe. Oni: And did you carve "Dirty Sanchez Rulez" into a table there? Dirty Sanchez: Maybe. Why? Oni: Because the manager of that Starbucks is my mentor, El Mono Enmascarado! Dirty Sanchez: El Mono Enmascarado? The legendary Masked Monkey? Oni: Yes, he trained me in the ways of lucha libre. Very painfully I must add. And you dishonored with your vandalism! Dirty Sanchez: Wow, I loved seeing El Mono Enmascarado when I was young. Tell you what, I'll send him a check for a new table with an apology. That sound cool? Oni: Well... Dirty Sanchez: What? Oni: Well, I came all this way believing you were some big scary thug and we would fight. Dirty Sanchez: I'm scary. See my big lizard? The Komodo dragon looks up with what appears to be a smile. Dirty Sanchez: His name is Rodzilla. I'm a big Chicago Bulls fan. Have you ever seen a lizard this big? Oni: Well, I know where you can find a really large snake... Dirty Sanchez: Oh no, you didn't! Oni: Oh yes, I did! Dirty Sanchez: Oh no, you didn't! Oni: Oh yes, I did! Dirty Sanchez: I like you, stranger. I know you were hoping for a fight, so why don't we have a little brawl in bedroom? Oni: Sounds excellent. Dirty Sanchez: An attack in the sack. Oni: I look forward to it. Dirty Sanchez: Getting rough in the buff. Oni: Anytime, anyplace. Dirty Sanchez: You know I'm talking about sex, right? Oni: Of course. Dirty Sanchez: Good. I just wanted to be sure I wouldn't get you in my place and have you start beating me like a red-headed stepchild. When you work with idiots like these, sometimes you can't be subtle. One-Eyed Hector: Hey! We got feelings! Dirty Sanchez: Shut up, idiot. Randomly Giggling Jorge: Hee hee hee! Dirty Sanchez: You too. So, Mister... Oni: Oni. Tsunami Oni. Dirty Sanchez: Mister Oni, shall we retire elsewhere? So off goes Tsunami Oni and Dirty Sanchez. And they pretty much go at it like the Bunny Army of Doom on Viagra. Afterwards, Dirty Sanchez is lying in bed, smoking a cigarette and looking very pleased as Tsunami Oni dresses. Dirty Sanchez: Must you leave? Oni: I am afraid so. I have to start my career as a supervillain-slash-pro wrestler. People to see, heinous acts to commit... Dirty Sanchez: Anus acts? Oni: Heinous. Dirty Sanchez: Oh, for a minute I thought you were talking about Alejandra. Oni: Do not go there. Dirty Sanchez: Well, before you leave, I have a little gift for you. Oni: What? You gave me the clap? Dirty Sanchez: No, no. Penicillin cleared that up for me weeks ago. I've decided to give you Rodzilla. Oni: Really? Why? Dirty Sanchez: Because last night you showed me who the real Bull is. Oni: Well, I can not argue with you there. I shall take good care of him. Dirty Sanchez: I should warn you, he had a tendency to bite people on the genitals. Oni: Oh really, then we should get along just fine... The mists of memory blow again and we return to the present day. Tsunami Oni, in his current unmasked sexiness, is staring at Rodzilla with a look of nostalgia on his face. Or it's nausea. Never buy gas station sushi. Oni: Ah, Rodzilla, those were simpler times. And we've had so much fun since then. Rodzilla licks his teeth, as if he is remembering all the testes he's chomped on. Oni: And whenever I look at you, I can't help but remember that wild night in El Abismo. That was one freaky chica. Just then, a cell phone. It's Tsunami Oni's. Like Rodzilla would have one. Yeesh. Oni: Hello: Yes, I'm just mentally prepping myself for the tournament, Shiatsu. Oh, really? What's his name? Raven Calloway. Any relation to Cab Calloway? No? Too bad. Well, I'm taking Rodzilla for a walk then coming home. By the way, would you still have that leather vest? Great. See you soon. Tsunami Oni puts away his phone and looks at his mementos. Oni: All right, Rodzilla. Enough of old memories. Time to make some new ones. Tsunami Oni walks out of his warehouse with his Komodo dragon trotting behind him. The dark place stand empty and quiet, except for one lone voice... Bizarro Oni: Goodbye? Goodbye? Is anybody not here? Me can't breathe in this! Goodbye? Layout by [name withheld].
