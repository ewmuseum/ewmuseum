Massacre of the Mind '09 Chapter 1: In the Voice of a Duck

28 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2009-11-28 http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIleBDuZitY --Woody Guthrie, 1913 Massacre To catch you up I was told by an angel to get back into wrestling as the world was ending and I was needed. Moses told me through a burning bush that Satan had taken over heaven. Which means we’re all in the same boat; because if heaven doesn’t exist then upon death just like you neither do I. So I tracked down Jesus at a bar in California who has take the form of a very hot woman. At least I’m fairly certain it’s Jesus because just as I got some information he/she vanished. The only way to beat Satan was to lure him out of heaven back to earth where I can destroy him as we’d be on an even playing field. So I knew I had to get back to round two of GTT because the angel told me it’s all about the wrestling, win my match, and get back on my journey to beat Satan. Never put a sock on your pecker in a wrestling locker room as that’s where trouble always begins. Hitler didn’t turn crazy until he saw Mark Lewin place a lederhosen on his der Gockel in the lock room in Stockholm after beating Ulf Herman Sr. in a loser leaves town match. The whole thing sent Hitler bat shit crazy, and really who could blame the guy, that’s a tragic thing for anybody to see, let alone a psychopath. Fuck’em if they can’t take a joke. Never tell the company shrink, you think you’re the son of God outside of the ring, especially after placing said sock on your said pecker. Apparently they take shit very seriously. The boys in the back get it, but the stiffs in the suits don’t, and the quacks with the white jackets –forget about it. Now here I am locked up in a psyche ward after winning my round two match of the GTT tournament. A rising star yet here I am trapped inside a rubber room as the world moves closer to the end; as Satan fidgets with his digits conspicuously. I hear voices in my head, well actually whispers, but what they are saying is very loud and clear. Satan is in control and unless I can get him to come to earth before he can kill me, the end of us all is near. I have as much to lose as everybody else if he has indeed taken over heaven, as I just explained my immortality is no longer. What a predicament now in more peril after the whole pecker incident and me coming under the care of GTT head doctors. By head I don’t mean the ones in change but rather the nut doctors. You can only stare at the walls for so long before you start to crack. That’s how they break you and turn you insane to justify the incarceration. I can’t take it, and the red of the padded walls just enrage me more like a bull staring at a red cloth. I can’t take it so I sleep, the answer most Americans default to when their problems become to much to handle. When you’re young you’re ready to take the world by storm, as you get older the more you start to understand you bit off my then you could chew. Thank God I have a big mouth. I awoke to the sound of the orderly opening my cell. “You have a visitor,” said the black man wearing white, “he claims to be your Lawyer Al L.” “I don’t know no lawyers, nor do I know Al L.,” I said in the voice of a duck. “He’s a good man to know, he’s about to get your ass released into his care,” smiled the black man in white with slyness, like he knew something sinister I didn’t. The lawyer was a fat man, but the jolly kind of fat you just like, and didn’t know why, in the vein of Santa Clause or Tony Soprano. I didn’t have a reason to trust him but I also didn’t have many options left and another day of solitaire confinement would split…my very soul. Was the fat a man a man of God or a solider of Satan sent to make sure I met an early and final grave, somewhere in a prearranged hole in the desert? Damned if I do, damnation if I don’t. Trusting this fat man was my best route at that moment so I have to take it; however I can always find another road if I get stuck in traffic. “Fellow Traveler I’m your legal counsel,” said the fat man with the Ray-Bans. “I don’t like lawyer, or the obese for that matter, now state your business and move on.” I jelled back just to make things stickier and perhaps cause some kind of jam in the communication process. The trick with lawyers is don’t speak a language that makes sense. Babble as much as them and make bold aggressive hand gestures like you could kill like a maniac or conduct the most beautiful orchestra. It equalizes the playing field, and given their pride they give in and you get your way. “I’m here as your legal and medical counsel licensed both as a lawyer and a therapist. I’m here to take custody of you on behalf of your FATHER.” “How can I trust a slick talking lawyer/shrink? Is that even legal?? Plus I’ve heard Duke’s stories of his lawyers and you people are three times as sick as that man is. Are you even licensed in the sun state of California? “I am, as well as in the Universal Law governing the universe. I’m God’s top counselor. You understand Universal law don’t you?” “No. I need a refresher course,” I lied for your benefit. “The concept of law is universal. Just as law is governed here on earth the same is applied to the stars and skies, and Gods. You’re God El, or Jehovah, or in this cause your actual father; he is in charge of this universe in an executive role. His laws are met with opposition and balance just as yours are here on earth are, only our political parties are based on the known earth words such as Arts, Science, Imagination, Geometry, and military. Each very similar to their definition of those words here on earth. So the process is very similar however with us once a law is passed they are forever, and can’t be repealed. Basically your system is ours, given to the Egyptians and Babylonians through the poetry of their writers sent from the Heavens thousands of years ago. Think of it like this. You look at a compass and it always points North. Is it magic? No, it’s due to a magnetic pull created by the LAW OF GRAVITY which governs the planet earth. It is a law that was created by God and mandated by galactic enforcement. An absolute and a reality, even if not fully understood by your simple human mind,” the fat man boasted. I tuned out, as he went on and on clearly winning this mind game of will. To be fair he was a shrink, AND a lawyer, and I had been locked up for a few God damn days. It was the kind of time and the kind of place it was ok to damn God; as it really may be his own entire damn fault. At this point I couldn’t catch a break on a union shift. “Enough you long winded egomaniac I need no more of your pedigree and legal babble. What’s the next move?” I demanded in the kind of tone that sounded demanding yet sane. “You promise me to keep your socks on your feet and your pecker in your pants, and then you’ll be released into my custody. At which point we need to find your brother Jesus, and file a summons on behalf of the high galactic council that would require Satan to meet us here in court on earth.” I hate science fiction stories. “I promise,” I lied again. Lying was becoming a habit, in this, our lord’s year of ’09. The fat man drove a Cadillac which was impressive for a bible and legal thumper. I sat next to him in the front seat still wondering if he was sent to kill me or help me. Where we were going and why we were going there I did not know I was just happy to be moving. “Where are we going, and why are we going there?” I investigated. “To fight the zombies holding Jesus captive at the Cactus Ranch in Yuma,” he responded. “Now take these and call me in the morning star,” he said as he handed me two pills. “Are you fucking with me?” I questioned as I popped the neon green pills into my acid baring mouth. “Beba un Snapple, y actúe más responsable.” He said in Spanish using a Mexican dialogue none of which I understood. “I broke my mind I hope you can patch it up Doc,” I said. “Do you have insurance?” Said the fatman. “New job, not for another two months,” I laughed “Your right, I hope I can patch it up until then…..” Until then what? What in God’s name is happening? It’s so cold but honestly it’s so peaceful like watching a football game in a cold room with a perfect blanket and an old dog at your feet panting loudly. My childhood. Bunkbeds, best friends, and Nintendo. My mother now long dead awhom I haven’t seen since greets me as we drive by with a friendly wave. The air tastes like buttered popcorn. Heaven? No, it can’t be as my mother killed herself a sin that prevents her entrance into the kingdom of heaven. “Slow down I see a sign in the distance,” I yell madly! The sky was red like a sweet marinara sauce, and the cold turned into a blistering heat that boiled my blood and made my eyeballs sweat. Tornados whip across the desert sand as lightening strikes, and thunder booms, but the rain never comes. What a gyp! I've always liked to walk in the rain the best. The clearest head is found in the cleansing of the drops at that exact moment when your whole body just accepts being wet. Then, two days later you have a stuffy nose and a head cold, with all clarity lost for a few day. Everything comes at a price. Although where I was started resonating long before I read the sign, “Welcome to South Central Purgatory”. “You killed me you bastard,” I yelled at the fat man. “Never trust a lawyer, dipshit.” He factually stated. “Are you even a lawyer?” “Nope, a sketch comedian and grave yard salesman. “ What an asshole. I had never been to purgatory as I’ve always had a direct path to heaven. Purgatory is one big traffic jam where the closer you get to heaven the hotter it gets, and you don’t have air conditioning, and every sentence runs on, and it only has one lane to heaven, and its way over populated and congested. Once you are stuck in purgatory you are there for a long time, as it’s a long ride out of purgatory to heaven. Some have given up and just settled in the northern section of purgatory where the weather is a little more like that of the Earthly Midwest, and so is the lifestyle. Some people would rather just exist then get to heaven and deal with the traffic. Interesting attitude if you were in a philosophical mood, something I was not. “Very funny.” “I try,” laughed the jolly fat man. “So what now?” I wondered out loud. “I wasn’t kidding an army of zombies have invaded a trail park settlement and Jesus your brother is being held there by them.” He informed me. The only thing I hate more than sci-fi is zombie movies. I still didn’t know if I could trust him or not. How did he know that with heaven compromised, killing me would send me to my earned state of purgatory? Was this where Satan wanted me, to keep me busy trying to save my “brother” throwing me into a horde of angry zombies? It made sense, and really is the only thing that is making sense right now. It had to be. “Pull over I have to piss,” I exaggerated the truth. “Save it and piss on the zombies it’s like acid to them; we’re almost there.” “I’m going to piss on your mouth if you don’t pull over fat boy.” He hit the brakes and as soon as we stopped I bashed his face into the steering wheel. I’m pretty sure I broke his nose. “Aww you broke my fucking nose,” he yelled as his face turned as crimson red just like the sky. I bashed his head in again, and again, and a third time to complete the trinity. He was out cold, maybe dead. I jumped out of the car, opened his door and dragged his body out leaving it in the road under the hot Purgatory sun. I turned around and headed back in the direction we came leaving him in the dust of the cars spinning wheels. I spun the dial on the radio until I pulled in some Ramblin' Jack Elliott and relaxed as the ‘1913 massacre’ eased my bashed soul. I couldn’t beat myself up over the decisions I’ve made so I buried my black boots right into the pedal and chased reality. Speed is the ultimate exhilaration in heaven, earth, or hell. I was full speed back to Earth to kick Satans ass. Satan sent me to purgatory to get me off of earth and out of his hair. No doubt in my mind Earth is where he is. I just needed to make one stop and I hope you’ll understand. It is self indulgent of me but this is my one and only chance to make this happen. The tears filled my eyes as I saw her approaching in the distance. It’s been nearly ten years since that fateful day she stepped on a rainbow by standing in front of the midnight train. She was poetic right up until the end, and you have to give her points for that. I smiled proudly as she waved happily for me. I pulled up to the curb, turned off the radio, and wiped the tears from my eyes. She was imperfect, and was most of my life. She was a child who never grew up and never knew how to raise one. She did her best. She fought for me, and in her imperfection I knew love, and everything in this world was explained. For it was in her faults that defined humanity to me, and made the fireworks explosions art, and not destruction. I never appreciated her when she was alive because I was too consumed by what we didn’t have instead of what we had. Her late night’s ‘out’ paid the bills even if it left me scared and alone all night. I could dwell on the month without electricity when I was a freshman living like a colonist or I could remember the most perfect Christmas and how perfect she made it. How I always got what I asked for on my birthday and how she let me live without rules and structure. I got out of the car and she ran up practically tackling me and we hugged. I didn’t want it to end and neither did she as we stayed tightly embraced for what seemed to be an eternity. The love of a mother is forever and it can never be replaced. Here was a second chance even if only temporary. “I’ll never leave you again,” she cried into my ear. It’s true she wouldn’t. I knew for as much as I loved her and wanted to forever be with her, my home was earth and I needed to save my friends and family who still existed around me. To be honest I always felt she left earth because I left her chasing the wrestling dream pushing her away in the process. I guess it’s natural for every child to eventually push their mother away it was just sad she took that the wrong way. I had so much I wanted to tell her as she knows nothing of my last ten years. The world titles, the movies, the fame, the power, the family, the failures. So many questions I also had about our heritage and what to do in certain life situations I’ve never faced before. So much to say and to ask her I just froze stunned in confusion of the whole damn situation. “Are you alright,” she asked. I cried on her shoulder and had nothing to say. I had no answer to the question as I really didn’t know and I really didn’t care. I was back in my mother’s arms. After a while it got uncomfortable and I pushed her away to break the hug. “You’re leaving me this time aren’t you?” She asked me as I looked into her blue eyes. “The world needs me and I must.” I cried. “No, you’re my son and you are not leaving me.” “The world needs me.” “I need you.” “Stop making this so difficult.” She yelled ‘No’ in a blood curling scream as she started to shake and collapse. I caught her and laid her down as she curled up like a baby crying uncontrollably. It started to snow as I begged her to calm down but she refused. She begged me to stay. “Please don’t leave me I need you baby.” She cried. “Please don’t do this to me. This is very hard as it is and you’re not making it any easier.” I argued. “NOOOOOOO please stay why do you hate me.” She reasoned. “I don’t hate you mom I love you but this isn’t about you.” She was mentally ill and most of my conversations with my mother went down this road. We both would yell and cry and fight until the mood would change and everything would return to normal. I was too young to understand it and to me it was normal as its all I knew. “Oh so it’s about you then? You were always greed Hoyt. Always. Fine go and just leave me here to be alone again. Just like your father.” She snapped. “You know what go fuck yourself I’m glad I haven’t seen you in years and I’m happy I’m leaving again, never to see you again. You’re suicide was the only unselfish thing you ever did and you should have aborted me but you were too fucking selfish you crazy whore.” I slammed the door to the car and drove away. I always went too far because I didn’t understand it as nothing of the real world was ever real to me. I would hurt her with my words. She would do everything in her power to get me to that point, but regardless I did. So when I pushed away I pushed away hard and left my mom to her own solitaire confinement for both our safety. I wanted to come back and learn from her and understand her but she didn’t know that day was coming and I was her only thing. She lost her beauty, she lost her money, she lost her sanity, and she lost me. So that was it. I pulled over next to a set of train tracks and knew what I had to do. I needed to feel the pain of death and experience the steal ripping by torso from my limbs just as she did. By doing so I would die in purgatory and perhaps return to Earth. I wasn’t’ sure but I had to give it a go. Part of me really wanted to just give up and stay. Sit in purgatory with dare mother and fight loudly and madly until the neighbors couldn’t take it anymore and we were thrown out. It was the easy way and for as lazy of a man as I am I’m not ready to give up yet. I stood and watched as the white of the train light moved closer and closer. An angel wrote on an etch-a-sketch, “Jesus is in Jersey” just as the loudness of the train’s horn hurt my ears right up to the very end as my body exploded from the force of the speed of the midnight train right out of purgatory. A felt a great warmth that was not very comfortable at all. “Turn the heat down.” I sighed. Fuck it was all a dream. I was back in the Cadillac, back on earth, back in the hell that is my life. “Turn the God damn heat down you fat fucking bastard,” I yelled. I opened my eyes to find that the fat bastard was gone and I was driving the car. The steering wheel was bloody causing me to slam the breaks and nearly get run over by the truck behind me. I saw the fat man’s Ray Bans and put them on as I tried to clear my pounding head. What was happening? Where was I? I must find Jesus in Jersey and get a grip on all that was happening or I would never win the GTT or save the world. Both seemed like the most logical thing to do at the moment. Maybe I was crazy and my fantasy world is bleeding into my real world. Perhaps. But how would you explain the fat man who got me out of the nut house? If I was truly crazy I would still be there. So what’s happening has to be real which means my next stop was New Jersey.
