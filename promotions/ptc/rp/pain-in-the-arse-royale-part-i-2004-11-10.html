Pain In The Arse Royale - Part I

10 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-10 It seemed to Jimmy Bong that, with every new discovery he made, his world made less and less sense. (That was saying something of course, because the world of Jimmy Bong never made much sense to begin with.) Thanks to the events of the past month, Bong found himself caught in a web of mystery, intrigue, and delightful hilarity. However, there was nothing delightful nor hilarious about the shocking discovery he'd made just hours before he tangled with Steven Caldera in his fourth-round GTT4 match. It was an earth-shattering revelation that sent shockwaves throughout the British intelligence community and, on a personal level, broke his heart. Deep within the bowels of the United States Central Intelligence Agency, in the office of Agent Walter Durnstrom, Bong found clear and conclusive evidence that OHMCS had been betrayed by U, their head of Weapons Research & Development. Bong was stunned by this information. He had known U since the very first day he had been called to duty as one of the elite Double-Oh-Things of OHMCS. He had always known U to be fiercely loyal to the British government. In fact, Bong would go as far as to say that U had always been like a father to him -- a very spiteful father who would take great delight in seeing him thrown into the turbines of a hydroelectric dam and torn to shreds, but like a father nonetheless. A flurry of events took place shortly after Bong reported back to N on what he had found: In London, U was unceremoniously taken into custody in the middle of night, as a team of British government agents stormed into his house while the old man was out of bed for a trip to the bathroom. The front page of several daily newspapers bore the headline "BRITISH INTELLIGENCE SCANDAL" the next morning, with a picture of U being led away in handcuffs. Concordantly, a London tabloid displayed a photograph of U sitting on the toilet, with a look of shock on his face as the door to his bathroom was kicked in by the men who had come to arrest him. The headline read, "EXTRATERRESTRIALS ABDUCT DEFECATING ELDERLY MAN ON PRIME MINISTER'S ORDERS!" Meanwhile, back in Washington, Bong continued his inexplicable -- and indeed unprecedented -- run of extremely good luck, as he somehow managed to topple Steven Caldera to advance to his bracket final against the upstart Craig Goat, who scored an even more impressive victory over the legendary Seymour Almasy. Thanks to his win over Caldera, Bong was able to retain his cover for at least a few more days, as he continued his search for his archenemy -- Johannes Frederick Sebastian Billy-Bob Garfeld, the World's Most Fiendish Fiend -- who was planning to take over the planet with the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle. (Editor's Note: If you really, really want further details about what the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle is, it is highly recommended that you go back and read "The Big Shiny Sight Receptacle - Part II" for further information, as it is too complicated to explain for a second time. Otherwise, just know that it's an old Soviet doomsday weapon left over from the Cold War.) All the while, Bong's progression through the GTT4 tournament was earning him quite a reputation in the professional wrestling community at large... and he still didn't even really know how to wrestle. In fact, he was gaining widespread fame and popularity completely by accident. "People keep asking me to sign things for them," he once mused to his human shield, Majorly Shafted, "but I never get any packages! Why would people want me to sign things for them if I don't get any packages? America is a very strange country." Then, just a day or two later, Bong was contacted by his superior, N, with the latest details of what was happening back in London... --- WASHINGTON, D.C. - BONG'S HOTEL ROOM A DAY OR TWO AFTER YET ANOTHER STUNNING VICTORY OVER THAT CALDERA FELLOW DAYTIME, GIVE OR TAKE TWELVE HOURS A slight breeze wafted through the opened sliding door to the balcony of Bong's hotel room, gently stirring the white curtains that were draped shut in front of it. The two queen-sized beds were made, albeit with the covers being slightly rumpled -- a sure sign that the room's occupants had done the job, and not a member of the hotel's crack staff of maids. There was also a crudely constructed cot, which didn't look comfortable at all, sitting on the floor between the two beds. With Russian intelligence agent Iva Gunnonmi now under the protection of OHMCS, the hapless Bong had been forced to be a gentleman and give up his bed, so Iva would have a nice, warm place to sleep. The television, as well as just about every light in the room, was turned off. In fact, the only thing that apparently was turned on was the satellite communication console that Majorly Shafted had hooked up to the television in order to communicate with N's office back in London. A small red light on the control panel was flashing on and off, emitting a small buzzing sound with each successive blink. Other than the wind, and the sound of city traffic, it was the only noise to be heard in the empty room. A moment later, the front door swung open. Into the room burst the trio of Jimmy Bong, Majorly Shafted, and Iva Gunnonmi, all of who had apparently been working out. Shafted was clad in a white t-shirt that read "Oxford University" on the front, and a pair of grey sweatpants. The stunning Iva was, naturally, dressed in only a black sports bra and very short... well, shorts. As for the dim-witted Bong, he was -- you guessed it -- dressed in his trademark tuxedo. All of them were exhausted and dripping with sweat from what surely must have been a long session. Bong looked rather distraught, as he plopped down onto the foot of the bed nearest the door, and just stared blankly at the floor. Iva gave Bong a mean-spirited glare, and just shook her head as she walked into the bathroom and closed the door behind her. Shafted frowned at the sight of Bong wallowing in his own self-pity. He crouched on the floor in front of him and spoke sternly. "For heaven's sake, Double-Oh-Nothing!" he started. "What in the world has gotten into you? You didn't concentrate at all during today's training session! In fact, you've gotten exponentially worse over the past twenty-four hours! I thought you'd finally mastered being able to get into the ring without getting yourself tangled upside-down in the ropes, but today you nearly hanged yourself to death on them! We wasted three hours on that alone! And then when you finally were in the ring, and I instructed you to throw a punch at me, you somehow managed to hit yourself in the face! And it wasn't even a punch! It was a bloody kick! Honestly Bong, how you managed to defeat someone of Caldera's caliber is beyond my comprehension..." Bong sighed. "Well, that's no surprise," he said. "How I've managed to beat anyone in this tournament is beyond everyone's comprehension. It's a mystery that, sadly, will go unsolved until the end of time. But to answer your question about what's gotten into me... I'm just thinking too much about U, I suppose." "Why in the bloody hell would you be thinking about me?" asked Shafted, arching an eyebrow. "Not you!" said Bong. "U!" He paused for a moment. "My goodness, that joke really is annoying. Is that what I've been putting people through? No wonder people don't seem to like me very much. Why, I probably deserve to be shot!" he chuckled. Shafted, after briefly considering taking action upon that last comment, clapped a hand on Bong's shoulder. "We can discuss the shortcomings of your social tact some other time, Bong," he said, calming down. "Now look, you have to focus here. I know it must be devastating to you that U turned out to be the one who allowed Durnstrom to shut down OHMCS operations in this city, but you have to keep your eye on the ball! Garfeld is still out there, and still trying to somehow get his hands on the operating codes for the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle. It's our job to track him down, right? And our best chance of doing that is for you to stay in this bloody wrestling tournament as long as possible, or else the Americans are going to expel us from Washington -- and if that happens, it's going to be damn near impossible for us to get to Garfeld." Bong looked up at Shafted and smiled. "You're right, Shafted!" he said cheerfully. "And good show on recapping the plot for those who are just tuning in!" "BONG!" shouted Shafted with an angry frown. "Right, right, fourth wall, terribly sorry," Bong said quickly, getting up from the bed. "Anyway, it's not that simple, Shafted. I can't just forget about this whole situation. Something doesn't seem right. Why in the world would U betray his country, and put the world in jeopardy? What could he possibly have to gain from helping Garfeld?" Shafted paused to think for a moment. "Good questions," he said quietly. "But I'm sure N realized that she couldn't take any chances. After all, U had the only existing copy of the operating codes in his possession on the OHMCS database. There's no telling whether or not he's already sent them on to Garfeld. Surely you must understand that." Bong folded his arms and looked down at the floor. His eyes trailed to the communication console, and noticed the blinking light. "I say Shafted," said Bong. "Our satellite com seems to be winking at me! I think it's trying to come on to me or something..." Shafted covered his face with his right hand. "It's not winking at you, Bong!" he said in a tone equal to that of a parent patiently explaining something to a small child. "That's the message light! N must have tried to contact us while we were out." "Oh," Bong muttered in disappointment. "Well, I got my hopes up for nothing, then." Shafted gave Bong another strange look, as he clicked on the television and took to operating the control panel. After a few moments of static, N's face appeared on the screen. "Good day, gentlemen," she said. "Hello, N!" Bong said with a smile. "How are you do--" "I have an update for you regarding the current situation," she continued. "HEY!" exclaimed Bong indignantly. "That's very rude, interrupting me when I'm trying to ask you how you're doing!" "It's a recording, Bong," said Shafted. "I fail to see how that makes a difference, it's still rude all the same!" Bong replied. "As you know by now, we have taken U into custody, and unfortunately this means our Weapons Research & Development branch has been temporarily shut down," said N. "I read about it in the paper," said Bong. "Although it seemed odd that the Prime Minister would send extraterrestrials to fetch him..." "We've been interrogating U over the past several hours, naturally," N said gravely. "He has yet to make any sort of confession, despite the evidence we have against him. We've made a thorough check of our databases, and it doesn't appear that the operating codes for the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle were transmitted to anybody outside of our network. So, the only good news out of this whole mess is that we know Garfeld still doesn't have them." "Well, that's a relief to hear, N," said Bong. "So what should--" "However, there's still the matter of finding Garfeld." "Hey, you interrupted me again, you old cow!" Bong shouted. "It's a bloody recorded message, Bong!" Shafted snapped. "U keeps insisting that he knows as much about Garfeld's location as we do," N went on, "which of course is close to nothing. I have a feeling that--" Suddenly, the message cut out. Bong looked oddly at Shafted. "Bloody hell, we've lost her transmission!" he said. "Now we'll never know what her feeling was about!" "We didn't 'lose her transmission,' Bong," said Shafted with growing frustration. "For the millionth time, it was a recorded... message. And there's only about two minutes of recording time allotted for these transmissions for some reason. At any rate, you're a complete idiot." "Why thank you!" said Bong with a genuine smile. "Shut up, Bong!" snarled Shafted. "Don't you realize that N wasn't interrupting you at all!?" "Well, she did start talking while it was my turn to talk," Bong replied. "But you don't find it odd that she didn't get angry at you when you called her an 'old cow,' do you?" Shafted asked. Bong thought it over for a moment. "You're right!" he said. "How could I have missed it!? My goodness, I can be such a fool sometimes!" "There's no denying that," said Shafted, rolling his eyes. "But for the sake of my own sanity, I'm glad you've at least figured it out, finally." "Indeed!" Bong said with realization. "N has gone deaf!" "Yes, N has -- what!? NO!! ARGH!!" Shafted looked as if he was about to cry. "This is fantastic!" Bong went on gleefully. "She can't hear a word I say! Now I can finally get some things off of my chest that I've been meaning to, with no fear of repercussions whatsoever!" Shafted looked up at Bong, then grinned a devilish grin. "Is that a fact?" he asked. "Again I say, no repercussions!" Bong declared. "None whatsoever, I tell you!" "Well then, let me see if I can er... restore the transmission," Shafted said slyly. He punched a few keys on the console, and in a few moments, N's face was once again on the television screen, this time on a live feed. "Bong! Shafted!" she said. "It's about bloody time, I've been waiting for you to return my message!" "We got it," said Shafted. "Unfortunately it cut out after only a couple of minutes." "Blast, that's right," N mumbled. "Those things only give you two minutes of recording time. Anyway, how much of the message got thr--" "Ho-HO!" exclaimed Bong with a triumphant look on his face. "Now it's my turn to interrupt you, N! It's a shame that you're deaf and completely unable to hear this, but I've wanted to tell you this for a long, long time!" He cleared his throat, and continued his tirade, whilst N looked at him in shock, and Shafted was struggling not to burst out laughing. "N, you're nothing more than a wrinkly old sack of horse manure and crabapples! You smell like a urinal cake in a men's restroom! You have more hair growing out of your nose than I have growing on top of my head! My God, woman! You're ugly! You're an ugly old pile of stinking, rancid, rotting flesh! Your husband must be blind, because I find it inconceivable that any man with good eyesight could stand going through the reproductive process long enough for you to bear children! Furthermore, you have the disposition of a rabid Doberman! If Satan has a mother, then surely her name is N!" Bong smiled as he finished talking, and looked over at Shafted, who was still desperately trying not to burst out with laughter. He looked back at N, whose mouth was agape in stunned silence. Slowly, her expression changed from that of shock, to that of murderous rage. As this occurred, so too did the smile fade from Bong's face. He looked at Shafted again, this time quite worried. "She heard everything I just said, didn't she?" Bong asked with a gulp. Shafted just smiled and nodded. "Bloody hell!" said Bong. "She's not deaf!? That means the last transmission was just a recording! Why the blazes didn't you tell me that, Shafted!?" "I told you an endless amount of times!" Shafted replied. "Well you didn't do a very good job of it!" Bong snapped back. "DOUBLE-OH-NOTHING!" came the fearsome yell of the enraged N. "Blimey, there goes the neighborhood," Bong said to himself. "I'm going to give you five seconds to explain that atrocious speech of yours, Bong," N seethed. "Er, uh... well..." Bong stuttered. "Um... April Fool's!" N raised an eyebrow. "In November?" "Oh! Right, November Fool's, then," Bong corrected himself. "THERE NO SUCH -- forget it," said N. "Just forget it. I can't express how incredibly lucky you are, Bong. If I didn't need you to go to Atlantic City right away, I would sack you here and now." "No!" Bong wailed. "Please N, don't sack me! I'm sorry!" "I just said I wasn't sacking you!" N replied. "I'm sending you to New Jersey!" "Oh," said Bong. "Well, that doesn't really sound much better." "Just shut up and listen," said N. "Anyway, as I was saying when I tried to contact you earlier, it doesn't appear that U is going to tell us anything about how we can find Garfeld. I have a feeling that there may be only one other person who can help us out on that front -- Walter Durnstrom." "Durnstrom!" Bong repeated with a snarl, shaking his fist skyward in anger. "We've learned that he's on leave for the next two weeks in Atlantic City," N continued. "I want you and Shafted to tail him there -- oh, and you'll be going as well, Agent Gunnonmi," N said, as Iva came out of the bathroom, freshly showered and wearing a white bathrobe. "Our governments are working together to bring down Garfeld, so we will be most grateful for your assistance, naturally." Iva nodded, and clicked her tongue in reply (as Bushman Swahili was the only language she was capable of speaking, which has been noted previously). "Find Durnstrom, Bong," said N. "Get as much information out of him as you can, but do be careful about it." "Hang on," said Bong. "If I just show up in Atlantic City, and start asking him questions about Garfeld, then won't that expose my cover to the CIA?" N was quiet for a moment. "Yes," she said, "but only if you let Durnstrom get away." "So we're to take him into custody, then?" Shafted asked. "That has to mean breaking about a dozen international laws!" "Under these circumstances, it's a risk I'm willing to take," said N. "Remember, Durnstrom is on a two-week vacation. The CIA won't notice he's missing until then." "Right," Bong nodded. "Well, no time to waste!" "One more thing, Bong," N interjected. "There will be a British operative in Atlantic City to meet you. He's keeping tabs on Durnstrom for the moment, and should have a full report for you when you arrive. Goes by the name of Tom Knight." "Tom Knight, you say?" Bong asked, a bit surprised. "Well, that's a nice change -- working with someone who actually has a normal name! Thomas Knight, it has a nice ring to it." "Actually Bong, it's short for 'Tomorrow,' not 'Thomas,'" N corrected. Bong raised an eyebrow. "Tomorrow Knight? Who the bloody hell comes up with all of these stupid names?" "I suggest you stop wondering about that, and start wondering about how you're going to capture Durnstrom without the CIA catching wind of it," N answered. "And the sooner you do it, the better. I highly doubt you'll be able to keep up your charade in the GTT4 tournament much longer, against the likes of Mr. Goat." "Hey! What's that supposed to mean!?" Bong asked indignantly. At that moment, the feed cut out, and the television went back to static.
