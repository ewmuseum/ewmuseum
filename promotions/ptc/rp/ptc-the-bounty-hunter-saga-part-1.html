The Bounty Hunter Saga (Part 1)

17 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tokyo: October 4, 2003 The woman was leaned back in her chair. She was Asian, her beautiful raven black hair, fell just below her shoulders. She was dressed in a black business suit, showcased by a short skirt, and black high heeled shoes, which she had rested on top of her desk. Her office showcased her money and elegance. As she leaned back the soft, red fabric cushioned her back. It relaxed the woman, she felt comfortable and at peace, it helped her to deal with the intensity of the man on the phone. She needed his help, but it appeared that the man on the phone wouldn’t cooperate without a large sum of money, deposited into his account. “Listen Myasato, I don’t care how far in debt you are. That is your problem not my own, but however, I do need your help. So give me a figure of how far in debt you are, and I will see what I can do, to ‘aid’ your financial situation,” said the woman. “I’m in deep, Ishuzu. I need a lot of help,” said the man on the other end of the phone. “How deep, Myasato?!” she furthered. “About one hundred and fifty large,” answered the man, the shame shining through in his tone of voice. “Alright, who’s the collector?” Ishuzu asked the man on the other end of the line. ”The guy’s name is Drewett; he’s a Brit, a ruthless one at that. He’s got visitors coming to see me next week, and I have nothing to give him,” answered Myasato. “Okay, where can I find this Drewett?” Ishuzu furthered Myasato for more information. She needed it if she was going to help him out. “He mostly hangs around at the places he owns. He has a couple of clubs in Tokyo, the Elixir, the Revolution and Her Majesty’s Palace. He’s also got a pool hall up-town; it’s called the Corner Pocket. I’d check there first, he likes to do a lot of ‘business’ out of there,” answered the man. “Alright Myasato, I’ll take care of your debt, and we’ll meet next week. I’ll give you your ticket, the fake identification papers, and I’ll give you the mission details. Ummmm, let’s say The Lazy Fox at about noon-ish?” said Ishuzu. “Okay Ishuzu, I’ll see you there. Noon! See you later,” Myasato chimed in closing. “Good-bye Myasato, see you soon,” the woman answered quietly. Tokyo: October 11, 2003 Ishuzu sat impatiently at the small table for two. She was seated at the far end of the restaurant, close to the bar. She sat with a glass of water in front of her, and a lot of drunken males sitting behind her. She looked around the restaurant impatiently, looking for any sign of Myasato, the man who was supposed to meet her more than twenty minutes ago. Finally she noticed the man rush into the restaurant. He was dressed in a white business suit and a pair of Oakley sunglasses. His face was red, and he was breathing heavily, possibly a sign of the events before arriving at the restaurant. He looked around nervously, looking for the woman that he was supposed to meet. He couldn’t believe he had been so late, damn taxis. This woman had wiped away his debt for a mere favor, and this was thanks he was giving her. He noticed her sitting in the back part of the restaurant, not too far away from the bar. As he walked over, he was surprised she was here, there was a lot of noise around, but then again, maybe that was why she had chosen this location. He hurried over and seated himself across from her, so he could look at her as she spoke. “You’re late!” she said as he sat down. “I know, I’m sorry. The traffic was brutal, and the taxi moved slowly as hell. Damned driver had to milk the traffic for every penny he could get,” answered Myasato. “Speaking of money, you should have received a call this week, from your friend Mr. Drewett?” asked Ishuzu. “Yes, I did, and thank you very much. Now as I said, what is it that you need me to do for you?” questioned Myasato. Ishuzu smiled, she knew that Myasato was a man of his word. She also knew that with his success she might surpass that bastard Nakazura, for top control of Tokyo. “You are familiar with my rival, Mr. Nakazura are you not?” “Yes I believe I am. Right now he controls slightly more of Tokyo, and wields slightly more power than you do. Does he not?” answered the man. As truthful as it was, it still bothered her to hear it. She showed her disapproval on her face, and Myasato felt slightly taken back, by her facial expressions. “That he does, but not for very much longer,” she smiled as the words came out of her mouth. “He also has a very large weakness. He loves his sons greatly, and with great reason. Together, they happen to be the most known, respected, and feared street-warriors in the entire city.” “Yes, but they are not in the city, at least that is what I have heard. To my understanding they were on some sort of mission in the USA,” answered the man. “Yes, on a mission to the United States, from a close personal friend of Nakazura’s. However, this is not known to many, and the brothers are still feared. Even those that know of their absence do nothing, simply because of the fear of the brother’s return,” said Ishuzu. “Yes, but their great reputation comes earned, does it not. I have heard many stories of their actions and accomplishments,” interrupted Myasato. “You are right; they are powerful and have many triumphs. However strong, they are human, and they can be defeated. They can be killed, and that my friend is where you come in,” answered Ishuzu. “You want me, to kill two brothers? TWO MEN! Not to mention two of the most proficient killers that roam Tokyo’s streets!” exclaimed the man. “Yes, you are a bounty hunter are you not?” asked Ishuzu. “Yes, but…” answered Myasato. “So, I paid for your services, and now you must deliver. Besides, you visit the United States often do you not?” the woman continued. “Yes I do, what’s your point?” inquired Myasato. “So, the brothers are out of place in the US. This is their first time in America; they are not in their normal environment. You however, are quite familiar with the area, and it gives you a distinct advantage over the two of them. Or if you’d prefer, I can always give Mr. Drewett a call back if the check happens to bounce somehow?” Ishuzu knew she had him, now she just had to convince him, and get him confident enough to go. Myasato sat and thought quietly for a minute or two. H reached across the table and grabbed the woman’s glass of water. He took a long sip, downing nearly half of the cup. He put the glass down on the table and smiled at his employer. “Alright, give me the info.” “I’m happy to see you have made the right decision Myasato. In this folder is everything you will need to know. Right now the Nakazuras call themselves The Souls of the Black Lotus, and they wrestle in a federation called Global Wrestling, it’s based out of Dallas, Texas. That is where you’ll be headed,” Ishuzu finished speaking, and slipped a tan file folder, filled with paper and trinkets across the table to Myasato. He glanced through the file and then put it down. “Looks good, I will look over this all in detail tonight. But right now, how about some dinner? I’m starving!” Myasato said anxiously. Ishuzu smiled and called the waiter over to the table. She ordered a bottle of fine wine, and then when it was returned they gave the waiter their food orders. They shared wine over some small talk as they awaited the arrival of their meal. Dallas: October 12, 2003 The two brothers stood inside the small ring. Two small lights illuminated the inside of the ring, and left the rest of the arena blanketed in darkness. They are focused only on each other and to nothing else in the world. They had to train hard, if they wanted to win the PTC Tag Team Titles. They had only been sparring for about twenty minutes, and they had agreed not to use their special, ancient technique, until after about an hour and a half of intense training. Ichiro, the older and taller of the two men, was trailing his brother, with his head ducked, awaiting the right moment to strike. He was dressed in a pair of baggy white sweatpants; they hung slightly low revealing the trim of his boxers to peak out. He wore nothing to cover his upper-body except for a white bandana to hold his hair out of his face. His arms were covered with the common full-arm tattoos of Yakuza members. He wore white tape around his wrists and a pair of white grappling gloves over his hands. He was barefooted but had a pair of kick pads on over his shins to protect his shins, and his younger brother. Jushin, the younger and quicker of the two men stood moving quickly trying to avoid his older brother. He was concentrated more on defense then offense and it had been working for him so far. He was dressed in a black pair of Nike shorts, a black muscle shirt, and a black baseball cap, that he had turned backwards. He also wore a pair of grappling gloves, his black gloves contrasted more against the white tape, wound tightly around his wrists. He was also wearing a pair of kick pads over his shins; they were a black that matched his muscle shirt. Jushin smiled as he danced around the ring, he had always been the quicker of the two brothers. He was slightly more agile and nimble than his larger and older brother. He had been hitting and running against his brother for the entire twenty minutes, and it was beginning to prove successful. He smiled and adjusted the black grappling gloves on his hands. Ichiro stalked in, trying to get close enough to land a kick or punch on Jushin. Anything would do, he hated to admit it, but his younger brother was kicking his ass. Just as Ichiro was brought back to reality, he was met by a jumping roundhouse kick that connected right to his jaw. The kick sent him sprawling and just as Jushin’s feet touched back down onto the mat, so too did Ichiro’s back. Jushin reached a hand down to help his brother up. Ichiro looked up at his little brother stunned, but accepted his hands and pulled himself up. He smiled as he returned to his feet; he patted his brother on the back. Then simultaneously the brothers walked to the edge of the ring, and reached down. They each grabbed a water bottle and took a long drink. Then Jushin first, followed by Ichiro they put the water bottles back down on the mat. They walked away from each other and took their places at opposite ends of the ring. They turned to face each other and they resumed their fighting stances, preparing to engage in their training once again. The brothers square off. They nod their heads at each other as a sign that they are both ready to use the ancient technique that was taught to them. Each man stood staring at each other, before they both quickly drew their eyes closed. Ichiro and Jushin simultaneously began to clench their fists together, as their muscles contracted and relaxed, creating a pulsating throughout their entire body. They opened their minds and slowly they felt the coming of the Lotus. Their body began to surge with adrenaline, as it seemed an electric current was beginning to run through their veins. Almost at the exact same moment both of the brothers’ eyes shot open and they glared into each others eyes as the white faded and a total blackness consumed them. They watched as the Lotus slowly began to consume their entire bodies. They could feel its presence, their muscles bulged as their strength grew, their legs pulsated as their speed increased, and their senses heightened as their awareness was stimulated. The two brothers looked at each other, their eyes were now completely black, and not a sign of life could be seen in them. But, they were alive, very much alive. Just an overwhelming power had taken control over them, and with this technique all elements of their fighting had been improved. This ancient technique had helped them train for only a few weeks now, but Ichiro, the older of the two brothers, had been using the technique for years, and it had helped him to gain countless triumphs over his enemies. The brothers ran at each other, each with fury and pride marked on their faces. Neither man was willing to give even the slightest advantage to the other, as they sparred with each other. The rest of the world faded from existence as they were engaged in combat, the building could have collapsed around them, and they would not have noticed. Finally, Ichiro began to gain a slight advantage. He began to force Jushin back towards the ropes. The flurry came quickly and accurately, Ichiro threw everything he had at his brother. Punches, kicks, slaps, back-hands; however none of them seemed to land their mark as Jushin threw his hands around, and managed to knock all of the punches away. Jushin continued backing away and avoiding the blows with quick footwork, and body movement. Jushin ducked down to the ground; out of the way of a huge round-house kick from Ichiro. He swept his leg around, and in the process sent Ichiro’s legs out from underneath him, and Ichiro fell to the mat. Jushin popped back up to his feet and strode away from his older brother. He waited for his brother to regain his footing before charging towards him. Jushin rushed at his brother, and led at him, lunging with a left hook. He just caught his brother’s jaw, but it was enough to knock his brother off balance. Jushin quickly took advantage and began to pummel his brother. He lands several shots to his brother’s face, rights, left, hooks, jabs. Finally, with his brother reeling Jushin landed a stiff uppercut on his older brother, and sent him sprawling to the ground. Ichiro hit the mat, with a loud thud. His body ached slightly, but his face and jaw ached severely. His brother had indeed begun to master the ancient technique bestowed in their power. Jushin stepped back and smiled, as Ichiro began to slowly massage his aching jaw. Jushin stepped towards him, and extended his hand down to his older brother. Ichiro looked up, and then finally took his brother’s hand. He held on tightly, as his brother pulled him to his feet. Calmly, the two brothers walked away from each other, and to opposite sides of the ring. They looked at each other, and once again prepared to engage in combat. They began to train once again. As each man would throw a flurry of punches and kicks while the other tried to block them. They continued to train into the late hours of the night. They had no idea how much trouble they were really in. They had no idea of the danger that would be landing in Dallas in mere hours. Still they continued their training for the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. Dallas International Airport: October 13, 2003 The man stepped out of the airport terminal, slightly tired from his long plane ride, but, excited about his whereabouts. He was now back in the USA, a place he loved to be. However, this visit was strictly for business, and there would be no time for play. He was dressed in professionally in a black business suit. His shoes glimmered in the morning sunlight shining through the large window overlooking the airport. He had his carry-on bag with him, slung over his shoulder, and the same file folder that Ishuzu had given to him, in his right hand. His body was tired, but his mind felt ready and able to go. He had read the folder through; start to finish at least seven times throughout the course of the fifteen hour flight. His mind was racing now that he was finally in Texas, he longed to find the two Nakazura brothers and get this mission over with. As much as he loved being in the United States, he did not enjoy being there for this reason. He began to walk down the long airport hallways, he needed to collect his luggage and get to his hotel accommodations. He needed to put the final touches of his plan onto paper. Suddenly as he began to walk away, his gaze was caught by the shimmer of light coming through the large transparent walls to the outside. The airport was loud and busy, a situation which he did not like a whole lot. The people around talking, people rushing hurriedly to their flights, the roar of engines as planes began to take off of the runways. He glanced out the windows and into the morning light. The sun was just hitting its peak, finishing up its rise. The man looked out into the orange daylight. The sun shone orange, yellow and red rays across the earth, bringing what seemed to be an endless light to all parts of the world. He stared out, as the sunlight seemed to mesmerize him. He stood motionless for several moments before muttering to himself. ”The sun rises beautifully this morn’, but soon, soon enough it shall rise crimson and dark. For in one of these nights closely approaching blood will be shed, the Souls of the Black Lotus shall soon be robbed of their life, and their souls!” With that the man turned from the window and began to walk down the hallway. He passed under a sign that read Gate B-13. Ironically, it was the current day, and supposedly the number of bad luck. And with this man walking on American soil; it could yet spell bad luck for the Souls of the Black Lotus. Dallas Days Inn Hotel: October 13, 2003 The man looked up and down the hallways, as he got off the elevator. He was now in search of room 321. He scouted the hallways looking for any sign of information. Finally he saw a small grey sign, with black lettering reading “316-330, this way.” He followed the arrow that would point him towards his room. He looked tired, which was not surprising after his long travels as well as the late time of night. His once fresh and clean white, silk shirt was now beginning to wrinkle up. His black, business suit pants had become ruffled and messy. He was now beginning to tire and wanted nothing more than a little time to plan his week out in advance, a warm shower, and some sleep. He had his suitcase in his left hand, a small duffel bag slung over his right shoulder, as well as a tan, file folder in his right hand. He placed the luggage down, and stuck his hands inside his pockets. He jingled and felt around, looking to find the sliding keycard which would allow him access to his room. He finally felt the card in his hand, and pulled it out. He quickly swiped the card through, and as the machine beeped with acceptance of the card, he turned the handle, and swung the door open. He walked into the room, flicked the small switch on the wall on the inside of the room, and immediately the room was illuminated. It was small, but sufficient. There was one bed, a night table, a television set, and a bathroom with a shower and a toilet. He walked into the room and threw his luggage down. He put his suitcase, standing upright between the night-table and the bed. He threw his small duffel bag, into the closet, where he had just hung his business suit jacket. He placed the file folder onto the bed and walked into the bathroom. He quickly began to run the water from the taps. He turned the handles, and immediately a stream of warm water began to pour from the spout. He cupped his hands together and placed them underneath of the tap. He cupped a handful of water into his hands, and splashed it on his face, he felt his senses rise slightly, and then cooled the water slightly before doing it again. He then turned both of the handles, and turned off the taps. On his way back into the bedroom, he grabbed a white hotel towel, and wiped his face off. Myasato walked into the room, and laid down on the bed. He took his shirt off and threw it onto the night table. He did not have very many plans, he wanted to change that. He wanted to have a good idea of how he was going to go about, taking care of the Souls of the Black Lotus. Then he wanted to have a shower, and then he wanted sleep. He sat up, fluffing a pillow and placing it against the wall as a backrest. He grabbed the folder, and opened it up spreading the papers across the bed. He looked over the documents that Ishuzu had placed inside the folder. There was very little information, but what information there was, was thorough and useful. The folder included, ads featuring the Nakazura brothers, local sightings, photos of the brothers, information about Global Wrestling and the GW arena, hot spots where the brothers might be found, and several other small tidbits of information. All in all, he didn’t have anything solid to go on. He ran through his mind the things he would need. The provisions, he could live off of little food, and what food he did need, he could get at the hotel or the many local fast-food locations. What he would need, was guns. He quickly pulled out the complimentary hotel pads of paper, and a pen, and began to jot down the things he would need. The list was relatively short, but important. He decided to make that the top priority for the next day. He put the list down when he felt satisfied with it; he could always double check it in the morning. He decided that his mind was now too weary to try and attempt any further planning and Myasato began to put the documents back into the file folder. Myasato then threw the folder onto the small night table set up beside him. He stood and walked towards the bathroom once again; he flicked the light switch on, and began to undress. He hopped into the shower, and turned on the water. The water poured out of the shower head, and the warm water began to patter against his body. Myasato felt soothed as the water seemed to massage his skin. He used the crappy complimentary hotel soap and washed his body. He also used the complimentary shampoo to wash his hair. Although his hair now felt slightly cleaner, it was not perfect, but it would do. After about fifteen minutes in the shower, Myasato turned the water off and stepped out of the shower. He grabbed a white towel from the pile on a shelf in the room. He dried the water from his chest, then from his arms, then down the rest of his body. He felt better and relaxed; he was now ready to sleep. He didn’t bother getting dressed again; he merely threw on the pair of grey boxer-briefs that were lying in the pile of dirty clothes. He left the rest of the pile and walked back into the bedroom. On his way through the door, Myasato flicked off the bathroom light, and then on his way into the bedroom he flicked off the light switch. The entire room became engulfed in pitch blackness as the Japanese man attempts to make his way to the bed. He finally feels the blankets brushing against his leg in the darkness, and slides into the bed. He pulls the blankets around himself, to keep him warm in the air-conditioned room. As he sat in the darkness, he thought about the mission that he was currently on. Was there any way he could complete it successfully? He thought not, due to the stories he had heard, but that’s all they were, stories only. He had no real way of knowing, but he would soon find out. With that the fatigue in his body consumed him, and he fell into a very deep sleep. To Be Continued
