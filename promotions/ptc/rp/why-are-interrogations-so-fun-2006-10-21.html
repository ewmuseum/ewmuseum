Why are interrogations so fun?

21 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-21 October 1, 2006 6:02 AM Location: Rocky Mount Police Department "So, am I under arrest?" Semore walked into the interrogation room of the police department followed closely by the officer that had greeted him at the front door of Mr. Wachter’s gym earlier that morning. "No Mr. Smoke, we just simply wanted to ask you a few questions about Ms. Jones and your whereabouts at the time of her death." "If I’m not under arrest, then why were you asking me if I wanted a lawyer on the way up here?" Semore asked as he sat down at the end of the table, furthest from the door. "I wouldn’t need a lawyer if you just wanted to ask me a couple of questions. This isn’t my first picnic you know!" The officer sat down across from Semore and set a tape recorder between the two. "Yes, we know all about your past record with the police," the officer said as he switched the tape recorder on. "Agent Ryan will be here soon, but until he arrives, would you please state your name for the record." "Semore Smoke, S-M-O-K-E," Semore said as he leaned back in his chair. "You even remember the protocol of spelling it, good for you. Now, Mr. Smoke… why exactly were you in Detroit a week ago?" The officer asked as he eyed over Semore for any possible quirks that he might have that would point towards a lie. Semore let out a sigh as he leaned forward in his chair and rested his elbows on the table. "I was in town for a meeting at the Primetime Central headquarters," Semore said, looking the officer square in the eyes. "I received a letter from them about two weeks ago asking if I might be interested in participating in one of their upcoming tournaments. The letter detailed that I would have to meet with one of their executives before receiving an application for the event." The officer nodded his head as he listened intently to Semore’s answer. "Do you still have a copy of this letter?" The officer asked as he started to thumb through a manila folder. "Yes, it’s back at my pad on the second floor of the gym you guys picked me up at." The officer nodded again as he listened closely to Semore and closed the folder. "Is that where you first met Ms. Jones? At her place of work at the PTC HQ?" "Yeah, I met her as I walked into the building. She was working there as one of the receptionists. We had a moment of some harmless flirting while she gave me directions to which office I was supposed to have my meeting." As Semore finished answering the question a man dressed in a standard black suit walked into the room. Since the man wasn’t dressed in normal police officer attire it was safe to assume that the man was none other than Agent Ryan as soon as he walked in. Soon enough that was verified as you could clearly make out Agent Ryan’s FBI badge sitting squarely on the left side of his chest. "Sorry for the lateness, have I missed anything?" "Nope, nothing at all Agent Ryan. Mr. Smoke here was just detailing his visit to Detroit for a meeting he had at the PTC HQ," said the officer. "Would you like to sit down and continue the interrogation yourself?" "No, I’ll just stand here and observe. You’ve already started so it wouldn’t be good if I intruded. Carry on," Agent Ryan said as he leaned against the large one-way mirror towards the back of the room. The seated officer looked back towards Semore and continued his questions where he left off. "After your meeting, did you talk to Ms. Jones again as you left the building?" "No, well, not initially at least," Semore said as he started to twiddle his thumbs. "After I was done with my meeting I left the building so I could get an early jump on the twenty hour drive back home, which consequently never happened…" -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- September 23, 2006 2:15 PM Location: Flashback PTC HQ / Detroit, MI While Semore walked out of the office building he looked down at the application he had received from PTC executive John Davis. "It’s funny how these damn applications never change," Semore said to himself as he made his way towards the front parking lot and his car. "When I get this application in then all that’s left is praying." Semore walked to the driver side door of his teal 1999 Nissan Altima and immediately unlocked it and sat inside. Semore tossed the application over into the passenger seat and closed the driver side door. Semore soon followed that action by placing the key into the ignition. "Ok baby, you made it all the way here. Now it’s time to make it all the way back." Semore turned the key in the ignition, the starter started to click around and the radio turned on but the engine didn’t roar… "No… don’t do this to me…" Semore turned the key again, and once again, no engine roar. "The starter’s turning and the radio works… it’s not that battery… the starters turning normally so it’s not the starter… There’s something else wrong that I can’t fixed," Semore continued his habit of talking to himself as he tried to clear his head. "Isn’t this just great… I’m stranded… In Michigan of all places!" Semore took the key out of the ignition and grabbed the application from the passenger seat before climbing out of the car. He continued to mumble several words of profanity to himself as he made his way back towards the large office building. Semore made it to the front revolving door and stopped before re-entering the building. He looked at his reflection in the glass door and tried to calm himself down before entering. "Calm down Semore, you definitely don’t want the young lady to see you like this…" Semore successfully calmed himself down and entered the building through the revolving door… "Mr. Smoke? Is there something wrong?" Joanna the receptionist asked as Semore made his way back through the lobby towards her. "Yeah, my car won’t seem to start. It looks like I killed it after the long drive up here. I’m not sure how I’ll get back home now but my car is definitely not an option anymore." "I’ll call upstairs and see if I can get someone for you and see if they’ll be willing to help you out," Joanna said as she picked up the front desk phone and entered in a few numbers. After a few seconds she was connected to someone up stairs and started to plead Semore’s case for him. "Yes, that’s right. Mr. Smoke’s car won’t start." "Can I speak to whoever it is?" Semore asked as he beckoned with his hand for the phone. Joanna nodded and handed Semore the phone so he could speak to the individual on the other line. "Yeah, this is Semore Smoke. Look, my car won’t start after the long drive I had up here, is there anyway that you guys might be able to help me out?" "Sure we can help you Mr. Smoke. We’ll be happy to pay for a plane ticket so you can fly back home." The voice on the line was still very familiar as it was PTC executive John Davis on the other line. "Unfortunately we won’t be able to schedule a flight for you until tomorrow at the earliest. So how about we find a hotel room for you tonight and you can fly back home first thing in the morning." "Yeah, I guess I’m down with that. It’s the least you guys can do since I sort of did waste a drive up here just to grab an application and have a five minute meeting." "Yes, you’re right. It’s the least we can do to help out one of our loyal veterans. If you want, you can wait in the lobby for awhile and I’ll have the hotel information for you soon." Semore raised his eyes from the floor, which he had been staring at the entire time while on the phone and met the eyes of Ms. Jones who was staring at him. "Mr. Davis, just one second," Semore said as he put his hand over the receiving end of the phone to try and muffle it from what he was about to say. "Hi, I know you barely know me… but would you like to go out for that coffee we discussed earlier?" "Right now?" "Yes." Joanna looked down at her watch and then back up into Semore’s eyes. "Sure, I actually get off here in about fifteen minutes. Oh, my name’s Joanna Jones by the way. I never gave that to you when we had our chit chat before." "A beautiful name for a beautiful woman," Semore said as he put his charm into overdrive and took his hand off the receiving end of the phone. "Mr. Davis, yeah, sorry about that. I’ll be leaving the building with one of your receptionists, Ms. Jones. When you have the hotel information then you can contact me on my cell phone. My cell phone number’s [detail withheld], will that be fine?" "Sure thing Mr. Smoke. You’ll be the first to know as soon as we get a hotel squared away for you. Once again, thanks for coming out here and sorry about your vehicle. Speaking of which, what would you like us to do with your car?" Semore looked back down at the floor for a moment as he thought things over. After a few seconds of thought Semore lifted his head back up and answered Mr. Davis’ question. "Yeah, this entire trip was about starting all over again. You guys can do whatever you want with the car, I won’t need it when I get back and with the money I’m hoping to earn through this tournament you can bet that I’ll easily be able to get another. It’s parked out front and it’s a teal 1999 Nissan Altima with North Carolina plates." "Understood, we’ll just tow it and have it junked then. Hope you and Ms. Jones have a good afternoon and I’ve got your number written here when we get things squared away for you." "Thanks," Semore said as he handed the phone back to Joanna. Joanna set the phone back down on the base and looked back at her watch again. "My replacement should be here any minute now. So, does Starbucks sound good to you?" Semore nodded and let a gentle smile slide across his face as Joanna finished her sentence. "Starbucks sounds great right now." -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- October 1, 2006 6:30 AM Location: Rocky Mount Police Department "Pretty interesting story so far Mr. Smoke," the officer said as he stared down at the manila folder that was placed in front of him. "Your history of drug abuse is extensive I see. Did you by chance partake in any sort of similar activity while in Detroit?" It didn’t take long for Semore to erupt with anger from such an allegation. "No! I’ve been clean for more than two years now. Those drugs ruined my life and I’ll be damned if I’ll let them ruin it again." The officer nodded as he expected that response from Semore. "Settle down Mr. Smoke, settle down. It was simply a question so don’t get all riled up about it. What did the two of you do when you left PTC headquarters?" Semore let out a sigh as he let the stress of the early morning combined with the recent atrocious question slip away. "We climbed into her car and drove to a Starbucks that was probably just a few blocks away. We ordered a couple of tall coffees and just sat down to talk for awhile." The officer continued to nod his head as he listened to Semore’s story. "What did the two of you talk about?" "We talked about the wrestling business mostly," Semore said as he stopped twiddling his thumbs and clasped his hands together on the table. "She had been a fan of the business for years and even mentioned that she had just started graduate school when I doing my tenure in PCW." The officer stood up after sitting for the majority of the morning and began to pace back and forth along the length of the table. "How long did the two of you chit chat about the wrestling industry, Mr. Smoke?" "Wow… We must have sat there for hours just reminiscing on the old days of professional wrestling. It was about nine o’clock that night before we knew it had gotten late so we left the Starbucks," Semore began as he leaned comfortably back into his chair. "At about that time I had realized that there wasn’t any call from Mr. Davis so I checked my phone and I saw that it had been turned off the entire time. So, I turned it on to check my messages and found out that he had called a few hours before to give me the hotel information." "And what did the two of you do then?" Interjected the police officer as he stopped pacing momentarily to listen more intently. "I let her know that my hotel was on reserve so we said our goodnights and I caught a cab to the hotel." The officer walked back over to his chair and sat back down again. "What was the name of the hotel that Mr. Davis had placed you in?" Semore looked back at the officer square in the eyes. "It was The Westin Detroit, located at the airport so it would be easy for me to get to my flight, which was scheduled first thing the next morning." The officer looked up at Agent Ryan who had remained quiet most of the morning. The officer nodded towards Semore as to ask if the FBI agent might want to enter with a question or two now. "How short of a leash are you on right now Mr. Smoke," Agent Ryan finally spoke up as he walked towards the front of the room so he could stare Semore in the face. "Microscopic. At this point if I even get a ticket for jaywalking I’m behind bars for twenty-five years," Semore said as he looked up at the agent. "Why do you ask?" "Because we know you haven’t been exactly truthful with us this morning. And lying to us would definitely put you behind bars and end any chance that you might have had to make a return to wrestling," the agent said as he started to pace over the same ground that his police officer counterpart did during the interrogation. "We know for a fact that you went with Ms. Jones back to her apartment. Your fingerprints were found just mere feet away from her bed. If you went to the hotel immediately after leaving the coffee shop then how did your fingerprints end up at the scene of the crime?" Semore looked visibly shaken as the agent spoke his words. "All right, we did head back to her apartment but it was strictly consensual. She invited me back after the coffee because she said that she had had a long-time crush on me, dating back to the days she watched me in PCW." The agent stopped pacing and stepped forward, edging closer and closer to Semore and eventually leaned in so far that he was no more than a foot away from Semore, face to face. "Why did you choose to leave out this bit of information?" "She’s got a boyfriend, and a kid, I figured it was hard enough on them to have just lost her but they didn’t need to know that she had been cheating as well," Semore emotionally let out. "I was only protecting their interest, I didn’t have anything to do with her death I swear it! As soon as we were done I called a cab from her place and left for the hotel." Agent Ryan leaned back and took a few steps back from Semore. "Also leaving out that information takes away any possible chance to catch the real killer," Agent Ryan said as he motioned for Semore to get up. "That’s all we’ll need from you today. If you need a ride back to the gym then one of the officers here will gladly give you one." Semore stood up out of his seat and looked at his reflection in the mirror as he stood up. "Thanks, I’ll be sure to leave the information on how to contact me while I’m traveling for this tournament with the officer that gives me the ride just in case you guys need to ask me anymore questions." Semore stepped towards the door and eventually out through it. As soon as Semore was out of earshot of the conversation the officer looked towards Agent Ryan. "At least that gives you guys a motive to pursue the boyfriend." Agent Ryan turned his glance away from Semore as he eyed him walking down the hallway and stared back at the officer. "That may be, but I still have this very odd feeling about that guy. When you asked him whether or not he had done any drugs that night. He became quite offended and showed quite the attitude towards a very innocent question." The officer nodded as to agree with the FBI agent. "We’ll definitely keep an eye on him." -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- October 1, 2006 7:30 AM Location: Mr. Wachter’s Gym "Good to see you made it back safe and sound kid." Semore entered the large front door of the gym and made his way towards a row of chairs that sat against the sidewall to take a seat. "Yeah, well, I wasn’t under arrest or anything so it all turned out fine." Mr. Wachter made his way towards Semore and as he did Semore immediately stood up and turned to make his way to the stairs that led up to his pad on the second floor. "Well, are we going to finish the training today?" Mr. Wachter asked as Semore made his way up the stairs. "Not today my good friend. I’ve had a rough morning and the only thing I want to do this morning is to get my shit packed and get ready for my flight out," Semore said as he was almost at the top of the stairs. "When I get there I’ll work something out so you can make your way out for the match too. It’ll mean a lot to me if you stand in my corner while I kick things off. Speaking of which, did any word come in this morning about my first round opponent?" Mr. Wachter had made his way to the bottom of the stairs so the two could have a somewhat decent conversation without yelling across the large room. "Yeah, a guy named Sledge from UWF." Semore let out a snicker. "Talk about biting the bullet, that had to be one of the better match-ups that I could dream of for the first round. Thanks again, I’ll be back down a couple hours before my flight leaves so I can say my goodbyes and what not. Oh, one more thing, did you see the trash bag I had left outside my door?" "Yes I did, I already took it out to the back dumpster and the trash guys came by while you were gone," Mr. Wachter answered back. "Speaking of which, I found one of your old PCW shirts in that bag that had some red stains on it. Did you get in a brawl or something and forget to tell me?" Semore froze at the top of the stairs, almost like a deer would in front of a car’s headlights. "Yeah, it’s really nothing. The blood would have never come out so that’s why I threw the shirt out." Mr. Wachter just nodded his head as he turned back away to look at all the food that had been left in the middle of the ring that morning. "I guess I’ll have to call the guys early this morning and see if any of them want a free breakfast." Mr. Wachter turned to look back up the flight of stairs and by that time Semore had entered his room and shut the door. Semore walked around a corner and was followed closely by an unfamiliar man into the locker room. "Yeah, well, that’s what happens when you put me up against beer drinking idiots. I not only had way more talent but was definitely a lot hungrier." Semore walked over to where his stuff was placed on the bench and noticed Mr. Wachter sitting there. "What did you think of the match Mr. Wachter?" Mr. Wachter looked up into Semore’s eyes and showed a proud smile. "You did really good kid, you’re in the toughest bracket of them all and you did really good for your first round," Mr. Wachter said as he stood up to allow Semore to sit down next to his things. "That means a lot coming from you," Semore said as he started to take off his pads and tape to be placed into his gym bag. "It felt good to be under those hot lights and in front of a real crowd again. I just can’t wait until they get all the results done for the night and hand us our round two assignments." "Don’t take a rush on everything, you got a lucky draw for this first round. You’ll definitely have to take time to train during the week for your next round opponent," Mr. Wachter stated as he walked towards a poster listen the brackets and the current announced winners. "You’re in a bracket that is stacked from head to toe and you just happened to get one of the weakest links this round. I’ve got an odd feeling that next round you won’t be as lucky." A loud cheer was heard echoing through the hallway. "Sounds like the last match of the night has finished, I wonder who won," said the unidentified man in the room. "We’ll find out here in a minute Julius," Semore said as he stood up to get a better look at the locker room doorway. "Julius, remind me when we get back home to give you a few more bumps than usual." It appears that Julius is one of the wrestlers that Semore has been training with over the past couple of years while living in Mr. Wachter’s gym. "It’ll be my pleasure Semore," Julius responded just in time as one of the PTC officials came rushing into the locker room. "John Covel just defeated Dusk in one of the closest matches of the night and it’s been announced that Semore Smoke will face off against the FUSE LiveWire champion in the next round," the winded PTC official said as he leaned against the locker room door jam. "Well, there’s our answer on my next round opponent. Anything special I might need to know about this John Covel?" Semore looked around the room at the two men standing there with him. "Yeah, besides being the FUSE LiveWire champion he’s also a wrestler who doesn’t want to be a wrestler," Mr. Wachter said as he patted Semore on the shoulder. "He’d rather be on a baseball diamond right now playing catch so you’ll definitely have the hunger edge in this next round." Semore showed a face of satisfaction and nodded towards the old man. "Too bad no one ever told this guy that baseball’s for pussies," Semore said as he began to zip up his gym bag. "Maybe he should watch some George Carlin specials sometime." TO BE CONTINUED…
