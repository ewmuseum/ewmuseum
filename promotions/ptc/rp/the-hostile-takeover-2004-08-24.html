The Hostile Takeover

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 “Where the hell is he at?” “Patients, Mr. Yakuza.” “I can’t wait any longer.” “You are going to have to.” “I am much too powerful to be jerked around.” “Are you?” Standing in the middle of an office are two Japanese men. They slowly turn from the chairs in which they are seated in. Both are in well cut Japanese suits. Something only the upper class would wear. Something that the lower class could never afford. Their gaze is upon a man standing in the double doorway behind them. He is flanked by two men on his right and on his left and one directly behind him. Blocking the light and casting a shadow over him and onto the desk in front. “About time you arrive.” Says, Mr. Yakuza very angrily. “MR. YAKUZA!!!” snaps his cohort. Slowly the lead man enters the room. He is followed, by his entourage. Though you can not see it. His eyes are locked with Mr. Yakuza’s. The gaze never breaks as the man in a fine cut Italian suit sits opposite of the desk. His men exactly behind him as they entered the room. Two more men enter the room and close the door. They take their positions right behind Mr. Yakuza and his cohort. “Mr. Yakuza… Yoshii. I apologize for keeping you waiting. My flight from Las Vegas was a bit delayed. I was there on business and not pleasure, I assure you of that.” “Mr. Stecchino, how dare you keep me waiting. I am a very busy man.” “Mr. Yakuza. Silence.” Stecchino’s voice a bit tested and stern. I have had a very gracious weekend of business and now I am here to further our friendships across the seas and you seem to be very tipped off. Is something the matter?” “You had my sons killed.” “It had to be done. Simple as that.” “I LOVED THEM!!!” Mr. Yakuza rises up a little bit, Yoshii, grabs him and seats again firmly. Obviously the emotions running like a breeze in the room. “You raise your voice with me again and you will be reunited with your sons. Coppice?” Yakuza nods slightly, tears in his eyes as he looks into the eyes of the man who put down his sons a week ago. “Yoshii, you have a fine head on your shoulders and will go far in this business.” “Thank you Mr. Stecchino.” “You are welcome young one.” “I am older than you Mr. Stecchino.” says, Yoshii laughing. Stecchino removes his eyes from Mr. Yakuza to Yoshii. Yoshii drops his eyes from Stecchino’s. Anthony’s eyes could stop a train. The power and fear that protrudes is not matched by another in the world. “Why? Mr. Stecchino, why?” “I was left with no choice. They were going to break the deal that was originally signed in blood mind you. And you do not break a deal that you signed with the blood of my mother.” “You’re a sick man.” “Sick yes, respected yes. Feared by all and loved by none. Trusted by no one. Do you trust me?” “No.” “Then we no longer have a business deal and I am going to take control of your former assets. They now belong to me.” Stecchino reaches into his desk and pulls out a stack of papers. He sets them on his desk and pushes them right in front of Mr. Yakuza. “I will not sign these. You dishonor me.” Stecchino places his hands together in front of his mouth blocking most of his face. He leans back in his chair, his right eye fixed on a very angry Mr. Yakuza. “You are not making me very happy here Mr. Yakuza and I am trying to be civil.” said a muffled Anthony Stecchino. “You will have to kill me to take my empire. My men will not follow you.” Stecchino sits up agitated and calmly places each on his desk. He is right hand pops up for a moment and his fingers flick and then mold into that of one that would be holding a gun. Trigger motions of the finger occur in the direction of Mr. Yakuza. Stecchino smiles anxiously and claps a couple times. He reaches into his dark jacket and pulls out a gun. Mr. Yakuza’s eyes open with terror. He knows he is going to die. Yoshii remains calm. “Take this.” The guard behind Anthony grabs the gun and holds it behind his back. “Thank you Gerald. At least some one cooperates in this room.” “I knew you could not kill Mr. Stecchino.” “The night is young.” This worries Mr. Yakuza and he sits back in his chair and looks at his trusted partner Yoshii, but is met with cold eyes. Yoshii is now employed by Mr. Stecchino. Mr. Yakuza realizes that everything that he has worked hard for over the past thirty years is about to be all taken away from him by this young kid. Mr. Anthony Stecchino. “I grow impatient Mr. Yakuza. Sign the papers or I will have to take action.” “Then kill me.” “No… Somebody else I think.” Confused Mr. Yakuza looks at Stecchino as he hits a button on his phone and then there is a ringing and then a clicking of someone picking up. “Yes boss.” “He is being very uncooperative, so yeah.” “Got ya boss.” There is a gun shot and then a thump over the speaker phone. “She’s dead boss.” “Where was the bullet shot through?” “Back of the head down through the neck sir. Probably down into her chest. Never exited.” “Excellent!” “The name of the girl, Marcos?” “Tez Yakuza.” “MY YOUNGEST DAUGHTER! YOU SON OF A BITCH!” “Thank you Marcos.” Stecchino pushes the end button on the phone and then reaches across the desk and slaps Mr. Yakuza across the face. “You daughter’s death is on your head and the blood is upon your hands! This was your doing not mine.” Mr. Yakuza is in a full cry. “How could this be? You killed my daughter. I had nothing to do with it.” “I want your assets Mr. Yakuza. That is all.” “So be it.” Mr. Yakuza reaches across the desk and grabs the papers. He pats his jacket for a pen. His eyes still watering. Stecchino clicks on the desk three times. Yakuza looks up into the smiling face of Stecchino. “Take mine, Mr. Yakuza.” Mr. Yakuza takes the pen and Stecchino nods once in delight. Yakuza starts slipping through the pages initialing and signing in the correct spots. Stecchino looks on in complete satisfaction. Yakuza finishes and pushes the papers back to Stecchino. “Excellent. Yoshii if you would do the honors.” Mr. Yakuza looks over at Yoshii and Stecchino sticks the papers in a briefcase and begins to stand up. Yoshii has a 9mm aimed right at Mr. Yakuza’s head. A single silenced shot is fired. Mr. Yakuza slumps back in his chair. Stecchino not even looking stands up and walks toward the door. His men and Yoshii follow. Yoshii tucks the gun back in the jacket. The Yakuza era has ended. The Stecchino era has begun
