They have cures

20 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-20 Luckily for us we were born in to a society where we have choices and for this we should be eternally grateful. Whether it be choosing between cereal or toast in the morning for breakfast, or what colour shoes you want to buy. There are places in this world where choices are snatched from your bleeding hands where any question of this will sink you in to deep trouble or you get what you’re given and like it! We should feel so privileged that we live in a free world, and no matter what any government hating hippy says it is a free world we live it and they don’t realise how lucky they are to have the choice to even think like that without being killed. But with this in mind perhaps there are times where choices are a crushing burden. Sometimes there are choices that are so complex they will span in to a web of dysphoria and it would just be easier not to have the choice in the first place. Across the hall from Red Rock lives the Darling family, a family that has been burdened by choices. The most notable of all is the father Charles Darling a former police chief superintendent framed by the criminal he preyed. This resulted in his eviction from the force with a lengthy prison sentence after making the choice to endanger his family or his colleagues… But that’s a story told for another day because sat alone in the modestly furnished living room of their small flat is his young daughter Sarah Darling. She is perched on the edge of the worn down plush sofa with her knees brought in to her chest. She simply stares at the TV screen, not watching just staring with an absorbed expression about her pale face with a tiny stream of tears rolling down her plump chubby cheeks. Meanwhile across the hall where our Anti Hero Red Rock resides in his slightly "over modest" flat with his good friend D’avid… They have decided to make some breakfast after waking up at 1:30pm. Red Rock stands in front of the gas hobs glaring over at D’avid who is making himself as useful as a chocolate ashtray on a motorbike. look at D’avid, fucking lazy shitting bastard Red Rock: D’AVID! Are you going to help me cook breakfast or are we going to have the shits for weeks again? D’avid: Heh… OK although it is only bacon. Bacon or not, it is a well documented fact that Red Rock is a feeble cook, the extent of his skills ends at tea and toast, so bacon is very daunting for the young man. D’avid who is upside down on the sofa watching some strange Dutch soap opera on DVD flips himself back over before skipping over to Red Rock with a gleeful smile. He’s been like that for the last couple of weeks now… Red Rock: D’avid, what exactly are you so happy about, and what’s with all the skipping? D’avid spins on his toes like an intoxicated ballet dancer with no sense of balance before reaching Red Rock where he uses his shoulders for stability. D’avid: Would it surprise you if I told you that I got laid? LAID?! YOU? You’re a ginger fucking pervert how the bastarding hell did you get with in 10 feet of a girl?! Red Rock’s raised eyebrow, evasive body language and obvious attempts to refrain from laughing does not go unnoticed by D’avid. D’avid: Say what you think Red! Red Rock: Err… no I’m not surprised at all… it was only a matter of time before you finally got some… yeah right… D’avid stands proud with his hands on his hips, grinning like a super hero whilst Red Rock redirects his attention to the bacon. However upon doing so he hadn’t realized that the bacon has unexpectedly burst in to flames. Red Rock: ARGH! FUCK IT! Red Rock dithers around flapping his hands in a brief moment of hysteria before finally acting. Red Rock: WANK, WANK, WANK! Red Rock grabs the pan before furiously swinging it over to the sink to run cold water over the "well done" bacon and with a disappointed sigh and a peeved frown Red Rock turns to D’avid. Red Rock: SEE! I told you, everything I touch bursts in to flames! D’avid: It’s a good thing that none of the fire safety stuff in here actually works otherwise we’d be in big trouble! Red Rock: The fire safety stuff doesn’t work? Red Rock shrugs and is content with the fact that he isn’t stupid enough to start a fire anyway… well he thinks so. Red Rock: Meh. SO…D’avid who’s the idio… err… lucky lady then? D’avid: Well I think I scored big this time… but shh I got to keep quiet! Heh… D’avid checks over his shoulder before placing his arm round Red Rock’s shoulder to draw his ear closer to his lips. Red Rock: What are you doing, does she live next door or something? D’avid sucks his bottom lip in to his mouth in and his eyes widen to draw a sheepish expression to his face. Upon realizing who D’avid boned Red Rock swiftly recoils in disgust. Red Rock: AWWW! NO D’AVID! Not again! You bedded fat disgusted imported baby bingo winged Sarah from over the hall? …YOU SHAGGED THE PILE! What is WRONG with you? Red Rock cringes and shudders with a genuine feeling of befuddlement. Red Rock: I don’t even understand how you even found her vagina in the first place! Then his imagination begins to come in to play. He probably just slipped his penis between some of her fucking rolls…SICK! D’avid: I can’t help it Red! She’s fucking GORGEOUS! Red Rock: She’s not even human D’avid! She’s some kind of cross breed between a Big Mac and a human! D’avid: What are you talking about? Just because she has a few curves. Red Rock: A few hundred curves… Ok D’avid sorry…hold on…I said that the wrong way round Red Rock: Ahem…ANYWAY! D’avid crosses his arms in order to exclaim his disapproval at Red Rock’s opinions in his taste in women… Well D’avid’s taste in women is anyone he can trick in to saying yes. D’avid: Well I don’t have to stand here and listen to this! Red Rock: You’re sitting down… D’avid surveys his surround and noticed he has sat down on a stool by the breakfast bar. D’avid: Oh… WELL I still don’t have to listen to this! You’re just jealous because no one wants to sleep with you! Red Rock: HEY! I get plenty of sex! … Red Rock: OK it’s been a little while… … Red Rock: OK!!… 3 years but that’s because I have standards! D’avid’s face at this point is swollen and red due to the strain caused by trying to with hold his laughter; this only irks Red Rock further thus sparking a mild grade 3 tantrum. Red Rock: ARGH! FUCK YOU! Red Rock, in frustration reaches out for the nearest solid object, which just so happens to be a half eaten packet of mini batten bergs. Red Rock projects the two batten bergs complete with plastic packaging towards D’avid who quickly ducks with a panic stricken squeal. D’avid: My last flat mate didn’t treat me like this! Red Rock: That’s because you had him chained up in your closet and held him as a sex slave! D’avid: I miss Greg… **Meanwhile back over the other side of the hall…** So the pieces fall in to place, who else wouldn’t be mortified after spending a night of intimacy with someone like D’avid? But the arrival of Sarah’s best friend and Red Rock’s love interest Ren raises questions to the severity of Sarah’s mental state… Ren walks through across the living room floor right towards Sarah who is close to tears on the sofa; she is expelling shame and regret like a fat kid expels sweat. Ren throws her self down on the sofa next to Sarah before wrapping her arms around her super sized body. Sarah takes comfort as she sobs on Ren’s shoulder as her tears seep in to her 2nd hand charity shop jumper. Ren: Sarah, what’s up? What have you done that is so terrible that you feel that the only way to gain forgiveness is to be anally fisted by the big show? Sarah retracts her head, leaving a mess of mascara and tears on Ren’s shoulder. She then achieves eye contact with Ren with her bottom lip wobbling like a jelly in a high wind before finally confessing. Sarah: You know Red’s friend D’avid? Upon utters the words of our Anti Hero’s ginger side kick Ren instantly becomes over whelm with horror, her eyes swell to the point where they hurt and her jaw drops to the carpet to add exclamation to her disgust. Ren: NO! Sarah: I WAS DEPRESSED! AND HE WAS PERSISTANT! … and that’s not all… Sarah’s head sheepishly lowers; she can no longer look Ren in the eye as what she is about to say crosses the line of rationality. She places her jittering hand softly over her sloppy bulgy stomach, thus heightening the foul grimace sported by Ren. Ren: NO!!!! Sarah: Ren I think I’m pregnant! OH MY GOD I THINK I’M PREGNANT! Sarah breaks down in a fit of emotion and all Ren can do is desperately try and support her friend. Sarah holds Ren tightly as she lets all her anguish rain down on Ren’s shoulders but Ren sort of half hearted places her hands on Sarah’s shoulders like a person would if they were forced to touch a homeless person who smelt like they had pissed themselves. Ren: Sarah, it’s going to be ok… now what makes you think you’re pregnant? Sarah points her quivering finger over to a small pile of 6 pregnancy tests, all different brands, and all test positive. Ren: Oh… right! That’s it! I’m going to have words with D’avid about this! Sarah: Ren please! He doesn’t know! Ren: No Sarah! If you’re going to have his baby he’s going to have to support you every step of the way! Now if he thinks he can knock an 18 year old girl up and still frolic around doing what ever he does he’s got another thing coming! Ren is one of those self righteous hot headed people who love nothing more than to meddle in other people’s business. She doesn’t really like to see people wronged and nothing infuriates her than watching acts of gross injustice. But if only she would stop and think what the people in question want first, but again she is just one of those people. So… on that bombshell she throws her self forcefully to her feet before marching across the living room floor, over to the front door and out of the flat. She now stands at the foot of Red Rock’s door with one hand hammering on the door, one hand on her hip and a face for war. On the other side Red Rock naively opens the door and is taken back with surprise at this expected visit from Ren. Ren has been the object of Red’s desire for the last few months, but having no spine he’s found it rather difficult to express his feelings especially having friends like D’avid who consistently get him caught up in all kinds of trouble. Red Rock: Crikey! Ren! Err… HI! Red Rock leans on the frame of his door, trying to play it cool but Ren is a woman on a mission and accepts no bollocks; she simply shoves Red Rock through in to his flat before scanning the open plan area for D’avid. Ren: Piss off Red, where the hell is D’avid?! Red Rock: Oh… you’re here to see D’avid… Red Rock’s voice expresses a tone of immense disappointment, mainly due to the way Ren is furiously pacing around searching for D’avid and paying no attention to him. Red Rock: Well he’s just gone out; he said he was going out cruising down the park… mentioned something about dogging, which I found most peculiar seeing as we don’t own a dog. Ren: Well I hope he’s having a LOVELY time because my best friend is over the hall in tears because of your stupid mate! Red Rock: D’avid has screwed your fat mate, so what? We all make mistakes. Ren: URRRRGH! You’re an idiot! Red Rock bites his bottom lips, realizing he has let his big mouth drop him in it again. Ren stomps across Red Rock’s kitchen floor in a rage at Red Rock’s blunt words. She throws his door open and steps out in the hall way she stops to rant some more just before Red Rock jogs hastily over to Ren to apologize for his actions. Red Rock: Ren, I’m sorry I didn’t mean to call her fat let’s just say she’s curvatious? Ren: You don’t get it do you, she is pregnant! Pregnant with D’avid’s baby! Red Rock’s face at this point is a complete copy of Ren’s previous reaction but before he can utter any words the confrontation between Ren and him self is interrupted by the sound of shopping bags hitting the floor. As a tin of baked beans trundles up to their feet they slowly gear their heads to the right to investigate. Charles: My Sarah…pregnant? Red Rock and Ren stare each other in the in the eye, both reading the words spelt out by one another’s reaction. Red Rock: Uh oh… Ren: Mr Darling, please don’t be mad at Sarah! Ren begins to plea with Charles but all he can do is stand, frozen in his stride not even caring that the milk he dropped is spilling all over the hall. He face drops several shades whiter before he can finally find something to say. Charles: That’s stupid bloody ginger nut carrot top bastard has got my beautiful princess pregnant?! Ren: Please don’t overreact, its all D’avid’s fault! Red Rock: HEY! Don’t blame D’avid, it’s not his fault Sarah was desperate! Charles: I’ve always wanted grand kids…one day I imaged my wonderful girl would marry a good person with a good job… Charles’ cold blank expression breaks as tears gather in his eyes, he then drops to his knees and weeps in to the palms of his hands. Charles: It’s going to be ginger…A GINGER! AWWWW NO! Red Rock slowly approaches the emotionally unstable ex police superintendent to offer a support pat on the back. Red Rock: It’s ok Charles; with the scientific technology we have today the can develop cures for gingerness It’s called hair dye… From nowhere through the double doors leading to the bottom of the hall… D’avid: Hello to you everyone! Heh I forgot my binoculars… why is Mr Darling weeping? Suddenly Charles’ ears prick up and his pale face turns from white to red faster than the blink of an eye. Charles: YOU GINGER BASTARD! YOU KNOCKED UP MY SARAH! D’avid: AIIIIEEE! Charles lunges for D’avid with both hands, and before D’avid can even think about escaping Charles already has he hands around D’avid neck, crushing his wind pipe. Ren: MR DARLING, NO! Charles: I’D RATHER CLEAN OUT A PEADOPHILE’S BEDROOM THAN HAVE MY DAUGHTER BARE YOUR CHILD YOU PRICK! Ren: Violence isn’t the answer Mr Darling, it doesn’t solve anything! Red Rock: Yes it does, of course violence solves stuff! How do you think wars are won? Ren: SHUT UP! Ren eventually manages to pries Charles’ hands away from D’avid neck, leaving D’avid confused and disorientated. D’avid frantically struggles for oxygen as he scrambles back to his feet but what now stands before Red Rock, Charles and Ren is a different D’avid to what we normally see. D’avid: I’m going to be a father? The word father means so much more than just a guy who knocked up a girl. It spells a life time of responsibility; it’s being there for every stage in the child’s live, offering them support, guidance. It’s more than just changing nappies and taking them to school, or buying them clothes, it’s about modeling a person fit enough to blend in to society and create a good life for themselves. D’avid was born to the father of Dutch sex lord, a pimp, an owner of brothels and sex shops. D’avid is a free spirit who is happy to do nothing but perv over the women at Tescos and watch porn when Red Rock is in bed. But upon hearing that he might be a father it’s as if that part of him got choked out for good at the hands of Charles and what is left is a man willing to accept this responsibility.
