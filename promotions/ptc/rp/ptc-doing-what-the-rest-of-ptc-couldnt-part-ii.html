Doing What the Rest of PTC Couldn't, Part II (Extreme Title Match)

21 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME’s [COLOR=“Blue”]Overkill, the Cow Palace, San Francisco, CA, May 15th, 2005 “Peanuts! Get yer peanuts here!” An indifferent concessions employee wanders through the arena crowd, his box of trusty legumes jutting out from his chest. “Peanuts, folks, plenty of peanuts here for sale! Come on, this is a shit-job, someone buy a bag of peanuts…” No one is paying him any mind. Halting in his well-traversed peanut-peddling path, the man realizes that everyone around is him is standing in relative quiet, all eyes locked on the same point. He turns slowly, and his eyes widen in surprise. “Holy crap.” More than twenty feet above the ring, Nova grunts as his hands cling to the floor of the scaffold, the rest of his body dangling precariously in mid-air above the mat. He can’t help but think about the scene from Fellowship of the Ring where Gandalf clings to the ledge, bugging out his wizard eyes at Frodo and the gang. He might debate shouting “Fly, you fools!” except that his opponent isn’t up on the scaffold to appreciate the LotR reference. She’s hanging from his legs. The Rising Star spares a glance downward at Shadow, her fangs bared as her long fucking nails dig painfully into his ankles. The crazy vampiric bitch had given him quite a run tonight in her efforts to remain PTC’s Extreme Champion. “Aack!” Nova utters as his grip slips back further, leaving him and his undead cargo dangling from the scaffold’s edge by his fingertips. Thinking about it now, Nova can’t help but regret picking a Scaffold Match over the Bra & Panties stipulation that had been his second choice. The crowd utters a collective suspenseful gasp as Nova’s hand-hold slips further. Shit, he curses to himself, his hands relating back to his brain that he has about five seconds left to hang on before it’s time to check in with gravity. Shadow saves him the time, swinging out and pushing against the scaffolding with her feet and ripping both of them free. Flashbulbs erupt in the Cow Palace as they plummet. Nova closes his eyes and for a moment is frozen in the free-fall, his mind peacefully blank. No concerns, no regrets, nothing happy, nothing sad. Hopeless. Fearless. He feels like he could stay in that moment for a long time, enjoying the full-brain massage of the void. But reality doesn’t work that way for non-vegetables, and the champion and challenger hit the table waiting below at max velocity. Lights out, Beansprout. Doing What the Rest of PTC Couldn't, Part II The lobby of the Crowne Plaza Hotel, downtown Columbus, OH, December 20th, 2006 Nova stands outside the door to the conference room, within which a host of wrestling press wait to grill him about the upcoming Extreme Title match against GCW’s Vivica J. Valentine and renegade free agent Archibald MacGregor. He fights the urge to light a cigarette, knowing that the overzealous hotel security would climb out of potted plants and ceiling shafts, demanding with their pepper sprays drawn that he find a garbage can for it immediately. It’s one thing he misses about staying in shitty hotels. You could get away with about anything in those places. You could stumble black-out drunk into the lobby, pistol poking out of your belt, joint hanging out of your mouth, girl passed out lying across your shoulders, and they’d probably give you a room. Come to think of it, Nova recalls seeing that happen once…in the Sandpiper Lodge in Santa Cruz, CA. Wild night. Nova gives himself a shake, pulling out of his reverie, and pounds his fists together. His arms are wrapped in black tape with white “F U’s” across the backs of his hands, and PRIME’s Universal Title is slung across his shoulder. It acts as a beacon of confidence, shouting, “That’s right, mother-fuckers. Listen to me.” “Ready, sir?” a young man asks him, and when the Risen Star nods, the man pushes open the door and holds it for him. Nova winces as flashbulbs assault him on his way up to the podium. He takes his place in front of it and offers a forced grin to the media gathered in front of him. “Thanks for coming. I’m here to answer questions about the PTC Extreme Title match coming up involving myself, a representative from GCW – Vivica J. Valentine – and the free…” A chubby balding man shoots up in the third row. “Nova, we know the booking!” “Ummm…alright, then,” Nova replies, obviously irritated, “then while you’re up, do you have a question?” “What I wanna is, what’s the story with this match?” the man asks, “Practically no hype, no response from the reigning champion so far…” Nova holds up a hand, happy to cut the bastard off who saw fit to interrupt him moments before. “Yeah, well, the story is I got offered a shot at the champion after he retained at PTC vs. Archibald MacGregor, as did a few others. Valentine accepted. Brandon Youngblood chose to hold onto his shot and take on the winner of this contest in a Submissions Match. Scheduling conflicts arose involving GTT6, and here we are today, with cleared schedules, happily ready to beat each other’s faces in.” The last comment gets a few laughs, and another man stands. “You just said some interesting things there, Nova.” “Why thank you,” Nova interjects, a grin on his face, “what’s your name?” The reporter laughs. “Drew Thomas, RingRat.com. Archibald MacGregor DID walk into a Pay-Per-View set up to take back the Extreme Title from free agent hands to one of the PTC member feds. He went up against the likes of Valentine, Youngblood, Damien Cruz, Cereal Killer, and Karina Wolfenden, among others, still managing to retain the belt. What makes you think you can accomplish what they couldn’t?” “Well, Karina Wolfenden never beat Tchu either, did she?” Nova replies with a grin. Another reporter pops up. “Yes, we all can see the shiny PRIME strap on your shoulder…” Dick, Nova thinks to himself, delightful visions of tossing the smarky bastard in front of a Hummer dancing in the Risen Star’s head. “…but I have to ask, are you going to make this belt a priority?” the man continues, “You’re the top gun for what many consider the flagship federation in PTC, and your schedule will be full trying to evade the proverbial target dancing around on your back. What does this match mean to you?” “I am proud of the ‘shiny PRIME strap’ on my shoulder,” Nova answers, sans grin this time, “but the Extreme Title is very near and dear to me. I went through a lot the first go-round to obtain the belt, and by ‘a lot’ I mean fifteen people, and in the end I didn’t get a chance to defend it. So I’m putting every ounce of that effort back into this match to do what no one has ever done before…win the Extreme Title twice.” “Once upon a time you were 2 – 0 in PTC competition,” the same reporter goes on, “and now you’re 3 – 2, having been eliminated in GTT6 in the second round by one of the people who didn’t beat MacGregor, and also failing to re-enter the tournament in the Infinite Gauntlet in your inaugural foray into that competition. Are you worried that your momentum in PTC has waned, that this match, as well, might be another flubbed opportunity?” “First off, I had to forfeit that GTT6 match with Cruz,” Nova replies, hints of anger in his voice. “A drug test, Nova?” someone calls from the back, and the room fills with laughter. “Right,” Nova says with a boyish grin, “no, it was other regulation bullshit. And no, I’m not worried about my ‘momentum.’ You make your own momentum. This match outcome isn’t up to fate. You can have the worst night of your career and go out the next night and take it all home. I’ve done it.” A tall, lanky kid with brown hair stands up, holding out a microphone. “What are your thoughts about Brandon Youngblood and a submissions match if you manage to win the Extreme Title?” “I’m not here to talk about Youngblood,” Nova says, waving off the question, “I’m here to talk about Vivica J. Valentine, who no one seems to be very concerned with, and Archibald MacGregor, and this match. I’m watching out for Valentine. She seems to be taking this opportunity a lot more seriously than Archie Mac is.” More hands go up, but Nova holds out his arms for quiet. “Guys, I appreciate you coming out and giving this match some thought, since we’ve kinda flown under the radar here. I just want to say a few things before we call it a day. I’ve waited a long time for this rematch…a long fucking time. I sat on the sidelines and watched Hoyt Williams walk out of a clusterfuck with the Extreme Title around his waist; Hoyt Williams, who I beat in our last encounter and who I didn’t think would be able to lick me and take the belt if Nova vs. Hoyt had been the booking. I watched as Shadow got a golden ticket and went on to become Global Champion, the same woman I beat to win the Extreme Title in the first place. GTT6 was a fuck-up. My hopes for that, quite high I admit, were dashed…but I’ve been thinking about this one for a long, long time, and I don’t intend on marking up another one in the ‘L’ column for my PTC record. You guys take it easy.” More flashbulbs go off as Nova walks away from the podium, trying his damndest not to sprint out of the lobby. The cigarette is already is his mouth. ------*~*~*------ PRIME’s Overkill, the Cow Palace, San Francisco, CA, May 15th, 2005 “Nova…” “Hey, Nova…” “NOVA!” The Rising Star’s eyes flutter open, and for a moment all he sees is a grayish blur with patches of color swirling around in front of his eyes. He hears something akin to the sound of running water in the next room, a hum growing louder and louder. As feeling returns, he can’t help but think that this is the softest the mat has ever felt. After a moment, he realizes this is because he’s lying on the body of Shadow. Nova looks up to the smiling face of referee Thomas Giles, who extends a hand. He takes it, and is pulled to his feet. Giles slaps the PTC Extreme Title against his chest. “You did it, Nova! Shadow hit first!” He looks around. The crowd is out of control, cheering for him and for the accomplishment. He doesn’t realize (and won’t for a few days) that his victory completes the PTC triumvirate, officially marking the first time a fed has ever held all three titles at one time. Still shaky from the fall, Nova hoists the title above his head, a goofy grin spreading across his face. The crowd erupts. It’s a good night.
