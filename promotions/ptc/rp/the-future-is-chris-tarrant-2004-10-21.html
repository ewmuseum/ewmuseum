The future is Chris Tarrant

21 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-21 You know, I don’t know why I did it….not really at least. It may have been because I was tired of living within my own shadow for well over 15 years, maybe it was because I wanted people to know who I truly was, I don’t know, but what’s done is done, and now I have to live with it. I told my agent I was dropping my gimmick, the long lived Tarrant character, the man who had for years and years been a dark plague sweeping across the wrestling world was no more, and all that in his spot now was me, myself, Chris Tarrant. It was weird to not dress up in the usual attire I would for interviews, promos, in ring work, but it was something I had to get use to if I hoped to make this transition work, which I really waned to, but I had to admit I had creeping doubts in the back of my mind that I just simply wouldn’t be accepted by the fans of this great business. I realize as I sit here and write this, that the fact of the matter is; only time will tell, just as it is with all things in the world. It’s weird that my alter ego of so many years was the God of Time and Fate, and now here I sat, wondering what the future would bring to ME, an odd little twist if you think about it, well I think so at least. I hope that the road ahead of me isn’t filled with to many bumps, holes or things that could case to much trouble as I travel down it. As I enter this last stretch in my career, it is imperative that things go off without a hitch, if I am truly to be remembered as Chris Tarrant the great wrestler and performer, and not Tarrant, the sadistic Godling then thing would need to go as exactly I had planned, for if they didn’t, both alter ego and myself would likely fade away into nothingness for all of eternity, and quite simple, I don’t think my mind would allow for such a thing. ~~*****~~ “Well hun, are you about ready to go?” The female voice of my long time wife Amber rang out like church bells on a beautiful Sunday morning. Shaking me from my writing, I closed my journal and looked up to see her standing in the door way of my study, dressed in a business skirt and blouse, it was hard to take my eyes off her as she entered the room. “And just what are YOU looking at?” she said with a playful voice. I felt the features on my face curl up into a smile as I placed the feather quill pen, the only pen I ever like to write with, back in its holder, caped the ink well and reached my arms out to her. She obliged my longings and she entered them and in turn, wrapped her own arms around me as well. “My, aren’t you in a loving mood this morning.” Her words dripped with the very essence of love and affection. All I could do was sit there and soak in her warmth, her strength was something I had come to rely on over the years, and now, in these difficult times, it was no different. I let out a sigh and gently pushed her away so that I could stand up. “I am always loving with it concerns you, but this morning, I have to admit, I had other motives for that hug.” I said the words while going across the room to the coat rack where my suit coat hung, and in one fluid motion I flung it around myself, slipped my arms into it and buttoned the two buttons on it. “Other reasons? Well, I hardly think there is time for such things, but I suppose we could try, the children are away fro the weekend after all.” Looking up sharply I saw the mischievous look in her eyes, laughing I sat down and began to put on my shoes. “My dear, if there was time for such a thing, believe you me, we would already be half undressed and working our way up the stairs this very moment!” I laughed at my own words and look up at her, I could see the smallest twinge of disappointment in her face, but she then laughed it off. “Then what on earth could your other motive have been?” I stopped in the middle of lacing up my left shoe and without even looking up at here the words ran over my lips before I could stop them. “I am scared Amber, deathly scared.” I hadn’t meant for it to come out like that, but it did, and what was done was done. As I sat there I heard her soft footsteps walk over to where I sat and she placed her soft delicate hand upon my shoulder. “Things will work out, you just watch, your fans will be just as impressed with Chris Tarrant the man, as they were Tarrant the wrestler. Have faith dearest, it is a powerful weapon, even your old alter ego understood that.” Her words rang true and hit close to home. It was true that Faith was something Tarrant had said over and over was one of the most powerful things in the universe. If people had faith in you and you had faith in yourself, any deed, no matter how grand, could be accomplished. It was odd how even though we were the same person, how truly different we really were. “I suppose your right….” I let the words trail off with a sigh. I felt her hand grip harder on my shoulder, in response I looked up at her only to be met with a very passionate kiss. After a few moments of that she pulled away, and looked me strait in the eyes. “Everything will be fine, you always manage to make things turn out for the best, and no will be no different.” She sounded so sure of herself; I only wish I shared her confidence. Just then I felt her hands slide down my chest and undo the two buttons I had just buttoned, he lips brushed my left ear and I heard the words as if the wind had whispered them to me. “What say you we try that “our clothes being half off and working our way up the stairs” idea you had?” As she said it, her perfume washed over me, her scent was intoxicating, and whatever it was she was wearing, I reminded myself to buy her another bottle of it the moment she was about to run out. I couldn’t help myself as I found my arms wrapping around her slender body and drawing her closer until it felt as if our two bodies had become one. I knew we didn’t have the time, we would be late for my interview, but I just couldn’t help it. ~~*****~~ A few ours later we were in the car on our way to the PWI offices for an interview I had promised them. Once they had learned I was dropping my gimmick, they called strait away to get an interview with me, and being they were the leading wrestling magazine in the nation, I was happy to oblige, I had hopes that this would help my cause with the fans to accept me for who I truly was and not who I for years pretended to be. Pulling up to the office, I noted the time. 1:37pm, we were supposed to be here at 12:30pm for the interview, maybe they wouldn’t notice? I know it was a fat chance, but other things had called, and some things you just can’t put off, especially when they involve the opposite sex. Parking the car, I got out and walked to the passenger side and opened the door so that Amber could get out. IT may have been the modern day, but I always prided myself that I treated her like a queen, and would until the day I died. It was little things like opening the door for her that let her know how much I respected and loved her. We got to the front entrance, and again I opened the door for my wife, allowing her enter first and then I was quickly to follow. A receptionist desk was in the middle of the small lobby so I walked over to it and waited for her to notice I was standing there. It only took a few moments to realize she wasn’t alone and she stopped her typing and looked up at me with a smile. “Hi, can I help you?” “Um, yes, my name is Chris Tarrant, I am here to see Jason Richards for an interview?” She then looked back to her monitor and began typing, after a few moments she looked back at me smiling. “Better late then never eh Mr. Tarrant? Please have a seat over there.” She said pointing to a set of chairs nets to a large wooden door. “Mr. Richards will be with you in a moment.” I’m sure my face turned red with embarrassment as she pointed out, even jokingly how late I truly was. “Um, thank you very…much.” Walking over to the chairs I saw the door swing open before we could even sit down and a man who I assumed was Jason Richards walked out and greeted me. “Ah, Chris, better late then never eh?” “Uh….yes, so it would seem.” I responded a bit embarrassed. “We were tied up in traffic Mr. Richards, sorry to keep you waiting.” My wife’s voice piped in. Amber to the rescue, god bless her. Mr. Richards nodded and smiling he motioned for us to enter his office. I stepped aside allowing Amber to enter before me and was quickly to follow. The office was about he size of my study, not huge, but big enough for what it was needed for. “Please sit and make yourselves comfortable. May I interest you in some coffee or tea?” “No thank you” both my wife and I replied in unison. Smiling, Richards went around to his side of the desk and opened a drawer and pulled out a small digital recorder. Placing it on his desk he looked up at both my wife and I. “Shall we get started?” I nodded to him in agreement and I heard the button click on the recorder that signified that all words were now “on the record”. “Ok, Mr. Tarrant, thank you for taking the time to sit down with me today.” “It’s my pleasure Jason.” The interview itself was pretty much a run of the mill interview. We talked about my past, what I thought of other wrestlers, where I saw the business going in the next few years, it was pretty much the same as the hundreds of other interviews I have had through the years. I was relived to see how smoothly things were going and felt a small measure of my confidence returning to me as each moment passed. I looked over and saw a small bowl of nuts and helped myself. Everything was going more smoothly then I had even hoped. Or so I had thought. “So Tarrant, Many consider switching gimmick’s, or even dropping a gimmick that they have had for YEARS is career suicide, how do you hope to overcome such a thing happening to you?” The question threw me off guard to such a degree; I inhaled a peanut and began to choke on it causing me to go into a coughing fit. “Um, are you ok Chris?” I help up my hand to give the sign of “just a moment” and took a drink from the glass of water that was brought into us about midway through the interview. “Sorry about that, I was choking on your nuts.” Oh good lord I though, I wonder how THAT will sound on tape when played back. “Um, ok, well do you have a response for my question?” Well, now was my time to let the world know my reasons, and pray they accepted them. “Well Jason…” I tried to sound as sure of myself as possible. “My reasons are my own, but I did it because I have to face facts, I am not nearly as young as I once was. I have entered my forties recently, and the fact remains, I am not the wrestler I was ten years or even five years ago. Granted, I am still able to do a 720 and other airel moves that men my age could only wish they could do, but I feel it more and more each night. For years I have entertained the masses as Tarrant, the dark lord of time and fate, but to be honest, that’s not how I started out and became the wrestler I was and am today. My long time fans remember the days when I was Chris Tarrant, where I invented a new move every week, I was innovative and entertaining. Now that my career is winding down, I only wish to go out and be remembered for who I am, not who I pretend to be. I want the younger generation of fans to see who is behind the gimmick, and I want them to see who Tarrant REALLY is. I want them to remember the man, not the gimmick.” It was a response from the heart, and the words flowed from my heart and soul as if some dam were let loose. Jason took it in stride and went onto his next question. “Well, why now? Why do it when GTT, the biggest tournament in wrestling today is going on?” “Well, I guess that is actually one of the main reasons I DID decide to do it now. What better time to show the world who I truly am, when I know the entire wrestling world will be watching my match this weekend against the very talented Blaise "Hazard" McClancy. Blaise is a wrestler who has many of the younger generation of fans in the palm of his hand, he is an awesome wrestler and great performer, why not now, of all places take the opportunity to show all those people who will be watching who I truly am, what I am truly capable of….the match will be one for the ages, I have all the respect in the world for Blaise, but I feel I have a lot more on the line ion this match the he does, I feel my career is on the line, while to him its just another tournament in which if he losses, he will have many more to be in. For me, this could be my last, and the end of my career. If that happens, and it very well could, I want to give it my all as Chris Tarrant, so at least I can say it was ME and not the gimmick that poured his heart and soul into that match.” The interview when on from there, it was a lot of small talk, and I couldn’t help but feel that Mr. Richards though I was a complete idiot, but I figure what was done was done. At the end, he thanked me and my wife for showing up and told me that the interview would be added to the new edition of PWI, it was about ready to go into its final print and would be on news stands in a few days. I thanked him and said a small prayer that everything would turn out well and I wouldn’t like a complete idiot. After Amber and I were well on our way home she put her hand on my thigh. “You did perfect Chris, don’t worry, people will see your passion and know what you are doing isn’t daft, but it’s the wish of a man who has done everything and anything possible to entertain them through the years, you just wait and see.” Her words for once did not comfort me in the least bit, I couldn’t help but feel that Jason was right, this was carrier suicide, but at least if anyone was going to kill the Tarrant dynasty off, at least it would be. For some reason, that wasn’t very reassuring either…….. ----End Scene ***PWI is a registered trademark, I thank them and Jason Richards for allowing me to use their names in this RP.
