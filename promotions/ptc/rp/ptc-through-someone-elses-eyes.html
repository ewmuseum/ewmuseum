Through someone else's eyes...

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I. “Angel Eyes” My head rested on the headrest of the Taxi still soaking in everything that had gone on in the last few hours. Had I really just done what it seemed? Did I, Roberto Abitua win the Global Championship Wrestling WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT championship!? I lowered my gaze to my lap where the shiny gold and leather belt rested and a smile came across my face. It really did happen, there was no doubt about it now. I have fought for four years to get to where I am now and I can honestly say it was all worth it. Enduring humiliating name callings, such as “GCW’s Official Jobber”, being tossed aside for lesser talents like Octavian and The Jhub, and I believe there is no doubt in anyone’s minds that I can beat either of them on any given day. GCW or more specifically, Steven Caldera has put me through hell … stripping me of my own self confidence at one point, but in a scary way I probably owe him one. Hell if it weren’t for those hard times I would not have trained as vigorously as I had after I left GCW. I believe the cliché , “From Zero to hero” almost says it all. Okay well I am not a HERO per say, but I am successful. Over and over the events of Dangerous Games replayed in my mind. Dread and I brought the house down, literally in most cases. Steven would probably be taking out the money needed to fix his ring, from my pay check. He can take the money though, I have the one item I have been clawing and scratching for. Physically I was drained, Jason put up a hell of a fight in fact it was more of a fight than I expected from him. He came out firing on all cylinders and nearly caught me completely off guard. Its too bad I have been in that situation before. Since returning to the GCW I have been beaten to the punch in the early goings, it’s a sign of respect if you think about it. They know they don’t want to stand toe to toe with you because if they lose any kind of leverage early on, they WILL NOT get it back. They all run out of gas eventually though, evident by my record breaking United States Title reign AND my forgettable two losses since the grand opening. Life was looking up for me that was for certain and only one thing could make this night one of perfection. A smile came across my face as then cab stopped and I looked out the window at the dark outline of my house. Inside would be my bed, good food, and of course Mary. It had been far too long since I had been with Mary and it was eating me up inside. Just the very thought of her touch, her kiss, her love made life worth living. Sure this Title belt felt amazing but not nearly as amazing as being loved by the one you love. I threw my black gym bag over my shoulder before paying the driver and scurrying out the door. I could hear the car drive off as I began my walk up the sidewalk to my front door. With each step I could feel my happiness growing, it was time to celebrate the most important part of my professional career with the most important aspect of my personal life … probably involving a lot of alcohol and some wild animal sex. I could picture Mary waiting in the bedroom, wearing combat boots ONLY, her raven curls bouncing around wildly and those big beautiful eyes staring at me seductively. Maybe she would be waiting at the door when I walked in? Wearing that red silk robe I bought her before I left for Boston with nothing else underneath it. Candles lit everywhere and a bottle of expensive champagne resting on ice. Maybe some soft music, probably the silky voice of one Luther Vandross. Regardless of what she had planned or what was going to happen, this night was destined to be perfect. Destiny has been on my side for the better part of two years now. I finally reached the door and ripped the keys from my jeans pocket. After a little bit of fiddling with the key the door swung open, and I stared into a completely dark house. Okay I was a little disappointed that those candles I could see in my head weren’t lit all around but it was late and Mary did not know when I’d be getting in. I dropped my gym bag and scanned the dark house for a moment, maybe Mary had been hiding, she was always one to play games. I smiled at this thought, remembering some of the great times we have had since we’ve been together. A slight chuckle escaped my throat as I look over to the coffee table where a folded up piece of paper rests. Yeah she was playing games. I shook my head as I walked over to the table, flipping on the lamp that also was on that table. Picking up the piece of paper I chuckled again just thinking about what MIGHT be in this letter. Anxiously I unfolded the letter, spreading it out in front of me and began reading it out loud. “ Dear Rob-Bearto,” She always had a knack for calling me off the wall names. “You wonts oonder stand Mary when you gets this, cuz Mary don’t even get it yet! Mary has to get out tho Statty, she jus gotta. You have changed and it scares Mary, like it did with Damey. Mary can’t wait for her to have her heart ripped out again … too painful. Pleeease don’t come diggin for Mary, shes dead to you now.” I stood, frozen in disbelief as I stared at the letter in my hands. It was if this letter was meant for someone else, this couldn’t have been for me, I have treated Mary like a queen! I love her with every ounce of me being. Why would she leave me NOW!? My hands were shaking, eventually shaking so bad that the letter dropped from my hand and landed on the floor. I bring my hands up to my face, shaky hands that I could not control. I began feeling sick, perhaps love sick. I could feel my bottom lip begin to quiver uncontrollably. My eyes burned as I fought back the tears, I fought as hard as I could. The first tear slowly crawled down my cheek, with a shaky hand I wiped it away. I closed my hands into fists to stop the shaking, to try and control these feelings. I slammed my eyes shut hoping to push this all out of my mind … maybe even to wake up from this horrible nightmare. Soon I’d wake up, sure I wouldn’t be the GCW World Champ anymore but I would have Mary by my side. I would wake up with Mary in our bedroom and tell her that she needed to come with me to Boston and watch me crush Jason Dread and win the world title. This was all a bad dream! My eyes shot open … The nightmare had just began … II. “Soul Searching” Here he was, Roberto Abitua confronting a truth and reality that maybe he was not ready to face. The love of his life had yet again left him creating the vulnerability needed for him to lose his grip on his own life, his very soul. Since the last lapse Roberto had been very good at keeping that “Dark Half” of his in check, only releasing it when the time was absolutely necessary. Roberto figured that He would just keep it at bay until he figured out a way to destroy it, or at the very least control it. What he has failed to realize is that Evil doesn’t die and PURE Evil cannot be controlled, it controls you. So here he is weak, beaten, depressed and now staring at the one thing that he did not want to see right now. His Dark Half … Just like any creature that feeds on being in control, that bleak part of your soul lays dormant until you are at your worst point. Sure it may flare up once in awhile to smile at you, but it will not strike. As insane as it may seem, that part of you knows that it is nothing without the strength of your fear, anger, and especially that feeling of hopelessness. With most people this dark side of them cannot gain too much strength because they don’t allow it to come out. Everyone has thoughts of kicking the neighbor’s dog, slapping the hell out of that obnoxious co-worker, maybe even breaking the leg of that guy who keeps looking at your girlfriend, but it won’t get too serious. Common sense and reasoning comes into play, almost like checks and balances. Roberto on the other hand has lost control of this, he has no barrier separating the good and the evil in him. Its merely a line in the sand. It has gotten so bad that his dark half has become a whole other entity entirely. Most see Roberto and automatically put him and the one he plays on camera, LeStatt Knight as one and the same, how wrong they are. Roberto has fed LeStatt so much that he grows stronger and stronger with each and every passing moment. Now like a shark, LeStatt smells blood. With everything from confusion to depression to anger building up inside of Roberto, that line in the sand is about to be wiped away completely. Roberto, deep down, knows that he can’t fight off LeStatt now but he will try … he knows he has been too close to complete happiness to let it die now, without a valiant effort. Roberto’s teary, blue eyes look directly into those dark, emotionless eyes of LeStatt Knight and instantly that sickness turns into anger. Roberto’s facial expression changes completely into more of a snarl. LeStatt smiles back at the one who has held him captive for far too long now. “Hola Roberto … how is life?” His arrogance made Roberto’s blood boil even hotter now. Roberto’s breathing became heavy and erratic. There was a fire in the man’s eyes that may never have been there before. LeStatt took pleasure in being able to do this to Roberto, as a matter of fact he relished every moment of it. Roberto’s breaths were being blown out through gritted teeth, his face red, and those icy blue eyes of his seemed to have melted. “Fuck you LeStatt!” Roberto charged, grabbing LeStatt by the throat and driving him to the wall creating a hole in it. LeStatt’s dark eyes widened by the force of the blow, and immediately his hands wrapped around Roberto’s wrists, fighting off the choke that was being applied. LeStatt forced a smile but this smile was nowhere near the arrogant smirk that was there prior. “Why are you angry with me Roberto?” LeStatt managed to squeak out. “It wasn’t me who left you.” “Yes it was LeStatt … Your silly games are what drove her away! She watched what you have been doing during the GCW shows and was afraid of you!” LeStatt managed to get a chuckle out before Roberto tightened his grip, which stifled the laugh quickly. Those dark eyes of LeStatt now dug right into Roberto, as if he were trying to read into his very being. “Ro…Roberto, face it … Mary left YOU not me.” He adjusts a bit, trying to get a better breathing pattern. “How could I have made her do that? Roberto you have just become too infatuated with your work and winning the GCW World Title. You drove her away Roberto and now I hope that title is good enough.” Roberto’s eyes dropped for a moment, obviously taking in the words spoken by LeStatt. Soon a tear runs down his face once more and shortly after he releases his grip on LeStatt, taking a few steps backwards. “You’re right … I did this.” He brings his shaky hands up covers his face. “What was I thinking? I had the perfect life, love, wealth, happiness…” “NO!” LeStatt shouted, cutting Roberto off. “You did not have the perfect life Roberto, I merely wanted you to let me go when I said this was your fault. How can it be? She claimed to love you, unconditionally right? Well how is this unconditional? This is MARY’S fault! She used you Roberto, she used you to get what she wanted and then she took off.” “NO she wouldn’t do that LeStatt…” “Oh but she would, ANY woman would. You are a rich man Roberto and being seen with you can help one’s career and personal life immensely.” “She never took a dime from me!” Roberto screamed with conviction and stares into Knight‘s black eyes. “Don’t you try to drag her name through the mud.” LeStatt sighs and shakes his head in mock disbelief. “Love can blind a person Roberto, you should know that better than anyone. Just like with Wylie you are trying to pretend that you weren’t fucked over by a member of the opposite sex. When will you realize that they can’t be trusted?” “YOU can’t be trusted LeStatt.” Roberto said coldly, which causes Knight to laugh outright. “ME? Please Roberto I am not the bad guy here, you just like to blame me for every wrong thing that happens to you. Tell me when have you NOT been able to trust what I have said? I am up front with you Roberto, more upfront than anyone is with you. When I say I am going to do something, I do it … right, wrong, bad, or good I accomplish my goals. If I stand here now and say that I am going to drive a knife through your heart then you better damn well believe it.” LeStatt takes a step closer to Roberto but is smart enough to stop there. “The women in your life have been far from honest with you though Roberto, and that is a fact.” “Not Cindy…” Roberto said quietly speaking of his late wife. “CINDY IS DEAD!” LeStatt snarled. “That’s your biggest problem Roberto, you live in the past … dwelling on it, crying about it when you should have your sights set on the future!” “MARY WAS MY FUTURE!” All got quiet now as these two glared at each other, with a mixture of hatred and fear. It was so deathly quiet that it was almost possible to hear the steady beating of their hearts. How long the silence really lasted one may never know, but in both of their minds it could have been an eternity. It wasn’t until LeStatt Knight decided to take matters into his own hands, that the silence was broken. “Listen to me Roberto, Cindy is dead, Wylie is marrying RaZiel, and Mary has left you alone. You have nothing and no one left. It is time to do what is needed for you to succeed. Do not let your love for that women rule you, because obviously she does not love you back. The future Roberto isn’t with Mary…” He points a finger to the GCW World Title which was sprawled out on the floor. “THAT is your… NO OUR future! We will not just be legends but GODS amongst men. Embrace this, embrace ME and five yourself the strength to beat this and any other hurdle that may arise.” Roberto backed into the wall and slid down it slowly. He sat there almost as if in a daze staring at the wall behind LeStatt. TO Knight it didn’t seem that Roberto was staring AT the wall though, more like through the wall. Looking at his life and how far things had come in that life. “ROBERTO!” He snapped back and turned his glare to LeStatt. No words came out at first, just that blank stare of a man who has lost everything. Another tear fell from his right eye, going down his cheek and eventually falling from his face to the carpet below. In a cracked voice Roberto finally spoke. “I can’t fight anymore LeStatt …” Roberto had to fight the tears from flowing. “My life always seems to fuck itself up, no matter how hard I try to be the good guy of the story.” “The GOOD GUYS never win Roberto, face it. It is time to take what is rightfully yours.” “Ye-yes it is…” “I can take away this pain you are feeling Roberto and you know it. Let me take control and watch as we reach glorious heights that have never even been dreamed of. Watch as WE take control of our own destiny and bring down an iron fist which no one can battle against.” “But …” LeStatt now walks to Roberto’s curled up body and kneels down in front of him. “NO buts Roberto! It’s time! The future is NOW and this is your one shot to take the reigns and leave everyone else in the dust.” Roberto finally breaks down, tears flow from his eyes like a waterfall. He buries his face into his hands and lets the emotion flow from his soul. Knight watches on calmly, knowing that he has just won this battle. Lestatt pushes himself to his feet and extends his hand to Roberto. “Take my hand Roberto and let us put an end to this pain and suffering.” Roberto looked up at LeStatt, a being which he hated and feared but somehow respected. He gazed at the extended hand in deep contemplation. His eyes were red from crying and his hands were as shaky as ever. Something inside of him knew that he couldn’t give in to this dark being … but it just wasn’t a battle he cared to continue fighting. Slowly Roberto began reaching out. III. “The Devil’s Eyes” Inwardly I was smiling as he grabbed my hand and allowed me to pull him up to his feet. I had been waiting for so long to put this sad son of a bitch in his place. Roberto had tried to control me, contain me, lock me up, and throw me away for so long, and now it was time for me to lock him up in shackles and treat him like a fucking dog. I have let him drag me around by this leash for long enough. I have smiled at him and tried to keep myself under control but now it was the opening I needed to take everything and run it the way I see fit. Maybe now I could stop hearing Roberto gush about Mary, whine about how he is mistreated in GCW, or how he can’t believe his best friend was banging his ex-fiancé… for fuck’s sake I would have screwed her until I couldn’t walk anymore … or at least until she couldn’t. I stood in front of this broken down man and saw the desperation in his eyes. He was looking for any escape from this hell that he had found himself in, and he knew that only I could give him the release he longed for. Life had taken its toll on poor Roberto, something that I had counted on since he created me back in 2001. He is a strong and stubborn bastard I will give him that, but no one is invincible. I saw this coming though, Mary was far too unstable and give the mix of Roberto’s fucked up mind she was bound to get scared. So here it is LeStatt Knight getting his chance to shine, his time to rule. It is a day that the wrestling world has dreaded for so long. The whispers of the untapped potential will cease and I will make sure that my name rings out loud and thunderously through time and history. I will carry the GCW World Heavyweight Title will the kind of pride and honor that a man like Jason Dread could only dream about. Every time someone steps between those ropes I will make sure fear fills their heart and soul like a canteen full of water. No distractions of love, sorrow and pity will interrupt my run at the top. If there is one thing that Roberto has gotten right this whole time its when he told the world right before he stepped out at Dangerous Games and proclaimed that the GCW was now in “The Dark Ages.” No words were ever truer … even if even he had no idea how truthful they were. A black cloud will now hover over the lives of those who inhabit GCW. Roberto was gone now and I stood in the dark living room alone. The pain was certainly gone, the pain of the past would stay in the past the way it should be. No need to dig up skeletons because when you do that … all you get is dirty. At my feet was the letter sent by one Typhoid Mary claiming that her love and trust for Roberto had vanished. Reaching into my pocket I pull out a black lighter. I flick it on just to make sure it works before bending down and lifting the letter in my hands. I shake my head and let out a low chuckle. “Fucking bitch…” Then I put the flame to the paper and watch as it catches fire and immediately burns up. I drop the flame on the coffee table and watch as it burns. As it burned somewhere inside of me I could feel Roberto’s pain, hear his crying and deep down I liked it. His pain and fear brought me strength, the strength to do what is needed. I watched as the flame on the paper died out and all that was left were the ashes. A thin cloud of black smoke glided upwards and rested in the air. I could not take my eyes off of the smoke, it was almost hypnotizing. “Sweet Dreams Roberto … Sweet Dreams.” A smile came across my face right before I turned and walked through the threshold and left my Mansion, hopefully for the last time. I had to leave everything behind from my former life and start anew. It was my rebirth…
