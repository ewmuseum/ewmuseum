Some sort of Vacation?

20 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-20 PTC is in need of new tag team champions. Why is this? Because the last ones weren't good enough. They got them and then felt the need to go somewhere else, thus vacating them. For a year now? More? Less? PTC has been without a tag team that could stand up and represent it. But now it's time to crown a new one set of champions at an event where everything is on the line. Some would say that the biggest tragedy to hit PTC lately had been Archibald MacGregor, the free agent who now has the Extreme Title actually beating Hoyt Williams to win that belt. But they would be wrong. The worst tragedy to ever happen to a PTC title was The Killy Pirates ever coming within a breathe of the Tag Team Titles, and now they're within that stance for a second time. You couldn't do much worse then them being there. But now we step into a match at this coming event. Four teams representing four feds. The aforementioned Killer Pirates representing Global. A place we know of well. A place we left because they closed. But then they reopened and it's obvious they were desperate to save money. They hired teams like this to fill in their ailing tag team division that wasn't up to snuff when we were there. In fact, we had defeated every other team they had and in the end, instead of allowing a team that could carry the division singlehandedly on it's shoulders take the ball and run with it, they killed the division until after they had reopened under this new, cost cutting regime. They've not been the same since. And it's blatantly obvious as to why when you have teams like this, or have matches with people taking on the likes of washed up actors looking for a quick paycheck after their piss poor TV runs failed. You have the Furious Fists of God, representing the Atlantic Wrestling Club. A federation that has to get cheap heat by being… invaded. That's right INVADED by such an evil menace as the Pacific Wrestling Club. That's right, they're invading force represented the surfers and volley ball players of California. How Evil is that? And then again their tag team for this match? A couple of guys who envision themselves as the Furious Fists of God. Even if such a thing could be proven to exists, would God really take it unto himself to give that distinction to Tim and Liam L.? Nay, S/He wouldn't. They're no better then the black dots on this industry as they use the obvious religious references to get themselves cheap heat. Oh, let's toss you the Furious Fist of God, or the Book of Revelations. They're nothing bot a poor man's Hoyt Williams. It's been done, over and over again. They're nothing new. They're not what is needed to carry a federation's division on, let alone an interfed's. Didn't PTC already go through this schtick with Williams, who is the man who lost to MacGregor in the first place? And then whom else do we have? Oh, look… It's Team Baked. Well we have only one thing to say about them: Been there, done that. These guys couldn't hack it in GCW, what makes them think they can hack it in PRIME? Well they do enough of the Seattle Home Grown to be delusional. Again though, been there, done that and got the win over them. We know what they're about… utter laziness. When the shit got to hard to handle in GCW they bolted like children after a night at Neverland Ranch. They're worthless. It's got to be realized eventually. They smoke all the fed's earnings going to them anyway. But that leads us to something else here. People say that we're not right for it either. But we have to question that. We are GCW's Tag Team Champions. We are a team of brothers who fight in the ring. We don't hide who we are. Some question the validity of some of our claims… very similar to that which they do with Shadow and Virgil Ambrosi, two of our allies. Some question the actual fact of the existence of the Order of the Hammer, the organization which sponsors us and the rest of the KnightMare Brigade. But they'd not question the fact that a clown and a pirate can be aliens and form a tag team. Or that a couple of guys can claim to be the ministrations of some creator Diety on a war streak. Or that a couple of 420 followers from the Pacific Northwest can manage to even make it to the ring, let alone do anything worth a damn in it. Who are we? It would be time for PTC to find out. Together we go by the name Unite. We've been forced to convert it to English for all to see, based on the fact that my brother and I originally wanted it to use the language of our forefathers and family for it. It's one character in the Salish based language of our people. Our people, yes. We are of the Saquamish Tribal Nation in Washington a Loot-shoot-Salish tribe. The same one that gave everyone the greatest diplomat that the Native people's ever had.. Chief Noah "Seattle" Sealth. But unlike some, we don't use the fact that our skin is darker and our ancient people's are our link to the past. We are our own. My name is Joshua Bodaway Brand. I was born in Saquamish, Washington on the Kitsap Peninsula twenty two years ago, give a few weeks. My mother, Margarette, was a college student studying Native American Cultures. She met our father after his original wife passed on. I'm the younger of the two of us but, admittedly I'm also the most detached. Fortunately for me I have a guiding light in my life. My girlfriend, a fellow wrestler by the name of Danielle Northsky. She's currently the GCW Television Champion and has been in a few other federations. My brother, Grayson, or just Gray to the rest of us, is a few years older. We share a father, but his mother was the full blooded woman our father had wed first. A man born with a few genetic differences though. His key one is his hair color, a grayish color from birth. Basically how he got his name. He's a big man, the power of our team. Some say he's other things, but we won't bring that up here. It wouldn't make a difference anyway. It doesn't need to. What else are we? Well Gray is the guardian of a woman who is a goof friend of Lia "Shadow" Vlassy. A woman by the name of Alexis Day. When things happened that forced Alexis to need access some of the Order's resources, she came on board with us. Due to Gray's training as the equivalent of a bodyguard, he as assigned to her and is now her protector. He takes his job pretty seriously though. Within the Order, Gray holds the official rank of Master Sergeant, while I'm a Corporal… still. He's also the acting second to the leader of our KB Faction, under Blaise McClancy, our Captain. We are Unite. We are the tag team that's going to take PTC by storm. Not some pirate clowns, not some religious freaks, and assuredly not some baked pot heads. ~Seattle, Washington~ ~The Former 4 Winds Condominium Tower~ Gray and Alexis were actually staying in a different room then both Josh and Dani, and even Blaise and Ukyo. Things just worked better that way. Although unlike the others, in this condo, both bed rooms were occupied. Gray stood in the kitchen, cooking from the sounds of the grease in the pan. His chest was bare and he was wearing a pair of sweatpants that looked like they'd seen much better days. His just past shoulder length hair was pulled back to keep it out of his face while he was fixing up his traditional morning feast. The knock that came to the door wasn't unexpected. He knew his brother was stopping by this morning. KNOCK KNOCK! Alexis, Gray's charge, shot up from the couch where she had been looking at the morning paper. She was wearing what appeared to be a loose pair of shorts and a UNITE T-shirt that was about three times the size it should have been. The much smaller woman was quick to get to the door, and open it up. "Come on in. Gray's just fixing up some food. I don't know you guys are included on that though, you know he eats like some animal or something." She looked over at her guardian and gave a half smirk, half smile. Like the three that then joined them in the room. Recently she'd found out things about him that only his brother and friends knew. But she was adapting. "We're good. My mom cooked for us this morning." Josh's answer was what Gray had expected. The three people who walked in though? Joshua, Dani and Josh's mother, Margarette. Gray was a bit surprised by this one. She still lived on Bainbridge Island, just a few miles from the reservation that the brothers Brand had been born on. Since GCW had come to the Pacific Northwest, Josh hadn't missed a beat with spending some time with his mother though, since it had been over a year since they had been back here. "Hey, Little Wolf… you ready for the news?" Josh looked over at his brother. He could make out the three eggs going down on a plate in front of him, as well as the toast, waffles and bacon on the plate. A man his size, both physically and otherwise takes a lot of energy to keep going. Especially after his morning workouts. "What's that? You called me to come over here for a meeting. You tell me." Gray poured himself a large glass of some fruit juice and then moved the cup and large plate over to the kitchen table. Before he sat down though, he reached over onto the counter and grabbed a pile of papers. He walked over and dropped it into his brother's lap. "You and I, due to GCW having to rebuild under us for the Tag Team Division after our challenge this last week, is giving us some time off if we want it. We'll still be at the shows, but until there is a contender, we are not going to wrestle." Josh, being a natural Seattle born slacker, had no problem with that. "So where are we going then? Hawaii? Or Alaska?" Gray, the more serious of the two didn't laugh at his attempt at humor. "Well first off, we're heading out to this PTC event. PTC versus Archibald MacGregor." Josh was a bit stunned by that. "Why? We're not contenders for the Extreme Title." Gray shook his head. "No, but we are a tag team. Steven Caldera has chosen us to take on the contenders for the Tag Team Titles that have been vacant for some time. We're facing three other teams." Josh perked up some. "Who?" Gray grabbed his own copy if the papers from PTC that had been sent to them by GCW's front offices. "From AWC, we have the Furious Fists of God. A pair of brothers who are doing the twin thing. They're booked as far right wing Christians who try to convert people or take them out." That illicited a snort of distaste from Joshua. "Then you have the team that was the last champions, The Killer Pirates from Global." Josh's jaw dropped. "Yeah, those guys." "Why did they loose the belts last time?" "They quit. They left PTC and didn't get to keep them. So they were vacated." Josh showed a bit of disgust on his face. "So these are the guys who took out Almasy and his late wife?" Gray nodded as he took some attention to his rapidly cooling food. Josh continued. "So, in doing this we'd be paying back Lia's group in getting a bit of payback for them taking something from Seymour?" Gray swallowed a bite of food and then answered. "Yeah. Blaise already spoke to Lia about it. After the recent crap between them, it might be a good peace offering. Things aren't tight yet, but it could really help things out. Fixing up those loose ends that are still floating out there. Not to mention, after everyone gives people like Lia crap for how she looks and acts, we have to do our fair share to stop people like the Pirates from reclaiming those tag team belts." Josh nodded this time. Dani and Alex had started to talk over on the other side, adding Margarette in after a few more moments. Dani had apparently taken the papers from Josh's lap while he and his brother talked. "Who else is there?" Gray took a drink and then offered up his answer. "The bounty." "What?" "The Bounty. The turncoats. The ones who give Seattle a bad name." Josh's anger came up. "Baked… Bammy and Mooch?" "Yes, Team Baked." "They quit GCW though?" And Gray chuckled. "Yeah, they got rehired by someone already." "Who would be so… stu…" "PRIME." That had Josh seething. He wasn't the biggest Team Baked fan, not in the slightest. "Well PRIME just went as far down hill as Global did hiring the Pirates. Who the hell can take Team Baked seriously? Especially when they dove as soon as the going got tough and they had to actually learn to wrestle to mean anything." Gray chuckled again. "Even rodeos need clowns, Josh. Even rodeos need clowns." That got a chuckle out of Josh. "So, what's the plan then? The Grayson Brand training regime? Am I a suplex dummy for the next week or so?" Gray looked over at the huddled women. "I think they've already found that out." Josh turned his attention to the ladies, and tried to reach in and grab the envelopes and other loose paper from Dani's lap. She slapped his hand and pulled back what looked like a glossy flier. "Hey, that was given to me." Dani stuck her tongue out at him and held the paperwork tightly. "Well the training will happen, don't worry about that." Gray was speaking to him again. "However, according to Blaise: After the last few weeks, we all need a bit of a break." Josh perked up again. "So is it Hawaii or Alaska?" "It's a bit closer then that, but it'll be just as fine. A few hours from here, on the Olympic Penisula is a place called the Sol Duc Hot Springs. A resort there and everything in the middle of a temperate rain forest. He knows how much we all like camping, especially Dani. So we're heading there for a little rest and relaxation." Dani and Alexis both looked up at the same time and asked the same question. Dani herself was in need of a good relaxing trip for a week after defending her GCW TV Title ever week for almost two months, even at house shows. "As soon as your stuff is in my new truck." His new truck… recently his other new truck had been totaled by a wreck caused by people who wanted Alexis. So he was going to break in this new one with a trip. "We've got three days. I'd advise you to go get ready. Cause after that, we're off to Portland for GCW's next show and then gone to PTC's Event. It'll take a few hours to get there. So, let's get packed and get going." Josh had one last question. "So how many are going?" He glanced at his mother. He knew that Dani and Alexis would have been included in Blaise's plans. "Well the reservations were made for five. It's two cabins. Margarette will be staying with Alexis and I. While you and Dani get some time to yourselves." Josh looked at his mother and then back to his brother. Gray had always been standoffish with Margarette. She had replaced his mother's place after her death. She was only a bit more then a decade older then him. She'd been twenty one when she married their father, Gray was eight when Josh had been born. "You sure?" Gray nodded. "Yes, I am. You and Dani need some time to yourselves. Even if you are going hiking with us during the day. Or doing some salmon fishing with me." Margarette stood up and spoke in protest. "No, I couldn't interrupt you two either. I know Josh and Dani are close. But you two are such a cute couple as it is. I couldn't interfere either way. I could always pay for my own cabin is the…" She stopped as she saw the look on both Gray's and Alexis' faces… she had put two and two together and got five. Josh spoke up before Gray could. "Uhm, mom. They're not a couple." She was visibly shaken by what she'd said. "I'm sorry. I didn't know." Gray then spoke up. Margarette had been a member of the Order for many years. They'd paid for her schooling. She had been the one that had directed Gray to the Order when the changes that made him different then everyone else started to take place… "I'm sorry, but I thought you knew. Alexis is a friend. And currently she's assigned to me as a ward under my guardianship." She know that that meant. It meant she was important to their sponsors and they had decided that she needed a bodyguard. And by picking Gray, a man of unique talents in the Order, it was a pretty important mission for him. However, Margarette was much like her son, Joshua. She could feel things that others couldn't. And her senses had her saying that there was something there. She could only be embarrassed. Gray broke in. "I think it's time we got ready to go. I've already packed what I'm taking. I did it last night. If we can get done in an hour or so, we can head out and get there by noon by catching the next ferry over to Bainbridge." Dani and Alexis are quick to get up and head towards their rooms to get their things together. Josh and his mother nod to Gray to take his leave and walk out slowly. Margarette held her sons arm. As soon as they were clear of the others, she spoke up to him. "I have to apologize for back there. I think I've been reading things that aren't there based on their reaction." Josh smiled at the still youthful looking woman who had given birth to him. "No, mom. You didn't. They're just not admitting it yet. I've been sensing it from her for some time now, and he's recently starting to warm up to her. You just caught them while they're in the denial stage." The woman laughed, almost a chiming giggle. "Speaking of which, I don't see a ring on Danielle's finger yet. I gave you the engagement ring that had been given to me by your late father for a reason, Joshua." Josh looked down at the ground as he approached the door. "It's not been the right time. With everything that's been going on, I've not wanted to toss something else to worry about on her. Bad enough how bad I've really messed up recently." Margarette just gave him a knowing look. "Don't let her slip away from you Josh. I've been to Sol Duc Springs before. Kinda romantic. In fact, I looked at the itinerary. Blaise booked you two the wedding suite." Josh shook his head. "When the time is right mom, when the time is right. You gong to need any help hauling your stuff out to the truck?" She nodded to him, and gave him a coy smile. "Yes, but only after you help that woman of yours. Only after you help Dani. I'm second in your life now, remember that." She headed off to the condo she's been borrowing, with permission from the owner, Blaise McClancy, for while they were in the Puget Sound area, leaving Josh standing at the door. ~To Be Continued~
