A Hype of Prophetic Proportions!

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 The scene starts off inside a production studio where we see one lonely camera filming a man with a dark obsession, an evil passion, and an unholy desire……. "Pain and agony, pain and agony were all I felt for that whole year….a year of solitude and defeat. Last GTT Tournament I achieved what a lot said was unachievable by beating Tessa Windsor in Round 1 and know one year later I am back in the PTC circuit facing…a Chimp! What insanity caused this to degrade me to fighting animals? How pathetic…but if it’s a monkey I must fight to reach my ultimate goal of dominance then a monkey I shall fight! Too many times has people been looking the other way with me, too many times has people took me for granted, and too many times people has laughed at The Prophecy that I preach day in and day out…but no more, the time has come for me to instead of ask for the respect but to rip it from the very depths of your soul and claim it as my own! In GTT it’s not about friends or who you are representing in the tournament, it’s about one thing and one thing alone…yourself! I will have no allies for in GTT5 I will go through each and every one of them to live out my destiny of being the GTT5 Champion! Some may not remember the chaos that I bring, some may not remember the hell that follows me on a daily basis, and some may not remember the Prophetic Destiny that is mine for the taking…but they will as I go into round one facing UWF’s local Chimp and straight beat him for the 1…2…3! From the days of EPW to LOW was the first part of my transition and now that I am with Core it’s a whole new Prophecy with GTT5!" The scene fades to black as we see John "The Prophet" Adams give off a wicked smile.
