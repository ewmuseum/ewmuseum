The Truth "They" Don't Want You To Know (2005 WOTY Nomination Hype)

26 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Math is fun, especially when it’s shallowly and senselessly exploited to pretend to make a point that would resound more with simple words. Witness: The average man is 170 pounds. Angelo Deville is 255 pounds. Therefore, Angelo Deville is a man and a half. Or, in mathematical terms, since one man = 1, Angelo Deville = 1.5. Now expounding on earlier proofs by esteemed nominees, we get: 1.5 > 1 (Angelo Deville) > 1 (Angelo Deville) > (Karina Wolfenden) In the Dual Halo clusterfuck at Culture Shock (PRIME’s Royal Rumble, for you WWE idgits), after Angelo Deville got jumped by two Mexican jumping beans, a circus clown, and three – THREE! – banana peels, Karina Wolfenden managed to blindside him with her Whirlwind Romance and pin him for three seconds. Months later, Karina Wolfenden tried to rain on Deville’s parade of defeating Xavier Kannon by calling him out for a match at Colossus (PRIME’s Wrestlemania). Without interference, without the absurdity of 30 people molesting the ring, Angelo Deville hit Karina Wolfenden with her Whirlwind Romance and pinned her for three seconds. Observers of the spectacle insisted afterward that he very easily could have bitten Bundy’s style and held her for five. 5 > 3 (Angelo Deville) > (Karina Wolfenden) Karina Wolfenden used to be a man. Think about it. Who would know – you or I? It’s the reason her and Angelo Deville broke up. She also used to be Mexican, which explains her angst and hatred of The Man. It also explains her victory of the Global Title, since Emilio Rage is a known Mexican sympathizer. Last but not least, it all comes together to explain not only her forfeiture of the Global Title, but her dropping of the PRIME Universal Title before ever defending it; she knew thrusting herself into the spotlight would open her to this sort of scrutiny, drumming up the music to make the skeletons dance from her closet. Proud Dick > Bobbited Dick (Angelo Deville) > (Karina Wolfenden) Not Mexican > Mexican (Angelo Deville) > (Karina Wolfenden) Brandon Youngblood never wrestled a single match in the NCAA. He made it all up, even pretending he won the championship proudly earned by Tim Hartung. His relationship with Chris Benoit, however, is completely true; they met when Youngblood broke into the wrestling world for real, in his most convincing and genuine role to date, as The Gobbledy Gooker. Baltasar got rid of Angelus to pursue singles wrestling because their team, Los Diablos, was affectionately renamed Los Di-Job-Loads while feuding with Angelo Deville. They lost to him, like, a thousand times straight. The Deville, the pinnacle of not just evolution, but benevolence, finally had mercy on Los Di-Job-Loads and faced them in a handicap match for the straps, graciously letting them win. By their own baffling admission, that charade was the biggest moment of their lives, and without it, they never, ever, ever, ever, ever, ever, ever would have won the Tag Team Titles. Even with the help of international superstar Jeff Garvin, Brandon Youngblood jobbed to Los Di-Job-Loads. Yeah. It was hilarious. Cereal Killer disappeared from the world’s admiring eye after he lost a “change your name, bizzle!” dark match to a bowl of Cheerios. Rich Rollins exists only as a CGI effect. The man really died about a month ago in a car crash, but PTC, refusing to abide another sad-sack Global Title reign, stifled the news and recreated him digitally. In an act of unquestioned mercy, Angelo Deville offered to lay the ghost to rest and finally give PTC a Global Champion it could be proud of, but someone forgot to tell the special effects guy. The cheering live crowd barely noticed that Angelo was wrestling by himself, against himself, such was their awe. The spectacle has since been nominated for Match of the Year (and you, Karina?), a true testament to The Deville’s in-ring godliness.
