Just Another Face.......

30 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A voice speaks quietly... softly... but with intent... I can understand perfectly well that there are not many among the ranks here that know me at all. The ones that do, maybe only heard the name once or twice in the past, and as they see the name announced again, as a challenger entered into the PTC Extreme title rumble, deja vu creeps into their minds... an apparition of a dark figure pops into their heads, but nothing exact at all.... ‘I could have sworn that I heard that name before.’ ‘He’s from XUW? So?’ ‘Was that the guy in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup... you know, that guy?’ I can understand perfectly well what many of you are thinking. I can see already that I am but an overlooked soul, simply a name in a match full of names, names more recognized than my own... names more famous in the PTC than my own. Perhaps in time, that normal, understandable sentiment, will change. Perhaps as my name is seen on more and more cards, or after more and more unknowing, and therefore ignorant fools, step into my path and become educated on just exactly who I am.... maybe my name will strike more than simply deja vu into your minds, and your hearts. I am here now though... and for the time being, I shall accept my role... for I am just another face in the crowd. ..................... The voiceover fades out, and the darkness that filled the screen transcends into a flashing spectacle of lights and excitement. Welcome to Club Eden, where all of your fantasies come true, where the party has been heated and going strong for an undetermined amount of time. The scene is that of dry ice flowing onto the most spectacular and vibrant of dance floors, which by the way is packed to the brim, full of half naked men and women, dancing and grinding to their heart’s content. Strobe lights flash around so much, and the lights are such an enormous part of the atmosphere, that on the door there is a sign preventing people with epilepsy from even entering. There is a large line at the bar, and sitting at the bar, attempting to hit on the scantily clad barmaid, are the usual suspects in regard to club-hoppers, guys who go from club to club almost too often, guys who try just a little too hard when flirting, hinting at a bit of desperation. You know the type. Security is very much a concern, and a large presence at a place like this, as the atmosphere here spells out ‘sex’ and ‘drugs’, and the potential for violence looms in the air. Almost unseen, and nearly missed entirely due to the sheer size and massive layout of this place, a sole figure walks in through the crowded entrance. He can barely be seen, but there is no question that he does not belong here. He is not dressed as a club-goer. He is not wearing black dress shoes, and a presentable shirt and slacks. No, he wears steel-toed boots, blue jeans that have been worn down, consisting of brutal holes, tears, and stains. He also wears a leather jacket that has not been cleaned or repaired in quite some time. His hair is long, thin, and wet, covering his face and falling down slightly past his shoulders. These qualities alone single him out in this crowd of the beautiful people, but the one thing that does so even more... is the solid black mask that covers his face. He is The Shadow. He is an unknown in this club, and receives strange looks from those he passes by, as he disappears into the crowd. The party continues, since nothing has changed in the grand scope of things. A new person has entered... no big deal. The men and women on the dance floor, practically having sex through their clothes, do not seem to take notice at all. Only those in immediate contact do. They look at him oddly. But he has disappeared. The music changes, and the crowd cheers as though they are one entity, for whatever beat now blares through the speakers, is a favorite, and people rush to the dance floor to get involved. Tables are then revealed, as everybody flocks to the dance area. The tables, for the most part, are empty, except for the occasional couple exchanging saliva in a more private forum... or a group talking, drinking, and enjoying each other’s company. But there, in the very far corner of this oversized room, a dark corner where not too many people dwell, sits the masked man. He rests against the wall, on a sofa of a seat, leaning back with his leg up on the sofa as well.... surveying all around him. You can just feel the lust in a place like this. That aura is about us all... everybody enjoying themselves, letting go of all inhibitions in a public community... immersing themselves into the culture that surrounds them, and letting their minds flow free. Through the smoke and strobe lights, it is nigh on impossible to even see the masked man known simply as The Shadow... as he is shrouded in more darkness due to his location, and lack of lighting. One would have to look very hard to spot him there, lurking, observing... maybe hiding? Directly in front of this masked man, are two women. They are a perfect example of the attitude in this club, in this environment, as they have completely let loose. They are grinding with each other, feeling each other’s bodies in all of the usual and unusual places, and staring into each other’s eyes longingly, not knowing that a masked man is but merely a few feet away, watching them, stalking them with his nearly hidden eyes. Others are looking at them as well, the guys sitting at the bar, and that does not faze them... other dancers who think they are going just a little bit too far, but that doesn’t faze them. They are used to those leering eyes, but not from the newcomer... I wonder what those two are thinking now? I wonder if they have plans for later tonight, if perhaps they wish for somebody else to join their potential orgy... who knows? I, of course, have no idea, for I am new here, I am not involved, nor considered... The two women take steps closer to where this masked man sits, and they notice him. They appear shocked, as they did not see him until they were upon him, and after staring at him and whispering to each other, they turn and leave, to put on their show elsewhere, for other prying eyes. The masked man sighs. In this place... apparently what they were doing is considered normal. It is accepted. It is understood... it is welcomed. am I? Am I... welcome here? Is what I plan on doing... going to be accepted? He sits up a bit, and looks around the extravagant club, nodding as he takes notice of a bit of a mosh pit forming amongst the more angry of men there. There is, of course, the normal amount of screaming, the normal amount of shoving and near fighting that one might expect here, and the masked man observes it all... and a slight grin creeps across his face... the part that can be seen that is. I wonder if this place is ready to accept somebody like me. It has accepted so many, from various diverse places, who live different lifestyles outside of here, but would they accept me? Would those two gorgeous women invite me into their orgy? Would the moshers continue to mosh if I joined the fray, and released some anger? Would the men at the bar allow me to sit there as well? Perhaps... perhaps, I would fit in here nicely one day. Who knows, maybe there is something I could add to this place, for better or for worse... The Shadow’s voice trails off as he sees a woman approach him, pad and pen in her hand. At first, she also was not aware of him, and is slightly startled as she sees him staring at her. She is obviously a waitress of some sort here, as she is wearing the same black, skimpy uniform that others have passed by wearing. Woman: Can I... get you something? The Shadow sits there for a moment, looking her up and down as she appears to be very, very uncomfortable at the fact that a leering masked man sitting in the darkest of corners is undressing her with his eyes.. The Shadow: Two shots of vodka. The lady nods, and promptly departs, once again leaving The Shadow alone, alone in a place where several people inhabit. He sighs again, and looks down to the floor... The dancing continues in the club. The grinding, and the kissing, the lights and the smoke... everything continues, as The Shadow sits alone in a corner of the club, with his head down, until the waitress approaches once again. He lifts up his head to observe her once again, and she seems uncomfortable as she sets the drinks down and promptly leaves. But this time, there is another, a large man, with a portable radio in one hand, glaring down at the masked man. He merely glares back... Security: May we help you with something tonight sir? The Shadow scoffs... The Shadow: You may... by leaving my presence and letting me be. Security: Are you sure I cannot help you with something? Why are you here? Why are you sitting there, where nobody can see you. Surely you have to understand that you look very suspicious right now. The Shadow nods in agreement... The Shadow: Perhaps I do seem suspicious, of course. I am an unknown factor here, aren’t I? Security: Well... yeah. .. The Shadow: I understand you concern, but you have more important matters to tend to, don’t you? You must surely have your typical troublemakers here, the usual suspects as far as serious threats and matters of importance go. Perhaps I seem suspicious, but I am merely new here, and have yet to fit in. I am merely sitting here, away from the masses, to watch, to watch and observe. Is that acceptable to you? Security: What’s with the mask? This is not a costume party, and it’s bothering other people here. The Shadow, ignoring the question completely: I have yet to impose myself on anybody. I have merely sat in this very spot, and addressed those who have addressed me. Now unless you have plans on attempting to throw me out of here for no reason, you should turn around, and tend to more important affairs... The security guard mumbles something under his breath and walks away... The Shadow: Because as of this moment, you are naive to just how important, or how much of a threat, I may truly be... but do not fret, for you are not the only one... The dancing continues... the liquor pours freely. Tempers have begun to flare in parts of the club, where people have started to disagree with others over some inane matter. It is a standard thing, as most nights at a club, there is at least one fight. Security is all over the matter though, and The Shadow drinks one of the two shots of Vodka in front of him... Of course, it’s understandable that some people would find me to be suspicious, and ask questions... because apparently I do not fit into the mold here. Maybe they don’t know how it feels to be the outcast... the one who everybody looks at oddly... the one whom people fear to speak with. Surely they must understand that as the ‘new guy’ here, it is an uncomfortable situation for me. Surely, they must see the insecurity that I have, hoping to one day fit in, but as each second passes by, beginning to doubt more and more. Maybe because I have nothing in common with anybody in here... but at the same time, I have something in common with everybody in here.... And it’s finally happened. A fight has broken out in the middle of the dance floor, and it has caught most everybody’s attention. Some are running away from the scene... others are running towards it, curious... and others are still kissing and groping each other, not caring one bit. The Shadow leans forward and carefully observes the situation. He notices that the security guard who has been watching him, has gone to try and break up the fight, leaving the masked man unsupervised... What do I have in common? I have passion... I have lust... I crave for my mind to be free of all inhibitions, just like they do. I want to let everything go, and be just like these people, but not like this. Those who know me... those who have come into contact with me, they know exactly what I am talking about. They understand my desire and lust... and who knows. Perhaps one day, this place will as well. Maybe something new can be brought here, well... maybe not new, exactly... but a new way of thinking, a new way of living life. When I became entered in the Chris Williams Memorial Tournament late last year, I felt the same that I feel here, in this place. I was an outcast.... I was there for reasons different than all others. I was not accepted there... sadly, and in the end, it caused my elimination from the tournament. Why was I eliminated? It was for a reason that nobody else was eliminated... disqualification. It was because one of the final three involved, was decimated by a steel chair that I wielded. Who knows... perhaps if I obeyed myself... perhaps if I had forced myself to adapt... conform to the attitude of the CWMC.... the attitude that most others had, maybe I would have been involved at the very end... but alas, I did not fit in there. They would not accept me for who I am... Perhaps this place will accept me for who I am, and for what I desire... The Shadow slowly rises to his feet and downs the second shot of Vodka, before making his way out from behind the table. The music, the lights, everything seems to pick up a notch now, as this masked man begins to walk toward the crowd... as an outsider who wishes to join in on the action... I hope these people accept the same type of lust that I accept. I hope that they appreciate the violence... the drama.... the excitement of it all... The Shadow reaches into his jacket... I hope I am not an outcast here, as I am in most places, as I was in the CWMC. I hope these people can understand what it is that I crave, what makes me happy inside, what motivates me to do what I do... Ever so slowly, an object becomes revealed as The Shadow removes his hand from within his jacket... There has to be some place in the world that would acceot me. There would have to be some people out there that respect just what it is I do... and would not consider me an outcast, but would welcome me with open arms. There has to be someplace out there, where the patrons would not bestow upon me foul looks of hate and dismay. I know that place is out there somewhere... I know that there is a forum for me, just as there is for these people.... I just haven’t discovered it yet. but I must force myself to be optimistic... I cannot dwell in my own misfortunes here... I must strive forward, and find that special place... Where Ultra Violence can be accepted, where Ultra Violence can be unleashed freely, with no consequence, with no holding back... The item is revealed... a lead pipe. It is old by appearance, and has been a part of many battles in the past, with blood of various textures and colors staining it completely. The pipe is nearly red and black... instead of the normal color. Nobody has noticed this masked psycho yet though. Nobody has spotted him making his way toward the unsuspecting men and women of Club Eden... Perhaps not here, but in an extreme environment, I will have truly found a place that will accept me. Perhaps in an environment where anything goes.... ANYTHING... there I just may be looked on as a normal standard, and not observed oddly, and given horrible looks as I pass people by. For we will all share something special... we will all become one in an environment of pure Ultra Violence, where our minds will flow freely, where our desires and cravings for blood and violent acts can be satisfied... where we can let go of all inhibitions in a public community, as screaming, bloodthirsty fans cheer us all on. He taps the lead pipe, his personal instrument of violence, against his hand. He is only a few feet away... Who knows? Maybe I will be accepted in a place like that.... but as I properly introduce myself, just as I am about to do here.... I will fill the role that is expected of me, I will simply be another face in the crowd... another face... a face to not leave unsupervised... He lifts his weapon high into the air, and picks up pace... ...A face that shall soon become anything but a stranger... anything but an outcast, soon...... soon. ... The music cuts off all of a sudden. This lights cut out. There is no sound... there is nothing, except for an eery laughter, a laughter that suggests sadistic intent... I hope I fit in properly here.... I hope that I am accepted...
