Cloister of Trials II

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-16 “Can’t I go fight the Omega Weapon instead?” It was a very reluctant Seymour who stood with his wife on the fifth floor of a New York City apartment building. On the other side of the door to Apartment 5F live Aerith Heartilly’s parents. Neither has been especially supportive of the marriage between Seymour and Aerith, but with Seymour’s retirement impending, the Final Fantasy understood the need to play nice. Still, with Pietro Geist looming on the horizon, Almasy’s mind was anywhere but entirely on Mr. and Mrs. Heartilly. Aerith Heartilly: No, you may not. Seymour frowned at the denial of his request. While the Omega Weapon was certainly a ferocious creature, Seymour would rather face it and the horrors of its Level 5 Death attack than speak with Aerith’s parents. Seymour Almasy: Awww, come on… Aerith Heartilly: You know we have to do this, Seymour. Seymour Almasy: I know, I know Aerith. Just give me a little time. The Final Fantasy paced back and forth in front of the apartment door, like a caged tiger. He let out an exasperated sigh, as two words escaped his lips, the name of the man who wished to be his undoing, the man to end the dream. Seymour Almasy: Pietro….Geist. Aerith Heartilly: You know, Seymour, I’ve been thinking…isn’t it kind of silly to think that Pietro Geist is possessed by a demon? Seymour Almasy: Not at all, Aerith! Most of the religious officials I have encountered have been corrupted. All of the officials of Yevon were unsent, men who should have died and gone to the Farplane, yet stayed in order to preach false teachings to the people of Spira. Pope Zera was the embodiment of Valmar, the evil god that attempted to destroy the world of Grandia! These men were possessed by forces, so why should Pietro Geist not be? Aerith Heartilly: Maybe it’s just a coincidence? Seymour Almasy: Besides, what priest do YOU know that uses the style of puroresu! Most priests are non-violent, caring souls! When was the last time you saw a priest run down from his pulpit and hit some guy saying “Amen!” with a Shining Wizard? Aerith sighed, but she had to admit Seymour was right. Geist had learned how to wrestle, but whether it was simple training or demonic influence she couldn’t say. Seymour Almasy: In the end, it really does not matter if he is a demon or not. Either way, he shall attempt to scare me with his rituals and shows of power. His flair for the dramatic shall not affect me, however. He may be knowledgeable of the divine word, but inside the Battle Arena, his skills pale in comparison to mine. Of this I am quite confident. Aerith Heartilly: I’m glad. After all, believing in yourself is half the battle. It was something Seymour knew to be true. From the beginning of his career, he had often been the only person to believe he could be victorious. At times, even Aerith wondered if Almasy could pull out a victory. But now, with everything on the line, Seymour knew that he could defeat Geist. He knew that he could be victorious, no matter what the corrupted priest came up with to use against him. Seymour Almasy: Yes, Aerith. I must believe in myself. Not simply in the Battle Arena, but in life in general. It is ridiculous to be afraid of your parents. I have faced those who have attempted to destroy me physically, attempted to render me paralyzed or dead. And yet, every time I triumph. From what you have told me, there is little chance of your parents challenging me in the Battle Arena, thus, I shall use my mental abilities to render their concerns moot. His eyes rose to meet Aerith’s, eyes full of conviction and determination. Aerith smiled back at him, returning his deep gaze with her own. Aerith Heartilly: I’m proud of you, sweetie. I’m so sorry to put you through this, but I want my parents to know you. I want them to grow to love you like I do. I want them to see what a truly good person you are. Seymour nodded, and together, husband and wife knocked on the door. Several seconds later, the door opened, and Seymour saw his mother and father in law for the very first time. Both were shorter than he had figured, considering Aerith was 5’9”, but Seymour still found himself looking up at Aerith’s father. Both mother and father smiled, but even Aerith could tell it was merely out of courtesy to her. Aerith Heartilly: Mom, Dad…this is Seymour. Seymour, these are my parents, Rachel and John. Almasy extended his hand to shake with both. As he did so, he noticed surprised looks on the faces of each. He realized rather quickly that they probably weren’t used to seeing a man clad in armor, a long sword attached to his side, and with long, white hair on their doorstep. They stepped back, allowing Seymour entrance to the room. Rachel: Um, Seymour…can I take your…sword? It was an awkward moment, but Seymour removed his sheathed sword from his side, and handed it carefully to Aerith’s mother, who placed it in the closet next to the family’s coats. John: Please, sit down. The comment was short, to the point, and bordered on being rude. It was not the type of thing Seymour had the courage to ignore. He moved calmly over to the living room area, and took a seat on the smaller of two couches. Seymour Almasy: Soooo, umm, it’s nice to meet you both. John and Rachel walked over to the couch opposite Almasy, and sat down. They looked across at the man who was married to their daughter. They tried to be pleasant, but their looks had a touch of steel. Aerith, sensing this, immediately went over to Seymour’s couch, and sat next to him. Both Seymour and Aerith noted the slightly pained expression on their faces as Aerith took the Final Fantasy’s hand in hers. Rachel: Well-- Seymour had seen quite enough. He had been disliked by these people, despite them never having met him. Now that he was here, they seemed perfectly nice, but even the naïve Almasy could tell it was an act. It was time to get to the heart of the matter. Seymour Almasy: Let’s cut to the chase, shall we? From what Aerith’s told me, and what I can see in your faces right now, neither of you especially seem to like me. I would just like to know what I’ve done to upset both of you so. The comment surprised both of Aerith’s parents. They hadn’t expected Almasy to put it so bluntly. It sent them both into damage control mode. Rachel: Seymour, we don’t dislike you-- This time, it was Aerith who cut Rachel off. Aerith Heartilly: Don’t lie to him! Mom, Dad, you told me that if I stayed married to him you would DISOWN me! Why would you say that if you didn’t dislike him? If Aerith’s parents had been surprised before, they were positively shocked now. Perhaps the only person in the room more shocked was Seymour. Aerith instantly realized that she hadn’t informed Seymour of this development before, and clung even tighter to him. Seymour’s shock slowly gave way to anger. Seymour Almasy: Why…why would you do such a thing? Aerith Heartilly: Seymour, it’s okay, they-- The look on Almasy’s face indicated quite clearly that it was NOT okay. He rose to his feet, still wrapped in Aerith’s arms. Anger now gave way to sorrow, a look of sadness that made even John and Rachel feel guilty. Seymour Almasy: I don’t understand. I really, really don’t. I have done nothing. To either of you! I love your daughter. Is that a crime? I love her more than anything in this world. She means more to me than you could possibly imagine. She has helped me since day one. You know that she loves me as much as I love her. Aerith nods, as her mother and father shrink back. Seymour Almasy: You’re her parents. You should support her. You may not like me…I don’t care if you despise me. What you are doing to Aerith is disgusting. She’s twenty-one years old, for crying out loud! She can live her own life! You have no right to make her choose between myself and you two. It’s not fair at all. Almasy brings his wife closer, and she eagerly wraps her arms around Seymour tightly, pulling him into her grasp. Aerith Heartilly: He’s right, you know. I love you both. But I also love my husband. There’s no reason for you to dislike him. But you do. And we want to know why. Rachel is too thunderstruck to speak, but John is somewhat calmer. With a sobered expression on his face, he answers his daughter. John: It’s not him, per se. We don’t know him very well. It’s how it all happened. You quit college to be with him. You travel across the country with him as he goes to all of his silly fights. It’s no way for you to live. He doesn’t know it, but the words “silly fights” were the worst he could have spoken. He was about to get quite the earful from Seymour. Seymour Almasy: My fights are NOT silly! I fight to make the world safe for goodness! This week, I battle Pietro Geist, a corrupted priest! A messenger of evil! I am the only one who can stop him! Aerith’s father prepares to fire back, likely with a comment that would destroy Seymour’s world, but Aerith speaks before the unintentionally vitriolic words can come out. Aerith Heartilly: Is this about his job? What’s wrong with his career? He earns good money, and he’s fully capable of supporting me. Besides, he’s going to be retiring from the Arena soon, and I’m going to go back and finish college. So, really, you have no reason to be upset. John and Rachel sighed. This was difficult, much more so than they had expected. They knew that Aerith loved Seymour, loved this misguided professional wrestler. Now, however, they saw just how much. It was a difficult thing to watch. Something had to give, and it turned out to be Rachel. Rachel: You really do love him…although, I should have known that when you left college to be with him. John: But Rachel, do we really want someone like HIM with our daughter? The comment was another slap to the Final Fantasy’s face, but to his shock, his mother-in-law came to his defense. Rachel: John, we don’t even know him! He’s right. He’s done nothing to make us dislike him. We owe him a chance…if nothing else, for our daughter’s sake. She stood, walking over to her son-in-law, apologetically placing a hand on his shoulder. Rachel: Please, forgive us. Give us another chance. Seymour nodded. There was no point in harboring a grudge. To do so would be to become like they had been. Seymour Almasy: I will. Thank you. But, seriously…it has to be more than just my profession. Why else does your husband dislike me? A loud crash could be suddenly heard, as a young boy bounds down a hallway into the living room. He turned to face Seymour, and his eyes grew as wide as saucers, before running over and clinging to Almasy’s waist. Seymour Almasy: I…I, who’s this, Aerith? Aerith Heartilly: That’s my little brother, Jacob! He’s four years old, and so nice… but I thought he was supposed to be sleeping over at his friend’s house while we had this talk, Mom… Rachel: I’m sorry, Aerith, his friend was busy. So we set him up in his room with some movies to watch. Almasy managed to pry Jacob off of him, so he could look down at Aerith’s little brother. Jacob: I can’t believe you’re here! You’re my favorite! Aerith’s father sighed deeply, while her mother simply smiled. Rachel: That’s the other reason John gets so resentful sometimes…his son thinks you’re the best. Jacob jumped playfully into Seymour’s arms. He is caught by Almasy, who began to toss him in the air and catch him on the way down. Aerith turned to her husband, a somewhat urgent look on her face. Aerith Heartilly: Seymour, would you mind going off with Jacob for a bit? I need to talk to my parents alone for a bit. Seymour Almasy: Sure, Aerith. The Final Fantasy put Jacob back down, and was immediately grabbed by Aerith‘s brother, who tried to drag Seymour back down the hall. Jacob: C’mon Seymour! I want to show you my room! A smiling Seymour gave in, letting Jacob take him to his room. John and Rachel nodded to Aerith. Seeing Seymour safely in Jacob’s room, she could now speak freely. Aerith Heartilly: I know he’s detached from reality…but please don’t dislike him because of it. It’s not his fault. He had a really bad childhood. His parents worked, and were away so much that his videogames became his parents. His father died in a car accident, and his mother sent him away to live with his grandfather because he embarrassed her. Please…don’t be hard on him. Her mother screamed suddenly, recognition having dawned on her. She remembered her daughter’s childhood, remembered her daughter’s best childhood friend. Rachel: You mean…is Seymour Jason? Aerith Heartilly: Yes, mom…he is. Mother and father look at one another in a state of shock. Aerith had told them precious little about her husband. They flushed bright red with embarrassment, embarrassment at treating someone they had once welcomed into their home so poorly. Rachel: Why didn’t you tell us, Aerith? Aerith Heartilly: I couldn’t bear to…after all the things you said about him without even knowing… Tears rolled down her cheeks, as her father rose to embrace her, doing his best to comfort his daughter. John: It’s okay, Laura. Aerith’s breath drew in at the mention of her real name. It had been a long time since she had been called by her real name. She was with Seymour so much that, at times, she almost forgot that her name was Laura Winters. Laura Wilson now, actually…as she had taken Seymour’s real last name when they married. Her tears slowly began to stop, as her mother now smiled. Rachel: That poor dear…he doesn’t remember who he really is? Aerith Heartilly: I…I don’t know. It’s difficult to tell. Sometimes I think he does, other times I think he’s repressed it too far to know. John: Laura, I know it’s a lot to ask, but I think you should tell him. She nodded. It was something she had wanted to tell him ever since she met him again. She had been married by Seymour Almasy, as Aerith Heartilly: She wanted to be married to Jason Wilson, as Laura Winters. With any luck, it could happen soon, now that Seymour was almost done… Aerith Heartilly: I know. I will. But not quite yet. I want to let him enjoy the end of his career for as long as possible. The heartfelt conversation is broken up by a loud scream from the direction of Jacob’s room. Aerith Heartilly: I’ll go check on them… She chuckles as she walks down the hallway, seeing her little brother wearing the foam replica armor Global Wrestling put out as merchandise for Seymour. He was beating up a pillow, and commentating the action as he did so, with Seymour looking on. Jacob: And Seymour picks up Pietro Geist, and nails him with a clothesline! The four year old tore into the pillow with a clothesline, sending it down hard to the bed. Jacob paused to soak in the cheers of the imaginary crowd, then picked up the pillow, placing it between his legs. Jacob: He’s going for the Time Compression! The boy pretended to cross the nonexistent arms of the pillow, lifted it vertical, and then sent it crashing to the makeshift canvas with one of Seymour’s signature maneuvers. He covered the pillow, as Seymour slid onto the bed, making the count. Seymour Almasy: One…two…three! Jacob hopped off of the pillow, arms raised in victory. Aerith Heartilly: Awww, how cute!! She embraced her husband, as Jacob wrapped his arms around his brother-in-law’s waist. Seymour couldn’t help but blush, as he smiled broadly. Seymour Almasy: This is why Pietro Geist cannot defeat me. For on my side are the powers of love and friendship. Emotions that are pure. Pietro Geist, you thrive on the perversions of these emotions, lust and favoritism. These are not emotions that I possess. My purposes are clean, my intentions pure as the winter’s snow. I am incorruptible. As such…I am your worst nightmare. Aerith Heartilly: Not even one potentially wielding the power of Hell itself can corrupt my Seymour. Besides, that’s MY job! Seymour Almasy: Geist’s fantasies of world domination and corruption shall end once we meet in the arena! And if he believes otherwise… Jacob: He’s living in a fantasy world! Seymour looked down, and chuckled at the delivering of his catchphrase by his newest fan. Jacob smiled back, and it is on this touching scene that we fade to black.
