III (Snow Day) - Ash “Snow falls. The sky is grey, and sullenly glares With purple lights in the canyoned street. The fiery sign on the dark tower wreathes and flares . . . The trodden grass in the park is covered with white, The streets grow silent beneath our feet . . . The city dreams, it forgets its past to-night.” -Conrad Aiken, The House of Dust: Part 02 III Snow Day Wednesday, 12/17/2008, night time It was cold tonight, as cold as the city’s dead heart that never beats. Never thought it would be like this. A snow storm in Las Vegas. Not unprecedented but nothing to this extent has been seen in years. On the far western side of town, over in the Elite Empire area, this happens almost every year to a smaller extent in the winter. This is different. It’s going to be more dangerous tonight that usual. The snow may hold the city in its icy grip but it still won’t stop the criminals from going about their business. I know this for a fact. The crime rate is rising faster the snow on the ground. I’ve tailed some ninjas across town for a full month trying to figure out their robbery pattern. They belong to a group called the Red Bamboo. That is the only information I know about them at this time. At this moment in time they’re right across the street from me robbing the Full Moon Art Gallery of paintings and sculptures. That really pisses me off because some of my artwork is in that gallery. Watching them pull the paintings and sculptures makes my blood boil. Personal relationships with the other artists only enrages me further. I watch them for a few more moments. They put the paintings in the back of big four wheel big rig moving truck they brought with them. After this all the ninjas who are outside go back inside the gallery, I see my chance. Running across the snow covered street I use the shadows to hide myself before sneaking inside. Once there the first thing I notice is the ninjas moving about, stealing the last bit of artwork. Clinching my fists, it’s time to take down this ninja scum once and for all. “I think it’s time you guys put down the artwork and be judged for your crimes.” The ninjas look over at me and I know what’s coming. These Red Bamboo bastards are going down faster than a Vegas hooker. Three of them immediately lunge at me, trying to use numbers as a weapon. This doesn’t work, of course. I dodge a kick from one of them before coming around and smacking him in the side of the face. The rest of them join in and before I know it they all start attacking at once. Fists, feet, and bodies are flying from all directions at this point. I start hitting at anything that moves until there is nothing left to hit. Looking around at the bodies lying on the ground ends my rage fueled violence session. After this I begin to look for clues as to why they are stealing and whom they are stealing for. After searching through all the bodies, only one clue is left behind. It’s a card with the words UD written in yellow ink on it in graffiti style writing. Simple to figure out. The Urban Decay was behind the hiring of these ninjas. After my investigation I tie them all up with the PlastiCuffs I brought with me before leaving the gallery. I go back outside only to find a police car with lights flashing waiting for me. A ninja suddenly leaps out from the building and jumps on my back. I toss him down and give him a karate chop to the neck. A flashlight is pointed in my direction from the police car. “Shit.” The officer gets out of his car and closes the door. I look up from the body of the ninja I’ve just beaten to see the officer walking in my direction with a flashlight in hand that he points in my direction. His breath rises from his mouth in the cold night air. “I should arrest you right now,” he says. This statement causes me to raise an eyebrow. “But why aren’t you handcuffing me already?” I ask him with a puzzled look hidden by my mask. The officer gives me a strange look right back. “You shouldn’t be here.” Standing up to get a better view, I recognize him immediately. “Nice to see you too, Kane,” I reply. My brother the cop. The only person who knows who Judge really is. He found out on accident a few months back when he caught me beating a mugger during his patrol. A bit of a shock when he found out. I need to spend more quality time with him to be honest about it. Too bad the bastard didn’t come over for Thanksgiving dinner. I could’ve gotten him back for that April Fool’s Joke him and Todd pulled when they Rick Rolled me into believing that Jessica was cheating on me with him. Assholes. “What are you up to this time?” he asks. “Smashing bad guys brains in with metal pipes again?” I give him a look. “That was the one time.” “Not what I heard at the precinct. Now you’ve got about two minutes before back up arrives. Get out of here before they arrest you and me too for not disclosing that I know who the vigilante Judge really is.” He looks back at his patrol car and realizes he’s being taped on a camera in his car but it has no sound. So he pulls out his gun and points it at me. He starts yelling to make it seem like he is trying to get me to put my hands up and get down on the ground. “GO! BUT BEFORE YOU DO TAKE THE GUN FROM ME!” His yelling makes perfect sense. In order to keep the ruse on that he doesn’t know me, I have to disable him and get away. Smart man my brother. He’s the one who stayed in college, not me. I’m the fuck up of this family. So the fucked up thing to do is to run through the snow, punch him in the face, and then run through the snow to my parked car across the street. I do all of that except for really punching him in the face. I fake punch him. He acts like it hurt so i can escape. My car is a black Toyota Supra that has been modified for street racing purposes. I stole it from one of the hoods that I apprehended. I judged him to death and took his car. Luckily I put snow chains on it before I left on my little adventure. Once inside my car, I turn on the ignition and then wait a second for the car to warm up. While waiting I hear something on the police scanner about a drunken man walking naked down a street downtown and something about the criminal Rick Roll shooting two gangbangers or something weird. Car’s warmed up by this point. After turning the lights on, I put the car in gear and speed off down the snow covered street headed in a eastward direction. Unfortunately for me two police cars come whizzing by at a high rate of speed. The cops know the make and model of my car. There’s no license plates so they don’t have to worry about looking that up. Sure enough the cars make a quick spin move and start chasing me down the street. Of course my car is too fast. The snow tires are what’s behind that. Much to my dismay they catch up and now I have one police car on each side of me. Just when they try to smash me in-between their cars but I hit the breaks and pull a Top Gun. The two police cars smash into each other and spin off the road. Once that happens I speed my car back up and drive away from the scene before any more police cars start arriving on the scene. * * * Fifteen minutes later... Trying to take advantage of the snowstorm, disgruntled former McDonald’s executive to the Western Region [name withheld] is on the prowl. Ever since he was fired months earlier after having a nervous breakdown in the workplace, Roger has taken on a new identity to get back at his employer. He is now a masked criminal named the Hamburglar. The irony of this nickname is that he has chosen to dress as the McDonald’s character the Hamburglar to commit his robberies against his former employer. He is a chubbier and older version of said fictional character, but the main thing he is looking for is revenge. He is dressed in a black and white striped shirt and pants with a tattered red cape, an old wide-brimmed black hat that he found at a garage sale for two bucks, and red leather gloves. Roger chose the character to model himself after because he takes things from children instead of gives them, thus in his sick and demented mind, he is the anti-thesis of Ronald McDonald. A criminal who steals money and hamburgers from the very company who used to employ him. After robbing the McDonald’s on the corner of Sahara and Arville using only a toy gun and locking the doors with chains so no one can chase him, he quickly exits out the side door and runs across the street to his car which is hidden in the shadows behind the 7-11 across the street. The snowfall is something that hampers his movement but he pays it little mind. He moves as fast as he can and then arrives back at his getaway car, a crappy old gray colored Cadillac, but someone is there waiting for him. “Why do you chose to rob hamburger stands using only plastic little toy guns?” the man asks. From his angle the Hamburglar seems to think the man is wearing a mask. Luckily for him in his rinky dink crappy apartment he has a small little television set that gets the local news. He’s seen this man before and knows what his name is. “I chose to do so to get back at the ones that hurt me Mr. Rick Roll,” he replies with a grin. The man comes out of the shadows and sure enough it is the master criminal Rick Roll. Rick Roll is notorious for Rick Rolling people before robbing them and taking their money. He also got his name because of the Rick Astley mask that he wears when he commits his crimes. Hamburglar is cautious because he also knows that this man has killed five police officers and would most likely show no hesitation in killing him. “You chose to get back at the people who hurt you?” Rick Roll asks with a laugh muffled under his breath. “Please, spare me the stupidity.” “That’s not a stupid reason.” The sirens of police cars can be heard in the distance. Hamburglar is visibly starting to sweat it a little bit but Rick Roll is as calm and collected as usual. “You’re nothing more than a scared little man who lost his job,” Rick Roll says. “I am something more than you. See this is the thing…you have no balls.” Hamburglar looks a bit bewildered upon hearing this comment from Rick Roll. In fact he seems a bit frazzled by it. “WHAT?!” “You have no balls,” he says again as he pulls out a handgun he had concealed and points it at the Hamburglar who looks like he‘s about to shit his pants. “You don’t have what it takes to be a criminal. See you have to insert a little gunpowder into the situation in order to get people’s attention.” Rick Roll then hands the handgun to the Hamburglar. “In order to make them shit their pants, however, you must do a little more than show them a gun. The cops think that Judge is more dangerous than any criminal in this town.” In the background two police cars arrive from the south and park at the McDonald’s directly across the street from where they are talking. Two officers get out of each car. The siren lights keep flashing as they start to investigate the robbery that just took place. “Well I think it’s time the cops knew that the criminals are more dangerous than some vigilante in a mask.” “What are you talking about?” The Hamburglar can almost see Rick Roll’s smile underneath the mask. “I’m talking about people needing the shit scared out of them to get their attention. These people don’t fear things like you and me used to when we were kids. They’ve seen some fucked up shit in their lives. In order to get your message across now days, you have to go above and beyond what you normally would do…” Rick Roll pulls out a control switch and presses the large red button on the front of it. Behind them the McDonald’s explodes. Two of the police officers fly through the air and land on the ground near the McDonald’s. The other two police officers get up and stumble about in the snow holding their ears. Both seem dazed and confused, stumbling about in the snow. Rick Roll turns to Hamburglar. “Shoot them…” Hamburglar looks flabbergasted by this idea. “Wha-what?” “Shoot them before they can call for back up.” “But I-I can’t.” “No, you won’t. You can do anything…you just don’t want to do it. But you have to otherwise more police will come quicker.” “I-I just can’t do it. I’m sorry.” “Pussy.” The cops stumble over toward the car door nearest to the street. Rick Roll sighs and takes the gun from Hamburglar before shooting both of the cops in the back. They both fall down into the snow. Hamburglar watches in shock as the snow turns red around their bodies. “HOLY SHIT!” “Damn right!” “What have you done? What is all of this? Some sort of a demented plan that you made up?” “The ultimate Rick Roll is what this is.” “Huh?” “See while you were inside robbing the place and stealing everyone’s food I was outside setting up the explosives. Lucky for me you locked them all inside with no way of getting out.” “You…you’re crazy.” “Of course I am. I’m just another common criminal infesting this town except I commit my crimes in style.” Rick Roll walks across the snow covered ground and over to the closest police car to his position. He walks over and pulls out a piece of paper from his coat. “Sending a message to someone else by violent means is more fun than I could ever imagine. I was an explosives virgin before this, you know.” Rick Roll leaves a note that says ‘Judged to Die’ in big red letters with a CD taped to it next to one of the dead officers. He laughs about it before turning and walking back across the street to where Hamburglar is. He looks down at the watch on his wrist before looking back up at Hamburglar. “Now we’d better get a move on. This is Urban Decay territory. If they find us they’ll kill us. This is their territory and they don’t like nobody fucking with it. So unless you’d rather stay here and get shot up by them or arrested by the cops, I suggest you get in your car and drive us out of here.” Hamburglar looks at the flaming building through the snow still in shock over what’s gone down. The smoke fills his lungs as he looks on at the burning building. More sirens can be heard in the distance coming from the west. He’s cold and his face is freezing so he makes the best rational decision he can. He walks over to the car and gets in the driver’s seat to chauffeur the maniac in the Rick Astley mask. He gets in and closes the door. Rick Roll looks over at Hamburglar, cocking his head a little as he does. “Smart choice, fat man. Now drive.” Hamburglar turns on the ignition and starts up the car. He puts the windshield wipers on and they drive down south on Sahara to the snow. The slow moving getaway car drives away and is out of sight by the time the reinforcements get there. Police cars and fire engines swarm the scene of the burning, blown up ruins of what used to be a McDonald’s. * * * An hour later… At Rick Roll’s condominium hideout, Rick Roll and Hamburglar enter closing the door behind themselves. Hamburglar notices he nice furnishing of the place. Another peculiar thing is a large stack of newspapers on the coffee table in the living room. Rick Roll motions to the couch after locking the door. “Sit down,” he says. Hamburglar walks to the couch and sits down. He immediately loves the Laz-E-Boy and the feel of it as he sits down. His maniacal new associate sets his stereo down next to the couch before he grabs the stack of newspapers off the coffee table before walking over to sit down on the couch next to him. “Firstly welcome to my humble abode,” Rick Roll says before taking the top newspaper off the batch he brought with him. “Now I want you to look at something I started noticing while looking over the newspaper.” He shows Hamburglar the first newspaper headline. “Judge dispatches of five robbers at liquor store.” Rick Roll tosses it aside and shows him another one. “Judge saves woman from mugger.” He tosses the third newspaper aside and shows him a third newspaper headline. “Judge kills three perps!” Rick Roll then tosses all the rest of the newspapers all over the place. Many land on the floor, others on the coffee table. Some even land on Hamburglar. Rick Roll looks menacingly at Hamburglar as he pulls a concealed gun out from his suit jacket. “I’VE HAD IT WITH THE JUDGE!” The gun is then pointed at the ceiling. “Every time any criminal does anything, in the Review Journal it only makes the third page or the Nevada section whereas Judge makes the front page. That’s why I decided it was time to blow some shit up to make these people fear the criminals like they should be instead of worshipping some vigilante. That‘s why I did what I did. I had a reason for all of it.” Hamburglar still seems a bit confused as to his reasoning. “Why…why did you do all of that again?” “I have a plan.” “What plan?” “Well when I’m not doing this. You know what I do? I have affairs with married women and knock them up but they think they’re husbands are the fathers.” “Whoa. What the hell are you talking about?” “I’m talking about saving people’s lives! They stay with their husbands after that and live out happy lives all the while they got knocked up because of their affair with me. You see I bring order to their lives through sin. It’s the ultimate Rick Roll and fun to boot!” “You’re crazy!” “No I’m not. I haven’t even told you what my plan is for this city yet!” “Ok, then what exactly is your plan?” “My plan is to create as much destruction, public anarchy, and chaos as possible. I’m going to Rick Roll this city into chaos. It’s going to be like Detroit back in the sixties! Killings and death…amazing what one little song can do.” “What kind of a plan is that?! All you want to do is kill everyone.” “No. If I did that, then we‘d have nobody to play with.” “Then what are you thinking?” “I’m thinking that the Urban Decay and the big bad Gerson Park Kingsmen street gang have a truce. And once they find out what happened, they will blame the other. Thus the truce will end…” Just then the Hamburglar realizes the truth of what he has just been told. “You’re Rick Rolling everybody into fighting each other!” “Yes.” “And that’s not all.” “What else?” Rick Roll stands up. “I know who Judge is.” “WHAT?!” “And I’m going to make him pay. I’m going to make the people of this city pay too in both lives and money.” Rick Roll points the gun at Hamburglar, cocking the trigger. “Put it in your mouth.” Hamburglar does as he’s told. The barrel is pulled over to his mouth and he puts it in. Sweat begins to drip from his forehead. “I…I mean, WE have to show these people that they still have something to fear. We have to show them that no matter how many people the Judge saves or the police arrest, they’ll always have a gun in their mouths with the triggered cocked and ready to fire. I think I’m going to demonstrate my idea using you as Exhibit A.” Rick Roll pulls the trigger but nothing happens. The gun wasn’t loaded. Still Hamburglar’s eyes go wide with shock and then he faints falling over onto the couch. “I got you good with that one.” After putting the gun back in its hiding place under his suit jacket, He then reaches down to his stereo and presses the play button. He waits for his favorite song to play but nothing happens. After a moment, he opens up the top case of the stereo and pulls out the CD. Upon looking it over he wipes the CD off using the bottom end of his suit jacket and puts it back in. “That ought to do it.” Pressing the play button again, he waits for a moment before hearing sweet music to his ears. ‘We're no strangers to love You know the rules and so do I A full commitment’s what I’m thinking of You wouldn’t get this from any other guy I just wanna tell you how I’m feeling Gotta make you understand Never gonna give you up Never gonna let you down Never gonna run around and desert you Never gonna make you cry Never gonna say goodbye Never gonna tell a lie and hurt you’ "And that's music to my ears." Rick Roll presses the stop button on the stereo before turning back to Hamburglar. “And I didn’t hurt you now, did I?” After waiting for a response from his unconscious partner in crime, he gets the response he was looking for. “I’ll take that as a no.” * * * At the same time… Back at the McDonald’s restaurant, Police Chief Mike Twain has arrived to oversee the operation. Upon arriving and getting out of his police car Officer Owens hands him the CD and the note that was left behind by the perpetrator of his heinous act. “Hello chief,” Officer Owens says to him. “We got a major thing going on here.” “What happened here?” Chief Twain asks as he looks at the firefighters putting the finishing touches on the fire. “From the looks of things some costumed man dressed as the Hamburglar robbed the joint and chained the doors shut so Judge could blow up the building.” “But that’s not like Judge at all. Not his style. This is something else.” “We did find that note at the scene.” Chief Twain notices the CD the note is taped to. “What about the CD? What’s on it?” “I dunno.” "You mean we haven't got a stereo to play the damn thing on?" "No." The Chief starts looking around. He has an idea to find out what’s really going on. “ANYBODY GOT A CD PLAYER?! ANYBODY!” Chief looks around and sees somebody in the crowd behind the police tape holding up a stereo that he was listening to the news radio on about what happened. “Here man,” he says. “You can use mine.” Twain walks over and nods his head. “Thank you.” He then puts the CD in and motions for some of the other officers to come over before pressing play. A song then begins to play… ‘We're no strangers to love You know the rules and so do I A full commitment's what I'm thinking of You wouldn't get this from any other guy I just wanna tell you how I'm feeling Gotta make you understand Never gonna give you up Never gonna let you down Never gonna run around and desert you Never gonna make you cry Never gonna say goodbye Never gonna tell a lie and hurt you’ At this point Chief Twain presses stop and turns to his officers. “We’ve been Rick Rolled,” he mutters, a bit perturbed by this development. “Judge isn’t the one who did this.” “Then who was it?” Officer Owens asks. “It was Rick Roll. He Rick Rolled us into searching for Judge instead of him so he could get far away.” The Chief throws the paper down in frustration as Owens collects the CD from the man‘s stereo. He also collects the paper off the group as the Chief calls off the search for Judge after this incident on the police radio and informs the officers on duty as to whom they should really be looking for. * * * Journal Entry, dated 12/17/2008: I’d been following those ninjas for weeks, trying to get a pattern to their behavior. Ironically their next target was the art gallery where my works were displayed. However there was one intriguing clue that I found. A card with the initials UD on it. That is the recognized symbol of the Urban Decay street gang that has been wreaking havoc all over town. However, I don’t buy it. This is not their style. I think the ninjas were given the card to throw me or the cops off from whose really behind it. The heist was a big money heist and that’s more along the lines of something one of the big casino bosses or mob bosses would pull off. The only problem is there are hundreds in this town who would be capable of a heist like this. So I’m right back to square one. And whomever planned out these robberies knew this would happen to the investigation when the card was found. But there is another issue that takes precedence over this. Rick Roll. The criminal bastard blew up a McDonald’s last night. And not just any McDonald’s, the one I used to go to all the time when I was a kid. It’s gone now because the maniac wanted to get everyone’s attention. Well he got his wish and I’ll be overjoyed to bring his ass to the police station personally. Nobody blows up a building in my town and gets away with it. Judge * * * 12/18/2008 The next morning I decided to go out and do some visual photography pieces of the snow covered landscape in my area. I’ve been toying with visual photography for awhile now since it’s cheaper and easier to do than my sculptures. The snow is still sticking to the ground and the vehicles all around. However the clouds have left the area. The sky is as blue as the ocean on a summer day. I walk over to the bus stop next to my complex to get a better picture of the snow covered street in front of my apartment building when my best friend Todd strolls over in his usual attire of a fancy suit but he’s wearing a very large and fancy coat to go with it. “Hey Ash,” he says to me as I take a picture of a snow covered car across the street. I turn back to him and smile. “Hey Todd,” I reply. “How was your night?” For some reason he has an unusually devious smile on his face. He was up to something last night. Probably banging some married woman and getting drunk in the midst of the snow storm. “Blah. Had too much fun last night. Don’t remember nothing.” I give him a look. He’s lying, I can tell. His forehead gets all wrinkled when he lies and it’s pretty wrinkled right now. “Oh…too shitfaced to remember what you did?” He nods his head. “Yeah. Stayed up till four am and woke up on my floor about an hour ago. I got like no fucking sleep last night. And then it was fucking freezing when I woke up. So I decided to come hang out with you before my hangover gets any worse and I fall asleep on the floor.” “Ok, cool. Well I’m just taking some photos for the gallery show on the twentieth.” “Speaking of which, on the news I heard that the Judge took down a ninja gang or something at the gallery last night.” This makes me smirk a little bit and raise an eyebrow just so I seem amused rather than jubilant over my victory last night. “Wow. Didn’t know about that. Ninjas in the gallery? Interesting.” “And your brother Kane was knocked down by the Judge before he escaped.” “Oh…hope he’s alright.” “He is. And that Rick Roll criminal or whatever he is blew up a McDonald’s across town.” This is something I heard on the police scanner last night but decided not to go near after the police car wreckage incident. I play it up like it's something not known to me at all. “WOW! That sucks. Hopefully everyone made it out alright.” “They did. Apparently they escaped through an employee emergency exit in the kitchen before the blast.” Todd mumbles something under his breath but I can’t tell what. He seems angry about something. “You alright?” He looks over and nods his head. “Yeah, I’m fine.” “That’s what the woman you fucked last night said after three drinks.” He looks over at me and starts laughing. “You’re such a dumbass, Ash.” I start laughing too. First time I’ve laughed in awhile. I haven’t really laughed since my parents got killed because of a drunk driver earlier this year or my fiancé Jessica was killed by a mugger who robbed us. Am I starting to enjoy life again? I’ll have to ponder this later but now I can’t really think. Too busy laughing. “Yes, yes I am a dumbass.” “Good, we agree on something. Now let’s get the hell back inside before my nuts freeze off and have some beers.” “We agree on two things in a row. Scary.” “I know. Scarier than any horror movie that’s come out the last ten years.” “True, very true.” We smile and then head back inside the apartment building. I can’t put my finger on it, but something seems to be troubling him. Hopefully it’s nothing too serious. Last time he was like this was after he found out his wife was cheating on him. Well at least we’ll be inside where it’s warm. The coldness should be gone tomorrow. Changes are on the horizon. I can only hope they are good for the city’s sake. After the anarchy of last night, they’d better be. * * * "Hope" is the thing with feathers -- That perches in the soul -- And sings the tune without the words -- And never stops -- at all --” -Emily Dickenson NOTE: Lyrics used in this are from the song “Never Gonna Give You Up” by Rick Astley It is also a direct sequel to the following promo - http://prime.e-wrestling.org/forums/index.php?topic=2964.0
