The interview with the 3B

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 *Scene opens inside the NGW training facility. BigBadBrad is seen on the bench press lifting about 450 pounds. His spotter is leaning against the wall behind him taking a nap, obviously not needed. NGW reporter Mark Robinson walks in with his microphone and grabs a chair, sitting down next to Brad. Brad sets the bar up on its holders and sits up. He wipes the sweat off his face with a towel and turns his attention to Mark.* 3B: What the hell do you want? Can you not see that I’m busy man? Mark: Yes, I can, but I just thought you’d want to say something towards the Survival match you have coming up...I have some questions prepared. 3B: You have questions prepared? You mean your not going to mumble and bull*beep* around through this one like you always do with me? Mark: No sir, this is very important so I decided to take time to make it good. 3B: Your bull*beep*ing me right now! Just get it started! *Brad shakes his head in disapproval as Mark gets his paper ready to read off of.* Mark: Ok, here is the first question I have for you. Will your wrestling in this tournament have any effect on you being the NGW’s Commissioner? 3B: Ha! Of course it will, bro. I haven’t had the chance to show that since becoming the Commissioner I haven’t gone soft. This will definitely show that I still have what it takes, and that I always will. I was chosen to be Commissioner because the name BigBadBrad means power. And power is exactly what NGW has. The same power that makes those watching keep on watching; the power that makes the crowds explode with cheers and boos for the wrestlers; the power that means whatever is going to happen next will be huge and not soon forgotten. Mark, nodding his head and going over his papers frantically: Umm, well, you did a good job. Brad shakes his head and slaps Mark in the back of the head, laughing: Get the hell outta here, I knew you would f*beep* this up again. What is it about me that makes you not able to do your job right? *Mark just shrugs his shoulders, standing up. Brad stands up, too, towering over Mark by about two whole feet. Mark nervously looks up at the 3B.* 3B: What is it? Is it my extreme power as the Commissioner that makes you forget what your doing? Or wait......is it because your gay and you want my body? I can understand the first one, but the second one.......you better just keep that to yourself if that’s the case. Nothing against it, just don’t want it. You understand? *Mark shakes his head then quickly starts nodding, but can’t make up his mind how to answer.* 3B, laughing: Get on outta here, man. *Mark leaves and Brad sits back down on the bench. His spotter sits down next to him, revealing himself as the one and only legend Johnny Hitchin. Brad looks at him and nods his head with that look that means he’s going to talk seriously and truthfully and honestly.* 3B: Guess I’m ready, man. Hitchin: You guess? That doesn’t sound very convincing to me, Braddie. 3B: I haven’t wrestled in a very long time, bro. I don’t know how I will really do against those other federation greats. Hitchin: Man, you got to put all that stuff away. Your THE 3B man! We’ve wrestled together forever, since just about the beginning of both of our careers in the professional wrestling world. I KNOW you have what it takes man, I’ve seen it time and time again. Besides, where you leave off - Dallas Reeves, Chris Johnson, and Asesino are there to pick up. No worries man, you have a great team to back you up. *Brad nods his head, but still has a sort of worried face. Hitchin puts a hand on Brad’s shoulder, smiling.* Hitchin: I know what will make you feel better. 3B: Hold on man, I’m not like Mark...... Hitchin, laughing: No man! I’m not that way at all either. I was thinking more towards a beer or two or twelve. 3B: Oh yeah, man. Yeah, I’m up for that like always. Maybe a little beer in me is what I need for the Survival match, you know how the beer works wonders for me in kicking some ass. *The scene fades to black with Hitchin and Brad laughing as they get up to head to the bar.*
