Tactus Spiritum

27 November 2005

Date: 2005-11-27 The big screen TV in the living room of the McFarlane Estate boomed with noise. At the sounds of the commercial, most people would have stopped paying attention. Alexis Key, prisoner to vampires, secretary of sorts to a palatial estate, ex-college student, ex-vampire hunter… she was paying rapt attention. All because the acronym "GTT5" had been shouted with rampant child-like abandon by the overly-hyper mid-twenties announcer at the beginning. She tilted her head slightly, taking in every detail, every single face shown on that screen. Naturally, this was of great interest to her due to the fact that three of her four flat-mates were participating in this tournament. Yet, strangely enough, Shadow, Virgil Ambrosi, and Dawn McFarlane had had surprisingly little conversation about the tournament, despite the fact that Alexis knew that all three of them were hell-bent on tearing limb from limb to get as far as possible in it. That struck Alexis as slightly odd. Yet, three seats down, on the other end of the couch, Shadow, otherwise known as Lia Vlaszy, sat perfectly calmly, scribbling in a cloth-bound book, and paying no attention at all to her surroundings. Alexis sighed. All of this was getting to be too strange for her, and for God’s sake, life was already strange enough, running from a league of vampire-hunters who was trying to kill you for cutting out on them, and ending up in the arms of your enemy, living in a mansion with a woman you didn’t know, and a couple of grotesque dogs that you wouldn’t have petted if you’d been paid. On top of it, Lia was back in the Estate, a week after Dawn had left for Japan, and Virgil to look for Lia. Conveniently, Caitlin Valentine was out of the house as well. And Alexis was sworn to secrecy about it all. Even if Virgil came back, hangdog and dying for answers about the wherabouts of his paramour… she couldn’t say a word. Deathly fear of the woman on the other end of the couch was enough to keep her lips sealed, and utterly. Add to that, respect for Lia’s wishes, and there was no way she was going to let this slip. "Aren’t you worried about competing against all these people?" Alexis queried, tilting her head slightly towards the television. Lia, in fact, hadn’t been quite as out of it as she seemed. Methodically, she closed her book, and thought on it a moment, before shaking her head. "No. There’s no point in being worried about it. I’m only in this tournament to prove something to myself, and what everyone else says is highly irrelevant. It’s a lot like GTT4." Alexis winced slightly. She remembered GTT4, because it had been an important link in The Den’s staking out of Shadow’s wherabouts, almost a full year ago. Lia’s eyes rankled her, attentive as they were. Alexis tried to shrug it off, feeling her skin begin to prickle with goosebumps under Lia’s fixed jade gaze. "What about Virgil? And Dawn? Especially Dawn… I saw you guys ended up in the same bracket…" That cut close to the quick, as Alexis had expected that it would. The vampiress’ eyes flashed bestial for a moment before calming back to their normal coldness. "I can’t worry about that either. A good few of the biggest matches in my career have been unsuccessful matches against Dawn. I don’t suspect this one will be any different." "Why?" Alexis’ eyes widened. "It is difficult to have my mindset, and wish to use it to compete against those you care about." An uneasy feeling surged from the very gut upwards to Alexis’ brain. She would never get used to that, these gut feelings about Lia and Virgil that she got sometimes that told her simply: run. But she never had, had never even considered it. Perhaps that was a mistake. "I," said the vampiress, her eyes turning away from the young woman, without noticing the almost visible relief on Alexis’ face as she did so, "have nothing to be concerned about as far as GTT5 goes. Nothing matters, save what I myself step into the match possessing. Thus, I’m not concerned. My allies, my dire enemies, those whose names are unfamiliar… they are all outside my mindset at the moment, and must remain so in order that I am prepared for the challenge that I face." This was all a little bit above Alexis’ head. A former pre-med student, she had not often had time for philosophizing of any sort, much less philosophizing about the proper mindset one needed to win a professional wrestling match. "So… are you going to do what these people are doing?" Alexis gestured towards the television, now blabbing some empty diatribe about a brand new vacuum, patented to clean twice the grit and grime of its lessers on the market. "Are you going to talk about your opponent? You know, make promotional videos about your career, stuff like that?" That elicited a rather strange reaction—Lia laughed. The woman who called herself Shadow, and fit the bill with her dour humor… laughed. Alexis attempted to reign in her shock. Luckily, Lia filled the silence. "I don’t see the need. They either know who I am, or they don’t. If they don’t, nothing that I can say in front of a camera, no fancy videos, no clever tricks will change their mind. I offer myself as I am, and I’m no camera-whore." At that, Lia stood up from the couch, and dusted herself off, giving Alexis a sidelong glance. "Na shledanou, Alexis. Remember… don’t tell anyone I was here." A cursory nod was all Alexis was able to fire off, before quick footsteps echoed out of sight. The front door to the McFarlane Estate shut, and quite gently. The palatial manse was again nearly devoid of life, again plunged into its now-typical sad silence. Yet, Lia lingered somehow. No matter where she went, she left her indelible imprint.
