My Time

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 Noah Hanson Estate Saturday, September 11, 2004 2:47 p.m. I sat looking over the records, the tapes everything that I could get my damn hands on to get a look at my opponent for GTT4 some guy named Ricky Summers. I had done a little research, finding out that he used to work for the now closed EPW. That does little to help me now though. I sat in my chair frustrated by the thought that I am going blind into a match without much info on my opponent. I turned as my fax machine started making noise, kicking out a series of papers. I scooted the chair over, watching as the papers kicked out the PrimeTime Central logo ablaze on the top of the letter head. I snatched the papers from the fax, looking over them as I scooted back in front of my computer. Dear GTT4 participant, You have already gotten your welcome packet for the tournament; we here at PTC welcome you to the elite tournament of the year. Enclosed are you password and keyword to get into the building for the tournament, you will need to get into the building. Again we would like to welcome you and wish you the best of luck. Also enclosed is a bit of back history on the tournament and please go to the GTT4 website for any further info. We look forward to seeing you at the tournament. Sincerely, Argyle {Tournament Director} I looked over the packet, it was interesting all the useless info that the packet included. The one thing that did intrigue was all the prizes that will be handed out, including a shot at the Extreme title and the Global title. I thought what a cool and novel idea it was to have awesome prizes like that. I had long dreamed of going after Seymour Almasy at sometime during my career. He is the guy that is at the top of the proverbial mountain, the man everyone wants to be and the only two time Global champion. It means he is doing an incredible job, and is one of the early favorites to win this tournament as he is also entered in this thing in a lower bracket. I looked down at the word that I was given as a password to get into the place when the tournament finally started. I kinda of laughed at the thought of walking up to a door, having a little slit open and having to utter my password like this was some kind of secret cult or something. But it did little to change my thoughts on my opponent; he was simply in the wrong place at the wrong time. I have no real feelings toward him as he is nobody to me, just somebody that will be looking across from me when the match begins. He will lose that is already settled, he will lose badly that is also a given. I can expose this guy for the fraud that he really is, pretending to be a wrestler is a serious offense. I thought this would be a problem cause for two years the PTC vets have kinda pushed me aside they think that I am not a real threat, they think that I cannot show everyone that I am a real deal. I have long been scorned for the last few years and that drives me each and everyday I lace up my boots and get dressed for the job I do. It seems that at times so many people have a lack of respect for me or my talent but that is part of the reason why I signed up for this thing was to prove everyone that they were wrong. I clenched my fist as I thought about all the whispering and the snickers every time I walked into some tournament or match. Most of the time I am not even considered a real threat; it was that way when I first signed with Wrestling Midwest. Nobody gave me a real chance then either. It was funny cause almost 3 years later, and I have become one of the most respected wrestlers in the whole fed. I may not be the biggest, the strongest, the fastest but I will out wrestle them and out last them. I turned as I heard a noise before me, it was Kristina standing there she was carrying a couple of beers as she walked in. “What is on your mind??” She asked as she sat on my lap, handing me a beer. “Well, I have a pretty full plate at the moment. I have the match with Slayer, and then I have the first round match up with a guy named Ricky Summers.” I replied as I opened the beer, taking a long swig. “You worried?” She asked I looked at her, a little shocked by the comment but she always was like that. “No, I am not worried at all. This is just another day that Noah Hanson goes out there and earns some more respect from all those guys that hang out in PTC. They have long time disrespected me, but those times are about to change. It was last year in the PIT that I defeated my first round opponent easily, only to be handed a resounding defeat at the hands of Seymour Almasy, and that is something that I have never forgotten it is etched into the deepest parts of my mind. I still think about that loss from time to time, it makes me wish I could get my hands on for at least one more time to wash my hands of the stench that has become that loss." I looked down, sighed deeply it pained me to go on and on and on about something as irrelevant as a loss that happened over a year ago. I was better than that; I have evolved as a wrestler, as a person. The match that I have in GTT4 will just cement my legacy in PTC as the new era of dominance, I have the tools to be the new dominate force, and the match with Summers is where it all begins. I have to make an example of him, I have to go out there and be as dominate as I have ever been. The bracket is loaded with some big name talent like Unstoppable Eugene, Tessa Windsor from that craptastic fed RPW, Kenny Mercury, Dave Harley just to name a few AND then we have a man that draws some particular interest in me personally and that is the Global Wrestling World Champion “The American Youth” Josh. I watched him at Crossfire, saw how talented he really is and thought that maybe just maybe I could have beaten him in that very ring, on that very night. But I was stupid enough to get myself involved in a suicidal match with the man that is an extreme lunatic Donny J. McNasty. We had that brutal match at Crossfire, one that could have easily ended my wrestling career, but instead I actually think that it made me a more popular wrestler in some kind odd, weird way. Not that I mind it, or anything but I am going out into this tournament and doing what I do best and that is to make this joker look as ridiculous as possible. I clinched my fist at the mere thought of losing it angered me like little else. It would be something that all the naysayers in PTC would love for to happen to me. I would lose, and become the butt of everyone’s jokes for weeks to come; well I have news for everyone that will never happen. Kristina could tell there was something wrong, she looked into my eyes. She smiled, kissing me as she pressed her body close to mine. As she pulled back, she could still tell some thing was bothering me. “What’s wrong, sweetie?” She asked as she got off my lap. She walked over looking at all the trophies, awards and other stuff on my walls and in my trophy case. “Everything is going so fast, the matches, the money everything…” I said as I got up from my desk, I tripped and then everything went dark… Dream Sequence I was not sure what was happening, because I was now in some kinda dark, dank, depressive room. There was little lighting, so I fumbled through the room. As I did I could tell that whatever I was walking through was wet, because my shoes were sloshing through it? As I walked I reached into my pocket, to my surprise there was a flashlight in it. I pulled the flashlight form my pocket, turning it on and flashing it towards the walls. To my dismay they were covered in what looked like blood. What kind of place was this, and why was there blood all over the wall?? What did this have to do with me? Suddenly I started to hear footsteps behind me, I turned shining the light towards where the sounds were and to my surprise it was me walking towards me. “What are you doing?” the other me asked. She looked down at my clothes shaking his head in disappointment. “For god what the hell do you do when you get dressed?” I looked down, trying to figure out what he meant. “What there is not a damn thing wrong with what I am wearing I am styling and profiling.” “Ya, look like ya are some kind retard from a porn movie, and a bad one to boot.” He said as he pointed to my shirt and pants. “Come on man, have some respect for me because I am part of you also.” “What do you mean?” I asked as I was unsure what he meant. “Look, I am the part of you that drives you to be better at everything you do. And all this bullshit you keep spitting out needs to stop, you are a fine wrestler; all the mind games and shit have to stop.” He said as he started to walk towards a small door on the far end of the room. “Just remember one thing…” He said as he opened the door, and looked back. “What??” I asked. “Just quit with all the bullshit, everyone will respect you more if you are just yourself!” He then walked through the door which was now open. I looked around, not seeing any other way out I decided that I would go through the door also. As I walked through the door I was blinded by a bright flash of light. As I put my hands up to try and protect my eyes from the light I realized that I was somewhere different, I was in some kind of meadow in the middle of no where. There were trees swaying in the wind, in the distance I could see a person sitting on a bench under a tree. I put one hand over my eyes to block the sun so I could get a better look, but it really did not help much. I walked over to place where the lady was sitting; she had her back to me. “Have a seat next to me, Noah!” She remarked as she continued with whatever she was doing. “Who are you?” I asked. “That is not important…you are looking for answers though, correct?” She replied calmly. “Things never seem to be the way they should be. I lose my parents, my son but everyone thinks I should be strong and live my life to the fullest!” I remarked as I looked down in frustration. “Greatness, it is a heavy burden to carry. It is unforgiving, unsettling and unjust to most. But I see something different in you, you are different than most you have something that most do not have.” She calmly replied again as she reached back patting me on the leg. “I do?” I asked. “You have the courage, the guts and the determination to take it to another level. A level, which most can only dream about going to. That is what makes you something more than just your average person, or wrestler.” She remarked as she turned towards me, I jumped as it was my grandmother. “I should Noah my dear grandson, I helped raise you. If anyone knows you it would be me.” “How can you be here? You’re…” I stammered to finish the words. “Dead…well yes, I am dead but in your heart I have always lived on. And again that what makes you something special, something that most people will never understand.” She said as she hugged me warmly, like it was as if she was not dead at all. “Grandma, I have missed you so much. Sometimes my life gets all confusing, and I have no idea what to do!” I exclaimed as tears started to roll down my cheeks. “My dear son, you will always have the answers you need if you just simply open you heart!” She calmly remarked as she again patted my leg. “I am always right here…” She pointed towards my heart. “That is always where you can find me!” Was it really that simply; was everything in my life that simple? I was having a hard time grasping that thought. It did not seem that easy, because nothing I my life ever has been, but for some strange reason I felt what seemed like the weight of a thousand men pulled off of my shoulders. I felt more calm and relaxed than I had ever felt. It was something that I liked, but was unsure how to cope with it. I just know that my life is more complicated now than it has ever been before, and sometimes thing can get a bit overwhelming. The burden can sometimes be too much to handle. But I think that with everything that is happening, this match could in fact be the match that turns all of this into the time of Noah Hanson. A time where people realize that I am for real, that I am truly a star and not just some flash in the pan. “Just remember that everything is not always as it seems.” Grandma replied. I turned towards her, but she was gone. I got up, looking around the other trees but she was gone. But was she there to begin with? END DREAM SEQUENCE “Ohhhh, my head…” I tried to sit up, but Kristina pushed me down. “You need to relax!” She replied as she had a cloth on my head. “You need to stay put for a bit.” “What happened?” I asked as I felt the cloth on my head. “You tripped, smacking your head on the desk. It knocked you out cold.” She replied. “Really, so it was all a dream?” I asked as I looked at her in shock. “What was?” She looked at me strangely. “Nothing…I guess things are not as they seem sometimes…” *end*
