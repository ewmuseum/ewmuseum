The Killean Chronicles: Holiday Feelings

6 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-06 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "You're hiding in your hall, alone with your hunger Can't break through the wall, of the prison within No reason to be proud, don't go so near it You bow your head in shame, afraid you will fear it Open up your dreams Seven silver keys To open up your dreams You need seven silver keys" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The home of Killean Sirrajin was normally a barren wasteland around and during the holidays. Usually the holidays involved him visiting friends in other cities. Last year, Matt and Mary opened up their home to him and shared all the joys of the season. Given that Killean and Matt were not on good terms to say the least, it was not an option this year. However the Sirrajin home was bumping with holiday cheer this year. Stockings were hung by the accessory fireplace. There were four of them all with names neatly written on the white band on top. Reading from left to right were the names of Killean, Desiree, Vivian and Vegas. Vegas had flown in from San Diego to spend the holidays in snowy Winnipeg with Killean. On the outside, Killean was rather unfazed with the holiday spirit that was bouncing off the walls of his loft. He looked around at the decorations, seeing many streams of garland coming from the corners of the room and meeting in the middle where a ceiling fan hung. Lights were illuminating the area around the windows and doorways creating extra shadows that lead to some interesting designs falling upon the walls and the floor. The Christmas tree was the pristine example of the time and care taken to decorate the roomy loft that four people were to spend the holiday season in. Inside, The Supreme Machine was a bundle of joy and emotions as he continued to scan the neatly decorated room. This was indeed his first Christmas spent at his own home since Kira had passed away. Since then, he always took the journey to friends upon himself. Now he had a baby girl to care for and the travel would not be easy. Vegas had been happy to come see Desiree for the first time and he had already taken a liking to his adopted niece. Vegas was long like a brother to Killean and even his heart melted after hearing his first earful of holiday laughs from the beautiful baby girl. Vivian had watched on with adoring eyes as Killean and Vegas had become kids themselves in the last few days. They were rolling on the floor more than Desiree was, pushing a ball back and forth between them and making Desiree laugh with glee as it passed by her as she sat in her bouncy baby seat that hung from the ceiling. It was a joy to see a guy like Killean, who had come across so many misfortunes in his life, enjoying every moment of the season and every moment he got to spend with his new daughter. Even the diaper changes were no longer a chore. He seemed to cherish every second with Desiree as if it were their last together. Killean was still scanning the room when Vegas emerged from Desiree’s room, carrying the child as she stared bewildered at all the decorations. The twinkle in her eye as she passed under the mistletoe with Vegas was hard to miss. Vegas puckered up and gave the child a few kisses on the cheek which brought a joyful smile to her face and a small laugh. Killean laughed and watched as Vegas took a seat near Killean and planted Desiree in between them on her play mat on the floor. Vegas stuck out his right hand, which Killean promptly shook as he greeted his best friend. "Merry Christmas brother." He said, shaking his hand fiercely. "Right back at ya boy." Vegas nodded in response. "Happy to share it here with you. And of course I can’t forget this little darling." He reached down and tickled Desiree, which brought out another joyful laugh. Vivian came out from her guest room with a ridiculous looking Santa hat on her head. It may have been the holidays but Killean and Vegas could not hold back a laugh as she walked by them. Vivian then reached back and playfully slapped Vegas on the shoulder. "You boys stop yer laughin’. This hat is for the baby’s enjoyment." She bent down and brushed away Desiree’s hair and shook the fuzzy ball that hung from the hat. Desiree kept on staring at it as it moved back and forth, never moving her gaze. Vivian made her way to the tree, which had almost thirty gifts piled under and beside it. "Wowee, this is certainly a load of gifts." "Don’t get your hopes up Viv. More than half are for Dizzy here." Killean replied while he moved a plush dinosaur back and forth across the mat to the enjoyment of the little girl. "Come on let’s get on with the gifts!" Vegas exclaimed, causing Vivian to begin handing out the neatly wrapped presents. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "A war against yourself, you win or you lose here Something new to fear or nothing to do Paradise hotel, bombs in your cellar Mr. clean'n'safe, your guvenor of hell Open up your dreams Seven silver keys To open up your dreams You need seven silver keys" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ A family type warmth filled the air as Christmas wrapping littered the floor. The lights on the tree twinkled brightly and they radiated throughout the room. Many gifts sat alongside Desiree on the floor as she closely examined each one, picking the large stuffed teddy bear as her favorite. Killean and Vegas sat and showed each other their gifts as Vivian made her way to the kitchen to tend to the turkey in the oven. Desiree flung out her leg and kicked over one of the gifts, startling herself a little. Killean then bent over to pick her up and began to comfort her as a few tears began to stream down her face. "Oh, I almost forgot." Vegas said as he rose from the couch and made his way over to the large storage closet. "Now I promised one big gift to you man." Killean looks down at the High Definition cable box on the couch beside him. "Yeah, you already gave it to me." Killean said as he held up the box with one arm and continued to hold Desiree in the other. Vegas laughed and put his hand on the doorknob of the closet. "Oh boy you are misled. I never said that was the big gift." Vegas turned and opened the door and began to move some items around. Vivian moved out of the kitchen around the island and into the living area. Vegas began to roll something out of the closet. "THIS is the BIG ONE!" He exclaimed as he rolled the new LCD TV with stand out into the living room. On the side of the stand stood a new surround sound system still in the box. Killean rose from the couch wide-eyed and with his jaw practically sitting on the floor. "My word that’s a big TV." Vivian said with a hand on her cheek. Vegas laughed and rolled it by the window for everyone to see. "Fifty inches of High Definition LCD technology complete with DTS compatible surround sound system. Perfect compliments to your new HD ready cable box." Vegas explained as Killean made his way over to the TV where Vegas stood. He still had a shocked expression on his face. But then a smile broke as he looked towards the smaller thirty inch plasma TV on the current stand in the living room. "Care to help me hook it up?" Killean questioned as he extended his free hand to Vegas while still holding Desiree. Vegas shook it tightly. "Wouldn’t have it any other way my friend. Merry Christmas." He moved forward and gave both Killean and Desiree a hug. "This just barely makes up for the great system you had installed in my truck last year." He laughed as Vivian came over to take Desiree from Killean as the two boys began to work on the new entertainment system. In a matter of thirty short minutes, Killean and Vegas were sitting on the couch behind Desiree who was back on her mat. They were flipping through channels on the new TV but came to a stop on a channel that was high definition. The sounds of a football game began to vibrate through the sound system as the colors of the screen lit up the room. Killean nodded as Vegas sat back and put his hands behind his head to enjoy the Chicago Bears and the Green Bay Packers playing on Christmas Day. "I don’t think I’ll ever watch football the same again." Said Killean who kept his eyes glued to the screen. He took a sip of his beer that was sitting by his side on the table. "You won’t, unless you go see it live." Vegas nodded in agreement as Killean bent over to pick up his daughter and sit her in his lap. He stared at her, relishing in the thought that this was their first Christmas together and most definitely would not be the last. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "The vultures of the mind, the vampire guerilla Invite the underground, give in to your self Let loose the hounds of Hades, that means you can kill her Unlock the secret wells, your life can begin Open up your dreams Seven silver keys To open up your dreams You need seven silver keys" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ It was already dark outside and the glaring picture from the new TV reflected off all the walls, illuminating dark corners of the room. Stepping out from the kitchen, Killean stood and looked out the window into the streets. He watched as all the cars drove by down below, trying to make it to their destinations, most likely families and friends houses for the holidays. He remembered that since Kira passed, he always made the trips that the people below were doing. Then he remembered why he wasn’t making those trips this year. The first reason was Matt and Mary. He still thought of Mary as family to him but he no longer knew what to think of Matt. Just a short week ago, he had counted the three that officially crowned Matt the Universal Champion of PRIME, a title that he once proudly wore. He knew the feeling of immortality; it had enveloped him for quite a few months. Once he had lost that title, his soul didn’t die. He only went on to become a living legend, a top superstar in the wrestling industry. It wasn’t some fluke title reign. Now Matt was wearing the shoes that Killean did back before July 24th when Colossus II took place. Would he be able to fill those shoes like those before him? Only time would tell. Would his relationship with Matt and Mary ever be repaired? Time won’t mend that friendship. It would take something much more… violent. It was take something that was thicker than water. It would take crimson… blood. Removing his mind from the violent thoughts that he had about his former friend, the second reason was sleeping quietly in her crib. Killean made his way to Desiree’s room where he stood in the doorway, leaning against the frame. He crossed his arms and looked through the bars in the crib to see his beautiful pride and joy sleeping on her back, moving her legs a little. ‘What was she dreaming about?’ He wondered aloud. He still couldn’t believe that he was a father, much less a responsible one. He had always planned on marrying and having a full family with a mother that took care of their child. Instead, after the passing of Kira and their unborn daughter, he turned back to wrestling. But this was by far his most important job. It wasn’t wrestling. It was being a father to his daughter. Vegas came around the corner and put his hand on Sirrajin’s shoulder and took a look at the little darling resting comfortably. He then patted his stomach. "What a dinner huh? Viv really outdid herself today!" He said but Killean shushed him, pointing over to Desiree who was now moving given the new sounds that had penetrated her slumber. But she didn’t wake up; she moved and seemed to drift right back off. "Yeah, she did make a great meal." Killean whispered back to Vegas who nodded. "Did you help her with the dishes?" "You just stay concerned with being a daddy." Vegas replied making Killean break a large smile. "I’ll take care of all the cosmetic things for ya." "You know, looking at her right now, right here makes me think." Killean whispered with Vegas turning back to him after getting ready to make his way in the living area. "What’s that?" "The day she was left on my doorstep, that crazy bitch didn’t even wait for me. She didn’t even try to call me. She just abandoned all her motherly instincts for caring for a child and left her alone there. Anything could have happened. What if I didn’t come home that day? What if I found my parents?" He was then quiet for a second as he tried to take the images of a despicable mother out of his mind. Vegas looked at him with wonder, not knowing how Killean could possibly feel about being a father. "I can’t say I can relate." "Well I wouldn’t expect you too. I have had this responsibility dropped into my lap. I was not willing at first, but I can’t help but love every moment of it. I feel like I am one of the luckiest people in the world. I am sharing Christmas with my friends and my… daughter." It still sounded strange to him, even though he had said it multiple times every day since he found her. But as strange as it sounded, it was a natural thing as well. "You are one lucky mofo to have a beautiful baby like Desiree. I envy you man and you’ll make a great father." Vegas said as he patted Killean on the shoulder, reassuring Killean to the point of another genuine smile. Vegas then made his way to the living room, passing by Vivian who came to stand with Killean now. "What a darling." She said, watching Desiree sleep peacefully. Killean nodded in return, leaning off the door frame to give Vivian a hug. "Thank you for a wonderful meal." Killean said, trying not to sound too emotional before he released the bomb that almost brought Vivian to tears. He broke the hug. "And thank you for helping me. I will be forever grateful. And since you are already like a mother to me, I would be honored to have you as godmother and honorary grandmother of Desiree." She happily nodded and almost wept at the request, but she managed to keep her composure. "Of course my dear." She said as she hugged Killean again. Given that Killean didn’t have any relationship with his parents whatsoever, he was always grateful to have Vivian around. He thought of her as family and more like a mother figure. She was always there for him, during the good and bad times. "Coming hun? We are going to start the movie." Killean just nodded in response, taking the time to watch his daughter once more as Vivian made her way into the living room. He came into the bedroom and gently brushed the hair of his daughter, bringing forth some movement from her but nothing to wake her. "I am lucky…" he whispered as he smiled. He then turned and switched on the baby monitor and made his way into the family room to join Vivian and Vegas. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "The seven rounds of GTT are the doors People will fill the arena from the rafters to the floors.
