Tales of a Forgotten Tommorrow

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Solitude. The soft wind howled through the midnight sky, gathering ruffled leaves along its way. Tree branches scraped each other, leaving their flake remains to scatter amongst the dry soil below. The December night boasted its claim of chill, evident abundantly in the trails of crisp leaves and bare bushes that it sprinkled among the Bogota’ soil. Night that once played host to casual engagements of grandeur now left a vacancy on the otherwise laboring land. Workers were retreated to their homes, aware of the hostile and hazardous working conditions. The land was scarce of citizens…but yet beaming with distress. The air in the room was thick, mainly because of the situation at hand. Bearing only a tender age of seven years, I sat at the very top of the stairway, glaring at the occurrence below me. One figured, engulfed by the bodies of three physicians decked out in white coats, cocooning the image of the suspect at hand. Protection came in the form or rubber gloves, rubber boots, and a face mask, clearly exclaiming the high relevance of the operation being performed. I could hear the soft jargon being murmured amongst the team, but could not completely comprehend just exactly what was going on…At such a tender age, though, the urge to know was almost unbearable. My glistened crystal eyes grew big at the sight of silver metal reflecting off of the light that was hovered above the body of the subject. The sharp endpoints and humming of the electronic machinery made me cringe in pain, and the mild yet audible screech of the subject at hand did little to tame it. My palms shivered in the cold sweat that engulfed them as I watched the operation occur, clueless as to whether panic or remain calm….the sudden uproar informed me, though, that the decision wouldn’t be very difficult in a matter of moments. "Oh sh-…Code Red!" "But sir we-…" "CODE RED NOW!" The sudden panic of the physicians made me quickly grasp the rail beside me, squeezing it as tightly as my sweaty palms would permit. I sat up a bit in order to obtain a better glance at the occurrence, but it achieved nothing as the scrambling of physicians crowded the scene. "He injected serum AB instead of serum BA…the subjects life could very well be on the line! Quick, grab the Biotic Antidote!" "He’s not in a stable enough condition to receive the antidote, doctor…he’ll die!" "GET ME THE DAMN ANTIDOTE!" The urgent and ghastly temper of the head physician sent chills down my spine. Suddenly, all three physicians leaped backwards as the patients body rolled off of the platform and unto the solid cold ground. …It was the first time I’d seen my father’s face since the accident. Four days ago, Bogota’ was bombed by an opposing empire…my father’s face was nearly demolished by the incident. But as I saw him lying there…his face covered in scars and bruises, all which hid behind the warm blood that flooded down his face…it made me scream out in terror. All of the sudden, he directed his blood shot eyes at me with a look of death. Even at a mild age, I could tell he was on the verge of death, and the mere thought made me emotionally unbearable. As he lie there, crawling towards me with those bloodshot eyes…all I could hear him screaming out my name… "Vangelus!!" I cried. "Vangelus!!" I screamed. "VANGELUS!!" Suddenly I shot my head up out of the A.P. History book in which I had fallen into a deep sleep on. As my eyes peeled open, I witnessed Professor [name withheld] standing over me with his arms folded and a not so welcoming look on his face. "Mr. Olsig…that’s the second time I’ve had to wake you this class period, now if this occurs once more, I’m afraid I’ll be forced to dismiss you from my classroom. Now…As I was saying t-…" [detail withheld]. Stationed dead in the center of Montgomery, Alabama…it was the college in which I sought to earn my masters which would hopefully bring me out of the slums of wrestling and into a real career. Touring the globe with PRIME, doing house shows and hopping midnight tickets just so you could take a one-day-a-week class was not my idea of a good time, and is showed in my constant falling to sleep during Professor [name withheld]’s class. Nonetheless, I rubbed my eyes and began what would hopefully be a better attempt at remaining awake. BBBBEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEPPPP!! I nearly leaped out of my seat, startled by the loud school tune that rang through my ear and signaled the end of this class period. I shook my head, attempting to gather myself as I closed by AP History book and placed it inside of my backpack. A large crackle sounded through the school intercom system, meaning an announcement was abroad. Students, the cafeteria is now serving. I repeat, the cafeteria is now serving. Also, I would like to make an exciting announcement. Be sure to come out to the Garret Coliseum this Friday Night to watch one of our very own, Vangelus Olsig, compete in a singles wrestler bout. We are excited to have such a star in our school, so show that excitement by coming out this Friday night and support Mister Olsig. Thank you. I smiled as the intercom clicked off. [detail withheld] was a small community college, so having someone who had achieved a considerable amount of fame in their school was something they made big deal out of. Constants of people surrounded me at the cafeteria, dozens crowded around me in the halls, all asking for tickets to the next ReVolution…it was a stint of fame that I never really cared about…but one which seemed to love me in return. I stood out of my seat and grabbed my backpack, placing it on my shoulder as I exited Professor [name withheld]’s room. Before I completely exited, though, I heard his deep toned voice once more. "…Mister Olsig…" He stated. I stopped in my tracks and turned around to see just what it was he wanted this time. To my suprise, though, he smiled and winked, giving me a thumbs up. "Break a leg Friday Night." He stated. He wasn’t the most generous of men, so such a comment came completely out of left field. I returned a smile and a swift nod before making my way out of the classroom. "Hey Olsig…I’ll be there Friday night dude!?" "Anymore tickets to Revolution, homie?" "I love the way you perform in the ring, man." "Could I get your autograph?" "Knock em’ dead Friday Night, my friend." As soon as I my feet touched then neutral ground of the [detail withheld] hallway, I was bombarded with the usual masses of people, congratulating, begging, and sucking up. Placing my not-so-present ego aside, I tried to return a smile and nod to each of them, but after awhile it became nearly impossible. Suddenly I felt someone’s arms wrap around my shoulder. I looked over, and it was David Harpbridge…one of my two best friends in the entire world. He’s one of the few people who liked me for who I was, and not for what I could do for him. "How’s life treating my best bud." He stated with a smile on his face, taking his arm from around my shoulder in order to prevent any rumors from arousing…these are college kids, ya know. "Well…" I began "I fell asleep twice in Mister [name withheld]’s class…I’m as exhausted as human man could possibly get…I’m being bombarded by people I don’t even know…" "The usual, eh?" He stated with a smile on his face as we continued down the hall. "Yeah…the usual." I responded as we both shared a small giggle. Continuing down the hallway, we basked in conversation until something particular caught the eye of us both…Maria Jackson, my second best friend in the world, being harassed by a gang of guys. Upon seeing this, we both stopped our conversation and made our way over to the scene….and in doing so, everything became so clear. Jerry, Seth, and Ben. Three self proclaimed "Bad Boys" who got off on harassing girls and trying to talk big to the boys. They were particularly interested in Maria, though, because their ring leader, Jerry, was her former boyfriend before she broke up with him and had a brief relationship with me. For that, Jerry has never forgave either one of us, and quite frankly wouldn’t mind either of our heads on a platter. Maria and I, though, decided afterwards to just be friends…which did little to lighten Jerry’s mood. "Leave her alone, Jerry." I called out as all three guys turned and looked my way. "Oh and what are you going to do about it, Mr. Wrestler Dude?" He joked, laughing at his own pathetic joke as his two lackeys joined in the laughter as well. "Don’t test me, Jerry…leave her alone." I replied with a less than pleasant look on my face. It became obvious that he was catching on to my foul mood as he and his cronies took a few steps backwards while maintaining a foul expression on their faces. "Fine, Mr. Big Shot…have it your way…but don’t be too suprised when the consequences hit ya." He stated, winking as if he had a plan in place. I shrugged it off as they walked off and turned my attention towards Maria. "You alright Maria?" I asked. "Yeah…I-I’m fine. I hate those guys." She replied. "What’d he mean by consequences, though?" Asked David "Don’t worry…I know his kind, I work with people like him on a daily basis…their all talk." I replied, wrapping my arms around Maria in order to better comfort her…but as David looked on at the trio walk away and the vengeful look in their eyes, he could only reply in one form… "…I hope you’re right." Ding Ding Ding The sounds of the three bells signaled the end of the contest as I rolled out of the ring, fatigued after a 14 minute matchup with one of my independent arch nemesis, T.J. Strife…a match that pushed me far and beyond my limits. The adrenaline rush that ran through the atmosphere…the energy of the fans, all screaming and yelling out in favor…even a small chant rallying up from the group of five in the middle of the fourth row…it all filled my heart with that one emotional element that kept me in the wrestling business, despite my inner hatred of it all. I reached the curtain, but before exiting backstage I turned around to soak in that last bit of adrenaline which seemed to grow even stronger. I smiled and turn around, opening the curtain as I stepped backstage and was immediately greeted with a hug by Maria. "Wow, man…Great job out there!" She stated with a smile on her face. Her luscious scent satisfied my nostrils after having to bear 14 minutes of that horrible scent of must and sweat. She guided me to my nearby locker room where we both took a seat. "Ah…that was hell." I stated, grabbing a towel from the counter beside me and wiping away all of the sweat that drenched my body. I heaved the towel to the ground as Maria fixed me a nice glass of Scotch. "Yeah, I could tell…looks like Strife has gotten even tougher since the last time you two faced off" She stated, handing me the glass of scotch in which I took. "He has…" I began, stopping for a moment to take a sip of the scotch, sure to lick my lips to retrieve all of the lingering flavor. "Hopefully this is my last independent booking for a while, though. With PRIME, College, and a returning stint in GTT5…I doubt I can keep up with such a hostile way of living." "Got that right. Traveling all through the night, eating various types of foods, putting your life on the line each time you step in the ring…you need to calm your jets man. I only get to see you twice a week at most these days, so when I do get that opportunity, I would like you to be physically and mentally stable, you know?" She stated, sharing a slight giggle as she took a sip of scotch. "I feel ya…hey, where’s David?" I asked, glancing around the room to see if he was anywhere in sight. "I don’t know…He left this note, though, saying to meet him in hallway B." She replied, handing me the note to look at. "Why would he want us to meet him in Hall B?" I queried. "Who knows…We better get going, though…it could be important." She stated, grabbing her coat and slipping it on as I placed the letter in my pocket and slipped on a T-Shirt as we made our way outside of the locker room and unto the hall, seeking David. Shift We made our way down Hall B, surveying our surroundings carefully in search for David…but yet we still found nothing. It was if he was hear at one instance, but has now suddenly disappeared without noticing anyone. The thought alone that something may have happened to him tortured my already fragile soul, therefore I denied any negatives thoughts and replaced them only with positive probabilities. Maybe he went out for a walk…. Maybe he got lost… Maybe he hadn’t arrived yet… Maybe he… No. I shook the last part out of my memory, realizing the negativity in it…a negativity in which I was seeking to avoid. Mrrrmmmm… Suddenly, we both turned around after hearing the noise. "Where’d that come from?" Maria asked, surveying the surroundings just as deeply as I was. Mrrrrmmmm.. It sounded once again, but this time I had a clue where it was coming from. I signaled to Maria to follow me as we both made our way to the janitors closet. I twisted the knob which seemed stuck at first, but after a few jabs at it, I finally pried it open, but after witnessing the image inside, I’d wish I hadn’t. David…. Bloody. Beaten. Abused. Scarred. Crying. I wanted to break down right then, but suddenly I felt the weight of Maria fall into my arms as she pounded on my chest while tears streamed down her face. I wanted to be strong for her…but as I stared at the image of a battered and unconscious David, my tears pelted her head. I already knew the suspects…and my inner hatred for them boiled inside of me. As I sat there weeping with Maria, I could only think one thing… God have Mercy on their souls. By God…Have Mercy on their souls. And as I heard the sounds of the ambulance and police sirens echo throughout the cold and deceitful night, only regret ran through my mind….regret that I hadn’t been there. A knot formed in my throat as I watched his covered body being loaded unto the ambulance. And as I reached down and picked up a small tear of the shirt David was wearing that was covered in blood, I was taken back unto something my father always told me… "A wise man pardons not an act of tyranny, but instead he reenacts the proper punishment." The Real Deal. Round One Opponent. Last year, my round one opponent came in the form of Hoyt Williams, who is now heralded as PTC Extreme champion and Universal Champion of my home federation, PRIME. I was made out to be the weak underling whom stood no chance against the massive legend. Polls across the country had the odds stacked against me, barely one vote to my name… But I triumphed. That triumph brought me instant recognition. I was hired by PRIME, placed in top tier matches with the likes of Karina Wolfenden, Angelo Deville, Nova, Aimz, Brandon Youngblood, Emilio Rage, Killean Sirrajin, and a host of others. This year, I stand in the opposite position. Now I am the favored, and you, Real Deal, are the underling. But the one thing that differs in the Vangelus Olsig of last year and the Real Deal of this year is that inner determination to succeed. You’re here for a prize… I’m here for a purpose. You may be in the same position that I was… But the outcome will be totally different. Because unlike the heralded Hoyt of last year I will triumph. And my triumph will be… The Real Deal. No pun intended.
