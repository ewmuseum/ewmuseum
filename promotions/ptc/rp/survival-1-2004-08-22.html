Survival #1

22 August 2004

Date: 2004-08-22 Our father, who art in heaven; hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us, and lead us not into temptation; but deliver us from evil. Amen. It’s morning and Asesino has made a stop. He has decided to stop at a catholic church and attend service before he goes into battle. We watch as Asesino receives the sacrament of the Eucharist and as the service comes to a close. The priest exits the church and people file out behind him but Asesino stays behind kneeling in a pew with his head on the pew ahead of him and his hands clasped together. Asesino is dressed in his Sunday best, a blue suit, though not the bright blue one that we’ve seen him in before, rather a dark blue suit that looks nicer then the bright blue one. Asesino seems to be preparing to pray and we listen in as he does, he’s words come out to us slightly muffled. Asesino: In the name of the Father the Son and the Holy Sprit Amen. Please God bless all those participating in the Survival PPV. Protect the combatants from serious injury and evil doings. Please God bless all the feds that face NGW, they're feds that have lost their way and live unfit lives, but protect them from injury nonetheless. Please God bless them, the men who claim to be beasts and those who respect no man, but does have families and beautiful little children, so please don’t let anything happen to them that would harm their families. Please God bless them, some of them seem to be unprepared for this matches and I fear for their healths, please protect them from serious injuries that can come from being unprepared. Please God bless them, I don’t know much about them besides the fact that they're in the match, and that is reason enough for me to pray for them. Please God bless them, some of them are great athletes and good men who I’m sure are praying for all of us too, don’t let anything unfortunate happen to such great competitors. Please God bless those who are living tough lives out on the streets and need your help to get back on their feet. Please God bless them, some of them are ignorant men who are cocky and self centered, please just make sure that they don’t get themselves in anymore trouble than they already have and get themselves hurt, please grant them a speedy exit from the match and a safe match so that everyone is better off. Please God bless every other wrestler on the PTC roster and their families although they may not see the light please help them to one day find it. Please bless DVD and my family wherever they might be . . . And God please give me the strength to make good on my promises, please give me the strength of the Holy Sprit while I battle these men in an attempt to give Team NGW the victory, please let this happen because it is the biding of the Assassin Fanatics, your servants and your Son’s followers. I pray to you Lord. In the name of the Father the Son and the Holy Sprit Amen. Asesino does the sign of the cross and gets up from his kneeling position then exits the church as the scene fades to blue. Our father, who art in heaven; hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us, and lead us not into temptation; but deliver us from evil. Amen. Lord Hear Our Pray Later... It’s a normal day on the sunny Asesino Cove. The birds are chirping and the fish are swimming. In fact The Assassin is swimming. Asesino is enjoying the warm summer afternoon by taking a dip on the glorious Reef with a snorkeling set. We see Asesino swim around with his head down for a while then finally he dives down into the deep the emerges walking up from the deep as jaws music hits. Takes you back doesn’t it? Asesino is wearing nothing accept an orange and blue pair of board shorts and smiling eyes. We notice that there is a little more muscle tone on Asesino’s body than we’re used to. Asesino grabs an NGW towel that ironically has an animated picture of John Hitchin on it. Asesino finds a suitable lava rock and takes a seat throwing his towel to the side. It looks as though the Assassin might talk then he literally jumps up as a man enters the frame. A skinny man with brown hair and an official looking NGW shirt approaches Asesino. The man looks familiar to us, although we aren’t sure why yet. Asesino: Shane? Shane is that you? Shane: Well it sure is Asesino, your number one fan and personal interviewer. Shane says this with a huge grin on his face. Asesino: Ah, come on man you left to presume other "career options" like a year ago. Shane: I know I did Asesino, but it didn’t work out so I’m here to ask for my old job back. Asesino: Well Shane I don’t really need an interviewer . . . I’m always up to helping an old friend out, so welcome back buddy. The two engage in a short embrace then Asesino sits back down on his rock and Shane sits on a smaller rock next to Asesino. Shane: Asesino last time you wrestled on different occassions during Death Wish 2004 in an attempt to win the Untamed Title, what are your thoughts on those matches and your performances? Asesino: Well Shane days ago I was part of the most trying and exhausting night of my career. Many different matches against many different men. I thought I was the Untamed Champion but the referee decided that the winner had to pick up the title and not just hold on to it but let's be honest Scottywood was completely against me he attacked me many times when he was suppossed to be the referee but that's ok because I'll get my hands on Scottywood soon but for now my next target is John Hitchin since the stipulations were that whoever held on to the Untamed Title the longest would get an Extreme Title shot against Hitchin and I was the longest Untamed Champion. Shane: I have to point out though Asesino, the finish of your match with Jeff James was controversial to say the least as you had the title on your hands before Jeff took it from you. Asesino: Well I have to figure that Scottywood had something to do with that ruling, I’m sure that Jeff will want to wrestle me again and I’m sure that I will beat him. Shane: And what about your opponents this week at PTC Survival? Asesino looks a little confused. Asesino: I have a match? Well so much for a nice little break. It’s okay though, All the other feds in PTC are basically a break. There are veterans that I have never wrestled, but I’m not afraid of them either. I’ve seen their matches and they’re average at best. We’ll beat them on Survival and then beat who ever they throw at me next. I’m ready to move on and look forward to John Hitchin. Shane: Thanks for your time. Asesino: No problem Shane, but like I said before, times have changed so could you only come around every now and then instead of well . . . always like before? Shane: Sure no problem Asesino. Asesino: And here’s some money and the number of the NGW front office, maybe they’ll give you a real job. Shane: Wow thanks Asesino! Asesino: Don’t mention it. Shane leaves as Asesino closes his eyes and lets the sun beat off his chest.
