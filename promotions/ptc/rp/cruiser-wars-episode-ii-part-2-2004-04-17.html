Cruiser Wars! - Episode II (Part 2)

17 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-17 We open the scene in the hallowed hallways of the XUW arena. We cannot be bothered to be more specific than "XUW arena," but rest assured that the hallways are indeed hallowed. Anyway, when we last left our hero, The Cockjockey, he had just delivered a stirring, intellectual discourse on the troubled state of equal rights and predjudice concerning midgets in today's world of professional wrestling. He condemned Coricidon's idea that a midget wrestler such as himself does not belong in a serious tournament. This was a point he found poignantly ironic, coming from a person whose gender and sexual orientation are generally down-trodden and undervalued by similar, ludicrous premonitions. Having gotten that off of his chest, and not having anything further to say, The Cockjockey excused himself from the Commissioner's office, seeking a few moments of quiet solitude and time to think. And what does a midget think about when he, or she, is alone, you may ask? He thinks of the simple things in life, of course. He thinks about his friends. What else is there to think about? The Cockjockey is sitting atop a wooden crate in a storage area backstage. It is quiet now. The buzzing of the fans, the hustle and bustle of the backstage technical crew, the idle chatter of various XUW superstars... all absent for the time being. The only noise is the hum of the flourescent lights, hanging high above the spot where our midget friend is sitting, a solemn drone to compliment his pensive reverie. In his lap rests a photo album, bearing the words "Boomer Sooners - The Memories" on the cover. With his tiny midget fingers, the Cockjockey opens the book and flips through the pages upon pages of photographs, documenting the wily escapades of himself and his friends... Fatbadger... JR... Cardboard Chase Wilson... the real Chase Wilson... Dave Kipfer, before he turned on his stablemates... Sakie... and finally, Saij, the one who first introduced him into XUW. The only one capable of understanding anything he said. Probably his best friend in the world. The Cockjockey's fevered page-turning suddenly comes to stop as his eyes come across a picture of him and Saij, laughing and high-fiving each other while JR appears to be running around in the background, with his pants down around his ankles and his hands glued to his ass. Saij is holding a tube of superglue, while the Cockjockey has a hold of JR's belt. He looks up from the album, and stares off into the distance. A single tear rolls down his cheek from his left eye, as he thinks back to January... when Saij's career and life was nearly destroyed, after he had been mercilessly shoved off the catwalk atop the Ultitron by the dastardly Ric Austin, and sent crashing through the stage below. The act put him in a coma, and his future remains in doubt. The Midget Wunderkind, eyes welling with tears at the thought of what happened to his friend, slams the album shut. Now is not the time to dwell on such things, he thinks to himself. There is a match to prepare for. A tournament to win. He must do everything he can to overcome the odds, and to make his fallen comrade proud. He must honor his friend. Losing anything else near and dear to him would be unbearable. The Cockjockey tucks the album under his right arm, and hops off the crate, heading down the hallway back to the Boomer Sooner locker room. As he turns the corner, he freezes at the sight before him. A few EMTs and policemen have flooded into the hallway. A sense of dread and fear seems to hang in the air, like a cold, dreary morning fog. With a worried look on his face, and a sinking feeling in his heart, The Cockjockey quickly strides toward the scene. Each step he takes adds to his growing trepidation, as he braces himself for the worst... He stops in front of the door to the Boomer Sooner locker room. The door is propped open. EMTs and police officers scurry in and out of the room. The Cockjockey steps into the locker room, and sees his friends, JR and Fatbadger, huddled together and sobbing. Something terrible has obviously happened, but The Cockjockey can't quite figure out what. Fatbadger catches the midget out of the corner of his eye, and slowly walks over to him. The Cockjockey looks up into the fat Wisconsinite's face, wet with tears, and starts to become misty-eyed himself, as Fatbadger breaks the tragic news... Fatbadger: Cockjockey... oh man... I don't know what to tell you little buddy... but... but... Fatbadger gets down on one knee, bringing his face level with that of The Cockjockey. He clasps a hand on his little midget shoulder, and looks him straight in the eye. Fatbager: Cardboard Chase... is... dead. The Cockjockey slumps to his knees, and pounds his fists into the ground, a la Charlton Heston from Planet of the Apes. He shakes his head wildly, refusing to believe it! It cannot be true! First Saij gets put in a coma, and now another one of his close friends is dead!? Impossible! How can this be!? This is too much to bear! Fatbadger: Dude, I'm so sorry... I don't know what drove him to do it... the cops are sayin' he took his own life... The Cockjockey, tears streaming down his face, looks up as a couple fellows from the coroner's office wheel a gurney through the locker room, with a sheet draped over what appears to be a very, very flat corpse. Coroner #1: Hey, are you sure this guy's really dead? Coroner #2: I dunno, maybe we oughta check. They lift the sheet off of the corpse, right in front of The Cockjockey. To his horror, and to the horror of everyone else, we see that Cardboard Chase's head has been completely cut off, with a pair of safety scissors!! Coroner #1: Well, he ain't movin'. Coroner #2: Yeah, he's dead alright. Coroner #1: Yep, I reckon you can't get more dead than that. Coroner #2: Right, let's get him out of here so we can chuck 'im in the incinerator. The bawling continues as The Cockjockey's deceased cardboard companion is quickly wheeled out of the locker room... and out of his life, forever. One of the police officers walks up to Fatbadger and The Cockjockey, holding a notepad and pen in hand. There is a slight hint of pity and sympathy on his face, but for the most part his is very business-like. Officer: Excuse me, gentlemen... I'm terribly sorry for your loss, but I need to ask you a few questions. The Cockjockey doesn't even look at the officer. Fatbadger manages to compose himself, however, and speaks. Officer: Thank you. Now, this is going to sound strange... but do either of you happen to be competing in any sort of... professional wrestling tournament, right now? Fatbadger and The Cockjockey both stare disbelievingly at the officer, their eyes wide and jaws hanging open. Fatbadger: What the hell kinda question is that!? Officer: Well sir, my department has been swamped by a recent string of deaths that occured under suspicious circumstances, and for some weird reason they all seem to be related so some sort of wrestling tournament... The officer narrows his eyes at the two Sooners. Officer: And if you ask me, I think there's foul play afoot! Fatbadger: That's a bunch of crap! Why would you think that!? Officer: Don't you think it's rather odd, Mister... what's your name? Fatbadger: Fatbadger! Officer: ... right, what's your REAL name? Fatbadger: Uh... Fatrick J. McBadgerson! Officer: ... whatever. Anyway, don't you think it's rather odd, Mr. McBadgerson, that there just happens to be a string of mysterious deaths occuring at the same time as this tournament? I've been informed by one of my colleagues in another city that a similar occurence... um... occurred... at a hotel where a Ms. Coricidon was staying. Do you not find it odd that it just happened to occur now, in the midst of whatever this tournament is? Furthermore, do you not find it even more odd, that these sort of overly-done, melodramatic events just happen to... well, happen... at precisely the same time as a huge professional wrestling event!? Fatbadger: Uh... you lost me at "...whatever." Officer: BAH! What I'm trying to say, Mr. McBadgerson, is that I have a hunch that these tragic deaths are NOT the result of an accident, or as the result of a suicide! I'm saying they were done on purpose, solely for the purpose of garnering attention, or ratings, or sympathy, or whatever the hell would drive some sicko with a hidden agenda to murder a close friend of theirs!! The Cockjockey looks indignant, and nearly jumps onto the officer's face, but Fatbadger restrains him. Fatbadger: Dude! Cockjockey! Calm down! The officer looks strangely at the midget. Officer: Oh-ho, so you're The Cockjockey! Mr. Jockey, I understand that you're involved in this tournament that Ms. Coricidon is in... in fact, you're her opponent, aren't you!? I see it now! Yes, it's all coming together! Mr. Wilson didn't commit suicide... he was murdered! Murdered by The Cockjockey, in an attempt to play one-upsmanship with his tournament opponent! That's what you did, Mr. Jockey, didn't you!? DIDN'T YOU!? The Cockjockey is speechless. Which isn't really anything new to begin with. Officer: I see you have nothing to say in your defense. In that case, I'm placing you under arrest, for the murder of Cardboard Chase Wilson!!! Despite the unadulterated absurdity of arresting somebody for murdering a piece of cardboard, The Cockjockey and Fatbadger get very worried looks on their faces. The officer takes out a pair of handcuffs, intending to arrest The Cockjockey and take him into custody. Fatbadger's mind races furiously. The camera zooms in on Fatbadger's face, and we see a sort of cartoonish "x-ray" of his skull. There is an image of a slug, slowly crawling along on a treadmill. The camera zooms back out. Fatbadger's face lights up, as if he has an idea! Fatbadger: Wait a second! Cockjockey didn't murder Chase, and I can prove it! The officer looks incredulously at Fatbadger, but nods, curious to see this "proof" that he has. Fatbadger quickly walks over to a closet, and opens it up. Inside, there is a large stack of Cardboard Chase Wilsons! Fatbadger takes one out, and brings it back to the officer with him. Fatbadger: See? Cardboard Chase is alive! Nobody killed him at all! The officer, to say the least, is flabbergasted. Officer: What the!? That's impossible! None of this makes any sense! JR, who had been standing in the background this whole time, speaks up. JR: By God, of course it doesn't make any sense! This is the Boomer Sooner locker room! It's like a black hole of nonsense! Now you realize what ol' JR has to live through every day with these boys! The officer looks blankly around the room for a second. Officer: Well... come to think of it... it does seem rather silly to be investigating the death of a piece of cardboard. I'm sorry to have wasted your time, gentlemen. Have a good evening. The officer quickly exits the room, and the Sooners are alone once again. JR: By God, that was strange. Ol' JR is confused as hell. Fatbadger: Yeah, I mean... one minute we were crying about Chase being dead, and now he's alive again... in a manner of speaking. Fatbadger, JR, and The Cockjockey all stand silently, looking nervously around the room, and not quite knowing what to say next. Thus making this a perfect spot to fade to black.
