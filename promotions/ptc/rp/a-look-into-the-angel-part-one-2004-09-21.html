A look into the Angel >Part One

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 {Begin Scene} Arch Angel laid peacefully on his couch as he fell into a deep state of dreamland. Flashes of Angel’s childhood flew through his head, playing back through his mind like a movie being fast-forwarded. Freezing for moments at memories that had a great impact on Angel’s life. Sixteenth birthday, first kiss, parents car accident, graduating high school, and then his mind fizzled into a white screen with nothing to be seen. Suddenly Angel has flashbacks of his days at the NYPD training academy. Back when there was no Arch Angel only a cadet officer by the name of Michael Staton. “So Staton, you think your ready for this?” A man dressed in medals and a black wand in his right hand questioned. Michael smirked as he replied, “I was born for this sir!” The man that Michael remembered as his sergeant shouted, “GET YOUR ASS THROUGH THAT COURSE STATON!” Once again Michael’s mind faded to white as he saw himself sprinting towards the SWAT training course. Images once again flew through Michael’s mind back and fourth as if trying to find a bookmarked page. Finally halting at one of the most important days of Michael’s life, the day his life would change drastically. “So,” said Michael. “What are we looking at?” Nobody cared how they came to their conclusion – SWAT only moved if they were ninety-five percent sure of the facts. They knew which building to hit, and were a block away. Sector seventeen was a residential sector of detached houses and bungalows owned by people who got paid more than most but not enough to stop working; middle management, lawyers, doctors and the like. It was a quiet sector by New York standards, though the high rises of Manhattan Island were easily visible on the eastern horizon. As New York had expanded and expanded, individual street names had been lost to a new sector scheme and widespread use of GPS systems. The businesses and residential areas were divided into sectors: Middle class homes in sector seventeen; strip clubs, bars, night clubs and similar enterprises in sector seven, though it had become known as sector Heaven; the legal firms were all found, funnily enough, in sector thirteen, but there had been fierce litigation in the courts since the implementation of the sector scheme for changing it to sector twelve-b. After more than a century, the sector was still officially listed as number thirteen. But sector seventeen was a peaceful, content, out of the way, uncontroversial area built specifically for rich people who wished they were richer. And Michael hated it. The traffic was still shit, but it was still too goody-goody, even in the shitty rain that was still hammering down. He was going to enjoy fucking it up for the locals. “It looks like they are likely to have automatic shotguns,” said John, Michael’s best friend and partner since SWAT cadet academy. “Assault rifles and small arms go without saying, and there is a possibility that they have one automatic machine gun.” “House diagrams?” “Here.” They studied the blue prints, visualizing the space as they had been trained to do. Michael then put his own body armor on and selected an automatic shotgun from the rack in the back of the APC. He slung it across his back and then pulled his pistol and badge out and stepped out of the van. He and the SWAT team marched up the front lawn and waited while Michael knocked on the door. A young boy answered the door. “Yes?” “Watch out, kid,” Michael said. “NYPD. We're coming into your back garden.” The boy stared at the badge and then at the assortment of guns on ready display, and stood back mutely as Michael led his men through the house. The boy's mother shrieked once, briefly, when she saw the intruders, but Michael flashed his badge and paid her no further attention as he opened the back door and marched to the end of the garden. John looked carefully through a small periscope. “Curtains are all closed.” “Gimme a boost,” said Michael, and was helped over the fence. On the other side he unslung his automatic shotgun and moved towards the house in a low, quiet crouch. The other SWAT officers followed. They lined up, looking at the back door and the curtains pulled neatly over the glass upper half. John fitted small demolition packs to the handle and the dead locks and stood back, holding a sub-machine gun and the detonator. Michael nodded. John pressed the button. The relative stillness of the sector was split by three sharp and, above all, loud cracks which blasted half the door out of existence and smashed the other half back on its hinges with another loud bang. Michael was first in, shotgun to his shoulder, the SWAT team close behind. The kitchen was clear but for a few bundles of money, some bags of drugs and a couple of guns, so they moved quickly through into the lounge. And [COLOR=Cyan]froze...[/COLOR=Cyan] Arch Angel lunged up in a cold sweat breaking out of his deep dream state. Wiping the sweat from his forehead Angel stood up and gave a huge yawn followed by a couple stretches. Shaking his head a little Angel headed into the kitchen to grab a quick snack. “Man, what was that all about? Why now when I need my head straight for GTT4 these dreams come back!” Angel questioned as he rustled through the refrigerator. Finding a container of yogurt Angel grabs a spoon and sits at the kitchen counter enjoying his snack. A few moments later Rachel, Angel’s fiancée, walks through the front door and into the kitchen. Setting a couple grocery bags on the counter Rachel pecks Angel on the cheek then rest on the stool by her feet. Rachel took a drink from Angel’s glass of water as she spoke softly, “So you proved me wrong and won your first round match-up now what?” “Now it gets serious.” Angel firmly stated. “Just don’t go and get yourself hurt Angel. I’m sorry I doubted you but I’m just worried, ya know?” Rachel questioned. Angel shook his head a little, “Totally but you got to understand that I know what I’m doing and nothing will stop me.” Angel tossed his finished container of yogurt into the trash and set the spoon in the dishwasher. “So who is your next match against?” Rachel wondered. Smirking a little Angel replied, [COLOR=Red]“Johnny “Blaze” Medina[/COLOR=Red]. Some bum marine from “the streets of LA” as he puts it. He has skill just runs off the mouth a lot if you know what I mean.” “Oh, well I have to run to my moms for a little bit what time does your flight leave for the tournaments next round?” asked Rachel. Angel shrugged, “Like two or three hours I think, Ill probably be gone before you get back.” Rachel gave Angel a big hug and kiss on the cheek before she walked out the door. “Good luck babe, go get em!” Rachel exclaimed. “You know that’s what I do. See you later dear.” Angel laughingly said. {End Scene}
