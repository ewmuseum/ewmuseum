TTD - New Royal Legion -v- Metallo Zords

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Remodelling the PTC – New Royal Legion Style The New Royal Legion, XUWs most decorated tag team came to the Tag Title Division last week claiming that there was a conspiracy against them and their Legionnaires. This conspiracy has been plaguing The New Royal Legion since their early days in XUW when they were starting out as a tag team and even goes further back into the days of the original Royal Legion when it was comprised of a team of Bailey and Raven X. In fact the conspiracy just turned two years old this past month as XUW just showcased the 3rd Reborn PPV and it was on the first Reborn when the conspiracy first reared its ugly head, as the Royal Legion were screwed out of the tag team titles. 2 years and counting now that Bailey has been dealing with this and almost two years for his tag team partner Danyon, as it has been 20 months since the New Royal Legion debuted. 2 years and counting now that the conspiracy through many different plots and ideas has been trying to keep the greatest tag team ever down and never reach their potential. And it has been 2 very long years of fighting and defeating conspiracy theories and thinking that it is over with, it pops back up again when they least expect it. The conspiracy did have one benefit, however, in that it was a cause for some fans to rally around as the New Royal Legion continued their pursuit in eliminating the conspiracy once and for all. These fans that started to rally behind the New Royal Legion soon gave themselves a name, they called themselves Legionnaires, apparently unaware it is a potentially fatal disease one gets from drinking stale water. Still, the Legionnaires, lead by the first Legionnaire ever, Larry, started to group and organize into much more than a regular fan club and before they knew it they were a million strong. Furthermore, the Legionnaires became fanatical and obsessive in their loyalty to cheering on the New Royal Legion leading many outsiders to the club observing for the first time describing them as a cult. It was this conspiracy that led Bailey and Danyon to the Tag Team Division in the PTC and the PTC in general. Before Larry’s debut match in the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament, the conspiracy had only affected The New Royal Legion in XUW and had never beyond the federation. However, after Larry inexplicably lost in the Cruiserweight Tournament against Horazon in the most controversial decision on the first round Bailey soon realized that the conspiracy had spread and infected the PTC as well. This belief in the conspiracy getting involved in the PTC was even furthered by the fact that originally the New Royal Legion were not entered in the Tag Team Division, even though they are generally considered the greatest tag team ever. Once, they were allowed to enter the odds were definitely against them from winning the division and having a chance at winning the title. They were at a severe disadvantage in the way the point scheme worked for victories and losses and since they were not involved in the first round of matches, they were started from scratch. The New Royal Legion were almost mathematically eliminated from the championship match even before they had a chance to wrestle their first match. The Detroit Tigers probably had a better shot at winning the pennant or the Cincinnati Bengals of winning the Super Bowl than the New Royal Legion had of winning the championship match. To top all of this off, The New Royal Legion’s first match was against the pre-tournament favourites, the Souls of the Black Lotus. The odds were definitely stacked against Bailey and Danyon but that didn’t mean they were giving up. The odds have always been stacked against them with the conspiracy but they would continue to fight on and try and defeat the conspiracy. And fight on they did, the New Royal Legion, much to disbelief of the pundits that picked the Souls of the Black Lotus, the New Royal Legion won in a hard fought battle. With the win The New Royal Legion still had a slim, albeit a very, very slim chance of advancing to the final match but it was still there for them to grasp. In order to do that, they’d need a little luck on their side in defeating the conspiracy and having certain teams win and luck is not necessarily something that is has been the New Royal Legion’s side in history. *** The scene fades back into the most historical hotel in all of XUWville, the Howard Johnson Hotel, which is the legendary home of the New Royal Legion and their Legionnaires. Outside of the hotel, like the hundreds of other times scenes of faded into here are the Legionnaires cheerfully wandering around with large smiles on their faces since the New Royal Legion won their first match in the PTC Tag Title Division. This was also their first match that they have won since October and the first match they have fought since November due to their abductions in December. And the last reason, but certainly not the least reason, the Legionnaires were happy was that Danyon was back and won in his first return to the ring since December. So, the Legionnaires had quite a few reasons to be happy this fine morning. It would take a lot to dampen their spirits at this point in time. Like always, the Legionnaires had faith in the New Royal Legion and their ability to pull off another major upset and advance to the finals of the tag title division for the championship match. Of course, to the Legionnaires, the New Royal Legion would always have a shot at winning and are of course very god like. Since it was still a little early in the morning, not all of the Legionnaires outside in the hotel’s parking lot were up. Some were still sleeping as the victory party from last weeks match against the Souls of the Black Lotus had gone on for almost a week now and the Legionnaires were recovering and sleeping off the hangover. There were still many up but they were wandering around or cooking breakfast or making some signs but there were no campfire sing-a-longs today, at least not yet. In other words, this was one of the few times that the Legionnaires weren’t that exciting to watch............. So, the scene slowly cuts away from the Legionnaires and up into the 15th Floor of the Howard Johnson Hotel where the New Royal Legion Suite was located. However, as the scene fades into the suite there is something different today about this. Something drastically different. Something that has never probably been known to exist. The New Royal Legion Suite was empty. Bailey and Danyon were not in what is essentially considered their home now. This is almost unthinkable or unimaginable, the New Royal Legion are always located in their suite. They are rarely ever seen anywhere else. However, upon closer inspection, the room itself looks a little different than the other times. There is a large tarp over one wall, a ladder is located in one corner and a very large table saw as well located in the room. Actually, the room itself is bare from all except for these tools. There is no bed, no tables, no chairs, nothing. Although, this somewhat makes sense seeing that since Danyon returned to action in XUW a few weeks ago, that meant there would be 4 people staying in the suite but only 3 beds. So, it looks as if the New Royal Legion Suite is going under a being remodelling for the time being, which is what they were contemplating doing the last time they were seen. But that begs the question, if the New Royal Legion are not in their suite, where are they? The scene fades out of the construction zone and to a door of a room that says, “1403 – The Presidential Suite.” Standing in front of the door is the greatest tag team wrestler in XUW history, Bailey, with a key in one hand and a suitcase on his other one. Beside him is his tag team partner, the most famous wrestler ever to hail from New Zealand, Danyon who is also carrying one suitcase. Beside Danyon, is none other than one of the all time fan favourites and legendary wrestlers there is. He is now an honourary Legionnaire but is still considered a living legend given his previous success in the now defunct GIWF. Standing beside them with a case of beer under each arm is the whiskey warrior, the pungent pugilist, the flatulent fighter, the smelly superstar, the one, the only, “The Bastard” Al Crud. And located behind Al with about 3 suitcases on each arm is the first Legionnaire ever, their lawyer, Larry the Legionnaire. Bailey takes the key and opens the door and walks in with his one suitcase and takes a look around at the Presidential Suite. Danyon closely follows him in and they both stop in front of the King Size bed. Al Crud walks in with his two cases of beer and places them on the ground. Poor Larry has to be downwind of Al Crud, which no one wants to be given Al’s history of gas problems, but he follows him in or at least he tries to follow him in. He struggles with the 3 suitcases under each arm and keeps dropping one as he takes a step forward. Bailey and Danyon look at each other and shake their heads. Bailey: Larry, get in here will you and explain this. Larry drops all the suitcases and takes a few steps further into the room. Larry: What is it? Bailey: Explain this? Larry looks at the King Size Bed. Larry: It’s a King Size Bed. What’s the big deal? Bailey: The big deal? Do you see any other bed in this room? Larry pans around the room and then he realizes that indeed there is only one bed in this room. Danyon: Why is there only 1 bed in this room? Larry: I don’t know. There’s a couch over there in the corner. There could be a hide away bed in that. Danyon: There better be cause 4 of us aren’t sleeping in that one bed. Danyon walks over to the couch and lifts up a cushion and tosses it back down hard on the couch. Danyon: There is no bed under there. How are the 4 of us going to share that one bed? Larry: Well……..umm………Al could sleep on couch and I could sleep on the floor. We’ll have to make do while the room upstairs gets renovated. Bailey: Make do? We have a major match coming up against the Metallo Zords and now we have to worry about sleeping arrangements. It’s bad enough that we have to fight off the conspiracy against us in the PTC let alone worry about who is sleeping where. I thought you said that we were getting the next best suite in the hotel. Larry: This is the next best suite in the hotel. This is a Howard Johnson Hotel, they aren’t exactly the Four Seasons you know. Al Crud: But they are better than the Motel 6’s that I normally would stay at. Danyon: Do you mind sleeping on the couch Al, since Bailey and I have the match soon we should get the bed? Al Crud farts as he rubs his hand through his greasy hair. Al Crud: I don’t mind. I’ll probably end up passing out in the bathroom anyways like most nites. I’m more worried about the fact that there is no fridge to keep my beer cold. I might have to fill up the bathtub with ice to keep it cold. Bailey: Larry, are you sure there are no other rooms available in this hotel? Larry: No, there isn’t Bailey. It’s all booked up. This is the best suite next to ours. Bailey: We’ve only been living here for the past 20 months and they can’t get us a better room than this. This is a conspiracy is what it is. I bet the conspirators are involved in this somehow. First, Larry loses in the Cruiserweight Tournament…………… Danyon: You know you never did explain to me how he really lost that match. Al Crud: I farted, that’s what happened. Danyon: What? How does that cause a loss? Larry: Apparently, the ref of the match was unaccustomed to smell of Al’s farts and Al happened to let a big one rip when I was pinning Horazon. Well, just as he was coming down to hit the mat for the 3 count the ref stopped inches from the ground and sniffed the air and started to sway a bit from the smell of Al’s fart. Al and Bailey were at ringside and the smell drifted into the ring and distracted the ref. So, instead of continuing on the three count after the ref regained his composure he started the whole count over and Horazon kicked out at 2. Then he went on to defeat me a little later even though I had clearly won the match at that point in time. Danyon: That’s what happened? Larry: Yeah, that’s what happened. Danyon: Damn, no wonder you guys were upset. Bailey: See, clearly the ref was a part of the conspiracy and only stopped and started the count over on purpose to screw us over. Of course that was only the start of it as then they went on to practically ruin our chances of becoming tag team champs because we weren’t allowed into the division initially. Then once we do win a match, they screw us over by infiltrating the hotel and booking us in this room. It can’t get any worse. Larry: I hardly doubt the conspirators were able to infiltrate this hotel and book us in this room to hurt your chances of winning the PTC Tag Titles. This hotel only employs Legionnaires……….. Bailey: Which means that they have infiltrated Legionnaires……….. Larry: I don’t think that is possible………. Danyon: I’m with Larry on this one Bailey. I think we are over reacting a little here. Although, this room is clearly not as nice as our suite upstairs, it’s not all that bad of a room. We’ve stayed in worse. Remember when you brought us up to Canada and we had to live in those igloos for a week. That was way worse than this and we still won then. We can win even if…….gulp……..we have to share a bed together. Bailey takes a deep breath and lets it out. Bailey: You are right. I’m just probably being a little too paranoid right now. I’m just a little frustrated right now that’s all. We have a lot going on. I have my IC Title in XUW to worry about. We have this PTC Tag Team Division to worry about. We of course have the conspiracy to worry about like always. I just didn’t want to have to deal with staying in an average hotel suite. I guess, I just thought that the conspiracy wouldn’t show its face as we seemingly hadn’t had to deal with it for a few months. But after Larry lost his match in the Cruiserweight Tournament I knew it was back. I thought we could have come to the PTC and proved just how great we really are and not worry about the conspiracy but now it looks like we have to deal with the whole thing again. Danyon: When I returned from my abduction, I didn’t want to deal with the conspiracy anymore than you do or Larry does or even Al Crud does. We showed them in our match against the Souls of the Black Lotus what exactly we were made of. We increased our odds of winning this thing slightly but we will just have to go out there and prove to everyone once again exactly how great we really are. We didn’t become the most legendary tag team by backing down to any fight. We always went out there and won the matches and fought down that conspiracy. I guess it is time we have to do it here in the PTC as well. As long as we get passed the biasness of the conspiracy we can win against the Metallo Zords and bring back the PTC Tag Team Titles to XUW. After that room gets remodelled upstairs we will have more room to put up all of the gold we won. Not only is our suite getting remodelled but the PTC as a whole is getting renovated and remodelled now that we are here to eliminate the conspiracy. The Souls of the Black Lotus said it themselves that they had never faced a team like us before and soon the Metallo Zords will realize the same thing. There is no other team like the New Royal Legion in the world. No other team can legitimately claim to have one million fans worshipping them and cheering them on no matter what. There is nothing like us and that’s why we’ll win. Bailey: I know. I know. I’m just sick and tired because I’ve been battling this damn conspiracy for two long years now. Danyon: Bailey, when we win the PTC Tag Titles, there won’t be much left for them to do to keep us down. Bailey: I’m sure somehow they’ll find something. They always do. But to make sure they don’t try to do the same thing twice. Al, I’d really appreciate it if you would stay in the back this time in the locker room area. I don’t want any smell coming from you distracting the referee. Al Crud burps. Al Crud: I’ll do that. Just give me a case of beer and I’ll gladly stay behind. Bailey: Sounds good to me. Danyon: Alright, let’s unpack our stuff now. Larry, get to work and bring in our suitcases and unpack our stuff. Bailey and I have to rest up for our match. Larry: I have to unpack everything by myself. Danyon: You don’t want us pulling a muscle or something now do you? Larry: No, I guess not. Danyon: Good, now get to work. Larry shakes his head and walks back over to the suitcases he dropped at the foot of the door and starts bringing them into the room. Bailey hops on the bed and Danyon pulls up a chair and puts his feet up. Al Crud rips open his case of beer and pulls one out and the scene slowly fades out of the temporary home of Bailey, Danyon, Larry and Al Crud.
