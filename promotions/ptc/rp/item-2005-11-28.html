Date: 2005-11-28 "Where is it?" Rudolph shouts from the other room. "Where is what?" Christian Montiero replies, glancing at the closed door to Polenski’s room from the couch. "That damn item!" Polenski shouts, seeming frustrated. "What item?" Montiero seems completely lost. The pair have a remaining three days to pack the contents of their ramshackle Auburn Hills apartment. And although the majority of Polenski’s stuff has already been packed in preparation, it’s the small and simple things which still need to be thrown into a box. Montiero’s packing, however, is a different story. Seated in front of the television with a large bucket of popcorn, both his first round match against Bryan Arson and his rigorous packing tasks seem to be the last thing on his mind. "You know exactly which item I’m talking about." Polenski now seems infuriated, with his tone of voice implying he knows where the item has gone. "No, I don’t know exactly which item you’re talking about." Montiero mimics Polenski’s angry tone, shovelling a handful of popcorn into his mouth. "Is it something to do with narcotics? Are you a drug dealer, Rudolph?" Montiero pauses momentarily. "Am I going to hear your name on ESPN radio tomorrow?" Montiero briefly laughs at himself as he swoops another pile of popcorn from the bucket. The door to Polenski’s room bursts open. "Seriously Christian, I’m not in the mood." Polenski appears to have calmed down slightly, if not for the sake of finding his missing item. "If you know where it is, please return my item." "Why the hell do you keep calling it your item?" Montiero asks, somewhat perplexed. "If I knew what your item was, then I’d probably be able to help you." "Well…" Polenski glances at the ground. "It’s a little embarrassing, this item." "Is it big?" Montiero enquires. "Not quite," Polenski says uncomfortably. "Is it electronic?" "Not quite." "Is it something used in an event of sexual orientation?" Montiero looks at Rudolph’s face, which has now turned bright red. His eyes wander the room as a broad smile slowly emerges across Montiero’s face. Polenski responds in as feint a tone as possible. "…possibly." Montiero bursts out laughing, the popcorn bowl falling from his lap to the floor. "See…" Polenski attempts to explain, his voice now much quieter than before. "I’ve had this item for…quite some time now…" He glances at Montiero, who has now fallen onto the spilled popcorn. "PPPPPPPOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORRRRRRRRRRRRRRRNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN!" Montiero can’t help but shout. "It’s not pornography!" Polenski detests. Montiero’s laughing now grows more intense. "LLLLLLLLLUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUBBBBBBBBBBBEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!" Montiero’s face is now turning a purple colour. "It’s not anything directly sexual, you idiot!" Polenski is now infuriated with Montiero’s immaturity. "It’s…a rather precious item. And…well, I enjoy watching it from time to time…" Polenski is interrupted by Montiero, who is just shaking off his laughing fit. "It’s okay, Rudolph. You don’t need to explain." Montiero picks himself off up the floor, brushes the fragments of popcorn from his shirt and vanishes into the other room. "By the way, Christian." Polenski talks as Montiero leaves the room. "An important tape’s arriving by courier tomorrow. I’ll be out of the house, but I need you to sign it and watch it." "Is it something for your new spice rack?" Montiero asks. "No, nothing like that." Polenski replies, seeming puzzled over Montiero’s remark. "It’s a tape I acquired from [name withheld] which was found a while back. It’s about Bryan Arson. He seemed a little eager to send it, as it happens." "Sounds good," Montiero says. "I’ll be sure to watch it over some spicy food." "You…do that," Polenski replies, again seeming confused as to what Montiero is saying. Montiero emerges from the other room, a smug grin on his face. "I’m not even going to ask where you inquired about that tape, or why Lanier was eager to release it to you," Montiero starts. "But while we’re on the subject of releasing tapes, I’ve found it." An uneasy smile comes across Polenski’s face. "I’ve found your wacky item, pun intended." Montiero hands Polenski a video tape from behind his back.
