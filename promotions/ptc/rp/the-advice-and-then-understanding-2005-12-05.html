The advice and then understanding!

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Earlier in the week A slow and silent falling snow can be seen as a large man is seen chopping wood with a double bladed axe. Slowly and methodically each blow comes down hitting the wood and splitting each piece as he turns and throws it onto a sled being pulled by a massive draft horse. Another large man walks forward and places a hand on this mans shoulder. The other large man is slightly smaller than the man swinging the axe, But is just as stout and strong looking. "Ho little brother for you are overworking yourself before a great challenge. You should be preparing for war not chopping wood. That is the stable lads jobs. This is a grand day for celebration and feasting. Come back to the great hall and join us for we will celebrate like our ancestors of yore!" The larger man waves the other man off as he turns and tosses more chopped wood upon the sled and then turns grabbing the reigns of the draft horse and begins to walk him through a grove of chopped trees as the two large men begin to walk side by side. "Little brother you are quiet. Does this stranger who has come to our village disturb you? He seems rather hearty and serious, But seems to know a lot about you? Is he another warrior from another tribe?" Turning his head the larger man speaks. "In a manner of speaking big brother. I have invited him here even though he despises me. He likes very little about me, but from one warrior to another he knows what is in store for me in this thing called GTT% and this thing the WWA calls Birthday Brawl." Frowning the smaller man turns and shakes his head. "Jorge you are a massive mighty Viking warrior no advice is needed. You can handle three men in a tag team match while this von Doom guy tries to insult you. Damn man a Mexican Viking he is almost too funny. Guess he isn’t intelligent enough to realize that your name pronunciation is YorG that is how our people say it not Whore Hey!" Samuelsson looks at the smaller man and speaks to him as if he is a child. "Understand this Erik von Doom is a good opponent, But lacks the ability to speak on the terms most people want to comprehend.he is a braggart and a pathetic waste of human gene. He has to use other people to carry him through a conversation. He is a champion only because he has to use underhanded tactics to remain that way. He is more like a politician than a warrior always hoping someone will laydown and give him his chance." The smaller man looks over at Jorge as he leads the draft horse to a barn next to a grand hall much similar to old Viking archeology. They both enter as a man sits before a blazing fire in robes unseen as he sits mysteriously there unmoving and raises his head slightly and acknowledges Jorges presence. "About time Samuelsson I was beginning t wonder if giving you advice was worth my effort. You and I still have business to attend to." Samuelsson turns and walks by the cloaked man and sits in a large furred throne of sorts overlooking the earthen fire hearth that lays before him with a large blazing fire. "You are here at my behest and my graces. Do not abuse my gracious offer of invitation" Chuckling the cloaked man laughs louder. "Samuelsson you are funny. You would never think a peon like yourself would actually have the gall to think me unable of finding you and handling business whenever and wherever I deem fit. I have more resources than you and I have many ways of getting things done. Remember that well and your lessons will be easier as they come." Groaning Samuelsson sits back and speaks. "The reason I invited you here is to speak on terms of war. This GTT5 match has me a bit concerned. I am wondering what you have learned in your many quests to do with this type of match up?’ The cloaked man crosses his arms and stands towering over the smaller man Erik as Erik looks over in awe. "You crushed Shaman in a match up and he was a fine warrior and established warrior, but you did not kill him nor finish the job correctly. He will comeback to haunt you in time." Samuelsson looks at the cloaked man. "That is not either here nor there. I asked for advice on GTT5 this idiot named von Doom, The man who tries to insult and gets nothing but empty emotions for his effort. He seems serious about facing me. He thinks I am an easy amrk for him to get to the next level. I am nothing but dangerous to this man who is ignoring all aspects of the danger he has walked into. I am not impressed with this man or his UWF Heavyweight title belt. It is just leather and tin. He is nothing, but an annoyance." The cloaked man chuckles as he raises his hands and holds them outwards as his hands for the first time ever revealed seem strong and weather very strong to the naked eye. This man has experience and Jorge knows this. "You seem to underestimate this mans possible abilities. He has talent and skills. Being a braggart and a man who is over confident can be a dangerous combination to this situation. You would do well to take heed to your matches before hand. He comes quick for you and you must battle this man with ferocity and do not lead into anger for it may be your main weakness." The cloaked man turns and stands as he walks away out of the great hall as Samuelsson stands insulted. "Where are you going I did not finish this conversation!" The cloaked man stops and raises a hand and turns his hooded head. "That is your mistake Samuelsson you overstep your bounds. Stay within your means and you will see victory. If you progress beyond them you will only find your own defeat at a man who seems to know not what he is doing." Samuelsson sits back down and looks at the man as he leaves and he turns to Erik. "Get the vessel I wish to cross the lake and return to the land of men. I must walk among them to understand this von Doom and what type of man he is going to be. I must know my opponent first hand and will be the one I wish to study in all aspects. I must start my travels again to become enlightened. I must go to Las Vegas Nevada and start in the beginning." Standing Samuelsson heads for the door to the great hall as Erik follows him out as the camera focuses into the fire and begins to blind and flash the screen white…. Latter part of the week. ….The camera pans back in through the reflecting sun off the bright glassy constructs called casinos. They are much like conquering warriors in an unforgiving landscape. In the desert it is an oasis of sin and hunger. Greed and sloth are the makers here as men and women both come for pleasures of the flesh and the wallet. Some come and leave empty handed. Not today one man comes and receives and does not leave empty handed. This man is Jorge Samuelsson the one who faces James von Doom. The man who wants to hurt James von Doom and stop his run through the GTT5. Samuelsson stands in a police department with a folder as he grins trying to collect more info on James von Doom. "You can learn a lot about an opponent from their records and life history. According to police he has no history. Yet how come I believe there is something much deeper than this. The people I have spoken to have given me some insight about his childhood." Turning Samuelsson raises his hand and stares out across the desert. "In a land where only the strong will survive he survived by being silent and weird, Tortured and battered, The kid who was being bullied and becomes the bully, Very strange in deed. Where did your drive to become a wrestler come from I wonder. At age nineteen there is a significant portion of life yet to come and you lose it in the shuffle of the wrestling business. Being drawn into a wedding on national television and hitting a woman with a chair regardless of her stature. This is not a man who bears confidence and honor. Not even a man of pride and self confidence. It is the side of a man who is a coward. Yet you tend to try and insult me. Your mark was well off as mine is on target little man of Las Vegas." Turning he begins to walk in the streets of Las Vegas as many men and women avoid this large man and simply stare at him as they walk past. He turns his head occasionally to glare down at those he considers lesser being for being where they are. "This disgusting scar on the face of the earth should beburned.. It should be raised and pillaged and destroyed. No wonder it produced a weakling like you von Doom on the 9th day of the 3rd month of the year 1983. Pathetic little cowering boy who could not fight back against bullies finally breaks free and holds the world in his grasp and all he can do is insult a man who is not caring about the words said just actions. You’re leaving your destiny in my hands von Doom. All of your misgivings and your self doubts will come to fruition against me von Doom. Have you ever tasted the air after you have been knocked out. It is salt and rather stale. It happens because of the air still in your lungs and the sweat of exertion. I will guarantee there is something that will accompany this taste. It is the taste of blood as I break bones in your body and tear muscles from them. I will extend your joints far beyond that of tolerance. You are going to learn what it really is to feel pain von Doom. Your pathetic ramblings about believing and truth comes to an end the evening we face." Standing watching the road before him bustle with life and cars go screaming by with little notice of him as he grins sadistically. Turning his head he watches women in lavish outfits run across the street to a casino and he then turns his gaze back forwards and begins walking on as he shakes his head. "Pathetic isn’t it von Doom. You come from this placed that is nothing but hedonism at its finest and yet you think you are the greatest being ever with no flaws. Your biggest flaw is simple. You are who you are and you can never change it by hiding behind the many facets of the world you live in. You’re trying to fight a hard fight and be violent when all you are showing is a child throwing a tantrum and getting nowhere." Clearing his throat he stops and places his hand upon his forehead. "Perhaps I am looking at this all wrong. You’re looking past me von Doom that is mistake number one. Open your eyes little man to the man who stands before you, the man who does not fear you either. You thin you can so easily defeat me." Samuelsson simply shakes his head as he looks down the road to the desert. "Your words have fallen so you have failed there to get my goat as you Americans would say. I am not the man you should veer take lightly. I am not this man who is going to laydown and let you pass. I am not the man who is going to allow you to get under his skin von Doom. I don’t care about anything especially you. Get with the program get a new act and face me when your world comes full circle. Open your eyes von Doom for the reality is staring you in the face. Someone is going to hurt you and enjoy themselves doing it." Samuelsson stops as he notices the sun beginning to go down over the mountains in the distance as he turns and faces away from the sun as the sunlight causes him to be a shadowy outline. He speaks slowly as he raises his hands. "Glorious is the world I live in." Lowering his head he stares at the camera his eyes only thing being clearly seen. "Shamed is the one you live in von Doom." Laughing loudly. "Glorious is the world for battle" All emotion leaving his face as he leans down again. "Shamed is the world you breathe in." Standing straight and proud as he watches the sun fading. "Glorious is the world where I cause your blood to flow and spread across the ground unstoppable. Glorious is the world where I can conquer a lowly man who beats upon women and hides behind many faces and a has a man named the Mathematician as his best friend. Glorious is the world where a Viking can return and laugh at a man who is nothing but a child acting out his fantasies of being a bully. Glorious is the world I can conquer with pathetic weakling like yourself to try and stop me." Samuelsson stops and looks around as he cracks his neck back and forth. "You wanted my undivided attention von Doom you have gotten it and I will do what I need to do to make sure you go no further. Your world is going to be full of heartache and despair. Much like your childhood was. Go back to your sci fi and books boy for the son of the easier has decided you are not worthy to breath the same air as he does. You are nothing but a minor nuisance in my plans. Once I am past you I will look onto my next opponent. I will come fighting and in the end I will take my victory and go forward to the next battle as I tread on your corpse and wipe the bottom of my feet on your bloodied face." Samuelsson turns and walks away as he begins speaking of the glorious reasons he is going to enjoy hurting von Doom. "Glorious are the punches upon von Doom’s head. Glorious will be the deep struggling breaths von Doom must take through his shattered ribs and blood filled lungs. Glorious are the reasons I will enjoy taunting him with his defeat. Glorious are the reasons von Doom has become the lowly gnat he is. Glorious are the reasons I will show the world how truly pathetic von Doom is." Samuelsson disappears as the lights in Las Vegas come on and the streets and nightlife begin to come to life.
