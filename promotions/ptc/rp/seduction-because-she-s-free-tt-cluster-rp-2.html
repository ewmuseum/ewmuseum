Adriana Milagros Affiliated Pimp Joined: 01 Apr 2003 Posts: 6 Location: Home Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 3:32 pm Post subject: [Seduction]Because She's Free(TT Cluster rp 2) The mid-afternoon sun burned down on the runner's trail of Diamond Head in beach in the southern end Waikiki. Her hearing covered by a pair of Sony headphones, she runs down the trail in total ignorance to the world around her. All she sees is the pavement under her feet, and the road ahead. All she hears is her own thoughts and the mainstream media flowing through her Discman into her ears. She's not worrying about what she is going to wear to work tomorrow, or tonight. She's not worried if that guy she met last night is going to call her like he promised. To her all that is trivial. She only has one thing on her mind...run. She has no time for games. She has no time to worry. She's free out there in road. She's free to run wherever she may roam. Only the black pavement of the trail lies up ahead of her, but it would be for her to turn where she sees fit. Thus she continues to run on and on, with only one goal in mind: Go Forward. Others join the trail alongside her. They all range from teenagers to adults desperately trying to regain whatever form they had. Woman, and men of all ages each running besides her and behind her, Woman worrying about how fat they think they look running harder. Men trying to retain their physique so that they can goggle at themselves in the mirror some more. Each runner with a goal in mind. This isn't a game. This isn't a contest. It's not even an event. Every one of them just running for their own reasons, be they good or evil. But that lone woman running up ahead, she doesn't run to impress. She doesn't run because she has to. She runs because she wants. She runs because she wants to win. She runs to defy you. She runs because she is free. She ignores him and continues to rest of the arm bar. Her time however is shortened, as the Caucasian male besides her grunts loudly. Again she said nothing but rather clenched her fists in anger and furrowed her eyebrows. "What the hell are you doing?" the Caucasian asked her. "You trying to prove something to me?" The Caucasian laughed and commenced to pedal as well. Adriana had nothing on her mind but to upstage this buffoon. How dare he insult not only her, but also all the females in the room. He was the reason why Adriana and her sister resorted to reducing men to heaps of garbage, Men like him were why Adelina would rather flirt and invite them on to steal their money, than actually befriend anyone. They no longer were the center of attention and as time wore on, the onlookers just ignored them. The Caucasian too began to ignore what the commotion was all about. As time wore on and on, the Caucasian began to taunt less and pedal less. The fact that Adriana never resorted to insulting him and just basically ignored him pissed him off. But it did not piss him off to the extent to go on taunting her. He could not taunt her. He was getting tired. Whilst he concentrated on taunting her and pedaling faster to upstage her, Adriana just worried about doing her own thing. And as time wore on, it was Adriana who kept on pedaling, faster and faster. The Caucasian male could not taunt her anymore, and seemed to labor him breathing more than Adriana did. Imagine that, the person who spoke the loudest had the least action. Adriana caught him out of the corner of her eye and in her mind she smirked. In her mind she was also screaming a string of profanities at him. But she stayed silent because she knew she had won this small battle. There was no need for her o resort to his actions. The Caucasian male gradually reduced his pedaling more and more until he could not go on anymore. In a fit of disgust he punched the bar and got up and walked away, cursing under his breath. Adriana smiled, and stopped pedaling. Her leg muscles were throbbing with pain and she cringed as it finally caught up to her. She leaned on the armrest and breathed heavily. "That fucking asshole..." she muttered under breath. She breathed heavily and all that came to her mind was how much of an asshole that sexist had been. But that was not the only thing that came to her mind. She also remembered about the way men had treated her, her whole life. She remembered why she hated men so much. But then she remembered something else. "Wait a minute..." she whispered to herself. She remembered what her sister had said earlier. Adriana remembered about her doubts before about being able to fight men. Well, she had no doubts anymore. She knew now she didn't have to have bigger muscles or testosterone to win a fight. She just won a fight right now with a man who was in good shape. No, it was not a physical battle, but it was a psychological win. She won through her own determination. Now if only, she could find a way to do that to her sister and herself. "Maybe, this whole fighting thing isn't such a bad idea." she said to herself. "I'm sure Adelina has a plan. I'm sure of it."
