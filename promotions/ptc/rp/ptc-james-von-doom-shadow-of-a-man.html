The Shadow of a Man, James von Doom (UWF)

27 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is what it feels like to be James von Doom. Cold, empty, and filled with one thing. ANGER. An uncontrollable rage that many have not seen. He blames them all for what happened to his darling Layla. About how he let her in too much and had to take care of her. The darkness has consumed him now. He's attacked wrestlers in the back with no warranted reason. The dragon in the furnace that is his heart is coiled up and ready to strike. He sits in the darkness of his reality and now lives amongst the shadows. But he has been living in the shadows for much of his troubled and tormented existence. Nobody knows about his haunted past. In fact, nobody bothered to ask. He has merely gone about his wrestling career moving from federation to federation, winning championships and fans alike. But all of it has led to this moment. This moment of pure and adulterated hatred. HATE. Hate fills his heart at this very moment. Hatred toward those evil creatures who kidnapped his lover, even though he told them to himself. But also to himself for letting his anger get out of control and attack unarmed wrestlers backstage. He covers his face in shame. "I never should have let her get so close," he cries out with a whimper. "She made me weaker than I really am. Do I really need any more weaknesses? No. I don't." RAGE. That is what fills his heart. The dragon that is coiled around his beating heart is filled with rage. Rage about his career, his lifestyle, the men he's managing, and, most especially, himself. James von Doom is a very bitter man. The view pans back to show that James is in his basement lab formulating even more evil schemes and characters. This is where the magic truly happens. This is where James comes up with his most devious of ideas. What ideas would he come up with this time? McDonald's PlayPlace from Hell matches? Perhaps a three-legged wrestler with no teeth? Maybe a Flaming Pit of Wookies match? Who knows? His inner-madness is starting to get the best of him, as is evident by his outburst on the last SAS in the UWF where he competes regularly. He is a very eccentric man. Many people may not realize it, but he is a genius of the highest order. But his genius has slowly driven him insane... Reality and fantasy are blurred together. The line between them no longer seen. He is wearing a dark cloak, like one you might see a certain character from "Star Wars" wearing. His eyes are flaming blue with hatred and anger. Despair is seen in the rings around his eyes. This is a man driven insane by his own genius. He now believes himself to be a Sith Lord and no one can control his violent rages. He even killed the stunt doubles of DICK~!, the Nookie Monster, and the Masked Monkeys. No one is safe from his rage... Not even his friends. Friends are something he has too many of. He has spread himself too thin. The inner-workings of his stable has fallen apart. TAN is not going as well as he had planned and are practically jobbers now in the AWC. DICK~! is nothing more than a sideshow freak. The Partners In Crime are too much of a liability for him and he has no time for them anyway. Eon Blue Apocalypse is doing good, but they still have a lot of work to be done for them to be contenders. The Evil Pigs have the Exorcist, but he's really the one pulling the strings and he doesn't know how long he can work with them. Psycho Bunny is a lunatic and he cannot control him but, yet, he can. The Nookie Monster is an idea he had that might be good down the line, but not now. The Killer Pirates are slowly going down the tubes but still show promise. Now, he is alone... Alone with his thoughts. Alone to brood and go even more insane. This is what it feels like to be James von Doom. ALONE. What ideas must be going through this madman’s head? Thoughts of lynchings? No. Thoughts of crazy matches? No. Just more character ideas. Here are some of the better ones going through his crazy mind. The Horny Mathematician. A paper on James’ little table is shown with a little light from a lamp above. Here is what is written down in the script James has scribbled on the paper. [The Horny Mathematician looks at the Announcer.] HM: “The square root of penis is pie.” [The Announcer looks perplexed.] Announcer: “How does this have anything to do with you attacking so and so?” [The Mathematician looks at him.] HM: “Bad news for vaginas.” And that’s all it says. Nothing much there, just ideas. More papers are strewn about on the table with things written on them such as “TAN’s A Bad Idea,” “DICK~!’s Insurance Forms,” and “Don’t Forget about the Deadlines you Moron.” Let’s face it. James is a busy man and it’s starting to show. You can just tell by his eyes. This is not the same man who once wrestled for over an hour to win a World Championship. This isn’t the greatest jobber and one of the hardest workers in NWC history. No. This is James von Doom, shell of who he was. A man hidden by a cloak. A shadow hiding in the darkest parts of all of us. He is nothing more than what he plays on TV. A Sith Lord. A bitter and fragile beast with an appetite for violence. No one knows the inner-madness of this man. The thoughts that cross his mind now are strange and unnatural. He’s been in his basement lab for does doing very strange experimentations. His friends haven’t a clue what he’s been up to. They’ve just been going in and out of his house as they please. Jon Collingwood’s been in von Doom’s house for 3 straight days drinking beer not having a clue what’s been going on. Only Igor, von Doom's trusted personal assistant who moves in and out through the tunnels beneath the von Doom House has seen him. Weird noises and explosions have been coming from James’ little lab. It’s almost become the Hogwart’s Alchemy lab. Beakers with all kinds of fluids in them and tubes are strewn about all over the background, just out of the reach of the light. There are books all over the floor and in the bookcase by the wall. There are all kinds of books there. Books with such subject matter as the occult, astrology, astronomy, witchcraft, Sith arts, black magic, alchemy, chemistry, creative writing, meteorology, psychology, sociology, and anthropology. James von Doom is truly a genius of our time, but is now merely nothing more than the ideas he creates. And all of his ideas are insane. James slowly gets up from his chair and stalks over to the pile of books closest to him. He begins thumbing through them, trying to find what he is looking for. “Bollocks,” he says to himself in a whisper. “They know not what they deal with.” He looks through the pile until he finds what he is looking for. It’s a book entitled “PWNAGE and How to Win the Smarks Over.” James cackles to himself maniacally. “Soon no one will be able to handle the evil of the Sith,” he says with a demented smile. Just then, there is a knock on the door. James looks a bit bewildered. James then does his best Darth Sidious impression. “Who is it?” A voice outside his door answers “It’s me, Master.” “Me who?” James replies with a scowl. The tinge in his voice seems a bit irritated by this unwelcome interruption. “It’s me, Igor, Master,” the voice shakily replies. “Good,” James replies and magically, the door opens like it was never locked. Igor looks in like he has no idea what to do. His Master is way across the room and yet, the locked door opened all by itself. Maybe von Doom has actually become a Sith Lord. Maybe his grasp on his storyline and his reality is completely unhinged and now he has become his character. Von Doom is now Darth Scourge. And no one can tell him otherwise. Igor gulps and walks in with some rolled up papers. “Master, the plans,” he says as he sits them down on the table in front of James, who smiles at him. “Good,” he says with a smile before pulling a chicken bone out of a bucket on the ground and feeding it to Igor. Igor chews on the bone and makes some sickening sounds as James looks over the plans with demented glee. “Master,” Igor says. “When are we going through with this?” James pats him on the head the way you would a dog or a small child who doesn’t know any better. James replies “In due time my faithful servant.” “But won’t anybody know?” Igor asks with a puzzled look across his ugly face. “With me, anything is possible,” James replies. “They’ll just think it’s one of my demented schemes or storylines. No one knows the true evils lurking within my mind. No one is safe from it. No one can be safe from it. We must move quickly before my plot is discovered.” “What plot?” Igor asks. “These are just the plans to expand the guesthouse for Eon Blue Apocalypse’s Church of Maynard.” “Quiet you insignificant little fiend,” James replies with a scowl. “No one questions a Sith Lord! NO ONE~!” “Sorry Master,” Igor replies. “I didn’t know any better.” “Leave me,” James says, pointing toward the door. “Yes, Master,” Igor replies as he sulks out of the room. The door magically slams shuts as he leaves and locks itself as James starts going over the EVIL~ renovation plans for his guesthouse as the scene fades out. Cut.
