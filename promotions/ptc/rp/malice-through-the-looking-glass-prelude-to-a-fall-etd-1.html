MALICE THROUGH THE LOOKING GLASS: Prelude to a Fall [ETD #1]

Posted: Fri Apr 11, 2003 2:38 am Post subject: MALICE THROUGH THE LOOKING GLASS: Prelude to a Fall [ETD #1] Lucretia, Is my love in vain when thy tears bleed sweeter then the midsummer rain? Be-winged, infested belfries Toll o'er the sobbing throng A writhe of lethargic, terrored nudes Whipped and welted 'neath the barbed wind-song… - Funeral in Carpathia. Ravage. Torture. Brutalise. Kill. Eat. Exploit the weak. It’s only fair. The weak are nothing but plain-as-day heathens. They are who you least expect it to be, through the simplicity of the habitual human psyche. They are the people who force an illusion upon you. These heathens are the types of people who elude their real selves to you. They could be your acquaintances. They could be your kin, your family. They could be your significant other. They could be your deity, your idol, and everything you’re not. But when the tangible genuineness is told, they are just lulled in their own insignificance. They want to be what they flaunt themselves to be. But really, they are nothing. They are just the thin line that separates the typical person, and the vengeful person; example of that, being my first and second lives. I was once like you. I didn’t want to be. I was just a dime-a-dozen man. I was far from the epitome of the model human, very far. I took the lives of others because I thought it’d be pleasurable. I had blood on my hands every night for as long as I can have off pat. It was a thrill. It was an adventure that was addictive; it was superior to any drug or high anyone can ever experience. There were no grudges. There was no animosity. It was all for the sport of it all. Enthralling cannot even begin to describe the way I felt as I tore through my INNOCENT victim’s flesh. The sound of their blood-curdling screams was like MUSIC to my EARS! Does that make me morally wrong? Do I care if it was morally wrong or not? No. The taste of their blood was like nothing I’ve savoured before. It was always boiling, the blood was. I never murdered in cold blood. No, I killed in blistering, crimson blood. The smell, the smell was breathtaking. The smell of a human being; be it male or female, at their last seconds on earth is out of the ordinary. Their breath, their clothes, their body reeked of absolute fear and trepidation! The visage of my victim’s innocent blood steering through the blades of grass down to wherever it may have roamed, the sight of their ravaged bodies lying in my own arms, dead, cold, and unmoving was something that one would have to experience for their own selves. But killing was something I enjoyed; perhaps the only thing I’d ever enjoyed in my first life. …That is, until my own life was taken from me! It was STOLEN! I was robbed of my own self. Do you know how that feels? It’s devastating to know that someone else, no matter how contradictory this may sound, is taking away your self-image and livelihood away from you. Granted, I did it for fun…as often as I could find a fool ignorant enough, I killed. But my own undoing was out of vengeance for the people I killed. He was a one-dimensional figure that took my own verve away. The ‘Lord’ I was informed by the masked executioner, however, it was truly more in terms of the fucked up law that wanted me dead for claiming so many others in the same way. I’d never forgive my wrongdoer again. Fuck the Lord. I was burned alive. My skin ROASTED in the scorching, hellfire, or so what seemed it. My hide melted off of my body. Imagine the PAIN I went through. Imagine how much it pained me to be agonised for something that I couldn’t stop doing. Imagine it. I LIVED through it, for an eternity one would believe. If imagining it troubles you, just thinks what it was like to be tied to a post, unable to move, and be scorched. To be eaten by the flames is something even I wouldn’t want to go through again. But it doesn’t mean I wouldn’t kill again. I was resurrected because of my love to kill! I was resurrected to be a merciless monster, and that’s what I am today. I was enhanced. I became swifter, and stronger than I’d ever been before…and why’s this? It’s because I was reincarnated to be a trained assassin. Not by actual training, but by who I was awoken for. I was REBIRTHED for one man…to be his one-man army, and to do whatever he’d want me to. To kill whomever he pointed his finger at in resentment. I was remade to be a heartless, unforgiving, unremorseful wrecking machine. It’s not something I brag about, however. I kill for him, yes. Killing is what I LOVE to do, right? Correct. But to do the sinful deed because one wants another dead takes the thrill out of it all. It reminds me of euthanasia. It is just mercy killing, in a way, you don’t do it because you want to…you do it because you have to. But that’ll change, and I guarantee it. I will once again rise to what I was, some three hundred years back. I will once again be Tobolsk Unimak, and not Maelgrim. I will NOT be Julius Corbin’s. I have to be my own…and I will do it, one step at a time. _________________ I mark for SHAKIRA and no one else. Don't feel bad, it's nothing personal. None of my friends made the list either. The X.
