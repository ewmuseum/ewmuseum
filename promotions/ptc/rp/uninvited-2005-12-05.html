Uninvited

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 The Mifune School of Wrestling Excellence was nestled just outside the city of Sapporo on the island of Hokkaido, the island located north of the Japanese "Mainland". Though it had started small, Toshiro Mifune was as skilled a business man as he was a wrestler and the school had since grown in size, renown and popularity. Mifune had settled upon the final location for the school due to it’s proximity to Hokkaido’s capitol, as well as proximity to a local hospital should any of the students suffer injuries that could not be tended to on the grounds themselves. The Mifune School grounds were ringed by a hand crafted stone wall to shield the academy from outsiders, and just inside the wall were the prefabricated buildings where students, applicants and servants worked or lived. The innermost ring of the grounds, however, had a more traditional touch to them, with painstakingly crafted wooden buildings sitting on raised poles around a well kept pond and rock garden. At this time of year the entire grounds were blanketed in a thin covering of snow, snow that even now continued to fall gently from the heavens to further cover the grounds in a soft layer of white. To an outsider the inner ring radiated peace and tranquility, backed up by a profound sense of quiet. Tonight, however, the inner ring of the Academy was anything but quiet or tranquil. "Then why am I even fucking HERE?!" Dawn’s bellow echoed across the normally quiet courtyard as she tore open the shoji screen she had been sitting near. Without so much as a glance back over her shoulder she slammed the screen behind her again, stalking across the pinewood walkway with such force that the entire floor shuddered beneath her normally light footfalls. She stalked over the ornate bridge that arc gracefully over the small frozen stream that ran from the pond to a point outside the grounds, halting at the halfway point when she heard her name, "Dawn wait!" She turned as Michi Noketsuna emerged from the same room she’d stalked out of, rolling her eyes in annoyance as he paused to respectfully close the shoji screen behind him. Long legs carried him quickly to where she stood, the dark skin around his eyes creased with concern. "What was all that about? You were very rude to Master Mifune." "Rude?!" Dawn exclaimed, gesturing angrily toward the closed screen. "He was being rude to ME, not the other way around! You saw how he was treating me, the things he was saying! I might have been short with him, but it was nothing he didn’t blatantly ask for!" Dawn nostrils flared and her shoulders heaved, most of her nearly six foot frame practically shaking with anger. Michi held up his large hands in a gesture of surrender, trying to placate the angry Lioness before him. "Whoa whoa whoa, slow down there Little Dragon, why do think he was being rude to you? Master Mifune was trying to make you see why you’re here, and what you need to do while you’re here." "Why do I think he was being rude?!" Dawn snapped back in disbelief, eyes flashing. "He was MOCKING me Michi, openly mocking me!" She drew back for a moment, pitching her voice in a mocking mimicry of her Sensei’s accent: "I did not bring you here to break boards Little Dragon, I brought you here to teach you how to live again. I’m sorry that does not meet the expectations you had in mind. Perhaps you would rather I have you run water up and down Mount Fuji? Perhaps play a montage for you? A little ‘Eye of the Tiger’ playing in the background maybe?" Her imitation finished Dawn threw up her hands in anger, stalking to one side of the bridge and resting her forearms against the railing with a huff, her breath pluming out into the night air like fire from a dragons lungs. Michi sighed, his broad shoulders slumping a little as he shook his head slowly. "Dawn he was teasing you, you must be able to see that. He didn’t mean any harm by it I’m sure." "If he’s so concerned about my state of mind then why is he mocking me in front of all of you?! I didn’t come here to be treated like a novice again, I came…fuck I don’t even know why I came anymore." Michi approached Dawn carefully, laying his large hands gently on her shoulders and rubbing a little at the muscles there. "He wasn’t mocking you Dawn, he was teasing you, there’s a big difference. The Master has changed a little bit over the last year, he’s a little more relaxed around the senior students then he used to be." Dawn made a non-commital grunt, rolling her neck from side to side and staring moodily at the frozen surface of the pond. Michi shook his head with a smirk, starting to knead his fingers a little more forcibly into her shoulders, massaging the knots of stressed muscles that had formed. "And don’t you think you might have offended him a little by assuming? I know this is Japan and everything, but this isn’t the movies…Master Mifune isn’t going to make you break boards for two years so he can teach you the Crushing Eagle Claw technique." She chuckled at that, letting her head droop forward so Michi could massage the aching muscles at the base of her neck. "I…I don’t know Michi, I’m still not sure I should have come here in the first place. Everything’s happening so fast…and it’s weird to not be home when it’s all happening." "You left your friend there to watch for Lia, and she knows how to contact us. If something happens while you are gone Dawn, we’ll hear about it I promise. Master Mifune is nothing if not thorough." Michi’s broad thumbs slid up and down the back of her neck, drawing a pleased rumble from the back of Dawn’s throat. "I know and…I know I’m being difficult but…that’s the whole reason I’m here isn’t it? He brought me here so he could help me; he’s got to know I’m not really grounded right now! I slit my goddamn wrist Michi!" Dawn pulled away from her friends hands, rolling up her sleeve and holding her right wrist up. Pale moonlight bathed the equally pale skin in white light, illuminating a series of angry red scars laid over several smaller, more faded ones. "He could cut me some fucking slack…" "Dawn…" Michi sighed, taking her exposed wrist in one massive hand and pulling her into a gentle hug. Even at nearly six feet Dawn’s head pillowed just under Michi’s chin, and she rested one cheek against his chest. "…this is going to be difficult for everyone, you know that. This is just as unfamiliar ground for the rest of us as it is for you, especially Master Mifune. How many times has he openly displayed emotion to us before now? And yet how many times in the last few days has he hugged you or not looked embarrassed when you cried?" Dawn buried her face in Michi’s broad chest, letting out a long sigh of frustration. "You’re right of course, as usual…I just…I was hoping for something more." She sighed again. "I suppose I should go apologize…" Taking her gently by the shoulders, Michi held her at arms length and shook his head. "No, Master Mifune is just as proud as you are, and going to him right now would only make matters worse. He’s probably ranting to Yukiko-sama about what an ungrateful brat you are." He softened the words with a wink, drawing a chuckle from the Steel Rose. "You’re probably right, again. I just don’t think I could sleep right now, I’ve got too much on my mind." "So don’t sleep just yet then. Go for a run, or draw a hot bath or something. Clear your head a little before you lie down. I bet you’ll be out like a light in no time." Dawn laughed, patting one of his large forearms. "Sounds like a plan to me. Would you…look in on Master Mifune for me? I’d feel better knowing he wasn’t about to throw me out on my ungrateful ass." Michi laughed as well, patting her on the upper arm. "I doubt it’s as bad as all that, but I’ll stick my head in and take a look okay? And then I’ll see you in the morning." "Good night Michi." Dawn said with a smile, turning away. "Oyasumi Ne-chan." He replied. ************************************************************************************** Dawn eased into the steaming bath water with a hissed sigh, letting the near scalding water work its magic on her pale skin. With her muscles still bunching and knotting from stress, a run had been out of the question and so she had drawn herself a bath as Michi recommended. She fancied she could feel each individual muscle slowly loosening as the hot water eased her aches and pains, letting her head droop back against the rim of the tub. The bath here wasn’t nearly large enough to accommodate her lanky body, but with her knees tucked up a little she found she could lean back just enough to take the pressure off her neck. "Gods I’m such a mess." She breathed, "I haven’t even started to train for GTT5 and my body is ready to call it quits." Her GTT5 acceptance had arrived at the Estate just a scant few hours after she had left, thankfully Caitlin had the sense to open the PTC stamped envelope and call Dawn to ensure she knew where she had to be and when. Though Lia’s whereabouts and wellbeing were the first things on Dawn’s mind, GTT5 was a big deal for her…and the very prospect of competing in a PTC ring again made her very nervous. "Didn’t make such a grand showing of myself at CW2." She grumbled, picking absently at her thumbnail. Her first round elimination wore heavily on her mind, even several months later, and had almost been enough for her not to submit an application to GTT5 at all. Which was not to say Dawn McFarlane wasn’t confident about her chances for the upcoming tournament…she simply wished she’d made a better accounting of herself overall. Expectations had been high for Dawn after she’d eliminated Tessa Windsor in round one of CW1…but she had failed to deliver, falling back into a sense of mediocrity overnight. Or at least, that was how she’d seen it at the time. Although the months in between had not dulled the pain of her quick elimination, Dawn had made the effort to see that loss in a different light…and learn from it. She shifted uneasily in the tub, looking at the shaft of moonlight coming in the small window set near the ceiling. "It won’t happen again…" She murmured to herself, tucking an errant strand of strawberry blonde hair behind her ear. "This time I won’t be afraid…won’t be afraid to win." GTT5 also marked the first time Dawn had entered into a major tournament without the support of one of PTC’s member federations. Her flight to Japan had closed the door on her arrangements with Global Wrestling, leaving her without the Four Million dollar contract she had earned at Masters of the Mat…earned by defeating the woman she loved one on one. "Oh Lia…" She sighed, pillowing her head in her arms. "Where are you? Why haven’t you contacted me? Why haven’t you just told me you’re okay?" No answer magically materialized in the moonlight, nor did Dawn’s absent lover suddenly make herself known. She squeezed her hands to her head, as if the motion would ease some of the pressure ricocheting around her skull; Lia’s absence, her own depression, the pressure of competing at GTT5, the loss of her Global contract… "Gods I think I’m gonna be sick." She moaned to the empty room, scrubbing her hands over her face. "I need to get this all out…" "Tomorrow." She breathed, heaving herself up out of the tub in a cascade of water. "Tomorrow I start training again, make sure I’m ready for Shaun Styles…for GTT5. Maybe that will help…" Dawn tucked a towel around herself and pulled the plug, watching the bath water spiral out of control down the drain. The skin around her mouth tightened into a hard line. "This time I won’t fall…this time I’ll go all the way." *************************************************************************************** With her blonde hair tightly wrapped in a towel about her head, Dawn flopped down onto the futon in her guest room with a groan, not even bothering to wriggle under the covers as the mattress slowly formed to accommodate its new guest. She missed her canopy bed back at the estate, but had always enjoyed the sheer space provided by a full sized futon. The school was well heated, so pajama pants and a tank top were enough for Dawn to feel warm as she finally wriggled underneath the heavy comforter. With heavy lidded eyes she stared up at the simple ceiling, the chaotic maelstrom of her mind having subsided to a sluggish whirlwind. Her normally pale skin was still rosy pink from the heat of the water and her normally aching muscles were limp and unresponsive. A jaw cracking yawn fought its way free of her throat and she smiled sleepily, her eyes lids drooping slowly closed. "Michi was right…again…bastard…" Her upper eyelashes brushed against her lower lashes as a warm feeling spread out from her belly. She snuggled in against the warmth of the futon, her mind just starting to drift into… "Dawn…" Like a gust of wind, her whispered name carried to Dawn’s ears. "Mer?" She murmured groggily, rolling over to face the guest room door. "Michi?" "Dawn…I’m here Dawn…" The voice was whispering, almost sibilant and as the words finally penetrated into Dawn’s mind she propped herself up on elbow, staring in uncertainty at the door. "Hello?" There was no answer to her call, but a shadow fell across the crack between the door and the floor, temporarily blocking the light from the hallway…and then was gone again. With a growl of frustration Dawn pushed herself up, swinging her legs out of bed. "C’mon Michi this isn’t funny, I’m fucking exhausted." "I still want you Dawn…" A shiver went down Dawn’s spine, her flimsy tank top no protection from the sudden feeling of cold that permeated her body. She cast about the room for some kind of weapon, eyes flicking over each surface in the hope of finding something. "Dammit…isn’t there supposed to be a katana in every room or something?" Slipping off the side of the futon, Dawn crept to where her duster hung on the wall, just over her winter boots. She slipped both garments on and slid silently to the door, easing it open and looking out into the hallway. Nothing. "Wait." She breathed. A shadow moved at the far end of the hallway, slipping into her field of view for only a moment before it ducked around the corner and out of sight. Tucking her duster firmly around herself, Dawn set off after it, determined to catch her late night tormenter. "This better not be some sick joke. " She grumbled, "Or I’m gonna break somebody’s face." The sound of the outside door closing caught her ear and she picked up the pace, jogging around the corner to the closest exit. The air here was colder then the rest of the hallway and a fine dusting of snow hadn’t yet melted on the floor. Dawn grinned triumphantly as she swung open the door and stepped into the cold. "If you have to use the door, you’re not anything special." The antechamber was very cold, being protected from the outside by only a thin layer of shoji screen doors…which had been left wide open. Dawn peeked around the edge of the door into the courtyard, squinting in the blackness in an attempt to make out anything at all. The usual electronic lights were off, and even the ancient stone torches set throughout the rock garden had been doused, leaving the entire courtyard illuminated by and eerie pale glow from the moon. "Dawn…" "Fuck YOU!" She yelled out, stepping onto the walkway in frustration. "This isn’t fucking funny anymore, cut it out!" "But we’re just getting started…" A finger slid across the small of her back, and Dawn spun hard on her heel and lashed out with a spinning backhand…and caught nothing but cold air as her fist connected with nothing. A hand slid across her toned bottom and she spun again, this time shattering the wooden railing with a sharp kick…and again making no contact with flesh. "Please…I know all your tricks Dawn…I watched you train for hours…" "Who ARE you?!" She screamed in frustration, putting her back against the wall. "You’ll remember…" Cold breath wafted across her face and something slipped across her breast, pushing on the fabric of Dawns coat. She screamed then and pushed off the wall, striking at thin air with a rushing elbow. She failed to connect and slipped in the freshly fallen snow, sliding through the broken railing and stumbling to her hands and knees in the frozen rock garden. "Mmmm…that’s a position I’ll always remember…" Cold hands slid down her slides to her hips and Dawn kicked out savagely behind her, snapping a small bonsai tree on the edge of the garden clean out of it’s planter. A heavy weight hit her in the lower back, driving her breathlessly into the cold sand. She tried to rise, coughing, but someone grabbed the back of her head and forced her face into the sand. A flailing elbow connected with bone and the pressure let up suddenly and she pulled her face back out of the sand, choking and gasping for air. "You never were a match for me." The blow that hit the side of her face lifted Dawn off her knees and sent her sailing into one of the larger rocks in the garden. Her duster fell from her shoulders, leaving the pale skin exposed to the harsh winter air. Feet crunched in the frozen sand, and a shadow fell across her, blocking out the moon. "Did you really think I’d leave it at that? That I’d just let you go after so much time together? Stupid girl…" Another blow struck her under the chin, snapping Dawn’s head back into the rock and causing spots to dance before her eyes. A powerful grip seized the front of her tank top and lifted her to her feet…and then higher until her toes dangled an inch or so off the ground. Fetid breath hit her in the face like a slap, and she struggled to clear the blur from her eyes. "You were mine once little girl…and you’re going to be mine again…forever." A leering, rotting face shoved into Dawn’s field of view and she screamed, a sound of petrified terror that rung about the courtyard like a gong. Perfect fangs glinted like silver in the moonlight as the face ducked past her chin. One hand gripped her roughly by the hips as a searing pain tore into her neck, tearing a violated scream from Dawn’s lungs… …as she sat up in bed, her face drenched with sweat. Dawn scrambled free of her blankets, falling to the floor in her haste to be free and jarring her elbow against the hard wood. Lights came on in the hallway and she could hear concerned voices…but they were like a distant sound, trying to penetrate through a fog. She drew her knees to her chest, one hand to her face. "No no nononononononono…not Marcus…" She whispered brokenly.
