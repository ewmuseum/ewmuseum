Date: 2004-04-01 ----------------------------------------------------- 4:31 pm est, Thursday March 25th ----------------------------------------------------- "Earlier today, wrestling tycoon, Primetime Central announced its Cruiserweight tournament as well as the brackets," the redheaded female news reporter said holding her microphone high and staring straight into the camera. "There will be 4 brackets with each bracket consisting of 4 matches." The woman stared into the camera with a fake smile on the whole time. She then stopped for a slight breath and began again, "That is all for today's show, join me and the PA Squad as we talk about the violence inherited into the system, this is Jennifer Bacilos tuning out." A catchy theme plays and then a man's voice from off scene shouts, "ANNNND CUT! That's a wrap people." The woman who was speaking, named Jennifer Bacilos, immediately loses her smile and heads off the set towards a shadowy backstage where people are putting on jackets and getting ready to leave. She takes a seat on a two-cushioned love seat and picks up her leather purse from under the chair. She pulls out a cigarette and then begins to dig for her lighter as she places the cigarette in her mouth. She finally finds the lighter and places it at the tip of the cigarette and begins snapping down on the lighter. It soon lights and she tosses the lighter in the general area of her purse, which now lays about a foot in front of her. Jennifer sits back and a stout bald man comes up to the attractive woman with a handful of papers. "Jennifer, ratings from last week's show are in and if this week's show is anything like it we are never going to get that network deal." The little man said pointing at some charts and scribbles. "Let me tell you something, Bob, you've got me reporting on some jackass story about wrestling!? Who the hell gives a damn about wrestling? I sure don't..." Ms. Bacilos responded while she brought the cigarette to her mouth and took a puff. "I like wrestling..." The stout man spoke with insecurity and returned the charts back under his arm. "Which is probably why you haven't had a date since god knows when. Look we need to get better stories; I am a television broadcaster, not a reporter. So how about you go get me some good stories or you can kiss me, and your precious dreams goodbye." She replied and than uncrossed her legs and stood up, simultaneously grabbing her purse and beginning to walk away. ------------------------------------------------- 4:31 pm est, Thursday March 25th ------------------------------------------------- "Yeah," The accent is clear in the speech, "Mmmm Hmmm, no dude don't go, I'm trying to be one of those ass hole drivers. Yeah the one's that piss everyone off by talking on the cell phone, eating and drinking all that the same time as driving." The scene opens up and Emilio Dervante is driving a red 2004 Corvette with the top down. The camera acts as a passenger as Emilio talks on the cell phone. "No Holmes, I'm on the way to the front office, heard I got in a tournament or something... hold on... gotta swerve...” Emilio Dervante looks behind him and then a swerve in and out of the right lane and then after awhile settles in the original lane and a car drives past flipping off Emilio. "Yeah, I'm going to go out later tonight, I'll meet you around 8 bro... I'm out." Emilio removes the phone from his ear and folds it in. He tosses the phone down by his side and speeds up as the camera leaves him. ------------------------------------------------- 6:49 pm EST, Thursday March 25th ------------------------------------------------- The dark apartment awakens as the jingling and jangling of keys can be heard breaking the threshold of the lock. The lock gives up the battle against the key and the door unlocks and swings open. The same woman from before, Jennifer Bacilos, enters the door with her high heels and hand and keys in the other. She tosses the keys onto a small table to the left of the door and her shoes on the right side of the door. She takes off her blue blazer and walks over to a little coffee table, which is resting place for an answering machine. She pushes a button right next to a blinking red light and a voice began play. "Thursday, March 25th... 2:11 pm....." The monotone voice introduced a different female's voice. "Hi Jenny, it's Sarah... I know it's only Thursday but Tom broke up with me and I need to blow off some steam... So I'm going to go out but I was wondering if you want to come to... Call me back hun." BEEP! "End of Messages." The monotone voice signified the end of it all and Jennifer Bacilos, who had left the seen for a second, returned in her bra and panties to rewind the answering machine. She hits two more buttons and then picks up the cordless phone on the side of the machine. After 7 tones are heard she puts the phone up to her ear and waits. "Hey... Sarah? Yeah I just got your message, and I don't know if I want to go tonight... Long story... What do you mean more of a reason to drink away? I have to work tomorrow... you promise we'll only be there for a few drinks? Fine I'll go... pick me up around 8 or something... Ok, I'm going to let you go... Bye." Jennifer pushes another button and the places the phone down and leaves the scene again as it goes straight to black. -------------------------------------------------------------- 6:54 pm EST, Thursday March 25th -------------------------------------------------------------- "I've had enough," Emilio's voice echoed and then the cameras peek in at him in a gray hallway with nothing but a darker gray molding, "This is what they post a bracket on? A friggin sheet of paper, the thing looks like its hand drawn, I mean you couldn't get a computer to do this or something... Oh well...” Emilio crumples up the paper and then turns around and fixes his white button down shirt. "These people don't work enough here. I mean c'mon look at this place, it truly is a mess. Not one poster up or anything, the colors like they are from the 70's... What is wrong with this place? I enter a stupid tournament to maybe get some recognition and get the money rolling in, but it took me a damn week to get confirmation that I was in it. Then on top of it all they give me a match up against two BRAND NEW jobbers?! Now I know I'm no big name but what the hell, do they want me to blow through these two like a hurricane through Haiti?" Emilio, who is now navigating the hallways, questioned and criticized. "I mean you guys are following me around for some reason... Do you want to put PTC on the map or something because you don't get any better than Emilio Dervante. And you can't even expect me to talk about those two losers, Marcel something or other and Jimmy Diablo...? First off they're not even worth the roach that just crawled out of the wall over there, and their names are ridiculous." Emilio said as he ventured out into the bright out doors. Emilio begins heading into the parking lot, that isn't far from being totally empty, besides a t-shirt and a broken down shagging wagon that says 'Mark Out, With Your Cock Out,' and Emilio's car. "Yeah this place is still booming... I wouldn't touch a wrestler who has been in that ring for more than two minutes, you'd catch more STDS, well then... her." Emilio points to a woman who has now just crawled out of the van and looks very raggedy. "Either way," Emilio spoke as he opened his car door and stepped inside,” you will be seeing a lot more of Emilio Dervante, and you will definitely see Emilio Dervante in the second round... you can bet on it..." Emilio shuts the car door and starts up the car. He sits there for a few seconds and then puts in reverse, backs out, back into drive and drives out of the parking lot, which looks more like a junkyard. ---------------------------------------------------------- 8:15 pm EST, Thursday March 25th ---------------------------------------------------------- Dusk lost the battle to the night and the crowds begen to grow in front of a night club. Jennifer Bacilos sat in the middle of the mob with her friend, recognized as Sarah. Both were dressed to kill wearing black leather mini skirts and tops smaller then napkins. The mob, slowly, moved closer to the door and as it did the two spoke. "I can't believe it..." Sarah said as she got bumped from behind, "that good for nothing bitch of a man broke up with me... I mean me. He actually had the nerve to call me and not even tell me to my face." "You would've killed him if he said it to your face." Jennifer butted in as she looked around for a possible familiar face in the mob. "Ugh... Well either way he should've been a man..." Sarah said with a bit of discouragement in her voice. " But instead he watches that gay wrestling shit all day and all night. He didn't even care about me, I could so do better then him." She said as she tried to make herself fell more self confident. "Men will be men... All they want is 'boobies', ass, beer and their sport of choice. You need to find a man who likes football, that's the shortest season that I know of. They have less excuses not to go out dancing with you." Jennifer spoke with a certain tone as though she had been dealing with failed couples. "Last week, on our anniversary, I walked out in my red bra and panties that I bought the Sunday before, with you... And all he did was tell me to get out of the way, and wait until the match was over...I HATE MEN!" By this time they had already reached the front of the line and as Sarah shouted they had stepped up in front of the large bouncer wearing a black t-shirt. Sarah looked up at him and looking a little embarrassed paid him, and he moved out of the way so that they could both step inside the club. As they walked Sarah spoke, "I should just find a really desperate guy and take him and fuck him..." Jennifer turned in surprise of what Sarah had just said and replied, "You leave me here alone and I will go and have sex with Tom..." She smiled afterwards to show Sarah that it was a joke and nothing to be taking serious. -------------------------------------------------------------------- 11:31 pm EST ; Thursday, March 25th -------------------------------------------------------------------- BUMP BUMP BUMP! some outrageous crazy hyper uber-cool techno beat is pounding on the walls of the club and the ear drums of Emilio Dervante, who is grinding on some chicitas... as he would say. The song stops and Emilio Dervante and partner in crime head off the dance floor to the bar. Emilio shouts something at the bartender and looks to his left to see a brunette wearing a top smaller than a wrestler's spandex attire. "Hey..." Emilio said as he bobbed his head to the beat of the new song that was just starting to blare over the speakers. "Hi." The woman replied after she looked at Emilio and grinned a little. "I'm Emilio... you from around here?" He said noticing that the girl has some kind of interest in him. "I'm Jenn... and yea... I come here every once in awhile actually." She said as the bartender had placed a cup in front of Emilio. "Well, I'm going to go back to the dance floor... maybe I'll see you out there." Emilio returned, after the girl had turned her head and he realized that she must have a boyfriend or is waiting for someone. Emilio downed his drink and then turned away but before he could step away again, Jenn reached out and grabbed his arm. "You wanna get outta here?" Jenn had questioned looking up at Emilio, and sliding out of the bar stool. "Are you sure? Thought I saw you come in with someone." Emilio said, obviously in a rare mood and not looking for a fight. "She was just a friend... probably already gone with some creep... What's it matter to you if I came in with someone...? You afraid?" Jenn said as she let go of Emilio's arm and fixes her little mini skirt. "I'm the least person you're gonna find fear in..." Emilio spoke with almost TOO much confidence and then his hand was again grabbed and Jenn lead the way. ------------------------------------------------------------ 6:04 am EST; Friday, March 26th, 2004 ------------------------------------------------------------ The sun is now peaking through the blinds of a hotel room. The rays of sun show a dance of dust that is being fed by a slight breeze coming from the open window across the room. The rays formed a path to the foot of a bed where white sheets are tangled in two legs. The legs lead to the body of a male and then to back of Emilio Dervante's head. A slip, and bang awakes Emilio, who pushes up and looks towards the hotel room's only bathroom. Emilio Dervante rubs his head, yawns and rolls over the bed in the direction of the bathroom. Emilio stands up and places his arm up against the wall to use for some support. After a few steps he turns into the bathroom to find the woman from the club, Jenn, sitting on the toilet with her panties down at her ankles and a bra on. She looked up at Emilio and spoke with a very regretful voice. "I fell..." The hangover almost oozed out of her body as she spoke and tried to sit. "Oh... Ok." Emilio said as he rubbed his eyes and turned away from the bathroom to head back into the bed. Emilio Dervante crawls into bed and just has he flattens out a loud "cop" knock at the door causes him to look back up. "OPEN UP!" The voice from the other side of the door sounded like every other cop in the world. "EMILIO DERVANTE! WE KNOW YOU ARE IN THERE, OPEN UP OR WE WILL BE FORCED TO KNOCK DOWN THE DOOR!" The voice this time got a little more impatient and not long before that, as Emilio was getting up the door went down and Emilio was tackled by four cops. Jenn then stumbles out of the bathroom and is told to get down, and she does gentally. The cops cuff them both, read their Miranda rights and are out the door.... ------------------------------------------------------------- 4:10 pm EST; Friday, March 26th, 2004 ------------------------------------------------------------- "This next story has to do with our afternoon reporter, Jennifer Bacilos.
