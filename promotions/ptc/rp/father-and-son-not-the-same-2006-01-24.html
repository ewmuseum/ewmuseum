Father and Son, not the same

24 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-24 The letter that Johnathon had told Salaok would be waiting for him at the house was there, resting on the wooden floor inside the entrance in a small envelope. While it no longer surprised Salaok that Johnathon was able to have someone get into his house and plant the letter there during the small period of time that he had been to see his brother, it did slightly bother him that someone had been inside his house. The constant question when the liking’s of this occurred wasn’t so much of who this person or persons were but were they still in the house when he was there? Not a single sound in the house other than the humming coming from the refrigerator two rooms across in the kitchen. They couldn’t still be here, surely. Reaching down and pulling the envelope up from the floor, feeling the cold of the wooden floor boards with his finger tips as they scrapped the grain, out of the corner of his eye he sees something. It’s not a person, but the television was on with nothing but static on the screen, which draw Salaok to walk towards it, forgetting about the letter in his hand for a brief moment. His head turning every which-way, trying to determine if whoever it was that had left the appliance on was still in the area or had left any sort of clue as to who they were. But nothing. Shrugging his shoulders, although not entirely convinced of the safety of the situation, Salaok’s attention returns back to the envelope, which was already open and pulls the sheet of hand written paper from it. The detail of the writing was something of an art form as it was all old style hand writing done in what looked to be a dark red calligraphy styled pen which oozed with elegance. Elegance: While it wasn’t something that most people would have though fitted with Salaok’s older brother Johnathon, it was the one word, which could describe him perfectly. Well it does when fitted with another word of not so beautiful context. Evil Elegance was more fitting for the older of the two brothers. Dear Salaok By Now I have no doubt in my mind that you will have noticed that the television set has been left on for your convenience and all you have to do is press the Play button on your DVD remote. On that DVD you will find information regarding who you are and what you’ve done. Bare in mind Salaok, while I was with you to help you through the memory of Jamal, I will not be with you for what you are about to go through so you must try to stay in control. Your progress this far has been well guided apart from the small break down after you found out about your son’s death again. We can not afford another breakdown, as time is too precious at this point in time. Johnathon As always, Johnathon’s games left him in full control of everything and always the very last detail had been thought out. Or had it. Johnathon’s game relied now on Salaok wanting to press the Play button and watch the DVD, but what if he didn’t? What if Salaok just walked away from the TV screen and turned his back on whatever memory it was that Johnathon wanted to taunt him with now? Tomorrow Salaok would be needing all the energy and more importantly, all the concentration he could find within him as he steps into the ring with Tsonda, Deville and Samuelsson which is no easy task by any man’s understanding. The very name of Deville was enough to strike fear into most men’s minds and the other two men were just as power in this tournament. But Salaok didn’t care much about all three of those men, as he knew little to nothing about them and didn’t care to either. Stepping stones to a bigger target if you will. Turning his back on the screen for a mere moment, he takes one single step away from it before the screen comes to life itself as if someone had pressed the play button for him. As if someone was indeed in the house with him, watching every move that he made. Johnathon’s voice sounded out from the speakers, which encircled the room and his sinister face appeared on the screen, grinning as always the same evil grin that had plagued Salaok’s life since a child. "Brother, what you are about to see on this disk will remind you of the man in whom you were destined to be and the man I need you to be again in order to run the ‘business’ the way we had always intended." The way in which Johnathon has said business sounded odd as if there was something behind it that Salaok was meant to have recognised but didn’t. The image of Johnathon disappears from the screen and images of Salaok back in his old fight days appears while Johnathon’s voice continues to narrate the disk. "My memories are what drives this disk young brother and one of my personal favorites was our triple threat match against Brian. The match which we turned into an inferno match, here watch what happens." The screen shows Johnathon dousing the ring in gasoline and then lighting it on fire while Salaok holds the face of Brian Welch, a family rival in the flames. The screams from Brian are ear piercing but there is no letting go from Salaok has he continues to push Brian’s face down into the flames without a single sign of mercy. "You were a monster once Salaok, and you made me proud." Salaok continues to stand there watching the screen, almost in disbelief that he could ever have done such a thing but the memories of the situation take over forcing a smile to his lips. "And then what about when you had a first blood match with one of your students? Here, watch this one." The screen shows Salaok and his student Draven during the testing period for the student and Salaok pulls something out of his boot. Salaok’s arm swing out violently at his student and the knife in his hand slices Draven straight across the check, sending blood raining to the floor. "No one saw that coming my brother but it was a piece of sheer Davis brilliance. Back they brother you would go to any lengths to get the win and that didn’t just go for in the ring either." The screen changes and shows Johnathon standing at the graveyard that Salaok had visited a few times recently to find out who it was that he had caused the death’s of according to Johnathon and Michael. Just as Salaok remembered the graveyard, most of the headstones where covered by a white sheet so he couldn’t read the names and be overloaded with the information of his past. But as Johnathon stands in the graveyard, his back robe blowing in the slight night breeze and his white wizardish hair tied back, he grins and grabs a hold of one of the white sheets. "You, my brother are the soul reason this man is in the ground today as it was at your hands that he died. I don’t mean that he died in your hands, but because of your hands. Are you ready?" There was no way of Salaok answering but the grin on his face which had been brought about from the in-ring memories was still evident. Thinking that he might actually be ready for such a thing, Salaok nods to himself anyways as Johnathon begins to pull the sheet off the headstone to reveal something that maybe Salaok wasn’t ready for. Chris D. Born: ? Past: 2004 Wife to Cheryl Davis Father to Johnathon, Salaok, Eve Davis and Mani Shigeru Grandfather to Darrell and Emmanuell Forgotten by none. Perhaps Salaok wasn’t ready for this one just yet as his grin faded to a look of horror. Not only were the memories of his father coming back, but the fact that so many names had been printed on that headstone gave him an inside into the many family members he had but had forgotten. His sister Eve, how could he ever have forgotten the little sister that he had tried to look after while his father was missing and Johnathon was in jail? And her son who had been like a brother to Salaok’s son Darrell. He had to find them again. But then the memories of his father became more and more evident to which he wasn’t wanting as the two men had never gotten along well in life death Chris had staged his own death in order to run off with the Chinese slut which he had been cheating on his wife with. The memories of finding his father when Salaok was just a teenager, death in his bedroom, or at least he thought so. Slowly as the memories filed through the years, they locked onto 2004 where Salaok held a gun under the chin of his father. Salaok’s face changed again from a look of horror, to a look of cold, emotionless nothingness. His eyes stared off into the void as his mind played the images in his mind over and over as Chris D.’ head snaps back and blood and skull flies up into the hotel room. Again and again the snapping of Salaok’s father’s head and the blood rains down onto everything, including Salaok. Slowly he began to remember how it was he could be driven to do such a thing and the memories swarmed like a cyclone inside his mind until finally they all stopped. Everything stopped. Collapsing to the floor with a thud, Salaok passes out. Passes out with a grin sprawled across his face. The same evil grin that he had always seen on his brother’s Elegantly Evil face. Very little had been know of Salaok when he was entered into the tournament known only as GTT5 and now that three rounds have passed and the fourth was about to take place, still very little knew anything of the man. In fact, the people who had been watching his matches had been the same few people with him drawing no extra attention from anyone which didn’t bother him greatly. Although it was only now that Salaok had made it once again to round four that people had begun to speak, but wrongly and misinformed about who he was. A Social recluse, while not in those words exactly, that is how one of his opponents, Samuelsson had dared to describe him while attempting to do the old school rip on all three of his opponents for this round. Some may say it was a brave move, other, a stupid move by a brave man. However when looking at the situation, it was the same move that everyone else had done at one point in time in their careers and no doubt would end up doing again despite the fact that it isn’t always the best way to gain positive attention for yourself. And yet that is what all of this is about now isn’t it. A wrestling tournament which the heads have decided to go out and mess with everything by putting all four remaining contestants in each bracket against each other. Good move? For the level of competition in the final rounds, of course it is because this way there is no chance in hell of anyone getting a free ride into the final 8. Only the best two from each bracket will go through which means that Salaok and someone else will have to face off next week. Coming back to wrestling after 2 years of not just not doing it, but not remembering it, had been a little harder than most would have expected. However it had all come back to him fairly quickly and now being in the top 16 out of 126, he knew that there was still steps to be taken into getting further. One of those steps was winning this match. Not coming second and still getting through, but actually outright winning the damn thing to prove to those who didn’t give him a snowballs chance in hell that he is that damn good. You don’t climb to the top of a federation 6 times without having the skills to control a match or take on more than one man as a time. And that is what this round is all about. Proof. People say that proof is in the pudding, a stupid saying but it means well. Proof is not believing the hype. Proof is going out there and beating everyone to an inch of there lives just because you want it that little bit extra, and in this case, no one was wanting this more than Salaok. As he drove into the arena parking lot, the same eligantly evil grin stretched across his face and he knew then and there that he wanted this, more than anyone else. It wasn’t about wanting to win for the sake of winning, but to remember just how much fun he used to have when causing pain. Salaok is finally back.
