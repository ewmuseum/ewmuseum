In a New York Minute

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-20 {On a cold and damp night down below in an old deserted condemned building. We find ourselves in a very dimmed, but flooding with cigar smoke room. Where we see Ace playing cards with Chris “Money Maker”, Christian Ferguson, and Comedian Tom Green.} Tom Green: "Hey Ace it's your deal.... hey guys could I get a loan from you guys I'm a little short?" {Everybody looks at Tom with a lot of disgust for not brining enough to the table. The four of them have been playing for six hours straight and so far the big winner is Christian Ferguson.} Christian Ferguson: "Sure Tom like it would make any difference the way my luck is right know there is no chance of you guys winning any of your money back!" {No answer just eerie silences as the other three just stare at him angrily.} Tom Green: "Ok I'll let you hold these naked pictures of my EX-Drew!" {Tom hands the pictures to Christian Ferguson in return for the small loan. We also see Ace bandage from the bruising, cuts, and the gargantuan knot on his head. Plus the Ace of Spades is down to his last nickel.} Ace: "Christian, Tom shut the hell up before I break both of your necks!" Chris “Money Maker”: "Anybody have any predictions on who is going to win at I-PPV?" Ace: "The only prediction I have for you is if we don't get back to this game I am going to Spade all of you dumb asses in about two seconds!" Tom Green: "Damn chill Ace, you’re about as steamed as Drew was when she found out that I suck worst in bed then I do as a Comedian or actor!" {Ace and the others stare at Tom in amazed that anybody could be that stupid. Then Ace finally reveals the next game.} Ace: "I got it; I'll put my car in to the pot. Then you guys will follow by putting in all that you guys have on hand. Then we each will pick a card, just one. And the highest one wins." Tom Green: "Ok I'm in." “Money Maker” and Christian Ferguson: "You can count us in as well." {Ace starts to shuffle the deck which seems forever till he is done. Then he passes each one at the table a card.} Tom Green: "Wooo hooo, Read them and weep boys a Queen of Spades!!!!" Chris “Money Maker”: “So what it doesn't beat my King of Hearts!" Christian Ferguson: "I have a King of spades, what do you have Ace?" {Ace holds his card slowly letting loose a sinister grin without revealing the card identity to quickly.......} Ace: “Well what do you know boys an Ace of Spades and we all know Ace's always beats the King. Just like team EPW will do at the I-PPV when we will be the last team to survive. Now give me my damn pot you half witted losers!" {And with that Ace takes his pot and walks out the back of the door heading into what he believes to be a win at Survival.} {Ace comes home to his penthouse suite in Manhattan New York after the charity he received from Chris “Money Maker”, Christian Ferguson, and Comedian Tom Green winning all of there money. Tired and sore he gets out of his Limo in front of his Hotel building.} Front Doorman: “Good Evening Sir." Ace: “Yeah whatever Ben just make sure my limo is ready in the morning Dip Shit!" {Ace goes into the extremely beautiful 25 floor Hotel. Ace starts to head over to the elevator to go up to the penthouse suite. When he gets there he can hear the TV going on inside with some Wrestling match inside. When he gets inside he sees a blonde haired bimbo that has been staying at his place since one of Aces parties a couple of weeks ago.} Blonde Bimbo: "Hey baby how was your Poker Game?" Ace: "Yeah I won! What are you watching?" Blonde Bimbo: "I’m watching Monday Night Rush. That Phil Botwan is sooo sexy!” Ace: "Phil who???????" {Ace starts to watch the TV as he sees Phil the Neo Nazi Neon wrestler on Monday Night Rush.} Ace: "Damn what in the Hell do these losers think they are doing? That’s not wrestling! That settles it baby go in the other room I have to use the phone!" Blonde Bimbo: "But the show isn't over yet!" Ace: "I said move it!!!!" {The Blonde Bimbo goes into the other room as Ace gets on the phone with a purpose!} Ace: "Hey......sorry to disturb you Kable but have you checked out the television? Yeah isn't that some shit! Well I know you have your own problems and you’re doing some FBI mission…..I don’t care if we lose! All I care about is getting my hands on that Nazi bastard! Right…..I do see your point. Cool let’s teach Neon and UPWA what it means to have The Deck Stacked against You! Later." {Ace gets off the phone and goes into the room where he sees the Blonde Bimbo touching her self in special places. Ace with a wicked grin licks his lips as if he is looking at a New York Tri Tip Steak and goes to join her. With four of the most promising talents in EPW one can only imagine the type of hurt they have in store for Neon and UPWA. But you have to check out I-PPV to find out more.} {The scene fades to black.}
