There's a Bloodsucker Born Every Minute

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-21 Tsunami Oni hasn’t exactly been friends with the forces of law and order. Heck, he hasn’t even been casual acquaintances with them. Still, the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea is rather surprised to be escorted out of Planet Bollywood with some of San Francisco’s finest. Sure, maybe Oni tried to take over the world a couple times back in the day, but really, where is it written that attempted world domination is illegal? And why would the SFPD be sent after him? The FBI would make more sense, or the CIA. Maybe even the JLA. These are the questions running around Tsunami Oni’s blue-haired head as he is taken past the crowds of reporters and riff-raff outside and into a police cruiser. The stocky plainclothes cop opens the door to the back, motioning for Tsunami Oni to get in. Oni does so, and the cop joins him on the other side. The two uniforms get in the front. As cameras flash, the cruiser drives off and heads down the street. There is a long moment of silence. Finally, Tsunami Oni speaks up. “So,” says Oni. “What’s going on here, detective?” “Inspector, Mister Oni” corrects the plainclothes cop. “OK… Inspector… what?” asks Oni. “Spectre,” the cop answers. “Inspector Spectre,” Oni repeats. “Any relation to a guy named Lew?” “Distant cousin,” replies Inspector Spectre. “Don’t worry. I ain’t pissed that you whipped his ass all over the ring. Man’s a real bastard.” Tsunami Oni chuckles. “Yeah, I’m with you on that,” he says. “So what is the story here? You haven’t charged me with anything and I notice I’m not in cuffs.” “All in good time, sir,” says Inspector Spectre cryptically. “All in good time.” The police cruiser drives on through the San Francisco night. After several minutes, Tsunami Oni notices they’re turning into a parking lot. He looks up and sees a brightly lit sign that reads “Krispy Kreme.” “You’re stopping for doughnuts?” asks an incredulous Oni. “Isn’t that a bit of a cliché?” “Watch it, bucko,” warns Inspector Spectre. “Us cops can’t afford fancy restaurants like you can. How’d you get so much loot, anyhoo?” “Well, being UWF World champ pays pretty good,” says Oni. “And then there’s all the marketing. My image in TOTALTARIANISM COMICS, the bath toys Oni-O’s breakfast cereal. And I’ve branched out into other businesses. There’s my nightclub, Deepblue. Not to mention Shiatsu’s adult entertainment production work. And I’ve been working on an idea for a chain of housewares retail shops with a hip-hop theme.” “What’s that gonna be called?” asks the inspector. “Bed, Bath, and Beee-yotch,” Oni answers. The cruiser goes through the drive-thru and the cops get a dozen doughnuts. All three are munching away on the sticky goodness. Inspector Spectre offer one to Tsunami Oni, but he declines. “It’ll go right to the waistline,” he explains. The cops keep chowing down on Krispy Kremes as they go back on the road. Several minutes pass, then the car comes to a stop. “OK, we’re here,” says the inspector. Oni looks out the window and sees the Planet Bollywood building. “Back at the restaurant?” asks a befuddled Vivaldi of Violence. “Nah,” say Inspector Spectre. “We need you across the street. See that office building? Well, some psycho has taken a bunch of civilians hostage. And the only person he’ll talk to is a professional wrestler. And conveniently enough, we got a big one here in town. We saw you going into Planet Whatchamcallit there and knew you were the man for the job.” “So why did you drive me all the way to Krispy Kreme?” asks Oni. “We could’ve just walked across the street.” “And work on an empty stomach?” the inspector exclaims. “I got this blood-sugar deal. If I don’t eat, I get all woozy.” “Fine,” says Oni. “So what do you want me to do?” “You’re gonna help?” says the inspector. “I thought we’d have to browbeat you with stuff about civic duty and love for you fellow human being. This is really great of you.” “Eh, no worries,” says Oni. “Something weird always seems to happen right before I have a match. I don’t know why, it just does. So where’s the perp at?” “Hey!” yells the cop behind the wheel. “You can’t say “perp.” Only we get to. You don’t want us talking about “getting pops” and junk.” “Uh... sorry,” says Oni. He rolls his eyes before turning back to Inspector Spectre. “So do I get a bulletproof vest?” “Afraid not,” the inspector replies. “all of ours are being dry-cleaned.” “What about a taser?” “No room in the budget for batteries.” “Pepper spray?” “Used it all up in the chili cook-off last week.” “K-9?” “She’s getting ready to have puppies.” “Is there anything you can give me to protect me against this nutjob?” asks Oni. “We got some puppets we use when we give anti-drug lectures at schools. Think those would help?” the inspector offers. “That’s all right.” “One’s a frog. Like that Kermit fella.” “I’ll pass,” says Oni as he opens the door. “Wish me luck.” The cops try to say something, but their mouths are full of doughnuts. Tsunami Oni sighs as he gets out of the cruiser and slams the door behind him. “This better be worth it,” mutters Oni to himself as he enters the office building. “I should be playing footsie with Shiatsu as she simulates fellatio with a carrot. Do they use carrots in Indian food? I never got to look at the menu. My Rod, I shouldn’t think about food right now. I haven’t eaten all day knowing we were going out and then this happens. Maybe I should’ve had a doughnut after all. I can’t believe I got sucked into something stupid again. This never happened when I was a heel.” The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea finds an elevator and pushes the call button. As he waits for hit, he thinks over his situation. “Maybe I should take this more seriously. Serious stuff happens to me. Well, sometimes. I mean this his some sociopath holding people hostage. Someone could get hurt. I could get hurt. I could get killed. No, Tsunami. Don’t think like that. Think positive. Think positive. The elevator door opens and as Tsunami Oni steps in he hears Buddy Holly singing over the speaker. “That’ll be the day-hey-hey… that I die…” “Lovely,” mutters Oni as the door closes. He hits the button for the top floor. As the elevator rises, Tsunami Oni continues his mantra. “Think positive, Think positive.” The elevator reaches the top floor and the doors open. Tsunami Oni is greeted by the sounds of Roberta Flack in the hallway. “Killing me softly with his song, killing me softly…” “Gaaaaaahhhhh…” moans Oni as he steps out of the elevator and into the carpeted hallway. He walks around looking for the stairs to the roof. Tsunami Oni finds them and climbs up to the top. As he gets to the door outside, Oni takes a few deep breaths for courage before slowly opening it. "Anybody order a professional wrestler?” asks Oni through the crack. Suddenly, there’s a gunshot and a bullet blasts through the door and whizzes past Tsunami Oni’s blue hair before imbedding itself in the concrete wall behind him! “Hey!” yells an understandably perturbed Oni. “It’s a good thing I’m a cruiserweight or you’d be talking to some bad-ass UWF attorneys! You’re the one that asked me up here, clown shoe!” “Oh yeah, sorry,” says an oddly young sounding male voice. “Come on out, dude.” Cautiously, Tsunami Oni opens the door the rest of the way and steps out onto the roof. He sees several frightened people sitting on the concrete. There are whites, blacks, Asians, Hispanics, straights, gays, rich, poor… truly a celebration of the rainbow of diversity in San Francisco’s population. There is one person standing, a scrawny young man wearing a Marilyn Manson shirt and black jeans, appearing to be in his late teens or very early twenties. His pale skin is covered in acne scars and his dark hair is at that in-between length. You know, the length where he wants to grow it long but his dad won’t let him, so he has it as long as he can get it without his old man yelling at him to get it cut. That length. Oh, and he has a gun. “So I guess you’re the... hostage taker... dude...” says Oni as calmly as he can with his hands up. “Uh… konichiwa.” “Who the hell are you?” asks the young man, swinging the gun to point at Tsunami Oni. The Devil from the Deep Blues Sea’s eyes grow big. “Whoah!” he yells. “I’m Tsunami Oni. UWF World champion. See the blue hair? I even spoke Japanese a second ago. You asked for a wrester and you got a superstar, kiddo!” “Damn it! You’re not a wrestler!” whines the young man. He looks annoyed and Tsunami Oni looks confused. “Yes, I am,’ he states. "If not, what the hell have I been doing rolling around with oiled-up guys in tights and why don’t they ever call the next day?” “See!” shouts the gun-toting goth kid. “That’s what I mean! You’re not a REAL wrestler! You go around saying stupid shit like that, trying to be all smart and funny and shit, but that doesn’t mean shit, man! And you know why?” “Does it have something to do with fecal matter?” “Because you ain’t ever killed anyone!” The Vivaldi of Violence does say anything for a second. “Well, you got me there,” Oni admits. “Now all you have to do is tell me what the hell that has to do with anything, errr… whatever your name is.” “Benny,” says the young man. “But when I become a wrestler, I’m gonna be called Bloodsuck!” “All right… Benny Bloodsuck,” says Oni. “So you want to be in the wrestling industry. Well, I’ve done some training so I could-” “I don’t need no training!” snaps Benny Bloodsuck. “All those moves don’t mean shit! You know what really matters?” “Catchphrases?” offers Oni. “Because you’ve said “don’t mean shit” twice now in the last minute. I’d run with that if I was you. But only on cable.” “No, a body count!” says Benny Bloodsuck with a smile that would be disturbing if it wasn’t on such a dorky guy. “Oh, that was my second guess,” says Oni. “So how did you figure all this out?” “All my life I just wanted one thing,” Benny Bloodsuck explains. “I wanted to be a pro wrestler. So as soon as I was eighteen, I went to all these federations trying to get a job. But they all took one look at me and laughed in my face! Except for this one old guy from Quebec that said he’d give me a back rub. But the rest of them laughed at me! They wouldn’t give me a chance! I had no idea why!” “Could it have been your lack of athletic ability, ignorance of wrestling skill, unattractive physique, or unappealing personality?” asks Oni. “Stop making jokes!” yells Benny Bloodsuck, waving the gun at Tsunami Oni. Some of the female hostages and a couple of the males squeal in terror. “Shut up!” Benny yells at them before turning back to the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. “I had no idea what I was missing. Then I was sitting in my mom’s living room watching GTT4. There were all these hit-men and psychos in it. They were killing people left and right! So I channel-surfed all these wrestling shows ffor and there were even more killers out there! And some of them were even vampires slaughtering people like they were sheep! That’s when I knew what I had to do! Even I wanted to be a superstar wrestler, if I wanted people to take me seriously, I was gonna have to kill and kill and kill! And that’s what I’m gonna do here tonight! I’ll show them all! All those shitheads that laughed at me! They’ll all be begging Bloodsuck to join their shitty feds!” “Uh-huuuhhhh…” says Oni. “Listen Benny… Bloodsuck… I know what it’s like when life disappoints you. When you just can’t reach your dreams.” “Really? What happened to you?” “Well, I can’t really think of anything right now,” Oni admits with a shrug. “I got it really good. Pretty much everything I could possibly want. But just in case, I have this little song I wrote…” Tsunami Oni sees a convenient hippy hostage with a convenient acoustic guitar. Oni politely asks to borrow it and the hippy hands it over. The Vivaldi of Violence experimentally plucks a few strings, then begins strumming a soft melody. “Now everybody gather around,” requests Oni. “We all can learn something from this little number…” “When the bills are overdue And it’s not just my hair that’s blue I just stop… And have sex with my hot bisexual Asian girlfriend! When I’m feeling down in the dumps And my Komodo dragon has the mumps I just stop… And have sex with my hot bisexual Asian girlfriend! When my Suzuki Hayabusa gets dented And “Desperate Housewives” is pre-empted I just stop… And have sex with my hot bisexual Asian girlfriend! When the country doesn’t seem so great And we’re stuck with Dubya until 2008 I just stop… And have sex with my hot bisexual Asian girlfriend! I have sex… With my hot bisexual Asian girlfriend And all my troubles… My troubles seem to end… And if they just won’t go We wait fifteen minutes or so And then we have sex agaaaaaaiiiiiiiiiiiiiin!” The hostages cheer wildly as Tsunami Oni bows. He raises the guitar in triumph. Then there’s a gunshot and the body of the guitar is turned into splinters. “I don’t have a hot bisexual Asian girlfriend, you asshole!” yells Benny Bloodsuck. “Hey, I was just saying what works for me,” says Oni as he hands the disembodied guitar neck to the rather morose hippy. “You stupid shit!” screams Benny Bloodsuck. “You’re laughing at me, just lie everybody else!” Tsunami Oni feels the tension rising. He knows if he doesn’t do something soon, this could all end in tragedy. Well, maybe more like tragicomedy. All the hostages are in fear for their lives. Tsunami Oni slowly walks towards Benny Bloodsuck. “Benny,” he says calmly. “You’re right. I have been making fun of you. But that’s what I do. I make fun of my opponents, even those that are among the greatest and most feared wrestlers around. So you shouldn’t feel insulted, think of it as a complement.” “Really?” says Benny Bloodsuck. He seems to relax slightly. “Really,” Oni replies. “Why don’t we step away from these nice people for a bit, talk this out?” “Uh, OK,” Benny Bloodsuck answers. Tsunami Oni walks away from the hostages and close to the edge of the roof. Benny Bloodsuck follows him. “Now get really close to me because I don’t want those guys to hear what I’m going to tell you,” says Oni. “It’s industry secrets and I don’t want any of them babbling to some Internet fansite like smarkass.com.” “I love that site!” says Benny Bloodsuck as he steps up close to Tsunami Oni. “So what what’s the big secret?” “Look into my eyes and pay very close attention,” Oni instructs him. “I’m only going to tell you this once.” Benny looks at Tsunami Oni with big brown eyes filled with wonder, like a puppy dog about to be given a treat or a child at Christmas. And that’s when Tsunami Oni spews the EVIL BLUE MIST~ in his eyes. “You asshole!” yells Benny Bloodsuck as he blindly staggers around, teetering very close to the edge. “You shithead! You fucking piece of shit!” “Don’t get all pissed off now,” says Oni calmly. “Because it’s only going to get worse.” And that’s when Tsunami Oni kicks him in the nads, sending Benny Bloodsuck off of the roof. “AAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!” screams Benny Bloodsuck in a rather high-pitched voice. Tsunami Oni peers over the edge and watches his descent. There’s the sound of a crash and Oni makes a wry face. “Ooooo… glad I took a limo,” he says. The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea turns to the horrified hostages. “Anybody driving an orange Gremlin?” “Uh, I am,” says the hippy, still holding the guitar neck. “Sorry, just not your night,” says Oni. “Hope you got insurance.” “Dude, I can’t believe you just killed that guy!” says the hippy. “Oh please,” says Oni with a roll of his eyes. “Like everybody didn’t see that happening a mile away.” And with that Tsunami Oni leaves the hostages and heads back down As he exits the building, Inspector Spectre runs up to him with the two other cops. “How’s it going, officers?” asks a rather perky Oni. “How’s it going?” echoes the inspector. “You just killed a guy in front of witnesses! Hell, we witnessed it! Four different camera crews filmed it! You’re under arrest for murder, buddy!” “No, I’m not,” Oni replies coolly. “And why the hell not?” “Because I have this,” says Oni. He takes out his wallet and removes a laminated card, which he hands to Inspector Spectre. The inspector looks at it and reads…. FRATERNAL ORDER OF SPORTS ENTERTAINERS CHAPTER 619 MEMBER NAME: TSUNAMI ONI “What the hell is this?” asks the inspector. “My union card,” Oni answers. “It shows I’m a certified sports entertainer. Which means I can’t be arrested for any acts I perform in the course of a promo, no matter how illegal, heinous, or just plain ludicrous. “He’s got us there, inspector,” says one of the cops. “You don’t wanna mess with a union!” “Yeah, you’re right,” admits Inspector Spectre. “You’re free to go, Mister Oni.” “Thanks,” says Oni. “Could I have my card back? It gets me discounts at my dry cleaners.” “Oh, eyah,” say Inspector Sprectre, handing the card back. “Good night, gentlemen,” says Tsunami Oni with a polite nod before going back into Planet Bollywood. He walks up behind the lovely Shiatsu and gives her a peck on the neck before taking his seat. “I went ahead and ordered for you, sweetie,” says Shiatsu. “So what was up with those cops?” “Oh, they had a situation they wanted to consult me on,” says Oni. “Well, I hope it worked out all right.” “I think everything fell into place,” Oni replies before taking a sip of his kamikaze. “But it’s just…” “Just what, sweetie?” asks Shiatsu. “It’s just…” Tsunami Oni begins, a look of deep concern on his face. “I have this feeling this evening is only two-thirds of the way over.” And the San Francisco night gets a little bit colder…
