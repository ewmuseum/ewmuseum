Your own death is the least painful.

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-09 It had only been two matches, but Salaok was finding that this combat stuff was coming back to him faster than what he was expecting it to. However, getting into a ring and fighting is more about the person you are than the memory of how to fight as the Flight or Fight theory comes into play. There are two types of responses when faced with a threat. One of them is to run away, thus being the Flight aspect. It’s better to be safe than it is to face the threat and deal with it despite the fact you may be hurt. The other is to Fight the threat and go face to face with it knowing full well that the conflict in which you are about to meet head on, you may not come out the victor. Salaok was quickly relearning the fact that there was nothing he would back down from and this tournament was proving to be just what he was needing to find out the kind of man he used to be. However, there was more of his past life which was needing to be uncovered and while day after day small pieces were coming back to him, the big picture was yet to be revealed. Who was the mother of his son, was he married to her, where was she and the question that plagued Salaok’s mind each night was what had happened to him? After leaving the venue from his round two match, Salaok had headed straight back to the graveyard which was a mistake and he knew it. However this JD person was that had been showing him segments of his live, clearly was trying to keep things in control for some unknown reason. Perhaps what Salaok was going to be remembering in time needed to be spread out in order to stop any sort of mental overload. First this JD had shown him the house and given the car back to him, then revealed the fate of Salaok’s son which not only had knocked Salaok literally on his ass, but had made him wonder if it was all worth it. The life in which Salaok was living during the time he knew nothing of who he was, had been a simple life but one that he was enjoying. Little to no responsibility or a care in the world as he found the world and himself for the first time. Or so it seemed. But now he stands, paused in time in front of the headstone of his only child Darrell while the snow begins to drift down from the heavens above. As the thin layering of white begins to cover all that is lucky enough to touch it, he does not move, just stares. Over and over his eyes move across the etching on the headstone but the line that struck him most was the last one. One day I will hold you again To hold his child again. A wish that he shouldn’t have to wish, nor should any man. Slowly his eyes begins to move up as he looks over at the other headstones in which litter the area of the graveyard, still covered, hidden from his eyes as to not overload the memory of the pain in which he has caused the world. "You want to know who they are don’t you?" A voice from behind him said quietly but didn’t surprise Salaok as he continues to stare off at the dark markers of death and simply gives a nod. "You will find out in due time Salaok but Johnathon doesn’t want you to have too much revealed to you all at once." This last comment caused Salaok to spin on his heel and face the man who was a lot closer than Salaok was expecting as their noses touch in the cold icy air. Stepping back with a look of utter confusion on his face, his eyes squint and his mouth opens but at first nothing comes forth. That name. That cold name that Salaok held once idolised and then feared, only to then walk side by side with the man. "You know that name don’t you Salaok? You remember Johnathon, your brother?" The man’s voice was controlling and sounded almost mocking as he toyed with Salaok’s memories again. It was this very man who had led Salaok to the house and then brought Salaok to see his son’s final resting-place. But now he simply has to mention the name of Salaok’s older brother in order to get a response from his which was exactly what he had been hoping for. The battle in Salaok’s mind was a strange one as so many memories begin to pop back into existence, each one wanting more attention than the one before it until finally one of the flash backs locks in. Young children screaming in a circle as they watch three young boys in a school playground, one laying flat on his back with blood trickling out of his nose, one boy kneeling, crying out for someone to help him, and the third, older boy with his hands wrapped around the kneeling boy’s head with a grin on his face. "Should I do it Sal?" The older boy says to a young Salaok laying on the ground. Looking up at the older boy and then at the face of the one who he holds with hatred in his eyes from the beating in which he was just given, Salaok grins and nods to his old brother Johnathon. "Do it." A look of pure fear runs clear across the face of the boy in Johnathon’s hands but it’s all too late as his hands tighten in position and twist the neck slow in one direction first. "You know what really cool about what I’m about to do to you Josh? The fact that I’ll say that I didn’t know what I was doing and then it was all just a little accident. But you and people like you have to learn that you don’t place your hands on my brother and after what I do next, you never will again." Johnathon’s voice was cold as it whispered into Rob’s ear, hinting at the fact that he did intend on getting away with this murderous crime. Salaok’s eyes widened as he watched his brother kill another boy in order to protect him and it was that day that Salaok fell in love with the power in which his older brother had. The confidence and the control to be able to do whatever he wanted when he wanted. That day was the day Salaok wanted to learn how to be just like his older brother, but it was also that day that his older brother was taken away from him for a number of years. "You remember your brother don’t you Salaok?" The man questioned as Salaok shook his head, snapping out of the world of memories and into the world of the now. "Can I ask what you were thinking?" The question troubled Salaok, as if this man would somehow understand what it was that Salaok’s mind was going through at this point in time and yet with everything that had gone on so far, it wouldn’t surprise him if he actually did. "You want to know what I was thinking? All I could see when you mentioned his name was the school ground…" "Ahh yes, one of Johnathon’s favourite stories." The man cuts Salaok off and catches his off guard as he continues. "Yes, Johnathon said that was the day that you changed from a weakling to a person who had belief in himself and the drive to be better. But now look at you Salaok…" Salaok’s eyes flared up with anger as he listened to this man in who he had no idea of his identity dare to question the kind of person he was. Salaok’s hand not only presses hard against the chest of his tormentor, but also grabs a hold of the seemingly expensive silk shirt and pulls him close. "I am the person I am because no one has been willing to let me know who I am and the only one who had given me a glimpse into my former life is a man who I know nothing about and holds back so much information from me. So tell me, what the fuck is your name and where is my brother?" Silence as Salaok holds the man close to his face and yet there is no backing down nor breaking of control as the man grabs a hold of Salaok’s hand and slowly forces it to the side and away from his body. "I am Michael, and that is all you will know me as during this time. As for your brother, he does not wish for you to met with him yet." "That is something which is out of his control I dare say. Now there are two situations which can happen here and neither is what you would have had planned for tonight’s trip down fucked up memory lane. The first option is you take me to see my brother and have him explain everything to me, or the second is that I will remove the covers from these headstones and find out exactly who it is that I’ve caused the deaths of as you so put it." His voice booming with frustration of knowing that this man knew the answers to his questions and yet couldn’t get a single one of out him. And yet there was one small issue with this Michael person. While he seemed to know all of the answers regarding Salaok, why did Salaok not remember him? It was a question which didn’t need to be answered now for he had no doubt that just like everything else so far, it would be revealed to him in due time. Time which Salaok simply was growing short on. "I will do you a deal Salaok." Michael answered back, completely and utterly in control still despite the booming demands of Salaok. "I show you the one thing which I had intended on revealing to you tonight and then I will take you to see your brother. Deal?" Salaok nods, not fully getting his way but the end result would be the same regardless as a meeting with his brother would open so many of the doors which his mind still held locks on for some reason. Locks which had begun to control his life and cause questions to be asked as to what could be so bad behind those doors that he couldn’t afford to look at yet. With more pieces of snow continuing to dance down from the sky, Michael walks over to the covered headstone two over from young Darrell’s and places his hand on the sheet. Michael looks back at Salaok who looks on with eagerness and fear in his eyes know full well that the reason that the headstone existed was because of him. As the snow covered sheet is pulled back from the headstone, many thoughts enter Salaok wandering mind as he sees his name is big italic lettering. Salaok Davis Harvester of Minds Born 8th of May 1978 Died 16th of August 2004 That was it! There was no message from a loved one or small quote about his life, just a plain straightforward headstone that gave the impression that it was organised by the funeral company with no help from family at all. More importantly, Salaok wasn’t dead! "When you were shot in the head, the doctors had actually pronounced you publicly dead at the request of your brother in order to make sure your recovery wouldn’t be hindered in any way shape or form as he knew then it would be a long one." Michael’s voice carried the same cold tone that it always did, showing no sign of emotion of care for what Salaok was actually going through. "Did you not think that the strange looks you got when you entered the PTC tournament were for a reason?" "Did my family know the truth?" His question was a direct one aimed at getting Michael to spill more information than what he was meant to be giving, however it didn’t work his Salaok’s favor this time either. "Johnathon is your family and that’s all you need to care about at the moment." "What about my wife?" Salaok’s eyes dart from the headstone from which they had been locked to, up to Michael’s to get his full attention. "Did my wife know I was still alive? I have this gold band on my finger Michael, I know I was married." "I dare say yes, your wife would have known, now no more questions about her. You will find out what you need to find out in due time." Michael moves away from the headstone as snow begins to collect on the top of it, much like everything else it was touching. Walking past Salaok, Michael heads to the car and waits for Salaok who simply stands there looking at the headstones that had been revealed to him. The first was of his son and the second was him which lead him to think that the grave between the two must be another family member of fair importance. Another child? His wife? It was propelling every thought pattern in his head and he couldn’t help but walk over and grasp a hold of the sheet and begin to wrench it back. Bang The crack of a gun sounds and stops Salaok dead in his tracks as Michael holds a gun pointed in Salaok’s direction and with it, motions for Salaok to step away. "You’re not ready yet Salaok, don’t do something that you will regret, thus forcing me to do something I will not regret." He says while waving the gun, hauntingly. "Let’s go visit your brother." Slowly letting go of the sheet, not wanting to be shot, Salaok takes one final look at the middle headstone before walking over to Michael in the parking lot. As Salaok walks closer to the awaiting car, Michael opens his door and sits in the driver’s seat but doesn’t close the door, wanting to say something to Salaok before he gets in. "Remember Salaok, you’re the one who decided to do this, not me." Michael’s voice, for the first time sounding like a small piece of care was slipping in as he words echoed more of a warning of things to come to which Salaok merely nods in response as he walks around the front of the car becoming illuminated by the headlights. Dropping himself into the passenger’s seat, Salaok closes the door behind him and knows that the next time he gets out of the car will be to come face to face with his brother for the first time. His thoughts however are disturbed as Michael speaks while driving out of the graveyard parking lot. "He will not tell you about your wife if that’s what you’re looking for." Salaok’s response was the one that Michael was expecting him to give in, "Why not?" "He would rather have you relive it all over again." And with that, nothing else was spoken for the rest of the trip.
