PTC Tape #1: The Bomb Scare

18 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-18 "Another tape from Phil Botwin has appeared. This time though, it is not for the folks of Neon, but for a bigger stage. The world stage. These tapes will be seen by a number of people that can only be dreamed up by movie stars. This tape is like the long-awaited sequel in a popular series of movies. Nothing can be of the calibre this movie brings. Now, though, all we can do is watch." ::: The scene opens up in the main lobby of a very elegant building. The secretary sits at the desk directly in front of the doors. Her head is bowed over the desk, sorting through a stack of papers, and paying no attention to the world around her. A small-framed man wearing all black ventures shiftily into the building, glancing furtively around him. He sidles up to the desk. Oblivious to the secretary, he places a big brown envelope on the desk, and in the same manner as when he came in, wanders out the doors. Just as the man leaves, another walks in, wearing an expensive suit, and holding a briefcase, looking curiously at the man who just exited. He walks up to the desk, immediately grabbing the attention of the secretary, and leans on the top surface. ::: Man-"Do you know who that was?" ::: The secretary frowns. ::: Secretary-"Who?" Man-"There was a man dressed in complete black that just exited the building... I didn't recognize him, so I was wondering if you did." ::: The secretary looks at the man, confused, for a minute, as she knows very well that she didn't see a man in black enter or exit the building. The man keeps his slouched pose against the desk as the secretary shakes her head. ::: Secretary-"There was no man in black here today – as far as I know – but I guess I can call for a copy of the security tapes to find out.” ::: The secretary points at the camera above the desk, which rotates slowly, as she picks up the phone and punches in a few numbers. After a few seconds, she begins to talk into the receiver. ::: Secretary-"Yes, would I be able to get footage of the last 20 minutes in this lobby?" ::: She nods her head several times, then places the receiver down. She looks up at the man as the scene cuts out. ::: -------------------------------------------------------------------- ::: The scene opens up back in the lobby. The secretary and the man are now behind the desk, along with a security guard with the initials 'PTC' embossed on his baseball cap. They are all surrounding a small monitor that has been moved to the top of the desk. Currently playing is a tape of the lobby from the viewpoint of the CCTV camera located above the desk. The secretary watches the footage, frowning as the man in black walks up to the desk and places the envelope down. She looks over, and sitting right on the edge of the desk is the envelope. She stands up, and walks over to the desk where the brown envelope is. ::: Secretary-"This is why the man in black was here..." ::: The woman goes to pick up the envelope but the security guard swats her hand down. ::: Security-"You can't touch that until we're sure it isn't dangerous. Back away, lady. Back away." ::: The secretary backs away from the desk as the scene cuts back out again. ::: -------------------------------------------------------------------- ::: We are once again in the lobby, but now there are crowds of people all huddled into the small room. Part of the room is marked off by police tape, including the half of the desk with the envelope. There are cops on the inside of the tape telling people that there is nothing to worry about, and a small group of heavily armoured men are moving ever closer to the envelope. Two of them carefully pick up the envelope, and place it down on the ground beside them. One of them pulls out a small knife, and begins to cut off the top seal to the envelope. They cut off the last part and they all step back, expecting something big to happen. After a few seconds of silence from everyone, one of the armoured men moves forward again, and reaches his hand into the envelope. He pulls out a video-tape, and drops it to the ground. A big sigh goes over the crowd at the thought of it not being that interesting – not being a bomb, as most at the PTC headquarters were thinking - and many begin to file out of the building. The security guard walks through the police tape and picks up the cassette, bringing it around the desk and disappearing below it. The big flat screen monitor hanging above the desk changes to static for about 10 seconds, then the guard re-emerges as the monitor comes to life. ::: -------------------------------------------------------------------- ::: The video starts up showing the small concrete room that Phil Botwin, Neon Wrestling Cruiserweight champion and escaped convict, has been inhabiting for a while now. The camera is steady for once: no one is holding it. Phil sits at the corner most likely designated as 'dining room', with a square table shoved into place, and two chairs, one tucked under the table, and the other occupied by Phil. He takes a sip from the mug he holds in his hands, then places it down on the table, looking over at the camera. ::: Botwin-"I see that there was a bomb threat at the PTC building." ::: Phil laughs to himself, a giant smile appearing on his face. ::: Botwin-"I don't know if it really happened, but in the new age America, you never know. They blow everything out of proportion, even the smallest things, like losing. This brings me to the real reason I have sent in this tape, the one you are watching right now... the PTC Survival event that I have been recruited to perform in by my dear old President Hyde. I've proven to him, and all those that watch Neon, that I am the best of the best, and now all I need to do, is prove it to the rest of you… which shouldn’t be that hard.” ::: Phil stands up from the chair, and moves across the room, and out of the picture. The view pans around to where Phil is, which is the designated kitchen with a small sink and a mini-freezer. He opens up the mini-freezer, and pulls out a small bottle of water. Sitting on the mini-freezer is Phil’s recently won Cruiserweight Title. Phil picks it up, and brings it with him over to the couch that the camera sits on. The camera pulls back, shaking a little, and changing to a high up shot of Phil relaxing on the couch with the title belt sitting over his lap ::: Botwin-“You see this? This is one of the reasons people know how great I am. The filth in this world will not recognize you as a superior, unless you have some sort of trophy to prove it, and now I do. But this is only my first trophy - later I will claim my second, the Neon World title. I can already feel the metal plate, and the cheap rubber straps around my waist. Hyde may be a good businessman, but when it comes to the conditions within his company, he couldn’t care less. It is only a matter of time. Time that I do not wish to spend waiting around, so that is why I am here participating in this event. You all shall feel the pain that Colin Dzur felt… the pain that Jakob Knight felt, and the pain Shawn Tryant most definitely felt when I knocked him out with his own finishing move. It doesn’t matter who you are, or why you’re here, but if you step in my way, there will be consequences to face… and medical bills for you to pay.” ::: Phil rubs the title belt, smirking, and then moves it to the cushion next to him. He opens the small bottle of water, and takes a drink from it, the view beginning to shake a bit more as Phil shudders from the cold of the water. ::: Botwin-“Well, I have to bid you all a farewell for now… I have a match to prepare for, and in not as much time as I would wish.” ::: Phil gives the thumbs up, and the view goes down to the floor. After a few seconds, the scene cuts out, and the footage ends ::: -------------------------------------------------------------------- ::: The lobby is left staring at the static. The security guard has joined everyone else on the other side of the desk, and just stares blankly up at the screen. ::: Security-“Well… we know it’s not dangerous.” ::: The security guard moves around the desk and takes the tape out. He places it on the desk and looks over at the secretary. ::: Security-“Make sure someone high up in the company views this… they can probably get more from it than we can.” ::: The secretary nods, then moves back behind the counter. The security guard adjusts his ball cap, and moves out a door labelled ‘Authorized Personnel Only’. The rest of the people in the lobby begin to file out as the scene comes to a close. :::
