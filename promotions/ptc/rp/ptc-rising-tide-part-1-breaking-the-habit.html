[IG 26] Rising Tide (Part 1: Breaking The Habit)

5 February 2007

I know what you're thinking... you've heard all this before. But things have changed now, and adrenaline was not my only drug After all. You see, I was addicted to something many people in my line or work have fallen victim to... success. I was on top of the world, with the united states title in hand it seemed like I was destined for greatness. On the verge of becoming the next big thing. Then, just like that... everything was gone. One by one the stages came: 1. Denial - After the loss to Archibald Mc...whatever, no one gives a shit how to say it, and my US Title loss to Brandon Youngblood. I held my head up high. It was just a phase, in a matter of time I'd get my title back and everything would be okay. Things had gone so well in the past, why would they change now? 2. Rage - A couple weeks went by, and I couldn't even seem to win a single match. Victories that once came to me easily started slipping away through my fingers. As everyone in the locker room quietly snickered at my misfortune, I was too busy throwing chairs and screaming at people to realize what was really going on. A few injuries of GCW staff members later that took a significant dent in my wallet, I had come to the conclusion that maybe this wasn't the route to go. I had to think, I had to get help from the outside. 3. Bargaining - So I made a deal with the devil himself, Mr. Christian Ulrich Zenith. Don't be fooled by the name, he's everything but a true gentlemen. By going back to his ever waiting arms, I was sure to regain the trust of my once close friends The Hunting Grounds. When the time was right, I'd use them to get back at Archibald and take the Extreme Title that was rightfully mine. Things weren't meant to be, and my master plan came crashing down during the match where my own "allies" turned on me and left Nova to take my title. Little did I realize the consequences of my actions would be so severe. I suppose stage 4 is where this story begins: depression. Locking myself in my room every night contemplating my future was a typical night for your Fearless Phenom. Wondering what would go wrong next, throwing a pity party for anyone who would listen. Being subtlety has never been one of my strong points. Maybe if I was just a little more observant and a little less selfish, I could've prevented this whole thing from happening in the first place. After all, this was about me... not him. Markus had nothing to do with this. I'd used one of my closest friends as a pawn towards my own fucking selfish desires. None of which paid off, by the way. Playing with fire one too many times, I deserved to get burned. He didn't. He deserved better than this. How could I let this happen? How nieve could I have been? TWO: The Fall Of The House Of D'Artifice Click... Click... Click... Although not much louder than the drop of a pin, the click of footsteps sliced through the silence of the estate like a knife. Resonating through the dimly lit hallway, the hours upon hours of homework and surveillance of the McFarlane Estate had finally paid off. For the first time in nearly three weeks, everyone was asleep and locked tightly in their rooms. Like a cobra stalking its prey through the brush, the keen cadet blue eyes of a killer pierced through the darkness. Putting his hand on the cold doorknob he held his breath. Slowly easing the door open, he could see the dim yet dusky lights of the hallway cast upon the wood floors of the room. With every small creak the door made, the chance of everything going wrong grew exponentially. The light stopped just a few inches short of an oak framed bed on the far side of the room. Inside the bed, a man laid... looking almost as if he were dead under the moonlight. His chest was still, his face almost frozen in time like a photograph. Slowly sliding through the door, his shadow cast upon the floor. He used his shoulder to slowly close the door behind him while keeping the doorknob turned with his freehand. Concealing himself in darkness, the man put his hand against his chest and slowly crept it under his jacket. His palm gripped the cold hard steel of a familiar handheld weapon. Emotionless, he moved forward towards the oak bed... looking down upon the body that lay before him. The moonlight accented his icy blue eyes, looking down at the young man. Holding the gun steady and true, he moved it closer to the young man's temple... not making as much as a peep. A blowing breeze outside the window was the only sound to be heard, this was the work of a professional. Years upon years of training and experience lead towards this moment. With the pull of a trigger, everything would fall into place. "Do you take me for a fool?" A voice broken threw the silence like a crowbar through a windshield. With that said the young man's eyes show wide open, and before he could even react the man with an icy stare found himself pinned up against the wall... with the gun bouncing on the hardwood floors before sliding towards the door. "Did you forget what I am? I am a creature of the night..." The young man spoke in a hushed tone, "Or is your head so far up your ass that you've forgotten what I've become. No... what you've turned me into. Give me one good reason why I shouldn't snap you half right now, and feed you to the starving wolves in the forest." "Markus..." The man's icy stare turned into a bit of a smirk as he looked back at Markus Eaux D'Artifice, "I think you're in no position to be making idle threats. My job here, is already done." The professional flicked a needle sticking out of the back of Markus' right shoulder blade. Suddenly the implications of his words became very clear. A tingling sensation shot down the left arm of The Childe Of Destiny. Dropping the intruder, Markus quickly lost control of his left arm unable to move it. The tingling sensation turned into a slow burn. Trying to scream out for help it was too late. He was fully paralyzed unable to move his mouth enough for a single sound to escape. His eyes blinked repeatedly looking up at the man who had brought about this twist of fate. Picking his gun up off the ground and adjusting his outfit, Markus tried desperately to move while the world around him faded to black. Leaving nothing but the sound of footsteps to vibrate his eardrums... Click... Click... Click... THREE: Breaking Down I remember the next morning just like it was yesterday. It was the second time I can ever remember Lia looking at me the way she did. The first was the very first time we met, in a tennis court outside of the Zenith Corporation Office in Chicago. If I remember correctly, she was sent there to make sure we never met again. After seeing that look the second time, I still don't know how I survived. It was a glare that ripped the heart right out of your chest and stomped it into the ground, leaving you breathless. It wasn't the look of a best friend that I'd grown to love. It was the look of a woman scorned. The kind of look that you see in your nightmares. The kind of look that I hope I never have to see again. In my defense at the time it didn't seem like such a big deal. After all, how much damage could a fucking vampire be on his own? He eats people for breakfast... or at least, most do. Markus didn't have that killer instinct. I suppose that's Lia was so upset about. Looking back, I really don't have an excuse. I was tired of being shoved down. I was ready to shove back... I just regret that Lia was the one who took the brunt of it. "Vivica." Lia Ambrosi spoke out over the television destroying the hearing of The Black Mariah. Turning around, Vivica J. Valentine looked back at her Dies Irae stablemate. With a sigh Vivica hit the pause button on the video. Stuck on the screen was a picture of 'The New Shit' Bobino... in the middle of another trash talking 'masterpiece.' By masterpiece is meant Vivica would much rather listen to K-Fed's 'Playing With Fire' album than listen to Bobino talk about his almost-victory over her... AGAIN. "They should seriously play this tape for hostage situations..." Vivica responded, putting her hand against her forehead and shaking her head. "A couple hours of this preschool shit throwing would have any terrorist on his hands and knees giving up promises of 80 virgins in exchange for not having to listen to another 30 seconds of this." "Where is Markus?" Lia asked, walking into the room and closing the door behind her. A look of seriousness crossed her face while she leaned up against the wall. "He probably killed himself from hearing this garbage in the other room." Vivica replied sarcastically, shrugging her shoulders. "Quit screwing around Viv." Mrs. Ambrosi walked up to The Black Mariah, who had been sitting in the chair in front of the television for longer than anyone in the house can remember. It seemed like it was all she did anymore. Lock herself in her room, watch old tapes of her former US Title victories, take a couple of seconds to look over her opponent, show up to shows, lose, repeat. "I don't know Lia..." Vivica sighed as she looked back at the GCW World Heavyweight Champion, "I have things I need to do. I have an Infinite Gauntlet defense in a couple days against a couple of upstart assholes who have nothing better to do with their free-time than take half ass shots at me, apparently. I'm having enough problems trying to do something with my life as it is. I don't have enough time to babysit Markus too." "I don't think you understand Vivica." Lia responded, obviously becoming irritated with The Fearless Phenom and her 24/7 pity party. "Markus isn't like everyone else. He can't run off and check into a hotel room and live off of a couple hundred bucks. He can't just pull into a McDonald's when he's hungry. All of his blood packets are still in the cooler, not a single one is gone. You know he doesn't just run off like this. He hates being out and about." "Maybe he just needed a bit of fresh air Lia..." Vivica retaliated, "He sits in the house all day, I'd have cabin fever if I were him. He'll be back. He's practically immortal, what can possibly go wrong?" The look in the Ebon Goddess' eyes could kill any man under the age of 16, and spoke more volumes than any words ever could. Quickly The Black Mariah felt herself get smaller. Her tongue swelled in her mouth and her throat began to close. "What Lia? What am I supposed to do?!" Vivica stands up, choking out words in obvious frustration, "I sit around in the locker room all day during events and take shit about how I never win. I watch tapes all day and all night, trying to figure out what in the fuck is wrong with me. Now I'm supposed to go out one some chase to find Markus with absolutely NO clues that anything is wrong at all. So I can show up to PTC, lose the only fucking thing I have that means anything in this business, and take more shit in the GCW locker room the next day?!" "You know what Vivica, if I were you..." Lia began to speak but was quickly cut off. "That's exactly the problem Lia. I'm not you." Vivica's voice became more cracked as she spoke, amidst an emotional break down her rant continued on, "I don't have a legion of people worshiping the fucking ground I walk on. I don't have accolades upon accolades to prove my greatness without a shadow of doubt to any asshole who dare questions me. I don't have the undying support of a loved one to help me along the path. I don't have your knack for this business. I have NOTHING. Except for giving my heart and busting my ass out there... which is NEVER fucking good enough. What in the hell do you want from me? To be perfect?!" Lia looked down at her watch, wondering how long Vivica was going to go on before she finally came to her senses and realized that her best friend was in trouble. This explosion of feeling though, was a long time coming. It just took something like this to set it off. "You're not even paying attention to me are you?" Vivica squeaked out through her voice, which felt almost as wide as the end of a ball-point pen, "You're just like everyone else around here Lia. A 'what have you done for me lately?' friendship. I'm getting out of here. I can't take this anymore." "Vivica what in the world is wrong with you?!" Lia tried to talk some sense into The Black Mariah, but it was far too late for that. "I'll show all of you. You'll fucking see!" Vivica screamed back, "I'm going to walk into PrimeTime Central and hand those two wanna-be Angelo Deville's their asses and come walking back to GCW with my championship. Then Markus is going to turn up, and I'll be there to remind all of you that I TOLD YOU SO. Just you wait and see... JUST YOU WAIT AND FUCKING SEE." With tears steaming down her face, and cheeks blood red with frustration, The Fearless Phenom picks up her car keys off the inn-table. Walking right past the world champion, Vivica grabs her black leather jacket out of the closet and shoves her arm into the hole. Angrily, she struggles to get her other arm into the jacket before finally pulling the door open and slamming it behind her with a thud that would even wake Dusk's well-deceased ability to talk about anything other than his past fed accomplishments. In the room Shadow looked over at the baboonish face of Bobino attempting to intimidate Vivica J. Valentine paused on the television. Looking back at the door and listening to the sound of Viv's Chrysler Crossfire starting outside the house, Lia took a deep breath. "Dammit." FOUR: Acceptance It was around 4:00am the next morning that I had realized what I'd done. The reality had set in as I looked down at the night stand in my hotel room. All I had left was 4 dollars and some change, and no sense of direction for what in the hell I was doing. Me, 4 bucks, my ring attire, not a single clue on Markus, and my Infinite Gauntlet Championship. That is what my life consisted of. Staring out of my fifth story hotel window at the Toronto skyline, I realized that maybe it was my job to Markus' baby-sitter. I did get him into this mess. I was the one who convinced him to leave Zenith Corporation, where he was well tested and taken care of, to the road with me and my friends. Maybe I hadn't been the best person in the entire world when it came to looking out for him, but I did care. That was more than I could say for a lot of other people in my life. A lot of people who weren't Markus. Welcome to stage 5: Acceptance. It was almost as if deep down inside of me I had some sort of life changing revelation. Maybe... just maybe, there were things in this life more important that my win/loss record or how often I won. Maybe being a great friend to someone I care about, and who cares about me, would be just as rewarding as any silly championship victory. I was just a few hours and a sleepless night away from my Infinite Gauntlet defense. After I was done welcoming "The Old, Repetitive Shit" and Generic Goth Sounding Name #1 to the major leagues, maybe I would take some time off. Spend some time with Markus, apologize to Lia and hope this whole thing would smooth over. Unfortunately, someone else had other plans for me... and my own ignorance kept me from ever seeing it coming. Even though in the back of my mind, I know I should've seen it coming from a mile away.
