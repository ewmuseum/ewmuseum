Vagrant's Choice: It gets you f*cked up 'n quick!

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 Fade into a shot of Maggot holding a bottle. The bottle is too far away from the screen for its label to be made out. MAGGOT: Hey everyone! Is you like me and is a ragin' alcoholic? Does regular beer 'n liquor not do it for you anymore? Is you so bad that you'se resorted to drinkin' rubbin' alcohol to try an' get a buzz? Well have I gots the solution for you! Maggot holds up the bottle and the camera zooms in. The label reads Vagrant's Choice Mash Liquor 199.5 Proof. MAGGOT: That's right folks. Vagrant's Choice. The highest alcohol content allowed by law in these United States. Even the most hardcore alcoholics, like myself, will find that you get drunk off yer ass quicker and you stay drunk longer wit' Vagrant's Choice. Plus, you gots more bang for yer buck. Cut to a bum taking shots of Jack Daniels. MAGGOT V/O: As you can see here, this bum is drinkin' Jack. He damn near finished the whole bottle before he started stumblin' around, peein' his pants and startin' fights with yuppies who won't give him a quarter. Cut to the same bum drinking Vagrant's Choice. MAGGOT V/O: But now look at the results of him drinkin' Vagrant's Choice. Only one shot gets him fucked up like he was drinkin' the Jack. Two shots and he's blacked out on the sidewalk! Cut back to Maggot. MAGGOT: So c'mon an' give Vagrant's Choice a shot. If it doesn't get you fucked up beyond all recognition, then we'll send you yer money back, pending a blood alcohol test. So try it. It's the only mash liquor that's sponsored by me, Maggot! Try it today! Fade out. What's the moral of the story kids? That Maggot is a raging alcoholic, and that raging alcoholics are better fighters than you and me and vampires.
