8 June 2004 The Day the Whole World Went Away- RP1 What a feeling. It is something great when you are told by a lot of people that you are worthy, worthy of something hundreds want. People from all over the world had wanted a shot at this prize, the biggest prize for everyone in the Primetime Central Community. It was the one thing that most people would kill to get, the one thing that moves the masses whenever it is defended. The Global Title is the most important thing in PTC, and Digital Mortality was going to have his chance, his chance to represent the UWF and bring it's past glory back to it. Digital had taken a break from wrestling, feeling burned out. He thought he couldn't continue going, didn't feel wrestling pumping through his veins. The wrestling world had taken a toll on him for so long that he needed out, at least for a short while. Then he received a call from none other than John Edwards himself, otherwise known as Moraless. He asked Digital what was going on in his life, and offered him something that would change the UWF forever, a union of the two forces, Digital Mortality and Moraless together taking on the world, he could not say no to this offer, after that call his old love, his passion was resurrected. That is how Resurrection came to be; Digital and Moraless were there to destroy the UWF completely so that it could be resurrected once again. It has already begun. They both took on the tag champs and destroyed them, the first time Digital won the Tag Team Titles, the flame kept getting bigger and bigger. They recruited two more men to help them in their cause, Bondage the Sex Fiend and Malcolm Kennedy. The Resurrection is complete, now they could resurrect the UWF as a whole. Take away the stagnation that Mike Montgomery and his Player's Club brought to the UWF and put Resurrection in their rightful places, right at the top. The battle with Hessian and Saij will be his hardest since he had to go one on one with Angelo DeVille during the GTT tournament. He won his bracket but had the disadvantage to have him as his opponent. He lost, and that is still in his mind. He must prove that he can take on the talent from the other federations, and what better way to do it than to win the Global Title. Digital Mortality is not very known around the PTC circles, but he intends to change that soon. With this match for the Global Title he will make his name a household name, like Hessian has done, like all the greats before him have done. Storm, Jamester, Almasy, every single one of them have something in common, everyone respects them, respects what they have done, and that is what Digital wants, that is what he craves. To be remembered, isn't that what we all crave? To leave a mark on those who knew us, and better yet, to touch the souls of those who watched us day in and day out. That is the dream of a wrestler. To walk down a street and have a kid come towards you and tell you how much they admire you, to have a book with your name on it, all those things are what Digital Mortality craves. This life though, it robs you of the simple pleasures of a normal life. Normal people can go to the movies without a crowd coming towards you for an autograph, or to go to a restaurant and have a nice dinner without interruptions, all those things are lost. Don't get it wrong, Digital chose this life by himself and for himself. He thought this life would have more ups and downs for him, and he was right, except for one minor little detail. Digital Mortality had this girl named Justine a while back. The girl that he himself says is the one he loved, and the only one he will ever love. What happened you may ask? Well, this life, the want of giving his fans everything made his relationship take a backseat, how he regrets that. He loved to be around her, to be close to her, to simply hear her voice made Digital keep going. He needed her with him, two years they had spent together and it was then thrown away, thrown into a trashcan because of one single mistake. He loved her, plain and simple. Months ago he used to park his car close to her house, and wait for her to leave, just to see her face. It inspired him to go on, to know that she was still there, and that some day they would be back together at last. Now that he was going to go into his most important match in his life, he needed her the most. We see Digital Mortality in his car, looking at himself in the mirror before him. Before he opens the door to exit his car, he makes sure to check his attire, fixing his clothes so they look proper.The wind is blowing slowly through his long red hair, his face full of determination, but his eyes full of fear. He was going to step into a place that he had feared to go into for the last seven months, but he was going to do it. He kept walking through the street of the middle class neighborhood. He was looking ahead all the time, to what was in store for him. Was she going to be happy? Was she going to slap him? Would she even open the door after seeing him? All these questions going through his mind, and none of them had answers. When he got to the house, he stepped up through the stairs and he was now standing at the front door. He went to ring the bell but pulled his finger away. He couldn't do it. Digital turned around and sighed heavily; he turned around once more. "I have to do this, I just need to..." He rang the bell. He waited. Will she feel good seeing me? I need her; I don't think I can beat Saij and Hessian without having her by my side. The door was opened and Justine's mother appeared. She looked at Digital's clothes and at his red hair and sighed heavily herself. She opened the door and told him to come in. "Gracias Señora Alvarez. Esperaba poder hablar con su hija." He looked left and right trying to see if he could see her, nothing. "Ella está? Yo puedo esperar o venir más tarde." Then he saw the tears on her eyes. Pouring down through her face, and he understood. He couldn't believe it, but somehow he knew. "Ella murió... mi hijita..." It hit him. His eyes swelling and red now. The worst thing that could have happened to him had happened. She was dead. Justine, the woman he couldn't live without, the girl that he wanted to spend the rest of his life with, taken away from him in an instant. It had been two months since he hadn't come to look at her, how could she have dies in that time? How could she leave him when he needed her most? Was it fair? No it wasn't, but it was life. "How did it happen?" "An accident... a drunk driver." He was shocked, tears going down his cheeks now. He held to that envelope like his life depended on it, because he did depend on it. He knew he would do a lot of crying these days; he couldn't remember the last time he cried. He had to be strong and he knew that. His gaze always ending in that letter; the letter that could destroy his life forever. How could he fight now? He is supposed to be ready for the fight of his life. He knows Saij won the Chris Williams Memorial Cup and Hessian beat Jesse Jamester and a couple other opponents for that Global Title. He should be in his gym training as hard as he could, thinking what his strategy is going to be when he steps in the ring with these two great wrestlers from different federations, people who he has never faced before. People who are just as thirsty for fame and victory. People who don't want to fail their organizations, and they don't want to fail themselves. He was on the sitting in the driver's seat of the car, driving on the way to the cemetery. Could he do this now? Should he do it? Yes, he had to do it. He had to do it for himself and for the memory of Justine, it didn't matter if the visit would get in the way of his training, no it didn't matter, because he needed this. He needed to see the tombstone with her name on it; he needed to know that she was with him, at his side. Digital was never much of a religious person, but he had to believe that she was with him, could she just be dust now? A memory? No, he could not bring it to himself to believe that. He arrived at the cemetery, it was a half an hour trip, but to him time did not exist that day, time was of no consequence. What mattered was that he was going to see the last resting place of the woman he loved. He stepped out of the car when he arrived, the ground moving a little bit, it was very surreal. He had never lost someone so close, it was a new experience, and a devastating one. He walked for about five minutes and finally saw the grave from far away, the same one he had accompanied her to see when her father had died. He could remember how much she cried that day, and he didn't want to look like such a fool. He finally arrived at the grave, the name giving him the last painful thrust, like a knife stuck in your stomach. It read..."To our dear and beloved daughter Justine Alvarez." The pain now unbearable, he looked away and could feel the water in his eyes. The sun shining from the sky right on Digi, it wasn't like in the movies, when someone important dies it is always raining, thunder and everything, but this day was beautiful, the blue sky and the bright sun reminding him that the movies were not real. The tears were falling down his cheeks and at that moment, he really wanted it to be raining, so his tears might be disguised. It was over... that which had been for so long. That with which he had been able to recline on. A shoulder to cry on, gone. He grabbed a rose that was on the grave and smelled it. Inhaled the scent of roses that she always wore. He had to do this; he had to win this for her, and for himself. He needed to prove to himself that he is worthy of this chance that he is able to step in with the big boys and battle it out. For all the fights Justine and he had about the toll wrestling was taking on their personal life, he knew deep down that she would have loved to see him victorious in that battle. "For you... All the cards were laid on the table and it was time to rock and roll. This was the last opportunity for someone like Digital to show his worth, and that was exactly what he was going to do. He was going to take all these pain he was going through and put it on Hessian and Saij, he was going to wrestle for his life. He was going to show the doubters in the UWF and the world that he was the real deal, that he did deserve this shot, that he was all that the hypes were saying about him. He was going to prove it. "That's the first good news I have had in a while..." he said dropping his head once more. He looked at the UWF Tag Title on the table right beside him and sighed. He grabbed it and looked at it, he concentrated on nothing but the belt. He remembered when he won the UWF World title and lost Justine for the first time. He had become cocky and stubborn, he had pushed he into the back of his mind when she was supposed to be the first one there. He had forgotten that she gave him the strength to move on when he couldn't do it anymore, when he took the long brake. That brake had done wonders for Digital, he had become tired of the backstage shenanigans of the wrestling world. He had timer to rebuild his burned down house, he had time for surgery on his scarred face, he was happy. He didn't have Justine though, after she cheated on him with his nemesis Joshua Dunn, he had decided that he didn't want to have anything to do with her anymore. He didn't want to see her, he didn't want to talk to her. He felt like he had a hole inside of him that would never be filled, but he needed to keep on living, in time it would close back up, he thought. Now that she was dead, any chance of filling the whole was gone, any chance of them getting back together was shattered, and he had to fight two of the greatest in the world. His head was full of things to think about, and he was not good at concentrating in things. He needed to, but it was too hard to think now. He got up and put the belt on the table. "You ok man?" Burton asked with a concerned look on his face. "I'll be ok, I need to rest," he told Burton before disappearing into his dark room. He needed sleep, he needed rest if he wanted to win this match. He needed to channel all these negative things into a positive if that is even possible. He was going to do what he could. He knew Justine would have been happy with him just giving it his all, trying his hardest to succeed, that is all she wanted. She couldn't care less if he lost, or if he didn't have a belt or money or even fame. It was not what she was looking for, she was looking for a nice guy who was not going to give up on his dreams, she wanted a guy with ambition, but enough self-control to understand when to stop. She wanted Digital Mortality, she wanted Antonio Vargas. He wanted Justine Alvarez. They would never be together anymore. Death had made that a fact. We all want love, fame, recognition, and money. He had it all; Digital Mortality was a happy man. He could say he had found true love. The Holy Grail. True Love.
