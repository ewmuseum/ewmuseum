IG29: 'Mean' Dean Jokerlin

7 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A single door spins in the middle of the huge vacuum of space. The familiar music that is associated with the long remembered show "The Twilight Zone" fills your ears. That familiar voice that has been recorded long ago comes once again. "You unlock this door with the key of imagination." The door then opens to show you a window. "Beyond it is another dimension. A dimension of sound." The window shatters and the falling glass makes that memorable crackle. "A dimension of sight." A single eyeball floats out the shattered remains of the window. "A dimension of mind." Einstein's famous equation floats by as the eyeball goes out of sight. "You're moving into a land of both shadow and substance." A poor manikin of a woman floats through now. "Of things and ideas." A clock then floats by where the woman once was. "You've just crossed over into....The Twilight Zone." A well groomed suit wearing man then takes over the scene sitting it what seems to be a backstage area of some type of studio or arena. "Tonight we have a story of a man. A man that is torn between two identities. What he is and what he aspires to be. Tonight he will explore his identity and he will find himself in....The Twilight Zone." Do-Do-Doo-Do Do-Do-Doo-Do. --- Meet 'Mean' Dean Jokerlin. He has been a long time wrestling connoisseur, he even has the build of a person who could break into the scene. Alas some early traumatic wrestling experiences had left him sidelined to be the back fiddle in this Texas Band. He is merely another disposable backstage reporter in another disposable extreme wrestling federation. He has suppressed his desires so far that he has actually convinced himself of happiness. We come in on another EXTREME Tuesday Night Fight, to another backstage interview in which 'Mean' Dean finds himself once more talking with the Shadow of Death, a past his prime Gothic weirdo who gets his jollies in knocking around lowly reporters (like most of these EXTREME fighters). 'Mean' Dean comes to the man microphone in hand. "Excuse me! Excuse me! Mr. Shadow...er Death. What are your plans tonight as you are in the title bout against you sworn enemy Captain Extreme?" The Shadowy fellow gives a cold blank stare to 'Mean' Dean possibly to look menacing, but truthfully because he can only speak as well as Kenyon Martin. Instead of even trying to answer Dean he gives him a hard elbow shot to the temporal lobe that takes Dean to the floor partly because Death has the vocabulary of a 3rd grader but mostly because that's EXTREME. Now, as you might have already guessed in a place like this Dean is susceptible to many of these shots a day in is line of work. After years of the abuse he slips into what seems to be...The Twilight Zone. Do-Do-Doo-Do Do-Do-Doo-Do. --- BEEP BEEP BEEP SLAM! An alarm clock is thrown against the wall. A man rolls out of bed. This man is at least 6 foot tall and looks to be in decent shape maybe a little bit of what some would call a 'spare tire' starting to form around him. His hair is balding into a great rendition of the grandpa-cut. The man is Dean Jokerlin. He slowly walks out of his room to find that it is not his room at all, well at least it hasn't been in the last 15 years. He walks down a hall with the great aroma bacon swirling around his nostrils. He groggily stumbles his way to the end of this hall to find himself in the kitchen of his parents house. A small old lady slaves over the kitchen stove to make breakfast as an older gentleman who looks to be in his mid to late fifties sits at the dining table in the middle of the kitchen reading a newspaper. The lady looks away from her skillet to greet Dean. "Well, hey there sleepy head you finally roll out of bed?" Dean rubs the side of his forehead to feel excruciating pain. He has no idea what is going on. "Mom, what am I doing here? Am I dead?" The man at the table drops the newspaper from his face a stares at Dean awkwardly. "Sit down, son. You must just be underfed." Dean slow and unsure takes a seat beside his father at the table. He has so many questions about why he is there and what is going on, but oddly enough the thing that keeps coming to his mind more often than nought is the question of why is everyone speaking in rhyme. "Why are we speaking in rhyme?" Dean's mother then comes to the table with two plates of food and sets one in front of both of the men. "Oh, quit with that nonsense you don't have the time." See there it is again! Dean ponders in his mind unsure of anything that is going on. He looks around the room bewildered for a moment. Before speaking to his parents. "Hey where is Larry?" Dean's Mom turns to him with wonder in her eyes. "Who is Larry? Dean's Dad then chimes in because he thinks he is funny. "Probably his boyfriend....god damn fairy!" Dean stares at his food still reeling, still trying to grasp what is going on. "No, No, Larry my brother!" Dean's Mom comes up behind him at this moment grasping his shoulders in the most loving way. "Oh, dear you know your our only and we want no other." Dean is lost now. What has happened to his brother? He jumps up from the table startling both of his parents. Dean's father is the first to regain his cool after his sons weird outburst. "Yeah, that's what I like to see some energy! Now, you best be on your way." Dean's Mom then grins wide as she grabs Dean's plate to eat what he leaves. "That is true, you do have a big day." Dean's confusion knows no bounds as he stares at his loving parents in bewilderment. "Ok, just where exactly am I suppose to be?" Dean's Dad smirks at him. "Now, quit fooling around. You know you are going to have to go answer to your Sensei, Bruce Lee." Okay, Dean is officially past the point of confusion and genuinely scared now, as he hears a car horn come from outside of the house. Dean's parents now begin to push him out the front door. He walks out to see that it was not a car horn at all, but rather an elderly Bruce Lee sitting in a rickshaw with a fog horn. How the rickshaw got there with Bruce in the back who knows? The real question you should be asking is why is Bruce Lee alive? "Hey! Dumbass! Get to pulling!" Okay, now a whole new question is brought to light. How does Bruce Lee not have a Chinese accent? Dean wonders this as well, but thinks it is best not to defy Bruce Lee. I mean it is....Bruce Lee. So, he follows instructions and takes the helm as the rickshaw labor man. Bruce sits in the back gnawing on an apple and then shouts to his sweaty com padre. "Now this is what I call rolling!" --- Enter The Dragon. The Dragon Dojo that is, where Bruce offers his knowledge upon those he deems worthy. Luckily for Dean somehow he has been deemed worthy. He sits on the floor in this bare room with only his master, the Bruce, pacing in front of him. Bruce stops and looks down upon Dean. "Welcome to Weiss..." Dean's mind can not comprehend that which is laid out before him. He has so many questions. "I don't understand. I don't understand. I don't understand this at all so I had to say it thrice." Bruce chuckles and nods. "I knew you wouldn't, I mean you couldn't, this place only exist through the rift." Dean's bewilderment continues as he stares at his Sensei in disbelief. "I wish I could only catch your drift...." You can hear the defeat in his voice and his mind can not comprehend what is being thrust upon him. Bruce comes closer and puts his hand on Dean's shoulder. "Dean I know you are lost......but you now know of these two separate universes that you may cross." Dean stares at his master in awe. It all starts coming together for him now, he knows that this place is not his home. He realizes now that he is in a parallel universe that is distantly connected to the one he knows. "Master, how are you the only one here who knows of this?" Bruce smirks. "I'm Bruce Lee. Now, I'm going to tell you one last thing you must not miss." Dean looks up with great wonder as his master breaks it down. "Dean, you must return to your world and you must return to the life of wrestling, but you must over come your fears and step back into that ring. You must go back through the rift and reign over all others, only this will allow you to break free of this life stuck between two worlds and propel yourself into the nirvana every man seeks." Dean nods in understanding, but only one thing runs through his mind.....that didn't rhyme. --- Enter Blackness. What is this? Where did Bruce go? Where am I going? Where shall I be at next? How do I know that is the only other "world"? Am I crazy? Enter Dean's Mind. How can I revamped my life to return to ring? How can I put the things in my past behind me? How can I tell this to Larry?.... My eyes open. Deep Breathing. Where Am I? Come on Dean get a control on reality. Calm down... Ok, I'm in bed, sit up. --- Dean has been awakened to find that he is in a hospital surrounded by 3 people. On his left and right stand the parents we saw in the land of rhymes, in Weiss. In front of Dean a younger man sits in a wheelchair. Once again that look of bewilderment creeps into Dean's face. "Larry..."
