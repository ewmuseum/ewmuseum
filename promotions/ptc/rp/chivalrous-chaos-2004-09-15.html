Chivalrous Chaos

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 It seems like so long ago, but for me, I relive the experience everyday. The crowd on their feet, the sweat dripping from my brow in anticipation, and the feeling of the cold air blowing all around. My heart racing at a mile a minute, my breathing getting heavy, having to sit down and take a break before I went into an asthmatic state. It was all worth it in my eyes, for that night I figured that no matter what I did, I was going to go down in history as a fighter, and a damn good one at that. What came next, it seemed to go in slow motion. Walking down to the ring with all my other opponents in there, awaiting me. And when I finally got in, I was blindsided by all of them, but I fought to the very end, only to come up short in it. Falling down the steel rungs, onto the canvas, only to see one of my opponents gather the biggest prize in the game for themselves. What runs through somebody’s head after something like that? The answer would be different for everyone, depending on who you asked. But for me, I took a loner’s way out, evacuating myself from the ring for days at a time. Thos days in solitude went by so slowly, but I was so embarrassed at the fact that I came so close, only to fall miles away at the end of it all. I thought I would get made fun of, so I stayed away from the business all together. You don’t know what it’s like to be alone, you think that everyone would have forgotten about you over time. But in my case, nobody would forget my name, and they would welcome me back with open arms in the next contest. Funny how you can step away from the business, and people will remember you more for your guest appearances than they would if you fought every flagship show, house show, and pay per view. Sometimes when I wake up, there’s such a vile taste in my mouth. Other times, it’s the sweetest thing since salt water taffy. The bitterness and sourness represent my failure to achieve things at the top of the game, and when it comes crunch time, I’m always reminded that sometimes not all things will go my way. The deliciousness represents my success, success which I’ve tasted a multitude of times during my career. But, with this being a major climb to the top, I’ve had a hard time grasping the texture. How bizarre it would be if this all were to turn around in my favor. A rebel without a cause, outcast in his first week of wrestling for complaining about the lack of competition, a loner who lived in solitude for months at a time until his next dream job came up, only to crash under him again. Imagine a human house wreck surviving all of his opponents, beating out all the odds, and finally overcoming the haunting of his previous failure? It would be the sweetest taste of them all. Who would stand in my way though? Surely, everyone else had the same taste in their mouths. I was always the underdog, no matter what the odds were. I’ve been placed against them all, and proved against them that without a shadow of a doubt, I could and did outwrestle them. It perplexed them, how could a smaller framed man beat such a powerhouse? The resolution was simple, but it wouldn’t be given away, for that was a secret of my trade. So now, I look upon this first round as a chance to redeem myself, a chance to finally prove that I’m over the hump that dwelled over me like a thunderstorm for so long, and a chance to finally prove that I’m ready to hoist the biggest prize of them all over my head. The competition would be fierce, but I believe that I was more so. This time, there wouldn’t be any fooling around. There would be no stories about how my parents grew up in poverty or how I was raised, the past was exactly that. This was about one thing and one thing only, the future. And I was taking it into my grasps, and climbing the mountain one step at a time. Soon enough, the climax would be reached, and all the power would be mine. What would I do with such a thing? The organization would just have to wait and find out now wouldn’t it... I needed this first victory to prove a point, so now I must pass a warning down to my first opponent, the great wrestler that is Venneman Pendragon of GW. You see “Thy Lord King”, I apologize for the fact that you thought you had a bye week, for now you must be slightly annoyed that you have to train and deal with me. But let me tell you, I have trained for this since I’ve been away from the ring. And let me tell you another thing my dear, dear man. This time, the training will pay off. Sure, you may yield a shiny sword of chivalry my way, but you won’t be able to use your shield to defend yourself when it comes time to do battle. You wear a silly mask to hide your precious face, but you won’t be able to hide the scars that are soon to come. I promise you the most hell-ridden fight you will ever experience, and I doubt highly that you will be able to slay an unstoppable dragon, the dragon that is hiding inside of my bosom... I will unleash such a force unto you that the black plague will look like the common cold. I will turn your medieval world upside down and slam you into the 21st century, and when you look up at the sky and realize that you aren’t in Camelot anymore, but rather being pinned for a three count, it will but all be over for your round tables and merry men. Can you sense my anticipation in the air? It will all come to be, faster than you can think. And when I move onto the next level of this fight of fights, maybe then you will realize that I am for real. Maybe then, will the industry realize that I’m going to finally take what’s mine. Maybe then, will the other opponents realize that I’ve come with a purpose, and I won’t be made a fool of any longer. It will be sweet revenge for yours truly, a truly gratifying notion if I do say so myself... A man that came with nothing... will leave... with everything
