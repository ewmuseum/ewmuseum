Dirtsifting

29 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dirtsifting “Hey what the fuck are you doing! Hey what the fuck are you doing! WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING!” More statement than anything. More “Is he really doing this?”, less inquisitive. The ‘why’ of the entire matter held no weight in his eyes, like a fucking feather, just a rhetorical question, hey what the fuck are you doing resonated with the fellow patrons, the Big Mac inhalers, the inhaler inhalers, Super Size me please they’d yell as they melted DVDs into yellow-red clowns. Hey what the fuck are you doing, no, shh, shhh, it’s cool, I’m famous, you’ve heard of me, Cary Turkington, remember, I think I was in GCW or ACW or WWE or WWF, I was a bald eagle but they stopped caring and now rednecks out in Montana take pot shots, I don’t even like Montana, who the hell likes Montana, so shh, I need that, I’m famous you’ve heard of me, Chicago is waiting and she hates to wait, most in the Midwest do, I’ve never been but tried a hot dog from there, Chicago style, the rambling man they called it, gourmet dogs, poodles and everything, hey what the fuck are you doing, no, shhh, shhh, it’s ok, I’m famous remember? Orders stopped. Food sat in hands, mouths gaped not in wait but awe, a weekly, weekly+ occurrence in this profession. Yes, on Maple St., it’s him again, the “wrestler”. Nah, not yet. Not this time, no. They’re yelling at one another, she wants her burger back, he’s on that Chicago shit again. Ok, thanks. Mr. Turkington, how was your trip? Food sat in hands, I didn’t even have a bite, I’m starving, it’s cool, I’m famous, Fox is hiring and this guy right here, this guy with two thumbs, can play a pretty convincing lawyer, see, look, we’re arguing now, right, so I’m going to lay out the defense, ladies and gentlemen of the jury, we hold these fruit pies to be self-evident, self-evidently mine, finders keepers am I right, I saaaaid amirite, like it was this way all along, it’s cool, I’m famous, most artists strive to starve, most in this line need a gallon of whey a day, not me, I don’t even use anymore, the difficulty of standing on three feet while banana slipping is like skateboarding, does that make sense, the food sat in hand and you squeeze it from your palm and some whore deep throats it, not you, not you, not you, I just wanted a pie. Yes, that’s him. No sudden movements—he’s more dangerous than he looks. I just wanted a pie, what does a man in this fucked up world have to do for pie, what does he have to do for his name, what must he write at the top just to get 200 points, just to prove he’s not an idiot, just to raise that line where we go left or right from because it’s normal, because the standard deviation drives creativity and not the ability to say fuck it to math and science, I just wanted pie, I just wanted a chance or a helping hand or three bucks, I don’t ask for much, those who do live with parents or rats, both dirty things, one tries to live forever, the other finds itself caught with the rest in a tangle or flesh, screeching and purring because there’s no quit in their heart, I just wanted a pie, a banana cherry pie with real bits of fruit, this tastes like cherry, there used to be people who’d help, animals that I’d spoon with, warmth for the nights in the alleyways, there used to be Firehose Frank and some other gangster dude, I’d travel with them and our exploits would be zany and crazy, the gangster dude one time killed a Mexican janitor and I think I won but if memory serves me correct that year is next in line, I just wanted a pie and here I am, wearing bracelets.
