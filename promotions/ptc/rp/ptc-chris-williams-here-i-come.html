PTC — Roleplay

Chris Williams, here I come.

27 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme A word that is being used to describe a title belt. A belt that the PTC would like to raise to new levels. The PTC: PrimeTime Central would like to take this title belt and show the world who the most extreme wrestler in it’s confines truly is. But what makes it so extreme to go for such a title? What is it that raises the bar on this title to something more then just another strap of gold? It’s not the Global Title Championship. What makes it so that 8 people would want to beat the living hell out of each other and try to make a name for themselves, heading into something like the Chris Williams Memorial Cup? What makes 7 of these men think they deserve to compete for the Chris Williams Memorial Cup? Let’s take a look at the definition of the word, extreme. 1a: existing in a very high degree b : going to great or exaggerated lengths : RADICAL c : exceeding the ordinary, usual, or expected 2: archaic: LAST 3: situated at the farthest possible point from a center <the country's extreme north> 4a: most advanced or thoroughgoing b: MAXIMUM 5a: of, relating to, or being an outdoor activity or a form of a sport (as skiing) that involves an unusually high degree of physical risk b: involved in an extreme sport Ah, the 4 definitions are nothing about what this is. But the 5th one. Something that involves an unusually high degree of physical risk. That’s what they’re all coming together for. This is about High-Risk-Hardcore-beat-the-hell-out-of-you-opponents-extreme-wrestling. Now you have the one’s that you will see in this match. West, Fathom, Marvelous, Roswell, Johnson, Ice and a self proclaimed “Lee-gend” in Mr. Smith. 7 men who have a lesson to learn. And their headmaster comes for school. What is there to fear from these men? Fathom is named for a nautical measurement equal to 6 feet. What is there to truly fear from that? Is he trying to use it for something it’s not? Oh, we must FATHOM his words? Believe you me, it’s hard enough listening to that bilge water he’s spewing out. A hard life? Please, spare us. Why is it that everyone in Wrestling has had a hard life? You mean there are no silver spoon in their mouth born brats? Oh wait, that’s Mike Marvelous. But truly, does you really think that the world cares that Fathom has had a hard life? That’s he’s have a hard time to get where he is? Or as Marvelous put it, that he’s Belmont’s bitch? Who cares? Maybe he’s Belmont’s bitch because Belmont owns him. Belmont’s better. Face it. Fathom, you aren’t extreme enough. And then you have Ice, a man who thinks he’ll scare the likes of me by attacking a cameraman. Some 150 pound college drop out with a goatee, and he scares the rest of the group into thinking that he’ll be the one to face Belmont at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. I don’t think so. Or how about Noah Johnson? Well, the use of those special effects to produce the dream sequence was so good. I’m quaking in my boots. Truly, I am. Roswell? Who cares? “The Lee-gend” Lee Smith? Lee-gend? A legend? What the hell is he a legend for? Oh wait, you know what legend means? Fairy Tale. Yeah, he’s a fairy tale. A damn fallacy, a fable, a short story, a children’s book. He’s a tool to learn a moral. And that moral? Well, we’ll get to that. And now, we can also bring up the other 2 in this match. Men that I know all to well. Men that I see parade around like peacocks on a weekly basis. Mike Marvelous. A man who walks around with a little bit of gold already. The equivalent of a Television Title. The 4WW’s South Wind Title. A title that is not about gimmicks, or being extreme. However, a man whom I can respect. Even though he hides with his boys in Featured Attraction, his new stable of men that he’s taken up with. And then you have Christopher West. A man who last week earned his rights to a full on schooling at my hands. A man who took a chair to the woman I live with, spend my life with. The woman who was getting a shot at going after the North Wind Title. Chris West took a chair to Patti Anderson, the possible future Mrs. McClancy, only due to the simple fact that her half brother is someone Mr. West has an issue with. Well now Mr. West has an issue with me. Who am I? Well since the rest of you like to spout off your accomplishments, how about I give you a little bit about me. I’m a former GIWF TV, Intercontinental, North American and Hardcore Champion. I’m a former 5 time SCWF Insanity Champion, and 3 time Superior Champion. I was a contender in the second Chris Williams Memorial Cup, and was at one time a contender for the TV title that bore his name. I’m the headmaster of the School of Hard Knocks. The man who crushed Ace Thunder’s career to lay claim to the true title of the One True Hardcore Icon. I’m from Dublin, Ireland. I’m 6’ 4” tall, and coming in at about 265 lbs. of red haired fury. I’m “The Irish Bastard” I’m Sean Michael McClancy. No, no funny names like Ice or Fathom. I’m not a legend in my own mind. I’m not “Insane”. I’m not “ Mr. Main Event” by name only. I’m not someone to come out here and talk about growing up in Ireland during combat times. I don’t need to. I’m the headmaster of the “School of Hardknocks” and class is about to be in session for the PTC. [And we find ourselves in an empty arena. The 4 Winds Arena, a little converted warehouse on the outskirts of Phoenix, Arizona. This is the place that Sean McClancy does his work. This is the place of which he performs for the crowd. The arena seats are empty. The ring is setup, as it always is. Standing in it, leaning against a set of turnbuckles, is the Hardcore Icon, “The Irish Bastard”, himself. He’s standing there in his black and green wrestling tights, and a muscle shirt with a flaming unicorn emblazoned upon it.] McCalncy: Good day, I see ye’ve come t’ find me. I’m sorry I’ll ‘ave t’ disappoint some o’ ye. Because yer na’ goin’ t’ see me doin’ any form o’ torture on some poor souls, ‘er spoutin’ off with a oriental teacher, ‘er anythin’ else like tha’. Na’, instead, wha’ ye see is wha’ ye get. [He leans in further into the camera, getting into a comfortable stance as he’s there. His scruffy red goatee, something that’s only been growing for a few days, shines with the lighting from the camera hitting it. His short hair looks mussed, and unkempt.] McClancy: I’ve been watchin’ these guys as they’ve cut their promos. As they’ve tried to get th’ rest of us t’ be scared o’ them. I could run ye all over. It don’na really matter wha’ ye said ‘er did. Yer still na’ goin’ t’ faze me. Yer na’ goin’ to stay in me way. Ye see, six o’ ye ga’ a little somethin’ t’ realize this comin’ week. [He leans back, moving away from the turnbuckle a bit.] McClancy: Six o’ ye ‘ave ga’ t’ learn tha’ it’s School o’ ‘ardknocks 101 time. Class will be in session. Some o’ ye will look a’ this as a chance t’ move up in th’ world. Bu’ ye’ve never seen anythin’ like me in th’ ring befer. I’m na’ comin’ ou’ there t’ show everyone tha’ I’m some mythical creature, ‘er some one who would be a false promise o’ the wrestin’ world. I’m na’ comin’ ou’ there t’ play with ye. I’m comin’ ou’ there t’ win. [He steps back some.] McClancy: Ye see, gentlemen, this comin’ event, th’ one after we al face each other, will be fer a cause I believe in. I am a member o’ the Order o’ the ‘Ammer. I’m a member o’ the KnightMare Brigade. I’m a member o’ the Unholly Alliance from th’ day of th’ PWW. Kain Threat’s old stable. I competed in th’ Chris Williams Memorial Cup II. Bu’ at tha’ time I was but a rookie. Now I’m a former 3 time World Level Champion. There is no more games t’ be played. [He heads for the middle of the ring.] McClancy: I’ve ga’ 7 opponents in this match. Th’ winner goes on t’ face the champion a’ the next Pay Per View Event. They all be wantin’ this fer personal glory, personal gain. I want it because t’ take it ‘ere would mean I’m once again in a Chris Williams Memorial Cup. An’ as me sister will be competing in the tourney, I’ll be lookin’ across th’ ring a’ one o’ the black spots on the sport. A man who thinks ‘imself a vampire or somthin’ like tha’. A man who ‘olds the title tha’ ‘as me name already on it. The Extreme Title fer all o’ PTC. I’ve now ‘eld World Level Titles, bu’ I’ve never ‘eld an interfederation anythin’. Th’ Extreme Title is in me destiny. It’s a goal I can see an’ feel. [He stops, as the camera comes forward, getting up closer to his face, very close.] McClancy: Bu’ fer one o’ ye. One o’ ye. It’ll be a time o’ reckonin’. It’ll be a time tha’ yer abou’ t’ regret. Chris West, ye ga’ a graduate cource comin’ fer ye. Ye don’na get th’ beginner class. Ye ge’ the special crash course in ‘Ardknocks. Ye stuck yer nose int’ somethin’ tha’ did’na concern ye a’ all last week. Ye ‘it me Patti with a chair. An’ now, now ye’r goin’ t’ see jus’ wha’ it is tha’ I’m all abou’. I can stand ‘ere an’ tell ye ‘ow I’m goin’ t’ break ye. I can stand ‘ere an’ tell ye tha’ ye suck, ‘er tha’ ye are ‘omosexual. Ye could return all yer Irish Jokes ye can. Bu’ in th’ end, it’s all based one th’ simple fact tha’ I’m determined, I’ve ga’ th’ skill, an’ I be wantin’ t’ pay ‘omage t’ Chris Williams again. An’ getting’ me ‘ands aroun’ th’ neck o’ Chris West is just a simple added bonus t’ the whole scheme o’ things. [He starts to walk towards a different corner. He’s moving pretty good.] School is in session gentlemen. Jus’ remember tha’. I’m goin’ t’ teach ye all a good lesson in somethin’ called ‘umility. An’ when I’m all said and done, Belmont won’na ‘ave t’ worry abou’ someone who’s worried abou’ facin’ ‘im. Bu’ instead ‘e’ll face one man who will look a’ ‘im in th’ eyes an’ grab tha’ title righ’ off ‘is shoulder. I’m ‘ere. I’m movin’ through. An’ I’m the one who will raise the Extreme Title Division t’ it’s highest levels. [He pauses. Turning back to the camera.] McClancy: If ye don’na be likin’ tha’. Well, I ga’ a few words fer ye. Pog Me Hoin! Get used t’ it. I’ll see ye boys in th’ ring. Especially YE Chris West. [And he climbs through the top and middle rope, climbing out of the ring. Fade to Green.]
