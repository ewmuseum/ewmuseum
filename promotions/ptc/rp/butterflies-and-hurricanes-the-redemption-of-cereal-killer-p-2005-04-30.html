Butterflies and Hurricanes: The Redemption of Cereal Killer - Part Three:

30 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-30 Our past makes us what we are. We come to be ourselves through the actions we have taken and the scenarios it places us in. Our reactions to mixes of stimuli are as different as our fingerprints; as different as our DNA. How often is it heard in the news that people from past histories of abuse turn out to become abusers themselves? It’s by no means a rule, but something inside those people was altered when they were young, in the years that formed their outlook on life. Similarly, Frank was formed in his younger years. A combination of sports, loneliness, and an undiagnosed chemical imbalance in his brain left him as perfect fodder for the professional wrestling industry. Tagging first with a strange, dark man whose motives were never clear, he had success beyond that which could ever have expected. He had a chance for greatness, but after striking out, away from the Army of Darkness, his loneliness and tendency towards psychosis got the better of him, and the wrestling trade ate him up and spat him out. So imagine his emotions years later when he turns around and sees the man who caused his fall standing there in his own apartment. “You’re going to lose her, you know.” Those were the words spoken by The Ripper, a man whose domination of XUW was the stuff of legends. He was someone not content to merely physically dominate his opponents, but he also needed to overpower them mentally. Many of the top stars in the business had fallen to him and emerged a shell of their former self. Cereal Killer was one of those men. How do you speak to your past? How do you talk to someone that ruined with your life without ever seeming to take an interest in it? “How are… but you… when did…” “You do not seem pleased to see The Ripper.” CK stared at him, open-mouthed. Nothing was said for several moments, as the XUW entrant in PTC’s second annual cruiserweight tournament stood contemplating this most unusual turn of events. “Why… why are you here?” “You don’t know, do you.” There was something about the way The Ripper spoke. It was as if he constantly asked questions, but always phrased them as if they were facts, somehow incontrovertible and impossible to argue. It was like he knew the answers before they were spoken. “This doesn’t make sense!” yelled CK. “Since when did your life make sense,” was the reply. Another non-question. The Ripper walked over to the Ikea table, his gaze fixing on a bowl of apples. A smile crossed his face before he turned back to face his old partner. “You are wondering how this is possible.” CK nodded. “You’re thinking this couldn’t possibly be happening.” Another nod. “Just because you saw The Ripper die…” ----------------------------------------------------------------- It was Sunday March 9th, 2003, just two months after Cereal Killer had appeared for the final time, that Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy wrestled his final match in XUW. It was for the World Title, and was fittingly at the biggest PPV of the year; Reborn. The Ripper had lost matches in his time, but in the end he always got his man. In the end he broke every man he faced, and that match looked to be no different. Yet, it was a triple threat match, and the combined power of Henrik the King and Samurai Bob managed to overcome the single colossus, and Henrik picked up the three count in one of the most sensational matches seen in the history of the federation. It was not to end there. In a furious rage at having failed to regain the title he saw as his, The Ripper went after not the winner of the belt, but his long-time nemesis, Bob, and his wife. In desperation, the happy couple were forced to climb the scaffolding that surrounded the Ultitron, the large screen that relayed the matches to the fans. With an immense display of strength, The Ripper began to dismantle and break the heavy duty metal with his bare hands. Bob and his wife escaped via a judiciously timed jump to the relative safety of a stack of tables and assorted debris, and The Ripper tore down the entire set, causing the scaffold and the great screen to fall down on him in a rain of metal, glass and electricity. They said that there was no man who could have lived through it. ----------------------------------------------------------------- “I did… I did see you die,” protested Cereal Killer. “You saw what the darkness wanted you to see.” “What?” The Ripper smiled at Cereal Killer, the smile of a man who knows more than you; the smile of a man in complete control of his future, and yours too. “The federation saw what it needed to.” “So you didn’t die? There were rumours that you broke out of Hell to return and fight at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup!” “There are rumours that Elvis lives on the Moon. Do you believe those also?” Cereal Killer shook his head, and rubbed his chin for good measure. His stubble was starting to show through, but he liked the unkempt look. What he didn’t like was this intrusion into his life. “So you’re telling me you faked your own death to piss people in XUW off?” “The Ripper has told you nothing.” “Why the hell are you here? Have you come to gloat that I’m going to lose to this guy from PRIME? After all, they’ve been kicking the crap out of everything that moves in PTC recently. So what if he’s just a manager; I’m just some semi-retired guy who hears voices and sees dead people in his damn living room!” “You really do need to calm down if you want to continue winning in this tournament… and The Ripper does want you to continue winning.” The precise, clipped tone of The Ripper was not doing what it usually did to Cereal Killer. On a normal day he would be intimidated; he would be scared of the enormous man who stood in front of him. Instead, he was merely intrigued and interested by his words. What trickery was this? Was this The Ripper trying to be… perish the thought… conversational? “You want something.” The Ripper just stared at Cereal Killer, and it was as if he was nothing, as if he was just a speck of dust in the Universe, important to nobody or nothing. CK tried to hold the stare, but it was like looking into infinity, and he had to turn away. “As a matter of fact, The Ripper is interested in you.” “Why though? Why now?” pleaded CK. “We were tag team partners for a year, but you didn’t show any interest in me the entire time! You picked me from nowhere, from wrestling dark matches and we became something. We were a team, we were going places, but you never cared about me!” “If The Ripper recalls correctly, you were the one to start drinking heavily, causing the Army of Darkness to lose the titles.” The words were spoken like knives slowly slipped between the ribs of Cereal Killer. “Yeah, but… but… but…” “Your excuses flow like wine.” CK stopped and looked down at his feet. Surely The Ripper would have said ‘like the blood of the victims of darkness’, or something equally graphic. “Are you really who you claim to be?” “You show how little you know The Ripper. You are like a child, seeing for the very first time and trying to put names to the objects. You were in a team with The Ripper for almost a year, and yet you know nothing. Was it truly that The Ripper never paid you any attention, or was it that you were too wrapped up in your own neuroses and never saw what was really unfolding around you.” Cereal Killer was torn again. The Ripper had once more spoken a question as a statement, but this time it was something he was unsure of. CK was sure that he had been ignored, a footnote in the history of the stable that overtook the entire federation, but could he really be sure? It was, perhaps, worth a gamble. “Why are you here now?” The Ripper paused, as if considering which words might make the biggest impact. “Do you know what it is like to build something with your own bare hands, and then see it torn down brick by brick?” “Well…” “Do you know what it is like to set plans in progress that might take years to fulfil, and then see them potentially dashed by the stupid acts of someone completely unaware of what they are doing?” “I don’t…” “No, you don’t, and that has become the problem.” Cereal Killer’s head was spinning with possibilities. “The Ripper has patience. The Ripper plans things over a longer period than you would consider possible. Yet your actions, and some of your delusions, have been causing The Ripper some… difficulties recently.” Despite the spinning head, CK felt that was a momentary victory. Never before had he heard his old partner ever admit weakness, even for a second. “You need me.” The Ripper nodded his head. “Finally your eyes can see a little of the picture, yet they will forever be occluded from the true goals, the true aims of The Ripper. That is…” He left the words hanging in the air as he stepped towards the doorway. It was an invitation that CK could not turn down. There was too much at stake, and his feeling of being a pawn in a larger game that he knew nothing about might finally be assuaged. He had a chance to grab the arm of fate and twist it himself, for perhaps the first time. “That is?” he asked. “That is unless you wish to truly become at one with the darkness.” Frank almost laughed. The Ripper was always speaking like that, as if every decision was motivated by some stupid force that he called the ‘darkness’. He lived in the ‘Domicile of Darkness’ and had once been the leader of the ‘Army of Darkness’. Yet looking into his eyes, seeing the dull gunmetal grey of the irises, it always ceased to become a joking matter. It was just the way he was; anachronistic; inimitable; inhuman. “Is that an offer?” “They are only words. It is actions that truly speak for intentions. It is actions that will seal your fate, in whichever way you choose to take it.” The Ripper’s hulking frame still stood with its back to Cereal Killer. “One question,” said CK. The Ripper didn’t move, as if inviting the question. “Why now?” asked Frank, for seemingly the millionth time in his life. A look over his shoulder was the closest that CK came to full recognition of his presence. “Plans are afoot. Were you listening properly it would tell you all that you needed to know. This tournament has effects beyond those of a personal nature to you. If you can see of one more opponent then maybe you will be worth redeeming. Until then you are but one variable in a complex equation. Your choices have surprised The Ripper at times, and he truthfully never expected to see you back here, but here you are. Your decisions, and those of… your other half, they impact upon plans laid in your absence.” “But…” “Your one question is gone. It is not yours to reason as to the ends of these plans, but let it be known that they exist, and if you succeed in progressing, then you will know more, and you will see more. More than perhaps you ever wanted to.” Cereal Killer dropped back down into his sofa as he watched The Ripper leave. What plans were these? Did he need to know them? Hell, did he even want to know them? There were too many questions, and too many variables in his life. ‘A blue couch’, he thought. ‘I have a blue couch’. The simple solidity of the statement reassured him. Thoughts beyond that seemed pointless. He was a pawn in a game created beyond his knowledge. There was nothing he could do but move on. Move on and defeat his next opponent, be him from PRIME or from the streets. He had to know what was next. He had to be more than just a pawn.
