The Lion in the Jungle

9 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
9/9/2005 [The scene opens in the world famous James von Doom house. James is wearing a black "Shaun of the Dead" movie T-shirt and jeans with socks on but no shoes. James is sitting on the couch, reading the newspaper when he comes across the weirdest new story ever. It's like something he'd come up with but it's real. He checks the newspaper to see if it's Weekly World News or something but it's not. This is for real. James almost bursts out with laughter as he reads the story aloud.] JV: (reading) "Prominent ecologists are floating an audacious plan that sounds like a "Jumanji" sequel: Transplant African wildlife to the Great Plains of North America." * [James starts laughing.] JV: I can't believe this. Are these guys fucking stupid or something? [James shakes his head as he reads on.] JV: (reading) "They believe the relocated animals could restore biodiversity on this continent to a condition closer to what nature was like before humans overran the landscape." * [James is laughing hysterically now. Just then, his two best friends Adam Wilson and Matthew Litten come barging in. The pop in is something they readily like to do. Adam is a skinny doofus looking Goth type that has five things on his arms, glasses, and messed up looking hair. He's wearing all black too. Matt is a tall, lanky doofus with a goofy grin on his face and has a mind that's almost as insane as James' is.] Matt: Hey James. Adam: What's so funny? Matt: Yeah. What the hell are you doing now? [They close the door behind them.] JV: Reading what these psycho ecologists want to do. [Matt raises an eyebrow.] Matt: Psycho ecologists? [James nods his head.] Adam: What do they want to do? [James can barely contain his laughter as he tries to spit it out just what he's talking about.] JV: (holding out the newspaper) Read this... [Matt grabs the paper and holds it so he and Adam can read it.] Matt: (reading) "Ecologists see elephants, cheetahs roaming Plains." * [Matt starts laughing.] JV: Dude, this is like the greatest news story EVER~! Matt: What about the one with the thirty midgets fighting the tiger? [James has a big devious grin on his face.] JV: No. This is WAY better. And besides, I heard that one on the Howard Stern Show. Adam: Here James... [Adam hands James the paper and James starts skimming through the article.] JV: Wait. You guys haven't heard the best part. This is an ACTUAL news story. It's not like Weekly World News where the Bat Woman has gone on a bloodsucking rampage in New Mexico or the Slug of DOOM~! is slithering its way through New York City. This shit is for real. But this part... [James continues searching for what he was trying to find.] JV: ...is the best. Adam: What is it? Matt: Are they going to create a mutant race of pig men to take over the world? [James and Adam shoot him a look. James looks like he can't believe Matt said that out loud.] JV: No. That's my job. [Matt starts laughing as James finds it.] JV: Here it goes. [James starts reading from the article again.] JV: (reading) "But the plan is triggering thunderclaps of criticism, with discouraging words as 'stupid' and 'defeatist' raining down." * [Matt and Adam start laughing.] Matt: Stupid...that's great. Adam: Where the hell do you find this stuff? JV: I actually found this by accident. I was reading through the newspaper like I always do and then BAM~! I find this golden nugget of comedic hilarity right there on the bottom of the front page. [Adam looks at him funny.] Adam: That made the front page? [James shrugs his shoulders.] JV: Well, yeah. Adam: How in the hell did that shit make the front page? JV: It did. Adam: But why? [James looks a bit irritated.] JV: Why the hell are you asking me for? Do I look like I run the fucking newspaper or something? Huh? Am I the editor? Am I the head newswriter? Huh? Am I? AM I? [Adam and Matt are laughing hysterically now as James stays seated and looks normal, like nothing happened.] JV: But I did find this nugget of words of wisdom at the end of this little article. Adam: (still giggling) What is it? Matt: (sarcastically) Yes. What wisdom do you have to impart on us O' Great Malachi, King of the Saturday Night Bowling? [James and Adam turn and give him looks.] JV: What the hell are you talking about? Adam: Huh? [Matt shrugs his shoulders.] Matt: I'm just saying... [James can only shake his head and read the 'words of wisdom' he has found in the news article.] JV: Are you guys ready for this? [Matt and Adam nod their heads.] Adam: Yes. [James grins deviously and reads from the article.] JV: (reading) "Lions eat people," said co-author Josh Donlan of Cornell." * [Adam, Matt, and James start laughing hysterically over this. James then gets up and turns intently toward the camera.] JV: Just like a lion, I will devour my opponents whole. [The intensity in his face is remarkable. He looks pissed, angry, and furious all at the same time! Adam and Matt are too busy laughing hysterically in the background to notice.] JV: You see, this is my fourth shot here in the Infinite Gauntlet and I may not get another one. I am out to DESTROY Michael Stevens and Jason Jameson. Because, you see, von Doom is the lion in these PTC jungles. I am the super predator. You two are nothing but my prey. I will have your blood on my hands by the time the Gauntlet is over. [Adam and Matt come over.] Adam: Hey James? [James eyeballs the camera with much anger and fury. You can almost see the flames in his eyes.] JV: Yeah? What is it? Adam: Who are you talking to? [James then acts like nothing just happened.] JV: No one. Just mumbling to myself over what to watch tonight. Matt: I heard Lost is on. JV: But those are reruns. [Adam and Matt put their thinking caps on, which is a very scary thing if you know them.] Adam: What about Smackdown? [James shakes his head.] JV: Fuck that shit. All they're gonna have on there is Orlando Jordan losing in thirteen seconds to Benoit, a bad Randy Orton match, the Mexicools beating up jobbers, and Christian being jobbed even though he pwns their entire fucking roster. So, NO, I am not watching Smackdown. [Adam takes a step back.] Adam: Whoa...calm down there James. It's just a show. [James turns and shoots him an angry look.] JV: Just a show? Is that what you said? [Adam gulps.] Adam: (hesitantly) Yes... JV: Well, am I just a wrestler? Aren't I in that industry as well? [Adam doesn't know what to say so he starts clamoring up.] Adam: Well, I, um... [James throws his hands up in disgust.] JV: I am a wrestler and you'd best remember that. But, that's right. I'm James von Doom. I'm not Main Event material. Yeah. [James starts going in and out of insanity and tranquility like it is nothing to him.] JV: I was told that I would NEVER be a Main Event caliber athlete by the bingo hall losers on the Yahoo Circuit. I was told I would always be a jobber in the NWC. I was told that I'm not what they're looking for. They're looking for somebody to carry the torch. Oh yeah... They chose the Icon over me in GHW. THE ICON. Who is he? He's NOTHING. He thought he was something special. The moment he got moved to the NWC, all he did was what he always does. Try and get some power and some backstage stroke because that's all he ever wanted to begin with. Adam: James... [James ignores him and continues on.] JV: Back in the NWCP, I was jobbed to Lou Gagliardi because I fucked up. they kept me off three straight shows and you know what I did? I showed them that I was the superstar I knew I could be. I went out there and did thirty segments for three shows. And do you know what they got me? [Matt seems really interested in this conversation. Adam seems a bit scared.] Matt: What? JV: It got me a first class ticket back on the show only to be jobbed for six months. But then, I started helping out backstage and I was going to write a match for the Icon. You know what he tells me? "Don't do that silly bullshit like you always do. Keep it simple." [James looks flabbergasted.] JV: Keep it simple? HELL, I already apologized to his ass and he tells me NOT to do any bullshit? Fuck him. I'm James von Doom, who's he? He's a nobody! Then I got Brian McCoy on my e-mail after I write a match for him to help out because they needed it just like SCW needed it and I had somebody no sell a move in the match. He says "Don't do that crap again." Hell, they can do that in AXIOM wrestling but not the DWS and they're in the same fucking organization. But do you know why it all really happened? [Matt shakes his head.] Matt: Why? Adam: James... [James ignores Adam again.] JV: Because they were all afraid of me. [An uneasy silence fills the room.] JV: They all knew that if I put my mind to it, I'd be running the joint. But you know what happened? It closed down and I came here to the PTC. I was in the UWF and beat 13 people in my very first match! I then went on to win all but two titles they've got there in less than seven months. So yeah. Who's laughing now? Who's the poster boy of the post-NWC? It's not Wyatt Connors, Foster Nackedy, Ryan Stephens, Devin Fox, Riktor Arkane, BloodAngel, Lazarus, William McDormant, or even Paul Cain. It's James von Doom. [James has an intense look on his face but also has a proud smile at the same time.] Adam: Will you just... [James ignores him again.] JV: I'm the one who came out of the ashes of the NWC and started kicking everybody's asses. I've lost one singles match since I've come to the PTC. All of my other losses are in tag or cluster matches and the tag ones were from no shows from my partners. I think that speaks for itself. I've won 30 championships in three years. Three years ago today, I started wrestling. Now, it's my time to shine. First, I'm winning this pathetic little Gauntlet thing I signed up for and then, I'm winning the UWF Regal Rumble. Now who's going to stop me? [James cackles evilly, almost looking like Kane when the camera gets a little bit closer.] JV: (coldly) No one can stop me. [Adam tries to get a word in again.] Adam: James... [James turns in his direction.] JV: What is it? Adam: We came to get you so we could all go to In & Out Burger and then go see Transporter 2 again. [James smiles.] JV: That movie kicked ass. Adam: Oh hell yeah... Matt: That should be your new gimmick. [James shakes his head.] JV: Nah. I've already got like 50 gimmicks, so that's plenty. [After a short pause, Adam breaks the silence.] Adam: So what are we going to do? [James smiles and grabs his car keys off the coffee table.] JV: What're we waiting for? Let's roll before people realize that this was a work. Adam: (giving him the thumbs up) Yeah... [James grabs his shoes from his room and puts them on. The three of them then head out the door, closing it behind them as the scene fades to black.] Cut. End Scene. * = taken from the actual newspaper article The article (best link I could find): Ecologists Plan to Move African Wildlife to the Great Plains
