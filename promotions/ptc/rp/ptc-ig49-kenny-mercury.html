Mercury. Infinity. A taste of which was thought to be reserved for the divine. And as such… I always thought that my legacy applied. There was a point in time and space where it seemed as though the legacy… the influence… the power… the name of KENNY MERCURY would indeed live on forever. But that legacy has proven to be empty… a mere revolution or three around Sol and now where is Kenny Mercury? Where are the whispers of his name? The anxious… quivering combination of both anticipation and fear that oozed from the very mention of such a wrestling phenomena… The name once caused more fear than the devil himself, much to the horned one’s chagrin… Heh… the subject of this undying envy. Where is it? The absence of these once omnipresent sentiments has lead me to realization that Kenny Mercury is… Finite. This realization has haunted me for the past few years… it’s taken everything I ever was… my body, mind, and soul and tore it down to the swampy depths of MORTALITY… where the likes of the uncleansed, mindless, and ultimately expendable masses dwell… So after all the victories… all the battles fought and won… all the enemies defeated… Kenny Mercury is… FINITE… a mere mortal just like all the other insects with their futile dreams and piss-ant errands… To think that at the climax of it all… when the force that is KENNY MERCURY was strongest… that puissant torrent reigning down upon the world of wrestling… legends turned to dust… icons were forgotten… and the visions of grandeur by those fledging wrestlers DIED… all because of Kenny Mercury… I had done it all, I had defeated all, and had proven myself to be the very pinnacle of wrestling… personified. I had one last task to solidify my legacy… to achieve INFINITE glory and adoration… To truly be considered A GOD. The World Heavyweight Championship… The one obstacle was the devil himself… a demon from the darkest reaches of the almighty sea… One that was rumored to be as equally as potent as Mercury… whose story and aura had captured the mythic imaginations of the entire populace of this dreck planet… He had achieved more than me, as he was the holder of the World Championship… HE WAS MY FINAL DEMON!! Tsunami Oni… heh… and that demon I did slay… Mercury met Tsunami Oni and in the most monumental battle that made Revelations look like a chipmunk’s queef… I VANQUISHED MY DEMON! I DESTROYED TSUNAMI ONI! I WON THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! AND I TOOK TSUNAMI ONI IN THE PALM OF MY HAND AND I SMITED HIM, FOR HE WAS A DEITY LESSER THAN I!!! … Tsunami Oni... would never wrestle again… I made sure to… end him. Yet I find that he is still revered… still worshipped… STILL LOVED MORE THAN I, WHO SLEW HIM AND ALL THE REST… well, that pains me… as I watch my name crumble into the sea… Will I ever know infinity?
