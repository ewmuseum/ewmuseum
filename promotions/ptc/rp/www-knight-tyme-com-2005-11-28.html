Date: 2005-11-28 29 Nov 05 Greetings all, My name is [name withheld] and I am the webmaster of the very site that you are reading right now, www.knight-tyme.com, the official home page of former DWA World Champion and current GCW World Champion, LeStatt Knight. This is the first blog entry during "launch week". There will still be different aspects of the site that cannot be accessed just yet but for the most part everything is up and running. First thing on the agenda is to tell you a little bit about the company, Knight-Tyme Incorporated. Formerly under the name of "De La Vega Enterprises" the name was changed when Mr. Knight took the company over after his Uncle, Juan De La Vega’s untimely death two years ago. Under LeStatt’s control the company has flourished by getting it’s hands into the entertainment industry, sports, medicine, etc. We have headquarters in Spain, Mexico, Florida, and now California. Now the important part, a run down of the background of LeStatt Knight. He was born Roberto Juan A. in Mexico City, Mexico on November 10th 1979 along with a twin brother named Damen. Their mother did not make it out of the delivery room, dying shortly after giving birth to Roberto. The twin boys were separated when their father claimed unable to take care of them. Roberto was sent to live with his Uncle Juan in Spain. When Roberto was eight teen years old he was told of a twin brother and so we decided to come to the United States for the first time to find him. It was here that wrestling became a big part of his life. Damen Abitua, the twin brother that he sought after was already in training and the two went under the tutelage of a David "RaZiel" Sandy. Their skills were undeniable but their attitudes left much to be desired, it was that attitude and bad choices that led to Damen’s murder barely a year after the brothers were reunited. Roberto disappeared a few days later remaining out of sight and out of mind for a year. David Sandy, his old trainer found him by chance. David convinced Roberto that he needed to harness that aggression into something more efficient or else it would eat him up inside. Wrestling. David had Roberto training like never before and it was quickly paying off. Roberto really did not spend much time on the Indy circuit before meeting a man named John Reed. Mr. Reed had offered Roberto a contract to a small fed named HWF that he was starting up. Sadly the fed never jump started but Reed saw the potential and told Roberto and David that he would talk to his boss at another fed about getting him in. A few days later Roberto got a phone call from a Steven Caldera about joining Global Championship Wrestling. Under the name of Khris Krowe, Roberto did his best but things did not turn out as he had hoped. His face was hidden behind a mask as Krowe and it just did not suit him. At Dangerous Games 2001 Roberto lost the mask and came out with a fresh start. LeStatt Knight was born. Success did not come to Roberto quickly still, even with so many claiming that he had unlimited potential. GCW closed sometime later and Roberto still did not feel fulfilled. He bounced around to a few places, even building quite a reputation for himself along the way, winning one World Title although his Uncle’s death and his consequent taking over of the company ended that after only one night. It wasn’t until February 2005 that Roberto found what he was looking for. Global Championship Wrestling Since the reopening of GCW LeStatt Knight has been almost unbeatable losing only two matches in the entire tenure. His United States Title reign has only been matched by one other title reign in GCW history and that is his own World Title reign that is still going strong. Sunday, November 27th at NC-17 LeStatt Knight will once again climb into the ring and defend his World Title against Michael "SpitFire" Stevens, PTC Infinite Gauntlet Champion. It was signal the end of a feud that has raged for the better of five months. In PTC, LeStatt has joined in on the Wrestling world’s largest stage, GTT5. He is set to face Clyde Walkin of PRIME fame in what is speculated to be quite the match up. Thus far the fans and other PTC superstars seem to be torn on who should or will win this match. When asked about GTT5 earlier this week this is what LeStatt had to say. "Clyde Walkin and PRIME in general mean very little to me in the grand scheme of things. I am out to prove that not only is LeStatt Knight the greatest Champion walking around in the wrestling world but that Global Championship Wrestling is the single most dominant force in this industry. Clyde Walkin is merely a small stepping stone in my quest for immortality." It is not known how much LeStatt knows about his opponent for the first round of GTT5 but few can match his work ethic or determination in the GCW. That being said if he puts in that kind of work into GTT5 then his critics would be wise to rethink what they say about him because I am here to let the world know. LeStatt Knight is here to stay. Sincerely, [name withheld] Knight-Tyme Inc. VP www.knight-tyme.com webmaster
