Torment

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Prelude The Last time we saw Dani Furher, he had just been released out of prison until more evidence was found that he killed trainer Rhett Mcnamara…but only more trouble lied ahead as Furher happened to stumble across a dead body in the middle of the road. He picked up the body and reported it to the police…only to be accused to be the person whom killed the dead body. Later that night while he was in the prison cell, Furher received a visitor from who he did not know. Once in the visiting room, he sat down and could not recognize the man…that is until the man exposed himself to be the true killer of the body found by Furher. Furher was destined to turn him in, that is until the man threatened to kill Furher’s family if he were to turn him in. Furher threw a fit and charged the man only to have guards come in and hassle him down. Now he has been placed in his cell for a week minimum and has been threatened with a sentencing of death. As one who was always known to be Passive…Furher began to doubt himself, and gradually…his faith began to decrease. [ " Admission into the Madness " ] The sounds of the large prison doors slamming are sounds that never left my mind. That horrible Hollow echo sound bouncing off of one steel wall and rebounding off another, traveling for more than a quarter of a mile to wake up each and every prisoner who is confined within this prison's large steel unforgiving cage. Just a 27 year old man was I, locked up and accused of something that held false legitimacy. Locked up, not only physically, but mentally inside of a caged mind that had seemingly broken down into a sorrow corporation, all for the fear of one man whom had threatened to bring harm to my family...something that I would rather die than have occur. My cell door opened up as I stepped out and joined the other prisoners in a line led by four security guards. We all walked, as silent as a steady sheet of paper, all walking along the concrete and steel walkway in a perfectly formed line. We continued walking, three stories above the concrete earth below on the third floor. It was time for us to partake of our breakfast...if you could truly call it that. Upon entering the large steel dining area, still half asleep, the echo of clanging, banging, hammering steel never ceases or stops. The noise of the steel walls, the steel doors, the steel pots, the steel pans, the steel trays, the steel silver ware, the steel benches and the steel tables are all a constant reminder of the very terrible, unforgivable deed that he committed against the society in which he once was allowed to live. I shuffled along ever so slowly dragging along my sacred, dented steel tray along the walkways as my fellow inmates proceeded to toss, slam, and splatter there disgusting meals at me. I didn't really know what this was a sign of. Maybe it was a way to bring in the new guy...or maybe it was just a pathetic way to have fun in a place where fun was limited to a minimum as short as a rice grain. I took my hand and wiped away a cold solid splatter of grits that had been pitched at me and landed on my arms and hands. Finally I had made my way through what seemed like a tunnel of despair. I reached the lunch line where I placed my tray on the hard cold counter and slowly advanced down the line as the Prison Lunch Workers splattered cold lumps of grits unto my tray. As I continued down, I was granted a bottle of water which looked like it had been consumed from a swamp, as well as a single pancake as cold as the grits and finally a slice of toast...that was as black and burnt as any black marker that crayola could possibly create. Once Progressing from the lunch line, I walked towards an old painted peeled steel table that has probably been painted grey for the fifty-third time, where finally I sat down placing my face into my hands, just for a moment, in order to give thanks to God for the little bit that I do have in his life, whatever that would be. No tears came from my soul, as tears were a sure sign of weakness in a hell like the one I was presented in.I reached over and grabbed one thing that I was for certain was made in the " Free World "...a package of Welch Grape Jelly. I held the package up to the light...staring at its purple gaze wondering if maybe, just maybe, some free beautiful woman in a small factory somewhere along this earth might have touched that same package with her warm, kind, gentle hand…sending it off to him in this cruel malice home. I placed the package down and picked up my fork, digging into the hardened egg that lied there before me as I picked the egg off of the cold hard steel tray and placed them within my mouth. I took about two bites off the egg before opening my mouth wide, allowing the disgusting slime to fall from my mouth and back unto the cold hard steel tray in which it originally sat. I pushed the tray away as the Prisoners quickly jumped on me with the same question...asking, almost pleading for my untouched food. I willingly allowed them to pick and choose off of anything they wanted as I placed my head down and felt a single tear steamroll down my cheek...but I hid the tear, just as I hid my inner emotions that quite frankly... I could not stand this place. [ " Visitors " ] I had just returned from my first breakfast as a Jailbird...and quite frankly, it was the most surreal thing I had ever bared witness too. I had become afraid of this place in which I was destined to call my home. I was willing to do anything I could to get out of this hell hole...but inside I knew that, no matter what..there was truly nothing I could do. I walked over to the desk...I still had forty seven minutes before nine o' clock, the time where the guards extinguished my personal lamp that I had placed in my cell. I sat at the desk, flicked the lamp on, opened the notebook, and once again began to write in my notebook. This time it wasn't about my life as a Prisoner though...I still had thoughts that overflowed concerning the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. I had no one to talk to, no one to speak with...so I simply wrote them down. Here I sit...in a place where suffering is the only known way of living But instead of constant complaints...I have learned to deal with such sufferings and now, one man, one John Adams, will learn to deal with it as well The suffering from which this cell I have suffered will be inflicted on you Adams You have done nothing but down my name, in a poor attempt to up your status as somewhat of a...legend Tessa Windsor, you defeated her...thats good for you But no man, nor woman for that matter is invincible...you proved that with your win over Tessa but now your head..your ego has swollen to a point where it puts you in danger You think that just because you defeated a household name in Tessa Windsor that you're going to be able to cruise by me A man that the world has never heard of Never seen but guess what Adams I am not Tessa Windsor I am Dani Furher I am an Immortal I am a Prophecy And you are the one to whom I shall bring defeat Speak your worst Adams Curse my name Insult me with your jokes but in the end, only one things matters and that is who hears there name called by the ring announcer and quite frankly Adams you'll have to KILL me....in order to make sure that somebody is you Welcome John Adams, into a world of... Eternal Bliss As I wrote those final words unto the paper...the ink with which they were written with became soaked in a single tear that had rolled off of my face and unto the sheet. I knew deep down inside that no matter how much I tried to hide the fact that I was truly frightened...I was, and that was enough to cause me mental destruction. I walked over to my mattress and I laid upon it, looking up at the ceiling...taking a look back on the past..and a glimpse at the future. All of the sudden though, the room became completely dark. I knew it wasn't nine o clock yet so the lights were not supposed to be turned off. Things became even more strange as I began to see smoke shoot up from the center of the ring. I grabbed my mattress and began to squeeze it as hard as I could...for I truly knew not what was happening. Finally a man, making a evil laughter sound, appeared from the smoke. It was not a regular human though...as a matter of fact, it had no body...only a face...a large red face that held on it black horns and intimidating red eyes. It looked like your everyday assumption of satan himself...and quite frankly, it was enough to almost make me scream a cry of mercy. The demon like creature began to speak to me with a tone so deep, that it sent a vibration throughout your soul. Demon: Dani....muwahaha....DANI !! You do not know who I am...for I am Lucifer... Dani Furher: What the ... Demon: Now now Dani, dont speak. I am here to help you out. You are in this hell for a crime that you did not commit. You are going to be sentenced to DEATH for something that you are completely innocent of...not if you take my advice though. Dani Furher: You...you...you're not real. Demon: Hahaha...I am if you make me...and Dani, I am here to get you out of here Dani Furher: Ho..How do you plan on doing that. Demon: It's simple Dani...if you want them to believe that you're truly innocent...you have to make an example out of them. Dani Furher: And how am I supposed to do that ? Demon: You're still here because of another man's crime...he made an example out of you...and it was enough to keep you quiet...now you must do the same. Dani Furher: Get away from me...GET AWAY FROM ME !!! I will never follow you satan. Demon: Fine...but when you aren't able to take care of your family...and they end up DYING because daddy wasn't there...you will have wished you would have listed to me. There's still time Dani....there's still time. I looked on in a state of shock as the demon's head begin to sink back into the smoke with those last final words bouncing off of the cell walls. Finally everything turned back normal as I began gasping for air. A state of Disbelief...thats what I was presented with. I started to become so desperate that those words of that Demon...they actually...they actually began to make sense. [ " Persuaded Action " ] An officer sat in his chair in front of the main entrance to the Prison, watching a portable television. An episode of " Family Ties " was his chose of shows as he sat there calmly munching on a donut and sipping, and drinking a cup of coffee. All of the sudden, as he lifted the cup to his mouth, he was knocked out of his chair. a man in all black and a ski mask began to brutally attack the officer with a metal pipe...battering him over and over again. He then took the wooden chair in which the guard had been sitting in and cracked the chair over the back of the officer almost knocking him out cold. The man then took the officers Nightstick and stripped it from his waste and began to cave the officers skull with the nightstick leaving the officer lying in a pool of his own blood. Finally the man let up on the attack and stood over the body of the bloody officer before taking off his ski mask revealing himself to be.... Dani Furher To Be Continued
