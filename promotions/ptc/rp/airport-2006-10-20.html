Airport

20 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-20 "Sir, we have noticed a slight problem with your carry on luggage." Now this was unexpected, he had gotten through hundreds of airport scanners before, why would now be any different? Yes, he knew that security was tighter post 9-11 but he had nothing more harmful than a packet of cigarettes and a porno in his bag, what could they have a problem with? "Problem?" Looking at the x-ray screen, the customs officials look at the man in question, eyeing him up and down suspiciously. "There appears to be something in your luggage that is not allowed by aviation rules, well, when I say something, I mean someone!" Ah, now this was a problem. The little bastard had assured him that his cloak was lead lined and thus would have made him invisible, but apparently not. Time to think quickly, one would imagine. "Oh that, that’s not a person, it’s my ventriloquists dummy, he goes everywhere with me." Not a particularly good lie but he hoped it would over his tracks enough to get him out of here. "I would believe that sir, apart from one small detail. Your … doll appears to be breathing, and if I may say so, it seems to be incredibly realistic." Oh indeed it is, but that’s not for this official to know. "Great isn’t he, I got him from Japan last year, fully automated, looks like he is breathing, he can sweat, everything but think he can do it." "Including smoking apparently." The official turned the screen around and amongst the other contents of the bag, imported pornographic magazines, jockey shorts and a wash bag, this apparent dummy appears to be, and is in fact, puffing away quit merrily on a cigarette. "Now that I can’t explain." The bag is pushed through the rubber flaps on the other side of the x-ray machine and there is a small plume of smoke coming from the top of the zip. Had someone taken a smell, they probably would have been able to tell the brand of cigarette but instead, the customs officers just open it up and they are confronted by a man, a small man obviously, chain smoking his way to an early grave, lit cigarette in one hand a c copy of Razzle in the other. "Jesus guys, leave me in peace, can’t a guy relax on a long trip?" With that, the small blue man zips the bag up, enclosing himself in the smoke filled duffel bag. "Ohhhhhh yea." SLAM Hitting the cold floor of a jail cell is not fun at the best of times, but when you are still zipped inside a duffel bag that has now been padlocked shut, well that just takes the piss. Rolling to a stop under the bunk, the bag, or rather it’s occupant let’s out a cry of mild discomfort … there was supposed to be more but life sucks major league, apologies to Youngblood.
