Funeral for the condemned

22 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-22 If we were deemed worthy to inherit the Earth why have we deemed ourselves worthy to destroy it? Humanity, the failed experiment of a Father whom most have lost faith in, now destroy that which was given to us. Our holy union with the Father nothing more than words that we no longer adhere to, that we no longer care for. It must be nice, after all, to ignore the divine calling and to please only yourself. To wallow in your own arrogance and conceit while trying to convince all around that you are good and holy. The demons that come for them I am meant to protect, and yet, I see them almost as the demons do. I realize their greed. I realize their lust. And, above all else, I realize that most now have accepted the absence of their God as proof of his nonexistence. While I might pity them I cannot save them from themselves. Only he could save them. This is the record of my journey. This is the final chapter of my birth and eventual realization. Origins Chapter III – Funeral for the condemned 1300 A.D. “I found it…” His hand shook as he scribbled on the tan pieces of parchment. The lines of the ancient Pagan heretics had been written long before the age of Christianity and yet they held the answers. It was a strange occurrence and something he should have noticed. But he didn’t. Instead of noticing how strange it was that the weapons of the Pagans were now aiding Christianity, he took it as a sign from Jehovah himself to finish his work. He intended to find the answers that so many feared. It was his intention to show them the face of God so that none could claim his none existence again. For all their reasoning, for all their blasphemy, Father Taylor still wanted to save them. The people who had persecuted his faith, the people who had fought against the Church, those people deserved salvation as well. He believed that he could help lead them back to the Father and that it was his destiny to do so. It wasn’t arrogant to him to think of himself as a Shepard. It wasn’t something that didn’t make sense to him. In his mind his rational was sound and even reasonable. Lead the rest of the followers home so that one day we might all be allowed to bask in his glory. Yes, that was his original intention, but had he known what was going to happen as a result of his intentions… …he might have killed himself rather than stepping out that door. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Cardinal Neviana you can’t be serious.” The round face of the Cardinal smiled as he continued to gather his things and place them into a small, brown knapsack. He was serious, as usual, and though his brothers might not understand, he didn’t expect them to. There was quit chatter about the room but Neviana paid it no mind, he just stopped listen to them, much like God had done to him. Taking his rosary from the wall he slid it over his head and let it fall down around his neck. “I am leading a final expedition to the Landlock and I am taking Father Taylor with me. I know what will be said and I understand full well that my place within the Church might be forfeited by these actions. I don’t care. Whatever is down there we must know for the good of not only mankind, but the good of the Father himself. Should we continue to fail in our pursuits how long do you believe it will be before Satan and his minions are loosed once again upon this world? How long are we going to idly sit back and watch as our people are led astray? I understand my orders completely and I disobeying them. You can let the Pope know of my reasoning and when I return I imagine I’ll be taken before him myself to face trial. However when I return I will bring answers to the riddles that so many have failed to answer. I will have found what we seek within the Landlock and bring proof of his existence to the world of man. Faith wanes in these days, gentlemen, but I intend to restore it.” It was a rousing speech, so much so that a few of his fellow parishioners were actually swayed. They stepped back and nodded their heads. Long ago the Church had begun to test the faith of the men who made up its order, many of those men. However for each that failed it only strengthened their resolve to find the proof that the world would need. They no longer wanted to face their test of faith but rather simply give the answer to the world. Be it for a need to repent for their own failure or for the failure of others, only they themselves knew. And so they watched as Cardinal Neviana finished collecting his things, as he gave a slight nod to them and headed towards the dormitory chambers of Father Taylor. Many wondered what would happen next, not only to them, but to the Church as well. Some knew what they were dealing with, the type of entities that existed when humanity went beyond its own limitations and searched for me. They knew but there was nothing that could be done. Cardinal Neviana had made up his mind and until the Vatican herself sent word back to remove him from his position, they were still under his command. Deep down they knew that the trial of Cardinal Neviana would take place upon his return, most likely brought before them inquisition and charged with heresy. Some felt a twang of sadness at the thought. Others believed upon his return he would get what he deserved. None of them knew what truly lay in wait. None but Cardinal Neviana. And as he approached Father Taylor’s room, Neviana had a hard time wiping the smile from his face. He knew what they were going to do and he knew what would happen upon completion of their project. They would awaken Avion, the abomination, and with his fury released upon the world there would be none who could dare challenge the Church. If only Cardinal Neviana had realized the truth of his mission. He believed he was on a pilgrimage for God to cleanse the world of sin. If only he had known that it was not God who called his name. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “This can’t be.” Padre Carliz Santino starred down at the scrawls and scribbles that had been left behind by Father Taylor. Symbols were etched into the wall using black chalk, he had completely rewritten the bible in his own blood and worst of all, he had carved the message of the Pagans tome onto the monastery wall. As others flooded into the room to see what the commotion was about Padre Carliz turned and ran from the room. He knew what had to be done. As he returned to his room he removed his robes and said a prayer. Turning slowly he pulled out the bottom drawer of his clothes cabinet and starred down into it. There lay the instruments of the inquisition, the weapons of war that the Church had deemed most fit. Light reflected back from the shinning silver armor and he ran his hand over the symbol embroidered on the upper right breast plate. It was a rose, the symbol of beauty and tranquility, the symbol of the damned order of Church. The Templar Knights. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- With two quick swipes the creatures face fell to pieces. The undead minions of the demon were no match for him and he knew it, it was their master whom he had to worry about. Yet their master still hid in the back row. He was not afraid to let his congregation of the dead do the dirty work which was his by right. Instead he would simply hide away behind his flock and hope that his target wore himself out. But Monroe knew better. Their groans and undead curses came towards him but he was not affected, he tore through their ranks, the dead people of the town he’d known his entire life, and continued along his course. His blade bit down hard through the skull of one of them, and he twisted the dagger and sent the head flying off into the distance, kicking another to hold it at bay as it approached. Sneering he slid through them, slicing off limbs and leaving the rotting corpses useless on the ground where they could do no more harm. Yet the more he took down the more there seemed to be. The demon got further and further away as Monroe continued to slaughter his flock, and yet, they just kept coming. He knew that there couldn’t have been that many people in the town and wondered what exactly was happening. Snapping the neck from one he snatched the head off its shoulders and leapt into the air, driving his foot down onto another shoulder and taking aim. He sent the decapitated head flying straight for the demon, which was completely and totally unprepared. While it had been deep in prayer, doing whatever it took to keep the flock going, it was Monroe who found a weakness in their numbers. As the skull struck the creature in the face Monroe let out a smile as he heard the nose crack. Yes, the demon had taken human form to toy with him, and yes, now the demon would pay for its arrogance. It stumbled forward for a moment and fell to its knees. As it was weakened so too were the members of his flock. Monroe tore through them easier than before and found that when their numbers fell this time, they stayed down. Coming to stand before the fallen creature, Monroe stopped and smiled as he lorded over him, letting the demon know that his time upon this plain was at an end. But when Monroe reached down to take hold of the creature and prepare it for the final blow, something happened that he did not expect. The demon threw his arm away and screamed in the horrid language of the damned. As quick as he was he wasn’t quick enough and the creature plunged a clawed hand into his chest. It exploded out of the other side and for a brief moment Scott Monroe believed that he was going to do. He had failed them, failed everyone. His grandparents, Samantha, his Father, he truly was the weak little child that so many had claimed. Was it dementia that had let him to this road? Was it arrogance? He didn’t know. The wind blew the black leather hat from his head and his long silver and black hair blew in the wind. The demon starred at him with a grim smile as he fell to his knees. He looked up, the last light he believed he would ever see, and watched as the face of Father McArthy completely fell away. He watched as the jaw broke and extended itself. He watched what he believed would be his doom. But from on high another watched as well. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Father Taylor stood before the Landlock and starred in disbelief. It was a gateway, a portal, to where none of them could imagine. Taylor and Neviana had their own ideas of what was locked within but in truth they were completely unprepared. From his knapsack Father Taylor removed his writing and notes. The large stone monument that stood before him was written in Pagan language and he was forced to decipher the words using his work from the Pagan tome. As he stood before the obelisk Father Taylor laid his hand upon the smooth, hard stone and gave a slight nod. His heart beat quickly as he moved the pieces of the puzzle to and fro. They slid easily from place to place, clicking with every correct movement. His eyes grew wide as an eerie light began to pour from within the Landlock. It blinded him for long moments but his hands continued to move. He knew the puzzle better than he knew his scripture and there was nothing that could stop him from completing it. Cardinal Neviana stood back with a broad smile upon his face. It had been his life’s work to bring about the resurrection of the damned creature. In tomes and literature about it he was spoken of as the Black King. The one entity born into existence and meant to curse the name of God for all eternity, for this entity was more than just a spirit, it was a direct defiance of God’s law. Neviana, however, believed truly that with this creature loosed upon the world that the Church would be in complete and total control. He believed his magi and his scholars, he believed in the sacred order of the Knights Templar and their ability to control the demon. Arrogance, it seemed, was indeed Cardinal Neviana’s greatest flaw. For as the streams of brilliance leapt from their cage the Earth herself began to rumble. The eyes of Gaia turned to the Landlock and for the first time in a millennia, Gaia herself openly wept. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Heavenly Father I know you can hear me. It is I, the Mother of creation; I seek your counsel and wisdom in this, our darkest hour.” She waited patiently for long moments before she heard a voice. It was warm, inviting, and she knew instantly that the Father of creation had returned from his travels abroad and was now, once again, paying full attention to his children on Earth. “Yes, my beloved?” The Earth trembled as she spoke. Volcanos began to erupt in the middle of the ocean, the plates of land began to shift and a huge tidal waves were born from it. The surged forward, towards a small island in the Indian Ocean. The people turned in time to see the wall of water rush towards them. In a futile attempt to flee they turned to their man made science and their weapon. One of the five stones that had been taken from the Gauntlet of Ruin, one of the few Heavenly artifacts to have fallen to Earth and remained safe since it had been found. But the people there were afraid and they turned their weapon against the Ocean. They tried to fight back the wall of water. And the eyes of the Ocean turned and looked upon them. Leviathan, the keeper of the Sea rose from his slumber at her command. “Avion is being released from his cage, my love, and there is nothing that can be done.” But the voice that spoke to her was sure and confident. It warmed her heart to hear him speak and she longed to bask forever in the greatness that he was. “Fear not for them, my love, for what Avion is and meant to be are two truly different things. There is one among them, one who will be born near the end days, and he will be trigger to set in motion the events of the apocalypse. Even now as your tears race towards Atlantis, you are preparing the unborn soul for the life that he must lead.” Yet she didn’t understand. “Would it serve your purpose to let them be?” She knew as she spoke that it wouldn’t. Yet she longed to hear him say it. “No, my love, for too long have the mortals of Atlantis toyed with my creations. For too long have they looked to the Heavens and even brazenly considered trying to rebuild the Tower of Babble. Now they turn our own weapon against us, and I pity them for what they shall endure because of it.” She thought for a moment before speaking again. “If you pity them, my Lord, then why not spare them?” There was silence for a long moment. She could tell that the thought had more than crossed his mind, that it had become a weight upon his shoulders, that when the time came for the destruction of his people that he had no choice. He knew what would happen to them before he had ever created Adam. “Because to stay my hand would be to let them play into Lucent’s’.” Leviathan rose slowly behind the wall of water that was being held back by the power of the weapon of Heaven. He watched as the energy surged forward and held back the wishes of his master and he grew angry at the parasites and their insolence. “Then I know what must be done.” The thought came to him as an order and the beast was more than happy to comply. With a single swish of his mighty tail he sent the wall of water over the small island. The people were swept away in the current as he dove beneath the sea and found what he was searching for. Where the island connected to the mountain beneath the sea, where it teetered on the brink of being submerged forever. The great beast sent itself hurtling through the water, creating tidal waves of his own that surged towards the mainland. He struck the piece of Earth full force and Gaia herself turned to watch his poise. The great beast rose beneath the broken mountain and brought it high into the air, then leapt from the sea and returned to it head first, dragging the content deep beneath the Ocean currents and destroying everything that had come near its wake. “Leviathan, my pet, you have served me well. Return to your slumber until the seven seals have been broken.” And as Atlantis sunk to the bottom of the Ocean it was Leviathan who continued further down. Into the depths where there was no light. Into the chasm of darkness where he was allowed to sleep until it was time for him to answer the call of his master. As for the people of Atlantis, they were the first to feel true fury of the curse that all heretics were meant to know. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Padre Santino, followed by a host of others, tore down the long winding steps that led to the Landlock. His hope was that he wouldn’t be too late. That hope, however, was shattered as he stepped foot into the room. “Lord..no..” Cardinal Neviana turned and his eyes grew wide. “Taylor, hurry, they are here.” But Father Taylor did not hear him. He didn’t hear anything that was going on behind him. All he could hear, all he could see, was the infinite beauty of the light. All he wanted was to bath in that light forever. An endless sleep, a wonderful dream that would mean he would never have to wake again. But Padre Santino had other plans. Without a thought he drew his blade and stepped forward. “Cardinal Neviana you are under arrest by the Knights Templar for heresy. You will be taken to the Vatican and held on trial before the Pope himself. There you may repent for your crimes and pray that God is as forgiving as well believe him to be.” But Neviana sneered and threw down his knapsack. “You think that you can stop him? You think that you can take me back to stand trial when it is you, not I, who are truly the heretics. While you murder the innocent for the hand of the Church do you believe that you curry favor with God? You, not I, are the ones guilty of heresy and soon you all shall be judged.” Padre Santino stepped forward with his sword at the ready. As he neared Neviana he could feel something was wrong and a voice in his mind screamed for him to run. The hesitation lasted only a moment but it was enough. The final barriers between the seals of the Landlock and Earth were torn down and Father Taylor stepped back to behold what he believed would be the true face of God. He smiled and knelt before that which he thought was his creator, prepared to offer his praise and, if need be, his penance. But the being that emerged from the darkness was not God and as Father Taylor looked upon it, he knew that he had been used. A great hoofed beast that stood on two legs as man does, with black leather wings of the angels and two great horns extending from its head. It reached out with a clawed hand and took hold of Father Taylor by the chin, lifting his head and starring into his eyes. All others in the room stopped, some believed it to be Satan himself, others believed it to be a demon unknown, but no one truly knew the answer to what it was except for Father Taylor and Cardinal Neviana. “It was your bloodline that cursed the Father. I see a premonition of a future time, a time when our kind will be one, a time when I shall indeed have revenge for what has happened to my Father. Kneel before me, descendant of Judas, and understand the curse that has been placed upon you.” Padre Santino sneered and ran towards the creature. “You will not defile this world with your unholy existence. Back to Hell with you, spawn of the damned.” But Avion, son of Michael, simply turned and starred at the mortal charging towards him. As Padre Santino drew closer and closer the beast smiled a broad smile. Santino spun his blade and brought it around, only for Avion to stop it with his clawed hand and to stare down at it. For a moment the beast continued to smile, and then it realized what blade that Padre Santino was carrying. “Cursed wretch…” Avion threw his arm back and sent Padre Santino flying through the air. He landed hard on the ground but held a firm grip on his blade. “You dare assault me with that which once belonged to my Father?” His rage was instant and he turned to the remaining Knights Templar who now were making their way towards him. With but a thought he raised his clawed hand into the air and pointed at them. Instantly and simultaneously, their bodies began to break and blood covered the walls as their heads exploded. And as Padre Santino watched the massacre of his men, he then understood why he had been sent to live on the mount. Why his entire life he had been raised differently from the rest. And why he had been given the Archangels sword. Pushing himself up from the ground he took hold of the blade with both hands and sneered. “Normally, beast, I would offer a pray to the Father to cleanse not only your sin, but mine as well.” Stepping forward he held himself up and looked the creature directly in the eye. “But today there shall be no such prayers from me. Today I will seal you back into the void where you belong…or I will die trying.” With that he charged forward and pointed his blade directly at the beast’s chest. The creature stood its ground and snarled in rage as the insolent mortal raced towards him. The two collided and clashed, the sound of the Archangel sword was a light humming that could be heard every time that Padre Santino took a swing. His blade bit hard into Avion’s arm, while the clawed fingers tore through the outer layer of his armor. He knew that he wouldn’t survive another shot like that and summoned his courage and his strength. “Heavenly Father please guide my hand…I beg of you.” And Padre Santino stepped forward, beneath one of the beast long claws and bringing his blade directly beneath the soft flesh that covered its chest. The sword hissed as it tore easily through the beast body and exploded from the other side. A spurt of black blood escaped from the creatures’ mouth as it leaned forward and seemed to lose its footing. Cardinal Neviana starred forward in disbelief and horror, the impossibility of a human overthrowing the will of God, he wouldn’t stand for it. Looking about the room he grabbed one of the fallen knights blade and let out a primal scream as he charged forward. The blade tore through the unprotected back of Padre Santino’s armor and severed his spine, protruding from his stomach and letting his crimson drip in droplets to the floor. He turned slowly and looked upon the eyes of the man who had felled him, the coward who had struck him down from behind. “Neviana…” Santino gasped as his breathing became slow and ragged. “…you are a blind…” His head slumped as his body fell to the cold, stone floor. “…fool.” And with that all light that once existed in the eyes of Padre Santino was drained away. Cardinal Neviana left to stand over him confused by his final words. Before he could sort them out he heard the beast begin to speak once more. “Thank you, Neviana, for without your ignorance this moment could have never come to pass. Now allow me to grant to you the greatest gift that I can offer.” The great clawed hand went above Avion’s head as Neviana shook his round head and tried to scream. The long nails tore through his neck and severed the head from the body. There was no scream. There was only the sound of his life force dripping onto the rock. “Kneel before me, Judas-Son, and understand the plan that your God has for you.” Father Taylor found he was no longer in control of his own faculties. He knelt before the great beast and starred at the wound it had suffered. God was infallible; this beast could not have been a God, much less his God. With all his might he struggled and reached up, taking hold of the blade that still hung in the creatures’ chest. As he tried to pry it loose Avion screamed in agony. The beast literally was paralyzed by the pain of the wound and the pain that the sword itself had caused. Father Taylor managed to pry the sword free and made one final attempt to slay the beast, swinging wildly and catching the creature in the face, he sent it tumbling back towards the Landlock. “Taylor, your soul…” Taylor charged him determined to right the wrongs he had caused but in those final moments the great beast rose from the ground and snatched him up, hoisting him into the air and knocking the sword from his hand. “…is mine!” Father Taylor screamed as the great beast reached inside of his chest and laid a clawed finger upon his heart. “This is the knowledge of the past, present and future. This is the knowledge of the failure that your family has known and how that failure has affected the course of this universe. Your God, Taylor, has now abandoned you. Your bloodline shall now be tainted with my touch and there is nothing that you can do to stop me. Kneel before me, my disciple, and prepare to spread my doctrine of hate.” Again he fell to his knees as a strange black light began to glow in the creatures eyes. The energy seemed to seep into him and he suddenly felt himself becoming very small, very thin. The energy exploded out of his mouth, nose and eyes. He screamed as he learned the story of what he was and what his family was to become. He was given the vision. He saw Judas. He saw himself. And he saw his descendant. Two silver eyes starred back at Father Taylor as he realized that this man was also a priest. And he began to laugh. “Avion, you fool…” Taylor gasped as he began to lose his strength. “…You think my descendant is going to help complete this vicious cycle and yet, you are wrong. Your vision has done nothing but offered me a chance to die in peace. To die knowing that for my failures and for my families failures, we shall repent.” Father Taylor took his final breath and he thought of the son he’d never known. He thought of the woman who he’d given up to follow his calling as a priest. He smiled knowing that sooner or later, his family would finally be at peace. Avion watched the mortal die and wondered about his words. He wasn’t given long to think before another voice came to him. A familiar voice, a voice who had remained with him during his exile from existence, the voice of his Father. “Avion, you have been struck by the sword…” The creature stumbled and fell to its knees. “Yes, Father, I have failed you.” A vision appeared before him. Angelic and beautiful the winged creature took hold of his son and held him close. “Your body will die, my son, but your soul shall remain alive.” Kneeling over the broken body of Father Taylor, the Archangel began to chant. His words were the words of the Pagans. The creatures who had lived forever outside of God’s wrath. Now their existence and God’s apathy towards it would prove to be his undoing. The soul of the creature was transported into the broken mortal body. The soul of Father Taylor was banished forever into the void. Never again to know the light of life, the light of happiness or the light of God. Yet the body of Father Taylor rose from the ground and the Archangel let out a laugh. A laugh that was both excited and psychotic in tone and meaning. He believed that it was time for them to have their revenge. The first step had been taken and now all that was needed was a little patience. Striding slowly towards the Landlock, Michael stood before the darkness and looked towards the vessel of his son. “I shall seal myself away to protect you from the coming hunt. Upon finding a being still trapped within they will be foolish enough to think that their warriors have done their job well. When the time comes, my son, you will awaken me and we shall stain this land in the blood of their kind. Until then know this…” The body of Father Taylor looked to the Archangel and starred at him. “…Your mother and I love you very much.” And without another word Michael cast himself into the pits. Avion reached out from the body of Father Taylor but it was already to late. The incantations and murmurs began before Michael even took a step towards the gate and as he stepped through he was completely and totally sealed away from this reality. Avion, the abomination, fell to his knees and wept. As the tears fell and the beast knew what it meant to know emotion, he tasted, for the first time, the bitter taste of hate. Looking to the skies he screamed a promise that would bold ill for the world. “Jehovah, know this, as you are my witness I shall slaughter all of their kind. And when I am done I will be coming for you and the Heavens themselves.” And in the darkness of the Landlock, left alone and meant to finish the plan set for by his Father, Avion rose from the ground and began to the long trek towards his mission. He had a reason to exist. He had a soul purpose now. The extermination of the human race and the torment and eventual death of Jehovah. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Is your rage so easily extinguished?” The voice called out to him from the void. It was the voice of the Archangel, the one who had set the entire plan into motion. He stirred and tried to bring himself to his feet but he could not. He was weak at the moment and didn’t even know if he was still alive. “Think of what they did to your family…” His grandmother and grandfather, two of the most important people in his life, slaughtered by the hands of these savages that now walked the Earth. He could remember what they had done. And the spark of hate was instantly reignited. “Think of what they did your fiancé.” And the vision came to him. Frightening, cruel, he watched as the demons danced about her as she hung from the cross. She was to be a martyr for their sins. As the armies of the damned walked this plain they took great delight in causing the humans who knew nothing of them pain. They were bitter and angry, upset that they had been thrown aside by God so that he might enjoy his new creation. They tore away her flesh and began to gnaw at it. Her screams filled his ears and his rage began to burn bright. Like a candle in the cave in of time, from the darkness the flame began to burn. It danced about the wind of his soul but now it could not be extinguished. He was seeing first hand what she went through. And then she mouthed the words. “Save me Scott.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The creature leaned forward and meant to consume his prey but found itself stopped short. A black aura had come around him and was now protecting him. The demon slowly retracted its jaw and rose from the ground, four legs tearing from beneath the flesh of what was once Father McArthy, his face falling backwards and hanging like a tumor from its side, great boned limbs tore through the rest of the flesh and allowed Scott Monroe his first true glimpse at the evil he had been forever doomed to stand against. “Avion I see you…” The demon spat as it spoke. “…But know that I do not fear you, as I did not fear your Father.” Monroe rose from the ground slowly and stood before the demon. His long leather dust danced in the wind as the flock of the damned made their way towards him. There was a voice in his mind driving him forward, it was time for the awakening, time for him to understand what he was meant to become. “Scott, bring the flames upon them and let it consume them as they were meant to be consumed.” In his mind he could hear the choir begin. They played his song, his tribute, and it would be the hymn of the dead that called him to action. His wound was nothing more than proof that he had gone beyond their limits. That no longer was he bound by their laws and no longer would he be held in place because there were stronger creatures before him. Now, he became that which they feared most, he became the Arcane. “Look upon me, agent of Lucent, and know that my creation bolds ill for you and your lot. For too long have you trampled over the law of God and ruined the soul of man. For too long have your kind been allowed to prosper while humanity waited for salvation. Now, despite whatever might be said or thought, I shall represent mankind as that salvation. I shall lead them home, back to the Father, and forever shall I abolish you and your lot. Look upon me, great deceiver, and know it to be true.” The creature snapped its gapping maw at him but Monroe paid it no heed. His hands fell calmly to the weapons that had brought him here, hope and faith, the twin blades of the chorus of Heaven. Forged in the fires of creation and passed down to humanity to serve as their only hope to hold back the coming tide of the damned. The silver lining of Heaven itself had been torn so that those weapons could be made. Jehovah even put a bit of himself into them. There was no way to shatter them, no way to stop them, they would cut through the hardest materials ever known and retain their original properties. Now they were in the hands of the one man who would wield them as the Father had intended. And from on high Jehovah turned his attention back to man. Their time was coming to an end and their only hope for salvation lay in the same bloodline that had once cursed them to this fate. There would only be once chance to repent and Jehovah himself was curious to see how strong man had really become. It was not his hope but rather his belief that should this one rise, that so too would all the rest. Lucent would have been turned back and perhaps their disaster could be averted. It was time for God himself to pay attention to man once more. Though none of them knew it the great passion play of Christianity now rested completely on the shoulders of one man. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Do you…” The voice took his concentration away from them for a moment. “…really believe…” It was a familiar voice and one that Jehovah had longed to hear for a millennia. “…that your pilgrim can stop them?” Without turning to look at his friend Jehovah gave a slight nod. “Yes, Lucent, I am afraid I do.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The long bone claws tore through his flesh and sent him flying across the landscape. But as he landed he rolled and smiled, spinning the blades between his fingers and leaping back to his feet. The ruined flock stepped towards him but he quickly cut them down, creating a path of dead corpses in his wake. The demon starred at him with contempt but knew that his hatred was strong. That as long he controlled the power of Avion he would forever be a threat. With each step he drew closer and as he tore through their ranks he laughed aloud. It wasn’t the laugh of pleasure and happiness. It was the laughter of insanity. The fact that he was here, doing this and it wasn’t just a dream. He charged the demon and swung wildly, faith and hope tearing away at its mass, and he nimbly ducked his head as a long bone claw swung for it. Stepping around the creature he brought hope down at an arch and sliced one of the tendons in its ankle. The creature stumbled for a moment and Monroe sneered, his movement speed increased and he slid faith around and snapped another tendon. From two legs to four and back to two again the creature went. Unable to control itself and nearly off balance, Monroe took his opportunity to go in for the kill. Leaping onto the creatures back he scurried his way to its shoulders and starred down at the back of its head. In one moment it would all be over. In one moment the first of them would fall. And then he felt the pain tear through him. The demon leaned forward and as it did so a spike exploded from its back and tore through his chest. Impaling him upon the demons exoskeleton and lifting him higher into the air. Slowly the demons head spun around, coming to face him as he hung from its back, smiling at him with a knowing face. “You see, young priest, there is nothing that you or Avion could have done to stop us. We are the immortal, the final pieces in the puzzle to dethrone Jehovah and make the angels the ones who exist on high. Your faith in a God who does not care was your downfall and I shall take great pleasure in knowing that you died for nothing. Worthless human, know pain most immense at my hand.” “No!” She screamed as the demon prepared to rend him in two and his eyes opened wide. It was a voice he hadn’t heard in years, not since their days in Catechism together when he had been thrown out of the Church for believing that Christmas was a capitalist holiday. His vision was blurred but as it came into focus he could see her standing there. She was real. She was human. And now she was there for him. As she had promised him all those many years ago. “Racheal?” She gave a slight nod as the creature looked from his victim to this newest addition to the puzzle. Before it could act she began to speak and her voice did something that Monroe did not expect. It filled him with a divine light. “Scott we knew this day would come for a long, long time. Try as we might we could not advert our future or the future that was planned for our friends and family. We were meant to know torment to prepare us for the war that we had to rage. Everything we’ve experienced up until now has just been building to this point. Take hold of yourself and realize who and what you are. You are the Arcane, Scott Monroe, and your hand shall defy the laws of man and God alike, in the end bathing the world in his beauty.” The demon screeched at her as she stepped forward. “And he will not let you go alone.” Reaching down she snatched the crucifix that hung around her neck and held it aloft. The demon fell back; even the sight of the object was painful to him. Stumbling for a moment it dropped Monroe from its pike and turned its full attention to Racheal. “We are not alone.” The demon stepped forward and charged towards her but doubled over as it felt a pain in its midsection. Starring down it could see its dark life dripping down the blade. The blade attached to a long wooden handle and at the end of that handle stood a large, mountain of a man. He starred at the demon with a look of disgust on his face as his two blue eyes looked out from behind a red painted face. “We are here, Scott.” Steil Harbinger stood before the demon with a sneer upon his face. His axe planted deep within its midsection, causing his hold on the flock to falter and fail. The bodies began to fall, one by one, until none of his undead minions still remained. The demon lashed out and tore a chunk of flesh from his chest, but Steil did not waver. Instead he looked into the darkness. “We have known our entire lives what was meant to happen to us, Scott, and now we are here to serve.” Megan Santino emerged from the darkness, in her hand she held an exquisite long sword, it shined with a radiance and beauty that none of this plain had seen in a very long time. She stepped forward as did Racheal, and the three surrounded the beast. She held her blade aloft and plunged it deep into the creatures’ chest. Still it continued to writhe and wiggle, still it tried to finish what its dark master had sent it do to. “NOW SCOTT!” She called out to him and he responded. Instantly he seemed to come back to life and his attention turned completely towards the beast before him. “This is for my grandparents..” He screamed as he charged the demon. Steil, Rachael, and Megan all three stepped back. “This is for Father McArthy…” And as he approached the damned deity of the abyss the tears flooded his eyes. “And this is for Samantha….” Hope and faith sung their Heavenly song as Scott Monroe took flight for the final time. His body spun and his blades spun with him. As he landed he brought them down in an arc and slit the creature from its head all the way down to its torso. The wounds sent blood flying into the air in small spurts and then slowly the pieces of the creature fell away. All that stood were two legs and a moment later they fell to ground and became dust before them. And there they stood. A union of four meant to stand before the call of darkness. Four beings bound together by faith, hope, love and unity. It was for their Father that they fought and won this victory. And for the revenge of their friends and fellow townsfolk who were used as pawns in Lucent’s game that they claimed this victory for their own. In the darkness the damned were waiting and they knew now that the champions of Jehovah had finally come. Now the final battle for Heaven, Earth and Hell would begin. Now he would walk the final steps of his families cursed nightmare. -Finale-
