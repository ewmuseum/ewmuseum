The Dating Game Reloaded #1

27 March 2004

Date: 2004-03-27 Life has a way of keeping us on our toes. For days even months we can get stuck into a routine. Wake up, go to the shops, tidy the flat, go to the gym, come home, watch television and go to bed. The same occurrences day in day out but we don’t complain about them simply because we are used to it all. Yet every now and then something special happens to set aside one day as special. A day that will be remembered for the rest of your life. A loud creak echoed around the store as James Edwards reclined heavily on a large lazy boy sofa, the leather rippling under his clothes as he stretched his legs and let out an extremely content sigh his hands resting behind his head. A large smile that was so often customary for the lunartic during his time in PCW, a time when he had risen to the giddy heights of…well that doesn’t matter he’s happy now. For a long time he hadn’t been. After PCW had rather ironically fallen down around him his life even more ironically fell apart. At first it was just the usual, falling in with the wrong crowd a certain raven haired woman who tried to take apart a federation from the inside, tried to own it. Then again what do you expect from a woman that goes around calling herself the devil. Then of course it was a crazed magician who no matter what refused to leave him along stalking him and hunting him day in and day out then of course there was a little trip to the Atlantic but the least said about that the better. That’s all in the past and that is where it shall remain for a new dawn was well on the way he had put off the inevitable for a long long time. Just over three months in fact. Three months of being chased around by an assortment of people each of whom with their own agendas his own brother one of them, but through there he heard of a new federation dawning, old faces but something knew where everything just seemed to click. Where the past would be forgotten, yet embraced at the same time. A new place to call home. RPW “Sir are you interested in buying that chair or not” spoke a voice from above James snapping him out of his little trip down memory lane causing him to bolt upright and stare up at the agitated looking salesman who had apparently been there for some time his arms folded tapping on his arms in a demeanour that more than suggested annoyance. “My good sir I believe that I shall purchase this chair this fine afternoon and do you know why? Because life is good so yep wrap this baby up and deliver it to my apartment post haste.” The words spewing from James’ lips as though he were in a medieval recreation as he smiles from ear to ear his green eyes twinkling with joy. “Very good sir” retorted the salesman who stepped away pulling a note pad out from his pocket and scribbling lightly on it before walking away mumbling to himself about stupid crackpot bastards. James took the time to recline in his chair once more, relaxing and just taking the time to take a step back and look inside himself whilst the salesman tapped away on his computer. He hadn’t even debuted yet but he could feel that things were going to go well, he had friends there sure there were some people he didn’t exactly get on well with too but things were going to go well. There was a lot of buzz around the industry talking about the success that they had already had, it was going to be huge possibly the largest thing out there and he was going to be part of it. “Sir…SIR” the voice raised a few decibels this time as James shot up once again. If you put him in the ring then he had the reflexes of a cat however if he was in a day dreaming mood that very cat himself would be able to scare him. Panting slightly he looked up at the salesman who by now was extremely angry. “Your credit card has been accepted now please kindly get out of the store and stop annoying me” the salesman through the receipt and his card down at James who scooped it up and left without saying another word leaving to the street outside taking his time to walk down the sidewalk, letting the world pass him by. That was part of the problem though, he had no problem with the world passing him by on his own time. In fact he liked that he was able to walk down the street and not be noticed or disturbed. His life was his own, he had seen how some superstars lives had been effected by success, unable to go to the bathroom without having some one trying to get an autograph or even worse a bit of closing. Yet when it came to the ring, he knew that he was a nobody. The doubt going through James’ mind was unbelievable when he had signed on the dotted line he knew that he was going to be starting from the very bottom rung again, that he would be deemed a nobody within RPW and looking around it was clear to see why. Jason O Neil, Hessian, Dave Gibson, Shawn Stewart, and Gideon Poe he could run off names forever and every single time come to one single conclusion. That they were better than him. That’s why he had made a choice. James turned in from the street into a quaint little coffee shop, the aroma of freshly ground coffee beans hitting his nose the second he pushed the door open. That was always something that freshened James up, just the smell of coffee. The irony was he didn’t actually like the stuff and always had the hot chocolate, but today there was a different reason for James entering the coffee shop a reason that he himself was not to be aware of, not yet anyway. “The usual honey?” a soft feminine voice from behind the counter asked. The voice belonged to an attractive blond girl in her mid twenties smiling sweetly at James who simply nodded back at her and sat down in a seat he had sat in everyday for the past few months it was part of a routine at the end of the day after all. The waitress began preparing the drink for James as he simply stared out of the window at the world with a strange sense of calming just from watching everyone else rush through their lives. It had taken a lot of courage but he had decided to enter a tournament for cruiserweights. It turned out the RPW had become a part of Primetime Central and although James wasn’t entirely sure what it was he had been told that it meant exposure. Little Al seemed to want that for RPW at the moment almost every cruiserweight on the roster had showed interest much to his delight and granted them permission to enter. Names like Matt Jackson and Karina Wolfenden whom he may have to face along the way but that was a long way off round one was fast approaching and it was certainly no walkover in fact the odds were greatly against him. “You’re a bit later than usual, I thought you had run off and left me here” came the voice of the woman once more who was now standing above James with his large cup of hot chocolate in her hand, placing it gently on the table in front of James smiling as she made eye contact with James breaking his trance from the window smiling back up at her. “You know I’d never leave you” replied James, not really paying attention to what he had just said the waitress’ cheeks began to glow as she blushed turning away from James slightly to try and hide the fact. “I just had to do a bit of furniture shopping” James added quickly the hesitation in his voice all too noticeable. To anyone looking on the attraction between the two would have been obvious yet life isn’t always simple is it and the sad fact that James has always been very naive when it comes to matters of the heart doesn’t help situations like these. “Will there be anything else James, a muffin, biscuit or something” stuttered the waitress, curling his finger through her shoulder length hair as she tried to fumble her way through the sentence but unable to find words. James shook his head but smiled at her always maintaining eye contact as she nodded and turned to walk away. James sighed deeply and let his head droop staring into the brown mass of his hot chocolate. James liked tournaments he always seemed to do well, a chance to face people from different federations. Facing the unknown not knowing what the other person is going to do next. It’s a feeling unlike any other, exhilarating waiting to see what they throw at you. In the past Universal Title Tournament he had made a number of advancements before being knocked out, this time it would be different though. Before he had been training week in week out and wrestling in OSW his body was primed and in top condition whereas now it was a totally different story. He had been out of action for a long time, his brief stint in axiom barely able to be acknowledged three fights in a poorly ran federation not knowing where the next pay check was coming from. The whole nature of the tournament was different as well. No longer would he be considered the little man it was an equal playing field no mismatches in size or weight thus changing the style of the bouts. It would be a lot more intense having to throw your all into it, not holding back and in the back of his mind James wasn’t sure if he could handle that for very long. James sipped his hot chocolate and raised to his feet walking to the counter, the shop was relatively empty so the waitress had no problem coming over to see him again, she looked different this time though, not physically but her expression. A quiet determination had taken over her eyes as she gazed into James’ making him feel a bit uncomfortable, yet he didn’t back away standing his ground mostly through intrigue over what she was about to say. “James I was just wondering what you are doing tomorrow night, it’s just I’ve got the night off and I was wondering whether or not you’d like to do something.” Her words didn’t so much flow from her mouth as stumbled out like a drunken man from a pub however it wasn’t her words that James had noticed. It was the fact that she was rubbing his hand gently with her own sending a weird tingling sensation through his body, a sensation he hadn’t felt in quite a while. “Sarah I…I’m not sure I mean.” James paused for a moment thinking about the words that were coming from his mouth. He had been hiding from getting attached to another woman for a long time holding onto the vein hope that maybe just maybe one day she would return. That he wasn’t sure of but he was certain of this, he liked Sarah and it wasn’t as though she was proposing marriage it was simply a date. “I mean…what would I wear” James said smiling as he did so much to the delight of Sarah who squeezed James’ hand slightly the delight evident in her eyes. She quickly dashed to a back room in the coffee shop leaving James standing alone, somewhat bewildered for a few seconds before Sarah returned to view pushed a piece of paper into James hand and closed it for him. “It’s my home number call me tonight and we will plan something. I’ve got to get back to work now but…thank you it’ll be fun I promise.” Sarah said as she leaned forwards over the counter and gave James a quick peck on the cheek before bounding back to work humming to herself. James moved slowly away from the counter in a state of shock returning to his seat not being able to take his eyes off her, drinking his hot chocolate slowly, not really wanting to leave. Triple threats were uncommon in tournaments but not unheard of it was the luck…or lack there of, of the draw and James had been unlucky. One of them a total unknown Raijin, not aligned with any federation wore a mask and a complete enigma pretty much text book stuff but Coricidon, there was a different kettle of fish all together. He had asked around and it had always been the same reaction. OOOHHHH nasty, unlucky, that’s a tough one. Yet he still knew nothing about her except that she was odds on favourite to win and represented 4WW and yet he was not bothered. He enjoyed challenges and although he did not believe he was going to be in any position to win he knew that it would be fun and a battle at that. James rose to his feet and waved in the direction of Sarah who teasingly blew a kiss at him winking which caused James to blush this time but smile also. He turned and headed to the door opening it and looking back at Sarah he stepped out onto the pavement knocking someone over in the process. “Oh crap sorry I wasn’t paying attention to where I was going and I…” James froze as he stared down at the figure on the floor. He knelt down and helped the woman to her feet as she dusted herself down before looking up at James, she in turn froze. For a moment there was no noise, nothing else in the world for sometimes the world likes to spring surprises on you, a special day that you will never forget. “James?” spoke the female. “Michelle?” replied James as he stared into the eyes of the woman he had lost just eight months ago…
