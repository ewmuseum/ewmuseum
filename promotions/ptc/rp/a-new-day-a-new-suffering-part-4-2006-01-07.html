A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 4

7 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-07 The tossing and turning wouldn’t stop. It had been going on like this for days. Ever since he realized what day was approaching. He just couldn’t believe it had been so long. His body was sweating profusely on his bed, all alone. It was nights like these that he hoped to be able to feel the body of someone he loved beside him, to comfort him. These days things like a simple hug would do wonders for him. That is all he ever wanted, to feel like someone cared for him, that someone was concerned about him. He woke up with a start. His head pounding and sweat all over him and the bed. The room was lit by the light of the stars coming through the sliding door of the balcony. He loved to watch the sky. He remembered the full moons he had with her… he couldn’t forget them as much as he tried. The feelings of guilt were too strong for him to bear and insomnia had been his worst enemy. A lot of things can happen while you sleep. Some people think that dreams are a way of looking into the future. Dreaming can be a form of escape from your biggest fears, but most of the times, dreaming takes those fears and enhances them. From what might have been a bad experience, your brain turns it into fears. His brain had been doing this to him for two years now. The way he discovered the news was even worse. It all had happened without a chance to tell her how he felt. He never got a chance to meet him. The sonogram had said it was a 'him'. That fateful day, he found out not only that the woman he had loved for more than six years and the woman who he thought would be his future wife was dead. They had fallen apart for a few months after she cheated on him with his nemesis. Digital had left her in a physical sense, but his heart had never left her. When he found out about the accident, Justine’s mother told him all about it, he was given a letter that she had written but never sent out. On this letter she had asked for forgiveness, told Digital that she wanted to marry him and gave him the news that she was pregnant with his baby boy. His eyes watered when he remembered this. He got up from his bed and went to the bathroom. He was surprised at how he had been able to stay in his house even though it still smelled like her… or at least he thought that. He had decided to splash some water on his face, maybe that would take him away from these horrible memories. He knew it wouldn’t, but he could hope. "What am I becoming? Here I have a wonderful woman who loves me and I love her, and I’m still thinking of what happened two years ago. What am I supposed to do?" He had asked himself that question in the mirror many times before. The answer never came though. It was like expecting a mathematician to understand that life was more than numbers. Digital couldn’t understand that this experience like every other in his life was meant to shape him, to mold him into who he would eventually be. He felt that it was a shitty way of making a person. As he looked at himself in the mirror, he remembered the days where the reflection was completely empty. He could see a blurry figure and nothing more. It was not fair, but it was his life. They were his memories, and he would carry them forever no matter what. He walked towards the balcony and opened the glass door. A burst of cold winter air hit him as he went outside. The balcony overlooked the beach; he could hear the waves and smell the salty water. On most days, the ocean gave him rest. If he would be alone, he would make solitude his armor. That armor didn’t work on Veronica though. She could use her magic to destroy Digital’s defenses; he had never felt anything like it. How could it work? He still loved Justine, the woman who carried his child on her womb but died two years ago, but at the same time he loved Veronica with all his heart. He never felt better unless he was around her. He knew what he must do now. He had to finish burying his feelings for Justine. He needed her to stay a memory. A wound that time would eventually heal. He had found the answer to all his questions. --- It was dark… but everything had been dark for the last few days, and he had not had a chance to see the only person that brought him radiance. Gloom was all he felt when he was not with her. As he walked over to the rusted metal gate, he realized that no one would be there to open it for him. He started to climb. He went over the gate and landed on his feet. It was dark, but the full moon gave Digital enough visibility as he walked towards where he needed to be. As he walked through the cemetery and looked at the headstones protruding from the ground, he realized that many people go through this. Many people lose loved ones to death, it is inevitable. The untimely fashion and the gruesome manner of their death were the things that irked him the most. He thought that waging a war against the Russian mobster who ordered their death would make him forget, but it hadn’t. Revenge was not really what it is hyped up to be. After you are done, you feel empty. Nothing drives you anymore. He had gotten his revenge but nothing had come off it, she was still dead and he would never see his unborn son. He had to live with that though; he had to live with being the cause of their death. Veronica’s father was not so wrong in his accusations. Digital should have been there with them. Died with them. He finally reached the headstone he had been looking for. On it where written the words ‘Here lies Justine Alvarez and her unborn son.’ Nothing else. No mention of him anywhere. He had been too late, always too late. Always hesitant and always having to feel the consequences of his uncertainty. As he looked at the grave, he could not hold it in. All the great memories of their time together came crashing on top of him. "I’m sorry… I’m so sorry. All I ever wanted was for you to be happy. I am not perfect, but I never thought that you would get hurt because of my past. I never meant for any of this to happen. Siempre te he amado y nunca dejaré de amarte. I don’t know what you would say right now. I hope that you would tell me to continue living my life… to carry on. I’m scared though. You are still on my mind as your death’s anniversary approaches." He stopped for a moment. Remembering the day where he was told of her death. The searing pain that had hit him in the gut after hearing it, the silent tears streaming down his face, he felt it all again. "I… I don’t know what else to say. I wish things could have ended differently, but if there is one thing I have learned in life is that happy endings do not exist. Everything is too messed up. I love you Justine, but you are gone and I need to move on. If I don’t, I will rot." He took out a ring from the pocket of his jeans. He looked at it longingly, but he knew he could in no way use it again. He kneeled to the ground and made a small hole on the dirt right besides the headstone. Inside, he put the ring, and with the ring he buried all his memories of Justine. He would remember her because it was not easy to completely forget, but he left his feeling there for her, where they could grow closer to her. "Adiós mi amor." Digital turns around and walks away with only the moon and the stars as witnesses. They saw him that night as he really is, the real Digital Mortality, the real Antonio Vargas. Life is about difficult decisions, about giving yourself to your decisions… no hesitation. He had hesitated all of his life and he had paid the price for it. He knew that this would be a hard time for him, but he at least knew that out of all this turmoil and confusion something good would come his way. He could feel it now. He missed Justine and he missed the son he never met. He sat in his car after the small ritual and contemplated what to do next. He knew he couldn’t go back to his house. The memories would come flooding back. He started driving aimlessly around town. No direction, he just needed the time to sort out his thoughts. Digital took out his cell phone and dialed a number. The voice on the other end was groggy from sleep but it was a voice that he knew very well. His best friend Burton. "Burt, I need to talk to you. Could you meet me at the usual place? I would really appreciate it." He hung up and finally he had a destination. ----- They were both sitting there. Not a word had been said between the two of them yet. The only other people in the establishment were the late crowd of twenty year olds that finished their night of hanging out and needed to get some food inside their stomachs. Digital had not said a word because he was trying to get his mind straight and Burton had not said anything because he knew that Digital had a lot of things on his mind. "You know what this day is the anniversary of right, Burt?" "Of course. Justine… she was my friend and I miss her like hell. It is hard for me as well, although I understand how tough it has to be for you. I wish none of that would have happened." "I went to her grave tonight. I needed to see it one last time. I took the wedding ring I had offered her long ago and buried it there. Its just so… I miss her." He put his hands on his face and stayed like that for a couple of seconds. Burton could see that Digital was trying to cope with everything at the same time. Seeing Killean with his new found daughter was maddening. He knew that there were people that didn’t know what they had and he loathed every single one of them for it. He had always wanted to hold his kid in his arms. "Digital, you have to realize that you have another wonderful person that really loves you. Veronica is someone that you should never let go. She has been there for you when you were down. I can bet that she is one of those people that make you feel better whenever you feel down. I know you love her. I can see it every time you are with her. Is that what you feel for her?" "Love is just a word. That word could never fully describe how I feel for her. It was just that I was getting the nightmares again, but I think that from this night on, it would never be the same again." He meant it too. He felt for her something that could never be depicted with words. It was special. He knew that he could never let her go, but would she love him the same as well? He knew she loved him. Deep down inside he knew what he was afraid of was that she would be adamant about marrying him. "You know something Burt. Tomorrow night, I am going to ask her to marry me. It would just be so perfect if everything else would just disappear but I know it won’t. The whole thing with her father has her very stressed out. He now has an apartment in Condado and apparently he is seeing a psychologist. I really hope everything gets better for them." He truly did. He knew first hand what the whole problem had been doing to Veronica and Christian. It was not a pretty sight. Digital just wished that he could make her happy. In the past, he had thought that happiness was something that could be bought. He knew it was not like that now. A wise man once told him that happiness only comes in small moments and what we need to do is embrace those small moments as much as we can. Everyday with her was one of those moments, so a life with her wouldn’t be all that bad. He knew the answer to his questions… she was the answer.
