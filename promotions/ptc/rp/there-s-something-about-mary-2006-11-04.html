There's Something About Mary

4 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-04 To feel like you’re floating is a feeling of absolute relaxation. Your conscious body relinquishes control of your mind ending all active thoughts. Instead, your mind goes into a state of rest, taking away from the stresses and agitation. Fluttering his eyes open and closed repeatedly, Killean found himself somewhere between a deep sleep and reality. His mind was wandering but his vision wasn’t all there yet as he sank further into his imaginary world. He felt weightless, his soul practically gliding through what felt like a cool breeze. The light above him was bright, bright enough to play with his vision so that he couldn’t see the approaching obstacle that would soon become the first crossroads of his adventure. Suddenly, the feeling of weightlessness was replaced, gravity pushing down on his body as his feet touch the ground. He blinked a few times and realigned his sight to see a dark room with three doors. The walls were like ancient brick, giving the room a cold, castle type atmosphere. The look on Killean’s face was void of emotion. His stare was intense, yet he was not angry. He just went back and forth, looking at one door and then the next. Their dark brown wood glowed slightly, obviously holding something of importance behind them. Taking his first step, it felt like he was walking on clouds. His feet didn’t make a sound and when he looked down, he was still floating and walking on nothing. Moving his feet, he didn’t look worried about the lack of proper footing as his head rose back to look at the first door. He took a few more steps towards it, grabbed the handle and pulled it open with a click. The hinges creaked like the lid of an old coffin and the light from behind began to pour into the room, enveloping him and dragging him into its essence, his soul becoming one with its aura. ~+~ "Are you enjoying this?" The voice was clear and beautiful. He was looking up at the stars and lying in the back of a pickup truck. However he could not see his own body nor could he move anything below his neck. When he turned his head, he saw himself lying in the truck beside Charity. Instantly he remembered it was the first date that he went on with Charity. They had gone out for a nice dinner in downtown Winnipeg at a restaurant called Hy’s Steakhouse. He even remembered the meals that they ate. She really enjoyed the Peppercorn New York Steak topped with a signature homemade brandy green peppercorn sauce. He was a sucker for the Gorgonzola Fillet Mignon. After the beautiful dinner they shared, they drove off to an area near the Winnipeg International Airport and sat in the back of the pickup truck at first. After watching the sunset, they both lied back, taking a look at the stars appearing in the sky as it got darker. He was reliving this whole experience but he was not a part of it. "I’m having a good time." He replied to her as he shifted his gaze to look at the near full moon that was lighting up the sky. A quizzical look surfaced on the face of Charity as she turned to her side and leaned on her elbow, smiling brightly. "Something bothering you?" She continued to smile as Killean turned to look at her. He fondly remembered this moment as he watched himself slide his arm under hers. He then pulled her closer as she laughed playfully. He smiled now too. "I have just had so many relationships end in heartbreak. I told you about Kira and Stacey. Stacey just turned into a delusional psycho and I lost Kira tragically. I just don’t know if I can take that heartbreak in stride again." His voice was soft and full of worry. Charity caught on to it immediately and put her hand through his hair. This was in fact their first official date but Killean had known her and had been speaking to her for over a month previous where they became friends. Pretty soon they had discovered their feelings for one another and ended up on this date. They weren’t really movie people so they decided to sit out in the open air watching the stars appear in the sky. "I know and I have too. If there is anything I can offer you, it’s my undying friendship. I have never met someone like you before Killean. You have quickly become my best friend." "Thank you Charity, that means so much…" Their eyes locked and after staring into each other’s souls for what seemed like an eternity, they shared their first kiss, lasting for a few moments as a shooting star was seen flying across the sky, burning out into the darkness of the night. After the star, a loud rumble was heard as they broke their embrace and looked over their heads. There, a large passenger jet flew over after just taking off, the lights from the plane flashing brightly. They looked at each other again as the plane passed and they hugged and laughed together. He watched himself share another embrace with Charity as the scene began to fade out of his sight. He began to panic, trying to move or do something to prevent the scene from disappearing. He wanted to relive that experience over and over again. It was the merely the first night of many beautiful ones that they shared together. He felt the love coursing through his soul, filling him up with the desire to see her again. ~+~ He blinked, his vision flashing quickly and he found himself standing in the room with the three doors again. As soon as he discovered he could move, he went towards the first door again, grabbing the door handle and pulling hard. But this time the door remained in its place, not budging from the frame. "Locked…" He thought as he tugged at it once more in vein. The door wouldn’t move. He looked at the small keyhole above the handle and bent down slowly. Upon looking through the keyhole, he saw the pickup truck on the gravel road and the stars shining bright in the night sky. The scene was just as he remembered it, but nothing was moving. It seemed as if everything was frozen in time. Getting back to his feet, he placed his hand on the door, trying to get another feeling for what that beautiful night was like. Sensing that he had to move on from that experience, he looked towards the second door. As he moved towards it, he noticed it was already open a crack. As he put his hand on it and pulled it open, he was once again encircled by the light and he was dragged into the bright vortex. ~+~ Once again, he was stationary. He could not feel his limbs and could only look around at will. He was in the backseat of his own car, watching himself drive. As soon as his eyes found Charity sitting in the passenger seat his memory kicked in, instantly reminding him of the scene he was watching unfold. "You never told me that American Travel Stops were so wonderful!" Charity said in an ecstatic voice as he looked at the many souvenirs in her hands. She quickly put them into a plastic bag and took a sip from her drink, pausing to turn the knob on the radio. The sounds of music filled the compartment of the car. "For sure, these places are great. You can get anything there." Killean replied as he made a left hand turn to get back onto the Interstate. He merged with traffic as Charity went digging in the bag of goodies she had picked up. "Look at this, I enjoy these little trinkets." She held up a small keychain that had a spinning coin mounted inside of another ring. Written on it was ‘Wisconsin’, which was where they were. Charity had never really been on a road trip in the states. Growing up, she only knew the experience of taking a plane where ever she needed to go. She was always spoiled by her parents. "I’m really glad that you decided to come along Charity. It’s gonna be fun having you around PRIME with me." He recalled this event to the day. He was on his way to ReVolution, the first ReVolution he attended after Tyler Nelson contacted him to return to the federation. His main objective was to remove the Universal Title from around the waist of Clyde Walkins. "Hey, if all the road trips are like this, you won’t be able to get me out of the car. I’m sure the fast food will eventually prevent me from fitting in the car though." She shared a hearty laugh with him and then she grabbed a hold of his right hand. They looked at each other briefly and then Killean returned his sight to the road. Charity put the small keychain back in the plastic bag. She then grabbed a French fry from the tray sitting on top of the middle armrest and fed it to him. She smiled and took another sip from her drink, getting comfortable in the seat and staring at the road ahead along with Killean. It was the first of many road trips they had taken together. When they were on the road, it seemed as if all the stresses of life were lifted off their shoulders. It felt like they left all their problems at home. These trips were always a time for them to bond and a time for them to develop their already deep relationship. It was something that Killean enjoyed very much and Charity was always very excited about hitting the road to drive before and after the many PRIME events they attended together. ~+~ For the third time, Killean found himself back on his feet, staring at the three doors. The light behind the first one had disappeared and the second was fading slowly. Instead of looking through the small keyhole, Killean just wanted to go ahead and open the third door. But as he walked towards it, it opened on its own. The light from behind it faded and out stepped Charity, just the way that Killean remembered her. She was wearing a cream colored top that perfectly accented her raven locks of hair that just passed her shoulders. Her makeup was perfect although she didn’t need much of it. Her short jean skirt swayed from side to side as she came to stop standing in front of Killean. "Hiya baby." She said sweetly as her bright smile seemed to give the uninviting confines of the room a warmer feel. Instead of being dragged into another vortex, Killean was mobile as he reached out for Charity. Her hands locked with his tightly. "Care to sit and talk?" "Where?" Killean said as he looked around and saw nothing but the four cold walls and the three doors. "There…" She let go of one of his hands and pointed behind him. As he turned to look, he could see a stone slab that resembled a bench and a fountain that was flowing freely. A light shone down on the scene as it changed the complexion of the scenery around him. Charity smiled at him sweetly as they made their way over to the bench hand in hand. They both sat down and stared at the fountain. Killean was silently wondering if everything was still a dream when a small gecko appeared from the fountain and stood on the edge. The creature smiled and waved at him before diving back into the water. Yep… still dreaming. "Killean, how’ve you been hun?" She asked, her voice showing deep concern and emotion. This was the Charity that he knew and loved. He tightened his grip on her hand and turned to face her. God she was beautiful. He knew the instant that he looked at her that he had never lost that powerful love. He felt warm and complete just being with her. "I’ve been a wreck Charity. I have been trying to come to terms with all the problems that we had. I just don’t know where to begin. You always held me strong if we had problems and now I don’t have that… that… cushion to fall back on." Killean was stumbling with the last sentence, his emotions overcoming him as he let a single tear fall from his right eye. "I know and I’m sorry that I left the way I did. Actually, I’m sorry that I didn’t come back when I was supposed to. I guess my parents are pretty worried aren’t they?" She looked down at her feet as she tapped the soles of her shoes together. "But I just thought we should discuss all of this because I don’t think you understand exactly why all of this happened." "I have an idea as to what happened." He replied quietly, looking at the flowing water of the fountain overflowing from the top level and dancing down to the bottom. "Do you really?" Charity’s tone was firm, yet it still carried that same emotion she always showed. Killean looked down into the pool of water and the image of him and Matt fighting was seen. His PRIME feud with Matt W. had lasted a long time and the animosity between the two was very high. Killean took the hint right away. "I know… I was so obsessed with beating Matt that I tended to neglect you." He spoke as he watched the constant images of fights with Tchu. "That’s only part of it though darling." She reached up to brush his long hair away from his face and behind his ear. She placed her hand on his cheek and turned him towards her once again. "You changed. You were a savage in the ring, but you always were. You always knew how to keep your personal life separate from your life inside the ring. But when all of this took place, the two lives mixed and you eventually brought the uncaring, beastly Killean home with you. You were different with me and with Desiree. Do you remember that night at Colossus? I had brought Dizzy in to see you just before your match…" The fountain once again started to project an image and Killean looked down into its depths. He saw the personal dressing room that he had inside his private box. He saw Charity standing there, holding his daughter Desiree. He bent down to kiss her and the scene faded out with the ripple of the water falling from above. "That was the first sign of the old Killean but it was short lived. I could see it in your eyes, the greed, the power and the desire to take Matt to hell and back. It had completely replaced the desire that you showed for me. Essentially, Matt came between us but it wasn’t his fault. You let it overcome you and you were lost." Charity explained as she rested her hand softly on his shoulder. This was nothing like the fights that they had experienced together. Even though it was all his fault, she sounded forgiving, almost wanting of his love again. His soul melted just hearing her speak so lovingly. "I was blinded by my rage. I… fucked up." Blunt as can be, he admitted his defeat to his feelings but Charity held him tight. "You may have messed up, but something you have always done is learned from what you’ve done wrong. I have always admired that about you dear. You always correct your mistakes. This is another one of those mistakes." She paused and planted a kiss on his cheek. He brought his hand to it after she pulled away and he swore he could still feel the warmth of her breath. "I had always wondered when you would come to your senses and return to the loving partner and father I had known you to be. But sadly that day never came…" His head fell in embarrassment. He looked to the cold floor and kicked a small pebble around with his foot. What could he say in retaliation? Sorry? Yeah! He could do that! "Charity…" He looked up at her and managed to capture her feelings just from looking into her beautiful eyes. "I’m sorry, I know I messed up and I drove you away. I have felt empty and half-dead without you. I NEED you." Her face lit up in delight as he spoke and it felt like the love was back. "Oh Killean, I know you’re sorry. I always knew you would be. It’s just sad the way that everything had to go down." She began to change right before his eyes. The warm inviting smile faded into a frown, the same frown she wore when she came back to the loft for her belongings. Her clothing changed into the same clothes that she wore that day. The room grew cold and he once again felt like he was alone. "I needed to do what I did…" She spoke with such power that she no longer had that inviting warmth to her. "But… you can change it all back." "HOW?!" Killean tried to move closer as he questioned her, but it’s like an invisible wall was stopping him. She moved a little further away. "Tell me please! What can I do?" "I need your help now Killean." She spoke as she seemed to move away more with each passing second. "WHAT CAN I DO?!" He got up off the bench and tried to move after her but he couldn’t move his legs. He reached out for her but by that time she had moved far from his grasp. "Help me…" She pleaded as she grasped the gold necklace around her neck. "You can do anything when you put your mind to it." "I… I can’t…" He sounded weak once again as she was almost gone. "You can… don’t let me down my love…" She reached out with her free hand as she faded away. He reached out further, trying to prevent her from disappearing but his attempts were futile. The light faded from above the fountain and the water stopped flowing. A moment after, the flow continued. A bloody, crimson color began to saturate the pool below. He recoiled in fear of the gruesome sight. Turning his head to move away, the powerful stance of Seymour Almasy filled his vision. Already emotionally defeated, he backed away from The Final Fantasy slowly wanting to avoid total annihilation. Almasy stalked him, removing his sword from the sheath at his side. Finally, Killean could retreat no more as he found his back against the cold cement wall. Almasy halted his approach, standing about ten short feet away. He then lifted the sword high above his head and cried out in a bone chilling scream. He charged forward, ready to bring the sharp blade down and cut The Supreme Machine in half. Just before he struck, the entire scene froze. The wall opened up behind him and a light shone through. Almasy was not moving, his mouth still wide open in is war cry and the sword about to make its way down upon his opponent. Just then, a deep gravelly voice cried out and echoed in the room. "BACK TO YOUR EXILE! Below him, Killean heard the chilling rattle of steel chains. The ankle bracelet that he had worn appeared from the light behind him and he felt it lock to his leg tightly, making him wince in pain. The chains that were attached to it began to quickly retract and once they became taught his leg was pulled out from under him as he was swallowed up into the light. The last image he saw was that of Seymour completing his war cry as the sword came crashing down making a sickening sound as it struck the concrete where Killean was standing one short second before. ~+~ Killean squirmed around under the covers of the bed. Suddenly his eyes shot open and he quickly sat up in a blood curdling scream as he reached for his ankle and began to tear at the bracelet that he wore. There was no give to the device and he continued his cry. As his voice faded, he began to take in his surroundings. The clock radio had gone off and was pumping music into the room. If you just walked away What could I really say? Would it matter anyway? Would it change how you feel? I am the mess you chose The closet you cannot close, The devil in you I suppose 'Cause the wounds never heal. But everything changes If I could turn back the years If you could learn to forgive me Then I could learn to feel The beautiful tones of "Everything Changes" by Staind flowed through the air. The words all striking a chord with The Supreme Machine, he quickly jumped from bed and shut the radio off. Only one thing was repeating in his now active mind. "She needs me!" ~o~ He was standing on the balcony getting some fresh air and looking down into the streets below. In one hand, he held the cordless phone. It had been in his hand for over two hours as he continuously debated calling Mary Ward. He knew he had to call, but he was worried what would really happen once he got her on the line. In his other hand was a lit cigarette. He brought it up to his mouth, his hand shaking as he took a long drag from it. He hadn’t smoked in well over five years. The last time he inhaled the deadly toxic smoke was well before he started wrestling. He was outside in the cold not to get fresh air but to hide the disgusting habit from his loft. Surely the first person to step inside would be able to tell if someone was smoking. He looked down at the cordless phone again and turned it on. He was about to dial the first of a series of numbers that would connect him with Mary but he quickly turned the phone off again. He squeezed it tight in his hand as he took another drag from the cigarette. He exhaled and stubbed out the tobacco on the outside of the black railing before flicking the remains down to the street below. He walked back into his loft and closed the balcony door behind him. Removing his coat, he had to put the phone down but once he had his arm free, his hand closed around the plastic piece of technology again. He actually felt sick after he had that smoke. Perhaps it wasn’t a good idea to smoke after all this time. Now he remembered why he quit. He sat down on a stool by the island counter and looked at the face of the phone. He continued to fight with himself. Should he call or shouldn’t he? He turned the phone on and dialed the numbers fast to avoid a quick change of mind like before on the balcony. Once it began to ring, there was no going back. Of course, there was always the chance that no one would answer. However, that thought was erased once he heard the click of someone answering. "Hello?" "Hi Mary…" Killean said after being greeted with an enthusiastic voice. He wondered silently if she had missed the number on the call display or that she was really happy to hear from him. Good thing he wasn't a betting man. "How are you, Killean?" Her tone gave away nothing. Instead of an answer, Killean just had more questions. "As good as I can be I guess. I mean…" He paused, trying to find the right words as their last actual conversation together had been in the ring just before Colossus. It wasn't the most pleasant of experiences for either individual. "… I dunno if you've been watching ReVolution." "I try not to." There was a sigh before she continued, "But Matthew has told me about your… situation. I'm sorry, Killean. For whatever it might be worth, Matthew thinks you're innocent." With the raise of an eyebrow, Killean was speechless. Matt had said he was innocent? That didn’t sound like something Matt would say. But Mary wouldn’t lie. "Thank you for that. I could always use support in that department. But there is also this thing going with Charity." Killean had distress in his voice. "Thing? Matthew hasn't said anything about you and Charity? What's going on?" "She left and joined a cult." Killean was straightforward as can be, trying not to sugar coat it. There really was no sugar coating something so serious. "I know about the discussion that you guys had just after Colossus. She was here to pick up some of her stuff. She had changed and she said that she… h-hated me." It was hard for him to get out and he was obviously rattled trying to recall the intense situation that went down. "She didn’t happen to say anything…?" "What we talked about was pretty simple, Killean." The exhuasted nature of her voice had given way to a much firmer tone. "Sometimes, it’s not easy being a wrestler's wife. She was angry about how personal things had gotten between you and Matthew. She was tired of being 'that other thing' in your life and she didn't want to become just a pawn in the battle of bad blood." There was a split second to let that all sink in… a pause out of habit… certainly not enough to allow a man to make peace with any of it. Then, she continued. "When you think about it Killean, you set her up to call me out and get me involved all to rattle Matthew. Charity was just a weapon in the war, lumped right in there with steel chairs and barbwire and baseball bats." Well that was a shot to the foot. The scary thing was that when Mary was telling Killean all of this, he was flashing back in his head to the days and weeks prior to Colossus and he was able to pick out perfect examples. The worst part? He didn’t give Charity a chance to even say anything as he was buried up to his eyeballs in repaying what seemed to be an eternal debt to Matt W. "I can say I’m sorry… But I’m afraid that just wouldn’t be enough to make up for what’s been done." "I'm not the one you need to be apologizing to." Mary was right for the most part. His actions were directed at the person he loved and wanted to spend the rest of his life with. But he wanted to ensure that Mary knew he was sorry too. "I know, but I also tried to drag you in just to get under Matt’s skin. I had no right doing that and I’m sorry about that." Killean sounded sincere. It was the least he could do after the months of lies and deceit. "This may sound bad… but I didn’t just call to say I was sorry. I called to ask for a favor Mary." "Ok. Ask." That was too simple, but Killean didn’t want to look a gift horse in the mouth. He knew that Mary was his only chance. It did catch him a little off guard though. "Uhhh… okay… I found out that the cult was going to the small town of Fountain City, Indiana and well…" God he just wanted to spit it out but the heartache was stopping him. He was asking for a favor from someone that he used to be friends with. "I found out that it’s not far away from you. The police won’t do shit and I’m at the end of my rope Mary. I don’t know what to do… I have no one else…" "That's a pretty big favor, Killean. Matthew's just gonna love hearin’ this one." "No, you can’t tell Ma…" "He's my husband. I love him. I can't go behind his back with this." Her voice trailed off, and in the background, Killean could barely make out an echoing voice that seemed to say who's on the phone? To assure him he wasn't losing his mind, Mary resumed her answer. "Especially when he just walked in the room. I've got to go, Killean" Now knowing Matt was standing just feet, maybe just inches from her… the dropping of his name assured he wouldn't get his wish to keep Matt lost in the dark. "I'll get back to you soon. Take care of yourself." Mary then hung up the phone, ending the conversation. But Killean didn’t get the point at first. "Mary! Ma…" He then heard a dial tone. He turned off the cordless and put it on the counter and buried his head in his hands. She was going to get Matt involved. Killean knew that Matt would never support him in this; much less let Mary get involved. It was his only shot, but the chances of this working out were slim and none. And slim just left town.
