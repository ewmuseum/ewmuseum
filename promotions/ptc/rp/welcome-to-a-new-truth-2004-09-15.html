Welcome to a New Truth

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 Narrator: It becomes my dubious honor on this occasion to introduce a whole new world of people to the real champion of champions. Welcome to the world of Drayton MacPhereson. I’ve heard of the big names in this tournament. I’ve seen a lot of their work in and out of the wrestling ring. And one by one, as you go up and down the list of names… I’m not sure you can truthfully find any bigger than his name. A two time and current champion in the Chaos Heat Zone. The creator and inspirer to The Triumvirate. And of course, the owner of the Revolution Wrestling Network. He’s a man of many businesses. He’s a man with so much on his plate. But his quest for ultimate success takes him here on this day. Starting now you have to look at this man as different from the rest. That’s the best advice I can start you with. He’s not the average person and no where near being the normal wrestler of any kind. He has redefined what it is to be a champion. He has changed the balance of power wherever he goes. He has taken anything that he has wanted and made it his own. He wanted to be the champion of the CHZ. So he won it. He wanted to take the CHZ to the top of the wrestling world. So he created The Triumvirate and he took the company to the mountaintop. He wanted to own RWN and to take it to another level. So he bought it. Task after task that he has set before himself, as hard as they may have been, he has completed. The GTT4 Tournament is another task. People can question his motives. They can wonder why he has decided to invest so much time towards a tournament that his own company has nothing to do with. They can even be upset with him for focusing his time in the PTC when he knows there is so much more he has in store to take RWN further. But some times in a man’s life he has to set aside his followers so that he can accomplish his own journey. Drayton MacPhereson has conquered many worlds… and these days he finds himself in a universe where he has limited areas left to challenge. This tournament has shown itself to him as a bright light… a new place… a new challenge. From the watchtower he has been called here… and so be it… he must once again take his place atop the wrestling world. (The scene opens from up above, looking down over the Sydney Opera House and the rest of the long stretch of water that surrounds the area. As the Chaos Heat Zone tours the world, the new champion finds himself in the city of Sydney, Australia relaxing in one of its top hotels, The Quay West Suites. As the camera pans back showing the window of the hotel room, it slowly swings around showing off the rest of the cities skyline and then portions of the room. Drayton sits at a table in an old wooden chair, hunched over the desk going through some paperwork and flipping through various papers. With a quick grace of his signature to one of the documents he briskly pushes the papers back into a folder and diverts his attention towards the camera. With a tip of his head and the rolling of his eyes, he directs the cameraman towards where he wants to sit.) Narrator: This is Drayton. A busy man. But in the end… he will push everything away from you, the people. The less you know… the better. The more you see him just as a wrestler… the more successful he is in his attempts. He feels the camera. And at that instant… the scene will change and his attention becomes clear. Because the key to this man is that he has found away to hide everything about himself except for what you need to know. Weaknesses become exposed as you lay everything out. So he doesn’t lay anything out at all. It’s quite a simple strategy. But an effective one at that. You look at this man and you see a perfect world. The hotel. The beautiful imagery. The power of his signature to that paper. The nice clothes. It’s a package. This is his life… or at least the life you know. That’s the missing piece to Drayton MacPhereson that really enrages people. You get what you are given and that mystery surrounding the rest of him leads people to a forced anger. People hate not knowing. People hate being controlled and led on. And with the way he goes about his business… he does not allow for himself to be controlled. Exasperation. You may not feel it now. But as you delve deeper into his world and as you try to peal him apart… that frustration and that anger will hit you. Because the farther and farther you get into it with him the more and more you give and the more and more he takes without giving back. And this pattern of continuing control leads to greater and greater hatred for him. It’s truly the highest honor you can give a man. For you to look him in the eye and to say you hate him… is the greatest thing you could ever say. It’s in your rage that he finds his greatest successes. Resentment is the greatest respect that can be given to a man like this. (The hotel room remains at peace as Drayton is now sitting on one of the couches in front of the large picture window overlooking the water. He sits in a relaxed position on the couch, wearing a pair of khaki pants and a white buttoned up dress shirt. The CHZ World title rests gently over his right shoulder in its rightful place. It’s all so simple… as every scene with him his. So with the setting to his liking he slowly begins to speak in an eloquent and slow manner taking his time with the words and doing his best to drive home each individual word and line.) Drayton: Am I the perfect man? No. But I begin to look at my life in these days and I start to realize that by the day I find myself getting that much closer to perfection. This isn’t ego. This isn’t me just trying to pump myself up. This is truth and theory and my thought process exposed for you. I keep myself to a schedule but in that there is time to reflect and there is time to look over what I have created for myself in this time of great achievement. I’ve done so much. I’ve gone so far. I’ve worked so hard… and the awards now are starting to make it all worth the while. With this title over my shoulder and with that company trying to find itself once again I know that my life is a worthy one and my life is a successful one. I am happy with who I am and what I am and why I am above all. Where as so many people in my life try and take that away from me… I accept how I have come to this point of greatness. They can say that I didn’t earn it all. They can say that I was given too many opportunities. They can say I was aided all along the process. Say what they say… I know the truth. Because in this business, above all, it really comes down to what a man can do in the ring. It’s not how hard you train or study or try or even how well you speak. What it really comes down to is what you do that once a week in that ring as you go face to face with another man who wants it all just as bad. Once a week that ring calls us all and forces us to not only face another man but to face ourselves. But still… what I have done in the ring becomes washed away in other’s minds because their own insecurities force them to try and take away my successes. By trying to tear me down so many have tried to build themselves up. And with my position and my power and my prestige I walk with a large target surrounding myself constantly. Luckily I came to realize this all at a young age. Luckily I grew up in this business knowing that other people and other thoughts not of my own mind do not matter. Any person that questions me… is jealous of me. They are jealous of how I grew up. They are jealous of the ease in which I transitioned into this sport. They are jealous of the manner in which I took power in situations like the CHZ and RWN. They are jealous because I have it all and they don’t. I’m not trying to say that I’m a better man than you because I have a title or more money or anything of that nature. I say in all seriousness that I’m a better man because I understand the truths to life and I have adapted those to fit my life. It’s not only that I know it. But I start to realize now that you know it, too. You know it but you have trouble expressing it. You know it but you can’t admit it. So you cover it up and you hate me and you loathe me because I’ve been given a life you can only dream about. You want the accolades. You want the money. You want the fame and most of all you want the title. But those are things you are a long way from. Hate me for having the things you want. That’s fine. I just see it as another one of your compliments. My success in this business is so controversial because a lot of people say I don’t belong here. A lot of people disdain the fact that I grew up in a privileged family and that I was so successful in all of my other lives before coming to the wrestling world. Some call it a silver spoon syndrome… and that’s fine. But in this business… I think that gives me an edge like no other person. Because for all of those wrestlers that experienced hard times and struggle… they can adjust that much easier to this tumultuous business. People like me shouldn’t succeed here. People that grew up like me would quit when faced with the slightest amount of pressure. People that came up like me would run away at the first sign of adversity. But those people… that are supposed to be so much like me… aren’t me. I beat my own burdens and I shaped myself into what I am today. (Suddenly a ringing phone can be heard. Drayton casually goes into his pocket and pulls out his cell phone, flipping it up and answering the call. His eyes shift to the right as he stares out into the distance, concentrating on the conversation.) Drayton(on the phone): Yeah, yeah, yeah. Get that done. That’s first priority right now. (There is a mumble heard coming from out of the phone as Drayton listens on intently and shakes his head with a serious look across his face.) Drayton(on the phone): As long as I’m over here… finish up that business and move on. This trip has screwed up a lot of our planning and timing… but you can handle this. You have before. I have total faith in you. (As the man on the other side talks again, Drayton nods his head in approval.) Drayton(on the phone): Get in contact with those guys. Get the matches made. Get it finished… and I’ll handle the marketing of it all. Then we go back to square one. And we build this back up to what it should be. (He listens in one last time and eyes begin to open up a bit more.) Drayton: Okay. Sounds good. Get that done and get back to me as soon as possible. I’ll be busy tonight at the Thunder Dome but after that I’m just traveling. You know where to get me. Later, man. (With that, he closes his phone and subtly sticks it into his back pocket. He turns his head back to the camera and with no delay returns to his words like there was no interruption.) Drayton: There’s always something that needs to be done. A promo here… a show there… matches that need to be booked for RWN. It never really ends. But I can’t complain. Because these are the things that I need in my life in order to be complete. It’s these business transactions and these matches that really make me different from any other person in this business. No one else owns a company like I do and then also stands alone as the champion of another place. People see all that I do… and they can’t stand how easy success can come to a man like me. If you want to call this bragging then that’s fine. But in the end you will realize at some point in your life that I truly am something bigger than you’ve ever known before. The longer it takes you to realize that… the better for me. If there is anything left you think you need to know about me… it all sums up very easily. I don’t look out amongst the world and desire anything else but what I already have. I don’t need to be anyone other than what I really am. All of the riches and honors and titles that are mine now… are all that I need. But I know there are others who look at my possessions and my lifestyle and want what I have. It’s those… the needy ones… who hate me the most. A hate bred from desire. Desire bred from jealousy. Jealousy created from self-loathing. I accept your exasperation. I accept it and grow stronger from it. I grow stronger… and I never… look… back. (Drayton finishes off the words in a slow and plotting manner… slowly developing a smile out of the corner of his mouth… as the scene slowly… fades… out.) Narrator: Choose to believe what you want of this. That’s your decision of when you accept your own reality. We can take you only so far until your own mind blocks you from the truth. Trust us that we’ve seen this before. We’ve seen people that just live off in their own world and don’t want to accept the fact that sometimes in life you just have to face people who are clearly better than you. You can talk and believe that you’re something… but you look to that other man and you see that he just has something that makes him so different from the rest. Look back over what he has said. Rewind, play back, and do it over again. Because the words of this man, Drayton MacPhereson, are words that will lead men to greatness on the day they choose to listen. He knows the way. Don’t hate him for it. Respect him for it and follow him along the way. Your annoyance can only take you so far. (And with those final words… it officially fades… to… black.)
