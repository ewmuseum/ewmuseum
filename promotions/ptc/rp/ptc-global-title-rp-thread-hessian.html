GLOBAL TITLE RP THREAD

8 June 2004 Global Domination: Part I - Gold Poisoning “Egotistical shit. He needs to get what’s coming to him.” His hands clasped, he exhaled and sneered through the faceless crowd before him at the giant readying for the coming storm. Thunder shook with each click of the camera; lightning bombarded him with the flash of the bulb. He would have to brave this as he made his proclamation to be heard around the land. “What the fuck does he know? So concerned with this foes he fails to see his greatest nemesis is the one staring right back at him in the mirror…” Words escaped his throat in a whisper, choked by the intense atmosphere enveloping him and heard only by he. Were he in any other situation those around him would have acknowledged him, granted a moment of his time. Today and now he was lost amongst them, another face in the crowd while the champion lavished in the glory and attention. Who spoke with such ill will towards Hessian? What manner of creature would bestow such negative waves upon the great PTC Global Champion? Digital Mortality had made little noise as a contender…and the exclusion of Fark or any of its derivatives from this individual’s quiet ponderings were proof Saij was elsewhere, no doubt taking out life insurance for the bout that lay ahead of him. Alas, the world was poised ready to record the words of the PTC Global Champion, not unlike a by-gone era when the immortal Muhammad Ali uttered those historic words, “I’m the king of the world, I am the greatest…”. The time was fast approaching when any promises or threats Hessian made were to be proven in the ring. Hessian kept his word when he defeated Jesse Jamester for the title, now he would have to do it once again in the face of adversity that was Saij and Digital Mortality. But what of this individual? Cloaked in shadow and gazing at Hessian through the crowd, his clasped hands escaped the confines of his sleeves. The purple tint of his skin was a clear-cut indication that this was not any foe Hessian knew of…this was perhaps his closest friend, the one that stood by his side as he ascended to the top ranks of wrestling. This was Minion the Imp. Winning the Global Title was a re-awakening for Hessian. Prior to the match he was faced with challenges, a simple undertaking for any normal human…but for the 7’2” Mercenary of Satan this was a vicious blow capable of disabling him. The notion of humanity was a dark spectre looming over him for a long time. He was Hessian, the darkest evil and a punishment his enemies had to face for their sins. In an attempt to overthrow the ultimate evil in Clyde, Hessian shed part of his being in the form of his mask…a major factor in his intimidation, now re-invented in the cold steel adorning his face today. As if that wasn’t enough, one of the toughest decisions Hessian ever made was the inclusion of his own name into his career. As Angelus Von Kelsig he feared that he would no longer be the insane killer the world had come to know and hate. What he didn’t foresee was the psychological edge he would gain over his opponents. As Hessian, he was seen as an inhumane monster…but as Angelus Von Kelsig he was even more…the brutal slayings and vicious attitude were not of a supernatural devil…they were of man. The challenges he faced before the Global Title match were a ghoulish reminder that he was all-to human. Hessian was confronted by debt, something he had never contemplated before it reared its ugly head before him in the form of Jim Carlisle. While money wasn’t enough, the skeletons in his closet were also thrown out for the world to see. He was more aware of his physical limitations, even after such hellish encounters as the Weapon of Choice match in Rush Pro Wrestling where he finally rid the world of the demon known as Clyde. He had overcome every obstacle in his path even at his most vulnerable. This alone was the catalyst that sparked the change in Hessian as he grabbed the Global Title from above and roared proudly as the new Champion. As though he had reached up into the heavens themselves and stolen from God himself, this was the final nail in the coffin, a goodbye and fuck you to his past. Now he had assimilated a cocky attitude and attained a more laid-back approach to life. Dormant now was the die-hard attitude of the killer Satanist as Hessian favoured greed and sleaze and all the trimmings accompanying the PTC Global Title. It was this new attitude that brought such hate of Hessian to Minion. However, as much as he despised this bastard with a belt, it was still Hessian. They had been through too much for Minion to just up and leave. Unfortunately in his current state of mind, Hessian did not feel the same. Looking out into the crowd of reporters, Hessian smirked and huffed on his Global Title, wiping a sheen onto its glorious golden surface. To his left sat the RPW representative, the notorious “Angry” Matt Jay, clad in jeans and an RPW t-shirt and reading over various documents before him on the conference desk. Glancing back at his Global Title, Hessian turned to the man on his right, a middle-aged gentleman in a suit with a twinkle in his eye and a winning smile on his face. He was the Global Title Division representative and organizer of the press conference Hessian was the centre of at this very moment. He cleared his throat and pulled the stationary microphone before him a little closer. All three men had one, each hooked to a cheap PA system situated behind them in two corners of the room with one smaller amp before them. This was a wrestling press conference, not Congress…cheaply does it. Even in the Global Title Division. Scattered beside each microphone were tape recorders from each reporter, recording away even now. “Ladies and Gentlemen of the world’s wrestling press, welcome to this, the Primetime Central Global Title Division press conference! In little over a week, the PTC’s finest are going to battle it out for the greatest prize in wrestling today, as currently held by Rush Pro Wrestling’s own Hessian! He will go toe-to-toe with Saij of Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling and Digital Mortality of the Universal Wrestling Federation in a triple threat match in Hessian’s first defence!” A chill ran up Hessian’s spine as the words “first defence” echoed in his mind. A lot of pressure was mounting and he had to deliver this time to establish himself as a true champion. Too many men had failed and as a result the Global Title was losing credibility. In his mind he knew he had to triumph, for he was touted as the new hope to be a great champion, a better champion than those preceding him. His throat dried up and his palms sweated. A bottle of water purchased earlier stood before him on the table, little droplets of condensation sliding down the plastic and almost beckoning him to sup of its refreshing content. He grabbed hold of the bottle and took a swig, more to clear his throat than quench a thirst. The last thing he needed was to look predisposed or frustrated in the face on an important question posed by the world’s leading wrestling magazines and Internet sites. The flashbulbs continued going off as the Global Title Division representative cleared his throat to go on. “We should be joined shortly by the two other participants in this match and their federation representatives, and then we can get this thing underway. For now though let’s-…” It was then that he was interrupted. By “Angry” Matt Jay. Any other interruption would involve a correction or perhaps an idea or opinion…but this was “Angry” Matt Jay. Interruption. Fucking. Meant. Interruption. “They ain’t coming…” he boomed into the microphone. The GTD representative’s face was filled with immediate shock. Cupping a hand over his microphone he leaned across to Matt Jay and questioned him in a very serious, monotonous tone. “…What…do you mean…they’re not coming?” he began. Before “Angry” Matt could reply, the rep continued on. “…These men…are Global Title Contenders…this press conference isn’t just about Hessian-…” “…Oh, it is now.” Matt Jay chimed in, and Hessian couldn’t help but smirk as his representative let the bombshell drop. “See…some information might have found its way to Saij and Digital Mortality. Possibly concerning the fact that this press conference was cancelled on account of fumigation of the facility. Sounds like a lie doesn’t it?” Matt Jay pointed at the GTD rep, nodding and winking sinisterly. “They didn’t seem to think so. Let’s keep this about the only fuckin’ federation and the only fuckin’ wrestler that matter, yeah?” Hessian felt more at ease now. Jostling for mic time between the “Creativity of an 8yr old trapped inside the body of a 22yr old XUWer” that was Saij and Digital Mortality would have been stressful enough, at least now he had the floor to himself and had the freedom to say whatever about whoever he wanted. Sitting back in his own chair the GTD representative smiled politely and spoke into the microphone. “Ok folks scratch that. Due to complications we will not be joined by Saij and Digital Mortality, rather we will proceed as scheduled with the Global Champion who I’m sure you can’t wait to question. So let us begin…with………you.” He pointed at a portly man with a goatee and spectacles in the front row. The reporter stood poised with pen in hand, gazing at Hessian and snorting before he spoke. “Norman Whiley, The Finisher Monthly. Hessian it was only this past Sunday at RPW Adrenaline that you sustained a broken nose courtesy of Violence Jack. How do you see this affecting your chances in the next defence?” Hessian scoffed as the attention was drawn to the bruising on and around his nose and the small bandage adorning it. Luckily after sustaining the injury at the hands of Violence Jack and his deadly top rope double arm tornado DDT, the nose was reset and bandaged. “It won’t affect my chances. The injury is minimal and come the defence on the 19th I’ll be ready to defend my belt without any worries. I doubt that’ll stop all the morons I’m facing pointing it out and screaming my downfall at the tops of their lungs. Point is in this business we all get hurt sometimes, this is the second injury I’ve received in around three years of wrestling, the first being a fractured forearm at the hands of Dave Gibson at the first OSW Ascension PPV. That barely affected my in-ring performance and this one will be no different.” Hessian felt a flutter inside. He knew that just then he had told a blatant lie, that in fact the fractured arm had sidelined him for a few weeks, however the number of injuries he had sustained over the years were accurate. Whiley noted the reply down while more hands shot up in the air and the GTD representative chose. “Chris Goldstein, No Holds Barred Magazine. Hessian, how are you finding life as the new PTC Global Champion? Anything different, new or changing?” Hessian chuckled, pondering for a moment and glancing at “Angry” Matt Jay before answering. “It’s a lot different. Before I only had to worry about one guy I was facing in RPW, maybe a couple more if I gave a damn about a random match-up. Uh…since I won the Global Title I’ve realized just how much the wrestling world has changed. See in the early days a wrestler could win the championship and be hailed as something special, and with each successful retention of the title more and more respect would build for them. Nowadays, and in instances such as my winning the Global Title, the second that thing was wrapped around my waist I had every goddamn moron that was taught how to apply an armbar chasing after me and trying to get a piece of me. I have no problem with jumping right back into the ring and defending this title, the only problem is with desperate guys that neglect the facts and fail to realize you’re the best in the business and swear blind they can take you down with little to no proof of their ability. That’s the business for you; everyone wants to be Number 1, even if they’re barely scraping the top 100.” He took another guzzle of water; looking satisfied with his answer as the reporter from NHB magazine jotted down his response. In the back of his mind however he was restraining thoughts of how he had changed as a man. Once already he had battled with personal demons, and once he had overcome them. Were they to re-emerge every time someone asked a question or he had to defend his title? Thankfully he could mask the fear and give the people what they wanted at the same time. Thankfully he could keep his mind at rest, let lie the beast inside trying to reclaim his humanity in the hopes of destroying it and allowing him to become the Mercenary of Satan he once was. “Since winning the Global Title you appear to have undergone a change of character, favouring greed and money-grubbing to simply destroying men and preaching evil as you did prior to your defence. Why is this?” Or maybe not… “Well…” Hessian began, trying to stall without looking perturbed by the question, posed by a lanky woman with buckteeth and sunken eyes. Adorning a cocky grin, Hessian continued. “Quite simply, the money is there. Before I took pleasure in the pain and hurt…but now I get to do that plus make a hefty wage from it. It’s like being back in Old School Wrestling, only the opponents aren’t of the same high-class calibre. Guys like Saij think they have “it”, but they can barely contend. What kind of a champion would he make anyway? Look at him. He’s a joke. People see me with the belt and they see a true champion. I’ve got the strength and endurance of Ivan Stanislav, the mean streak of…well…Meanstreak…and the bling bling of Tyler Nelson. Then look at Saij…what kind of champion would he make? He won one tournament and talks like a kid. What the hell is ‘Fark’? He’s not even half the man I am. As for Digital Mortality…he’s just another Jesse Jamester, only without the title.” He took yet another drink of water, but the lump in his throat remained. While he had dodged the bullet, he realized how fragile the subject was and how easy it would be to approach for others. The atmosphere had an air of tension in it still, and before the woman sat back down Hessian piped up, seeing a few onlookers approaching the door at the back of the hall. “Uhh, actually now that you mention it…just because I’m rolling in moolah these days doesn’t mean I’ve given up on the whole evil thing. You’ve all heard the saying that ‘Money is the root of all evil’?” Hessian smirked and nodded at Matt Jay, a sly grin growing on the RPW representative’s face as Hessian slid a hand inside one of his pockets, fumbling around and removing a fistful of dollars. Getting up out of his seat, the Goliath pointed to the back of the hall at a couple of wrestling fans lingering by the entrance and called to them. “Excuse me for one second.” He began, gesturing to the reporters and whistling at the two fans in their early 20s. “HEY YOU TWO!” He hollered. Surely enough they took notice and pointed at themselves anxiously. Hessian nodded and gestured for them to enter, something the two had no problem obliging to. He was the PTC Global Champion after all; anyone would have to be crazy or timid not to do as he asked. Jogging over to the desk they were so taken aback at seeing Hessian in person that there minds practically ignored the logistics behind his request as he held a hand over the microphone and whispered to them both. As he explained his idea to them, they seemed apprehensive. The reporters held off taking pictures to try and hear what Hessian was saying but to no avail. Stepping back, the two fans seemed to pass up on whatever Hessian was offering or asking. A couple of moments later a few of the reporters and certainly the two representatives could see a substantial amount of dollars passing hands, not before being signed by the Global Champion of course. All of a sudden the two men seemed more enthusiastic, and shaking hands with Hessian took off, exiting the hall and looking around drastically. One of them suddenly pointed to the left at another conference hall, and like greased lightning the two ran off. Smirking cheerily, Hessian clasped his hands and looked into the crowd of reporters. “Any other questions?” At this, more hands shot up in the air. The chosen one came in the form of a man with ruffled hair, glasses and a slightly creased shirt. Anyone looking at him on the street would assume a heavy night on the tiles…but the bronzed 12-sided dice pendant around his neck and the black ‘Dungeons & Dragons’ t-shirt visible beneath his white work shirt pointed to a late night playing D&D. Nudging his glasses further up the bridge of his nose the reporter remained seated, trying to balance a laptop on his lap and waiting poised ready to type. “Ehhhhh…” He began. “Rob Moschker, Ringbuster.com…Hessian in your last defence you fought five other men in a ladder match. Ehhh, this time around you’re facing two other men in a straight up triple threat match…what are your thoughts, do you have a game plan?” Hessian stared at Rob for a moment, shooting a ‘Do I know you?’ glance at him before shaking his head and answering. “I didn’t really have a game plan for the last title match and I don’t plan on one this time around unless I’m shown the need to. With five opponents it was easier to just grab and slam the first thing that moved. With only two opponents this time however…well, it will be a lot easier to watch my back and looking at their stats it doesn’t look like a challenge anyway. But saying that I am the Champion and I do my research like everyone else…doesn’t mean to say I can’t be a cocky son of a bitch as well does it?” Rob Moschker let out a final Ehhh before burying his face back in the monitor of his laptop. As he did so, bloodcurdling intense screams echoed out from the entrance. Everyone turned to the doorway in time to see the two fans that Hessian had been talking to sprinting past and crying at the tops of their voices, in their possession the PTC Extreme Title! Barely a few seconds after they shot past, a blur that could be made out as UWF’s Moraless flew past, skidding to a halt and peeking back into the GTD hall to see Hessian. Pointing at him with a shocked expression on his face, the Extreme Champion muttered something under his breath before taking off after the title thieves once more. Grinning like a 7’2” evil Cheshire cat, Hessian looked into the crowd of reporters. “Whether it’s the buy-out and downfall of an entire corporation or something as simple as paying a coupla kids to bitchslap Moraless and steal his title…money really is the root of all evil.” A hail of flashbulbs bombarded Hessian as he grabbed more cash from his pockets and threw it up in the air looking proudly on as it rained upon him. Chuckling loudly, Hessian posed for the cameras while “Angry” Matt Jay and the GTD representative looked on, satisfied with the conference thus far. As the cameras settled, hands rose into the air once more and another reporter was chosen. “Wesley Grant, Knockout Wrestling Weekly. Hessian, how do you respond to claims that the streaker which ultimately saw the downfall of your opponents and the former champion Jesse Jamester was part of a conspiracy by Rush Pro Wrestling and the Global Title Division figurehead at the time to bring the Global Title and more fame to RPW? Do you see your win as tainted at all?” “Angry” Matt Jay cocked an eyebrow at the ratty reporter, obviously out to stir up some controversy. Nudging Hessian in the side, Matt Jay gestured below the table to keep the calm…only to see a sinister twinkle in Hessian’s eye. Sighing, Matt turned back to the crowd and waited as Hessian made his reply. “My win was far from tainted. Let’s put it this way…why would the great Primetime Central taint its image and reputation by allowing a streaker of all things to ruin a match concernerning the most prestigious title in PTC? I assure you and everyone else that Rush Pro Wrestling merely put my name in the hat for title contenders, aside from that they have had little involvement with the division. Perhaps you should be questioning the fedheads at LOW or the head of security that allowed such a tragedy to occur? There are far more devious plots afoot than a federation simply setting up its representative for a title win. You could put any of the RPW roster in a Global Title match and they would come out victorious.” Hessian paused for a moment, an idea coming to mind and with a slurp of bottled water he continued. “Why don’t you ask Saij about it? He seems to have a vested interest in the Global Title. What’s to say he didn’t pay someone to interrupt the match to allow Blake Wolfson to take the win for his federation so Saij could easily manipulate himself into the title scene quicker than usual? All it takes is a disappointing performance from Wolfson for everything to go down the drain…that it did. As far as any controversy surrounding the Global Title…I was chosen…I delivered…and I am the PTC Global Champion. Let me assure you there is no camera trickery involved here…no illusions of the mind…I really am this good a wrestler. It’s just the way it is and if people don’t like it, put up or shut the hell up.” Eyeballing the reporter with confidence, Hessian shot him a mean glare as he noted down the Goliath’s response before nodding in approval. It was then that Hessian noticed the looks on the faces of most of the reporters…but most importantly of one man seated in amongst them all. The sneering eyes and a mouth contorted into a saddened grimace belonged to Minion the Imp. Before now Hessian had been dismissing Minion on RPW shows, travelling to Adrenaline on his own and leaving Minion to find out at the last minute he wasn’t involved in the show. Not since the Weapon of Choice match had he much involvement with Hessian, but for some reason staring at this little Imp manager caused something to stir within him. “Hey…can we take five…I need a small break…” Hessian asked quietly before the Global Title Division representative could choose another reporter. “What for?” came the reply. “We’ve only had a few questions and you want a break already?” “I just need to clear my head for a minute…this room is too stuffy.” It was a blatant excuse and the GTD representative knew it to some extent as Hessian appeared to be covering for something. He felt the lump in his throat almost choking him, but sighed with relief as the representative stood up and hailed the press before him. “Ok folks we’re going to have a small intermission. The conference will resume in ten-…” “-…Thirty…” Hessian chimed in. “Ugh…thirty minutes, and then we’ll continue, OK?” With that the reporters got up, some disgruntled but others nonchalantly taking their recorders to go and revise the material for their articles or columns or interviews or whatever they had planned for it. Getting up from his seat, Hessian seemed more withdrawn all of a sudden as Minion continued to gaze at him before hopping off of his seat and taking off. The Imp had an important phone call to make. A split-second impulse to follow quickly deteriorated as “Angry” Matt Jay slapped Hessian on his shoulder. “Hey what’s going on, why are we taking a break?” He asked. “I just need some time to think, and I’m the only one here so we might as well take five so these guys don’t get bored.” Matt Jay nodded and walked over to the GTD representative as Hessian took his leave of the conference hall. As he passed the various reporters and fans and other wrestling personalities in the main hall he couldn’t help but feel empty, scanning the area for Minion with no luck. Those that made eye contact with he shot a cocky glance to, showing his belt off still and chuckling, though inside it didn’t feel like he was smiling at all. Entering a small office that would serve as his quarters for the remainder of the conference period, Hessian shut and locked the door…and as he turned around his eyes glazed over. “I-…I cant do this any more…” Uttering those words, Hessian collapsed back against the door, slowly sliding to the floor and crumpling in a heap on the ground. Looking at the Global Title he held in his hands, Hessian growled and with a frustrated roar tossed the belt across the room, ignorant of it as it smacked against a wall and crumpled to the ground as he had done. Holding his head in his hands, Hessian sighed and stared blankly at nothing, trying to hold back the tears as his heart sunk in his chest. …What am I doing? What have I become? This isn’t me………this isn’t Hessian… ---- 10 June 2004 Global Domination: Part II - Change He was the dealer of fate to the immortals, the 7’2” Mercenary of Satan. He was the greatest wrestler in PTC today, the most intimidating man to be faced in the squared circle. He was the PTC Global Champion. …He was Hessian, the man sitting in the corner of the room, hugging his knees and holding back the tears. Hessian thought to himself, trying to concentrate while the four walls seemed to be closing in around him. Today was to be a day he showcased his supremacy over men such as Saij and Digital Mortality, before physically doing so in the ring. On attaining the Global Title something inside him changed. A choking pile of cold, hard cash had extinguished the hellfire that burned inside him. Certainly Hessian wasn’t made of money but it was this that had tainted him, pulling him from the dark recesses into a world where the bright lights shone his name for all to see. The Global Title had brought him straight to the top, and for once the Mercenary of Satan was tempted by the bright lights and fame. Money was the root of all evil, but in Hessian’s case it was not that he bought out companies or indulged in the slave trade, quite the opposite. The evil of money was the fact that it had lured him from his dominating position at the top of the Dark Gimmick hierarchy. After fighting for so long to preserve honour within the bloodline and claim his rightful place at the top, Hessian had thrown it all away…and sold out. For so long he was content with simply wrestling and being his evil old self, even holding the OSW Tag Team Titles and the Hardcore Title he simply used them as a lure to draw in more prey. Yet this Global Title was more than just a lure, it was a trap. Hessian was the last person thought to succumb to such temptation. Yet there he sat, in realization that his actions throughout the past month were those of a sell-out. The money-grubbing, the cockiness, the odd changes in behaviour. It was as though he was possessed by the same demons as the legendary Tyler Nelson, although his greed was on a much lesser extent. Getting up off the floor, Hessian approached the Global Title and picked it up off of the ground, dusting it off and looking it over. The gold shimmered across his face and he felt himself go into a slight daze. Just looking at the title made him feel warm inside. It had consumed him, just as Vampir Nosferatu had done all those years ago, turning him into what he was today. Gazing at the title for a few more seconds, Hessian suddenly gasped and threw it down once more, this time on the office desk before him. “This title is a curse.” Hessian whispered softly to himself. “I’ve given up everything I loved and stood for…and for what? A piece of tin and grief from some fucking peons? This isn’t who I am.” Sure enough, as he won the title it began to change him. He wasn’t the hell raiser he had been with a new belt; Hessian didn’t have the PTC Global Title. The PTC Global Title had him. Even as he dismissed it and fought against its enticement he couldn’t help but glance at it again and again, overcome by its beauty. It was because of this title that he had sat through the press conference. Being in the public eye so prolifically and even answering such brutally bland questions hadn’t entered into his thoughts, all that was important was the Global Title that lay before him for the entire world to see. Now that he was alone and thinking for himself. There were no PR agents around, no managers, no representatives…no one. Just Hessian. He felt worn and dirty, like everything he did was not of his doing or to his satisfaction. He thought back to Adrenaline, the same show in which Violence Jack broke his nose. Shuddering, the images flashed in his mind again of the promos that had aired showing Hessian advertising for a local Red Cross and car dealership. He felt his legs shaking slightly and took a seat at the desk, bowing his head and sighing as he reflected back on what could only have been a bad decision. Hessian stared at the title, sneering at the thought of those adverts, and everything he had done. What came to mind most often was Minion the Imp. Hessian had spied him in the crowd of reporters, yet he hadn’t come to the press conference with Minion. In fact in the past month Minion and he had only ever interacted a few times backstage. His head ached as he thought about all they had been through together. Hessian was projected into the spotlight with Minion by his side, without him he would have very well died with NTICW. One of the most charismatic and easily recognisable managers in wrestling history, and Hessian turned his back on him. Once again the greed was to blame. After his success in Old School Wrestling, the last thing Hessian expected was to be signed to Rush Pro Wrestling. However he made the move from OSW, a global corporation and one of the greatest federations ever to grace the wrestling world to the RPW, a federation that could barely afford to keep its wrestlers never mind splash out on luxuries. Hessian made the transition without a complaint to be heard. That didn’t mean he wasn’t against it. Sure the cream of the PCW, OSW and PWC rosters were there but his decision to sign was partly thanks to one person…Minion the Imp. His manager. Winning the Global Title was like a bad wake-up call. For the first time in many months Hessian held a title and he did it on his own. Minion was barely around to manage him, and perhaps in a moment of extremely poor judgement and cockiness, Hessian made the mistake of severing the link between the two. Minion however couldn’t bear to part with his greatest success and stayed loyal in the hopes Hessian would see sense. Today proved that it might have been too late, as Minion took his leave once more, possibly for the last time…while Hessian only now realized the error of his ways. “I’m going to change this!” Hessian said to himself. “I’ll be the champion I want to be, I’ll do it the way I want to…I will reclaim my lost self…” Easier said than done. Standing up, Hessian glanced across the room at a mirror hanging above a small sink. Staring at himself it was obvious nothing had changed. His attire still screamed evil and intimidating, inside however he felt about as intimidating as Tessa Windsor did before being powerbombed over the top turnbuckle onto the steel steps. Cursing under his breath, Hessian approached the mirror and removed it from the wall, taking a seat and staring at his reflection. His face looked old and worn. He didn’t have to look at it twice before shutting his eyes tightly, feeling the sting as tears tried to form once again. He would not succumb to his own urges, subconscious or otherwise. He would not show that vulnerability. There was only one way to prove himself. He had to find himself again, not spiritually…but mentally. He would have to once again harness the demons that made him the Goliath. Looking to the sink, Hessian’s eyes widened and moving over to it began to empty his pockets of all the money on his person. As the small heap of cash lay before him, Hessian reached into his pocket and pulled out a lighter. Taking one of the notes, the Global Champion lit a flame under the paper, watching as it began to burn. Dropping it in the pile of money Hessian watched as the money began to smoulder and burn, turning to ashes in so little time. Yet as Hessian watched the last of the notes burn into nothing, he felt nothing change inside. His lip quivering, Hessian sighed and ran a hand through the short crop of hair on his head. Looking back to the desk Hessian saw the mirror next to the Global Title, and a marker pen lying in an in-tray next to that. Taking a seat, Hessian grabbed the marker pen and tested the felt tip on the glass. Surely enough it left a black mark, and with that Hessian began to sketch something onto the mirror. His mind was working a mile a minute, and the stress was showing in his eyes. Glancing at the Global Title, the Mercenary of Satan sneered and continued sketching. Finally he capped the pen and tossed it back into the in-tray, examining the fine piece of work etched onto the glass………a pentagram with all the occult trimmings. Scanning the room, Hessian spied his next necessity in the in-tray once more, a safety pin. Producing the needle, the Global Champion Showed the extent of his desperation…pricking his finger and allowing the blood to flow to the surface. Tossing the safety pin aside Hessian squeezed his finger until an adequate drop of blood dripped into the middle of the pentagram.
