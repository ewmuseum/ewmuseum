PTC Tag Team Titles Match Roleplaying

17 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Los Mucho Macos Mascaras in... Train-ing Daze White Tiger, Chief of Staff to the owner of the UWF and one-half of the PTC Tag Team champs Los Mucho Macho Mascaras, is at an old Chicago train station. He stands there in a business suit and lucha mask, looking at a piece of paper, a request for his presence at the station half an hour ago. White Tiger looks at his watch and sighs, seeming quite impatient. “Ah, forget it,” he mutters. He is about to walk away when he hears the sound of a train coming into the station. The whistle blows as White Tiger turns to look at it. It’s an old fashioned engine covered with red, white, and blue streamers. As it comes to a stop near the Tiger, a man peeks his head out from behind the controls. He is wearing an engineer’s cap on top of his rather stupid looking bird mask as well as matching overalls. “Hola, Big Chief White Tiger!” says Pájaro de Presa with a big dumb smile on his face. “What the hell is this?” asks White Tiger. “It’s the Mucho Macho Lucha Choo-cha!” he answers gleefully. White Tiger knows he’s going to regret this, but he asks anyways. “And what is a… choo-cha?” “It’s how you say “choo-choo” in Spanish,” Pájaro explains. “No, it’s not.” “Sure it is,” the Rudo with Attitude-o insists. “I should know. I’m from Mexico.” “So am I,” White Tiger points out. “But enough arguing. What is this thing for?” “So glad you asked, Big Chef White Tiger!” says Pájaro. White Tiger thinks that makes one of them. “As you know, Los Mucha Macho Mascaras defends our PTC Tag Team championship in the first ever Purgatory with a Piñata match!” “Yeah, I know,” says the Tiger. “Against Unholy Matrimony and Soul Purpose.” “You said it wrong,” chides Pájaro. "You need to say it like SOOOOOOUUUUUUULLL PURRRPOSE! Like that disco show “Soul Train.” It’s how I got the idea for the Mucho Macho Lucha Choo-cha.” “So television does rot the brain after all,” White Tiger observes. “What does this have to do with our match?” “This is a big deal!” Pájaro explains. “The first ever Purgatory with a Piñata match! Six kendo sticks! Three teams! Two belts! One piñata!” “Zero chance of anyone leaving with their dignity,” adds the Tiger. “Right!” agrees Pájaro. “So this match needs hype! It needs hoopla! Especially if I’m going to impress the love of my life!” “Ah,” says White Tiger. “Karina Wolfenden.” “Her? That slut?” exclaims Pájaro. “I’m finished with her! Did you see the way she just threw herself at Tsunami Oni?” “I believe she was making a cover,” White Tiger points out. “She was making something!” the mortified Rudo with Attitude-o continues. “And he has a girlfriend! Plus she was in some perverted orgy with six men! And I wasn’t one of them!” “You mean the Seven of the Best battle royal?” asks the Tiger. “I don’t know what those kinky freaks call it!” cries Pájaro. “I just remember seeing all those guys so worn out by that sex-crazed man-eater that they were just laying on the ground! She never treated me like that! I don’t mind my Kare-Bear being a deviant nymphomaniac as long as she throws a little sugar my way! No, Big Chief White Tiger, I have a new love. A pure love. And that love has a name that is like music… Aerith Heartily!” “Wait,” says White Tiger. “You have the hots for one of our opponents again? Not mention one that’s married?” “I think she got divorced from Seymour,” says Pájaro. “But didn’t they get back together?” “They did, but then she betrayed him.” “Yeah, but they patched things up if they’re a tag team now.” “I think now they’re just friends.” “Madre de dios!” exclaims White Tiger. “Married. Divorced. Friends. Love. Hate. Love. He’s got white hair, then it’s blue, then it’s blue and white together, then it’s red. Seymour’s good, now he’s evil, now he’s good, now he’s evil, it’s the necklace’s fault, it’s not the necklace’s fault. Her name is Aerith, now it’s Laura, now it’s Aerith. He’s in one fed, she in another, now he’s there and she’s here, then they’re both nowhere and now they’re both here. You need a road map to keep up with their plot twists!” “But all that doesn’t matter!” Pájaro declares. “Because Seymour Almasy isn’t a Latino man! And everybody knows… once you’ve had Chicano, the rest is just guano!” “Wow, didn’t see that coming,” says the Tiger wryly. “So what’s with all the red, white, and blue streamers on the train?” “As El Presidente of the UWF, it is my duty and honor to be patriotic and display our nation’s colors with pride!” states Pájaro proudly. “But you’re Mexican,” White Tiger points out. “So?” “Mexico’s colors are red, white, and green,” explains the Tiger. “You sure about hat?” asks Pájaro. “How would you know?” “Because I’m Mexican,” says White Tiger like he’s speaking to five-year old. “We established that earlier.” “Well, you don’t have to get snippy,” Pájaro retorts defensively. “Maybe I didn’t do so good in school. Maybe I wasn’t the teacher’s pet like some people.” “Maybe you’re just an idiot.” “Maybe! You just don’t know!” Pájaro de Presa looks heartbroken. Tears well up in his big, brown, stupid eyes .White Tiger lets out a heavy sigh. “Look. I’m sorry,” he apologizes. “The train looks great.” “You mean the Mucho Macho Lucha Choo-cha?” says Pájaro, peking up a little. “Yes,” answers the Tiger. “The… Mucho Macho Lucha… Choo-cha.” “Muy bien!” shouts Pájaro. “Hop on, amigo! We have to spread the word about Purgatory with a Piñata!” White Tiger sighs again and climbs into the train next to his partner. “QUACK!” Startled, White Tiger looks down and sees a duck dressed in an engineer’s hat and overalls just like Pájaro. “Oh. Sorry, Plucky.” “Quack.” “All aboard!” Pájaro calls out. “There’s no one else here,” White Tiger points out. “Oh,” says Pájaro. “Then we’re off to do our job!” “Which is to do THE job,” adds White Tiger. And so the Mucho Macho Lucha Choo-cha heads off from the train station bearing Los Mucho Macho Mascaras as well as Plucky the Cockfighting Duck. The final destination? Purgatory with a Piñata!
