Noah Hanson(WMW) vs Fnord(4WW) vs Kasper Sky(LOW)

16 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Going Shopping …for an extreme match Sometimes we have to live life to the fullest. Like every moment may be the very last day we wake up too. Life is precious to be taken lightly and should be held in the highest regards and when you get a chance to make an impact in life you should do everything in your power to seize the moment and make it everything you can make it your own special moment in time something that future generations can see, look at and decide just how great you were at that moment in time. This moment in time will have Noah Hanson from Wrestling Midwest facing Fnord from 4 Winds Wrestling and Kasper Sky in a Grocery Store match. This match will not be for the faint of health as this will be a brutal and destructive match to say the least and then the fact that just about anything in the store can be used as a weapon makes the match just that much more dangerous. Things from wooden pallets, fresh produce to whatever else they can think of is legal and able to be used in the match. The forklifts on the loading docks could even be used as weapons if the match goes that far back into the store! Either way this is sure to be something for the ages! I could hear the rain pounding on the roof of my house it was almost hypnotic. I sat there listening almost entranced by the calming sound of the pattern of the rain drops. I reached for my remote and turned off the TV and stereo so I could just listen to the sound of the rain. As Blood Factory quickly approaches everything I do is gonna be magnified and this Grocery Store match is one of those things that will magnify things a lot. This is no ordinary garden variety match this is gonna be a brutal match and doing this all within a few days of Burnout is a real dangerous proposition. Doing a match like this means that someone is gonna get hurt and maybe even seriously. I can only hope that I can survive long to come out on top cause my competitors are ones that are outside of my usual stomping grounds of Wrestling Midwest. Fnord is a wrestler that I am familiar with he is a foe of the Extreme Division he is talented there is no doubt about that but with a name like Fnord I wonder about his credibility and those damn goofy goggles that make him look like “Goggles Pizano”. I mean kinda name is Fnord sounds like he outta be Swedish or something! Then there is the other person in this match that goes by the name Kasper Sky. An odd name to say the least kinda sounds like she is a light blue ghost or maybe some kinda weird porn star name! I heard a knock at my door and got up to go and see who was here at this time of night! I opened the door and there was Kristina she was soaking wet. I grabbed her and pulled her inside and closed the door quickly she was shivering as she tried to talk! “What are you doing up so late sweetie?” She muttered as she kicked off her heels and started undressing as she walked towards the bedroom. “You should be trying to get some rest cause you have a big week ahead of you!” She was right I do have a big week! I have a fatal four way in Wrestling Midwest against AWS Man aka Bill, Matrix and Jeff Hardkore, I also have to make an appearance to Global Wrestling to get back something that belongs to me and that is my Ace Superior title that the Irish Blarney Stone Donny McNasty stole from me at High Stakes and then to top it all off I have this Grocery Store match in the Extreme Division. I mean this is really nothing new to me as I have always loved a good challenge and this was nothing different cause we all have to analyze, adapt and overcome every obstacle that is trust in front of us. The only difference is how each person deals with the situation. Not everyone is like me and will try to take it on head first but that is just me. I got up and walked with Kristina to the bedroom and handed her my robe. She smiled as she tied the sash. She turned to me and smiles and she pressed her body against mine. “Your not looking past this match in the Extreme division are you cause that would be a huge mistake?” she asked as she looked into my eyes “No of course not I cannot afford to look past anyone and just because this a match that is not in my home fed does not mean it is not that important to me!” I replied calmly “Look this match is just as important as any other match and maybe more so cause this match will give me a chance to show off against two top stars in a match that will draw attention from a lot of the stars from all over the PTC!” I am actually looking forward to this match as this was always a dream was to do a match like this and now I get a chance to star in one and take out two of the top stars from two great feds 4 Winds Wrestling and Lights Out Wrestling. I have the utmost respect for both of my adversaries but in this match they are simply gonna be bagged just like the rest of the groceries and stuck in the back of the mini van. I wanna make sure that I do not over extend myself with everything that is going on these days cause doing to many things could actually spell disaster for me and I cannot let that happen under any circumstances! “You ok? Kristina asked as she put her arms around me “Yeah just thinking about all the stuff I got going on that’s all!” I replied as I forced a smile to appear like the groundhog coming out to see his shadow “Anything you wanna talk about?” she asked as we both walked to the couch and we sat in each others arms “Well this is gonna be a dangerous match with it being so close to Burnout and everything!” I am kinda scared that this all could be a mistake and a big one at that!” I said with a concerned look on my face “What you’re worried?” she asked with a slight smile on her face thinking I guess that I am joking or something “Well this is a very pressing time for me I mean this is a career defining time in my career this could make or break what happens to me in my quest to become the most dominate force not just in Wrestling Midwest but in PTC! My goal in the long run is to be one of the most successful wrestlers to ever grace the PTC. I wanna be able have my name mentioned alongside names like Seymour Almasy, Tristan Belmont,Chryste, The Souls of the Black Lotus and Mike Marvelous as one of the most dominate wrestlers to ever compete in the Extreme division!” I proclaimed with a huge smile on my face “Well everyone in Wrestling Midwest knows that you are the brightest star in the fed today!” she beamed “You have had a stranglehold on the title since you won it in January and have shown no signs of slowing down and even though they have you in a fatal four way at Burnout I see you as the favorite not AWS man or Matrix and surely not Hardkore but you!’ I had to admit that having her in my corner always behind me and looking out for everything that I do and that is always good cause sometimes I seem to miss things like that and having her around will only be a good thing for in more ways then one. We were meant to be together we are soul mates and she is the love of my life and whether she is at ringside watching me, backstage watching the match or at home where she is safe all I know is that she will be watching me and praying for me to come out of this match alive and well! And not only will I come out healthy, wealthy and wise but I will also come out victorious and that is not being cocky or a smart ass it is just me stating a fact plain and simple! It’s not like anything that I have ever been involved in this match is something totally new here so I need to figure out a good strategy and come up with a way to turn this into my advantage an advantage that I will be able to capitalize on. “Well Kristina get dressed in some warm, dry clothes I am gonna run to the store and find something to cook on the grill!” I added as I grabbed my trench coat and grabbed my keys and headed towards the door “So what are you getting?” she asked as she appeared from the bedroom “I think maybe a couple of steaks and some ribs” I said as I put my coat on and opened the door and walked out “Why don’t ya make us some hot chocolate?” I walked down the hall and pressed the elevator button the doors opened and Jerome the security. I walked into the elevator and looked at Jerome who was wearing a pair of headphones. “What ya up to Mr. Hanson?” Jerome asked as he took off his headphones “Just going out to get some dinner and to do some homework for a match I am involved in!” I replied as he buttoned my coat “I heard ya was in some kinda triple threat match that seemed really weird a Grocery Store Match?” he asked I nodded as I had almost had enough of talking about the match and was eager to get it over with now “Yeah a Grocery Store Match versus Fnord and Kasper Sky it should be interesting but if ya do not mind I would rather not talk about it anymore kinda starting to get old ya know what I mean?” I said with a deep regretful sigh “ I understand completely people always wanna know how you’re going to approach a certain match, what kinda strategy ya might have and all kinds of other crap well I will leave ya be but I wanna say good luck and I am pulling for ya like I always have my friend!” He said with a reassuring pat on the back “Thanx...” I said with another deep sigh as the doors finally opened I looked around and stepped out of the elevator and waved to Jerome as I walked through the parking garage to where my Lotus was parked. I looked at the car and pressed my thumb to the security panel. The doors unlocked and opened and then I stepped inside and put the keys in the ignition and started the engine and revved it hard and put it in reverse and slowly backed up and pulled into drive and sped through the parking garage. I came out to the street and as it has been lately it is again raining. I sit and wait for traffic to pass and turn on my Pioneer stereo and “The Reason” by Hoobostank blares and I pull into the street and shift into second and then third, and as I flew through the rain soaked streets and came to a stop sign, a Mustang GTO pulls up with a custom touch two it pulls up and the rims keep spinning. I looked and saw the rims and kinda shook my head in disappointment as I hate the whole idea of rims that spin while your car is idling. I guess every generation has this type of thing and well whatever. I watched as the car hopped like a jack rabbit from its hydraulics. The kids seemed to be enjoying it but it made my head hurt just watching them. The car sped away and I pressed onward to the store and when I reached a small family type store named McDougles’ on the edge of town I pulled in the parking lot and locked the doors and set the car alarm. I walked to the store and entered I looked around then I heard the campy music playing softly over the P.A. system I believe it was “Copa Kabana” by Barry Manilow but the song sucked ass just like the fact that I have to face Fnord and Kasper Sky. I have heard all the chatter about Fnord and yes he is talented but everyone is beatable just cause you have a whole fed backing you like everyone backs Chris Storm. I am not totally sold on the fact that Fnord is THAT great I still have to see the fact for myself. I am a PERSON THAT BELIEVES IN the OLD ADAGE that seeing is believing and I need to see it firsthand for myself. I walked through the isles and I picked up a mop and looked it over. I flipped it once and twisted and thrust it forward like a samurai sword. Man this would make a great weapon in the match and maybe even the mop bucket. I was looking through the baby section and was wondering if baby food really does taste like what the jar says. I opened a jar of peaches and stuck my finger in it and tasted it. I shrugged and kept eating it as I walked around the store and then placed the empty jar on a shelve. I came to the meat section and looked around as I grabbed about six T-bone steaks and then grabbed a bag of steak fries as I walked past the freezer. Man the things I could use in this match are really unlimited and this is gonna be a load of fun I am really gonna enjoy myself. I came upon the checkout line and went to the rush lane and placed my steaks and steak fries on the counter and the lady scanned everything and then she looked up. “The total is 65.57.” she said in a nasally voice that almost seemed like she was holding her nose closed as she spoke I handed the lady a 100 dollar bill and she handed me my change then bagged it and then handed me my bag. “Thank you and have a nice day!” she said “Thanx I will...” I said as I looked around as I walked out the door “To bad Fnord and Kasper Sky won’t be having any fun!” I walked back to my car and unlocked the car and placed my bag in the passenger seat. I started the car and slowly drove away as I looked at the store. “This is gonna rule…” I said as I drove away *end scene*
