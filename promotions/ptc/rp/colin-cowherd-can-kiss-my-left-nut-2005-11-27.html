Colin Cowherd Can Kiss My Left Nut

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 Radio transcript of The Herd with Colin Cowherd from 11/16/05 Cowherd: Now here’s something you don’t see everyday. I saw this on the front page of the Los Angeles Times sports section. Apparently, a professional wrestler by the name of Brian Arson was taken into custody the other day as police raided his Santa Barbara home. Now, I don’t know anything about Mr. Arson or his career, wrestling or drug trafficking… (Cowherd laughs to himself) Cowherd: But apparently, and I heard this from someone from the office earlier this morning, apparently this Brian Arson guy was rather well known among the professional wrestling circuit. Now once again, going back to a couple weeks ago and Eddie Guerrero, which got me more than my fair share of hate mail, I’m sticking to my decision that I don’t think this is sports news. (The other host butts in, and for the sake of this bit we’ll call him Billy) Billy: I hate to disagree with you Colin, but not everyone thinks the way you do. In fact, I’m very familiar with Arson and am a big professional wrestling fan. Cowherd: Yes, well, that’s why you don’t have your own show… anyway, just because there’s a raid on a person’s house doesn’t make it news. (Billy interjects) Billy: But this is a big drug trafficking conspiracy case here. Cowherd: Well, if every time a sports figure was involved in a Mexico-United States drug ring, we wouldn’t be talking about Santa Barbara but rather the San Francisco Giants and their Caribbean-dominated ball club. Billy: That’s horrible. Why haven’t you been fired yet? Cowherd: A little thing called ratings. Billy: But your ratings are horrible. (Cowherd changes the subject) Cowherd: So, how about some callers? Who’s this? [name withheld] from the San Diego Union Tribune? Care to shed some light on the subject? [name withheld]: Hi Colin, I have to say I agree with you about wrestlers making the sports pages, I’d never consider putting them in the sports section. I also have something to say about your San Francis— Cowherd: Thanks, David, I completely agree, it’s ridiculous to think of wrestling and professional sports in the same light. Billy: Aren’t you going to let him finish? Cowherd: Next caller! Yes, who do we have, [name withheld], sports editor of the Santa Barbara News-Press? Ah, excellent, straight from the source! Tell me Rob, where’d you guys put this story about Mr. Arson? Robert: Colin, Colin, Colin… you son of a bitch. (Cowherd laughs) Cowherd: Careful now, this is being broadcast all— Robert: Now you shut your mouth radio boy and listen up! This isn’t [name withheld], in fact, it’s even better, because you’re speaking to Brian Arson himself. (Cowherd springs to life) Cowherd: Brian Arson! To what do we owe the pleasure? They let you out of police custody, I see? Good to hear! Brian: You shut it, Colin! Now listen! I’m sick of you and every other sports analyst that knocks professional wrestling. When it comes down to it, you’re sitting there in your little radio booth, your skinny ass, with your pathetic wife and kid— Cowherd: Excuse me? Brian: I’m not finished, now shut the hell up! Yeah, that’s right, I know about you… Portland, Oregon, I’m sick of the garbage that comes out of your mouth day in and day out. I’m a very powerful person Mr. Coward, so I advise you to not speak on the subject of professional wrestling ever again unless I hear some sort of praise in your voice. (Cowherd laughs nervously) Cowherd: Now, eheh— Brian: I’m not done talking. You keep your trap shut on the subject, you hear me? Now let me make a few plugs. I’m going to be entering a little thing called the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament this month, so I want you and your listeners to know about it. It’s the World Series of professional wrestling, if you will; now from here on out I better hear a plug each week for this tournament, or else you and your family, well… you’ll see. I hope I haven’t made you too nervous there. Oh yeah, and one last thing while I’m on the air… go Spartans! (Arson hangs up the phone, there is no talking within the radio booth.)
