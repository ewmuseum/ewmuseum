Scent of A Woman: It's Love

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-01 She hits the bags with all of her might as her taped fists begin to fill with crimson from the impacts. With every hit she exhales a grunt that shows her anger and frustration, trying to vent out all of the different feelings that have been bottled up inside of her body. Nobody else is around and that pleases her because if anybody else was in the room, she would probably let that frustration out on them and that is probably something that nobody else would want to happen. More punches reign down on the bag as her frustration makes her adrenaline rush even more, unleashing all of the emotions inside her until she stops hitting it and drops down to her knees, panting as the sweat trickles down her rose-red appearance although some of the liquid may be tears. Her bloody hands fall down into her lap as the black cotton pants slowly soak up some of the fluids and stain them. She cannot believe what she has been put through, all of the wear brought upon her by that one word, the word in which she never wants to hear again. Love? What is love? It is nothing. All of the emotion comes back as she gets back to her feet with a sneer dawning across her usually lovely face. She can barely lift her hands but as long as there is emotion in her body that needs to get out, she will let it out and that is what she does as she begins to fire shots at the red bag once again. But, for some reason, the emotion is not going away and she concentrates on it more and more. Love. Please, what does it even mean? For someone to say ‘I love you’, it isn’t anything but a mere phrase that is supposed to link one to another. The shots at the bag continue as she can feel her arms getting weak, she can barely lift them but continues to do so as the emotion is still in her body and doesn’t want to be released. It is something that she must deal with but the time is not right, the time for this will never be right as she knows what love is supposed to be and knows she will never feel such an emotion, at least not anymore will she. Finally she stops, her fists drop to her sides and even though she thinks that she should pick them up and keep going, she has decided that the time has come to hash out all of the issues running through her head and she is a depressed woman so there is a lot of them. She puts her hands onto her shapely hips but it doesn’t take long for them to fall back down to her side as they are too weak to have to support themselves. The air blows quickly in and out through her mouth as she is completely exhausted and just wants to leave. Coricidon turns around and walks away, leaving the bag motioning back and forth, still reeling from the blows that have landed on it. She suddenly stops near a set of barbells and looks towards the door, resting her weight against the rack containing all of the weights as she looks out of it into the night almost scared. She isn’t scared of the night but she is scared of what awaits at her present residence. It is almost as if she can already feel the awkward silence between her and Sky as soon as the motel room door opens and she doesn’t want that. Coricidon and Sky should be together, the names just sound so compatible but love; love isn’t something that should be around. She remembers the last time someone ‘loved’ her, it was her father and it was in his own sick and twisted way, she didn’t want to have to go through that ordeal once again. But the facts still remained the same, she must return home to Sky and sort this all out and hope that Sky will understand what is going on. That is when the walk to the door feels like the longest walk ever as she isn’t even halfway to the outlet of the gym before more doubtful woes begin to fill up her mind. What happens if Sky doesn’t understand? What if she walks out on Coricidon altogether and decides it is just not worth it. While she doesn’t feel any love, it will sure as hell leave her heart reeling in such a way that would completely destroy her. And that is that, she will not go home. She cannot because if she did, her whole life could fall apart and that was not an option before a big time tournament. She drops her head down, as she doesn’t want anyone to see the sullen look on her face although the only other person in the entire gym resides next to the door and most likely cannot even see her from this vantage point. “Why did she have to go and do that?” Coricidon asks herself. “If she wouldn’t have told me that she loved me then I would have never left and if I would have never left then I would have been in a better mood. She’s just going to have to wait for me, at least until she is ready to realize that love isn’t for me. I would be willing to spend the rest of my life with her but not for the reasons of love, that wouldn’t work for me.” All of the sweat has disappeared from her face yet a stray liquid has dropped from her face, a tear. She watches as it drops to the ground and then wipes her right eye with her right hand as she looks up and sniffs, trying to regain any sense of pride back to her reputation before stepping out the doors back into the real world. “James Edwards and Raijin may both be strong and fierce competitors”, she says as she tries to pull her mindset back to the match. “But one thing that they do not have is the one the that I have in my life and that is a PISSY attitude.” She storms off and exits as the man waves her out. He sits in his tiny cubicle watching the news as something on it strikes us as strange. A closer inspection to the television screen shows the television anchor sitting at his desk in front of a blue backdrop while a small box sits at the top corner of the screen next to his head and inside of the box reads ‘Teen Suicide’. We continue to watch this as the man speaks on the subject. “In news today, a 19-year-old woman was found in a hotel room dead by a maid today”, the news anchor says, trying to keep a somber look on his face although it is obvious he does not care. “It wasn’t said how she died but authorities are not ruling out the thought of suicide.” The news anchor slams his papers against his desk to make all of the papers equal before he turns to a different subject. She drives in her Cadillac Escalade as dawn is approaching. Her first tournament match is just on the horizon and she mentally prepares herself. “I’m going to hurt them”, she says with her hands at ten and two. “I’m going to grab them by their scrawny little necks and strangle them until they cannot breath anymore.” A small, devilish grin grows on her face as she grips the steering wheel harder while her foot pushes down more on the gas to take off down the nearly desolate highway. All of her concentration the entire time has been on match, paying no attention to her earlier thoughts about Sky. “I’m gonna win!” she screams out joyfully. “I’m gonna win and I’m gonna do it for..” Suddenly everything stops as it is like she hit a brick wall. She could do it for Sky but Sky was mad, not on the outside but she knew, it was that obvious. What was she to do? Why did this all have to happen? “God!” she yells out as she swerves from the fast lane to the off ramp of the three lane highway as she heads up it and sits at the end before turning onto the bridge. She’s going to have to do it and she knows it. The only way that she can be back in Sky’s good graces is to say those words. She has to tell Sky that she loves her and that is that. She is going to turn around right now and tell her that she loves her. The car squeals its tires as it fishtails into the lane of the bridge and goes straight before turning left and heading back to the motel, heading back to where Sky is supposed to be. A flip of the dial on the radio signals one of the only radio stations in the area to begin playing as it is of a news station going over the happenings of the day. “And it has been confirmed that the cause of death was sui-“, comes from the radio before being interrupted by Coricidon. She turns the dials around looking for something else as she suddenly finds something. Her hand pulls back while she sits and waits to see what will come through her speakers. At that point, ‘I’ll be missing you’ by Puff Daddy begins to resonate throughout all six speakers in her Escalade as she continues driving back to her ‘love’. ”I could get used to this song”, she says bouncing her head to the music. And the Escalade continues bounding back towards where Sky is as Coricidon plans to tell her what Sky wanted to hear all along but will she be there to hear it?
