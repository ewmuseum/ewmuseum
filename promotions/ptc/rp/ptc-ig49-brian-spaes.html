IG49 RP Thread

18 September 2008

Brian Spaes Too often we hear about failure. Either in our industry or elsewhere, failure is evident. Burnouts, has-beens, and even worse, are those who over-inflate whatever small slice of fame that they achieved. Everyone has seen them. They are the ones who sit at the indy shows demanding 20 dollars for a Polaroid. 30 dollars if you want one with the belt they never won. Even worse are those who we don’t see. The ones work at your local Wal-Mart, cause they didn’t get an education. The ones that wake up every day with pains all over their body, and don’t have the medical insurance to take care of their injuries. They can’t even capitalize on their small try out match and turn that into a career. I know it more so than others. I had an opportunity that most would crave. I could have been the man you idolize. The paragon of the business, the king of the sport, the ace of the industry. I faltered. I fell upon my vices and took comfort in escapism. For some it’s alcohol, it could be the desire to fight, or for others it could be a love of ring rats. I loved methamphetamine. Crystal Meth. It got me to a new place. The pain from throwing myself around would disappear. The boring life of a wife, three kids and a home business never satisfied me. The boring ranch in Texas didn’t exist anymore. I was alive. Then it consumed me. I saw things that didn’t exist. I found myself in situations that were surreal. My teeth turned brown and my arms had near holes through them. I lost my ordinary normal world. All I would do is sit around and tweak. No more wrestling, no more wife, no children. I lived in this pit for almost four years. That’s not me anymore. I’m getting myself back on my feet. I’ve been clean for almost 16 months. I’m wrestling again. My life feels exciting. I haven’t felt this way in a long time. I have rediscovered why I hurt myself this way. They say life is a matter of need versus want. This isn’t a matter of wanting. I need it. I live this life. Everything I do is for the betterment of my sport. To become a better fighter, stronger than the rest, more powerful. I will reach the pantheon of wrestling excellence. I will reach the place that I was destined. I am getting close to it in Legion. I will achieve it in the Infinite Gauntlet. This title will be a step in my immortality. I can be the Ronin, going around world, fighting all comers. I can be the hero, the one that inspires the written epic. I can be the king, and the wrestling world is my dominion. I am Brian Spaes, and I will be redeemed.
