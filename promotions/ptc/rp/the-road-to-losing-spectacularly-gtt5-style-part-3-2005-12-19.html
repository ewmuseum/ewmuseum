The Road to Losing … SPECTACULARLY!! GTT5 Style Part 3

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 Fatbadger emerges from his toilet stall, looking proud and ready and a good ten pounds lighter. A well dressed man enters the bathroom, looking for similar relief, but all he finds is a foul smell that isn’t even appropriate to be found in a public restroom such as this. He backs up a few steps and fumbles for the door handle, his eyes blinded with tears. His gags can be heard for several diminishing seconds after he finds his way out. Badger just smiles and makes his way over to the sinks, rinsing his hands under some water. He’s approached from behind. Voice: FATRICK J. MCBADGERSON!!! Badger spins around to find … the three masked men in black that he met before his first round match!! Ya know, those three men who have the same build and mannerisms as Fatty’s XUW teammates, the lovable Boomer Sooners, yet strike fear into his feeble heart. As such, Badger drops immediately to the floor at the sight of them and huddles under the sinks. It’s probably a good thing that he just emptied his bladder moments before. Attacker 2: You made it out of the first round Fatty! HUZZAH!! That would normally be Saij’s trademarked greeting, but this certainly couldn’t be him, because Saij doesn’t wear a mask. And Saij knew that masks caused Badger to become irrationally scared and easily suggestible. So why would Saij be wearing one? Attacker 2: You won just like we told you that you had to! Attacker 1: Now we don’t have to make sure you never see Funyuns and Code Red again. The kind of logical follow up would normally be spoken by Badger’s good friend, Dave Kipfer. But Dave also doesn’t wear a mask like this man. And Dave knows that threatening to take away the life blood of Fatty’s diet of Funyuns and Code Red would cause him to go into a kind of fear and rage induced stupor. Fat Attacker: By god! Can’t we just kill him this once?! JR-Not wants to kill Fatbadger!! And that sort of hatful dialog would usually be reserved for the man in charge of the Boomer Sooners, Jim Ross (again, yes, that Jim Ross). But this heavyset man just referred to himself as JR-Not, not "JR". That maybe close, but it’s not close enough to be suspicious to the likes of Fatbadger, who continues to cower amongst the plumbing. The three masked men still brandish the badly spray painted water guns, waving them about each as they talk, since ya know, they have masks on otherwise and we can’t see any mouths or facial expressions (just like shadow puppets!!!!). Attacker 1 leaps forward and pushes the gun in the face of Fatbadger. Attacker 1: Now Fatbadger! What do you know about your new opponent?! Badger’s eyes bulge and converge on his nose, where the tip of the water rifle meets his face. His bottom lip quivers in his fear. Fatbadger: Like, I know that, his name’s like, StarDust … or something. Attacker 1: IT’S DARKSTAR YOU NOOB!!! Fat Attacker: By god! Let’s hurt him! The Fat Attacker advances and clocks Badger on the top of the head with his gun, the hollow, plastic butt of the gun making a distinct noise against the hollow, spongy skull of Fatbadger. He holds his head and checks for blood. Fatbadger: OW dude! I’m dying! Attacker 2: Dude, JR-Not, we don’t want to hurt him, just scare him. Fatbadger: Why scare me dudes? Attacker 2: WE’LL ASK THE QUESTIONS HERE MOTHERFARKER!! Badger somehow tucks his body even more under the sinks. Attacker 2 turns to Attacker 1. Attacker 2: Uh, why’re we scaring him again? Attacker 1: Because if he wants to advance in this tournament thing, he needs crazy masked guys to terrorize him. Guys with guns and yelling and a fat guy. Fat Attacker: By god! Attacker 1: Because if he wants to accomplish his jobberish goals of becoming a big name jobber in PTC, he has to win. Attacker 2: He has to keep winning to accomplish his goals of big time losing? Fatbadger: That kind of logic works for me dudes. Attacker 1: WHO SAID YOU COULD TALK!!? NOW BE SCARED AGAIN!! As told, Badger returns to whimpering on the dirty tile flooring. Attacker 1: Now, what else do you know about this DarkStar? Fatbadger: He … he’s got like … the most BEAUTIFUL eyes. Attacker 1: What does that have-- Fat Attacker: By god! He does?! JR-Not would love to see those! JR-Not must see this DarkStar fella! Attacker 1: Forget about all th-- Attacker 2: I really wouldn’t mind seeing those either. He’d have a hard time having more beautiful eyes than my mother though. Attacker 1: Look, whether he does or doesn’t have the most beautiful eyes isn’t going to help you get into the next round of the GTT. Fatbadger: I’m just lucky it isn’t a match based on eye beauty. Attacker 2: That’s for sure! Attacker 1: You’re lucky it isn’t based on lots of things, like intelligence, breath, or who stuffs their trunks less. Badger gasps. Fatbadger: GASP! Attacker 1: That’s right, we know. We also know that DarkStar … may or may not be … the former king of dreams. Badger double gasps. Fatbadger: DOUBLE GASP! Dude, let’s hope for the "not be." Attacker 1: But what if he is? And he challenges you to a dream match? Where you have to wrestle him in your dreams. Then what? Fatbadger: Then he’d have to get by my army of like, naked chicks and stuff. Yeah! Attacker 1: But what if--. Actually, that IS a pretty good plan. Attacker 2: Yeah man, let’s go get us one of those armies! I salute for BOOOBIES!! Fatbadger: FWAHAHAHAHA! Attacker 1: RETURN TO COWERING!! Badger, being easily suggestible due to the masks and all, does as he is told. Attacker 1: But we should really look into that. Fat Attacker: By god! Agreed! Attacker 1: So how about this Badger … we also have it on good authority that DarkStar may also be … dead! Badger opens his mouth, but he must have pulled something from that double gasp, because he just sits there with his mouth hanging open. Attacker 1: Or undead!! His mouth drops open-er … or something. Attacker 1: We haven’t quite got that part straight yet. So we’ll just go with him being a zombie for now. How you gonna fight a zombie? Badger shrugs. Fatbadger: Chainsaw? The masked men think for a second. Attacker 1: That works. We also would have accepted shotgun or flame thrower. Attacker 2: Personally, we were hoping you’d have gone with flame thrower. Or midget with a giant inflatable penis. Badger smacks his forehead like he should have thought of that. Attacker 1: So now we’ve got all that covered. You’re officially prepared for the former king of dreams … who is also a zombie. Fatbadger: How, like, did you find out all this stuff, dudes? Attacker 1: Oh, we have our sources. Attacker 2: We may have also smoked a couple glowsticks and hallucinated the whole thing. Attacker 1: But better safe than sorry. We missing anything? Fat Attacker: By god! The dramatic cliffhanger!! Just then, Christopher Lloyd bursts into the bathroom, dressed in a white lab coat with welder's goggles on his forehead. Fatbadger: Dude, I LOVED you in Roger Rabbit! Christopher: Fatty! I just went back in time and attached a bomb to your unborn fetal self! If you lose this match against DarkStar … I’m just gonna have to blow up your mother’s womb and negate you’re entire existence to keep this loss of your record! Fatbadger: Why?! NOOOOOOOOOO!! Badger screams to the sky as everyone strikes a ghastly pose and dramatic music plays. Attacker 1: That should work. Christopher: Good lord! Did somebody microwave some cat shit in here? That’s awful! The scene fades out.
