Second Guessing...

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 _______________ ::[Introduction]:: _______________ To be crowned a champion, is to be guided to destiny. A hand pushing you down a path of salvation, to achieve what some thought could never happen. The pinnacle of a career is often based on the success in one's own league. For Seymour Almasy, he has become bigger than the UWF, inlight of a great former Global Champion in Saij and Jamester. Almasy climbed to the pinnacle of the PrimeTime again. While Darek had fought with his heart and soul, eventually leading to a victory over Patti Anderson, but a precarious tie with Kyo Kushinaga. Both men were handed North Wind titles, Kyo now wore Patti Anderson's championship and Darek, he glared down at the title handed to him. The name was very familiar, a name that had drawn Dark Angel into the Four Winds. The imfamous Todd Dream's name was etched across Darek's North Wind Title. He felt more and more as if he didn't earn it, knowing full well that Todd Dream was the one who lead Patti to her eventual downfall. Dark Angel hated Todd Dream, the demon wanted to destroy the former champion but Darek was uncertain to what to think or feel about Todd Dream. He knew the man could fight, while Dark Angel was in possession of Darek's body, it was though Darek was watching his actions from outside his own body. He knew how Dream fought and what he was capable of, but the hatred died when Dark Angel died. Becoming the North Wind Champion was just the beginning of the journey Darek had embarked upon. Sooner or latter he knew fate would bring Todd Dream and Darek DeAngleo together for one epic battle. For now, he was content on knowing that his opponent for the second round of the GTT was Seymour Almasy. Darek entered this tournament to cleanse the souls and purify the world of it's darkness, one man or woman at a time. Almasy was up next on the list, and though his feelings for Almasy should have been subdued when the demon was destroyed, Darek still spited the man. It was a feeling he didn't understand, everyone was worthy of a second chance but the way Almasy spoke, with such disdain, even Darek could see the blatant lack of respect. Having very little respect for Almasy the man, Darek did however respect the person's accomplishments, who wouldn't? It was obvious to him, this task was going to demand Darek's best. A best that Darek has been striving to reach, but coming off a South Wind Title loss, a North Wind Title tie, he was losing his confidence. To stand before the man that defined PTC Global Champion, would be a lesson hard learned through the eye's of the Final Fantasy. Darek felt the preasure, he felt the tensions rising and the blood boiling. This was going to be more than a lesson. It was going to be instruction. Divine intervention. Prufication... ___________________ ::[Darek's Thoughts]:: ___________________ And so things have changed. I've walked out of Maelstrom the champion as I proffesed I would. Only, I am not the only champion. It's a privallege that I share with the Deathscythe Outlaw, Kyo Kushinago. A man who fell victim to Dave Hurst, some believe I share the same fate, only the victory will be Seymour Almasy. If or when this happens, the champions of the Four Winds will have fallen. Patti Anderson the former champion to Jade in round one, and Kyo. If Seymour can climb the mountain I am, so be it, I will fall like any other. But this is one conquest you shall never forget. You and Dark Angel started this battle long ago, now we finish the war. Before an audience of our colleagues and peers, you and I shall stand before each other. The Global Champion, the THC Champion, a UWF star to stand against the rising force, North Wind Champion, master of the Four Winds, Darek DeAngleo. A war that shall not be forgotten by either being, anytime soon. For me, this is about a chance to prove once and for all that Almasy can be defeated by those he does not respect. Infact, It will be an eye opener for the entire PrimeTime, a message that states: The Four Winds is here to stay, signed the North Wind Champion. Who knows what the future holds? Should I lose, I will have failed in my attempt. But theree is always another opportunity around the corner. Should I win, I will have earned the respect of the legend, the mighty Seymour Almasy. Though he mate despise what I stand for, he will know what I fight for. Kyo, he and I are destined to meet again. There is no question, an Ironman match that went to Sudden Death overtime only to have a tie result in two men being declared the champion. Not exactly my idea of a great performance on my behalf, inexcusable aswell. Perhaps we will set the standard of excellence to a new level within our great land. But for now, with all due respect to the Outlaw, I find myself more concerned with Seymour. Not to mention the many complications that surround the issue of GTT and what I have been through recently. It seems as though my world has been spiralling down and nothing can stop it, but I swear to the Heavenly father, if I'm going down. I am going to bring Seymour Almasy down with me... _____________ ::[The Story]:: _____________ ...concluded... Using his index finger, Darek brushes Amanda's hair away from her face behind her ear. Using his other hand, he lifts her head by placing his hand under her chin. "I know this is a lot for you to take in. I know what Dark Angel did is inexcusable, but please remember I love you, the demon didn't. Right now, I have a horrible path of trails and tribulations ahead of me, I was hoping ot have you on my side for that." She doesn't answer him. Instead she closes her eyes and more water flows from the sides, rolling down her soft cheeks. Letting go of her face, Darek does his best to give her the space she needs. Grabbing his shirt from off the counter he walks passed Amanda, stopping he looks back at the beautiful woman, crying in a heap on the floor. Shaking his head side to side, he closes his eyes and feels her pain. He knew it had to be killing her inside to digest what he had fed her. Both of their worlds were turned upside down and flipped inside out. Not by choice, by fate and for a reason. For the lord does indeed work in mysterious ways. "I am walking my path Amanda, a path of light, I have been given a second chance, just as you have." his words seem to have no affect on the woman, "I am destined to face Kyo Kushinaga and Patti Anderson to determine the fate of the North Wind Title. Then I fight for the Survival of the Four Winds in an attempt to climb Mount St. Hells. Knowing my luck, I'll end up facing Almasy in round two of the tournament. I could use your support." He walks away from her, back into the room. "I didn't want you to find out this way. I wanted to tell you. I'm so sorry." He walks into the bedroom and sits on the edge of the bed, the light form the washroom continues to draw his attention to the woman on the floor. He can't help but feel the raw suffering that she was enduring now. His heart ached for her, not for his actions, but for what she had been put through. All for what? So a demon named Dark Angel could become known to the world, to shed the fear across the land. It was true, Dark Angel did seek fame and glory, however Darek sought redemption. The sins this demon has scarred Darek with, have risen to the surface and become wittnessable to all. His life was now like an open book, they could read from the beginning, yet it was like the last pages had never been written. Darek has chosen to write his own fate. Starring gloomingly at Amanda, Darek gets up and moves around to his side of the bed. With the light on, Amanda had chosen not to folow Darek on this morning. Instead she curled up into a ball on the floor and closed her eyes. She felt vunerable, but in some way believed what Darek was saying was the truth. She couldn't put her finger on it, but somehow this all seemed familiar, like a distinct feeling of deja vu. Her heart had finally slowed to a smooth, continous rhythm and she drifted away to sleep. Her mind had a whimsical time playing with her sub concious as she endulged into dream after dream. The worst fears, her deepest desires, all of the emotions were played on in horrible visions that danced through her mind. The nightmares ravished her through the entire night, not to mention sleeping awkwardly on a cold linolium floor. Eventually her mind forgot about the dreams and she slept, a long peaceful sleep. ------------------ ::[2 days later]:: ------------------ The anger was still there. But alot of it had begun to subside. Slowly the hatred she had felt for Darek over the last couple days crept from her body. Coming to an understanding that Darek was not himself, Amanda knew that she had to support him in his efforts. Yet, in the back of her mind, she also swore vengeance. For everything that Patti Anderson had done to the once demonic monster, Amanda swore to send the payback ten folds over. Holding the adoption papers in her hands, she flips through the pages finding the court date listed for the hearing. There was no chance in hell that Darek or Amanda were signing these papers and Patti's best hope lied in proving that Darek was an unfit guardian for the girl. Despite the rage that ravished through Amanda's soul, she still knew that bringing Patricia down would not be an easy task. Darek hadn't said anything more than 'good morning' to the red haired beauty, he called his wife. Knowing full well the wrath of her anger, Darek made the attempt to avoid the situation. He felt horrible as it was for everything happening, now he had the guilt of knowing he killed any goodness in Amanda's soul. Telling her that Dark Angel, his own body, a demonic alter-ego of Darek, shamelessly sought to bring Amanda to damnation. Now Darek was living with the burden of that sin. His journey to salvation had been brushed along and though Patti Anderson cost him the South Wind Title, she couldn't stop him from claiming the North Wind Title at Maelstrom. An honor and distinction that Darek shared unwillingly with the Deathscythe Outlaw. But that was just the beginning, Darek had managed to focus his ability and mind into bringing one Chaplain Graves to his knees. Flipping on the light switch, the white light illuminates a large room filled with training equipment. To the far right, a wrestling ring is set up. Scattered along the walls are various photos and accomplishments that Darek and Amanda had obtained in their careers to date. Looking around the home gym, Darek's eyes find the cabinet holding his most distinguished career orientated possessions, his old title belts. Walking slowly over to the glass cabinet, he overcome with emotion, joy, pain, fear, hope, as his mind takes him through a journy of his past. Running his index finger along the glass, he spells out the letters: GCW. It was the Legacy Scott Gibson, Madison Squared Garden and an independent federation under the Run of CEO Billy Brahma. The fight was booked as the champion, Scott Gibson former colleague and ally of Darek defended his Global Championship. It was then Darek had tasted his first heavyweight championship. Crashing the champion through the ring with a No Mercy finisher and making the pin. He was proclaimed the new age Global champion and Gibson, the golden age champion. A federation that was soon purchased and run by fellow bracket member, Steven Caldera. Darek smiles as he familiarly recalls his championship reign. His thought is suddenly interupted as his eyes glance at the next title. The gold shimmer's brightly in the light, the reflection shines into Darek's eye beckoning his attention. The Global Championship Wrestling was a fond time in Darek's life, his debut into professional wrestling. Yet, Darek's mind dwelled on one of the greatest moments in his career. The When Titans Collide: 2 PPV extravaganza. Smiling at the prize position, Darek closes his eyes, remembering those three letters: GWA. The GulfSouth Wrestling Alliance was a federation that turned Darek from mortal to immortal. His mentor was killed at the hands of one of his enemies, his stable betrayed him and he had undergone a very dramatic change. Turning away from God, Darek was consumed by a demon called Dominic. From that point on, Darek truly was a Dark Angel of Death. Risking himself each week, he put it all on the line hoping he would not fall. The Championship was decided in a Fall From Grace match, the Angel's own specialty. Eight men, one survivor, one true champion and the Dark Angel was that champion. Opening his eyes Darek's smile fades as he moves to the next title. The federation had signed Dark Angel to a massive contract to fight in their warehouse based federation when GWA sold out. The smallest title of the grouping, he also had the least feelings for this championship. Looking over the embroidered golden buckles, he can't help but recall his short say in the UW. Untouchable Wrestling was a small federation that had aquired the talent of Dark Angel to run a program with their current champion, Zakk Wylde. Wylde was a man of dignity and ambition, his ambition was to be better than the rest, but it took only one month before the mighty Dark Angel stole the Wylde man's coveted world championship. The Untouchable Wrestling had reached a new height under the guidance of the Angel of Death. However, none of it was meant to be. UW closed it's doors and Darek remained the only undefeated World Champion. Looking at an empty shelf, marked SCWF. Darek tasted the sweetest of all victories. West and the Angel had managed to bring the Saviour Todd Dream and Heretic down to become the number one contenders to the Tag Titles. After only a three week stint in the SCWF, the doors closed and Darek walked from the fed empty handed. Now starring at the empty shelf, Darek turns away from the display case and glares soley at the North Wind Title sitting atop the mantle on the other end of the room. Weaving through his fitness equipment, Darek makes his way towards the Four Winds most valued prize. Leaning on a aerobic bicyckle, Darek makes the motion of a cross and begins to pray while taking his eyes off the North Wind Title. Reaching over to the mantle, Darek pulls a remote control off the top and pushes a red button. Instantly the basement is filled with music. He thought to himself, about the fame, the recognition. None of it mattered to him, all he commanded was the respect of the same individuals who worshiped the false icon, Seymour Almasy. Turning his head as he takes a look at the home gym, he begins to visualize his routine. For him, facing Seymour was just as much of an honour as becoming the North Wind Champion. But to be the one to make the mighty fall, that would immortalize him in the eyes of the onlookers. Walking over to the Stairmaster, Darek marches past the machine and posts a picture of Seymour Almasy on the wall. After tacking up the picture, he pushes another botton and a large TV across the room begins to play fottage of Seymour's UWF victories. Including becoming the THC champion. To the Angel, this was personal. He had the opportunity to prove to the world that as much as they hated the Four Winds, those who worked under the syndicated logo would fight for their great land. Darek was one of those warriors, he sought to bring a good name to his league and if that meant bringing Almasy down for that excrusiatingly long three count, so be it. It was a case of two birds, one stone. Darek commanded respect and was going to get it, the federation marked for the same thing and again all it could take would be to defeat Almasy. The Global Champion for a reason, the man would give his entire existence to holding onto that title, ever so dearly. Climbing onto the stairmaster, the pedals move up and down as Darek begins his daily routine. Watching the footage of Seymour's recent GTT round one victory he climbs off the stair simulator after half hour. Walking over to the ring, Darek places his hands onto the apron and pushes himself up. Climbing in, he walks over to the far end staring at the footage. Almasy finishes up his opponent and advances with little surprise at all. Nodding his head, Darek turns away from the television and closes his eyes. The music flows through his mind, through his soul and body, taking him into another state, calming him. The problem Darek was facing was one that tore him between fixing his homelife problems and doing what people would perceive as the greatest upset in GTT history, defeat the Final Fantasy. Had this been a charity event, a weekly show, perhaps a monthly PPV, Seymour felt Darek had a chance but as this was a tournament and not just any tournament, the tournament; Darek really had no chance. The door to the basement opens, and a pair of feet are seen making thier way down the stairs. Stopping at the bottom, Amanda tries to smile at Darek. "Look, I can't pretend I'm happy about what I've found out and what you've told me. But I can do my best to be there for you when you need me." "What's the point of living a lie Amanda?" Darek asks, "I'm standing in this ring, thinking about the two things that matter to me the most. You and my mission. God has brought us together for an obvious reason, but he has sent me on a path to purification. No matter what I choose, one of us will be neglected." "Decisions in life are often hard to make. No one ever said that life was going to be easy." Amanda steps further into the home gym, turning to face Darek she smiles at him. "I hated you for what you told me, but I chose to believe that you had no control of the situation. In life we can learn to deal with what is dealt or quit. What are you going to do Darek?" He turns away from her and walks over to the corner, resting on the turnbuckle for a moment, a period of silence passes. "I don't know. It seems as though Almasy is more prepared now than he was ever before, faster, stronger, even smarter. I'm almost starting to buy into his hype." "Look at what you've accomplished Darek. In less than one year in a PTC federation, you've become a South Wind Champion, East Wind Champion, Stood before Seymour Almasy and Tessa Windsor, named to team 4ww for Survival and finally defeated Patricia Anderson to become the North Wind Champion. Ten months love, just ten months and you've done more than you give yourself credit for." "Ten months in the Four Winds and I have climbed to the pinnacle they have to offer, but am I truly ready to partake within the PrimeTime community, to compete with Seymour Almasy as the Global Champion." He stops and glances over his shoulder. "At one time, I thought I was. But then all this shit happened, Raven being adopted, admitting to you the truth once and for all. Everything has collapsed around me and feel as though I'm suffocating." Amanda walks to the ring and reaches up resting her arms on the ropes, peering through the first and second she smiles at her husband. "You tell me you've changed since I've been away. That you've found God." Darek turns away from the corner and looks his soulmate in her aqua green eyes. "You don't sound as if you have a lot of faith in the path he has chosen for you." She had stumped him there, facing Almasy had to be a part of God's plan. Victory would mean Darek was ready for success. Defeat brought the harsh realisation that the once Immortal Angel of Death was not ready to seek his salvation. A look of distraught came across his face as he crumbled down to the canvass, leaning against the corner. "If I fail, all that I have worked for will come to a complete hault, if I fail... I have to begin again." "You can't fail." The former GulfSouth Wrestling Alliance champion and only female to be crowned so, pulled away from the ropes. "You've never quit before. If you go out there and fight Almasy with all your might and win, what will you have accomplished?" There's an awkward moment of silence, "that I deserve respect, that the Four Winds is in a new era. That we will not be pushed around." "So aside from proving you have bigger balls, what will you have accomplished?" Darek thinks about what she has said a little more thouroughly, "nothing." "And if you lose, what will he have accomplished?" The answer had to be the same, "...nothing?" he answered with uncertainty. Amanda nods, "this tournament decides the one best wrestler. Unless you win, it doesn't matter who you beat because losing is losing and there is only one winner. If you beat Almasy and lose in round three, you've done nothing and are back at this square, vice versa for him." Darek lifts his head and eyes from the canvass and into the deep gaze of his wife, "I suppose you're right." He straightened his legs along the canvass, and leaned back using the ropes for a back support. "After everything we've been through, we can make it through this, win or lose." "Mind if I join you?" Amanda slides into the ring, and begins to worm her way towards the messy, spiked haired monster mopping in the corner. "I mean, we haven't 'wrestled' in a long time. Maybe I can help you get your focus back." _____________ ::[The Moral]:: _____________ It comes down to respect. The things The Final Fantasy has done in his illustrious career has brought him a certain adjulation from Darek. That was were the feeling of respect stopped, Almasy hated the Four Winds and almost everyone within, it was no secret. Darek vowed upon becoming champion to defend the good name of his federation at all costs. To stand before the haters, the doubters and the opinionated to bring them to the light, the light of the God. Darek has sworn to keep the Four Winds free from scrutiny, from hatred. A mission that is seemingly endless, but he has chosen to undertake one person at a time. His mission was to keep the Four Winds free of sin but also free of harm. The opinions and feelings of contempt from the PrimeTime Global Champion had drawn Darek's attention and now it was time for them to step up. The battle is on, who respects who more... who will survive.
