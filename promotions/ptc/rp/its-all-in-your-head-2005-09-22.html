Its All in Your Head

22 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-09-22 I’ve been stuck on this god forsaken island for two months now. I think the heat is starting to make me go crazy. I’ve been seeing things, weird things, that I know can not exist. Am I going mad? Why won’t my head stop hurting? The sun just keeps beating down on me, beating me into the ground, and there seems to be no escape from it. Even the shade feels like a hundred degrees. Am I going to die here? Why won’t someone save me already? ---{The Hunger}--- I was on my way to Japan, having just left Hawaii, when the ship ran into trouble. I do not know what cause the trouble, I was asleep in my cabin at the time. I can just remember the water pouring in. There just did not seem to be any end to the water. I fought my way past the oncoming flood and managed to reach the deck. A blanket of night had been draped over the sky making it hard to see even a foot before one’s nose. Rain had been pouring hard from the angry skies. The wind pounded against my body, nearly knocking me off my feet. I turned to my right, to try and look for any other passengers, when a deck chair came flying at me. The impact threw me off the ship and into the icy cold waters. My body went numb and I fell asleep, sure I was as good as dead. It had been the warmth from the sun that finally woke me. It took some time before I could open my eyes. The bright sun hurt them, causing a great pain through my body. As strange as it may have sounded, I was grateful for the pain. It let me know I was alive. “That we were alive, dick”. When I finally was able to open my eyes, I realized I was not alone on the island. A man with bright golden hair, almost as bright as the sun, greeted me. “If by greeted you meant that I kicked you to check and see if you were dead, then yeah, I greeted you”. His crystal blue eyes matters the clear waters of the ocean that surrounded the island. “Did anyone ever tell you all that poetic shit is gay? If I have the share this island with some faggit, then I’ll take my chances and try and swim to Japan.” My first thoughts were what to do about food and for shelter. I knew the water from the ocean would be undrinkable, so I needed to find another source for water as well. Deciding it would be best, I spent the first day looking around the island. I was interested in what resources the island would provide for us. “Meanwhile, I debated if I should bash this fucker’s head in with a rock and eat him if I got hungry. To hell with that surviving off plants shit, that crap is for hippies.” By the time night fell, I had a good idea of the layout of the island. Food, shelter, and water would be hard to come by but not impossible. There were a few fruit trees on the island with the likes of coconuts, bananas, and papilla. I had also noticed something moving in the bushes, a wild pig I figured. There was also a small pond which seemed to have formed toward the center of the island. The water appeared to be clean at least. The largest problem seemed to be trying to build a shelter. “If this fruit had any plans to share shelter, I would have drowned him in the pond.” At the rise of the next sun, I woke myself and headed into the thick of the jungle. Today would be about survival. I needed to first find food and gather water, and then I would start construction on a shelter of types. I asked my friend on the island for any help he could lend. He agreed and for the first time we greeted each other. He told me his name was Chris Storm. “Don’t let the little piece of shit lie to you; I only agreed to help to get the guy to shut up. The guy went on about survival and team work and all this other bullshit crap you see on Survivor. What a bunch of horseshit. And for the record, I still planned on drowning him in the lake.” We were lucky on that day. Within an hour Chris had managed to find a bundle of bananas in a tree. There looked to have been maybe twelve to fourteen bananas in the bunch. Using the husk from the bunch, I had been able to make a bowl to store water in, for now at least. If rationalized, the food would last us for two or three days and the water in the bowl could ideally last us the rest of the day, which is if it did not leak. “The second that loser turns his back; I had full plans to gulp down that water and hide a few bananas. Let him rationalize, I’m dying on a full stomach.” Shelter is what had proven to be difficult. There were little resources to build on the island. There were trees for wood, but whenever I tried to sculpt a cutting utilize out of rock it broke before yielding a decent blade. Night had already fallen by the time I managed to cut down the first coconut tree. At least the husk from the bananas would prove to be a roof as the bowl had never leaked, though the water seemed to not last as long as I had expected. “Yep, that’s right bitches; I drank that shit as soon as the fucker turned his back.” It was not until the end of the first week on the island that I had finally constructed the shelter. I had carved six poles to serve as post for the walls and used the bark for walls. The top was crudely sewed together husk. It had not been much, but it kept out the rain and sun; which after a week of enduring, had been welcomed greatly. “How in the hell did Gilligan manage to build his hut so much better than this Professor wannabe? I can’t believe I had to share that piece of crap with the fucker.” With each passing day, our hope of being rescued decreased. Moral on the island had reached an all time low around the same time the food started to become harder to obtain. Since the first day on the island, we had yet to see the wild pig again. I started to get the feeling that it had been merely the wind playing a trick on me. “Oh great, the Survivor Castaway is going to get us killed. Fuck that shit.” After a time we started to lose count of the days. I had lost count of how long we had been there; the days just seemed to stretch on like the endless sea that surrounded us. Nerves were being grinded away, like dust on stones. “In other words, we were getting on each other’s nerves. Course that fucker has been on my nerves since the moment I first met him.” It had been dark; I remember not being able to see well. I asked Chris to follow me into the jungle to search for the wild pig. I knew the pig did not exist, but I needed Chris to obey. I did not know why I chose the dead of night for what I did. We were the only ones on the island. But the darkness made me feel secure some how. “I can’t believe the little shit had the balls to do that to me. I am Chris Fucking Storm! You don’t do that shit to me!” Blood poured faster than I had expected. I ditched the rock into the pond and rolled the body in after. No one would find the body on the stinking island, but I felt safer knowing it was lost in the depths of black at the bottom of the pond. What was the old saying? “Out of sight, out of mind?” The dirty deed had made me ravenously thirsty. Bending forward, I cupped my hands and dipped them into the cool and crisp water. Blood mixed with the water but I drank it nonetheless. It felt satisfying, like it cooled the burning fire inside me. I could feel Chris’ essence flowing through my veins as I drank. Despite the satisfaction I got from the water, my hunger for it grew with every plunge. I found myself with my head dunked into the pond, not even realizing how I had gotten to that point. I started to suck in the water, mouthfuls at a time. The blood, Chris’ blood, entered my body through my mouth, ears, nose, and eyes. It filled me up and fused with my own blood. For the first time, I, Scott Drake, felt complete.
