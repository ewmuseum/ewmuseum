We all end up a naked slab on an examiner's table, Henry: the First

Date: 2009-10-16 Doctor Nate Harder killed the engine of his over-priced BMW Roadster to signal the end of another long day. He slumped into his seat and exhaled the weight of the world, running his fingers through his hair to relieve the tension. Released the top button of his shirt, tugged on his tie and sighed the deep sigh of a man ready to finally relax for a change. Pushing open the driver’s door, Nate stepped out into his garage, inhaling the fumes of spilled motor oil and petroleum rising from the garage’s concrete floor. There was nothing more Nate liked to do than tinker under the hood of his Roadster. It was his pride and joy. Since Doreen had passed on, he needed things to capture his imagination. Halt it from wandering off and wallowing in self-pity. The Roadster’s engine was just the thing he needed. He was sure his psychology buddies would be more for him embracing the depression that he was on the brink of, but he had no time for self-deprecation. He had sick people who needed the attention of one of the best Cardio Thoracic surgeons in the United States. Nate stepped out into the brisk night air, closing his eyes and letting the refreshing chill lap at his face. He was glad to be home. Glad to be able to finally put his feet up. At least until the pager strapped to his belt would allow him to. The ruler of Doctor Nate Harder. Subconsciously, Nate patted the pager like a Sheriff in the old West would do his six-shooter before making his way down the garden path to the front door. His mind wandered to the TV dinners in the freezer. Then to the alternatives. Maybe he’d order out for a pizza delivery. A greasy pepperoni pizza would be just the thing to dissipate the troubles of the day. Stepping across the porch, with dreams of a pepperoni pizza and an ice cold Coors Lite, he pushed open the front door. Stepped inside the hall and halted. A chill rose up his spine and froze him dead in his tracks. The door was unlocked. “You’ve reached Doctor Nate Harder. I’m afraid I’m unavailable right now, but if you would like to leave a message after the tone than please feel free to do so. If this is an emergency please contact me on my pager.” BOOOOOP! The sound of his own answering machine left a heavy feeling sitting in his gut. He hadn’t heard the phone ring. Somebody was playing the recorded message. Was his daughter home from Afghanistan? She’d only been deployed two months ago on a six month tour of duty. He had doubts that the American Air Force would send her home so soon after deployment. Panic began to set in and his palms grew slick with sweat. Slowly, gently and silently, Nate Harder tip-toed into his home when he heard the message repeated by a voice unfamiliar to him, as if someone were trying to mimic his tone and pitch. “You’ve reached Doctor Nate Harder. I’m sorry- Fuck.” The voice had confused the wording of the message. Whoever that voice belonged to, they played it back again, trying to get the words down pat so they could practice to make perfect. Nate heard the scurrying of his antique answering machine, the clicking of the buttons and then the message playing through for a second time in its entirety. “Ahhhhh… I’m afraid.” They corrected themselves in the process of listening. “Ol’ Nater is just too damn polite for his own damn good.” Whoever it was, they knew Nate. He wasn’t sure if this put him at ease or it increased the burden of his worry, further tangling the knot forming in his belly. Holding his breath and clenching his quivering asshole tightly, Nate prayed that he were in safe hands. He missed Doreen but he wasn’t ready for their reunion. Silently, Nate Harder hoped that trouble had not wandered into his den and that the good Lord was on his side. Uncertainty ringing in his head, Nate could hear his heart beating in his ears as he peered around the corner. The next thing Doctor Nate Harder saw was a fist flying with deadly precision. It connected on his chin with a devastating CRACK! He felt his brain lunge forward, slapping against the front of his skull. Saliva slung from his mouth and Nate was now airborne, barrel rolling from the collision of fist upon chin. Nate blinked at the darkness which began to consume his vision and succumbed to it, hoping it would block out the collision his face was destined to make with his polished floor boards. With a screech, Nate's face stuck to the floor boards on impact. The timber scorched his skin, leaving what a basketballer might refer to as ‘court burn’ down the right side of his face. When his skidding came to a standstill, the man who delivered the knock out blow stepped over him. Head tilted slightly as he examined his pray. Watching the heave and fall of his chest as he breathed, subdued in his unconsciousness. “Well that was easier than I thought,” the man remarked, to nobody in particular. He bent at the knees, thoughtful of the strain it might put on his back, and dead lifted the unconscious doctor, heaving him onto his shoulder and carrying him into the den with ease. The assailant dumped Nate into the office chair before he picked up a roll of duct tape. He spoke to himself like he was speaking to an old friend. “I hope he's gonna listen. Be a shame to make him. He's so damn polite.” ---- ƒ ---- The wind was ridiculously gusty in the Big Apple and it was upsetting Henry DiMaggio's day. A comforting sip. Henry DiMaggio thought himself to be the smartest man in the underworld. Un-fucken-touchable. There was nobody remotely smart enough to get near him. Nobody. Doctor Harder’s attention was taken by the nurse, spinning his head over his shoulder as he heard footsteps making their way toward the opposing entrance to the Day Procedure Theatre. “Who have you called?” “Mr. DiMaggio gave me five thousand dollars to make sure things went smoothly, Doctor.” “You don’t know what you’re doing.” “Neither do you.” “I KNOW PERFECTLY WELL, BITCH!” The change in pitch and tone, even the texture, of Nate's voice startled the surgical nurse so much so she took a step back, hand on her chest as is to still her beating heart. Desperately, her eyes flagged to the door, almost begging DiMaggio's men to burst through. Pressing one hand onto the sternum of his subdued patient, Doctor Nate Harder launched himself over the operating table, pressing all of his weight onto the abdomen of DiMaggio as he leaped over easily. A volcanic surge of blood erupted from the incision he’d made as he dashed across the theatre room toward the exit. Catherine swung her right arm, attempting to scratch at Nate’s eyes to slow him down for Henry DiMaggio’s men. Yet she failed to do so. She only managed to grab a handful of skin and ripped it from Nate’s face. He did not flinch a muscle. Catherine looked at the flap of skin in her hand and it was noticeably missing something in common with the ripping of flesh from someone’s face. Blood. A closer look would have told her it was latex and just as Doctor Nate Harder was about to disappear through the doors in which he came from he turned to look her in the eye. Half of his face had been ripped off into her hand, revealing a second and different face beneath it. He grinned at her. Gold tooth shining in the fluorescence of the theatre lights. “GET HIM!” KRA-KOOM! boomed the first bullet it, as it burst through the Perspex glass of the door Harder just disappeared through. “His face,” Catherine muttered in disbelief. “I've got... I've got his face.” The second man pushed past her, knocking her to the ground in a state of confusion before remembering there was a patient bleeding to death on the table. The two men burst through the door hunting the man she was not so sure to be the famous Cardio Thoracic surgeon, Nate Harder. The first of DiMaggio's lackeys burst out into the hall, gun arced and ready to fire at will. He was big and burly, all shoulder with little neck to hold his over-sized head up. He looked a sight in the surgical scrubs, a health conscious precaution of the allusive Henry DiMaggio. The man even had his expensive Italian leather loafers protected by shower caps to prevent the spreading of unwanted visitors to Henry DiMaggio's operating table. Once in the hall, he turned to his left. They always turn to their left. It's the natural instinct of a right handed person. When he spun to his right, the surgeon drove his elbow down onto the wrist of his gun toting hand, causing his hand to open involuntarily and he dropped the weapon the ground. Poor instincts told the henchman to lunge for his weapon. Diving toward the ground, the surgeon's knee rose with precision and a guttural roar escaped Nate's throat. The force of his knee drove straight through his attacker's jaw with such a ferocity that his downward motion was diverted upward. Momentum carried him backward and sat firmly upon his ass, giddy and dazed, he could only blink blankly while his brain caught up with proceedings. Blood poured down his chin and dripped into his lap as Harder recoiled onto his back foot and struck him, point blank, with a devastating Buzzsaw kick in the ear. THUD! went the wall when the assailant's head bounced off it, leaving a hole in the plaster as a souvenir of his quick visit. Awkwardly, he folded forward over his body, unconscious from the stiff and devastating strikes the surgeon delivered in what seemed like the merest of moments. The door burst open again, the second of Henry DiMaggio's men peered out into the hall looking just as stupid in his surgical get-up as his predecessor. The first thing he noticed was his partner unconscious on the floor in a heap. His mouth fell open, gaping at the idea a man he knew to be so competent were able to be taken down so quickly, complete with weapon in hand. He glanced left and right, there was no sign of the doctor. Dropping to one knee, gun still cocked and eyes still fleetingly glancing back forth down the hall, he checked his partner's pulse. He was still alive. The cavalry came pouring out of halls and doors, nurses and doctors alike, all with one common goal. To get to the Day Procedure Theatre before the man on the table bled out and they had a Code-4 on their hands. The last thing the Shady Acres Retirement Village needed was a dead gangster headlining the newspapers, especially one getting illegal surgery for an undisclosed amount of money. Dead gangsters bring police. Police bring enquiries and investigations. The Shady Acres medical crew would be doing everything they could to keep Henry DiMaggio alive. Rising to his feet, DiMaggio's man plucked his mobile from his pocket and pushed a few buttons, inspected it was doing the job it was supposed to before putting it to his ear. The look on his face was a mixture of anticipation and disappointment as opposed to anger for not catching the man who tried to put his boss inside a box. “Yeah. This is Grima. Someone just tried to lay a hit on DiMaggio.” “Any idea who tried it on?” “Got a good idea.” The silence was encouraging for Grima to speak on. “Fiend. Could only be him.” “How the fuck did he get in? Did he get him?” “He's flippin' around on the table as we speak. Doc's are doin' their thing now. I reckon it's touch and go. There's a lot'f fucken blood, yeah.” “Let's hope this is a problem that goes away. When I find Fiend...” “Yeah. I know. I'll give that motherfucker a kiss right on his forehead.” “Me too.” Grima snapped shut the phone and tended his partner again, trying to sit him up against the wall, pinning him against it with one hand as he glanced the corridors for some assistance. Just at first glance, his partner's jaw looked a right mess. Possibly broken. Fractured at the very least. Shaking his head with disbelief, an unavoidable smirk rose across Grima's face. He couldn't comprehend how Fiend could manage to put together an operation like this. How he could get in undetected, posing as one of the nation's most decorated Cardio Thoracic surgeons. He was baffled beyond recognition but he knew that after this, Henry DiMaggio would be lying lower than his reputation if he ever recovered. Silently, Grima hoped Fiend had got the job done. Hoped and prayed. “Where are you, Fiend? Where the fuck are you?”
