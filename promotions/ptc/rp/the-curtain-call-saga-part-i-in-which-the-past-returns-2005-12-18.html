The Curtain Call Saga: Part I, In Which the Past Returns

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 [Brief OOC note: I know its frowned upon to do something that involves past material, but I’m really only in this thing in order to have motivation to finish DS’s story up since his death and put the character to rest. My IG stuff was very well received and read by enough people that I figured I could take the chance and do a continuation of the arc.] DarkStar shook the cobwebs out of his head and got his first good look at the man in the expensive suit. He had shoulder length blonde hair and his features were slightly feminine but were absolutely flawless. The man was the definition of perfection. He realized whose presence he had found himself in and cursed himself. He knew beneath that expensive blazer and designer dress shirt, the man wore two scars where his wings were rent from his back "Long time," DarkStar forced, "But where would divine mercy be found in the halls of anything you lay your hands upon?" With that the man snickered, and this time it wasn't sinister. It was warm and foreboding, "Why Drazail, you should know that God is not the only one capable of offering divinity and promise. I will answer your questions in due time, and if I still deem it fit I may even offer you the mercy I am prepared to give. So for now you are safe." DarkStar was satisfied. He had no other options, without his powers he was stuck here, and he needed the answers of why things ended up this way. So although he was not entirely trusting of the man, he accepted his hand and was helped to his feet. One way or another he would find out the truth, even if he had to accept Lucifer Morningstar's divine mercy to do it. DarkStar stood up and simply glared at Lucifer. This being had been a thorn in his side for years. Once he gave up the keys of hell he simply blended into the rest of the peons on Earth. It was always assumed that Lucifer was the devil, the prince of lies and the bringer of misfortune. This was all false. Lucifer was simply in the wrong place at the wrong time and given a shitty job. Ruling over the kingdom of hell for all eternity was not exactly on his to-do list when God willed him into existence all those millennia ago. The ‘devil made me do it’ is a phrase made up by psychopaths and murderers as an excuse. Never once did Lucifer make any human do anything they didn’t already want to do, sure, in a few cases he gave them a nudge in the right (or wrong) direction in order to really piss off God… but that was all in his warped sense of fun. A sense of fun that DarkStar found repulsive. But to each his own. The true bad blood between the two exists because of the past treatment of the Dream King’s predecessors, which had been documented in mediums past and will not be delved into here, where Lucifer was less than hospitable. Years after DarkStar had raised the mantle of his position again he had made an attempt to mend the bridge between his Kingdom and the Kingdom of Hell, but once again he was treated as little more than a unwanted guest. Really, the hatred between the two was fairly petty, but the hatred did exist. This is what made this meeting so perplexing to DarkStar. "What do you want from me?" Lucifer smirked, "Nothing Drazail, I simply felt the need to aide a fallen warrior. Despite your disrespect you at the very least deserved a few moments in the sun. If you would have only followed Loki outdoors you would have seen that this area is very accommodating." None of this actually mattered to DarkStar, Lucifer’s intentions were not his main concern. "Where is Talena, if you have done anything to her…" The Morningstar held his hand up in order to keep DarkStar from saying anything he would regret, he was powerless here, "This may come as a shock, but I have not one clue to her whereabouts. She is mortal and beyond the reach of my powers, I have risked overextending myself and revealing myself to others that I would rather not be revealed to simply by giving you this bountiful gift." "Gift?" spat DarkStar "What gift is this? A pathetic pocket universe. I wonder where I truly am- possibly in some forgotten corner of whatever dilapidated section of the InBetween you are calling home now?" Lucifer shook his head, "I am being patient with you right now Dream Lord, or should I say ex-Dream Lord. You very well know I spend my time on Earth amongst the useless, I keep myself out of the gods realms. Their self-importance sickens even me." "Something can sicken the main purveyor of debauch? Surprise, surprise." DarkStar interrupted. "No, you are in no realm of the InBetween. In short, your destruction was felt across the universe to all peoples in tune to such events. I simply plucked what remained of your consciousness out of that maelstrom and gave you a place to stay. It would take me longer to explain this places existence than it would for you to meet your fate." DarkStar looked around himself, noting the rather boring and bland hospital décor. "From the ruler of Hell to the creator of an uninspired ethereal hospital. And you say I’m the mighty one that fell?" The ire of Lucifer had finally been raised, "You speak harsh words for a man who was so easily handled by that arrogant fuck the Catholics call God! You know that I cast my rule of hell down by my own volition. At least I believed so at the time." Lucifer had indeed given up the keys to hell long ago, he was tired and worn from the infinite taxation that hell took. He had gained enough power to simply disappear from amongst the demons, closing hell and casting its evils back into the InBetween. The chaos that ensued was great, the former demons attempted a war against Heaven that was quickly squashed. Some returned to Earth and caused unspeakable crimes against an unsuspecting humanity. In the end God had the last laugh, claiming the keys for himself and placing two of his choir in control of the gates. All in the name of balance. "I realize my mistake now, Drazail, and I attempt to atone for it." DarkStar raised an eyebrow, "And what of your thousands of past mistakes are you attempting atonement for?" Lucifer once again ignored DarkStar’s sarcastic approach to this problem and continued, "When I gave up my kingdom, I believed it would be the last thing HE would expect. One last ‘fuck you’ so to speak. In the years since, I realized that it was what set off this chain reaction that has seen your rather violent… disassembly. I believe that he took back Hell and destroyed the balance. He has been drawing power from the sinners." The severity of that statement was setting in, "And trust me, Drazail, there are plenty more sinners than believers. You would be surprised by how many of his devout are actually earmarked for the deepest levels of Hell. With both sources leading to himself, he had more power than he had had since before Original Sin." "But why the genocide against the old gods?" DarkStar still did not understand fully, why would God himself need more power? "The old gods died out themselves, he simply annexed their former kingdoms as his own. He used them to draw what little power they had left into himself, many of the Egyptian gods had felt that fate and by now none but Ra stand fully. Not even God can rightly unmake the power of the Sun." These answers were still leading nowhere, and DarkStar had grown tired of this hospital setting. "What need does God have for more power? For the eradication of the old gods? He already has the power that matters. He has the minds of the people more so than any of us had ever gained." There was a moment of silence as Lucifer collected his thoughts. The knowledge he had since gained had cost him much, and he knew that no matter what he said he would never convince DarkStar that his true intentions were actually to keep his essence alive for long enough to discover a way that could bring him back- bring him back at a strength that would help him combat what was going to happen. For a second his concentration waned and the hospital he created out of mist rippled ever so slightly. "Drazail, the old gods have felt your death and are rallying what is left of their numbers to attempt the suicide mission against Heaven and its hosts… but that is not what worries me, that is not the cause of my guilt. Their lives are not of importance, but it is their power that is at stake here. The cause of my guilt is HIS true plan…" Lucifer froze and a fire blazed in his eyes. He hated the feeling of guilt, and he hated its cause. God was more evil than he was, and not half the deity in Lucifer’s own opinion. As a ‘god’ he shouldn’t be tied to petty human emotion. The fire in his eyes blazed greater than it had since he was cast into Hell by God himself. "The universe has grown beyond even his control, even his vision. He wishes to unmake his own creation. He wishes to destroy it all." Whatever sarcasm and whatever fight was left in DarkStar was removed. The former Dream King had suspicions that God was harvesting power for a terrible reason, but he assumed the reason would be against his people, not against the very people that provided God himself with his powers. God had grown so angry that his creation had blossomed beyond his control that he would rather see it taken out of existence instead of watching it flourish. "How… How do you know this? How can this be possible?" It was then that Lucifer stepped aside, revealing a figure that had quite literally been standing in his shadow. Somebody from both of their pasts, and somebody that would pain DarkStar to see as much as it had pained Lucifer to consort with them again. DarkStar’s eyes widened as he gazed upon the person who stepped from behind Lucifer. That same crooked smile… that same malevolent look… most importantly, that same insane costume. Apparently death was far less permanent than he had once been led to thought. "’Ello mon amour. Et ‘as been an eternity, ‘as it not?" Despite having his voice returned to him, if only for these moments, DarkStar was speechless.
