Date: 2005-04-30 Title: Redemption Chapter 2: The Beginning I can still hear the sounds of the buzzing of bees as they flock from flower to flower; eagerly extracting what available nectar was in them. The whispers of the wind as they breeze by the leaves, accompanied by the singing of the birds gliding through the sky with apparent ease. The glow of the evening sun as it slowly sank beyond the horizon, streaking the deep blue sky with fragments of yellow and orange. The luscious carpet of green grass as they lay upon the ground with their blades held high. Nature seemed at its finest when you are about to die. The minutes seemed to slow down almost to a complete stop, but yet somehow you knew that the seconds were still ticking by without care, as if impatient to call upon your time in this world to an end. I know how it is to feel that way now. Lying here in this empty worn bed, just waiting for the dealer of death to finally make its call. But yet, I can somehow hear the faint steps of his feet as he trampled through the streets making his way here to get me, to escort me up to the heights of eternity where I will stand for the rest of time. You meanwhile, Alexander, have still some way to go before I can finally see you again. To hold you in my arms, watching your crystal blue eyes beam brightly in the morning sunlight. I don’t know where to start to describe that magical feeling to you. One day, when you have a child, you’ll know. To be able to watch him grow up from a young boy to an honorable man, that feeling can never be exchanged for any value of money nor anything else that this world can give. I have achieved that feeling, and now I must go. And I will go with a smile, knowing that I’ve already done what I had been set out to do in this world. In closing, I forgive you for all the sins and mistakes that you’ve committed in your life thus far. Just fulfill my dream for me, that is all I ask. As for your mother, I forgive her too. She makes mistakes just like everyone else did, and I will not blame her for that. I can now feel that my time is almost up. I wish I can spend another hour with you, but time can be cruel sometimes. Until eternity beckons your way… With love, David. At that time that he had read the letter by his father’s deathbed, he ignored the usage of the present tense when his father described his mother. But now, it stuck out like a sore thumb in his mind. He could still remember the letter word for word, replaying in his memories like a broken record seeking for an end. “It can’t be true, she’s dead.” Alexander snarled and looked up, his blue eyes glistened menacingly. “She died while giving birth to me. My father told me that.” Alexander clenched his fists until it turned pale from the lack of blood. Michael Rivers stood up and simply shrugged his shoulders. “I only pass assignments, Alexander. I don’t go checking on those people in these folders to see if they are still alive or not.” He walked across the room to the door and opened it, beckoning Alexander to leave. “I think you better go. Right now, time is precious to you. You have seventy-two hours to complete your assignment. Like always.” Without a word, Alexander paced himself out of the room with a cold stare directed at Michael. And then he stopped. Turned. “Wait for me.” ========== Jessica Knight rummaged through the stack of files on her desk, trying to find a certain folder containing valuable information in relation to the latest series of suicide killings. But in her exasperation, it seemed to have simply disappeared. “Can someone please get me another copy of Willow’s file!” She screamed from her desk to her secretary outside her room. “I want all information including family background and whatever else you can get your hands on.” Something about Alexander Willow intrigued her, but she could not put her finger down on what. She pursed her lips and heaved herself down on her black leather chair, folding her arms across her chest. She had gone through his file yesterday, and something seemed out of place. “Here you go, Miss Knight.” Her secretary spoke in front of her, with a smile on her face, while slowly placing the white folder at the side of her messy desk. “Thanks,” Jessica muttered without looking up, “Now get me the profiles of each of the suicide victims.” As her secretary walked out, Jessica reached for Alexander’s folder and flipped it open. Ten minutes passed as she slowly read through the whole folder again, trying to memorize each and every detail that had something to do with Alexander’s family. “Everything seems fine, but I can still feel that something is wrong.” She muttered to herself, rereading the folder again from scratch. Then she suddenly slammed the folder on the table, and stood up straight. Her eyes widened as she stared blankly across the room. “According to David, his wife Christine died while giving birth to Alexander.” She paced around her room, her steps quickening with each word. “If that’s the case, then why does Christine Willow have parking tickets issued under her name just over a year ago in the state of California?” Jessica wiped the beads of sweat on her forehead as she called for her secretary again. “Give me all details relating to the name Christine Willow. Make it fast.” ========== “I didn’t think that you would come back.” Chelsea spoke, smiling from behind her desk as Alexander entered the door of the well-known specialist. Alexander refused a reply, and managed to force out a smile as he offered himself a seat. “I just came here for my test results, that’s all.” “Like I said Alexander, the results that keeps coming in will always be the same.” Chelsea replied, wrinkling her forehead. “So there’s no improvement at all?” “None. And there won’t be as long as you are still wrestling and putting your body and health in danger.” Chelsea replied shortly. He fought off the urge for a sarcastic reply to the already often-debated topic of him wrestling. “So how long do I have left?” Alexander muttered under his breath, just loud enough for Chelsea to hear. “You mean how long you have before you die? Not long at all.” He sat back and leaned against the chair. “Give me a rough timeframe.” With his arms folded, he looked directly into Chelsea’s eyes. “A couple of years if you’re lucky. Either way, you’ll still die before your time.” “Thank you.” With that, Alexander stood up and left the room and subconsciously a tear flowed down Chelsea’s cheek. “Goodbye.” ========== “Do you have the profiles yet?” Jessica Knight questioned from inside her room, projecting her voice to her secretary outside. “Almost done.” The reply came almost simultaneously, as Jessica heaved a sigh of exasperation. Patience was never one of her strong points. Another ten minutes and her secretary stepped in again with a bunch of folders. “There you go.” She spoke while handing over the stack. Jessica didn’t bother with a reply and just slammed her door shut. And there she spent the next five hours pondering over each folder, furiously making rough notes on her notepad. After the hours passed, she slammed her herself against the table in sheer tiredness, her head bouncing off the wooden surface. Her eyes looked up, and stared at her notepad. After a quick glance over, she smiled to herself. Her initial observation was correct. All the suicide victims wrote a confession note in some way or form for their past sin, before taking their own lives. But somehow that still didn’t make sense to her. Why did everyone want to take their own lives at the same period of time? How did Alexander Willow come into the picture? Why was his presence there in each and every suicide scene that had happened in that city for the past couple of weeks? More importantly, how did Alexander Willow make them kill themselves? “Miss Knight?” Her secretary’s voice spoke from the door as she popped her head in. “What is it?” Jessica replied, annoyed that her secretary had burst her bubble of thought at the wrong moment. “I got the information you wanted on Christine Willow.” With that, Jessica’s eyes beamed with excitement. She grabbed the files and rushed through it, her eyes darting back and forth trying to digest all the information on it. And then her face grew pale. Almost white. About twenty-five years ago, Christine had changed her name to Rachel. But the parking tickets still used her former name because she never bothered to change her driving license. Christine had changed her name right about the time that Jessica was born. And this was before Alexander was born. Two years before. She wouldn’t have put herself in the picture if it weren’t for the fact that Christine had now changed her address to hers. That wouldn’t even really bother her all that much. But right now, it did. Christine Willow had changed her name to Rachel Knight. And to her recent memory, Rachel Knight is her mother. ========== She stared at herself in the mirror, her blue eyes glazed in deep memory. With slow acute precision, she combed through her light blonde hair, observing the apparent wrinkles that appeared on her once beautiful face. She smiled to herself, as the memories flowed though her veins, reaching out to her from within. A sad smile, but a smile nonetheless. “Hello mother.” A voice spoke from behind her. She turned her head, and looked directly at her daughter’s face. Jessica had tears streaking down her eyes, staining her beautiful face with sadness. “You’re back early today, dear.” Rachel replied, trying to ignore the tears on Jessica’s face. “What’s wrong?” “I need to know the truth, mother.” Jessica urged, taking a step closer to Rachel. Her eyes were pleading Rachel for some form of mercy, to tell her that everything was just a big mistake made by the database. Rachel looked down at her palms, and sighed. She knew that this day would finally come, but she didn’t expect that it would arrive so soon. “I’ll explain everything.” She whispered. “There’s nothing to explain, Rachel.” A voice spoke from behind Jessica. Jessica turned around only to find Alexander standing by the doorway, his shoulders leaning against the wall. His eyes had a form of intensity that made her shudder. He stood up straight and walked past Jessica, ignoring her presence in the room. “My mother is dead. She died while giving birth to me.” “I am your mother.” Rachel replied, gazing straight at Alexander’s crystal blue eyes as tears welled up in her eyes. “No!” Alexander refused her words flatly, his face tensing up in emotional pain. “Your name has been written in my folder, so you have a sin that you need to confess to. So confess it now, and let my work be done.” “Yes, I do have something to confess.” Rachel replied, as Jessica’s eyes widened in apparent shock. “I killed David a couple of years ago. I poisoned him to his death.” Alexander jerked his head at those words, and in my swift movement, flipped his jacket at pointed the barrel of a gun straight at the temple of Rachel. “You killed my father?” He whispered menacingly. Jessica almost fainted in fright as she saw the appearance of a deadly weapon. Rachel meanwhile, kept her calm, ignoring the coldness of the metal pressed against her forehead. “David’s not your father.” ==========
