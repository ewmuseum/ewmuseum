Date: 2005-12-19 His blood splatters up on my face. Overwhelming him. Overpowering him. My white knuckles become red with his cries to stop. Beating him. Destroying him. They are screaming. All I hear is the sick pounding of flesh to my fist. Killing him. Killing him. Killing him. … I love waking up to find my Sunday papers posted through my door. I love the sensation of just getting out the shower and feeling completely clean. I love watching a film on mute and guessing what they are saying. But I also love Ketamin. I wake on this Sunday morning to my newspaper at my front door. You have 2 new messages. First Message. "Steve, please pick up. Don’t do this to us. Think about the ba-" Message deleted. "Hey Steve, this is Doctor John Garner. I was given your telephone number by the officials at the WWA. They want us to have a chat. Drop by at my clinic sometime. The WWA have my details waiting for you. Hope to see you soon." Looks like im not the only one who thinks I have a problem. Better get onto that problem. Breakfast first. Chefs speciality. Ketamin delight followed by Jacks ole’ cough medicine. How life has fucked me up. … "The blood samples we took have some disturbing results Steve." Surprise me. "We found a high amount of Ketamin, Steroids, Cocaine, and a alcohol content ten times above the average person." Still waiting for that surprise. "I am astounded that you have not had any real problems with this kind of abuse to your body." Thank you, I say. I hate clean rooms. Unnerves me. I don’t believe anything can be completely white and clear. I can feel the sweat appearing above my brow. Just give me my dopamine release. "The head office at the WWA say they wont let you compete in this kind of condition. They want you cleaned up." I wish he would stop with things I already knew. "What I have devised steve, is a 12 step plan…" Admit we are powerless. Believe god can restore us to sanity. Put our lives into the care of god…because he understands us. I forget the rest. "Your first meeting is at 7 in the Matthew Street church. For now, take these. They should stop your cravings for the drugs for a little while, as you get into the support group regime again." Ironic how they give drug addicts more drugs. The fact that he is trying to help me beat a problem I don’t want to fix isn’t ironic. It’s a joke. Because the joke is we all have the same punch line. I just seem to have it coming a lot sooner then most of this fucking world. … Tick tock. There goes the clock. Death creeping ever closer. Tick Tock. Tick Tock. And here is my name badge. "Big fella’ you eh?" Nah I wear stilts, I say. Sarcasm never goes down well at these support groups. Its all very truthful. So they add a fucker like me into the mix. For shits and giggles I guess. "Ok, I’d like to welcome you all tonight. What we are hoping is to give each other the support we need, to get through these painful times we are in." And there is me thinking it was for the free coffee. Some ugly guy stands up to my left. I can smell his breath from here. Southern Comfort and no toothpaste is a killer for chicks. He is not fat, just that beer belly is giving the impression. That shirt just shouts out alcoholic at me. "My names Henry…And I have an alcohol problem." His voice sounded like a cat had been put in a washing machine. "I don’t know when I started to drink…I think I was about 12 or 13. But my older brother came to me one night, with a silver flask in his hand. He just urged me to take a swig. I didn’t want to…I begged him not to make me. But he forced it on me. And I enjoyed it…I really did." Ugly wasn’t the word id describe him as. Hideous maybe. "Ever since then, I have moved onto harder drink, just to get that kick again. At the moment, im drinking whisky and vodka a lot. But I need help…I need your help to beat this. Thanks for listening." They all start clapping that slow forced clap. Christ I hate these things. Always the same. I am John. Im an alcoholic. I can not stop. I am George Im an alcoholic. I can not stop. The real victory is if you can help yourself. And the fact that these shits can’t do such a simple thing makes me sick. "Never been to one of these before…kind of know why after that sad sack of shit before me." It was like someone farted in church. I lifted my head up to see the shocked faces all looking at this one man on the stage. All he done was crack a smile. "Well, im Frank. Frank Huntington. I have a well paid job, and I spend that money on enjoying myself. My employers sent me here to tackle the alcohol; but to be quite blunt, there is no problem to be fixed. But hey, they pay the bills…" He looked like I would imagine myself to be in within 10 years. Short greying hair, smartly dressed. Seemed healthy. The murmurs started around me. "Excuse me, I am not finished." His voice grew in stature. Im starting to like this dude. "I’d like to say one thing to you all today. You are here because you want to help yourselves. But why do you need other people to help yourself? Couldn’t you just sit at home, and look at yourself…and just stop?" My words coming out of his mouth. "I want you to all go home, and help yourselves, for fuck sake." Everyone left that meeting with a new sense of hope, coming from one person that they most likely wanted to emulate. As they all stood, I just watched as Frank went to grab his long black coat; the typical business man type. I become an obstacle in his path. "Excuse me, Steven." He focused on my name badge. "I’ve got places to be." Like what you said up there, I say. "Glad to hear it, now I’ve go-" The real victory in life if "You can help yourself." He cuts me down with a smile. "What’s your real name?" Read the name badge, I say. "Man of honesty, I see. Why you hear Steven?" Like you, I say. "Employers are a bitch." He smiled as he picked out a card in his pocket. "Here." Frank Huntington 56 Cedar Avenue 088-177-277-367 "Give me a ring in the next few days, and we shall chat a bit more." His hand rests on my shoulder as he brushes past. He whispers in my ear. "I know that alcohol isn’t the problem here. And I can help fix it." And then he left. Gone. Me with a piece of card and a sense of hope for the first time in years. … I often what people do to kill time. Do they just sit there, thinking? Do they shake their leg or tap their foot? Do they watch TV programs they hate? Do they read a book? Do they inject heroin? Do they go on the internet? Do they wack off to porn? Or like me. Do they realise that death is inevitable, so why try to prevent it? I also realised why I hated fancy fucking restaurants. Caviar this, Lobster that. Whatever happened to good old fish and chips? "Can I take your order Sir?" I don’t know if he was patronizing me with that tone, or he was just an annoying fuckwit. Someone else coming, I say. I hope. It was 3 days since I met Frank. 3 whole days of thinking about what he said. My mind was playing havoc with those final words. Hope is a word I don’t use often. But hope is all I had for Frank to fix me. "Sorry I’m late, traffic was appalling out there." Not quite the shining armour like in the fairytales, but he is here now anyway. "I’m glad you turned up." If only he knew. "Are you gentlemen ready to order?" … "You know Steve; it all stems from your anger inside." He scoffed his words, as I catch a piece of half eaten Lobster in my eye. "That’s why you let yourself get into this cycle." Three meetings with Frank led to a conclusion I could of told him myself. I am Steven Murphy. I have anger pent up inside of me. I do drugs because of this. Nothing of any Nostradamus level revelation yet. "I know what you can do to stop this." The words i wanted to hear for so long. "You need to give it your all in GTT." My jaw didn’t completely drop with shock. But I was certainly surprised. Its only a little job really, I say. "It can start a new cycle for you Steve. Instead of this drug ridden road your on now, you can take a new path. You can direct all this anger you have, and take it out on your obstacles in this event." Maybe he is right. "You got Jack Murphy haven’t you?" Think so, I say. "He is a big fish in this isn’t he?" Cue shrug of shoulders, expression of I don’t give a shit. "Then this can be your escape Steve." Looking into his eyes I could see he was saying some truth. "Beat him…and you can beat your demons." He pulled out that phone that always seemed to ring. "Well, I’m going to have to cut this meal short Steve." I stood up to shake his hand. I had the sudden urge to spurt out words I haven’t said in a long time. Thank you. He just smiled that smile again. "You can beat your demons." And then he was gone. And the cheeky bastard didn’t leave money for the bill. … Brushing my teeth, I saw that handsome fellow in the mirror. Look at his talk, dark, scar ridden head. What a sexy beast. Life had become a little easier to handle since Franks advice to say the least. Ring. Ring. Hello, I say. "I would be weary of Frank, Mr. Murphy." I didn’t recognize the voice. Who is this? "You will learn soon enough. But I’d like you to turn your attention to your post." I scanned across the hallway, and seen the usual bills, but with one red letter. Who the hell is this? "Pick your friends carefully Mr. Murphy." Click. Hello? Hello? Anybody there? The writing is rushed on the envelope as I tear it open. WWA Head Offices 12 Grange way Square New York City New York To Mr Huntington We are enquiring how you are dealing with the issue of Mr Steven Murphy. Have you changed his Focus onto the GTT5 Tournament yet? It is essential that you do, because it is vital that all our WWA roster members participating in the tournament will progress far. The fact that the WWA brand name is being represented in this tournament is a big factor, and the erratic Murphy cannot be a weak link for the WWA. You will receive the next part of your payment when we have seen visible improvements in the attitude and commitment of Murphy in his upcoming match-up against Mr Jack Murphy of AWC. Yours WWA Head Office Used. Abused. Liar. Liar. Liar. … "Glad you’re here Steve." He outstretched his hand, and I felt like spitting on it. I need to ask you something, I say. "Shoot." Judas. Liar. Traitor. Are you working for the WWA? And then I could see his eyes shake. Tremble. Fear. And then I snapped. His blood splatters up on my face. Overwhelming him. Overpowering him. My white knuckles become red with his cries to stop. Beating him. Destroying him. They are screaming. All I hear is the sick pounding of flesh to my fist. Killing him. Killing him. Killing him. Used. Abused. I was a puppet. And he was pulling the strings for WWA.
