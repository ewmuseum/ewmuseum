Squideon's Tale (Gauntlet Roleplay)

24 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler: Squideon/Naked Squideon Fed: GLOBAL Squideon's Tale The Origin of Squideon Two weeks ago... [The Horny Mathematician is in his lab cackling like a mad idiot. It's dark and stormy outside. Lightning flashes once, then again a few seconds later. Now, thunder booms from the heavens. And his creation rises alive.] Horny Mathematician: It's alive! [Darth von Doom enters the room upon hearing the proclamations of his mad master. Darth von Doom is in his full Sith get-up and has blood stains all over his uniform. After those pesky Killer Pirates escaped from his clutches yet again, he killed Crab Man and Lobster Man, the two minions he sent to kill the Pirates. After they failed, well, let's just say they had a little lightsaber punishment that took their lives. Treachery is the way of the Sith. All familiar with it's culture should know this. Darth von Doom, however, is less reminiscing over his self-slaughtered comrades and more interested in what the Mathematician is cackling about.] Darth von Doom: What is it, Master? What have you done this time? [The Mathematician turns and smiles. He then notices the blood stains all over von Doom's outfit. He motions toward him like a woman who doesn't want coffee stains on her carpet.] Mathematician: What is that mess? [Darth von Doom looks down and notices the blood stains.] Darth von Doom: It's blood, Master. Mathematician: I can God damn well see that. But whose blood is it? Please tell me you killed the Pirates this time. Please tell me they didn't escape. [Darth von Doom answers without hesitation.] Darth von Doom: How can I when I get stuck working with insolent fools like Crab Man and Lobster Man. They let the Pirates escape by their bumbling antics and crustacean stupidity. They let the Pirates escape, so I squezzed the life from their bodies using the Force and watched them die at my hands. [The Mathematician can only shake his head.] Mathematician: Can't you go through one day without killing any of my minions? [Darth von Doom shakes his head.] Darth von Doom: No, Master. They are all fools! Your Circus of DOOM~! is more like a Circus of Idiots. [The Mathematician sighs and shakes his head.] Mathematician: Well, maybe you're right. But in any event, there is something I have created that may change your mind about part-man, part-sea creature monstrosities forever. [Darth von Doom doesn't seem to pleased.] Darth von Doom: So what have you created this time? [The Mathematician smiles evilly. Not the "I just stole a million dollars" robber smile, but instead the "I just came up with a plan to kill a million people and hold the world ransom for one billion dollars" Dr. Evil kind of smile.] Mathematician: I have something new to show you. [The Mathematician walks over to a portion of his lab where that is pitch black. But in the blackness, something is moving. A shadow, to be exact. The shadow of me. I am the creature pacing back and forth. For I am the secret the Mathematician is about to show Darth von Doom. This was the moment of my conception. This was the moment I got my name. The Mathematician, my creator, goes over to a lamp on the nearby desk and turns it on, lighting the entire side of the room where I am standing. Darth von Doom looks pleased. He should be. He's looking at me. Hi, that's me. The guy with the Squid head and the human body. My creator cackles and motions to me.] Mathematician: Behold! I give you...SQUIDEON! Half-man, half-squid, all evil. And he's ours! BWAHAHAHAHAHAHA! [Darth von Doom crosses his arms.] Darth von Doom: Impressive. [Deep down, I know that he is already debating on how long it's going to take him to kill me for screwing up one of his missions. I heard the Mathematician talking with his assistant Igor about this von Doom character. Apparently, he's a real badass. He turned on just about everybody he knew and turned homicidal. He was apparently a weird, eccentric, and demented young man before he went temporarily insane and tried to make everything into his own sick, twisted image of what the world should be. He then was shot and killed by a deranged fan, resurrected by the Mathematician, killed all of his friends at the behest of the Mathematician, had his limbs cut off and was burned, and then was saved by a life-sustaining body suit that is heavily armored. Yes, I knew it already. This would be the man who would be out to kill me for the rest of my days.] * * * Command Room Two Days Ago... [I am in the Command Room at the Jobber Temple with my fellow Circus of DOOM~! allies Bizarro Freak, the Used Car Salesman, the Creature from Beyond Space, Cow Man, Bear Man, Moose Man, and Worm Man. They are all standing around the control panels working away until Cow Man gtes hungry. He rubs his belly with his hoof.] Cow Man: Moo! [Everyone looks over, including myself.] Used Car Salesman: Huh? Bizarro Freak: I think he's hungry. Squideon: Oh great. The cow's hungry. So what do we do now? [The Used Car Salesman smiles at me. I know what he's going to say and then he says something like what I had thought he would say. But he says it so cheesily, I want to go buy a car or something.] Used Car Salesman: We send you yo go get the food for us. Bizarro Freak: Snack run! ["Oh great" I say to myself. "Food run"...what is this, college? Wait, I never went to college. Does that make me illiterate? Am I uneducated? Yes, I am. So what? So are all of these other freaks. Just then, the Used Car Salesman pulls out a pen and paper and starts taking orders.] Cow Man: Moo! Used Car Salesman: Okay. You want hay, so yeah. Bizarro Freak: Bring me back some Fritos and a pack of Miller Lite. Used Car Salesman: (writing) Hay, Miller Lite, Fritos... Bear Man: Growl! Moose Man: Argh! Worm Man: Stthhh... [The Used Car Salesman writes feverishly.] Used Car Salesman: Okay. Fish, grains, and dirt. Okay. Anything else? Creature from Beyond Space: I want some sushi. Used Car Salesman: (writing) Sushi...got it. And bring me back some Cheetos. [He then stops writing and hands me the list. I look at the list and then look at him.] Used Car Salesman: Now go get it and make haste. There apparently are Ninjas all over the Island and they're attacking our zombie troops! We can't stop them, so double time it. [I salute him sarcastically.] Squideon: Sir, yes sir. [That draws some giggles from my brethren. The Used Car Salesman looks like smoke is gonna come out of his ears. I turn and exit the control room to go to the store and pick us up some grub. Little do I know that this is the pivotal moment of my life. And the moment that saves it as well.] * * * The Last Survivor Later that Evening... [I'm in the forest drinking my Slurpee as I head back with everyone's food, which I am carrying. AFter drinking too much of my Slurpee at once, I get a brain freeze. Wowzers...that didn't feel good. Anyway, I come back through the forest and see Ninjas surrounding the Temple entrance talking with Darth von Doom and the Killer Pirates! Now there's a shocker, considering they are sworn enemies.] Squideon: What the hell is going on? [I then decide to be a sneaky peeping Tom and listen in to their conversation. So, using my squid skills, I dash through the underbrush and hide close-by so I can hear what they have to say. Darth von Doom has his hands on his hips. I see that the Killer Klown or whatever his name is has his face bandaged up. I think to myself "Maybe he wants to be a mummy." Yeah, right. The pirate guy, Jack or whatever, seems to be a little more sinister than I had thought he would be given the information I was told about him by the Horny Mathematician in the days after my creation. He doesn't seem nice or perky at all. He just seems a little...sinister. I stop thinking and decide to listen in on what they have to say.] Darth von Doom: Find that insolent little squid and crush him like the invertebrate he is. Ninja Leader: Yes, Master. [The Ninja Leader runs off.] Morgan: Lord von Doom, how is one insignificant little minion named Squideon going to effect our grand plan? Darth von Doom: It will because he's the only one who knows of it. He knows about the Death Star the Mathematician made for me and he also knows about what happened here. His comrades are dead. He is the last Circus member. That makes him a threat to us. We must kill him before the Coalition recruits him. He could be a dangerous threat to us. Morgan: He's right, Harlequin. We must kill him. His blood must be spilled by the next nightfall. [My mouth goes dry and I can't move. I'm on their hit list. What should I do now? I decide to wait here until they leave and hope no ninjas see me heading toward the docks to get off this Island. They continue talking as I try to keep from crapping in my pants in fear over what is going to happen to me if they find me.] Darth von Doom: Harlequin, come with me to the Medical Facility. Morgan, you too. We have much to discuss. Dark Harlequin: Rightiando. [They then turn and leave but Darth von Doom angles his head and looks right at the bush I'm hiding behind. Damn Sith Lords and their magical powers of doom! After they are gone, I slowly back up and run into the woods, dropping the food and going on Slurpee Power to reach the docks.] * * * On the Run Yesterday... [I'm a boat now. No, not a good one. One of those ones with like 1500 people on it and it smells. It think it used to be a fishing boat. In any event, it was the only way off the Island of Misfit Wrestlers. Luckily, I made it to the docks while the Ninjas were still scouring the countryside for me. I sniff something in the air and turn around to see some giant fat guy eating a chicken leg. It smells like a cat died.] Squideon: EWWWW! [He farted. Fat ass. I hate people. They all look at me like I'm some kind of freak, but really I'm not. I'm one of them. I'm just on a boat, sitting on my ass as the events transpiring around me change my life for the worse. Who'da thunk it. Me saved by a Slurpee. How sad is that? That is my claim to fame. I sigh and look around at the people on the boat. But the fat guy turns and I can't see anything.] Squideon: Grrr... [I turn back around and shake my head, trying to hold back my anger and think of what my next move should be.] Squideon: Dammit. Okay, I need a plan. So the boat will be in Europe for three hours. Where should I go? What should I do when I get there? I need a plan, now. [Just then, I listen in on a very interesting conversation between some businessman and some hooker he's trying to hook up with. I check out her ass as I look over. Well, squids have to get their action too. I'm only human.] Businessman: I've got two First Class tickets to Las Vegas. [The Hooker looks intrigued.] Hooker: Las Vegas? [The businessman nods his head.] Businessman: Yeah, Las Vegas. The most fun place in the universe! And I would like for you to come with me when we go. Nice ass, by the way. [The Hooker bitch slaps him across the face and he drops the tickets.] Hooker: You asshole! [She storms off and he gets on the ground, trying to find his tickets. He finds one but the other one is missing. He starts looking around, asking people if they have seen his other ticket. He doesn't ask me, though. He should...because I have it my pocket and plan on hiding out in Las Vegas for awhile. That is my plan and I'm sticking with it.] * * * Fitting in with Society Today... [Our hero Squideon, namely me, has been wandering the streets of Las Vegas alone. I don't really fit in with society. These people don't care about me. They look at me like I'm some sort of freak. I walk through the open air market on the corner of Maryland Parkway and Sahara. Las Vegas really is a desolate place. No wonder Darth von Doom turned out how he did. I walk past some Australopithocenes and Hominids as I contiue to make my way through the crowd. Las Vegas is like the Stone Age. Despite being home to the Temple of the Sun and the Temple of the Moon, it's more like the wild, wild west. A dystopian city built in the middle of nowhere. Said to be Paradise, it has in reality become Paradise Lost. As I contemplate the very thought of leaving this place, some big guy in a trenchcoat walks right into me. We bump and he turns and gives me a look.] Man: Watch where your going, freak. Squideon: Pfft... [This coming from a man who looks like a cross between Cave Hulk and Wolfen really makes a lot of sense. So, I kindly smile at him and flip him off before jaywalking across the street. I dodge a stagecoach caravan being pulled by oxen and some stray cows as I walk across the sandy desert pavement and make my way toward my destination. Nordicstroms, the Icelandic apparel outlet that will help me become normal. It's at this point I notice even more gawkers. Fucking cavemen. I hate living with the prehistoric Neanderthals and Hominids that live in this place. The adobes and huts that these people live in are all over the Vegas valley. I walk past some more of them and notice the gawking gaves of two female Neanderthals. I turn and smile at them.] Squideon: Need a picture to savor the moment of our eternal passion? [They laugh and continue walking as I enter Nordicstroms. This place has all kinds of techno-European club wear and clothing, including deer skin parkas and Eskimo mocassins. All the trendy stuff. The denizens shopping here notice me and immediatly I feel out of place. I start looking around and realize that no matter how I dress, I will still be out of place. I then smile and take a shirt. I know what to do now. It suddenly hit me like a lightning bolt. Why should I hide my differentness when I can use it to my advantage? I sneak over to the changing room and close the door behind me.] Squideon: I can do this... (sigh) I can do this... A few minutes later... [A few minutes later, I emerge from the fitting room completely naked. That's right, Naked. My ding-dong out there for all these gawkers to see. The customers turn and scream like they just saw a murder or Godzilla or something along those lines.] Neanderthal Customer: EEEEEKKKKK! A SQUID! Hominid Customer: AAAAAAHHHHHH! A NAKED SQUID! [I laugh to myself and think of that old wrestler. What was his name? The one with the gut, the tattoos, the long grungy hair, and the manboobs.] Squideon: Hmmm... [What was his name? I can't remember. His name rhymed with mine. Who was he? He was funny, he was ugly, he was naked...he was an ugly naked man. But those are a dime a dozen. Grrr...this is going to drive me nuts for days on end if I can't figure out his name. Then it hits me. I remember his name and I modify it to fit my own end. I stand up triumphantly in all my squid glory and proclaim myself to the world!] Squideon: (in a boastful voice) I am Naked Squideon! And soon I will be streaking all over you! [Newly redubbed Naked Squideon, I run past all of the scared customers and flash every single one of those racist freaks. I love it! I love seeing the scared and frightened looks on their faces when I run up to them. I then run out of the store and start running naked down the street, flashing everyone. I love the feel of cold air against my body...that is, until, I remember the "shrinkage" episode from Seinfeld and decide to dash back to my apartment and get inside using my spare key. Good move. Apparently, unbeknownst to me during my streaking, the imperialist police state running the city said I raped three women and will be put to the chair for my misdeeds. I put on my pajamas and decide to go to sleep. I walk into my bedroom and pull the covers down. I slip underneath the covers and close my eyes, dreaming of a better reality than the one I call my own.] [Cut.]
