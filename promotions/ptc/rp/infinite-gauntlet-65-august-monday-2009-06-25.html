His breathing beginning to warp within the hysteria as Scott Stewart tried with all his might to press the kid into some sort of restraint. Monday wailed tremendously, letting his head bow down, trying to use darkness to subside the emotion. He opened his eyes as his head lulled upright, catching a glimpse of his passenger. Screaming for his love yet no sound came out. The hysteria had taken his voice and his shoulder shuddered violently as he could only look at what remained of his true love. He reached across and stroked her lifeless face, running a finger over her eyelids to close them and put her at rest. To let her sleep. Ben Leary stuck the second lot of tranquilisers into Monday’s arm but there was no need to. It was just the icing on the cake and the world as August Monday knew had been ripped from him. Taken right out of the palm of his hand when they had everything going for them and their whole lives ahead of them. Their love had been interrupted. In this world at least.
