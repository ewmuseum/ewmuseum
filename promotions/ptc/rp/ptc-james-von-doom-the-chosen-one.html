The Chosen One

29 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"There is a Chosen One from the wrestling prophecies." [An old, dusty book is shown lying in the sand somewhere. The wind blows the dust all around it.] "It is said he will bring balance between the rudos and the technicos." [The book suddenly flutters open and pages begin turning.] "He will come from the desert and bring equilibrium." [The pages suddenly stop turning and it ends on a picture of said hero.] "His name will be James." [The scene quickly fades out.] * * * THE CHOSEN ONE Across the world, at the exact same time... [The scene opens in James von Doom's pad where James is sitting on his plush couch, chatting on his phone. James is too cheap to use his cellphone at home, so he just taps into the hardline at his pad. This place has been the scene of many von Doom promos and promos involving his friends as well. This is the place where Jon Collingwood kicked his own ass in the garage, where mobsters tried to kill James, where the "Killer Klown from Outer Space" at all his food, and where James lets his friends mooch off him for food and hang out because their lives are just boring without him. James' friends are lurking around somewhere, including DICK~!, Layla, and all their inanimate buddies. This is why James likes hanging out with the Killer Pirates and Eon Blue Apocalypse. They never come here. Anyway, back to subject matter, James is talking to someone on the phone.] JV: (laughs into the phone) "Yes Mister Hayes. I am the Chosen One..." [James listens for a moment.] JV: (laughs) "That's funny man! I didn't know about any prophecy! That's so cool. I mean, think about it. I've been in the business for under 3 years and I've already won 28 championships by my count, that is if you count my 5 second Jobber Championship reign. (pause) You do?" [James cackles evilly.] JV: "They held me back in the NWC because I was too much of a threat to their old veterans. (scolding) THEY HATED ME! BUT I HATE THEM! (snarls) I HATE THEM ALL! They made fun of me and said I sucked. Well, whose sucking now? They're sucking balls somewhere off in the Far East whereas I am here kicking major fucking ass!" [James pauses for a moment to catch his breath and listen to what Hayes has to say. A smile comes across his face.] JV: "I foresee that I will become the greatest of all wrestlers. (laughs) No one will be able to match the sheer determination of James von Doom and the wit I have...forget about it! No one can match up with me one on one, not even Big Ted's Arm! (pause, listens, laughs) A Jedi Knight? A few victories and everyone gets delusions of grandeur. I could care less. I am the GREATEST of all Jedi wrestlers. And that's sad given how much less experience I have than all the old farts that were in the Elimination Chamber." [A long pause as Hayes is yelling into the phone.] JV: "Well, it's not my fault you dug them up from the grave for that match! Everybody knows I could've won that thing! I'm the BEST there is! I'm guided by LOVE now! I CAN'T lose Layla from petty loses to dick suckers or DICK~! or...well, DICK~! I am the greatest wrestler alive today and you can rest assured I will be pwning this company for years to come." [Hayes goes into a long speech on the other side of the phone. James rolls his eyes and sighs, listening to his boss' speech.] JV: "You done? (short pause) Well let me tell you something. You sign me to face Shock and we'll see who the greatest Jedi wrestler is in the universe because I would pwn that twat like a penis pwning a vagina all night long." [James slams the phone down as Jon Collingwood comes lumbering into the room with a shitload of beers in his arms. He walks in and dumps them all on the coffee table in front of James. James gives him the raised eyebrow treatment.] JC: "Sorry dude, but we need to drink some fucking beers!" [Jon is a big, tattooed, army combat uniform wearing maniac who drinks beers, chews tobacco, cusses, shots guns, kills people, thinks that non-hardcore ECW style wrestling is for "fucking pussies," loves violence, has a temper, is a chauvinistic pig redneck, and has been known to speed while driving. He was known as the Drunken Redneck Supreme during his time in the NWC.] JV: "Nice Jon. How many beers do you need?" [Jon smiles.] JC: "I should've bought a keg." [James nods his head and then stares at the massive mountain of beer on his coffee table.] JV: "Oh yeah. I would assume that to be true." [Jon grabs one of the beer cans, which are all Budweisers because, according to Jon, "light beer is for sissies and queers," opens it up, and toasts James.] JC: (holding his beer up) "Cheers to the most hardcore mother fucker in wrestling and the man who actually got me back into the business, James Fucking von Doom! Now drink to that bitches~!" [Jon chugs down the beer in two seconds flat and then crushes the can with his bare hand before throwing it across the room. It hits the wall and then lands in a stacked pile of crushed beer cans left over from yesterday. As said earlier, Jon is over there A LOT.] JV: (motioning toward the pile) "Nice Jon. Now, are you going to hire me a maid or something?" JC: (snickers as he opens up another beer) "Not in this lifetime, pretty boy." JV: "Asshole." JC: (smiles) "Wanker." [Jon chugs down the second beer and repeats the throwing process from before. This time, he belches very loudly.] JV: "Nice." JC: "Thanks." [Just then, Danny Hernandez and Swede Johanssen a.k.a. "The Swede," known as the tag team of Criminal Behavior, walk into the room after playing a round of pool in James' garage (which is something they do at least 4 times a week) and smile.] DH: "Hey guys! Yo James! Thanks for getting us back into wrestling!" Swede: "Oh yeah. Man, I thought our one match would destroy us but man, THANK YOU. The passion is back in the Swede and that's something I thought would never return. For that, I thank you." JV: "Your welcome." [Layla and DICK~! come into the room too. Jon, Danny, and the Swede are all immediately repulsed. Not by Layla, who caused Jon to lick his lips as he stares at her boobies, but DICK~!, who is holding the Huckster in one hand and the Ultra Pleasure 3000 Dildo in his other hand. DICK~! waves to his new "friends."] DICK~!: "HELLO FRIENDS~! ME DICK~! ME "BIG" DICK N. URANUS. ME YOU FRIEND. ME LOVE YOU LONG TIME." [Jon gives James a look and then motions toward DICK~!] JC: "Who the fuck is this fucking dipshit?" [DICK~! smiles.] DICK~!: "ME DICK. ME DICK N. URANUS. ME YOU FRIEND." [Jon shakes his head and turns to James.] JC: "So what's his story." [James takes a deep breath and begins the story.] JV: "He's a retarded, in-bred ex-porn star who I trained to be a wrestler. He was originally going to be in the Crotch Crusaders in the NWC with Wood N. Pecker, but it didn't go through and now he's here as Layla's bodyguard." [Layla waves to them.] Layla: "Hi." JC: "Oh..." JV: "On the bright side, he's already won the UWF Television Championship and beaten Seymour Almasy in his short career." [Jon smiles.] JC: "Nice." [Danny walks over to Layla and tries to play it cool, thinking he has a shot. He brushes his hair back and tries to be Rico Suave with her. He offers his hand.] DH: "Hi. My name is Danny." [Layla brushes him off and walks over to James.] Layla: "Nice try, player." [Swede and Jon start laughing as James cops a devious smile. She goes over and holds James' hand. Danny turns and glares at the Swede, putting his hands on his hips like a pouty pre-schooler.] DH: (angrily) "And what the fuck is so funny?" Swede: (laughing) "Nice going Sir Mix-A-Lot!" [Danny looks pissed as Jon and the Swede continues laughing as James and Layla join in on the fun.] DH: "Seriously, that wasn't that funny." JC: (grinning) "Yes it was Don Juan." DH: "Shut up." [Jon glares at him, about to declare war.] JC: "Don't make me skull fuck you Danny. I swear to God..." [James motions for him to calm down, which he does. James is the clear leader of this band of misfits.] JV: "That's enough, Jon." [Jon turns to James.] JC: "So, anything else we should know about?" [Just then, the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean from PRIME, Eon Blue Apocalypse from HSW, the Porno Power Rangers, Porno Yoda, the Savage Brothers (who along with James and Jon where GHW's Four Horsemen), Space Condom 3000, Molester the Clown, Vigilante (Jon's former partner when he was Judge as the team of Vigilante Justice), and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Robots with Ratbot enter the room with food, drinks, condoms, toilet paper, pinatas, balloons, and dozens of other assorted goodies. They all stop when they see Jon glaring at them.] JC: "Hi." All of them in unison: "HI~!" [Jon turns to James, cracking his knuckles.] JC: "James..." [James shrugs his shoulders.] JV: "What?" [The scene fades out with James wiping his brow. Layla called some of her stripper friends over and that eased the tension Jon had as well as turned the party into a stripper-rama!] [Cut.]
