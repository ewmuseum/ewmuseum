Blood of an Innocent

Date: 2004-09-16 WARNING, THE FOLLOWING ROLEPLAY CONTAINS SCENES OF A GRAPHIC NATURE. The scene opens to that of an open book, next to it lay a piece of paper with a feather quill in an ink well; upon the piece of paper the following is written. --Sorry I wasn’t able to be there in person for the interview that I was scheduled for but unfortunately I am detained at the moment with other things. The book that lay open next to this is something I am beginning to compile about my life and I thought it best in my absence that I leave it here so that you may read it to learn more about me and parts of my past. I know I have a match against Khaled in the first round of GTT 4 and I knew it wouldn’t due to NOT have something from me concerning this, so here it is, once you read it and after my match with Khaled when I come out the victor, I will show you the result of everything that happened in my anointed histories. Be warned, come GTT 4, I will show the same compassion to Khaled that I showed to the women in the history you are about to read. I do hope you enjoy, especially you Khaled. ~~Tarrant --Passage taken from the anointed histories of Ehlexander Tarrant You know, looking back in my life I have no regrets, through the passage of time and through course of my life I have done everything possible and have trampled over everyone in my path to attain the success that I have today. Does this make me a bad person, or does this simple make me someone who is willing to do everything possible in order to better themselves? I would have to agree with the latter part of the statement, but then again, I suppose I am bit biased on the subject, it being me after all. My deeds of the past are nothing secret or to be ashamed of but others may not think the same, I have been told time and time again I am a heartless and uncaring beast who is only concerned with self preservation and power. That may be true, you wont ever hear an argument out of me about it, I know very well of the “atrocities” I have committed though my life and looking back on them, I hold no regrets what so ever. I am aware that some out there aren’t aware of me or my past, so I am now taking it upon myself to MAKE you aware of it, so that you may see with your own eyes and you may make your own opinions of me and my life and my past. These histories are for your (the readers) reference so that you may become more familiar with me, Ehlexander Tarrant. The particular instance I am about to take you back to is a time when I had to go through he rights to open a passage to the VOID. Those of you wondering what the VOID is, it’s a rift between realities, a gap in time and space where beings of great power dwell. The reason for me doing this was I needed to make contacts there in order to gather clues on how to defeat the vile women I was warring with at the time. She was known as FATE, of the three gods, she was neutral in all things, she controlled the destiny and fate or all of creation, and her powers were something I had to attain in order for my war with her two brothers, The Lords of Light and Darkness to go into my favor. The scene fades out then fades back in to Tarrant standing out side of what appears to be a small run down church, its shudders hang low, barley attached to their hinges, they wave side to side slowly in the gentle breeze. The church is indeed in need of dire repair, its roof has large gaping holes, the wood siding shows signs of rotting and the front door is missing, it is obvious that just at a glance that it hasn’t been used in many, many years. The soft patter of rain drops can softly be heard as the heavens above slowly begin to let loose their pay load of life giving water to mother earth. Pulling his coat about him tighter, Tarrant takes his first steps to the small shambled building, running his hands though his hair he pulls the wet mass out of his eyes so that he can see more clearly, his eyes glowing faintly an icy blue, he walks up the steps to the front door, with each step, the wooded stairs groan in protest, and at one point, Tarrant’s foot even goes through the rotten wood. However not missing a beat, Tarrant recovers as if nothing even happened and continues his walk into the abandoned holy grounds. Walking inside, Tarrant sees the inside isn’t in much better shape then the outside as the pews are tipped over, rotting away he sees small insects have taken up residence in their rotten remains. Looking above the gaping hopes seen from outside are very much viable in here as well, the rain is beginning to fall at a faster rate now, the storm promises to be a good one it would appear. None of this is a surprise to him however, as he was here a few days ago to set up for the ceremony that would take place this very night. He had to get into contact with the being sin the VOID if his war was to be a successful one, the one known as FATE was powerful indeed and as much as he hated to admit it, her power outmatched his own. In order to defeat her and take her power to make it his own, he would need help, those in the VOID would be able to provide that help. At least he hoped they could. In order to do it though, he needed to perform the proper ceremony’s, he needed a sacrifice, the blood of an innocent needed to be spilled at just the right time on this night, All Hollows Eve. Looking up to the alter; Tarrant saw all that he needed for such a ceremony, as he had made the proper preparations earlier today. Up the three steps to the alter was a large stone slab inscribed with runes that he himself had painstakingly written into the stone itself. Upon the top of it lay a women of average looks, her eyes were wide open and were bloodshot, large bags were also under her eyes, indicating she had been crying for quite some time. Her head wiped to the side and spotted Tarrant for the first time and a wave of fear washed over her face. “Please, for the love of god let me go!!” the words exploded from her mouth and she pleaded to Tarrant. Shaking his head, Tarrant’s only response was tightening up the wrist and ankle straps that held the women in place. “I am sorry dear lady but I cant do that, I need you, or rather, what’s inside of you.” Looking down, Tarrant put his hand upon the women’s stomach, which was enlarged a great deal. The woman was with child. “No, leave my baby alone!! I did nothing to you, please let me go, please have mercy!!” The woman was beginning to go into hysterics and began thrashing wildly trying to break her bonds. Up above there was a hole in the ceiling right above where the women lay and looking out of it lightning began to streak across the storm ridden sky. Ignoring her pleas to be spared, Tarrant walked over to the pulpit that was right next to the alter and pulled out a large ancient looking book and began to leaf though it. “Please, leave me alone!! Leave my baby alone, spare me please, I did nothing….!” “Please silence yourself miss.” Tarrant spoke the words in an every day manner, as if nothing was amiss. “I assure you, you will leave from this place unharmed, though it will be without your baby, I need him. You will be free to walk from here once I am done to conceive another child and forget any of this.” “YOU MONSTER!!! I can’t forget my child, if you are going to take him then take me as well for I would rather die then live without him!!” Her screams were s mixture of anger and fear “Don’t tempt me.” Tarrant responded in annoyance. “I care nothing for you, your feelings or opinions on this, it is something that needs to be done regardless of whether you understand it or not. Your feelings for your unborn child are of no consequence to me, plead and scream all you wish it wont change the outcome of what is about to happen.” “Now I advise you stop pleading and brace yourself for what is about to happen for I think it will hurt very much indeed.” He said in an uncaring fashion The women was now weeping uncontrollably, though Tarrant paid her no attention, he appeared to have found the page he was looking for and began chanting in a language long dead to the ears of mortal men. Almost at once there was a visual response to the words as a feint red glow began to surround the screaming women and though she was visibly still screaming, no sounds came from her mouth, all that could be heard was the words coming from Tarrant’s mouth and the sounds of wind that was no whipping through the church. “Mor’keth sala nor esith, qlesid hel reth mor canith!!” Tarrant was now screaming the words and the very elements responded as bolts of lightening were now ripping through the ceiling of the church and striking anything in its path, the increasingly harsh wind was beginning to pick up debris and throw then about the church as if it were trying to destroy what was left of the run down building. His eyes now blazing forth their icy blue color, Tarrant continued with the chanting and finally in response a bolt of lightening came down and struck the woman right in her stomach. Once screaming in protest, she now screamed in pain as the bolt of energy actually began to carve her open, the bolt dissipated finally when a large circle had been cut around the diameter of the woman’s stomach. Lifting his hands what happened next was gruesome indeed as the baby fetus was lifted from her body and suspended above the now unconscious women. “The blood of an innocent.” Tarrant remarked to himself before he began chanting again. In responds another bolt of lightening streaked down from the heavens and struck the fetus full on causing it to explode in response, its blood and remains falling to his mother below as well as the alter. Tarrant stopped his chanting and in response the winds and lightening dissipated, but the rain remained. He watched as the blood slowly dripped down the sides of the alter and covered the runes he had inscribed upon it. In response the runes began to glow a deep crimson until finally they flashed brightly. When the flash subsided, a large black hole was standing next to the alter. “The gate to the VOID, as planned.” Tarrant said with a smile on his face. Looking to the bloody women Tarrant reached his hand over the woman’s stomach and at once her wounds were healed, only moments later she opened her eye as Tarrant freed her from her bonds. Immediately her hands went to her stomach and her face dropped as she saw her baby was gone. “Why?! WHY?!” She pleaded. “I told you why, stop asking the same damned question over and over, it’s annoying. Just be glad you are escaping with your life.” Tarrant said as he turned from her walking towards the gate. “DON’T YOU UNDERSTAND?! You took my child!! Don’t you even care that you took what would have been my one and only son from me?! Don’t you understand the pain of a mother loosing their child?! I will never see him, Never hold him, Never tell him how much I love him!!” Tarrant stopped in mid stride just as he was about to cross the threshold, turning his head slightly he responded. “If you wish to tell him, do so before your bath, that’s him all over you right now. I’m sure your words will bring the both of you SOME comfort.” The words were cold and uncaring and hit the women like a freight train as she screamed in horror as she notice for the first time the blood that covered her, and the small pieces of flesh that were scattered about. Tarrant not even hesitating took his last remaining steps and disappeared through he gate leaving the women alone, covered in her unborn son, crying. The gate closed as he walked through and the woman was left alone with what remained of her unborn child. The scene begins to fade away from the church as we now hear only the soft sounds of a weeping women and rain falling to the ground, the old church from the outside shows no signs of the horrifying events that took place within as the scene fades completely --End Scene
