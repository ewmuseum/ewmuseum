The Reasoning

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Our scene fades into a lonely locker room in some nameless gym somewhere in the heart land of America. This is just a normal locker room, a few rows of lockers, a shower, a toilet, a water fountain. You know, the basics. The only thing special about this locker room is that Luke Chadbourne is in it. Luke is just walking out of the shower as our camera catches view of his terrific body. The fluorescent lights glimmer and shine off Luke's washboard stomach and tones pecks. He has a white towel wrapped around his waist covering the goods. (Sorry Ladies, nothing to provocative here) "Shit that was a hell of a crowd for an Indy show. It took a lot out of me, but at least I know each and every one of those people walked out of here entertained." Luke speaks with a sense of pride as he talks about his last Indy show for a while, considering he has just signed with 4WW. He rotates his right shoulder and his arm crackles and creeks like an old door. He rolls his neck and the old door sounds start again. Luke walks between a couple rows of lockers and strolls over to a locker that is open. He looks in the mirror hanging on the door. He pushes his soaking wet hair off of his forehead to reveal a huge gash right below the hair line. It's all stitched up now but about an hour ago it released a crimson mask that covered Luke's face. "Not a bad like reminder of final Indy show." With those words like lets his hair drop and the reality finally hits him. His thoughts now run wild. 'My last Indy show?!? I can't believe it. I have finally arrived. A little bump in the road with the closure of PCW right after I started. Nothing big. Now I have finally arrived, 4WW is where I will make my name.' Luke reaches into the locker and pulls out a pair of black silk boxers and slides them on under his towel. After a few seconds of adjusting himself, he rips the towel off and places it in the locker. He then grabs a pair of black trousers, very nicely pressed and folded. He unfolds them and slides them on over the boxers. He buttons the Dickies and they sag a little, showing off the silky undergarments. He sits down on the bench that is right behind him and settles in as he waits for the main event, Jerry "The King" Lawler vs. "Rockin" Randy, to conclude. He reaches back into the locker and pulls out the September issue of Pro Wrestling Illustrated and begins to thumb through it. The feature article is about GTT4. The "professionals" are analyzing the outcomes and the competitors. Luke quickly looks for his name. He finally finds it and begins to read. Point by Mark Madden "Luke Chadbourne- Grand son of the late great Indy Legend, 'Sneaky' Steve Chadbourne has a lot of potentional but not a prayer in this tourney. He is an overly zealous young man who has chosen the exact wrong time to try and make a jump to the big times. Recently signing with 4WW was a wise choice. 4WW is a great place for a young talent like Luke to "learn the ropes", but entering himself GTT4 may ruin his career. In this tourney there is sure to be a lot of injuries, maybe even career ending injuries. If Luke was to suffer one of these injuries, he would be cutting his career off before it even really starts. The kid is green and he is going to get himself killed in the ring with the veterans who are going to dominate this tourney. So my only advice for Luke, withdraw from the tourney and be patent, your time will come. Why not wait?" Counter Point by Mike Tennae "Luke Chadbourne- I agree that Luke is a great talent with great potentional. And yes he may be jumping the gun a little bit by throwing his hat into the ring so to speak. But think of the results of a victory or two in the tourney. Even making it to the second or third round could get Luke's name out there to the people who may not already know about him. Think about it. He hasn't done all that much out of Indy shows. He has the cult following; now it's time to go mainstream. And if a miracle does occur and he does win the chance to face the Global Champion, he will have risen to that level faster then anyone before him, furthering his fame not to mention padding his pocket book. So in conclusion, I want to wish Luke the best of luck, he's going to need it." Luke finishes reading the two passages about himself and throws the magazine back into his locker. Now his mind starts racing with any and every thought you would imagine would be on his mind after that analysis. 'Why GTT4? Why now? Why have I chosen now to shine? The answer is simple enough. Because I am ready. I am ready to step up my game to the next level. I am ready to take on all comers and over come all obstacles. I am still green and yes I am inexperienced, but never before have I been so ready. Never before have had I felt so good, never before have I been more focused. With victory in this tournament will come great fame and great recognition but it's more then that. This is not a search for fame and fortune; it's the realization of a dream. The realization of 6 years of hard work, getting clean, staying clean and tuning my body into the machine that it is now. And all the hard work will soon pay off, as I stand atop the mountain, looking over the battle field of fallen warriors, taking my rightful place as the best. It's taken 6 years to reach this point. From the rehab, to UPW, to PCW and now in my new home of 4WW. But not even 4WW is big enough to hold me. I am going global, and my focus is on the Global Title. Critics have asked me, "Do you really think you have a chance in this tournament?" And to these questions I have no answer, because they are asking the wrong ones. It's not a matter of if I have a chance; it's whether they can bring some one to the table that stands a chance against me. I am 6'1" 258 lbs. of pure power and technical skill. I am psyched and ready for any thing that is going to get thrown at me. And I have confidence that I will walk out of GTT4 with a shot at the Global Title. But that is just the beginning. After I defeat everyone placed in front of me in this battle for supremacy, proving what I have said all along, that I am the greatest, I will move on, further cementing myself as a living legend in the game, capturing the Global title at the young age of only 25. And that my not be record. I am sure there have been younger champions, but who has risen to stardom and fame faster then me. I have been in the big leagues for only about 9 months yet I am in one of the biggest tournaments our sport has ever seen. But is it my time?? Is now my time to shine?? Your damn right it is. This is not about a tournament, this about my destiny, my fate. This is where I was meant to be. This is my place. I flopped in PCW. Shit I may have even dragged PCW into the ground, but this time it is all different. I can feel. I can feel it with every ounce of my being. This is my time. And there is noone short of God himself that is going to keep me from taking my thrown.' Luke is pumped up, adrenaline sending the blood rushing through his veins like roaring white water rapids. Luke leaps up to his feet and glances in the mirror, his face filled with anger and intensity. The sound of the locker room door swinging open is heard and Luke snaps, head butting the locker right next to his own. The impact rips the stitches right out of his forehead and leaving a splatter of blood on the baby blue locker. The crimson blood begins to run from the gash. A few wrestlers file into the locker room and gather around to see what's going on. They stand there and just watch for a minute as Luke then punches the locker a few times. Then finally, a young wrestler by the name of Casey James speaks up. "Hey Luke....." Casey rushes over to Luke and tries to calm him down, putting himself between Luke and the locker; his arms up to block any stray punches that may come flying his way. "Luke.......Calm down man........You're gonna fuck yourself up before you even have your first match in 4WW." Luke calms down a little, but the intensity and anger still lingers in his eyes. Casey places his arm around the shoulder of Luke and ushers him toward the door of the locker room. "Let's go get you stitched up again." Luke pushes Casey’s arm off his shoulder with and aggressive push. "GET THE FUCK OFF OF ME!! I CAN TAKE CARE OF MYSELF!!" Casey puts his hand up again to block himself. Luke reaches into his locker and pulls out a black duffle bag. He shoves in a pair of red, metallic trunks and his chain mail vest. He then picks up the magazine and throws it at Casey. He slams his locker and walks out of the locker room as our scene fades to black.
