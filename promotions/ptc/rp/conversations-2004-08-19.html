Conversations

19 August 2004

Date: 2004-08-19 What looks like the Boomer Sooners locker room, sounds like the Boomer Sooners locker room, and *sniff* yup, smells like the Boomer Sooners locker room? Hmmm. Ha! I’m not falling for this one. I know that this is just another elaborate scheme to get me to try and intro straight to the Boomer Sooners locker room like last time, but after close to three years of this, I’m finally starting to catch on. I’ve fallen into many traps, but for once, I’ve seen one coming. Oh happy day! Hmmmm, ya know, for a farce, this sure does look like the Boomer Sooners locker room. And to tell you the truth, it’s smells a little tooooo much like their locker room. I’m actually getting kind of woozy here. No one in their right mind would dare reproduce this smell, no matter how evil a scheme. I mean, it’s like expired baby food and cat ass. Ok, so maybe this really is the Boomer Sooner locker room, but at least I wasn’t tricked into doing a rambling intro … or at least I don’t remember doing one. The smell is really making it hard to concentrate. Oooooooooooooooooooooooh man. Anyways. We open on a familiar face. It is the face of one Fatrick J. McBadgerson, the man who was told not long ago that he was the key to XUW’s embarrassment in the upcoming PTC Survival Free For All pay per view. But who’s to say if Fatbadger can remember that happening, or even what happened five minutes ago. Yup, that’s the Fatty that we’re all familiar with. His expression though, is one that we are not at all familiar with him having: determination. No, this is not his usual expression, which is comparable to that which you might find on a coma patient who is still aware enough to realize that he just received a warm enema from a large, tattooed male nurse with slippery fingers. You know, uncomfortable pleasure. Anyways, as I said, that is NOT the expression on his face now. He is concentrating immensely on his task at hand, his eyes not blinking or departing from their target. His face is covered in sweat and trembles slightly. It’s about at intense as that one time he dropped a Funyun just out of reach behind the toilet. We switch to another familiar face. The face is that of the PTC Global Champion and XUW superstar, Saijwick Hu Zzahberry. The expression on his face is also one of determination. Saij’s kind of determination that is. His eyes are squinted and his eyebrows cocked with his tongue peeking out of the corner of his mouth, but his eyes are also locked on something requiring his full attention. The camera pulls back to show that Saij and Fatty are merely inches apart on the couch in the locker room, sweat dripping from their brows as they grit their teeth and both stare down toward the couch. What could cause these two Sooners to exert so much energy and concentration??!! The camera quickly pans down and zooms in on what has these two men are locked in on ………… A THUMB WRESTLING MATCH!! Saij attempts a double-knuckle under hook … but it’s blocked! Fatty reverses into a pinky lock! Saij goes to tap out, but somehow gets out of the hold! Saij flicks him in the palm. He goes for the Fingernail Huzzah! AND HITS IT! He rolls Fatty’s thumb over and pins. 1 2 3!!!! Saij jumps and pumps his fists in the air. Saij: Winner … and … STILL GLOBAL CHAMPION … ME! Fatbadger: SPECTACULAR!!! Badger rubs his thumb. Fatbadger: Ow dude. I never see that move coming. Hey, do you really think that you should be, like, defending the Global Title in unsanctioned thumb wrestling matches? Saij: Well, they didn’t get mad when I lost it to a twelve year old girl, so I figure I better test the limits so that I’ll know where the line is. Badger thinks for a second. Fatbadger: Makes sense to me. Saij takes a seat back next to Badger on the couch and kicks his feet up on the coffee table. Saij: So Fatty, you excited for your first PTC match? Fatbadger: Well, I like the idea of losing, but, ya know …*whisper* … Jack. Saij: Ah he’s just a big pussycat! Fatbadger: But he wants to win, and, ya know, kill me. Saij: Well, at least losing won’t make things any worse, except … Saij smiles shyly. Badger gets paranoid. Fatbadger: Except what dude?! Saij: Well, if you lose in PTC, they feed you rabid ferrets! Badger gasps. Fatbadger: GASP!!! NOOOO!! Saij: Seriously man, that’s why I haven’t lost there yet … cuz I’m allergic to ferrets … and rabies … and death. I have an adverse reaction. Fatbadger: But, like, didn’t Blake lose in PCT? Saij: You telling me Blake doesn’t look like a pile of ferret turds? *sniff* Speaking of turds … *sniff* … what is that smell? Badger looks down at his lap and cringes. Fatbadger: Jeez, I think I like, crapped my pants a little dude! Saij: *sniff* Holy fark Fatty! Do we need to put you in diapers … again. Fatbadger: Hey, that last time I just couldn’t control myself. This was just a little accident. You had to mention ferrets dude. Saij: Man, you’re gonna be worse than JR pretty soon. You better get in the shower and scrub your ass. Fark! That’s bad. Saij gags and pulls his shirt up over his mouth and nose in an attempt to hold back the smell. Fatbadger: I guess it is about time I take a shower. I mean, it’s been like, a month or so. Saij: Fatty that’s gross. Why on earth has it been so long? Fatbadger: Don’t you remember when I said that I met Yu-Gi-Oh at Subway that one day when he was eating a meatball sub, and I shook his hand, and then swore not to wash it, and stuff? Saij looks confused and shakes his head. Saij: Uh, ok, first, you told me that that was just a dream you had after smoking a bad glowstick. And second, you just washed your hands ten minutes ago before the thumb wrestling match because they were Funyun-yellow. Badger squints as his head makes clinking sounds. Fatbadger: Oh yeah! You’re right dude. Saij and Badger exchange nods and then just blankly look ahead for a couple seconds before Saij gets another sniff of the air and looks quickly back at Badger. Saij: Get the hell in the shower!! Fatty jumps up and flinches like Saij was gonna hit him before heading toward the bathroom. Scene fades. /////////// TIME PASSAGE MUSIC \\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\ We reopen in a steam-filled shower and a naked Fatbadger … from the waist up. He’s nodding his head back and forth. Could he possibly be going through wrestling maneuvers and reversals in his head? Well, probably not. Fatbadger: Iiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii feel pretty! Oh so pretty! Told ya. Fatbadger: I feel pretty … and gitty and [this word has been censored for fear of half a point loss]!!! And I pity … any girl, who isn’t me, to-DAYYYY!! Voice: LA LA LA LA LAAA LA LA!! Badger stops singing and cowers, looking around frantically and covering his lower regions. Fatbadger: Uh, who’s there? Cuz like, I got a gun in here! Voice: I gotta tell you, if that’s a gun, you’re holding it all wrong. Ha! It’s me Fatbadger. Badger relaxes a little as he finally recognizes his inner voice. Doesn’t everyone have an inner voice? Well, Badger didn’t until everyone in XUW started hearing them, so he didn’t want to feel left out. Now, during showers, he wishes he did. Inner Voice: We wouldn’t have to go through this every time if you’d just shower more. Fatbadger: Well, this time I had to wait because I shook hands with -- Inner Voice: Yu-Gi-Oh, yes I know. And last time it was Shrek. And the time before that it was … the Hamburgler I believe. Maybe you should just cut back a little on the glowsticks. Fatbadger: FWAHAHAHAHA! Good one dude! Inner Voice: Yes, yes. I somehow saw that reaction coming. Anyways, we have much to talk about. You have quite a big match coming up. Fatbadger: Oh crap! Do I have a match on Mayhem that I don’t know about again?! I really gotta, like, start checking the cards and stuff. Inner Voice: NO! NOT A -- Inner Inner Voice: Stay calm now. You know how his mind works. You live there you know. Did I forget to mention that Fatbadger’s Inner Voice has and Inner Voice? Sorry. Inner Voice: For the love a … I was hoping you were gonna sit this one out. Fatbadger: Uh, are you talking to me? Oh, and Badger can’t hear it. Inner Voice: Nevermind. The match I was talking about was your part of Team XUW in the PTC pay per view. Fatbadger: Oh that. Yeah. It should be pretty cool. Inner Voice: Just ‘pretty cool’? You’re gonna get to do what you do best on a much larger scale than ever before. You get to lose … Inner Inner Voice: … SPECTACULARLY!!! Oh, and the Inner Voice’s Inner Voice is a big Fatbadger fan, to the dismay of the Inner Voice. Inner Voice: As I was saying … *ahem* … you’ve lost in your spectacular fashion in the biggest match of the year, the main event of Reborn, but this … this is a much bigger stage. This is the best of fourteen federations. You just won’t be losing in front of XUW fans, you’ll be losing in front of everyone’s fans. That’ll make the loss fourteen times more … Inner Inner Voice: … SPECTACULAR!! Inner Voice: Can I finish? Inner Inner Voice: Sorry, sorry. I can’t help it. Fatbadger: You can finish whenever you want dude. Inner Voice: I wasn’t talking to you. Fatbadger: You weren’t? Oh, right. The voice you hear. Badger twirls a finger around his ear. Fatbadger: COO-COO! Inner Voice: Can I finish? Fatbadger: You talking to me this time dude? Inner Voice: Yes. Fatbadger: Well then finish. Inner Voice: … I’m finished. Fatbadger: Ok, good, but like, dude ... *whisper* … what about the ferrets? Inner Voice: Say what? Fatbadger: You know. The ferrets that the losers in PTC get fed to. Inner Voice: … Inner Inner Voice: … Fatbadger: Hello? Inner Voice: For all my experience with you Badger, you still say some things that make me shake my head. Fatbadger: You have a head? Inner Voice: You know what I mean. Fatbadger: … no. Inner Voice: Nevermind. There are no ferrets in PTC. They’re myths. Fatbadger: No dude, they’re rabbits! Inner Voice: Rabbits? Inner Inner Voice: I think he means rabid. Inner Voice: I know what he means! Look Badger, there are NO ferrets. Fatbadger: But Saij said -- Inner Voice: He lied. Fatbadger: But … but … Saij doesn’t lie … Fatty’s bottom lip starts to quiver and his eyes start to tear up. Inner Inner Voice: Ohhh, look what you did. Just let him believe in the ferrets. Inner Voice: No, forget the ferrets. Fatbadger: What about the ferrets? Inner Voice: What about … Jack. Fatbadger: Jack? Inner Voice: That’s right. I told you I was going to bring him back to punish you and I did. Badger cowers. Inner Inner Voice: Oh you had nothing to do with that. Inner Voice: He doesn’t know that. Fatbadger: Know what? Inner Voice: Uh, know that … that Jack really wants you to lose at Survival Free For All. Fatbadger: But I thought he wanted to win. Inner Voice: That’s what he wants you to think. Look, in the past, you’ve lost to Jack, Blake, and Woof in spectacular fashions, so now it’s time for you to lose with them in that fashion. That’s what Jack wants. Fatbadger: Well, I guess that makes sense. Inner Inner Voice: Hey, no fair using stoner logic to get your way. Inner Voice. Haha. Whatever works. Fatbadger: What? Inner Voice: Look out for the soap! Badger suddenly vanishes from the shot, and you hear a thud as he hits the bottom of the tub. The steam thickens and the scene fades.
