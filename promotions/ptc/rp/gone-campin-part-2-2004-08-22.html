Gone Campin Part 2

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-22 It’s just past 7 o clock PM and over the horizon on Mudsling Lake the sun has just begun to disintegrate lower and lower, in the introductory stages of becoming less of a distinctive and vibrant circle and more of a broad canvas of paints. Paints of all colors too; lavender, orange, yellow and blue all dabbled upon the sky. The actual water on the lake is beginning to grow more restless by the minute, slapping like a knife edge chop up against the rocky walls that bound it. It’s a majestic sight, each wave a piece of art in it’s own regard. So many wrinkles and crevices weaving through, each rhythmically marching across the water like warriors in combat. There’s so much detail to these waves, they’re so well thought out and precise. The mossy birch barks of a thousand plus trees surrounding the magnificent body of water all stand tall and stolid. However a brief and timid breeze does manage to flutter the olive green leaves just a tad. Lakes Mudsling is so calm and tranquil at this hour, minus the menacing chaos of the mosquitoes of course. If there’s one downfall to the otherwise beautiful atmosphere of camping it’s the mosquitoes. To and fro they motors themselves about, all in groupd of thirty or so. It doesn’t appear they’re headed in any particular direction or have any objective but to simply buzz around each other, not a care in the world. They’re such mindless creatures, the purpose of their existence has boggled mankind for years. But on this night it seems the mosquitoes are a bit less intent on harassing and pestering any other living organisms around them. In fact they’ve stayed completely out of sight and out of mind of the one human being on the lake tonight. Out trolling* just down the east most ankle of the lake is a boat. It’s a default grey structure with a stylish blue stripe down the side and the words “Magna 17 Fish” in block letters on one side. To it’s opposite is a larger and more artistic logo with the word “Tracker.” It would appear not the most suited boat in the world for fishing at first glance, nor the most well equipped but just like an old red pickup truck it serves it’s purpose… just not exactly in stylish fashion. The guttural wheezing and seemingly toxic clouds of smoke coming barreling out of the rear motor certainly don’t seem to be just what the doctor ordered and is a very alarming sight to first time riders of the Magna 17. The large amount of patch work with duct tape on the glove compartment doesn’t add any charm to the allure either. But it’s a good boat, and it’s been through some good and some bad in it’s day. It’s seen hours on the water with not a single fish caught, and it’s seen mornings with a thirty incher on every cast. The fishing tonight hasn’t been great tonight. Well, perhaps that’s a modest statement. There hasn’t been any fish caught the whole night, not even a bite. The biggest highlight of the 45 minute fishing expedition tonight has been the hook catching a snag on a group of weeds in the water. But Munson doesn’t mind as he sits proudly at the driver’s seat slowly navigating around a cluster of algae. To him this isn’t about the fishing, it’s about the peace of mind. Out here on this lake, with not a man or a man-made creation in sight there’s a true sense of peace. For miles and miles it’s nothing but water as the sun reflects off it to add to the shine. Out here there are no champions, no bone breaking maneuvers, no drunken fans, no squared circle. In Lake Mudsling Munson doesn’t need to worry about his body’s well being, this boat can take him back to land within a minute, certainly no risk of drowning. No need to get upset over punching harder or impressing someone. There’s nothing like that out here. The only thing to worry about for Munson when he’s fishing out on Lake Mudsling is… nothing at all. No mistakes should be made when interpreting his love for this place, he doesn’t hate pro wrestling. He doesn’t lean on the edge of his seat everyday just dying to get away from the ring. He loves the ring, he’ll love it when he comes back and wrestles against 4WW for Team Core at the Survival Event, he’ll love wrestling again that night in the Survival Main Event, he’ll love all of it. Wrestling, a lot of the time is Munson’s life, it’s consumed him for the past twelve months he’s been doing it. It may be his life, but it isn’t what he lives for. He lives for moments like these, for a chance to sit out here on the lake all by himself and not feel lonely, but to feel complete and one with Mother Nature. Wrestling is a provider of financial stability, it’s an outlet for all the negative feelings and angry fury that burns inside Munson, but nature is a caretaker. It’s beautiful. They may call themselves Four Winds Wrestling, but none of them are as intoned with nature as Munson is. Four Winds Wrestling, the words come as a reminder. Time has begun to grow shorter and shorter, it’s beginning to approach. The event of a lifetime is coming at full blast straight towards Munson and there is no way to dodge it or run out of the way. He needs to take it head on, but he’s ready. It has nothing to do with the fact that he’ll be ‘backed up’ by three other men from Core in his match. The brooding rivalry between Munson and Matt Epic is possible to explode at any moment they’re in the same arena. But it’s quite the same story for most of the teams out there one would imagine. Always a few wrestlers put on the same team because of their superior skills that just can’t stand each other. But Munson will be able to fend for himself if worst comes to worst. And that’s the reason he’s ready for Survival, he knows he can depend on himself. In only a few short days out camping at Lake Mudsling and already he feels so rejuvenated and ready to go again. Before coming out his will to wrestle was admittedly dwindling, he didn’t have the same fiery passion but now it’s gleaming again bright as a bonfire. He has the pride and will to fight for himself, not for Team Core or to represent Core Wrestling, but for himself. Is it the most ‘all for all and one for all’ type of sentiment? Not at all. But this is a PTC I-PPV, this is as big as a wrestling card gets and Munson is on it, literally millions will see this event. This is worldwide exposure for Munson and if he outwrestles everyone else he’ll have a name solidified for himself in stone for all eternity. And if he does wrestle like that, with the idea that he can make a name for himself on this one night alone then he will have an ‘all for all and one for all’ sentiment because he’ll be helping Team Core stride to victory. As Munson calmly casts out his line once again on Lake Mudsling he looks out to the lake around him. Just miles and miles of water, but inside that water he knows that the place is sprawling with fish. Kind of like the world of pro wrestling. Every fish is trying to outdo the other one, they’re trying to survive. Some won’t help others out, and will just help themselves to all the food they can find. Some types of fish may not like other types of fish, just like some wrestling promotion’s rosters may not like others. But in the end only one type of fish will outlast and survive through all others… the strongest. There is no wrestling promotion in Prime Time Central with any better wrestlers than cW and soon the world will know it. For months Core was under whelmed with publicity, it’s only been in the past few months that cW has truly become a household name in Prime Time Central, and one of the most prestigious promotions on Earth. The majority of PTC and it’s fan base consider Core Wrestling to be huge, they don’t feel that way about Four Winds Wrestling, and there’s a reason for that; 4WW is not as good. They are a weaker breed of fish, a breed that can survive underwater and live in harmony, but will never be the top species, the alpha fish. They will never climb to the top of the mountain, in this case Mount Saint Hells and become the nearly undeniable best in PTC. But Core Wrestling will. They are the best, the greatest fish in the sea and all others will learn to adjust to the waves on which cW travels. 4WW stands no chance. * Trolling refers to slowly driving a boat through a lake and casting out a fishing line and just holding it out as the boat carries it along, as opposed to standing on a non moving boat and just casting out, reeling in and then casting out the line.
