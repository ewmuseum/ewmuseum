TTD - Rodriguez Bros. -v- Souls of the Black Lotus

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nicky Jam: [The faint waves of static slowly crept into view, overtaking the darkness behind. Slowly, the static began to grow in both volume and intensity, before the screen had been completely blanketed. No sooner had the screen been covered, did the static freeze, holding in place, before pieces of it began to fall away, cascading over the remaining image.] [When it had all been stripped away, a scene slowly came into view.] [In a colorfully decorated bedroom, a young woman in her middle to late teens stares into the mirror across from her, gazing at her own reflection. By any standard could she be considered attractive, her light brown hair pulled back into a ponytail, fully exposing the soft, supple skin of her face. She sighed heavily, and blinked her bright blues eyes once, allowing something of a pout to overtake her face.] "Everything is a matter of perspective," came the voice of Nicky Jam, booming in from out of view," objects and characteristics take hold a specific meaning for each person." [The view of the girl in her room slowly shifted, panning around her that the view was now one directly over her shoulder, and of the reflection mirrored back.] "Take our friend here," he continued, his voice cold," attractive, pretty. Most people would agree, but if you were to ask her what she thought?" [With the sound of a faint electronic pulse, the image rippled, and a wave of green overtook and tinted the image.] [The reflection in the mirror was no longer that of the girl and her bedroom, but of an aged, haggard old crone, her face laden with pock-marks and warts, her hair stringy and unkempt.] "The response may very well surprise you." [A brief wave of green static faded into view, blurring the image slightly, and when it faded away, the girl and her reflection were no longer present. Instead, the room was barren, save for the reflection in the mirror, that of Nicky Jam, visible from the chest up.] [From what could be seen in the mirror, he was dressed in a black t-shirt with the words "Fear the Jam Slam" written across it in bold white letters. Over this was a black leather jacket, and a pair of silver rimmed sunglasses that hid his eyes from sight.] "Of course, regardless of what you may think," he said, pulling back his right arm, "I can always shatter your beliefs." [Thunder then let fly his right hand, which struck the mirror, shattering the glass, and dropping a few pieces into the abandoned bedroom. His own image now was skewed, warped, and refracted in pieces in the glass that remained.] [Evidently, this pleased him to some extent, as an arrogant smile made its way onto his face.] [The camera began to rotate, using the broken mirror as an axis. Slowly, the room began to turn away, and a new image started to spin into place, with a quick flash of light as a prelude. By the time the light had flashed and faded, the new image was firmly in place.] "Remember gentlemen, who it is you're dealing with." [In the middle of a large, empty room, Nicky Jam now sits reclined in a high backed leather chair, one leg up and over the right arm of the chair, as his back found a niche in the indentation made by the left corner. His jacket removed - hung from the top of the chair - and his arms folded across his chest, he looked rather comfortable in this makeshift throne.] [The wall behind him was made mostly of windows, the majority of which had been shattered, pieces of their glass lining the floor at the base of the wall, with a renegade rock found in their midst every few feet. What glass remained had yellowed due to the strains of time, and provided little light during the day. Now, with night having fallen, the only light in the room was provided by a single bulb - also yellowed - that hung uneasily from the ceiling, that cast a dingy glow on the room and its lone occupant.] "From the sound of things," he continued, adjusting his position in the chair,"some people are either profoundly ignorant, or unwilling to accept the hand that they have been dealt by PTC." "Hello, Souls of the Black Lotus, I hope you make the best of your time leading to this week's Tag Team Title Tournament Match. It may well be the last bit of peace you're allowed to enjoy." ] [A soft gust of wind blew into the room from so many holes in the windows, turning up a small cloud of dust on the black tar floor.] "You may believe that you will actually be leaving victorious," he continued, slowly shaking his head in disdain,"and to some extent it pains me to dispel this for you. I had such high expectations of you, and you've done nothing but shatter them for me. I would have expected you to harbor at least some fragment of intelligence, but unfortunately such is not the case. If anything, they are the ones that I am least worried about, for I fear that the shock which Souls of the Black Lotus is about to experience may well be more than they are capable of dealing with." [Nicky Jam swung his leg from the arm of the chair, and placed both feet firmly on the floor, before slowly standing up, and beginning to move away from the chair, headed towards the bay of windows on the wall behind him.] "Oh Souls of the Black Lotus, what fools you've shown yourselves to be. You have chosen to involve yourselves in The Rodriguez Brothers affairs, and for that you have my attention. Your intentions however, are somewhat questionable," he said, turning towards the camera and bringing his eyebrows to a point behind his sunglasses,"and for that, you will have my wrath. The team you should be worried about is the Rodriguez Brothers, and they are far more dangerous than you may have made them out to be. There have been many teams before you, Souls of the Black Lotus, all saying the same things, all trying to make the same point, and one after another, each one has ultimately failed. By the time our encounter has ended, there will be one more team we will be able to add to that list." [Now only a few feet from the windows, Nicky Jam cocked his head to the side and allowed a sly grin to creep across his face.] "I'll make sure to write your names in big, bold letters." [Nicky Jam turned his attention to the windows, and exhaled, his arms still folded across his chest.] [Slowly, the image began to deteriorate, as once again the pale green static began to creep in, washing the scene over in its wake, until there was nothing left but a sea of noise. Once again, an faint electronic pulse sounded, creating a ripple in the static. By the time the ripple had reached the bounds of the screen, the green tint had been removed.] "And sign it in your very own blood."
