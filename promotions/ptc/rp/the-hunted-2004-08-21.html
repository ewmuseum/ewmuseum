The hunted.

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-21 Night had fallen and no more evidence of this was needed, the pitch-black sky decorated with dancing stars served as proof that the darkest hours had arrived. Beneath this dark canopy I stood at the edge of a forest standing directly behind Xavier Crysto whom I had only known previously as the shadow man. Xavier was predictably dressed in his attire of a black hooded robe, with a large silver crucifix dangling from the chain of the same metal that adorned his neck, clutched firmly in his aged and weakened hands was a walking stick made of the thickest oak. At least four inches in diameter and an easy six feet in height this splintered cane served as a third leg which supported his bones and muscles, which had slowly began to deteriorate from years of life. Xavier still had a few surprises in him despite his age and poor physique. His gnarled fingers clutched the stick tightly as if his very existence depended upon it and he slouched forward slightly showing signs of a back that had devolved slowly throughout his long life. Yet still, beneath the light of the stars I could see the same fire in his haunted blue eyes, the same fire that gave me chills every time I saw it. Goosebumps ran down my arms as my bluish gray eyes met his pale blue orbs and a sick feeling settled in the pit of my stomach as the acid therein churned, protesting my entry into these woods. The voice of Xavier broke the cool night air as silence and the illusions of security were shattered by his voice. "They say that many things roam these woods. Witches, warlocks, wizards, predators for the most part…the human kind." He hissed in a low tone of voice. "Vampires?" I questioned with a sneer of disbelief upon my features. "Yes, those that prey upon the flesh and blood of the living." Said Xavier, his voice still in a docile state. "There is no such thing, Xav." I said with a nervous chuckle, laughing off the fact that my heart had skipped a beat as they heard his previous statement. "Which is why I have brought you to such a place, you, the hero of the masses, conqueror of Satan's allies. You believe not in what I have instructed you, so you will be taught the hard way. Perhaps faith is not enough, instead seeing is believing and once you see you will have no choice but to believe." The shadow man known as Xavier spat out, not raising his voice although I could still detect a hint of anger at my unwillingness to believe in the paranormal. "What's running through that twisted little brain of yours Xavier? Do you intend to hunt these woods in search of a vampire? For a man of such wisdom that isn't exactly what I would classify in the category of brilliant." I defiantly retorted. "No, I don't intend to hunt the vampires, nor do I intend to enter woods of such evil. Perhaps I can be accused of evil deeds yet my misdeeds pale in juxtaposition to what lies in the forest." Came his reply and my eyebrows quickly rose in shock and astonishment. "You aren't going into the woods?" I asked, a look of surprise crossing my face as I tugged at my ragged black locks in a confused manner. "No…you will go, and your journey will be made alone." Xavier returned, with a sinister air emitting from his voice. "No, I'm not going into the woods." I said in defiance, thrusting my chin forth and shoving my hands into the pockets of my tattered Macro Will Diesel jeans. "Why not? He asked, raising a thick white eyebrow in suspicion. "There is nothing in there, or so you've stated in foolish arrogance. Do you fear that your nightmares will come to life once you step foot into the realm of all evil?" "I'm not afraid, I know there is nothing in these woods but owls and the imagination of a twisted soul" ! I snapped back, an icy glare following my response. "Such is not true. Your lies cannot deceive me my son, they will only serve to garner my wrath." Xavier stiffened, clutching his walking stick with both hands now. "Fear is emitting from every pore of your being. Your hands are hidden within your pockets to mask their trembling, even as we speak a lump of emotion has risen to your throat and taken residence there." "I don't fear your stupid…vampires." I responded in protest of such an honest accusation. "You fear more than vampires. You fear what the vampires may do to you…you fear what pain they may inflict upon the masses, and when the masses suffer, you fear that it will be you who shoulders the blame and guilt within." Said Xavier in response, his voice now an icy dagger aimed at my integrity. "I fear nothing." I spat out in defiance. "Prove it." He responded with equal, if not greater defiance. "How can I fear something which doesn't even exist?!" I snarled, now beginning to grow angry with this man of mystery. "Seeing is believing. If you believe that no vampires exist then I'm sure it will be so very easy for you to enter the forest, to step into their realm. What do you suffer to lose? You believe in nothing, so there is nothing to fear." Xavier said, the corners of his lips raising upwards to form a cruel smile as he realized that he had scored the winning point in this argument. "Fine. You win." I said, admitting defeat. "You want me to go into those woods to prove that vampires exist only in the minds of those who believe in the paranormal, then I will do exactly that." "Go, my child…see for yourself what evil awaits you." The smile on the face of Xavier only seemed to widen as he encouraged me with these chilling words. Without another word I brushed by Xavier Crysto, and walked away from the shadow man, not even bothering to look back upon the enigma that had served such a vital role in my life the past few months. My breath seemed to escape me as I took quick steps not hesitating lest I show signs of fear to the man who watched me walk away into the forest. Quick steps distanced me from the shadow man and I was at last, free of his haunting orbs and out of his sight. Suddenly as I stood alone in this forest I noticed how very different it was on the inside. This Romanian forest may have been painted beautifully during the day, yet the shadows of night told a different story. The branches of trees seemed to claw desperately at the sky while others drooped under their heavy burden of leaves. The sky once illuminated by the moon and stars seemed to have been suddenly painted ebony as it's ceiling now cast no light upon my path. Beneath the soles of my black Converse all stars, the crunch of leaves seemed to alarm every creature in the forest of my presence. Thickets of bushes and grass rose throughout the forest socializing with the trees and making it difficult to walk safely in this environment. In the pit of my stomach the acid seemed to boil, rising to my throat and burning my esophagus, from that very pit the acid seemed to form a lump that grew in my throat forcing my breath to escape from my lungs. My heart, once calm seemed to pound violently as it threatened to spring from the very body, which housed it. Thump, thump, thump, came the redundant beat in rapid succession, my blood now racing, and my nerves on end. Taking quick steps and denying all fear that had risen within me I felt a twig snap beneath the sole of my Chuck Taylor's. I gasped softly, startled by the sudden noise yet with great determination I pressed on. Taking swift steps I progressed further into the forest distancing myself as far away from Xavier as possible. "There is nothing out here…" I whispered to myself in reassurance, refusing to allow false beliefs of the paranormal to haunt my mind. Pausing momentarily I tuned my ears to the sounds of the forest surrounding me, nothing but pure, unbridled silence. The silence seemed to almost comfort me as I began to believe that I was alone. Sighing in relief I suddenly jolted upright as the silence was broken by the sounds of an owl hooting his lonely call into the night. Chuckling slightly I shook my head, unnerved by the owl. Taking another step forward I suddenly stopped…against my will. Gasping I spun around yet the hand on my shirt seemed to follow me! I thrashed about, twisting and turning! It was true, it was all true, vampires did exist and I was in the grasp of a vampire at this very moment! Crying out in fear I dropped to my knees and the shirt slid over my head, I felt the icy fingers of fear dancing along my spine as I rolled over backwards and out of the shirt, which was still suspended above me. Reacting as no hero should react I dove away from my attacker and landed on my knees. Placing my hands on the ground I crawled quickly in the opposite direction waiting for my attacker to resume his strike at any moment… No strike came and I quickly whipped my head around to see where my attacker stood…yet he wasn't there. Panting slowly I gazed about, scanning the forest. Rising to my knees I spun around…he wasn't to my left, neither to my right, nor was he set before me. Pushing myself onto my feet my shirtless figure whipped into another one-eighty as I looked throughout the forest. What…" I asked no one in particular as I trailed off in mid-sentence. My eyes focused upon one thing…the black "ato" T-shirt that danced eerily in the wind almost as if a ghost had suddenly donned it as suitable attire. However it was no ghost that held it aloft, but rather a single tree limb that had poked through one of the large holes from where the sleeve had been torn off. "You've got to be kidding me…" I moaned, shaking my head in disbelief. Stepping towards the tree I couldn't help but crack a smile as I plucked the shirt from its captor, the branch. Holding the soft cotton tee in my hands I pulled it over my head and shoved both arms through their respective openings. Slowly the painful realization that a mere tree had sent me into such a state of paranoia and fright sunk in deeply and I couldn't help but find humor at my own misfortune. "I'm not telling Xavier about this little episode." I said, with a smile still upon my face. The smile was erased quickly as my eyes met those of a creature in the forest that seemed to approach me. Stepping back warily I closed both hands into fists and waited for the creature to approach, he did. The creature with glowing yellow eyes now stood approximately twenty-five feet away from me and despite the lack of illumination I could make out the shape of a wolf. This wolf observed me carefully, he didn't approach any further nor did he seem to be threatening. His matted brown fur failed to rise and his teeth were not bared. He seemed of no threat and as anxious as I was to escape these tortured woods I turned away from him and walked in the opposite direction. My steps were quick as I made my way through the thick forest, passing trees and bushes that looked familiar…or at least I thought they did. Whispering a prayer in my heart I begged God for his guidance out of this evil realm. Suddenly something was upon my shoulder, only this time it was not a tree branch…but rather a hand. The fingers were as cold as ice and I swiveled around reacting immediately as I shoved the unknown being away. The creature before me was my superior by at least two inches and a good thirty pounds, he stumbled backwards as I shoved him but he grabbed my wrist in the process taking me to the ground with him! My body slammed hard into the terra firma with such an impact that the wind was driven from my lungs, the vampire and I landed side by side and we both struggled to climb atop the other and gain control. I, being the quicker, leaped on top of him, yet his strength shined as he threw me over and regained control. Rolling onto me his legs pinned me to the ground and his hands clutched my wrists in a vice like grip! Slowly he opened his mouth wide and I saw his fangs gleaming… Now, I suddenly wished that I had remembered that vampires were actually shape shifting creatures. As a vampire he had the ability to transform into a wolf, bat or even a puff of smoke, and I had been duped by what I believed to be a wild canine. His eyes met mine, such cold hard eyes. No compassion therein, no love, no honor, only lust and hunger…lust and hunger for blood, my blood. My life flashed before my eyes, a display of sadness as I stared up at the vampire. No emotion was on his face but simply the hunger for blood. His hair, black as his soul was pulled back into a ponytail, a ponytail that swung about as he dropped his head. Closing my eyes I could feel his ponytail fall across my face and the stench of his hair filled my nostrils. "My Father…" I gasped in prayer. Closing my eyes I waited for the inevitable… His hands were no longer on my wrist… His hair no longer fell across my face… His stench no longer flooded my nostrils… His lips were no longer pressed against my neck… Opening my eyes I saw a cloud of smoke hanging in the air above me, rolling over I looked about in confusion before I saw the hem of a black cloak. Raising my eyes I stared up into the cold sockets of the shadow man. In his hand he held a large silver crucifix that had at one point dangled from his neck. With a sinister smile across his face he knelt down to my level. "Seeing is believing."
