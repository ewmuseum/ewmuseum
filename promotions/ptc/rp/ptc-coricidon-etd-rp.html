Coricidon - ETD RP "Have you ever killed somebody?" "Yes..." Her voice has no sorrow in it, only the cold and calculating sound of an uncaring woman. Her black hair, usually just sitting on her shoulders, is curled and looking very professional. The professionalism extends all down her body as she wears a very nice black suit without creases or signs of her usual self. An ashtray sits in front of her but she opts not to use it. The only visible appearance that reflects her normal self is the slightly maniacal grin that is just stealthy enough to pass for a happy-go-lucky girl. "Were these murders justifiable?" "I would say so." Across from her, an older man with thinning brown hair sits, staring at her very intensely. He also screams of professionalism, albeit, an obvious issued suit and tie uniform. He has no smile on his face. Nor a scowl or any expression at all for that matter. His lips move and his words speak out with absolutely no emotion. His hands are clinched to each other, no movement from them at all unless it is to reach forward to his cup of coffee which, in itself, is few and far between. Their eyes have been locked on each other's since he entered the room, not a blink or any type of movement. "Did you enjoy them?" "Some." They both sit in this room, a large mirror to their right that obviously has spectators on the other side while the rest of the room is very plain, a light gray adorning the walls while a black camera hangs from the ceiling in the corner with an annoying red light that blinks every few seconds. There is no uneasy feeling in the room, for her at least. The conversation stays very monotone, neither have any real emotion in their voices and neither care what they are even talking about. The questions just keep coming and are answered even quicker. "Why did you enjoy 'some' of them?" "Some people just deserve to die..." There is the emotion that keeps subtly popping up, however. That small smile, the one only identifiable to the very trained eye. Some people in the other room may have it but the people who matter usually don't and don't listen to the people that have it. Her thumbs slowly twiddle on the heavy steel table while waiting for the next question as the man takes long pauses between questions like he has to register the one sentence responses, or he just has to think of the next question. "Does anybody really deserve to die?" "Every person in the world has one gift, their lives. Nobody deserves to lose that gift." Confused, the man stares at her. A completely hypocrisy to her previous statement. There is a small murmur from the mirror, obviously showing that all of the other men are sharing the same emotions. The man's hands unlock from each other and lay flat on the surface of the table. "Well if you believe that, why did you kill these people?" Her smile grows a bit wider. She knows that she has them questioning her choices now, which is right where she was expecting them to be. While some have questioned her smarts simply because she was a girl, although she has enough smarts to know when the pawns are playing right into her hand. "I am a murderer, however, the gifts I have taken were gifts that needed to be returned." The man's face continues to be perturbed by her answers. She can tell this as her grin continues to be as big as it has been once she realized her plan had become a success. "You see, the people I killed have killed before. They took gifts from other people and it was just my way of giving those gifts back to those people. Why should a man be able to kill another without any sort of poetic justice to end the chapter? " The man slowly begins to understand as his confusion slowly disappears from his face. His hands clinch back together and he gets a small smirk on his face. The smirk actually makes her laugh a slight bit as he actually does not understand much about what she is talking about. "So, you have killed murderers, kind of like an avenger?" "Yes." He shakes his head, thinking he is at complete understanding of what she is talking about. He begins to feel at ease and is now beginning to show some sorts of emotion, an ego. "Do you ever plan to kill again?" As he says this, she slumps back in her chair. She had thought about this question as well before. It was a question that she had asked herself every night that she sat behind the bars. Actually, the fact that they had caught her in the first place is what she thought about at first. But, then again, the fact that her bare hands had killed began to be her thoughts as of late. She was a wrestler by day but, after the last lights in the building went off, her mood began to get as dark as the inside of the arena. People didn't know what made the time bomb go off but, when it did, it spelled bad news for everyone involved. The doctors diagnosed it as bi-polar in jail, it was discovered after she killed another woman during the night who merely got up to go to the bathroom, she was a murderer as well, they always grouped the same type of offenses together. No need to put a murdered in the same room as a sex offender, although most of the world would prefer to have it that way. This offense put her in solitary for many days before she got her meeting with the psychiatrist, a person she saw many times before she had left. Suddenly she snaps from her thoughts to remember that she had never answered the questions, long pauses usually admitted guilt. As she stared back at him, she realized that he was along this same train of thought. He awaits the answer but has already came up with his own answer for it from the long silence. "Look at my point of view", she says. "I am a professional wrestler. Well, I was. Before I was arrested, I was an amazing superstar who held many titles. Hell, I know the reason that I even got this parole hearing after only three years is because of that stardom. I am bi-polar, I must control it but, when I step back into the ring for Primetime Central's final Extreme Title match, there are no guarantees that I can make. There are a lot of people that will be wrestling in this match and, if one of them gets on my bad side, there is no assurance that I can make that I won't kill them..." This makes the man uneasy as he shifts in his seat. She is right, the reason that she is getting this hearing is because she is a superstar and she knows that no matter how this hearing goes, she is going to get out. As soon as PTC made the call, all of the officers began approaching her, wishing the murderer luck in her next match. She didn't even know she was having a match yet when they began talking to her. But, it was at this point that she began preparing again. Unlike how most people prepare, however, she just began preparing mentally, to show that she is not crazy so that she can unleash that in the ring, which she plans on doing. "Well", he replies. "I see a clean bill of health and you are good to compete. You are free to gather your things and go." She gives him a smile and stands, he also stands and extends his hand. Instead of shaking it, she looks down and then walks out the door. He turns to the mirror and gives a thumbs up before trying to walk away, not realizing that his left leg is handcuffed to the steel table. He makes movement in his leg to hear the clinking of metal on metal as he looks down, confused. He stares back at the mirror to have the light on the other side go on, making the mirror transparent. Inside of it is the image of Coricidon, a smile on her face as she stares at the squirming man. "You have fun. It is time for Primetime Central to receive something they haven't had in a very, very long time." The small smirk turns into a very maniacal grin. He stares with a slight horror in his face as he turns to the camera, realizing that the red light is no longer flashing on the camera, instead there is just a black bulb to reveal that it is off. He turns back and looks on to see what unfolds. "Everybody will receive... A fall from grace." She turns and walks out as the lights in both rooms go out and, inside of the darkness there is a scream of a man. A scream of a victim.
