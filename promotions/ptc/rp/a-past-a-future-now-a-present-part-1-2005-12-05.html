A Past. A Future. Now A Present…(Part 1)

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Sometime in the 1600’s… Somewhere in Jerusalem… The dark land was settling in the downing sunlight. The shadows had came in and were covering the land. Sixteen hundred. The day in age of the horses. The settling people of this holy land. The land of god. The land of the religious servant’s of the man upstairs. The dust rose up off the ground, flying towards the church that stood in the distance. Brick over brick, towering above everything else. Red banners reviling the name of this holy land as Jerusalem. The birth place of Jesus or at least everyone who thought it was. A grassy noel leading a way to the little bridge that was in front of the church. Water surrounded the place, separating the wondrous structure from the little people of the near by town. The clouds came rolling in to cover the lasting bit of sun that was going down. It was about six or so at night. As the darkness took over, a red light came shining from around the corner. A case of people, some wearing hoods over their heads, covering around in a circle around one man. Black coat lashing around his entire body, twirling him like the bark of a tree. Chains wrapping his arms at the wrist to the point of twisting till his hands were going to fall off. The red light, a blazing fire that let off a massive amount of heat. Enough to force the sweat to roll down the face of the mystery man standing above them all. "Tonight," Raising his voice up high. "Tonight is the night that my will becomes strong." Screams from the other members that stood in the circle, "WOO!" The will of his anger was strong. His voice rose above everything else. Firing his arms up high and towards the skies above him. "Tonight we sacrifice this woman in the name of everything unholy. Tonight, we replace God’s name with one name," Pausing, slowly turning towards the men. "… Reamond" "Reaaamond… Reaaamond…" The group of men chanted in a stoic tone. The fire was blasting away, trying to take over the night as the cries of one single innocent girl could be heard. Her whimpering and sniffling drawing the attention towards her over the stoic chanting of the group around her. She was hung on an inverted cross that was suspended with a makeshift stand that had been built here just for her. She hung upside down, on the ground underneath her was a pentagram drawn into the earth with the blood of this man.. This Reamond. Her eyes, dropping tear after tear that flowed upwards over the smooth features of her forehead, soaking into the dark black locks that hung suspended, just barely over the earth below. She had to be under the age of twenty-five. She was wearing a loose fitting dress that had been cut a great deal to reveal long slender legs of pale white flesh. Her body hanging right in front of this man. "Please…let me go." Cries the girl. "You should be proud petty human. You are going to be the holder, the outer shelf, to the new a new god." Caressing her pretty face with his right hand. A black glove covering it as it rubs over her smooth red lips. "Your crazy." Screaming as she spits right into his covered face. The crowd grew silent. Anticipating what he was going to do again. Stepping away from her, watching as a bolt of lighting crosses the sky above them, slamming right into the towering wall of the church. The blast struck hard, knocking off a entire piece of the right side, the same side that they were standing on. Yelling, fearful screaming of the masses, the men standing the circle raising up their arms and hands like it was their new god who did it. The girl wiggled, trying to get anything loose from the tight binds that had her snugly attached to the cross.. "Followers. Your new god. Me. Will take his place in this woman and finally be reborn." Raising his hands to the Heavens in hated defiance. "HAIL!" Cheers the followers. He began walking towards the girl once again but this time it felt a little different. The skies were changing. Switching from the pitch black shiny color that they were to a crisp red orange color. His left hand began to move out of the coat he was wearing. Long, sharp, and everyman’s death wish. The blinding light from the fire came off the knife in his hand. Her tears began to pick up, coming down like Niagara Falls. Crying and screaming for anyone to help her. Raising the knife up, ready to inflict the pain that would soon kill her, a crowd of people came walking up the hill from the town below. Massive amounts, some with cross bows, some with swords, and some with pitch forks coving in fire. "Peasants. Followers, your god needs you. Hold them off." Pointing towards the crowd of people that were coming. "AWEEE!" They began to race up the hill towards sudden death. Their screams were beginning to get mumbled over by the tremendous thunder that had began to roll in. The rain came down a little hard, slamming the fire in every little direction, placing smoke in replace of the fire. "NOO!" Screams the mystery man and self proclaim demon. "You will die tonight and I will take my place inside you to be reborn." "HELP ME!" One by one, the slaughter began. Swords slicing into body’s, ripping their arms off with one slice. Blood went down the trail hill, rushing into the river below. The crowd began to get closer to him, racing for the safety of what god would want. As they got closer, the demon, the mystery man took his knife, pressing it against the girl's left inner thigh, he jerked, making a quick and precise slice into the femoral artery, moving to the right thigh, the same was done as the blood began to rapidly flow from the wounds, soaking down the front of her dress, around her throat and into her hair. Eventually it would also drip to the center of that pentagram placed in the earth. Her eyes glistened in a mix of pain and sweet surrender. The longer she bled, the less it hurt. "…uuughnnn…" Her last breath came by. "Till the cross is mine I’ll be forever in your family…", His words trickling out from his mouth with her last sigh. The crowd came right to his back. One man, lifting up a giant sword and impaling him from the back. His eyes, turning from the bright blue that they were to a sharp red color. As if blood was filling them up. Slowly his breath went away, falling against the chest of the dead woman. The crowd began to settle, standing in the rain as it began to pour the blood away. The demon was dead. The girl was dead. There was a sudden lift of smoke going towards the girls mouth, filling her up. The trembling sounds of fear began to go over the crowd as the rain continues to pour down. -------------------------------------- Several Hundred Years Later… "This damn rain…" The power of mother nature. She did everything against what you always wanted. She was harsh. She was crafty. She could blow you off course no mater where you were. It was night time. Pitch dark, whether you had glasses on or not. The sound of the wind was settle. The rain was coming down fiercely, bouncing off my head like a hammer on a nail. Other then that there was no sound. "What are we doing here?" Asking the man that came passing by the steel gate. "What do you think?" Replies Elia as she comes walking in behind him. His hand, tied in behind him with rope, twisting around so that they were locking together. A cemetery. A place covered in dead souls. Fleshless bones of people who had died many years ago. Forgotten family members or friends who either passed away with honor or without it. The man stood there, wearing a bright white outfit that resembled an inmate. His eyes, wiggling through all the flying leaves and other light weight stuff. Looking at the headstones, passing by ones with missing sides and colors that were so star struck, you couldn’t even see the names of them. "Elia, did your mother ever tell you about the history of this family?" The man says, turning around as they pass by the headstone of a young girl. "Johnny…", grinning a little. "I am the history of your family." Star struck hit the fan. His eyes, twisting like a mad man, looking right at death in the eyes like he was about to die. "What did you say?" Johnny asked. "You see Johnny, Elia is gone from her body and it’s only a matter of time before I find the cross and complete the ritual." Slapping him right in the chest to get him to go. "You’ll never do it. The guardians of the cross and the Smith family, my family, won’t ever let you near it. Besides, if time is right, you only have so long till…", pausing as he trips backwards. Landing smack on his ass, banging the back of his head, Johnny lays there, looking up at Elia. Her eyes, glowing with fear and anger inside. Johnny’s hand raises up from the ground, flipping off the plugs of dirt that were on his face. As he did, his eyes caught the glimpse of the name of the headstone he had tripped on. It was a name that gave him a great shock. The starting glimpse of a heart attack. A painful look that just froze him right where he sat. "Shocked?" Elia says. "What are you really here for?" Responding to the strong question. "Do I really need to explain it?" Laughing as the rain trickled down the side of her head. "Reverend Tim Jones. Founding father of the Holy Catholic Church. And descendent of one of the most important guardian of the cross." Johnny, pressing back in the mud that was now covering his fingers and hands, pressing back against the headstone that was there. His eyes began to show signs that he might know, that his mind might have a single clue as to what Elia Smith’s internal demon was talking about. "It’s simple Johnny. Roomer has it that he was buried right here with a clue as to where the cross lies. And tonight, that secret gets me one chance closer to fulfilling my destiny." Grinning from ear to ear as she tosses her hair back. Shaking his head, Johnny pressed up from the headstone, looking right up in to her eyes before responding. "I’ll never let you finish it. You’ll have to kill me…", raising up off the ground ready to strike but the blood thirsty Johnny was caught in a sudden shake of the wind. Steel, painful and harsh with it’s blow came around, slamming right into the head and upper face of Johnny from Elia. The lights of the world went out for him. His body, fell limp in the rainy dark night. Thunder began to crash down in the back ground, blasting out all the sounds of the fall that Johnny took. Landing on the ground, Elia stood over him, pausing in the darkness as her lips went with the motion, looking down at the motionless Johnny Crisp. "I won’t need to kill you…." Elia says with her dark eyes perching on Johnny. "You’ve already killed yourself." --------------------------------------------- 2 Hours later… The rain had stopped. The clouds were still covering up the bright stars that hung in the moonlight. The wind that was once blowing terribly, tearing down limb after limb of the trees that were scattered through out the cemetery had settled down as well. Johnny’s eyes began to creep open just a little bit. Flipping each lens up and down a few times to get a feel for were he was. The temperature of the area was going down. Right now, a bitter cold fifty degree’s. Blood was coming down his head as he took a look around the area. His eyes, wide open now, seeing something right in front of him that shook his feet as they stood. Turning to his right, his eyes caught the glimpsing glance of an open grave. The dirt, piling up in the front corner, showing the hole to be wide open. Johnny began to wiggle his feet but they weren’t moving. His arms were next but they happen to be the same. It was at if he was in a solid position. "Something wrong Johnny?" Johnny pauses for a moment. "Is that you Elia?" Johnny asks. Nothing. Not even a word came from the young girls mouth. Just the misty feeling of darkness. As Johnny began to wiggle more, he realized that he was indeed, tied down. Looking at the dark bark color that the piece of wood that was held up by the ground underneath him. His arms, hanging from the same substance. It might of took him a minute, an hour, or forever, but Johnny’s mouth went straight through the floor as he realized what he was hanging from. "CROSS!" Screaming. "Yes Johnny…", laughing in the background of the soulless field of death. "…A demo of the real cross of Vinaca. And you will be a victim of what I will do when I get a sacrifice with it." "Sacrifice? Come on Elia. You know you need me to find the cross. Killing me will end your search." Begging, Johnny began to pour out the tears from his two green looking eyes. "Need you?" Elia asks as she walks around the dirt pile, going towards the lower, the more disgusting man, Johnny. "I don’t need anyone and differently not you. I’m Reamond. The most powerful demon. And when I fully embrace the final step of my immortality, you will not get to see every man and woman beg just like you are doing." Her hands, wrapping around her waist, caressing her warm silk body that was underneath that black coat. But there was a reason that deep coat was on. Placing her right hand in to that deep plaque of space. Reaching for anything the imagination could founder up. But the one thing she did grab for, the one thing that history knew best, was the knife that murdered the first Smith victim, Samantha Smith "My god have mercy on you Reamond…", responding, Johnny closes his eyes, locking his lips as if he was letting in the hate, expecting what was about to go and letting out the fear. Elia, feeling the rush, brought the knife right into the gut of Johnny. Watching his eyes light open, widening with every inch of the knifes tearing of his inside. Feeling the blood that he holds dripping through the inter gut of his body. Johnny’s breath adjusted from a powerful bit of wind to a calm storm, blowing in and out like a dying patient. "…Uuunnnnhhh…", Johnny breaths out. Elia leans in a little bit, getting closer to the opening of his ear. "Tick tock, tick tock Johnny. It’s only a matter of time because your wish, your wish of me killing you…will not come true." laughing, Elia pulls away from him just so he can see her fully. "You see Johnny, I want you alive when its complete. Their coming for you and when they do, I want you to tell them my name…" Johnny’s eyes closed. Sealing shut with the pain that his gut was feeling. But from all the darkness that was surrounding him, one piece of light came through a crack. Johnny’s pupils came through the lids of his eyes, listening at the screams that came from the distance. "ANYONE OUT HERE?" The voice screams. "…Uuuhhh…over here…", Johnny says in a fading voice. The light gets bigger and bigger, flashing over the name of the headstone that was dug open. The name on it simply said… "Reamond…"
