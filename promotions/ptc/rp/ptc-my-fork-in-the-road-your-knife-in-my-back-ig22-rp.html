My Fork In The Road (Your Knife In My Back)(IG22 RP)

10 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Prelude: M.I.A. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ My first PTC event... It was supposed to be the greatest day of my life. It was supposed to be the end of a long road of pain and sacrifice. You were supposed to be there with me to share it with me. I looked out into the crowd just to see your face, hoping that you'd be there and things could go back to the way they were. But you didn't show up. You couldn't find time in your "busy" life to support the woman you were supposed to spend the rest of your life with. Now it's time for me to go. I just don't get it... I was always there for you, every step of the way I sacrificed my wants and needs to help you succeed. I put myself in the line of danger for years for your cause, your wants and your needs. I followed you to Global Wrestling where we were to start a new life in professional fighting. A personal dream of mine, it was nothing more than a game for you. Yet you did so well, and I got lost in your shadow. I stood by your side, never complaining about never getting booked. Never mentioning the fact that everyday someone jackass online would mention something about how worthless I was. Knowing damn well I was being held back in favor of you. Now I ventured out on my own and continued my dream. The spotlight was on me. People wanted to see more, I was the one succeeding. Following in your footsteps I was expecting you to be so happy for me. You were too busy to be happy for me. You were always too busy to give a damn about me or my wants and needs. I hate what you've become. That shell of a once great and inspiring man I once fell in love with. Now you're just pathetic. So selfish and paranoid about failure that you're bringing everyone else around you down with you. Not me, not this time. I'm flying above, with or without you. I'll be damned if you make this about you again. Fuck you... this my story. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ First Stanza: Movin' Out ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ A spark of lightning ignites the sky, silhouetting the outline of the Zenith Manor. One of Chicago, Illinois most recognizable residential buildings, it reminded most people of the haunted house from a scary made for television movie. If only the general public knew some of the things that had happened in that house. Never the less what was living inside of it. As raindrops beat against the ground like a hale of gunfire, a black limo began it's ascent towards the building. Up the winding drive, the headlights shone against the brick structure. It sat ominously atop a hill, surrounded by an almost forest like landscape. The Zenith Manor observed all the residential area below, but the large cast iron gate and rows of trees and brush blocked the view from below. Even if you were to see the building, the black tinted windows would prevent any spectators from finding out exactly what went on inside the mysterious estate. While the tinted windows were first installed as a privacy measure, they were increased at the beginning of the year with the arrival of Markus to the estate so he could roam about freely. Although, these days he rarely gets the opportunity. Inside the vehicle an eerie silence filled the cabin. Looking out the side window, her blue-green eyes looked back at her in the reflection. Brushing her blonde hair our of her eyes she watched the rain pour down from the heavens. It was almost symbolic for the woman known to most of the world as The Black Mariah, trying to hold back the tears staring up at her home. Well, what used to be her home. They say home is where the heart is, and she wanted her heart to be anywhere but here. Memories of approaching this same driveway overwhelmed her. The late nights wanting to come home and take a long shower and kicking her feet up. Those days were gone, always on the move, she was much more like a nomad than the million-dollar princess she was believed to be. As the vehicle slowly came to a stop, Miss Valentine closed her eyes and took a deep breath before looking out the window once again. Burning the image of the estate into her mind, Vivica slowly opened the door... much to the surprise of Marques who was approaching to open the door for her, like he's done the past several years. One glare from The Black Mariah made Marques back off a little. Usually Vivica was the most comfortable person for him to be around... but of all the hissy fits and temper tantrums he's seen her thrown, he'd never seen her quite like this before. The brash and loud-mouthed Vivica was quiet and calculating. Her actions began to remind Marques of Christian Zenith himself. As he popped the trunk, Vivica walked behind him and jerked her own empty duffle bags out. Not knowing what to do with himself, the housekeeper and errand runner of Zenith Manor simply stood away from her. He could see the fire in her eyes as another bolt of lightning sparked the sky. Anger, Pain, Hurt... all of the emotions could be seen in her eyes as she looked up at Zenith Manor from the steps leading up to the entrance. The rain beat down upon her face as she closed her eyes. It almost appeared as if she was crying as she tried to control her emotions. Maybe she was. "This can be fixed Miss Valentine..." Marques spoke from the other side of the limo as Vivica took a step forward, "You just need to talk it over. I'm sure Master Zenith is just a little carried away. You know how he gets sometimes. He probably lost track of time and..." "Stop making excuses for him Marques." Vivica interrupted, clearly trying to restrain herself from exploding, "I've heard them all for years." Never turning around to look at him, she kept walking towards the door. Focused on her goal, Vivica shoved the door open and looked around the room. Everything looked as it did last time. Untouched and aging. What has become of this beautiful house? "You're ruining everything!" Vivica screamed, "You're ruining your life, you're ruining my life, and you're ruining everything you've ever worked for!" "Wait!" Marques shouted towards The Black Mariah who slams the door shut and locks it behind her. Loud banging accompanies the sound of the downpour outside as Vivica makes her way towards the staircase. Her eyes burned with anger as she marched up the stairs towards Christian's office. Atop the staircase Vivica made a sharp right and tried to open the office, but it was locked. Screaming out Vivica began to pound on the door. "What don't you wanna see me Christian?!" Tears began to fall down her face as she slammed her fist into the door, "Don't you love me Christian?! Or do you love your precious fucking hunt for Tera more? I was ALWAYS there for you... you son of a bitch! Why aren't you here for me?! WHY?!" The once angered filled eyes of The Black Mariah quickly turned to hurt as she fell down to her knees outside the office. On the ground her fist remained against the door. Weeping like a baby, Vivica didn't know how to control herself anymore. The whole ride here she wanted to explode and seeing the estate only made it worse. Her gasps for air and moans of sadness echoed throughout the house. "Why..." She choked out between gasps of air, "Why don't you love me anymore?" The silence was much worse than anything The Midnight Messiah could've possibly said to her as she was crouched down outside his door. Waiting for anything. Just one word from his lips could've made her stronger. Yet the quiet was eating her away from the inside. Pulling herself up to her feet, she felt something behind her... "It's not worth your time." A voice startled her from behind. Turning around she looked into the cold eyes of Markus Eaux D'Artifice. He couldn't stand to look at her like this. He found it hard to believe the cared about the well being of someone he once hated so passionately. After all, what other impression should he have had upon meeting a woman hired to bring him back to Chicago collared like a wild animal. "I've heard that my entire life Markus..." Vivica replied, still choking out words through her tight throat. "No, I mean he's not here. He hasn't been home in three days." Markus said from behind her, "He's worked at the office, slept at the office, ate at the office. It's just been me and Marques... and he's not much company. I'd almost rather Christian be here carrying on about killing that assassin he's always talking about. It's at least more... entertaining." "Heh..." Vivica replied, Markus' dry sense of humor was lost upon The Black Mariah in this state. Her whole body was shaking in anger still. Although the presence of Markus seemed to have calmed her down a bit, it was typical for Christian to not even be around for her to confront. "I'll leave you alone to cry or something..." Markus said as he began to walk away. "Wait!" Vivica spoke up as he began to walk away. Markus stopped but never turned around, waiting for her to continue. "I don't understand..." Vivica goes on, "If you're so miserable here why do you stay?" "The same reason you've stayed for all these years I suppose," Markus paused, "I have nowhere else to go. My family thinks I'm dead, my girlfriend is dead. I can't let anyone else see me like this. I'm a fucking monster Vivica. It's not like I can just walk around outside like everyone else. Do you think someone would knowingly rent an apartment to a guy who sleeps all day and receives weekly shipments of blood?" "Others have done it before you, you know..." Vivica continued, "Look at Shadow and Virgil. To the best of my knowledge no one has tried to burn down the McFarlane Estate with torches and pitchforks. Not recently anyway." "Hey people I've barely met, can I stay with you?" Markus continued on, "Sorry Viv, I'm not the type to mooch. It's not my style. If I leave this place, it'll be on my own terms and it'll be alone. Don't you see? I'm obviously destined to wander alone forever until I either get killed or decide to kill myself.." "It doesn't have to be that way." Vivica retaliates, "I don't know about you, but I've had enough of this place. I've had enough of his shit. I've had enough of everything that follows around his shit. I have trouble even looking at this place anymore. I'm getting the hell out of here, and I don't know where I'm going or when I'm going to get there but it's not going to be here. It's only going to get better from here." Markus turned around and looked into the eyes of The Black Mariah. Just looking at her he could tell how serious she was about this. Her eyes were still bloodshot from the crying and her cheeks rubbed raw from wiping away from the tears. Her blonde hair with baby blue tips was messed up almost beyond repair and two duffle bags draped from her shoulders. She was a wreck, but if anything... she looked like she was a very confident wreck. She almost looked... caring. "You can come with me Markus." Vivica said, "Just you and me, and wherever we end up... as far as fucking away from here as we get." "Yeah last time was a blast..." Markus responds with fond memories of being locked up in a cage in an animal control truck in the hot deserts of Mexico. The shouting matches that accompanied them, and all the horrible times they had having to deal with each other when no one else was around. "Or you can stay here with Marques and The Conscious Crybaby." Vivica said, trying her best to crack a smirk, "Whatever you want to do. But I'm getting the hell out of this place." Walking past The Childe Of Destiny, Vivica entered her room and slammed the door behind her. Turning around, Markus looked at the Zenith Corporation logo that sat on the wall next to Christian's Office. Could he really just leave? Was it that easy all this time? Or would Christian just send a new goon after him just like he did last time when he entered Total J Cup. If there was one thing Markus learned since accepting his new unlife, it was that it doesn't seem possible to escape the sights of Christian Zenith. But if anyone could do it... it was The Black Mariah. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Second Stanza: Turn The Page ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ SLAM! The sound of the car door echoed around the tranquil night. A light breeze blew through her hair as she looked up at the building in front of her, making her blonde hair waving in front of her teal colored eyes. Wearing a black leather bomber style jacket over a black Bullet For My Valentine baby-tee and a pair of black and white Tripp pants, The Black Mariah stood next to the black 2006 Chrysler Crossfire. "This seems like home to me..." Vivica J. Valentine said aloud looking up at the building known throughout Toronto, Ontario, Canada simply as The McFarlane Estate. With a smirk across her face Vivica walked around to the back of her vehicle, wondering what would happen next. Not a single person inside even knew she was there. What if they didn't want her? What if there wasn't room? Although she'd been friends with Dawn McFarlane and Shadow for quite a while, she still wasn't sure if they'd accept her into their home. After all, the building was quite crowded enough. Lifting her bags out of the trunk, she put the straps over her shoulders when suddenly something startled her. SLAM! The slamming of a car door made her jump a bit. Perhaps in the back of her mind she was still worried that Christian was looking at her right now. Following her, tracking her like he did for Tera to make sure she didn't get away. Or maybe it was just wishful thinking that he'd care as much about her as he did about that sorry excuse for a sister that tried to kill him. Looking out from the trunk she tossed a bag over to her left, where it was caught by a man wearing a black trench coat with a hood covering his face. "Looks like we made it." Markus said holding onto his suitcase. "Yeah, and with a couple hours to spare until sunrise." Vivica said before slamming down the trunk of her vehicle. It was only 4 days since PTC vs Archibald McGregor had passed but everything had changed, it felt like months. Now she didn't have a home or a fiance. Almost all she had left was her GCW United States Championship... and the people who lay behind the doors of the building up ahead. She needed something more, something to feel like she belonged, something to make her feel needed. Anything... "Hey Markus?" Vivica asked as they began to walk towards the building. "What?" He replied, seeming a little nervous upon meeting Shadow again, the woman who claimed to be his new mother. "I wonder if PrimeTime Central has any other titles I can win?" Vivica replied with a smirk, "I'm still feeling a little lonely." "And I will never, ever be your lover again And as far as I am concerned we are not even friends This may not seem too subtle to you The point I am trying to make is we are completely through" -- "My Fork In The Road (Your Knife In My Back)" by Atreyu
