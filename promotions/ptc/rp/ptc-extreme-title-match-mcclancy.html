2 April 2004 Tables, chairs, bottles filled with all kinds of booze, Pool stick, billiard balls, Darts, drinking mugs, hanging lights. Yes that is describing all bars, but it also describes Sean’s playground since he could reach the bar. Sean’s way of life before he joined GIWF, all those years back. He trained in the back yard of his father’s pub and lived above it. Trained in the very ring that had trained many other, including his sister and twin younger brothers. This will not be the first brawl that Sean would be in. And it wouldn’t be the last either. Sean knows this match. Sean knows that this match will entail. Mike thinks he is the Marvelous one. He has no clue what he is in for. There is no way to prepare for this type of match outside of getting to into many bar fights. The camera moves thru the door of a dimly lit bar. A lone figure sits that the far end. He has a mug of something in his and one can only guess that it would be beer of some brand. The bar tender, or more properly, the publican, stands behind the long wooden bar drying a glass with a towel. He watches as the Camera moves closer. But still, he talks to the lone man. Publican: Looks like someone is ‘ere fer ye Seano. Ye can na’ ‘ide from these guys can ye? The accent… The looks… he has to be a McClancy. Sean looks up from his glass and smiles. The scruffy beard still adorns his face and his hair is slicked back into a pony tail. He is wearing a black T-shirt with white wording in a stylized Celtic font, the words that everyone knows by now POG ME HOIN. Sean: Ey, Welcome to me family’s pub. Welcome to this fine town of Dublin. I ‘ad figured ye’d find me. Surprised it took ye so long t’ do so. The interviewer steps forward. But it’s no nameless interviewer. It’s the bane of the non-wreslting existance of one Mike Marvelous. It’s none other then the 4WW’s own “Smart Mark” Matt Cummings. The only interviewer to ever get his ass handed to him in just about ninety-eight percent of all his interviews with either a Childress, or a McClancy. Let’s not forget a young lady named Anderson. Matt moves up slowly watching the publican, who has put down the glass and towel and is now standing with his arms folded. Matt: Hi Sean? Long time no see. Your brother-in-law and sister told me where I could find you. Although I find it funny I get to come here and interview you, while people like Eliza are showing up on Maelstrom III. We might as well get this started, right? You know you’re a week or so from your match with “Mr. MidCarder” Mike Marevlous, shouldn’t you be practicing? Sean Laughs Sean: I am! Er didn’na ye read where th’ match is takin’ place a’? Mikey-boy ‘as no chance in ‘ell t’ win this one. It’s me turn this time. Me type o’ match, me type o’ place. Me ground, na ‘is. I ‘ve the bar lined up an’ all. Cummings: So, this is where the match will take place? You’ve been in Ireland off and on for weeks now. He’ll have jet lag and all. You know he’ll be making exucses and such. Sean: Na’. Na’ goin’ ‘t give ‘im tha’ chance. But can I ask ye? Do ye be wantin’ t’ see ‘im bashed an’ beaten? ‘Er are ye wantin’ t’ see ‘im get ‘is jus’ dues? Cummings: What do you think? He stole my award with the 4WW, he stole my interview time. He’s belittled me, and basically been a pain in my ass. I want you to kill the pice of … Sean: We’ll leave it a’ tha’. I guess I can’ let ye in a lit’tle somethin’. The match will be takin’ place in a bar, in the states Bu’ ye see, it don’na matter. I’ve barely ‘eard from th’ man. ‘E’s hidin’ an shakin’ in ‘is wrestlin’ boots. ‘E’s lookin’ a’ takin’ a real vacation from th’ sport. An’ it’ll be at me ‘ands. Matt: Why do you think that the Marvelous one would be afraid of the likes of you? Sean: Th’ likes o’ me? As in, why would ‘e be scared o’ the Irish Bastard? Why would ‘e be scared o’ the man who’s ended many other careers? I dunno, ye tell me. Matt: You’ve been hiding out and haven’t gotten into the ring in a while, your looking bit on the scruffy side. What is Patti going to say when she sees the beard? Sean: Well, fer th’ first part, jus’ because I’m na’ beatin’ th’ likes ‘o Jerms, Summers ‘er Dream on a weekly basis on TV, don’na mean I’m na’ gettin’ me workou’s in. An’ fer the second part, I don’na know, why don’cha ask ‘er. Sean eyes go wide and he nods his head for Cummings to look over his shoulder. As he does the crowd that has been forming behind him part like the red sea itself and Miss. Patti Anderson comes into view. She’s wearing a pair of pants that look like they’ve been painted on and a halter top in a matching color and material. What ever it is, it’s shiny and purple. Patti: Hello Matt, now what the bloody hell are you doing here? Patti stares a hole clear through the man that she had put through a table once before, but her hazel gaze didn't rest there for long as she stared at Sean. Patti: What the bloody hell did you do to yourself? What is with the beard and growing your hair out long? You tell me you’re going home to fix things up, and here I find you sitting in the bar going all to bloody hell. Patti walked across the floor, her shoulder purposely bumping into Cummings and knocking him off balance for a short moment. Patti continued to stare at McClancy, still not sure whether she can handle the new look. Patti: Do you like you're look? I can get used to the hair, but the beard, that may prove to take sometime to get used to. Matt Cummings recoils back from Patti like she is the most poisonous snake from Australia , the Fear Snake itself. Matt: Hello, Umm, Miss. Anderson. How are you this fine Irish Day? Patti turns and faces Matt with a slight crinkled nose. Patti: Bloody fine until I smelled your cheap cologne. Sean moves forward to meet her and he wraps his huge arms around her body, in a great big bear hug. Sean: ‘Ow do ye think she is doing? We jus’ flew in last night, bu’ she didn’na stay up long. Ye are lookin’ a’ the new 4WW North Wind Champion. She’s goin’ t’ be spendin’ th’ week with me ‘ere. Anyways now tha’ you’re ‘ere, darling, I guess we can get this party started. I know after yer match last nigh’ ye chose t’ sleep in a bit. Now I’ll get t’ show ye around me ‘ome town an’ show ye th’ fine Emerald Isle. Bu’ first I ‘ave t’ entertain Matthew ‘ere. Mikey boy keeps calling me ou’, but he then goes int’ ‘idin’ and now wants t’ put th’ stipulation on th’ match tha’ if I lose, I dropout of the Extreme Division all together. Patti: And what is he bloody well putting up? Sean: When ‘E loses, ‘e drops out of wrestling, ye don’na think someone is afraid of me do ye? I feel bad fer my sister. She will be losing a medicore piece o’ talent when I am done with him. He should o’ never put ‘is career on th’ line. Some noise arises from the back of the bar, the noise of a bar fight that is about to begin. Sean: Aye, me trainin’ partners are getting’ ‘ere. They started a’ the pub down the street. Sean grabs his tankard of beer and downs it. He finishes it by slamming the tankard back down to the hard wood bar top. He wipes his scruffy beard with his hand and then gets up from his bar stool. He grabs Patti on either side of her face and pulls her into a kiss. Sean: Dearest why don’cha go an’ wait fer me a’ this address? I will be there shortly. Jus’ let th’ lady that answers th’ door know who ye are. It’ll be me mum. He lets her go, and then grabs Matt by the arm. Sean: Matt, come on. Let’s take a walk. The camera follows Sean and Matt Cummings out of the door. As the door closes you can hear something crashing on the other side of it, causing Matt to jump a bit. Cummings: Wait, we’re not still in the bar? Aren’t you staying for? Sean: One o’ these days, they will learn na t’ start a fight in tha’ bar. The owner is one tough bastard. I should know. Tha’s Duncan McClancy. Me pop. Matt: Really? So do you have anything to say about Mike Marvelous? Or PTC? Or the Extreme Title Division in General? Sean: First, The Division: there are some damn fine talented people in th’ running for me belt. If its one thing I know; it’s ye don’na underestimate the unknown. There is one tha’ is always bet’ter then ye, wantin’ yer spot more then ye do. As fer Mikey, I am tha’ one bet’ter tha’ wants ‘is spot. I know, I know, it ‘as been said thousands o’ times befer. I’m going t’ beat ye, I’m going t’ strangle ye, I’m going t’ leave yer body bloodied an’ broken. Now, that it ‘as been said, one more time, let’s get t’ wha’ this match is abou’. Tha’ title around Mr. Marvelous’ waist. I win tha’ title, an’ bring it back t’ me fed. That sounds like a good start t’ me. Cummings: But you and Marvelous are currently under contract with the 4WW. Isn’t this all about McClancy, and only Sean McClancy. Sean: Yer damn riogh’ it is. I’m done with this, be a company man type crap. Bu’, other then tha’ we have the stipulations that Mike ‘as put on this match. ‘Is first one: ‘is career when I win? All I ‘ave t’ say is this… Sorry Sis, and Ian. I ‘ope you have a backup fer this clown. I know ‘e’s one o’ yer tag team champions riogh’ now. I know ‘e’s a big named man in yer federation an’ all. I know ‘e’s ga’ some sort o’ stupid comedy movie deal comin’ up. Bu’ the boy was stupid enough t’ do this. Then ‘e gets all tha’ is truly comin’ t’ ‘im. Then ye have the second one. If by some poor chance in ‘ell, ‘e wins th’ match, I no longer compete in th’ Extreme Title Division fer PTC ever again. That one is a lit’tle ‘arder fer me t’ swallow. Bu’ I don’na see it ‘appening. Matt: You’re seeming more confident then you ever have been before. And I see Patti is here to cheer you on. That should make your night go better. Sean chuckles at that knowing how Matt really feels about Miss Patti. The Women on more then on occasion has beaten on Matt Cummings. He’s known to be a letch, and has been known to stare at some of the ladies in the many federations they’ve known each other in. As they stand there, two men walk up. Hulking giants that they are. They have the McClancy look about them. For the most part they’ve not been seen since some of the final days of the SCWF that Sean McClancy once graced as it’s two time Superior Champion. His younger brothers. Both over 300 lbs of muscle each bound the Fomorians. Sean: Tom, Pat wha’ are ye two doing ‘ere? Can’cha you see tha’ I’m slightly busy. Pat: Mum sent us t’ the bar lookin’ fer ye an’ pop said ye left riogh’ befer th’ fight. So we figured ye’d come out ‘ere. What’s th’ worm doing ‘ere? Matt cranks his neck trying to look up that the two huge hunks of meat standing in front of him. He shudders at the thought of if he said something wrong to them. Matt: Well I have to say, you two look good for taking so much time off and out of the ring. You two getting ready to maybe join up with the PWW once it opens? The twins look at each other and both smile. Tom: Sean, ga’ a message from Penny fer ye. She’s th’ one in charge ‘o th’ Extreme Division now. Ye need to call ‘er as soon as possible. It’s about yer match this week. The show has been picked an’ they need t’ ‘ave th’ number to the bar so PTC can set up t’ cover damages an’ costs. Insurance an all tha’ other crap, just incase the building collapses er somethin’ like tha’. Matt chuckles at that. Matt: Your “sister” making sure your Pop doesn’t go out of business, because of your ego? Sean stares at the smaller man. Sean: Who said it would be me father’s pub tha’ we are usin’ fer this? I though I jus’ said in there tha it was a place in th’ states? I don’na ever remember stating th’ place just the type of match I wanted. All ye need t’ know is that Ireland would never allow someone like Marvelous in withou’ provin’ tha’ ‘e was’na a cocoa drinkin’ pansy. So I’ve saved ‘im th’ trouble an’ found a place stateside. Anyways I have to get goin’. I’ve ga’ a North Wind Champion o’ a fiance tha’ is supposed t’ be meatin’ me mum an’ pop finally t’day. Ye’ll be getting’ a call from Fey, I’m sure on where t’ meet me fer th’ next interview, if I give ye one. Either tha’ ‘er ye can spot me an interview after I become the PTC Extreme Champion. Until then enjoy yer stay ‘ere in Ireland an’ ‘ave fun. Cummings: Yeah, give a few to Patti for me tonight. Huh? All three McClancy’s turn and stare at Matt for that one. He quickly decides to leave it where it was and we… Fade to green……… ---- 3 April 2004 Place about 3,000 feet above the earths Crust. After a slight pause Sean: Ahhh, yea’ love can ye ‘old on one minute? Patti is still sleeping; let me go ou’ t’ th’ front room. There is a slight pause and the door opens. Sean steps out Bare Chested with a set of light gray Sweat pants on. He closes the Door behind him, quietly. Sean: Yeah, whats up? Sean stops and listens again. Sean: Yeah, the whole clan will be in Las Vegas fer it. Another slight pause as the voice that now can be heard as a female talks again. Sean: Patti doesn’t know yet. It’s a surprise fer ‘er also. Ok, love, talk to ye then. Sean hangs up and sets the phone on the couch, above his head. He drifts off back to sleep. As he does, the door to the room on the plane opens and you can see Patti Anderson standing there in what looks to be a babydoll with tap pants in a purple satin. Her face has a look of death on it.
