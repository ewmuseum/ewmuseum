Opportunity Cost

19 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-19 Mal Somers toyed with the brown wooden box in his calloused hands as if it were some sort of plaything. But it was far from that. The box carried his hopes and fears as well as some things best left unsaid, that is until tonight. He got to his feet, and paced the well worn carpet of his small bedroom, holding the box in both hands. It was light, however the magnitude of what was contained within weighed heavily on his mind. It had done so for several years now, and Somers let a wry grin cross his face as he realised he had been in this same position on more than one occasion, treading a path on the deep pile, holding the box, thinking about what he should do next. The time was right, well as right as could be. No time ever seemed like the right time, and as his stomach churned like a washing machine that had seen better days he decided that tonight was as good a time as any. He stopped pacing and turned his gaze to the photograph that sat proudly on the chest of drawers on his side of the bed. It was a picture of himself and his girlfriend Penny, making goofy faces at one another at the back of the Staten Island Ferry, what seemed like a lifetime ago. When Mal first started wrestling over five years ago, Penny was assigned to him as his valet. A clever turn of phrase had her accompanying Somers as "Miss Penny Wise" his dutiful personal assistant, coming out to the ring with him in a business suit and thick rimmed reading glasses. Mal chuckled to himself as he knew that Penny was hopeless with figures, but she played the part well. Hopeless with figures, but a great figure Mal thought to himself. It was pretty much love at first sight, and they had been together ever since, through all the ups and downs that the rollercoaster of life has to offer. He wouldn’t have had it any other way. But now, he stood there in his room, and realised he had never felt more alone. He would never have true happiness unless he shared the secrets that the box contained with his beloved. The more he thought about it, Mal realised he should have shared the secret sooner. Coming home early to make sure she hadn’t checked the mail, hiding bank statements from her, having a sinking feeling in the pits of his stomach when he got home and she looked at him in a certain way. Does she know the secret? He could never be too sure, and it had plagued him for years now. He started to open the box but he couldn’t. He never could, no matter how much he tried. Suddenly there was a sound from the hallway, and Somers nearly jumped out of his skin. Penny wasn’t due home from her six-week long visit to see her family for another hour and yet he could hear her keys slam on the kitchen bench as she entered his apartment. Somers dropped the box towards the floor, and he instinctively kicked out at it, deflecting it under the bed. The door opened, and Somers hoped to God that the box had landed far enough under the bed. "Hey babe" greeted Penny, smiling widely. His breath was already taken away, as all the thoughts he had just evaporated like steam from a boiling kettle. "Hey Penny, you’re home early!" chuckled Mal, a little too uneasily. She was already at him, her arms around him, her lips at his neck, kissing away. "Alrighty then!" exclaimed Mal, in a shocking Ace Ventura impersonation. He placed his hands in the small of her back and held on, going a little weak at the knees. Suddnely, she recoiled, stepping back, coughing. "Penny! What’s wrong?" screamed Mal as she spluttered away like a cute little car running out of gas. "You…you…stink! What the HELL?!" roared Penny, wiping her lips with the back of her hand, struggling for air. Mal sniffed the air, then under his arms. He frowned, putting his hands on hips and stared at the ground that he had been pacing just a minute earlier. "I, errr, hey you must need a drink," said Mal meekly. "I’ll go fix you one." He started to walk but her concerned glance stopped him dead in his tracks. "When was the last time you bathed?" Penny asked, direct as always, and Mal went bright red, as red as the red as the words "X-Corp" on his customary wrestling tights. He really did not want to answer that question, but yet he knew he had to. So, he mumbled. "Excuse me?" "Mrphahrrhahhmppphh" She just kept staring. He knew he was cornered. "They night before you went away?" Mal said quizzically, even though what he was saying was correct. Miss Wise’s jaw dropped, and Mal giggled. "You think that’s funny?" "No!" "Why?" Another question he didn’t want to respond to. She may not have understood the answer. Penny sat on the edge of the bed and then shook her head at him. "On second thought, I don’t want to know. One thing is for sure, I have not seen you for weeks, so you better go wash up and then we will pick things up where we left off." She fluttered her long eyelashes at him, but he stood dead in his tracks for a moment. She started to speak, but he shrugged and walked past her, down the hall and finally into the bathroom, closing the door behind him. Mal started the shower and then he turned and leant against the basin, and proceeded to stare at his own reflection in the mirror. As the steam fogged up the glass and his visage disappeared, Mal knew that Penny would be in the next room wondering why he didn’t race down the hallway like some randy teenager. It all started innocently enough. Mal farewelled Penny at the airport and then drove straight to his first GTT5 match against Dave Storm. He arrived at the arena, joked around with some of the guys he hadn’t seen in a long time and then made his way to the ring. He waited, and waited and then it was announced that Storm had not arrived! I was into round two without breaking a sweat! This is easy! Mal had thought to himself. He drove home, and relaxed, played some Scrabble with Rookie his prized retired Title belt and fell into a deep sleep, content that he had moved one step closer to the ultimate honour, winning GTT5. The next morning he awoke, and thought to himself that his next match against Django Smith was only a couple of weeks away. He hadn’t broken a sweat in round one, and heck, Penny was away. So he made the decision not to shower. He didn’t bathe before facing Storm, so why ruin a good thing? Only problem was, he did appear on several World Wrestling Alliance shows as well as finishing second in their annual Birthday Brawl Pay Per View to Ember. But still, Mal decided he would not shower for his GTT5 matches. So, two weeks later, Mal drove to the arena, in fact he would have flown but he was aware that the was emitting a strong odour. He arrived at the arena, said hello to the same people he said hello to last time around and made his way to the ring. Mal could feel the steam in the bathroom drip off his face as he remembered the announcers call; "Your winner, as a result of a forfeit, and making his way to Round 3 of GTT5…Mal Somers!" Mal stood alone in the ring, smelly, and looked around at the crowd. Sure they clapped, but they were waiting to see competitors like Digital Mortality, Shanty Jones and Masquerade battle it out in the squared circle. He felt a little…hollow. It wasn’t his fault that the "no-showering" trick was working…heck it had worked for Almasy for years! Mal was a former WWA World Champion, he had challenged for the PTC Global Title, he knew he could mix it with the big guns of PTC. He knew it, but wanted the opportunity to make everyone else know. But nevertheless, this trick of not showering was proving very tempting. The time between Round 2 and Round 3 was a little more trying. His World Wrestling Alliance colleagues would quickly wriggle out of rest holds, so horrible was the stench eminating from him. He had to order take away and slide the money under the door. Even Rookie was giving him the cold shoulder, but still he continued. He drove to the arena, took cover in a dustbin and when the coast was clear, he ran out to the ring. He waited for Arch Angel to arrive, but alas; "Your winner, as a result of a forfeit, and making his way to Round 4 of GTT5…Mal Somers!" Even though he was standing taller than them, Mal could sense that the crowd were looking down their noses at him. That is, until they caught a whiff and covered them for dear life. He knew he wanted a hard fought match and he knew that was what the fans had paid their hard fought cash for. But yet…the longer he went without washing, the longer his winning streak was extending…as well as some other streaks that did not bear recalling. Mal was now in Round Four, facing three of the toughest and most respected competitors in the business. Masquerade, Digital Mortality and Shanty Jones. He knew he had what it took, to come second… You don’t even have to win to advance! But yet, if he survived the week without a wash, then surely he would win the match. It was scientifically proven! As the water kept running, Mal snapped back to reality and knew he had been in the bathroom long enough. Hopefully Penny had fallen asleep from the long flight, and Mal could sleep in the bathtub, a can of deodorant at the ready. Suddenly there was a knock at the door. Mal gulped. "Honey?" "Almost done babe!" The next fifteen words chilled him to the bone. "I’ve just opened this box I found. Is there something you should be telling me?" Before he knew it, Somers had jumped fully clothed into the scalding hot shower. Somehow he still felt cold. As he felt his Round Four aspirations wash away down the drain, he knew it was time to come clean.
