PTC Tag Team Title Contenders Match

14 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Pain Syndicate's RP Title: Working Hard for the Money --------------------------------------- Desolation: I like this number four horse, in race eight. She's got a good blood line. (A head shot of Desolation is shown as he talks into his cell phone while reading from a folded race program.) Desolation: That's like, what.... 50... 60 dollars to place? Right... (A hand reaches in and grabs the cell phone. A full shot of the entire room is shown and a balding man with a goatee and a gray suit presses the "end call" button and clasps the phone shut. Desolation's brother Agony sits across from him at a long table, the kind you would see in a meeting of shareholders at any major corporation. The balding man in the gray suit is standing at the front of the room. A projector points at a pull-down screen on the front wall.) Balding Guy: Sir, as you financial advisor, I suggest you pay attention to my presentation. (Agony waves his index finger at his brother and "tsk"s him, mockingly. Desolation kicks Agony's legs underneath the table. He yelps and grabs at his shin.) Agony: Ow! (Agony throws a wad of paper that strikes Des in the forehead. Des picks up the wad off his lap and wings it back at his brother.) Balding Guy: Boys. BOYS! (The Pain Syndicate stops bickering and they look up intently at their financial advisor) Balding Guy: May I please continue? Desolation: By all means. (The financial advisor dims the lights and presses a button on the black device in his hand, starting the projector. Desolation shoots a dirty look towards Agony. The projector displays a graph showing a steadily decreasing red line.) Balding Guy: As you can can see from this graph, the fiscal first quarter earnings are substantially lower then the totals from last year. Far lower than what our analysts expected. Part of this can be blamed on the state of the economy, but most of your losses can be attributed to your obtuse professions. (The Pain Syndicate stare blankly, trying their hardest to absorb their financial advisors words, but thoroughly confused by his elevated English. Agony is the first to speak.) Agony: Meaning... Balding Guy: You're broke. So broke, that I dried up three red pens to write your last financial statement. So broke, you can't even afford to pay me or my analysts for our services. And, it's all because your occupations just aren't generating enough revenue to accomodate your lavish lifestyle. Desolation: Which occupation? Wrestling or the crime business. Balding Guy: Both. Sports Entertainment hasn't been a profitable business since the wrestling boom of the mid 90s. Organized crime hasn't generated significant capital in... God... 70 years. Neither can support your over-spending. (Desolation clasps his hands together and nods affirmatively, hoping that this act hides the fact that he is thoroughly bamboozled.) Balding Guy: I see only two options. #1: Win gold. And lots of it. The WWA tag team titles are a nice first step, but you really need to win this PTC tag title contendership match, and then beat the New Royal Legion. The more gold you win, the more chances there are for you to get noticed and the more endorsment deals you'll recieve. Desolation: And option #2... Balding Guy: Get a real job. If you don't win the PTC Tag Team Titles, then you'll need something to fall back on. (The Pain Syndicate sit in stunned silence) Balding Guy: I'll leave you two to talk amongst yourselves. (The financial advisor leaves the room and Desolation and Agony stare at each other for a moment.) Both: Get a real job. FADE TO LATER IN THE WEEK (Desolation sits on a stool in front of a painting canvas. Holding a pallette in one hand, he swashes his brush to-and-fro, splattering paint all over the canvas. Several art collectors, both men and women, stand behind him, trying to get a look at his artwork, but, everytime one of them glimpses over his shoulder, Desolation suddenly jerks his body to block their view. Desolation makes one final brush stroke.) Desolation: C'est finis! (Des removes the canvas from the easel and turns around with it still in his hands. The collectors seem shocked as they get the first look at Des's masterpiece: a stick figure woman with an ample bussom.) Desolation: Just the way I likes 'em: busty and as thin as a rail. Now, let's start the bidding at... oh... $400,000. (The collectors look at each other, collectively shake their heads, and walk away.) Desolation: C'mon! I need the money! TRANSITION TO A LATER DAY (Agony, wearing a white apron and a chef's hat, stands over a cutting board and next to a hot stove. A tall man with a clipboard stands next to him, looking over his shoulder and taking notes. Agony looks almost like a professional chef as something sizzles in a pan and the sound of a knife chopping rapidly reverberates through the kitchen. Agony lifts the pan off the stove, flipping whatever is in it. He wipes his brow with his sleeve and turns off the stove.) Agony: It's magnificent, isn't it? Man with Clipboard: There has to be 1000 ways that you can make a grilled cheese sandwich, and you found the one way to do it wrong. (Agony lifts a mound of mulitated bread and dripping cheese out of the pan with a spatula, offering it to the man with the clipboard.) Agony: Come on. It's carb friendly. (Agony places a fistful of raw ground beef onto the grilled cheese sandwich) Agony: Mmmmm... nothing like a grilled cheese a la meat. (The man with the clipboard looks at Agony without expression.) Man with Clipboard: Get out of my kitchen. (The man with the clipboard walks off) Agony: I'm a WWA Tag Team Champion, for christ's sake! I'm a contender for the PTC Tag Team titles! Just give me a job! I don't even have enough money to buy plane tickets to get to my next match! Please... hey! I saw that! (Agony runs off camera, after the man with the clipboard) Agony: You better put that finger down! ANOTHER DAY, ANOTHER JOB (A pack of five sprinters make their way around a running track. Trailing the group, but still putting up a valiant effort, is Desolation, who's wearing a revealing pair of blue running shorts and a blue tank top with the word "TRACK" written in white letters over top of a picture of a shoe with a wing on it.) Desolation: Huff... huff... huff... (Suddenly, Desolation begins gaining ground on the leaders. In no time, he is ahead of the rest of the runners.) Desolation: Huff... huff... huff... (Out of no where, wearing his street clothes, Agony catches up with all the runners and strides side by side with Des. Agony reaches into the pocket of his leather jacket and pulls out a single cigarette. He lights it and then holds it in front of Des's face. Des takes a long drag. Agony pulls the cigarette away and moves off camera.) Desolaiton: Huff... huff... huff... (Desolation continues to lead the pack, but he begins to wheeze and cough and starts losing ground. He grabs his side and falls to the ground. The other runners are able to jump over him, but Des isn't out of the race yet. He stumbles to his feet and tackles two of the other runners. He reaches up and grabs the ankles of the other athletes, tripping them as well. He gets back to his feet and races to the finish line. He raises his arms in the air victoriously.) Desolation: I won! I won! Where's my prize money? LATER, AT A PAWN SHOP (Agony stands in front of a counter as a guy with small magnifying glass examines a ruby pinky ring.) Pawn Shop Guy: While, this is beautifully crafted, I don't think this is real gold OR a real ruby. (The Pawn Shop Guy looks up at Agony) Pawn Shop Guy: And what is this purple stuff all over it? Agony: Jelly doughnut. Pawn Shop Guy: I'm sorry, but this is of no value to me. (Agony sighs. Desolation appears from off camera, behind Agony. Agony turns to his brother.) Agony: Well, I guess we'll have to win the Primetime Central Tag Titles, if we want to get out of our financial troubles. Pawn Shop Guy: Primetime Central Tag Team Titles, you say. Now, I would pay a pretty penny for those belts. Granted, they don't have jelly filling all over them. Desolation: Really... Agony: Don't even think about it! FADE OUT
