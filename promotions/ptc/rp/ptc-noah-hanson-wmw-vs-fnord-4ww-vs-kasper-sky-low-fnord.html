Noah Hanson(WMW) vs Fnord(4WW) vs Kasper Sky(LOW)

19 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Grocery Store: a place of relaxation; a place where one can gather what contents they need to make an enjoyable meal for their family; a store where groceries are sold; a store selling foodstuffs and various household supplies. This is an interesting place for three wrestlers in the PTC Extreme Division to battle it out. I personally, can think of many different ways of using the grocery store supplies. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Fnord has changed as of late. He’s been possessed by two Egyptian Gods of past, Horus and Seth. The two Gods battle inside of him and this causes him to have the power to control both of them. Now, he was somewhat crazy before. This event has pushed him over the edge. If he ever was psychotic, it is now.] [The scene is a northwest suburb of Chicago, Illinois. It’s about three in the morning and Fnord is out to play. As he steps out of his limo, his leather coat drags on the ground a tad. A falcon rushes through the sky and lands on his arm, gripping on as tight as possible. The falcon and Fnord seem to be communicating and then the falcon flies away. Fnord steps towards the brightly lit building. This is the neighborhood ‘open 24 hours’ Jewel/Osco. Fnord wearing his normal all leather get-up and goggle sunglasses steps up to the door, which opens automatically. Yet, Fnord doesn’t step through the door. He stares oddly at the sensor that opens and closes the door automatically.] Fnord: What an odd contraption… [Fnord puts his foot in front of the sensor and it opens. He removes it and it closes. He gets a huge child-like smile on his face and starts to hop back and forth looking totally foolish, making the door open and close. He jumps in front of the sensor one last time and then just stops. He shakes his head as if something was loose and his face turns back into the stern look he had before. He walks through the entrance and into Jewel.] Fnord: … [Fnord looks around in amazement on how busy Jewel can be at three in the morning. The waitresses that get off work pick up groceries afterwards. Some drunk or drugged-up teenagers are looking for munchies. One whole ‘fat family’ consisting of a fat father, a fat mother, a fat son and a fat daughter are all crowding the area. There are others around, but when the time comes, they will be mentioned. Fnord sees a hurried waitress grab a shopping cart and zoom away with it. So, he too walks over to where the shopping carts are and pulls one out.] Fnord: To carry my supplies… It seems sturdy enough… [Fnord grabs the shopping cart and starts to push is along. It starts to hobble because one of the wheels is loose and has some sticky crap on it. Fnord starts to get irritated and his eyes turn completely red. He lifts up the shopping cart and throws it into a display of 12 packs of Diet Pepsi. You can almost see the cartoon-like smoke emerging from his ears as he stares towards the cart. One of the teenagers that totally looks drunk/high walks by pushing a cart full of snacks that seems to be running smoothly. Fnord pushes the teenager out of the way and dumps the contents out of the cart.] Fnord: This is now mine. [Fnord pushes his new cart to the first isle. All the way to the left of the grocery store is the produce section.] Fnord: This is where the little chicks get their food that they don’t barf up. I can see that Kasper Sky chick buying her lettuce or whatever the hell those chicks eat here. [Fnord walks past the lettuce and hocks up a huge wad of spit, which he does spit on the lettuce. An anorexic looking chick walks by and sees this. She gets disgusted and turns to the side of her shopping cart and pukes.] Fnord: Oh, like you needed an excuse to do that! [Fnord pushes his cart away from the puking chick and far away from the vegetables. He goes all the way to the back of the store where the bakery and ‘hot chicken’ is.] Fnord: Ah, THIS is paradise! [Fnord walks over to there the heat lamps are keeping BBQ chicken, fried chicken, hot wings, and potato wedges warm. The lady behind the counter, who is currently cutting some meat (not cheese, which would be gross), looks up towards Fnord.] Lady: Yeah… what ya want? [Fnord gives a curious but evil look towards the lady behind the counter.] Fnord: Do not speak to me as if YOU are above me! You insolent fool! [The lady, who we now just notice is annoyingly chewing gum, stops cutting the meat and turns towards Fnord. She puts her thin plastic gloved hands on her hips, tilts her head to the side, and gives a nonchalant look towards Fnord.] Lady: Look, I gots things to do. Either you want some damn chicken, or I gotta go cut this meat… So what is it? [Fnord sternly looks towards her. He looks up at the menu and the prices and then he smiles. He looks back down at the meat-cutting lady.] Fnord: Three pounds of Wing Zings… Three pounds of potato wedges… Lady: Will that be it? [Fnord glances at the food and then back at the menu and then back at the lady.] Fnord: That’ll do pig, that’ll do… [The woman gets a little red in the face as she weighs the chicken wings, or as Jewel calls them ‘Wing Zings’. She bags them all up and pops one more in the bag while giving Fnord a wink. Fnord winces back as he glares at the twenty foot by twenty-foot mole hanging off her nose. The hair almost pokes hi m in the eye! Fnord nods and takes his potato wedges and Wing Zings. The throws them in his cart and runs away, pushing his cart as fast as possible to the other end of the store. He gets to the dairy section at the other end of the back of the grocery store and turns around. The meat-cutting lady is waving at him from the other end of the store! Fnord quickly cuts a left turn into the frozen food section.] Fnord: THAT woman is scary! I think she might be related to that chick Kasper Sky. They look a little similar! Now, what can be used in this isle? [Fnord starts to look around at his surroundings. The glass doors and all of the frozen goodness…] Fnord: It’s like an icy heaven! [One of the members of the ‘Fat Family’ is busy looking through some hot pockets when you can almost see the light bulb go off over Fnord’s head. He smiles real big as he leaves his shopping cart and starts to lurk over to the ‘Fat Father’ very evil-like. Fnord sneaks over and looks over the fat man’s shoulder. Gallons and gallons of drool are flowing out of his mouth because the breakfast Hot-Pockets are on sale. The fat man grabs about 6 packages of them and turns around to see Fnord right in his face.] Fnord: Excuse me sir. I need to show Noah something. [Fnord knocks the half a dozen hot pockets out of the fat man’s arms and slams his head into the glass door. At first, it does nothing but bounce off like a soccer ball. He does it a second time and glass goes everywhere! The fat man lets out a squeal that embarrasses all little girls in the world as his face starts to gush cherry-red blood. He runs, more like waddles fast, away crying and holding his face.] Fnord: Stupid fat fuck… [The other members of his fat family walk into the isle looking very angry. His fat wife, fat son, and fat daughter all charge towards Fnord swinging their fat arms, fat hands, and pudgy fingers. They let out a fat battle cry.] Fat Family: AAAHHHH! *OINK!* [Swear there was an oink here and there. So, the fat family all run after Fnord. Fnord, being the badass that he is, stands there ready to take them on. The fat daughter gets to him first, seeing that she was the less fat of them all. Fnord grabs her by the pigtails and puts her fat head through the door. She is in licking distance to the frozen burritos. The only reason this is known is because, as bloody and hurt as she is, she still tries to lick the burritos.] Fnord: ¡Obtenga esos burritos, el perro! [The fat wife runs towards him. She is actually scary looking to Fnord. She’s the spitting image of Kasper Sky, but added a few pounds here and there… Fnord starts to run away.] Fnord: No! Don’t do it Kasper! [This stops here and she starts to think. Calling her Kasper must have confused her. Maybe that’s her other daughter’s name? Who knows! Using the confusion to his advantage, Fnord opens up one of the glass doors and brings out a 24-pack of frozen waffles, blueberry of course.] Fnord: You’re mine, bitch! [Like a dog hearing a noise, her ears perk up and she turns her head towards Fnord. Fnord breaks open the package of frozen blueberry waffles and starts to Chinese Star-style launch them at the fat woman. The first few are caught in her mouth and she happily chews them up. The fifth hits her square in the forehead knocking her down on her back. She rolls over to her stomach and attempts to get up, but she can’t move. She rolls back over to her back and tries to get up, but with no avail! Now, she’s stuck like a turtle on her back. And since the fat son can’t see anything below his enormous gut, he trips right over her head and he falls right on top of her… face… in… crotch!] Fnord: That’s gotta smell! [The fat son lifts his head, looking very dizzy. He starts to get up, kneeing his fat mom in the face in the process. With his drunk-like demeanor, he tumbles back to the ground as soon as he gets to his feet. He is out cold for days probably. Fnord drops the rest of the frozen blueberry waffles and dusts the frozen crap off of his hands.] Fnord: Fat people suck! [Fnord turns around to see a couple of security guards standing there in complete shock. The father is hovering in the back of the isle still crying. The fat daughter is head first through a glass door trying to lick some frozen burritos. The fat mom is stuck like a turtle; she ain’t getting up unless they bring in a crane. And the fat son is KO’d on the ground. The two security guards right away go for Fnord.] Fnord: Hey, I was defending myself from the fat family! [They both go for an arm, but very stooge like, Fnord moves out of the way and the two guards crash into each other. They crumble to the ground in a heap. Fnord looks down at them and shakes his head.] Fnord: I guess my fun here is done… you bastards… [Fnord looks back at his Wing Zings, Zing Wings, or whatever the hell they are called and a single tear leaves his eye. He lowers his head to the ground and makes for the exit door. All of a sudden, one of his trusty falcons, Seth I believe, swoops over to the shopping cart and picks up the chicken wings. He zooms back over and lands on Fnord’s shoulder. Fnord grabs the bag of chicken wings and brightens back up.] Fnord: I love you Seth! [Fnord gives the falcon, obviously named Seth, a huge hug. The falcon barely gets away and it flies off towards the pet supplies. Fnord takes his chicken wings to the checkout line and sees that there are about a dozen full shopping carts in the only line open. Fnord asks the many different people if he can get ahead of them with his only one thing, and they all nod and smile. That is they all do it until Fnord gets to the last person. It’s the cursed evil SOCCER MOM! She must be doing some late-night shopping after hoaring herself off on the corner. She completely ignores Fnord.] Fnord: Excuse me ma’am. Can I… [She abruptly turns around and sees Fnord in his complete leather wrestling gear. Her jaw nearly falls off.] Fnord: I guess that’s a yes… [Fnord moves her out of the way as she stands there as still as a statue holding her coupons. Fnord gets up to the checkout dude, who’s an 18-year-old pimply guy. His nametag says ‘NOAH’ and this makes Fnord chuckle.] Fnord: So… Noah is it? [The pimply teen Noah just tiredly stares at Fnord.] Fnord: How much do I owe ya? Noah: $10.42 Fnord: WHAT! Fine… Let me get it for you… you damn robber… [Fnord goes for his pocket and then remembers that he’s in his wrestling gear. His face gets a tad red as he realizes that he has no pockets.] Fnord: Yeah… about that money… [Fnord grabs his chicken wings and runs out the door. It doesn’t open at first, so he slams into it. But then he stands back and lets the door open all the way. And then he books it out of the door and down the street laughing very evil-like!] ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ So, I’m not a big fan of grocery stores. Hell, I’m probably outlawed to be in a few of them here and there. Did you see what I did to that fat family?! They were freakin’ disgusting! That’s the same thing I am going to do to Kasper and Noah. Hell, they don’t have a chance against me. I’ve been studying grocery stores all over the country and I’ve learned how to use them for my advantage. A little baking powder in the eyes… A frozen blueberry waffle to the forehead… A frozen glass door… Hot grease from where they make those nice chicken wings… A squash up the ass! These are just some of the skills that I learned while traveling through the grocery stores. I gots skillz in the grocery store! Kasper the friendly hoar and Noah with his damn animals… You two are going down and I’m going up and up and up and up the Extreme Title Division! You two will be sorry that you EVER tried challenging me! MUWAHAHAHAHAHAHA!
