Everybody Dies In The End (part 1)

14 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-14 12/15 12 noon A poster of Claude Rains in his staring role in the 1933 horror flick Invisible Man hangs proudly on the wall of the bleak and bare apartment. Claude was one of those actors who acted in everything from Casablanca to Lawrence of Arabia but never was a top star. Most assassins admire him for playing their hero the Invisible Man, but also for a great career of playing supporting secondary characters. An assassin is just that a supporting actor in an action film. First off action films never get nods come Oscar time and especially secondary characters or guys like Tom Sizemore would be as well known as Tom Hanks. You know the names of The Boston Strangler, Son of Sam, Jesus Christ, Hitler, and a list of serial killers that could go on and on. Now name one convicted assassin. Assassins have three names and unless they kill a president or a Beatle they are forgotten about or never caught. I guess the expectation would be Charles Whitman. Charles was a university of Texas student who on August 1, 1966 killed his mother then climbed to the top of the University of Texas observation tower with a gun in his hand and took the lives of 14 people and wounded 33 others before ending his own life. Most assassins don’t consider Charles a true assassin since he was just crazy and not doing it for hire or pleasure. Just a nutty kid with a gun and given the current cost of a college education I’m surprise we haven’t see it happen again. To be an assassin you must be invisible in a very real world. No personality, a nondescript look, and the ability to speak yet not be remembered. Al Gore would have been a great assassin but he went down the corrupt and evil road of politics. Not a very noble profession in the eyes of most of the world then again neither is pro-wrestling which has to make you question if Jesse Ventura is indeed Satan or not. Our assassin Donald Oscar Grabowski stares at the poster as he finishes his two hundred and fifty morning sit ups. Most criminals are influenced by the devils in their veins but not an assassin their high comes from killing and Donald Oscar Grabowki is no different. His first kill was at fourteen when he shot a high ranking mafia capo from a tree house bunker with a snipers rifle he was trained on by another mafia family. Now fifteen years later Donald Oscar Grabowski is the world’s top assassin killing more then thirty three units and has never even been brought in for questioning. He fits the role of an ideal assassin standing 5’7, with dark hair, and no distinguishing features or traits. The kind of guy who Tom Sizemore would ignore at a party and Claude Rains would respect. A week ago Donald Oscar Grabowski was met in an empty parking garage where he was hired to kill a political powerhouse slash mafia don, Hoyt Williams. It would not be easy as the RISP has become so powerful in the United States stealing from organized crime one day and buying politicians the next they were well protected. Allegedly the RISP is under the direction of God but many including Donald Oscar Grabowski’s new boss points the finger at the public head of the RISP Mr. Hoyt D. Williams, professional wrestler and perpetual leader of the world’s largest secret society the RISP. A less then stellar hitman attempted to take Hoyt out two weeks ago but the plans were foiled when Hoyt’s special guest at a speaking rally turned out to be the President of The United States George W. Bush. Security was so beefed up it was impossible to penetrate and the hitman had to pull out. He was then eliminated himself after he failed his mystery boss. Donald knew this but the pay check of 5 million dollars was too hard to pass up. Donald dressed in his a non-descript suit as he prepared to go to work just as any nine to fiver would do. He buttoned his dress shirt, adjusted his black tie, put on his pants, combed back his hair, and then slipped his foot into his shoes. The one superstition he has is to always tie his shoes left lace first in a perfect bow then give him self a prep talk in the bathroom mirror. Donald Oscar Grabowski: If God exists and has the power to give life and take it away why do men like me still stand? Perhaps God isn’t as powerful as one would like to believe an amateur or more so a fabrication of self serving men looking to control. HEY GOD, FUCK YOU I TAKE AWAY WHAT YOU CREATE. You don’t have the balls to stop my rein of terror you fucking amateur. Donald takes his penis out of suit pants through the zipper and begins to stroke with a thunderous rage. He looks like Charlie Daniels fiddling hot and heavy on the Orange Blossom Special or perhaps a construction worker jack hammering a New York street. Donald Oscar Grabowski: I own you because I don’t fear you, or your fucking vengeance. HEY DICKHEAD I KNOW HOW TO SWIM SO BRING ON THE THUNDER CLOUDS. You see the difference between you and me God is I take action while you watch. You fucking sadist. You allow the world to rot while you sit on your fucking throne with a thumb up your ass and a sick smirk on your face. Why so passive if you’re such a God damn tough guy? You are the Shepard, the world is your flock, and I am the Big Bad fucking Wolf and there isn’t shit you can do with your staff that I can’t do with my teeth you weak fucking sap. GOD FUCK YOU AHHHHHHHHHH Donald orgasms all over the cabinet, dripping a mess of DNA onto the floor. He runs the water washing his hands and private parts smiling and winking at himself in the mirror. Besides being a sociopath Donald also has a touch of narcissism a trait of all hitman who have to think they are the most important person in the world to do what they do. Again, Al Gore would have been perfect at this. 12/15 1:13pm Today is the day Hoyt Williams hosts one of his largest events selling out the Houston Astro dome for a sermon and a healing session in they styling of Benny Hinn. Also if Donald has his way this is the day Hoyt dies not in the styling of Jesus but more in the styling of Hoyt’s contemporary peer JFK also perhaps ironically in the state he met his untimely death. In many ways Hoyt and JFK have a lot in common one loves flying the other is an airport. Both have famous fathers, who enjoy drinking. Both are good at conning the world with their boyish good looks and powerful speaking style. Donald arrives right on time for his meet and greet with Hoyt Williams. He obtained this privilege by rigging a radio contest on the gospel station. Donald passes through security which really isn’t an issue as most people at these religious events are very lax. The crowd is mostly obese African-American women who if they spent more time on the tread mill and less time on their knees praying for god to help them they would probably have less salvation and a smaller ass. Donald isn’t a fan of shines as they make terrible hitman and even worse employers. He took on a job from a gang leader two years ago where he was stiffed on the bill causing him to make the gang leader who hired him a stiff. From that day forward Donald not only didn’t like blacks he also didn’t trust them. Jews were the best when it came to payment, where they my try to get a good deal on other items when it comes to hits they always pay top dollar. The Irish are the worst as they all think they can do the job themselves and try to Christen you down on the price of a hit. That always pisses off Donald so he tends to only deal with woops and Jews. This hit was different as he was paid upfront so the race of his boss was really not an issue to him. Donald: Is this the line to meet Hoyt Williams? Black Lady: Praise Jesus it is! Donald: Thank you ma’am. Donald waits patiently for his turn in line doing what he does best, blending. He didn’t speak, smile, or do much more then survey the security around him taking very detailed mental notes. By his estimations the hit should be as easy as making Shake and Bake pork chops. After twenty minutes or so Donald gets close enough to see the table in front of him and is a bit upset as he doesn’t see Hoyt there. Instead Hoyt’s father Duke Williams is signing books and smiling for the people in typical meet and greet fashion. Duke is dressed in a big cowboy hat and leather vest fitting right in with the Houston surroundings. You don’t see many cowboys around these days then again you don’t see many phone booths either. Coincidence? The good news for Donald is this is not where he was going to kill Hoyt anyways so the plans have not changed. Donald likes touching the person he is going to kill to connect with them. It helps him mentally take the shot knowing he looked into his victims eyes before he takes them out from a distance. Donald’s heart rate begins picking up faster then a fat man at a strip club or the Old Country Buffet as he approaches Duke. He try’s to look into Duke’s eyes but Duke’s chrome shades only reflect back the eyes of a killer. Donald slides his copy of "Chapped Lips and Malice Sticks: The Duke Williams story" in front of the tall wrestler and father of the man he plans to cold slab with in the next hour. Duke Williams: Hey hoss thanks for the support. Donald Oscar Grabowski: Where is Hoyt? Duke Williams: He had to push a turd out of his asshole. He’s in the john taking a Digital Morality. I’ll tell him you were looking for him what’s your name. Donald Oscar Grabowski : Pontius Pilate. Duke stares the strange man in the eyes for a moment as a chill runs down Donald’s spine. Something is rotten in Denmark and it’s more then the Dutch and Duke senses it but can’t place his finger on it. The Malice Man stands up out of his chair towering over Donald getting in his face putting Donald on a bit of alert. Slowly Donald reaches towards the small of his back for his gun. He doesn’t break eye contact with Duke and he also understands that if he shoots down Duke the mission fails. He can’t risk it but that doesn’t stop him from placing his hand on the gun. Duke Williams: Well double P I’ll let him know. Thanks again hoss. Donald lets go of the gun unnoticed like a pro and shakes Duke’s hand as he is directed towards the door by RISP staff keeping things moving. This was a close call for Donald as he made him self less invisible then he ever wanted to do. Mistakes were not a commonality for Donald and this upset him. 12/15 2:55PM Five minutes until Hoyt takes the stage as a gospel group plays and the band is introduced. Donald Oscar Grabowski was given the tickets to the event from the person who hired him. It was also the job of his hired boss to smuggle his weapon in. This wasn’t a request Donald made and isn’t protocol of a job but it was something his boss wanted to handle and Donald didn’t object. The killing is scheduled to happen at around 3:08PM just as Hoyt takes the stage. Donald’s nerves are acting up not over the job but over the fact he still doesn’t have a weapon to begin preparation. Donald tries to relax by sitting back in his last row seat in the rafters of the dome watching the singers but he can’t. Just then his cell phone rings. Donald Oscar Grabowski: Hello? Rough Voice: You still want to go skiing later? Donald Oscar Grabowski: Yes. I’m just looking for a new set of ski’s. Rough Voice: Arm rest, seat cushion, vent under seat takes you to the slopes. Donald Oscar Grabowski: Still snowing? Rough Voice: Perfect amount. Break a leg. Click. Grabowski wastes no time at all lifting each of the arm rests finding the barrels to his weapon. The seat cushion opens up like a brief case revealing a hollowed out section filled with the bulk of the weapon and three bullets, along with a small hand gun. Grabowski picks them up and in a matter of moments the gun is assembled and ready to fire. Nobody notices as all eyes are on the stage in front him them and he’s three rows behind the nearest person. Donald crawls under his seat to find a ventilation duct already open. He crawls in like a Mexican through a boarder fence. He is silent as he can be crawling military style through the tight tin passage way. He stops as he passes by a vent where he peeks through to see Duke Williams fucking some blond girl doggy style over a desk. Donald Oscar Grabowski doesn’t watch for more then a second as his job is all that matters. He is a focused man who doesn’t believe in the pleasures of the world as they simply slow a person down. Love is just one of life’s anchors grounding you to one person making you give up your ambitions and basic human drives. Then again what Duke was doing seemed like fucking not loving, a big difference. The hitman arrives at the other end of the ventilation system kicking out the other vent and crawling out. It’s an attic type room with a birds eye view of the arena and the podium Hoyt Williams is about to die at. It appears the room is some sort of bed room perhaps for a care taker of the arena or an exec to escape to. Donald begins to set up placing his gun out of crack in the window and aiming it at the podium. He aims it perfectly at the person at the podium and smiles as he now finally relaxes. The only thing left to do is kill Hoyt Williams when he takes the podium in three minutes. 12/15 3:05PM A door opens and an old man steps into the room and spots Donald with the gun in his hand. He is startled and begins to back peddle unnoticed. Donald Oscar Grabowski: Hold it pal. The hitman spins around to look at the old man who has friendly eyes. Donald Oscar Grabowski: My name is Marty Robbins I’m in charge of Hoyt’s security detail I’ve been perched up here for protection. Now who the hell are you? Old Man: Jim Rogers I’m a caretaker here I moved in here to keep an eye on this place 24 hours after my wife died. My granddaughter is downstairs I was just going to grab my wallet to buy her some ice cream. Donald nods as the old man moves in quickly and grabs his wallet and begins to head out the door. Donald Oscar Grabowski: One more thing pal, can you do me a favor? Old Man: Sure. Donald Oscar Grabowski: Say hello to your wife for me. The old mans eyes go wide in fear as Donald pulls a gun from his pocket and shots the old man in the head spattering blood all over the wall. Donald pulls him in the door and shuts it locking it behind him getting back in position. 12/15 3:07PM ONE MINUTE UNTIL HOYT WILLIAMS DIES.
