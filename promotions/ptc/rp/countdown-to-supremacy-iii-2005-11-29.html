Countdown to Supremacy III

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 The final 12 hours. 12… Look out the window, look into the sky. 11… Look into yourself, believe that you can fly. 10… Look into your heart, see just who you are. 9… Look into the future, know you can go far. 8… Look into the past, and all that you have done. 7… Look forward to the night, know you could have won. 6… Look at everyone, the people here with you. 5… Look into the crowd, chanting for everything you do. 4… Look into your mind, focus on the battle ahead. 3… Look at the reality, through your eyes you will see red. 2… Look across the ring, it will be me that you will see. 1… Look at the pity, you thought you could beat me. The countdown is on.
