Piece by Piece: Part Seven

23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-23 Power. Some have it, others don’t. It’s the story of the world. In the profession of wrestling, however, most have it, some more than others, of course. However, Craig Goat, Seymour Almasy’s next opponent, is a completely powerless individual. Powerless in the ring. Powerless to prevent Nurse Wretched from walking all over him. Powerless to help his parents. To Seymour, Goat is but a gnat to be swatted aside. For in the ring, Seymour Almasy is mighty. And yet out of it, the PTC Global Champion slowly has begun to come to grips with how powerless he truly is… --------------- Seymour Almasy: Come on, come on… Almasy sighed, as yet again, the cheerful voice on the other end of the phone informed him that the number he sought did not exist. Seymour clicked the portable phone off, and sunk back down onto his bed, despair starting to set in. Seymour Almasy: It figures. I bet Laura’s making plans to go out with some hot guy right now. He had to fight off the image of some stereotypical stud doing a body shot off of his prone wife. It made him shudder violently, and yet, he didn’t know exactly why. The fact that there were issues between himself and Laura was obvious, but he still loved her. Seymour Almasy: By the time I finally realize what life is, my existence turns into an RPG again. Typical. Falling silent, Almasy began to rack his brain for the proper analogy. Various Final Fantasy games and other SquareEnix epics began to flow through his mind, before he finally selected a more general one that best fit the situation. Seymour Almasy: This round against Goat is like traveling through a difficult dungeon, with dogged and determined enemies attacking me at every turn. Finally, I scrape it together long enough to make my way to the end of the dungeon. I grab my sword, and prepare for the worst…and there awaiting me is a frail man, unarmed. That is what Goat is. On one level, Seymour was insulted that Goat had advanced to face him, that three worthy warriors had already lost to a man with little actual experience. On the other hand, it intrigued him. A man such as Craig Goat could be lucky. But lucky three times? Seymour Almasy: I swear, I should invite Goat over to play some Final Fantasies with me. I can let him do all of the slot machine attacks, I’d win every time. His fortune is unparalleled, and were this world what I thought it once was, his Luck stat would be a perfect 255. The Final Fantasy chuckled. Life was sometimes far easier to explain in terms of RPGs than anything else. Seymour Almasy: If anything, I feel bad for the poor bastard. Forget the fact that he has to face me, he deals with an evil nurse on a daily basis, and completely seems to lack the ability to stand up for himself for any great period of time. He is the same in the ring as he is out: lacking assertion, and any real talent or skill. But as he has proven, in wrestling, three seconds is all it takes… It was a sobering thought. Even after all of the time he put in at the gym, and the time that he spent devising new and dangerous maneuvers to put away opposition, wrestling came down to three little seconds. Seymour Almasy: I could slaughter him for an hour, and if for three lonely seconds he manages to put my shoulders to the mat, it’s over. Perish the thought of a string-bean like him making me submit… Shaking his head violently, Almasy tried to put the thought out of his head. Doing so, however, only made him question his social life more. For once, the personal was winning out over the professional. Seymour Almasy: I can’t function like this… He picked up the portable phone, and reached into his pocket. From its depths, he unearthed a little black book. The numbers written on it were mostly unremarkable, as for the most part, Seymour hadn’t grasped the concept of the little black book. Grandpa Cid and his attorney had their numbers inscribed here, but it wasn’t their numbers Almasy was after. Seymour Almasy: Now, to take Laura making out with a better looking man than I off of my mind. Almasy began dialing rapidly, fingers moving of their own accord. Before he quite realized what he had done, the phone speaker was to his ear, listening to the lovely female voice on the other end. Ashley: Hello? He had called Ashley, the woman who he had gotten into this whole mess in the first place with. Seymour Almasy: Ashley, hi! It’s Seymour. Ashley: Seymour? Hi! It’s been a while. Seymour Almasy: Yes it has…and I was wondering, would you like to come over tonight, and hang out? Ashley: Sure, I’ll be right over! Before he could say another word, Ashley had already hung up, presumably already on her way. Above all else, Ashley was one of his biggest fans…a groupie, almost. That made him smile. Seymour Almasy: It’s nice to have fans. It’s nice to have people care. Poor Craig doesn’t know what its like. Nurse Wretched only cares about him for what he is and can provide for her. His girlfriend, from what I hear, ran off on him. Even though he’s always with someone, he’s as alone as alone can be. He may as well have been talking about himself. Seymour needed someone to cling to. In the ring, he was completely independent. Out of it, despite his numerous shows of independence, he needed someone to rely on. With Laura gone for most intents and purposes, desperation had begun to set in, which was why he called Ashley in the first place. Seymour Almasy: I can’t underestimate Goat. As much as I’d like to completely right him off, I can’t. I’m way more worried than I should be for an opponent of his cal--err, lack of caliber. To his shock, he heard a knock on his door. Seymour Almasy: No WAY she’s here already. Rising, the Final Fantasy opened the door to the apartment, and quickly found himself proved wrong. Standing in the entryway was Ashley, as beautiful and vibrant as he had remembered. She threw herself into her arms, crushing him with a hug. Ashley: Seymour! It’s been WAY too long! Almasy could only grin and hug back, before escorting her to one of the couches in the apartment’s living room. Seymour Almasy: How did you GET here so quick? Ashley: You called my cell! I was out running errands, and I was about a block away when you called. She beamed, kissing him on the cheek. Still locked in her embrace, Almasy continued. Seymour Almasy: So, what have you been up to? Ashley: Watching you on TV in the tournament! You’ve been doing great so far! Seymour Almasy: Thanks. On occasion, Seymour wished that she would be a little bit less, well, obsessive. But such was the way of the fan girl, Seymour knew. He had spent enough time frequenting various fan fiction websites to know that fan girls were relentless in the pursuit of the objects of their desires. Ashley: Easy match this round, right? Craig Goat…never even heard of him. Seymour Almasy: Eh, I don’t know… Ashley: But you’re SEYMOUR ALMASY! You’ve beaten far better than him! You’re the best! Best might have been pushing it a little bit, but Seymour still understood the point. Seymour Almasy: It’s not worth concerning you with, but I will say that I can’t afford to underestimate any opponent. Doing so would court disaster. Ashley: Oooohh…I get it! Carrying on a conversation with Ashley was a much duller activity than it was with Laura, Seymour decided. It wasn’t that Ashley wasn’t bright. She was just a little, well, ditzy. To keep his interest, Seymour decided to focus on Ashley’s good qualities. As luck would have it, two of them were staring him in the face. Seymour Almasy: So, Ashley, you wouldn’t happen to have found a boyfriend, would you? Ashley: Nope! Still single! Good, Seymour thought. Exactly what he was hoping for. He was trying to keep the conversation going, he really was. But the image of Laura in bed with another man was overpowering him. Never mind the fact that what he had in mind to do with Ashley was probably what would drive her into the arms of another man…it was the only comfort he could draw. And while not as pretty as Laura, Ashley could be a very…comforting woman, indeed. Seymour Almasy: Would you like to come to the show to watch me face Goat? I have an extra tick-- Fuck. Ashley: Sure, I’d love to go! Fuck fuckity fuck fuck fuck. Seymour Almasy: --et… This was decidedly not good. Seymour knew instantly he’d screwed up. Inviting his wife and his once and again mistress to the same event. Almasy instantly began to hope that the tickets Tournament Director Argyle gave him were in spread out locations. Before he could ponder this any more, Ashley had already clambered into his lap, and was giving him another warm hug. Ashley: Aww, thank you! You’re so sweet, Seymour! Each looked into the eyes of the other. Ashley’s eyes were confident and calm, while Seymour’s were nervous and erratic. For a brief moment, Seymour wondered exactly who was in control of the situation. Then, Ashley stood, and extended a hand to him silently, and Seymour no longer had a shred of doubt. She was. He stood as well, taking her hand She slowly began to pull him towards the bedroom, a bedroom that they had shared together. Flashbacks began to assault the Final Fantasy, memories of cleaning up after he and Ashley had romped for hours so that his wife would have no idea, and go to bed with him on the exact same mattress later that evening. The memories were almost too much, but Seymour pressed on by reminding himself of how good Ashley was in bed. Illogical? Probably. Seymour Almasy: What are we doing, Ashley? Ashley: You know EXACTLY what we’re doing! You’re way too tense. You need a massage. The Final Fantasy gave a low, happy moan. He WAS way too tense. He worried about Craig Goat, worried about his wife with another man. To relieve this tension, he would sleep with Ashley. In turn, this made him more worried Laura would go to another man. And, well, you can see how the cycle goes. Throw in some Craig Goat every now and again, and Almasy’s life isn’t the most serene thing in the world. But the next few hours would be. Precious hours of relaxation. As Ashley guided him down to the bed, Seymour closed his eyes. Gone would be thoughts of Craig Goat. Gone would be worries of what Ashley and Laura would do if they saw each other. Gone would be all of his tension. It was, at best, a temporary panacea. But if it could get him through to the end of the day, that was enough. He thought, before Ashley’s soft hands began to massage all coherent thought from his mind, that it was quite sad that he had to live trying to simply get through to the next day. And soon, even that thought had disappeared.
