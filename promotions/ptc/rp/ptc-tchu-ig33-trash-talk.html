IG 33 - Trash Talk Here!

4 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
4 July 2007 Tchu does Late Night TeeVee “Do I have to do this, Lenny?” The PRIME rep backstage straightens the collar on The Inhuman Being’s polo shirt and gives him the thumbs up. Lenny: Yes. You’re getting paid for it, remember? Tchu: Oh yeah. That’s why I agreed to this. That, and because they actually let me write some of the list myself. Can you believe they actually wanted me to just go out there and read some other guy’s rabble from a cue card?! Lenny: Yeah… I can believe it. I think that’s usually how it goes. Just stay focused, have fun… and remember that you’re representing PRIME on national TV. Tchu: Don’t I do that every Wednesday night? Lenny: Sure, but by punching people in the face, not by making jokes. Now stay calm. You’re sweating for Christ’s sake. I can tell that your nervous. Dragging his left palm across his forehead, The Inhuman Being took a deep breath, then shook the perspiration from his hand, flinging a dozen tiny drops onto the tile floor. Tchu: That’s cuz I’m a lot better at the punching. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ A black screen. Nothingness. Just a dark void. And then the shot rifles back, pulling the viewer out of the gap between the front teeth in an otherwise perfect set of pearly whites. It’s the grinning mug of one David Letterman! “Ahehehehehe” The crowd claps, applauding the world famous late night host… and his goofy smile. David Letterman: Ahehehe. It’s time for tonight’s Top 10! Grabbing the cards in front of him, Letterman smacks them across his desk, straightening their edges. David Letterman: Tonight’s Top 10… The Top 10 reasons professional wrestler and PRIME legend, Tchu, will win IG 33! (Yeah, David Letterman is doing a Top 10 on pro wrestling. What of it?) The drum roll kicks in… David Letterman: Here we go! #10. With filming of VH1’s “I love the 2000s” years away, Jericoholic Anonymous is suffering from serious withdrawal! #9. Von Doom is in it! (the crowd starts their chuckling) #8. No one told Jason Snow that Superkicks aren’t finishers. They’ve gone the way of transitional moves! #7. Hunchblack? When was the last time Notre Dame won anything?! (Get it? Hunchback of No…) “HAHAHAHAHAHA” (the crowd gets it) #6. Von Doom is in it! #5. Jason Snow just got his butt kicked by a guy who wrestles with a jockstrap on his face! #4. Jason Snow has to worry about defending the Unified title against a guy who wrestles with a jockstrap on his face! (funny how that worked out, huh?) #3. The gap in talent between Tchu and the rest of the field is wider than the gap between my teeth! Ahehehehe! (The crowd likes that one almost as much as Dave.) #2. Sonny Silver and his alter ego Mr. Silver have officially split, and they weren’t that talented with their powers combined! And the #1 reason Tchu will win the IG 33… The cameras switch to a shot of Tchu standing in front of a backdrop of the city nightlife. Tchu: In the yeeeeeaaar Two thooousssaaaaaaaaannnnnnd… David Letterman: WRONG SHOW! Lenny punches Tchu in the shoulder. Tchu: Uh…sorry. The number one reason I’ll win IG 33… The mere SIGHT of me has been known to cause Jason Snow moments of unconsciousness lasting at least…(There's a loud 'clap' as Tchu smacks his hands together) ONE… (again) TWO… (one last time) THREE seconds! The camera cuts back to Letterman, who laughs mightily with the audience. David Letterman: There you have it! Tonight’s Top T… “Just watch the fucking tape of the Dual Halo”. Tchu’s words float in from across screen. The audience falls silent, but Dave never misses a bit. David Letterman: Ahehehehe! They’ll have to edit that shit! ---- 5 July 2007 At the Mall with Tchu! It was 11: 30. He had a microphone and a turkey sandwich. David Letterman had gone great… gone well…ok… it hadn’t been a disaster. Sure, he’d had that little hiccup with Conan’s show and the year 2000… but the top ten list had made its point. From national TV, he’d moved on to department stores. Now, with thirty minutes to dispose of before his two-hour autograph session at the local mall began, PRIME’s Wrecking Ball… the one and only, Tchu… wove in and out of the maze of tables and chairs that adorned the food court, all the while munching on his sandwich. Certainly, with the promise (even if it was a bit of a stretch) to appear on television, any number of individuals would be willing to give their thoughts on the state of pro wrestling. He had a camera man in tow (on rent from the PRIME payroll). And all the people, he knew they’d be fans, after all… they were all their to meet the former Universal Champion, get an autograph… maybe a Polaroid (probably not… he found posing for a picture about as painful as watching a Jason Snow match). Finding someone with a handful of opinions wouldn’t be hard. Tchu: Excuse me, sir… A large man rotated his pupils towards the superstar, but didn’t stop the process of shoving at least half of his twelve-inch meatball sub in his mouth. Tchu: I was wondering if I could pull you away from your deepthroating of that Subway sandwich long enough to get your thoughts on PTC’s upcoming IG 33 and champion, Jason Snow. Large Man: Jsnohgwwogaaay! Tchu: Jason Snow is gay?! Well… while I can’t say that I disagree with you, to each their own, sir. I really don’t see how his sexuality is releve… Large Man: No! Chewing in exaggerated form for a few seconds, the man swallowed a large portion of the slop in his mouth. Large Man: I said… Just go away! I don’t even know what you’re talking about Tchu: Well that’s lame. Tchu turns away. Tchu: How about you? The Inhuman Being jogged over towards a man… early twenties… purchasing a slice of pizza from La Rosa’s. The camera man followed as best he could, staying just a step behind. Tchu: Would you like to state your thoughts on PTC’s IG 33 for all the fans out there? The former PRIME superstar shoved the microphone forward and took a bite of his sandwich. Guy: Yeah… I think it’s gonna be fucking awesome! Tchu: Whoa… easy on the language frat boy. Can I get your thoughts on Jason Snow, the reigning champion? Guy: I think he’s a prick. Guy’s got no talent, ya know? Tchu: Absolutely. If we can’t call monkey’s flinging fistfuls of crap at each other ‘talent’, then there’s no way we can call Jason Snow a talented individual. Pose, brag, brag, pose, superkick finisher! It’s so predictable, I’ve heard scientists are ready to proclaim Snow matches a law since they’re confident the results will never vary. Guy: Hah! And what’s with that little ponytail uptop his head? Tchu: I dunno. At first, I thought it was for protection, but I dropped him on his head back at Culture Shock and laid him out cold, so it must be for attracting a mate or something. Like a rooster. Guy: Dude’s a cock. Tchu: That’s what I said… like a rooster. And what about Ian Nackedy? Guy: Who? The Inhuman Being took the last bite of his sandwich, and scratched his goatee, clearly contemplating something. Tchu: Ya know… tall dude. Guy: He’s tall? Tchu: Tall… maybe short. Tall or short. And he’s got that really dark hair… that’s kinda blondish-bald. Clean-shaven bea… ok… I have no idea who he is either. Anyways… what do you think my chances are? Some big names out there. Sonny Silver, Jericoholic Anonymous… Guy: What?! Is there a 12 step program for that sort of addiction? Tchu: Yeah… probably something like: Step 1. Admit your homo-erotic man crush. Step 2. Take down all those Y2J posters and throw away all your glitter shirts. Guy: That’s crazy! Tchu: It is. Well… I want to thank you for your time. Be sure to tune in and watch as I try and topple the world, or at least whoever’s in the match. With a shake of the hand, The Inhuman Being headed off towards the arcade (or whatever’s left of the things at local malls these days.) Inside, a half a dozen kids, the oldest probably 14, gathered around a couple of the more popular shooter games. Except for one boy who stood across the room, alone, playing ski-ball…unsuccessfully… and hanging his head in shame. Tchu: What an awesome game. Always was my favorite. Let’s go talk to him. Emo kids make the best interviews! Hey buddy… The boy turned around and smiled, recognizing The Inhuman Being. Tchu: Do you watch wrestling? Boy: Of course! Tchu: And what do you think about the current Infinite Gauntlet Champion, Jason Snow? I think he’s as shitty as the restrooms at Taco Bell! Boy: Fuck Jason Snow! The Inhuman Being’s eyes widened and he stood speechless for a moment. Tchu: How old are you, kid? Boy: I’m seven! Tchu: Wow. Must be a Nitz Donnelly fan. Shaking his head at the state of America’s youth, PRIME’s Wrecking Ball turns to face the camera that’s been filming over his shoulder. Tchu: Well, there you have it, folks. Jason Snow… hated by frat boys and first-graders alike. Not since the days of Coke II has something garnered this much genuine hatred across the board! Jason Snow… you’re reign as champ is about to be as dead as crystal clear Pepsi. The camera man gives the thumbs up, then shuts down the recording, sliding the camera from his shoulder. Tchu: Hmmm… now I’m kinda thirsty. Better grab a soda to wash down that sandwich. ---- 6 July 2007 Very Tchuperstitious “What the hell are you doing?!” Tchu pulled his hair in a momentary freak-out. Across his living room, Scott Ward, younger brother of PRIME’s most decorated superstar, froze in place with an umbrella in hand. Scott: Dude… I was just gonna open my umbrella and let it dry a…” Tchu: No! First of all, its not raining inside and I got that leak in the roof fixed over a month ago. Secondly, that’s bad luck to open an umbrella indoors! Tchu shook his head in dismay as he carefully… very carefully… hung a mirror on the wall above the couch. Scott: Since when were you superstitious? Tchu: I’m not. But I really want to win the Infinite Gauntlet Championship… and I’m not taking any chances. I don’t want bad luck anywhere near me. Scott: Ok ok. Whatever you say, bro. Scotty left the umbrella closed and tossed it on the floor “I think you’re losing your mind.” Tchu: If you would have opened that thing, I woulda kicked your ass worse than this trash talk theme is kicking von Doom’s. Scott: That’s harsh. The younger brother made his way across the living room, pausing for a moment at the ladder in the middle of the space. Scott: Why is this here? Tchu: I’ve got a ‘superstition’ theme going on here, Scott. No list of superstitions is complete without a ladder to avoid walking under. Scott: Does it say ‘Rook Black’ and ‘Phil Atken’ on the bottom rung? Tchu: Yeah, my picture is at the top if ya wanna climb up there and check I out. Cocking one eyebrow while laughing under his breath, Scotty just shakes his head and heads into the kitchen to raid the refrigerator. Scotty: I’ll take your word for it dude. Man, your fridge is empty. Tchu: Emptier than Big Bear’s career achievements list. I need to run to the store today. First though, I’ve got something I want to take care of. The Inhuman Being walked over to the desk in the corner of the room and sat down at his computer. Navigating the screen, he popped open his email. Tchu: Bigger penis, bigger penis, I’ve won a million dollars, bigger penis, fleshlight… God this junkmail stuff is out of control. Like there’s actually a pill out there that can triple the size of the male organ. That’s about as believable as a pill that could make a guy enjoy an Easton Hall promo. He highlighted each of the worthless messages and clicked ‘Delete All’. Tchu: Now, down to business. Scott: Shit! The voice carried into the living room from the kitchen. Tchu: What? What happened? Scott: I knocked over the salt shaker. Tchu spun around in his seat and shot an evil glare towards his younger brother. His brow furled and his face turned a shade of red that wasn’t natural Tchu: You throw some of that over your shoulder before you come back in here! You here me?! Scott: Yeah… whatever. So…what are you doing? Scotty tossed some salt over his shoulder and opened up his Pepsi as he headed towards his brother. Tchu: I’m going to send Jason Snow one of those annoying chain emails. Just in case, ya know? His fingers began to dance across the keyboard faster than the crowd exiting a Nackedy match. To: [detail withheld] Cc: Bcc: Subject: Hey buddy! Hey Champ, Don’t read this! If you have started reading this, its too late! You must send this email to 259 people on your email list within 5.4 seconds. If you don’t have 259 addresses on your list… well then you’re fucked. You will be cursed. Your career will fall to shambles It will all start in your home fed. You’ll struggle in PRIME. You’ll lose matches. Your paranoia and extreme lameness will force you to hire a corpse-humping bodyguard. This will only make you more paranoid and force you to walk around covering your asshole, because even though you’re not dead… your career pretty much is, and you might as well be a corpse. You’ll never sniff PRIME gold. People will begin to doubt you. You’ll become a joke that amounts to nothing more than a handful of catchphrases. Main events will become a dream. Curtain jerking will become your livelihood, and… Scott: Matt… stop, dude. The Inhuman Being turned around in his chair and gave a puzzled look to his brother. Tchu: Why? Scott: Killean already sent that message to Snow weeks ago. PRIME’s Wrecking Ball turned back to his computer and stared at the message. A light turned on in his head. Tchu: Well, I suppose that explains everything. Guess these things really work! ---- 6 July 2007 At the Flea Market with Tchu! “It’s authentic!” A handful of people at the Flea Market turned in the direction of the yelling, 6’2” celebrity, who was trying to close a deal with a customer. Customer: No it’s not. It’s just a towel you bought and wrote ‘IFE’ on. Tchu: I’ve known The Face for a long time, and I can assure you, this is the very cape he wore for his PRIME debut. And for just $500, it can be yours. Customer: I’ll give you five bucks for it, just cuz I could use a new beach towel. Tchu: Sold! The Inhuman Being and the customer exchanged money for ‘cape’ and the man walked away from the superstar’s booth. Tchu: Hehe. Sucker. That towel wasn’t new. He swatted at a fly buzzing around his head as he glanced around the building. It was busy… more people than Phil Atkens had ever wrestled in front of. And just as he expected, it wasn’t long before another customer was there to greet him. This time, it was a teenage boy with his baseball cap on backwards. Boy: Got any good stuff? Tchu: Sure. Tchu turned and rummaged through his belongings, then produced an empty can of Diced Tomatoes, sitting it on the desk in front of him. Boy: Geez, that’s not good stuff! Tchu: That right there is better than anything von Doom has brought to the table. Boy: Hmmm As the kid was contemplating whether or not to purchase the can, a Japanese man approached Tchu’s booth and smiled kindly. Man: Do you have any Look Brack items? Tchu: Haha! Luke Brack… there’s a name I haven’t heard in awhile. He and I go waaay back. One time, we took about five gallons of gasoline, three feet of rope, a dozen oranges, and… Boy (cutting off the PRIME superstar): Uh, Mr. Tchu… I believe it was a little mix-up with the ‘L’s and ‘R’s… he asked you if you have any Rook Black items. Tchu: Never heard of him. The man frowned and walked off. Tchu: Anyways… so Luke and I take these oranges and… Boy: Do you have any posters of wrestling stars? With a heavy sigh, Tchu scratched the back of his neck. His story about his old pal would have to wait for another day. Looking through his collection, he located the posters and pulled the stack out from underneath a few pieces of junk. Tchu: Yeah, I’ve got a few from the Squared Circle collection… including a mint condition, limited-edition Mr. Silver poster! Boy: Whooaaa! Can I see it. Tchu: Sure can. He handed the awe-struck kid the small poster. Boy: How much? Tchu: Fifty bucks. Boy: Awww… I don’t have that. Will you trade it for a Hunchblack? Tchu: No way! I’ve seen better humps on Skinemax. The boy hung his head in dismay, until a thought occurred to him. Boy: Do you have anything a little cheaper? Tchu: It doesn’t get much cheaper than Mr. Silver, son. Boy: Nothing? Tchu: Well… I do have a few things. I know I’ve got a couple of cards from that PRIME trading card game they released a couple months ago. Ya know… the one like Magic, only with men in tights instead of dragons and trolls. Boy: Awesome! Who do you have? Tchu: I’ve got an Easton Hall… Boy: Sweet! I’ll trade you a Nackedy and Big Bear for it! The Inhuman Being snatched back the card and shook his head. Tchu: Not happening, kid. Boy: Ok.. how about… I’ll trade you my Nackedy and Big Bear cards for someone I’ve actually heard of. Tchu: Nope. Boy: Well… can I just give you Nackedy and Big Bear? I don’t want ‘em. Tchu: Neither do I! Now, are you gonna buy something or not? The boy shook his head ‘no’, looked up with some lame ass puppy dog eyes, got no reaction, and so he trudged off across the floor with shoulders slunked at his side. Tchu: Kids… The Inhuman Being opened up the cash box in front of him and looked at his earnings for the afternoon. A lone Five dollar bill rested in the bottom. Tchu: Now I remember why I wrestle. It’s hard making money out here in the real world. ---- 9 July 2007 Tchu Cuts a Promo The scene opens on something… it’s hard to tell. The picture seems to be shaking a bit, but the large image that rests just inches from the lens makes it impossible to say for sure. Then slowly, the answer starts to take focus. A set of hands pull away from the camera, having sufficiently steadied its position. Those hands belong to PRIME’s two-time Universal Champion and reigning Dual Halo winner; and, with a quick smile that disappears faster than Bobino’s job security, Tchu sets himself down in a chair, right in the middle of the picture. Tchu: For von Doom…this is a ‘trash talk’ promo. Since he can’t seem to wrap his mind around the fact that trash-talk doesn’t have to be done with a sound and camera crew locked away in a small little studio… I’ll oblige, and go old-school. The Inhuman Being shifts his weight in the chair. Tchu: I’ll keep it simple, not only for von Doom’s boxed-in brain, but so that I don’t give Mr. Silver anymore chances to rip me off again. I know, Sonny… blew your mind, didn’t it? It was scary enough when I was just the wrecking machine that steam rolled everyone in my path… but all of sudden, you find out I’m as funny as a dinner with the ghosts of Richard Prior and Mitch Hedberg (RIP fellas) and more creative than Leonardo fucking Da Vinci. Must be killing you there, ‘Chairman’, to have to deal with the fact that I’ve always kicked more ass than you, and now… I’m funnier to boot. Leaning back, PRIME’s Wrecking Ball seems to make himself comfortable, as if he’s settling in for awhile. Maybe he’s got a few things to say. Tchu: So, this where I sit here and tell all of you a few things about my fellow participants in the Infinite Gauntlet. I can tell you that Jason Snow actually hired himself a bodyguard in PRIME that has managed to kick more ass in the last month, than Jason has in his entire PRIME career. Or that Mr. Silver somehow managed to stay relevant in the world of professional wrestling by actually riding the coattails of a guy riding coattails to the top. Pretty easy to seem ‘cool’ when you latch onto a star like Nova or a leech like Snow, who managed to use Rollins and Deville’s fame to make himself “G-reat” and drive out two legit legends in the process. Come to think of it… FU may have been the largest collection of free-loading losers ever assembled in one place. They ought to call guys like myself, and Deville and Nova ‘The Escalators’ cuz the rest of you trash just hop on and ride to the top. The Inhuman Being cracks his knuckles, then his neck… and keeps rolling. Tchu: Here’s where I tell everyone a few jokes. Where I point out that Jason Snow’s face looks like a vagina with razor burn about ten days after its been shaved, and that those leather pants of his look like they’re straight out of the ‘South Park: Mr. Slave’ collection. Did you know that Nackedy and Bobino are so insignificant that they don’t even register on NASA’s radar? Time to take a breath. Tchu: Bobino. Bobino… for someone who calls himself ‘The New Shit’, you sure have earned yourself a helluva reputation for the same old tired crap. I’m not going to waste any more breath on you because by the time I finish this promo, you may have already dropped out and moved on to the “Free Spirit” Wrestling Federation, or whatever the hell your delusional mind might call the unemployment office. And there’s guys like Hall… who have the nerve to question me all of a sudden being funny and breaking character, then flip into full-hypocrite mode and start singing about as well as Big Bear actually backs up all that ‘chump busting’ bullshit. Doesn’t make a bit of sense. The reigning Dual Halo winner pauses and rubs his chin for a moment. Tchu: Speaking of completely senseless… how about Hunchblack walking around talking about how he’s “due”. I’d sure as hell hope so… the guy’s blown more chances at this thing than Paris Hilton’s blown guys. PRIME’s Wrecking Ball sits forward, and locks his eyes straight on the camera Tchu: And now everyone’s laughing… everyone’s having a great time. And that’s fine… good, even. Get it out of your systems now… come IG 33, there won’t be much laughter, cuz there just isn’t anything funny about broken bones and cracked skulls. It’s hard to find the humor in the dreams of men being crushed by one pissed-off sonofabitch who’s had his fill of being known around PTC as ‘that PRIME guy.’ I made it clear a week or so ago that I was entering the 33rd Infinite Gauntlet. Tchu pushes himself forward, out of the chair, standing tall in the middle of the room Tchu: Now, I’m making it clear that I’m going to win it. The camera fades to black The preceding message was brought to you by The Inhuman Being… who just had to squeeze this note in here since Mr. Silver had the nerve to steal his doctored-picture idea. Payback, bitch! Tchu will say hello to Lindz for ya, Sonny, when he sees her at dinner. You would have been invited, along with Snow, but the restaurant would only take reservations for people who actually know what its like to be a Universal Champion. Sorry!
