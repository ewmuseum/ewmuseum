A night on the town

Date: 2004-09-24 Sometimes the toughest things are meant to be there for a reason, if you can overcome something that is tough it can only make you stronger in the end. Determination Faith Courage Conviction Hope Perseverance These are all words that people use to describe a champion, someone that fights without fear and does not care about the consequences. I have been around for a while and even though I have not been around as long as Salaok I realize the challenge that lies before me. He will be one of the toughest men I have ever faced, will that change the way I look at the match? No, it means that I will come out there swinging harder and faster than I ever have. It he thinks that for a moment that this is gonna be a gimme or some kind f match where I no show… he is sadly mistaken. I never make a point of not showing up to a match that I have been signed to. Win, lose or draw I will go out there and fight this guy as hard as I possibly can. Yeah I know that a lot of people do not expect me to win but that is even better cause it makes me wanna work harder for it all. I may not be a big name in all of this but this tournament has proven that big names are really only names at that and nothing else. Names that can be beaten… 8:25 a.m. Friday, September 24, 2oo4 The Marriot Cleveland, Ohio It was early in the morning; I slowly opened my eyes, wondering why I was up so early. I was scheduled to do anything for WMW till later in the day. I was still a little uneasy about everything though as I was in one of the most dangerous matches in my WMW career at Darwin’s Revenge against Slayer for the Shoot title. I was really wondering what kinda match to expect but, in the back of my mind I was still thinking about Salaok and the fact that he would get a math with me shortly after my ppv encounter and would get me in a damaged state. I will be bloodied and beaten that is a given. Not that I am offering any kind of excuse but this match with Salaok is coming at a really bad time as far as wrestling goes. Does not mean that he is gonna beat me but, I would not wanna be in the Freedom locker room the next day after he loses to somebody that was not even a 100 percent. I mean could you imagine the shame in all of that. I rolled my eyes at the clock as it looked way too early; I stared blankly at the ceiling of my hotel room. I watched ER last night seeing the season premiere was great even more since I taped a few episodes as a guest on the show for this season. I climbed out of bed, walking to the bathroom, unloading my bladder in the toilet and then flushing the toilet. I looked in the mirror, I was a little disappointed that I was back in Cleveland again, it is a ppv for Christ’s sake is it to much to ask to go somewhere for a big show? I looked through my bad grabbing the toothpaste and the deodorant and taking care of the morning before I headed out for the day. I got dressed settling on some jeans, and a D12 t- shirt. I grabbed my lime green shades, opening the door my friend and tag team partner Fury as standing there ready to knock. He looked up, smiling pulling his shades down slightly, looking at me. “Ya know I hate it when you do that?” he remarked with a weird look on his face as he curled his lips like Elvis. “I know…” I said smiling as I looked at my partner. “What up?” “Just thought I would drop by as we have a big weekend planned, although not as a team but we each have big matches at Darwin’s Revenge.” Fury remarked as a slight smile developed on the edge of his mouth. He plopped down in the chair that was across from the bed, he opened a drawer seeing what he could find. His hand appears with a bible. “You ever use one of these?” I watched as he flipped through the pages and then tossed it back in the drawer and closed the drawer. “Well I am Catholic and was brought up very religiously, so yeah I know a thing or two about the bible. Now I have lost the faith for a while after everything that has happened in my life. But I guess that should be a little more towards the faith and the good back after all the lord does work in mysterious ways right?” I asked as I looked at Fury. “Man, whatever look I am the last person to talk to about religion bro.” Fury remarked as he stood from his chair cracking his neck in the process. “You ready to go out for a bit?” “Yah, sounds like it is exactly what I need.” I said as I grabbed the keys to my Hummer. I needed to get a little loose, things had gotten kinda tough lately and I wanted a chance to unwind and get back the old Noah the fun loving, free spirited person that used to be around but has kinda disappeared as of late. In a lot of ways I hated the person that I have become, but it was out of a necessity not because I wanted to or anything. I hated having to stoop to levels of a common criminal, it was not me. I had entered this tournament for a certain reason but it clouded my better judgment, something that does not usually happen. But it does not change anything cause I still have to face Salaok, I still have to beat Slayer and I will still chase down the dream of winning the Great Lakes title. It is something I call fate, it may not be my fate to win the match against Salaok but it is my fate will help me win the match at Darwin’s Revenge and that is all that really matters in the long run. We decided to drive around the area; we cruised a local strip mall and then finally settled on a gentleman’s club. We both stepped out of the car. We smiled and headed inside. 1:46 p.m. Showgirls III Cleveland, Ohio September 24, 2oo4 We both walked into the brightly lit up bar and scooped the area. The attractive dancers slid along the poles and stages like elegant snakes on a log. I watched as a seductive brunette on the main stage caught my eye. But then I felt a hand on my chest and looked down expecting a sexy tanned arm, but instead there was a hairy, pale almost ghost like arm attached to the hand that was touching me. I looked up at the burly man smiling casually. “I.D??” he asked directing us to the main desk, where I was guessing we would have to provide our I.D.s to. “Hold on big man no need to get all physical and such.” I replied as I looked at the man. “Don’t mind “Little Phil” he is just protective of his girls!” the door man replied as we handed him our id’s. “Hey, its cool!” Fury remarked. “We all have jobs to do!” The doorman nodded as he handed us our ids back and then stamped the tops of our hands. We walked towards the main stage and sat down. The bartender approached us with a smile placing down a pair of drink napkins one in front of each of us. “What will you guys have tonight?” he asked. “Two Killians Irish Reds and a couple shots of tequila!!” I remarked as I placed a 100 dollar bill on the counter. “Keep it coming till we run dry!” The bartender looked at the bill, smiling and then nodded as he got our drinks. “There ya go fellas enjoy the night!” I watched as the brunette grinded the pole dancing to Tina Turners “Private Dancer”. She seemed to have her eyes locked on me from the other end of the stage. I was lost in her eyes, she was beautiful, exotic and everything a lady should be sexy, naked and dancing in front of me on a pole. I seemed to be almost entranced by her elegant dancing of the pole, the way she twined her legs around and slid down it like she was some kind of exotic fire fighter running to put out the flames my heart was engulfed in. “Hello?” I saw something in front of my face as I grabbed it assuming it was the dancers hand I kissed it. I looked down and to my surprise it was Fury’s’ instead. He snatched his hand away from me and sighed. “You do that again and people are gonna talk and think we are in the wrong strip club.” “Sorry, I don’t know what has gotten into me tonight?” I sighed as I drank my beer, and then chased it with the tequila. “I can tell you what has gotten into you!” he replied as he drank his shot. “What Mr. Wizard?” I replied sarcastically. “Her…” he said pointing in the direction of my bronze goddess. “Ohhh…yeah her!” I said as I sighed as I watched her dance again. I was just lost in her beauty, she also had a nice set of tits that were also very appealing to me but honestly her beauty was far beyond that. “You know what can you put a leash on that thing for one night?” he asked like he was implying something about my manhood. “Hey, there is no need to get all personal like that D.” I said as I waited for her to come to our side of the stage. She finally started to make it over to us as to which I frantically dug into my pockets for dollars. “Shit! I need some dollars!” I watched as Fury rolled his eyes as I dug through my billfold for dollars, I had none all the money I have and no dollars what is the world coming to? I looked at the bartender, frantically waving him to me. He approached us, smiling as he probably knew what I wanted. “Yo, need some one?” he asked reaching into his sash and returning with a wad of ones. “Yeah” I nodded as I tossed a $50 on the counter; he counted the ones out in front of me. “This gonna be a long night…” Fury moaned as he downed another beer. “What is her name?” as I pointed to my brunette on the stage. “Shadow!” remarked as our collective jaws dropped to the floor or at least the counter. “Excuse me are you serious?” I asked for a repeat of the answer to my question. “You heard right!” he nodded as he walked back to some other customers. “What are the chance?” I said as I looked at Fury. “I dunno…” he shrugged as he started drinking a third beer. “I just hope you have a lot of money on ya, cause I am real thirsty tonight!” I was just stunned as to hear her name was Shadow the name of the women that beat me for my Ace Superior title. Hell who knows what she does with the damn thing now that she has it, but I will say this she has done the title and myself proud as she has fought the defenses with pride and toughness. After Corvus turned on her and Epitome leveling both of them in one fell swoop I kind of felt sorry for her, not for the fact that she got beaten down but, the fact that Shadow still stood her ground. I commend her for that it’s not easy to stand your ground like that I for one have learned how to stand the ground that is front of me. I have had to that with each and every day I have been employed with Wrestling Midwest, if I did not I would have been buried a long time ago. This is my time and whether or not I beat Salaok. I could really care less cause my time is in WMW not PTC… it’s that simple… *end*
