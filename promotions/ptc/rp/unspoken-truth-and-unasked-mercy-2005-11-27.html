Unspoken Truth and Unasked Mercy

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 Before The Press Conference Jake poured himself another drink. He swirled the thousand dollars around in the glass, "Who gave this to you?" "I’m not revealing my sources!" Tim looked Jake up and down, he was something Tim had never suspected. A man without principles maybe, who thought his money and fame entitled him to things. "Your brother, your brother-in-law and from what I hear your nieces and nephews are all dead!" "You forgot my Great Aunt and some friends." Jake sipped the scotch. Tim slammed both his open palms down on the desk, "THIS ISN"T FUNNY!" Jake stood up. He kept eyes locked onto Tim, but there was more than just anger and resentment. The depth of shame and sorrow in his eyes looked like someone nearing a breakdown. Goff took his hands up and backed away a step. Jake finished the liquor without ever breaking eye contact. "I know who sent you those photos without even asking. The same man who did that to my family, and has tried to do even more. I’m not fighting to hide some shame that will make you a great journalist, I’m fighting to hold on to the family I have left." Tim folded his arms over his chest, "The noble Jake Hix." Jake shrugged his shoulders, "Think what you will. I don’t have the power to stop you Tim. We’re both about to walk into a room and you could drop it on me in front of the whole world. Just…just remember there was a time when you wouldn’t have sneered at the idea of me being a noble man. If I was responsible for those things, then Xavier wouldn’t forgive me. Alexandria would never forgive me, and Pitt would damn sure put my body in the ground." Tim kept his arms folded, but he knew his brother Pitt would never relent from punishing whoever had done that. "I loved John like a second father, and those images you see are nothing compared to being there in front of it. The nightmares stalk me all the time, and I keep facing the worst nightmare. If I don’t play this just right, you’ll have pictures of the rest of my family." Jake walked out from around the desk. He stood before Goff, staring down at a man he had known for twenty years now. "You were a friend to my family, and I hope you still are." Jake looked down at the floor, "Do what you have to do, but think about who this benefits. We’re fighting for the lives he didn’t take away. He’s just trying to keep us distracted while he tries to finish us." Jake walked to the office door. He reached out for the doorknob when the question came. "Who is he?" "He’s the devil Tim." He opened the door, "But you onc4e called him Sid. I once called him my brother." The Press Conference His heart stopped…he breathed out…and the world quit spinning on its axis for an instant. Jake recovered, "Did I get you? I bet you all thought I wasn’t ready for this." Laughter rippled through the room. Jake had a broad smile as he looked out at the reporters. "Come on, messing with the press is a time honored tradition. I think I should have gone to Washington with as much as I enjoy the game. I think enough has been said about the event. How about we get to the good part where you try to rip me apart with searing questions and I simply respond with vacuous verbosity." The reporters were all raising their hands, jockeying for the first question. Jake turned to the side of the room where the venerable, respected reporter leaned against the wall. Jake pointed at him, "Tim, did you have a question?" Tim was holding onto the manila folder. The images would haunt anyone, and he couldn’t believe Jake hadn’t flown off into a rage. The man was stuck in something he had never been involved in before. Tim believed Jake; mainly he believed the family would not have lied about all of this unless there was a damn good reason. "I’ll be nice to an old, trusted friend." Tim pulled the folder under his arm. "What do you think about Michael Stevens as an opponent?" Jake laughed, stroking his goatee as if in deep thought, "There’s a funny little story about Stevens. You see, I’m sure he’s overlooking me. Why do I say that? Seems a Mr. Steven came by the Global offices trying to find some tapes of me in action or something. He didn’t get very far." Another reporter spoke, "They like you that much in Global?" "No, there’s a few who would suspend me for the fun of it like last time." Jake laughed, "No, it seems Stevens asked for Jason Hix. If he doesn’t know my name, he doesn’t know me, and that just means come the match he will be shocked to learn what I do to people. Same shit, different day. I’m gonna’ take him down, break him down, and make him my bitch."
