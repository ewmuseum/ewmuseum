Arrogance

9 November 2004

Date: 2004-11-09 And the winner is…Sebastian Moraco! Not even a staged accident could deter the underdog from pulling out another upset. In round four of the Prime Time Central’s GTT tournament, the upstart Sebastian Moraco pulled out all the stops and defeated another upstart Elia Smith. Both competitors were thought to be easy pickings in earlier rounds, but through their sheer heart and determination they defied all odds. In the end however, it would the amateur wrestler from Spain who would get the win and a chance to face Chris…Doctor Curiosity. With rewards like that, who wants to lose? Nothing To See Here He stood proudly in his office looking outside the window. He stared down at the streets below smiling. For Jonathan Sams, the tournament was a huge success thus far. It was an even bigger success for him personally. The Prime Time Central’s GT Tournament drew thousands in the audiences and millions at home on cable television. Merchandise, shirts, action figures, condiments, everything was being sold like hot cakes but none of the aforementioned materials were as profitable as the wrestlers themselves. These were people who were worth millions of dollars in contracts, bonuses, and endorsements. Jonathan knew that, and he knew that it would be the lesser-known fighters who could be worth the most money, providing they performed well. Aside from other underdogs who knew nothing of the finances being sacrificed in their favor, Sebastian became his favorite guinea pig. While Sebastian thought he was fighting to redeem his glory and get back his chance at Olympic gold, the truth was he was fighting to fatten the wallets of strangers and acquaintances alike. However, he couldn’t do that each round unless he was at a clear disadvantage. Steve Spinerall, a hustler disguising himself as a janitor, made sure of that last round. The staged accident he feigned with Sebastian put Sebastian out of training for the entire week before his match with Elia Smith. It was feared that he would not be able to compete, but compete he did. He won. Now Sams finds the tournament in it’s fifth round, or better known as Bracket Finals rounds, and the money to be won is astounding. There’s just one problem: No one is giving Sebastian a chance. “This is Mr. Sams,” Jonathan Sams answered his cellphone. “Yes, Mr. Moraco performed as well as expected. He’s a real good fighter, not too bright though, but a real good fighter nonetheless.” Jonathan walked back towards his deck and opened up his briefcase ogling at the money he had won in the previous round. “I know damn well who he is facing this round!” Jonathan Sams snarled slamming his hand down on his desk. “Doctor Curiosity is a strange and senile man with uncanny success. You may think our joyride with this kid is over, but I guarantee you it isn’t!” “Excuse me, Mr. Sams?” Jonathan Sams looked up to see one his young female interns carrying a stack of papers in her hand. He quickly closed the briefcase tossing it to the ground besides him. “What the hell do you want?” “I….I came by to tell you that all formalities have been sent to the competitors for the next round. Also, here are the budget plans for the remainder of the tournament,” the intern replied hastily. “Just leave them on that chair, and get out of here,” Jonathan replied. The intern did as she was told and closed the door behind her. She stood outside the door for a moment wondering what she saw. She knew she saw the briefcase was full of money and Mr. Sams was adamant about ignoring that fact. She shrugged it off and continued on her way. Whatever is was, it wasn’t her problem. Round Five Is Here, Isn’t It? Sebastian walked out of his shower wrapped up in his white cotton towel. In the living room, he spotted the formal letter from the committee that was routine now for every round. If you were to ask him, he could almost tell you word for word what it said. Every round, the letter was the same. Congratulations on winning, a reminder of the facilities provided by the Committee, and a time schedule of the upcoming matches. Sebastian knew whom he was fighting, and he knew when. Yet, there was another letter besides the one he was so used to seeing. This was odd he thought, the Committee never gave him anything else other than the routine schedule. At first Sebastian thought that this was another form letter congratulating him on making it this far, but then he began to read it. Due to personnel problems, The Latch Key Kid Vs Karina Wolfenden match has been cancelled, and your match with Doctor Curiosity has been moved to the opening spot. Well, well, well. Karina Wolfenden had withdrawn from the tournament, and now Sebastian’s match was the opening match of the night. Sebastian continued to read the letter, but the rest of it was standard blurb. The last line was different however. It told Sebastian to keep on fighting and ‘good luck, he will need it’. Sebastian scoffed at the last line and crumpled up the paper. Ever since the Rockies Bracket was through with the round four matches, all Sebastian heard was how his run had ended. He was too lucky to get this far. He had chicken bones in his pocket. He was fighting people that made too many mistakes. There is no way he was going to beat Doctor Curiosity. No way in hell. Pencil in the good doctor’s name in the semi-finals. Sebastian’s run has ended. Sebastian Moraco picked up the daily local newspaper from his coffee table. He laughed as he saw the pictures of the next round pairings. He didn’t laugh when he saw the superscript comments made on his match. Doctor Curiosity Vs Sebastian Moraco with the good doctor being a 20-1 favorite to win. Sebastian growled and ripped the paper in half. He threw the pieces across the room and walked towards his bedroom. “I know I can beat him,” he muttered. “I know I can beat him.” False Attractions Sarah held up a baby blue shirt in front of her and surveyed it up and down. Between Starbucks and medical school, it was about damn time she had some time to herself. Hey, a woman needs to be able to play or at least shop. “I saw you going out with that guy from the fighting event.” Sarah turned around and saw one of her classmates, Megan, standing besides her. Megan was a southern blonde, from Georgia actually, and like Sarah set her eyes on a medical degree. She wasn’t Sarah’s closest friend, or friend at all, and Sarah felt a sort of animosity towards her. “What are you talking about?” Megan rolled her eyes. “Don’t play dumb with me. I saw you going out with that kid from the show, Sebastian Morroco, Monaco, Macaso…something like that. Come on admit it. You’re only going out with him because you think he’s money. I admit he is cute though.” Sarah scoffed in disgust. “No, for your information his name is Moraco. Secondly, I’m going out with him because I enjoy his company.” “And the money has nothing to do with it?” Megan asked raising her eyebrow. Sarah put the shirt back. Suddenly, buying a new wardrobe didn’t seem as important as getting away from Megan. “No, it doesn’t. Look not every girl tries to find these famous guys for the money, alright?” Megan watched Sarah walk away. “Whatever Sarah, you know the guy’s making a lot of money. I don’t blame you though. I would do the same thing. All these guys are stupid. Just flash them a small amount of interest coupled with tits and ass, and they’ll be groveling at your feet.” “Why would you want to do that?” Sarah asked turning around. “Never mind, I forget some girls like to be called sluts.” Megan laughed. “It’s okay Sarah. I won’t tell anyone. Not that it matters though. Personally, I think you need to find a new money train.” “Why is that?” Megan yawned and feigned caring. “I hear he’s fighting someone really good. Doctor Curiosity. He’s supposed to be almost unbeatable. A kid like Sebastian won’t be able to compete with a man like Doctor Curiosity.” “He can beat him,” Sarah said. “I know he can.” Unwanted Ryan Howell lay down on the couch and made himself feel at home. He had spent the entire night barhopping and had to be carried to Jeff’s house for fear of his safety. In the middle of the night, he vomited on Jeff’s carpet, something that Jeff still hasn’t been able to fully clean. Jeff looked at the mess in his living room, and then growled at Ryan lying on his couch without a care in the world. “Why the hell did they bring you here?” he asked. “Doesn’t your dad like have a condo set up for you or something?” “Relax old man,” Ryan snorted. “You get too worked up over little shit.” Jeff sighed. “Why are you here in D. C.? Can you at least tell me that?” Ryan nodded. “I’m here to make sure that my project is bearing its fruits.” “What kind of project do you have here?” Jeff asked. Ryan sat up straight. “Look at me in the eye and tell me you honestly think my dad and you came up with the plan.” Jeff turned away. “I have no idea what you are talking about.” Ryan began to laugh in disbelief. “I’m not the police, Jeff. You don’t have to hide anything from me. I know about the scandal you and my dad are in. In fact, I’m the one who suggested it to him.” Jeff turned around in awe. “You? Why the hell would you do that?” “Please…” Ryan rolled his eyes. “Did you think I was going to let my good friend Sebastian Moraco just mope all summer long? No, I was going to make sure he got on top of the world again and then take out the pedestal from underneath him. He’s an idiot. He always was an idiot. He thought he was the best on the team. The fact of the matter is he was only the best because I made him that way. I made everyone on the team who they are now. He was second best only because he was the best of the pretenders.” “And now you’re playing on his dreams again,” Jeff replied finally understanding what was going on. “Yeah, now I’m playing on his dreams.” Ryan admitted. “The best part is that I’m getting rich off him. But all the money in the world won’t have the same satisfaction as watching his face when I tell him the truth.” “What?!” Jeff snarled. “You can’t tell him that! He’ll talk!” “No, I meant the truth about their not being a spot for the world championships,” Ryan explained. “When I see the look of disappointment and anger in his face, it will be orgasmic.” [name withheld] sighed. Not because he was white and was confused with being a Mexican, but because he knew that the scandal he and Ryan’s father in was too much. They had played on Sebastian’s dream, on his hopes, and on his blind ambitions. They even went as far as injuring him, nearly causing him serious damage all so they could benefit from his labor. But now with Doctor Curiosity looming around the corner, it seemed like their fun could be ended. “There’s a good chance he could go out this round,” Jeff reminded him. “He’s facing the doctor, Doctor Curiosity.” “How that old quack got this far is beyond me.” Ryan scoffed. “You’re right though, I don’t think our friend Sebastian will beat him. Shame really. Not that it matters. All this means is that I get to see him earlier.” “Then shouldn’t you be getting to work with this plan of yours?” Jeff asked. Ryan laughed. “Work? Please this city is full of hot women. Beer. Girls. Beer. Girls. What kind of guy puts wrestling over partying with the opposite sex?” Jeff scoffed. “Yeah, sure, but it’s not like you’re ever gonna score with one. At least with this tournament you have a slight chance of winning.” The Odds Aren’t In Your Favor Sebastian took the last bite out of his hotdog and threw the napkin into a nearby trashcan. Monday’s was usually his strenuous workout day, but this Monday he felt uneager to continue. There were many reasons for it. For one, the injury he suffered while trying to help Steve Spinerall was still lingering with him. The pain subsided just a day before his match with Elia Smith. However, the match was fast-paced, and Elia took full advantage of the scouting report she read on Sebastian. In the end, however, Sebastian still won, but now seems to have injured his leg again. Secondly, he hadn’t seen Sarah for a while and was beginning to wonder if the feelings weren’t mutual. But worst of all, his desire went low when he heard of his next round opponent. It’s not that Sebastian fears Doctor Curiosity or thinks he will lose. It’s that he has the utmost respect for the man, but he hates the blanket of immortality that’s been woven around him. It hasn’t been unusual for Sebastian to be an underdog in this tournament. As a matter of fact, he was the underdog in every round. The difference here is that he had a chance of winning in earlier rounds. The betting spreads, the fans, newspapers, everyone always gave him a chance to win. He was an underdog, yes, but he wasn’t a mistake. But now, he’s beyond being an underdog. The odds against him are at 20-1, and that’s being modest. It’s one thing to read that you’re an underdog but you have a chance, and another to read that you’re an underdog and you have no chance. Maybe if you’re in hell you might have a chance. “Hey, you’re Sebastian!” a voice cried out from behind him. Sebastian turned around to see a tall and skinny kid. He was possibly around fifteen years of age. “Yeah, I am.” The kid laughed. “I’m sorry, I thought you fighters were all like big shot celebrities riding around in your limousines and shit.” Sebastian laughed. “I’m not a celebrity. In fact, I make the least money out of all us fighting.” “I think that’s cool,” the kid commented. “You don’t let all that fame go to through your head. You think you can beat Doctor Curiosity?” Sebastian smiled a bit. “Do you think I can beat him?” “Of course,” the kid answered. “The guy’s good, but he’s old. He just gets lucky. I even heard that he told the media that he was going to win. I don’t like him. I think you can beat him. Don’t you?” “I do,” Sebastian agreed. “I know I can beat him.” “You beat him and someone is going to have a hell of a payday.” Someone spoke out. Sebastian looked around and saw a man, in his thirties leaning against the wall. “Who are you?” Sebastian asked. “My name’s Martin Booker,” the man answered. “Who’s the kid?” “Andrew,” he answered. “Anyways, I got to go. Good luck in the match.” Andrew left and Sebastian turned back towards Booker. “Why are you saying if I win someone is gong to have a hell of a payday?” Martin smirked. “Have you been reading the papers? You’re a 20-1 underdog, and the word is the odds are only to get worse.” “Who are you?” Who was Martin Booker? Las Vegas knows. [name withheld] knows. Martin was a bookie from Las Vegas, and he was here making sure the spreads on the tournament wee to his liking. Much more than that, he was a hustler, a criminal, greedy, and selfish. He was also a prime supporter of Ryan Howell’s plan. Something that Sebastian knew nothing of. “Who am I?” Martin repeated the question. “I’m just visiting. I live in Las Vegas, actually. I’m just here to enjoy this great annual event.” “You’ve come pretty far,” Sebastian commented. “So you think I don’t have a chance.” “I think that it’s going to be really hard for you to win,” Martin answered. “Curiosity is one of the best fighters in the tournament, and there is no reason an amateur like yourself can even give him a challenge.” Sebastian scoffed. “You’re just like everyone else. Always writing me off. Whatever happened to giving people a chance? I went into every damn match as an underdog. And I won all of them. The one I lost was to someone you people refer to as a legend. Even then, I nearly beat him.” “He wasn’t Curiosity,” Martin answered. “But I like your attitude though. You’re so angry and full of hatred. If you keep fighting like you have so far, I think you can win. It will be close though. Doctor Curiosity is no slouch.” Sebastian rolled his eyes and walked away. So far, only one person thought he could win. And it was a kid. Just one vote is all he needs though, Sebastian thought. He knows he can beat Doctor Curiosity. He will beat Doctor Curiosity. Sebastian continued to walk down the street, looking at the posters, newspapers, and magazines all claiming his end. Sebastian shrugged it off, but still felt anger at the Doctor’s arrogance. “I fucking hate arrogance.”
