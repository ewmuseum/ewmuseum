In the time of need…

9 February 2006

Date: 2006-02-09 In the time of need, we often turn to our loved ones for support This was indeed the life, he thought to himself, as he lay on his back with his hands behind his head in the thick green grass. Looking up into the bluest of blue skies as the sun’s rays warmed all that it touched, he smiled and accepted the fact that he could relax and be happy. It had been years since he was able to just take time out and relax with his family but now there was more time for that since he retired from Global Wrestling and there was nothing that would ever make him go back into the business or sport as some called it ever again. A slight breeze blew a few strands of Salaok’s hair into his face which didn’t bother him, only to then be followed by a giggle of a female. Not just any female, but the woman he had chosen to spend the rest of his natural life with and cherish every moment he had with her and there son. There had been no breeze, just a simple blow from between the luscious lips of his beloved wife before she lends over and softly brushed the hair out of his face, only to then plant the softest of kisses on his forehead. The sound of children laughing drew nearer as the excitement of life filled their lungs as they ran towards Salaok and Lisha. Propping himself up on his elbows, Salaok watches as his son Darrell and his nephew Emmanuell come running towards them with smiles and the sound of joy filling the atmosphere. "This is what we’ve been missing Sal." Lisha leans in and whispers into her husbands ears as he watches the children with a smile of his own stretched from ear to ear. "I know Lisha, but I had to make sure we were going to have enough money to enjoy these kinds of moments for the rest of our lives. Global was an easy way for us to do that and still spend time together." Salaok’s response sounded almost rehearsed as if the conversation had happened before a number of times but there was no malice here, just pleasure. Lisha nods as if to say she understands and then wraps her arms around Salaok and wrestles him to a laying position before jumping on top of him, pinning him down with his knees. "You’ve only been in retirement for two weeks and you’re already getting beaten by a girl!" Lisha jokes, knowing full well that Salaok could lift her up with one arm easily. Again she leans down and plants a soft kiss on Salaok, this time on his lips which they both hold for a moment and enjoy the simple exchange of love. A love which was growing more and more with every day they spent together and a love that brought laughter from the children as Darrell and Emmanuell giggled and then jumped on top of the two. This was life how it was meant to be. Surrounded by his loved ones and without a care in the world, Salaok smiled to himself under the pile of people on top of him knowing that for the first time in many years he was truly happy. In the time of need, we often turn to our loved ones for support As the four of them continued to play, an odd smell drifts into the air, like that of burning skin which makes Salaok stop for a moment and roll out of the play fighting which had begun. The perfect blue sky was no longer as such as think dark clouds had begun to roll in from no where and the still air now carried a breeze which caused the trees to dance like a drunk, swaying back and forth. With a giant burst of light and a thunderous crack so loud that even if deafest of person could hear, the rain begins to fall down onto the lush green grass underfoot. Or at least it was one lush green grass but now with every drop of rain, the grass begins to almost melt away, shrivelling and turning a deathly brown in colour. Salaok’s eyes move up from his fixation on the grass to those who had been playing oh so happily together just moment before hand but now stand with their feet sunk into the muddy ground and their hands reaching out towards Salaok for help. Salaok however was no help either as his feet had also sunken into the quickly forming mud but he tried as hard as he possibly could to wrench his feet free but it was of no use. Looking up at his family and the looks of pain on their faces, he realises that they are calling out for him but no nose comes from their screams. Their innocent family, distorting with pain and then the rain seems to change colour to a thick red that with every drop rips at the skin of Salaok’s loved one. Unable to reach them and carry them off to safety like a good husband, father, uncle should have, he had no choice other than to stand there watching in horror as the rain ripped at their skin. Blood begins to ooze from the young family as the red acid rain eats away at their very beings and then their screams become audible. He had wished they had remained silent for then the screams wouldn’t have haunted him. If the screams couldn’t be heard then he could close his eyes and it wouldn’t be happening, but it was. All three screamed out for help, screamed out in pain, screamed out in horror as they saw what it was doing to each other. And soon the screams began to gurgle as blood filled their airways. And soon the gurgling stopped with the screams. There, lying in the mud covered with blood and most of their beautiful skin ripped from their bodies, his family, as he knew it and everything he loved in the world. Finally he was able to get his feet free and run over to his family, motionless, silent, dead. With the looks of pain still etched into their faces, Salaok struggles to look at them as the tears well up in his eyes and as he shuts them the screams begin again. In the time of need, we often turn to our loved ones for support Opening his eyes, the scene is gone from sight and he lies on his back in bed, staring up at the roof through his blur morning vision, thanking God that it had only been a dream. For a second he forgets. Rolling over to look at his wife, he moves slowly, not to wake her, as he knew how much she enjoyed her sleep-ins when they were available to have. Nothing. The cold hard reminded that Lisha had passed away over two years ago now strikes at his heart as he looks at the empty side of the bed which he can not lay in. When she was alive, the two of them would always have their own side of the bed, much like most couples as they get accustomed to sleeping a that certain side. There had been one night where he had rolled over by accident, not long after the death of Lisha and the smell of her was still embedded into the pillow and he had awoken, only to then cry the rest of the night away. That pillow has never since been washed. He had been wanting a good nights sleep tonight, but like most nights, that was never going to be the case as his new memories of his past were not only haunting him, but controlling everything in his life at the moment. The reason for wanting a good nights sleep was because later in that day he would be stepping into the ring once again with Tsonda. The very man that had beaten him the round before but due to the two men advancing out of the four, they would fight again this round. And this round Salaok had vowed would be different as coming second this time around would mean he had lost. Losing wasn’t an option, never had been. Although that match was some time away, he would now have the whole day to stew on it unless he found something to fill the time. And while he knew most of his family was dead, there was one he knew was still alive and today he would see her again. Today he would see his baby sister. In the time of need, we often turn to our loved ones for support The weather was a stark difference to that of the first half of his dream as the snow was still falling this winter and the air felt like it was being generated by an industrial freezer of some sorts. Moving himself out of the car, he feet hit the snow and he sinks down into it a good half a foot bringing back the thoughts of his dream, being stuck in the mud. Although this time he was able to walk through it and with each step, it became a little bit easier as the muscles adjusted to the difference. Each step bringing the door closer and each step bringing the nerves in his gut closer to his throat as he worried about the reaction that his sister would give him. Eve had not involved herself in the family business that Johnathon and Salaok had run together for the past two decades, instead opting to be out of the picture and the spotlights of those who hunted her brothers. A normal life is all that Eve had asked her brothers to allow her and they had both agreed to leave her out of the war which had followed the two of them. A normal life? Is there indeed such a thing when your brothers are heavily involved in the Gothic underworld movement which at times during the 90’s had parts of the world in a strangle grip? Each day Eve would have to check the news papers, waiting for the day that she knew was coming and that was the day her brothers would not only be found out, but brought to justice or killed on the spot by one of their rivals. While Salaok and Johnathon were often at war with each other in the later years, they were very much alike which meant their rivals had common goals. At times it was merely the fact that Johnathon and Salaok held common enemies that they had stopped themselves from killing each other. Reaching the steps at the foot of the house, slowly Salaok begins to ascend them, his heavy boots clopping on the cement, which he knew, had drawn the attention of someone as a shadow appeared through the glass window in the door. As the final step was left behind him, the door opens and Eve stands there in all of her innocent beauty and the look happiness across her face. A single tear runs down her check before she lunges forward, wrapping her arms around Salaok, holding him tightly. Surprised at first by the overwhelming show of affection, Salaok just stands there before finally hugging his baby sister back only to have her whisper in his eyes. "I’m so glad to see you Sal. And to have the two of you here at once is a surprise that I never would have believed could happen." Salaok’s eyes widen in shock as he realises what Eve had just said as a figure walks around the corner of the door. "Hello Salaok." Johnathon’s voice full of arrogance, as his guess of Salaok’s next move had been a correct one. This was one game that Salaok was indeed getting sick of playing but Johnathon clearly had some other plans for his brother. "It’s been such a long time Salaok, your sister and I have missed you. Lets catch." Not wanting his little sister to see the look the two brother were exchanging out of hatred, Salaok holds he tightly to his chest knowing that today, something was going to happen and he wasn’t going to like it one bit. With Eve’s back to Johnathon, the older of the two brothers mouths his words to Salaok. "May the games begin." In the time of need, we often turn to our loved ones for support
