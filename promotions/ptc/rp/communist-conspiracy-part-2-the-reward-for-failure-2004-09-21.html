Communist Conspiracy Part 2 - The reward for failure

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 Old age isn’t so bad when you consider the alternative. -Maurice Chevalier “You failed.” Granddad George English sat lashed to the Goofy chair in the depths of the Disney Store’s basement. A thousand plastic eyes from a thousand different shelves stared on in silent witness; amongst their plush and stuffed number were Mickey Mao, Chairman Duck and Scrooge McCapitalist. The jolly characters were all part of the Communist Conspiracy: a master plan Granddad George had unwittingly stumbled across, to infiltrate communism across the Western World. When the time was right Disney was to throw off the façade of the loveable children’s institution and rise in a night of bloody revolution, this time not to overthrow a repressive Czarist regime but an old enemy: the capitalist pigs. Success would once more establish the United Soviet States of Russia as the premier global superpower. With military precision, each theme park would act as a command centre to coordinate the garrisons of Disney Stores that were located in every prominent shopping mall near to a significant urban population. London, Paris and New York would each fall in a single bloody night of mouse ears and talking ducks in sailor suits. It was no laughing matter. No seriously. So where does the elderly George English fit into all of this? Well let’s bring you up to speed: after unwittingly accepting a large amount of roubles in the Seventies to “sleep” for the Russian government, English now found himself an indebted sleeper agent of the Red Menace. The lynch pin of the Bolshevik plan was to re-establish Lenin’s cryogenically severed head -- a.k.a. Walt Disney -- onto a donor body. Okay so Brock Lesnar may not have “donated” his body as such, but the Russian alternative was a viable option more likely to transpire than any deluded football career the big lummox had envisaged. With a super brawny Lenin at the head (badaboom) of the Disney Duma, world domination was more or less guaranteed. Fortunately for the rest of the planet, head transplants are notoriously difficult operations. For starters, due to the United Nations Ethics Committee, it’s pretty much illegal in most civilised societies; one would have to go to Afghanistan, Iran or Amsterdam for the outlandish procedure and then find a scientist crazy enough to attempt the swap. And so George English, Grandfather of PRIME’s most successful non-wrestling wrestler, had been unwillingly inserted into the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament with the aim of defeating and capturing a man depraved and twisted enough to guarantee surgical success. For all intents and purposes the plan was sound; it had been meticulously calculated down to the last detail by the supercomputer inside the Magic Kingdom: a fusion of Pentium Processor, chimpanzee, tinfoil and typewriter. But the Xeon Primatium 4 hadn’t...indeed couldn’t...account for a crucial ephemeral factor in it’s parameters. A very large mallet. A “curious” mallet. George English remembered very little from his first wrestling match - he wasn’t greatly concerned at this though, being ancient and senile, memory loss was nothing new. He did get the occasional flashback; a large boxing glove approaching at tremendous velocity, but that image had to fight for space and time in his head with all the other flashbacks centuries of haphazard escapades had produced. Nonetheless his defeat and failed attempt to capture Doctor Curiosity had foiled the plans of the Red Menace, rendering Lenin armless and harmless. George had unwittingly saved the world for the nineteenth time in a row. For now. When Granddad George came round from the concussion he was tied to the novelty plastic chair. Each Disney Store was a microscopic representation of the company as a whole, like an iceberg the visible part was only ten percent of the operation. As such, the basement was filled with Russian Mafia henchmen...as well as piglet and Winnie the Pooh. “Hard cheese, I failed, what you gonna do about it eh? Torture me?” George made an attempt to stand and then discovered he was bound to the abnormally weird dog/man creature that is Goofy. (Answers on a postcard if anyone actually knows what Goofy is...) “Yes.” beamed Red Ivan in monosyllabic affirmation. The guy towering before the captured geriatric was the local Russian Maffia Don and Ex-KGB Director of Foreign Intelligence. Any description of his size would have been an understatement; it was rumoured that in the harsh tundra of Belize, Red Ivan had impersonated a Kodiak bear by killing the creature with his bare, bear-like, hands. For the next four terrifying days, wearing it’s torn head over his own as a disguise, Ivan had preceded to pick off a hunter force of US Rangers assigned to capture, or failing that, kill him. Tales from the barracks of the Green Berets are still told of how the mad Ruskie stalked them armed only with the waist-elastic from his boxer shorts. “Oh I’m so scaaaared,” began the sarcastic old man. “Mummy, please don’t let the mad, bad, ex-KGB pain inducing interrogation specialist...aah.” the sarcasm slowly drained from George’s words as he reconsidered the situation. Firstly, he didn’t have the upper hand. Secondly calling someone’s bluff when they’ve threatened to torture you was asking for trouble. Red Ivan’s second-in-command frowned down at his toolbox, “Which pliers should I use?” “The long-nosed ones Boris.” “You can’t!” it almost came out as a shrill shriek; George made a mental note that if he survived the encounter he’d change that to a confident utterance of defiance followed by a knowledgeable laugh. “Why not Comrade English?” “I’m old and weak and feeble...I have a, err, wooden leg. Yeah a wooden leg! Surely you wouldn’t hurt a cripple.” >Knock< >Knock< >Knock< Granddad George rapped on the timber prosthesis in a shameless bid for sympathy. “That’s the chair leg.” came the flat reply. “Ah, so it is. I see I overestimated my initial underestimation of you Ivan.” Boris sat on the sidelines scratching his head at that one, Red Ivan was losing patience though and gave the command in Russian: “Pull out the old cunt's front teeth!” “But I don’t own any sheep...” said the puzzled old git. George’s Russian was a little rusty, his vocabulary limited mainly to words like MIG, Smirnoff and Kalashnikov. Boris closed in with the pliers a sharp toothy grin fought to emerge from the unshaven bristles on his face. The more George thought about it the more he realised that these nouveux commies had a Bluto identity crisis going on. Red Ivan took a sidestep so he could see past Boris, the Maffia Don was bedecked in his trademark crimson suit (another rumour has it that it was once white) and furry hat -- you know the kind -- it looked like Ivan had shoved his head up a giant rat’s arse, well a weasel’s then. Others in attendance were Simba, three of the Seven Dwarves and Captain Hook, looking nefariously pleased as usual. Any more onlookers and English would’ve had to apply for a PPV licence. Granddad George’s pleadings and protestations went unheard, it was time for the visceral gut knotting pain, or “fun,” if you go by the loose interpretation they have in Moscow. Those of a delicate nature skip this part. The henchman bore down on the old geezer grabbing him by his Indiana Jones-esque leather hat to steady his head - George was no fool though and clamped his mouth shut. Boris had anticipated such a tactic, nipping English’s bottom lip with the needle-nosed pliers breaking tender leathery skin cracked by age. “ARGGHHH!!” George screamed out, quick as a whippet the Russian jammed the pliers inside establishing a beachhead. A fetid aroma of soft rotten gums and the stench of piss on the old fart’s dirty unbrushed teeth almost made Boris gag. He fought down the bitter rising bile in his throat while scrabbling the tool around for purchase: producing a metal clinking sound like a stonemason playing the xylophone. Finally grip was gained on the soft two front teeth; rasping metal bumps bit and scraped along the enamel grinding away their delicate surface. Squeezing the handles together, Boris yanked back. Like a pitbull on a man’s arm George was yanked up, the novelty chair taken clean off the floor. (Ironically after this piece of amateur dentistry, poor old English would become whatever the reverse of goofy was.) One of his front teeth snapped under pressure, the jagged shards falling down the back of his throat, then he crashed heavily to the floor as the needle-nosed pliers (still attached to his teeth) slipped from Boris’ grasp. Determined to finish the job, the Ruskie mafioso planted a big size thirteen shoe on George’s sparrow chest, gripped the pliers in both hands and heaved back with all his might. This time, like ripe seeds popping from a squidgy watermelon, his teeth were ripped apart from their gums with a gruesome schlop. Such was the force of separation that Boris stumbled back into a shelving unit. Unfortunately he was saved by a resident herd of podgy, grumpy-looking Eeyores whose soft stuffing broke his fall. He rose and held the crescent of yellowing tombstones up like a trophy to show his sadistic boss. “Agh ha ha haaarghh!!” It was a horrible guttural noise, the death rattle of a clogged drainpipe. The two Russian’s looked quizzically down at the old man, both more than a little disturbed despite their past expertise in shooting defenceless Chechen children and fluffy Bosnian puppies. “I gueshh I’ll haffa buy shhronger gum adheshhive eh?” slurred the toothless wonder. Next time he’d try the ‘concrete’ grade of Fixadent. Boris flung the slimy dentures at Red Ivan faster than an amorous face-hugger trying to skull fuck his head without buying dinner. Ivan caught the substitute teeth, then bellowed in realisation. He also bellowed because he was the boss and the others expected it - you don’t become top nut in the orchard without a healthy blood-boiling frustration for incompetence. It was a fine balance to strike though: bellow too much at something like the lack of paperclips and the minions will eventually grow immune to it and not take you seriously. “Shay Ihfan whuh kin’a Mickey Moushh organissashhun are you running?” “Don’t ridicule me you old shit!” “Whuh you gonna do eh? Make me shhuffer shhom more? I’m shho shhcared...” “You should’ve quit while you were ahead Comrade English.” “Shho shhud’ve Lenin, haah, geddih? A HEAD! HAHAHA...” “Boris, we are moving up to stage two - bring...the ELECTROSHOCKER!” Boris looked puzzled. “The car battery you idiot!” for the love of Mother Russia! Red Ivan was considering taking out an ad for a new second-in-command. You just couldn’t trust job agencies to provide good henchmen anymore. Granddad George cursed his big mouth and rotten luck, “You can’h do thissh to me - I’m a war hero, I goh medals!” “Yes that is true Comrade English, you received the Iron Cross if I remember rightly.” “Issh shhtill a medal!” protested Germany’s only British war hero. 3rd Janury 1941 It was early in the morning as thick fog, surely from Davy Jones’ bum crack itself, seeped through the railings on the deck of HMS Neptune. The £1,600,000 British Destroyer was part of a larger fleet operating against the enemy close to the hostile shores of the Mediterranean. Not that any of this mattered, especially to one man in particular; George English was still patting himself on the back for getting a free holiday cruise...better than that, the fools were paying him to be here! Joining the Royal Navy had been a stroke of genius on his part and so far the fleet had spent the majority of its time docked at Gibraltar. Sun, sea, women and monkeys - it didn’t get much better than that. Now that the war was stepping up in earnest, Africa Fleet had been dispatched on manoeuvres to try and engage and if possible sink the Nazi High Seas Fleet. The Second World War was becoming a mild inconvenience for George because as "sonar operator and communications specialist" this meant he’d be cooped up in the communications nest: a hastily constructed tin shack, built as an afterthought for the new-fangled technology the boffins back in Portsmouth were developing. It baked him in the day, froze him at night and there was no way it’s flimsy construction would save him from direct shelling, nor help him work on his sun tan. There was one consolation though. He had control over the ship’s wireless. The most expensive high frequency band radio developed by mankind so far was at his fingertips - sure it was there to intercept enemy communications and coordinate fleet formation but it picked up the BBC World Service just as well. At this moment in time The Jitterbug blared out from his tin shack. Suddenly the hiss of static broke English’s jiving: “…pssshh, come in HMS Neptune this is HMS Kandahar. Over.” “HMS Kandahar this is HMS Neptune what the hell do you wankers want? I was busy...err, listening for enemy communications. Over.” “HMS Neptune you seem to be straying in the fog, tell your navigator to put his glasses on or you’ll find yourself in the minefields. Over.” “Yeah, yeah, whatever, Roger that...now go Roger yerself. Over.” George scribbled down “minefield” and put the note in his pocket - there was no way he was going to disturb the Captain during breakfast. Instead he twiddled with the dials: if the reception was good he could sometimes pick up on some Yank music bounced off the stratosphere. Maybe some Elvis Presley. Twenty minutes later and Glen Miller rocked and rolled from the communications nest. >>BOOM<< English was pitched from his chair, his first thought was that he’d blown a transistor tube in the wireless. With limited supplies the Captain was going to kick his arse this time; George was already thinking up feasible monkey-related excuses. Then, with an almighty judder the ship stopped dead in the water, they were drifting. “We’ve taken a direct hit to the aft engines!” came the alarmed cry. Stern/aft, starboard/port, what the hell did that mean? Why couldn’t the tossers use front/back, left/right? “We’re in a bloody minefield chaps!” came another shout as panicked realisation erupted on the decks outside. The first thing George did was take the “minefield” note from his pocket, put it in his mouth, then swallow it whole. “ENGLIIIISH!!” the Captain had also mastered the art of bellowing as his voice came through the intercom. George nearly choked but regained his composure. Any damning evidence was now on it’s downwards journey to his bowels, “Yes Sir? It wasn’t me Sir!” “Why the Hell would this be your fault sailor...unless there’s something you’re not telling me.” suddenly the Captain sounded suspicious. “No Sir, I know nothing.” “That much is apparent. Get on the wireless and broadcast to the rest of the fleet. Tell them that the Neptune has strayed into a minefield...the last thing we want is for other ships to assume we’ve come under enemy fire and rush in, trying to save us.” “But what about us Sir, surely we need help?” smoke from an oil fire had reached the Limey’s nose, that and the smell of sizzling bacon... “We’ll do what we can but The Neptune is as good as lost English, do I make myself clear Sailor?” “Crystal. Sir.” George switched channels to a wide band frequency, “MAYDAY! MAYDAY! THIS IS HMS NEPTUNE - WE’VE COME UNDER ENEMY FIRE AND ARE GOING DOWN, I REPEAT, WE ARE GOING DOWN!! OUR COORDINATES ARE...” “ENGLIIIISH, WHAT IN BLAZES ARE YOU DOING!?” “Saving my arse. Sir!” - old habits died hard. “I’LL SEE YOU’RE BROUGHT BEFORE THE ADMIRALTY AND HUNG FOR THIS!!” >>BOOM<< All went quiet over the intercom as the ship pitched to whatever the nautical equivalent of left was. A second passed, then the moans and bloodcurdling screams from the bridge began, George hastily cut the intercom. Lifeboats were out of the question with pressure sensitive balls of TNT floating about, so he tucked his head between his legs and prayed for help to arrive. (And to kiss his arse goodbye.) An optimist -- when left with no choice -- English kept telling himself that at least one of the vessels from the flotilla would make it through to the Neptune. Out of fifteen ships the odds were in favour of one surviving. The explosions outside began anew as George’s rescue party surged into hostile waters. Two hours later and all was quiet apart from the occasional groan of twisted steel. The men on HMS Neptune had long since succumbed to the oil fires that had ravaged the deck, or drowned while pumping out the lower levels. All the men but one that is. George English untucked himself, tired of waiting for rescue or death. Besides - the smell of fried meat was making his stomach rumble. He dragged the makeshift barricade from the front of the steel door and unscrewed the seal to step out onto the smouldering deck, now bathed in the glorious midday sun. The vista before him was one of burning hulks and triangular hulls jutting up like giant shark fins from the azure depths of the sea. The whole of Africa Fleet had been scuttled upon the minefield, what were the odds of that? No wonder you don’t meet rich gamblers. Bollocks. Not only had Britain lost it’s fleet guarding the supply routes from the Southern Hemisphere, but George had lost his only means of escape. The Neptune shuddered as if trying to shake off a parasite, before the Limey knew what had happened he was falling into the sea. He hit the water with a... >>CLANG<< What the fuck? Water wasn’t meant to be that hard! He got up onto his hands and knees, he was riding the back of a giant whale. Like Jonah -- without the being eaten part -- George really had to give himself a break and stop pissing off God... A man poked out of a hatch in the whale’s blow hole, “Ahoy zere Englunder! Velcome aboart mine U-boat!” The German Captain spoke quite good English, “Qvite a mess jah? I sthink this vill be a short vor.” “Look mate, I know you’re a Jerry and all, but from one sailor to another do yer think you could drop me off at Dover?” “Vell to be honest ve ver goink to dive und take bets on how long yout survive.” “German wankers.” uttered George on his charm offensive. “Vanker? I am familiar vif dis term - are you George Englund jah?” “Close enough, wos it to you?” “Ven ve listun to ze enutmy communications you are a favourite vif ze crew...un fact you a big hero in ze Reichland!” the Captain seemed generally enthused, it made a nice change to be appreciated, “I insist you come aboart as mine guest.” “Well since you insist...and been as I’m stuck in the middle of a sea surrounded by mines.” Two months later and George English was in Berlin a whole four years before the Allies. The crowds of gathered people cheered as the uberman, who had sunk Africa Fleet single-handedly, accepted the prestigious Iron Cross from the Fuhrer. It was the second time English had bumped into Hitler. “I took yor advice Herr Englunder, ze German people zey luff ze mouztash - it hass got me to vare I am today.” “And there was me thinking it was the policies of terror and mass genocide...” Present Day Boris was still struggling to conduct a charge from the car battery. “How many times have I told you imbeciles?? Whoever uses the electroshocker last is the one who has to charge it! Is that not so simple!?” Red Ivan was more than a little displeased. George English wasn’t too comfortable either; his yellowing shirt was undone displaying a physique that made Ric Flair look in shape. Pinched either side of his chest, on a warty old nipple that could’ve been mistaken for a withered raisin, were crocodile clips on leads that were fixed to the battery terminals. Under managerial pressure, Boris resorted to Man’s quick-fix solution since time immemorial - he hit the car battery. BZZZZZHHH Static filled the air, George jolted bolt upright, “ARGHH MY HEART!!” Red Ivan gloated, “It hurts yes?” “I wouldnh shhay thah...ishh more like a beahing shhenshastion.” a rhythmic thudding stirred in the crusty old Limey’s ribcage as his heart began to pulse once more. “Give him another blast.” The battery let out a final fizzle and gave up the ghost, Boris shook his head in the negative at Ivan. The big Ruskie mentalist was about to flip out again, “How are we meant to take over the world when we can’t even maintain the torture devices??” “Sorry boss.” Right then and there, Red Ivan would have shot Boris on the spot and made an example of him to the rest of the Disney workers...but help of any kind was hard to come by. The problem was Ivan was an old school pro; he took great pride in maintaining the tools of his trade. Ever since the Communists had gone underground and the KGB disbanded, standards had slipped, especially with the young wannabe gangsters that were signing up with the Mafia. He reminded himself the most important thing was the big picture -- the New Revolution and Mother Russia -- the final destination was the most important thing, not the journey taken or means of transport. And right now Lenin needed a new body, Communism had to adapt if it wanted to survive. Time for some improvisation, “You try my patience and hospitality with your unwillingness to submit to a painful torture Comrade English. I think maybe there is more to you than meets the eye, yes? Well no matter, we will arrange for entrance back in the tournament.” “Buh I loshh, I woshh knocked ouh.” in every sense of the word. “Work with me here Comrade English; we have to maintain the tenuous link as to why you, a non-wrestler, would participate in a wrestling tournament. That tenuous link will be the pursuit of Doctor Curiosity for Lenin’s head transplant...that is if he doesn’t get eliminated, or we’ll be in real unconvincing shit then.” “Buh the brackehs have been decided.” George had his doubts. “Hah, this is still a capitalist democracy -- for the moment -- bribery and extortion will see you back in the running. I will try to find you a similarly elderly opponent, or as near as I can get... until we can arrange a rematch with the good doctor. Maybe you could get a few lessons from your grandson?” Like Hannibal from the A-Team, Red Ivan loved it when a plan came together, and right now he was feeling The Jazz. Boris un-nipple-clamped the elderly Limey and gave him back his false teeth. George English vowed that at the first sign of opportunity he’d save his sweet ass, flee from this crazy Red Menace and the Communist Conspiracy that was perpetually building around him.
