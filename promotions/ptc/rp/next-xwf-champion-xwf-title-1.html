Limp Bizkit Affiliated Pimp Joined: 07 Apr 2003 Posts: 1 Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 8:35 am Post subject: Next XWF Champion (XWF Title #1) (Scene opens showing a large and timely home of some sort. White on the outside, very well done on the inside. Camera cuts to the inside of the house, a large hallway, appearing to be somewhat of a corridor due to its size. Limp Bizkit and Illianis are stood. Illianis is wearing a belly top with denims on the bottom. Limp Bizkit wearing a shirt unbuttoned and a pair of cargo pants, 3/4 in length. Camera PA sound is tuned in to perfection as the conversation is heard.) Limp Bizkit: Yeah I agree totally. So what exactly was it you called me over for? Illianis: Well. I just wanted to see you really, and talk about your match. Limp Bizkit: Oh okay. That's cool. So what is up that you called me over? You don't think I’m scared or anything do you? Illianis: No, don't be stupid. Just wanted to know what your going threw what’s up? Limp Bizkit: Well, I’m cool. As always I’m just chilling, laying back enjoying the last few days. However I’m a bit pissed off to hear that I didn’t get this title shot a lot sooner. Illianis: But, don't you think, that due to the friction between C.R. & Asesino don't you think that will work in your favor? Limp Bizkit: Well, yeah I guess so. However ... (Turning to the camera.) Limp Bizkit: Let me just inform you. If anyone gets their paths crossed in mines. I will not hesitate to lay only a hand on you, but my full in complete fist. You see, I do not have time to be lumbered and caught up in between problems and path crossing. Whether it be me Vs you, or me Vs C.R. I’M telling you know, just get out of my business, and let it be! I’m only here to beat C.R. and win the XWF World Title. So people just mind your own business, and see the ref counting to three, for your going to see at New Era when I pin the World Champion. (Camera pans to Illianis.) Illianis: Limp Bizkit, I do not know if you know, but if you win at New Era it means you will defend that title at Payback the next XWF PPV. Limp Bizkit: Yeah I heard, and quite frankly, that does not catch my attention as it once did. The World title? I have other things to do as well. I mean it's still going to be my belt, however at this moment in time, I have some of my toughest challenges to get threw, then my focus will turn to my championship belt! C.R. it begins with you and ends with the title you currently hold. Then the conquest of one Limp Bizkit ends, with an outstanding win, and a 15lbs of gold to top it all of. Illianis: Wow! You've really though this threw! Limp Bizkit: Damn right! You see Illianis, I've been planning something like this for a long time now, just not knew when to begin, and well, what a better way to start than with defeating the current official World Champion. I mean come on, Limp Bizkit, every one loves him, Limp Bizkit, every one idolizes and wants to be him. And by beating C.R., at New Era I am getting to that title that every one wants me to get to, the XWF World Heavyweight Championship. Now C.R. big stuff, let me just tell you, I am going into this match to grab a win, and I am going into this match to win the XWF Title even better. Then at Payback, after I defeat whoever the challenger is, the hunt is on, whether your in XWF or not, it really doesn't matter. I'll defeat you come New Era, like I’ve done before. It may even be P.R., your brother, I’ll also defeat him for my title if he quits jobbing, it may even be Asesino, for all I care. I will however be on a mission to get my belt, and when I do, you'll be sure to look back and ask yourself, "Wow is he really that good?" And I'll be sure as hell the first to tell you, "YES I AM THAT GOOD!" Illianis: Damn. Your all set and ready to go to New Era aren’t you? Limp Bizkit: Damn right! You see I’m going to make all those disbelievers believe. I’M going to make all those unfaithful people, faithful. And all those Rigido fans. Illianis: C.R. has fans?? Limp Bizkit: (Looks confused) I-Think-He-Has-a-Few. Well, I'll make the majority of what he has the new and improved Limp Bizkit fans, for after defeating him and beating him the way I intend on doing. They will not want to support such a chump-changed has-been like C.R. Oh no, yet they'll be supporting the forever, show stopping, headlining, week in week out, champion of all champions, Icon of all Icons, me! Limp Bizkit. Illianis: Cool. Hey I was going to ask you, with this been a special event and all, if you'd like me to accompany you to ringside? Limp Bizkit: Um.. Not that your not needed, but, no thanks babe. I want to do this alone. However if C.R. tries any of his antics against me, you'll be sure to get out there right? Illianis: Of course. Limp Bizkit: Great. The Corporation said they'd do the same too. So his little brother has no chance in this one. Haha! As if they would stand a chance anyway. Illianis: Hmm, yeah you got a point. However what do you think Asesino will hold against C.R.? Limp Bizkit: I don't know. A screwing? A punch? A kick? A bitch slap? After all, C.R. is a little b*tch! Who knows, as long as he stays out of my way, I’ll keep my cool. But if he gets in my way I’ll be sure to cut him down to his penis size! Illianis: 4 inches? Limp Bizkit: 2! Illianis: Wow! So Limp Bizkit, what time is our flight? Limp Bizkit: Um... 8 hours from now. We're hitting the airport at around 6. Should get in LA for around 8 or 9. Depending on flight delays etc. Illianis: Great, I’m all set anyways, so we'd be as well, just making the most of it for now. Want a drink? Limp Bizkit: Sure why not.
