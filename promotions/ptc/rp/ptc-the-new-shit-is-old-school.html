"The New Shit" -IS- Old School

2 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Why do any of these people feel they are worthy of gracing a wrestling ring?" The question rang in our ears as the scene opened to the inside of what appears to be a FUSE locker room. We get the idea from the large insignia on the back wall depicting the words "FUSE Wrestling". The voice continues from off camera. "Archibald MacGregor, a man whom can't even join the company he want to be a champion of. A man who cannot earn his shots as the rest of the world must, but would rather sneak in and steal his prize from those that deserve it. Steal from fighters whom have busted their ass for too long without recognition in this world. People whom really deserve the title of 'champion'. People like me." The man steps out in front of the FUSE logo, dawning dark sunglasses and a long black trench coat. His black hair hangs down to his shoulders, and falls partially in front of his face. His body language screams that he is ready for a fight. He wears black jeans, and a black t-shirt under the trench coat with the words "Prepare to Evolve" across the chest in bright red lettering. He cracks a sadistic smile as he tilts his head up, looking into the camera. His smile seems more than sadistic, it's the type of smile you'd see on an arsonist as he watched a farm house burn. There's something deeper under that smile. "My name is 'The New Shit' Bobino and I am here to declare war on Archibald MacGregor. I'm not just going to destroy Archy, I'm going to eradicate his entire kind. I'm going to commit genocide on every last person who feels they're worthy of stepping into a scene, stealing a title from people who truly deserve it, and then expect praise and recognition for it. I'm going to use Archy as my example. I'm going to teach him what a true tactician of this sport can do when he constantly changes, when he constantly eliminates his weaknesses... when he evolves. Archy isn't the type of person who can think beyond the here and now. He looks at what he wants, and goes for it. While this trait can be looked at as determination, I simply see it as ignorance, and when I step in the ring, ignorance shall not be tolerated." Bobino took a step back and looked at the wall behind him. Gazing at the name of the company he currently represents. "FUSE Wrestling. I've been with this company, under different names, for longer than most people have even been in this business. They've always been near the top of the industry in innovation and entertainment, and having competitors like me, just further cements that position. Now, don't get me wrong. FUSE is far from perfect. They, too are not without their backstage politics, they're overpushed stars, and the off-the-wall gimmicks. Even with all that, and even with the animosity I may hold towards my parent company. Every person who samples a few of FUSE wrestling's shows must admit one simple truth. FUSE represents how wrestling -SHOULD- be done, and people like MacGreggor piss all over that." Bobino steps back into center camera, and removes his sunglasses, folding them carefully, and sliding them into an interior pocket. He brushes the hair out of his face as he continues. "Don't get me wrong. I'm not here as a voice of the people. I'm not standing up for the average joe. I'm simply stating a point. I'm not in this for wrestling. I'm not in this for FUSE. I'm not in this for PTC. I'm in this for the only thing that truly matters in this business... ME. When it comes to the World versus MacGreggor, I'm not going to be satisfied with the result, unless Archy lays flat on his back at -MY- hands. Other people are fighting for the name of PTC, or their company, but my company didn't trust me to fight for them. My company chose Tigera, a -WOMAN- over 'The Master of Darwinism'. Tigera's nothing but a one hit wonder. She'll hold the FUSE Custom C.H.A.O.S. championship for a month or so, and now that she's out of action, I'd be surprised if we ever see her with another championship again. Tigera's simply a blip on the radar of FUSE and of the wrestling industry. Archy and Tigera are a lot alike, actually. They both came in fast, took a title, and expected instant legend status. Soon, Archy will meet the same fate as Tigera, too weak to carry the belt, so they simply crumble." Bobino's grin widens as he pauses for a moment,pacing a bit. He finally stops, and looks towards the ground as he continues to speak. "As far as PTC goes, they're even worse than FUSE. At least FUSe has some decent talent to back up a lot of it's big talk. PTC has nothing but garbage who think they belong at the top. That's probably what attracted Archy here in the first place. Like a fly to trash. When you look at the list of people 'representing' PTC to face Archy, there isn't a single viable name on the list. Valentine, simply another overrated woman who wishes she could be in the men's league. Red Rock has nothing more than a snappy name and a desire to hide his obvious flaws. Cereal Killer thinks that if you put a violent undertone in your name, competitors will fear you. Kushinaga should simply go back to whichever crazy Asian county he came from, his airfare would cost less than the lifetime of humiliation he'll endure once people realize he's a talentless hack. Wolfenden doesn't even have the slightest bit of an advantage over anybody else in that match. Nothing. Simply weak in every category... Want to know how to bring honor back to the now undercard Extreme Championship? Simply add one name to that match. One name alone can bring the belt that has been shat on by all these unevolved chimps back to the glory it once had. One name can simply dominate the rest, and show PTC, FUSE, and any other disbelievers out there what -TRUE- talent and skill is about. You want to know that name? 'The Master of Darwinism', 'The New Shit' Bobino. I can...and will show these people what happens when evolution is put into the right hands. Expect the Extreme Title to come back to the PTC, and make home in FUSE. Not because the competitors are lacking. Not because Archy is in over his head. Not even because I'm simply the most deserving of holding the belt. No, I'll gain the Extreme Championship, simply because... This -IS- The New Shit!!" Bobino laughs to himself as he slides his sunglasses back on. He exits the camera to the right, as we are left staring at the chair and four simple letters as the scene fades to red smoke. F-U-S-E
