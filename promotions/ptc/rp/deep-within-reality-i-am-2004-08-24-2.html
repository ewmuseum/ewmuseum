Deep Within Reality (I AM)

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 {{Please Delete Other One...Many Errors...Sorry.}} ------- "There are some, that if they stood here, in my boots, some would likely find themselves to be delighted. Having said that, I am not overcome by joy. Rather, I am disappointed to see what some might call the end, and what others might call a vacation." --- [The scene opens with Kevin Thompson at a cemetery sitting by an unfamiliar gravesite during sunset on a Tuesday night. The wind is blowing as is begins to lightly rain. ’The New Revolution stands there staring up at the sky as rain falls down onto his face.] “I never liked the rain until I met you. Now every time I'm out in the rain it reminds me of the night we was dancing outside in the streets. It started raining and I was ready to go inside. You., you just stood there smiling and laughing. That was the first time I admitted to myself that I loved you.” Kevin sits there and a tear falls down his cheek. The wind blows through the trees. And once again he hears the words that have been a recurring theme since the beginning of his career, or in reality, since the loss of her…I AM…I AM “ What are you trying to tell me?” Kevin kisses his fingers and presses them against the headstone. “I have to go. I'll be back in a few days. I’m sure you know where I’ll be and what I plan to accomplish…PTC will see something from me that they haven’t seen for over a year.” ‘The New Revolution’ walks towards his Mazda MX-6 as the wind continues to blow…and he continues to hear the words within his head…I AM...I AM. Kevin drives back to his apartment and walks inside. Instantly the phone begins to ring. He steadily makes his way to the phone and answers it to a familiar voice. “Hello?” “Hey man. I just wanted to check up on you and see how you're doing.” Jayson Mecca…Kevin’s best friend through the good times as well as the hard times and the sad times. “I'm fine bro.” Kevin replies in a monotone, yet relaxed voice. “You sure man? I mean I know today would mark two years since the accident and I know that this day is usually the hardest on you than any in the rest of the year. Also, we’ve got about a day before the huge match in Primetime Central to win with you leading Lights Out to the top. By tomorrow, you should be on top of the world, literally, and I just don’t want it to seem when the time comes that something’s keeping you down.” Kevin rolls his eyes in conjunction to Jay’s preaching. “Like I said, I'm fine. I just have a few things to work through.” Kevin pauses a second to soon hear a response from Jay. “Well man, I hope you know that if you ever need anything we're all here for you. You're a part of the best talent inside Lights Out and Primetime Central and the last thing that you want happen is to put not only yourself, but everyone else down who knows what you’re capable of.” ‘The New Revolution’ smirks. “I appreciate it and I’ll see you in a little while man. I’m about to head out to the gym for a few hours. “Ok Kev. I’ll see you at the gym. I’m going to see how ready you are. “Then I’ll see you there Jay. Later.” ‘The New Revolution hangs up the phone and walks into his bedroom. He flips on the television while he's getting together his bags. But he quickly gets caught up in the show that’s on… TV ::When we come back from commercial we continue with Past Stars of the PTC : Where are they now? We've looked at “The Demonic Warrior“ Tristan Belmont, Chris Storm and Callisto Bane. Our next feature will come up right after this commercial break.:: A commercial for pet food fills the screen. ‘The New Revolution freezes at the brand labeled IAMS except the bad reception from the television covers the “S”. Kevin quickly turns off the tube and slings the remote against the wall. “Are you trying to tell me something! I don’t understand. Please stop pulling me into a fear of absolute broken reality!” Then the phone rings. X picks it up and yells... “What?!” “That’s a hell of a way to greet your best friend.” “Damn Jay, it's you." “Well hello to you too.“ Kevin releases a sigh and replies to Jay’s smart ass comment. “Bro...that’s not what I meant. I…” Jay interferes. “I know, I know, it‘s all good Kev. I’m just calling to tell you that I won’t be able to make it to the gym, I apologize. The car’s all fucked up and I doubt I’ll make it more than ten feet without either A) burning to death after the thing sets on fire, or B) get shot to death by the neighbors who are in that gang because the piece of shit is louder than a fucking nuke landing in your ear drums. Kevin chuckles. “Yeah man, I’ve heard it. A lot of fucked up things seem to be happening to me all at the wrong time and I decided not to go either. It’ll be easier if I just take the time to sit and vibe rather than be in a cluttered environment at a time when I want to be alone. “That’s totally understandable. Why don't you tell me what's up? I know something is, something besides the fact of what today is." Kevin looks down ad takes a deep breath, hesitating what he should say or not. “I think I've made a mistake. This…this isn’t me. “I could have told you that.” Kevin suddenly replies. “Then why didn't you?” Jay remains calm. “Because you needed to figure it out on your own. You work better than way. I’m just leading you into the right direction right now in case anything happens come tomorrow.” Kevin begins to talk about it. Seeming to be slightly zoning out. “I hear her voice. She keeps saying the same thing to me... I AM. “Bro, she's a part of you. She always will be. But she's a part of you.…the real you. Not the depressed, lonely, and questioning façade that you seem to be hiding behind lately. You’ve been having nightmares, hearing things, and avoiding people. Snap out of that shit and get in the mindset again…the mindset to kill. I think I may be able to connect you to those words in a way of you leading Lights Out to a win at the IPPV." “I think I do too. Maybe she's telling me it's time. Time to win or something?” Jay replies… “Close but no. Look at it this way. You know who you are, who you want to be, what you want to accomplish, and how you will accomplish it right?" Kevin lifts his eyes up and seems to think. He quietly responds. “That pretty much sums it up yea.” “I know it does, now look at this. You are who you want to be and you aren’t what you need to be.” The New Revolution reveals a confused look. “I…I don’t understand.” “Kevin, it’s right within you. You are the talent that was bred to accept nothing even compared to “just a win.” You were hit, bruised, broken…you cried tears, shed blood, sliced skin…for one reason…and that is to prove yourself worthy. You are the best that they have seen Kevin…” Kevin questions Jay… “Am I…?” Jay responds in a quiet, yet intriguing voice… “You are…” Suddenly Kevin’s eyes grow large. “I AM” Kevin now understands where he must be within his state of mind to accomplish any of his dreams. He stares into space and drifts within his thoughts for a moment soon realizing that he was still talking to Jay. “Jay, you’re the man, but I’m going to have to let you go.” “I know…take care.” A click is heard on the other end. Jay had hung up. His job prior to the IPPV has been done. Kevin slowly lowers the phone onto the receiver and makes his way over to the couch. He lays down and after about thirty seconds of analyzing his ceiling, his eyes drift into sleep. [The scene fades out.]
