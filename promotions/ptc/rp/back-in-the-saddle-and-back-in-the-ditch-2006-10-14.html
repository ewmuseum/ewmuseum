Back in the Saddle, and Back in the Ditch

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 "The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, a pinnacle of sports-entertainment in North America in the past decade. Past winners have had their names pasted on billboards, and have gained fame and un-denied recognition in the wrestling world. Rich "The Renegade" Rollins succeeded in becoming a GTT winner last year…he’s looking to repeat this year. The champion of the fourth incarnation of the tournament was a lunatic of a doctor, hell-bent on global domination, and he is again back in action. So anyone, really, could win this thing, let alone win it twice. Which means there’s a small glimmer of hope, that one man…a man that hasn’t stepped foot in the squared circle for over a year, could possibly walk away as a champion. Then again…it all depends on how you get the show on the road…" THIS IS BULLSHIT! :: John stands up from his desk, outraged as he throws the latest Wrestling Digest down. The cubicle around him barely contains his 6’9" frame. The rage inside of him builds as he goes to leave the cubicle, but his shoulders get stuck in the walls of the opening. He tries wiggling out of position, though he’s unable to do much more than look like a fat man doing the Macarena. Hitchin finally moves himself out of place, looking to his left at the empty water cooler. His eyes widen, throwing his arms up in the air. :: Hitchin – "John smash!" :: John’s arms come down with such fury, such force. Anyone caught in his tracks is sure to meet certain doom. Today, however, the water cooler is at the top of its game. Hitchin’s fists come down, hitting the top of the bottle, and then bouncing to the sides of it. John closes his eyes, as he realizes that the strengths in which he once had have faded into nothing more than flabby arms, and a basic happy-face tattoo that has gained the droopiness of a Native American healer." Hitchin – "My god…what have I done…I can’t go back to this sport…" :: Hitchin has a momentary lapse of reason as he calms himself. Giving in to the vast defensive capabilities of the water cooler, John grabs a paper cone, and starts to fill it with the H20 goodness that the belly of the beast keeps at a refreshing temperature. The cold water invades the big man’s mouth, as only water from a paper cone could. He swishes the water around, as if there were a bad taste in his mouth. Behind John, a co-worker approaches with a smile on his face. The man swings a hand at Hitchin’s back, slapping him hard, starting a chain reaction that ends with John spitting the, now mouth-temperature, water in a female co-workers face. John’s face turns a million shades of red as he looks at the woman, and then turns to the man that slapped him on his back. :: Co-worker – "Hitch! You’re going back to your old job with a bang! The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament! This is going to be insane!" Hitchin – "You know about this?! This was supposed to be top secret, how many other people know about the GTT? Ten…eleven…maybe even a dozen people? No one was supposed to know about this tournament…" Co-worker – "John-boy…it’s all over the news. It’s all over everything that has the word wrestling in it." :: A smile comes over John’s face as he starts to relax his body and muscles, especially his tragically sagging, left man-breast. Not to be confused with his right man-breast, which is still just as perky as it was last year, let alone 3 or 4 hours ago. He claps his hands a few times in excitement, like a giddy little cheerleader. :: Hitchin – "Oh wee! What have they said about my return?! Tell me tell me!" Co-worker – "Oh…uh…they…they’ve said some good…some good things…" :: Hitchin’s smile disappears back to the look of disbelief that he previously had on his face. His nose twitches slightly, like it usually does when he smells something foul. In this case, it’s flatulence that escaped during his couple of seconds of excitement. :: Co-worker – "That…is disgusting." Hitchin – "I’ll tell you what’s disgusting, the fact that I’m fighting Christopher Lloyd in my opening match of the tournament!" :: The man talking to John starts to burst out laughing. John just stares at him, not even knowing what to think. Continuing to laugh in a maniacal way, the man almost keels over in front of Hitchin. :: Co-worker – "Oh man…I’m gonna wet myself…" Hitchin – "Come on, it’s not that funny!" :: The man starts to get a hold of his laughter. John crosses his arms, waiting for an explanation of why the man standing in front of him was laughing as hard as he was. :: Co-worker – "No…I’m not going to wet myself because you’re a moron…I’m going to wet myself because I have an uncontrollable bladder and urethra combination that just goes off whenever it feels it needs to." Hitchin – "Disgusting…" Co-worker – "It sure is, and that’s not even the worst part…" Hitchin – "Ugh…just get on with it!" Co-worker – "Right…so you honestly think you’re fighting the Back to the Future guy?" :: John moves past his co-worker, making his way back to his cubicle. Trying to enter it, he gets stuck in the door opening once again. John takes a deep breath as his co-worker watches him. Starting to slowly move his shoulders forward, Hitchin thinks that he’s finally figured out how to travel back and forth from his cubicle. He lunges his right leg forward…no forward progression. He then lunges is left leg forward…nothing. John smiles as he gets an idea…try both legs at once! So he does so, and then again, and again, and again. Each unsuccessful try builds anger within him, but with each try, another follows until he is finally able to get through. John grabs the book and turns around to see everyone in visual range staring at him. :: Hitchin – "What?" Co-worker – "That just totally looked like you were power-dry humping the air when you were doing your double-legged pelvic thrusts…" :: John’s face starts to go red with embarrassment as the entire office is now in on what has happened, and are beginning to laugh hysterically. Biting his upper lip, and looking into everyone’s eyes as he starts to panic, John makes a bold move. :: Hitchin – "I HATE ALL OF YOU AND I HOPE YOU ALL CONTRACT SOME FORM OF GENITAL WARTS!" :: And like magic, everyone in the office was back to work. Not a single person looking at John. He puts his hands on his hips and smiles as if he’s congratulating himself on a job well done. John looks down at the book in his hand and then looks up to talk to the man that he was conversing with not ninety seconds earlier, only to find that he was gone. :: Hitchin – "That’s odd…Ricky? Ricky where are you?" :: John calls out, but there is no response from anyone. He cups his hands around his mouth, making a fleshy megaphone. :: Hitchin – "TIMMY!? WHERE DID YOU GO?! I WAS JUST ABOUT TO SHOW YOU PICTURES OF A SWEATY CHRISTOPHER LLOYD IN MY WRESTLING MAGAZINE! TIMMY…TIMMY?! :: Hitchin clutches his fist in front of himself. Shaking it slowly, he begins to speak to himself. :: Hitchin – "If only I had taken the time to learn some names around here…" :: John shakes his head, and then puts his arm down as he turns around and stands face to face with his boss. Well, ok, maybe he’s standing his little area, under his breast but above his ribcage, to his bosses face. But you know how it goes. John looks down at his boss, and his eyes widen just a little as he can see his boss’s face. Hitchin tries to speak, but is stuttering his words quickly. Mr. Featherstein, a man that can only be described as the spitting image of Clint Howard, points his finger at John as his lid is about to blow off the top of his head. :: Featherstein – "Do you have ANY idea how disruptive you are?!" Hitchin – "I…uh…well…I…" Featherstein – "Well? Uh? What kinds of answers are those?! Do you have any idea how much money you’re not making me by standing away from your phone and your desk? Do you?!" Hitchin – "The same amount of money it cost you to buy that hair piece?" Featherstein – "WHAT?!" :: John takes a step back as his boss starts to shake uncontrollably. Like a Tickle-Me Elmo that just did a bunch of crack, Mr. Featherstein looks as if he’s about to explode, and get little angry man intestines all over the office. Continuing to watch the boss throw a hissy-fit, John raises the magazine up to his face, to hide his expression of seriousness that is quickly turning into laughter. :: Featherstein – "This is all me! This hair, this is all me! It might not be head hair…but it’s all mine!" Hitchin – "I’ve noticed that your…quote-unquote hair…is quite tiny and curly…" Featherstein – "That’s it! You’re nothing more than a lummox crammed into a suit! You big…dumb…slow moving anim…" Hitchin – "Wait!" :: The big man puts a hand in front of Mr. Featherstein, as if he’s not supposed to go there, girlfriend. :: Hitchin – "Before you continue, there is something that I have been waiting to say to you for a very long time…" :: John stares at Featherstein with the most stern of looks. As anger starts, to build within him again, John clinches his fist, and points his finger at his boss. :: Hitchin – "You listen, and you listen good…you are NOT the only man that likes Boston Cream donuts mister…do you hear me? Every single time Judy brings in donuts, you ALWAYS have to take the last Boston! There are two in the box and two only! One for me when they are fresh, so that I can taste the oven goodness. And one for me when I’m off of work and the donut gets a little stale from the oxygen that gives it a nice protective layer of chocolate over the top of the dough." :: As John finishes his fit, he storms off away from his boss. Featherstein stands in the same spot, amazed and not even bothering to watch the man that just flipped out on him, walk away. He wipes his forehead and then adjusts his tie as he looks around at the other employees. None of them are looking up at him, fearing that one snicker could cost them their jobs. Featherstein slowly makes his way back to his office, looking around; making sure no one is viewing him as the laughing stock of the company. Entering his office, he moves to his desk and sits down. :: RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIING! RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIING! :: Featherstein picks up the phone. :: Featherstein – "Hel…" Hitchin – "Oh yeah! I QUIT!" Meanwhile, in John’s Car. :: Driving down the highway at just a little over the speed limit, John has his music blaring over his speakers. People on the sidewalks are staring at him as he drives by, but he doesn’t care. He just quit his job, and he’s in his zone. :: Hitchin – "Come on Barbie, let’s go party…" :: John uses his steering wheel as a drum between his lines. :: Hitchin – "…come on Barbie, let’s go party…" :: Aqua’s "Barbie Girl" has completely taken over the car that John drives, and the mind that sits within him. He’s right into it, and there is absolutely no stopping him now. He starts singing at the top of his lungs as the chorus starts. :: Hitchin – "I’M A HITCHY GIRL! IN A HITCHY WOOOORLD! LIFE IS PLASTIC! SO FANTASTIC!" :: With his windows down, the sidewalk traffic’s opinions are subconsciously heard. :: Man – "Give it up, hermaphrodite!!!" Hitchin – "YOU CAN COMB MY HAIR!" :: John’s cell phone begins to ring as he’s singing along at the top of his lungs. :: Hitchin – "UNDRESS M…OH COME ON! YOU CAN’T BE SERIOUS!" :: John turns down the radio, and picks up the cell phone, answering it. John’s look goes from one of happiness, to one of discomfort and sadness. :: Hitchin – "…I’m on my way." :: John ends his call and then throws his cell to the ground of his car, breaking the fragile flip phone into two. His hands tense up around the steering wheel, knuckles turning white. There is something terribly wrong; for a man that usually has no doubts or worries in life, is now putting the pedal to the floor, in hopes that his effort will somehow help the situation. Hitchin’s mind races at a million miles a second as all thoughts of his wrestling return at the GTT have vanished, replaced by a more emotional and human side of John. The mind and the heart are now at odds. :: "You’re thoughts are going to kill John, you know that right? Something comes up, and right away anger follows. It can’t be like that! It can’t always be like that! We’ll deal with one thing at a time. First we’ll go see her, and then we’ve got a flight to catch. "What do you know?! She’s in trouble! Alanna is in trouble and we have to go save her! I don’t know how but we’ll do this somehow! Screw the GTT!" "You don’t really mean that, you’re just not thinking things through. You know her; you know Alanna wouldn’t want John worrying over her like this. She knows the man he is, and she knows what he can do. We will go and see her as soon as possible, and then it’s up to you to get your head on straight. We need to be able to come to a compromise, John can win this match. He can silence all the Dr. Curiosity supporters. "A compromise you say? Hmm…then a compromise you’ll get…" St. Michael General Hospital "WHERE IS SHE?! TELL ME RIGHT NOW! PLEASE!" :: The frightened nurse grabs her clipboard and holds it up over her face, scared for her life. The huge fiery-haired man continues to scream and shout as security guards are closing in behind him. :: Hitchin – "PLEASE! YOU HAVE TO TELL ME WHERE I CAN FIND ALANNA ROSE!" Officer – "Calm down! Sir, calm down right now!" :: John spins around in a fit of anger, calming quickly after he realizes who the two men that are approaching him are. He corrects his stands, and begins to slow his heartbeat. :: Hitchin – "I’m not going to hurt anyone…and I don’t want to cause trouble…please…just tell me where I can find Alanna…" :: The nurse behind the desk gets on the phone, and engages in a conversation with someone on the other end. Throughout the conversation the nurse keeps nodding her head, and then she hangs up the phone. Looking at John, she explains to him where he can find the woman that he’s looking for. He thanks her for the information and starts to run through the halls to his destination. Into the emergency ward he goes, bed number seventeen. Laying in the bed, a woman in her early twenties. Her eyes closed, and her skin a distinct yellow color. John covers his mouth, astonished by the sight that begins to sink into his mind. :: Hitchin – "Oh my god…Alanna…" :: Alanna coughs softly as John’s words leave his mouth. Her eyes slowly open, and a smile invades her tired face. :: Alanna – "You…you came…" Hitchin – "Of course I did. I told you that I will always be there for you…and I meant it." Alanna – "How long have we known each other, John? How…how did you find me…how did we connect like this…I want you tell me…I…I need to remember…" :: John swallows hard, as he gathers his thoughts, and blinks away the building tears in his eyes. :: Hitchin – "I was on top of the world…I was a World Champion in a wrestling federation, making millions a year…and you were a young girl, on the streets. I found you on the steps in front of my house, junked out on cocaine. I cleaned you up…and took you in…" Alanna – "I loved you for it…and I still love you…" Hitchin – "Then you ruined my life, took all my money and ran. For what…for more drugs…more booze…what? What was some important, that you had to crush everything that we had for it? I lost my company, I lost life, and worst of all…I lost my heart." Alanna – "There’s no need for this…you know why I had to leave…" Hitchin – "That is NO excuse! NO EXCUSE! Why did you call me here…you knew this would happen…" Alanna – "John, I told the doctor to call you…my liver is failing me…and I’m dying…" Hitchin – "No…no you’re not…" Alanna – "I’ve known this for a long time…I didn’t tell you. I didn’t want to worry you. It’s something that I’ve learned to come to terms with, and it’s something that you shouldn’t worry yourself about. I just needed to see you one last time. I see you’re making a return to wrestling…" Hitchin – "I’m cancelling that plan…not with you like this…" :: Alanna puts her hand over onto John’s. Muscles so weak, John’s got to be the one to hold on to her hand. He can feel this, and knows that she doesn’t have much longer. But the fire is still in her eyes, and the passion is still in her spirit. :: Alanna – "Don’t be stupid. There’s nothing you…there’s nothing you can do for me. It’s too late. You have to do this…you were meant to do this. You can beat Doctor Curiosity…I know you can. It’s in you." Hitchin – "You might be on to something…if I can beat Curiosity…then…" :: John puts a hand on his chin, as he does some hard thinking. Nodding his head he smiles, and a plan has formed. Alanna squints her eyes, trying to read him. :: Hitchin – "If I can beat him…then…I can go to his house, take his silver DeLorian, and travel back in time, so that we can stop this entire thing from happening! YES! YOU ARE A GENIUS, MICHAEL J. FOX!" Alanna – "John…wh…what are you talking about?" Hitchin – "Back to the Future! I’m fighting Christopher Lloyd! This will be perfect, and you’ll be OK! I knew this would work out for everyone! You sit tight, and I’ll be back in one week. I’ve a plane to catch tomorrow night, and then a match to win!" :: John leans forward, excitingly he kisses Alanna on the forehead, and then runs off. She watches him as he exits her sight. A smile still on her face as tears begin to flow from her eyes. This will be the last time she ever sees him face to face, but she knows that she’ll always be right there behind him. John gets outside of the hospital, and runs to his car. He opens the door and gets in, putting the key in the ignition. He stops for a moment…as his final minutes with Alanna were plagued with denial. Beginning to cry, he starts the car, and listens to the radio. :: Who's gonna tell you when it's too late? Who's gonna tell you things aren't so great? You can't go on, thinking nothing's wrong Who's gonna drive you home tonight? Who's gonna pick you up when you fall? Who's gonna hang it up when you call? Who's gonna pay attention to your dreams? Who's gonna plug their ears when you scream? "This song…of course. It’s always this song when something goes wrong…I have to get to the airport…my flight for Hong Kong leaves in 2 hours. I don’t know what I’m doing…I’m throwing myself to the lions by putting my name into the GTT. The biggest underdog in the bracket…the biggest long shot…has just gotten that much bigger. I can’t do this…knowing that she’s here…fading away. From me…from life…" You can't go on, thinking nothing's wrong Who's gonna drive you home tonight? Who's gonna hold you down when you shake? "But you believe in me. You give me the strength to do things that only people can dream about. Doctor Curiosity has never lost in PTC competition, but it can be done. He can be defeated. And so it will. I’m going in there with a head full of steam, and fists of fury. I might not win this entire tournament, and I may never be the same after this first match. But John Hitchin is going to the second round of GTT6…I’ve waited for this moment all my life, and I am not going to let it go. Alanna Rose you give me strength…and you will give me the strength to win this. In return, my love…and my tournament run. This one’s for you, darling…Hong Kong…here I come…" Lyrics used in this RP are from "Drive" by The Cars.
