One chance at life

8 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-02-08 Most people merely get one chance at life, one chance to make everything work the way you want it to but aware that whatever decisions you made you’d be trapped with forever. Salaok on the other hand felt like he was in his third chance at this life. The first chance at his life had been one riddled with ups and downs as he lived his life the only way he had known how which resulted in his wife and son being murdered less than 6 months apart and then himself being shot. Being shot had left him with no memory of the past and thus his second chance at life had begun which would see him not worry about anything in the world. There was no pressure of living up to his name because no one, including himself really knew what that was. It had been a peaceful life but he would soon be found and offered the chance to remember the past. The offer would then begin the third chance at life for Salaok, as a slow and painful trip down memory lane would prove to be ripping the world that he knew completely apart. No longer was there the ability to do and be whatever he wanted, for now there was a background, a past and a legacy in which to live up to. This third chance at life was proving not only to be the worst one yet, but the most lonely. And while these chances at life seemed like separate chances with new beginnings and a fresh start each time, it was all bound together by one simple thing. Choice. It had been Salaok who had chosen to live his life the way he had during those first years and in turn would be resulting in the gunshot. It had been Salaok who had chosen to live his life without finding out anything, regarding his past once he had woken up from the coma. It had been Salaok who had chosen to allow the then mysterious person from his past lead him down memory lane and thus show him the death and destruction his mere existence had caused. But now, the choice in which he had made was to stop and let the world go by for a few hours while the thoughts of his up coming match with Tsonda entered his mind. The week before, Tsonda had beaten Salaok, which even Salaok admits isn’t that hard to do these days as not only is he out of practice, but out of shape due to the time he had away from his current life. While living without a memory for those two years, there had been no reason to work out at the gym every day or practice his wrestling abilities as he didn’t know that he was or had been a wrestler. So the body mass had reduced, well the muscle had anyways, while the flab had grown a little bit but that was quickly coming off with all of the work for GTT5 he was having to be doing. Most people had turned up to the tournament with an entourage of lackeys with them making sure that every spare moment during the tournament was spent wisely. The trainers, the physios, the managers and public relations crew and of course the training partner who is basically brought along for someone the main person can beat up on in the training ring whenever he felt the need. Salaok had brought none of that with him, however, when people began to find that out, it wasn’t long until the managers and trainers of those who hadn’t made it as far had jumped on his bandwagon, doing all they could to hopefully be on the winning team. Looking at his watch for a brief moment, he knew he had other places to be, mainly the training gym but for now he just wanted a moment in time on his own. On his own with the millions of people below. The sun had been up for a number of hours now and the streets had life breathed into them by the morning rush hour, as the mindless move off to work following their day to day routine in attempts to make money when all they do is make money for those they work for. Sure, they are paid their wages and told that is how much they are worth to the world because of that. A wage or salary is exactly that, the amount in which the world thinks you are worth for the day and no matter how good you are at your job, there is always going to be someone who the world deems as being more valuable than you are. It was a thought that had entered Salaok head as he thought back to his life without memory of the past as he had needed money and worked as a bouncer out the front of a nightclub. During that time he had done the exact thing which he vowed never to do again after he managed to come into the large sums of money which now rule his world. For some reason, while he pitied those who were travelling off to work today, just like that had done the day before and the day before that, there were some people down there on the streets below who he not only didn’t pity, but envied in a way. It was those who didn’t believe in being told that they are late to work, that they are worth only this much per hour because the world thinks so. Salaok seemed almost to envy those who didn’t care and went about their lives making their own way and battling for everything that they have for the simple right of survival. The homeless The so called bums The fighters. A long time ago he had heard these kind of people not only referred to, but calling themselves the Tragic Kingdom. The more and more this concept went through his mind, the more it seemed so fitting, so real and perfect for their kind. For the world views these people as lesser beings and looked down their noses which are held high by money, only to describe them and their situation as being Tragic. And yet while they say it is tragic, they don’t do a thing to aim these tragic ones, they simply look straight ahead and ignore their existence all together at times. And yet it is while the world views these people as being tragic, those who live on the streets and fight for their life every day that they wake up call it a Kingdom. Because it is just that, their kingdom that can not be taken away from them and that they rule with a lot more control than what the Royal Family controls theirs. The streets have their own rules, their own codes that they live by which makes walking the streets a task that no one should take the risk on their own after dark because you belong to them then. His vantage point from atop of a building allowed him to watch and yet draw no attention from himself at all as he sits on the ledge of the building like so many had done before him, the only difference was he had no intentions of jumping off. Seeing the world of The Tragic Kingdom as he remembers it being called made him wish that the choice he had made towards the end of last year, hadn’t been made. There are some things in life that once done, can never be taken back or made right again and this was most certainly one of those situations. The choice he had made was to allow himself to be shown information from his past that had been wiped from memory thanks to a gun shot from two and a bit years prior. In taking on that information, the life that he had become used to didn’t just slip away, but was wrenched from his grasp completely as he was taken from The Tragic Kingdom, into his own person kingdom that was dominated by it’s own hell. Each turn he had taken since beginning this trip into his past had been one that had hurt more than the last and each one was a choice in which he had made. The next choice however, he knew was going to hurt but there was someone in which he just had to know and that was the truth about his son’s death. While he knew who it had been that had shot Lisha, he had no clue as to who it was that had done the horrendous crime in bring the life of a six year old boy to an end in cold blood. While it was painful, he knew that the memory had to be fresh in his mind if he was to pick up anything that could give him some sort of hint as to who had been in the room before Salaok had gotten there. The door stands in front of him, wooden like all the other doors in the mansion, but this one felt colder, haunted if you will. Time after time he stopped himself from taking a hold of the door handle and turning it, each time worried that he once again wouldn’t be able to control himself from the images that would flood back into his mind. Yet, it was those images he needed to see, needing to relive in order to understand. As his fingers wrapped around the gold door-nob, the shivers from with strike through his body but he knows this is the way it has to be and so turns the nob and listens as the door unlatches. He pushes the door open and lets go of the door nob, letting it swing open and revealing the memories and visions all over again. Salaok’s face distorts as he closes his eyes tight, trying to control what he sees as it incinerates his entire being. The flames of hell dance wildly through his blood stream, doing their best to raise the temperature of his body and breaking him out into a sweat as he sees, or at least remembers his son laying there. At first the image was of young Darrell laying there seemingly unhurt but it quickly flashes to the image of the young six year old boy with his neck slit from one side to the other and the maggots eating him from within. Through his memories, Salaok tries his hardest to focus on everything he saw that fateful day he found his son murdered but his mind eyes kept going back to the boy himself. Salaok wanted nothing more than to pick up young Darrell and tell him everything was going to be okay but there was no way of that ever happening, again. The window. While everything in his memory was a still picture, there was movement out of the window which caught Salaok’s attention and then a look of knowing. For out the window on a tree, not too far from the house sat a crow with a gold ring around its foot, which Salaok knew as a family pet. The family pet that had belonged to his older brother Johnathon for as long as he could remember and was the one hint in which Salaok had wanted to find. With his blood near boiling and the images of Darrell haunting every thought that roamed around Salaok’s mind, he had no choice but to shut the door and lock the images away once again. Standing in the foyer of a house simply too large to explain, Salaok stood atop the plush red carpet which led towards the giant split stairwell. Surrounded by dark wood everywhere and polished floorboards, which the red carpet sat upon, Salaok, knew this place was for the reason of darkness and wealth in which it had created. His bother Johnathon wasn’t the most legit man in the world, in fact he was far from it which is why it never surprised Salaok to find something new and extremely expensive in the building. Most people would simply have painting of their loved ones made to hang on the walls, but in Johnathon’s case, he had paid large sums of money to have life sized statues created of those who played a significant part in his story as he so called it. For Johnathon’s existence to him wasn’t a ‘life’ as most normal people would call it, no, for him it was a story. His reasoning’s were simple as with a good story, it doesn’t end after the book is closed, there is always a chance for the follow up novel or the last page simply leaves the ending open for you to think as to what would happen in the future. And seeing as how Johnathon honestly believed that he couldn’t die, then his existence was indeed a good novel. Staring at the statue of himself standing side by side with Johnathon as the brother had often done in the past, now it is for reasons of hate that Salaok stands close to him. The crow that he had seen in the memories of Darrell’s death scene had revealed the link to the person Salaok was needing and that was his older brother Johnathon. Johnathon had also been a wrestler, much like Salaok and for as long as he had been in the business, he had taken the nickname of Scarecrow. Over the years Johnathon bought a pet crow which he trained and he would treat it much like a normal person would a cat or dog. It was that very crow Salaok had seen in his vision. "Johnathon!" Salaok yells but with no response other than his voice echoing around the massive building and then silence. "I know you are here brother and I know what you have done." "You know what I’ve done do you brother? And in what would it be regarding?" Johnathon’s voice sounds out, seemingly from the top of the stairs in a calm and controlling manner. "In regards to Darrell, my son!" Salaok’s tone aggravated as he turns to look at the top of the stairs, in hope of seeing where his brother is exactly as his hand moves around to the back of his pants. "Ahhh yes, my little late nephew. And do tell dear brother, what is it you think I have done?" Johnathon’s voice this time not only not at the top of the stairs, but no where near them as it sounds out from almost behind Salaok. Salaok spins, trying to see his brother but again there is no imagery from the area the voice sounds from. "You killed him Johnathon, you killed my only son." While his voice is filled with hate, his throat chokes up slightly in the realisation of what he has just said out loud. The thought of family killing family wasn’t something that sat well with Salaok and yet wasn’t the first time it had occurred. "My gosh little brother, such a horrid thing to have said. I could never lay a hand on my family. You disappoint me for even suggesting such a thing." Again, the voice has moved and this time sounds out from some distance although directly beside him. "Then why was your crow there you murderer?" Emotion was quickly beginning to take control of Salaok which was the exact opposite of what was sounding out of Johnathon’s mouth as he was always in control. "My crow is a free pet Salaok, it may fly where ever it wants and because it has been to your house a number of times, it wouldn’t surprise me that it would be able to fly there without my aid. But please Salaok, think this through calming now and stop with the name calling." Johnathon’s voice this time sounding from right in front of Salaok, which again causes Salaok’s hand to reach behind his back, grabbing for his gun. A gun that wasn’t there. "You looking for this dear brother?" Johnathon’s devilish whisper into Salaok’s ear from right behind him causes Salaok to startle and then the realisation as the barrel of the gun presses against the back of Salaok’s skull through his long black hair. "I wasn’t the one who killed Darrell you idiot." The feeling of the gun in the back of Salaok’s head stops as Johnathon pulls the gun away and Salaok spins around on his heel to face his menace of a brother. Nothing. Johnathon is no where to be seen. Shaking his head in disbelief at the whole situation, Salaok reaches for his gun once more thinking that it may have just my an illusion, a mind game but the gun is no where to be found. Sitting in what can only be described as a throne room, Johnathon sits at an old style, dark wood table in a suit and tie. His long nearly white hair falling down over his shoulders while his hands rest together under his chin in a entwined fist. The gun his had taken from Salaok sits in front of him but his gaze looks off into the distance out a window, watching his brother walk off, a defeated man. "While I may not have killed your son Salaok, I was the one who placed the order for it. Most people only get once chance at life young brother, but through what has happened, I am giving you another chance to be yourself."
