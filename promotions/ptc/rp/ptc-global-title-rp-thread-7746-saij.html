19 July 2004 The Plot Thickens! (Buwahahahaha): Prologue & Part I - Champion of Globes There he sits, inside an empty locker room. The PTC Global Champion. No, this isn't the same locker room that hosts those wacky and lovable Boomer Sooners, the stable of which Saij is a proud member. There is no jovial atmosphere. There are no shenanigans, nor is there any tomfoolery afoot. No Funyuns, or BBQ, or bottles of Mountain Dew: Code Red. No video games, no laughter, none of JR's cries of "BY GOD, STOP PUMMELING OL' JR WITH THE INFLATABLE PENIS, COCKJOCKEY!" None of that. This room is utterly devoid of anything remotely Sooner-esque, except for Saij himself, sitting on the long oak bench running parallel to the lockers along the left wall. Were there not twenty thousand screaming fans in the arena at present, the only noise one would be able to hear is the soft hum of the flourescent lights in the ceiling. As it is, the excited buzzing of the crowd has trickled down to even this tucked-away nook of the building. Even though he's dressed in his ring gear, Saij certainly doesn't look ready to fight. Something seems to be weighing heavily on his mind as he sits, slouched forward. His elbows, clad in the usual dark green pads, rest on his knees. He stares blankly at a cell phone in his hands, as if waiting for someone to call -- or, perhaps, deciding to call someone himself. Saij slowly turns his head to the left. Resting beside him is the PTC Global Title belt, which sports a recently-changed nameplate -- something that has become a monthly ritual as of late. The rest of the belt is in good shape overall, having apparently been freshly polished. Saij has taken quite good care of it... although... at second glance, something seems to be different about the Global Title belt than when it was last seen with Hessian. It's almost as if someone changed the engraved writing on the faceplate... The champion looks away from his belt, and back to his phone. He lets out a long sigh, and drops his head completely, now staring at the floor. What should I do? he wonders to himself. His reverie is interrupted by a knock at the door, and a gruff male voice coming from the other side. "Hey! Get yer ass in gear, champ! Match starts in fifteen minutes! Don't keep our fans waitin'!" Saij lifts his head slightly with an indignant scowl on his face. Great. Some backstage flunkie from a fed he didn't even work for, ordering him around when he's not in the best of moods. He thinks for a moment about getting up, walking over to the door, throwing it open, and bashing that grubby little bastard's face in. But then he remembers his responsibility. He's representing XUW, and he's obligated to do his part in maintaining healthy inter-fed relations. Plus, Saij is generally a nice guy, and it's not in him to act upon the desire to hurt someone who really couldn't fight back. "Yeah, I'll be ready in a few," he mumbles, surely not even loud enough for the guy to hear. Not that he really cares, anyway. The only thing of importance to Saij at the moment is his first Global Title defense against Aidan DelaCroix and AWS Man (also known as Bill), scheduled to begin quite shortly. Being the first guy to successfully defend the Global Title in a long time -- that's the only thing that matters. The only thing that matters. Saij wished that was the case, anyway. But something else is bothering him. Something else is gnawing away at him, as he continues staring at that phone. Staring at that phone. But what for? What in the world is so important about that phone, that it's keeping the PTC Global Champion distracted from his title defense, just minutes before it's set to happen? Oh, where to begin... The scene changes to the locker room of the Boomer Sooners, not too long ago. The place is messier than usual -- which is saying something, as it's usually in a slovenly state anyway. It appears to be the morning after a party. Balloons and streamers hang haphazardly from the ceiling, not to mention a large banner that reads BOOMER SOONERS - 2004 STABLE WARS CHAMPIONS. Empty Funyun bags and Code Red bottles are scattered everywhere in big piles, as well as empty bottles of a special customized Boomer Sooner treat: Mike's Hard BBQ. There is a particularly large pile of such refuse on the pale green couch in the center of the room. Seated in a recliner just to the right of the couch, still wearing a pair of pajamas, is Dave Kipfer (who, to give you the short version of events, was Saij's best friend until about a year ago when he turned evil and feuded with him over the XUW World Title, only to get his ass kicked terribly. Soon thereafter he was carted off to the loony bin, not to be seen again until his recent return to XUW, and now he no longer hates Saij -- there, that's all you need to know). Dave is sporting a few bruises and bandages on his forehead, injuries left over from the previous night's pay-per-view spectacular at which the Sooners finally became Stable Wars champions (hence the party). He is quietly reading a copy of the morning newspaper, enjoying the peace of having the locker room to himself. Or is he by himself? CLINK! Dave jumps out of his seat a little bit at the sudden sound of an empty BBQ bottle falling to the floor. He looks behind the couch and sees the culprit, rolling slowly along the floor before coming to a stop. It must have just fallen from the big pile on the couch, he realizes. Dave shakes his head and calms down before going back to reading his paper. Suddenly, the entire pile of garbage begins to shift! Dave nearly jumps out of his own skin this time... Someone is emerging from underneath the rubbish, like a zombie rising from its grave! A hand shoots up, and grabs onto the top of the couch. Another hand shoots up, and reaches back, holding into the couch's arm. The two hands push, and the torso of a man slowly sits up, causing bags and bottles to fall everywhere on the floor, compounding the mess. "Uuuuugh," he groans, smacking his lips. "Oh fark, it feels like someone's firing a cannon inside my head..." It's Saij. The Master of the Huzzah seems to have consumed a bit too much Mike's Hard BBQ from the night before. His short brown hair is mussed, and he doesn't appear to have shaved in at least twenty-four hours. His eyes are bloodshot, and his face is pale. Making as little movement as possible, Saij slowly gazes around the room with squinted eyes. He spots Dave, who returns his glance and shakes his head with a small laugh. For those who are unfamiliar with the Boomer Sooners locker room, the place has been outfitted with a kitchenette, a small dining area, two leather reclining chairs (in addition to the worn-out couch), and a big screen television with several different video game hook-ups. Granted, this is all rather silly, since the Sooners travel to different arenas every week. But then again, the Sooners use logic about as often as they use tampons. His next match could always be his last if he wasn't careful -- and he normally wasn't. He couldn't bear the responsibility of dragging another person into his life if something terrible happened to him. It wouldn't be fair. Saij couldn't tell this to Leanne, though -- he had to lie about his injuries in order to be allowed to wrestle again. He couldn't tell anyone. It would mean the end of his career. (Buwahahahaha): Part II - The First Date It is seven o' clock in the evening on a Friday night, in front of Antonio's Restaurant. Leanne is standing just outside, on a red carpet underneath a maroon awning bearing the restaurant's name in gold cursive. She is wearing a stunning dress -- strapless and deep sky blue, running down to her knees in length. She clutches her matching-colored purse in both of her hands, and looks around restlessly as she shifts her weight in her white high heels. "Where could he be?" she asks herself. "They won't hold onto our reservation for very long." As if on cue, a yellow taxi cab turns around the corner about a block down the street. It speeds toward the restaurant, and comes to an abrupt stop at the curb. Leanne's eyes go wide. She jumps back, as the vehicle kicks up a puddle of water and nearly douses her completely. Fortunately she gets away bone dry, but she's still slightly perturbed at what could have happened. The back door swings open, and out steps Saij. Now, what Saij didn't realize about Antonio's, where Leanne suggested they go for their dinner date, is that it's one of the fanciest restaurants in town. Renowned for its romantic atmosphere, elegant decor, and exquisite cuisine, Antonio's is a dining establishment reserved exclusively for society's upper crust -- or at least, for people with hundreds of dollars to burn. As such, patrons of Antonio's are expected to wear their finest suits, or their fanciest gowns. But this is Saij we're talking about. He steps out of the cab, and Leanne looks rather aghast. Saij is dressed in his everyday street clothes -- a worn pair of blue jeans, white sneakers, and one of his black t-shirts that carry different Saij-related quotes (tonight's selection is a new one that reads, in white letters, Farkness > Eternal Damnation -- a hot seller since he won the Global Title from Hessian).
