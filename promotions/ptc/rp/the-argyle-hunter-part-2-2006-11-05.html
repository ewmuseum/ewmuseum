The Argyle Hunter, Part 2

5 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-05 When we last saw Plucky the Cockfighting Duck, he was on a dinner plate being served to a passenger onboard a flight headed to Australia. That passenger… none other than legendary actor Samuel L Jackson. Samuel L Jackson noticed the pile of bones surrounding the little white duck. "Man… that duck ate all my chicken. That’s some fucked up shit right there. Practically cannibalism." The lovely stewardess was both confused and embarrassed. "We’re so sorry, Mr. Jackson. We’ll get you another meal." "Bet your sweat ass you-" "DID SOMEONE SAY ‘JACKSON?’" Actor, stewardess, and waterfowl all looked over at the sound of the outburst. Standing in the aisle was a nerdy looking man in the crappiest Superman knockoff costume ever seen. Instead of a big red S he had "JACK" written in block letters. Samuel L Jackson was not impressed. "And who the hell is this cracker? The costumed man laughed evilly. "Nyahahaha!!! I am… THE JACKMASTER!!!" "Who?" asked everyone in the cabin except for Plucky. "Quack?" asked Plucky. "The Jackmaster!" exclaimed the Jackmaster. "Master of all things named ‘Jack.’ My villainous career began when my parents named me jack as a child! Such a common name I knew I had to rise above all other Jacks to get the world’s notice. So I began committing Jack crimes! I rob Jack-in-the-Box restaurants! I cheat at blackjack! I steal Jackie Collins books from libraries! I illegally download Joe Jackson songs!" "Quack!" "Yes, "Is She Really Going Out With Him" is a damn good tune! I get drunk in public on Jack and Coke! I swipe the prizes out of boxes of Crackerjacks! I jack off into coffee pots that lots of people use!" At that moment, over half the cabin runs to the bathrooms. The Jackmaster grinned fiendishly before going on with his tirade. "And now I’m hijacking a plane! I command this flight go to Melbourne!" The stewardess looked confused still. "But we’re going to Sydney. You could’ve just taken a connecting flight." "But then it wouldn’t be a hijacking, bimbo!" barked the Jackmaster. And once we’re there, I will track down Australian actor Hugh Jackman and… give him a wedgie! It will be my greatest Jack crime ever!" The Jackmaster turned his gaze to Samuel L Jackson. "Or it will be… after giving YOU a wedgie!" "Aw, hell naw!" protested Samuel L Jackson. "Then face the wrath of the Jackmaster!" The Jackmaster reached into his utility belt and took out a special deck of playing cards… all jacks. He began throwing them at Samuel L Jackson. "Take that! And that!" Samuel L Jackson looked annoyed. "Dammit, you crazy cracker! I’m gonna be pissed if you give me a paper cut!" Plucky knew it was up to him to save Samuel L Jackson from a certain paper cutting, which could seriously hinder his film career. So Plucky flapped up to the over compartment and opened it with his beak. Out came dozens of delicious Hostess Twinkies, Cup Cakes, and Fruit Pies! The Jackmaster looked at the goodies with glee! "I love the chocolaty goodness of Hostess Cup Cakes! And the creamy center of Hostess Twinkies!" "Yeah, and I love kicking cracker ass!" Samuel L Jackson took advantage of the distracted Jackmaster to get out of his seat and kick the costumed criminal in the nutsack. After a punch to the jaw, Samuel L Jackson shove the Jackmaster’s head behind the seat and slam the tray on it repeatedly. "Take!" WHAM! "That!" WHAM! "Crazy" WHAM! "Ass!" WHAM! "Cracker!" WHAM! A small boy looked on with eyes wide. "Mommy! Mace Windu is making that bad man’s ear’s bleed!" "Don’t stare at famous people, junior. They get cranky." The little boy wasn’t the only one impressed. The stewardess looked at Samuel L Jackson with fluttering eyes. And he knew what that meant. "How would you like some chocolaty goodness, sweet thing? And I guarantee some creamy center too!" "Oh, Mr. Jackson!" As the actor and the stewardess headed off somewhere for some mile high fun and games, Plucky quietly swiped some delicious Hostess treats for Pajaro de Presa and himself. Hours later, when the baggage handlers wear unloading the plane in Australia, they discovered an open suitcase. All around it were white feathers, empty Hostess wrappers, and women’s undergarments soiled in such a way that a hazmat team was called in. The duck and his luchador were nowhere to be found. Instead, the pair was already in the outback, searching for argyles. Hours were spent under the hot sun until finally, a revelation came to Pajaro. "Look, Plucky! A kangaroo!" Sure enough, there was a kangaroo nearby. "Quack." "I know kangas aren’t as cool as tiggers. But maybe it can tell us where to find argyles! Kanga in the movie was so nice!" So Pajaro crept up quietly to the animal until he was close enough to touch it. "Hola, amigo! We’re looking for argyles! We heard you can find them in Australia!" The kangaroo looked at the luchador and growled. "Hmmm… if you don’t know, you can just say no. Don’t need to be grumpy. I’ll just ask Roo." Pajaro looked down and sees no baby kangaroo. "Where’s Roo? I don’t even see your pouch!" Pajaro decided to search for Roo by feeling along the kanga’s belly. "Not there. Nope. Maybe lower. Hey! Here’s something!" As Pajaro’s hand wraeds around what he found, his eyes grew huge behind his bird mask. "Ooops. That’s not Roo." The beast growled again. "And you’re not a kanga! You’re a kang-o!" The kangaroo dropkicked the horrified luchador. After he fell, it began jumping up and down on him! "Ow! Ow! Ow! Plucky! Get help! Ow! Ow!" Plucky waddled off to find aid while the kangaroo pummeled his pal. After several minutes, Pajaro is battered and bruised, but the jumping has stopped. "Thank you, Jesus!" Just then, he heard the sound of cloth tearing. "What?" And then his ass felt the worst pain he had ever felt in his entire life. The kangaroo was bouncing on Pajaro in a whole different way, and it was enjoying it far more than he was. "No means no! No means no! You could’ve bought me a drink first! Madre dios! Could this get any worse?" At that moment, Plucky pulled ups in a white jeep with a pink bunny logo. The driver was a dapper older man in a smoking jacket. Plucky shared the backseat with a trio of blondes with pneumatic breasts. The driver honked the horn loudly, which sent the kangaroo hopping off to the bush. Pajaro looked at his saviors with a mix of gratitude and awe. "It’s Hugh Hefner! And the Girls Next Door!" Sure enough, it was. But they didn’t seem as pleased to see Pajaro. "Did you just get assraped by a kangaroo?" asked Bridget. "Oh my God, he did! Just look at him!" exclaimed Holly. "Ewwwww! That’s soooooooo gross!" screeched Kendra. Hugh Hefner simply looked at Pajaro with great disappointment. "I have to agree with my girls," said the Playboy publisher. "I’ve seen a lot of naked women, but you must be the biggest pussy I’ve ever come across, sir. I forbid you from ever reading my magazine again." "That doesn’t sound too bad," replied Pajaro. "Or looking at the pictures." "Nooooooo!" Plucky flapped his way out of the jeep and waddled to his violated buddy. The Girls Next Door waved to the little duck. "Bye, Plucky!" said Bridget. "You can visit us at the mansion anytime!" piped Holly. "Bring a friend… except him," chimed Kendra. Hugh Hefner honked the jeep’s horn again and then they drove off. Plucky wrapped a comforting wing around Pajaro, who simply curled up in a fetal position and shivered. So concerned with his friend, Plucky didn’t notice the group of large men approaching them until in was too late. A bag was thrown over him and he was pummeled to unconsciousness. When he woke up, Plucky was tied to a chair in what appeared to be an aquarium of some sort. Pajaro was next to him, also tied to a chair. It didn’t seem like he was too comfortable with sitting. All around them were burly, grim looking men. None of them said a word. The whole room was very dark. Suddenly, a voice came out of what sounded like a speaker, unseen in the darkness "What are you doing here?" "Quack, quack." "Looking for argyles in Australia? You expect me to believe that! You were sent here to stop my evil schemes, Plucky the Cockfighting Duck!" "Quack?" "Don’t play dumb, duck! I know your friend is an idiot, but not you!" "Hey!" protested Pajaro. "Shut up!" snapped the electronic voice. "You know how close I am to fulfilling my dream of ruining all things Australian! Ever since I lost my chance to star in the film version of "Grease!" to that Olivia Newton whore, I swore to bring down the entire continent! And I have come so close! I convinced the studio that a Crocodile Dundee sequel would work! I said to give Yahoo Serious a chance! I told Michael Hutchence to try auto-erotic asphyxiation! I convinced Portia de Rossi to date Ellen DeGeneres, thereby ruining any appeal she had to straight men! I got Mel Gibson drunk and read parts of ‘Mein Kampf’ to him! Me! Me! Me!" "Quack?" "Who am I? Who am I? I tell you, you damned duck!" The lights came on and there in the tank was the most scary form of sea life ever. It flpped with menace and swung its evil tail. "I am the Stingray That Killed Steve Irwin! Nyahahahaha!" "Quack, quack." "What do you mean it’s still too soon for Steve Irwin jokes?" snapped the Stingray That Killed Steve Irwin. You’re not the boss of me! Men, beat them to a bloody pulp!" Just then, the door to the aquarium burst open and there stood several gaunt looking men in old suits. The Stingray That Killed Steve Irwin flapped angrily. "It’s Nick Cave, Agent of SHIELD and his Howling Bad Seeds!" Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds quickly attacked the goons. The simple thugs were no match for Australia’s best songwriter and his band. The Stingray That Killed Steve Irwin knew the gig was up, so it tried swimming from his tank through a secret hatch and out to the ocean. "He’s getting away!" exclaimed Pajaro. But Plucky had slipped out of his bonds. He flapped to the top of the tank and dove right in. "No!" yelled Pajaro. Nick Cave freed him from his bonds and the luchador rushed to the tank. Plucky was nowhere to be found. "Plucky…" sobbed Pajaro. Nick Cave put a hand on his shoulder. "He did it for all of us down under, mate," said Nick Cave. "That duck is a true hero to Australia, just like… I don’t know. I’ve done a lot of heroin, memory’s shot to hell." Suddenly, a white form burst from the water and landed on the floor! "Plucky!" exclaimed the joyful luchador. Plucky shook his feathers off, then coughed a few times. "BURP!" belched Plucky. Something fell out of his mouth. Everyone looked down and saw it was a tail… a tail from a stingray. "Plucky, you ate the Stingray That Killed Steve Irwin?" asked Pajaro. "Quack, quack." "Plucky the Cockfighting Duck," rumbled Nick Cave. You have done a great service for my country. As the Coolest Person in Australia, I grant you a lifetime supply of argyle socks." "Quack, quack!" "Now I’m afraid we have to go. We don’t want to be seen with some guy that got assraped by a kangaroo." And so our heroic team of bird and birdbrain returned home, knowing that they would always have argyle socks. Plucky looked forward to his battle with Saij while Pajaro de Presa looked forward to Preperation H. And somewhere, high above the ocean, Samuel L Jackson was banging a stewardess in an airplane bathroom. "SAY MY NAME! SAY MY NAME!" [detail withheld]
