I'll Make You See

16 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“You have already lost Michael Biddle, soon your world will begin to crumble all around you and you won’t be able to do anything but watch.” Mao prophesies to Mike with his eyes still closed. Mao’s “prophecy” hangs over Mike Marvelous like a black storm cloud as he wanders about the streets of Beverly Hills with no particular destination in mind. It has been several hours since he stormed out of the abandoned dojo and he has calmed down quite a bit, but his anger has now turned to worry. What if Mao’s words come true? Is it possible that someone as pathetic as Fathom could defeat him, causing him to lose all of his pride and dignity? Mike sighs heavily as he places his hands inside the pants pockets of his navy blue suit. He turns a corner on South Beverly Drive and finds himself walking down world famous Rodeo Drive. He walks past all the shops with his head hanging low trying his best to avoid the prying eyes of the tourists while he attempts to figure out how to handle his current problem. After walking down the street for a moment he raises his head and feasts his dark blues eyes on the cafe known as 208 Rodeo. “Coffee...that’ll calm my nerves.” Mike mutters to himself as he approaches the stairs that lead up to the cafes out door patio. He sits at a small table for two in the corner that overlooks the scenic street and runs his hands through his short blond hair in what seems to be an effort to jump start his brain. A young waitress with fiery red hair that goes down to her waist approaches Mike with a small notepad in her left hand. “What can I get you sir?” She asks in a voice that suggests a patience that wears thinner and thinner as the day goes on. “Just regular coffee...not any of that espresso crap.” Mike says as he stares blankly into space. The waitress turns on her heels and heads towards the inside of the cafe without saying another word, leaving Mike alone on the patio. As he stares off into the empty space before him he begins to wonder if perhaps Mao’s “prophecy” means that the old man is planning to have someone to take him out before the Extreme Championship match due to Mao’s jealousy of him. He quickly dismisses the idea though, only insecure fools think people are conspiring against them. Of course it wouldn’t be unreasonable to assume that Mao is trying to get into his head...cause him to doubt himself, which makes more sense than him being some mystic sage that receives visions of the future. The waitress finally returns with a plain white mug and a pot of coffee, she quickly places the mug in front of Mike and pours him a cup of coffee before she retreats back to the customers waiting for her inside the building. As Mike begins to drink the comfortingly warm liquid his composure starts to come back to him. Before he can straighten out his thoughts soft hands that smell of lilac, obviously the hands of a woman suddenly cover his eyes. “Guess who!” A soft voices lovingly coos at him. He sighs to himself, he knows exactly who it is and it’s the last person he needs to be around at this moment. “What the hell do you want Hillary?” He asks with an annoyed voice as he removes her hands from his face. While she is an annoyance, she does indeed have her uses...even though they are few and far in-between. Hillary sits down in the chair across from Mike with a loving smile formed upon her nymph like face. Even Mike has to admit to himself that only Hillary can look amazing in a Red Sox jersey and plain blue jeans. “I’ve been looking for you all day Butter Muffin! I need to tell you something that is really important.” She says while batting her baby blue eyes and rubbing her foot against his leg underneath the table. Mike rolls his dark blue eyes; he hates it when she calls him that stupid pet name. “Well what is it? I got things to do before I leave tonight.” He says with an irritated voice as he reaches down to bat her foot away from his leg, the last thing he needs is for her to leave dirty sneaker prints all over his expensive pants! A bit of a worried look comes over Hillary’s face and she begins to twirl her shoulder length blonde hair in her finger while biting her lower lip. “Those Primetime Central guys called me and said I couldn’t accompany to the Lion’s Den cage because they think I’ll help you cheat.” She says with a large frown on her face that accompanied by sad puppy dog eyes. “Damn it! There go all my plans for the match right out the window!” Mike says in a disappointed tone of voice before slamming his fist down on the table hard enough to rattle the coffee pot. He again starts to wonder if maybe Mao is right, that he’s destined to fail and there’s not a damn thing he can do about it! “No damn it, I’ll still win it all...I’m Mike Marvelous damn it!” He says as he rises from the table as if he suddenly has a new mission in life and tosses a twenty-dollar bill on the table for the coffee. As he starts to descend the stairs down to the street when Hillary catches up with him and taps him on the shoulder. “What are you going to do sweetheart?” She asks, worried that Mike is about to do something stupid. Mike turns to her with a cocky smirk present on his face, he doesn’t care about Mao’s stupid prophecy anymore...he’s the master of his destiny, not some idiotic old queer! “I’m going to open up their eyes and make them see Hillary.” He says in an arrogant manner before turning back around and walking off. -The End-
