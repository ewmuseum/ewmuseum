19 January 2005 "We have to fight women?" The Flying Vergomovs "We have to fight women?" DING… … … … … … … DONG! I hate those people. You know the type. The type of people that press the doorbell, and here the ding, but wait a few seconds to let go so that the dong is its own separate entity. Why bother? Sorry… back to the story. DING… … … … … DONG! Seriously, why bother? You’d almost swear I was doing this to fill my first page with nothing, but actually I’m trying to connect with my audience. Working? No I didn’t think so. Oh well… DING… DONG! And so the apartment of the Flying Vergomovs remained motionless after the third ring of the bell. The tranquil environment in the white studio plan apartment remained for a couple of seconds. DING… DONG! Finally, no waiting time. See even the person holding the button gets pissed off with themselves. And a door opened. Igor Arefiev stepped out into the morning sun rubbing his eyes. He was wearing blue shorts and a white t-shirt. He scratches his head as he cross the apartment. DING… DONG! “Yeah. Yeah. Yeah. I hear you. I’m coming,” grumbles the circus performer-turned-wrestler. Igor lifts the phone beside his door. “Hello?” questions Igor as he holds the phone to his ear. There is a silence in the apartment. “What?” Igor is not pleased at what he is hearing. A few seconds pass. “A week early? Why?” he protests. A few more seconds pass. “Fine…” he finally concedes, “come on up!” Igor presses a small white button and hangs up the phone. “For fuck sake…” he mutters under the phone. Arefiev opens the apartment door and leaves it ajar. A door opens. Constantino Zergolov moves his head into the new day. “Does that guy know it’s 7.30 am in the morning?” asks a disgusted Zergolov. “It was Henry…” “Henry?” “The guy that help us to settle in to the Lights Out way of working…” “Oh yeah… Short bald guy…” Igor nods. “What did he want?” questions Zergolov. He looks back into his bedroom. “We start training a week early…” “WHAT THE…?” Zergolov stops and looks into his room. He steps out of the doorway and moves closer to Igor on the sofa. “Why?” Igor shrugs. “Some PTC Tag thingy…” “What is PTC?” Igor shrugs. Just then, Henry bursts into the room. He is a short bald guy with thick dark hair around the back and sides of his head. He wears thick black framed glasses and beams at the Flying Vergomovs. He is wearing a grey suit, red tie and a white shirt. He finishes his businessman look with a black briefcase. “Hi!” smiles the wide awake Henry. The Flying Vergomovs groan back. “How are you guys?” questions the over enthusiastic businessman. The Flying Vergomovs groan back. “Good!” smiles Henry. “Was there something you wanted?” questions Igor. He sounds annoyed. Grumpy bastard, Henry was only to be happy. “Yes…” nods Henry repeatedly, “Yes there was… I came over here to tell you something. I have been sent to get to inform you guys of something. Yes… There is something I want to tell you.” “You’ve forget what it is…” mentions Zergolov. “Yes…” continues Henry, “I have been to sent you of something.” There is silence between the three. The Flying Vergomov stare at Henry, expecting some more words. Henry stands smiling and looks around the apartment. “Henry?” questions Igor. “Yes?” smiles Henry. “What do you have to tell us?” “Oh… Have I not told you already?” “NO!” “Oh…” Henry takes a noticeable step back after the loud “NO!” from the Flying Vergomovs. His smile slips a little. “It is regards your wrestling…” The pair on the sofa nod. But then there is silence. Henry returns to looking around the apartment, with his fingers crossed. “Henry?” “What?” “You were saying?” “I was…” Igor sighs. “It’s as though the Masked Monkeys have sent him to slow us down…” sighs Igor. “Who are the Masked Monkeys?” questions Constantino. Igor shrugs. “I’m don’t know. It’s weird. I’m in a weird… no I already said that, I’m in a strange mood today. It’s like someone else is deciding what I’m saying.” Both mean look at each other and then up at the skies. They both shake their heads in disbelief. “Oh yeah… I remember,” smiles Henry. The Flying Vergomovs turn to face Henry’s general direction. “You have a PTC Tag Team Title Match…” nods Henry, seeming very pleased with himself. There is no reaction from the two circus performers. “Don’t we work for LOW?” questions Constantino, turning to Igor. Igor nods. Henry nods as well. “Yes, yes… You work for LOW. But LOW is part of PTC and you have a shot at the PTC Tag Titles…” No reaction from the Flying Vergomovs. “What the fuck…?” mutters Igor. He gets up from the couch and walks into his room. “Where are you going?” questions Henry. The door slams. “Yeah! You go to your room!” snaps Henry. Zergolov eyes Henry cautiously. “So get ready…” beams Henry. “For what?” questions Zergolov, “Is the match today?” “No, no… But you have training…” smiles Henry. The guy is always happy. It is beginning to worry Constantino. Henry turns to leave. “So who are the PTC Tag Champions?” questions Constantino. Henry coughs. “What?” he asks. “Who are the current champions?” “Karina Wolfenden and Tessa Windsor…” smiles Henry. “Two women?” Henry nods. Henry leaves the room, and slams the door. Constantino is alone in the studio plan apartment. “We have to fight women?” ---- 21 January 2005 You... fucked... up! “We’re not fighting women,” exclaims Igor Arefiev, “Don’t be stupid. They wouldn’t have a chance…” And there they stood. The Flying Vergomovs. Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov holding sports bags and scratching their heads. No they don’t have hygiene problems. It just gets itchy under masks 24-7. “Where are we supposed to be going?” questions Igor. “Henry rang and said that we would meet our personal trainer in the Flesh Farm downtown… That’s where we are…” There they stood. The Flying Vergomovs stood outside the Flesh Farm. “Why do we always end up with pervy old men trainers?” sighs Igor as he spots the strip joint the pair stand outside. “Don’t say anything… We have this qualifying match as well as LOW programming. Just be glad of the extra training. We can iron out the creases next week…” reassures Constantino as he looks around his person. “Yeah, but what trainer meets his new clients at a strip joint?” Zergolov shrugs. Igor shakes his head with disgust and looks around. There is a silence between the pair as a car drives past. “We could always wait inside?” suggests Constantino with a slight grin growing on his face. “NO!” snaps Igor, “I’m not practicing leg locks on you if I know you could have a hard-on…” “I just want to look for our trainer…” Igor shakes his head. “NO!” Constantino’s slightly chirpy expression immediately drops. He bows his head and begins to kick at a stone on the ground like a naughty school boy. Just then, Henry, the tag team’s assistant marches by and begins to head into the strip club. Igor eyes the short bald guy. “Henry!” Henry looks around to his left. He has a shocked expression on his face. He didn’t expect to see anyone there. “Constantino… How are you?” smiles Henry. “I’m Igor.” “I’m Constantino,” smiles Zergolov as he kicks at the stone on the ground. Henry smiles. Igor look down at Henry. “So…?” suggests Arefiev. Henry’s expression is blank. He just continues to smile. “So…?” suggests Arefiev again. He is trying to lead Henry somewhere with his questioning, but it doesn’t appear to be working. Henry just smiles. “Where is our trainer?” questions Arefiev. “What do you mean?” questions Henry. Finally his smile has disappeared. Igor and Constantino both seem relieved over this latest development. “Where is our trainer?” repeats Arefiev. “They haven’t turned up,” adds Constantino. “Turned up where?” replies Henry. “HERE!” bites Igor. Henry takes a step back from Igor. He looks a little shaken. “Obviously your trainer hasn’t turned up here. You’re not meeting her here. You’re meeting her at the Fresh Farm downtown.” “The Fresh Farm…” mutters Igor. Henry nods and smiles. Igor turns his head and gives Constantino Zergolov the evils. Zergolov now motions a couple of steps backwards. He backs away from an Arefiev in a bad mood. “Why are you guys here?” asks Henry. Igor looks at Henry and gives him the evils. “Someone thought we were meeting our trainer at the flesh farm…” Igor spins on his heels and marches off down the street. “See ya later…” smiles Zergolov as he begins to follow behind his tag team partner. Zergolov has to jog a little to get alongside. “What’s up?” asks Zergolov as he finally gets on a level with his tag team partner. “You fucked up!” “What do you…?” “You fucked up!” “What are you…?” “You fucked up!” “Quit it!” “NO!” Igor stops and stares at Constantino. “You… fucked… up! We have a chance to earn a PTC Tag Title shot… And you fucked up! You couldn’t manage to pass on a message… We are late for training… training for the most important match we may have in 2005 and you… fucked… up!” Igor spins on his heel and storms off.
