Weapon of Choice: II

25 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-02-25 O'Hare International Airport Most people don’t know how to navigate airports. They get off their plane, and immediately get lost among the gates and the baggage claims and the hustle and the bustle. Then, there are men like Seymour Almasy. Men who’ve done this a thousand times before. Men who, God willing, will do it a thousand times more. Hurrying away from his gate, Seymour began power-walking for the baggage claim. It was a relatively long walk, and that meant that the Final Fantasy had plenty of time to think about everything that had happened to him. "That fuck," he breathed, low enough so no one else could hear. It didn’t take a rocket scientist to figure out who he was talking about. "That son of a bitch was the man in Laura’s bed when I was over there, and I didn’t even know it. God, I could have nipped this entire situation then and there. I could have sav--" Seymour’s voice trailed off. That thought was beyond painful, but it was a thought he was having more and more often. There were so many ways he could have saved Laura. So many ways he could have prevented that tragedy. But he hadn’t, and so his childhood friend and former wife was gone. Gone forever. Forcing himself to smile, he stepped onto the moving walkway. Darkly, he wondered if anyone could possibly imagine what he was planning. Seymour knew that he was an oddity to most, that short man with the long white hair. Strange, yes, but harmless. Hardly harmless now, considering his possession of the pistol that felt white-hot when it pressed against his body from its home in Seymour’s jacket pocket. In truth, Seymour had no idea how to use the weapon. He hoped it was as easy as it seemed on the cop dramas; just point and shoot. It was almost ludicrous when he thought about it. All his life, he’d played games featuring characters with great quests, and by God, now he had one. His quest had lead him to Chicago, and would now lead him to the more specific location that he sought. Stepping off the walkway, he made a turn here, a turn there. He’d been in O’Hare at least a dozen times, allowing him to cut down on the time it took him to do everything. That was important. The faster he found Rich Rollins, the less time he would have to second guess himself. Because the bottom line was simple. Seymour Almasy WANTED to kill Rich Rollins, with every single fiber of his being. He wanted to shoot the son of a bitch between the eyes--no, first, he’d shoot him in the groin and let him suffer, THEN deliver the killing blow. On paper, it was a great idea. But he knew that thinking about it and doing it were two different things entirely. Raising the gun…firing it…killing a man, it was all very serious. And it would have very serious consequences. Which was why the more pragmatic, realistic side of Seymour Almasy had packed for this trip as he did. Just before he reached the baggage claim, he made a hard right turn for the rental car counter. Seymour was going to get his car, fire it up, and make his way to the home of the Renegade. The site of the final showdown. And meanwhile, Seymour’s bag would continue to circle the baggage carousel, round and round and round, until airport staff finally took the piece of luggage off. When they did, the contents would confuse them greatly, just as they confused the security guards in the airport. For Seymour Almasy’s bag was empty. "Who would check a bag with nothing in it," an airport staffer would be heard asking, a few hours later. That answer, to Seymour, was simple. A man who needed a decoy to distract security so he could commit a federal felony by smuggling a gun into an airport. But more importantly.. A man who didn’t know if he’d be coming back alive. ------------------- UWF Locker Room Sometime in 2004 "Shit, man," Rich Rollins said, looking over his shoulder on the way out of the arena. "You sure you don’t want a drink?" Seymour Almasy looked back at the UWF newcomer, and nodded. He didn’t know a lot about the Renegade, but he’d proven himself in the ring thus far in his tenure. Rich wasn’t the most tactful creature on Earth, but then again, who really was? "Alcohol’s not my thing. Besides, I’ve got to get to some sleep. Going back home early tomorrow for a few days." "Ah, that so," Rollins asked, reaching a hand up to smooth out his hair under his red bandanna. "Where you live, anyway?" "New York," Almasy replied. "Penthouse suite." Rollins’ resultant whistle was enough to make Seymour chuckle. "Hey, wrestling’s been good to me, especially this year." "It’s been good to me too," Rollins responded, honestly, "but I can’t live like the fuckin’ upper class. Arrogant shitheads…" "No wonder they call you the Renegade," Seymour observed. "So, where DO you live, anyway?" "Chicago," came the response. "In an abandoned warehouse. Don’t worry, it’s not as bad as it sounds. I’ve fixed the place up a bit. Hey…if you’re ever in town, why not stop on by for that drink?" "Alright. I’ll take you up on that. Where exactly is it?" "I’ll give you the address." That was the most civil conversation Rich Rollins and Seymour Almasy have ever had. Not coincidentally, it was the ONLY civil conversation Rollins and Almasy have ever had. At the time, it seemed so useless to Seymour. Now, it was of the utmost importance. ----------------------- The Love Box Warehouse, south Chicago "Christ, how the fuck does he live here? This place looks like a shithole took a shit." Seymour Almasy emerged from his rented vehicle, stepping out onto a patch of dirt with weeds growing out of it. Reaching into his coat pocket, he patted the pistol, wondering just how the fuck he was supposed to go about this. Did he go in with gun drawn and hold the Renegade at gunpoint, demanding an apology for his actions? Did he just walk in and start shooting? Or did he go in and chat with Rollins, waiting for the right time to pull his firearm? Decisions, decisions, and he didn’t know which was the right one. No easy decision, this. Not with lives on the line. Finally, he made his decision. He was going in, gun drawn, but he’d let Rollins talk. See if he had a good reason for sleeping with Laura, not that he thought such a thing existed. Besides, he’d be alone with Rollins. All the time in the world to shoot the sorry fuck in the testicles. Suddenly, though, something happened to ruin his plan. The sounds of struggling reached his ears, and he whirled 120 degrees to the side. There, his eyes fell upon two men leading a handcuffed man to some sort of garishly decorated vehicle. The handcuffed man was Rich Rollins. "Son of a.." Seymour whispered, under his breath. What the fuck were the odds? Someone ELSE had a beef against the Renegade? …well, in Seymour’s line of work, the odds were actually quite good. But neither of Rollins’ two captors looked like wrestlers. Now, Seymour felt afraid. He was an amateur in this shit, really. Whoever the Hell had come here to take Rollins had obviously come prepared, both with handcuffs and the numbers advantage. Presumably a gun, too, because Almasy seriously doubted the Renegade would let himself be handcuffed without the threat of one. Seymour was sure he would be spotted. And then what? Would he be thrown in the back of the van, like Rollins was being now? Almasy held his breath, praying he wouldn’t be seen. Somehow, he wasn’t. Both of the captors entered the large vehicle, which began to slowly roll away from the scene of the abduction. Seymour was safe. And it was that very fact that allowed him to be enraged. "THIS IS MY SITUATION TO DEAL WITH," he bellowed, at the top of his lungs. "WHO THE FUCK DO THEY THINK THEY ARE?!" With rage fueling him, it was easy to be irrational. He damn near sprinted back to his rental car, put the key in the ignition, and took off from the abandoned warehouse’s parking lot, following the getaway vehicle at a safe distance. Adrenaline coursed through his body as he drove, right hand leaving the wheel to turn on the radio. Seymour Almasy rarely, if ever, listened to non-RPG music. But in a virtual car chase, with his life potentially on the line, driving rock as a soundtrack seemed highly appropriate. -------------------------------------------- Resthaven Cemetary Seymour was rather thankful for his nondescript rental car. Either due to its ordinariness, or the fact that the kidnappers were extremely focused on wherever the fuck they were going, he hadn’t been spotted. He’d been following behind for what seemed like hours, and a quick glance down at the clock confirmed that it was just past midnight. "Come on, you assholes," Seymour seethed, "get to wherever the fuck you want to go already!" If Seymour was clueless before as to what to do, he was even more so now. Now, not only was he planning on killing Rich Rollins, he was going to have to wrench Rollins away from two (likely armed) kidnappers. The van ahead of him turned left, into what appeared to be a cemetery. Seymour followed, blood pounding a little harder in his veins with every yard he drove. Almasy kept his car at a distance as he watched the van drive off the road, and onto the dewy grass of the cemetery itself, right next to a grave marked with the same yellow tape that had encircled the site of Laura’s suicide. Seymour’s breath caught in his throat as the front doors of the van opened, and both kidnappers emerged. One opened the rear doors, where Rollins had been placed, and the other man came into view, carrying something in his hand. "Gasoline…" Seymour said, recognizing the tell-tale can that the substance was carried in. Well, perhaps not gasoline, but whatever substance it was, Seymour knew from the can that it was highly flammable. "What in the name of Yevon are they going to--" It was about then that Seymour Almasy saw that Rollins was not alone in the back of the van. Even in the darkness, he could see a female figure, bound, gagged, and trembling. There was another captive. "No," he whispered, looking on horrorstruck as the scene continued to play out in front of his eyes. Rollins was forced to the ground by one man, with a gun trained to his head. Seymour could hear the sounds of speech, but individual words were lost to him. And then, the son of a bitch with the can emptied its contents all over the interior of the van. Over the struggling woman. The heartless asshole even stood on top of the poor woman as he poured, soaking everything. Now Seymour could hear Rollins screaming, agonized wails that cut through the night air like daggers. And then, there it was. A single point of light in the kidnapper’s hand. A match. Seymour sat in his car, stock-still, as it fell into the interior. In a less threatening time, it would have been a full on Flare spell to the man who loved RPGs so. "NOOOOOOOO! THOSE ASSHOLES!" They were killing her. Killing her in a way far more inhumane and torturous than anything Seymour could have come up with for Rollins. And Rollins was being forced to watch, it seemed, as he was kneeling facing the inferno. The fat man closed the rear doors of the van, sparing Seymour the most horrific sight he had ever seen in his twenty four years on Earth. Adrenaline flowed through his body unlike anything else he’d ever experienced. This situation was far more serious than he’d thought. Far more involved. Far more deadly. A woman was being burned alive. Burned alive in front of the handcuffed man with a gun to his head. Burned alive in front of Seymour’s own watchful gaze. It was likely too late to save the poor woman. But it wasn’t too late to exact vengeance for her. The Final Fantasy pressed down on the gas pedal, sending his rental car careening towards the nightmarish scene in front of him. The man who had set the blaze regarded his handiwork with a disgusting reverence. The man with the gun to the Renegade’s head was laughing. Neither noticed the avenging angel driving up behind them, even as he put the car in park and stepped out onto the now defiled ground of the burial place. Seymour exhaled deeply, seeing his breath condense in the frigid night air. He reached into his coat pocket, and came out with the one and only gun he’d ever held in his life. Screaming at the top of his lungs, in a feral voice that he couldn’t identify as his own, Seymour addressed the sick waste of humanity. "TURN AROUND, ASSHOLE!" The fat man spun slowly. Clearly, he hadn’t expected any intruders. "Who the fuck are--" Seymour fired, a single shot that flew through the air and smashed into the man’s kneecap, shattering bone and dropping the man to the floor, sending the gas can flying. "Does it matter," Seymour asked, turning his attention to the--was it a police officer?-- who held the gun to Rollins’ head, training his own weapon on the officer. By now, the other man was well aware that his quarry wasn’t the only man with a gun to his head. "Let him out of the handcuffs," Almasy ordered, heart-pounding, "or so help me God…" The detective grimaced. All of this planning, all of this plotting…ruined by a completely unforeseen intruder? How? He didn’t have much of a choice though. He intended on living through this escapade. And so, like the sanctimonious son of a bitch he was, he pulled the handcuff key from his pocket, and reached for the handcuffs binding Rollins’ arms… …before promptly wheeling back around, gun aimed for Seymour’s head. Unfortunately for the detective, Seymour fired first, another shot to the kneecap that was as effective as the first. Darkly, in the back of his mind, Seymour acknowledged that this shooting thing WAS as easy as it seemed on TV. "Asshole," Seymour sneered, watching as the man dropped his gun to clutch at his knee in agony. "You deserve a Hell of a lot worse than this. My apologies in advance, but I can’t have you or that pyromaniac over there interfering in this." Seymour kneeled, shoved the barrel of the gun into the right palm of the officer, and pulled the trigger. What remained of the detective’s hand wasn’t easily identifiable as such. Almasy grabbed the handcuff key from the officer, before turning to regard the other man. He was still down, clutching at his knee pathetically. Good. There would be no interference now. No bullshit. Just Rollins and himself. Kneeling, Seymour undid the handcuffs of the PTC Global Champion, and jerked the larger man to his feet. Rollins, though, didn’t seem to be especially grateful, still screaming incoherently at what he had seen. Richard Rollins, PTC Global Champion, slowly turned around. Like his theme music, what he saw staring back at him was born of a broken man. ----------------------------------- It was a hallucination. It had to be. There was no possible way that Seymour Almasy stood in front of Rollins, saving his life with a gun in one hand and clenched vengeance in the other. They stood but three paces apart, staring at one another as the blaze that had once been a beautiful woman continued to roar, silhouetting their shapes against the unfeeling stone of the surrounding graves. Almasy was shaking; his gun hand rattled uncertainly as he thrust it at Rollins, seething with unbridled scorn. So he finally found out, Rollins thought bitterly. The Renegade spread his arms. "You picked a real bad time to talk about this, Seymour." His face was matted down by chlorine-soaked hair and bitter tears, but Rollins still managed to look invincible as he gazed back through the sight of Almasy’s weapon. "That van," he choked, "that woman, she was carrying my baby." Under most other circumstances, Seymour would have been apologetic. Sympathetic. But not today. The gun remained trained between the eyes of the Renegade as Seymour practically seethed with a combination of hatred and sorrow. "Not so nice," he whispered, bitterly, "when people fuck with what shouldn’t be fucked with, is it?" He tried to remain strong, but Rollins’ equilibrium finally crumbled. "You’ve been the hurdle that I could never jump for years, Seymour! I wanted to be you when I came to the UWF. I wanted to be the celebrity that you were, reach the top of the mountain and hold my ground like you did! I only went after the Global title because you held it, for fuck’s sake!" While it was touching, Seymour didn’t waiver. "That has nothing to do with you fucking my wife, Rollins." And there it was. The Renegade had hoped that Almasy would have found out much sooner, but running over the obvious with a highlighter was not in his job description. The Renegade’s eyes hardened. "I knew we’d cross paths sooner or later in this tournament, Almasy. I knew it. What better way to get inside your head than to get inside your ex-wife?" Seymour snorted, a completely out of place expression for the man considered by most to be a champion of good, of the sort of pure motivations and feelings that wrestling so often lacked. "So this is about the tournament, Rich? This is about a fucking wrestling match? Fuck, if you would have told me you were going to sleep with Laura, I would have dropped out of the goddamned thing! Do you have any idea what’s happened to me since this piece of shit tournament started, Rollins? ANY FUCKING IDEA?!" The Final Fantasy glared at the Renegade, but really, neither man was his nickname. This was Seymour Almasy and Rich Rollins, in the middle of a fucking cemetery, with a two now-corpses burning in a van and two men shot in the kneecaps looking on, wondering just what the fuck had just happened. Rollins’ breath sounded like a hissing snake as it slowly escaped through his teeth. He stabbed a finger at the burning wreckage, tears welling up in his eyes. "Yeah Jason, I think I have a pretty good fucking idea." "Don’t call me that," Seymour warned. "Or what, you’ll fucking shoot me?!" Rollins’ voice rose to near hysterical heights. "With the gun I gave her, no less!" *THWACK!* That was the sound of Seymour Almasy, hero to thousands of children everywhere, PISTOL-WHIPPING Richard Rollins. The force behind the blow wasn’t anywhere near what he’d like to channel, but the action itself felt better than anything he’d done in ages. "YOU. FUCKING. ASSHOLE!" The gun remained leveled at Rollins’ head, but he moved it over, lining up with one of Rich’s eyes. "You see the blood on the barrel of this gun, you fucking cocksucker? That’s Laura’s. THAT’S MY EX-WIFE’S BLOOD FROM WHEN SHE SHOT HERSELF IN THE CHEST! You know something, you fuck? I had this gun in my mouth for thirty minutes today, trying to work up the courage to blow my own fucking head off. But now? Now I’m fucking gleeful I didn’t. Because you? You are the lowest fucking form of life I have ever met. You may be THE ONLY PERSON walking this Earth who DESERVES to have his pregnant lover burned alive!" Still moaning on the ground, Frank Jacobs shot a sour grimace at Almasy. "He deserves a lot worse than that for killing my-…" "SHUT UP," Seymour screamed at him. The sickeningly slick lubrication of blood met Rollins’ fingertips as he caressed the side of his head, having now been knocked around all evening by just about everybody in Chicago. Struggling to his knees, the Renegade spat a crimson mouthful at Almasy’s feet. "I didn’t intend for her to kill herself, Seymour," he said as he looked into the Final Fantasy’s eyes. "I told her that you cared more about your career than you ever did about her. I told her to use that gun on you." Seymour’s eyes went kaleidoscopic, flashing from sorrow to anger to hatred and back again. His shoulders slumped forward in utter defeat, the gun lowering for the first time since he’d come face to face with the man who wanted to outdo him so, the man who wanted to replace him atop Primetime Central. "Richard, I wish she had. Because if she had, we wouldn’t be here having this conversation. Shit, everything’s gone now. For both of us. If what you told me is true, your girlfriend really just got set on fire. My ex-wife shot herself to death. You’ve got two fucking nut jobs after you for God only knows what reason, though if it’s anything like what you did to me, I’m sure it’s not pleasant." Bitterly, the Final Fantasy laughed, a laugh that went up to the sky and God, Yevon, whomever had decided to mock him so. "And you know what the shittiest part of all of this is, Richard? That fat fuck over there started the inferno. That detective douche bag tried to kill us both. I shot fuck and douche in the kneecap. We’re all going to prison. And you, the reason all this shit happened, the STUPID SON OF A BITCH WHO COULDN’T KEEP SHIT IN THE RING? You haven’t done anything illegal. You’re gonna get off scot free while I rot in a prison cell…wait." He paused, staring down at the pitiful soul who used to be Rich Rollins. "You know what? Fuck it." Wild-eyed, Seymour stooped, retrieving the gun that had fallen from the detective’s hands. Disgustedly, he pressed the pistol into the Renegade’s blood-slick palm, favoring him with the smile of a dead man. "One of us is gonna die today, Rich. Can’t you feel it? Can’t you sense it? I’m a fair man, fairer than I should be. That’s your weapon. There’s only a few shots left in mine. One’s in Laura, two are in the detective, one’s in the fat fuck. But if I fire, I’m not going to need more than one. Nor should you…" Trying to crawl his way around the empty grave of Margaret Rollins, Detective Newton released an agonized grunt. "What the hell are you doing? He’s-…" "Shut up," Rollins spat as he stumbled to his feet, clutching the firearm greedily. Making a slow walk up to Almasy, the Renegade’s eyes quivered with a multitude of emotions as he raised the firearm slowly, towering over his enemy who likewise pressed his own weapon into Rollins’ stomach. Their eyes were dancing as they danced with death. Hefting the loaded gun and pressing it into the soft tissue of Seymour Almasy’s neck, a bead of sweat trickled down Rich Rollins’ brow as he cocked the hammer with cold certainty. "I just want you to know," he all but whispered, "I’ve never killed a man." Seymour’s face was a blank void; it was the kind of place where sense and reason went to die. "You’ve already killed me," he said as he pushed his own pistol deeper into the folds of Rollins’ flesh. "Now finish the job." Rollins pulled the trigger. The bullet sailed over Seymour’s shoulder, drilling Frank Jacob’s left eye with frightening precision. The vengeful father jerked violently as the slug penetrated his brain, then fell silent. His hesitation was his downfall; startled beyond comprehension, Seymour Almasy fell backwards. The Final Fantasy could do little more than watch as Rich Rollins walked in turn over to the screaming detective, put the gun in the man’s mouth, and blew bits of skull across Margaret Rollins’ headstone. Seymour dropped his gun. "I… I… I…" but he couldn’t form the words. The fire played in sinister shadows across the Renegade’s features as he turned to regard Almasy. "I knew you didn’t have it in you," he said, the words ringing a hollow truth. Rollins doubled over then, spilling the remains of his dinner all over the ragged gravel that lined the tombstones. Horrified eyes trained themselves on Rollins and the handgun, the scent of charred flesh and vomit combined making Seymour wonder if he had indeed found Hell. He dry-heaved in turn, but nothing would come up. "You… my God…" Pathetically, he wretched and heaved on his knees until a sharp wailing sound cut through the heavy silence. Sirens. Both their heads turned to the cemetery gates. The red and blue strobes of Chicago’s finest were almost a welcome reprise from the carnival of carnage that surrounded them; between the human bonfire and the bodies spread around their feet, the sight of any sort kind of authority figure was like a beacon of humanity for both men. Seymour sat quietly as he watched a half dozen squad cars flood through the narrow entrance. Rollins stood in motionless shock, trying to hold on to his sanity by the barest of threads. The cars eventually screeched to a halt in a semi-circle around the scene as officers poured out like ants, taking cover behind open car doors while training a dozen pistols on the drained duo. Rollins stared into the headlights as he said: "I guess this means we’re disqualified, huh?" Seymour tried to chuckle, but there was no force behind it. "Speak for yourself. I’m not the one holding a firearm with two dead bodies and burning corpses less than ten feet away." "Lower your weapon!" they screamed over a megaphone. "We will use deadly force if necessary, so place the weapon on the ground!" Caught in nothing more than a pair of red swim trunks, Rich Rollins placed the gun at his feet as he raised his arms in the air, wondering just how in the hell he was going to explain this one. ---------------------------------- It occurs to me today that the world is a fucked up place. Over the past three months, I have seen things that are forever burned into my retinas. Done things that I will never be able to forget. Laura’s gone now. Because Rich Rollins had a plan for her to kill me, to wipe me out so I wouldn’t be alive to face him in GTT, in the inevitable meeting that he thought we would have. He slept with her, sharing an evening of intimacy with her that she shared with none but myself. He gave her the pistol that he thought would end my life, that instead ended hers. In this, he ended my life. What’s left? Grandfather is gone, the only natural casualty of this entire sordid stretch. My mother returned only to mock me. Even Justice turns a blind eye to the horrors of this day. The officers came and arrested Rollins, led him off in handcuffs for the murder of that man and the detective. For the burning of his lover. For attempting to murder me. And I, in spite of my role in this conflict, am getting off scot-free. They treat me with pity, as a victim saved from certain death by timely intervention. Their investigation is shoddy, to say the least. They haven’t even bothered to pick up my gun, the one with which I shot two men today. No, the police have their man. And by God, they plan on punishing him. That’s why I’m sitting in the back of this police car now. They’re going to take me down to the precinct for questioning. They expect me to be able to provide the testimony to take Rollins down. But what do I tell them? If I tell them the truth, I’m going to prison. And Rich Rollins, that bastard who gave Laura her suicide weapon…he’d get off for self-defense. Or at the very least, have his sentence mitigated. And he’d never be punished for everything he’s done to me. All of the crimes that he’s committed against my person. Although, by the law’s definition…there’s no proof. No ‘evidence’. To make sure justice is served, I must aid a miscarriage of justice. The hero must become the villain. Regardless, though, I will have my chance for revenge. My one chance to handle things the way they should be. For I heard one word slip from Rollins’ lips as he was taken away. His temporary salvation. My eternal salvation. Bail. He could meet it. And that meant that the semi-finals of GTT5 will indeed go on. In a wrestling ring, I will have my chance to make Rich Rollins pay for ruining my life. What happened to Rich Rollins was horrible. Unthinkable. And yet, to hear the police’s story, I can almost understand it. That man’s daughter had been killed by Rollins, however it happened. He had recourse for his twisted deeds. But Rollins? He did it to get an advantage on me. To fuck me up going into a match that we might or might not have. Because he COULD. Rich Rollins, enjoy your last few moments of freedom. Because in the ring, your sentence begins. No promises there’s anything left of you for the state to execute. ============================= (OOC: To echo my counterpart, it's been an honor to work on this story with him.
