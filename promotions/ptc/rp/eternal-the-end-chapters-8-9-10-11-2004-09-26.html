Eternal: The End (Chapters 8, 9, 10, 11)

Date: 2004-09-26 Eternal: The End Chapter 8: A Touch Of Fate Sometimes certain things are just beyond our powers. Even immortals, we hold no such gift of foresight. We are not gods, who rule souls with a clap of a hand, or control destinies from a snap of a finger. No, we hold no such gift where the limits are only via imagination. And sometimes when we think that everything had ended right in front of our very eyes, in a slight twist of fate, it all changes. Maybe destiny had every bend planned to the minutest detail, maybe not. Or maybe certain decisions had simply changed the way of life, defying the fates already written by the gods. Maybe within a split second, a last minute change of heart had, to put quite simply, cheated death. Yes, you heard me right. Cheated death. Unbelievable isn’t it? I’m sure you think that such an outcome can never be possible. If so, how can you explain me being here? You’re at a lost of words now, aren’t you? Relax, unlike a certain Deville, I didn’t come back to life. Nor do I speak to the likes of Lucifer constantly buzzing in my ear. No, I hold no such gift of being able to speak with highest order of devils. You can rest yourself assured. What? No, don’t worry, I’m not dead yet. Nor do I intend to be for quite some time. Come on; don’t give me that dejected look on your face to stand trial for your emotion. For sarcasm will go nowhere with me. Which right now brings me to an even simpler fact for you to understand. You haven’t gotten rid of me yet. Nor will you be able to for quite some time. It seems that time had once again changed sides, from an enemy to a friend. And I never thought that I’d be glad of that. You see; certain situations will evolve you into a different person. Making you think differently and having you appreciate more of the things that you have in your grasp. This is the end, Kasper Sky. Those are the words that I’ve been hearing a lot of lately. Somehow, now that simply seems amusing to my ears. Almost an irritation, but not quite as annoying. No, you are wrong. This is not the end. Quite the opposite actually. I would say, that this is quite simply the beginning. Chapter 9: Evolution Of Minds He opened his eyes slowly, finding himself shake slightly. He was icy cold, almost freezing. Am I dead yet, he asked himself in his mind. Trying to control his tremble, the colors of the world hit his eyes like a dart, sending a shooting pain down his spine. No matter how much time eternity presented to him in the years to come, he’d never forget something. He’d never forget the sound of the air split by the speed of a bullet, missing him by inches, creating a thunderous crack that rang through his ears and into his head. Moments before, the last thing he saw in this world was a girl holding a gun pointed at his head. No, he wasn’t dead yet. He was sure of that. There was no pain running through his body, no blood rushing out staining his clothes. He was as healthy as ever. And for once, he was glad of that. It took him a while to take in everything in front of him. All of a sudden, he felt like a stranger in the world, feeling as if he didn’t really belong there. Rightly enough, the first thing or person he noticed, was the person who sent a bullet revolving through the air heading towards him at breathtaking speed. Beautiful and gleaming in the sunlight, suddenly she wasn’t the confident person that he had always seen before. With the gun dangling from her hand, pointing to the ground, her face was tear-streaked. She seemed to be on the verge of a breakdown, and he could feel no pity for her. In fact, his anger was rising when he realized what had just happened. And then his eyes caught something else, and his face grew pale. The silver locket, hanging lifeless on her life hand, suddenly was the world to him. “Where did you get it?” he managed to force out the words. The girl dropped the gun, the impact and sound muffled by the thick grass. She clenched the silver locket and held it to her chest, her eyes watering again. “I’m…sorr…sorry…” she stuttered out in a soft tone, trembling slightly. He ignored her words, and pressed her further. “Where did you get that silver locket?” he continued, while subconsciously holding on to his own silver locket around his neck. “I’m sorry, Kasper.” She whispered once more, hanging her head, not wanting to look directly at him. Kasper raised his eyebrow, having gotten over his earlier shock. Yet somehow, the pieces seemed to move closer together to complete a picture. Only this time, it moved naturally instead of him having to force the pieces in even though sometimes they didn’t fit. Just when he thought that he had found a sister and fulfilled a promise to his mother, things had changed. After thinking that Athena, the girl who he found years back in Athens, is his sister he thought everything had finally changed for the better. In fact, this girl standing in front of him right now, even made him believe more concretely the other night that Athena is in fact his sister. But now he was beginning to have self-doubts, especially after seeing that silver locket. The silver locket that was supposed to be a bond of blood between two siblings, was in the hands of a girl he had never met before until a couple of nights back. He had even rejected Athena’s feelings for him, thinking that she is his sister. Kasper winced of the thought of her accepting the rejection in silence, and looked down. Deep inside, he knew that he had feelings for her, only that he had never made it known before. And lately, he tried to avoid those feelings because he thought that the two of them were siblings. He clenched his fists, taking out his frustrations. She smoothed her creased black jacket. “This locket belongs to me,” she spoke in a small voice, hesitation in her tone. That made Kasper look up and his eyes widened. “What is your name?” Until now, he had never thought of asking her that question. He never knew why, but now he understood. He was afraid of the answer. Light blonde hair, crystal blue eyes, a pale complexion – she was like a perfect replica of him. “You already know my name,” she whispered. She looked up and looked at Kasper, her eyes begging him to accept. He stared back at her, realizing that this was the moment in his life where things could change for the better or the worse. Secretly, he didn’t want to know. “Tell me your name,” he whispered back to her, making direct eye contact. She forced a smile, as another tear came rolling down her right cheek, creating a line through her tear-streaked face. She looked down, and stared at the ground. “Krystal.” Chapter 10: The Ride Back The flight seemed to take forever. In all the generations that he had lived through, he never thought that his sister would be sitting beside him on a plane one day. Well, the realization still hadn’t hit him properly, with him glancing over to her every couple of seconds making her nervous and uncomfortable. “Stop staring at me,” she muttered. She pouted her lips and crossed her arms, while glaring the guy sitting beside her on the opposite aisle, who was staring at the both of them, apparently amused at their behavior. Kasper looked away hurriedly. “I just can’t help it,” he replied in an exasperated voice. “You know that I’ve waited generations for this day. And now that it is finally here, I still can’t believe it.” He whispered in her ear, a smile playing on his lips. “Can I help you with anything?” a voice spoke beside Krystal, startling her slightly. A pretty blonde stewardess stood over her, a beautiful smile on her face. Krystal apparently wasn’t all that impressed as she muttered, “No, thank you.” “Well, you better believe it.” She continued on, as the stewardess walked away, her footsteps trailing off. Krystal smiled back, but there was still sadness in her eyes. Kasper pulled away from her, settling back in his own seat while resting his head. “Tell me something,” he spoke as she cocked her head in his direction, “Why did you intend to shoot me?” Krystal looked away. “It should have been done. It was only right.” She whispered. Krystal clasped her hands together, and closed her eyes. His eyes widened. “You mean you still think that you should have killed me?” he replied incredulously, while staring hard at his sister. “It had to be done,” she answered in an insisting voice, her facial expression full of stress. “Why?” “You should know why.” Kasper rolled his eyes slightly, impatience overcoming him as Krystal ignored. “Well, I don’t. So go on and tell me.” “Calm down,” she whispered while turning her head slightly, “With time, you’ll know.” Krystal looked deeply at Kasper, as a slight glint was seen in her eyes. Chapter 11: Lost And Found “Athena, meet me at the top of the hill. Yes, I’m back in town. Meet me there in about an hour, I want to introduce you to someone.” Kasper spoke, his voice clearly full of excitement. He couldn’t contain himself as he hanged up the public phone that he was using. “You’re going to love her,” he spoke enthusiastically while turning to face Krystal. He straightened his white jacket and smiled at his sister. Krystal however had a distinctly worried look on her face, which Kasper readily ignored. She forced a smile back at him, but unnoticed to him, her fists were clenched. “I’m sure I will,” she whispered back to him. “By the way, you better start preparing for your upcoming Round Two match. It’s coming up real soon.” Krystal hurriedly shot a sentence back at him, wanting to get off the topic of Athena, for some odd reason. Kasper raised his eyebrow and shrugged, “I’ll prepare for it soon enough. There’s still time.” “You’re up against Tony Stecchino right?” she enquired in a soft tone. “Yeah, I am. Don’t worry dear sister, I’ll get the job done like I always do.” Kasper reassured her, with a slight playful smile on his face. “You better, or this is going to be a short trip.” She forced a grin back at him, trying to get rid of the worried look invading her emotion. “Don’t worry. I promise.” One Hour Later It was the perfect setting, almost too perfect. A clear night with a full moon bearing down on it, and a slight breeze sending a chill through the surroundings. The trees seemed to be whispering to each other using leaves as a form of communication. And from atop the hill, you could the lights glaring from the city. Athena stood there staring at the glimmering lights, her arms crossed across her chest. She glanced at her watch and grumbled under her breath. She was wearing light faded blue jeans with a white tank top, with the moonlight shining on her. “You’re early again, Athena.” A voice spoke out behind her, with slight echoes being heard. She recognized the voice and smiled to herself. It was Kasper, no doubt about that. She slipped her hands in her pockets and turned around, with a smile on her face. And at that very instant, all blood just seemed to drain out of her. She was as pale as a ghost, assuming that ghosts did exist. Her right hand automatically came out of her pocket as she covered her mouth, trembling slightly. Kasper’s smile faded away as he saw her reaction. He glanced at Krystal who was standing beside him, and notice a tear sliding down her left cheek, a sad smile playing on her lips. He was at a lost of words, not understanding the reaction between the two girls. They had never met before, and he was here to introduce them. “The both of you know each other?” he managed to blurt out, while turning his head in the direction of Krystal, expecting an immediate answer. Athena crumbled to her knees as tears started flowing rapidly down her face, streaking it with water. Krystal looked away from her direction and turned to Kasper. “What do you mean?” she replied back, trying to smile softly at him. “Well, do you know her?” he muttered, while glancing his head at the broken down Athena. Krystal gave out a short laugh. “Do I know her? Of course I do. Don’t you remember?” “No, I don’t remember a thing about my past. Except for just some bits and pieces of memories.” Kasper murmured. “Kasper…” she responded in an almost blunt tone. “You took her away from me about ten years ago.” Kasper Sky jerked his head up and looked straight in Krystal’s blue eyes, as another tear streaked down her cheek.
