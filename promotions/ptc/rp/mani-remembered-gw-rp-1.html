Silent Wind Affiliated Pimp Joined: 08 Apr 2003 Posts: 6 Posted: Fri Apr 11, 2003 4:08 am Post subject: Mani: Remembered (GW RP #1) I stand in the middle of the stone room, amongst the rubble. Mani… I whispered his name, listening to it echo slightly off of the stone walls. It had been a long time since I had spoken his name. Months, in fact, since I had ever seen him, but not really seeing him, only the emerald glow of his eyes. The same night that Brian had disappeared, leaving Lisha in my care, to carry out his wicked revenge for the death of his wife. I kicked a small pile of rocks, some of the remains from where Mani had busted into the Oublie, rescuing Lisha and returning her to Salaok. So much for wicked revenge. Mani… I knew it was only a matter of time before he came back. Only, I was hoping it would be later, rather than sooner. The raZed. The Destroyer. The Lost Angel. Only to be found. The Broken Angel. Lost memories retrieved from the dark recesses of his soul, overlapping with the new, causing pain, conflict, anguish. The Harvester of Blood. The third Davis brother found. Mani… I knew why he was upset with me. Perhaps upset is too light a word. Angry, maybe. Pissed off? Then again, I don’t think they make a word strong enough to describe Mani’s feelings towards me. But, who could blame him. Wouldn’t you want the soul of the person who had killed your pregnant wife to enter into the elite field of assassin for hire. The hit went down flawless. It was the timing that was piss poor. Kiana. Mani’s wife. She lay at my feet, beaten to death, her face swollen under the impact of my lead lined gloves. A door shutting. Mani coming home earlier than expected. I faded into the shadows of their bedroom, blending in perfectly with the darkness. Almost perfectly. In the struggle with Kiana, her claws raked across the mask covering my face, exposing my two toned eyes, one white, one black. Mani entering the bedroom, running to Kiana, holding her to his chest, looking up and into the shadows through tear streaked eyes, seeing my exposed ones. I have never heard such a guttural scream as the one that issued forth from his lips. He charged and we fought. Gone was the kinship. Gone was the friendship. Gone was the brotherly bond between us. A well-placed kick sent me flying through a glass window, falling from second story of their house and landing in a bush. I saw Mani leap down after me, landing like a cat, only to collapse after standing back up. I stayed in the bushes and watched as men went to where Mani lay, picking him up, and carrying him away. I watched, unmoving. I should have gone after them. But, I didn’t. To pursue them would mean failure. Failure of the hit. Failure was not an option. I would keep the ruined mask, stitching it back together with red thread, leaving three red slashes across the front. It was to be my reminder. Mani… I did not see nor hear from Mani for years to come. And then, one day, out of the blue, he would return. His body dead, kept alive by nanotechnology. His memory erased, with no recollection of the past. Through some faint glitch, though, he remembered me, but did not know why. I took this as a sign. A way to bury Kiana in the past, and to move forward with my brother. This, however, was not to last. Mani… Somehow, the corporation called Omni-Tec, the very corporation that stole Mani’s memories, returned them to him. The same hatred, the same anger, the same rage from so long ago had boiled back to the surface and focused directly one me. And at the height of this red haze of anger…..nothing. Mani disappeared. Only to surface, every now and again, to torture me. The last time he resurfaced, was to deliver a young woman named Samantha to the warehouse from which I normally operate. He nailed her body to the floor, driving a railroad spike through her chest. That night, we fought on the rooftop of the warehouse. Not really fought, but spared. I threw a katana blade in his general direction and he responded by hurling a mass of throwing knives at me, several of them digging into the flesh of my arms and legs as I ducked to avoid them. He disappeared into the darkness once more, vowing his return. And now, he was back. Mani… I pulled the cloth mask off of my head, uncovering my scarred face. I ran my hand over my skull-cropped black hair. God damn you, Mani. Problem upon problem upon problem began to pile up before me. First, the ever continuing conflict with Quartz, my father. Then, having to babysit Lisha in Brian’s absence. The reemergence of the Harvesters. Granted, I am the Harvester of Death. However, the Harvester’s do have a code of honor, and striking a fellow Harvester is not in the rules. And now, Mani. Mani, who would now challenge me, calling me out for my mantle. The Life Taking, Widow Making Silent Wind…. of Death. I pulled the mask back over my face, concealing all emotion behind it. The code. The code of the Harvesters. Limiting my options, containing me but leaving them open. By these rules, I was destined to fail. Failure is never an option. I would have to prove him wrong. Him. Father. Quartz. Friend. Salaok. Brother. Mani. I would prove them wrong. FADE _________________ Sleep child... Sleep the eternal sleep... Sleep the forever sleep...
