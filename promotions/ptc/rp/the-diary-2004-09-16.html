The Diary

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 I know it was wrong of me. I know I should never have done it. And I know the repercussions that might come from this. It all happened by accident, when during my cleaning I accidentally discovered what appeared to be a secret room. The room was the complete contrast to the rest of the rooms in the mansion. The room was surprisingly small, and shockingly unfurnished. In fact the entire floor was bear except for one small coffee table, on top of which lay a black padlocked leather book. In my accidental discovery of this room I tripped on my untied shoelaces and collided with the coffee table on my descent down to the concrete floor. The coffee table itself remained undamaged but the padlock on the black leather book unfortunately broke. In my hast to cover my tracks I accidentally kicked the diary open as I lifted the coffee table up. After about half an hour of a mental conflict based on whether I read the diary or not I eventually decided it was wroth risking my livelihood to understand the numerous mysteries that surrounded my master. The temptation of the answers to the millions of question was too much for me to refuse. It seemed my master had begun his diary on a pivotal day in his life, the day of his tenth birthday and the day that mysterious voice my master has often mentioned was first heard… I never really wanted to start a diary, never saw the point but something today inspired me to start one. Today was my tenth birthday, and following a ridiculously hard day at school I arrived home to find my supposed friends and parents had planned a surprise party behind my back. As I stepped through the open front door I was greeted by idiotic smiling faces, a homemade “Happy Birthday” banner and a horrible whiff coming from the kitchen. As the excited mob of friends, family and neighbours all eagerly chatted to me I found myself suddenly wearing a hideous homemade birthday hat which someone had placed on my head. I was paraded into the dinning room where the older people smiled as they looked at the “birthday boy”. I found out that the whiff belonged to my mother’s roast chicken which must have suffocated to death from the stench that surrounded it. As usual for my birthdays the rather disgusting meal was followed with me blowing out candles on what looked like a cake which someone who had never seen a cake before had baked. Apparently “Aunt Joyce” had worked extremely hard on it, if she did I feel great pity for her for she must truly be terrible at baking cakes. Meanwhile my father who has seemingly gained a paranoia when it comes to flames had lighted the candles, apparently it had taken him an hour. The paranoia to flames his odd considering he used to be a coal miner and the sight of flames used to be a welcoming sight, compare that to now where he urges me never to use fire. He seems so sure they are evil. As if he knows that the flames are dangerous, when in reality he is only clever enough to know his own name. However in what was a terrible day one good thing did come out of it. And that was admittance. On my walk home from that hideous place called school a beautiful and elegant voice embraced me. It told me nothing but the truth. It revealed to me how everyone around me was waiting to stab me in the back with a knife. And I know that what that voice said was true. Though it was certainly a surprise, it at least was good surprise unlike my surprise birthday party. Well, I can imagine how hearing that mysterious voice for the first time must have been a shock for my master. His father’s paranoia of flames is certainly interesting, especially with what is about to follow. In fact I’ll flick a couple of pages, I would love to read his thoughts on that incident. Today was perhaps the greatest and most monumental day in my life. Today I surprised all those betrayers that were waiting to stab me in the back. Today I went behind them, and stabbed them in the back first. It all started earlier tonight when the voice beckoned me to the forbidden forest. In a clearing is when I first saw her. She was a skeleton which was surrounded by both wind and a blue glow, flowing from her head was long silver hair. She looked like something out of my nightmares from when I was a little child. From those days that I was certain there was something in the dark. As I stood there, paralysed in fear, she helped me. At first my body was wrapped in unimaginable pain, I closed my eyes and screamed into the night and then…it stopped. The pain vanished, the worries and concerns that flooded my mind disappeared into the night. As I opened my eyes I saw the true side of her, she was beautiful. An elegant creature that had helped me. No longer was she a creature of nightmare, instead she was a pale female, draped in a white cloth and nothing else, her silver hair had turned black and she kissed me gently on the cheek. “I promised I would make it more better, but there is still one thing left to do. You must wipe away your past. And to that you must use these,” suddenly a can of gasoline and a torch which was already alight appeared in her hands. She handed them to me and nodded her head. And without much more persuasion I did what I had to do. It was simple, easy but so fulfilling. The internal hunger that I felt would be wiped out. The night was silent as I poured the gasoline around the village, I simply let the flame travel onto the gasoline and in a matter of seconds the small village was encircled by flames. The night was suddenly filled with activity as women screamed and babies cried. The first house I reached was he one that belonged to my Aunt Joyce. She was so deluded, she thought I was scared of the flames and hugged me tight, trying to calm me down. It was then that I saw it. A small vein pumping in the side of her neck, and suddenly I pounced. Afterwards I gazed down admiring my handy work as I saw her lifeless pale body lay still and silent while I stood there with her blood smeared across my lips. With gasoline still left over I lit her corpse and watched as the house set alight before making my retreat. The rest of the betrayers were as simple as this. All except for my parents. It was my mother that greeted me at the door, she had seen what “monstrosities” I had done and she had tears running down her cheeks. I could have finished her within seconds but then I realized that as great as tasting her blood would be, I would rather let her suffer. I pierced her neck, the blood slowly trickled out. She tried to slap me and would have succeeded if the voice had not have warned me, I managed to duck her hand and pierce her neck several times before pushing her to the ground. Then my father came out. He stared into my eyes and I stared into his, we both seemed to be daring the other to make the first move. But instead my father nodded at me and walked toward my mother. He held her tightly and then turned his attention back to me. “Do it! Set us alight! Destroy all of this!” He challenged. Unfortunately I called his bluff, minutes later I was watching from outside the house as the flames engulfed it. The last visual of my parents was them staring into my eyes. My mother with a look of shock and horror and my father with a look of acceptance. Hideous. Surprising. Traumatic. All are words I would use to describe this. It seems the voice was controlling him for the writing seemed different to what I would expect from him. Having known my master for the past several year I know what he is like now and it is completely different to what he was like then. Or is it? Recently my master did something surprising, something no one was expecting. It all happened at the WWA Labour Day Brawl pre show, it all started with an argument between Mr. Darkness’ former mentor Shaman and Devils Own member, Quietus… Mr. Darkness: Shaman, Quietus neither of you want this to happen; it is not worth fighting over a petty squabble. Both of you relax. Right, Quietus and Shaman, Rocky gave me a leaflet on why not to fight earlier. Please read it before you make the wrong decision. (Mr. Darkness turns to Shaman and calmly talks to him while giving him the leaflet, obviously trying to cool him down. Quietus simply pushes Mr. Darkness away and walks right up to Shaman. The two yell at each other as Mr. Darkness frantically tries to get them to stop. Once again Mr. Darkness moves to get in-between the two but this time they both push him away and then Quietus pushes Shaman, and Shaman of course returns the favour. Quietus simply smiles and raises his middle finger at Shaman who slaps the taste right out of Quietus’ mouth.) (Shaman begins to hand it to Quietus, and then Mr. Darkness smiles.) Jimmy: What the hell. (Shaman turns his attention away from Quietus to see the brawl on the outside, only to have Mr. Darkness meet him full in the face with the metal briefcase!) Jimmy: Good God! (Mr. Darkness bends down and picks up the fallen briefcase, as he does so the camera gets a close up shot of Mr. Darkness’ face; there is madness in his eyes and a sick demented smile on his face. Suddenly Mr. Darkness turns to Quietus with the briefcase still in his hands and the crowd yell their approval as Mr. Darkness lifts the briefcase up into the air, ready to nail Quietus over the head) (Rocky suddenly races down the ramp, he fights his way through the chaos on the outside and clambers into the ring. He immediately steps between Mr. Darkness and Quietus and yells at Mr. Darkness.) Jimmy: Rocky is accepting Mr. Darkness’ apology and is trying to consol him here, good job too, Mr. Darkness went psycho just a few seconds ago. (Mr. D thanks Rocky and then turns to leave the ring but then punches Rocky in the face.) (Ian Tense and Lockdown step into the ring having finished with Genesis and watch with Quietus as Rocky and Mr. Darkness tear each other apart, then DO begin to laugh at the scene.) Jimmy: I really don’t have a clue what it going on here. (Suddenly Lockdown steps forward, somehow controlling his laughter and taps Rocky on the shoulder, Rocky turns around only to be met with a devastating big boot! Lockdown suddenly stomps away at Rocky as Mr. Darkness looks up.) (Mr. Darkness stares at Devil’s Own and he bends down and picks the briefcase up, he holds it in both hands and steps towards Ian Tense, his face shows no expression as he nears the evil that is the Devil’s Own) Jimmy: Is he crazy? (Suddenly a smirk spreads across Mr. Darkness face and he hands the briefcase to Ian Tense, who immediately takes it and opens it. The camera catches a shot of the inside, the briefcase is filled with notes. Tense nods his head and reaches out his hand, Mr. Darkness shakes it.) (Mr. Darkness suddenly turns back around to stare at Shaman and then signals for Ian Tense and Lockdown to hold Shaman up) (Mr. Darkness then slaps Shaman right across the face. As Shaman shakes the cobwebs away from his head Mr. Darkness grabs Shaman jaw and yells obscenities at the Genesis leader. Shaman simply looks at Mr. Darkness and relies with two very choice words, Mr. Darkness shakes his head and suddenly lets fly with several hard punches. Then Mr. Darkness runs back, getting extra momentum of the ropes and knocks Shaman down with a huge and final right hand. Finally Mr. Darkness lifts Shaman up by the hair and simply tosses over the top rope and to the floor.) (Mr. Darkness suddenly points towards Rocky and both Tense and Lockdown lift Rocky up. They hold him up as Mr. Darkness hammers away at Rocky with closed fist after closed fist. On the twentieth shot Rocky’s forehead becomes lacerated but still Mr. Darkness refuses to stop as he continues to send blows into the wound. Mr. Darkness then winds back and sends a huge fist into Rocky’s forehead, Tense and Lockdown finally let go and Rocky falls to the canvas lifelessly.) (Mr. D then picks up the microphone, bends down so his face is in Rocky’s and raises the microphone to his lips.) Mr. Darkness: Turn into the light? You my friend just turned into a world of eternal darkness, courtesy of DEVILS OWN! (DO laugh as they watch Mr. Darkness rub his hands in Rocky’s open wound and as he laughs menacingly as well he smears the blood on Rocky’s chest, writing two letters which would make anyone’s heart skip a beat, “DO”. Mr. Darkness slams Rocky down having branded him and stands above Rocky, arms raised as he stares at Ian Tense, the devil himself.) Looking at my master’s life it seems full of surprises and full of traumatic experiences. But then I also get a sense of a circle, almost as if my master has returned to the wicked ways that lead him to doing such monstrosities on that fatal night. And there is something else, something which perhaps could be viewed as good news. My master has lived a life of surprises, and what bigger surprise would there be then Mr. Darkness winning the Global Turnbuckle Tournament? I think my master’s run in the tournament will be a long and surprising journey, and it all starts with the first victim, Mr. Fellar.
