Shades Of Gray: Episode Two: Beyond Control

27 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-27 "That's cold, bro. I'll leave you alone, just don't call me when you need help." The words hit Bobino like a cold, hard bitch slap. Before he could process what just happened, the steel door slammed shut, and Bobino was left alone, again. He stood there stunned, not quite sure what to think, or how to react. His life was crumbling around him, and he honestly had no control over it. He suddenly lashed out, kicking the nearby chair over trying to regain control of his anger. At this point, it was the last thing he could even remotely control. He ran his hands through his hair, trying to think of when he last had control over anything. It all leads back to him. It was him, Bobino knew it, now he just had to prove it. Without proof, Bobino was no better than him. He lifted the discarded chair, setting up and plopping himself down to scour the past for the evidence, because without evidence, Bobino was just another lunatic. You know that feeling where you know something's out of place, but you just can't seem to touch it? Well, imagine having that feeling in every room you set foot. Imagine how it would feel to think everything everywhere has been set up specifically to lead -YOU- somewhere. Imagine how it would feel to have the smallest details of the world changed, simply to see if you notice. ---------------------------------------- Bobino sat in the crowded room, completely oblivious to the conversation around him. He knew that some higher-ups were talking about ratings and other near-meaningless shit, but he wasn't interested. What stole his attention was the abnormality in the document passed around. Bobino focused on the sheet, almost seeming interested to the unknowing witness. His eyes widened a bit, as he finally noticed what had been pestering him. There was a spelling error. No big deal, one of the paper-pushers might have been in a rush and missed it. The problem was that it was a pretty obvious error. Bobino re-read the paragraph about the spike in ratings for the "Custom Khaos" division. The choice of words to misspell and -HOW- it was misspelled almost seemed to be deliberate. Bobino started shaking his head, and quietly tried to deny the thoughts that lurked in the back of his mind. He attempted to keep his unsettled feeling to himself, but suddenly realized that on either side of him, Majin and John Covel stood, holding his shoulders as if trying to keep him under control. Majin nodded in apology to the people talking, as he brushed off Covel and dragged his partner into the hallway. "What the fuck was that, bro?" Majin had never been one to beat around the bush. Bobino stared back at him, not sure of what he meant. "What the fuck was what, Maj? I don't know why you just pulled me out of there." Bobino looked at Majin, wondering what had happened. "You just started talking about it not being possible, and how he was here. You were talking some crazy conspiracy shit, bro." Bobino blinked a few times, before shaking his head in defiance. "No, I didn't. I'd remember that. There's no way I'd not remember something that happened a few seconds ago. What kind of drugs you think I'm on? That's not possible." Majin, in an attempt to calm his partner placed a hand on his shoulder. "Listen, forget about it, go sit in the locker room, I'll fill you in on what ya missed. We'll talk about this later, alright?" Bobino didn't respond, not because he didn't believe his C.H.A.O.S. Brother in arms, but simply because it was too hard to admit such a large weakness existed in himself. There's no way he could've let an innocent spelling mistake send him over the edge. He walked down the hallway, trying to make sense of everything. He entered the locker room, and looked through his bag. He dug towards the bottom, and pulled out a tattered, old looking photograph. He looked down at the picture, and questioned everything. "Why? Why won't you just stay where you belong?! Leave me alone. Stay out of my life. I left you behind for a reason. Stop ruining my life. Listen, I can understand you being bitter, but you need to understand, it was the best thing for both of us. You got what you needed, and I got what I needed. You know damned well you would've done the same thing!! Mom even told us, if it doesn't kill you, it makes you stronger, I was just making us stronger. Why won't you just leave me alone.?" The anger and passion in Bobino's words almost made him believe he was speaking to the cause of his paranoia. Instead, he gripped the near-discarded photograph in his hand, and became more angry everytime he realized he was speaking to a piece of photo paper. With all this going on, the other voice in the room startled him, breaking his believed personal monologue. "Ja'know, every time you let it change your life, it wins." Bobino spun around, shoving the picture into his bag, trying to hide what was going on. He stared up at his partner. His only friend, Majin looking at him, trying to figure out what was wrong with his teammate. "I don't know who or what's got you so on edge, but you need to realize the best way to combat it, is to ignore it. Something's obviously in your head, and the best thing you can do is to let it go, and keep on like normal. You dont want to let things out of your control get the better of you." Bobino shunned Majin's advice, refusing to believe what he knew to be the truth. "Nothing's 'In my head'. Nothing's changing my life, or altering how I work. I honestly think that I'm not doing anything out of the ordinary." Majin stepped forward, realizing that he may have to take a more harsh stand point to get his point through. "Is screaming in the middle of meetings normal? Is staking out the locker room with a weapon normal? Bob, you've been anything but normal lately, and if you let me help you, we can shake whatever's got you in this funk." Majin tried to console his partner, but Bobino pushed his hand away, and created distance between the two. "Leave me alone!! I don't need your help!" Bobino regretted the words the moment they left his mouth. Those aren't words you can just take back, especially to the only person that seems to care about your slow downfall into lunacy. Majin got the hint, he backed away, and made for the door, leaving Bobino alone. The thoughts seemed to come full circle, giving Bobino a small dose of deja vu. The next words hurt him as much as ever, even as the words replayed in his mind. "That's cold, bro. I'll leave you alone, just don't call me when you need help." The door closed, and Bobino's anger got the best of him. As Bobino sat, pondering the entire escalation of events, he dropped his face into his hands, and realized how quickly he was becoming the very weakness he hoped to amputate. He choked back tears, as he himself, and apparently whomever could hear him a simple, yet complex question. "Why?… Why?"
