Do you really know anything?

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 An Introduction Things are not always what they seem, are they? Take for instance this match up for GTT5: Danielle Northsky versus Christian Zenith. The underdog versus the favorite. It's always got to be that way, doesn't it? On one hand you have the man whom everyone else wants to win this match. Or shall we say they think he'll win. They're looking at his name value alone. Sure he's held the Global World title twice, but truly, does that make him really the man who will undeniably beat this woman? The rookie versus the veteran. The classic David versus Goliath match up. When it comes down to it, your name can only get you so far. The question here, is just how far can the lack of one take Ms. Danielle Northsky. How can that decision be made when there is not much known about her? What everyone on the outside knows is that she's a twenty-one year old woman from the Black Hills of South Dakota. She's a full blooded Sicangu Lakota, born on the Rosebud Sioux Indian Reservation. The daughter of Daniel and Lynn Northsky, she was orphaned at a young age and spent most of her early years growing up in a Catholic orphanage. At the age of 12, she was found and adopted by her grandfather. From that time on, she worked at finally learning what the real world was about. A few years later, she was accepted to the West Wind Academy, run as a front for the Order of the Hammer to train fighters. This is the same academy that has had many greats in this sport teach at it. Most notably, Dani's advanced instructor: former BDW and SCWF World Champion, and former PTC Extreme Champion, Sean McClancy. She doesn't come off of some low grade stock, that's for sure. She's got her talents, and her exotic looks that are making sure that she's taken care of. From her photo shoot in FHM last year, to winning wrestling titles, to getting a dedicated boyfriend in Joshua Brand… Dani's doing pretty well. As far as you all know. But behind the scenes, it's not all fun and games. It's not all cocktails and dancing. It's not all cashing a paycheck and enjoying life. There is more then meets the eye even with this woman on the genetic level. She's anything but normal. In fact right now people are mostly taking conjectures as to just what she is. Evidence points to the fact that in her past she has a major Sioux hero in her blood line. Six generations ago, the Sioux hero, Fallen Star was about the earth for her. Fallen Star was an immortal by legend, a giant, a warrior. That shouldn't have been anything major, things only happen on the seventh generation, right? If that's the case, then people forgot that Fallen Star had been born from a human woman and a father who was one of the Wakan Takan, one of the stars of the sky. Morning Star was his name, a man from the sky who came down and took a human woman for his wife. But what are these Wakan Takan? Right now if you ask most of the Order's research specialists… they're something that shouldn't be. Angels. Yes, the feathered wings, the halo nimbus about their body… angels. Something that should only be in the Christian teachings. There are stories in the bible about what happens when Angels mate with people. Giants is what they were called… Great Men. Warriors who knew no equal. Everything in the legend of Fallen Star equates that he was one of these "Gibborim" or "Anakim" by nature. To use the biblical names for their race of half breeds. What does this leave Dani? Why would this effect her? A little known fact of Genetics. Dani is no giant. She's barely five and a half feet tall. Very proportionate for a woman of her build and exercise routine with two exceptions, both of them on her chest. So what is she? Well the now two times that Dani has manifested her other side, it's been obvious. She's a throwback not to Fallen Star, but to Morning Star. She's an Angel that was born on earth to human parents. The seventh generation from a particular parent has always shown signs of being a throwback to a previous genetic host. In this case, evidence points to it being an Angel who was a member of the holy host known as the Virtues. One who's name meant "Star of God" and used the Morning Star in the sky as his symbol in the sky. His name: Kokabel. Where does that leave this young woman trying to make a living for herself? She's already gone through her rebirth and has manifested her wing, not once, but twice. She can't control it yet, and it's not like there is really anyone around who could show her how it's done. Then again, how does it come about that fate would put the most basic powers of an angel, wings and the potential power of flight onto a woman who's deathly afraid of leaving more then a few stories above ground… Interlude or Prelude? Dani found herself kneeling in a field, looking out into the darkness of the clear night sky. She could see the stars above, serving as the only illumination for the snow covered ground of the Black Hills. The moon as non-existant in it's new state. A large black disc could barely be made out as just a place that was void of any small lights from the far off stars up in the Heavens. She recognized the area immediately. It was an area that she had used to hide from her cousins when she had just come to live with her extended family. She had picked up climbing the rocks and that around like one of the billy goats her great aunt and uncled kept. No one else could ever catch her when she was trying to not be caught. She smiled as she remembered that this was also the first place where she had let a boy from a short lived romance in high school catch her one spring evening. Memories of good things. But a question lingered in her mind too: Why was she here? The last she remembered, she had rolled over once she had heard Josh slip off to sleep back in their bed in Las Vegas. But this wasn't at all what she expected. Looking around, she couldn't find anything that even resembled a clue as to what might be going on. She felt the cool air on her body and looked down. She appeared to be dressed in traditional, barely there, clothing for her people centuries ago. She'd done this before, she knew that. But this time there was something different. Speaking, with no one to say anything to, she tried collect her thoughts. "Okay, this is the last time we do Sushi and Sake late night. I thought 'Toxic Hell' dreams were bad." A few steps forward was all she could handle. Then she heard it. A sound she had heard before in a similar dream. The sound of something in the air, coming down towards her on a pair of large wings. She knew this part… She ran. She ran for her life. She knew that if the large eagle caught her, she would end up just like all of it's victims. She knew she had to run to the point where she's meet her ancestor, Fallen Star, standing there in all his glory with his giant bow. There he would shoot down the eagle and all would rejoice. She continued to run and run. Down the path in front of her, heading to what appeared to be the old cliff that she and her friends used to climb up and down to find gold deposits that had long been forgotten. Where was Fallen Star? Where was her savior for this dream? She stopped, almost at the end of the path. A few more strides and she would have ended up falling down the edge of the cliff. She could hear the sound of beating wings still. She chanced a glance over her shoulder behind her… It wasn't a bird, it was something else. It had large wings, many of them. It was coming right at her with outstretched arms. She didn't recognize the face or the shape of his body, but it was a man. His long white hair flew out at odd angles from his head with the wind currents. Fight or Flight… Fight or Flight… Fight or Flight… Fallen Star wasn't there this was very different. She chose the later choice and threw herself over the edge, trying to get some distance on the jump and will her own pair of ashen gray wings to come out on their own. "What the hell." It was only a dream, right? It's not like she could really get hurt. She swallowed with gulp and looked back again. It was still coming. It was time to act. At that, she bent her knees and jumped. But jumping also meant falling. She fell, head first into a dive, headed for the rocks down below. If it wasn't a dream, she'd have crapped her loin cloth by now. She watched as the rocks below rapidly approached her, and it wasn't something that she could hide in any way except to close her eyes and wish harder to get out of the situation. And then it came… The feel of slowing down, of being able to change the direction she was falling in. It was a surprise to her as she opened her eyes and looked down. Big mistake. Arms and wings flapping, feathers flying off, she tried to get her balance and try to head off at a vector to escape her incoming attacker. But she couldn't. Looking down had set off the usual dizzy spell she had whenever she was up high. She could feel the muscles in her shoulders tiring as she was making more like a poor imitation to a humming bird instead of the kestrel that her wings mimicked. Leaning forward she tried to get some forward momentum as the dizziness got worse. She finally stopped flapping and just straightened things out. At that, she launched forward at a slight dive, gaining speed as a glider instead of a flier. It wasn't to last though. She'd wasted too much time in her gamble. She felt the pair of outstretched hands grab the upper edge of her wings, about a foot or so from the shoulder blade where they protruded. She looked back at the assailant, looking into his golden eyes surrounded by the darker skin of the natives in this area. A big man, bigger then her. More powerful. Someone who was already known up in heaven as a source of power. He had to be. She felt the breeze on her back from his multiple pairs of wings flapping. She tried to think of how many pairs he had and fall back onto her old Catholic school teachings. Three… Three pairs of wings. One to cover their head, and one to cover their feet. The final one used to fly about the Lord as they sung his praises. In their arms they bore large swords to carry out the Lord's will as his warriors and knights. This was a Seraph, the highest of the holy choirs. Well outranking Dani's supposed position as the daughter of a Virtue. She watched his mouth move as he cursed her. "Oh, little angle is afraid of flying? Figures one of Kokabell's whelps would be nothing but a coward! Well you know, daughter of the Morning Star, it's not the flying that gets you… It's the landing!" At that, she felt the most intense pain she had ever felt. Even worse then the pain from her awakening into the world of the Immortals, her second birth. On that night the wings had manifested for the first time, and her body had gone through all of it's changes it needed over the course of a few hours. But now, in the course of a few seconds those very appendages were ripped forcibly from her back. As she let out a scream, she fell again. This time there was nothing to stop her decent, but the rocks below. Challenges Dani awoke with a start, shooting right up in the bed, almost tossing the comforter and silken sheets to the floor. She caught herself as she did, making sure that she didn't uncover Josh as he slept. She looked around really quick, making sure she was back in her room. Then it hit her. Covering her mouth she jumped out of bed and headed towards the bathroom. She knew others would hear, but modesty wasn't a paramount concern right now. Wearing only the blue lace teddy she'd worn to bed she made it to the toilet just barely. Apparently her inertia sickness to flying could carry through, proven by the mess she made in the toilet as the sushi from that night's dinner was expelled. In the most unladylike way, her body tried to recover from what it thought was real. The feeling of a slight burning sensation in her shoulders didn't help either. After throwing her dinner up, Dani stayed there, making sure everything was going to be okay. At that point loving voice came from the doorway. "You okay?" Using a hand towel to wipe her mouth, Dani addressed her lover. "Fine. I guess the tako sushi didn't set well." Josh nodded. "I can see why, it's fucking octopus. That's not food." She smiled wide at him in an attempt to keep her cool. Josh was only half native, from Washington State. He'd grown up in Seattle, not on the reservation like his half brother, Gray. Sometimes he really was clueless as to things. His own people used to eat pacific giant octopus when they could get it. "And we won't even mention what you guys tricked me into eating." She smiled again. "Uni is just sea urchin." "Yeah, the parts of it that you'd not eat on anything else. That's just gross. Now, are you okay?" He reached out and touched her face, softly. She felt the warmth in his touch. "I'm fine. Just a little upset stomach. Just let me go get something to drink, maybe some milk, and I'll be fine." She hit the handle to flush the toilet and walked past him on her way to the main door to their bedroom. "Ahem…" Josh cleared his throat as a means of getting her attention. "Yes?" Josh pointed to her robe that laid over the back of a chair next to the cage that housed her Hycanith Macaw, Angel (An-hell). The bird was apparently sleeping at the moment, it's head turned almost completely around, the bill laying across it's back. "What?" Josh shook his head at her. "Gray's still awake. You're wearing a thong back there. You know how embarrassed he gets if he sees you. For my sake, please? I don't want to listen to him when he has one of his over reactions to female body parts hanging out." Josh smiled. "Yeah, well you don't mind. But out of respect for your brother, I'll cover up. Sometimes you'd not think that it was Gray who thought up the design for what I wrestle in. I know he wanted something that was functional, but was still almost traditional looking. But he sure as hell can't take anything like that around." She grabbed her robe and pulled it up onto her arms and shoulders. She was still tying the belt to it as she stepped out into the hallway to the living room and kitchen. Just as her brother predicted, Gray was sitting there, at the breakfast bar, having a cup of coffee and a small danish. Dani looked up at the clock and noticed that it was about four thirty in the morning. "What the hell are you doing up?" Gray looked across the bar at her as she got out a glass and a half-gallon carton of milk. "Studying." "Studying what?" "Your opponent for GTT5." "Who cares? It's that blow hard who parades around with the World Belt like he's a fucking peacock trying to attract the pea-hens." Gray nodded. "And he's also a World Champion, multiple times, and I'm the chief trainer for our little group. We want to make sure that you're ready." Dani cocked her head to the side, like a dog that doesn't understand you. "We?" "Blaise, Ukyo and I. Ukyo is our manager right now. Blaise is our commander through the Order. I'm the head trainer and Sergeant at Arms for this group. Your career could be made for this match. It won't be broken by it. You're too low down the totem pole for that to happen, but it could be made at this very event." "I sure hope so. I'm sick of being told things like 'maybe next time, rookie', or 'you're just not there yet.' The thing is, I know I'm ready for something like this." Gray nodded at her again. "You that confident?" She took a drink of the milk, but didn't answer. "I heard you upstairs. You'll remember that there is little I don't hear in this house. Going to claim it's indigestion? Or is it maybe the jitters?" "The JITTERS?" "Yeah, the jitters? Nerves? Are you feeling the pressure on you yet?" Dani put the glass down in front of her and stared at it. All of this time over the last year has come up to this point. In the CCWA, she had won their North American title and held it for three months, before the fed closed again. In Core she had become a victim of a piss poor contract and found that not all of the federations out there in the bigger time were perfect. Now in Global she had been a Platinum Champion, and had even converged that title with two others. She was constantly rated in the top 10. She'd seen the path of the top 10 change almost constantly as long as she had been there. But this was big. This was more then she had ever asked for in her career yet. Stuff just wasn't fair. She was moving too fast on many things. From her manifestations, to waiting for Josh to get up the balls to finally ask her the question that went along with either of the two rings he'd been given by both his and her families… to her career. Rookies don't win matches with world champions. Rookies don't carry federations on their backs. Rookies don't do anything but lay down for talent like that, right? "Yeah… nerves. I'm worried Gray. I'm worried that I'm going to do in the ring with him, just like I did twice with Jake Hix so far. After hearing all the crap that Blaise went through last year, I'm… scared." Gray nodded again. He was always an almost emotionless mass sitting there, except for when he was in the vicinity of one woman that would be seen in the Global Locker room, one Dawn McFarlane. "I take it you've seen the internet boards about this match then?" She nodded at him. "Yeah, no one thinks I can do it. What gets me is how hated this man is in the ring, and even backstage in the locker room. But no matter what, less then ten percent of them think I even have a chance. It's almost like they forget who people like me are. It's not really fair." Gray stood up and took he small plate and cup over to the sink. He slid them in so that they'd not break and then turned back to her. "You know that Zenith thinks he's the best there is as it were. You know that he's also an asshole of major proportions. You also know that he's not really that good. He can be beaten. Any man can beat any other. That is one of the rules we're taught to remember. When Sean McClancy was going up against Blasie in the SCWF, no one thought that Sean would win. He'd never been rated as one of the best. He won, he took the world title. When he won a rematch against Tyler Blaze to get his title back, he wasn't thought to have a chance… and he won it." "What does this have to do with me? Sean McClancy was and is ten times the wrestler that I am." Gray put his hand on her shoulder. "At one time, he wasn't considered to be really worth anything. You've done more in your short year in going to the big leagues then he did. You're good. You can beat someone like this. The problem here is that you're starting to believe all of his hype. The internet believes all of his hype. In the end of it all though, he's only a man. He's a mortal. And he's not perfect. He's not the capability of being such. He'll make a mistake, you can capitalize on it. He'll screw up somehow. Hell, we know he likes to look at the goods. He's got vices that can be worked on." She tossed the glass into the sink. "Why are you telling me this at this time of the morning?" Gray grinned. "Because it's the right time. You need to know that we think you can do it. You need that boost. Josh is blinded by his feelings. He'll talk you up to anyone. But the rest of us, we have faith in you. We know you can do it. You need to know this." Dani looks down at the ground at these statements. "Thanks guys. It means a lot. I don't know if dinner didn't agree with me or if it's the nerves. I'm becoming a mess. Almost as much as Josh is after finding out what Blaise and Ukyo did in Japan." Gray smiles. "He's got his own set of nerves there." "So, why this pep talk all of a sudden?" "Well, I wanted you to know the reason why Ukyo did something yesterday, when you found out. So you're not mad." "And what's that?" Gray blushes slightly at the deviousness of what's happening. "Well… " "Spill it Grayson!" Gray snarled at her for using a first name that he wasn't born with, but most don't realize that isn't really there. "Ukyo filed a petition with the Committee. If you beat Zenith in GTT5, as your manager she wants a shot against Zenith booked for the World Title." "What?" Dani was glad she had put the glass down. It would have been shattered by now if she still had it in her hands. "Why?" She wasn't one for too many words right now. "Because you deserve it. You beat him, you have earned the shot. Hix won his shot by beating you. You're a main eventer in Global. You pull this off, it's time for you to ascend to the top." Dani didn't feel well all of a sudden. "I don't…" She slapped her hand over her mouth again, just as when she had woken up. Quickly she ran off, heading for the bathroom that was just down the other hallway on the other side of the dining room. "What the hell?" Gray watched as she almost tripped over her only partially closed robe, stumbling into the bathroom. He then turned around and looked into the deep green eyes of his smaller half brother. "Really bad sushi? None of the rest of us are sick. She's got to be worried about this match really bad." Josh shook his head. "That's twice this morning. I sure as hell hope your right about the cause." "Why?" Josh just pointed at the clock. Gray's eyes went into a blank expression, remembering days to his own past, a few years back. "She's not supposed to work the same way as humans. Let alone that as far as we know, she's immortal. That means she choses when body functions like that happen." Josh frowned. "We don't know fully what she is. There has never been another one of her kind ever found. We can't use the stories of anything else to figure out her. We know her kind can get that way, it was done before the great flood." "For all that is right, you'd better be wrong little brother. Otherwise I'll have to finally put a shotgun to your head to get your to grow a pair." Josh didn't have a come back for that, not even a snide remark. He just headed off down the hallway to see if she needed anything. ~End~ P.S. To Christian Zenith: Your name can only get you so far. Remember the moto for GTT5… Don't Believe the Hype.
