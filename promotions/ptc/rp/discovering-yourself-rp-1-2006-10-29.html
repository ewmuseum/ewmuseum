Discovering Yourself - RP#1

29 October 2006

Date: 2006-10-29 _________________ Regional Hospital 1:02 P.M. Seattle, WA, USA Oct. 26/06 _________________ It's dark. Very dark, no matter how long your eyes have to focus; they can't. It was almost as if someone cut all the power to the entire nation and then blocked out the sun. An eerie feeling of discomfort and unrest begins to tingle. A faint beam of light can be seen peering beneath the small opening at the bottom of the room. Creaking, the door knob begins to twist; the sound was enough to send shivers down your spine. Slowly, the light peers into the room and then disappears again. There was someone in the room, someone or something. Angelica could hardly stand to keep her eyes open but feared even more to close them. Thoughts ran rapidly through her mind: 'what do I do?', 'should I hide?', 'where would I go?' It felt like an eternity since the door opened and the light faded again. Steady rolls of tears begin to fall down Angelica's face. That stench. The god awful stench. It was putrid and it was also this monsters calling card. Angelica knew exactly what was going to happen, since it had happened before. Taking a deep breath in, she tried to ignore that smell, that monster, she closed her eyes and just wished she were somewhere else or better yet; someone else. What had she done to be forced into this situation, where had she gone wrong? These were the phrases she tried not to think about as that horrid smell grew stronger… he was getting closer. Praying was something Angelica learned did not help her. No matter how many 'Hail Mary's' or 'Lord's Prayers' she recited before bed, that smell still returned. Now, here it was, the smell, the monster, here for her, again. Pulling her sheets and blankets above her face, Angelica covers her eyes and utters another small prayer. A hand reaches forward and grabs the blanket. The darkness makes it very hard to even tell the hand is there from this close. Angelica's breathing quickens and slowly the blankets are pulled back. Her heart rate quickens, he face slowly loses its color, beads of sweat form all over her body. The intensity begins to grow with even more anticipation; what was she going to do? Let it happen all over again, day after day, week after week? Reaching under her pillow, she runs her fingers along the cold, stainless steel object that she had been hiding all night, just waiting for this one chance to slay the demon. She knew he would be back, she knew he could never have enough. He lusted for what she offered and she was tired of having him take it. Pulling the object out from under her pillow as the covers are now completely removed. Angelica thinks her plan of action through, it seems like an eternity passes as she calculates all possibilities. The hand reaches down and then finds comfort on Angelica's shoulder. If she was going to do anything, this was her chance. She sprung upward from her sleeping position and with sweat pouring down her face she opens her eyes… "Angelica?" Kirsten says while holding Angelica's hand. "Are you ok?" "It was just a dream," Angelica replies as she wipes the cold sweat from her brow. "The same dream as always." "About your step father?" Kirsten presses. "That's the one." Angelica looks around the room. "When do I get out of here?" "In time dear. But first we have to make sure you're fit for competition. You have been on the receiving end of a brutal assault two out of the last three weeks." Angelica closes her eyes and quickly her vicious matches with Kokane for the R4GE Custom Championship play through her mind. "I'm a survivor. Don't worry about me." Kirsten takes Angelica by the hand. "Since I've known you, you've never had anyone but me to worry for you. I like the job. Besides, this is beyond R4GE now. Hence the specialist here in Seattle." Angelica nods, "I can't believe you had me entered into a tournament, and not just any tournament, a GTT." Rubbing Angelica's hand, Kirsten sighs. "R4GE is a great federation for you to work on your ability, your skill and technique but the PTC is the place where we can truly find how you stack up to the best of the best." "And this Danielle Northsky?" "You'll have to be on your 'A' game to take down that titan from GCW." Kirsten says with a look of concern on her face. "She will be a tougher opponent than anyone you've ever faced." A long moment of silence fills the room. "When we get these results back to your tests, we'll see if you're even fit to compete." Kirsten assures Angelica. Then a voice from beside the door. "Ms. Carnes? May I have a word with you?" A man draped in a long white lab coat and wearing thin framed glasses asks. "Just outside here please?" Kirsten releases Angelica's hand and follows the doctor out of the room. "Yes?" She says, the shakiness in her voice more apparent than she thought. "These gentlemen have some questions for you." The doctor replies, then points at two officers standing just down the hall. "It has to do with Angelica." As the doctor finishes the sentence, the two officers approach the room and Kirsten. "What can I do for you?" Kirsten asks. "Are you Kirsten Sylvia Carnes?" The taller, heavier set officer questions first. "Of course." "And who is that in the room behind you?" The other, smaller officer jumps in. Kirsten looks back over her shoulder and her eyes meet Angelica's for just a second. "Her name is Angelica." "We have reason to believe that this girl may not be who you claim she is." The taller officer steps closer to Kirsten, peering into the room himself. Reaching into his pocket, he pulls out a picture of a young girl, maybe eleven or twelve years old. "Does she look familiar to you?" Looking at that picture sparks a flood of memories swarming Kirsten's mind. "I do." The second officer looks at Kirsten, then at the picture. "This girl's name is Kiara Leigh Martell, she went missing in October of 1995. Do you know anything about that?" Kirsten looks at the officer and then back over her shoulder at Angelica. The picture they'd shown her was indeed Angelica, but who was Kiara? "Are you sure you have the right person? I mean Angelica was found in December of 1995 and I filed the appropriate reports with the Vancouver police department." "So you did have some involvement with this girl at that point in time?" Before long Kirsten doesn't even have time to explain anything anymore, her wrists are being clasped into a pair of handcuffs. "But I didn't do anything." "You're under arrest for the suspected kidnapping of one, Kiara Leigh Martell." "But I-" Kirsten tries to get out an explanation of some sort but is promptly cut off. "You have the right to remain silent, anything you say can and will be used against you in a court of law." The officer begins. Kirsten struggles a little but eventually gives in. "Her name is Angelica, she was found in a part in West Vancouver. I brought her to the hospital myself! I didn't abduct that girl." "We'll let the judge decide that." "Angelica!" Kirsten calls out, "tell them who you are! Tell them that you and I were going to PTC to compete in a tournament!" Angelica has gotten out of bed and watches on in amazement. She just had a dream about her past, but that past wasn't the one these officers were referring to. Just who exactly was Angelica Dominique? ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ …I believe that Angelica is hiding more than she lets us on to believe. Her behaviours have been peculiar and odd at times. After a few days of observation and testing, I've come to the conclusion that Angelica is more than she seems. It's almost as if she's locked away her entire past within the dormant confines of the brain tumour that lies with in the astrocytoma glands and cells. The swelling from this tumour has created an increased amount of pressure on the cerebellum cortex. This has given her the perception that she wants to be someone else, somewhere else. This is not uncommon in patients suffering from different forms of tumours and even this for of tumour present in her brain. She has created a world to escape into, but once there, she locked her past in the dormant part of her brain… ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ _________________ Calgary Regional Hospital 2:28 A.M. Calgary, AB, Canada Oct. 31/83 _________________ "God Dammit!" A screaming voice echoes through the small room. A moment of awkward silence passes before a dignified reply, "In all honestly my love. I think we want him on our side right now." A single bed, a single window, one nurse, one doctor, one husband and one woman in excruciating pain. The heart monitor hooked up to the woman's finger indicates a steady increase in heart rate. But no monitor is needed to see that, her face is red enough to be a good indication that her blood is just pumping. As the pain comes rushing through her body, Mrs. Martell tightens the death grip on her husband's already pale-white hand. His otherwise rather bronze colored skin has lost all color and tone in his left hand; his deep brown eyes watch on with concern and passion, but a glimmering beacon of hope as well. His eyes seemed to tell the story itself, without any need for introduction. "Mrs. Martell, I need you to breathe." The nurse says lifting her eyes to meet the woman lying in the bed before her. "Relax and breathe. Deep breath in…" she waits a moment, lingering off. "Now exhale." "I am fucking breathing!" She screams in reply. "Why is this happening?!" You'd think that to be a simple enough answer… "Jesus!" Slamming her head back into the pillow after leaning forward to meet eyes with the nurse, Mrs. Martell cries out in absolute agony. "It's going to okay honey," the tall, dark man at her side says soothingly. "This is our miracle, now. A gift." Doctor Joseph Marcel Benson, a tenured physician and child psychiatrist, also family doctor to the Martell's unfolds his arms after reading the chart on the door. Heading to the small closet in the room, he removes his long white coat and hangs it on a hanger. He turns back to the nurse and Mrs. Martell flashing a caring, warm smile. In all his years as a doctor, never had he met any child so determined. "Looks like your gift is early," probably the understatement of the century, but he continues nonetheless. "Almost a month earlier than expected. Truly unbelievable." He stops for a second wiping his glasses off on his tie. "Nurse, I'm going to need you to get the emergency prepped for this young one when we get 'em out of here." The nurse pulls away from Mrs. Martell and scurries out the door in a hurry. Doctor Benson walks to the position where the burse had been stationed and takes over for her. Looking up at Mr. Martell, the doctor nods. "It's going to be ok John. Really, I promise." John Martell had longed to be a father. Since he had married Angela they had tried many times to conceive their first child. But each time was just as futile as the last. Then it happened. Both he and Angela were ecstatic that they could together create something more beautiful than love… life. Now he stood here, his hand numbed from pain, his eyes so worried but trusting exactly what his long time family friend had said to him. "I swear to God if you don't get this kid out of me, I'm going to kill someone!" More threats from the pain filled woman. "Angela I want you to listen to me." Doctor Benson interrupts her violent spree of verbally blasphemies. "I need you to focus on breathing nice and easy, and in between breaths pushing as hard as you can. Okay?" No response. Of course not, if it wasn't in vain or obscene, she wasn't going to acknowledge it. "Angela, love?" John begins. "You can do it, this is our miracle." After thirty-two minutes of labour, the doctor was right; everything was alright. Doctor Benson rises to his feet and holds up a beautiful, tiny, baby-girl. Her father steps in and cuts the umbilical cord. With a bright smile on his face, the doctor turns to the nurse who wraps the baby in a clean blanket and takes it to the emergency room for cleaning and examination. "Twenty-Nine days early." Doctor Benson manages to stammer out while John is embracing his wife. Looking down at his watch, he notes the time of birth. "3:00 a.m. on October 31st." There was indeed an evil deed committed against the innocent on this witching hour of All Hallows Eve. This child was like no other. She was rare, even special in a way. But no one knew… no one would ever find out. ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ …This form of disorder generally begins in the late teenage years or early adulthood and tends to occur in withdrawn, reclusive individuals. Calgary, AB, Canada Oct. 08/95 _________________ "Kiara?" The soft soothing voice filled the crisp afternoon air. The leaves had already changed colors and many were already scattered across the ground. The brilliant earth-tone colors give a warm feeling to a generally cool day. Overhead, the sky is clear; with the exception of a few puffy white clouds floating high above our heads in the gentle breeze. Shining down light mixed with small warming kisses, the sun doesn't appear to be working overtime on this particular day. Yet, standing in the cool afternoon draft, her raven-colored hair dancing with the floating debris; dressed only in a short summer dress with red and pink flowers, Mrs. Martell anxiously checks over both shoulders. Squinting her eyes, she takes a step forward off her cement porch and down the two steps onto the front sidewalk. Leaning forward jus enough to peer around the tall pine tree (the same one that blocked her family room view), she plants her hands firmly on her hips and frowns. "Kiara Leigh Martell." She calls out once again, this time with more feeling and perhaps a trace of concern. "I'm not playing this game with you today, get your butt inside. Lunch is ready." No response except the discreetly ominous howl of the wind through the leaves and pines on the ground and in the trees. Dust and debris begin to swirl around Mrs. Martell's legs, almost as if trapping her in this very spot. She didn't know whether to hesitate as her first instincts had told her too or if she should attempt to let a calmer head prevail. A thousand thoughts went running on a rampage through her mind, most of them starting with the phrase: 'What if?' And as she continued to play each scene through her mind quickly, a steady stream of tears begin to roll down the side of her eyes. Glistening in the faint sunlight, each tear falls to the earth like a beacon of hope; waiting to summon the return of her beloved Kiara. True, the girl had wandered off on her own many times since she learned to use those legs of hers, but Mrs. Martell couldn't stand to wait for her to return each time. Every time Kiara left, a piece of her mother's heart fell, just like her tears. Taking a few more baby steps towards the gate and fence which separates her yard from each home on either side of her, Mrs. Martell extends her right hand and slides the latch to the left. With a firm tug, she pulls the gate open and stops just outside her yard taking in her surroundings. The streets were abnormally busy, albeit, it was Sunday she had thought to herself. Sunday's were the worst; any house near a church had a hell of a time find their own parking space. The street was alive with people standing outside the churches, holding out a hat or a case, just about anything that could hold change. Glancing for a few minutes in both directions, a look of concern finds its way onto this loving mother's troubled face. Looking closer into her eyes, they tell a story; not just any story. One filled with love, tragedy and unresolved issues. This moment, here and now; the real tragedy of her life, the one moment that she wished she could close her eyes and wish had never happened. This was the moment that she realised… Her feet are moving relentlessly, heading in a direction, any direction. Just trying to help her get to someplace or someone that just know where exactly the love of her life was. Passing the two houses just to the left of hers, Mrs. Martell is suddenly stopped. Her eyes catch a glimpse of a slight reflection calling to her while lying in the deep grass across the street in the church lot. It was almost as if she was the only one meant to see it, like fate had taken her hand to give her a clue. Sticking her arm out in a frantic rush, she cuts in front of the on coming traffic and scampers across the street. Dropping to her knees, instantly the small flow of tears because Niagara Falls. Within her hands she has a small, gold chain and dangling from the middle of the chain, a gold heart locket.
