The Search For Cassandra

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 ::Disclaimer:: Well, to sort of understand this rp, you'll need to read this one. http://www.aogames.net/4ww/roleplays/messages/962.shtml Okay, well I hope you enjoy the rp! Narrator: Don't you feel like you are empty when something that you truly love is lost? Like Robb' wife for instance, Robb is torn into two because he cannot find Cassandra. Ever since that mysterious man kidnapped her, Robb has had no chance of finding her. Robb has been searching and searching, never giving up. Robb will search the rest of his life if he has to, because he loves Cassandra so much, and if something were to ever happen to her... Who knows what Robb just might do. The scene opens up with Robb Hardy wondering around the streets of Los Angeles looking for the kidnapper, trying to find out who it was, but so far Robb has no luck. Robb is just standing under a street light in the night frustrated with his fists clenched. He has black baggy pants on, a black Kobe Bryant Lakers jersey, black Nike 20's, and a black du rag on. Robb falls to his knees in anguish and pain. Robb just begins to speak to himself. Robb: Why, why does my family always have to get hurt. WHY! Damn it! It's all my fault. It always is. I am always putting my family in dangers way! Damn it. Damn this gangsta life! Because of this gangsta life people in my life have always been getting hurt or killed. Like....... my..... sister. Robb looks down and shakes his head as tears roll down his cheeks. Robb: Why did she have to die God? Why? Why didn't you just take me? I was the bad one, I was the Gangsta, she was only 15 God. 15! She was bright and young and full of ideas. She was going to do something great for this country, but she died because of me. Because the streets were looking for me and they decided since I moved on from being a gangsta, they had to take out part of my family. That... Was one of the things that brought me back though. Everytime I ran away from it, someone had to come and mess with me and bring me back. Hell, the people that killed my sister, they're dead too. I'll admit it, these three guys were just 16 year olds. I guess they did it to get initiated into a gang, because I had no beef with them. But you never know what your going to do to people who rape then murder your sister. You never know what your going to do to people that nearly kill your brother. No, you never know. I do feel sincerely bad of what I did to those guys, even if they killed me sister, but I cannot change the past. They killed a piece of the little family I have left, so I had to avenge my family. No one understands me. No one understands why I do the things I do, and they never understand it when I stop trying to be a gangsta, but no.... THEY KEEP PULLING ME BACK IN! THEY WANT THE ANGER OF ME TO COME OUT, WELL IT'S COMING BACK OUT! THE MORE EVIL AND SADISTIC ROBB HARDY IS COMING BACK, AND NO ONE IS GOING TO WANT TO FUCK WITH ME DAMN IT! Robb is breathing very heavy, and decides to calm down a little. Robb then stands up, brushes himself off and puts his hands in his pocket. Robb looks like he is about to explode but he doesn't, because he knows if he does, many people will get hurt. Robb doesn't want to hurt anyone, he just wants to find his wife. Robb wants Cassandra back, and he will do anything..... ANYTHING to get her back. Robb just continues to walk as he begins to think to himself a little. Robb: Why do people always want to take away the women in my life. Cassandra was taken away from me once before, Veronica, my second wife who I divorced already, was taken away from me on several occasions. My little sister was taken away forever... Because her life was taken by some sick fuckers. My mothers life was taken by accident when my father pushed her over the ledge, then my father goes and blows his brains out. Thanks for ruining my life dad. Really, you did great as a father. I had to raise the family myself, my grandma did nothing. I shouldn't of took off though when I started to pursue my dream. My dream all started when I finally got into wrestling and married my high school sweet heart, Cassandra. But it all went down hill from there. I thought Cassandra had died, but the CIA needed her because her dead father was a secret agent and she was the only thing that could unlock something of his. So she left me for two years, and I remarried thinking she was dead while she was pregnant. I married Veronica, and many times she was kidnapped. Then I had Robb Jr. with her. Finally I found Cassandra again, and my love for her was too much, and I ended up cheating on Veronica. Why did I do it? Why did I have to leave Veronica? She was so sweet and nice to me and never decieved me once, but I betrayed her. I am happy that I am back with Cassandra, but no doubt I still miss Veronica, but nothing can change the fact that her and I will never be together again. Who knows, Cassandra and I may never be together again, unless I figure something out quickly. This sick person is even threatening to take my son. Why does he want to hurt me and my family? Why? Nonetheless, I am going to get Cassandra back, it doesn't matter to me if I have to search my whole life around the world, my love for her is undeniable, and I will get her back, I promise that. I cannot have any doubt in my mind, I need to be ready for anything, who knows, maybe it's someone in 4WW or PTC playing a game with me. If I do find out it is someone in 4WW or PTC, then they are going to feel so much pain and anguish! I will show them no mercy! Dave Harley, I barely even know you, but if I do find out you layed a finger on my poor Cassandra, I will swiftly take your ass out for it! Everyone is to blame right now Harley, so don't feel bad! Everyone is a suspect until I find out who took my love. Robb just continues to walk down the streets of Los Angeles when suddenly he stops in front of one particular house. Robb turns towards the house and walks up the walk way. Robb then climbs up the patio steps then gets to the door. Robb then knocks on the door and just waits there until someone answers. The door then opens and a short skinny black man in pajamas answers it. Man: Robb, what you need at this hour nigga? Robb: Smokey, I need to find out whats circulating on the street you hear me? Smokey: Yeah dog, I hear you. Come on in. Robb follows Smokey into the house and it is all smokey inside. Robb: Damn fool, what you been doing around here? Smokey: What I usually be doing! Robb: Toking it up, right? Smokey: Hell yeah fool! Robb: No surprise to me. Smokey: Want some? Robb: I'm not a stoner anymore. Smokey: Shit fool, once a stoner always a stoner! Robb: Nah, for real dog, I don't smoke. Smokey: For real? Robb: For real. Smokey: Cool then. So, what do you need to know Robb? Robb: Well, you know that Cassandra was kidnapped and I have been searching for her for two weeks now! Smokey: I feel for ya playa. Robb: Thanks dog, your a true friend. But I need to know if anything is circulating around the streets. Smokey: I haven't heard shit yet. Robb: Alright, I believe you. Smokey: I wouldn't lie about that shit man, I mean, it's your wife, people shouldn't mess around like that! Robb: Well, once I find out who did it, it's going to take twenty people to hold me away from him, because no one breaks into my home, bashes me in the face with a shovel, then kidnaps my wife! NO ONE DAMN IT! Smokey: Yeah man, that's some bullshit right there. After saying that Smokey lights up a joint and begins to smoke. Robb: Damn fool, how much do you smoke a day? Smokey: A lot, I got bushes of that shit in my backyard man. Robb: Damn. Smokey: Yeah, so hows the wrestling business going for ya? Robb: Great I guess. I am in the second round of the PTC GTT4. Just like I was in GTT2. Smokey: Yeah, but your ass got eliminated! Robb: That ain't going to happen again smokey, trust me, I got some jackass named Dave Harley to fight. Oh, and incase you didn't know, that guy that beat me in GTT2, I got revenge on his ass three times! Smokey: What you mean by that? Robb: His name was Drummond Base, and about a year later both of us ended up wrestling in the same area and I beat him three times! Smokey: Oh, I see what you mean. Robb: Yeah, that fool got lucky that one time. Luck won't be in Harley's corner though, nothing can save him! Smokey begins to snore after Robb finishes his sentence. Robb: Damn, that fool is out. I guess I'll be leaving, the little guy needs his beauty sleep. Heh. Laughing at what he said, Robb leaves the house and just walks out in the middle of the night again. Robb: Man, that cheered me up a little bit. I think Smokey may know something though, because that fool always knows something. But maybe it wasn't the streets that took my Cassandra this time. Maybe it was the thing I do to get away from the streets. Maybe it was wrestling. Standing there in shock of what he just thought, the scene then fades. Scene Fades After Robb says his famous quote, he walks off into the night, still searching for Cassandra. Scene Fades.
