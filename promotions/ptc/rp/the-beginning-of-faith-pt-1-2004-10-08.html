The Beginning of Faith (Pt. 1)

8 October 2004

Date: 2004-10-08 Faith… “With a grain of faith, they say you can move a mountain. And from the crater made, your dreams will flood like from a fountain. This is my life, what I raise my hand for. This is what I... what I stand for.” -Geoff Tate, “Grain of Faith” The one thing that you can count on to lead you through your life; to overcome the things that get in your way, to climb the barriers. But not faith in something that’s intangible. Not faith in something that you can’t control. There is no such thing as destiny or predetermination. No such thing as spirits or some wayward dieties that decided to make things, and make them bad. You can only rely on yourself. Nothing or no one else. That was the song that at one time, Blaise McClancy came out to the ring to. “With a Grain of Faith…” He lived it. “This is my life, what I raise my hand for…” It was so true. And then it happened. But just what was it? The night his own cousin taught him something called humility. Something that he found goes hand in hand with its sister, faith. Sean Mcclancy took his title, his winning streak, his knee and his pride all in one night. That was over 2 years ago. Before that, Blaise Mcclancy, aka “Hazard” had been the hottest star in one of the top federations around. It was the one that was the top of the Fedwars ladder, the top of the Revolution Studios TV ratings… the top of it all. He’d won tournements and all else. He was unstopable, and he had everything he ever wanted. Recognition… Presitge… Titles… Money… A woman… A year of recovery was what he got for that night. A year of trying to get back onto his feet, literally. After that year, what opportunities did he have? He tried to go through the UTT tourney, and he bombed out in the first roun. He tried to go through PTC’s Cruiserweight Tourney… and he bombed out in the first round. Then he tried to go to the PWW, the mother federation of the 4WW… but some low ball tactics by the 4WW and the UWF lead to the PWW fizzling. And then he lost all faith. Rebecca Fortune, his fiance and mother of his two children at the time, di the worst thing anyone had ever done. Worse then his cousing breaking his knee. Worse then all those others who had decided that hurting him would be the way to keep him down. He came home from the failed opening of the PWW, only to find her in bed with another man. And not even a man who had what he did for her, but still a man with pull in the Las Vegas area. That night, Blaise McClancy lost his faith. He lost everything he cared about. “With faith...you can move a mountain.” But then within the last few months, he’s found himself regaining faith in himself once again. He got in contact with his cousin, Fey McClancy, and she helped get him into the second school in the 4 Winds Training program. He was already a graduate of the South Wind academy, and now holds the accolades of the 4 Winds Academy to his belt. He got back into the game, and has become a champion again. Most important of all, his tag team partner and co-champion, Ukyo Kushinaga. The big sister to the 4WW North Wind Champion, Kyo Kushinaga, has become his new love. Not just a woman… but someone he can trust and who returns his love to him. But while that is something that has proven to be a key to getting his faith back, his biggest leap was a leap of believing. A leap of finally accepting who he truly was. A McClancy. A man who is from a wrestling family that never gives up. But also more then just a man. Something that none of the McClancy’s never openly advertise is their connections to the outside world. The world that humans never see. The world of fables and myths that have grown up around everyone. The lands and creatures that people think are only in the minds of children. When in reality, the children just haven’t been taught to ignore it. Where a man sees someone, like his cousin, Sean, as just a large man with an Irish Accent and flame red hair. A child looks at Sean and sees the great future king of the Tuatha De Dannan and The Fomorians; the future king of the combined Seelie and Unseelie Courts. To get where he is now, Blaise, after a lifetime of denial, had to finally accept who he really was. He had to accept that which made him who and what he is. He is of the Tautha, from the royal line; a minor noble, a lesser prince. And he has embraced that which he now knows as destiny, that which is his deed in life. And now, after his home federation has finished its tour the Pacific Rim, ending with its huge Maelstrom show in Ukyo’s home town, Tokyo. He’s passed through two rounds of the GTT4 with only his own faith and that of his lady to guide him. But this is now getting to the steeper slopes to the mountains of which the GTT has taken it’s bracket names from. Two opponents down, two matches already… only so many more to go. This time, he’s got to face a man who calls himself “The Prophet”. A religious man who comes out to a Metallica song about getting revenge for the villagers buring your witch of a mother. A huge, 7 foot plus of a man who can throw a smaller cruiser weight like Blaise across the ring with the slightest whim. A man from the resurection of a federation that Blaise was in for one whole match before it closed so many moths ago during his time of trying to recover his past. The first two opponents fell to a man who had accepted who he was. But this man, this round, it was time to shore up the defenses, and to get ready to knuckle down and get ready for facing the next round and the round after that. It was time for Blaise to go home, and to finally become that which he was always meant to be. Turren Mac Nauda Las Vegas, Nevada Mcarran International Airport Baggage Claim for Northwest Airlines. Terminal 1 The escalator was moving too slowly. Blaise wanted to get the first part of today done, and done as quickly as possible. Ukyo had agreed to join him in his home town, to do something that she’s never done before. She was going to meet his parents. None of her other lovers, boyfriends or ‘bunkmates’ as Charlene, their manager, had called them, had ever gotten her to go to meet their paranets. But then again, this relationship was heading down a road of serousness that showed that even to her it was nothing like she was fully expecting when they had first started down this path. The sex was one thing, but the feelings were entirely something else. Blaise was in front of Ukyo on the escalator, dressed as he usually was with just jeans and a T-shirt. His face was adorend with his almost tradmarked shades, and a ballcap for the Seattle Mariners he picked up as they had caught a connecting flight down to here. Slung over his shoulder was the bag that carried his electronic gear, as he had become a techno junkie as it was. Behind him, wearing a cap of her own to hide her dyed blue hair was Ukyo in jeans and a t-shirt of her own. Her own small gym bag being drug along by the wheels on it’s bottom. They both reached the bottom of the escalator and headed to baggage claim. As they then stood there, Blaise whipped out his cell phone and checked the time. “We’re making good time. My father should be here in a few moments. If they don’t piddle around with the bags, we should be meeting him in the pickup area without too much of a wait,” said the red haired man, who was trying to hide the best he could. Ukyo nodded as the baggage claim converyor started up and the lights began to flash. Quickly the bags started coming out with absolutly no concern as to their contents or anything. They watched as a few boxes came out marked “Fragile” and just slammed down onto the side of conveyor itself. Ukyo winced at the sound produced by the boxes. “I’m glad I didn’t bring anything valuable,” Ukyo had said that in her native Japanese, a language that Blaise had learned while living in Okinawa in his late teens. He just smiled and let out a slight chuckle. Finally a few bags came down. Both were of the same luggage set, with large red X’s spraypainted on their sides. Both of them reached down to grab them. Ukyo reached for her own bag, but Blaise reached for both. He and his lady both grabbed her back at the same time. She eyed him as they both pulled it away. “I don’t think so,” She said to him. “You’re my guest this time. I insist, “ he responded. The others looked on as the two argued in Japanese. Finally Ukyo ripped the bag from his hands and turned, heading for the large line of glass doors. Blaise didn’t bother waiting for anyone to say or notice anything. He almost ran to catch up to her just as she stepped out of the door into the complete change of weather that she was used to. The energy almost visibly drained from her as she was confronted with the still over ninety degree weather of the Upper Mojave Desert. Blaise comes up behind her and puts his free hand upon her shoulder, “its better when we get into the Air Conditioning. Don’t worry.” As Ukyo turned her head and looked at him, he noticed something familiar in the parking area across the street. Through all the hustle and bustle a familiar truck was sitting there… A Chevy Avalanche in black with a group of gray wolves imposed along the side, as if they were running in a pack. The Nevada licence plate “HZRDOUS” stood out like a neon sign to anyone who would be looking for it. Howver, as he looked at his own truck, Blaise could only let out a sigh as he noticed that leaning against it wasn’t his father, Conner McClancy, but instead it was an old man with dark, weathered skin. A man who was obviously well into his 60’s, if not later and had a braid in his hair that went down to the middle of his back. “Ah, shit,” Blaise slipped out. “What’s wrong?” Ukyo showed concern on her face as his face began to drop. He answered, “Leave it to my crotchedy old grandfather to ruin my day.” He directed Ukyo to the cross walk and the pair walked across to the parking garage. As they both walked up, Blaise’s grandfather tossed him a small set of keys that operated the truck. “So, the lost finally return home? They always do. Ran out of money, huh?”, The old man said as he just smiled a smile of yellow and partially missing teeth. The remains of a dip of chewing tobacco could be seen sitting there. “You going to introduce me?” Blaise took the hint and started, “Ukyo Kushinaga, this is my grandfather on my mother’s side, William or Billy Slyfox. Grandpa Billy, this is my guest, Ukyo Kushinaga.” “Pleased to meet you, William,” Ukyo nodded as she greeted him. “My, so this is the famous Ukyo. My, looks like you got a good one this time, boy. You know, when I was in the war, I used to take my leave to Japan. Some of the prettiest women in the world there. Oh, you got one with both aspects they usually lack. Got a nice butt on this one, from the looks of it.” Blaise looked on with a sour look on his face as his grandfather made a wonderful first impression. Ukyo had a shocked look on her face. “Oh, sorry. I’m a little crass in my old age. They say wisdom comes with it, but I’m still waiting for my ration. Just think, this boys father named him after me,” Grandfather Billy continued. “I know of his middle name, William. So you’re THE William then,” Ukyo asked? Billy just laughed at that. “He always brings this up. He means my full name. Blaise William Todd McClancy. Todd is the Gaelic word for Fox. So as far as he’s concerned, I’m named after him. He spent most of the first ten years of my life trying to convince me to go by my second name. So, old man, why are you out here?” Blaise glared at his grandfather, who was blatantly trying to look down Ukyo’s shirt. Ukyo looked nervous. Her normal reaction would have been to punch the man. But he was at least three times her age, and was related to Blaise. For once in her life, she didn’t act on impulse. “Your father wanted me to. He felt the need to not get invovled too much today. He and your mother are waiting for you at their home on the “Rez”. I’m to take you there, and while the lovely lady here enjoys some small talk and the parental interrogation… You and I have a date with… your destiny.” The old man smiled a wide grin on that one. Blaise’s face went long. He knew the real reason why he had come her, but he dind’t want to burden Ukyo with all of the details. “You gave me the keys, so I’m driving. Get in,” Blaise said as he opened the back gate to the currently SUV setup on the dual purpose and convertible truck. He tossed his bags in and turned to take Ukyo’s. “What’s this? Blaise, you dind’t let her carry her own bags, did you?” A look from both of them let Grandpa know it was time to back down. He climbed into the back seats. Blaise opened the passenger door for Ukyo and let her climb in. After that he went around to start them on their hour long trip past the North Las Vegas area. As the truck pulled away, Blaise added one little bit… “Well Baby, welcome to ‘Lost Vegas’…” They took the path to Insterstate-5 North and kept going towards the Moapa Pauite Reservation and Blaise’s home. On the way there, Grandfather Billy tries to entertain them by talking their ears off during the 40 minute drive. The trips ends though as we now find ourselves on the Moapa Paiute lands, also known as the Valley of Fire. The Chevy truck pulled up into the gravel laden yard of a large house, well just a little larger then the other houses around it on this little suburb styled cul de sac of homes. The house is a single story deal, looking like it’s got 3 or 4 bedrooms by its layout. In front is a large porch of stained wood with a matching table and chair set upon it. The three climbed out of the truck almost in unison, as a large red haired man walked out onto the porch. The man put his hands onto the railing and looked down upon the trio. As Blaise moved around and pulled the luggage from the back, he looked up at his father for the first time in months, and just cracked a slight smile. Grandpa Billy helped him grab the bags and all three headed up the porch. Placing the bags down, Blaise didn’t wait for his father to ask. “Hey Dad. How’s it going?” “It be goin’ fine son. Glad t’ see ye ‘ome.” His father still spoke with the stong Irish accent from his home land. “Dad, this is the woman I told you about on the phone, Ukyo. Ukyo, this is my father, Conner McClancy.” Ukyo looked up at the large man. The similarities to Blaise’s cousins, Sean and Fey McClancy were outstanding. Then again this was the identical twin brother of the man who had helped spawn them also. Blaie looked different from him though. The hair and eyes were the same, but the rest of it came from his mother. And that woman now joined them on the porch, carrying a tray of small snacks, a pot and some small cups for tea. She placed it down upon the table and then nodded to her only son. The woman was definatly the daughter of Grandpa Billy, although she had not allowed herself to become something of ridicule. She was still beautiful even in her motherly age. “Ukyo, this is my mother, Maria,” Blaise added as Ukyo was shaking hands with his father. “Good day, Ukyo. It’s a pleasure to meet you.” Maria McClancy started pouring the tea as everyone began to take a seat. Maria motioned to her father, who then took the luggage inside. Mr. McClancy pulled out the chair for his wife as Blaise mirroed this for Ukyo. As the elder McClancy’s both took their cups, Conner McClancy stared his son down a bit. Ukyo could feel some tension there. “How’s everything going for you, Mr. McClancy?” Conner looked at the tall Japanese woman with her dyed blue hair. Ukyo smiled at him. “It’s goin’ jus’ fine. Me boy ‘as come ‘ome fer a few days an’ all. ‘Owever, there are things tha’ ‘e must get done. ‘Is grandfather will be takin’ ‘im in a few. Bu’ we figured it would be nice t’ get t’ know ye. ‘E speaks so ‘ighly o’ ye. So, can I be askin’ ye ‘ow ya came t’ be with me boy?” He was straight and to the point. Grandpa Billy wasn’t kidding when he said she’d get interrogated. She knew of the secret of the McClancy’s. She knew that she was looking on the current living embodiment of King Nauda of the Silver Hand. Blaise had prepared her for this. Both of the elder McClancy brothers had been found to be the bearers of the mantles of two former Seelie Kings. The Irish creatures of legend that currently lived among men, hiding… and trying tnot to be found. Blaise was his son, and had finally after years of refusing to accept it, had taken on his own mantle within the Seelie Court. And while it was currently ruled by Oberon himself, the time would come when Blaise himself could see his time in the courts. But then again, Ukyo herself was something that wasn’t quite human to any eyes that knew what they were looking for. She also knew what Blaise had come here to do. She looked as Conner McClancy stared at his son, waiting for her answer. The air was getting thick with a tension. “It was a mutual thing. Blaise had asked me to be his partner, in the wrestling ring, and I accepted. During out months on the Pacific Rim tour, we found ourselves just attacted to each other. I must say that I initiated the relationship. In case you’re wondering, your son was the utmost of gentlemen to me and never once attempted to dishonor me.” She knew that Conner at one time had been a tag team wrestler of his own, with his twin brother. She also knew what it was that Blaise really was on the inside. A creature that if it got someone ‘interested’ in it, they would be drawn to it forever. Something that Blaise said they called an ‘infection’ upon the spirit of their lovers. Blaise didn’t advertise this, then again no one would ever belive it if they saw it. She could see the look that Conner was giving his son though. He hadn’t liked her answer. Blaise could see it too. He knew his father would think of him like his cousin. Everyone did. No one thought anything of him being his own man. Especially after that night Sean had beaten him in the ring. “Dad, I…” Blaise went to answer him, but Ukyo put her hand on his thigh and spoke up instead. “Mr. McClancy, Conner, I assure you that I initiated everything between us. I’m fully aware of your son’s heritage and birth rights due to it. I knew exactly what I was getting into. It was done of my own free will.” Mrs. McClancy smiled at Ukyo, and put her hand upon Conner’s shoulder and whispered something to him. Conner smiled. “I see tha’ Blaise is a bit of a blabber mouth then?” “Actually, his cousin, Fey, told me. She and I had a rather long talk a few weeks ago, the day before her babies were born.” Ukyo was right there with the answers, and Conner seemed to be warming up to her. “Well, if Queen Maeb thinks yer alriogh’, who am I t’ argue.” He said that with a smile on his face as he raised his cup to his mouth. Ukyo and Blaise both looked at each other, as it appeared that everything was the way it needed to be. Maria interjected suddenly. “I belive Old Slyfox is ready for you, Blaise. We’ll entertain Ms. Ukyo while you’re gone. Your Grandpa already took her luggage to your room. We’ve got her. You best be going.” “Yes, Mum.” At that, Blaise got up and then leaned over to give Ukyo a kiss. His grandfather grunted towards him as he walked by. Blaise stopped and then walked down to his truck as his grandfather was climbing into the passenger seat. “So, Ukyo, please, tell us about yourself a bit?” Maria started up the conversation as the truck started to pull away. Ukyo didn’t have Blaise here for this anymore. She had to go through with meeting his parents as he went off to meet his true self for the first time. Guess it was a first time all around.
