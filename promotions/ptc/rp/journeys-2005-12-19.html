19 December 2005

Date: 2005-12-19 Sam Wolack is walking down a seemingly endless corridor. As he reaches a red door, he looks to his left and then his right, wondering how he even came to be there. He opens the door to a small room. Not small like a broom closet, but still was able to give off that claustrophobic feeling. At the end of this room hung a picture. Wolack couldn't quite make it out so he enters this room. As soon as he clears the doorway, the door slams shut. The room is now cloaked in sheer and utter darkness. Not even a glimmer of light is coming from under the door. Wolack can't seem to adjust to this darkness. He searches for any source of light that he can find. He then feels at his waist and finds a flashlight. "Funny, I didn't have this before." Wolack turns on the flashlight, which emits just enough of a beam for Wolack to see what was directly in front of him. He points the flashlight at the picture and walks towards it. He sees that the picture was of his opponent at GTT5, a man named Salaok. "Confident looking, isn't he." Wolack says to himself. Wolack then pulls his cell phone from his pocket and goes to make a phone call. There is no signal in the room though. Wolack decided that it's time to leave and turns for the door, only to see that he's back in that corridor, which is brightly illuminated. "What the…." Sam says as he turns back to look at the picture, only to find that it's the corridor behind him. Wolack looks down to look at the cell phone and flashlight, only to find that they are both missing. "Where the hell am I?" Sam asked aloud. He walks down this corridor a little bit and decides to go through another red door. He opens the door to find another corridor. This corridor is different. The doors are all blue. Wolack walks down this corridor and opens the third door to his left. This door leads him into an arena. Fans are cheering as Wolack walks down the ramp to the ring. Wolack looks down to see that he's now in his old wrestling gear. He then feels his eyebrow to find that he's wearing a barbell piercing. Wolack walks around ringside and heads towards a commentary booth. Sitting there is ML Kurley and Harvey Jacobs. "ML, what are you doing here?" Wolack asked. "Prime, I'm getting ready to call your tag team match against the Mercs." The mention of the name Prime shakes Wolack a bit. He finishes circling the ring and heads back up the entryway to the back. "This isn't right." Wolack says to himself as he walks through the curtains and finds himself back in the corridor with the red doors. Wolack looks around, trying to figure out what's going on. He then turns and opens another red door. He now finds himself in a corridor lined with green doors. Wolack opens the first door he comes to and finds himself in a funeral home. There are two caskets in this empty room. Both are open. Wolack walks up to them and sees that his late wife and son are in the caskets. "Son, they are with God now." As these words are spoken, a hand is put on Wolack's shoulder. Wolack turns to see a minister standing there. "He will watch over them." Wolack brushes past the minister and heads out the door. Once again, Sam is in the corridor with the blue doors. "What the fuck?" Wolack starts sprinting down the corridor. No end seems to be in sight. He just continues running until he eventually starts to tire out. He stops and starts to breath heavily. Wolack reaches out for a doornob and opens it. He blindly walks through the door and finds himself in a hospital room. "Hey, Sam." Andrea Scabbia said from the bed. "I have someone that I think you should meet." Wolack walks over to the bed to see that nestled in Andrea's arms is a baby girl. "Elizabeth, say hi to your daddy." Wolack, finding himself not out of breath, walks to the other side of the bed and picks up his daughter. "I'm sorry that I couldn't be here." Wolack said to Andrea. "Don't worry about it. You're here now." Wolack looked back at little Elizabeth, and noticed that a wedding band was on his ring finger. He smiled and handed Elizabeth back to Andrea. "I'm going to go get some coffee." Wolack exited the room to find himself back in the corridor of red doors. Wolack still has a small smile on his face. The fact that he was back in this maze of doors didn't phase him. Wolack walks across the corridor and opens the red door in front of him. Again, he finds a small room, only this time it's lined with the various ring outfits that he's worn over the years. From the tights he wore as a rookie in MSW down to the last mask he ever wore as X. Wolack enters this room, and the door shuts behind him. The only light in this room comes from the lights above each ring outfit. At the very end of the room, he sees a very different outfit. One that he thinks he's never seen before. He walks to this outfit. Looks it over and starts to recognize it. This wasn't a wrestling outfit, but rather the clothes that he wore in a picture of him as a young boy. One with him, his sister, and his older brother when he came to town and was part of a wrestling show. Only this outfit wasn't sized for a young child, but for an adult. "You want to know where you are?" Wolack says to this particular outfit. "You are with the outfits that I have worn in my wrestling career." Wolack looks at this outfit even more. After a few moments of silence, Wolack turns around….. …..and wakes up.
