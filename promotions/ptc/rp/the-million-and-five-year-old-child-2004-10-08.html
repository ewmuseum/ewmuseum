The Million and Five Year Old Child

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 Quote of the Day – this one does have a point, as I think it will prove an apt description of this RP (From Futurama) Calculon : That was so bad I think you gave me cancer! (The scene opens in the luxurious apartment of Unstoppable Eugene and his tender, sweet mother. If the previous sentence seems a little out of place to you, dear reader, you might do well to replace “luxurious” with “meager,” “tender” with “malicious,” and “sweet” with an adjective of your choosing that roughly equates with the sensation of hearing nails being scraped on a chalkboard … while having your eyes chewed out by rabid birds. If birds can even get rabies, that is. Eugene and his matriarch are in the midst of having another birthday bash, presumably in celebration of Eugene’s last tournament win. The two are seated on opposite sides of a card table in the middle of living room. The table is covered with a cheap papery tablecloth, on top of which sits an open carton of vanilla ice cream with a lit candle stuck firmly in it. Eugene is again wearing a child-sized cone party hat atop his enormous head. One could say this makes him look ridiculous, but this would be redundant, since that’s pretty much a constant assumption with Eugene. The teenaged behemoth looks at the rather dismal “party” scene with a slightly confused frown on his innocent face.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : IT MY BIRTHDAY?! (Eugene’s mother thankfully doesn’t look as under the weather – translate: massively hung over – as she did last time, although she still doesn’t look too enthused over spending quality time with her son. She sits in a bored manner, calmly sipping from a plastic party cup.) Eugene’s Mother : (Smiling forcibly) Yes, Eugene, it is indeed. Again. Unstoppable Eugene~! : HOW OLD IS EUGENE NOW?! Eugene’s Mother : (Distractedly) However old you want to be, honey. Not like you’ll remember it anyway. Unstoppable Eugene~! : YAY! EUGENE IS A MILLION AND FIVE! Eugene’s Mother : That’s an excellent age, Eugene. (Eugene looks down at the ice cream and candle again, still seeming hesitant.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : IF IT’S MY BIRTHDAY, I WANT TO GO TO CHARLE S. CHEDDAR’S AGAIN! Eugene’s Mother : I don’t think we’re really welcome back there just yet, honey … or ever. Besides, until my impending lawsuit with WWE for gimmick infringement is settled, we really can’t afford to have an expensive birthday party every time you win a match. In fact, next time you might have to just settle for whatever birthday goodies you can suck out of the carpet. (His mother’s mention of her lawsuit seems to strike a chord with Eugene.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : BUT I LIKE OTHER ME! Eugene’s Mother : No, Eugene, he’s not another you; just a character similar enough to you, who preceded him by three years, that Vince had better pay me big money out of court to avoid any unpleasantness. And until then, you need be sure not to emulate the other Eugene in any way. That could jeopardize our case. (Eugene pauses for several moments, as if trying to mull this over, then points at his mother’s chest.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : I WANT TO SEE YOUR BOOBIES! Eugene’s Mother : (Shaking her head) Oh, Jesus Chri- Eugene, I don’t want you watching Raw anymore, ever. I shouldn’t even have bought a new TV after you wrecked the last one. Unstoppable Eugene~! : OKAY, MOMMY! (Eugene lumbers up and starts to advance on the TV, but his mother jumps up and stops him with a tiny restraining hand on his arm.) Eugene’s Mother : No, no, we don’t need to go through that again. (OOC: See my first GTT4 RP for the reference.) Let’s just sit back down and eat your ice cream. It’s getting wax all in it … not that you’ll mind, but that can’t be healthy. (Eugene obediently sits back down at the table and pulls the ice cream carton towards him. His mother takes her seat as well, and notices her son repeatedly trying to raise the carton to his face, but singing his eyebrows on the lit candle.) Eugene’s Mother : (Sigh) Blow out the candle first, sweetie. Unstoppable Eugene~! : OKAY, MOMMY! WHAT SHOULD I WISH FOR? Eugene’s Mother : How about that you’ll keep making your mommy proud and dominate the GTT? Unstoppable Eugene~! : …I’D RATHER HAVE A KITTY! Eugene’s Mother : You can have a kitty after you win the tournament, Eugene. Unstoppable Eugene~! : YAY! (Puzzled) WHY FOR YOUR FINGERS CROSSED? Eugene’s Mother : Because that means I’m being extra truthful. Now blow out the damn candle! (Eugene does so, with a mighty gust that not only extinguishes the candle flame, but sends the tablecloth lifting up off the table, and bits of melted ice cream spattering across his mother across from him. She closes her eyes in restrained irritation and methodically begins to wipe herself off with the napkins on the table, since Eugene certainly isn’t using them as he starts to shovel handfuls of ice cream into his gaping mouth. After making short work of nearly half the carton in about a minute, he offers the battered container to his mother in one of his sticky hands.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : (While chewing on a candle piece) WANT SOME?! Eugene’s Mother : No thanks, Eugene, Mommy’s got her own dessert right here. (With that, she refills her plastic cup with a bottle of rum that she pulls from under the table. It’s coconut rum, so at least she’s being somewhat festive. Try not to judge her drinking habits too much; having to cope with the migraine personified that is Unstoppable Eugene every day could’ve led Mother Theresa herself to substance abuse. She takes a pull and makes a satisfied smacking noise.) Eugene’s Mother : So, Eugene, are you excited about your next tournament match? (Eugene grins and nods his ice cream and wax-smeared face.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : YEAH! WRESTLING ARE FUN! Eugene’s Mother : Yes, it is are- I mean, is. And not only that, but hurting people who are mean to you and Mommy is fun too, isn’t it? Unstoppable Eugene~! : (Shrugs) IT HAS ITS MOMENTS. (Eugene’s mother looks at him in a startled fashion after this uncharacteristically coherent response. After a moment she just shakes her head, figuring it was something he saw on TV.) Eugene’s Mother : Well, I hope you get a kick out of it this round. Because your opponent, a Mr. Dallas Reeves, had some really meanie…pants things to say about us. And me especially. (Her son pauses from scooping more ice cream into his face and looks concerned.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : DID HE CALL MOMMY A WHORE?! (Eugene’s mother spits out her drink her shock, most of which flies into Eugene’s ice cream carton.) Eugene’s Mother : What?! Where did you hear that word?! Unstoppable Eugene~! : MY ROOM, WHEN YOU WERE PLAYING WITH ONE OF MOMMY’S SPECIAL FRIENDS IN YOUR ROOM, AND YOU YELLED FOR HIM TO CALL YOU- Eugene’s Mother : That’s enough, Eugene! (She laughs nervously while glancing at the camera, her first indication that she is aware they’re in the midst of a taped promo.) Eugene’s Mother : (Nervous laughter) You have quite an overactive imagination. I’m sure you just got into Daddy’s old collection of grown-up movies and had a … sick and twisted dream about it. Unstoppable Eugene~! : (Frowning) NO, I ‘MEMBER IT- Eugene’s Mother : It was a dream! And I don’t want you ever saying that word again. Well, unless you’re describing one of the countless young tramps that work in this industry. No class whatsoever, any of them. Unstoppable Eugene~! : SO WHAT DID MISTER DADDY REED SAY ABOUT US? Eugene’s Mother : Oh, that. Well, uh, it wasn’t on camera, because I guess he doesn’t want to come off as the politically incorrect asshole that he is. But trust me, I heard from … people that he said he hopes I get hit by a bus, and that you’re taken back that awful children’s home for the rest of your life! Unstoppable Eugene~! : (Smiling) I LIKED THE CHLDREN’S HOME! MISS JENNY WAS NICE, AND I GOT TO PLAY WITH MY FRIENDS! Eugene’s Mother : …OK, fine, it wasn’t that awful then. But don’t you much prefer living here with Mommy in our beautiful home? (Eugene hesitates on this, seeming to actually have some grasp of the concept of tact.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : UHHH … I GOT TO PLAY WITH MY FRIENDS! Eugene’s Mother : Yes, I know! But Mommy’s your best friend in the world, right? Unstoppable Eugene~! : UHHHHHHHHHH … YES? Eugene’s Mother : Yes, of course! So, when you get in the ring with Dallas, you know what to do. Unstoppable Eugene~! : (Excited) PLAY WITH MY NEW FRIEND? Eugene’s Mother : Eugene, he’s not your friend, he wishes I was dead! Unstoppable Eugene~! : OH YEAH … PLAY WITH MY NEW NOT FRIEND? Eugene’s Mother : (Sigh) Well, as long as “playing” with him involves breaking every bone in his body – or at least the important ones. Unstoppable Eugene~! : I’LL PLAY WITH HIS FIBULA! Eugene’s Mother : Uh, that’s actually right. Play with that fibula to your heart’s content. And once I say he’s had enough, pin him. The farther you get in the tournament, the more money we get from it. And I plan on getting a lot of money from it. Do you understand me, Eugene? Unstoppable Eugene~! : EUGENE UNDERSTAND! Eugene’s Mother : Great. Don’t disappoint me, Eugene. Don’t ever disappoint Mommy. (The massive child nods somberly. The scene fades out as he finishes the carton, and his mother puts herself on the path to another hangover.)
