Itachi Kobayoshi versus King Blueberry

6 October 2008

(King Blueberry) Appendix D :: Alexandra Pierce Appendix D :: Alexandra Pierce There are few people in this world that I find as undeniably fascinating as Alexandra Pierce, but there are a few things about her that need to be addressed before going any further. One, her last name is Pierce, and while this may not mean a hell of a lot to most people, in certain circles it’s synonymous with “Hi, I’m a giant asshole.” Her brother, Ray Pierce, and the only other Pierce that I’m terribly familiar with, was kind of a prick, and with her older brother being something of a dick it’s no surprise that she turned out how she did. Second, she was part of a group of people who called themselves ‘The Illuminati’. They worked together to close the National Wrestling Council, a company that I had called home for a number of years and the place where I had my greatest success. If it’s any indication, a guy named Wyatt Connors? – also in that group, also kind of a dick. Third, she has the strangest eyes I have ever seen in another person, and I find them to be both terrifying and alluring at the same time. They’re gray, which is just kind of creepy. People tell you that the eyes are the gates to the soul, and if Alex Pierce’s eyes are that colorless than what does that say about her soul, hinging somewhere between light and dark? They have this uncanny ability to give you the feeling that they’re staring right through you, which is also creepy, but it also makes you wonder what’s on the other side. We’ve met a number of times in the last few months, though at times I didn’t know it was her that I was dealing with. Throughout the summer she’d had me, as well as a number of other people, believing that Charlotte Ramone was a real, separate human being. I assume that some people knew the truth, people outside her Dead Man’s Hand, but if they did they certainly weren’t talking. What I find especially ironic about our first meeting, her as Charlotte and me as King Blueberry, is that we were both trying so hard to be other people. I’ll concede that her façade worked infinitely better than my own, and it was so convincing that sometimes I wonder whether there were moments when she believed she was someone else. We were going to be competing in the same match that night, and I wanted to let her know that because she was a woman, and because of what I believed, that I wasn’t going to come after her. She probably thought me weak for it. Had I known then what I know now I wouldn’t have changed anything, and I would have kept my word not to touch her. She actually tested me with that the second time we met. It happened the night that Jonathan Rhine’s career was ended at the hands of Amy Campbell. I had made it a point to talk with her, but this time it was Alex Pierce as herself but I was still King Blueberry. She offered me the LiveWire Championship, and I failed to find any hidden agenda in her words. She asked if I would have extended the same courtesy to her as I did to “Charlotte”, and again I failed to read between the lines. I did answer honestly, however. The whole thing fucked me up. I was nervous going in, and she could certainly sense that, and when I left I did nothing but throw up for the next fifteen minutes. Fortunately the third time we met it went a little better, and there wasn’t any vomiting. Because of that third meeting she is now in my debt, as we’d made a bet prior to going into a 6-person match that evening. Because I scored the pin, because I won for my team – with maybe a little help from Lance Marshall, I now have a wish to cash in at her expense. For the first time in who-knows-how-long, Alexandra Pierce is at the mercy of someone else, and I can’t help but wonder what she thinks about that. Twice since it happened that thought’s kept me up at night. Truth be told I wonder what she thinks about anything. I can’t imagine anything as interesting as getting insider her head for a few minutes and wandering around her mind. If there were a way I could Matrix my way into her brain I would do it in a heartbeat. I’m also not entirely convinced that all of “Charlotte Ramone” was an act. Oh, sure, there are rumors floating around about her sexuality, but that’s not at all what I mean. “Charlotte Ramone” was a good kid. You don’t just up and fake that. Nobody is that good. Not her, and certainly not anyone in her organization. Not that I’ve seen, at least. She’s the reason I took my own mask off, why I ended my charade a little earlier than I would have expected. Even though she’s probably got a file on me somewhere (though I doubt she’d ever admit to it), I didn’t think it was fair that if I was going to try and get to know her that I should deny her the chance to reciprocate in kind. I tend to think of myself as a pretty good judge of character, and while I’m sure she’s done some vile shit in her life I don’t think Alexandra Pierce, the woman they call Desade, the Spider in the Web, the Director, and who-the-hell-knows what other clever nicknames, I don’t think she’s as evil as we’ve been led to believe. It’s on her to prove me wrong.
