The Fight Of The Wild Cats

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 The luscious green grass stretched for miles upon miles. All the thin green blades rejoiced together and danced merrily in the light breeze. The day was perfect, the sun while out was not baking the ground and a very light drizzle refreshed the plants and the insects all of which seemed to play together with out any interruption of the human kind. However, all was not well. There was a part of the mass of countryside where the trees all huddled together, creating a small forest. Only those animals that were big enough and vicious enough dared to enter, in fact it was a common folklore around the area that a wild cat of some kind lived in there and picked off any small pray that accidentally crossed it’s path. The tale is that a long time ago there were two wild cats, both of which had escaped from a circus. At first they themselves played and danced with the insects and the plants, a big mother nature party if you will. But one day man broke the one simple law. He was a hunter, presumably from a different part of the country for otherwise he would have not been so foolish as to interrupt the party of nature. Regardless this old hunter stepped into the reserve having heard of the infamous pair of wild cats. There was of course a mass of scared animals, all of which hid behind the biggest creatures, the two wild cats. One of the wild cats was an old wise lion who had seen many a scrap and knew exactly what humans were capable of. It was he that was expected to battle the human, and courageously he did. The fight was vicious and brutal, rumour has it that the battle lasted days upon days with both creatures equal. It was about a week later that a body was found. It was of course the rotting carcass of the hunter, who had met his match. Mother natures’ children all rejoiced and worshipped their hero who was of course only too welcome to their love. This continued for weeks on end, everything was joyful and praising their saviour, the one who defeated the odds. Everything except the other big cat. That big cat was the first to venture into the forbidden forest, and there it lurked in the shadows. Desperately wanting to steal the spotlight. It planed and it schemed, and eventually it made a challenge. A challenge to the one who defeated the odds. A challenge to the other big cat. The celebration all came to a sudden halt. The whole of the reserve gathered to see what they knew would be one of the wild cats’ death. Many predicted the lion would win but the lion wasn’t so sure. He wasn’t going to take any risks and he fought as hard as he could. The battle went on for what seemed like an eternity as the two sides struggled and battled. There were cries of pain, blood lost and fur flying and eventually after months of conflict the fight ended. One side survived, the other didn’t. So was it the big brave lion that had already defeated the odds once before, or was it the other wild cat that lurked in the shadows? In other words, will it be Mr. Darkness or Nick Persia that wins the round three match?
