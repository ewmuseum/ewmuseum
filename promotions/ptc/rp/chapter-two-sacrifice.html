Chapter Two: Sacrifice

Donny Diamond Emotionless Monster Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 59 Location: My own personal Hell. Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 1:08 am Post subject: Chapter Two: Sacrifice Chapter Two: Sacrifice This chance he has been giving, wasn’t done so on a silver platter. His chance to go one-on-one with the World Champion was done because he earned it all. He has made sacrifices that most people would never ever think about giving up. True, there could have been easier ways for him to reach this destination, and different paths he could have taken. However the road he has walked has not been an easy one, because he has lost most of his friends in the process, and has spent time behind bars for the crimes his has committed. Though now we can see if it pays off, or not as he goes on to face Kaiowas himself. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~ ¤Swirling around my life is surround in darkness Deep down inside the monster is lurking Lurking around waiting to be unleashed My body is the temple where I am worshipped¤ ~`~ Old House In Desert ~`~ | ~`~ April 7, 2003. 9:04 AM ~`~ ~†~ We’re inside of the house, on the top floor, the only light is the one coming from the sun that is beaming through the windows. Standing in front of the window is where he stands, dressed in complete black the sun can be seen crossing the left side of his face. His arms are crossed in front of his chest, as he continues to stare outside of the window across the endless miles of desert. ~†~ Donny Diamond: Solitude. Whenever I wanted something I always had to do it alone, no one was ever there to help me out. For some however that is not to be, if you have a high rank in society others will be more than happy to lend you a hand. Though did you ever think to stop and ask yourself one question, what would happen if you were taken away? If you were in a situation where if you were not to be saved death would take you away, would your friends come to save you? [He opens the window, and wind immediately comes gushing in with bits of sand. Looking at his face you can see the reflection of sand on his sunglasses as he looks down slightly towards the ground.] Donny Diamond: That’s what he thought. The younger member of the Ktulu tribe. He was lost, and he stumbled upon this old shack for some rest in hope that the other tribe members would come to save him. I brought his death over him, but the reason for his death will forever dwindle in your mind Lord of the Ktulu. It was your fault he met such a sudden and hasty death, because he was waiting for you to be his savior. You were his light of hope, now he is in Hell forever wishing you were there to join him… ¤Most of you flee in fear At the mere mentioning of my name You hold onto your shame Because you know I am the game¤ ~`~ Outside of the house in the desert ~`~ | ~`~ Unknown Date ~`~ ¤Deep down inside I give you the answers Still you come and question me Never giving me a chance to prove my worth Just like everyone else¤ [In the desert mixing in with the horizon and heat steaming from the sands a dark skinned young boy can be seen dragging himself along. He collapses in the desert, and the canister that was in his hand falls to the ground as the final drop of water dribbles out and mixes in with the sand. The sun can be seen gazing down with massive heat in the sky, as buzzards can be seen flying in a circular formation waiting for the body to die. Across the desert we can see someone else coming. This person is very pale, and is only wearing a pair of cut up black jeans.] [Obviously this person is Donny Diamond. Closer, and closer towards the body he comes with no emotions on his face as the scent of flesh can be smelt in the air thanks to the heat from the sun. He starts to kneel down by the corpse, but a buzzard scopes down from the sky towards it. With a swift twist of his body he turns, and slams the back of his fist into the head of the buzzard knocking it to the ground. From the desert sand he grabs hold of the winged animal and lifts it into the air by its throat. The boy laying in the sand slowly lifts his head up to take a glance at the man before him as Donny continues to squeeze the neck of the buzzard and the bones inside can be heard snapping before he lets it drop down to the sand below.] [Looking down at the helpless boy before him, Donny grabs him by the arm, and pulls him up to his feet. Using his strength he lifts the dehydrated boy onto his shoulders, and starts to walk back towards the old house. Due to the weight of both bodies his foot prints can be seen in the sand for a moment as he steps forward, only until the wind blows for a moment causing the sand to wash away his path. Finally arriving at the old shack, he walks up the stairs, and then opens the door for them to walk inside. Without thinking he drops the boy into a banged up reclining chair, then walks towards the kitchen to fill a glass with water. Coming back towards the young boy he places the glass of water towards his mouth, and forces him to intake the water so that his body can regain some of the lost nutrients.] ¤Passion as strong as can be I hold it closed Mixed with the fuels that keep my fire going Though for some my flames should be extinguished¤ Donny Diamond: So exactly who, or what are you? Young Boy: … Donny Diamond: I see. [He turns quickly to look at the boy that remains speechless as he sits in the chair. Removing his black sunglasses he places them on the small wood furnished table before him. Slowly he looks ahead at the boy before him, his eyes begin to glow a dark red as he stairs him deep inside his soul.] ¤So you want to be the one that ends me? Wrong for role for you to be playing I am the slayer You’re the next victim in line¤ Donny Diamond: I welcome you into my home. I save you from death in the desert, and you will even answer a simple question? Young Boy: … Donny Diamond: Is this how one of your “kind” shows gratitude when their life was saved? Kid, I suggest you answer me because I am not a very friendly person when I am angered. Young Boy: Por favor não me fira que o senhor, mim lhe deve minha vida. Eu sou um do Ktulu. Donny Diamond: O brasileiro é sua lingüeta que nativa eu v. Você fala alguma criança inglesa? Diga-me, o que é seu nome? ::Translation:: Young Boy: Please sir do not hurt me, I owe you my life. I am one of the Ktulu. Donny Diamond: Brazilian is your native tongue I see. Do you speak any English child? Tell me, what is your name? ::End Translation:: ¤Want to try and switch places with me? Then come on Try to be the one that takes me down Show me your not the weak fool we all believe you to be¤ Young Boy: ..K… Kh… Khiku. Donny Diamond: Khiku, one of the Ktulu. How did you wonder away from your tribe and come across this desert land? Khiku: E… Donny Diamond: Silence! The answer for this is very easy, and simple to understand. It was all destined to happen. You coming into my hands was destined. After all does your tribe not call me the Slayer? [He turns back to face Khiku and notices him trying to escape from the room. Quickly he steps in front of Khiku and slams a closed fist into his face knocking him down onto the ground. Out of pure fear Khiku backs against the wall, as Donny puts his sunglasses back on, then proceeds to slip on his black finger cut gloves. Glancing back over to Khiku in the corner, Donny walks slowly towards him.] Donny Diamond: You know all of this pain you will be feeling, and all of this fear you hold in your heart could have been avoided. Instead of telling me the truth as to who you are, you could have said you were just a lost child. Of course not though, your Lord has showed you to be truthful. Now you see how painful the truth can be. ¤I the reaper of innocents Begs you to come forth Show me what you’re made of If you dare that is¤ [Grabbing the arm of Khiku he pulls him up, then forces him to walk forward towards the stairwell. Khiku tries fighting Donny, but to no avail as Donny holds him tightly and walks with him down into the basement. Donny presses him up against the brick wall, then turns the torch on the wall to the side as a section of the wall starts to slide open. Removing Khiku from the wall, and forces him to walk once more as they pass through the opening in the wall, and it closes behind them.] [Donny takes the smaller tribe member, and tosses him against the table fixed in the center of the room. Walking slowly towards him, Donny pulls him back to his feet only to force him onto the stone table. Using his strength Donny forces his arms down onto the table, and he latches to his wrist some chains, and does the same with his legs.] ¤Thoughts That is all you’re really made of You have no reason to attack me I, however, have more than one¤ Donny Diamond: You know what’s the funny thing about tribes? I’m sure all of the tales you heard about me, depicted me as some great beast. I am a normal man just like you. However I know where my loyalty lies, unlike you. I do not go worshipping a fool named Kaiowas, and proclaim him to be my lord. He is no lord; he is nothing but a leech that feeds off of the lives that belong to others. You want to bless him as your savior, then where is he now in your time of need. Khiku: Você não é um monster. Seu o filho de Satan! Donny Diamond: Indeed, I am the son of Satan. At least my savior is real. He has allowed me to be reborn, and I will do what I must to repay him. That price is simple; it is to make your lord feel ungodly amounts of pain. Think of it as a bargain game, Kaiowas has something I want and my father of darkness wants him to suffer. Through your death, and through the falling of the Ktulu tribe Kaiowas’ reign as Champion, and Lord shall come to an end. [Donny Diamond laughs for a second, as he can smell the fear rising from the frightened Khiku. Walking around the table, Donny grabs some small needles. Khiku starts screaming out in terror, as Donny walks back in his direction. Sweat can be seen forming on his forehead, ignoring this Donny takes the needles and slowly starts pushing them underneath Khiku’s fingernails as his hands contract outwards from the pain.] ¤I am the nightmare children have I am the fear that people get before they die Things must be done to prevent you from coming forth This is my chance, and I will not let you stop me from achieving glory¤ Donny Diamond: Does it hurt? I know it does, but the pain will continue unless you admit Satan to be your only savior. Kaiowas is nothing but a sinner that feeds off of the dreams you have. All of your dreams will be crushed because of your Lord being a self-sinner that will use your unity to help propel his ego to new heights. He believes with you around he is untouchable, however that is where I enter the picture and start to eat way at him as if I was the plague. [As the screaming continues, he ignores it as he grabs thicker needles. With these needles he places them beneath the eyes of Khiku, slowly he pushes them up till they go in the skin. The pushing of the needle increases until they pierce the bottom of his eyeballs forcing his eyes to shut as blood drops from his fingertips, and from his eyes. Khiku screams louder as Donny grabs another needle and places it above his heart and presses it in to pierce the skin barely.] Khiku: Kaiowas meu senhor, por favor excepto mim! Donny Diamond: Is he here? No. I gave you the chance to denounce that fool as your savior, and accept my father as your newfound leader. You turned my offer away, now I will silence your screams of help for all eternity. [With those final words said, he presses the needle further in as blood starts to drain from the nose, ears, and mouth of Khiku. He arches up from the table as all of the veins in his body push against his skin and his final breath is exhaled from his lungs. After unlatching the restraints, he proceeds to remove the needles from the now dead body. He lifts the body up from the table and carries it into the cell where he drops it and closes the door afterwards.] ~`’~ Present ~`’~ [Once again Donny is standing in front of the window on the second floor. Reaching to his sides he grabs both sides of the windows and closes them, stopping the wind from entering the room.] Donny Diamond: You know something Kaiowas? No matter how harsh reality gets, and how many different paths we take, one thing always remains true. In this business we all were forced to make sacrifices to get what we wanted. Some of our sacrifices may have been more wrongful than others, but still we see these times not as sacrifices, but as opportunities. Donny Diamond: Honestly Kaiowas, if our places were switched would you make the same choices I did to win the most valuable prize this industry has to offer? Of course you would, and you say otherwise we know your lying. The slaughter of innocents in order to achieve ones goal is human nature. Deep down inside we’re all animals, just some of us are more intone with it. Soon Kaiowas, I will unleash my beast within on you. And make you the ultimate sacrifice! ¤The beast has awakened His eyes have made you the target In order to gain what he wants you must be defeated You are the sacrifice that must be made!¤ _________________ DWO Record: 9-5-0 PTC Record: 1-0 -I only mark for those deserving, and you know who you are.-
