What is insanity?

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 What is insanity? Can you breath it? Can you eat it? Can you feel it creep up to your ear and scream obscenities? Or is it that stereotypical "I think I am a duck" feeling that people might have? What is insanity? What if I told you that everyone was insane? What would you do? Call me insane, right? Right? Well, that’s just it. We are all insane. On one level or another, each one of us is going crazy, or in fact already is. For example, you obsess over school work to a point where it keeps you up. That is insanity. Or that every Tuesday, at 8 pm, you become a zombie and watch your favorite show, without fail. That is insanity. You’re feeling better about everyone else, now, aren’t you? You can relate to people, saying "Wow, now I *AM* like everyone else!" Or are you? Because, you see, there is a level of insanity that you cannot even grasp. A level so deep that, if you even BEGUN to try to grasp it, you would collapse. Examples? Examples aren’t your fucking homework assignments or your obsession with that after school special. True insanity is every night and every day having that same dream. The same one that has haunted you for years. The same one that makes you keep your eyelids open until your body punishes you. The same one that while it occurs, makes you want to jump out of bed and out of the window, splattering your body all over the pavement. The same one that sparks a world of pain. And true insanity is what comes from this. True insanity is remembering the years you spent not moving an inch of your body, resting with only your thoughts. True insanity is losing your personality to loneliness. True insanity is feeling the devil slowly pull your heart out as he covers your charred body in binding bandages. And true insanity is also that urge to kill. True insanity is that constant thought of "If that mother fucker says one more thing, I’m breaking his neck." True insanity is killing a fiend but turning into one yourself. True insanity is thinking nothing of death. So, tell me, what is insanity? Do you know it? Because I can breath it. I can eat it. I can feel it creep up to my ear and whisper all those horrid obscenities. But I cannot kill it. But, believe me…it will one day kill me.
