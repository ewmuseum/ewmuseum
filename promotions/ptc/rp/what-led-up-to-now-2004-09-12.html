What led up to now...

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 ***THIS PROMO BE ON BEEATCH*** The scene opens in a dark, dirty alley somewhere in Los Angeles, California (yeah, like there's another Los Angeles, right?). There, the walls are all adorned with some kind of graffiti, or blood, or piss, or shit... or something unidentifiable and you don't even want to attempt to identify it. There is trash everywhere: old newspapers and porno magazines, used toilet paper, used needles, old worn out mattresses and other furniture, old dirty clothes, dead rats, cats, roaches, and every so often, people. There are also things lying around places like these that no description can do justice. The stench here is just awful. This is not the place you want to be on a brightly lit day, much less a dark night. For 17 years, this place was known as home for Jonathan Medina. His mother, whose name Johnny has never known, died shortly after giving birth to Johnny due to complications from Johnny’s birth. His father, a drug addicted deadbeat, raised him... if you can really say that. Johnny's father was already addicted to heroin prior to his wife's death, but afterwards, he just quit living and merely existed, waiting for his next fix. Johnny grew up on these streets, fighting every day to survive. Often, some other bum would brutally beat him, and any food or clothing that he had would be stolen from him. Johnny had to learn to fight, and fight dirty, if he wanted to live to see his teenage years. His father was really of no help. Johnny didn’t really know anyone from his family outside of his father, and his uncle. However, the last time his uncle had seen Johnny or his father, they still had an apartment. You see, his uncle was a Professional Wrestler. Known to the world as BoneCrusher, Johnny’s uncle had his first match on December 31, 1988 at ASWA SuperSlam Series. It was a one-night single-elimination tournament for the vacant ASWA World Heavyweight Championship. BoneCrusher shocked the world by winning SuperSlam Series and walking out as Champion. Upon returning to L.A., BoneCrusher seen the situation that his older brother had gotten into, so he used the money he had won from his title tournament victory to buy a house for Johnny and his father. However, soon after that, BoneCrusher returned to the road to defend his title around the world. Johnny’s father had pretty much given up on life, and basically blamed Johnny for the death of his wife. He never got off of heroin, and in fact, was on it even more now than he was before. Because of this, they eventually lost the house. Johnny’s father blamed him for that as well. Fast-forward a few years… Although Johnny had no home, he had gotten a few clothes off the streets, learned to fend for himself, and actually attended school for a short time, but he had to drop out at 16 because he could no longer keep up the act. Soon after turning 17, Johnny walked up to his father to tell him that he vowed not to end up a drug-addicted embarrassment like his father did. However, he found his father lying dead in the gutter… a heroin needle still stuck in his arm, and a trickle of blood running down, into a small puddle in his hand. Johnny could’ve notified someone about this, but he figured that his father never really did anything for him, so why should he do anything now for his father? There was one thing his father had done, however, that made Johnny a better person. By living the life that he had, he had showed Johnny an example of what Johnny did not want to become. So, having little education, no home, and an ability to fight… he enlisted in the Marine Corps. Before he could actually enter, he had to complete his G.E.D. since he didn’t have a high school diploma. The recruiters helped with this, and soon, Johnny was off to the Corps. Although his upbringing wasn’t exactly a model of how a child should grow up, Jonathan Medina became a model Marine. He became a Specialist in both Weapons and Reconnaissance, and he worked in operations including Somalia, Bosnia, and a few Recon missions in Iraq during Operation Southern Watch. He attained the rank of Corporal, and was recommended for Officer’s Candidate School, as well as SERE (Survival, Evasion, Resistance, and Escape) school to transfer to the Navy SEALs. While this sounded good, Johnny had known for quite some time what it was he wanted to do… become a Professional Wrestler. So, when Corporal Jonathan Medina completed his active obligated service in the U.S. Marine Corps, he returned to Los Angeles in 1999. He looked up a few wrestling schools, and was planning to go to the All Pro Wrestling Boot Camp in Hayward, California. However, during this time, he got back in contact with his uncle, BoneCrusher. Bo’Cru’s eyes lit up when his young nephew announced that he wanted to become a Professional Wrestler. It took some time, but like everything else he had done since he swore not to end up like his father, he applied his killer instinct to his training, and soon became ready for the ring. Johnny Medina made his debut in Professional Wrestling in 2000 in the newly reopened ASWA. While it was a loss, Medina took it as a learning experience. Fast-forward a couple years… Johnny “Blaze” Medina, fresh off his stint in the Internet Wrestling Zone, has joined the XUDF, the Xtreme ULTIMO Developmental Federation. Everyone who is planning on entering xUw must first pass through the XUDF. However, there is no exact time frame… you join xUw when you are deemed ready. Blaze Medina entered xUw out of the XUDF in record time, just over three weeks. It’s not all that surprising, as Blaze had several high-profile matches in the IWZ and through hard work, he managed to break into the main event picture. Now, he’s in xUw, and while some people have thought him a mere rookie, they soon suffered the embarrassment of being defeated by Blaze. They really shouldn’t be embarrassed, as Blaze is no easy person to defeat. However, Blaze started to feel like he wasn’t being respected, as people were writing him off… this, after defeating xUw’s Hardcore Icon DDT, and former xUw World Champ Ric Austin. So, when DDT got a shot at the Submission title against X-Factor, Blaze decided to step in and make it known that he will not suffer the embarrassment of watching a guy he had just defeated take a title. Blaze was then put in to face X-Factor, who was the current Submission Champion, as well as a former xUw World Champ. Factor, like others before him, simply wrote off this match… and ended up being embarrassed, as Johnny “Blaze” Medina ended up leaving as the new xUw Submission Champion. Still, when xUw announced their team for the Survival PPV, Blaze was not even thought of. This, after Blaze was the first one to speak up on xUw’s behalf. Yes, it did seem a little embarrassing to have stood up to some of the greats in the PTC, and then not even be considered. But now… now things are different. Now, Blaze is in the GTT4. Representing himself, as well as xUw, in a tournament full of people who have already written off the 26 year old. Not only is Blaze not a favorite to win… he’s not even looked at. Now, he could look at that as an insult, but after everything he’s went through just to get to this point, he knows he can take this… and those who stand in his way, one-by-one, will suffer the embarrassment of defeat. Blaze: “The time draws near. The grandest of the PrimeTime Central tournaments, the GTT4. So many people, from so many promotions around the world, looking to claim their spot as the absolute best in the world. Me? Heh… all I really care about is that I don’t live here, in this dump anymore. But, there is the little matter of how none of these people think I have a snowball’s chance in hell of winning this thing. Now, I’ll be the first to admit, it’s a hell of a hill to climb. There are many great competitors in this thing, many who can win it. There are also many who, let’s face facts, won’t see the second round. Token Weed, for example… all that bullshit he was woofin’ about how he’s been fighting for all of two and a half years… and he’s drawn The Shadow in the first round. Damn, he’s in for one hell of an embarrassment when he makes that first round exit. There’s also Karina Wolfenden, who’s drawn Fatbadger, the xUw InterContinental Champion in the first round. She too, will be one of the ones embarrassed to have entered this grand tournament, only to make a first round exit. But, the one I’m really looking at is the so-called ‘Immortal’ Jeremy Page. Although he’s entered as a free agent, I believe he either is, or was, a member of the Four Winds… I could be wrong. 4WW… a promotion who as of late has talked a hell of a lot of talk about how they are the greatest thing in the PTC. Well, whether Page is currently involved with them or not, I could give two shits and a motherfuck… the fact is, I’m about to make sure he suffers the embarrassment of leaving in the first round. The only thing I haven’t decided yet is how? I am the xUw Submission Champion, so maybe I should beat him with the Silent Assault Submission. But, since this isn’t a Submission match, I could just as well beat him with the Full Metal Jacket, the Trail Blazer, the Direct Assault, or my personal favorite, the Funky Cold Medina. Or, since I bring such a diverse arsenal with me to the ring, I could defeat him with any move I choose to. That really is unimportant tho, as long as I beat him, who gives a damn how it happens? Jeremy Page… you drew Johnny ‘Blaze’ Medina, xUw Submission Champion, in the first round. Be ready for a first round embarrassment, because you are about to be the first to learn, the hard way, that you ain’t even on my level. Now choke on that, beeatch!” With a sly grin on his face, Blaze Medina turns about face, and struts off down this dingy, dirty alley. He has come a long way from here, and damn sure isn’t about to be embarrassed by Jeremy Page in the first round of the GTT4. Fade… …to… …black. ***THIS PROMO IS OVER BEEATCH***
