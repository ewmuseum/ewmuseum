A lesson and understanding!

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-10 A long expanse of beach lays before your very eyes. Upon the beach is a lot of flotsam and seaweed. Turning there are several sheer cliff faces and a forest above them and a spotting of trees dotting the sides of the cliffs face. A lone man comes walking down through the bushes nearest the beach as he walks forward it is an old man seemingly in his late sixties and the man is large for an older man and holds himself tall with pride. He seems very interested in something further on down the beach as he makes a steady pace towards his destination. The man moves with easy and grace much more expected of a man 30 years his younger. The man stops as he looks towards the ocean as the gulls sound above his head and he then turns and walks towards the ocean as a vessel begins to come ashore. Three large men come off the mighty vessel as a large fur cloaked figure steps out into the water and walks forward towards the beach and stops before the old man and speaks. "What is it Svenhol you wished to speak with me?’ Looking at the large man squarely in the eyes the old breaths a deep breath and puffs out his chest a bit showing he is not intimidated. His voice comes strong and steady as he begins to speak. "Samuelsson you have come back to your homelands. Why have you returned here? You have gone on and taken your heritage to the United States and proven that you can battle all men and manner of beast. You have yet to prove that you’re a man of honor and a man of you own destiny." Samuelsson looks at the man angrily as he goes to say something the man cuts him off. Poking a finger into Samuelssons chest. "You seem to forget Samuelsson that it was I who raised your father and trained you in the ways of the warrior even though you were driven to hold up your heritage. Do not choose to believe that you are not to be humbled. There are many men in the world who can and will defeat you regardless of what you say. This Alec Lancer wants to win. No that can not only be enough. I bet he is preparing out of sight as you are angrily showing your rage and petty trinkets." Samuelsson looks at the old man and nods as his rage seems to abate. As he looks at the old man he reaches out and grasps the mans shoulder. He looks down for a second and he raises his eyes and looks into the other mans eyes. "I see your point old man. My returning here was for education and finding. I am searching for answers and come to this beach specifically for those answers. I have learned that my ancestors have come directly from this general location. This beach is a mighty historical point for our ancestors and I wish to learn something of this. Why was it simply abandoned and lost to history. Where is the mighty city that was here. Where are the ancestral markings here? Cutting Samuelsson off he then raises a hand palm up and points to the cliff faces. "It is here Samuelsson you must open your eyes and listen. I will show you what you seek and leave it to you to choose the answers you seek. Some of the answers you will enjoy and others you will not. There are answers that lay beneath the surface and then there are some that only will be found within you. Come we will begin this little journey of spirit." Turning the Old man begins to walk as Samuelsson follows and waves to the men to stay behind with the boat. The old man leads Samuelsson up through the cliffs as each step is taken with precise footing as to not fall to a soft death upon the sand, A substance soft and giving yet unforgiving in a fall. Soon they reach the top of the summit. The very top of the cliff face as they walk forward the old man stops and points to a pile of rubble as a single pole sticks straight up through the rubble as Samuelsson stops and looks at it. "A direction marker, this is the south base of the city. Why have I never noticed this before? I have walked past this area over a hundred times and I never noticed this." The old man shakes his head and then speaks. "You weren’t looking until I showed you. This was strategically placed to blend in with the landscape. It is a marker for your ancestors not your ancestors enemies. You do not stand alone in the world for your ancestors and true Viking heritage stands before you. Come lets walk further I will show you a vast city for which you have never noticed." Slowly both men walk into the forest nearby as the old man seems to turn and walk in between mounds and small stone walls as Samuelsson begins to pay attention. He notices little things the mounds were remains of buildings and hearths. The stone walls were just that stone walls. He began to pay attention more as each and every stone and blade of grass began to turn into something more something vast. His heart began to race as he watched and learned as the old man brought him further and stopped before a vast opening where the green grass that grows there is perfectly squared with the rest of the forest growing in around it. The man waves his hand in an outward motion as Samuelsson turns and stares at his surroundings as everything has become clearer to him. Looking to the old man he clears his throat. "You never showed me this as I was growing. You knew I would come here time after time and did not realise any of this was here. Why did you not tell me of this?" The old man smiles as he walks forward towards the centre of the grass and then turns looking back at Samuelsson. "Would you have truly understood the significance of the place you now stand? Would you as a child open your eyes and see the grand gift you have received. I don’t think you would have truly understood your position in the presence of the greatest people of your life. Your ancestors waited for you to open your eyes and understand. You needed to be worldly before all of this was revealed to you. Please stay as long as you like Samuelsson I must be off now for I have more important matters to attend." Samuelsson watches the old man walk away as he crosses the green grass. The old man slowly pushes some low brush aside as he enters the forest and disappears a few moments later. Samuelsson then turns and walks amongst the ruins of the once mighty Viking city. His mind seems to begin to imagine the look of the great city. His eyes go from the tree lines to the ground and then he stops and looks at a road that he had unintentionally stopped on and begins to follow it back towards the beach and cliffs. He slowly treads the grounds his ancestors once tread as he takes careful steady steps with confidence. Confidence is something that he has had abundance of , but what makes this moment different? Slowly he walks back towards the cliffs as they seem to part before him down to a mast expanse leading to a harbour that had been blocked in by the collapsing of the cliffs on either side. He simply stares at the harbour. It seems inaccessible from the ocean and well hidden by the forest behind him. The water in the harbour is clear and green. He slowly walks to the edge and looks around as it just barely moves with the wind. He wonders if the tides change with the harbour. Stopping his walking he turns and looks at everything around him and then smiles. "So this is where my ancestors began to sail out to see and begin the conquests of many countries and nations. I am enjoying my run through the PTC’s GTT5 tournament. It is a history lesson all in itself. Much like the reason I came home the GTT5 is learning I have reasons for being there. Alec Lancer I don’t really care about your personal problems and trust me when I say this I am going to give you more problems then you currently have. I may be brash and straight forward, but there is a reason for this. I am a Viking proud and happy to be where I am and doing what I do best. Now there is a time when things should be set before you and the choices you make have consequences." Laughing a deep throaty laugh Samuelsson raises his arms and looks around. "A truth is revealed to me and now there is a simple truth you must understand Lance. I am going to tear you apart at the foundations and watch you crawl and struggle to get back to your feet. I do not need nor want anyone sympathy I win because I want to win. I lose because I wish to lose. Nothing will stop me from being a conqueror and nothing will stop me from watching someone throw away everything he had ever had. Welcome to the reality of the fight Lancer. A reality where you will be wondering where you are when the hospital brings you around and begin to give you painkillers. See you soon." Turning Samuelsson begins to walk away as the sun goes down over the fallen cliffs from which the harbour is blocked. Samuelsson walks up between the cliffs on the road that leads back up to the city he came to see. This road seems to symbolize the direction this mighty Viking warrior has taken in his life. He seems to walk taller and stand prouder. There is a man and what makes man. Does a mans destiny make the man who he is. A mans true path and direction comes from within his spirit. Which direction this destiny takes the man can only be seen in the future and from someone elses stand point in history.
