WWA Meltdown: Insider presents…Jonathon Winters!

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 [The camera opens up to a screen-wide image of the WWA logo, which after few seconds, slowly dissolves into a distanced shot of what appears to be some sort of studio; it’d coloured in a mixture of different shades of blue, with the WWA logo prominent in the background and a shadowed figure sat on some kind of stool at the very centre of the shot. The camera pans around and zooms in just as -obviously on cue - the studio lights flicker into life. The darkened figure is no longer darkened and we can now see that it is WWA reporter Wallace Coleman.] Coleman: Greetings wrestling fans, I’m WWA reporter Wallace Coleman and this is WWA Meltdown: Insider; a special show focusing on WWA superstar Jonathon Winters. I have been chosen by the WWA owner, Vik Ventressa, and the various other WWA officials, as the official representative of Jonathon Winters, in order to introduce him to those within the audience of Primetime Central’s special Global Title Tournament 5, that are perhaps unaware of Jonathon‘s abilities and accomplishments. [muttering under his breath] because we all know he won’t do it himself… [The picture of Coleman promptly disappears, and is replaced by a still image of Jonathon Winters, kitted out in his full wrestling attire, standing at the centre of the WWA wrestling ring, his Television title strapped around his waist, his Extreme Title casually draped over his right shoulder. Then, his theme music hits ("Aerials" by System of a Down) in the background as the picture slowly fades into a slow motion vignette of Jonathon Winters in a grapple with fellow WWA superstar Armaan.] "Life is a waterfall, We're one in the river, And one again after the fall" [The vignette cuts to Jonathon Winters hitting the "Percentage Victory" (spinning piledriver) on fellow WWA superstar Kid Cool, during their epic battle within the WWA Extreme Title Tournament Final.] "Swimming through the void We hear the word, We lost ourselves, But will we find it all? Cause we are the ones that want to play, Always want to go, But you never want to stay" [The scene cuts to Jonathon Winters hitting his finishing move the "Indifference Maker" (superkick), on Freddie Arson to win the WWA Television Title.] "And we are the ones that want to choose, Always want to play, But you never want to lose." [The scene cuts again to see Winters hit "Down to the Numbers" (Spinebuster) on Armaan in the WWA Extreme Title Tournament semi finals.] "Aerials, in the sky, When you lose small mind, You free your life." [The scene finally cuts to see Winters hit "The Indifference Maker" (superkick) one more time on Kid Cool, to win his second championship title in the final of the Extreme Title Tournament. The scene fades out (accompanied by the music), back to see Wallace Coleman on his stool.] Coleman: There are three facts that the uneducated observer must know about Jonathon Winters… Number One: Since he came to the WWA, over four months ago, Jonathon Winters has remained undefeated. In fact, in the nine matches he’s had within the WWA, he has demolished each and every opponent that he’s ever been put up against. Number Two: He is the current holder of two different WWA titles. As a resultant of his undefeated run, he won the WWA Extreme Title Tournament picking up - yes you guessed it - the WWA Extreme title, with ease. Before that, Jonathon was booked against long-time the WWA Television Champion, Freddie Arson, in a match with the TV title on the line. Winters walked away with the title with relative ease, after the former champion had been successfully defending the title against any and all opponents up until this fateful clash. Number Three: Although, with his great success comes a price; his personality. Some would say that Jonathon Winters isn’t the most sociable wrestler in the locker room, and goes out of his way, to keep himself to himself and has unintentionally upset a number of his fellow workers in the past. In essence, he is the ultimate loner and that character trait has hindered his relationship with the rest of the WWA roster. After a number of bad choices in his part - appearing on another wrestling promotion’s television programme for one - Jonathon is considered to be a traitor to the WWA, and the rest the roster have been making their feelings on the matter abundantly clear. The whole situation recently came to blows when fellow WWA superstar Ian Tense viciously attacked Jonathon in the backstage area of the Pit Arena, in New Mexico. Jonathon Winters may not be well-liked in the WWA locker room, but his history in the WWA cannot be questioned. He is not constantly victorious because he likes to hype himself up to the fans, nor because he likes to play mind games with his opponents; he is constantly victorious because he is the very best. He has proven himself extremely efficient at winning, at all costs, regardless of the odds he comes up against and that is the mark of a true champion. Whether you can call this showcase of Jonathon Winters here on WWA Meltdown: Insider, a piece propaganda or just plain old hype, know this: everything I have told you all tonight is the truth - nothing more, nothing less. The facts speak for themselves.
