Family BBQ

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 We are outside at a park in Washington D.C. The sun shines bright in this hot summer day with not a cloud in the sky. A BBQ or picnic is going on at this park, tables, buffet lines, even a stage is seen throughout the park. Cars aren’t parked in a parking lot however, a few of them are even pulled up onto the grass. The one obvious thing is that the BBQ seems to have nothing but black people there. You look around, and you’d realize this must be a family BBQ or reunion. That leads us to T. roC who is wearing some baggy shorts and a Washington Redskins jersey. He stands around with a cup in his hand, sipping slowly until he’s finally approached by Tony Rolo, one of the team PRIME members. “It’s about time.” T. roC states. “Well if this place wasn’t in the middle of nowhere maybe it’d be easier for me to find.” Rolo quickly replies. “Where the hell are we anyhow? Didn’t they tape the Blair Witch Project not to far from here?” “Ain’t that the one where three white people for no apparent reason went into the woods and died?” “Yeah” “That was a great movie then.” T. roC laughs. “Where are your sidekicks?” “My sidekicks? English wouldn’t be my sidekick if he was plated with gold. Joey, he’s on his way. Last time I checked he got lost, and I don’t blame him.” “You couldn’t follow the directions I gave you?” “Of course not. Nobody could understand those things. ‘Go down 14th Street for about 4 rap songs and take the next left. Then head down road for 5 hookers and a drug dealer before you take the right.’” Rolo folds his arms, waiting for T. roC to explain. “Hold on now, is it my fault that I listen to music when I drive? I don’t know how long your country songs are, but that was pretty good information.” “And the 5 hookers and a drug dealer? Some of the hookers WERE drug dealers!” “Hey, they need part-time jobs too. And as long as you don’t stop and ask them for directions, you won’t get robbed.” “Which reminds me, your ass owes me a watch, a wallet, and the change out of my ash tray.” Rolo says angrily while T. roC laughs hysterically. Rolo’s tone changes as his cell phone rings. Rolo pulls it out of his pocket, surprised that he wasn’t robbed for that as well. He looks at it and realizes who it is. He sighs for a second before answering it. “Hello.” The screen splits and we see Joey Troy in his car, talking to Rolo on his cell phone. “I’m having a problem finding the park.” “Thank god…” “Shut up douche-bag, let me talk to T. roC.” Joey Troy says. Rolo turns and looks at T. roC who is now engaged in a conversation with a family member. Rolo snickers and goes back to his conversation. “He’s busy at the moment. He just got into an argument with a guy who complained about you showing up. They think you’re a redneck.” “What?” “Yea, they said you’re not….’tight’ enough to be at this BBQ. I say you prove them wrong.” “How would I do that?” Troy asks. “Simple, just show up in some hip hop clothes, and spit their slang, they’d love you if you did that.” Rolo covers his mouth to laugh. “That’s a good idea.” Joey Troy says. “I’m gonna stop by the store and get some new clothes right now. But wait, how do I get there?” “What street are you on?” “G Street” “Ok, just stop and ask a guy with the hat down to his eyes and his hands in his pockets, he’ll give you directions.” Rolo laughs yet again. “Ok man, see you there. And don’t worry G, I will be in my flyest gear yo…” Troy hangs up the phone and the split screen goes away. T. roC turns back to Rolo who is laughing. “What’s so funny?” Rolo quickly stops laughing, “Nothing, it was just Joey being himself.” You see Ian English walking thru the park, getting stares from people at the BBQ, wondering if he’s lost. He quickly walks towards roC and Rolo who were on their way to get something to eat. “Hey Ian.” T. roC greets, “Let me guess, you had a hard time finding the place too?” “Not at all.” the Limey replies. “I just followed a car that looked to be worth $200 but had a $1,000 speaker system in it. That lead me right here.” “Rolo was wrong about you, you do have common sense.” T. roC instigates. Ian English gives Tony Rolo a dirty look before roC leads them to the buffet table. T. roC grabs a paper plate and starts putting his food on it. He starts at the first serving tray and adds food on from each tray after that. Both Ian and Tony add the food from the first tray onto their plate, but then seem confused. T. roC is about 5 trays down when he realizes that his teammates aren’t piling on the food. “What’s wrong with you two?” “Ummm…roC, I don’t mean to disrespect the food or anything, but shouldn’t there be a larger variety?” Tony asks. “What are you talking about?” T. roC asks. “We have all sorts of different foods.” T. roC then starts pointing to each tray to let Rolo and English know what’s in them. “We’ve got chicken, baked chicken, roasted chicken, fried chicken, lemon seasoned chicken, BBQ chicken, deep fried chicken, and at the end, Kool-Aid.” T. roC looks back at Tony and Ian who still wear puzzled expressions. Ian remains confused as a line forms behind him. “I dunno mate; it just seems like a whole loada chicken to me…” “You don’t like chicken?!” A large black guy behind Ian yells. His loud mouth gets the attention from a lot of people including a group of guys who huddle behind the large black guy. “Hey everyone, this guy doesn’t like chicken!” “Woah muppet, I didn’t say that.” Ian replies. He turns his head to check who’s behind him, Tony Rolo nor T. roC are anywhere to be seen. Both have retired to the Kool-Aid table, to spectate on the proceedings and see how the slimy Brit gets himself out of this one. “All I said was…” “You know what we do to people who don’t like chicken?” The large man asks rhetorically. He then pushes his chest up against Ian, showing how much he oversizes him. This guy is near seven feet tall, and has the build of a football player. Ian English backs up once, but finds that with each receding step, the large black man and his friends step forward. Ian continues to try to back talk his way out of it, “Naah it aint that I don’t like chicken…err, I’m just a vegetarian….” Ian finds himself walking backwards up a set of steps until he realizes he has positioned himself up on the karaoke stage. The black guy and his crowd are about to go after him until T. roC jumps up on stage between them. “It’s about time…” Ian mumbles. T. roC grabs the karaoke microphone and gets everyone’s attention. “Hold on guys, lets let this nice young man explain himself…” “Yea!” Ian yells from behind roC. “MAKE HIM SING!” Is yelled from someone in the crowd and gets an uproar from everyone else. English looks at roC and shakes his head side to side, but roC just smiles. “Yea. That’s a good idea. Here you go Ian, lets hear a song!” T. roC hands Ian the mic and hops off of the stage. He looks out into the crowd who are all staring and waiting for the entertainment to begin. Ian English starts mumbling to himself… “Okay Ian man, wot can I sing that will get these homies off of me back….AH-HA I KNOW, IAN YER A GENIUS!” Ian steps into the middle of the stage and starts singing... “Lyrics, fill in the gap -- Drop that bass and get the NINJA RAP, Feel it, if you know what I mean -- Give it up for those heroes in green, Just flowin, smooth with the power -- Kickin' it up, hour after hour, Cause in this life there's only one winner -- You better aim good so you can, hit the center, In it to win it, with a team of four -- Ninja Turtles that you gotta adore it's, the…” Nobody in the crowd can believe this… “Ninja, Ninja, RAP! Ninja, Ninja, RAP! GO GO GO GO, Go Ninja, Go Ninja, GO; Go Ninja, Go ninja, GO! Go Ninja, Go Ninja. GO; Go Ninja, Go ninja, GO! GO GO GO GO” Ian pumps his fist in the air and tries to get the crowd to do it when he realizes that people are just laughing and taking pictures of this. Obviously they didn't appreciate old skool hip hop. It’s like everybody suddenly owns a camera. Ian just slides off of the stage and skulks over to Tony Rolo and T. roC who are also pointing and laughing. T. roC looks at Tony and they start pumping their fist and singing in unison, “Go Ninja, Go Ninja go!” They yell mockingly. “I hope youse both burn in hell. Cheers fer 'aving my back up there arse bandits. Really appreciated it.” Ian moans. Tony Rolo takes a sip of the Kool-Aid for the first time. He drinks a bit, then his face suddenly cringes up. “God damn that’s sweet.” “Really?” T. roC takes a sip of his. “Not sweet enough for my taste.” “Are you serious.” Tony Rolo goes over to the Kool-Aid pitcher and grabs the spoon that laid beside it. He then puts the spoon straight into the pitcher and watches it sit straight up from all the sugar that’s within the drink mix. “What’s your point?” T. roC asks, shrugging his shoulders. Tony Rolo just sighs and walks away. The trio walks away, assumingly talking about their wrestling match at the upcoming I-PPV. They continue walking until loud music is heard. Now, this wouldn’t be unlikely at a normal BBQ, but this one sticks out… “Give it to me baby, uh huh, uh huh Give it to me baby, uh huh, uh huh Give it to me baby, uh huh, uh huh And all the girlies say I'm pretty fly for a white guy “ The whole entire BBQ turns towards the parking lot where a sputtering 1979 Orange Volkswagen Rabbit has just pulled up. T. roC’s mouth drops in shock, and Ian English covers his mouth in laughter. Tony Rolo does the same as the Limey as they watch Joey Troy climb out of his car. He closes the door with one hand, while trying to hold up his pants with the other. Joey wears some baggy jeans that can barely stay on his waist. He has a large white t-shirt on and a necklace daggling around his neck. He struts his way towards the park with the du rag around his head, and his hand on his crotch. T. roC hurries to be the first to meet Joey as there is a lot of people not happy with this arrival. “What the hell are you doing?” T. roC asks. “You look like an extra from a Friday movie.” “Whut’s really good yo?” Joey Troy asks T. roC. “Was really hood wit u? I was peepin these new threads at the crib in my closet, and decided I should toss them on my back and roll wit the flow yo.” “You do know if you talk like that to my family, they will shoot you…” T. roC tries to scare Joey, but it doesn’t work. Joey Troy then whispers to roC, “Don’t worry, Tony told me how your family thought I was a redneck. I’m just proving I can be hip hopish…” “He what?!” The same group of five guys that were trying to intimidate Ian English earlier, are marching towards Joey Troy now. They are in a straight line, coming at Joey like some rival gang. They are lead by the same large black guy as before, the one who’s built like a football player. “Yo White boy!” The large man yells. At that same time, you see Ian English and Tony Rolo slowly back away towards the buffet tables for a new spectator sport. “We got a problem with you mocking us!” The large man yells. “Well G, you know how we can settle this don’t you?” Joey states. T. roC tries to stop him, but Joey resists. “I sure as hell do.” The large man says while pounding his fist into his hand. “Ight then, it’s on BEOTCH!” Joey Troy runs at the large black man and slides on the ground. With his palm on the ground, he then starts spinning around. Joey Troy does a spinnaroonie back to his feet before he continues to break dance…. 10 Minutes later…. Joey Troy has a bag of ice under his right eye as he sits in the back of the ambulance. His white t-shirt is now brown and red. Brown from when he was spinning on the ground, and red from when he got dropped on the ground by the 5 guys who whooped his ass. His jeans have been stolen from him and he sits with a blanket around his waist that the EMTs gave him. He removes the ice from his eye to reveal the large black trophy he won for break dancing. T. roC approaches the ambulance where Joey Troy is, and roC can’t help but to laugh. “So let me get this straight,” T. roC starts. “You listened to Tony…” “And it all went downhill from there.” Joey Troy mumbles. Both Tony Rolo and Ian English both arrive to the back of the ambulance, both laughing and chuckling at Joey’s misfortune. “And you two fuckers didn’t help at all!” Joey yells at English and Rolo. “Sure we did,” Ian states still grinning. “We told them about yer bad knee and everything!” “Yea Joey, and when you got to your feet, I tried motivating you to run faster.” Tony explains. “You yelled ‘run white boy, run!’” Joey exclaims. “And who yelled out that I didn’t like chicken?” Ian English then busts out with a self-congratulatory laugh. “Sorry about that. But there’s one thing I gotta find out Troy.” Ian English grabs the blanket that was wrapped around the waist of Joey. “Under all that hip hop gear, youse decided to wear Smurf boxers?” Ian snaps the blanket off and reveals the Smurf boxers that Joey Troy was wearing. On the front was a picture of Papa Smurf with his thumbs up. “You’re just jealous. I’m going to the car, I think I have some shorts in there. You guys coming with me?” “Sure,” T. roC says. “I think we’ll just stay about 10 feet back so nobody thinks we know you though.” The four walk to Joey Troy’s car, with the other three staying their distance, to get Joey a new pair of pants. Joey Troy opens his trunk and starts digging thru some of the random garbage and miscellaneous items he keeps back there. T. roC, Tony, and Ian are all leaning on English's car which is about 4 spaces down from Joey’s. “What a day,” T. roC begins. “I haven’t seen a more dysfunctional time since Stevie Wonder was trying to hit a piñata.” T. roC’s cell phone rings and he turns to pick it up. He slowly walks away as it must be a private call. Meanwhile, Ian and Tony are standing around, waiting for Joey to find some pants. The Brit is looking in the car bodywork at a reflection of himself and pulling various faces, while Tony has his eyes locked on a car across the street. “I wonder what’s in their trunk.” Tony Rolo talks aloud to himself. “Why don’t yer go find out eh?” Ian says, not taking an eye off of himself in the car reflection. “I 'ear they just sell bootleg CDs or summat.” “Cool, I wonder if they have that new Garth Brooks CD…” Tony Rolo then walks towards the car. Rolo is heard by the group of guys who were looking into the trunk and they all turn around quickly, a couple of them reaching for their waist. They realize it’s just Tony and they calm down a bit. “Relax guys, I just want to buy something.” “Buy something?” One man asks. “Yea…” Tony Rolo suddenly feels that he’s unwelcomed. Back at Ian’s car, T. roC has gotten off his cell phone and returned. He looks at Ian who is just waiting on his partners to return. “Where’s Rolo?” T. roC asks. “He’s over there trying to buy CDs.” “Over where?” “Over there.” Ian points, “In the boot of that car.” “Oh no…” T. roC says to himself. “What is it with you white people? Are you just attracted to danger? I mean sky diving, bungee jumping, the Titanic. You people always ask for trouble.” T. roC looks over at the car where Tony was and he has a five dollar bill out, still trying to buy something from the guys. “Come on guys, I know you got movies or something. You got the bootleg Shrek 2 yet?” Tony then gets some strange looks from all the guys. “Umm…I have a kid…” Tony says, taking all the strange looks away. “Listen….dude.” One of the black guys say mockingly. “We don’t have anything for sale for you over here. Unless you have something you want to trade…” “A barter system huh? Well, lets see what I can do for you…” Tony Rolo reaches in his pocket to find something. A few minutes later, Joey Troy finally comes back dressed in his regular attire. He regroups with both Ian and T. roC who are watching Tony Rolo in amazement. Tony returns with 5 CDs, 7 bootleg movies, and a 19” rim. T. roC can’t seem to believe it. “What did you pay to get all of that?” He asks. “I gave them a two for one Popeye’s Chicken coupon.” Tony calmly states. T. roC and the others laugh as Rolo thinks he got over. Joey Troy looks over at the black guys who are delighted at their deal. Joey Troy, can’t help but think about how Rolo set him up earlier, having him dress up for the BBQ just to get beat up on. He rubs his chin with thought until he finally thinks of something. “What did you say Tony Rolo, the one in the blue shirt?” Joey Troy asks loud enough so everyone in 30 feet can hear him. “What the hell are you talking about? I didn’t say anything.” Tony replies. “That was a fake coupon?!” Joey yells. Tony’s facial expression turns into anger. “You don’t even like chicken?!” Joey Troy slowly steps away from Rolo. T. roC looks over towards Ian but the Limey has already made a startegic withdrawal and is at the end of a cloud of smoke where he's sprinted away. “Joey shut up!” Tony demands. “You think Kool-Aid tastes like Ian's asshole?!” A group of black guys show up right on cue as Rolo turns around to greet them. “I’m going to kill you Joey.” Tony says while staring back at a group of 12 black guys, all pretty upset. Tony goes to find T. roC who has already hopped in his car and slowly started the engine. Ian English isn’t even in sight anymore. Joey Troy goes to enter his car until Tony realizes he's about to leave. Tony Rolo is now set to run as well. “JOEY HATES TUPAC!” “NO!” Troy yells and at that moment, the 12 men start their chase, but this time after both Rolo and Joey now. Joey can’t seem to open his car door and is forced to escape on foot as well. Rolo runs right beside him. “Damnit, why couldn’t you just go down alone?” The two are chased off of camera. We focus back on T. roC who is about to drive off. “My mom told me two things as a child, don’t have white friends, and don’t have sex with white women. And I chose to listen to neither.” T. roC just shakes his head and drives off. The scene slowly fades away with roC’s car driving down the road.
