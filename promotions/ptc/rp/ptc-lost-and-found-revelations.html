[For those that care, here are parts one and two of Lost and Found.] Lost and Found Part Three: Revelations “The mind is its own place, and in itself, can make heaven of Hell, and a hell of Heaven.” - John Milton Location: Washington, D.C. Date and Time: July 1st, 2006 @ 6:30 AM “Shit, shit, shit…” mumbled Detective Luis Rand as he felt the hot coffee hit his skin. He set the coffee down, and began to clean up the coffee off of his hands immediately. Already the morning had not started off well, and it looked like it wasn’t going to get any better. As he picked up his coffee, and started to head towards his office, he felt a hand settle on his shoulder, causing him to spill his coffee even more. As he placed the cup of coffee down once again, he turned around to find his boss, Randall Jones, standing right behind him. “Everything okay Luis?” his boss asked him, concerned about the jittery manner of his best detective. Randall noticed Luis wearing a very nice three piece suit, but the most noticeable thing about him was the fact that he looked like he was wired. “Yeah, just a little tired. Not sleeping much as of late,” the detective told his boss, his eyes never quite meeting with his. Randall nodded his head and handed Luis some napkins to help clean him up. “You still worrying yourself over this Silsbee case?” he asked him as Luis nodded his head while cleaning off the coffee from his hands once again. For the past year, Rand had been assigned the Silsbee case, one that troubled him from what he had done over the years, and he hadn’t been able to get sign of him once as of yet. Little did he know that his luck was about to change. “Well then, you’ll be happy to know that we’ve got a tip on him. Guy down in Florida was involved in a bar fight, and as they were taking him to the hospital, mentioned his name. No idea if he was involved in the fight, but the fact that his name was mentioned we thought was important, so we’re sending you down there,” Randall told him confidently. Luis just looked at Randall, not sure how to take this bit of news. “Okay. When am I heading down there?” he asked him, his mind starting to race at a hundred miles per hour. “Immediately. We need to find out more about this entire situation, and if this man has seen him. If so, we’ve got a good idea on where he’s at, and we can start locking down the state to get this son-of-a-bitch. The plane is waiting for you,” he told Luis, to which Luis just nodded his head, and turned around to pick up the files he was holding. “Then I guess I need to get going,” he replied, starting to walk away from his boss. “You’ll be briefed on the plane Luis!” he yelled after him as Luis raced down the hallway. This was the break he had been waiting for. He had been trying to find a lead on Silsbee for a year now, and he finally was able to get one. For the past year, he had read up on what he had done to those children, and now retribution was at his door. Rand would just be the one to pay it to him. Little did Detective Luis Rand know that his whole entire life was about to be turned upside down. --- Location: Leesburg, FL Date and Time: July 1st, 2006 @ 7:15 AM There wasn’t a sound heard anywhere inside of the hospital room. As the saying goes, you could hear a pin drop, that’s how quiet it was inside of the room. It was almost surreal and eerie at the same time. The main focus in the room was all on one person, Craig Maloof, who lied in a bed, reeling from one piece of news after another. Over the span of six hours, he had gotten into a fight with a man who was a serial murderer and a rapist, been stabbed in the abdomen four times, been told he was suffering from two different kinds of amnesia, and now had just found out that he was married. His mind was spinning from everything that was going on, and he didn’t know from which direction the next piece of news was going to hit him. The biggest piece of news to him could easily split down the middle between having two different kinds of amnesia and the latest news, him being married. For him, he knew he was going through life with a certain kind of daze. He remembered specific events, specific days, but nothing concurrently. It just felt like he was floating through life without an anchor to hold him down. When the doctor had told him that she had suspected he was suffering from global amnesia, where he didn’t remember anything from the day he was born until two years ago. The other kind of amnesia he was suffering from was anterograde amnesia, which as the doctor told him made it impossible for his memory to transfer memories into long-term memories. For whatever reason though, he was able to remember a few days. For him, he had to figure out who he was, and what happened to him to cause all of this. He felt like the weight of the world was on his shoulders, and he couldn’t figure out why. As the room remained silent, the sun exploded into the room without a moment’s notice. The crimson sky filled the entire room as if someone had just lit a candle in the room. Craig just simply turned his head and looked out the window, as he continued to process each piece of information into his mind. The latest had just hit him within the last few minutes as he found he was married. A woman had walked in upset, told the man who had saved his life to leave, and informed everyone that she was married to him. He had a wife? He couldn’t believe it. He wondered if he had children, what his past job was, and who his parents were. The desire to find out about his past overwhelmed him, and he looked back at the mysterious woman, his wife, waiting for more, and yearning for more. “You’re my wife? I… have a wife?” he said as if the words almost stumbled out of his mouth. He was still in disbelief over that piece of news coming from this mysterious woman’s mouth. He then looked at both James and the doctor. James had a mixture of two looks on his face; one was anger, while the other was shocked. The doctor looked a little shocked as well from the bombshell that had been dropped in the room. He then looked at the other woman, and noticed a smile on her face as she stood there. The first thing that popped into Craig’s mind was how beautiful she was, and if she really was his wife, then he had picked a beauty. However, he couldn’t get over the fact that before the announcement, that James had called her a devil woman. He needed to know more. “This is NOT your wife Craig. This can’t be your wife!” he yelled back, his anger and eyes focused on Jocelyn. However, her demeanor didn’t change as she just stood there, taking the verbal abuse from James, before looking back at Craig, and putting her hand on his leg. Then, she looked back at James, a smile still on her face. “James, that’s no way to speak to me, especially when you respect Craig so much. We got married just days before he disappeared, and I’ve been looking for him ever since. I’m happy that I’ve been able to find him now,” she said sincerely enough to fool everyone in the room. Deep down, she knew she needed Craig for the information stored in her head. Little did she know that the information she was seeking, Craig would be unable to give her. “No, it’s not possible! Craig, this woman is evil!” he yelled back at her, his anger starting to get the best of him. Craig looked at James before looking at the woman. There was much to be said here, but he could only still think about the fact that he was married. “I don’t even know your name…,” he began to tell her before his words trailed off. He tried to search his mind for the answer to what her name was, but he couldn’t find it for the life of him. He just kept drawing up a blank. “What? Craig, you know my name. We’ve known each other for ten years,” she told him, before looking at the doctor. “What’s happened to him?” she asked her, suddenly feeling tension in her chest, and wanting answers that immediate second. “He was stabbed four times in the abdomen--,” she started to say before she was interrupted by Jocelyn. “No, I know that. What happened to his memory?” she questioned her again, needing the answers immediately. The blood was rushing through her body as she was trying to comprehend what was going on. It was as if her world had come crashing down around her. If he had forgotten his memory, then she wouldn’t be able to find out what she needed to know, find the answers that she had been searching for so long. She stood up and walked towards the doctor as the doctor hesitated. Immediately, the doctor started to tense up from the intimidating look on Jocelyn’s face. “He’s suffering from amnesia,” she finally told her, and as she did, Jocelyn could feel it all happening at once. Her blood continued to rush through her body as the sweat started to build up on her entire body. She placed her hands up on her head, running them through her long, luscious, brown hair. She couldn’t comprehend this. This couldn’t be true. She wondered to herself how over two years of searching had brought her to this point, where the man who could unlock everything she needed to know, had his entire memory missing. “No… no, no, no…” she continued to repeat as she paced around the room, in shock and disbelief at the news. Then, it all dawned on her. She had Craig in the perfect spot. Now, with him out of the loop, not remembering anything, this was her chance. She had married the man she loved, and now she could use him to her full advantage. She then turned and looked at James. “I want him out of here, now!” she yelled at the doctor while pointing at James. James was just seething to kill Jocelyn, but was restraining himself to the best of his abilities. “You want me out of here?! You’re lucky I don’t kill you right here for what you’ve done to Craig! You don’t know what she’s done to you Craig, how she’s tried to kill you!” he yelled out with an immense amount of rage in his voice. Jocelyn’s jaw then dropped at hearing the news, feigning shock and surprise from James’ news. “What?! How dare you accuse me of trying to kill the man I love James?! I’ve always told Craig how insane and crazy you were, living in your fantasies and dreams, living out your delusions! Just face the facts! You never approved of our marriage, and now you see the perfect opportunity to ruin our love once and for all. I’ve loved him for ten years, and I’m not going to let you ruin this! I want him out of here now before I call the cops on him!” she shouted at him, arms flailing as she was beyond upset at what James had accused her of. “I’m going to have to ask you to come with me James,” the doctor told her, surrendering to Jocelyn’s demands. “But, Jocelyn…” he began, but never was able to finish his sentence. “James, let’s go,” she told her before opening the door to escort him out of his room. He stood there in disbelief, staring at Craig, then Jocelyn, and once again at Craig before exiting the room. The doctor followed behind him, and shut the door as Jocelyn and Craig were finally left alone. Jocelyn then looked out the window, noticing the beauty of the sky, before pulling up a chair next to Craig’s bed, and sat down next to him. “So, where do we begin?” she asked him, ready to give him all of the information he needed to know. --- Location: Unknown, FL Date and Time: July 1st, 2006 @ 3:15AM Rebecca instantly opened her eyes as she heard the door slam.
