Date: 2005-11-27 Transcript of radio dispatch communication with car 57 driver Officer Marcus Lopez. Dispatch: Caller at Dickson and College reports drunken man causing a disturbance outside Mickey Finn’s Pub. Lopez: Car 57 responding. Dispatch: Be careful Mark. Lopez: Always am Sheila you know me. You have a description for me? Dispatch: Subject is a white male, mid to late twenties, red hair. Lopez: Any visible weapons. Dispatch: He may have a busted beer bottle. Lopez: Fair enough. Dispatch: What’s your ETA? Lopez: I’m just pulling in now, I’m not seeing anyone. Wait, there’s a guy with red hair sitting on the curb. Dispatch: That’s probably him, you know how drunks are. Lopez: Yeah, all fire and brimstone one minute claim as a Hindu cow the next. Dispatch: Yup. Lopez: Ok, well I’m going to check it out. Dispatch: Like I said be careful never know what’s going to happen. ****************Ten Minutes Later*********************** Dispatch: Officer Lopez? Please respond. Dispatch: Officer Lopez, what’s your twenty? Dispatch: Mark come on this isn’t funny. Dispatch: Calling all units possible situation at Dickson and College, Mickey Finn’s Pub. Officer not responding. Car 22: This is Car 22 responding, I’m about a block away. Dispatch: Hurry up, you know Mark never just doesn’t respond. Car 22: I know. Pulling onto the street now….SHIT Dispatch: What what? Car 22: ALL UNITS ALL UNITS OFFICER DOWN. *************Five Minutes Later******************** Lopez: Sheila calm down I’m going to be fine. Dispatch: What happened? Lopez: I got jumped from behind almost before I got out of the car. Dispatch: Are you ok? Lopez: Just a bump on the head, bastard had a blackjack or something. Dispatch: Was it the suspect. Lopez: Nah, he didn’t even move it was someone else. When I came to found out the bastard took my gun and handcuffs. He always drug the red headed guy away kicking and screaming, according to the witness. Dispatch: If they saw it why didn’t anyone call 911? Lopez: You know civilians. It was like pulling teeth just getting that much. Only thing is apparently no one saw the guy’s face who hit me. Dispatch: Any description at all? Lopez: Medium build, long black hair, possibly Asian. Dispatch: It’s a start I guess. Do we think the red head is involved with the attacker? Lopez: Possibly, they almost certainly knew each other. But something tells me he’s not involved he smelled like he shouldn’t even be able to stand. Guy was in no shape to hurt anyone Dispatch: Anything else? Lopez: Not really a few people have said the red head looked familiar, like maybe he was famous or something. Dispatch: Every little bit helps I guess. Lopez: I suppose. 11-26-05
