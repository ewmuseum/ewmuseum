This Is Why My Heart Is Still Beating (IG Roleplay)

29 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My first RP with Kyle "Velocity" Lamen in about three years or so. Hope it's not too disappointing. ----------- "This Is Why My Heart Is Still Beating" * * * * * Tell me. Just what it is I'm fighting for. The more my dreams change, The more my dreams hit the floor. But I'm not the one. To give it up so fast. Fight again tomorrow. Won't be enslaved by my past. -'New Haven' by Lost City Angels * * * * * Two days ago... The nights in Osaka, Japan are very pleasant over the summer. Between the breezes coming off the Pacific Ocean, the air is still and silent...waiting, especially in the spring months before the typhoon season. Some nights like tonight, when there's no rain, Kyle Lamen sets up his tiny futon out on the fifth floor balcony of his apartment and sleeps outside, enveloped by the city air and the sounds it brings. Someone pushes past on a bicycle, the tires rolling and sounding sticky on the street. A young woman giggles, and then hushes quickly, as if stifled by a friend or lover. There's a mild rumbling as the Nanken Line, one of the trains that runs though Osaka hustles past Kyle's apartment. For some, the thrum-thrum-thrack-thrack-thrum-thrum-thrack-thrack of the train could be an irritation, but Kyle doesn't mind the noise. It's all music, of a sort. Everything here, everything everywhere...it's all music. His heart beats in time with the trains of Osaka. * * * * * Three and a half years ago... His heart is so close to the breaking point, that the stress cracks are starting to show as little red blood vessels in his eyes. His legs flailed about sporadically, kicking the white sheets off the bed. Sure, it's four in the morning, and sure, he's suffering from the strange pain of heroin withdrawal, but there's hope, even in this hotel room with the unfamiliar ceiling. I've been saved. I've been saved. I have a father for the first time in my life, and I've been saved... The door to the hotel room opened, and a man with a grizzled face and three days of whisker growth stepped in. Kyle had never been so happy to see another wrestler in his life, especially considering this wrestler was Johnny Lamen, who finally met Kyle and let him know that he, in fact, was his father. Johnny wandered over to the bed, his eyes bloodshot and his steps uneasy. “Kid, no more guys in white coats. No doctors, especially of the Paul Arovnik and Big Brother kind, okay? You've gotta learn to trust your body and stay away from anything stronger than aspirin, understand me, son?” Johnny told him as he wandered back away from the bed and towards the minibar, which he opened and took a Bud Light from. “Ugh...does alcohol count, dad?” Kyle half-asked, half-moaned. The sweat ran in small beads off his forehead, and he thought his own breath tasted rotten in his mouth. “Oh hell no, kid.” Johnny assured him. “That's not dangerous at all. In fact, we'll go out for a beer after you get all this other crap out of your system. And since you're making all this money, I'll even let you buy.” Kyle writhed a little more in the bed, his stomach twisting into bitter knots, but his heart was already clean. Thanks, dad. * * * * * Yesterday... Hrm. Wonder what brought that on? An older Japanese woman with eyes like chiseled marble and a face like a train wreck held up a five-foot katana, and fumbled with the ribbon tying the scabbard to the hilt of the sword. The bustle of the Osaka airport rumbled behind her, the entire station buzzing like an electrical appliance. Is there anything left for me back there? Will they even have me back? I'm entering the Infinite Gauntlet as a free agent, because they'll take anyone...but would any federation worth it's salt have anything at all to do with me since the accident? “So, you check this?” she asked, finally becoming overcome with frustration and setting down the sheathed sword. The scabbard was a laquered black wood, polished smooth and shiny, with flashes of pink cherry blossoms falling down from the hilt to the tip. I just can't believe I'm doing this. Giving up the new band, leaving Japan. All to try to make this wrestling thing right again. To find the fans and friends I've wronged and try to come up with some sort of penance. Kyle Lamen lifted his Orix Blue Wave baseball cap and ran his hand through his hair in frustration, pulling free a few small flecks of green hair dye. “Sure, if I have to. But please be careful with it.” He looked longingly at the sword, and fished around in his satchel for his permit for it. The woman smiled unconvincingly, and used her right hand to wave him forward, into the lines and the wait for America. * * * * * Three years ago... Kyle sits in the darkness of a locker room at the newly-built North Pacific Wrestling Academy, crumpled in the corner, back to a line of lockers. His green eyes are almost glazed over. A door opens, and light filters in around the silhouette of Kyle's friend, Kensuke Suzahara. Kensuke, despite being Kyle's friend, storms up to Kyle and pulls him up to his feet angrily. “What the FUCK were you thinking, Kyle!? Tell me exactly what the FUCK you were thinking!” Kensuke's pretty-boy features were mucked about in anger, his brown eyes burning in his face. Kyle just looked away, ashamed, mouth drooping at the corners and eyes averted. Kensuke reached back and slapped Kyle solidly across the mouth with one hand, while still holding Kyle up with his left hand. “Talk to me!” Kyle's lip revealed blood as he spoke, and flecks flew from his lips. “I did-I didn't mean to hurt the kid. I-” he stammered, before Kensuke cut him off with another slap across the face. Blood began to run in a dripping drizzle out of the corner of Kyle Lamen's mouth. “You fucker. You are not medically cleared to wrestle, you've been fired from Five Star Wrestling for not reporting a severe injury and endangering others, and the first thing you do is you try to lift a smart-mouthing trainee with an arm that DOES NOT FUCKING WORK and you accidentally drop him on his head! Fuck, Kyle...you might've killed him!” Kensuke spits. Kyle Lamen's tongue didn't work, and felt like cotton in his mouth. Kensuke just shook his head and walked towards the door. “Go away, Kyle. Just get the hell away from here.” * * * * * Yesterday... Away from here...but I can never get far away enough from myself... There's an almost zen-like calm on the flight from Osaka to Detroit. The plane's other passengers are businessmen, housewives, and students...none of the screaming children on the coach flights he used to take when he was starting out with CCW and PCW before the big money started to roll in. But here he was flanked by a sleeping Japanese woman and an American man reading the Wall Street Journal. It amazed Kyle when he stepped back and thought about it...he was sitting in a giant tube of metal with a bunch of other tiny humans, where they took some old decayed plant matter, and blew it up, continuously, in an attempt to throw the big metal tube halfway across the world. “Frickin'-A.” Kyle mumbled to himself. The woman next to him rolled in her sleep. The guy with the newspaper and cornflower blue tie next to him glanced over to see if something were wrong, and then returned to the paper. “Win A Date With Tad Hamilton” was still playing on the plane's movie screens. Everything inside is normal. Kyle wanted to ride the wing of the jet, singing into the wind, feel the energy and aggression of a world of action. Undaunted, Kyle opened up a packet of lightly salted peanuts, and watched the insipid romance provided to him. No substitute for the real thing... * * * * * Three months ago... “Konban wa, minna-san!”said a light airy voice, and it initially took Kyle's attention away from his piece of beef grilling on the table in front of him. He turned to look over his left shoulder, and saw the most beautiful pair of eyes he'd ever seen in his life. Large, like a doe...a deer, a female deer..., vibrant, and shockingly violet. They shone brightly in the low-light of the yakiniku joint, and very slowly the right eyelids closed together in a slow wink that Kyle knew, absolutely knew, was meant for him and him alone. When he next regained his sense of things beyond those eyes, he found that the woman possessed a classically beautiful face, terrific fashion sense (a blue Killers t-shirt instead of the trendy 'J'ador Dior' things that most pretty Osaka girls wore, and a great black mini-skirt). Miho Kurosaki. The most perfect woman who has ever lived. Sure, she was a pop idol who received about fifteen marriage proposals a day, and sure, she was one of the most perfect physical specimens ever to grace the Pacific, but Kyle's smile said everything that needed to be said to her. He could work out the specifics later, about how to woo a woman who could be said to have everything she already needed, or to deal with the problem of an existing boyfriend or an overzealous family. She walked up to him first. His stomach grew three sizes and his heart made a leap for his throat. She smiled like a pinball machine lights up when you hit the jackpot. “Kyle, right?” Kyle didn't know whether to stand up or sit down, so he almost fell over half-assing a stand out of his chair, and nearly knocking over the smoking brazier on the table. Miho giggled a little, before Kyle held out his hand to her. “Kyle is right. You'll have to excuse me, but I think I'm in love with you.“ She smiled again, blushing, and Kyle knew the shape of his heart. It looked just like her. Nothing ventured, nothing gained. The bigger the risk, the greater the reward. * * * * * Today... The office building where the PTC offices are located (apparently) has a kiosk in the front of the building where you can sign up for the Infinite Gauntlet. A flare of black robes or cape here, some spandex there, and there is the bustle of professional wrestling all around. It's like a freaking car wreck. Kyle, electric guitar slung over one shoulder, katana slung over the other, approaches the kiosk. Could just try and get an audition with a smaller fed, but no. Straight into the breach one more time. “Hey there.” Kyle said, trying to grab the attention of the kiosk attendant. “Kyle “Velocity” Lamen. I spoke with someone before about the Infinite Gauntlet signups?” The guy at the kiosk nods, and responds “Yeah, you faxed all your information over before. You just need to sign the release, and take a drug test, and you'll be all set.” You're kidding. They actually let me in. I mean, three years is a long time, but...I thought they were serious about blacklisting me... “Does everyone have to take the drug test?” Kyle asked, looking left and right. The guy at the kiosk looks around, a bit nervously. “I dunno. I think so.” He shrugs. Kyle just kind of nods, his hair bobbing around him. Paranoid much, aren't I? There's only one goal at stake here. Win the first gauntlet match, then win enough to earn a shot at the Global Title. Offer himself up as a free agent with a pending GT shot to any high-paying, high-profile federation that will take him... A paycheck, penance, and a life exciting. But first, one match against what could be dozens of men. Already, he could feel the biggest rush of a lifetime filled with them, the chanting, pounding thrust of a crowd. Kyle Lamen has been, is, and will always be a performer, feeding off the music of the crowd... ...his heart beats in time with it all.
