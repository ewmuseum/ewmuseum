John "The Prophet" Adams vs Dan Logan

17 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recommence @ Start @ Daniel Logan V.S John Adams Name: Daniel Logan Location: The Park Word: Recommence Daniel Logan pulls up on Granbury Crescent and steps out of his car onto the grass. Soon the other member of this little gathering would show up, but until then Daniel knew that rest would be his best option. It has been a long while since he last fought, a long time since anyone really bothered to take any notice in him and he had faded off into obscurity quite easily. His contract in Four Winds Wrestling still held firm but it hadn’t been exercised in months, Dan was quite safe that they were paying him for nothing. It’d probably change after this and he knew it. Walking up the small grainy path towards the park benches he glances at the red moon almost hidden by the wispy clouds and then back towards the street lamps glowing around him lighting up the darker areas of the park, preventing unwanted things from happening. He sits down calmly on the wooden bench and relaxes, taking out a small piece of paper he begins to read as the night around him changes. At first the challenge match had come as somewhat of a surprise for Daniel. There had never been many supporters for him and he wasn’t the biggest name to just vanish from the lime light so someone wanting to fight Daniel had been strange. When Dan found out that John was wrestling in the same place which his brother had fought, the coincidence was a little less so. Daniel had cried when it was announced his brother had died, just like everyone else he had loved him as nobody else could. Yet the shock of his death had worn off now and though Daniel had missed him, they never really saw one another that much anyway. There wasn’t much on the paper that he needed to read and after little more than five minutes Daniel put it back into his long black suede jacket and dropped his head back staring at the stars. They’re always there, always exactly the same he thought to himself as he glanced at the constellations. He stares at the constellation “Phoenix” as he thinks for a moment of the wrestler. Jay Phoenix, were it that Daniel had been like Jay he would have been hassled throughout his rest period, yet Daniel’s life had been quiet. Was John Adams a new Jay Phoenix? The question alone was preposterous, no man could emulate another and in truth it was known that LOW was no longer the place which Daniel had challenged members of previously. Adams was part of the new era of Lights Out Wrestling, one which in many people’s opinions had been disastrous. “You look worried boy-o… You worried the Prophet might beat you?” Daniel shoots round to look for his friend but sees no-one close by, he calls out still unsure whether he has drifted off or merely that David is just hiding away from him. “David? Where are you?” “Don’t worry boy-o I’m here, just don’t fancy you seeing me the way I am right now. I’m not exactly pretty anymore” Daniel laughs, the vainest man he knew of hiding his face from Daniel a man with a bigger scar than he’d seen even in this business. Daniel strokes his face and traces the faded lines of the scar as he talks again. “You think it’ll matter, me with a face like mine?” For a moment there is silence, the whole park seems emptier and colder than it was before and Daniel slowly lets out an involuntary shiver. As suddenly as the silence began it ends again as the birds in the oak tree beside him chirp disturbed and flutter off to the sanctity of another branch on another tree. Daniel stares as they fly away and for a moment wishes that he like they could just up and leave, but unlike the past he couldn’t now and Daniel knew it. Complications had arisen since he had last fought and now Daniel, father once again was determined that he would give his child (Sarah) all he could, to always be there whatever she needed. Daniel looks forwards again and speaks out to David. “Come on. It’s not like I’m going to care… maybe I’ll be the pretty one for once” The chuckling sound from behind the tree reassures Daniel that his friend had not lost the entire person he was and tempts him again to come out. “Come on David, I need your help” “You only ever call when you need help. What is it that you need then?” “Can I beat him?” “Who? I thought you were retired! What you doin’ wrestlin’ again gob shite!” “I was, well sort of. John Adams…” “Nope, he’s gunna kill ya” It had been a long while since he’d heard the dry wit of David but Daniel still knew there was some love in the air. He laughs with him for a few minutes and then adopts a serious look and asks again “Is he really that good?” Instead of replying David finally makes himself known. He walks out from behind the tree and takes a seat beside Logan and smiles. For a moment Daniel can’t even see what had changed about him but after a few moments the grey in the hair and the appearance of a few wrinkles were apparent, Daniel just laughs. “Don’t laugh, it’s not funny” He carries on laughing only louder this time. David looks shocked and angry for a while as he watches his friend laugh at his misfortune but after a few more minutes seems to see the funny side and begins to laugh too. The threat of John Adams doesn’t seem to have bothered Logan yet, possibly it’s because in truth it doesn’t matter if he looses David’s right. Daniel should be retired and thinking about his mortgage not coming back wrestling a young kid because he was bored, but that’s not him. He looks up again as “Phoenix” reaches its highest point of the night. 9 O’clock, he says to himself, the time didn’t really matter to him now but it’d matter come training tomorrow. He brushes his hair back with his fingers and stretches out as David begins to settle down and talk again. “He’s good Daniel, better than you are” “Bigger, heavier, stronger too by the sounds of this bio…” Daniel takes out the paper from his jacket and hands it to his friend. David takes it from him and looks at it for a moment shaking his head disbelieving that Daniel would even bother attending the match-up let alone asking whether or not he could win. “But the question isn’t that, its can I beat him” “You forgot younger. He’s beatable and has a match in LOW this week too but I don’t think some rusty piece of work like you is going to do much in the way of stopping him now do you?” Daniel shrugs his shoulders obvious that he does believe that he can win. For a moment the two men look at one another, each wanting to speak but too worried at what the other might say. The silence again holds for a few minutes becoming increasingly uncomfortable until they both speak at once… “I with Marie…” “… I can win” They both look at one another again and laugh not taking in fully what exactly the other had said. Daniel gestures to David to speak first and nodding his approval David begins “Marie and Max split up. He was hitting her, she’s with me now…” Daniel nods understanding what had been said and accepting the reason why. He’d guessed that they were seeing one another before Marie and Max split but that in truth had nothing to do with him, he was happy for them both they were good for one another. David turns from Daniels gaze sure that Daniel would hate him for taking their best friends child and turning her into his lover and in truth ashamed that he had allowed himself to do it too. “She’s a woman now. Old enough to decide for herself what she wants…” “I approve David, don’t worry” “You do?” Daniel nods his head and offers his hand in congratulations, David grasps it tightly and the two men shake. David breaks out a smile for the first time that night and brings Daniel in for a hug, Daniel pushes him off though and the two men laugh again. “You really don’t think I’ll win?” David shakes his head “Marie doesn’t want you to fight, she thinks if you reckon you’ll loose, you won’t bother” “You think it’ll work like that?” David sighs and shakes his head. No he says to himself, he knows that won’t work as did Marie really. But letting you into the ring Daniel is like giving you a knife and leaving you in the room with Emma for an hour, something you just wouldn’t do. Daniel knows what’s going through David’s mind; the same in truth had been running through his. If he was to step into the ring he wouldn’t be able to hold himself to just the one fight and that’d be where the problems would start again. Four-Winds-Wrestling would reel him in and he’d been book able, something that he hadn’t been for the best part of a year. The thought of it scared him a little, non competitive wrestling like he was about to have with Adam’s was fine. He could no show and there weren’t many people who’d give a damn, sure his opponent would be pissed but it wouldn’t be such a sin. Once you’re back in the ring competitively though it’s hard to get out of. It could mean travelling around and with Sarah still only young it would mean that Liz would be reluctant to travel with him. He’d loose them for months on end, quiet possibly forever. “No Dan I don’t. I think you’ll fight it and if you loose fight again until you start winning. It’s the way you are” Daniel nods his head accepting his fate, in truth accepting what he’d known he was going to do all along. John “The Prophet” Adams would be his first fight back and could possibly end up being his last. The doubt on him being his last though was big enough for Daniel to start planning in his head now. Planning for how he’d tell Elizabeth later on that he was going back on the road, planning how he was going to make his return. “I know David, but I can’t help it…” David laughs and the two men embrace like the friends they are. Daniel smiles as they do, knowing that he has made up his mind to fight and in truth that was the hardest part of the battle already won. Daniel Logan, “The River Avon” was ready to flow again, and this time, take with him the seeds of self doubt that he had planted in the dry ravine that was his heart. “Alright boy-o, you’ve got training to do… and I’ve got to get back to the wife.” “Wife!” “Erm…” @ End @
