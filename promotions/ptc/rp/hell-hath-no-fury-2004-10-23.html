Hell hath no fury...

23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-23 Craig stood under the shower, yawned expansively and scratched his armpit. The floor had been as uncomfortable as he had expected last night. But even still, he would have been able to sleep were it not for the little noises Nurse made in her sleep. She sounded so sweet and innocent and it was an image of Nurse that he would cherish. He had always maintained that beneath the layers of her hard façade and cruel image, there was a sweet soul waiting to be let out. The peacefulness etched on her face as she slept and the way in which she had been treating him recently coupled with her advances last night cemented this for Craig. Nurse WAS a nice person, no matter what everyone who had ever met her said. Even the conversation they had had last night didn’t change his mind. Craig had wondered why Nurse had denied Laura Almasy a match. Everyone claimed she had fixed it so that Laura would never get a shot at her. When he asked Nurse about it, she had laughed and said, “She was desperate to face me. I denied her that chance simply because it was amusing to see that etched on her face, to know that she craved everything that I had. I teased her with the prospect of a match against me, let her think she’d get her precious chance, and then whipped the rug out from underneath her, just so I could watch the light go out in her eyes as she realised she’d never be as good as me. She lost to Rhinox three times for chissake!” Yes, it was cruel of Nurse to do that to Laura, but wrestling was a harsh business and nice people got walked over. It didn’t change his mind about her; Nurse had a heart… somewhere. Lost in his musings, he didn’t hear the sound of the bathroom door being opened. The shower curtain was roughly pulled back. Craig hastily covered his naked body as best he could as he stared in shock at the person standing there. “Have you washed behind your ears yet? I brought you some special soap, we can’t run the risk of you getting ill because you haven’t scraped off all the nasty little bacteria.” Horrified, Craig screamed at his mother, “I’m naked… in the shower! You can’t come barging in like this!” His mother tut-tutted at him and reached in armed with a sponge. “It’s not like I haven’t seen it all before! Don’t be such a silly boy. I’m sure you haven’t been looking after yourself properly while I was away, boys never do.” Craig fixed her with a hard stare. “I know what I’m doing, just go.” Seeing he wasn’t going to cooperate, she sighed and left. Craig started to remember exactly why it had been so easy for him to go with Nurse’s plan and get rid of his parents. He did love his mother, but she could be so… so infuriating! Were all mothers like this, or just his? Having tasted freedom, or as much freedom as Nurse had allowed him, was he going to be able to go back to his old life? A life where his mother wrapped him in cotton wool, poured cod liver oil down his throat daily and refused to let him go outside to play in case he caught germs from other boys. Was it any wonder Craig had spent most of his teenage years sat at his computer… that was the one part of his world she couldn’t control. Nurse slowly opened the door and crept into the room. She quietly stripped off her clothes and stepped into the shower. Craig screamed. “W-what’s going on?” Nurse smiled seductively. “I know the only reason you wouldn’t take me last night was because you were afraid we’d be caught by your mother. I don’t think she’ll intrude on us in here, so come on, let’s get down to it!” Nurse pressed her body against his and rubbed her hands over his thin chest. Craig groaned with pure pleasure, his eyes drinking in the sight of her. “We can’t… we shouldn’t!” “Oh come on Craig,” Nurse giggled, “we are man and wife now!” It was the mention of her deception that brought Craig back to his senses. Was this just another ruse? Another way for Nurse to rule him? He wanted her – desperately- but not like this! Not if it was only her body and not her heart that was involved in the act. Craig had always taken sex seriously. “My Mum will come in, she already did in fact! You’ve got to leave.” “Your mother,” Nurse spat, “is that bitch all you care about? What about me? Would you take her side over mine?” Craig looked into Nurse’s eyes, they were as hard and cold as agate. The caring and compassion he had seen in them last night was no where to be found. Perhaps he had imagined it? “Esme, you’re just as bad, if not worse than her! How many times do I have to tell you, she’s my mother, and I love her. Can’t you understand that?” As he sensed her incomprehension, Craig realised the futility of his question. Nurse had gouged out the eyes of her own mother and left her for dead not long before he met her. No, she wouldn’t understand the bond between a parent and child. A feeling of sadness rose in him. He grieved for all that she must have missed out on during her childhood. Nurse looked at the tears running freely down Craig’s face to disappear as they mixed with the water from the shower. Disgusted, she stepped out and proceeded to dry her body. So, he was all talk and no action? If this tack wasn’t going to work, then, she’d just have to find some other way of making herself indispensable. But how? A moment later, Nurse slapped herself on the head. Of course! How could she have forgotten… GTT4. Her mind had been so full with all that had happened yesterday that she’d almost forgotten about the tournament. Craig needed her for that. Nurse dressed and peeked around the shower curtain “Are you still crying? There’s no time for that. We have work to do. You’ll never beat Almasy without my help. Gym in five minutes.” Craig shook his head, “No, I don’t want to. I don’t care about Almasy. And besides, I doubt Mum will let me wrestle anymore. It’s too dangerous, I could get hurt.” Nurse stood there, astounded. Had his mother really managed to rid him of his drive in just twenty four hours? “I’ll sort that bitch out! I won’t let her do this to you, to us!” Angry now, Nurse stormed out the room, slamming the door behind her. Craig pulled his clothes on his still wet body and ran after her. What the hell was she up to? Nurse found Mrs. Goat in the lounge, calmly arranging some plastic flowers in a vase (real flowers cause allergies) while her husband slouched on the sofa his eyes glued to the television. She made no acknowledgement of Nurse’s presence. Nurse hated being snubbed and so with a sweep of her arm, knocked the vase to the floor where it smashed. Furious, Mrs. Goat raised her hand to slap Nurse but quick as lightening, Nurse grabbed it and dug her nails into the wrinkled flesh. “We had such a… nice… chat last night. But I don’t think I made myself clear enough Mrs Goat, or may I call you Gabi?” “No! You most certainly may not! Now let go of me or I’ll set my husband on you.” Mr. Goat looked embarrassed and sunk even lower onto the sofa, eager to be out of sight, out of mind. Nurse laughed, almost cackled in fact, “Him? I don’t think so! He’s weak… just like you’re trying to make Craig weak! How can you do this to him? How can you turn your own son into a snivelling mess of a man?” Nurse let go of Mrs. Goat and glared at her, hands on hips. “This is MY family and I’ll do whatever I want with it. Craig IS weak, he always as been and that’ll never change.” Craig stood in the doorway, his eyes filled with fear as he watched the battle of will between the two most important people in his life. Nurse pointed to him, “He may look it now, but I assure you, he’s NOT a wimp, he’s a wrestler, and a damn good one at that!” Mrs. Goat sighed, “Yes, I know all about it. His precious little internet efed. Is he still doing that? I know all about the Intercontinental Hardcore Bloodbath of Doom and Destruction Title Belt he won. I’ve heard him go on and on about his victories over The Amazing Wildman, Dr. Death and El Mysterious Magico. It’s all make believe writing… that doesn’t make him strong, it makes him a geek.” Nurse knew all about Craig’s career as a wrestling role player, and dismissed it with a wave of her hand. “You’ve been stuck in a nuthouse for too long! Did you know that your son is a real wrestler with real blood and real broken bones and real victories? In the past month, he has beaten three well known stars; Jonathan Borealis, Jeanna Ryder and Flyin’ Brian Calvin. Even if you don’t recognise those names, then surely you’ve heard of Seymour Almasy?” Seeing Mrs. Goat nod she continues, “Craig is facing Almasy in the bracket semi-finals of GTT4 this weekend, and up until you arrived and robbed him of his hard-won confidence, he was on target to beat him!” Mrs. Goat screamed. Her son involved in violence? Her sickly boy letting himself get beaten up for money? Surely Nurse was lying? She met her son’s eyes, imploring him to deny this. “It’s true Mum, I am a wrestler, and I could be a really good one too.” Craig declared with pride. Mrs. Goat shook her head vigorously. “No. No, this won’t continue! Craig, I forbid you. You’ll have to tell Mr. Almasy that you won’t be able to come out to play this weekend. It’s too dangerous and I can’t let you get hurt!” Nurse saw the light starting to dim in Craig’s eyes. Mrs. Goat was winning! “Who are you to stop him doing what he wants? He’s a grown man with the ability to make up his own mind!” Mrs. Goat smirked slyly, “You think he can make his own decisions? That’s fine with me. Craig, which life do you choose; Nurse Wretched and your little wrestling career… or your mother?” Craig lowered his head and stared at the floor mumbling, “I don’t wanna lose my career, and I don’t want to lose you Mum. Can’t we find a compromise?” Disgusted at Craig’s weak answer, Nurse turned to him, grabbed him and kissed him roughly on the mouth. “Goodbye Craig, I can see I’m getting nowhere with you. I can’t stay here and watch this, you sicken me. If you ever find the balls to stand up to that bitch, you know where to find me.” With a quick backhanded swipe at Mrs. Goat, Nurse stormed from the room. A few moments later, the front door slammed. Craig sank to the floor sobbing. A few days ago, he was happily getting ready for the biggest match of his career so far. Nurse was being pleasant to him for the first time ever and life seemed perfect. But now? Now everything had changed, he was back to being plain old Craig Goat, nothing special. The bubble had burst. Unable to stand the smug feeling of victory emanating from his mother, Craig dragged himself up the stairs and into bed. Later that night… Unable to sleep due to the mandela of emotions coursing through him and the disparate loyalties pulling him apart inside, Craig got up out of bed and headed down towards the kitchen. Seeing the light of the TV still flickering in the lounge, he paused at the door. “Dad? Is that you?” Mr. Goat was lying on the sofa his eyes half closed watching a documentary with the sound down. Seeing Craig, he smiled softly, sat up and patted the cushion beside him – an invitation for Craig to join him. “Nature films. Watch enough of them and you can answer all of life’s questions.” Craig sat down, curious. This was the longest sentence his father had uttered since leaving the asylum. “Why’s that? Are we so similar to animals?” “Not really. I just find them so boring I have no choice but to entertain myself with my own thoughts and philosophies. Out of nowhere, Craig blurts a question he had been longing to ask for years, “Dad, why did you marry Mum? You’re so different from each other.” “Well, if I’d married a woman more like me, we’d just sit across the dinner table from each other night after night with nothing to say to each other. I’m shy and insecure, I know that, but you’re mother brings out the best in me. If it wasn’t for her, I’d never have found the courage to have had so many exciting affairs.” “What!?” The thought of his father, the original mild mannered man, having an affair was almost too much for Craig to take in. Mouth agape, he stared hard at the man beside him wearing a silly smile. “Oh come on Craig, don’t be so naive. Still waters run deep and all that. I love your mother, but she can be a bit… prickly at times.” “Prickly? She’s more than that. She’s controlling and manipulative and evil even!” Not denying this, he replied, “Just like your young lady-wife, eh? Listen, the thing you need to understand about your mother, and your wife, is that they do what they do purely out of love and a conflicting need to both protect you from the world and push you to the heights of your potential.” Frustrated, Craig slammed his fist on the arm of the sofa, “But they never get it right!” “Of course not, they’re women! When it comes to your mother, just do what I do; nod, agree and then do what you were going to do in the first place anyways. If I’d done everything your mother had told me to over the years, we’d have lost this house and everything in it through her bad stock market choices. The trick you need to learn is getting her think everything was all her idea in the first place. Give me a few weeks and she’ll be claiming to be your manager and waving a foam finger in the front row at all your matches. Until then, be careful, lie convincingly and do your own thing. I’m proud of you son. So is your mother, but you’ll never hear her say it.” Not for the first time that day, tears welled up in the corner of Craig’s eye. He quickly wiped them away with his sleeve and looked at his father properly for what seemed like the first time and said, “Thanks Dad, I love you too.” Mr. Goat waved his hand meekly in the air, “No need to act like a pansy, son. That’s just the way families are. You’re a grown up with your own wife now, so about time you understood that. You have your own life to live, don’t let us old folks hold you back from that. Just go kick seven types of crap out of that Almasy bastard and get your wife back!” Almost exhausted from the effort of such unaccustomed speech, Mr. Goat turns his gaze once more to the TV. As Craig slowly left the room, a smile spread across his face. Only seven types of crap? Surely he could do better than that!
