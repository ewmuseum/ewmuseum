Jimmy Who?

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 Mike Montgomery…World Champion, Executive Vice President of Talent Relations for the UWF, stands in front of his office window, staring down on the Chicago cityscape. The VP’s desk is remarkably clean, computer on one corner, an open manilla folder with the card for the September 12 Sunday Afternoon Supremacy in the middle, and a printer on the other corner. Sitting on the far side of the middle of the desk, the only item seemingly out of place, is an open briefcase. Sitting near the briefcase, is Montgomery’s seven foot two inch, three hundred and twenty pound bodyguard/personal assistant, Gavin Frost. Hands clasped behind his back, Montgomery bounces up and down on his feet for an instant before asking, “What’s next on the agenda Mr. Frost?” “GTT 4 and your first round match.” Comes Frost’s immediate answer. “Right…right…I take it we’ve found out who the opponent is?” Frost nods, pulling a manilla folder from the briefcase. He slides the folder across the desk, towards Montgomery. Montgomery turns from the window, picks up the folder, and begins leafing through it. “James ‘Jimmy’ Bong,” Frost recites, “Born March 13, 1976 in London, England. Five feet, nine inches in height and one hundred sixty five pounds.” “Anything on his history?” Montgomery asks, now looking more closely at the contents of the folder. “Mr. Bong, it seems, doesn’t really have a history. As of yet he has not signed with any federation anyone’s heard of. He's only made two appearances at the PTC Offices. Once to register, and once to sign up for the tournament." Mike slides into his chair, continuing to look over the file, "Mr. Frost, I am not finding myself overly concerned with what I'm seeing. Am I to understand that Mr. Bong has had absolutely no in ring time whatsoever?" "It's looking that way," Frost answers, "apparently he's some sort of secret agent." Montgomery stops scanning the file and looks up, "You're joking." "I wish I was. According to what your people at Asylum Industries dug up, Mr. Bong is a member of the OHMCS.." "OHMCS?" "On Her Majesty's Catering Service. Agent Double Oh Zero to be precise. Even then, he hasn't exactly been overly successful." "No?" "No. Thus far, Mr. Bong has yet to complete a successful mission for her Majesty." "Agent Double Oh Zero indeed. What about his fighting skills, Mr. Frost? There has to be SOMETHING that makes this guy SOME sort of threat." Frost simply shakes his head, "Well, if things go his way, he has his own Frog Splash, but otherwise, there's not much. Punching, kicking, perhaps a karate chop, and lots and lots of running." Montgomery rubs his temples for a moment before burying his head in his hands. He stays in this position for a moment, contemplating his first round GTT match up. Finally, he brings his head up, hands falling to his chair. He simply sits there, seemingly frozen in place. Eventually, slowly, a smile begins to make it's way across his face. "You seem rather...non-chalant about all this." Frost muses. "You could say that," comes Montgomery's answer, "in fact...I think non-chalant is just the word to describe my attitude toward this match. You, of course, weren't around for my Universal Title Tournament match last year Mr. Frost." "True." "Well, let me summarize for you. There I was, complete rookie to the inter-fed scene. The Universal Title Tournament...my first entry into a non-UWF event. In essence, I WAS Jimmy Bong. And who do I draw? The Legend. The man who's not only climbed to the top of the mountain, but he's taken up residence there. He's seen it all, he's done it all, he's pretty much won everything there is to win. Jay Phoenix. It's amazing how things can change in a year, isn't it? Now, I've joined Jay at the top, and it's Jimmy Bong that's going to pave my way to the top." "You're not seriously comparing yourself to Jay Phoenix are you?" Montgomery shakes his head, "Nooo...not in the least. Once I beat Jay, I can compare myself to him, but until then, no. I'm not that foolish. Who else do we have in my bracket." Frost takes a sheet of paper out of his briefcase, briefly scanning the bracket, "Misfit...Rollins…Seymour, a very strong UWF presence in the Appalachian division. Not to mention guys like Joey Vendetti from the XUW, Darek DeAngelo from 4WW…the list goes on and on." "In other words, after this first round I'll have my work cut out for me." "Basically, yes." Montgomery stands up again. He arches his back, reaching toward the ceiling. His back speaks, several popping sounds heard as he stretches, "Well, I think we deserve a little something special. After all, we've got a bit of a flight ahead of us." The UWF Champ walks across his office toward the far wall where a walk-in humidor can be seen. He opens it up, reaches in and with draws two, top of the line cigars straight from Havanna, Cuba. He also grabs a lighter and cigar snipper, then returns to his desk. Montgomery snips off the end of one before passing it to Frost. He sits down, swings his feet up onto the desk, and snips the end off his own cigar. Montgomery chews on his cigar for an instant, then lights it before taking several puffs. He passes the lighter over to Gavin Frost who lights his own cigar. Montgomery leans back in his chair, smiling as he watches the ringlets of smoke curl toward the ceiling. The two men sit this way for several moments before a thought suddenly occurs to Frost, "Do you remember that Brandy snifter your brother sent you after Terror in Tokyo?" Mike smiles, closing his eyes, "Brandy's in the fridge, there's a cart beside it with everything else." After handing his cigar to Montgomery, Frost walks over to the fridge located on the opposite side of the entrance from the humidor, and takes out the bottle of Brandy. He takes out the ice tray from the freezer, and fills a bucket sitting on the near by cart. Bringing the cart over, Frost places a cube of ice in each glass, then pours in the brandy. He passes a glass to Montgomery, then returns to his own chair, glass in hand. Montgomery, for his part, swirls the amber liquid around the glass, then takes a sip, "This...my dear Mr. Frost...this is the life." Montgomery takes another sip of brandy, letting the liquid sit in his mouth for a moment before swallowing. Putting the glass down, Montgomery takes another puff from his cigar, leaning his chair back once more, and savors the moment. "Yes it is Mr. Montgomery. Without a shadow of a doubt, this is the life." Still smiling, Montgomery comments, "And just imagine...if I were to go all the way...if I were to do the unthinkable and de-throne Seymour Almasy, if I were to become the PTC Global Champion...what oh what will life be like then?" Montgomery takes another drink of brandy, before enjoying his cigar some more. "And Jimmy Bong?" Frost asks. "Who cares? Jimmy Bong is nothing. A stepping stone. No more, no less." “That’s all you think of Jimmy Bong?” “Jimmy who?” Montgomery asks, swirling the ice cube around his glass for a moment. “Non-chalant indeed.” Frost mutters, smiling.
