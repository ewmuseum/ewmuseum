The Insanity of The Prophecy.

7 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-07 ~ There's a fine line between genius and insanity. I have erased this line. Oscar Levant (1906 - 1972) ~ ~ As I was walking among the fires of Hell, delighted with the enjoyments of Genius; which to Angels look like torment and insanity. I collected some of their Proverbs. William Blake (1757 - 1827), "The Marriage of Heaven and Hell", 1790 ~ ~There has been many Prophets over the years in history, some of them great men. Some though have been known in the world as witches, warlocks, and in league with the devil himself! And why was that I ask you? Was it because they would prophesize the coming of Wars, famine, and Death? Or was it because people were just blind of the truth that was laid before them? The truth I lay before you is that the Mountain known as GTT4 is a stepping stool with the promise of greatness, and I can see the top of that mountain from where I am, question is will you be able to stop me? ~ Location: Riverside County’s Mental Institution for the Criminally Insane. Time: 12:35 AM Date: January 15th, 1996 ~The beginning of Insanity! ~ ::::: The scene opens up to a hospital that was built in Riverside County to have the mentally insane stay for awhile. It is the same hospital that a one Mr. Frank Adams had built before the tragic murder of his wife, and it is the same hospital that Frank Adams put his only son in to keep him away from the world as punishment for his existence to begin with. The Hospital sat on a large hill in Riverside County, the hill had little to no vegetation growing, and if the building inspectors weren’t owned by Mr. Frank Adams then the building would never pass code then. But the existence of the hospital has happened and it is a little too late to complain about the shabby off white containment facility of the mentally insane. The Hospital has 4 levels, each with its own level of severity. The largest level of severity is at the bottom level, where the Criminally Insane is kept. And thanks to a large amount of money being passed along, that is where the young John Adams is being held. ::::: ~The end of innocence! ~ -Room 24 – ::::: The room is very dark and cold, there is an old dirty rusted white sink in the left hand corner of the room. And a metallic dirty silver pan in the right for a toilet. A squeaky old rickety bunk bed is seen in the middle of the room as it catches the drops of water from the leak on the ceiling of the room. The only light that can be seen is coming from the window of the old metallic steel door containing its two residents. The walls of the room have a lot of cracks hiding the maggots and other bugs that reside there. In one corner opposite of the toilet, curled up in a ball is the young John Adams roommate rocking back and forth as to shake the voices in his head. His name is Russell and the story with this mid 40’s, and balding with shit filled clothes individual is that he thought the only way to keep his family safe was to burn them alive in his house. But where is the young John Adams? We can see him on the bottom bunk wishing and hoping for a miracle to set him free. ::::: ::: A small brown rat can be seen crawling to the floor towards Russell from a nearby hole. ::: Russell: Ahhhh Charles how nice of you to join us again! ::: Russell fondles the rat named Charles in his hand with a pleased calmed look on his face. ::: ~The corruption of a child! ~ Russell: What’s wrong Charles? Are you afraid of the big mean people getting you my friend? Well as your friend and only companion it is my duty to hide you, but where? ::::: Russell gets up to have a look around. He looks inside the soaked covers of his top bunk, inside some of the cracks, and even looks under the covers of the young John Adams. ::::: Young John Adams looking at Russell in disgust: Get the hell away from me! Russell: Well I’m hurt Johnny! I can’t believe you of all people would not help me hide Charles, and after all the nice things he said about you! (Looking at the rat.) Well Charles don’t worry my friend, I do have one place that they would never think of finding you! And the good part is that me and you buddy can be together forever! ::::: Russell looks at Charles the rat with deep eyes of friendship before he takes a big bite out of him. The last screech of the rat pierces the young John Adams ears as it sends a shiver down the young lad’s spine. And John’s gag reflex had to work overtime when bits and pieces of the rat’s once whole body flew and splattered John’s clothes. John Adams looks at Russell in disbelief as Russell with a pleased look in his face concentrates to make sure that he didn’t leave any of Charles remains anywhere but his mouth. ::::: ~With the consumption of love and friendship! ~ Russell after he is finished: Ahhhhh there you go buddy, now you and me can be together forever! ::::: In a burst of a second three guards in white coats come into the room and grabs Russell. You see everyday around this time the Doctors would send in a couple of orderly’s to fetch the residents for a mild does of electric shock therapy followed after by a extra heavy dose of medications. And for the residents of room 24 today were no different, and Russell was lucky number one. It didn’t mind John Adams any for he took this time to get some sleep and for a 14 year old kid he took every minute of it. A couple of hours had pass witch is strange for the treatment only would take a hour tops, when the orderly’s came into the room and woke Adams up from his only means of escape from this hell he was in. ::::: Orderly #1: Adams, wake up! Your roommate Russell died tonight cause of a heart attack! You’ll be getting a new roommate in two weeks time! Orderly #2: Yeah, so get up! It’s time for your treatment with the doctor! ::::: John got up sluggishly due to sleep deprivation and tried to follow the orderly’s. But after two feet weakness got to his knees as he collapsed onto the floor. This didn’t please the orderly’s one bit, (Why did they have to waste there time with this snot nose brat!) they thought as two of them proceed to kick the weakened youth. ::::: Orderly #1: Get up you stupid piece of shit! ~The ever constant cycle! ~ ::::: Piece of shit, words John Adams was all too familiar with as sleep had still taken its toll with him changing the orderly into the image of his father Frank Adams. Teary eyed and still weak John musters up the strength and courage to carry on to the destination of his treatment. The rest of the night after his treatment he had nightmares, nightmares of Russell’s dead corpse getting eaten by rats, with maggots and worms crawling in and out of every crevice of the body. But not all bad as a ray of sunshine shinned upon him, he realized that he had two weeks of rest. Two whole weeks of getting all the sleep he could before another lunatic becomes a roommate of room 24. During the two weeks John Adams does get some more sleep, but not much with the recurring nightmares of Russell and even his Father. Then the day happened, an Orderly and a Doctor come in to room 24 with a patient. The Patient is about 24 year’s old, brown hair with one bloodshot, lazy eye. ::::: ~The meaning of fear! ~ Doctor: Hello John, how are you feeling today? John Adams: I’m doing fine Doctor; really I don’t need another roommate! Doctor: Now John, we talked about this. There I not enough rooms in the hospital to hold everyone in a separate room. So this John is your new roommate……..Colman Dowell. I’m sure you and Mr. Dowell will get long quite nicely. ::::: The Orderly pushes Colman Dowell into the room quite forcefully as him and the Doctor take there leave of room 24. As the door closes Colman Dowell turns real quick and stares a hole right into John Adams, as if he was staring right into his soul. And with no purpose what so ever he let’s out an evil diabolical laugh that sends fear right into the heart of John Adams. (The man is a complete loon!) Thought John Adams as he assumed the position of fear like the one he had when Russell was alive. This was going to be a relationship made purely of fear. ::::: ~The teachings of a Prophet! ~ Location: Riverside County’s Mental Institution for the Criminally Insane the outside facilities. Time: 12:00 PM Date: January 25th, 1996 ::::: It was a beautiful day if any at the Hospital and the doctors decided to let there patients use the outside faculties to work out. They had weights, basketball, volley ball, and a wall for some of the patients to play hand ball. John Adams decided to do a little hand ball since none of the others ever liked to play. A couple of men named Freddy and Sid, who are in for raping children in the name of God head towards John Adams. They place there hand on each of John Adams shoulder and startle him. ::::: Freddy: Hey Johnny boy! How are you today my son? You know my child that God loves you as he does all of his children. John Adams: He does? Then wh..why am I in a place like this? Sid: So you can be graced with the presence of us, it was your destiny and God’s will that led to this. Freddy: And our job as children of God is to follow his will! Sid: And Johnny, his will shall be done! ::::: And with that they grab John Adams with the intent to molest him. But from the shadows behind the two men comes Colman Dowell. He grabs for Freddy’s head and does a DDT to the hard ground below, causing Freddy’s head to literally split open spilling the blood unto the soil beneath. Colman looks at Sid with a blank expression on his face. Sid rushes towards Colman like a rabid animal, but Colman counters with a kick to the gut and also delivers a lightning fast DDT to the ground next to Freddy. John Adams looks on in amazement of the moves that were displayed. I mean he always was interested with wrestling because of TV, but to see it displayed up close was certainly a treat. That night in room 24 he opened up to Colman Dowell for the first time. And for the first time since the tragic death of his mother somebody listened. ::::: ~The birth of a Prophecy! ~ ::::: From there on Colman Dowell not only taught John Adams the philosophy that went into wrestling, but also taught him the mechanics of it every time they went to the outside facilities. And over the years John Adams grew and got stronger with each passing second. Colman Dowell inspired John Adams so much that he decided when he got out, continuing the training and getting into the business he so very loved and to this day he still remembers the teachings of his Trainer, and friend Colman Dowell. ::::: ~ I decided that it was not wisdom that enabled [poets] to write their poetry, but a kind of instinct or inspiration, such as you find in seers and prophets who deliver all their sublime messages without knowing in the least what they mean. Socrates (469 BC - 399 BC), In "Apology," sct. 21, by Plato. ~ ~Fin. ~
