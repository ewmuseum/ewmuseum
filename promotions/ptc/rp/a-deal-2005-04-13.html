A deal

13 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-13 <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Professional wrestling...those words mean something different to just about every single person you could ever talk to. It has the potential to mean absolutely everything good or bad that you could possibly imagine, and that would just be within the first five people you spoke with. What does it mean to Enrique Morales? That is a question that many have wondered and yet none have had the ability to get the answer to. The answer to that question is at the very heart of why I've chosen to take part in this tournament to crown the best cruiserweight wrestler in the world. My desire to succeed and be rememberd can often over ride my better judgement in matters such as this one. Is it wise to enter a tounament at the age of thirty-four and attempt to go toe to toe with all these young pups who attempt to make either a name for themselves...or a bigger one than they already have? Should I worry that my athletism won't be quite up to scale with all the men and women who are going to come bounding into this tournament full of energy and desire. Ready and willing to bounce off those ropes, throwing caution to the wind, in the hopes of not only entertaining the fans but of winning and becoming the next big house hold name in our sport. It would be very easy to get all caught up in the moment and fall prey to someone who is throwing themselves around like a weapon of mass destruction. Still... This tournament isn't about bouncing off the ropes, doing plancha's, and wowing the damn fans who will be at the matches. Being the best cruiserweight in the world isn't going to make me sell out to those American ideals of what a smaller wrestler should be. No, in fact, this tournament is about redemption. Not just for myself, not just for Mexican superstars the world over, but for cruiserweights the world over. No longer will smaller men, or women, who choose to get into this business have to be held down by the stigma that a smaller wrestler simply can't do anything but fly around a ring and act like a human pinball. Redemption is the answer to all the questions of this tournament, gringos, and it's the answer that Enrique Morales will deliever. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "Senor Morales I respect your decision, as much as anyone possibly could, but you know you don't have to do this." Those words flowed from the lips, in the most beautiful and perfect sounding spanish accent, of none other than one half, and possibly the better half, of Los Diablos...Baltasar. The masked Mexican who refers to himself as The Red Devil has been friends with Morales for many years now. The desire to make Mexico proud that flows through Baltasar is matched, and possibly even bettered, by only that of Enrique Morales. The man has no quit or desire to be out done in his body and he'll show it to the world every chance he is given. "Baltasar, my dear friend, you know I must. I can not sit back and watch as yourself and Angelus do all the work for making our country proud...then go along and bask in the glorly of it like I had some sort of help in accomplishing it. I must compete in this tournament, and if at all possible, win in order to place my stamp in the book for all Mexican wrestlers of the future." Morales replied with the deepest of honesty. You can, and will always be able to, tell that when Morales speaks of his love of not only Mexico and it's great athletes, but also of his desire to change stereo types in the eyes of other countries...that a person couldn't be more sincere than he. "Some how, essa, I knew you'd say something like that to me." Baltasar replied with a friendly sigh and a pat on the back as he sat down in the leather chair, directly across, from the black sofa that lined the back wall of the living room area of their home. Los Diablos and their manager, Morales, all live in this nice, but not extravagent, four bedroom home in San Antonio. It's not a permanent residence, as they all still have homes in their home country of Mexico, but it's their refuge when in the United States due to their passion for the wrestling business. "That, Mr. Morales, is what will be your downfall." Both Morales and Baltasar quickly turn their heads to the entry way of the living room. A look of shock comes over their face as a man, mid twenties or so, with some dark hair about shoulder length stands in the doorway. His fairly muscular frame blocks the view to the otherside of the door. "How did you get in here...and just who the hell are you, essa?" Morales questions as him and Baltasar rise to their feet and look the man up and down, as he walks out of the shadows of the other room, and into the soft light of the living area, where mainly only quiet reading or reflection had been done before. "I walked in same as both of you," the man replied with bit of a confident grin on his face, "and the name is Jacob Cross." Both the members of PRIME's current roster just looked at one another in confusion. Neither one had ever met this man, nor did they know of him, so why would he have just walked into their home without warning, or more importantly, an invitation? "That means nothing to us, chico, seeing as how we've never met. The only thing I can figure is you must be crazy and that is why you have just barged into my home." Baltasar took a step forward a moment after finishing his analysis of the person in their living room. Cross, simply stood in the middle of the room, never looking in Baltasar's direction as he admired a Spanish painting on the wall. "Easy El Chupa Cobera. I am not here to fight or cause trouble. I'm here to lend a helping hand to your manager and friend there." Cross smiled as he turned away from the painting and headed toward the other chair that set in the room, usually where Angelus would sit, as the two continued to look at him with baffled looks. "Nice painting you have there. Little cheap for my taste but nice for fella's like yourselves." Cross once again smirked, this time more arrogantly, as he sat down. The two Mexican men before him didn't take kindly to his comments and once again Baltasar took a step forward while speaking. "What do you mean by that gringo?" Baltasar questioned as he went to take another step. Morales placed an hand on his shoulder and motioned for him to stop and calm down as he looked into the visitors eyes. "Senor Cross you'll have to forgive us. It's not every day that a stange American gringo simply walks into our living room and offers me help as if he is someone I should care to have the help which they offer." Morales explained with a bit of irritation in his voice and a scowl crossing his dark, tan, face, "Tell me why I your help is suppose to be anything special to me." "Simple. I know the path to success in this business, not just PRIME, and your leaving it far behind with your focus being all wrong." Cross just set back into the chair, another leather one but this one more of a biege, as he stared intently at the manager of Los Diablos. "In PRIME you say? Now I believe I know who you are, essa. Your the Jacob Cross that was once a member of the PRIME company." Morales smiled knowing he must be right and had finally figured out who the man who sat in his living room was. "In the flesh homes." Cross replied without hesitation or even the hint of a smile. Morales shuddered at the mockery in the tone, as Baltasar simply grumbled something under his breath about damned arrogant gringos, and shook his head. "Yes your the man who had your ass handed to you by Rock Startling on your way out of the company. Now your going to help me with advice? I find that quite comical puta." Morales simply grinned to keep from laughing while Baltasar continued to seeth under his mask in the corner of the room, a few feet from the two men. "Puta...thats cute. Listen we can sit here all night and insult one another in any language you like but it won't get you any further in the tournament, or in leading your tag team in PRIME, than you are right now." Once more Cross continued to just stare at Morales, almost like he were burning a hole through the skull of the Mexican manager and wrestler. This time he also sat forward, as did Morales, putting the men almost nose to nose. "How is some never was in the history of PRIME going to help Los Diablos or myself become more successful," questioned the annoyed but intrigued Morales, "I'm intrested to see what you've got to say now." "Good. Before I begin I'll just note to you that my loss to Startling was strictly a good faith deal to a friend. I was leaving PRIME anyway at the time and did him a favor on the way out. Thus helping him push his string of victories along futher and allowing him to set that record that he has been able to brag about every since." Before Cross could continue he was interupted by Baltasar who found his description of the loss to be quite humorous. "Leaving PRIME were you? Why is that homes, couldn't handle the competition?" Both of the Mexican men laughed as Cross simply rolled his eyes at the sheer ignorance of the two. "Personal reasons chica. Now I'd recommend you keep this on topic or I'll bid you good day and you can learn the information about this business that I already know...the long and hard way." Once more Morales simply smirked. His intrest level in this gringo continued to rise as the man displayed himself with more confidence by the moment, and he hadn't lacked any confidence from the moment he walked into the room in the first place. "So, essa, enlighten me. How do you intend to help me out?" Morales questioned while now leaning back into his seat and getting comfortable for the explanation, funny as it might be, that was about to come. "Not so fast Morales. This is America, not Mexico, where all information comes at an equal price. We've got to strike a deal first." Cross replied with a satisfied and confident grin on his face. "A deal? What type of deal homes? You want money or something?" Morales questioned almost offended by the suggestion that he pay this man, who didn't accomplish a ton in his PRIME career, for information. "Money? Don't insult me burrito breath! I've got more money than you could possibly hand over in ten years. Money isn't an issue here," Cross shot back, "a favor is." The request brought a raised eyebrow to the look of Morales. A favor could be many things and often not the best of things as well. "A favor you say? What type of favor?" "The secret kind. The kind that you'll find out about after I help you accomplish what needs to be accomplished." "How do I know I can trust you gringo?" Morales questioned while crossing his arms over his chest as Baltasar now leaned down over the back of his own chair, the conversation intresting him now as well. "You don't. You just have to." Cross replied with a look of stone set across his face. If he was up to something, or had an Ace up his sleeve so to speak, he wasn't giving it away. "Intresting. But trusting someone I know really nothing about would be like asking a thief not to rob you while you sleep, it doesn't make a whole lot of sense," Morales remarked before a grin grew over his face,"besides you look like the kinda guy who would be found working for that dog, Deville. You've got his smug and arrogant attitude and this just smells of a Angelo set up." "Angelo Deville? Are you joking? If I had the time to give a crap about what you actually thought of me personally, I'd be almost offended. Deville, while possibly a powerful man in your eyes, means jack to me. Now do we have a deal or not? I haven't go all day and this deal could be made with anyone looking to be helped into making themselves a superstar." The reply was quick and cold and actually some what startled the leader of Los Diablos. Baltasar however simply chuckled at the thought under his breath. "Well despite all that you've raised my intrest level enough to take the bait my friend. It maybe a risk...but hell I'm all about risks." Morales got up and extended his hand toward his visitor. The idea of striking a deal with an unknown might not have been the most sound move ever, but it seemed like something he could learn something from...whether it be good or bad. "No need to shake hands. I'll take you at your word and you'll keep it." Cross replied coldly as he stood up and brushed off his silk shirt. "Why is that gringo?" Questioned the monsterous Baltasar as he came back into the center of the room and joined the two as they stood there. "Cause a man without his word...is no man at all." Both Morales and Baltasar simply smiled, both knowning that is exactly the way they felt about a mans word. It was his bond, the thing that made him a man. Without it...well you just didn't even think about doing without it. "So when do we start this "lesson" on becoming more successful Senor Cross?" Morales asked as he walked toward the exit of the room, while Cross and Baltasar followed. "Soon enough. Your brain has taken on enough information for one evening Senor Morales. We shouldn't over due it or you'll miss the entire point I'm going to present to you." With that final remark Jacob Cross simply walked out of the living room door and out of the site of the Los Diablos members with a smirk on his face. Morales stood, leaing against the frame of the doorway, a look of wonder on his face. "Do you think he can really be trusted Senor Morales," questioned the Red Devil with a tone that displayed a less than convinced attitude. "As much as any other gringo can be trusted I suppose. We'll see what he has to offer us. If nothing else he'll make good practice for Angelus and yourself when it comes time to go after your PRIME tag titles once more." The big man simply nods his head as he walks out of the room and leaves his friend and mentor to ponder the days events.
