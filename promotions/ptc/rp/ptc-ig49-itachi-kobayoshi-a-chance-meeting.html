Title: A Chance Meeting Character: Itachi Kobayoshi Handler: Bob ~*~ The rain was fierce. Pelting down on the man's boyish face, drenching him, regardless of the overcoat he was donning. He began to feel uneasy, and even a bit foolish. “What am I doing here anyways?... It's raining, and I'm following some weird note like some sort of victim. I should just go...” His voice quivered a bit. Perhaps from the cold, perhaps from the darkness surrounding him in this unseen alleyway. He began pacing softly, as his patience wore thin. “This is foolish. Master would be telling me how stupid I was for following a note... this could be anyone...” His voice quickly losing every tone of hope. His pacing became more frantic, as he kept looking around, hoping something, anything would happen. “Five more minutes... then I'm gone. I need dry clothes... and I need to get ready for class tomorrow.” He kept arguing with himself out loud, as if trying to talk himself out of his own plan. He walks to the far end of the alley, and peaks around the corner, hoping to catch his host on his way. He shakes his head as all he sees are the headlights of cars, and dim lights in the windows of a few shops. Quickly pacing back down to the other end, he does the same thing, with the same disappointing result. He reaches into the pocket of his coat, and pulls out a small piece of paper, trying to ensure he wasn't mistaken. “11pm, Sunday Night, 1205 Morningstar Way. I've got everything exactly right. This was all just a a bunch of false hope... again. I should have known better... They told me he was dead... why can't I just believe them?” He drops the note on the ground, as it floats on the surface of a puddle. His body language tells the tale of disappointment, not just at his false meeting, but at the loss of his final shred of hope. “Master... if you can hear me somewhere... I'll always miss you...” His voice was on the brink of tears. “It is hard to miss... what is not gone...” a low voice said from seemingly nowhere. The man quickly turned, and looked directly into the eyes of a man, who seemed to materialize from the very rain itself. He looked him up and down, a look of absolute shock covering his face. “Mas... Master...? Master Itachi?” He could no longer hold back the emotion as he wrapped his arms around his mentor, nearly screaming in joy. “It's been much too long, Gai. I'm sorry to have kept you waiting... but what's a good surprise without a bit of build up? Now, let's go... We have much to catch up on, and much to capture.” “...Why Itachi? What do we have capture?” Gai asked, confused by his mentor's words. Itachi merely turned and with a determined sparkle in his eye... smiled at Gai. “Primetime Central needs a champion... and you're going to help me answer the call.”
