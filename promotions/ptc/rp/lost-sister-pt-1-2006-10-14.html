Lost Sister (PT.1)

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 CH.1 The Revolution The night sky is dark and luminous; the clouds all over are patterned with all different shapes and sizes. The swirling pattern of dark and gloomy colors was foretelling the pain and turmoil being felt by all in the city. A city with not much to offer, maybe some old stucco style shops with a mom and pop that has been running it since 1905. There were a few more buildings in this small town…a law office with a small white police building on the side. The street in between all of the buildings was an old paved road that had cracks with weeds growing through the up from the earth underneath. The dreary clouds up above acted like the eyes of God, for after looking upon the world he saw, he began to cry. Following the rain was a steady but not to overpowering wind blowing into town, searching for things to knock down to the ground. Near the front of the town was an old sign with a half faded words to welcome any visitor that happened to pass along the way. In a hotel near the border of town, there he waits. Room number 75. He waits for the most grueling match of his career, GTT6 a Match with Jorge Samuelsson. The pinnacle of John Adams career…, two men go in and only one survivor comes out. Yes tomorrow is the biggest match alright, but why can’t John Adams get any sleep? He sat there on the broken down bed looking at a letter that he found recently…. Dear Mr. Adams, We are writing to you to inform you that our company has found one Samantha Reynolds for you as a direct relative of yours. We have set up a meeting for you in an old town near Dallas Texas, Thursday night at 11:00 pm. Thank you sincerely, Mrs. Lois Mane Family Care Associate. Another family member…the simple idea was obviously unreal. All surviving members of his family were found already and yet John Adams has another one? Well according to the other papers he got in this letter he is in the right place. John Adams laid down on the squeaky bed to reflect about his recent developments when all of a sudden the door knocked. Adams gets up to answer the door. When he answered the door there was a woman on the other end of it…she had red hair tied up in a bun and wearing a black power suit. Right next to her was a blond teenage child…she looked about fifteen years old. "Mr. Adams I presume?" The red haired woman says as she slowly enters the room with the child. "Yes, and I guess come in?" Adams says trying to beat her rudeness for just coming into his room. The woman looked all around the hotel room with her right nostril curled up. The Child looked all around and then sat on the squeaky bed. "So Mr. Adams, this is the best you could get in this dumpy town?" "Ahhh, yeah, well first thing first…how is she related to me again?" John says looking at the woman who seems to be the child care official who was just disgusted with the hotel room. "Nice view," the child care worker smirks as she looks out the hotel window at the graveyard down below. "Could this town get any creepier?" she rolls her eyes as she sits, albeit unwillingly, on the bed next to the now seemingly unhappy Child. ‘Yeah, that is kind of ironic,’ John thinks to himself as he himself looks out the window. He turns to face the worker and his newest family member. "So, how is she related again?" "This is Samantha, your sister." The shock of it all was quite overwhelming to Adams that he didn’t know what to say. But as this unexpected family reunion was going on…something much darker and the reek of an unholy stench of evil was going on at the graveyard in the town. The graveyard had stone tablets marking the past this town once had with its inhabitants. The air was all too heavy with the stench of death and decay as a man is seen walking the hollowed ground where souls lay there eternal rest. With every step this dark individual took more death and decay laid in its wake…for example towards the left side of the gravesite a patch of lovely smelling wildflowers filled with gold and blue flowers…now it is all but black filled with the shell of what it used to be before the dark individual walked past. A hand is seen on an old stone marker in the graveyard; the hand is pale and white as new snow falling to the ground. On the ring finger is a silver skull ring to symbolize the marriage to evil this man posses. In the opposite hand he holds a black cane with a red glowing skull as the head of the cane. It was revealed to be John Adams evil Twin Brother Devilin Rubis. Being an identical twin has given him the ability to see what his brother sees. "So we have a sister…interesting, time to give her a family reunion fitting of an Adams!" Devilin says with an evil smirk on his face. Devilin reaches out of his left pocket and pulls out a somewhat large packet of blood. He starts to spread the blood around as he mumbles something to himself, the shape he creates on the ground is that of a pentagram. After he gets done with his devilish artwork he then stands in the middle of the pentagram. The staff he holds begins to glow more intensely of the color red as smoke starts to slip out of the mouth of the skull. The outline from the pentagram starts to glow brightly too. He gets more intense with the chanting when all of a sudden the earth underneath him starts to shake violently. Devilin doesn’t seem to mind as he continues his chant…but as for Adams, Lois Mane, and sister it was more of an inconvenience. "Earthquake!!!" Lois Mane started to scream as she ran to the outside of the door of the hotel room. Adams grabs his sister as he begins to follow the Lois Mane to the outside. Debris starts to fall all over the place from the ceiling of the hotel. While at the graveyard where Rubis is skeletal hands all over start to break through the earthly crust that had held them captive all these years. "It has begun!!" Rubis cackles as his own eyes begin to glow an eerie red. The skeletal figures come fully erect out of the earthly ground…hanging flesh hanging from each bone and random body parts that has not been eaten away by the decay of age were visibly fused with the dark magic that Adams evil twin has used. "Take them, take them all my minions!" Devilin commanded as he pointed his fingers towards the direction of his brother and sister. Meanwhile John Adams, his sister and the Lois Mane stood there dumbfounded as they witnessed the hotel that Adams was staying at crumble down to the ground from witch it was built from. "And you called this safe for you and the child!" Lois Mane complained as she takes the child by the hand. "Listen, you told me to meet you here at a place like this in the first place!" Adams tried to defend himself as he started to follow the two of them. The continued their argument all the way to Lois Mane’s red 2005 Lincoln Continental. They were stopped all of a sudden by the gruesome site of deformed zombies with gruesome, decaying flesh hanging off of them. "What the hell is that??" Lois Mane says as she is suddenly stricken with fear. "Don’t know, but now would be a good time to run, wouldn’t you say?" Adams responded. The three began to run for there lives; intact at the moment. The gruesome ghouls begin their chase as there bony feet begin to pick up speed. Lois Mane is trying to run with red high heel shoes on which apparently is not working for her as she trips over them and lands hard to the ground below. The impact that connected her head with the unforgiving pavement cracked her front teeth. She screams as she sees tiny fragments of her teeth in front of her. The skeletal ghouls take advantage as they pounce on their innocent prey with an unforgiving passion. Adams runs back by the ghouls so he can grab his sister and protect her from the monsters. "Help…Dear God HELP ME!!!" She screams as the monsters send there supernatural hands through the now late Lois Mane’s skull to feast on there preys brains. Adams quickly covers the eyes of the innocent as the two begin there run of panic. The Monsters continue to follow as part of them stay behind to feast on other body parts of the Government agent. One of the Mom and Pop shops opens its doors as they see Adams and his sister running. "Hurry your asses! In here quick!" The elderly gentleman said as he signaled for them to come in the shop. They quickly went inside the building…it was a gun store that the old man sold to hunters hunting desert creatures. "You two okay?" The old man said as he stood by the window of the store. "Yes but we need some of your guns sir…NOW!!" Adams says in a panic. Before the old man could answer a skeletal hand broke through the glass and grabbed the old man. He screamed in pain and horror as the skeletal hand started to rip through the old man’s flesh as it raked upwards back towards the top of the broken window. Blood started to spray all over the place from the scratches left from the monster. The old man still screaming in pain reached for his gun with his last dying breath…tried to shoot through the window before his heart gave out but it was too late. Adams quickly rushes for the counter where the rest of the guns were held and proceeded to grab them as he had his sister stay behind him. He loaded the gun as the skeletal monsters came into the store one by one. "Stay behind me sis, and whatever you do…don’t look!" Adams ordered as he began to shoot into the hellish monsters before him. As he continues his shooting of the monsters, part of his mind wanders to if there is a car or any vehicle for that matter so he and his sister can get out of this hellish town. The bullets tear away at the old decaying flesh making the pieces fly all over the place. The Monsters start to break the front door down as it becomes apparent to Adams that they may just make there last stand there. In there darkest hour without an apparent ray of hope is when Adams mind becomes open to a visitor. "John…John head to the back of the store with the girl…there is a back door leading to a black pickup truck for you two to make your escape." A mysterious yet familiar voice says inside the mind of John Adams. John didn’t have time to question it as he gathers all the guns he can, before booking it with his sister to the back of the store. "RUN!!" Adams screamed to his sister who was partially paralyzed with fear. The two made it to the back, there was an old wooden door blocking there path from escaping to the outside. Adams wasted no time and kicked the door down with all of his might. With the incredible force of Adams the door broke down and fell outwards revealing the outside with the black pickup…just like the voice had said in his mind. Adams loaded up the truck with the guns. As he loaded his sister into the truck, that’s when he sees the skeletal monsters follow through the broken door trying to get Adams and the girl. Adams climbs inside the truck over his sister to the other side by the drivers end. Guided still by the mysterious voice inside his head he was able to find the keys placed in the top visor. "I swear I thought that only happened in Movies!" Adams thought to himself as he started the truck. The Monsters take a giant leap onto the truck but are quickly flown off as Adams begins to head out of there. As he heads out a sense of safety flows all over him…but is quickly abolished as he sees his path blocked by none other then his evil twin Devilin Rubis. Adams covers his sisters’ eyes as he floors it running the master of darkness over with the pick up. "What was that?" The sister says as the truck’s vibration of running someone over is obvious. "It’s a road bump, just keep quiet as I’m trying to drive, ok?" Adams responds as he concentrates on driving away from this horrible place. What does Rubis want with the sister goes through his head, also what was with the mysterious voice…Adams realizes it’s about time he searches for the answers. As he drives off down the road, we see the mangled body of Rubis on the ground…or what’s supposed to be mangled when a heavy truck runs over it. Rubis stands back up with a supernatural presence of floating in the air as his eyes are set to where his brother drove off too. "No matter where you go brother…you can not save her from the destiny I have in stored for her. Your demise will be my triumph!" To be continued…
