Date: 2006-10-27 Some people say there was a first time for everything. There were people in this world who have never had sex. Those people, pure and untainted of the touch of the opposite sex. They have never known the pleasures and fruits that come with sexual intercourse. For some people, it was a personal choice and lifestyle; abstinence being worn like a medal of honor, letting everyone know that they’re waiting until marriage. For others… well, it wasn’t much of a choice. They just didn’t have the abilities to get laid. They were either too ugly, too poor, didn’t lack the charisma to woo a girl into bed, or just couldn’t seal the deal if they were lucky enough to even get a woman into the sack. All the Ross Jeffries and David DeAngelos and "Mystery"s in the world couldn’t teach them the art of speed seduction, or the most simple form of the art period. Some people were just hopeless. Looking at Vetra’s case, he himself was one of the more hopeless ones. A life full of complications definitely is not a morality booster, no matter how you can look at it. Sure, you can say all the optimistic stuff you want, but high hopes don’t erase facts… and the facts are that Peter Vetra’s got two ways of winning against Xian Phillipse. 1) Xian Phillipse gets locked in a room with 5 sexy women who want to do nothing but have hot, sweaty, slutty passionate unprotected sex with him. … Okay, maybe not unprotected. XP keeps it clean, ladies and gentlemen. Wrap it before you tap it. But if that’s the case, he better be getting the rest of the deal fully equipped. Maybe fucking a bitch in the ear, blowing her brains out or something… But back on to the second reason Vetra would win. 2) Xian Phillipse dies… Thus becoming another + to the ever growing total of deaths in the wrestling industry, before and behind the cameras. "The only funeral I really plan on going to is my own… and that won’t be anytime soon." Bizzy looked over her shoulder from the passenger seat, her brow quirking up at the teen. "You’re kidding, right?" Our boy, Xian Phillipse, was lounged across the back seat of the relic they trekked across the highway on. Somewhere between XP and Bizzy finding her mom celebrating the death of their distant relative Hanson Love and XP putting the beating on Wildlife in the first round of GTT, Summer Love had decided she was going to pack up her VW Bus and truck it for the nation’s capital. Never one to pass on a road trip, Bizzy tagged along, and even managed to talk XP into going along, even though he really had no desire to go. He was more concerned with training for his next round opponent, as always. But the debate ran short with Bizzy’s opening statement. ‘You can train with DDR once you get up there.’ After that, for some strange reason, XP was packed and ready before everyone else. But back to present events… "Nope, I’ve never been to a funeral." His eyes were closed as he tried to relax to some degree, though the bumpy and rumbling of the ancient automobile made things a trifle difficult. "I mean, I don’t really have a family besides Coach, and he doesn’t have family at all… at least no one that he talks about. So we never really had to go to anything really… family oriented." Bizzy’s mom looked in the rear view at him with a pitied look on her face. "Oh you poor thing. You must have a huge lack of love in your home." "Love?" Such foreign things made XP’s head hurt. "Nah, afraid we were fresh out of that in Castle Phillipse. All we’ve got is respect and discipline." "What is Mr. Mason up to these days, anyway?" That was something only he would know. "Beats me, Mrs. Love." A scrunched up shrug accompanied the reply. "I really don’t speak much with the old man since I moved out and got that job with WWA. I mean, he didn’t have to support me anymore, and I didn’t have to put up with his drill sergeantry anymore… so we’ve reached a period of peace." "Some periods end up bloody if you leave them be, you know." If there were any other words put together in the English language that could put disgusted looks on Bizzy or Xian Phillipse… it was that. You might want to reference other phrases, like "Xian… I’m pregnant," or "Bizzy, I really do love Vana Zell." Speaking of the little pixie… The scene opened up on Casa del Zell, the lavish estate in Greenville, South Carolina. "WAAAAAAAAAUUUUUUHAAAHHHHAAAAAAAAAA –UUUUUUUUUUGHGHHHHHHH!!! EEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAUUUUGHHHHHUUUUUUUUHHHH-HUUUUUHHH-HHUUUUUH!!!!" Vanderbilt Zell looked up at the ceiling from his oak paneled den, a displeased look on his face. It wasn’t long before his wife walked in, wearing a white cotton bathrobe and matching slippers. Vanderbilt glared at her as she sat down with a glass of wine. "Why did you mention that bastard’s name?" Drunkenness was already heavy set in her eyes. "Huh? Oh… well I just asked her if she was going out with anyone tonight, and she started crying! Guess she knew who I was thinking about." The frequency of XP entering both mother and daughter’s thoughts had increased since that fateful night about a year ago when XP broke up with young Princess Zell at Thanksgiving. Now every time someone even mentioned love or boyfriend, or cream pies for some strange reason, Vana would burst out into tears. And rodeos… something about those made her bawl as well. But enough of that, back to the boy wonder. "But it’s no big deal right? I mean I’ve seen plenty of funerals in the movies. You go, you sit there and listen to people talk, people cry, throw rice, and that’s a wrap, baby." Bizzy didn’t even bother to look back at that response. She was too busy playing Elder Scrolls: Oblivion on her Alienware laptop. "Rice is weddings, dumb ass." "Yeah, I got that mixed up. I mean they’re both in churches… and I saw a wedding and a funeral in the same movie. Plus I was busy during that movie… no details." "No questions." The camaraderie between them was immaculate. Summer Love still didn’t want to let XP’s repressed childhood go. "Oh honey, If you ever need a family to be there for you, you know you can always count on us. I mean you and Bijou are already like brother and sister! I can be your mother!" There was something dirty about Bizzy being his sister… especially with all the thoughts he’s had about her, or all the times he’s gotten wood off seeing her in revealing outfits like her PJs. He was only human, despite being a machine in the ring… and the sack… but only Vana called him that… among other things. "Ahh… thanks, Mrs. L." Might as well go along with it, he thought. "I mean, Mother Love." Now Bizzy had to pause the game on that one. "… When I hear the name Mother Love… I’m not wrong for thinking of a big Black motherly woman from the South, am I?" XP shook his head. "I wouldn’t think so. I kinda thought of that too. But trust me… I’m not racist. Some of my best friends are…" "Don’t do it, Jackie boy," Bizzy had to cut him off right there, or else it’d be Sin City all over the place in that decrepit piece of vehicle. Summer reached back and put her hand on XP’s knee, patting it lightly. "Well we’ll walk you through your first funeral, dear. Don’t worry… it won’t kill you." She giggled, an airy high pitched sound that could infect anyone with a smile. Anyone… but Bizzy, that is. Night and day, those two were, and while Summer Love was full blown Good Morning, Bizzy Bontecou was Midnight, the apex of each moment beautifully. "There’s a first time for everything, Xian honey!" XP managed to catch a slice of snickering from the passenger seat. He sat up and leaned over her, putting her in a playful sleeper. "What’s so funny?" "You virgin bitch." You’re as cold as ice, Bizzy… willing to sacrifice. But Summer was right… there is a first time for everything… Now take Xian Phillipse. He’s a nice looking kid, well built, and he’s got a mouth on him that could talk a crippled man into standing up. That was the type of person he was; he just was blessed to have the gift of gab about him. Most people would say that he gets it from his parents… but he never knew his parents, so he wouldn’t know. And the person who raised him… well, that person definitely wasn’t the one who raised him to be charismatic. There were other things that he taught the young XP… But the Coach taught him how to be a winner, how to dominate in any type of fight that he’d find himself in. And that’s how XP lived his life… getting past that first fight, breaking his combat cherry, and fucking everyone up on his way to the top. And now here he was against Peter Vetra… someone who’s probably had more fights under his belt than this teenager here did. But that doesn’t mean he’s better… just means he’s had more failures, more times to fuck up and look a fool. XP’s hit it fast and strong each time, and left everyone flat on their backs. So as far as Vetra was concerned this round… he was just another pussy waiting to get fucked up by XP… and he doesn’t like it anyway but rough. Everyone was a virgin to XP in the ring… ripe for the rape. That’s just hard luck.
