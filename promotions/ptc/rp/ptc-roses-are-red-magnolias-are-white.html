Roses Are Red, Magnolias Are White, I Forgot to RP, Cuz I Been Smoking Ganja All Nite Sorry, folks. I wrote this portion this morning in the airport, then after I got home I completely spaced on the midnight deadline. Here's what I had: The heart of the Roman Empire, in a time when the men were strong, the women were good-looking, the children were above average, baseball was America’s pastime, watching drunken Yankees wander out on ledges of tall buildings in New York City was America’s second pastime, Yellow Jackets still had ephedrine in them, and it snowed occasionally… Emperor Caesar Novarian I (known also to the citizens of Rome as Nova the Bomb-Chronicus) stands at the edge of a circular marble platform. Around the platform Ionian columns support another ring of marble above them. Truth be told, the “room” (if you broaden the definition to include this structure) seems kind of useless…but it’s majestic, and to Nova the Bomb Chronicus, majestic is, like, thrice as sweet as useful. One of Caesar Novarian’s minions scurries into view from a dark corner, the beeper attached to the waist of his toga informing him that the Emperor might say something. “Lo,” Nova the Bomb-Chronicus begins, his tone, as always, heavy with the burden of responsibility, “I see-eth hordes of Mongols at the edge of Rome. They come with a thirst-eth for booty…and blood.” “Eep!” the minion offers helpfully, “what doth you want-eth to do, Emperor?!” “Bring me a gaggle of young boys to fan me and tickle my balls while I watch 106 & Park on BET,” Novarian instructs. This isn’t the beginning I was really going for. Let’s start again. The regal private quarters of a medieval castle, in a time when dudes loved slaughtering infidels and banging wenches like whut. Thaaaaaaat’s better. King Nova of Smoakalot stands in front of a stone wall, peering out of the castle through an arrow slit. “I see them, Lucius,” he says quietly, his voice cold steel, “I see the Barbarians approaching on the horizon.” “Sire, would it not better serve you to peer out of the giant window your Majesty had installed specifically for spotting Barbarians?” Lucius inquires. “Quiet now,” Nova dismisses the notion with a wave of his hand, “Smoakalot is in grave danger, Lucius. Grave danger, indeed.” “You don’t even want to use your telescope?” the Medieval Star’s assistant asks, baffled. “They come for me, Lucius,” Nova continues, still peeking out through the thin line of the arrow-hole before turning back, “or more accurately, they come for it.” Dramatically Nova dashes across the room. His foot catches on a stone and he tumbles to the floor, rolling a few feet before coming to a stop. He stands up and dusts himself off, the only injury being his pride. “Okay, in a stone floor, all the stones are supposed to be even so things like that don’t happen. That’s how it works. Kill the man responsible for this injustice.” “Sire, you cobbled this floor yourself,” Lucius replies hesitantly, “remember? The day you were bored with conquest and wanted to renovate? You replaced the old floor made up of the bodies of your ex-wife’s relatives with this stone? Sire?” “Ah, yes,” Nova says with a nod, doing his best to keep regal composure, “well, I guess that’s what I get for wanting the gratification that comes with do-it-yourself projects.” The King of Smoakalot walks back over to the arrow-hole and peers through it again before turning back to his patient assistant. “They come for me, Lucius…or more accurately, they come for it.” Nova dashes across the room a second time, careful to bound over the ajar stone. Reaching a tall case, he pulls the blanket from it to reveal a wooden belt with decorative carvings on it and “NOVA” inscribed below a carving of the globe (square, with storm clouds over Africa and “ EVIL” scrawled next to where China should be. “There it is, Lucius, in all of its ravishing splendor,” Nova says, his voice hushed with awe, “the source of all pwn. The token of my Extremity.” “It’s glorious, your Majesty,” Lucius agrees. “The Penisaurus Rex carved there on the side was a personal touch I requested to sort of customize the whole thing,” Nova explains, “ya know, really make it my own.” “I noticed that, it’s very nice,” Lucius says supportively, “very kingly.” “Take a long hard look, Lucius,” Nova instructs, putting an arm around his totally hetero companion, “this is what the bloodthirsty mongrels seek.” “If I may be so bold, sire,” Lucius inquires carefully, “why?” King Nova looks over Lucius’s head, first left, then right. When he’s sure no spies will hear, he speaks. “Lucius, this belt…this belt guarantees my entry into Heaven.” “Wow, really?” his assistant replies, visibly impressed. “Really,” Nova answers, “and I’ll prove it to you. This is great.” He walks over to the corner and grabs a bucket. “This is a bucket of boiling oil,” he explains as he carries it over to a hole in the wall, “and this shaft leads down to several openings in the ceiling of the infirmary.” “Your Majesty, there truly is no need to prove…,” Lucius begins. “Ah-ah-ah!” Nova interjects, “Seriously, I wanna drive my point home. It’ll be funny.” Before Lucius can plead with him any further, Nova dumps the oil down the shaft and pumps his fist in the air as he hears it splash several times. “AAAAAHHHH!!!” “OH GOD!!!” “WHY?!!?! AGGHH!!” “IT’S BURNING MY BURNS!! WHAT THE FUCK!!!” “Wait ‘till they discover the heat-resistant fanged leeches,” Nova says delightfully, rubbing his hands together. “OH MY GOD, THIS ISN’T HAPPENING!!” Nova looks down at his wrist as if to check his watch. “Aaaaaaannnndd…yup, still going to Heaven.” “Amazing, sire,” Lucius agrees with a nod. King Nova stretches his arms and yawns. “Man, being this much of a badass all day every day will take it out of you, Lucius.” “I wouldn’t know, sire,” Lucius replies expertly. “I suppose not,” Nova agrees, “maybe someday, Lucius.” I probably don't have to tell you, but the rest involves a flock of hood-rats to satisfy the King, a full-body condom, the obligatory flashback, and general tomfoolery. These things will happen.
