ETD Title Match (Moraless (c) vs DDT)

28 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
28 June 2004 5:23am. The red neon lights emanating from the bedside clock bore into John’s eyes causing him to roll over and pull the sheets over his head. Mentally and physically John Edwards was tired. The past few weeks had been extremely intense taking its toll on his body. Since picking up the PrimeTime Central extreme championship his life had been on a roller coaster. If it wasn’t press conferences it was invitational tournaments. His mind was close to breaking point and yet he still couldn’t sleep. He hadn’t been the same since Kyle Stone had drugged him in the process of the wrestle god tournament, the craving ate away at John wanting him to succumb to the hunger, to lose all control and go back for more. It wasn’t his way though. As soon as he found out the truth, he hated that Kyle had done that to him, he felt dirty and most importantly of all, he felt as though he had cheated. His win tainted. John closed his eyes; it was a futile attempt, but an attempt none the less to get to sleep. Of course it would just end up irritating him at his inability to do so, but in the end he didn’t care. At least this was attempting to be productive. It wasn’t even as though the conditions were wrong, it was a cool evening with a soft breeze blowing majestically through the window and there was no major background noise, nothing to disturb him. Well not quite nothing. John was always someone who would dwell on things, the past for example. Its stupid really, the past is something that cannot be changed. It does not mean anything with regards to the future. The past does not shape the destiny for every person in the planet, it is something that is simply there, it has been and gone there is no point worrying about it. John had a problem with this though, even if he was able to sleep he wouldn’t, he wouldn’t be able to the one solitary thought going through his mind was that in a few days he was going to be facing one of the few men in the past year to beat him. DDT. John through the covers off of him, frustrated and exhausted before pulling himself out of bed and stumbling across his room, dodging the discarded pizza boxes and newspapers on the floor. His legs were shaking as he walked, his face full of steel grit and determination as he slowly but surely placed one foot in front of the other walking towards his bathroom. As he extended his hands towards the sink and turned on the cold tap he gazed at himself in the mirror, staring back at the man in front of him. Large sacks hung under John’s eyes, which were glazed and bloodshot. “Some champ you are kid” spoke John to himself as he filled his hands with water and splashed it over his face, the sharp and harsh coldness causing him to open his eyes quickly with shock, the water began to drip down his chin into the sink below. “This should be a piece of cake, you’ve advanced since then. Why are you so worried about facing him again? Just a few days ago you beat Jesse Jamester, he’s a former Global Champion. You’re still the UWF tag-team champion and most of all you are the PrimeTime Central extreme champion.” John turned his head and looked into his bedroom, the two belts hung high above his dresser, on show to those who would venture in. A constant reminder of what he had accomplished. As a champion, he was expected to be one of the best. The extreme nature of the belt didn’t help either; in the past week he had battled three matches against five other competitors in live competition. That didn’t include the matches at house shows, which UWF put on around the United States. He was slowly losing the fight within him, every day he got in the ring, his bumps were becoming less extreme, and his moves were becoming sloppier. Everyone around him had noticed it; they were just too polite to say anything. In the past when John went simply as Moraless, he would fly around the ring nailing every move with pinpoint accuracy. Now though he had become a liability not only to himself, but those around him. John stepped out of the bathroom and slowly made his way through the darkness of his house to his living room, before collapsing into his arm chair, the remote control which was resting on the side fell gently into his lap as John allowed himself a little smile. He loved it when it did that. Flicking on the television, news stories from around the world began to bombard John, stories of death and destruction throughout the world. This was what people around the country were waking up to, eating their cornflakes to stories of famine before walking out to work. No wonderwork stress was high. John sighed a deep sigh and reclined in his chair, clicking a button on the remote control muting the sounds of bomb blasts, allowing himself to be alone with his thoughts, either way it was going to be a destructive amount of noise attacking him. John had known about the decline in his performance for about six months. It was just little things at first, taking longer to get up after a moonsault, or hesitating before a springboard shooting star press to the outside. Little things that he had previously taken for granted. It was about then he started to develop a more extreme style or performance, the main emphasis was not to show off and fly high anymore. Hardcore bouts were a gift to those who could bring themselves to see blood. The crowd hungered for something more in extreme matches to the true hardcore fans, wrestling just got in the way. A two by four shattered over someone’s skull was entertainment there. John flicked the sound back onto the television and curled up slightly, his eyes had finally begun to fall heavy and he was sure as hell not going to let this opportunity slip. Slowly and silently John descended into slumber. Five Hours Later. “Now I understand that you have something to say to Jolene here” “Yeah, look maybe I cheated on you but that doesn’t mean I don’t love you. Its not my fault you’re as frigid as a bitch!” John slowly came to, curled up on the armchair, his neck bend in an awkward position, dangling limply off the edge of it. His eyes turned towards the sound, which appeared to be in his room, somewhere, before coming to the realisation that he had left the television on. The simple rants of simple people on simple daytime television. He sat up his neck clicking painfully as he did so, causing a small whine of pain to come from John, yep the past few days had really started to get to him. Despite the minimal amount of sleep John felt reasonably re-energised, a small bounce in his step feeling better than he had done for a number of days. It was that moment just after you had woken up and your brain was not full y functioning yet, the world seemed to be a much more pleasant place and things seemed that little bit brighter. “Ah crap” John mumbled to himself as the world suddenly hit him like a sledgehammer, along with the realisation that the upcoming bout with DDT was even closer now. John had lost titles before; it would be nothing new to him if he did lose it. The trouble was that he had never truly cared about a belt before, not like this one. The Extreme Title to John was more than just a championship. The belt itself delivered recognition, you knew that you were a target for all of those around you. They would try and take you out give it their all just to clasp at what you owned. That sense of power and responsibility that was the true buzz. The problem was that DDT proved to be an all too real threat to John’s crown. The previous time that they had met John had lost plain and simple but the way the division works meant that John still remained the number one contender before finally capturing the title against Sean McClancy. In an event when John had given it his all, DDT had gone one rung higher and was able to shock John with a tazer and pin him for the three count. The polyphonic version of summer of sixty-nine by Bryan Adams began to slowly filter into John’s brain, once again disturbing him from his own train of thought. John followed the sound through the apartment until he arrived back in his bedroom, his mobile phone ringing on his bedside table. John raised the phone to his ear, pushing the answer button in the process, he wasn’t a fan of mobile phones, something about them just didn’t feel right half of the time. “Hi, John Edwards speaking how can I help you.” Spoke John before growing tired of holding the phone and simply placing it on speakerphone, resting it on the side of the bed in the process. “John, its Kyle here” The words crackled through the phone and immediately made John cringe. John did not trust Kyle one bit. Kyle was the sort of man that the most venomous snake in the world would be cautious of. “Look I know you’re still pissed with me and sure I understand that, I just want you to know that there is something you might want to see on the wrestling channel right now, its your previous match again DDT. I know you have probably been up all night worrying about it.” John sneered in the direction of the phone as Kyle Stone spoke. Kyle had become an acquaintance of John’s during the wrestle god tournament in Columbus, Ohio. Without a doubt his intelligence knew no bounds, neither did the levels he would stoop to, in order to gain what he wanted. John began to think about what happened but got stopped by Kyle talking once more, now was not the time or the place for that story. “John are you there, are you day dreaming again. Go and watch that damn match and learn where you went wrong” The words were sharp and harsh as the phone on the other end was quickly slammed down. John turned and began to head back towards the living room. Kyle was right, whether he wanted to see it or not, it would definitely help things along, he may even learn something. As he took a few steps forwards a ray of sunlight beamed in through the window, the PrimeTime Central Extreme Title, bathed in a golden aura, which immediately drew John towards up, pulling it gently off of the wall and onto his shoulder, before returning to the living room. By the time he had got there the match was nearly over, the wrestling channel choosing to only show the highlights as opposed to the entire bloodshed of the match Moraless charges, and starts to swing the chair… but DDT dives forwards, something in his hand, and it hits Moraless… … …ZZZZZAAAAPPPP!!!!! BJ: … Mikey: The tazer! The tazer he has used time and time again! The hand held tazer hits the stomach of Moraless, and a squeezing of the trigger sends incredible voltage through the body of the UWF star. DDT holds it in place until the eyes of his opponent roll back in their head, and he collapses to the mat, still shaking slightly, and definitely smouldering. DDT drops down, nothing left in him but the energy to cover his fallen foe. ONE! … … TWO! … … … THREE!!! DING! DING! DING! Mikey: He did it! DDT won the match! John sighed as he clasped his title tightly against him. Watching him fall so ungraciously as the voltage flowed through him was a horrible sight. However he had not been watching himself, his eyes were firmly fixed on DDT. (Men are nearly always willing to believe what they wish.) n Julius Caesar, 102? - 44 BCE 29th June 04 Throughout life, there are many events, which will occur that you have no control over. They will happen around you everyday of your life, most of the time you won’t even notice that they are happening. Take the tide for instance. There is no-way for it to be controlled; believing so would mean that you are crazy. Look what I’m trying to get at is that sometimes things are going to happen that no one will be able to stop. Not you, not anybody. I truly hope that when it comes you deal with it better than I did. John Edwards. There are many events that you have to go through as a wrestler, countless promotional appearances in which the crowds heaved around you, just staring slack jawed at you hoping to be able to get to shake your hand or the ultimate goal of having a few seconds to speak with you, before running home to tell their friends. It was ultimately the price of fame. The success and the money. When you were a champion the appearances became longer and ultimately more stressful. At first the crowd interaction was somewhat pleasing, hell it was fun. After months of telling the same old stories and hearing the same old questions though it began to grow dull. A longing for a normal life had begun to attack John. The chance to blend in with a crowd, something he had not been able to do in close to four years. It was beginning to take his toll on him. At the moment the only thing that was getting him through was the gold that hung above his dresser back home. The PrimeTime Central Extreme Championship. When John looked at the belt, he felt important, no it was more than that, he felt proud. When he looked at the names of the former champions, he knew that he paled in comparison, but that was just his personality. John Edwards wasn’t someone like Tristan Belmont of Fathom, the type of wrestler who could draw a crowd just by waving their little finger. Belmont, Fathom and McClancy all had that special x factor that made them true champions. What did John have? Technically John wasn’t even the champion, Moraless was, the person who everyday of his life he tried to hide from, the person who had tried to kill himself just over a year and a half ago. Walking silently down the street, John Edwards hung his head low, his black leather jacket was taking a battering as the rain came coursing down from the sky. There was something that John liked about the rain; it was soothing more than anything else. The streets were empty today, it was as though everyone had packed their bags and moved away. This in itself made John feel slightly uneasy, he had once heard from someone very wise that the weather determines what was going to happen, and as he was paying a visit to the doctor for a basic medical. These were the sorts of omens that he could have done without. John was never a fan of doctors, or the medical profession in general. He knew that without them he would have been dead, but that was in the past. Doctors now just reminded him of his own mortality. He knew that he was going to die eventually; it’s the one guarantee in life. Although this was just a simple medical to clear him for his title defence against DDT, the nerves and apprehension were still there. It was just the way he was. John turned right and entered a wooden door and entered the doctor’s surgery, closing the door quietly behind him, the sound of rain still splattering on the pavement outside. He glanced around the unfamiliar surroundings of the clinic before heading towards the reception desk. “I have an appointment with Dr Crawford” John spoke in a quiet dulcet tone as the receptionist flicked through the large books in front of her, using her finger to trace down the large collection of names. ”Ah yes, Mr Edwards. Dr Crawford will be with you shortly,” Replied the receptionist who glanced over the top of her glasses and smiled at John, indicating for him to take a seat. John did so and simply stared forwards, he didn’t even bother looking at the pile of tattered magazines, which would no doubt tell stories of miracle births and infidelity, choosing rather to simply close his eyes and lean against the wall. ‘Just a simple medical’ John thought to himself ‘you can go in there get cleared to wrestle, then go and try to defend that damn belt of yours’ John’s arms crossed over his stomach as he sighed and reclined slightly. Deep down he knew that something bad was going to happen, he had the strange feeling in the pit of his stomach, just like every time England played football, he just knew something bad was going to happen. “Dr Crawford is ready for you now Mr Edwards” The receptionist spoke, causing John to shy slightly and open his eyes, a weary look on his face as he pushed himself up and made his way through to the doctor’s room. As he passed the receptionist she smiled sweetly at him as though to say not to worry. Was he really giving off that sort of negativity already? As he entered the room, Dr Crawford rose to his feet and shook John’s hand. John didn’t look the doctor in the eye, choosing to avert his glance slightly before sitting down on the bed as the doctor sat next to him at his desk. “So I understand that you are here, for a simple check-up and looking for clearance to wrestle, is that correct?” Asked the doctor, not looking up from his notepad. “Yeah, that about sums it up” John replied rolling his eyes as he did so. He hated questions that people already knew the answer to. It was just them being pedantic; he hated being treated like a child. “Ok John, if you can just remove your shirt and I’ll listen to that heart of yours” Spoke the doctor as he span around from his desk and removed the stethoscope from around his neck. John hated this part; the look was the same from every single person who saw it. The look of shock and fear. Removing his plain white t-shirt John sat bare chested in the doctors room. His chest covered in cuts and scars, the largest being a large blade wound entering which had entered his stomach. His arms were marked too, the words REAL and SOON engraved on his forearms, a message to past foe, someone who had not come back to challenge him. The doctors eyes scanned John’s body unable to comprehend the amount of abuse the body had clearly taken. These weren’t war wounds, self inflicted most of them. Cases of self-harm were sadly not that rare, but never to this extremity, not without a body bag being involved. “When did these occur?” asked the doctor as he placed the cold stethoscope onto John’s chest causing him to jump slightly. John rolled his eyes, once more and breathed in heavily as instructed by the doctor. He didn’t want to answer these questions. “Lets just say it was when I was a different person ok” spoke John as he allowed the doctor to continue with his examination. In all truth he was right. Moraless and John Edwards were completely different people, different attitudes to life and different ways of dealing with whatever the world through at them. For four years Moraless had been a sanctuary for John, a sanctuary which slowly turned into a prison, which spiralled out of control into self hate and harm. The fact of the matter was Moraless was the X-factor that John needed. The problem was he was scared of what would happen if he released him. Just before he won the title, John had found himself, he was happy with who he was now but as the time drew closer with his match with DDT, he knew that he would need that extra kick if he was to break the dreaded champions curse. “John” spoke the doctor, his voice sounding extremely serious. This in itself through John’s concentration way off. The ill feeling in the pit of his stomach grew, anticipation flooded over him as he waited for the doctor to continue speaking. “Have you been feeling ok lately? Any sharp pain? Tingling sensations in your arm at all?” Asked the doctor, the look of concern on his face being more than enough to worry John. John thought back, all in all he actually was feeling fine. He was more lethargic than usual but that was just him, sure his work in the ring had diminished but he was just going through a rough patch, things would pick up soon. ”I’ve been fine, well I’ve felt fine anyway. Why what’s wrong” John questioned. “Well I’m not sure yet but I believe you maybe suffering form Atrial Fibrillation. Also known as an irregular heart beat. If you have been suffering from anxiety attacks lately or a feeling a weakness it’s due to your hearts diminished ability to pump adequate amounts of blood around your body.” The doctor spoke, trying to make the words as understandable as possible. John stood bemused; he could not comprehend that through all the things he had suffered in his life a dodgy heart would be the thing that would have the doctors worried the most. John shrugged it off and rose to his feet. ”Wow, not what you learn everyday is it. So anyway I’ll be good to go against DDT right.” Spoke John as he pulled the t-shirt he previously wore back over his head and rising to his feet. The doctor looked at John, his face displaying shock at the idea John still wanted to wrestle. “John I don’t think you understand, this is quite a serious condition. I can’t clear you to wrestle; I just don’t feel its safe. Also I’d recommend you to stop wrestling all together, at least until I can put you on some beta-blockers whilst you wait for surgery” The doctor spoke, John however was not listening, he didn’t want to believe what the doctor was saying. Men are always willing to believe what they want to believe after all. “Look doc. I’m going to wrestle DDT no matter what you say, I can’t pretend to be someone I’m not. I worked for years with this condition; I know enough to know that this doesn’t just occur over night. Its not some sexually transmitted disease you catch off a cheap hooker.” John shouted. There were tears in his eyes, he knew that the doctor was right deep down, but pride was too big a thing to lose. “But John you aren’t listening to me. If you carry on like this it’s only a matter of time before something happens. You aren’t God John, you can’t fight this without my help” The doctor retorted his face was red as John simply turned away slipping his leather jacket onto his shoulders heading towards the door. “John! Are you listening to me? If you continue to wrestle you will die!” cried Doctor Crawford in one last plea to get John Edwards to stop, which he did. John hung his head for a moment staring at his feet. All of the work he had made finding himself, all the time he had spent locking the man inside away so that he wouldn’t run his life. ”Don’t call me John, I’ve always hated that name. Call me Moraless” spoke Moraless as he stepped through the doorway and into the rain, it felt lighter somehow.
