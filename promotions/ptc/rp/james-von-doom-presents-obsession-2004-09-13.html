James von Doom presents Obsession

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-13 9/12/2004, 9:14 PM Las Vegas, Nevada [The scene opens in a little place called Dystopia, also known as downtown Las Vegas, Nevada. People come to this city every day to obsess about money and strippers. But now, there is but one madman who rules this city with an iron fist. The camera angles around him as he turns slightly and smiles at the camera.] “Hello. I bet you don’t know who I am.” [Who are you anyway?] “I’m in the Pyrenees Bracket of your prestigious Global Title Tournament 4.” [Yeah? So?] “And my name is James von Doom.” [So that’s what it is. *pause* Who are you exactly?] “For those of you who don’t know my background and have been jazzing over Seymour Almasy all week, let me tell you a little about myself.” [That might help.] “I’ve been competing for 2 years. I haven’t won any title of any major significance, which makes me a major underdog in this thing. But at least you know I never go half ass. I’m here to win it all.” [Oh yeah? And how are you going to do that with guys like Deville, Almasy, and Hanson to compete against?] “Well, it’s simple really how I’m going to win this thing. I’m going to give my opponents a false sense of security, much like I’m giving you now Josh.” [Josh? From GW?] “Yes. The same Josh who some of you think will go far in this tournament, but not when he’s facing ME.” [Why are you pointing to yourself James?] “And why is that Joshua?” [Yeah. Why is that James?] “Well, let me explain it to you. You may be a former Cruiserweight and World Champion, but that doesn’t impress me one bit. I don’t care what you did five minutes ago. I only care about the here and the now.” [Again, why is that James?] “Because all I care about is winning the GTT4 tournament and going down in history as pulling off one of the biggest upsets in our sport’s long history.” [Yeah…like that’ll ever happen.] “Josh, I’ve been obsessing about winning this tournament for years. There’s egomaniacs like Almasy out there who think they own the world. Hell, he’s a jackass and you’re nothing but just another pretty boy punk to cater to the female fans.” [What?] “Yeah Josh. Or, should I say, Kid Flash.” [Kid Flash? Huh? What the hell kind of gay name is that?] “We have a lot in common Josh. Both of us are young superstars on the rise, but the difference between us is that I never had a stupid ass stage name like Kid Flash. I mean, what were you thinking? Kid Flash sounds like some jobber who wrestles at the local high school on weekends against guys with names like Harold the Garbage Man, Jaguar Boy, and the Hippopotamus.” [What the hell is he talking about now?] “Boy, you sure picked the wrong guy to draw in the first round.” [And why is that James?] “I have been obsessing over winning this for YEARS. It’s been my obsession ever since I was in high school.” [Obsession? What obsession?] “I CRAVE the gold. I NEED the gold. And I’ve been OBSESSING over the gold ever since I first got some in a small indy fed many moons ago.” [Yes. That was a long time ago, James.] “I’ve been on the cusp of greatness my whole career and I may actually become one of the greats by winning this tournament and doing all of my brothers and sisters in the DWS proud.” [Good for you, James.] “But, this is only round 1. I mustn’t get ahead of myself. I need to prepare for the later rounds if I am even going to be at the end of this thing.” [Yes James. You could steal the thing.] “Now Joshua, don’t get me wrong. I’m not over looking you. I’m just…looking to the future. A future where I am the winner of the tournament and NOT Seymour Almasy, some egomaniacal idiot who’s too wrapped up in his video gaming to look beyond the fact that his head’s inflated so much, it’s suffocating everyone in the tournament.” [Oooooo…low blow.] “And Seymour, you forget about your stint in the NWC. You didn’t do all that well, did you Seymour? Hell! I suck and I’ve got a better record that you do there!” [Oooooo…another low blow! OUCH!] “Ok Seymour. You’re not all that. I’ve been obsessed with being the best ever since I first started and now that you “ARE” the best, you’ve let it get to your head.” [Damn right James. Stick it to the man.] “And that makes you vulnerable.” [Why is that?] “Because you think you are invincible, that will lead to your demise. It might be against me and it might be against somebody else, but as long as your name is on the marquee, you’re a target.” [Bullseye.] “You’re not the hunter anymore. You’re the hunted.” [Exactly. Now go get a rifle and hunt his ass down.] “But Josh, you’re the hunted too. You see, I’m the super-predator of this tournament. You give me an opening and I will eat you alive.” [Yuck! That’s not a pretty picture.] “Our match is going to be like one of those wildlife documentaries where the lions eat the zebras and they show all the blood and guts and everything.” [That’s nasty.] “You see, Josh. I’m the lion and you’re the lowly, pathetic zebra being eaten.” [Nice analogy.] “Joshua, I’m going to eat you alive.” [Time for some evil laughter now.] “BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!” [And now that that’s over with…] “Josh, I must now take my leave of you. But let me tell you this. NOBODY has been obsessing over this tournament more than me. It’s time for me to step up to the plate and become one of the big boys. And it’s time for you to get your ass kicked.” [Smile evilly now and leave with a parting shot.] “Bye Josh. The next time I see you, you’ll be looking up at the lights in a pool of your own blood before being taken to the hospital. Good night.” [James walks off camera and the scene fades to black.] [Cut.]
