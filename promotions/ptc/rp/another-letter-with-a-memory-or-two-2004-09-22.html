Another letter with a memory or two

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 Dear Sarah, What a roller coaster ride I have experienced. One day I’m a has been, former hardcore hero and the next day the world knows me as one of the most exciting, hybrid American heavyweight wrestlers of the moment. Press, fans and even Rose have all wanted me. Finally what my heart has desired for so long my heart will receive. Rose was before you, Rose was my one chance at near perfection of love but it wasn’t to be, Rose became wayward falling in with the wrong crowd and soon broke contact with me when I became a crippled man. It was a double blow to me, felling it in both my heart and soul. (Pausing from his writing Jamie places his pen down. Hi eyes close gradually so he can search deep within himself to remember the feelings of their encounters.) I’m bouncing of the walls again… I’m looking like a fool again… A much younger Jamie Fletcher can be seen walking through a dark room. The loud Sugarcult music can paint the picture itself; party, teenagers, drink, drugs and sex does the scene party or blatantly you could say… PARTY! Avoiding the drugs and drink being the straight edge spunkmonkey he is Jamie approaches a girl. She’s small, cute and has curly red hair. When she looks up you can see the twinkle of hope in her eyes a twinkle, which will one day make her famous. The two embrace one another with a gentle hug giving off an impression of friendship between the two. ‘Happy Birthday, Alice?’ Remarks Jamie in a very soft voice. ‘Thank you!’ The girl’s voice is squeaky like a mouth but clear and precise giving you a clear impression of her perfect English skills both vocal and written. ‘Sorry I’m late…’ Jamie continued to ramble but that really isn’t important… Fast forwarding his own memory Jamie reaches the important part. Alice leads Jamie into the garden where we can see light, the summer months in England lighting up evenings until 10pm allowed for the sight of two of his best friends and some girl who was simply stunning. One problem, she was drunk, second problem she’d only bloody well had one bottle of Barcardy Breezers. She was sat on her knees with her bottle of Barcardy she turns gently in her mouth subconsciously flirting with Alan. Pulling the bottle out of her mouth Rose made a little laugh, which kind of said not funny but damn your hot so I’m going to laugh anyway which Alan couldn’t even pick up on. ‘Alice!’ She screamed in delight jumping up off he knees. Hugging each other tightly Alice seems a little shocked at Rose’s pace both with her feet and with her alcohol. Handing her bottle over to Alice a toxicated Rose turns her beady little eyes to Jamie. Jamie’s eyes her full body up for the first time. Every inch of it heavenly, her long raven hair falls down to her shoulders its blacker then the night while her strong brown eyes are deeper than the craters that grace the moon and her well toned body displays curves even the best driver would find it hard to turn round. Neither spoke, just for two seconds they stand bewildered looking at one another. Finally Jamie smiles at Rose who compliments him back with a Smile. ‘Aren’t you going to introduce me?’ barks Rose. ‘Oh! Yes sorry Jamie this is Rose, Rose this…’ Interrupting ‘Jamie!’ Alan charges Jamie down to the ground with what in Jamie’s profession would be labelled a spear. Laughing Jamie pushes Alan over onto his back, he winks at Rose and Alice before applying a sleeper hold to Alan on the floor. ‘So how are you doing, old buddy?’ Exclaims Jamie. (Again Jamie’s memory jogs forward like a cd player without anti shock… ‘Sorry I got to leave so early Alice.’ Remarks Jamie smiling at Alice. Just before he could leave the room Alice’s boyfriend Tom stopped him. ‘Errr… I’m going to Jamie.’ He shouted from the next room. Jamie looked into the room where Tom was climbing over a little mountain of bodies with teenagers pilled high as if they where dead. Again before they could leave the drowning noise of after the night before moans could be heard from the top of the stairs. Rose walked down them she drifted closer to Jamie. Alice and Tom eloped in a goodbye kiss while Rose walked down the stairs. ‘Morning.’ Sighed Alice directing her disappointment at Rose after her lightweight appearance the night before (no not wrestling… drink!) ‘Errr’ Rose couldn’t even speak only groan. For a second time Alice and Tom kiss while Jamie pulled the latch down on the door and opened it allowing the sun to beam into the house. As the beam rises to Roses face she shudders for a second and move away behind Alice but to the left so Jamie could still her whole face. Looking at her Jamie get’s lost again for the second time, this time getting lost was express pass. There eyes glancing only for a split second. [iAgain Jamie’s mind goes dark and re open’s… ‘Holy shit’ Are the chants, thousands of people’s voices can be heard going crazy. ‘Ohmigod Deadly J has picked up that steel chair!’ exclaims Steve Logan ‘What in the world.’ Responds Matt Russo with huge empathies of wonder. In the ring Jamie Fletcher is standing tall over an unknown wrestler holding a black steel chair with barbwire hung loosely around it. This allowing for extra pain, almost with a Philly like atmosphere the crowd call out abusive chants, over step the common boundary of worker and fan, they break kayfabe and most of all break new guys like twigs. This guy on the mat was a new guy and Jamie was about to do just what the crowd wanted, break him like a twig. Under his current gimmick Deadly J that was his job. ‘Shell I’ screams Deadly J to the blood hungry mongrelised English men. They go nuts and the chair swings down onto the rookie head causing blood to gush out of his head like a water fountain. He stands in the middle of the ring and winks to a member of the crowd, the camera shows that member of the crowd to be Rose who is with Alice. Alice always goes to Jamie’s events to support her friend and tonight Rose had agreed to trot along too. Her eyes looked focused on Jamie but she didn’t enjoy it, she enjoyed it because he did, maybe Jamie was barbaric, maybe he is barbaric. Throwing the chair down Jamie left the ring and makes his way out of the arena. Before the EMT’s could remove the young wrestler out of the ring Jamie reappeared on the ramp petrol can in hand. This sent the arena into a frenzy, shockwaves of mark out moments circle the arena while Mat Mannions rubs his pocket in the back feeling the money pouring out of the bloodthirsty dog’s pockets. He doses the injured body of the rookie with the fuel and then the steel chair. He reaches deep into his tights retrieving a lighter. Holding it up the crowd worship him like a priest with a bible. In slow motion Jamie lights the chair, his head tilts to the right to see no Alice or Rose. Throwing the chair down to the mat Jamie exits the ring to boo’s. ‘Rose’ he shouts at the top of his voice but she had long gone. Furiously Jamie storms to the locker room. Trenching up and down in his room he contemplates on how sick he is was about to be. Reaching into his suitcase Jamie retrieves a small nokia mobile phone making another attempt to get hold of Alice or Rose. He dials the number and pulls the phone close to his ear. The phone rings but there is no answer just a lady telling you the phone is engaged before Jamie can make a second attempt Mannion flew into the room his face red. Pissed off Mannion steals the phone straight out of Jamie’s hand. Woosh! Crash! Jamie’s phone find’s itself flying into the stone cold brick wall. ‘What the?’ ‘Shut up!’ Say’s Mat Mannion cutting Jamie off. Mat riffles a sheet of paper under Jamie’s nose. ‘Want a job?’ He pauses waiting for an answer. No answer… ‘Do you fucking want a job or not?’ Getting anger Mat begins to turn a horrible dark red. ‘No.’ simply replies Jamie as anybody who had any common sense would. ‘Hi.’ Both Jamie and Mat turned to the door to see who had the courage to interrupt them. The soft voice had come from the younger of the Fletcher brothers, the late, annoying, small, cruiserweight who never got paid because he ended up getting fined every week for one reason or another. ‘And you!!!’ Screamed Mannion. ‘Woah Mat… Just take it out the wages.’ The hippy child returned. ‘Take out of the wages, eh?’ Said Mat in a calm creepy way. ‘Yeh.’ David took of his t-shirt and pulled his wrestling gear out of his bag. ‘I’m afraid I can’t do that boys!’ ‘Why?’ Both the Fletcher brothers asked at identical times. ‘YOUR BOTH FIRED!!!!’ Screamed Mannion at them before leaving the room and slamming the door behind him. After a slight chuckle David regained his voice ‘What did you do to piss him off?’ ‘I didn’t pull a fire chair spot off!’ replied Jamie in disbelief at Mat Mannion’s overreactions. Sitting down Jamie takes a huge sigh. All he can do is think of Rose. All he can do is smell Rose. All he can do is see Rose when he shuts his eyes, when he goes to sleep even when he’s on the toilet he knew it he was obsessed and being obsessed isn’t a good pass time. (Jamie continues to write in his diary after finally falling out of his own little dream world) It’s a long story, I haven’t got the time to write about it now really Sarah it would take me a lifetime to explain. Well I’ve found out my second round opposition is a guy called Justin Evers. He seems like a nice guy I hope we can work well together. He works for one of those old school dusty promotions based in the dirty states of southern America. I’ve been reading into him, hell I even sent some tapes of my matches to Studio B to save him the time and effort. You know as well as I do once you seen one Jamie Fletcher match you seen them all. I’m going to take the trip to Wrestling MidWest’s next card to scout the guy maybe even get myself a dark match. Surprisingly it’s a Pay Per View they call ‘Darwin’s Revenge 2’ I know sounds like the name of a cheesy horror film. What makes it even more ironic and pushed further into cheesy movie realm is the guy is up against a dude named Shadow. Oh! Wait I lie she’s a dudet. Space Wars!!! Can Justin Ever’s beat the evil Shadow and take back what is rightfully his the ‘Revenge of Darwin trophy and save the empire from all round uncoolness or will Shadow destroy Justin Evers… Find out in Darwin’s Revenge 2… coming to a toilet near you soon. It’s settled; I’m going to WMW this weekend with a ‘STAR WARS’ Sign!!! While I’m on the subject of matches last week I also had the bout in the SCW back in England. Mat and me won and finally made love; lie, hugged in the middle of the ring putting the famous incident behind us. Hell they even dropped their lawsuit against me in return that next month I take on Pyschoduck for the SCW Title. I have good news from your legal team your getting out of there in three months they think they can win the appeal. Their going to prove you innocent Sarah just like you are, right? You never have actually told me if you did or didn’t do it. Surely a three hundred pound man wouldn’t punch a one hundred pound seventeen year old teenager and throw him into a brick wall, would you? Not much else to say really I’m off I have to book flights to Cleveland if they even have an airport there! Yours sincerely, your friend, your enemy… ‘The Sensation of Innovation’ the man who makes… eh chucks I’m full of myself Jamie Fletcher Jamie turns around looking at his bed his bag packed, his tickets already bought sitting against his wallet he grabs them. Switching attention to the clock he notices the time and quickly hoists the bag over his shoulder and runs out of the door… forgetting his coat!!! It’s a cold day.
