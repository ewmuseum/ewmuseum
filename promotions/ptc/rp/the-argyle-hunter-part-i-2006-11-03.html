The Argyle Hunter, Part I

3 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-03 For Bonita Suarez, being a single mom wasn’t easy. Dreams of going to college faded away, friends moved on, strangers passed judgment on her. The grief she got from relatives still haunted her. And then there was the deadbeat that knocked her up. Asshole lasted less than two minutes and took off when she told him she was pregnant. Bonita got by living in a studio apartment with her son Alex. She loved her son more than anything, but he could be a handful, especially when at the laundromat. He just loved running up and down between the machines. But this time he stood perfectly still next to her. Maybe he was growing up some. Or maybe the naked man in the bird mask had his attention. "Hola, cherry cola! I’m Pajaro de Presa, the Rudo with Attitude-o! And you are one sexy MILF! That’s a Mamacita I’d Love t-" "Hey!" snapped Bonita. "I got my kid here! So why don’t shut your dirty beak and gets some pants on!" "I can’t. All I got is my wrestling tights and I’m washing those right now. So why don’t we do a little permanent pressing ourselves? Once you’ve had Chicano, the rest is just guano!" Pajaro gave her a big dumb smile, but Bonita just rolled her eyes. "I’ve had Chicano. And he turned out to be guano." Pajaro tried to protest, but he got interrupted by a small middle-aged Asian man He stormed up to the Bird of Prey and shook an angry fist at him. "Hey, you freak! What have I told you?" "Uh… not to give away your ancient Chinese secrets?" "My family is Korean, retard! And I was born in Sacramento! I told you to stop walking around here with no pants on! It’s not like you got anything to be proud of, tweety-tweeter!" Bonita snickered as Pajaro sheepishly covered his naughty bits with his hands. "It’s chilly in here, mamacita…" "Whatever you say, big boy." The laundromat owner smacked Pajaro upside his head. "Hey, retard! And what up your duck?" "What about him?" "Well, look!" The angry little man pointed to a washing machine. It was filled with water and running in spite of having the lid open. And inside a small white duck swam merrily. "Quack!" "Oh, that’s Plucky!" answered Pajaro. "He’s training for his big match in GTT6. Right now he’s doing cardio. Feel the burn, Plucky!" "Quack, quack!" The owner doesn’t seem very impressed. "He’s getting feathers in my machine! Get him out of there, get your pants on, and get out!" The little man stomped off. Pajaro looked over at Bonita, who made a shoo-ing gesture at him. Dejected, the nekkid luchadore wandered away. Little Alex tugged at his mother. "Mama, that man was funny!" "It’s not polite to laugh at the retarded, sweetie." Pajaro opened up a dryer. He removed his wrestling tights and put them on, much to the happiness of everyone else there. He then took out an argyle sock. He felt around the dryer for the mate o it, but came up empty. The befuddle man in the bird mask called out to his avian amigo. "Plucky! Come here!" Plucky, dressed in a terrycloth robe, waddled up to the dryer. "Quack?" "I can’t find my other sock! It was in here when I turned on the dryer, but now it vanished! What could’ve happened to it?" "Quack, quack!" "Australia? Why would it be in Australia?" "Quack!" "Argyles are from Australia? Are you sure?" "Quack! Quack quack quack!" "Well, if you saw it on the PTC website then it must be true! No one ever lies on the Internet! Well, looks like we’re off to the Land Down Under to find my other sock!" "Quack!" And with their minds made up, bird and birdbrain left the laundromat and headed off to find their way to Australia. As they walked down the street, Pajaro stopped in front of a bar and stared through a window. Plucky shook his feathered head. "Quack." "C’mon! Just one drink before our epic journey! What’s the worst that could happen?" Several hours later, Pajaro de Presa was standing in a police station in front of a cell. Behind the bars, Plucky sat with a tall man with brown spiky hair. Man and duck glared at each other with barely contained rage. Meanwhile, a rather bored and portly policeman interviewed the Rudo with Attitude-o. "All right, Mr. de Presa. Teel me exactly what happened." "Well, officer, it happened like this. Plucky and I decided to have a quick drink before our trip." "To… Australia." "Yep." "To find… your sock." "You betcha." "I think I’m going to regret saying this, but please continue." "So Plucky and I belly up to the bar. I get a Corona. Plucky did some shots of tequila." "The duck drank tequila?" "He likes to eat the worm." "Oh. That makes sense." "So we were having a good time when that big gringo came in.!" "Mr… Saij?" "That’s him! And he was already drunk when he got there! He orders a white wine spritzer and pounds it back! Then he orders another and drinks it like it was water and he’d just come in from the desert! Saij is knocking back spritzer after spritzer! He is totally smashed! He looked over and saw me and Plucky. That’s when he became belligerent." "How so?" "He started telling jokes. Racist jokes." "About Mexicans?" "About ducks! Like that one from "The Breakfast Club" that Judd Nelson told? About the naked that walked into a bar with a duck under one arm and a two-pound salami under the other?" "I thought it was a poodle." "That’s what I said! He yelled at me to go give Judd Nelson a hummer! Then he told another one. A duck walks into a bar and asks, "Got any crackers?" Bartender says no. Duck walks out. Duck walks in the next day and asks, "Got any crackers?" Bartender says no. Duck walks out. Duck walks in the next day and asks got any crackers? Bartender says, "I told you yesterday and the day before that no! And if you ask that one more time I'll nail your beak shut!" Duck walks out. Duck comes back the next day and asks, "Got any nails?" Bartender says no. Duck says, "Good. Got any crackers?"" "That’s a good one!" "That’s awful! He made a joke about nailing a beak shut! I had to hold Plucky back from attacking him! I went up to Saij and confronted him. That’s when he reached behind the bar and grabbed a shotgun!" "A shotgun?" "Yes! He got this evil look on his face and said it was duck season! So I pushed the barrel towards Saij and said it wasn’t!" "What is it then?" "It’s rabbit season!" "Oh, Jesus…" "And Saij pointed it at me and said ‘Duck season1’" So I pointed the barrel back at him and said ‘Rabbit season!’ ‘Duck season!’ ‘Rabbit season!’ ‘Duck season!’ ‘Rabbit season!’ ‘Duck season!’ ‘Rabbit season!’ ‘Duck season!’ ‘Rabbit season!’ ‘Rabbit season!’ ‘Duck season! FIRE!’ KA-POW!" "What?" asked the cop. "Saij shot me!" Pajaro exclaimed. "Right in the head!" "But… you’re alive." "Well… yeah. It just shot gunpowder all over my face. But it scared me plenty good!" "And that’s when the duck attacked." "Plucky was just trying to defend me. Next thing you know, fists and feathers are flying! Chairs get thrown! Tables get thrown! Toilets get thrown!" "Toilets?" "Just the ones from the men’s room. It was a gentlemanly scuffle." "That’s nice." "Then before things got out of hand…" "You mean they weren’t?" "… you boys in blue showed up." The policeman closed his eyes and slowly massaged his temples. Then he walked over to the cell and slid the door opened. "Out." The man and the duck inside looked confused, but they warily walked out of the cell. The policeman gave the odd trio the hairy eyeball. "All of you get out of my sight or else I bust out the rubber hoses." Not needing to be told twice, the three exited the police station. Once they were outside, Plucky and Saij stared daggers at each other. Pajaro de Presa nervously cleared his throat. "C’mon, Plucky We got a plane to catch!" After several tension-filled moments, Plucky turned and walked away with Pajaro. Saij merely smirked to himself before he went on his way. After a great deal of walking and waddling, Plucky and Pajaro reached the airport. As they stood outside the terminal, Pajaro realized something. "Hey! How are we gonna afford tickets? Neither of us has any money!" "Quack." "Really? You got a plan?" "Quack, quack." Some time later, a silver jet plane was winging its way towards Australia. Inside were people of all backgrounds and colors. Except that none of them were either a small duck or his pet luchador. But if someone went into the cargo hold, they would’ve seen a large suitcase. This normal looking piece if luggage began rocking back and forth until it falls over. The sound of a zipper could be heard, then the lid flew open. Plucky poked his head up, a woman’s bra around his neck. "Quack! Quack!" Pajaro de Presa sits up, a pair of panties over his head. "Gosh, Plucky! This is the only way to travel… buried in ladies’ underwear! You’re a genius!" "Quack." "But you know what? I’m kinda hungry." "Quack, quack." "You’ll get us some grub? Sweet, amigo! I’ll stay here with the unmentionables!" So Plucky got out of the suitcase and waddled over to the ladder leading to the passenger section of the plane. As stealthily as he could, Plucky flapped his way up the ladder. He found himself right in front of his goal… the food and beverage cart. But before he can pat himself on the back, Plucky heard voices. Quickly he hid himself on the cart. One of the passengers in first class could feel the eyes of everyone on him. He was used to it. There was the fact that he was an imposing but handsome black man with a shaved head and sparse beard. But more likely it was because he was a celebrity.. famed for playing some of the baddest dudes ever of the screen. Some of his peers complained about the travel, the lack of privacy. But not him, he was a pro. "Sir, your meal." The flight attendant smiled at him while holding a covered plate. He lowered his tray and she set the dish on it. She lifted the lid and revealed the duck on the plate. Not a cooked one, but one very much alive. "Quack." Samuel L Jackson looked at the duck, then at the fight attendant. At the duck, then at the flight attendant. Then he looked at the duck once more before staring at he flight attendant, his eyes dark and grim. "I’M SICK OF THESE MOTHERFUCKING DUCKS ON THIS MOTHERFUCKING PLANE!!!"
