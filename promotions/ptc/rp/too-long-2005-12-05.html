Too Long

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 The water spurted out of the showerhead erratically, soaking the newly bald Shaun Styles. He allowed it to drench him and slowly creep down his face, falling off the chin. The crystal clear water allowed him an escape from the harsh conditions that have become infused into his life. This was his exile from the "destroy Shaun Styles coalition"…but all he could do was think of an unhappier time. Images of his boxing career flashing through his mind, racing in a blurry of broken dreams. His mob connections, the same ones he naively grasped, led to his downfall. It led him to be in this undisclosed, unnamed location. He began to visualize the angry crowd throwing away their bets at him while he lay staring up at the empty warehouse ceiling. Out of the corner of his eye, he was able to catch a glimpse of Don Laproni simply staring with a cold – yet neutral – look on his face. Shaun Styles’ hands began to shake at this thought as he leaned up against the tiled wall and placed his face under the soothing water. The sound of a door opening is heard in the background Shaun Styles immediately opens his eyes, showing their ice blue appeal. His head turns to the bathroom door as a look of worry creaks up on him. He turns the water off and jumps out of the shower cubicle. Grabbing a pair of loose, black jeans, Shaun puts them on while wasting no time. He slowly, attempting to be cautious, approaches the bathroom door and puts his ear to it. Automatically, he backs off and opens up his medicine cabinet above the sink. Styles spots his hidden desert eagle and grabs the silver handle. All of the sudden his mind races off into a memory bank once again. He sees himself barge into Don Laproni’s office and hold him up with the very same gun he’s gripping now. Shaun Styles is quickly brought back to reality as the sound of a floor creaking is heard outside of the bathroom. He walks over to the door and crouches down to look through a small hole in the old, wooden structure. The room looked exactly how he left it. That was odd… Shaun Styles stood his ground for as long as he could before turning the knob and slowly creaking the door open. As more and more of the room is visible, Styles felt relieved since there was no unwanted intruder. However, he maintained a firm grip on the gun and held it up while walking out of the doorway. The small, old-fashioned television was still on…That was where the mysterious sounds were coming from. He walked over to it and pressed the power button while putting his gun down on a clear coffee table. His hand ran over his stubbly head as he tried to calm his nerves. He rubbed his eyes until his vision was clear again. Turning around, he immediately spotted the plague of his dreams for the past two year. Don Laproni stood next to the entrance to the bathroom, leaning against the paint-pealing wall. Instinctively, Shaun Styles rushed to pick up his silver desert eagle but a bullet came whizzing towards the coffee table, shattering the coffee table and dropping the gun to the floor. Styles looked up and saw the figure of Young standing at the entrance to his one-room apartment, holding up a smoking gun. It quickly became pointed at the former boxer by the mammoth hit man. Don Laproni: I like your haircut, Styles…You know, you almost look like Young over here. Don Laproni bounced off the wall and walked up to Shaun Styles with a grin on his face that was made to intimidate. He patted Styles head while looking at Young and letting out a small chuckle. Don Laproni: What? You brothers or somethin’? Young kept a still face while keeping the gun pointed at Shaun Styles, apparently his long lost twin. They did look alike now that Styles has shaved off his dirty blonde locks and went for a more menacing look. If there was one thing Young was it was menacing…and fat. The epitome of an Italian, complete with a dirty wife beater and black dress pants. Don Laproni: What’s with this tension? What? No warm welcome into your piece of shit abode? Nothing for an old friend? And here I thought you would be glad to see me. Don Laproni took his hands out of his pockets and fixed his black and grey suit. He ran them over his stubbly beard and scrunched up his forehead before continuing around the apartment. Shaun Styles: How’d you find me? Shaun Styles made a move towards Laproni but was intersected by a bullet that missed him by a hair. Shaun Styles: This is how you treat an old friend? No wonder I never invite you. Don Laproni: It’s good to see you still have your old sense of humor. Don Laproni continued to circle the small room, touching the oven, a small case of books, and picking up the telephone. Shaun Styles: Find anything you like? He was visibly pushing Laproni’s buttons. Don walked over to him after putting the phone back on its hook and gave Shaun’s face a playful smack. He looked deep into his eyes before quickly grabbing him by the neck and pushing him back. Shaun bumps against the television set, knocking it over, and finds himself pressed against a wall. Don headbutts Shaun’s face continuously causing it to grow severely red. It begins to swell up as blood drips down from his nose and Styles lets out a hearty groan. Don waits until his smaller opponent is not struggling any longer and reaches into his pocket, pulling out a black metal flapjack. Holding it high into the air, he drops it quickly on the back of Styles’ head and he immediately falls to the ground, right next to his television. Laproni takes a step back while he takes a handkerchief out of his coat breast pocket and wipes Shaun’s blood off his hands. Don Laproni: I hate how messy this job gets…Young…bring him out, I got Tony bringing the car around. Don Laproni throws the handkerchief down before walking out of the apartment. Young stuffs his gun into his pants and walks over to the fallen Shaun Styles. He gets a good grip around his waist and lifts him over his shoulder with considerable ease. The henchman follows Don Laproni out of the apartment building and descends down a sturdy staircase. Shaun Styles’ blurry vision grows more and foggier as he begins to fade out. Soon enough, his distorted perspective on life becomes a silent darkness. He began to see swirling shapes and bright lights. Sounds filled up the atmosphere around him, a car horn, screams coming from a distance. He saw himself back in that cold and empty warehouse. The high ceiling appearing untouchable with its few broken windows nestled high above. A small group of folding chairs set around a raggedy ring. This was his downfall. Styles was able to visualize Jade Taylor somewhere among the chaos. Where ya been, man? It’s been too long. He could hear himself utter the words as he slowly jogged up to his best friend and give him a warm hug. Taylor returned the gesture and grew a big smile on his face and the two men embraced as brothers. Shaun tried to remember more of their meeting but a sudden, halting stop interrupted his noir. Shaun’s eyes slowly opened as he was tossed like a rag doll off of Young’s shoulders. A giant metal door closed behind him as he struggled to get to his feet. Wiping the blood out of his eyes, he began to see an image. It was the very same warehouse that ended his boxing career. Instead of the raggedy ring in the middle of the complex, there was Jade Taylor tied to a folding chair with duct tape covering his mouth. The thought of his only friend gagged gave Shaun Styles the power to crawl over to him and use the chair as support to get to a vertical base. He slowly removed the tape from Taylor’s mouth as he began to open his eyes. His youthful face was ruined by bruises and covered with blood flowing from his forehead and nose. The slick black hair that was his trademark was all out of place and ruffled while his usual classy attire was now merely a dirty white shirt stained with crimson blood. Shaun was able to let out a whisper. "Where ya been, man? It’s been too long."
