Homo/phobic #2 - The Vice Tightens

21 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up where we last left off, outside the office of the Tag Team Commissioners and right outside the Primetime Central Tournament Planning room… purely back by popular demand you realise. From within, voices can be heard. "Hey, boy!" "Yes, sir?" "Where's my stiff?" "In my hand, sir… and I still don't recall this being apart of my contractual duties." "Hey, if it's good enough for the FAW Wrestlers, it's good enough for you. Besides, it was in the fine print. And I don't mean that, I mean the one we're dedicating the new tournament to, remember? … I explained why that's important right?" "Yes sir… and it's over there." "Where?" "You see that coffin that you've placed your coffee on?" "Get to the point!" "Well, sir, it's in there." "You always have to shift things don't you?" "A place for everything and everything in its place, sir." "So you did read the fine print! Right, I'll just go over and check that it's ok." *Creek* "Hey! This isn't Magnum Wolf!" "You're very observant, sir." "Why not? I specifically recall asking for his carcass." "Well sir, ever since he turned nomad he's been hard to track down." "What do you mean 'ever since'! He's been mad for years!" "… I meant since he was booted from OSE he's been hard to track." "Well, I always say that to track a wolf, you have to use a wolf." "They were out of Wolves at the store, sir, so I bought a lettuce." "And?" "Well. Though it's as clever as Magnum Wolf, and comes from the same gene pool… it didn't find him sir." "So who's this dead guy?" "A Cop." "A Copout more like." "Yes, sir." "So what's his name?" "Err… 'Edward the generic, featured on one episode only, former Police Chief with a 'comically' similarly named second and surname Roland Roland'… I think. I dug him up from a Vice Squad promotion... literally." "Now there's a claim to fame. Edward eh? What happened to him?" "He was killed on a drug bust apparently." "There's a Tomahawk in his back!" "Yeah. Jay Phoenix hates to be disturbed at home." The sound of a drum beat rings out. The cameraman steps away from the door. He moves the camera from side to side in a visible display of disdain as it slowly begins to fade out… The true scene opens up outside another corridor, this time it is not in keeping with that of the PTC. The corridor seems somewhat run down; somewhat under maintained though tidily kept and clean. The walls are dented in places and far too many notice boards adorn it to be anything other than for practical purposes. An old red coffee machine with a half torn Nescafe sticker forms the centrepiece of the whitewash scene. Standing besides it, closely huddled together in similarly creased suits that adorn the rigors of a hard day's work, are two men. The younger of the two men stoops forward to collect his coffee from the dispenser, the hot steam issues forth from the Styrofoam just as his words penetrate the scene. Lees: "We have to stick together. No matter what department we belong to, no matter what precinct, city, or even jurisdiction, we have to show a united front." The other, larger, man simply nods as he impatiently tests his coffee with a tiny sip. Lees: "Let's face it… it's not fair anyway." Again, the larger man simply nods, all the while his eyes set firmly upon the coffee that he now holds at waist height. Lees: "With Vice Squad preoccupied with their own personal vendetta, it's up to us to take up the impetus… to help them out. To even the playing field so to speak." Again, the larger man nods, this time with a determined and grizzled expression. His monotonous bobbing suddenly ceases. Grant: "Exactly how are we going to do that?" Lees smiles, finally the avenue he'd been waiting for to reveal his masterminded scheme presents itself. Lees: "Well… since our boys can't train, we make sure that Homo/Phobic is afforded the same courtesy." Grant stares at his partner blankly. Lees rolls his eyes. Lees: "We haul them in, there's already an outstanding warrant on their asses for that WWA stunt they pulled." Grant nods purposefully, feigning his level of understanding somewhat. Grant: "Right! So, how do we find them?" Lees coolly replies. Lees: "We could give Magnum Wolf a call. I hear he's a close and personal friend of the Bum Boozlers." Again Grant affords his only gesture to date. Grant: "Yeah, I saw the graffiti in the toilets." He now offers a pleading expression, his only free hand held palm upwards. Grant: "But come on! Magnum Freaking Wolf! He's a massive Out Of Control Problem!" Lees smiles. Lees: "I thought you might say that… so I went with plan B." Grant: "Plan B?" Lees: "We raided the PTC and gathered some of the Boozler's known associates up, we're hoping a witness from the UWF, a Mr Duc Chu Song, might help us finger his closest associate out of the bunch." Grant: "And then?" Lees: "And then we question them. Find out where Shane and the Boozler are hiding out, then we nab them, rough them up, lock them up, then plant them on the FAW doorstep just in time for their bout with Vice Squad!" Grant grins. Lees reaches out and tugs on Grant's jacket. Lees: "Come on, Mr Song is waiting." The pair turns around and heads down the corridor as we fade… (_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_) Everything is super! And why wouldn't it be? It's a glorious morning. The sun is shining in the sky, and the wet dew twinkles atop the crisp grass as it rises to meet the sun blessed kiss. The birds frolic in the sky above, chasing one another in tweeting courtship and lovers wearing nothing but pink PVC underpants lay beneath a tree upon a tartan blanket. Tom: "I can understand why we might be here this morning Bobby… but again, why do I have to wear the pink PVC short shorts? They do nothing for my figure." Bobby turns around to face Tom. Twisting a small flower between his index finger and his thumb; he sighs a heavy sigh of carefree bliss… ignoring Tom's question totally. Boozler: "Oh, sugar, isn't life just wonderful?" Tom: "I thought you wanted to kill yourself?" The Boozler's face drops. Boozler: "Oh yeah." The Boozler gazes ahead for a moment across the still lake. He begins again. Boozler: "Isn't today wonderful?" Tom: "It'd be better if I weren't wearing PVC." Boozler: "I have the love of a great man, the PTC Tag Titles… still… We were voted PTC's Best Tag Team of the Year, I have hoards of adoring fans screaming for my UWF return, and today is just soooo super." Tom allows the Boozler to have his joyous moment… for a while, a short lived while. Tom: "Of course, that could all change very soon you know?" The Boozler immediately sits up. He adjusts himself and falls back onto his elbows, twisting his neck he stares starkly at Tom. Boozler: "How, poodle plumbs?!" Tom: "Well… now you have to defend your titles again. And this just may be your toughest challenge yet." The Boozler playfully taps Tom's bare chest, allowing his hand to linger long enough for Tom to feel even more uncomfortable for a man wearing PVC pants in a park full of people. Boozler: "Oh. You always say that, babe." Tom: "I know! But this time I might be right." The Boozler pays him a little more concern. Boozler: "So, exactly who are they…?" Tom: "Vice Squad. They're mean cops on the wrong side of the law?" The Boozler grins. Boozler: "Dirty cops?" Tom: "Yep, but not how you mean." The Boozler's smile falters. Boozler: "Oh. Do you mean dirty like taking bribe money from that Chryste bitch?" Tom: "No… that was the PTC's 'Some Guy Is Leaving So, Hey, Let's Have a Random Award Ceremony Polling Committee'." Boozler: "Oh yeah. I always get those two mixed up sugar." Tom: "As I was saying, Vice Squad are more your garden-variety Copyright breaking, plagiarising evil doers, borrowing from such works as Airplane, Phoenix Nights and an assortment of others via their obviously indispensable UK Gold Subscription package." Tom takes a deep breath. Boozler: "Huh?" Tom: "Helo, hello, hello… Listen carefully, for I will say this only once: Whoa, whoa, whoa sweet child of mine." The Boozler scratches his head. Boozler: "Eh?" Tom: "Great British comedy, Bobby." The Boozler shrugs. Tom: "Phoenix Nights? Ring a bell?" Boozler: "Oh yes." Tom smiles. Boozler: "Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays." Tom: "What?" Boozler: "Phoenix's Nights…" Tom: "What?" Boozler: "Those are Jay Phoenix's nights. I fit Trigger in on Tuesday, and Belmont on Friday too… Thursday I wash my hair and Sunday is Wolfy's night." Tom screws his face up in disgust. Tom: "That's not what I meant." Boozler: "You didn't?" Tom: "No. Forget it Bobby, you really need to start watching more TV. And people say that everyone in FAW is uptight." Boozler: "Who do sugar?" Tom: "Oh you know, people… them mainly actually.” Boozler: "People are so nasty some time." Tom: "They sure are." The scene slowly fades away as the Boozler pulls out a sausage from his picnic basket. Lees: "Mr Song." Duc Chu Song: "Yes you capitalist pig?" The sound of a small scuffle erupts. Lees: "SIR! I take offence at that!" Duc Chu Song stares at the man, unwavering and unremorseful. Lees: "Well… if you must, then please swap the word pig, we're tired of that label." Duc Chu Song: "Is Chipmunk ok?" Lees: "Yes." Duc Chu Song: "Yes, you capitalist chipmunk?" Lees: "Sir, can you identify the man most closely associated to the Bum Boozler and Shane Stone?" Duc Chu Song stares off camera for a long while, his head moving from left to right slowly as he gazes at the line up. He raises his hand, pointing off shot, presumably towards someone. Duc Chu Song: "That's the man!" Lees: "Take your time sir, look closely now." Duc Chu Song tries to pry his squinty Vietnamese eyes open some more as he cranes his neck forward. Duc Chu Song: "Yes, number six, that's him!" Lees: "Are you sure?" Duc nods. Duc Chu Song: "I'd recognise him anywhere." Lees: "Honestly?" Duc Chu Song: "Honestly!" Lees: "Sir, thank you for your time." Duc Chu Song: "No problem you capitalist chipmunk, NYAHAHAHAHAHA!" Lees turns around to Grant as the diminutive North Vietnamese man walks away. Lees: "Let's take him down for questioning." (_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_) The scene slowly melts away from the Police Station and rejoins the Bum Boozler and Tom as they continue their discussion. The Boozler still sucks on his sausage, taking the time to savour the taste as he slips it in and out of his pink lips. Tom: "Luckily, I doubt they'll be focussing on you too much. It turns out that their old Chief was killed by Cleetus, so they'll be preoccupied." The Bum Boozler tosses his sausage away and bites his lip in shock. Boozler: "Oh those poor darlings. But it sounds like it was the Chiefs own fault, he should have known better." Tom: "What do you mean, 'known better'?" The Boozler cocks his head to the side. Boozler: "Many a brave adventurer has died during the tiresome search for the clitoris, it's why I advice against it… go with the Bum, the Bum is Yum." Tom shakes his head as the Boozler continues. Boozler: "God would never have hidden it if he'd intended for man to find it." Tom: "Bobby, I said Cleetus, not clitoris… apparently Cleetus was doing a drug deal, and you'll never guess why?" The Boozler scratches his head. Boozler: “To make money?” Tom: "Yes! How did you guess? Did you see the Vice Squad promo?" Boozler: "No. I thought that was why all the dealers do it?" Tom: "Hmm… you'd think wouldn't you, silly Vice Squad." Again the scene fades away. (_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_) The scene opens up immediately and seamlessly thanks to the editing genius of the PTC Production crew, this time the scene is that of a small square room. The room is coloured in several shades of bland grey, from the walls, to the floor and including the ceiling. The face of Grant graces the screen as he sits at a desk that bares the burden of a small tape recorder, eight empty cups of coffee and Grant's own tired slouch. Behind him, leaning against a very large mirror, stands Lees. Lees' sleeves are rolled up high, his shirt is creased all over, and stubble has firmly taken residence upon his jaw. Lees looks at his watch. He stares off shot, beyond the camera. Lees: "Listen Sir, tell us where the Boozler and Shane are and we can all go home." There is no reply. Lees: "Wouldn't you like to go home sir?" Still no reply. Lees brings his hand up to his weary eyes and rubs them. He gazes through his fingers at the man that has refused to provide any real answers to any of his hard hitting question for the past four hours now. A man that has stood steadfast under all of their greatest tricks, good cop - bad cop, threats, deals, understanding… they were fast running out of options. Lees suddenly dashes over to the table where Grant sits and slams his hands upon it; the cups tip, spilling their cold dregs before rolling off the table. Lees: "Look you fucking perp, tell us where Homo/Phobic are, now!!!" … still silence. Lees looks at the running cassette tape. He stares back out of shot, towards the interviewee. He looks down at his hands… his hands that now reach for the cassette recorder… Lees: "Interview suspended at twenty three hundred and five." Lees' finger presses the stop button firmly. He coldly stares back up. Lees: "I was hoping it wouldn't come to this, sir. Grant." The large Grant rises from his chair as the scene slowly fades out once again. Boozler: "Anyway sweet pea, Vice Squad can't hurt me because I'm a Bobby too.” Tom shakes his head. Tom: "No. They're Bobbies, you are Bobby." Boozler: "Exactly. They'd never hurt one of their own." Tom: "Bobby, you're not a Bobby!" Boozler: "I am sugar. Ask anyone! I was christened Bobby Brain Bangemhard!" Tom: "Yes, but you're not actually a cop." Boozler: "Cop, Policeman, Bobby… it's all the same sugar." Tom: "You do realise that the term Bobby and Bobbies doesn't mean that only people called Bobby are policemen right?" The Boozler blankly stares ahead for a while. Boozler: "… yes… of course I do, sugar, I'm not stupid." Tom: "Ok. So we've established that they will be only too happy to use police brutality on you." The Boozler thrusts his groin up towards the sky. Boozler: "Yeah baby!" Tom: "They'll try to take the belts too." Boozler: "Who cares? It's never been about the belts, it's always been about Shane and our love for one another, and our suicide pact." The Bum Boozler hugs himself at the thought of his eternal love for Shane. Tom: "Ok. There's also a distinct possibility that they might lock you guys up." Bobby grins excitedly. Boozler: "Locked up with hundreds of big, burly, sexually deprived men, all of whom are happy to spend all day long looking for the soap in the shower block. And Shaneypoos and I will be forced to share bunks… It sounds super!" Bobby closes his eyes and pictures a mass of writhing soapy bodies, with him in the centre. Tom: "I don't think you get it Bobby. If you guys get arrested and locked up you won't be put together." The Boozler looks at Tom oddly. Boozler: "Why?” Tom: "Because, remember Igglepop, your seven year old lover?" Boozler: "How could I forget, Babe. He was the best boy I'd ever had… and the reason I came to the PTC ion the first place.” Tom: "Right. And because of that you won't be put in general population. They don't put the likes of you with the others for fear that they'll tear you a new asshole." Boozler: "That'd be swell… the old one's starting to chaff." The Bum Boozler adjusts his cutting pink PVC short shorts. Tom: "Bobby, listen carefully. They have a special place for people like you, away from others." Boozler: "Does it have internet access?" Tom: "I wouldn't be at all surprised. But the point is that you'll be separated from Shane." The Boozler's eyes seem to enlarge! Boozler: "No!" Tom: "Yes! He'll be with the other men, and you… well, you'll be with the worst fiends that human society has to offer." Boozler: "No!!! They can't!!!" Tom: "Yes, Bobby! So, you have to defeat them, make sure they're not in any fit state to arrest you and Shane." Bobby pounds his fist into his hands, he scowls… his coarse expression then fades to a gentler one. Boozler: "But, I bet they're sooo cute.” Tom: "Maybe. But you'll just have to have your way with them during the bout. If you don't, not only will you have to say good bye to any plans of you and Shane killing yourselves in a romantic suicide pact, but you'll have to say goodbye to Shane period!" The Boozler leaps up. Boozler: "Take away my Shaneypoos? NEVER! It's time to show Vice Squad that their truncheons are nothing compared to the hard wood of the Whole Twelve Inches Baby!!!" Tom grins. Tom: "Let's get training!" Boozler: "It's time for FAWplay baby!!!" The scene fades as the pair pack up their picnic. Yes?" Lees grins with tremendous satisfaction… the system works. Lees then looks off to the side where Grant stands stoically. Lees: "Put him back on the chair." Lees moves back over to the table as Grant walks out of shot to help the interviewee back into his seat. Lees: "Interview resuming at zero hundred and five hours." The sound of a scraping chair precedes the table being rocked slightly. Grant steps back into shot. Lees: "Ok Sir… where is the Bum Boozler and Shane Stone, where can we find Homo/Phobic?" "Mugh mhive mhin mha mhapartment mhock mhon mha mhest hide mhof mhown." For the first time the camera zooms out to reveal the Interviewee. The man affectionately known as Gimp, the Boozler's valet, sits on the chair opposite the cops. His full leather body suit is all mashed and scratched, his multitude of zippers and piercing all bent out of shape, twisted and deformed, like the leather-entombed creature himself. Gimp scratches his leather encased chin as he continues. Gimp: "Mhey mhent mho mhe mhark mhis mhorning mhand mheft mhe, mhy?" Lees looks at Grant. Lees: "We may have to rethink our plan?" Gimp: "Mhi mhidn't mho mhanything, mhi've mheen mha mhood mhimp. Mhi'm mhalways mha mhood mhimp." Gimp nods. Lees looks at Grant. Lees: "Do we have Magnum Wolf's number on file?" Grant slaps his hands over his eyes as the scene slowly fades…
