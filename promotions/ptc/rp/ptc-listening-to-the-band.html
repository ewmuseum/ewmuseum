Listening to the band

1 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Smoke fills the room, as GW commentator, Brandi Sights walks into a hole in the wall bar, looking for Ice. As she is followed by her camera man the three men who are in the bar stop their game of pool, and watch the beauty walk past them. Smiles of pleasure, creep across their grizzly bearded faces, as the red headed bombshell, in her short leather mini skirt, and white button up dress blouse glides across the room. Brandi walks up to the bar, and begins to talk with the bartender, as the camera pans around the bar. In the far left hand corner of the building is a stage, with a band getting ready to perform. The band are plugging in their instruments, and doing microphone tests, before the nearly empty bar. The lead singer turns around and bows his head in disappointment at the sight of the crowd, or rather lack there of. The band begins to play, and a screeching sound fills the small bar. The drummer cracks the symbols three times, and the band begins to jam. Singer:"Welcome to my world. This world I made. In my world I'll break your face..." The band continues to play as the cameraman turns his attention away from the stage and back to the bar where Brandi Sights is still talking to the bartender. Brandi appears to be getting frusterated with the bartender, as her face slightly begins to turn red, and her voice appears to raise. However, what she is saying still cannot be made out clearly do to the noise being produced by the performing band. "Donny....Donny....give me the usual." The bartender turns his attention from the beautiful Brandi Sights, and to the new guest sitting at the bar. After hearing the request, from the new voice, the bartender just up and leaves Brandi, in mid sentence, and goes to a door on the back wall behind the bar. Brandi lets out a deep sigh of frustration, and puts her hands, in frustration, over her face before screaming. "Who in the hell do you think you are buddy! I wasn't finished talking to him!" The camera turns and sees Ice sitting at the bar with a cocky smirk on his face. The camera turns back to Brandi who still has her head in her hands. "How about I make it up to you and buy you a drink?" Brandi violently pulls her head away from her hands, and snaps her head in the direction of the voice. Her eyes widen in shock as she finally sees just who it is talking to her. "Donny....better make that two." The bartender smiles as he sets down Ice's drink, and heads back into the back to make another. Brandi stands up from her seat, and walks over to the stool next to Ice. She takes a seat, and begins to fix her hair, as if preparing to do an interview. Ice notices this and quickly cuts her off at the start. "No, this is not the place or the time for an interview. When Donny comes back with your drink, just enjoy it." "But, I really have some questions I would like to ask you." Ice looks away from Brandi and cracks his neck, then turns his attention back towards her. He takes a small drink from his glass, and sets it back down. The bartender walks up and sets a drink identicle to the drink Ice has, in front of Brandi. She looks down at the drink, and then nods in appreciation towards the bartender. "You drink, and then you ask. But you only get two questions Ms. Sights. Is that understood?" Brandi smiles at Ice and takes a huge gulp of the drink in front of her. She immediately begins coughing violently upon swallowing the drink, and Ice begins to cockily chuckle to himself. "A little kick to it eh? It's a little hard to swallow. Kind of like the truth. Kind of like when you realize your time has come, and fate is about to lead you in the direction it wants to take you. Right now, seventeen other men are having a hard time swallowing the fact that in this battle royal, their time has come. They will meet their fate, and what fate has in store for them, is not what they had planned on. So they all had better come prepared, because I don't come to play games. I come to win. " Brandi, looking a little woozy, laughs out loud at Ice's comment. "You said come.TEHEHEHEHE" Ice raises an eyebrow in confusion at Brandi's hysterical laughing. Ice's confused look quickly subsides, and is replaced with anger. Slowly he leans in and gets face to face with Brandi. "DO YOU FIND ME FUNNY?! DO YOU FIND THIS FUNNY?! WELL DO YOU!?" Brandi jumps back, startled by Ice's unprovoked yelling. "THIS IS NOT A LAUGHING MATTER! I'M SICK AND TIRED OF BEING LAUGHED AT! I'M SICK AND TIRED OF NOT BEING RECOGNIZED! I HAVE PROVEN MYSELF TIME AND TIME AGAIN! YET I STILL GET NO RESPECT FROM YOU! FROM THE PIECES OF CRAP THAT ARE GOING TO BE IN THE RING WITH ME IN THIS MATCH. FROM NOBODY! WELL EXTREME RUMBLE YOU ALL WILL LEARN TO RESPECT ME! BECAUSE LIKE IT OR NOT.....I AM THE BEST THIS BUSINESS HAS TO OFFER. IF YOU BEG TO DIFFER....PROVE ME WRONG." Ice leans back and regains his composure. He takes another long drink, and motions to the bartender for another. The bartender quickly refills his glass, and Ice swallows his drink. Blankly he stares down at the empty glass, while motioning another with his hand. The bartender fills up another glass and sets down another drink in front of Ice. Ice finishes it all in one gulp. As he swallows he sneers and throws the glass, shattering it against the wall behind the bar. Without hesitation Ice then stands up, and walks out the door. The bartender quickly rushes over, and begins to clean up the mess. The camera turns to Brandi Sights, who has a stunned look on her face. FADE OUT........FADE IN A picture of the PTC Extreme Champion is shown. The glass covering the picture begins to freeze up, and crack. The cracking starts at the center with a small dot, then splinters out across the entire thing. Finally the entire glass cover shatters into a million pieces, slicing up the picutre.
