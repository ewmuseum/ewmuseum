Date: 2005-03-20 Bum Bump... Bum Bump... Bum Bump... The pulsating rhythm of the human body seemingly echoed around the dimly lit room. Shadows danced across the otherwise boring gray walls, accompanied by various grunts and screams all the cadence of a human heartbeat. The heart was a metronome, in which the actions were to occur... it was to the fight as a drumbeat is to a song. The artistic motions of which is known as fighting trudges on, because without the metronome... the fight would be no more. The cadence skips off beat, and air is forced out of the body with a hard smack on the padded concrete floor. Her chest rises up and down as she stares up at the ceiling trying to regain the consistent rhythm that she once had. Her lungs expanded begging for air, and the tempo accelerated in response... Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Slowly she rolled over to her side, taking a few much needed breaths of air. Forcing her way up to her knees and elbows, the inconsistent cadence began to level out at a much more comfortable rate. Shades of blonde blinded her vision when she looked up at her adversary, before brushing her beautiful golden locks out of her eyes. Her brilliant cerulean eyes narrowed, as if burning holes in the man standing before her. One fluent motion was all it took for her to reach her feet, battered but not beaten she stood strong in front of the enemy... “Is that all you got?” She said between breaths with a confident smile, the passion in her eyes told the story. The dim lights from above glistened off her moist, tanned skin while she adjusted the black elbow pad on her right arm. The cadet blue eyes of her adversary looked back at her... cold and unforgiving. She know nothing she could ever say would phase the stone wall that stood before her, yet she always thought it was worth a shot. Perhaps it was by habit, it had worked so well over the years. The man cracked his neck in response, ready to give the fiery vixen another shot. With a battle cry she lunged forward at break-neck speed... barely missing her target, nipping the side of his right ear. In response he brought a mighty taped forearm down on the back of her neck, sending her staggering forward. She screamed as the man grabbed a handful of her blonde hair and used it like a rope to send her back towards him. Before she knew it, she found herself locked in what appeared to be a dragon sleeper. His teeth clinched together as he tightened the hold, trying to block the blood and air passages from reaching her brain. She waved her arms aimlessly in the air trying to survive, but slowly everything was growing a little blurry... Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump Bum Bump “Stop being so brash!” The man yelled in response. She tried to listen, but it has hard to hear his instructions over the rapid pounding resonating from her chest. She relaxed her body in the hold, and tried concentrate... ignoring the room that seemed to be spinning around her. Running out of breath, she had to do something quick... it was only a matter of a few seconds before she blacked out... Bum Bump... Bum Bump... Bum Bump... The man tightened the grip on her neck, making her cringe in pain. She felt as if she was drowning... watching the sky fall farther and farther away as she plotted to break free. Suddenly, on her last breath she grabbed around the man’s stomach and vaulted into the air. In mid-air, she turned the backflip into a flying head-scissors, breaking free of the hold and sending the man skidding across the mat. Down on her knees she took deep breaths, filling her lungs with much needed oxygen. “Well done Vivica... that’s what happens when you concentrate and don’t let others throw you off your game.” He said sitting on the mat now, staring back at the panting beauty on the other side of the practice mat. Regaining her normal breathing pattern, Vivica J. Valentine stared back at her counter-part, dressed in a sleeveless dress shirt, a black suit vest, and black slacks. She still got a little angry about comments like that, and she had every right. Almost 5 years ago she almost defeated him, but a lot has changed since then. Rolling her eyes, she pushed herself off the mat and back up to her feet. “So Christian... is that how you’re going to beat Jamester? Lecture him to death? How fearsome...” Vivica sarcastically replied. She hated to be such a bitch... no that’s actually a lie, she really loved being a bitch, but not so much to Christian. Force a habit gets the best of you though, and most of the time she wouldn’t even think about who she was talking to when she said it. Zenith didn’t reply right away, but instead decided to start unwrapping the tape from around his fists and forearms. “Jamester doesn’t pose a threat to me.” Christian said as he ripped the tape off of his forearm, “I’ve done things in my life that Jamester has only dreamed of. He made it his mission in LIFE to claim the GW World Title as his own... he’s failed on so many attempts, I take it in 6 months. All the hype surrounding Showdown in Tokyo returning winner Jesse Jamester this year in Tokyo, I lay his ass out and beat him in my first try and take it as my own.” “Yeah, I’m starting to get a little tired of all this ‘Former PTC Global Champion’ bullshit myself...” Vivica stated before pulled her left elbow pad off her arm, “it reminds me a lot of Showdown in Tokyo. I don’t see what’s so special... I could take him myself. I can’t see what has made him so praise worthy over the years.” “He’s The Jackal...” Christian paused before looking over at Vivica J. Valentine, “and the nickname couldn’t be anymore fitting. The Jackal was once believed to be a God by the Egyptians, but recent generations have found them to be nothing but mere dogs. They hunt in groups... too much of a coward to hunt anything bigger than themselves without help. The let the real hunters, like cheetahs and lions... or me, kill their meals, so they can scare them away and scavenge their leftovers. That’s exactly what Jamester is Vivica... all bark and no bite. A scavenger who leeches off the success of others and slowly degenerates it into their own, how pathetic. I lost to that coward once, I know everything he has to offer now... and it isn’t much. He don’t stand a chance in hell.” Christian Zenith pulls off the last piece of tape and rolls it into a ball. Vivica J. Valentine looks over at the GW World Championship laying on the table next to the trash can, while Zenith shoots a 3-pointer into the trash that goes in. Valentine stares at the stars through the windows that go all the way around 3/4 of the room... “Let’s call it a night.” Christian said as he picked the GW World Heavyweight Championship belt off the table and threw it over his shoulder. With a sigh of relief, Vivica turned her back on the night sky and follow Christian towards the exit, marked above a blue door on the opposite side of the room. With a flick of his finger, Zenith sent the training room plummeting into darkness. Suddenly, an explosion broke the sweet tranquillity of the darkness. Vivica J. Valentine was no where to be found... until he looked down on the floor. Vivica laid face down... still breathing but unconscious, with a dark sticking out of the back of her neck, covered in a blanket of glass. “Who in the hell are you?” Christian Zenith asked as he threw he GW World Heavyweight Championship to the floor and stood in front of the unconscious Vivica. The men moved towards Christian... emotionless, like robots set to kill. Zenith’s cadet blue eyes shot back and forth between the 8 soldiers, as they closed in like wolfs on a kill. “So... you want to die?” Zenith said to the emotionless predators, as they grew closer and closer to him and the limp Vivica J. Valentine. Without warning, the army sprinted towards Christian Zenith at break-neck speed. Christian stood his ground, poised for a fight as they approached him. One of the soldiers threw a punch, Zenith ducked under and drilled it in the chest with a kick, knocking it into one of their partners. After the kick, one of the soldiers grabbed Christian from behind, trying to choke him. Zenith gagged as he tried to break free of the choke. Finally, Christian threw the solider over his shoulder into another solider who was trying to get a free shot. Directly to his left, Christian could see one approaching out of the corner of his eye. Instinctively, Zenith slammed his foot against the side of the soldier’s knee with a snap. The solider screamed as it reached down to grab it’s shattered knee. Zenith took this opportunity to grab the soldier’s shoulder and snap it’s neck like a tree branch. Christian watched as the solider quietly fell limp onto the floor. A punch blindsided Christian from the left side and sent him stumbling across the room trying to catch his balance. The soldier ran towards the groggy Zenith, only to be met with a spin kick by Christian, sending him rolling across the glass covered floor. Christian watched as they all just got up and brushed themselves off... except of course the one with the broken neck. With the numbers dwindled down to 7, it still didn’t look good. Much to Christian’s surprise however... they began to retreat towards the windows. Zenith watched in confusion as they all began to walk backwards towards the windows, leaving their fallen comrade behind in agony. Suddenly one of the soldier’s picked up Vivica J. Valentine off the floor, and began to run towards the window. Zenith’s eyes narrowed and sprinted across the room towards the soldier in question. Suddenly, an explosion when off and Zenith felt something stab into his lower left side. Christian looked over in time to see a wire coming out of his side and leading back to one of the soldier’s, and at the very second the solider pulled the trigger and electricity was sent through Christian’s body. Zenith watched Vivica being carried farther and farther away from him. He struggled through the tazer and made his way towards Vivica, while the solider tried to catch his grappling hook on the opening where the window once was. Another tazer sunk into Christian’s back... and another into his right shoulder blade. The currents of electricity swarmed through his body as he dropped down to one knee. His vision began to fade to black as he gave out one last scream... “AAAAHHHHHHHHHHHH... He looks around the room in a panic, his cadet blue eyes darting back and forth ready for action. A dresser... A television... A mini-fridge... these are the things he saw around the room. Zenith looks down at the covers as he sits in his bed in confusion, wondering if it was real or if it was all just a dream. Quickly he caressed his left eye with his hand to see if the swelling had subsided... it had... as a matter of fact it didn’t even hurt. He quickly examined his side and the rest of his shirtless body for some sort of scar from the tazers... nothing could be found, not even a scratch. Suddenly Zenith’s bedroom door swung open, and with one motion Christian reached over to his night stand and pulled out a Desert Eagle, pointing it at the door. Behind the door Jacques jumps and ducks down on the ground after looking down the barrel of Zenith’s gun. Christian drops the gun and puts his forehead against the palm of his hand, rubbing his head back and forth. “Get up Jacques...” Zenith spoke to his quivering butler. The butler got up to his hands and knees and looked up at Christian Zenith. “I heard you yell sir... I thought something was wrong.” Jacques responded to Christian, who still looked confused as he scanned the room. Zenith watched as his butler reached his feet and dusted himself off. “Nothing is wrong Jacques... just a bad dream I assume,” Zenith said as he shoved his covers aside and got out of bed, dawning only a pair of black basketball style shorts, “is Vivica awake yet?” Jacques looked dumbfoundedly back at his billionaire master. Christian grabbed a white Under Armor T-shirt out of his drawer and pulled it over his head while Jacques replied. “Excuse me sir?” “Vivica... Zenith walked towards the 52-year old butler and with one arm pushed him out of the doorway. Christian stormed down the hallway, elegantly decorated with skylights and ambient lighting. He walked through the staggered beams of light shining down from the ceiling, his eyes determined as he fixated on a door up ahead. The gold doorknob shone underneath the sunlight from above as Zenith reached out and grabbed it. With a twist and a push, the door squeaked open... follow by Christian’s jaw almost hitting the floor. He looked around at the room... everything made from walnut wood. A desk sat in the far right corner with a decorative lamp, against the far wall was 3 bookcases... all crammed full of reading materials. Adjacent from the bookshelves was 3 file cabinets, filled with paperwork for Zenith Corporation. His footsteps squeaked as he walked around the warm room, and looked at the ceiling fan up above. “What the hell is this...” Christian said to himself aloud as he looked around the massive reading area. “This is your study sir. Would you like me to call a doctor? You have been acting much strange this morning and I really do think you should get a checkup.” Jacques suggested as Christian read the books on the shelf, trying to get some idea of what was going on. “Actually Jacques... I’d like to be alone.” Christian replied. “As you wish sir.” Jacques said before leaving Christian alone in the dimly lit study, completely baffled.
