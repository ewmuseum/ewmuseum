Kore ga Raijin

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-03 Origins. The scene opens to a field, an open grassland somewhere in Japan. The horizon glows a deep red, the sun having just crept below the horizon. Once the site of a fierce battle, few things remain here that would indicate as much, perhaps a few corroded arrowheads buried in the soil. This battlefield has quite a different significance as well; it is the place where Raijin made his first appearance in this world. In fact, the scorch mark from the first blast of lightning that ushered him in to this world still exists upon the ground here. Raijin stands within that space, as if he had never left it. Kneeling in front of him is his follower and mentor Kenzo, wearing traditional hakama and keiko-gi. He bows his head to his new student and master. Words in Japanese begin to flash across the screen; a deep voice translates them to English. When the World is in Chaos, The Greatest Shall Converge. Atop the World A Man Will Arise, On Earth as in Heaven To Stand Among Them All. The kana fade, and the camera zooms slowly into Raijin, closer to his masked face. He stands motionless, never once blinking, and not a breath escapes him. Kenzo rises stands next to him, keeping his head bowed at all times. This was written over a thousand years ago, found on a tattered scroll with other Shinto artifacts. Our scientists and religious scholars dismissed it as nothing, an unfounded writing with no base in fact. It seems those who wrote it indeed knew what would transpire though, even if it did occur one thousand years later. To those who wrote it ages ago, it was truth. Their lives were steeped in spirituality; they knew nothing else. When they foresaw something as momentous as this, they recorded it instantly, for their faith told them it would most certainly happen. For them, this was a prophecy that would one day come to pass. Raijin bows his head as Kenzo has. In the distance, dark clouds begin to gather. The sun’s light begins to slowly fade, further darkening the evening sky. The darkness does not last long though; dazzling sparks of heavenly electricity arc between the clouds, a brilliant light show, perhaps through some remaining power of Raijin. Those who saw Raijin though now know what it means. Many who were not believers now find themselves faithful. To some, it appears as just a fake wrestling stunt done to get ratings, the same that has been done for so many years. It is much more than that though. It is reality. It is Raijin’s reality, and his existence alone is proof of what was ordained so far in the past. The dark thunderclouds gradually flow overhead to fill the sky above their heads. By now, the lightning within has grown from innocent little flashes to full fledged strokes, then sheets, of lightning. That they stand out in the open does not concern them, for they are protected from this heavenly power. The force that Raijin had commanded in Heaven would not destroy him, even though he is a mortal now. Those of little faith will be the first he will defeat, the first to fall to his heavenly wrath. Those who have no belief in him will find him to be more than they ever imagined. Faith is stronger than any armor they can don, for it is only those who know and accept him that can recognize what he is truly capable of. Thunder rolls, at first softly, then louder as it all intensifies. Eventually it crashes so hard it can be felt in the ground beneath them. They stand their ground though, never moving so much as one muscle. Though he is far from knowing what being human truly is about, he is on his way to discovering it, piece by piece, bit by bit. Every day he learns more, from his own experience, and from Kenzo, his solitary comrade. Every day he becomes just a bit more like everybody else. He will never completely be one of us though, for his divine spirit keeps him tethered to the world of the kami, kept in a place where he is safe from the faults of the human soul. The human conscience does not weigh him down, nor does the human ego push him beyond his limits. His psyche cannot be fractured, for it is not as frail as an eggshell as those of his opponents’ are. Both Raijin and Kenzo stop bowing their heads; for a moment, they both look straight into the camera, but then they turn their gazes skyward. They clench their fists and slowly raise them into the air; the close their eyes. It is through this that he shall triumph. Not through strength of arm, or wit, or mind, but through the strength of his soul, firmly established in Tengoku. One of his adversaries... Coricidon... has already shown what a weak soul will do to a person. Love tore her apart. She is but a mere shell of what she should be because of that, and now she insists that she can hold up. That Raijin faces one so fragile as Coricidon means nothing to him. That she is at her weakest is immaterial now... she is the one who entered herself, knowing something such as this might happen. She knew her own heart. She knew the risk she was taking in facing one man that she had seen, and another that she had not. James Edwards is affected differently by love, though... it is not an almost debilitating emotion for him, but rather, something to induce a rare state of bliss. It is something that he, unlike Coricidon, wants to have in his life. Raijin will have none of it though. It is immaterial what his opponents may feel, for what happens outside of all of this means nothing to him. What occurs in the ring is all that matters in this world. With their hands thrust into the sky, something mysterious but strangely supernatural begins to happen. An aura begins to surround them, a faint bluish-white that radiates from all around them, seemingly coming from within. Raijin can only hope for a quick and merciful end to this battle on his part... too much suffering is never welcome, even to one such as him. Though he was a kami, looking down upon the earth he could see it all around, but he could do nothing, nor did he want to. The affairs of the Earth still troubled him at times, but he was forbade to affect any realm of Earth but his own, the realm of thunder and lightning. Now that he is a denizen of the planet he once helped oversee and not what he once was, all feelings he had toward it on the grand scale that he once felt them are gone. The sight he once had that extended to all directions and all angles as he watched Earth from Heaven is gone as well; now he can only see in one direction, a straight line which leads to the same point as all others in this tournament. It is the point which shall answer the questions he asks himself, and the point which will prove his worthiness to stand in Heaven as he did before. He stands ready for the upcoming battle, one that will surely be left in the annals of history... a history he is prepared to write. The aura grows in intensity, until it becomes almost blinding. Then, in an instant, a blue-hot bolt of godly fury strikes they earth where they stand. The cloud of dirt, and smoke, and ozone it produces is slow to fade, but in time it disappears, revealing something new, or rather, nothing at all... Raijin and Kenzo have vanished, the spot where they stood now empty. Thunder booms overhead once more, and the scene fades slowly, and finally, to black. As it began, it too shall end.
