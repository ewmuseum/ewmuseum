Brotherly love

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-30 (The scene opens in an empty gymnasium where the WWA’s Rocky Adams is beating a punch bag with everything he’s got. The echo of the rattling chains holding up the punch bag and the sickening impact of Rocky’s fist hitting it.) (We get a side shot of Rocky in padded gloves and helmet giving it a final few thumps before spitting out his mouth guard, which falls slowly off his face due to the amount of saliva its gathered. Rocky wipes his face with his forearm and begins speaking.) Rocky: “Eba-“ (Rocky’s jaw is jammed shut with the helmet strap making it practically impossible to talk. He takes off the padded helmet and throws it aside.) Rocky: “Ever since I was knee high, I’ve pushed my body to it’s limits. On the trampoline at kindergarten, I pulled a somersault and the kids stood there in awe as I landed on the rocking horse.” (A video rolls while Rocky’s voice can still be heard, it is a collection of clips showing his intense training sessions which earned him the nickname “Rocky”.) Rocky: “I don’t need another title to my name, I’m already SCWF Champion, I’ve main evented on the biggest stage of them all: WWA Rage in the Cage.” (We fade back to the scene with Rocky Adams still talking, he removes his gloves and throws them aside.) Rocky: “I don’t need to prove that I can be the best, but I want another title run, so I can show the RPW fans just what one of the WWA’s finest has to offer. Not only that, but to prove that we are leagues away from you.” (A picture of Ethan Knight appears on screen, Rocky Adams’ opponent for this first round cruiserweight match.) Rocky: “Brother Ethan, does that mean you have a leader? You take orders from someone? I’ll be your boss for this match, all I ask is that you lay down so it will be over quickly, because you don’t want this…” (We fade back as Rocky is pointing his thumbs towards himself.) Rocky: “Brother Ethan? You’re not my brother, you’re my son, because I’m your DADDY!!!”
