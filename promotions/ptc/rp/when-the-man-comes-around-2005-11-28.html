When The Man Comes Around

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 The clouds fly quickly across the moon before they come to a screeching halt, and a bolt of lightning strikes. After the lightning strike the picture turns black and the sound of what seems to be an old record player is heard… And I heard as it were the noise of thunder One of the four beasts saying come and see And I saw and behold a white horse On the words "white horse" a man steps out through the darkness wearing a three piece custom black suit. He pulls a Desert Eagle out of the inside of his black suit jacket and cocks the gun while a guitar begins to play. He looks back up at the camera, a emotionless stare appears on his face before the camera zooms in on his cadet blue right eye. There's a man goin round takin names And he decides who to free and who to blame Everybody won't be treated all the same There'll be a golden ladder reaching down When the man comes around Christian Zenith stands under the GlobalTron with his right fist high in the air, surrounded by Vivica J. Valentine, Demitri Miasnikov, and Jason LeMaster. The scene changes to Christian Zenith looking down at Ken Partridge Jr. on the floor, from inside of a cell suspended in the air holding the Global World Championship in his hand. Zenith throws Gunner Addison down, tied up on a train track… with a train coming fast. The hairs on your arm will stand up At the terror in each sip and in each sup For you partake of that last offered cup Or disappear into the potter's ground When the man comes around Christian Zenith eyes the PTC crowd as he makes his way down to the ring to face Jesse Jamester at Seven of the Best. Christian Zenith reverses the Jwister and hits Jamester with the Chicago Killing Spree for the win. Lightning strikes again, lighting the face of Zenith and the Global Wrestling World Championship over his shoulder. Christian hits Karina Wolfenden with an unexpected lariat after her Seven of the Best match. The crowd begins a "HOLY SHIT!" chant after Christian Zenith powerbombs Karina from inside the ring, to a table outside the ring where she bounces off the table and into the audience. And the whirlwind is in the thorn tree The virgins are all trimming their wicks The whirlwind is in the thorn tree It's hard for thee to kick against the pricks A dim light swings back and forth from the ceiling, vaguely showing Christian Zenith polish off his Desert Eagle 'Ares'. Christian Zenith stands in front of a packed warehouse on top of a crate as his followers cheer him on. Zenith stands above Salaok with his arm raised in the air after winning a Steel Dungeon match. Christian hits Ken Partridge Jr. with The Freefall, to make him a double champion. Till Armageddon no Shalam no Shalom. Then the father hen will call his chickens home The wise men will bow down before the throne And at his feet they'll cast their golden crown When the man comes around Christian watches a thunderstorm from his suite high above the streets of Chicago. Zenith and Josh Curtis stare each other down in the middle of the Global octagon. Josh Curtis falls to the mat after getting hit with repentance. Christian Zenith pats the GW World Championship over his right shoulder underneath the dim lighting. Zenith's hand is raised four consecutive times after four consecutive victories over Josh Curtis. Whoever is unjust let him be unjust still Whoever is righteous let him be righteous still Whoever is filthy let him be filthy still Listen to the words long written down When the man comes around Christina Reno’s door flies open as Christian Zenith marches in followed by his Hunting Grounds teammates. An image of an army marching down the street is show, with women and children cowering in fear. Zenith cracks Train over the head with a trash can. The scene then cuts to Christian hurling Train head first into a pile of abandoned chairs. Christian suplexes Train off the top turnbuckle through an announce table outside the octagon. Zenith looks down at Train, covered in blood with a snarl on his face. Hear the trumpets hear the pipers One hundred million angels singin Multitudes are marchin to the big kettle drum Voices callin voices cryin Some are born and some are dyin It's Alpha's and Omega's kingdom come Zenith looks on with a smile as Train is carried past him onto an ambulance, courtesy of Mike Biddle's services. Christian Zenith points at you, with the light reflecting off the title over his shoulder. Quickly images flash by: Christian Zenith hits Train with a DDT on the steel steps, Train is hit with Zenith's Enziguri 'Repentance', Zenith grins as Train screams in pain from a Romero Chinlock, and Christian Zenith finishes the match with The Freefall on a battered Train. And I heard a voice in the midst of the four beasts And I looked and behold a pale horse And his name that sat on him was Zenith. And Hell followed with him… Zenith's arm is raised in the middle of the octagon with the World Championship, looking down at a bloodied and beaten Train on the mat. The scene fades into Christian in the darkness once again: "Why am I here? I came here to find a challenge, not a half naked Pocahontas with an attitude problem. She couldn't beat Vivica, she cannot beat me, and she will except her destiny as a total failure. What does Danielle Northsky have that I don't? A modelling contract? Breasts? No one cares about that, beacause I have something she'll never have… a GW World Title reign. Two of them. You can't stop me Dani, but don't worry little girl… no one can. Watch me." Musical Credit: "When The Man Comes Around" by Johnny Cash
