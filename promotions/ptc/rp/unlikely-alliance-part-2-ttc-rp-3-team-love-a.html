Roulettes_rising Public Access Pimp Joined: 02 Apr 2003 Posts: 15 Location: Mississippi Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 7:20 pm Post subject: "Unlikely Alliance" part 2: TTC rp #3 (Team Love a "Legacy" "As we grow we each leave behind many things in life...we leave behind achievements as well as dreams of achievements never reached...loved ones and loved ones lost in the flow of time....victories and mistakes that can never be erased...All of these moments in our life become woven together over time and become our very own legacy. A legacy, a story of our past to pass down to future generations...a way for our souls to be forever remembered in this realm long after our physical forms have left it all behind. However each legacy is unique to its owner, and while some may cherish their legacies handed to them by ancestors long passed, some may not wish to remember their legacies, some to the point of hiding their past or simply disclaiming it completely. It is these souls of less fortunate past that have the hardest road ahead in life, for it is these brave pioneers of life that shall cast their pasts aside and create a legacy of their own...one that they can look upon with pride and satisfaction...but the road will always be paved with obstacles as the constant reminders of our true pasts are always revealed to us when we least expect it and are most vulnerable. Some fall from these taunts from the grave and follow the doomed past of their ill fated ancestors, but for those who succeed their story shall be remembered forever. So many paths in life...which to choose...." Location unknown: Saturday 12:00PM "At first glance we seem to be staring at a nothing more than a simple bullet train as it swiftly races across its predetermined path with awe inspiring speed, carrying its precious cargo to wherever their destinations lie. From within excited chatter can be heard as the passengers chat amongst themselves, their only way to pass the time until their stop arrives at long last. Yet something seems different about this place...something unnatural about this train and its cargo....as car by car is traversed it becomes obvious that the company kept within this train's shell is anything but normal. A young girl clad in a traditional school uniform of a blue rippled skirt and white blouse can be seen talking to her assumed boyfriend judging from the loving gazes on their faces as they coo at each other. Leaning forward and placing her elbows atop the glassy table between them she flips her strawberry blonde hair aside, exposing her cat like ears which begin to twitch as she purrs at the object of her desire, a blonde haired blue eyed poster boy who in turn flashes a million dollar smile only to expose his fanged teeth. Suddenly noticing they're being watched they shoot annoyed glances our way and angrily snatch the curtain and shut it with an angry hiss. Upon further glance we see that all are this way...different from us...one young female stretches and in place of arms raven wings like that of a crow raise to the sky, while a little ways away a gargoyle of massive proportions sits down, his massive girth taking up an entire bench only to see the bench collapse and give way mere seconds later, a disheartening sigh escaping from his lips as he rises once more in search of a new resting spot for the remainder of his trip. And yet amidst this strangest of traveling companions she sits....leaned back into her seat her raven hair stirs ever so slightly as the train makes a sharp turn, rushing around unseen tracks...at least unseen by the eyes of most mortals anyway. Clad in a pale blue silken blouse and black leather pants she sits in a half awakened state, her hazel eyes staring off into space..unaware or simply unafraid of her surroundings, she reflects in thought on the past days events...." Two days earlier....the meeting room.... "From the room of this unknown abode that Kayna resides in from time to time an animated conversation can be heard plainly through the intricately carved oaken door. Inside the spacious room the two figures of Kai Shinobi and Kayna Trielle can be seen sitting opposite one another in sturdy mahogany chairs with blue plush seats, only a table between them. Admiring the rich interior Kai let out a low whistle as his gaze then fell to Kayna, who sat with an all to serious expression on her face. Flashing a big goofy grin he scratched the top of his head as he spoke." Kai: Wow....fancy get up you got here...musta cost a pretty penny....work hard or born rich? Kayna:....... "Seeing her face somewhat flush from this outburst he quickly stopped his babbling and sat upright eyeing her with concern." Kai: Hey you ok there..uhh miss? "Shaking her head as if to rid herself of a bad dream or unpleasant thoughts she quickly composed herself and after clearing her throat began to speak." Kayna: Yes, I'm fine, and please call me Kayna. Its just been a very long day for me Sir Shinobi. After that last match I had...words...with Callisto, and now I come home to find this in my house... Kai: Find what? Kayna: Follow me... "Getting up from her seat she made a sweeping gesture with her hand, signaling Kai to follow in her stride. Taking the cue he stood as well as they both made their way out of the room and down the darkened hallway once more, only this time to stop about halfway down the passageway. Seeing no door in sight he shot her a puzzled expression as he watched her move toward a light switch on the wall. Pausing her hand upon the switch she started to speak." Kayna: As you came in all you noticed were the broken vases and paintings I'm sure.... Kai: Well...yeah..I mean this place is pretty dark after all... "Waving her hand to silence him she threw the switch with a forceful thrust. As she did so brilliant light flooded the once darkened hallway, illuminating and reflecting off of the many pieces of broken glass, causing Kai to throw his arm up to shield his eyes for a moment as everything comes into focus. As the blurred vision faded and his eyes adjusted he saw what she was talking about. Backing away his lips began to move but no sound came out other than a startled gasp. On the wall small streaks of a brown dried substance could be seen. Following the trail of this mysterious stain it became all to clear what these brownish streaks were...the blood from the long dead corpse of a once beautiful goat...or more precisely the head of a goat...a long metal spike through its eye holding it up in place on the wall. Looking at the head it was easy to see the creature had been long dead as part of the flesh was beginning to decay and slump off, its stench attracting flies like sweet nectar to a butterfly. Above the rotting skull was one simple phrase written in the same dried brown blood..." "The innocents fall one by one, so shall your time come." "Stifling back the urge to hurl at the gruesome sight before him Kai swallowed deeply and still fixated in gruesome curiosity he managed to speak with a weakened voice, his hand still covering his mouth." Kai: W...w...what exactly did you to talk about, taxidermy? "A forceful chuckle escaping her throat she shook her head slowly as she continued." Kayna: No..she screwed up my match...gave me that victory...there was no honor in it and I let her know that later. Apparently she's a bit ticked off at me and ready to make an example of me.... "Lowering her head she spoke through clenched teeth, her voice filled with restrained anger." Kayna: but she won't succeed...I'm not her toy....SHE WON'T WIN!! "Unable to restrain it any longer she punches the wall with such force that the remaining shards of glass fall to the carpet with a resounding thud, as the goat skull simply swings in place, the nail drove in to deeply for it to fall. Without a word she quickly spun around to face Kai, who just stood there unsure of what to do or say. Grabbing him by the shirt she pulls him toward her and began to speak once more." Kayna: That is why you MUST let me join you for this! I HAVE to prove that I can take her down, that I am her equal and not some stupid coward she can boss around! "As massive sweat beads pop out from Kai's forehead he grabbed her arm and in a frantic voice begins to speak quickly." Kai: Ok ok ok! Calm down you it! You've joined! So just let me go! "Suddenly realizing the strength of her clutch on his shirt as the fabric can be heard ripping she quickly released him, sighing deeply as she spoke apologetically." Kayna: I'm sorry..as I said it's been a long day.... "Snapping his fingers a bright grin spread across his face as sing song music begins to play from nowhere. With goofy smile intact he begins to speak." Kai: I know just the thing! You're obviously stressed and if you stay that way the entire time we're together in this you'll stress me out, so whaddya say we go celebrate Spring Heaven? Kayna: Spring Heaven? "Laughing boldly he holds up his two fingers up in the air forming a peace symbol as cherry blossoms begin to rain down from absolutely nowhere. Laughing still as the flower petals fall he begins to talk even more excitedly than before." Kai: Yes Kayna, you too can enjoy Spring Heaven...a festival of love..of peace...of Spring...but above all else.... "As mysteriously as the rest a drumroll begins to resound as the suspension builds." Kai: It's free! "As victory trumpets begin to play Kayna steps back and considers this option for a moment. Cautiously she eyed this strange man before her who makes cherry blossoms and music appear from nowhere.." Kayna: What exactly do I have to do...... "Flashing a winning smile he appears beside her and laying one hand on her shoulder he waves the other hand in front of her, forming a makeshift picture frame motion with his hand as he carried on." Kai: Absolutely nothing! This festival is all about relaxation. Why even the setting suggests it...the beach...pristine waves...sunny days all day every day..sunbathing..surfing..volleyball its all there..so come on, whaddya say? "Suddenly her face lit up as she gently place a hand on his shoulder and began to speak once more in a sultry tone." Kayna: So I get to go swimming? And sunbathing? And volleyball? "His eyes becoming starry eyed he nodded excitedly as thought bubbles of a bikini clad Kayna fill the air around him, steam pouring from his flared nostrils." Kai: Yea! Now you've got the picture! So now if we hurry up and pack we can make a day of it tomo.... Kayna:...Forget it. "A look of dread overcomes Kai's face." Kai: Why? Kayna: You just want to see me in a swimsuit! Well, forget it buster! "Slumped over a sigh escapes his lips forming a mushroom cloud as all the thought bubbles burst into a million poofs of smoke. Suddenly though this depression stopped as a gleam appeared in his eyes and with a mischievous chuckle he speaks." Kai: Then you can't join me. Kayna: WHAT? "His laughter rising he points his finger at her and continues, his voice filled with glee." Kai: You heard me right, you don't come then you don't join! I guess that means Callisto will never know what your capable of... "Her face livid she muttered under her breath and stomped her foot angrily." Kayna:...yes... "Enjoying the moment, in a singsong voice he continued to tease." Kai: I can't hear you.... Kayna: FINE I'LL GO ALRIGHT! But only on one condition..can we stop the music and cherry blossoms now? Look at my floor! "Looking down at the petal covered floor a sheepish grin escapes him as he nervously laughs. Looking up at the ceiling he begins to yell." Kai: Okay Sume, Shinzo, that's enough now! "Okay!" "Two small flying demons come down and collect the petals and take away the radio, giggling as they leave." Present time: 2:42 PM "Giggling to herself she opens her eyes and stares out the train window." Kayna: And that was how I it all started...I hafta say though..he was right..I needed this. I saw his homeland of Japo...met Athena and Ryoko..what an interesting pair.. "At once images of the golden haired demoness Ryoko and the violet haired Athena appeared in her mind...Ryoko playing volleyball with her which turned into dodgeball with her as the target....Athena using her magic to topple her surfboard...eating dinner with them as they both hovered around Kai....yes an interesting pair indeed. Laughing to herself at these memories she begins to speak aloud." Kayna: Yes it was worth it.. "Frowning she cups her head in her hands as a thought occurs to her." Kayna: Kai showed me his hometown and I promised to show him mine, but I don't really have anything to show....well what he sees is what he gets I guess... "Slowly the train comes to a halt as the attendant comes over the PA system and begins to announce the arrival of the train to the Earth realm. Collecting her things she gets up and moves toward the front of the train and upon exiting blinks in astonishment as it had somehow stopped right in the middle of Vegas! Lugging her bags to the nearest hotel she pays for a room and goes to flop her things down when a voice from nowhere startles her. Looking toward the bed she sees none other than Kai Shinobi perched atop it, sitting in the Buddha position with a big grin on his face. Hopping off the bed he speaks in his usual cheery voice." Kai: So when do we start? "Looking down at the floor she speaks, her voice barely a whisper." Kayna: We don't..... "Confused he scratches his head in thought." Kai: But isn't this the place? "Taking a deep breath she sits down on the bed her head still drooped down." Kayna: Yes it is...but this realm isn't like yours. I mean I was born here but there's not any grand festivals here...and no family either.... "With resentment she adds." Kayna: ...just people who don't care who they hurt as long as they make a dollar! Kai: Oh...I see...I'm sorry Kayna. you looked so happy a second ago and I just made you sad again.. "Looking up at him she forces a grin on her face as she replies." Kayna: Don't worry about it. It wasn't like they were the parents of the year anyway.... Kai: It still hurts though doesn't it? Kayna: ....sometimes..... "As silence ensues for a second he snaps his fingers again.." Kayna: No! No more cherry blossoms! "Laughing he quickly assures her no more blossoms will fall as he speaks." Kai: No no! I was just going to say that there still has to be some place you like to go here, so come on pick a spot, any spot and let's go! Then we can call it even! "Pausing in thought for a second, she smiles and says." Kayna: Well there is one place.... Minutes later.... Kai: This is SO not what I had in mind.... Kayna: Well you DID ask for it did you not? Kai: .....grumble... "As Kai mutters under his breath the surroundings become all to clear...the withered willow tree...the many aligned stones....as he stares around images of ghosts and zombies enter his mind. Shuddering he shakes his head to clear his thoughts as he continues to complain." Kai: But REALLY! A GRAVEYARD? "Kneeling down and placing a delicate bouquet of roses on the grave she looks up at him in bewilderment." Kayna: Don't you ever pray to your ancestors or anything? Kai: Well yea... Kayna: Then quit your bellyaching! Kai: Fine.... "Flopping down near her he watches as she kneels in silence at the Trielle grave. In this light she seemed softer than her hard exterior seemed. Quiet until she stirred he spoke to her softly." Kai: Can I ask what happened? To them? "Kicking up some loose dirt she takes a seat beside him." Kayna: ....They were murdered... Kai: How? Kayna: Fire...it was a long time ago... Kai: Ooh flashback time! Pass the popcorn! OW! "He whimpered as she proceeded to smack him across the head." Kayna: If you ruin my train of thought the flashback will go away.... Kai: Oh..yeah sorry.. Kayna: As I was saying..it was a long time ago.. "With that images of a younger Kayna come into view, seeing the two men prowling around the gardens of the Trielle mansion." "I was only seven...I didn't know..." "As the small Kayna approaches the men, we see them conversing the dreaded plan to the unknowing child, her head eagerly bobbing up and down as she jumped around." "It was just supposed to be a harmless prank...to make daddy give them their money back..." "Got any butter? OW!" "Quiet!" "Spray butter?" "Fine!" "Thanks! Mmmmm....buttery..." "Happy now?" "CRUNCH....yep perfect" "Good can we move on?" "Roll 'em!" "The young girl leads the men inside as they instruct her on what to do...she strikes the match and let it fall to the ground, the wooden floor bursting into flame....." "There wasn't supposed to be so much fire...I couldn't escape..." "Frantically the girl runs from room to room trying to find a way to douse the flames as they grow ever larger, consuming the entire room, licking away at the curtains..." "I just wanted to get away...." "Running up the staircase she sees the master bedroom.." "Mommy...Daddy...help me..." "Inside the room the two figures are near death from smoke inhalation, bound to each other by thick ropes." "This wasn't the plan...." "Frantically she tugs away at the ropes but the small body is to weak..." "Forgive me...." "Aroused by the cries of the child and the tugging they look at her longingly before one last utterance was made. Father looking her straight in the eye he demonstrates one last act of cruelty." "Take her away...I have no daughter..." "I tried Daddy.....why?" "Immediately the child is snatched away and carried outside, the conversation between these two ensued." "Now's your chance to be free...he doesn't want you...let it all just disappear.." "Like a bad dream...it was supposed to go away..." "The flames have died down now as nothing but ash remains." "But it never really goes away, does it?" Kayna: ....and that's what happene....hey what's the matter? "Looking at Kai his eyes are huge and water filled as he stares at her for a moment before bursting into tears, small waterfalls cascading down each side of his face." Kai: T...t..th..that..w-wwas so sssaaaaad! Kayna: Hey...pull yourself together Kai.. "Going to him she places her arm around him and proceeds to hug him." Kai: So warm..... Kayna: HOW DARE YOU! Kai: But-but-but...OWWWW! "This was all he could say as the many fists came down on his head as he tried desperately to get away only to be chased around by a livid Kayna." Minutes later...again.... "Both parties lay exhausted on the ground, Kai gingerly touching the bump on his head and wincing in pain. Finally Kayna sits up and after taking a deep breath she speaks." Kayna: Look its almost time for the match, so let's not kill each other before then. Kai: Good idea... "Getting up she helps her comrade to his feet and snapping her fingers, starts to speak." Kayna: Hey to make it up to you I'll show you my other favorite place here! "Looking leery he speaks." Kai: Not another graveyard is it? Kayna: Heh...nope! This place it much cooler! Kai: Well then lead the way! Kayna: Pmmmph..mmm... Kai: Huh? "Looking at her he burst into laughter as where she once stood was a pile of cherry blossoms now." Kai: Tsk tsk...shouldn't have snapped your fingers.... "Moments later they both are seen sitting atop a high tower overlooking most of the city. Smiling she looks at Kai and back to the city below saying." Kayna: This rooftop is off limits but I used to climb to this lookout all the time for the view. "Frowning Kai looks at her and back again." Kai: It's a great view but what's so special about it? Kayna: Wait 5 seconds ...4 ...3 ...2 ...1 ...and NOW! "As she said this the final rays of the sun sank beneath the horizon and as if the entire city waited for this very moment it exploded in light as each building lit up with its own design, like a dazzling array of jewels the town shone like a diamond in the desert. Looking at Kai she smiled as she turned her gaze to the city once more." Kayna: Well..what do you think? Kai: Beautiful...and this happens every day? Kayna: Yeah...I may not have really liked this place, but from far away like this...its beautiful. "Letting out a low whistle he smiles and says." Kai: Yeah...I can see why people would come to such a beautiful place.. "Letting out a small laugh as a small gust of wind whips her hair around she turns to him." Kayna: Nah..they don't come for the beauty...they just come to party, eat and mmaybe get rich. Kai: Kinda like the Spring Heaven huh? "A thoughtful gaze appearing on her face she replies." Kayna: Yea...when you look at it that way, I guess you're right. "Flashing a winning smile he turns and gazes into her eyes." Kai: Thank you Kayna, for showing me your hometown, I know it was not easy for you. "Softly touching her cheek he continues." Kai: I don't know what all exactly happened to you in the past but seeing you now..I have no doubt you are a good and honest person...and any town that can produce such people can't be all bad. I think I will enjoy working with you very much. Alas... "Making a low sweeping bow he looks up at her blushing face." Kai: ...I should be on my way now...gotta be rested for our match! Kayna: Yea..right...good night Kai Shinobi... Kai: Remember.. "Making a peace symbol with his fingers." Kai: FOR LOVE AND PEACE! "As he hopped away, rooftop to rooftop she couldn't help but smile at his childish ways. Nodding her head she looks once more at the night sky as the she thinks aloud." Kayna: He's right..no matter my past my path is up to me. It is time I grasp it and make it mine. "Looking down at the city lights below the wind whips through once more as the scene slowly fades to black. As the scene fades one thought can be heard spoken aloud." "It is here that my legacy will truly begin..."
