Crawl before you walk

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Each day, we draw on our pasts in order to learn from our mistakes, to know what we do and don’t like in life. It’s the effortless memories that we treasure that we can take a minute to pause and remember the good old times from yester years. But what happens when you can no longer draw on those times? No longer look back and remember the day you first fell in love, the day you got married, the day your first child came into the world? Worse more than that, what if you didn’t know that you had ever fallen in love, gotten married or had a child? Memory is just another part of our being that we take so for granted but for Salaok, this could no longer be the case. When he had awoken in the hospital, those days of the past were no longer there and nothing of what Salaok had been in the past was now apart of what he was becoming. With no memory or information about who you were, life becomes somewhat of a battle with no choice other than to simple go along for the ride until the day comes when memories return and you find out about the life that you once indulge in. Salaok had been content with just living his life day by day, doing what he could to make money and with the size of him, it wasn’t hard to find work. Standing outside of nightclubs or bars, choosing exactly who to let in and who to turn away because management only wanted the attractive people in their building. Everything in Salaok’s life was so far removed from the one of his past that the chance of anything jogging his memory just seemed impossible but it didn’t bother Salaok at all because this was his life now. Most people don’t get the chance to reinvent themselves and start new in life, but Salaok had been granted that chance and it was a life that he was quickly starting to enjoy. While the money wasn’t the best, the hours seemed to suit him more than just a day to day job and being allowed to take someone out the back of the building and beat them to within an inch of their life pleased Salaok. It was this part of his life that seemed confusing to Salaok and he knew that it would hold some link with his past. Each time he was instructed to "take care" of someone he enjoyed himself and wanted very much to be allowed to resolve the matter in front of those standing at the front of the building or in the club itself. There was a yearning to show people just what he could do to another human being. Salaok had wondered why it was that people coming into the club new his name and spoke to him like they knew him but was never confident enough to ask how they knew. While the past and the memories in which it holds was important to Salaok, this new life of not caring was fitting with him perfectly. He knew that like any man, he would have had the dreams of being rich and famous but that was the normal for most of mankind and yet he lived his life day by day hating those who had it. Those who walked around like they were invincible and that even God wouldn’t be able to bring their power to an end. He loathed those people, and yet, they were the people that the owner of the clubs wanted in the buildings. Their fancy clothes made the clubs look better, their fat wallets made the clubs more money, and the fact that people liked being around other wealthy people made it sure that the club would always have customers. It sickened Salaok. Until the day came when one of those fancy clad men walked up to Salaok, ignoring the line of people completely and coming straight up to him. A move that resulted in Salaok’s massive hand pressing firmly against the man’s silk shirt, stopping him dead in his tracks. "Back of the line there pal." His voice calm and in control after having learnt that when you confront these type of people with attitude that it makes the entire job harder. But the response from the man who still had Salaok’s hand pressed into his suit jacket wasn’t the one that Salaok had been expecting. "Actually Salaok Davis, I’m not here for the club, I’m hear for you. Can we speak privately for a moment as I have something that you will be interested in." The man, although much smaller than Salaok oozed with power and control, the kind that comes with years of experience and enough confidence in ones self to control any situation. The situation was strange to say the least as not only did this man just tell Salaok what his family name was for the first time, but he comes offering information, the one thing Salaok has lacked since leaving the hospital. Nodding to his Co-worker, Salaok walks away from the front of the building and around to the side alleyway where a lot of his "other duties" were performed throughout the nights. Littered with empty of smashed bottles of alcohol, thousands of cigarette stubs and a number of used condoms, it was hardly the place to be having such a discussion but neither men really seemed to care about the surroundings, only about the fact that the conversation was about to take place. A conversation that Salaok had been waiting for, for a year now. Resting against the dark brick wall with his arms folded over his chest, doing his best not to look as excited as a young child at Christmas time, Salaok waited for the small blonde man to speak. "I know you have no idea who I am young Salaok, but I am the one who holds your memories in my hand." Again, the voice very controlled and powerful but it wasn’t so much the tone of this man’s voice that concerned him, it was the words and how he was putting them together. Holding his memories in his hand? Was this some sort of taunt and bribe attempt? While Salaok would do anything to find out who he really is, there were lines that he had told himself he wouldn’t cross and sucking this man’s dick was one of them. The words continued to play on his mind, that was at least until the man once again opened him mouth while putting his hand inside his suit jacket. "See Salaok, all I want is your word that once you remember, you will not hold it against me." A strange thing to say when offering someone exactly what they are wanting. "While I don’t know who you are, I hope that whatever it is that you know will help to restore my past thoughts and for that I would be forever grateful. You have my word that I will hold nothing against you." Salaok felt strange just saying that as his word is something that he holds close to his heart and believes that it is the only thing in his life that he can offer someone. A man’s word. It’s the only thing of value that you can give when you have nothing. Other than respect that is, and he knew this man just five seconds which isn’t enough to hand that over. "Well then, your past shall be your’s again." The smaller man says as he pulls a white envelope out of his jacket pocket and holds it out for Salaok to grab. And grab is scarcely the word for what he does as his hand shoots out and ultimately thieves it out of the man’s hands, eager to open it and see his past. However the man’s voice interjects the unveiling of Salaok’s life with sharp words. "No Salaok, not now. You may open it once I am no longer in your sight for I do not want you coming to me asking more questions than what I truly believe you are ready for yet. This will be a journey that you must take small steps in order not to overdo yourself." The words seemed caring, as if this man didn’t want to see Salaok uncover anything that could possibly hurt him. "I will see you next time Salaok." With that, the man turned on his heal and walked off, out of the alleyway and into the sea and people out the front of the club. See you next time? Without warning this man had found Salaok when no one else had, comes with information regarding his life and now ends the conversation with "see you next time"? There was something about the entire situation that just didn’t feel right but for the time being Salaok didn’t care about this, as his adviser was now out of site and the envelope was free to be opened. Ripping at the parcel and then running his finger across the top, tearing it open, he hold it wide and pulls out a few pieces of paper. The first, a very small piece of paper with a hand written address on it. The second was what looked to be an acceptance letter into some wrestling tournament. The third was a letter from the man himself. Dear Salaok, While I know this encounter will have filled you with hope of your past, it has also put confusion and doubt into your heart and mind. I am doing what I am in order to see you become the man in which you once were and grow into the person you were meant to become. The address in this envelope is that of your old house. I have no doubt that by just looking at that building again some memories will return and that you will remember where you would leave the spare keys. The acceptance letter to PTC’s GTT5 is another place where I hope you will remember who you once were. I have entered you in the competition, as I believe once you get the chance to go into battle against another man again, the things of your past will flood back into your mind. Even should those memories of how to fight come back, the process will at least toughen you up for the coming weeks. This trip you are about to embark on will not be an easy one for you and I will be keeping a close eye on you but never from where you can see me. You need to take this journey on your own and decide the man in which you wish to become. Behind your foot I have dropped another envelope, but it contains no information, only cash. It will be more than enough to get you back home and to the tournament. Good luck young Salaok. From JD As soon as he had read those final two initials, he looks down behind his foot and there it is, just as it mentioned in the letter, another envelope. Reaching down swiftly, he snatches it from the grime and opens it with the same rush of the first. Enough money to get him home and to the tournament? There was well in excess of ten thousand dollars in one hundred-dollar bills. Who was this man? J.D. The initials he knew where familiar but why? This J.D. person had called Salaok by his full name, which was Davis. Perhaps, just perhaps the D was for Davis as well. Had Salaok just met a family member and not recognised the face? Sure it had been dark and the whole situation had very much been a shock to the system but surely he would know a family member’s face if he was confronted by it. But what if he was family and Salaok hadn’t been able to pick the face? How could it be that this JD character was so sure that once seeing the house that Salaok would begin to remember? How could it be he was so confident that Salaok would remember how to fight well enough to withstand this GTT5 tournament without having his life handed to him on a platter? There was only one way of finding out and it would require some, but not all of the $10,000 that this man had left him. "This the place bud?" The taxi driver’s voice booms out, snapping Salaok out of the daydream he was absorbed in. Looking out the passenger side window, he sees two colossal iron gates, encased by two gargantuan pillars and then a brick wall. With no idea if this was the place or not, Salaok looks around for some sort of information that might help him identify the property to which the address was moulded into the art work of the iron gates. Peering down at the piece of paper that hadn’t left his hand since it first arrived in it from the envelope, he confirms it’s the same before giving the driver a nod in the rear view mirror. Digging into his wad of money, the payment is made and Salaok steps out of the car only to have it speed off to another job before getting to shut the door fully behind him. This was it. The address that J.D. had given him was now the place in which he stood in front of knowing that once inside, things will never be the same again. Etching closer to the gates, his stomach begins to turn, but he fights it, not wanting his first new memory of his house to be that of desecrating it with regurgitated bacon and eggs. The gates were slightly ajar which gave the impression that either J.D. had planned this, or that Salaok was lazy and hadn’t shut the gates when he left here over a year ago. He stepped through the gates and so far nothing came flooding back although his eyes were continuously drawn from the house in the distance, and a small grassy hill off to his right. The driveway was a long one and with each step, the house came closer and seemed less and less like a house and more like a mansion. Could this massive estate belong to him? Everyone dreams of being rich and famous, but no one expects it to just be waiting for them at the end of a taxi ride. And yet, for a property who’s owner had been away for the past year, it was still in perfect condition as the grass was all short and well kept, the leaves which had fallen had been raked and stored out of site and the hearse sitting in front of the house was well polished A hearse? Upon closer look, the hearse wasn’t stock standard and had all the trimmings of a rich man’s car with the extremely oversized sound and entertainment system, the chrome wheels and an extended body kit, which made it look chunkier. A hearse that brought a smile to his face. A smile which drew attention from Salaok as he remembered it was his. Memories of driving it, working on it and buying it were all there. He remembers a car, but not the face of someone who could have been a family member? And then he peered up at the mansion in front of him, looming above like a giant monster with all the windows looking down at like eyes of a creature that knows. As excitement begins to riffle his body, he can not help but run up the stairs, all 42 of them, only jumping over steps 18 and 27 for some unknown reason and then stopping at the doorway. Two grand old red wood doors great him with an old style Gothic knocker instead of a doorbell which dated the entrance perfectly. The two doors in front of him held all the answers to the past and all he had to do was go inside and hope he remembers. But first there was something else he had to remember. Where the hell were the keys? Looking from left to right along the front of the building and then he grinned to himself as he looked up above the doors at the small statue of the raven sitting above them. "Nevermore." He said to himself with a smile and excitement pulsing through his body as he reaches up and feels behind the statue. They were there. They keys sat behind the statue and now rested in his hand wanting ever so much to just be inserted into the lock on the door and yet he couldn’t. If you were faced with finding out everything you knew about your life wasn’t the real story, could you jump headfirst into it without taking time to think? Maybe you could, but for Salaok and the situation in which he finds himself in, he couldn’t. Each time he put the keys forward towards the lock, he stopped himself. He couldn’t help but feel ridiculous and foolish as it was the one thing that he had been wanting since walking out of the hospital a year ago. For as long as he could remember, knowing who he was is all he had wanted. What if he didn’t like who he once was? What if the life he leads now is what he is comfortable with? What if he was to take the Hearse for a drive to clear his mind. You must first learn to crawl before you can walk and entering that house would be running straight into the unknown. With a heavy heart, he turns and walks down the stairs, again missing the 27th and 18th stairs without thinking about it, towards the one thing he does remember loving. After fumbling with the spare keys for a few seconds, he realises that car is opened by the button on the black casing, and not only does the alarm disarm, but the driver side door swings open. Nodding to himself in appreciation of what his former life had acquired, he slides into the seat only to find a note taped to the steering wheel. Hello Salaok, I knew you wouldn’t be able to go inside the house yet, as you fear what you may uncover. Once you’ve started the car the navigation system will instruct you how to get to the GTT5 tournament and the DVD system has a file on Chris Collins who is your round one opponent. Your wrestling gear is in the trunk. Good Luck Salaok, From J.D. How this man was able to know the situation so well put a touch of fear into Salaok mind but this J.D. had helped him this far so he might as well trust him again. As the keys turn and the engine roared to life with what could only be the pure sound of a V8 engine, the journey was about to begin, only this time in a direction away from the house which holds all the keys.
