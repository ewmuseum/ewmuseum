Noah Hanson (WMW) #8 vs Ashe (MVW)

13 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
13 March 2004 [The scene opens with Noah walking down the street and turning a corner. He is dressed in a Chris Webber Sacramento Kings pullover and shorts and Nike Air Force Ones. Noah reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pair of Ray Ban sunglasses and puts them on the sunlight gleams off the sunglasses. Noah has a cocky look on his face as he confidently walks down the street of his home in Shaker Heights, Ohio. Noah stops at a newspaper stand and looks through all the available magazines and newspapers from across the nation. Noah looks them over and picks up Kansas City Star, Cleveland Plain Dealer and a Pro Wrestling Today. He hands the cashier a $50 and collects the change.] Cashier: Anything else Noah? Noah: Not today Ralph thanx…hey how are the wife and kids? Ralph: Good Noah thanx! So how did the ppv go last night? Noah you had a big match against Jackson Dre in a Hotel Hell match! I could not afford the ppv fee so I had to wait and see what the internet said but no one has reported on it yet? Noah: Well how bout I set ya up with a copy of the ppv on me? Ralph: Really? That would be sweet! Noah: Look I always have to look out for my old friends! And now I have some kinda special challenge match in the PTC Extreme Division. The guy is named Ashe or something. I mean I like a fight as much as anyone and this will be no different. He wants a Last Man Standing match something I am very unfamiliar with but hell I love hardcore and this is fine with me. I dunno where the match is gonna be hosted but all that matters is that this guy is gonna get his ass handed to him thanks to the “Instant Ratings Boost” Noah Hanson. Some lucky fed is gonna be graced with my appearance in their fed and that will mean a big ratings jump and an increase in ticket sales! [It was a hell of a match and of course I came out on top like everybody knew I would I mean really did anyone not expect me not to win? I am the biggest rising star in Wrestling Midwest and almost the man that no one wants to face. I am the hardest working man in Wrestling Midwest. But for this match I am gonna be a little out of my element as a face off against an unfamiliar opponent. Not much I can do scouting wise cause of him being a newcomer to MVW so there really is no sense in trying to get any advance scouting on this guy. The only thing I can do is train and prepare as best I can and do everything I know to come out on top and make sure I do not slip any further down the Extreme Division ladder. I mean I am not very high on the list so to be worried about losing is not really something that big a concern for me except for the fact that it could be kinda embarrassing to lose to some newbie punk that plays with Pikachu, Squirtle and Bulbasor all day!] [Noah continues walking and comes to a small café. He enters and takes a seat at a booth. A waitress comes to his table and takes his order…] Waitress: Hi what can I get you? Noah: A coffee, light sugar, light cream and a cream cheese Danish! Waitress: Ok I will be right back with your order. [Noah watches the young lady walk away and then picks up the Wrestling Magazine and flips through the pages. He comes across a headline “ Noah Hanson…next superstar or wannabe?”] [A wannabe? Me? Never I am the real deal and will prove that each and every time I get into a ring. I have successfully defended my title against two holders of MY Ace Superior title Jeff Hardkore and Jackson Dre and have beaten them convincingly. Hell I even put Dre on the shelf after the title defense at Ides of March 2. So what the hell do these mags say? Noah Hanson wannabe someone that is not gonna meet the expectations of not just what the fans expect of me but of what I expect of me. I always expect the most out of everything I do in not just in the ring but outside and this match with Ashe will be no exception. I have to push myself just as hard for this match as I have for any Wrestling Midwest match. I have never faced this guy or even heard of this guy but rested assured this guy will know well who I am cause I am gonna brand him like a steer at this show. Hell I may bring a branding iron with me and use it during the match!] [The waitress returns with Noah’s order] Waitress: Anything else? Noah: No I think I am ok for now thanx! Waitress: Ok well just give me a yell if ya need anything? [Noah nods] [I needed to just get some MY time a little bit of alone time where all I did was just relax and not think of anything but me. But As I looked out the window I saw a familiar face and knew that today that would not happen. I was hoping that she would not be coming here cause she knew I liked to come but she was making here way towards the café and with a purpose in her step. So I kinda figured there was only one place she could possibly be heading and that was to find me.] [The door of the café opens and there is Noah’s old flame Tina she is dressed in a very revealing dress and heels. She is also carrying her purse and has her hair done up very seductively. I tried not to notice but it was hard seeing as our past history would suggest that I have a lot of interest in her. She strutted across the floor with the confidence of a runway model. I waited to see if a spotlight would suddenly appear so she could do her turn on the runway but it never appeared. As she made her way towards me and older man stared at her chest wit a vast blankness on his face until his wife smacked him in the face. Tina smiled, she walked by. A busboy that was clearing his table dropped his cart and dishes shattered all over the floor. I simply smiled as she approached my table and sat down across from me and then placed a small tape recorder in front of me. I looked at the recorder and reached for it and turned it off. She looked at me almost pouting!] Tina: Now why did ya go and do that for? [Noah takes a drink of coffee] Noah: I know why you’re here! [Tina smiles] Tina: What can’t an old flame come to see you and have a cup of coffee? [Noah looks oddly at Tina] Noah: That would be fine if that was all it was about! But you’re here with this… [Picks up the recorder]...so I know it is not about Just seeing me! You are here for your wrestling site! Tina: Look I will admit that it is about business but it can be about more you know. [She began sliding he foot up my thigh and although it brought back a lot of memories I could let it cloud my judgment. I pushed her foot away. She sighed deeply as the waitress approached.] Waitress: Can I get anything for you miss? Tina: Just a hot chocolate! [The waitress leaves] Noah: Some things never change! Tina: Always go with what feels right! Ya know you was what felt right for along time Noah! [She was right we had a lot of good times I mean I really thought she was the one. Bu things happen for a reason sometimes it is fate or at least luck but in any case we ended up going our separate ways. I always thought of what might have been with Tina but after it was all said and done I ended up marrying a wonderful and beautiful women, my wife Karen and that is something that I will never regret doing.] Noah: Ok so you’re here for an interview…fine let’s get this over with I wanted a nice relaxing day but I can see that like you and me a relaxing day off is not meant to be! [Tina grabs the cassette player and turns it on.] Tina: Great! Ok well what are your thoughts after such a tough, hard fought match at Ides of March 2. You beat your toughest opponent in Jackson Dre? Hell you even broke his arm when you threw him out the window from the second story. [Noah takes a drink of his coffee] Noah: Look it was simply a miscommunication I mean really I tried to get him from jumping but he knew that the only way to escape from getting his ass kicked anymore was to run away and hide as fast and as far as he could. Is it my fault that he jumped from a second story window? No I just wished he had done it from a higher story. But either way I am man that got rid of my stiffest competition so I got it made easy! Tina: So what are you looking for as far as future challengers? I mean your division is pretty bare! Noah: Yeah I guess it is let’s see who is left? Dre? No injured broken arm out for at least a month. Jeff Hardkore? Possibly but he is like the old man that is sitting in that corner over there. He is simply old and busted and not worthy of my time and talent in the ring! Tina: What about former champ Epitome? He is one of the former champs you have failed to mention are you afraid of him or something? [Noah nearly chokes on the question!] Noah: Are you insane? Afraid of Epitome you have got to be joking I mean Epitome is the past of the Ace Superior title I am the future of the title! [The waitress returns with Tina hot cocoa.] Tina: Thank you! [The waitress places the cup down and walks away.] Tina: Now then what about this challenge match and some wrestler from Maximum Velocity Wrestling run by Taj Escobar? Noah: Yeah I know who runs that joint! He was employed by WMW for a while. And hell I tried out for MVW but things did not work out there so I left I mean I have the highest regards for MVW and a lot of respect for the talent over there! But as far as this Ashe guy goes well I dunno this guy is someone I have never even heard of until now. But he comes in here and decides he wants a match with me well fine I am game and really have nothing to lose. Being 8th on the rankings ladder just means that there is realistically not very far to fall if I lose. Tina: But it is a very dangerous Last Man Standing match that kinda match means that you have to be down for a 10 count that means you could be seriously injured! I have seen you fight before and I know your penchant for putting your body on the line and I am very sure that you are gonna open up the play book and do whatever you gotta do to beat this clown. [Well she was right I will do whatever it takes cause even though I am only so far up on the ladder does not mean by anyone’s imagination that I will simply allow some new talent hack leapfrog over me. Not gonna happen I got news for ya junior It is not gonna be that easy! You see I have fought, clawed and bled to get where I am now so you want this spot your gonna have to knock me from plain and simple.] Noah: Look do we always have to talk shop every time you come to see me I mean really we were such a hot couple but now all you want is the next big story or what the “great” Noah Hanson is gonna do next. I am not the next big story all I am a wrestler trying to do the best he can nothing more nothing less! Tina: So what is that it ends of interview!? [Noah drinks the last of his coffee and slams the cup on the table shattering it. A few of the people look at Noah then turn away.] Noah: Yes the interview is over and if you wanna talk to me again set it up with my press agent! [Noah grabs his food and stuffs it into his mouth and throws a twenty on the table.] Noah: Mmprkgf women!! [Noah storms out of the café as the scene fade.] *end scene* ---- 14 March 2004 Sometimes life is unfair whether you are dealt a bad hand by being financially strapped, or not being the most attractive or maybe not the best dancer or even the most charismatic. Life is not always fair in those respects cause there is only one person in wrestling that has all those tools and that person is the “ Hardcore Icon” Noah Hanson. The man every woman fantasizes about and the one man every other man fears and wishes he could be…. [The scene opens with Noah sitting in his leather chair in his study. The Wrestling Midwest Ace Superior title is draped over another chair in the room and Noah is seen looking at his computer screen.] [Man this Ashe has a lot of guts...not a lot of brains but a lot of guts. I mean this guy wants to come out here and throw down a challenge to me the Wrestling Midwest Ace Superior Champion. I have just come off of one of my biggest title defenses to date when I threw Jackson Dre from the second story window and walked out of the Hotel Hell match with my title around my waist. I have faced off with legends and icons in the squared circle hell I have even been in that ring that Ashe now calls home hell in my first match I beat hardcore legend MotoPyscho in my debut match and as far as badasses go Moto is at the top of the list. So Ashe all I got to say is a Last Man Standing match means that all I need to do is beat you totally senseless and sit back and wait for you to try and answer the call!] [Noah looks through a few emails] [Seems that this Ashe has bitten off a little more than he can chew. He has opened himself up to a world of pain and suffering. [Chuckles confidently] You see Ashe a Last Man Standing match is basically just a hardcore match and all you have to do is answer a ten count. A simple little ten count really not a very hard task not very hard at all. But the task is much harder when you have been beaten senseless with a set of brass knuckles or maybe have your head bashed in with a steel chair. This is not a career defining moment Ashe at least not for me it may be for you it may be something that you can dust off in say five years and go to your grandson “ I wrestled Noah Hanson in a Last Man Standing Match and got my ass handed to me. He kicked my ass so bad and so hard that I decided after the match that I was in the wrong profession. After I lost I decided to go back to the job I held before wrestling and that is a professional pooper scooper!”] [Noah gets up from the desk and slowly walks around to a set of pictures and title belts that are trophies on his wall. He comes to the WMW tag team title and polishes it with his shirt sleeve.] [You see this belt Johnny Blayze and I ruled the tag scene while we held these belts. We cut every corner, broke every rule and did everything we could possibly do to keep those belts. We fought in one of the most brutal matches called Hardkore Hell and even though we lost the belts then we cemented the fact The Dream Team is in fact one of the greatest tag teams ever. And I am one of the most decorated tag team wrestlers in history.] [Noah walks over to a picture of Noah getting speared off a ladder by Jeff Hardkore.] [You see this was my very first singles title match I was in Ladder match against Jeff Hardkore for the WMW Hardcore now Shoot title. I was pretty nervous for this match pretty much the way Ashe is for this match with me and not that I cannot blame him hell I am nervous about this match cause I wanna put on another good showing for all my fans out there.] [Noah walks over to a picture of Noah standing with the Ace Superior and Shoot titles over his head.] [You see this was the proudest moment of my career I had defeated 3 of the best wrestlers in Wrestling Midwest in a fatal four way and walked out as a double champ the first in WMW history! I look back on that moment and I have to say that it was then that I realized that I truly have what it takes to go very long way in this business.] [Noah hears a knock at his door and gets up to see who is there. He opens the door and there is his trainer Shadow and sister Kasandra.] Shadow: What the hell ya looking at whitey? You knew we was comin over and ya have a look on yer face liked you were just circle jerked! [Noah looks a bit irritated at Shadow] Noah: Do you always have to be so freakin angry about stuff! Shadow: I am not angry just sexually frustrated! Do you know how hard it is to find lesbians that are goth I mean come I do not live in San Francisco where that kinda stuff is the norm! This is the wholesome as white bread Midwest where the strangest thing ya might see is straight sex upside down! Kasandra: You’re a lesbian? I had no idea but hell you’re hot for a vampire! Shadow: Hey bimbo there is a difference between and I am not a freakin vampire ya freak! Hey speakin of freaks where is your little boyfriend Romeo at? Is he a little worried about showing up at Noah’s house? Noah: I do not see Romeo being afraid of much Shadow! I know Romeo better than most people. The Dream Teams’ battles with The Underdogs were an intense battle and even though me and Justin Evers were never able to get the upper hand on them I learned a lot from that feud with the former tag champs and that is expect the unexpected and with Ashe I really believe that will hold true! Kasandra: Do you know anything about this guy? Anything you can use to your advantage? [Noah shakes his head no] Noah: Not really I think he even wears a mask to cover up the fact that there is an insecure, pathetic little man behind that mask! Other than that I really do not know much beside the fact that he works for Taj Escobar and Maximum Velocity Wrestling. And yes that is the same place I worked for briefly before ya ask me about that! Kasandra: You worked for MVW? Noah: Yeah I decided to moonlight for them. I mean things never really worked out there and I left after like a month or so! I mean there was no hard feelings between me and Taj just never really got into a very good groove and decided to leave before things got to complicated! [I really liked working for MVW but the fed just never really clicked ya know! I mean I won my debut but after that it all went down hill. I loved the place but sometimes things just are not meant to be! I mean I wish every place I ever wrestled. I really believed in putting my heart and soul into every fed I have ever been in but not every fed turns out like my run in Wrestling Midwest and get some of the lucky breaks that have come my way since I started working there! I have been graced with a lot of luck and god willing I will have some more luck in the next few week and months ahead as I feel that this summer will be the biggest summer in my career. I feel that things of biblical proportions are gonna happen. Kinda like the skies raining frogs, the seas turning red and animals standing on two legs and walking upright that kinda magical! Ya know of biblical proportions the kind that Moses will stand and go “holy Crap!”] Kasandra: So then you know Ashe right? Noah: No not really he is just some newbie punk that wants attention and thinks that by going after one of the big dogs in the yard he can garner some kinda hardcore reputation. His mistake is for stepping into my world and thinking that there would no retribution for going somewhere that he has business going. He stepped into my world and after our match he will lucky to remember his freakin name although all he will have to do is watch Pokemon on Saturday morning and he will hear his name loud and proud Ashe Ketchum! Shadow: Well I know that you have been training but you still look in bad shape after your match with Jackson. Are you in any shape to be accepting challenges like this? Noah: Come on really? [smirks] I am more than ready my ribs are still taped but other than that I am fine! Besides I am not gonna need to be 100% for this match I could beat him with half a tank and one wheel. But unluckily for him I am nearly fully charged and in charge of this match! And this will be a fine match and an instant ratings bonanza for MVW I will instantly bring their shows ratings to newer and better heights! Hell I might even show up there a little early and see the sites it has been a while since I have seen the inside of an MVW ring and I might wanna test the ring and see what I might need to improvise during the match cause not every ring is the same and not every fed sets up there rings the same way! [Kasandra goes over to the fridge and grabs a couple of bottles of water and hands on to Shadow.] Shadow: Thanx but Noah have you been training I mean in ring training? Noah: Oh come on training smaining I got all covered this guy is gotta it coming and he has no idea just who he is dealing with I will take him to the freakin cleaners! Kasandra: God I hate it when you talk like you’re full of shit! You have won a few matches not been salted King of Hardcore! [Walks out and goes into his study and comes out with a box.] Noah: You think that huh well wait to you see this! [Noah opens the box and pulls out a crown with an inscription that reads “King of Hardcore”] Noah: See I am too King of Hardcore! So there! [Noah walks off into his bedroom pouting] Noah: No one ever thinks I am hardcore I always have to prove myself over and over again and it is fuckin ridiculous I hate it I hate it I…HATE ….IT!!!! Shadow: Just what the hell are you doing? Are you gonna flake out on us! Noah: I am not flakin out god dammit! Geez I hate that too. I am hardcore always have been always will be and goddammit as god as my witness this match with Ashe is gonna be the way I show everyone that I am Hard freakin core and after I deal with Ashe I will cement my legacy as the true hardcore icon no strike that a hardcore legend!! Mike Marvelous the champ is a scam, a fake I am a true hardcore legend I deserve that belt I live for hardcore. I live, breath, eat and sleep hardcore just having the belt in your possession does not a worthy title holder make! So to make things short, sweet and to the point Ashe you are just a speed bump on the way to my destiny! My destiny is the Extreme title and the titles destiny is with me plain and simple! And Ashe will be done and dead and buried! Sorry bout that but I just needed to get that outta my system! [Kasandra looks oddly at her brother] Kasandra: Not that I haven’t heard some odd ramblings from you before but damn you hardly know this Ashe guy! Noah: And that is where it all starts some newcomer thinks he can just waltz into MY territory and throw down a challenge to me? [shakes head] Not happening just not happening I will conquer this newcomer and show everyone why I am the real thing! [the scene ends with another knock on his door and Noah answering it] *end feed* Noah’s movie quote of the day From Charlie’s Angels [Dylan looks over all the fallen bodies] Dylan: And that’s me kickin your ass! [Dylan moonwalks out of the room to the theme of Beat It from Michael Jackson}
