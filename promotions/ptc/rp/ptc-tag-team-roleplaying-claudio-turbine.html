An underdog.. That’s what you’d call me... the nerve. Just because I’m new to this little part of the game, doesn’t mean I’m new to the business. Hell, One has caused hell... one has caused pleasure, pain, sweat and tears and you will know that. Make way for the underdog. Make way for the new name to come through like a jet plane... these other guys, yeah... they might mean something where they come from, but they mean nothing to me... just...with regret for stating the obvious... stepping stones for one’s ladder to victory... you shall see... and after everything is said and done you’ll remember me not as the underdog, but as the Reflection... of Perfection... A little Introduction.... [Scene begins with the light shining on something, a piece of gold... the camera slowly pans out revealing it to be one half of the East Wind Championship belts from the 4WW... it’s draped over Claudio Turbine’s shoulder as he looks at it and then at the camera smirking before flashing a million dollar smile. He pats the belt before taking it off and placing it down on an end table...] ][Claudio Turbine][ I know what you’re all thinking...”Who is that?!”... Well, I’ll tell you... The names Claudio Turbine, but you can call me the Reflection of Perfection. I hail from the 4WW, and I’m part of a dominant stable known as the DMC... I feel you know enough now... So go ahead my friends refer to me as the underdog, don’t put your faith in me, don’t think I have what it takes to climb the PTC ladder? Well, then take a look at what sits on this table... I’m Co-Holder of the East Wind Champions... though this may cause some conflict since the people we beat to win these titles are the same people that I’ll be teaming up with to face all the other punks in this match. Ukyo Kushinaga, such a beauty wouldn’t you agree? What makes her more precious is the fact I’ve plowed her head through a table on more then one occasion, but... that’s in the past isn’t it Ukyo? I mean... a woman like yourself can put this behind you and try your best to co-exist with myself... Any women in this damn world would love to be in the position you are right now. To call themself the partner of Claudio Turbine... You better not let me down my love... or things you’ve experienced thus far will seem like a day at Chuckie fucking cheese! [Claudio picks up his championship title and walks off....] Displaying your Talent... [Scene opens in a training gym... Claudio is in the ring with an unnamed man. The two lock up in a test of strength with the other man getting the best of Claudio. The man tosses Claudio into the ropes and goes for a clothesline, but Claudio ducks underneath the attack and hits the man with a standing drop kick directly to his face. The man hit’s the canvas hard. Claudio gets up and lifts the man up in the process tossing him into the turnbuckle post. Claudio runs and hits the man with a huge body splash. The man stumbles from the corner and Claudio leaps from behind him hitting him with his signature move the 21:13 (John Cena’s Throwback). Claudio then leaps to the top ropes and hits the man with the “Everything Evil” Shooting Star Press... when all of a sudden a pair of hands can be heard clapping together... the camera pans around showing two men... “The Archangel” Lucifer Priest (one half of the East Wind Champs) and “The Dream Maker” Eddie B. Eddie B. continues to clap his hands together as Claudio looks out of the ring at them.....] ][Eddie B.][ Excellent display of talent there my friend... [Eddie B. and Lucifer enter the ring as Claudio gets up from it...] ][Eddie B.][ But do keep in mind your opponents ain’t some jobber like this guy is... they’re top talent from other federations... it’s gonna be something unlike you’ve ever experienced, but I guess not having faith in you would be a waste of time considering you and Lucifer pulled off the ultimate victory the other night beating Future Shock and ending their reign as champions... [Claudio smirks as Lucifer nods his head....] ][Eddie B.][ I have faith in you. I know you and Lucifer can take the PTC world by storm... they know nothing of us yet, but they will won’t they? ][Claudio Turbine][ Yes my lord... they will know.. ][Eddie B.][ Excellent... [Eddie B. smirks as the amulet on his chest pulsates giving off a sort of glow before the scene slowly fades out...] Getting a glimpse of the past [We open up on the outside of a little french café. Claudio is shown sitting at a circular shaped table with a beautiful young lady... between them sits a large drink with two straws inside of it, they both take sips from the cup staring into each other’s eyes when Claudio pulls away. Some whipped cream sticks to his nose and the girl giggles leaning towards him and licking it off. Claudio turns his head to the side somewhat embarrassed only causing the girl to giggle even more.] ][Amy][ You’re too cute... ][Claudio Turbine][ Eh... I don’t think so.. ][Amy][ Good thing too... all these comments I’m feeding you will make your head explode... ][Claudio Turbine][ I won’t let my ego get the best of me.. [Claudio smiles and Amy leans to him kissing him softly..] ][Claudio Turbine][ I love you … ][Amy][ I love you too… [Smoke filles the scene and the camera pans around Claudio and Amy at a fast rate before Amy vanishes and Claudio appears in another location... he looks around and he’s standing in his nightmare from the other night...] ][Claudio Turbine][ Watch this... come here boy. [Claudio stares at himself and the girl as the Dream state Claudio whistles and a little pomeranian comes running out and Claudio leans down putting his arms out and the dog leaps into them. Claudio grabs the dog by the collar pulling something out from it. Claudio places the dog back down and he runs off as Claudio drops to one knee opening the thing he took from the dog revealing it to be a small ring...] ][Claudio Turbine][ I know it’s not much, but it’s something... The ring is small, but we’ll come into money someday my luv’... I love you more and more with each passing day. There’s no one in this world I’d rather spend my life then you. Ever since the day we met I knew I loved you. I feel as if I loved you before I met you that’s how deep this love goes... And I don’t want this feeling to ever stop... it’s like your eyes tell the stories of the day you wish you could bring back the moment that once have... I don’t know what I’m saying, but every time we’re together it just feels right... I want to spend every waking moment and every one asleep with you... you are my better half and there’s nothing I wouldn’t do for you...will you marry me? [Amy places her hand over her mouth tears forming in her eyes..] ][Claudio Turbine][ ... now would be the time you answer.. [Claudio nervously chuckles..] ][Amy][ Yes I will mar— [Before Amy can finish a pair of hands come into the scene and wrap around her head snapping her neck like a twig] ][Real Claudio Turibine][ NOOO!! [The Real Claudio lunges going straight through Amy and the Dream Maker as if he were a ghost... Amy hits the ground dead as Claudio’s eyes go wide with horror as he stands and takes a few steps back. The camera does a 180 showing the Dream Maker standing by Amy’s fallen body. Claudio stares at the Dream Maker with pure hatred, the love in his eyes now has left and probably will never return..] ][Claudio Turbine][ YOU MONSTER I’LL KILL YOU!! [Claudio charges at The Dream Maker swinging, but before he connects the Dream Maker’s body turns into smoke and Claudio falls onto the floor. He gets up and the Dream Maker grabs him by his shoulder pulling him forward onto a knife. Claudio gasps for air grabbing at the knife as blood comes out the side of his mouth. The Dream Maker smirks evilly at Claudio before bringing his foot up and kicking Claudio off of the knife. Claudio hits the ground hard and lays there by his lover’s side... when all of a sudden he sits up quickly and the background scenery changes to that of his bedroom....the same reoccurring nightmare...] ][Claudio Turbine][ Fuck... [The scene fades to black...] Knowing Your Opponents... [The scene opens back up on Claudio Turbine standing alone inside of a cemetery. He exhales sharply his breath flying in the wind as he does so.] ][Claudio Turbine][ I have a chance to make the people in the PTC know my name... I have the chance of a lifetime, me and Lucifer were had chosen from the 4ww to represent the 4ww along with Future Shock... and you know what? I’m not as confident as I make myself out to be... in fact I’m some what scared. I have no idea who any of my opponents are, what they bring to the table and what they can do to me... But I do hang my head high in pride... because I know what I can bring to the table... I know my skills and a lot of people do as well. So in a way I’m not as scared as I said I was, I’m sure you understand all too well what I mean. I don’t plan on losing, but shit happens. Claudio Turbine isn’t here to win... he’s here... to get noticed... [Claudio sighs once more and walks off as the scene fades for the last time.]
