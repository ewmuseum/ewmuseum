Love of the Moloko

17 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-17 Let me tell you a story. This story isn't about a great leader, but rather, just an ordinary man. This ordinary man's name is Samuel Francis Wolack. You see, young Sam wasn't some hotshot banker, or even a politician. Young Sam was a professional wrestler. Like many, professional wrestlers, he strived to be at the top of his profession. He looked to win GTT5. How did he look to do this? By simply working to be the best. "Yes, I hear what you're saying." Young Sam said while on the phone. Apparently, the CIA had just located his kidnapped friend, one Andrea Scabbia. But this wasn't completely on Young Sam's mind. The main thing on Sam's mind was how to be the best. Whether it was chemical enhancement through HGH or supplements to new styles of wrestling being created, Sam studied whether or not they could make him even more effective as a wrestler. But everything that he looked at in becoming the best has pointed him away from these chemical enhancements and learning new moves that would clash with his chosen style. So there Sam is. Sitting at a Washington DC diner, drinking a glass of milk. It was a similar situation, years ago in Japan when he came to the realization on what he would have to do to make it to the top. Unfortunately, that night he was involved in a severe car accident that caused him to not remember anything from that day. So there Sam is trying to remember what it was that would make him the best there is. He does this night, after night, after night. His dreams are about being the best. His thoughts are about being the best. For Sam Wolack, being the best is all that there is. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- All of a sudden, Sam Wolack sits up. He rolls out of his bed at his hotel room and walks towards the mini-bar. He opens the door and pulls out a carton of milk. He opens it and starts to drink. Some of the milk starts to dribble down the side of his face. Sam then picks up the remote to turn the hotel TV on. ‘The life of a pro wrestler. What a weird fucking dream.’ Sam thinks. Sam starts to flip through the channels and stops at one of the many cable news channels. "FBI officials have identified the body of a young lady found in San Francisco earlier today." The anchor said. "Fingerprints from the body have revealed that the victim is Andrea Scabbia, a Los Angeles based actress who was last seen in Scottsdale, Arizona, who was visiting a friend, Sam Wolack, a professional wrestler with the Global Championship Wrestling promotion." Just that moment, Sam’s cell phone rang. He hesitantly answered it and put it on speaker. "Hello." "Samuel Wolack, I’m Agent Dunbar of the Federal Bureau of Investigation. I’m just calling to inform you that we recovered the body of Andrea Scabbia." Sam dropped his cell phone to the floor, effectively breaking it. Next he dropped his milk, spilling it all over the pieces of the cell phone. Sam just stood there, looking at the TV. Sam walks out the door of the hotel room, down the hall and to an elevator. He takes the elevator down to the lobby of the hotel and leaves the hotel. He walks for a while before coming to a bar with a retro look to it. The sign above the door says Korova Moloko. Sam enters and sits at the bar. "What will it be?" asked a rather surly looking bartender. "Whatever you have on tap." The bartender sets a glass in front of Sam Wolack and starts to fill it with a milky substance from the tap. Sam takes a drink from the glass. "What is this?" "Moloko-Plus." Answered the bartender. "It’s good." Sam continued to drink this milky-white concoction as another young man sat down next to him. "Welly, welly, welly, welly, welly, welly, well. To what do I owe the extreme pleasure of this surprising visit?" Sam turned to the other young man. He took a look at him with a bit of confusion in his eyes. The fake lashes on his right eye, the white suspenders and the black bowler hat. Sam quickly got any idea about this unusual character out of his head quickly. "I’ve had a long day. I would like it if you just left. Are we clear?" "As an unmuddled lake. As clear as an azure sky of deepest summer. You can rely on me, Sam." And with that, the young man left. "Strange one, that was. Don’t you think, barkeep?" "I wouldn’t know. He and his droogs often come in here for the Moloko-Plus." "What’s in it?" "If I told you, then you’d go home and make it yourself. Just think of it this way, it’s going to make you sharp. Forget all about that pretty girl of yours and make you think of making yourself the best. After all, Mr. Wolack, isn’t that what you want to be?" "I guess." "You shouldn’t guess, Mr. Wolack. It doesn’t become you. You should know." -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- Once again, Sam sat right up. Lying down next to him is Andrea Scabbia. Sam looks over to see her there asleep as he gets out of the bed, trying not to disturb her. The screen on his TV is showing the DVD menu for "A Clockwork Orange". Sam walks over to the mini-bar and opens it. First he reaches for the carton of milk, but stops. He takes a quick look again at the bed to see Andrea still sleeping there. Sam heads back over to the bed and gives Andrea a kiss on the forehead before grabbing a pair of jeans and a T-Shirt out of the dresser drawer. He then puts them on. Then he grabs a pair of tennis shoes from the foot of the bed and puts them on. "That was a weird fucking dream." Sam says to himself under his breath as he turns off the TV. "Sam, where are you going?" Andrea says as she turns towards him. "I’ll be right back, I just need to get some air." Sam answered as he stands up. He heads towards the door and leaves the room.
