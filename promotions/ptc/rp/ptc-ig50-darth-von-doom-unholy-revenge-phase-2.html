Unholy Revenge, Phase 2 - Propoganda and Project: Apocalypse (IG50 Promo #2 of 5) Title: Unholy Revenge, Phase 2 - Propaganda and Project: Apocalypse (IG50 Promo #2 of 5) Word Count: 1000 Exactly Wrestler: Darth von Doom Inside the Horny Mathematician’s laboratory in the Castle of Blood, the Church of the Unholy’s leadership council consisting of vampiric physician Dr. Acula, the werewolf Wolfen, the Horny Mathematician, and the mass murdering psychopath C.G. Murder are being shown the newest evil instruments of destruction by one Darth von Doom. After being shown the Evil Bong and the Zombie Robots, the Church leadership is in awe. Darth von Doom sets the Evil Bong on the table where he grabbed it from earlier Wolfen: *snarl* What do we need an Evil Bong for? Darth: We’re going to make them get high to the point where we scare the shit out of them using some of our humanoid sub creatures. The Mathematician giggles loudly. Mathematician: I remember getting high in college. These chicks, when they get high, just wanted to fuck all day long in my laboratory. C.G. Murder looks confused. C.G. Murder: You had sex in the bathroom? Mathematician: No you idiot! I had sex in my actual laboratory. We knocked over some beakers and created the Blob. Dr. Acula: You created that Blob that killed all of those people? Mathematician: Yes I did. I’m so proud. It was made from my own semen. Darth looks like he wants to kill him at this point. Darth: Enough about the semen blob or whatever it is you guys are talking about. We need to focus on the Infinite Gauntlet 50 should be our main focus. If we want people to fear us, then we had better make them fear us. And the only way to do this is to unleash the Zombie Robots at just the right time and then make them high from the Evil Bong before they get sucked into the Bong World. Wolfen: *growl* What’s a Bong World? Darth: It’s the evil strip club of death located within the Evil Bong. Mathematician: I’ve been there. This chick’s bra tried to eat my cock. C.G. Murder: What happened then? Mathematician: Tommy Chong got high and helped me escape before trying to blow up the Bong World with a timed explosive device. C.G. Murder: Explosions aren’t as fun as…murdering people. Darth: Anyway, I feel that this plan will work. Dr. Acula: I feel it will work as well. We should go through with it. However, we must start the first phase of this before we unleash any of these horrors upon the masses. Darth nods his head in agreement. Darth: Good plan. C.G. Murder: What are you two clamoring about? Wolfen: *growl* How come you two didn’t tell us about this? Darth: Sorry but this was top secret. Above top secret actually so we went ahead with it without your consent. Dr. Acula: This will be our first message to the outside. Our attentions must be known to the American people so that our actions are clear. Wolfen: *growl* What message are you talking about? Dr. Acula grins deviously. Dr. Acula: Something that will get the people talking. * * * The next morning on the local television station, a new ad comes on during the commercials of Barney and Pals… An ad that most people would think was for the Presidential race begins showing. However, this ad is different. For one there is peaceful Zen style classical music playing with little fluffy animals jumping around in a meadow followed by Darth von Doom appearing on the screen. Darth: Hello America. Remember me? I’m sure you do after the Russian Dictatorship aligned with my Ninjas of Doom tried to take over the world two years ago. But since then my organization has been doing all it ca to help bring peace and prosperity to all nations of the world. Images of spring flowers blooming and children holding hands are shown followed by Dr. Acula appearing on the screen. Dr. Acula: We here at the Church of the Unholy have reformed our ways. In the past we have been labeled as a terrorist organization but now we only want to bring you peace. Darth: And to have peace you have to join us, by force if necessary. Dr. Acula: The choice is up to you. You have one day to join us or face the consequences of your poor decision making. We leave the decision up to you and look forward to your answer. Darth: Choose…choose wisely. The ad is ended with the statement “This ad has been paid for by the Church of the Unholy” and the ad quickly fades to black. * * * The next day… In the dining hall located within the Castle of Blood, Darth von Doom and Dr. Acula are sitting down at the dining table looking at the New York Times and Los Angeles Times newspapers. Darth: What are they saying about our advertisement? Dr. Acula: They think that it’s all a ploy. Darth: What do you mean? Dr. Acula: They think that we’re only out to create war, not peace. Dr. Acula motions to Darth’s newspaper. Dr. Acula: What’s yours saying? Darth: They bought it. Well, the writer of the news report on it bought it and thinks the government should comply. Dr. Acula smiles deviously. Dr. Acula: How much longer do they have until time runs out? Darth: Time ran out two hours ago. They’re not going for it. Dr. Acula: So we go through with the plan? Darth: Yes. We have to show them we mean business. They want peace so we’ll give them peace. No more nations, only one Church to rule them all. One Nation of the Unholy. The Horny Mathematician walks in with a grin on his face. Dr. Acula: How are things going with Project: Apocalypse? Mathematician: Things are going extremely well. Two of the Horsemen have been created thus far. Dr. Acula: Good. Darth: I can provide a third Horseman once the invasion begins. Mathematician: How so? Darth: I will personally lead the attack against the Fishermen’s home city and capture one of them. They all smile deviously. (To be continued)
