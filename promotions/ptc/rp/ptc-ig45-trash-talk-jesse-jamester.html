IG45 Trash Talk Open Invitation thread (all vs Chris Storm)

5 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
5 June 2008 A hoarse crackling of the throat interrupts a line of people as a man in the back with long brown hair and bright blue eyes raises his hand. "Excuse me! Did I hear you properly? Did you just say... Chris Storm?" the man says as he scratches his scruffy beard and looks at the people in line for this autograph signing. Stepping out of line the man looks up at the people and begins to laugh so hard he buckles over a bit before he starts coughing. Pounding his chest with a closed fist he spits up a wad of mucus on the sidewalk and looks at it. "That right there..." as he points at the mucus on the sidewalk, "is better than Chris Storm." The fans in line boo him and some even start to throw empty pop-bottles and batteries. Juking and jiving to the other side of the street the man looks over at them and points to his shirt which says 'E-P-DUBYA' with the initials meanings underneath it 'Evolution Pro Wrestling'. "The name is Jesse Jamester, and if you find this crock of a champ entertaining then you'll think I'm like the GOD of wrestling! At least thats what Tessa said... uh, forget what I just said," claims Jesse as he almost slips up on something that may back fire from this mob of Storm fans. Walking up the concrete sidewalk so that he's right across from the entrance to where Storm is signing autographs, Jesse smiles and looks around. "I have an idea!" he mutters to himself. Running down the block he disappears beyond the crowd of mingling bystanders. A few minutes later Jesse is sprinting down the side walk with a briefcase, which happens to look very odd in his hands due to his shagginess. Stopping at the back of a car Jesse opens the briefcase up and pulls out a load of papers and a black sharpie. "FREE AUTOGRAPHS! Yes folks thats right, the one and only, the guy who beat Chris Storm for the Prime Time Central GLOBAL Championship giving away free autographs! Step into my space and you will be dazzled by my generosity!" hollers the Nemesis Warrior to the crowd of people. At first the line is a little shaken, the Storm fans seem content to stay in there places but then again the word 'Free' has some people thinking twice. "What's the price over there for an autograph huh?" Jesse asks with a yell across the street at some large obese man wearing a shirt that makes every roll on his chest, stomach, and hips look a hundred folds bigger. The man looks from side-to-side and then behind him, finally he points a finger at himself and looks back at Jesse. "Yes I'm talking to you oh-behemoth one," states the Jackal with a little sarcasm in his voice. The man yells back, "Five dollars!" and Jesse nods before giving a thumbs up and saying 'Thanks' back to him. "I'm not going to say it again... Actually I will, FREE AUTOGRAPHS from the only man to beat Chris Storm when it mattered!" yells Jesse as he looks around. From behind a few women come up to the Nemesis Warrior and look at him with devious eyes masked in mascara and eye liner. A few of them look drunk, then again, who cares. "I know you all want an autograph, right ladies?" says Jesse in a more friendly tone as he approaches the ladies and outstretches his hand to shake the middle woman's hand. She complies and puts it out but then is taken back when he lifts her hand to his lips and kisses the back of it like a scene right out of Romeo and Juliet. "You won't get class with a man like Chris Storm, he's more known for fart-jokes and calling himself the greatest thing in the world. Nah, he's the kind of guy you hate once you start dating because he spends half his time in front of a mirror and the other half spending too much time on his hair. Now does that sound like a guy for you Miss?" The woman blushes as he lowers her hand and oddly enough she responds without a scent of alcohol on her breath. "Not at all, uh..." "Call me Jesse," as he puts his arm over her shoulder and starts to walk down the sidewalk. Looking over across the street he says,"Storm, when you get some balls come look for me over at E-P-Dubya. I'll take you on any time, any place, under any rules. That is... if your MAN ENOUGH!" Jesse and the broad walk off down the street sidewalk chit-chatting away. The two females that were with the woman pick up the briefcase Jesse left and find hundreds of copies of Jesse Jamester with the PTC Global championship hanging over his shoulder. ---- 6 June 2008 Move aside you peasants, I wasn't speaking to you. Did I mention any one of your names? Uh... no, so shut your mouth and step in line for your turn to speak! See I believe and I know I'm not mistaken, but theres a man named Chris Storm who has been dodging my bullet since he lost his first Global Title here in Prime Time Central to me. Now some would say that I'm over exaggerating the truth, giving a little sugar coating to the story, but fact is he runs and hides like he's the bitch and Tessa's the butch of their relationship. When are you going to step up and take on your challenges Chris? Ah... I get it, you know you can't win, easy enough. Hell, even if you don't want to take me on in the Gauntlet, I'll be waiting for when your done with whoever. This isn't over. Not by a long shot! No matter where you go, where you hide, what you do, I'm looking over your shoulder, watching your every move. Take a moment to take that in, because the KING of Wrestling has returned to the squared-circle. Let the pawns gawk and squawk about how they deserve a shot. The truth is you want to be on top, so then prove your worthy! Since I came back I haven't seen a drop of impressiveness roll off that pretty dome of yours. I've seen monkeys wrestle better than you Chris, remember UWF, yeah, MONKEYS! One more time, when your ready, I'll be waiting. Round two; do you have what it takes? I'll understand if not... I already figured you would bitch-out. ---- 7 June 2008 Angelo Deville has never been in the ring with me. Chryste has never been in the ring with me, Seymour Almasy lost to me in GLOBAL Wrestling. You can keep wimping out by saying I'm not a threat but you still lost to me and you've never faced me since. I don't give a fuck who you faced in all those other federations that mean shit to me. Oh and Union Jack, who the fuck is that? If he wants to he can take me on for the gimmick he wants back. Regardless he won't get it, because I'm not playing a gimmick, I'm just stating the truth. Storm, just say yes, you know you want to. Come on... what other challengers here have anything to prove? They're all leagues below us while we're sitting pretty on the top. Regardless I will get a chance at you, and when I do... I'll humiliate you like I did before.
