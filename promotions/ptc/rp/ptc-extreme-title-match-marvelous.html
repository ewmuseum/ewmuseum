1 April 2004 The private jet of Mike Marvelous makes a safe landing in a small airport in the Irish countryside. Once the jet comes to a stop a small man runs up to it while pushing a step ladder, he props the ladder against the plane just as the cabin door opens and out steps Mike Marvelous himself, followed closely by his manager William Bastafar. Mike wears blue jeans and a black leather jacket along with dark sunglasses that look out of place in the Irish countryside. William wears gray slacks and he is mostly covered by a long black trench coat. Both men carry suitcases in one hand and they don’t even bother to acknowledge the airport worker as they make their way towards the airport. “What an ugly country,” Mike exclaims with disgust to William as they stroll from the landing strip to the small building that somehow passes as an airport in Ireland. “Yes, Ireland will never be able to compare to the beauty of Scotland,” William replies with a sigh, pausing to adjust the gray fedora that protects his baldhead from the Irish winds. “I can’t wait to get this title defense over with so I can do something that’s actually worth my time...like getting a root canal,” Mike says with a sarcastic tone as both he and William finally arrive at the small airport. “Speaking of your title defense...I have an idea I’d like you to try lad,” William says as they step inside the dimly lit and un-kept lobby of the airport. “What’s that William?” Mike asks in an uninterested tone, as he looks around to see if there is anyone around he can get to carry his luggage for him...much to his surprise the building is empty except for himself and William. “I was thinking you could hire a camera crew and film some interviews like the other lads in the Extreme Division do,” William answers, ignoring Mike’s tone. Mike stops dead in his tracks and drops his suitcase on the floor with a loud thud as a shocked expression forms on his face. “I refuse to be like those attention whores and waste my time doing pointless interviews that have no bearing on the outcome of the match to begin with! It‘s a wrestling match...not a debate club and the sooner those idiots realize that the better,” Mike says with an annoyed voice as he reaches down to pick his suitcase back up. “It was just a suggestion,” William mumbles under his breath as they make their way to the front of the building. “Did you notify the limousine service of our arrival?” Mike asks as they step back outside into the cold and windy weather, which is quite the change from Mike‘s home in Beverly Hills. “Of course,” William replies while pointing his left finger towards an oncoming car as proof. The long black limousine pulls up alongside the curb and eases into a complete stop. The driver quickly exits the car after popping the trunk. He takes Mike and William’s suitcases and carefully places them inside the trunk as quickly as possible. The driver then opens the rear door for his two passengers. William quickly climbs into the car, only pausing to thank the driver. “Excuse me driver,” Mike says in a tone of superiority. “Yes sir?” The driver asks with nervousness showing through his thick Irish accent. “How far is it to town on foot?” Mike asks. “Only a few minutes sir,” The driver replies with a much more relaxed voice. “Good,” Mike says as he then turns to William. “I need to take a walk and stretch out a bit...I’ll catch up with you later,” Mike says with a wave of his hand. Before William can give a reply back Mike takes off walking towards the town as if he’s on a mission of some sort. “Well don’t just stand there you imbecile! Take me to the hotel!” William yells at the driver, whom for some odd reason was watching Mike as he walked off. The driver quickly blurts out an apology and closes the door before running back to the driver’s seat. Within minutes Mike is walking down a small dirt road, the stipulations of his title defense weighing heavily upon his mind. Mike HAS to beat Sean McClancy so his career can continue and so he can continue to cleanse the Extreme Division of the ignoble bastards, like Sean McClancy, that clutter the division and give it a bad name. Besides, Ireland is the last place that Mike would want to make his last stand at. “I can’t wait to see the look on Sean’s face when I beat him in his own stupid little match,” Mike says to himself with a cocky smirk as he comes upon a sign that indicates the small town is only a few feet away. As Mike makes his way into the small Irish town he realizes that all this thinking about his upcoming title defense has made him need a stiff drink. Fortunately for Mike he’s in Ireland and the minute he walks into the city limits he notices a large white building with a sign that proudly proclaims “O’Malley’s Pub”. “Might as well start getting used to being in a pub,” Mike mumbles to himself as he makes his way to the pub. When he steps inside the building the first thing he notices is that the dimly lit establishment is empty except for the bartender and four young men sitting in the back sharing a large pitcher of beer. The minute Mike walks inside all eyes are on him, causing him to smirk since he thinks it’s because they envy him but in reality it’s because he’s a stranger and the locals are already suspicious of him. Mike then strolls over to the bar in a cocky manner; sitting down at a barstool like a king would sit upon a throne. Once he sits down it seems that the men in the back decide that Mike is just a harmless tourist and divert their attention back to their beer. “What can I get for you?” The bartender asks Mike in a heavy Irish accent as he looks at Mike like most Europeans look at American tourists...with contempt. “I’ll have a shot of your finest whiskey,” Mike requests casually, paying no attention to the contempt in the glare of the bartender. The bartender reaches under the bar and produces a shot glass along with a large bottle with a white label that proudly says “Irish Whiskey”. The bartender pours the shot and then slides the glass over to Mike. Mike picks the glass up and with a smile takes his shot...only to spit all over the bartender. “Is something wrong sir?” The bartender asks with a barely controlled rage. “I asked for whiskey...not sewage water you worthless peasant!” Mike says as he stands up and slams the shot glass down on the floor, earning himself the full and undivided attention of the four men in the back as the glass shatters. Mike begins to reach over the counter in such a way to suggest that he’s about to punch the bartender’s lights out. “I wouldn’t do that if I were you,” One of the men sitting in the back say to Mike as the four get up from their table and start to approach the bar. “Wha-” Mike turns around and sees that there is four VERY large and upset Irishmen standing in front of him and they look as if they’ve been looking for a fight for quite some time. “He said he wouldn’t do that if he were you, and if I were you I’d listen to him,” One of the men say with a bit of anticipation showing in his voice. A nervous smile comes over Mike’s face and he begins to chuckle a little bit. “I wasn’t going to do anything...I was just going to pay for my drink and be on my way!” Mike says, pausing to dust off the shoulders of the bartender before reaching into his pants pocket and throwing a one hundred-dollar bill on the bar. “That’s right and you‘re never going to come back here either,” One of the men say as Mike begins to slowly back out of the pub. Once Mike reaches the stereotypical swinging wooden doors he turns around and quickly makes a run for it, causing the men left inside the bar to have a good round of laughter at his expense. Once Mike is a safe distance away from the bar he turns around and angrily shouts, “Your whiskey is still shit!” While defiantly waving his fist in the air. Suddenly a barrage of empty beer bottles and rotten potatoes come whizzing past Mike’s head, sending him into a quick retreat as the Irishmen continue to laugh at him. Once Mike feels that he is a safe distance away from his attackers he shoves his hands into his pockets and walks off towards his hotel grumbling to himself about how it’s going to take more than cheap whiskey and rotten potatoes to beat him. -The End For Now- ---- 3 April 2004 As Mike lay in the bed of his dark hotel room he knew that he wasn’t alone. He had heard the door creak open and he listened intently as the intruder tried to tiptoe inside unnoticed. Mike hasn’t done anything but lay very still in the hotel bed as his late night visitor sits in a small armchair that is located at the foot of the bed. Mike’s first assumption is that the visitor is in fact his not so bright valet, Hillary Watson. It has to be her since the sounds that the visitor makes suggest a person of a lightweight since an overweight person would make much more noise in such a limited space and never be able to move about the small hotel room with so much grace. Since he’s so sure that it’s Hillary, Mike has decided to lie perfectly still in hopes that she will go away. The last thing he needs to do is expend energy that he’ll need in his upcoming match against McClancy by giving into the bedroom activities that she most definitely would try to get from him. Just as Mike gets a mental image of him sleeping with Hillary he hears the sound of the intruder clearing it’s throat...its male sounding throat. “Oh my God,” Mike thinks to himself with great alarm. “I’ve heard of this kind of thing before...some Irish queer breaks into a random tourist’s bedroom and tries to ravish them with a potato!” Mike begins to quiver with fear under the sheets as he clenches his butt cheeks together in hopes of avoiding being violated by a potato! “You have come very far since we last spoke Michael Biddle,” A voice that is eerily familiar to Mike says with a hoarse whisper. Mike peeks out from under his covers with a disbelieving look showing in his dark blue eyes when he sees the outline of his former Sensei, Mao, sitting in the dark. “What the hell do you want? And how in the hell did you get here?” Mike asks the elderly Asian man with a bit of anger in his voice. Mao doesn’t reply at first, he just stares at Mike with a blank expression on his face. “I’ve come to give you a warning young Michael Biddle. You know very well that I have my ways and can enter any room that I wish Michael Biddle,” Mao says as the lights in the room magically come on by themselves. The lights reveal the frail figure of Mao sitting upon the small armchair with his legs crossed and his hands resting upon his knees. The silver Asian characters that are written across his blue robes of silk shimmer in the artificial light and his grayed ’Fu Manchu’ mustache looks as if it hasn’t been groomed for quite some time. “I hate it when you do that,” Mike says from under the covers, referring to the lights coming on since it’s obviously another one of Mao’s tricks. “I know,” Mao says to his former pupil with a smile. Mike sits up in the bed, pausing to quickly groom his blond hair with his hand. “What kind of trite are you going to bore me with now Mao? I’ve already proven your other two little prophecies wrong by beating Fathom and the Knightmare Brigade.” Mike says with a bored tone in his voice. “Deep down inside yourself you already know that you shall fail against Sean McClancy. That is why you made the stipulation for your retirement for when you lose. Your ego would be too damaged to allow you to continue on after losing on such a grand stage...and retirement is the only way you can escape the embarrassment.” Mao says while looking Mike right in the eyes. Mike lets out a howl of laughter after Mao finishes his sentence. “Actually,” Mike begins while wiping the tears of laughter away from his eyes. “I made that stipulation so McClancy will think he actually stands a chance against me. He’s the one that’s going to retire because of embarrassment when he realizes that he’s not ‘The Hardcore Icon’. And you know for a fact that there’s no way in hell some drunk Irishman can beat me!” Mike finishes while still chuckling a little bit about Mao’s comments. “That is why you ran away from the Irishmen in that pub earlier in the day...correct?” Mao asks, challenging Mike’s comments. Mike suddenly stops laughing and his face becomes contorted with anger. “There was four of them damn it!” He says while waving an angry finger at Mao. “Besides, they’ll get theirs soon enough,” Mike says through clenched teeth. “What are you going to do Michael Biddle? Pay them a great deal of money to leave you alone?” Mao asks mockingly. “NO! I’m going to buy their damn pub and turn it into something useful...like a parking lot! Hell, I’m going to buy the McClancy family pub and turn it into a parking lot too!” Mike nearly yells while slamming his fist against the bed in a small temper tantrum. “There you go letting your ego get the best of you again Michael Biddle,” Mao says with a serious voice. “Shut your trap old man!” Mike yells at Mao while pointing an angry finger in Mao‘s direction. “Just realize that your ego will be your undoing Michael Biddle. The sooner you rid yourself of it’s dark presence the sooner you can achieve true greatness,” Mao says in such a way that makes it sound like he actually wants his former pupil to succeed. “I’ve already achieved true greatness you colon cake! You’ll see when I beat McClancy at his own game in a few days and then you’ll eat your words,” Mike says with a much calmer voice. “What you think and reality are two completely different things Michael Biddle,” Mao says; suddenly vanishing in a puff of gray smoke. A shocked look comes over Mike’s face since he wasn’t expecting Mao to just disappear like that, hell he wasn’t even expecting the old bastard to show up in his hotel room! “I really hate it when he does that,” Mike says to himself. Mike watches as the smoke rises to the ceiling, towards the smoke alarm that hangs from the wall. As the smoke left by Mao’s departure drifts past the alarm it suddenly causes the alarm to be set off. It wails louder than a banshee and suddenly all around the building several Irish voices are heard yelling “FIRE!” as Mike sighs and just goes back to sleep. -The End-
