Date: 2006-01-09 Half open eyes. Blinded by the spotlight shining down above. I could feel the itchy rope around my red raw skin. I could see my breath in front of my tired eyes. I could feel the damp air. The freezing air around me. Smothering me. Suffocating. Suffocating. I struggle for each breath, as my head sways down towards my blood soaked shirt. My head played out a dull roar, ringing between my battered ears. I couldn’t focus. Waster. Fucker. Dickhead. How could I let myself be here? My eyes wandered, looking at my dimpled skin. Shivering like a fucking drowned rat. All I was at that moment was worthless. Nothing but a lost life. Just a speck in gods little game of life. I could see the gates. I could see him greeting me with open arms. I could hear his voice. And it seemed like this moment would last forever. Then he spoke. "I’m saving you." … Turning up high to a WWA taping in Tampa, Florida. Hit Sarah while drunk. Stole one crate of Corona one night when high and drunk. The scruffiness of my scrawled writing is added by the fact that it was wrote nearly 2 years ago. The old, browning paper has little rips on it’s corners. A 100 words with a thousand meanings. Welcome to the fourth step of becoming clean. I swigged a bottle of Jim Beam down my neck; the smooth liquid burned the back of my mouth, as I re-read the comments. Assault, two men in hospital. Damage to property. Broken knuckles x2. They say, that what you have to do to come clean, is list everything that you have done wrong in your life because of your addiction. I had two, so I guess my list was more a novel. Tell your story. Shout it out to the world. How this has destroyed you. How it has overcame you. How it has became you. They said anyway. But I never thought any piece of A4 paper can hold all my problems. Assault, one week in jail. The leader of these sessions always used to say the same thing at the beginning. Standing on his little soap box, he looked upon us like god himself. Albeit, a fat, bloated, yellow skinned faggot of a god, but a god nonetheless. And Mr Whoever shouted before us; with the same old shit that is said every week, without fail. I want everyone to relax. I want you all to relax your arms. Relax your elbows. Unclench your hands. Feel the tension draining from you. Feel it run through your veins, and out of your body. It is leaving you. And your becoming clear. You can see. You are free. And then their was a pathetic shit telling us about how pathetic their life is. Or job is. Or wife is. Or how small their dick is. Fact is, there is not one universal reason why people in this world let themselves be consumed by something that will edge them closer to insanity. In the past 2 or so years which I have attended these; in back rooms so no-one will ever know your there, all kinds of people have graced the meetings. John was a successful businessman. Good looking, rich, and had it everything. But the fact his wife left him because he was about as good in bed as Mini Me in the NBA. Jackie had three children she loved dearly, a husband that gave her security and a house that she kept immaculate. However her high sex drive drove her husband away, and left her with nothing but a double post bed to finger herself on. Those are the kind of people who are drove to such extremes that they let themselves go. Let it consume them. And im finding it hard to stop it from consuming me. Ring. Hello, I say with a soft tone. "Hello again." The tone took a dramatic u-turn. Who the fuck are you? I say viciously. Ever since his phone-call about Frank, it has plagued on my mind like a cut in your mouth that you can’t stop tonguing. "That’s not very nice Steven. I help you out; from the goodness of my heart, and you give me such a hostile reception." He starts to tut under his breath. Mocking me. Fucking mocking me. How about you tell me who you are? I say. My voice trembles, as I hold back the anger. My knuckles turn to white, I’m clenching so hard. "We shall get to that Steven. But not yet. All I would do, is watch yourself tonight." Click. Hello?! Hello?!?! Answer you fucking prick! Like talking to a brick wall. The phone breaks as I slam it down so hard. I charge to my shitty flat door, standing over my shitty clothes, when I suddenly remember. Need my novel for the group tonight. … "Maybe if you just, loosen up a bit. Maybe you’ll like it more then." She looks up at me with her puppy dog eyes, and bright red lipstick. I could feel her soft lips around me, making me pulse. I grab the toilet, my grip tightening every second. My little cubicle whore. Every week, the same thing happens. I meet Katie and we make those same eyes at each other. We listen to every little sob story from the same sad fucks every week, looking for a salvation that will never come to them. Looking for a release from their crippling addiction. And then we fuck like school kids in a run down toilet. "Steve, what’s up?" She stops, as her cold hand rests on me, bringing me back to the real world. "You don’t seem yourself tonight." Nothing, carry on going I say. She couldn’t understand. She would be too fucked up on heroine half the time to take it all in. "If you want hun." She makes me sit down on the toilet, as she pulls up that skirt of hers. "No panties, like you asked." I pull her down onto me, as she grabs the back of my head. We rock back, as the men’s bathroom became a shower of moans. She bounced to the moon and came crashing back down to me, again. Again. Again. Again. And the door opens. "I swear, if its Bill again I’m gonna-" Katie whispers in my ear before I silence her with a finger to her soft lips. She sits atop of me, as the footsteps are the only thing I concentrate on. My heart raced. I could feel Katie’s heartbeat upon me. Her breath in my ear was as loud as the ocean crashing into the rocks on a windy day. Or it seemed like that. And then he broke out into a whistle. "He is probably just having a piss." Katie whispers reassuringly. "Then we can finish off." I give a half smile, before the whistling stops. And all I can see is two black boots underneath the cubicle door. Knock Knock. What do you want? I shout out. Knock Knock. What the fuck do you want? I shout again. Knock Knock. I push Katie off me, as I pull my pants up. I open the door with force. People with near death experiences say they see a bright light, at the end of a long tunnel just before they wake up. I guess seeing his face again was just like that.
