TTD B1 -v- B2

5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene 1 (Nicky Jam) [ We are looking through a long shot of some mountains, the camera flies in between each one and we get a close up of a guy hanging on to an edge of a mountain.] [He has a good grip with his feet and he places his hand in a bag of chalk that rests around his waist in a small bag, he is wearing a white vest and black pants and some climbing shoes, he really does look the part.] [Soon, he later looks over his right shoulder where he sees a small overhang in the cliff, after some thought he finally jumps quite some distance to the other side, grabbing it with ease and grace landing on the heel of his left shoe. But something goes wrong, something gives way and he slowly falls down the cliff with rubble and smoke surrounding him, but he grabs ahold of an overhang and he is once again safe.] [As the camera zooms out we see the guy is hanging over a huge drop, with just one hand keeping him from safety, his face expression does not change and he just hangs there carelessly in the small breeze. He quickly gets his other hand onto the overhanging edge and manages to pull himself up to safer ground. He begins to climb some more until he is at the top of the mountain, looking down on the world taking in his amazing achievement.] [But a noise can be heard, some kind of buzzing, we see the dust around the man's feet flying into the air as he stand there with a puzzled look on his face, we then cut to the noise.] [As suspected it is a helicopter, one pilot and one other man carrying some kind of weapon pointing it towards the man on the cliff and telling the pilot to keep it steady. The weapon is fired and the small missile lands at the feet of the guy before the helicopter flies off away from the cliffs.] [Once again we return to the man on the cliff that is shaking his head with a small smirk on his face, he bends down and looks at the missile. The guy knows exactly what to do and the missile opens at the top and a pair of glasses fly to the top of the capsule, the guy smirks some more and takes out the glasses.] [He places them on and we cut to the view he gets from the glasses. Straight away we can tell these are not normal glasses, a green kind of image flickers on the screen and words begin to fly around. Soon some writing appears that we can make out, it says "Mission: PTC Tag Team Titles", and then moves to the bottom side of the screen we are looking into. A voice can then be heard from the speakers:] Voice:Your mission, should you choose to accept it, requires you to defeat a certain tag team, designated The Hellraisers. Essential to the mission is the destruction of Chris Johnson and Dante Locks. The Hellraisers are highly capable professional wrestlers currently active in PTC. Their dossier's available on NGW.com. You have one match to defeat them and move forward in the tag team ranks and file of PTC, meet me backstage to receive further booking details. Should you or Chivo be mamed or killed, the UPWA will disavow all knowledge of your actions. And Mr. Jam - the next time you go on vacation, please be good enough to let us know where you're going. This message will self-destruct in five seconds. [Nicky Jam takes of the glasses and begins to speak.] Nicky Jam: If I let you know where I'm going I won't be on vacation. [We then see Nicky Jam standing there with the wind blowing his hair up around the sides, but suddenly he screams out in pain as we see a spark in the left hard corner of the screen and a small bang go off over the sound of screaming and obscenities.] Voice: Cut, cut....Nicky Jam what the hell was that? You're supposed to throw the glasses towards the camera. Nicky Jam: What the hell? They nearly blew my arm off, damn misfunctional shades, you wouldn't get that kind of crap from wal-mart. Voice: They are supposed to do that, its part of the promotional video. God damn wrestler, you were supposed to throw the glasses towards the camera so they could explode? Nicky Jam: I was supposed to throw the glasses? Voice: Yes...just like it says in the script. Nicky Jam: Script? Voice: Yes the thing you were supposed to be studying before the filming? Nicky Jam: You mean your filming this? Is my hair ok? Voice: Of course we are filming this, it's a god damn video...what did you think we were doing? Nicky Jam: A video? Since when have I ever worked in a video? Voice: Mr. Jam look, you have the leading role in the promotional video for the PTC Tag Team Titles, one of the greatest tag team tournaments to be released this year. I'm John Woo the director. You play yourself, the good guy. Nicky Jam: Wow, a video, I thought this was some training course the UPWA has signed me up for. Cool, so what's the video about? Woo: Have you not read the script? Nicky Jam: I guess not. Woo: Have you even seen Mission Impossible? Nicky Jam: Well I guess I must have if I was in it. Woo: You weren't in the film, you are just starring in a video based on the film. Nicky Jam: Are you sure you know what you are doing? Woo: After meeting you I'm not so sure anymore. Nicky Jam: So, I'm not in the film, but I'm in a video like the film. This may just be me, but I think you've made a big mistake. Woo: Yeah, I think we have. Nicky Jam: This is a joke right? Woo: The only joke son is you, please leave the set. Nicky Jam: Hey you can't kick me off the set, I'm the leading guy, I may not know the script but I can't have that big a part. Woo: You're the leading guy, what type of part do you think you have? Nicky Jam: The leading one? Woo: Are you really as stupid as you look? Nicky Jam: Listen Woo, I'm your leading guy, it's not my fault I didn't know to throw the glasses, I climbed up a damn mountain for you with no safety helmet or anything, a damn guy in a helicopter tried shooting me with a damn bazooke and then I have some chinese guy giving me stress. I am the leading guy, GOD DAMN YOU DIRECTORS. Woo: Security! Nicky Jam: Ohh, your gonna play the hardball game are you? Well two can play that game. [Nicky Jam rips the pad out of Woo's hand and rips it too shreds right before his very eyes as he stands on top of the mountain while repeatedly shouting out "ha ha ha do you like that".] Nicky Jam: What are you going to do...get The Hellraisers to play my part? [Nicky Jam takes another step closer to Woo's face as he keeps screaming over and over, Woo steps back and calls for security who come over and grab Nicky Jam. Security finally releases Nicky Jam, who in turn just begins walking off the set.] Nicky Jam: I'm the leading actor, not The Hellraisers. [Nicky Jam walks to the door when he stops and turns around and begins to walk back to Woo who now looks nervous. Nicky Jam looks him straight in the eye before grabbing down on a desk that stands next to both men and he picks up some shades before once again walking off to the door.] Nicky Jam: I'm the leading actor, just go right ahead and try to make the video without me. Because Chivo and myself The Rodriguez Brothers will be the ones to win this tournament and become PTC Tag Team Champions so if they pick someone else for the video it will look bad when they lose. The Rodriguez Brothers will make an impact in this tournament and when it is over and done with not only will we have the UPWA Tag Team Titles but the PTC Tag Team Titles. [The glasses then explode in his hand again as a slight smirk comes across Woo's face. Nicky Jam never flinches, he just continues walking off the set.] Nicky Jam: Those damn stupid glasses. [We once again return to Woo, who is continuing to giggle somewhat at the sight of the glasses exploding in Nicky Jam's hand.] Woo: Yeah...Hi, PTC we need another guy on the set now, we have a leading spot open for him. [FADE OUT...] ###The full making of the PTC Tag Team Title Tournament promotional Mission Impossible video is now available on home video and dvd and includes a free " I met Nicky Jam, and I really, REALLY liked him" t-shirt.### Scene 2 (Chivo) A.N.I.M.O.S.I.T.Y.: 1. Bitter hostility or open enmity; active hatred. 2.A hostile feeling or act. 3. Violent hatred leading to active opposition; active enmity; energetic dislike. 4. Enmity be dormant or concealed; animosity is active enmity, inflamed by collision and mutual injury between opposing parties. "Animosity fuels my fire in my fight against The Hellraisers" Animosity fuels my fire. I let hatred lead the way. Not hatred for the person themselves. Hatred for what they have done to me, or could do to me. I don't respect you anymore Limp. When you double crossed me, not only did you lose all of your fans, you lost your dignity, your intense love for the sport, and you lost your freedom. You're a slave to the evil man. Basically...you're La Envidia Mata with a smidgen of talent. Limp, after the tongue lashing I gave you yesterday, you still came back like this, "Wah waha wah wahahw wah" and that's basically all that I heard coming from your mouth. That and you were spewing sh*t left and right. You wanna be tough guys like us there Hellraisers? Work on your "acts" a bit more. You'll be tough guys IF you make it out of the tournament. You'll be able to throw your weight around. Here's what you will NOT be able to do... You will NOT be able to defeat us when we're focused on you. You won't be able to get the better of us, and if you do, it won't last long. We’ve beaten better people than you. I’m a convicted killer. When I killed, I killed a man of your height and build. He was my cousin. I killed a man of Limp’s height and build. He was my step brother. This tale is not a happy story, but it’s the lesson that lies from within you, in which you will find the meaning of what I’m trying to get across. I killed my cousin Wayne. I killed my Uncle Ryan. I killed my step brother Robert. There’s nothing you can do or say that will put us down in that ring. The squared circle is our home. It is where we learned everything we know. We learnt it all on our own. We did not have trainers. We have one person to thank for how good we’ve become… that’s us. But because I am my own trainer, I see my own flaws better, and constantly try to improve them. One day….I will be the UPWA World Champion. I can smell it in the air. ..Or maybe that was just a Hellraiser farting. I can smell it getting stronger and stronger. We will defeat the best the PTC has to offer, and we will ascend that final rung in the ladder. Our time is coming soon. You’re just in the way, Hellraisers. You’re just in our way, Hellraisers. The bigger they are, the harder they fall. The bigger they are, the more bones they break. The bigger they are, the slower they are. The less agile they are. The stupider they are. I’ve got issues with certain beings. One is The Hellraisers. You get under my skin. You annoy me. You don’t keep my thoughts on you with what you say….It’s hard to describe. You agitate me. When I’m agitated, I go where no one wants me to go. I lose all caring for my mentality. I lose all hope for you. I go hostile. You don’t want that. I don’t want it to come to that. I have issues with God. He gave me this mutation. I’m a mutant/human. Yeah I look like a human, but my mind….that is my mutation. He cursed me with many voices in my head. Over the years, as I have become less angry with him, I have realized that the less angry I get, the more control I have over my mutation. The voices are of my cousin, brother, and uncle. The ones I killed, sought vengeance upon me and they cracked their way into my head. These voices, I cannot suppress. It drove me to insanity the last time I had a wave of them like I just had now. Sometimes I just lose it. It’s hard to suppress yourself from your own thoughts before acting on them. “Kill him!” That voice is always in the background, no matter what I do. That’s Robert. He and I shared a somewhat sacred bond between step brothers until he betrayed me. “Steal his money” That’s Uncle Ryan…always thinking of money. “LET’S GO KICK SOME ASS!” That’s Wayne. Very big, and a menacing beast to fight. I fought him once before his timely demise. The lesson here, is that, every person who crosses my path in a bad way, ends up wounded or dead. Hellraisers, listen up. You’re starting to piss me off, and you’re on the road to crossing my path in a bad way. I won’t stand for it. I will stop you in your tracks, and throw you back to where you came from. PTC is my home. I will protect it from all evil. Even @ssholes like you Hellraisers. Limp, you listen closely. Only the three men I killed, betrayed me as much as you did. Take this as a warning. After what I’ve stated earlier, you’re on your way to getting hurt or worse. I won’t stand for your sh*t any longer. You will get what’s coming to you…and when all is said and done, Chezina will stand, victorious, over you. Yes, that’s right. Chezina, not me. He has a vendetta against you too, and he’s a fun loving guy. That doesn’t say much for what you did to him. He let’s everything go. A loss, meh, oh well. He doesn’t hold it to heart if someone hurts him unintentionally. What you did to him, was bruise his inner child. You gave him a savage beating. You’re a savage now Limp. You’re not a human. You’re an animal, craving the blood of others. Welcome to the family Limp. I know what’s racing through your mind right now, and yes, I am coming for you in time. There’s nowhere you can run to, or hide in, for me to not be able to find you, and punish you. Hellraisers, let’s leave this in the ring. I don’t want to have to kick the crap out of you to the point where you never wrestle again, we’ll see you before our match. We’re going to “chat” about a few things first. Hellraisers, you’re really going to have to prove your worth on Sunday against Nicky Jam and me. We’re not pushovers, so don’t think that you can just walk all over our bloodied corpses and get the win. It won’t happen like that at all. I can f*cking guarantee it. You’re in for the best fight of your careers in PTC, and it should not be taken lightly, just because it’s the first round of the Tag Team Title Tournament. I won’t take it easy on you, and neither will Nicky Jam. Let’s dance, Hellraisers.
