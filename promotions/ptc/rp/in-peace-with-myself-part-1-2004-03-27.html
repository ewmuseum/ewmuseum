In Peace with Myself Part 1

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-27 The scene opens on a picnic area. It is the lunch time eating hour and a jolly sunny day, so lots of families are out. The camera scrolls around the picnic area and comes to a out of the norm sight. It is Limp, he is donning a traditional Indian wear oddly capped off with an American Football Jersey and a pair of sunglasses. He is not alone. Limp seems to have a couple of people both dressed similar but without the football jersey or the sunglasses. The cameraman wodels over to where Limp is. The cameraman catches Limp in mid conversation. Limp- So he says that's my Coca Cola, so I says errr would your coke have my spit in it and he like says err what and I like spit in it and he's like what the hell you doing and I'm like bite me and he like goes to hit me and I'm like SECURITY SECURITY and they carry his ass away. Man those were the days. Limp looks up at the sky and people around him who have heard the conversation as of Limp's loud voice look at him with annoyance. Limp then looks down and notices there's a cameraman standing by him. Limp takes out a cigarette from a packet of Lucky Strikes, flips it into his mouth in a showing off manner and lights it. Limp takes a few drags and then begins to speak. Bret- Hey Petey you made it, marvelous. I thought I would have a nice relaxing day in the park. I thought this was going to be an interview? Where's that little kid spanky or whatever his name is? At that point before the cameraman can get a word in another man comes stumbling up with papers flapping in his hand and begins to talk when he is in ear shot. Bloke- Sorry, Fredric walked out on the company so I was only told half an hour ago I would be covering this interview, sorry. Limp nods to say its fine and the interviewer fumbles through his notes, puts them away and then begins to speak. Interviewer- Lets start then shall we. Limp we have seen shades of this image in past wrestling organizations. We have seen so many mood swings. Some say that you have not seen the light but you just have a short attention span and jump from one horse to the other, what do you have to say about that? Limp smiles and then speaks. Limp- Who said that? Was it porky Pete the cameraman? I find that rather insulting for you to say that spanky. If you had had your body physically destroyed like I had at XWF and been told your going to be murdered and have your dead lying body raped or whatever you would see things a little differently too. Its like I was telling little jackie here *Limp points at one of his friends* We can play the blame game but all in all we all know I didn't really steal the Christmas Turkey. The Interviewer quickly cuts in. Interviewer- Christmas Turkey? sorry what has that got to do with your change of face, being killed or having your body mangled? Limp- It has everything and nothing to do with it spanky. You see if I hadn't have stolen that turkey then Christmas would have been ruined! The interviewer looks confused. Interviewer- I thought you said you didn't steal the turkey? Limp- Yes that's what I have been saying all along. See its people like you saying I did that makes people think I did. God dam it its only a turkey. And what the hell are you talking about a Christmas turkey for square pants this is an Interview about wrestling and we are in March. Limp shakes his head as if its the interviewer who is mad. Limp extinguishes his cigarette and then begins to speak again. Limp- Anyhow I'm fading off topic. In the not to distant future I have a match with my old buddy Chezina. I remember the day we went to the zoo to celebrate Chezina's XWF world title win. Man that was a good day, we saw a Tiger you know. Man those were the days. But now me and him have to square off in the ring one on one. Now I'm not the violent type as you know. But this isn't no bar room brawl this is a professional wrestling match between two top wrestlers. Two guys who know each other inside out and upside down. This will be a close match, well obviously it will as we're wrestling but that is not what I meant. We both want to go at it and we both want that world title. We both want a chance in the Global Division of PTC. Limp pauses to light up another cigarette. The interviewer is about to speak but Limp cuts him off. Limp- In this short time I almost feel back up to 100% you know why? Meditation, Yoga and a bottle of pain killers. The way you feel is all in the head. If your not mentally ready your not Physically ready. Pain is just a feeling we get, it can be cut out even eliminated. I have a completely new outlook on this wrestling game, I shed my rough neck wrestling style I once had and now concentrate on the martial Arts and technical skill. I will take down Karina Wolfenden and get the one, two, three before you can say "how much wood can a wood chuck chuck, if a wood chuck, chucks wood" or however it goes. Limp pauses and the Interviewer finally gets the chance to speak. Interviewer- We haven't heard anything yet from Karina Wolfenden about the match. Do you think she is confident? Limp cuts in straight away. Limp- Oh I expect our big ego'd friend confident, I have no doubt of that in fact. I will destroy him and move one step closer to winning my second consecutive Cruiserweight Tournament. When that ref counts the one, two, three and my arm is raised and you are lying on the mat looking up in a confessed manner, I will take your hand, lift you to your feet, shake your hand and give you a special big hug and a nice big flower. There's only one thing to say, only one thing worth saying, give peace a chance my friends. At that point Limp does a peace sign with his hand and turns back to his friends and carries on the conversation about his cup of coca cola. The camera fades back to the PTC studio.... Later... It is a hot sunny day. The scene is located in Elizabeth Park, in the city of Bath, England. Lots of people are out soaking up the sun in the tranquil environment of the park. As the camera swoops around the park it comes across the peace loving hippy that is Limp. He is sitting on top of small hill. Limp is wearing a pair of yellow short-trousers and a tight orange T-shirt. He is sitting in a circle of people, they are all laughing, chatting and smoking. The cameraman steadily comes closer to the group. As the cameraman does Limp and Co notice. They all stare at the camera until they quickly get board of doing so. Limp flicks away the cigarette he was smoking, clears his throat and begins to speak in a mellow tone. Limp- Hey my man Pete the cameraman and your on time, super! Yes indeed I thought I would take a vacation for the weekend in England, in the lovely city of Bath. A rare city is Bath, were ever you look you can see green, wonderful! Anyhow I didn't drag you half the way around the world to talk about the City of Bath. If you did or didn't know I have a little old match with my little old friend Chezina coming very soon. I've said it once and I'll say it again this match is for the world title. The winner proves to everyone that he is the best in UPWA. Really though does he even know what he's talking about half the time? Do you even think he thinks about what he is about to say before he stumbles it out of his big small mouth? Me would think not. Nothings been seen or heard from Chezina yet, maybe he is hurt more than we thought or maybe he found an abandoned house of dead bodies and he is having a party, who knows. Not me. Limp pauses and motions a friend next to him to play the instrument in front of him, which happens to be a satir. Limp nods his head in approval and continues rambling. Limp- Now lets move onto my friend Karina Wolfenden. She seems a little upset, or is it my imagination? Silly little bunny, playing with tricks. But you know, the message I have been trying to spread in only a short time may work, you know. I will give Karina a flower to make her happy. See, what I have been saying all we need is flowers and smiles. Like Christopher Columbus said "all you need is love", oh now wait no the Beatles said that, close enough. Limp pauses. From a bag next to him he takes out a framed photo. It is obscured from the camera. Limp is looking at it fondly. Finally Limp turns the picture to the camera and we see that it is the UPWA World Champion himself Chezina. Limp places the picture on the ground next to him and begins to speak once more. Limp- Oh my big friend Chezina. Or should I say Peter Pan! No Chezina actually. Chezina you really do seem to have a bee in your pants don't you. Saying I want to be like the big old angry Chezina. Calling me a nobody, the man who has beaten you before. The man who carried you for so long. Your friend, your former teammate. I bought you a packet of chocolate Hobb Nobbs and you insult me! Well that upsets me, I thought we were going to have a professional good old fashioned wrestling match but it seems you want to make it personal, you want to fight like a dirty raccoon! Limp picks up the framed picture of Chezina again and stares at it. Then a smile comes over Limp's face. Limp- Ahh, how can I stay mad at you big man. I hope you can sort out your little problems with me and we can have a top tastic match. Now I thought we could end with a song. Limp picks up the acustic guitar from beside him and strums a few notes, pauses then breaks into a tune which is closely followed by words Limp- Hush, Chezina, don't say a word, Mama's going to buy you a mockingbird. If that mockingbird won't sing, Papa's going to buy you a diamond ring. If that diamond ring turns brass, Mama's going to buy you a looking glass. If that looking glass gets broke, Papa's going to buy you a billy-goat. And if that billy-goat runs away, Mama's going to buy you another one today. And all of these things your parents will do, Because you're the best little child in the world. At that point Limp bows his head. Then looks up and speaks. Limp- That's dedicated to my best buddy Chezina. Limp puts down his guitar, turns back to his company and speaks amongst them once more. The camera shot fades back down to a pink screen and then into a commercial break. Later... The scene opens in a lounge type area. The camera pans the room. It seems pretty luxurious. Leather chairs, big screen TV, HiFi Separates, the works. The room is capped off with an over sized realistic looking daisy in a pot in the middle of the room. There are two men sitting in the room on the leather chairs. One is wearing a pink pair of trousers and a T-shirt that says "home" on it and the other man is wearing a pair of back jeans and blood stained you guessed it black T-shirt on. Its is Limp and DVD. Booming out the HiFi we can hear the classic track Puff the magic Dragon. Both men are concentrating hard on the song. When the song begins to fade down Limp jumps out of his chair and flips the HiFi off. He then goes back to sit down on his chair, spins his chair towards DVD and stares at him with clenched fists seeming to await his verdict. DVD stares at the flashing standby light on the HiFi with a cocked head and then with a confused look on his face begins to speak. DVD- Wait a minute you didn't write that, that's from that film isn't it, written by what's his name? Limp looks as though someone just pissed on the carpet in front of him, but finds the words to speak. Limp- Sure I wrote it skip. Anyhow its not important that I wrote it. Did you get the message from the song? DVD shots back quick. DVD- That I should puff the magic dragon? Limp- If you want, but it's a calming song my friend. Look I told you I'm a doctor do what I say and you will be better. DVD- Your a doctor now? Where's your qualifications? Limp looks around the room, then speaks. Limp- errrm yeah the board thingy people forgot to post it to me. But I am, see my title is Doctor Limp so I must be. Anyhow I'm thinking your suffering from a rare condition called "hydro-imperial". Take two of these pills a day and you will be fine. Limp chucks the scratched and label less bottle to DVD. DVD looks at it and then inquires about it. DVD- What are these pills? Limp snaps back quick. Limp- How should I know, what do you think I am a doctor? DVD- errrm that's what you said you were so yes. Limp- Oh right yeah I remember now. Yeah there "anti hydro-imperial" pills dude. Yeah that's what they are. Ok lets recap what we have been talking about today. That love is much more important than hate. The system is f*cked yes I agree, but we must challenge it with love. The people running the system want you to loose your mind, your just playing into their hand. DVD considers this for a minute and then begins to speak. DVD- Yes so what your saying is I need to go and kill them all with a tooth pick so then they can't f*ck me anymore? Yes that sounds like a good idea. Limp- errrm sort of. Yes you got it in one if you take out the killing. DVD- But then I would just have a tooth pick. Limp- Right, look you go paint another picture your good at that. Again when I said paint I meant with actual paint and brushes. You know if you use your own blood you can only get shades of red and that's just silly bananas. DVD jumps out of his seat and retreats to the door muttering the words "paint a picture yes, I'll paint a really pretty picture". Limp shrugs off DVD's disturbing behavior, turns to the camera and begins to talk on in his usual manner. Limp- Well well well. So much has happened this week. Well only one thing I give to grape fruits about. Yes Your friend,. your savor, your floating hymn Limp is in the Cruiserweight Tournament in PTC. Yes Limp will win his second straight tournament with ease, letting down all the little fans my opponents have on their over sized heads. But you know Limp I hope we can be friends. It had to be done I had to show you that your evil ego was not all it was cracked up to be. I showed you that my talent and love for all could out shine you. Look at DVD under my guidance he is really starting to dig himself out the hole he put himself in. DVD can be heard outside the room erratically arguing with himself, Limp ignores this and continues to talk. Limp- Sorry I digress, lets move on to the current play of events. I will star in the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament, against who, you hungry dogs ask. Well I will tell you, my opponent is for some odd reason the favorite to win the entire tournament and I'm talking about Karina Wolfenden who could probably win the tournament if I wouldn't be in it. I'm being mean aren't I? Limp pauses to take out a cigarette, he lights it chuckles away to himself for a second, takes a few drags and then begins to speak once more. Limp- You know I find it so funny how serious all these guys in the back take the game we play, thinking they're in fact talented wrestlers. You know I have realized a lot since that nasty little match against Rey. The first which we have already touched on which would be my new outlook on life and the second is, you can try and try and have as much talent in the ring as Rey but the simple fact remains that it doesn't really matter, its just entertainment for working-class heroes out there who pay there hard earned money on a few beers and a ticket to the wrestling extravaganza. Quite frankly I couldn't give two tits if I'm liked or not or if I get up or stay in bed or even if I win or lose, you see all in all everything revolves around karma, if you got good karma things will go your way and if you like are mentally challenged like Karina Wolfenden who have bad karma things will go wrong. Ah those guys crack me up. Man the joker is right behind them in hysterics of laughter at the rubbish they say and believe yet they really think people take it seriously. You know Chezina was just a annoyance, a bug on the tip of my finger I flicked away, which is kind of what Karina Wolfenden is but you know she piss me off, she made me laugh yes but quite frankly I have had enough, they're like your president Bush. First you laugh at what an idiot he is and the crap he comes out with and then you begin to realize this bloke has a nice amount of power and hasn't a clue what he is doing. You see that's what you are. I laughed at the thought of Chezina being world champ but now the realism of it has set in that some teenage sheep has become world champ and I have to first take down Karina Wolfenden and then wait for a fight with Chezina to damn well get the world title into the right hands. You know I'm a loving guy, hell I even forgave little Chezina for his back stabbing and I forgave DVD for not using a Costa for christs sake, but you know Chezina and The Rodriguez Brothers. You don't amuse me anymore and I don't care for you much, you have no substance just walking around in your big boots. Maybe you should become one of those pop gothic acts then at least you would be dishing out your own sh*t instead of swallowing all those other acts sh*t. Limp pauses to put out his cigarette, looks down then up again with a smile on his face and begins to speak. Limp- Love, smiles and flowers my friends it's all you need and all you will ever want, let it in and let it flow. Peace my brothers and sisters. Limp holds out a peace sign and the scene fades with the sounds of DVD still arguing with himself in the hall way. Later... The scene opens on top a hill top. The air is clean as you would expect up in the middle of no where with no pollution to destroy it. Everything looks so perfect on the hill top, the trees growing from it the green green grass, the perfectly shaped rocks, the type of bluetie people look at with open jar or with a tear in the eye. The camera swoops to the other side were a man is standing. He is wearing a cow custom, with the head piece tucked under his arm, we can see the man has attempted to put on some clown make-up. The man is Limp. Limp stands there for a second moments but then finds the words he was looking for. Limp- Hey hey it's the PTC crew. Maybe your wondering why I am wearing a cow custom and clown make up, well I'm not wondering why I am so there you go. Limp takes out a cigarette from his cow costume pocket, flips it into his mouth in a show off sort of way, lights it and begins to speak again. Limp- You know I have been sitting on this hill for a week now, watching the shadows, thinking about a few things. Mainly about the state of the British railway, it's in a terrible state you know, wouldn't have happened under a Monster Raving Lonely Party Government that's for damn sure! But that's not what you plant pots want to hear now is it, cause there is also something else I have been thinking about that thing is the UPWA World Champion himself Chezina. My friend, my bosom buddy. Why would the UPWA make me fight just a scared big kid who never got the action figure he wanted, that poor big kid. Damn his parents, giving him a Barbie doll instead. All those kids laughing at him while they were running around making bang bang sounds he was sitting on a park bench combing his Barbie dolls hair, while the kiddies laugh and torment him with "you big girl". Limp pauses to shed a tear, and then continues to speak. Limp- You hated your parents didn't you Chezina, you blamed them for having no friends and for being called a "Big Girl". But then you began to like Barbie dolls, but you hated what people thought of you for liking them, you hated your parents for buying it for you which started the whole obsession with them off. You didn't know what to think. Then you found wrestling, it made sense to you, you thought this will prove I'm not a big girl, be a big macho man. Hell you did not do so bad, you became world champ, but lets face it there wasn't much competition. Which brings me to what conflicts me so. I know when I take that title belt from you soon your going to go mad again. Your going to start painting with blood, run around with dead flowers, because you think you have failed, not showing you are a tough man. The voices will begin again won't they Chezina "big girl, big girl, big girl" and "why mommy, why". But you know you shouldn't think like that. There is no shame in being beaten by the best. It will not be hate like usual that beats you it will be love, goodness and hope. Me! Also, I have a match in the first round of the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament against Karina Wolfenden. Karina, don't get upset when I hit you with the Limp Kick. I don't want to fight a woman but you decided to be in this match so you give me no other choice. Limp lowers himself to the ground, flicks his cigarette away and sits cross legged in front of the camera, clears his throat and then begins to speak once again. Limp- You know I was thinking what would it be like to be world champ, which springs another thought into my head to which gives me the conclusion that Chezina isn't suited to be world champ. You see Chezina being world champ speaks volumes, it makes the boldest statement that can be made in this way of life. It says your the best, it says you wanna be the best, well the road ends at the world champ, that you are the last challenge, the biggest challenge. But look at you. You are world champ and you still act like an idiot, like your a scared little pigeon. You should be living it up, having half naked people feed you grapes while sitting on the finest crafted leather chairs money can buy having jesters entertain. Soak up the power and the glory. You see that is what you will get if or should I say when I win that world title. I will be that champ, like a Caesar not the salad kind as I'm sure your aware but of the Roman army kind. We see Limp looking at the sky, with power and focus, but the mood is ruined as of the obvious flaw of wearing a cow suit and clown make-up while saying it. Limp then moves on to a another topic. Limp- You see it is fate, I was booked for this match last week in a house show, but decided that the time wasn't right, the moon was not aligned right, you know all that crap. But now is the time, well not right now, you know what I mean, soon a new breed of champ will be born. A fun loving laid back dude slash Roman Caesar. It will be a beautiful night, when the count is made, I will rise from the blood soaked Chezina after a hardy effort, flowers will fall from the ceiling, the title will be thrust into my arms, Get Up by Limp will blare from the PA system and I will fall to my knees and cry, cry with joy my friends at being the first Hippy to win a world title. Oh yes this will go into the UPWA record books, the fascists will not silence my words anymore, the ice will fall from the roof and the birds will sing, they will know my name as LIMP the man with a plan! Limp has tired himself out from the very emotional speech he made, lays back and begins to twirl a daisy in his little finger, with a delighted smile of a 5 year old on his face. The cameras fades back to the UPWA arena with a silent crowd, not sure how to take what Limp said, not understanding it themselves or thinking does Limp even understand what he says.
