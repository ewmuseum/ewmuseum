Stuey, our trip induced genius.(IG round 25 RP, character: Sam Landry, FA)

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(OOC note: the title extension was not meant to be pretentious, but rather to clarify that neither the Fists or Drake Coleman are the character(s) Im using) “No, dude, it’s more like an oxen.” The trip is an experience that not every human being can handle. You need the mental fortitude, the right atmosphere, and the fucking common sense not to jump off a building because “you can fly, dude, no seriously!” Most people have the fear that they’re gonna be one of those stupid sons of bitches, the ones who think that they’ll be jumping into the ocean because they want to see what it’s like to drown in a sea of maple syrup. Not Sam Landry, though. “An oxen? What type of oxen has fucking 8 wings man?” Sam Landry was a druggy, and he loved every moment of it. There were no qualms ever, none of this “Why do I chase the dragon?” BS that’s so common in everyone else’s bullshit. No sir, just straight up stoning, tripping, and overall an enjoyable insanity that was catered to the fact that it was accepted fully. “Probably the same oxen that’s making those tacos.” Today was like most other Tuesdays, which, in his circle of friends, was Tuesday trip day. At 2:17 every day (why 2:17? “Because it’s fucking craaaaaaaaaaaaaazy, man!”), they would drop an 1/8th of good ol’ American boomers and just start going nutty. Today, the nutty involved a bird—or an oxen, or whatever the fuck it was—that was not really existent at all. “Dude, no way is that bird making tacos.” “Oxen, dude.” “Shut the fuck up.” The persistence in Sam’s voice was strong as he sat there, blankly staring off the porch into the distance at the “bird”. He was a stubborn mother fucker, always insisting HIS hallucinations were the ones that were happening. What the fuck is this Taco-making oxen-bird shirt?, he was thinking, No mother fucking oxen-birds know how to fucking make TACOS! The only thing close to a bird in Sam’s world was the 8 winged beast… “Stuey, dude, his name is Stuey.” Yes, Stuey, the 8 winged beast that was a common place in his world. He was about 15 feet tall and constantly changed colors, all the while giving advice to Sam on what to do. Some days it was which scratch tickets to buy, and at other times he’d choose a porno to masturbate to. The bird was a smart mother fucker, no lie. Today, though, the bird was just kind of chilling out, annoyed at the fact that Sam’s friend, Mel, was totally not seeing him. “Sam, what the fuck is Mel thinking? I’m a fucking Oxen? What the fuck is this, Oregon Trail bullshit? Tell Mel to shut he fuck up and roll another join, he needs to chill the fuck out.” “Mel, my man, CHILLLL OUT! You need to get your mind right, man! You’re seeing all these colors, you’re experiencing all these experiences, and you haven’t even heard to gospel!” Sam reached deep into his pocket, looking for the Blueberry bud he bought earlier. Delicious stuff, man. “The Lord been preachin’, Mel, and he blessed us with the cannabis plant! And now you gotta roll yourself a…oh, heh, oops.” Oops. An empty bag. “Dude, we smoked all that earlier!” Tripping and stoning has its drawbacks. “Oh yeah. Oops.” “No problem man, we got some money…” “No man,” Sam said with a grim voice, “We spent it all on the shrooms and weed!” “No way!” “YEAH FUCKING WAY!” “How the FUCK are we gonna stone now, huh? What are we gonna do until, like…Wednesday, when we get paid? Huh? We just gonna, y’know, do our taxes? Fight the communists? I don’t know what people do sober, man! I’m scared! I’m real scared!” “SILENCE, YOU FUCKING DUMB SONS OF BITCHES!” They looked over at the empty skyline, which to their eyes contained a 15 foot bird-beast with 8 wings named Stuey. “Ohhh, you mean THAT thing with 8 wings. You’d call that a bird? I’d say it’s more, like, an eagle.” “That’s a bird, Mel, you dumb son of a bitch. An eagle is a fucking bird.” “It doesn’t matter what I am, you fuck ups! Last time I checked, you fuckholes have holes in your pockets, and it’s really hard to smoke what you don’t have.” “Tell me about it, dude.” The bird erupted, but not really, since it doesn’t actually exist. So while Sam and Mel cowered in fear as the bird combusted and formed back together again, the rest of the world moved on. “Now, unless you fuckers want to fight the communists…” “NO! NOOOO!” “Then you’ll listen to my idea! Remember when you used to watch wrestling?” “Yeah man! Randy Savage! Hulk Hogan?” “Who are those fuck heads?” “It’s WWE…” “SHHHHH, MEL! We’re e-fedding, not real lifing!” “Oh shit right! Uh…how about…Adam Dick? Doctor Curiosity? Tchu? Digital Mortality?” “Yes, but guess what happened to those little bitches? Eliminated!” “Eliminated? What the fuck are you talking about, Stuey? We just want to get high and you come out here with this wrestling bullshit. Look man, it’s not even real! THE MOVES ARE FAKE! THE STORIES ARE FAKE!” “Exactly!” Huh? What is it with 15 foot tall, drug induced flying beasts and not making sense? “You enter the tournament they were eliminated from! GTT6!” “GTT6? Golden Turnbuckle Tournament?” “Gran Turismo Train?” “Giant Testicle Touching?” The bird shook his head at the stupid fucking stoning sons a bitches. Why the fuck do they hallucinate ME?!, he thought, Can’t they pick Jerry or Carl? Those fuckheads are back at the compound, just chilling… “Great Tit Tube?” “IT DOESN’T MATTER WHAT IT STANDS FOR! All you need to know is if you win this fucking thing, CHA CHING! MONEY UP THE WAZZOO! And you know what that means.” Visions of nuggets danced around their heads. Little mushroom workers whistled as they carried bottles of beer over their shoulders. It was green that was flashing in their eyes, but there were no presidents. “A few problems, though…” “What’s that?” “One, the matches are fixed. Right now they have Sage to win it all…” “Sage?!” ‘Yessir, Sage. He’s a fan favorite, fucking shit up. It’s all angled. So we gotta do a couple things. First, we gotta convince the guy running this boat to make changes…” “How we gonna do that?” “That leads to more problems. We need money to help convince him, and with that money we gotta buy drugs, and those drugs need to get into that president, or tournament director, or whatever it is.” “I see. Well, what after that, Stuey?” “Well, then you need to learn how to wrestle.” “O…k. Is that hard?” “Well, think of it this way: you just need to get a few moves off. If you can speak on the microphone, you’ll be all set.” “Stuey, half the time me and Mel’s mouth are cracked open with drool pouring out.” “Hm…right. Looks like I gotta teach you how to talk, too.” Silence. “So, a few problems, eh Stuey?” “Aye. Let’s get at it, then.” Now, unbeknownst to the two boys and the imaginary bird, Sam was to receive a pay check Wednesday. Essentially, this whole plan was kaput. But that’s the thing about a druggy: a plan is a plan, and unless a better worse-idea comes along, it’ll be stuck with. “Dude, narrator man, that made no sense.” You’ve just been told by a bird to enter for a play in spot in the 3rd round of GTT6 so you can buy more drugs. “His name is Stuey, man, and he’s an 8-winged flying beast.”
