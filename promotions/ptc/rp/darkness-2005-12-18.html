Darkness.

18 December 2005

Date: 2005-12-18 ‘The Greatest Trick The Devil Ever Pulled Was Convincing The World He Didn’t Exist’- Baudelaire This however is not true, for we know that he does. He takes the form of personal demons, which plague our very cores, all our anxieties, all our worries, every little negative aspect, in it’s own little way, is a devil. People also believe in the real devil for one simple reason. They need the fear. It keeps people on edge, at the top of their game, but most importantly, it keeps that grounded in reality. We need to know that evil exists in the world that life isn’t a utopia, we strive, we bleed and we die. Evil is the sole reason that there is good in the world, yet evil could quite happily survive without good, it’s overbearing, consuming and it rests in every single one of us. What I’m trying to say is that no matter how hard you try, you will never be able to beat evil. The devil cannot be beaten. Not really. Even with minor victories over him. He always finds a way back. ~~~ Round one had come and gone. Sixty-Four competitors had been thanked for their time and effort and were now returning home to their respective companies to continue their regular schedule. The tournament for lack of a better word had been cruel on a number of people, names who were deemed to be tournament favorites had fallen at the first hurdle, the talent that remained all felt lucky they had got to where they were, trying not to think too far ahead, simply because they knew that from here on out every round was going to be a bonus. Sure they could hide behind the bravado of their personas, but deep down, every single one of them were worried about their next matches. People seem to think that after the first round, the pressure is off; they couldn’t be further from the truth. It increased, dramatically, once you were in the second round you had already claimed a scalp, people knew what you could do and they expected it again. There was another reason to worry though, something which had rattled the entire tournament to its core, the first round exit of Angelo Deville. Yes, Angelo Deville. Rich Rollins had pulled off, in the eyes of the public, one of the biggest shocks in wrestling history, that said of course anyone who had seen Rollins perform in the past year had seen the change in him, the passion and the hunger he once had, had returned. He was fired up after all he chose to face Deville, he chose to make a point on the biggest stage possible and he had done so with a flourish. Ethan Frost along with countless others had watched as the referees hand slammed down onto the mat for the third and final time, Rollins wearily dragging himself to his feet and raising his arms in success, the disheartened face of Deville as realization kicked in. The arena had fallen into a murmur, the type that grabs every single member in the moment, for a few brief moments they are a part of history, frozen in time, yet watching the events unfold. Did they really just see what had occurred? Ethan Frost had been caught up to, having previously beaten Kid Krazie in a bout which had passed through un-noticed really, a mediocre crowd turned up to watch, mostly UWF and HATE faithful, it didn’t have the drawing power, things could not have been more different for the Deville/Rollins encounter though. The air was tinged with electricity through the entire bout, not a single person dared to move for fear of losing their seat, rumors were flying, that people were cueing around the block begging to be let in, others had packed bars to watch the bout. People would talk about the match of 2005 for a long long time. That was yesterday And this is now. Lying silently, images flashed through Frost’s brain. It was four am and he could not sleep. The hotel room was totally silent, bar the small intakes of breath that are oh so vital to living. The events of yesterday had been circling his mind for hours, unable to shake them. In just a few short hours, the press conference revealing round two would take place. Yet something didn’t quite seem right, he felt on edge, uneasy, like something was going to happen, something bad. There could be many possible explanations for this, anxiety over the quickly approaching round two, the unfamiliar bed or, the unfamiliar idea that memory isn’t just linear, but spherical. Images of the future, some call them foresight, some call them dreams are things which have happened, but not just yet, memory works both ways you see, it’s just that we don’t know how to comprehend the images of the future, so dismiss them as dreams and crazy prophets on the street. But that was still to come and now Ethan Frost simply lay in silence, considering this. Eventually his eyes fell heavy and his breathing deeper, until he drifted sweetly into unconsciousness, his chest rising and falling lightly, rhythmically. If we could understand dreams it would all be simpler, if we could understand what they were trying to tell us, life would be devoid of surprises, dull yes, but at the same time easier. In his dreamlike state Frost allowed his eyes to gaze over the landscape, it was a cold desolate place, no one would come here of their own free will. It was the type of place you were sent as a punishment for something, the sense of being alone, totally different from solitude. Solitude is something that you can choose, time to think, to dream, being alone is the loneliest feeling in the world, even though thousands of people will be feeling the exact same thing. Through the misty haze in front of him he thought he could make out a shape, a figure in the perpetual darkness that was beginning to consume him like a swarm. Suddenly it became clear, like a beacon guiding the way, or a Siren calling through the mists. Although it’s shape seemed to fluctuate in front of Ethan was a unicorn, by definition a creature of great beauty, or so it was said. Yet something here felt wrong, ignoring the shimmering mane and the aura around it, looking into the creatures eyes, revealed it’s true nature. Its eyes glowed red, as though fueled by hatred, by anger. Its head lowered, the horn, appearing to be sharpened from this angle, now directed directly at the chest of Frost. Frost stood his ground, his eyes not moving from the Unicorn’s and almost in a challenging nature, stepped forwards. This confused the Unicorn, in times past it had been known to cause people to flee just at the sight of it, yet this thing on two legs, was daring to challenge him. The Unicorn stomped angrily at the snow beneath his feet. Frost glanced around, he hadn’t noticed it before, but he was standing on fresh crisp snow, the air tasted of ice, sharp and bitter. The darkness had formed a circle around the two now, as though it was watching events unfold, for a moment there was silence until the unicorn charged. It’s eyes burning with rage, it’s target had been chosen, right for the heart, it was the quickest way to end things usually. Frost remained unmoved as the legs of the Unicorn thundered towards him, he closed his eyes and waited, the noise grew, as did the vibrations under foot, every second it was getting closer until Frost thrust his arms out and caught the Unicorn’s horn, skidding backwards in the snow, the momentum carrying him. Still the Unicorn charged on frustrated and angry, that this human had dared to touch him, dared to dephile him. Gradually though the Unicorns steps became shorter and slower until he was not moving at all. Frost still holding onto the horn. He could feel the strength of the mythical creature pushing against him, he was equal to it however, but nothing more he would not be able to beat this creature, deep down he knew that, eventually he would get tired and he wouldn’t be able to hold him at bay any longer. "You can’t win…give up." The words flowed into the very mind of Frost; he knew that no one had spoken them so to speak, but they were there, in the atmosphere, echoing off the darkness that had started to move in on them. "Maybe not, but who is going to get tired first, who will get bored, why don’t we both walk away, it’ll be easier that way. Frost paused; the weight didn’t shift in the slightest the same amount of pressure being exerted on his hands. Then something happened, the Unicorn stepped backwards, its fiery eyes had died down, and in front of Frost now was a docile gentle creature, not the enraged beast of before. "Maybe you’re right, maybe we should learn when to give up the fight." Frost was tentative for a moment, his arms were still outstretched, ready to brace another impact, but none seemed to come, he allowed his arms to fall to his side and enter the pocket of his jeans. The surrounding darkness remained a few feet away, it felt unsatisfied, it knew that something was missing. It was at that moment the Unicorn charged, impaling Frost through the chest, he felt his skin rip apart as the horn of the mythical beast burst through him and out of his back, for a moment he looked into the creatures eyes as the same voice from before entered his mind. "Don’t trust everything you hear." It was then the darkness descended. Cold and full of anguish. The darkness didn’t care what it consumed, nor did it care about consequences all it needed to do was feed, to preserve itself, to take in everything around it. "This was something you could never win. ~~~ Frost sat up sharply and glanced around panting heavily, the night had been replaced by a blistering sunshine that was now shining brightly through his hotel room. He allowed himself some time to compose himself, regardless of how outrageous the dream had been, it had still un-nerved him. He had trusted this creature, it had given him his word, yet at the same time it used that moment of weakness, that moment to attack and ultimately it had led to the end. On the bedside table Frost’s mobile phone flashed frantically, drawing Frost away from the horrible state between asleep and awake, his eyes began to focus on the room around him, the cold beads of sweat still seeping down his face. The time was ten twenty five. He was supposed to leave the hotel room at seven; he’d slept through, having not been able to sleep through the night. "Oh shit" Frost ignored the phone and quickly threw on some clothes and dashed out of the hotel room, the phone could wait. He was supposed to be at a press conference for round two of GTT. The phone continued to ring. ~~~ When Frost finally arrived, sweating profusely the press conference was long over, the tables were being packed away, banners taken down and the press were no longer there, which Frost felt personally was a blessing in disguise, they would probably have come up with a some sordid sex story as a reason for being late and sweaty. As he scanned the conference room a figure he knew all too well was sat staring at him from across the room. This was not the gaze of star-crossed lovers but of a woman, extremely pissed off, as though she’s been stood up at the altar. It was his boss Sam Gordon, UWF commissioner and one of the biggest bitches on the planet, in his opinion anyway. Silently she stood up and walked across the room towards Frost, walking right past him without so much a blinking and into the street. Frost simply followed like a scolded dog, his head hung low, he knew he was about to get an earful, probably something about letting down the company blah blah blah, another fine blah blah blah. At last count Frost had been fined four months wages and had only been there for two. Luckily money wasn’t a problem for him. Gordon, wearing her power business suit came to an abrupt holt just at the entrance to a side road and turned around sharply on her heels. Before Frost could even open his mouth, Sam unleashed her barrage. "I don’t want to hear your excuses! You let the company down, you are a face of UWF and you will not show us up, we are working hard to change the reputation of the company and we don’t need clowns like you ruining that, you’re fined another months wages!" Gordon ranted at Frost, who was trying with all his might to not point out that UWF once had a clown for a world champion, he got the feeling though, that the irony would be lost on her. "I suppose you want to know who you are up against then." With those words, something changed with Sam Gordon, a look of pleasure, took over her face. He hadn’t seen her like this, it was un-nerving, she was actually happy about something, this didn’t bode well for him. Before he even had a chance to reply she said two simple words, which would change Ethan Frost’s life, forever. "Angelo Deville." At moments like this the world often seems to stop. This time it didn’t, Frost just wanted to throw up. "Deville? What? How?" Frost’s eyes were as wide as a man who had just been sentenced to death. Surely it was an admin mix up, Frost was there, he had seen Deville eliminated the day before, he was there when it happened, what was going on? "Well, there were issues, complications, needless to say it is not an error and you have the delight of facing Angelo Deville." Sam Gordon smiled at Frost, there was no warmth, nor comfort in the smile, she was like the cat that had just got the cream. Whilst Sam Gordon was the UWF commissioner, she didn’t like Ethan Frost, this was a well-documented fact, she would much rather he lost than for the companies advancement. There were plenty other competitors left in to represent UWF, but Frost being eliminated was a dream come true for her. "I’ve taken the liberties of forfeiting the match for you, save you the embarrassment." Frost’s mind was still racing, he was going to be in the ring with Angelo Deville, Deville was a legend, hey was the pinnacle of the industry, up until his defeat to Rollins, his standing in tournaments was untouched, his only loss, to Dave Hurst, coming through rolling too far in a German suplex bridge, inadvertently pinning himself. Where as Ethan was just a man who had found luck one day, money fell into his lap and he was able to pursue a long lost dream a past time. His name was going to be in lights so soon into his career, Angelo Deville bought the best out in people and now he was going to… Forfeit. "What?" Frost’s voice was filled with rage, he was normally a man who had total control over himself, he felt he was in touch with his emotions, but this was pure unbridled emotion, which cannot be chained. If she weren’t a woman he would have hit her, regardless of who she was. Sam Gordon stood opposite him, smiling, her arms folded across her chest, this wasn’t business anymore, this was personal. "Ethan, lets face it. You wouldn’t be able to win, thousands of people would see you fail is that what you would really want, to let UWF down, to let yourself down. This isn’t Kid Krazie, this is Angelo Deville, THE Angelo Deville, unlike anything you have ever faced before. Lets face it Ethan" She paused for a moment, taking a number of steps forward towards Frost, who was shaking with rage, his knuckles whitened, a slight trickle of blood flowed from his palms where his nails had dug into the skin. "This was something you could never win" "What was that?" A memory stirred in Frost’s mind, quickly his rage returned. "This isn’t about embarrassment, or letting UWF down, this is about you and me. What is your problem with me bitch? Since day one you’ve had it in for me, why?" Frost was breathing heavily; every ounce of his being was going into not swinging for this woman, his boss and the cause of all his problems. Sam Gordon thought of this for a few moments, seemingly giving it considerable thought before she leaned in a whispered lightly in his ear, her lips brushing it gently causing him to shudder. "I just don’t like you, I’ve had to work hard to get to where I am, every day a struggle, striving for success. You are everything that’s wrong with the world, rich boys who have everything handed to them. Now you are handed the biggest match of your career, something that will make you a star, or at least boost your profile. I’ll be damned if I’m going to let that happen. "Venom flowed from Sam’s lips as she glared at Frost, who was equal to the gaze. The hatred between them reaching boiling point. "It doesn’t matter, I’ll just go and let them know that a stupid bitch in the office sent the wrong form or something, when they see I’m fit to wrestle I’m sure they will over turn the decision and there’s nothing that you can do about it, you may have power in UWF but you have nothing here, this isn’t about you and your sob story life. This is my story!" Frost was now nose to nose with the significantly smaller Gordon, bearing down on her. To an outsider looking in it would have looked bad. An outsider looking in would also have noticed the two burly men sneaking up behind Ethan Frost. An outsider looking in would have noticed the glint of metal in one of their hands. "But Ethan, you aren’t fit to wrestle." An outsider looking in would have noticed one of the men, hitting Ethan Frost around the back of the head and him sinking to his knees. To Ethan Frost the darkness descended and took over once again. "Put him in the van, quickly."
