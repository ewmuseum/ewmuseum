The UWF is holding their Terror in Tokyo Pay Per View here, and Sean is taking a much needed break from the usual trials and tribulations of being a celebrity. If everything goes well he’ll not only be the brother of the UWF Tag Team Champions, but be the new number one contender for the UWF world title by winning the Royal Rumble. The opportunity to add the UWF world title to his list, a feat he never was allowed to accomplish when it was the GIWF.
