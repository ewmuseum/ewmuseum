Recap

Date: 2005-11-26 "I had reached an impasse." This man, unfamiliar to many viewers, stands alone on the vast stage, his voice echoing through the empty auditorium. His attire is smart today, a dark brown pinstripe suit paired with a light blue shirt, brown Prada shoes completing the ensemble. "How to introduce myself to the masses without seeming trite, that was my dilemma." The hall itself is large, with a capacity somewhere in the mid-hundreds. The seats are bright blue, the fabric unblemished, obviously just installed. The walls are a generic white, dazzling in the light that streams in through large windows down one side, the view through them that of a crisp autumnal morning. "Where to start was the first problem. My martial arts training seemed an option…" We remember back to the old footage, in the dojo. A more fresh-faced version of the man before us trading kicks with his mentor. A slightly older version explaining techniques to a class. "But really, it’s too long ago to have any real significance. The person I was then and the person you see now are worlds apart." The man moves over to the lectern that sits mid stage, rests his hands on either side and looks out into the emptiness in front of him. "Moving on then, I considered my time in Japan…" Images of countless tours and innumerable matches flash through our minds. We see the figure getting stretched by llave practitioners and chopped by those that favour strong style, the background of polite applause providing an interesting contrast. "However, that too is irrelevant. I’m not in Japan anymore. By this stage my options were running low. Up next, the UWA, scene of some of my greatest triumphs…" This is more fresh. The big matches, the title reigns, the legendary feuds with Casper, Jester, Taizor - they are all imprinted indelibly on our minds, sharing space with the narrow defeats and the eventual unhappy departure. "That was the place where I made my mark but again, I was different then. Why, you may ask, what could have changed in such a short space of time? A lot of things but mainly me. So UWA was gone as an introduction. This left me with a mere two choices - XCW and FUSE." We remember this so well that it might as well have been yesterday. The wars with ACE, the unholy alliance with CHAOS, the eventual garnering of gold. "Then it hit me, how I was so stupid I have no idea but just then I had what some would call an epiphany. I realised that my past is irrelevant, just like the histories of everyone else in this tournament. We come from all over, bringing different styles, different personalities and different ethos’ to the table. But once the first bell sounds, we are all the same. We are all vying to become GTT5 champion." The man moves off the stage now and begins making his way up towards the back of the hall. "I’m facing Buddy Kingfisher. My first reaction was ‘Buddy who?’ but needless to say I’ve been doing my homework. I know the task before me is not easy but then again, when has it ever been?" As he opens the door to leave the theatre, a though hits him and he pauses, turning around. "How rude of me, I didn’t even tell you my name. It’s Evan Jackson, see you soon Bud."
