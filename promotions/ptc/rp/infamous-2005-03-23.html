Infamous

23 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-23 Infamous 'It was the best of times, it was the worst of times…' A famous phrase by the author Charles Dickens, from his novel A Tale Of Two Cities. The phrase though, was strong, almost like a bond. It described so much, in such little wording. It alone upheld a story in the most bizarre sense, as it is the best way to describe life… At least my life anyway. My names Jesse Michael Fristik the sixth, or as I'm generally referred to as, Jesse Jamester. I'm twenty-eight years old, and have been wrestling for twelve years. My life, well it has hit some bumpy roads. Some above and beyond what I'd have ever expected, yet I've managed. Lately, life seems to be falling into place for me. Like the world around me has taken the burden from my shoulders, a relief it is I must say. I've never thought my life to be easy, but now I'm realizing, when I step back, that theirs not much reason to be worried anymore. That, everything I've ever had to worry about is now past me. See… for once in my life, I'm looking ahead, into my future, into what will be of this man once his career is over. I've finally taken the responsibility to realize, theirs more after I've passed on from the squared circle, and now, I'm searching for that thing I want in my life. Yet, I'm still grasping on to what I have in me. The ring, the company, the legacy… all those terms are familiar to be through this business. It's like cancer in some sense. You get this knack for something, like smoking, or wrestling in my case. And then you start doing it for a long time… but then you realize, it's slowly killing you. Whether it is the cancer you get from cigarettes, or the aches and pains you feel from years of wrestling. Either way, it's a cut in your life span. It's what makes you fall to pieces in your forties, making you crawl from the chair to the phone, and dial that number for the help you said you'd never need. Well, I'm not ready to give up just yet, and I'm not going to. I've still got one last thing to do before I hang up the laces, before the fans see a last showing of the Jackal. What that is, I don't know yet… maybe it's win a title, maybe it's put on one last match, hopefully… it's going out on top. As that, would be ideal. In do time I'll find out what that may be, in do time..." states Jesse as his face is lights up from his charming smile. The camera light flickers a few times before the blue light finally goes off. The woman sitting next to Jesse begins to clap, her hands repeatedly smacking against each other at such a quick pace that it seems she's forcing the claps rather than really showing any care for what the man just said. Her eyes glimmer at Jesse, bright as they can be, before she stands up and shakes his hand. "Thank you so much Mr. Fristik for coming down here and doing this. It definitely means the world to us. I've followed your career for so long, and have had such a great understanding of how wrestlers deal with reality, that I'm so privileged that I could finally get raw emotion such as yours caught on tape. Honestly, this is what will make my career Mr. Fristik, I owe you so much…" the woman babbles on shaking Jesse's hand franticly. Whether it be sincere, or an Oscar performance, she doesn't let go of his hand till she's done talking. "I wish you best of luck in your tournament Mr. Fristik, I'll be rooting for you. Take care," Nodding, his eyes roll as he finally is let go of her vice handgrip, and walks past her. His nerves shot most definitely from all the talk of the tournament and how much pressure is on him. Jesse just walks right out the door without even stopping to say bye to any of the other production workers in the studio. He knows his place, it's not in an office, it's not in a fake world of media like this, but in the ring, bouncing from rope to rope, and doing what he does best… wrestle. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** Swish… Brushing his knee off the canvas and then back off it again, Jesse delivers a double-legged takedown to the air. Stopping after he does the move he analyzes his skill of how he does the move, adjusting his hand slightly as he resets himself and does the move again. It's simple… Skill and speed, it kills. Beads of sweat pierce his eyes, stinging them as it drips. Yet no hesitation by the jackal is necessary as he moves from the canvas to his feet hitting the ropes and then going right back down to his knees for a quick leg take down. … perfection is needed… to win. Huffing, his body is drenched in sweat, yet he doesn't slow down, and doesn't show exhaustion. Grabbing the ropes he whips himself up and over the top rope turning mid-air and landing on the apron with his feet planted firmly. It's all I have left in me… win, or go home. Springing from the apron into the ring, his body does a roll and then as his feet plant the floor, he pushes off and springs up kicking the air leaving another swish echo the basement brick walls. Plunging down from the air as quickly as he sprung up, he lands on his feet and stops. Letting the sweat trickle down his cheeks, soaking his goatee, and causing it to glisten with sweat on its tips. His eyes flicker once, causing the sweat to roll from the corners, and then they close. This is what I've done for twelve years. I know nothing else. I have no other desire. I am what I am… a wrestler. Why is that not enough? Why! He gulps as his dry throat swells like a balloon, before he finally turns and goes to the corner grabbing a bottle of water. Squirting his mouth he swishes it around and then spits it out over the top rope. Staring at the ropes, Jesse gives them a quick flick with his wrists, and then bounces into them and goes back and forth across the ring. Theirs no win in quitting. I have to win… if I want to be something. His body pulsing red with exhaustion, as he stops finally and just stares at the canvas, before falling on his back and looks up at the light above. I can't afford a loss. Otherwise, I become what I was… a nothing. For the first time, he wipes his eyes with his fingers. The sweat still stings, yet the satisfaction of a break is enough to make him not care. Rolling over onto his stomach he stretches his arms out and lays on the canvas, just soaking in the coldness it gives off. I've given so much to be where I am, I can't see myself ever restarting from the bottom again… ever. Closing his eyes, he sniffs once, and then snoozes off. *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** *** I sit here and I ponder. What is wrestling anymore? Is it a home? Or is it a made up job for us who can't cut it in reality? I've gotten so use to having to beat the living day lights out of jobbers, that I forget that they are people who are trying to get by too. It's alright to second guess, or so I've been told. As we all do it, and it's just a second nature to do so. Yet, I don't want to be a hypocrite. I can only do what I know how to, and when I say wrestling is my life, I mean it. I have no formal education, no other areas of interest, wrestling is what makes me complete. Whether I win this tournament or not, I know I'll have to think about this again. The feeling of being lost in the open circle, yet safe by the square around that keeps from falling out. It's a harsh reality out their, but I know it's about time… to hang up these old boots, and move on. Tears stream down the Jackal's navy blue eyes, as his face stares blankly ahead into the mirror. Splashing water onto his face, he turns around and walks to his locker. Opening it up, he takes his mask and looks at it, staring at the torn and re-stitched breaks. The water drips down on the mask, then rolls off of it, like holy water on pure skin, it doesn't leave a mark. Smiling, he unties the drawstring and places the mask on his head, then ties it. Bowing his head, he does the prayer ritual, and turns towards the door, stopping as he is about to step through it. Forever… this time will be infamous to me. Hair standing on the back of his neck he shivers just as he walks through the door and out into the hallway, disappearing.
