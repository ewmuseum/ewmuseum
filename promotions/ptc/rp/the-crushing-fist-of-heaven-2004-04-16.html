The Crushing Fist of Heaven

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-16 The war continues. The sky fills with the intense blue-white color of lightning. It is accompanied by a powerful wind and a rumbling of thunder, the creation of the mythical Raiko, the massive mystical drum of Raijin. Below this stellar scene lies the training grounds of the lightning god himself, the humble dojo that he has utilized to prepared himself, and the place that he has worked so hard to find his humanity. Raijin stands at the entrance to this dojo, looking outward to the night sky, filled with the product of his own summoning. He raises his hand, and just as before, Kenzo steps up behind him, this time holding a black hooded cloak. Raijin holds his arms out, and Kenzo slips the cloak on him. He drops his arms to his sides and takes a deep breath. As he speaks, the words appear below him in English as before, preferring to speak only the language of his native land. For ages men have waged holy wars upon each other, calling for the annihilation of those not of their own faith. In the heavens, gods have waged wars among themselves as well, though perhaps not for the same reasons as men. Rare is the occasion when a god finds himself ready to engage in combat with a man though. Very seldom is the time when men have actively sought conflict with one of the denizens of heaven. Now is one of those times. He lowers his head and pulls the hood on. He turns around, the heavy cloak sweeping behind him. He looks to the inside of the dojo one last time before he ventures outdoors. Something becomes visible upon the back of his cloak, a word written in kana... the word “seisen”... holy war. A word symbolic of what he is about to fall upon with Ethan Knight. He turns back and steps out of the dojo onto the wind-swept field. Ethan Knight is the one. Not only does this man seek to destroy those I am sworn to watch over, but he spreads lies about me and my kind. Though few men have sought war with the gods, this man has found himself having to combat an opponent greater than he has ever seen. This war did not originate by his hand, or by mine, but fate has made certain that we meet in battle, and in defense of my kindred, and in defense of my people, he shall be destroyed before the entire world, so that all might see what lies in store for those who do battle with deities. Wearing the cloak over his body and the hood over his already masked face, his feelings can only be heard within his voice. His face is beyond the view of any and all who try to see it, leaving no person with knowledge of his thoughts. Raijin gazes up to the sky to the sliver of the moon that is visible through the clouds. Though little of it is observable, it seems to radiate brighter than ever before... a message to Raijin from the heavens perhaps. His cloak flutters in the wind. This world to him is but a harvesting ground for new followers, a place where he might augment his power by gathering other weak-minded individuals such as himself to do his dirty work for him. He will come to realize, though, that this world is much more. Every good being on this Earth is my ward, and I must protect them all at any cost. It is the task of all good kami to preserve this world and save it from misfortune and evil, and this is what I shall do here in the defeat of Ethan Knight and the “Old Ones” he so claims to represent and serve. My hatred shall allow me to strike him down more quickly than even he can imagine, and contrary to what he considers to be true, the kami in my heaven are not the wholly compassionate gods that some worship that must love all people and all things. Hatred is an integral part of our existence, something we hold against those who steadfastly defy us with violence toward our people. He seems to hold me to a Christian ideal, whereas the men on Earth who worship the kami living in Tengoku follow an ideology called Shinto, the doctrine that follows our true existence the most closely. His belief of what one in heaven should do and how one from heaven should act is imperfect, for he has never seen within my soul. Those things he has read that he considers canon are but a man’s flawed interpretation of divine truth, twisted within his black heart. It is quite clear that Raijin believes in all he says. His voice is resolute and strong, his divine willpower empowering him, giving him the strength to confront Ethan and all he claims to stand for. Now, more than ever, he seems ready to impose his punishment upon his opposition. If he continues to believe in the false dogma that has been planted within his mind with the intent to disprove and destroy me, he shall find himself falling further and further into a pit of his own making, one that he will never climb out of, and one that I will refuse to save him from. It is then that he shall find no support... none will help him as he is destroyed, his lies silenced forevermore. His fate lies within my hands, and should these hands become stained with his black blood, so be it, for I shall carry out whatever must be done to ensure that fate. The wind unexpectedly dies down. Rain begins to fall, gently at first, but soon it becomes nothing short of a deluge. Once this crusade against Ethan is done, however, I cannot return to my old ways. Before Ethan manifested himself here, I had no use for my true full power, but he has proven to be a more powerful enemy of my heaven than any of us could have anticipated. I must continue to prove my divine dominance in this tournament, for once my true power has been revealed, I must continue to utilize it until my objective in this world is complete. I must prove my worthiness to hold my place in heaven, to watch over this world, to command the forces of lightning, and to call myself Raijin. Kenzo steps out from behind Raijin and stands to one side of him. The wind increases to its former level, driving the ever increasing rain into an almost opaque curtain that veils Raijin and Kenzo. There is a final flash of blinding lightning, and the scene ends. The true false prophet shall fall.
