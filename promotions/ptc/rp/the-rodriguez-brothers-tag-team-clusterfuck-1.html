The Rodriguez Brothers (Tag Team Clusterfuck #1)

Chivo Public Access Pimp Joined: 05 Feb 2003 Posts: 14 Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 8:22 am Post subject: The Rodriguez Brothers (Tag Team Clusterfuck #1) The scene opens upon the cluttered streets of New York City, Times Square. The aroma of vender-cooked hot dogs fills the air, as people rush by, in a hurry for their next meeting, or hustle, or drug deal...Whatever the case may be. The cameras slowly approach a hot dog vender, a rather built man standing with his back to them, ordering a hot dog. The man is wearing a pair of boot cut blue jeans, and a tight black T-shirt, his massive arms and chest bulging from within it, as if they are attempting an escape from their never ending exercise. The camera crew stands behind him as he orders his hot dog... Hot Dog Guy: "Hello Sir, How may I help you?" Chivo: "Yeah, I need a..." Hot Dog Guy: "Wait! Holy Shit! You are Chivo, from XWF!" Chivo: "Yes..." Hot Dog Guy: "Damn! Is it true that you are in the PTC Tag Team Clusterfuck?" Chivo: "Yeah, the rumor is true. I have moved on to bigger and better things...Speaking of how about that hot dog?" Hot Dog Guy: "Oh, yeah, what would you like on it?" Chivo: "I just want your Times Square Special." Hot Dog Guy: "Just one minute sir..." Chivo turns around while he awaits his hot dog, revealing his face to the PTC world. He looks shocked to see them at first but then gives a little "I thought so" smile. Suddenly one of the people among the chaos of Times Square runs into Chivo, rocking Chivo slightly but knocking himself to the ground. The young man looks scared as Chivo stares down at him with his anger filled eyes. The young man turns and jets as the hot dog vender yells for Chivo's attention... Hot Dog Guy: "Sir! Sir! One Times Square Special is ready! There you go!" Chivo: "Oh! Thanks." Hot Dog Guy: "It’s been great serving you! Wow! Chivo ordered a hot dog from my stand! Could you sign this for me?" Chivo grabs the outstretched napkin, marking a quick signature across it for the vender and then turning around. Chivo looks at the crew and then motions for them to follow him as he begins to speak... Chivo: "Before I tell you anything, there is something you must understand about me and how I work...I have been in this business for only a few short years as compared to the veterans who have established themselves here, like the Callisto Bane's, the Chryste's, and even the Kaiowas'...But I am different. I have molded myself around success and at any price. A champion is only as good as what he puts up with to gain it...And I can handle it all." Chivo stops talking long enough to take a bite from the huge Times Square Special in his hands, and then clears his mouth. Resuming his train of thought... Chivo: "Anyway, I have looked over the card and I have decided that since I am scheduled to wrestle, I will be there. I must keep a close eye on the stars now; figure out where my road to PTC success begins. I will be keeping a close eye on Feral, PoE, and Seduction...But this La Envidia Mata...He is almost right. The self proclaimed "God of Wrestling" from this point forward shall be known as "Like ya even Mata" because everyone could give three shits about him... Chivo has arrived now!" Chivo pauses to take another bite of his hot dog while one of the crewmembers began to actually open their mouth and blurt out their thought. Crewman: "But La Envidia Mata is a veteran! He has built up a reputation and in fact had one of the longest undefeated winning streaks in CWF history...If not the longest!" Chivo finishes chewing as he snaps around to look at the cameraman. The man obviously cowers behind the camera as Chivo's eyes begin to enrage with more fury... Chivo: "And you think I am not aware of this? His winning streak...To Me...Is nothing more than a sign of the lack of competition, but he can believe that the competition has arrived and that I am ready. Tell him to bring himself and all his Corporation friends because no Envidia tamer will tame this beast!" Chivo takes another bite of his hot dog and then dispenses the rest into the trashcan as he walks past it. He finishes chewing as the crew follows him down the street, avoiding people as he just pushes through them... Chivo: "Which ever of you see Mata first, let him know. That I am the best of the past. That I am the best of the present. And that I am the best of the future. Just let him know that "The Weapon of Chaos" Chivo has arrived and I have set my sights on him!" Chivo suddenly stops, opening a door and disappearing into a door. The crew stop, not sure what he just entered, so they back away, looking above the door at the name of the building. "Adam and Eve's Coverings: Clothing for the stars". The crew all look at each other and begin the hike back toward the van, through the people... The scene opens outside the now empty arena in Miami. The cameras pan the parking nearly vacant parking lot, the wind blowing paper and trash across it. In the center of the parking lot is a yellow Mitsubishi 3000GT. Leaning against the car is Chezina, alone. He is weaing some blue jeans and a tight black T-shirt, his arms and chest buldging from within it. Chezina sees the crew and walk up to them... Chezina: "Hey, you seen Stevenson?" The crew points toward the direction of the arena garage. Chezina walks across the parking lot, toward the garage, the crew on is heels. Chezina walks into the garage, finding the exact vehicle that he thought he would find, a black stretch limo. Chezina approaches the limo, heading straight for the limo driver standing outside the car... Chezina: "Hey, where is he?" Driver: "Who Sir?" Chezina: "Who? The boss, Charles Stevenson!" Driver: "Oh, we are still waiting sir." Chezina: "Oh good! He will hear me!" Chezina walks around the limo and into the arena, heading straight for the GM's office. Chezina stalks through the bare hallways, quiet for the first time in a few hours. Chezina rounds a corner, running directly into the General Manager, Mr. Stevenson... Chezina: "Stevenson! Just the man I am looking for. I think its time we had our first meeting." Stevenson: "I don't have time now...Reschedule with my secretary tomorrow." Stevenson attempts to cut past Chezina, but Chezina outstretches his arm, cutting him off. Stevenson looks irritated yet scared at the same time with the unstable Chezina standing in front of him... Chezina: "That’s where we run into a problem...You see, this cannot wait!" Stevenson steps back, giving up on getting past him. He looks at Chezina, and then gives in... Stevenson: "Fine...Let's hear it!" Chezina: "Well, it seems to me that I am one of the most talented wrestlers to grace the XWF in awhile, and I think you should consider something?" Stevenson: "Go on?" Chezina: "Consider this? I have had a lot of success in wrestling, I am a superstar professionally, but not on the PTC circuit. I have reigned supreme throghout the XWF promotion and I think a shot at the World Heavyweight championship would be a great shot for me since I already have the Corporate Title." Stevenson: "Its not a huge request? How about soon?" Chezina: " Chezina, World Champion? Yeah, that will do. I would not be asking this request but I had no idea that C.R. would be successful this time though...I would love to prove who the better man is against C.R....again because I gave him a shot at my title and I beat him." Stevenson: "That’s settled then...Can I go now?" Chezina: "Oh, you want to get out of here? Go ahead!" Chezina drops his arm allowing passage for General Manager Stevenson. Chezina turns and watches as Stevenson disappears through the doors of the arena and into his Limo, pulling away... Chezina: "Oh yeah! World Champion Chezina...It definately has a ring to it. Boy, C.R. your ass is off the hook for now, but eventually I will catch up to you and prove that your title reing you have is nothing more than a lack of competition. But speaking of rings..." Chezina pulls his cell phone from his pocket and dials a number. The person obviously picks up... Chezina: "Hey!" Voice: "Hey, whats up?" Chezina: "Where are you at?" Voice: "I am at the bar!" Chezina: "Which one? There are only about 50 of them in Miami!" Voice: "Ummm...I think I am at the Pussy Cat Club?" Chezina: "Damn! You aren't even sure! You already started to tie one on...I have a lot of catching up to do huh?" Voice: "No! I am definately at the Pussy Cat Club and I have tied one on...Tight!" Chezina: "The Pussy Cat Club? That’s not even a bar!" Voice: "Sure it is...I am drinking here, a lot! It’s a bar with boobs. I love it!" Chezina: "I bet you do...I will be there shortly...Where is it at?" Voice: "Somewhere between the arena and the airport? Just look for it on the left!" Chezina: "I will be there shortly...Order me a few beers, Budweiser. I hate that other shit!" Voice: "Alright...One!" Chezina: "Oh, I have good news too, but it can wait...I’ll tell you at New Era brother!" Chezina hangs up his phone and walks through the areana, coming out through the back door closest to his 3000GT. Chezina walks out, hoping in his car and lighting the tires up as he speeds away toward The Pussy Cat Club to meet Chivo... _________________ Current XWF World Champion Former XWF Corporate Champion Former XWF Xtreme Champion Former XWF Puerto Rico Champion Member of The Rodriguez Brothers and the man that will kick DVD's @ss this Sunday.
