Meet Michiru Kaiou

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 The scene opens inside the "Egg Dome" in Tokyo, Japan and a mass of media from the US, Europe, and of course, Japan are all assembled for a chance to meet the newest wrestler to emerge from the "Army of Old School" in Michiru Kaiou and her manager Joshua Curtis. Michiru walked out first wearing a sky blue suit jacket with a white shirt and tie, matching dress pants and black loafers. Joshua wore a complete black suit with a white shirt and tie and matching loafers and bowed to the assembled media as he walked out to show proper respect to everyone before they took their seats at the table and began to speak… Joshua Curtis - Good Evening. Tonight we are gathered here because one of the newest competitors in the wrestling ring in Michiru Kaiou is debuting in the ring at PTC’s GTT Tournament. We in the "Army of Old School" are pleased that she has wanted to go into the tournament and know that she will represent us well….ok the floor is yours. Reporter #1- Michiru there are reports that you are in negotiations with feds here in Japan and have been seen freelance wrestling for a while now trying to catch on with a federation. Is there any truth to this rumor? Michiru Kaiou - I’ve been wrestling in Japan extensively yes that is true, but not so much to become a member of a federation. I generally have been competing here because Joshua wanted to get me "battle ready" for the tournament. Reporter #2 - How long have you known you’d be the one going into the tournament? Michiru Kaiou - Since I heard Ami was recovering from an illness and Joshua needed a representative to go to the tournament and I was very willing to go for him. Reporter #3 - Any particular opponent you want to face in the tournament? Michiru Kaiou - Well seeing as how this is my first tournament…check that first match ever ALL the matches are matches I’m looking forward to but if you asked me one person I would love to face I’d say that I’d love to face Tessa Windsor in the tournament because much like myself she is also fighting for a chance to be signed with a company. Reporter #1 - Is there any concern that due to the reputation of your manager that you might not get signed to a company? Surely that has to weigh on your mind? Michiru Kaiou - That doesn’t worry me though. To me I’m playing with the house’s money just by being in round one and any returns on the investment at this point are 10 fold! Reporter #1 - Josh what are your expectations for Michiru in this tournament? Joshua Curtis - My expectations? I expect Michiru to do her best in this tournament. I expect the "Army" to be well represented and I’m hoping that with her showing in the tournament she can get signed on with a company but even if she can’t I’m proud of her stepping up to enter the tournament in the first place! Reporter #2 - Michiru they call you the "Anti-Karina Wolfenden" correct? Michiru Kaiou - *Laughs* Yes that is true. Reporter #2 - Why? Michiru Kaiou - Because darling Karina is a supremely angst type of person and Joshua has followed her closely since he’s been around PTC. Joshua wanted to have someone represent the "Army" whom didn’t walk around with a chip on her shoulder and go "Woe as me" all the time so he asked me to go to the tournament for him to show just how a "classy" woman can be just as competitive as someone like her. Reporter #3 - What would you consider someone like Tessa Windsor? Michiru Kaiou - I LOVE her! She is something like me in that she doesn’t have to dress like a two-bit prostitute and she is someone that I could see myself wrestling against in a federation down the line. Reporter #2 - Federation of choice? Michiru Kaiou - WWA, AWC, PRIME, HSW…you decide! Reporter #1 - If you and her got signed to the same fed would you try to align yourself with her or would you want to face her more? Michiru Kaiou - Good question…I’ll have to get back to you on that. Reporter #1 - Are you trying to dodge the question? Michiru Kaiou - Hardly my dear! Its just that right now I’m looking more on trying to get a spot in a fed and going somewhere in this tournament. If I can accomplish at least one of those goals I’ll be happy with myself. Joshua Curtis - I would just like to say that if any of you think that I’m trying to use Michiru to get hooked up with a fed myself then that’s just bull shit! I want to see Michiru succeed in the tournament and face those that she wants to face! Any more questions? *reporters remain silent* Joshua Curtis - All right then. Thank you and good day!
