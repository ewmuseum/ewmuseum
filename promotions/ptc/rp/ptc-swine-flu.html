IG 64 Promo - Swine Flu.

26 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Santa Maria, California Awakening from the black out I just had, my first reaction is to look around and see if whatever it was that was chasing after me is around anywhere in my vicinity. Sitting up on the ground, I take a moment to shake the cobwebs out. Looking around from side to side, noticing that I am inside of an empty apartment building. See an open door to my left and crawl inside. I see that I am alone in a seemingly vacated apartment. Maybe it has something to do with the mortgage crisis. It has hit my area pretty hard so that wouldn't be too far fetched. Outside I hear scratching on the outside wall. I duck back down on the ground and look toward the window across the room only to see a shadow when I do so. Thinking quickly, I see a dusty old couch over by the same wall where the window is located. Crawling on the floor, I use it as cover as whatever it is outside goes away. My heart beats ten thousand beats a second as sweat beads roll down my forehead. Slowly peering over the couch, nothing seems to be outside the window anymore. Taking my sweet time, I finally rise to my feet and stretch. This gives me the chance to look around for something to eat. I've got a hangover right now that's killing me. Having the munchies at the same time isn't helping matters at all. The kitchen seems like the best place to look judging by the state of things in the apartment. Walking over there, the dust on the counter lunges at my nostrils like a tractor beam pulling me in just to make me sneeze. I can feel the sneeze coming and try to stop it. My face probably looks like it just got hit by a frying pan at this point. Trying as hard as I can, I finally get the sneeze to stop only to sneeze moments later. Yes, this really helps me with hiding my position from whatever it was that was trying to get at me before I blacked out. Wait to be attacked by something for a few moments but nothing happens. Go to the fridge and open it. Not too much inside. However there is no power so it's probably all spoiled at this point anyway. I risk it and go for the pizza box on the second of the three shelves. Grab the box and yank it out of the fridge before I close the door. However, as I do this, the last slice of cheese pizza falls out of the box onto the floor by my feet. Looking down at the slice, I shrug and reach down to the floor. Picking up the slice, I bring it to my nose and sniff it. Smells alright so I start to eat it as I put the box on the stove. Gaze around some more and notice a pair of black sunglasses on the kitchen counter. Naturally I walk over and grab the sunglasses. Put them on with one hand before using the other hand bring the pizza to my mouth for another bite. Suddenly it hits me. There might be some news on the television. Look around and see a flat screen over by the floor where I was laying so I go over and press the power button. Walk to the couch and sit down as I wait about two or three minutes, which seems like forever, for the Digital Cable to actually turn on. Once it finally comes on, there's nothing but static on the airwaves. Grabbing the remote and flipping through the channels, the static becomes more annoying with each passing station. Now I know how the Iranians and Chinese feel. Finally I turn off the television in disgust before sighing and trying to figure out what to do now. I'm alone, stuck in the industrial zone of town, and inside of an apartment that is not mine. What am I going to do now? This gets me thinking. I continue to think...and think some more. Boredom finally gets to me and I stand up from the couch. Look down at my right forearm and see a phone number written in big, blue letters on my arm in misshapen letters. [detail withheld] Raise an eyebrow. Don't remember where that came from. Shrug my shoulders and head for the door. Slowly open the apartment door and peer outside to make sure whatever it was that was trying to get at me is not outside anymore like it was before. After waiting a few seconds to die, no one is out there so I step out casually after closing the door behind me. The open courtyard in the apartment complex for some reason makes it seem like I'm more open for an attack. Quickly I scamper for the apartment building entrance that I came in from. Coast is clear so I exit from the building onto the sidewalk where I was to begin with. Turn to my right and see the factory that exploded that caused all of this mess. Smell the smoke. It's probably covered the entire city with its putrid smell. The fire inside the factory is still blazing. I can see it from my position very clearly. Looks like a catastrophic event has taken place. Another boom comes from the factory. Duck and cover but nothing flies in my direction. Notice some emergency and police vehicles nearby but no one around. Silence fills the streets as I wander around outside once more. The only noises are hear are from my own footsteps on the pavement below me. It's a really eerie experience to have. This must be how it feels to be the protagonist of a horror movie. At this point I'm just waiting for something to lunge out at me before I die. Most likely the video of my death will end up being a Youtube hit thanks to some cameraman hiding in the bushes who tapes the whole thing. As my mind wanders, I hear something coming close to me. I listen closely. It sounds like the footsteps of someone running. Looking toward the factory again, the being that is making all the noise is running right toward my position. Suddenly a young woman comes running toward me. Something is behind her but I don't even want to know what she is running away from. "RUN!" she yells at me. Without hesitation I turn and run after her. Following closely behind her, I hear loud noises behind us but I am too scared to take a look. Sounds like some sort of creature from a science fiction movie or something to that effect. This causes me to run even faster than I was before. What the hell is going on? Maybe I'll find out whenever we find a nice hiding spot and stop running. Still hear noises behind us as I turn down another street, following the mysterious young woman toward another set of buildings. More loud, strange noises behind me. Running as fast as I can, I'm even having trouble keeping up with her. She's like the Flash or something. She points to an empty store and yells "IN THERE! QUICK!" We quickly run across the empty street and inside of the store. We close the door behind us after going inside. Quickly pulling down the security gate on the inside of the store, we then run to the back of the empty store. Looking around, I try to find something for us to hide behind. However she beats me to it and runs behind a large shelf filled with sporting goods. I quickly run over and hide beside her. Hear some more noises outside. Don't know what is behind them. Hearing her breathing heavily beside me, I turn and give her a look. She seems to be frightened out of her mind by whatever she saw outside. Looking more closely, she appears to be maybe nineteen or twenty. She is wearing a red tank top and white shorts. Her shoes are also white. Her black hair is scrunched up into a ponytail. At this point I try to catch my breath. There's some crazy stuff going on and I have no clue about any of it. Strange feeling to have. To be in the middle of a newsworthy event and have no clue exactly what it is. Turning to her, I finally decide to see what's going on. "You ok?" I whisper to her. She looks at me and nods her head. "Yes," she whispers back. "Thank you for saving me," I whisper back to her before motioning toward the doors. "What the hell is going on outside?" She turns to look at the doors. I can tell that she knows something but is hesitant to say what it is. Finally, after a few moments, she finally speaks up about what she's seen. "They...they did it," she mutters out, shaking a lot as she does so. This takes me aback a little bit when I first hear these words come out of her mouth. Many thoughts begin swirling through my head. Some realistic, some not so realistic. I need more answers to see which direction the rest of this day is going to go. "They?" I ask as quiet as I can. "They who?" She looks like she's about to tear up at this point. "The government," she replies as the hairs on the back of my neck stand up. "The government did it!" She starts to weep for a moment as the shock of what is going on suddenly rushes through my entire body. She wipes away the tears before continuing. "I heard some of the scientists they dispatched here talking a few blocks away from the factory," she says. "They were talking about some sort of satellite that fell. The satellite was experimental and was being used to somehow test how the weather effects infectious viruses in space." "However, when the satellite crashed on the factory here, they found some side effects of a virus. It mixes with other viruses and makes them into a more potent super virus. It mixed with a virus from Earth upon crashing down. The swine flu, to be exact. The mixture of the mutagenic space virus they brought back and the Swine Flu created a virus even more potent and deadly than they anticipated." I raise an eyebrow at this point not knowing what to think. "So you're telling me that they created some sort of a deadly super virus on accident?" I ask. She looks in my direction and shakes her head. "No," she responds. "This was no accident. They planned this. Some sort of weapon to use in the case of a biological war or to use on enemy nations. They just didn't know what would happen or what virus it would mesh with." "So what happened with the virus?" I ask. "It turned people into monsters," she replies. "And the government guys are just as bad. They tried to kill me before I ran into you." The look on her face scares me more than anything else so far. It's a mixture of fright, melancholy, and shock. The whole situation seems a bit preposterous but I'll take her word for it. She seems scared to death and that makes it all too real to me. "What's your name?" I ask upon realizing I have no idea what the young woman's name is. "Stephanie," she replies. "James Monroe," I reply, offering a handshake which is accepted. I then look around the store, taking a peek around before turning back to Stephanie. "We need to find weapons while we still can," I say as I look around once more. "If the government and whatever the infected people are turning into are both going to try and kill us, I think we should be prepared to fight for our lives." "What kinds of weapons can we find in a store like this one?" she asks. "Good question," I respond before turning to look at some of the sports equipment on the shelves we're hiding behind. The first things that stand out are a hockey stick and a baseball bat. Smiling, I reach over and grab one each. I hand her the hockey stick while I hold onto the baseball bat. There are more but I only grab one of each. You never know what will be effective and what wont. Looking around some more, I find nothing more than some baseballs, basketballs, volleyballs, and some running accessories. That's about it. This doesn't make me too optimistic about our chances going outside and surviving this government created Swine Flu epidemic. I then start crawling around the floor of the store as Stephanie is motioning for me to come back. I turn and shake my head before continuing to go look for some more weapons. I for sure am not going outside unless I'm armed. Reach the food area and it's basically barren of weapons so I keep crawling around on the floor. Keep finding nothing but food on the shelves. The fact that it's dark and I'm lugging around a baseball bat in one hand isn't helping matters either. After a few minutes of looking, all I can find is a BBQ poker and a long, red BBQ lighter. Other than that, there's nothing else of use for us unless we go on a picnic or something but I don't see that happening in this situation. Head back over to the sports area where Stephanie is hiding. She's still there when I come back and still seems a bit frightened. "Didn't find anything much that could be used," I whisper to her as she turns towards me. "We need to wait here until something happens and not give up our position at any cost." She then has a look of fright on her face. I haven't seen a look like that since I saw the movie Quarantine last year at the theaters. Well, I saw that look in a porno once last year too but that's another story. "What's wrong?" I whisper. to her. Shaking, she points her finger behind me. Looking puzzled, I turn around and see three men in white full body suits like the kind the government scientists wear in the movie ET: The Extraterrestrial. They look like astronauts or something along those lines with the outfits they have on. One of them points at us as we freeze upon seeing them. "Get them," he says coldly, pointing at us as he does. As his two scientist minions come walking toward us, I pull out my baseball bat and whack the two of them in the stomach before grabbing Stephanie by the hand and running toward the back of the store. We find the back area door and run through. Just like the actual store, it is devoid of any signs of life. With Stephanie in hand, we see the back exit and run for it. Hear the scientist goons somewhere behind us. Their footsteps are very close to us too which only makes us move with more haste. Going through the exit doors, we arrive outside in the back area behind the store. We turn to the west and head in the direction of the hills that my journey started off at this morning. However, we stop when we are confronted by something that scares the holy hell out of both of us. Stephanie shrieks and I clutch onto her as we run right into two infected humans. However they do not appear to be totally human anymore. Now they have pig facial features and now have mutated, misshapen (but still humanoid) bodies. This most likely is the mutagenic side effects of the Swine Flu mixing with the outer space virus that came here when the satellite fell. "HOLY SHIT!" I exclaim as the two creatures lunge at us. With Stephanie still in hand, I run with her back toward the exit that we came from. Running past it, we just miss the three government scientists coming out. In turn, the two infected Swine Flu people then veer off from chasing us and attack the scientists instead. Hearing the screams in the background, we run onto the street and head for the quietest direction, which is east. Still running, we hear noises behind us. I turn and look to see more government scientists chasing after us. Running as fast as we can, we head away from town. More noises and screams. We stop and look to see the scientists now fighting a massive horde of infected. "RUN!" I yell as the two of us continue to run. We run as fast as we can, running and running until there's no more city in sight. We're in the hills outside of town before we finally stop to take a break. I'm puffing and wheezing. She's out of breath too. Looking around, I see that the coast is clear. We then lay down and take a break from running. "What the hell were those things?!" I ask in a shocked and frightened tone. "And is it just me or does the United States government always seem to be behind every sort of viral outbreak and apocalyptic event like this in movies? Seriously, this is like the frickin' Stand for crying out loud!" She nods her head and replies, "I agree. This is kind of like 28 Days Later," "You know there's no way they can contain this," I reply quickly. "I mean, that virus wiped out an entire city in less than four hours. I think this is the end of us all." "They probably will want to kill us and cover up all the evidence that they were behind this," I continue. "So we'd better make this rest a brief one." "Agreed," she replies with a nod of the head. We lay down on the grass in that covers the hilly area and rest for a few minutes. It's very quiet now. Nothing seems to be alive anywhere near us. Maybe the government nuked them already or killed them all with chemical weapons of some kind. Today's been crazy. This is the craziest this town has been since the Michael Jackson molestation trial a few years ago. The whole city went from being a peaceful little seaside village into an apocalyptic nightmare sometime between my first drink last night and me wandering back to the city this morning. It has been something like out of the Book of Revelations today. These events must be what the ancient prophets saw in their visions. I wouldn't believe all of this if I hadn't seen it all for myself with my own eyes. Just then I hear a helicopter in the distance. The sound of it makes it sound as if it is headed in our direction, which would not be good for us. Stephanie and I both look at each other for a second. We both appear to be thinking the same thing. Without hesitation, we both get up off of the grass and start running in the direction of the Pacific Ocean to the west of us. The hills seem to be endless as we run over them. Closer and closer the ocean appears before us. I can hear the waves crashing the closer we get to the beach. Finally, after what seems like forever, we reach the beach. Behind us I hear a loud boom. Turning around, we see a mushroom cloud rising from the city. Can't believe what I am seeing. It's like something out of a movie created with CGI effects. However, I thought the same thing when I first saw post-Katrina New Orleans when the city was engulfed by water and New York on 9/11. Both of those incidents were real and that leads me to believe that this is too. Looking more closely at the cloud in the distance, I notice a large shockwave coming from the blast. It's slowly getting closer to us. We don't have much time. Either we find a place to hide and protect ourselves from it or we die. It's that simple. When we reach the beach, we run south away from the wave that is coming towards us. Looking desperately around, there is seemingly no place to hide. However, we soon come across a pipe that leads somewhere. Most likely it is part of the city's sewer grid. "IN THERE!" I yell frantically. "IN THERE!" We quickly head for the pipe and climb inside. She goes first and I follow. Going in to the pipe, the wave hits and we hold each other tight as the ground shakes all around us. We continue to hold each other for a few minutes until all is clear. Looking toward where we came in the pipe from, I notice that the entrance is blocked now. I turn and look in the direction behind us. "I guess we're stuck in the pipes," I mutter as I start crawling. "Yeah," she responds. "And with no flashlights either." "Shit," is all I can say in response to that. I can't blame her for saying that either because that's what it smells like in here. Smelly, stinking human feces. I hope we don't run into any sewer rats. The smell gets even worse the further we go in. After crawling around for a few minutes, we find an area big enough to stand up in. Rising to our feet, we continue to trudge through the muck and grossness that is beneath our feet. "Where are we?" Stephanie asks as we both look around. "I have no idea," I reply a few moments later. "But we can't stay in here. They'll find us. We have to keep moving." I start to walk and Stephanie is right behind me. Our footsteps splash the water beneath our feet. Hear drips all around us. Nothing seems alive where we are except for the cockroaches that are crawling all over the ground. Trekking through the sewers is much harder than I thought it would be. Both of us are having problems as the water level beneath our feet is slowly rising. However there are noises in the distance. We both stop and listen. Drips of water are the first things we hear. But soon we hear clawing noises and a scream followed by something dropping to the ground. We stand still and wait but hear nothing after that. Quietly, I motion for her to follow me. At this point we tip-toe through the sewer system heading toward where the noises came from. Continuing on, we finally see a light in the distance. Running over toward the light, I see that it is not natural light. It is coming from a flashlight. However, the flashlight is next to something else. Stephanie sees it and screams. Pointing the light of the flashlight at it, it's a dead body of a scientist like the ones we saw before in Santa Maria. This scares me. Never seen a dead body before until now. My body starts to shake. Suddenly there's a growling noise next to us. This makes me shake even more. Pointing the flashlight toward the noise with my flashlight, I see that it's another one of the mutated infected Swine Flu people that we see back in town. However this one is different from the others. This infected person is much bigger than the others were. It also has clawed hands, big tusks, and looks almost like a feral warthog that you would see somewhere in the wilds of Africa. Looking into the eyes of the beast, I remember the baseball bat I have in my other hand. The infected creature snarls at me and the takes a swipe at us. Stephanie freaks out and ducks to the floor. This leaves me to face down the giant creature. It snarls and growls before charging at me. I duck an attempt to claw my face, blocking it with the bat before I loop around behind it. The beast sees Stephanie and is now attempting to attack her. Swinging the bat, I smash the beast hard in the back as it howls out in pain. It then turns around and lets out a primal roar in my direction. The beast then takes another swipe at me. Again I block it with the bat. As I duck and roll backward, I get back to my feet to attack it. Holding the bat to attack, it seems lighter for some reason. Looking over, the first thing I notice is that it is missing the entire top half. All that's left of the bat makes it appear to be more like a sword if anything. Quickly I turn back and see the creature bearing down on me. I'm screwed and not in the good way. It lunges forward and I hold out the remnants of the bat. As the creature charges, it runs right into the bat. The bat's now spiked pierces the beast in the chest and it falls over onto me. For a moment I'm expecting to be flying up to Heaven guided by a blinding light. However, nothing happens. Taking a moment to look at what happened, the creature appears to be dead. The remnants of the bat stabbed it in the heart most likely killing it instantly. I get up and walk over to Stephanie who gets up to her feet. Just then we hear a lot of footsteps and see lights. Turning, we run like hell in the other direction before we get captured by the government scientists that started this mess to begin with. We don't stop. We just run until we reach the light at the end of the darkness, hoping that it will take us to salvation and not the perpetual damnation that I'm expecting us to find at the end of the tunnels. This will not have a happy ending...
