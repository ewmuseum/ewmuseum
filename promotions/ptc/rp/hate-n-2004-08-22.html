Date: 2004-08-22 Tattoos. Ink injected into the skin. There is no more permanent form of marking. It is painful to have done, and painful to have erased. After nearly five hours of needles stabbing into his flesh, Seymour Almasy’s artwork has been finished. He is now marked, literally. Just as in his mind, he has been metaphorically marked by fans too blind to see the real evil in the world. As such, his only current ally is one like him. A woman marked in the exact same way. ------ “Wake up, Seymour…” Seymour Almasy felt his body being pushed and prodded. He was aware that his eyes were closed, and slowly opened them. At first, he had no idea where he was. But his eyes opened, and the sight of a beautiful Japanese woman brought the memories of last night screaming back. Seymour Almasy: What happened, Aya? Aya: You practically passed out the second your tattoos were finished. Not that I could blame you, it took him until 7:00 AM to finish. Seymour Almasy: 7:00 AM?! What time is it now? Aya: 10:00. I remembered you have a flight at 12, so I figured that I should probably wake you up. Seymour Almasy: Thank you… Slowly, Seymour staggered to his feet, getting off of the cheaply carpeted apartment floor, and looking at himself in a mirror across the room. Seymour Almasy: Ah. It’s even better than I’d pictured it. Aya: Told you he knew what he was doing. Seymour Almasy: Did I manage to pay him before I passed out? Aya: Yes. He kind of insisted on that part. But he’s out now, taking care of some more yakuza-oriented errands. The prostitute looked at Seymour. His eyes were much calmer than they had been last night. It seemed as if he had been possessed, even as he had approached her on a corner, and purchased her services. Now, he almost seemed sobered… Aya: Regretting last night? She asked the question with a tinge of dissapointment. Thus, she was pleasantly surprised to hear Seymour’s strong response. Seymour Almasy: No. Not at all. Aya: Good. I was hoping you wouldn’t. You really aren’t in the wrong here, you know. Seymour Almasy: Thanks for saying that, but as much as I appreciate it, most people aren’t going to agree with you. Aya: Fuck most people. They don’t know you, who the hell are they to judge you? Besides, I know what it feels like to be looked down on by people. Fucking high and mighty pricks thinking I’m a bad person because I’m a prostitute. It’s not like I ASKED for this… Immediately, she realized she’d given Seymour an opening to ask her about her life. She had brushed his questions off last night in the afterglow of wonderful sex, but now, she doubted she could repel them. Aya: Eh, fuck it. I may as well tell you. My mother was a stupid bitch who didn’t save her money, and my dad left us at an early age. She got a job in America and left me to rot when I turned fifteen. I don’t know how much you know about Japan, but the culture’s somewhat degrading to women. Watch our pornography and you might understand a bit better. Anyway, in addition to that, we don’t discuss sex in public a lot, so prostitutes may as well be lepers. Yeah…that’s how I ended up where I am today. Learned English in school…fuck. Going from straight As in school to selling myself to American tourists on a street corner… Almasy reached into the pocket of his pants. There, he felt his plane ticket back to the United States, and the second ticket that had been meant for his wife. The solution to the problem of an extra ticket was evident. Seymour Almasy: You want to come back with me? I’ve got a spare ticket that my wife’s surely not going to use. Aya: I don’t want charity, Seymour. Seymour Almasy: It’s not charity. You want to know why I really want you to come with me? Because I want to get laid again, and I don’t want to fucking pay for it this time. The words sounded ridiculous coming from Almasy’s mouth, and both realized I. Aya laughed loudly, before shaking her head, and giving in. Aya: Why the hell not? It’s not like sitting on a street corner’s going to get me anywhere. Seymour Almasy: Good. I need someone. She smiled at him, but then suddenly shook her finger at him, practically shoving it in Seymour’s face. Aya: You’re not going to get all lovey-dovey on me now, are you? Seymour Almasy: No. But that also doesn’t mean that just because now a lot of people hate me that I can’t still be a person and have friends, does it? Aya: Absolutely not. Seymour Almasy: Especially a friend with such notable benefits… Leaning forward, she kissed Seymour firmly on the mouth., a gesture that he eagerly returned. Aya: Hells yeah! Besides, when you’re fucking for love, you’re not focusing on the feeling aspect of it. You’re focusing on the other person, the scene, and all of that other shit. It’s just like I told you….you’ve got to look out for yourself. No one else will. Seymour Almasy: Not even you. Aya: Absolutely right. Right now, we’re friends… Seymour Almasy: But that could change at any time. Aya: Right again. But while we’re friends, I see nothing wrong with enjoying ourselves. Seymour Almasy: Good. Neither do I. The former Global Champion made a move towards her, but Aya held up a hand. Aya: Not yet, tiger. We’ve got a plane to catch, remember? Seymour Almasy: Damnit, that’s right… In the rush of self-discovery, Almasy had nearly forgotten about his new mission. A mission that began by recapturing the title that was once his. Seymour Almasy: I need to get back in time to go find some training partners. Got a whole bunch of new ideas on how to torture people that I want to introduce Saij and Jamester to. Aya: Ah, it’s always nice to see a man with ambition. Seymour Almasy: My entire strategy for the past two years has been to absorb blows until an opponent makes a mistake. What the hell was I thinking? I’ve got the ability to take it to an opponent from bell to bell. I’m going to bumrush whichever of those idiots is unfortunate enough to look at me the wrong way. Even as he said it, though, he didn’t especially feel it. Aya had proven herself to be quite intuitive already, and she wasn’t quite done proving it yet. Aya: Look, Seymour…don’t feel the need to be as bitter and loud as I am. It took me quite a few years to get like this. Hell, hopefully, you won’t ever end up like this. The fans will realize that this guy’s a jackass, start cheering you again, and everything’ll be fine. Still though…you don’t need to try to act tough around me. Besides, you’re not going to be able to fool me by inserting ‘fuck’ in between every other word. Your innocence is kind of charming…if I was the type of person to say such a thing. Seymour Almasy: Alright then. I’ll try to act a bit more like myself. Aya: Good. Then, how do you REALLY feel about all of this? He thought about the question, a bit harder than he had on the past occasions he had pondered his new, foreign situation. The usual anger still flared up…but there were deeper feelings… Seymour Almasy: I feel hurt. Used. And, to a lesser extent, guilty. Aya: Well, hurt and used are obvious. When people start booing you after cheering you for a long time, that can lead to such feelings. But why guilty? Your wife? Seymour Almasy: Yeah…she was willing to go along with me. Willing to do whatever I wanted to do to get vengeance on those who wronged me. But I had her attacked by one of my new ‘allies’. And, now that I keep thinking about it, I really have no idea why. Aya: It’s because you hate this guy. A lot. Perhaps even beyond reason, but there’s nothing you can do about it. So you did whatever it took to put yourself in a position to finish him off…anything it took. Seymour Almasy: And then I wanted to run out and justify it, so I found you, to prove that I really didn’t miss her. Aya: But you do, don’t you? Seymour Almasy: …yes. Aya: Let me give you a piece of advice. If you really want to get revenge on the fans and this guy…don’t make yourself miserable in the process. The best thing that you can do is to go back to America and win this title match. More importantly, go back and apologize to your wife. She’s not going to take you back immediately. She might not take you back. But apologize to her. Eventually, she may forgive you. If not, go find someone else that makes you happy. Living well truly is the best revenge. Almasy shook his head. It was something that he hadn’t thought about. In his rush to get vengeance for what he considered a grevious wrong, he hadn’t thought about the impact that his plans would have on him. Seymour Almasy: You know, you’re right. I can’t believe I screwed things up like this… Aya: Don’t be too hard on yourself. Things like this happen. Every makes mi-- Seymour Almasy: Don’t say ‘mistakes’. Having my wife attacked was a mistake. Meeting you and doing what we did was not. Aya: You’re just saying that because I’m a damned good lay, aren’t you? Seymour Almasy: No, I’m not. Today, you’ve done more for me than you can possibly imagine. Much more than you could ever do by getting me off. Aya: Well then, I’m flattered that I could be of help. Come on, let’s get you out of here. You’ve got a plane to catch. Seymour Almasy: My offer’s still on the table, Aya. Her jaw dropped. She had to admit that she was caught off guard. Even after making him realize that he still wanted to be with his wife, he would still give her his spare ticket. Aya: Thank you, but I don’t want char-- Seymour Almasy: It’s still not charity. And like I said earlier, you’re my friend. You’re right. My wife may never forgive me. Even if she does, you’re a friend now. And I like to surround myself with my friends. Aya: So, what the hell does this make us? Seymour Almasy: Friends…and if my wife’s still not going to forgive me, friends with benefits and perhaps more in time. I realize that you’re not the type of person who can love, and I’m really not right now either, so let’s not even worry about it. You can come with me to watch my match, watch me get some revenge on the idiot fans by becoming the Global Champion for a second time. Aya: It’s not going to look good to your wife when you come home with the prostitute that you slept with. Seymour Almasy: I don’t care. I want you to come with me. If my wife takes me back, I’ll be faithful to her until the day I die, but that doesn’t mean that I should never be able to talk to you again. This is what I want. And finally, I’m going to get what I want. Aya: If they’re going to hate you, Seymour, give them a reason to. We don’t hate people who are assholes who hate themselves. We hate the successful assholes. We hate arrogant pricks who somehow have everything we’ll never have. We tell ourselves that we would never ever act like that, when in reality… Seymour Almasy: It is the very thing that we crave. Being the geek in high school, you try to make everyone believe that you don’t give a damn about popularity, but of course, you do. You’d sell anything, do whatever it took to be the very asshole that you hate the most in the world. Aya: Damn straight. And you don’t know it Seymour, but you’re almost there. You’ve got to be in your early twenties. Seymour Almasy: Twenty-three. Aya: You’ve been all over the world, done things most people couldn’t dream of doing. And, take it from me, you’re built like a fucking god. You can have damn near any woman you want. But if you’re not happy….it doesn’t matter. If you really want revenge…you’ve got to beat Saij and Jesse Jamester. You’ve got to take that fucking title belt and raise it in front of everyone, and you have to drip style as you do it. Seymour Almasy: I have to shrug, like I’ve been there before. Wrap that title around my waist, and look down upon my fallen adversaries like the insignificant creatures that they are. Aya: And then, your beautiful valet will enter the ring, and drape herself around you…sort of like this… The beautiful Asian woman stood, approached Seymour, and wrapped her arms around his neck, pressing her body to his. Aya: And then, she’ll kiss you, making it absolutely clear that it’s only a FRACTION of the celebration that is to come… It played out exactly as she stated. The kiss ended far too soon for Seymour’s liking, and left him quite, well, motivated. Seymour Almasy: You know, I think I’m going to like getting my revenge… Aya: You most certainly will. But for now, it’s time to get to the airport. Almasy still had many questions. He had to sort out exactly what he felt for this woman he had just met. He also needed to prepare himself physically and mentally for the challenge of Jesse Jamester and Saij. But now, he had the one thing that he hadn’t last evening. Last night, he was a man filled with anger, sadness, and guilt, but no focus. Now, his emotions were focused. His guilt wasn’t assuaged, but he could at least ask his wife for forgiveness. And if she didn’t forgive him? Well, there was always the single life…and Aya. Still, one thing bothered him. If he really DID want to be that which he hated most….that meant that he wanted to be Tsunami Oni. But Oni could wait. Right now, he had even bigger matters on his plate. Like getting revenge on the fans of the wrestling world.
