21 April 2004 Conflict of Self: Part I - How The Mighty Have Fallen It happens to the best of them. Walking down the street and minding their own business when the inevitable happens, and they hear their name. It’s not exactly what could be construed as a burden to most, but for those in the wrestling business who have planes to catch, meetings to attend and so forth it can be a frustrating waste of time. Hessian had no planes to catch and no meetings to attend; yet the frustration was still there. Why? “Hey Dad, is that Hessian!?” the boy asked rather excitedly. After Old School Wrestling closed down it became harder for the fans to follow their favourite wrestlers, although with a good majority of them signing with Rush Pro Wrestling it seemed to put the fans’ worries at rest. Being in the vicinity of the last show however, it was almost impossible not to be spotted, especially when you were wearing half a faceplate upon your weary face. “Well I’ll be, it is Hessian! Wanna go over and see if we can wrestle an autograph out of him, son?” the father replied, chuckling as his boy nodded, eyes bright and wide. Unaware of anyone approaching him, Hessian sighed. Turning his head every so often into the shop window adjacent to the wall on which he was leaning, the Goliath sighed again, looked back into the sea of bodies as they lumbered past almost like trawled fish. His stance was without care, yet the look in his eyes told the vigil onlooker he was perhaps a little more anxious than he cared to be. He had reason to be, after all…waiting next to an adult bookstore. Now, any normal wrestling fan hearing that for the first time would imagine the 7’2” Mercenary of Satan complete with flowing black cape and menacing attire looming over the public outside a sex shop, threatening death and agony upon passers-by. To add to their amusement possibly also the addition of dancing dildos and singing genitals by Hessian would complete the image. On this day however Hessian was without his regular outfit, instead favouring the rather bland combination of jeans and a bomber jacket, that in itself a strange notion Hessian almost blended into the environment. Becoming increasingly agitated as more glances were shot his way, Hessian turned to the entrance of the adult bookstore just as it opened, and the reason presented itself. The reason why he was frustrated stood before him, holding a shopping bag and grinning up at him cheekily. Why was he frustrated? Minion the Imp. “What the hell’s your problem? You look like you’re about to relieve yourself of constipation or something, lighten up!” was the jovial reply to the look on Hessian’s face. Glancing about the somewhat busy streets, Hessian turned back towards his companion and manager. “The problem is everyone looks over here and sees me standing next to a seedy store like this and you’re nowhere to be found. How do you think that makes me look?” Hessian retorted, scoffing at his 3’11” counterpart. “Hey man I got 30% off of the first volume of MIA: Midgets In Action! Whatever humiliation you go through is worth it for these beauties!” Minion said, removing one of the magazines and showing it off to Hessian. Not even wishing to gaze upon the smut Minion had purchased, Hessian grabbed the magazine and thrust it back down inside the shopping bag, closing it tight and growling at the so-called Walking Apocalypse. Minion was quick to pip in before his besmirching at the hands of the Goliath. “Oh, and since when did you give a damn what people think of you? Don’t be going soft on me now!” Those words were enough to stop Hessian in his tracks, prompting him to back up slightly against the wall again, looking over the sea of people passing by. For some strange reason unbeknownst to him Minion’s words had had an effect on him. Lost for words, Hessian simply snarled and turned away from the Imp to take his leave off down the street…and stumbled straight into the father and son who had been watching him. As the Mercenary of Satan opened his mouth to roar down the clumsy oaf that had bumped into him, he spotted the child by the man’s side and forced himself not to cause a scene. “Hey there, sorry to bother you! You’re from that Rush Pro Wrestling ain’tcha? Hessian isn’t it?” asked the man politely, trying to maintain the respectable father figure in front of his son. “That I am.” Hessian replied, a hint of anger still evident in his voice. Nudging his son, the father pointed to Hessian and smiled at his little boy. “You were right son, it’s Hessian! Now…what do you want to say?” he remarked condescendingly to his boy. The offspring waddled forward, clutching a piece of paper and a pen close to his chest, biting his lip and staring all the way up at Hessian with bright eyes. “Could I please have your autograph Mr. Hessian?” he asked, holding the paper and pen out. Sighing, Hessian took them from the boy and stretched his hand out, holding the paper flat out on his palm and clutching to the top of it with his fingertips. Glancing at the father, Hessian cocked an eyebrow. “Who do I make it out to?” “Hmm…make it out to Dad, Jeff and Son, Brad…then whatever you want to put, thanks!” came the polite reply once more from the father. For the first time Hessian was up close and personal with fans, and from the politeness of the son even in the face of the Goliath, his mind was fraught with thoughts of wonderment. Surely the child before him should be cowering, or at the very least have a look on his face that suggested Hessian was dangerous in some form? Instead the boy ogled the Mercenary of Satan with doe eyes as though he were his hero. Topping off the autograph with his signature Hessian knelt down, handing the pen to Minion and allowing him to scribble his name on the bottom as well. Taking the autograph, Hessian handed it to the boy, whose eyes lit up even more than thought possible. Looking up at his dad, the boy nudged him waving the autograph in front of him. As the boy did a little jig and read it over and over Hessian got back up and turned to the father. “How old is he?” Hessian asked. “He’ll be eleven in May.” The father replied. “Do you really let him watch all the violence I partake in?” Hessian’s queries were being influenced by wonder of the boy’s fear – or lack thereof – shown towards him. Brad’s father took a moment to think. “Well…” he began. “He’s been watching wrestling for a few years now and my wife and I have always told him that it’s dangerous and professionals do it and so forth. Thankfully he’s never tried any of the moves, and when he sees you on television doing all that supernatural stuff we try to tell him that it’s not real, even if we aren’t sure ourselves y’know, heh. So we just let him watch it, there’s no harm in it.” The father’s words struck Hessian as the look of the boy had. It’s not real was the specific phrase that kept circling around in his mind. As a dark gimmick he should have reduced the streets to a flowing river of blood and flesh…but instead he found himself taken aback by the boy, and a question that haunted him to his very soul. “Hey Mr. Hessian can you make me float like you made Nerezza?” the boy asked innocently, referring to an incident many weeks previously just after RPW’s Maximum Overdrive PPV where Hessian had somehow summoned the power to make his own vampiric bastard son Nerezza elevate in a bout of anger. Hessian ignored the father’s response trying to convince his son that was fake too, as he felt his stomach drop and something wrench in his gut. Quietly he uttered two words he didn’t want to believe were his own. “I can’t.” ---------- The flickering candlelight was the only salvation from absolute darkness. Sat before it, his head bowed, he placed a finger over the dancing flame. Slowly he began passing his finger over the fire, cringing as it burned. The warm glow emblazoned across his half-faceplate crafted of steel, the colours swirling in the silver almost epitomizing the pain he felt as the rest of his face hid behind a veil of shadow created by his hand positioned before the candle. His mood was made worse not by simple torment, more so the fact it stemmed from that fateful encounter with a naïve 11 year old. Hessian’s character, that which he was, his very being was questioned innocently by a child and for the first time he was questioning himself too. Too swept up in bloodbaths and violence he was never in contemplation until it was thrown in his face at the first given opportunity. Until now he was Hessian, ruthless destroyer of men and king of the dark gimmicks since the death of his own bastard father, Clyde. He was the Mercenary of Satan. Brought into creation by the vile hand of the infamous Vampir Nosferatu and unleashed upon the world. He was the man who had slain hundreds. He was the man who nailed his own tag team partner to a hanging crucifix and watched with glee as it hurtled through the canvas of the ring destroying Azrael Cain forever. He was the man who dragged the bloody carcass of his own mentor Vampir Nosferatu to the top of the OSW Jumbotron, thrusting a knife deep into his back and watching as the supposed immortal plunged deep down into the stage, never to move again. He was the man who bludgeoned “The Reverend” Jon Hull into eternal slumber with the force of two steel stairs from ringside. He was the man who took his own life and laid in wait for almost a year in order to finally pounce on the unsuspecting burned man Clyde, finally sending him to hell in a gory demise featuring the removal and annihilation of the demon’s heart… …He was that man…until now. What had changed? Where had the veil been drawn between fantasy and reality? He racked his brains going through past and present trying to determine where the constant had been interrupted. Surely he was overlooking some minute detail that provided the answer. He had to find it should he wish to avoid being subjected to apparent accusation by the boy and father earlier in the day. Glancing across the room at the grandfather clock he listened to the forlorn tick…tock…tick…tock of time passing by. Noticing the time, Hessian turned his gaze towards the bathroom door and winced. It was then that he realized what had changed… …Nothing. The walls seemed to crumble all around him as in his mind the answer formed in grotesque fashion. The great Hessian, a 9th generation mercenary, spawned by the greater evil to forge a path of death and destruction, powered on by a sinister bloodlust the likes of which could not be quenched. This was the dealer of fate to the immortals. This was the Mercenary of Satan. This was the Hessian the fans, the people knew. Up until now this was the Hessian that Hessian knew. Now, he was a miserable and lonely man sitting in the dark contemplating his existence and waiting for the dull chiming of the grandfather clock that would deem it time to accept fate. Staring at the clock as though it were his worst enemy, Hessian followed the hands as they slowly but surely made their way around the face, losing track of time slightly until the chiming boomed from within. Hessian sighed and without hesitation got to his feet and walked the short distance to the bathroom, dragging his feet along in some kind of protest, though no one was around to argue. Opening the door he entered, heading straight for the medicine cabinet and stopping before it with his hand poised ready to open it. Looking into the mirror for a moment he stared at his reflection, running his hand down the steel of his faceplate before opening the cabinet door and reaching in, taking hold of a bottle that required the grasp of his entire hand around it and removing it from within. Closing the door, Hessian opened his hand out with the bottle before him and read the name as he had done many times before. Lanoxin. It was at that moment everything seemed to click, and for all the wrong reasons. Why was he so desperate to find the change in his past that rendered void the opinion of the boy and his father? Why was he unable to pinpoint a time he could accept what they had said? His reality had been questioned, yet neither he nor they were wrong. What was the point of this long-winded worry and doubt? How many dark gimmicks were not of supernatural origin? How many required prescription drugs just to keep on stepping into that squared circle night after night? How many were so firmly rooted in their beliefs that they had completely ignored their physical limitations and state of reality? None it seemed…except Hessian. He was thankful those thoughts belonged only to him. No matter what he thought he was still the Hessian that everyone feared, the hell raising, war waging Titanic Satanic Insaniac that was gunning down everyone in his path. Popping the lid off of the bottle of pills, Hessian shook out enough for a single dose. Twisting the cap back onto the bottle Hessian placed the pills back in the cabinet and stared at the tablets for a moment before tilting his head back and allowing the handful to slide into his mouth. Cupping his hand under the tap he let water splash into it, and with enough to assist with the swallowing was moments from gulping it all down when he heard a knocking at the door. “Who ish it?” he said, juggling the pills between his teeth and contorting his lips so as to prevent them from falling out as he spoke, while keeping the handful of water from seeping through his fingers into the sink and down the drain. “What the fuck do you mean ‘who is it’? I’m the only other one here except the damned cleaning lady coming in ever so often…and you KNOW she ain’t gettin’ anywhere past the bed-…” was the disturbingly sultry reply by what could be none other than Minion the Imp. Hessian sighed and quickly drank up the water from his hand, clenching his fists and swallowing hard. He stayed rigid for a moment as the concoction of pills and water barrelled down his oesophagus, then swallowed again before splashing a little more water on his face and drying his hands off on the wash towel, turning his head back to the door. “It’s open, what do you want?” he said firmly. Minion waltzed in, holding a cordless phone and covering the mouthpiece with his impish little hand. “It’s for you…” he said, whispering slightly. “Some guy in Detroit calling about a debt you owe him? Didn’t leave a name…” Hessian was puzzled. Taking the phone from his Imp comrade he thought nothing of what the call could be about. Little did he know that what would follow would lead to his past being unearthed and far bigger problems being created for him. With doubts of his being plaguing his mind, the call he was about to take would not help in the slightest…or would it? Only time would tell for the Mercenary of Satan. Hessian was slightly apprehensive as he listened to the heavy wheezing of whoever was calling him, waiting for him to answer. Thinking for a moment, the Goliath opened his mouth to answer only to be interrupted by the impatient caller. “Hello? Angelus Von Kelsig?” “Yeah, yeah…this is Angelus…” he replied. “This may come as a bit of a surprise to you Mr. Kelsig, but-…” The voice on the other end of the line was suddenly cut off and forced into silence as Hessian piped up. “…Wait, how did you know I was here?” he asked, his curiosity piquing and rightfully so. As far as Hessian was concerned the only people who knew he was staying in the hotel were the important people at RPW. Even people looking to get a hold of him from the PTC Global Title Division had to go through the offices at RPW to get in contact with Hessian. How then did an anonymous caller acquire knowledge of Hessian’s whereabouts? The caller paused for little more than a second to get their story straight before continuing. “My friend, there are television adverts, posters, promotional vignettes and all manner of advertisements showcasing the upcoming Primetime Central Global Title match included in which is yourself. It merely took one of my more avid wrestling fan-like accomplices to identify you, thus leading us to this conversation…” came the reply, which as vague as it was made a little sense…to anyone except Hessian. “Well listen here, friend. The only people who should be calling me here address conversations a little differently than you, so unless this is of vital importance I will bid you adieu…” Hessian said languidly. With everything on his mind the last thing he needed was some jackass prank caller. “It IS of vital importance Mr. Kelsig! I’m calling about a debt that is yet to be paid. Your father Jack Kelsig borrowed a lot of money from me some time back and I still await repayment!” The determination and persistence of this caller seemed to suggest a legitimate purpose however as he yelled out on the other end of the line. Hessian’s jaw was as close to dropping, as it would ever get. His father’s name was mentioned, his deceased father. Never before had anyone mentioned any member of Hessian’s family. He had never made any mention of them after all, and after that fateful night in October, 1996…he never wanted to mention it… ---------- Life was good. 25yrs old on this fine day and living the life of luxury in the family mansion. Everyone came to visit. Aunts and uncles and grandparents and cousins and nephews and all the rest attended the birthday party for the Kelsig lad. There was no particular reason for the massive celebration, the family were simply close-knit and at every special occasion added to the festivities by praising accolades and achievements held by each member of the family. On this day however all was not well. Angelus should have been celebrating a new contract to work alongside his father as well as his birthday, and indeed he did. His father however had spent the entire day looking over his shoulder as it were, worrying about something. Only Angelus realised this, but confrontation only brought his father to lie. “I’m just making sure everything’s going ok for your birthday, son!” was the idle reply Angelus received each and every time he asked. With that he shrugged off the worry and went about celebrating. Much of the day passed with joy and laughter as the entire family enjoyed party games including a piñata filled with dollar bills. A barbeque in the evening was the prelude to the opening of the presents. Much of the family looked forward to this as what should have been a mouth-watering feast turned into a banquet of barely edible food thanks in part to Angelus’ uncle who couldn’t cook, but mostly the blame lay on Angelus’ father who once again was not concentrating, and seemingly jumpy. Questioning only lead to more lies, and soon the family was hurried inside the house for Angelus to open his presents. Of course he received everything he had hoped for plus a little more. The keys to his father’s old Cadillac were the favourite with a $20 gift voucher from his grandparents taking up the rear as least appreciated present. Afterwards everyone enjoyed a tipple from the bottle of whiskey his uncle had given him, and Angelus happily joined in family conversation, while glancing at the clock until it would be time for him to head out for the night with a few friends. He quickly began tiring of the family as they babbled about this and that, and only the constant look of worry upon his father’s face stopped Angelus submitting to the foul tipsy state he was in. Still he waited patiently for 11:30pm and his escape from his now-boring family, most of whom had thankfully retired to bed for the night. As the clock struck 11 o’ clock however, Angelus stared at it as the chime inside seemed to make a muffled smashing sound. Getting up and shaking off the tipsiness, Angelus checked on the clock to make sure it hadn’t broken, and surely enough everything was going like clockwork. He thought for a moment and heard another smashing sound, followed by a thud directly above him! Thinking nothing of it other than one of his relatives drunkenly stumbling around, he collapsed back into a chair and sighed, waiting for time to pass. Little did he know that the smashing he had just heard was a deadly Molotov cocktail soaring through one of the windows upstairs, and landing in the hallway before the staircase had rendered escape impossible. What followed was the brutal murder of Angelus’ family. Some died from smoke inhalation, some died screaming as the fire cooked them alive. Hearing the screams Angelus raced to the stairs, only to be forced back by black billowing clouds of smoke. He had no choice but to leave the house, to get out before he too was burned alive. He was running on instinct, backing out through the front door and tumbling down the front steps, too confused and scared to think. Angelus watched, as the house erupted in fire, destroying everything within. Surely he would have passed out at that moment hadn’t he heard the laughing from behind. Looking back, Angelus set eyes upon a man…a man with an evil smile upon his face and a small army of goons surrounding him. A man that had just murdered his family… ---------- Hessian was silent, holding onto the phone and grimacing as he thought back to the night it happened. The silence proved a frustration to the man on the other end of the line who was quick to interject, snapping Hessian out of his forlorn recollection of memories he wished he’d never been cursed with. “Mr. Kelsig are you still there?” he asked innocently. “………Who are you and what debt are you speaking of? My father was never in debt…” Hessian asked, trying to hide the shakiness in his voice. Something he had never had to do before. He was dealing with thoughts he had never contemplated before nor had any want to. He was scheduled to compete against five other men in a match for the greatest prize in Primetime Central. He was already being pushed mentally and physically would soon be pushed just as hard. More mental pressure was the last thing he needed, especially when it came from the darkest corners of his past. “My name, Mr. Kelsig…is Jim Carlisle. In 1996 your father Jack Kelsig came to me and made a withdrawal of $15,000. He fully intended to pay it back of course, the only problem was I don’t think even he knew when he was going to pay it back. After his unfortunate passing it was obvious I would never receive my money. Then a couple of weeks ago one of my accomplices made the connection between you, Angelus Von Kelsig, and your father Jack Kelsig. Being a blood relation Mr. Kelsig you are entitled to pay off your father’s debt…and I expect it paid back in full.” Jim Carlisle spoke with a hint of aggression in his voice. Was everything this man said true? He thought back to all those times his father would leave on business trips, and after he returned made so many changes to the shopping budget and luxuries and so forth. He couldn’t believe it but the facts seemed to be there. Hessian was almost lost for words. “…What are you-…did you have anything to do with his death?” Hessian asked, not thinking of what he was saying. Carlisle chuckled while Hessian gripped the phone tight, feeling taunted by the man. “Heh, I assure you Mr. Kelsig your father’s untimely passing was nothing to do with me. I do not operate like that…you have my word. I just want my money back, and unfortunately it falls to you to pay me back. I apologise but it’s the way it works.” He replied, treating this as nothing more than another business transaction. To Hessian this was more important; this was personal. This was his life. For now though Jim Carlisle’s words sounded honest, and so Hessian sighed and continued. “Well…what makes you think I even have $15,000 to give you? What even gives you the right to ask me for that money?” Hessian said, trying to trick Carlisle. “Mr. Kelsig have you ever heard the expression, “Don’t bullshit a bullshitter?” I’ve done my research and with your success in the wrestling world I find it very difficult to believe you would have trouble coughing up 15 grand…” Carlisle broke off for a moment, and listening carefully Hessian could hear papers being shuffled. “…Especially when taking into consideration your salary when you worked for Old School Wrestling. Now unless you’re spendthrift, which I very much doubt…you must have money lying around doing nothing somewhere…what am I saying? Mr. Kelsig this was your father’s debt! Do you really want to dishonour your father by ignoring something which was such a burden to him? Surely to pay off his debt would be to put his soul at rest?” he said in an almost preachy manner. Hessian sneered at the mention of the soul and cleared his throat. “Do not patronize me Mr. Carlisle, and do not try to sway me with talk of dishonour. If what you say were true my father would not have dishonoured his family by turning to a damned loan shark! Now the money I have, the money I earned is going to stay just that, mine. If you want $15,000 you’re going to have to find it somewhere else. You’ve obviously done without it with no trouble for the last 8 years, I’m sure you can go without for-…” “NO TROUBLE?!” Hessian was interrupted and surprised as Carlisle’s voice boomed down the line. “No trouble? Listen here Mr. Kelsig…I run a business. I give money to make money. Do you realize just how long it took to make up for those lost earnings? Your father’s inability to keep his word meant I had to find money elsewhere. The only problem was that I couldn’t find money elsewhere. Mr. Kelsig you may find it interesting to know I was forced to take out a loan of my own…a loan that I spent the better part of three years trying to pay back. Do you know what that did to me? Do you know what it did to my family? You have no idea how this affected me, yet you feel comfortable in accusing me of inconveniencing-…” “OK! I apologise Mr. Carlisle it was rude of me to assume…” Hessian said, interrupting him before the loan shark completed his life story. The phone call was frustrating to begin with, now it was just bearable and no more. Wasn’t he talking to a loan shark that knew where he was when the rest of the world didn’t, Hessian would have hung up by now. “Ah…excuse me Mr. Kelsig. I shouldn’t have snapped like that. You have to understand I need the money, and like I said you have to be the one that pays it to me. Do you understand?” he asked in a more polite manner. Hessian knew what kind of person he was talking to however, and would not be swayed by the lies hidden in Carlisle’s voice. “Like I said Mr. Carlisle, I don’t have your money. I can’t help you. Now if you don’t mind I-…” before Hessian could continue, Jim Carlisle butted in with words Hessian should have laughed at…but instead found a cold shiver run down his spine. “You know it would be really unfortunate if you couldn’t make it to your Global Title match, would it not?” said Carlisle. At this point, Hessian would have laughed and hung up. Then that night or perhaps the night after when Carlisle came to exact revenge on him, he would feel the wrath of Eternal Damnation. His spine would chill and his gut would wrench as Hessian picked him apart both mentally and physically and left nothing but the splattered blood on the walls and a sickly smell of fear in the air. That is what Hessian, the Mercenary of Satan would have done. Instead he cocked an eyebrow and held back a sigh of disbelief and perhaps also worry. Thinking for a moment, the Goliath replied. “Are you threatening me? Mr. Carlisle you have just spent the last five minutes telling me how you never got your money in the first place due to my father’s passing…and now you threaten me? You’re either hypocritical or just stupid…” In saying this, Hessian was defending himself as best he could. The realisation of who he was and what he wasn’t was quickly dawning on him, and rather than fight it and stand up for who he was…Hessian was lying down and admitting defeat. He was accepting who he really was. Thankfully he still stood up for his right to wrestle. In his mind he may not be the immortal dark gimmick who commands the apocalypse or the monstrous and evil mercenary to the dark lord Lucifer himself…but he was damn sure still a wrestler. “Mr. Kelsig…Angelus. I simply said you would not attend your Global title match. I made no reference to your father or his passing or any connection to you. That being said you won’t make it to your match…if I don’t get my money. Do you understand?” “Listen. You don’t scare me. Threaten me all you want, but if you try anything I’ll-…” Hessian was determined in his reply, but stopped dead as Carlisle uttered words that struck Hessian hard. “Or what if something happened to your little Imp friend there? He looks very happy chasing after the cleaner ladies and receptionists here…we wouldn’t want to end that happiness would we?” “………You’re in the hotel?” Hessian asked, now in utter disbelief. Indeed, Minion had left the room a short while ago which only added to Hessian’s worry. “Why of course I am…where else would I-…” Jim spoke but was once again cut off by a shocked Hessian. “Wait, I was told you were in Detroit! How the hell can you be here?” “Oh you must have been mistaken. I told your little friend I was from Detroit. Now Mr. Kelsig…do we have a deal? Or does your little friend come with me?” Hessian listened carefully as in the background the animalistic howling of Minion erupted as he chased after the ladies working in the hotel. Something in his stomach contorted tightly as he heard his friend and manager’s muffled yells of “Come here you sexy little beasties! Minion wants to swat yo’ ass! WOOHOOOHOOHOO!” close to Carlisle. Hessian didn’t know what to do. It was obvious if he didn’t comply Minion would be in great danger. It was becoming scarily obvious also that Hessian could not burst into the lobby and rip Carlisle apart in a blinding rage of hellfire and apocalyptic carnage. After his recent revelations, Hessian was being faced with an entirely human problem. He could not kill or maim his way out of this one. This was a human problem and had to have a human solution. The root of this problem? Money. The solution? Hessian did not know. He was running on empty and had little to no choices but to comply. Sighing in defeat, the Goliath gave his reply. “…Ok, tell Minion to bring you up to the room. We can talk more in person.” … The path of destruction was being set. Stood in his way now, the incompetence personified of Primetime Central. Indeed, Hessian would be in full battle mode in preparation to wrestle the Global Title from Jesse Jamester and all other second-rate competitors jumping for a chance at the gold…wasn’t his mind still plaguing him. He had still not resolved the issue that had arisen from the incident with the young child and his father. Strangely though the incident with the two was not the cause, but rather a reminder. It all came about when the haunting began. Not believing for a moment that the burned man Clyde could be the perpetrator, Hessian hunted down the vampire Nerezza demanding he explain himself and his connection to the cryptic messages left for Hessian. A prankster in his prime, Nerezza shot Hessian down and then some. Speaking a truth Hessian feared, he was left shattered in the barren and empty hallways a broken man. His fate had been realized, and was now soon to be believed. The root of his fear however was not the humanity within, but the predator that would seek him out soon. If what Nerezza had said was true and he was not behind the haunting that the Goliath had been subject to…could the demon have returned? If the possibility were fathomable, Hessian would be in great danger. In a state of doubt and fear he was easy prey for Clyde, should the demon be hunting him down. Still, only Nerezza spoke the truth and as the one who helped carve Clyde’s heart from his chest, would surely not divulge the truth to the burned man. But what if he did? The world seemed to be ganging up on him. 5 men, supposedly the elite of their federation would try to defeat him for the Global title. Jim Carlisle, the man who was truly behind Hessian becoming what he is…or what he was…would be watching closely, ready to disable him shouldn’t his will be done after they met for the first time very shortly. Then there was Clyde. A creature that cared not for humanity or championships, but simply the suffering brought upon mankind. For now though, Hessian waited to find himself face to face with a creature not unlike Clyde. Although entirely human, Carlisle could and would dismantle Hessian as the burned man could, just in entirely different ways, as the Mercenary of Satan would soon find out. To Be Continued. ---- 21 April 2004 Conflict of Self: Part III - Defeat Hessian didn’t even bother waiting for a reply, instead hanging up on Carlisle and collapsing into the nearest chair. He held his head in his hands waiting for the inevitable to happen, while his mind was still at work. It didn’t take long before Minion came waltzing through the door singing after a successful sexual harassment spree, while a man dressed in black followed…not as happy as the Imp. Waiting by the door, he looked around; keeping his arms folded while Minion did a little jig before spinning on his heels and pointing at the man. “Wait here Chico, I’ll go fetch him for ya!” Minion pipped innocently, having no idea of what had transpired in the last few minutes. Skipping through the hotel room, the God Among Midgets searched for Hessian, finally happening upon him as he emerged from a bedroom. The Goliath glanced down at the Imp. “Go in there and don’t come out until I tell you to.” He said, motioning to the bedroom behind him. “What? Why? Something goin’ down I can’t be a part of?” Minion asked, folding his arms and cocking his head as he tried to look up at Hessian. “Yes. Now do as you’re told…” “But I-…” Minion was given no opportunity to argue, instead being pushed inside the bedroom, the door shut forcefully in his face as Hessian turned and walked to the front room where Jim Carlisle was indeed waiting, sitting in a chair and admiring Hessian’s belt from his ring attire with the logo adorning it. Glancing up at the Mercenary of Satan, Carlisle flashed a Jack Nicholson-like grin and adjusted the pair of sunglasses he was wearing. “You certainly look bigger in person than on T.V. Mr. Kelsig. Here, take a seat.” Carlisle said, motioning to the sofa opposite him. Hessian complied and took a seat, glaring at Carlisle as he placed the belt down and clasped his hands. “So…how are we going to solve this little problem of ours?” “You tell me. I don’t have $15,000 to give you and anything else I say will probably be wrong. So make whatever damn deal you have to and be on your way. I’ve got more important things to be worrying about than some goddamn debt that…my-…” Hessian trailed off all of a sudden as Carlisle removed his glasses, revealing a wicked old sneer on his aged face. It was not the sneer that stopped Hessian, but seeing Carlisle’s face. Something in his mind clicked, and seeing his gruff face, wrinkled eyes and thin-lipped sneer Hessian suddenly thought back to the night of the fire, to falling down the front steps and turning around to see the man who had started it all. Gazing at him, Angelus took in every detail before the rage set in and he attacked… Snapping out of his daze, Hessian looked up at Carlisle once again, examining his face and slowly coming to realize just who sat before him. “It was you. You…you lying son of a bitch.” He said, staring blankly now at Carlisle. “What was me, Mr. Kelsig?” replied Carlisle, unaware of how much danger he was quickly going to be in. “That night…the fire. My family’s death…you were there. You caused it! YOU SON OF A BITCH!” Hessian dived out of the sofa, charging towards Carlisle and roaring out. Lunging at the loan shark, Hessian was stopped in his tracks by a clicking sound, and something jabbing him in his gut. Holding out an outstretched arm and a balled-up fist, Hessian looked down at his stomach and saw the gun pointing directly at him. He froze as Carlisle looked up at him, grinning even now. “You took a gunshot to the arm and healed up pretty nicely if I remember correctly…think you could handle a bullet to the guts? No? Do me a favour and sit the fuck back down.” Jim said, waving the gun over Hessian’s stomach and prompting the Goliath to back off towards the sofa, and once again Hessian complied. “Now. I already told you I had nothing to do with your family’s death and I stick by that…” came the blatant lie from Carlisle. He knew fine and well what he had done, but even he had been taken aback by Hessian’s outright rage towards him and maintaining a lie seemed mandatory now. Perhaps it was Hessian’s size or the intimidating faceplate but something had him spooked, and he was thankful for the gun in his possession. “…I’m not here to be accused of murder, I’m here to reclaim my $15,000. I’m not going to sit here all day and listen to you tell me you don’t have it…so I’ll be nice and accept that fact.” He said, to a relieved sigh from Hessian…until he continued, “…So how much do you have?” Hessian threw his hands up in the air and rolled his eyes. Rubbing the temple not hidden behind the steel faceplate he sighed. “Not $15,000. What I could give you? $2,500. That’s it. I can’t give any more than that.” “That’s it? Well fuck me, I might as well take nothing!” Carlisle said, thinking for a moment and scratching the side of his head with the barrel of his gun. Hessian winced; hoping some mechanism in the gun would malfunction and send a bullet driving through Carlisle’s skull, ending his miserable life. He had only known Carlisle for little more than ten minutes and already he wished he were dead. Wasn’t the curse of humanity flowing through his veins he would have granted his own wish a few minutes into the phone conversation. He almost smiled as the thought of Carlisle’s blood spattering across the walls, yet the smile disappeared after a few moments as Jim spoke again. “Ok…tell you what. Here’s what we’re going to do. You’re going to give me that two and a half grand. There’s a guy I know back in Detroit, a bookie. The difference between him and any other bookmaker with brains is that he takes bets on wrestling matches. If my estimation of the scale of this match is accurate he’ll be taking bets on the outcome for sure. Excuse me for a moment.” Hessian had no say in what was going on, asking himself if he even wanted a say, or just do whatever it took to get rid of this nuisance. Sneering at Carlisle, Hessian rested his head against the sofa, staring up at the ceiling and sighing as Jim Carlisle held his cell phone to his ear and waited for an answer. Surely enough someone whose voice had a weasel-like sleazy tone picked up. Carlisle grinned and cleared his throat. “Yeah, Benny? It’s Jim here, Jim Carlisle………yeah I’m good, you? Hey that’s great, listen Benny I need favour. You taking any bets for this Primetime Central Global Title match comin’ up? Oh really, great…………yeah I know but bear with me, what are the odds on Hessian from RPW winning?” the conversation went back and forth as such until Jim went quiet as Benny checked the odds for the match. Hessian looked back across the room at Carlisle as his eyes lit up all of a sudden and he piped up again. “Really? Well I’ll tell ya what Benny…stick $2,500 on Hessian winning for me, alri-………yeah I know it’s a lot of money………oh no I’m plenty serious………ok good man Benny, I’ll be in on the Monday after to collect my money………heh, yeah I know, all a game of chance. Ok I’ll talk to you later Benny, thanks again…ok, bye.” Carlisle flipped his phone shut and pocketed it, glancing at Hessian and grinning an evil grin. “Well, well, well Mr. Kelsig it appears as though a lovely little coincidence has played into our favour and your odds to win stand at 7/1. So in giving me your two and a half grand and winning your wrestling match, not only do I get my money back paid in full but you also get your money back! For now though I will require it as a deposit…you’ll have noticed the ATM across the street I’m sure, why don’t you mosey on along and get it for me?” Hessian got to his feet and left the room, saying nothing. Stopping outside of the bedroom where he had left Minion, the Mercenary of Satan hesitated to enter. Was he really going to give in to a sleazy human like Jim Carlisle? He leaned back towards the front room almost as if to head back in and power through the gunshots to Carlisle, wrap a hand around his throat and choke the very life from him. Instead he simply grabbed the door handle and walked in to the bedroom to see Minion, who surprisingly enough was practicing hand gestures and poses for future venues as opposed to………charging “adult” phone calls to the hotel. “I need you to run a little errand for me.” Hessian said, almost forlornly. Minion could tell something was wrong with Hessian yet had no clue about just how much recent events had affected him. “What do you need? I just called out for some Portuguese hookers so we should be-…” He began, only to be cut off by the Goliath as he grabbed Minion’s travel rucksack, removing the contents and handing the empty bag to Minion, as well as a credit card. “Go to the ATM across the street and withdraw $2,500. Don’t ask why, just get the money, put it in the bag and bring it back here. Got it?” “Oh, this is about that debt thing isn’t it? I see………ok.” Minion replied, nodding and taking the bag and card from Hessian and exiting the room, the Mercenary of Satan following close behind. Ignoring Carlisle, Minion left the hotel room as Hessian took a seat back on the sofa opposite the loan shark. “Good. Now remember Mr. Kelsig, you better win this match. If you lose, I get collateral, namely both your legs. You don’t want that I’m sure.” Carlisle remarked as he watched Minion waddling out of the room. Turning back to Hessian he was surprised to see a new look of confidence upon the Goliath’s face. “After this? Don’t worry. There’s no way I’m going to lose.” Hessian was of course thinking in his mind of Carlisle as motivation now. It would help a lot in the ring to see him in the face of every one of his opponents, and make the process of Eternal Damnation run all the more smoothly. All the same, Hessian had just found another purpose in his match. Winning the Global title was a given, but now he had to win to pay off Carlisle. To some extent he would do it for his father and the good life he was given by his parents. He would win the match and pay off the loan shark as a way of defeating this all-too human problem. The others could do as they please. They could whine, piss, bitch, moan and gloat all they wanted and make all the threats their hearts desired. Hessian would swoop in and silence them like a hitman in the night. He wanted the belt…and he needed it. If not to conquer the problem of Jim Carlisle then to reassure himself that the hellfire still roared within, and the spirit of the bloodthirsty, demon slaying Titanic Satanic Insaniac was very much alive. Only then would his doubts be put at rest, only then would the scepticism be quelled at long last. Hessian was a marked man on all sides, and the only way to prove to both himself and his foes that he was still a formidable and powerful nemesis, to prove he was still the destructive force – the Mercenary of Satan – was to defeat. Defeat in all sense of the word. Hessian would defeat those 5 men physically in the ring, dashing any hopes they had of winning the Global Title. In winning the Global Title Hessian would defeat Jim Carlisle, stopping him from carrying out his human threat and letting him have his money at long last. In defeating those 5 men and becoming the most dominant man in Primetime Central…Hessian hoped with all his might that his dominance would in some form or another defeat the desires of Clyde and Nerezza. Hessian had to do it…or he would be defeated. Minion returned shortly thereafter, and Jim Carlisle got his money. The first half of the battle was over now, all Hessian had to do now was enter that ring against the best that XUW, GW, UWF and 4WW had to offer. Certainly the ball was in his court with all the hype surrounding him and intimidation cast upon his opponents. All that remained now was to win, to prove…and to bring Eternal Damnation to the Primetime Central. The End.
