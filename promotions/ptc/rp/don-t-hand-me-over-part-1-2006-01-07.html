Date: 2006-01-07 "Life in Northern Ireland (The Ballad of Gerry Conlan)" From deep within the depths of Portlaoise prison, The walls all around, screamed out for clemency. Bloodied fists and forced confessions stain the walls Oh Christ on high, why is this happening to me? It was fifteen years ago when it all started, As a forced confession he was made to sign. Conlan, a mere scapegoat for the masses Born in Belfast, everyday he’d walk the line. Dozens of men could testify to the screaming, Of the pleading shouts that pierced the stuffy air. By hook or by crook, you’ll give us what we want, The young Conlan soon fell deep into their snare. Who the hell are you to take away his freedom? What gives you the right to hold his liberty? Beaten and starved, you made him a guilty man. You’ve locked him up and thrown away the key. Deprived of human rights, he lay in prison, Waiting until his trial he’d soon live to see. The defense told the dark tale of his ‘confession’. But the unjust judges in the court would not agree. The farce of an appeal was to follow four years later. Puppet judges simply brushed away his pain. Sneering, in well rehearsed collusion. And the guilty jeer the innocent again. From deep within the depths of Portlaoise prison, Through concrete and steel, a whisper came, My frame up, is almost completed… I’m innocent, Gerry Conlan is my name… Who the hell are you to take away his freedom? What gives you the right to hold his liberty? Beaten and starved, you made him a guilty man. You’ve locked him up and thrown away the key. Who the hell are you to take his freedom? What gives you the right to hold his liberty? Beaten and starved, you made him a guilty man. You’ve locked him away and thrown away the key. -By Michael D. (aka me) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ With a heavy heart, I ask this question. As I watch my countrymen dying all around me, I’m compelled to ask this question. It’s happening again isn’t it? The hate, that many thought to be the dying flames of what was once a raging inferno, it’s all coming back, isn‘t it? It’s been rekindled, right? The ‘peace’ and ‘prosperity’ of Northern Ireland, the tranquil Irish-British nation of Western Europe is being cut down the middle once more as men demand this and that and this and that. Yes, some peace. Slowly it is being exposed as nothing more than a blanket to cover the crown’s shame; men on both sides pick up their armalites once more, cock them and take aim. Soldier O‘Connell/Smith, reporting for duty sir, are we going to be killing us some Brits/Paddies today? I shouldn’t be making jokes…hell, go on then, one more! It’s another ten round slobber knocker folks! The ‘Bruising’ British soldiers versus the Irish ‘Ready to Rumble’ Republicans! Thing is, as always…everyone else is caught in the crossfire. Because the IRA, a five hundred man force, is made up almost solely of Irish Catholics, every man of that origin is now a target to the British, you say? Yes they could and have bombed England, yes they have killed British soldiers. But you’re burning the wheat with the chaff, you surely wouldn’t blindly label innocent men guilty…would you? Just like last time? Again, if that word is more to your palate? Back when the government was a farcical theatre. You tell me the system’s crumbling again? Well I’m telling you, you’ll bring yourself down with your actions. Yeah, I can make jokes about it until I’m blue in the face, but it won’t be funny. Men are caught in conflict and whether you strongly believe in a United Ireland or you strongly believe in union with Britain, once again, it’s those Irish Catholics of the North who sit on the fence and refuse to partake that come off the worse… Don’t think we have forgotten either. How men disappeared from their homes and how their kin were only informed through a court proceeding. We haven’t forgotten the shattered bones, the half choked throats. We haven’t forgotten your damning statements, laced with lies and deceit, that you handed to the prosecution with a smile. We haven’t forgotten the years of solitude, in desolate prisons in desolate areas. We haven’t forgotten and we won’t ever forget. Now that we’re all a target again, it’s important we don’t forget. I mean, how the hell do you fight to get your right back, to walk freely down the street, when your name and address singles you out, for every damn Peeler on the force? We march, you shoot us down, we take arms, you shoot us down, we stay silent, you shoot us down. When does it end? When does Britain live up to it’s claims of equality and fairness? Why’s does her hand ball into an iron fist as she cast her eye to Eire? Why would an IRA man stay in his own house anyway? Don’t you think that as a Guerilla force, they would stay in hiding, only showing their face to attack? Never thought about that before, have you? Allow me to elaborate with this simple question: why the hell are you dragging innocent men from their homes? Through the streets of Derry and Belfast, why are British soldiers tearing homes apart with scorn? Why are the sounds of children screaming piercing the air, as they watch their Father dragged downstairs by defenders of Her Majesty’s realm? Why are Mothers kneeling in the streets, mopping blood from their sons heads, as Uniformed men run off, their batons stained a crimson red? Why? Why? Is it because of the uniform, that they have a special status that they can do such a thing? Does the clothes they wear make them superior to all others? Or is it their frustrations that they haven’t caught the men they are so eager to see sent down? Because the fruits of their labor have not grown, is this justification for their senseless acts? No, it’s bigger than all that. These men do these things, because the British government, the law, allows them to do so… The law. Well, they call it the law…I call it a partite, internment, conscription, partition and silence. It’s a law that they make, to keep you and me where they think we belong. All the while, the Ringleaders sit comfortably behind desks. They hide behind steel and bullet proof glass; machine guns and spies. They spray us with tear gas then tell us, we’re in the wrong. Yeah…wonderful world indeed… The law…when a law that governs the people can no longer be associated with the face that created it, when democracy has become a mere desecration in the minds of the people, there is only tyranny. And the people won’t stand for that yoke. And that is why men are called to arms. Yet, we’re all guilty, aren’t we? Our religion and nationality makes us so. And I have to ask once again. It’s happening again, isn’t it? The hate…it’s all coming back…isn’t it? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ No time for love if they come in the morning, No time to show the tears of our fears in the morning No time for good bye, no time to ask why… And the sound of the sirens, they cry out in the morning. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Let’s be unoriginal, undramatic and as creative as a badger’s left testicle. Let’s go old school, let’s go geocities… The scene fades in from black… ..and into the bedroom of Paddy O’Shea, located to the extreme North of his trailer caravan, that sits rigged up to his ‘89 Ford Granada outside. A beige wallpaper, highlighted with a yellowish tinge, cold be mistaken for the product of an LSD induced trip. But it’s not. Instead, it’s plastered to the walls. A rabid, pungent smell is also on a podium awaiting feedback. Even in the dimness of the early morning light, it’s quite evident the room is in dire straits. Holes protruding from the walls that match the odd gap in the floor, are only the under linings of an incredulously surreal setting. A thin mist of light has filtered in through the threadbare, linen net that has acted as a makeshift curtain since fuck knows when. It’s probably this intruding light that causes the beast stirs. All five foot seven of the fecker. From amidst the masses of his duvet, Paddy yawns, the yellowing picket fence teeth lining his gaping orifice very much evident. Then he returns to sleepy time. Having a nice sleep are you Paddy? Paddy nods his head, a small smile now creeping across his worn torn face. Which has pizza seemingly stuck to it/ That’s good Paddy, I’m glad. It’s nice to sleep after all those hard nights busting your balls wrestling. It’s nice to let the swelling go down on your knuckles, ice that knee up and just sit on an armchair and do absolutely nothing. So, what are you dreaming about then buddy? Are you seeing the AWC Transatlantic title lofted above your head? Maybe you’re seeing a family, a wife and son, in a stable family unit. Not that you’d know what anything about creating stability, but hell, that’s what dreams are for, huh? Maybe you’re dreaming about GTT, always a good topic by me. Are you reminiscing triumphs? The way men asked "What fell the Uberjudge?" and "Who grounded the WarrMachine?" It’s good to be in the spotlight, the centre of attention, ‘that guy’. Yes, you certainly have a few nice experiences to recall. Yet, maybe you dream of what the future holds. Maybe of the prize that comes along with beating Doreen Pepperpot. I know you, I know you very very well. And I know how much a win against her would mean to you. She’s young with a career very much ahead of her. You on the other hand, are keeled over, creeping towards the end of yours. It would be nice to go out with a flourish, wouldn’t it? Strike a deal with the old knees, and go out with a bang. You feel sorry for her, because she is one with the short straw in your dream. But you mustn’t. One day, they’ll be chanting Doreen from on high. But not today. If it was any other day perhaps, you’d walk away. But today, you stand your ground. And why shouldn’t you dream of such things Paddy. I mean, knock yourself out, they’re your dreams. Surely nothing could bring you down from this pulpit of euphoria now? Could it? Maybe you dream of other things. What did you want to be when you grew Paddy, when you were younger? It wouldn’t be wrestling, you barely knew it existed until you left the bare knuckle boxing circuit. Maybe a Fireman? Did you want to help those in need? A doctor? Same as above? A charity worker? Er, same as above? Maybe you dream of lost dreams…maybe you dream of the places you’ve never been and the places you’ll never see again. … Maybe you dream of a room with no windows and no lights; gray walls that are plastered with dried blood. Maybe you see twenty men surrounding you, clubs in hand, a ready made confession in the other. Everywhere there’s eyes staring at you. You try to look away but when you do, they hit you. Stare to long and they hit you. Maybe you see a solitary seat on which you sit, tied up. You can’t defend yourself, and they like it like that, so they just keep on hitting. By hook or by crook…you’ll give us what we want…you’ll sign this sheet…you’ll give your life to us… Paddy’s face frowns so that a solitary line develops between his eyebrows as they arch downwards. His eyes still tightly shut as he bathes in his subconscious, his mouth opens in naked defiance to the picture being painted in his head. Don’t worry Paddy…it’s only a dream…and just like any hope you had of going any further in that tournament, just like any desire you had to be the best you could be, I’m going to snatch it away. Just like I’m going to do to your freedom. Let’s do this. BANG! The bedroom suddenly springs into a colorful arena of activity as the door is barged open and splinters of wood spray through the air like a dense mist. Paddy, now awakened, swings around but he might as well have stayed where he was because a swing of a baton merry-go-rounds him back to neutral. He hits his pillow face first and feels a warm trickle of blood meander down his cheek, from a gash freshly cut below his eye. "On your feet Mick". Paddy feels the word ‘fuck’ developing on his lips. He needn’t have bothered because an open fist slap to the side of his head, sends the word flaying and scorches his ear burning hot for good measure. Now there’s hands around his shoulders, which he fights desperately to struggle from; punches from three different directions tame that resilience somewhat. Now he’s being dragged, naked from his bed. Finally, he’s able to see the men whose doing this - six British Soldiers, their eyes absolutely dancing with insanity. And Paddy screams, he screams Goddamn hard as he’s dragged from the room. And you want to know why? Want to know why he screams so hard he thinks his lungs have ruptured? Because he sees his whole future in a split second. It involves chains and windowless rooms. It involved blood and my God, it involves pain… And he screams some more. There’s confusion in his mind, his screams give him temporary clarity because he knows when he’s screaming, he’s doing it for a damn good reason. A single word now flashes into his head. It flashes again and again like a neon light, and so like a moth, he’s guided to it. Injustice…injustice… Three days…they’re going to have their way with him for three days…by hook or by crook, they’ll get the confession they want… "Don’t hand me over.." Oh Jesus. Oh Jesus Christ on a cross. They need scapegoats. And for that, they’ll gladly trade his freedom. No time for love if they come in the morning No time to show the tears of our fears in the morning… ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Oh crap, oh fuck, oh crap, oh fu- That’s not going to get us anywhere now is it? Anyway, me and you are part of Paddy’s mind…we don’t panic. So calm down…calm down…calm? …Oh crap, oh fuck, oh crap, oh fu- Calm down! How the hell can I be calm. How the hell can you be calm? You know as well as I do what’s going to happen! They’re going to take Paddy away, to England maybe, put him in a room with twenty of the bastards and knock a confession out of him. Jail…that’s the rest of his life in one word! Wait, Paddy didn’t do anything! It doesn’t matter! They need scapegoats! Who or what won’t matter. Evidence won’t matter. As long as they have his signature on their already written confession, then that’s it. You remember the eighties don’t you? Stories of the Birmingham Six, the Guildford Four? These people were dragged from their homes, just like Paddy; beaten, just like Paddy; and made to sign things THEY WOULDN’T EVEN LET THEM READ! We’re fucked, you hear me? FUCKED! But…I…. And all this time, all I was thinking about was GTT5 and the beached whale. God…how could I have been so stupid. Beached whale? Doreen Pepperpot, pound for pound the most graceful wrestler on the planet, pound for the pound, weighs the same as a planet? No? Come on, you have got to know her. Big fatty. Fatty boom batty? I know of the wrestler. That description wasn’t exactly necessary now was it? Yeah but it cheers me up to laugh at fat people when I’m down. Thing is…now that’s scaring me too…because a) we’re going to prison or b) we go free, fight the match, and probably get killed by El Porkchop. Man, what the hell are we going to do? I mean, we were over the border for ONE day, ONE day. And they nail us? What could we have done in one day? We would remember that, right? Maybe it was something we didn’t pick up on. Something sparked something because they didn’t even know we existed until yesterday…something pointed them in Paddy’s direction. I just… I wonder what… ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The handcuffs are hurting his wrists badly. He supposed that in an hour or two, he would gladly sodomise a cat for a pain so mild. The future heralds the coming of a tear from his eye. Don’t hand me over…please…aye didn’ do anything’…please!" Men didn’t walk out of those rooms as men. They either walked out, handcuffed and shamed. Or they were rolled out in wheelchairs, shaking so badly they had to sit on their hands to keep still. Men developed stutters over these experiences. Innocent or not, the fact of the matter was, you wouldn’t ever be the same… Years in the ring had taught him to be fearless…now every cell in his body has turned yellow because he just doesn’t have the strength to walk with his chin up right now. They’re going to hurt you Paddy. It won’t be a slap and tickle…it’s going to be bats and fists…they’re going to threaten your family. They’re going to tear your head apart so much, you’ll buy a rope as soon as you get out and look for a nice tree. This isn’t television Paddy. This is the dark side of British imperialism. Long live her Majesty. Paddy opened his eyes and looked around him, scanning the interior of the armed jeep that carried him. Sixteen eyes were affixed on him; six of them soldiers and two of them seemingly detectives, all sat in silence all just staring. There was an element of general doom among all. And they were baiting him. All they wanted him to do was sniff the wrong way and they;d take his fucking Irish nose off. When you’re locked up in a cage for so long, the animals take full advantage of their temporary freedom. So go on Paddy, give the animals a good show. Paddy bowed his head and he suddenly felt he needed to throw up. Nervousness had long since been replaced with uncompromising dread, and any harmless butterflies of anxieties had been digested and shit out at this stage. But he still had to hurl. Maybe if he did, he wouldn’t feel so scared anymore. Then he caught the gaze of a thirty something year old man. His lightning blue eyes were highlighted by his black hair (save the few grays that were emerging at the sides). And they were talking to him without saying a word. Go on, vomit on my shoes…do it…then I’ll rip you’re fucking throat out. "We’re going to send you down…" a voice whispered to his immediate left. He turned around and caught a glimpse of a detective in a tan suit. The impact of a balled fist from directly in front of him, blinded him of the face that had whispered so casually in his ear. Lightning blue eyes…that was you, wasn’t it? "Face forward Mick!" Yes, it was you. Red flashed over his inner eye. And it came in the form of it’s own message. Kill them it said. I’m going to fucking kill them Paddy replied to himself. Anger diluted the fear. He liked that, so he let himself get more and more angry, allowing himself to think of breaking into each and everyone of their houses and ripping their fucking eyes out. They won’t be staring at anyone then. Despite the rage, a voice of reason told him, to struggle would to make matters worse. Not only would they have an excuse to beat him within an inch of his life, that they would surely take, but now they would have him for assault. And then he would go down. Just like they wanted. Then the voice returned. "You scumbag…you think you can get away with detonating bombs? Did you think we wouldn’t find out about you? You didn’t make it very hard, I’ll grant you that, leaving your evidence about? Amateur it would seem. Don’t worry, there won’t be anymore of those activities. Not when we’re done with you anyway." "What are ye talk-" SMACK! That same damn fist cut him off. Next time he’s biting partner. "No talking!" Yadda yadda. You want to converse, you don’t want to converse. That same lie is generic, you’ve said it to every man and woman whose been pulled into a wagon like this. It’s getting old, your lies are getting old, and you’re not going to get an inch with your mind games. He was confused, but he wasn’t stupid. He hadn’t planted any bomb, and they sure as hell weren’t going to talk him around into believing it. And furth- "In a skip, one hundred meters from the scene…you made it bloody easy for us, didn’t you?" There was confusion in Paddy’s face as the detective held up the Irishman’s navy puffer jacket, one he had last seen the day before. Then his face contorted into bitter realization. They had his jacket, ‘discovered’ it at the scene of the crime…by Christ, they had him, they had hardened into a concrete block and dangling over the edge of a bridge… He didn’t feel angry or afraid anymore. He felt damn suicidal. By hook or by crook…we‘re going to send you down. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Oh Christ….they have us now…they really have us pinned to the wall now. Tell me again, Paddy didn’t do anything. I mean they’re talking about a crime scene! Paddy didn’t kill anybody…right? Of course we didn’t. But, that jacket…something isn’t right. Was Paddy even wearing that jacket?…When he went into town that is. He wasn’t…I’m almost sure he wasn’t… So…you’re saying they planted it? Possibly…but that still doesn’t explain why HE was the one they swiped. Back in the 70’s and 80’s, they framed people they believed to be in the IRA. The policy of "punishment for another crime". Yet Paddy hasn’t been in the North in ten years and he’s back a day and they frame him? It doesn’t make sense. Man, admit it, we’re fucked. …I don’t know…I just don’t know….but it’s that jacket…it has to be something about that jacket… … Tune in next time folks when we find out what happened the day before.
