Sometime's, they screw up... (Final RP)

23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-23 “How was your day?” Did that question really get asked? Did it even really have an answer that could fit the problems that occurred to me in this day? But as I sit here, gazing at the fresh upbeat lights and aroma smell that came from the kitchen, I could help but try and understand why I went through it all and I still managed to get here. From the attack of the woman to a soft passionate kiss that I could still feel, it was like the old saying, mo money, mo problems. But in this case, it wasn’t money that was the attachment. It was just my life. I sat there in the chair, holding the dark red wine that was poured into my glass. I just gazed up at Thomas who sat there in his sharp looking black suit. “It was interesting.” Interesting? What that all I could say? I doubt that anyone would call this day interesting. Thomas had a smile on his face as he placed his hand on mine. “Well I hope it was everything that I promised. I tried so hard to make it special.” Thomas said, pulling my hand up to his soft looking lips before kissing it. That was what I loved about Thomas. He knew how to be romantic. I just sat there, taking a long sip of my wine before the waiter came walking up to us, looking straight at me with a smile. “Hi. My name is Eric and I’ll be your waiter for tonight.” His eyes glimmered a little. He couldn’t of been any age over twenty. “You must be Elia Smith.” He knew me? Oh this was good. Thomas must of went all out for tonight. I lowered my head, trying not to think of the girl that Thomas was kissing but she kept ringing in my ear. Even though that was just several hours ago, it seemed as if it was just seconds ago. “Well Eric, I’ll have the Salmon tonight.” I said, closing the book that was already in front of me. I figured what the hell. If Thomas was good enough o cheat on me, I was good enough to make him spend money on me. That was what mom did to dad. I remember she told me that when I had my first boyfriend. Thomas just nodded, like he wanted the same. I slowly pulled my hand away, pushing back on the chair from the table. “I have to go to the ladies room.” The place was beautiful. Somewhere that I could never afford without hard work. But as I passed by the people that were in the place, smelling the warm food that was brought to their tables, I couldn’t help but feel hungrier. I walked into the bathroom, having to step back a little from the bright lights that were set up. “Damn…” I could see anything at the start but as my eyes cleared, I could do nothing but stand in awe. It was like nothing I had ever seen. Fresh white tile on the walls with pearl like marble on the counters. It was a dream come true. I just knew that I was going to have a bathroom like this. Someday, someday after I was old and retired from wrestling, I was going to be the rich girl in my family. Doing my business, I walked out of the room, looking over the long room that was in front of me. It seemed more crowded for some reason and I didn’t get a good eye on my table. Searching though the rich old people and their nice clothing, I saw Thomas. Standing up at the table with a woman. I was startled for a second but as she turned around, that dark hair gave way. It was her. What was she doing there? I walked through the crowd, as they stood, talking, holding hands, and what not. Did he think I was a fool or something? Thomas leaned down, giving the woman a long huge before she stepped away, going in to the crowd of people as my man, Thomas, sat right back down. “Who was that?” I asked, trying not to sound jealous or scared or even angry. Thomas’s face gave way that he knew something was up but wasn’t sure. Looking around the room like the answer was there, he just grinned, drinking from the glass of wine that was sitting in front of him. “Don’t know who you are talking about. No one here but me.” Thomas’s words gave way of him being scared. The tight and sharp words that usually mean that you did something and got caught. Thomas sat the glass down as he reached for my hand. Not really wanting to give it, I did anyways. But for a few moment’s, all the rage that I did feel deep inside sort of went away. Looking over at the window to see the waterfall that the restaurant had in the back. I looked at it like it was my life. Going down hill into a puddle of nothingness. I pressed up out of the table, walking towards the door that lead to the back of the place. I couldn’t do it. I just couldn’t do it but I knew what I did have to do. “What’s wrong Elia?” Thomas said, walking right behind me. The place was cold and it made my skin get goose bump’s. I kept walking towards the water, listing to it’s sharp slaps from the rock’s that made the waterfall. Do I tell him the truth or not? I knew that I didn’t want to live a life like this but I knew I couldn’t live alone either. I did love him but my heart had gone through enough. I pressed my hands on the rocks as Thomas looked right at me, showing his eyes with a few small tears almost coming out. “Listen,” Thomas said, placing his hands on mine. “There is something I need to ask you. I wanted to wait but this right here, it is the right time.” “Elia,” “Yes?” “Will you marry me?” The words that every woman would love to hear. And at this very second, those words were asked. As I looked down from the nice water fall, I could see the sparkle in Thomas’s eyes. Just like a kids who got the bike that he wanted at Christmas, he eyes danced from side to side. His smile connected from his left to his right ear as my hand was held, feeling the sweat pour off the two of our hands. “Ye…” What was I to say? I wasn’t sure of anything as I felt the tears work up. Not sad tears, no. They were the kind that you got when you have your first baby. They were the kind that you got when your live was changed by something that you only dream about. I shook my head as the rain from the clouds above began to pour down. “Elia Katrina Smith,” Thomas said, looking up at my crying and wet face, staring down towards the love of my life. “Please say yes so that I can spend the rest of my life with you forever.” I knew the answer. I knew the words that I was to say but they just wouldn’t come out. They were so clear. So right for me to place out but I couldn’t get them to. I looked down at his sparkling screaming face, ready to hear me say that one word… “No…” Did I just say that? Did I just say no to the man that I loved, to the man that was there for me, but the man that had hurt me just as well? Thomas stood up from the ground, looking deep at my face and confused. I just couldn’t. I wanted to say yes to him. Grow old with kids and be the woman that he wanted both in life and in his mind. But there were to many questions that I needed answered. I need to know who the woman was. I needed to know how he really felt about me. I needed to know, most of all, how I still felt about him. I pulled my hands away from him, feeling the cold of the night once more before turning away, walking back into the restaurant. And as I did, I felt the need to look one more time. Turning around, Thomas stood with his back to me. Holding on to the rocks for support. Sometime's things don’t go as planned in life. Sometime's, they screw up…
