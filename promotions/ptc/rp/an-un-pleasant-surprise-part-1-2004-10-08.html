An (un)pleasant surprise Part 1

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 After the death of his father Nick had been nothing but sitting in a corner being sad. But now he appears to enjoy life again. At least so it seems to be the case. But maybe he is so enlightened because he is seeing challenges again. Instead of facing Inphino Blitz, already declared the winner of the bracket before it ever happened, he is now facing Mr. Darkness. He didn’t prepare for this. Nobody gave Mr. Darkness even a chance in this match. Nick Persia was astonished when he heard the news. But he wasn’t happy. If people were giving the bracket to Inphino Blitz, and this man beat Mr. Blitz then he is even more of a threat. Although they didn’t give Nick a chance against Jerms either. But Nick beat the odds as well! In wrestling there is an unspoken rule: never say never. Today it’s Friday, Nick’s favourite day. The last day of the workweek and on the end of the day you are really anxious to get home so you can spend your whole weekend with your family and friends. It would begin tonight when he would go over for dinner to his only sister. His nephew just turned seven, to be exactly a week ago. So Nick could give his only nephew a present and could catch up with his sister. Nick was driving to the city of Breda, which is near the border with Belgium. After being stuck in the traffic for over an hour Nick became somewhat moody. He took a turn to the right with his car and now he was already in the right street. A place to park was hard to find, but luckily there was one spot left, accidentely right before his sister’s house. As he stepped out of the car a cold wind breezed into his face, sending him shivers on his back. In comparison to the United States of America it was quite cold here, especially when you only wear a t-shirt. He quickly ran to the door, to warm his body and to escape from the cold as soon as possible. He knocked on the wooden door with his bare knuckles, which gave him a big splinter in his hand. The door hadn’t been painted at all, nor had it been giving any kind of treatment at all. The door was opened with a fast, swift move and a girl appeared in front of the door. Her skin was tanned, much more than Nick was. Her eyes were brown, chestnut-brown. Her hair was painted red, as it obviously didn’t match the colour of her eyes. She was just a foot smaller than Nick, but she looked so small and fragile. But although all the differences even a blind man could see she was Nick’s sister. Finally in the place where he could truly be himself. The house of his sister now, but it once belonged to his parents. This is where he grew up as a child and a feeling of nostalgia fell upon him. He remembered a few things, but no real memories, just a few flashbacks. Not that he really had the time to remember the days of old since his seven year old newphew was already asking him to death about him. “Uncle Nick, I saw you on the telly last week,” the young boy said when he noticed him. “Oh really?” Nick asked. “What did you see me do then?” “I saw you kicking that other guy,” he said full of enthusiasm in his voice. “When you nailed that Dutch destroyer the man was laying on the floor doing nothing anymore. Can you teach me that? Since that asshole next door is always bullying me!” “Wow, watch that language a bit here! I don’t think your mum will appreciate that,” Nick told his nephew. “And I won’t teach you my very own finisher, because all the children in the neighbourhood won’t be safe for you anymore.” “At least I can still them you are my uncle,” the boy said with a smile. “Yes, you can, Nathan,” Nick replied chuckling. While the boy kept chipping away at Nick, his sister entered the room with some coffee. The steam was still damping off the coffee telling us it’s still very hot. However, the two boys in the room didn’t seem to pay any attention at all to her as they were talking to each other. As she looked at the two she smiled for a small moment, before coughing on purpose to draw some attention. “I brought some coffee,” she notified. “That’s mean,” Nathan complained. “You know I don’t like coffee.” Both brother and sister exploded into a laughing buster by this serious comment. The boy looked on with a surprised look on his face, since he didn’t seem to understand why they are laughing. “Of course, you can get your cola,” she said still half laughing. “You really worked on the house as of late, Chantal,” Nick spoke full of admiring. “Especially after the problems you had with the condition with this house just over a year ago. Really amazing. I like what you have done with the place.” As Nick spoke those words, his eyes catched something, or didn’t catch something. “But where is the old clock from Grandma?” Nick asked completely shocked. The clock had always been there for as long as he can remember. It used to hang on the opposite of the door so you could see the time if you walked in. Their parents as well, and that’s the most important reason why he remembered it. All the times he was late due to that little clock. “I sold it to a collector,” Chantal answered. “It doesn’t match the other furniture in the room anyway and he is going to be happy with it, so why not?” “Probably the best thing to do, anyway,” Nick agreed. “Where is Ron by the way? I believed he was supposed here as well?” “Oh yes,” she confirmed. “But he’s still working I believe. Now that he has his own company he is working a lot more hours.” “He still managed to make this a nice place,” Nick commentated. “And the boy is a happy kid as well.” Young Nathan was watching television now, as one of his favourite series: Power Rangers had now begun. Nick looked at him, as he had no idea what the cold outside world could do to him. Besides a bullying neighbour. “He is, but he also completely worships you,” she added. “He is doing nothing but talking about you at school. His mentor has called me twice about this already!” Nick walked towards the boy, and dropped himself right next to the boy. “Nathan, there’s something I need to tell you.” The boy turned his head to Nick, while Nick continued. “I heard from your teacher that you have been acting like me as of late. Now you must know…….this can’t go on. You need to become your own person on this world. Not the second Nick. Do you understand what I am saying?” “I guess so,” the boy said with his eyes fixed on the ground, avoiding Nick’s face. “So no more powerbombs, no more wrestling at all. You could seriously hurt another child with this. If I ever hear you do that, there will be hell to pay!” Nathan was pretty impressed with this speech, as Nick normally didn’t say anything about what he should or should not do. But what Nick says is the gospel. “Now look at that action!” Nick pointed towards the television. A huge massacre had now broken out between the Red Power ranger and his arch rival. Suddenly, a big explosion could be hurt and the enemy was blown away. “The red power ranger won!” the boy exclaimed. “Nathan,” a voice shouted from behind. Nathan looked up and a large man, a bigger copy of himself appeared in his sight. “Dad!” was all he said as he ran towards his father. Nick got back to his feet again. He was feeling pretty stiff, after that long trip he took from Amsterdam to Breda. He stretched his muscles for a bit, and he felt a tiny bit better after that. “I’m sorry I am so late,” the man explained. “But I had to finish a project today.” “No need to apologize, Ron,” Nick said on a gentle tone. He didn’t like people who arrive too late, in fact he hates them. *Ding* The microwave was done with the warming of the spaghetti. A few moments later Chantal walked with a big pan to the table and everybody took their place. Nick was the last person to take a seat. They all take a big amount of spaghetti out of the pan. Without saying a word they eat the whole meal. They stare to each other, but don’t say anything. That’s a habit over here. It’s a habit. Its origin is that if you talk while eating the devil can poison it if you speak badly. Of course none of this is true, but people don’t speak while eating for so long now it became a habit. After they finished dinner, Nick starts to speak again. “It was a really nice meal, Chantal!” Nick complimented her. “Have you moved already?” she asked. “Not yet! I’m busy looking, but they are all so expensive. And I am busy with my work also.” “You have all the money in the world to let people move your stuff for you, Nick!” she remarked. “I think you are still too lazy to even bother watching.” “I do watch, as often as I can. But I have a very tight schedule. This week I’m facing Mister Darkness and I have my UWF commitments as well. I’m not a super human.” Nick spoke with some fury in his voice. “And when I am not wrestling I am training or preparing for a match.” “I heard all about the fame he gathered as of late,” Chantal said. “I think that’s excellent. This way, people won’t be expecting anything from me. I can go in the ring with absolutely no worries. The pressure is on him, not on me,” he replied. Nick took a look at his watch, and to his shock he found it is six pm already. “Nathan, I think it’s time we go.” The youngster had a confused expression on his face, as he didn’t know what he meant. “Where are we going then?” He was going to see hell and Nick was going to hell itself. It’s a fairly ironic question Nick thought to himself. Because in some way you could answer almost anything. Fame, hell, money, people are just a tiny example of all the possibilities. But they had one thing in common and that’s the fact that all the answers were correct. “You’ll see, Nathan,” Nick replied. “We wouldn’t want to ruin the surprise, now would we?” Even for Nick Persia it would all be a surprise. Since a lot of things have changed in the past few weeks. Even for Nick Persia………
