Date: 2005-11-27 "Gwynn's impressive." Langsuyar (PrimetimeCentral.net) "Gwynn all the way because he simply PWNS anyone … " BraveHeartFan (PrimetimeCentral.net) " … irreplaceable talent … " [name withheld] (CoreWrestling.net) "The Avatar" William Gwynn does not pretend to be either blind or deaf to his surroundings. He knows that this tournament features wrestlers world renowned, famous and infamous. He knows that some names barely acknowledged by many in this tournament are heroes and villains of little-known federations about the World, and pose a great threat of being that Dark Horse that so many fear. Just weeks before "The Avatar" William Gwynn joined Core, there was once such dark horse, in a tournament known as WrestleGod Four. That man was aptly nicknamed "The Dark Horse" in that tournament. His name was Chris Neptune, and he was one of William Gwynn's several mentor in Stone's Academy in Phoenix, Arizona. Chris Neptune swept through that field of one-hundred and twenty-eight with many of the names that are in this one. The tournament never finished. Eight were left at the end, and several of the other seven are calling themselves "co-victors". William Gwynn's trainer has often said that William is the most perfect technical wrestler he has ever had the opportunity to train. He has told William repeatedly that he could go far in any tournament, in any federation. Stone says that William has the two things that are needed in both a champion and a victor, incredible drive and incredible talent. Perhaps these are simply words meant to motivate "The Avatar" William Gwynn, but William knows better. William knows that the talent is there for him to do well in this tournament. He knows that no matter what fate decides, what destiny determines, he has the talent to slice through several rounds of this Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. But, as in all of his matches, he knows that the key to his success is his will, what he is willing to do to go out and win matches in this tournament. The truth is that William Gwynn has horrified himself in what he is willing to do to win matches in this tournament. He has sickened himself. "I cannot believe," he has said almost too many times to his two greatest friends, "that you convinced me to do this." The other two haven't been able to stop laughing since William agreed. Their names are frequent throughout the lore of William Gwynn, or what shortly will be. Thus, it will be critical for fans to get to know them. George ("Gus") Killibrew and Walter ("Igloo") Kulowiyi have been William Gwynn's greatest friends and fans for many years. George was his best friend in Scotland, before he moved to Arizona. And Walter was his best friend in Arizona. When George moved to Phoenix several years after William, the three became quick friends, and George and Walter became William's support system during his early, unstable wrestling career. "Oh, William," laughs Walter, "it will be fun. I mean, come on. How often has this side of you been shown on camera? All we get to see nowadays is charitable William. We get to see you with kids from the "Make a Wish Foundation". We get to see you giving money to museums. But never have we gotten to see one of your classic idiot moments with women. Believe me, Gus and I can tell this story better than anybody, about that time when you - " "No, no," Gus orders, "don't give that away yet, we want to surprise William as well as his fans." Walter shakes his head. "William, you've got to admit," he says, "this is so much more entertaining than listening to you talk for days. You can't scare people like Seymour Almasy, Angelo Deville, Rich Rollins or Kimbusa by tellimg them that you're Core Wrestling Legacy Champion. Seriously. Scaring those guys is out of the question. You're one of those guys that has to come out of nowhere and make people notice and fear you. What is it they call them?" "Cinderella stories!" Gus contributes. "Yes!" Walter says. "I prefer "Dark Horses"," William mutters. "It doesn't matter what you prefer," Walter declares, "you're going to be the GTT5 Cinderella Story!" Walter grins, and William rolls his eyes. Gus yawns and stretches. "So anyway, William," Gus says, "we're going to show these people the most humiliating moments of your life. These people are going to feel BAD for you after all this. They won't care about your huge contract, of which you contribute a whole hell of a lot to charity, or your wrestling skills. They're all going to be talking about how pure your intentions were with - " "Don't give it away!" " - right," Gus says, with a smile, "but for once, William, as whats-her-face said, 'You've got all the wrong moves'. Now, let's go get some ice cream." "Excellent," Walter grins. The two leave the room, and William is left shaking his head. "How can you be a Cinderella Story when you're favored by that infallible nerd crew at Primetime Central?" William asks, he's not answered. The door opens, and Walter and Gus pull William through.
