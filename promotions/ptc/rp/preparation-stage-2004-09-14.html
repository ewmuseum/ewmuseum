Preparation Stage

14 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-14 [6:00 pm, Tuesday night] [Preparation Period Has Begun] ::: The scene opens up in a large, elegant room. The walls are covered in paintings of famous people, and the objects scattered through the room go along with the theme of an old victorian house. The camera pans around the room, every single detail of the room visible to the lens of the camera. The camera finally pans to a shot of Phil, sitting on the edge of a very comfortable looking bed, with his head lowered, and his hands shuffling through a box at his feet. He pulls out several objects from the box, and places them on his left side, not taking long to look at any of them. After taking out about 5 items, he slides the box underneath the bed, and stands up. He stretches his back and walks over to the near by table, and takes a drink from the water bottle laying on it ::: Botwin-"Another federation closed... as soon as I leave a federation, it always seems to close, and Neon is no different." ::: Phil smirks towards the camera, a small glimmer of joy in his eyes ::: Botwin-"A week... exactly ONE week after I am booted out the doors, the doors close for good, and Neon holds a 'closed' sign over its now dead heart. Why do these people fail to see that I am the best thing that happened to their federation. I am the reason people tune into their programming. I am the reason they have viewership, and I am also the reason other wrestlers are drawn to the promotion." ::: An egotistic grin appears on Phil's face as he flexes his muscules towards the camera. He takes a giant gulp of water from the bottle, and continues to speak ::: Botwin-"They want to try and defeat me, even though they don't stand a chance. They stand opposite me in the ring... hoping I will falter... hoping I will screw up and allow them to capitalize and take home a victory... but for what?" ::: Phil takes another drink from the water bottle ::: Botwin-"So that they can told a few minutes of spotlight clinging to a victory won from a fluke? Claiming that they are superior because they have pinned me down for a small, uninpressive 3 seconds? Shock, suprise or being stunned can cause someone to not be able to react right away and cause them to lose... but it takes someone with a lot of skill to be able to claim a victory with no suprise attacks, no big stunning move that renders his opponent out for longer then the 3 seconds required to win... longer then that small amount of time people claim big victories out of." ::: Phil takes a drink from the water bottle, and walks back over to the bed, the water bottle still lingering in his hand. He looks over the stuff he has placed on the bed, and picks up a picture with his free hand. Displayed on the picture is Phil holding up a title belt from an unknown federation ::: Botwin-"I, like most people, started off small... really small. Wrestling any event possible just to make ends meet. Yearning for the opurtunity to make it big and have your name up in lights. Doing anything to stop fighting in front of crowds that barely extend into the hundreds, to crowds that exceed the hundreds of thousands. Everybody is in wrestling for the money... not to prove anything to anybody... not to be loved by millions of people around the world... but to claim the hefty cheque that comes every 2 weeks." ::: Phil puts up 2 fingers, representing the amount of weeks in between pay cheques, then laughes to himself, his head lowered, and the emotion on his face invisible to the camera ::: Botwin-"To claim the six figure income that makes putting your life on the line day in and day out feel all that more secure... all that easier to do, and all that simpler to swallow. Come on... who would put their life on the line just for the thrill of preforming in front of thousands of people, when you can just get a talk show, and do it safely from the comfort of your chair. Look at Jerry Springer... the most he has to worry about, is tripping down the stairs. He doesn't have to worry about someone bashing his brains in with a steel chair, or throwing him 50 feet to a slab of concrete, yet everyone still knows him." ::: Phil places the picture back down on the bed, and moves to a table on the other side of the room, where a suitcase sits open, full of a few sets of clothes, and other items people normally pack for a trip. He puts another black t-shirt into the suitcase and closes it. He lifts it off the table, and puts it down next to the door before moving back to the memorabilia on the bed ::: Botwin-"If you were to poll 100 people... how many of them would recognize the name Jerry Springer? 80? 85? Probably even higher. Yet, what does he do? He walks around on a stage insulting people in front of a crowd. That is only half of what wrestlers do, yet if you were to ask 100 people if they knew even the most famous wrestlers... how many would have heard of them? 40? 30?" ::: Phil's face changes to one of disappointment. He shakes his head in disgust and looks deep into the camera ::: Botwin-"It is because wrestling is no longer considered a sport. It has been drawn into the world of 'sports entertainment', where no one dares to venture, except for the few brave ones. Were a sub-culture in todays society. Were viewed as entertainers, not athletes... our entire sport is not taken seriously. We are all judged based on our ability to talk... not on our skills, and certainly not on our ability to take a beating and get right back up. It sickens me..." ::: Just as Phil finishes talking, the door to the room swings open and in walks someone resembling a stereotyped butler ::: Butler-"Phil... you have mail in the library." Botwin-"Thank you Mr. Thomas." ::: Mr. Thomas exits the room, as Phil takes one last look over the stuff laid out on the bed. Phil then walks towards the door, leaving all of the memorabilia on the bed. He grabs the suitcase and exits the room as the scene cuts out ::: [6:20 pm] ------------------------------------------------------------------ [6:35, 15 minutes later] ::: The scene opens up with Phil stomping back and forth in the elegant library of his huge mansion. He has a crumpled piece of paper in his hand, and an angered look on his face. He slams the piece of paper down onto the table, and slides out one of the chairs. He sits down in the chair, and slides the paper back over to himself, unwrinkling it and looking down at it ::: Botwin-"What is it with people, and giving me the nobodies! Who has ever heard of this 'Horazon' guy, and who really cares what his past accomplishments are? None of that will matter once I get my hands on him... the only thing that will matter is, if he can afford the many hospitial bills I will be throwing onto his lap. All the rehibilitation he will need to become 100% again... He shouldn't be so much worrying about before, or during the match, but should be preparing for his post-match situation." ::: Phil throws a file of papers that is laying on the table across the room. He looks around the room, an obvious look of disgust on his face ::: Botwin-"I can't give anyone the exact details, but I can assure you, it will involve a long recovpry time, and a wheelchair. Ever been in a wheelchair? It really doesn't look all that ammusing to be stuck in the sitting position for as long as you'll be needing the chair. How can I assure you this? Just look at my shoulder, and you will see the Neon Cruiserweight Tit..." ::: Phil begins to grab at his shoulder, looking for the Cruiserweight Title. He looks over at his bare shoulder, and his arm falls to his side ::: Botwin-"Crap... I had to give it back to that crummy dictator Mr H. when I was fired by him... even though a week later, his beloved company went belly up... damnit.. " ::: Phil's head falls forward, landing onto the table in front of him right onto the piece of paper. His eyes are closed, and he begins to pound his fist down onto the table. He brings it down for a third time, when he opens his eyes. He begins to repeatidly say damnit, getting six out before stopping, and lifting his head from the table. He runs his finger along the paper, coming across the name of Tony Aliso ::: Botwin-"Ah... my old buddy from Neon... it seems as though he has found his way to this tournament as well... hope you make it out a few rounds... you might be able to get the match we would have had in Neon." ::: Phil turns the piece of paper over, and begins to scribble something on the back of it ::: Botwin-"You know... the one where we would have decided the first ever World Champion, if I hadn't been fired, and the company had stuck around for a few more weeks? Yah.. that match. Hopefully we do end up meeting in this tournament... I want you to feel the pain that I inflicted onto Colin Dzur during my first match in Neon... hah... did Colin ever return to Neon after I broke his leg? If he did, it was only to hand in his retirement slip." ::: Phil laughes at his own joke then looks around the room for a minute, then continues to scribble on the piece of paper in front of him ::: Botwin-"And what happened to Shawn Tryant? After he faced me... didn't he end up leaving the company before it shut down? Do I not have a streak of causing retirements in that fed before its 'unfortunate' closure? I had Colin Dzur and Shawn Tryant leaving the company at my hands. It felt so good to be the cause of a retirement... but the second one just gave me the best feeling... even better then the one I got when I won the Cruiserweight Title. Just goes to show you how worthless the belts actually are compared to some of the things you can do in a federation." ::: Phil lifts the paper up in front of him, reading over what he has down so far, but the paper is too far from the camera for the words to be visible. The paper is half full of writing, and the rest is blank. Phil lowers the paper back down and continues to write ::: Botwin-"How exactly do you prepare for a match with the best a company has to offer? How exactly do you get ready for the wrath you are about to face? It's impossible... and Horazon, you should take these words to heart. No one that has come into contact with me, has ever lived their life to the fullest. I take years off of peoples lives, and I do it with a smile. I'll break your arm... your leg, and I'll even have the manners to ask you if you've had enough... but your answer doesn't count towards anything, I will just continue to inflict pain upon you until you cannot answer my question... inflict enough pain that you will forget what being healthy is like, and what exactly it feels like to have use of your limbs. I am a one man wrecking crew... not by my own claims, but by what I've done to other people... and you shall be no different." ::: Phil finishes scribbling on the paper, and tosses the pencil over his shoulder ::: Botwin-"Ah.. nothing more I'd like then to face any of my old Neon 'buddies'... maybe take a few of them out of the tournament that I will end up winning by a long shot. Maybe show them what they missed by not having a match with me in Neon, and show them what Colin Dzur felt like when our match was done." ::: Phil begins to crumple the piece of paper once again, but stops halfway through. He straightens the part listing his match, and looks down upon it ::: Botwin-"No matter how many times I look at your name Horazon... it just never really gives me any sympathy for you. It's not as though you weren't given the chance to back down. You could always not show up for our match." ::: Botwin half-heartidly smiles ::: Botwin-"But then again... what kind of fun would that be... at least... what kind of fun would I have with you not showing up? I would lose the oppurtunity to kick your ass, AND show the other competitors what I'm made of. Just like I showed the wrestlers of Neon... look at everyone I beat. Shawn Tryant... Jakob Knight... Kris Krimzon... ah Kris Krimzon... what happened to him after I beat him? Oh! I remember now! He quit!" ::: Phil laughes at his own joke about Kris Krimzon, flipping the paper over, and re-reading what he wrote down ::: Botwin-"He couldn't stand the fact that I ended his undefeated streak, so he cried like a little girl, and quit the federation. He couldn't stand up to me, so he decided to put his tail between his legs, and scurry off where no one would find him. Horazon, if you have even half the intelligence I have, which would be a lot... you would not show up for our match... a functioning body is worth a lot more then dignity... keep that in mind." ::: Phil stands up from the table and moves to the door, exiting the room. The camera moves over to the table, and hovers over the wrinkled piece of paper. Only a few words are readable, all the rest is in handwriting that would take closer inspection to read. A few of the words visible include "victums", "Horazon", and "GTT4"... ::: [6:55, The End]
