He also lost his dignity as he became the second worst South Wind title holder ever. Yeah, the man too good for Hollywood feels like he’s barely good enough for a predictable Bollywood film involving culture and all of that bullshit… :: :: And there are two things on Chris’ mind which make the thought of his defeat excruciating. He never even touched that fucking South Wind title. He wanted to feel what the Nightmare title felt like and he never because of that scum-sucking bastard Lee Satima. Not only is Chris thrown back into the championship scene after his humiliation on the 7th, but he must face three wankers in a fucking TLC match. What’s going to happen when Chris Sanchez wins that? It is very much connected to his first loss. It was in the match against Dark Angel. Something that Chris Sanchez sat alone in his study in his LA mansion all week playing on his video, rewinding, playing, rewinding and playing again. DING!!! :: :: Rewind… Play… Knuckles falls to the floor in great disappoint. Rewind… Play… Knuckles falls to the floor in great disappoint. Suddenly he violently threw the tape against the wall causing it to smash into several pieces that were covered by loose tape. :: Sanchez: Everything’s the fucking matter. I have some drugged up vampire running away with my property! Then when I defend my South Wind title against Dark Angel, which should have never happened, I lost it! Since Lee Satima has decided to join the Shadow’s Ascension, anybody in the Shadow’s Ascension who gets in the way of me fighting Lee will pay. Because if they get involved into matters that not concern themselves personally, they become my business. You know my mind has been ticking. I am so fucking sick of acting like the bad guy. The big-ass motherfucking Hollywood star. Always in my heart and mind, I’ve known that dark people like Lee Satima and the Shadow’s Ascension are wrong. It makes my body ache thinking about them. And I guarantee death to Lee Satima, however if Bullet, Tarrant, or even Patti if she stops being a whore by showing herself off like a piece of meat, I will try and end them. Fuck the consequences, man. Knuckles: I’ll be there right beside you all of the time. I will make sure nobody hurts you on your mission. Sanchez: Ah… Um… Speaking of you being beside me. Knuckles, I’m afraid I’ll have to suspend you from duty until Sunday 27th. Knuckles: What?!??! Sanchez: The simple fact is you failed. Your work has been two-hundred times better than what I expected when J-Lo recommended me to get a bodyguard. You’re big, strong and can fucking fight when you can. But with a big match like a TLC match at the first 4WW pay per view I’m in coming up, we all need to rest. So do you. I want you to go relax, visit family etcetera and see you Sunday night. :: Knuckles didn’t know how to react. He literally felt like Chris was his best friend and in five years, had never been more than twelve hours without speaking to him on exception of Christmas. Knuckles turned a bit upset. What if Chris got harmed in the next few days or so? But reluctantly, Knuckles slowly walked out of the room with his head held down. If this was what a holiday was like for Knuckles, imagine a firing. :: :: Meanwhile Chris stood there reflecting on what he had just preached to Knuckles. Thinking about it, he didn’t want to fight any of the Shadow’s Ascension except for Lee if he had the choice. But he could imagine with this cult, that if one gets hurt, the rest get pissed off like killer bees do. The biggest match of his career was rushing towards him and Chris’ mind was about as clear as a New York road. He needed to clear his mind. :: :: Chris waited until the door closed meaning Knuckles had exited before walking over to the light switch and switching off the light. He then slowly closed the door as he exited and sneaked away to leave the house himself to go stroll around the streets of Los Angeles. And for the first time ever, he was strolling around without his bodyguard and he wasn’t strolling around in a vehicle either. :: :: Chris Sanchez’s mind is going, going, gone. Losing his first match and his first championship in one match adds to the triple whammy of becoming the second shortest South Wind champion ever of just one week. Everything that he once knew about his ego and all of the theories that he was an undefeatable machine have fallen into a vortex full of crap. :: :: Because of this, Chris has becoming very uncomfortable to confront. His own child was reluctant to talk to him and his wife is baffled to know what the cure is to her husband’s problem. Chris, himself, has hated Chris Sanchez and is finding it uncomfortable for him to look at himself in the mirror. :: :: Chris’ mind has been making harsh and weird decisions too.
