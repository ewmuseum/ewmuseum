Bad Trip

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-11 [Unexpected it might have seemed, but somehow Smokey Wead managed to pull off the win against Kirk Urban. The little pot head managed a minor upset by actually getting beyond the first round match. Nevertheless Smokey still has 4 matches at most against him, the next being against a much more tough foe than the last. He needs to regain some focus, otherwise he’ll be facing two opponents in the bracket semi-finals] [The Scene Opens, as it does so the first thing to hit the viewer is the sound of Hippie freak out music playing softly, as the music can, in the back ground. As the scene becomes more visible, we can see it’s an aerial view of Smokey Wead’s apartment. In the main focus of the scene, lying limply on a pile of assorted stuff on the floor is Smokey. Still wearing the Hawaiian shirt with the vest and the baggy jeans he lays sprawled as if he was just dumped there by someone, he probably was going by his track record. Still, the look given to the camera isn’t the most hopeful of looks, sure he may be smiling like everything in the world is perfect, but the look itself is unnerving, as a few giggles pass his dry lips, we can see the end of a smoked roll up dangling from one finger. Eyes half open, the visible parts blood shot. The camera spirals in closer to him, the music still playing over the entire scene. The close up face looks worse than it did further away. Obviously the natural high after advancing in the tournament wasn’t enough for the skinny man. The grin present is almost as bad as his friend Bubba’s, possibly slightly worse, as the expression going along with it is more frightening than any horror picture you may come across, this is the face that epitomises the phrase “Too much of a bad thing”. More giggling and whines like that of a mentally handicapped person pass the lips of Smokey, his brain a mince of memories and experiences, all leading to the pink cloud around his common sense area of his brain keeping him cheery and dumb. One limb vaguely tries to reach for the camera, the grin staying as it is, not changing for anything] [Looking on to this shameful episode of the wrestler is his Tag Partner Bubba and the man in the suit, the man keeping Smokey in tune with the tournament side of things, both looking on in despair at the man who’s now just a shell of what he was just a few hours before. They look at each other with blank expressions before looking back at Smokey who lets out another baby like noise as he tries to reach for the camera above his head] [Doesn’t look too good does it when you think about it, man in his prime wasting away, killing himself a little bit each time he goes for a cheap thrill, or not so cheap with the prices this stuff he’s on costs. His hair is all matted and all over the place, still that rather depressing gaze on his face, we can only imagine what’s going on in his head, and with the wonders of modern television techniques, we can show you] [So now we see the scene through Smokey’s “eyes”, we can see a swirl of brightly coloured shapes, yellows reds and oranges and some greens. The music that is playing over is now all distorted, somehow making more fluidic sense, as if this is how the music is intended to be heard. Two distorted figures are seen in the bottom of the scene lost in the yellow mist of colour, these two figures have to be assumed to be the man in the suit and Bubba, Smokey’s tag team partner. There is another shape in the scene, this is that of the camera hanging above his head, but in Smokey’s eyes it appears to be more like a mobile that would hang above a baby’s cot. Distant echoing voices bellow out, although distorted again, they can still be made out quite distinctly] Voice1: What-at are-re we-ee going-ing to-o do-o with-th him-im? [One of the larger shapes turns towards a littler shape and speaks, the red top expands in Smokey’s minds eye and the redness engulfs the entire figure, soon the jolly fat man is instantly recognisable as Santa, and Smokey decides to tell the world] [The Scene Switches back to the original view, and Bubba has just turned to the man in the suit, Smokey tries to raise his head, but instead just ends up tucking his chin into his neck, arm outstretched, pointing at Bubba] Smokey: Aaana, aaana, eeees aaana! [This makes Bubba and the man in the suit turn back to looks at him. Smokey just wilts back to the relaxed position he was in] Man: So what shall we do with him? [Bubba looks solemnly at his wasted partner, reaches down and takes the spliff end out of his fingers] Bubba: Sleep [The View goes back to Smokey’s world as it stands now, the yellow being chased around the scene by red, the two figures still standing there, the Bubba representation no longer looking like Santa Claus, evidently the Colour ran out of his clothes and proceeded to chase the yellow around his head. The large figure bends down over him and touches his hand briefly before rising up again. As he does, the shadow he cast over him remains across him, sending a cold shiver down his back, the Blue-greyness engulfing both red and yellow, leaving the vivid colours no room to be seen apart from a few flecks here and there. Then the best idea ever to think of goes through his head at that minute making perfect sense] Voice2: Sleeeeeeeeeaaaap-aaap-aaap [And at that moment Smokey Yawned] [Back in normal vision, looking down at Smokey, his mouth opens wide bearing all his teeth, every mouth muscle contorting at the same time and holding for a few moments in a painful grimace. Bubba and the man in the suit look strangely at him until his face returns to the ignorant bliss previously seen] Man: I don’t like this; he’s going to kill himself if he’s not careful. Then what? Jimmy Diablo goes on by default? He’s the only UWF Representative left in the tournament. This- [He points at the wasted Smokey lying on the floor who smiles back up at him vacantly] Man: - is the UWF’s only hope of Cruiserweight glory in this tournament now, after Thai Trang got disqualified. Get him to bed, let him sleep it off Bubba: OK [Bubba Reaches down to Smokey, and instead of just staying limp and lifeless, he starts squirming and screaming in fear at his long time tag team partner. He flails around, limbs going every which way his motive muscles can throw them. Bubba just hits him over the head and he passes out, now hanging like a sack of potatoes from the giant. Thankfully the big man’s had quite a lot of experience with carrying sacks of potatoes] [Scene cuts back to Smokey’s view, in the dark blue gloom we can see a little goblin creature talking to a massive troll, which we can identify as The man in the suit and Bubba, but to Smokey it’s another story. He over hears their garbling speech for a moment] Goblin: Flur mur gur tur bur. Mur mur? Jurr durr bur bur? Blur gur flur mur ger ter mer [The goblin then points at Smokey, obviously ordering the Troll to take him away, this is disastrous] Goblin: Blur mur tur gur bur ger mur, fur tur tur gur mur mur. Gur bur tur ger mur Troll: Mur Mur [The troll bends down and reaches for Smokey, He’s trying to take him away… he’s not getting to, you hear me?! Not getting to! Smokey then tries to get out of the way by running, but from his lying down state this is quite impossible, so instead he starts thrashing at the troll as he picks him up but to no avail, he tries once more before the lights go out] [Scene’s back to normal view, and Bubba now has Smokey in a fireman’s carry position and hauls his scrawny ass to his bedroom. When he gets there he looks at the bed, which is quite close to the open window, and sees the horrible mess that it’s in, the sheets a tangled mess at the bottom, expelling a little sigh he automatically decides that it’s best to do the bed before putting him in it, he grabs the sheet and moves around to the window side. He leaves Smokey balanced on his shoulder as he rears back to flick out the sheet, and as he does so Smokey moves ever so slightly further towards the ground and slips off Bubba’s shoulders and out the window] [We see the apartment block from outside, and from the second storey of the apartment building we see a little brightly coloured figure appear and start plummeting downwards, back first. The Scene then moves to a close shot of a yellow dumpster sitting quite tranquilly on the pavement, before a brightly coloured figure falls into it and it expels a dust cloud a couple feet high, a few cardboard boxes also fly out of it in the sudden updraft, and the softer thud as you might have expected disturbs the surroundings for only a few minutes] [Back in the apartment, we can see that Bubba has now finished making the bed and reaches up to his shoulder for Smokey, and only grabs thin air, he spins about and looks at the window, he sticks his head out and spy’s him in the dumpster] Bubba: Smokey? Smokey: aaaaaaaeeeeeemmmmph [Bubba takes his head back from in the window, jives a little shrug and then climbs into the bed himself, folds of himself hanging out over the bed intended for littler persons] [He begins to snore] [Outside in the dumpster, another snoring noise can be heard] [The Scene Fades to Black]
