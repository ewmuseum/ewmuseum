Soldier of Roses

9 January 2006

Date: 2006-01-09 "I think I’m losing my mind." The words hung heavily on Dawn’s mind, disrupting the previously smothering silence that had enveloped her precious few moments of privacy before the day began in earnest. The reflection in the mirror had no answers for her, staring back with that exact same pair of emerald green eyes, reaching up to absently brush away a strand of strawberry blonde hair as she did the same. The adjoining bathroom was one of the luxuries Dawn was enjoying as a guest of Toshiro Mifune, had she been attending the school as a student she would have been expected to share a large communal bathroom with the rest of her gender. Though not large by any stretch of the imagination the bathroom was heated to protect against winter chill and equipped with all the basic things she needed. Flannel pajama bottoms and a cotton tank top already hung from the hook on the back of the bathroom door, leaving Dawn with nothing to cover her nakedness as she stared into the mirror set above the sink. Every bruise and scratch stood out in stark contrast against her creamy skin, every scar and abrasion creating a break in the otherwise smooth tapestry of her body. Fingers played absently over the most recent of these "wounds’", tracing gently and gingerly over a host of bruises and mat burns obtained during the weeks of struggle for GTT5. She shivered a little, the bathroom heat unable to hold out all of the Hokkaido winter that was cooling the blue tiles pressed against the bottoms of her bare feet. "I’m trying to focus Master, trying to keep my eyes set on the end goal." Master Mifune was obviously not present at the moment, but Dawn’s whispered appeal was intended for no other ears but his. She dropped her head a little, eyes searching the bottom of the sink before they squeezed shut so tightly the skin around her eyes crinkled up like old paper. "But there’s just so much going on…how am I supposed to focus on my wrestling career with all that’s going on?" When she looked back up the mirror still had no answers, staring back at her with distraught eyes before she turned away and forced it to do the same. "Lia." She choked out, half a sigh and half a whisper, "Where are you? I…I need you." Dawn’s absent lover did not materialize in the shadows of the bathroom, nor did she come swooping in through the small window on wings of darkness to carry Dawn away. It had been month’s since the object of Dawn’s affections had disappeared from the McFarlane estate with nothing but a farewell letter and a claim that she needed to sort herself out before she could return. Their chance encounter backstage during Dark Rituals had been like a breath of fresh air for Dawn, like cool lips pressed to her own it had been oh so sweet…and oh so brief. "I’ll wait for you." Dawn had spoken those words then, and she mouthed them now in the silence of the bathroom. How naïve had she been to assume her lovers self imposed exile wouldn’t last more then a month or two at most? She had meant ever word of the promise spoken at Dark Rituals, but there was a hidden fear that rankled in her breast, a sour sensation in her stomach that boiled every time the voice in the back of her mind asked that most important question… "How long will she keep you waiting?" The words from her own lips startled Dawn, drawing out a wry chuckle that she had jumped at the sound of her own voice. With Alex and Michi out searching for Marcus, she’d not expected any sound to disturb her reverie, much less the sound of her own voice. She forced a quiet grin, padding over on chilled feet to the shower stall set across from the sink. "Talking to myself…maybe I really am losing my mind." A deft twist started the shower; one hand assuring the water had turned from icy cold to steaming hot before she stepped into the stall. Water pounded down on her neck and shoulders, slipping down her back and legs, hugging the curves and contours of her body until she was covered head to toe in hot water. Steam floated up from the floor in gentle waves, washing over her as she eased her eyes shut and leaned against the wall of the stall. Problems and worries spun about her mind in a chaotic tempest, whirling thoughts of Marcus and his abuse to the forefront one moment, replacing them with thoughts of GTT5 and her flagging career the next. Only one thought held any precedence over the others, constantly pushing it’s way to the front of her brain and stemming the tide of concerns long enough to force a feeling of importance. Lia. To say Dawn was confused by her feelings for another woman would have been an understatement. It had been bad hard enough to cope with while they lived under the same roof, and even after they had admitted their feelings for each other there was a period of discomfort and confusion…but it all paled in comparison to the emptiness she felt now. Despite the danger and the importance of the situations surrounding her now, she could not dislodge thoughts of her love from her mind. Dreams of raven hair and blood red lips haunted her sleep, while feelings of loneliness and worry dogged her every waking moment. She pressed her hands to the sides of her head, digging fingers into her temples as though attempting to dislodge such feelings by sheer force alone. "Goddammit Lia…why have you left this so long? Why havn’t I heard from you? Why…" Why did you stop caring? The last question slipped into her mind with ease, startling her eyes open and her heart to almost freeze solid in her chest. What if Lia’s absence…was her answer to all these questions? "No!" Dawn almost shouted the reponse, slamming her right fist into the nearest tile. Pain spiked up along the delicate bones of her hand, but she was gratified to see a spider web of cracks spread out from the point of impact. The pain was jarring…and at the same time welcome, cutting through the fog of her mind with a clarity of thought. When I have resolved my mental state, I will return. Do not think that I would ever leave you; I would not, "Do not think that I would ever leave you." Dawn said aloud, trying to draw what strength and comfort she could from those words. "Do not think that I would ever leave you…I would not." The note containing those words, among others, had been carefully folded and placed into a small picture box that now traveled everywhere Dawn went. The scented paper and flowing hand writing gave her a small reminder of her departed love, just as the fiery stud in her upper right ear reminded her of the promises that had been made late at night with whispered breath. Dawn sighed longingly, her skin goose bumping at the thought of her lovers touch, her mind burning with the hope that they would be together again…and through it all was the same voice in the back of her mind, the voice she could not banish for all her hope and all her trying. "But I have to keep trying." She whispered, "I made a promise." The steam had risen to around shoulder level, leaving the impression that Dawn was almost flying through a bank of clouds. With a wry smile she flicked the taps off once again and stepped out onto the bathmat to towel dry. ***************************************************************************************** Dawn: As I write this, I know without a doubt that you've been terribly harsh on yourself. I don't have ESP, but I do know you well enough to know about how you beat yourself up whenever something goes awry. Don't. That's all that I ask of you. Do not blame yourself for this. To tell you the truth, my need for a time of solitude came from no one place. An Emoticon jab here, a Josh Brand jab there. What really made me realize it was Emoticon's attempt on Virgil's life. I don't know whether he sees it as such or not, but what matters is that it was, and it scarred me more deeply than I feel he will ever know. He broke my trust-and now I understand why you distrusted him so much yourself. I'm not running to get away from you and Virgil. I'm in no state of mind to love anyone right now, because I don't love myself. I am convinced that I am a selfish being who needs to seriously re-evaluate practically everything she has done in the past six months. I don't know if it was anyone's intent to provoke that reaction, but again, intent is not the question-only the result of what was said. When I have resolved my mental state, I will return. Do not think that I would ever leave you; I would not, after what you have been through, both what you have told me, and that which I gathered from Eryc some time back, dim sense of things though it was. I will not abuse you as you have been abused in the past. I want you to trust that I will return. Hopefully, I will have reconciled myself to a few things, namely finding a way to cope with being myself. If I can't do that… Well, I'll just have to, there's no question. I haven't been the same for two weeks now. That's why it's better that I be gone for a little while. I left you something, though-check your right ear. I hope that you like it. La tienne, L.R.V. It was a struggle for Dawn’s hand not to clench around the sides of the worn note, for to do so would have surely damaged the painstakingly neat handwriting contained within…it was a struggle though, as her hands shook like leaves in the wind, her heart fluttering painfully. A small dark spot appeared near the lower right corner, another following not far away, and Dawn quickly put the note back into the picture box as she scrubbed at her eyes with one hand. Hot tears had leaked down her cheeks and chin, dripping off the point to spatter onto the letter Lia had left her. "Goddammit." Dawn cursed softly, scrubbing hard at her face, "I’m a grown woman now, you’d think I could avoid crying at the drop of a hat. She sniffled a little, further damaging her self image of invincibility, and closed the picture box slowly. "I want to wait for you…but are you waiting for me?" Her thoughts were interrupted by a sharp rapping at her door that startled her into almost dropping the delicate box. "J-just a minute!" Placing the box back inside her travel bag, Dawn shrugged her black duster over her shoulders, covering the nakedness that was only barely mitigated by the small towel she was wearing. Hugging the coat close to her frame she opened the door a crack, "Yes?" The Japanese man outside was powerfully built despite his age, wiry muscles visible just beneath the fabric of the shirt he was wearing. Steely grey streaks shot through his hair and mustache, staining his beard a salt and pepper colour of grey and black. Master Toshiro Mifune smiled at Dawn and bowed politely. "Good morning Kaiden (Little Dragon), I hope I am not disturbing you." "No master not at all, I was just getting dressed after my shower." Dawn tried not to show that she was flustered, hoping the high collar of her duster would hide the creeping blush spreading up her neck and into her cheeks. "Ah. Well if you could finish getting dressed, perhaps you would be willing to join me for a small walk in the gardens?" "Of course Master! Give me a quick moment please." Bobbing her head in a rough approximation of a bow, Dawn ducked back inside and closed the door. Dropping the towel and duster to the floor in one motion, she dashed about the room for clothes suitable to the winter climate. Cargo pants and thick socks were followed by a t-shirt and sweater, the duster finally being retrieved from the floor to hang from her shoulders. As she opened the door to her room once again, Mifune turned from where he was gazing out the window to smile at her. "Ah, finally dressed I see. I hope I wasn’t interrupting anything…private." Dawn fell into step at her Masters shoulder, shaking her head. "No Master, I was just finishing up my morning routine." "Does your morning routine often involve crying?" Dawn stopped in her tracks, taken aback by her Masters blunt forwardness. "Y-You heard that?" Mifune nodded, beckoning her to continue to follow him as he walked. "Not exactly no. I may not be the man I once was, but I still find it difficult to miss the signs that a woman has been crying. Your eyes are still red Dawn." "Sorry…" She mumbled, falling into step once again. "Why are you sorry? I’m not disturbed by your sorrow…just a little confused I think." "Confused?" Mifune chuckled, stroking one hand through his short beard. "I wonder if you are unhappy here. I know there has been some…difficulty between us since your arrival, but I had thought that you were still happy here." "No no!" Dawn said waving her hands, "It’s not that Master it’s just…I miss Lia." Mifune cast Dawn an appraising sidelong glance. "Your lady friend?" Dawn blushed, running one hand sheepishly through her hair. "We’re…more then just friends Master." He nodded in response. "I know, but I was making an effort to be polite." The corners of his mouth twitched up in amusement, drawing another blush from Dawn. "I…didn’t know I was so easy to read Master." "You are not, but I believe I have learned enough about you during your time here that I can pick up some less then subtle signs from you. Why do you miss her, has it been so long?" "Yes Master, almost four months…" "Mmm, that can seem like a long time when you’re so young." He winked at her, turning the corner towards the rock garden. "It’s more then just missing her master I…I wonder if maybe she doesn’t care. That I havn’t heard from her because she isn’t coming back." Mifune turned his head to regard her, one eyebrow raised in surprise. "After four months of absence you doubt her commitment to you? Is she really so flighty?" "N-no not at all, she’s very loyal but…" She sighed, feeling ridiculous. "…it’s just been so long since I’ve had even a glimpse of her." "Hmmm." Mifune fell silent for a long moment, pushing open the door to the snow covered garden and stepping out onto the wooden landing that ran around the edges. "But she has been seen, so you know she’s alright." Dawn nodded bitterly, trying to shake a sudden foul taste in her mouth. "Yes, she was in the first round of GTT5 before she was ousted by that masked clown." "Mmmm…you’re facing that ‘Masked Clown’ in this round yourself aren’t you?" She nodded firmly, sitting down on the edge of the landing when bid to do so, careful to keep her feet from trailing in the carefully manicured sand below. "I am…and I’m going to beat him. I can’t forgive that he ruined Lia’s dream of another GTT run, so I’m going to ruin his." "Such venom." Mifune replied absently, "Do you think she would want such a thing from you?" Dawn nodded firmly, her hands clenching into fists. "I think she would want me to avenge her loss, we’ve always looked out for each other. I can’t just let a slight like this go…it has to be addressed." "Heh. Like a Knight defending his lady love." Mifune chuckled. "So this round you fight not for yourself, but for her?" "Yes Master." Mifune sighed, leaning back against the railing that covered portions of the landing. "You have to be careful placing such an emphasis on your ‘Revenge’ you know, since it’s not only you who would suffer." "Master?" "Let me put it to you another way. The histories of Kenjustu (Japanese Sword Fighting) speak of two principle schools of thought. First is Satsujin-Ken, the art of the killing sword. Such a philosophy holds that a sword is used only to kill, that the swordsman worry only about his own life in combat." "Master what does…" "Hush a moment and let me finish." Dawn fell silent, looking confusion at her master. "The second school of thought is Katsujin-ken or "Swords that give life". This philosophy holds that swords are used to defend those who cannot defend themselves. Though perhaps a more wholesome thought in regards to sword play, Katsujin-ken has one major flaw…should the weilder ever lose, the people he is fighting for suffer with him." Turning her head away from her master, Dawn stared out into the frozen garden, her eyes taking on a distant look. Mifune turned to face her, his own flint grey eyes deadly serious. "If you fight for the honour of your lady love, you leave yourself no room for error, no place for failure. To bring such a burden onto yourself…is to demand victory in a way that is much more real…are you ready for that kind of commitment?" "Am I…ready?" Dawn’s voice was barely a whisper, carried about the garden by the winter wind. "How will I know?" Mifune smiled kindly and folded his arms over his broad chest. "When you are confident that you will achieve victory for both you and your beloved…then you are ready."
