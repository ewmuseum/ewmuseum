Expect A Fight

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 The image fades in to show the viewers a black backdrop with the letters GTT In silver in the dead centre. For a few seconds the three simple letters are focused on but then the camera fades back to allow for a wider shot. Standing to the side of the logo is Mr. Darkness, dressed to his best of course. “GTT is the biggest tournament in the wrestling world, a tournament where legends collide, where youngsters try to take the spotlight and where even underdogs can score impressive victories,” informs Mr. Darkness in a very manner of fact tone. Though it’s the eyes that give him away, as he always said, look at a man’s eyes not anything else. And it’s the eyes that tell us he is scared, not as in nervous in facing a legend but as in paranoid of what he may have to do to beat a legend. And then there is the butler, who stands just out of shot with his eyes on his master like a loving obedient dog. He knows exactly what he saw just the day before and he knows exactly what it means. He seems rather hesitant yet also desperate, perhaps he knows he should talk to his master about it but is afraid of the repercussions. “Each man in this tournament must prove themselves and will prove themselves if they are to reach the finals. It seemed like I was doing well on my journey, I gad my strategy all planned out and then a rock appeared in the road. A rock called Inphino Blitz, a wrestling legend. All strategy, all momentum vanished in an instant. Immediately I was told several hundred times that I would have no chance against such a talent, that I would just be another one of his long list of victims.” Mr. Darkness hangs his head though, as if almost ashamed that he is not on the level of Blitz. And then a smirk spreads across his face. “What these people fail to see are the simple facts in life.” Suddenly the Butler’s eyes widen, a bead of sweat suddenly emerges from his forehead as he relives the events that took place just a mere twenty four hours ago. He remembered a scrap of dialogue from that day containing those last words, the simple facts of life. “What they see when they look at this battle is a legend taking on a no body. A legend taking on someone who is not at his level, a legend taking on a person that knows how much lower that the spectrum they are. Unfortunately, these people are merely looking through rose coloured glasses, even worse is that there hopes and dreams will crash to the ground!” The smirk has widened by now and now Mr. Darkness lifts his head up, he catches a quick glance at his Butler who merely nods his head as he frantically tries to regain his composure. The only problem is the Butler knows, he knows everything now. “This match is not one sided, for I am not like any other normal person that faces such odds. I do not quit, I do not lie down. While I can still move I can still fight, while I am not in a still state I can fight. Even down on the ground I can still fight, and I will.” Mr. Darkness steps closer to the camera, with his face now filling the whole image. Meanwhile the Butler is trembling, he seems to be contemplating calling for a stop to this interview but he can’t. He is paralysed with fear. For he knows, he knows by now exactly what is pumping around the veins of his Master. He knows exactly what is driving him, and he doesn’t like it one damn bit. “Blitz, there is no doubt you are one of the toughest challenges I have ever faced. But that does not mean I will fear you like everyone else, I am not the brave young and up and comer who merely trash talks for the sake of trash talking while knowing deep down that he cannot win. Everything I do Blitz I do for a reason, to beat you. This is not so I can gain money but to beat you Blitz. Right now you will not understand but when we stand in that ring, that is when the pin will drop Bltiz. That is when you will understand. That legend, will be too late.” For a second a glimmer of doubt seems to come over Mr. Darkness but then the confident and controlled rich man returns, all except for in those eyes. They seem to have turned a shade of red, the slits have narrowed and they seem to be projecting the death rays of a hideous stare. “Blitz, there will by no laying down. I will not do you the favour. If you are to win this tournament like everyone believes you can then first you must beat me, and I mean beat me. Blitz, expect a fight. Expect to have to go to any lengths if you are to beat me!”
