TTD New Royal Legion -v- Souls of the Black Lotus

13 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The New Royal Legion Reunite! So, does that make it the New New Royal Legion? In December of last year, the two members of the most decorated team in XUW history – The New Royal Legion – Bailey and Danyon fought in a Death Match against each other after months of turmoil and build up much to the chagrin of the loyal Legionnaires. The Legionnaires are of course the greatest, most loyal fans ever seen in wrestling. Some would describe them as a cult while they themselves believe that they are faithful to the New Royal Legion and cheer them on no matter what and a cult is not the right word to describe them. Faithful, zealous, passionate they are all perhaps but a cult – no way. If by off chance that they were a cult, however, Bailey and Danyon, would be their saviours. Back in December when Bailey and Danyon faced off in the Death Match, there was no real winner to the match. The match ended in an apparent no contest as both wrestlers were abducted from the middle of the ring when the lights suddenly went out. For over one month nobody had seen or heard of their whereabouts. However, Larry the Legionnaire, the first Legionnaire there ever was and is now considered the lawyer to The New Royal Legion and The Legionnaires, showed up on an XUW Mayhem as he was told that Bailey was going to be entered into the Deadly Draw. A few weeks later Bailey returned to XUW showing very little effects from the abduction. It turned out he was abducted by Legionnaires over fear that him and Danyon would literally fight to the death. The ever thoughtful Legionnaires were afraid for the New Royal Legion’s lives and abducted them at that the end of their match and separated them. However, it was only Bailey that returned to XUW as Danyon was still missing. Bailey continued to wrestle for two months not knowing the whereabouts of his former tag team partner and best friend. But then heading into XUW’s biggest PPV of the year, Reborn, Danyon made a startling return to XUW much to the surprise of Bailey and Larry. His intentions and reasons why he returned to XUW were unknown and he did state them, in fact to this day, two weeks after Reborn Danyon has yet to really state why he felt the need to return to XUW. Still, heading into Reborn when Bailey was fighting in his largest singles match ever for the IC Title, many Legionnaires were worried about the stress from Danyon returning on him and were leery about just what Danyon was doing back and what his intentions were. At Reborn, Bailey won the IC Title, and then Danyon let his intentions be known to all and this came as much relief to the Legionnaires as he came down to the ring and embraced Bailey in the middle of the ring. It seemed that he let bygones be bygones. The New Royal Legion had reunited as the Legionnaires cheered on. Even though the legendary tag team is back together, there are still many questions that remain. Like for instance how much will that past feud affect their ability in the ring? Has their previous disagreement affected their ability to trust each other in the ring? Or what will their first tag team match be like, seeing as they haven’t fought as a tag team in over 5 months? Even then, their last few matches weren’t that memorable as they lost a few before heading into their singles match. Or the fact that Danyon hasn’t wrestled a match in over 4 months has to leave some questions about the New Royal Legion’s ability in the ring heading into their next match. At least all those questions would arise if you were talking about a normal tag team with normal fans, not the most legendary tag team ever in the New Royal Legion and their fanatical Legionnaires. Of course, if you were to mention anything about the New Royal Legion not winning a tag match since last October the they’d dismiss it and talk about how they are greatest tag team ever in the history of the world and one or two losses doesn’t matter. That’s because to the Legionnaires, Bailey and Danyon are considered next to gods, if not gods themselves. Still the New Royal Legion is back together and seemingly on the same page. Given their history and success, many would have to think that is good enough for them to secure a victory in any tag team match no matter who the competitor is. *** The scene fades into the most famous of all the hotels in XUWville, the Howard Johnson Hotel. This hotel is so famous because it is the home of the New Royal Legion and the parking lot is a converted camp ground for the Legionnaires as they sit and wait on every single word and movement of their saviours. The Legionnaires are outside the hotel on a bright sun shining day wandering around keeping to themselves in the parking lot of the hotel. They seem to be in a bit of a more upbeat mood since Danyon returned. Things are looking up for them. Both Bailey and Danyon are back in XUW and are active wrestlers. Bailey is now the IC Champion and just recently The New Royal Legion have been accepted into the PTC Tag Title Division. So far, the PTC hasn’t been too kind to the Legionnaires as Larry lost in his first round match in the Cruiserweight Tournament but that’s Larry and he isn’t a professional wrestler, he is the Legionnaires Lawyer, so that’s not much to complain about. Or is there? Larry’s lost was very questionable. There is some talk amongst the Legionnaires that maybe the conspiracy that has plagued the New Royal Legion since the beginning of their time together may spread beyond XUW and into the PTC. And that’s the real reason why Larry the Legionnaire lost in the Cruiserweight Tournament. It seemed as if the conspiracy that the New Royal Legion fought against for so long in XUW was dormant because of the recent success of the New Royal Legion but this may have just been a ploy. At least that’s what some Legionnaires are now speculating. (It was this conspiracy that was the rallying cry that led the Legionnaires to following and cheering on the New Royal Legion in the first place.) Nevertheless, the Legionnaires are happy that Danyon has returned and are smiling about the compound. Some are making banners welcoming home Danyon for the next XUW TV Show so they can make an appearance. Others are just going to hold them up as Danyon and Bailey walk by in the parking lot so they can see them then. The Legionnaires aren’t being too original this time and the signs are just saying basic things like “Welcome Home Danyon,” “Danyon = Greatest Wrestler Ever From New Zealand,” and “We Never Forgot You Danyon.” The scene fades out from the Legionnaires in the parking lost compound of the hotel up to the 15th floor and the appropriately named New Royal Legion Suite where Bailey, Danyon and Larry the Legionnaire are all located in the room this time. This is the first time that Danyon has returned to the New Royal Legion Suite since his abduction. He is sitting on the edge of one of the beds, Larry is sitting in his usual chair next to the desk in the room and Bailey is standing up looking out the window. He is looking down at the Legionnaires below. He turns around with a big smile on his face and with his XUW IC title resting on his shoulder. He walks over to the fridge in the room and pulls out two beers and a vodka cooler. He hands Larry the “girlie” drink and Danyon the other beer. Bailey goes over to a chair and before he sits down he holds up his beer. Bailey: I’d like to make a toast here if I may. I want to make a toast to the return of the greatest international superstar ever to grace the ring in XUW, the return of the most decorated wrestler in New Zealand history, Danyon. Danyon, I want to say that I’m glad you are back and that the New Royal Legion is back together. So, this is to you buddy. I’m proud to call you my best friend…………To you Danyon. They all take a drink from their respective drink. A slight smile goes over Danyon’s face as this is very nice gesture from especially Bailey given the last few months of their relationship before December. But all of that seems to have been in the past. Bailey: There is something else I must do…….or we must do Danyon. I was thinking about it since you returned. I know that we shouldn’t really bring up the few months at the end of last year leading into our epic battle in December but I feel I have to, just this once. Danyon: Fair enough, I would have thought at some point it would come up. We might as well get it out of the way now before we have our first match because that could lead to major problems. I’m willing to accept some blame on my actions. Bailey: I’m not looking to put the blame on anyone here. We both had our reasons but I want to let bygones be bygones. I think that abduction changed us both clearly for the worse. Being abducted by the aliens in June on the order from the conspirators really affected us and I don’t think that was the true us back in those few months after returning. So, I’m not blaming you or myself or even Larry for that matter. I think it was what it was. And I want to do something to prove to you that I have no hard feelings. Danyon: That sounds good to me. I’ll admit that jealousy or whatever it was back in those few months after returning wasn’t the real me. I was frustrated but yeah, I’m willing to let it all go. It’s in the past. We have the whole future to look forward too. We have the tag titles to go back after, we have the tag division to dominate and we have the PTC Tag Title Division to conquer as well. Bailey: Exactly, that’s why I proposing we bury the hatchet of that past. And I mean literally. Danyon: How’s that? Bailey gets up to his feet and walks over to a walk in closet and pulls out a fish tank full of dirt. He walks it over and puts it on a counter top. He walks back over to closet and pulls out a hatchet. He walks back towards the counter top with fish tank of dirt. Bailey: I am purposing that we literally bury this hatchet in the dirt here. Danyon: I always liked your thinking. Danyon gets up and comes over to the counter top and looks at the hatchet. He notices that on the head to the hatchet is says Oct., Nov., Dec. 2003 scribed on it. Danyon: You literally meant to bury the hatchet with those months on it. Bailey: Oh yeah. If we had used a hatchet in our Death Match I would have gone and found that one too. But I guess that was the one object we didn’t use. Not that I remember the whole match or anything from all the head shots I took but I don’t think there was a hatchet used. Danyon: I don’t remember much either. It’s all a bit of blur to me but I can’t see a hatchet being used either. Nonetheless, this is just as good. Danyon looks at the hatchet in his hand and moves some of the dirt with his hand and slowly pushes the hatchet down in the dirt. Bailey walks back over to the closet and pulls out a pail of dirt and comes back to the counter top. He looks at the half buried hatchet and dumps the rest of the dirt on it. A smile comes over both of the faces. Larry the Legionnaire watches on with a big smile on his face as well. And if you look close enough Larry’s eyes seem to be a bit watery too. If the Legionnaires outside could see this, they’d all be crying over this momentous sign between the two members of the New Royal Legion burying the hatchet of their past indiscretions against each other. Bailey: This had to be done, Danyon. I’ve thinking that the conspirators are back at it again and we need to be on the same page heading into this PTC Tag Title Tournament. The conspiracy against is back or really it never left, as it just lied dormant for a few months. Danyon: How do you figure? Bailey: Well, let’s take a look at the facts for a second here. First we are mysteriously left out of the PTC Tag Title Division. Now some would say that the reason is that you were still missing but that didn’t stop XUW from placing me in the Deadly Draw when I was out of action. They could have easily entered us in the division. But that’s a moot point. Danyon: I see your point. I mean, Larry did know of my whereabouts when I was missing so it’s not as if I couldn’t be brought back just for the division anyways. Bailey: Right. But that’s not the only funny thing that has gone on since we first expressed interest in the PTC. Larry lost a controversial match in the Cruiserweight Tournament. He clearly should have won that match but for some ridiculous reason he lost. And that ridiculous reason can only be because of the conspiracy. Danyon: But Larry isn’t much of a wrestler. He’s a lawyer. Larry: Hey I’ve won one match in my career. Remember I did wrestle last year at this time while you guys were on strike and I was forced to wrestle. I won a match. So, it’s not as if it couldn’t happen again. Danyon: Oh yeah, I forgot that you did wrestle last year. Larry: And I tell you another thing, it’s too bad there is no tape footage of my match or else you’d all see that I had Horazon beat for a one, two and three but was screwed over. It’s unfortunate that the Cruiserweight Tournament wasn’t televised or else everyone could see that I should have won that match. Bailey: Hey, Larry, I was there and you did deserve to win that match. It’s typical conspiracy tactics – lose matches that you should have won. That’s the second point as to why I think or rather why I know the conspiracy has expanded beyond the confines of XUW to the PTC. Danyon: Hey, I wish I had been there. Bailey: Al Crud was there too………….speaking of which where is Al Crud? Is he still in the bathroom? Danyon: I think so. Bailey: He’s been in there for like 30 minutes. Danyon: You have to tell me the story later on he became an honourary Legionnaire. I still don’t understand this whole thing with him staying here and being best friends. Bailey: I will, don’t worry. Danyon: Whenever is fine with me. Bailey: There is also a third reason why I know the conspiracy has infiltrated the PTC. I think it’s funny that after Larry inexplicably loses his match, we are all of a sudden allowed into the Tag Title Division. And on top of that, our first match is against the pre-tournament favourites, the Souls of the Black Lotus. We are at a major disadvantage here by being put in the tournament late because we lost the opportunity to get points in the first round. This is so typical of the conspirators always putting us at a disadvantage and seemingly insurmountable odds to overcome. This time we are not going to fail. This time we are going to go into that match and win. We are going to prove to the PTC just why we are considered the greatest tag team ever. Why we are legends. And why I’m considered the greatest tag team wrestler ever. We’ve fought and won against the conspiracy before and this will be a new place to overcome it but that it hasn’t stopped us before. It’s time for the New Royal Legion to make itself known in the PTC. And what other way is there than upsetting the pre-tournament favourites? Danyon: I don’t think there is a better way than beating the Souls of the Black Lotus to show that we have arrived on the scene. So, what that I haven’t wrestled in almost 4 months, I’m rested and ready to go. It’s not as if I haven’t been keeping in shape or anything. Our greatness in the ring can overcome any obstacles thrown in our path. We have proved it before and now we have to prove it in the PTC. There is a reason why I’m the most decorated wrestler in New Zealand history and I’m going to prove to them that I’m the best international wrestler to ever grace the XUW ring. Wait a second……….correction………the greatest international wrestler to ever grace the PTC ring. And we will show why we are living legends in the tag team ranks. The conspiracy will ultimately fail in the end and we will be the PTC Tag Team Champions. That gold can go next to your current IC Title, your Submission Title, our Intergalactic Titles we won off those aliens, and our other XUW Tag Team Titles. The suite could use some more gold to decorate I think. Larry: I most definitely agree that we could use some more gold to go on the walls. But you forgot the most important titles you ever held Danyon. You forgot the L.O.S.T. Titles. Danyon: Sorry, I just assumed that everyone already knows about how we hold the greatest tag team titles ever created. Stuff like that doesn’t need to be mentioned. Larry: Good point………. But before Larry can continue there is a flush from a toilet in the bathroom that can be heard. The door to the bathroom opened up and out walks the whiskey warrior, the smelly superstar, the flatulent fighter, the pungent pugilist, the one, the only, “The Bastard” Al Crud. Al Crud was or is a legend in wrestling as he was a multiple time world champion in the GIWF. But in XUW he is not much more than a loveable jobber who is on a major losing streak. He has however recently announced his loyalty in being a Legionnaire and was the million and first Legionnaire ever. Now he has been hanging out with Bailey and Larry for the past few months and is even staying over in the New Royal Legion Suite. Essentially he took over Danyon’s place in his absence, which is all fine and dandy except that means you are living with Al’s habits and problems as well. Al has a rather large gas problem that makes him belch and fart quite often not to mention his repugnant odour and filthy eating habits. Still, Al is a loveable character and Bailey and Larry and the other Legionnaires love having him around as he always tells stories of the days when he beat the likes of Lew Sprectre and the Phenomenons in GIWF. Anyways, Al walks out of the bathroom with a large smile on his face and walks towards the fridge to presumably grab another beer. Danyon: You were in there for 30 minutes. What happened? Al: Perfection takes time my friend. Paris wasn’t built in a day you know. Danyon: Don’t you mean Rome? Al: Uhhhh…….sure. Larry: I’m sure Paris wasn’t built in a day either so he was technically right. Danyon: But the saying is Rome wasn’t built in a day. Larry: But what he really said was right, technically……… Danyon: Yes, technically…….. Larry: There you go. Leave poor Al alone, will you. Danyon shakes his head in disbelief. Danyon: Now I know why I didn’t mind it up in that cabin at times by myself. Danyon pauses for a second……. Danyon: You said earlier today that Al essentially stays and lives here now with us. But my question is this, there are only three beds yet there are 4 of us here now that I’m back…………. Danyon didn’t need to finish asking that question as the other three men in the room stop what they are doing all look at each other. Bailey looks at Danyon and the both of them smirk as they turn to look at Larry. Larry sees the sly look they are giving him and his eyes widen. Larry: No, please no. You are not saying that I have to share a bed with Al are you? Bailey: It’s only fair given that we have the PTC Match to prepare for, we should be having proper sleeps in those beds alone. Danyon: Plus, you like to defend him a lot, it’s only natural that you two share a bed. Larry gulps at the idea of spending a nite in the same bed as the notoriously smelly Al Crud. Larry looks over at Al trying to smile. Al looks back at Larry and winks at him as he farts. Al: Don’t worry, Larry, I usually just pass out most nites anyways from drinking so much, so I don’t move around much if that’s what you are worried about. Larry: That’s not what I’m worried about……. Bailey: Don’t worry, Larry, it’s only temporary until we find something better. Perhaps this place can be renovated and made bigger. I think we deserve a bigger suite. We are back together now and I think we deserve something to mark this occasion. The New Royal Legion is back and that’s all that matters. Larry lets out a big sigh and goes back to the work on his desk. Al goes back to his usual chair and starts drinking his beer. Bailey and Danyon get back to their discussion about how they will overcome the conspiracy yet again and defeat the Souls of the Black Lotus in their triumphant return to the ring as the scene fades out on the New Royal Legion Suite. Or is that soon to be known as the New New Royal Legion Suite?
