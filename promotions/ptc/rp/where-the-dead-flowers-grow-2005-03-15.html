Where the dead flowers grow

15 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-15 In the midst of the cold winter night he stood, starring forward at a creature that wasn’t real, that couldn’t have been real. Yet there he stood, the Executioner of Reality, a demon so old that even the Scholars had forgotten of its existence. If only he could have been so lucky. Surrounded about him were the black crosses symbolizing the remains of a dead town, where he once spent the happiest childhood memories that he could ever have, now those memories were gone forever. Replaced by the knowledge that his path had been chosen long before he had any choice in the matter, long before any other than the Father himself could have known. And this, this demon whom had fallen from the face of the Earth, it’s need and wanton lust for power was the reason he was doomed to this existence, and he knew it. Upon lithe fingers it held the designs of a new time, the architects of the damned having found their own uses for this world. Not the first world, mind you, for this Earth is nothing but a replacement shell for a failed experiment, but rather for their vision of it. Hell would come to this place if he didn’t accept his destiny, and Hell would drive his life into a spiral from whence it could never return. Of course, his entire life he had known something was wrong, that he was different somehow, but now all the voices and thoughts that had plagued him for years had a name. And that name was Jehovah. Humanity is a frail and fickle thing, meant to be recreations of the only infinitely perfect being to have ever come into existence and to offer praise unto him. Yet so completely and totally flawed that despite the best efforts of a perfect being, Humanity still cannot find a way to achieve enlightenment and peace. Driven forward by their insatiable bloodlust for power and their never ending need to control, the twisted creations of a perfect being have become that which should be despised most. While Lucent has in the past tried to pry apart the bonds that were created between us, it was in fact humanity, with their roadmap to the satanic age, that has proven that the Father should have simply started anew. Unworthy of his love and unappreciative of his gifts, the entire flawed project has only seen the squandering of Gaia and the rebirth of the damned. We live now in the age of irrelevance, look around and know it to be true. Lost in thoughts of self love, rather than thoughts of repairing the damage we’ve done, the focus of humanity is quite simple; to be the plague. The mist rolled about his feet, covering them completely from view and reflecting light upon the numerous false memorials that were lined about him. Indeed these crucifixes had all been painted black as a symbol. Black is, after all, not a color but nothing more than an area devoid of light. The representation of their souls having been cast into the never ending dark, into the bowels of the beast to have been abandoned by the Father, and yet he knew it couldn’t end this way. Despite the creature that stood before him, despite the fear that plagued his heart, he knew he had been given this role for a reason. He was to become something else, something more, not the evolution of man, but the evolution of man’s spirit. He was preparing himself for the next step upon life’s great journey, and he would be one of the few. The beast stood before him in man’s clothes, holding the appearance of the only Priest whom had ever reached out to him when he was weak. The only person in their religious society who truly seemed to care about the faith and well being of his patrons, now however, there was nothing more than the twisted vision before him. A balding head with horseshoe hair reflected back the luminous light of the moon, while empty, hollow eyes starred forward ready for the feast. This was Hell, after all, and he was all that was left of hope. The hope for Father McArthy’s soul, the hope for the people this town, and the only hope for his grandparents… Welcome to the Gospel of a man who has seen more than society can accept. Welcome to the words taken from the tome of the Arcane, twisted into form and submitted for your approval. This is not the story of a life we all know and lead. This is not the story of a life that we all dream to lead. This is the story of a life that only one has forever been doomed to lead, by this faith, by his bloodline and by his curse… Welcome to Oblivion Origin Chapter I – Where the dead flowers still grow “Tell me something Scott, if you would.” Monroe narrowed his eyes, this creature still held the thoughts and ideas of Father McArthy, yet he could smell the stench of the undead upon it. “Speak quickly, blasphemous wretch, for your time is quickly coming to an end.” The creature smiled a broad smile, tearing at the skin of McArthy’s face and letting it hang loose as it fell away. “Why have you accepted what was not your dream?” -Why indeed?- -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Hey baby, how are you?” Monroe lowered his head; he had another dream last night, this one far more vivid and frightening than the last. “It’s the dream again, Samantha, it’s getting worse.” He could hear her take a deep breath on the other end of the line. “Scott there’s something I have to tell you…” The moment he heard her pause he knew that something was wrong. Deep down he didn’t want to hear what she had to say, he wanted everything to stay the same, he was happy and the last thing he wanted was that to change. Unfortunately for Scott Monroe, life had never been kind. From his beginnings in New Orleans, to his time spent with his grand parents spent in Killian and Springfield, it seemed to be one test after another. Now, he knew that there was more to this than he wanted to hear, more than he would ever want to hear. “What?” On the other end of the line he could he hear swallow hard. Whatever it was, she was having a hard time dealing with it as well. His assumption, the eventual breakup that long distance relationships are doomed to know. He prepared himself to hear all the usual reasons. They didn’t see each other enough, they were growing apart, or even worse, there was someone else. He took a deep breath as she began to talk, but what she began to say, he was completely and totally unprepared to hear. “Scott those voices I was telling you about, well, they’re getting worse. Last night I sat in bed until three in the morning, listening to them tell me of the things that were coming, listening to them tell me that those dreams you’re having, they’re not just dreams. You’re seeing the future, Scott, you’re having premonitions that are showing you what you have to do.” It wasn’t quite what he expected, and there was hardly a way to counter with, “but I’ll find a way to see you more.”, but deep down he knew that she wasn’t lying to him. It was in her voice and evident in her words. Samantha had always been a lot of things, but a lot like Scott himself, she’d never been a liar. It was something she saw no use in, because the truth was always more damaging than the lie. Yet on the other end of the line she sat, telling him of things that weren’t possible, that couldn’t have been possible. He began to trail away, his thoughts going to the dream, remembering himself standing there and starring at someone. A dark figure whose eyes gave way to no emotion and who’s smile was frightening and hateful. For everything he had seen and done to this point in his life, he was completely and totally unprepared for what happened next. “Scott.. I talked to God last night.” He almost dropped the phone from his ear and had to fumble to keep it from striking the ground. “What are you talking about?” On the other end Samantha let out a loud groan. “You don’t think this is hard for me to say, Scott, you think I’m just tooling you?” It was his turn to groan. “I’m having nightmares Sam, something might be wrong with my brain, now’s not the time to tell me you’ve been talking to angels and God and voices and whatever else that fantasy might create.” She took a deep breath on the other end of the line and it was evident that he had upset her. “Look, I’m sorry, I didn’t mean it like that, I’m just extremely stressed about my own…problem..” She cut him off quickly. “You don’t have a problem Scott. What you have is a divine calling from on high. Someday you’ll understand that. Someday you’ll be given the touch just as the rest of us have…” She trailed off for a bit before continuing. “…I know why they chose us.” This was insanity, the talk of someone that ended up in the Ward in Mandeville, yet, he knew Samantha wasn’t one to lie. It wasn’t her, never had been. “In your dreams you keep seeing the field, it’s littered with small, black crosses, and the moons hanging over you. You’re starring at someone, someone you feel you should know but yet you feel there’s something wrong. That they’re detached from themselves somehow and because of your caution you notice something wrong with them. You see their visible changes, you see the visible signs of the fact that whomever they are, they’re not themselves anymore.” His head lulled forward… “Sam…” But she wouldn’t let him interrupt her. It was too important. For them, for their families and for humanity itself, he had to know what was going on. “In the dark, alone, you and the figure stare each other down until you look down at one of the crosses. I know what the cross said, Scott, because they showed me the dream last night.” He shook his head as tears began to well in his eyes. “No, this isn’t possible.” Her voice was hollow now, empty, as if she was a monotone droid reading from a prepared cue card. “Here lies Samantha Crowley, age 18, left behind by her loving Mother, Father, two Brothers, and a fiancé.” She began to outwardly cry, unable to control her emotions anymore. “Sam it’s not going to be like that. Those are just dreams, my own imagination running wild and I can’t control it. Nothing is going to happen to you, I promise.” There was a muffled sound on the other end of the line. He knew that he was losing her, that whatever was happening to him was happening to her. Was it the Pot? Was it the Acid? Was the Alcohol? He didn’t know and right now, he didn’t care. He just knew that they both needed help. “Scott, why can’t you accept this?” For a moment he wondered the question to himself. What if everything that was happening to them was real? What if the things that she had said were true? “Because I refuse to fail you…” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Why, Chosen Son, have you made the decision to follow someone else’s path rather than your own?” For a moment Scott Monroe stood in the dark and considered the question. Why, after everything he had been put through, did he still trudge forward towards a goal that was not his own? Why bother to continue upon a journey if his role in the play was already set? Why bother? He starred to the ground for a moment, his heart was heavy and his head now began to thud. The pounding of his veins kept him from realigning himself and for a moment the beast looked at him in amusement. All it had taken were a few words to throw the mortal off his game, to ruin Jehovah’s great salvation. All it had taken was the truth. His eyes welled with tears again, he was only Nineteen years old, this was too big for him, too big for anyone other than God himself. As he began to lose himself in hopelessness and despair, his eyes fell upon a symbol that shook him to the core. A memory of a distant time when he promised not to fail, when his belief and strength were unshakable and the Lord was infallible. A time when he believed in his family, his country and his God, a time before the truth had emerged. Aloud he spoke the words he saw, he knew exactly what he needed. To hear them said out loud to wake him from his nightmare. “Here lies Samantha Crowley, age 18, left behind by her loving Mother, Father, two Brothers, and a fiancé.” The creature looked to him and tilted its head. It was confused by the situation and made no attempt to hide its confusion. Instead the creature took a step forward and sneered. Monroe, however, starred at the cross and knew what had to be done. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Scott you won’t fail me.” Her voice cracked as she spoke, she had something to tell him, something that he wouldn’t be able to accept and she knew that. The message was not one that she had a choice in delivering, as if any created on this plain were given a choice, so she took a deep breath and spoke. “I’m not going to be allowed to make it past my Twentieth birthday. Already they’re looking for me, I’m nothing more than a tool to them, nothing more than a piece of a greater puzzle. It’s because of who we are, Scott, and the blood that runs through our veins. It isn’t a coincidence that we met because it was meant to happen. I was sent here to prepare you, Scott, to prepare you for the day you take that first step…” Pacing back and forth he held the cordless phone to his ear and tried hard to fight back the growing anger inside of him. This was insane, it was crazy, these were nothing more than dreams, apparitions that weren’t meant to be taken seriously. Yet there she was, a woman of intelligence and poise, giving into the hallucinations. “Sam please…” But she cut him off for the final time. “Scott when you look down and see those words, know that down deep, a part of me is still with you. I’ll stay with you forever and help you to understand what you, and I, have become. We aren’t the people you think we are, Scott, we aren’t easily defined shells that you’ve convinced yourself we are. To think that one day you’ll become a Priest, the Priest, the one who stands against them…” Again she broke into a sob but he couldn’t speak, he didn’t understand and wasn’t sure he wanted to. “When you look down and see those words I want you to remember your teachings. I want you to remember that in the Valley we are no alone, Scott, we are never alone. He is there with us, his protection and grace offer us salvation from the devils and damned that have spawned. It is through him, my love, that humanity can once again become what we were meant to be. Remember the Valley, Scott.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Why do you continue, failure of Jehovah, when you know that your strength shall never be enough to topple me?” There he stood, the disease, looking into the eyes of the creature and knowing exactly what he had to do. Two silver eyes starred from beneath the brim of his black leather hat. Locked onto the decaying face of a figure he once knew and trusted; now there was nothing left for him to do but accept his place, accept his purpose, and to achieve his only goal. The wind blew about him as the eyes of Lunar turned their vision away, a red moon rising high in the sky, an omen to those who knew to stay away. This was their battlefield now. “The Lord is my Shepard, I shall not want.” The creature narrowed it’s eyes as Monroe starred directly into it’s soul. There was nothing there but hatred and anger, no reason or sanity, driven only forward by the Black Kings thoughts and ideas. “Even though I walk through the Valley of the Shadow of Death, I fear no evil for thou art with me.” The creature began to chant and murmur, the darkness enveloped it, and the black crucifixes about Monroe’s feet began to shake and tremble. The Earth herself began to give way under the power of the demon, but Monroe stood his ground. “Thy rod and thy staff, they comfort me.” The soft sod and soil began to uproot as muffled screams and groans began to escape from beneath the surface of the Earth.” “Thou dost prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies.” Decayed hands with bits of rotting flesh, torn and hanging muscle and insects of all manner reached from beneath the ground. Tearing their way up from what was to be their final resting place and knowing life once again thanks to the demon. “Thou hast anointed my head with oil and my cup overflows.” One by one they burst free from their prison beneath the hard dirt. Their moans and screams of agony lent knowledge to the fact that somewhere inside they still felt, that somewhere deep inside humanity still existed in their soul. Unfortunately they had been twisted and changed, carved into mockeries of life by the very creatures whom insult it most with their existence. “Loving kindness will follow me all the days of my life and I shall dwell within the house of the Lord forever.” They stood before him, the servants of the Black King, fallen victims who have seemingly lost their souls forever. Devoured by the damned and forced forever to drink from the cup of hatred, the puppets of Ragnarok trudge slowly forward. For there was only one thought upon their collective consciousness, that being to fulfill their master’s order, to destroy the Priest before them. “Amen!” And from the back came the order, hiding behind the creatures he had created, the demon now stood and waited beyond his own flock to see if the savior of Jehovah would even make it that far. Reaching back beneath his long, leather duster, Scott Monroe let his hands each slid onto the hilt of two small blades. He took a deep breath and then smiled a broad smile. The creatures didn’t notice this but the demon did and instantly he knew something was wrong. Something was different, Monroe had gone from being mildly afraid to suddenly excited. He could hear the mortals heart beating faster and faster, almost like the beating of the drum, it was picking up speed. The demon knew something wrong but there was nothing it could do. Scott Monroe leapt into the air and spun his body, starring down the first of the creatures as he began his descent and spinning the two blades in a counter clockwise motion between his fingers. If it was war that they wanted, then war they shall have. -Finale-
