Frown With Style?

21 August 2004

Date: 2004-08-21 OOC: Writer's block sucks. This is the best I could do. [12 - Truths and Consequences - “Frown With Style?” ] Dr. Drake’s Log: August 20, 2004, WARNING: This is not to be read or opened without the consent of Dr. Drake, or following his passing away. It has become my humble opinion as a doctor, and simply as an exerciser of common sense that the Prometheus Serum experiment has proven to be a failure. Over the past few weeks, a multitude of strange side effects have surfaced due to some reaction with the serum. One: the serum is not completely flushing itself out of my bloodstream. This has resulted in “fumes” being left behind after every injection. After a few injections, enough of the fumes are present to influence a spontaneous Prometheus Effect as if the serum had been applied. This random Prometheus Effect is short, but still not welcome at the dinner table while I’m on a date. Two: notable physical impairment following the end of the Prometheus Effect. For every injection of the serum I take, I seem to be exponentially weaker once the serum wears off. Theory: the constant increase and decrease of my body’s testosterone levels has become irregular. As the body gains testosterone while under the influence of the serum, as the serum flushes itself out of the system, it takes too much testosterone with it leaving the user with lower and lower testosterone levels with every application of the Prometheus Serum. Three: irregular growth, loss, and discoloration of hair as well as skin. The Prometheus serum makes my usually bald head sprout a full head of scraggly medium length pallid hair – my natural hair color is brown. Once the serum wears off, the hair immediately falls out. In addition, my skin color changes from an Irish kind of pale to pure and absolute white. Theory: once under the influence of the Prometheus Serum, the user’s heart stops pumping blood and the entire circulatory system operates purely on the Prometheus Serum as if it were blood. Once the serum wears off blood begins to flow again and thus the skin returns to its normal tint. The hair, however, I cannot explain yet. Four: temporary mental disorders. While under the influence of Prometheus Serum, the user seems to be under the influence of some kind of alter ego. User will suffer temporary insanity while under the influence of the serum. Theory: this alter ego is made up of the user’s repressed thoughts and memories. Solution: Re-write the Prometheus Serum formula from scratch. Signed, Dr. Kasidy Williams Drake III “You know, you really don’t have to be up here watching me Dex,” Dr. Drake said as he lightly stirred the pinkish mixture that was boiling in his Bunsen Burner. “I already paid you the rent... what more do you want? Another hundred?” The tall and thin doctor laughed at his own wit as he checked the temperature of the brewing elixir before him. It was a rather strange sight: Kasidy was standing in the middle of his tiny South Philadelphia apartment dressed in a white lab coat and glasses; a clipboard was in his hand. He was cooking up what looked to be a highly complex scientific formula atop a cheap kitchen table that he’d found in the trash years ago, while his trustworthy friend, Dexter Williams, was sitting on the couch decked out in a Roca Wear jogging suit, sunglasses, and a white du rag. “Yo, and dat stuff over there radioactive dawg?” Dex said eyeing the open canister on the end of the table. “Why you ain’t wearin’ no gloves?” The doctor rolled his eyes. “I’m injecting myself with this stuff week in and week out Dex,” he said as he poured a tablespoon of a green liquid into the beaker. “None of this stuff is any less radioactive then and I’m still alive. My method of making Prometheite safe hasn’t failed yet old friend.” Dex sighed. “Fo’ sho’,” he said with a sigh that said he didn’t believe a word he said. “And I’m up here ‘cause I’m worried ‘bout you playa’.” Kasidy chuckled a bit and put the spoon in a waste bin as he replaced his nerdy glasses with an even nerdier looking pair of protective goggles. “Oh so now you’re worried?” He said with a smirk. “I’m touched. The big strong ‘gangster’ is concerned about his braniac friend! If that’s not a movie for Lifetime I don’t know what is.” “Oh shut up yo,” Dex said playfully as he picked up a pillow and prepared to throw it at his friend. “Eh, I wouldn’t do that if I were you,” Kasidy said laughing. “Prometheite is a rather unstable chemical, and is pretty combustible-” “What?” “Nothing,” Kasidy said shortly. “Um, anyway-” “Are you sure that you should be mixing this stuff inside a small apartment? Shouldn’t you be doing this stuff fin a lab?” “Should I?” “Probably.” “Yeah probably. Anyway,” He said as he went back to stirring. “I’ve got a match in the morning at a house show, so I really need to get ‘Smiley’ perfected by then... God, I should have rewritten the formula ages ago.” Dex raised an eyebrow. “Yo man... you sure this gonna’ work this time?” he said scratching his head through his du rag. “Cause ... I don’t want you comin’ home every Saturday lookin’ like you just got down fightin’ a war and shit...” Kasidy smiled at his friend. He’d always looked out for him – even when they were in high school. When bullies came and tried to fry his head with his coke-bottle-thick glasses, Dex was the one who had scared them off. If he’d allow himself to admit that he had had one true friend in this crazy ride called his life, Dex would be the one and only candidate. Now, as an adult, he’d narrowly escaped loosing his life’s work – this apartment building – because he’d let Kasidy fall behind on rent. Even after all of that, he still was by Kasidy’s side making she he didn’t blow himself up. “It should be fine if my calculations are correct,” Dr. Drake said sniffing the boiling potion. “Not only will I maintain control of my higher brain functions while under the influence of the serum, but I should maintain my appearance and everything.” “You gonna’ take off dat ... voodoo mask of yours ‘den?” Again, Kasidy smiled. “It’s Aztec Dex, not Voodoo,” he explained. “And despite what you think I don’t think it’s haunted. At least with the mask on I don’t get bum rushed by interviewers and paparazzi. Plus ... er... if Smiley suddenly unmasked himself as seemingly-mild-mannered interviewer, Kasidy Drake, I know of a few people who might come after me.” “Like...?” “Hawke Poe for one,” Drake sighed. “I still feel horrible for what Smiley did to him last month. Every time they try to assign me him to interview, I get out of it because... because I can’t look that man in the eye. If I ever did, I’d just blurt out ‘I’m sorry!’ before I could control myself and... well, even if he’s a good guy, my face would probably be splattered on the wall. Plus, Lanier would fire me for double dipping as a masked wrestler and an interviewer. Not to mention what would happen if the people at the GoodAid laboratory found out what happened to their missing batch of Prometheite...” “I get da’ point yo’” “Yeah,” the solemn doctor sighed. “But once I fix this damned formula, Smiley will never do anything bad again. Maybe then I can make it up to Hawke...” Dex sighed and got up. “Well I gotta be out, yo,” he said heading towards the door. “Gotta collect rent... or try anyway... make sure you don’t burn down my apartment playa. Peace out.” “See ya Dex...” The door opened. The door closed. Alone again, as was his normal state, Kasidy turned off the Bunsen Burner. It was finished... the new and improved Prometheus Serum. And this time it would work. He hoped. * * * “You could fly me to the moon, Bury me underneath the sun, I don’t think that you’re the only one, That hides behind a gun!” The crowd began to boo from beyond the curtain, which kind of disturbed Kasidy. What disturbed him even more was the fact that he’d wrestled five entire matches on national television – winning all but two of them – and yet this was the first time he’d really experienced his entrance. “Jesus Christ,” he said covering his ears as he stood behind the curtain. “How in the hell does he deal with all of this ... noise!?” “Why don’t you just sit on the couch? Don’t even try to move your mouth! I think I’m getting’ tired of everything ... everything insane!” “Aaaannnnd the opponent,” announced Vicki, the ring announcer whose voice he was also “hearing” for the first time. “Weighing in at two hundred and thirty-four pounds with a record of three and two, the former Core Classic Champion.... SMILEY!” Dr. Drake lowered his hands again, and felt a twinge of sadness in the fact that he had to hear himself announced as Smiley, instead of Kasidy Drake. It passed, and he began to walk through the curtain as his theme music, “Bleed,” by Puddle of Mudd, continued to make the entire arena vibrate. “Never wanna bleed for me... Never wanna bleed for me... Bleed for me...” Resisting the urge to plug his ears and shield his eyes from the intense lights that seemed to be burning a hole through his brain, Kasidy began to stiffly hobble down the aisle dressed in a black singlet and boots. “Well Taylor,” Eliza was saying at the commentators table even though Drake – obviously – couldn’t hear her. “It looks like Smiley has gotten a bit of a makeover ... we usually see him out here in a T-shirt, jeans, and a bowler hat, but barring the ever-present smiley face that is his mask, you’ll note not just a change in attire, but his skin isn’t as pale either!” “Yep Eliza,” Taylor agreed. “And he even cut his hair! I guess he’s decided to follow the fad and do the bald thing...” “Meh, his hair wasn’t that great anyway, Taylor,” Eliza said with a laugh. “He doesn’t seem his usual cocky self though... we usually see Smiley dancing down to the ring, but he looks ... well he looks afraid!” “A mind game perhaps?” “Perhaps.” Not noticing that he was talking at a slant, Kasidy bumped into the barricade and was shoved by a random fan who uttered “YOU SUCK!” lethargically. Flustered by the amount of people that looked like they wanted to stab him – including the elderly and small children – he ran the rest of the way to the ring bumping into the apron and falling over. “Well if this is a mind game Taylor, then it’s the greatest one of all time...” “Gee Eliza... you can be such a bitch when we aren’t on the air...” Quite disoriented, Drake got to his feet and finally slid into the ring – and cried out as his outstretched hand was stomped on by his opponent. “SON OF A-“ Ding, Ding! The referee began yelling at the doctor’s opponent, an Asian juggernaught named Mahn Hahndel, continued his attempt to stomp Kasidy into oblivious with his boot. He was yelling out gibberish, while the fans cheered their heads off. “Ugh! Ugh! Ugh! Ugh! Ugh!” Kasidy cried as he felt more and more pain – yet when the referee finally pushed Hahndel off of him, he instantly felt at one hundred percent again. “The serum?” To Hahndel’s surprise, Kasidy got back up as fresh as a daisy like the assault had never happened. “You no-selling son of a-” Before he knew what he was doing, Kasidy instinctively charged forward with impossible speed while lowering his head. “What the-“ Contact. They both flew out of the ring through the ropes and ended up as a heap in front of the Spanish announce table. “¡Lanza! ¡Lanza!” the Spanish announcers cried. “Spear! Spear!” Eliza and Taylor cried. Again, Kasidy was back on his feet as if nothing had happened and landed a stiff kick to the side of Hahndel’s head as he tried to get back up. Hahndel cried out and went back down. “ONE!” “TWO!” “THREE!” Kasidy climbed back into the ring breaking the count feeling as fresh as a daisy. Not only was the serum increasing his strength and agility – but it was causing his body to regenerate as a sickening pace. “And Smiley seem to be on fire here tonight folks,” Taylor said from ringside. “I mean... we’ve seen Smiley show his strength and all, but he seems to not even be PHASED by anything that’s going on in this match. It’s amazing!” “Meh,” Eliza responded. “Where’d they find this Hahndel guy anyway? The YMCA?” Hahndel was back up looking more flustered than hurt. He checked his nose for blood, and then dived back into the ring charging at Kasidy who was leaning against the opposing ropes relaxed. ”Just let the serum do it’s work,” he said to himself. ”Just let it do it’s work...” “YAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!” Mahn screamed as he continued to run towards Kasidy – who simply pulled down the top rope and leaned back sending Mahn flying to the floor again using his own momentum against him. “This is just too easy!” Kasidy said feeling confident now. “Hey Mahn ... your mother called... she asked if you wanted your TESTOSTERONE BACK!” “Motto yasashii kotoba de itte-kure-masuka!?” Mahn yelled out as he got back up and stumbled to his knees again. “Gaeshi!!!!!” “Is that Japanese for ‘I’m ugly’?” Kasidy chuckled, his voice muffled by his wooden mask. “’Cause if it is ... you shouldn’t feel bad... if it weren’t for ugly people how would women know who was handsome or not!?” “Gomen NASAI!?i” Mahn screamed out looking offended. “...FIVE! SIX!” “I said!” Kasidy began to yell out cupping his hands to his mouth to make a makeshift microphone as if Mahn didn’t hear him. “You! Are! Fugly!” “GOMEN NASAI!?” “YOU! ARE! F-U-G-L-Y!? DO! YOU! UNDER...STAND!? D...O.......I......N....E...E...D ...T...O......S...P...E...A...K......S...L...O...W...L...Y......F...O...R... Y...O...U!? “EIGHT!” “Youma! Makaiju!!!” Mahn slid back into the room and suddenly, Kasidy felt a strange sensation overwhelm his body. He suddenly felt weaker. “Uh...oh...” WHAM! “YAH!” Kasidy cried as he literally flew backwards landing on the mat painfully. The pain didn’t leave him this time. “Oh shit!” Mahn approached him as Kasidy stared up at the bright stadium lights. He made an animalistic grunt as he grabbed the fallen masked scientist by the neck and picked him up. “Hey... hey...” Kasidy choked. “Hey... I didn’t mean that stuff about you being ugly, I was just trying to get into the competitive spiri- AHHH!” The next thing he knew, Mahn had picked him up and rested him on his shoulders, with Kasidy’s his crotch facing his face. Then Kasidy fell backwards and was suddenly looking at everything upside down. “Well Eliza... looks like it’s time for the Doom Tree – Makaiju as Mahn likes to call it. A hangman’s piledriver, is it not?” “Lights out, Mr. Smiley!” “Hey! Hey!” Kasidy yelled suddenly very frightened. “Put me down! I’m not Smiley! I’M NOT SMILEY! I’m not-” But then, he was falling. And then everything. Everywhere. Turned black. * * * Dr. Drake’s log: August 21, 2004, WARNING: This is not to be read or opened without the consent of Dr. Drake, or following his passing away. The trial of the new Prometheus Serum has failed. Although it succeeded in raising my physical capabilities and raising my pain threshold for a little while, the effect gave out seemingly without reason leaving me with twelve stitches in my head, and a bruised ego. I don’t know what happened after that... Makaiju thing, but I most certainly have the scars and bruises ... in places that I probably shouldn’t. Could it be that the removal of these... side effects caused the formulas premature disruption? Could it be that this “Smiley” persona that creeps deep beneath the surface of my mind somehow is ... powering the serum? I don’t understand. I’ve checked my notes and the formula at least a hundred times since my match on Thursday. It SHOULD have worked... It should have worked. So... why didn’t it? Signed, Dr. Kasidy Williams Drake III TBC
