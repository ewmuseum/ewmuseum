"Driven to be the best."

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 Time: 6:45pm Location: Los Angeles, CA. [Fade in] [The Los Angeles sun beats down in the early stages of this splendid Sunday evening. Around us, there is nothing but greenery, which is unusual for "The City Of Angels". Still, despite the city's reputation for crime and underhanded deeds based on hate and revenge, it does have its peaceful side. Perhaps the best example of a peaceful area in the raucous people-domain that is L.A. is Lincoln Park. Set on a steep yet scaleable hill, the park has long been a place of meeting and fun for the city's souls. Lonely men and women who have lost loved ones come to the park on a regular basis. For most, the place becomes associated with time-out to relax and reminisce on the good times which have passed us all by.] [We are focused on a massive wrought-iron gate as the blinding light of the sun reflects off the two rectangular plaques which have been nailed to it. A myriad of flowers and memories seem to flow at us all at once as we approach the gate tentatively. Without even reading the plaques, it becomes obvious where we are. The text is legible but written in an old-fashioned style. It reads: "Welcome to Los Angeles' finest city park - Lincoln Park!" [Below this, there is a large block of text which, again, is barely legible to the naked eye. Only the last few words can be clearly seen through the glare: "... and where memories rest, people come, Their loyalty cannot be undone." [The gates open, as if on cue, and a man steps into view. Judging from his youthful, vibrant looks, he is not the caretaker. Though he remains stony-faced as he stares into the camera, he beckons us to come into the park, to enter the realm of memories that Lincoln Park has inspired in so many people. This man, whoever he is, is one of the "loyal" ones who has returned. He turns his back to us and slowly makes his way up the steeply rising hill. His walk is controlled and careful, as if he is trying to avoid damaging the land. When he reaches an oak tree, standing approximately forty feet high, he turns to us. Removing a long rectangular case from beneath his smooth leather jacket, his dark green eyes catch our attention as he stares at us. This man is elusive, there is no doubt about it. He places the case on the faded green grass below the oak tree. Amongst the leaves that have fallen, the case is barely discernible. Finally, the young man turns his full attention towards us. Standing there with a purpose, he utters his first words.] "For those of you who don't know me, I believe it's time you get to know me. The name's Tony Aliso but friends call me Tone. Up until yesterday, I was the Heavyweight Champion of Neon Wrestling. The reason I say up until yesterday is because Neon Wrestling has closed. I'll delve into that later, though. Right now, I ought to bore you with the mandatory history lesson of my life." [Aliso smirks for the first time, revealing a set of pearly white teeth.] "One thing to keep in mind when you're dealing with Tony Aliso is this: wrestling is my passion. Always has been, always will be. Sure, I've been doused by this city's brush plenty of times before. Whether it was being involved in gang wars as a teen or joining the Los Angeles Police Department's elite services, I know deep down that I'm involved around here. I'm in the know, I've got the "savoir-faire" of Los Angeles built inside of me like an innate sense. But let's not go off on a tangent here... let's get back to the point. The point being - wrestling. My history with wrestling may not be long and it may not be decorated with championship after championship, night after night, show after show... but it's the impact I've made in the wrestling world that is important to me. The hard upbringing in L.A.; that has only gone in my favour. In any given match, I'm the man to watch. The man who can sneak up on you at any given moment and swipe away victory at the drop of a hat! Now, I'm sure you're asking yourselves why I'm not back in my crib training for my very first tournament match. Look down... there's your answer." [Aliso points down to the leaves where he placed the case just moments ago. He kneels down on one knee and opens the case's clips just as a gust of wind catches his magnificently styled hair. As Aliso opens it, it is obvious that the object inside, though covered with a red velvet sheet, is a championship belt. Aliso removes the sheet and straight away we notice the nameplate on the belt - "Tony Aliso". A look of resignation crosses his face as he stares down at his belt and he covers it up again, then closing over the case. He looks up to us again, still kneeling down on the grass and foliage.] "This championship belt -- the now defunct Neon Wrestling's Heavyweight Championship -- was the first gold I ever tasted in my career. It meant a lot to me everytime I walked out there with it. I held it high over my head and at those moments, it seemed like everyone in the world stood in awe. The fans' appreciation for me grew and I will forever remain loyal to them. Forever. But the reason I've brought the gold here isn't because of my ever-living loyalty to my fans... no. This goes back to the time of my father and I visiting this site of memories - Lincoln Park. This park was the only place my father and I could go... to get along. At home, we were constantly at war. Two big personalities clashing everyday; it seemed like we'd never have a good relationship. That changed when we came to Lincoln Park, though. I'm not saying this place is the holy grail of getting along or anything, but my father and I got along like Bonnie and Clyde here. The point is, one day I promised my father that when I won my first championship, I'd come here to this exact spot and place it under the tree. Don't know why I said that... must have been the kid in me talking. But I stand here today and I'm leaving this belt behind. I'm moving on to the next big obstacle of my career. The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament..." [A faraway look crosses the young Aliso's face. He turns his face away from us now, as if looking back on a distant memory, only for him to know about.] [Fade out] Time: 8:49pm Location: Los Angeles, CA. - Tony Aliso's Mansion [Fade in] [We appear to be in the back garden of Tony Aliso's huge and luxurious mansion now. Similar to Lincoln Park's greenery, though not as vibrant and colourful, Aliso's garden is certainly impressive. Sprinklers are strategically positioned all around the garden to keep it in tip-top condition. There are many garden ornaments, one of them looking distinctly like wrestling legend Hulk Hogan. The ornament's biceps are the most stand-out feature, probably the reason for the Hulk Hogan lookalike claims. As we pan over to the right, we spot none other than Tony Aliso walking down the lawn, hands in pockets. This time, Aliso is joined by another man, probably a friend of his. The man is in his mid-forties but very well built physically. He is wearing a sleeveless t-shirt and a pair of jeans, along with a cap reading "The Guy To My Left Is An Idiot". That guy just so happens to be Aliso. The two men stroll casually down the lawn, taking in the surroundings and the fresh air. We catch them in mid-conversation.] Aliso: "So, you're really gunning for me to win this tournament? Maybe you don't realise the level of competition in this one, Ricky. We're talking the best of the best here." [The man standing beside Aliso is now recognised as the unsung hero of wrestling legends, "The Bomb" Ricky Metton. Metton's bulging belly is almost bursting out of his jeans. It seems the former wrestler needs some weight-loss treatment. He's the kind of guy who should have gone on the Atkins diet. Still, Metton has a look of unnerving confidence on his slightly chubby face. Aliso looks over and sees Metton grinning.] Metton: "Kid, I've seen you in action before, what, a million times? Well, maybe that's a bit of an exaggeration but hell, I know deep down in my heart that you can pull this one out. One hundred and ninety-two men or a thousand of 'em, Tony Aliso has the ability and the talent to accomplish victory in Golden Turnbuckle Tournament... numero cuatro!" Aliso[with a bemused smile]: "A'right, a'right, enough of the Espanol. I'm glad you have faith in me, man. Really, I am. I think it's about time we actually discussed the strategy for my first match, don't you think? I was thinking -- " [Metton cuts the young superstar off suddenly with this comment.] Metton: "Just do your thing." [Aliso shoots Metton a quizzical look.] Aliso: "That's taken as done, Ricky. When I'm out there in front of thousands of screaming fans, I'm always out there to impress. You don't want a bunch of people heading to the toilet to take a crap when you're wrestling! I'll do my thing, Ricky. What I need to discuss with you, my man, is how we go about getting through to the second round." [Not a second goes by before Metton comes out with his response.] Metton: "Well, you see Tone, dealing with a tournament match is a lot like dealing with a woman. They're both challenges, hard to get and beautiful in their own way. Strange comparison, I know, but bear with me. This match is the start of a journey for you. Your first tournament in your career and possibly your biggest challenge in your career thus far... happens in four days time. There's no point in getting nervous, though. Okay... listen up for a second, Tone. Way back when dinosaurs roamed the earth when I wrestled..." [Aliso chuckles at Metton's weird sense of humour.] Metton: "... I had a big following. Maybe the people who cheered for me were just like me - twisted, strange and a little horny - but that's not what I'm getting at here. They supported me night after night, Tone. You've felt it already, haven't you? The warm embrace, almost, of the fans accepting you. It's something you can use to your advantage in any contest, Tone. Their loyalty is what kept me going and I promise you that it's what will keep you going, too! Aliso: "You know what, Ricky? That's all I need right now... thank you... thank you for your words of wisdom. You never let me down, do you?" [Metton shrugs his shoulders and takes a playful punch to the belly from Aliso. Aliso goes to walk off but Metton calls after him.] Metton: "Hey Tone! Bedtime's at nine, yeah?" [The two men can't help but laugh as they go off their separate ways. Aliso enters his mansion through the sliding door at the back while Metton continues his blissful stroll in the idllyic area of Tony Aliso's mansion.] [Fade out] Time: 10:25pm Location: Tony Aliso's Mansion - Aliso's bedroom [Fade in] [The camera scans the various bits and bobs of Tony Aliso's bedroom - a black comb sitting atop a tub of Bryllcreem gel, a series of wrestling photos, all of them of legends such as Hogan, Andre The Giant and others. A small book, most likely a diary or photo album, sits in the corner of a shelf unit along with photos of friends and family. As we pan over to the left, we see the young man himself, Tony Aliso, sitting at the edge of his bed. Aliso looks anxious; not his usual confident self. His hair is messed up and though he is still dressed, the clothes he is wearing are disheveled and creased. He looks over to the mirror and sees his reflection in it. As he stares into his own eyes, a revival of his confidence quickly strikes him. He stands up now and takes a few steps over to the mirror, closer to his reflection. Realising that the camera is behind him, he makes a 180 degree turn. The face of Aliso has "untouchable" written all over it as he stares at us in a very meaningful way. His stare is almost spooky. Finally, he speaks in a low voice.] "Determined to succeed... willing to go to any lengths to win... almost possessed. Right now, I am all of those things and more. Like the world and its ever changing climate, my entry into the tournament known as Golden Turnbuckle Tournament will cause a serious stir. Sure, you may perceive me as a guy who likes to speak highly of himself but in truth, I'm not. For the time I last in this tournament, I plan to prove to myself and to the other participants in this tournament that Tony Aliso is here to stay. It may sound cliche or even a little repetitive but I'm in it to win it. That's my philosophy whenever I step into the squared circle. With the support of the fans, I know their loyalty will get me through." "That brings me to my next line of thought: loyalty. The relationship that two people have with one another relies on several things, but their loyalty to each other is what keeps it firm. It is the very foundation of the relationship. Take a look at my relationship with the fans. Each and every night, I go out there and give them my all. They cheer for me until they're blue in the face... and why? Simple -- they're loyal to yours truly. Unlike a man of egotism and bigheadedness, however, I don't revel in their loyalty, using it as a tool to further my ego. It does bring me great happiness to hear their cheers each night, though. And that is why I'm participating in this tournament. Not so much for my own gains... but for them." [Aliso rubs his forehead softly and nods as if reassuring himself of something. Then, he tilts his head up and we see what he is looking at so intently. It is a poster of Aliso's championship win in Neon Wrestling. In the poster, Aliso holds the championship high over his head. He appears to be standing on the rampway. His nose and mouth are covered in blood but the battle wounds only make this shot mean more. Aliso turns back to us.] "There are many times in our lives when we accomplish things we never thought possible. That night I won my first championship meant something to me. Who am I kidding; it meant more than anything. Now, I'm going to go it again. I'm heading for glory again no matter what I have to go through. If I need to step through Hell's gates themselves, I'll do it. The thing is, one man needs to face the onslaught that is coming from me. There's an envelope sitting over there on my bedside table. In that envelope should be a sheet of paper with my opponent's name on it. As long as I'm in a determined mindset, I wouldn't like to be the guy wrestling me." [Aliso lets his eyes wander over to the bedside table where the envelope sits. It cries "Open me". Aliso seems to answer its silent call, strolling over and ripping it open in an instant. His eyes scan the page until finally, they stop at the bottom. It appears Aliso has just read his opponent's name. He whispers something to himself and then clears his throat.] "Zane Ozz... let me be the first to wish you good luck in this tournament. There's just one small problem; you're wrestling moi in the very first round. How sad that your adventure may come to an abrupt end. You see, there comes a point in every wrestler's life when he must face unimaginable pain, go through a submission hold so painful that you start to see your life flash before your eyes, look anguish right in the face and scream "No" at him... all to find out it was in vain and you find yourself tapping to my finishing move... the Breaking Point. Now... there's just one more thing, Zane. Are you ready to reach Breaking Point?!" [With that, Aliso grins at us. He looks out his bedroom window to the lights of "The City Of Angels" and simply appreciates the scenery, taking it in. Finally, the former Neon champion turns and, giving us one last wink, heads out the bedroom door into his home. He shuts the door behind him. We zoom in on the poster of Aliso's championship win. His gold stands out in the poster as the prize... one of many to come for him.] [Fade out] [End]
