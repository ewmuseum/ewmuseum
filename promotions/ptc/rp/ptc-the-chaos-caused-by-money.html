IG 71 Promo - The Chaos Caused by Money

23 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Promo Title: The Chaos Caused by Money Team: The Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean (representing Civilization) Opponents: Mega Job Theme: Chaos Word Count: 1996 ------ 01/20/2010 It’s been months since they had made any money but ever since they got a call from their old friend from the wrestling business named von Doom, the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean have been acting strangely. However their best friend and closest confidant, another wrestler by the name of Cave Hulk, has noticed this change and it’s clearly bothering him. Hulk has been waiting inside of the dark, gloomy houseboat that the three share as a home. The fact that he’s wearing torn up jean shorts and a tattered shirt only adds to his troubles. He feels like a homeless person half the time due to the fact that most of his clothes don’t even fit him and are torn because of that. The boat he’s on is named the Black Nemo and is currently docked in the port of Miami, Florida. Hulk is really worried now. His two friends have been gone all day without giving him any idea what is going on. His theory is that it’s something to do with von Doom which is normally the case with weird things like this. That would be obvious judging by what has been going on recently. As he is contemplating whether or not to go look for his two best friends, they arrive back at the boat. He can hear their footsteps on the top deck before they come down the steps to the living quarters where he is currently sitting at. Captain Jack Morgan is the first down the steps followed by the massive ‘Killer Klown from Outer Space.’ Morgan is dressed in a darker colored version of his usual pirate attire which almost fits his recent personality change. The ‘Killer Klown’ has on a black and blue diamond patterned clown costume with black clown boots and clown gloves. His face paint clown pattern is more dark, which only makes the Hulk worried about what is really going on. His blue hair is more frazzled than usual too which only makes him look more psychotic than normal. The pirate and the clown start looking around the living quarters for various items as the Hulk looks on. Cave Hulk, to his credit, is keeping his rage to a minimum. However first he decides that probing for information which is exactly what he does. “Where were you guys?” the Hulk asks them as they continue to look through shelves and boxes in the living quarters. “I thought you guys got mugged or something terrible had happened since you were gone so long.” Morgan and the clown both stop to give him a nasty looks before going back to doing what they were doing beforehand. It’s clear to the Cave Hulk that they are hiding something from him that they don’t want him to know about. It is well known that Hulk has anger management problems. This has been well documented throughout his wrestling career, most notably in GLOBAL wrestling when on numerous occasions he tried to injure his opponents instead of going for the win. “What were those looks for?” Hulk asks after getting no response to his first inquiry. Again he gets no response from his two closest friends which only annoys him even more. Finally his anger gets the better of him and he lashes out at his two friends with a nasty, chaotic verbal barrage. “WHAT THE HELL HAVE YOU GUYS BEEN DOING ALL FUCKING DAY LONG?!” Hulk screams out at them. Both the clown and the pirate give him looks of death after this screaming tirade. The tension is so thick you can almost cut it with a knife. It almost appears as if the three men are about to come to blows. However the pirate and the clown keep their cool. “What we did is none of your concern, Hulk,” Morgan says to him with a little bit of a growl to his voice. “You need not concern yourself with what we were doing all day long. Nothing that we did concerns you Hulk so need to lay off this subject before you find out some things that you aren’t going to like.” Hulk doesn’t know what to think now. It’s obvious that whatever is going on is on the verge of ending his friendship with his two closest friends. Cave Hulk contemplates what to say next in his mind. Instead of taking the high road, he decides to take a chance and find out what information is going on. “I don’t care,” Hulk replies. “I can take it.” Morgan stops what he’s doing and turns to give Hulk a look just to see if he‘s serious. Their eyes meet, neither man willing to concede defeat by turning away. They continue to look at each other for a few tense moments before Morgan finally speaks up. “You want the truth Hulk?” Morgan snarls out. “Well you’ve got it, mate. As you know, none of us have been making any money or finding any buried treasure for months on end since as long as I can remember.” “Yeah, I know that,” Hulk responds. “Good,” Morgan continues. “So now you know why me and the clown made a deal to join this new organization that von Doom has put together. Money is flowing into our bank account even as we speak. That issue has now been resolved due to this deal we have made to join said organization. All we have to do are a few jobs here and there for the organization that we joined. That’s it. The stupidest things we’ve ever done in our lives is leave the umbrella of von Doom led groups. We’ve done that twice now and that is a mistake we’re not making again. Third time is the charm as far as we’re concerned, mate.” Hulk looks at him disapprovingly before turning to the ‘Killer Klown.’ “Is this true?” Hulk asks. The clown nods his head in response to this question much to the dismay of Cave Hulk. “Truiando,” he replies in his strange alien language that no one understands except for two people, von Doom and Morgan. Cave Hulk sighs after hearing this. He’s been dreading this moment for the past few days but knew it was coming. “So what you’re telling me is that you and the clown value money over our friendship,” Hulk snarls out in an angry tone. “Is that correct?” Neither Morgan nor the ‘Killer Klown’ answer him after he asks this question. The room fills with silence once again. This is the calm before the storm and it’s clear that the aftermath is going to be on par with Hurricane Katrina after it ravaged New Orleans. The remnants of the city after the levees broke is a good analogy of what is most likely to happen after all of this blows up. After waiting for Morgan or the ‘Killer Klown’ to say something, Hulk finally is fed up with waiting. This has gone on long enough in his mind. This is the straw that broke the camel’s back as far as the Hulk is concerned. “Basically, loosely translated you two aren’t man enough to tell me what you were doing for all that money and that this is just a cop out?” Hulk snarls out, glaring at both men one at a time. After impatiently waiting, the Hulk finally yells at both of them. “ANSWER!” Morgan finally stops what he’s doing and looks in the Hulk’s general direction seemingly calm. When he starts speaking, he tries to calm Cave Hulk down by speaking in a calm voice. “You need to calm down, mate,” Morgan says in a quiet tone. “We’re all friends here. There is no reason for you to be acting like a wild savage.” Naturally, since he’s a pissed off maniac, Hulk takes that comment the wrong way and only makes matters worse. “So you’re saying that I’m a wild savage?” he snarls out at the pirate as the clown quickly turns around to interject, shaking his finger and his head in unison. “Noiando,” the clown says as he stops shaking his head. “Wesa nosa sayiando yousa a savvagiezendo! Friendo! Yousa Friendo!” Cave Hulk motions to the clown and then turns to Morgan. “What the hell did he just say?” Hulk asks with a raised eyebrow. “He says you’re our friend,” Morgan responds. Cave Hulk scoffs at this. Once again he doesn’t believe what he’s hearing. “That’s not what he said,” Hulk responds. Hulk is smarter than he seems. Despite the fact that he’s been mutated his whole life because of the Chernoybl disaster he does have a high intelligence level. Von Doom knew this and exploited it. Hulk caught a break though with von Doom being the only one who knows that fact so everyone else, including his normally clueless best friends, thinks he’s nothing but a mindless brute. That won’t last long with von Doom being allied with the Killer Pirates now. “Yes it is, mate,” Morgan says to him. “And you’d better stop this arguing now otherwise we’ll kick you off the team.” This raises an immediate red flag from the Hulk. No matter how many arguments they’d have in the past, the three of them stuck together like glue. But now with von Doom back in the picture, times had changed and money apparently is valued more by his friends than friendship. Cave Hulk reacts accordingly. “You know what?” Hulk says angrily. “I’m leaving.” He goes over to the closet door and opens it up. Reaching inside, he grabs his duffel bag as well as his giant club before closing the closet door once more. He then goes to one of the dressers and opens up two drawers where his clothes are. He starts throwing his clothes in the bag, talking out loud so his ‘friends’ can hear him. “You both are so full of shit, it’s unbelievable! You two only care about your ‘precious’ little treasure like two little Gollums from Lord of the Rings. Both of you should be ashamed of yourselves. Consider our friendship over as soon as I walk off of this boat onto the pier.” After he has all of his clothes in the bag, Hulk closes the drawers as his former friends remain silent. He looks at Morgan first and then at the clown. Neither of them says anything. Hulk then heads toward the stairs and walks out of the living quarters onto the deck before walking over to the bow and onto the pier. Almost immediately after leaving the Black Nemo with all of his belongings, Cave Hulk walks down the pier only to be confronted by a blue-clad assassin named Blue Jay who is a member of a group known only as the Exiles. “What did you do with them?” he asks the Hulk, who is more than a bit bewildered. A lot of this has to do with the fact that the Hulk has no idea what the hell this guy is talking about. “What are you talking about?” Hulk asks with a puzzled look on his face. “YOU KNOW!” Blue Jay shouts at him as Cave Hulk continues to look at him like he‘s nuts before sighing. “I don’t need this shit,” Hulk responds as he walks past Blue Jay. “Not now, not after what just happened.” This only makes Blue Jay have to take drastic measures. His friends are in danger and he needs some information. As Hulk walks away, Blue Jay grabs a metal pipe that is laying nearby on the pier by where someone’s boat is docked. He then runs up behind Hulk only to smack him in the back of the head with the pipe! Hulk goes down with a thud and is knocked unconscious. Blue Jay tosses down the pipe before looking down at the fallen behemoth laying in front of him. “Now I can get some answers,” he says aloud before thinking of a plan to get Hulk to a quiet place for some interrogating.
