Homo/phobic #3 – The Ninja Saga

21 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Homo/Phobic. That’s what they call them. The bastards in Primetime Central. The virgin sons of bitches that stand aside and gave them “Tag Team of the Year.” Tag Team of the Year!? They’re not a team. One man is my friend. One is a monster. They kidnapped my friend. Destroyed the Phenomenons. Scattered the Phenomenal Clique to all corners of the world. They’ve destroyed our lives. I’ve never seen a camel cry so much. We’re losing our minds. The Phenomenons must be brought back together. The Bum Boozler must be destroyed. Homo/Phobic must be forgotten. Shane Stone must be rescued! And he will be. He will be. I am a ninja, and it is my job to do just that. This is my story. The Vice Squad’s dog has been killed by evil Cuban smugglers?! We’ll go in with SWAT, the FBI, and three night security guards … the smuggling must be stopped! For they killed my dog too!!!!” [He races into the night. The ninja bangs his head against the wall, before glancing at the address again.] “Success, señor cameraman! He moves far too slowly, though, and our heroic rescuer has already disappeared back into the shadows once more.] “Gimp?” [Bobby’s face lightens immediately, positive his missing Gimp has finally returned after a horrible set of days without his leather … umm … without him.] “Gimpy-poos? Is that you sugar?” [The black flash zooms past our scene again. Again the Boozler swings, barely missing the intruder.] “Oh I get it, poodle-plumbs. You want to play hide and seek … you cute little devil.” [Bobby bends over, his eyes scanning the room. A moment of pause … and suddently he leaps against the couch, flinging aside a basket of assorted “laundry” and a large chest.] “Darn.” [Bobby leaps up again, barely missing the black flash that flies into the kitchen.] “Got you this time, sweet cheeks!” [The Boozler skips forward delicately, his PVC tights creaking with the only sound in the apartment. He darts into the kitchen … our heroic ninja flies into a corner of the kitchen a moment before he’s spotted.] [Bobby twists at the last second, however … he turns, charging into the alcove where our hero has found himself trapped …] [But ninja’s are sneaky.] Bobby: “Gasp!” [The stealthily dressed rescuer is no where to be seen … in his place, much to the Bum Boozler’s rather obvious delight through an immediate bulge in his tight PVC pants, is …] Bobby: “Magnum Wolf!!!” [Bobby leaps with joy, clicking his heels together and clapping his hands. Could you be a dear and stay right there for a moment?” [The ninja looks back awkwardly from where he hangs upon the ceiling.] [Bobby, meanwhile, has turned his attention to the kitchen cabinets. Stealthily?] [The ninja darts into the bedroom … his goal at hand.] “Señor Shane?” [His darkened head darts back and forth, his eyes searching frantically.] “Señor Shane? Yo have come to rescue tú!” [His head falls.] “No señor Shane.” [His head leaps back up.] “El lavoratory!” [The ninja darts forward, and he bursts through the door … breaking into the bathroom.] “Señor Shane! El Phenomenon!!!” [The ninja stands at the edge of a gigantic hold, dug into the very floor of the bathroom … within it, a pair of eyes look back out, terrified.] “B … Borrico?” [Slowly, Shane Stone’s head peeks over the edge of the hole … his hair unkempt, a beard now covering most of his face … but his eyes still innocent and free of anal violation.] [He leaps from his hole.] “Borrico! You’re alive!” [The ninja nods frantically.] “Sí Shane! I have come to rescue tú! We must escape … I have distracted el monstro with cardboard likeness of man he be obsessed with and make pop-shots at every three minutes in his promos.” [As if in response, the Bum Boozler’s voice rings out, hard and loud.] “ALL TWELVE INCHES, BABY!!!!!!!!!!!!!” [Shane’s face drops.] “I … I can’t be rescued, Shane.” [The ninja looks up quizzically.] “But … but señor Shane, it easy. We race out, Boozler still make insults at Wolf board, we escape, Phenomenons back!” [Shane shakes his head solemnly.] “I just can’t. It won’t work, Borrico … I’ve tried.” [Shane begins wandering forward, leaving the bathroom … for once not looking for the Boozler in horror. He passes past his ninja/bodyguard, leaving him to follow, still staring stupidly.] “Eric has cast the Phenomenons into the distance, Borrico … I escaped, went to him, and he beat me with his bed-pan! I … I can’t go through it again.” [Shane turns dramatically, staring at Borrico in horror.] “The Phenomenons are dead … this … this …” [A single tear rolls down his cheek. No soundtrack has begun to play … yet.] “This is the only place for me now, Borrico. If I escape, he’ll only come after me again … and again, and again, and again. If Eric was there …” [Shane drops his head, again walking from where his rescuer had come.] “But he’s not! And there’s nothing left for me now, Borrico … it’s time to give in. My only hope is to keep defending the titles, and … and … and …” [Shane searches frantically for a way to fill the hole in his plot.] “Err … and maybe if we keep winning, Eric will want to bring the Phenomenons back! It’s my only hope!!!!!” [Shane points defiantly towards the exit.] “You have to leave me here, Borrico … I know what I’m doing, and you must carry on your life without me.” [The ninja gapes.] “But … but señor Shane! I no speak no language good … I be go bye bye with no Phenoms!” [Shane still points.] “Go … and flee quickly, my young liege … flee quickly!!!!” [The ninja darts outwards.] (_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_)---(_|_) [And then he stares awkwardly.
