Family Problems

13 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-13 T. roC enters his personal wrestling school where he walks thru the gym, walking by about a dozen men and women lifting weights and doing in-ring work. To catch you up on the past week in the roC household, it seems that his wife Shannon wants to have a part-time job as she’s tired of being a stay at home mom. This of course leaves roC to do a lot more around the house, such as breakfast, cleaning, and the other chores that his wife use to do during the day. T. roC isn’t in a great mood as he walks straight to the back offices of his wrestling school. In his hands are a few letters and a large bag. Machine is reading his mail in the office besides the secretary who is signing for a package from the UPS man. T. roC enters the room and is glad to see the three of them. “What a great day this is!” T. roC announces, surprising everyone in the room. “You know how I always say you’re like family to me? Well it can never be more true.” T. roC tosses his secretary his keys, Machine a piece of paper, and the UPS guy his bag. “I need my car washed, Machine, there’s the grocery list, and I need my laundry washed.” “But sir, I’m the UPS guy.” The UPS man states. “Then it absolutely, positively needs to be there.” T. roC replies. He then starts for his office until he realizes his secretary has put the keys on the desk to answer the phone. “Girl, the car isn’t going to wash itself!” “Mr. Bossman,” The secretary replies, “Your wife is on the phone!” “Thank you Sonya.” T. roC enters his office to pick up his phone. “Hey Hunnie, don’t tell me you quit on your first day of the job already.” “No I didn’t quit Terrel.” Shannon says on the other side of the phone. “I just wanted to call you and say thank you for my orchid.” “Orchid?” T. roC looks out his office window to his secretary who holds up a sign reading, ‘I sent the orchid’. T. roC smiles and goes back to the phone. “Well you know there’s nothing too good for my baby girl.” T. roC looks back up at the secretary who holds up a second sign, ‘You owe me $75’. T. roC covers the mouthpiece to the phone to yell at the secretary. “Seventy-Five dollars! Can I smoke the damn plant afterwards?!” “Anyways,” Shannon continues on the phone. “Michael has one of those parent-teacher beginning of the year conferences, can you take this one?” “Me?” T. roC almost seems shocked at the question. “Can’t you get off work early? I mean, you are missing the children grow up.” “Terrel, I’ve been gone for 3 hours.” “I know, but this morning the kids wanted to see a picture of you. I…I don’t think they even remember what you look like anymore.” T. roC replies. “I am not leaving early on my first day.” Shannon replies in a forceful nature. “Fine.” T. roC gives in. “But just to let you know, Michael got on the school bus today and hugged the driver, calling her mommy.” Shannon is heard laughing on the other end as the conversation comes to a close. We go later in the day where T. roC is waiting in the classroom for Michael’s teacher. He sits there on the opposite side of the desk win the teacher walks in and starts talking immediately. “Hello Mrs. Rocker…” the teacher stops in shock. “Wow, we have a father here…that’s a first…is Mrs. Rocker coming? …Cause we can wait.” “Or we can get started.” T. roC says. “So how do we start, with like a prayer or something?” He says while putting his hands together. The teacher mildly laughs at roC seeing as he has never been to one of these parent-teacher conferences. “Nice hair by the way.” The teacher comments on roC’s cornrows. “I had a perm once.” The teacher looks and sees that his comment didn’t get a laugh out of roC at all. “Ok, well lets get down to business. We have a problem with Michael…” T. roC puts his hand up and stops the teacher in mid-sentence. “Did he get the M&M stuck in his belly button again? All you got to do is knee him in his back and it’ll pop right out.” “No Mr. Rocker, Michael’s scores have dropped forty percent.” The teacher says in a very serious tone. “That’s a red flag for a slight learning disability.” “Whoa, wait a second here. A learning disability?!” “Now there’s no need to be alarmed…” The teacher says, trying to calm roC down. T. roC stands to his feet. “ALARMED?! I’M NOT ALARMED, YOU SHOULD BE THE ONE THAT’S ALARMED!” “Oh…I am.” The teacher says, rolling back in his chair as roC is getting close and closer. “Mr. Rocker, we just want to give you son a small test.” “No, what you REALLY want to do is label my son.” T. roC replies. “You’ll say ‘the black kid is dumb, the black kid is slow, the black kid isn’t as smart as the white kids.’” “But Mr. Rocker, we put your daughter in the accelerated class…” “But she’s a kissass!” T. roC uses as an excuse. “This is why I prefer the moms…” The teacher mumbles. We fast forward out of the teacher’s classroom back into the Rocker house. We find that T. roC, his wife, Dave, and Machine are all standing around talking bout the parent-teacher conference. T. roC paces around with a potato chip bag while Shannon tells Machine and Dave about what happened. “So since Sally is a certified psychologist, she’s going to give Michael the test that the school wants to give him.” She explains. Machine then comes up with an idea. “Learning disability huh? You know, Michael could be like Rain Man. If the boy can count cards, than you’re sitting on a gold mine.” Machine shakes his head in enjoyment while the rest of the people in the room just look at him funny. “You should get him into sports.” Dave says, but everybody just looks at him funny too. “Come on, if Mike Tyson can do it, then Michael should be fine.” Sally walks in with some papers in her hand. “Ok, we’re done with the testing.” Michael soon walks in the room right behind her and the room gets quiet as they don’t want to talk about him while he’s in the room. Machine decides to break the silence. He grabs a handful of mints from the candy jar. He then throws them all on the floor. “Quick Michael, how many are on the ground?” Michael is stunned and is forced to try to count as quickly as possible. “Ummm….all of them?” He asks as he looks back up at Machine. “Well…maybe he’s a musical savant, like Marlon Manson.” Machine says and gets confused looks from everybody again. “Come on, there gotta be SOMETHING wrong with that guy. Come on Michael, lets see if you can play an instrument or something.” Machine and Michael then exits the room. Sally waits for the two to exit the room before telling Michael’s test results. “Michael has dyslexia.” “OH GOD NO!” T. roC exclaims. “TAKE ME INSTEAD!” “Do you even know what dyslexia is?” “No, but it ends in xia, nothing good EVER ends in xia!” T. roC exclaims in response. Sally just shakes her head. “You can help him though. I’ll give you some literature, you’ll do things like read with Michael and….” Sally pauses as she realizes who she’s talking to. She instead turns to Shannon. “You’ll do things like read with Michael, point out the words that he’s mixing up, stuff like that. And I don’t think it’ll hurt to get a tutor.” “Oh god he’s going to have to wear a helmet!” RoC puts his head down in shame. Sally and Dave just pat Shannon on her back as they walk out to leave the couple alone. T. roC then looks at his wife with a sad face. “Maybe this was my fault. You know, I didn’t do good in school, maybe I was dyslexic.” Shannon pats her husband on the back. “Oh hunnie, you weren’t dyslexic, I think you were just plain ol’ stupid.” Shannon pats her husband as he doesn’t appreciate that last comment. T. roC leaves and goes into the living room where Michael was watching TV. “I know you guys were talking about me in there.” Michael says. “Am I going to have to ride the short bus for this? Cause then the kid with the big head will start making jokes about me…and trust me, he owes me some.” T. roC takes a seat on the couch next to Micheal. “No son, but a handicap sticker wouldn’t hurt at all. That way we can park in the handicap spaces when we go to the store.” “Dad, we do that anyways.” “Yea, but now you don’t have to limp as we walk out.” T. roC tells his son. “We’re going to help you. And to start, we’re getting you a tutor.” “A tutor? Am I that bad?” “No, you’re mind just works differently than others. Quick, what’s the capital of Wyoming?” “Ummm…I dunno.” “Don’t worry son, black people don’t go there anyways.” T. roC reassures his son. “It’s ok son, everything will be fine.” T. roC gets up from the couch and goes back into the kitchen where Shannon is. “I hope you’re happy! You go to work for one day and Michael’s retarded!” We are now a week later in the Rocker household. T. roC walks in and his daughter, Clare is at the breakfast table. He serves her what he calls breakfast. “Here’s your breakfast dear.” T. roC says while serving the food. “Spaghetti with orange juice. Mmm, what a meal.” Clare takes a bite of the food, “Dad, mine’s is cold.” “Don’t complain and take a meatball. Matter of fact, lets just switch yours with Michaels. He’s dyslexic anyways, he won’t know the difference.” “Dad,” Clare responds, “Michael is only dyslexic, he’s not stupid. A lot of kids at school have it. Some people call it a gift.” “That’s a great gift. Why don’t we just go to the mall and pick up a box of hepatitis then?” T. roC sarcastically states. Shannon walks in the room as T. roC takes a seat to eat his food. “Michael is upstairs drowning himself in your aftershave. Must mean the tutor is on her way.” “Damn tutor, always rubbing it my face about her intelligence, her degrees, how she went to Harvard.” T. roC complains. “I could’ve went to Harvard!” T. roC pauses as his daughter puts out a small laugh. “Yea, chuckle now but my GED paid for that meatball.” The doorbell rings and T. roC gets up in anger. “Why does she have to use the front door? I don’t want the neighbors gossiping!” T. roC makes his way to answer the door when Shannon has a reply. “Terrel, Dave is in his front yard washing his boat in a Speedo and singing ‘Come Sail Away’, I doubt anybody is talking about Michael’s tutor.” T. roC walks to the door, mocking the tutor. “’Oh, I went to Harvard’, I went to the Jackson Family Reunion and you don’t here me bragging.” T. roC finally answers the door. “Well if it isn’t Professor Pain-In-My-Ass.” “I told you before, that’s DOCTOR, Pain-In-Your-Ass.” The tutor replies. “Did they teach you to talk like that a stupid Harvard?” “No, I just use small words so you can keep up.” The tutor laughs. The tutor walks into the house and roC can’t help but to hear somebody singing outside. “COME SAIL AWAY! COME SAIL AWAY!” “Dave put a damn shirt on! That’s way too much white!” T. roC yells outside before closing the front door. “You know Mr. Rocker, they say there’s 6 degrees of separation. But between us there’s only three, my Associate’s, my Bachelor’s, and my Master’s.” The tutor gloats. “That’s funny, all those degrees and you still work for me.” T. roC replies, leaving the tutor stumped. “You know Mr. Rocker, Michael got an 85% on his last test, I told you he could do it.” “Oh like you had anything to do it, he’s probably just sitting by some Asian kid.” T. roC replies. “Come on Mr. Rocker, I told you shouldn’t put down Michael ten times now…” The tutor puts up ten fingers, “That’s this many.” She says like a smart ass. “I have ten fingers too…wanna pick one?” “Where’s Michael at Mr. Rocker? Or are your braids too tight for you to remember?” “He’s upstairs…” T. roC fails to think of a smart comeback. As the tutor walks up the stairs, Shannon walks into the room. “Were you two fighting again?” Shannon obviously already knows the answer to that one. “You know, Michael’s test scores have gone up, we can’t complain about her Terrel.” “I don’t care, I want that woman gone like all the black off of Michael Jackson’s skin!” “Well I’m not firing her.” “Fine, I just won’t stay in the house while she’s here.” T. roC goes and opens the front door and you can hear somebody singing…. “COME SAIL AWAY! COME SAIL AWAY!” “OH GOD NO! NOW I’M BLIND!” T. roC covers his eyes and drops to the ground. We now find ourselves in a change of scenes where T. roC is back in his wrestling school. He sits in his office with Machine there as well. T. roC sits behind the desk while Machine stands up and plays with the rubix cube. “You know what Machine, I realized that I need to get rid of that woman. But if I fire her, I’m a bad parent…that’s where you come in…” T. roC looks up and smiles. “Oh no Terrel.” Machine quickly replies. “If I fire her, Shannon will kill me. Besides she’s helping Michael with his dyslexia, and SHANNON WILL KILL ME!” “That’s ok Machine, I understand.” T. roC gets up and states. “It’s just that I thought that’s what friends were for. You know like 15 years ago when you set me up with…ELEANOR RICHKILL!” “Come on man, don’t go there.” Machine pleads. “Yea Chine, I go out there with Eleanor Richkill so you could date her best friend. Oh sweet Eleanor, skin like fish scales and a look that made you want to go blind. Kinda like Hali..” “Halley Barry?” “No! Halitosis! That girl’s breath was kicking like a eurpoean soccer team was in there!” T. roC exclaims. “So you owe me one.” A day later… T. roC stands above the sitting Machine… “You slut!” “Wait a minute Terrel, now I got over there and started talking to her and saw a whole different side of her. Did you know she traveled thru Europe the same year I tell be that I traveled thru Europe? Not to mention that in bed she…” “YOU SLUT!” With that plan obviously foiled thanks to in bed activities. T. roC will now resort to his last plan. We go back to his household later in the day where he is sitting in the living room, waiting on the tutor. In walks Dave with Michael, both wearing baseball gloves. “Thanks Uncle Dave for the game!” Michael yells as he runs up the stairs to wash up. “No problem man, what an cannon for an arm you got! I mean it to! You’re going to do big things!” Dave realizes that Michael has now made it all the way up the steps. He takes a seat on the couch besides Terrel, “You better teach the kid how to read cause he’s garbage at baseball.” Dave watches Terrel sign a piece of paper. “Hey man, what’s that?” “I dunno, for some unexplained reason, Shannon has decided to fire the tutor via note. And I forged her signature to prove it!” T. roC smiles as his next plan “Terrel why are you going to do that man? You’re just going to piss off your wife, then next thing you know you’ll be outside washing your boat in a Speedo.” Dave gets up and starts exiting the house. “Note to self: next time you watch the lesbian neighbors, be sure to point the telescope back up at the stars.” Dave opens the door just as the tutor was about to knock. Dave is then stunned by the beauty of the tutor. “Hi….YOUR the tutor?” Dave seems shocked. “Hey, if you think Michael is stupid, you should get a hold of my kid. Gotta card?” “Dave get out!” T. roC yells. “Ok, I’m going to go home and grab my telescope now.” Dave then leaves the house. “So where’s Michael?” The tutor asks. “Oh well that’s not going to be necessary. Here, Shannon wanted me to give this to you.” T. roC hands the tutor the note. “Shannon wanted to give her what?” Shannon suddenly walks thru the door from work. “Damn! How the hell you get home so fast? Did you beam down here?” T. roC questions. “Apparently,” the tutor starts to explain. “You just fired me.” Shannon and the tutor both give T. roC a very dirty look when Michael comes running down the steps to break the tension. “Hey what’s going on? What’s this?” Michael grabs the note that the tutor had in her hand. “Dear Coral, you are hereby fried.” Michael reads aloud. “Fried?” T. roC grabs the paper to check his spelling. “Fried? It’s says fired, see? Fired, can’t you re…” T. roC then pauses in mid-sentence, realizing the situation. “Mom, you’re firing my tutor?” Michael asks in an innocent manner. “She’s not firing her Michael…” T. roC explains. “You know how your mom can overreact sometimes. Now, why are you just standing around. Go ahead and teach the boy how to read.” T. roC feels like an idiot now. He watches the tutor and Michael leave into the kitchen. A few hours later, T. roC is going thru old pictures in his photo album. Shannon walks in to notice her husband’s sad state. She sits besides him as he points out certain pictures. “There’s Michael with a plunger stuck to his big head. There’s him stuck in a bucket. Oh! And there’s Michael stuck in our driveway.” “What’s wrong Terrel?” “Well Shannon, now that Michael has dyslexia, I can’t make fun of him. I use to call him dummy, good for nothing, and pumpkin head, that’s how he knew I loved him. Now what am I suppose to say? I love you?” T. roC quivers at the thought. “I can’t do that.” Michael comes stumbling in the room with a sweater over his head. “Help! I can’t get it off!” You can hear him mumble thru the sweater. T. roC rushes over and pulls the sweater from over his head. “There ya go son….I love you.” T. roC hugs Michael who is very confused. Michael pushes away. “I love you? What? No insult? Come on pops, give me your best shot!” T. roC looks at Shannon first that approves. “In that case, you’re so stupid that they’re going to have to make a REALLY special Olympics so that the special kids won’t laugh at you! Hahaha.” T. roC stops laughing as he realizes that Michael isn’t laughing with him. “Dad, that hurt…I have a real problem.” Michael states with a sad face. “Son… I was just joking…” “HAHAHA! Got ya!” Michael exclaims laughing. “Who’s special now?” Michael bolts out the door with T. roC chasing after him to end the scene and this episode on roC-TV.
