Certificate To Harm - Part I

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 GTT4 TOURNAMENT SITE THE MORNING AFTER JIMMY BONG'S FIRST-ROUND GTT4 MATCH PRECISELY 10:24:09 A.M., FOR NO REASON AT ALL The life of Jimmy Bong, Agent Double-Oh-Nothing, had certainly become much more interesting in recent days. The most hapless secret intelligence operative in Britain's history had been thrust into the unlikeliest of situations. He was entered into a prestigious international wrestling tournament -- and he didn't have one bloody iota of wrestling skill. Why on earth would someone, who had absolutely no business of stepping between the ropes, ever dare to sully the hallowed corners of the squared circle with his pitiful inexperience and utter lack of athletic ability? Because he's stupid, that's why. Well, that, and because his boss made him do it -- for good reason, too. Intelligence gathered by OHMCS gave very strong indications that international terrorist (and part-time hairbrush salesman), Johannes Frederick Billy-Bob Garfeld (yes, that's Garfeld, not Garfield), was about to enact a plan that would allow him to conquer the world. Exactly what Garfeld was planning to do was anybody's guess, but when it comes to the world's most fiendish fiend, one would be ill advised to take any chances. It was presumed that Garfeld had connections with Mike Montgomery and Asylum Industries. Hence, Bong was entered into the GTT4 tournament in order to get close to Montgomery and learn whatever information he could. Got all that? Excellent. Bong was casually walking down an empty hallway in the arena where the GTT4 tournament was being held, dressed as always in a black tuxedo. Where thousands of packed fans had shaken the building's walls down do its very foundations the night before, not a sound was to be heard this morning, except for that of Bong's footsteps echoing off the whitewashed walls. The arena was a veritable ghost town, which is exactly what Bong had been hoping for. He stopped at a green door with a "Men's Restroom" sign hanging on the front. Taking a moment to gaze around the corridor, and satisfied that nobody was following him, Bong quietly slipped through the doorway and into the bathroom. A portly custodian, with shaggy grey hair, a white t-shirt, blue overalls, and an olive green hat pulled over his eyes was the sole occupant as Bong walked in. The janitor was completely preoccupied with mopping last night's leftover grime off the tiled floor, and took no notice to the British secret agent, who nonchalantly made his way to the last stall in the room. A sign on the door declared the stall was "Out Of Order," but Bong opened it and walked right in. The janitor looked up as he heard two distinctive "splooshes" coming from the defunct stall. Arching a curious eyebrow, he waddled over as the sound of someone frantically jiggling a handle could be heard. "Blasted... thing... why won't this work!?" came Bong's frustrated voice from the stall. The janitor knocked on the door... then, with no regard for another human being's privacy, he opened it! "Uh, sir? Why are you standing in the toilet?" That's right, Bong was actually standing in the toilet, and frantically jiggling the handle. He froze as he looked up at the janitor, and his mind raced quickly to create a plausible fake excuse. "Erm," he stuttered for a moment. "I was... just... well you see, I'm a British agent, and I this is the entrance to my secret headquarters!" He paused, realizing the fake excuse he gave was actually the real excuse. "Blast! That's not what I meant! What I really meant was..." But the janitor just waved his hand and shook his head. "Look sonny, whatever. I think you've got the wrong stall. You want the next stall over." "The next stall over?" Bong asked, confused. He stepped out of the toilet, and poked his head out of the door to look. The first stall to the right also bore a sign, which read "HIDDEN ENTRANCE TO OHMCS SECRET ON-SITE HEADQUARTERS." "Oh," Bong said sheepishly. "Well, I'm much obliged, good sir." The janitor just shrugged and returned to dutifully mopping the bathroom floor. Bong switched over to the next stall, and once again stood in the toilet. He reached down and grabbed the handle... "This is it!" Bong said to himself excitedly. "Once I pull this lever, I'll be flushed down the toilet and right into OHMCS Secret Headquarters!" Bong pulled the handle, and the toilet flushed! Yep, the toilet flushed. Nothing else happened, except for Bong's tuxedo pants and shoes becoming even more waterlogged than before. "What the deuce? Why didn't it work?" Bong looked around at the walls. He noticed a note posted up to his left, which read "NOTICE: The toilet entrance to OHMCS Secret On-Site Headquarters is closed for repairs. Please press the red brick on the rear wall for entrance. Authorized personnel only. Trespassers will be severely looked down upon. Thank you." "Well that's just bloody fantastic," Bong growled. He stepped out of the toilet and shook off the excess water, creating a respectable puddle on the floor. "HEY! I just mopped in there!" wailed the portly janitor. Bong paid him no attention, as he turned to press the red brick on the wall behind the toilet. With the slightest touch from Bong, the brick sank back into the wall. The sounds of whirring and clicking and other various gadget-generated noises could be heard, as the toilet slowly descended into the floor like magic, and the wall swung open to reveal a steel-grated spiral staircase leading downward. Bong straightened his tie, and then proceeded to step forward. The wall closed again behind Bong as he walked down the stairs, which led several stories underground. It was quite dark, and the stairway was entirely surrounded by a cramped, circular wall made of granite. Water dripped from the ceiling, falling all the way to the bottom of the staircase, making quiet pattering noises that bounced off the corridor walls. A light shone many meters below, the only faint source of illumination. It grew brighter as Bong drew closer and closer, until he finally reached the bottom. He stood before what looked like a steel security door, in front of which was a welcome mat that read "WELCOME TO OHMCS -- PLEASE WIPE YOUR FEET." Bong cocked an eyebrow at this, and just ignored it as he stepped right up to the door. There was a narrow window in the door, which slid open as Bong approached. A pair of eyes stared out at him. You know, just like in that one movie, where witches are murdered by getting squashed by flying houses and melted down by having water splashed on them. "Halt! Who goes there!?" shrieked the person behind the door. "The name is Bong. Jimmy Bong. Agent Double-Oh-Nothing," Bong replied. "I'm here to see U." "You're here to see me?" asked the voice. "Hey now, we did that joke last time!" Bong said, frowning. "Just let me in! I need to see U right away!" "Forget it! Nobody gets in to see U! Not nobody, not no how!" "Oh come on, don't play like this! Open the bloody door!" "Never! You didn't wipe your feet, so no dice!" "Really, what sort of justification is that for not letting me in?" asked Bong. "Wiping my feet just happens to be against my religion!" "What? Which religion are you talking about?" "Oh you know... it's uh... something... something-anity." "Insanity?" "Well, that sounds close enough. Anyway, be a sport and let me in, please." "I already told you, NO!" screamed the voice. Bong rolled his eyes. "Fine then. I'll just force my way in!" "Ha!" the voice guffawed. "Good luck doing that! This door is triple re-enforced steel!" "No it's not, it's just painted cardboard!" Bong shot back. There was a moment of silence, before the voice grumbled, "Bah, fine, you win." Bong smirked triumphantly, and immediately the door swung open. Actually, it didn't so much swing open, as it was just taken down completely and then folded up by the man behind the door. “Welcome to the on-site branch of headquarters,” he mumbled bitterly. “U’s laboratory is just down the hall. Try not to trip and break your neck or anything.” “Erm, right,” replied Bong. He strolled through the entrance and found himself within the bowels of OHMCS Secret Headquarters. There was another door at the end of the hall, which Bong opened up into what appeared to be hybrid between an armory and Frankenstein’s laboratory. There were walls and shelves lined with all sorts of pistols, rifles, rocket and grenade launchers, and just about every kind of ammunition imaginable. There were also loads of various gizmos, doohickeys and whatsits lying about. There were a few OHMCS personnel in white lab coats scurrying about, carrying all sorts of strange objects and frantically writing things down on clipboards. One person walked by Bong, carrying a what appeared to be the remains of a desk chair that had exploded. To the left, two people were testing out a flame-throwing shower head. Two more people walked past Bong, in the middle of a conversation concerning poisoned condoms. Off in one corner, there was a mannequin wearing a black bowtie, similar to the one Bong was wearing. He stopped and watched it for a moment. "Nice tie," Bong said to himself. Then, a moment later, the tie exploded, blowing the mannequin's head to pieces! Bong nearly leapt out of his shoes! "EGAD!!" he screamed. He put a hand up to his chest, and gasped for air after the sudden fright. He nervously adjusted his tie, and began thinking he might want to remove it. Before he could though, an elderly man approached him. The man had thin frosty white hair, dark brown eyes, and was dressed very oddly. He wore a short-sleeved red dress shirt, a sky blue bowtie, and oddly enough, a pair of brown lederhosen. This was the man Bong had come to see -- U, the head of OHMCS Weapons Research & Development. Bong smiled at the oddly-dressed old man, thinking for a split-second that nobody could ever accuse OHMCS workers of having boring wardrobes. "U! How are you doing, U?" he asked. "Double-Oh-Nothing, I told you a million times already," said U with an unpleasant tone, "don't greet me like that. It's both confusing and annoying!" "What do you mean, U? Don't you like it, U? I only wanted to say hello to you, U," Bong continued. "Shut up, Double-Oh-Nothing!" growled U. "Stop that right now!" Bong, being a complete moron, looked genuinely befuddled at U's testy mood. "Stop what?" "Stop saying... well, anything," U answered, hoping very much that Bong would stop talking altogether. "Well, I'm afraid I can't do that, because otherwise how am I supposed to give you my report?" Bong asked. U sighed. "Very well then, go on." "Well," said Bong. "As you know, I met with Mr. Montgomery last night as planned. I did everything I could to figure out what his connection with Garfeld was, but he kept insisting on wrestling me, and was calling me some very nasty names. I kept trying to get information from him though, even while he was pummeling me into ground beef." "Egad!” U said with a hint of concern. “That sounds like a brutal beating, Double-Oh-Nothing." "Oh, I didn't mean that as a figure of speech," Bong explained. "He literally wheeled out a tub full of ground beef, and he shoved me into it quite a few times. They have some strange fellows in UWF." U arched an eyebrow. "I see," he said slowly. "Anyway," continued Bong, "it soon became clear that Montgomery wasn't going to talk, and I was in a very bad position, what with him trying to kill me and such. I have to say, U… if it wasn't for the equipment you gave me, I don't think I would have been able to defeat him." U gave Bong a surprised look. "Equipment? What equipment? I didn't give you anything!" Bong's jaw dropped open. "But... but... what about this tuxedo I'm wearing? I found it in my locker room when I arrived here! I assumed it was something you'd developed for me to fight in, and that it had some super technology to help me wrestle!" "No!" U said, raising his voice. "That was an ordinary tuxedo! That’s the only bloody thing you ever wear, Double-Oh-Nothing! You were supposed to report down here before your match so I could give you the equipment you needed to help you! But you didn't! No, instead you had to be stupid and defeat the UWF world champion all by yourself, without any help whatsoever!" Bong hung his head in shame. "Dreadfully sorry, U," he mumbled. “But give me a little credit! Don’t you think it’s extraordinary for somebody with no wrestling experience to walk in and defeat a seasoned veteran, let alone the champion of a respected, well-known federation?” “No,” came U’s stark reply. "But enough about that. I take it you didn't get anything from Montgomery, then?" "Nothing," Bong said dejectedly. "Well, that's quite alright," replied U. "According to the latest updates from N's office in London, we're apparently on the right track. Montgomery probably didn't have anything to do with Garfeld as we first thought, but our latest intelligence indicates that this wrestling tournament has something to do with his fiendish plan to conquer the world." "It does? Do we have any new leads?" Bong inquired. "Well, one perhaps," said U, turning to a shelf of folders on the wall, and fumbling through a few small stacks. "Your opponent in the next round has a rather nasty disposition, and we have reason to believe that she means to kill you." Bong gulped. "Kill me? But why?" U found the dossier he was looking for, and turned back to Bong, handing him the folder. "Most likely because Garfeld knows you're on the right trail, and he's looking to get you out of the way before you can uncover his plot. We believe that your opponent may have been hired by Garfeld to carry out this task. Ordinarily, I don’t much mind seeing you marked for death, but not this time. I personally would rather not see the world taken over by an evil madman." "Uh, thanks... I think," said Bong looking through the folder. "I say U, she looks rather like a female version of the Shredder from Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. Hm, Luna Morbid. Well, that's a rather cheerful name!" he said with a smile, not joking at all. "It's not 'cheerful' in the least, Double-Oh-Nothing! She's dangerous, and an expert in pain!" "I see that," said Bong. "Locked up by some mysterious figure and forced to fight all sorts of strange fellows... deprived of comfort and rest... goodness, what an extreme training regimen! Who in the world would use torture as a means of developing their wrestling skills!?" "Actually, about half of the world's population of evil wrestlers," U commented. "Surprisingly, it's quite commonplace. You’d be amazed how many wrestlers have gone through some sort of experience with torture in their careers. It’s almost an epidemic, I must say. Still, in all likelihood, she's going to be much more of a challenge than Montgomery!" "Oh," Bong said. "Well, if she means to kill me, then surely I'm allowed to do the same to her? I mean, I have to defend myself and all." "No Double-Oh-Nothing, I'm afraid not," U said gravely. "Remember, you do not have a license to kill. You only have a certificate to harm." "Well that's bloody unfair!" Bong complained. "She's allowed to kill me, but I'm not allowed to kill her?" "Do stop whining, Double-Oh-Nothing!" U shouted. "I'm going to provide you with some defensive equipment to protect yourself!" Bong smiled now. "Oh goody, more toys!" he exclaimed with childish glee. U sighed and shook his head. "Yes, Double-Oh-Nothing, more toys. Actually, I just have one thing for you right now. Please do me a favor and bring it back in one piece, for once in your life." "Hey, I brought back that Aston Martin in one piece after that mission in Beirut!" "It was crushed into a cube!" "But it was all in one cube!" "Shut your bloody pie hole and follow me, Double-Oh-Nothing!" U motioned to Bong and made his way across the room, to where a bespectacled young man in a white lab coat was standing. He ran a hand through his curly brown hair as he turned to see U and Bong approaching him. Immediately, he stood at attention and looked straight ahead, remaining absolutely silent. "Here we are, Double-Oh-Nothing," U said. "Your latest piece of equipment." Bong looked around with eager anticipation. "Where is it, U? I don't see anything!" U sighed heavily. "He's right here!" He pointed to the young man in the white lab coat. Bong looked at the young man curiously, then back at U. "Him?" he asked unbelievingly. "This is just one of your helpers!" "Actually," explained U, "he's much more than that. He's our latest development in agent defense... a human shield!" "A what?" "A human shield, Double-Oh-Nothing," U repeated. "If you find yourself in the line of fire, you just hold him directly in front of you, and you're completely safe!" “Madness!” Bong exclaimed. “How is that even possible!?” “I absolutely refuse to offer you any sort of logical explanation, so you’ll just have to trust me,” U replied sharply. “But, wouldn’t he be killed after one use?” asked Bong. “Logically speaking, absolutely. But when the world is in crisis, there’s no room for logic. You’d be surprised what this man can live through,” said U. "I... see," said Bong, turning back to the young man and looking him up and down. There didn't really seem to be anything particularly special or remarkable about him as far as Bong could tell. He shrugged, and extended a hand. "Bong. Jimmy Bong." The young man in the lab coat reached out and shook Bong's hand. "I'm Majorly Shafted," he replied. "I'll certainly say that, I'd probably feel the same way if I'd been delegated as a human shield," Bong quipped. "No, Double-Oh-Nothing, that's his name," said U, irritated. "Anyway, Shafted is all that I can provide you with for now. The rest of your equipment is being shipped over from London by one of our couriers, including some of our latest developments in field operations. I'm sorry that we don't have any of it here at the moment, but there was only so much we could bring over to this location." U reached into his pocket, and handed Bong a slip of paper. "The courier will meet you at this time and location. Take Shafted with you, and do be careful. Garfeld's henchmen could be lurking anywhere, and there's no saying whether or not Morbid wait until your scheduled match to make her move. Good luck." “Thank you, U,” smiled Bong. U rolled his eyes, and turned away to attend to something else. Bong read the slip of paper once, then folded it up and tucked it away in his pocket. He started to make for the exit with Majorly Shafted, when something caught his eye. It was a big red shiny button on a nearby control panel. “A big red shiny button!” Bong cried with jubilation. “I always loved these things! I wonder what this button does…” U overheard Bong’s exclamation, and quickly whipped around in a panic. “NO, Double-Oh-Nothing!” he shouted. “That button launches one of our special ‘surface-to-Bong’ missiles!” “Launches the what?” asked Bong, as he pressed the button. Suddenly, the floor opened up about twenty feet in front of Bong. A small missile launcher ascended from the ground, with a deadly missile pointed directly at Bong! The British secret agent looked around in a panic, trying to find a place to hide. Suddenly, a thought occurred to him… “Shafted!” he yelled. Bong dove at Shafted, who was standing just a few feet away. Bong tackled him to the ground, and then pulled Shafted on top of him, just as the missile fired! KABOOOOOOOOOM!!! The explosion rocked the laboratory! Everybody was thrown off his or her feet by the shock. Papers and many pieces of equipment fell off shelves and tables everywhere. Dust and debris crumbled and fell from the ceiling, adding to the chaos. After a few moments, as the smoke and dust started to clear, Jimmy Bong rolled the charred body of Majorly Shafted off of him, and stood up, brushing himself off. He looked down at Shafted, who was moaning in pain. “I say Shafted, are you alright?” Bong asked stupidly. “No… problem, sir,” he muttered and coughed. “Just a few third-degree burns is all.” “Splendid!” Bong said cheerfully. “Come along then, we have a match to prepare for!” With that, Bong turned on his heel and walked merrily out of the laboratory. Amazingly enough, Shafted managed to get up to his feet a few moments later, still looking rather charred from head to toe, but otherwise undaunted. He hobbled out of the laboratory, following the man he was assigned to protect from Garfeld, Luna Morbid, and even his own stupidity. TO BE CONTINUED...
