To sleep perchance to dream

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 Watching the chest rise and fall rhythmically, Ember breathed a small sigh of relief. Only mere moments before Khaled’s eyes had closed for the final time after a struggle to keep them open, a struggle that he eventually lost, and his body had relaxed back into the chair. He was asleep before he knew it. I don’t know how he lasted this long Ember thought to himself. The last few nights hadn’t been easy for either of them, but at least he had had small periods of sleep, no matter how fractured they were, and he was much more used to going without than Khaled was. Sleep deprivation was a well-known friend to Ember. Khaled didn’t have that luxury. Each and every time that he had woken up, more often than not still breathing as heavily as if he had run a marathon when in fact it was nothing but night terrors that froze his lungs, he had found Khaled there. Always there. While Ember played down the nightmares that had haunted him for the last few days he knew that without the constant, calming presence of his only friend he would the dreams would have driven him insane by now. Well, even more insane I suppose he thought with a wry smile. He didn’t truly believe that there was anything wrong with his sanity but he knew that anyone else who had been through what he had would be questioning his or her mental state. Even Khaled, if he only knew. Better that he doesn’t then. The realisation that he couldn’t – wouldn’t – share what was going on with the one person in his life that he trusted most brought a sigh from Ember’s mouth. Long and melancholy. "Trust the most," Ember breathed softly, barely above a whisper, "but not enough. Never enough." Getting slowly to his feet, his movements catlike and measured, he didn’t make a sound as he reached behind the couch on which he had rested and lifted up a patchwork blanket. Holding it open he reached out and tenderly placed it across the sleeping form of Khaled, smiling as the larger man brushed at an errant lock of hair that had tickled his face. Waiting until the breathing became steady again Ember simply stood over his friend, watching him. Just as he, Ember knew without having seen it, had watched him. The only difference being that Khaled slept soundly, his dreams undisturbed. "To sleep perchance to dream …" Ember whispered as he walked away from Khaled. He couldn’t help a twinge of jealousy running through him as he watched how soundly asleep his friend had become in such a short time. Then again, he didn’t go through what I did he thought to himself as he wandered out of the barely lit living room and into the hallway that led to the other rooms, his mind bringing him back to just a few days before. - - - - - - - - - - He remembered the day itself. It had been a beautiful winters day, cold and crisp. Every breath that he had taken had filled his lungs with frigid air, which was then expelled to form crystalline clouds that danced in front of his face before dissipating into intangibility. It still made him laugh at the absurdity of taking the name Ember when in fact he loved the cool climate of winter. Adored it. With perfect clarity he recalled the run in with the grifter, the charlatan, the so-called street ‘magician’ and his little parlour tricks. The rush of adrenaline that the memory evoked in his body was nothing as to that which he felt when he actually went toe-to-toe with the fraud. The look of terror in his eyes as the flames weaved around him was enough to make Ember laugh out loud. The envelope that he had discovered on his doorstep when he returned home was etched into his memory, the words on the card inside it burned deep into his soul. For so many people, one hundred and twenty seven others to be exact, they meant different things. An opportunity to prove themselves perhaps, a paycheck in the waiting, even nothing more than the buzz of performing on the biggest stage in the industry. The GTT. None of that, however, went through Ember’s mind as he took in the words, not for him was the anticipation of entering something prestigious. Not for him was the thought of the merchandising that would come from simply appearing in, let alone winning, this tournament. No. All that went through Ember’s head, in that one instant, was control. The loss of it at least. For the first time in a year his ironclad grip on what he had struggled so long for, so hard for, faltered. And then broke completely. As his body slipped to the floor below him his mind turned inwards and with a scream that never left his mouth he felt himself sinking into the darkness, into the oblivion that he had clawed his way out of. Fleetingly, for a mere fraction of a moment, he felt something, another, brush by him and with terrible certainty he heard a scream coming from a distance and recognised the voice instantly. He knew that at that very moment his body was lying curled up and unresponsive. Just as he knew that while it was screaming, the long wail going out banshee-like, it wasn’t his scream. The wind was knocked out of him as he landed. He knew that he hadn’t fallen but there was no denying the impact that shook his body, nor the pain in his face as it slammed into the unforgiving solidity of the ground. Taking a deep, involuntary, gasp Ember found himself choking as a fine dust was sucked up through his nostrils and clogged his windpipe. "That has got to hurt". The voice came as the third surprise, the landing without a fall being the first. The second surprise, and possibly most shocking, was the fact that as the female voice filtered into his hearing he felt himself picked up from behind and lifted into the air, while something struck his back. Hard. Choking on the dust, and with the little air remaining in his lungs being knocked out of him, Ember struggled to free himself and succeeded – somehow – in dropping to the ground once more. Coughing up a mixture of dust and mucus he rested on his hands and knees. "Well that is attractive," quipped the female voice, "but I have always heard that it is better out than in." Looking back behind him, up through the gap between his arm and his body, Ember found his eyes tracing the curves of a female leg. As the coughing subsided, Ember allowed his gaze to work it’s way up the body, taking in the tight black leather that coated the form, like a second skin, all the way up to the strikingly beautiful face at the top. "You finished yet?" A smirk played across the vibrant red lips of the woman as she pushed some of her pale blonde hair behind an ear, allowing the rest to flow loosely down to her shoulders. Bright blue eyes stared at him, seemingly twinkling with their own inner light. Mockingly or mischievously, Ember couldn’t quite decide. Without waiting for an answer she reached down and hauled him to his feet, using only one arm. "Yeah, yeah, I work out," she stated simply, as if reading his mind. Reeling slightly, from the change in momentum as well as the shock that his body had gone through, Ember glanced around him and nearly stumbled as he realised that he was staring into a sea of endless grey. There was nothing here – even the concept of ‘here’ felt alien to him as he felt waves of nausea rush through his stomach. "It gets some people that way," the woman stated as he reached out to grab Ember by one shoulder, halting his momentum before he could fall over. Staring down at his feet, focussing on the one area of stability, Ember gasped in shock as he saw wisps of steam like tendrils coming from under his boots. "Jesus Christ," he said through clenched teeth, trying – and failing- to pull away from the woman’s grasp, "I am in Hell!" "Don’t be stupid," the woman said with a short bark of laughter. "You have been watching too many bad movies or listening to gossip about Angelo. This isn’t Constantine you know!" Stomping her foot down, hard, onto the grey covered ground the woman laughed as Ember took a faltering step backwards as more ‘steam’ rose up. "It is just dust you fool" she said through her laugh, "and I never thought that you would take the godling’s name like that!" At the sound of the confusion in her voice Ember glanced up, and was again struck be the sheer beauty of her face, though couldn’t help the flash of intimidation that ran through him. Beautiful though she was he couldn’t help feeling that there was something ‘wrong’ about her. Something alien. "Kutnahin, " she said, softly, staring straight into his face, "what’s wrong?" Ember stared up into her eyes and was amazed to see concern there. Concern that he felt reflected within himself, though without knowing why. It took him a few seconds to realise something else that was different, something that he hadn’t felt in a long time. In almost a year. Every time that the woman breathed he felt her breath, warm and soft, against his cheek. Reaching up with his free hand he touched his face and felt skin. His heart skipped a beat as he realised that his mask wasn’t there. "What is going on?" The woman stared deep into Ember’s eyes and he felt himself losing control once more. He fought against her hold but couldn’t break her grip, couldn’t even move an inch. Her eyes filled his vision and it felt like his soul was being stripped to the core, that every single part of him was exposed to her. "How can this be?" She screamed out as she broke her stare. Ember felt another wave of nausea run through him and would have fallen to his knees had not the woman held him up. "It is him" she shouted out, almost forgetting Ember. "I don’t know how he is doing it but he is interfering!" Conflicting emotions ran through Ember has he gazed up through heavy eyes at the woman. Flickers of recollection played over his mind, though seen as if looking at hazy photos of someone else’s life. He realised that he should recognise her, that he should know her, but didn’t know why. All that came to his mind was a single word. "Raven?" He barely croaked the word before he felt a wrench deep within his body. Nothing physical, he could still feel all of his sense firmly in the dust filled plain, but there was something else, something different. A pull. "No!" The woman obviously sensed something too because she tightened her grip on Ember’s arm as she glanced around her, her stance predatory as if scouting out a hidden enemy. With a cry of despair she threw back her head and screamed as Ember felt himself falling, though he knew that there was nowhere to go. Floating. Lifeless. Weightless. Nowhere. Everywhere. Darkness. … light. He had ‘awoken’ with his lungs burning, his throat on fire. It took a conscious effort to stop his body from screaming but eventually – minutes or hours later, he wasn’t sure – he managed it. The last thing that he remembered seeing was the sea of faces around him, above him, staring down at him with a mixture of emotions playing over their faces. He didn’t know where he was, or how he had got there, but he remembered the beautiful woman in black – in black – black. Black. He awoke in the hospital and was grateful to find that Jasper Sinclair was already there. A quick feel of his face reassured him that his mask was in place and he smiled, faintly, as he heard his lawyer informing the doctor of the consequences of removing the mask. He didn’t know that it was possible for doctors to turn that shade of grey but then again he had hired Sinclair because he was the best. Hours later and Khaled was there, and hours after that and Ember was home. Finally home. It took a lot of persuasion to force Khaled to allow him to continue with the tournament and – while he wouldn’t admit it to him – it was nearly a very bad idea. Only a large amount of luck had allowed him to defeat his opponent, and even then his body had nearly given up before the end. Even Ember knew that he wouldn’t be so lucky next time that Skar was too good to be beaten by luck. He knew that he HAD to be ready. - - - - - - - - - - Two nights later and the nightmares were still affecting him. He never recalled exactly what they were, but he knew that whatever it was it scared him. Terrified him. He couldn’t see the face in the darkness, but nonetheless he knew who it was. "It can’t be though, " Ember whispered to himself as he walked into the kitchen, not bothering to turn on the lights, "I killed you." "They say that talking to yourself is a sign of madness," the sultry voice stated mockingly, "or is it getting an answer?" Ember spun around, his hand reaching out automatically to the light switch, oblivious to the scream that left his mouth. Sitting on the worktop, one leg crossed over the other and a large smile on her red-tipped mouth, was the woman from his dream. Reaching out to place her hand gently, lovingly, on Ember’s cheek the woman grinned. "Oh no lover, I am not a dream" she stated with a small laugh, "I am real. Very real." Ember winced as he felt her nails bite into the cheek of his flesh and pulled his head away, stumbling backwards. He screamed again as his body collided with something warm, something that moved against him and in blind panic he lashed out but found himself wrapped up in something that refused to let him move. He couldn’t breathe and tried to scream again but had no air left in his lungs. Crumpling downwards he felt himself lowered to the floor. "Jesus Ember, what’s wrong?" Staring down at him, his eyes wide with concern, was Khaled. Holding tightly to Ember he kept him contained, trying to ignore the shivering that was over-taking his friends body. It took a few seconds for Ember to realise that it was Khaled that had held him, Khaled that had stopped him, and he managed to get his body back under control. Staring over his shoulder he gasped in shock as his eyes took in the empty kitchen. There was no-one there but him and Khaled. Nothing to mark the fact that anyone else had ever been there. Nothing except for the small rivulet of blood that seeped from three small tears in his face, running our from under his mask.
