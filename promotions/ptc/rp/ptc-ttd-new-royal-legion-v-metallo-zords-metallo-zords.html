TTD - New Royal Legion -v- Metallo Zords

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[b]Committee Chaos[/b] ::The scene opens on a picture of a large castle that is set on the dark side of the moon. The shot moves up the cold grey stone of the castle wall and in through a balcony entrance at the top of one of the towers. Once inside, we are treated to a view of the evil Queen Mogombu and her top henchmen as they sit, circled around a table and discuss how best to deal with the team of intergalactic superheros known as the Metallo Zords. As the shot moves closer, we begin to pick up their conversation.:: Queen Mogombu – It has been too long that those metallic menaces have foiled our attempts at world domination! It has been even longer that they have garnered success and accolades in the world of professional wrestling and it is all your fault! ::Queen Mogombu’s henchmen hang their heads as she continues her verbal assault on them.:: Queen Mogombu – You disgust me! You have thousands of Ich Van Herbes at your command yet you cannot seem to defeat those two metallic morons! ::At this point the general of Queen Mogombu’s army, Tim, raises his head and begins to try and explain the massive amount of failures.:: Tim – You have to understand my Queen, the Metallo Zords are not as easy to defeat in battle as they seem. ::At this remark Queen Mogombu’s head snaps around in Tim’s direction as she jumps up from her seat in a furious fashion. She then starts towards her general, huffing and puffing with anger, as she pierces through him with a furious look in her eyes.:: Queen Mogombu – Not that easy to defeat!? I do not care if they are easy to defeat or not! My army is supposed to take care of whatever I want done and I want the Metallo Zords destroyed! I want their PTC Tag Title quest stopped in it’s tracks and I want it done now! ::Drawing Queen Mogombu’s attention away from Tim, Lulu De Bleu, the raging Frenchmen, speaks up.:: Lulu De Bleu – Zat iz true mon Cher . . . but I still do not understand why it tiz so important zat we stop ze advancement of ze how do you say . . . Metallo Zords. ::Queen Mogombu looks to the Frenchmen as she responds.:: Queen Mogombu – It’s quite simple really, we must stop the Metallo Zords advancement because as of right now their powers only work on earth. If however they somehow manage to conquer the entire Primetime Central universe, their powers will span the entire wrestling universe! Then again I wouldn’t expect you to understand that, you are French after all. ::As Queen Mogombu begins slowly circling the table now, she continues speaking.:: Queen Mogombu – But enough talk of the past for I Queen Mogombu have managed to come up with a new plan that is sure to falter any chance those metallic morons have of advancing in the PTC tag team division. A plan so devious yet at the same time so simple that even a bunch of buffoons like yourselves cannot mess it up. Now listen closely . . . ::Queen Mogombu’s voice trails off as she begins to explain her plan to her henchmen. It is also at this point the scene fades out.:: ********************************MEANWHILE******************************** ::The scene reopens to a picture of the Metallo Zords and their friends sitting around a round table. They are all sitting on wooden chairs facing into the table, save for the Zords, who have their chairs facing away from the table but are seated on them backwards. They lean over the backs of the chairs, facing into the table. The group of friends has a table full of sodas, as well as orders of cheese fries and onion rings. The group enjoy their time together as the hustle and bustle around them is made lower so we can hear them talk.:: Gold Ranger - So I said to him, "Well why don't you just take a hike buster, you are in Metallo country now!" ::The group of friends smile in appreciation of the Gold Ranger's well told story. They give a slight applaud to their Golden friend, before the Silver Ranger throws his two cents in.:: Silver Ranger - Yes, that was a classic encounter if I do say so myself. ::The Gold Ranger seizes the opportunity to make a cheesy transition in the conversation.:: Gold Ranger - Speaking of classic encounters, the Silver Ranger and I have a big announcement for all of you! ::The whole gang eagerly awaits the news. Brainiac Benji begins speculating what it could be, while Afro Otis grows impatient with the anticipation. At the same time, Pleasantly Plump Patti does her best to figure out how it could relate to her and her eating habits. Finally, Thin n' Trim Tammy inquires as to what the news is.:: Thin n' Trim Tammy - Well boys, don't keep us waiting for ever, what's you're big announcement? I bet it's a doozy! Silver Ranger - Oh, we will not disappoint. As the Gold Ranger was saying, speaking of classic encounters, we are due to have quite a few of them in the near future. ::Eager to understand more, Brainiac Benji interrupts.:: Brainiac Benji - And why would this be? Gold Ranger - Well, the Metallo Zords have been accepted into the Primetime Central Tag Team Division! Is this not awe-inspiring? ::The Gold Ranger lets out a robotic chuckle in his mechanical voice. Excited for her friends, Thin n' Trim Tammy voices her joy.:: Thin n' Trim Tammy - Wow, guys, that's great news! I'm so happy for you! I can't believe it, a PTC tag title contender, sitting here at this table with me! Afro Otis - Yo, you know where weez come from, news like this be asking fo a partay! Brainiac Benji - Yes, a celebration does seem to be in order. Afro Otis - Paaaaaarrtttaaaaaaaayyyyyy! Pleasantly Plump Patti - Yay! A party! That means cake! Gold Ranger - Indeed it does, my doughy friend, indeed it does. Well, I am glad to see you are all as happy to hear the news as we were. I want to thank you all for being such great friends. Silver Ranger - Yes, you are truly the bes . . . ::As the Silver Ranger is in mid-sentence, the Com Link on his wrist begins to beep. Panicked, he looks at it and then at his partner. He raises his wrist to his helmet and taps a button, activating the link. Bobbabu's voice emanates from the Com Link.:: Bobbabu - Zords, it is imperative that you report to the Command Center, post haste! I need to brief the two of you, there is a major situation that is as sticky as goo! Silver Ranger - Sure thing Bobbabu, we will be there as sure as the ocean is blue! ::With that, the Metallo Zords excuse themselves from the table and quickly head for the outside of the community center. Outside the Metallos find an open area. Once they do, the Metallos stand side by side as they both widen their stance and grab onto their belt buckles. Instantly, they are engulfed in a gold and silver beam respectively and are transported up and away, presumably to the Control Center. The scene fades out.:: ********************************MEANWHILE******************************** ::The scene once again reopens, this time in the Command Center of the Metallo Zords where the normal calm and quiet serenity has been shattered. In the room, a loud alarm echos throughout as the room flashes with red light. Over at the computer center, Bobbalu darts back and forth in front of the machines collecting the data. Just then the Metallos arrive at chaotic scene in a flash of light and smoke. Before they even have a chance to get settled Bobbabu calls to them over to him in an unsettled tone as Bobbalu continues to gather the information on the cause of the alarm..:: Bobbabu – Metallos come here quickly. Let us look into our viewing globe to see if we can find the cause of this alarm. It is obviously an enemy, but who? ::Without hesitation, the Metallo Zords quickly rush over to the glow of the viewing globe and look down at it’s smokey interior as a picture begins to come clear inside of it. As the smoke inside of the viewing globe dissipates, the Silver Ranger speaks out to Bobbabu with a question.:: Silver Ranger – Do you think this threat is new Bobbabu? Bobbabu – To tell the truth Silver Ranger, I have no clue. ::The Metallo Zords and Bobbabu watch the viewing globe intently as the picture finally clears up enough to be viewable. Inside of the viewing globe the Metallo Zords are treated to a view of an attack by the general of Queen Mogombu’s army, Tim, on some sort of board meeting. The Metallos watch on as Tim enters the conference room at the meeting and flips the oversized table in the center of the room over causing the many people around it to scatter. Tim’s attack does not stop there however as he closes in on two hapless members o the meeting and corners them against a wall. He then reaches out with his claw like hands and laches on to each man’s arm. Then with a wicked laugh, Tim and the two business men are engulfed in bright beam of light and smoke. In an instant, all three men are transported out of the room through the beam. It is then that the picture inside of the viewing globe blanks out once again. The Metallo Zords back away from the viewing globe in shock. The Silver Ranger then turns and looks to Bobbabu as he begins to speak.:: Silver Ranger – What was that? Why did Tim attack those men? Who were those men? More importantly, what happened to those two? ::From by the computers, the voice of Bobbalu begins to emit from his small metallic frame as he responds to the Silver Ranger’s question.:: Bobbalu – It appears as though that attack was a lot more then the normal random violence then we are used to seeing from Queen Mogombu. This time around she seems to have a plan . . . a plan to stop your advancement in the Primetime Central Tag Team Division Silver Ranger. I know it’s hard to believe, but trust me when I say it’s true. ::Judging from his body language, the Metallos look to be confused by Bobbalu’s statement and the Gold Ranger responds accordingly.:: Gold Ranger – What? I do not understand how what we just witnessed affects the Metallo Zords’ future in Prime Time Central. What did Queen Mogombu do? Bobbalu – No, you are not understanding me. Those two men that we just witnessed being kidnaped . . . well it looks as though they are two members of the PTC Tag Team Championship Committee. It seems that Queen Mogombu has the idea that you cannot advance any further in the PTC if there is nobody to make any matches. ::The Silver Ranger jumps back in shock as he responds.:: Silver Ranger – If there are no matches, there is no division . . . if there is no division, there is no tag team titles . . . if there is no tag team titles, the quest of the Metallo Zords in Primetime Central has truly been foiled! ::The Metallo Zords look on in disbelief as the scene fades out. What will the Metallo Zords do? Is this the end of the Metallo Zords chances in the PTC Tag Team Division? Have the Metallo Zords finally been beaten? Why can’t a couple of replacement committee members be assigned so the matches can continue as planned? Find out this and much, much more as the Earth hangs in the balance, next time we join the Metallo Zords!::
