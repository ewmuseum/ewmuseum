Beginning and an End?

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 "Get out of the road!" He barley hears the voices of the drivers on the roads around him as he walks over them without paying attention. He stops only once before reaching the street his hotel is on to ask for directions twice he’d been lost already and minute by minute the route he’d been given to get back from the PTC offices seemed worse than trying to be directed anywhere in London. The night air was cold on his face but the scarf his mother had given him before she died still did its job keeping out most of the chill. He was uncomfortable and worried he’d still not been around America long enough to know his way around and the people weren’t as friendly as he thought they would be. Twice hookers standing brazenly on the street had attempted to stop him before finally he needed the directions badly. They didn’t care that he was lost, nor that he was a wrestler all they cared for was being paid so Logan handed over the money for the directions. He drifts across the road through the hotel lobby and straight into the elevator. The day hadn’t been kind to him and he highly doubted that it was going to get any better any time soon. The days and nights had flown by since his initial application to GTT had been accepted; the first flurry of excitement amongst the folks backstage had done nothing to help him get motivated to perform on such a big stage either. Sometimes you just can’t motivate someone to perform and no matter how many times you kick them they just won’t budge. Over time he guessed he’d improve with this early lag of confidence but that was only if he managed to get past Damien Ryan which right now he highly doubted. Getting involved in the wrestling business hadn’t exactly been easy for him. Yes the actual wrestling was hard, getting to know the moves and stuff was even worse but convincing you folks that you don’t want to be a policeman and that you want to be a wrestler was a truly sticky point. Logan guessed that for an American and in Damien Ryan’s case a Canadian it’d probably be really easy. Your folks are cool because you don’t ever really have to leave home. As an English kid though that safety blanket isn’t there for them and they tend to fall flat on their face with it. Logan had been one of the lucky ones. His wrestling companies owner Ed Raymond had known a family who were willing to put him up and Logan had pretty much just transferred from one almost empty household into another more, well lets say lively one instead. TTW had provided Logan with a great way of breaking free from England and showing his dad what he could do. Prove to him that Logan wasn’t just another kid failure who walked out on a good thing just to spite his elders. Logan wanted to prove to his dad that wrestling really was a good option for Logan and the GTT was probably the best chance yet to do that. Logan was a technical wrestler he’d watched the tapes of former stars eagerly during the first few months after landing. Old stars from places like PCW and newer ones from PRIME Logan had gone through them all trying to perfect his chosen wrestling abilities before his debut. The fans took to him easily, they saw in him a youngster who was trying hard and new a lot about wrestling… well that was what he’d hoped they’d seen. Logan lost his first match but came back only two weeks later in a tag team match to prove that it’d been a small blip as Logan started to show the crowds flashes of what Logan could really do. Things had gone quickly though for Logan he’d been pushed to far much too quickly and had managed to drop the ball. A nineteen-year-old British kid can probably be excused a few mistakes here and there so Ed and the rest of the management hadn’t said much about it. Still he understood what dropping the ball could mean and had braced himself for a hard lesson that still hadn’t arrived. Back in the present though in his room Logan sat on the edge of his bed looking into the mirror at himself. There were probably more strange mantras in the wrestling business than anyone was willing to actually admit but when someone believes that they’re lucky they tend to perform that little bit better. For Logan it was mirror staring, he would stare at the mirror for almost an hour (52 minutes every day to be precise) just before he hit the sack and each time he’d forgotten to do it he’d lost. He knew as we all do, that luck wasn’t real. That really it’s the person that creates good and bad luck by their actions but somehow he couldn’t get out what had become an old habit. Sat there staring into the mirror he probably had his best ideas too. Watched his on coming match and played through each of the different scenarios which could occur. In his mind there was always a question when he strolled into this blissful future. This time it was simple… "Is Damien Ryan a typical Canadian?" If he was he knew there was going to be some real trouble. Canadians in the wrestling business he’d found tend to excel under pressure and what was GTT if not full of it. The match he played out happened many different ways swinging back and forth between each of them but the final result always ended the same way. Damien always took the win, always progressed further. That wasn’t exactly a comforting sign sat there on the bed watching again and again taking a loss where he felt he could win. There is a call from the bathroom that knocks him out of his relaxation and general focus. Looking at his watch he’s satisfied that his regular time facing the mirror had been taken and he could continue on with his night. As terrible as it had been Logan felt calmer now that he had performed his last task of the day. The dim glow in the hotel room seemed almost brighter and for the first time he actually smiled. The camera moves outwards and turns around showing us the hotel room Logan’s half-unpacked case still lying on the floor. We focus in on it, slowly passing by the bed and through the small gap between the cupboard. We finally lay down and focus on his tee shirt. Simple black with his name across the left breast where a pocket would usually lie and a large circular TTW logo in the middle. The camera shakes a little as the cameraman gets off the bed and tries to focus a little better before we fade away. "Wanna come and get in baby… it’s big enough for the both of us" @ Wrestlers in America live in a totally different world to most other people. Once they become famous they seem to thrive on that fame and when its gone they talk about the old days instead of fighting to get it back. In England or in the majority of the people in the "real" world people don’t talk about what they had, its about what they have and what they’re gonna do to improve on it. Maybe its because education is big still in the UK that people are like that, whatever it is wrestlers don’t seem to have much of it. The day isn’t the best really, its raining and if we’re gonna be totally honest it doesn’t much look like its going to change at all in the near future. Rain doesn’t bother Logan he’d grown up with it and to be honest he wasn’t much bothered by it but Elizabeth was. She was the new love in Logan’s life, his first girlfriend this side of the waters and she was still a little nervous about being seen on screen. Logan liked that about her, they’d been together for a month now and yet nobody but them even knew what exactly she looked like. They met up in private and left different ways in different cars. Logan liked the kind of secrecy and maybe that as well as the fact that she was simply beautiful kept the relationship going just that little bit better. Over time they both realised that they’d have to come clean about who she was and where she lived but its always good to have a secret that people can’t find out sometimes and this was theirs. Today shut off from the world they were planning to have a day alone together. The media wouldn’t be told about their hotel stay until after Logan had left (he was certain of this) and they wouldn’t arrive after that for an hour at least. They were in peace and they both realised it. GTT could quite possibly take that peace away from them, quite easily force her face to be shown on the big screen and make them admit to their relationship earlier than they were ready. GTT could easily destroy their relationship without ever caring so why put themselves through it, why risk it for the GTT. Only a wrestler could answer that question. Only a wrestler can tell another person why he put his body, mind and spirit on the line each and every week. It was a disaster waiting to happen getting involved with someone as a member of this cloth yet time and again they all did it. Logan couldn’t answer as to why; he couldn’t explain what it would feel like if he didn’t either. He just knew that doing it would hurt less… hurt them both less. And if she left him because they weren’t ready then fine, maybe they didn’t need one another as much as he wanted them to need each other. Lying together on the mattress the next few weeks floats by in both of their minds. Both are thinking the same things only in different ways. Logan cradles her close to him hoping that she stands by him whatever happens, Elizabeth hoping that the exposure to the media doesn’t make her arrogant or cause her to hate Logan. Both of them wondering if the GTT will bring them any closer together or maybe push them apart. A relationship in secret can be so many things yet once exposed to everyone can it ever truly be the same again? @
