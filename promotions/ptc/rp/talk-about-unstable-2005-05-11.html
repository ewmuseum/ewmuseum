Talk About Unstable

11 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-05-11 Going into round two, Tessa Windsor never expected to walk out alive. It was enough of a surprise for her to get past round one, and considering she barely managed keep her head above water when her head was in the game, why should she believe she’d be able to do it when she not only was completely unfocused, but unmotivated as well. Still, even with her win column on the rise the beautiful brunette had little reason to smile. There was still plenty more to conquer, the rumors tarnishing her reputation the first on the list. It wasn’t often that Tessa spent more time than required away from her children (and Chris when he wanted to be around), yet it was the only thing that seemed logical at times like these when what was best for her always seemed to be the one thing she never did. For yet another night in well over a week now, the woman who would face Drake Coleman at the end of this week had crashed on the queen sized bed inside close friend, Dee’s, guestroom. Sprawled out beside her on the bed laid Dee, who couldn’t help but feel guilty for seeing her friend falling apart before her very eyes. “I just don’t understand it.” With tears dripping down her cheeks, Tessa Windsor hardly seemed like she could be the one to end Drake Coleman’s run in Primetime Centrals annual Cruiserweight Tournament. “What did I ever do to deserve this?” It was the one question the woman who hadn’t had much luck since her earlier days in PCW seemed to always be asking. Why did she deserve to have her wedding crashed? Why did she deserve to have her fiancé ditch her when times were getting rough? And most of all, why did she deserve to have her name tarnished when all she wanted was to earn the same sort of respect earned by the other surviving members, classified by the media as the elite eight. Granted they weren’t female so chances of sex tapes and nude photographs of them surfacing were rare, and even if they did, they’d be held in even higher regards among the male population. Not that Tessa wasn’t, but with two kids of her own who looked up to her, and surely more across the country, it’s not the kind of allegations she wanted floating around her name. As much as Dee wanted to supply Tessa with an answer, the only thing she could offer was a shrug of her shoulders as she cradled her weeping friends head inside her arms. “I couldn’t tell you..” “Why can’t he just leave me alone?” Tessa pondered aloud in between the sobs that were muffled out by the wet strands of her own brunette locks that provided a protective layer between her rose colored cheeks and the feathered pillow resting underneath. Who he was, Dee wasn’t quite sure of herself. Chris seemed way too angered by the whole situation to be guilty. Even though his heart was still crushed over Tessa’s rejection, Jackson Rust seemed highly incapable of betraying his best friends trust in such a heartless manner. The only other he left was Evan, and at six year old, it would be absolutely insane to even think of him as a suspect. “He? Who says it’s a he?” Dee deduced, using the logic skills Tessa seemed to be lacking at the moment, as the steam from Tessa’s ears continued to sublime into tears on her cheek. She was past the overwhelmed state at this point, and just needed something to keep her from tossing in the towel right now, or ripping someone‘s head off, depending on who the person was. There was one man however, Dee had forgotten all about. His name slithered off Tessa’s tongue as she hissed in disgust, just thinking about having to deal with him again. “Because I know Gaz well enough by now to know this is something he’d do.” It seemed like a perfectly logical connection in Tessa’s mind. Ninety percent of the time when things had gone wrong since she had begun in this business three years back, either Gaz or Chris was to blame. Seeing as though the second half had already been ruled out, it seemed like perfectly common sense to conclude that this time the Prince of Pranks was to blame. Dee happened to see things the other way. Why would a man who had already ruined her life enough by marrying her and destroying the once perfect relationship between her and Chris, need to pop into her life once again nearly two years after all the damage had been done? “I don’t know about….” “Just trust me.” Tessa jumped in as her head shot up from the pillow it had been mounted to for hours now. Though the tears were gradually fading away now that she had something new to think about, the dark circles under her eyes had shown just how long the issue had been tugging at her heart beforehand . “He’s tried before.” “And failed.” Dee interjected by raising the amount of concern in her voice, making sure to ground Tessa’s theory before she took off too far and made a fool of herself. But all hope was lost, as Tessa had already disregarded her the difference between fact and opinion, and led the way for Dee out of the tan colored room, down the carpeted stairs, and to the front door, where she fetched her light pink fleece. Halting in front of the large oak door before her, Tessa looked over her shoulders towards Dee who remained speechless as she reverted to her nervous habit of twirling her long blonde locks around her slender fingers. “Do you still have Champs number?” Replying with a nod, Dee curled her ruby colored lips into her mouth and prayed that giving the truthful answer wouldn’t backfire on her. “Yes.” “Good give him a call and let him know we are coming to visit.” She’d never been much of a leader in the past, but when Tessa took control, it was useless to argue back as Karina Wolfenden would be sure to say. So instead of arguing back, Dee simply waved to Tessa as she walked out of the door with her head held high. The only thing left for Dee to do now was to swallow her fears and let it settle on her stomach. It was a lot better than trying to talk Tessa out of it, especially when she saw Tessa’s true potential shining through most at moments like this. She only wished Tessa would take this kind of control with Chris, her biggest weakness without a doubt. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “What am I going to say?” With a pair of droopy blue eyes doing all the talking for Chris Storm as he paced the length of the living room, one could only wonder if it truly was him who had Tessa wrapped around his fingers. The clock had just struck ten, and here he was stuck at home with the kids, for the seventh consecutive night. And while he was tempted to revert back to his old self, and take off without the kids, he instead kept his eyes glued on the clock. Even if he was an ass to her seventy five percent of the time, he at least was in tune with her enough to know she was probably wondering the same exact thing. So he waited. Ten minutes, and still no sign of life. Twenty minutes, and no call to go with it. Come ten thirty at night nearly a week later, and even one of the most egotistical bastards will turn into a sap, without the protection of their security blanket. But his fears were appeased by the clanking of Tessa’s small collection of keys at the front door. Chris took a minute to catch his breath as he wrapped his arms around the petite brunette he had the pleasure of waking up to just about every morning, until he caught sight of who had come home with her. Know, he knew exactly what he was going to say. “I guess a week wasn’t enough time for you two to finish that latest collection.” He muttered as he stood tall behind Tessa, wrapping his arms around her so that there was no escape from his sarcastic anger. “It’s a shame I’ve wasted almost three years for a preview, and yet in the end all I needed to do is find some cheap way to get you on top of someone for few seconds, so you win some little persons tournament.” “I would have thought a week would have given you plenty of time to prepare for you match against Hughes and get you the win, and look what happened.” Tessa shot back, hesitating none to raise her voice as she leaned the back of her one hundred ten pound frame against the broad chest of the Perfect Storm. “Can’t always get what we want, can we?” Fully expecting Chris tempter to boil over, Tessa took a moment to inhale as much air as her lungs could hold. Keeping her eyes locked on Dee, who had all the confidence in the world in her, she couldn’t help but notice that even Dee seemed to be cringing of the thought of what could come about with Tessa attempting to take the reigns of the relationship. Any second now Tessa expected to take the brunt of Chris anger just as she always seemed to be doing. She forced her own eyes shut and prayed that it would be another one shot knock out,. At least then she wouldn’t have to worry about seeing the guilt break him apart, the same way it had broken her. So she waited…. …but it never came. Instead, an uncomfortable silence felt the room as Chris sat back and took the blow to his ego. As much as he wanted to spurt up a defense, he couldn’t help but stand there amazed that Tessa would dare to try and fight back. Sure, she had a huge heart for competition inside of the ring, but outside of the ring she was merely a puppet on everyone’s string, until now. For the first time ever, it almost seemed that Chris had been hurt by the lack of respect paid to him by the woman he had once hoped to marry. What had happened to his angel, the woman who was everything he didn’t have the courage to be? He wondered just that as he silently crept towards the stairs, muting his annoyance, and instead taking the role Tessa often had to take. “Thanks, I‘ll remember that next time you need a favor from me.” Naturally, the response tugged at Tessa’s heart and tempted her to grovel in apology before Chris, just to make sure she wouldn’t lose him again. “I’m sorry.” She whispered, as she watched the distance between them grow, as she prayed he‘d have it in his heart to forgive for both what she had said, and what she was about to say. ”Tell the kids I love them. I‘ll be back in a week.” "You just got home." "I know," she replied, hoping to prance around the guilt trips that Chris may toss her way. "What is it now, more tapes?” Chris mumbled angrily in attempts to disguise how he truly was feeling inside, hurt. “No. Don’t worry about those.” Her patience shown through. “I’ll make sure that I put a stop to those, and all his crap.” “And who would he be?” “Who else.” The roll of her eyes said it all. No one else could elicit quite a response from the perky pink diva, other than the Prince of Pranks, Gaz Green. Chris, however, didn’t take this so light heartedly. How could he when this man had stolen the hand of the woman he truly loved. Add in that it was against her will, and it only made it all the more understandable. “I thought you already slept with him.” “You know that’s not true.” Her shouting did little good in convincing him to stick around and just hear her out., and only provoked more questioning as to why taking off on her family for yet another week would do any sort of good. “Then why bother trying to prove your innocence if I already know? Huh? ” “Because I love you. I just want you to believe me.” Unfortunately for her, her words seemingly fell on deaf ears as the only response was the slamming of their bedroom door, as her heart glued itself back together temporarily. At least the hardest part was done. “I hope this is worth it.” Tessa whispered to herself, as her eyes wandered away from the stairs and locked themselves on the large glass frame surrounding a photo of her two people who looked up to her most, hanging opposite the front door. “I promise I wont let you guys down anymore than I already have.” “You ready?” Dee quietly questioned from behind Tessa, hoping to stir her back to life and keep her from falling back on her word. Casting her inner doubts aside, Tessa turned to Dee with a confident smile that hid those doubts from the rest of the world well. “More than ever.”
