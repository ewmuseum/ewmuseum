My thoughts about my next few weeks

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 ** "Self observation is essential to self growth. You must first understand the motives for your own actions in order to underrated others"-Aristotle ** **The meaning of this quote is simple really, In order to figure out what you need to surpass an opponent, you must first figure out what , in you, makes you want to win. What makes you do what you do, and why you do it. I ties this to wrestling because without taking the time to see why you do this, you deprive yourself of the joy that can be had when you figure it out.** "As I sit and look over this quote from one of the most brilliant philosophers of all time, I can only think of one thing, my actions as of late. The actions that have been shown over and over again this past week. The action of me, joining the Black Riders and turning on Jake in our tag title match. So, as the quote recommends, I try to understand my reasons for doing such a thing. Why would I turn on him, and turn my back on every thing hat Fury taught me. Fury taught me to respect those who have fought hard for what they have received. But I do believe in what Fury has taught me, I respect Garvin for taking Fury's championship from around his waist. Garvin worked hard and the two of them put on one hell of a match. Now, I am faced with looking at Fury, Jake, and all those who raised me, from a crossed the ring. I look at what is good and right from a crossed the ring. I do so because now, I am on the side of the ring who most consider "A bad place to be". I don't see it as bad though, I see it as an opportunity to show what I can do, both for myself and for this business. I am here to make WMW the best, and I will do so -- by any means necessary. Just like Garvin taught me" [The scene opens in Skylar's house in Atlanta, Georgia. He is sitting alone, in his living room, on a large black leather couch. His feet are crossed and leaning on the glass coffee table. His arms are stretched a crossed the back of the sofa and on his lap is a magazine. Oddly he is not watching any wrestling tapes, which would be what you expect from a man who is stepping into the ring against someone that he has never seen wrestle. Instead, he sits, looking at the dark black screen of the plasma TV he just recently bought. The funny part is, he has a large, beautiful house -- great furniture -- lots of appliances, yet his refrigerator is bare as is his closets. It is easy to see that this young kid has recently been able to enjoy success as a wrestler. As the camera slowly moves around into the front of Skylar, we see him on the sofa reading something. He is reading an article in a magazine about the recent WMW show. He reads how the fans reacted to him "turning his back on them" and laughs a bit. Slowly he licks the tips of his thumb and index finger and turns the page. He reads about how Jake felt so betrayed and smiles again. Finely it appears as if Skylar will look at the camera as he tosses the magazine onto the glass coffee table and sits up. Just as he is about to speak his phone rings.] Skylar: (to himself) Who the hell would be calling me today. (answering the phone) Hello? [The voice on the other end can be slightly hear, but not enough to understand them] Skylar: Oh, alright I can do an interview. [The voice on the other end begins to apparently go into a few questions at this point} Skylar:, Why did I make the decision to turn on him... Simple, my decision was made the day Garvin and I met at the cafe. We talked a lot, but I learned a lot about him from what he didn't do. Alot more came out in the conversation then what we had said. [Another question from the person on the other end of the line] Skylar:....A student of the Black Riders means one simple thing. I get to learn from some of the best guys in the industry today> Its no secret that I am new to this, I mean, I feel that I can show something tot he people who watch my matches... But if there is something to learn from anyone... Its gotta be from the Great Lakes Champ and the Ace Superior Champ...Right? [One final question is mumbled into the receiver of the phone. This time Skylar takes a second before answering.] Skylar:...Task?...Joining them wasn't a task...A task entails work and a "I have to" mentality. Joining the Black Riders was more of an opportunity. Think of it this way...Call it a "staging point". It was a main event match, against the GLC and ASC... It was a stage that any man would love to be on. I just changed my "lines". [The voice on the other end sounds satisfied and the conversation is over. Skylar hands up the phone on the end table and finally looks into the camera. HE stares for a bit then leans back and places his hands over his face and sighing. He takes his feet off the coffe table and lets them hit the floor hard, making a thud sound. At first very quietly, as if talking to himself, you can hear what Skylar is saying.] Skylar: (To the camera but still not looking at it) I have a busy couple of weeks ahead of me I suppose. I have to go to PTC and fight in this tournament against a guy from Global names "El Serpeni". Now, I don't mock any man who I don't know, so since I have never seen this guy in the ring, I take him with all the seriousness in the world. What I don't take seriously is all of these accusations about who did what to who and why I did what I did. So I figure going into this tournament would help me in two ways. It will get me away from all of the WMW fans who think I am now Satan, and also it will help towards one of the biggest goals I have right now... And that is the betterment of Wrestling Midwest. The problem is, this tournament comes at the worse time for me. I have a match in UGW defending my title, and I have to take on...of all people...Jake in what I guess he would call a "Grudge Match". It makes me laugh really, he says I betrayed him... But in order to betray someone you have to be tied to them in some way. Be it honor, or friendship, or even respect, I was tied in none of these ways to Jake. I saved the guys ass when Sub-Beast and Marlot attacked him, but I never even got a thank you, or did he ever return the gesture. So believe me, binded we wasn't. Ah well, I guess he will feel how he will. But more importantly, I need to feel onehundred percent for this tournament and I have to take out Serpenti first so... I guess I should focus on him and on that "task". [Skylar suddenly sits up and glares into the camera] Skylar: Serpenti, lets go over this... I am about to tell you something about me... think of it as Skylar Kelly 101, and Im going to teach you what Im all about and why you seriously need to take me as a threat. Any one who knows me, both love or hate me, they know Im all about goal. The thing is, people think you have to have a plan to get to thoes goals but I think its all about the ability to think on your feet. That is why I am so confident about the tournament, and that is why I am not sitting here watching tapes of matches and promos. I will lear all I need to know when I climb into the ring. Don't worry though, I need a way to judge you, so I will let you get a few hits in. I'll move on to the next round and do the same untill I am either crowned the new CW champ, or Im sent home, wwhich believe me, it don;t bother me to lose... because it is just another hurtle on the way towards my goal. Like I said, Its all about goals Serpenti, all about goals... [Skylar stands up with a smile on his face and begins walking out of the house.] Skylar: Guess its time to head out... Its always time to head out I guess... [The camera keeps closeto him as he walks out of the front door. Skylar grabs a black jacket off the table next to the door and throws it on. He turns around and shows the camera the "Black Riders" logo on the back. Skylar walks past the normal red and black bike that the WMW fans are used to seeing him ride and instead gets on a newer looking bike that is black and white, similar to the red design but the "Black Riders" logo is also on the front fairings. He looks at the camer as he holds his helmet chest high in both hands] Skylar: Im comming Serpenti, make no mistake about it, Im comming, and I'll be bringing an ass whoopin'. Don't look to much into it though, you have to lose once and a while. Even a great Global Wrestler like yourself must know how it feels to be pinned by a rookie, and WMW rookie at that. [Skylar slowly begins to puts his helmet on as a smirk slowly opens a crossed his face. He climbs onto the bike and puts the kickstand in. He leans back and hold out his hand and salutes the camera with a "middle finger" and starts his bike. He revs it a few times to show off before pulling out of the driveway and slowly shrinking into the distance.] [The scene fades as the camera watches Skylar slowly shrink into nothing. It fades to black while words appear in white.] "This was brought to you by Skylar Kelly, First student of the Black Riders"
