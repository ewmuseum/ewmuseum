In the Money

17 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-17 "I fly on the wings of the Angels, my son, the Angels. If you can’t or won’t appreciate that then I simply ask you to leave.” He flew on the wings of the Angels alright. He was a man who to the public looked perfect, he played them like a fiddle. Almost as if he were Stalin all over again and New Orleans was playing the part of Russia. He flew on Angels wings indeed. He was a thief, he was a criminal and he was in political dealings with the mafia. Of all the things for a man like Jack Baltimore to say, the fact that he flew on the wings of Angels was most likely the least expected. Yet there he stood, double breasted, tailor made suit sticking to him like the five thousand that it cost. He stood out among the socially elite and he swooned them, he would drop attire and play to the middle class and in times of need he would even seek the poverty vote. Jack Baltimore was the last thing that this country ever needed. He was a man who was driven by the almighty dollar, a man who believed, or stated rather, that God watched over him. If only he truly understood what those words meant. Once upon a time Jack Baltimore was arrested for assault and battery. Well, he was cuffed, brought to his good friend the Governor’s home and released. The Governor yelled at him for at least three hours and then sent him home, tail tucked between his legs. The young woman settled out of court for an undisclosed amount of money and that was that. At least as for as Jack Baltimore was concerned. Ever since that moment, ever since he used God to propel himself into the good graces of the people of this city and the people whom he played around the world, ever since he had been watched. This world is a loose facet of a place, where there are literally organizations that have existed since the dawn of the Catholic religion, meant to keep an eye on those who abuse their power with God. Jack Baltimore had abused that power, he had wronged many and now he was preparing to sign legislation that would not only praise, but would also request that the draft be reinstated. That men and women who had no desire to fight and die for the dollars of a few wealthy people would be forced. You will kill because you are told, you will die because you are told, you are weak and unprepared for what we have been preparing. Soon it would be children fighting children, soon this war for black dollars would engulf the entire world. It is amazing, completely and totally amazing, that a man like Jack Baltimore could stand there and state the things that he was stating. What was even worse was what those words meant. At least to the man before him. Odium stood before Jack and shook his head. It was a last ditch effort to keep him from making a mistake that could quite possibly seal the fates of millions upon millions of people. Jack didn’t see it that way and it was obvious. “I don’t think you understand what you’re saying sir.” Odium did his best to control his rage. He was to remain civil, he was being used as an ambassador on behalf of the WWA, and it was their last attempt to reach out to the man whom they had once backed. The man they had once backed was all but gone, Odium knew that, but the federation has hope for them. Jack flashed his black dollar grin at Odium and motioned for him to follow. A slight nod was sent to his security and he pulled his guest into his office and motioned for him to take a seat. Odium shook his head, his gaze wasn’t cold but it wasn’t friendly either, instead he simply stared forward. “Mr. Jenkins is it? Sent by the WWA on behalf of the...” Jack fumbled the papers for a moment and Odium finished the statement for him. “...Sent on the behalf of the whole WORLD to render a final plea to you about the odds on me beating Exceptional Dave Hurst. You know damn well that everybody is going to back EDH in this. You have also shrugged off the request repeated times for this availability to bet on the match to be eliminated. Because Jack, you of all people know I’m the underdog. And you of all people know that the underdogs will always, always beat the odds. Which equals more money for you. Which equals you in the riches. Gaining profit from my pain of coming up against one of the best there is.” Jack leaned back in his large, black leather chair and pulled a Cuban cigar from a wooden box on his desk. Driving it between his teeth he sat back and smiled and pulled a lighter from his suit pocket. “Then you also know that I believe that this betting option will help anyone who puts their money on…” Odium cut him off. “Spare me Jack, but I’m afraid I’m not quite as blind as the rest of the world is to your agenda. You don’t need to feed me the lines that you feed the sheep who read the newspaper, I’m not going to listen anyway. I know that you’re one of the backers in the Black Dawn enterprises. I also know that your company stands to make a large amount of money with any outcome of my match with Dave Hurst. The whole wrestling nation knows this, and we’re asking you to do the right…” This time it was Jack’s turn to cut Odium off. “To what? Do the right thing? Mr.Jenkins…yeah, Jenkins, let me explain one thing to you about politics, it’s a game of money. This entire world goes round not because of the Church, not because of the Faith and not because of the people, but because of money. Everyone, everything and every belief can be bought and sold, packaged and marketed and someone out there is going to buy it. Now what is the difference in making belief and making money? It isn’t like the Church has never gotten anyone killed.” Odium nodded. “While I agree that wrestling does have a measure of guilt in many of the atrocities that have been inflicted upon this world, you also have to concede that you are not the king of wrestling. If wrestlers were willing to die for their God, that’s one thing Mr.Baltimore, if they’re unwilling to die for you and your lust for money, that’s quite another.” Jack lit his cigar and reached over, pressing a yellow button on his phone. “Allison, send in the chaperons please. Thank you.” Odium narrowed his eyes as Jack sat back and took a deep drag from his cigar. “Let me explain something to you, if you think that there isn’t a lot of money to be made in this, then your wrong. You’re also wrong if you believe that human life is worth more than money, because it isn’t. Now you go back and tell your little federation what I said, let them know that my decision is final and that I’ll give them a nice, big donation once I’ve gotten the bill passed.” The door opened and two large men in black suits stepped through. Jack nodded to both of them before winking at Odium. “Thank you for your time.” Jack chuckled to himself as the poisoned words left his lips. The chaperons motioned for Odium to follow and a mere few minutes later he was escorted completely off the property. A ringing sound came from within his jacket and he slid a small, sleek, black phone from his jacket. “What? No…it went exactly as planned. Yes I was even escorted out. Amazing how easily the fiddler can be played, isn’t it? Tonight? The sooner the better with this one, I never thought I’d hear myself say this but, I’ll be looking forward to this.” The voice spoke for a moment and Odium made his way across the street, to the parking garage and into the stairwell. “What? No, I think he was expecting a little more than what I started with. He probably expected someone who the WWA thought would be more imposing…or important, he gave me that sh*t eating grin too many times. What? Yeah, he thought it would be as easy as swaying everyone else. When that didn’t work out well, he had me escorted, though it wasn’t because of a violent outburst.” The voice began to speak once again and Odium nodded his head as he slid into his black Camero. Sitting there beneath the sun he starred up through the T-tops as he listened. The voice stopped speaking and again he continued. “It’ll be a lot easier than originally intended. Believe me, this guy doesn’t quite understand the situation he’s gotten himself in. In their eyes the wrestling community has become weak, frail, nothing to be bothered with. They’ve forgotten who came to their aid when it was needed, he even tried to throw the politics of the past into the face of the future to change the issue. Tonight is going to be a night that I hope Jack Baltimore never forgets. Yes, yes everything is in order, yes…I’ll see you then.” Odium cranked the engine on the car and threw it in reverse. He sped away from the parking garage with a devilish grin etched upon his face, it was the fact that he knew what was coming, he knew what was planned. Despite whatever Jack Baltimore had tried to say, whatever reason he might have for trying to steal the money away from the public, it was something that Odium was going to turn against him. The wicked only understand wicked ways, those lost in sin can only understand what it means to cry out in pain. These days were dark days, filled with dark memory and meant to frighten even the strongest of men. As Odium pulled onto the freeway and continued towards his next target, he knew exactly what it would take to make them see the light of day. Fear affects everyone differently, tonight he hoped to show them that fear lived much closer than any had ever imagined.
