Satan has No Power!

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 [Satan runs through the woods and hides behind a tree. A demon, named Damon, runs into the woods looking for him.] Damon: Satan. Hey, Satan. Satan: Oh, oh. [Satan crouches down and sucks it in to hide behind the tree better.] Damon: Hey, Satan, where are you? [Damon scratches his head.] Damon: I would have sworn I saw him. [Damon sees movement behind a tree.] Damon: Hey, boss, are you in here? [Satan, still hiding behind the tree, shouts back.] Satan: Who wants to know? Damon: It’s me boss, Damon the demon. [Satan turns and walks out from behind the tree.] Satan: Oh, it’s only you. Damon: You hiding from me, Boss? Satan: Hiding? Me? No. I was… tying my shoe! Yeah, that’s it. I was tying my shoe. What are you doing here? [Satan walks closer to Damon.] Damon: I came to ask if, maybe, you could reassign me, Boss. Satan: Reassign you? Why do you want me to reassign you? Damon: I’m bored to death, Boss. I haven’t wrecked havoc or ruined any lives in years. Satan: Who did I assign you to again? [Damon pauses and tilts his head.] Damon: You feeling okay, Boss? Satan: I’m feeling fine. Why? Damon: You’re Satan, Prince of power of the air. You know everything. Don’t tell me you can’t remember who you assigned me to. Satan: Keep your voice down! [Satan looks back and forth making sure that no one is close by.] Satan: They’ll here you! Damon: Hear me? Hear what? I didn’t say nothing. [Satan lowers his voice to a loud whisper.] Satan: If people find out… My goose is cooked! Damon: Find out what? That you don’t know? [Satan rushes towards Damon and claps his hand over Damon’s mouth.] Satan: Shshshsh. Not so loud. [Satan takes a step forward and looks around he sees nothing.] Damon: Oh know don’t tell me! Are you saying you don’t… [Satan walks back toward Damon ready to cover his mouth again.] Satan: Shshshsh. [Damon speaks in a loud whisper now.] Damon: Oh, Boss, are you saying you don’t know everything? [Satan looks around again to make sure no one is still around.[ Satan: I’m warning you, Damon. Not a word of this to ANYone! [Damon laughs.] Damon: Oh, Boss, you’re such a joker. You’re just putting me on. You’re just making fun of me. [Damon laughs some more.] Damon: The next thing you’re going to tell me is that you can’t be everywhere at once. [Satan still talks in a loud whisper.] Satan: Keep your voice down! [Damon lowers his voice.] Damon: Oh, no! You’re NOT kidding! You mean all this time…? Listen, Boss, I was never the brightest angel in Heaven. But I distinctly remember during your rebellion in Heaven that you told all of us that you were omniscient and omnipresent just like God himself. Satan: I never used those exact words. I MAY have said something that SOUNDED like that. Damon: You lied to us! Satan: Well, I didn’t want to leave Heaven all by myself! I had to say SOMETHING… Damon: …you lied to us and we believed you! We followed you from Heaven, like sheep to the slaughter! [Satan gets a proud look on his face.] Satan: Yes, I was rather persuasive, wasn’t I? Damon: So, you’re powerless… [Damon gives Satan a dirty look.] Damon: …absolutely powerless. [Satan looks around again to make sure no one is around.] Satan: I wouldn’t say ABSOLUTELY powerless. Remember in the time of Moses, when God turned the Nile River to blood? I duplicated that feat. Damon: I remember. But you didn’t even try to duplicate eight of the ten plagues… [Satan hides his face with his hands and turns away from Damon.] Damon: Oh, no! That was a lie too?! You SAID you WOULDN’T try to duplicate those miracles. But the fact is you COULDN’T duplicate them! Satan: Can you keep your voice down, please!? Things are bad enough these days. You don’t have to make them worse! Damon: Worse? I thought things were going good. Or was that a lie too? Satan: After two world wards and the spread of Communism, I thought I had mankind down for the count. But… Damon: But what? Satan: People are turning to Jesus in record numbers. Damon: But you said… Oh… No wonder the Bible calls you a liar and the father of lies. Satan: Just between you and me, it’s the only weapon I’ve got left. [Satan points his finger directly in Damon’s face.] Satan: And if you breathe a word of this to anybody, I’ll… Damon: This don’t make no sense, Boss. Everybody still thinks you’re all powerful. I mean, even Christians say that you put thoughts into their heads. Are you telling me…? Satan: People don’t need any help injecting thoughts into their heads. The same brain that injects dreams into their sleep also injects juicy thoughts into their prayer time. Damon: So, that’s not you? [Satan starts to get boastful.] Satan: Well, if they want to give credit to me, who am I to correct them?! Damon: So, you’re… you’re powerless. [Satan wags his finger in Damon’s face again.] Satan: If you say that once more I’ll assign you to Antarctica! Damon: No wonder I haven’t been able to wreck havoc or ruin lives. I’m as pow… I’m as you-know-what as you are. Satan: Once the Holy Spirit in-dwells a believer, there’s not much we can do to them. Damon: So, why did you assign me to that preacher? Satan: Preachers don’t fall very often, but when they do, they keep more people from trusting Christ than ANY catastrophe we could cause. Damon: Oh, yeah. Almost every time a preacher messes up, it ends up in the newspaper. Satan: And so, since we can’t be everywhere at once, we concentrate on the people who can do the most damage, the high profile Christians. [Satan points over to the direction Damon came from.] Satan: So, get back to work. Damon: Just one more question, Boss. Satan: What is it? Damon: How come you want me to get my preacher to preach AGAINST you. Isn’t that dividing the house against itself? Satan: It’s all part of the deception. The more we can get a preacher to mention the name of Satan, the less he’ll mention the name of Jesus. [Satan starts to laugh as Damon starts to walk away laughing.] Damon: That’s pretty devious, Boss. [Satan catches up with him and walks with him as they both start laughing.] Satan: Yeah, ain’t I just awful?! [Satan laughs fiendishly as they both walk away. Suddenly, there is a mighty applause. Lights come on and it’s found out that they are on a stage! Wesley Williams steps out of the backstage area with Damon The Demon and Satan following him. A few large men remove the trees from the stage as Damon and Satan take a bow. Wesley claps his hands and dismisses them. The crowd dies down and all take their seats. Wesley has a huge smile on his face.] Wesley: That was nice, wasn’t it? Give them another round of applause. [The crowd does so. They quiet down again as Wesley walks up to his pedestal.] Wesley: I don’t normally talk to you, my people, about my life as a pro-wrestler. I don’t normally bring my two lives together as one, but I think it’s about time. Steven Caldera is a rotten and cruel sinner and it’s time that he is saved. Everyone in this room knows my procedure with sinners. At first, I will try to save them. I will try to convince them through words to find our Lord. If that doesn’t work, I will use the gifts that He has given me to prove to them that God is good. [A short moment of applause before he continues.] Wesley: God has given me the gift of being skilled in professional wrestling. I use the gifts that God has given me in the squared circle. Steven Caldera will get his chance at the beginning of our match. He will get his chance to be saved and join the right side, but I sincerely feel that he will not take the chance. I have seen how this man treats other people and it’s not right. He is rude and obnoxious and needs to learn God’s way. God’s way is the right way. [Wesley slams his fist down on the pedestal.] Wesley: As we saw in this skit, it’s not Satan that is persuading Steven to act the way that he does. It is himself that is doing this. Deep inside of him there is an evil that needs to be taken care of and I am just the man to do it. God has given me the wrestling skills that I have and He has told me that it is my destiny to win the GTT4 and rid PTC of some of the sinners that dwell there. Satan has no real power. If he did have any persuasion or power, it would not affect me at all. I have God in my heart. I have God in my spirit. I have God in my power. [The whole congregation jumps to their feet in applause. Wesley quiets them down and then looks out among them.] Wesley: Nancy Reagan made the best quote in the world. “Just say NO!” She used it against evil things. You must say no to evil things. Tell Satan NO! Tell Satan that his words mean nothing to you! Like Steven Caldera, Satan holds no power over me! [Wesley continues with the sermon as the scene fades to black.]
