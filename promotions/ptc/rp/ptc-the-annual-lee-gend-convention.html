The Annual Lee-gend Convention

26 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Yawn * “How long, driver?” I said, leaning back on the leather seat and placing my elbow on the open window of my black limousine. My chaffeur turned, removing his navy cap and speaking in a deep American accent. “7 minutes, sir” He muttered, and I took a sip out of the orange juice beside me. It was early in the morning, around 8 o’clock……I wasn’t used to waking up that early; and I didn’t like waking up that early, but today was an exception. I placed the grey tray on my lap, removing the croissant from the plate and shoving it in my mouth. I never did understand the term ‘table manners’. I chewed on the fresh croissant, swallowing it in mere seconds. I took another sip out of the cup in my left hand, bored by the repetitive taste of orange. I needed something with….more of a kick. “Do we have any cans in here?” I said, as the driver drove with one hand, his back to the wheel, and replied. “No, sir.” He said, as I responded by cursing under my breath. So, why is today such a big day for me you ask? Well – it’s the first ever Lee-gend Convention. The Annual Lee-gend Convention is its official name of course… And no, It’s not an ego boosting festival of mine. Well, I suppose it is actually – but it was set up by some of my loyal fans. I like to pride myself on not having that big an ego. Unfortunately, the same can’t be said for my ETD opponent Chris West. His ego is almost like an inoperable tumor, whereas his in-ring ability was once compared to that of a corpse; and that’s a compliment. Suddenly, the limo came to a halt. My chaffeur exited, and opened the door. I followed his lead, exiting the limousine and stretching my arms, yawning once again. As I looked around at my surroundings, I noticed the presence of a group of autograph hunters. I sighed….I had a feeling that there would be a lot of them around today. I put on my black shades….with it being so early, the sun was still low and was blinding. I avoided them, my chaffeur summoning some security to escort me inside. I couldn’t help but feel guilty, I like to look after my fans…which was why I was here today. To make a speech to my fans about my career, my hopes and dreams, and most importantly – my upcoming match which will determine the identity of the number one contender for the Extreme Division title belt. Not only did I intend to win that match, I also intended to defeat the man known as Tristan Belmont; a victory which would lead to the prestigious belt being around my waist. And that is just what I planned to tell them, my followers – except in a helluva lot more detail. The security guys took me to the lobby and then abandoned me….leaving me in a sea of technical staff who were helping to set up the stage and sound. Out of nowhere, I felt a warm hand on my shoulder. I turned…..it was a man, mid-40s, small with glasses, he smiled. “Lee, it’s nice to finally meet you. I’m Karl Brady.” He said with a tint of an Irish accent…..my expression was probably the one he didn’t want to see. Karl Brady….I knew that name from somewhere. Then it hit me. “Ah, the convention organiser. It’s a pleasure to meet you to Karl.” I mumbled, switching my expression to a friendly grin. We walked down the corridor, discussing the events that were to occur later that day. And then we separated, although I was the one who orchestrated it. I decided I needed some time alone in my dressing room, and I needed to use it wisely. - I sat down, relaxing on the crème leather couch. I switched on the television using the remote, and immediately typed in the numbers 6, 6 and 6. The official PrimeTime Central channel. Since the abolishment of the network and cable system PTC had adopted a temporary channel….and this was it. I was just in time for the early morning news round….the end of it, anyway. “In other news…..on Sunday night 8 competitors from various PrimeTime central federations, all scattered around the globe, will do battle….the winner will emerge the number one contender for the Extreme Title Division. Among the competitors are Ice, one of GWs top stars, and “The Lee-gend” Lee Smith, former Furiously Addictive Wrestling owner and currently among the best of the best in the elite federation.” The newsreader said, flicking through his sheets of paper. I couldn’t help but muster a smile - the reference to my ownership of FAW brought back old memories; some good, some bad……I had always liked being an authority figure. It gave me a sense of respect and control, and really just made me feel good about myself. But, as I had discovered recently, you didn’t need to own anything to be an authority figure. In FAW…I am an authority figure, or I like to think so; an authority figure that the younger wrestlers look up to and respect…and I really like that feeling. I don’t want to lose it. The only way I can maintain that is to defeat the other 7 men…..to win. A difficult task, when surrounded by thousands of fans…..knowing that many more are watching at home. I grabbed the remote off of the side table, and pressed the ‘off’ button. I looked around the room, examining the clock. 3 Hours till my turn…..and so with that I yawned, lied on the comfortable couch and slowly but surely dozed off……. ---It was a cold, dark night. The cobbled streets were empty, and the sky was full of stars. I was wrapped up warm…..as warm as could be, but that did not prevent me from the sense of insecurity that was overcoming me. I was walking…..walking along the pavement; it was like I was being watched….spied upon, my every move examined. I carried on walking. I could hear snorting….the breath of men. “Hello?” I said, turning around and calling out. “Anyone there?” I continued…but to no avail. Whoever they were, they were too afraid to show themselves. I turned around again, and guess what? Carried on walking. But soon, I had had enough. I turned around, my fists angry. Ready to fly, at supersonic speed. Someone tapped me on the back; I turned. I couldn’t see who they were, not in this darkness….but I could see there allies, all 6 of them. They gathered round, pounding their fists together. But I was not to be intimidated, and my fist did indeed fly. 7 times.--- “Lee, Lee, wake up!” Karl wailed, shaking me frantically. I turned over (I had always been a deep sleeper), but eventually submitted to his persistent shaking. “What’s the time?” I asked, enquiring in my most politest tone. “Time you got ready; you’re on in 5 minutes.” He shouted, as I cursed under my breath for the second time to today. “Ooops……” I said, not making eye contact with him. “Have you written your speech yet?” He asked, as our conversation became less frantic and more friendly. “Written? I don’t write anything, I speak from the heart.” I replied, annoyed at his presumption. Before he had time to respond the bell went, signalling it was time for me to make my way onto the stage. I breathed deeply, and stepped out of the door, following Karl. We came to a halt at some steps. A fan offered me a drink of water….I swigged out of the bottle a little and then handed it back to him. Then, I heard the voice. Introducing me. It was time. I began my ascent, a short one it may have been, and arrived at the red curtain. I heard my name, and pushed the curtain aside….stepping forward. I couldn’t have imagined anything more picturesque. The hall was decorated with ribbons, many of them with images of me on them, and was absolutely jam packed with Lee-gends. I even spotted a few old FAW superstars in the crowd, my former valet Jon The Bomb was there, relegated to a wheelchair – as was Xing; he didn’t look as though he was enjoying himself though, I sensed he was hear only to scout me; sent by my arch rival The Meditator. I leaned on the stand, scanning the crowd. Suprisingly, I actually remembered seeing a lot of them from FAW shows. “Hello!” I shouted, unsure of where to start. My loyal fans responded with a huge pop, which provoked a smile from myself. “I must say, it is quite an honour to be speaking to you all on this stage, I just wished my mother could be hear to witness it.” I paused, thinking of my father….the man who I loathed more than anything else in the world. “The next week is crunch week for me. On Sunday I face 7 other men…..the winner will become the number one contender for the Extreme Title Division. Max Roswell, Ice, Fathom, Chris West, Mike Marvelous, Noah Hanson, Sean McClancy…and me. We will all meet, in the squared circle, in the centre of the wrestling universe….in PrimeTime Central. There will only be one winner. And that winner will go by the name of Lee Smith!” I spoke, the fans chanting my name. “You know…I’ve been involved in PTC for a long, long time. Longer than anyone else in this match? Yes, and by quite a steep margin I imagine as well. I know how things work, I know how they do things around this joint. This match is just a preview, a taster meant to get the fans to watch the ETD Title match, which will star the winner of this match. They already have a pre-determined winner in their minds, the winner who will face Tristan Belmont in one of the most money making matches in wrestling history – period. Perhaps I have a big ego, perhaps not….or perhaps I am correct in thinking that that winner will be me. Who else can challenge me? Max Roswell? That guy has serious problems. I’m not sure whether the guys over at GW have noticed, but he’s…well, to put it bluntly, insane. Not that that is a bad thing, I suppose he will be well equipped for the match, but in my opinion, the winner will be the one with the strongest mind, the will to succeed. I am the cleverest, I have the strongest will to succeed, I have the best physique in this match, and that, my fellow Lee-gends, is why I, Lee Smith, will triumph. Ice…jesus, and I thought Max was insane? It seems GW is more of an asylum for the insane, rather than an actual wrestling promotion. But, Ice is insane in a different way. He goes to fortune tellers….a sign he is unsure of his skill. I do not need a woman in a cloak to tell me my future, for it is all planned out. But, what makes me think he is even more insane than Max is the fact that he has engaged in sexual intercourse with a horse. Yes, ladies and gentleman, a horse. Her name is Star, the most irritating horse in the industry. And to top it all of….she’s pregnant. This is mind boggling, this guy really is nutty. Hell, he brings a whole new meaning to the word. And then there’s ‘Insane’ Chris West; WHAT THE HELL IS UP WITH THESE PEOPLE?! Over at FAW we only have one insane person – his name is Apocalypse although I think he thinks that he’s Arnold Schwarzenegger with all the adventures he gets up to. So, what about this Chris West guy. He has a wild temper, and doesn’t seem to know the rules of baseball. Well, neither do I, but that’s besides the point. He’s convinced himself that no one can imagine him winning anything, but that he actually will…? Chris, the reason no-one can imagine you winning anything is quite simply because no-one knows who the **** you are! The same can’t be said for me, looking at the evidence before me. Mike Marvelous…another egomaniac, he thinks he’s the best thing since free pornography; but he’s forgetting, that’s me! It’s nice to finally be able to insult someone who isn’t insane, although I must admit with an ego the size of his he is on the brink of insanity. And, with a little advice from my chauffeur, I’d be happy to drive him there. Then there’s Noah Hanson….it seems other wrestling federations don’t have strict drugs guidelines like FAW – Noah, its steroids that improve your physique, not ecstasy. I’ve heard about your dreams, and I can recommend you to a councillor. This Noah dude is another one who is unsure of his future….sigh, this really is going to be a walk in the park. Confidence is a factor in my worldwide wrestling success, I have every match planned to precision. If I wanted to, I could tell you which leg I will hook to get the one two three, and the angle it will be from the ring post. You see; I am a clever man, and the strong mind never loses to the weak, insane, mind. And then there’s Sean McClancy, The Irish Bastard. You know, the only Irish people I’ve ever met have been farmers, I know that sounds weird – but its true. I never saw them with anything other than straw in their mouth….and guess what, every single one of them was a potato farmer. A lucrative business, I suppose. The McClancy family are an exception though. They have decided to become involved in the wrestling business, but Sean is going to have a change of career – I am going to help him. Together, on Sunday, we are going to dig a massive hole….dig for potatoes – and then we are going to buy a wooden casket…and then? I’m going to hit him over the head with my trusty shovel and bury him, alive. And now, finally….Fathom, perhaps my main contender. This guy has had a tormented life….scary stuff, and has been the bitch of Tristan Belmont in the past. Fathom, you have a strong mind. I am indeed shocked that you have not contemplated suicide as of yet. You have gone through so much pain, so much suffering. But you know, as I was saying to my trash talk opponent Brian Arson the other day, to be a Lee-gend is to be strong, even when you feel that you should give your opponent the victory, as some would have contemplated when facing him as he is currently unemployed in PTC, although I hear he has had a few offers since I kicked his sorry ass. Fathom, I’m not going to fall for your act. I’m not going to hand you victory on a silver platter. It would be an insult to the fans in this hall today, all gathered here in celebration of……ME. And so, I am going to defeat you, and the other six men, and emerge the number one contender for the PTC Extreme Title which is currently held by Tristan Belmont…I am.” I finished my piece, and then walked off the stage. The next time I would be on the stage would be on the ramp of Primetime Central, on my way down to the ring, on the path to victory.
