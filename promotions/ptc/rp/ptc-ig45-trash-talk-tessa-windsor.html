IG45 Trash Talk Open Invitation thread (all vs Chris Storm)

2 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2 June 2008 I have to say, Seymour is right. It really is kind of pathetic that the best shots at Chris some of you guys can muster up are ones regarding me. First off, let me set the record straight. All this nonsense regarding Knight and I is nothing more than that. If you guys can furnish proof that what Knight and I share is something more than a friendship, please humor me with it. And whore? Whore is the best insult you guys can come up with? That's all us sucessful women ever are is whores. Whores that belong in the kitchen. Whore's that are transexual. I'm sure Vivica and Lindsay will agree with me on we've heard it all before. At 26 years of age I've dated about as many guys as you can count on one hand, and slept with even less. How that constitutes me being a whore is beyond me. What humors me even more is that it's coming from people such as Kael and von Doom. When is the last time either of you even mattered? Have you even managed to score a contract, Kael? Please tell me that you have. It'd really be kind of embarassing if you're around here tooting your own horn and haven't managed to score a spot somewhere yet. And von Doom, for as long as you career has lasted, has anyone ever so much as considered you a threat? Speaking for Chris, I'm pretty sure the last thing he's intrested in is facing someone with a losing record. What's that going to prove? ---- 2 June 2008 I'm insignificant? Right. I'm so insignifcant that I'm going on my fourth month now as GCW US Champion. What have you won while you've been there? You beat the Cowboys from Hell. Wow. That's some mega accomplishments right there. I've beaten one half of their team in Dorian Ryan, what, three times now? I'm so insignificant that I've got people such as Seymour Almasy, Lestatt Knight, Digital Mortality, and Chris Storm concerned over who I side with. And what do all four of them say? They all say that I have it in me to be one of the best. That's four legends and potential hall of fame canidates tug of warring over who I side with. Who is worried about you? Axel Action. Wow. So threatening you are. And before you go do the predictable and say it's because I'm a whore, please present me with some cold hard evidence. I'm still waiting for it. After all, if I'm as much of a whore as you claim I am, there ought to be plenty out there.
