Adventures in Babysitting Part One

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 A quick glance at the line-up for Survival reveals a star studded 4WW Team that is capable of waltzing out with the crown and prestige of ‘best Primetime Central Organization’. However, despite all the talent that has been brought together there is the question of whether or not Team 4WW will be able to get along with each other long enough to even make it past the first round. Four men that each have a history of not playing well with others, the only conceivable way for them to win is if one of the team members step up and keep the rag tag team in line. When Mike Biddle learned of his addition to the team he knew that the role of ’babysitter’ would be assigned to him. Of his three teammates Darek DeAngleo commanded the most respect from Mike. The powerful monster had a history of dominating not only the Four Winds but a handful of other federations as well. The only problems Mike had with Darek was the “Dark Angel’s” tendency to be a sentimental fool when it came to unattractive women and the fact that the man obviously suffered from some sort of multiple personality disorder. Of course Darek blamed his shortcomings on stupid things like demonic possession, hardly a good excuse for wanting to pump Patti Anderson full of man fat. Still, the monster gave an imposing presence to the team, one that would be felt as long as DeAngleo didn’t start problems between himself and Fnord. The problems between the so-called Modern Messiah and Fnord were a result of Darek’s infatuation with the manly Patti Anderson. Fnord felt jealous that Darek would rather spend time with Patti instead of him. This of course led to the Ian Childress Butt-Kissing Club beating down Darek, quite pathetic that a woman that has the looks of a warthog could cause so much trouble. Mike doesn’t really have any problems with Fnord; he just finds the freak to be annoying. There has been much evidence of Mike being Fnord’s hero, something Mike could use to his advantage as he tries to inspire Team 4WW to victory. The third member of the team was the one that Mike loathed the most, the one that made him think that victory might not be possible due to the problems each member has had with this particular man. Jerms had made a habit of pissing in Mike’s cornflakes, making him Mike’s arch-nemesis. Jerms was like a spoiled child that expected to get everything he wanted, if he didn’t he’d pitch a huge fit in public until someone pacified him. If Team 4WW won it all Jerms would be the first one to take credit for everything, however; if they lost he’d be the first one to point the finger elsewhere. It was mostly Mike’s fault that Jerms had become this way, Mike had fueled Jerms ego by extending an invitation to join the now defunct Featured Attraction. Jerms’ entry to the elite stable led to a short lived South Wind Title reign, a reasonable reign as one-half of the East Wind Champions, and of course the trophy from the Four Winds Tournament. Would Mike be able to hold this team together long enough for them all to bask in the glory of winning the Survival challenge? It was quite the interesting problem but Mike had made a point of not worrying about it. Instead he contented himself with putting the finishing touches on the large, new, and very expensive aquarium he had purchased while on tour in Japan. The aquarium rested upon a table that was behind the black leather couch in the living room of Mike’s Beverly Hills home. He had just finished filling it with water and was about to go pick up the fish he had ordered from the local pet shop when he heard the doorbell ring. Mike was slightly surprised to know that someone was at the door, he had arrived from Japan late at night and had not bothered to let anyone from his social circle know that he was in town. With a sigh he climbed down from the ladder he had set up on the side of his aquarium and made his way to the front door. Normally he wouldn’t have to answer the door himself but he had made a point of giving his servants the day off so he could enjoy some time to himself before concerning himself with the upcoming Inter-fed Pay-Per-View. Before stepping into his foyer he paused in front of the mirror on the wall to make sure that his blond hair was still perfectly combed and that the rest of his handsome appearance was in order. He probably would have remained at the mirror nit-picking over unnoticeable flaws in his appearance if the doorbell hadn’t of rang a second time and been quickly followed by a loud knock. With an irritated sigh Mike finished the short journey to the front door, he paused in front of the door in order to look through the peephole so he could see who his annoying caller might be. The woman standing on his front step gave Mike a great surprise; her name was Carlotta Valdez, a beautiful brunette that Mike had dated a few years ago and had not seen since he dumped her. She looked to still be in great shape despite having put on a few pounds, though the extra weight was not easily noticeable due to the baggy dress she wore. Mike was lost in a trance as he stared at his ex-lover through the peephole, however; he was quickly brought back to reality as the impatient Carlotta rang the doorbell yet again. Startled, Mike opened the door and tried his best not to act surprised by Carlotta’s sudden appearance. As he laid eyes on her a cocky smirk spread across his face as he longingly stared at her perky breasts. “Look at my face Mike,” Carlotta said as snapped her fingers in front of his face, instantly grabbing his attention. “I see you haven’t changed at all.” She finished with an amused smile. “Well you’ve obviously changed,” Mike said as he pointed to her stomach, which wasn’t as flat as it was the last time he saw her. Carlotta glared at him with an intense fire burning in her chocolate colored eyes. She moved the hand he was using to point at her stomach with the firmness of a Mother scolding an insolent child. “Look,” she began. “I know I’ve gained a little weight and it’s all your fault too!” She firmly said while wiggling a scolding finger in Mike’s face. Mike tried his best not laugh uncontrollably at her accusations, she was just like the other women he had been with; quick to blame every one of their problems on him. “What the hell do you mean it’s my fault?” Mike asked while biting his lower lip in a lame attempt to stop his laughter. “You’ll see in a minute,” Carlotta said. “But first I need to know if you’ll do me a HUGE favor.” Mike sighed, he should have expected that she had just come here looking for money. It was the very reason he had sent her packing, she was just another in a long line of gold diggers. “What kind of favor?” He asked, his demeanor suddenly changed from humorous to being serious. Carlotta didn’t say anything, instead; she turned around and motioned for someone or something that was standing out of Mike’s field of vision to come into view. Mike became startled when he saw that it was in fact three little boys, all with blond hair, and matching outfits that had the names Ben, Glen, and Ken printed on the front of their shirts. Mike suddenly gained a healthy fear of the favor he knew Carlotta was about to ask of him. “Say hello our children Mike,” Carlotta said with a proud motherly tone. Mike’s jaw dropped at the word ‘our’, there was no way he could believe this but after a moments thought it suddenly made sense and he began to curse the condom manufacturers. “What is it that you want then?” Mike cautiously asked, as he looked at the triplets that were most likely his sons. “I need you to watch them while I go to an audition,” Carlotta said. “It’s the least you could do for your sons.” She said this as she gently pushed the children past Mike and into his home. “I don’t know,” Mike began. “I’ve got a few things to do around the house today and my home isn’t exactly childproof.” “Don’t worry Mike,” Carlotta stated with a proud smile. “They’re little angels. You won’t even notice that they’re here. I’ll be back in a few hours, bye.” She said with a wave as she quickly retreated before Mike could offer any further protest. Mike slumped his shoulders and closed the door. He then felt something grab onto his leg and looked down to find one of the boys clutching onto his leg. “Are you my Daddy?” The boy asked. “I guess so.” Mike said with an unsure tone. It was a simple statement but it caused the boy to hang onto Mike even tighter. One of the other boys, whose shirt identified him as being Ken, came up to Mike and kicked him in the shin as hard as he could. “You’re not my Daddy you stupid queer!” The boy yelled at the top of his lungs before running off. A look of great annoyance formed on Mike’s face as he made a mental note to never include Ken in his will. His thoughts were interrupted by the sound of the third boy, whose name was Ben, wailing loudly as tears streamed down his face. “Why did you kick our Daddy?” Ben called after his brother as he then took up pursuit of the other boy while still crying. Before Mike could say anything to the boy, the one attached to his leg suddenly bolted off, nearly tripping Mike in the process. It was at that precise moment that Mike realized that there was no way he’d be able to watch these children by himself. He need back up and he knew the right person to call for the job. Mike reached into his pocket and produced his cellular phone, scrolling through the various numbers stored in his personal directory he finally found the one he was looking for. He pressed the call button before putting the phone to his ear. After a few seconds the familiar and sultry voice of Hillary Watson answered. “Hillary,” Mike said as tried his best not to sound desperate. “I need you to come home and help me with something.” “Okay Butter Muffin!” Was Hillary’s excited reply, it was obvious to Mike that she probably thought that he was asking her to help him with the same thing that got him into this mess in the first place. Mike quickly clicked the phone off before Hillary could talk any further, she had a tendency to talk too much. Feeling slightly relieved Mike returned his phone to his pocket. He began to wonder if he had any swim trunks that would fit the boys, perhaps letting them swim in the backyard pool under Hillary’s supervision would give him the time he needed to put the finishing touches on his new aquarium. As Mike made his way back to the living room he heard a loud crash followed by a few surprised shouts of “Look what you did!”. Mike ran to the living room and found that the triplets had knocked over his new aquarium. Mike was at a loss for words, hours of work had just gone to hell and he knew that there was no way to repair the damage done the aquarium and his living room. “How the hell did this happen?” Mike asked with barely controlled anger as he glared at the children. The three boys looked at each other and then pointed accusing fingers at each other while simultaneously proclaiming “He did it!”. Mike suddenly hoped that this wasn’t a sign of things to come. -To Be Continued-
