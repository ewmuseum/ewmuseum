Baaad Beastor!

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 [The black and frosty December night gives way to the gentle falling snow. This particular plot of land in the Muskoka Region, Ontario's cottage country, belongs to the yet-again-unretired "Hollywood North" Dallas Reeves. His rustic cottage seems rather uninhabited, the lone porch light being the only source of illumination. Soon enough, the ever-nearing headlights of some vehicle careening down the driveway serve as notice for, well, people to show up. A 1983 GMC van pulls up close to the house, and as it nears, the airbrushed work of 'art' on the side reveals itself. There stands a viking woman, clad in her underskivvies, holding the reins of two albino saber tooth tigers. From the driver seat hops a young man. The camera freezes on him, with these words appearing on the screen] FRANKIE "The Cameraman" CALRISSIAN [Wearing a red plastic visor, a poor attempt at a pencil thin mustache, reflector sunglasses and a rather large parka, Frankie sports a rather weird look on his face; a look that makes him look as though he just made out with his aunt. I assure you he didn't, it's just that during a freeze frame, you can't expect your face to look cool. Another set of headlights dawn on the driveway, and rather non-chalantly, a silver Cadillac pulls to a halt. Out steps the 54 yr old frame of one "Alberta" Jack B. Carrington. Clad in his trademark white stetson and sharkskin suit, the old-time wrestler-turned real estate mogul-turned wrestling manager chomps at his rather expensice cigar. The camera freezes on him, painting a more favourable view than that of Frankie.] "Alberta" JACK B. CARRINGTON [The two congregate on the back porch of the cottage, exchanging solemn hugs. There they wait the arrival of, well, I'm guessing of who is the owner of the next set of headlights coming down the driveway. As this last vehicle draws near, Frankie and Carrington straighten themselves out a little, in hopes of looking their best. A purple 1969 Charger slides to a halt beside the house. The chassis heaves as the car idles, and then the driver kills the engine. He hops out and slides across the hood, Hazzard-style, and the camera freezes on him.] "Hollywood North" DALLAS REEVES [The scene kicks back into regular time. Frankie stares in disbelief.] Frankie: Man, that freeze frame probably looked way better than mine. Dallas: I think that goes without saying, Frankie. [Frankie has long been the official cameraman to Dallas, so much so that Frankie has exchanged his technical services for wrestler training. Mind you, he is only 5'11" 178 lbs, but he's the first to tell you he's 178 pounds of Pure Fury. Frankie looks at the camera.] Frankie: Damn straight. [He flexes a paltry bicep, before the scene is overtaken by Reeves, who is shaking the hand of his long-time family friend and some-time manager, Carrington.] Dallas: So, J.B., GTT5 already. What's it been, a year? [The rough and tumble Albertan hauls on his cigar] Carrington: Yeah, it's a yeah-ly event. What Ah wanna know is…ah ya see-us about retuh-nin' ta rasslin'? Ah mean, ya got tha ring rust an' all. Dallas: Oh J.B., there's no need for you to worry your pretty weird little head about. See, I'm all covered. Walk with me. [Reeves begins to walk towards the back door of his cottage, across the as yet unshovelled mess of his back porch. Carrington follows, as does Frankie.] Dallas: I got a hold of you, secured your services if you will, because I know that if it's one thing I need, it's guidance. Carrington: Ya don't have ta buttah me up. Ah'm alreadeh he-a. Dallas: Okay. So my next logical step would be to get my prodigy…well, my student at least…and personal documenter, Frankie back with me. And I know all he'd be doing is playing video games. [Frankie pipes up from the back of the line] Frankie: Level 29 on Jezzball! Carrington: Need Ah ask what ya thoughts on his attendin' all those HATE matches? [When Dallas Reeves left the NGW, Frankie opted to say, and test out the solo scene. He was beaten often and gratuitously. He hooked up with Scott W., who then began to teach Frankie his own version of wrestling in his home fed of HATE.] Dallas: All I gotta say is, watchin' Woodson wrestle is like watchin' some fool rubbing his belly and trying to chew off his own ear at the same time. If I can get Frankie away from that, I'm already ahead in the game. [Reeves has his hand on the door to his cottage. He opens it wide and holds it for his two guests.] Dallas: So Frankie my sidekick and J.B. my manager…all that leaves is my bodyguard, Beastor Brandt. [The camera cuts to a close up of a heavily bearded man, who's black hair would give his facial hair a run for it's money were it to challenge it to a dishevelled contest. Standing 6 feet 8 inches and tipping the scales at nearly three hundred and forty pounds, the Beastor has proven himself a formidable ally. His toughness, forged in the heyday of the 80s, was of near epic proportions, as were his appetite and body odour. Beastor had stood beside Dallas through thick and thin.] Dallas: I'm just glad that after all this time, we can all get back together, working towards a common goal…me winning the GTT5. Or making it into Round 2, at least. [The trio enter the cottage, which is decorated in older furniture, the type that you'd probably throw out had you not a perfectly good other house on a lake to send it to. To the shock and chagrin of Reeves, there are papers strewn about, puddles of an unknown liquid…presumably water…and the sofas are chewed.] Dallas: Beastor! [From the back of the room steps the Beastor, looking guilty as all hell. It seems that the returning Dallas Reeves may be in need of a new bodyguard. Trick is, he has to see Round Two first.]
