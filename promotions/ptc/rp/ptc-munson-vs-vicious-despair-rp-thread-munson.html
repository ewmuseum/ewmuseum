Munson VS Vicious Despair RP Thread

10 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THUMB Oh, what a waste of a beautiful thing, What chaos and havoc have scarred it! But in the end the truth shall finally ring, To those who would dare be near it! Vicious Despair, is trapped in this place, With little freedom to roam And locked away he shall stay forever alone Because this time the fight is for home And no one will hear his cry! Or wonder why he fails flight, Shadows will protrude his ghastly dearth In all but a whisper for the mind of mirth! No one will notice the empty nest, No one ever knew his tender breath, He has not even been born. And now comes… death! (Ashen grey clouds float, smothering the sky in their size. The weather is gloomy on this day, almost sepulchral. Raindrops are not yet about, but their presence still looms ominously over the cleanly paved, abandoned roadway. There is nothing but a mass of dirt and weeds on either side of the road, except for one man; Munson. There is a shadow outlining a thumb pointing westward reflected off the pavement.) Munson- “This ere thumb is sure well rested ain’t it? I can stretch all sortsa ways, crack it if I want, I can use the darn thang fer juss bout everything a thumb was designed fer I reckon. But it sure wouldn’t be that way without Core Wrasslin. That’s right, cW. It’s cuz of them that I got myself a spankin new Dodge Durango, scarlet as a tub a blood. It’s cuz of Core that I ain’t gots to stand out on the streets all day with my thumb out like this tryin to hitchhike my way out to the next Bingo Hall so I can wrassle fer minimum wage. I ain’t gots to wrassle in bingo halls no more at all cuz a Core ya see. My thumb works and is full healthy, cuz I’m employed by Core Wrasslin.” (The outline of the thumb and fades out, and a fade in occurs to a masked man. A man in a jet black mask covering all of the facial features above his cheekbones. Armor-like spikes protrude out the top of the mask, they shine clean as a thistle, mostly due to the fact that behind this man’s head is the scorching hot sun, burning down behind the man’s head making it difficult to even look directly into the man’s eyes. This is Munson.) Munson- “I gone got me all them thangs I ever wanted, cuz of cW, and now there’s some sonnyjim runnin his mouth off bout me by the name a Vicious Despair. Some fooly little wooly’s out talking bout me and next thang I know I’m booked in a match with him. There sure is some interesting stipulations gone been added to this match, and I spose I’ll be gittin to those later. But the most important thang there is to know, is that what it says right on the match cards is “Munson, from Core.” Everythangs cW’s gone given to me, and I wouldn’t say I ain’t given nothing in return. I’ve bled pints for them, specially in that Taipei Deathmatch they booked me in down at the payperview Second Chances. But ya see, sad as it is, I ain’t so sure blood is zactly common currency round these parts. Despite all the blood, the sweat and all them tears I shed fer Core in the time I been there I ain’t really dun much to pay em back. Well now comes long my chance. Me and Vicious Despair, wit me repuzentin the place that I gone callin home, Core Wrasslin, this ere’s my big ole chance to repurzent and help promote the name of the place that keeps my thumbs workin. And ain’t no one gone stop me from doin that, ain’t no one gonna beat me or something like gat. Cuz I’ve given my blood and everything I had in me fer Core, and it wasn’t enough. So now in this ere match, I’m gone be givin even more than that. Until my legs collapse out from under me and I can’t operate my own limbs or work my own parts, I’m gone be fightin. And by the looks of things, I reckon I’ll have to do just that.” (His face fades out, and switches into the most devastating weapon possessed by Munson the Montanian Madman; his fists.) Munson- “This match as I understand it, can only be won by complete knockout of yur opponent. And as if that ain’t brutal enough, ya ain’t allowed to be usin no weapons in the match. That’s the part et just puts me o’er the top right there. Fer my whole career I been hearin wrasslin announcers sayin they ain’t never seen no punch as hard as mine. Hell, some the matches I won I ain’t never even hit the Munslam, I juss gone and used my fists. Spose I’m a bit over weight, maybe a wee bit not agile enough fer barekuckle boxin, but I reckon if the money or the passion ever slips out from under me in this business I could make myself a killin in that sport. This ere no weapons stipulation is purely to my advantage. Knockin some sonnyjim out ain’t gone be none to tough fer Ole Munson.” (The chubby, yet devastating fists of Munson no longer become the focus as a fade in is done to a small dandelion on the side of the road. Not a yellow dandelion or a cheerful one, a dead withered grey dandelion, consisting of only a bit of dust and a stem.) Munson- “But as much as this match is all bout Core Wrasslin, it’s all bout my ability to knock the livin bejesus outta any and all sonnyjims there sure is one sonnyjim that clearly gots to git himself a bit of my attention here. Vicious Despair. That’s my opponent in this ere match, I juss gone went walkin through the PTC building mindin my own business and bein a good Montanian, and this boy comes up to me and starts gittin all up in my face. I spose it’s all ya can really expect though from a jimbob just startin out. Heck I was juss like it myself when I started out in the business, I’d go after anyone around me and do everytang I could to juss cause some ripples in the scene. That’s what this Vicious kid’s tryin to do, it’s what ya have to do. But ya see he picked the wrong Montanian to mess with, I only been workin this wrasslin industry nine months of my life. Sure, bein 34 years old and all I spose it would be expected that I’d a been workin a whole lot longer, but it just ain’t the case. I spent most of my adult life just widdle waddling through tryin to find where I was goin and what I was lookin fer. And I found it inside the squared circle. But when a rookie like Despair is messin with someone like me, who’s basically juss gittin outta bein a rookie I still remember what it’s like to be in his position. It’s still fresh in my mind bout the nerves and jitters of havin a first match. My first match was a cage match, I went in and broke my tibia bone in that match. Coulda been it right there fer me. And now this Vicious Despair feller, he’s sure got my respect fer debutin in a match even more dangerous than a cage. And gone up against me nonetheless. But this boy’s a rookie, and accordin to an interview he done with PTC he seems to be convinced that it bein his first match is a huge advantage fer him. This boy thinks that it’s great news for him, cuz I ain’t never seen him wrassle before. But Vicious my boy, you gone out and thinking your rookieness is a good thang fer you shows just how much a rookie you is. Look at pros and cons, yessireebob you do have yourself that one pro that I ain’t seen yur work before. But ya also got the con, that you ain’t never been in the ring. Wrasslin school ain’t exactly the same thing as this I reckon. Ya got the con that yur drowned in yur own ego. I ain’t even sure how ya git an ego when ya ain’t wrassled a match yit, but you seem convinced that after watchin my work that someone ‘as impressive as yurself’ won’t have no problem takin me out. Who told ya you was impressive? Yur wrasslin coach? Boy that’s meaningless. Maybe ya think yur so darn spashell cuz you got yourself a contract with LOW. And I ain’t got no problems with LOW, nosireebob, they a great organization with some rocket sized talent, but just cuz yur IN the LOW, don’t mean you’re great. Gittin IN, mean you have the CHANCE to be impressive. The CHANCE boy, just like you got yourself the CHANCE to beat me next Sunday. Munson Knows you got a chance Vicious. Munson Knows! Ya seem to be convinced that I’mma underestimate ya. That I ain’t gone take ya serious. Is that how yur gonna go into this match? Are ya gonna go in expectin to win simply off the fact that I ain’t gone take ya serious. Boy, I don’t underestimate a bumblebee on the brown side of tree bark and of course I don’t underestimate ya neither. Yur issue seems to be yur comin into this one thinking yur gone win cuz of my mistakes, not cuz of yur own skill. That ain’t the right mindset boy.” (As the clouds get darker the view shifts from the dead dandelion to the stormy looking clouds.) Munson- “Look up at them clouds Vicious, that’s where yur head is ain’t it? Juss look at em, them clouds is grey as concrete ain’t they? They’re like time mines, just waitin, just bout to explode with all sortsa ferocity and power. Thas what ya say it’s like fer you too, all the problems ya had this week I’m gone feel the wrath of? Boy it ain’t my fault that yur havin whatever problems yur havin now is it. And when these clouds explode it ain’t nothing special, it’s just rain. Raindrops never harmed nobody, juss like yer ‘wrath.’ Yur an angry feller, I mean ya have to be to call yourself Vicious Despair I’d imagine, but ya ain’t got yourself no harness or control o’er that anger. Yur attitude and the way ya act ain’t smart right now boy, take it from someone’s who’s been through the same type a deal before and learn from it.” (As the clouds unleash a tenacious clap of thunder it fades out, and once again back in to the Montanian Madman, Munson. Not just his earth-shattering fists this time, or his head, his whole body this time. He’s a rather plump man at 324 pounds, somewhat of a beerbelly it would seem. He’s dressed in his usual wrestling attire for some odd reason, a scarlet red spandex shirt and black tights, along with of course his spiked mask.) Munson- “Well now I gone talked about my opponent Vicious Despair, but sometimes yur opponent ain’t always the most important thang in the world I reckon. It ain’t best to look inside yur opponent, and study him from head to toe, I feel it’s more important to study yourself. Munson Knows! So who am I? Who is Munson the Montanian Madman? And that don’t mean talking me bein born out in Billings, Montana, or my childhood, or how I got started wrasslin. It don’t even mean talking bout my farm and all my lil animals. It means talking bout who I am as a person. Naw, not even who I am as a person, who I am as a wrassler. Henry Thurman and Munson is two entirely different folk ya see. As a person, I’m a jovial, hunkey dorey type a guy. I like foosball games, bearclaws for supper, lunch and breakfast, and of course a lil bit a archery out in the country too. But when I git myself in that ring it’s like I’m a completely different sonnyjim almost, I git more aggressive, more bloodthirsty. I see a weakness and it ain’t even take me a thought to attack it and work on it til the cats come yippin home. I hear my own bones achin and it’s like the high school marchin band or something. I hear my opponent’s bones achin and it’s even better, like a symphony or something. This match, win by knockout only, is the ideal match fer a sonnyjim like me. And with the name of Core Wrasslin on my back there ain’t no way I’mma let this one slip away without the fight of my life. Core’s been a place with not a whole darn lotta respect round the Prime Time Central parts ya see. But them cW folk kept my thumb from achin from all the hitchhiking I woulda been doin, and now I’mma pull their name outta the dirt. This one’s fer Core, yeee doggies. Munson Knows!” (The focus switches off Munson and back onto the thundering clouds, that are nearly black now. Cacophonous thunder fulminates in the sky as everything quietly and slowly fades to black. Fades to absolute nothing, just like either Munson or Vicious Despair will inevitably do next Sunday on BDW’s Sunday Night Static.)
