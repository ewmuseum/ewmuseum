Birth of Malformation

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Birth of Malformation It didn’t take long for the man to flea, for a gun was pointed at him. Michael stood at his doorway, holding a magnum while he watched as the masked man ran for dear life. When the man could no longer be seen, Michael lowered the gun and closed his door. This wasn’t the first time it had happened. On numerous occasions Michael found himself centering the crosshairs on one’s head, but he never had the wits to shoot. To many people, Michael didn’t appear very thoughtful nor would one presume that he had a bit of decency in him. He grew tired of quoting himself over and over again with the words, ‘don’t let the look’s fool you,’ so he just stopped caring what people thought. With his dense black hair ceiling his eyes from the world and his usual five o’clock shadow shading in, not many would see this man as kind. It didn’t bother Michael, though; he enjoyed his long dark hair and the facial hair that never seemed to change. He wouldn’t change for anyone but himself and as he lacked any sort of physical discomfort he had no reason, he simply liked who he was. As he latched the door shut, a shadow appeared in front of him illustrated on the door. Michael turned his head and faced a lanky yet lofty man. His blond hair was parted down the middle, revealing a set of bright blue eyes. His blank expression sprouted into a peculiar grin as he eyeballed the gun which was held firmly in Michael’s right hand. "Had to scare them off again, eh?" "To hell with them," Michael announced with a bit of anger still locked inside. Marcus nodded his head and didn’t speak of the matter again, for it was not unusual now days. Michael smirked and stretched on further into the house, stopping at a table which was positioned in front of the television. Michael plopped down onto the coach and as he sunk deep in comfort, Marcus beamed down at Michael. "What now," Michael asked, paying no attention to Marcus as he glanced at the TV which sparked to life with the push of a button. Marcus did not acknowledge Michael, but simply parked himself right next to Michael and watched on. Michael turned his attention to Marcus and looked directly at him. The dialogue drifted into his audio range but there was no picture for he still kept his attention on Marcus. "Marcus, why is it that people always try and break in, why am I so hated?" The expression on Marcus’s face narrated a bitter story. Michael was not ignorant but he did not know much about his past. He thought he knew it all, but Marcus, a childhood friend would provide Michael with the truth. The words simply couldn’t just poor out of his mouth, though. It would take much more, Marcus needed strength himself to answer and Michael would have to brace him self for a mental head on collision. The hair dangled in front of his eyes, covering a secret that not even Michael knew about. "They’re— black," Marcus protested with emotions running wild. It didn’t seem like such a big deal, but to Marcus it was. The people Michael had to deal with, all of the tormenting and never once did he stop and realize the real eyes that had put such a burden on him. Michael exhaled and parted his hair; Marcus turned his head in disgust. As he watched this, he rose from the coach and slowly crept over to a mirror that hung upon the wall. His reflection had no effect, he still didn’t see them. He had grown accustom to them and the instant he stopped caring about people’s opinions was the day he became stupefied. Those dreary black eyes could send one into shock, it just wasn’t right. After all of those years Michael was left stranded on his manor, with the only friend he had. Marcus placed his had on Michael’s shoulder and began to comfort him with words. "It’s okay though, Michael. To hell with all of the others, what matters is the one thing you have now. Wrestling and you’re going to make a name for yourself at GTT5 when you defeat Killean Sirrajin." "Marcus, what is wrong with my eyes? They define who I am and like I’ve stated in the past, I could care less about people’s opinions. Don’t make this something it is not, I am fine Marcus. And just for the record, I enjoy scaring those bastards off with my revolver. Just you wait and see, one will get what’s coming to them." "They can be frightening, Michael. Your eyes are vacant of white; black consumes them with and plagues those who look within them. People see evil in you Michael, pure evil." "That is nonsense and you know it! I can’t help what god gave me; I can only bear with him and accept who I am. Why can’t you accept me and these beautiful eyes I was granted with?" "I have Michael, but it is not me who does not accept. You must realize what the others think, how can one fathom a shade of black that colors in all light visible. There is no light in you, at least to them." Michael shook his head and peered over at Marcus with a look of despise. "You make me sick, did you know that?" The comment was directed at Marcus, though he tried to explain that he had no grudge with Michael. There was no hate in Marcus, but Michael could bear no more and carried on into his bedroom. SLAM The door sent vibrations throughout the house as it was slammed shut. Michael was raging and he needed to release it. But on who, it then came to him like that! Killean Sirrajin Who is this man who claims to be a risen star? I have heard little about him, but from what I have heard he is nothing. He is respected among those in PRIME, but not I. For all I know he looks down upon me. These godforsaken black eyes have cursed me! I will have my revenge though. For all of those who look at me with disgust can go to hell! I am sure that Killean will have no problem making a joke of me. I hope he does, because I will have my way with him. There is no doubt that this match will be an upset. Michael rests himself by lying down in his bed. He looks up with pride, for he knows his own strength. The thoughts still pile upstairs, what good have these eyes brought? Have these eyes served me any good? "What in the hell," The man stumbled backwards but was stopped as he backed into the wall. Michael stood where he was and eyeballed the man. The man’s actions were misleading and left Michael wondering. "Don’t come any closer," He protested with fear, his eyes locked onto those of Michael’s. They seemed to put the man in a trace; he fluttered around as he tried to keep his desistance while Michael still held his position, watching this mans state of hysteria. Michael was only a young boy, and this man was so easily frightened and poor Michael had no idea. The black hair which was tangled and knotted sprung out in every which way. The bit of chin hairs hung freakishly long off of his chin. His bold black eyes completed the boys’ grimace, which poured out a cast of evil to all who saw them. "What’s wrong," Michael shouted with fear begging to grow in him as he watched the man in horror. He tried not to let it get to him, but his emotions were swept away with tears and fear was the only sign of life left. His hands trembled as he rushed to whip his tears and nose drippings from his face. "Get away, get away from me," The man yelped as he slid down the wall, positioning into a ball to protect himself from Michael. "Dad, what’s wrong?" "Get away!" Those were Michael’s last memories of his childhood. Michael jumped out from the bed and opened his door swiftly. He glanced out into the living room and saw that Marcus was still sitting on the coach. The television was not on, meaning he was sitting alone to think. Michael made his way into the room, and looked down at Marcus and sighed. "Look, I’m sorry, I didn’t mean to go off at you, but I couldn’t take it anymore. I just realized something though, you were right." This got the attention of Marcus who jerked his head to the direction of Michael and smiled. "Really," He softly spoke, still with a look of integument. Michael nodded his head and took a seat next to Marcus on the coach. For a brief moment, Michael didn’t responded, instead he thought about something. "You know what, I think I can win." He continued. "I also thought about the one event that scared me. My very own father could not look at me with out squealing. I was the demise of my own family, because of the way I looked." Marcus squinted and a rather unusual expression centered on his face. "Don’t be silly, you know damn well that it wasn’t your fault. Your family broke up when your father committed suicide, and—" Michael suddenly cut him off with a jumble of words. "That was my fault too." "Come again," Marcus asked as he challenged Michael’s own words. But before Michael could elaborate, Marcus threw a fit. "Have you gone mad, Michael? Stop it now; I can’t stand to hear you talk about yourself in this manner. Curse me for putting these thoughts into your head!" Michael shook his head with rage and pounded his fist into the table, causing Marcus to flinch in surprise. "No Marcus, you were right. People are afraid of me, but it doesn’t seem to hurt as bad now. I have accepted the fact that my father hung himself due to my presence; I was the one who sent him over the edge. It will always be my fault, but I can’t let it haunt me." Marcus covered his face with his own hands as he listened to Michael’s nonsense. "Just stop it," Marcus shouted, and it definitely did stop Michael from speaking the matter any more. "No, Marcus, I will not stop." "I can’t listen to this anymore; you’re turning this into something much worse than it really is. Can’t you just talk about what is occurring now in your life? What about Killean Sirrajin, how do you feel about your match in GTT5?" Marcus wanted the subject changed and hopped that Michael would not continue with his rambling. GTT5 was more important than the past; Marcus just prayed he would answer. "Why must you change the subject like that, and rudely cut me of while I was speaking? But if you must know, I will tell you. I speak for myself when I say that I have no respect for the man. Marcus, you must realize that people like Killean have not seen what I have. The passing away of his wife doesn’t compare to the death of my father. He never had to endure my pain; I had no one to turn to but you my friend. I have no use but to fight, and that is what I shall do." Marcus smirked and put his arm around Michael. "You see what you did; you put a smile on my face. I can’t stand it when you talk about yourself like that. Your eyes are brilliant and people just aren’t use to such beauty." Michael laughed, and looked at Marcus strangely. "You’re calling these eyes beautiful, I’m sorry but that put a smile on my face." The two men began to chuckle as they started to rough around with each other. End
