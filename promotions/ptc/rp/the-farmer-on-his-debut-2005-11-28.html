The Farmer on: His Debut

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 "I got the phone call two weeks before the show was set to happen. I was accepted and I was going to be apart of the federation. I arrived at the arena a bag of nerves but had to keep out of peoples way so as to not ruin the surprise. The secrecy helped me with my nerves and I was able to calm down before my big entrance. "… When I threw that caravan door open and saw the crowd for the first time I knew I was apart of something special…" AWC Solarized 1st July 2005 One rung from the top… and a blur of motion. The caravan door flies open and a tall man bursts out, throwing himself into the ring and at the ladder, which he superkicks down with authority! Krimzon panics as the mat rushes at his face, and the next thing he knows, he’s hit the canvas with crushing force. "Paddy and Crimson had told me what they wanted from me when I got involved and I just went about it. It was all about evening the odds and making sure they won the Alliance Title Belts…" Truth Waters: WHO IS THIS?! George Cassidy: I have no idea, but he’s just knocked the ladder down and given Krimzon a concussion and possibly some broken bones in the process – AND HE’S COST THE EDUCATOR THE MATCH! Expel him! Truth Waters: So that is who was driving the caravan – he must be in league with Emerald Isle! "Going toe to toe with The Educator and knowing that the fans were behind ME felt unreal. I had finally made it as a wrestler. Granted I had to lie about being Irish and put on a fake accent but I had finally made it. The Irishness AWC could forgive me for later." Eyes blazing, The Educator approaches the new arrival with vicious intent. Whatever was on his mind has to be forgotten, though, as the man from the caravan evades his attack and slings him into the ropes, knocking him down on the rebound with a brutal clothesline! Truth Waters: CLOTHESLINE FROM HELL! George Cassidy: Other-worldly power in that right arm! "And then to connect with The Harvest on him seconds later I knew my debut had went perfectly. Standing over his limp body with the fans cheering it felt like I had arrived. " Truth Waters: Wait… I know who this is, Cassidy! It’s none other than "The Farmer" Mickey Fitz-McCarthy! "To help my two ‘countrymen’ become the first AWC Alliance Title Belts was the kind of debut that dreams are made of. I was the guy that tipped the balance in favour of Emerald Isle and Eire Og and to this date there-slash-we are still the Champions. I had finally been given a chance on professional wrestling and I intended on taking it." Quoted from AWC Solarized http://awc.supremepixels.com/ppvs/solarized2.php
