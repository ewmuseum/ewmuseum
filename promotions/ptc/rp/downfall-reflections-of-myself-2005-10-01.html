Downfall: Reflections of Myself

1 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-10-01 "Dawn slid the back door to the estate closed and stepped out into the unseasonably cool air, drawing in a deep breath thought her nose. She'd always enjoyed the cool dryness of autumn, and the temporary cold snap provided by erratic weather conditions was a welcome change from the usually gradual cooling off period that came after summer. "A sudden gust of wind swirled her long black duster around her legs, temporarily hugging the oilskin coat close around her like a blanket. Dawn snuggled into the heavy fabric as it gusted around her, relishing the familiar smell and sensation of her favorite piece of clothing. She hadn't worn the coat for months, both because of the intense summer heat and because she had been desperately seeking a way to reinvent herself inside the ring. But the onset of the suddenly cold winds sent her back to the familiar coat, like returning to a spurned lover only to find her waiting patiently for you. "Thoughts of spurned loved only served to bring Lia's slim face to mind, those wickedly beautiful green eyes sparkling in Dawn's mind like precious gems taken up from the earth itself. The words of Lia's farewell message still hung clearly in Dawn's mind, seemingly burned into the cells of her eyes and her memory, where she could just call it up with a thought. She squeezed her eyes tightly shut, trying to block out those hateful letters the same way she could block out the rays of the late morning sun. "Dammit..." She whispered, her hands clenching around the varnished wood railing of her deck. "Why couldn't you just talk to me?" "The answer was plain enough for her to see, but she didn't want to admit the truth, didn't want to remember how strained things had been between them when Lia had first come to the estate. "â€˜You pushed her away.' Came a small voice from the back of her mind. â€˜And when the truth came out for the first time, you ran away too. Can you really begrudge her a little alone time to sort herself out?' "A deep sigh gusted its way up out of Dawn's chest and she let her head droop forward, her chin resting awkwardly against her breastbone. For a long moment she studied the darkened grains of her deck, trying to lose herself in the myriad of patterns and lines. She sighed again, moving away from the railing to an empty portion of the deck, settling herself down onto the cold wood and tucking her legs up under her. Resting her hands loosely in her lap, Dawn took a deep breath and let her eyes slip shut. A cold wind rushed across the deck, flapping the parts of her coat not pinned under her long legs and rushing her expelled breath off to only it knew where. "Another deep breath in through her nose, and then expelled through her mouth...but focus wouldn't come, and seemed as though it would actively fight against her attempts to bring it to the fore. Unwanted thoughts and feeling clustered in her mind, refusing to be pushed aside or stepped over as they jostled for a position ahead of the others. "She had failed. Again and again failure had visited her, bringing with it the bitter sting of self recrimination and the knowledge that she let people down again. "Her Television title was gone, lost on only her second defense to a man she thought she could have beaten with her eyes closed. A simple roll up from that painted fool had spelled the end of her extremely brief TV title reign and had planted the first seeds of doubt about winning the 2005 Mistress of the Mat. "Her World title shot was also gone, botched to an overly caffeinated girl who wasn't even half Dawn's size. She could still feel the sting of Mindy's Sugar Induced Nightmare on the point of her chin, the unwelcome feeling of numbness in her extremities and the detached part of her mind that screamed at her to just GET UP as somewhere in the distance a three count spelled the end of her ambitions. "And Lia...the thought pushed a small moan from between Dawn's lips before she could stop it, her throat clamping down around a strangled sob before it could follow. Lia was the crack in her armour Dawn never thought she would find, a love that was "forbidden" in so many ways it made Dawn's head spin. But when they had been together...for that one blissful morning, everything was right in Dawn's world...everything was where it should be, and she was finally being truthful about her feelings for the woman she loved. "And now she was gone. "Dawn's eyes snapped open and she slapped one hand down against the deck in anger and frustration, growling as she pushed herself back to her feet. "I need to get out of here, even if only for a while." Meditation was usually her escape, the way in which she found a presence of mind to handle the things life threw at her...but not today. Today all she could bring to mind was the myriad of ways she had failed and failed again, the bitter taste in her mouth would not go away so easily. "She stalked back into the house, slamming the porch door closed behind her. Booted feet thumped against the tile of the kitchen floor and then the hardwood of the hallway as she headed to the garage, snatching her car keys off the key rack as she went. "Where are you going?" "Alex's voice paused Dawn at the door, her hand halfway through turning the knob. She looked back over her shoulder with an amused smirk on her face, one corner of her lips turning up in that familiar half smile. "Out. What are you now, my mother?" "In the hallway to the kitchen Alex scowled, folding her arms across her chest and glaring at the Steel Rose. "No...but Virgil said we shouldn't go chasing after Lia all half cocked." "I know what Virgil said," Dawn snapped in response, startling Alex's eyes wide. "But I can't just sit here and do nothing; I'm going stir crazy in here. So I'm going out for a bit, I'll be back by tonight." " "Dawn turned back to the door, yanking it open...before pausing again as a small hand came to rest on her upper arm. "Can I come? Please?" Alex's voice was small but insistent, reflecting the same need to get out of the Estate that Dawn herself was feeling. "Alex...I don't really want any company..." "I won't be in the way! We used to go out together all the time right? Grocery shopping and stuff? It'll be like that again." "Dawn couldn't keep the sneer from her face as she looked back her guest, a mixture of anger and hurt brewing beneath her breast. "You know, I'm not the one who suddenly started acting weird around you." "Alex flinched back as though shoved, taking her hand from Dawn's arm and looking away. "I'm sorry okay, but what did you expect from me? You never said anything about it before then, how was I supposed to know?" "I didn't even know myself." Dawn's response was short and clipped as she stepped through the door and into the garage. "But I guess I was expecting something different from you." "I said I was sorry, what else do you want from me?" Alex's voice was pleading. "I dunno...support maybe." Slamming the door behind her, Dawn took the steps down into the garage with one leap, landing lightly on her feet and striding towards the only vehicle still in the garage...her midnight blue Corvette Coupe. She tucked the sweeping end of her black duster around her long legs as she slid into the driver's seat, reaching over her shoulder for the seatbelt. "The passenger side door opened and closed again quickly, snapping Dawn's head around to see Alex buckling herself in. She refused to return Dawn's angry gaze, instead focusing on getting her seatbelt secure. "We need to talk about this you know, leaving it to fester won't do anything at all." "Dawn glared for a moment longer before turning back to the steering wheel, wrapping her hands around it as thought she could strangle the life out of it. "Why do we have to talk about it *now*?" "Because I don't feel like being left alone in the house since Virgil is one hundred percent absorbed in trying to find Lia, and Cyrus spends most of the day sleeping anyway. And neither of them are terribly great company to being with." "Fine." Dawn practically growled the response, the Corvette's engine growling in sympathy as she revved the engine and guided the sleek blue vehicle out into the driveway. As she turned onto the highway her foot tromped down on the accelerator and the Corvette took off, wheels squealing. "Do you HAVE to go so fast?!" Alex demanded, clinging to the "oh shit" handle. "I LIKE speed, I would have thought you'd have figured that out by now." "Oh I figured it out just fine when I was peeling you up off the pavement with a goddamn spatula!" Alex winced as the words came out before she thought about them. Since the accident Dawn had been a little easier to live with, but that didn't mean Alex wanted to go rubbing that night in her face. "Dawn eased her foot back off the accelerator, settling the car into a more comfortable pace, though still not taking her eyes off the road. "Yeah well...thanks." "Alex blinked in confusion, looking from the road back to Dawn. "What? Thanks for what?" "Dragging my ass back to the house afterwards, I'd have never made it back on my own." Dawn was studiously avoiding Alex's eyes, continuing to focus exclusively on the road. "Uhm...no problem. I wasn't gonna just leave you lying there." Alex watched the side of Dawn's face for a moment, drawing up a little courage inside of herself. "I don't think you're a freak or anything you know." "Dawn snorted, a slight pinkish tinge creeping into her cheeks. "You could have fooled me with the way you're tip toeing around me. It doesn't mean I'm going to toss you down in the hall and rape you y'know." "I know! Gods, just because I'm a little uncomfortable about it doesn't mean I think you're less of a person Dawn! Jesus." "I don't understand why this changes anything, it's not like I'm in love with YOU." "Alex sighed, slumping back against the chair. "No you're not, but you COULD be right? That just makes things kind of weird." "That's retarded. Do you walk on eggshells around every single straight man you know juts because he COULD fall in love with you at some point?" A hint of bitterness had crept into Dawn's voice and she looked at Alex sidelong from the corner of her eye. "Well no..." Alex sputtered, "But I also wouldn't trust a lot of straight men I know with the kind of stuff I'd do with my girlfriends...er, you know what I mean." "Dawn smirked. "Yeah I know what you mean; you don't have to explain "girlfriends" to me." "It just feels a little...strange that's all. And not just because you like women, I'm not totally sheltered you know, the concept isn't as foreign to me as you think." "No? Then why the sudden discomfort huh? When you first came to the estate you spent most of your waking hours hanging out with me, and now I can barely find you when I'm looking around my own goddamn house." "Well...Lia and I havn't always been friends you know, it's not just that fact that you like women, it's the woman you've chosen to like." Alex stared at the glovebox, playing absently with one fingernail. "How so?" "Well...I don't know how much Lia's told you about me and what I used to do but...well...my group and I tried to kill her once, her and Virgil." "You WHAT?!" Dawn's head snapped to the side and she stared at Alex incredulously, emerald eyes wide in shock. "I used to be a hunter...then they turned on me and I went looking for Lia. So I guess it's kind of hard for me to accept that anyone human can love a vampire like her." "Dawn stared at Alex for a moment before turning her eyes back to the road. "Like I told you before, it's not the vampire I fell in love with. She wasn't always like that right? People aren't just born with a sudden and permanent case of Vampirism...I fell in love with the woman underneath all that." "So if you love her so much how come you never tell her?" "Dawn hands tightened on the steering wheel, the rubber and leather creaking in protest under her strong hands. "I don't know...it never seems to be the right time or the right place...and now she's gone." "Alex reached out a tentative hand and rested it gently on Dawn's upper arm again. "She's coming back you know, she said she would." "I know, but I'm afraid things will be different when she does get back. I think I've blown my chance to be really open and honest with her...failed again." Dawn stared moodily out at the road ahead. "But...if she's coming back, what makes you think you're not going to have another chance?" "Dawn didn't reply, thoughts of her perceived failures still jostling for position inside her already very crowded mind. Alex waited a long moment for an answer, before deciding that perhaps it was better to simply let the matter drop and approach it again later. "******************************************* "We HAVE to go in here Dawn; I want to try on that skirt." Alex beamed over at her companion, indicating a long purple skirt with a silvery spider web pattern. "Besides, this is the kind of stuff you like to wear too right? It'll be fun." "'Gother then Thou' isn't exactly the kind of place I usually do my shopping. Besides, I thought we were doing window shopping, not actual shopping." "Alex narrowed her eyes in frustration. "Look, maybe YOU want to wander around downtown Toronto under a black cloud all day, but I'd like to actually DO something while we're out of the house. Would it kill you to come in here for a few moments while I try something on?" "Dawn sighed explosively, turning back toward Alex and heading to the front door. "Fine, but don't be long." "Alex grinned and followed Dawn inside, quickly heading to the front counter to ask about her beloved skirt. Dawn took the opportunity to slip between the rows of clothing, using the already gloomy atmosphere of the store to hide from any potential salespeople. She flipped aimlessly through the rows of clothing, turning up her nose at the wide array of overly "goth" clothing she found. "As she passed a series of trashy and classless t-shirts, Dawn paused as she came across an article that seemed out of place, pulling it from the rack to get a better view. It was a black bustier with matching skirt, but what had caught Dawn's eye was the patterns snaking down the front and sides. A white blossomed rose was snuggled between where her breasts would be, and from the stem snaked long trails of green thorns that spread down the bustier and skirts, the points of some thorns bright with drops of blood. "Omigawd! You HAVE to try that on!" The sudden outburst of sound made Dawn jump nearly out of her skin, rounding in surprise to see a young girl in an employee's shirt standing before her. Heavy black mascara had been caked at the corners of her eyes and she beamed with unbridled enthusiasm at the Steel Rose. "That would look SO good on you!" She gushed as she hustled Dawn towards the dressing rooms. "It's totally classy!" "But I was just looking..." Dawn tried to get out, suddenly finding herself standing before an empty dressing room. "I just gotta make sure it's not on sale or anything, so if you like it just bring it right up the counter okay? Have fun!" "Heaving an annoyed sigh Dawn stepped into the dressing room, pulling the curtain shut behind her. "It HAS been a while since I went shopping for anything new." "Hanging the bustier on the closest hanger, Dawn reached up to undo her silk choker, slipping it into the pocket of her cargo pants. She peeled her t-shirt up over her head, discarding to one corner and quickly chasing it with her cargos. She reached up for the bustier and paused, catching sight of her own pale skin in the mirror. "The scarring on her neck was minute, but to Dawn it stood out as clearly as blood on fresh snow, small gashes of sickly white in her otherwise creamy skin. She reached up to run her fingers along the rough patches, only to pause again at the red ribbon wrapped around her hand and wrist. Much like her choker concealed the scars at her throat, the ribbon concealed a host of small scars and one large one along the surface of her right wrist. She clenched her fist at the memory off it, trying to will her shoulders to stop shaking. "Dawn sat abruptly down on the small bench, unable to take her eyes from the mirror. It had been almost two years since she had stared at her nearly naked self in a full length mirror...in two years she had accumulated more scars then she had in the previous twenty one. "Two years..." She whispered, her hand at her throat once again. "Two years of failure." "She brought her hands up to her face, blocking out the sight of her scarred body in the mirror...but not from her mind.
