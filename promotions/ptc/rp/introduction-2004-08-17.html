Introduction

17 August 2004

Date: 2004-08-17 I walked out onto the dirty gravel road that led now where. There were no trees or bushes or anything in sight. There was just a road. This road was my journey. It was my journey for the PTC Survivor matches, thad had just started to take place. I was picked to be on Team EPW, which was the federation that I was in. I'm sure most of you know what the PTC Survivor match is like, but for the ones that dont know, I will tell you. Throughout PTC, there aare different federations. Ones from Team GW, to Team PRIME. The owners of those federations had picked 4 from there roster to represent them in the PTC Survivor match. There were matches made, such as Team EPW -vs- Team NEON -vs- Team UPWA. Each of the matches had a little gimmick stipulation added to it. The team that would come out on top of those matches, would move on to other matches against the other teams that beat there opponents. Why I was picked to be on EPW's team? Maybe because of my winning streak in EPW. Or maybe it was because I'm the biggest franchaise player in EPW. Whatever the reason was, I was picked, and it was for a good reason. EPW will make it past the first round and hopefully the other rounds, but to do that we need our team to give it all that they have. Suddenly a large truck started coming down the gravel road. Smoke was coming from all of the gravel that was rubbing against the truck's sides. The truck skidded to a stop right beside me. I stopped walking and lookd at the window of the truck. The window stated lowering down so I could see who was drivng this truck. It was a man, about the age of 32, with a trucker's cap on. He had a cigar in his mouth that he was taking puffs at. He blew some of the smoke out of the cigar, right into my face, and then lowered his sunglasses. The guy looked chunky from my view of him. I took the cigar out of his mouth and set it on the dashboard of his truck. He looked at me with his green eyes. By the looks of his eyes, he wanted to know why I was out here walking. "You lost?" he said to me. "Not really. Just taking a walk." I replied back. "You might not want to walk down that road because that road don't go now where. You get to a dead end, which would be a 500 feet cliff. Not sure you want to go off that. Your better taking your chances and turning around and going that way towards Chicago." "Who the hell are you to tell me where to go. Your not my mother." "Whatever. Your funeral." The guy started up his truck again and went a little ways up. Then he did a spinout and turned back around. He drove away spinning out dirt on me when he passed me. I spit all the dirt back onto the ground. That stuff wasn't made to eat. I turned around and took the guys advice. That road only led to a 500 feet cliff. I started walking. All that was going through my mind was kind of giving me a headache. I could see the skyline of Chicago. It was a very nice view. *15 Minutes Later* I had just walked into the out skirts of Chicago. The part of te town that I was at, the streets were all dirty and so were all of the buildings on the sidewalks. This must have been the junkyard of the town. Everything was rotting away and there were only 15 people walking on the sidewalk other than me. This place even smelt like shit. I started walking down the sidewalks as all of the people staring at me. I looked at there clothes and they were wearing ripped shitty clothes from the dumpster. I was wearing a white sleevless shirt with black jeans and black boots. My messy black hiar was dangling from in front of my face. I twisted my head so the hair would goto the back of my head. I walked out of that part of town and stopped holding my breath. That's how hard it stunk. There was a highway going right into Chicago. Maybe I could hitch a ride with someone and then get to the city real faster. That would be a good thing to do. I went to the side of the road and stuck my thumb out until someone came to pick my up. *Scene fades*
