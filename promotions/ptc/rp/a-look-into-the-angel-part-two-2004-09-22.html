A look into the Angel >Part Two

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 {Begin Scene} “Excuse me sir, would you like some peanuts?” The flight attendant questioned. Angel nodded, “No thank you, I’m okay.” “How about a pillow sir?” The flight attendant again asked. “No really I’m fine, thanks.” Angel implied. The flight attendant pushed the cart forward a little then quickly turned back and pulled a notepad out of her pocket. Rushing back in front of Angel the flight attendant stuck out the notepad and a pen. The flight attendant stuttered a little, “Okay I’m really sorry for bothering you so much I just really wanted your autograph!” Angel cracked a bit of a smile, “Well that’s all you had to say. I’m always open to my fan base.” Grabbing the pen and notepad Angel scratched away his Herby Hancock. “Here you go miss, glad to brighten your day up.” “Wow thanks so much!” The attendant exclaimed while grabbing the notepad back and glancing down at it. “Arch Angel? Who the hell is Arch Angel!” The flight attendant looked stumped. “What do you mean, that’s me Arch Angel, superstar wrestler!” Angel stated. The flight attendant threw the pad back at Angel as she walked away. “And I thought Colin Farrel was actually on my plane, damn!” “Wow! Isn’t that wonderful.” Angel scratched his head. “Well everyone will know who I am after I win GTT4, and that my friends is only a hop skip and a jump away from the Fallen Angel.” Angel sat his head back into the seat as he put his set of headphones on and began to enjoy the in flight movie, “Homeward Bound”. Angel’s eyes slowly come to a close as he falls into a deep state of unconsciousness. Angel’s eyes begin to roll back and forth rapidly as if he is already deep in a dream. Nothing but white is to be seen...suddenly off in the distance Angel notices something colorful approaching him as he collapses... “Holy...” said Michael, but couldn't manage the rest of the sentence. Instead, the team lowered their weapons and closed the door to catch their breath. “John, fetch the van,” said Michael. “We'll take some cash and some shit before Forensics gets here.” John nodded weakly and left. The remaining officers cleared the rest of the house and then counted the money on the table, did some quick mental arithmetic and came up with fifty thousand dollars apiece. It was almost amusing how studiously they could avoid the gazes of everybody else's eyes whilst they did so. Michael, as Homicide, was left with the daunting task of going back into the lounge. He took a deep breath and opened the door. Ragged strips of burnt flesh hung like Christmas bunting from any surface they could stick to – curtains, lampshades, armchairs and sofas, the mantelpiece, the side board. Blood had dried black onto the carpet so thickly it was impossible to see the actual pile beneath the crusted coating. The memories of screams so terrible they made Michael shudder seemed to echo from the corners, waiting for someone to evoke them, someone to hear them other than the victims and the tormentors. The stench of rot and the acrid smell of dried vomit were inescapable, pulling at whatever was inside Michael’s stomach, beckoning it to join the carnage before him. There were three bodies slumped on the furniture. One in the armchair, one on the sofa and one tied to a table. Flesh had been gouged out of there bodies; some of the wounds were black, others crusted red, suggesting that the black ones had been burned out as opposed to being cut. White enamel showed through the remnants of flesh the corpses retained. Michael could see pierced eyeballs, tongues cut out and power stapled to skulls, and the one tied to the table appeared to have been disemboweled. There were large Vs of brain and skull matter, pointing along the carpet or upholstery – depending on where each body was – to mostly intact faces resting on disintegrated skulls. Michael opened the front door and breathed relatively clean air. John was backing the van down the drive, and a crowd of yuppie neighbors had gathered – attracted, no doubt, by the sound of the door charge and the presence of the big navy blue APC. Michael held his badge aloft. “NYPD!” he shouted. “Please leave the area! The situation is completely under control!” “Under control,' he thought.” ‘That's a fucking laugh. There're three bodies in the lounge and we need Forensics to tell us which ones were male and which were female. There is a homicidal maniac on the loose and we have no idea who he is. That situation is under complete fucking control.” The rest of the SWAT team emerged from the house as John turned the engine off. They had a black hold all between them. “We put a few kilos of cocaine in there,” Michael was told. He nodded, and the bag was safely hidden inside the APC. Then, two men were posted on the front door and the other two on the back, as Michael sat in the cab of the APC with a cigarette and radioed in for a Forensic team. His task complete for the time being, he sat back and watched the rain running down the windscreen, feeling the comfortable and secure burning of cigarette smoke in his lungs, wondering obscurely if he might get a closer look at Rachel’s tits this time. He knew the answer, but at least it stopped him thinking of the lounge. John came up on the side of the APC and knocked on the window. “You okay in there Mike?” As the window slowly came down Michael answered, “I don’t think I can do this anymore bro.” “Do what?” John questioned. “THIS! John all this! Crooked cops, battered bodies, organs splattered all over the walls. I don’t think I can do any of this any more. Michael shouted. John waved his hands in a downward motion, “Lower the voice man, you trying to expose us all? We got a great thing going here. Where else can you make 50 G’s a day and barely do anything but stare at some burnt bodies every now and then?" Michael grabbed his head, “Listen to you John, those bodies are people too and that money belongs to someone else! I did not come onto the force to turn out like this, all my morals have been flipped upside down! I don’t want this anymore! John smirked a little as he replied, “You think it’s that easy Mike? You just can’t step outside the box whenever you feel like it!” “WHAT!? Michael took a step forward, “Who’s gonna stop me? You John, or any of them other flunkies?” Throwing his gun into the APC Michael began to walk away from the crime scene. “MIKE!” John shouted. “Were all you got bro, you don’t have anything else!” Michael stopped and turned around, shaking his head he reached into his jacket. Pulling out his badge Michael tossed it into the air. “I’ll manage.” Michael sighed. As the badge splashed into the puddle at John’s feet everything went blank white again then flashed black. Arch Angel came to as he whipped the headphones off his head and threw his lap tray up into place. “Man. Why is this happening? Why can’t I just let it go?” Michael thought to himself. “I have too much on my plate right now to go through this again.” DING... DING... “We will be adjusting altitude for landing so please connect your lap belts and position your trays to their upright position.” A voice came over the plane intercom. A few moments later the plane made a nice landing and Angel exited only to be surprised by GTT4 reporters and a small group of paparazzi. “Could we have a moment of your time Angel? Just a couple minutes?” A reporter asked while being hovered by a camera. Angel shrugged and replied, “Sure why not.” The reporter glanced at her pad as she shot her first question at Angel, “So with only hours to go till you step into the ring with Blaze Medina what is going through your head? What is your strategy or do you even have one?” Angel smiled, “Well Ms. Reporter lady I’d have to be pretty stupid to go into the ring with an ex-marine and XUW superstar without a strategy. Now will I discuss my strategy with you? Sorry no but I will tell you that I am well prepared believe that.” The reporter nodded as she scratched something down on her pad, “So with some comments being made by Blaze about your past injuries, will they be a problem in your round two match?” Laughingly Angel replied, “Look at me do I look like I am afraid of being injured again? That was the past and this is the present. I am in my wrestling prime and will stop at nothing to win this tournament whether it be another broken vertebrae or whatnot!” “So with all that out of the way do you have any comments for your round two opponent, Johnny “Blaze” Medina?” The reporter asked. “Just one...Hey Johnny boy, THIS PROMO BE OVER BEEOTCH!” Angel taunted as he walked away from the crowd of cameras. The scene comes to a close as Angel disappears into the airport. {End Scene}
