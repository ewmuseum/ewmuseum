Scottywood Peter Vetra Title: Through the looking glass Featuring: Jay Phoenix Date: 9th Oct 2004 "So, are you really sure that this is the best thing to do?" he asked plaintively as he looked at his reflection in the mirror. Staring back at him was someone that he finally recognised, someone who had been missing for the better part of the week. Ever since he had walked out of the ring after beating, soundly defeating, Tex Chatanooga to find out that his next opponents was also one of his best friends in the industry he had been living in denial. While his sleeping hours had been filled with dreams fuelled by anxiety; being chased by faceless, formless people, constantly running but getting nowhere, turning a corner to find himself plunging over the edge of an unseen precipice, and then falling in endless darkness until with a start he would awaken to find himself sitting upright, sweat soaked, safe in his own bed – his waking hours had been spent ensuring that his mind and body were totally consumed by anything and everything that would keep him from focussing on the fact that in order to achieve his desire of winning the fourth annual GTT he would have to face one of the few men in the wrestling business that he called friend. … not just face him, but beat him. In order to get the redemption that he sound fully believed that he needed he would have to crush another man’s dreams, and not just any man, not just any wrestler. Dave Hurst, the man called Exceptional … his friend. Secluding himself from everyone and everything that reminded him of his forthcoming match, he pushed himself physically harder than he could ever remember. By doing this he made sure that he was simply too tired to dream at night, instead passing out on his bed almost before his head hit the pillow; too exhausted to think clearly during the day. For the past five days and five nights the fact that he would be facing Exceptional Dave Hurst in the third round of the GTT had been the one factor that controlled every fibre of his being, and the one factor that he had deliberately forced himself to not think about. Just like an alcoholic taking his first ride on the wagon whose only thoughts were for the one thing in life that mattered, the one thing in life that they couldn’t have, his own thoughts kept edging back to the one thing that he tried to bury deep within his mind as if by not thinking about it then it wouldn’t happen. The days of fighting off the monster in the closest by hiding his head under the covers of his bedclothes had long since passed however, and similarly the only thing gained by forcing his mind away from facing Dave Hurst was that he nearly drove himself to a breakdown. Something that had been finally, and forcefully, pointed out to him by another of his friends … his best friend … Rick James earlier that very day. The same Rick James who now sat watching as Jay Phoenix stared into the mirror. "Of course it is the best thing to do Jay, you need a night out to relax and refresh your head" Rick replied. Looking at Rick in the reflection of the mirror, Phoenix nodded before his eyes moved slightly to the side so that once more he took in his own reflection. His long auburn hair was shining and freshly washed, hanging loose around his shoulders and trailing nearly to his waist. A tailored black Armani suit was worn over a round-necked silk black shirt that was opened at the top two buttons to show a hint of the defined chest beneath it. Bringing both hands up to the lapels Phoenix rearranged the jackets slightly as he stared into the eyes of his two dimensional twin. Yes, he definitely recognised the man staring back at him finally even if there was still a shadow of doubt in the Jade green eyes that were normally bright with humour and good grace. " … and I promise that we don’t need to talk about the match or Dave at all tonight Jay" Rick pointed out with a small smile. "There will be plenty of time for that tomorrow when we are flying back to the arena … two more days and it will all be over with anyway Rick so like you say tonight we just enjoy ourselves!" Phoenix stated firmly … trying hard to convince either Rick, or himself. "Well if you are done admiring yourself in the mirror my friend, the car is waiting downstairs to take us to the restaurant, so let’s go!" Rick stated as he got to his feet, holding the door open and giving a mock bow as Phoenix walked through it. They both walked down the stairs, chatting amiably to each other as they exited the house and clambered into the back of a waiting black limo in the driveway. As the driver got into the front himself the car started it’s journey. "Was the limo really necessary Rick, I mean I could have easily driven?" Phoenix asked as the vehicle pulled out into the main traffic. "Necessary … probably not, but like I said for this one night you are going to relax and not think about anything at all, just sit back and enjoy my friend" Rick laughed in reply. "So what is the plan for tonight then?" he replied as he took his friends advice and settled back into the leather seat. "I was thinking that we could take a walk up forty-second street to this new restaurant that I have heard about, before we …" Rick started and then abruptly cut off as he was almost thrown out of his seat as with a screech of it’s brakes the limo came to a halt. "Hey what is going on up there?!" he called up to the driver. "Sorry about that sir, but we ran into some unexpected traffic ahead, it seems that there is a Hurst in the middle of the street" the driver replied, looking back over his shoulders as he apologised to his passengers. "What did you say?" Phoenix asked, startled, as he pressed a button on the side of the door. The window slid down soundlessly allowing him a full view of the exterior of the car, and as he poked his head out through the opening he saw a funeral cortege passing across the junction ahead of them. "I said there is a hearse in the middle of the street sir, but it is passing now so we should be able to continue shortly" the driver stated neutrally, keeping his gaze forward as he watched the traffic pass. "You ok Jay, you look a little shaken?" Rick asked with some concern. Phoenix sat back into the chair, wiping one hand across his forehead as he stared out through the window as the traffic cleared up. Clearing his throat a couple of times he gave a weak smile towards Rick. "Erm … yeah I am fine Rick, just thought that I heard something …" he trailed off with another smile "…doesn’t matter though, what were you saying again?" "Was just saying that after dinner we can go and catch this comedy act that I have heard a lot about, if you fancy it of course?" Rick offered. "Sounds good my friend, will be nice just to kick back and enjoy myself for a change" Phoenix said with a forced smile, still looking slightly on edge and not fully relaxed. As the cortege finally cleared the road ahead, the limo begins to move forward again. Placing his head against the back of the chair, Phoenix closed his eyes as a long slow sigh escaped his mouth. Finally starting to relax for the first time in nearly a week he smiled to himself. "Mind if I put the radio on Jay?" Rick asked him. Without opening his eyes Phoenix waved one hand as he muttered his acquiescence. The motion of the car and the air blowing through the open window helped him to drift even further towards full relaxation, and the previous nights of troubled sleep caught up with him. Relaxation turned into the realm between being awake and being asleep as music filled the inside of the limo as the radio was turned on, advertising musak for the latest offer on automobiles that would change your life trying their best to infiltrate both the subconscious and the wallet. "Hey wake up sleepy head, we are there" a voice intruded on the insides of this head as he made the return journey to the waking world. Looking out through the window beside him Phoenix realised that about an hour must have passed, twilight now intruding on the World as the Sun began to set. Stretching his arms up over his head Phoenix smiled to himself as the cartilage in his back popped slightly. "Oh I needed that" he said, half to himself and half to Rick who was grinning at him. "You sure did, you passed out pretty much as soon as we started driving and have been fast asleep ever since. It seems a shame to wake you up but I thought that you would rather get to the restaurant instead of sleeping in a limo" Rick laughed as he reached over and opened the door, stepping out into the evening air. Phoenix followed him out and took a deep breath, rubbing one hand across his face as he fully woke up. "So where is this place?" he asked as he looked around at the busy street the limo had stopped on. People were milling around and moving along their way, young and old, singles and couples, all out in the World doing their thing. "You really need to wake up Jay you know!" Rick said as he places his hands on his friend’s shoulders and slowly turned him around so that he faced a red-bricked building. Above it’s large windows was a sign that read, simply, ‘Mange’. "I knew that" Phoenix laughed to himself sheepishly as he walked towards the restaurant. "Sure you did, but still it is good to hear you laugh" Rick replied, following his friend inside the building. Almost immediately the maitre de met them with a smile on his face. "Aha Mr Phoenix we have been expecting you" he gushed in a French accent as he escorted them into the restaurant proper, indicating that they should sit down at a well laid out table near the centre of the room, in which only a few of the tables had patrons. "If I could be so bold, I would like to recommend the chef’s speciality tonight gentlemen, it is a delicacy that has to be tasted to be believed" he said as Phoenix and Rick seated themselves. "That is fine with me" Phoenix said as he exchanged a glance with Rick, who nodded his own agreement. "Excellent, then I will be right back with the wine menu" the maitre de said with a small bow before turning off and walking towards the kitchen. The minutes passed in companionable small talk as both men sipped from their drinks, Rick’s glass of white wine and Phoenix’s own filled with still water. The décor of the restaurant and the fact that the clientele was exclusive being talked about and more and more Phoenix’s face relaxed as the cares of the past week were forgotten. The maitre de, trailed by a waiter carrying two silver topped plates, arrived at the table once more with the self same smile on his face as if it was tattooed there. As the waiter placed the plates before the two men, the maitre de reached down and lifted the lids off with a flourish. "The chef’s speciality my friends, duck a l’orange … bon appetite!" he said as he turned and walked away. Pushing his plate away from him, his face pale, Phoenix leant back in the chair with a small groan. "Are you ok Jay?" Rick asked, noting the change in his friends complexion. "Duck … it HAD to be duck didn’t it?" Phoenix muttered under his breath before, with a movement so sudden that it caused his chair to screech along the floor, the already quiet restaurant becoming deathly still as all eyes descended upon him, getting to his feet. "I have to get out of her Rick" he said as he brushed past his friend and walked towards the exit. Slapping one hand to his forehead in realisation as he stared at the meals on the table, Rick hurriedly grabbed his wallet from his jacket pocket and pulled some folded bills out, handing them to the maitre de who had come over to the table in concern. "Is everything ok sir, was there something wrong with the food?" he asked, his accent forgotten as a typical American brogue rang out instead. "No, nothing really just some bad connotations with the choice of meat is all, my friend has … erm … an allergy, yeah that is it … he has an allergy to duck!" Rick improvised as he patted the man on the shoulder before hurrying towards the exit himself. Opening the door and walking out onto the street Rick looked around for Jay, who he saw leaning against the wall only a few feet away. With an audible sigh of relief he walked over and stopped in front of him. "You ok?" he asked gently "No … I am feeling pretty stupid to be honest!" came the muttered and sheepish reply as Phoenix stared at his feet "I mean can you believe I just made such a scene about a meal of all things … and just because it had a duck in it!" "Listen I know that you have a lot on your mind right now Jay, so not wanting to eat the meal because it reminded of EDH and Ducky is perfectly understandable" Rick said consolingly. "It is not understandable it is crazy Rick!" Phoenix blurted out as he looked up at him, his face incredulous. "Yeah it is isn’t it?" Rick laughed, relief evident on his face. Taking a deep breath, Phoenix seemed to steady himself as if a great weight had been lifted from his shoulders. Looking directly up into the early evening air he stared at the cloudless sky, stars becoming visible scattered as far as the eye could see. A constant reminder of the wider universe that existed outside of the small concerns of the human race … a constant reminder of everything that existed outside of the small concerns of just one man. For the first time in days a relaxed and open smile appeared on Phoenix’s face as he brought his eyes down from the Heavens to the grounded reality he lived in. "As crazy as everything else I have been doing this week Rick. As crazy as beating myself up over facing Dave. If this was anyone else … if this was Angelo Deville … I would simply go in there prepared to win, knowing that I would win, and not worry about how my opponent thought and felt being the loser" Phoenix opened up, speaking calmly to Rick who listened intently. "… but this is different, and simply because Dave is my friend. I am more worried about how he will feel when I eliminate him from the tournament than I am about actually facing him, and that has me tied up in knots inside. So much so that I am jumping at shadows, I am hearing things that are not there, and I am seeing symbolism in things as stupid as a meal!" Phoenix finished with a self-deprecating laugh. "Does that mean that you have figured out some way to get over this then Jay?" Rick asked with a small note of concern. "Nope, just means that I have decided that we should get something else to eat" Phoenix laughed as he draped an arm over his friends shoulder, starting to walk away from the restaurant and up the street. "Well that is a start at least I suppose Jay, anywhere in particular in mind?" Rick asked in evident relief as he saw the friend that he knew so well returning. "Don’t really care as long as it is not called Exceptional Dave’s … or serves duck as a speciality" Phoenix said wryly. "How about in here?" Rick asked as he indicated a bar that had a food menu in the window. Both men looked at the menu for a few seconds, reading through the basic but hearty and wholesome fare on offer. The door beside them opened up as a patron left, and from inside the strains of a very familiar song played out, the YMCA by the Village People. Looking first at each other, then at the window of the bar itself a little closer, both Phoenix and Rick burst out laughing as they saw the poster advertising that the main attraction that night was a tribute to the Village People with a special guest entertainer being Phillpe Rose, the former Indian of the group. "How about we find somewhere else Rick, I mean for all we know Dave is in there right now with Ducky talking to that guy to see if he can find out all he can about me from another so-called Native American" Phoenix stuttered out between laughs. "Come on Jay, even Dave wouldn’t be that crazy would he?" Rick asked as he wiped tears from his eyes. "YES!" both men laughed in unison. Realising that the tension was firmly broken in Jay Rick started to relax himself. Looking closely at his friend he breathed a subtle sigh of relief as he saw that the laughter had done much more than anything else to chase his worries away, and being thankful for the fact that Phoenix seemed to be getting back to his normal self. Out of the corner of his eye he noticed a dark shape falling from the building above them, a shape that Phoenix himself had not noticed. "DUCK!" he shouted at Jay, his won feet rooted in place in shock. "Oh that is VERY funny Ri …" Phoenix started, still laughing, until he was abruptly … and violently … cut off as the mirrored sign from above the bar’s door, which had somehow become dislodged, slammed into his head and knocked him to the unforgiving concrete sidewalk. "Jay … JAY … are … you ….. o………… k………………" Rick’s voice faded away as if he was being pulled into the distance. "Of course I am ok Rick, but I still don’t think that the ‘duck’ joke was very funny" Jay said then trailed off as he looked around him. Just moments before he had been standing in a busy city street, talking to his friend and trying to find something for dinner. Now though he was … somewhere else. Where he was though was most definitely an interesting question. If total and utter darkness could be defined as the total and utter lack of light then Phoenix found himself in a place where shadows never got a foothold, where darkness was a concept that would never be understood. Stark harsh white was the only thing that could be seen everywhere that Phoenix looked. Three hundred and sixty degrees of whiteness surrounded him, all encompassing. Directly above him and directly below him was made up of the same unending white. Reaching up to his forehead, suddenly disorientated as he realised that he didn’t know if he was standing on a surface or hanging upside down … not even being able to rationalise what WAS up or down … Phoenix did the only natural thing. He threw up. Kneeling down … though not even thinking about just how or on what he could be kneeling … Phoenix held onto his stomach as he emptied the little food that was left within him until nothing but dry retching noises came from his throat. Standing up … or at least becoming vertical again … he wiped a hand over his mouth, his face pale. "Well that was pleasant" came a sarcastic comment from out of the aether. Spinning full circle Phoenix came face to face with a full length mirror that did nothing to help his disorientation as it seemingly hung in mid-air, unsupported. His face shocked at the sudden appearance of an object that had not been there moments before, Phoenix kept looking for the source of the voice, even going so far as to … gingerly, until he realised that he COULD walk on this surface… walk completely around the mirror itself. Coming back to the front of the mirror he stared in confusion at his own reflection. "Dammit, what is going on?" he muttered to himself, wiping a hand across his eyes as he tried to clear his head. "Well all I know is that you are currently making some rather colourful changes to this place, though for me I would have gone with a nice throw rug instead of the pavement pizza my friend" came the sarcastic … yet somehow familiar … response. Phoenix dropped his hand from his face in shock, looking around him once more and still not finding the source of the voice. "Not very quick on the uptake are you?" came the voice. Staring into the mirror Phoenix suddenly realised that his reflection was not doing what was expected. It did not stand the same way he did, it did not move the same way he did, and it most certainly seemed to be speaking for itself. Stepping back away from it, Phoenix’s mouth gaped open as he realised that no matter ho much he moved his legs he didn’t place any difference between him and the silvery surface. Either he wasn’t moving or the mirror was keeping up with him. "Close your mouth Jay, you look like a fish out of water!" his reflection sneered at him. "Wha … what it going … who are … where a …" Phoenix stuttered. "For fuck’s sake man get a hold of yourself, anyone would think that you had never talked to yourself before … or perhaps it would be better to say that you had never talked to your BETTER self before!" the reflection snarled, pointing a finger at Phoenix who reeled back in shock again, which elicited a mocking laugh from his mirror image. "Well it seems that you have the swooning and angst ridden worrying down pat at least Jay, so we can be proud that you are good at something … but I spend most of my days throwing up too. Mostly because I am sick to the stomach of being part of you!" the reflection shouted throw the glass, hammering it’s hands against the surface from the other side. "I don’t understand …" Phoenix started to say softly as he played with a ring on his right hand’s third finger, rotating it round and round. "I see that you still have that annoying habit of playing with Mom’s wedding ring whenever you are worried Jay … whatever next, are you going to start sucking your thumb, or asking for your blankey perhaps?" grinned the image, as he held his left hand up to show the selfsame ring there. "No … I .. " Phoenix started to say, but was again interrupted by the image in the mirror. "I don’t care what you were going to say ‘Little Flame’ it doesn’t matter to me, all that matters is that for some reason I am able to finally meet you face to face so to speak. I have spent my whole life … OUR whole life … looking out at you and seeing what a waste of life you are, seeing what a worthless piece of shit you are and wishing that I could grab you by the throat and spit in your face as I force the air out of your lungs. You have wasted EVERY opportunity ever given to you, you have backed down, you have played the nice guy, you have never wanted to rock the boat!" the image ranted at a stunned Phoenix who stood as if the only reason he didn’t fall over was because his brain had shut down beyond even that action. "Always doing what is right, always so concerned about your fans and their expectations … never reaching out and taking what you … WE … fucking deserve. Oh no, Mr high and mighty has to walk the straight and narrow. No drinking, no smoking, no swearing … just being the perfect man for your retard of a grandfather!" it continued, the volume rising as the venom exploded from it’s mouth, it’s hand pressed flat against the other surface of the mirror. "Even forgiving that piece of shit Vampir Nosferatu for taking away the one thing that would have made your worthless life meaningful … at least if you had won that title the first time you may have been corrupted enough to fuck some of those bimbos that would have thrown themselves at you. Now when you are given a second chance at that self same glory you are more fucking worried about how your little girly friend Hurst will feel when you beat him than you are about winning this thing for yourself!" the tirade continued as the reflection’s face become sweat-soaked as if he was under immense pressure, though it seemed that he only leant against the mirror himself. "I can tell you this I would have forgiven Vampir, I would have cut his head off and shit down his throat for what he did to you … to us … but someone beat me to it didn’t they? I must remember to thank Hessian someday for that. Another thing that I can tell you is that I wouldn’t be worrying about Dave Hurst and his little plastic piece of shit, I would be going into the ring to beat him … to hurt him … to break him!" the image suddenly stopped speaking, relaxing into a small smile that brought a gasp of shock to Phoenix himself as he realised that it was his own smile he saw. "You see, one thing that you have never known, never realised, is that without me you are nothing my friend … my twin … without me, without my anger, without my hate, without my strength you would BE nothing. Sometimes, not very often but normally when you really need me, a little of me slips out into YOUR World you know. When you are in the ring and you need that little extra to win, that is me you know." grinned the image as it leant on the surface, arms folded. " … but that little isn’t enough, I want more Jay, I want everything that you have, everything that you have wasted … ARE wasting … with you posturing and whining … I want it Jay … I want YOUR life!" As he spat the final words towards a stunned Phoenix, the reflection in the mirror suddenly threw itself towards the surface and unexpectedly … unbelievably … it stretched and strained like wet material. It’s hands reached out through the silvery surface towards Phoenix who stood rooted … like a rabbit caught in the headlights … to the spot, his eyes unfocused in shock and his breathing shallow. The fingers of the reflection stretched out further that would have been humanly possibly, their ends becoming clawed, and as one latched onto Phoenix’s arm the other scraped across his face, opening three bloody gashes across his cheek. The pain of these wounds suddenly seemed to awaken and galvanise Phoenix, who suddenly reached up to fend off this waking nightmare, punching and striking out at it with more force than he had ever used in training or combat … almost as if his life depended on it. … and it did. With a mocking laugh the reflection ignored all the blows, holding on to the struggling Phoenix like an adult calming a child throwing a tantrum and inch-by-inch it brought Phoenix towards itself, towards the surface of the mirror. With a shriek that was filled with despair and inhuman pain Phoenix threw back his head, his mouth wide open, as his body touched the surface of the mirror. Instead of stopping, instead of meeting an immutable surface of silvered glass, Phoenix screamed as his journey continued on and into the mirror itself. Becoming one with his own reflection, merging with it … melding with it … moving into it and then through it … slowly but surely, inch-by-inch, molecule by molecule, Phoenix and his reflection passed through each other. With a final shriek of all consuming agony … harmonised with one of eternal and triumphant glee … everything suddenly went quiet. Phoenix reached up a hand and winced as his fingers traced the lines embedded into his face, staring in shock at the blood that covered his fingers when he pulled them away. Then with a grin he put his fingers to his mouth as his tongue snaked it’s way out of his mouth to flicker against the scarlet liquid. "Mmmmm … tasty!" he snickered to himself as an almost orgasmic shudder played across his body. Staring into the mirror, the grinning figure watched as it’s reflection curled up into a foetal position … arms wrapped around legs, head ducked into it’s chest … and started to violently shake; dry, racking sobs coming from it’s unseen face, like a lost and frightened child. "Pitiful Jay, really pitiful, but don’t worry about it you will have plenty of time to get used to this … and just think how much fun Jay Phoenix is going to have now that I am in charge of things!" the standing figure smirked as he looked around at his white and featureless surroundings, marred only by the floating mirror. A small look of consternation passed across the figure’s face, which was quickly replaced, by longing and understanding. "Time to go now Jay, but I’ll be seeing you in the shaving mirror my brother … but for now … GO TO HELL!" the figure snarled as he reached out and punched the surface of the mirror. With a deafening explosion the mirror seemed to implode in on itself, shards of swirling silver glass spinning around and around, like some new born cosmos, until … simply … there was nothing. Nothing but whiteness. Nothing but the figure standing in the whiteness and grinning. Nothing but … " … k … c’mon Jay talk to me, are you ok?!?" Rick asked with evident concern as he knelt down beside his friend on the sidewalk. Reaching out one hand Rick wiped small shards of broken mirror away from Phoenix’s face, watching in relief as his friends eyelids started to flutter. Taking a handkerchief out of a pocket, Rick dabbed at the three cuts on Phoenix’s face wincing in empathy as he saw just how deep the falling mirror had cut his friend. "Where am I?" Phoenix asked weakly as he started to sit up, his eyes glazed. "You are with me Jay, with Rick. A sign just fell from a building and it knocked you out for a few seconds … someone just went to call an ambulance. As soon as we get there I will give Argyle a ring and let him know that you have been hurt and will have to pull ou …" Rick stammered quickly, his words condensing into one breath in his shock, before being cut off as Phoenix reached out to grab his arm … his green eyes suddenly blazing with something that made Rick … … nervous? … for a second. Shaking his head to clear it, Rick cleared his throat and shivered as if chilled. Why would he be nervous, this was his friend in front of him, his friend who was hurt. Supporting Phoenix’s head Rick gave a quick smile to his friend as he realised that he was probably in shock himself and passing everything off as a result of that. "Seconds … only seconds … it feels like a lifetime to me Rick, it feels like I was gone for his … my whole life!" Phoenix started, grinning suddenly, the streaks of blood on his facing making him look maniacal or demonic. "… and don’t worry about the ambulance, or phoning Argyle my friend, I am fine, in fact I am more than fine, I am fucking fabulous!" Phoenix shouted, causing the crowd that had gathered around the fallen man to back off in shock as he clambered to his feet. "We need to get you checked out though Jay, who knows what that sign hitting you could have done? … and as for wrestling Dave that may not be the best idea …" Rick started, but again was cut off as Phoenix interrupted him. "I know what that sign did Rick, so stop treating me like a fucking child, you are not my mother … I am fine ok!" Phoenix started, and then laughed as if he suddenly thought of something. "… maybe you had better phone that ambulance though Rick, because someone is sure as Hell going to need one soon, but it isn’t me!" he said with a grin that exposed his teeth. "What Jay … I don’t understand?" Rick queried, very confused and more than a little shocked. "I got me a tournament to win Rick, I have that faggot Angie Deville in my sights so that I can screw him over before taking what is rightfully mine … four years late … the GTT tournament!" Phoenix crowed to himself, oblivious to the look of shock on his friend’s face " … but first of all I have to teach a little wrestling truth to that idiot Hurst on my way, a lesson if you will about what happens when a no talent piece of shit like him steps in the ring with someone as great as me … when someone like him DARES to get in my way to the end of the tournament … and as we all know the truth hurts Rick. So book that ambulance" Leaning into his friend, who recoils from him, which elicited another laugh, Phoenix placed his left hand … the wedding band glinting on his finger … around his head and pulled him close to his own mouth, his tongue wetting his lips as he whispered into Rick’s ear.
