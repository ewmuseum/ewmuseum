Devour/Destroy

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 -Prologue- The temperature is freezing and the baron back streets of the unknown city are completely deserted….well nearly… Standing against a graffiti covered wall is a figure shroud by darkness. The bulk mass of the figure suggests that it is a man and on closer inspection of it’s shape suggests this also. It is indeed a man but no ordinary one, for this man is The Real Deal and tonight he is on a mission. The following will outline why… -Drown the anger with fluid- On a night a week before, which was filled with Jack Daniels, was just like any other night for The Real Deal as he sat at the bar alone. The frown which was across his face barely left him unless he was angry. This night though he was too drunk even to be angry. He lifted the bottle of Jack and poured himself a drink. The barman had just left the bottle with Deal as per usual, the bill would be paid later. After knocking back roughly a double, Deal slammed the glass down on the bar. An elderly man who was also sitting at the bar turned his head swiftly to look at Deal. Old Man: Do you mind? This place is always quiet till you turn up! Shouldn’t you be off wrestling somewhere. Deal just turned his head slowly and tried to focus on the man. Once he has accomplished that, which was quite hard at this point, he gave the man a piercing look. Real Deal: Shouldn’t you be off to your death bed old man?! You couldn’t be that far off it. The old man laughed. He and Deal were no strangers to each other. The old man in question was known as Ryan Tyron. Everyone who drank in "The Nook", as the bar was called, knew him as he was pretty much there every hour it was open. While still laughing, as he was also a little drunk, he replied to Deal. Ryan: I’ll be asking you that same question soon it would seem. Especially the way you’ve been getting on lately. Deal shook his head. Real Deal: What do you mean? I get on fine in here. What do you mean old man? Ryan stood up and lifted his stool so he would be able to sit closer to Deal. Once he had moved and at down again he lifted the bottle of Jack and moved it from side to side in front of Deal’s face. Ryan: This is what I mean. You are in here every night and you drink more than I do in the whole day. You are not doing yourself any favours. Deal sighed and grabbed the bottle off him. He unscrewed the top and took a swig of the fine whiskey. He then set the bottle back on the table. Real Deal: That’s rich coming from you Ryan o’ lord of the nook. I could of swore they put your picture up in here somewhere. Like come on, you’re practically a legend around here. Ryan lifted up his hands and smiled. Ryan: Well I’d hate to let my fans down by not being here you know but seriously, you need to lay off the whiskey kid. Do you want to have a future? Deal rubbed down his face with the palms of his hands before muttering… Real Deal: I have nothing to live for… Ryan, who has hearing problems, tapped Deal on the shoulder… Ryan: What was that? Deal’s face turned sour and he was obviously getting angered by how deep Ryan was sifting into his personal life. Real Deal: Why the fuck do you want to know Ryan? Why the fuck?! Ryan put his hand on Deal’s should which was swiftly pushed off. Ryan sighed and sat back. Ryan: Because I want to help you. I don’t want you to become what I’ve become. You are quickly falling into the abyss which took me in and there is no escape…believe me, I’ve tried. Real Deal: You’re drunk! Ryan: Of course I am you idiot! So are you or didn’t you notice how you can’t focus on me for more than a few seconds. Something is clouding you mind, I can sense it from you. You come in here and you sit at this bar every night without fail. It intrigues me why you do it. There has to be something major on your mind. Deal slammed his fist on the bar just as Ryan was about to continue… Real Deal: You really want to know what happened? Well let me tell you. Once upon a time I had a wife, I had a career and I had the most precious piece of stone in the entire universe. In one foul swoop each of these were taking from me. Ryan: Well then son, I’m going to help you whether you like it or not! I’ve been through it all before and it’s time for me to help someone as I didn’t help myself. Grab your coat, you’ve just gained yourself a new manager! Real Deal: Manager? Why the hell do I need a manager? Ryan laughed and pulled a leaflet. He handed it to Deal who was somewhat surprised… Real Deal: GTT5? Are you kidding me? I’ll get destroyed! Ryan: Is that really you in there? Behind the curtain of hate and betrayal? You want to get noticed in someway that you can bring these people to you? Then this is it! They obviously did this to destroy you but if they see you in that tournament kicking ass they’ll fear you like never before…come on! What have you got to lose? Deal sighed and set the leaflet down to one side before looking back into Ryan’s eyes… Real Deal: This is crazy but fuck it…I’m in! -Running away- The reason why Deal stands in these back streets shrouded in darkness is because he is running from the police. Three or four days ago the police had arrived at his door and basically told him he had murdered his daughter. This was not on in Deal’s eyes and he somehow escaped them. In his mind it is not the time to be getting thrown in jail for some bullshit he didn’t’t do. Kill his daughter? He doesn’t even know where she lives. It seemed to him though that Erin had told the police that, she would do everything she could to ruin him. Tonight is the night and all is set for his revenge. As he stands against the wall he lights up a cigarette and takes a number of draws. A calming sensation comes over him and he lays his head back. Looking up to the sky he cracks a smile for the first time in months. Happiness fills him as he realises he’s done it again, he’s beaten Erin again! One he finishes his cigarette he moves off quickly, time is at the essence. First he must complete his revenge and then meet up with the old guy Ryan for his "special training". Getting distracted would take away from this which he doesn’t’t need. Vangelus Olsig will be a tough opponent. After about a twenty minute or so walk, The Real Deal finds himself in front of the hotel that Erin works at. He laughs at the name… "The Glitz and Glamour hotel. What a pile of crap!" He walks up the steps holding on to the centre rail as he moves. After whisking through the revolving doors as quick as possible he has a quick look around. Finding Erin would be a problem now, she might not even be working. Spotting the receptionist coming back to the desk, he darts over with his arms open to her. A friendly voice follows as his intentions must not become clear. "Hello there! I was just wondering if you knew a girl called Erin Stevenson?" The reception smiles like she’s been trained to. She is also trained to protect her colleagues. "I do know her but I must ask your business with her Sir." Without hesitation Deal pulls a small box out of his jackets inner pocket. The receptionist sees the shape of it and assumes the most likely thing that is in it. "Oh! You’re him! Oh! She’ll be so pleased. She’s been talking about this for months." This could be a problem. Did this girl really think that The Real Deal was going to propose to Erin? If so bad things were going to happen. The girl picks up her phone and dials a number while looking up at Deal. "I’ll just call her down for you now." Deal backs off a little knowing that this will definitely be bad. The receptionist speaks for a moment on the phone quietly before putting it down and looking back up to Deal. "Ok Sir she wants you to make your way down the hall on the right of the desk her and into the third room on the left. You get that?" Deal smiles and nods before turning off towards the hall. Before he exit’s the sight of the receptionist though he turns to her to enquire about what she had said earlier. "She’s been talking about it for months? Damn, I wanted it to be a surprise for her." The receptionist laughs and gives him the thumbs up. "I’m sure she will be surprised. Just make it special for her ok?" Deal nods once again and winks to the receptionist. "I make it as special as I can." With a smile a and glint in his eye Deal turns away again and makes his way down the hall. As he reaches the third door on the right he takes a deep breath before knocking. A female voice shouts from within the room, it is Erin’s voice. "Come on in!" With another deep breath he pulls down the handle of the door and enters. Confusion now hits as he finds himself in complete darkness. This confusion turns to shock though as he feels someone’s lips meet his in a passionate embrace. This is short lived though as the person giving the kiss breaks it off and pushed Deal away. Erin’s voice rings out in anger. "What the fuck have you been doing?! Your breath stinks!" Deal laughs to himself knowing now that he is alone with Erin. As they remain in the darkness he gladly answers her. "You should be used to it by now my wife!" Erin can be heard gasping and shuffling about in the darkness. Eventually she finds the light switch and flicks it on. She shakes her head as she looks Deal up and down before a sly grin appears across her face. "You really shouldn’t of come here hun. You do know that I could get you arrested for even just standing there?" Deal responds immediately as he in no mood for bullshitting. "Just like you did a number of days ago. Accusing me of killing our daughter? You are one sick bitch Erin you know that? Why bring her in to this?" Erin laughs as she knows that her actions have struck a nerve. She moves closer to Deal and places her hand on his chest. "You used to have it all. You had a great career, a great family and of course your great orb! That last thing I stated was what made me want to destroy you. It consumed you and turned you into a person I didn’t know anymore. It turned you into a monster that I didn’t love anymore. I needed to break you." Deal grabs Erin’s hand softly and looks straight into her eyes. A grin now appears on his face knowing she has not succeeded in her plan. "Unfortunately for you though my dear you failed. I am still here, even though you left me for dead! How does that make you feel? You never liked being a failure before but now you really are one!" Erin pulls her hand away and turns her back on Deal. "I have nothing more to say to you! I have a new life now. One without wrestling and one definitely without you with your power trip!" Deal drops his head to make Erin think he is sad about her not talking to him anymore. Inside though he is laughing beyond comprehension. "If that’s the way you want it. I will grant you that wish." Erin stands in silence as Deal turns to walk away. He stops in his tracks though as he remembers about the box he brought with him. "Oh, I have this for you. I’ll just leave it on the floor and you can look at it when your ready." Deal opens the door and makes his exit, leaving Erin alone in the room. Her head drops this time with a feeling of disappointment. She sighs and turns to the box which sits on the floor. She kneels down and lifts the box up. It is the size and shape of a wedding ring box. She opens it up to find a small picture, two rings and a letter. The picture is of her and Deal back when they were a happy couple. The two rings are the ones which bind them in marriage. Finally looking at the letter it reads. "You are a shadow of your former self, a failure and a liar. The two things you never ever wanted to be. You broke the binds that tied us together in marriage and it is now time for me to end it all. This letter is just to let you know that I’m still around and that you will never rid this world of The Real Deal. Yes…I said The Real Deal! Watch out for the GTT5 as I will be there to show you that I will live on even though you took everything I had. Enjoy the picture and the rings, maybe and your new man can make a little shrine to them. For now and forever…goodbye!" Erin drops to her knees, gripping the two rings and letter together in her right hand. A single tear rolls down her cheek as she stares into complete space. "I only wanted you to change. I need you…come back to me…come back to me….please" Outside the room Deal holds his head in his hands beginning to comprehend in his mind what he had just said. He looks up with his eyes glazed over. "Now…for preparation." He starts off down the hall and to the door, not looking back or around him. He gets out the front of the hotel and stands with his eyes closed in the rain that started when he was inside. It’s soft touch calms him and he makes his way down the steps before hailing a passing cab. The driver stops and motions for Deal to enter. Once in the back seat of the cab the driver turns to Deal. "Where you for?" Deal sticks his hand into his pocket and brings out a crumpled note. He looks at it for a second before looking back up at the driver. "It’s ummm…a place called The Omissen Lab?" The driver smiles and turns back to look in front of him. "No problem! I’ll get you there in half an hour." Deal does not reply but instead lays his head back against the headrest of his seat. He closes his eyes and falls asleep. Half an hour passes and the cab pulls up beside a sign stating: "The Omissen Lab for Biological Testing. Trespassers will be prosecuted!" The driver turns to Deal and speaks in a loud enough voice to wake him up. "Hey! We’re here! That’ll be $20!" Deal awakes startled and rummages in his wallet for some cash. He only has a fifty but decides he’ll do a good deed for the driver. "Here’s fifty. Keep the change." Before the driver gets to answer Deal jumps out of the cab into the pouring rain and makes his way past the sign of the building. About one hundred metres away lies a barbwire covered gate with a guard posted at either side of it. Deal begins to wonder if this is the correct place. Soon he has reached the gate and he takes a look at both the guards, both of them are armed with rifles. He motions to them to take notice of him. They do not move but both shout in unison. "WHAT IS YOUR BUSINESS HERE?" Deal looks both of them up and down again, somehow impressed by their professionalism. He moves closer to the gate and speaks clear so they can both hear him. "I’m here to see Ryan Tyron!" Once again the men do not move but shout louder than before. "OPEN THE GATE! VISITOR FOR MASTER TYRON!" A few moments pass before the large complex gates open up. Once they are fully open Deal walks past the guards and into the complex. He observes the area with much detail. The most outstanding thing about the complex is its lighting. Large street like lights enlighten the insanely detailed artwork which is carved into the buildings walls. It truly is a piece of art and the lighting adds so much to it. The building itself looks to be shaped like a U with Deal making his way towards the curved bottom. At either side of the path in the middle grow nurtured patches of grass and flowers, a pretty sight if you’re into that sort of thing. Once Deal finished enjoying the view he makes his way down the long path to the front entrance. It is something he did not notice before but the entrance to the building is completely different to the rest. It is more up to date, maybe even going as far as saying it is futuristic. A large glass sphere revolves above the metal covered door and walls, clearly high security it would seem. Above the door is a camera facing down to show whoever is trying to make their way in. Beside it is a large speaker, maybe 100watts or more in power. Deal moves into view of the camera and is met by the voice of his "manager" Ryan Tyron. "Ahh! You made it! I’m glad to see you. I thought you might have gone to the bar or something. Just wait a second there and I will active the lift" Deal looks up and sees nothing above the entrance. He decides this must be a very special place if it is to be underground. The metal door in front of him opens and he makes his way inside. The door closes behind him and he feels the lift shakes slightly before commencing to move. After about a minute of waiting the door opens again and Deal finds himself at a plain white room which just a table and a bottle of beer sitting at it. He moves quickly to quench his thirst but finds out it isn’t going to be that easy to get the beer. A clear sheet of plastic stops him in his tracks and his head bounces off it and he drops to the ground in pain. Ryan’s voice yells over a speak in a content voice. "I knew that would happen! HAHAHA!" Deal jumps up and turns to make his way to the lift but it closes before he makes it. He looks around for a camera to vent his frustrations to. Once he find it he gives it a middle finger before turning and kicking the plastic which covers the whole of the table. "You bastard! Why tempt me like this!?" Ryan laughs over the speaker before explaining his actions. "You know, you’re probably still a bit drunk from the last time you drank which probably isn’t that long ago am I correct? Actually don’t answer that, I know I’m correct. Anyway, this is a kind of experiment which will make you so angry that you can’t get the beer that everything else that’s on your mind will be pushed to the back. Now concentrate on the beer and now imagine it is Olsig." Deal looks up to the camera. "Huh? That’s just random." Ryan taps his microphone hard and turns the volume up to punish Deal’s ear drums. "Envision it! If you don’t this will continue!" Deal looks away from the camera and to the beer. "But why a beer?" Ryan taps the microphone again. "Because you dummy it’s something you crave and by the time I give you it you will want to devour it! But if you envision that it’s Olsig then you’ll just destroy him when you get to him. Do you get it?" Deal sighs before sitting on the floor, just about able to see the sole beer sitting there. "I think so. I’ll try!" Ryan taps the microphone even harder an more rapidly than before. "You don’t think! You don’t try! You just freaking do it! Now I’m off to the bar. Enjoy your night, food will be brought to you with some water. Make sure you do what I say, if you prevail you will be stronger than before." Deal does not reply. Ryan’s words echo in his mind and the beer becomes the centre of attention. Soon he finds that Olsig is also in his mind and he begins to work on a strategy… Simple strategy…devour…destroy!
