Nobody Does It Worser - Episode 5

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-10 MAYBE ENTERPRISES, TRAINING FACILITY – CHICAGO THE NIGHT BEFORE JIMMY BONG FACES CHANDLER TSONDA LATE EVENING (BUT NOT TOO LATE SO BONG CAN STILL CATCH THE COLBERT REPORT) "Honestly Ears, must you stand so close to me while I’m reading the paper? It’s quite irritating." He was not a haphephobic man by any means, but like any normal human being Jimmy Bong preferred not to have his personal space violated. He found it particularly all the more irritating when he was trying to peruse the Chicago Sun-Times for his daily news—or at least for his daily Fox Trot. The gargantuan Ears simply looked down at Bong and smiled his menacing smile. "And what’s more," Bong continued, "I think you’re taking this ‘bodyguard’ business a little too far. I do appreciate you taking every possible measure to ensure my safety, but was it really necessary to join me in the loo?" Bong gave Ears an annoyed glance upward from his seat on the john. His bodyguard was in fact standing inside his bathroom stall, without any regard whatsoever for another man’s privacy. Ears nearly took up all the free space Bong would have had for movement, blatantly pervading his three-foot bubble. Fortunately, Bong was not a claustrophobic man either—nor a homophobic one. But that was beside the point, really. "Now kindly step out, will you? I’m just about finished here." Ears gave a big shrug, then turned around (with some difficulty) and exited the stall. He lingered just outside for a few moments more, until the flushing of the toilet signaled that Bong had completed his business. The former British secret agent emerged, looking quite relieved. "Well then, let’s head back to the hotel, shall we?" The monstrous bodyguard nodded in agreement as they turned and walked toward the exit. The evening had been quite successful for Bong, having just endured another rigorous workout and making the final preparations for his impending bout against Chandler Tsonda. Even the knowledge that one of the very people he was working with had designs to murder him wasn’t enough to dampen his spirits. As he stepped through the restroom door, he couldn’t help but feel… Suddenly, a knife whizzed through the air right in front of Bong’s face, nearly taking off his nose! "JIMINY CHRISTMAS!" he yelped, jumping backwards into Ears (though not fazing him in the least). Bong’s heart practically leapt out of his chest. He looked to his right and spotted his trainer, Sgt. Gordon Gloucester, holding a set of throwing knives and looking back at him rather angrily. And with Gloucester, knives plus anger equaled not good. "SHIT! Do you have a death wish or something!? Are you crazy, boy!?" he fumed. "Me crazy?" Bong repeated indignantly. "You’re the one who just tried to throw a knife through my head! You could have killed me!" His last statement made him pause thoughtfully. "Wait a minute! That means… was it you that tried to do me in the other night? It must have been! You’re my attempted murderer, you fiend!" "No I’m not, you puerile jackass!" Gloucester snarled back. "And I didn’t throw the damn knife at you. You’re standing in the middle of my throwing knife practice range!" "What?" Bong spun around to see a mannequin set up on the other side of the large workout room. The poor doppelganger had already endured several would-be fatal wounds to the face, chest and abdomen—and one a bit lower down with such nasty precision that it made Bong wince. "Well… Well that’s idiotic!" he exclaimed a moment later. "Who in their right mind would practice knife-throwing in front of a restroom door?" "It’s in front of the women’s restroom, you dumbshit," Gloucester replied. "I figured it was a safe place to practice because everyone around here over three feet tall has, you know, DICKS! Everyone except you, apparently… What the hell were you doing in there, anyway? Can’t you read signs? How fuckin’ stupid are you? Actually, don’t answer that," he added quickly as Bong opened his mouth to respond. "Just get the hell outta here, we’re done for the night." Gloucester frowned at Ears, who was staring back with that trademark demented smile spread across his face. The tension was palpable, even to someone as thick as Jimmy Bong. He knew that if Gloucester was really going to try and murder him, he wouldn’t dare do so with Ears around. Even the homicidally tough military man was wary of the silent behemoth. Bong strode quickly away from Gloucester, wanting only to leave the training facility and get back to his hotel for a good night’s rest. Unfortunately for him, he wasn’t going anywhere just yet. Erik Lang stopped him before he could reach the door. "Oh Mr. Bong, I just had a WONDERFUL idea for you!" the oddball publicist practically moaned with excitement. "You’re going to love it, LOVE IT I SAY~!" Bong stared with bewilderment as Lang clapped his hands together like an overly-caffeinated cheerleader having an orgasm. "What is it, another one of those daft t-shirts?" he asked, not really wanting to hear the answer at all. "No, but it DOES have to do with clothing!" Lang beamed. "You see Mr. Bong, before Professor Maybe handed you over to me, you were nothing more than a ball of clay. A poor, lonely ball of clay, waiting for someone to come along and mold you, sculpt you, caress you, fondle you, love you!" Perhaps Bong was a little homophobic. "You were a lost little lump of unrefinery, searching to discover what you truly were, looking for your place in the world!" "I uh… I don’t think ‘unrefinery’ is actually a word," Bong muttered. "NONSENSE~!" Lang shouted quite unnecessarily. "If you can say it, it’s a word! Anyway, ever since I began working with you, Mr. Bong, you have been transformed from a worthless, putrid nobody into a dashing, lemon-scented SOMEBODY~! You have metamorphed into a hero for the masses! Someone who stands up to men like Chandler Tsonda, men who are PURE EVIL~! We have your name out there and we’re slowly gathering a contingent of loyal fans, fans who are pledging their allegiance to the Forces of Stupid… there’s just one more thing to complete your image makeover! The coup de grâce, if you will!" Lang looked expectantly at Bong. Bong wasn’t going to ask—he really, really didn’t want to know. But he knew he was going to find out anyway, whether he liked it or not. "A NEW RING COSTUME~!" Lang finally burst with glee. "Oh for the love of God," Bong nearly wept to himself. He drearily covered his face with his hands as Lang continued. "You’ll love it Mr. Bong, absolutely LOVE IT~! A spangled leather jumpsuit with a flowing cape and your own superhero emblem on the front! I even picked out the colors myself! Lavender, cream, ivory, a dash of canary…" "Please, please, please let me either be dreaming or on crack," Bong breathed desperately through his fingers. "It is, dare I say, an absolute masterpiece! A harmonious marriage of leather, fabric, and sweat!" "Bloody brilliant, he wants me to wear a polygamous outfit," Bong groaned to himself. "The fans will go WILD, Mr. Bong, simply WILD~!" Lang finished with a lick of his lips. "It will be a vast improvement over those drab tuxedos you wear all the time—and exactly how you manage to go through life with such a tasteless wardrobe, I’ll never figure out. It’s time for the tuxedos to go ‘ta-ta!’" Bong gave Ears a backwards glance and wondered briefly if he could order him to kill Lang on the spot. "Are you mad!?" he spat a moment later. "I’m not letting you get rid of my tuxedos! I love my tuxedos! I absolutely refuse to wear that stupid outfit you’re talking about!" "Oh please give it a try, Mr. Bong," said Lang quietly. He reached into his pocket and procured a cloth tape measure. Slowly, he walked up to the near-seething Bong. "I assure you," he continued, "it will be simply… TO DIE FOR~!" With that, Lang quickly wrapped the tape measure around Bong’s neck, and began choking the life out of him! "GACK! EARS, HELP!" Bong croaked as he struggled against Lang. In a split second, Ears reached into the melee and grabbed Lang by the throat. He hoisted the smaller man up into the air and threw him against the wall with incredible ease. Lang fell to the ground, yelping in pain. Bong bent over with his hands on his knees, gasping for air. He didn’t know what was more astonishing—the fact that Lang nearly strangled him to death, or that Ears actually came to his rescue. "What the bloody hell was that all about, Lang!?" he demanded finally. Lang slowly got up to his knees. A horrified expression came over his face as he examined his hands. "A NAIL~! I’VE CHIPPED A NAIL~!" he wailed. "Forget your stupid nail, you just tried to kill me!" Bong snapped back. "I did no such thing, Mr. Bong!" Lang scowled, getting back to his feet with a flourish. "I was measuring your neck size for the GLORIOUS new costume I’m going to make for you! WHERE did you get the idea that I would EVER want to kill you!?" "You yelled ‘TO DIE FOR~!’ at the top of your lungs and nearly choked me to death with your tape measure, you raving crackpot!" "NONSENSE~! You’re being paranoid, Mr. Bong!" Bong and Lang stared each other down for a long, long moment. The room had fallen silent. Bong looked over his shoulder to see that even Gloucester had stopped his knife-throwing practice to watch what was going on. All at once, Bong felt very uncomfortable. He just wanted to leave, and he wanted to leave immediately. "Forget it, just forget it," he said hastily. "I’m getting out of here before anything else crazy happens." He bolted for the door, and nearly ran over Dr. Dennis Kent who happened to be coming in at exactly the same time. "Ack!" came Kent’s startled cry. "Oh m-my goodness, I’m sorry Jimmy… didn’t expect you t-to be r-running out of here in such a hurry." Bong felt a tinge of regret upon hearing the nervous, apologetic tone of Kent’s Piglet-like voice. He had almost stampeded the only man who truly didn’t seem to be trying to kill him, accidentally or otherwise. "No need for apologies, Dr. Kent," Bong sighed, dusting himself off. "I’m just anxious to get out of here. I feel like I’m just about going out of my mind…" He paused as Kent’s face darkened. His voice became steadier, almost demonic. "Is that so?" asked Kent with a devilish grin. "Then perhaps we can help put you out of your misery!" "What?" Before Bong could react, he felt his arms being restrained from behind. He frantically looked around and realized that he was being dragged over to a nearby bench by Gloucester and Lang, who were both laughing maniacally. Kent followed them, also laughing. "Help! HELP! EARS! EARS, WHERE THE BLOODY HELL ARE YOU!?" Bong screamed, as he was slammed back first onto the bench. "Your friend can’t help you now," Kent cackled. "Just look!" Gloucester grabbed Bong by the hair and roughly turned his head so he could see Ears lying on the ground in a puddle of his own blood. Sweetie was standing over him, dancing in tribal fashion on his chest while brandishing a bloody knife high above her head. "H-How the hell did Ears manage to get himself killed by a midget!?" Bong cried in disbelief. "Oh don’t worry," sneered Kent, reaching into his white lab coat. "You’ll have the chance to ask him soon enough." He pulled out a syringe, which was filled with a sinister-looking green liquid. It was also labeled with a skull and crossbones, which Bong knew couldn’t possibly have meant anything good. "Wh-What are you going to do to me?" Bong whimpered. His voice had been reduced to what Kent’s had been only a moment earlier. "What’s that?" "Oh, it’s a nasty little concoction of mine," he said offhandedly. "Quite lethal. It inflicts death in ten seconds… well, ten seconds after putting you through ten full minutes of the worst pain you’ve ever experienced in your life, that is. Now say goodbye, Mr. Bong!" Lang rolled up Bong’s left sleeve, and Kent shoved the needle into his arm… --- JIMMY BONG’S HOTEL SUITE (BACK IN REALITY) – CHICAGO VERY EARLY MORNING ON THE DAY OF ROUND THREE A FEW HOURS BEFORE DAWN, AT WHICH POINT OUR STORY TAKES AN UNUSUAL TURN FOR THE SERIOUS "NO, DON’T!" Bong awoke with a start. He thrashed about, still fighting and trying to get the imaginary forms of Kent, Gloucester and Lang away from him. After a moment, he realized that he was in his hotel room. He sat up in bed, heart pounding, breathing shallow, body drenched in cold sweat. The sinister faces that had just been hovering over him were gone. His left arm felt sore, but no lethal drug had been injected. Just a nagging injury, he suspected. The television was still turned on, bathing the room with its artificial crepuscule. The volume had been muted—the only other sound within the room was the rattling of an air vent in the corner by the floor. Bong had called hotel management several times to have it fixed, but to no avail. He figured they either didn’t like him, or they were excessively lazy. They probably just didn’t like him. Bong remained sitting up, surveying the room for several moments until he was satisfied that he was alone, safe, and unharmed. "Thank goodness," he gasped. "It was all just a horrible dream sequence." He fell back onto his bed. The mattress was a bit too rigid for his taste, but at least the pillows were quite comfortable. Bong looked over at his clock. There was still time to get a couple more hours of sleep if he tried. He felt around for the TV remote. His hand brushed over the maple nightstand sitting next to him, and found a folded piece of paper he’d put there earlier. It was a message that had been left for him when he arrived back at the hotel that evening. It simply read: "Meet me in my private box tomorrow evening, after your match. Good luck, and don’t lose—your life may depend on it. - X" Fantastic. As if matters weren’t muddled enough already, now Bong had a cryptic note from Xavier Pendable, the one man that Professor Maybe warned him to avoid at all costs. He’d spent nearly all the remainder of his evening pondering what Pendable wanted with him. The last words bothered him—your life may depend on it. What was that supposed to mean? Was it a warning or a threat? Perhaps Pendable was merely trying to lure Bong up to his "private box" so he could finish him off behind closed doors and have time to escape before Maybe or the police found out! Yes, that had to be it! But that didn’t completely make sense—if that were true, then why include a warning that Bong’s life was dependent upon the result of his match? Something wasn’t right, and Bong had the terrible feeling that the only way he’d get answers would be to comply with Pendable’s request. He wasn’t comfortable with it, but he would have to meet with him. Bong searched again for the remote, which he finally found underneath one the sheets—it must have gotten tangled there during all the thrashing and kicking, he figured. He clicked off the television, darkening the room with harsh immediacy. His stomach turned with dread as he stared into the blackness with only his thoughts for company—and that wasn’t very comforting. They were relentless, coming one after another in rapid succession, an unavoidable synapsal frenzy that would turn even Rip Van Winkle into a severe insomniac. Normally a pauper when it came to a penny for his thoughts, Bong now found himself in the undesirable position of a mental millionaire. There was plenty on his mind, naturally: His encounter with Tsonda was less than twenty-four hours away; whoever tried to murder him was still at large; Pendable’s note simply exacerbated the situation; Gloucester nearly impaled his head with a throwing knife… Wait. Gloucester nearly impaled his head with a throwing knife? "Hang on," he whispered to himself. "That’s right. Most of that dream actually happened this evening. Ugh, I must be going out of my mind…" He was correct. His mind had been racing so furiously all night that he’d managed to re-live the evening’s events in his sleep—with a few creative liberties of course. In true Jimmy Bong fashion, he really did mistakenly use the women’s restroom after his workout had ended. ("I just thought the sign was a picture of a man wearing a kilt, like in Scotland," he told himself.) Gloucester actually did almost throw a knife into his head. And, sadly, Lang most certainly professed his ambition of putting together a ridiculous costume for Bong to wear in his matches. "Damnit," he growled as that thought plodded through his head. Thankfully, Sweetie hadn’t killed Ears after all—Bong could hear his snores coming from the other room now. Furthermore, Kent hadn’t gone to the training facility to murder him. In fact, Kent hadn’t been there at all. Nobody had seen him since the attempt on Bong’s life, come to think of it. Why was that? He was a suspect in the case too, although Maybe insisted he was the least likely of the five to have done it. But why did he vanish all of a sudden? It didn’t make sense. "None of this makes sense," Bong mumbled in frustration as he rolled onto his side. "I’ve been out of the secret agent business for over a year. So why is this stuff happening to me? Bloody hell…" He wasn’t sure he would be able to get any more sleep that night. The whole world seemed to be against him. Back and forth, the questions desperately bounced around his addled brain. Who wanted him dead, and why? Who could he trust, if anyone? Couldn’t he just run away from this match and just disappear? Certainly not, if Pendable’s warning had any truth to it. Besides, he had to stay in the tournament. He had no other choice—he had nothing left to his name, which was exactly what enticed him to enter GTT5 under the sponsorship of Maybe Enterprises to begin with. It was either succeed and have enough money to get back on his feet, or fail and probably starve to death in the middle of a London street. But now it seemed more like fail and be killed for Lord knows what reason. It was a very lonely position to be in. Then again, Bong was used to this sort of solitude. Still, he wished there was at least someone he could confide in. He had no friends, and for all anybody knew no family either. He only had himself to rely on, and he knew that he wasn’t very reliable even to himself. "You were a lost little lump of unrefinery, searching to discover what you truly were, looking for your place in the world!" He was surprised to hear the words of Erik Lang of all people echoing in the back of his mind. Maybe there was some wisdom hidden deep behind his insane ramblings. All of his life, Bong never knew what it was to be a success on any sort of level. Not even in all of his years as a British spy. Then, he entered GTT4. He became—very unwillingly, but still he became—a professional wrestler. And in doing so, he’d done something he hadn’t before. He won. He didn’t win the whole tournament, but he made it far. Surprisingly far. And a year later, he was back in GTT5 doing the same thing again… wrestling and winning. Why couldn’t he just do that for a living? Perhaps Lang had a point. His whole life, Bong had been like a lost lump of clay, looking for his place in the world. He was waiting, searching to discover who he really was. Maybe he was never meant to be a spy. Maybe he was meant to be a wrestler. He didn’t have very much talent, but at least he was halfway decent at it. The question burned brightly in his mind for a brief moment—who was he, anyway? It fell away, back into the jumbled sea of anxiety that washed the farthest shores of his mind. There was no time to worry about that. Someone wanted him dead. Bong wanted answers. The only way he would get them would be to press on. Someone out there knew what was really happening… Someone out there knew… Someone… --- OHMCS HEADQUARTERS, N’S OFFICE – LONDON THAT SAME DAY EXACTLY THE SAME MOMENT, ONLY SEVERAL HOURS LATER DUE TO THE WHIMSICAL INSANITY OF TIME ZONES Somber clouds had been hanging over the skies of London for weeks now. N had not seen the uplifting rays of a sunrise in quite some time, but it was just as well. There was nothing cheerful about the phone call she was about to receive. A green telephone sat on her oaken desk, half-buried under the paperwork she had been sifting through all morning. The grandmotherly director of OHMCS—grandmotherly in stature, but by no means in disposition—was so incessantly absorbed in her labor that she nearly jumped out of her skin when the phone rang loudly, blasting away all traces of the tense quiet. She set aside her work and picked up the receiver after the second ring. "Hello?" "It’s me," came the voice of a man, with a distinctly American accent. "About bloody time," she said coldly. "I haven’t heard from you in days. What’s going on over there?" "There’s a complication. Bong is still alive." N closed her eyes. "I thought," she snarled through her teeth, "that you said you were going to take care of him. What the blazes went wrong?" "I had him right where I wanted him, but…" "Please, drop the accent," N sighed. "There’s no need to maintain your cover while you’re giving a status report." "Very well," the man’s voice changed to its native British accent. "As I was saying, I had him right where I wanted him. He was in my sights, but the idiot sneezed himself out of harm’s way just as I pulled the trigger. I couldn’t believe it! That man has more luck than the whole Irish nation combined!" "That’s a very, very pathetic excuse," N started. "I’m sure you know very well that Jimmy Bong has a knack for going on a run of stupid luck," the man protested calmly. "Anyway, things got worse from there. Stuart Maybe enlisted the help of a bodyguard to protect Bong. I can’t get near him." "A bodyguard?" N asked with an incredulous tone. "Balderdash. You’re an OHMCS operative. Surely you can handle a measly bodyguard." "It’s Ears, ma’am." N was taken aback by this news. "You’ve got to be joking," she rasped with mock amusement. "Bong and Ears, together? That news would make Garfeld turn over in his grave!" "It’s true, and as long as Ears is around, I can’t do anything to Bong… at least not without running the risk of blowing my cover. However, I have taken steps to ensure that Ears will be removed from the picture very soon." "You’re not going to try and kill him are you?" "No. That won’t be necessary, and I’m not that stupid. Maybe has launched an investigation to find out who tried to kill Bong. I’m using that to my advantage—he’ll never know it was me. Rest assured, the matter will be handled." "It had better be," N found her authoritative tone again. "Jimmy Bong is the lynch pin of Stuart Maybe’s entire operation. Eliminate Bong, and we stop Maybe dead in his tracks. We’ll prevent worldwide economic disaster. But we’re running out of time, so get it done… and quickly!" "Yes ma’am. Will that be all?" "Yes it is. I’ll expect to hear from you again soon, Double-Oh-Nothing." N placed the phone back on the receiver, and solemnly returned to the paperwork on her desk.
