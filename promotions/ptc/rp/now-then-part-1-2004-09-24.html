Now&Then Part 1

24 September 2004

Date: 2004-09-24 On a normal day in a normal situation most people wouldn’t be here. Who in their right mind would miss his own father’s funeral so he can compete in a second round, not even the final or semi final? Nick Persia Who would give up his father so he can save a little boy? Some wouldn’t even consider giving up their father. But Nick did. He gave up his father because his father would have wanted it is this way. Nick did this to give a young boy with a whole life in front of him a chance to life again. He gave the boy some hope again. He kept his future prospect hopeful. Nick Persia kept this young boy’s dream alive. Does this make Nick Persia a good man? Not at all. Anybody could have done it. Persia just fulfilled his father’s last match. He did what his father asked him to do. He did what his father wanted him to do. He did what was told him to do. The scene opens in a hospital. We see Nick Persia standing in a hospital. He’s sitting in a chair. He’s wearing a long black coat with a sweater underneath it. Also he is wearing blue jeans and black shoes. Underneath his eyes there could be some bags detected. A door opened and a doctor stepped out. It was a young man who had just graduated from college. He frowned as he looked on the paper. He chuckled while he was walking towards Nick. Persia noticed that the man was approaching him and stood up. “So you must be Nick Persia,” the doctor said extending his hand to Nick. “That’s how they call me,” Nick confirmed. “The operation with your father was successful,” the main said firmly “but it remains to be seen if the boy accepts his new kidney.” Nick Persia exposed a glance in his eyes expressing he was glad to hear that. “That’s great news,” Nick said edged “but when can I see my father?” “Well, I think they are ready with him in an hour,” the man estimated. He waved his hand as he turned around. While the doctor walked away Nick sat down again. His thoughts went from making the call to his sister to the round two match. As time passed by Nick fell asleep for several hours until he was waked by a nurse. “Are you alright, sir?” the nurse asked at a concerned tone. Nick scanned the environment with his eyes and gathered his thoughts. “Oh yes,” Nick said “thank you.” “I didn’t mean to cause any trouble,” the nurse explained “but I noticed you were sleeping for several hours so I thought…..” “It’s all right...” Nick spoke those words to comfort her “I appreciate your concern, but I am a big boy and I can take care of myself.” They stared at one another for a small second. “I guess I’ll leave you alone then,” she said “and I still have some work left to do.” Nick quickly replied “Can you tell me if I can see my father now?” “I think I can,” she said “but it would help if you told me who your father is.” “Max Persia is…..” Nick corrected himself quickly “I mean was his name.” She didn’t say anything, but instead typed his name into the computer behind the desk. Her eyes were moving from right to left in the eyes of Nick. In the meanwhile Nick waited impatient as he ticked with his finger on the desk. “Here it is,” she said “he’s in room 247. That’s on this floor. Just walk to the end of this hall and you’ll see the number on your right side.” “Thank you,” Nick said with a betraying smile on his face. As Nick walked to the room he got the feeling something was just not right…239….Each step he took was getting heavier. Each step he took made him wonder how his dad would look now……..241….243….Each step he took made him doubt about what he would tell him….245…The last words he would ever speak to his father…247…That was the room. He looked at the wooden door with the iron doorknob attached to it. While breathing heavily he brought his hand closer to the doorknob. His fingers surrounded the knob slowly. He tightened his grip and he opened the door open with one hard push. There his father lay, so peacefully with his eye closed. His wrinkles look so big now, but the look on his face told him he shook of all his troubles off him and he left this world for what it was. Absurdly or not, Nick experienced a sudden rush of joy what made him smile. The face of his father was a tad bit more pale than it looked normally. Nick walked towards him in a slow pass, taking a good look at his father. His eyes went from the top of his body all the way to where the blanket covered his body. With his right hand he grabbed the left hand of his father, closing the handshake with his left. When he was done with that he started to talk. “I guess this is it for you then. A new chapter begins for you, just like a new chapter begins for me. I never thought it would happen here.” Nick’s voice started to tremble as he continued. “Quite some time ago I remember seeing myself as a youngster. The people were saying that I would never become a professional wrestler. I heard them saying I should revise my future. But I kept my dream. I never thought I couldn’t do it. And you always remembered me that if I didn’t put the faith in myself nobody would.” Nick got tears in his eyes, but does not stop talking. “It was you who kept me going. It was you that convinced me to train each and every week until I just fell because I was so tired. You even signed me up for that show once ‘never give up’, and I’ll never ever forget that. I will not ever give up! Never did, never will do. But as far as my doubters go….” He stopped talking for a moment and he gathered his thoughts. It looked like he formed his words out of his mind. “…..I will prove them wrong again! I know people don’t give me a snowball’s chance in hell to beat this 4WW Legend Jerms. I know people didn’t really care about me or my matches. I know that Jerms is the favourite! But as we all know reputations are meant to be broken. Just like bones and rules! And I will do anything to get what I want. Just like I did, just like I’ve always done.” Nick coughed but quickly continued. “But this time I will show them why I was the one who received all the attention this past week on UWF! I got attacked by the likes of Kenny Mercury, Moraless and Seymour Almasy! But do you remember dad when I got attacked for the first time?” Nick stayed quiet like he expects an answer from his deceased father. “I was beaten into the hospital by some punk called Adien Pryde! I was in the hospital for two long weeks. That’s two weeks too long! I was some new guy walking into the federation full of aspirations, full of life and full of energy. But as time passed I found out not all people are fair and square. So when I was gathering my strengths I said to myself that I would never be surprised another day of my life!” He put the exclamation on life, as he found it very important to say and that his father ‘had to know’. “I returned to the UWF with glory with becoming the longest reigning The Hardcore Champion ever. It was not before then I first stood in the spotlight. It took me an injury to gain some attention. So I know one thing, you need to do something special to make it to top. You have to have something extra.” A smile appeared on Nick’s face while he told his story. “And now I have had to make a choice between you and the GTT 4 Tournament. It was hard for me, but I can do only one thing at a time. Unfortunately, I can’t do more. If this is the road I need to take then so be it. In some way I feel a bit guilty. In another way I am just picking up my life. Either way, I want you to know that I am doing this for you.” Tears rolled out of Nick’s eyes. “You always said if that I was the only one who could make my dreams come true. You always said that I must find my spot on this world. And I found it. You know how much trouble I had in the past! I couldn’t make any decisions, but since then I’ve learned a lot in every way. And I’m planning on doing just that!” Nick started to whisper instead of talking. “I will take Jerms down. And I tell you why! Because I PROMISE you I will do this for you. I promise you. And as you know I am a man of my word. This is a promise I intend to keep. This one is for you, Dad” His voice became louder again as he yelled the following words. “Long live the dead!” Nick pulled back his arms and his father’s hand slipped out of his. The tears continued to fall of his face. He got to his feet again and took one last look at his father. “Goodbye,” was all he said as he turned around. The pace was quick when he walked away. He had just one thing on his mind leaving the room. And that is to keep a promise.
