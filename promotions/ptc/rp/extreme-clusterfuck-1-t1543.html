Extreme Clusterfuck #1

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Asesino Affiliated Pimp Joined: 06 Feb 2003 Posts: 6 Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 10:20 am Post subject: Extreme Clusterfuck #1 (The scene slowly fades to a dark room. The camera pans around the darkness, suddenly the camera falls. The cameraman has tripped over something in the black room. The camera picks up him cursing as he lifts the camera to his shoulder. He turns and there stands Asesino. The cameraman jumps back again and lets out a sharp yelp.) Asesino: Shhhh..... You might wake the dead. (Asesino smirks at him and walks past the cameraman to sit on a chair in the corner. In the darkness, the camera can only pick up the reflection of the sunglasses Asesino is wearing. Asesino sits for a few seconds then begins to speak again.) Asesino: Many, many questions have been directed my way lately. Why did you leave Limp Bizkit in the middle of your match is one most commonly asked of all. Well, to clear the minds of you people who wonder, I believe I will answer that. And my answer, it is none of your damn business why I did what I did at the house show. But to make a few things clear, I'm not a coward. Many people have been calling me a coward since the house show but I'm sorry to tell all of you guys that is the last thing I am. (Asesino pauses, adding that extra emphasis to his words to make each and every person watching, know that he is being 100% serious. After that brief pause, Asesino continues his speech.) See I've been called lot of things in my life but I have never been called a coward. For the people who don't know what a coward is. A coward is one who lacks courage or shows shameful fear. I'm sorry but that's just not me. If you think about it, I'm the total opposite of a coward. I do not show fear because I fear nothing... Fear. See everyone deals with it in his or her lives except me. Fear of failure, fear of conforming, fear of becoming just another number to the all-powerful government. See this does not faze me at all. I have never felt fear and I never will. See nothing scares me; Fear of Failure has never even crossed my mind. Because I lose a match or a title, doesn't make me a failure. One of many definitions of failure is a lack of success. See I have succeeded in XWF like no other. Yea, yea, yea... C.R. is the closest one to my success but everyone knows that him holding the title is just a fluke. (There’s a gleam in the former European Champion’s eyes, as he leans forward, staring deep into the camera, with his trademark smile starting to return to his face. He suddenly breaks free of his hypnotic grip, before leaning backwards again and sitting in a more relaxed pose.) And talking about success. Many people say that they will win the Extreme Clusterfuck at New Era but I'm sorry to tell you guys but only one can win the Extreme Clusterfuck. See after New Era, a shot at the PTC Extreme title match lies ahead for me. PTC will SO graciously grant me a shot. But again the odds stacked against me. See they put me in this match against all the top stars from other federation. For the people who aren't so good in math. Like Kai Shinobi and Necro Wraith. (Asesino pauses, as if to add emphasis to the names. He laughs to himself while shaking his head, and then continuing on.) That means I have to go through many people. Hmm... Can I do it? Yes I can do it. I can beat many people with ease. All my life the odds have been stacked against me. But did I worry? NO!! Did I overcome the odds? YES!! Will I overcome the odds at New Era? YES!! Can anyone stop me? NO!! So everyone in the Extreme Clusterfuck, on New Era you will feel my pain!!!! (With those final comments, Asesino simply stares into the camera with a evil look on his face. Asesino then stands upright and looks to the left and then the right, before walking off into the deeps of the dark room.) (The scene cuts to an empty parking lot of an abandoned warehouse. The camera pans around, looking for any sign of movement, but do not prevail. Suddenly, a silhouette of man walks underneath a lone streetlight and stands before the camera. The camera pans into the figure’s face and is revealed to be the one and only, Asesino. In place of his usual smile, He wears a disgusted look upon his face. Upon his body, his long black trenchcoat lightly rests as the wind has died down. Asesino brushes a few strands of hair away from his dark black sunglasses, then he looks into the camera.) Asesino: The question on my mind right now is, where do I begin? Where do I begin talking about someone as pathetic as C.R.? I'm sure there must be somewhere for me to start... (Asesino leans back onto the streetlight, revealing his plain black shirt with the words "Corporation" written on the front in red letters with blood dripping off. He briefly smiles but it is just a little different then his normal trademark smile. It looks more like an evil smirk. As he carries on.) Asesino: C.R., I just want to tell you great win at Anniversary. You really deserved it... but only thing is I don't think it was the smartest thing you could have done. I mean as you could see at the end of the house show. The Corporation left you lying in the middle of the ring in puddle of your own blood. See that is the only beginning of what is to come. C.R. not only do you have yours truly trying to get ahold of you, you also have Limp Bizkit and the rest of the Corporation wanting to kick your punk a$$. As you can see we always get what we want.... (Asesino shoves both hands deep into the pockets of his trenchcoat in a vain attempt to warm them up somewhat from the cool, chilly night air. He takes a deep breath and lets out a slight sigh as he slowly begins to speak again) Asesino: And one thing we want the most of all is the XWF World Title. See we don't care how or even who gets it but I promise you this we will get it soon enough and not only will we get the world title. We will get the Corporate, Xtreme, and the Lightheavyweight titles. But wait... why am I telling you this? I mean come on you don't have a chance in HELL to beat Limp Bizkit at New Era. I guess what I'm trying to say is the winner of the Anniversary match should have been someone who can take the world title from DJ, not a little coward like you. See I should have had a title shot a long time ago but now I finally get it at Payback. The whole time I was kicking everyone's a$$ like it was nothing. I was throwing people over like it was nothing but then you messed the whole thing up. As you all know you stole the XWF Title from DJ. I'm telling you now you will feel the wrath of the Corporation. (After seemingly getting slightly worked up over the fact that the match from Anniversary didn't end exactly the way he planned.) Asesino: Now going back to C.R., It seems like Mr. Charles Stevenson doesn't seem to like you. I mean he put you against DJ at Anniversary, against Limp Bizkit at New Era and against me at Payback. That's just like sending the pig to the slaughterhouse. Those matches should have been total massacres. I'm going to tell you all how it goes down. DJ and Limp Bizkit soften you up. So that leaves you and me, C.R. in the middle of the ring at Payback. You'll go for the "Rigido Stunner" but to no avail. I'll duck and nail you with the most vicious move in XWF, The Asesino Unprettier. After that, I'll go to the outside and grab my trusty steel chair. I'll get back in and nail you right in your fucking head. Just like in the past. With any luck you'll be layed out in the middle of the ring in a puddle of your own blood once again. (After a brief pause to add extra emphasis to his words, Asesino begins to continue again....) Asesino: I have one more thing to say to you. I promise you this I will make your life a living hell.... AND THAT'S THE LAST WORD PUNK!!!! (With those final words Asesino walks off into the night as the camera fades to black.) _________________ Former XWF Lightheavyweight Champion Corporation member and along with Enishi we will win the Tag Team Title Tournament to become the XWF Tag Team Champions
