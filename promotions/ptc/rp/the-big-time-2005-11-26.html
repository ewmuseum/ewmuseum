THE BIG TIME

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 "I think this is a big mistake, boss." exclaimed Belial of one of the premier wrestling families of Efed Entertainment, when talking about his acceptance into the GTT5. On November 24th Belial Kahn received word of his being accepted into the world famous GTT5 tournament at WWA's headquarters in Minnesota. "I'm still like how did this happen." finishing off his RedBull and returning to his dead lift reps.It is been rumored that Kahn can bench press about 900 lbs . "It didn't hit me how big this was until I arrived at the PTC headquarters to finish all the legal stuff." As he continued his reps with sweat pouring off his massive frame.At 7'5 one would not expect to have a giant feel like the under dog but as Kahn says himself."This is the first time i have ever felt like I am the under dog here, this guy is associated with a winning team and this is my first run ever in something like this, so honestly I am nervous as all hell." "But , It's either do or die, and right now I want to live it up." Even the management team of Belial is well aware of the up hill battle that they face in there first opponent at the PTC classic GTT5."He is going to have to be one hell of a focused warlord these next month or two in order to make as big an impact as I know he can."stated Weslie Buchanon , manager of the giant Kahn. "Being 7'5 and over 500 plus pounds is not all that will get him over with the crowd and organization." "This guy Jones is associated with some serious handlers, who know how to get things done and done right." Buchanon refers to the managers of the new Shanty Jones , former Military man and English broker turned wrestler. "With all do respect , it's a new year and a new day and who really knows , sometimes the best comes out of those whom are meet with impossible odds." Where most managers would slack or crumble in underestimating there opponents skill in the ring as a rookie, Buchanon feels otherwise. "We definitely need to bring the A game to be successful and if I have my way Kahn will bring his A game full blast to GTT5." as he closed the doors of his brand new 2005 BMW."Expect that and nothing less.Its Warr Time."
