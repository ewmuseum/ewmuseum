The Not-So-Thin Blue Line

Primetime Central presents: CW2! Final Round Matches | Rules | Player Login | Roleplay Board | Discussion | PTC Website Author: Tsunami Oni Fed: UWF Match vs:: Cereal Killer Title: The Not-So-Thin Blue Line Date: 15-05-2005 Location: The suburbs ofModesto, CA Round: 3 Roleplay So much has been said about the human capacity to endure pain. But does anyone ever talk about the human capacity to FEEL pain? Pain tells the body when it is experiencing something that could be dangerous to it. Does it really take that much to do that? Why is the nervous system capable of transmitting so much agony to the human mind that people cry out for death to take them away? Was our creator that sadistic? These are the questions going through the mind of a solitary runner. Each step feels like a stake is being hammered into the bones of his leg. His lungs are being turned into old newspapers left out in the sun… dried and crumbling at the merest touch. Each and every cell in his body screams for him to stop. But he can’t. Not with the Devil behind him. “C’mon, Flabby! Get your ass in gear!” Tsunami Oni urges the chubby member of his Lil’ Devils fan club on. The trio of tykes volunteered to represent the wheelchair-bound Vivaldi of Violence at CW2 against the so-called Cereal Killer after the Youngbloods severely damaged his legs. Oni was impressed with their heart and agreed. But it takes more than that to win a match. That’s why Tsunami Oni has his wheelchair tethered to the near-exhausted Flabby as he pounds the pavement in suburban Modesto. “Mister… Oni…” pants Flabby. “Please… let… me… stop…” “No!” snaps the blue-haired taskmaster. Not to be confused with Kevin Sullivan. He was blond. How did that freaky runt get to nail Woman, anyways? “Please!” begs Flabby. “Hmmmm… this calls for extreme measures.” Tsunami Oni takes hold of a fishing pole he has on his wheelchair. Hooked to the line is a Zinger. For those of you out of the loop, a Zinger is like a Twinkie except it has a frosting roof on it. The roof covers up where they injected the frosting into the sponge cake. The Peanuts gang pimped Zingers, which is almost as sad as those Met Life commercials with Snoopy. Did Charles Schultz need the money that bad? Oni dangles the Zinger in front of Flabby who starts running for it with all the energy he has. The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea smiles with satisfaction as the travel down the street. But suddenly they come to a stop. “What’s wrong, Flabby?” asks Oni. “There’s… a… wall… in… the… street…” Flabby gasps. “A wall?” Tsunami Oni peers around Flabby’s bulk. Sure enough, there’s a massive stone wall across the road. “Oh no,” moans Oni. “What… is… it…?” asks a breathless Flabby. “It’s a Writer’s Block,” Oni explains. “Once that shows up, there’s no way around it. Sometimes you start out pretty good, then a Writer’s Block just pops up and ruins everything. It’s the bane of every wrestler. Well, we might as well head back, Flabby.” “Can… I… have… the… Zinger…?” “Of course, Flabby. Of course.” And man and fat kid start on the lonely road back home… Time Remaining 0 days 0 hours 0 minutes ~ Promos ~ © primetimecentral.net 2005. Site Header and Splash images by [name withheld].
