From Russia With Disdain - Part I

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 OHMCS ON-SITE HEADQUARTERS - CONFERENCE ROOM NOT LONG AFTER JIMMY BONG'S SECOND-ROUND MATCH SOMETIME BETWEEN 12:01 AM AND 11:59 PM "Amazing, Double-Oh-Nothing, simply amazing! How the bloody hell did you manage to defeat Luna Morbid?" U, the head of OHMCS Weapons Research & Development, stared at Jimmy Bong with astonishment as they stood in the main conference room of the OHMCS secret on-site headquarters, hidden deep beneath the men's restroom of the MCI Center in Washington, D.C. (Well, one of the men's restrooms, at least. It would be rather silly to have only one restroom for an entire arena.) Bong, standing alongside a finished pine table, which ran most of the room's length, closed his eyes for a moment to collect his thoughts. U was situated at the table's head, his back to a large white screen on the wall directly behind him. He awaited Bong's reply. "Well," he said after a moment, "that's a very good question, U. I honestly have no bloody idea how I beat Morbid, just as I'm equally baffled as to how I beat Montgomery!" "It's inexplicable," U mused, "that a complete idiot like you has managed to survive both a world champion and a tortured, dangerous killing machine." "What's even more inexplicable is that all footage of both of those matches have been destroyed forever, so we'll never know what happened!" Bong added. "Although, I think I managed to beat Morbid in the same fashion that she destroyed those poor chaps she was forced to train with. At some point, I lost consciousness, had some strange daydream, and then when I woke up, I had won!" U cocked an eyebrow. "I see. Dare I ask what the daydream was about?" "Hrm," thought Bong aloud. "I believe it was about that one time I accidentally spilled a beaker full of some strange chemical directly on your lap." U frowned. "Double-Oh-Nothing, that wasn't a daydream! That actually happened! AN HOUR AGO!" "Oh," said Bong. "Well, then I guess I really have no idea how I won. By the way U, were there any side effects from the chemical spill?" "Not really," said U, now sitting down across the table from Bong. "Except, it seems that I've somehow managed to develop a severe case of feminine itching." Bong's face screwed up with confusion as U continued. "Anyway, have a seat, Double-Oh-Nothing. The conference call with N's office in London will begin any moment now." Bong nodded, and took his seat. He quietly sighed as he pondered the current situation: Garfeld was still out there somewhere, drawing closer to enacting his fiendish plot to conquer the world with each passing second. Time was running out, and to make matters worse, the investigation was going nowhere. Bong had been entered into GTT4 because OHMCS suspected there was some connection between Garfeld's plan and the tournament, but as far as he could tell there wasn't much of a connection at all. Sure, one of Garfeld's hired goons tried to eliminate him recently, but that certainly didn't necessitate a connection with a simple wrestling tournament. Either he was missing something, or he was being led on a wild goose chase. If the latter was the case, he was certainly not amused. Bong was growing weary of being forced to compete with professional wrestlers for the sake of global security, especially with the knowledge that he was very lucky to escape both of his matches without breaking his neck -- let alone actually winning. Could he sustain this string of astounding good fortune much longer? "I'd rather not have to find out," Bong mumbled to himself. "Having my neck broken in a wrestling match is most certainly not on my list of things to do." Bong reached into his pocket and pulled out a piece of paper, which read "My List of Things To Do." To Bong's surprise, "Having my neck broken in a wrestling match" was listed near the top. "Bloody hell, I thought I crossed that off!" he said, taking a pen out of his pocket and making the correction. At that moment, the big white screen on the wall flickered to life, displaying the face of N, the head of OHMCS. Bong cringed at the sight -- her visage was so greatly magnified that the liver spots on her wrinkly face were the size of dinner plates. There was also a large mole on her left cheek with hair growing out of it. "Greetings U, Bong," she said, nodding towards both of them in turn. "I trust you are both doing well?" "For the most part," said U, reaching under the table and scratching himself vigorously. "I'm slightly nauseated," groaned Bong, trying not to look directly at N. "Well, you'll have to get it together and listen to what I have to say, Double-Oh-Nothing," N replied sternly. "We're withdrawing your name from the tournament." Bong's eyes widened. "Really!?" he exclaimed. "Oh goody! I was rather dreading the thought of having to fight again, especially since it's highly probable that my opponent in the next round would have destroyed me. Why, it's not far-fetched at all to think that I might have been horribly disfigured, or paralyzed for life!" "Yes Bong, I'm sorely disappointed too," N sighed. "Disappointed? But I didn't say--" "Anyway," said N, cutting off Bong, "we're pulling you out because we've received some new information from the American government. The CIA has been trying to track down Garfeld as well, and they believe he's somewhere in Washington, D.C. just as we thought. However, it now appears he has nothing to do with the GTT4 tournament, or any of its participants, as we initially believed." "That is according to CIA intelligence reports?" asked U. "Correct," N answered. "And you know that I prefer relying on information gathered by our own agents, but this particular intelligence has high credibility. Plus, given the severity of the crisis we're facing, I would be most unwise to look a gift horse in the mouth." "So, Garfeld is hiding here in Washington," Bong mused. "Does the CIA have any idea what his fiendish plot is?" N frowned. "They were much less cooperative on that point, I'm afraid," she grumbled. "My intuition tells me they do know, but they don't seem so keen on sharing that information with us. In all likelihood they're trying to keep something hidden. As to what that might be, or why they're keeping it from us, I have no idea." "Probably something that would greatly embarrass them," scoffed U. "Probably," N agreed. "Regardless, I want you working with the CIA on this, Bong. We've wasted enough time keeping you in this tournament, and all the while Garfeld has been drawing closer to his goal." "You can count on me!" said Bong cheerfully. "Actually, no, I can't count on you, Bong," said N. "Because lest you forget, you're an utter moron." "Oh yeah? Well prove it, then!" Bong retorted. "Go on!" "You thought that fake courier who tried to kill you was from London, even though he clearly spoke only Spanish!" N exclaimed. "Oh come now N," offered Bong with a stupid grin on his face, "anyone can make that mistake!" After a moment of silence, his grin faded. Bong looked sheepishly about the room, and scribbled a note on the back of his list of things to do. "For future reference," he muttered to himself, "Londoners generally don't speak Spanish as their native language." "Anyway, as I explained to you at the beginning of this mission, Double-Oh-Nothing," said N, "the only reason I put you on this case is because you were the only Double-Oh-Thing available at the time. I'd sooner send a mentally challenged rock to save the world than you, but sadly our mentally challenged rock was killed in the line of duty six months ago." "Fair enough," said Bong. "But N, I wonder about something... if Garfeld has nothing to do with the tournament, then how is it that our own agents acquired intelligence indicating otherwise?" "How the hell should I know?" barked N. "There's no time to worry about that right now! What's important is we now know that Garfeld is somewhere in Washington, and the CIA seems to be close to finding and stopping him!" That answer didn't satisfy Bong. There was certainly nothing wrong about his OHMCS superior being focused solely on the mission at hand. However, the idea that OHMCS had received faulty intelligence bothered him. Were they purposely led to believe that Garfeld had a connection with GTT4? And if so, to what end? It didn't make any sense, and it looked as if N wasn't terribly concerned about that -- a fact which bothered him further. Aside from that, Garfeld already tried to have Bong killed! Why would Garfeld go that trouble, if Bong hadn't even been on the right track to stopping him? So many questions, and no apparent answers... "So," N continued, "you will give the CIA your full assistance, Bong. I want OHMCS representation on this..." N was interrupted abruptly, as the door to the conference room burst open. Bong and U nearly jumped out of their seats in surprise, as a tall, slender man, wearing a dark suit, tie and sunshades, briskly walked into the room, waving a piece of paper in the air. He was followed immediately by Majorly Shafted (Bong's human shield, as you may recall), who was frowning quite fiercely at the man. "What the hell is this!?" N shouted angrily. "Who let this man through without authorization!?" "Put a sock in it N, I have all the authorization you need right here!" the man snapped back with a distinctly American dialect. He held up the document in his hand, which U immediately snatched and began to read. "I tried to stop him, ma'am," said Shafted, saluting N. "But he just walked right past us, saying something about emptying out the place and shutting down operations." "Shutting down operations?" N repeated, confused. "What's going on? I demand an explanation!" The American man removed his badge from his pocket and displayed it to the room. "Special Agent Walter Durnstrom, Central Intelligence Agency. I have official U.S. Government orders to close this place down." "He's right," said U, now finished reading the document. "That's what it says here, an executive order to expel British intelligence operations from this city." "That's preposterous!" N exclaimed indignantly. "On what grounds, Durnstrom!?" "Look," Durnstrom began, "I know our offices have been communicating with each other over the Garfeld threat, right? Well, the United States has everything under control, and we're going to catch Garfeld before he carries out his plan. Things would be a lot easier though, if you," he pointed at N, "would stop pestering us about petty little details and let us do our job!" "Petty details!" said N unbelievingly. "I wanted to know exactly how Garfeld is planning to take over the world! His fiendish plot is not a petty detail!" "All you need to know," Durnstrom seethed through his teeth, "is that we'll take care of things. And we'll do it without you. You've been hindering our progress with your constant nagging, even though we've been gracious enough to allow you to carry out secret British operations on American soil at this facility. Well, you've stepped on our toes once too many times, so we're kicking you out. You have until tomorrow morning to clear everyone out of the building, and out of the city." "Out of the city!?" exclaimed Jimmy Bong, rising from his seat now. "Madness! You can't just..." "I don't recall asking you for your opinion!" Durnstrom sneered. "And we most certainly can. I should also add that we're going to be keeping an eye out for you, or any other British operative, and if we so much as catch one of you putting a toe over the city limits, you'll be arrested!" Bong, U, and Shafted all exchanged looks. N appeared to be thinking intensely. "So," said N, after a moment, "what you're saying is, none of our personnel will be allowed in Washington?" "That's right," said Durnstrom. "Until we've apprehended Garfeld and put a stop to his scheme." "Well then, I guess that means you can stay in the city, Bong," said N with a smile. Bong turned to N, confused. "What? What are you--" "Because Agent Bong here has tendered his resignation, which I have accepted and will go into effect immediately," she finished. Bong's eyes went wide. "N, have you lost your mind!?" he nearly shrieked. "I did no such--" U practically dove across the table to cover Bong's mouth with his hand, catching on to what N was thinking. "Shut up, Double-Oh-Nothing!" he hissed. "Therefore," N went on, "as Bong is no longer an agent of the British government, he is entitled to stay in Washington, just like any other civilian." Durnstrom guffawed. "HA! I'm afraid you're wrong! Mr. Bong still has to leave the city! He doesn't have any civilian business here!" "He certainly does, Agent Durnstrom!" replied N. "Didn't you know? He's taking part in a professional wrestling tournament here!" Bong was flabbergasted. "That's not...mmmmph!" U covered Bong's mouth again. Durnstrom sneered at Bong. "You're kidding! This shrimp has the athletic build of... of... well, a shrimp, like I said. I'll have to confirm that what you're saying is true, but if that's the case, then I guess it's alright if he stays. But the rest of you had better scram by sunrise tomorrow!" With that, Durnstrom turned on his heel and briskly walked out of the conference room. "Shafted, I want an investigation conducted to determine how Durnstrom got past OHMCS security," N said quickly. "Yes ma'am," said Shafted, excusing himself from the room. Jimmy Bong looked utterly distraught. "N, why the bloody hell did you say I resigned from OHMCS!? What's all this nonsense about my still being in the GTT4 tournament!? I thought you said I was pulling out!" "Not anymore, Bong," said N. "If the Americans are forcing us out of Washington, then they definitely have something they don't want us to know. That's why I definitely want you to stay here, so you can try and figure out exactly what that is." "But you just said I--" "I was lying, Bong," N continued, rolling her eyes. "If the CIA thinks you're no longer working for the British government, then they won't arrest you. Furthermore, as long as you're still participating in that tournament, then you won't be kicked out of the city. You're the only shot we have at locating Garfeld, and uncovering what the Americans are keeping hidden from us. Therefore, Bong, it's more vital than ever that you win your next match!" "How am I supposed to that?" asked Bong. "OHMCS isn't even allowed in the city anymore! How am I supposed to get help?" "Shafted will still be protecting you," said N. "Although he'll have to be heavily disguised. I'm afraid we can't risk letting any more personnel remain here other than Shafted. U and the rest of his staff will be pulling out of Washington immediately." "This is all starting to sound like some sort of poorly-written spy novel," Bong remarked. N turned her head to the side, as if somebody in her office was talking to her off-screen. She was handed a piece of paper, and turned her head back as she read the message. "This is interesting," she said after a few moments. "I've just received word from the Russian government. They're pursuing Garfeld as well, and they want to assist us. Seems the Americans have also been giving them the cold shoulder, and they think they may know why." "You mean the Russians know what the Americans are trying to keep from us?" asked Bong. "Maybe," replied N. "I'm going to find out exactly what assistance they're willing to offer us. Meanwhile, Bong, you should go get into shape. After all, you have a wrestling tournament to win! Or at the very least, remain part of until we get Garfeld." "Uh... right. Fantastic," Bong muttered sadly. "Right then, end transmission," N said, as the screen flickered off. Bong sighed. Things were continuing to get stranger by the minute. It seemed as if he'd gotten his wish, and that he would no longer have to continue with GTT4. As it turned out, however, it was now imperative that he remained in the competition. On top of that, several questions plagued his mind. Where did the faulty intelligence come from? What information were the Americans withholding from them? Why did Garfeld try to have him killed if the investigation was going entirely in the wrong direction? And now, what did the Russians have to do with all of this? But most importantly... where is Garfeld? And what the bloody hell is his plan? So many questions, but no answers yet... and time was running out.
