Who is Virgil Ambrosi?

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 Cue up "Vampire Killer"… which for those who don't know is the background music for the first stage in the classic NES game Castlevania and for the Grim Reaper stage in the just as classic Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse. Fade into a headshot of the mysterious Global Wrestling star. Announcer V/O: Who is Virgil Ambrosi? For that matter, who really cares? MAGGOT V/O: I do, ya douche. AV/O: Oh, well duh, you are facing him. MAGGOT V/O: Who hired you anyways, Professor Tremendous? AV/O: No, this is PTC. I follow Curious Rules. MAGGOT V/O: *grumbles* AV/O: Anyway, who is Virgil Ambrosi? For those of us who care, he is a vampire! Cut to a shot of Max Schreck as Count Orlok in the classic film Nosferatu. AV/O: A creature of the night! Cut to Gary Oldman as Count Dracula from Bram Stoker's Dracula. AV/O: A… running wrestling joke? Cut to Leslie Nielsen as the infamous count in Dracula: Dead and Loving It. AV/O: No matter how you slice it, Maggot will be dealing with an undead spirit that feasts on the blood of the living. And let's face it, vampires are a different breed. You need special means to defeat them. Will Maggot topple him with holy water? Cut to a shot of a baptismal pool in a Catholic Church. AV/O: Will he use a stake right through the heart? Cut to a still of a wooden hammer and stake. AV/O: Or will he do it by pwning the crap out of him with a chain link whip? Cut to a moving shot from Castlevania of Simon Belmont going apeshit on zombies, vampires and bats with his whip. MAGGOT V/O: Nah kid, nah. I ain't gonna need none of that fancy crap to beat up on no vampire. Vampire schmampire, he ain't no different than any other wrestler I done faced before. He may feast on blood rather than bleed it, but he still feels pain. Cut to Emoticon delivering Chivalry's Last Stand, a leaping enziguiri to Ambrosi. MAGGOT V/O: And if he thinks he felt a good thrashin' before… Cut to Emoticon delivering a cross body block into a ground 'n pound offensive to Ambrosi. MAGGOT V/O: Well then, wait 'til he gets a load of me. "Vampire Killer" fades out, cue up Nirvana, "Hairspray Queen." Cut to Maggot delivering the Trash Compactor, an electric chair drop, to Lindsay Troy. Cut to Maggot driving Freakfish's skull into the canvas with the Driver Fantastique, a lightning quick tilt-a-whirl tombstone piledriver, in the harsh environments of a Hardcore Hell match. Cut to Maggot backing Troy Windham into a corner and thrashing him with lefts and rights. Cut to Maggot on the apron with Spoiler, delivering him the Mutilator, a chicken wing chinbreaker, that knocks him off the apron. MAGGOT V/O: And that's that! AV/O: But for good measure… Cue "Vampire Killer" back up, cut to an old-school NES screen that looks like Castlevania, but instead of Simon or Trevor Belmont, it's an 8 bit sprite of Maggot. Instead of a whip, he's pwning the zombies, bats and vampires with his bare hands all through a murky castle stage. Maggot advances up to the boss, Virgil Ambrosi in sprite form. After a brief scuffle, Maggot-sprite drives Ambrosi-sprite into the cold, stone castle floor with a Trash Compactor, vanquishing Ambrosi-sprite. The screen fades to the GTT5 logo.
