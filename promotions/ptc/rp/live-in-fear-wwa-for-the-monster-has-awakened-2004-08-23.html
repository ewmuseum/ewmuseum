Live In Fear WWA For The Monster Has Awakened

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 This is it, the big stage has finally arrived, the stage to demonstrate and awaken fans to real wrestling. I team up with men I do not trust but men who desire success like I do. Imagine it, the best of the PTC teaming together in their name of their home federations to decide what federation is the undisputed, best of PTC. This is my chance, my time to light up the word with real wrestling, there may never be another chance like this, ever again. Yet as determined as I am, as focused as I am there is still one black cloud over head which may mess with my judgement, I still remember the time Cyrus Skar hit me with that steel chair, I still remember the coldness crashing into my cranium and I still crave for revenge. I have to choose between saving wrestling and personal animosity, my heart is torn between two desires, two addictions. ---- The time is getting closer as the days go by, the big PTC survivor show. Normally, as a closet wrestling fan, I would be looking forward to this great event where the best federation in the PTC collide to find out who is the undisputed best but this time there is a problem. Representing the World Wrestling Alliance is my master, Mr. Darkness. Since the creation of the World Wrestling Alliance I have been an avid fan, and I truly do believe it is the best federation in the PTC but it was a bad decision to include my master in the PTC Team. As of late he has been a troubled man, to allow him to compete in his current way is suicidal and the responsibility of clearing this problem rests on my shoulders. I must save him, for the good of the WWA. ---- I need saving. I need a hero. I need saving from myself, I need hope, inspiration, I need a miracle. I can cry out for help as much as I want but I see no hero flying in my direction, no cape flowing in the wind. You see there is more than just two enemies clawing at one another to make my heart decide, there is a third. ---- At first when I heard of my master’s inclusion I knew there would be two things fighting inside of him, his hatred for Cyrus Skar and his desire to save wrestling. Then suddenly I saw it creeping in the shadows, he had been there all along yet it was only now that I really opened my eyes and because of this the third enemy has grown to be big and strong. --- The third enemy is doubt… ---- This big and strong enemy lurking in the shadows goes by the name of doubt… --- So what can I do to surpass this enemy? After all these years, through the good and the terrible I have never had this enemy challenge me, it never reared it’s ugly head and brought the fight to me. And why would this enemy advance anyway? Why am I in such doubt? Is it because I have only won two matches in my entire wrestling career or is it something else, something way back when that now so many know about yet no one understands…not even me. ---- Buy why doubt? My master has never doubted his abilities before, he’s one of the most confident men I know. I know at first the losing streak really aggravated my master but still he never doubted his talent, yet perhaps if I look closer, perhaps if I think back to those nights where he sat by himself in darkness, perhaps that demon was always there. Perhaps only now though it is big enough and strong enough to defeat my master, perhaps my master has met an opponent he cannot beat. ---- I still remember their faces. The faces of friends and family who screamed in pain and agony as they looked out at me with shock and horror in their eyes. Those that I had attacked lay still with one last fleeting look in my direction. I still can see the images of my mother, limp in the arms of my father, they both looked at me through the flames that engulfed the house I had been brought up in. They looked at me as if I had changed, they looked at me as if I had grown suddenly into a monster. Then there’s Fred. He was an old bitter man that lived at the end of my road, he spent his days moaning and complaining about anything and everything. Yet there was something different about his reaction to me. ---- I remember the recent promo he cut at the place where he grew up. Here was the man I have served for so many years, the man who was confident in his ventured to the business world and even into the wrestling word, and he was crying. The past will always haunt him, the traitorous acts of which he did to friends and family will never go away and now it seems to be destroying him. Some would say he deserves it, some would say he’s a monster but I beg to differ. ---- At first it confused me, sure those memories always played with my mind. Sometimes they made me drown in a sea of despair and others time they made me fume with rage but never doubt myself. I never killed them, the monster did it. The monster that controlled me that terrible night, the monster which I have kept away for so long…the monster that gets stronger every waking minute, the monster that is now beating me once again. Look at team WWA, some of the best wrestlers around today, men that can take on the representatives of any other PTC federations, men that put all their trust in me. They put their trust in ME, yet as the Survival event nears it looks more certain that they will not be teaming up with me, they will be teaming up with a monster. An uncontrollable monster addicted to watching those that wrongly trust it suffer, sorry Muppet Man, JMA and Desolation, and more importantly. Sorry Mr. Darkness… ---- Something’s wrong, I can smell it in the air. My master’s eyes have changed, dramatically. They seem angered, like a raging ball charging at a daring Spaniard. The eyes don’t belong to him, I’m sure of it. It’s as if something has awakened inside of him, another being, another creature, a monster.
