This Is Who I Am

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-12 Perhaps you should waltz over to your most comfortable of chairs and enjoy the warmth of it while you partake in the listening of my life. Take off your shoes and kick up your feet while you become the audience for once and hear my words, instead of your own. Hold your breath and close your eyes, because you are in for one hell of a ride. You see, I never claimed to be perfect. In fact, I’m nothing close to it. I’ve made my mistakes in the past, but I’m smart enough to correct them. And I know better to not make the same mistake twice. Life is just a game of trial and error. You may get lucky most of the time, but one day, you’ll land on tails and lose everything. Hopefully, after failing so many times, you want to slap yourself on the wrist and want to quit. Too bad the world just doesn’t work that way. You can become the heavyweight champion of your organization, but one day, no matter how remarkable and amazing you are, you will lose. Trust me, I’ve seen it before. My name is Sage Tse Ling, I would tell you a little about myself, but I don’t want to waste my time. You only need to know the essentials. If you already know how to build it, why read the instructions, am I right? Of course I am. You might know me as Sage Sabbot. Or some of the nicknames I have been given or earned over the past couple years. They call me the Soul Survivor, and if you question that, let me know so I can prove it to you. I’m known as The Sorcerer and The Warlock. That is easy, from birth, I was given these powers by a greater being. If you asked me who that was when I was a child, I would have laughed at you and told you a name at the top of my head. Being a smartass was a hobby of mine. But now, I know that the Lord who lives in the Heavens above us, granted me these powers. I know what you are thinking, you want to know if I can kill people on command, bring back the dead, survive a cannon blast to the face, and levitate in the air. If you actually thought that, consider yourself the most ignorant individual walking this disgusting planet we call home. I have my boundaries. My limits. I don’t expect you to run thirty miles per hour now, do I? Not at all. But if you never tried it, how do you know you can’t. That is my theory. When you are a child, all you want to do is to bring back your deceased pet rabbit or to create myself a baby brother, you know, somebody to have fun with in the backyard. These things, they were all beyond my control. Hell, if I could bring back the dead, I wouldn’t have to worry about dying every single night before I go to sleep…again. But that was a long time ago and it is a very long story. I don’t really care to tell you because that was the past…and so is Maverick Reno. You may look at me and chuckle. A little girly giggle slides out your throat. You would never believe a man of my age to do anything in the professional wrestling world. But that is fine. That is dandy, I love to be underestimated. I prefer it. I enjoy watching my opponents bask in glory and brag about how they are going to put the old man back retirement, where I should be to begin with. Or, or…break my hip. I’ve heard all those jokes before and to tell you the truth, I find them hilarious. I lift my head towards the skies and I laugh harder then I ever laughed…out of pure stupidity. If you know anything about me, you know that I’ve broken my arms, necks, legs, and jaws then I have bones in my body. What, you don’t believe me? How about asking Brick and Angelus Sorrow? Because they’ll tell you first hand, who not to fuck with. People have underestimated me countless amount of times in my life. In fact, I was rated the most underrated wrestler in Global Wrestling in the year 2002. Especially those who don’t know me, which is why I’m going to enjoy my time in PTC the most. If you looked into any documentation of my professional wrestling history, you will see the numerous title belts that I’ve worn around my waist. From the Global Wrestling Cruiserweight title to the Global Wrestling Heavyweight title, I’ve won them all. Well, except the tag team titles, but give me a couple weeks and that could be arranged. You might read my biography and read the words, “Harvester of Tears”. That means that I am apart of the most group of heartless men in the entire world. The world feared us, not because we are nefarious and proved it, but because we were a family and were not to be messed around with. Together, we dominated the Global Wrestling for years. Names like Salaok, Quartz, Johnathon Davis, Silent Wind, Grendel, and Mani Shigeru, we reigned up high for such a long time. Now, all of those names have left the Global Wrestling…except for me. I am the Soul Survivor of the Harvesters and the Nevermore. And if you know me, you already know not to question that. Sitting on this genuine, black leather chair, I reach for the lever on the side and pull it. The front base flies up and connects with the heels of my shoes. I lean back into a comfortable position and interlock my hands behind my head, resting. My genuine, black leather chair. I open my mouth as wide as possible and yawn… Don’t you wish you were as relaxed as I am? Suddenly, I get a sickening feeling in my stomach. My relaxation is about to be disrupted. I know the door is about to be knocked on before it very happens. I guess my powers are more of like a classic Spiderman sense, just that it is very real… Knock, Knock, Knock ”Come on in Leinad…” The door swings open and I’m graced by the presence of the one and only, Leinad Soma. You see, back in the day, Leinad and I despised each other. He was a member of The Upper Echelon, which was a group of wannabe Nevermore members who ended up defeating us somehow. They tried to take over the Global Wrestling and fill the shoes of The Nevermore. But they learned that it was a hell of a lot harder then it seemed. They were going to have to dethrone us from our spots and push us off the ladder of success first. Swallowing hard, I remember that they did just that. They took out Harvester after Harvester, Nevermore member after Nevermore member…until I was the last one left. Until I was the Soul Survivor. Of course I wasn’t going to accept what they wanted to do, after trashing their warehouse, they followed my car along a high speed chase filled with rage and frustrations. I ended up getting in a car accident… And not because I’m old. But because they cut me off. I smashed my forehead off of the windshield, slamming shards of glass into my face and leaving most of my body parts broken and bruised. I crawled out of my car in excruciating pain, only to be taken away by Echelon leader, Maverick Reno, with a 9mm shot to the forehead. I don’t really remember what happened after that other then having Leinad Soma awaken me from a long sleep that no one has ever woken up to. Not even Jesus Christ himself. Apparently, Leinad was about to embark on a mission, a journey and needed my help. I thought about it for awhile and decided that I would help him. What else should I do? The guy brought me back to life after I passed away. It is the least I can do for him. It was a long road we traveled on. Trust me, it wasn’t easy for me to trust him after him and the Echelon did, but it was my only choice. He helped me in hiding from everyone. It wouldn’t be too fantastic to see a former dead man walking down the street now, would it? He transformed from my enemy, to my friend, to my mentor. He guided me along paths of virtue and success. And now, he is my trusted ally inside and outside the ring. Soma walks a couple slow steps up to me, very patiently. He has the most patience in the world, unlike me. I have the tendency to snap every now and then. He stands beside me and crosses his arms…waiting for me to do something… ”Can I help you?” I say in a sarcastic tone, and he knows it… ”Lying back, enjoying your time off I take it?” I lean back further and close my eyes… ”Of course, what else is there to do?” I can see the blood and veins in the middle of my eyelids… ”I don’t know, maybe train for the PTC Pay Per View coming up?” ”Oh, come on Leinad. You know that professional wrestling is more mental then it is physical.” ”Oh, grow up Sabbot. You always complain about being out of shape and not being able to do all of these amazing things you used to be able to do.” ”Cry me a river. And don’t tell me to grow up either. I’m over 40 years old.” ”I’m thousands of years old…” Damn vampire, he always wins the battle of age against anybody… ”Just…just…shut up.” He laughs, he knows he got the best of me. You can never outwit an immortal bastard. ”You should get your old butt in the gym today and start to run a lot. Build up your cardiovascular system for the big event.” ”I’m too lazy to drive.” ”Sabbot, I have a gym only two floors above you.” You see, I live with Leinad. He is one of the wealthiest men in the entire world. He has more mansions then he has fingers and toes. When he kept me in hiding, he kept me with him in his home. He gave me a bedroom with everything I need. My bedroom. But I’ve been saving up my own money to move out, only a couple more hundred thousand dollars and I’ll be fine and dandy. If my thoughts can sigh, they would be doing that at this moment. ”Well, my back really hurts today.” ”Great, you can work your biceps and pectoral muscles. And when you are done with that, there is always your traps and lats.” ”Okay, okay. I suppose some relaxation time to myself is out of the question then.” ”Sage, all you do is sit around the house.” ”Not true.” ”Okay, then what did you do so far today?” ”It is only 8 o’clock, we have the whole day to workout.” ”Yeah, 8 o’clock PM.” He shakes his head side to side. I wasn’t always this lazy, seriously. ”Alright already. I’ll go up to the gym and I’ll work out…” He pats me on the back and smiles… ”That’s what I like to hear.” I shake my head side to side and roll my eyes. Soma leaves the room while I get changed into some more comfortable clothing… I walk into the gym, with gym bag in hand and throw it to the ground. I look around for a place to start. Should I use the treadmill? Or should I use the inverted bench press? Or what about the leg lift? I begin to walk to the treadmill, planning on developing better endurance for the match that waits. But instead, I find myself standing in front of a punching bag. I push it slightly. It rocks back and comes forward into a tiny front kick. I don’t have the heart to work out. Hell, I don’t have the heart to do anything anymore. The world has corrupted me over the past couple years. The punching bag rocks and forth, back and forth into an elbow, sending it flying back time and time again… I admit, I’ve changed a lot since my Harvester of Tears days. I’ve changed a lot. Sometimes I wish I could just go back to what I was, because I was better. I had much more talent and a drive to succeed, a lot more then now. I jump in the air and place a knee towards the top of the bag, dodging it on the way back… I’ve heard a lot of talk about the new Sage Sabbot. And how a lot of people don’t believe I could ever live up to how I was… I rush a countless amount of fast punches into the bag, rocking it towards the left, and towards the right each time… People say that I’m too old now. That I’ve matured in a way that your own grandfather decides to give up on life…I’m not about to give up. I spin around, delivering a kick to the bag, the metal chain comes loose, sending the bag into the dry wall in front of it… I’ll leave the giving up to you.
