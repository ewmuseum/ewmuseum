So, if I am late you may delete.}} The Chronicles of the Deathscythe Outlaw Side Volume 1:The Crusades Into the Lion’s Den Again =+=It’s a late night at a crowded local arena that’s only a few miles away from the major London Arena in Great Britain’s capital, as an exclusive late night event is being held before 4 Winds Wrestling’s highly anticipated PPV airs.=+= =+=At this moment a match is about to begin.=+= =+=Inside the arena, the crowd in the stands are cheering and chanting for their favorite 4 Winds wrestlers along with holding their homemade signs in hope that the many cameras will catch a glimpse of them. Suddenly, the cheering comes to an abrupt end when the lights go off where a certain dark Outlaw is about to make his entrance, as the crowd starts up the cheering in reaction to this while others are booing out loud.=+= Tania Brown: Heading at the ring at this time, he’s from Tokyo, Japan, he’s known as the Deathscythe Outlaw, as he’s none other then Kyo Kushinaga! =+=The long silver-haired Outlaw in his usual all black attire comes out on stage after Brown’s announced him and soon silently walks down the ramp way alone like a traditional Japanese warrior in preparation for a great battle, as he’s not fazed by the crowd’s reactions instead he’s focus on casting his opponent into the great darkness known as defeat.=+= Morales: This is the second time that the Outlaw is coming alone without his valet and girlfriend, Caitlin Fairchild. Candler: Well, you know that is truly a marked man now, so I guess he doesn’t want her to slow him down or get caught up in his mess. Either way, I believe he’s doing the right thing and leaving the young sister of Patti Anderson in the back. =+=Kyo enters the ring through the second rope and goes stands at his corner, as he briefly eyes his opponent who’s a no name that’s trying to make a quick name for himself by challenging the former North Wind Champion in this exclusive event.=+= Tania Brown: Already in the ring, his opponent, he’s from Mobile, Alabama, he’s known as the Southern Showstopper, as he’s Billy Bob Davidson! =+=The newbie who wears no shirt with bright red pants is playing the crowd with great cockiness and completely ignores the Deathscythe Outlaw.=+= Morales: Don’t really know anything about this Billy Bob Davidson. However, I know that earlier today he demanded to have a match with the Deathscythe Outlaw Kyo Kushinaga. Candler: He’s a fool. He’s honestly a damn fool I mean Kyo is already in a foul mood after last week’s StormFront. So, in lamest terms, the fool just signed his death warrant by challenging the Outlaw. =+=The bell rings starting this match.=+= =+=Billy stops playing the crowd, as he soon turns his attention over to the silent Outlaw who still stands at his corner and still hasn’t taken off his trench coat let alone his orange-tinted sunglasses since he already knows that his match with the newbie wouldn’t last long. Billy walks over to Kyo confidently and begins to make cocky remarks towards him while Outlaw just quietly stands there with a cold expression on his face. Kyo didn’t see Billy through his shades instead he sees an endless darkness where there’s perhaps a bright light at the end, as this is his own serene state of mind and this is the same state he’s always in whenever he’s engage in combat with anyone in and out of the ring.=+= =+=The crowd who are against the Deathscythe Outlaw are cheering and chanting for Billy to defeat Outlaw, as Billy is absorbing all the crowd’s praises and continues his verbal abuse on Kyo.=+= Candler: Jeesh, will this guy stop playing with the crowd and just start this match. Morales: Well, they don’t call him the Southern Showstopper for nothing. =+=Billy decides it’s the best time to strike the motionless Kyo, as he attempts to throw a punch, but Kyo finally shows sign of movement by swiftly moving the right side of his trench coat aside to expose his right leg and quickly throws a kick at Billy’s face. Billy looks in great shock from witnessing Kyo’s swiftness, as he closes his eyes bracing himself from Kyo’s foot making contact, but he soon opens his eyes when feeling nothing and sees that Kyo’s foot is a few inches away from his face.=+= Morales: Kyo stopped attack! Candler: What the hell!? =+=Billy looks in confusion from wondering why Outlaw didn’t go through with his assault, as he watches Outlaw move his foot away from him. Kyo slowly turns his back on Billy, as Billy still looks in great shock from how fast Kyo is, but the shock soon disappears when hearing the crowd who are against Outlaw cheering for him and causes the great confidence he had at the start of this match to come back. With Kyo having his back turn, Billy uses this opportunity to charge at him for a behind attack, but Kyo senses it coming and quickly turns around to strike with a brutal chop to Billy’s throat. Billy clutches his throat with both hands and violently coughs trying to breathe, as Kyo watches him with great coldness through his shades.=+= Morales: What a cheap shot to the throat by the former North Wind Champion! Candler: What do you mean a cheap shot!? That shot was completely illegal! =+=Kyo still watches Billy choking for a bit longer before he finally finishes Billy off with his finisher, the Heavenly Lights, as Billy’s body bounces off the hard canvas in response to the move. Outlaw covers the motionless Billy with ease, as the ref makes the three count and the bell soon rings ending this match.=+= Tania Brown: Your winner…the Deathscythe Outlaw Kyo Kushinaga! =+=Kyo stands over Billy and stares down at him with great coldness, but soon turns his attention to the crowd, as he listens to the crowd cheering for him while others are doing the complete opposite by booing at him loudly. Kyo seriously glares at the opposing crowd not caring at all if these people are with him nor against him and with a swift swing of the trench coat he begins to leave the ring.=+= Morales: Hey wait a minute! Something is happening in the ring! Candler: What!? =+=During the crowd’s commotion, Billy has secretly slid out of the ring and got hold of a chair, as he re-enters the ring with the folded chair in hands in attempt to strike Outlaw down with the chair. Billy lunges at Kyo from the behind and goes for a vicious shot to the back of Kyo’s head, but Kyo senses Billy’s intention to stop Billy’s assault with a swift front flip that kicks the chair out of Billy’s heads, as Kyo just connected with a Reverse Rising Outlaw. Billy looks with great shock from having the chair knock out of his hands, but his shock expression turns into a look of fright when he saw Kyo coldly looking at him over his shoulder.=+= Candler: What the hell is wrong with this Southern Showstopper!? Why in the hell would he try to attack someone that he couldn’t even defeat!? Morales: Even I agree with you on that. =+=Kyo turns around and faces Billy with death in his eyes, as Billy slowly cowards away from him, which instantly causes Kyo to lunge over to him. Billy’s eyes go dull when he receives a sharp mid-air roundhouse kick to the side of the head by Outlaw, as the force of the kick knocks him over to the side ropes where he bounces off them and come running back at Outlaw. Billy abruptly stops running when Kyo tightly grabs hold of Billy’s right arm, as Kyo sharply kicks Billy in his calf knocking him down backwards on the mat while Kyo still has a tight grip on his arm. A loud chilling crack of a bone is faintly heard underneath the crowd’s commotion, as Kyo’s recent move brutally shattered Billy’s shoulder, but the crowd soon goes dead silent after hearing the crack. Billy yells out in great pain while frantically favoring his shoulder.=+= Morales: Damn it! Kyo just broken Billy’s arm and perhaps ended his career! Candler: His career already ended when he decided to step foot in the ring against him! =+=EMTs soon come rushing out on stage with a stretcher ready for the injured Billy and medical equipment to treat him, as Kyo notices them from the corner of his eye, but he turns away from to look down at Billy. Kyo finally leaves the ring by slipping through the second ropes, as the EMTs rushes the ring to treat Billy.=+= Morales: Now you must admit the Outlaw went too far! He broke Billy’s arm and possibly ended his career before it even started! Candler: I’ll admit that it was uncalled for, but Billy tried to attack from the behind and he paid for it, severely. =+=Kyo silently walks up the ramp way while listening to the silence from the crowd, but the crowd soon response with massive boos to him, but still that doesn’t fazed, as he disappears behind the curtains while the crowd continues.=+= =+=In backstage, the Deathscythe Outlaw Kyo Kushinaga walks alone through the hallway, as the staff members who are in the back quickly moves out of his way in fear of him. Kyo simply continues walking, as he lights up a cigarette and begins to smoke in relaxation.=+= “Excuse me,” a male voice called out. =+=Kyo stops walking and looks over his shoulder to see a man in a business suit with a suitcase in his right hand is standing behind him distant.=+= Man looks at Kyo with a look of great business, “I’m a representative from Primetime Central who perhaps has an intriguing offer to make.” He makes a curious sound and looks with surprise from soon seeing Kyo walking away from him. “Hey wait! Don’t you want to hear the offer I have to make.” “I’m not interested at the place that is full of pampered incompetents,” Kyo remarked in a cold tone. Man tries to catch up with Kyo, “What if I said if you have a chance to redeem yourself in Primetime Central after your previous your lost in the ETD Royale.” Kyo stops walking and looks over at the representative from his shoulder through his shades with seriousness in his eyes, “I’m listening.” =+=Couple of hours later, the Deathscythe Outlaw sits alone in deep thought about his previous meeting with the representative of Primetime Central at the bar counter in a local bar, as he drinks a warm glass of whiskey while the other customers in the bar are enjoying themselves with cold beverages and other means of entertainment that a bar has to offer. Suddenly, Outlaw’s thoughts are broken when the bartender places a tall glass of cold beer before him.=+= Kyo lifts a brow and stares at the bartender with his brown eyes, “I didn’t request this.” Bartender nods his head in response to his Kyo’s query, “It’s from that young lady over there.” He points over to the far right of the bar. =+=Kyo looks over in the direction from where the bartender is pointing and sees the young magazine journalist, Ami Toyota from the magazine Wrestlers’ Tribute sitting at a table alone in comfortable street clothes, as she holds a cold sweating glass of beer in her hand. Kyo looks in dullness when seeing her not in the mood to being interview by her.=+= Ami soon walks over to where Kyo is with beer in hand and takes a seat on his right, “What a surprise to see you here, Kyo.” “I highly doubt that,” he remarked. Ami takes a sip of her cold beer, “I caught your match.” She whistles in mockery in response to that brutal match. “I didn’t know that a person’s arm could bend that way. Where did you learn a move like that from? Was that a martial arts move, or a military move?” “Don’t you have something better to do like bothering a no name wrestler like perhaps Syckle,” he responded in a dull tone. Ami chuckles after hearing Kyo’s comment, “Nah, Syckle is not as interesting as you are I mean we already know who he is while we don’t know anything about you.” She leans closer to him and closes her eyes halfway looking at him with an amusing gaze. “However, I know that you were discussing some business with a representative from a major wrestling company that you have no love for. Care to mention what you two were talking about?” =+=Kyo finish drinking his warm glass of whiskey in response to her comment.=+= Ami rests her shoulder against Kyo’s left shoulder and lightly nudges him there to get a rise out of him, “C’mon, you can tell me.” Kyo coldly looks at her, “We were talking about how annoying journalists are.” “That was mean,” she pouted. =+=Kyo soon gets up from his seat and almost causes Ami to fall off her stool, but she catches herself and sees him pay for his whiskey with a few British pounds, as he is about to leave the bar.=+= “Your little chat wouldn’t have to do anything about Primetime’s upcoming Infinite Gauntlet,” she questioned in an amusing tone. =+=Kyo stops walking in reaction to the young journalist’s comment about the Gauntlet.=+= Ami curls her lips in a grin, “Oh, so it does.” She tilts her head and looks at him intriguingly. “Hoping to get enough wins to go for the Extreme Title once more. If you are, you won’t be facing the vampire wrestler, Shadow, as she lost the title in a very close match to Prime’s upcoming star, Nova.” =+=Kyo sharpens his eyes in seriousness in sign that he has spark his interest, but changes his expression to a frighteningly calm one.=+= “Who ever said I would be aiming for the Extreme Title,” he seriously responded. =+=Ami looks at him curiously when hearing that, as she wonders what he meant by his pervious comment.=+= “I believe it is time to for this Outlaw to move up to the next level,” he remarked in slight amusement. Ami looks with surprise from realizing what his pervious comment meant, “You don’t mean–…” Kyo looks over his right shoulder at Ami with burning determination in his eyes, “Perhaps it’s time to go that extra distance that the many people you call 4 Winds superstars couldn’t go without facing humiliating defeats from likes of those pampered incompetents over at that company.” He looks away from her and faces the close bar door. “Or whatever comes first.” =+=Kyo soon exits the bar leaving Ami alone at the bar counter to think on his words.=+= =+=Outside in the late night, Kyo walks alone on the concrete sidewalk with both hands in the pockets of his long leather trench coat with a lit cigarette in his mouth and his gleaming orange-tinted shades over his shades giving him that more eerie look. While walking, his mind begins to think once more about the offer that he agreed to.=+= “It’s no secret to anybody that I don’t have any love for Primetime Central. I think that Primetime Central is no more then a sacred place where only those with strong connections and reputations can be admitted within the place. Well…those are not the only ways people can gain admittance, as the people can fight others like them to gain admittance to the sacred lands.” =+=Kyo removes his cigarette from his mouth and flicks the ashes away before putting it back in his mouth, as he continues to walk alone through the cold night.=+= “I’m not fighting to gain admittance to their so wonderful lands. For all I care the sacred lands can be torched on fire with the outcasts dancing around the open fire in celebration. I’m just a typical Outlaw who wants to raise some Hell while starting a revolution and it appears that the revolution will be moving to their lands now. I consider this my own little crusade to claim names of the admitted and climb through their ranks.” =+=A cold wind blows that goes through Kyo making the back end of his long trench coat fly up along with his long silver hair, as he continues his thoughts.=+= “Back to the Lion’s Den I go…” =+=Kyo sharpens his eyes in great seriousness behind his shades.=+= “Let the Crusade begin…” =+=The scene fades away with him disappearing through rising steam that is coming from a sewer vent.=+= =+=End=+=
