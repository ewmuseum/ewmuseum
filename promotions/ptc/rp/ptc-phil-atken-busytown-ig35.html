We find Mr. Phil M. Atken and Dr. Dick Dirkwood PhD (completely legit) walking down the street of BUSYTOWN, USA. It is universally regarded as the busiest town in the whole wide world. Unless you're a filthy communist. Phil: You know, I've always wondered why is it every single time I find a new company to work for I have to climb some sort of imaginary ladder to success. Shouldn't I be a certified legend at this point? Dick: Well maybe if you weren't a completely repugnant human being people would like you more. Phil: I'm not repugnant, I get along great with da kidz on da street, yo diggity. Dick: I think you just proved my point. Anyway those kids only like you because you throw your leftover beer at them. Phil: Not AT them, to them. Dick: At full force? Phil: Kids have to learn to perfect their hand eye co-ordination in they wish to succeed in life. I don't want all those homeless children in the streets to be miserable failures. Dick: You'd think with them being homeless at a teenage age they're already miserable failures at life. Phil: Oh look at you Mr. McKillthehomeless. I suppose you wish to go out and destroy the livelihoods of all the pathetic street urchins worldwide. Dick: Only the smelly ones. Phil: That's all of them... THEY'RE HOMELESS. Dick: They could at least bathe in the puddles. Phil: I don't think you understand life. Dick: I don't think you understand how much they smell. Phil: I don't think you understand puddles. Dick: Are we going to do this all day? Phil: I believe so, we don't have anything better to do. Dick: Actually... Phil: What? Dick: I got you involved in another one of them matchy things at that place you lectured about your penis. Phil: M.I.T? Dick: No, the other place. Phil: That was an eight university tour. I was asked never to return in each and every case. Dick: Not, the universities, the wrestling place. Phil: Oh that makes much more sense. I thought you were making little sense but in reality you were making lots of sense. What a crazy language we have. Dick: It's not that crazy, you're just exceedingly stupid. Phil: I can't be exceedingly stupid, I got a Masters in Entertainment Salvation. Dick: Isn't that the course you made up for you to make yourself look good. Phil: I have no idea what you're talking about, many of the world's most successful people are Entertainment Saviours. Dick: Like who? Phil: Emmy award winner Barry Manilow. Dick: You're kidding right? Phil: You may scoff at my good friend Barry but WHERE'S YOUR EMMY? Dick: It'll never come until I can disassociate myself from you and get some real clients. Phil: Oh, never then. Dick: Sadly I like money more than pretty awards. Phil: You know, an Emmy would make a great wrestling weapon. Better than a Slammy anyway. Dick try and get me a guest spot on some popular televisionalised entertainment programme. I'll do the rest. Dick: I would but again and I'm sorry to come full circle but you're repugnant. Phil: I doubt that, I have a certificate to prove that I'm both endearing and charming. Dick: How many certificates do you have? Phil: Just the one but it covers a wide range of my adorable personality. You see Dick, as a certified Entertainment Saviour it is my job to bring joy to the word. It is my job to amuse, charm and entertain those who are less fortunate than myself in the charisma department. It is my job to rid them of the evils of the dull, mundane and the suicide inducing people from there televisular entertainment magic machines. It's just an aside it makes me an assload of money too, of which I deserve each and every penny. It's hard work being overlord of peoples entertainment. I mean for every me after all, there is a you. Dick: This is why I can't get you a job. Phil: And that's why your face causing me to vomit. Dick: That doesn't even make any sense in context. Phil: Sure it does, remember I'm the entertaining one around here. I know what's a zinger and what isn't. Dick: Who actually uses the term zinger these days? Phil: Inbred college mutants. The very target market we're trying to appeal ourselves too. Your lack of knowledge on target markets is very disturbing considering you are meant to be a Superagent. Dick: The inbred college student market isn't one with which I'm too familiar with these days. Phil: That's due to your small penis not being attractive to college whores. In fact it's what I specialized in for a year and what a great year that was. I made the Dean's List due to it. Dick: Enough of your imaginary non-sense blabbering about your non-existent college years. We have actual business to attend to, very important business too. If you win this match I've managed to sneak you in the back door, your name may actually mean something again. You may actually get the recognition you think you deserve, but you HAVE to win to do so. If you win my life will probably cease being the fucking failure that it is for a while too. It's a high profile bout, big names are certainly involved, I don't want you to fuck ME over by doing your same stupid schtick time and time again. Phil: Yeesh, who let you have sugar. Dick: This is exactly the problem I'm talking about. You spend all your time making stupid jokes around me and absolutely no time training. What the hell did you do back in the days without me? I mean you won a World Title, god knows why, without me, how the hell did you achieve that. Phil: I'm very good at politics and ass kissing. Dick: Well those days are over, you need some actual standing before you can play politics of which you currently HAVE NONE. Do you understand that, back in the day you were the young smug kid climbing the ranks and at least had a decent physique. Now you're an out of shape joke who can't seem to actually win a match that doesn't involve some sort of kick to the balls and I'm sick of having to get involved in each of your matches just so you can kick someone in the balls. You do know I have a family right? I've spent a year traveling the road with you because I was aware back in the day you were quite the little cash machine. Those days are for the moment over and you seem to piss away any chance I have at rebuilding your name. Phil: Wow, that's a lot of anger. What's even more shocking is you actually have a slight personality! Dick: Of course I'm bloody angry at this point, what I thought was my little money making machine decided to piss away each and every one of the high profile gigs I've landed him to date and now you feel like walking down a busy street and discuss BARRY FUCKING MANILOW than discuss a plan of action for yet another high profile gig I've managed to find it. You are quite possibly the most worthless mess I've had the great misfortune of being an agent to, and I used to work in the porn industry. In fact the very reason why I've bothered to put up with you for so long was because I had it on EXTREMELY good authority that you were a cash cow. What they didn't inform me was you were also a drug addled neurotic with a tendency to go hooker hunting rather than show up for work. Phil: Well to me it was quite a simple choice, sex with hot women or groping man-ass in front of thousands of people. I mean I would have shown up but I'm not a flaming homosexual. So with that ruled out hooker hunting is the only way to go. Dick: If that's your line of thinking, why the hell did you even start in the wrestling industry. Phil: You know, I'm not entirely sure but I'm pretty great at it. Especially those mic promos. I'm especially good at them. Dick: If you're pretty great at it WHY DO YOU AVOID MATCHES. Phil: Only with those technical guys. I swear to god at least half of them try to cop a feel. It's really icky and as I said I'm not gay. Dick: NEITHER ARE THEY YOU PARANOID DOLT. Most of these men have families and people who care about them, which may I add you do not. Phil: Yeah they have families, GAY FAMILIES. Dick: No, no, no, no, A THOUSAND TIMES NO. The people you wrestle against are in general well adjusted people trying to make a living. Phil: What about those ones with real deep story and mob ties and the ones who when not wrestling live in a fantasy land where they ride unicorns into swimming pools filled with popcorn in which aliens give them head. Dick: Those are the exceptions. The point I'm making, at this point in your career, at least until we rebuild it acting like a five year old with a diminished mental capacity will get us close to nowhere. In fact it will get us completely nowhere, which in turn will make me go on a murderous rampage, the blood of the innocent will then be on your conscious, which would be bad. However, the minute we're back on track you can go around your bosses office with a megaphone implying he sucks cock again for all I care, but not until we get settled again. Certainly that will take some time. Heck if you actually get settled somewhere I'll buy you a fucking megaphone. Phil: That does sound like a good deal. I do like implying men enjoy oral sex with other men in a megaphone. It was part of my degree don't you know. Dick: Okay, for starters you have to drop that shit. Actually for starters you need to get involved in this match I've been telling you about. I've already forged your signature to sign up for it, so that's not a problem, now you just have to get in mild shape so you don't cramp up entering the ring. Phil: Isn't cramps another term for a period? I don't plan to have a period. Now Victoria, there was a woman who personified the term river of blood. From all the reports I've heard those children never swam again. I don't think they ever washed again either, which is particularly disgusting considering they walking around... Dick: That's quite enough of that talk. This is the very problem with you, I make some sort of breakthrough into talking some sense into you and then your mind wanders off to a vile place. Look, we have very little time to prepare for the Gauntlet of Infants. I think everyone participating has to beat up as many eight year olds as possible. The one who beats up the most wins. Phil: It's not the infants from the swimming... Dick: No, it's not. Phil: Because that would be disgusting. Dick: So beating up regular eight year olds is not a moral problem to you. Phil: So long as they don't smell. Dick: They're eight year olds, at least half of them are guaranteed to have crapped themselves prior. Phil: You know, the more I think about this, the less I think it's legal. Dick: Maybe it's something different. Phil: YA THINK? Dick: Hrrmm, I'm going to have to re-think this strategy, most of my game plan so far was "kick their tiny orphan heads in". Phil: Yeah, that would certainly not be legal. Dick: I'll be back later. Phil: And you call me the drug addled retard. With that Dick skips off down the wonderful Mugging Lane of BUSYTOWN, USA on the sunniest day of all time. Many people began suffering from cancer the next day, proving that old song about Sunscreen victorious once again. Which was the true moral of this story.
