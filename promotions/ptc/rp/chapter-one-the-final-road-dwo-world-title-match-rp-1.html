Chapter One: The Final Road (DWO World Title Match RP #1)

7 April 2003

Donny Diamond Emotionless Monster Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 59 Location: My own personal Hell. Posted: Mon Apr 07, 2003 1:35 am Post subject: Chapter One: The Final Road (DWO World Title Match RP #1) Chapter One: The Final Road Finally all of the hard work has paid off for him. Marked in ink, Donny Diamond versus Kaiowas for the Dominant Wrestling Organization World Heavy Weight Championship. Someone famous, or perhaps not, said that good things come to all of those that wait. He has waited, but he was never “good”. Damned, that is a word more fitting for him. Everything about him is damned; no matter where you look everything is the same around him. People hate him, love is an unknown passion, and sorrow however is something he feels for himself every day. Make a note however to mark this day as the beginning for when his road of solitude comes forth to end, and his road of deserving domination begins. ~-~-~-~-~-~ ¤Fear me For I am one of darkened souls Keeper of torment When we battle this demon will loose control¤ ~`~Location Unknown~`~ | ~`~April 1, 2003~`~ ~†~The surrounding area is far from being known, not much can be seen in the area. Light. It comes from the five candles lit in the center of this room, each of them positioned as the point of a star. Connecting them together is a dark red liquid, to thin to be paint so it is obviously blood. In the center of this demonic blood-drawn star sits Donny Diamond. Pale as always, his eyes are closed as he sits on his bent knees with his palms resting firmly over the top of his thighs as he breathes slowly.~†~ Donny Diamond: With the blood of the fallen innocent I call forth. To the father of complete hate, show me the passageway so that my words can be heard freely. Give my soul the gift of complete damnation as you welcome me once more into your world of death. [The room where he is sitting slowly begins to shake as dust falls from the ceiling forming a thick blanket around his circle. The scars across his back begin to open up as blood leaks from them, his eyes open and blackness can be seen swirling around his pupils. Candles that were once there are now replaced with skeletal hands that have risen up from the cracked open holes in the floor. Clutched in each hand is a human heart throbbing as blood drips down onto the floor.] ¤Listen to the throbbing of your heart Thump, thump, thump Which of us shall make the final mark Thump, thump, thump¤ Echoing Voice: The doors have opened to you, darkened soul. What is it you seek from this realm or torment? Donny Diamond: I seek the messenger of finalized souls. Here I have one to give forth as proof to my destruction since my rebirth. Echoing Voice: Lay him forth Slayer. His soul shall be given to your father as proof of your conquers. [Everything starts to get blurry as flames rise around the hands, and blood can be seen leaking down the brick walls. Slowly he gets up, and walks out of his circled portal to the underworld. Across the room he walks until he is by the small cell. Rattling of metal can be heard as he opens the door, leaning forward he grabs what seems to be a dark arm covered in blood that is attached to a beaten dead corpse. Walking backwards out of the cell he drags the limp body across the floor, and back into the center of the portal.] ¤Fearing what you do not know Deep inside my head is where you lie Slashing away at the walls you cannot escape Hell as my kingdom you are my new slave¤ Donny Diamond: This is the sinner I send to my father. God could never save his soul, as he was already damned for Hell. Let him be punished, and let his screams mix into the pleasurable moans of Satan as I send him forth. Echoing Voice: Demon of this realm you do us proud. Take from this body the blood that once flowed through it veins. Mark it across your own as a trophy of proud accomplishments. [Leaning forward across the body he grabs a ceremonial dagger, and holds it with both his hands gripped together as he points it up towards the heavens.] Donny Diamond: From Heaven to Hell, I hope your soul is marked with the pain I have felt all of my life. Even in death I show you no remorse, now fill Hell with your screams of pain as I mark your body one final time. [He places the dagger in his right hand, then slowly places the edge against the neck of this now dead teenage boy. Slowly he presses harder against the flesh and starts to move it downwards as the skin is ripped and blood pours out. Using his finger he passes it in the fountain of draining blood, and then licks it with the tip of his tongue before drawing a squiggly line beneath his eyes.] ¤Deranged is how I make your body look Death is only the beginning Twisted is how your soul shall be You will be the last to remain¤ Donny Diamond: With your blood across my flesh I call you my victim and I send you forth into the pits of Hell to be tormented. Echoing Voice: As the son of our Lord, I wish you to deliver this sacrifice yourself. [Once again the room begins to shake causing more dust to fall off from the floor above. From the center of the star a bright light appears and steady it grows bigger, and bigger until the room is completely filled. Everything begins spinning, faster and faster as each second passes, then it makes a sudden stop. The scenery has changed greatly as we can see steam rising up from the stone covered ground. With the body clutched in his arms, Donny remains standing there for a moment as two beasts walk towards him.] Donny Diamond: And you are? Beast #1: We are the guardians that protect your father’s kingdom. Beast #2: Welcome back home our Prince. May we lead you forth into your home? Donny Diamond: Yes you may. ¤Enemy of everything you fear Listen to my words even though you will not be calm The echoing voice if mine you hear Echoing deep down inside¤ [Slowly they tilt their heads up and we can see red eyes glowing inside of their hoods as the darkness within shadows their faces. The one on the left turns and points forward as both start to walk that direction with their Prince following them with corpse in arm. After a decent walk they finally reach the gates where the two hooded beasts lift their fur-covered arms into the air. Loud screeching noise can be heard as twin black gates of mass proportion open slowly to welcome in the three beings.] Beast #2: Everything within these gates is destined to be yours one day. Beast #1: All of these lost and tormented souls will be lead by you. This is your army of darkness to command as you one day make Hell spawn on Earth. Donny Diamond: Why are all of these souls allowed to walk freely as if this was to be some form of Heaven? Beast #1: Perhaps my Lord, if you took a closer look you would see the reality of these souls. ¤Even the innocent is tormented Crying because the pain past death is to intense Weaklings of the world above I strike you down¤ [In the center of the town a woman can be seen standing, with her body bent over as her arms and head are bound by two boards. Screeeeeeech! That’s what you hear as he watches the blade slides down from the top of the guillotine, and comes crashing down across the neck of the woman trapped inside. Her head rolls forward across the ground as blood squirts from her neck, however this only goes on for a moment before her head rematerializes on her body, and the process is repeated over and over again.] Beast #1: In her days on Earth she flaunted everything she had in the faces of those less fortunate. Beast #2: She laughed as she watched the town execute the innocent people every week. Donny Diamond: This is her Hell. Her punishment is to have everything she laughed at happen to her. Beast #1: Another example would be that old man sitting over there by the river, with the tin can in his hands. [Slowly he turns his head to glance towards the left. A very dehydrated man sits, with a can in his shaking hands as he leans over towards the small pond of water.] Beast #1: He was alone in a desert for three weeks. Beast #2: During those weeks he begged to God to show him some mercy, and to give him some water for he could be revitalized. Dehydrated Man [crying]: God… Please save me. Beast #1: God never saved him. In his final hour he gave his soul to Satan for a sip of water. Donny Diamond: So here he sits for the rest of eternity with a can used to gather water. Beast #2: Yes. However his thirst will never be quenched no matter how much he drinks. Donny Diamond: This corpse grows heavy. Lead me to my father’s quarters. ¤In the shadows of Hell I wait Listening to the clock Tick, tock, tick, tock For your final hour¤ [Past the many different punishments of the tortured souls they walk. Out of the small village of the damned they exit, and across a large bridge that is build over a river of flames and lava. They reach the other end of the bridge where a great gigantic palace is before them. Guarding the entrance are several shadows each with a long blade placed in their hands. Slowly they enter. Down the hallway they walk entering the throne room, where the thrown is turned away. Donny drops the body in front of the stairs, and the two beasts beside him vanish.] Donny Diamond: Father I have returned. Face me now, I wish to speak with you. [Slowly the throne turns and we see the figure dressed exactly how we saw him during Donny’s rebirth. He looks down towards the bloody corpse before him, then back up to the eyes of his son.] ¤Cower even more when the scent of me is what you smell For I am the reaper I have no sorrow for the sick No honor for the noble¤ Satan: So I see you have been completing your duties with ease. So why is it you have come to deliver the corpse to me yourself? Donny Diamond: Because it is a body of special importance to you father. He is one of the tribe members that escaped to follow Kaiowas. Satan: One of the Ktulu? How does one capture and defeat one of such a strong tribe? Donny Diamond: He was lost and I saw him wondering. Did I do you proud by forcing him into his end? Satan: I am proud of you Donny. You have proven to me, that giving you a second chance was not a mistake. I know now that when my time is up as Lord of the Underworld, you will arise as the new king. Donny Diamond: Indeed I will. I have passed the trial you set forth for me. I have defeated the undefeated. Satan: Indeed you have, but now the true test lies before you. You killed one of the Ktulu, now you are the slayer their tribe fears. Donny Diamond: Slayer? Why would you call me a slayer? Satan: To their kind that is what you’re known as. You have slayed one of them. So now the entire tribe knows you by this one name. Donny Diamond: Then let them call me by this name, as I will do it justice. Each time I come across one the same fate shall fall over them, just as it has the others. What is this next test you speak of my Lord? Satan: Take a look at the ground before you, then you will see. [Looking down towards the ground in front of him he watches in amazement as the ground starts swirling around in a circle. Slowly it stops and forms a watery stone colored portal. It swirls once more for a moment, before we can see some yellowish colored grass. Coming into view is a man, his back is turned but we cannot see who he is. Braids can be seen flowing around before the swirling begins once more and the stone floor returns to normal.] Donny Diamond: Is… Satan: That’s right Donny. The Lord of the Ktulu, he is the next you must slay and bring forth to my kingdom to feel my wrath. He is the biggest sinner of them all, because he is stopping you from achieving what you deserve. Go now and slay him forcing the world to see his end! Donny Diamond: Yes father. [Once again the familiar light comes into view and it grows bigger each second. The scenery starts spinning and rotating in different directions until it suddenly stops and we are once again in the basement or dungeon area that we were in from the start. The blood can once again be seen on the walls, as Donny is knelt down in the center of the star as the candles start to burn to the bottom end of the wax, spilling it across the floor. The body of the fallen Ktulu warrior is still before him as well.] ¤My master has sent me forth To look at you and taunt I am superior Crumble at my feet¤ ~`~Place Unknown~`~ | ~`~ Present is all we know for a date, and time ~`~ [We are seen outside in a desert where nothing but sand and cactus can be seen, except for the house we’re near of course. On the left side of it we see Donny Diamond with only a pair of black jeans on, and some gloves on his hands. He has a shovel in his hands and appears to be digging a large hole into the sand.] Donny Diamond: His soul has been set to eternal punishment. Now it’s time for me to bury the body so that the maggots can have a fresh taste of flesh. [Donny Diamond walks back towards the front of the house where we see the purple body of the Ktulu warrior. Clutching it around the wrist he drags it off the steps and it comes crashing down onto the hard sandy ground. He drags it across the desert, then swings it around and lets go causing it to come crashing into the freshly dug grave. After looking down to the body six-feet under he once again grabs the shovel and starts to refill the grave with sand.] Donny Diamond: I saw who my next victim is. Everyone knows him by this new name, Lord of the Ktulu. Some may even call you Champion. If you’re worthy of that name, is not my place to judge. Though the name I call you, well it is echoing inside of my head. Kaiowas, Champion of DWO. I am on the hunt for you. ~†~ After saying those words he shovels the final toss of sand onto the grave and starts to walk back towards the house. Outside the wind picks up, blowing sand around hiding where the grave of the fallen Ktulu is. ~†~ ¤Demented is what I am Pain is only the beginning And Death is far from the end…¤ _________________ DWO Record: 9-5-0 PTC Record: 1-0 -I only mark for those deserving, and you know who you are.-
