Dissapointment isn't a choice

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Throughout history people have always second guessed themselves and people will continue to second guess themselves, why shouldn’t Peter Vetra and well educated man who is now in the midst of wrestling for PRIME and in the GTT4 tournament. Second guessing oneself is human nature it is almost like hesitation, when you are unsure of what to do with your life you hesitate on which path you want to take in your life. Peter Vetra is now faced with this hesitation continue on with his decision or turn around and make his father proud. FLASH BACK It’s around June 1996 and Peter Vetra is just becoming a active member of his father’s secret group. With many ideas running through this young man’s head he is unsure which to pursue to try and help the Illumaniti get known to the world for their good not their bad. Peter is standing in the office of his father’s lab where he is working on the discovery of a new type of hydrogen energy. The curious and educated Peter is looking around at what his father is doing but you can tell that something is bothering him and so can his father. Michel Vetra (in a heavy Roman accent): Son, what is the matter with you? Peter (unlike his father in an New York accent): Father, you know we try and prove to people that we are good people, we are not trying to discriminate nor are we trying to persuade anyone into believing what we do. But I have this urge to go and show the world what we are. Michel: You know you can’t do that son, many people look down on us, and we are not like the other people of this world. People of great knowledge are looked down in today’s society we are nerds we are geeks and we are the future which no one realizes. Peter: Father, I have come up with an idea, but I am a little scared, shaky even hesitating to tell you and the other members. If I announce it and everyone shoots it down many will look at me as a failure and no longer as competent as seem to be. Michel: Peter, you know I would never look down upon you, nor would I ever frown upon you so I will make you a deal. You tell me and if I feel it is not a competent idea it will never be mentioned again. Let me close and lock the door and then you can let me know and no one will ever know unless we so choose. Michel starts walking towards the door closes it and locks it and then turns his full attention towards Peter. Peter: Well now a day’s entertainment is at an all time high, we have movies, music and television hitting every home more and more everyday. I have decided that I want to become an entertainer. I will get the word of the Illumaniti out by this and we will see fame and glory all of a sudden. Michel: What do you mean by entertainer? Peter: Well as you have noticed just like me that wrestling is on in almost every house every Monday night. This is where I will come in I have started training and I have actually started enjoying some of the wrestling moves. When I get to the top we will see the Illumaniti word in every home in the nation. Michel just looks at his son with obvious disappointment and starts working again no words are exchanged as the two continue their scientific discovery. We switch back to modern time we see Peter Vetra sitting in an open field in front of a statue; the statue is the world famous statue of Atlas. He is sitting on the base and is looking deep in thought. Inside his head things are flying in different directions. Father, you are no longer here, I didn’t listen to you and I hope you are not mad at me. You made me stop and think and for one actual moment I was hesitating coming into this role as a performer and wrestler. But now it is paying off, the word of the Illumaniti will be out their soon, the world is not ready for this group of higher intellectuals but the time has come. Our society has taken me in and they have agreed to allow me to spread the word through the world of entertainment. Father I have disobeyed you, I didn’t listen to you but what you did was make me stop and think, I was not sure if this was the route to go through. You being the smart man you always were wrong on this subject, the Illumaniti will become known and I will be the one to let the world know about us. You did one thing you made me hesitate about my decision you were the one who was my idol, and you didn’t agree but I went forth anyways, I will not let you down. Science has always been something I have enjoyed, the ideas of how all these different substances can add up in different variations to make a different substance each time. Science you have to study to understand what is going to happen in this life, I have never seen a thing that takes so much intelligence nor so much time to try and figure out. Well I have finally figured it out and we will soon see what I can do with the most recent discoveries. Science takes patience and skill like many other things in life, I have decided to put both my patience and my skills into this tournament called GTT4 was that a smart choice? I do not know and we will soon find out as I will be able to get the words of the Illumaniti out to the whole world. Round one a competitor I have never heard of, actually one I could care less to even know about but what will happen is we will see me surpassing him in wrestling ability and getting the one the two and the three taking home the win in which I will hopefully go on to the finals and get the world to recognize the intelligence of the Illumaniti. Paulton will see a new wrestling style one that is not fixed upon the move I used nor the amount of pain that is delivered but it will be strategically and all my moves will flow into one another showing the skills I posses include brains not just brawn like my opponent. The time will come for him and he will see the wraith I have and he will see that it was a mistake entering such a tournament of this caliber with being someone with no wrestling skills. Paulton you will see what it is like to be of higher intelligence, moves need a rhythm and every other move needs a rhyme so Paulton you are going to step into the ring with a man of high intelligence. Peter Vetra stands up to see the surrounding area, he whips his face as he tries to understand and end the battle of all the different discussions going on inside his head. He walks away from the statue as he does he takes one last look at the statue he tries to model himself around someone who can control and hold the entire world.
