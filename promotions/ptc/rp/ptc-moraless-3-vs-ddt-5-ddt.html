Moraless #3 (UWF)vs DDT #5 (XUW)

12 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
12 March 2004 The Darkness. It's that place outside the light, bordered by the shadows. It's nothing and everything all at once. Alpha and Omega. The Darkness is power. Its power has a history in the XUW. The Darkness, and the Army that has served it, has proven that despite all the claim of it being a “gimmick” used to scare those who do not understand its words and ways, that it is an unstoppable entity, that takes what it wants when it wants. With it, The Army of Darkness did everything, without worry of repercussion or regret. Until now. The Darkness, even to those who draw upon the power of it, must ultimately give something back. A sacrifice if you will. It's not right away. A moment will come. You'll be sick or feeling troubled or deeply in love or quietly uncertain or even content for the first time in your life. It doesn't matter. Beyond anything you can trace back too, you'll suddenly feel it. You'll no longer be the person you once were. You'll detect slow, subtle shifts going on all around you, and most importantly, shifts in yourself. Worse, you'll realize it's always been shifting. You won't understand why or how, and you'll even forget what granted you this awareness to begin with. Old distractions - TV, books, movies - no longer provide the shelter they once did. You'll try to sort it out on paper, writing in a journal, scribbling notes. It's then you begin to really feel it. You'll no longer trust anything. The rooms seem darker, hallways longer, and the shadows much, much deeper. You'll try then, to find a sky so full of stars, it would blind you. You'd stare at the sun for a glimpse of something else. But even the sun is dark. The starry sky, even with all of it's iridescent magic, will no longer cause your eyes to linger on the light, or trace the constellations. You'll only care about the darkness, and you'll watch it for days, months, years, trying to believe that just by looking at it, you can keep it at bay. It will get so bad, you'll be afraid to look away. You'll be afraid to sleep. Then no matter where you are, in a restaurant, in the comfort of your own home, surrounded by your best friends, even in the ring with tens of thousands watching on, you'll watch yourself dismantle every assurance you ever lived by. You'll stand aside as a complexity intrudes, tearing apart, piece by piece, all of your conceived denials, whether deliberate or unconscious. Then, for better or worse, unable to fight it any longer, you'll face and turn at what you've become, what you've always been, the creature you are, that we all are, buried in the nameless black of a name. Then the real nightmare begins. This is what has befallen the Army of Darkness. Raven X. Fae. DDT...... A nightmare for three who’s lives were to bring pain to whomever they wished. To cause suffering at their whim. To hurt, for the sheer joy of it. The Darkness took all of plunder it’s Army had brought it. It flourished, and it gave back to it’s Army, undeniable gifts. Fortune, respect, fear, titles.....The Army of Darkness was unstoppable. Until the Darkness turned upon it. The Ripper paid the ultimate price, perhaps an unconscious sacrifice for the one who served it best, by giving his life. It’s impossible to say now, but perhaps The Ripper’s death should have been the first warning. Now, a year has passed since that day, and the Army that once marched at will, hides in a foxhole, moving between the mortar shells, trying to stay alive. Each has chosen their own path, but each path has the same destination. To leave the foxhole, and begin the march again. To raise its flag, stare into the eyes of whomever they wish, and see the fear as they stare back, knowing the odds are against them, no matter what. To do that, they must do what they themselves have said, time and time again, could not. They must defeat The Darkness. _________________________________________________ Everyone has a place that they go. When they’re tired, hurt, both emotionally and mentally, they ind that place, to heal, to rest. DDT has been in this place for some time now. Day. Weeks. He last stepped in a ring in an unknown place. The PTC Extreme Rumble. He walked in unknown to anyone, and walked out, though in defeat, someone that many would not soon forget. But DDT could not linger on the respect he gained, only on another failure. He’s failed many times in the last weeks. The war between the Army of Darkness and Skeletons of Society has begun to near the final battle. A battle that will take place on the biggest stage. But as that time approaches, there must be preparations. DDT knows what he’s up against in the SoS. It’s the unknown he must battle. The unknown within himself. The Darkness that once saved him, has begun to destroy him. Before he can defeat the evil trying to take his body from him, he must defeat the evil that has his soul......... He sits on the cold gray floor, leaning against the equally cold and gray wall. The room, much like an unfinished basement, is as blank as the stare on his face. He stares across the room, and we notice, the entire wall, is a mirror. He stares at the mirror. He stares at himself. While DDT stares with no expression, somehow, the reflection smiles back at him. The reflection stands, and walks towards DDT, stopping at the undeniable border between it’s reality and the other. The reflection speaks............. Is DDT finished sulking yet? Is DDT finished feeling sorry for himself.........feeling bad that he loses. The reflection laughs. DDT doesn’t move. His face like stone. Oh? At a loss for words? Interesting...for one that talks so much....and does so little. Where would you be without me DDT? Not an Icon of Hardcore. Not even in the AoD. Without me DDT.....DDT is nothing. But that’s what DDT has seem to become. DDT is nothing. DDT loses, limps home, and hides. The reflection shakes it’s head, and turns it’s back. DDT stands, and walks to the mirror. The reflection stops, and turns back again. Something to say DDT? Anger begins to grow on DDT’s face. I’m not hiding. I’m standing right here. But what about Vendetti? Overkill? They’re hunted by someone that isn’t DDT. DDT lets another do what he could not. Why? DDT closes his eyes. It’s not about doing things that way anymore. (In a mocking tone)It’s not about doing things that way anymore. Silence. There was once a time when DDT would do ANYTHING. DDT stalked Naron and her child, just to cause Henrik to go over the edge. DDT used a tazer to electrocute, and beat the legendary Samurai Bob. DDT viciously attacked Jack Nobbledy, just to bring back The Ripper. DDT did these things without a second thought, and things much worse. Now....DDT doesn’t do these things anymore. I don’t need to. Oh really? How has DDT been doing these days? Losing to Overkill in a hardcore match? Losing to Wildlife and Kata? Losing in the Extreme Rumble? The reflection stands there, awaiting an answer. It gets none. Now DDT is challenged by someone he’s never faced...alone anyway. This man....this Moraless. He talks of respect of DDT. Why? Why does he respect DDT? He says he respects that he challenges despite the pain he feels He respect the raw grit, the talent he saw in the rumble. Is this the same DDT? The one who doesn’t need to do whatever it takes? DDT looks into the mirror and steps nose to nose with the reflection. He breath causing the glass under his flared nostrils to fog. No. This fight with Moraless is a return. A return to something I once did for fun. I fought in that ring. I did battle with others who also, found enjoyment in the pain they gave, and the pain they received. It’s not a match born of hate, or jealousy, or anger. It’s not about blood feuds, it’s about blood. Blood that we will stain the canvas with. Not for anyone but ourselves. You. You’re a creature born of hate, of jealousy, of anger. You need those things to survive. You, not me. This war with the SoS, they did start it. They’ve looked since day one to end the AoD. Out of anger, out of hate, out of jealousy. Pete left the AoD, out of hate, out of anger, out of jealousy. I nearly ended my life for these reasons. AND I SAVED IT!!!! The reflection points to the scars on his arm. The scars that clearly spell the word DDT has lived by.....Hardcore. You didn’t save my life that night...you took it for yourself. Ever since, I felt it slowly slip away. I want it back. This battle against Moraless, is the beginning. A battle of respect. A hardcore battle. Nothing more, nothing less. For the love of the game. What if I don’t let you? The reflection begins to laugh. DDT steps back, as the reflection laughs louder. DDT winds up, and punches the glass, causing it to shatter....and then...a flash. DDT sits up in his bed, drenched in sweat..... ---- 16 March 2004 No one knows what it's like To be the bad man To be the sad man Behind blue eyes No one knows what it's like To be hated To be fated To telling only lies But my dreams They aren't as empty As my conscience seems to be I have hours, only lonely My love is vengeance That's never free -The Who It’s been a while since I’ve slept. A few hours here and there, but nothing solid. DDT sits in a large chair. So large, it engulfs him, making him seem almost childlike in it. His face is unshaven, his eyes bloodshot. He looks like crap. They’ve never been this bad...the nightmares. The human mind being torn in two can be quite.....quite painful. It’s been almost 2 days since I last dozed off, and I’m afraid, I can’t hold back much longer... DDT sinks deeper into the chair. He’s not going to be very happy...... FLASH into DDT’s mind.... He stands in a ring. His head cut. His face masked in crimson. His body bruised, and drenched in sweat. Across the ring from him....himself. This version of himself though, walks with a strut, unhurt, not a drop of sweat. He’s laughing as the other DDT labors to breathe. What’s the matter DDT? Isn’t this what DDT wanted? In the ring? Two men? Battling each other? Isn’t this fun?!!!! A chair materializes in his hands, and he smashes it over DDT’s head, sending him crashing down. He unfolds the chair, and sets it over DDT, the bar across DDT’s neck. He sits down, leaning over the back, staring down at DDT. You see DDT. You can’t beat me. I’m too much of what DDT is. The competition you seek.....that comes from me. The want to feel pain...to cause pain...that’s me. You can’t be rind of me DDT. Without me.....there is no Darkness within you. Without the Darkness......you’re nothing. He laughs in DDT’s face, as he struggles to breathe. Then, with a final breath, he lets out a very primal howl, which ceases the laughter. He stands, taking the pressure off his neck. The room grows dark. Then....a figure appears behind him. He turns...there stands Sidhe Nihm. Not the loveable Fallen Fae, but the cold blooded man known as Sidhe Nihm. Next to him, another figure. His body covered in a purple and white latex body suit, stands Raven X. Behind them...an large figure looms, staying in the shadows. DDT’s enemy takes a step backwards, as his friends step forward. He stumbles over himself. Now he is on the mat, crawling backwards.....and stopping at the feet of DDT. You are the Darkness within me...that’s true. You’re the result of the pain and suffering an innocent child had to endure. You are the result of the torture, the evil, the hate. You are the reason I needed to feel the pain, and why I needed to cause so much pain. You’re the reason Damien D. Teri became DDT. You are the Darkness within me. Now....it’s time for you to go. Raven X and Sidhe pick up the figure. I’m not ungrateful mind you. You see, you also were responsible for these two being here. These two, my friends as they once were, when the Darkness ruled them completely, are constant reminders that we all changed. We all evolved within the Darkness. All except me. I’ve never changed. DDT has always been, as you made him. The Hardcore Icon...nothing more, nothing less. I don’t want that anymore. DDT needs to be more then that now. For The AoD, for XUW, for DDT. And what of Moraless? Can you fight in Extreme conditions without me? What of The Skeletons of Society at Reborn? Can you truly believe that without me, you stand a chance?! DDT sighs and looks down at his feet. His alter ego smiles back, until DDT raises his eyes to meet his. Yes. Moraless and I both know that each of us are something more then what we were last time we met. I can’t say what he’s done to prepare differently. He knows, just as I do however, that each of us have the desire, the will, and the ability to be more extreme then anyone else in that realm. We know that when we face off, we stare at a man who knows to get what they seek, they’ll have to look deep within to find that desire, to be fueled by that will, and bring out that ability. This is my evolution. I used to say something that, until now, didn't make much sense. I used to say "I'm afraid......not of you.....of myself." My evolution begins when I stop being afraid of myself. I'm nothing until that happens. As you go........it happens. The large figure in the background reaches out, and pulls the dark DDT into the shadows. Sidhe and Raven RX nod, and follow into the black, leaving DDT alone. The lights fade, till all is shadow. DDT stands alone in his mind. He's not gone forever, he'll always be right here. DDT taps his head. I can't kill him off. It's impossible. That's always been the failure of those who opposed the Darkness. They tried to vanquish it. They tried to be the ones to end it. You can't. You can't end the evil you feel within you own soul. We all have it. The most virtuous man walking the earth harbors ill thoughts somewhere in the depths of his being. It's how we're made....imperfect perfection. Negative and positive, constantly in a state of battle for supremacy. I accept that now, and while somewhere down the road, that part of me may take over again, I know next time, the battle within myself will be just as difficult, and the result just as necessary. I couldn't be that person to fight you Moraless. I couldn't be that person to finally end this war with the SoS. That part of me served no purpose to the times I exist in now. But a warning... Just because this part of me is gone, don't believe I no longer can do what I must. I am still DDT...I am still the Hardcore Icon...only now...I'm much more. How much more...we soon shall see....... So it is written...... So it is done............ FLASH..... DDT sleeps peacefully......
