A Rude Awakening (RP vs The Phantom Republican)

16 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-12-16 The room was dark, the only light in the room came from the monitors that jumped to life with every beat of the patient's heart. The identity of the person is not known, but it is easily noticeable that the person is a man. The mustache and beard matted with blood gave it away immediately. His face and neck were covered in bruises, it's assumed that the rest of his body was probably littered with them as well. Like a tattered old quilt, stitches traveled across his face like railroad tracks to keep his skin from separating. His right eye was swelled shut, where blood had collected in the corner of his eye and scabbed over. His cheek looked ready to explode at any moment, as if the slightest breeze would cause it to erupt like a pimple on a prepubescent teen. He almost looked like a Jack O' Lantern that had been left out in the sun too long. The scene was horrid, like something you would have seen on an old Twilight Zone episode, yet there was someone at his side; holding his hand. This person was much younger than the gentleman with a tube jammed down his throat, forcing air into his lungs; which were slowly filling with blood from the cracked rib that had punctured one. With a fuller head of hair, you almost wouldn't recognize Sammy Brown as he sat there with his head bowed and his eyes closed. Deep in prayer, he clutched the man's hand between both of his own, a steady stream of tears drying up on his cheeks. A flood of light illuminated the room, a soft iridescent glow surrounding the silhouette of Sammy; giving him an angelic appearance. "Excuse me," someone whispered from behind him, breaking his concentration long enough to get him to turn his attention over his shoulder. "Visiting hours will be over in about five minutes, and Doctor Williams asked me to be sure that mister Brown was given ample time alone to rest." "I understand," was Sammy's reply, as he lifted his hand to wipe away a few of the still moist tears trickling along the edge of his nostril. "Can you let my mother know, so she can come spend some time with him before we leave." "Your mother has been given permission to stay the night," the nurse was in pale green hospital scrubs, her tawny brown hair pulled up into a messy bun, eagerly awaiting the graveyard shift she's been working for the last nine months. It was apparent that she did not like having to tell Sammy that he had to leave, but regulations were set for a reason. "I'm sorry to cut your time short, but we do have rules." He took note of the awkward smile she flashed him, then gave her a curt nod before kissing his index and middle finger and pressing the tips to the man's forehead. "Good night, Dad." Sammy then staggered back a few inches, surprised by the sudden jerk of his father's arm. Lights began to flash, machines started to whir to life while others began to beep and buzz uncontrollably. "You need to leave now," the nurse yelled out as she rushed past him. She began to press buttons on one of the monitors, as three other attendants barreled through the open door. The original nurse turned back once more and screamed, "Get him out of here!" He felt a clammy hand grab him by the nape of his neck, before he was suddenly lifted into the air. "What the hell," he screamed out, as he was pulled out of his nightmare and dropped into yet another one. This one far more realistic, considering he was no longer asleep. His face had slammed against the cold hardwood floor of his apartment, but he was certain he had not fallen off his bed. Further proven by the bottom of someone's boot being pressed against the side of his face, which was now sandwiched between the hardened rubber sole and the frigid floor. "How ya doin', Sammy?" He could not recognize the voice, but the person knew his name. A sudden rush of fear began to tickle his senses, as his heart started to beat faster with every passing moment. "Mr. DeAngelis isn't too happy with your decision to drop out of that wrestling thingamajig," you could almost hear his lips separate into a smile, "so he sent me over to see if I could make you reconsider." Sammy didn't have time to answer the question, though he did not expect anything to change; given the answer that was dangling by a thread on the tip of his tongue. The pressure on his head, as the man added a bit more of his body weight, caused his head to throb from the echo that seemed to bounce around infinitely. "So what do ya say, huh… You gonna play nice?" He opened his mouth to answer, then everything went black as the pressure went away… then slammed back down.
