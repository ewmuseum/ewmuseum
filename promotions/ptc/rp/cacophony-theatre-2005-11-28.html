Cacophony Theatre

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 "Everyone ready?" The speaker had to look down on the assembled group. He stood above them far above them in fact, he was wearing six foot stilts. He wobbled just a bit, clearly not being used to wearing the devices. On his head a wig hair the color of cherry kool-aid sticking straight up. The other performers at his feet all nodded in agreement, most dressed in pure white coveralls with a matching pure white ball caps. In the back stands a lone figure, dressed as a poodle complete with poodle mask, the kind you get at the dollar store with the rubber band to hold the thing in place. "Why in the hell are we doing this again?" It was the poodle who spoke, arms crossed over his chest clearly somewhat annoyed by the situation. "Because it’s funny. Besides, Stevens is awesome, we should do something in tribute." The speaker this time a paunchy member of the group dressed as one of the dog catchers. "Christ Phyl, I should’ve known this was your idea. And how the hell is dressing up like a poodle and having you guys chase me a tribute to some wrestler?" "It’s real simple in the first round of the GTT5 tournament Stevens is facing a guy called the Big Dawg Jason Hix." "I thought you said my character’s name was Jake Hix?" "Jason, Jake, who cares they’re both J names. Some people get those things confused you know." "Only you Phyl, only you." "Ass. Besides this is going to be good, after it’s all over I’ll treat everyone to cotton candy." "Alright, sounds good to me." "Hey wait I’ve got a question, why the hell do I have to wear these stilts anyway?" This time it was the stilts who spoke, and to be perfectly honest, if you didn’t know that, you should be drug out into the street and shot. "It’s supposed to symbolize how Stevens is like a giant among men, or something. I dunno, stilts are cool." "Fair enough. Now, we’re all ready, lets do this thing." The poodle man came forward and stood next to the doorway. Did I mention everyone was in a big room, like a warehouse or something? Yeah I did, you just must have missed it. Anyway pushing it open he charges forward onto the sidewalk, with each step he lets out something vaguely resembling a bark. The people on the sidewalk watch him pass perplexed looks on their faces, occasionally a small child cowers in fear, it’s not often a giant poodle terrorizes the streets. Coming out of the warehouse half a step behind him are the army of dog catchers. As they run, they chant, Gregorian style. "Whoooooooooo let the dawwwwwwgs out, oooooooooooooh whooooo leeeeeet theeeeeeeem ouuuuuuut." The small army valiantly tries to leash the beast, but he’s simply too fast. With every attempt to lasso the creature he bobs and weaves away. Clearly this is one poodle adept at evading capture. Suddenly Poodlezilla turns on his heel and raises both fists in a fighters stance twirling them in front of his body like the cowardly lion. "Put em up put em up." The dog catchers come to a quick halt. One ventures forward cautiously the portly one known as Phyl. Holding out one hand in a friendly gesture, he spoke. "It’s ok puppy, we’re not going to hurt you." "WOOF WOOF, I’m a big dawg!" The poodle charged forward, Phyl yelped and dove to the side, desperate get out of the poodle’s path of destruction. The rest of the dog catchers follow suit, parting like the Red Sea, and "Hix" bounds past them alternately barking and shouting "big dawg." Again the dog catchers chant. "Whooooo let this dawwwwwwwg ouuuuuuuuut. Nooooooow thaaaaaaaaat heeeeeee’s ouuuuuuut whooooooooo shaaaaal saaaaaave uuuus." "I WILL!" He came out of nowhere, in fact I’m not even sure how he got there but the stilted man had arrived. Behind him a group of scantly clad dancing girls throwing confetti and rose pedals at his feet, er um stilt pegs. At the sound of his voice the poodle turned, growling low in his throat dropping down to all fours the poodle barked furiously, as The Stilted "Stevens" strode forward. "I am Stevens the dog trainer! It’s time to make you OBEDIENT BIG DAWG HIX!" The women swooned, and sang. "Our hero, our savior Stevens the king, taking it out of the ring." The stilts took another step forward and started to wobble. Cartwheeling his arms for balance, he was unsuccessful and hit the ground in a heap. "OW! I TOLD YOU THE STILTS WERE A DUMB IDEA!" "But they’re cool." "Bark." The poodle bounded forward launched itself through the air as the people on the streets looked on in shocked silence. It landed on the stilted man’s chest his wig now on the ground near him, he couldn’t recover it. Still on all fours the poodle man "walked" a circle on "Stevens" before finally lifting a leg to piss. "Dude that’s not supposed to happen, Stevens was supposed to catch you, you’re not supposed to pee on him." "Bark, every dawg has his day." "Will someone please get this asshole off me, I think I broke my leg." "Bark, serves you right for wearing stilts." "Yeah maybe they’re really supposed to symbolize over confidence." " I told you they’re supposed to symbolize how cool Stevens is." "Who cares, the cops are coming." "What?" Want to know who said what? Yeah I do too. In any case it’s true, a couple officers on foot were beginning to approach they didn’t look pleased either. At the sight the poodle jumped off the stilts chest and broke into a dead run full speed ahead. "CALL ME MINT JELLY! CAUSE I’M ON THE LAMB!" The dog catchers and dancing girls all scattered as well, knowing the police couldn’t catch them all while the guy stilts whimpered on the ground. "Assholes, I want my damn cotton candy." The end.
