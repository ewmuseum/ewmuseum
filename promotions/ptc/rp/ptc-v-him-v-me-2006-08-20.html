PTC V Him V Me

20 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-20 It's a proven fact. Nobody wanted "the New Shit" Bobino in this contest. Still, even after proving himself worthy in a free-for-all, nobody wants him here. He wasn't chosen by Primetime Central. He wasn't chosen by FUSE. The only reason his name is even remotely associated with this match, is because he made it so. It all began when Primetime Central announced this thrown together, pathetic excuse to steal a championship away from a man they did not want it on. The FUSE roser was assembled, the men in charge were informing the wrestlers of the situation, and why they should care. As their words rattled out meaninglessly, the roster actually looked interested, especially one figure, who sat in the middle of the crowd, his black trench coat hanging from his shoulders, down to the side of the chair, onto the floor. He listened, actually caring for once about something other than his own glory. Ok, you caught me. That was a lie. He made it appear as if he cared, but down inside, he really just wanted to be able to claim he was the savior of not only FUSE Wrestling, but of all of Primetime Central. He only wanted the recognition of being what he believed himself to be: Better Than Human. As the executives babbled on, Derek Silver, the television station's executive opened the floor to the roster with the statement that brought them all there that day. "Primetime Central has asked us to send one, and only one, represenative from FUSE Wrestling into this match. Whomever we choose, for a free Extreme Title shot. We're going to be fair about this, so we want you guys to choose who you think is best suited to represent what FUSE stands for, and to take the PTC Extreme Title home for FUSE." Instantly, everyone started murmuring, trying to figure out the best canidate to take out the "enemy" that is Archibald MacGreggor. As everyone spoke at once, one man suddenly demanded everyone's attention, jumping to his feet in the middle of the crowd, his eyes locked onto the Derek Silver, the man that raised the question. "The Master of Darwinism's" grin widened a bit, letting his teeth barely show through. He chuckled as he addressed the entire roster of FUSE Wrestling. "There really is no choice here. Basiclly, we have to figure out who -IS- FUSE, and I have the answer. The man that has been a part of this company longer than every single person in this room. The man that has sat in the back, and let the new blood rise. The man that's done everything for this company. The man that will take down Archibald and anybody else Primetime Central brings in. The man who will take the PTC Extreme Title. Your Senior member of the roster, 'The New Shit' Bobino." The room sat still in time for just a moment. Every single eye in the room had been glued to Bobino, and every single mind in the room simultaniously thought the same thing. As everyone watched on, Evan "Damage" Jackson spoke the words everyone else thought. "Bobby, that's real brave of you, to you know, kill yourself like that, but we want to -WIN- this match. We need somebody that can really put the fight to everyone else." Bobino's eyes narrowed, as every head in the room bobbed in agreement. He had never been so embarassed to consider himself a part of that roster. He was speechless for a moment, absolutly crushed that his FUSE Family didn't believe in him. "I can win this, Evan." Jackson looked at him, as if in disbelief that the word "win" would even come out of Bobino's mouth. "When was the last time you won anything, Bob? Even a dark match? Face it, you've been here the longest, because you're the only person that gets beat this many times, and keeps coming back. we need somebody who can win, and we need somebody we can all trust in this match." Bobino slowly slumped back down in his seat, as everyone looked on. They all felt a small amount of pity for him, but everything Jackson had said was true. Bobino had been FUSE's jobber as of late, and maybe they did need someone who was on a better winning streak to take over. Bobino didn't hear much more of that roster meeting. He didn't really try to listen. He had been so disheartened that the people he had worked so hard to help, couldn't give him the same chance he gave them. at some point, someone suggested that Tigera go into the match, and she was voted in by the entire roster, except one. Bobino showed his passive defiance by voting agaisnt Tigera, although his one vote was meaningless. The next day, Primetime Central announced their latest supershow, the world versus Archibald MacGreggor, with FUSE Wrestling's own Tigera as one of the represenatives trying to take down the rogue free agent. --------------------------------- Later that same night, Bobino thought long and ahrd about his future with FUSE wrestling. He had been with the company literally longer than everybody that currently was with them. He held numerous titles, and gave everything he had everytime he did something for them. Every match he participated in could be called a Match of The Year canidate. In the past few years, he had been letting the younger group get wins, to make the future of FUSE Wrestling look even better. He didn't lay down for them, he let them triumph in bloody, violence wars that would be remembered for many years to come. He had never asked for anyhting in return. Until now. All he asked for in return for all his years of work, and multiple angled losses, was a simple shot in a clusterfuck of an attempt to dethrone an unsigned champion. Bobino went to sleep that very night, with every intention of retirement, and at the time, he beleived nothing could change his mind. --------------------------------- Within the next few weeks, it had become apparent that an injury that had plagued Tigera for the past few months would finally require major rehabilitation. The very day that she told FUSE management that she would be taking time off, another meeting would be held to name the replacement in Primetime Central's Extreme Title match. Bobino's hopes rose for the first time since being crushed, as now there was surely no way they could say no. "Absolutly Not!" The words stabbed Bobino as if he had just heard that his best friend had just died. He was dumbfounded by how they could seriously decline his offer to represent the company. "Why not? Who has a better chance at winning this thing more than me? Who's done more to deserve to be given this shot? Who do you think has done more for this company than me?" Bobino waited for a response, even though he honestly believed there would be none. He knew that there could not be a single person in the room that could honestly say they deserved anything more than him. Problem is, there was a man that could dishonestly say he was more deserving. "I do." Damien Cruz, FUSE's superstar walked forward from the crowd, stepping in front of Bobino, making his intentions clear. "I stand a much better chance of winning that match than you. In fact, I'd go as far as to say that you can't beat me, and that is proof enough that I will be going to this thing, not you. Your shit's old, Bobino, and none of us want to have you be the center of FUSE." Bobino assumed that everyone agreed with Cruz. He assumed, because he didn't stay in that room another moment. He took one last look around the room, and picked up his trench coat on his way out the door. --------------------------------- The next day, Bobino's day was set to be ne for the books. He had been woken at five-thirty in the morning by a neghbor's loud pet. He burned himself cooking breakfast, and wehn he got in his car to drive to the FUSE arena, his car stalled out. He arrived over an hour late to the arena, and he was pretty sure that his taxi driver had somehow run up his fare. Needless to say, it wasn't looking good for Bobino. Let me take this time to point out that sometimes, life chooses the oddest time to do us a favor. You'll find the cheap gas right after filling your tank. Your lotto numbers come up the one day you don't buy a ticket… OR In this case, you'll find a chance to get what nobody wants you to have, after you've given up all hope on it. Bobino found his way to the arena late, and performed at another show, losing a match to Xantippe in what could only be described as a "valiant squash". He made his way home, but when he arrived, he found one message waiting on his answering machine. --------------------------------- Bobino waited outside the building for half an hour before it opened. When they unlocked the doors, he emmidiately walked in and became the first to sign his name on the line. He had entered himself in a Free-For-All match to determine the last person to get a shot at Archibald MacGreggor's Extreme Title. The match lasted for close to an hour, and at the end, Bobino capitalized on a mistake to hit the Un-Natural Selection and win the match. He celebrated his victory by icing his muscles, and making a phone call to FUSE Wrestling's Owner, informing him that Damien Cruz would not leave the event with the Extreme Title. That brings us to the present day. Bobino had qualified himself, against everyone's will to walk into Primetime Central's Event as a challenger. The programs say that he represents FUSE Wrestling, but I doubt Bobino would tell you the same. In fact, I think that if you asked Bobino he'd tell you something about eliminating the weakness from Primetime Central and FUSE Wrestling. He finds irony in the fact that he's as much of an outsider in this amtch as Archibald MacGreggor, and he damn sure doesn't think that he's going to embarass himself or FUSE Wrestling. No, I think that if you asked Bobino right now, his prediction would be that the last two men standing would be the two men nobody wants to have the championship: Archibald MacGreggor and "The New Shit" Bobino. I'm sure you know who he thinks will walk away from that confrontation.
