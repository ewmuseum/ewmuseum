Dreams do come true!

11 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-11 Dreams do come true… ~v~ Angel sat quietly tapping his pen on the wooden table. Scribbling a second pen onto a pad he jotted random thoughts down. I am the wind whispering of eternity. I am silence. I am the junkie on the street corner. I am the moralist who condemns. I am the one who stops to help the stranger. I am the one who looks away. I am the sick pervert slavering over the dead. I am the lamb who nails you to the cross. I am that wild joy that sends you running through the meadow. I am the rotting corpse that astonishes you on the battlefield. I am the slaughterhouse of ideals, the apostle of despair. I am all. I am all you wish for. I am all you fear. I am the worm, the leaf and the stone. I am every character in every play, in every book. I am proud, despicable, profound. I am wise. I am the prancing fool, the voice of nonsense, the sage of reason. I am transcendent. I trail worlds behind me. I am oblivion. I am the gate to Heaven. I am truth, and I am hell. ----- It had been a week since she had caught the avian flu, and every day had been a fight once they detected its presence. Immediately, she was bused to the hospital for intensive care, but even the most experienced doctors couldn't find her a cure. They said it would be a matter of days, and her parting wish was to see her fiancé before she left. ------ Angel had received a call during his GTT5 press session that his high school sweetheart was dying of the avian flu. For days, he had been waiting to hear any news of what was happening to Rachel. His parents picked him up from the airport at 11:38 in the morning, the rest period for the tournament was almost over, to give Rachel her final wish; her moment of peace. When they arrived at the hospital in grim procession, only Angel was allowed to pass into the quarantined section. They handed him a mask to save him from any potential bacteria, but he threw it into his pocket with a silent curse: if she was dying, he might as well risk his life also. As he saw her, he took in a sharp breath. This wasn't the vibrant, laughing beauty he had once known. Rachel was now anemic, pale, and cold looking, far from what he knew. He slowly walked over to the bed, trying to take in what was happening right in front of him. She was dying, there was nothing he could do to save her, though he desperately wanted to. As he sat down on the bed, the metal springs clinked, and she opened her eyes abruptly, the sharp grey green contrasting to her very white skin. She smiled, and whispered one word, "Michael." Rachel tried to sit up in bed, but her muscles didn't have the amount of oxygen required to move, Angel saw. Each breath was rasping, painful, and it took all her strength to keep drawing in air. He hated to see her like this, but it was too late to run away from it. He rested his hand on her cheek which he noticed was already cold and thought with a sudden, stark realization, "She’s on her way out already, she's only alive to see me." He gave her a soft, sad smile, risking breaking into silent tears. She wrinkled her nose and whispered, "Well, don't you go crying, or I'll start crying and then we'll have a huge crying fest. Then my mom will start cying and then the doctors will--" And there she broke off, set into a coughing fit. Angel heard that the fatal flu had taken over the tissue in her lungs, and was slowly dissolving it inside out, leaving fluid and blood inside the remaining cavity. "No talking sexy", he said quietly. She shut up and looked at him. He rested his forehead against hers, amazed by her willingness to still comply with what he wanted. There was really no need for words in the next couple of moments. Angel picked Rachel up gingerly and cradled her in his arms, surprised by the lightness of her body. And together they both cried silently, not wanting to let each other go. "I hate seeing you like this… You can let go now; it's okay to, I'm here." Angel kissed her nose gently, something he relished in doing, but occasionally forgot about how much he enjoyed it. Once Rachel broke her moment of silence. "But.. I'd rather live in suffering then let go of you. It's not fair!" The soft defiance broke off there and Angel smiled through the silent tears. "Stubborn to the end", he thought. "I know it's not fair; it's never fair… but you know I'll see you again." Rachel glanced up at him with a smile. "Is that what you really think, or what you want me to leave thinking?" She broke down into coughs again, this time worse, the spasm lasting longer. Angel winced at the thickness of the cough. "No, it's what I think…. Besides, you know you want to see your cat Midnight again… And when I die, we can all run around with each other doing stuff!" There was a tinge of sadness in his voice as he said the "happy" exclamation, keeping them tied down to earth as they both knew that time would be very far off. "But… I don't want to die… I want to stay here, with you, and live the rest of my life with you, and make sure you're taken care of and ---" Angel broke of the wishes with a kiss; he didn't want to hear it. It was already enough seeing her die, he didn’t want to think of what could've and should've been. Rachel pulled away gently. "You'll get sick. Don't. It's disgusting." "Better me than you", he said as he stroked her hair gently, savoring everything about her he had taken for granted in the last months. "Cause then you'll be alive." Rachel smiled. "Great last few minutes. Some of the best I've ever had." She laughed, but it came out more like a wheeze. "I wanted my last with you… partly to make sure you didn't do anything stupid", she continued. "I want you to keep living, move on, live you're life… but don't forget the fragility of a human life." At that point she began coughing again, and gulped for air after a few seconds, her body shaking with effort. Angel nearly started crying again at that remark. How could he live without his best friend and his most trusted ally? How? Then there was a silence, a natural break in time, until Rachel got over her wheezing. "Just… don't let go of me until I die… please…." A tear rolled down Rachel’s cheek and Angel wiped it away. "I don't want to ever let you go. Not even after. They'll have to pry you from my arms." He tightened his grip slightly and a tear from his cheek landed on hers. ------- It was a few minutes until Angel heard his sweetheart's shaking breath stop coming, and felt the life leave her body, but for about thirty minutes after, he held her in his arms and cried silently, remembering the good times and the bad, until he finally half accepted that she was gone. He raised his tearstained face and remembered her last words: "Don't forget the fragility of a human life…" He repeated this to himself until he saved it in his memory forever, along with Rachel’s beautiful smile and her fight to live. He gently put her down on the bed again, wiped his tears off his face and hers, and looked at her. She was still wearing the pieces of jewelry he gave her. He smiled a bittersweet smile. "My only diehard fan…" He kissed her lips softly, tenderly, as though she was sleeping and he didn't want to wake her. "I'll see you in eternity, my love." With that, he tore himself away and walked to the door. He hesitated. He turned back and whispered something he hated whispering to her on a regular basis. "I love you baby, I love you." With that, he walked out the door, and got ready to go home to save the pictures in the memories, forever in her image. What else to look up for, bound by contract to finish the GTT5; depression already sinking in; look on life plummeting on a downward spiral. How will this affect Angel…for better or worse?
