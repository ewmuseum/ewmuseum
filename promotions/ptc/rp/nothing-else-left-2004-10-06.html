Nothing Else Left

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-06 Okay, this is a reworking of my previously DQed 2nd Round rp vs Kenny Mercury. I got the ok from Argyle to do this. When last we left the cottage retreat of Dallas Reeves, his entourage had headed off to bed, leaving him alone with his thoughts. Thoughts of his own distressed and splintered personal life, and thoughts of his upcoming matches, in the NGW and in the GTT4. Not much has really happened since then. Dallas had a glass of water, and then went to bed himself. There was an early flight they all had to catch, compliments of “Alberta” Jack Carrington’s company jet. So now we join the four men…wise old manager Carrington, bushy and unkempt bodyguard Beastor Brandt, young and fresh-faced protégé Frankie Calrissian, and of course, none other than Dallas Reeves…as they make their way down the damp tarmac at tiny Muskoka Air Terminal. It’s around 8 am, and the sky is still a little grey from the previous night’s rainfall. Their jet is boarding, and Frankie and Beastor carry the bags. Frankie, all 178 lbs of him, seems shaky with his burden. Frankie: Again…why do I have to carry all these heavy bags? Dallas: I don’t hear Beastor complaining, Frankie. Frankie quickly looks to Beastor, who shows no expression, and then back to Reeves, who is walking ahead. Frankie: That’s ‘cause he hasn’t spoken a single word since you got him out of semi-seclusion in northern Quebec! Dallas: Yeah, well…that’s just, like…you’re opinion, man. Reeves cringes, disbelieving his own voice. He’s usually not found to be at a loss for words, but his mind is many different places right now. Carrington notices Dallas’ unrest, and pats his long time friend on the shoulder. He speaks in a hushed voice, so only Dallas can hear. Carrington: Na worrehs, ma boy. Ah had Remy outfit tha jet with a case a Moosehead. New Yo’k is a few ahhs away, ya can jus’ relax. Dallas: Thanks, J.B. The four of them arrive at the jet, where two attendants relieve Frankie and the Beastor of their baggage. Frankie snags a little pouch from the pile before it’s packed on the plane. A thirty-ish looking man with a thin French-style moustache and nametag reading “Remy” greets Carrington with a nod. Frankie searches frantically through his pouch. Remy: Bienvenue, M’sieu Carrington. ‘Ow are you today? Carrington: Fine, Remy, thanks. We-uh inna rush, ah we readeh ta take off? Remy: Oui, m’sieu, we are just wai-ting for zee clearance from zee ta-wair. With Remy’s French accent and Carrington’s, uh, “western” accent bouncing off one another, Reeves can only shake his head. Remy: Zees are all your bags? Dallas: Ah, oui. Frankie: WAIT! Attention is turned towards Frankie after his outburst. Frankie: I can’t find the batteries for my Game Boy! Dallas: Frankie, what did I tell you about making sure you were packed and all ready to go? Frankie: You said, “Make sure you are packed and all ready to go”. Dallas: And? Frankie: I forgot the batteries! Aw, man! Remy: Not to worry, p’tit m’sieu, zair are some on zee plane. Frankie, while 23 yrs old, is still in many ways an uncultured, immature kid from Medicine Hat, Alberta. He stares nearly cross-eyed at Remy. Frankie: Hunh? Sea plane? Dallas: They have some on the plane, c’mon, let’s go. You know how much of a hard-on Woodson can be when we’re late for a meeting. Actually, he can be a hard-on about- Carrington: All abo’d, Dallas. Carrington follows Remy up the steps into the jet, with Beastor in tow. Frankie darts in front of Beastor, then looks back and sticks out his tongue at the hairy behemoth. Frankie: I’ve got dibs on shotgun, Beastor! Nyah nyah! Beastor actually looks a little upset, but boards the plane anyways. Reeves stops on the steps, pulling his cell phone out of his pocket. He checks to see if anyone has called, but they haven’t. He still hasn’t heard back from John Hitchin, not since their match at the Eternal Embers pay-per-view in Athens, Greece. The match put a lot of strain on their working relationship, and the team seems to still be up in the air. As well, Dallas has not heard back from his on-again, off-again girlfriend Jessika. With this in mind, Reeves enters the jet in a sombre state. The attendants close the door and single the pilot, as the jet begins to taxi out to the lone runway. The scene fades to black, as “It’s a Good Life if You Don’t Weaken” by the Tragically Hip begins to play. When the colour of the night and all the smoke for one life gives way to shaky movements, improvisational skills, a forest of whispering speakers let’s swear that we will get with the times, in a current health to stay let’s get friendship right get life day-to-day The scene opens again, on Dallas Reeves, calmly staring out the window of the plane, frosty bottle of Moosehead in his hand. Sure, it’s only like, 9:30 in the am, but who amongst you hasn’t had a beer that early in the day before? The song continues. in the forget-yer-skates dream full of countervailing woes In diverse-as-ever scenes proceeding on a need-to-know in a face so full of meaning as to almost make it glow 0’ for a good life, we just might have to weaken For many years, Dallas Reeves has done nothing but wrestle and entertain. However, lately he’s been faced with thoughts of settling down. He knows he’s not washed up; he’s only 27 yrs old. But maybe it is time for him to cut back his travelling…maybe it is time for him to stray from the vocation that he has chosen, in the best interests of his future…maybe it is time for him to settle down, at least a little. After all, that is one of the topics he wanted to discuss with NGW’s owner, Scott W., when he gets to New York. Reeves thoughts are soon broken, as his protégé Frankie rather excitedly plunks himself down in the next seat. Dallas: Yes, Frankie? Can I help ya? Frankie: Wow, New York, eh? I’ve never been there before! Man…I’m a long way from Medicine Hat, now! Dallas: Frankie, you’ve been to New York a few times. And weren’t you sitting up with the pilot? Frankie: Yeahhh, I was, but I wanted to push some buttons, then he said somethin’ ‘bout me “killing us all” if I touched anythin’, so I figger’d I’d come back and see what I could see! Dallas: Groovy, Frankie. Look, I’ve got a lot on my mind right now- Frankie: I know, Dallas. I might not be able to grow a decent moustache yet- The camera switches to a close-up of Frankie’s upper-lip, sparsely covered with a “BTM”; bad teenage moustache. Dallas nods in agreement. Frankie: But I just wanted to let you know that…well, you’ve really shown me a lot since I’ve been under your toot-…tutel… Dallas: Tutelage, yes, Frankie. Frankie: Yeah, that’s it. Well, I just wanted to thank you for all your help. Most of the other guys I did camerawork for in the NGW just laughed at me, and bullied me around, but not you. You always treated me good. Dallas: Frankie, I don’t want this turnin’ into a moment here, so go sit your ass back down on the other side of the plane. Frankie: HAHAHA Sure thing, Coach. Frankie, always in good spirits since he figured he’d “made” it big-time, dutifully leaves the side of Reeves, who can only smile to himself. Sure, with Reeves as his manager, Frankie the Cameraman has managed an above .500 record in the NGW, and captured the Untamed Title, once defended under 24/7 rules, five times, but Reeves isn’t ready to become a manager full-time. That much he knows. Then where? Actor in Hollywood? Retirement from wrestling? House-husband for an international model? Sha. 0’ for a good life, we just might have to weaken and find somewhere to go go somewhere we’re needed find somewhere to grow grow somewhere we’re needed Dallas turns his attention back out the window, and to the nearing skyline of New York City. The camera fades out on his face. The scene next opens at the front steps of NGW HQ, on a bustling street in NYC. A black limo eases itself to the curb. The driver exits, and makes his way to the back passenger door. He opens it gingerly, and out steps “Alberta” Jack Carrington, wearing a charcoal suit, bolo tie, and his trademark white cowboy hat. He runs his 53 yr old fingers through his 30 yr old moustache, and approaches the building. Stepping out next is young Frankie Calrissian, wearing blue jeans, a light blue sportcoat and a red visor. His attention turns from the Game Boy in his hands to the city before him. He looks skywards, totally revelling in the large buildings and bustling sidewalks, a far cry from his small home town in Alberta. He saunters after Carrington, still seemingly amazed. Next to exit the limo is the burly and bushy-bearded Beastor Brandt, looking surprisingly dapper for a man with his…relaxed hygiene regimen. His savage hair is pulled back in a savage pony-tail, and his free-flowing beard is pulled tightly into an even more savage-looking beard-tail thingy. He adjusts the lapels on his impressive black suit, stands beside the car door, and waits for his boss to emerge. One black leather boot hits the pavement, then another, and up stands Dallas Reeves, wearing an off-white suit, with purple shirt beneath. He runs his hand along the brim of his off-white cotton hat, then follows Beastor towards NGW Headquarters. The camera speeds up, and in fast motion the foursome walk up the steps, in through the doors, across the grey marble floor, and to the elevator. Reeves stops to sign the odd autograph, kiss some hands and shake some babies…well, he does some general fan appreciation as he crosses through the lobby. The four men board the empty elevator, and the doors close on the camera. Moments later, it reopens on the floor containing the offices of NGW owner Scott W. As the doors slide open, Frankie, Carrington and Dallas are all making gagging faces, while the Beastor can only manage a blush. Carrington pushes his way past his younger counterparts and into the waiting area. Carrington: Lemme through fi’st, Ah’m oldeh! Carrington rests his hands on his knees, and takes some deep breaths. Frankie and Dallas follow in a hurry, with the Beastor non-chalantly sauntering out of the elevator. Frankie is half-laughing, half-grossed out, whereas Dallas just has a perturbed look for his bodyguard. Frankie: Aw, man…that’s grosser than my Aunt Edna’s- Dallas: Beastor…what have I told you about doing that in enclosed spaces? Dear lord…did you eat garbage for breakfast? Woodson: AHEM, gentlemen…and Frankie. You’re late. Again. The foursome turn their heads towards the sound of the voice of the man that pays their salaries. Standing next to his receptionist’s desk is Scott W., his dreadlocks hanging over the lapels of his designer suit. Sure, he may dress champagne, but his wrestling skills are purely malt liquor. Frankie: Hiya bossman, what’s shakin’? Carrington: Misteh Woodson. Dallas: (muffling his voice) Hard-on. Woodson glances at Reeves, who straightens up a little, then winks. Woodson checks his watch, and motions towards his office. Woodson: Alright, I’m strapped for time here, so get it in gear. Frankie, I want to talk to you in a minute. Dallas, Mr. Carrington, right this way. (Woodson turns to his receptionist) Hold my calls, and keep trying to get a hold of Hitchin. Carrington and Reeves enter the office, while Beastor plops himself down on a leather chair in the waiting area. Frankie looks dejected, but Woodson points him towards a box nearby. Woodson: There oughta be a colouring book in there for ya, Frankie. Beastor…just try not to eat anything, and for god sake’s, keep your pants on this time, please. The Beastor smiles mischeviously, and Frankie’s eyes light up when he sees a colouring book that tickles his fancy. The office door closes, and we are then taken within. Woodson’s office is decorated with NGW memorabilia, as well as New York Rangers crap. Carrington and Reeves sit in the leather chairs opposite Woodson’s desk, as Scotty himself sits on the edge of it. He stares at Reeves intently, making Dallas quite uncomfortable. Dallas: Rangers suck. Woodson: Dallas, you’ve always been up-front with me, so I’m going to give you the straight goods. The NGW will be shutting its doors within the coming weeks. Reeves has a look of shock at first, but then of disappointment. After a few moments of silence, he speaks up. Dallas: Greeeat…well, I’d be lying if I said I didn’t see it coming. Woodson: That’s why your match against this Unstoppable- Dallas: Oh, he’s stoppable alright. Jus’ because the American education system didn’t- Woodson: Can it! You’re the last NGW competitor left in the GTT4, so I want you to win the whole damn thing, and give the NGW it’s proper send off. Dallas: I figured as much, boss. I’ll try my best. Cut me some slack, though…the final illustrious 49 of us are pretty high calibre guys and gals. Woodson: “Try”? “TRY”!? I don’t want you to “try”…there IS no “try”! Do you understand me?! Woodson’s face has become quite angered, as the closing of his beloved NGW has saddened him deeply. Reeves looks to Carrington for alleviation, only to find Carrington smiling at him. Dallas: Okay, okay, before you go off and try and hit me with a hockey stick, yes, I will uphold the good name of the NGW to the best of my abilities. Woodson: We’ll get back to that…I want to talk to you about our final pay-per-view event. Are you going to be available? I want you there at Madison Square Garden. To save you from the semantics of public relations, the camera cuts back outside the office door, where a rather inebriated John Crigger, NGW member and ex-GTT4 participant, is holding a brown paper bag up to Beastor. Frankie looks horrified. Crigger: C’mon, big man…just fill the bag up…it’s not like I’m askin’ ya to light it on fire and put it in front of Woodson’s office door. I’ll take care of that. Just fill the bag up…c’mon Beastor…a guy your size has gotta be full of poo… Frankie: I don’t think Mr. Carrington’s gonna like this idea, Beastor… The receptionist finally takes notice of Crigger’s antics, and with a terrified look on her face, begins to call for security. Receptionist: Security? It’s me again. Mr. Crigger is back. Yes, right away, quicker if possible. With a wary eye, Crigger picks up on her movement. He shakes his fist in her direction. Crigger: You may have beat me this round, but I’ll be back…with a full bag of tricks! And like that, Crigger is off like greased lightning down the hallway. Frankie looks relieved, Beastor a little let down. We cut back into the office. Dallas: Okay, well, yeah. Eugene is a bit of a “character”, as they say…but I’m a little baffled. If he’s a “special” person, should he be allowed to compete in events such as this? Woodson: Just because he’s different, doesn’t mean he need be excluded. Frankie sticks his head inside the office. Woodson notices immediately and points to the waiting area. Woodson: Get out of here, you’re excluded from this Frankie. Reeves breaks character and looks at the camera with a confused look due to Woodson’s double standard. Frankie is every bit as “special” as Eugene is. Dallas: Anyways Scotty, why are you worrying about me? Don’t you have your own agenda to worry about, having to deal with the NGW closing? Are you still sore because Muppet Man beat you out last round- Woodson: This is a meeting to discuss your situation, not mine. Dallas: Fine, I was just sayin’, that’s all. Woodson: Well your time is up, so get out of my office. Carrington stands and shakes Woodson’s hand, and Reeves, shocked by the abruptness of the meeting’s close, gets up as well. He shakes Woodson’s hand, and the duo head out the door. Reeves turns back to Woodson. Dallas: Oh, and boss…I told you, just because your opponent was named Muppet Man, didn’t mean you could try and stick your hand up his arse. The anger boils in Woodson’s eyes. The two have a tense working relationship, but it’s all in good fun. Woodson relents, and cracks a smile. Woodson: Reeves…just send Frankie in here. Reeves and Carrington exit the office door. Frankie and Beastor stand there, waiting, looking ever the guilty parties. Dallas: What did you two do!? Frankie talks hurriedly, and excitedly, as if his life depended on it. Frankie: Mr. Crigger wanted Beastor to poo in a bag so he could light it on fire on Mr. Woodson’s doorstep but he didn’t and was sad so he ate my colouring book and then took a whiz in one of the plants…actually, in about three of the plants. Dallas: Aw man! I missed Criggy? That guy’s like “Polkaroo”! Frankie is ushered into Woodson’s office as Reeves high-fives Beastor. The scene fades down, and in a rushed montage scene, we see the four head out the building, get into the limo, arrive at a small airfield, and fly their jet to Regina, Saskatchewan. The plane touches down, and they then begin to walk throught the terminal. Frankie checks his watch, trying to reset it. Frankie: Mr. Carrington, if it’s 3 pm in New York, what time is it in Regina? Dallas: 1954! Ha! Carrington scowls. The four are then greeted by an older gentleman…with a “haircut” similar to Hulk Hogan’s, only silver. He also has an eye patch over his right eye, and appears to be missing his left ear. He shakes hands with Carrington. This man is “Pepsi” Joe Trudeau, Reeves’ specialist trainer. Carrington: Ah, “Pepsi” Joe, good ta see ya again. Trudeau: You, too, Jack. So Dallas, are ya- In the blink of an eye, Trudeau hits Reeves with a perfect bagtag, sending Reeves to the ground in pain. Dallas cringes, in the anticipation of pain that is sure to follow. Dallas: Wait for it…yaarrrrgghhh! Reeves wriggles ‘round on the ground of the terminal, as Frankie points and giggles. The Beastor, ever the loyal bodyguard, eyes up his old rival Trudeau; it was actually during a feud back in their wrestling days that Beastor was responsible for Trudeau’s disfigurement. Carrington steps between the two. Carrington: Na na, Beasteh…git Dallas off tha groun’ an’ ya can do what ya gotta do when we git back ta Joe’s farm. Beastor somewhat reluctantly helps Reeves up to his feet, a pained grimace still on his face. Carrington takes Trudeau by the arm and leads him outside the doors of the not-so state-of-the-art Regina Airport, with Frankie trailing close behind. Carrington has his own people to retrieve their luggage from the baggage carousel, so the five of them head off on their way to Trudeau’s ranch on the outskirts of the Prairie city. Reeves composes himself. Dallas: Sheesh…I can’t believe I fell for that bagtag ploy again…good thing I surround myself with a non-speaking wildman, a crazy speaking Albertan, a man-child of a protégé and a full blown nutcase of a psychotic trainer…it might just give me the edge I need to stop the Unstoppable Eugene(~!) Reeves rests his hands on his knees for a moment, taking his last calming breaths. Dallas: Man…”Pepsi” Joe has a wide hand…he got both of ‘em. Dallas Reeves…his best friend has forsaken him. His girlfriend isn’t around. His home federation is soon shutting its doors. If he hadn’t been blessed with his current success in the GTT4, who knows where he’d be right now. The only thing he has going for him these days is this tournament. And there’s nothing more dangerous than a desperate over-achiever, counted as the underdog, in search of glory. Beastor dutifully guides Reeves out the doors as the scene fades to black. The words “Dallas Reeves vs Unstoppable Eugene” appear in gold lettering, then fade out.
