Girls Girls Girls

9 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-09 The damp and decrepit ruins of a building long since abandoned had temporally become the home of Joshua Taro Freedom. Alone and single he rested his head on the resemblance of a wall and allowed the tears to roll slowly down his cheeks. “Why did she leave” he cried, broke down and void of any confidence he had spent fifteen months building. “Fame grabbing whore” spurted out as he visualized his now ex love Jessica posing for some magazine at the release of her new movie. Joshua smacked his head on the brutal wall as he remembered introducing her to now boyfriend and movie producer. “Fucking bitch” he whined and closed his eyes as if he could block out the pain or stop the world seeing him. Joshua was motionless as a rat rushed across the rotten dirty filled floor. The rat nibbled at Joshua shoe, Joshua kicked out throwing the rat across the room. Upon landing the rat rushed away from Joshua and out of the building through one of the many holes in its walls. Opening his eyes Joshua looked around his surroundings. For so long Joshua had been surrounded by luxury, yet now he was a far away from that as he could be. His marble covered tables, silk sheets and three thousand dollar carpet where nowhere to be seen. “Am I destined to be alone?” The voice was so broken it barely made enough of a vibration to echo back at Joshua. “Are all women the bane of a man’s life?” This time there was a sense of hope that carried the voice. Hope of an answer some reasoning to help ease the pain that was tearing Joshua apart. But no reply came, Joshua was met with nothing but his words echoing back at him to torment him just alittle more before he shut his eyes and heart once more. The coldness of solitude was taken hold. Joshua sat alone and felt his soul’s kindness burn ever so slower away. “Women, nothing I have ever done or seen comes close to the torture women do. There whole being is to screw over honest and caring men like me. I worthshipped that bitch, I would of killed a man for her, I almost did. Yet this fucking bitch took my heart from me, and served it up with a side order of deceit.” Joshua slide across the floor, his hands crawling through rat droppings, dirt and glass. Joshua showed no signs of caring as blood start to appear through his figures. Joshua came to a stop and looked down at a piece of mirror; the shattered piece reflected an image that Joshua himself would describe as a pathetic humanbeing. “Look at what she did to you, once so strong. Look at the lows you have hit, if I could I would kill you.” Joshua was looking at himself; he was stunned by the reflection. He saw himself so weak, so naked. Any man passing by could of looked at Joshua and know the pain he felt, know the feelings he felt. “Don’t let her do this to you, don’t let any of them ever hurt you again.” Joshua sat on his knees amongst the dirt and rat droppings. As a small gust of wind flows through the ruined building Joshua raps his hand around the mirror piece. Joshua was picking up a piece seven years bad lucky, someone or something had broken this piece off and gained that very bad spell. Squeezing the shard piece Joshua forced yet more blood from his hand. Joshua sat disappointed as the pain failed to overpower the loss of Jessica. “I hate all women. I hate those whores more then all of my past opponents. The pain they cause ends now.” Holding up the mirror even the rats felt the change. As the windows to a man's soul, the eyes showcased a man's true emotions. Joshua eyes were burning, burning so fiercely. He himself had never seen such rage within him; Joshua was a man who had beat his father, tormented opponents. Joshua had thrown men it cages, and had not one but three bitter and bloody feuds in Prime. Yet a woman had broken his limits. Joshua had never seen the world from such a low level, such low ground. Everything around him felt bigger seemed more challenging. Yet everything was come right back around to his new found and burning passion to do harm. He hated her for this torment, he hated the fact a woman, a sex he had considered weaker had hurt him so much. Joshua wondered what the old family friends back in Japan would say. Women were for the home, a man made the rules. Yet here he sat, raging at his heart break, raging at all women. “She should pay, she should feel my pain, she... ...They all should, they.... ...Tessa, Tessa Windsor will pay.” Throwing the shared of glass at the front facing wall Joshua finally awoke to the disgusting smell of his current environment. He looked around before turning his gaze to the shared mirror now in two pieces on the floor. “She’ll surfer; she’ll surfer for every thing they ever did.” Joshua looked at the blood coming from his hands. His right hand and been holding the glass and was pouring out as a steady rate but not enough to cause Joshua any concern. “I refuse to that one of them do this to me. I'm a man, I'm better then this... then them.” Joshua finally seeing his surroundings for what they were stood up. His movement caused dust to fall from his body. Joshua had spent just a few hours in this room yet was covered in dirt. “I'm going to crush that bitch; I'm going to put that bitch in her place.” Joshua took a finally look around and then walked away. Joshua had a message to send, he wanted Tessa Windsor to know just how what fate was sending her. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Joshua: “Tessa Windsor, how do you live with it? How do you life with the knowledge that you’re a tramp? You’re just another whore using her body to further her own selfish needs? Some might say its taking advantage of male weakness, but strength, true strength comes from knowing you can take the low road but choosing to take the high one. You stand there in your mighty glory, one of few female wrestlers that stand toe to toe with the men, the boys. You stand there married, with your two adopted children so proud. Yet you and I both know you stop yourself getting pregnant because you wouldn’t know who the father would be? Tell me how many men do you fuck each week? Two? Three? More? I wouldn’t be surprised, glorified rat is all you’ll ever be to me. You took children into a home where your pink walls and precious fluffy toys have seen more sex then a porn director. You disgust me, a noble act tainted by your selfish perversions. Now I know I know many people would see this as abusive, sexist but kiss my male ass. I don’t treat you any different then the sad pathetic morons that drop at your feet. You think because you have a pair of tits I'm gonna change who I am? You, a woman that marries out of need rather then love. You’re a common whore, you just happen to have learnt a few things from the men you bed. However... ...Tessa, you and I have been somewhere alittle special. You and I both made it through the doors at everyone’s favourite company. Prime, that castle of bullshit that those on the outside believe to be great. Yet we came and went from that holy land. Now Tessa I checked up on you, I had a really good look over the career of one of the hottest females ever. That’s the opinion of the moron's, not mine. Sadly for you Tessa, you’re beginning to fit the mould I have come to find most woman fall into. No surprise came when I read that upon arriving in PCW you have two men fighting over you. Sure you can sit there all prim and proper and believe male ego caused friends to fight. But that song stopped working on me, I see why, I see your loose morals and ease in which you lay on your back ended that friendship. Men can and are the best of friends, through think and thin they would stick together. Peace rules the world until a woman walks in-between and then hells doors open and war breaks out. History is prove enough my claims are true. Time and time again do women like you break two men apart and spilt there spirit. You see a man you want, and instead of taking him as he is, and his friends. You squeeze everything you hate away, crush his opinions, crush his thoughts and dreams. Then after every thing has gone, you make up his mind for him and in slave him. Chris Storm, time and time again I see he’s tried to break away from you. On and off on and off you claim your friendship has been. But beneath the PR, beneath the gloss you place upon that statement, lies a man begging to be free of you. I, I, Joshua Taro Freedom have been caught in a women’s evil web. I was used and toyed with so a women like you could get what she wanted. My feelings, my love killed by the actions of this whore. So when I see another like her, I have no choice but to take action. Good people Tessa, good talented men like me should be freed from the evils you use. History Tessa, history speaks nothing but the true, women use there body to cheat men. But history for you, that also shows you cannot, and will not move beyond this round. You’re a first round bunny, here to trap morons in you tasteless web, and maybe even make a couple bucks after your match with some of the boys. You’re a rat Tessa, you move about like you own the world and all in it. But when you’re face to face with me, you’ll see the rage, actions and torture a man back from the bottom can provide. Round one for me Tessa, your final.”
