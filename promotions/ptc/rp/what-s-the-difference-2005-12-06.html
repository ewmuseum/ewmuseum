What's the Difference?

6 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-06 Distinction… is there any? Alone I sit. Wondering. Pondering. I’m just confused. What makes every person different from the one another? Is it the color of their hair? The color of their skin that matters, possibly it is some other shallow exterior feature? Or is it actually what is on the inside? Is that what really matters? Is it the numbers that come back from an IQ test? Or is it that fact that you can make someone laugh or can inflict pain on someone? What is it that makes me different from you? I have yet to figure this out. On paper… where it doesn’t matter The truth is what matters. So I look over this sheet, what do I see? You know the usually shit, we have height, weight, moves, styles and previous achievements. What does that tell me? Absolutely nothing that really matters, I mean we can look at the fact he has been the World Champion a total of 31 times. Now personally that tells me he has to be old to have won it that many times, but yet his age says only 25. Does that mean he is this superb wrestler? Looking at his stats you might think he is, but this still does not tell me why he is better than me, or anyone else for that matter. So what he stands a good seven inches taller then me? I mean the old saying is "The taller they are the harder they fall." So then maybe when I hit him he falls harder? But that still doesn’t make a significant difference. But then you look and you see that he is a good solid well built man, but wait just because he weighs in at more then Fifty Three pounds bigger then me. Should I be shaking in fear that this man is taller and also bigger then me? I think not, I mean look at it this way that just means I’m faster then him, that just means I will run circles around him in the ring and show him that weighing more then me doesn’t mean that you are going to out power me nor does it mean you are going to be able to beat me. So now I have sat here pondered and wondered about how you were bigger and supposedly better then me. Don’t get me wrong, there are some reasons that you could be better then me, but you aren’t. Why? None of them are solid enough to prove your better then me. Honestly you want to gloat you have won the world title thirty one times? Well I congratulate you, but that isn’t an accomplishment cause you no longer hold it, which means you also have lost it thirty one times, not including the matches you were in where you didn’t enter as the champion. On paper you look like the obvious pick to win, but paper is not where it matters, the height, the weight, the moves and most importantly previous achievements are not where it matters. Maybe it’s the heart and instinct… guess what Tony that is where I am superior, that is where I excel. I was named the ‘Silent Assassin’ cause I’m known for out witting my opponent, not out weighing. You Tony Stecchino, will be a victim of this epidemic not the first, not the last, but just a simple number in the middle similar to your title reigns.
