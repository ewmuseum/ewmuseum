The Big Shiny Sight Receptacle - Part III

23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-23 WASHINGTON, D.C. - BONG'S HOTEL ROOM CONTINUED FROM PREVIOUSLY RIGHT ON THE HEELS OF U'S STARTLING DISCOVERY O'CLOCK "Inconceivable!" U exclaimed. "This disk! It contains the clearance codes for operating the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle!" "WHAT!?" everyone shouted. "That's unimaginable!" U continued. "What in the world was Kolehnkov doing with the clearance codes for the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle!?" "That might explain why he tried to kill Iva," Shafted offered. "Maybe he was secretly working for Garfeld. The only way Garfeld can access the codes for the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle is to get them from the Russian government, and clearly he would need an inside man to accomplish that. Maybe Kolehnkov was only pretending to help us. After all, why else would the Russian government allow him access to such precious information?" "Good point, Shafted," said N. "And if that's the case, then that means one thing... Garfeld That means his plan can't move forward!" Bong smiled. "And that means all we have to do now is find him, and it will all be over!" "Yes, and the CIA knows where he is," N continued. "So Bong, you need to pay them a little visit." Bong's face sank. "What? Are you saying I have to break into the CIA now?" "Exactly," said N. "Bloody hell, I'll be killed if they catch me!" "Indeed," said N with a smile. "That's just great," mumbled Bong. "And why exactly do I need to do this, N? Surely we can figure out where Garfeld is hiding without resorting to breaking and entering the nerve center of American international intelligence?" "I have my reasons, Bong," N said, looking serious now. "What, you mean other than the fact that you'd love to see me killed in the line of duty?" Bong retorted sarcastically. "Watch your tone, Double-Oh-Nothing," N said sternly. "But honestly, N!" Bong shot back. "What about my cover, eh? What about the work I've done to stay in the GTT4 tournament to keep this investigation intact? You expect me to throw that all to the wayside, purely because it's a more convenient way of finding Garfeld? Come on! The one thing he needs to complete his plan is in our posession! We have all the time in the world..." Bong paused for a brief second, then broke out into song. "...tiiiime enough for life... to unfoooold... all the precious things love has in store... we have aaaaall... the tiiiime... in the wooooorld..." "Bong! Quit singing Louis Armstrong!" ordered N. Bong shook his head, snapping out of it. "Right, sorry," he said sheepishly. "Where was I? Oh yes... anyway, we have all the time in the world to track down Garfeld! I fail to see the necessity in this foolish business of sneaking into CIA headquarters!" N sighed. "Double-Oh-Nothing," she said with a stern tone, "I will not tolerate having my authority questioned. I think it's time you and I had a closed conversation." " Bong gulped. That sure didn't sound good. Usually a "closed conversation" with N meant standing in the unemployment line the following Monday. Majorly Shafted let out a long whistle. "Oy, you've gone and done it now, mate," he said. "I'm going out on the balcony for a smoke. I probably don't want to hear what's coming next." He got up and walked out onto the hotel room balcony, closing the sliding glass door behind him. "I suppose I should take my leave then," said U, who almost appeared to be delighted at the fact that Bong was in hot water. "I'll see if I can find anything else of value in this data. Have a good night." With that, U's picture-in-picture box disappeared from the television screen. Bong stared blankly at N, and nervously adjusted his tie. He was beginning to sweat profusely. "Please N!" he pleaded. "Don't sack me! I'm sorry! I'll never question you again, I swear!" "Oh please," N scoffed, "quit your pathetic groveling! I'm not firing you, Double-Oh-Nothing!" The secret agent paused. "Oh," he said. "Well, uh... in that case... I still think breaking into the CIA is a stupid idea!" he finished with growing confidence. "Just shut up and listen to me!" N snapped. "I couldn't tell you this with U and Shafted listening. The fact of the matter, Bong, is this: I don't want you breaking into the CIA to discover Garfeld's location. You were perfectly right in what you said earlier. We know Garfeld doesn't have the codes for the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle, so there's no need to be hasty in finding his whereabouts." "Come again?" Bong asked, confused. "Then what the bloody hell do you want me to do?" "I've been doing quite a bit of thinking since the day that Durnstrom fellow shut down our OHMCS Washington facility," N said slowly. "I've been trying to wrap my mind around how in the world Durnstrom gained proper security clearance. There's no way he could have gotten in without one of our own people letting him in. Do you follow me so far?" Bong nodded. "Good," said N. "Now, think about it -- because Durnstrom put a halt to our operations, it greatly impeded our investigation. In fact, if not for my quick thinking and your involvement with the GTT4 tournament, there wouldn't be any investigation at all. Realizing this, I arrived at only one conclusion." "That this plot is becoming entirely too complicated?" asked Bong. "Ye--NO!" N groaned. "I'm saying that there must be someone within OHMCS who is working against us! I'm saying, Bong... there is a double-agent inside OHMCS!" "You're joking!" Bong gasped. "Who in the world could that person be!?" "Well, I know that I can eliminate you as a suspect," N continued. "Because you're entirely too stupid to be a double-agent." "Goodness, you sure have my number on that one," Bong mused. "Right," said N, rolling her eyes. "Other than that, I can't entirely be sure who else I can trust. To be honest, my intuition tells me that its your human shield, Majorly Shafted..." "Shafted?" Bong asked incredulously. "Why in the world would you think that?" "It's his theory about what Kolehnkov was doing with those clearance codes in his posession," N explained. "I don't buy it. If Kolehnkov was really planning to deliver the codes to Garfeld, then why bring them to us? Why not just deliver them straight to Garfeld himself? It doesn't add up. I have to wonder why Shafted was so quick to offer an explanation like that." "Don't forget though, he did save Iva's life when Kolehnkov tried to kill her," Bong pointed out. "Indeed," said N. "But still, I'm not ruling him out. I'm not ruling anyone out, for that matter. I can't do anything until I have proof. If I'm correct though, then there ought to be some sort of incriminating documentation in Durnstrom's office. If we find out who gave him security clearance to OHMCS, then we've got our double-agent." "Right," said Bong. "How soon should I do this, then?" "As soon as possible," N answered. "If someone in OHMCS is under the employ of Garfeld, we need to bring them down quickly. Do it tomorrow." "Wait, tomorrow!?" Bong exclaimed. "But tomorrow is the next round of GTT4! I have a match! I can't afford to miss it, not when the investigation has gotten this far now!" "Then you'd better finish the mission before your match, Double-Oh-Nothing," responded N. "And you'd bloody well better make sure that you're not seen inside the CIA building. Otherwise, whether or not you make it in time for that match will be a moot point. And one more thing, Double-Oh-Nothing... make sure you destroy the data disk with the codes on it. I don't want them falling into the wrong hands." Bong nodded. "Right, over and out," said N. Her face then vanished from the screen, which returned to static. Jimmy Bong rose to his feet. He let out a long sigh and massaged his forehead, as he took mental stock of the long day he was going to have. First things first, however... Bong leaned down next to the console that Shafted had been working at, and removed the red data disk containing the clearance codes for the operation of the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle. As he did so, the glass balcony door slid open, and Shafted re-entered the room. "Well Bong, I'm dreadfully sorry about your being sacked," he started. "Come off it, Shafted, I didn't get sacked!" Bong shot back. "The old hag was just grumpy, that's all. Now, if you'll excuse me, I need to pay a visit to the CIA tomorrow, and I'd like to get some sleep. Before I do, however..." At that moment, the bathroom door opened up. Iva Gunnonmi, who had been in the shower this whole time, stepped out, wearing only a white towel wrapped around her slender figure. Bong could only stare, his jaw agape. In his testosterone-induced stupor, he briefly forgot that he was holding the key to a poewrful doomsday weapon in his hand, and he dropped it onto the floor. "Good gravy!" Bong managed to get out. "I've never seen anything so... so beautiful!" Iva cracked a small grin, thinking perhaps the idiotic Bong was sweet -- if only a tiny little bit -- for saying so. To her surprise, however, Bong walked up to her, and kneeled down to inspect the towel itself! "Look at this embroidery work!" he exclaimed. "Superb! I've never seen a more beautiful hotel towel in all my life!" Iva frowned, and if not for the fact that she was wearing only a towel, she likely would have kicked Bong in the face. "Honestly, Bong," sighed Shafted. "You're making a bloody fool out of yourself. And be more careful with this data disk, will you?" Bong stood up and turned around to see Shafted with the data disk in his hand now, having picked it up off the floor. "Oh don't worry Shafted," smiled Bong, taking the disk from him. "There's no need to concern yourself over being careful with the disk." With that, he set it on the ground, and smashed it underneath the heel of his shoe! "BONG!!" Shafted shouted at the top of his lungs. "HAVE YOU GONE MENTAL!? YOU JUST... you just... you just destroyed the codes!!" "Oh calm down, Shafted," said Bong nonchalantly. "U still has a copy of the data back in London, remember? Don't fret about it. Besides, I was under N's orders to destroy that thing." "Bloody hell," said Shafted, calming down. "You could have told me that before you went and smashed it to pieces. I was about to have a coronary." "Right," said Bong, yawning. "Well, I'm off to bed. Lots to be done tomorrow. You know, sneaking my way into a high-level government facility, wrestling in another tournament match, both for Queen and country and all that." "I take it I'll be joining you on this little excursion to the CIA," Shafted mumbled. Bong's eyes went wide. Shafted couldn't come along, because what if he really turned out to be the double-agent? "Erm, no Shafted, I'll handle this one by myself," Bong said quickly. "What?" asked Shafted, surprised. "Bong, you can't handle flushing the toilet by yourself, let alone snooping around CIA headquarters without being detected!" "Don't worry," Bong said with a smile. "I have a brilliant plan to get in." --- CIA HEADQUATERS - LANGLEY, VA THE VERY NEXT DAY A FEW HOURS BEFORE BONG'S FOURTH ROUND MATCH AGAINST THAT CALDERA FELLOW The sun was beginning to set over Langley, Virginia, home of CIA headquarters. The work day was wrapping up, and most of the CIA's employees were exiting the building -- the most noteworthy of which was Walter Durnstrom. He nodded and tipped his hat to the security guards at the entrance as he strolled away. Just off to the right was a row of hedges. There was nothing particularly unusual about them -- except for the one hedge about twenty feet away that seemed to be moving under its own power. The hedge lifted off the ground slightly, and a pair of feet were visible underneath. With extreme caution, the person inside the bush slowly made his way toward the door, undoubtedly thinking he wouldn't be detected by the guards. Sadly for him, he was wrong. "Hey, look at that! It's a walking bush!" exclaimed one of the guards. "Man, I hate bush, especially the kind that walks!" yelled another. "Let's go whack the bush with our batons!" a third one suggested. "YEAH!" "No!" whimpered the man inside the bush. "Well, the 'yays' outvote the 'nays,'" said the first guard. "Let's go!" In a second, a swarm of security guards were upon the bush, clubbing the everloving snot out of it and the man who was hidden within its foliage. He groaned and wailed in pain, pleading for them to stop. While this was going on, Jimmy Bong strolled up the sidewalk to the front door of CIA headquarters, which was now completely unguarded. Bong looked over at the guards with bewilderment. "Wow, I'm sure glad that isn't me in that bush," he said to himself. "That was certainly a convenient distraction." Bong walked through the front door, and found himself on the inside of what looked less like a government building, and more like an art museum. There were paintings, statues, and busts decorating the hallways. The corridor off to the right showcased a wall of oil paintings, each of which was a portrait of every CIA director in the organization's history. A few feet in front of Bong was an elevator. And unbeknownst to him, a security camera was located above him and to his right... and it was turning towards him at that very moment! But he didn't notice, because he was looking down at the ground... "Oh goody!" he exclaimed. "I found a quarter!" He stooped over to pick it up, just as he came into the camera's line of vision! Luckily for Bong, he was no longer in the shot. It would still, however, take a moment for him to move out of the camera's range once more. "Blast!" he muttered. "Somebody has glued this thing to the floor! Maybe I can pry it off with my fingernails..." Bong struggled mightily to retrieve the 25-cent piece from the ground, but alas, he had just trimmed his fingernails the night before, and they were of no use to him. "Dash it all," he sighed. "Well, no use wasting any more time with this, I suppose. There are more important matters at hand!" He stood upright once again, just as the camera finished turning away from him. It was a miraculous stroke of luck on Bong's part, and he was completely oblivious to how close he'd come to being caught. He was, however, incredibly disappointed with his failure to get that quarter, which he lamented as he rang for the elevator... The fourth floor of the building was quiet, as everyone had gone home for the day. There was an unmanned reception desk just in front of the elevator, which oddly enough had a bust of Janet Reno sitting on top of it. Also, there were three different hallways going left, forward, and right, respectively. Other than that, there wasn't much else worth noting. The silence of the fourth floor was shattered by the "DING!" of the elevator. The doors slid open, and out stepped Jimmy Bong, straightening his tie and taking a glance around. "Durnstrom's office is on this floor somewhere," he mumbled quietly. "But which room is it? Maybe there's something at that desk that can tell me..." Bong quickly made his way over to the desk, and started leafing through drawers, folders, and miscellaneous papers. "There has to be something here..." he said to himself. But several minutes went by, and he wasn't finding anything. "Curses," he hissed. "Do I have to search every last blasted room until I find his office?" At that moment, the elevator door opened up again... and out stepped two security guards! "Egad!" Bong nearly yelped out loud. He quickly ducked down underneath the desk, as he heard the footsteps of the guards approaching. "Hey Merv!" one of them barked. "Check this out! The reception desk is a mess!" Bong froze. He was surely as good as caught now. "Well duh, ya idgit!" scoffed the other guard (who Bong presumed was this "Merv" chap). "Phil is the most slovenly slob of a receptionist I've ever seen! In fact, it looks a lot less messier than usual." "Huh, so it does," the first guard said, scratching his head. "Ah well, let's go to the next floor." Bong heard their footsteps move back to the elevator, and he decided to chance a peek. In doing so, however, he accidentally knocked over the bust of Janet Reno that was sitting atop the desk, causing the head to shatter into pieces! Bong's mouth and eyes went wide with horror. "Hey, what was that noise!?" yelled the first guard. "Y'think we oughta turn around and look to see what it was?" asked the second guard. "Seems like the thing to do, don't you think?" the first guard asked back. "Yup, guess so," answered the second guard. And so, the guards turned around, shining their flashlights on the desk. During their brief, pointless discussion over whether or not they should turn around, Bong had managed to set the Reno bust back into its original position. However, his options were rather limited when it came to addressing the issue of the shattered head. Consequently, he ended up positioning his own head where Reno's head had been just moments before. He hoped the guards wouldn't notice the difference. "Hey," said the first guard. "I never noticed before, but that Reno chick is actually kinda attractive." "Yeah, I know," said the second guard. "That's probably the most aesthetically pleasing piece of Reno artwork I've ever seen. Anyway, let's get outta here." "Yeah, it was probably nothing," said the first guard. "It's not like some secret agent from the British government is snooping around here or anything." "Hah, that would be the day!" laughed the second guard, as the both of them stepped onto the elevator. Bong breathed a huge sigh of relief when the guards had gone. Then, he immediately went back to searching through files and papers, and it was only a moment longer before he found what he was looking for. "Aha!" exclaimed Bong. "Here it is! Room 487!" He slapped his hand on the desk and smiled broadly, knowing he was close to completing his task. Bong stood up, and ran down the left hallway. A moment later, he ran back to the elevator lobby! "Blast!" he said. "It wasn't down that hallway. Maybe this one!" He ran down the center hallway now... and once again, returned a few moments later, a bit more out of breath than before! "Dash it all!" he gasped. "Well, it has to be down this hall, then!" Bong then took off down the hallway on the right. Once more, he returned after a moment, gasping and wheezing for air! "What the... bloody... hell?" he spat between gulps of air. Bong made his way back over to the reception desk, and double-checked the paper that had Durnstrom's office number on it. "Damnit!" he said. "That's not an '8,' that's a '5!' I don't know who this 'Phil' fellow is, but his handwriting is deplorable! And it's bad!" Bong returned to the center hallway, and finally found the door to Durnstrom's office. He paused to think for a moment. How was he going to get the door unlocked without a key? "Hm, perhaps I can jimmy the lock... or is it lock the jimmy? Anyway, perhaps I can unlock this thing with a credit card." Bong reached into his pocket for his wallet, and was startled to find what else he found in there... "The ammo clip I took from Shafted's rifle?" he asked himself. "Hm, I guess I forgot I still had that... now where's my wallet? Ah, there it is." He opened his wallet and took out his credit card, then turned his attention to the door. "Now let's see..." Bong jiggled the door handle to test it out a bit. The handle turned and the door opened. "What the?" Bong said, startled. "Are you kidding me? The man's office is deep within the bowels of the CIA, and he leaves his door unlocked? This is turning out to be much easier than I had thought!" Bong looked up and down the hallway to make sure he wasn't being followed by anyone, then slipped through the door. Durnstrom's office looked in a bit of disarray: there were shelves upon shelves with all sorts of folders, books, and papers strewn about. There were several boxes on the floor, which contained even more papers. But the only paper of interest to Jimmy Bong, was the one sitting on top of Dunstrom's desk... "Unbelievable!" said Bong. "This is a document confirming Durnstrom's security clearance with OHMCS! This is it! Now, I just need to find out who sent this..." Bong's eyes quickly skimmed the document, searching for a name. He found it. He couldn't believe what he saw. "You... you've got to be kidding..." he stammered unbelievingly. "The double-agent is..." Bong sank onto the floor, sitting against the desk. He couldn't believe what he'd just read. He took the ammo clip out of his pocket once more and idly turned it in his hands. It didn't make any sense, but there it was, the answer staring him in the face... "U."
