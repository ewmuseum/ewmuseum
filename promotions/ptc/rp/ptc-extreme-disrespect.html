Extreme Disrespect...tsk..tsk..

2 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Perhaps I’ve been rather rude here. As I strive to fit in... as I prepare myself to make a wholehearted effort to be accepted, and share what I like to call Ultra Violence with everybody in the PTC who considers themselves extreme enough to partake in this match, I’ve noticed that many here have been sharing some important, personal... extreme experiences from their past. As a viewer, I’ve been allowed into their homes, into their lives. And as I sit here, watching and observing, I’ve come to the conclusion that perhaps I should do the same... I should show the masses the place in which I dwell... So... in the spirit of fitting in, and finally being accepted in a place where my lust for violence will surely be shared by all involved in such a dangerous environment as an Extreme Rumble... I welcome you all to my humble abode... the place I have called home for nearly ten years... Fade in now, as The Shadow is shown sitting down in a ring and leaning back against the corner of it, with his head up and looking at the camera. The ring is very undesirable in appearance, as it has not been maintained in over ten years. Bloodstains detailing many important events in The Shadow’s past litter the torn mat, and one of the four corners has given out completely, and has sunk into the ground. A worn and torn wall can be seen in the background, with large chunks of it either having fell off already, or are still in the process of unlatching themselves. It is very bright in this place, due to sunlight entering through holes in the walls, ceiling, and through shattered windows. ‘Tis also very dusty, creating a blurred atmosphere that could almost be that of a dream. Welcome to where I train, where I spar and prepare myself for events such as what will transpire soon. I have been using this ring for years and years. It is here, where I trained to become a professional wrestler, before I was ever known as The Shadow. It was here... where I first became The Shadow. He sits at the corner, still dressed as he was earlier this week, maybe causing some to wonder if he has even showered since then, or changed his clothes at all. He is still wearing the same torn jeans, the same black wrestling boots, the same dirty leather jacket, and most importantly, the very same black mask over his face that is never removed. His hair is not wet, but instead clumped up and entirely too messy as he leans his head against the turnbuckle. As I sit here though, in this ring, I think of another who is involved in this match. I saw a very familiar face in a ring earlier this week, and just as I suspected people might have deja vu when my name was mentioned, I experienced the same when I saw her there, sparring with some young fool. ‘Who was she?’ I thought to myself... and then the answer became crystal clear... I saw her in another battle royal type of match in the past. But she is of no concern to me... apparently her brother is though... The Shadow’s voice trails off as he slowly pulls himself up to a standing position, and he begins to pace around the ring, making an effort to avoid walking over a few of the bloodstains, as they must have some special significance to him. A lot of men in this upcoming war, have proclaimed themselves the most extreme. Even though they do not know many men in this fight, they still state that there is no question that they are the best. Those are the type of fools that I do not take seriously, for their overconfidence and complete ignorance only serve to play into the wishes and desires of more dangerous souls... who wish to hurt them for their proclamation of greatness. They make themselves targets, and of no concern to me. On the contrary though, the few in this war that are fully aware of what they are about to get into, they are more cautious... they know who to look out for. Take, for instance, the brother of the lady who remains very familiar to me, Sean McClancy... The Shadow stops pacing, and leans his arms over the top rope, still glaring now into the camera... He is the number one seed for this match. He is, by default, the number one contender and likely favorite to win this match, if for no other reason then the fact that he enters last. To become the number one seeded soul in this division, you have to know what you are doing. You have to be somewhat intelligent. You have to recognize who to ‘look out’ for, who to concentrate on... perhaps who to fear. And he knows something that not many others know... and he knows it based on personal experience. A slight grin creeps across Shadow’s face. His sister, who was not eliminated by me, certainly came into contact with me in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. She experienced a small taste, a very small taste... and based on that minuscule meeting in the ring, Sean McClancy singled me out. Why, I’m flattered... The Shadow takes a few steps back, and instead of slumping slightly as per usual, he now stands, in his own home, a bit more proud than usual, and he continues to glare into the camera, and speak in his normal low, intense voice... Everybody may consider me an outcast in this match. Everybody may look upon the name ‘Shadow’ and not think twice... perhaps make an old, tired joke about the name as Noah Hansen did and not have a second thought whatsoever. Even I consider myself an outsider, an unknown factor in a match full of unknown factors... but the number one contender for the Extreme title, the top seed in this match, he does not consider me to be such an unknown... he does not look at my name and share the same unconcern as Noah and others have. Personal experiences from his sister tell him that even though I enter third, I am anything but an outcast... I am his number one concern, a ‘viable threat.’ I think I like that title... viable threat... You said it yourself Sean, you hope that I make it to the point where you can get to me... and I hope I do, if only to provide you with that opportunity. You are the top name here Sean... and by default, the name I wish to personally eliminate from this battle royal. But first, before we get to the technicality of being eliminated, I want the two of us to dance Sean... The Shadow slowly unzips the leather jacket... Just as you wish it, I want the two of us to be the only two left. I want to dance in a cosmos of pure violence with you Sean. I want you to get back to your pure hardcore roots... forget about World titles, forget about any sort of image that you have, and remember who you truly are... and then I want you to dance with me in that environment. What do you think of that... McClancy? What do you think of immersing yourself in an Ultra Violent environment with a man like me, with a soul who lives and breathes for this rush, for those rare moments in life when the most intimate of brutal acts are realized on a mutual level... The jacket is unzipped, and the coat comes off. The Shadow is shirtless, a spectacle all in itself, as his upper body is fully exposed. Scars, scrapes, and bruises line his body completely. There are a few overly large scars, from his car wreck ten years ago, but the others came from his time wrestling, his time in an extreme environment. Despite his sculpted physique, it is not a pretty sight at all, because instead of having tattoos to decorate his body, his overwhelming scars have taken care of that task. The Shadow pays no mind at all, as he looks back up at the camera, a tattered and physically torn man surrounded by his world... his tattered and torn home... You have something to prove, don’t you Sean? You want to prove that you can still live in this extreme world, that you can get back to your ‘roots’ and thrive in a place that you used to call home. Well, I have something to prove as well, and maybe.. just maybe, if we are the final two men left in this sea of worthy.. and unworthy challengers, we can both achieve exactly what it is we desire... we shall both prove ourselves to the masses Sean, but only one of us will win. Only one of us will advance to relinquish the title from the upstart Mike Marvelous... only one of us will return credibility to a title that so justly deserves it. Do not worry about me lasting until we meet Sean... worry about WHEN we will meet, and when the dance will truly begin... It is people like you, who wish to delve into a world of violent deeds, who wish to engulf themselves into that world, for whatever reason, that are the reason I am here in the first place. I hope that you match up to your reputation... that you do not fail to disappoint. The Shadow nods, drops down, and rolls out of the lopsided ring. He walks through the large area of what used to be a gym for young, aspiring wrestlers. There are broken, battered, and very dusty pieces or workout equipment scattered about... heavy punching bags busted open and left on the ground, their innards scattered about the floor. This place has not been a thriving gym in years and years, not since The Shadow claimed it as his own. He walks into a room that serves as an office of sorts. The former owner of the gym used it as his office... a small room up a flight of stairs, with a large window that served to overlook the entire gym. The window is now shattered, and the fragments are scattered all about. As The Shadow steps into his ‘office,’ he even steps over pieces of the glass shards. He makes his way to his desk, a fine oak structure that has, in contrast to pretty much the entire facility, held up through the test of time and mistreatment. The Shadow reaches under the desk, and pulls out an item that he has never before been shown holding in his life... ever... In contrast to my thoughts on McClancy... pure contrast... there is one man who stands out in a particularly opposite light in my mind. He has an ego, which is of course, the standard in this environment, as you must have an ego to survive. You must consider yourself to be the best, even if you know in the deepest region of your heart, that you lack what it will take in the end to advance. The egos of men such as Noah Hansen and Ice are to be expected, and taken lightly, as I do... but there is one man who took matters to an entirely new level. The Shadow looks down at the object that lingers in his hand, a hand held pistol... a gun. Stevie Vertigo... What were you attempting to prove? What was your goal in what you allowed cameras to videotape? Did you wish to prove to the world that you are a cold-blooded man... that you and you alone possess the mental skills needed to not show mercy in the slightest way, or pity? When I saw you standing there, pistol in hand, I decided to pay special attention to what you had to say Vertigo, and I paid even closer attention to your actions... The Shadow’s voice trails off as he takes the gun in his hand and checks for bullets... there is one bullet in the holster. I listened to you accuse the other men and women in this upcoming war that they are telling false stories... that what they say of their extreme lives is nothing but contrived nonsense... that they are simply trying to build up an image for themselves. And then, in an act of pure hypocrisy, you grab a pistol... and on live television, intended for a national audience, you take it upon yourself to presumably end the life of an unfortunate soul. Well, nobody saw the life ending act... so either you are about to be arrested for obvious murder, or you are doing the exact thing that you accuse others of doing. You are building up an ‘image’ for yourself. You want the world to believe that since you have a, what was it you said? Oh yes... since you have an ‘unbiased killing mind’ and was ‘baptized in the water of a corporate society,’ you will emerge victorious, without question. Of all stories told this week, yours reeks of contrived nonsense the most, you ignorant fool. The Shadow scoffs as he takes the weapon of death in his hand, and slowly raises it up toward his face... If we are to believe your story... if we are to believe that you have such an unforgiving mind, then congratulations Vertigo, you will fit in here nicely... for in order to be truly extreme... in order to survive in a world such as this, that is the proper mindset surely. Perhaps since I have never ‘killed’ anybody though... and perhaps since I was not raised under the exact same circumstances as poor poor unfortunate Steve here, I am, by default, not worthy of victory. Stevie Vertigo, your actions have only served to tell the world that you are just another name in this match, that is all... nothing special, just another name with another story, another reason for claiming himself to be the most extreme... but to me Stevie Vertigo, just between you and I, your actions only serve to prove that you are the true outcast here... not I. You are the one who does not belong here... not I! The Shadow places the end of the gun just under his chin and slowly cocks back the trigger... Anybody can pull a trigger... any fool in the street can do this one simple act and play god by taking the life away from another. What you will come to learn Steve, and what I plan on personally showing you, is that wielding a gun is a far opposite from extreme. An image of being a cold-blooded killer, a man with no remorse in that regard, is something... not extreme though. It is the work of a coward, Vertigo... nothing more, nothing less. Whether or not you actually committed a felonious crime such as murder, is unknown to me... but your actions are that of the purest of cowards... regardless. You are nothing Vertigo but the most insecure of fools in the Extreme Division, a man that feels the need to compensate his lack of backbone by putting on a facade... a spectacle of false strength, with the help of a tool that any idiot can hold. You are a flat out disgrace to this division, Vertigo. The Shadow sighs as he removes the gun from under his chin, and places it back underneath the table. Your words and your actions, Vertigo, have only served to make you the target of targets, the target of my own personal brand of extreme... of Ultra Violence. You claim that you have an unbiased killing mind? In the infamous words of a famous movie star in his most famous of roles... ‘wait’ll you get a load of me.’ Wait until you are trapped in a place where guns cannot save you Vertigo. Wait until you are outnumbered by the most violent, the most sadistic of men from various feds around the world. Wait until you step into that ring, fully expecting your victory, fully expecting people to cower before you and your ‘unbiased killing mind,’ and you come in contact with a man who needs no gun to prove just who he is, and what he intends on doing to you in the most extreme of fashions. You are my number one target for swift elimination Vertigo, because you are not even worthy of entering this ring. You are unworthy of the Ultra Violence, the hell that you will be put through as soon as you come into contact with me... for I will be waiting for you. I will stalk you in that ring, and with no remorse in my veins, with absolutely no pity in my heart, I plan on embarrassing you in front of the fans, in front of your so-called peers, in front of everybody that you hold near and dear... and you will be eliminated from this rumble... and you can go back to talking a big game.. of ‘killing’ people, but you will know that you are not extreme. You are certainly not qualified to live THIS life. Prepare to be a victim Stevie... prepare to wear the other shoe... to be the one who is dealt with in the most inhumane of fashions. This world is my life Vertigo, and your blatant disrespect of it calls for a swift, and vicious elimination from this rumble. I shall say my goodbyes to you now, for in the ring, there will be no talking, only violence. Your weapon of cowardice will not help you there... Welcome to my dream Stevie vertigo... welcome to your nightmare... The Shadow is nearly shaking and convulsing now due to his intense nature. He leans over the desk, and takes a few breaths in an attempt to calm himself. True, he did not raise his voice one bit, but his intensity is something rarely matched. His passion for the one thing he does well in life, is a strong influence, especially when dealing with those unworthy of claiming themselves to be what The Shadow truly is. He lifts up his hand and makes a mockery gun signal with it. He then grins sadistically as he ‘pulls the trigger’ and ‘blows the smoke away from the end of the fake weapon.’ No remorse Vertigo... no mercy. You should be used to it, right? The Shadow departs from the office, and takes a seat on the old, decrepit stairs that led the way up to the office, and The shadow stares out, surveying all of his earthly possessions in front of him. He takes a deep breath, and looks back into the camera. I think that I may be happy in my second home, in the PTC extreme division. I hope that it is not all talk, and no action. I pray that Sean McClancy is as prepared and as hardcore as he says that he is. I hope the same from everybdoy else who claim to be extreme as well. I hope that Stevie Vertigo accepts in his heart that while he may be a coldblooded man, his next accomplishment in life is to be a victim, in coldblooded fashion to The Shadow. I hope that everybody else involved in this war will come to the realization by the time the bell sounds, and this war becomes underway, that an unknown force, a simple man with a simple desire, a name amongst other random names... is perhaps, just perhaps... a name to recognize as what Sean McClancy recognizes it as... a viable threat. PTC, show me exactly what you have to offer. I want to see how this division holds up... how it manages to deal with, and survive against.... Clouds have effectively covered over the sun, and the room has become drastically darker than before. An eeriness has taken over everything, as shadows grow from each corner of the room, as the ring in the middle of the oversized structure casts many shadows of it’s own... the mat itself becoming so dark that it covers the many bloodstains that flood it. The Shadow is shown, and darkness begins to shroud him as well, covering has scars and scabs, his messy hair, his torn jeans... and only leaving truly visible his dulled, gray, bloodshot eyes. He glares into the camera a few more moments, before closing his eyes, and now nothing can truly be made out. against Ultra..... Violence.... Fade to black. I cannot wait.
