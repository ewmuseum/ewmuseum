The turning of the Goat

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 Craig’s eyelids flutter, once, twice, and then open. He groans and brings his shaking hands up to rub his temples. Why does his head feel like it has been stuffed full with mushy peas and a couple of jackhammers? Struggling to sit up, he looks around and sees that he is in his own bed in his own room. That at least was a relief. His arms, exhausted from the effort of holding his body in a sitting position for even a few moments, give way and he collapses back amongst the pillows. As his senses slowly wake up one by one, he realizes that there is a terrible smell in the room, like garlic and wet dog. The smell is him. How long has he been lying here in bed? What the fuck is going on? “Good morning Craig.” Craig lifts himself up once more to stare at Nurse standing by the end of the bed. “You drugged me again, didn’t you?” Nurse shrugs, “You looked like you needed a proper nights sleep.” “Just one night?” Nurse shuffles in her chair guiltily, “well, maybe I got the dosage wrong. I’m more used to metric then imperial ya know. Still, no harm done. After seventy two hours of down time, you should be more than rested enough for the next round.” A look of hatred flashes across Craig’s face, “GTT friggin’ 4! If I say I won’t wrestle, what will you do? Kill me this time? You’ve already destroyed everything I ever cared for.” Nurse assumes a mock expression of both shock and concern, “You know I never set out to hurt you… too much, but this is important! I’m just trying to help, honest.” “Huh, did you really think that kidnapping Trixie was being helpful? You’re such an evil twisted bitch. Where is she by the way? You promised I’d get her back if I won, which I have.” “She’s downstairs with Medium Dave, not a scratch on her. He looked after her very well. You can see her once you’ve washed a few times. Did you know you stink? It’s like, really offensive.” She throws a towel at him, blows him a kiss and leaves him to his ablutions. A little later, Craig stands under a cascade of hot water and examines his body. He may still be thin and look like the geek he’s always been with his clothes on, but naked, the outline of his muscles are clearly defined. Flexing his arm, he stares in awe at the hard knot of muscle that jumps out. “I’ve changed so much” he thinks, “so why do I still let her push me around? I’m not the sniveling little twerp that hung onto her every word with adoration that I was a year ago. In fact, seeing her this morning, I can’t really understand what I ever saw in her. She’s pretty, sure, but that’s all.” Smiling now, Craig realises that he may finally be truly over Nurse Esme Matilda Wretched. Feeling powerful, he hardens his will and resolves to never let her control him so thoroughly again. He quickly dresses and runs downstairs, eager to be reunited with his girlfriend. Craig finds Trixie curled up on the sofa, her brow creased with concentration and her lips moving as she reads the latest Cosmo. He coughs to get her attention and she looks up at him with a grin. “Sugar lump! You’re awake. Has that weird looking guy with the black cape left then?” “What guy?” “The one who’s been in your room with you, silly! I heard Esme say that he was helping you get ready for Calvin.” Nurse appears in the doorway and interrupts. “Can’t you ever keep your mouth shut? He doesn’t need to be bothered with little things at the moment.” Angry and confused, Craig swings round to face her. “What’s been going on here? Who the hell is Trixie talking about? I’m not taking any crap from you anymore Esme, so spill, what devious little trick are you up to this time?” Nurse sighs, “It’s such a silly little thing really. I asked a friend of mine to come have a look at some of those cuts and bruises that evil little slut Jenna Ryder dealt you. Nothing sinister.” Trixie is about to ask why it took three nights to patch up a few scrapes, but is distracted by a free shampoo sample stuck to the pages of the magazine. Shame really, things might turn out very different if Craig pushes harder to find out the truth now. Craig is still suspicious but all his thoughts are wiped clean from his mind at the sight of Medium Dave, an ex-mafia thug and Nurse’s current trainer, tottering in from the garden wearing a lime skirt, blouse and ridiculously high heels for a man already seven feet tall. As he comes closer, Craig notices he is also sporting false nails, breasts and eyelashes and has enough make up plastered on to make even an air hostess shudder. He actually looks kind of sexy in a very odd indefinable way. Following him are his brothers, Big Dave and Little Dave. “Nurse! What the hell did you actually drug me with! I’m freakin' hallucinating here!” Medium Dave, or Davina as he has recently come to be called, air kisses Trixie and Nurse before stumbling over to catch Craig in a bone crushing hug. “I knew you could beat Jenna Ryder! You’re such a good boy.” Craig wriggles out of his grip and turns to look around the room. All the others are treating Dave/Davina as if everything is perfectly normal. Now convinced that he’s experiencing some sort of schizophrenic reaction to Nurses medications, he decides to ignore what he sees in the desperate hope that it’ll just go away. “I’m glad you’re all here. I have something I want to tell you all.” “I have something to tell you too sugar lump!” “Not now please Trixie, whatever it is can wait, I’ve had an epiphany!” “You’re constipated? I think my news is far more important than that.” Exasperated, Craig holds his finger up to his lips and shushes Trixie. “Please honey, try to pay attention. This is about our future.” “Well, my news is about our future too, and I’d really like to just get it over with and tell you.” Glaring at her, he continues, “I have decided that I’m going to do my best in this GTT4 tournament. I’m going to win that title shot ladies and gentlemen, and I’m going to do it all by myself.” Nurse laughs, “That’s your big news? Are you feeling Ok? Don’t you remember that we decided like, ages ago that you were going to win this thing. Why the hell else would we have been pulling out all the stops for you in the last few weeks? I put my career on hold to help you, so you better goddamn win!” “You don’t understand, I never really wanted to enter any tournament. You forced me into it. First, you put my name in for it without my knowledge. Then you took advantage of the fact that I was a bit loopy after stopping my Zanax to convince me that I could beat Borealis even though I’d never been in a proper wrestling match in my entire life. And don’t forget that you kidnapped my girlfriend and threatened to cut off her fingers to ensure I’d do my best against Jenna. So far, all I’ve done is let myself be pushed around by you. But no more. From now on, I make the decisions. I’m not going to do this simply because you want the reflected glory. I’m going to do it for myself, without your interference.” Nurse starts a slow hand clap as she moves closer to him. Leaning in so that her face is only inches from his, she grins a twisted smile and laughs, “Oh well done! You’re trying to act like a grown up for the first time in your life. Just one problem. Now is not the time for this rebellion. You will fail without me, and I can’t let that happen. Understand? I know you think I’ve done all I could ever possibly do to hurt you, but believe me when I say that I haven’t even scratched the surface yet. There are not many things worse than a red hot poker up the arse, but I know them all.” Craig stands his ground puffs his chest out to make himself appear bigger than his 140lb frame and replies, “Yeah? Well, yeah… just fuck you!” “Can I tell you my news now?” Craig and Nurse turn to Trixie in unison shouting, “SHUT UP!” Big Dave stands up to grab everyone’s attention, which when you’re the kind of guy who feels cramped in a Hummer and gets rained on ten minutes before everyone else, is a fairly easy thing to do. “Guys, you’re both agitated at the moment and arguing isn’t going to get you anywhere. Why don’t you drop the topic and cool off for a bit? Craig, you’ve been very mean to Trixie and that’s not like you. Why can’t you let her say her piece?” Craig nods and sits with his head hanging in shame. Trixie perches beside him, takes his hand in hers and drops a bombshell. “This may come as a shock to you; it certainly did for me, but, well… I’m a lesbian! Stunned, Craig can only stare at her as she continues, “I never meant to hurt you, and I didn’t set out to find someone else. It just happened so quickly, I couldn’t help myself. When you were so busy training last week, I just bonded with Davina. We share so many interests, and she’s so cute for a big girl.” With that, Trixie stands on her tippy toes and starts kissing Medium Dave/Davina with vigor. More confused than ever, Craig stutters, “B-but that’s Dave! He’s no woman! Just a big guy in a dress.” Dave/Davina winks at Craig and covers Trixie's ears with his shovel-like hands. “Sshh! Don’t let on. She really does think I’m a girl… even with the five o’ clock shadow!” Thrusting his crotch forwards with a leer he continues, “Let’s just hope she likes BIG surprises eh?” Medium Dave/Davina blows Craig a quick kiss before picking Trixie up ala ‘An Officer and a Gentleman’ and whisking her away to his awaiting carriage, erm, Hummer. Reeling from shock, Craig emits what can best be described as a howl before pouncing on Nurse Wretched. With his hands gripped tightly about her neck, he growls, “You’ve really done it this time, Bitch! This Stockholm Syndrome thing is all your fault and now you’re going to pay!” Big Dave pry’s Craig loose from Nurse’s now unconscious body and throws him across the room. “Don’t do this Craig. I know you’re angry, but you can’t blame Nurse for Trixie’s feelings. Let it go.” Craig glares at Big Dave, “I should’ve known you’d take her side! If you don’t want her harmed, then I suggest you take her away with you now. She is never to set foot in MY house again… understand? Oh, and Dave? Hide her well, you really don’t want me finding her... I won’t be responsible for my actions.”
