Alone in the Fire....

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 22 . . . the rich man also died, and was buried; 23 And in hell he lift up his eyes, being in torments, and seeth Abraham afar off, and Lazarus in his bosom. 24 And he cried and said, Father Abraham, have mercy on me, and send Lazarus, that he may dip the tip of his finger in water, and cool my tongue; for I am tormented in this flame. 25 But Abraham said, Son, remember that thou in thy lifetime receivedst thy good things, and likewise Lazarus evil things: but now he is comforted, and thou art tormented. 26 And beside all this, between us and you there is a great gulf fixed: so that they which would pass from hence to you cannot; neither can they pass to us, that would come from thence. 27 Then he said, I pray thee therefore, father, that thou wouldest send him to my father’s house: 28 For I have five brethren; that he may testify unto them, lest they also come into this place of torment. (Luke 16:22-28) [The scene opens up to show a living room with a lit fireplace in the background. The radiance of the flames is seen reflecting off the walls forming the shadow effect of ribbons being waved around. As the camera begins to focus in on the dark and quite room it notices several well documented things. Crosses, paintings of Jesus, and several bibles are seen lying around. A sudden hissing sound is heard from across the room. The camera immediately zooms to the direction of the sound to see a dark figure sitting down on a black leather sofa seat. The figure is obviously Core Wrestling Superstar Rzarector.] Rzarector: Welcome to my holy abode. [The camera gets a closer look at this mysterious figure to reveal him wearing a black hooded cloak. The eyes of this Black Priest glare out in a bright red spreading the room like wild fire. White fangs are seen flashing at the base of the hood as to resemble all that is unholy in this world. Sitting in a prayer position Rzarector begins to speak in a low demonic voice.] Rzarector: The story of Rzarector is not a simple story to tell. It would take ages to finish everything that has happened to the former Prince of Evil on Earth, and then what happened to the current God-abiding warrior. Yet that story will be saved for another day. Tonight we will just give a summary. Better yet, I will just let the fans at home watch a home movie I made just from my experience in Hell. [The camera fades into the television as the screen opens to pitch black. The frightful sounds of howling, screaming , and the gnawing of teeth can be heard in the distance. Nobody or nothing is said for quite some time before a huge ray of light pierces the ground from the sky. From that light falls a man screaming right before he crashes into the ground. Immediately flames consume his body and the surroundings. The only sound coming from him besides the crackling of fire is that of howling and gnashing of teeth. The eyeballs then burn out, the skin slowly melts away, and all that is left is the bones of a former human. Yet he does not die. The victim arises once again from the ground, but this time with a different look. The look is that of a demon. Red eyes, third degree burns, red hair, and white fangs are now the physical makeup of this once evil man. The burning continued on the outside, but not on the inside of the body as three months passed like a bad nightmare. The individual prayed everyday asking for God’s forgiveness yet received no answer from the Almighty One. Then one day something was different. The same ray of light once again shown, but this time a huge hand came in. The man was pulled out of Hell by the hand of God.] [The video then stops as the camera zooms back to the Black Priest Rzarector whose red eyes are now glaring as bright as ever.] Rzarector: That story was about me and my survival of Hell. The torture was unbearable, but what can you do when there is no choice but to burn? There was no angel weeping over the suffering of billions of people. Yet the demons laughed and cursed the fallen. So I prayed and tried to get God to listen to me, and finally he did. That day or night God gave me a second chance. He told me to come back to Earth and spread the word of God and destroy all that is evil. So I agreed to do his work for him. There was a problem though. The problem was that I still had the look of a demon. So God told me I would keep the look and wear a black hooded cloak until I proved my loyalty. So that is where I am at today. [Rzarector gets up and begins walking around the room looking at all the bibles, crosses, and paintings of Jesus. The God-Like Warrior stares at a painting of Jesus hanging on the crucifix.] Rzarector: Jesus died alone. No one would stand up for him, but everyone seemed to curse and punish him. That is how my life is, I’m alone. I was alone at birth, and alone at my first death. I was alone in Hell, and alone in my resurrection. I am alone now. [A sudden tear comes to the eye of Rzarector.] Rzarector: Who is this Jacob Mayhem I shall be facing in the first round? I have seen him on television once! Now I hear that he is wrestling for Wrestling Midwest? That is odd considering I just saw him on Prime Time Television and his vocal skills weren’t that impressive. Neither was the build of his body, but then I guess you can never underestimate an opponent right? [The Black Priest gives a hideous laugh as if it was Satan himself.] Rzarector: So you want your fame? Well beating me wouldn’t give you the best fame, but it would give you some fame. My history is impressive in some eyes, and those who fight me never underestimate me. I conquered the glorious FAW by becoming the Tag Team and World Champion. Upon my arrival to Core Wrestling I was the Rising Star and have capture Tag Team Gold. Now I am feuding with Munson for the future Pride Championship. I am a former Extreme Beating 3 winner by defeating all 30 of my opponents in the FAW. On top of that I was ranked #6 out of every wrestler in the entire top ten of the PTC. So does that answer your question? If so I’m glad. [The white fangs flash with a slow stream of saliva dripping off of them.] Rzarector: God has mercy on people like you Mayhem, but first you must have control over yourself. The money, cars, women, and fame are no way to repay the debt God gave you. He sacrificed his own son to wash away our sins. Give up your ventures for this wicked intention and realize that God is the master of everything. [Rzarector goes over to the fireplace and stands directly in front of it.] Rzarector: The fire no longer burns. It scares some people when they see me so close to heat so high, but they don’t know me. What is there to fight for when you got no one to fight for? I will tell you. Fight for God or Burn in Hell! Mayhem, fame comes with pride and the only way to gain pride is by starting at the bottom and working your way up. I am prepared for you, and there is no way in Hell that you will get by me with an easy win. I have defeated some of the best, and then again I have lost to some of the best. This time I shall go further and declare God glorious over the entire PTC! [Rzarector begins to hiss at the top of his lungs as he immediately drops to his knees in front of the fire. In a whisper he begins to pray.] Rzarector: God take my daily vitamins of life and supplement myself to live one day further. Give me the courage and strength to be able to defend myself in the ring. The fate of my soul rests on this so please guard it with accuracy. One day I shall reign supreme with you God! Amen. [Rzarector stands back up with a gruesome, but mesmerizing, look on his face.] Rzarector: Mayhem, back down or I shall resurrect you into the eternal afterlife of FIRE!!! [Rzarector begins hissing savagely as the camera slowly fades to black.]
