Disrespect

10 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-10 Maria Moraco, Sebastian’s mother, sat on the couch Indian style watching the Spanish Channel ten o’clock news while she waited for her husband to arrive. Juan Sebastian had been working late hours the last couple of months just to make ends meet. When Sebastian left for his trip to D.C., which he explained as being necessary for school, she thought that she would have more time alone with her husband. She was right. She would have more time alone, all alone. Maria dangled the remote control in front of her gazing over at the clock. It was almost going to be eleven. Keys dangled outside the apartment door, and Maria came to live. She tossed the remote aside and opened the door hastily to welcome her husband home. “Como te fue en el trabajo?” Maria asked. “Work was the same,” Juan answered exhausted. “Poor equipment, people being late, bad attitudes. I’m getting tired of the shop, but there’s nowhere else to go.” Maria kissed Juan on the lips passionately. “I’m sure there’s something else you can do. You can’t be out of the house all day. We need you here. I need you. I’ll reheat your dinner.” Juan went to the bedroom to change out of his working clothes and Maria walked into the kitchen to reheat dinner. Moments later, Juan came back in his home clothes and sat at the dinner table looking at the TV. Maria gave him his dinner which tonight was ‘Chaulafan’. It was his favorite dish. Brown rice cooked with shrimp, scrambled eggs, peas, hot dogs, and beef, served with ribs and a touch of Zhumir wine. “Gracias tesoro,” he thanked his wife and turned the channel to ESPN. After a hard day’s work, he was eager to watch some sports. At least, he hoped, his favorite soccer teams won. “Have you heard from our son lately?” Juan asked. Maria sat down next to him. She hadn’t heard from Sebastian since last week. It was unusual considering that Sebastian had been calling them every night since he first went on the trip. Like any mother, she was concerned but convinced herself that her son was all right. “No, I haven’t heard from him since last week. I’m getting worried.” Juan was worried too but he tried not to show it. He knew he and Sebastian weren’t seeing much of each other lately, and with his late hours he seldom was home to answer his son’s phone calls. Their only communication during the past couple of weeks had been in the form of messages delivered between them by Maria. “How has he been doing?” “He’s doing good,” Maria explained. “That’s what he says anyways. He says he’s got a ton of pictures for us when he gets back.” “Did he say when he is getting back?” Juan asked eager to see his son again. Maria shook her head. “No, says they’re still doing the training. I hope he finishes soon. It’s so lonely here.” Juan didn’t say anything. He knew he was at fault too. However, he couldn’t help it. He was working late to make ends meet, but was sacrificing time with his family. He knew with Sebastian gone that his wife was alone most of the day. He hated that. He didn’t want Maria to feel neglected. “I…” Juan began to apologize for his neglect but then stopped cold when something on ESPN caught his attention. Maria looked at him awkwardly. “Que pasa?” she asked. On the screen was a picture of their son, Sebastian Moraco. “Our son seems to have become famous,” Juan explained in a mixture of both joy and disbelief. Maria looked at the screen and sure enough her son was on TV, along with a picture of another man she knew nothing of. That man would be Doctor Curiosity. Now we switch from the opening week of the NBA, which has been full of surprises, to the ongoing GT tournament. Four rounds ago, fighters from all over the world came to Washington D. C, to find out just who will be the best fighter in this year’s tournament. We’ve seen men and women do the impossible, seen favorites defeated, and even seen guys who have no business in the ring winning. It’s now round five of the Primetime Central’s GT Tournament and the action is hotter than ever. The first match of the round will be between one of the favorites to win the entire thing, Doctor Curiosity, and the upstart amateur wrestler from Spain, Sebastian Moraco. Juan dropped his fork on the table and everything passed in slow motion. Tournament? Fighting? Sebastian Moraco? Just what the hell was going on here? Juan raised the volume hoping that maybe he was hearing the news wrong. He wasn’t. Yes, his son was competing in a gruesome and profitable fighting tournament. “What is this GT tournament he keeps talking about?” Juan asked hoping for answers. Maria shrugged. She had the look of uneasiness and anger in her face. “He said he was there for school.” The kid, Sebastian Moraco, the youngest fighter left in the tournament has been amazing. I mean he’s battled from beginning to end defying all odds. To think that we almost saw the last of him after round one, is amazing to now see him fighting such a revered fighter. I don’t know where that kid came from, but he’s going to be a big name when this is over. Which might be soon. He’s been a 20-1 underdog, but that spread has now been changed to 46-1. No matter what, he’s going to walk of that tournament with a lot of money and a lot of publicity. “Sebastian said he was doing some training for school,” Maria explained. She was trying to convince herself more than her husband. She knew Juan hated fighting, and hated glorifying it even more. She felt the same way and that was the reason they gave Sebastian a hard time when he said he wanted to be on the wrestling team. However, they learned to accept that sport, but this was an entirely different situation. He wasn’t in a sport. He wasn’t competing for a greater good. He was risking his body and life to hurt others just to get money. That was something she and her husband could definitely not approve. “It doesn’t look like he told us the truth, Maria,” Juan commented taking a sip from his juice. “Why would he lie to us? Why didn’t he tell us the truth?” “Because he knew we would say no.” Maria answered. Sebastian Moraco had lied to them. Lying is one thing, but to lie to your parents beckons punishment of another sort. Progress Sebastian Moraco slung the towel over his shoulder and took a drink from the faucet. He had spent the entire day yesterday touring the city and visiting Sarah at her workplace. He hadn’t felt like training for his match with Doctor Curiosity, which was coming soon. Between reading articles about his chances, or lack thereof, and hearing the voice of the public, Sebastian hadn’t felt too eager to train for his match. However, he knew he had to train. He knew if that he was to neglect the good doctor, that everything everyone said about him was going to be true. It would be his last run. Neglect the doctor? No. To neglect and ignore Doctor Curiosity was to invite death. Death was the personification of losing in this tournament. For all its definitions, to lose here was to die here. Once you were out, you were out and the only amount of life you had left depended on how you went out. Doctor Curiosity was a shrewd man. A man who wouldn’t think twice of breaking the law if it meant certain victory. Doctor Curiosity had rampaged through the competition with seldom trouble. His methods were both unusual and effective. It was his deranged mind that made him such a formidable opponent. He was neither brutal nor violent. He was deranged, cynical, and a genius. It is the latter combination that made him deadly. Sebastian realized that but didn’t care. He was going to defeat the good doctor. Thus, he spent today refining his physique. He had already learned enough wrestling moves over the past few weeks to make a worthy showing. Now all that was left was to continue to training and remain strong and swift enough to continue winning. Today had been a hectic workout and Sebastian continued to drink after the strenuous cardio activity. He stopped drinking and stood up straight rolling his eyes. Like a saiyan sensing the energy of his enemies, Sebastian could sense someone watching him. He knew who it was as well. “What do you want this time?” Sebastian asked not turning around. [name withheld] laughed in his hoarse voice. No matter what, Sebastian was surprising him every week. Maybe that is why he was feeling uneasy about the scandal he was involved in. He never had any ill will towards Sebastian, but even his good intentions couldn’t help his greediness. Jeff cleared his throat and tapped Sebastian on the shoulder. “You are on amazing kid, you know that?” Sebastian scoffed. “Excuse me, but I have to get pack up my things. I’m supposed to meet up with Sarah in an hour and I still haven’t showered yet.” Sebastian left Jeff alone, but Jeff kept following him. “You can’t neglect me throughout the entire tournament. You know damn well I’m going to be here running neck and neck with you. I’ll be running alongside until you reach the finals.” Sebastian stopped from his forced actions of looking busy. “You think that you will see me in the finals?” Jeff shrugged. “I think you and I should be together ‘til the end.” There was much more to what Jeff was saying than Sebastian knew. Jeff was honest when he said that he hoped that he could see Sebastian in the finals. He WANTED Sebastian to go that far, because the farther he went the more money he was going to make off him. However, the part of them being together ‘til the end was a lie. There was no companionship. There was no trust. There was no concrete friendship. Jeff knew that. But with Ryan Howell wasting his time and spoiling the fruits of their labor, there was no one else for Jeff to turn to. Ryan had gone beyond his own glory and now was a sore for sight eyes. Sebastian was the new hero in town. A completely naïve and ignorant hero. “I don’t think we need to continue to have this coach-player relationship you seem desperate to keep,” Sebastian explained to him continuing to pack his gear. “I’ll thank you for giving me this opportunity. The last couple of weeks have become exciting to say the least. I never thought I could be fighting at center stage with so much at stake. It really puts the collegiate amateur wrestling divisions on a whole different level.” “No we don’t,” Jeff agreed. “But we do need to make sure that you’re prepared.” Sebastian flashed him a smirk. “You don’t trust me to be ready?” “I trust you to be competitive,” Jeff explained. “I can’t trust you to let me know what you have planned for these matches. Come on. Trust me a little bit here. I can’t help you get the publicity you want, if you’re going all island about this.” Sebastian chuckled. He didn’t care. With each passing round, he was beginning to realize that Jeff had an ulterior motive. He just didn’t know what it is. Jeff sighed. “Tell me your not hatching some elaborate plan.” Sebastian finished packing the last of his workout gear and zipped his obsidian duffel bag closed. “It isn’t anything that you…need be made…privy to.” With that said, he bid farewell and turned away from Jeff. Jeff was not in the mood to be ignored again. He grabbed Sebastian by the arm forcibly and pulled him back. “Listen to me, punk.” Jeff scolded. “I gave you this opportunity. I gave you this chance. The least you can do is trust me as your assistant coach.” Sebastian pulled away from Jeff and their conversation had attracted the eyes of a few bystanders. Sebastian glared at them and moments later they all resumed their own businesses. “I have everything under my own fucking control,” Sebastian replied. “I’ve won enough times to get this far, haven’t I? I know what kind of man Doctor Curiosity is. I know no one is giving me a chance to beat him. I’ve heard the same song repeated over and over again in previous rounds. However, I’m still here.” Jeff nodded. “Yeah…” “Shut up,” Sebastian snarled. “You want to know what I have planned? I can show you.” Jeff’s eyes gleamed. “What are you saying?” Sebastian motioned for Jeff to come closer with is index finger like a hunter calling for his dog. Jeff walked closer as if he was some elementary school girl listening in on a secret. “What is it?” he asked. “You know I have been paying a lot of attention lately,” Sebastian whispered. “I’ve seen their weaknesses and their strengths. I’ve even seen their similarities. You’ve got a bum knee, don’t you?” Jeff was taken aback. “Yeah…” But before he could answer that, well more specifically, Sebastian landed a closed right fist to his jaw and snatched his legs out from underneath. He put his left leg in between Jeff’s, overlapped his, and then turned him around and pulled back on it. It was amateur. It was also his attempt at doing a moved known as the sharpshooter. Jeff began to grimace and scream in pain. “What the fuck are you doing?!” He bellowed. “You know a man widely known as Bret Hart called this move the ‘sharpshooter’,” Sebastian lectured him. “It’s supposed to bring pain to your knees while bending them backwards against your back. Honestly, I think it hurts your back more than your leg. What do you think?” Jeff couldn’t answer. He was trying desperately to find a way out of it. He was clawing the carpet, screaming, and reaching for anything to use. A few of the people around them had started to notice the commotion and they crept closer to them. “I don’t think Curiosity has any health issues,” Sebastian wondered as he continued to apply the bold move. “He seems to be doing alright. But, you and him are both roughly the same age are you not? If I’m not mistaken at your age, your body doesn’t take too well to pressure. So, if I break your back and maybe leg, then I can break his as well.” “Stop it,” Jeff snarled in pain. “Let…me…go.” Sebastian shook his head. “No, you see this is what I have planned for Curiosity. I’ve made it this damn far and no one is going to tell me what to do. No one is going to tell me I don’t have a chance. My victories haven’t been a fluke. I’ve earned them the hard way. I didn’t resort to underhanded methods like certain people are forced to do because they lack the talent.” “Ack!” Jeff screamed in pain. He felt as if his bones were going to snap. The ruckus the two were causing now had attracted more curious eyes. No longer were they watching though. Now they were all yelling for Sebastian to let go of the poor old man. Typical. How can a young kid like Sebastian beat an old man? Easily “Don’t ever question my spirit,” Sebastian warned him as he finally let go. He walked back away from the scene motioning for the crowd to remain calm. “I know you’ve been hiding something from me, Jeff. I don’t know what it is, but I am going to find out.” Sebastian glared at Jeff waiting for him to explain himself. Jeff wasn’t going to talk anytime soon. Even if he could say something, he wasn’t about to let Sebastian in on his secret. It isn’t often you see people betting on this sport. Worse yet, you don’t see people betting on people they claim to be friends with. Yet, that is more understandable then hitting piñatas in the hopes that it can help you defeat someone else. It is more understandable than letting someone else run your life. It is more understandable than feigning illness and using underhanded methods to win. How ignorant people are to the reality of everything. “Sebastian Moraco?” A voice bellowed from the crowd. Sebastian turned to see a middle-aged man with a husky build. “Yeah, what do you want? A court case?” “The name is Mr. Sams,” Jonathan introduced himself. “You can call me Jonathan.” “All right, Jonathan.” Sebastian mocked him. “What are you a lawyer?” “No, I’m a member of the GTT Committee,” Jonathan explained. “And you better get your act straightened up.” The crowd had begun to disperse and Jonathan was left alone with Sebastian. Oh yes, and with Jeff still on the floor trying to recuperate. “I don’t know what the hell your problem is with this man, but I am not going to have some blatantly aggressive offspring ruining my show. You better hope this man doesn’t decide to sue the company, or you and your family are going to be in huge trouble.” The man Jonathan referred to was Jeff, but he knew that already. Jonathan was also involved in their schemes, so he had to feign ignorance for the more evil cause. “Relax, Mr. Sams,” Sebastian replied. “That man won’t be requesting any court cases. I know him too well.” “I hope you are right,” Jonathan lied. “Now go on and get out of here.” Sebastian picked up his duffel bag and made his way out of the gym. On his way he caught the stares of a few bystanders who were still mesmerized with his actions. No matter what sex they were, they all quickly turned away as soon as his eyes met theirs. Were they afraid of him? Did they hate him? Sebastian was feeling a little differently now. He never once thought that he could attack someone in the middle of a crowd. Worse yet, he felt great doing it. It was one thing to fight in front of thousands in the GT tournament. Yet, this was different. There wasn’t a reason for his fighting. He was just angry. This new feeling both made him nervous and joyous. Maybe it was the constant fighting. Maybe it was the constant stress. Maybe it was the constant isolation. Maybe it was the constant underestimation. Whatever the cause was, Sebastian was changing. He no longer was the goody poster boy looking to win gold. Now he was more in tuned with his violent side to win. Winning had another purpose. Winning meant he was better. Not by a little, but by much. Winning defined him now. Winning was natural. Naturally, he was going to win again. “Excuse me, Sebastian?” A cute female voice called for him. Un fucking believable! Sebastian turned around. “Who the hell are you?” The intern, the same intern in Jonathan Sams’ office, apologized. “I’m sorry but you got a message. A lady named Maria called you from New York. She said you can reach her at this number.” She handed Sebastian a folded white piece of paper and left. Sebastian opened it up and the number he saw looked very familiar. “aww…shit,” Sebastian muttered. He grabbed his obsidian cell phone out of his pocket and dialed the number. He was somehow hoping that no one would pick up. He hoped no one would pick up. He didn’t want to explain himself not now. The phone rang once. No answer. Twice. No answer. Trice. No answer. Four times. No answer. Five times. No… “Hello?” the other line answered. Sebastian was quiet for a good moment. He was speechless. “Hello?!” the other line demanded. “Um…hi mom,” Sebastian finally blurted out. “Sebastian!” Maria cried out in glee. “It’s so good to hear from you.” Sebastian felt nervous. He knew something was up. “Yeah, I’m sorry I haven’t called much lately. How’s everything?” “Everything is fine, son,” Maria answered. “Your father is still working late. I’m still cleaning the house. We’re still waiting for you to finish your training in D. C.” “Um…” Sebastian tried to explain to them the truth. He knew that she knew. Otherwise, how could she leave a message for him? “I want to tell you about the trip. The truth is…” “Aww, you don’t have to explain anything, son.” Maria said. “I would like to know what this GT Tournament is all about and, more importantly, what are you doing there?” “I can explain…” “I thought your father and I told you to get away from those ridiculous things!” Maria scolded him. She was angry. She had been angry the entire day after she learned the truth. Now, Sebastian knew how angry she was. “Your father and I strictly told you not to fight for selfish reasons. And what do you do? You lie to us? You lie to us just so you can go and hurt other people. You put your own life at stake. Did you even think about that? Didn’t you think of all the things that could go wrong? Those people don’t have a conscious, goddamnit!” “Mom, I…” “Don’t ‘Mom’ me, Sebastian Miguel Moraco,” Maria scolded him. “I am so disappointed in you. I thought we raised you better than this. I thought we taught you to respect other people. But you don’t even respect us. You lied to us and you put your own life in danger.” “Mom, I’m sorry…” “No,” Maria replied. “Your father and I want you to return home, immediately.” With that she hung up. Sebastian stood still in the middle of the entranceway. He couldn’t believe it. Why now? He was finally beginning to enjoy himself. He was finally beginning to see the fruits of his labor. He finally met Sarah. The intense match with Doctor Curiosity was drawing near. After all his hard work, Sebastian was being forced to let it go. However, he couldn’t let it go. Sebastian clenched his fist and walked out the door. He had come too far. He had done too much to let it waste away. He can’t let go. Your father and I want you to return home, immediately.”
