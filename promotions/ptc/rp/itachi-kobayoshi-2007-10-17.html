Itachi Kobayoshi

17 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Itachi Kobayoshi Honor and Respect. It's a simple lesson taught to all children in the country of Japan. Honor yourself, and others will respect you. Respect others, and you will earn their honor. Americans can't seem to grasp this ideal. It's all about the here and now. All about what's best for number one. This is a sport. A sport that was founded on this idea; Honor and Respect. Since the founding on this foundation of morals, men like Clinton Sage and his Affliction have warped the meaning and the purity of our sport. What was once called the Sport of the Kings has been reduced to a joke in your country. I come from a land that still respects wrestling, and pays it the honor it deserves. Every man before me, and most likely every man after me will give you the reasons why this match should revolve around them. I simply want to put in a request. I saw what the Illusionist can do. I want to test myself against a man like that. I want to add the Illusionist to the list of names of men I've stood across from and earn the respect of. I want the Illusionist to feel the same way Seymour Almasy, Jason O'Neil, Carson Riley, and soon-to-be Tsuyoshi Tanaka felt when they stepped into the ring across a man that will do nothing but bring out your best. I want to honor the tradition of the Infinite Gauntlet, a tradition of building men to be their best in a place where no man is below any other. I want to earn the respect of not only the Illusionist, but the other men and women vying for a chance to dethrone him. My plea to the authorities of the Infinite Gauntlet are just that, requests. I demand nothing. I simply want to see if I can manage to bring some honor to FUSE Wrestling. I simply want to earn the respect of my peers. By now, you've figured out my message, my goal, and my ambition. They're all summed up with two simply words: Honor and Respect. My name is Itachi Kobayoshi, and I came to America, to FUSE Wrestling, to the Infinite Gauntlet to spread the word that the nation of the rising sun has been preaching for centuries. Honor and Respect.
