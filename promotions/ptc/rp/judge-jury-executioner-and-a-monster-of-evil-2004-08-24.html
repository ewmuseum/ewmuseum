Judge, Jury, Executioner And A Monster Of Evil

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 Something is so eerily different about my master, those experienced eyes I used to love have been replaced with eyes that have been crafted from the depths of hell. His voice once cocky and posh seems to be rough, like sandpaper. He sits just a few feet away from me staring at the rain as it patters against the windows, he smiles as he looks up at the heavy black cloud that sweeps over the sky like death during the plague. He suddenly turns to me and for a few seconds he seems to challenge me to say what I feel, it’s as if he knows every thought that runs through my mind. He smiles as lightning suddenly cracks the sky. “Butler, do you want to know the truth? The truth is I have lived a lie.” His eyes lock on to me, he seems to be sucking away from me, feeding on my mind. I pretend to busy myself by rearranging his desk where his recent business contracts already lie in a neatly stacked pile. He walks towards me, I feel like an animal trapped inside a cage as the vet comes to put it to it’s final sleep. I see a slight space to my left and turn to walk into it but he reacts instantly and I find myself walking straight into him. I attempt to back off but I simply walk back into the wall behind me. He looks at me like a lion ready to pounce on it’s prey. “Admit it Butler, I am evil. For all these decades I have been hiding the past but why? I should be proud of what I did.” I give in and try to look away but his cold empty right hand grabs me by the chin and holds me so I have to stare into his devilish eyes. I realize that struggling is of no use and instead I close my eyelids. I feel his cold breath biting at my skin like an icy wind in winter. “Those people I surrounded myself with were weak, selfish and liars. They pretended to care for me, care for others, but in all honesty they looked out for themselves. And lying is a sin, a sin which they were punished for, a sin for which I was the trial, jury and executioner.” I felt tears swell up in my eyes, this was the man I held onto, the man that perhaps I even lived for. And here he was, being proud of what he did. I knew the tale as well as everyone but perhaps I didn’t know his feelings, I always believed he hated himself for it but now he seemed proud, as if he donated millions to a charity. “Do you know what is even more ironic Butler? In just a couple of days I team up with three sinners, the Muppet Man, JMA and Desolation. Each one is only entering this event for personal glory, each one reminds me of my own mother and father. The ones who I convicted for their sins, the ones that watched as their only son paid them back for their crimes. The ones that are no more than ashes.” I felt ready to vomit, the tears started to roll down my cheeks but I quickly wiped them away, desperate to not show any sign of weakness to my…to this monster. I imagined his parents staring at him through the flames as he wreaked havoc through his own home, this being that stood just inches from me was a human. A lion who ripped the flesh of his own mother’s bones. “Being honest my old friend, are the men I team with rule abiding citizens?” I refused to answer yet he knew I’d have shook my head if I wasn’t so sickened. He laughed, amused at my weakness. I clenched my fists, knowing that he could lash out at any time and be done with me in a matter of seconds but then again that wasn’t his way. To be honest I know the only reason he keeps me is because I’ve always been emotional, it’s as if I’m his gate way to being human. Yet this wasn’t the same man, in fact I don’t think it’s even human. “Butler, the only reason I will show up to this big survival event is because these men are so sinful, so evil. I love it Butler, all these sinners supposedly teaming up to represent their respected promotions when actually they are looking for glory, yet as they commit their sins they fail to notice the big evil lurking around the corner. Here I am, waiting for them, with ever blind step they take they advance nearer to me. Soon, at the survival event they will take their final steps and there in the centre of the ring they will come face to face with true evil and they will fail. For every sin they have committed I will be their judge, jury and…their living nightmare.” He finally released me and I rushed away, frightened to look back this monstrous being that once was my beloved master.
