Survival RP#2

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-22 [ "It must be really nice living in your worlds. One where you are the center of the wheel, where you are so very important that everything revolves around you."] ["Its just too damn bad that neither the real world, or even PTC is like that, you see. Because, you just aren't very important at all."] [Fade up.] [Since its smacktalk time, it means we've lost our totality of imagination. Nicky Jam is in a half-seated/half-lying down position upon his La-Z-Boy, a nearly empty bottle of VO sitting in the cupholder on the armrest, and a copy of this month's "Hustler" sitting unread at his feet.] Nicky Jam: "Contrary to your personal belief boys, not everything has to do with you. The problem with believing that everything has some greater meaning, either to you or to some higher power, is that you start looking for explanations and causes where none exist. You see conspiracies in every shadow, and find some way to bring them back to you. "That way lies delusion, paranoia." [Nicky Jam is dressed rather casually for an evening at home. Blue jeans and a black t-shirt is pretty much his entire ensemble, with thick, white socks as well. In case you were wondering.] Nicky Jam: "You seem to think everything is about you, and you couldn't be more wrong. Whether it be something I'm doing. Or something that upper management is doing. Or something that someone else is doing, doing to you because they have an ulterior motive. You're entitled to your beliefs, of course, but don't try and force them upon me when you're this wrong. "I wonder, what exactly would you be doing this week if the Powers That Be hadn't awarded you people this matches against UPWA? Would you even be booked, let alone in something close to a main event type of match? Or would you be sitting at home, pouting that you're unloved and unwanted, and trying to figure out what you could do next to try and drag the product down? "You are booked as part of the PTC Survival showcase, because I am here and I am important enough to elevate you to that lofty status. This matches, and its standing, are about us, and this matches would be killer no matter who who we were booked against. Its that simple. "Where as you fools, are not very important at all. Except to yourselves." [Pause. Deep breath.] Nicky Jam: "Everyone in PTC and in the world of wrestling has an ego, and I'm prepared to accept that you have one as well. But the level to which yours has grown, and to which you speak out, is ridiculous even by the standards that have long since been set around here. And I'm not just talking in your thirty-second sound bytes that you pollute the airwaves with before every match. I'm referring as well to what you say when you're at the arena, to people like the Interview guys. "Like your words during any given promo, for example. You pretty much come out and say that you think you deserve a shot at a title. Nevermind the fact that you barely got a decent won/lose record around here, and that your fed has many more qualified people, most of whom have not had that chance management is giving you already. You believe that you ought to be the top contender to every major title in your fed, despite again there being many other qualified contenders. "Hell, girls, you're getting your shot at UPWA, and you won't get the job done. Explain the match any way your little heart desires.... make your pitiful excuses to try and look good. Go ahead and tell the world that you were dominating the match and that we got 'lucky', if that'll make you feel better. Claim we win our matches with nothing more than smoke and mirrors, as if that will grant you any credibility. Call it whatever you want. You see, it all boils down to this nonetheless.... "We will win, and you will lose." [Nicky Jam grins.] Nicky Jam: "By the way, I'd like to congratulate you on completing some of your rites of passage into manhood. Like the ones where you're so freaking proud of yourselves for just being part of this special thing we call PTC, and how that makes you something more than you are. And like the ones where you're so freaking proud of yourselves because you're closing in on fame and fortune. "The only reason you take those small victories away from your matches is because you don't have the big ones. Black and white, there is a winner and there is a loser, and you both fall on the wrong side of that line. "It doesn't matter if you ever walk out of a building with gold around your waist. Deep down you'll still lost the match to us, and if you feel gratified that you were so damn stubborn to not give us the satisfaction of actually killing you, that's great for you. Personally, I don't care if you live or you die, winning is the thing. And if you don't win, there really isn't very much to say about it. So you can have your 'I didn't back down' talks and you're welcome to it. "It doesn't matter that you might almost beat us. The fact of the matter is that you won't, and you don't get bonus points because you almost won. You lost, and that should be the end of the discussion. I don't think people like you keep me awake at night worrying that if we fight again, we might lose this time because we 'almost' lost last time, at least by your thinks-so. "In the words of former Yankee GM George Weiss: Either you're first, or you're nothing. "I guess we all know where you fall on that equation." [A small cafe in the downtown of any old city, near the campus of a small midwestern school. Its the middle of the school day, and it is also cool, cloudy, and a rather windswept afternoon, so there shouldn't be much of a surprise that the crowds are sparse. Nicky Jam and a friend are finishing up a late lunch, working out the last of a handful of details for their ongoing operations.] Nicky Jam: "We're all done here?" Friend: "I think everything is done. I need a small favor from you, though." Nicky Jam: "Go ahead." [The friend reaches down into the briefcase at his feet, pulling out a thick manilla envelope.] Friend: "When you see Mac in a few days, please give these to him for me?" Nicky Jam: "Important papers that must reach him posthaste and with the utmost caution?" Friend: "Actually, no." [Nicky Jam's eyes narrow.] Friend: "To tell you the truth, there's nothing to these papers at all. But since you're such a high profile and people have connected you to both of us, eyes are going to be on you. You are nothing more than the stalking horse, while our real courier goes back and forth without the eyes of the world upon him." [Fire lights in the eyes of Nicky Jam now, who stands up, reaches across the table, and grabs his friend by his collared shirt -- lifting the man two feet into the air in the process.] Friend: "Put me down, man." [The friend motions to his bodyguards at nearby tables, who sit back down but don't take their eyes off the situation.] Nicky Jam: "I am so freaking important to you right now, and you want me to be a decoy?" Friend: "Look, give me a chance to explain...." Nicky Jam: "Screw your explanations, dude. You know damn well that my time and energies are too important to waste on being a decoy. I've saved your ass a couple of times already, and I'm certainly more important than to be the smokescreen you hide behind." Friend: "Nicky Jam, I'm only asking because its not like you're going out of your way if you do this.... you're already going there, I'm just tacking on something you can do to help me. You understand the need for misdirection and discretion as well as anyone else.... when people see that you haven't done anything worth a damn at all in this situation, that deflects attention from us both. And that can only help out our other endeavours." Nicky Jam: "Then you clearly don't understand the parameters of our relationship. Other than our mutual interests here with my brothers in the backstage area, I want nothing to do with your syndicate, and you should not have anything further to do to me." [Nicky Jam shoves backwards, sending his friend lying a few feet behind his chair on the ground. The friend picks himself up and dusts himself off.] Nicky Jam: "Remember I can screw you over a hell of a lot more than you can screw with me. Something to keep in mind the next time you intend to screw around with me." [Nicky Jam picks up the thick envelope and storms off. The eyes and ears of the mid-afternoon diners return to their own conversations and their own concerns shortly thereafter. The friend turns to one of his own bodyguards.] Friend: "Keep a close eye on that one. I'm not so sure how much longer we can depend on him to stay in line, not with that attitude. Nicky Jam: "I know this will come as a monumental surprise, UPWA opponents, but you're not nearly as good as you'd like to be. "You have been around PTC, and you haven't done much of anything. Sure, I know you'll happily go on and on about how you beat this person or that person. And you'll use that one victory to establish your legacy and convince us all that you're this master of the ring that we should all quake in fear from. "But, guys, have you ever really looked at it, and seen it as the exception to the rule, rather than the rule itself? Because, the fact of the matter is that you are mediocre talents at best who got lucky on one occasion when whoever chose you guys to be on this big event. Because, guys, you have no other big victories over major names to back it up. "To keep things current, I'm going to recount your histories in truly big matches since you joined PTC. I guess this is going to be a very short subject since you really haven't had any. Let's see...you're going to lose to UPWA. Believe me my friends...you don't get a star on the walk of fame for crap like that." [Nicky Jam holds up his hands, as if to stave off the explanations and mitigating circumstances that are verbally coming his way.] Nicky Jam: "I know, I know.... there's a good reason for all of that.You're children fighting in a man's world . Or maybe it was that you were in the wrong place at the wrong time. Or maybe it was your egos that got in the way to lead you to believe you were people who were on so much of a roll that you could beat a great team in one single night -- if your hubris lumps you into that category of grappler. "And that changes nothing. The fact of the matter is that you've got a couple of wins, then dropped the ball when it came time to get a big win. And if we went further back, to the years you've spent in other companies, your record is equally as sorry when the stakes are high and the chips are on the line. Face it, guys.... "You're losers who are unable to bring their game up to the level required when it counts. And no amount of excuses will change that fact." [Nicky Jam reaches into the cuprest, uncaps the bottle of VO, and takes a long swallow, draining its contents dry.] Nicky Jam: "Success, by the way, is often measured by winning matches and titles that are important. It also means that you're one big win against an over-the-hill wrestler is buried under an avalanche of losses against people who matter considerably more than him. "Some people are at least capable of winning a title before they fall on their ass. And normally, their pretty good about keeping their focus on the match. Not like you, when you're already talking nosense about nothing. Not like you, when you spent most of the week leading up to our match-up talking about a whole lot of nothing. Not like you, when you blather to anyone that will listen about how you're going to fight us and whomever else PTC books you against. "Go ahead and tell me how you beat that person. Or make up some other chain of wrestlers, each of whom beat the one below them, you on top and us on the bottom. And tell me how that makes you a success, and better than us. Only there's a fatal flaw to that line of reasoning.... "We will beat you. That cuts the legs out from another argument you might think about making as to why you're within screaming distance of a ghost of a longshot in this matches. "That's the plain, unfortunate truth, guys. And you can't dodge it no matter how you try." [Nicky Jam lets out a deep breath, taking in another one to start a new.] Nicky Jam: "If you had any credibility in PTC right now, I'd call you a photocopy of a photocopy of a photocopy of past opponents. Other people who claimed they were going to beat us, and couldn't come through. "Credibility, by the way, is often measured by being consistent, doing what you say you are going to do, and having the balls to own up to your own achievements. And on that score, you are zero against us. "First, there's this whole thing about you're not being consistent, except consistently choking in big spots. Let me put your records aside for the moment, though, and concentrate on your characters. You don't fool anyone with this latest babble, guys, because everybody out there expects you to act that way." [Nicky Jam holds up one finger, as if a pause sign on his rant.] Nicky Jam: "Though my congratulations go out to you on breaking the PTC record for most meaningless promos in one event, which I believe all of you were the previous holders for." [The finger comes back down.] Nicky Jam:"You are hard to take seriously when we don't know from week to week what kind of persona and mindset you're going to bring to the table. And I doubt the fans really care after the umpteenth time you switch sides of the fence for whatever reasons you'd like to give as self-justification. We can't be bothered by such trivialities, when it would be rendered moot a few couple of later. "They'd care if were I to turn to the other side, because I'm an established icon of law and order and doing the right thing. They'd care if I were to set aside his dastardly ways and deeds and become a champion of goodness, because I'm one of the biggest villains out there today. As for all of you, you've flipped so many times that its meaningless. "Then, we have this whole thing about doing what you say you're going to do. The only thing I really have to point out in that regard is that you come out week after week, and you say you're going to beat someone and get the job done. Most of the time, you can't even back that one up. "Go ahead, make your excuses once more about why you lost each particular match, because that will flow nicely into my last point regarding credibility. And that point is that you gain some credibility by taking responsibility for yourself, whether for good or for ill. "Its really easy to go out there and say 'This is me!' when things are going well. To claim that its simply because you're the better wrestler, or out-thought your opponent, or found another innovative way to screw him. Its not nearly so easy when fortune turns against you, but its just as important then to do it. "When you spend your entire career making excuses for yourself, you become a parody. You belittle yourselves to such a point that the excuses become you, and that you always have another reason at your fingertips why you can't come through. And pretty soon, nobody expects you to succeed, because they're so inured to your failures by your own words. "Surely, guys, if you were any good you'd find a way to overcome such immense odds more than once in your life. You'd be able to handle the pre-arranged interference, deal with the interferer and still have enough to win the damn match. If you can't ever do that, sooner or later you're not even worth the extra effort. You'd be able to beat somebody without resorting to getting yourselves disqualified and slapping the disclaimer on that you weren't really trying to win the match. Nobody is buying that line of tripe, that you didn't want to win. And this whole destiny thing, that someone was pre-ordained to win and you just got in the way, that falls a little short, too. Either you're good enough to win, or you're not. And not just in this pathetic moral victories standard of yours either. "Its ugly, but that's a fact of life. It doesn't have to be prettied up just because you'd like it that way." [Another pause, another deep breath.] Nicky Jam: "If we could still judge you as 'having potential', you'd be equated somewhere with Lobo Xcalibur by my reckoning. Another person who thought they would beat me with a title on the line, and couldn't come through. [A lady friends's SUV, pulled off Interstate 94 at a rest stop. The woman in question is sitting in the front seat, cellular phone to her ear.] Lady Friend: "Yes, yes, everything is going exactly as planned, you need not worry about a thing...." [We don't hear the other end of the conversation this time, or what its regarding. Sorry, that's just the way things are today.] Lady Friend: "No, he has no idea what is really going on.... "You were right all along, he's too busy with himself and sees only what he wants to see.... "Yes, I said he's too distracted.... too many things on his mind, and he lets his mind fill in the blanks the way he thinks they ought to be filled in. He has no clue. "No, I have no doubt that the surprise will go off as planned.... just be assured that I'll play my part and the show will be worth the watching.... "I love you too.... buh-bye." [And that's all we have to show on that front.] Nicky Jam: "Lets get back to my initial point, guys, that you're not nearly as important as you think you are. Not to me, and not to much of anyone else, either. "I didn't address you that much before, because quite frankly you didn't give me much of a reason to do so. I have better things to do with my life than issue a brief soundbyte every couple of hours and fight a battle of wits with an unarmed opponent. You might forget this, guys, but its your job to convince people that you're going to be suitable opponents for us and have a shot at doing some damage. People know why we're here and what we're capable of doing, and that's really all the job we have in this pre-match hype unless you give me a damned good reason to go beyond. "If you want to believe that its me playing mind games with you, that's your choice. I'm not, because you honestly are not worth the bother. But if the belief will affect how you fight this matches and give us a better chance to win, we'll seize upon it and twist it to our advantage. To us, winning is the thing, and not those pathetic moral victories you take such pride in. "As for Mac, I doubt he cares that much about you either, no matter how much of a pestilence you try and make yourself out to be. Management tends to hold the high ground and the club, which makes them awfully hard to uproot or really even bother unless you've got a lot more in terms of irritation power than you ever could possess. You see, most management types have a really easy solution to mere employees who push them too far, which is overly elegant in its simplicity.... "They remove the problem entirely, most often by firing them and making sure they stay out." [Nicky Jam grins, not very nicely at that.] Nicky Jam: "You see, you can't be that much of a handicap to someone like Management if you just keep hanging around, and he lets you do so. You don't do more than foam at the mouth and let off hot air at anyone who will listen. You make your demands, many of which are completely ignored, and even when you get what you want, you can't do very much with it. I know, I've been watching the tapes even if I don't take a sabbatical in a video room and memorize every little aspect of my opponents' life. "Like when you demanded whoever in a match, and couldn't get the job done. Maybe beating them down will make you feel better about yourselves, but I doubt management really put much thought to it at all anymore. "You know, this whole 'credibility' issue I've spent some time on today." [Nicky Jam nods. We remember, yes we do.] Nicky Jam: "You're not that much of a pest to anyone else in PTC either. I doubt your names are brought up very often by much of anyone who is important enough to be someone -- be it inside this community or outside it -- unless we're making a joke at your expenses or using you as an unflattering comparison. Your past match opponents of any significance don't really talk about you in favorable terms when they discuss you at all. The staff and crew only mention you when they have reason to do so. "Face it, guys, you're has-beens and never-will-be, fit for jerking the curtains and fighting for meaningless belts like the ones I don't want. And that's not very likely to change." [Another pause, another deep breath.] Nicky Jam: "You seem to have this huge problems with management, and then you seem to have bigger problems when they don't really pay you much heed, much as we don't pay you much heed. You're not important enough most of the time for us to give a damn, and you don't give us a reason to change that way. So you go out and you spout your unpleasantries at them, and maybe you win a few fans among disgruntled employees of humdrum industries for your trouble. "And any credibility you gain in that fashion you've long since thrown away during your tenures in PTC, from the number of times you've taken handouts from the Powers That Be. "Be it from them giving you the matches you demand, only for you to fall flat on your faces like the fools you are. Be it from people awarding you this shot at us -- they're part of the management too, or have you forgotten? "Its really easy to mouth off and damn the Powers That Be. It just doesn't jive very well when you go out and take the baubles they scatter in your direction, because it undermines your whole argument in and of itself. Again, that whole pesky 'credibility' thing. I'm beating that drum an awful lot today, but it fits and I'll use it." [Yet another pause, and yet another deep breath.] Nicky Jam: "So now we get to this match that you've poured your hearts and souls into, that you've built up as the focus of your entire existence. Tell me guys what happens when you don't come through again, like you haven't come through in the past? "Well, I'll tell you what happens.... "First, you're going to find some excuses for why you lost. You're going to lay the blame on someone else and dodge responsibility for your own actions and inactions, much as you have your entire careers. You're going to make yourselves out as the victims, because that will make you feel important, as if you matter to someone else. You'll probably commiserate over the evils of PTC with your jobber unions, all of you wondering why you're at the bottom of the heap and making excuses for your lackluster performances. "Then, everyone is going to have a good laugh at your expense, because you'll have gotten all worked up over this and not delivered the goods. And when they're done laughing, maybe they'll book you against whomever is just entering into your fed, and see if you have enough self-respect left to crawl up from the bottom far enough for it to be worth kicking you back down. "As for me, I'll just take my belts and head to the next show, against whomever they've dredged up to face me. I know you're throwing a name or two out there, but right now that isn't an issue to me. You're looking ahead, and probably plotting your strategies, much like you have so many times in the past. Only once again, you'll be blindsided by reality, because you have never shown an inclination to learn from your mistakes and use those lessons to better yourselves. "Maybe when you do, you might become important enough to somebody and be worth their notice. But I truly doubt it." [The unpleasing grin widens upon Nicky Jam's face.] Nicky Jam: "Nothing personal guys, because you're just not important enough to us for it to get personal. "Nothing personal, guys, but you've got to be put down, the way a host of others were all put down before you. Nothing personal, just business. "You understand the importance of that one, right?" [End Feed.]
