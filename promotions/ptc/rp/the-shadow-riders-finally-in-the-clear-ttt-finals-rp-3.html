[The Shadow Riders] Finally in the Clear (TTT Finals RP 3)

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Shadow Riders Affiliated Pimp Joined: 08 Apr 2003 Posts: 4 Location: Over here now. Posted: Mon Apr 14, 2003 3:17 am Post subject: [The Shadow Riders] Finally in the Clear (TTT Finals RP 3) (The scene opens outside of the MGM Grand in Las Vegas, Nevada. A small light blue Pinto pulls up to a halt in front of the hotel. Two hotel valets quickly rush to open the doors of the car. As the doors swing open, “Superstar” Eric Russo and “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage step out. Russo is dressed in a suit which is almost a crayon like color of blue. Kage wears almost the exact same suit but in bright red. Russo tosses the keys to the valet as the two Shadow Riders head into the Casino section of the hotel. Once inside, Kage strikes up a conversation as the two men walk along the casino floor.) Kage: “I can’t believe Calestro really got us through with that mess so quickly and easily.” Russo: “I know, it was close there for awhile though.” (Russo’s expression falls.) Russo: “I just wish we could have kept a little more of the money. I never really thought it would cost much. I mean we had already done the hard part with bringing Johnny in. All Calestro had to do was a little clean up. That doesn’t warrant him walking away with more money then the two of us combined does it?” Kage: “No it doesn’t, but were you gonna be the one to stand around and argue with him?” (Russo’s face fills with trepidation as he stammers for an answer.) Russo: “I . . . uh, no . . . um . . . you we’re gonna do it. Right?” (Kage does not hesitate to tell Russo his true feelings.) Kage: “Hell no! I was gonna be more then happy to pay Calestro whatever the hell he wanted just to get him out of the house before he cleaned us too.” Russo: “You know what, good thinking. Besides, we still got some money left and where better to increase the amount of it then in my own home town of Las Vegas, Nevada? I got how to play these games down pat from watching some of the best over the years.” (Kage turns to Russo with a questioning look on his face.) Kage: “Tell me, do you know how to play these games legally or illegally?” Russo: “Well there’s all different definitions of legal. I mean they let Homo/Phobic into the ring with us. Some people would consider putting a team as horribly homosexual as them against the ring from the greatest tag team to ever walk the planet, a crime. PTC officials seem to feel otherwise though.” (Confused, Kage responds to Russo’s comment.) Kage: “How does that have anything to do with the legality of cheating at a casino?” Russo: “I’m saying that some people see my style of card play as illegal but I don’t.” (Kage lets out a sigh as he responds.) Kage: “I was afraid you were gonna say something like that to me. Look, just don’t get us caught while we’re doing this. I can’t deal with anymore problems today. I just wanna kick back and relax here in Vegas before our match at New Era.” Russo: “We will, but won’t it be cooler to kick back with three-hundred thousand dollars rather then thirty thousand?” (Reluctantly, Kage concedes to Russo’s point.) Kage: “Yeah . . . it would. What did you have in mind?” (A sly look crosses Russo’s face.) Russo: “The name of the game is distraction. I’ll draw the attention of the guards, guests, and dealers towards me while you grab some of the chips from the other players at the table. Got it?” Kage: “I think, but what exactly are you gonna do?” (Russo reassuringly pats Kage on the back as he responds.) Russo: “Just trust me. Now get going.” (As Kage moves off in search of a poker game with quite a few people at it, Russo walks over to a small area where a man sits behind a piano providing the gamblers with some musical entertainment. There is also microphone by the piano which the man uses to sing into from time to time. Without hesitation, Russo walks over to the piano player and snatches up the microphone. He then proceeds to shove the piano play off the bench which Russo then uses as a step to boost himself onto the top of the piano. Russo raises the microphone to his mouth as he begins speaking to the shocked onlookers in the casino.) Russo: “How’s everybody doing tonight?” (Russo pauses as though waiting for a response from the forming crowd but receives nothing. He begins pacing back and forth on top of the piano as he continues.) Russo: “Good to hear it. Now then, let’s get down to the real reason I’m up here shall we? My name is Eric Russo. I am here tonight to tell you people a little about my up coming match as one half of the Shadow Riders as we take on Homo/Phobic next week right here in this lovely hotel. Now I know what you are all thinking, because I thought it too when I first heard about this match. Despite popular belief however this is not a joke. Yes it true that the finals of the PTC Tag Team Tournament is about to take place after four log months of inactivity. There is still the downside to the match however. While it will be the finals of the Tag Team Tournament it will not be the stellar match-up that you have all been awaiting for so long. You see while Homo/Phobic has been rotting away in that red headed step child of a federation , the Shadow Riders have been selling out arenas as part of LOW’s illustrious roster.” (At this point the security team becomes aware of the commotion and begin to move in. Russo however continues unaffected.) Russo: “Now tell me, how does anyone expect the Shadow Riders to have a good match against Homo/Phobic? We’re gonna rip them apart when we get in the ring with them . . . it’s a no contest. What do they think their gonna do drink a bottle of Cherry Coke and somehow get the ability that they so greatly lack in the ring? Is that gonna help? I don’t think so otherwise they would have gotten the all UWF final that they so greatly wanted for this tournament rather then having us lay waste to a team they toted as one of their best. The simple fact of the matter is that no tag team has ever been able to even come close to us. We have always stood head and shoulders above the rest just as we will do once again.” (After grabbing up a few chips, Kage looks up and takes notice of the public display Russo is putting on. Not wanting to be left out of the ranting and raving, Kage turns and heads over towards Russo pushing by the casino security guards slowly closing in around the piano. He now jumps atop the piano and snatches the microphone from a shocked, Russo’s hand. Kage raises the microphone to his mouth as he launches into a rant of his own.) Kage: "My name is Jason Kage and as you all know, the Shadow Riders are coming up on quite a high profile match at PTC's first inter-promotional pay-per-view, New Era. In this match, after a seemingly never-ending postponement of the tag tourney, the first Primetime Central tag team champions will be crowned, and the Shadow Riders will finally be given their due. All we gotta do is beat another UWF team to take the gold home, and judging by the last UWF team we faced, that shouldn't be much of a challenge. But in all seriousness, I can't say the match doesn't have me at all worried. Any time you step into the ring with the Bum Boozler, there is adequate reason to worry. He's the only guy in the PTC who . . . no, wait, he's not in the PTC . . . how the hell did he get into this match? They're gonna stick the PTC tag team titles up for grabs in a match with non-PTC wrestlers? I don't think I follow this. But then again, I don't follow how a gold plated leather strap is going to wake a guy from a coma either. Well, anyway, about the Bum Boozler. He's the only guy in recent memory to ever make it this far in the industry by simply sodomizing all who stand in his way. No, wait, I guess I can't really say that either. After all, you have guys like Tristan Belmont, Jesse Jamester, and Mike Meyhu in the main event of the PPV who made names for themselves by performing sexual acts on other men. Here's a tip for you Boozler, if you want to be given PTC gold, go to Trigger's office and offer him your services. That little bastard would be more than happy to push you after hearing what you can give him. Well, I'm kind of rambling on now, none of that is neither here nor there. I guess what I should be addressing is Shane Stone's recent words to the Shadow Riders. I'd like to start by setting a few things straight. First off, we are not mass murders. There has never been any proof of us intentionally murdering anybody. Secondly, we're not being chased by police. As you can see, we're right here in the MGM Grand Casino in Las Vegas, Nevada, and we're not being pulled out at gunpoint." (Kage, now having a public platform to speak on, isn't his usual self. Rather than bragging of his exploits, he does his best to ride them off in front of people. He speaks in his most convincing voice as he pleas his innocence.) Kage: "We've never robbed any place. Never a bank, never a home, never a business, and never a casino." (Kage finishes this line as he looks down at his pocket, which is bulging with stolen chips.) Kage: "We're not bad people. We're just your typical run of the mill American citizens. We've never taken a life, let alone decapitated anyone. Where Homo/Phobic gets off saying things like this is beyond me. Slandering our good names to further your own characters. I'm shocked and appalled. When we tell you we're honorable people, we mean it. We would never lie to you." (Kage laughs to himself as he continues.) Kage: "And most of all, Shane, we'd never take candy from a baby. Our personal lives are not the matter of dispute here. Our souls are not on trial, and you're not the judge of anyone. Dignity was not a factor in this match, but Shane, you've made it one. You've made this personal. And if the Shadow Riders are called to bring Justice, then so we will. We'll give you your just desserts. Sometimes I think the Shadow Riders are the only upholders of justice left. Even the two of you have all ready proved to us all that you're not confident you can win this match on your own. Hell, you appointed your little friend Lunica to the booking staff for this match. That isn't a very just thing to do. You need to face the music. You need to own up for your heinous actions. I myself will be on a personal crusade from now until you are. I will hunt you down and make you face your just punishment. I will do this in the name of truth . . . justice . . . and the American way." (Kage's voice trails off now as he starts to lower his front and slip back into his true character.) Kage: "And besides, you guys suck anyway." (Kage jumps off of the piano to a rousing ovation from the gullible crowd. He begins bowing in appreciation and walks through the people. As he passes an older couple with their two or three year old grandchild, Kage extends his hand down and steals the child's lollipop. Russo picks up where Kage left off.) Russo: “Because Homo/Phobic will end up just like the rest, nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders.” (Russo jumps down to join Kage on the floor. The two men begin heading through the crowd of people until they are intercepted by the casino’s security team. The security team grabs hold of the two Shadow Riders as they begin to roughly escort them to the front door of the casino. Once there Russo and Kage are unceremoniously thrown out of the casino flat on their back’s onto the street where they started. Not even attempting to move off the ground just yet, Russo turns his head towards Kage and begins speaking.) Russo: “So did you get anything?” (Kage looks to Russo as he responds with a slight smirk.) Kage: “Yeah . . . I got us a little something.” Russo: “Strip club?” Kage: “Sounds good to me. Let’s go.” (The two Shadow Riders push themselves from the ground and head off away from us set to embark upon a new adventure. As they do this the picture fades out as the screen once again fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... "The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.". The screen now fades to black.) _________________ "You are nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders." - "Superstar" Eric Russo
