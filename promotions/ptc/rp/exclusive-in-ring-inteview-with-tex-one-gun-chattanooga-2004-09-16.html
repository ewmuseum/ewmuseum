Exclusive In Ring Inteview with Tex 'One Gun' Chattanooga

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Tex “One Gun” Chattanooga walks up to a microphone placed in the center of the ring. The crowd in the arena cheers him. They love Tex and Tex loves them like a bowl of chili in the early morning of a Texas morning. Tex: Evenin folks. I got announcement to make. The crowd quiets down in awe of Tex. Tex: Thank y’all for being here tonight. I am not wrestling, but I got something I would like to say. I am coming out of retirement for one match and that match is gonna be in GTT4! Now I know what y’all saying. But I still can compete and I will prove it. The crowd cheers. Tex: Thank. Now I am gonna be facing Fnord, a man that I have had an encounter with in the past. And it’s gonna be a hoot and a holler to get back at him. I hate him with a all my heart and soul and I despise his cotton pickin, lilly livered ass! The crowd cheers. Tex: Now Fnord I am gonna beat ya up one side a cow and down another and I hope you are ready for a Texas sized ass whoopin. Because you mess with me and you mess with Texas. Don’t mess with Texas. The crowd cheers as Tex takes off his cowboy hate and exits the ring. Everyone leaves and the lights turn off. GOOD NIGHT CHICAGO!!!
