Nightmares

13 October 2003

Hush little baby don’t say a word… Momma’s gonna buy you a mocking bird… And if that bird don’t sing… Momma’s gonna buy you a diamond ring… “Ugh…where am I?” mutters Syko, his eyes trying to adjust to his surroundings. “What is…this place?” mutters Syko as he slowly walks forward. In front of him…nothing Behind him…nothing To his left…nothing To his right…nothing Syko grabs his hair in a clutched fist and cries out, “What the hell is going on here?!” Syko begins to breathe heavily and frantically looks all around him. No matter where he looks all he can see is air. Pure oxygen. Pure hydrogen. Pure air. Above him, pink clouds fill up the sky in one blanket. They move…fast, too fast for clouds maybe. Syko closes his eyes and slowly looks down on himself. He opens his eyes slowly, afraid of what he may see. “I’m still…in my clothes” mutters Syko, “finally, something that I can relate to.” Syko sighs and leans his head back, once again gazing at the mysterious layer of pink clouds overhead. He was still wearing his blue jeans, and his white muscle shirt, with his favorite Black leather jacket on top of it. It was a sigh of relieve. A relieve to see something normal. He couldn’t explain what happened. Not in words at least. A few days ago, he had taken Mandy and Dawn to the outdoor restaurant, Bazaar del Mundo. He promised he would. A man of his word, is what he was. If only…he was like that all the time. Syko slowly begins to walk forward, “I know I am still alive. I can remember everything clearly. I remember, what I had this morning for breakfast, scrambled eggs. I remember the time Mandy got up to go work out, 5:30 am. I remember…Dawn running away. And unlike last time, this time she came back. I cannot be dead…” Whoosh….. Suddenly, as if out of nowhere, gusts of winds begin to pick up. It seemed odd to Syko. Very odd. Emptiness all around. Then suddenly, an aura of suspense, mystery, and horror. It was a feeling he couldn’t explain. He hadn’t felt this way, since Sebastian Richer kidnapped his wife over a year ago. Whoosh…it’s over…Whoosh… “Huh?” wonders Syko, stopping to look around. “What was that?” Did he just hear someone? Someone else? In this…place?! Syko looks around, and to no surprise he sees no one. Not even a small insect could be spotted. Syko looks down at the fall beneath him and then jumps back in fright. “What the hell is going on here?!” shouts Syko, his breathing becoming more frantic. There is no floor beneath him. No rocks, no cement, no pavement. No tiles. Nothing. It’s as if he is walking in air. Floating. Floating in this distant space. Not even sure if you can call it space. Syko begins to walk forward again, looking back as if expecting someone to sneak up on him, “How did I get here? I remember everything to the exact moment. But…this..i cannot remember. Where, how did I get here? Where did that small time frame of memory loss go? Did I pass out again? Am i…asleep? Yeah, that must be it. I must be asleep…” You wish you were asleep What was that?! Syko was sure he heard someone this time. There as no wind. No boots squeaking to make that noise. It was as clear as the sun. As clear as a diamond glint on snow. There’s no way, his mind could have played minds on him, or was there? “Who’s there?” cries Syko, listening back to his own echo, “is there someone out there?” Syko stops once again in his trail, expecting to hear an answer. But only the echo of his words is heard. Syko sighs and continues to walk forward. A faint hope. That’s all he had. A faint hope that he wasn’t alone. How right he was… Whoosh…keep walking away, just like you always do…Whoosh… Syko screams out, “Who the hell is out there? Whoever you are show your damn self! I do not have the fucking time to play these stupid games! You want to play games go see Inphino Blitz, I hear he enjoys games very much.” Silence. Whatever, whoever it was, seems gone now. Not that they were physically present to begin with. Syko looks around, and no one is there. In fact, there is no place to hide. Syko nods, as if saying “I told you so”. He continues to walk forward. As if there is any other way to go. ”Look at yourself. You think of nothing other than that childish tournament. Once again, you think of your own selfish personal glory than anything else!” That was it. “Whoever the fuck you are, you better show your damn self!” cries out Syko. But once again there was nobody there. There never was anybody there. Why do you worry about childish things. Who I am is of no concern to you. But you damn well who I am. Tell me Syko…why do you worry about mindless questions like these, when there obvious other issues you have to address to. Syko responds, “What the hell are you talking? Other issues?! There are no other issues! The only thing I care about right now is knowing who you are and getting the hell out of this…this…warped space.” There you fucking go again caring about only but yourself! There you fucking go again ignoring what really matters! Is it going to be this way with you all the damn time!? Syko in his rage takes off his leather jacket and throws it hard on the floor. He whips his head back, and his long red hair swings back in slow motion. He raises his left hand, and slowly removes strands of his red hair from his left eye. Revealing the scar that runs through his face. “You are no one to question me like that. Until you show yourself, you will be another piece of filth who can only talk big. If you are any kind of man or woman, you will show your damn self! And you better…not…fucking question me again…” Silence Whatever it was, it was gone now. But how long will it last? Syko continues to walk forward, his mind racing on what had just happened. Was it real? Was it a figment of his imagination? And how long will it be gone? He didn’t know. He didn’t even know if it was gone for good or not. He didn’t even know if he should have felt happy or sad. Now he truly was alone. One hour later… It seemed as if one hour had past. But Syko had no way of telling. His watch was no longer on his wrist. And even if it was, he had no way of telling if it would work. Probably not. Didn’t seem like anything that normal could happen here anywhere. Wherever here is… “Other issues? What other issues are there? What was he…it talking about? Was it my subconscious telling me I am forgetting something? Am I really forgetting something? I know I did everything today. And now, I have to get out of this place. There are no other issues. Mandy and Dawn, how can I help them…if I do not even know where I am? Blah this is too much for me. Too much for me to bear…” Syko turns around hoping to see anything. A shift in the clouds. A small insect flying by. Even someone sneaking up on him. But once again, all he saw was despair. Emptiness. Syko sighs and continues to walk forward… “I do not understand what it was trying to tell me. Was there something I should know? So much has gone on in my life. So much has happened these past few weeks, and it is not over yet. Now I face the biggest challenge of my career. Inphino, the Black Dragon Master. A man who is looking for the ultimate fight. A fight to fulfill him. And who can deny him? Who can argue with him? To test the limits of one’s own strength is what life is about. It’s the rule of Survival. Only the strong survive. And in this, only the strong will survive. What good is being the best if there is no competition? I will show Inphino the ultimate fight he so craves. As soon…as I get out of here…” Whoosh… The winds began to pick up again. They had been going on and off for the past hour. And it wasn’t until half an hour ago that Syko felt such a strong gust of wind. But it did not stop there. As if a Storm was brewing, the winds began to pick up. Faster. Harder. Syko clutched onto his jacket for fear that it will fly off his back. Syko looked up and saw the pink layer of clouds above him was beginning to shift furiously. It was as if someone was fast forwarding time, and he was stuck in it. “Ugh…what…is….going on here?” The winds were blowing furiously now, and Syko struggles to walk forward one step at a time. The only promises you keep are the ones that are for your own personal benefit, but when it comes to others. You just brush it off your shoulders. Syko gasped, and his eyes grew wide.
