The Killean Chronicles - A Fathers Decisions

10 January 2006

Date: 2006-01-10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Dead as dead can be The doctor tells me But I just can't believe him Ever the optimistic one I'm sure of your ability To become my perfect enemy Wake up and face me Don't play dead 'cause maybe Someday I will walk away and say You disappoint me Maybe you're better off this way ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Scenario I The holidays were almost over. It was a time for sharing, caring and giving. His friends had been there for him through the good times and the bad. For once, the holidays turned out better than ever this year. In years past it was aimless traveling and the uncomfortable feeling of not sleeping in the same bed night after night. This year, his dreams came true and Christmas was spent at his home. It was all because of one little treasure. Killean still couldn’t believe the holidays were almost over. After countless gifts and countless times saying thank you to his friends, the greatest holiday season ever was slowly coming to a close. With PRIME and GTT5 taking a holiday break, Killean took full advantage of his time off. He did however go and work out a few times with Vegas to stay on his game. But most of his time was spent with Desiree. Dizzy had given him a new hope in life. Beforehand his life was a directionless merry-go-round that kept spinning and had no real feeling. Killean had accomplished much in his PRIME tenure but even though he once stood at the very top of the mountain, it was never enough. Something was always missing. Companionship was once thought to be the problem after Kira had passed, but even women couldn’t fill the void that he had. But finally, through a twist of fate, the piece missing from the puzzle was dangerously left on his doorstep. He didn’t know why, but the gleeful little girl had power over him. Her presence and smile had him captivated and now they were inseparable, just like father and daughter were supposed to be. It wasn’t supposed to happen so soon, but it did and Killean would not change it for anything in the world. But Desiree was sleeping at home on New Year’s Eve. Vivian insisted that Killean and Vegas deserved a night out. While she offered to stay at the loft and care for Desiree, the two brothers went out to a club and celebrated the New Year. With Killean driving to the club earlier in the night, he was certainly not in any condition to get behind the wheel after downing multiple drinks, shooters and beers. In his eyes, the room was spinning and he saw at least two of everything, including the drinks placed in front of him which made him think he drank twice as much. Even though he didn’t, he seemed twice as drunk anyways. But even though he could barely keep on his feet, the keys to the car were still in his pocket. Vegas came walking back to the table, although not very fast and not in a straight line. He almost fell into a woman sitting at a table with a bunch of her friends. He quickly apologized as Killean attempted to glance at his watch and found the time to be either 1:45 or 9:08. Since it was now January first after the New Years party, he knew it was almost 2:00 AM. Vegas had begun to sweet talk the woman at the table and was taking his time. In doing so, this made Killean a little antsy. After finally waiting long enough, he rose from his seat and made his way over to Vegas. He could see the empty glasses on the table where the three ladies sat as he stumbled over to his friend. Vegas was just pocketing the brunette’s phone number when Killean attempted to steady himself by using the shoulder of his bigger friend. This almost sent them both crashing to the floor. "What d-d-did they say?" Killean was slurring and even stuttering his words. "Well there isn’t an available cab in town for a couple hours. But this guy outside with Operation Red Nose said he would be back in twenty minutes to drive us." Vegas was a little more sober than Killean, but was still intoxicated none the less. "Twenty minutes? I can’t wait that long. I n-n-eeeed to get out of heeere nowww." The slurring was getting worse, but Killean continued to remove the keys from his pocket and pat Vegas on the shoulder. "Let’s go." "Only if you’re sure man." Vegas said goodbye to the brunette whose name was Sandra and turned to leave with Killean. He made no visible attempt to stop his friend from taking control of a car under such intoxication but in his defense, he really wasn’t aware of his own actions at the moment either. It took almost twenty minutes to get out to the car anyways as Vegas, always the player, talked to about four more women on the way out of the bar. But once in the parking lot, he bent over behind a dumpster and let his inhibitions, along with anything he consumed, go. The constant heaves and choking was almost enough to make Killean sick to his own stomach, but he persisted and tried to help his friend to his feet. They both stumbled to the car and Killean helped Vegas into the passenger seat. Killean them made his way around the front, but bumped his leg on the bumper and went face first into the hood as his keys dropped to the concrete. Trying to regain control over his senses, he bent down to pick up his keys and stumbled over to his own door, opening it and literally dropping into his seat. Within seconds, he finally got the car started. But given his relative intoxication, he didn’t wait for the car to warm up and didn’t clear the windows of the little bit of snow that had fallen. Instead he began to drive off, keeping his composure best as he could and keeping the car straight as he could. Driving by the front door of the club, Vegas turns to see the guy who drove with Operation Red Nose standing there, looking at his watch. Remembering that he told the guy that he would accept the ride, Vegas realized that he was waiting for them. So as they drove by, he got the man’s attention by waving. The driver saw Vegas in the car driving by with Killean at the wheel. He remembered what Vegas had told him earlier when looking for a ride. "My friend is way to drunk to drive and I am too." The Operation Red Nose driver quickly flipped out his cell phone to dial 911. Meanwhile, a little further up the road, Killean was having problems staying in one lane while he was driving. Vegas really didn’t take notice and just kept bumping to the music coming from the Sirius satellite radio that Killean had picked up just before the holidays. He remembered to reach around and fasten his seat belt. He really wasn’t all that bad, he had been worse but he was definitely in no shape to drive. Come to think of it, neither was Killean. "Yo bro, you sure you’re ok to drive." Vegas questioned, turning down the stereo so he could hear a response. "Of c-…" Killean stopped as he hiccupped. He was now almost slamming into another car on the passenger side because he was swerving into the lane uncontrollably. "Of course I am." Vegas went back to listening to the radio. About forty yards ahead he saw a red light shining. Some cars were stopped in the lane beside them but Killean made no motion to slow his forward progress. Vegas left it alone for a couple seconds, but he noticed no difference in the speed they were traveling. Suddenly becoming very aware of what was happening; Vegas attempted to call out to his friend to stop. But he didn’t have enough time. Another car began to make its way through the intersection coming from the left. It was a rather large sedan and was not traveling very fast at all. Killean was going way to fast to stop and by the time he snapped out of his drunken daze, it was too late. Sirens had begun to sound and they were coming closer very quickly. People began to gather around the scene as the first ambulance began to arrive. Its lights illuminated what was left of the Dodge Charger that Killean so proudly owned. He had babied that car, although not to much recently with the arrival of Desiree. As paramedics began to filter out, an old couple emerged from the car that had the right of way at the light. They looked relatively unharmed and frightened at what had just transpired. Vegas began to come to when a paramedic shone a light in through the broken window. He quickly became aware of what was happening and felt a small trickle on his forehead. He reached up but found that his right arm had an extreme amount of pain running through it. He used his left arm and pushed a weight off of it to feel the skin of his brow. His hand came back down covered in crimson and he looked forward to the car that was driven by the old couple. It all came flooding back. The light, Killean not slowing down and the final moment before he couldn’t remember anything else. The weight returned back to his shoulder as more lights shone in from the driver’s side. A couple paramedics were talking to his left and he could barely turn to look before one of the men spoke up. "Killean Sirrajin is what his license said." One man said. "Wasn’t he a wrestler?" The other questioned. Vegas seemed a little confused that the doctor would use the past tense. He painfully turned to see what was going in beside him against the order of the medic tending to him. He found no one sitting beside him. Instead, outside the crushed door, he could see his friend lying on the ground on top of what looked to be a black blanket. Relief could not sweep over him though as the zipper came up much faster on the body bag that Killean had been lying on top of. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Leaning over you here Cold and catatonic I catch a brief reflection Of what you could and might have been It's your right and your ability To become my perfect enemy Wake up (Why can’t you?) Vegas suddenly became aware of himself and Killean that he quickly walked straight over to Killean and now helped his friend up. He picked the keys up off the ground and patted Killean on the back. "Look bud, you are not in any shape for this. I’ll drive." "You sh—sh—sure?" Killean questioned, trying to get the drunken words out. "Yeah I only had a few, I’m fine." He lied. He had more than a few but was not feeling too bad after losing his late night lunch beside the dumpster. He loaded Killean into the passenger seat and made his way unscathed to the driver’s seat where he started the car and let it warm up a little. Down the road, Killeans head was leaning against the window. He really wasn’t feeling too good and Vegas’ sudden braking was not making it feel any better. He briefly toyed with the option of asking Vegas to pull over but quickly dismissed at as he just wanted to get home. Vegas turned down the radio and looked over to Killean with a worried frown. Killean had gone pale and looked like he was about to be sick. But Vegas went back to the road and thought that Killean would definitely say something before getting sick in the car that he pampered and rubbed with the proverbial diaper. He turned the music back up and began to hum to the tune. He heard humming coming from Killean and thought that he really wasn’t that bad. But Vegas didn’t notice that Killean was beginning to slouch until it was too late. Killean had lost consciousness and flopped over. Vegas suddenly tended to his friend, taking his eyes off the road. He tried to wake him but it was to no avail. He finally turned back to the road to see a bunch of stopped cars whipping by his vision. He then noticed that the traffic signal was red and he was moving through an intersection at a rather fast speed. A car driven by an elder gentleman had made its way into the intersection and Vegas could not brake fast enough. As he began to stir, a medic was tending to the small cut on his forehead. The airbag was all deflated in front of him as he found his neck a little stiff. Vegas shrugged his shoulder to try and get some feeling back in them. He looked over to see an empty passenger seat and a large hole in the windshield. The airbag had not deployed and he suddenly feel into a state of panic. He rushed the medic away that was tending to him and he swiftly made his way out of the car, ignoring the blinding pain that came with it. He looked forward and saw a couple medics writing up some paperwork with a couple tarps on the ground. The old couple in the other car were giving a statement to an officer when he made his way around to see one large black tarp with a foot sticking out of it. It was Killeans shoe but certainly was not large enough to contain his whole body. He looked to the car they hit. A large blood mark was seen on the fender and hood of the vehicle, directly in front of the gaping hole in the windshield of their car. He looked beyond the car driven by the elder couple and noticed another tarp with another foot sticking out and an elbow visible from the third. Vegas suddenly became ill as he fell to the concrete in a sitting position. He overheard the officer who was taking the elder couple’s statement move over to his fellow officer and explain the situation. "Obviously he wasn’t wearing his seatbelt." The first officer said. "Right through the window and smack dab into the other car." The second said quietly, but loud enough for Vegas to hear. "He felt no pain upon impact." The first officer said, bending down to look under the biggest tarp at a mangled carcass. "Prolly dead once he hit the window." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Maybe you're better off this way (×3) You're better off this (×2) Maybe you're better off… Wake up (Why can't you?) Vegas had made his way over to a dumpster to spill his guts on the concrete. Killean went over to help him up but Killean stumbles backwards and lands flat on his behind. His jacket flops around a little but nothing falls out of the pockets. "We should get going. Desiree has probably been diffi-c-cult without me." He hiccupped and stumbled a little before Vegas made his way past him, not feeling too shit hot. As they approached the car, the passenger and rear doors were open and waiting for them. In the driver’s seat was a young man, about 23 years of age. He had already started the car and let it warm up before Killean and Vegas arrived. A few minutes later they were on the quick, and safe, track home when a car pulled up alongside them. Vegas was first to look out the window and he spotted the same girls that he was chatting it up with in the bar. He rolled down his window and began to yell some words that really didn’t make much sense. The girls turned to him and the one that gave Vegas a phone number was making the ‘call me’ sign to him. He nodded as Killean just put his head back against the head rest. The girls were watching the road but the driver seemed to be a tad intoxicated to say the least. She was swerving from lane to lane constantly and couldn’t keep her speed at a constant rate. In front of them, a light turned yellow and then red. The driver of their car came to a slow stop at the light. But the girls, who had fallen a little behind didn’t seem to slow as an elderly couple began to make their way through the intersection in front of them. Suddenly a squeal of tires was heard and the impact was deafening. Vegas, Killean and the young driver from Operation Red Nose looked on in horror as some fuel was leaking out of one of the cars. Vegas and Killean both opened their door and darted out as quickly as they could. Killean however stopped and yelled ahead to Vegas. "Stop!" A small fire broke out and a small explosion came from the car with the girls in it. It was enough to knock Vegas backwards. He landed on his back and then propped himself up to look at the car. Screams were heard from inside for a short time before they slowly died out. The elderly couple was able to get out and sirens were beginning to approach them from the distance. Medics arrived on the scene what seemed like only seconds after. They tended to the older couple and a couple firefighters came from their truck with a hose while another connected it to the fire hydrant near by. The water started flowing shortly thereafter but there was absolutely no movement from the car that the girls were in. Vegas rose from his seated position and made his way back to the car, rather confused and speechless. He took the phone number from his pocket and looked at it for quite sometime. Firefighters began to open the doors of the car. Vegas dropped the phone number to the concrete and stared at it. He shook his head and Killean came to put a hand on his shoulder. They both nodded and began to speak to the authorities. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Granted you have earned the name of PRIME killer. You have beaten Hoyt Williams, a man who was thought to be unstoppable. But that does not make you unstoppable in turn. It makes you ripe for the pickings. Killean hung his coat in the closet near the front door and walked over to the living area. Vivian had fallen asleep watching TV. Killean used the remote to switch the TV off and Vivian began to wake up. Killean helped her up and walked her to her room. He had sobered up quite a bit after watching the accident that took place. He glanced over to Desiree’s door which was partially open and noticed a little shadow being cast down onto the floor from the crib. He walked over and opened the door. As it creaked a little, he saw his little girl moving around a little. Approaching the crib, he was looking right into her big blue eyes. She looked back at him and broke a tiny smile on her rosy face. Killean picked her up from the crib and gave her a quick kiss on the cheek. He cradled her in his arms and she stopped moving around and became quite content in being with her father. It was as if she was waiting for him. He made his way into the living area, the lights from the downtown area of Winnipeg shining in through the windows, being reflected at some points by the small panes of frosted glass. He walked over to the table that stood behind the couch and switched on the water fountain that rested there. The water began to trickle down and made a dripping sound, creating a relaxed atmosphere in the room. With Vegas passed out on his bed and Vivian in bed now, Killean really didn’t feel like retiring alone. He made his way to the front of the couch and sat. Looking into the eyes of his little girl, he was glad that he made the choice of waiting for a ride to get home. "Who knows what could’ve happened? Being a father really changes your perspective. You really change my perspective." Killean whispered to his daughter. He lied down on his back, stretching the length of the couch. He had his daughter lying on his front but leaning to the back of the couch so she could not roll to the floor. He held her up for a second and blew on her small tummy, generating laughter from his little girl followed by a small saliva bubble that popped shortly after. He smiled and rested her back on his front. They lied there for a few minutes, neither making any sound at all. Just as Killean was beginning to doze off with his daughter in his arms, he whispered his final words of the night to her. "I love you Dizzy…" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Lyrics courtesy of http://www.sing365.com "Passive" by A Perfect Circle
