All I Want for Christmas is….

30 November 2005

Date: 2005-11-30 Note: This article has been taken from MSNBC. MIAMA, FL.- What are the best selling items for the children this year anyways? What will god ole Saint Nick bring along with him in his sleigh to put under your Christmas Tree this year. We went around the country and found the Top 10 Most Coveted Toys for Christmas 2005. 10. Matel SWAT TEAM Barbie This is a sure item on the list of those young girls who like to give punishment to their younger brothers for breaking the rules. No longer will Barbie be walking down the street without carrying her .45. 9. Kick My Butt ELMO Don't tickle him this time, just beat the ever living crap out of him. He doesn't laugh, he doesn't have fun, he just wails, cries, and pouts, even tossing an "Ouch!" in every kick or two. Great for those kids who take ritalin. 8. The Ambiguously Gay Duo Action Figures That's right. You don't have to wait till Saturday's to see these guys late night, they can be at your house 24-7. For those boys who spend too much time with the girls, coloring the driveway with chalk and doing eachother eachother's nails, this is a great gift. 7. Mini Bass Guitar So the mini guitar didn't go to well with your child last year? Give them something two strings easier for "your little dummy". The bass guitar will be perfect for those less-than-perfect children. 6. HATE Damien Ryan Action Figure Some may love him, some may "hate" him, but this Canadian will be selling millions this year on toy shelves. Get your kid what he really wants with "Mr. I Don't Give A F*ck!" 5. McDonald's Work Gear Know your child won't hack it in the future? Won't live up to becoming a lawyer, or a teacher, doctor, or even a school crossing guard? Well get them started early with this gift, or check out the next… 4. Toy Gas Pump A lot like the Work Gear, this is another great toy that will boost the self-esteem of your little buddy. You can even change the prices like the one million dollar a gallon gas stations we have here in America! 3. George W. Bush Mask Be just as smart as our wonderful president…. 2. "Color in the Lines" ELMO After you're done beating the hell out of him, let him teach your child how to color in the lines with his coloring book. 1. Life Size Saddam Hussein and Osama Bin Laden Dolls Number one on our list, don't let these guys push you around any longer. They'll fear you when you send them to your "Toy Gas Pump" and you "accidently" light a book of matches on fire.
