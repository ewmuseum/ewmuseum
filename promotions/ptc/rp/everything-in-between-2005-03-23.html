Everything in Between

23 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-23 October 25 1998, Non-Descript High School Gymnasium A well worn wrestling ring sits in the dingy gymnasium, surrounded by around eighty fold up chairs, approximately half are occupied by mainly males, most of them in their late teens. All eyes are on the ring which is empty bar one individual, a man in his thirties, wearing a suit which is several sizes too small. He grabs the microphone that is dangling above him. "Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to tonight's Main Event!" Some claps are heard over the crinkling of potato chip bags and slurping of drinks. The tinny sound of the gymnasium PA starts up, as "Counting the Beat" can be heard. "Making his way to the ring, weighing two hundred and ten pounds, hailing from Silicon Valley, he is...THE MATH MAN!" A clap or two is heard, as a masked man makes his way from the mens locker room. He holds an abacus in one hand, and he salutes the apathetic crowd with the other before rolling into the ring. The music stops before "Volcano" by the Presidents of the USA blares over the PA, and around three quarters of the crowd get to their feet. "And his opponent, weighing two hundred and thirty pounds, hailing from Hawaii, its your District Champion...Jimmy Caldera!!" From the locker room emerges a larger man in traditional blue wrestling tights. He runs to the ring, slapping hands with various fans, and rolling into the ring. Math Man attacks with a couple of boots to the back. DING DING The match is underway, as Caldera grabs Math Man by the leg and flips him to the ground, some people in the crowd cheer. Both men rise, and Math Man attacks with a punch, which is blocked by Caldera. He grabs Math Man around the waist and throws him to the ground with a sloppy belly to belly suplex. The ring side announcer calls the action. "What a move by Caldera! He picks Math Man up, and whips him to the corner!" Math Man holds his back, and Caldera charges, only to be met with a boot to the head by Math Man. Math Man takes advantage with a fishermans suplex, bridging for the pin. "That's what he calls the Googol-plex! It's over!" Not quite, as Caldera kicks out at one and a half. He picks Math Man up and disposes of him with a powerslam. The crowd cheers. "Eruption! Eruption! Its over!" ONE! TWO! THREE! And indeed it is, as Math Man is pinned in short order. Caldera celebrates to the crowd, as Math Man skulks away. Suddenly the crowd starts to boo, Math Man has returned, clutching his abacus like Gollum would clutch his "precious". Math man sneaks up, holding the abacus aloft, until Caldera turns, and punches Math Man in the chest, and disposes of him with another Eruption! Caldera stands over his downed foe, as we fade to black. March 20, 2005 Mal Somers stands in front of a WWA backdrop, flicking his wet hair back and patting Rookie (his beloved retired Championship Belt) which is draped over his shoulder. He is engaged in some sort of conversation with his buddy. "I must have improved since then!" "..." "Even just a little?" "..." "Whew!" Somers wipes his brow, and addresses the camera. "We all have to start somewhere, and yes, the truth is out, I began my career as the inauspicious Math Man in a local fed." He grins, rubbing his mottled red beard. "I'm sure Nick Worrell, Emilio Rage and the Latch Key Kid all had to start somewhere as well. I'm not that proud of it, but I'm proud of a lot that has happened since then. Winning the WWA World Title, making friends with Rookie here, forming the Extreme Corporation...they are all things that I will forever be very proud of. Challenging for the PTC Global Title is another occassion that I am very, very proud of. And you know, as embarrassed as I am about my beginnings, I wouldn't have gotten to where I am today without that first single step out of the high school gymnasium locker room. One day, my career will be over, and I will look back to where I began, and where it ended. And everything in between." He smirks. "Hopefully that is a long, long way away. Until then, I can only concentrate on what lies ahead. And that is me taking on the three top guys in Primetime Central for the ultimate prize. Is that something I will look back on in the years to come, and somehow tie in with my days as the master of the Googol-plex? Only time will tell. All I know is that every time I get into the ring I learn something new, I better myself, I improve. I know I am in for the fight of my life...but just the same, I am going to take it to Worrell, Rage, and the Latch Key Kid." Mal reaches into his pocket and pulls out a photograph, and holds it up to the camera. It is an oft imitated photo, Mal can be seen visiting the Statue of Liberty, which stands proudly in the background. Mal is holding his hand out, and it looks like Mal is holding the statue in the palm of his hand. "Sometimes, to grab a hold of something so big, something that appears to be out of reach, you just have to stand back. Take a step back and analyse everything, from start to finish... (fade to black) ...and everything in between."
