Character - Darth von Doom (FA) Sahara Desert I wander the burning desert, have no clue what my name is or who I am. Look down at my hands and see black gloves. Body bulky, hard to maneuver in the endless desert sands. For some reason I'm wearing a cape. Move my hands up to my face. There is a mask covering my face. Wonder why that’s there. Wonder how I got here. Had to have gotten here somehow. Desert seems more endless now. Sand in all directions coupled with the blazing sun overhead. Looking out to try and see any kind of life, instead I see none. Sweating profusely, I wander aimlessly until I fall onto the sand. Unable to move anymore, I lay down, closing my eyes until everything fades into blackness. * * * A few hours later… The body lays in the sand amidst the endless dunes of the Sahara until three travelers come across it. The mysterious men look at the body first before turning to one another, a bit bewildered by what they have found. “Who is it?” one of them asks, turning to the man next to him. “Is that who I think it is?” The man next to him looks a bit concerned. “Do you think they’ve found us?” he asks the others. “Do you think they found us, after all this time?” The third man is examining the man closely and shakes his head. “No,” he responds. “This is different. It doesn’t feel like an attack. I mean, where are all the soldiers and creatures they have at their disposal? It’s just him.” “But he is their leader,” the first man says. “We should leave him.” The third man continues to ponder what to do. An eerie silence fills the air as the wind gusts pick up. They look around as sand starts whirling around them. All three men cover their faces and turn to each other. “Well, should we just leave him?” the second man asks. The third man shakes his head and replies, “No. He goes with us.” “But what if it’s a trap?” the second man asks, a bit concerned. “Then so be it,” the third man responds. The three men lean over and pick up the large, hulking man. * * * AWAKE! My eyes burn as I awaken and see that I’m inside a cave somewhere. Smells like dirt. I can hear the wind of the sandstorm blowing outside. Rise up and look around to see a bunch of strange looking guys looking at me. They all appear somewhat familiar to me. Upon closer inspection, some of them don’t appear to be human. “You alive there, buddy?” one of them asks me. Nodding my head, I look at them. “Yes, I feel fine,” I say before standing up and shaking out the cobwebs, turning to them after I do so. “Who are you guys, anyway?” “We’re the unwanted, the exiled members of the Circus of DOOM~! and the Church of the Unholy,” he says with a smile. “We never expected in a million years to find you here, Darth.” “Who?” I respond, a little confused. “You know my name?” They all look at each other, a bit concerned for some reason which I pick up on very easily. They know who I am but aren’t willing to tell me. That’s what it feels like. “Who am I?” I ask once again but they all just look concerned. Again no one replies and it’s starting to get a bit troubling. All I want to know is my name, but no one will tell me. “Just tell me my name!” I exclaim in a frustrated tone. The room shakes, most likely from the echo. They all look scared at this point. The man who first talked to me then gives me a look and sighs. “Your name is Darth von Doom,” the man says. That name does sound somewhat familiar. But before I can say anything else, the man continues. “You were a member of both the Church of the Unholy and the Circus. Before that you were a regular human who go severely wounded and burned in a battle before the leader of the Circus saved you and made your cybernetic replacement parts. As a member of the upper leadership of both groups, you committed acts of homicide and genocide the likes of which haven’t been seen on the world stage in ages. After your time machine experiment almost destroyed the world, you disappeared into space chasing after three pirates only to return. You then were manipulated by the Church of the Unholy before leaving that organization and disappearing until we found you just now.” That’s a real mind blower if there ever was one. Finding out that you are a mass murderer. “But I don’t remember any of that,” I reply, more startled by the revelations than anything. “I don’t remember anything at all. Other than my name, I don’t know anything besides what you have just told me.” “We can help you with that,” a man in the back of the cave says in a drool tone. I look a him and he appears to be a pirate. There is a clown and some sort of monstrosity with them. “How can you help?” I ask. “Because we’re the three pirates you chased to the end of the universe,” the man replies as all three of them give me strange looks. “We can help you remember.” * * * I let them put me to sleep with some sort of serum that the one man says will bring my memories back. After injection, I lay down on the ground. At first I feel nothing but then everything becomes a blur. World goes fuzzy… I then enter the world of dreams and start to remember. Wake up on a couch. I was at the party a long time ago and she walked into the room. Noticing her from across the room, I followed her with my eyes. Her hair swayed as she walked. Her eyes shined like diamonds in the sunlight. The aqua colored dress she wore pulled me in like a sweet, scented flower. She goes over to get a drink. I watch her pick up a glass and pour some vodka into it. As she takes a sip, I watch her lips sip the vodka from the glass. She swallows it and then turns in my direction. Our eyes lock and it was like magic. The hairs on the back of my neck stand up and my hands tingle. I know that she’s the one… Suddenly everything around me fast forwards through my relationship with the woman. Then I see myself fighting some pirates and two other guys I don’t know. After this I see myself being burned after the battle where I lost three of my limbs. Then I see my transformation into the cyborg monstrosity that is Darth von Doom soon after that. This is when I started getting scared. The images I see after that are of me slaughtering many, many people. Some are humans, others are weird creatures and humanoids like my new buddies. Then I see myself in battle against creatures in space, flying from planet to planet. Next comes a chase through space and the universe after the three Pirates. See a dark man there too. He is telling me to continue after them. Upon reaching the end of the universe, I somehow end up back here on Earth and awaken from this dream to see the entire group staring at me. I feel a bit shell shocked by what I have seen. It horrifies me that I was capable of such things that I saw. The group seems a bit intrigued and yet still frightened of me for some reason. “What did you see?” the man asks me. I turn to him and reply, “Things you could not even imagine.” * * * The next morning… After a long day of thinking and discussing, I have decided to join their ranks before I went to bed last night. Upon waking up from my slumber as the sun beams down from overhead like a diamond shining in the sky, there is a loud rumble. We all awaken at the same time and look out from the cave that we now inhabit. Instead of looking out, I decide to look at the rest of the team. Wolfen, the werewolf who escaped from the death squads sent after him by my former allies. The Killer Pirates, three strange men who traveled through space to the end of the universe before coming back here and joining the Somali pirates before escaping from Kenyan authorities. Blue Jay, a blue clad assassin that used to work for various organizations. Nebula, a female that was experimented on by the Church only to escape with help from Blue Jay and the Pirates. Ash, who apparently was captured by the United States government only to be rescued by Bishop. Bishop, the leader, is a mysterious man who kind of found all of us and formed up the group. He is very wise and intelligent. After looking at everyone, I then turn back to the outside of the cave. Something is calling me outside. Looking out of the cave, I see the image of a dark man. The same man that has haunted me ever since I was in space. Running outside to see him, he suddenly disappears. However something very large leaps down into the sand. Sand flies everywhere and the ground shakes beneath me. Looking up I see a giant robot standing above me! It’s shadow covers everything, blocking even the sun. The giant monstrosity looks down at me and lets out some sort of an enormous computerized roar before looking down at me and pointing it’s gun a me! Suddenly a force comes over me. I’m glowing red and now a sort of red bubble surrounds me. I bend over, trying to contain it but finally have to unleash the power! “AAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!” The red bubble is unleashed and causes a giant red explosion that destroys the robot! The robot vaporizes upon impact. Nothing is left. I fall to my knees on the sand and try to regain my composure. The rest of the group comes out from the cave to see the results of what happened. Bishop walks over to me and puts his hand on my shoulder. “They’ll know we’re here after that,” he says in a solemn tone. “We’ll have to move out, Darth.” Looking up at him, I reply, “Bring it on.” * * * Somewhere in North America… A blip is shown repeating on a radar screen. Someone looks down at the screen and then motions for someone else to come and look at what it is showing. The person then looks up at the second person and seems to be a thinking something. “What do we do now?” the first person asks. This person clearly has a man’s voice. The second man smiles and replies, “Now we know where they are. We have to make a deal with the one we were talking about before. He can help us destroy Darth.” “Who do you mean?” “The one they call Anarchy.” (To be continued…)
