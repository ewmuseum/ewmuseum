From Russia With Disdain - Part II

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 A VERY WELL-HIDDEN TRAINING FACILITY A FEW DAYS LATER EARLY IN DA MO'NIN' The mystery and intrigue surrounding Jimmy Bong's pursuit of the dastardly Johannes Frederick Sebastian Billy-Bob Garfeld were becoming more twisted than a phallus caught in an egg beater. Or perhaps something less droll, yet equivalent in meaning. Anyway. As if it wasn't bad enough for him to discover that he had spent the last couple of weeks on a fruitless wild goose chase, now the American government was vehemently excluding OHMCS from the mission to save the world. Furthermore, with the exception of Bong himself, OHMCS operations had been expelled from Washington, D.C. Bong was their only hope of continuing their own pursuit of Garfeld, and the only way he would be allowed to remain in Washington is if he managed to continue his progression through GTT4 (a seemingly outlandish technicality, but effective all the same). Even more intriguing was N's recent contact with the Russian government. The Russians, it seemed, had a hunch about how Garfeld was planning to dominate the world -- and they were the last chance OHMCS had of discovering exactly what that plan was. After all, if Bong couldn't discover that information, then the global populace would be doomed (DOOMED I SAY!) to enslavement, or perhaps destruction. The truth was that N didn't feel at all comfortable with sitting back and letting the CIA capture Garfeld -- assuming they had the capacity to do so. If the Americans were keeping something hidden from the British, then N wanted to know what it was. They had to find Garfeld before the Americans did. If not, then the secret might be forever kept from them, and that was unacceptable. It was a perplexing puzzle, indeed. Soon after his very eventful meeting with N, Bong received word that the Russians were sending their own operative to the Washington area, in order to assist OHMCS with their investigation. If the Americans wouldn't cooperate, then the British and the Russians were just going to have to rely on each other's help instead. Bong was faced with several issues. There were many questions that needed to be answered, and those answers weren't going to come easily. But the most pressing issue at hand was Jimmy Bong's utter lack of wrestling ability. Even though he was on a recent string of impossibly good luck, he needed to get good. Sadly however, there was no way he was going to get good. After all, Bong was an idiot. But at least, perhaps, he could get halfway decent. Bong stood at the edge of a wrestling ring, within the dank confines of a run-down gymnasium. The building looked as if it had been abandoned for quite some time: The walls were grossly unkempt, blanketed with mildew and cobwebs. Plumes of dust kicked up from the floor with each step Bong took. A few lights hanging from the ceiling flickered incessantly, but it was extremely fortunate there was any electricity in the building at all. It was a terrible place. Probably the last place anybody would ever want to be. That's what made it the perfect place to keep hidden from the CIA -- nobody in their right mind would want to be there, and Jimmy Bong was never in his right mind. In fact, he didn't really have much of a mind at all. Majorly Shafted was there as well, dressed in a white t-shirt and grey sweatpants (Bong, of course, was still dressed in his tuxedo). He was going to help the infamous Double-Oh-Nothing with his ring skills. As it turned out, Shafted had been something of a combat expert during his OHMCS tenure (that is, in the "pre-genetically engineered to be a human shield" days). It wasn't exactly wrestling, but at least Shafted could lend Bong some sort of fighting style, albeit crude. Bong coughed as he inhaled a cloud of dust. "Good heavens!" he hacked, waving his hand in front of his face. "This place is an absolute disaster area! I wish U could have invented a giant Swiffer, or perhaps a tanker full of Endust, so we could tidy up this mess. It reminds me of the time I made a large-scale model of the Sahara in my mother's living room, only with a lot less yelling, and not being beaten with a sack of potatoes." "We're not here for spring cleaning Bong, we're here so you can learn how to fight properly!" said Shafted sternly. "Well, I should certainly hope not," Bong replied. "After all, it would be rather silly of us to be doing spring cleaning in October." "That's not quite what I meant," mumbled Shafted. "Anyway, let's start with..." "Although," Bong interrupted, "I suppose if we were in the southern hemisphere, then it would work. After all, October is springtime in the southern hemisphere..." "BONG!" Shafted yelled. "Enough with the damn spring cleaning already! Now get in the ring and we'll begin!" "Oh, we're going to be mopping and sweeping the ring, then?" asked Bong. Shafted groaned, and covered his face with his hands, shaking his head. "Are you even paying attention to anything I say?" he asked. "I'm terribly sorry, Shafted," Bong said apologetically. "It's just that my train of thought has sailed for Rio now." "Trains don't sail, Bong," said Shafted. "What?" asked Bong. "Well that's rather silly. If they could, they'd be able to travel much faster through water." "Argh!" Shafted wailed. "Just please, get into the ring, will you?" "Oh, alright then!" said Bong cheerfully, climbing up onto the apron. He stopped there, and stared thoughtfully at the ropes. "Hm, now this is the part I always have trouble with. Let's see..." Bong pressed down on the bottom rope with his right foot, and pulled up on the second rope with his hands. "Like this?" he asked himself, stepping with his left leg between the ropes. "Hm, that feels like an awkward position. Perhaps if I tried going between the top two ropes!" Shafted watched in astonishment as Bong made a complete ordeal over something as simple as stepping through the ropes. Just how did this buffoon manage to defeat the likes of Montgomery and Morbid? And how in the world could he defeat someone like Gordo to keep moving in the tournament? Why, the fate of the world may very well rest on Jimmy Bong's shoulders... "Ow!" yelped Bong, as he lost his grip on the top rope and it snapped back down into position. "That rope nearly took out a piece of my shoulder!" "Good lord," Shafted mumbled. At that moment, the front door of the gymnasium swung open, sending a billow of dust several feet upward into the air. Shafted turned around to see who was intruding upon the training session. Two vague figures became visible through the cloud, though still greatly obscured. "Who's there!?" demanded Shafted. There was no reply. "I say, who's there!? Make yourselves known!" he shouted again. Once more, no reply came. The two figures, whoever they were, stood in silence as the dust began to settle. Shafted began to see their faces more clearly, and they were two people he certainly did not recognize. He sensed trouble afoot. Quickly, he dropped down to the floor, reached under the ring apron, and pulled out a pistol. A split-second later, he was back on his feet, and taking aim at the intruders. "Alright, I'm going to warn you only once!" he shouted. "Put your hands in the air, and walk towards me very slowly! Do it now or I'll shoot!" They immediately thrust their hands above their heads, and carefully stepped towards Majorly Shafted. He could see them clearly now as they came closer: a short, pudgy man with a thick mustache and a receding hairline was one of them. The other was a taller, devlishly beautiful young woman with brown hair flowing down to her waist. She was wearing a black leather jumpsuit, which had lost much of its luster thanks to the dusty state of the gymnasium. "Good lord, comrade, do not shoot!" barked the man, with a decidedly Russian accent. "We are representatives sent from de Russian government to asseest you!" "Bloody hell," said Shafted, putting his gun away. "Why didn't you say so in the first place?" "We were holding our breath because of all de dust!" the Russian man exclaimed. "Eet causes terrible sinus congestion, da? Perhaps a leetle... how do you say... spreeng cleaning?" For a brief moment, Shafted reconsidered his decision not to shoot the man. "Argh... yes, that's right," he replied through his teeth. Anyway, you're from the Russian government? Excellent. I certainly hope you can shed some light on the Garfeld situation, since the Americans are being dreadfully nasty to the both of us." "Indeed," the man replied. "Oh, I beg your pardon! I forgot de proper eentroductions! My name eez Dmitri Kolehnkov, senior Russian intelligence officer. And thees woman eez Iva Gunnonmi, one of our best field operatives. Eet eez a pleasure to make your acquaintance, Mr. Bong." "Bong? Jimmy Bong?" asked Shafted, surprised. "Oh no, I'm not Bong," he chuckled, turning around. "This is..." He paused in mid-sentence. Bong, who was trying to figure out how to properly get into the ring, had succeeded in tangling himself upside-down in the ropes. Shafted sighed. "This is Agent Bong." "Hello!" said Bong with a cheery smile, waving to Iva and Kolehnkov. "Ah... pleasure to meet you, comrade Bong," said Kolehnkov, raising an eyebrow slightly. "Are you alright?" "Eh? Oh, this," he said, referring to his entanglement. "I'm quite alright, just a bit stuck is all. I say Shafted, could you be a chum and get me loose from here?" "Certainly Bong," Shafted mumbled. He climbed onto the apron and -- after putting the thought of strangling Bong to death on the ropes out of his mind -- helped the hapless OHMCS agent out of his predicament. "Much obliged," said Bong, straighening his tie and tuxedo jacket. Iva crossed her arms and rolled her eyes, clicking her tongue a few times. Bong's eyes lit up. "Egad!" he exclaimed. "I recognize that tongue clicking! That's a form of African Bushman Swahili, or whatever the bloody hell it's called!" "Very observant, Mr. Bong," said Kolehnkov with an impressed smile. "Eet eez a strange case, indeed... Iva speaks only dat language, and can only communicate through cleecking her tongue." "You're joking!" said Bong with astonishment. "Da, eet eez true," Kolehnkov continued. "On an assignment een South Africa, she had a terrible accident. She suffered some brain damage, and de... well, I do not know de word for eet... but de part of her brain responsible for language was damaged, and she lost all capability of speaking Russian, English, and every other language she had learned... except for de Swahili!" "Amazing!" Bong exclaimed, looking wide-eyed at Iva. "I'm surrounded by freaks!" Shafted whimpered under his breath, walking away from the conversation. "This is an excellent opportunity for me," Bong continued. "As it so happens, I took four years of Bushman Swahili in high school! Let's see now..." Bong looked at Iva, who was staring curiously back at Bong. He clicked his tongue a few times. Iva's jaw dropped with shock. "Wait a minute, did I say 'hello," or...?" Bong asked himself. After a moment, Iva's shock turned to anger, as she pulled out her pistol and aimed it at Bong. "Horrors!" Bong gasped. "Don't shoot, Iva! Whatever I said, I didn't mean it! Yet!" Bong looked around quickly, and ran towards Shafted. Iva's aim followed Bong, as he sprinted and leaped through the air towards his human shield. "What the hell are you doing, Bong!?" Shafted exclaimed. Everything seemed to happen in slow motion. Bong's arms were outstretched, and his body was parallel to the ground. "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!" he yelled. Just as Bong sailed past Shafted, a shot rang out from Iva's gun! BANG! Bong was right in the path of the bullet! However, he was diving behind Shafted, who took the shot. Bong landed safely on the ground, as Shafted fell backwards. "OW!" yelled Shafted. "Bloody hell, that stings!" "Sorry about that, Shafted," said Bong, getting back on his feet and dusting himself off. He turned back to look at Iva, who was taking aim at him once more. Bong froze like a deer in headlights, but Kolehnkov stepped in to stop Iva. "Iva, NO!" he exclaimed. "Whatever he said was an accident! He deedn't mean to insult you! He's de man we're supposed to help to stop Garfeld from taking over de world!" Iva frowned at Bong, but lowered her gun. Bong breathed a sigh of relief. "Phew, that was close," he said. "Good thing nobody got hurt!" "Speak for yourself, you thundering dolt!" groaned Shafted, who was getting up now, dazed but undaunted. "So, Kolehnkov," said Bong. "I presume you may know what Garfeld is up to? The Americans certainly aren't willing to tell us." "Indeed," Kolehnkov replied. "We aren't certain, mind you, but we have a solid idea. But thees eez not de place to talk right now. We'll need to secure a location where we can discuss matters. De sooner de better, as I fear we are running out of time. What I can tell you, though, is that Garfeld is plotting to utilize a terrible weapon left over from de Cold War." Bong frowned, as his mouth dropped. "A weapon?" "Da, very powerful weapon," nodded Kolehnkov solemnly. "Dat eez all I can say right now." "Right," said Bong. "Well, in the meantime, I suppose I should be learning how to fight. Are you ready to get going, Shafted? I think we've fooled around quite long enough." "I hate you!" growled Shafted angrily. "Excellent! Let's get to it, then!" Bong climbed onto the apron to get into the ring. Shafted let out a long sigh, then turned once again to Iva and Kolehnkov. "You'll let us know when you've secured a meeting place?" he asked. "Da," said Kolehnkov. "We weel be een contact. Come, Iva, let us go." Iva clicked her tongue, and nodded. The two Russian agents headed out the door, as Shafted turned back to the ring. "Right then, Bong, let's get started with... oh, dear lord, not again!" Bong was tangled upside down in the ropes again. "Erm, could you please get me out of this, Shafted?" he asked sheepishly. Questions, mystery, and intrigue all faced Jimmy Bong. What was this weapon that Kolehnkov spoke of? Was it powerful enough to allow Garfeld to realize his goal of world domination? And if it was so powerful, why were the Americans doing everything they could to prevent the British and the Russians from helping them? The answers, hopefully, would come quickly... ...but first, Bong needed to figure out how the hell to get into that ring.
