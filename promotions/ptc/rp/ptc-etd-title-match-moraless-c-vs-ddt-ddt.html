ETD Title Match (Moraless (c) vs DDT)

28 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
28 June 2004 A computer screen As I sit in front of the computer, I find it difficult to decide what it is I want to say. With all that I have to face in the coming days, Moraless for the PTC Extreme Title, as well as Stable Wars, my mind is both blank and unbearably full. But I guess those are things I’m not given the opportunity to worry about. Losing control of my mind is. I can’t understand any of it. No...I understand part of it. I understand him. He is me...or was...or wants to be. Ok, maybe I don’t understand. I do know that he’s evil...the Darkness that drew me to The Ripper and the AoD. He’s always been there, he knows too much not to. But why now? All these years, all this time, he’s not tried to do, that which he tries now. The boy. Very quiet, very shy. The woman. My mother? But no... Then recently, a trio who’ve yet to show more then what they seem to be... A junkie.. An elderly man... And another man...very shy...sickly almost. All these people...not really people...but I see them. I hear them. I can’t ignore them either. Not since I learned he could hurt them. I don’t know why, but that bothered me. Who am I kidding...I’m hurting them. No control in the ring? No control out of it? Am I even in control now? The doctor is in... One week ago... When DDT went to the AoD lawyer, he didn’t know what to expect. He’s learned that if anything, his visit would be interesting. Sirius’ “friend” was very much like Sirius, without the crotchetiness. Doctor Thomas entered the Domicile of Darkness, and quite possibly was the most ordinary and sane person to ever walk it’s halls, yet unlike most others, he was seemingly unaffected, or just uninterested by his very strange surroundings. He was interested in who he was visiting. He asked to be left alone with DDT. He and one Damien D. Teri, would talk, and hopefully understand what was going on. DDT told him, all there was to tell. He went back in time. He told him about his childhood tragedy, and the tortured life that followed. He talked about how he came to this XUW, and the eventuality of joining the Army of Darkness. He spent most of his time on the good days of not being so good. The death of The Ripper was met with pause, but not as much as the attempt to take his own life. DDT tried as much as he could to recall the first time he felt within him the conflict, when one became two. He continued on, until two became too many to follow. DDT talked for hours. Then, when DDT had nothing more to say, the doctor rose, looked at DDT and said.... Doctor : Ok. He shook the hand of DDT, and left the room. DDT sat, puzzled. DDT : That’s all? OK?! More confused then ever, DDT stared blankly at the walls around him for hours, before slowly leaving the room himself... Friday, June 25th... With the questions mounting, DDT finds the tasks at hand even more unbearable. He’s thought the unthinkable...bowing out of his match with Moraless. The PTC title had become somewhat unimportant in his mind...some parts anyway. Even as he looks upon the Intercontinental Title he’s been fairly lucky to keep, the thought of another item that places a target upon him, is fairly unpleasant. These feelings bring out the worst in him...... I grow tired of DDT feeling sorry for himself. I don’t hold you here. DDT faces himself. More specifically, his “evil” or “dark” self. No...this much is true. For if DDT did have such power...DDT would also be able to let go. Isn’t that what you’ve wanted all these months DDT...to let go? To no longer feel the inner turmoil? To end the conflict within? I ended it once... You did no such thing. Can you remember DDT? DDT was about to face Moraless...a strange opponent...one born out of respect. He came to DDT to fight for the sheer pleasure of fighting. Does DDT remember why he wanted to fight Moraless? DDT is silent. DDT wanted to a return. A return to something DDT once did for fun. DDT fought in that ring. DDT did battle with others who also, found enjoyment in the pain they gave, and the pain they received. It wasn’t a match born of hate, or jealousy, or anger. It wasn’t about blood feuds, it was about blood. Blood that stained the canvas. Not for anyone but DDT. DDT defeated Moraless after that...after he thought he defeated me. And what happened after DDT defeated Moraless? How did DDT uphold his want for a “return to what he loves”? Nothing. What “Extreme” of “Hardcore” events can DDT call his own? DDT looks at the XUW Intercontinental Title. Bah...I did that. DDT closes his eyes. Now DDT again has not only Moraless to face again, but Stable Wars. Remember DDT? Remember losing last year so horribly? This year may be no different...and worse...it may happen to a group from the XUDF...developmental prospects. How would that be DDT? How “Extreme” would DDT feel then? DDT stands and walks from the room The vision stays, but his laughter echoes in DDT’s mind. You can’t run from me DDT. You can’t run from your destiny.... hours later... DDT was beginning to feel beaten. He’d taken his lumps in the ring as of late, but mind over matter was definitely occurring. His evil has had the ability to take control whenever he chose, to the point DDT didn’t even fight it anymore. The spells were more frequent, longer then ever before. He looks over at his friend Raven X, as he paces the room. Ray’s own demons have haunted him as well, but DDT has been little help. Ray too has noticed something of his partner, but like DDT has his own worries... Raven : It’s him...I fucking know it! DDT : Calm down Ray...this will get us nowhere. Raven : You don’t understand D... DDT : Don’t I? In a moment, the friends look at one another. They, for a moment, understand that they suffer in the same hell, only alone. Raven : Something’s been wrong, hasn’t it? DDT : Isn’t something always wrong? Raven sits across from DDT Raven : He’s been to see you too...hasn’t he? DDT stares at Raven, as a voice sounds in his head. What does he think he knows? DDT : Who? Raven : Rigor...he’s done the same to you? He knows nothing.... By reflex, DDT responds to himself. DDT : What do YOU know? Raven is puzzled by the tone of the question. Raven : Rigor...he’s the one who kidnapped me...who’s fucking with my head. He’s done it to you too, right? Answer.... DDT : No...no. Raven looks on puzzled. Raven : You believe me right? DDT : I thought you said it was a dream of some sort? Raven : Yes...no....I... Raven stands and begins to pace again. Raven : I don’t know what to think anymore. DDT laughs DDT : Now that...I understand. Raven chuckles at this as well. Raven : So...still going on with the PTC thing? DDT : Contracts and such...you know Sirius... Raven : Aye... A silence...for a moment... DDT : I’m not sure what to think of this Moraless. He’s changed since we faced last. Raven : For the better? DDT : He feels such, and besides, he does hold the PTC Extreme Title. Raven : Yes he does. DDT : Yeah.. Both stop for a second...thinking to themselves... Raven : How important is this title? DDT : Well... DDT stops for a second. Raven looks at him. He sees DDT face change. The Hardcore Icon’s tired eyes suddenly burn. DDT looks across the table at Raven with a smirk... DDT : It’s not the title that means something, as beating Moraless does. When we first met those months ago, it was for respect he said. But what happened when I beat him? Did he show the respect to me I deserved? No...he went along with his life as if the match never happened. Then, when it came for him to face Sean McClancy for the title, where was the respect? I beat him, yet he stood ahead of me. But there was no mention of my defeating him. No...he only mentioned why he so deserved this shot...why this chance was his to have by right. But his feelings of doubt...they betrayed him! They proved he felt a conflict with himself. Even still...he ignored it all. DDT shakes his head. DDT : Tsk...tsk...tsk John Edwards. Raven : So...you’re doing this to teach him a lesson? DDT : No. I’m doing this, because, as he once said, it’s destiny. "Destiny is everywhere, you cannot help but face it head on, because that’s destiny, it’s unavoidable." My destiny from the time I became involved in this ETD was to prove why I am who I am. Why DDT is a name feared. Why DDT is a name synonymous with pain. A name synonymous with hardcore. A name that was to be known as Extreme. And this match is only the beginning Raven...this match is but an appetizer to the greater victories to be had...Stable Wars...The World Title...and...and... DDT blinks...and the fire fades... Raven : And what? DDT : Huh? Sirius walks in. Sirius : Nice to see you both preparing so diligently for Stable Wars? Raven : Pfft.. Raven shoots Sirius a look of disdain as he leaves the room. Sirius : What’s with him? DDT : I’ve no idea. Which isn’t far from the truth... Sirius : Doctor Thomas called...he’s prepared to see you again. DDT : Prepared? Sirius : He needed to understand what he needed to do before starting any therapy as it may be. DDT : When? Sirius : This week. DDT : Is that wise? Just before Stable Wars? Sirius : I think it will be the best thing...not only for you...but for the greater good of the AoD. DDT : Well...alright... DDT leaves the room, more confused then before. He doesn’t remember what Raven wanted the answer to...he’s forgotten the last few minutes prior to Sirius’ entrance. What he told Raven doesn’t seem important right now, as it seems he’ll be getting something soon that he’s craved for so long....answers. Still in the room, Sirius smiles at that very idea. ---- 30 June 2004 Sunday, June 27th DDT sits at his laptop. He’s taken to Sam’s idea of writing in a journal to clear his head, even if it just clears space for more insanity.... Crazy. That’s what the next few days will be. That’s what the entire Army of Darkness has become. Ok...we already were...but still. The disappearance of Rigor Mortis was met without much worry from Sirius, which in itself was worrisome. Rigor had been Sirius’ pet project, his new found evil monster we were all sure he was grooming to be what The Ripper had been. Yet here was Raven X, the one who was against Rigor’s joining of the AoD from the very moment he suspected Rigor to be his tormentor in the flesh, feeling uneasy about this disappearance. He’s shown no concern for Rigor, but I do know he’s thinking what I am...something is very wrong. Being afraid...not something I’m used to...despite feeling it for so long now. The voices in my head are constant now. I can usually go about my day without acknowledging them, as they seem to be more concerned with one another then me. Only HE seems to talk to me. When he does, everything stops.... . . . . . . . . . The doctor will give me my answers...I hope. Monday, June 29th DDT enters the same room he met the doctor in as last time. He sits down and waits for Doctor Thomas. He looks at the clock on the wall...9:30am. “He’s late” DDT thinks to himself. DDT’s not one to nitpick over time, so he waits patiently. An hour passes. No Dr. Thomas. DDT stares at nothing in particular, thinking to himself about his upcoming matches. For the first time in a long time, his thinking on them have been uninterrupted. The Extreme Title of PTC was something he told himself he didn’t care about, but it wasn’t completely true. DDT’s entire carrer in the ring had been defined by one word...Hardcore. PTC calls it Extreme, but it’s the same thing. He wasn’t lying to himself when he said joining the ETD was a help to return to something he loved. He loved the pain. He enjoyed every drop of blood that poured from his head, and every drop of his opponents blood on his hands. It’s why after the Extreme Rumble, he faced Moraless. Moraless was one of the few in that match he had little to think poorly of. He was very similar to DDT. Hell...if he was in XUW, he and DDT would be much as Raven X and DDT are, heated rivals who grew into great friends. He’s watched Moraless since he defeated him those months ago. He knew Moraless would get a title shot before him, and secretly wished he’d win, if only to make him the champion he’d face. Despite some inner turmoil, he did just that. Now DDT looks at this, and realizes that you should be careful what you wish for. DDT knows the inner turmoil he’s experiencing far surpasses that of his opponent. While he battles with being Moraless over John Edwards, he knows that he’s not kept awake at night by conversations with the other him. 10:45...he’s very late. It’s also the first time in over an hour DDT’s has thought of his evil half, or any of the “people” who’ve haunted DDT’s every waking moment. Hell, it’s the fist time they’ve left him alone this long, which of course, means that they’re due to show up any minute now... Any minute... 11:00 12:00 DDT has grown tired of waiting. It’s now come to a point the missing doctor is no longer his concern. He’s franticly looking around for some sign that they’re here. The boy...the woman...even the annoying junkie. He’d even settle to be tormented by the dark side of him he’d often wish would go away. Then, like a brick to the head, the realization overtakes him. He’s grown so used to them...their presence has become like the air he breathes. At this revelation, a whispered laughter echoes in his head. DDT : So that’s your game. You’ve made me dependent on them...on you. I can’t function without any of you, can I? The voice echoes as if it’s far away, despite being so very close. DDT asks me questions I cannot answer. DDT : Why can’t you answer them? DDT knows.... But DDT doesn’t know. He doesn’t know why he suddenly pains for the company of fictional characters in his mind. 1:00 pm. DDT stands to leave the room, but as he reaches the door, he finds it locked. DDT : What the hell? Who’s doing this. I am. The voice rings out from nowhere. This voice however, is very real, and not a figment of DDT’s imagination. DDT : Doctor Thomas? Dr.T : Yes Damien...it’s Dr. Thomas. DDT : Don’t call me Damien... Dr.T : Why? That’s your name. DDT : I’m not the person. Dr.T : But you are Damien, you’re very much that person. DDT plops down on the chair again. DDT : Ok...so why are you watching me from somewhere else? Why am I locked in this room? Dr.T : I’m observing you. I need to understand everything about each of the personas in your head. DDT : So why am I locked in? Dr.T : Because you cannot leave until we’ve put reasons behind each and every one of them. DDT : So, you’re gonna leave me here until you find out everything you need to know? Dr.T : You catch on quick... DDT : This is ridiculous... Dr.T : Ridiculous now, but when we're finished, you'll feel right as rain. Somehow, DDT doubted this. He knew that this couldn’t help him with Moraless. While the PTC Champion prepared, DDT would sit in this room. While the rest of XUW prepared for Stable Wars, DDT would sit in this room. How DDT could come out of this any better... Another hurdle placed on the path.... drowning in the sea of myself, crashing on the wakes of evil and the waves of good. i pray for the return of me from the very one who has taken, knowing i do so unknowingly yet with little regret.
