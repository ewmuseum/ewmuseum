A Political Message

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 "Jake, what about the rest of the field?" "Packed with the best in the world. What more do you need to say?" Jake shrugged. "I’m taking this one match at a time. Right now I’m focused on Stevens." Another reporter shouted over the others, "Well what do you think about him?" "I’ll let me video do the talking." Jake turned. As the lights went down, a video came onto the big screen behind him. A waving American flag came into view, and then slowly the image of Michael Stevens appeared in front of the flag. A deep voice spoke. "Michael Stevens, American hero? He says he wants to help the starving children of the world, but what was said about his charity event behind closed doors?" As Michael Stevens smiling face was shown a voice could be heard, very choppy and strange. "Thanksgiving is more than anything about we starving. We raised three quarters of a million dollars this yearfor business." An image of Stevens head superimpsoed on Stevens body came up as Bush’ voice could be heard, "They misunderestimated me." The image shifted to show Michael Stevens with his US Title belt. Stevens was speaking in some interview, but the words were muted. "Michael Stevens can’t even remember the name of his opponent. When asked he seemed confused, gesturing like a wild man. Do we really want someone who can’t remember who he’s facing in GTT5? Can we trust someone who thinks that…" An image of Stevens head superimpsoed on Stevens body came up as Bush’ voice could be heard, "Well, I think if you say you're going to do something and don't do it, that's trustworthiness." The final image showed Michael Stevens with animated flames coming out of his mouth to attack the image of Jake Hix. Jake’s animated figure blocked the flames, sending the attack back to burn Stevens to a crisp. "Michael Stevens… Soft in the brain… Soft in the ring… Primetime Central deserves a strong competitor." The scene shifted to Jake Hix walking along an old fence on his ranch in a black GLOBAL tee shirt and jeans. He set his boot on the rail and stared into the camera, "Michael Stevens can’t even remember my name. Let him underestimate me. I think Primetime Central deserves a real leader, a man of the people, a man of vision, a…of this is horse shit!" Jake kicked through the fence and grabbed the camera, "Let’s put all out there. I don’t want you to know my name, I don’t want you to know anything about me! This isn’t about names or anything else. This is all about what happens inside the ring. This is about me busting you open and beating the Hell out of you. I’m going to throw you around like a cheap Thai prostitute! See you soon Stevens, see you soon."
