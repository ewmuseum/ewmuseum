Match

5 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life is good at the moment. Chris Johnson has not been able to say that for a long time. After spending the past few years going from federation to federation, watching them come and watching them go CJ has now finally got to the place he’s been fighting to get to. “A big fish in a little pond” That’s what some would say about CJ after a month of joining a federation. Every federation Chris has been in he’s won the world heavyweight championship. But for CJ winning the biggest prize in the business has come to early ends due to federation closures. During his stay with EPW, CJ won the world heavyweight championship only once…but would have won it two more times had EPW now closed it’s doors the night after the championship match. But every where Chris Johnson goes for the first time, they take a look at him and say the same thing. “Plain Name, black baggy spandex pants…you’ll never be able to market a guy like that” And within weeks, he’s become the federations bigger franchiser. It’s happening now at NGW, nobody on the NGW roster wants to look at the Storm card and see that they are against Chris Johnson. For a man who speaks a lot, will flip you off to your face, will use “colorful” language and spit on your grave, he will certainly back that up. His undefeated streak of seventeen matches, the very fact that he is part of the greatest tag team in the history of this business “The HellRaisers” who currently are the second longest serving Tag Team Champions in NGW History. The 2003 Rookie of the year sitting on his mantle piece and most recently…the NGW National Championship after defeating NGW Legend and well known PTC star Kraze in a ladder match at Bloodfest 2004. And to top all that off….in this week rankings Chris Johnson has been named at number five in the Revolution Wrestling Network top ten. So chances are he’s going to be in a good mood. The scene opens up in a jungle area. The ground cannot be seen before of low plant life and trees all over the place. Various wild life is about with a monkey darting through the branches and a snake slithering down a tree. A quiet whacking noise is heard in the background and it seems to be getting louder and louder. A set of bushes begin to shake when a machete comes swinging down clearing a path. A short man standing at about five foot three walks over wearing a creamy British type jungle shorts, sleeveless jacket and hate with sun block on his nose. Coming from behind is Chris Johnson towering over Carlos despite being only five foot twelve himself CJ wears black trousers and a dirty white t-shirt with the NGW logo stitched on the right side of his chest. A few rips stand out from where he’s obviously caught some branches. The shirt fits reasonably tight as his large arm muscles and shoulders stand out. “That man right there…that’s Carlos. I’ve known him for a few years now when I was back in XCW and when they closed, he just sorta hung around. He’s pretty much stayed away from the wrestling business since the demise of XCW and being trying to find his “true self” as he claims to say.” Carlos – I thought they said Monkeys were friendly! CJ – Monkeys have never been friendly. The wild are aggressive and sexually aroused 24/7…think of them as a bunch of rapists. “He’s also so much more trouble than his worth, petting a wild monkey isn’t on his things to do list anymore.” CJ – A Jungle Walk though? Seriously Carlos… “And despite all the trouble he gets himself into I normally take his advice, like this Jungle walk in the Amazon because it would “refresh my mind”…what the hell was I thinking? The guy can’t even smoke a cigarette without coughing his guts up, and he’s been smoking for two years so what in the hell is he going to know about refreshing my damn mind? Last week was the hardest wrestling week of my life, I captured two titles…the NGW National Championship and the XWF Genesis championship. Now it takes a lot to be prepared for any championship match, but for two and to win them now that takes something special. Lets just say that I go out there and I believe that I am the absolute best there is, and that’s all there is to it. So Kasper Skye, I ask…what have you achieved in the wrestling world over the past seven days which says you are one of the very best in the business? What have you done that shows that you are even capable of lasting more than thirty seconds with me INCLUDING your intro time. Oh yeah on a side note it’s currently LOW 0…..that’s zero…naught…...null……and NGW……1. After I defeat you it will prove that NGW has the better talent than LOW…Just thought I’d point that out…that your LOW career could just possibly be on the line here. But hell even if you have a good day, maybe…just maybe you’ll be able to limp out of there.” Carlos – That was one hell of a win for NGW you and Robb Hardy got. CJ – It was more of a slap in the face for LOW from Robb Hardy getting the pin because over the recent moves…Robb Hardy never pins anybody. Carlos – Who is it your facing this week from LOW? CJ – Kasper Skye I think. Carlos – Know anything about him? CJ – Nope. Carlos – That’s bad then isn’t it from my last check on knowing nothing about your opponent. CJ – He also doesn’t know anything about me. Doesn’t matter how many video tapes you watch you don’t know an opponent until you’ve got in the ring with him…or her in Joey Campbels case this week. But your forgetting that this time it’s just going to be Chris Johnson representing NGW and bringing NGW up yet another level like me and Dante have single handily done ever since we arrived there. It’s only inevitable that Chris Johnson will at one point become the Prime Time Central Extreme Championship. Just like you’ve seen this week with the National Championship and the Genesis Championship. I’m a triple champion, that backs up that I am one of the best of the absolute very best there is in this business today. CJ and Carlos carry on bushing walking through some bushes keeping one eye on the ground before coming to a small over grown area which has a path leading to a old rotting looking rope bridge. To the right there is another well built bridge leading across to the other side. Carlos – No chance! CJ – Cool Role Bridge! Carlos – There’s no chance in hell you are going to get me going acr.. CJ grabs Carlos by the collar not letting him finish what he is saying and puts him in front of him shoving him forward onto the rope bridge. CJ – MOVE GO GO GO!!! CJ’s fast paced and loud voice ushers Carlos forward a few yards before he stops holding onto the tatty ropes either side. CJ – Whatever you do don’t look down… Carlos then looks down and sees the fifty foot drop with something at the bottom squirming. Carlos – AHHHHHH! CJ – I told you not to look down!!! Carlos – EVERYBODY LOOKS DOWN WHEN YOU TELL THEM NOT TO!!! WHAT THE HELL ARE THOSE SQUIRMY THINGS DOWN THERE?!?!? CJ – Those squirmy things are alligators. Carlos – WHAT?!? CROCS?!?!? CJ – No Alligator. Carlos – Seriously…what in the hell is the difference? CJ – When a crocodile closes its mouth, the fourth tooth in the lower jaw sticks up outside the top jaw. When an alligator does the same thing, this tooth is hidden. Anyway this isn’t a wildlife lesson so MOVE! CJ boots Carlos in the back shoving him forward. Step by step he begins edging further and further across the ladder. “Thinking about it, I’ve not really done that much reflecting on my up and coming match against Kasper Skye. It’s probably because I secretly have a big fear of heights, and it’s amazing what you can forget when you smoked a joint half an hour before.” Chris now seems to slow up as well as both are now taking synchronized slow small steps across the ladder, with CJ taking a quick glance down. “Yeah this is definitely going on my stupid things I’ve done in my life list. Ok think of happy thoughts……smoking one big fat mother of a joint sitting back to a pizza….any pizza…a few beers and the Tom & Jerry collection in my Lazy Master arm chair. But I’ll do with pinning Kasper Sky to the canvas 1 2 3 and making it NGW 2 LOW 0.” Carlos – Hey, y’know this isn’t so bad, and I think this bridge is safer than I think it is. Carlos tests it by doing a jump, but jump meaning his feet don’t leave the ground. “Inner Conscience - Chris, be a good chap and don’t let that fool jump up and down will you?” Naturally Carlos begins jumping higher and higher. CJ – HEY! CUT IT WILL YOU! A snapping sound is heard and Carlos immediately stops and stays frozen on the spot as does Chris himself. “ah crap” “Inner Conscience - Oh for the love of God do I have to tell you everything? Run fool! “ CJ – GO GO GO!!!! CJ and Carlos begins running along the bridge as fast as they can as the end they came on begins to weaken on the ropes. Carlos puts his foot through one of the wooden planks which sails all the water into the water at the bottom, but CJ is quick to grab him by the collar and pull him up with one arm shoving him forward. It’s noticeable that Chris is the faster runner out of the two and gets frustrated at Carlos’s lack of speed before slowing down and taking a run up hitting a frontal drop kick into his back sending Carlos the last ten yards onto the other side. As Chris steps on the other side the other end of the bridge finally gives way and comes swinging down slamming against the cliff side. CJ – Jungle Walk my ass….next time to refresh my mind it’s going to be a mother fucking joint, in my lazy boy arm chair, with a pizza….any pizza and a load of beers sat watching Tom & Jerry DVD’s non stop on my 52 inch plasma screen television! “Lets get one thing straight…I never make mistakes normally…and the only mistake I’ve made was taking Carlos’s advice for this shitty walk to get in the right frame of mind for my match against thingy…….wait a sec…” CJ – Hey….was I supposed to bring the compass or the map. Carlos holds up the compass that’s strapped around his neck as CJ fumbles into his trouser pocket and pulls out a compass as well. “Like I said…..I never normally make mistakes. And trust me, there will be no mistakes against Kasper Skye”
