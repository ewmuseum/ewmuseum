Yori Yakamo. jr vs. The Terminating Dildos from the Future vs. Trystan, Apeman Hitler's Collaborating Dinosaur-Man Sous Chef

Date: 2006-11-16 (cue up the theme from Soap) When we last left YORI, he was running from a REPROGRAMMED MARAUDING ARMY OF TERMINATNG DILDOS bent on preventing a World War between man and sex toy. He escaped to Tsarist Russia and met CATHERINE THE GREAT who he plied with his sweet sweet love, but from whom he could not procure any Cossacks. He tried to recruit some of the half-man, half dinosaurs from the caverns of inner Earth under Antarctica, but found that they had been enslaved by Yori’s old pet/science project/power mad dictator APEMAN HITLER who captured Yori and Randalls. What horrible fate awaits our lovable pervert? Find out next on Yori Yakamo, jr. vs. The Terminating Dildos of the Future v. Trystan, Apeman Hitler’s Collaborating Dinosaur-Man Sous Chef. --- "I have to admit, Apeman Hitler, this is a hell of a last meal. The okonomiyaki is amazing. The dough is so soft." "Well, I can’t take all ze credit. My dinozaur man zous chef/collaborator, Herr Trystan, is quite ze wiz in ze kuche." "Yeah, if you weren’t about to kill me with a horribly convoluted laser beam contraption I would totally ask for the recipe." "Technically, it’s an electromagnet." "It’s still gonna kill me, though." "Good and dead." Today hadn’t gone so good for Yori Yakamo, jr. Not that it was the first day that had ended with all four of his extremities handcuffed to a giant metal bulls-eye. Not even close. However, it was the first time there was a giant laser….excuse me, electromagnet pointed at his crotch. Probably. I mean, there were some parties in college…that got a little crazy….he doesn’t remember so well. Anyway, Yori wasn’t afraid of death. He was quite prepared to die. He just never thought it would happen this way. Not so much the junk being blown to bits part, that was hardly unexpected. Yori always figured he kick the bucket after a Martian farmer blew off his junk with a space shotgun after he caught a 90 year old Yori planting his seed in the three-breasted farmer’s daughter. Yori thought this so likely, for some reason, he had it written into his life insurance as a double indemnity clause. He didn’t think, however, that it would end at the hands of a hirsuite, primate, fascist. "So Apeman Hitler, can’t you just like, put a chip in my brain that will make me a sleeper agent for your armies of dinosaur men and Hokkaido fisherman. Then, when you activate it, it will turn me into a killing machine and make my penis grow three inches." "I’d like to do zat for you, Yori, I really would. Ve simply don’t have ze technology yet." "For the sleeper agent thing, or the penis enlargement thing?" "Either, really." "Oh, well." "Don’t worry, you von’t feel a zing, Yori. Ze magnet will simply break every bone in your body, and zat vill be zhat." "Then why is it pointed at my junk?" "It seemed like ze vay you vould vant to go out." "You know me so well, Apeman Hitler. This is quite the impressive contraption." "Yori, you vouldn’t be trying to stall me, vould you?" "Never, Apeman Hitler." "Vell, if you must know, it is based on an old prototype of Tesla’s." "Wow, does it play ‘Modern Day Cowboy’" "Nikolai Tesla, Yori, not ze mediocre 80s band." "Cold as ice! Hard as stone! As he walks into the room! Hey…there is nothing mediocre about the intricate and bluesy guitar work of Frank Hannon, Apeman Hitler. TESLA ROCKS! Who the hell is Nikolai Tesla, was he like their college roommate who they named the band after?" "Ugh, I have had enough of zis. I had to listen to enough of your bantering about zecond rate metal acts ven you vere trying to bribe Rippongi co-eds to test your prototype dildos vith Cutty Sark and Dokken CDs. Start ze machine." Herr Trystan finished busing the last of Yori’s exquisitely plated dishes and flipped the switch on. The machine began to shutter and emit a high-pitched whining noise. "Auf Wiedersehen, Yori Yakamo, jr." Yori closed his eyes and imagined a happy place, a place where Thai Hookers roamed free on the grassy plains. Where Yori sat under an old apple tree in a straw hat and sipped on lemonade while watching his twenty or thirty illegitimate grandchildren play a pick-up game of touch football. He watched his old pal ROBOYORI, reconstituted and frolicking with ROBOYORINA (still in the prototype stage) as one happy sexbot couple. It was a future that was never to be. Ah well, Yori thought, 100 virgins, here I come. "Yori, you are not of the Islamic faith." "Randalls, get out of my brain. I spend my last few moments of life looking at imaginary frolicking hookers and sexbots, not conversing with you." "Oh, you aren’t gonna die yet, Yori." "It sure looks that way." "Looks can be deceiving, my fine, perverted friend." With a rather resounding crash. Randalls plowed through the door of Apeman Hitler’s Secret Underground Interrogation Lair. He was carrying two dinosaur men security guards on his back, as the reptilian creatures sank their teeth into his back, and clawed at his face. "Vhat is se meaning of zis. Stop him Trystan" Nothing would stop Randalls from his goal though. All three of the combatants plowed into Apeman Hitler’s giant electromagnetic execution device, knocking it to the ground with a thud. The discharge shook the room and split the ground in two, opening a chasm in the earth where Randalls promptly deposited the two security guards. Their pitiful roars slowly faded away to mere echoes. Randalls quickly unfastened Yori from his now upside-down metallic bulls-eye. Yori started to thank Randalls, but caught a flash out of the corner of his eye. "Randalls, look out." Randalls was a second too late to stop Trystan from sinking his teeth into his spine. But not a second too late to stab him in the eye with a shard of the machine. Then stab him in the throat. A few times. The collaborator’s acrid blood flowed a small river across the floor of the torture chamber, coming to a stop at the feet of Apeman Hitler. Who laughed maniacally. "What are you laughing at, you damn….dirty….ooooooh boy." Randalls never finished the insult, as he promptly collapsed in Yori’s arms. "Randalls?" "Zhere is no hope for your little friend, Yori. Trystan’s bite is incredibly poisonous. Like zat of ze Viper. Zhere is no antidote." "Randalls, noooooooooooooooooooo!" Randalls coughed up a bit of blood. "Yori, remember…in the end, there can be only one." "Ummmmm….are you feeling okay, Randalls?" "Not really, I’m dying." "Right. This sucks. How am I supposed to get back to the GTT6 tournament, get these Terminating Dildos from the Future off my back, and stop Apeman Hitler from taking over the world." "I will be watching over you from the depths of Vallhalla. Or more likely, hell." "They can’t send you to hell, Randalls." "I’ve killed a lot of people, Yori." "I’m sure it was all justified." "I have a private island in the Carribbean where I hunt men for sport." "Oh…" "Yeah…I regret nothing." And with that "The Wolf" Mike Randalls breathed his last. "Randalllllllllllllllllllssssssssssssss!!!!!!!!! DAMN YOU GOD! DAMN YOU TERMINATING DILDOS FROM THE FUTURE! DAMN YOU APEMAN HITLER!" "I’m still here, Yori." Indeed he was, and pointing a Luger Pistolen-08 right at the Cerebral Cocksassin. "Ve Vill end this right now." Apeman Hitler fired once at Yori, but the giant pool of green light that appeared between them quickly engulfed the bullet. "Well, this is just great." Out of the light stepped the Y-2000. Riki III still droned on from the TV in its chest. "There you are, Yori. You can’t escape us forever. Even if you hide in a secret underground fortress in Modern day Antarctica, we will find you and kill you." "No, you can’t kill him. I get to kill him. He zold me to a cosmetics company." "Who the heck is this primitive primate?" "I am your worst nightmare, Terminating Zexrobot." "Apeman Hitler, I have no time for you. I am trying to prevent a post-apocalyptic future here. I can’t be bothered to dispense with your furry fascism. Does it really matter which one of us kills Yori." "Jaa, jaa, JAA! It does. I am Apeman Hitler, dammit! I vill someday rule the world." "Look, the army of sextoys beat you to it. I can only deal with one possible future post-apocalyptic scenario at one time, okay. Y-2000 do your thing." "Y-2000 SMASH!" "How crude. Vhere is the sense of style? Ze sense of flair? Ze use of a giant electromagnet?" Yori was caught between a marauding sexbot and a giant, fascistic Ape. Not quite the ménage-a-trois he had in mind. Hey, giant electromagnet, that’s like a regular magnet, right? Yori’s thoughts were unclear. He wished he had paid more attention in science class, but anything was better than being at the business end of a spinning spiked dildo. Yori dove for the switch on the wall, and the electromagnet started to sputter into action. "Y-2000 STUCK! Y-2000 ANGRY! Y-2000 DOWN!" Yori’s plan had worked perfectly, as the giant sexbot was pulled inextricably towards the giant magnet and was now prone on its back. Of course, Yori was slowly being pulled towards the magnet as well, crotch and nipples first, but so far he could manage. "Hah, you are dead now, Yori. Dead, dead, dead you are." Apeman Hitler tried to steady the pistol at Yori once more, but found it wrested from his hand and affixed firmly to the electromagnet as well. "Damn you, Yori Yakamo, jr. You are ze zorn in my zide." Smoke began pouring out of the electromagnet. Alarms went off all over the underground lair. "Uh-oh. That can’t be good." "Ve vill meet again, Yori. If you get out of here alive. AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!! I hope you do, though. For I vant to be the one to end your miserable, perverted exzistence." Apeman Hitler quickly made his way to the escape hatch and into his reverse engineered Alien pod, where he sped off for parts unknown. Yori tried with all his might to escape the room. He managed to get his nipple piercings off, and they rapidly clanged against the super magnet. But there was just too much heavy metal in his genitalia to remove in time. He needed some sort of miracle. What he got instead was a giant explosion. Yori grabbed onto the giant bulls-eye with dear life and was thrown clear of the Secret Underground Lair of Apeman Hitler. He was flying. It was awesome. Then he started falling. Distinctly less awesome. Yori hoped no one heard him screaming like a little girl. That would be embarrassing. At some point his pants were blown off too, doubly embarrassing. Stupid electromagnet. Stupid Tesla. Yori landed with a resounding thud in the upper mezzanine of the Dinosaur People’s Gladiatorial arena. The bullseye broke some of his fall, a poor, now probably dead dinosaur-man popcorn vender breaking the rest of it. Yori dusted himself off. He certainly lived a rather charmed existence. Really, he should have been killed a few dozen times over by now. But instead it was his best friend Randalls who had found himself touched by the cold caress of Death. Yori didn’t know if he could soldier on alone. He had no one. Not the YORobot, not Randalls, not even Jesus Christ, Vampire Hunter, left at his side. For the first time, he had to make the journey alone. THUNK "OWWWWWWWWWWWW!" Yori went down like a ton of bricks. Good thing it wasn’t an actual wrestling match, or he would be down for the three count. He slowly gathered his small modicum of wits about him and looked to see what had conked him on the head. Staring back at him was the severed head of ROBOYORI. "Hey, old buddy, it’s good to see you again. We gotta see what we can do about getting your body back. But hey, at least I have someone to talk to." Reinvigorated, Yori picked up the detached head of his best sexbot friend and set off into the wilderness, still determined to beat those damn dildos, beat Apeman Hitler, and at some point, maybe even make it to GTT6.
