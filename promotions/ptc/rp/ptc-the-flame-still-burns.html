The Flame Still Burns (A Prophetic Theory #1) The Flame Still Burns (A Prophetic Theory #1) Prologue Was it that time already? Was he ready to return to action? Will this team reunite? These are just the first three of many questions that ran though the head of Lights Out Wrestling’s former Extreme Champion Juvian Ramorez. Last week at Infinite II Juvian made his historical return by making a shock announcement. That not only was he re-forming his alliance with John “The Prophet” Adams, but Juvian will now also challenge for the World Heavyweight Championship that Jason Kage, the long time shadow rider holds. But on top of all that happened we have learnt that John Adams and Juvian Ramorez have nominated themselves for a inter-promotional war to win the newly establish Prime time central Tag Team Championship. This inter-promotional war has started this very week and features a fatal four way match with the winners of the match climbing the ladder ever so closer to getting themselves a shot at the titles. There is no question that tensions around Juvian and Adams are running high. With a week off from lights out wrestling they have full concentration on this match, but what will happen? Will A Prophetic Theory pull off a somewhat upset and move ever so closer to them belts? Well only time will tell. End Prologue Days I waited for him to call on the phone, I just sat in my chair watching the clock’s big dark wooden hand move round and round, I would almost fall asleep just through exhaustion waiting then just to wake me up. I would look over the phone where if it rang I would have leaped across in a movie-star like fashion just to pick it up, but there was nothing…Nothing but silence. The days would pass more and more and it would look like nothing was happening, was I holding on too close to a lost cause?, I would question myself every hour if this call was ever going to come, or was it one big wind up, yea because the way I looked at it someone could just playing the trick of kicking a man when they are down. So just as I was about to leave this whole thing it happened, a flash in the corner of my eye and the phone began to ring, the ring tone echoed around the room and my face immediately begin to grin, so big that it looked like my face had been hooked by a fishing hook. With no hesitation I rushed over to the phone before picking it up. “Hello” I slowly spoke into the phone trying to hold back the exhaustion after racing over to the phone. My hand clenched the phone so hard that my knuckles started to turn blue through lack of blood circulation. “Mr Ramorez….” As the other man on the end of the phone began to talk relief suddenly broke through my body at a fast rate. This was the call that I was waiting for, this was my reason for waking up this week, I will admit I wouldn’t have gotten out of bed so early if I never thought that this phone call would have ever came, but here it is and I am happy. Well in all honesty I was more than happy, there was so much good feeling around my body it was if I had just won lottery America. “This is Confirmation that you will be placed in the fatal four way match against The Flying Vergomovs, Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean and The Masked Monkeys.” I instantly raised one eyebrow because I was puzzled why I would be facing two other teams from Lights Out Wrestling, but then it clicked in my head, we wasn’t what I had once thought, that was being the number one tag team in lights out wrestling, obviously there was a mix up and the FCC entered three teams instead of one. But it doesn’t matter much really to me, because that’s just another victory, we will do it next week and we will also do it when we face if we ever do at home in LOW. “Your match will take place on the 23rd of January, Mr Ramorez thank you for your entrant into what will be a historic Tag Team Championship and further more good luck” It was then that I knew that we had a chance to make history, in just over eleven days time I will be competing at an unknown arena with the chance of stepping just that one step closer to those title belts. Don’t get me wrong this isn’t the first time that I have been in a cross-promotional event with Prime Time Central. No far from it Infact, I have competed in numerous events over the past three years, but in all honesty this is the first one that I actually think that I have a chance of winning. Of course I cannot win these titles alone so I have got my original Lights Out Wrestling and current S.I.N member John “The Prophet” Adams along with me for the ride. And I am a true believer that the first rule of all successful tag teams is that you Must be able to trust your tag team partner. The fact that John and I work at the same company and are in the same faction makes me realise that I trust no-one more than john in this match. I know that he has what it takes to not only win this match, but he also has the experience to guide the team to victory. If I was asked yesterday if I was confident that I was going to win the first round match I wouldn’t be able to give you such a positive answer because of not knowing my opponents. But as of this moment in time I can give you that answer, and my answer is nothing less than I believe that we will come out victorious. I slowly placed the phone back down on the receiver and just sat back into my chair staring up at the ceiling, I sat there for moments just to requiem all that had just happened. It really took around twenty minutes for the thought to actually set in, but by the time that I did I also realised something else, I realised that now was the time, and I had to prepare for this match. Then one of the main questions came into my head again, I asked myself the following question and it took along time to answer, the question simply was can I become a great fighter like I once was several months ago. I began to think to myself what has changed and could it still all work. Don’t get me wrong I know I am a great fighter, but before last month I was sat at home and going to the hospital quite frequent, I would attend wrestling promotions that arrived near my home only to look on with envy for the sport that I loved was nearly taken away from me and I could do nothing about it. I felt so low when it happened, first my brother died then I got injured, could I possibly get any lower into a ditch I used to ask myself but then it happened, because I was out of work I couldn’t pay my bills and they repossessed my home, that right they just barged in and took away everything that I once owned to try and make up what I owed them. So I made a vow, not only to myself but to my god Lucifer, I will make a return. Just like the phoenix inside my body I will rise once again from the aches. I will put nothing on hold this time, I wont hold back and I will just rise to the top, not only as half of the soon to be tag team champions in Prime Time Central, but also I will rise to the top of Lights out Wrestling. You see I had a private visit from my lord and he told me that the only reason I was saved from retirement was the sole reason to make a change. Some people grow up all their life’s in a cushy twenty four seven job, in a nice office…get paid a nice wage and drive around in a nice car, but in the back of their minds they know that’s not what they dreamt of when they was little. Maybe they dreamt of being rich but not in the way that they actually had to work for it. Just like some people when they are younger dream of being movie stars, so they dedicate their life to acting, they go to the special colleges; they get special tuition and spend big bucks going to places like Julliard. And after a few years of pumping their green into the American schooling system they are brought to the un-dramatic climax of “Sorry you won’t make the cut” And then their lives are turned upside down, they are sent into a bottomless pit and all for what? well they all tried for a dream that was always failing before it could ever succeed. But you see that’s why I am a different from all of those other people in the world, because I am the guy who tried so hard that he made it. When I was young I used to think that I was a Sethan Payne, back then he was the man that everyone wanted to be. I used to watch him on the television and then imamate him when I was around the other people I grew up with, then when I grew up I found out that I could become a wrestler myself, so I only eat the right things, I would start in the gym and tried to always give myself the look. When my friends where out in the street playing ball or round at a girls house trying to chat her up I would be at home doing weights or pushing my stamina to the fullest. I never stopped training until I got to the top, not only the top of my game but also to the top of wrestling, now I am there. I can been seen every week in Philadelphia wrestling for Lights out wrestling, and the only difference between myself and the others in the world was the simple fact that I never gave up. If someone was to look at me and tell me to my face that I would never make it I would look into their eyes smile back and tell them. “Just watch me” And that’s what happened, the once trainers of me would see me in the street and tell me how they knew I would really make it, and what are they now?, nothing but sheep. Sheep following the street rage of what is the local lad turned good. Now I have a new focus, I have a new challenge to complete and that by winning the Prime Time tag team championship belts. Many people don’t think that me and john can really do it but what I have to say to you all is Just Watch Us. Come next week you will all have a big apology to say to us, and then you can be like all the other sheep, because I am your shepherd, I will lead you away from the light and into the darkness, for I am the Evolved Enigma. As I slowly close my lips that have been preaching to the world I sit back and relax with the thought on my mind that this time in two weeks we could be a step closer to holding of belts in our hands. Will we do it?, that’s a question not even I can answer right now, but I know that I will sure as hell do everything in my power to try and win those belts, but like I’ve said before first we need to win this match. Until next time, let me leave you with this little rhyme. “For whom we are Is this what life is meant to be? So many decisions In so little time Very soon… Those belts will be mine” Blessed Be…
