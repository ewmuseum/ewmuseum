Christopher Flores Public Access Pimp Joined: 23 Feb 2003 Posts: 22 Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 5:56 am Post subject: Mission of Death #2- Koji Sasuke Xtreme Clusterfuck **The time has arrived. Will the plans of this mission go as told? Every time something of this measure lurks around, it’s hard to see that he was chosen to find someone, he doesn’t eve know. A person he has never seen before. He knows nothing, but as the chance for greed arrives, anyone would dig at the moment. The money is the only issue; it shows everybody how vulnerable everyone is when it comes to money. Time is the only enemy is there enough for a situation of this magnitude.** ::Koji is shown as he stands across from Mr. Manning. Manning is suppose to hand some information about some mission.:: Manning: Listen, these papers will tell you were there at. I will say this it won’t be that easy. Koji: I was expecting an easy mission. The girl’s name is Mercedes right? Also who was that who shot the gun off? Manning: We will take care of that sooner or later. Just do the mission. We have a car waiting for you, its are treat. ::Koji turns his head looking at the black Mustang near him. He begins to walk towards the Mustang and jumps into the car, and looks seeing the keys are already in the ignition. He pushes the gas pedal and begins to maneuver the wheel and drives away. He gets up on a street and glances at some of the papers. He nods his head and continues to drive. He follows the street directions and turns at the green light, and continues down the street. He glances at his watch, and continues to drive. After continuously driving based on his directions, Koji stops infront of a two-story house that is surrounded by fence. The front part has a few cars parked in the front. Koji stops his car and gets out of the car and looks at the house.:: Koji: Damn, that thing is huge. ::Koji goes at the trunk of the car, and opens it, and sees some weapons. He looks at the various rifles and swords. He supplies himself with various weapons and slams the trunk down running forward at the fence. He dodges up at the fence and jumps over the fence, landing on his feet. He pulls out a .44 handgun and looks around. He shakes his head as he walks up the driveway. He continues to walk around and gets to the front door unharmed.:: Koji: This can’t be this easy. ::Koji kicks the door open and slowly looks around holding his gun up in air. He continues around and walks right towards a door. He enters the door and looks around seeing a girl wrapped up in a ball in the corner.:: Koji: Hello anyone there? ::A girl slowly walks into the light as you see her sobbing.:: Girl: Who are you? Koji: Is your name Mercedes? Girl: Yes that is my name and how do you know that? Koji: Don’t ask, I’m here to save you. Mercedes: Seriously there is no way out of this place. ::Koji walks closer to her as she lurks back in fear. She finally digs in and walks towards Koji. The two walk out the door and Jeffrey Manning stands across from them.:: Koji: What are you doing here? Jeff: Listen, don’t ever do businesses with a man you don’t know. Koji: So this is the end? I am going to die off here? ::Koji starts to walk forward towards Jeff as a crowd of men come from behind Jeff holding guns.:: Jeff: You think it was going to be that easy? ::Koji turns his head looking over as Eric and two larger men and another man walk with him.:: Jeff: Al, how’s it going? Al: Fine, so who is this Koji man? Koji: It’s me you dumbass. Al: Watch it before you are dead as an ass. Koji: Kill me now, see if I would even care if I died. ::Eric grabs a gun from one of the men from behind his father and looks at Koji.:: Eric: Hello Mercedes, I thought you would have known to not break up with me. You had a choice. Mercedes: Shut up asshole, I did it for a reason. Eric: A reason, well that’s real nice. There is one thing I have wanted to do for a long, long time. I mean for years I have wanted to do this. Now I am given a good chance at it. ::Eric clicks the gun once and points it forward at Koji. He then at the last second turns it shooting his father. His father quickly falls down and lays down unexpectedly.:: Eric: Sorry dad your day was coming. Al: Get these two out of here. Send them to the bottom room. ::The crowd of men escort Koji and Mercedes out of the room and head off. Koji looks around at the room and shakes his head looking mad.:: Koji: I figure money is my greed and my greed leads me to this pain. Which hasn’t be suffered just yet. I knew something was wrong. Mercedes: I can’t believe Eric would be the one behind this whole kidnap and even kill his own father. How am I going to get out of this mess? It’s your entire fault. Koji: My fault, you get it they planned this until they’d find someone stupid enough to fall into it. I fell for it and now they played it out. See if I said no to it they would have found someone else to play the part. Mercedes: Yeah, maybe your right. Koji: Maybe I am right. I might as well just think of a plan to get the heck out of this dump. I find it hard to see why I even agreed. I just don’t get it all. ::The black fades away as Koji and Mercedes impatiently walk around trapped in a room.:: _________________ XWF Koji Sasuke 4-2 2003 XWF Anniversary Cup Winner
