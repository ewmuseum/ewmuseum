(IG70) von Doom: Money, Women, Fame, and a Life of Luxury

30 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler: von Doom Feds: SCCW, GCW, NFW, AWE ------ September 2002... Backstage at a crappy independent wrestling show, I am about to make my debut in the wrestling world by taking on some hardcore junkie that the fans love. Naturally I want these fans to hate me. I want them to despise me. Heroes are a dime a dozen in the wrestling world but a good villain is hard to come by. However I do have a plan to beat this veteran hero guy. As I continue to wander the backstage area filled with losers and drunken idiots, I see someone worth talking to. He’s the referee for my match. Walking over to him, he looks at me like he has no idea who I am. Doesn’t matter. He’ll know my name in a few years as the guy who’s taking over the business quicker than Riktor Arkane and Leonard Aarons combined. “Hello,” I say to him. “My name is James von Doom. I’m new around here.” He gives me a strange look. “You’re the new guy I’m supposed to work with tonight, huh?” he asks as I nod my head. “Yeah, that’s me,” I respond with a cordial smile. “I’m supposed to go over the finish with you before we go out there,” he says as I raise an eyebrow because of the tone he uses to say this. “What’s the finish?” “Kaos is going to roll you up for the win after a power bomb attempt by you is reversed,” he says as I give him a disapproving look. That’s when my real plan goes into motion. I reach into my pocket and pull out a wad of cash before handing it over to him. “I’m changing the finish,” I tell him as he looks at the cash. After looking at the money for moment, he then looks back at me before getting a big smile on his face. I can tell that things are going to go smoothly. * * * Seven years and three months later… 11/28/2009 Inside of my luxurious mansion, I’m holding a very large social gathering. It’s a Thanksgiving formal party to celebrate my glorious return to the wrestling world. Since returning from the nadir of my career I’ve garnered a 5-2 record and a GTT win as well as number one contendership to the GCW World Tag Team Championship belts. I also beat Legion for the first time and am a serious contender to become the first AWE Universal Champion. Things are going well. The party is filled with all sorts of rich people, celebrities, gorgeous women, and various goons, ghouls, and minions that are roaming around the party to do my bidding. However, instead of mingling with the beautiful people, I’m stuck sitting in a lounge chair having a question and answer session with a scraggly cokehead in a suit named Rick Malloy. After asking me if I knew if Storm Shadow lived at the end of ‘G.I. Joe: Rise of Cobra,’ he then asks me one of the most interesting questions anyone has ever asked me. “von Doom, why did you get into wrestling?” he asks. “You don’t seem like the type that would get into this kind of stuff.” “Now I want you to look at something,” I tell him as I motion around to the women all around me at the party. “See all that?” He looks around and sees all the women roaming about like wildlife on the savannah in Africa. Malloy looks like his eyes are about to pop out of his head. “Yeah,” he says with drool coming down from his mouth. “Now look around at this house,” I say to him as I motion around once again. “Look at the lavish decorations and furniture. “Look at how posh and elegant everything in this house looks. This house looks like something a multimillionaire would own, does it not?” “Well yeah…” “Because that is why I decided to be a wrestler. Money, women, and power. I live a life of luxury. I get to hang out with beautiful people all the time. That is until I go to the arenas and get surrounded by humanoids and homeless vagabonds that make me sick to my stomach. These people that come to the shows are disease carrying scumbags that are the lowest forms of life on this planet. That’s the only drawback. However I have been associated with some of the greatest talents in wrestling history. I’ve teamed with and managed some of the best wrestlers of all time. No one gives me any respect, but I know deep down that I am one of the best of all time. But that’s the drawback.” Rappin’ Rick looks at me like I’m nuts. “What do you mean?” he asks. “I will never be viewed at as a legend. However I live like a legend. I’m the most conservative wrestler in the business, scrapping and saving every step of the way. That’s how I made my fortune and got all of these minions, goons, and other assorted buddies to do my bidding.” As I say this, one of the Church of the Unholy Executioners who is wandering around my party peers at me for direction. I nod my head at him. He nods back and goes off in some other direction. Malloy turns and gives me a look. “von Doom, what the hell is going on?” “Oh nothing,” I reply with a devious grin. “Just got something in the works for one of my opponents. This is going to be something the idiot will never suspect. However he’s not over, he never will be over, and I’m going to win. Of course I can deny everything, as usual. I had nothing to do with it. I’m an innocent man.” “Bullshit.” “You know it’s bullshit but they wont.” Malloy starts laughing as my sports agent William Smith comes over to join us with a beer in hand. He’s in his usual sleek suit and is checking out the mamacitas the moment he sits down on the couch next to Malloy. Malloy then motions to me. “How they let this guy get away with this stuff is beyond me,” Malloy tells Smith as he starts laughing. “It’s hilarious,” I tell them. “I cause all of these problems and nobody gets mad at me. Everyone’s totally cool with me. I sweet talk them and tell them what they want to hear. I tell them that I’m lying and they believe it but I burn’em every time.” At this point I look over across the party scene and see a very angry former protégé of mine storming over toward me. The man looks vaguely familiar. Upon looking closer, I remember him now. He was the referee from my first match that I bribed. He got fired a few days later and became a drug addict. Most likely he’s here to kill me so I’d better come up with a plan fast. He looks like he’s been living on the streets. In fact, he looks worse than Malloy. His clothes are all dirty, his beard is dirty, and he looks like he just survived the forest battle scene from Return of the Jedi just to get here. He comes over toward me and starts yelling loudly. “YOU RUINED MY CAREER!” he screams at me. I jump over the couch and start begging off, luring him back toward the bedroom so that it doesn’t disrupt the party anymore than it already has. “I didn’t mean it…” I mutter out as he stalks me into the back hallway as I continue to beg off like a coward to sucker the loser into my trap. “It was a long time ago! I was a different person back then! I’m a nice person now! I love people! I’m a great friend! I always had your best interests in mind!” He continues to stalk me through the hallway as I beg off. In actuality I’m trying to lure him into my bedroom where I can make some sort of a deal with him to get out of this. “YOU’RE NOTHING BUT A DIRTY ROTTEN BASTARD VON DOOM!” he screams out as I crawl into my bedroom with him right on my heels. Once inside the bedroom, I grab my wallet and pull out some cash to bribe him with. “I’ll pay you!” I exclaim. “I’ll do anything! It wasn’t me! It was somebody else who told! I always tried to help you out! I’m your friend! I ALWAYS WAS YOUR FRIEND!” He stops trying to kill me and takes the money out of my hand that I’m bribing him with. “This is just a small amount that you owe me,” he growls out. “I’ll be back for more before I kill you.” He turns and walks out of the room as I continue to act like I’m scared to death. The moment I don’t hear his footsteps anymore, I stand up and brush myself off. I’m not letting a five hundred dollar suit get ruined just because of some jackass that wants to kill me. I quickly pull out my cell phone and speed dial this guy I know named Ricardo. He’s at the party, actually. After two rings, he answers his phone. “Hello Ricardo. It’s von Doom. Listen, you see that guy who smells like pee that just left with a wad of cash? Good, good. Take Raul and Juan with you. Do the usual routine on him. I don’t care about the clean up, just get the money back.” I hang up my cell phone with a smile on my face as I know this problem will be taken care of both swiftly and shortly. At this point I walk back through the hallway and into the party before sitting down back where I was before the maniac tried to beat me up. Malloy and Smith both give me looks. “What was that all about?” Smith asks. “Because, as usual, some crazy shit goes on the moment I get here.” I smile back at him. “Oh nothing. Just TCB - taking care of business.” “Dear lord,” Smith replies, shaking his head as Malloy starts laughing. “I saw that Raul and his buddies left too. I sure hope…” “They’re just helping me out with an old problem from my past in the wrestling world.” “Care to explain?” Smith asks me while giving me a knowing look almost as if to say that he knows what I'm up to. I shake my head in response to this. “Not really. Now, back to what we were talking about before. I got into wrestling for money, fame, women, and a life of luxury. Now that our little discussion is over, let’s go get shitfaced with some hot babes.” "DAMN RIGHT!" Malloy exclaims as all three of us get up and starts mingling with the various babes roaming around my quaint little mansion before anything else happens this evening that involves someone wanting to kill me.
