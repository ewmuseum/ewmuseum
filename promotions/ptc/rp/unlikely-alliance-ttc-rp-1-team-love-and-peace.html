Roulettes_rising Public Access Pimp Joined: 02 Apr 2003 Posts: 15 Location: Mississippi Posted: Fri Apr 11, 2003 5:29 am Post subject: "Unlikely Alliance" TTC rp#1 (Team Love and Peace) "Honor" "When we are born, lain before us is a clean slate...a fresh path tainted not with nobility or shame. As we grow we are taught of right and wrong...taught of religion and truth...we are taught many things as we grow, but above all we are taught of honor. Yes, honor...mortal's way of ranking each individual on the grand scale of life. Those of righteous birth or of wealth and fortune are received high statures of honor among society, while those of tainted background receive hardly a glance. And yet in these less fortunate souls the desire to attain and protect their honor run deep within their being...driving them to rise above the rest whether it be by noble deeds or treachery and schemes, for once the sweet taste of what great stature brings is instilled into a mortal's soul it is impossible to forget. Like a forbidden drug it takes hold of one and be them rich or poor there is no escaping the desire etched into the mind to maintain this state of praise in the eyes of all. For this reason honor is above all the most dangerous of human traits, for once lost it is anyone's guess what one will do to get it back......" Location-unknown Thursday 6:59PM "Once again the familiar surroundings of this nameless estate surround us as it has so many times before, the ornate cherry grandfather clock counting time away tick by deafening tick as the fire consumes the kindling placed before it in its golden trough, throwing its brilliant rays amidst the room casting an eerie glow about the place as it dances across the sturdy mahogany walls and plush cream carpeting. These sounds are disturbed however by yet another overpowering one, each shriek sending chills down one's spine as they continue to erupt from another room not to far away as it echoes past the massive clock down a long hallway framed with paintings and small end tables holding small vases of flowers. However as we descend down this ever darkening path as we move farther and farther from the glowing fire we see all is not right, the frames smashed and broken and the vases overturned, flowers and water cascading in soft waves down into the carpet, vanishing as it is absorbed into it and slowly forgotten as it dries. Deeper still the shrieks grow louder, the sheer pain and anger in them now clearly heard...and then we are there, a room far less elegant than that which we had previously seen, with barren walls and only a mat on an otherwise plain concrete floor it becomes apparent that this must be some sort of training room. In this room lit only by several small candles the bearer of so much pain can be seen as she hammers away at the sandbag placed against the wall. Clad entirely in black the anger can be seen burning in her eyes as she digs her shin into the now worn bag with a well placed kick. With one final cry she digs her fist into the bag with such force that the bag itself rips into, and as it begins to spill its contents across the concrete she sinks into it in exhaustion, sweat pouring from her forehead plastering her crimson bangs to her face. Still she remains motionless, oblivious to it all as the thoughts that haunt her so continue to play through her mind." Weeks earlier "The scene shifts to that of the last DWO PPV. As the crowd exploded in round after round of delight she sat on the cold steel bench in the locker room of white walls and tiles. Clad in her trademark garments eagerly prepared for the upcoming bout with her old foe once more. From atop the corner of the room a wall mounted television set played, the images of the ongoing bouts scrolling by. Watching the matches she chuckled to herself as one by one was decided by demonic entity, simply called Callisto..." Kayna: Look at them all....scampering away like mere animals afraid of the hunter...fear written all over their faces..how pathetic. "Shaking her head she in distaste she got up from the bench and sauntered over to her private locker. Opening it the corners of her mouth turned in a frown as she noticed the smashed mirror still was not fixed, its presence an unpleasant reminder of the loss bestowed to her by this opponent she was to face again. Shaking her head she diverts her attention to the small hand weights sitting in the locker, and once taking them from their resting place she closed the locker door and leaned her back against the cool metal, her attention again drawn to the television as she curls the weights, causing her muscles to contract and relax in time with the motions as she thought aloud." Kayna: Shameful...have they all no morals or honor whatsoever? An honest loss would be more befitting than a tainted victory such as these people receive..... "Her thoughts were interrupted as an all to familiar figure approached through the opened door, sending a draft from the outside flowing through the room causing Kayna's hair to blow softly across her face, her eyes narrowed at this most unwelcome sight. Walking with slow, purposeful steps the hooded menace made his way to his delicious target. Stopping in front of her he looked up at the television as the beautiful beast tore apart yet another victim and declared the spared one the victor. Nodding his head in satisfaction he turned to Kayna and speaks, his voice low in tone." Hooded figure: Beautiful isn't she Kayna? Kayna: I see nothing to admire in her, using her talent to manipulate the Fates. She has no honor, the Wheel of Life shall not favor her in passing judgment. Hooded figure: Of that you speak the truth...the wheel did not favor this soul and banished her to exist on this plane for the rest of her days. But do you see a suffering soul before you dear? No, for instead of succumbing to her fate she has created her own paradise of pain on this existence, and that is why she is beautiful sweet Kayna...a soul who lived as she pleased even after judgement....free from such human emotions as fear and sadness, pain and anguish, knowing only victory, prosperity, and yes even honor. "Spinning around angrily she scowled at him as she spoke, her voice flavored with bitterness." Kayna: How dare you say she has such a noble trait as honor? Hooded figure: If challenging the very ways of life does not merit honor and respect then what does Kayna? Did you think the only way to obtain greatness is through an honest life? Hah...really child..look at your family. The Trielle family was in their peak the most respected family in Vegas. Wealth, power and prosperity are what the people saw when they looked at your family, but of course you know well how that respect was acquired..... Kayna: SHUT UP! "Seeing her livid face and fists that are clenching ever tighter around the small metal weights he let out a long and resounding laugh escaped from within the shadowy void of the hood as he quickly composes himself and speaks once again." Hooded figure: Heh..seriously Kayna you are so easy to manipulate...but before you attack me I suggest you save that strength for your match for it draws near. For as you know, no win means no clues for you...if I were you I would win any way I can...my master grows impatient...hahahaha.... "As he said these words he left as quietly as he came, shutting the door behind him as she slumped to the ground, the weights rolling from her hands, making a metallic thud as they slam into the metal locker doors. Amidst the silence that ensued only one phrase can be heard..." "But....I am not like my father..I have honor, don't I?" Moments later: at the match "We now see the long awaited rematch well underway as both Roulette and Konstantine proceed to explode with action in the ring. As the punches and kicks escalated so did the crowd's intensity as their deafening roars fell on the arena, their gaze transfixed on the site before them. Mike and Jay continue to report the action as it unfolds before them, and then suddenly..all was quiet...the crowd quickly silencing their cries as the see the entrance of this menacing demoness from before, quickly breaking up the action as both fighters look on knowing only one is safe from her wrath, each hoping that one is them. Remembering the words spoken by the hooded figure earlier Roulette simply stared at her as the phrase echoes through her mind." "If challenging the very ways of life does not merit honor and respect then what does dear Kayna?" "I have honor...she is empty.." "Are the two of you so very different Kayna?" "Her attention is quickly snapped back to the current situation as Callisto brutalizes Konstantine, causing mixed feelings to race through her mind at blinding speeds." "I should stop this! It isn't right!" "I need the win...but is this really a victory?" "Is this how all the other's felt? Why they would not attack?" "Or am I just kidding myself into thinking that truth prevails?" Callisto: Wakey wakey dear... "Jerking her head around she sees the beast staring at her, a wicked grin on her face as her eyes burn with excitement. With a sweeping motion of her slender but built arm she points to the downed Konstantine as she speaks." Callisto: Looks like the odds were in your favor today dear...devour the prey I leave before you and claim your victory.... "Walking forward as told like a child following a mother's instructions she walks over to the fallen Konstantine and pinned her, and as the count begins her thoughts continue to race." 1... "This isn't right!" "But I need this!" 2...... "It's all her fault..." "She should've kept her guard up, that's all.." "Yes...that's all..." 3!!! And the stilllll reigning champion...ROOOOUUUUULETTTTTTE! "As Karma slave began to play once more signifying the champion of the match Callisto slowly walked out of the stage, stopping at Roulette's ear for a second. Her lips move as she speaks, her words drowned out by the cheering crowd, but they came through all to clear to Roulette..and then her lips stop and she resumes her walk down from the ring and out the exitway. But as the champ is left with only her crowd and her battered opponent what was said was the only thing on her mind..." "You innocent ones are so easily swayed...I'll convert you yet lil lady luck..." Kayna: YOU WON'T DO THIS TO ME!! I WON'T LOSE MY HONOR TO THE LIKES OF YOU!! "Whoa! Chill out girl!" "Quickly she gets up and dusts the spilled sand off her black shorts as she searches the darkened room for the mysterious voice. Finally spotting the shadow she rushes it, tackling it to the ground only to be taken aback by the familiar laughter. Flipping the nearby light switch she sees it is none other than Kai Shinobi! Staring up at her as she straddled him he let out a sheepish grin as he blew a dark strand of his hair away and spoke." Kai: Well well, is this how you greet all your guests? "Noticing his gaze fall upon her chest just above him her cheeks turns deep crimson as she jumps off of him and hugs herself to hide from his gaze. After letting him get to his feet she hauls back with a mighty slap. He jumps around in pain as a red handprint can be plainly seen on his left cheek. Finally calming down he speaks again." Kai: OW! OKAY, wrong approach! Kayna: What are you doing here you pervert?! Kai: Aww you don't need to flatter me. I just came to ask for your help but... "Looking around the trashed room he scratches in head before finishing his sentence." Kai: ..you seem to be busy. Spring cleaning perhaps? OW! "He wails as another red mark can be seen forming on his cheek right cheek this time. With an exasperated look on her face she looks at him and the conversation resumes." Kayna: Tell me what you're doing here NOW! Otherwise I'll throw you out! "Raising his hand and forming the peace symbol he winks at her and resumes." Kai: Love and peace ok? I just needed a partner for the PTC Tag Team Championships and well I remembered how well you looked.. "Seeing her clenched fist he quickly rambles on." Kai: ...Errr fighting! Looked good fighting! Ha ha ha.... "Receiving no reaction from her whatsoever he drops his had and sighs as a mushroom cloud of air emanates from his mouth." Kai: Seriously I could use a good partner for this match, you fight well and you're the Lightweight Champ so I figured who better? So whaddya say? "Furrowing her brow in contemplation she stares at him a moment before replying" Kayna: Hmmm...I dunno...I mean I would love to and all but well... "Dropping his head he mutters to himself as he turns to leave." Kai: Sure..that's ok...who am I gonna get to fight against Callisto's team now? Kayna: Callisto is gonna be in it? "Sighing even deeper her slumps even farther as he says." Kai: Yeah..of course I suppose I could always ask Surreal.. "Perking up she runs over to him, blocking the door with outstretched arms, her voice suddenly syrupy sweet as she talks to him with a much sweetened attitude." Kayna: Why didn't you tell me?! I would be happy to join you in a tag team...after all you were kind enough to rematch me so it's the least I could do... Kai: Umm no really it's ok... "Throwing out her arm she loops it around his shoulder and begins forcing him to walk forward out of the trashed training room as she continues to talk to him sweetly." Kayna: No no I insist! Here, let's go into a more decent part of the house and discuss the details... Kai: Umm sure..ok.. "As the two chatter excitedly they head off into the inner room closing the door behind them as the scene fades to black with only a single phrase heard from before darkness consumes all..." "For Peace and Love!"
