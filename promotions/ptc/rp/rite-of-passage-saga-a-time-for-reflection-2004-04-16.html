Rite of Passage Saga: A Time For Reflection

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-16 Ethan Knight's eyes rested as his mind wandered through the scope of recent memories. The sights of the outside world passed by swiftly, its clear skies and sun-swept pastures only a blur. The archaic dagger still bound in its black shroud and cradled in his arm. The tip protruded from beneath the copper-tinged lining of the garment, the blade still encrusted with blood. The lieutenant of the Sect of Black Wisdom only lounged in his seat, entertained by his own inner musings and a series of distorted retentions. It would be a gratifying trek back to the manor, one Ethan had elected to savor in its (hopefully) long-term glory. It had been a long time since he had journeyed through the mid-west by car; the thrill of victory in Peoria was the last great memory he had of his tour of the mid-west wrestling circuit. The final cognition of the midwest in the past 8 months before his retreat into the mountains of Vermont...... "Do you wish to stop yet, Brother Knight?" solicited an apathetic voice from the driver's seat. "There are some restaurants off the next exit." The youth wasn't interested in food right now. He had already dined in a sense several hours ago. His blade has feasted on the blood of Jago Sensaki, the Old Ones on the ravaged soul of his offering, and subsequently Knight on the blessings imparted by his unseen lords. He was content with the newfound tidings of his spirit. The grin didn't disperse from his face as he responded with a simple 'no'. The driver, a dark-skinned member of the Kansas City-based Fraternity of the Bearded Serpent, only nodded. The affiliation between the two factions was young, an alliance forged only weeks prior to Ethan's mortal offering, but the bond was already unbreakable; worshippers of the same deities were spiritually bound by the company and alliance of their kind. The worshipper of Byatis was tasked with 'chauffeuring' the highly-ranked Sect member back to the Vermont retreat. "Have you ever seen those that lurk on the other side, Brother Ignalls?" asked Knight cryptically, letting his eyes fall on the exposed part of the weapon. Angled in the rear view mirror, Ethan could see his compatriot purse his lips before meagerly shaking his head. "No, Brother Knight, that is an experience which I am yet to foretake. But I trust someday I will be able to bask in the aura of their divine majesty. Vigilance is the key." Ethan nodded his understanding, the driver's gaze floating to the mirror and the brooding blond-haired man in the back. "And you, Brother Knight? Surely one of Bruce Shanahan's direct advisors has experienced direct fellowship with the Old Ones at one time or another?" "Not yet. But soon. I have been promised my spiritual ascension. And I have done what I need to attain it...." His forefinger lightly brushed over the blade, the fingernail gently peeling the congealed blood. The bruise on his cheek still ached and his left tricep throbbed, but it was a fleeting sensation in light of what it all had reaped him. "What do you know of the Blackhelm Coalition, Brother Knight?" inquired Ignalls, his demeanor composed but curious. "From what I understand of their ranks, their brotherhood will be rather displeased with what you have perpetrated on one of their own." Bruce had warned Ethan of such inevitability. In time, he was sure Craven, Stryker, and the rest of the Blackhelm Coalition would seek his head. But for now, there was still tranquility. That matter would be tended to when the time arrived. "I knew the risk when I agreed to the task, Brother Ignalls," affirmed the officer of the Sect. "I have no problem trifling with some ultimately powerless secret society if it helps progress the big picture." Knight leaned his head back, vision dwindling as his eyelids closed. It had been a long night. He had seen his share of trials for a couple days. "Wake me around the Ohio line, Brother Ignalls," he requested in a tone wracked with exhaustion. "I need rest. If you wish to stop somewhere, feel free to do so...." He was half unconscious before the reply was even complete. * * * * * One so twisted and depraved cannot be permitted to attempt to take this world for his beliefs. His sacrifice will have been made in vain... none can destroy me with the powers of heaven on my side. The words rang in Ethan's head as he drifted into an unsettled slumber, pondered over with a sense of grotesque glee as similar questions entered his head from earlier in the Nebraska cabin. The idea still hadn't completely left his mind. Maybe his antagonist really was a deity descended to Earth. Maybe he did intend on smiting Knight and his brethren for their dark inhibitions. But intent is a far cry from execution. The Earthly plane isn't one of fairy tales or incarnated dreams. Heaven and Earth are divided by more than just the keys of spiritual access. One is Utopia. The other is a fractured spectrum of ever-mingling polar opposites. This madman, Raijin, wasn't in his divine Valhalla of gold-enshrined steeples and rivers of crystal healing water. And as far as Ethan was concerned, he was unwelcome in this domain. He was a visiting prophet of false pretence and nothing more. This was the yet-unclaimed kingdom of the Great Old Ones, not a province of weak and ideal-infested immortals. If light didn't exist, nor would darkness. If darkness didn't subsist, light would be extinguished. There was equilibrium in every element of creation. Raijin and Ethan Knight both seemed intent on altering that scheme, warring on behalf of two opposing sides to swing the pendulum in their favor. Raijin had professed to wanting only to maintain the balance of yin and yang, but Knight was avowed to have seen the lies of Heaven in the past; Shanahan's subordinate felt the 'kami's' intentions were drastically different, just fiendishly veiled as opposed to his own openly-admitted ends. Nothing of his ideals made sense....... With that transition into mortality, Knight had sensed something in the proclaimed god of Heaven upon his return. Hatred, darkness. Surely, in the light of the situation, this was a thing of beauty. A god of Heaven was imbued with the presence of hatred. One who couldn't see the irony of a divine, righteous agent struck with the mortal notion of malignity was blind. Broadcast for all to hear, and lost in the astral realm, Knight basked in the hypocrisy of the prophet's conclusive logic. The traditional faith of those who believe in me, in us, holds that there is no sin, only evil deeds which tarnish the soul...... Striking down a foe with hatred in one's heart, Ethan recalled, was an evil deed. Anger was normal. Anger turning to hatred was sin. Acting upon that sin was an evil deed, unless the heretic Japanese denied his own gospel code. It wasn't on the conscience of Brother Knight if a Heaven-sent god opted to ignore the doctrine of his dominion and essentially violate every law in the proverbial Heavenly rulebook. Could the God of Lightning, this new-age redeemer..... .....could he actually be cast down like Lucifier, a being akin to a rebellious angel? A false prophet of of infernal tidings perhaps? Bah, the wretched snake. Yet another deceiver borne of human ignorance. That fool had already been upended by the female Coricidon and mercifully granted another chance; surely he didn't believe he had the power to overtake Ethan Knight with what strength now consumed him. The Heavens had instructed him well. He was a thoroughy brainwashed drone just like the rest of those celestial flunkeys. .....and my opponent, a disciple of a false religion. The blasphemer would be silenced once and for all. And the 'heathen' Ethan Knight would be his iconoclast. * * * * * ......I can't wait to be blessed by the energies and magicks of the ethereal realm. Through my body now courses the power to surmount the strength of a fallen god, an act which will further progression toward that penultimate objective. What they deem cruel, ghoulish, and insane I instead call the appropriate road to evolution. No wretched Earthly immortals will save the charlatan from his appointment with the reaper. He may blaspheme his own domain and his own celestial brotherhood, but sacrilege of the Old Ones will not be tolerated. Screaming back into Heaven he will not. For when I am finished with the fallen one, he will be sent careening into the abyss where he belongs. And the court of the daemon-sultan Azathoth shall revel in its latest acquisition. I am among the betrothed of the darkness, beloved of the abyss. I shall help maintain the universal balance, and when the time is right, shift it to the favor of the Old Ones and the Outer Gods. The day will come when Great Cthulhu rampages over mountains and plains to overtake a fear-engulfed world, and Nyarlathotep helps send mankind spiraling into self-imposed oblivion by courtesy of its own evils. That is the way of the truth, as painful as it may be. After all, in the great scheme of things, comfort and truth cannot simultaneously exist. The truth of the cosmos isn't as pleasant as 'some' ancient books would have one believe. Insurgents will be dealt with whether the false god likes it or not. Raijin seeks honor through mortality. I seeks progress through ascension. Paths radically different but due to cross through a conflict of crowning ambition. When he looks into my eyes, into my very essence, he will see his purgatory personified. And his weakness will be further punished, just as those of Heaven shall be when the prophecies come to fruition. The Sect of Black Wisdom will become a name synonymous with pain. And Ethan Knight will be a title synonymous with power. Divinity shall prevail. But it shall be the divinity of the infernal. Blessed are we, the damned......
