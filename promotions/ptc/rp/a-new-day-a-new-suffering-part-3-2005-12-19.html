A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 3

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 The night was clear as he got out of the car and checked his watch. He was a few minutes late, but it didn’t matter, he was the boss. The moon was full and it was so bright that the street lights were not really needed. The gentle December breeze made him shiver a little since he was not accustomed to it being so cold in Puerto Rico, unless he was on the mountains. He wasn’t right now. In fact, he as in the middle of the metropolitan area. If you wanted to have a successful business you had to be in this area. Here is where all the action occurs. He walked towards his establishment. The faint sound of music could be heard coming from it. This pub was the best inversion he had made yet. This was a place where people could go have a drink, meet new people and listen to the best bands in the scene. Digital was not there all the time, but when he was, he ran anything that had to do with the place. When he was on the road, Burton took care of the place. He walked up the stairs and into the second floor of the two-story building, He opened the door and the aroma of booze and smoke overpowered him. He hadn’t been here in a few months and he would be leaving again soon to participate in the round two of the tournament of all tournaments. He went inside, surprised that the bouncer was not outside yet. It was early though, so that didn’t matter. The place wasn’t completely empty and soon he saw that the bouncer was forcing some fool out of the place. It seemed that he had thrown a bottle of beer to the floor and broken it on purpose. Digital had told Nick to not take any bullshit from customers. If they gave him any shit, he could kick their asses to the curb. Sheila was there cleaning the mess that had been made, it would be worse if some idiot got cut on the broken glass. The last thing Digital wanted was a nice lawsuit on his hands. He went over to her. It took her a moment to finally finish cleaning the mess and after that, she noticed the man that stood before her. It had been a long time since they had seen each other. They had been pals all through college and when he opened his place, he knew just who to call to work as a bartender. She went over to him and hugged him. She had heard what was happening to Veronica. "Are you okay?" "Yeah. Fine. How are you doing? How has the place been running the past few weeks?" He asked her because Burton had told him he was not going that night. So he needed to get all the info he could get to properly run the place and make some important decisions that had to be made. "It has been smooth as butter the last few weeks. A few days when there is not a lot of people, but most of the times it gets pretty full when we have a guest band playing. We made a little experiment with a hardcore band, but it was a mess. Kids these days are retarded, man. Poor Nick had to kick about five of them out. It didn’t work. This place was overrun with way too many volatile hardcore kids. The regular crowd was so disgusted that they almost waited for them outside to give them a beating. I think we can safely say that no hardcore band will ever play here." She said it with relief. She, like most of Digital’s friends of the scene hated hardcore. I think they hated more the attitude than the music they played. They had that ‘I am better than you’ attitude even though they couldn’t play themselves out of a cardboard box. Digital hated people like that in all walks of life. And they were all through the wrestling business. "Good. The other thing I wanted to talk to you about is Christian. Is he here yet?" "No, not yet. Are you going to talk to him? He has been very somber these past few days. After the incident he just seems to have gone to a very different place from where he was. He seems more agitated and quick to snap at you for anything." Digital sighed. He knew that it was hard to go through all that, but if it affected your work, then something had to be done. He was not going to fire one of the best guitarists he had heard in the island and he was also Veronica’s brother. He was also the one person that had been there to protect Veronica, her mother and her sister from the wrath of his father. He owed him that. "Okay, as soon as he arrives, tell him to come by the office." "Sure thing." Digital moved towards the stage where the band was fixing their equipment. He shook hands with the other members of the band and moved towards a door located to the left of the stage. He opened the door and walked down the corridor into his office. He took off his jacket and his tie. He hung them on the wall. He went to his chair and sat down. He lied back and thought of how his conversation with Christian would be like. Digital had to tell him his father’s problems as well as try and make him understand that he didn’t want to end up in the same position as his father. It was hard, to hear these things from someone that is not really family…yet, but he had seen first hand how bad the problem was and knew that they had to fix it, if not for Vero’s sake, for the sake of their little sister. Digital started to look at all the papers in his desk and looking to see if Burton had been up to anything. He saw a bill, it was huge. Digital looked at it more closely and couldn’t figure out what it was, but it was for a fair amount. "What the hell? What would he be buying without my permission?" At that moment there was a knock on the door. His time as Robert Langdon was over and it was now straight to business. "It’s open, come in." The door opened and in walked Christian, the man Digital wanted to see. He shook hands with Digital and sat down in the chair opposite Digital. He could see the sadness and the pain in the man’s eyes. A lot of things can be discovered just by looking into the eyes of a person. Digital could see the pain that all these problems had been causing him and his hatred for his father. "How are you doing, Christian?" "Well, you know… pretty well. I’m sure Veronica has been keeping you informed of all that has been happening." "Yes, she has. How is your sister doing? Is she back up and walking about?" Digital knew that this is what Christian was most worried about. He had always been very protective of his sisters and would never want anything to happen to them. He would put his life on the line in order to be able to see his sisters thriving in their lives. They were more important to him than anything. He had also always protected them from his father’s "tyrannical reign" as he liked to call it. "She has started to walk again and is in a much better mood. I, on the other hand, am pissed off. Pissed at my father for being a monster who doesn’t think before acting. Doesn’t realize what his actions might have caused in all our lives. He doesn’t deserve to be a part of that church of his and he doesn’t deserve us." Digital had to try and reason with Christian. It was not easy to convince someone that had been haunted by his father since he was a child. He was never abused physically, but the burden of being the preacher’s son had been hard to carry. "You have to understand Christian; your father is not in the right state of mind. He is delusional. He needs psychological or psychiatric help. You know that he would never put his hands on anyone if he hadn’t been drunk. Your father is not a bad man. He just has had different priorities in his life. I know how that can be like." Christian’s eyes were burning red. Digital knew that he had hit the nail in the head when it came to Alberto and his relationship with his children, especially Christian. There was nothing else to do though; Digital had to make Chris understand that his father needed him, that his father was in as much pain as he was. "I spoke to him the other day. Vero and I went to see him in San Juan. Look, he thinks that he was having a discussion with your mom and that suddenly you were trying to kill him." "Carajo! El todavía no entiende cuanto su mierda de iglesia nos ha jodido la vida a nosotros. He still doesn’t realize all the bullshit that I had to go through because I was his son. Do you know the expectations that were put upon me all the time? He wanted me to be his follower instead of his son, he wanted to be my mentor and guide instead of my father. It was always, you can’t do this, you can’t do that…" It was obvious that being a preacher’s son must have been pretty hard. The feeling that anything you did would be disapproved by your father but worse than that was disappointing him in the eyes of the parishioners. The dreaded ‘What would they say about me if you did that?’ It hung over them all. Chris found music and was good at playing guitar, very good, but his father didn’t approve of the music that Chris liked. He would go into his room and burn all his CDs. He even had an exorcism done on his room. All of those things put together make for one mad son. Chris felt like his life didn’t mean anything to his father like his sisters were the only ones his father cared about. This was not really true. Alberto just didn’t show his love for his son because he thought that he was preparing him for the hard life of a man of faith. "Chris, I wouldn’t know how it feels like to be the son of a father as strict as that. My parents were mostly traveling for business. You have to understand that your father does love you; he just has to understand that you are not going to walk the same steps as him. He has to understand that you are a very different person, and he has to learn to accept you for who you are. He can’t do any of these things if he still believes that you tried to kill him. As long as he has that vision, that delusion in his mind, we won’t be able to move anywhere." This was true. Both of them had to make amends. Both of them had to stand up to the consequences of what they had done. If it wasn’t an effort from the both of them, then it would never work. "Why are you doing this Antonio? Why do you care so much?" Digital thought about it. He knew why, but he didn’t know if he should tell Chris about it yet. It was supposed to be a secret. Not even Burton knew about it yet. It was hard lying to a friend like Chris, but he had to do it. "Because it’s you and because it’s Vero. I can’t stand and let this all happen without trying to help you guys out. I can’t stand to watch Vero crying, it tears me apart." The real reason was much deeper than that. "You are a good guy Antonio. Thanks for all your help." He went to the door and closed it behind him. Digital sighed. This was going to take more than a few talks to set straight. It was going to take some very radical changes in the lives of more than one person in this family in order for it to actually work. It would be hard, but it could be done. Why was Digital so interested in this? It wasn’t only because Vero was the most important person in his life, but because he planned on being with her for a long time. She was everything to him. His job, the fame, the money, the tournaments, the title belts, they all came second to her. That is why this was so important to him. The battle he had to go through seemed unimportant when he looked at it from this angle. Hoyt Williams would be the toughest challenge he would face in the tournament, but he would go in there and do his best. He would put on a great show and if he played his cards right, he would succeed. If not, then he would go back to the drawing board like always. But this time he had something to go back to. This time he wouldn't go home to silence, to loneliness. He felt like he could conquer the world with Vero at his side. He had big plans for their life. He had already asked her to completely move into his house, and that was a big step. Digital had been adamant about it at first because he remembered the last time that he had fallen deeply in love with a person. He remembered the pain and the suffering he had to go through when the accident happened. The feeling of loss had been almost unbearable to sustain. He lived though. He was able to put that pain and that torment and put it onto others. He used it in the ring to be able to survive in it. The walls that he had built around himself had been tough. Strong enough to let him continue his life. Only one person had been able to crack those walls, and eventually destroy them. Vero had taken the walls down and made Digital’s life. She had taken a broken shell of a man that he was and filled it with a longing for life. Knowing that it could all be taken away in the time it took to snap your fingers. They hadn’t been going out together for that long, and they had never spoken about it, but deep in his heart, Digital knew that he loved her. Did she love him as much as he loved her? He would never know unless he asked. He would never know unless he dared to go to the next step. When Digital was young, he had not been good with women, and he had met this wonderful girl that made him feel complete. He had a chance to be with that girl, and be happy. He was scared though, he was scared of what might have happened if he did ‘go for it’. His opportunity went by and he never got the chance. The same had happened with the late Justine. The same wouldn’t happen with Veronica. It was time. It was time for him to actually do that one thing that everyone expects, that one act that cements a relationship. Maybe that act would be the light in the tempest that is her life right now.
