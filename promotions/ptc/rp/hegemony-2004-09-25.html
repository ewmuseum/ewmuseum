Hegemony

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 It’s all I keep hearing. Over and over and over and over and over and… well… you get the point. “Doesn’t stand a chance.” “Desolation, who?” “Not even close.” Why should I even bother showing up? It’s a forgone conclusion, isn’t it? I went out in the first round. How will the second round be any different? … That’s what a lot of people would like you to believe, Eugene. That I would give up. I would see the insurmountable hurdle in front of me, and I would just give up. But that’s not me. I don’t give in to… to… the hegemony. … That’s just what this tournament has become, hasn’t it? A hegemony. The influence. The dominant ones who ignore… disregard… snub their opponent until the match comes, and then annihilate them with ease. “Eugene? You’ll be in the final three. The final six, for sure. You’re too, good. No one else stands a chance.” … Don’t say that, Eugene. You know it’s not true. It’s rubbish. It’s all rubbish. And you know it. … Don’t play dumb with me. I showed everyone in the first round what I was capable of. Before the tournament even started, it was all “Jay Phoenix this” and “Jay Phoenix that”. But, nearly beating the everlasting Phoenix must have impressed someone, right? I wouldn’t still be here if it didn’t. … I AM GOOD! Can’t you see that, Eugene? Its the hegemony. The elite. Eugene, you’re a part of it, whether you like it or not. Phoenix is one of them, too… although, somebody is probably questioning his position as tournament favorite, now. But what about the subjugated. What about me? I almost beat a wrestling legend, but nothing changes. That’s the hegemony, for you. Holding down the nobodies… the unknowns. … Eugene, dear Eugene. Your colossal girth, your callous rage, and your sadistic mother should instantly make you a favorite. But the hegemony… that’s what makes you unstoppable. Heh… that must be where you got the nickname, huh? “Unstoppable” Eugene. … You bastard! You don’t even know how good you have it. To have the hegemony… on your side… it must be great. … I knew you’d say something like that. But, it’s not fair, dammit! It’s not fair! You don’t even know how good you have it… you… you… … Okay. I’m better now. Sorry about that… … No, I’m not mad at you. It’s… it’s… the hegemony. It’s just so unfair, is all. But, I’m not mad at you. How could any get angry with someone as… special… as you? … Yes you are. The hegemony wouldn’t have you if you weren’t special. … No… no. That’s not what I meant. Being special is a good thing. … Yes, the hegemony is bad, but that doesn’t make you… … You’re not getting it… … That’s not true… … I am not… … How could… … SHUT YOUR MOUTH, YOU FUCKING RETARD! … Sorry. I did it again. Pay no attention to my… emotional outbreaks. I just get angry. The damn hegemony. They’ve got me all… wound up. Can’t you see that Eugene? Eugene? (That’s when I realized Eugene had left. Or had he been there in the first place? )
