Don't fear the Clowns

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 ::Well the confidence is finally back after advancing to the second round of the GTT4 tournament, even if it was cause his partner resigned from the tournament. But that doesn’t matter to Scottywood because just like everyone else, he knows that even if the match went through that he would have pulled off the win. But this week will not be as easy, this weeks he faces one of the odder members of WWA……then again all the member of WWA are pretty odd. But he faces off against a man called Muppet Man, who wears a clown’s mask. Now as a kid Scotty was terrified of clowns, it was something about the big red nose and the crazy laughing that just made him freak out. But he has long grown out of that fear, I think it was when he knocked out the clown at a kid’s birthday party, but then again what kind of 15 year old kid has a clown at his birthday party. I’ll tell ya what kind, the kind that also have pin the tail on the donkey. But atlas Scottywood is going to actually step into the ring this time and it will be one hell of a fight.:: ::We see Scottywood sitting inside his hotel room with long time friend Ice who is staring out the 15th floor window. Scottywood keeps shaking his head as he is looking at the piece of paper with the 2nd round matches for GTT4.:: Scottywood: A clown….I am fighting a clown. I mean I have fought Calvin Riley, Tony Weston and Robb Hardy…..but they at least didn’t wear a mask. I mean what the hell is wrong with this guy? Does he come out to the ring in one of those really small cars that has like 10 guys packed in them….and that makes he think, what kind of sick when wanna be cramped in a small car like that together? Ice: I think you need to concentrate less on the clown aspect of Muppet Man and more on the wrestling part of him. Like how to counter his moves or predict what he is gonna do next. Scottywood: Well I can’t help it, this Doink wannabe just brings it on himself by wearing that mask. I mean what next? Is he gonna start making ballon animals in the middle of the match? Or will he try and paint a smile face on my cheek. This guy needs to seriously grow up and ditch the clown mask because nobody is gonna take a man in a clown mask seriously. I almost crack up everytime I see his face. Ice: Well I do have to admit he should loose the mask but that isn’t your problem. Now do you even know what this guys finisher is? Scottywood: Nope and I could care less. I don’t need to know anything about my opponent beforehand. Because once I get into that ring and the bell rings I go on pure instinct. Knowing his finisher is not gonna help me. Ice: Well that is one of your weak points, when you start getting cocky you take everything for granted and then you wonder why you suck. Its because your not putting any effort into your matches. You put more effort into playing Day of Defeat on that damn laptop. Scottywood: Well excuse me if I have a hobby. I find it fun killing guys on the computer, unless you’d rather have me do it in the ring? Ice: No, you don’t need anymore lawsuits against you, like that Walmart one. Scottywood: Don’t bring that up again. You loose your balance for one second and knock 3 shelves over and suddenly 2 people are suing you. Ice: Sure, if loosing your balance means chasing a man down the aisle for the last video game. Scottywood: Hey GTA Vice City was damn well worth it. ::Well it seems that Scotty’s confidence is way higher then last week, to almost the point of being too cocky. But that is Scottywood, he thinks he is invincible and it seems on some nights that he is. I may be a secret of his that he says it enough times then it will come true, or he just may be strong willed. Whatever the cause, like always he will give it everything he has got in that ring.:: Scottywood: Hey Ice, I wonder if I brings some strings to the ring that I’ll be able to control his every movement. Or I do wonder if he is related to Kermet the Frog, or his Father was Bozo the clown. Ice: Aren’t you tired of clown jokes? You have been making them all hour. Scottywood: Nah, I doubt this could ever get old. But I’ll give it a rest, don’t wanna disturb you while you look out the window at nothing. Ice: I find it peaceful and it distracts me some your sub par jokes. Scottywood: Bah! You never did have a good taste in humor. Anyhow I gotta head to the store, pick up some shit. I’ll see ya at the arena. Ice: Will do. Scottywood: Oh ya, gonna be a great match, Scottywood beats up a clown, this is gonna be a classic. Lol ::Scottywood gets up from the bed, grabs his keys and walks out the apartment room::
