Emerald-Eye: Double-Oh Dear pt. 2

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 I was told to meet her in this local pub. Apparently it’s a terrorist hotspot now that this guy has plans to take over the world with them. I just hope that poor Ducky is okay… I mean, I don’t know what the bloke might do to extract the chip from him. He could rip him apart or something! I don’t think poor Ducky has the strength to fight back! Oh that and he can’t move at all anyway… God knows why she wants to meet here. It’s worse than the last Thai bar we met in. And they don’t have exotic jugglers in here. Not after the country’s greatest juggler got horrifically stabbed by some monkey with an emerald in his eye. Bellows of smoke from old Cuban cigars fill the room, smoked by a bunch of Thai executives to show their colleagues who the most flash bastard is about the place. This is hardly a trendy wine bar though, it’s just some place in the middle of the city. I suppose these are the wannabe execs. I walk over to the bar and sit myself down on the steadiest looking stool. Most of them look like they’re going to fall apart the moment you sit down, but riding my luck with this one, I think I’m okay. “’Ello…?” I shout, wondering where the barman or barmaid has gotten to… nothing. “OI…!” I scream at the top of my lungs, “Can I have some flaming service please?!” “Yes sir…” A silent, squeaky female voice is heard from in front of me. I have no idea where this person is though! It’s almost as if the barmaid is invisible! Oh my God! A ghost pub! “Can I- I… ha-have a b-b-beer please…?” I say stuttering in fear of the ghost barmaid. “Sure…” A few moments pass and then out of nowhere a bottle of beer plonks down on the counter, spilling a few drops into my face. I resist screaming like a possessed schoolgirl for that moment. I don’t want to make a fuss, at least not when I’m a secret agent now and have to keep things hush-hush. Then, from under the counter, a flaming hand comes up at me! I nearly fall off my seat and have to shuffle to stop me from falling backwards! Oh! Damn, I forgot these Thai people were so flaming small! Her head doesn’t even come above the counter! That’s why I couldn’t see her! I reach into my pocket and pull out some small change to pay for my beer and turn to have a look around the pub for suspicious customers. Just some average Joes make up the place. Well, average Jin’s or whatever flaming names they have over here: A guy with a shredded brown leather coat smoking a Cuban cigar drinking with a well-dressed lady in black trousers, white shirt and black overcoat; Two younger guys in black shirts and denim jeans laughing and joking; a group of businessmen in their suits and ties talking about the stock market probably, or whatever; a guy in a white overcoat with blonde hair sticking out in all directions and an emerald for an eye talking to a midget in a striped two-tone green waistcoat holding a fishing rod and screaming about his lucky charms. You know just regular people. Hey wait a minute! “Dave…” Ah, it’s Uma, she’s arrived at last, “Alo!” Uma is looking amazing, straight blonde hair swaying about in wind that isn’t there. Oh yeah, there’s a fan next to her. Her eyes gleaming like two blue jewels (it’s anyone’s guess what jewel THAT could be…) even in the dim lights of the bar. She’s wearing a figure-hugging red full body PVC thing, showing all her womanly curves… … … Sorry, I was miles away! “Can I have a small gin and tonic please?” Uma asks the barmaid under the counter. A few seconds later a glass appears from under the counter full of G&T, the lady’s hand takes Uma’s change and then disappears once again. “Okay,” Uma says before taking a quick sip of the drink, “here’s the plan. This is probably going to be the first pub under attack by Emerald-Eye’s minions. You see that little midget leprechaun? That’s the original Smurphy, which will be used by Emerald-Eye to clone an army of Smurphy robots. We need to stop them before this happens.” “How the flaming hell are we supposed to stop them? That blonde bloke still has the samurai sword from before!” I say sternly to Uma, she looks back to me with a blank face. “Aren’t you supposed to be a rough tough wrestler?” She says to me patronisingly, “well go wrestle him or something!” “But he has a sword!” I scream back. “How the flaming hell am I supposed to wrestle a bloke with a sword for Christ’s sake!” “Okay fine, if I get rid of his sword, THEN will you use your wrestling skills to stop him?” She says angrily. If she can get the sword off this bloke, surely she could take him out herself as well, but if this is what I’ve got to do, then fair enough. “Okay, fine I’ll do it,” I say back to the moody cow and with that she leaves the bar and walks over to the table that Emerald-Eye is sitting at. I scan the table for a second. Damn, no sign of Ducky. They must be hiding him in the secret lab or something. Uma is really brave going over there to fight Emerald-Eye for his sword and all that, she’s approaching him right now. But she seems pretty relaxed in all honesty. She goes to him and whispers something into his ear. God knows what, but then the two embrace each other and trade a very long and sloppy kiss, ugh. What is she doing? She’s supposed to be kicking his arse! Oh wait… I see now! She’s taking the sword from his hand! Excellent! And he doesn’t even realise. They finish their long sloppy icky kiss and she gives him an alluring wink and wave before heading back towards the bar. I don’t know how she does it… she’s amazing! She walks over to my stool, drops the sword by my feet and says, “There you go, now go kick his ass.” Wow, the lengths that she’ll go through for me and for her mission, it’s incredible. Well, I suppose it’s my turn then. I get up, straighten my jumper and head towards the tall poofy blonde guy. My heart is racing, if this guy so easily brushed away my sword last night, he might be able to do the same now. It’s too late to think about that now, just hit the sod in the face and let’s get this over and done with. I stand behind the guy, puff out my chest and try and look as intimidating as a 6’2” cruiserweight can and wait for him to notice the shadow looming over him. He takes a sip of his beer, tilts his head to his side and says, “Excuse me, but you’re being awfully rude. Care to bugger off, old chap?” HE IS A POOF! I knew it! I take my arm around the guy’s neck, yanking him clean off the chair. No muppet with a bit of fencing skills is going to get the better of me, and no bloody poof is going to take away my Ducky! The guy comes clean off of the chair and falls to the floor on his back. “Ow! Please, I don’t want any trouble!” The poofter says as I stand over him, the intent to destroy showing clearly on my face. This bastard is going to pay. I drive a knee right into the guy’s throat, putting as much weight into it as possible so the guy can barely breathe. I’m going to choke the living daylights out of this geezer for what he did to Beatrix, and for taking my poor Ducky from me. “Dave…” comes a voice from behind me, almost alarmed at the ferociousness that I’ve just shown to this bloke, “you’re not going to kill him are you?” “Sorry Uma love,” I reply, the seriousness clear in my voice, “I think you may want to look away,” “Stop!” she pleads, god knows why she would want me to let this person live after all he’s done, I thought us secret agents were supposed to have a Licence To Kill and all that, bloody bureaucracy. “Please don’t kill him!” Uma pleads again, “erm… if you kill him, how will we know where Ducky is?!” She makes a good point. If I kill this bloke here and now then there won’t be any chance of me getting my Ducky back. “Scumbag…!” I scream at the scumbag, “Where’s my Ducky?!” He struggles for air, clutching his throat as if he’s about to choke to death. Oh shit yeah, he can’t speak with my knee in his throat can he? I take the pressure off of him for one second, giving him the opportunity to speak. Oh yeah, to breathe as well. “-Gasp- He’s –gasp- in –gasp- the –gasp- bag –gasp- under –gasp- the –gasp- table.” Blimey matey, we ain’t got all day! Excellent, so he’s under the table in a bag. It seems a bit strange that the guy would bring the key to taking over the entire world in a duffle bag, but there you go. I reach my arm under the table and pull out the bag. Looking over at the man, he doesn’t look particularly nervous as if the duck wasn’t in there. So I open it up, and pull out the contents. Low and behold the content of the bag consists of a plastic yellow duck. The world is saved. That was easy enough, wasn’t it? I must be pretty good at this saving the world lark! “My hero!” shrieks Uma, oh she’s changed her tune now that I’ve saved the world, eh? Although it didn’t seem the most sincere of ‘hero’ calls, I’m willing to accept the title of hero of the world. “Hero eh…?” I say to Uma with a wink. “You’ve saved the world Dave, how could I ever repay you?” she says, and as the notion starts to run through my head, she heads over to me, places one hand on my shoulder and whispers into my ear, “how about I show you how I can repay you?” “You what…?” I reply, no idea what she’s going on about. “I have something for you… in the spare room…” “Ooh! Goody!” I scream in delight. I wonder what she could have for me, some jellybeans perhaps. -----=====|=====----- She leads me off to a corridor in the house and it leads to a room, with an aptly named sign above it saying ‘Spare Room’. I think this might be the room she’s talking about! I can’t wait for my prezzie! She pushes the door open and we head inside, it’s basically just a bed, there’s not too much room for anything else. I still haven’t a clue what my prezzie will be. “Why don’t you make yourself comfortable on the bed hun, and I’ll get your presents out for you.” She says. Presents, as in plural?! Oh my! I’m a lucky boy today! I leap onto the bed and lie down, eyes wide open taking the moment in, ready for my special treat. She turns away from me, and starts to take her hands behind her back. I don’t know what she’s doing. She couldn’t have kept my presents in there! She takes the zip at the top of her PVC suit and gives it a tug, in one swift motion she pulls the zip all the way down! She steps out of her suit and turns around, with only a sweaty pair of panties and two hands covering any of her assets! “For saving the world Dave… here is your prize…” Okay. Stop right there. Now I know some people like to have roleplays with jizz-soaked women and stuff like that, but you won’t get it here people! So I will spare you the gruesome details and skip to the morning, alright? -----=====|=====----- I’m awoken by an unusual sound. It sounds like smashing, then a bit of thumping and a few rounds of clanging. It’s too dark to actually see what’s going on, but I can hear Uma screaming something about money… “Damn doesn’t this guy have ANY money?” is what she said, I’m pretty sure of that. I wonder what guy she’s going on about. Either way she’s heading to the door now, and has slammed it shut. Someone’s in a strop! Anyway, I might as well get up now. It’s probably barely even morning yet, but I’ve been woken up so I might as well make a move. Getting out of bed and switching on the light, I notice a flyer sitting on the side of the bed. I wonder what it says… ‘Emerald’s Sexcapades Erotic Adventures from the Finest Women Around 0-800-555-321’ Damn, she was a flaming hooker! The lengths those bloody slappers go to, to get some cash nowadays. It’s nigh on ridiculous! THE END AND STUFF…
