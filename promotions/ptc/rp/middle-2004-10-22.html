Middle.

22 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-22 Normally when a woman hung up on him after not letting him get a word in, he wouldn't be smiling. But with differing circumstances came differing reactions. And for Grant Wilson, he hadn't just got himself a date, he may have just got himself a footing on the first rung of his wrestling career's ladder. Perched on the end of a single bed, in a room that seemingly had everything he'd ever need to survive packed into it, Grant couldn't help but feel the chance meeting earlier that day may turn out to be one of the most important moments of his life. Having struggled to even get so much as a dark match at any of the bigger regional promotions, and with no friends in the business to give him the rub, Grant was left working at Pizza Hut and taking whatever measly bookings he could get to make his extortionate rent. So, as soon as Karina's hanging up had freed the line, he hit #1 on his speed dial. "Steve, I need a favour," he reeled off before the male answering the call could finish his greeting. "I need you to do a 'net search for me." A muffled sigh came back down the phone-line from his friend. "I am SO not downloading anymore porn for you, man," was the reply. "Last time it took me a week to convince Jessica it was for you... and I didn't exactly hear you stepping in to defend me." "No, it's not porn," Grant hissed back. "I need you to google Karina Wolfenden for me." "Who the hell is Karina Wolfenden?" his curious, and oblivious soon-to-be co-conspirator questioned. "That chick I met in the gym today," Grant explained. "Isn't it a bit early to start e-stalking her?" a confused Steve enquired, jokingly. "I'm not up on all this internet etiquette, but you're sounding like those weirdoes you used to e-fed with..." "Can you stop screwing around and do the search?" a frustrated Grant imposed, while some fidgeting from the other end of the line made it sound like his demand was being met. "Fine," came a grumble. "What was the name again?" "Karina Wolfenden... e-n-d-e-n." Collapsing back onto the limp mattress, Grant waited for the results to filter back to him. He'd read the internet dirt sheets whenever he had access. He knew HHH was where he was because of Stephanie, he knew that fWo's Aimz and Brand Frontier were married off camera too, and that a word from a friend into the right ear had won many a new star a deal. "Right," Steve announced. "Have a tonne of crap which I don't understand a word of, so I'll just reel it off and you tell me when to move on." "Okay," Grant impatiently agreed. "This is on my phone bill, so hurry it up." "First one is some RPW Adrenaline recap thing on 411," Steve began. "Supposedly she beat some guy named Invincible Ga-" "Next." "Alrighty," Steve acknowledged. "Number two is something about a PTC Cruiserweight Tourney, lost to a guy named Almasy in the fin-" "Next." "Third one is about a GTT thing," Steve tried his best to explain. "Karina Wolfenden versus Johnny Lexicon in the fou-" "Next." "Okay..." he replied while loading up the next mention of Karina's name. "We have something about the hottest free agents on the Indy scene..." "Anything interesting?" Grant perked up with, rising from his slouched position with a creak of the old bed's springs. "Stuff about a guy named Sebastian... Angelo Deville... Ah, Karina," Steve mumbled while filtering through the page. "It says, aside from her participation in the 4th annual GTT, many are surprised that the popular performer hasn't debuted in another promotion since the closing of RUSH Pro Wrestling. Some members of the now-defunct RPW have gone to the much acclaimed Squared Circle, and given that she is rumoured to be a close friend of the Circle's owner's cousin, top wrestler Coral Avalon, many expect her to follow." "Dude..." Grant could only mutter back. "I love you so much right now..." There was noticeable... very noticeable silence on the other end of the line. "Today is turning out to be the best day ever, in the world, EVER,"Grant explained, while only silence met his enthusiastic words. "I have a date with the friend of the cousin of the most important man in Indy wrestling! I, as in ME, have a date with the frie-" "Dude, you already said," the slightly worried Steve remarked. "You also said you loved me, which we will not be mentioning to anyone." Springing up from the bed, Grant wedged the phone between his head and shoulder as he began rummaging through his drawers. "I gotta go," he hurriedly dismissed Steve with. "Need to get ready for tonight... and hey, who knows? By tomorrow, I might have a tryout match with tSC, and a 'dark-match' of my own with Karina, if ya know what I mean?" "I don't think I ever want to know what you mean," the bemused Steve sighed back. "Anyway, where you taking her?" . . . "Pizza Hut?" Karina finally asked, despite having been at the table for nearly five minutes. "Hey," Grant shrugged, relaxing into his chair before holding his arms out from his sides. "I don't exactly have a whole load of cash weighing me down right now..." "We go to the same Gym," Karina reminded him. "I know how much you can afford for that." "Then you'll understand why I can't afford anywhere else," he winked back, almost enjoying the challenge that Karina offered... while still knowing he had to stay focussed on the task at hand. "And a guy I work with here owes me... so we eat for free!" "Great," Karina quipped back, mischief in her startling eyes. "I'll have the lobster then." "Oh, ha ha," Grant playfully retaliated. Despite her stuffy upbringing, Karina Wolfenden was far from snobbish. Especially about something as simple and basic as food. After rapidly tiring of the bizarre and palate-testing monstrosities that they'd serve in the restaurants her father chose to meet her in, something as simple as a Pizza with an overly thick, stodgy base would be a welcome change. Everything just seemed so relaxed. Every couple or group at a table were engrossed in conversation, bodies animated hands gesturing. It seemed to chip away slightly at Karina's stiff, awkward posture as she tried to not look too intrigued by the man opposite. Taking the actions of those around her as a cue, Karina dropped her shoulders down a little, and gradually began to make actual eye-contact. "So," Grant then re-started, changing the tone of his voice and leaning forward ever-so-slightly... just enough that the perceptive Karina noticed. "I was thinking about your match with Johnny Lexicon this weekend." He just had to turn the conversation to wrestling, didn't he. Just as Karina was starting to relax with him, opening her body language and partially lowering her frosty defences. The change in Karina was instant, as the slight smile that had crept onto her face evened back out, and her hands withdrew firmly back onto her half of the table. Anyone sitting close enough might have even felt the temperature dip a degree or two. "Funny," Karina remarked with more than a hint of a disappointed scoff. "I wasn't..." This was why she didn't date wrestlers. The last thing Karina wanted to talk about were matches and moves, spots and psychology. It was all performance, no extras for her. Surely no other profession in the world had its practitioners wanting to talk about their work after clocking off. No construction worker wanted to discuss cement mixes or brick-laying... just as no typist or secretary was interested in the latest version of Microsoft Works, or comparing audio-typing methods. "I'm free this weekend," Grant began to state, before Karina cut him off. "Not that surprising," she dryly commented, blurring the line of comedy due to her less-than-animated delivery. "So maybe I could come along?" he suggested, eyes taking on a slight pleading look, while his undeniably coaxing smile worked in his favour. "I'll even buy my own ticket... and hell, I'll even drive us!" "No thanks," was Karina's blunt reply. Nothing against him personally, since he'd gotten further than all but a handful. But there was a line, with a huge great big wall Karina had erected on it to keep her hobby distant from her life. If Grant was to stand a chance, then he wished her well when she left for bookings, and greeted her back - preferably with a full glass of wine - when she returned. "Why not?" Grant enquired, trying not to press too much. "At every match you always have a tonne of people cheering you on, so I just thought it would be nice if, for once, one of them actually knew you." He was doing so well. Emphasis well and truly on the was. "So you know me now?" Wolfenden hit back, raising brows and looking out the tops of her eyes at him. "Well," a confused Grant mumbled back. "Yeah..." "If you think you know me," Karina seemed keen to point out, "then you don't know me." Way to kill a mood. Karina Wolfenden never really wondered why she was single... it just wasn't something she often thought about. She was an independent young woman who found it hard to take an interest in other people's passions and pastimes. It didn't help that she often came off as cold, rude, and so far up herself that she was practically coming out the other end. At that point, Grant took a risk... something that he prayed would pay off, and get him even closer to the connections he desired. "Fine," Grant sighed, pulling his chair from the table. "If you don't want to be here, I'll just settle things up with Harry and you can go back to living your life minus m-" "Sit back down," Kari half sighed, half commanded... the smallest hint of an apologetic tone contained in the words. "I just... well... most people don't get me. Not that I blame them. I'm just uncomfortable with people thinking they're closer to knowing me than they are. If that makes any sense?" Her words managed to halt Grant's abrupt exit, and he dropped back into his seat. "Y'know..." he began to quip, "you can just call me an idiot instead of running through the critical self-analysis?" As he broke into a subtle chuckle while looking back at Karina, Grant was beaming a Cheshire Cat smile on the inside. His idea to walk away had played out perfectly, and he actually managed to open Karina up just a little. That tiny, almost miniscule bit of guilt could be exploited once it was shown. It had worked once, and at the right time, he could use it again. "Oh, I call plenty of people idiots," Karina seemed to joke, once more relaxing her posture after the short flare-up. "Comes with the whole staying single thing." A chink was in the armour, and the wily Grant zeroed in on it... wanting to open it up further. He needed to get closer to Karina. He needed to get inside the defences, under the layer of frost. If he could get his life in sync with hers, then they'd naturally share the same people and social occasions... all getting him known to the right people. Namely, the Avalons and their Squared Circle. "Doesn't it get lonely?" Grant moved in with, throwing it in as a casual question, when in reality it was brutally calculated. "I live in a world of 6 billion people," Karina confidently and assuredly replied. "I've been to 23 countries and I speak 5 languages... how could I be lonely?" Ugh, Grant grunted on the inside. NOT the answer he wanted. Think fast. "Yeah," he began, hoping the intro would buy him a second or two to think what to follow it with. "But can any other of those 6 billion drive you to your Sunday booking, let you choose the music, AND even supply the sodas and snacks?" For the first time in a long, long while, Karina actually felt compelled to do something for one of her potential suitors... to give them an inch. Wouldn't quite go so far as to call it generosity, more appeasement. "Fiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiine," she then gave in with, adding almost over-dramatic reluctance to her voice. "But I reserve the right to leave you stranded in the middle of nowhere if you start to annoy me, or drink the last Vanilla Diet Coke. Deal?" "Fine by me," Grant smiled back, while under the table his toes were wiggling franticly with eager satisfaction. He'd managed it. Somehow. When the GTT was looking for wrestlers from all over the world to fill its much coveted brackets, he wasn't even a consideration. Nobody that mattered had even spoken to him, let alone seen one of his matches. He didn't even get a call to be a fill-in guy, and give one of the bigger names an exhibition match. But now, he'd be there. Not in the ring, but somewhere far more important... as a backstage guest. While Karina continued to reel off more terms and conditions, each just blending into the next, all Grant could think of was the list of esteemed names left in that he could talk to with as much overly-lavish praise as possible. Doctor Curiosity, one of the hottest characters in all of wrestling would be there. As would Angelo Deville, the slick, stylish enigma of the United States Indy scene. "...and then I can safely say, you WILL get lucky..." Karina teased, seeing that Grant's attention was shifting. "Huh?" he snapped back into reality with, only to see a slight and very, very rare giggle from the K-Wolf rapidly disappear from existence. "Anyway, since you're determined to not let me get to know you, I'll tell you a little about myself..." In reply, Karina gave one of her teasing I'll try to contain my excitement eye-brow raises. "I was born a few hours from here, lived the-" Still wanting to make him earn her attention, and unable to resist the urge to frustrate her would-be-suitor once again, Kari was quick to cut him off. "Skip to the end..." . . . "So," the smiling Grant began as he buttoned up his coat. "How about I walk you home?" Cocking her head, Karina scanned up to his eyes, then gave him a mischievous and smiling shake of the head. "Sorry," she chirped, almost as if someone who wasn't an apathetic, aloof ice-queen had taken over her body. "I'm still not entirely convinced that you aren't a complete psychopath who collects ears to make jewellery with... so I'd rather not let you know where I live." Zipping her coat up as far as it would go, Karina then flung a stripy scarf around her neck and lower face, obscuring her vivid pink lips. "You're not the easiest woman to get close to," Grant chuckled, admittedly impressed by her resolve, and satisfied that he'd achieved the goal for the evening, "you know that, right?" "What's the matter?" Karina replied as she backed away, putting her earphones in. "Not up for a challenge?" "Always," he confidently assured her. "So, when's my next 'test'." "Tomorrow," she yelled, turning away and raising her MP3 player up to deafening levels. "Gym at 11... I need a kick bag that can move." Watching her depart, Grant rubbed his hands together... half to warm them, and half in smug satisfaction in realising that his plan may actually be pulled off... even if he wasn't to be that night. Deep within his conscience, a little part of him was also realising that he enjoyed the evening for more than just fulfilling goals. But whatever whimper it offered was easily drowned out by the overwhelming crescendo that opposed it. As for whether his morality could amplify it enough to be heard, it would have to wait for another day, mainly the next, when he could get even closer to the elusive Karina Wolfenden.
