Out of the Blue (Into The Fire)

26 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-26 I barely walked into my hotel room before the phone rang. It was loud and shocking, a beacon in the dark room. I though, Who could know I rented this room in this hotel? Who in hell’s name would realize I was here? This wasn’t enough to stop me, though. I walked towards the phone, picking it off the receiver. “Hello?” I said with a sense of urgency. “Drake, how’s it going bud?” It was the same man who called me last time. Every time I was in a scummy hotel room, this man called me. At the beginning of this tournament. In all my time in the IWL. This man, rather this voice, had been following me ever since I could remember. “What is it? What do you want?” I was annoyed. No, scratch that--I was angry. Who was this man to think he could invade my life and destroy my sanity? I needed to end this, for once and for all. “Oh, no one…say, Drake, remember that girl outside of this hotel?” This hotel? That girl? Was he in here? My mind was racing with confusion and shock. “Yes…why…?” I was now cautious and worried for Charlene. I held the phone gingerly and with caution. “Come down to the lobby now if you ever want to see her. Oh, and I think I’d like to give you a little ‘hello’ as well…heh heh heh…” As fast as I dropped the phone on the receiver I was out of my room and sprinting through the hall, the off-color walls blurring in my eyes. I couldn’t get down to the lobby fast enough, my eyes seeing the legs of this man and Charlene before I saw the rest of them, though. What I did see, it stopped me. Charlene was being held at gun point by a man, very young, no older than 20. He stared at me with sinister eyes, but the rest of him was beautiful. He had a square jaw and pearly whites to match his perfect smile. His cheek bones were defined, and his nose added a gorgeous character too him. His black hair was pulled back, the grease making it shine in the light. The white t-shirt and the dark navy blue jeans he wore were pristine, and the boots on his feet were a dark, thick black. Charlene was held in his grip, the 9mm handgun he had pressed firmly against her head. I recognized this man, though. This part was the most disturbing. It wasn’t a friend, but it wasn’t an enemy either. It was someone I used to know, but could never come back again. Someone I imagine I could never see again. It was a blast from the past. It was me. An exact replica of me, to be specific. This was me before the accident, before the scars and the burns. This was me before the off-white bandages covered my body and my dignity. This was me before I had lost all love and caring. This was the me no one could now ever see. I looked around the room, not sure whether to freak out or to do nothing. The man behind the desk was making no notice of anything. In fact, he barely looked up at me when I came rushing down the stairs. He must not have noticed Charlene there, or anyone rather. As I looked back at myself, he smiled, raising and eyebrow and flashing those pearly whites. He shook his head at me, letting out a little chuckle before he started. “Man oh MAN, do you look like shit or what?” He laughed at me, keeping the gun firmly against Charlene’s head. He sounded like a younger me, his voice not raspy like mine now was, his words still clear and crisp. “Who are you?” I knew exactly who it was. On a certain level, though, I needed to hear it from this thing. “Whats up, Drake, been too long?” He shook his head, spitting on the ground. “Its been years…how old are you now, 60? Jesus, doesn’t look like you aged a year! I figured, though, that after that injury, that accident…whatever the hell you call it, you’d forget me. Or did you not forget me? Yes, I think THAT’S it…I think you think about me EVERY day…EVERY day you say ‘Gee, wish I was me again’…hoping for a change in the atmosphere, that you didn’t sign up for this or that…man oh MAN, Drake-O!” The man laughed after he said this, shaking his head at me. He was right, though. I had never forgotten what I used to be…the firm, crisp man I was, the beautiful poster image of the good looking American-made man. I was respected in my town, in my state, in my country…everyone appreciated my work. Going into the Army made them love me even more. I was the ideal man…but after the accident… “I don’t regret a thing.” I lied just to shake the image out of my head. This thing couldn’t have been real…but how would you explain how he was gripping Charlene like that? How could I come back in physical form? “Ha! You’ve never been a good liar have you!” He pulled back the hammer on the gun. “Lying will only get poor Charlene killed here, got it?” I nodded. “So, where was I? Oh, yes…Drake, Drake, Drake! I bet you’re thinking right now ‘Who is this? Am I going crazy?’ You think that, maybe you’re lying will make me disappear, make your sanity return to you, Drake…maybe Charlene will be saved…” His words were hurting me, giving me a headache. I felt like I was being burnt again, the pain of these words hurting me. There was nothing I could do about it, though, because I couldn’t let Charlene be saved. “…But, Drake my man, don’t you worry! I’m not here to save Charlene! In fact, shit, you can have her!” His clutch of her was released, instantly sending her running to me. She grabbed a hold of my stomach, clutching me close. I stood there, staring back at myself, wondering what was happening. Suddenly, the thing across the room started laughing. “Drake,” he said, “Look down for a second.” My eyes bulged when he saw that Charlene was not there anymore. In fact, it was now just a skeleton, the jaw dropping open as it lifelessly fell to the ground. I quickly took to steps back, gasping for air, looking up at the thing across the room and then back at the skeleton. “Ha ha ha ha!” The thing’s laughter was booming and loud, just like how I used to always laugh. “Drake, my man, scared you, didn’t it?” I clutched my head, the aching and the soreness now getting to me. What was happening? Why is this happening? Did I have bad chicken earlier? What were these things? “What…what…WHAT DO YOU WANT FROM ME?” I was now screaming, the man behind the desk flashing an eye at me and me alone. The thing across the room was laughing even harder now, clutching his stomach. He walked towards me, stopping about 10 feet away and staring at me. “You…you still don’t get it, do you?” He was now curious, still bewildered at what he was seeing. The truth was exactly that, though…I DIDN’T get it. I had NO IDEA what was happening. I was afraid that, maybe I died and went to hell? That would be the best possible thing that could happen. As I shook my head, he walked towards me and put his hand on my shoulder. Even though I hate this thing, I accepted it. I knew that this evil was part of me, that bringing it in could only complete me. I stood there, staring eye to eye with myself, grimly awaiting his next truths. “Drake, you fool. Don’t you see? I am you. You are me. We are the same. I am following you around, I’m always there. You see, Drake, I’m what you want to be. I’m that person that you always pray to be every night.” The words hit me and shocked him. These brutal truths that he was speaking were mind-blowing. “Why do I come now? Why didn’t I come when you first wanted to be, well, me?” the question struck me for a moment before the thing continued. “It took you years to make me Drake. Years of sitting in that hospital bed crying and screaming because of the pain. Years of your brain deteriorating because you could not move from the injuries you sustained.. I was your only companion, your only friend that you sculpted until you regained your strength.” “That bomb, though, was your savior. You were in Vietnam, and when that bomb struck you, I came with it. I am in your mind, Drake.” There was no way this was happening. It all seemed too real. This beautiful image of myself telling me it was non-existent…no way this was real… “When you left the hospital Drake, I snuck into your brain. I hid away from all the active parts and stayed around in the world you now hate—your dreams. Every night, you have the same dream. Trust me, I know you do. You’re being shelled. The troops don’t know what’s happening. And then, with just one warning, the explosion, the injury, whatever you want to call it happens. I made that dream Drake. Now, though, you found Charlene…” I found Charlene? Outside I did…but she became a skeleton. This was all too confusing. I waited for some kind of clearance. “…Charlene came to you when you were in your darkest hour, your darkest moment. I knew Charlene…met her when I first came with that explosion. She was a beautiful girl then. Her essence, everything about her was huge. She was your hope, your desire. Remember her?…you don’t. The explosion killed her, destroyed her. Drake…Charlene was your wife.” I never had a wife. My memory tells me that I was single except for a few short flings in high school…but, then again, I couldn’t trust anything I remembered now. “You married Charlene before you left…you knew Charlene ever since you were 14…you and her went through high school together. Your memory of her was hidden for a while…but now, its just coming back. At first, its just going to be that young girl you knew…soon, it’ll be that girl you left…and then, she’ll be gone again. Just like me too, Drake. Pretty soon, we’ll all be gone…including yourself. Sanity is the end for you, Drake. Sanity will be your demise.” I couldn’t believe what I was hearing. He told me himself that he was a memory, something from my mind that I could get rid of. Getting rid of him meant the death of me, though? “To obtain sanity, Drake, you have to execute yourself. You have to finish the job the explosion never managed to do. You don’t have the guts for that though. You’ve become a softy. Sure, now you’re angry, a brute, whatever. On the inside, though, there’s still me, that hope that maybe, eventually, everything will go back to normal. Soon, it will be both me and Charlene, taunting you, telling you to become sane again. You’ve resisted it up to now…lets just see how long you can last.” He removed his hand from my shoulder, shaking his head at me. A tear fell down my face as he started walking away. He headed towards the exit, and now the bones of Charlene were gone too. I was left with myself, with the man behind the desk staring at me, a look of pity in his eyes. “Hey man, I’ve said your name a good 10 times now. You gotta shut up and get back to your room or GET OUT!” He was now yelling, his mouth wide open when he said that last part. “I’m…I’m sorry, but…did you happen to see a young guy here with a girl while I was down here…at all?” I was hopeful that, maybe, just maybe, that wasn’t my insanity. That maybe killing myself won’t be necessary to become me again. That maybe I can go through life happy and content. “What in the…you were the LAST man to come in here for HOURS now! Now, get back to your room or GET OUT!” I quickly turned away from the man after he said that, shaking my head. As I slowly made my way up the stairs, I replayed all the events in my head. How did he call me down? Was that in my head to? Was he even in my head? Was this all a dream too? Or, is he and Charlene really in my head, telling me to end my life? I had to clear my head, so I turned on the TV and turned to the PTC Channel, a special Direct TV channel. Surprising such a scummy hotel such as this one would have Direct TV, but I didn’t think much of it. The channel was an overview and another prediction on the second round. I happened to turn it on right on the Devito bracket and right on the analysis on my match with Jack Morgan. “Drake Coleman? LUCKY! He barely squeaked by this one!” The man on TV was a blundering idiot. “Second round, I see that babe Elia Smith of GLOBAL laying a royal ASS WHOOPING on Drake…he’s gonna go home broken, done, nothing!” I watched this, mouth wide open, my brain now mush. My mind was telling me to kill myself, the people on TV were saying I was worthless. I was tired beyond belief, on top of that all. I figured that I needed a nap, or at least sleep, so I laid back on the bed and closed my eyes, awaiting the dream to come up… ------------------------------------------------------------------ -(The room was white and blank, the ideal dream world. You can only see one person, standing there in a white robe. A beautiful young girl, about 18, standing there staring at you. You walk towards her and see her, but she stops you with a few words.)- “Drake…I love you…I’ve always loved you…” -(Its Charlene. Her voice is harmonious, echoing in the air. She is staring at you, smiling, putting a warm feeling into your heart. You reach out, but your arms go back to your side.)- “Drake…come to me…come to me…” -(She is beckoning for you to do something…but what?)- “Drake…join me…join me…” -(Suddenly, she becomes a skeletal figure, and in an instant, a loud booming explosion occurs and you get shooken into darkness…)- ------------------------------------------------------------------ I shot up from the dream. A different dream? I was breathing heavy now, unaware of what just happened. I was scared, more scared of the former dreams. This girl I supposedly married…now telling me to kill myself. Before hand, it was not direct…now the voices were giving me order…no, not voices…that’s too insane…I’m not insane…the people, the people I want telling me to kill myself… I need more sleep. -----([([([END FEED])])])----- "I was trying so hard to be myself I was turning into somebody else…" --The The
