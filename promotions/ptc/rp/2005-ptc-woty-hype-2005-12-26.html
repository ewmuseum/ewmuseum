2005 PTC WOTY Hype

26 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Christian Zenith Presents... Sponsored by Zenith Corporation... WOTY HYPE 2005 Fact: Did you know the second piece of hype for PTC Wrestler of the Year was posted by Ember. Not only is he not even the best member of his own fed, he doesn't even know what year it is. Ember may very well win Wrestler of the Year in 2205... when the world is nothing but an apocalyptic wasteland and WOTY is chosen by the roaches who used to live in Ember's clothes. Unfortunately for the world, Christian Zenith will be dead by then. Christian Zenith: Knows Which Year He's Running For Fact: Did you know two of the victories Josh used in his nomination never happened in 2005? That's right, Josh NEVER collected a victory over Dawn McFarlane or Shadow in this past year. Sure we know Christian Zenith didn't either... but they were his minions, so it didn't matter. Christian Zenith: Not A Liar Fact: Did you know Jesse Jamester's last match against Christian Zenith was at Seven of the Best where he was defeated convincingly in unanimous decision. After his defeat, Jesse Jamester disappeared for months... never returning to Global Wrestling, fearing the day he'd have to look at Christian Zenith again. Instead, he decided to flee to PRIME... where he still sucks today. Christian Zenith: Fears No One Fact: Did you know for Seven of the Best, there was a PTC spot available for a member of a PTC fed who deserved a spot in the tournament but wasn't awarded one by their fedhead. Contrary to whatever Angelo Deville might tell you, this spot was awarded to Christian Zenith, the man PTC felt most deserved the spot who wasn't nominated. Not only did Karina beat Deville to the punch, Christian Zenith trumped him for the open spot. Christian Zenith: Trumps The Rest Fact: Did you know before the last match for Seven of the Best took place, Christian Zenith picked up Karina Wolfenden high over his head and threw her out of the ring. She bounced off a table before landing into the crowd. Luckily for Karina, the match had not started yet but this proves an important fact. If the match was between only Christian Zenith and Karina Wolfenden, and the timekeeper would've known how to do his damn job, she would have been crying in the crowd in defeat. Christian Zenith: A Clear Cut Winner Fact: Did you know that Josh shaves his ass daily with a Gillette, and one time even had a blade stuck in the crack of his own ass completely unbeknownst to him. Christian Zenith: Not A Sasquatch Fact: Did you know that Seymour Almasy was wrestling in GW for nearly a year under a mask and a false name, Emoticon. While in this guise, he tried desperately to pick up and date every single member of Christian Zenith's stable short of Christian Zenith himself. Every single one of them rejected him, as will the voters for WOTY. Christian Zenith: Not A Loser Fact: Did you know that Hoyt Williams takes great pride in being a champion, but unfortunately for him God's Champion doesn't count as a championship. Christian Zenith was a true DOUBLE champion at Clash of the Titans, winning the GW International Championship while already holding the GW World Championship. Of course Christian Zenith could've easily been a triple champion by making up something like Santa's Champion, but Zenith doesn't name titles after things that don't exist. Christian Zenith: Doesn't Believe In Fairy Tales Fact: Did you know that recently in GTT5, Angelo Deville spent most of his free time telling everyone how much better he was than everyone else and how he has never lost in the 1st Round of a tournament. Then proceeded to be eliminated by Rich Rollins in the 1st Round. On the sideline he was joined by fellow candidates James von Doom, Brandon Youngblood, and LeStatt Knight. Danielle Northsky joined them. Why is that significant? Christian Zenith kicked her ass in the first round. Christian Zenith: Has Really Never Lost In Round 1 Fact: Did you know Peter Vetra has no respect for the Indianapolis Colts. Yeah... I laughed too. Christian Zenith: No One's Bitch Fact: Did you know that Rich Rollins wears a stupid bandanna to conceal the fact that he is really a balding 39 year old biker. Although the bandanna went out in the 80's, Rollins feels it's necessary to keep wearing the total fashion disaster to keep his adoring fans from seeing his Hoganesque hairline. Christian Zenith on the other hand, has a full head of beautiful hair that you can take home to your mother. Christian Zenith also didn't lost Infinite Gauntlet in Round 1, Round 2, and Round 6. With three shots, even a chimp could've won at least once... but Rollins needed 4. Christian Zenith: Realizes Hulkamania Died A Long Time Ago Fact: Did you know Christian Zenith is the only name you need to consider when pondering your vote for the 2005 PTC Wrestler of the Year.
