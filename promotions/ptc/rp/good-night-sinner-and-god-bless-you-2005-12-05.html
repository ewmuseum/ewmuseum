Good night sinner and God Bless You.

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Hoyt Williams: I like sunflower seeds as there is nothing more American then sucking on salt in seed form. It’s liberating and manly. Nothing says I love life more then walking down the street with a mouth full of seeds, cracking them open with your teeth then freely spitting the fallen shells out toward liberals, joggers, old ladies, and hippies. That’s the American dream. I like to go grocery shopping and take food out of people’s carts when they’re not looking. It really puts them on edge, makes them think they are going crazy when they remember fretting over the calorie count of the cream of mushroom soup only to find it doesn’t exist in their cart. You see mind games are what I play to stay entertained. Being a world renowned entertainer….PRIME Universal Champion…PTC EXTREME CHAMPION…the son of God…the PONTIFF of PTC… one hell of a guy….and your personal savior can be tedious and exhausting playing life’s normal game of Fogger. So I play little game and take up little activates to stay both active and on top of things. Being an American makes you one step closer to God then anybody else in the world as we are God’s nation. "One nation under God" to be exact. God told me this and that’s why he placed me here and not somewhere silly like Denmark or a pointless and Godless nation like Canada. Despite this front I put on I have to admit I’m a bit over worked. You see I have to defend my Extreme title this week, along with beating Lundgard in the first round of the GTT, not to mention I have a very pregnant wife, and a PRIME title defense coming up in two weeks. This much stress would turn Clint Black’s hair white and Betty White’s hair black. It reminds me of the old bible tale of Jesus during his short stint as a professional wrestler. You see God has always been a fan of the sport and pushed Jesus into doing some wrestling. Jesus was floundering on earth, no job, and a group of friends that most mothers would want their children hanging out with. I’d also like to mention all of them where dudes, no gals in his posse of apostles. Kind of odd if you ask me, it always made me wonder if you know what I mean. I only heard one story of Jesus getting nailed but well it was very different then the kind of nailing I do to my wife. Anyways Jesus got hired in Moses’ wrestling federation. Burning Bush Wrestling. It didn’t take much as God and Moses go way back as an old family friend. Moses wasted no time and gave Jesus the gimmick of the "Israeli Dream" just a common man. A working mans man and the son of a blue collar carpenter to boot. He wore a robe of polka dots with yellow sandals doing the Jesus shuffle all the way to the ring. Jesus won some matches against furhers and devilles (back then that is what they called guys who would wrestle just to lose and get paid.) Anyways despite a lisp…must people don’t know this but Jesus in fact had a lisp. Despite the lisp he wasn’t bad on the mic and played up the carpenter’s son gimmick real well. So Moses had to book him at the Parting of the Red Seas pay-per-view extravaganza. Moz would do this once a year where he would actually part the seas and put a ring in the center of the sea floor and fill the rest with seats and would sell the sea out. Jesus was having a particularly stressful month much like I am having now. The Jews were threatening to nail him to a cross, his best friend Judas turned on him at the last pay-per-view, and his father was questioning his sexuality. The fans were even beginning to turn on him as the Israeli Dream gimmick was starting to go stale and bomb. Now in BBW, Jesus was the face while an up and coming reformer named Martin Luther was making a name himself. You see two weeks ago Martin Luther with the help of Judas nailed a list of match demands and a challenge to the door of Jesus’ locker room. Jesus was so livid he accepted a crown of thorns hardcore match with a loser is fired stipulation despite being out weighed and out skilled by the man known as "Marvelous" Martin Luther. All the stress built up so Jesus had all his friends over for supper to relax. Things went well and Jesus even made up with his buddy Judas over red wine. The next day was the PPV and Judas again turned on Jesus costing him the match. Jesus was arrested after the match on charges of unemployment. It was the in the government was looking for like catching Capone on tax evasion. Sadly Jesus was put to death the next day. So you see I can’t take my eye off of the ball no matter how easy of a match or how difficult of a match it’s supposed to be. Mikael Lundgard has nothing and is nothing, and when you have nothing and you are nothing you have nothing to lose. Me I’m damn near a living legend at the tender age of twenty five and I have everything to lose. How would it look for PTC’s top star to lose to a nobody?? People would talk and speculate my greatness like a room full of red haired children. I can’t have that. So winning for me is just as important as it was for Jesus. Of coarse if I lose I won’t be crucified just humiliated which is much worse if you ask me. So Lundgard the anti American sinner must bet taken out with the unforgiving hand of god slapping him back to where ever he came from. You see America is the greatest land in the world and if you don’t love our milk and honey then I have no love for you….and neither does God. As I said earlier I do a lot of activities to keep my mind busy and today was one of the busiest. Perhaps I should have spent more time preparing for Lunggard or even proof reading this piece of shit but instead I enjoyed life and stayed busy. ----------------------------------------------------------- (Hoyt walks into his morning poetry reading class in Lincoln Park. He sits down next to some artsy type yuppie wife sipping on Starbucks and snacking on the peanuts on the table in front of them. This reminds Hoyt about the joke he heard the night before about a guy putting a cashew on the tip of his penis and springing it into his mouth. These were not cashews but the memory was enough to turn Hoyt’s appetite off of nuts and TV in general) Teacher: Ok welcome lets get started, Wendy why don’t you start. (With a certain shyness of a small child the women sitting next to Hoyt digs into her carrying case and pulls out her poetry written on a white piece of drawing paper. She clears a peanut from her throat and smiles a story book smile before beginning.) Wendy: Love Unending and powerful Like the freedom of a flying dove Landing in a garden flowerful. L-o-V-E LOVE (The group of mainly 20 something house wives clap wildly like she just invented sex for the first time. Hoyt rolls his eyes as the teacher shakes the young woman’s hand.) Teacher: Brilliant! Let’s hear some other feedback. Mexican Women: I felt the power of the freedom of love. Thank you for that. Cute girl in blue: I LOVED it pardon the pun. Fat chick with a mustache: It really touched me in places I haven’t been touched in a long time. Hoyt Williams: Like your cunt? (Hoyt stands up and scares the women as he is a large man.) Hoyt Williams: Let’s be honest here honey, fat women don’t get into heaven as clouds like elevators have weight limits. Don’t tell me you weren’t warned, the bible tells us that our bodies are our temples to the man upstairs. So chub rub you’re going down down down to the ring of fire so get used to the heat and the torment. But really folks flowerful isn’t a darn world is it?? Powerful and Flowerful doesn’t work you can’t just make up words to create a rhyme! Well you’re at it pagan why don’t you create another God to worship because you are never getting the love of the powerful one as he hates people who lie and you lied to us with that stupid word. Flowerful? HA! Teacher: MR. WILLIAMS please!! Hoyt Williams: SHUT UP you lily totting liberal I’m not done, let me read my poem before I exit. Jesus He loves each and every one of you sinners Except people who live in Nevis He also hates losers but loves winners So go out and do something worth a damn Spread the word of your God to better man Don’t be chubby, ugly or mean put down that ham You get what I’m saying fatass yea, you, Suzann Diet rite might be wrong for you But if you don’t do something soon You’ll never have a man to give tongue to Because your ass will be bigger then the moon. JESUS J-E-S-U-S Jesus what a sow! (Hoyt storms out the door) ----------------------------------------------------------- Sure I was a bit tough but you have to be to get through to these folks who are so consumed in sin they don’t even realize it. The bible does tell us about our bodies being our temples yet the world gets fatter everyday. Capitalism is about survival of the fittest which should be fairly easy with the expanding size of the competition. Sure Ben Franklin had a big ass but at least he was a God fearing man. Most of the people today don’t know the words of Jesus. They laugh at the idea of church because they are indeed better then believing in a blind faith. Rather they pray to the MTV GODS to tell them what they need to worship next like the vile soul known as Paris Hilton or perhaps the evilness of President Bush. But all you have to do is stand back and see the world is reflected. What the average man perceives as good is actual bad and vise versa. Saddam is an evil and dangerous man who tempted the US to come and get him. He felt Satan would protect him and he wanted to take us on. So he smiled and winked and said maybe I do have WMD’s but I’m not going to let you find out. So we shut him down yet the devil makes you think we are the bad guys. The devil teaches you food with no nutritional value is ok to eat. Twinkies, Big Macs, Fried Chicken are the modern day apples of the forbidden fruit in the Garden of Eden. Think about it, the logic and proof is right in front of you. People fight to keep creation out of school yet these same sinners couldn’t name a substantial scientist from the last fifty years. Yet they know back and forth the life of a pure evil like the child molesting singer named Jackson. Sure I’m in a bad mood now but it’s not supposed to be that way you’re supposed to know I love. I am LOVE I am your savior I am holier then thou yet you people doubt and mock me. Why? Perhaps because the truth scares you? Maybe the truth knocks you down a notch on the evolutionary ladder? If I am indeed the second coming of Christ, hell is to follow in a war of Armageddon? Mikael Lundgard is fueled by Satan’s hate. The devil has gotten to him by one of his oldest and dirtiest plays. Jealously. You see Mikael Lundgard is jealous of what we have in America and God’s love. So I must beat the love and truth of God into him so he understands where I’m coming from. America is the land of opportunity, power, industry, culture, and Hoyt Williams. It appears sinner Lundgard hates our way of life. Our way of life is too fast for him but that’s understandable he’s retarded. He’s a laid back foreign sinner obsessed with the power and success we have and he has failed to achieve. I can’t blame him I guess but its no need for Jealousy. Perhaps I am the shinning example of what our nation is all about. Heck I’ll say it I am the All American Boy and the greatest representative of our country. So Lundy you got a chance to take out what you hate the most. The son of God, an all American jock who wasn’t happy achieving the Universal Title in the top federation in the world. No I went out and achieved even more greatness taking PTC’s top title the Extreme strap. Not to mention I was the longest running God’s champion EVER…EVER!! Maybe I am selfish but that is just a term thrown out there by people who can’t have it all. I got it all kid. Now I’m going to have you after I convert and save you. Thank me latter Lundy but until then get on your knees and pray for forgiveness. Or better yet no show and let God pity you because sinner you can’t beat Jesus Christ, you can’t beat the good old USA, you can’t beat God, and you sure as hell can’t beat me. Go beat your meat it’s a sin but your best chance at beating something. Good night sinner and God Bless You. Hoyt Williams HW =w=
