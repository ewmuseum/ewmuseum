Seriousness? NAAAAAH!

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 Larson: Well ladies and gentlemen, we are here because… why are we here again Rick? Rick: Because we are gonna say a few words about our favorite wrestler! Larson: Woo! I didn't know Lew Spectre had come back! Rick: No you moron… it's Digital Mortality! Larson: That fool? Rick: *sighs* We here represent the UWF Announcers Team and *cough* some backstage representatives. Larson: I'm betting Digimon paid us well, right? Rick: Of course. Anyways, we just wanted to say a few words regarding our former World Title holder and inductee into the UWF Hall of Fame next to names like Seymour Almasy, Kenny Mercury, and Tsunami Oni (He got there first though! BURN!). Larson: Wait… you just said what we were supposed to say in fewer words… if they don't pay me my money I'll be so mad. Rick: Digital Mortality has been a mainstay over at the UWF HQ for quite some time now. Larson: Yeah, and I'm sick of him… Rick: Shush. He was part of one of the biggest stables in UWF history, the Iron Curtain, and they ruled the World, one capitalist nation at a time! Larson: Those were the days! Rick: Now he is looking to finish the career of his long time nemesis, Jamie Shock. Larson: Better known as 'One Testicle' Jamie. Rick: It will be time soon for SuperNova to feel his wrath. Larson: SuperWHO? Rick: Exactly. Larson: He got some guy called SuperNova and guys like Seymour get Winters? He has got it too easy… Rick: That is better for him! Because he will have to face some tough names *when* he passes to the next round. Larson: HAHAHA! Do you realize that if Digimon and Axel Action win they will have to face each other? Rick: That will surely be a great match! Larson: Are you kidding! Two buffoons dancing with each other in the ring? Wait… that is actually pretty funny! Rick: Anyways… anything else to say? Do we have like a picture or anything? Rick: What the hell is that? Larson: Some dude called Chris drew this and sent it as fan mail to the UWF Headquarters… wow, this guy has no life! Rick: That is pretty good. Larson: No it’s not. Rick: Yes it is. Larson: No it’s not. Rick: No it’s not. Larson: Yes it is. Rick: HA! Got you fool! Larson: Bah… you bastard… Rick: Anyways, I just got an email from some kid called SuperNova fan. Larson: He has fans? Rick: Apparently. It says here… "Why does Digital need to write a hype in Spanish… how the hell is everyone else supposed to understand him? This is just like him to do something no one will understand… that is why he sucks! Damned beaners!" Larson: Hahaha! He said beaner! Rick: I can’t believe he just offended our resident minority! Without Digimon we would be getting taxed to death for not having Latinos on our roster! I can only imagine how other federations can survive without a Latino on their roster! Larson: Beaners are funny! Rick: *SMACK* Larson: OW! You bastard! Rick: Anyways… back to the beaner… shit! See what you made me do!? Larson: *snickers* Rick: Gah… I don’t know why I put up with you… Larson: Because you love me. I know it. Rick: Rod… I hope the Bum Boozler catches you. [Door opens and in comes Digital Mortality.] DM: What are you two buffoons doing!? Rick: Err… I was trying to say good things about you but damned Larson keeps messing it all up. Larson: You rat! DM: Now what will people think of me thanks to you two!? Rick: That you are a wonderful, astute, intelligent, sharp, able, clever… Larson: He is using the thesaurus again! DM: You bastard! [Digital jumps them both.] Rick/Larson: AAAAAHHHH! [Digital jumps back up in front of the screen.] DM: Woo! Vote for Digital! Rick: Ack… this is not an election you fool! DM: No? The why the hell are we doing this? Sheesh… {This was paid for by the friends of Digital Mortality. Ok…ok… by the friend.} {If anyone got offended for the use of the word ‘beaner’ then he really should check himself into a mental institution. I am a ‘beaner’ and I can say the word ’beaner’ as much as I like dammit!} {Thank You For Your Time.}
