2205 WOTY nomination hype

25 December 2005

He didn't know what it was but something roused Ember from his sleep. Blearily opening one eye he peered through the darkness of his room, trying to figure out what it was that had woken him up. It was pretty rare that he even bothered to sleep so having it interrupted was more than simply annoying it was infuriating. Nothing in the room stirred and Ember peered towards the curtained window, straining his hearing to see if there was any commotion from outside. After a few seconds it was obvious that there was just the general background noise that one would expect from a downtown city street. Actually it was quieter than usual but then that was to be expected; it was Christmas Eve after all. Christmas Day actually Ember thought to himself as he glanced at the green neon display of the small clock on the bedside cabinet. It was just after one in the morning so even though he hadn't even been asleep for two hours it was now another day. Christmas Day. Resting his head back on the pillow Ember pulled the duvet up around his shoulders and let out a small sigh as he relaxed back into the mattress on his King size bed. He felt his eyes closing again, the warm and tender embrace of sleep reaching out to hold him again and welcomed it. The sound of footfalls, soft and almost inaudible, barely registered in his hearing; but barely was enough. Almost preternaturally Ember sat up and brought his legs out from under the covers. His feet touched the floor, his toes curling, and he winced as the cold bit into his exposed flesh. Fumbling around with his feet for a few seconds he suddenly sighed in relief as he found the slippers that lurked under the bed, slipping them on without aid of his hands. Stepping up he walked over to the door, still covered in darkness, with only a sheet wrapped around his body to cover his modesty. As he slipped the door open a faint light coming from without illuminated him. His hair, so pale blonde as to be almost white, cascaded down his body and shrouded his face from view. The white cotton sheet moulded to his form like a parody of a toga. The ears on the bunny slippers bent down and almost draped across the floor, the fur grey and spiky. Shuffling out of his room Ember started to make his way towards a door that was slightly ajar. A door from behind which cam the faint illumination and the sounds of movement. As he made his way towards the light Ember reached into another open door and grabbed the first thing that came to his hand. Armed now with a plastic cooking spatula he reached the lit door and peered inside. Holding his breath he could see a figure moving about the room, behind the large tree that dominated the area. It hadn’t been his idea to have a Christmas Tree, but Khaled had insisted. Ember couldn’t make out who the figure was but he knew that it wasn’t Khaled, the only other person to have a key to the apartment. It was far too fat. Pushing the door gently open Ember slipped inside and made his way to the tree, his eyes widening in shock as he realised that what – even if he couldn’t see who – it was. It’s a frikkin’ burglar Ember thought to himself as he saw the sack beside the man. I am being robbed. Moving as softly as he could, the fur of the bunny slippers aiding his silent journey, he sidled closer to the man, who seemed to realise that he wasn’t alone, turning to face Ember as the lithe wrestler brought the spatula around in a vicious arc, right towards the over-weight burglars head. “Ho, ho … oh that fuckin’ hurt!” came the shocked cry of pain, the deep and somewhat jolly baritone fractured as the spatula connected with the fleshy part of the face. Ember grinned and tried to pull the spatula back for another swing but was unable to, finding that it was somehow stuck to the burglar’s face. “Watch the beard you asswipe!” cried the burglar, his tone full of anguished pain. “Asswipe, who are you calling an asswipe you robbing son of a bit …” Ember retorted, reaching out to flick the light switch and stuttering to a stop as the lights came on and revealed the burglar in front of him. Dressed from head to toe in white trimmed red, with black leather boots, gloves and a belt, was a figure right out of fantasy. Well, truth be told right out of the intelligent marketing of a soft drink company, but let’s not quibble. With a rotund stomach and a white beard that touched his chest stood an irate and red-faced man. … and despite contrary descriptions of him through out the world Santa wasn’t looking very jolly at the moment. Reaching up with one hand he gingerly felt at his nose, which was obviously swelling before trying to disentangle the spatula that hung from the folds of his beard. After a few seconds of pain filled wrenching he gave up and gave a reproachful glare towards Ember. “You know I have given out lumps of coal for much less than this!” he growled at the smaller man, his voice sounding ‘jolly’ even in anger. Kicking down at a large package beside him he stared at Ember, his eyebrows raised. Another wince of pain followed this as it made his nose move too. “I am not a burglar you idiot, I am leaving gifts, not stealing them!” he said, again indicating the package. “Do you know what that is?” Ember looked down at it and thought about it for a few seconds. Considering it was only about the size of a briefcase he could quickly rule out the bike he had been thinking about, and it was too big for the DVD special edition of the best matches of Pete the PG Chimp. He scrunched up his forehead, brow creased in concentration – his face safely hidden out of sight behind his hair, of course – as he tried to fathom out what it could be. “Erm … peace on Earth and goodwill to all men?” Ember asked in desperation. “Did you ASK for that?” Santa asked, an edge of sarcasm in his tone. “Of course you didn’t you retard!” Reaching down he ripped the paper of the object and held up a wide belt of black leather, face plates of gold on either side of a golden disc. “It is the WWA World champion doofus!” Santa exclaimed as he threw the belt at Ember, who deftly reached out and caught it. He stared at it, fascinated by the fact that it was HIS name engraved on the belt, and only looked back up at Santa as he heard a choked gasp of surprise. Looking up he saw that Santa’s eyes were bulging and that he appeared to be choking. “Hey, are you ok?” he asked in concern. He knew that it would be a VERY bad thing for Santa to die in his house. He had a bad enough time of it with the fans for burning people’s faces so couldn’t even imagine the hate mail he got if he was involved in the demise of Saint Nick. “Jesus Christ, put some pants on!” Santa managed to choke out, pointing down at Ember’s lower body. Following the pointing finger Ember gazed down and realised that he had dropped his sheet while catching the belt. While bunny slippers may keep the feet warm they didn’t do much for the modesty. Slipping the belt a little lower than he was accustomed too he covered up his groin and smiled in embarrassment at Santa – who couldn’t, of course, see it as it was completely covered by his hair. “Oops, sorry about that” Ember said sheepishly. “Don’t worry, it is only a small matter, quickly forgotten” Santa said magnanimously. “Hey, it IS cold in here you know fatman” Ember shouted in defence of himself, “ and I bet that you have more wrinkles than inches anyway!” Santa stared at him, shock evident on his face as he struggled to think of something that he could say in response to a naked man wearing bunny slippers and a wrestling title belt. Hundreds of years of experience and a legacy of travelling to every place on Earth had taught him many things. This, though, was beyond him. “So, anything else I can do for you?” he said, trying to figure out a way to get out of the place as fast as possible. Thinking about it, briefly, a glimmer of inspiration began to light up in Ember’s eyes, quickly turning into an inferno of twisted genius. Of course, yet again, this was not visible to Santa, or anyone else. Oh no, Ember’s face was completely and utterly hidden away. Oh yes, indeed. “How about you make me the PTC wrestler of the year and get me the GTT victory?” Ember stated excitedly. Santa walked up to him and slapped him once around the face, ignoring the fact that the belt slipped out of the smaller man’s hands and that he was now standing far too close to his naked body. “Greedy shit” he said sternly, “ earn those yourself!”. Walking over to the fireplace he clicked his fingers and vanished up intot he night, leaving Ember standing forlornly in the darkness staring down at the title belt, or the sheet, or ... "... it isn't that small, is it?"
