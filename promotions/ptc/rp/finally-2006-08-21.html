Date: 2006-08-21 What can you say? For the most part it looks like some people just don't give a damn anymore. They don't care about their federation, or their stable, their families or anything. Look at this event so far. It's looking to be pretty pathetic. Of course just having two of the teams listed for this match in it makes it a veritable laughing stock of a match. Only some things can be done to save it, to save PTC. One of those was to get a few serious teams to compete in it too. Two comedy acts, two spoofs, two farces against two serious teams. If you can call them that. The one thing that's so funny is how things are actually perceived inside of wrestling. People look at people like Lia Vlassy, Shadow, our ally in GCW in the alliance between the stables known as Dies Irae and the KnightMare Brigade and they say…. she is a fake. Her lover, Virgil Ambrosi is a fake. If I told everyone out there that my brother, Grayson, could grow fur, fangs and claws they'd stare at me in disbelief. They'd look at me and call me nuts, crazy or anything else that would help them determine that it wasn't true. I could swear to it up and down. My brother, Grayson is a werewolf. A garou by what he's been told by others of that kind, they call themselves. This is the reason why his hair is of the colors it is. In that other form his 'fur' is the same soft gray color. Then again, if I told them I was sleeping with an angel, they'd just look at her, at her tits more likely, and they'd nod their heads and agree. But if they got the opportunity to see her in her full glory, with her wings spread out from her back, they'd go check themselves into a loony bin, or give up drugs and alcohol forever. Why? Because it just can not be true in their eyes. But then again, they don't show that in the ring. Like Lia and Virgil won't go around biting people's necks in the ring. Dani doesn't spread her wings anymore then any other wrestling high flier. And Gray… believe me, if he ever shifted in the ring on TV, it would just cause an uproar. And GCW would have to play it off as camera effects. But even though it would be undeniably true, no one would buy it. Yet, they look at people like the Killer Pirates and anything they do in Global. They look at a couple of clowns, in full definition of a comedy based actor who uses make up or whatever to become a character. It doesn't matter if only one of them claims to be a clown, they both are. But they come out to the ring, telling everyone that they're some sort of clown and pirate from where? Outer space? Yet no one gives them crap about it? Why not? Is that not just as fantastic as a werewolf or angel wrestling? Or the fact that I, myself… Joshua Brand, have shown signs of inheriting his mothers abilities of empathy? But no, we can't talk about that type of thing. Yet people like the Killer Pirates can talk about all their treasure, and their trips to Tortuga. It's real though, because they're not some fantastical made up creature? Got news for you boys…. Tortuga is a tourist trap now off the coast of Haiti, and it's not been a pirate hideout since the 17th century. It's covered in beaches, hotels and whatever else the government of Haiti wants to put on it. In fact boys, Pirates? Killer Clowns? They're just as made up as anything else. There haven't been real Jack Sparrow type pirates since the end of the 17th century. Got some news for you guys, your hero Jack Sparrow is a movie character, and they didn't even use the real Tortuga for the movies. It was an Island that Disney owns in the Caribbean that's pretty much all… man made. Or how about this one? Team Baked. Yes, I'm from Seattle. I've seen the guys who macramé themselves into a couch smoking a bowl. But when they smoke that much, they're not able to really do much else. But would you lead us to believe that you're stoned at all times? You're just so out of it and stupid that you can barely make it to the ring? Yeah, when we faced you before, it was almost that way. I'll have to give you this one. But still… Team Baked, Twice Baked, anything like that… how are they believable in their antics and things that live part of their lives on the other side of the Veil are not? This Veil you ask? That which separates the human's visual world from the true reality. But I guess that is the arrogance of our kind. If we can't see it, then it can't be. Unless you're someone like the Furious Fists of God. Faith is great guys, but come on. We include you here, because they say that we can not be. That others like us can not be. Yet you guys throw your faith behind something that has never been scientifically proven to exist outside of the Veil. I'll give you that, my girlfriend's wings and her grandfather still being alive after all this time, and him being an Angel too? Yeah, kinda proves part of your stuff right. But have you ever seen them? Have you ever seen a real angel? Have you ever seen your god? Or even seen his influence in a "Real" world miracle? And no, not things that are intended to work already, like rain during a drought or anything else that people can pray for. No a really, true miracle. No, you haven't. But it's in the book you proclaim to be the truth. Even though it's been rewritten so many times over the last few centuries, let alone in the last thousand years or so. To the winner go the spoils, and one of those things is the ability to rewrite history to match your own designs to it. So I've been told while I contemplate things for PTC, to look at it in a way that doesn't mention things such as my brother's condition and the likes of that. But I have to say this on that notion… Fuck that. I am what I am. It's my genetic code that was given to me. And my brother has accepted what he is. And Dani is beginning to accept her life. If you're willing to stretch the imagination for the likes of stoners, Killer Clowns and Pirates, or even religious zealots… then why can't you accept some of the rest of us for what we are? Is it that hard? I don't think so. ~Sol Duc Hot Springs~ ~Olympic Peninsula, Washington State~ The group of 5 had gotten to the resort right on time as Gray had planned it. His new truck, a replacement for the newer suburban that had just recently been totaled was able to haul everything they all needed for a two to three day outing, and seat them all comfortably for the trip. They'd taken the ferry from Seattle's waterfront to Bainbridge Island and stopped by Margarette's to ensure she had everything and then made the two hour haul for the center of the peninsula's national forest, which meant going all the way north to Port Angeles, where if you tried hard enough you could piss on part of Canada and then take the south road down to the Sol Duc/ Hoh Rainforest area. Once here, it was time to unpack, get settled and think about other things. For Gray, it meant being able to breathe for a bit, since there would be no ways the people who were trying to hunt down and find Alexis would find her out here on this short of notice. When they got to Portland, that could change. They had proven to be quite resourceful so far that night just a few weeks ago when they ran his truck off the road. The night that Alexis found out one of the reasons why Blaise and Aine had assigned him to act as her guardian while she was working with the Order, and using the Order's resources to help her friend Lia "Shadow" Vlassy. Gray though, of course was a work-a-holic and always found a way to get time to get his other work done. Some would look at Gray and see his 6' 4" muscular frame, long gray hair, beard and all of his tattoos and say… freak! Just like not many would say that he was what he truly was, most can't believe the man has a bachelors of science in biology from U-Dub. Let alone the other studies he did after joining the Order and becoming an 'expert' at looking at the biological side of things that weren't… Natural. The cabin that housed Alexis, Margarette and Gray last night had a wooden swing in front of it's porch. The two cabins were side by side, only a ball toss from the edge of the Sol Duc River. Gray sat there wearing his sweats and actually wearing a t-shirt as the sun was just coming over mountains to the east. It was very much morning, which meant at this altitude and setting, it was in the low fifty's, unlike Seattle and the other low lying areas. On his lap, his portable laptop computer. He was using the satellite card provided by the Order to continue some research work he'd taken upon himself over the last few weeks while he had a few hours to himself as the earliest riser of the group. But he wasn't usually behind certain people. Namely, Alexis. But on this morning, she was getting up at the same time as the other women. They had things planned. "Hey, big guy. What are you doing?" Gray turned his head slightly and saw Alexis was out and raiding the coffee pot that he'd set over the campfire he was sure to make as soon as he was out here. He wasn't as susceptible to the elements as others, but the comfort was still nice. "Just looking over the rest of the data from the tests on Lia and Virgil. I want to make sure we didn't miss anything that could have been involved. I had the rest of the samples sent to the main labs down in Texas and they're just getting stuff posted up for me to look at on the full battery." Alexis nodded. She had started working with him to help Lia and Virgil. Two twelve hundred year old vampires that had lost their memories. Something that no vampire should ever do. Recently the effects of this were broken and the two had realized why they had always been close. Technically, even in their undeath, they were still husband and wife after all of these centuries. They had been destined for each other. But with the memory block broken on their own, the cause of this phenomenon was still unknown, and Gray was determined to figure it out. "Well don't spend all morning on that. You promised to show me how to go after some Salmon today. I know Dani wanted to do some hiking up to the falls this morning. The desk clerk told her it was amazing in the morning time with the mist still hanging in the air. She's breaking out her expensive camera for it. I understand you've declined to join us though?" "Oh, yeah. I'm going fishing later on. Too early for good salmon. They'll be closer to the surface when it gets a little warmer. Although Dani is the impressive one to watch." "Really?" She actually looked interested. Gray had already guessed she didn't get out much in Ohio when she had been a part of, well a convent. Even if that convent trained their nuns to be voracious killers of the kind of people that ended up saving her… vampires. He heard Dani come out of her cabin. The sound said she had her hiking gear on, backpack and all. Dani was actually an amateur photographer that had pissed off a couple of those that do photoshoots for her, telling them the best way to take the shot. Inside that pack was one of the first things she'd ever bought with a signing bonus and it was a new camera. "Ready Alex?" Alexis finished capping off the thermos she had for the coffee and moved to catch up with Dani as she was walking along. "Oh, I'm ready. Isn't Marge joining us?" Dani shook her head no. "Nah, she wants to spend some quality time with her sons, and she said she'd have a monster brunch ready for us when we got back. So it's just you and me." Alex didn't seem to mind. For many months she'd been trapped on Dawn's estate up in Canada to keep herself safe. Now she had guards around her all the time. As they walked away, Gray found himself watching instead of looking at his screen. Another person, the one that had decided not to go was now outside also. She was drinking a cup of tea she'd prepared for herself inside but had stepped out with Alex to take in the morning air. Gray step mother, Margarette just smiled as she watched his gaze linger for a few moments. When Dani and Alex were nothing but shapeless blobs to her vision, meaning only slightly less then that to Gray's enhanced vision… he finally decided to go back to work. ~A few Hours Later~ Some could say Joshua Brand was lazy. Some could say he wasn't disciplined and that he was just as bad as most of the people his age in this world. Well to be honest, he is. If it's not his music as a drummer and singer, wrestling or, ahem, Dani. He doesn't wake up for it if he doesn't really have to. By the time he'd gotten up, it was about ten degrees cooler outside which meant he'd be more comfortable. Of course, there are normal reasons to finally wake up, from having to relieve yourself, to the smell of your mother's cooking over an open fire. And the smell of… coffee. His one true vice addiction. When he was finally ready to go outside though, it was a scene he'd only half expected to see. He knew that his brother and mother had been warming up a bit to each other, but to see them both working over the outdoor stove and everything to get food ready on the large picnic table was a sight to behold. If he'd not seen it with his own eyes, his brother would probably have flat out denied it. "I take it Dani and Alexis went for that walk they were planning?" Gray looked over towards him and smirked. Josh's hair, long enough to go down to the middle of his back, was in complete and total disarray. "Yeah, they should be back any moment. But you know Dani and that camera if she gets a chance. Some of the other families were out here and ran in that direction when word had it that they one of the eagle nests appears to have some young ones in it and it's visible from the trail." Josh shook his head. She'd be sending that whole disc of pictures to her grandfather on the Rosebud Reservation in South Dakota. His name was Old Man Soaring Eagle after all. Josh attacked the coffee pot, using the biggest cup he could find. If it hadn't of been so hot, he would have contemplated just drinking from the pot. But it wasn't too smart to try that. "So, everyone nice and relaxed this morning?" Gray smirked at him. "Yeah, I know I am. Although I've been up for a few hours. I think I got an hour longer then the girls did even. Of course, you should feel relaxed this morning." Josh suddenly double took on that comment. He wasn't quite sure how to answer it. "What's that supposed to mean?" Gray just smiled wide. "The reason why I volunteered to have Marge stay in my cabin, and not yours." Josh put his hand to his chest, as if to plead any innocence to anything that he could have done wrong. And in his short twenty two years, he'd done plenty of wrongs. Gray shook his head and gave a wide smirk. "Hey, what is that supposed to mean?" Josh's face sunk though as the answer came. But not from Grayson, from his mother… "What he's trying to say, Joshua… is that you two are loud. Especially her. It was hard enough to not have the urge to do like I did when I caught you with that one girl when you were seventeen and just walk over to the door, pound on it and tell you to shut the hell up." Josh turned to the small woman who as a native of France, but had now had lived in the states for going on three decades. "MOM!" She smiled wide at him. "Josh, if you think I don't know what you and Dani do behind closed doors, especially when I'm near by, you're grossly mistaken. You may have the gift like I do, but if it's one thing you have not learned yet is how to block others. In fact, you broadcast it. I've had to live with that since you were much younger. And Dani is louder then any of your other girlfriends." Gray couldn't help it, he busted up laughing. That was more then Josh wanted right now. He had just been lectured on how loud he and his girlfriend got by his own mother. Josh reached out and actually swatted his brother to the back of his head. Gray shot around to look at his brother. Gray always took such moves as a challenge by the smaller Brand. Josh cut him off before he could say anything though. "Not like you've not tapped that ass yet." Gray's mind read that really quick. He did all he could do to not just flat out punch is brother right in the face. Gray's anger management classes had actually been working. "I've what?" "You… Alexis. Don't tell me that you two aren't riding the hobby horse man. You two can't look at each other without falling into a day dream." Gray chose to cut this one off before he got too pissed off. "Are you questioning my integrity in my duties?" Josh stopped right there and took a few steps back. "Dude, she's good looking. You spend all day and all night with her. And you've got two empaths here. It's not hidden to us." "Look, there has been nothing of the sort happen. It's not right. I'm her guardian. I'm doing a job." Josh smiled. "And I'm Dani's guardian and we…" Gray turned around abruptly from him and took a few steps. "I'm not Kevin Costner in some Hollywood movie about bodyguards and their charges." Josh looked to be loosening up for some reason. "You can't say you don't find her attractive though." Gray turned around to face him again. "Hey, won't deny she's easy on the eyes. But you've already pointed out… I also find Aine's assistant, Kaylee, attractive and you're the one who gives me the most shit about going out on that dinner date with Dawn. I'm a single man. I'm allowed to look at attractive women. Even fantasize if I feel the need to." Josh's facial expression went to almost devilish. "Dude, your attraction to Dawn was almost stalker level. If she knew…" Gray took a few more steps towards his brother. "I will not have you make such claims…" "Gray… you had her middle name as your password on your computer for months!" That stopped Gray in his tracks. "Look, Little Wolf. I'll admit it, okay. I don't have what you have. I don't have someone like Dani. I've admitted to you before that I'm wary of it. I've got a reason to not want a relationship. Remember, there are times where I'm not exactly… myself." Josh threw his arms up in surrender. "Dude, you've shown your true self to two women so far. One is a nut case and one used to be a nun. Both of them give off the same signals, or have. You just need to fucking get a grip and get some balls and do something about it. Dawn only saw you as a prepared demonstration. But Alex, she saw you not only shift, but she saw you in action." Gray just looked down at the ground. "Twice." Still no reaction from Gray. "And the last time, she grabbed a rifle when you were in trouble and saved your hairy ass." Something had just happened. Josh didn't believe it, and for sure Margarette didn't either. Josh had just won a war of words with his brother. Twenty Two years, and he'd never done that before. Gray's shoulders slumped. He wasn't used to being on the loosing end of the verbal sparring. This was his little brother who barely got through high school due to his other ways. His little brother who he trained how to fight. His little brother who was giving him relationship advise when he wouldn't even finally do what he wanted in his own relationship. But he couldn't argue any of it. And Josh decided to capitalize on it all. While Gray was looking down, not paying attention, he struck. A football style tackle from the smaller Brand managed to take his brother down into the dirt. Gray was trained though, almost to the level of instinct. He rolled with the impact and tossed his brother off of him. "Alright, you two stop it. I'll not have one of your wrestling matches while I'm fixing brunch here!" Margarette had grown up with these two. Usually more rivals then siblings. But when they did get along, they were still boys; still rambunctious. Josh jumped onto his brother again, and started to use his own training, that of Kali-Escrima to try to lock at least one of his arms and keep him down. Josh was going to go for a win on both fronts here. All was going well, but was about to get worse. Margarette was yelling something at them about stopping when Dani and Alexis both walked up. Dani looked down at the pile of Brands on the ground as they were grappling. Dani didn't take time to ask for any explanation though. "What the hell are you two doing?" Neither answered her as the old sibling blood had run it's course. Gray powered his way to his feet and then whipped his brother off of him, with a show of the same force. A guttural growl escaped from his mouth as he did, giving away that he'd cheated in doing so. However, that same growl told Dani other things were going on. "What the hell are you two… STOP!" Josh dove for his brother again, catching him around the waist and forcing them both down into the mud on the riverbank. Dani didn't bother to ask any further. The sight of them 'fighting' got her madder then a wet hornet. She quickly marched right past them and went inside her cabin, slamming the door shut behind her. A few moments later, she walked back out. She still had her hiking gear on, but was now carrying her bow and shoulder bag that went with it. Alexis had moved over to try to talk to the boys but Dani was walking back down the trail she'd come from. Margarette grabbed Alexis, the one that was close. "It's nothing, they're just messing around." Alex turned just in time to see Dani disappearing down the trail. "You might want to tell that to her." Dani's power walk had her at a significant lead. Margarette decided to take matters into her own hand. She reached down and grabbed two handfulls of hair. One raven black the other gray as the moon. "Alright you two, enough." Both stopped and came up to their feet. "Now, who's going to go tell Dani that this wasn't what she thought it was." Josh looked at the quickly disappearing figure. "Shit." Gray looked at the woman he considered his little sister, also. "Crap." Gray looked at his brother. "She'll need cool off time first. Otherwise she'll take both of our heads off." Josh nodded. "Good that gives us time then." "For what?" Josh smiled wide. "Best two out of three. Loser talks to Dani to explain." Margarette let go of their hair and threw her arms up in defeat. "Come on Alex, at least someone will enjoy my cooking. You two hurt each other, and you get nothing." She put her arm around the smaller mouse of a woman and directed her over towards the table as Josh again tried to take his bigger sibling down. ~An Hour or more later~ Josh was getting tired. After that bit of impromptu sparring with his brother, he'd engaged in. But after winning the war of the words, he did not win the war of muscles. Gray, though, isn't above cheating against his brother. It's the only time he gets a chance to due to the rules and regulations of the Order in dealing with paranormals. But that meant that Josh had to head out. He'd quickly taken a shower to get the mud off of him, and changed his clothes. He didn't know how the trails would be, and wasn't as adventurous as Dani was. He'd gone jeans where she was a shorts wearing girl. However, even in the day's sun, he opted for his jean jacket to cover his now scratched and cut arms to hide what had happened. Alex had filled him in on where they'd gone and the spot she'd mentioned seeing some steelhead and rainbow trout just below the surface. She's shot things with a camera for the morning, and now she wanted to take out some agressions. He saw her up on a rock, the bow pulled back and her fishing arrow loaded. She was going to hit one. He knew very few times that she actually missed with this rig. He stopped, not wanting to break her concentration while she aimed. She loosed the arrow with a snap and it hit the water. It didn't take but another second to see she'd hit her target and she started to reel it in. He then climbed up onto the rock and got up there just as she pulled what looked like a rather large steelhead trout. "Nice one. Can I claim it for dinner?" She looked over her shoulder at him. "Yeah, I guess. I won't be able to eat this whole thing. But there are a couple more down there. Just have to get them closer to the top. This cold water is pretty dense." She didn't seem to be still pissed off. "I lost, so I got volunteered to come out here and tell you that we weren't fighting." "Could have fooled me." She took what looked like a smaller fish she'd caught before and tossed it, with a cut going up it's belly, into the water to act as bait to bring a few more fish closer to the top. "Heh, we were having a discussion and I took the first shot. Ended up being a little brotherly sparring. Nothing more." "Good." "Sorry." She didn't bother taking aim with her bow. She just turned to confront him. "Why is it that we have to have all this fighting though!" "What?" He knew she was under stress. Their recent confrontation had led to her doing things that were still affecting her. She'd done things that she still couldn't fathom, and it wasn't doing all that well. Not to mention the aftermath where their own friend, Blaise McClancy beat the living hell out of Josh to teach him a lesson didn't set well with her at all. "Everything. Why does it always have to be settled with violence? I get enough of that in my job. I don't need you guys fighting all the time." Josh did the usual Brand reaction to being confronted with words they couldn't fight. He just looked towards his shoes. "Sorry. But we're guys. If you're still up about Blaise's…" "I don't know if I am or not. You were a dumb shit on that. I wanted to beat him back for doing that, but I half understood why he did it." Josh couldn't argue. "No, I think he finally taught me a lesson. His points were valid and he was right. I don't listen to things put on paper. And if he did get to that point, it wouldn't have served my purpose for even listening to that bitch anyway. I'd loose you." Dani's face softened instantly. "Why did you go out there?" This was the one person he could never lie to. "I had heard you and Blaise talking. Through the door. You speaking the same things that have bothered me for months. I don't want to loose you. Ever. Not now, not in a few years, not in a century. But you, you're going to live forever. Me?" She got hard again. "You keep this shit up and you won't live too much longer! You almost made a deal with the devil himself!" "Actually his brother." Wrong answer. She threw the fish at him. He ducked, thankfully. "Don't you get it?" He looked up at her. "That's the problem, I do get it. You and I are meant for each other. But technically we're two different species. Even you mentioned it." He slid his hands into his pockets, bringing them high up onto his torso, as he continued. "I've got a lot of things on my plate, and I'm worried about you. After I'm gone, who's going to be the next one to take up as your guardian? Who will be the next one the Order assigns to watch over you?" She grinned. "I'm a big girl, I can watch myself, Josh." "Just like Blaise said, how do we know?" She had an answer for him. "Well, I'm the one who scored a kill in the last fight." She had a point, one he couldn't argue. "And that's another thing. What if he'd killed you?" She didn't answer. That was something she'd not wanted to think about. While she pondered though, Josh approached her. "I'm sorry. I'm a dipshit. I've done many things wrong. And you're still here. Hell, most of my antics have run off women for years." "Not bad for one that used to be a tomboy, out wrestle you and you wanted nothing to do with." He shook his head. "Oh, no. I've told you this before. I wanted you from the day you came into the training school and kicked my ass. It just took four years to get there. And you're worth it." His left hand in the pocket hit something as he embraced her with his now freed right arm. He knew what it was: another thing he'd been a dipshit about for months now. He looked over and saw the falls, the Sol Duc falls as they cascaded down the mountain side. A place that had been traditionally blessed by the original people of the area. Those that were a 'cousin tribe' to his own. "You know, I think I have learned one lesson from all of this, though." "What's that?" "Well actually, two. The first one is… don't make the same mistake Faust did. At least you all stopped me before I had sold my soul to any of those bastards." She cocked and eyebrow at him. "Good lesson to learn. What's the other one." Josh hesitated, pulling his left hand out of the lower pocket. He then reached into the inside of the jacket to retrieve what he'd felt from the pocket on his left breast. "That I need to take care of things. Especially you… while I still can." He pulled out what he'd had hidden there, away from the site of everyone for months. A box, a small box that contained one thing. "I think it might be time to finally show you this." He opened the small box to reveal the three stone diamond ring that it housed, a platinum band that held everything together showed very little signs of age and tarnish. Dani's eyes just grew wide. "This was the same ring that my father gave to my mom when they decided to join together as one. She gave it to me last year." "It's nice." "And it's already been resized to fit you." Dani was off in another land apparently. She just asked: "What?" He pulled the necklace that he sometimes wore out from his collar that bore one of her favorite rings on it. He'd worn it for some time after taking it from her and put it on a chain. "I had it resized already for you to wear." "Why?" For once he had her beat. This was two verbal conquests in one day. "Best I don't tell you, but show you." ~Heh…~ You want to see it don't you? Why would we want to go that far? After months of being a dumb ass and finding every excuse to not ask her, I did. Even though if I wanted to really bring things up, in the eyes of both hers and my nations, the Rosebud Lakhota Sioux and the Saquamish, we were a couple it was time to finally make it official in the eyes of the other laws and traditions that abound. You want to know her answer? Again, why tell you when I could show you. But that would make too many of you happy to see that. Let's just say that when we got back to the cabins, things were rather good for us all. Dani was showing the ring to it's original owner, that she was finally wearing it. All was fine. Except for when Gray got up and left in his truck for what turned into a two hour outing. When he returned he had a surprise for us. Well everyone but Dani and I. He'd gone and bought a box of earplugs for them to use. Dani almost wanted to kick the shit out of him at that one.
