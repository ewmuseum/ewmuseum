Extreme Division Match

12 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in a poetry cafe. The time is around 10.30 pm so it's beginning to get busy. The camera sweeps around. The place is a mass of people talking. Most seem to be attiring black dress. The camera then pauses at a table. The man at the table is alone. He is wearing a pair of tight black jeans and a black T-shirt. He has a rolled cigarette held in his mouth. The man seems to be in deep contemplation. It is Limp. A women is on the stage at the front of the room re-sighting a poem about Fredric Eagle, but people don't seem to be interested. The cameraman sweeps through the smoky atmosphere until it is situated at the table of Limp. Limp notices the camera. He puts out his cigarette, turns to the camera and begins to speak. Limp- You know it's not been a good couple of weeks for me. Two matches lost and I don't even know who I am anymore. So many things have gone on. One minute I am a peace loving hippy. Then bam I lost to Mr. Mysterious, bam then I lost to Karina Wolfenden in the Cruiserweight Tournament and bam I go a little insane. I am the next UPWA World champ after all and I now face Fnord in the Extreme Division in PTC. Limp pauses to light another cig. At the same time the women on the stage ends her poem with great emotion and is disappointed as she now relies that no one was listening to her. She mopes off the stage. Then a man walks on stage with a banjo. He begins to strum away and sings a song. Unfortunately we can not understand his strong Scottish accent, so once again the crowd ignore. Limp then begins to speak again. Limp- And now after 2 disappointing losses I have to face Fnord. Quite frankly I'm sick of all this. Now yeah sure fine give this guy his 5 minutes of fame with the big star like me but come on is this guy really in the Extreme Division? If he or Masked Avenger or Peter Maxwell or Ashe and all the rest of these guys were in the movies they would be B Movie stars, you know no one really cares to much about them, just there for a quick cheap thrill. Which brings me to what is going down in this Extreme Division. Now I know this is America and you love to say "anything is possible" but whatever happened to earning it. You know, did I ever get a chance like that, in America or out of it. No I earned my place at the very top. Does this mean even drunks like Fnord get a shot at me? Can you imagine someone like that as Extreme Champion? They would have sold it by the next day for beer money to a bloke named "Dick" with a Dan draft problem. Oh my God or if Chris Wrestling won the title. Would we ever hear the end of it? Limp shakes his head. The Scottish guy on stage end by jumping to his knees and shouting the words "thank you". He then stumbles off stage. The stage then goes black and Cannibal OX begins to play through the cafe. Limp then speaks again. Limp- You know this whole system is f*cked. No one wants me to be champ. Everyone seems to want an angry champ. Not someone who gives love and peace. Should I give them what they want? Should I go back to my old ways? Or should I try and change the system. History is full of people who have changed the system for the better. Or maybe not. Martin Luther King, did he really change much. I guess maybe the obvious racism like "white only" drinking fountains and the like but racism is still all around us. Winston Church Hill, did he really get England through the war. Voted Greatest Britain, what did he really do, make a few speeches and a great self prompter. It seems to me only the bad guys, the evil got what they wanted. Hitler, yes he didn't win the war but damn did he do a lot. Germany very small country at the time almost took over Europe pretty much single handedly. Henry 8th. As evil as they get but he grew the British empire to mass proportions, created the protestant branch of the Christian Church which is world wide now, and created mind boggling wealth. Then lets not forget George Bush, the man everyone hates yet is president, everyone wanting Clinton back. But you know Clinton was no different he was just smarter. What would you prefer a big Rat you can see or tiny termites that you can't, slowly crumbling the foundations. But you know I'm getting off topic. I have barely even talked about Fnord, lets address this issue. Limp pauses to put out his cigarette and lights another, then speaks again. Limp- Fnord I don't know you to well and really I couldn't care to much about you, you know. I'm sure you got a lot to say about what your going to do to me. This my ass, that my ass and all that red neck slash bling bling crap. You know all things must pass and this is your time to pass. I'm sure we will give the crowd a medico match you know a few thrills. But you know your just another brick in the wall. Maybe I have lost two matches in a row but really I don't have time to keep up with your little games anyway. But you know that's when it didn't mean to much. You know the Extreme Title is on the line in this one well at least a couple of points to move up. But you know maybe it is just the same. That title represents the best and as I have said before the best of the best are always the evil ones. Do I want to be remembered as the best of the best because that usually means you get remember as evil and hateful. Is that what I believe in. Is that what I suffered all that pain for as a kid on the streets of Fajardo. Is that what I suffered floating on the line of life and death for 18 years. You know the positioning maybe on the line but I'm fighting for me. The PTC in the palm of my hand but f*ck that, f*ck the title and you know f*ck it all. I'm tired of playing the mind games. Maybe I have lost it, maybe I'm like Rocky and have lost the eye of the tiger in Rocky three. I just need it back and hell and what could be a better time then when I face Fnord. Then a match against Chivo at Circus of Pain. I'll write a song about this one day you know. It will be called "Limp is lost and then finds out that all he had to do was get back the eye of the tiger by beating Fnord and the rest of the two bit losers Roster of PTC". Snappy title! Limp ends his speech, as he does Lady Madonna by the Beatles fills the room with it's swingy kind of sound which puts a child like smile on Limp's face and he bops his head. The Beatniks around him don't seem to be impressed. The camera fades to an advert this weeks up coming Circus of Pain.
