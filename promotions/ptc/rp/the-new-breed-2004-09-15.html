The New Breed

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 Awe- A mixed feeling of reverence, fear and wonder. Admiration, adoration , amazement. Part I I look through the brackets, and I am amazed. Over one hundred top of the line competitors pitted against each other in the ultimate struggle to decide who is the greatest of us all. I see scores and scores of names. Some names I know, some names I don't. Some I care about, some I don't. A few of them catch my eye. Seymour Alamsly, Odium, and the one that pertains to me the most, Kasper Sky. I read about him in books, and listen to stories of him from the veteran wrestlers of the older years. They speak of how he can not be beaten, for he has something that no other in this tournament posses. His unique feature is that he is immortal. One who cannot die by the curse that is age. Some call the immortals gods. Gods who the ancient cultures of eons ago worshiped, for they brought good fortune, or terrible reigns of horror. Some gods were not as powerful as the other ones, and were known as the demi-gods. Gods whose power could not even begin to rival that of the greater ones, the movers and shakers of the heavens. I don't see Kasper Sky as a great god, I see him only in the small form of a lesser god. I am not intimidated taking on a man who will not die. In fact, I enjoy the challenge. Can you see it now? I can. In one corner, the immortal, the god, the man who walks amongst the stars, Kasper Sky. In the other, the eternally damned, the one who is condemned to the depths of hell, the bringer of darkness into young, pure hearts, Hell Itself. Once I am in the ring with him, I will change. My body will open up the blood vessels even more, my heart will pump twice as fast, my mind will be erratic with activity and impulses. Why? My body and soul alike know that they are in the fight for their lives. A mortal such as myself could not begin to fathom the thought of overpowering an unkillable machine, but until my dying breath, I know I will try. Not only does my body recognize the danger at foot, but so does my inner self, who I truly am, my soul. The forces that no scientific instrument in the world can pick up. The forces that set off no alarm, but are within us and are tuned specifically for ourselves, they will guide me through my match against Kasper. It will give me the one hundredth of a seconds edge I need to take down the immortal that will stand before me. The best part is though, once I cover Kasper, in those three seconds, I to will become immortal. The thousands of people who witness the match will stand on their feet in awe on how the unknown man from the World Wrestling Alliance just took out the widely acclaimed loner, Kasper Sky. The masses will watch in shock at how a man such as myself could take down the immortal all alone. The night it happens, I hope Kasper sleeps, because I hope he realizes where he went wrong in this tournament. I want him to reflect on how he underestimated me before he even knew me. He needs to learn that you shouldn't underrate, what you haven't seen. The truth is that nobody has seen me. I live and thrive in the darkness that is my lifestyle, I will only emerge to compete to show what kind of threat I am to my opponents, to the world, to the universe. Once I am victorious, I will move on to round two of the prestigious Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, and the process will repeat. Just like how a cell will split in half to multiply itself, so will my win counts. I will continue and spread my disease into this tournament, round by round, eventually winning my bracket, then take on the grandest prize of them all, winning the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. After that my career will sky rocket. I will be on magazine covers, on talk shows, all the fame and fortune a man could want. I won't covet those things yet though, because the first obstacle in my way, is not a man, but an immortal, that goes by the name of Kasper Sky. Part II There are some wrestlers that fans adore, some that the fans hate, and some who are both loved and shunned. All the wrestlers in this tournament fall into one of those categories. I look into the mirror at night wondering which I am. Sometimes I am embraced, sometimes I am cursed at, so I suppose I am on the half of the issues, I am both hated and loved. Sometimes when I compete, it is against a man that the fans love. My opponents like that just can not seem to do anything wrong in the eye of the public, so if I bring any harm to them, the fans reject me on sight. I can't help it, wrestling is a war between two men. It does not matter who you are fighting, or what people think of you. The only thing that matters in your mind when you step into that ring in front of thousands of people is just try to survive and win. In the heat of the moment, your mind is racing. Wrestling brings an adrenaline rush that nothing else on mother Earth can come close to. The wrestlers who don't enjoy what they do, don't enjoy who they are. I for one don't believe Kasper Sky loves what he does. He walks around with an I don't really care attitude. People such as Kasper are worthy of being in this tournament, but have no place in it. His mind is else where. He is weak with only one train of thought, that thought is a quest he was given since the time he was conceived, to find his long lost sister. I adore his persistence in trying to find her. The man is immortal so he must have been searching for her now for more than a century. It's time he gets a reality check. His sister surely must be dead. How do I come to this conclusion is simple. It's all in the odds of genetics. Kasper lost his mother at a young age, not to a murder or any violent death, but naturally. That makes her a mortal. His father also died, which makes him mortal as well, but one of them passed down the blessed gene of immortality to Kasper. It's all scientific, check it on a Punnet Square Kasper, open your eyes. Only one person in your family out of four will be immortal, and you are the immortal Kasper. Your sister is truly dead. The truth is harsh, but what in this world isn't. May the thought of your entire life being a waste of trying to find your sister follow you throughout our match. Hate me for it Kasper, because you should. You didn't know the truth until you heard it from me. I want to become the object of your hatred. The icon of all the evil things that dwell inside of you. Unleash your immortal wrath upon me Kasper Sky, because that is what I want from you. I want the fight of your life in our match, this is why I provoke you so much. It is not worth winning, if I win without you trying to literally kill me. It's all a game in my mind. The ring is my chessboard, if you care to do war, then make a move Kasper. I will counter you, then I will unleash my plan. To destroy on sight. It's not worth my time if you and I don't put on the match of the century. I want the match to go down in history as the biggest of all time. The match where the largest underdog on the face of the Earth, took down the mightiest of all giants. You will be embarrassed beyond belief, and I will laugh in the shadows at your defeat. I want your fans to stare in awe as I defeat you, one, two, three. Part III There are plenty of things to fear in life. In the times we live in, their are numerous things to fret over. You can't walk ten feet in this world without wonder who is behind you at all times. We don't have eyes on the back of our heads though, that is why you have to concentrate on what is in front of you. Fear what you see Kasper, because all you will see inside of yourself as we stare each other down. You will feel emotions you never knew that you had, think the unthinkable, but don't worry. That's normal when you face me. It is not all brute physicality that wins matches. Strength is one of the deciding factors, but there is something that the smallest of men can bring to the table, mind games. The human mind is brilliant and powerful, but weak at the same time. Just thinking about something painful can make you feel the hurt that you are dwelling upon. In one minute, it can change all on its own and forget all the patterns that it has learned over your whole life. After all, life is nothing but a routine. We wake up in the morning. Work during the day. Then sleep at night.Each of us have our own little patterns and perks. Each person has their own unique way of going about this game of life. The same way I go about in the ring Kasper. My style is like nothing you have seen or experienced in your many years of life. It's all fluid, but sometimes choppy. All grounded, but the next minute I'm soaring above you. I'm unpredictable Kasper, you can't understand me. Fear what you don't know Kasper, because what you don't know is me. I will not lie though. I have my own fears. I act and actually am a person that likes to be secluded from the world, but the truth is I'm afraid to be alone. Things happen when you are all alone thinking to yourself. Some of the outcomes are good, but some are dangerous. When you think about who you are and what you have made of yourself, it can be scary. Some people can't stand who they are, some love themselves. I am neither, until I win this tournament. The path to my inner peace goes through an enormous road, I call it a treacherous path, others call it a bracket. When I first received word that I was to face you in the ring, I was afraid. I did not tell the world I was, I hid behind a wall of arrogance about how easy it would be to defeat you, but I knew the truth about myself. My mind raced at the mention of your name for weeks, but now that I had time to think about it, you don't scare me any more. I am ready to take you on. I have always said the hours and hours of extra work I put into wrestling would pay off, and I believe it will when I take you on in the first round Kasper Sky. You are the test that my body and mind need, so I can prove not only to myself, but to the world that I am truly one of the best, if not the best wrestler in this world. Now I thank God that I get to face you, because it will make my first Golden Turnbuckle Tournament victory all the more sweeter. You will be the message I am sending out to the rest of the competitors. That message is don't fear the pain, fear me. Part IIII Do you ever look up to the sky and wonder why we are here on Earth? I do. On cold, dreary nights I drive up to the most isolated place on a hill that I can find. I lie down on the grass at the top of the hill, put my hands behind my back, and stare at the stars. I don't know if all those stars are still there, but they keep me dreaming. I used to laugh at those corny commercials that you would see on television, or the banners in the school classrooms that said in big, beautiful, golden letters that said "Shoot for the sky, because even if you miss, you'll land amongst the stars." Now that I realize what that meant in my older years, I appreciate it, and live by it. I know it's a huge goal to have of wanting to become the best wrestler in history, but even if I don't, I will surely be one of the greatest. When I die I want the world to reflect on what I showed to them. I want them to thank me for showing them that if you work towards what you believe in, that it will come true. Others may feel that I am already as good as I am going to get. That I can only go down from where I am now. To them I say there is no limit to how great I can become. My determination and drive only grows with age. Those are the two things I need to be the best ever. When I land among the stars, I will be at peace. If I died knowing I didn't try to be the best man that I could be, I'd be devastated in my final hours. I might even rush the dying process with a sudden outburst of depression. That is what I am trying to prevent by facing you Kasper, because if I did not give everything I had in our match, then the rest of my living days would all be a little bit worse, all because I knew I did not try my hardest. I wonder what you are doing right now Kasper. I wonder if you even care about this as much as I do. Sometimes I think the accomplishments and accolades you have received will mess with your mind. It will keep you oblivious to the fact that you still have to compete. I think you are a great man, but if you keep letting the fame get to your head, then I think you need to look back on who you truly are. I was not ready for this tournament a couple of months ago. I was coming in with no practice at all because in my mind, I had this tournament won. It took an old friend of mine to show me that the only way to win this tournament, is to get back to what got me to where I am today. I trained in the hearts of the bayou, running through swampland, chopping Cyprus trees for firewood, and swimming through the rivers of my hometown. It showed me who I was, and it gave me the extra boost I needed to win our match, and the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. When I started chasing this dream of becoming a professional wrestler, people laughed. Whenever I told my friends what I wanted to become, they all abandoned me, I got the cold shoulder from society. I did not care though, because it was, and still is, what I know is right for me. People who truly stand behind what they care for will always come out on top. Would you stick with being a wrestler if you lost the respect of everyone you knew your entire life Kasper? I don't think you would. Once our match is over, and I am walking up the ramp victorious, I want you to sit there in the arena lights, drenched in sweat wondering how somebody like myself, could beat somebody as hyped up as you are. Ending I am a new person in this business Kasper. I am not a big name yet, but I plan to become a household one. You are the old fox, one who has already shown the world what you can do, and how you can win. You won't beat me though Kasper, because every generation a new breed of men are born. Just as the moon over takes the sun, I will overcome adversity and defeat you. It's inevitable, some people would call it my destiny. Wether or not you choose to accept this fact is entirely up to you. Yet, I know after I defeat you, and the torch is passed down to me, I recognize the fact that somewhere in the world, a child that will one day defeat me is being raised with every breath that I take. When that day comes, I will put every ounce of energy in my body into that ring, just as I do every night, but if I should lose, I will accept it. I will sleep at night, but I know a fool such as yourself won't. The thing to focus on now though is my match with you, and when it's all said and done, I will win, as the arena, the city, the states and nations will all be speechless, as they watch, in awe.
