Aspiring for Approval II

28 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-28 Has it ever occurred to anyone that sometimes the good guy wins? Because right now I sure felt like something was awry. Everywhere I look: on television, in books, in films, in plays, in novels and even in real life (the ultimate approval for life imitating art), the hip thing to be is the anti-hero, the not quite good guy who does what he feels is best for the moment and nothing more. He doesn’t have the noble goal in mind, he doesn’t have the long term intention to strive for anything above doing what’s right in the moment and often he falls short of this goal. He gets away with it though because he has issues, he has problems, he has conflicts. Something about that just doesn’t feel right to me. Everybody has problems in life, it’s a given. The thought of someone being completely happy with their life just never occurs to most as it’s safe to assume that if you feel that way, there’s something you’ve forgotten about. But what I don’t get is why people enjoy to see other people’s misery being projected onto the silver screen or in the papers. Maybe it’s because they like the thought that someone can conquer their problems and rise above it all to do something good with themselves. There’s one problem with that though. They don’t do that, anti-heroes never have. They’ve always stuck and clung to their problems because without them they aren’t the same anymore, they become normal heroes, apparently things that people don’t aspire to anymore because they might be too unrealistic. This just smacks of wanting too much at once and in a sense the characters they admire aren’t realistic themselves. It’s come into my mind like this lately because frankly, I’m just getting pissed off with how much Adam Dick has been absorbed in the consciousness of the populace. Despite his attempted pandering to me talking about how we did such good business, there is no secret about who came out on top, irrespective of how much back and forth there was. Adam Dick came out on top plain and simple. I knew right from the start that I never would be able to thwart his spotlight simply because I was too bland, I was too normal, I was too… …I was too much of a good guy. He’d always had the edge, the controversy, the excitement. He had the problems but he did it with style and class, showing off his lack of care for authority and pushing people’s buttons. I was just another chump on the road to stardom for him because people want crazy things like that. In essence, he was ticking the boxes to anti-hero status all along the way, unconcerned with whether or not he was winning or losing. He just continued on carefree like all anti-heroes do, unable to see their consequences from inside their bubble. And the people love them for it. And there he was again, rushing into my life to take away the spotlight once again. He was always on cue to have the pithy comment, the biting return, the knowledge that isn’t quite available to everyone yet. Once again, playing the anti-hero to the hilt. I wasn’t letting him get away with this one though. Maybe it happened that anti-heroes were getting all the respect right now, but not anymore. For once, a good guy was going to win. *** A sigh of relief as the sweet, sweet nectar coursed through my body once more. The painkillers had been doing their job admirably and I was right there to appreciate it fully. I kicked back in my chair in the living room and let the time ease by, feeling relieved that the aches and pains would be gone again for another couple of hours. For another couple of hours I could relax and feel myself again. Thankfully Jessica was out for the afternoon, presumably busy with work or again agonising over some store which specialises in baby clothes and apparel. There have been definitely a couple of times when I’ve found her, gazing long and hard at some sort of onesie, holding back some sort of tear in her eye. If I could count them on one finger I would…but sadly I couldn’t. Ah I didn’t need to think about that one now. I had more important things to deal with. For one thing I had to deal with Adam Dick. It’s been an entire year of obsession with getting back to this position, to be in the position to do something in the GTT and now that I finally have it, of course he has to get in my way. He has to be the one who would try to be the deterrent, the one who would be an opponent. Just like he had been in AWC, Dick was being an ass again, pissing me off and doing his best to take me away from my game. I wouldn’t let him work his magic again though, no more taking control over my life and taking it apart. This time I was stronger, more in control, more powerful and more at one with myself. I was totally safe from any of his shenanigans. In fact, I was going to be the one who would be doing the messing. A solid knock on the door erupted from one corner of my hearing, slightly taking me out of my drug induced haze. I sighed and ignored it, content to just relax and have my afternoon. Nobody was going to take me away from this, nobody was going to let me forget this moment of peace that hadn’t come in so long. Whoever it was though was persistent. He pounded on the front door and I could hear the odd muffled voice too. Nothing specific though that I could figure out and I simply didn’t bother. Nothing was going to… ‘Mr. Murphy, this is the police! I need a word with you!’ Shit. I couldn’t deny that I’d heard that one. The grim realisation that they knew I was there wasn’t lost on me either. And then the panic set in as the random painkillers strewn about my living room kicked in. I sat up cold and looked around, taking it all in before immediately flailing around the room, sweeping it up and trying to hide everything that I could. It was all one concoction of a blur, whizzing past me and in at one specific moment I wasn’t even part of my body to my mind. So weird. ‘Hello officer how can I help you?’ Hmm….I don’t even know how I got to be at the door, let alone talking to the man who was staring back at me. ‘Ah Mr. Murphy…did I wake you?’ ‘…Yeah, I was asleep in the living room.’ ‘What was with all the noise I heard then?’ ‘I was naked.’ The cop reeled a little bit at that and it seemed like I’d got past the potential sticky situation. It was only then that I really looked at the man standing in front of me. He was relaxed and at ease with himself, but something about his cocky nature reminded me of something recently… ‘Excuse me…but have we met before?’ He paused and looked me up and down, memories stirring within him as he did so. Unknowingly he straightened up and bore himself fuller against me. It was an instinctual response. It was a proud response and immediately we both recognised where we knew each other from. ‘The park.’ ‘The park.’ A flicker of respect went back and forth, igniting the flames of effort within us. If we were animals it would be all too clear the competition being bred between us. If I was a bull, snorting and wrinkling my nose in anticipation, he was a proud lion, getting ready to pounce and rend and tear at his opponent. The entire moment was held for me, a nice moment of competition, something I shared with very few people. In a moment however it was gone. ‘Yes…anyway Mr. Murphy, I wanted to ask you a couple of questions if you have the time?’ ‘Sure, I’m not doing anything right now.’ ‘Good, well it’s really only two questions. Firstly…have you been in contact with Adam Dick in the past couple of days?’ Weird, that was kind of out of sorts considering how I’d talked to him so recently. Guess we’ll find out now. ‘Yeah actually. I ran into him at the hospital. He didn’t stay for very long but he was there long enough to give me some news.’ ‘Of what?’ ‘Oh just that we’re due to wrestle soon, nothing more.’ ‘Oh.’ He looked disappointed by this one, hoping that there was gonna be some sort of fantastic thread that I was going to tell him about. Like I was in on some sort of drug deal that would send him right to the top of the pile. Something like that anyway. He wasn’t going to be getting anything like that though this time. ‘Is there anything else officer?’ He looked up and immediately remembered that he was actually on the job. Shaking his head he continued. ‘Just one more thing. Will you give me a ring whenever you hear anything about him? We really need to talk to him.’ ‘Sure…but what for?’ ‘I don’t think you really want to know Mr. Murphy. All that’s necessary is you call us once you’re in contact with him again and we’ll do the rest alright?’ ‘I suppose.’ ‘Alright thank you Mr. Murphy. I’d love to stay and ask you some more questions but I have things to get back to. Have a good day.’ He prowled off back to his car at the end of the road and I stood and watched him from afar. He was constantly watching and looking out for anything, much like Adam was except he was far more confident and strong in his movements. As he got into the car I noticed that somebody else was in there with him. Another person that I knew oh so well. Ellis Nash. Well, that certainly made things more interesting. *** I heard the padding of wet feet across the boards upstairs, letting me know that Jessica had had her shower, finally. I don’t understand why she was taking so long these days to have a simple shower. It was like something was bothering her. I couldn’t think of anything to merit being this hung up about this kind of stuff. Ah well, I’m sure she’ll be fine. For now, I needed to get a shower, relax my muscles after a long day training and then maybe I can just sleep, hold Jess close to me. The past couple of days had been uneventful for the most part, my routine of buckling down for training was coming along nicely, getting myself in the kind of shape that I felt could be in the right area for tackling Dick. I couldn’t ignore his constant, incessant attempts to mess with my business. He’d done it plenty of times before but I wouldn’t be letting him get to me this time. If it wasn’t messing with my relationships with Jessica or Sasha back then, it was sending me on wild goose chases, forcing me down roads that I never intentioned on going down. He had this kind of knack at getting to me, but through people. I hadn’t seen him get to me literally yet. And that sure as hell wasn’t going to happen this time. Ah, a shower would help ease the situation, let me mull over the varied possibilities for this match up. I prodded the bedroom door open to find an image of complete beauty…and sadness all at once. My heart deflated the instant I walked in. Something was wrong definitely…and I gleaned the reason once I focused on what was in her hand. Sitting on the bed was a naked Jessica, in plain view for anyone who wished to see her. The feminine curves and birthmarks all sparked a slight arousal in me but something I quenched as I looked at the object in her hand. A pregnancy test was in her hand. ‘Jess? What’s wrong?’ Her look at me told it all but I couldn’t desire to play the arrogant know it all this time, at least I intended not to. She reacted slowly, going to put some clothes on. I surged over beside her to halt her and try to talk to her. ‘Jess, please talk to me.’ ‘What is there to say? This is all that needs to be known.’ She tossed the pregnancy test in my face and I let it fall into my lap. The clear indication was what I feared but one that I couldn’t presume. I was trying to be supportive and the partner she wanted me to be. ‘I’m sorry…you know we’ll keep trying Jess, as soon as this…’ ‘As soon as what Jack? I’ve been waiting half of my life to bear a child and right now everything is conspiring to take it away from me. Why haven’t you been here to talk to me about it? Why haven’t you been here?’ ‘Jess you know that I have an important match…’ ‘Oh please Jack there must be some things in your life that are more important than stupid wrestling.’ I stopped dead cold. I didn’t flinch after she said those words and I could feel the anger burning in my stomach. I tried to fight it…but there was never a question of who was going to win that one. ‘How dare you…’ ‘What?’ ‘How dare you insult me like that…’ ‘How was I? Oh come on Jack you know how insignificant…’ ‘Shut up!’ She moved backwards in complete dismay as the tears flowed from her eyes in utter shock. My hands went to my face to wipe them clean but I never would be. ‘You…I…I’m…’ ‘No words Jack, that’s it.’ ‘What are you talking about?’ She was rushing for clothes now, just tearing them out of the closet and pulling them on as quickly as she could. Oh crap, oh crap, oh crap. ‘I’m going to leave for a couple of days, it’s clear you don’t need any distractions from your precious fighting. Don’t try and call, because I have no intention of answering. Besides, you’ve much more important business than your responsibility to your family right?’ I stood shocked and watched as she left. Oh you’re fucking dead Adam. This is your fault again and I’m gonna make you pay for it. I have a date with destiny and nothing is going to stop me from getting there. Nothing. Not even people walking out on me. Sitting alone in my room I could feel an ache travelling all over my body. Subconsciously I reached out for the painkillers again…
