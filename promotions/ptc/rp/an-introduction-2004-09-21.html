An Introduction

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 A Street dark and shadowy, a place where the tough survive and the survivors are carnivores. This place is a simple hell as many people walk in it pondering whether or not their lives are worthless. A single man stands in the center of the street. Looking about he grins nodding as he enjoys the miserable solitude of this hell. Looking about he notices each and every shadow. The man begins to walk his mind wandering over the future as he looks about his mind hiding nothing his intentions clear. Cross him he will hurt you in ways you can only have nightmares about. He has been known as many things throughout his career he knows this. One thing he prides himself on is being the underdog and underestimated. Why do they all continue to believe this will be a simple walk in the dark for them. Turning into a dark alley a single street urchin walks up to him and says something. “Sir could you spare me a few coins? Also walk with me through the alleyway?’ The man nods as he walks with the urchin as he quickly turns and darts down the alleyway and watches the man disappear into the shadows as suddenly several men charge into the alleyway behind the man and attack him. The urchin grins as he walks over and turns around the corner and speaks up as the ruckus quiets down. “Fella’s how much did you get from him? Suddenly a huge form steps from the shadows and looms over the urchin. “They got a beating is what they got kid! You would do well to head the advice that there are more things to be scared of in the dark. I am one of those things.” The urchin bolts fast as he runs hard down the street disappearing into an building as the man walks and looks down at a poster about a tournament. The tournament shows Shaman vs Reginald Atkins. The man nods and walks forward as he remembers. “Reginald Atkins you did well against me and I must commend you for your efforts. Now I have moved to round two and guess what I face a female named Shadow. Am I scared of facing a female? No I am not scared. Merely concerned with what she thinks it will take to defeat me. I am going to treat her as I would any other opponent. There will be much pain to follow in our match. Whether she wins or I win this will be something she will not soon forget. I am an equal rights type of person. I hate everyone equally and I think everyone has an equal right to a beating.” Turning he walks slowly towards the streetlights and the world of the city leaving the cold dark alley behind as he stops at the street corner looking back into the alley and smiles. “It is not everyday someone wants to test the hunter by becoming the hunter. They are merely beasts being led to the slaughter. Their only purpose is to provide for the strong and the weak shall only be cattle. Why do I say this? It is simple because it is true. The hunters are those who blaze their own paths and do not follow or rely on those around them. A survivor will do this on his or her own. Will I survive will I win this next round? It is believed by many that I will not? Why is that you ask. Simple really I don’t bend down and kiss their asses. I will and always have done for myself and I will survive. I am not a person to be taken lightly and I believe this Shadow person will begin to understand that very soon.” Stepping towards his right he turns and begins walking to one of the big sky rise buildings as he looks upwards and begins to walk to the doors. He enters and walks straight for the elevator and looks through the list and looks at the purposely omitted thirteenth floor button. He smiles and whispers. “Superstitions” Watching as the elevator rises to the highest floor available to him and he steps out onto an abandoned floor which seems to have never been in use. He begins making his way slowly across the floor towards some stairs on the far side. Stopping he looks out over the city from this vantage point as he reaches up and runs his hand over his chin slightly. “The world’s perception changes as you move about does it not? Why do people believe me to fear anything? I am not where I am for being afraid. I respect fear. I use it as fuel to feed my desire. That is why I fear nothing. It is merely an inspiration for much better and grander things. For an example of this I provide you with a glimpse into my early past in the WWA. I had to face some of the greatest opponents ever. Was it a mad house or was it merely an early hold by insanity to stay around? You make the choice I am going to speak with actions soon.” He turns and raises a remote control as he points it out at another building as the video add becomes a wrestling match with the WWA logo in the corner. He smiles as he watches it. <> "Devil Man" by White Zombie blast through the PA system. He examines them as he grabs a few things and then stops, turns around and waves for the security team to bring down a wheel barrel full of toys. Such as a Bumper from a Volkswagen Bug, Broken hockey stick, Sledge hammer, a broken Godzilla toy, a tomahawk and a FEW BOTTLES OF FULL TEQUILA. His shakes seem to be rather odd as his muscles clench and unclench in his jaw. He turns and looks about and then throws the remote control across the room as it shatters against the wall. “You see my first shot at a WWA title came against the Outlaw a wrestler who I had respected highly, but it wasn’t the shot that got my attention it was the loss I had against him. He opened my eyes to a whole different world of events out there, Events that I could conquer and win. I am not scared of what I am and will be. I am not scared of an opponent beating me. I am more afraid of going out there and not trying and losing rather than go out there and try and lose. I have faced many opponents over the years and I have ended many careers in the WWA. Magician and Troy Walcott come to mind right away. I even drove Wild Thing Chris Rogers out of the WWA. I have made an impact in the WWA and I am there till the end. Now I have entered this tournament to represent the Federation as a whole. A tribute as you will to the fans and people who supported me on the way there.” Turning he walks to a door opening it he steps through and begins to ascend a set of stairs beyond. He walks through the darkness and then opens a door to the roof top. Looking out past at the world lying beyond the roof’s edge he walks out and waves a hand out there looking at the city. “Take a good look Shadow at the uncertain world beyond. Your problems and pretty words won’t save you come the day we meet. Your problems with your old friends should be resolved. You need to come to the ring complete and full of life and energy. Do not dwell in the past I believe you are better than that. You have my respect as an opponent and someone with skills and abilities. Do not let the confusions of others cause you to slip and fall.” Looking out at the distance with the oranges and whites and neon’s of the cities lights his voice lowers as he speaks. “They are but mere ants in the greater scheme of things Shadow. Your destinies path has brought you crossing my path. What will the outcome be only time can tell. Win or lose does it seem possible for you to be involved in matters other than what is at hand? Concentrate on your current task and you will find answers amongst your thoughts and actions. You were betrayed big deal everyone will be betrayed in their lives.” Looking about he leans against the railing and closes his eyes. “I have rambled on and presented you with a glimpse at what has brought me here to this world and what things I use to drive myself forward. I am 30 years old Shadow and I am nobody to take lightly. One thing all of my opponents take with them is the fact that I do not nor will I ever quit or give in. I won’t hide behind false pretenses or claim that I am the best. I am not cocky and I will let everyone know my actions will speak volumes for me.” Stopping and frowning he looks at his hands and then raises up closing his eyes taking a deep breath. “Sometimes I tend to ramble on about things. People will begin to lose interest in what I am saying. Perhaps I do this to mainly just get things off my chest. I may say a bunch of things that make absolutely no sense, but then again that is just me. Your words make sense to me. Your confused as hell and very uncertain as to what is going on with your friends. I pay attention at least to what is going on with my opponents. You need to get your head in the game Shadow, because if you don’t your going to get hurt seriously. I am not threatening I am merely telling you as it is fact, you and me a match another round. We need to get this thing started and both of us need to have our heads into the game.” Stopping as he looks over the edge of the building he grins. “This is like a game of chess Shadow. I move my pieces and you move yours. One simple problem is this. You could have looming defeat over your head and so could i. Will your problems begin to create doubts in your head or will I defeat you when you are at the top of your game? It doesn’t matter to me. You wanna know why Shadow? It is a simple answer to tell you. The answer is this because now that I have sent a simple message to you about getting your head back into the game the real message will come.” Turning his eyes as black as night he stares at the dark sky beyond. “Fear is never boring and I am going to be the waking nightmare that you see in the week to come. What are you going to do when the ring opens its bloody and battered arms for you? I can say to you one thing. Win or lose you are going to suffer.” Stepping up he stands on the edge of the building looking out at the street below as he grins and then leaps disappearing over the edge as a rope zips off the roof disappearing into the darkness as it suddenly becomes tight and silence is heard.
