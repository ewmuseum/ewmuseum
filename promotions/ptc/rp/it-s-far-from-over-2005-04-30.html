It's Far From Over

30 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-30 To say that Tessa Windsor was ecstatic after advancing on to second round of the Primetime Central Cruiserweight tournament would be an understatement. She was more or less speechless, considering tournaments weren’t her forte. And considering her last three losses going into these type of events, she’d never expect to walk away victorious at a time when things seemed to be going downhill at lightning speed. Yet she did, and now the focus wasn’t on just getting past round one, it was on getting all the way to the finish line. Getting all the way would prove to be quite a difficult task though, especially considering she had more than her fair share of responsibilities to take care of. It didn’t help any that on top of all that where she came from, April was the rainiest month of the year. So, that meant chances on getting out of the house and to the gym were slim to none, especially considering Chris had beat her to it. Instead of dumbbells, Tessa found herself lifting the weight of pressure. Even though no one in the house cared what happened from here, Tessa had the pressure of not only her conscience, but Dee’s as well. The two curled up on opposite sides of the leather couch that stretched across the width of Tessa’s living room and let the silence speak for itself, until Dee finally did. "Well, you did it." Smiling from ear to ear, the only thing lacking in her enthusiasm was the tone of her voice. But it was understandable considering she had little to be excited about; she, just as dozens of others, had anticipated Dawn McFarlane versus Tessa Windsor rematch. It was her motivation for leading Tessa astray from the world of retirement, yet it crumbled right before her eyes with her eyes with a simple three count. "Now, you’ve just got to keep doing it." Even Tessa, who still was in ecstatic disbelief over her first round win, seemed to be lacking in enthusiasm as she allowed silence to take control once more, before realizing that she was the one being spoken to. "Yeah, I will," she replied, substituting the uneasiness running through her mind with a small smile of her own. "I at least owe you that much more for getting this far in." "Good, then go out and make an example out of RAD. It would be so rad." At the very least Dee’s cheesy remarked forced a chuckle out of Tessa, if only because of how incredibly half-witted it truly was. "If it keeps you from making a bigger fool of yourself, then sure," Tessa replied, as Dee’s cheeks slowly began to match the cherry-colored lipstick she’d put on earlier that morning. "You couldn’t possibly do that." Joining in with Tessa‘s chorus of giggles, neither Dee nor Tessa noticed the strapping six-foot-four blonde who glared on silently from the living room doorway with a newspaper tucked in between his bulky arms, which he kept folded against his chest. "Ahem." If there was something this blonde always seemed to be demanding it was attention, which is why neither Dee nor Tessa seemed all to surprised to find the likes of Chris Storm intruding his way into their conversation . Judging by the absence of his usual smug smile, he was hardly in the mood for jokes. Tessa, on the other hand, found it hard to believe that Chris Storm, the man who’d latch onto anything with breasts (or turnbuckles for that matter), could be angered over the sight of not just one, but two resplendent women stretched out across the same couch. “Take it someone‘s managed to chalk up another loss.” She teased, getting a small giggle from the likes of both Dee and Chris, only Chris’ was the sarcastic kind. The kind that instantly silenced both girls. “We need to talk,” he continued on once he’d cleared the air of any sort of dry humor. “Alone.” Assuming Chris was looking to talk about the same thing Dee had come here to converse about, Tessa went into autopilot mode just as she did every other time Chris hoped to talk strategy with her. “I already have a plan for the match.” “I’m well aware of that.” He assured as he slithered his way onto the middle of the couch, creating a human wedge between the women. “I’m sure Dee’s found yet another one of her ingenious ways to slip you into the next round , and I’d care to hear about this.” Gleefully, without a clue of what Chris actually meant, Tessa attempted to hand the spotlight over to her close friend who was indeed partially responsible for her round one success. “I’m sure she’d be more than willing to tell you….” Chris hardly cared to hear the rest of what she had to say though, and simply repeated the one thing he wanted. “Alone.” Catching the far from subtle hints dropped from Chris, Dee yanked her slender frame away from the leather underneath, growling silently at the self proclaimed Perfect Storm. “I’ll catch ya later Tess.” “Take care, catch ya later, ” Tessa returned as she waved goodbye to the tall blonde that got her into this mess, before turning her attention back to the other tall blonde who wanted nothing more than to get her out of this mess. Or at least, away from the woman that was seemingly trying to ruin all he was trying to rebuild, by shoving his own card right back at him. “Okay, now we’re alone. Speak your mind.” “Do I even NEED to say anything?” Chris snarled as he pulled himself away from the arms of Tessa, who judging by the widened state of her baby blues, seemed completely oblivious as to what was going on. Staying unusually calm for someone under questioning from an angered man responsible for ending several careers, Tessa wrinkled her brow and tried to make at least some kind of logical sense of why Chris could seemingly be so pissed off over her winning. Wasn’t that kind of the whole point of her stepping into the ring, “Uh, yeah?” “Look at the fucking papers!” The man who claimed to be the Last Great Technical Wrestler lashed out in anger, as he yanked out the latest edition of the local paper out from the small gap between his arm and chest. Surely enough there on the front page was Tessa, but with the kind of headline any kind of star, upcoming or legendary, dreaded. “Local Diva Exposed”. “Please tell me this is some kind of joke.” Tessa prayed to herself, as her eyes locked onto a blast from the past, her own face, plastered onto the scantily clad body of a former associates of Gaz Green, Natalia. The photos were already proven to be phony, but the media bit off anything they could chew. And with what followed in smaller text, they had plenty more where they came from. “Come on now Chris, you know those were fake!” “No shit, I could have told you that.” He grumbled, before dragging his oversized index finger down to the next line, to point out to the diva under siege the more important facts in questioning. “But tell me how the hell they could have doctored this!” “Several explicit tapes have also surfaced over the past few days over the internet, starring the two time Tag Champion and one time Television Champion of the now defunct Primetime Championship Wrestling.” As tempted as she was to stutter out the anticipated defense, “It’s not true!” she bit her tongue before the words could slip their way through her glossy pink lips, and simply allowed the attack on her integrity continue at the hands of her former tag partner, or fiancé, depending on who you chose to ask. “I knew that chick was no good. She’s gone and turned you into the a whore!” Pacing in a circle just as a mad man does when contemplating his next move, Storm tossed the paper to the ground, only bothering to look up to lock his ice cold blue eyes with hers, which were seemingly fighting to stay open and acknowledge that this indeed was a reality, not some kind of nightmare she could force her way out of by closing her eyes and opening them again to awaken to the distorted view of her ceiling as the sun peeked through the blinds, and the kids had their first argument of the day. “That’s not true.” Tessa mustered up not in her defense, but in the defense of someone who had calmed her on nights that Chris refused to. “Don’t play dumb with me Tess.” Letting out a mixture between a groan and a grunt, Chris kept his eyes locked on her, as she curled her legs up, and let her heavy head fall onto her knee caps as he continued on with his argument. “You think I haven’t played that card before? You don’t think half the women in this business haven’t tried shit like that? How else do you think Patti and Lady Luck managed to get anywhere without an ounce of talent in the ring? From what I understand that little Indian you’re facing had to do the same kind of shit. And here you are with two children who look up to you, dropping to the same fucking low. .I would have thought you’d have a lot more pride than to go and bare it all for some stupid tournament! Guess I was wrong about that? Then again, I‘m sure I‘m pretty wrong about a lot of other stuff as well.” “Chris, you saw me win it fair and square!” She attempted to plea, but arguing with a man as stubborn as Chris Storm was about as useless as arguing at a brick wall. “I don’t know why you think Dee would ever have me do such a thing.” “Because I know you.“ He hissed, battling against his own angered tears. “And I know you’d do anything to save yourself from looking bad in front of the kids. Let’s face it, nothing of this started happening until you started hanging out with her! You were perfectly content just stepping in the ring and putting on a good match. And now what?” “I can’t believe you’re buying into this shit.” Though she hated to swear considering at any moment one of the kids could decide to pop their head into the room, she could hardly contain the disappointment that had settled on her heart. They’d come this far to repair the gap that had grown between them during the months lost, yet the only thing they’d seem to be doing is driving the other further away. “And I can’t fucking believe that I was so stupid not to see it!” Clenching his trembling fist, he did all that he could to keep from punching a hole in the wall, or Tessa. “Why else would someone as sweet and innocent as you were supposed to be , be stretched to a piece of shit like me?” “Because I love you!” Words that had been spoken so often between the two that their significance seemed to have finally slipped away. Just as those words, it seemed Chris had finally lost his whole reasoning for being here, as he trudged his way towards the door with a simple explanation for his disappearance, “I’m going for a drive, I don’t know when I’m going to be back. I just want a fucking answer by the time I get back., got me?” The guilt extending from his index finger, wrapped around Tessa’s heart and chocked out the only word that she could muster up the courage to say. “Yes.” “Good.” Chris muttered in absolute disgust as she door slamming behind him, leaving Tessa to her thoughts, and even then her utmost fears conquered all. “He’s up to his old crap again.”
