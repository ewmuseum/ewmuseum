Don't Work in a wiener suit (unfinished RP)

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 “What the hell have I gotten my self into?” Spoke a distant voice. The sun disbanded on top of the city of Sacramento, California. “Oh man, oh man, why did I have to go through this!?” As the light from the big red star withdraws from the western hemisphere, the street lights illuminate one by one in precise succession. A giant man in a wiener costume slowly exits the shadows hidden within the alley way. “Delight Dogs, get your Delight Dogs!” The man says as he delightfully pulls on a fake smile and carries a silver plate full of tiny hot dogs. “Gnarly, dude. It is a giant wiener; I wonder what he tastes likes. Boys, let’s go beat the wiener.” The man in the wiener costume eyes bulged out his head. “I sure hope they are not talking about me!” he looks around and sees nobody else in a wiener costume. “Oh man, what the fu…” “FUDGE! Let’s throw fudge at him!” says the stoner guy. “OH NO! PLEASE! DON’T THROW FUDGE AT ME!” he yells. As he is yelling frantically, two guys tackle him. After minutes of fudge packing and wiener eating the stoners throw the wiener man against the wall. The uneducated one says “Look at the pretty name tag. H-h-h-hiirrr-o-o-o-o-o-o-k-a-z—u-u-u-. Dude, what is that word?” “IT IS HIROKAZU YOU FUCKING IDIOT! MY NAME IS HIROKAZU. GET A FUCKING EDUCATION.” Hirokazu, the wiener man yells. In rage, all the stoners kick the crap of Hirokazu and leave him for dead. After they leave, a hobo walks up to the beaten body of Hirokazu. “Ahoy there, fancy pants. Nice looking watch you got there.” The man takes the watch and Hirokazu passes out. As what it would seem, it is the midafternoon. The wiener man is lying down, nothing but his Scooby-Doo underwear. He emerges from is position. His hair is parted to one side. “What the hell just happened? Ugh, did I get beaten up by stoners again? Now what do I do?” Hirokazu shifts his eyes left and right and runs behind a dumpster, coincidently, he has a change of clothes behind the dumpster. He walks out behind it with a muscle t-shirt on that has a logo of a hamster punching a fish. He also has a pair of regular old blue jeans on. “Bah, that is the tenth time this week I was fired from a job, sigh, being a mascot for fast food joints isn’t my thing. I need something to do that will spark my interest. After my wrestling career went down the drain, I got fired, kick out of the house by my mo…girlfriend, yes girlfriend, my best of friends won’t let me stay with them, my parents got eaten by that Siegfried and Roy tiger! What the hell am I supposed to do, this blows.” Hirokazu parades on. “Damnit, now I bet I am going to have to pay for that wiener costume I had to rent because the stupid Delight Dog owner is a cheap bastard.” “Money problems eh, sonny Jim?” says an old geezer standing next beside him. “I gots a job for you if you are up to it.” “Hm? What kind of job, Abe Vigoda?” as he says that, he gets knocked on the head with a cane. “WHAT THE FUCK WAS THAT FOR?! Are you insane Oldi Wan-Kenobi?” “Respect your elders, you half wit. I want you to cook sushi in my restaurant.” He says. “A few things, Indiana Oldes, I am not Asian, I do not know how to cook sushi or sake or bukkake or any other type of Asian food. Find yourself another Wang-Chung to cook for you.” He says. As he tries to leave the old man trips him with his cane. “Grr, you are making me mad, Old Man Flew Over the Coocoo’s nest. Don’t make me hurt you.” “Calm down, Hirokazu. If you would work for me, I will guarantee that you will become the greatest wrestler of all time.” He smirkly says. “How the hell did you know my name, and how did you know I was a wrestler?” he asks. “Enough! You will not be mean to me anymore, Jimmy. Every time you are not delightful to me or anyone else, I will shock you with this tazor.” “Screw you….AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!” the scream in agony from Hirokazu. “MY FACE! WHAT THE HELL!” “I told you to start being delightful to me or you will suffer the TAZOR OF DOOM~!” the old man says. Another tazor shock goes into the abdomen. Hirokazu winches in pain on the floor in a fetal position. “FINE DAMNIT! I’LL BE GOD DAMN FRIGGIN DELIGHTFUL!” he yells. “Good, now get some rest.” The old man requests. The Next Day Hirokazu wakes up with a sheet on top of his face. “Huh?” he reads it, it states: PRIMETIME CENTAL PRESENTS: GTT4: ARE YOU READY FOR PRIMETIME? FREE AGENTS MAY APPLY AS JOBBERS. “What the heck is this?” “I don’t hear delightfulness…” the old crone says. “Erm, I mean how delightful it is to be given this gift of a piece of paper, I am really delighted, thank you senior citizen.” Ah shit, what the hell has this old dude gotten me into? Why the fuck do I have to be all cheery and delightful. I want to beat him so hard, but I’ll probably end up killing the old wind bag. “Hold on there, before you even enter that, you need to do a few jobs for me.” “OH GREAT A JOB! HOW DELIGHTFUL! YOU DON’T RELISE HOW DELIGHTFUL YOU HAVE MADE ME WITH A JOB! WOW!” I have no desire to finish this :-/ I'll probably lose anyway.
