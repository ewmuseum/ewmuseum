Goodtime's and Badtime's (Part 2)

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 “Is he ok?” That was the question that I kept asking myself and everyone else. From that long phone call, that was all I could think about. Is my dad dead? Is he gone from my life, the very life that I strive to get away from? Is he laying in a bed motionless from the hard crash that he was in? Yet all those question could not be answered till I was at the hospital. And yet I still had to keep my mind on Pandora. A woman that I had yet to even know about and a female with way more experience then I could have right now. But all my thoughts focused on a man that for twenty years of my life was right there. As I reached the hospital, racing in to the tall building in deep Dallas, Texas, I could feel a different atmosphere inside. A cold feeling that raced up my spine all the way up to my head. All I wanted to do was get to the room. Passing all the different people that were in front of me, I reached the waiting room, filled with nothing but the doctor and my mother, a woman that I respected but hated for so many different reason. “He is awake but very weak.” The doctor’s words weren’t comforting. They seemed to be unsure from the look of his hazel brown eyes. He lowered his head as he stood there, holding his little clip board as his eyes kept looking and staring at me. “Can I go in and see him?” My father, a strong a brave man, was laying in there in that hospital bed. I had to see him for some reason and deep inside, my life would be destroyed if I didn’t. I had to tell him the way I felt about everything we had said. I had to tell him that I loved him. And I had to tell him that I am sorry. I loved my father. He has always been there for me in all the deep days of my life. When I first went to school, he walked me all the way to the bus stop. Standing right there and telling me stories of what him and my grandfather would do. Even when I lost my first tooth, he was there to help me clean the mess up. Placing money under my pillow to replace a missing part of my body. And yet, I had a chance to replace all that and be there for him. “Yes you can but I want to be very honest with you Ms. Smith. I don’t…” Cutting him off before he could finish his comment. I ran past the doc. Running down the cluttered white wall hallway that lead to, “Room 222” that held my father. What was I to say to a man who had just had a car crash due to a heart attack? Was I to talk about the one thing that he didn’t want me to do? Was I to talk about the past or what might be in the future? Whichever it be, I would show love. Even through out the hate that my mother and father showed towards my choose, I knew they loved me to. And that was what brought me here today. Running up to the door, I planted my feet, looking inside the room at a sight that I thought I was prepared for but after seeing it through my blue eyes, a feeling of pain and sorrow grew from my broken heart and ran through my body. My eyes began to water up from the sight of all the wire’s that were hooked to his chest. This wasn’t my father. All the wire’s and the buzzing sound that clicked in every few minutes, it wasn’t my father at all. No, it’s a man without life laying in a bed ready to die. Though I wasn’t ready for him to. Every time me and my family fought, I was a woman that showed strength. I lead them to believe that I was ready to be alone in this brutal world. To go out and fly to the highest peak and spread my wings. But right now, seeing my father laying there, oh how my heart stopped. “Father?” Oh god I didn’t want to see him this way. His pain almost became my pain. As if we both were connected somehow. I turned away from the man, from my father as my mother want walking in. She didn’t follow me when I ran for the room. She stood at the doorway as I turned towards her. “You made it. He will be so happy. He’s been asking for you.” My mother was never strong. She drew off the strength of her loving husband, my father, every time something happen. She lowered her head as I walked forward. I could remember just a year ago me and my father dancing around on the street. How could this be? Was it punishment for me defying him and his will of me becoming something that he didn’t want me to be? Was this an act of god that caused him to be in this bed. I stood over him, his eyes gently open to show the off set brown color that they gleamed out. “Hi baby,” My father let out a smile. Grabbing my hand as he looked up at me. Oh god, I wish I could take his pain. Though, my father wouldn’t of wanted that. He hated showing pain and if you let out the littlest for him, he’s shot it right down. I gave a sigh, grabbing his cold hand with both mine. I tried to smile but inside, I was fighting much more. How could I even think of telling him that the very dream that we fought about wasn’t a dream anymore? Could I be that cold and destructive? No I wouldn’t as I stood there looking at my father, tears going down my face. “Are you ok daddy?” My eyes started to water even more. Letting more drops go down my face. I sat down on the bed, placing my hands still with him on my lap. I would give everything to have him be able to walk away from this but I knew it was not to be. He shook his head in a no answer. Squeezing my hand even tighter as I leaned down, propping all my weight on to my right knee. He tried to clear his through, seemingly trying to get all the crap that was in his throat. I held on tight, letting my the tears that I had go down my face. “I’m sorry…” My father cleared his throat again. Trying to complete a sentence that I didn’t know what was to continue with. “I’m sorry for what I said. I don’t got much time but I want to say that I love you…” I don’t have much time? He has all the time in the world. He can’t give up on life now. My father never gave up on anything in his life. I wiped the tears from my eyes, tossing the hair out of my face, before taking his hand into both mine. “Don’t cry. This is something that can’t be helped.” Yes it can I wanted to scream. I shook my head and lowered onto the bed. I kept my head down. Trying to wish that when I raised my head up my father would be ok. He would be ready to walk away from this place and go home. But wishful thinking was not going to work here. I rose from the bed, looking right at my father as his eyes began to look watery and cold. He let one tear go out his brown eye and go down his face before continuing. “I want you to know that I will forever be with you.” Those were the last words. His head raised up slightly, opening his mouth as a loud beep came from the machine. I screamed and squeezed his hand as his breath slowly went away. This couldn’t be happening. “FATHER!” My eyes closed, dropping my head right to the bed before my father’s hand fell out of mine. My heart almost seem to stop before I heard one last voice in my head. “I will forever love you”
