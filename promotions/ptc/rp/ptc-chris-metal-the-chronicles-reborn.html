IG 67 RP THREAD

3 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with a view of a lake covered in leaves. As the wind blows the water ripples and the leaves start to sink. Chris Metal observes as this happens and runs his hands through his hair. Chris Metal: That's kind of like what happened to my career. He turns around, and as we pan out from the scene we can see that he is standing with his longtime friend, confidante and manager Victor Thompson. Chris is wearing a white t-shirt and jeans. Victor is wearing a lilac polo shirt and his hair is now completely white. Chris Metal: I was a rising star, once. From that win over Jon Kano in PCW, through to Vintage Wrestling and being International Champion. And then... the weather changed. Chris observes the lake again where the water becomes still and serene. Victor raises a questioning eyebrow. Chris Metal: Vintage took a hit, my career took a dive. I took some time out, and when I resurfaced, I was a laughing stock. I was cheap-laugh material for a guy called The Farmer - I mean, how ridiculous a name is that? I've never known a farmer who can even string two words together, let alone take on a technician as experienced as The Metal One. Victor nods sagely. Chris Metal: Yet AWC expected me to be the comic relief. Unbelievable! Just like that, dreams can sink and a career can turn to dusk. The two watch the lake again as a white bundle of feathers emerges on the surface from amid the reeds at the bankside. It is a swan. A dead swan. Chris Metal: But for one night only, the WECWWF is jumping back a decade... to those glorious days. And the chronicles of Chris Metal... The swan jolts to life. Chris looks directly into the camera, noticing it for the first time. Chris Metal: ...will be reborn. We fade to black.
