Karma Karma (Lucas Quinton) ------ It was not always this way. Many years ago a person wouldn’t dare go that far. Fortunately for everyone else, I am not that type of person. I am whoever I need to be to get ahead in life. I can be a world class superhero, a vagabond, or someone you know and trust. What you see is not what you get, not all the time. Be as that as you may, I do what is right, and I if I have to go the extra mile to prove my point, I will do that, not because I have to, but because I want to. I am not to be taken lightly, like I have so long ago, I am a new person, a new force to be reckoned with. * * * THREE YEARS AGO * * * What we have here today is a man who doesn’t take no for an answer, who tries to get what he wants, but in the end, he ends up like the rest of the human race, another permanent resident six feet under. “Listen, pal, this is genuine gold, take it or leave it.” “What do you mean genuine? I can tell its fake, don’t try and con me.” “Who said about this being a con? You couldn’t tell real from fake, I know what I am talking about, so are you going to buy it or not?” “Real or fake? Heh, you absolutely have no idea what real or fake is, pal. I am in no mood for rants or some bullshit like that. I don’t want your crap wares; go peddle it to some high school kids.” “Last chance, man.” “Get lost, wanker.” BANG BANG BANG That’s all it took. Three shots to the stomach and I was down. That is when my life changed, thanks to that con man. He set me free, he let loose the old person that day and in its place, he stashed me. Of course I am pissed that I got shot, but also I am grateful that he did what he did, he put things in perspective. * * * “Dispatch, we have the shooting victim, en route to hospital, over.” “What’s it look like, Jim?” “He has three gunshot wounds to the stomach area, it looks like a mugging gone bad, I don’t know if we will make it time.” That bastard took my wallet; he won’t have it for long. The ambulance reaches the hospital and is greeted by doctors and nurses alike. The EMTs quickly get out of their seats and run to the back to open the doors. The nurses and doctors help to bring the stretcher to the concrete. All these wasted seconds are a matter of life or death for Lucas Quinton. “Rush him to the OR; I wanted him there 5 minutes ago! Let’s go people! This guy have a name by chance?” “No, his wallet appears to have been misplaced or stolen.“ “Sonuvabitch, come on people, let’s save his life before he ends up in the meat locker.” * * * I was in and out of consciousness. I could see those masks, those horrid masks that look over at me while they check my vital stats, while they clean the blood from their hands. Every hour they come in to change the bandages and my sheets. I turn my head from side to side, all these damn tubes running in and out of my body, like I was some type of science experiment. Good thing I am not dead. Or am I? Is this heaven? No it can’t be, can it? No, maybe its hell, that’s it, that is where I ended up, apparently all the good I did wasn’t good enough for the man upstairs and now I am paying the price by living out eternity as a guinea pig hospital lab rat. That’s all I really needed, the poking and the prodding. I have to get out of here. I can see a doctor a nurse come into the room…barely. They come over to check my EKG meter and all that technological crap. “This guy have a name yet?” “No, he has no identification on him at all, we got some fingerprints off him, and the police are running them as we speak.” “L-L-Luuu…” I can’t say anything. Of course, as soon as I try to say something, my throat closes up. This isn’t a good time to lie down, I gotta find that bastard who shot me, and he still has my wallet. “I think he is waking up! Hey, guy, hey can you speak? What did you say your name was?” “L-Luuu..” “Lou? Louis? Leonard? Talk to me!” Screaming at me won’t help. Trying the best I can here, but of course as soon as I am awake, I fall asleep again. I Awoke Several Hours Later in a Daze... Was I still me? Did I get hit with a blast of amnesia, no, no, I am still here, all my memories intact, I think at least. Did I get hit in the head? Oh, yes, that’s right I got shot, shot in the stomach, by that bastard. I’m gonna get that wallet back sooner or later. I was still pretty banged up. There is bandages around my waist, I kind of look like a mummy, no time to reminisce now, I need to get out of here. A doctor finally comes into the room. He looks over the charts and sees what’s on the up and up. “Mr. Doe, I see you are awake.” “What now?” “You are a very lucky person Mr. Doe, a lot of other people would have bled out and died, but I can see you are a fighter, Mr. Doe.” “Quinton…” “What?” “Name’s Quinton; Lucas Quinton.” “Ah finally, a name to put with that face. We thought we were going to lose you, seeing as how you have no identification on it.” “Wallet was stolen.” “Ah I see, well, your stats are all fine, you did lose a lot of blood, we are going to keep you for a week or two for more observations. Do you have anyone we can contact?” “Nah, they are all in another country.” “Oh, what are you anyway? Irish.” “English, mate, English.” “Well, anyhow, I am going to let you get some rest. Don’t try and sit up or anything. A nurse should be in here soon to change your bandages.” “Thanks…I didn’t catch your name.” “Dr. Rick Dagless, Md. Pleasure to meet you, Lucas Quinton.” The doctor left the room. Why did he ask me if I was Irish? He had a bloody English accent himself, oh well it doesn’t matter right now. I have to get out of this room. I have to get out of this hospital and find who ever the hell shot me. * * * A WEEK PASSES * * * Damn, I really need to get out of here, the surgery went well, I have no internal bleeding or anything like that, and do they really need to keep me here that long? I’m already paying an arm and a leg for this room and the surgery, what else do I need to do? “Hey there Lucas, my favorite patient, how are we feeling today?” That was Dr. Rick Dagless, Md. He was a maverick doctor, a rouge, a shadow who moved throughout the hospital, doing his best, because that’s all anyone could ask for, right? He reminds me a lot of myself. Not caring about the rules, he did what he had to do to survive…to make his patients survive. He definitely was the Alpha of this hospital; I could see it in his eyes through those thick glasses of his. He was and still is taking care of me during my tenure at this hospital. Maybe he can get me the hell out of here. “Doc...” “You’re going to be discharged today…” Well that was easy. “What? What do you mean today? I mean I sure love to stay here at a 5 star hotel such as this.” “Very funny, but yes, you will be discharged today, you are going to have some bruising around your mid-section for a while, you are also going to have a nasty scar there. Shame…well, what I want you to do is not doing any standing for long periods of time, because it will rip apart those staples, and then you have to come back here and have another fun week of sleeping in that bed. Now, I don’t want to see you around here for at least a couple more weeks. Got it?” “Yeah yeah, see you later…doc.” * * * 3 WEEKS AGO (Near Present Day) * * * “So that is how you got that scar? Damn, you are a fighter, Lucas.” says Chris. Chris was my manager, he knew everything about everything, he was also the one who knows what I think and how I think, we are very similar people. He will know what I am thinking at any given time and will get what I want, not that I have everything already, but he is a nice asset to have around, in case something happens of course. “You are correct on that assumption. Nothing will get in the way of what I want. Don’t underestimate me; you know that, I know that, everyone knows that. What am I thinking now?” “You are thinking what you are always thinking, you want a drink. So do I, get your coat, I know a great pub in town.” What would I do without this guy? He really helps me in these type of situations. I need a fucking drink. The drive wasn’t as good as I thought. It was night time and the roof was down, nice car he has, I have a few of them myself. As good as he was a manager; he could get so wrapped up in something that he would get lost, kind of like now. “We’re lost, mate.” “No we are not; I know where I am going.” “I am not going to argue with you like a married couple, just let’s hurry up and get that drink before I change my mind.” “Listen, I know this area a whole lot better than you.” “Is that so? So why did we pass the pub?” “God damnit…” Chris takes his lead foot and pounds it on the brake. I thank god for wearing this seat belt. I would have flown all the way across the United bloody States. He quickly pulls into a spot, he puts on his shades and looks over at me, and he grins. I shake my head at him, what an idiot. “See I told you I knew where I was going.” “Yeah, okay.” We walk into the bar and see a crowd of people watching a football match. No, what I meant to say is that I was watching them watch an AMERICAN FOOTBALL game, not the real football we have over here in the UK. Madness this place is. Coincidently, there are two empty bar stools right next to the cash register. We both sit down, and I immediately slouch over. “Hey keep, BARKEEP!” I yell across the room. This burley little man waddles towards us. “Hey man, give me some 10 year old bourbon.” “Ain’t got that pal.” “What do you have?” “Beer, beer, and more beer.” “Really? Give me two beer then, mate.” The bartender spits on the floor for some reason and pours the beers into the glasses and gives them to me. “Uh, thanks, mate.” The bartender groans. Can’t always get customer service the way you like it. “So you like the place, Lucas?” Chris still has that grin on him. If I was drunk, I would slap that grin off his face. “Well, it’s not bad, and it’s not great. I think 1 is my limit tonight.” Behind me I hear the scuttling of footsteps. “Once in a lifetime offer…” What the hell? “This is real genuine gold here, ladies, take it or leave it.” No it can’t be him. “This is a great deal and I can give it to you for half the market value.” That fucking little shit. I tap Chris on the shoulder and I walk over to the little bastard. “Hey, you wanker, remember me?”. That was the first time I have ever seen someone so scared. He eyes bugged out so badly, the look of oh shit on his face couldn’t be more subtle. He books it for the door, but me being the superior runner to him, takes him down easily. The focus from the football game turns to use, I am being stared at for over 100 different eyes. I am ready to punch this little bastard out. “HEY! Take it outside!” “Fine then…” I grab this weasel by the neck and force his ass outside into the alleyway. “So, you still are pulling this same old shit, are ya? I thought after you fucking shot me, you would have learned a lesson.” “Y-you are not dead?” “Do I look dead? Now listen here, I am going to get you to the count of 5.” “Th-then w-what?” “You’ll see. Okay? Ready? Go…5” I grab him by the neck and fling him in the wall. Now I am not the most violent person, but I know how to handle myself, this wasn’t any different than my days as an amateur wrestler, a lot more punches are thrown, some kicks too. As I am roughing him up, I can see him start to cry, almost remorse from him as to what he did to me. I almost stopped there. Almost. “Please, please stop! I am sorry!” “No, no, please don’t!” “Please I beg you!” “Let me go!” “Give me one more chance, just one more chance, I promise, I promise it won’t happen again!” “You had all your chances before you did what you did. You had all the chances when you shot me, you little bitch.” I throw him to the ground and start kicking him in the gut. “DO YOU SEE THIS?” I lift up my shirt, it is the scar that over my stomach. It is still hasn’t faded, when you move your finger over it, it feels like you are touching two different sets of skin, one is rugged, and one is smooth. Which one is which? Depends on the person that is touching it. Chris pulls me away and calms me down. My hatred for this man is still burning. He can see it, Chris can see, and I can see it. I reach down to him and pat him down. I pull out something. It was my wallet. After all these years he kept my wallet. I look inside and see all his information in it. “Scotty. Scotty, look at me…okay good. Now listen here, I have your name, your address, I have your whole life in this wallet. Do you remember this? This is the wallet you stole from me after you tried to steal my life. Now, if I catch you conning people or doing anything illegal again, I will find you and I will kill you. You understand that?” He nods to me. I know he took my words with warning. Did I do the right thing? Hell if I know. Not my place to judge, I could have dragged his ass to the station and have him arrested, but I didn’t. Scotty looks up at me and Chris and then suddenly jumps up and runs away. BANG BANG BANG As we run out the alley, we can see a man laying on the concrete in his own bloody mess. I run up to see what the commotion was. All the bar patrons are gathered around him too. As I get closer, I can see who it was. It was the bloody punk I just beat up, he got a taste of his own medicine, although this time it cost him his life. Suffice to say… Karma’s a bitch…
