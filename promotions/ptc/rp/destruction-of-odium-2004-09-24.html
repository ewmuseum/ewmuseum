Destruction of Odium?!

24 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-24 Heaven was now just a desolate wasteland. The once great kings and queens that lived here have now faced their doom and moved onto the next journey of life. The once great white walls were now red from the blood that had been shed on them. Who could have done something of this magnitude? The answer was simple, Satan. [The screen opens to show Rzarector sitting down on a park bench. The Black Priest is wearing his normal black hooded robe. The red eyes and white fangs shine out from the hood like a shooting star at night. Obviously Rzarector is asleep and dreaming of the destruction of Heaven.] Satan had finally escaped from Hell after one thousand years. Yet with him he brought thousands, upon millions, of demons to Earth and finally to Heaven. There at the golden gates a huge battle took place. Nine hundred and eighty thousand demons fought the illustrious five hundred thousand warriors of Heaven. The battle was going great for God until one sudden turning point. The angels had deceived the Almighty One and turned on him to join the evils of Satan. [Rzarector begins tossing and turning on the park bench. His red eyes are shining bright red.] The battle then turned ill. The demons were going to take victory. The angels had fallen and their wings crunched up like burnt toast. When all hope had failed the Almighty One himself had finally appeared with Jesus at his side. He spoke to the demons asking them to stop the attack, but how could the demons let God off the hook when they have been burning for eternity? So the demons continued and Satan took over Heaven killing all, including God and Jesus, which was good. [Rzarector popped up from the park bench in a panic state. He was shaking like a seizure and had sweat running down from his forehead. The red eyes of the Black Priest were slowly coming to a dim. He slowly stood up and began pacing back and forth.] Rzarector: Is it true? Has the Kingdom of Heaven finally met its fate? This can’t be true. The world would be full of black smoke, and demons would be running everywhere. [Rzarector begins to think of a world with creatures that look like him.] Rzarector: That would be horrible. I’m glad this didn’t happen, and it was all just a dream. If Heaven died then so would I. Yet who would do such a thing? Turn on God, and join Satan? [At that moment Rzarector began shaking again. His body lost complete control and the Black Priest fell to the ground. His red eyes shot out into the sky like two red spotlights as he hissed at the top of his lungs. Pain reeked through his body as God’s voice slowly came to his mind.] God: Hello my son. Rzarector: Hello father. The dream I just had was horrible. God: I know, and witnessed it all. Rzarector: Some say your dreams come true. Will this one? God: No. In the final days of the world there shall be Angels who deceive me and turn towards the evil side. Yet good shall always prevail. It was not Satan who created Satan; it was God who created Satan. Remember that. Only the creator can undo a creation. Rzarector: Who would deceive you? God: That question is simple. The people that will deceive me are people like Odium. Rzarector: The man I have to fight this week? God: Yes, he may seem all nice but that is just his public appearance. Deep down he is a wretched soul just waiting to break out hate for the person responsible for his life. Rzarector: So he will eventually turn on you? God: Yes. It is inevitable. Rzarector: That traitor! He has never burnt in Hell. Odium doesn’t know the consequences for his actions yet. People look at me like a joke when I try to warn them, and they don’t listen. What do they know? They think men were just created? God: Something like that. Yet it is no need to lose your temper. Rzarector: Lose my temper? Don’t you understand? This is a communist part, not a democracy. You’re the only ruler, and you shall rule forever. People like this need to be punished. Why does the bad person get good things, and the good person get bad things? God: I’m sorry. True followers at heart won’t worry about money, fame, or greed because they know that their reward shall be in Heaven. Do you know that my son? Rzarector: Yes, and everyday I thank you for letting me go from Hell. Do I honor you well? God: Better than I could. Rzarector: Should I destroy Odium in your name? God: That is up for you to decide. [At that time Rzarector slowly felt life coming back to his body. The shaking stopped, and he returned to a conscious level once again. Standing up, Rzarector shook the dirt off of his black hooded cloak as he sat back down on the park bench. A crowd had gathered at the sight of him going into seizure like movements, but quickly the Black Priest hissed and they all ran off scared.] Rzarector: Am I some spectacle? [Then out of nowhere came a small child. This girl was probably about five years old, and was wearing a nice flower dress. She had a daisy in her hand and quickly handed it to Rzarector before running off. A sudden sense of warmth came over his body as he felt loved for the first time because of his deformity.] Rzarector: She just handed me a flower? A child’s mind is priceless. The simplest thinking habits in the world, but the best. They have no worries yet, and all they care about is food, the bathroom, and playing. That is the wonderful thing about life. I wish that once you grew up there were still no responsibilities. Yet that is not the world we live in. [With that sudden change in mood Rzarector felt anger or some type of sadness come over his body. Odium flashed throughout his mind as he clenched his fingernails into his palm. Black blood slowly started oozing down.] Rzarector: Odium, death knocks on everyone doors. Sometimes we get lucky and it knocks on others. In your case the luck prevailed and your mother died. This may seem like harsh words coming from a Priest such as myself, but in reality it is the best thing that could ever happen to someone. Your life is still young, and her life was aging. I’m not saying the better person prevailed, I’m just saying the youngest person shall live on in her memory. Even though she beat you as a child you will never fill half her shoes as a person. So put your tears aside and just thank God that you’re still alive you selfish fool. [The flower, now covered in black blood, is crushed within the palm of Rzarector. The child who seemed to have walked off was only just hiding behind a bush. She is now weeping at the site of Rzarector crushing her flower. The Black Priest notices her and walks over to her.] Rzarector: Young child, why do you weep? Child: I gave you a flower and you killed it. Rzarector: The flower is just an object. The true meaning behind it is the fact that out of the kindness of your heart you gave me this. The warmth that came over my body is truly priceless. The flower will not live forever, but the memory of you will. [The child smiles at the kind words of Rzarector.] Child: Thank you. [The still smiling child rubs the hand of Rzarector as she walks off. Rzarector goes back to focusing on what’s important and that is Odium.] Rzarector: Where was I at? Yes I remember now. The greed of some people is almost unbelievable. God asks you to not be greedy. Yet why then are you, Odium, so greedy? Does the thought of burning for eternity not scare you? No! It doesn’t. [The eyes of Rzarector slowly start to turn a deeper, more blood red.] Rzarector: I can tell you why! Odium, you have never been burned in Hell! Notice I said burned not burnt, because the burning does not stop. The scream, cries, and gnawing of teeth is enough to drive a person insane. Which you would have to be insane I guess to have to go against God’s word and commit sins. What’s the price of Sin? Death! That’s right; I have witnessed Hell from a personal experience. I have also witnessed Heaven from a person experience. Yet Heaven is much too beautiful to talk about in front of scum like you Odium. [Rzarector stops for a second and pulls out a portable DVD player. He begins watching the tape of the interview with Odium from earlier today.] Rzarector: See you didn’t even shed a tear when your mother died? Don’t you know that the only way to move on is to express your emotions? The world is a better place with out people like you. That is why when we fight this upcoming week I will be glad to stomp you back into place. Better yet, I’ll be glad to stomp you right to Hell! The leeches sucking on your skin shall always make you think of me from this point on. [Rzarector watches the part of Odium making his promise.] Rzarector: So you decided you would repay your mother by beating the Black Priest? Yet you seem to forget one thing, and that is your mothers death doesn’t mean anything in my eyes. The fight that Odium is carrying on compares to nothing that Rzarector is carrying on. You fight for your mom, and I fight for God. In that one sentence you should know who is fighting for the greater purpose. Your mother was a witch who beat you as a child, but you still have enough hope to fight for her name? I still don’t understand why God just didn’t take both of you out at the same time. [Rzarector stops talking as if to calm down for a moment. He kneels down in a prayer position and stays silent for a moment while he prays.] Rzarector: That outburst was unacceptable. God has always trained me to think with my brain, and not with my anger. Yet sometimes the heart can fill you with rage at how pathetic people are. Just looking at Odium makes me want to shoot him. A weak individual Odium is indeed. This week it may be my toughest fight so far in this tournament, but one thing shall prevail and that is the name of God; not the name of Odium’s mother. Be warned….be prepared….for you shall… [Rzarector pauses as he hisses at the top of his lungs. His red eyes light up as the open mouth reveals saliva running off his white fangs.] Rzarector: BURN IN HELL! [At this time Rzarector gets up and walks over to behind the bench. He slowly picks a flower out of a small garden built on the walkway and starts walking in the direction of the child. He finally catches up with her to see that she is with her family. Handing the flower to the young child, Rzarector smiles and walks off into the dying sunlight.]
