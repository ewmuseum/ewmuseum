Space Condom Monster of Death (FA) - All ur bases r belong 2 da Church~! The backdrop is the Castle of Blood in Transylvania as the scene opens on the imposing figure of the notorious Darth von Doom standing in front of what appears to be a floating Space Condom Monster of Death that is big, latex skinned, and furry at the bottom. It has arms with claws on the end of the fingers and a shark-like mouth filled with numerous sharp teeth. Darth von Doom: Hello PTC. I am here to formally hijack this transmission to inform you all that the Church of the Unholy has indeed returned. With me as part of the new leadership of the organization, the Church merged with the Circus of Doom and we have pooled our resources in order to make a splash on the PTC scene once again. Denizens of the unholy, demons of death, and other creatures of darkness will be unleashed when we win the Infinite Gauntlet. We have tried and failed many times in the past due to poor direction, sending in members who have no clue how to speak in correct English, or were just plain retarded, like Squideon. But, like a phoenix rising from the ashes, we have come back with our most formidable member yet… May I now present to you, the SPACE CONDOM MONSTER OF DEATH!!!! Behind Darth the giant condom of pain shows it claws. SCMOD: RAWR! Darth cackles with demonic glee. Darth von Doom: This is why we deserve to win this time. We have a creature that none of you can destroy or tame like a wild horse. This is just the first part of our invasion of the world. Not just a single country, but the WORLD! No one can handle battling this monster. It is the ultimate power in the universe and will destroy all who get in its way. Darth starts cackling evilly until a screech is heard in the background. Darth looks in the distance to see the Giant Man-eating Vagina coming close to them. The Space Condom lets out a primal roar and pulls out it’s main weapon, a dildo of death that shoots lasers from it’s head. Darth von Doom: Behold the battle to the death! The Giant Vagina charges at the Space Condom but it dodges and the Vagina growls with anger. The Vagina turns around only to be blasted in the G-spot by a laser blast from the dildo laser shooter that the Condom is using! The Vagina has a fatal orgasmic overload and dies after bleeding out all its juices. Darth smiles deviously. Darth von Doom: And that is why the Space Condom Monster of Death will win. It’s the most bad ass creature in the universe and no one can defeat it… Just then alarms start sounding as the Castle of Blood is ambushed by Green Peace! Darth von Doom: ATTACK! An army of flying monkeys comes blasting out of the Castle and begins fighting with the Green Peace as the scene fades out.
