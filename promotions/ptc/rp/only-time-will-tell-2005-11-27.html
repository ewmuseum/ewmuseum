Only Time Will Tell

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 A restigious event rolls around year in and year out, the names of men are brought to a higher level. The realm of Primetime Central brings the tournament referred to as only "GTT". The winners of the tournament, their names are held to a level beyond comprehension, their names are whispered with a sense of fear in a person's voice. "Angello Deville, Dr. Curiousity, Seymour Almasy…" I whispered to myself looking at the list of men who had been classified as "the elite" from the previous years of GTT. The small compartment darkened around my body, I whispered softly to myself. Light passed through the small area as the screen slid open. The reverend on the other side began speaking to me. "Aiden, you visit once more." Reverend Tobias said to me softly, peering through at me. "Father, I have sinned once more, the lifestyle I lead only brings me into the temptations that the devil has set forth before me." "Aiden, what must you seek forgiveness for now? You’ve murdered Aiden, you’ve lied, and you’ve done it all. Yet the lord still tells me that you are righteous." "The burden I carry on my shoulders, is the heavy burden of envy, and the equally heavy burden of greed. You must help me." I spoke softly to the reverend on the other side of the wall. "Aiden, how have you allowed these sins of the devil overcome your body?" "The sins I carry are closely related to the business, once more. I live in a world of sin due to this business, every time my name is mentioned, I can only hope it is mentioned in the likes of men who’s names are only described with a mere whisper. I must provide victory in GTT5 to create the roaring sensation…of the name… Aiden Jackson." "My Son, you are forgiven of your sins. Shall you not succeed in your task; the lord will not punish you. Aiden, you must let go of the names of these men, you must establish glory for yourself. Whether you win or not, you should be the person whispering your name… in the likes of every man equally." "I understand Father." "Aiden Jackson," he whispered as he closed the window between us and exited the small divided compartment. I quickly rose out of the compartment, chasing Reverend Tobias down, he looked me in the eyes, looked down at the paper held in his hands and handed it to me. The Reverend then turned away and walked into his office. I rushed out of the Church, the pews passing me quickly, the pane glass windows passing also. I slowed as I exited the church and opened the paper up. GCW Superstar Reports LOS ANGELOS, CA – Reports from GCW Headquarters came out today announcing several of the superstars in GCW whom were competing in the annual GTT tournament. The likes of former Cruiserweight Champion Aiden Jackson, Eva, LeStatt Knight, Sam Wolack, Michael Stevens and several others have been mentioned. Anonymous sources have reported that they can see LeStatt Knight taking this one all the way to the end; reports also noted that Aiden Jackson has not been the type of superstar that would be willing to put in the world necessary to achieve even a first round victory in the tournament. Aiden Jackson just hasn’t had the same type of in ring presence as he had when he first joined GCW. Sources noted that Aiden has had past struggles with the loss of his parents and many believe this is the current burden lying on Aiden’s shoulders. Aiden is distracted and doesn’t seem as though he will be willing to dedicate what is necessary to this tournament to achieve success. If the same Aiden Jackson that started in GCW comes out once more, there is a possibility that he could do something special in this tournament but as of now we just don’t see that happening. Outraged by the article, I threw it onto the ground and stared at is as the ink began to fade away from the wet ground. The ink faded, just as those blasphemous lies would fade, I would not accept the words that were spoke by these men. I would not only destroy my first round opponent, I shall send a message to every man in the tournament, and Aiden Jackson is not a force to be reckoned with. "Aiden Jackson…" I whispered to myself as I knew that this name would be whispered after the first round, the second, the third, the finals, whatever it took… to be whispered by the likes of "the elite". Primetime Central shall receive a seemingly rude awakening; they’ll learn to love the hate that I bring.
