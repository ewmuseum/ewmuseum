Fatal Four Way Tag Match Roleplaying

A Prophetic Change in the World! ~ Victorious warriors win first and then go to war, while defeated warriors go to war first and then seek to win. Sun-tzu (~400 BC), The Art of War. Strategic Assessments ~ ~ What is life? It is the flash of a firefly in the night. It is the breath of a buffalo in the wintertime. It is the little shadow which runs across the grass and loses itself in the sunset. Crowfoot's last words (1890) (Blackfoot warrior and orator) ~ ~ The time has come, for all to see in the world the domination that I bring! A sinful prophecy was born out of the flames of hell and I John “The Prophet” Adams plans to lead this prophecy straight into the very heart of PTC starting with the ever so popular Tag division! 4 teams in a grueling confrontation to prove that they have what it takes to be the best and challenge for the belts! 4 teams and three are from the very federation that I have called my home. Frankly in my mind the match has turned into a circus freak show! Pirates, Circus Performers, and Monkeys who love to wear mask! Here is a theory you can ask yourself, “When you are stuck between a circus of freaks, how do you survive?” The answer is simple, by showing those freaks the true meaning of what pain is! ~ ~ The hour of forgiveness has passed…… ~ Time: 3:30 PM Date: Monday January 17, 2005 Location: Pittsburgh, Airport :::::A airport, the one thing in today’s world that people are afraid of. May it be fear of a terrorist attack or that the plane will just simply crash into the mountains below. Either way the plane has signified fear in the world. What would fear signify to you? Spiders, fear of a clown, or maybe fear of success even. Fear is every where and is the whole major feeling that holds us back as human beings. Well for this warrior of the squared circle fear has no bearing on his life, for he has stared at the hellish life of his own fears and survived. ::::: ~ The time of The Prophecy will now be heard from all……… ~ ::::: The dark and gloomy man that is John Adams walked into the busy airport getting ready for his flight out. The airport was filled with the usual crowd of people who were on business trip or was just planning on visiting loved ones. For John Adams it was business, a business that of course gave him pleasure. The air inside the airport was real stuffy from all the people inside. He and Juvian were planning on attending a CCWA event to take on three other teams in a PTC event. As he passed by one of the airport bars he heard the TV that was on…………:::::::::: ~ The world’s last line of hope……. ~ {Welcome to another addition of IMAX on Fox Sports Net, I’m Max and what a match up we have to talk about folks! The Prophetic Theory vs The Flying Vergomovs vs Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean vs The Masked Monkeys, in what promises to be a classic battle in there hunt for the PTC Tag Team Championships!} ::::: It was all over the news and the TV, yes this tag match did promise to be one hell of a confrontation. The anticipation was overwhelming to Adams as he headed towards his gate. “What will be the outcome of this match?” He thought as he was getting his bags checked in. He stepped to one side so that the security could do there check, as this was going on a man who’s nationality was that of Middle East was getting harassed by a group of security people. ::::: ~ Foolishly put into a couple of Monkeys, Pirates, and High Flying Circus performers…… ~ Man: What is the meaning of this? Security: Sir calm down and just step over here please! Man: I’m not even from Iraq! I was born in New York City!!! ::::: The man was hauled off by security, and all it took was the fear from the appearance of this man from New York City. Was it going to be like that during the match, with the other teams taking on these weird gimmicks? A couple of Pirates, Monkeys and Circus performers, makes you think where Juvie and Adams fit in to this. ::::: ~ Only to be disappointed that the Prophecy will rein supreme….. ~ {Last call for gate number 18…. } ::::: John Adams flight was about to leave, all of these thoughts and feelings for the Prophetic one, makes you wonder, we all know as a Wrestler what he has in stored for his opponents, but as a man who loves the aspect of the mind games…..what does he have in stored for them in that department?::::: ~ The theory of any other winner would be preposterous…….. ~ ::::: So many people leaving to there own type of destination as we see a series of planes leaving from one end and out the other. All with a story to tell and a destiny to fill, for the Prophetic Theory, their destiny has been written in the stars but will they complete it in this tag match? ::::: ~ As the only team that will get up from this hellish confrontation will be The Prophetic Theory! ~ ~ There is timing in the whole life of the warrior, in his thriving and declining, in his harmony and discord. Similarly, there is timing in the Way of the merchant, in the rise and fall of capital. All things entail rising and falling timing. You must be able to discern this. Miyamoto Musashi ~ ~ If you're trying to take a roomful of people by surprise, it's a lot easier to hit your targets if you don't yell going through the door. Lois McMaster Bujold, "The Warrior's Apprentice", 1986 ~ ~ So there it is, it’s almost here…..can you feel it boys? The stage has been set up for your untimely demise! I must admit though these gimmicks you have are far from amusing, reminds me of a regular trip to Disneyland! Boys you have no idea of the hell me and Juvie plan for you……but you will! Welcome to my Realm!! ~ ~ FIN ~
