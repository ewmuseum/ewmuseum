Burn Baby, Burn (1): Forgive and Forget

29 April 2004

Date: 2004-04-29 Alex Caine… Defeated The Angel…Defeated The Fool…Defeated Carys…soon to be Defeated It’s been several months since my last outing before this tournament.. That being a match with the infamous Roulette of Dominant Wrestling Organization fame. Since then, I’ve done nothing to keep up with my wrestling practice or physique. In fact, I haven’t wrestled at all nor have I trained. I’ve gone and lost twenty pounds; just enough to be eligible for the Cruiserweight Tournament. Yet despite my lackadaisical efforts, I’ve still remained good enough to advance to the quarterfinals of this tournament. A part of me feels good about. It feels good to know that I can still dominate in this business. It feels good to read all the hype about my success and see how many people bet their savings on me. Then again, a part of me feels disappointed. This isn’t what I want. This isn’t what I yearn for. When I first became a wrestler, the promises of rich and glory seemed to be the only thing in the world. I got them, and I felt completed. It wasn’t until I met Emilia did I realize that I was fighting for all the wrong reasons. She changed me forever, and now she’s gone. I should have listened to her when I had the chance. But now it’s too late; nothing will change the past. I no longer want to fight. I no longer want to be associated with wrestling. I’ve lost my life, my friends, my love. The money? You can have it. I’ve been injured, put in a coma, and watched my fiancée murdered before my eyes. I don’t want to fight anymore. I hate it. I try to avoid it. Yet no matter what I do, evil seems to find its way to me. My two friends Mandy and Dawn are no longer. Victims of my curse. One lost her life; the other lost her mind. I’ve gone several months without fighting. They have been good months. But now, the fighting rears its ugly head again. A close friend whom I had just gotten to know stuck his nose in where it doesn’t belong. So, he made me be his remedy; his solution. Now as the tournament progresses, and everything falls into place, I cannot go back on my word. This is my fight now. It’s not about his debt anymore. Then again, I feel like in some strange way it was always my fight. Maybe this was God’s way of putting me back in the squared circle. Maybe I was meant to fight for the rest of my life. Whatever the case, this will be my last chance. I’ve already stated that I have been good enough to reach the quarterfinals. Now I wonder if I may be good enough to survive the Seventeen Sensation. Carys, the Rush Pro Wrestling Eclipse Champion, has been dominating in her performances. I would be lying if I said she doesn’t worry me. She does. She’s so frail, yet stubborn. Young, yet strong. She will be my greatest challenge in a long time. I vowed not hurt any woman…and for the most part, I’ve kept that promise. Beating Carys won’t be easy, but she will be beat. Then when this is finally over, I can turn my back to the wrestling world. I can say “hey thank you, have a nice day” and just be on my way to freedom. But first things first, I must win this tournament. I know what I have to do. Yet, I can’t help but feel just a little bit…lonely. <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Night befell the city and blanketed it with the moonlit sky. An R-34 Nissan Skyline roars up to a driveway in a rather rundown neighborhood. Definitely out of place. The engines purrs down like a tiger before coming to a complete stop. A few bystanders, most of them young, stop to gaze in awe before walking off in his or her own direction. Inside the Skyline, Syko sits at the wheel slouching back with Jacob Spencer at his side. “Thank you for everything…” Jacob says to him. “Without you, I honestly don’t know how I would have paid the debts.” Syko didn’t have the respect to look at the older man in his eye. He didn’t need to. He was just a “remedy” for the situation. “Don’t ever mention that, Jacob.” Syko retorted. “What’s done is done. You are out of your debt, so leave it at peace.” Jacob turned to open the door, but then stopped himself midway just as he was remembering something. A light bulb seemed to go off in his head and he turned to face Syko again. “What about you?” he asked. “You don’t have to fight anymore. My debt is paid.” This time Syko turned to face the man that was talking to him. “Your debt is, but mine isn’t.” Syko explained with remorseless eyes. He turned back in his seat, and Jacob could not help but inquire what he meant. “Debt? You mean you’ve dealt with these people before?” “Sort of, yes. The woman you saw the other night at the Coliseum was the owner of the Morir Sonando Casino. A few months ago she betted her casino on me in a tournament held long ago.” Jacobs’s eyes lit up with astonishment. “What happened? You lost?” Syko sighed and nodded. He remembered it all too well. “It was, oddly enough, what she wanted. I did end up losing, to a wrestler named Roulette, but I didn’t throw the match away like she wanted me to.” Jacob sighed and looked down. “Well she got what she wanted, didn’t she? So why the debt?” “It’s a sign of respect. I refused an offer I couldn’t refuse. She got her money, but she lost her power and self-control. The last time I saw her, she was gunning for me. I doubt she’s gotten over it.” Jacob chuckled. “Typical women, they can never forgive and forget.” “In your case, you better hope she does.” Syko snorted. Jacob slouched lower in his seat. “You’re right,” Jacob apologized. “So who was this Roulette person? He any good?” Syko glanced over at Jacobs and snorted. “It was a she. She was really good.” “Oh…’ was the reply. Jacob felt a bit surprised. “That reminds me, your next opponent is a woman. Carys, I think is her name.” Syko stayed silent for a moment, and pondered about his next opponent. He knew Carys far too well. You don’t become one of the most hyped wrestlers in the Prime Time Central Networks and not know who the viable challengers are. Carys was one of them. Syko was ten years older than her, but Carys was far more experienced than he was at this age. Her progress in the Cruiserweight Tourney had been nothing short of spectacular. To make her resume better, she became her federation’s Eclipse Champion, having beaten one of the favorites to win the tournament: Karina Wolfenden. “I know. She’ll be more than a hassle.” Syko replied, but with less confidence then he had in earlier rounds. “I don’t want to talk about her right now. We’re here, you know. Go home.” Jacob opened the door and stepped outside of the Skyline. He poked his head in through the passenger window. “Thanks for everything.” “Are you going to tell your family what happened?” “Are you kidding me?!” Jacob responded in shock. “I want to make sure they never find out.” Jacob walked away towards his cream colored house and never looked back. Inside his Skyline, Syko slouched lazily back in his seat. He rested his head back on his seat and looked out his window to the sky. He closed his eyes and his mind wandered off. Please…just let this be over soon. He took a deep breath in and started the engine again. The engine purred like a crouching tiger, and Syko sped out of the driveway, and into the distance. Soon…soon he will have to face Carys. But for now…that has to wait. There are still some issues to resolve with an old friend. <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> The next morning Syko stood inside the training ring in his grey Nike sweatpants and tank top. He had his long crimson hair tied behind him a rather messy ponytail, and actually shaved this morning. In front of him, in the ring, was Amanda, the most competitive person in his gym, and Carol, one of Amanda’s friends. Amanda and Carol weren’t talking. They were wrestling with each other. Yes, actually wrestling. Syko walked around them like a lion stalking his prey. Yet, he wasn’t stalking them. He was paying attention to their moves, cutting in every once in a while if he so something wrong; like an improper headlock that may be suffocating. For the most part, the two girls were holding their own making few mistakes. The rest of the gym watched from outside the ring in black plastic chairs. Some of them were still trying to recover from their own exhibition match. Others were anxious to see this one get finished so they can have a turn. Then there were the new guys. Syko’s portrayal, and thus far success, in the Cruiserweight Champion has lured many more young men and women to the gym. Be it from the commoner to follow where a famous celebrity is. Especially when that celebrity wrestles and could get them into any federation they’ve dreamt about. Or so they think. Inside the ring, Syko watched as the new girl Carol had Amanda in her best impression of a headlock. It was short-lived, however, as Amanda simply lifted her up and slammed her down in a side suplex. Syko shook his head. He was impressed with Amanda, and shot Carol a look as if to say “be careful.” Seeing the two women fight impressively made Syko remember about his own affairs. In a few days he would be fighting one of the favorites to win the tournament: Carys. Women are taking over the sport, he thought to himself. Indeed they were. No longer are they valets, but now they are dominating the market. Crucifying people, killing demons, getting Global Title shots, or just plain mauling them in the ring. Syko lost his concentration for a split second before regaining his composure and looking up. However, he saw something far more interesting to him than the two girls wrestling. In the far end of the room, he saw Guile and Marc, Karla’s top henchmen, leaving his office. They just walked out of the office, eventually out of the gym, nonchalantly without so much of a care or worry. “I have some matters to attend to girls.” Syko said stopping Amanda and Carol. “Excuse me, but you girls can continue. I trust you know enough not to hurt her, or yourself, right Amanda?” Amanda was too tired to speak, so she just nodded. Syko nodded in reply and stepped out of the ring. He walked briskly towards his office, ignoring anyone that was calling him. He couldn’t see Jacob anymore and feared the worst. It’s not possible, he thought. They wouldn’t do anything harsh. Syko opened his office door and walked inside. Surprisingly to him, everything was the way he left it. Well, almost everything. On this desk was an envelope. It was a small gift card-sized envelope with a scented breeze on it. He picked up the rose-smelling envelope and opened it and took out its contents. Inside was a fragranced letter. Syko opened it up and began to read it. To my special “Syko”. Oh it is with great pleasure that I write to you. How long has it been? How long has it been since we have seen each other? Syko chuckled and handed Jacob the letter. Jacob quickly glanced over the letter and handed it back to Syko. “You’re seriously not going to see her, are you?” Jacob asked in a loud hoarse whisper. “You know it’s got to be a trap.” “Do I know that for sure?” Syko retorted. “In her mind, she has every right to believe that she can trap me. I don’t know for sure. It could be, it could not be.” “Don’t kid yourself. That woman has been nothing but trouble. You think she just forgave and forgot?” Syko shook his head and yawned. He didn’t care. “I don’t care anymore. Like I told you last night, I still have some unfinished business with that woman. I’m going to see her tonight, like she wants. Maybe, just maybe she’ll be different this time around. If not…so be it. One way or another, one of our miseries will end tonight.” Jacob couldn’t believe what he was hearing.
