Turning the badger

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-21 The appearance of Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy at the XUW arena a few weeks ago had been a surprise. The man had become a legend in his absence, with nobody managing to dominate the entire federation quite the way that he had. No name was quite so synonymous with fear and pain as his, and nobody had ever quite filled the void he left. Seeing the man in the flesh again for the first time in a year and a half had left many feeling surprised, or even elated to see someone who could make intimidation so entertaining. For the members of the roster, and those who had mocked him when they believed him to have passed away, it was a different emotion they felt. Dread. Still, not everyone was entirely surprised, as his memorable appearance at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup will have lingered long in the memory of those who witnessed it. Not everybody did, though, and for some it too has passed into the realm of the urban myth. The Wrestler That Returned From The Dead. Did that really happen though? Several theories have been put forwards as to what really happened to The Ripper as he vanished under a hail of glass, metal and fire at the end of the XUW Reborn show in March 2003. Before he returned it was a known fact that he died, but with him preparing to take part in the PTC Survival I-PPV, opinions have had to change. Rumours abound, like the one where he challenged the Devil himself and defeated him to escape Hell. Or the one where The Ripper did not die at all, and has been hiding out in the Domicile of Darkness for the past year and a half. Maybe this is just a ghost though, the spirit of The Ripper, somehow in corporeal form, come to haunt the people of XUW, and now PTC. One theory that is different from the others is that Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy did indeed die on that fateful day, but did not escape Hell by defeating the Devil. In fact, The Ripper was allowed to leave Hell freely, to wreak more havoc and destruction upon the world. The Devil could not lose anything, since a soul as tarnished as The Ripper’s could never change. Whichever story is to be believed, the only pertinent fact is that The Ripper has returned, and nobody knows his intentions, though they can be presumed to be dark. His appearance with the other group members in front of XUW Commissioner Mister Webster hinted nothing towards any new direction, or new desires. Of course, the strongest currents often run very deep indeed. ----------------------------------------- The Ripper stood in the corridor, his black clothing contrasting with the drab grey of the painted walls. He had been standing there for a long time, but patience was always one of his virtues… one of the few. He moved for the first time in hours when he heard the sound of a door handle being turned, and slowly the Prince of Darkness pushes away from the wall and walked towards the source of the sound. “Yeah dude, I’ve got to, like, get me some Funyuns,” came a voice from the other side of the opening door. A smile that had nothing to do with humour crept on to the face of The Ripper. This was the time he had been waiting for; the time where he would solve the problems inherent in his plan. “Yeah guys… I’ll be right back.” Fatbadger left the Boomer Sooners locker room, and turned to stand face to face with The Ripper. Or more correctly, face to chest. Fatbadger’s eyes turned upwards, and upon seeing the evil grin of The Ripper, he lost control of his bottom lip, which began to quiver uncontrollably. “P… please don’t kill me and flay my skin off and then harden it and use it as a raft,” pleaded XUW’s most lovable jobber. The Ripper took a step back, and Fatbadger even winced at that. “That idea had never occurred to The Ripper… but now that you mention it…” The words were left in the air as a threat, and Fatbadger did all that he could to avoid curling up in to the foetal position and starting to cry. Then, thankfully, Fatbadger remembered that The Ripper was dead. “Dude, I just remembered. You’re dead. So, like, you can’t be here menacing me at all. You must some kind of really ugly hallucination.” “Do you mock The Ripper, tiny badger?” “Like, whatever, dude. I need to get some Funyuns. I haven’t had a bag of those lovely treats since… ummm… well I just had a pack, but I want more. Mmmm, Funyuns…” Fatbadger started to drool at the thought of the oniony loveliness he would shortly be experiencing. Unfortunately for him, The Ripper was far from being a hallucination. “There are more important things in this world than your snacks, badger.” This seemed to scare Fatbadger. Even with the safety blanket of knowing that this had to be a hallucination, the thought of Funyuns not being the most important thing in the world worried the young Boomer Sooner. “Don’t say that, dude!” “There are more important things at stake in the coming weeks, Fatbadger. The darkness demands that The Ripper defy not only Mister Webster, but also the rest of the world. This tournament must be won by XUW, and you are the biggest obstacle to this goal. The Ripper knows that woof woof and Blake Wolfson can be fearsome competitors within the squared circle, and that aside from their lunacy they will prove to be no handicap when the time comes. You, on the other hand, are the worst kind of handicap. The Ripper knows of your desire to lose every match…” “SPECTACULARLY!” The Ripper grimaced. “Quite. Yet this is not the time for losing in such a fashion. Instead, this is a time to cast aside the last vestiges of your foolish persona and either join The Ripper in his quest, his mission to defeat the rest of PTC… or perish.” “What does perish mean?” “To die, Fatbadger,” said The Ripper, with a smile creeping back on his face. “Join The Ripper, or die.” Fatbadger seemed to be having trouble coming to terms with this. If it was really The Ripper speaking to him, he’d be terrified. This hallucination was getting boring though. He must have smoked a bad glowstick or something. It was fun in a spooky way, but there were greater things at stake here… FUNYUNS! “I’m going to get some Funyuns,” said the badger, which seemed to rile up The Ripper more than many could have imagined possible. “Do not walk away from The Ripper!” Fatbadger decided to ignore him, like he tired to ignore Frank, the pink elephant who sometimes haunted his dreams. Sadly, upon trying to walk through The Ripper’s body, Fatbadger was surprised to find that he couldn’t do it. “What the hell? I have solid hallucinations now?” “No hallucination, badger.” “Then that means… you’re real?” “Yes. As real as ever, Fatbadger. Do you now see the error of your ways? Do you now see that the darkness is the supreme force in this world? The Ripper will harness the forces available to him and make sure that XUW wins the PrimeTime Central tournament. The only thing that stands in his way is you. You must be fixed, or else you will assuredly perish.” “What does that mean again?” “DIE, Fatbadger… DIE!” The Ripper seemed to be losing his now famous patience in the face of Fatbadger’s now famous idiocy. “Waaaaaaaaaaaaaaah!” “Stop crying, tiny badger.” “Waaaaaaaaaaaaaaaah!” “The Ripper insists you stop crying, and listen to reason! Get up off the floor… what the… stop curling up in a ball! The Ripper demands it! The darkness commands you to stop squealing like a new born baby about to be devoured by the infinitely terrible beasts of another dimension far fouler than this one. STOP IT YOU WRETCH!” If a picture paints a thousand words, then a size 14 boot to the gut must weigh in at somewhere around the million mark. Fatbadger shuddered and groaned upon receiving one, but his eyes came back to focus, and from his position on the floor he surveyed the man who had been used as a threat against him for years now. “So… *sniff*… if you’re here, and you’re real… then… YOU’RE A ZOMBIE!” “What?” “You’re the Zombie Ripper!” “The Ripper assures you that he is real, and that he is not a zombie. There is no time for this nonsense now. The Ripper must instruct you in the ways of darkness so that you win matches instead of lose them… or destroy you in such a way that stories will be written of your demise, and made in to blockbuster movies that rake in hundreds of millions of dollars.” “I thought it used to be stories used by parents to frighten little children in to obedience?” “The Ripper has moved upmarket.” “That’s just what a Zombie Ripper would say!” “What?” “You’re a zombie! I’m doomed, doomed I tell you! If only I had a typewriter and a large box to store items in, the only true zombie repellents in the known universe.” Another boot found its way to the gut of Fatbadger. “The Ripper is not a zombie. The details of this are immaterial, but you must make your choice now. Life or death, little badger… the choice is yours.” “Ummm… life sounds good.” A smile crept on to the face of The Ripper. He was once more in control of all he surveyed. Not everything was the same as when he last walked these hallways, eighteen months ago, but as Fatbadger acquiesced it all seemed to all be coming back to him. “We must leave this place for the Domicile of Darkness. Once there you can be tutored in the ways of the darkness. You are weak and foolish, but if The Ripper is strong enough he will be able to make up for your lack of ability to ever win matches.” Fatbadger looked like a portrait in terror, still on the ground and unable to move, until one idea forced its way in to his brain. “I won the World Title. I can win matches, sometimes… I think.” “The Ripper heard tell of this achievement, badger, but he did not… could not believe that such a thing had come to pass. You defiled The Ripper’s belt? How did this happen? What kind of trickery earned you a title shot, and what kind of champion would lose their title to a jobber like you?” Fatbadger’s mind goes back to those fateful months around the turn of the year. Ric Austin had crippled his best friend, the current PTC Global Champion, Saij. In a blaze of revenge, Fatbadger had found the strength within him to fight, and fight well. He captured a title shot against the then World Champion, and pinned him to win the belt. It had been one of the greatest stories in XUW history, the nobody that became a somebody, and a champion to boot. Fatbadger eventually lost his hold on the title, but was shortly reunited with his friends again, and all was well. Fatbadger smiled at the memories, thinking of the best way to tell The Ripper of his transformation from lovable jobber to inspired wrestler. “I dunno really, dude.” Eloquence was never Fatbadger’s strong suit. “Then The Ripper will act as if you did not win a single match in his absence. Fate sometimes deals out strange cards, tiny badger. Your hand must have had a greater purpose somewhere, but for now The Ripper has an ultimate quest. This goal must be reached, lest everything that The Ripper has strived for be lost. This is the pinnacle, Fatbadger, and The Ripper will not let you endanger the darkness.” Fatbadger got to his feet and looked The Ripper up and down. “Dude, it’s just a wrestling match.” If looks could kill, Fatbadger’s body would have been beheaded, cut in to small pieces, thrown in to an active volcano and then hit with an atomic bomb. Twice. “Do not say those words to The Ripper. It merely shows that you have a complete lack of understanding for his motivations, and for the purpose of the darkness. The Ripper has been to the very fires of Hell and back. The Ripper has broken more men than you’ve had bags of onion flavoured snacks. The Ripper has carried this company for years, even in death, even when absent the name whispered in the back halls was always that of The Ripper. Do not pretend this is just a game, tiny badger, and do not pretend that this means nothing to you. Do you remember the rage you felt when you saw Saij being thrown down through the stage, and his career, indeed his life hung in the balance?” Fatbadger slowly nodded his head. His long hair fell forward over his eyes, shielding them from the glare of The Ripper; shielding The Ripper from the knowledge that he was tearing up again. “Do you remember the pain and the anger you felt when you saw Austin gloat about this fact on television? Do you remember how much you wanted to kill that man as he held the World Title over his head in victory, whilst you knew that it should have belonged to Saij all along?” Another nod from the head of Fatbadger, whose hands were starting to ball themselves into fists. The Ripper continued. “Those emotions are nothing compared to the desolation of the darkness. Those emotions fall in to the black nothingness of eternity and are mocked, badger. Those emotions are pitiful, your compassion and friendship making The Ripper sick to his very stomach. To succeed, little man, you need to find a strength within you that only the darkness can comprehend, and only the darkness can provide. Do you understand The Ripper?” Fatbadger nodded a third time, his eyes drying up as he realised that he might need to compete properly in this event. If not for a feeling of revenge or satisfaction in seeing someone fall, then for his own well being. While he was thinking this, though, his mouth was already talking without his brain’s permission. “If you can only get that strength through the darkness, then how come Saij won the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, and you didn’t?” The Ripper scowled, his scarred face radiating pure evil and menace. Fatbadger was terrified, but had sadly not told his mouth to shut up. “Yeah, and you lost the title to Samurai Bob a few times, and he was a good guy. I don’t think that he was using a strength provided by the darkness, or whatever it was.” To The Ripper of old, these words would have meant nothing, and would count for little in the grand scheme of things. His patience was legendary, but he had been changed, whether he knew it or not. Never before would he have felt a compunction to explain himself to someone as insignificant as Fatbadger, but things were different now. It would not be for a while until he found out just how different he had become. “Do not mention that mans name. The Ripper returned to crush him, and The Ripper did what he intended to do. Samurai Bob is no more, and that is all that needs to be said about him. As for Saij… he won that cup because The Ripper let him win it.” “Whatever, dude.” “Listen to The Ripper and listen well. Had there been just two men in that cage, The Ripper would have triumphed. However, while Saij lay comatose on the mat, The Ripper spied the other competitor, the one named ‘Chryste’. She had that aura of being untouchable about her. She cared less for the win than for her self. So, when The Ripper could have won the match and destroyed Saij’s dream, he took the other path, the other choice instead. To touch that woman, to defile her in front of the world, it was better than winning the match. Her dreams did not rest in coming out as the champion, as was proved later in the match, but for keeping her image, her precious self intact. The Ripper stole that from her, and her loss was more devastating than anything that The Ripper could have done to Saij inside that cage.” Fatbadger mused this over, his brain desperately trying to get in to gear. “So, you lost because you wanted to get some with that hot chick?” The Ripper sighed. Had he known he would be sharing the world with Fatbadger, he might have elected to remain in Hell. “You may never understand it, tiny badger.” “Dude, I understand wanting to get laid, totally!” A huge backhanded slap took Fatbadger off his feet and dumped him back down to the floor below. “By the end of this week, The Ripper will make you understand loss. Only then will you be ready to defeat the rest of PTC and stand atop Mount Saint Hells with The Ripper.” “Dude, it’s only a wrestling match!” And so it was that Fatbadger received another ‘million words’, courtesy of Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy’s right boot.
