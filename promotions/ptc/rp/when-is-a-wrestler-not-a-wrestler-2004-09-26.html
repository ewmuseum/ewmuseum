When is a wrestler not a wrestler?

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 "I should have taken the elevator..." Weary steps almost gave in to gravity as Karina Wolfenden climbed the stairs up to her apartment. With her pale skin still icy from the crisp morning, yet to reach 6am, Karina waited for the building's heat to seep through her clothes and start to defrost her body, which had slowly chilled down over the hour or so walk from the station. After pulling her woollen gloves off, stuffing them in her trouser pocket, Karina rubbed her hands together, letting the friction bring warmth and life back to her numb fingers. The journey back from Charlotte had been tiring. She'd forgotten her MP3 player, so had to find other ways to carpet-bomb her senses for the train rides. The experience wasn't helped by the fact that it would be her last ever trip back from an RPW weekend... the regret only increasing her weariness. Her feet barely cleared the stairs as her soles scuffed along them, Karina even seemingly having to lean forward to stop herself toppling backwards. Usually vivid green eyes were half-closed, while her pink fringe hung limply over her face, blowing forward with every sighing breath she exhaled. Only a couple of flights away was a bed, snug and warm in a minimalist yet homely apartment. It was a bed she'd been thinking about ever since she'd boarded the first of her trains back, and it was a place she fully intended to spend the whole of the day. Pity the poor person who did anything to prevent that. "Scratch that," Karina replied to herself with, adjusting the straps of her rucksack so carrying it felt less like a double arm-amputation. "I should have taken one of the ground floor apartments..." After her whole body rippled as a yawn was forcibly expelled, Karina slumped forward, placing her bare hands on grainy wood and almost scaling the stairs on all fours. Weighing her down was everything she had kept at RPW's home venue, well, minus her MP3 player. They were belongings and mementos she'd have to find a new home for in her apartment, belongings and mementoes that reminded her she would never again grace the RUSH Pro ring. Just about straightening herself up, Karina grabbed the banisters and used them to help pull her slender, overloaded frame further up the staircase that rose through the middle of the building... forming the spine from which the ribs of the plentiful floors extended either side. Finally, with one straining heave, Karina took two steps at once and reached her floor, just managing to resist the urge to collapse in the hallway and sleep on the surprisingly clean floor. Stumbling steps, as if she were being toppled forward by her load, took Karina to her door, before a frenzied scramble in her zip-up top's pocket produced her equally overburdened key ring. "Ahhh," the K-Wolf exhaled, pushing to door open and wriggling her shoulders free of their burden. With a thud, her rucksack pounded against the wooden floor, before Kari nudged it aside with her foot so that the door could be closed. "Psylocke!" she then called into her spacious home, seemingly having forgotten the early hour which she emerged from. "Betsy! I'm home..." Her two cats, who probably took being stirred the same as Karina's neighbours. Only flicking on enough of the spotlights to see her path through the apartment, Karina tiredly strolled inside, and was reminded of the state she left it in. The CD's that had been sent flying from their stand as her bag knocked against it on her way out remained strewn over the flood, some of their cases cracked. It appeared the two cats had also knocked over her airer, leaving a layer of clothes on the floorboards. And the less said about the dishes in the sink, the better. "I need a cleaner," Karina yawned, un-zipping her top and just letting it fall onto the floor behind her. "Scratch that. I really need a cleaner if I'm going to be here at weekends..." A domestic Goddess, Karina was not. Scanning her tired, yet observant eyes from side to side, Karina tried to catch a sight of her two pets... not an easy task given the low lighting and chaotic placement of belongings. Even in the open-plan, single large room layout, there were plentiful hiding places. Crouching down by their timed feeder, Karina made sure that they still had food left, and that their water bowls still had something in them. "How many?" Karina then sighed, rubbing her dry eyes as the bright red of her answering machine's digital display became blurred. "17... Great." Short of patience and craving rest, Karina wearily tapped play, before strolling off and not really listening to what was being said out of the machine's speaker. Again rubbing her eyes, Karina trekked across the apartment to the kitchen, doing her best not to look at the piles of washing-up as she opened the fridge. "Karina, it's your father," caused her to slump against the cold, white appliance, letting out a groan that screamed what now?. "You know I don't like asking this, but we're going to busy this week, and are already 5 admin staff down. Do you think you can help out when you get back? Give me a call when you get this message. Love you." Beep. From the door of the refrigerator, Karina plucked a carton of orange juice, taking a large gulp from it as the door fell shut. From the mug tree beside it, Karina grabbed one of the Kandinsky patterned mugs, then filled it to the brim with the juice... preparing the vitamin hit that would replenish her dry eyes and tight skin. Feeling only a little bit left in the carton, she took a final swig to finish it off, before discarding the empty packaging on the work surface. "I also need a dishwasher," Kari remarked, having found it impossible to ignore the plates, cutlery, and pans that were scattered all around the sink. Reaching up into the overhead cupboards, Wolfenden tried to find something she had the time and energy to eat, eventually settling on a raisin cereal bar. Making it clear that she hadn't eaten on the journey back, the bar only took 3 bites to devour, before it's wrapper was left beside the juice carton. No guesses needed to work out that she was single, huh? "Er... Karina?" the answering machine continued. "It's Jerry Quecard here... someone dropped an MP3 player by the lockers, and Greg said it might be yours? Ring me back with your address and I'll send it on to you." Beep. "Two down," she once again yawned, kicking off her sneakers and discarding them where she stood. "Fifteen to go..." As Karina walked back past her two sofas, she grabbed a pair of cushions, then threw them across the spacious room and onto her bed with eerie accuracy. Her zombie-like strides then changed direction, dragging her towards the floor-to-ceiling windows where the early morning sun lit a pale blue sky from behind the sky-line. With a tug on the cord, Karina began a cascade of blinds, hiding the outside world behind them. "Karina, it's Max," then came from the machine. "Dad's been on at me to help him this week, but I'm pretty much snowed under here... any chance you can bite the bullet and give him a hand? I'll owe you one... or seven. Hey, and someone at work told me about that RUSH Pro place you were at... sorry to hear about it. But if that means you're free at weekends now, me and Fran have our house-warming bash next Sunday. Anyway, get back to me. Take care, and keep out of trouble, okay?" Beep. Before the fourth had a chance to be heard, Karina hit stop, before kicking the phone line from the socket, ensuring her peace. A few sips from the mug of juice prevented it from spilling when she stepped over the pile of clothes discarded by the toppled airer on the way to her most inviting bed... the one corner of her room free from chaos, with pristine sheets and fluffed up pillows awaiting her return with their warmth and comfort. Breaking off from the bright beacon her bed offered, Kari scanned around in the faint light for something... eventually finding it on the back of a sofa. Aiming the object with precision across the room, Kari hit play, suddenly bringing life to the tranquil apartment in the form of booming music. "MURDER! MURDER! A RIPE BLOOD STAIN. PULL THE FUCKIN' TRIGGER 'CAUSE I'M SICK OF IT ALL!!!" She'd apparently forgotten the less-than-chipper mood she was in before departing for weekend. There were two sudden bursts of movement from the apartment’s shadowed areas as Psylocke and Betsy jumped out of their hairy skins, and scurried for shelter from the audio onslaught. As shocked her pets, Karina was jolted back to alertness, nearly spilling orange juice as it swirled high around the mug's brim. Finally managing to find the volume down button, Karina held it and cringed as the ear-splitting volume subsided. "Okay," she sighed, looking slightly shocked as her heart began to match its natural beat once more. "I'll hear about that one tomorrow..." Switching through the auto-changer, Karina settled for a little bit of Moby to soothe the senses as she drifted off to sleep. After putting the auto-off timer to fifteen minutes, Kari put the control back where she'd found it, then took another large sip of the orange juice. "We have music, vitamin hit, disconnected phone, bed, and unemployment," she reeled off. "I'm all set." After resting the mug down onto a coaster on her bedside table, Karina reached around behind her, and inside her black vest. In a simple movement of the fingers, her bra was unhooked, then pulled free from under her top... taking away what was most of Karina's perceived chest as her padded support was discarded onto the floor. As her clingy vest readjusted to her rather reduced figure, Karina unzipped her trousers and let them collapse around her ankles. Stepping free of them, Karina ripped the duvet back and flung her admittedly curve-free body onto the mattress. Letting the ambient audio softly flood her senses, Karina wrapped herself in the duvet, while hugging the pillows close to her... as if to make a substitute bed-fellow. As exhausted as she was, Karina's thoughts kept her clinging on to consciousness for a minute or so. As much as she'd tried not to think about it on the way home, Max's message had yanked it back to the forefront of her thoughts. "One of these mornings..." RPW was closed, she'd made her last trip back from the Dale F. Halton... and Karina wasn't sure if after her commitment to the GTT was over, her "free agent" label would be switched to 'retired'. It was a hobby that had began to push out the others, and almost became one of the things she feared most in the world - a job. The K-Wolf's piste-taming and globe-trotting lifestyle had been compromised, and was now free to re-assimilate her. "Won't be very long..." She never felt like a wrestler, and now devoid of any wrestling deal, open or written, she wasn't even one in name anymore. "You will look for me..." As Karina felt either Psylocke or Betsy jump up onto the bed beside her, she drifted off, hugging the pillows even tighter as her toes clenched, leaving the questions for what would hopefully be another day. "...and I'll be gone." Lyrical excerpts are from "Sick Of It All" by the Distillers, and "One Of These Mornings" by Moby. The name Psylocke is taken from Marvel Comics, credit to Chris Claremont.
