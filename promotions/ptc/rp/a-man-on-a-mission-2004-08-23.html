Date: 2004-08-23 Success. It can only be defined by the one who feels they have achieved it... And it's pretty hard to spell. But either, way whether you believe that spelling the word is success enough or actually becoming successful, it all depends on the individual. One may find being elected to CORE's team to represent the federation in PTC's I-PPV is successful and others may see it as good and look at bigger honors. Winning a federation's Tag Team titles, and driving out a whole tag team division would be a great success but not this one individual. Adoph Nilsson strives for greatness and after winning the titles twice with his brother and being elected to the PTC IPPV team representing CORE, he now looks to out do himself. There's not much more that one can possibly do with a double digit IQ and a shorter attention span but Adoph looks to prove us wrong. Who knew that it's possible to go from Detroit to Athens in less than one city block? Nevertheless, Adoph's success continues this PRIDE when he gets a chance to separate himself from a tag team wrestler to a wrestler after getting the pin last week he can now attain COREwrestling single's gold. Many critics are saying that he has too much on his platter with the PTC IPPV match alone but Adoph will find a way... Adoph Nilsson will find a way to prove everyone wrong and fill their Oreos wil toothpaste... bust still prove them wrong. "HOW THE HELL DID WE WIND UP LIKE THIS..." Nickelback's, "Someday", is quietly playing on VH1's Insomniac Video Theatre. The TV on which it plays is more bigger than 25" and it takes the job of lighting the room. Across from the TV is a small, unstable desk holding an old PC monitor which helps the TV in lighting the room. Sitting directly in front of the monitor typing away at the computer is none other than CORE's Adoph Nilsson. He stops every so often and reads off the screen out loud, then continues to type. After a few moments we can tell Adoph is deep in conversation with someone over the internet and Adoph begins to read louder and speak every word that he types as well. "I can't believe you did that though," Adoph reads off the screen out loud, "... Please it's not like I did it on purpose, Bjorn said he didn't want to fight for a title or anything and really didn't want to partake in single's," Adoph hits the ENTER button and the loud alert comes back a few seconds later with a reply. "Look Adoph," he reads from the screen, "I know that you are excited about your success but you have to keep your brother in mind, he really doesn't speak his mind, only when he is angery you really should know that by now. Anyway, I have got to go, I'll see you at PRIDE." Adoph ends and doesn't type anything afterwards but turns off the monitor and spins around in the black business chair and turns to the TV which is no playing, "1985", by Bowling For Soup. Adoph yawns and then stands up and walks over to the TV, pulling something of the top of it to reveal a small alarm clock with bright green digits that read, 5:47. Adoph tosses down the piece of cloth he picked up and then makes his way to a small black couch and proceeds to plop himself on it. The couch is too small for the legal midgets, nevermind the 6'6" Swede. But, either way, he manages to squeeze onto it and within seconds begins to snore. ------------------------ "ERRRR! ERRR! EERRRRR!" The alarm clock shouts and Adoph jumps up, falling off the tiny couch. Adoph recooperates and then looks at the clock which now reads 1:29 in red digits. He doesn't take much time to get annoyed by the clock and reaches it pushing a button on the top of it and heads over to the computer. A beam of light is coming from the window right behind the computer set up and dancing wildly through the beam are little tiny pieces of dust, as Adoph turns on the monitor and reaches from the top of it and puts on a cowboy hat as the monitor slowly and gradually brightens up. Adoph stops for a moment realizing that he just put on a cowboy hat but shrugs it off as the monitor finally finishes turning on. On the screen a small blinking envelope is the first thing that Adoph clicks which causes the CPU to suddenly flip out begin making loud gurgling sounds but before long a new window pops up and Adoph looks closer to the list in the window. He takes control of the mouse and scrolls down slowly and reads off some of the titles of the E-Mails to himself. "Natural Male Enhancement, penis pump, penis pump, free viagra, dogs hump sluts, free mortage, guarenteed approval, xxx, good luck, porn for free..." As the screen comes up Adoph can't wait long enough and reads it as it's still loading but gets nowhere and ends up sitting back and waiting for it. After a few minutes it finally comes up and he takes the time to read it word for word. "Dear Ad... hmm... I guess that's me." Adoph says after reading the greeting, "I had a lot of fun the other night and I will def be ringside with GB at the IPPV. Thanks for being in my video too by the way, it was a huge help to fill in and even got more money with just your cameo. Thanks again, Your Redneck Woman." Adoph stops and looks stunned for a moment and then confused and leans back in his chair. "What the hell did I do?" Adoph speaks to himself putting his hands on the back of his head and leaning farther back. "Redneck woman? Damn I don't remember I think, maybe it's just someone going under I new name, I mean it couldn't be Helga because I talked to her last night and she said she wasn't going to be at the IPPV but at PRIDE. Eh, doesn't really matter much to me she's not going to do anything but be at ringside." Adoph finishes off and ensures himself that it's not his fault that he can't remember this person, building his confidence as he stands up and heads off across the room to a doorway. Adoph is followed into the next room, in which he tosses off the cowboy hat on a fitting sized bed for Adoph. He heads to the right side of the beard where the only door in the room sits and he opens it. Adoph looks inside for a moment as he rubs his eyes and yawns again before finally taking a good look at his closet. "What the hell is this?" Adoph says in disbelief as he pulls out a dress down shirt with a black sleeve and a white sleeve. Adoph begins rumaging through the closet pulling out the same style of shirts with different colors. Adoph plops down on the edge of the bed on the side of the pile of shirts. He scratches his head as he takes a look again at his now emptied closet and then back at the pile of fashion statments. Without putting up much of a fight and not being one to cause a fuss about small things Adoph picks out one of the shirts and feels that the cowboy hat would actually look good with the outfit. He walks back out minutes later as he buttons up his shirt and plops down sitting up in the couch. The TV is still on VH1 and "American Idiot" by Green Day is just finishing. The video ends and as Adoph kicks his feet up on the small coffee table in front of the couch he watches the VJ speak. "Alright guys this is an old one. And when we say old we're talking you were young when this song came out. I know that a lot of peeps be raggin on the country genre but you have got to give props to Adoph Nilsson in writing this song, the song's timeless. So coming in at number 71, "Standing Outside the Fire". Adoph takes a double take at the screen in pure and utter shock as he sees himself on TV, wearing the same outfit he has on now with an acoustic guitar around his neck and a microphone attached to his ear. The song that Adoph, made, is actually Garth Brooks' song as we now it. But Adoph doesn't know, nor did he even know would he care at this moment. Adoph turns off the TV and then stands up and puts his hand on his chin in confusion. Adoph doesn't know what to do but shout one and one thing only..." BJORN!!!" Adoph screams at the top of his lungs and while there is no instant response soon a clunking is heard and then from the middle of the floor a hunched over Bjorn Nilsson carrying a shovel steps out. "Bjorn?!" Adoph asks as he rushes over to the secret passage in which Bjorn just came out of. "What do ye want!?" Bjorn shouts with a real gritty look on his face. "I think I'm a country star." Adoph answers with a tone just begging for help. "Of course ye are... Ye kidnapped that there Garth Brooks and then stole all his music and made it big with him! Then you called me and I killed him." Bjorn answers as though there is nothing wrong with that scenario at all. "I killed Garth Brooks?" Adoph inquires almost intrigued. "No! I killed him, you just used his material. And now you live the high life while I dig. I dig all night, and all day... I just like to dig, I ain't known where I'm digging for the last 5 years, but I've been digging since you made the San Diego Padres." Bjorn answers again and doesn't allow Adoph to ask anymore questions as he climbs back down the passage and closes the bulkhead-like doors. Almost as soon as the the doors are heard locking, the door is bashed down and a muscular man wearing a cop outfit jumps in pointing a gun at Adoph. "Is there a problem here?" The cop says as Adoph jumps back and begins to cry. "Isnsdlfka McTree and then the thing and Brooks and... SPAM!" Adoph manages to mumble out in between his moans and the cop reaches down and outside the door and picks up a boombox and hits play, cueing some funky music. "Well did someone call the Pantless Police?" The man posing as a cop laughs and then rips off his shirt and pants to reveal his oiled up body and oversized package. Adoph Nilsson you are under arrest!" The little man shouts but before he can finish the reasoning Adoph laughs. "...for being way to hot?" STOP RESISTING!"
