Firewalk (Sonny Silver, PRIME) PRIME HEADQUARTERS TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 11TH, 8:25 AM “I’ve gotta say, Sonny. It’s getting to be real tiresome having you come into our office and having this same conversation week after week.” “I concur with Lisa. Sonny, whether or not you get some sort of thrill off being some sort of grand shit disturber is not my concern. What IS my concern is the fact that I’ve got a nice set of migraine medication in my desk JUST for these weekly visits. Quite frankly, I’d just like to cut out the middleman and deal with these problems right here.” “Blaine, have you ever thought of just asking Lisa to loan you her Midol? On second thought, doesn’t seem to help her, either. Probably the fact that it gets offset by her daily intake of Carnation Instant Bitch.” “Ah, there’s that lovely charm that sells tickets, raises buyrates, and wins titles… oh, wait, you don’t do that last one, do you?” “…” “Truth hurts, doesn’t it? Well, sir, as much as we enjoy a little verbal tête-à-tête every now and again, I’m afraid I’m cutting out that part of these constant visits and getting right to the nitty-gritty. That little stunt you pulled on ReV 137 with having Nitz Donnelly bust Lindsay Troy’s kneecap isn’t just limited to Nitz. It was your brainchild, thus it’s your punishment, too. Because of you, the career of a young and talented individual that PRIME had high hopes for is on hold indefinitely. “You don’t see fit to punish Lindsay Troy’s VERY same action against Nitz? Wow… it stinks in here… smells almost like… hypocrisy.” “Troy received a fine, so she did not go unpunished. Plus, despite your unflattering opinions of her, she’s looked up to in the back and thought of as a leader for this establishment, unlike you two who make a daily habit of riling up our fanbase. She’s done very well spearheading PRIME and it doesn’t look like that’ll stop anytime soon.” “Anyway, to our point, you’re poison; pure and simple, straight from the Goddamn bottle. We can’t keep having to go through this crap every single week. You’re a draw, Sonny. You really are. People pay to see you get the ass-whomping you deserve. But you’ve already destroyed three careers… two of which were your own former lackeys when you decided to do a David Beckham impression with their skulls. As long as Blaine and I are running this ship, you won’t do so again.” “We feel it’s fit that in this particular situation, we do something that’s going to show you that every action you make affects somebody. You see fit to piss people off and revel in their hatred of you. They come to our shows and watch our Pay-Per-Views so they can call for your head. Since you like to see them come to you so much, we think it’s more than fair that you get to be thrown to them.” “Beg pardon?” “Blaine’s talking about Public Appearances. We notice you avoid them like the plague…” “…Cause people are ugly.” “We notice you depart them early when you do take place…” “…Don’t want ugly people crowding around my car.” “We also notice that you sometimes berate the fans.” “Ugly people are sensitive.” “So you’ll be making one in Atlantic City the day before Dice, Dollars, and Tragedy goes on the air.” “…Uh… the HELL I am.” “The hell you’re NOT. You want to make a living by pissing people off and endangering our talent? Then you better be prepared to deal with the consequences. We weren’t joking when we told you a few weeks ago that you’d be on a tight leash, Silver. Before you leave here, we’ll discuss complete travel arrangements.” “…And if I tell you that you can both you can rot in the deepest, darkest, horrid pits of TNA booking instead?” “Simple. You lose your spot in the Roulette match. ReV 138 would have REALLY been your last shot at redemption for the lack of a successful career you remind people of week in and week out.” “This is Grade-A bullshit.” “This is your job. Do it.” ~~~~~~ BORGATA CASINO HOTEL AND SPA THE NEXT DAY, 7:10 PM Ah, the wonderful splendor of Atlantic City. To think such a vibrant hub of activity could be confined within the proverbial gates of hell that were New Jersey state lines was remarkable to Sonny. To make it even scarier, just down the road away from all the neon lights and money-making hoopla, outsiders could clearly see the lines between rich and poor. The fancy-pants near-millionaire tycoons inhabited the buildings with perpetual glow while the lower, blue-collar classes were herded like cattle into dilapidated housing. It was horribly amusing to Sonny when he thought of it like that. But just because the lines were as plain as day didn’t mean said working class couldn’t – for one night – step into a world they only wish they could partake in. John Q. Public and Sally Workingclass were free to bring their hard-earned cash acquired from toiling away at their run-down businesses and try to improve upon their luck. All they had to lose was a few bucks… life savings for some. Quite sad as well… …But Sonny wanted no part of any of it; at least when he was being subjugated to looking straight at the working class up-close. Hosted from one of the many gargantuan conference rooms that made up the Borgata Hotel, Sonny knew he was in for a long night. Damn, he thought. I have a newfound appreciation for barricades. These barnyard animals are hideous even from a distance. “Next.” Two of the security staff accompanying Sonny’s public appearance session waved another body through their barricade. An older lady accompanying what appeared to be her proud baby boy approached the table. Sonny peeked up through sunglasses and looked at the ratty-dressed mother. Her face, too. Ew, pockmarks. Maria Silver, however, was able to see through outwardly appearances and see a mere mother/son tandem and wrestling fans that would jump at the chance to get to meet one of them. Just as she spotted the boy, Maria’s Internal Child Alarm went off. You know, the one where moms suddenly have their brains reduced to mush and start babbling incoherently at children they perceive to be cute. Guys, it’s annoying shit, no doubt. Sonny’s mother started to rub a hand through the boy’s hair and laughed boisterously. “Well, little guy,” she spoke. What’s your name?” “Uh… hi. I’m Brandon.” Sonny rubbed his temples and rolled his eyes upward. He thought back to the time he arrived on Maria’s doorstep a month ago when he asked her to accompany him on the road. It was a mutually beneficial situation in which she would be able to get out of her secluded abode in the middle of Washington and in return, provide an answer for the solitude Sonny woke up to every single day in a business where burned bridges and disaffection of his fellow man left him without companionship. It was all gravy at first, but her maternal instincts kicked in at the most inopportune times. The old stories, the tired anecdotes, the cutesy crap. He was beginning to remember why he deliberately placed distance between he and Mrs. Silver over this last year. The personality clash was starting to be too much for him to bear. After this was over, he decided. When this autograph signing was over, he’d break the news to her. But that was then. This… unfortunately, was now. Once out of his funk, he looked up and saw the observably shy child nestled closer to his mom, not knowing just quite how to react to seeing an actual wrestler in person. “He’s ten,” the kid’s mom laughed. “You’ve got yourself a cutie there!” Maria said. “My oldest was like that at that age. He was adventurous when it came to sneaking out of his room, but if it involved other people, he’d dart the other way like a bat out of…” “HELLO!” Sheepishly, Maria grinned. “Sorry.” Sonny ran a hand through his coarse hair and muttered at his mom for her impromptu trips down Memory Lane. Brandon and his mother, taking the hint, put a picture on the table of Sonny Silver in action against current PRIME Universal Champion Lindsay Troy at Colossus IV. He eyed the child and gave him an acknowledging nod before procuring a Sharpee from his shirt pocket. “So,” he started. “Any personalized message you want on here?” After careful consideration, the boy finally spoke up and laughed. “Lindsay Troy beat your butt! TWICE!” An awkward silence befell Sonny and his mother. Young Brandon’s mom took the comment as “boys will be boys” with a hearty laugh, but Maria – knowing full well the antagonistic relationship between Sonny and his former friend, Lindsay Troy -- was silently praying that for once in his lifetime, his sense of professionalism would outdo his hotheaded temper and he wouldn’t make an ass of himself in public. “Heh… heh… heh… That’s some funny sh- stuff, kid!” The unnatural sound of forced laughter educed from Sonny’s throat as he patted the kid on the head and begrudgingly continued to sign his 8 x 10 without making a scene. A sigh of relief escaped Maria’s lungs as she thanked God silently before smiling back at Brandon’s mom. “Quite a mouth on him, too. Reminds me of Son…” “Ahem.” Sonny tried to deliberately cut her off. “My son…” “AHEM.” “My s-“ “AHEM.” Finally getting the message and sighing at her oldest, Maria shook the hand of the mother. “Thanks for coming out to see us! Enjoy your day!” Now equipped with the autograph of the big PRIME superstar, Brandon and his mother left Sonny and Maria to their own devices. The mother and son duo courteously waved to some spectators in the crowd from afar who came armed with cameras/phones/soul-stealing picture machines of their choice. Unbeknownst to the crowd was the conversation going on between the aforementioned mother and son hiding under the façades of pearly teeth. “Why in God’s name do you embarrass me, Mom?” Sonny continued to wave. “As your mother, it’s one of my legal duties.” Before Sonny can offer up another patented Silver rebuttal, a triad of older, out-of-shape guys – each looking to be in their mid-20s or so – made their way through the barrier and advanced near the signing table. Sonny shook each one of their hands as they laid their pictures out on the table, awaiting his John Hancock. Oh, lord. Please, don’t be internet smarks. Anything but internet smarks. I’ll personally come up there and kick your ass, God, if they’re smarks… “Dude, you got robbed!” One man in an XXL sports jersey spoke. Another in an old-school Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME t-shirt chimed in. “Those bookers are idiots for making Troy go over you twice, bro!” The last guy sporting a ridiculously oversized red beanie had to throw in his two cents. “Man, you’re awesome! You throw some nasty kicks in that ring, dude!” I’ll job to Lindsay TEN more times if you smite them right now, Almighty. Meteor, anthrax, computer monitor exploding glass in their face. ANYTHING. Sonny could do nothing more in the situation but offer up a half-hearted smile to the three professional wrestling aficionados before holding out a hand for XXL Jersey’s hand. Oversized Beanie and Old-School PRIME Shirt continue to dance around behind him like giggy schoolchildren and snapped unsuspecting pictures with their respective camera phones. By tomorrow’s time, they’d be on their own Myspaces posting blogs about how they’d met their hero and pictures of Sonny looking like he was on drugs. Sonny wasn’t oblivious to this fact, but he rolled his eyes and pressed onward. While Sonny completed the rest of the trio’s autographs, Maria laughed at the energy these young’uns possessed and patted Sonny on the shoulder. “How long have you gents been waiting in line?” Maria inquired. Oversized Beanie waved his newly acquired siggie from one of his favorite wrestlers. “Four hours, lady! But it’s worth it! This is the shit!” She giggled. “Impressive. You know, Sonny was like that early on. He wanted those Lynard Skynyrd tickets and he’d camp out for hours…” “Mom.” A cold voice from her son cut off the story. She lightly jabbed an elbow into his sternum before waving off the three boys setting out to try their hands at the Slots or a nice game of Blackjack. That money wasn’t going to make itself. Maria turned to her oldest and shook her head. “You need to lighten up a little, you know. I’m not out here to completely humiliate you. You ASKED me to come, remember?” “I did, but that came with the proviso that the “naked baby pictures” and “when he was that age” stories were left back at the hotel room. Now bring something meaningful to the proceedings that don’t involve either of those.” She laughed to herself for a moment, enjoying what she’d assumed to be another simple family bonding moment. When she turned to see him again, the expression she was met with was not jovial. “You’re… you’re really upset about this?” He said nothing, but she knew that look on his face when it appeared. The “I’m hiding something and you gotta force it out of me” face. Icily, he waved the next person in line. Rather than having to sit through an awkward sense of nothingness between he and Maria, Sonny allowed business matters to take over and prepared for the next autograph. Sonny didn’t bat an eye, but Maria knew the next words emanating from his mouth were solely for her. “We need to talk after this show.” Maria’s heart sank, but before she had any time to process his suddenly grim tone, the next fan planted himself in front of the table. Sonny immediately noticed this man wasn’t like a lot of the other people that’d come up to him. Along with the fact the man seemed to be bigger than all three of the previous fatasses that’d waddled his way, there was a lack of energy in this man… no, rather, a different kind of energy entirely. There was no laughter; there was no picture in his hand. The look on his face was almost sour. “How you doing?” Sonny fired out to get a reading on this man. The thick finger of the man pointed directly at Sonny. “Dude, you’re a fucker.” Oh, lord, another disgruntled fan. Yippee. Security shook their heads and started to advance upon the man, but Sonny held up a hand. “Wait.” He eyed the man up and down. “Obviously, if this guy made it this far to say something, let him say it… THEN throw him out.” The two burly event staff members kept close, but allowed him to say his piece. He continued his tirade. “Dude, last time you went to a show here, my sister was in the front row cheering on the other guy you were facing. During your match, your punkass got knocked into the first row and you screamed at her for touching you.” In between his baited breaths, Maria’s head perked up again to look at the stranger blasting her son. “You said something to the effect of “get off me, bitch.” That’s just Goddamn rude! I know you guys are out there busting your asses for our entertainment, but that doesn’t give you the right to walk all over us, you prick. Without us, you wouldn’t HAVE a show you old piece of shit.” Sonny let each and every word from the heavyset heckler pass through one ear and ooze out the other. Trying to diffuse the situation entirely, Sonny put up a reassuring hand. “Guy, list-” “NO, YOU LISTEN. YOU…” Before he opened his mouth to retort, Maria bolted up from her seat and firmly places both hands on the desk, not backing down from the massive behemoth. “Don’t you have ANYTHING better to do than to heckle my son? I don’t know whether or not he did the things you’re accusing him of; sometimes, it wouldn’t surprise me! But have the common courtesy to not make a scene about it! ” The mammoth shot a nasty, yellow-toothed smile and chortled. “This your mom?” He laughed in the face of the PRIMEate to make himself look tougher to the crowd. “Come on, I thought you were supposed to be the total badass here! You need your little Mommy to fight your battles for you?” Sonny jumped from his chair and stared down the beast who was getting a kick out of riling the wrestler up. Sonny knew it and the heckler did, too. He held out both hands and laughed. “Come on. You gonna hit me? Do it and you lose your job. You deserve it anyway, you loser. I bet Mommy would love to see Sonny boy fail at something else.” Sonny would’ve liked nothing more in that instance to knock his fucking block off. The heckler puckered up his face and flared his nostrils like a bull. He didn’t appear to be intimidated at all. He welcomed Sonny’s knuckles to his face with open arms. Sonny would grant his dying wish and give him one last meal of a fucking knuckle sandwich. He wound up a fist and gritted his teeth, ready to strike him down. Suddenly, he shifted his focus to all the other rubbernecks at the scene. Fans were ready to see him kick some ass, security readied themselves to avert the nuclear disaster, but only one pair of eyes mattered. Wait, it wouldn’t surprise her for me to do something like that? Sonny knew deep down she was right. He was a prick inside and out. He’d made his bed and he prepared himself for the one day people got ballsy enough to do something about it. But it wasn’t gonna be this fat fuck. Sitting back down in his chair and taking the high road in the situation for once, Sonny nodded for security to remove the wide load from the premises. While being escorted away, his guttural laugh filled the building. “HAHAHA! WHAT A PUSSY!” That was his last proclamation before being escorted from the building. Rather than give the heckler any more sort of power over him, Sonny calmly remained seated and waved the next person in line to get their autograph. They’d waited a long time to get it and some fatass wasn’t going to ruin that for them. Maria looked on at her son in complete shock as he shook hands with the next person and spoke with them about the wording they’d like on their next autograph. Where was her son and what did this doppelganger do with him? Once that feeling subsided, she’d made a discovery about her son that made her smile just a little bit. There was some humanity in there after all. ~~~~~~ Three hours of hell was enough for Sonny Silver and he was ready to call it a night. He had a lot of business to conduct in the morning regarding Dice, Dollars, and Tragedy and a Universal Title to secure. Standing outside the lobby awaiting the next available elevator, Sonny broke the tension between he and Maria. “Some crazy night, huh?” he lightly chuckled. “Indeed,” she nodded in agreement. Suddenly, her mind traveled back to when his face had gone stone cold. She knew something was on his mind, but if she was gonna find out, now was a perfect time to ask. “So… you were saying?” “Huh?” “You said you had something to discuss with me?” Sonny’s face was an unreadable mask of stone. “…Uh… One more look into the clear blue pools of her eyes. “…Thanks for coming out with me today.” Maria was caught completely off-guard by something resembling a complement. She didn’t know how else to return the favor except with… “Oh. Uh… you’re welcome.” Having said all he needed to say for the moment, Sonny tapped the elevator button to the fifth floor where a King-sized bed would await him. For the first time during this entire trek between mother and son, an unfamiliar feeling washed over him. Comfort.
