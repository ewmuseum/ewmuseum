IG 33 - Trash Talk Here!

8 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
8 July 2007 Bobino - [IG:33] “The Motherfucking New Shit” Bobino – [IG:33] So... it seems that the world has forgotten about me... or after my latest missed appointment, given up all hope on me. It seems that I underestimated how quickly people like to jump on bandwagons of people they'll never see again given a few months. Do you people honestly think we'll be hearing from Tchu a year from now? Not likely. Expect him to give the usual “I've done everything there is to do” speech that washed up PRIMEates like to give when they realize they're outclassed by nearly all of the new blood around them. Expect it soon, as Tchu really doesn't mean a lot around here anymore. It's pretty sad that even the Great Tchu, couldn't get past the first round in this year's GTT6. Think about this... even I made it to round two, Tchu. Well, Maybe I should watch myself, since you're already being surrounded by fellow Overrated PRIME stars like Easton Hall and Sonny Silver. Maybe I should just let you guys settle this amongst yourselves, while I focus on the prize. Maybe you guys need to realize that in this day and age, even James von Doom has more credibility than you jokes. Maybe you guys need to realize the only place any of you people mean anything...is in PRIME. That's what I have over you. I'm a free spirit. I'm free to talk about whomever I want, and fight where ever I want. You people are bound to the mediocrity that is PRIME. Speaking of PRIME... where's your big hitter? Where's your Mark McGuire? Where's Jason Snow? Is he a bit too afraid to come out of the woodworks? Is he threatened by The Hunchblack, Big Bear, Ian Nackedy, and James von Doom? Is he too afraid of those massive superstars to even come out of the works? It's a sad day in Primetime Central history when you're called out for not showing up by me, Snow. Yeah.. you heard me, Jason. BOBINO is calling JASON SNOW... a flake. I may have missed a show here or there for the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. I may have been released from FUSE for attendance issues, but at least I have the balls to man-up, Snow. At least I've never walked out on a championship match. At least I'm a big enough person to admit my shortcomings and weaknesses. You Jason, you're just like everyone else in PRIME Land. You all think you're so important. It's time to face reality. Tchu, Silver, Hall, Snow...you need to realize that you're nothing to the rest of the world. In the time-line of Professional Wrestling, none of you will even be mentioned to people years from now. Jason Snow, you are the lone exception, you might get one aside, speaking of how you found limited success outside of PRIME. The sad fact of the matter is this. The world, and everything it contains lives by one simple rule. The Strong Survive and The Weak Just die. You will all shortly learn that since none of you adapt... you are weak. If any of you would like a free lesson, simply watch me dismantle each and every one of the people that step in front of my in this Infinite Gauntlet. Watch as I do something that must be against the rules in PRIME.... evolve. I can prepare myself for any onslaught, and trade blows with the best...as I'll prove now. I can prepare for anything, so here's a quick run-through for you. Yes, I've no-showed dates. Yes, I've been late quite often. Yes, I've been fired from FUSE. Yes, my name is funny. Yes, my name contains the word shit. Yes, Darwinism says I came from a monkey. Yes, I was just recently defeated by Jason Snow for the Unified Title. Now... with those facts that I refuse to deny out of the way... I'd like to see anybody make a valid argument against me. I honestly dare anybody to come up with anything original to beat me. Especially somebody from PTC's Retirement Home, PRIME. Come on Silver, rap or do something else stupid about me. Come on Tchu, pretend you don't care about me. Come on Armando, diga algo en español acerca de mí. Come on Big Bear, write home to Momma about me. Come on von Doom, keep the streak alive against me. Last but definitely not least... Come on Snow... keep flaking out on me. ---- 8 July 2007 Bobino - [IG:33] “The Motherfucking New Shit” Bobino – [IG:33] We're brought to the scene by a sharp, lonely sound. A sound all on it's own, and without much oomph. We're brought to the scene by a sound not unlike that found at the Easton Halls fan club. As the scene comes into focus we see an empty arena, with only one seat occupied, half way up the bleachers, and directly in the middle. Here sits the source of the strange, yet familiar sound. It's applause. Not a roaring group of applause, however. One man stands alone in giving Easton Hall's musical performance a standing ovation. That man claps in the most sarcastic, slow, and lazy pace he can muster. Bobino tries to look as sarcastically excited as he physically can as he continues. “Bravo! Bravo! Easton, That performance was amazing. Without a doubt, the crowning achievement of your entire career. That's right, this is the most memorable thing you will ever do, “Easy” Easton . Mr. Hall, I do have to say... you're simply pathetic. After all that time, the best thing you could muster was 'You suck Bobino' with a side of 'suck my nuts, motherfucker.' Wow, just wow. It says so much about you, the best insult you could possibly think of was to sing, very poorly, and say I suck. Hall, listen here, maybe next time you attempt to do a poor version of another PRIMEates shtick... you'll have slightly better material. Maybe next time, you'll save some of the money that you schilled out to arrange this whole thing, and buy yourself a clue. Nobody finds you entertaining, Easton. Nobody thinks you're funny, or even good at anything you do. You're simply a space holder on PRIME's roster, and really your existence amounts to two things. Jack and shit. Don't you try to come back at me with some drivel about being a “Five Star” champion or whatever. Before you do that, ask me if PRIME's championships mean anything to people -NOT- in PRIME. I'll save you the trouble. The reason I don't talk about holding over ten championships in FUSE's history, is because honestly, nobody outside of a specific company gives a fuck about that company's championships. So, Easton, go back to the drawing board, suck off Silver a bit more, and come back to me when you have something above a third grade level.” Bobino begins to walk towards the aisle, preparing to exit. He makes it the the steps and begins walking down as he continues, “Is that really the only thing people can say when I ask them to do something original? Is this really the best Primetime Central can throw at me? Is this place really as pathetic as I made it out to be? This is sickening. The only person who can even attempt to be original, yet fail miserably, is Easton Fucking Hall. Where is Sonny Silver? Where is Tchu? Where are all the other people that like to talk big? Most importantly, where is that flake, Jason Snow? This really is sad. Mark this day on your calendars, people. This is the day that Bobino takes a championship away from Jason Snow... via no-show. Really, that's what this has come down to. I swoop in, tell everyone exactly how this place works, and everybody is dumbfounded. I've simply proved my point so well, that nobody even dares try to dispute me, for fear of looking like a fool. People, think about this... this is the day that Bobino has silenced the entirety of Primetime Central. Bobino reaches the floor, shaking his head in disgust at the happenings. He looks towards the floor for a moment, before looking into the camera and muttering his final words. “It's a sad day indeed. The only man with enough balls to step up to me is Easton Hall. I've frightened Tchu, Silver, Snow, and countless other names you'll forget in less than six months. It was a good effort, Easton, but unfortunately, it just made you look more foolish than you already did... if that was possible. Let me give you a small amount of advice. This is the big time, Easton... save your grade school shit for PRIME, they've always been the minor leagues, anyways.” ---- 9 July 2007 Bobino - [IG:33] “The Motherfucking New Shit” Bobino – [IG:33] Bobino stands stare bullets into his watch. He taps it a few times, until he looks up and sees the camera watching him. “It's about fucking time. I've been waiting here all day, and finally somebody from that shithole you people like to call PRIME steps forward to try and defend the slim shred of honor left in that place. There's not a whole lot to defend, but Tony Fucking Gamble sure took his precious time jumping up to defend the Alamo. Think about this. Gamble doesn't say a single word, and suddenly, when he believes the rest of PRIME choked, he steps in and tries to steal the spotlight. I've got some rather bad news for you Gamble, the rest of PRIME didn't choke, they were just trying to put a bit of thought into their statements. See, Tchu, and Silver, while falling well short of their goal, at least -TRIED- to look like they put effort into the selection of their words. You, however, decided to go with the 'let's just babble on about shit nobody cares about' technique. Gamble, I love how you back me up in my claims about the lack of talent in PRIME. You said that this Infinite Gauntlet is the lowest of the low... but you need to realize, that everybody entered, sans myself and James von Doom, is from you guessed it, The lowest of the low... PRIME!” Bobino's face shifts from trying to understand Tony Gamble's frightened spotlight hogging, to the complete ignorance of Tchu. “You know, I made a simple request. All I asked for was that if you were going to try and address me, come up with something new. All I asked for was an original joke, or a new insult. Just like Tchu, though...never gives anybody, including the fans... what they want. The best to insults you could msut... were my job status, and my profane moniker. Wow... I've never heard that before, Tchu. Oh well... can't really expect anything from you, you waste every opportunity you touch. I mean after all, you said it yourself, you're just 'that PRIME guy' to us.” Bobino chuckles a bit as he continues to look around the room. He sees that the room is nearly empty, as he looks back to the camera. “You see this room, Silver? You recognize it, don't you? No, you've never been here. No, you never will be here... but it's still strangely familiar. You know why, Silver? Look around... you'll find the exact same thing here that's inside yourself, and on your achievements list. Absolutely nothing. Silver, I'll give you this... for a slim moment... you had my hopes up. You had me believing I was going to get something new, something edgy... then all I got was the 'you suxxor at the trash talks'. Wow. Chairman of PRIME for you right there. That's what PRIME wants to put their name on. A guy who can tell people they suck, and make fisting and child molestation jokes. Silver, I'm sorry, but it's a sad, sad day when a FREE AGENT is completely embarrassing every effort PRIME puts forth. I guess I should consider myself lucky that your savior, your hero, Jason Snow was simply too afraid to show his face. I guess I should consider myself lucky that nobody from places that Matter, like FUSE, or XUW, put any effort into this. I guess I should just feel lucky that anybody in PRIME that ever had talent, retired after realizing where they were. Face facts boys... Number One by definition, simply no longer true.” Bobino shakes his head, more in shame of his opponents “attempts” at trash talk than anything else, as the scene fades out.
