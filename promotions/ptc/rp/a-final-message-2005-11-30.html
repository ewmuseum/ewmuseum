A final message

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 A quote from Mr. Zenith: "Why am I here? I came here to find a challenge, not a half naked Pocahontas with an attitude problem. She couldn't beat Vivica, she cannot beat me, and she will except her destiny as a total failure. What does Danielle Northsky have that I don't? A modeling contract? Breasts? No one cares about that, because I have something she'll never have… a GW World Title reign. Two of them. You can't stop me Dani, but don't worry little girl… no one can. Watch me." I couldn't beat Vivica, huh? Well I like how Mr. Zenith goes on to denote that Vivica is below him, and thus if I couldn't beat her the one time we faced, I can't beat him. As far as I knew, Vivica J. Valentine was not Christian Zenith. Then again, supposedly she's his girlfriend. But many reports and observations, including those that I've done myself in the locker room of Global have been pretty adamant about: Vivica J. Valentine and Christian U. Zenith have never shown any form of public or private intimacy. Some would have to wonder if Vivica is just on his payroll. Just like the girl in Pretty Woman. "I'll pay you to be by my side and pretend". Except she doesn't get the happy ending side of the Cinderella story. Now, we can understand this if we look at how a man who thinks it's the Christian Zenith show in Global actually 'rides'… Chicago style, right? He's formed a stable with him and a bunch of beautiful women. He's even offered me that spot, twice. Oh, and before he says anything else. That spot was offered to me right after I got there, when I was actually losing on a regular basis. The occasional win, here or there didn't matter. He wanted women who could fight, and looked good to surround him. He thinks he's the god Adonis or something. That's all I'll give you. I've read up on your little psuedo-take over the Christian side of things story. It's about as fanciful as a Mother Goose tale though. Just like his supposed second World Title reign. How about we turn the tables, and just take a look at not the last 3 months of Mr. Zenith's career. But just the last month. Since the Dark Rituals Pay Per View, where Mr. Zenith came out smelling like roses, wearing the highest belt in Global. He's done, exactly what? Global has had 3 events since then. All 3 of them were the regular weekly shows known as 'Evolution'. October 17th: Mr. Zenith was seen in of all places, in the parking lot. Yup, Valentine had an opening match. Zenith was seen after Train defaced his limousine. Where was I? In the ring, wrestling agaisnt Jake Hix for the first time when Shadow no showed our match up. Did I win? Nope. But then again, Hix was a finalist in the Master of the Mat tourney, right? So loosing to him when I'm a rookie shouldn't be a big deal. October 31rst: Mr. Zenith was no where to be seen. Word has it that he was at home, refusing to show up. Jake Hix and Dani Northsky headlined that show. Zenith, disgraced a title. November 15th: Mr. Zenith was in the main event. Being carried by Shadow, Virgil Ambrosi and Valentine. I was in the middle match in a six person tag team match. Oh, and Zenith barely did anything in that match. It was Shadow who won it. But we've all seen that she's been carrying him for some time now. You'd figure her back would have gone out for how much work she has to put into that job. But then again, Mr. Zenith, the week before Dark Rituals lost to Shadow in an International Title match. How the hell he was moved up to the main event at the Pay Per View after that, we'll never know. But my own research goes to show the uniformed Global viewer the reality of the place. It's not the Christian Zenith show. Not in a longshot. He's nothing. He's not the ratings grabber. Who was on the shows every week, performing in the upper half of the cards? People who produce, who are good in the ring, and who can carry someone's sorry ass, like him, through a match. People like MacFarlane, Shadow, and… Me. Maybe Valentine, a floosey that appears to be on his payroll and not, really his intimate girlfriend, probably did some favors for him. Are you pimping her out to the committee? Speaking of which, Mr. Zenith. You strike me at a chord: "I came here to find a challenge, not a half naked Pocahontas with an attitude problem." I'm sorry, Christian Zenith. But if I was anything like Pocahontas, I would have turned against everything I stood for, and joined your little rag tag band of women you parade around with. Pocahontas was captured and sold her soul, her pride and everything to join the other side to avoid conflict. She gave up her heritage, her self identity and her self worth, to be something she wasn't. I would never equate to that woman. She was weak, she was a conformist. She was broken. But, you took the racial route there. I said it before, I'm a proud Sioux. I'm proud of who and what I am. But let's look at this through Zenith Logic again. If every American Indian out there can be equated to Hiawatha or Pocahontas… can't I equate a German born person like Zenith to a goose-stepping Nazi? Hell, with his superiority complex and supposedly wanting to re-write Christianity in his own image, you could almost equate him to Hitler! He wants the whole world of wrestling, if not more, to bow to him as their lord and master. He is God's gift to wrestling? Man, let me get out my waders for that one… So for Mr. Zenith and his supporters, I leave you with one final message. Here's some half dressed, my fine ass, and my attitude problem for you.
