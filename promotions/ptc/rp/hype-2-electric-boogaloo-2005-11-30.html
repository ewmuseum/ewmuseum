Hype 2: Electric Boogaloo

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 Today our scene opened from the view of a shaky b/w camcorder that couldn't have been three feet off the ground. Perhaps, in tribute to the documentary films of their heroes, men like STEVE and Beef of Mega Job only liked to work under these settings. Presently, they were filming Craig Miles who was smoking a cigarette and playing Madden 2006 on his X-Box. "HYPE," Steve urged strongly for Miles to say something to the camera. "Seriously Craig, I mean dousing El Janito in gasoline tickles my fancies like no other," Beef grinned widely in fond remembrance, "but you hired us to help get your name out there for this Golden Turtleneck thing…" Miles exhaled a smoke ring, "Why don't you get more honest opinions? You know I'm only going to lie about the size of my penis, then again, I heard that joke's been done three times in the last hour…" "Fine," Beef huffed disappointedly before pointing to the sky, "To the Beefmobile!" "HONDA," Steve answered in a grumbling tone. With a quick cut, the camera now was filming in a different location…what looked like the backstage of a wrestling event. Workers and athletes milling about, but the one in particular being filmed didn't look to pleased. "Brandon Youngblood!" The voice of Beef irked the greatest wrestler alive today so much he winced, "If YOU could describe Craig Miles in one word, what would it be?" Youngblood stroked his chin in thought hmmmmmmmmmmmmm'ing for a great length of time, "CRAZYASSMOTHAFUCKA sounds just about right." Beef nodded, "And is it true if I rub your head, I get three wishes?" "What the fuck you say?" Youngblood said indignantly. "RUN!" Beef shouted, as the camera turned to static amongst some clobbering noises. …but fear not… For the picture clarity reappeared somewhere in the dungeons of a science lab. "DOCTOR CURIOSITY," Beef exclaimed proudly. "Jah!" The Doctor returned in kind, while dipping a combat boot into a foaming green liquid (likely dishwashing soap). "If YOU could describe Craig Miles in one word, what would it be?" The Nefarious one stood up straight, dropping the boot in all the way causing it to combust in flames, however, this did not worry him as he calmly scratched under his ear in thought. "Quantum. Ze word is quantum." "And what about his continual gift of French hookers, my good German sir?" The Doc smiled widely, "QUANTUM." FLASH! ZOOM! Yeah, now the scene is somewhere else. A veranda overlooking a golf course, where Lindsay Troy is calmly sipping on a blended strawberry margarita. The fWo, fwrestling competitor tried her best to ignore the man staring at her with drool in his mouth. "Lindsay Troy," Beef said underneath the shade of his golf visor, "If you could describe Craig Miles in one word, what would it be?" Troy's face contorted in serious thought, and for some reason she took a much larger gulp of her drink. "Eccentric." Beef nodded, paused and then said, "If I rub your nipples, is it true I get three wishes?" "Only if you're Joey." Troy replied with a smile. Beef turned to the camera, "Dude, like sick. She'd do it with that Campbell freak." "IDIOT." FLASH! ZOOM! You get the cheap parlor tricks now, I assume? "SEYMOUR ALMASY!" Beef panted while crawling to the top of a jagged cliff. There, Almasy stood proudly his sword brandished in such a way that sunlight reflected off the blade in 360 different directions. Almasy turned around with an arched eyebrow and annoyed demeanor. "I'm busy, can't you see that?" "I'm here to ask an important question, sir. If you could describe Craig Miles in one word, what would it be?" Beef coughed violently, his chest heaving. "PUSSY." Steve mumbled with a shake of the head. Almasy's answer could've defined the ages, or the odds of Lyle Lovette getting half-a-decade to slap Julia Roberts' ass as he called her his bucking bronco… "…" "Are you afraid to answer because of Craig Miles' continual cheapshots at homosexuals such as yourself?" Beef asked desperately from the ground. "I am," Almasy said, shaking his head, "busy reflecting on what this tournament means to me for a later hype piece. Now go away before I sic the Tonberries on you." Beef looked confused, "Tonberries? Is that like dingleberries for gay people?" Thusly, with a swift motion Seymour Almasy kicked Beef off the cliff from whence he came. "CANNONBALLLLLLLLLLLLL!" Steve shouted. FLASH! ZOOM! "HYPE." Steve cued. Craig Miles stood in front of black backdrop w/ the GTT5 logo in the middle, a b/w camcorder filming him. His attire was simple; white t-shirt, faded blue jeans, black leather jacket, silver sunglasses…no shoes… Somehow, the sunglasses flickered in a dimly lit room, suggesting that they reflected, or observed a light not seen with the human eye. Miles' MILESwide grin suggested the same, the confidence and COCKYness of ten years in this sport (and hobby). A lifetime of building himself up, only to be torn down harder. Nothing anyone could do, he hasn't seen already…there's nothing to fear, when everything you've feared already happened. "I could finish with a great laugh, maybe a hypothesis explaining why Brandon Youngblood will look exactly like the Legend of Pete in ten years. Honestly, that would be too easy…" The camera closed in on Miles’ face as he pulled out a Newport cigarette, lit it with a zippo and exhaled a smoke ring. The quick flicker of the lighter exposed numerous scars hidden by Miles’ ever-present 5 o’clock shadow. "There’s alot of you who think you know me, and some of you that have never heard of me. After this is all said and done…" Miles inhaled off the Newport, exhaling a waft of smoke, "I promise you’ll never seen another one like me."
