Love... Love Changes Everything.

25 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-25 Dawn’s first fingers of light touched the horizon, bathing Craig in an eerie glow as he paced the mostly deserted streets of the city. Two prostitutes called out to him, offering their services for a very reasonable price. Lost in himself, Craig barely heard them. With his hands in his pockets, his shoulders drawn up and his head bowed, he struck a most forlorn figure. As a man usually in touch with his feelings, he found his current predicament almost impossible to comprehend clearly. Unknown to himself, he had been humming the tune to ‘Bad Medicine’ continuously. He knew Nurse Wretched was the worst possible person for him to fall for, but it had happened and couldn’t be undone. Once again, he wondered what his life would be like if he hadn’t entered GTT4. At its start, his relationship with Nurse had been clear and well defined: he loved her, she despised and used him. Although not the most ideal of situations, at least he had known where he stood. As the focus shifted from Nurse’s wrestling career to Craig’s, all that had changed. She had provoked him time and time again, pushed him to the very edge of his sanity, and briefly beyond it. Had she done it on purpose? Did she mean to drive him back to the sanatorium or was she merely testing his loyalty? Whatever her motives, with each sly dig or audacious scheme, his feelings for her had hardened and been buried deeper in his soul. Now he took that hard nugget of emotion and examined it in the cold light of the November morning. On the one hand, he hated her so much he could barely contain his anger at times. On the other hand, he loved her so unconditionally that part of him urged him to forgive her most heinous of crimes… the murder of his mother. Craig turned his face upward and he bellowed his torture at the sky. In that moment, the heavens opened up, drenching him in icy rain instantly. As he ran to seek shelter in a doorway, he found himself thinking that the last thing he needed was to catch a cold so close to his match against Chris Tarrant. Even in the very depths of despair, his thoughts still returned to wrestling. Nurse had indeed trained him well. Every day she had told him that he needed to be broken, that he needed to feel the very limits of pain before he could inflict it on others. If her theory was true, then Craig would not only win the entire tournament, but be capable of single handedly taking on all the beer swilling fans present at the finals too. As he sat in the filthy doorway, waiting for the rain to stop, he forced himself to focus on the situation with Nurse. He couldn’t walk the streets forever. He needed to make a decision and move on in his life, wherever, and whoever with, it would take him. It all boiled down to two questions; could he survive without Nurse? And would he ever be able to fully break away from her if he thought he could? He sighed as he realised that leaving Nurse would never be an option. She was so much part of him that it would be like cutting off a limb or living a half life if he was without her. They were opposite sides of the same soul. She was predominantly an evil vicious person, but with a good heart buried deep that could surprise at the oddest times. He was very much a moral and kind person, but he knew that he had within him the ability to commit terrible sins. You couldn’t talk about Craig and not mention Nurse and vice versa. They balanced one another out. His discussion made, Craig left the doorway and began his journey back to the Arena. If he was going to have Nurse in his life, he would have her completely. She claimed she loved him, and Craig was eager to test out the conviction of her claim. The weak sun had risen fully by the time Craig opened the locker room door, but the room is still in darkness. Covered in years of dust and grime, the small window blocked the daylight from ever entering this bleak place. For once, Craig didn’t sigh with despair at the sight of the ugly room that was now his home as he entered, he had happier thoughts on his mind for the first time in days. In the dimness, he could just make out the figure of Nurse Wretched curled up on the room’s single cot, her chest rising and falling slowly with the steady rhythm of sleep. He sat himself on the floor beside her and listened to the comforting sound for a few minutes. Then driven by the need to touch her, he reached out and stroked the soft skin of her cheek. He knew her face was probably a mass of ugly bruising after the incident the previous day in the ring, but in his imagination, she looked her most perfect. Nurse sighed with sleepy contentment as Craig’s fingers brushed across her face. This gave him the courage to make a more daring move. His hand slowly moved down until it was cupping one perfect breast. He gasped and hunger filled him. From the moment he first met her, he had yearned to do this. Her body had always held him in thrall, and even when she was half killing him in the ring, some part of him enjoyed the closeness of that body and begged for more. With Nurse still half asleep, he moved his hand down further… It was the sound of her own screams that woke Nurse, but they were not her usual screams of frustration and anger, but ones of pleasure. Disorientated, it took her a moment to realise what was being done, and what had been done to her body. Her eyes quickly adjusted to the dim light and she saw Craig sitting there beside her, his face inches away from hers, the smile on it mirroring her own. She reached out and grabbed him, pulling him into a deep and passionate kiss. So this was how love felt? Normally a woman in control, for once she didn’t mind that Craig’s actions had robbed that from her briefly. Craig was shocked at Nurse’s reaction. Her kiss was the sweetest thing he had ever tasted, and convinced him that for the first time ever, Nurse had been telling the truth; she did love him! He clambered into the narrow bed beside her, desperate to melt into her. It was entangled like this that they both fell asleep, smiling. Nurse was the first to wake hours later. Careful not to disturb Craig, she left the bed quietly giving him just the lightest of pecks on his cheek. Nurse was still reeling from it all. She finally had Craig’s love again and it was a fabulous feeling. Her chat last night with his mother had been strange, but it appeared that all the things Mrs. Goat had told her to do under the influence of the truth serum had worked. Nurse had been dubious about some of the suggestions, but desperate to keep Craig from leaving her, or worse, hurting her, she had complied with each one to the letter. She wondered what Craig had thought of it all when he first saw on his return that morning. It had obviously made a huge impression on him. She wished she could have been awake to see the expression on his face. Craig woke to the sound of humming. Disorientated and groggy, he turned towards the sound and tried to focus his eyes blurred with sleep at the source of it. A woman sat on the bench in the middle of the room, her back towards him as she brushed her long white hair. With a start, he realised that this wasn’t Nurse Wretched. He sat bolt upright in the bed, fully awake now, as he stared in shock at the figure. With happiness coating his voice, he yelled, “Mum! You’re back, you’re not dead!” Nurse Wretched jumped at Craig’s exclamation. She quickly glanced over at the locker where she held Mrs. Goat captive and seeing that the lock was still firmly shut, sighed with relief. Craig must have been just waking from a dream. She turned to him and smiled… …Craig screamed. The woman on the bench wasn’t his mother returned from the grave, but Nurse Wretched. She had changed her appearance drastically and now looked almost exactly like his mother, except younger. Craig recalled the photograph that had pride of place on the mantle back in Goat Mansion. Nurse was the spitting image of his mother on her wedding day, down to the finest detail. He sat there silently mouth agape for a moment before throwing himself to the floor, his guts heaving as he dry retched. He had slept with her! She had turned herself into his mother and he had had sex with her! Even knowing that it was still just Nurse underneath wasn’t enough to quell the sick feeling of disgust that welled up inside him. A part of him kept screaming at him that he had fucked his own mother. At first, Nurse had stared at Craig with incomprehension etched on her face. But as he roared over and over about his mother, it all came together. She ran to the room’s sole mirror, an old and cracked thing that showed everything with a slight distortion in the middle. As Nurse stared at the image in front of her, she too began to feel ill. She did indeed look exactly like Mrs. Goat, 40 years younger and with better boobs admittedly. Quickly scanning through her memories of the previous evening spent torturing the old woman, Nurse began to see how this had come to happen. Nurse had asked Mrs. Goat to tell her what to do to make Craig love her. Of course that was a mistake. As far as Mrs. Goat was concerned, there was only ever going to be one woman in Craig’s life, and it was her. She knew her son loved her and vehemently believed that he could only ever love her. So when the truth serum took effect and Nurse pushed her for details on what Craig’s ideal woman would be like, Mrs. Goat had described herself. Nurse had stayed up late that night bleaching her dark hair until it was almost platinum white, believing that Craig preferred the lightest of hair colours. She had followed Mrs. Goat’s instructions on how to apply make-up and the clothes she should wear, right down to the sagging stockings and clumpy comfortable shoes. Nurse had known it was a strange look she was putting together, but desperate for Craig to love her, she had been willing to try anything. Craig grabbed Nurse by the collar of her austere grey dress and flung her against the mirror, breaking it in the process. “Why? Why have you done this? To mock me?” Scared, Nurse shook her head fiercely. Pointing a wavering finger at the locker that held the real Mrs. Goat, she shouted, “It’s was her idea, it’s all her fault as usual. Your goddamned meddling bitch of a mother did this to me!” Confused, Craig switched his gaze from Nurse to the locker and back again. “What the hell are you talking about?” Nurse knew it was time to come clean. Craig loved her now, so surely he could forgive the fact that she had tortured his mother, after all, he had managed to forgive her when he thought she had killed her. “Your mother’s not dead. She wasn’t in the guest house when I blew it up; she was too busy setting charges at the mansion. She’s in that locker Craig. Your mother’s alive.” Craig dropped Nurse and ran to the locker. Picking up a hammer, he broke the padlock in a mere handful of blows. He paused briefly and vowed that if this turned out to be a sick joke on Nurse’s part, he would kill her. He slowly opened the door. His mother slumped out of her small metal prison and fell to the floor. She lay there, in a crumpled ball, not moving. Craig looked pleadingly at Nurse Wretched, tears beginning to stream down his face. “Is she dead? She doesn’t look right! Help her, please Nurse, help her!” Nurse stood there, her face a blank. Mrs. Goat wasn’t breathing.
