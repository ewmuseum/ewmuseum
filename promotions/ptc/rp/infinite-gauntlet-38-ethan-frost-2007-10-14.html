Infinite Gauntlet 38 - Ethan Frost

14 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My name is Ethan Frost and in a year I will be dead. Ok so that maybe a slight exaggeration, but I’m on a dark path I know that for sure. It’s the old familiar tale of depression and anxiety that takes hold of everyone at sometime in their lives. However instead of taking a hold of me, it consumed me and pulled me in and left me the shell of a man I am now. A man who has no strength, no resources. A man who struggles to even get up in the mornings. Now don’t get me wrong, I don’t enjoy feeling like this, I would give anything to not wake up every morning and hating myself, to be able to get out of bed without it being a chore.. And when I do get up, I cut myself, the pain seeps out of me as the beautiful soft crimson flows out of me the pain disperses. It’s a coping mechanism you see, for a small moment, I feel at peace. As though there isn’t a care in the world. Then it all comes flooding back, the pain, the hurt and the realisation that I hate myself and I hate who I have become. Call me weak if you want, think whatever you want too, because I guarantee you I think the same thing about myself. Ultimately I have lost a part of myself and as such I seek this challenge, I have been told that my state of mind is little more than an illusion, something that I must over come myself an inner battle if you will. If I don’t do something positive in my life now, then I fear I will never have the chance again. Day by day Ethan Frost disappears, the person I once was is fading away, I need to find myself once more, and the person I was. The man who never backed down from a challenge. The man who continued forwards with a broken arm and continued to wrestle against orders. The man who holds a victory over the mighty Clinton Sage. The man who was happy once… All I’m looking for is one chance, one glimmer of hope to find myself. A chance to find what I’ve lost, the drive and motivation, a small slant of motivation and happiness in my empty life. Simply because it has reached the point now, whereby I wonder if there is any point to any of this really, whether life is simply something I should escape from, take the cowards way out… My name is Ethan Frost and in a year I won’t be dead… but I wish I was…
