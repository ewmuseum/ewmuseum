The Capture

2 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-02 [Inhale. Exhale.] [Inhale. Exhale.] [Inhale. Exhale.] …and right now, the hardest thing for me to do is to open my eyes. A very simple motion, opening and closing one’s eyes; I had been doing it for twenty-two years prior to this point. Yet, tonight, in this instance my eyelids felt heavier than anytime before. I tried to bring my arms to my face, but as I tried I felt that my wrists have been tightly restrained. What the hell…is…going on? Again, I struggled with the task of prying my sight free, but it felt like something was actually holding them closed. My pulse began to quicken, as I tried to free my wrists. The situation was beginning to take hold of me; the sweat began to trail down the right side of my face. Then came the anxiety, sweeping over me and fueling my paranoia. Then, I remembered the stories some girls would tell about some of those sick, wrestler-dicks just trying to prove a point. Why was this happening? What did I do? Wasn’t my time in the madhouse before enough? Or, is it because I’m the weakest link or something? The quickest route to the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Championship was right before my eyes. But I thought I was going to lose, to go out in round 1 of the tournament. I got through. I also thought I was going to lose to the gothic mind-f**k that is Rzarector in round 2. I got through. I should be confident about my round three match with Fred Grest. I have the round 1 and the round 2 victories to back up the self-confidence I should be attaining. But, for some unknown reason, I’m not attaining it. For some reason, I still feel the worry that Fred Grest is going to beat me like I thought Lex Le Magnifique would. It’s like I want to feel confident, but I can’t. It’s like guilt. You try and shrug it off but it just keeps on coming back to you like the common cold even. [Inhale. Exhale. Inhale.] [Exhale. Inhale. Exhale.] [Inhale. Exhale. Inhale.] Soon, I began losing control of my body. Soon, the shakes became rapt within my total person, coaxing shallow, hurried breaths from my sore lungs. This can’t be happening…This isn’t real. This can’t be real. In Hollywood, this is the part where the damsel would be saved from her dire straits. Or even, the damsel would get to find some umph, some resolve to “Matrix-jump” or something visually spectacular to free herself and escape off into the night. Though this…this was real life, lacking in that element of special effects and I’m petrified. “Jack.” My thoughts screamed “Jack”, as I began frantically searching with my dreary eyes and bound head – looking, hoping he was here to save me, like he saved me from my father’s tyrannical rule. I churned my wrists, frantically, trying to grind the ends of whatever that bound my wrists against the back of the chair I was seated. As my hands began to numb, there was no give in the ties that bind me. “Jack, I’m so sorry.” Next came the hopelessness. The fear that I was alone and he was still gone…the suspicion that maybe this wasn’t a work at all. That maybe this isn’t affiliated with the business at all. The strenuous fear that I’ve been stalked and placed in a situation beyond my control…that I could actually end up like one of those tragic cases on the six o’ clock news, pursued by some mentally-disturbed freak for his sick, sexual fantasy. I’m not supposed to end this way, the son of a child-beater and an overall let down of a father? Or maybe…that’s what this is all about? I tried to open my mouth, to scream for help. Hell, just to scream at anybody. I wanted to yell and scream at Fred Grest’s ears, point blank, so he’d be able to digest and understand the level of hate I have for his little wrestling ability. I wanted to yell for him to come back, to let me beat down on him. I wanted to scream at that idiot and try to snatch his little, green eyes out of his skull. I wanted to wrench and yell at my captor, even if it would have no bearing. Then, I think that just a month ago I was ok, running my shop and not plodding around thinking about Fred Grest. I was fine, and I should’ve stuck with my first thought to stay gone after his first triumph with Moraless. By this time, my struggles came few and far between. My arms and legs were pumping battery acid from trying to grind away at the tightly-wound strand around my wrists and ankles, my brain was woozy and outnumbered by my thoughts, and my eyes were still nowhere close to being open…or even functioning. [shhhhhhhhhhh….] The watery sound came from somewhere behind me. I became very still, trying to stifle any sound as the sound of water splashed behind me, then to the right of me, in front of me and finally on to my other side. What was next, was the only question I had now… . . . “Our father which art in heaven, hallowed by thy name.” His scratched, almost garbled, southern tones sent my heart racing. Desperately, I tried with every ounce of me to open my eyelids. Please…I pleaded with my own body, who seemed to rebel against me at this perilous time of my life. This can’t be happening. Please God…I began to whimper against my mind’s own will. You never let your enemies see you sweat. One true statement of Chris rang in my head, as all of my energy became devoted to my eyes. God, I tried as I have never tried at anything before in life. Jack, I need you…now, it’s I who is sorry. ‘Thy kingdom come, thy will be done, in earth, as it is in heaven.’ His throaty, grumbling rendition of Prayer sent chills the length of my spine. I hated this dreadful feeling, and if I were to make it out of this predicament…Well, if I made it I don’t know what I would do, short of moving far away from here. Again, I tried with a fervor never needed before to pry my pupils free of skin…and finally, it happened. My eyes were opened, at least they felt open. My vision was so blurred, and shaky that I couldn’t make out my own body which, I knew, had been restrained. I would’ve brought my hands to my eyes, if I could. So I just shut my eyes as tight as humanly possible, squeezing fluid from my tear ducts which started to stream down my hot cheek Then, I heard that raspy, troubled laugh of the beast before me and just to be sure, I said a prayer of my own… ‘Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. Do you know what this is, child?’ I didn’t answer…I wouldn’t give the bastard the satisfaction. I just concentrated on my sight, which I desperately needed in this moment in time. I opened my eyes, possibly in vain, but the slight silhouette of various objects before me gave me slight hope. “It is known as the Lord’s Prayer. THY WILL BE DONE! AS IT IS IN HEAVEN! The will of our savior, child. Our savior who is ANGRY at the sorry state of those created in his likeness! Our FATHER who has given his life, who has suffered through persecution…and for what? The hookers, the scum-sacks, the vain selling pawning their, so-called, ‘perfect’ images off on the simple masses? The next wonder weight loss drug?” A heavy, labored sigh clutches at my heart and stomach. His madness, almost, heaving me to the point to nausea. I closed my eyes tighter still, causing more salt to follow suit. Then…I opened them and at once, I was inside our living room. Jack’s living room, no matter how hazy and convulsed, there was no way I’d forget those damned golden, leather straps that I used to know so well. From the left side of the large living quarter, everything was in shambles. The new trophy case and replica belts were trashed and vandalized with endearing terms like, “Sinner”, “Pig” and the wildly popular ‘splashes of blood’. In front of me, Jack’s expensive Plasma Screen was broken with a huge crack running the distance of the face and the glass-topped custom coffee table broken and strewn about in a heap. All around me, everything he worked and I loved and lived with –though material- had been demolished. The large, leather couch was sliced and damaged beyond repair. Then, to top things off…the place smelled of gasoline. Then, my weary gaze went over to the clearing near the stairs where the living room began and I caught the shadowy sight of a massive figure standing near the hallway closet…and for the first time, my fear had a figurative, living and breathing presence. My eyes struggled to get a fix, due to the lack of light throughout the loft. The only light that shined was that of the quarter moon that funneled through the circular skylight that landed a mere couple of inches before my bare, bound feet. “So, how does it feel?” He paused, waiting from some sort of response. “How does it feel to know your money can’t wipe away the sinful stench of you? How does it feel to know that your stardom has no bearing on your soul?? How does it feel…to be lost, child?” Then, he started toward me. The moonlight gleamed on the bottom gold front tooth, he showed with his blood-curdling grin. I tried to scoot myself away, but to no avail – he was too fast for me. He yanked my chair under the skylight, and I saw his face. What little that was protruding…he wore a black half-mask of some sort that came to an end at the corners of his thin lips. A scar dotted the butt of his unshaven, Caucasian chin. He looked like some twisted, distorted version of a priest with his black coveralls and white collar, but that wasn’t the weirdest part. That belonged to his eyes, which were covered by the black sheen of his mask…but I could still see them. They looked like black pools of hopelessness. Empty and devoid of anything good and black like charred flesh. I’ve seen eyes like that before, painful and disheartening. They reminded me of Jack’s sad eyes… [Smack!] And with one fall out of the side of his bed, Odium opened his eyes to see himself in his boxer shorts only, wrapped up on the floor inside his quilt. He looked up into the night sky out of his bedroom window, and sighed. “Whoa…”
