Believe the Hype

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 -scene opens up to a man walking down the streets of his hometown of Montreal, Quebec, it is snowing, the man is wearing a black coat that comes to his knees, an armani suit underneath, and a smirk on his face, this name is none other than, "The Sensational" Shayne Stylez, and he has some things to say- Shayne: Hello everyone in the Gtt5 tournament, my name is Shayne Stylez. And I’m here to tell each and every one of you, that I’m better than you. Not just a better person, but a better wrestler. You don’t go thru the many years of training, the hard times of getting your ass kicked by the likes of Chris Benoit, or Bret Hart to not become as great as I am, to become…Sensational. This is my first year in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, and well, quite frankly, I’m not sure exactly what to expect, but I can tell you what each and every one of the competitors should expect from me, and that’s action. I’m not the winner of the 1999 King of Xtreme Tournament for nothing. I didn’t win the 2003 King of UltraViolence Tournament by doing nothing. Shayne: Hell, when I was in LSCW, I made it to the finals to crown a new LSCW Pure Wrestling Champion. Weeks later, the man who beat me in the finals of the Pure Tournament, Massacre, teamed with me to make it to the finals to crown new LSCW Tag Team Champions. So as you can see, there is absolutely no reason as to why I shouldn’t win this whole tournament, but I’ll give everyone credit, you all, as well as myself were accepted into this tournament, so…I’m guessing it’s not a total loss. -Shayne’s cell phone rings, he looks at the caller ID and another smirk comes across his face as he answers it- Shayne: Hey, babe. Voice: Hey Shayne, your flight go okay? Shayne: Yeah, I just got in, I’m heading to the event now. Voice: Good. I don’t think Dorian is going to be too pleased that you signed up for this thing, he really wants you to be worrying about going after the CWA Tag Titles. Shayne: Don’t worry that pretty little head of yours, LC, Scorner and I will win them, don’t worry, besides, I’ll be back in time for Global Conflict. LC: Well, you had better be, I miss you already. Shayne: I miss you too, hun. I’ve just got some paperwork to sign, and then I’ll be back in town by Sunday. LC: Okay, I’ll be waiting, Mountain keeps giving me the "eye" thing… Shayne: What eye thing? LC: Y’know that scary thing he does from behind the mask…I feel like he’s undressing me with them. Shayne: Don’t worry about it, he can’t think for himself. He’s like the retarded third grader who writes all of Jimmy Riley’s material. LC-laughs-: That’s good stuff right there. I still miss you. Shayne: Babe, I miss you more than Mountain misses the point. LC-laughs again-: You probably shouldn’t say that to his face…we don’t want him getting upset. Shayne: The guy can’t think for himself. I wonder if he’s anything like the guy I face in the first round of this tournament. LC: What’s his name? Shayne: Ace Johnson. You heard of him? LC: Nope, you? Shayne: Nope…I’ll see what I can look up, I gotta go. LC: Okay, I love you. Shayne: I love you too. LC: Bye. Shayne: Bye. -Shayne hangs up his cell phone as the limousine pulls up outside the building the Gtt5 event is being held, the driver gets out and then goes and lets Shayne out as the scene slowly fades to black, the scene cuts back in and we see Shayne Stylez walking out of the building the Gtt5 event is being held, he walks to his limousine and nods to the driver, the driver opens the door as Shayne goes to get in, the camera pans inside the limousine and a smirk comes across Shayne’s face- Shayne: Well, it’s settled, the paperwork has been signed, and it’s official. Shayne Stylez is in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, and he will win the whole damn thing, because that’s how I roll…now if you’ll excuse me, I’ve got a Pay Per View to get to, Global Conflict here I come. -Shayne’s cell phone rings, he looks at the caller ID and smirks as he goes to answer it- Shayne: Hey LC. LC: Hey, everything taken care of? Shayne: Yeah, it’s all finalized. I’m on my way now back to the airport, I’ll be in tomorrow morning, maybe even tonight. LC: Good…hey, you got a package…I don’t know who it’s from, it doesn’t say. Shayne: Leave it be, I’ll check it out when I get back in. LC: Alright, hurry home. Shayne: I can’t guarantee anything, but I’m hoping to be in tonight. LC: Okay, I’ll see you tonight…I love you. Shayne: Love you too. LC: Bye. Shayne: Bye. -the scene focuses on Shayne Stylez as he hangs up his cell phone and smirks before the scene slowly fades to black-
