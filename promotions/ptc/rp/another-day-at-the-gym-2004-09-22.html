Another Day at the Gym

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 [The GTT logo explodes across the screen to a Gold’s Gym, 1 W Carrillo Street downtown, Santa Barbara, CA 93101. It’s mid afternoon on a Saturday and the gym is packed as people dedicated to perfecting they bodies get in what they can’t do during the week days, cameras show the “Iron People”, men and a couple of women hitting the weights either trying to bulk up, or tone their muscles. The treadmills are filled with the “Hamster People” running at selected speeds to both tone up, and send the unwanted pounds. Then there are the “Simon Says People” who to the beat of the music, and the instructions of a pi-piper Jazzercise, and Tae Bo into a sweat. All people from all walks of life, those who do it for health, those who do it to look attractive, those who do it to build strength, those who do it to get a look at other attractive people, and possibly try to hook up. Very little conversation goes on in a place like this, except for the small convos here and there, the grunts, and the yelling of the drill sergeant aerobics instructing over the loud techno type music. Today’s is packed with regulars, veterans to the gym who have gone for year. Only a sprinkle of new comers can be see here and there, and it’s the new comers that can be caught pausing to the sight which took the vets a couple of months themselves to get use to, that of a 7 foot female red head by the weight lifting section executing squats, while her purple haired companion decked out in a pink Nike sports bra, and tight pink matching spandex shorts counts.] Free Fall’s voice – 6…7…8…come on you can do it…push. [Cameras show Caitlin in a red Nike Sports bra, with a tight red matching spandex shorts on. Her knees are wrapped as she stands barefooted going down in a squat position with a bar full or weights across her shoulders, the total weight is 420 lbs even, and this is her fourth set. Free Fall can be seen standing off to the side counting as Caitlin going down tries to hold her form while pushing the inhuman amount of weight up with all her strength. Other bodybuilders save for a few who stop to watch in amazement toil away at their own tasks having knows Caitlin for a couple of years, one of the few places that Caitlin liked because very few eyes are on her, where Amazons walk freely.] Free Fall – 9…one more…one more…go strong! [Caitlin breathes hard taking in breath as she drops down for the 10th time, definition in her legs can be see popping from her calves, and thighs as they tremble to support the tremendous weight, while sweat drips all over Fairchild’s olive skin body causing her to glisten and drenching her gear; her long red hair frizzed and soaked from the massive amount of water it has accumulated. Caitlin screams as she pushes with every ounce of her strength to elevate the bar of weight. They clang as they go higher and higher until she’s finally back up to her vertical based. Caitlin wearily backs up placing the massive amount of weights on the rack behind her. As she gets out from underneath them to rest, she stretches her defined long legs getting the blood to circulate freely again.] Caitlin Fairchild – How much was that? Free Fall – Four sets. 40 reps. Not bad at all. Caitlin Fairchild –Next time I’ll see if I can hit 450. Free Fall – Damn girl! You went from 400 to 420, and you barely did that! Caitlin Fairchild – 450 is my mission. Free Fall – You better ease up, men don’t like rock hard legs ya know…you let some softness be there for them to feel. Caitlin Fairchild – Since when do I care what men want? Free Fall – Aight She-Hulk, come on …time to do some Sevens. [Caitlin nods as she follows Free Fall the a bar bell racked with two huge weights totaling a 110lbs. Caitlin runs her hand through her wet hair slicking it back, then stoops down grabbing weights readying for a curling position, she then raises to an upright position lifting the bar to her pelvis area. Fairchild raises the bar to her chest, and then slowly lowers the bar till her arm is into an “L” shaped position; Caitlin reps out seven straight reps, then reverse it where she lowers the bar to her pelvis position, and pulls it up into an “L” position, she reps out seven of these as well. Fairchild then executes seven slow full curls, which reveal the cuts of her slim shredded forearms, biceps, and traps.] Free Fall – So…dis Horazon kid, what do you know about him? Caitlin Fairchild – Not too much…(grunts.) He use to wrestle for the FAW, but apparently (grunts.) it went under, all the roster was disbanded, there’s not even a website …(grunts.) Apparently he’s a free agent…and a cruiserweight style wrestler…hasn’t been in action for quite awhile, so I suppose he’s got some ring rust to him, but guys like him are dangerous in the ring, then get that…come back career burn, and come looking to prove something. Free Fall – So what’s the plan…one more. [Caitlin grits her teeth as she elevates the weight one more time to her chest forcing her arms to failure. Upon completing her final rep she lowers the bar slowly back to her pelvis, and then kneels down slightly dropping it to the mat. Caitlin steps back wiping her forehead of sweat, and stands there breathing hard with her hands at her hips before looking at her manager and friend.] Caitlin Fairchild – Well considering he’s a highflyer/luchador…I have a feeling I’m going to be a LOT stronger than him, he’s probably going to try to chop me down with quick highflying maneuvers which means I’m going to have to just overpower him…lots of clotheslines, strikes, and quick hard counters to keep them off him vertical base, and because most Luchadores are known for slipping out of big power moves and countering I’m going to have to make sure I either lock onto him, or follow up with a faster counter of my own, more effectively strap on a submission maneuver. I still would like to more about this guy, but then again that’s when being a vet comes in…sometimes you have to go into a fight with just your senses and the ability to be ready for anything. One thing I know I’m not gonna do is hit him with a G-Spot. Free Fall – Why not? Caitlin Fairchild – Every time I get into a match with a cruiserweight and use that over the shoulder ¾ neck breaker, the person ends up swinging in front of me, and Tornado DDTing me into the mat. With the Burning Spike I’ll have less chance of being countered. Free Fall – Leg Press time. [Caitlin walks over to where she places her slip on Nike workout shoes, and slips them on before heading to the leg press. On the machine on each side are 6 sets of 45 pound weights totaling 540 lbs. Caitlin begins to grab more 45 pound weights off the rack and add them on as small crowd begins to form around the leg press, the whispering intensifies for when Caitlin is done she’s racked a full set of twenty-two 45 pound weights on EACH side of the leg press machine…total weight is 990 lbs. Caitlin then rewraps her knee caps tightly with knee support wraps, and takes a seat on the leg press positioning her legs. She takes a deep breath before pushing the unbelievable amount of weight up, and releasing the locking mechanism. Fairchild lowers the weight to her chest, and then braces herself as she pushes the weight back up remembering to keep her legs slightly bent. Free Fall watches keeping count as her wrestler reps out 4 times; each time her legs trembling under the pressure but holding firm.] Free Fall – Come on…that’s five…we’re going to eight…six…COME ON Caitlin, this is nothing for you! [Caitlin screams as she holds the side of her legs for support as she elevated the near thousand pounds of weight up again for the seventh time. Caitlin brings the weight down to her chest, and slowly pushes it upwards as her legs begin to shake violently as she pushed it up for the 8th and final time.] Free Fall – Come on Caitlin…one more! ONE MORE!! [Caitlin lets out a final scream pushing all the air out of her chest as she finally gets the weight up into the stratosphere. She pulls of the locking mechanism, and lowers the weight until it’s secure, then drops her long legs down out of exhaustion. Natives to the country clap and cheer impressed with the performance of the show of strength and power Caitlin as portrayed. Caitlin gives a faint wave and smile as the crowd disperses talking amongst themselves while Free Fall stands there smiling with pride.] Free Fall (Smiles.)- Show off. Caitlin Fairchild (Smiles back panting.) - I wish… Free Fall – (With a nervous look.) So…when are you gonna see him? Caitlin Fairchild – Charon comes back to the VSE on the 27th of next week. Free Fall – No…I mean…him. Caitlin Fairchild – Him who… [Caitlin stopped to think, and then her face turned into a frown as she looked at Roxy, she slowly pulled herself up towering over her 5’4” friend.] Caitlin Fairchild – Never…and I can’t believe you’re bringing this up here. Free Fall – I brought it up here, because I knew you’d be CIVIL here. Caitlin Fairchild – Like I said…NEVER. Free Fall – Caitlin he’s your fath… Caitlin Fairchild (Grits her teeth trying to keep her tone down.) - He is NOT my father…father’s don’t abandon their daughters when their babies…I want NO PART of him. I know my older sister…that’s good enough for me. For 19 years of my life he’s hurt me…now that I’m over that I’m not going to allow him to do it again. [There was a silence between the two as they looked around pretending not to be arguing. A month before all of this when Caitlin thought she was an only adoptive child, who was abandoned by a father, who apparently blamed her for the death of her mother during child birth, she has come to learn that she has an older sister, in the same profession as she is, and her father lived in a ranch only a state away from her. Once biologically alone in this world Caitlin is no longer alone, but a long lost sister she can handle…she didn’t ask to be born, and the burden of feeling responsible for the death of her mother was burden enough…it took years of loving care and attention from people who choose to take of the responsibility of being her parents for her to get over the pain of being thrown away and unwanted, close suicide, and near depression. In her mind, Scott and Emily Banner were her parents, not some man who casually spilled a few drops of sperm doing some pleasure and just walked away. Caitlin signed as she looked a Roxy pretending to look away with a hurt look on her face, she didn’t deserve to get her head chewed off, it wasn’t her fault, not to mention she wasn’t too comfy with this family reunion business in the first place, but she was doing her duty as a good friend, thinking it was time Caitlin got some face to face answers, the only problem was Caitlin wasn’t ready for that. Fairchild sighed as she nudged her friend’s shoulder.] Caitlin Fairchild – Look…I’m sorry…it’s just that I know what I’m gonna want to do if I see him standing before me, and before I let it come to that I’d rather not see him…jeez it’s hard enough to even process me having an older sister…I just need some more time. Free Fall (Gives her a fake hurt look.) – You hurt my feelings yo… Caitlin Fairchild – I’m sorry…I really am…we still cool? Free Fall – (Nods with a smirk.) Yeah, we cool. Caitlin Fairchild – (Look around.) Well I’m done for the day…so wanna do something? Beach? Free Fall (Gives her a sly smile.) – Low-Riders… Caitlin Fairchild – (Gives her a serious look.) Roxy no…. Free Fall – Come on!! I saw this hot 78” Caddy down in Crenshaw, it’s purple with silver spoke rims! Pimp status baby! Caitlin Fairchild – You just BOUGHT a Mini, and supped it up! Free Fall – Yeah, but that’s so Italian Job! Caitlin Fairchild – You’re Italian! Free Fall – Yeah from Compton! And you’re Half Brazilian and half Greek, whatcha tryin ta say? Caitlin Fairchild – I’ll take you to Westside Customs, we’ll drop the Mini, get you the turbo you wanted, and dual exhaust it, but we’re not buying another car, we can barely fit the two we have in the drive way already! Free Fall – That’s because SOMEONE had ta buy a damn Humvee, when she KNEW she could also fit in an Excursion! Caitlin Fairchild – (Points to Free Fall.) Don’t….don’t you dare talk about my Mona like that… Free Fall – I’ll TALK about Mona all I want! I was the one who picked out the colors! [The GTT logo explodes across the screen as the two friends continue to disagree as people walked by who knew them shook their head smiling at the cute scene, another typical day at the gym as the scene fades out all together.]
