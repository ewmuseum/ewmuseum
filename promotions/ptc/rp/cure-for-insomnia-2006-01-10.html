Cure For Insomnia

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-10 Mal Somers didn’t often lay on the hard wooden floor of his apartment, but this was no ordinary night. It was the night before his match versus LoC’s Arch Angel, and he was finding it hard to sleep. He had tried everything, counting sheep, counting to one hundred and even replaying Ember, Jorge Samuelsson or Jonathan Winters matches in his mind, but nothing had worked. So he thought he would try something new, lying on the floor. He lay on his stomach for a while, and then his side. eventually, he came to rest on his back, trying to fall asleep. Perhaps that was the problem, he was thinking too much. Don't you hate it when you try and will yourself to fall asleep? It never works, it has to come naturally. Otherwise, you lie there, thinking that you have to get out of bed in only five hours…three hours…one hour, and then it's too late - it's time to wake up. Funnily enough, that's usually when you are ready to fall asleep. He stared up at the door frame for a while, before closing his eyes. Finally, they began to feel heavy. That’s better, Somers thought to himself. His thoughts finally began to slow down and become easier to process, rather than the mad collage of images and intersecting thoughts that often assailed his mind during his waking hours. In fact his thoughts slowed down, to one sole thought alone. Arch Angel Mal realised that his inability to sleep was due to a fear of the unknown. Who was this Arch Angel guy? Things were different in the World Wrestling Alliance, where if Somers hadn’t already wrestled a guy, he would have been able to visually digest hours of footage in the WWA library. Somers prided himself on being able to study his opponent, and in this instance he was not afforded that opportunity. In round 3 of GTT5, Mal Somers would be stepping into the unknown, against an opponent he had never faced, let alone seen before. And he was more excited about this than he had been for a long, long time. He would have to think on his feet for the first time in what seemed like an eternity, and he was relishing the thought. He had heard a lot of impressive things about Arch Angel, and the calibre of the opponents he had defeated showed that he would be a force to be reckoned with. That excited Somers even more. Suddenly Mal’s eyes opened and he stared up above him once again. Though it was dark, he focused on the door frame that lay above him, and eventually he was able to discern it in the darkness. Somers lived in a decades old apartment which was characterised by some very detailed cornices and architraves. Somers smiled as the features in the archway above him became easier to see. Above the door sat an engraved angel, looking down at him. An Arch Angel. Somers smiled and finally fell into a deep sleep on the cold, hard floor.
