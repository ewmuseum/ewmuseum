It's all a ruse to, uh, throw off opponents, you see. Q woof: What?! A more scurrilous charge I've never heard! Q woof: You're a far too forgiving person. I'm sorry, really, it's just that I'm not really myself right now, you know? Q woof: Right, jetlag. But I think I'm feeling better now so let's get on with this interview. I promise I'll be civil. Q-- woof: Ah, the presnt, that's my favorite time period. Oh, sorry I inurrupted. Q woof: PTC? What the question was, no one would ever know. This is an interesting theory, unfortunately, it is really quite stupid. The real. answer to the question of Life, the Universe, and Everything was discovered by a young man named Floyd Flannigan. Mr. Flannigan lives, or lived, rather, in a small rural community in Nebraska. He worked as a cashier in the local Wal-Mart (which supported the towns entire economy. When Sammy and the boys packed up and left two months ago the town collapsed almost instantly, and I don't just mean in she sense of an economic collapse. We're talkin' full on total collapse here, folks, in 36 point bolded capital letters. Buildings fell down, dogs died, toilets stopped working, and, in the end, the whole fucker folded in on itself and left what is now known as the Nebraskan Black Hole. It's quite the tourist attraction.) One evening, Mr. Flannigan was watching an Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling broadcast, as he often did, being that he was quite an adamant fan of the company and their broadcasts. During this particular broadcast, the wrestler 'woof woof' (real name: woof J. woof) had a match against another wrestler of little to no consequence. And as he sat watching this match, Floyd Flannigan suddenly understood. It all fell into place and, finally, made complete sense. It all seemed to click into place. The answer to the question of Life, the Universe, and Everything was right there, in fifty foot high neon pink letters, flashing in his brain. Unfortunately, before he could write it down or tell anybody, his television, in a freak occurance involving some rather odd and out of the ordinary televison eave trajectories, suddenly changed channels. What it changed to was a GW (another professional wrestling organization) broadcast.
