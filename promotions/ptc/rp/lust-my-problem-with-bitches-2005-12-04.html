Luckily for her, they caught her. They didn’t get a single "thank you" before she swiped this one guy’s metal staff and began to twirl it. Most fools lead with the weapon instead of using it as an extension of their body and despite her vast training, she was no different. She started towards me while twirling the weapon. Mesuinu let loose a string of strikes that had be backed up as far as I could safely go. I tried to avoid most of them, but I had to block some and those fuckers stung like a bitch. Her final strike was a jumping overhead attack and I rolled out of the way with barely a moment to spare. That one would have parted my head let alone my hair. I got to my feet and noticed a goon nearby had my katana in his grasp. I struck him in the knee which caused him to fall forward and I swiped my katana while shoving him face first to the floor. Just in the nick of time too, because I had to use it to block a series of strikes from Mesuinu and her staff. We stood there in a battle of wills with her pushing down with her staff and me trying to fend her off with my still covered katana. We stared deep into one each other’s eyes and I wondered, if she saw her life flashing before hers. I directed her force to my right and butted her in the nose with the end of the sheath. My left hand kept the sheath in place near her face, as my right pulled back drawing the sword. As soon as the point escaped its covering, I moved the sheath and drove the blade through her throat. I watched her fall to the ground in a heap, as I released the handle. Her eyes were wide and blood flowed from her like a raging river. She fought for a few seconds, hoping to catch a breath and live one second longer. She never could catch one. The crowd around us watched in shock at what had just happened. I walked right through them all after reclaiming my weapon and took the detonator from her goon, but not without killing him first. That "just following orders" crap doesn’t fly with me. Everyone has a conscience. He didn’t listen to his. Not a single one of the remaining goons stood in my path, as I tried to leave. The fear and awe on their faces said it all. They have seen her kill so many people in so many ways. She was their unrivaled leader. They thought she was unbeatable. Well, it is time for a reality check. No one is unbeatable. They just haven’t found their match. Mesuinu Reiketsu found that out the hard way and in a short time, so will Eva VanLorne and the others in GTT5. You match is out there and for most, his name is Xin Xin Xiong.
