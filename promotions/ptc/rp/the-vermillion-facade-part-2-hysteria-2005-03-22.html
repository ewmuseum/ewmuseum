Click... Click... The sound of footsteps could be heard echoing across the room. As they grew closer and closer, a man... dressed in a white Under Armor shirt and a pair of black workout pants stood motionless. He looked at himself in the mirror accusingly, his cadet blue eyes peered into the reflectant surface unafraid of what was to come from behind him. For he knew the truth, he knew what was lurking in the darkness... “Something evil this way comes.” He said aloud as he continued to look into the mirror, and saw no reflection. Behind him, the silhouette of a man stepped into the doorway leading into the room. The man with no reflection took a gulp as the silhouette stepped into the sunlight beaming down onto the practice mats from the windows above. He wore a pair of black jeans and a black hooded sweatshirt. The hood was up and covered his face from the light, but the man with no reflection could feel his eyes peer into his back from underneath the darkness. The hooded man reached his arms out and cracked his knuckles while perspiration dripped down the forehead of his apparent adversary. The man with no reflection kept his back to the hooded man while he continued to walk towards him, with his New Balance shoes making zipping noises across the practice mats. “Why must you be so bothersome Ater?” The reflectionless man spoke to his adversary, whose melodious march the came to a halt, “It must be so hard for you to accept defeat by my hands.” A chuckle escaped out from under Ater’s hood... “Only in your wildest dreams could you claim victory over the likes of me. Ater pointed towards the GW World Heavyweight Championship on the table across the room, to which Fama’s eyes glanced over. “That wasn’t you dear Fama... that was US.” Fama turned around slowly to look at his adversary without the use of a mirror.
