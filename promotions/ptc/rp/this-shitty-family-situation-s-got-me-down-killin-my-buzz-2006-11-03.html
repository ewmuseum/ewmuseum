This Shitty Family Situation's Got Me Down, Killin' My Buzz.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-03 The scene fades in to British icon Judi Dench, seated in an expensive brown leather chair adjacent to a cozy fireplace. She gives the camera a warm smile. "Hello, I’m Judi Dench," she begins (unnecessarily, given the Dench Nation’s legions and legions of fans watching from home), "and I’m here today to bring you up to speed on events that recently occurred in the life of PRIME almost-superstar Chris ‘Nova’ Vega." She looks down below the camera shot for a moment, and her eyes widen before darting off-camera and no doubt shooting hot knives of elderly rage into some poor bastard. "Is…is there a problem, Ms. Dench?" That’d be the guy. "There’s only one line on this card!" she exclaims as though she were revealing the secrets of Area 51, "A single sentence!" "That’s right," comes the reply of the director from off-screen, "that’s, uh, all we need you to say." "I flew out here from Ibiza," Dame Dench growls, "where I spent six days in a palace of bubbles rolling on ecstasy while young island boys ate sliced pineapple and whipped cream off of my chest! I was in the throes of physical and spiritual bliss, communing with the soul of the planet and every living thing on it…and you asked me to fly out to…where the fuck are we?!" "We’re in Detroit, Michigan," the microphone guy offers helpfully. "Detroit Michigan!!" Dench repeats, so infuriated that the varicose veins on her thighs are probably dilating. I say probably because I’m too scared to check. "Actually, we’re in Dearborn," the microphone guy remembers, "it’s a smaller town outside Detroit." "I DON’T CARE!" Dench howls, slamming her fist down on the armrest of the chair, "you!" She points, and the camera swivels around at a man standing awkwardly in the crowd of workers, then back to Jude. "You asked me out here as a favor! A favor! Well, see if you get another one, you…you…twit! You can get Maggie Smith to drop one-liners on your…wrestling intros!" "We actually tried to get Ms. Smith first," the director confesses, "but her rate was too high." Almost purple in the face, Dame Dench sits back and begins rubbing her hands against her temples. "Can anyone scare up a glass of gin?" "Later," the director replies, "for now, just say the line, and we’ll pay you, and then you can grab some sauce and head back to your creepy sex party that I never want to hear about again. Deal?" Dench nods and expertly masks her misery with a warm, grandmotherly smile. "We’re rolling in five…four…three…two…" The Dame takes a breath, and delivers her assignment like a pro. "Nova and his ex-wife are stuck in the bathroom." "Beautiful! That’s a wrap!" … The quest continues. ------*~*~*------ The House Formerly Known As Vega, Directly After My Round One Roleplay… "Ariel, please. I need a cigarette." Nova is seated on the throne in the first-floor one-and-a-half bath, running a hand through his hair and staring up at his ex-wife with the fiercest puppy-dog eyes in the history of history, so fierce they’ve melted the bowels of lesser women in the past. Or that’s what I’ve heard. "Hell no!" Ariel replies, throwing her arms up in frustration, "we need to figure out how to get out of this bathroom, Chris! And there aren’t even windows in here!" "But I reeeeeeeaaaaaallllyyy need one," Nova argues, his efforts to torch tasty tobacco indefatigable. "It’s been forever!" "You had one right before we got ourselves locked in here," Ariel counters, "you need to cut that shit out, anyway." "You ain’t the boss of me," Nova says indifferently, reverting without shame to childish baiting. "Thank God for that," Ariel fires back as she searches around the room for tools of any kind, and Nova mouths ouch as she continues talking, "there’s gotta be something we can use to get the door open." "I could always bust the shit down," Nova offers. "No you couldn’t," Ariel says with authority, "one, we don’t know if our daughter is sitting outside this door right now, and two, I just had this door installed and it cost a lot of money." Nova rolls his eyes. "Yeah, really great find there, Ariel. Did they advertise the, uh, ‘Privacy-Enabled Door Lock?’" "Har, har," Ariel no-sells, "well, you know, between raising a child and holding down a spot at one of Detroit’s finer law firms, I don’t always have the time for home repair." "Damn," Nova winces, "I play around and you’re breaking out the big guns. Jesus." Ariel’s eyes soften. "I’m sorry, Chris. I’m just stressed out, and I don’t want to be stuck in here right now. No offense." "None taken," Nova says dismissively, knowing that he’s been ripping heinous ass the whole time and his ex-wife is probably dying right now as she bastes in the funk, "but, babe, no lie, I gots to have a smoke." "No, it won’t ventilate!" Ariel protests as Nova pulls out his pack. He stares down at the box of Camel Lights in his hand, feeling their pull on him. Somewhere in the distance, Ariel is still yelling, but her words are muted, unimportant. The cigs, baby. The pack in his hand suddenly morphs into his power animal, a blue Amazonian dart frog. "Whassup, Playa?" the frog says, standing up in Nova’s hand and snapping his fingers at the Rising Star, "you tryin’ to get yo’ nic on?" Nova nods, transfixed by the tiny reptile. "Well, burn one down, my hot-blooded homedog," the frog reassures him, "this country was built on tobacco. Non-smoking sections are for non-American cheeseball asshats. Trust me!" "You have a point," Nova replies with a nod. "I do?" Ariel says, a shocked expression on her face. Nova looks down in his hand, and his power animal is gone, replaced by the inanimate cigarettes. He begins to shuffle one out. "My addiction calls m-HEY!!" Without warning, Ariel leans forward and snatches the pack out of Nova’s hands before turning and sliding it under the crack of the door. "There! Enough about the cigarettes!" "Well that’s really great, Ariel," Nova says, arms folded across his chest, "just go on and toss those cancer sticks out into the hallway for our daughter to find. Hey, you wanna slip her my lighter, too? I have some weed as well, ya know, in case she wants to get her chronic on while we deal with this mess." "All the more reason for you to find us a way out of here then, hmm?" Ariel replies, her hands on her hips. Nova opens his mouth to reply, but his jaw just hangs there as the synapses in his brain desperately seek out a witty reply. Outside the house… "Larry! HEY, LARRY! Jesus, someone get Larry for me, huh?" A paunchy older man stands on the sidewalk outside Ariel’s house, shouting over the noise of a road construction truck. "YEAH, GET HIM FOR…YEAH! LARRY! IS THIS THE SPOT FOR THE JACKHAMMER?! I SAID IS THIS THE SPOT FOR THE JACKHAMMER?! YEAH?! YEAH, IT IS?! OKAY!!" With a noise very difficult to emulate with the written word, he fires up his jackhammer and plunges it into the asphault next to the sidewalk. (CUE UP: Whitney Houston’s "I Wanna Dance With Somebody" and the Microsoft Beziers screensaver while we wait for him to get a little ways in here.) The shot cuts back to the man, now grimy and sweating nuts, pounding away a few feet deep in the street. "I THINK I ALMOST-" the man begins before a clanging sound interrupts him followed by a jetstream of water that hits him in the face and knocks him on his tubby can. Inside, Nova still sits on the toilet, his head propped on his hands. Ariel paces back and forth, pondering escape routes. Suddenly she perks her head up. "Hey…do you hear that?" "That soft rumbling sound?" Nova asks, and his ex-wife nods in confirmation, "I believe so, yes." They sit in silence for a moment. "It’s getting louder, isn’t it?" Ariel wonders aloud. "Indeed," Nova agrees, "curious." Another moment of listening…and then a geyser of shit-water explodes out of the toilet, blanketing Nova and Ariel in a soggy film of fecal funk. "GET IT TO STOP!!" Ariel screams, backing away and attempting to shield herself from the spray. "USE YOUR T-SHIRT!" "Ariel, I can't do that," Nova replies, horrified at her suggestion, "this shirt…this shirt is from Rio. The last stop on Iron Maiden's 2001 Brave New World Tour, which saw the band rise out of the ashes of mediocrity in the mid-90s and find their voice again with the readmittance of Bruce Dickin-" "DO IT!!" Ariel shouts. "Forgive me, Bruce," Nova whispers, his voice laden with grief as water still sprays him. He removes the shirt, gives it one last look, and crams it down into the toilet, stopping the water after a moment. "WHAT. THE. FUCK," Ariel says after a moment, pushing strands of wet hair out of her face. "I could not have predicted that to happen," Nova admits, the beads of toilet water glistening in his beard like a field of stars across the night sky. "Oh, God, I think I’m gonna be sick," Ariel groans as she leans over the sink, the sudden stench overwhelming her. "Well, don’t feel confined to the sink," Nova says with a shrug, "puke anywhere. This is already going to be a hell of a clean-up job." Suddenly the Rising Star puts a hand to his ear. "Hey, wait…" "Oh, not another one!" Ariel moans. "No, no," Nova assures her, "no, not that…it’s…I think…YES!! ROCK ME AMADEUS! It’s on the radio in the living room!" "…what?" Ariel manages in disbelief. "Awww, girl, takes me back to the prom! Look out!" Nova cries happily, leaping off the john and breaking into his best robot, "AMADEUS AMADEUS! AMADEUS! AMADEUS AMADEUS! AMADEUS! AMADEUS AMADEUS! AH-AH-AH AMADEUS! COME ON AND ROCK ME AM-AHHHH!!!" His gr00ve gets out of control and he slips in the slime now covering the bathroom floor, falling on his ass. "Oooof!" Scum water immediately soaks through his jeans to his limited edition hologram Fairly Odd Parents drawers. "Fuck it," Ariel says, her voice heavy as she stifles the urge to hurl, "just kick the damn door down." "OH YEAH!" Nova pumps his fist in masculine satisfaction. He tries to do that thing where you fling your legs out and pop up, landing on your feet…but he falls back down before humbly crawling up the traditional way, by grabbing at everything bolted to the wall. He walks carefully over to the door. "Gotta listen to your boy sometimes, my dear. Now watch what we call in the business ‘the ol’ shoulder in the door.’" WHAM!! "Are you sure that wasn’t what you guys call in the business ‘the ol’ dislocate your shoulder?’" Ariel quips, flashing her first grin in quite some time. "This ain’t Hollywood, Ariel," Nova replies, indignant. "And I think you bought the reinforced steel one. Thanks for saying something beforehand." "It is not," Ariel denies, rolling her eyes. "Okay, here we go," Nova says, and backs up before charging the door and ramming it hard. It flies open and he stumbles out into the living room but doesn’t fall. For once, he doesn’t fall. Ariel storms out of the bathroom and up the stairs without saying a word. Nova watches her leave, and when the coast is clear he slinks over to the linen closet and takes out one of the blankets a particularly snooty friend of Ariel’s gave them a few Christmases ago. He wipes off with satisfaction. ------*~*~*------ That night, Nova sits on the couch in front of the fire, a mug of coffee with bourbon steaming in his hand. Ariel walks down the stairs, blankets in her hands. She sets them in a chair next to the couch. "What are those?" Nova asks. "Blankets," Ariel replies matter-of-factly, "for the couch. Where you’re sleeping tonight." "The couch?" Nova echoes in disbelief. "What, did you think you’d just pop in and spend the night in the sack?" Ariel inquires, her brow furrowed in amusement, "maybe fool around a little bit?" "Well, no," Nova admits, "but I didn’t think…" "No, you didn’t," Ariel interrupts, offering Nova a half-smile before turning back to the stairs, "goodnight, Chris." Nova watches her walk up the stairs. "It’s pretty cold down here, you know! Not at all like that upstairs bedroom, though! That room insulates like the fuck! PROBABLY PART OF THE REASON I CHOSE THIS HOUSE FOR US! PROBABLY…" He stops as he hears Ariel shut her door upstairs. With a "harrumph" the Rising Star turns, leaning over to the chair and snatching a blanket. He wraps it around himself and stretches out on the couch. His eyes are fixed on the fire, and remain there until their lids droop, carrying him to the land of GTT finals, Universal Titles, and happy families.
