Opportunity Knocks

14 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-14 Voice: Watch this … (The black screen is replaced by some words.) RPW Adrenaline … February 2004-09-06 Almasy threw a fireball in Harley’s face. A shocked challenger began stumbling up top, as Seymour went up with him, locking his arms around his waist, and pulling him off the top, jamming a knee into his groin and tailbone area for a super inverted atomic drop known only as SPHERE BREAK! The crowd groans in sympathy pain, as Aerith cheers from her wheelchair. Harley’s eyes are squinched tight in pain, as Seymour goes behind Harley, pulling his neck back across his shoulder, and dropping to his knees with the inverted stunner.. FULL THROTTLE! Harley recoiled, landing on the ladder face first from the blow. Almasy rolled him off, and covered, leaning his weight over the good arm, and hooking the leg. “ONE!” “TWO!” …… Harley attempted to kick out, to get the shoulder up…but his bad arm was too damaged to lift in order to get the shoulder up. He cursed under his breath, as the final count went down. “THREE!” The bell sounded, and Almasy rolled off of Harley, relieved to have defended successfully.. Relieved to have survived one more time. He laid on the mat, exhausted, as Jerry Quecard announced the result. “Your winner of the third fall, and thus the match, by pinfall, in 27:03, AND STILL PRIMETIME CENTRAL GLOBAL CHAMPION….”THE FINAL FANTASY” SEYMOUR…ALMASY!” (We hear a clicking sound as the image immediately is paused. The camera zooms out and we see Matt W. standing over a television set with a remote control in his hand. He looks distressed as his boy Khaled sits next to him.) Khaled: I don’t get it. (This fails to move Warr.) Khaled: What has Dave Harley got to do with anything? (The head of Warr moves to Khaled’s direction. The WWA CEO smiles at the New Era of Wrestling before responding.) Warr: It’s not Dave Harley … it’s that match. (Turning his attention away from Khaled, Warr stares at the Television.) Warr: That image. Seymour Almasy with the PTC Global Championship. You know it’s one of the biggest belts in the world of wrestling. A belt that should belong with the biggest company in wrestling … my company! (Upon hearing this, things seem to fall into place for Khaled as he nods along.) Warr: Do you know how that makes me feel to see a World Wrestling Alliance superstar lose? Do you know how it makes me look? Not such a Great Matt W. now that people can mock me, and mock my company! Khaled: Look GMW … it’s not your fault that Dave Harley lost. He let you down, you didn’t let us down. (This doesn’t seem to mean anything to Warr. It seems as if he is almost failing to listen.) Warr: Regardless, every time I watch that I feel a certain sense of shame. Like all my achievements in this business mean nothing because when it came time for my company to take its place at the top of the wrestling world, when it came time for me to stand atop the wrestling mountain … I blew it by putting my faith in the wrong guy! (Interestingly the eyes of Matt W. haven’t left the television set as his voice gets louder and louder. Getting up, Khaled puts his hand on his shoulder in a reassuring manner.) Khaled: Four months ago I was a wretched individual, plagued by mental illness … and I lost who I was. You saved me. (The strength of this statement briefly leads to Warr turning his attention away from the television set, but it isn’t long before his focus is back on the television, even as Khaled continues to speak.) Khaled: Like you saved the WWA from self destruct at the Independence Day Rumble this year. You should feel no shame for the failings of Dave Harley … you should feel pride for what you have done for the fans, for me and most importantly yourself. (For a while it seems that Warr likes what he is hearing, but he snaps when responding.) Warr: Don’t you get it. As long as this looms over me … as long as the PTC still looks down on the WWA because of this … as long as we don’t have the top man in wrestling … I am Matt W. the failure! (In this instance he turns his head away from the television and shouts at Khaled who is a little taken a back by this as he jumps back into his chair. Warr begins to laugh a little before moving in close to Khaled’s face and speaking.) Warr: But hey … it doesn’t matter any more. That was then and this is now. (The look on Khaled’s face at the sudden turn around in Warr is of sheer confusion.) Warr: Because right now something more then the PTC Global Championship is on the line. Something called the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. And you Khaled, are not just going to enter it … you are going to win it and end my shame! (From confusion to shock … that’s Khaled’s face.) Khaled: But … but … Warr: [interjecting] No but’s Khaled. Like you said, I saved you and now it’s time for you to save my reputation and end my shame. You’re already booked in the first round against a guy called Tarrant! (This seems to frighten Khaled a little.) Khaled: Tarrant … but he’s a former 4WW World Champion right? (This doesn’t seem to impress Matt W.) Warr: So … you’re a WWA superstar dam it! You’ve got in the ring with guys like Leviathan and you even this past week pinned former WWA World Champion Jim Black. (The only response the New Era of Wrestling has is one big gulp.) Warr: Come on Khaled! Have a little faith in yourself; don’t you know how good you are? (After pausing for a moment Khaled switches the television set off and moves in close to Warr.) Khaled: I know how good I am … it’s just Tarrant … Warr: Dam it! So he won a title … big deal. He’s not so big, bad and scary. Khaled: I know … but the man is a God dam freak! I’ve heard rumours about the guy, and I heard someone say something about him eating his own parents. Warr: What? Khaled: The guys some sick kind of time relic freak. I mean it’s Good to be Khaled … it’s not Good to have that guy within ten feet of you. Some sort of God complex about Cold Fire or something, whatever it is the man is verging on a trip to a psychiatric ward. Warr: Umm … well … don’t let that bother you. He’s not going to eat you or anything. (A look of shock springs across the face of Khaled.) Khaled: Eat me … he’s going to eat me. (Dropping into a chair Khaled holds his head in his hands but continues speaking.) Khaled: Freaking Golden Turnbuckle Tournament with a freaking Hannibal Lector wannabe. What the … (Rather unceremoniously Khaled is cut off by a big slap to the face.) Warr: You need to recognise who you are boy. (Warr is so close to Khaled’s face his spit is rebounding off him and back onto himself.) Warr: I don’t care if Tarrant can eat himself, the fact remains I need this … no we need this Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Pride is the foundation to which my life is built on, and shame is the bulldozer that prevents my success, and thus our success. Do you understand? (Silence.) Warr: I said do you understand. (Getting to his feet Khaled stares deep into the eyes of Matt W.) Khaled: This man is a notorious con man and thief. (Warr slaps Khaled hard across the face. A red faced Khaled turns back.) Khaled: This man thinks he is some sort of God. (With more force then ever Warr slaps Khaled hard across the face. Khaled turns back with a smile upon his face and starts to tense his body.) Khaled: This man is my first step to ending your shame. (That statement results in a smile from Matt W. But he continues to slap Khaled, as Khaled almost rhythmically beats his right foot down on the floor.) Khaled: You’re right, I’ve faced bigger challenges in my life and I’ve walked out a bigger man then I left. So Tarrant is different and unusual, I’m a guy who isn’t going to let you down. (The slaps seem to be getting harder.) Khaled: I’m a guy who can lock in any hold from dam near any position and I’m a guy who relishes opportunities … and this is an opportunity for the Warr Family. (The face of Khaled is bright red with all the slaps, but he maintains a look of focus.) Khaled: Oh Tarrant’s mine. I don’t care who he eats, there’s a reason why it’s Good to be Khaled … and for you GMW … they’re all about to find that out! (Suddenly Warr stops slapping Khaled and the New Era of Wrestling begins to calm down. Warr nods his head and Khaled leaves the room, Warr massaging on his shoulders as he does.)
