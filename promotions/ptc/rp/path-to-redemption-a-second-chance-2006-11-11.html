Path to Redemption: A Second Chance

11 November 2006

Date: 2006-11-11 Don't think too hard. Act on the heart and instinct. -excerpt from Sun Tzu's Life Strategies from the Art of War. Path to Redemption: A Second Chance The courtroom was silent as death. No one dared to breathe as all eyes rested on Geoffrey O'Donnell expectantly. All eyes, that is, except Mya's. Her gaze rested in her lap, staring at her hands as she clenched them together and trudged through the last few moments of what had happened. She was trying to make sense of it all. "She does not deserve to go to hell and you know it!" "Her sins far out weigh her virtues!" "An eye for an eye, Lucifer!" Revelation struck. Those eyes popped upward as her jaw gaped. Hell? Sins? Lucifer? Hesitantly, she swung her vision to the figure of the judge. And she knew. He was no judge. He was the Lord himself, the almighty, the father of all. She was dead and heaven and hell were battling over who would get her soul. " That is right Mya. You are in purgatory, though you're not quite dead." As God spoke, her startled gaze widened. She didn't want to go to hell. " And you won't. As least, not yet." Was he reading her mind? " Of course I am." Color stained her cheeks. She should've known he can do anything, but that natural human tendency to doubt was still freshly embedded upon her soul. Her gaze lowered briefly before raising to meet his again with the one question still foremost on her mind. What was going to happen to her? " You are going back. I am giving you a second chance to prove you belong in either place. The path you take will be yours by choice, but remember, whatever path you chose will be the path that leads to you life everlasting, or..eternal damnation." " WHAT!?" " WHAT!?" Both Cass and Lomax spoke in unison. Neither had won this case in the eyes of the Lord. He merely gave them both a silent look causing the pair to fall into obedient silence once more. Cass was pleased, perhaps more pleased than she should be. Lucifer, on the other hand, was fuming. He has lost many a case before, but this was the first in near a century that the decision to send a human back was made. It meant more work for him. He loathed having to redouble his efforts. Fortunately, he had a plan B for everything…. October 20, 2006: Bangkok General Hospital The first thing her groggy senses picked up on was the steady sound of a beep just off to her left. That was followed by the feeling of something wrapping her cheeks to cover her mouth and nose. None of this was enough to get those lids to lift. It was the pungent scent of a familiar cologne that set off alarms in her head. The monitor leaped in speed for an instant as her eyes snapped open. Slowly she turned that hazy gaze to her right in search of the annoyance. There he was, Smitty T. Duluth, with a leg hooked over the arm of his chair and that blond head tilted over the edge in such a way he was sure to have a crick in his neck. How long had he been there? She cringed as she pulled herself up to sit. The better question was how long had she been asleep? Her arms screeched with the disapproval of being moved, as did the rest of her slightly atrophied form. Smitty released a staggered breath that sounded more like a blubbered raspberry as he shifted in his chair and began to snore. Mya stared at him for a long moment trying to decide whether she was flattered he was here, or irritated. Pushing the thought aside she decided to take a look around the room. Everywhere she looked there seemed to be mountains of flowers. Frowning she turned her gaze to the nightstand and gingerly reached out plucking a card from yet another vase of tiger lilies. To my kitten. May you get well soon. You are missed. S.T.D. Mya rolled her eyes and set the card aside. He would never give up in his relentless pursuit of her. As her gaze swung over to Smitty once more she froze. He was staring back at her with a smile that made her stomach turn. " You are awake! PRAISE BE TO GOD! I was beginning to think you'd never return to me, my pet." Mya merely glared at the FUSE commissioner in exasperated silence. Smitty stood and rushed to her side. As he fell to his knees beside the bed he took her hand within both of his and kissed her knuckles. " You gave us quite the scare." Mya yanked her hand free and winced as her body groaned in protest at the jolting action. " How long have you been here?" He seemed undaunted by her denial of his affection as he got to his feet and sat on the edge of the bed. It was an uncomfortable feeling, his staring at her in such an intimate fashion. Shifting slightly, she pulled as far away from him as the bed would allowed. Smitty was oblivious to the action. " I have been here for a week. I came as soon as we got the news." " We?" " FUSE. The hospital contacted us two days after you were brought here. We were lucky that one of the nurses recognized you from T.V. If not for her there is no telling what would happen." Mya barely listened to what he said. Instead, she was trying to recall the last thing that happened before waking up in this place. As she delved into her memory banks, Smitty kept prattling on like a woman. Did the man ever shut up? He was ruining her concentration. " Apparently you were found in a back alley with two gun shot wounds to the shoulder and chest. It is a miracle you survived with as much blood loss you suffered." Mya's gaze snapped back to his face. His words triggered a foggy memory. " I was shot?" " Yes, but it appears you didn't go down without a fight. They found blood a few yards from where you were." Another flash of memory. A face. A familiar face. The face of her.. " Father…" " What was that?" Mya glanced to Smitty again and shook her head as she waved him off. " Nothing. So there was no other body there?" " No." She frowned. The memory was slowly becoming clear. He had taunted her after tricking her in that fog filled alley way. Then he shot her again, but not without being shot by her. She remember the look on his face as his hand came to his chest, blood spilling freely between his fingers. Surely, he could not have survived that wound? Could he? " Tigera…Mya, I realize you only just woke up but there is something I need to discuss with you." Smitty's words interrupted her rampaging thoughts. With a blink she returned her attention to him. " What is that?" " Gtt6." Her brows furrowed. " What about it?" Smitty cleared his throat, clearly uncomfortable with what he was about to say. That or he was fearful of her reaction. She wasn't exactly the softest hearted woman in the world. " You have a match scheduled against a," he glanced at the paper he produced," Mentok fellow. The match is set for less that nine days away. The doctors say that it would be too soon for you to wrestle. And, well…." Mya forced some air from her lungs as his words trailed away. " Out with it already." " …Well, we feel it might be best if you forfeit your match with him. For your own good, of course." Mya didn't say anything. She merely stared at him soundlessly for a long moment unable to form any words in reply. They wanted her to forfeit? To quit? The faded memory of a fight with her father over this very subject tumbled through her brain. " No." " No?" Smitty blinked in surprise. He recovered quickly and pressed the issue. " Mya, this was not a decision that was reached lightly. Both FUSE and the officials at PTC feel that for the sake of your own recovery it is best you forfeit and heal properly. No one would think less of you. Certainly not I." Mya growled slightly as she rolled to her side giving him her back. Silently she stared at the petals of a tiger lily, stubbornly refusing to listen to reason. This situation was different than the one with her father before. He had wanted her to quit for no reason other than to slake his own wants and desires. At least the wrestling companies had her health in mind when making the suggestion. It didn't make her hate the option any less. Smitty sighed wearily, the rustling of fabric her only clue to his standing up. He gently placed the papers on the nightstand and looked down to her back with a hint of remorse. " I know it is hard to give up something you hunger for, but I beseech you to think about this. You still have a few days to decide. I… I will leave you alone for a while and return to check on you in the morning. Rest well, kitten." Mya barely afforded him a glance as he stepped out of the room. Carefully, she rolled to her back and stared at the papers with a bitterness welling in her heart. Even now, her father was working at destroying everything she ever loved. Damn the man. ~*~*~*~ " Tell me the risks." Mya sat, propped up on her pillows staring pointedly at the doctor hovering just off the end of her bed. He looked displeased with her lack of cordial behavior. Placing his clipboard on the rolling bed table, he crossed his arms and gave her a level look. " If you insist upon wrestling in your match you run the risk of tearing muscle tissue. Your stitches have healed nicely but there is no telling what that kind of damage physical exertion will do. Your wounds could reopen. The tissue in your chest and shoulder could tear again forcing you to take a longer break from wrestling, perhaps up to three months due to rehabilitation. The choice is yours Miss Yamomuko, but I must insist on saying my recommendation is to forfeit this tournament and allow yourself the time to heal. If you had at least a couple more weeks, I would be more inclined to say you were okay to wrestle." It was evident by the scowl on her face that Mya was not pleased with the bleak out look he just painted for her. At this point in her life, wrestling was the only sure and sane thing she had. The awkward silence hung heavily in the air as the physician picked up his charts and turned to leave giving her the space needed to further consider her options. Mya's gaze drifted to the papers resting on her lap as a heavy sigh escape. She had a choice to make. Either forfeit Gtt6 and heal with enough time to headline Sin City. Or press on with the match in Gtt6 and run the risk of losing everything she worked so hard for in FUSE. Give up the chance at glory or give up the chance at becoming legendary. Pressing her lips firmly together, she reached for the bed side table and pulled it across her lap placing the papers on the surface. Shaky fingers reached out to the nightstand picking up the pen left there earlier by Smitty and returned to rest on the papers. She had no choice in the end. There would always be another tournament she could enter. Losing her belt due to all of this just wasn't worth the risk. Sharply inhaling she flipped the papers to the final page and clicked the pen prepared to sign. X Mya_______________ No sooner had she placed her first name on the document than her door swung open with gusto, an overly excited Smitty T. Duluth practically tumbling in. Mya glanced him briefly before turning back to finish signing away any chance she had of winning the tournament. " NO!" Mya flinched causing the pen to draw a streak across the page before lifting an irritated gaze to her personal thorn. " Excuse me?" Smitty rushed forward and before she could stop him yanked the paper off the table. " Don't sign! I have good news!" Mya drew a slow breath in attempt to calm the small storm already brewing from irritation. Smitty was unconcerned as per usual as he reached into the inside pocket of his suit jacket and planted some other papers down before her. " Read this." He beamed. Mya gave him a disgruntled look as she reached for the papers and began to look over the content. As she continued to read her irritation with him turned to elation. By the time she finished reading the entirety of the documents she was near the point of tears. Due to constraints on time and previous engagements, I, Mentok the Mindtaker, hereby forfeit my position in the GTT tournament…. Relief washed over her as she reached out and yanked Smitty to her planting a kiss on his cheek. Smitty blinked as his hand reverently felt his cheek where her lips had touched. " I should save the day more often if this is my reward." Mya laughed. She actually laughed as she looked at the documents again then looked skyward. Someone was listening to her heart's unspoken wants. With a happy rush of air she looked to Smitty and grinned. " Looks like I'll be competing after all." Smitty grinned back. " Indeed." November 9, 2006: Reno, Nevada Sweat slid into her eyes as she replaced the barbell to its cradle once more and sat up rubbing at the stinging in her gaze. Her shoulder ached horribly from the constant work she put it through. Wincing, she rolled it around as her other hand clamped down on the joint. The sound of the gym door opening brought her gaze to the front. Tsuyoshi Tanaka was all smiles as he made his way over to her. " How's the shoulder holding up?" Mya released a wry chuckle as she dropped her arm and signaled for a near by towel. Tanaka tosses it to her as she stands and wipes her face. " It's sore but nothing I can't handle. What did you find out about my opponent?" Tanaka shrugged as he took a seat on a nearby bench watching as she grabbed a water bottle and drank deeply. " To be honest, not much. He has never entered this tournament before to anyone's knowledge. He was eliminated in round one by a wrestler known as High Flyer then came back via play in in round two where he took on one of our fellow roster members." Mya perked a brow at his deliberate attempt at avoiding telling her who. " Who?" " Bobino." A rush of air left her lungs as she ran a hand over her sable mane. " Bobino? Really? How good do you think this guy is?" Again, Tanaka shrugged slightly. " To be honest, he is no slouch, Mya. You have your work cut out for you. The question is – are you ready for it?" Mya halted her pacing, spinning to face Tanaka with a grim expression plastered across her features. He put his hands up and shrugged his shoulders apologetically. " Hey, I was just asking." At her persistant look he repeated it with a more repentant tone. " I was just asking!' Mya shook her head softly as she walked away heading for the showers. Tanaka watched her for a moment before getting up and leaving the gym again. He had his own matters to attend to. ~*~*~*~ The steam still heavily shrouded the shower room as Mya briskly rubbed her hair with the towel and wrapped her head flipping back to a standing position. She reached up tucking her body towel tighter together to ensure it didn't fall as she opened the locker and withdrew her clothing. Buzzzzzzzzz. Buzzzzzzzzz. Her cell phone vibrated on the metal bottom of the locker. Snatching it up she looked at the display screen and frowned. Unknown number. Muttering she hit the send button and placed it to her ear. " Hello?" Nothing but silence. " Hello?" " Hello…..Mya…" Instantly, the phone dropped shattering on the floor as she stepped away and stared at it with morbid fear. It couldn't be. He should be dead! Her heart felt as if it was going to leap from her chest at the rate it was beating. Swallowing heavily, she hastily donned her clothing and slammed the locker shut before retrieving the pieces of her phone, shoving them into her gym bag. Shouldering the bag she made for the exit only to stop as the lights faltered then cut out completely. Squeezing her eyes shut, she planted her back against a locker and slowly sank to a sitting position. Oh, god please don't let it be him. Not now. Please.. Silently she continued to pray when the lights suddenly came back on and the sound of voices could be heard in the main room of the gym. Carefully she rose to her feet and prowled to the door. Opening it, Mya, peered outside for any sign of the faces behind the voices and sighed with relief. It was members of the staff. Without further ado, she threw the door open and rushed toward the front door as they called their apologies out to her about the dysfunction of the lights only a few moments earlier. She wasn't listening. The only thing on her mind was the sound of the voice that should belong to a dead man. November 11, 2006: The night of the Round 3 Matches " Tigera, you're up in two minutes." Mya glanced at the backstage hand and nodded as she continued her pacing anxiously waiting for her upcoming match against the Masked Marauder fellow. After a moment she paused at the heavy drapery separating the arena from the backstage area. Carefully pulling it aside she took a peek at the crowd casually taking in the excited faces as they eagerly awaited her arrival. In the midst of the chaos her eyes stalled on the face of one fan.That heart lurched violently against her ribs at the familiar viasge. She dropped the curtain as if it suddenly caught on fire. It was his face, the face of the man whom she had shot in the hopes to gain her life back. Frowning, she stepped forward again and drew the curtain aside to look for him again. Nothing. Perhaps it was a figment of her imagination. Then again, perhaps not. The phone call had been all too real. " Tigera." " MYA!" She flinched at the use of her real name, spinning to face the stagehand looking at her impatiently. He gestured to the stairs and lofted his brows in question. It was time. Putting her game face on, the Bangkok Tiger took the stairs and waited for the moment she would burst onto the stage from behind the screen. The moment the pyro went off she surged forward and went to face her fans… …and her destiny.
