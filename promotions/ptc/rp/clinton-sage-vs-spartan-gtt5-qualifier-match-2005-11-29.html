Clinton Sage vs Spartan, GTT5 Qualifier Match

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 * * * * * Clinton Sage lay on his back, staring up at the lights that lit the arena. His throat was still throbbing, his vision still hazy. The world seemed to melt together at the edges. A figure, cloaked in black, hovered over him. Even with his senses knocked off-kilter, Clinton could decipher the sound that rocked the arena: The roar of approval. The fans were cheering the victor. How could it have come to this? How could he have lost to someone afraid to show their face? This figure in black had given him the match he had never expected. Known as nothing short of extraordinary inside the squared circle, Clinton Sage held a victory over PTC’s reigning Global Champion…yet, he couldn’t defeat this man without a face? This man that shroud himself in a black mask…had beaten him. When it counted. The prize Clinton lost? A position in the GTT5 Tournament, where he was to make his ultimate stand. To square off against Almasy, or Deville, or avenge the victory that Rollins had taken back from him. With his vision clearing, the man in black extended a hand down towards Clinton, offering his assistance. Perhaps it was the lack of oxygen in his system, but before Clinton could slap the hand away in disgust, he had accepted it. Circling each other in a stare-down, Clinton Sage never removed his gaze from the masked man. As both men stood, the crowd was still roaring with approval over the victory, but was quickly melting away into a silent intrigue. Spartan’s blue eyes held such depth, such knowledge. Clinton had never wrestled anyone like him before. He moved with such immaculate precision, such knowledge. It seemed as if this Spartan assimilated everything Clinton had done inside the ring, and used it against him. Standing there in silence, Clinton could only think of one thing to say to this man in black, so he went right ahead and said it. * * * * *
