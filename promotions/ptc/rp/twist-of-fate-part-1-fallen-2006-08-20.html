Twist Of Fate (Part 1: Fallen)

20 August 2006

Date: 2006-08-20 -------------------------------------------------------------------- Prelude: Through The Eyes Of A Tragedy -------------------------------------------------------------------- Every little girl growing up wishes someday they'll meet their Prince Charming, riding in on his white stallion and sweeping you off your feet. Sometimes, we even wish so hard that we blind ourselves from the truth and see what we want to see… hear what we want to hear. Sometimes the truth hurts so bad, you act like it's not the truth at all. You hide in a false sense of reality where everything is fine, and nothing can ever break the Hallmark movie love. Unfortunately, life works in mysterious and sometimes painful way. Ways that leave you lost and confused, hurt and crying yourself to sleep every night. You wake up the next morning with your eyes almost crusted shut and rubbed raw, atop tear stained bed sheets. What a beautiful image that is. Yet you walk through the day with a smile on your face and your head up high, flashing your diamond to everyone who'll look. Everyone seems so happy, except for me. This isn't the life I dreamed of when I was a little girl. The perfect couple should kiss each other to sleep at night, call just to say I love you, and do all the little details that make a life together just perfect. My life isn't perfect. Not anymore. Time to get up Miss Valentine, today is your wake up call. -------------------------------------------------------------------- First Stanza: Some Things Never Change -------------------------------------------------------------------- Click… Click… Click… Her footsteps echoed through the barren hallways. Almost as if to compliment the melodies of Moonlight Sonata that played over the rather large estate. Everything was so dark, and the scent of musk had begin to set in. Although the manor had been properly maintained nothing seemed to be quite as it was. The appearance hadn't changed much at all, other than the piece of art done by Markus that now hung over the fireplace in the family room. After all, what else was there to do when you're forced to stay put in a room all day? She couldn't imagine being forced into solitary confinement, locked in a room all day alone. Not allowed to as much as speak with any sort of human contact outside of those who lived in Zenith Manor North, in fear of what they might find out about him. The Total J Cup Runner-Up had taken a liking to oil painting, and over time had grown quite talented. A far reach from his previous business major he was trying to achieve in life. It seemed that art was his calling in the afterlife. If that was what you could call it. In essence it was fitting that Markus Eaux D'Artifice had become the newest addition to the Zenith Manor. The estate itself was becoming much like Markus, appearing to be alive but completely dead on the inside. The once luxurious estate was full of life and promise for the Zenith Family, now it spends most of its time picking up dust. Christian Zenith spent almost 18 hour days at the office, not even Marques had seen much of him in the past few months. After Zenith Corporation's rendezvous with death, his work had become his obsession. Friends and loved ones had become a luxury that he had to earn. It was almost like he'd lost the zest for life that made him so charismatic to the underground scene of Chicago. The Midnight Messiah he was no more, The Conscious Crisis almost sounded like a joke. There was almost nothing conscious about the 24 year old Billionaire any more. As for Vivica J. Valentine… Click… Click… Click… Those were her footsteps approaching. Making her weekly visit to her home between GCW WorldWide card's and spending days at a time moving from hotel to hotel. She'd achieved more in-ring success than anyone could've imagined… other than herself of course. Although cocky, confident, and sometimes obnoxious, The Black Mariah had become a media darling. Always with something to say, a memorable quote, or a dazzling move. She'd almost become a spectacle herself. A member of Dies Irae, without a doubt Vivica is the person who the fans love to hate. Beautiful and smart, she had it all… she'd be the first person to tell you. The magazine and advertising offers had just began to pour in after her breath taking match with Eva VanLorne at Warpath. Her stock was on the rise, so much so that Steven Caldera (against his better judgement) asked her to represent Global Championship Wrestling at the next PrimeTime Central Event. That is what made this stop unusual. It was time to break the news to Christian. He would be so excited for her. What many would consider his shattering of the glass ceiling in Global Wrestling was a very impressive, although short showing at PrimeTime Central's Seven Of The Best. After spending her entire career walking in the footsteps of both her fiance and her better known Dies Irae associates Shadow, Dawn McFarlane, and Seymour Almasy… this was her time in the spotlight. A grand scale for the whole world to see what she was capable of. It was hard to contain a smile as she wandered around the hollow estate. Peeking into the entertainment room, the television was off but the stereo system continued to play Beethoven's finest. "Ah, hello Miss Valentine!" The sound of a familiar voice startled the GCW United States Champion. She almost jumped out of her brand new New Balance shoes. Turning around with a smile she looked into the eyes of Marques, their trusted housekeeper. He was of Spanish descent but grew up in Connecticut. Although a hint of his heritage could be heard in his speaking voice, it was almost impossible to detect without first being told. "Hey Marques." Vivica J. Valentine responded with a smile, containing the good news as she continued, "I'm so happy to see you. Are you the only one here? Is Christian home from work yet? I have something for him to see." "Well, actually Mister Zenith didn't go into the office downtown today." Marques spoke, almost as if he were confused, "He's spent all day up in his study. Most of it on the computer and the telephone. It appears something important must have happened. As you know, I am usually the last to hear about these things." "I have to see him." Vivica said heading up the stairwell towards Christian's study which lied on the second floor right next to the stairwell. "I wouldn't recommend…" Marques started before realizing Vivica was already halfway up the stairs. He should know better. The Black Mariah was never one to rationalize before taking actions. Headlong up the stairs she rushed, finally able to break the news to her fiancé. It'd be almost two weeks since the last time she'd seen Christian. It sounded from Marques like some things had changed, maybe Zenith Corporation wasn't in as good as shape as she'd thought. Maybe Christian Zenith wasn't coming back to wrestling. That was a thought she'd never considered as she reached out and touched the door. Cracking it open she peeked inside. Every light inside the room was off. The only source in the room was the blue florescent glow of the computer monitor and the pinkish orange hues of the sun setting over the horizon from the window facing west. Stepping into the door Vivica slammed it shut behind her, pulling an envelope out from the pocket of her blue and silver Tripp Pants. The return address of the letter read PrimeTime Central… it appeared to be an invitation. "Hey Chris you'll never believe what I got in the ma…" Vivica began to speak before she was interrupted by the young business prodigy. Never even taking the time to turn around in his computer chair to acknowledge her presence. "Do you knock before you enter rooms?" Christian said, completely monotone. It was almost like he was purposely speaking like a robot. He'd always had a cold demeanor about himself. Most of the time it was one he'd used for intimidation, this one seemed to stem from a different method… apathy. "Well I would've but I've got some really good news and I was sort of in a hurr.." Vivica tries to finisher her line once again before she's cut off. "Pack your things Viv. I need you to go to Seattle tonight." Christian said before turning around and looking into the eyes of GCW's fearless phenom. His cadet eyes pierced through the dimly lit room, making Vivica feel a little uncomfortable. For the first time in years, she was almost… afraid. "I can't go to Seattle, I'm participating in the next PrimeTime Central event. Just like you did. Karina Wolfenden is even in it." Vivica said, taking a step forward slowly trying to shake the awkward feeling she'd developed since stepping into the room. "It's a PTC Extreme Title shot. Against Archibald McGregor. I'd like you to come with me, Lia is going to be there too. It'll be just like old times." "I've found a lead on Tera." Christian continued, purposely ignoring Vivica's statement in order to get his point across, "I have to see what's going on. We can't have another series of murders going around with my name all over them. Not after everything we've done to clear myself. She has to be dealt with… I've heard she's already done a job for someone named Sekhet. Ask Keiko Yoshimoto if you don't believe me. It's the real deal this time." "Christian I can't!" Vivica responded, "You have dozens of people more than capable of doing this. Yourself included. I have a job in Global Championship Wrestling I love and I need to keep. Who knows what can happen to me when Tera is involved." "Wrestling is not a job." Christian interrupted again, a stern look crossed his face as he stood up out of his chair, "Wrestling is a game of amusement. Wrestling is a hobby, a pastime, a game. Zenith Corporation is a job. Protecting our investment is a job. Protecting the investment my father made before me is a job. We have REAL jobs to do. You have a job to do." "Wrestling is so much more than a hobby…" Vivica defended herself, raising her voice trying to get the message across to the man once known as The Midnight Messiah. "Get the job done." Christian said, "Pack your things. The plane leaves soon." Vivica J. Valentine bit her lip and looked across the room with a glare that only a woman can give. Unfazed Christian sat back down in his chair and turned back around to his computer, leaving Vivica standing in the middle of the room. Her hands balled into fists as she turned around and stormed out of the room. Slamming the door shut behind her. Zenith didn't even wince. The Black Mariah had never felt so alone. -------------------------------------------------------------------- Second Stanza: Words Of Wisdom -------------------------------------------------------------------- CRACK! The sound of the drawer being snapped out of the dresser could be heard throughout the massive estate. With a thump the once beautiful piece of carpentry became nothing more than a heap of firewood, nails, and scattered clothes. Picking up shirts, pants, and undergarments by the handfuls, The Black Mariah shoved them onto a black rolling suitcase on top of her queen sized bed. Tears streamed down her red face as she fell to her knees in the middle of the floor. She was so infuriated she didn't even know how to act. It had been so long since she'd felt this sort of anger, it wasn't the typical kind of anger. It was the anger that happens when someone rips your heart out and steps on it. At that moment in time, Vivica would've almost rather that happen. She wouldn't be around any longer to feel that way. Wiping the steams of tears and mascara away from her face, she headed towards the window and looked at the last glimpse of the sun as it disappeared behind the trees that surrounded the front of the estate. Thoughts of loneliness began to consume her thoughts. The one person who she thought would know how she felt, the one person in the world that knows her better than everyone. How could he just stop caring? How could he be so cold towards her? Together a long time ago, they'd made a pact to never say I love you. They'd made a pact to never consummate their relationship. Everyone they'd ever loved had died seemingly in their arms. It was a pact Vivica finally realized that she could never keep. She just couldn't tuck her emotions away in a corner whenever it was inappropriate like him. She was a living, breathing human being. How could anyone do that? It wasn't normal. It wasn't right. It wasn't how things were supposed to be at all. Her mind became ambushed by a million thoughts as tears rolled down her face like a waterfall. After so many years of acting like a Stepford Wife… it looks like she's finally lost her god damn mind. How horrible a sight she must be now, black mascara smears all over her face with her forehead against the window sobbing like a 4 year old who scraped her knee. For the first time in her life she thought maybe things wouldn't end happily ever after in the end. The Manor had finally done to her what it'd done to everyone else. It'd sucked the will right out of her, just like a vampire. "What in the hell is going on in here?" A voice resonated through Vivica's head. Turning around with her eyes rubbed raw and strands of blonde and baby blue in her face, she saw the last person in the world she'd expect to care about her well being. Enter the vampire, Markus Eaux D'Artifice. "Nothing." Vivica tried to regain her composure jumping off the bed and gathering her clothes off the floor real fast. Picking up pants and shirts she kicked the broken drawer under her bed. Concealing her face with laundry, she'd hope Markus would simply leave. "Really?" She'd hoped wrong. Markus walked into the room and shut the door behind him as Vivica turned her back to The Childe Of Destiny. Shoving her belongings into the suitcase she slammed the lid down and attempted to zip it up and leave. No luck. Punching the top of the bag, she began to throw a temper tantrum. "I heard the whole conversation." Markus finally admitted sitting down on the bed next to The Black Mariah, "So you're going to Seattle?" "Yeah…" Vivica replied reluctantly, "The plane leaves in two hours, I have to get out of here in 30 minutes to catch it in time. Although, I suppose a private jet would wait for me. I just want to get the hell out of here. Now if you'll excuse me." "Why?" Markus replied. "Why what? You heard the conversation. You apparently eavesdropped enough to know where I'm going… do you have ADD or something?" Vivica responded picking up the suitcase and heading for the door. "Why are you doing what he says?" Markus responded, "Why do you do everything he says? You had to join a wrestling tournament you were totally unprepared for just in order to keep an eye on me. Now he's making you drop out of something you've been waiting on for YEARS just to keep an eye on his older sister?" "You don't understand Christian." Vivica responded, "I've gone through a lot of shit in my life and I can't even compare my struggles to what he's gone through for me." "And that gives him the right to treat you like a child?" Markus responded, "Alright, have fun little girl." "I'm not a fucking child." Vivica said stopping Markus as he began to stand up from the bed, "He needs my help." "And he can't help himself?" Markus retaliated, "He can go to PTC Events and he can go run off and do whatever he wants and you have to be his little babysitter? You have to do all the dirty work that he's too 'busy' to do. The guy has gone nuts. Not sure if you've noticed. It has nothing to do with his life growing up, nothing can screw you up that much." "How in the hell would you know?" "I'm a fucking vampire Vivica." Markus raised his voice for the first time since he'd been turned into what he is today. It'd taken The Black Mariah off-guard. After what he's had to deal with over the past couple of months, it was amazing to her that he'd taken such an interest in the life of the girl whose job it was to cage him like an animal. Especially after the way she'd talked to him and treated him during Total J Cup. "If you want to go an do his bidding like a good girl you do it. But you have a life too, if you want to waste it doing stupid errands go for it. You'll regret wasting your life on such petty things. Trust me, I know… I lost mine recently." With that said Markus got up and walked out of the room, closing the door behind him with a quiet thud. The Black Mariah stood in the middle of the room looking over at a picture of her and Christian Zenith backstage at a Global Wrestling event. They both looked so happy then, it was amazing how much things had changed. In silence she remained in the room sitting on the bed thinking about what she was going to do next. The only sound that could be heard, was the sound of a diamond ring falling onto the hardwood floor next to her suitcase.
