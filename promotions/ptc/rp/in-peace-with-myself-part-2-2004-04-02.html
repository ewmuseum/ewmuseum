In Peace with Myself Part 2

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 The scene opens to what appears to be a big interview type of thing, kind of like the Michael Jackson one. Sitting in one chair is Dan and in the other is the self proclaimed hippy himself Limp. Today he has decided to don a smart suit and an equally smart pair of shoes. He has a Cuban Cigar in his right hand and is chuckling away with Dan in light banter while the tech guys get everything ready. A tech guy slinks up to Dan and whispers something in his ear, which prompts Dan to jerk out of his relaxed manner and scan through some notes. The lights in the room focus on Limp and Dan and the interview kicks off. Dan starts off with an introduction. Dan- Welcome ladies and gentlemen to this exclusive interview with Limp. Welcome Limp how are you today? Limp takes a large drag from his cigar and then begins to speak. Limp- I'm doing good thanks Dan, well you know as well as I can during this period of time. Limp curls up in the usual fashion people do when they want to show they are upset about something. Dan prompts with his hand for Limp to get it off his chest. Limp begins to speak in an unusual timid way. Limp- Well you know this whole Chezina thing. You know it hurt what he said. Well one thing hurt impartial. You know what he said to me? He said we weren't friends anymore, now I thought it was all friendly completive banter between friends but then he had to say it didn't he. *Limp begins to raise his voice at this point* he said we are no longer friends *now at shouting volume* Do you know how that made me feel Dan? I'll tell you it hurt you know? Chezina says our friendship is over. You know I thought we were friends, but it really does show that it meant nothing to him, as soon as that world title came into play he just forgot about all the special times we had together, painting, long walks on the beach and just plain chatting. Limp stops at this point as it seems to have been all to much for Limp. Dan looks a little strange at the master of the mood swing and then tries to change the subject slightly. Dan- errm well, what do you have to say about the things that Chezina said about you? Your a fake and all that? Limp composes himself and begins to speak trying to sound calm and collect. Limp- Me a fake? Limp brushes off some dust from his new expensive suit and takes another drag from his Cuban cigar and speaks again. Limp- No no no how am I a fake, come on. The suit well I just wanted to look good for this little interview and the cigar well I ran out of cigs, yeah that's right. My heart was in the right place. Now about the naked people feeding me grapes. What's so capitalist about grapes, they're a piece of fruit good for you you know. Dan shoots a question at Limp. Dan- I think he meant that having the "naked people" serve you is the problem. Seeing as how you claim to be a hippy and hate the capitalist way of life. Limp takes a big pause to think about it and then begins to speak once again. Limp- But don't you see it? Dan- See what? Limp- Oh I'm not going to waste my time on people who don't get the brilliance of my ideas, move on. Dan- But... Limp- Move Dan- Limp Limp- On. Dan gives up and moves onto another topic. Dan- So Limp there is word that you will be writing a book and also word of you staring in a film of your life story, is this true? Limp smiles now and begins to speak. Limp- Well you know I was thinking if that Tom Bloke can write a book, little old me must be able to. I was thinking of portraying my life story, completely openly and truthfully you know, my birth in Liverpool, the hard slog in Hamburg to try and get the band together, the amazing trip in India, the spilt up between the band, my controversial marriage, then my assassination, you know. Dan looks confused yet worried at the same time. Dan- errm that's not your life story, it sounds more like John Lennon's. Limp looks hurt but musters the words to speak. Limp- Dan how could you, your calling me a liar? Your meant to be a professional interviewer come on your better than that. Dan- Ok fine you were telling the truth but can I just get a few things clear? *Dan doesn't wait to get a response from Limp* you stated you were born in Fajardo when you came to the fed, now your from Liverpool? What band were you in? We have no record of you ever going to India? What marriage? And finally if you were assassinated how are you here today? Limp- They are good questions Dan and you will have to buy my book when it comes out to find out. Limp looks pleased at his response and goes back to smoking his cigar and staring up at the ceiling lights with a excited smile on his face. As with most people who interview Limp, Dan seems lost for words. Dan shrugs his shoulders and the camera fades back to the main UPWA studios. Later... The scene opens on a packed out arena. The crowd are in good spirits and the action laid out in front of them is not to shabby. A match ends between two joyful jobbers. There is a pause while the guys get out of the ring and backstage. Then suddenly "Get Up" by Limp comes on to the PA system. After a few seconds of play the lights fade down and white lights from the entrance ramp shine up through the ramp. The song reaches a high note and Limp emerges from the backstage. He is wearing a tattered clothes and a big necklace of flowers around his neck. He has his arms out reached like he is nailed to a cross. Limp stands like this at the top of the entrance ramp for a few seconds and then continues to walk down the entrance ramp. The ramp floor lights slowly follow him down. The crowd reaction is pretty mixed and are waiting to see what he says before they judge him. Limp gets to the ring, slides under the bottom rope, then slides to his knees and then finally jumps to his feet. Limp walks over to a tech guy who hands Limp a mic and in turn Limp places his necklace of flowers around the tech guys neck. The UPWA staff have got used to this sort of thing now so just receives it with a smile. Which makes Limp smile. The crowd seem to have grown restless with Limp's antics and the fact that they came to this show to see people get their heads knocked off, not see an 18 year old 6'0 guy give flowers to a tech guy. Limp seems unphased by this and goes to stand in the middle of the ring and awaits for the music to fade out. When it does Limp takes out a packet of cigarette from his right side pocket flips a cigarette into his mouth straight from the packet, lights it and takes a few drags and then begins to speak. Limp- Well well well. My first promo inside a PTC ring for quite some time. But lets not dwell on that as I'm sure you know bigger things are in the equation. The biggest event PTC will have seen, the biggest event PTC will ever see, yes that's right Limp will win the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament, the thing that Limp has been looking for, for oh so many years. Mpmmy and Daddy will be so proud! Limp suddenly stops in his joyful regal and puts a sad face on. Limp- Oh wait Dad's dead and Mom said she never wanted to see me again. The crowd don't seem to know how to react to this, but then Limp jumps back into a joyful stint. Limp- Any hoot yes this will be the day of days. The grand daddy of them all. I know I have gone on and on about the day I win the tournament oh so many times, explaining how it will happen. I will get the one two three after a good old fashioned wrestling match. I will kiss Karina Wolfenden and pat her ass so that she knows she did well, get to my feet be given the announcement that I have advance to the second round, the ref will raise my hand and I will drop to my knees and maybe even shed a tear at that buetey of the moment. Oh yes just another thought on how I will end it, you lucky lucky troopers will just have to wait and see how I will truly end it. But don't you worry it will be a sight to see for all! Well not for Karina. Limp pauses the crowd is pretty silent with the occasional small chants of "hippy" and "idiot". Limp then begins to speak in a slightly less joyful way. Limp- Now Chezina is maybe my friend, but not best. A piece of gold slapped to a belt is his best friend? See my friend you are still confused. It's not the actual title it's the prestige of being the world champ. Didn't you look sweet dancing away at little AJ's party, no I'm serious the happiest I've seen you in ages. But seriously your friends with those idiots? With "please please sir pick me" and the she male. Just laughing at you, laughing at the fact they consider themselves the "in crowd" and you the dorky idiot, Saved by the bell flashes through my head. You talk about me living the "plastic lifestyle" yet you are living the life one of those ridiculous moral lesson American teen programs show every friggin day. Limp pauses to chuckle. Limp- But don't you love those things, so true to life. No one drinks, no one smokes, no one does drugs, no one swears, no rebellion against the system and everyone goes to church. Wow yes that was my life when I was that age and everyone I knew. Well maybe that's what it's like when your in American childhood and teenage year. Who knows not me. Maybe I should ask Jason as he has certainly turned out the average American. The crowd are jolted by the memory of the old Limp and begin to boo. Bret smiles and speaks once more. Limp- Oh come on what's with you people just a little social comment. That's the kind caring guy I am. I have only transformed to my new and better image for a short time but I can see the love spreading, I can see the 60's ahead not behind us. I can see the boozehounds dropping their cans of beer or Larger should I say, picking up the bong and widening there minds singing songs and making love instead of fighting and feuding and destroying their brains with drink. You see first I thought it was just another ego trip of mine but then I relised I am in fact what I always believed I was. You know what that is friends? The crowd chant back "@sshole". Limp smiles and then gives them the answer with open arms like a preacher. Limp- Yes I am a messiah. Oh yes Jesus came down, Quran came down and Krishna came down and now yes Limp. I am all you should be, all you should ever want to be. Just follow me and do what I do and the path will become clear. Oh yes Karina Wolfenden you do not only have the great power of Limp to contend with. Oh no, you have the great power of God himself! Limp pauses so the mixed reaction from the crowd dies down and then speaks once again. Limp- I can see that you do not believe me. Proof will come my friends. Not when I beat Karina Wolfenden, as you know that won't take a miracle. I will show my powers to the world. I will perform a miracle mark my words. For all will love and live in peace at any measure I see fit to take. "Get Up" hits the PA system. Limp drops the mic and slides under the bottom rope of the ring and skips up the entrance ramp. The crowd now as confused as when Limp entered the arena of how to react to him. As there King? As there Messiah? As the Hippy? As the old Limp? As the king of mind games? Or as a mad man?... Later... The scene opens at a door of a locker room in a UPWA arena. We can just make out a tuft of hair from which we can presume is an interviewer. On the door the name “Limp” can clearly be seen. The interviewer coughs and then knocks on the door. There is a silence for a second and a voice can be heard saying, “come in”. The interviewer opens the door. Inside we can see in center stage Limp sitting on a thrown type chair. Either side of him there are two people. One is grown man in a pink leotard and the other person, as best we can work out is a woman dressed as Charlie Chaplain. The woman has a bowl of grapes and the man has a big fan. Limp in the middle. He is wearing a smile, a leather jacket and a pair of swimming shorts. Limp takes out a packet of cigarettes flips one from the packet into his mouth in his usual way. He lights it takes a drag and then begins to speak before the interviewer can. Limp- Well howdy, what a week it has been. I promised you a special victory and that is what you will get. First I will beat Karina Wolfenden, then I will win the UPWA world title and embrace my new special friend. But you see I must start with Karina since PTC decided that they want to put me, the next UPWA world champ, the peace loving guy Limp into a first round Cruiserweight match against Karina Wolfenden. A match guaranteed to end in some serious injuries. I don’t know what they have been smoking up in head office, but I wouldn’t mind a bit of it. Limp pauses to put out his cigarette and then speaks again. Limp- But you see when I saw that light, it changed me it made me realize you know. I will prove on the biggest scale it can that love and peace will beat the hate; fear and jealously that Chezina and his high school brothers could ever bring. Limp pauses again to clear his throat. Limp- Now what will I do with my world title you may ask? Well you see I will throw it away. Now Chezina is a nice young chap but f*ck knows what he did with that title and after he took those pills I gave him f*ck knows what diseases he has. Suddenly after saying nothing for the whole promo the interviewer butts in. Interviewer- You said they were anti-something or other pills. Limp- Yes I said that to make him feel better. Interview- Right lets get this straight. You told him he had a serious diseases to make him feel better and then gave him some pills that might have given him serious diseases ? Limp smiles. Limp- Right now you’re getting it spanky. But don’t worry I will have a new World Title made. Chezina Vs Limp will be upon us soon enough. Hell even I am interested to find out how things will pan out. Make love not war my friends. Limp turns to the she male and she hands him some grapes. The scene fades with Limp laughing away piling grapes into his mouth. Later... The scene opens in the docklands in London. The camera scans the giant luxury apartments over looking the Thames. We can see from the angle of the camera that he must be on the balcony of one of these apartments. A voice is suddenly heard "see man I told you it was a good view". The camera sweeps around to see jolly looking Limp. He is wearing a pair of track suit trousers and a mucky looking gray T-shirt which was probably white to start off with. There are two large couches sitting in them are five blokes. They all look pretty spaced out darting their eyes around the room. Limp looks at one of the guys on the sofas who appears not to want to make eye contact with anyone. Limp- Hey Billy go get the camera man dude a cup of tea, he's had a long journey I'm sure he would like a cup. Billy smiles and speaks. Billy- Ah yeah cup of tea. Limp- No Billy not that kind of tea, just a normal cup of tetlies. Billy- Oh. Billy looks down, then goes back to worried looking expression and scuffles off to get the tea. Bret then takes the seat that Billy once sat in beside two other unknown spun out chaps. The cameraman finally speaks. Camera dude- errm so you want to do this? Limp- Sure thing skip. Camera- Are these guys alright? Limp- Yeah why you ask? Limp pats one of the unknown guys on the shoulder and he suddenly bolts out of his seat. Limp speaks in a reassuring way. Limp- Oh don't worry about Fred he has this phoebe about breathing and is a bit jumpy. But you know that's not why you came all the way to England now is it? The answer is no, or is it yes. I forget. Anyway lets gets down to it. At the first round of the Cruiserweight Tournament I have a little match. Well not really little, little in ranking or greatness compared to me I guess but you know there are going to be quite a few strapping young men in that tournament. You see where's the love in it? It's all back handed these type of tournament and as I'm the next UPWA world Champ, the greatest living legend in the XWF with the facts to back me up. Everyone is going to go for me, even Wonder Woman herself Karina Wolfenden. Limp pauses to take out a cigarette. Fred beside him pinches a cig out of the packet in Limp's hand. Limp seems slightly irritated but lets it go. Fred attempts to light his cig but seems to be having problems with his lighter as it is only producing sparks, but he continues to keep trying. Limp lights his cig and carries on talking. Limp- Now Chezina should I trust you or not? We have spent some great times together before in and out of the ring and secured many victories together. BUT you broke a once thriving friendship, you say it was all a scam. I don't believe you. Look into your heart Chezina the answer will be there. Billy finally comes in with the cup of tea Limp asked him to get. The tea appears to more milky than tea colored and the tea bag is still floating around in it. As he takes it over to the camera man he spills bits of it, the cameraman takes it from him, looks in it in a worried sort of fashion and sips a small bit. His face goes from a concerned look to a look as if he is about to be sick. Limp- Billy makes a good cup of tea ah? Limp does not let him answer and continues to speak while the cameraman begins to cough making the camera shake. Limp- Chezina! Lets talk about you and me and us. I don't know what happened man. You knew that you could not be world champ while I was around. You knew it but yet you seem to be upset at how things went. And the attack after the match well you know, it was just good old fashioned ribbing. You know you seem to like pain and all so hell I was doing you a favor! Limp pauses to put out his cigarette. Fred is still attempting to light his but seems to be enjoying himself watching the sparks fly from the lighter. The rest of the men sitting on the sofas now seem to have also taken their worried looks off their faces and appear to be also enjoying themselves watching the sparks fly on Fred's lighter. Limp clears his throat and begins to speak again. Limp- But you know I'm sure those wannabes in the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament will have something to say about me, being the favorite and all. But lets hope all these no names have the brains to know not to sound it off. Limp pauses then speaks once more. Limp- Anyway at the tournament I hope for good clean fights and at the end we can all shake hands and sing a song, whatever. Remember kids make love not war. Limp finishes there. Fred finally gets the lighter to light and a "woooow" can be heard all around the room. Fred is about to light his cigarette with it when it suddenly goes out again. So once again he continues to spark it. The scene fades out with the whole room staring at the sparks of the lighter aside from the cameraman who is looking for the exit and Limp who is looking at his shiny red shoes..
