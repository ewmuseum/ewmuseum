Three days of Interests!

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 Somewhere in the darkness a person moves as many sounds of banging and crashing can be heard. Someone says something under their breath not quite loud enough to be heard as another crash is heard and a loud voice rumbles in the room. “Son of a…Damn where is that light switch?” Suddenly a light switch flips on as Shaman turns and looks about. His face red with frustration, he turns and sits down at a desk and then pulls a pen out. He begins to write in his journal. He seems to furiously jot something down as he is concentrating on it and grins as he leans back and begins writing more. Seeming to be relaxing he pulls a beer from the small refrigerator near the desk. “Now this sure as hell beats training any day. I did all that this morning and heading into the week I am going to relax and enjoy myself.” He begins pushing his pen gracefully across the paper as he begins to write in his journal. Things definitely seem different at this moment as he seems way too relaxed for this purpose. His task at hand is coming. An opponent he intends on beating in the ring. His mind just flows to distant islands as he writes his thoughts in his journal. “Never thought of a Journal as relaxing, yet it strangely enough is.” His mind wanders as he begins to write more into the journal. DAY 1 Journal Entrée number one: My first opponent in the PTC tournament is a guy named Reginald Atkins. A mysterious free agent that hails from the SCW a federation that tends to flow around the Seventh Circle. Uhm just a side note here this guy may be a bit off his turnip thinking he is from hell or something, Just wanted to give myself a personal note on this. Now I have been training hard and severe these last few days. I have decided to give myself a bit of relaxation and fun these next few days. I am sure I deserve this and rest and relaxation will do me good. I have a new opponent who knows nothing about me other than the WWA’s personal Biography stuff. He can read all he wants, But the fact will remain he never has seen me in action and won’t know what to expect of me. The next course of business is to see how far I can go in this tournament. Reginald will be the first to feel the fury that is I. I am known as the Native American Bad Ass, It wasn’t a title chosen by me it was a title given by the fans and it has stayed. Simple and disturbing facts to be known about myself are that I like pain, I like to bleed, and I like to hurt my opponents in the ring. I am sure Reginald will have a few rude awakenings come game time. I wonder what he is thinking at the moment. Will he truly understand that he has bitten off more than he can chew here. This will not be a cake walk for him. No little stroll in the park. His mother should pack him some band aids with his lunch. He is going to miss supper. Journal it is nice to be able to relax and just get this off my chest rather than rant and rave about it on Television. I mean after all isn’t that what everyone else is doing? Really I think sometimes things are easier done through relaxation. Hell if I was a mobster I could hire lackeys and send them out against everybody while I sit back and chill in a hot tub and watch. Another note journal, hot tub should be considered in my immediate future. Invite Suzy she could use some down time. I think she is tired of being at home while I am on the road. Hell this guy even has multiple personalities. He talks to himself and he has little voices speaking to him about his actions. Sounds a lot like someone has to be subdued with medication. Well Journal I am off to bed. Need some sleep before I enjoy my next few days in the sun. [i/] Shaman leans back and shuts the light off as he stands in the darkness and walks towards the door. He seems rather slow as he is feeling his way through the darkness and opens the door and steps through as the door shuts with a click behind him as he makes his way down the hall. The phone suddenly rings and he smiles and picks it up. “Hey Odium man what’s going on? Not much here heading to bed. Yeah got to train tomorrow big match you know. Yeah I’ve been watching everything that’s been going on.” Rubbing his chin he listens and then cracks his neck rubbing it and raises an eyebrow. “The Bar you say? I will consider it tomorrow morning I am sure we will have a blast man. Sure meet you guys later.” Shaman walks into his bedroom and climbs into his massive bed and folds his pillow and lays his head on it falling asleep. Waking up and rolling over, I stare at the window where the sun is shining through brightly. Thinking to myself do I want to get up and go work? I shrug as I get up and head into the bathroom and take a shower. I realize I am singing in the shower, pretty bad when your house is a nuthouse with camera crews. I turn and grab the towel as I finish my shower. Walking across the room I sit down on a recliner and I put on sweat pants and a t-shirt. Today is the first day of relaxation I figure so why not enjoy it. Placing a coffee cup down near the chair is Suzy my current girlfriend. She is good to me and has stuck with me for a long ass period of time. I turn to her and smile. “Suzy could you bring me some of the cinnamon rolls. It would be much appreciated and also the wrestling magazines in the den.” Suzy looks over at me with a look of why don’t you do it yourself and then she looks at me and smiles. “All right then. You get to cook dinner this evening.” Smiling I lean back and pull out my journal once again, deciding to write what’s on my mind at this point in the morning. DAY 2: Journal, Woke up this morning thinking to myself why the hell do I need to get up. It is not everyday that a man gets to relax and sleep in. Then again I guess I can relax and watch some old tapes of people in the tournament. Kick back with a cup of java and some pastries. Learn what I need to about my opponents. See who will continue and who will fail. I have been studying this Reginald fellow a bit. Free Agent I believe he is. Something baffles me about him. The fact that he is two sandwiches short of a picnic doesn’t bother me. The fact that he joined the tournament in the first place does. Now I simply ask myself why does he show up and take the chance to be eliminated by me. Well this is the morning and I want to simply kick back and enjoy it. I think I will return and write more this afternoon while I am relaxing in the pool or hot tub. [i/] Picking up my coffee cup I head into the kitchen to join Suzy before she brings all the stuff I asked for back. My mind is reeling with the possible outcomes of everything that is set before me in the Flinders Brackett. “Suzy I am heading to the kitchen.” Entering the kitchen I sit at my breakfast bar and relax kicking back and turn on the weather channel thinking about the people watching the Hurricane in the Gulf of Mexico. Lounging around the pool he can be seen kicked back relaxing with a bottle of tequila in one hand and Suzy laying to his right with her beautiful tan body glistening in the sun. Looking over at her he smiles as he speaks. “You know Suzy this is the first time in months we have been able to relax and enjoy our time together. I am glad I have decided to do this. Your worth a lot to me, I was just being stupid about the whole thing lately.” Suzy rolls over and looks at him and smiles sweetly as she speaks. “If you’re growing soft on me you better not. I liked the man who is a bad ass and kicks ass and takes names later. You are the man I met so don’t start trying to get mushy on me now!” Shaman slams down the bottle of tequila and stands up stretching. “You are absolutely right Susan. I think I do need to take care of a few things. In fact I think I need to make a phone call.” Picking up his cell phone Shaman dials a number and waits. “Hey Odium my man I think we need to talk. I need to ask you a few questions. Yeah yeah I know your training. Just trust in me man I know what I am doing. Yes Genesis for life or ever. Uhm Chucks doing what? Ok uhm try and call Toxic and ask him what the hell Chuck is doing this time! Thanks man I will catch up with you later. Oh yeah won’t be able to make it to the bar. Suzy and I have some plans. Heh get your mind out of the gutter man, Later Odium good luck with your match.” Hanging up the phone he reaches over and grabs his Journal. As Suzy looks over and shakes her head and then closes her eyes laying her head back down on her own arms. “Just a few notes Suze and we will be good to go.” Day 2 Afternoon: Journal, I am enjoying my time with Suzy this afternoon beside the pool. Reginald is still on my mind though. He seems to tear into my relaxation at the worst possible moments. Suzy tells me to ignore it and just focus on the training and get into his head and understand who he is. The problem is he is an enigma of sorts. He hides behind the paperwork of his federation. He doesn’t reveal who he is to nobody. Is he a man or is he a mouse. I will enjoy making him suffer. After all do I not like making my opponents hurt. I am Shaman the most sadistic Son of a Bitch to ever walk the face of the WWA. Why not spread the wealth here and beat down my opponents in the tournament? That is a question that deserves to be delved into a lot more in the next few hours. Perhaps I should question the mere sanity of our dear Reginald. The man seems to be losing his grip on this reality. Starting now I will do what is needed to make sure I end up on top. There is a goal and a prize at the end of this rainbow that I intend on capturing. Even if it means crushing some poor schmoe named Reginald. [i/] Placing the journal down Shaman turns and heads back towards Suzy and lays down near her on a different pool chair and he leans back popping open a cold beer. “It is going to be a nice few days after all Suzy. I think they will turn out just right. Tomorrow will be day three of the vacation and the final day Suze lets make the best of it.” Suzy props herself up on her arms, smiles and leans offer kissing him softly on the cheek. “That sounds good.” Shaman grins and leans back watching the sun begin to set on the horizon. His mind wanders across many thoughts. “This day is a good day. Tomorrow should be much better. So what do you want for Dinner Suzy?” Shaman smiles as he picks up his cell phone dialing out for a number. “Sukiyaki Garden yeah I want delivery of your Manchurian Buffet. Yes that is a delivery not a take out. This is a vacation and a lot of relaxation damn it don’t think I have mentioned to you guys before, but there is a big tip in this for the people who make it happen and quickly.” Shaman hangs up the cell phone and kicks back relaxing as he drinks his cold beer slowly. Looking as if he is in deep thought staring out at the road signs and telephone poles pass by. Shaman seems to be in deep thought with his feet propped up. Taking a cup of coffee slowly bringing it to his lips he sips it lightly continuing his thought process. He looks ahead up the road and notices a few kids in a playground as they pass it. Thinking to himself so innocent untouched by the world of greed and snap decisions. The world not affecting the lives they have in their own worlds. Allowing their parents to worry about everything and providing for them. Turning he finishes his coffee and sets it in the cup holder and looks out the window again as he speaks. “Hey Ernie, when are we stopping next?” Hearing a yell from the front of the tour bus he listens closely. “In few miles we need to fuel up!” Satisfied he leans back and watches the road pass by. Continuing to watch each line, each telephone pole, and each car pass the bus by, curious how much more relaxed one man can be at driving to face his future against an unknown opponent. Questions begin to roll through his thoughts. As the bus begins to slow and downshift he looks out the window and notices a small town with a few people. The town seems to be a town that hasn’t been touched since the 50’s. Smiling he stands and makes his way to the front of the tour bus, looking over at Suzy as she sleeps he continues past her and lets her sleep. Stepping up near the driver and waits and as the tour bus completely stops he steps out onto the dusty road. Looking down as he smiles and he speaks. “The road less traveled.” Looking across the street he makes his way towards a mom and pop grocery store. Probably in this town for as long as the town existed he thinks. His mind wanders to his childhood, a reservation much like this small town. His mother remarried another Native American where he moved outside Las Vegas Nevada near Death Valley. Sitting with a ground of teenagers Andrew looks about and awaits his turn to be passed the marijuana. Smoking as a teen was an odd experience for him. He took a long hit and then turned and looked at the Old man across the street shaking his head at him as he watched Andrew became disturbed and concerned he would be in trouble. Standing up he walks across the way to the old mans tin house. He reaches up and offers the man the joint. In the best way he knows to try and admit he is wrong. The other teenagers turn and runaway fearing they would be in trouble as well. Andrew looks at the man seems to be close to bearing his soul to him for forgiveness. The old man holds his hand up taking the joint and crushes it letting it scatter to the wind as he speaks. “This no longer exists so evidence is gone of what you have done. Now if you are ready to listen I am ready to teach you some things you can take to your future with you.” Listening Andrew walks with the man into the house and looks all around as the man has pictures of wolves, bears, and coyotes in his house. The Old man sits in an old wooden rocking chair and speaks as it makes loud noises back and forth. The old man speaks as his hands wave in odd circles and gestures keeping Andrew’s attention. “Let me tell you of a story young man. It is about a Coyote and a Fox. The Coyote was a fast and cunning individual, But the Fox was also fast and cunning. One day the Fox and Coyote made a challenge to each other. They wanted to see who could challenge the Fire Spirit and cause him to leave his mountain of fire.” Turning his Gaze Andrew watched and listened as the Old man knew he had gotten his attention. “They both agreed to the challenge and both knew the path to the Fire spirit was dangerous as well as being in the presence of such great power could destroy them. The Fix decided to go first. The Coyote agreed to this because he knew the secret he had all along. The fox quickly ran up the path and disappeared for three days on end. Before the Fire Spirit he spoke of his plight. The Fire spirit bade him welcome as they spoke of the days to come. The Fox listened carefully and followed the Fire Spirits instructions and at first strike of the moons beams upon the sand stood atop a plateau and his image cast upon the desert below a billowing flame appeared behind him. The Fire Spirit enjoyed the game that was being played. He awaited the Coyote’s visit, the Coyote who was a friend of his. The Old man smiled as Andrew was listening intently. He knew this young man could become a great man if he chose the right paths. “The Coyote went on the next day at first sun up. He visited with his friend the Fire Spirit after a few days of wandering through the desert. He explained about the deal that he and the Fox had made. The Fire Spirit pondered upon this and smiled as he spoke of something. The Coyote nodded in agreement as he made his way to the destiny of choice atop a plateau. On top of the Plateau the first moon beams spread across the desert making the desert bright. The Coyote was shadowed by the large moon rising behind him as he howled. The Fire Spirit watched from his mountain and laughed as he made it known the Coyote and Fox had separate destinies.” Looking at Andrew the Old man smiled once again knowing this time his words had finally sunk in. “The Coyote knows his place and future as the Fox searches for his path as he wanders the desert under the sun and watchful eye of the Fire Spirit.” Andrew sits and watches the Old man as he grows silent just watching and looking at the young man before him with a relaxed expression. Shaman quietly exits the store as his mind relives the tale told to him when he was a child. He notices a child running around frantically searching for something as he suddenly runs up to him and speaks way to fast to understand any word he was saying. Shaman kneels to one knee and presses the child’s shoulder gently. “Calm down take a deep breathes and tell me what is going on?” The child takes a few deep haggard breaths as he sobs in between. “The….the man is beating my dog. I tried to stop him and he hit me!” Shaman stood with a lot of anger forming in his face as he looked down at the boy. “Show me the way son.” The boy quickly bolted away as Shaman quickly followed. They rounded a corner and behind a building a man was beating a dog with a belt. The dog was yelping loudly and whimpering after each consecutive hit. Shaman saw this and grew angrier than he had ever been before. He stopped the boy and motioned him to stand back away from this. Shaman walked forward and reached out and snatched the belt out of his hand. The man turned and faced Shaman at barely chest level. The anger quickly turned to fear as he realized what he was facing. Shaman spoke calmly and very clearly as his anger flowed with each carefully chosen word he spoke. “Why don’t you pick on someone or something that is going to fight back? Like myself here.” Shaman quickly grabbed the man and slammed him against the wall. With a wide reach back Shaman began beating the man with his own belt. Shaman hit as hard as he could with each swing of the leather strap. Each hit raised new bruises to the surface. Leaving welts upon the man that would seem very scary to any person observing this. Shaman stopped as the man dropped sobbing and crying like a babe. Looking at the man he threw the belt at him and spoke clearly. “You have choices to make. This path was wrong and perhaps I am giving you the second chance to correct your mistake or I could simply be in a gracious mood and allowing you a chance to run away very fast!” As the man gets up slowly he looks at Shaman and runs off down the road leaving the boy and dog alone. Shaman looks at the boy and looks him over making sure he wasn’t hurt too severely and then smiles and rubs the kids head reaching into his pocket pulling out a couple hundred dollar bills and hands them to the boy. “Here go bring your dog to the vet and make sure he is ok. That part of someone’s life should have never happened. Learn from this and make it an example on which you’re going to be when you grow up.” The boy nodded and ran over to his dog and picked him up and walked away from Shaman calling back towards him. “Thank you, mister!” Shaman smiled and turned to walk away as he ran directly into the Old man from his childhood. He stared in bewilderment as he thought the Old man had died long ago. The Old Man began to speak as he looked at Shaman. “What have you learned Fox?” Shaman grinned as he looked at the Old man. “The Coyote has learned that the path he has chosen is and always will be the correct path for him. I am a warrior first and proud of whom I am. No more hiding behind false pretenses and pressures of the world.” The Old man nods and speaks again. “You have chosen the path that was meant for you. I am glad to see you are doing well Andrew.” Smiling he looks at the Old man and responds. “I am glad to see you are still with me Grandfather.” Turning to look at the direction the boy had gone he smiles as he turns and looks back to where the Old man was he notices he was gone. Perhaps he was never there in the first place. Turning he walks back to the tour bus and climbs aboard as Suzy looks at him and smiles as she speaks as the tour bus begins to roll down the highway once again. “Did you find anything of interest?” Sitting down as he grabs a Coke and opens it up and he speaks. “No Suze just ghosts,” Sighing softly with a happy feeling going through him. “Just some old ghosts finally put to rest.” Shaman quietly pulls the Journal out and opens it up and begins to write in it. Day 3: Journal I have confronted many demons in my past. They have become more prominent in my future as I have questioned many things about myself. I have answered many questions over these last few months and I have come to realize being true to myself was more important than losing myself in all that I do. My quest for personal peace has come to an end and has set me on a path facing a man named Reginald Atkins. A man who seems to think I am nothing. I have respect for my opponent. He has in ring abilities and a drive to win. He fights for something other than himself or his multiple personalities. Yet he does not realize I am fighting for more than what is about myself. I am fighting for my heritage, my pride, and my honor. Something’s in life are just as important as other things. What is important to him may not be as important to me. Reginald will be in for one helluva fight when he comes to fight me. I am no coward and I never shall be. Regardless of who wins or loses it will be a day he will never forget. This is my last Journal Entrée. Closing the journal Shaman turns and looks at Suzy and smiles as he turns and looks out the window. “Suzy, Reginald Atkins has already underestimated me for our match. Now the proper course of actions for him would be to relax and take a breather from training. I want him at top form when I come to beat him within an inch of his life in the ring. His history and who he is means little to me. I don’t give a damn about him and his pet boy. This is simply business now and the first order of business is to bring the fight to you Reginald.” Shaman kicks back and smiles. “Now for some relaxation while we ride Suzy.” Quietly kicking back Suzy joins him as they watch the world roll by on the tour bus.
