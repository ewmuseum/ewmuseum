Am i really extreme?

26 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Its funny what time does to a person, just a single year can totally change a person’s life, such as Moraless. If you look at his recent history, a lot has gone on and a lot has changed. One year past, Moraless was simply a nobody, someone who was fighting jobbers left right and centre in the UWF, fighting to defend his television title for all it was worth, a title which rather ironically, no longer exists. A year later though things have changed. Moraless is now I recognised name, not the likes of Seymour Almasy, Chris Storm or Mike Marvellous but rustlings are begin to appear. Just small underground whispers about the man from UWF who is beginning to take things into his own hands, who has started shouting his mouth off becoming more and more confident in his abilities and is now setting his eyes on a goal much higher than the UWF television title. The PTC Extreme Title. This is something that Moraless, had had his eyes on for quite a while, watching from the shadows as it passed from one member to another from Fathom to Marvellous in matches that were full of aggression and blood lust, matches which hold no boundaries, matches which were the true example of what extreme could and should be and deep down this thrilled Moraless. He had been at all of the events as a spectator, still recuperating from his incident in which he rammed a knife through his own stomach, hell you know how it is sometimes. He watched the fire and the passion that flowed through the competitors in the bouts and he knew he had to be a part of it, even if his body would not allow it. Of course things don’t always work out as planned now do they? He had called up and asked about the possibility of getting a shot at the new champion Mike Marvellous, the timing would be perfect, no-one else would have shown an interest yet and he would be able to take on a weakened Marvellous turning things in his favour. It wasn’t that Moraless was looking for an easy way out, not in the slightest it was just that he was far from one hundred percent fit yet, his stomach wound was still healing he could last about ten minutes in the ring without feeling fatigued then it became an up hill struggle. Every breath burning through his body, as it tried to shut down, but Morlaess did not allow it, something always kept him from giving in, his desire to win. A small office which contains the bare essentials of a filing cabinet, chair, heavily varnished wooden table and a telephone. An office in which a woman with shoulder length brown hair is sat tapping her fingers lightly across the table. In front of her stands a man just over six feet in height with a large scar under his right eye, his arms are folded as he stares down at the woman who is reading a piece of A4 paper in front of her, which has the word ‘application’ in large bold letters across the top. Woman: I’m very sorry John, but there are others who are better suited to compete for the title ahead of you at present. Moraless winced slightly as the woman read his name, he hated being called his birth name, it gave him a sense of being, a sense of identity which he hated. He always liked being the man in the shadows, the man who had secrets kept just to himself. Woman: It’s already been confirmed that Sean Mcclancy, ICE and Noah Wilson of the first to third seeds respectively for the title, I’m afraid the fact that you have been out of action for sometime puts you quite a way down the list. Moraless sighed deeply rubbing his forehead with his right hand, he had totally forgotten about the other participants in the match at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, of course they would be considered first, that had proved themselves already. Woman: Then when you consider the fact that others like Lacuna Debris, Jade and Jason Marah have already shown an interest in the Extreme title I’m afraid that it’s looking somewhat bleak of you getting a title shot anytime in the near future. Moraless’ eyes widened with each name released, he hadn’t realised exactly how prized a possession just a shot at this title actually was. He nodded almost regretfully before turning on his heels and heading for the door of the office, head hung low as he did so. He knew he had been a fool to even contemplate attempting something like this so soon into his return. In retrospect it would probably be for the best. He could sit back and observe for a bit, get a greater status in UWF and heal fully. Woman: John, hold on a minute I’m not done. Moraless shivered once more at the call of his name, if it wasn’t someone who could decide to make his life very difficult he would have more than likely turned around and pimp slapped her, instead however he turns around keeping one hand on the door knob as the woman continues to speak. Woman: What with all of these new interests in Mr Marvellous’ title the committee has decided that to get through all of the want to be contenders and assign rankings and matches would take a life time and that’s not what the extreme title is about. It’s about taking it to the limits, making everything that little bit higher up the ladder, one more step up the rung. Today’s public get bored of seeing straight technical matches they want to see blood. Moraless: I’m sorry were you actually getting to a point? The woman freezes and blushes slightly, her cheeks turning a nice rosey pink colour as she coughs and composes herself once more. Woman: What would you say if I told you that you were going to be in a match to determine the number one contender for the extreme title? Moraless’ eyes lit up, he had never in a million years dreamed that it would happen like this so quickly, he stepped forwards away from the door and sat down in front of the woman, wincing slightly moving a hand cautiously towards his stomach resting it on it lightly as a way to stop the pain slightly. Moraless: That would be fantastic, but you said yourself what about all the other contenders won’t they all be pissed off that I get to go in ahead of them. The woman behind the desk let out an overly loud laugh which caused Moraless to sink in his chair slightly, the truth was beginning to dawn on him that it wasn’t going to be that simple; it wasn’t going to be the simple in the slightest. Composing herself the woman smiles sweetly at Moraless’ whose face had faded from one of happiness to one of doubt and worry. Woman: I’m sorry John, you seem to have misunderstood me, what I was saying is that all of those who have voiced an interest in the extreme title are to be placed in a match with each other all at once… Moraless cringed before the woman could even finish he knew the three words that were coming next and he wished that it wasn’t going to be true, gingerly touching his stomach again thoughts dashed through his brain, there was no way he would be able to last in that type of match, especially if he got a low draw. His body couldn’t take it, please god just don’t let it be… Woman: A royal rumble match. Moraless: Shit. Something however began to stir in the back of Moraless’ mind, sure a royal rumble style bout would be gruelling and intense, hell it would probably be more than he could handle in his current state, but something bothered him. The woman had spoken at great length about how extreme was taking it to the next level, yet in the scheme of things a royal rumble seemed quite tame. Moraless: Excuse my ignorance for asking but a royal rumble isn’t exactly extreme now is it? A wry smile formed on the face of the woman, in fact it could almost have been described as evil Moraless’ head limped forward as he let out a long unrelenting sigh, he had to go and ask the question didn’t he. He couldn’t let sleeping dogs lie, he had to go and unleash the beast. Woman: You know that is exactly what myself and other members of the committee felt so we thought that it would be in the interest of all those involved, the fans and shareholders so we decided to spice things up a little. Moraless sinks further into his chair. Woman: To make things more edgy. Further down. Woman: More extreme. Almost on the floor now. Woman: The royal rumble will be under extreme rules, anything and everything will go so if you want the spot in the bout you are more than welcome to it, although if you are having a bit of trouble. The woman looked at Moraless’ stomach, clearly his subtle attempts to hide the pain, emanating from it had not been that great. Moraless has a choice to make, one that would make or possibly break him, literally. He knew that his body would not be up for the bout, especially not in extreme conditions. However he also knew that this was possibly going to be his last shot in quite a while, the rumble would determine future contenders, eyes would be on them to see if they actually fitted the extreme motif. In his heart of hearts he knew what he had to do and there was no-way that he could consider against it. Moraless: I’m in.
