Life After Dusk: An Infinite Gauntlet Special It's Saturday evening and you have pizza sitting on the counter. You take a slice of it and immediately take a big bite. You look up at the clock to see what time it is and notice that it's half past eight. In a few hours you'll be leaving the house to get ready to hit some clubs and talk to the fine ladies at them. Yet, until then you're stuck in the house just relaxing till that time comes. Hanging up in your closest are a pair of jeans and a nice shirt that you think will impress said ladies. However, for now, you sit down and turn on the television. Immediately, you pull up the Channel Guide on your DVR to see what's airing tonight. You flip through the channels and land in your wrestling package where the Primetime Central channel is showing some classic matches like the GTT6 finals. However, before it airs there seems to be a blank spot at the 8:30 slot that could be quite interesting as anything is capable of running on the PTC channel.. You hit the select button on your remote and at first there's just blackness. Then, the camera fades in and you see a man that at first you don't recognize. Yet, as you look at him, you notice that it's none other than PRIME and PTC superstar, Dusk. Dusk: Good evening, PTC world. I hate going into this short spiel, but let's have it. If you've been tuning into PRIME or even the good ol' FW universe then you know that I have a new television show that follows my life airing on NBC in a few short weeks. Trust me, I'm very excited though my demeanor would say otherwise at this point. Just having one hell of a week as I prepare for my Universal Title shot in PRIME. So, the execs over at NBC told me that not only should I do a preview for PRIME, but that I should do one for PTC. I told them that I'd be cool with that as I'm scheduled for the Infinite Gauntlet and all of that jazz. So, if you're a little lost then now you should be clued in. Dusk continues to drive through the city as he then makes a turn. Dusk: Right now, I'm in Los Angeles which seems to have become my new home as of late. Many people watch wrestlers these days and think that's all that we do, train and wrestle. Yet, that's not true at all. Some of us have actually decided to turn our good fortunes around and invest them in other areas of our life. The truth is that eventually we won't be able to wrestle any longer either by choice or not. So for me, I've decided to invest some money into a couple of different areas as PRIME has blessed me with a nice sum of money over the past ten months as well as my money from PTC and FW. Two of those areas would be a restaurant and a night club that I'm in the process of building right now. Yes, a night club and a restaurant. Odd, wouldn't you say? You would as you sit back and continue to watch this. Dusk: Yeah, I know, out of the realm of a wrestler who competes week in and out against the likes of Tony Gamble, and this week having to compete against a man I can't stand in Sonny Silver in the Infinite Gauntlet and some others who I don't have the time or energy to look up. Yet, it's what I love to do. I love being able to go to a nice restaurant, have some live music, and just enjoy myself. Likewise, I enjoy going to a club and being able to relax as well. Okay, maybe the first one and not so much the second one, but it could be exciting none the less. One of the reasons I got picked up for the show is because of the fact that I'm opening a restaurant and club so there's other avenues for them to explore as I go forward through the first season of Life After Dusk. Dusk then glances over at the camera and seemingly at you. He cracks a smile. Dusk: Trust me, I didn't pick the name. Marketing and surveys came up with it. I just take what they throw at me. He then pulls into a parking spot and slowly gets out of the car. The camera continues to follow him as he walks towards a rather elegant little spot that can only be his restaurant. Dusk: I know, I talk a lot. It's what happens when I start getting nervous. Not something that I can control really, and I know that you're at home asking yourself, Dusk gets nervous? Yeah, I do. The biggest week of my life, what else am I supposed to do? He continues to walk towards the establishment that has a coming soon banner across it. As you walk inside, you're struck by the beauty of it all. He actually has a restaurant and you can't believe it. You think to yourself that you might just have to book yourself a ticket to Los Angeles to take a look at this place and maybe even eat here. Dusk: This is it. You like? I hope so. I spent three weeks designing the inside of it and making sure it had a great feel to it. I thought about going with a different theme for each thing, but I didn't like that idea. Over there... Dusk then points to a stage that has nothing on it. Dusk: We're going to have live music every night. It's just one of the things that I love about a restaurant if they have it. I've got a jazz band coming out here and then some nights where we'll be showcasing new talent. I'm going to seriously doubt we'll have somebody like T-Pain out here. Just not that kind of place. Then you hear something ringing. You check your phone, thinking that it's you, but it's actually Dusk's phone going off. He looks at it and sighs at the name on the caller ID screen. Dusk: You know, this is why we can't do this show live. Because my agent will call me and then decide to harass me. Hold on one second, sorry. He then puts the phone to his ear. Dusk: What do you want Joshua? Joshua:: Look man, the restaurant is a nice bit, but that's more for your 18-49 crowd who make more than $60,000 a year. In other words, the non-wrestling crowd. Dusk: Good way to insult my current audience, Joshua. Joshua:: I know, it's not fair to them, and I know that it's insulting to them. Yet, they're a different crowd that you're trying to please. Showing them your restaurant and talking to them about how it's going to be is all dandy and stuff, but you're on the PTC channel live right now and they want you to talk about your upcoming match. Not about your restaurant. NBC execs are counting on you to bring this audience with you and they'll supply the other half of the audience. Dusk: Look, when you told me about this idea you said that I'd have complete control on what I do with it all. Joshua:: And you will, but this is a different beast. This right here isn't the show that's airing in a few weeks time. That will be your show and that will be the show that they want to air. This though is to get those who already know your name to get excited about the show. So, drop some of the restaurant talk and go for more of the wrestling talk. Dusk then sighs again as he looks at the camera. Dusk: Fine, so you want me to talk about how Sonny Silver is a cocksucker who can't grab a victory if he tried? Joshua:: See! There you go, go down that road. Dusk: And what about these no names that I've never heard of? Steven Daugherty? The Illusionist? Joshua:: Oh, I don't know... Dusk: Exactly. I can't go around talking about them as if I know them. That's only going to bore the viewers, you know? Joshua:: I see where you're coming from... Dusk: Good, I hope so. They don't care about those two as it is some mentioning them is just going to put them to sleep. Just how these things work. Joshua:: I think you have a good point. Dusk: See, now we're on the same page. On top of that, really, do we want to talk about how Silver was reportedly berated by his mother at the Borgata? Or how about that kid that got the better of Sonny? I mean, come on, Silver had his day in the sun, and I do mean a day. He lost to Troy twice and really nobody is picking him to do well in this thing. Joshua:: Okay, Craig, you've convinced me. Go on with your show. Dusk: Thanks Josh. Joshua:: No prob, Craig. Dusk: And Josh? Joshua:: Yeah? Dusk: Don't ever tell me how to do this again, okay? Joshua:: Yes, Craig. Dusk then hangs up the phone and looks back at the camera. Dusk: My annoying agent, Joshua. Trust me, I don't plan on having him actually show up on the show. However, I might not be able to keep control of him calling me. Oh, let me introduce to the person who will be managing my restaurant in my stead. Dusk then points to a woman off screen and in walks a rather beautiful lady with long brown hair and brown eyes. She's wearing a white tank top that shows off her... body and a pair of jeans. She's also wearing a pair of glasses that increases her sexiness factor. Dusk: This is Nicole and she'll be running the restaurant since I obviously can't be here all the time. Say hello to the crowd. Nicole: Hey guys. Dusk: Did you want to talk about the restaurant at all? Nicole: Sure. What do you want me to say? Dusk: You could start with that piece of paper I emailed you yesterday. Nicole: Oh, the one where you're a God in a man's body? Or how about you've been endowed with a penis larger than the Empire St-- Dusk: Okay! That's enough from Nicole. Nicole then starts to laugh as she taps Dusk on the shoulder. Nicole: Honestly though, Craig here has a vision for the restaurant that I'm just trying to make sure that I keep up, you know? Even though the place comes off incredibly classy and expensive, it's not as he really wanted to market it to the everyday person. You should be able to bring your dates here and the two of you should be able to have a nice meal and live entertainment without having to pay an arm and a leg to do so. We're in a nice district of Los Angeles as they remodel portions of downtown to bring in more money and better businesses. Dusk: Thanks, I appreciate that. And when do you think we'll be able to open? Nicole: Our start date is the middle of October with a soft launch on October 1st. Dusk: Sounds great. I've got to say, Nicole has been doing an amazing job as I've been travelling like a crazy person. I couldn't have chosen a better person and I'm sure that this won't be the last you see of her as I can only imagine that marketing will have her on the show every week. Nicole: As long as they pay me. Oh, and I'll keep wearing these revealing tank tops. Dusk: Thanks, how about you not? Nicole then laughs as she walks away and Dusk keeps walking towards the back. Dusk: The funny thing is that PRIME is very big on marketability right now. It's part of the reason you see somebody like Sonny Silver out there. The only problem with that is that Sonny isn't marketable. He's a bitter son of a bitch who feels like he's owed everything in the world just because he's been wrestling for seventy years. Dusk then walks into the back of the restaurant where you see a rather large kitchen. It's empty right now. Dusk: Yeah, no chefs here today. I have hired a head chef though that is in the process of hiring some other chefs that will work under him. Honestly, I've left the menu in his hands as I'm not going to touch that area. He knows the guidelines and the clientele that we'll be handling here, but I'm excited as he comes highly recommended. That normally means I'll have to fire him in the first month and then I'll have to cook. And trust me, you don't want me cooking anything. Then, you hear a ringing sound again. This time though, you don't even pick look at the phone as it's obviously Dusk's. He picks it up and sighs again before answering it. Dusk: Ladies and Gentlemen, Lisa Tyler of PRIME fame. What do you want, Lisa:? Lisa:: Look, I know you're in the middle of doing your PTC show, but could you take some time to hype up the upcoming PRIME PPV? Dusk: Are you serious? Lisa:: I know that it's extremely cheap to d-- wait, why am I on speaker phone?! Dusk: So people can see how annoying you are. Lisa:: Craig, I'm your boss. Dusk: I thought Blaine was the boss. Lisa:: I'm your boss. He's my boss. Dusk: Right. Look, Lisa, you want me to talk about the upcoming PPV, Joshua wants me to talk about the Infinite Gauntlet-- Lisa:: Look, I don't care what Joshua wants from you. I'm the one that signs your checks. Dusk: Correction, you're one of the people who signs my checks. I've got checks coming in from several different areas. Lisa:: Okay, I've been corr-- Dusk: Hold on Lisa, I've got another call. Dusk then hits a button his cell phone and another call comes through. Dusk: Ladies and gentlemen, my manager, Kate. What's going down, Kate? Kate: I have to apologize for calling you in the middle of your live show as well. Dusk: Don't worry about it, you're cute, Lisa's not. Then you hear Kate laughing through the phone as well. Dusk: What's going down? Kate: Look, can you get to my office tonight after the show is done? Dusk: Yeah, what's up? Kate: Just need you to sign those documents that I think you'd rather not have me discuss over the air. Dusk then looks up at the camera with a panicked look on his face. Dusk: Let me clarify now, it's nothing having to do with my sexual ability or preference. Kate, could you make it sound any less image damaging? Kate: Oh, yeah, it has to do with an appearance coming up soon. Dusk: Thank you. Kate: No problem. So, will you be able to make it? Dusk: Of course. I'll go ahead and wrap this up as it's just supposed to be a small fifteen minute piece before the re-airing of the GTT6 Finals. Kate: Okay, thanks. Then, you hear a click as Dusk switches over to the other line. Dusk: Where were we? Lisa:: You put me on hold for that? Dusk: Look, if I tell the audience to tune in next Sunday for PRIME's Dices, Dollars, and Tragedy featuring a ten man Roulette match for the Universal Title featuring the likes of Lindsay Troy, Sonny Silver, Danny Ferguson, myself, and others as well as a 5-Star title match between Jason Snow and Munson Monsoon and an Intense Title match with Devin Shakur and Wade Elliot, will you shut up and go away? There's silence as it's obvious that Dusk has embarrassed Tyler. Lisa:: ...yes. Dusk: Thanks, bye. Then, Dusk puts the phone in his pocket as he looks at the camera. Dusk: Okay, enough with the annoying people. I've got to run, and maybe, just maybe, I'll get to do another one of these before the show airs for you guys, one that is less filled with phone calls. Anyways, September 24th, tune into NBC at 10PM, after that hot show called Heroes, and watch Life After Dusk. This has been my restaurant, which still needs a name I should mention, and my life. Oh, and watch the Infinite Gauntlet tomorrow night as I earn the right to face Tchu. Next time, maybe something a little more interesting as according to my agent you guys have no interests outside of wrestling according to my agent. Right… Peace. Dusk then walks away as the camera fades to black.
