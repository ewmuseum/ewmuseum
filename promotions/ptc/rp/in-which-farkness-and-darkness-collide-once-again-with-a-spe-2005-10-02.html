In Which Farkness And Darkness Collide Once Again With A Special Guest Appearance By Another Teammate

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-10-02 The skies over the Domicile of Darkness were as black and cold as the icy sting of Hades himself. Indeed, the landscape surrounding the ominous structure appeared to have been ravaged and strangled by death’s infectious aura. The landscape was marked by a macabre waltz of crooked trees and dead bushes. The building itself ascended into the thick darkness borne by its namesake, blending perfectly as if they were one. A fevered chill gripped the night air, draping a skyward blanket of clouds over the scene like a thick burial shroud. No man, woman, or child with any kind of common sense or self-concern for their well-being would dare to venture out on a night such as this, much less stroll up to the doors of the Domicile. But that’s exactly what Saij, with his child strapped to his chest in a baby harness, was doing. Seriously, does that really come as a surprise? Saij wore his trademark goofy grin as he walked the pathway leading to the front steps of the Domicile, impossibly oblivious to his surroundings. The apple, it seemed, truly did not fall very far from the tree, as Saij’s baby cooed peacefully and clapped while they approached the entrance. “Here we are, kiddo!” Saij said with a smile as they stopped in front of the door. “The Domicile of Darkness! Man, this place is pretty farkin’ huge. I sure hope Jack is home.” It was certainly a strange thing for anyone to say—hoping that The Ripper was in close proximity, much less hoping for an audience with the man who was once the widely feared scourge of XUW. Then again, Saij certainly wasn’t known for his intelligence. He reached out and pressed the doorbell, covered in dust and cobwebs from years of nonuse. What sounded next was not the chiming of bells, but rather the high-pitched terrified screams of a grown man facing impending, horrible doom. “Ha-ha, these novelty doorbell rings sure add character to a home,” Saij chuckled like an idiot. No more than a moment later, the giant oak door creaked open. Spiders scurried about, disturbed by the sudden motion, as the wrinkled and insipid figure of Sirius Smythe appeared, dripping wet and clad only in a thick, scarlet bathrobe that seemed to weigh more than he did. He scowled, almost with hatred, as he instantly recognized the young man standing before him. “You,” he croaked with disdain. “Heya Sirius, long time no see!” Saij said wholeheartedly. “How’ve you been? I haven’t seen you around XUW since I kicked Pete’s farkin’ arse a couple of years ago.” Saij could tell through the old man’s hollow cheeks that Sirius was clenching his teeth. It was strange to think that it had already been two years since the last time the One-Man Army of Farkness had crossed paths with his old rival, M V Pete, who had been a protégé of both Sirius and The Ripper during his tenure in XUW. After many battles with each other, Saij finally managed to settle the score with Pete in an “I Quit” match that saw Saij retain the XUW World Championship—and leaving a bitter taste in the mouth of Sirius Smythe, which considering his disposition was saying something. Sirius loathed many people, and Saij was one of them in particular. “Bah! It hasn’t been long enough, and I’ll thank you not to remind me of… of that, again,” he spat. “Uh, sorry about that,” Saij snickered. “Anyway, why are you like, all wet and stuff? I didn’t catch you in the shower, did I?” “I’ll have you know I was upstairs on the third floor, relaxing in the Whirlpool of Darkness,” Sirius said tartly. “But then you showed up at the door, and interrupted me. Now, I can only surmise the purpose of your visit, so I shall make this brief. You may not see Cereal Killer. Now get the hell out of here!” Saij scratched his head in confusion. “Well uh,” he started, as his baby playfully kicked around in the harness, “I’m not here to see CK. I’m actually here to see Jack.” Sirius raised an eyebrow. “You came out here, to the Domicile of Darkness, on purpose…” His eyes darted down momentarily to Saij’s baby, who was looking around at the landscape with genuine amazement and curiosity. “…and you brought your child with you, because you wanted to see The Ripper?” “Well, I’m not here for my health,” said Saij. “That much is obvious,” Sirius muttered. “Normally I wouldn’t waste my time, but… well, if that’s really what you want, far be it from me to deprive you of your complete and utter destruction.” Saij wasn’t really paying attention to what Sirius was saying, as he had engaged in baby-speak conversation with his infant child. “Ga-ba-boo-boo?” he asked with a broad smile, as his baby laughed. “Boo-ga-ba-doo-boo?” Sirius sighed. “I’ll be right back.” “Sweet, thanks a bunch Sirius!” Saij said with delight. Sirius turned, paused, and then turned back to Saij. “Aren’t you going to ask me,” he inquired, “how I managed to get down here all the way from the third floor so quickly to answer the door?” “Huh?” asked Saij. “Oh, no, not really. I don’t wonder about those things. I just assumed there was a perfectly logical explanation for what’s an otherwise physically impossible feat for someone with your limited mobility.” “BAH!” Sirius shouted, as he turned around again and slammed the door in Saij’s face. “Wow, someone woke up on the wrong side of the bed today,” Saij said quietly. “He’s like, a grumpy old man and stuff, only not as lovable as Jack Lemmon.” The baby looked at its father inquisitively. “Oh,” Saij said, noticing. “I suppose Walter Matthau was pretty lovable too.” Saij smiled as his child giggled and clapped. A moment later, the door creaked open once more. Sirius was once again standing in the doorway. “The Ripper will see you,” he growled, “but you shall remain here.” “Well that’s no fun,” Saij sighed with disappointment. “Alright, where’s the big bastard at?” His question was answered immediately, as Sirius stepped back into the Domicile and allowed the towering figure of Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy to emerge from the shadows within. The Ripper nearly took up the entire doorway, obscuring what little view there was of the Domicile’s inner chambers. If this meeting had been happening within the squared circle, inside an arena packed beyond capacity, the roar of the crowd would be deafening. One of XUW’s most feared villains was now standing face-to-face with one of its most popular heroes. Saij and The Ripper never really had any issues or any scores to settle with each other. At one point, Saij had been the only man standing between the fabled Army of Darkness and their total possession of XUW’s championship belts, keeping the Intercontinental Title away from M V Pete. Other than that, however, Saij and The Ripper had never been nemeses, and Saij did not ascend to his heroic status until after The Ripper’s grip on XUW had waned. If there was ever a dream match for XUW fans everywhere, though, Saij vs. The Ripper was certainly on the short list. As it was, they stood silently on the front steps of the Domicile. There was no crowd, no lights, no motor home, not a single luxury. Like Robinson Crusoe, it was… well, you get the idea. “The Ripper does not find your visit to the Domicile unexpected,” he said quietly, his cold gaze piercing Saij’s dull-witted expression. “Heya Jack!” Saij greeted with enthusiasm. “How’ve ya been? Life treating you well?” “Do not waste time with formalities and meaningless conversation, which The Ripper fears the entirety of what you have to say will consist of.” “Sheesh, you’re just about as cranky as old Sirius here,” Saij replied. “BAH!” came Sirius’s voice from behind The Ripper. “But anyway,” Saij continued, “I do have something important to ask you, and I did take the trouble to come all the way out here.” “So, you managed to survive the many traps and perils set out before those who seek the Domicile,” The Ripper mused. “Like Mr. Spalzcutoff before you, you were able to overcome the deranged eunuchs, the opium-using fox hunters, the…” “Huh? No I didn’t,” Saij interrupted, confused. “I just mapquested the directions to the Domicile and got here from the interstate.” The Ripper paused, his gaze narrowing. He turned to Sirius. “Have the Internet destroyed,” he ordered. Sirius nodded, turned on his heel and disappeared back into the Domicile. “Now, what is it that you came to ask The Ripper?” the big man continued. “Sirius mentioned that you’re not here to enquire about the one you call Cereal Killer…” “Yeah, that’s right,” Saij explained. “I was kinda worried at first because I heard that you were holding CK here against his will and stuff.” “Against his will,” The Ripper repeated with amusement. “How interesting. Continue.” “Right, so anyway, I figured that since that’s the case, you’re not gonna let him go because you’re like, an evil bastard and stuff.” “You have a keen grasp of the situation, young Saij, as is evident by your intelligent discourse,” The Ripper said sarcastically. “I got to thinking,” Saij went on, “that since our team can’t really rely on CK to show up at Super Series, then we ought to have a replacement.” The Ripper’s expression froze briefly, and then turned slightly to suspicion. Was it possible that this ignorant buffoon, despite his utter ineptitude for noticing his surroundings, somehow knew what had transpired between The Ripper and Cereal Killer only hours before? “Go on,” he finally said. “I thought about it for a bit, and figured out the perfect replacement for CK! And that, Jack-o… is my baby!” Complete silence. “The Ripper wants to believe he misheard you, but knowing your history of insane ideas, that is likely not the case,” he said coldly. “Yeah, I know the kid’s probably a bit young, but I think it’s brilliant! XUW’s first-ever second-generation wrestler, making a spectacular debut by helping us to victory over PRIME at PTC Super Series! What better way to enter the business than by helping your home federation officially establish itself as the best fed in all of the PTC! It’s a farkin’ brilliant plan, I say! There’s only one thing, though…” Only one thing? This would undoubtedly be good, The Ripper thought to himself. “And what would that be?” he asked. “Well, I tried to teach a few aerial moves and whatnot, but quite frankly it’s not working out. I mean, babies are too farkin’ short to climb the turnbuckles and stuff, y’know? So I figured maybe power wrestling is the way to go. Do you think you could teach my baby how to do like, a powerbomb, or a chokeslam, or maybe even a Rip Saw?” Further complete silence. “Give The Ripper one reason why he shouldn’t destroy you this very instant for your complete stupidity.” Saij scratched his head again. “So like, is that a no?” he asked innocently. The Ripper stepped forward, threatening to do Saij bodily harm. However, he looked down at the baby strapped to his body. The five-month-old infant offspring of Saij was staring up into the eyes of The Ripper… into the eyes of evil itself… and was pointing and giggling. The eyes of The Ripper narrowed, as if he didn’t quite know what to make of the situation. The mere mention of his name made grown men quake in fear, yet here was a baby on his doorstep, clapping and babbling with glee in his presence, completely unafraid. Perhaps it was because the child was in the care and protection of its father… or perhaps it shared the same common sense as its father. In either case, The Ripper relented in his advance, and stepped back through the doorway. “Do not waste your time with this… ‘replacement,’ as you put it,” The Ripper finally said. “You need not worry about your fourth team member. The arrangements have already been made. He will be at Super Series, and he will finish what was started a year ago.” “Uh… oh, okay then,” Saij said, surprised. “So CK will be there to help us, then?” “The Ripper said the arrangements have been made,” came the reply with a twisted smile. “That is all you need to know.” “Great. So uh, if that's the case, can I come in and talk to CK about the match for a bit?” Saij asked. The Ripper frowned as he stared down at the Master of the Huzzah, his voice even lower and colder than it already had been. “You wish to enter the Domicile, do you? Are you truly prepared to face the unspeakable horrors that dwell within these walls? Do you really think you will be able to withstand the eternal darkness, and face the unimaginable torment that oozes from within its very bowels? Can you endure the unbearable pain of having every inch of your body consumed by raging fires so intense that not even a thousand Hells could withstand them? Could you possibly be so foolhardy to risk crossing this threshold, the very gateway to agony and despair, knowing that you invite complete and utter destruction upon your meager, pathetic, pointless existence, having your soul rended in twain?” Saij cocked an eyebrow and looked oddly at The Ripper. “Uh… yes?” “Begone from The Ripper’s sight!” the monster hissed. “Leave this place, lest the German Shepherds of Darkness be unleashed upon you! The Ripper has been merciful to this point, but do not test that mercy further with your chronic insolence! Now go!” With that, The Ripper slammed the door shut. Saij blinked. “Uh, okay, talk to you later then,” he mumbled, looking down at his child. “Sorry kiddo, guess you’re gonna have to wait for your in-ring debut.” But the baby just yawned, and then began to fall asleep with its head buried in its father’s chest. As Saij walked back to his car, he happened upon a strange-looking fellow who was strolling along the highway in front of the Domicile. “Whoa, it’s woof!” Saij exclaimed. “Dude, what’re you doing all the way out here?” “Oh, I was kidnapped by pirates, but I made a miraculous escape and now I’m walking home to have a warm glass of milk before going to bed,” he replied casually. This statement was too nonsensical even for Saij’s taste. “Uh, if you say so,” he said. woof woof shrugged and continued on his way down the road. Saij shook his head in wonderment. The brief encounter, no doubt, was as random as his enigmatic teammate. After securely strapping the baby in the back, Saij got into his car and drove off, leaving the Domicile and its inhabitants alone once more in a state of silent soliloquy. The time was drawing near when the question of who was the better federation at Survival would finally be answered. Saij hadn’t participated in the tournament at that show—he’d fought in the main event, attempting to once again defend the Global Title, only to lose it to Seymour Almasy. He hadn’t stepped inside a PTC ring since. But now, he was back, and he had a chance to redeem himself. He had a chance to finish the fight, to defeat the juggernaut known as PRIME, and prove to the world, once again, that XUW was the best. Was it mere coincidence that Saij had come back just in time to lead his team to victory? Was it complete happenstance that this man, who was regarded by fans everywhere as the hero of XUW, had returned to once again defy the incredible odds facing him? No. It was more than that. It was destiny. And at Super Series, Saij… and XUW… were destined to win.
