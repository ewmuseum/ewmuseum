Mother knows best.

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-11 The atmosphere at Goat Mansion had changed considerably since Nurse Wretched last had seen it. In less then a week, Mrs. Gabi Goat, Craig’s mother, had managed to clearly mark her territory. The games room had been returned to its previous incarnation as a parlour resplendent with china ornaments cluttering every surface and lace doilies draped on the chair backs. Still life paintings of bowls of flowers clashed violently with the cabbage print wallpaper and a silver tea service claimed pride of place on a low coffee table surrounded by over-stuffed arm chairs. The kitchen had been restyled in Mrs. Goat’s image too. Nurse’s precious coffee and espresso maker peeked out from under the lid of a trash can and in its place on the counter top was an ornate tea pot. Nurse’s face had fallen as she toured the house, everything had changed. It was as if Mrs. Goat had known she would return and in preparation had endeavoured to prove to Nurse once and for all that she was no longer welcome here, that this would no longer be her home. In a way, Nurse was glad to have this slight as a distraction, anything to stop herself from thinking about what all that had happened and what she’d said in the locker room. It couldn’t be possible that she loved Craig! Shaking her head to clear it of that thought, Nurse accidentally glanced at Craig. With her entire body convulsing, she ran upstairs to what had been her bedroom (but was now guest room number two) and slammed the door behind her. Bemused, Craig wondered at Nurse’s odd behaviour. Throughout the journey home, in fact, since he had regained consciousness, Nurse hadn’t made eye contact or even looked at him straight on. He had thought she was back to her usual self when she eventually opened the door to him and especially when she had knocked him out cold – just like the good old times. He shrugged and decided that she must have some scheme brewing, something that would likely hurt and half destroy him no doubt. Knowing there was little point in following her, Craig stretched out on one of the plastic covered sofas (Mrs. Goat saw potential allergens in everything), closed his eyes and let his mind wander where it would. Nurse stood under the shower and scrubbed at her tight lithe body ferociously. The thought of being in love, especially with Craig Goat, efedding internet geek turned wrestling star, was abhorrent to her. She had managed to get to this stage of her life without holding any genuine affection for anyone or anything and had always expected it to remain that way. To love was to be weak. That was the bottom line, and Nurse despised weakness. Her body was red raw as she tried to wash the feelings away, wishing they could disappear down the drain with the hot water. Craig had always professed that he was in love with her, but neither of them had ever believed that these feelings would ever be returned. How had this happened? Craig was weak. He was skinny and shy and cowed. Or at least, he used to be. Throughout the past two months, GTT4 had been changing him with each victory. The first few wins could perhaps have been down to good fortune and judges with questionable taste. But to win four times and displace the famous Seymour Almasy? Surely that required some modicum of talent. Each win earned him a prize far greater than entry to the next level; it fed the tiny seed of confidence within him and allowed it grow. “I’ve been a fool” Nurse muttered to herself, “I should have known this would happen and I should have crushed him instead of praising him. I’ve turned him into a man almost worthy of me.” Craig lay on the sofa thinking about his upcoming match with Jimmy Bong. He was excited. Bong was an enigma to the wrestling world; a tuxedo clad Brit who looked out of place and bewildered in the ring, but who consistently won none the less. Was it possible that this man was really the bumbling fool he appeared or was he so skilled a wrestler and showman that he could concentrate not only on his moves, but on putting forth a fabulous spectacle as well? Craig felt a tinge of remorse that he would have to end Bong’s reign this weekend. He felt an affinity for the oddly dressed man. Prior to GTT4, there was no record of either of them having wrestled professionally. Both of them were surprises in this tournament, rank outsiders who had delivered more than anyone had ever expected of them. But unfortunately, one would have to go this round. The field had to be halved yet again, and Craig was determined to be in the group that would be advancing, the final six. With this firmly on his mind, he made his way to the gym. His mother had been aghast when she saw the full size wrestling ring that had taken residence in what was once her grand dining room and demanded that it be removed. For the first time in his life, Craig had stood up to her and insisted that it stay. He wondered if Nurse had seen it yet? She had been so upset at the rest of the changes in the house, but surely she would agree that it was a fair trade in exchange for the gym still being there. Tired and sore, Nurse made her way back downstairs. She had considered simply avoiding Craig forever, but realised that that might not be an easy feat. If she wanted to be rid of all these disgusting feelings sloshing about inside her, there was only one thing she could do – break Craig. She needed to hurt him so badly it would shatter his new found strength and return him to the Craig that she knew and didn’t love. Hearing a key turning in the front door, she walked towards it and braced herself, ready to face Craig. Mrs. Goat screamed and clutched her bony hand to the general region of her chest where her heart lay. Simultaneously, they screeched at each other, “What the hell are you doing in my house?” “Your house? Miss Wretched, I assure you, this has always been and always will be my house. Now get out before I call the police! You may have married my son, but you’ll never belong here” Nurse winced at the reference to her fake marriage with Craig. Upon Mrs. Goat’s return from her time at the asylum, Nurse had claimed to be married to Craig in order to score a point against the old bag who had hated her from first sight. Craig rushed from the gym, his body still dripping with sweat and his breath coming in short gasps from the exertion of his work out. With a glance he took in the scene before him. His mother stood inside the door, a bag of shopping by her feet, glaring at Nurse who seemed rooted to the spot in shock just yards away. Turning to Craig with accusation in her tone she shouted, “You promised me she wasn’t going to be here! You told me you’d gotten rid of the rotten cow!” Holding up his hands in an effort to both placate Nurse and be ready to defend himself if she attacked, he murmured with embarrassment, “Actually, I didn’t say that, I said that my Mum isn’t staying at the house, and that’s true. She’s living in the guest house out back.” Mrs. Goat smirked, “But I still need to be in the big house daily to keep it in order, my little Craig needs someone to look after him, and I don’t think you’re up to the job.” Craig took a deep breath, pulled himself up to his full height and with all the authority he could muster pronounced, “Mum, that’s not true either. You can’t come in here whenever you like. You have to wait to be invited. Is that clear? Now hand over those keys.” Nurse could feel the contents of her stomach lurching, eager to escape. Craig was being strong again, and that brought all these alien feelings to the fore. Unable to handle the well of emotion, she vomited, square onto Mrs. Goat’s shiny leather shoes. Unable to handle the look of concern on Craig fixed her with, Nurse bolted into the kitchen. Mrs. Goat stared at the mess by her feet, her face a picture of disgust. Slowly, she turned to the door and left, leaving her bags behind. “Well,” Craig announced to thin air, “That went better then I expected.” He felt a tinge of guilt for deceiving Nurse and letting her believe that his mother was gone for good. But he still believed, quite wrongly perhaps, that if they just got to know each other better, they would learn to be friends. After all, they were so alike in many ways. Nurse schemed. No old lady would get the better of her, she vowed. She pulled a key from her pocket and opened a chest by the end of her bed. Satisfied that she had everything she needed, she grinned insanely. Now all she needed to do was get Craig out of the way for the evening. Mrs. Goat schemed. Nurse had always been a threat to her, and if her suspicions were right, then now was the time to fix it. Nurse was pregnant, she mused. She had to be, why else would she throw up so spectacularly. Mrs. Goat examined herself in the mirror. “I’m too old to be a grandmother” She told her reflection. “And Nurse is not good enough to be the mother of any grandchildren I may have.” Resolved, she phoned Craig – he needed to be out of the house if her plan was to work. Craig sat near the back of the auditorium stuffing handfuls of popcorn into his mouth. He hadn’t wanted to come, but both his mother and Nurse had separately insisted that he needed some relaxation time to in order to be rested for his match against Bong. His mother’s idea that he go see Rocky movie marathon at the Palace was genius. He glanced to his left, saw his father grinning widely and was glad Nurse had talked him into bring the old man along. Nurse looked at her reflection in the mirror. Used to always wearing a white uniform, she was pleasantly surprised to see that the tight black outfit suited her. She picked up a sports bag and climbed out her window, dropping lightly to the ground. Craig’s car was still gone. The plan was going perfectly so far, but just in case, she had left some pillows arranged to look like a person under the duvet in case he returned while she was gone. Mrs. Goat crept around to the back door of the mansion, careful not to make a sound. She slowly unlocked the door and entered the darkened house. Nurse should be asleep by now. Navigating by touch, she wound her way to the top of the stairs and paused. All was quiet. Stopping by Nurse’s bedroom door, she bent low to peer through the keyhole. Excellent, she thought, Nurse was asleep. Nurse didn’t bother to check the inside of the guest house. Mrs. Goat was a notoriously light sleeper and was bound to be in bed… old people were so lazy. She set the timer and placed the bomb on the outside wall directly behind which Mrs. Goat would be snoring. Satisfied that everything was in order, she crept back to the mansion silently. Mrs. Goat took the odd contraption out of her handbag and turned it over in her hands. Smiling she pressed she entered the sequence on the keypad that would activate it. Amazing the things you can buy on EBay, she mused as she set the clock to countdown from five minutes. Glancing at her watch, she left the house with a speed that belied her age, eager to back in her warm bed. BOOM! … BOOM!! The twin explosions shook the neighbourhood and lit up the sky. As the movie came to a close, Craig smiled. Life was perfect. He had his parents back in his life, a tournament to win and even though he didn’t believe her, Nurse had kissed him as he left and declared that she loved him. Nothing could go wrong now…
