The Story of The Prophecy (Part 2)

24 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-24 (Part 2) ~ A mother’s love for her son should never be taken advantage of, no matter what the situation may be! As I look back on it do I think I took advantage of my mothers love? That is a question that I can not answer for my time with her was all but too short for a mother and son! No child should ever have to go through the horrific experience of losing a loved one! Do I regret it? Yes but I don’t dwell on it for it has made me stronger in life! ~ 10-31-88 Time: 2:30 PM Location: Back yard of the Adams Mansion -----The air out today is clean and tranquility is in the atmosphere. The sun is out and is shining so bright with the random bluebirds chirping for any food they may find. The other kids in the neighborhood are getting ready for a night of trick or treat on what promises to be a great hallows eve night. Even a sense of peace can be felt at the Adams Mansion for the monster known as Frank Adams is currently on a business trip for three weeks. But what what’s that we see in the front yard…..isn’t that Frank Adams talking with a couple of clean cut men and a police man. ----- Police man: All the boys at the station have been silenced Mr. Adams. Frank Adams: Good, know how about you two. Do you know what’s to be done? Clean Cut Man #1: Yes sir, we wait for the two to be working in the garden and then we make sure that Mrs. Adams is no more! Clean Cut Man #2: But sir one question! What has your wife done to you that is so bad? And why do we leave your son alone? Frank Adams: Because you dumb shits, she has been taking the side of that little shit John for too long! And if we get a divorce then that’s just more money down the tube! No a funeral is more cost efficient! And don’t worry about my son; I’ll take care of him personally! -----What horrible news to be heard from Mr. Frank Adams. But what do you expect from a cold heartless business man like him. Now unbeknown to John and Loraine they get started celebrating what they believe to be the first birthday for John without his father. So Loraine decides to get a cake so she could celebrate her son’s birthday for the first time in 12 years. It was a beautiful cake with “Happy Birthday John” on it. ----- Loraine Adams: So son what do you think about your cake? Young John Adams: It looks great mom! I love it, thank you! Loraine Adams: And son this year since your father is out of town, I also got you a present. And after I was wondering if you would help me in the garden, and after we could watch a movie together. Young John Adams: Sure mom, this is the best birthday ever! -----As Loraine started to light the candles, she began to sing happy birthday to her son of 12 years. Even though John was excited about his first official party he couldn’t help feeling that something wasn’t right. But later shook it off thinking it was jitters for actually having a birthday today. After they ate the birthday cake John got his present. ----- Young John Adams: Thank you mom, it’s a crucifix. Exactly what I wanted! Thank you. Loraine Adams: You’re welcome John. I want you to wear that always and remember that I love you. Young John Adams: I will mom. -----After the exchange of the present Loraine and her son go out to the garden to pick tomatoes and cucumbers. The eerie silence in the air was quite disturbing if you really paid attention to it. But Loraine and John were just excited to be spending some quality time with each other to even notice. The sense of safety falls before the two as they begin to pick the vegetables. ----- Loraine Adams: John is something wrong honey? Young John Adams: No mom, I just feel that this is too good to be true! And I don’t ever want this to end! But I know it will as soon as dad gets home from his trip! Loraine Adams: Oh John, I’m sorry you have to go through this. But he is your father, and always remember that whatever happens I will always be there for you! Young John Adams: Mom, one of these days I will fulfill not only my dream but yours also. And then this nightmare will be over! -----The two hug not knowing that the nightmare was just about to begin. The mysterious silence got worse as John Adams witness the bullet enter his mothers head in the back of the cranium and through the front forehead. John flings back in terror as his Mothers blood is sprayed all over the young Adams. The one thing John notices before his Mothers body falls down to the ground lifeless is a single tear, a single tear that she had shed for her son. A single tear she had for all the pain, not that she had endured over the years but for her sons pain. Thoughts of all the times John and his mother had spent went racing through his head as he just witnesses his mother of 12 long years get killed before his eyes. After the brutal killing the young John Adams father reveals himself as he grabs John and shoves him into the car. What later happened was that the young John Adams was checked in to the County Mental Hospital and was locked away for 6 years. Frank Adams was found not guilty for the murder of Loraine Adams his wife. But everybody in Riverside County knows better. They know Frank had his wife killed. They know his son John Adams is rotting in a criminal mental institution because he witnessed it. But are they going to do anything……no Frank Adams has the police and the whole county on his payroll. ----- 10-31-94 Time: 4:30 PM Location: Riverside County’s Mental Institution for the Criminally Insane. -----6 long years in purgatory he has spent in this place of the mentally challenged. 6 long years it has been since he has seen the light of day. And on his 18th year of life he is free. Free to start his life over again. Free to accept a job offer like a friend of his in the mental institution. A job offer for a wrestling promotion named XEW for there development team. But there is one thing left to do, pay a little visit to his father. ----- 10-31-94 Time: 6:00 PM Location: Adams Mansion. -----The old mansion still looks the same since he left it last, as he approaches the door he can still see the red from his mother’s lifeless body after the shooting. He goes to ring the doorbell. He waits what seems to be an eternity till the door is answered. It was his father who answered the door. And that night they had the biggest argument and fight of there lives. But this time unlike others in the past John Adams, who know is a man got the upper hand on his old man. That night he left his father for the last time still alive but a bloody mess. But before he left he made sure to get together his mothers things and depart the Adams Mansion for the last time. ----- ~Some people may be confused to as why I still have thoughts about how it all began for me. But I tell them that to truly know a man you first must know where they came from! My family’s situation was only one filled with horror and hate. But without it I couldn’t have gotten to where I am today! Everyday when I get into that ring I fight for one reason and one reason only, and that is to deliver my message to people of “The Prophecy” Tessa Windsor felt it! And so will Dani Fuher for my will shall not be denied as I climb up this mountain in GTT4! ~ ~FIN~
