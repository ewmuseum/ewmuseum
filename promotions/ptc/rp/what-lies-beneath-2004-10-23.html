What Lies Beneath

23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-23 The mind is a wonderful thing. It can perform wondrous feats of calculations, teach the body to perform exceptional feats of physical agility, and that’s before you even think of the actions taken every day without the mind paying much attention to it. The brain keeps the lungs breathing, the heart beating and the spleen doing… whatever it is that a spleen does. A mind is a thing to be treasured, but it can often work against you. Mental illness can grab hold of you; chemical imbalances can throw your perceptions off kilter. You can imagine yourself to be someone else, or to be a whole host of people. You can hear voices and believe that it’s God telling you to massacre some children. Or, like Doctor Curiosity, you can lie to yourself. ---------------------------------------- The night was dark, but not stormy. Eegor approved of this, mainly because when you’ve been left homeless in a desert, you don’t want a storm coming and adding to your misery. He sat at the edge of the moat, pondering on what exactly he could do to make his brother see sense, to make him realise that Collette was only seeing him again because she thought she could make some money out of him. Eegor sighed. Since the tape got erased in the incident with the electromagnet, he had no solid evidence to prove that Collette was leading the doctor a merry dance. Whenever it came down to a straight choice of who to believe, Eegor always seemed to come off worse. That, he reflected, would probably be to do with him not being an attractive woman. Briefly he toyed with the idea of acquiring a dress and trying his luck, but really it was all too horrible to contemplate. To have his brother fail at this stage, this close to success; and for him to be just yards away, yet still unreachable… it hurt Eegor. There had to be a way in. ------------------------------------------ Inside the Curious Laboratory, a building that sprawled over several floors, Doctor Curiosity was pacing the floor. Despite his apparently rejuvenated love for Collette, and his new devices for the destruction of the Muppet Man, something appeared to be bothering him. Could it be that he doubted himself? Had he listened to Eegor’s protests more closely than it appeared? “Vhere’s ze soap? I still don’t get zat joke… stupid nuns…” The doctor’s less than intellectual thoughts were interrupted as Collette entered the room. She looked at the surroundings, and took in the multitude of books stacked against all the walls. There was also a fireplace set in the far wall, where logs burned brightly. Collette’s face grew stern as she remembered how her husband had burnt half of her hair off just hours earlier, but under immense effort, she softened it, and moved over to sit in one of the two large antique armchairs. “So, is this where you study nowadays?” asked Collette. Curiosity looked up, as if noticing her there for the first time. “My entire life is a study, dear Collette. Yet in all my years of doing ze scientific research, I have never come across anything or anyone as beautiful as you.” She blushed, almost feeling sorry for her motives in being there. She remembered that a few years ago this man had charmed her, and she had believed herself in love with him. She couldn’t remember why she felt like that most of the time, but there would be occasional flashes of his old self, or at least a rough approximation of it. “You say the sweetest things, my darling.” The doctor walked over to one of the bookshelves, and pressed inwards on a specific journal. The entire bookcase turned to one side, revealing a secret passage. “My sveet Collette. I have something else to show you… something magical!” Doctor Curiosity held out one hand, which was taken by his wife. Together they walked towards the passageway, which led to a spiral staircase going down in to the lower reaches of the building. He let Collette through the door, and then turned to press the button to reset the bookcase. Hitting it gently, the mechanism suddenly went into overdrive, swinging the bookcase round at great speed and smacking in to the back of Doctor Curiosity. He stumbled, falling heavily against his wife. Collette teetered on the edge of the stairs… and then she fell. Her screams echoed off the cold, hard, unforgiving stone as she tumbled down half the flight before coming to a rest. Doctor Curiosity looked on, aghast. “Collette! Are you okay? Vhat have I done?” He raced down the stairs to find her crumpled in a heap. Her groaning indicated that she was still alive and conscious. He reached her and looked down. No bones were too obviously broken, and there was very little sign of blood. His hands drifted over her body as she came back around, and opened her eyes. “What are you doing?” “You had a fall, my dear.” “Well, I’m fine. Now let me aaaarrrgggghhhh!” Collette screamed as she tried to put weight on her leg, and found that she had twisted her ankle horribly. “Let me help you back up ze stairs. My present to you can vait for ze time being. All zat matters to me now is your health.” “My health… ow… and defeating Muppet Man, right?” asked Collette, through gritted teeth. If he was to fail against the WWA wrestler, then all her plans, all the suffering she’d gone through in the past few hours would be for nothing. “Jah! He vill be vanquished by my curious vays!” Collette closed her eyes and tried to ignore the hands that were helping her up the stairs, back to the Curious Reading Room. She had some pretty strong painkillers in her bag, and she was going to need those drugs tonight. She might even take some to help with her bad ankle. -------------------------------- It was getting late, but tiredness and lethargy were as far away from the good doctor’s mind as could be. The match was close now, and the anticipation of being back in the centre of the ring was getting to the doctor. He’d always craved the acceptance of others, but it had been denied him through his life. From the children that bullied him, through the scientific community that would never quite accept him, all the the way up to the wife that left him. In the wrestling ring, however, he had found success. He had found an elixir, something to invigorate him, something that made him whole again. He would love facing Muppet Man, and he would love grinding his face in to the mat and picking up another brutal, bloody win. “What are you thinking about, darling?” asked Collette. “Oh, just how much I love you,” lied Curiosity in reply. Just then, there was a tap on the window. Four storeys up from the ground, Doctor Curiosity looked round in worried astonishment. There, against all the odds, was Eegor. “Vhat is ze name of all things scientific is going on?” mused the doctor. The tapping resumed, a little more frantically. Eegor’s eyes blazed like beacons, like crazy mad beacons four storeys up in the air. Collette turned to see what was going on, and her face dropped. There stood the man who stood the greatest chance of ruining her ambitions. Eegor could ruin everything. “Don’t listen to him, Chris! Whatever he’s doing, it’s just out of jealousy!” The doctor nodded, but slowly, ever so slowly, he crept forwards, towards the window. The speed of the tapping increased, as well as the volume. Eegor was now banging on the window for all he was worth. His hair was plastered to his head, and one bare shoulder was visible, his clothing torn away, along with a great deal of his skin. Crimson blood fell from the cut, and Eegor gritted his teeth as he banged, clearly in some distress. “I must speak to him,” said the doctor, in a flat tone. He pulled the window up, to receive thanks from a grateful Eegor. “Thank you. Master, thank you so much. Let me in, and throw her out of the window, please. You’ve got to believe me; she’s just after the reflected glory. She wants to divorce you after the tournament and become a celebrity, a rich woman in her own right. She’s still cheating on you, I swear.” The doctor looks over the edge. Somehow, Eegor had swum across the moat, and climbed up the ragged stone walls, finding handholds and footholds out of sheer desperation. Clumps of moss gathered under his fingertips, from where the stones had been unforgiving. Eegor had clearly used everything in his power to get to where he was. The love he felt for his brother, the need to make amends overrode his fears for his own safety. Doctor Curiosity was almost touched. “Eegor, I might let you in, but only if you cease and desist from making such scurrilous accusations about my vife!” said the doctor. “No! I can’t let her do that to you. Look at what I’ve done to get here; look at he risks I have taken. It’s a long way down from here, please, believe me. That woman is evil.” The doctor weighed it up for a few seconds. With a sigh, and something that looked like regret, he dropped the window. It fell across Eegor’s fingers, causing him to let go of the frame and plummet down into the moat again. Doctor Curiosity stood by the window, looking down to see his brother break the surface of the water and swim back to the far bank, exhausted, unable to try and breach the defences again. He shook his head as he returned to Collette. “It is terrible, ze tzings zat ze green eyed monster, jealousy, can do to a man.” Collette nodded, and let the doctor sit down beside her. They rested there for a while, each lost in thoughts of glory, and about how defeating Muppet Man and getting to the bracket finals of GTT could change their lives. After quite some time, the doctor reluctantly broke the silence. “As much fun as zis evening has been, my darling, I must now prepare for my match against Kermit ze frog, or whoever it is I’m facing. I must do this by studying his techniques, his past matches, and anything I can get my hands on.” Collette was impressed. She didn’t know that Curiosity spent so much time doing those kinds of things. “So, you’ve got his recent matches on tape?” “Nein!” replied the doctor. “However, I persuaded Eegor to create him as a wrestler for ze latest wrestling video game! I vill crush him!” Collette shook her head, winced, and then sat down. This was turning in to one of those horrifically long nights. ------------------------------------------ Sure enough, after forty minutes of his being defeated by the Muppet Man in every way imaginable (at least by video game creators), Collette got even more bored, and put down the book she had been reading. If all that the doctor was going to do was play games all night, then she was going to take the easy option. She had flattered him and put up with his ridiculous schemes and tricks for the duration of the day. Enough was enough though, and she wanted to go home. “Honey, it’s been a fantastic day, but I really need to get back to my hotel, and it’s such a long drive from here.” She braced herself for the inevitable complaints, the poor fool fumbling his way through some pathetic effort to seduce her. He really was predictable in situations like these… “Zat is fine, darling. Have a nice drive, and I shall see you at ze tournament.” Collette was overcome with both confusion and relief. In the end, relief won out, and she gathered up her things and left, limping out of the door and back down towards her car. She was to be seeing her boyfriend that night, and the touch of a real man was all she needed to forget the emotional cripple sat there playing video games all night and believing that they could help him to prepare. Doctor Curiosity waited until he was sure he had heard a car door slam, and the sound of an internal combustion engine droned off into the distance. She was gone, and he was now alone. With a smile playing upon his lips, the good doctor paused his game, just before his on-screen wrestler was about to be pulverised by the Muppet Man. Wordlessly, Curiosity produced a remote control from within the confines of his lab coat, and switched the channel over. Another click and the VCR was suddenly running. The screen went from a video game, to static, to the very real sounds and sights of Muppet Man wrestling in WWA. An innocuous notepad near to the doctor’s hand had the pages flipped over suddenly, and a pencil found its way between Curiosity’s fingers. His eyes flickered from the screen to the pad and back again, as he compiled notes, precious research carried out beyond the sight of those he was close to. As the match drew to a close, the doctor stopped the tape and looked at the nearby table. Scratching his chin, his mind lost in thought, he stood up and grabbed the divorce papers. He had signed and dated them a few days ago, prior to the ‘reconciliation’ between himself and his wife. Now, of course, the papers were useless, and could be destroyed. The smile returned to Curiosity. He spoke to himself, and when he did, there was not even the merest hint of a German accent. “Don’t think you’re getting away with it quite that easily, dearest Collette.” A little laugh accompanied the last of the words, and Doctor Curiosity sat back down a contented man. He felt a little bad for his actions towards his brother over the past few days, but the charade had to be kept up, it was too much fun to pass on a chance like this. Doctor Curiosity had often had trouble distinguishing between the real world, and the strange fantasy that he tried to live in, but this time he was the puppet master; the one pulling all the strings. When Collette had told him that she wanted to get back together, he had been intrigued. She was a beautiful woman, and he was no looker, but it had worked once before. What puzzled Curiosity was that the enmity between them should have stopped there ever being a chance of future romance. Just the way they had split up, with Collette leaving the doctor for his own brother, the man now trying to make amends by calling himself Eegor, that should have been enough for them to have been split for the rest of eternity. As such, the doctor was always wary on any matter involving his wife. He knew what she was capable of, and was determined that she could never hurt him again. Whilst Eegor had been off setting a bug in Collette’s room, to be able to listen to her phone conversations, Doctor Curiosity went one better. He actually employed a private investigator to look in to the life now being lived by Collette Goodman. Within a couple of days it was obvious that she was in this for the money, and also possibly for some twisted pleasure in inflicting yet more hurt on the doctor. Eager to turn the tables on the former love of his life, Doctor Curiosity had decided to play along with her little game. If she wanted him to fall back in love with her, then that’s exactly what he’d do. If she wanted him to act like a love-struck teenage boy desperate for the chance to pop his cherry, then he was happy to accommodate here there too. In return though, in return he could do whatever he wanted to her, and she couldn’t lift a finger to stop him. She couldn’t even complain. With this set in his mind, he needed to do two things. The first was to get rid of Eegor. The doctor was actually impressed by the dedication and diligence of his brother in finding out the truth regarding Collette. It was, perhaps, natural though, for she hurt and twisted Eegor in as many ways as she had done to the doctor. The time was not right though, and Eegor had to be out of the way for the plan to work. To say the plan worked would be an understatement. Just in the past day he had seen her cringe and bite back her normal acidic responses more than he ever had during their entire marriage. Not to mention that he had managed to set her hair on fire and thrown her down a flight of stairs whilst looking for an imaginary gift. It couldn’t last forever, but Curiosity was determined to get every last ounce of satisfaction he could do out of the situation. The doctor dropped the papers down on to the sofa, and picked up the television remote control, and the controller for the games console. Bringing the game back to life, the Muppet Man looked certain of victory… but then with a quick counter-attack, it seemed as if Doctor Curiosity had been in control all along. His finishing move came in like lightning, and the victory followed three seconds later. Satisfied, the doctor put down the controller and sat back in his chair. He often enjoyed the way people viewed him, and how everyone thought that they could take advantage of the image he projected to the world. Sometimes it would be tiresome, but being able to do the kind of things he had done to Collette, and would shortly do to his next GTT opponent made it all worthwhile. The smile returned to the doctor’s face; infectious, unstoppable. Who needs Muppets, imaginary friends and webs of lies, when it’s much easier to just manipulate people; and then defeat them?
