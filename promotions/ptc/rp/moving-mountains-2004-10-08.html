Moving Mountains

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 [The last few people are shoving themselves into the packed auditorium. With only standing room left, the last few people that come into the auditorium just line up against the back wall. As the last of the people walk in and the doors are closed, a man walks out onto the stage from somewhere in the back. He is the man once known as Fnord. He has found God, or some say that God has found him. He now goes by his given name, Wesley Williams. Wesley steps out wearing something that he at one time would never wear, nice clothes. He walks up to the pedestal and lays his Bible down on top of it. He opens it up to marked spot and starts to read.] Wesley: When they came to the crowd, a man came up to Jesus, falling on his knees before Him saying, “Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is a lunatic and is very ill; for he often falls into the fire and often into the water. I brought him to Your disciples, and they could not cure him.” And Jesus answered and said, “You unbelieving and perverted generation, how long shall I be with you? How long shall I put up with you? Bring him here to Me.” And Jesus rebuked him, and the demon came out of him, and the boy was cured at once. Then the disciples came to Jesus privately and said, “Why could we not drive it out?” And He said to them, “Because of the littleness of your faith; for truly I say to you, if you have faith the size of a mustard seed, you will say to this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move; and nothing will be impossible to you. But this kind does not go out except by prayer and fasting.” [Wesley walks away from his Bible on the pedestal and steps to the front of the pedestal.] Wesley: I want you to know today that everyone of us have a ‘this mountain.’ You know what this mountain is in your life! Is this mountain a marriage, debt, loneliness, sickness, depression, work related problem, or a relationship? Take a moment to identify your mountain or mountains and allow this message to speak deliverance into your life. [Wesley walks back towards the back of the stage area. He removes his coat to give himself some air and then turns back towards the crowd.] Wesley: Jesus had been up on Mount Hermon along with Peter, James and John. It was there that Jesus was transfigured or changed appearances before them. There’s some spiritual dissention about this mater because some would argue that Jesus shows favoritism because he only had the three disciples to go up on the mountain with him. But allow me to say that everybody is not going to receive the same blessing. God had twelve disciples and each had a special role to play. All of them were gifted in their own unique way. The place for Peter, James, and John at this moment was with the Lord on the mountaintop. The place for the other nine was with the multitude. It’s like that in the church. Everybody has a place. The problem with many believers is that they ruin the excellence of God’s gifts in them by serving in the wrong place. Everybody wants to be on top. Everybody is not qualified to serve in a position on top. All of us have our own unique ability and individual gift, which are to be used in the best place for effectiveness in the ministry. God can get the best of your service if you serve in the right place. The church operates better when people get in and stay in their place. Jesus knew what was best for his disciples. Jesus knew why he left those nine among the multitude. Likewise, Jesus knows the best place for you. [After saying that mouth full, Wesley walks over to his podium and picks up a bottle of water. He takes a huge swig to cool and refresh his throat. He places the lid back on and puts the bottle back near the pedestal. Looking down at his Bible, Wesley skims over the passage once again and then gets an idea.] Wesley: In our text, we find Jesus along with Peter, James, and John on the mountaintop. While they were on the Mountain Top, there was a problem on the ground. Jesus would end his mountain top experience by meeting confusion and despair on the ground. I want you to know that there’s always a fresh Devil waiting on you. You can have a breakthrough moment in worship tonight, yet walk out doors and meet a fresh devil. You can have a mountaintop experience tonight, yet get home and allow your mountain to rest on top of you. [Wesley steps off the stage and walks closer to the people.] Wesley: We notice in our text that when they came from atop the mountain and came among the crowd, a certain man met them. [Wesley clears his throat, rubs his forehead, and looks out amongst the crowd.] Wesley: The man had a request associated with a complaint concluded with blame. What I mean is that he was in need, he was complaining, and he was pointing the finger all at the same time. First, his request was Lord have Mercy on my son. Second, his complaint was that his son was a lunatic and sore vexed. The word lunatic is not used as criticism but it’s used to describe a condition. He was considered lunatic because of his condition. It says that often times he would fall into the fire and into the water. Therefore, he was very sick and considered a lunatic because he kept falling into the same stuff! He kept doing the same thing. He kept falling into fire. We know that fire his hot and a baby has sense enough to know not to touch a hot oven anymore after the first time. He also kept falling into water. We know that water can drown you if you’re not careful. But this man kept falling into the fire that burned and the water that could drown. Therefore he was considered a lunatic. Now I dare not stand here tonight and call anybody a lunatic. But somebody here keeps falling into the same stuff? The same depression, the same stress, the same burden, the same mess. Somebody keeps falling into the same type of relationship. The first married man you dated should’ve taught you a lesson! The first woman to threaten you should have been enough. But there you go falling into the same ole’ mess. [Wesley shakes his head and laughs to himself as he walks back up on the stage. He turns back towards the crowd, who all sits in silence and listen on.] Wesley: The first time you dated her almost cost you your marriage, but there you go again, drowning in relationships you can’t handle. You keep drowning in the same circumstances and according to definition you might be a lunatic. One of these days the fire will consume you and there won’t be an extinguisher; and one of these days that water might drown you and a lifeguard won’t be on duty! [After a small amount of chuckling from the crowd, Wesley continues.] Wesley: Finally, the man places blame for his son’s condition on the Preachers after he sought them. The man needed help, yet had an attitude problem! Listen, don’t you blame anyone else for your condition. If your marriage fell apart after the preacher counsel you it’s not the preacher’s fault. If he doesn’t stop drinking after we lay hands on him, it’s not may fault. I’m not to blame for your mountain. I’m not to blame for your debt. You did it! I told you to give God a dime and you won’t so he’s not going to let you keep a dime. Have you noticed that you’ve got just enough to barely make ends meet? No extra! In the words of a Rap artist allow me to submit this statement, “It ain’t my fault!” [Wesley gets a serious and stern look on his face.] Wesley: Stop blaming your children. Stop blaming the economy. If your mountain is not moved it’s your fault! Jesus heard the complaint of this certain man and he rebuked the Devil. Now we had no idea that this condition was caused by the devil until now. Your mountain can’t be moved until you recognize what kind of mountain you’re dealing with. I want you to remember that because we’ll come back to it. But we’re informed that the child was cured from that very hour. Then the disciples came to Jesus in private to ask him what happened. How come we couldn’t cast the demon out? Here you are wondering and asking the same question. Lord, how come? How come I couldn’t do it? Why couldn’t I open my own business? Why isn’t my life better? Why cant’ I get out of debt? How come my marriage didn’t work? How come I can’t get a house? How come I can’t get that new car? How come I can’t finish school? Lord why can’t that man find me? Lord how come I can’t find my good thing? How come I let stuff get the best of me? Why can’t I close the lid on my drinking? Why can’t I keep my pants zipped? Why can’t I keep my legs closed? Why can’t I get over her? Lord why can’t I get him off my mind? Why can’t I be healed? Lord what’s wrong with me? [Wesley takes a deep breath letting all of the information soak into everyone. He takes a gulp of his water and rolls up his sleeves, as he starts to get very warm and sweat. Wesley starts to pace back and forth on the stage as he is thinking of what to say. He stops at the far end of the stage, to his left, and looks out on that side of the people.] Wesley: Jesus gives a two-fold answer, a summarized answer, and a revelation. The two-fold answer is this; first he says that it’s because your generation is faithless. The Lord is saying to us that we keep the wrong company. You’re letting him bring you down. You’re letting her bring you down. You’re letting circumstances bring you down. You might need to change your surroundings. Then he calls the generation perverse meaning wicked and headstrong. Listen if you want to live in the supernatural ability of Christ, you need to change your attitude about God and the church. [A random person from the crowd shouts out.] Random Person: AMEN! [Wesley stops. A few seconds pass by before he continues.] Wesley: You can’t focus on your faith because of the attitude you have against the church. You stop coming to certain functions because you’re mad. You can’t sleep at night over trivial things and everything in your life is falling apart slowly but surely. You think you punish the church when you stop giving, but you only punish yourself. You think you hurt the church when you stop attending Church School and Bible Class or Sunday Night Worship, but you only hurt yourself. During his time in the flesh Jesus took twelve men and made disciples out of them. He instructed them and taught them their mission. It’s recorded that before he turned them loose to go out and minister in his name, that he gave them power to cast out unclean spirits and to heal all manner of sickness and disease. What they didn’t realize is that their power was ignited by their faith. [Wesley moves back to the center of the stage and takes a quick swig of water, finishing the bottle off. He places the bottle back down on the ground and then goes to the opposite side of the stage that he was just a few seconds ago. This time, to his left.] Wesley: Jesus summarizes his answer to the disciples question by simply saying that it’s “because of your unbelief.” You’re living without because you don’t believe. You’re not delivered because you don’t believe. Your marriage is failing or has failed because you didn’t believe. You focus on the circumstances surrounding minor indiscretions in your marriage and you listened to everybody around you. You never believed God for reconciliation, that’s why the relationship is destroyed. You never believed God for healing that’s why you remain sick. The disciples failed to focus on their faith in Jesus and focused on themselves. [The crowd starts to murmur with themselves. After a brief silence from Wesley, they too become quiet. Wesley clears his throat before continuing.] Wesley: You need to get control of your life. In order to get control, you need to initiate a focus. The focus needs to be centered on Jesus. Your focus on Jesus ignites your faith. With your faith focused on Jesus only, you can get control of your life by getting control of yourself. Once you get control of yourself, you limit the distractions around you. You cancel the doubt of the Naysayers. You walk away from folk who say you can’t. Listen to me good, you stop letting folk determine your outcome. You stop agreeing with the doubt crowd concerning your circumstances… [The same voice from before of the random person shouts out.] Random Person: AMEN! [After a few annoyed moments of silence, Wesley continues.] Wesley: Just because the folks in your house don’t believe doesn’t mean it not so. Your deliverance is yours. If you’ve got just a little bit of faith you can tell this mountain in your life to move! This mountain in your life is something that the people sitting in the row with you don’t know about. They may not even care. But the Lord said if you have the faith it can be moved. What God has for you; it is for you! It’s yours for the asking. Tell somebody God got something just for you! Listen you don’t need another woman’s husband part time when God got you a full-time husband; you don’t need to feel jealous of what someone else got, God’s got your new car, God’s got your new house. God’s got your deliverance; God’s got you a career, God’s got you some money. Whatever you want God’s got it; whatever you need, he will supply. [Wesley starts to get excited now as he gallops across the stage from one end to the other.] Wesley: Don’t let your mountain discourage your faith. If you have the faith, you can move your mountain. Jesus said that if you have the faith, the size of a rain of mustard seed, you can talk to your mountain and say to your mountain, BE MOVED; be moved to yonder place. Oh mountain, be moved. Somebody ought to tell sickness to be moved. Oh sickness, be moved. In the name of Jesus, be moved. Oh depression, be moved. Oh debt, be moved. Oh mountain, be moved. Loneliness, be moved. Work stress, be moved. Home mess, be moved. Trouble and trials, be moved. In the name of Jesus you can move your mountain. [Another anonymous shout.] Random Person: Hallelujah! [Wesley looks over to the man, this time he was looking for him. Suddenly, two huge men step out from the back of the stage and drag the man through the back curtains. Wesley watches and then turns his attention back to the crowd.] Wesley: That man needed more help than I could give him today. Now, where was I? [Wesley thinks a little bit and then remembers.] Wesley: Ah, yes. I was about to say that there are some mountains that require a little more. Jesus revealed that sometimes your mountain won’t move until you fast and pray. Fasting is the sacrifice that shows God that you’re giving up your ways to submit to his ways. Prayer is asking God for what you need. Prayer is communicating with God. You need to know that prayer will change things; prayer will change conditions; prayer will fix it every time. So pray one; press on; hold on a little while longer; everything will be alright. I know sometimes, it may seem like Jesus is not there. [Some of the more non-believers in the crowd start to whisper between each other. Wesley very loudly clears his throat and every member of the congregation becomes silent.] Wesley: But I’m reminded of a story about a preacher whose daughter was in the Intensive Care Unit. The preacher had received the news and rushed to the hospital. He went up to the second floor where the Intensive Care Unit was. There he found his wife in tears as he saw his little girl lay there with tubes through her body. The preacher began to talk to God and said “God, I know I’m your servant. I’ve done the best I could leading your people. I’m faithful to the ministry and I go out of my way to help others. But here I am, looking down on my little girl. I need to know if you’re with me.” [After a few ‘awes’ from the crowd, the go back to being quiet. A large man carrying a stool comes out from the back and sets the stool behind Wesley, who thanks him and takes a seat on it.] Wesley: God said “look around the room and tell me what you see.” The preacher said “I see my wife in tears and my daughter in pain. I see a woman all dressed in white at the foot of the bed. Every time that machine makes a notice or my daughter makes a sudden move, this woman jumps up to check on her.” God said “ask her how long she’s been there.” The preacher went to the woman and said “excuse me miss, how long have you been here.” The lady said “I’ve been here since six this morning and I’m scheduled to get off at four this afternoon.” The preacher looked at his watch and noticed that it was already four-thirty and said “It’s well after four, why are you still here.” The lady said “I can’t leave this post until somebody else comes in. Your daughter must have twenty-four hour care.” The preacher began to remember that the Bible said that God had angels encamped around us. But time went by and his daughter was getting worse. He said “God, I need to know if you see what’s going on, things are getting worse and I thought you were with me.” God said “preacher go out in the hallway and tell me where you are.” The preacher went out and looked around. He noticed the big red writing on the wall that said Second Floor Intensive Care Unit. The preacher said “God, I’m on the second floor of the hospital in the Intensive Care Unit.” God said “preacher, tell me where you are.” The preacher said it again “I’m on the second floor of the hospital in the Intensive Care Unit.” Just then a man got off the elevator dressed in pants and a brown shirt. On his shirt where gold letters that said United Parcel Service and UPS for short. God asked the preacher again “preacher where you at.” The preacher responded the same way. God said “preacher did you see the man who got off the elevator.” The preacher said “yes, I saw him. He was from UPS, he was dropping off a package, was it for my little girl?” God said “no, I’m not concerned about the package that he had, I want you to tell me again where he worked.” The preacher said “UPS.” God said “where?” The preacher said “UPS.” So God said “preacher let me tell you where you are; you’re at the I-C-U. I see you in trouble. I see your daughter in need. I see your wife in despair.” [The same man that brought out the stool hands Wesley a bottle of water, which he graciously thanks him for and takes a nice gulp from it. He brings the bottle down and looks out at the eyes of the crowd, who are awaiting the end of the story. A small smiles comes upon Wesley’s face.] Wesley: God is saying to you “I C U in your troubles; I C U in your dilemma; I C U in your sickness; I C U in your depression; I see everything that’s going on. Just hold on and keep the faith.” Anybody holding on? God will… [The lights dim slightly giving the room a strange glow. The lights on the stage get brighter.] Wesley: Somebody here needs to move the mountain. How much faith do you need? Just think about a grain tonight. I remember a time when I was on the beach. I kneeled down and picked up a fist full of sand. I noticed that most of the sand fell off the side of my hand. And I noticed that some of that sand seeped through the cracks between my fingers. But when I opened my hand, I noticed that in the middle of my hand was still a little bit of sand. I still had a little left. I want you to know tonight that sometimes when things are going well you have a lotta faith. One day trouble will come and some of your falls to the side. One day sickness, and some of your faith is like a handful of sand seeping through the cracks between your fingers. But I hear the Lord saying that it’s okay that some of your faith has fell off. It’s okay that some of your faith has seeped out. All he wants you to do tonight is open your hand and see if you have a small grain left. [In the front row, a small child, around the age of 8, opens his hand and looks. This brings a smile upon Wesley’s face.] Wesley: In other words, in spite of all that’s going on in your life right now; in spite of all of your trouble; in spite of all of your despair; search yourself real good. Do you still have a grain? Do you still have a little faith left? If you’ve got a little faith left, start talking to your mountains and watch God move those. Use that grain against cancer and disease, use that grain against financial debt and stress, use that grain against loneliness and despair, use that grain; use that grain; USE THAT GRAIN to move your mountain. You’ve got the power to move your mountain. Somebody ought to say move—mountain; move mountain; God is good; he’s good enough to be praised; he’s good enough to receive glory; he’s good; ain’t he good? Anybody know he’s good? Ain’t he GOOD; good to you! [The whole congregation jumps to their feet with cheers and applaud. Wesley grabs his Bible, turns around and exits through the exit that goes behind the stage. He keeps a steady pace and the crowd is still going crazy. He walks back and enters a nearly empty room. The only things in the room are a full-length mirror and a single bulb on the ceiling. Wesley pulls the chain on the bulb and it lights up. The force of the pull makes the bulb swing slightly, bouncing the light around the room. Wesley’s eyes go mildly creepy and dark as he stares into the mirror.] Wesley: Your mountain can be moved. Sometimes you gotta shake yourself loose; sometimes, you gotta praise your way through. You need faith, you need to fast, you need to pray, but don’t forget to praise. Your praise in the midst of the mountain, confused the enemy. When you praise God without a dime in your pocket, it confuses the enemy. When you praise God with sickness in your body, it confuses the enemy. You gotta learn to exalt God. Tell somebody I’m getting ready to move this mountain. Yes, I too have a mountain. Steven Caldera is my mountain and he will be moved. I have faith in the Lord that I shall pass up this obstacle and move on to the next round. I have faith in my Savior that with only but a few grains of sand, I will move Steven Caldera. He is my mountain and he will be moved. [The light keeps slightly swinging back and forth, giving the room strange shadows bouncing all over. Suddenly, Wesley shoots his hand up and grabs the light bulb.] Wesley: I will move my mountain. [Wesley squeezes the bulb and everything goes black.]
