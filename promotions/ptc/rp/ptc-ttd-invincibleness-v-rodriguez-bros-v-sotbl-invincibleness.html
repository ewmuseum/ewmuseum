TTD - Invincibleness~! v. Rodriguez Bros. v. SotBL

6 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
6 May 2004 You know, as far as people could remember, admitting weakness of any kind was possibly the hardest thing to do. Well, that and saying ‘no’ to sex, but that’s a whole other story. Right now, despite all that Invincibleness~! had gone through in their personal lives, somehow, they proved God wasn’t a big-ass meaniehead and have overcome challenges to get where they are now, that being the PTC Tag Title match itself. BUT…God did prove that he was at least a meaniehead from 9-5 when Rampage was stricken down with alcohol poisoning following then incident involving Alyssa, Gabriel Brown, and Scott Matthews. Gabe and Alyssa’s fighting was all that Scott could stand, they tried to get him out of the bar, but to no avail. He drank himself to dangerous levels and now here he was, as helpless as a newborn kitten lying up in a hospital bed with Gabriel and Alyssa never taking their eyes off him. I refuse to go into some long-ass description of what a hospital room looks like. You want to know? Dig up Hemingway and have him illustrate the entire scene to you. Why? Because he’s a good writer. Still don’t believe me? Take your eyes of this right now and go smack your face into a brick wall. Have a loved one, friend, roommate, SOMEBODY, take you to the hospital and get you checked in. That should do it. If not, may I suggest some tools or something? A good monkey wrench to the head is a surefire way of staying overnight, what with you being crazy enough to hit yourself just because an RP on the Primetime Central Forums told you to. They’ll be probing you crazy while trying to figure out just what the fuck hallucinogen you’re on. Once again, I digress. Perceptibly, it was a more solemn time for Gabriel Brown and Alyssa. And with them watching over diligently, Gabriel must finally put his demons to rest and stop his jealousy, otherwise Scott will just drink himself back here and next time, he may not wake up. “You know,” Gabriel started, somber tone and all, as he sat back in a brown chair next to Scott’s bed. “In all my years of knowing this man, I don’t think I’ve ever seen it get this bad. I mean, sure, there have been a few run-ins with police, but nothing so serious where he ends up in a hospital.” Alyssa Sanders, girlfriend to the behemoth passed out in the bed, had sat on the other side of the bed while wiping her nose with the 16th Kleenex tissue she’s gone through since this. “It’s hard to go through this,” she said, choking back another sob. “I…I…I just can’t stand to see him this way.” “Hey, hey, hey,” he got up and walked over to her. The man known on-camera as The Almighty Master Of Your Mom held Alyssa and tried to comfort her as best he could. He was no Rampage, clearly, but he made do with what he had. “It’s gonna be all right. I’ve seen this guy go through people in fights like a buzzsaw, this won’t keep him down, not at all.” She embraced him back as Gabe stood over her, her eyes still not leaving Scott’s form for a second. “What I’m really worried about, and I feel like the world’s biggest asshole from even mentioning it during such a clearly horrible time, but…the fucking hospital bill.” Gabriel let go of Alyssa for a moment and kicked hard at the wall out of sheer frustration. Yes, that dreaded hospital bill. An awkward silence filled the room momentarily as Gabe turned away and Alyssa looked over at her boyfriend. “I can pay for it,” she said quietly, but enough so that Gabe could hear her. He suddenly turned around with almost an offended look. “What the hell do you mean by ‘you’ll pay for it?’” She turned around in her chair to face her. “I mean I can pay for it.” “Since when, when the world is tilted over on its axis, Chris Storm can wrestle a good match, and money does INDEED grow on trees?” “No, dummy,” she said to him. “Since I don’t particularly wrestle right now, Kannon was nice enough to make me the head of marketing for RPW. Plus, I do happen to have some inheritance money when dad unfortunately passed away.” Okay, let’s back the fucking train up, Gabe thought. “You’re loaded?” “Well, not LOADED, per se,” she corrected him. “But I do have enough that we can pay for this no problem and not have to worry about RPW finding out about this incident. I promise.” “Okay, okay,” Gabe said, confused, as if she just called him a Snuffalupaguss for no reason. “Just what the fuck are you talking about? You mean…YOU…want to pay for something for once in your life? You ARE aware that you’ll actually LOSE money with this, aren’t you?” Alyssa looked back up at him. “You don’t know a damn thing about me and Scott going out, do you?” “Well,” he said, slightly agitated at the current circumstances. “I know that usually any money we make gets tossed down the drain because you and Scott go out constantly. That’s…kinda what started my little spiel back in the diner in the first place!” “No, no, no!” Alyssa shouted back. “That’s not how it happens at all, Gabriel. I’m the one that’s been paying for our dates. Our shows, our hotel rooms…YOUR hotel rooms, everything!” Okay, this picture wasn’t adding up at all for Gabriel. In his mind, he was going over many questions. Okay…SHE’S been the one paying for our stuff all this time? SHE’S the reason that we can afford half the stuff we have? Okay, now’s the perfect time to look like a big asshole. Way to go, fuckhead. “Please don’t tell me you’re joking…so all this time…this time you’ve been spending with Scott and myself…it’s all been YOU? And my reasons for being jealous were completely misguided? Fuckin’ perfect.” Gabe, defeated, took a seat in a chair and looked up at the wall, laughing to himself like he’d lost his mind. Yeah, watching a loved one in a hospital bed and finding out all the jealousy and hatred that you had for a third wheel was completely unnecessary was gonna do that to a person. “FUCK!” he shouted at top of his tiny lungs as he slammed a fist against the wall. “So all this shit, this fighting, him in the hospital bed…ALL MY FUCKING FAULT!” For a moment, it seemed like Gabriel Brown was in his own hell. It was he and his injudicious annoyance at Alyssa Sanders that caused the fighting which put him in here in the first place. Good fucking job, Gabe. And the kind of hell I’m talking about is bad hell. Like ‘You walk by a bunch of radios at Circuit City and you hear the song “Bia Bia” by Li’l Jon and the Eastside Boyz.”’ That kind of hell. He ran his hands through his hair, about to shed a tear at the events that had now just unfolded between Alyssa and himself. He felt like the worst kind of shit to ever exist. He was washed away in a sundry torrent of confusion and self-loathing. Gabriel looked over to Scott and sighed to himself at the fact that all this crap just had to happen between the three over the course of the past couple weeks. With Scott still resting, Gabe looked over to his best friend’s bedside and sighed to himself with Alyssa looking on. “You know,” Gabe said quietly. “You’re the lucky one right now, you big bastard.” He buried his face in his palms with Alyssa looking right at him, now worrying about the state of not one, but two people. What a fun day for Alyssa, yes? ---- 7 May 2004 Scott lay there, his body not functioning and his mind, well, let's just say it wasn't doing a whole lot better. It's kind of like when you come to in the mornings but you don't get up until your roommate throws cold water on your face and you get up and throw his or her bloody, bloody corpse out the window. Yeah, that, except without everything after the work “up.” It would have been fair to say that the whole experience was slightly frustrating to the Manbeast. A man who has power rarely matched, that keeps people off his back with intimidation and a hard glare. For his body to not respond is a scary thing. More than scary, really, since he can't even get his eyelids to open. But his ears aren't closed. The fact that he can hear might disturb his friends if they knew. But, as we've already established, that's really not such an option. Scott hears bits and pieces, and as best as he can, tries to filter them through his foggy head. “It’s gonna be all right. I’ve seen this guy go through people in fights like a buzzsaw, this won’t keep him down, not at all.” That was Gabe. He recognized that voice from growing up together. It was Gabe. It was. Who else would know that sort of stuff? Scott's mind made a long distance leap that he was probably comforting Alyssa. “I mean I can pay for it.” “Since when, when the world is tilted over on its axis, Chris Storm can wrestle a good match, and money does INDEED grow on trees?” What... what was that? In Scott's mind, they had suddenly switched topics, but in the amount of time for him to make that leap in his mind, he had actually used up a couple of seconds for one thought. But he heard those two sentences and tried to fight his way up. He tried to claw up through the quagmire that fogged his skull. “No. No. Gabe, I meant to tell you... I meant to!” That's what Scott wanted to say. But in a body as weak as a kitten and unable to even open his eyes, all the determination in the world wasn't going to get his vocal chords to work. He meant to tell Gabe, he did. It just never came up. Scott took things one at a time, and he never got to explain to Gabe during all the fighting... he should've, he told himself. He should have. Now Scott felt even worse, which he hadn't thought humanly possible. Up to now, he could only remember bits and pieces, snippets of time. Things like Alyssa sponging the crud of the bar floor and gravel off his body. Stuff like needles going under his skin. Now he could hear, just not interact. It was panic inducing. In fact, the whole ordeal was quite rough, but this was too much. Scott tried to move any part of his body, threw all of his will into it. He wanted to tell them he was there. He wanted to explain it all. He wanted to fix it. He managed to accomplish something. His heartrate went up. Give that Scott had almost bottomed out, he was hooked up to a monitor and when his heartbeat quickened, this just sent everything straight to hell. The sound of the door swinging open, the feel of Alyssa's hands on his shoulder, the movement of Gabe's hands on his other arm. The feel of a needle hitting his skin. “No! No!” he cried out. And he actually did. The sounds came forth from him and everyone froze. That's when he realized it. He had broken through. “No,” his dry throat rasped, “don't put me back under. Leave me alone. Let me wake up.” Those pleas were enough. Those pleas created a will inside Gabe and Alyssa that meant that nothing was going in him without passing the formidable wall of two loving people protecting their friend. “C'mon, man,” he heard Gabe say. “C'mon, look at us. Look at us, Scott.” Us. He was talking about him and Alyssa. Both of them. No self-serving there. His eyes pulled up slowly, reluctantly, and squinted against the harsh light. His eyes adjusted and there, looking down at him was a beautiful face framed in wavy raven hair and a concerned mess of a kid with bright blue sideburns. He smiled, a real smile of relief and comfort. His friends looked down at him. “I'm... I'm going to rest,” he said slowly. “I'll be back in a little while.” He meant it. It wasn't some game of making them feel better or shoring up hopes; he really meant it. To rest in a state without the influence of anything but exhaustion and to wake up soon. To wake up back to the world that had killed his father, that had forced him on the road to take care of his mom and sister. To the world where he went everywhere with his best friend. To the world where a woman loved him even though he was seen as an uncontrollable monster. He'd return soon. ---- 10 May 2004 To sit there and think that nothing was wrong at that very moment was a crime. Discomfited silence had filled Scott Matthews’ room for the past hour and the only sound that escaped was that of his breathing. Granted, Scott was okay now and he was simply gathering his strength Gabriel Brown simply let it all go in one ear and out the other. The thing that racked his 19-year-old brain was mental health: both his and Scott’s. Finding out all these new things about the goings-on of Alyssa Sanders and her beau and discovering all your assumptions, your quarrels, your insecurities were superfluous all along certainly didn’t help matters. Everything had gone so well. But then Alyssa came in and it started a whole sequence of events leading up to now. They fought through legit hardship not fabricated by any clever booker with an angle idea. This was real life and right now, it was kicking the fuck out of Gabriel, Alyssa, and Scott. Just when Invincibleness~! had earned a shot at the PTC Tag Team Titles, it seemed God, Allah, Buddha, Jesus; whatever mystical deity actually reigned supreme decided to take a big fat dump upon them. And in about five minutes or so, said deity would continue to unload. Gabriel Brown sat back in his chair while Alyssa was taking a quick nap. This whole episode certainly took its toll upon the young beauty and her mind eventually called her back to the Land of Nod. He’d been sitting around, doing next to nothing in this room while staring at Rampage. When he woke, he’d get up and they’d all leave, hopefully a little happier. With a bit of luck, the three would put aside all petty differences and just work together. But, hey: what’s a good story without that extra bit of conflict? Well, think back a couple paragraphs to that little comment about taking a dump. Well, that mystical deity is lighting a match now. Case in point: Mrs. Matthews, Scott’s mother and little sister, Stacy had heard about the incident when Gabe phoned them with the bad news. The two had come in to check on Scott, who was simply resting at this point. Gabriel took notice of Mrs. Matthews, wearing a black knee-length dress with a white blouse and black high-heels. Stacey had on more casual attire, ratty blue jeans, blue t-shirt, white sneakers. The mother-daughter tandem also had matching worried faces for their son and brother, respectively. “Is he all right?!” Mrs. Matthews said, frantically. “Yeah, what happened?” Stacey chimed in. Gabriel stared at them, then cleared his throat, relieve to give the mother and sister of Scott the good news. “Well…he’s gonna be all right. He woke up a while ago; he’s merely resting now. The alcohol poisoning isn’t debilitating him anymore, so when he gets up, hopefully we can go. We need to concentrate on our PTC Tag Title match for now.” While Stacy stood over her brother’s bedside and watched the big lummox take in large breaths, Mrs. Matthews didn’t look too happy. She had a look on her face that could only be described as a mixture of anger and sadness. Gabriel immediately took notice of this when he glanced her way. “Is something the matter, Mrs. Matthews?” he said, genuinely concerned. “How can you sit here and talk about your damned wrestling when stuff like this happens, Gabriel?” She said, accusingly. Gabriel stammered. The question had caught him off-guard and he was clearly not prepared to answer such a thing with a coherent response. “Uh…I…Mrs. Matthews…Kathy…I…I didn’t mean to imply work came…” “I don’t want to hear your damn excuses,” she said, bluntly. “You know, when you phoned us about this whole deal, do you know how worried I got? Do you have any fucking clue what you put me through with this crap, letting my only son go out and putting himself in such danger. You are irresponsible and all this comes back full-circle right to you, Gabriel.” “Kathy, you have to know I’d never…” But that was about as far as he got before Stacy had to give her insight to this situation. “She’s right, you know?” She forcefully added her voice into the conversation. “It’s ALL YOUR FAULT!” Now she screamed it out. “YOU! YOU! YOU! YOU’RE THE REASON HE’S IN HERE! YOU CALL YOURSELF A FRIEND, YOU MORON?! HOW THE HELL COULD YOU DO THIS?!” Alyssa was a light sleeper, even though she had been through everything just as rough as Gabe and Scott. The difference was she didn't feel like she was bearing the guilt of that Gabe felt like he had to shoulder. She came around slowly and looked up, seeing two other ladies she had only met a few times. “Kathy, Stacy,” she began. Big mistake. They rounded on her. “And you,” Mrs. Matthews began, “you supposedly care about him. How could you let this happen? You call yourself a girlfriend? What is WRONG WITH YOU?!” The whole degenerated from there. Gabe fired back, trying to defend himself. Alyssa was not only grumpy the way you get when you just wake up, but she was personally insulted. Both Mrs. Matthews and Stacy were flinging insults in ways that belitted even the Invincible One's usual tirades. To give a more medical update on Scott, the alcohol was out of his system. He was sleeping, resting his body and had an IV drip that was re-hydrating himself in order to manage to get his body repaired from the damage. There weren't any ill effects left anymore, so it was merely recovery phase. Or it was until this whole thing started. The IV stand flew past everyone and slammed base-first into the door. That stopped everything cold. Everyones' eyes went wide as they turned towards the bed. Scott sat up, his body bare to the waist where the sheet had fallen when he sat up. He ripped the needle out of his hand and threw it and the dangling tube to the floor. “Is everyone fucking done yet?” he asked angrily. The big Manbeast sat there, his eyes burning. This was Scott back and in shape. He wasn't still recovering, he wasn't still down on his back, he wasn't struggling to survive anymore. Rampage was back and he was pissed off. “I can't believe you two sometimes. Think about this for a damn second!” he added his own yells, cutting through the air with their deep bass. “You honestly think this has much to do with them? It's because I screwed up, you hear me?” Apparently they did. They sat there in shocked silence while Scott grabbed his clothes and shot a hard look around the room at the other people. He kept the sheet around his waist and marched into the bathroom attached to the room he'd been lying in. He emerged a few minutes later, looking like a bear coming out of hibernation. “Guys, we've got a tag title match to get to,” he said simply. “I don't think so!” Kathy Matthews stated. “You are not going anywhere, Scott, you are coming home!” “No, he's not,” replied Gabe, turning on her. “Who are you to be deciding this? You let him get...” she started again. “And you, you... hussy!” Stacy threw at Alyssa. “EVERYONE SHUT THE HELL UP!” as Scott again cut the air. “Will you two QUIT?!” he roared at his family. “You will quit blaming Gabe and Alyssa for what happened, you understand me? I'm going to this title match for one reason. It's not for the belts, it's not for the prestige, it's so I can send the big, fat bonus home and maybe Stacy can get a car.” Gabe and Alyssa knew very well what Scott had intended, but his family was speechless. He glared at them, his eyes burning. “I don't see where you get the idea I can just stop instead of going on to the next match. I go to get you guys the money you EAT ON, you hear me?” He turned towards the door, his eyes still burning. Gabe and Alyssa moved to follow him. It wasn't like Scott to leave his family without saying something. “You know,” he growled, “this wasn't their fault. This was the same thing that happened the day dad died. I'm providing for this family and you all can just deal. If you don't like how I do it, that's just too fucking bad. Go home. I sent a check last week.” With that, Rampage marched out. He didn't care how he felt, he didn't care how his body had been ravaged. He was back on his feet. He and Gabe had a match to win. He had a job to do. Being surrounded by plastic tubes and monitors wouldn't help any, but getting back in that ring, that would be the best therapy in the world.
