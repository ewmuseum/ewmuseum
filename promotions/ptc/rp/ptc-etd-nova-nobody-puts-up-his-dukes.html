22 March 2005 The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes, Part I (PTC ETD RP) Character name: Nova Federation: PRIME The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes, Part I “You did what?!” Nova winces as his wife’s eyes cut into his soul like so many hot knives through the proverbial butter. “I signed up for the PTC Extreme Title Royale.” “Chris, why the fu…well, first of all, what the hell is PTC?” Ariel growled, her hands rested antagonistically on her hips. “Well, I’m an employee of PRIME, which is one wrestling federation,” Nova begins to explain, “there are other wrestling federations, and when a few of them decide to…” “Nevermind,” Ariel interrupts, “I don’t care. Why did you do it, Chris? In light of all the work you’ve put into PRIME lately, why pile this onto your plate? You’re never home, Chris. We have our family back, and I barely see you more than I did after we separated.” “I know,” Nova replies weakly. “You know? You know?” Ariel echoes angrily, “If you know, then why don’t you make more of an effort to be home with your daughter, instead of heaping more work on top of your already hectic schedule and jetting off in the middle of the night to God knows where?” Nova lowers his eyes for a moment, and Ariel’s shoulders slump as she sighs loudly. “This family can’t work without you here, Chris. I need you here. I’m tired of trying to keep this together by myself.” “I’m not asking you to do that,” Nova replies, “all I’m asking for is a little understanding here, Ariel.” “You are asking me to do that,” Ariel argues, “tell me, Chris. Tell me what I’m supposed to understand.” “You should understand that I’m not trying to duck out on my family,” Nova replies, his voice beginning to rise, “It’s just that this isn’t AW. I’m not King Shit here, Ariel. I’m just another name with no credibility.” “That’s not true,” Ariel counters, “you’re doing great in PRIME. You’re more than just a name there, I know you are. You wouldn’t put in so much time if PRIME wasn’t yielding results.” “I’m not talking about PRIME,” Nova says dismissively, “I’m talking about PTC. I’ve never been involved with something that has so grand a scope. I’m nothing there, Ariel. I’m not important, and it drives me fucking crazy. I’m not worried about PRIME. I’ll get my shot there, I can feel it. But in PTC, I’m not gonna get shit. No one is going to ask me if I would like a main event, or if I wouldn’t mind a title shot. If I want to get noticed, I have to take initiative and be ready to grab a spot when a spot is offered.” “So you decided to ‘grab a spot’ in the Extreme Division Royale?” Ariel asks. Apparently, Nova doesn’t hide his surprise well enough, as a small triumphant smile creeps over his wife’s face before fading back into a concerned frown. “I’m not stupid, Chris. I know what ‘Extreme Divisions’ require…more blood, more pain, more potential for injury than any other type of match. Do you just not think about the future? You gave away your right to ‘live for the moment’ when your daughter was born…Hell, by rights you should have given it away when you put a ring on my finger.” “I know what I did when I had a child, and I know what the fuck I did when I put a ring on your finger! Don’t patronize me!” Nova snaps. His wife recoils back, and Nova pauses to take a breath. “I’m sorry…I shouldn’t have yelled. But Ariel, I don’t have a lot of options…do you think I would have signed up for the Extreme Division if I had more options? The fact of the matter is, I can’t just stroll into PTC Headquarters and ask to bumped into the Global Title scene because my wife thinks the Extreme Division is too dangerous. There are already people lined up who have earned those spots. I saw that the Extreme Division had open spots and I jumped on it.” “But why?” Ariel asks, almost desperately, “that’s what I still don’t understand, Chris. What do you have left to prove?” “I have everything left to prove!” Nova retorts, “Ariel, I spent years running with the same crew, and I did a lot of things that I’m still very proud of…but the fact of the matter is, that crew is gone, and gone with them is the respect that I worked so hard and bled so much for. Now I’m surrounded by people who could give a rat’s ass if I crippled myself tomorrow.” “Then fuck ‘em, Chris,” Ariel says, putting her hand on Nova’s arm, “because I care. Your daughter cares. If no one else does, then that’s their problem, not yours.” “No,” Nova replies, “I will make them care, Ariel. That’s the only way to do it.” “Why, Chris?!” Ariel shouts, exasperation and anger mixing a hellacious verbal cocktail in her voice, “why try to impress these people?” Nova pulls a cigarette out of his pocket and lights it, almost in spite of his knowledge of Ariel’s disdain for smoking inside the house. “Because I see the way people treat Seymour Almasy. I see the way they treat Dr. Curiosity and Angelo Deville and Karina Wolfenden. I stand in the corner and listen to them talk about the battles and moments that have made PTC what it is, and I want it.” “So you’re risking your well-being and our future because you’re jealous over other people’s accomplishments?” Ariel replies. Nova bites down hard on his tongue to prevent himself from screaming at his wife. “Don’t reduce my aspirations to the level of petty jealousy. All I’m saying is that when I leave, I want to be remembered for something. Is that being jealous or self-centered?” Nova takes a drag from the cigarette and leans over the coffee table to reach the ashtray. “I’ve seen first-hand, over and over again, how short this sport’s memory is…I’ve seen guys bow out, and no one gave a shit…it was just an open slot on the roster for someone else to fill. I don’t want that.” “And I’ve seen grown men unable to lift themselves out of a wheelchair to play with their kids,” Ariel replies, “and I don’t want that. Your concern is for the longevity of your fame, and mine is for the baby girl who’s sleeping upstairs. I only hope that whatever recognition you receive is enough of a consolation prize years from now if you can’t walk your daughter down the aisle.” “Oh, can you be a little more dramatic, please?” Nova offers sarcastically, but Ariel just glares at him for a moment before shaking her head and turning to walk up the staircase. “Ariel, wait…” She disappears at the top of the stairs and Nova sighs before taking another draw from the cig and putting it out. “Damn it.” With relatively little fanfare, we arrive at the qualifying Royale for the contendership to PTC’s Extreme Title. I don’t know what the other’s motivations are for throwing their names into the hat. Maybe we each have different reasons for taking time out of our schedules to compete for the shot…or maybe we’re all here for the same reason. It’ll be a moot point as soon as we’re all in the ring. I do know what my motivations are, and for better or worse, they’re all I have as fuel to keep me going. Maybe it is just my ego…maybe I just want to be famous, to have the kind of praise heaped on me that I’ve seen others receive. But I have the sneaking suspicion that there’s more it than that… …I have to believe there’s more to it than that. I have to rationalize my decision to pursue this. “Nova! Over here, man!” Emilio Rage stands up and waves to the former 5-Star Champion, and Nova returns the signal with a nod before making his way over to Rage’s table. He takes a seat and pulls out a cigarette. “Starbucks, Rage? We’re hanging out at Starbucks?” “What’s wrong with Starbucks?” Emilio replies defensively, “it was close by, it’s not too hard on the wallet, and I could use a few cups of coffee.” “I dunno,” Nova says with a shrug, “I just feel like such a…tool.” “Oh, I’m sorry,” Rage replies with a grin, “I should’ve found some hole-in-the-wall diner in the city that no one has ever heard of…maybe with personalized murals on the wall or something. Say, I’d be careful wearing that shirt…I hear someone else in the entire world has the same one, and you’d hate to look like a poser, right?” “Touché, touché,” Nova admits, holding his hands up in defeat before taking his cigarette out of the ash tray and pulling off it, “so what’s shaking, man?” “At this point? My confidence,” Rage replies with a chuckle, pushing a coffee towards his friend. “I hear that,” Nova says, lifting the cup to his lips, “Jesus…” “Did you get an ear-full from Ariel?” Rage inquires, an amused smile making its way onto his bearded maw. “As predicted,” Nova says tiredly. “She’ll be fine,” Rage assures Nova with a confident grin. “Easy for you to say, buck-o,” Nova replies dryly, “I’m the one who has to suffer the consequences in the interim.” Rage shrugs and sips from his coffee. “Sucks that your first PTC match is gonna be such a cluster-fuck.” “Tell me about it!” Nova says, blowing smoke out of his mouth as he talks, “That would be my luck, wouldn’t it? I’ve never seen any of these guys before, and I’ve gotta find a way to outlast thirteen of them. I would stab a guy in the stomach with a meat thermometer for a bracket-style elimination tournament.” Rage nods. “Gonna be a lot of pressure.” Nova stares at him. “Thank you, Emilio, for pointing that out. I mean, it completely wasn’t obvious at all to me until you mentioned it…any other helpful facts? A statistical run-down of my chances of winning, maybe? Or a brief get-to-know-ya session on the accomplishments of my opponents, perhaps?” Rage holds his hands up defensively. “Just saying, you need to be ready for it. I doubt PRIME will host the match, and that means fighting on foreign turf, with foreign crowds, and foreign announcers and officials, against guys who have been involved in PTC longer than…” “Jesus, you’re giving me the fucking willies,” Nova interrupts, “It’s gonna suck. End of story. Can we maybe change the subject?” Nova and Rage sit silently for a moment, staring at each other. “This is a nice place,” Nova fires off awkwardly. “Yeah,” Rage replies, staring down into his coffee. “I, uh, I see you have a different-patterned bandanna on,” Nova points out astutely. Rage nods dispassionately. “Yeah.” “Where...um…where’d you pick it up?” Nova asks. “Okay, this is stupid,” Rage says, “it’s obvious what’s on both of our minds, and it ain’t my friggin’ bandanna.” “Wow,” Nova replies with a sly grin, “looks like somebody woke up on the wrong side of la cama this morning.” “Clever,” Rage says, “I see you replaced an English noun with its Spanish translation, a witty and subtle homage to my ethnic origin…where do you come up with this stuff?” “All right here,” Nova answers, pointing to his head. “Say, you got the time?” Rage asks. “If you’ve got the money, honey,” Nova replies with a wink. “It’s just a laugh a minute with this guy,” Rage says, rolling his eyes, “seriously, what time is it?” “It’s half past four,” Nova replies after looking at the clock on his cell phone, “why?” “Shit!” Rage curses, “I gotta run.” “Por que?” Nova asks, feigning a dramatically disappointed frown. “Press release for the Global Title match,” Rage answers, pushing back his chair and pulling his jacket on as he stands up, “I’m late already.” Nova lights another cigarette. “Yeah, well, I got stuff to do, anyway…ya know, press releases and photo shoots and things for the Royale…” Rage unsuccessfully tries to mask a snicker. “Well, I don’t wanna keep you waiting for all that.” “No, no, the brass wouldn’t stand for it,” Nova replies, “I mean, it’s just damn hard being such a hot commodity. I don’t think a lot of people realize the work that goes into being totally freakin’ awesome, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, three hun-” Nova stops as he notices Rage walking toward the entrance to the coffee shop. He opens the swinging glass door and turns back to meet Nova’s eyes. “You’ll be fine, man. You can do this.” Nova nods, and Emilio Rage lets the door close behind him. Rage’s words of encouragement are far from original…Nova has heard them from countless friends, fans, and co-workers in the seven years he’s been involved with professional wrestling… …but they still help somehow. Nova grins and takes a drag off the cigarette in his hand. Nova’s Abode, Detroit, MI… The door creaks loudly as Nova opens it, a long, low moan echoing off the walls of the room. Nova gingerly steps inside, but doesn’t close the door behind him for fear of the noise. The wind against the window outside makes for a soothing hum in the dark room…the soundtrack of serenity. Nova walks across the room to Lulu’s crib and stands over the railing, staring down at the little pooch of her stomach puffing in and out as she breathes calmly in her sleep. You’re the one person who believes, unwaveringly, in me, and you’re the one person I have the potential to let down the most. Nova bends down and puts his fingers through the slats in the railing to touch his daughter’s curled up fists. “I’m sorry,” he whispers. She stirs, but doesn’t wake up, sliding her head over on the blanket and emitting a high-pitched groan that sends spittle bubbling out between her lips. “You’ll understand some day,” Nova continues, “I wish I could stay home all the time, but I can’t.” He grins as his daughter clenches and unclenches her fists in her sleep. “Though if I had known how cute you’d be, I might have chosen a more family-friendly occupation.” As Nova stands up, his right knee pops audibly. He looks down at his leg, running a finger over the scar that he can feel through his jeans. His gaze shifts back to his daughter. I hope I’m doing this for the right reasons. My daughter, my family means more to me than some silly strap… …don’t they? ---- 25 March 2005 The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes, Part II Somewhere along the coast of the Gulf of Mexico, a lanky kid with long blonde hair sits down in front of a lap-top computer. Character name: Nova Federation: PRIME “I don’t believe in the sun, How could it shine down on everyone?” - Magnetic Fields, “I Don’t Believe In The Sun,” 69 Love Songs There are no walls, and yet Nova feels claustrophobic, constrained. He can’t see anything…or maybe he can, and everything is just black. He hears a noise and turns around. “Kirk Cameron, star of the family-friendly Disney series, Growing Pains? Dressed in a zombie costume? What are you doing here?” “I’m not really Kirk Cameron, star of the family-friendly Disney series, Growing Pains, dressed in a zombie costume,” Kirk Cameron, star of the family-friendly Disney series, Growing Pains, dressed in a zombie costume, replies, “I’m actually Denis Leary.” Denis Leary rips away the Kirk Cameron, star of the family-friendly Disney series, Growing Pains, dressed in a zombie costume, costume. “Whoa,” Nova replies, “so if you’re really Denis Leary, and not Kirk Cameron, star of the family-friendly Disney series, Growing Pains, dressed in a zombie costume, can I ask you a question?” “Umm…sure,” Denis concedes, “but I was just about to go on one of my long-winded, fast-paced rants, the style of which helped bring my edgy brand of stand-up to the fore-front of 90s comedy.” “Hmm…let’s not do that,” Nova says after a moment of consideration, “okay, here’s my question…is the ‘bad-ass’ persona thing just an overcompensation for only having one ‘s’ in your first name?” “I might be able to answer that question,” Denis begins, “if I were really Denis Leary…” “You’re not?” Nova inquires, lighting up a cigarette. “No,” Denis replies as he begins to rip away his Denis Leary costume, “I’m actually…YOUR CHILDHOOD SOCK-PUPPET, GUYBRUSH THREEPWOOD, JR.! AND I’M HERE TO DEVOUR YOUR ETERNAL SOUL LIKE SO MANY BOXES OF STOUFFER’S LASAGNA!” “NOOOOOOOOOO!!!!” Nova shoots upright in bed, panting heavily as a cold sweat glistens on his upper body. The dream had haunted him for the better part of a year, and he vowed a long time ago to interpret it, no matter the cost. He’ll have the answer…someday…but today is not that day. No, today Nova pursues a wholly unrelated conflict… …today, Nova must find a way to overcome thirteen of PTC’s most dangerous mid-carders. I’ve got three cigarettes, one of which is broken in half, but I’ll smoke it anyway, a Vespa scooter with one tire that’s annoyingly low on air which means the seat will ride into my crotch and make me nauseous, it’s twelve-hundred miles to the HSW show, it’s dark, I’ve got UV-resistant, protective eyewear on, I was lying about the Vespa scooter, and I don’t know how far away the HSW show is… …let’s roll. The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes, Part II “The fungus fell through, man.” Nova watches as a disappointed frown overtakes the painted visage of PRIME’s own psychotic clown, Ozric Mortimer. “How? I thought they were a sure thing.” “The guy thought he was gonna yield twenty ounces, and almost all of ‘em crapped out,” Nova explains, “the batch yielded eight.” “Eight?!” Ozric repeats, dumbfounded, “Jesus H…and we couldn’t grab two eighths out of eight ounces?” “Bought right out,” Nova says with a sigh. “Damn, man…,” Ozric groans, “I really wanted to trip, too. There goes our Tuesday night, eh?” Nova lights a cigarette. “Maybe not…you ever Robo-trip?” “Robo-trip?” “Yeah, man,” Nova says with a grin, “think of it like dirty tripping…you chug four ounces of extra-strength Robitussin and it can rock you pretty hard.” “No kidding?” Ozric replies, skepticism still present in his voice, “I dunno, man…sounds kinda like high school shit to me.” “Say what you want, man,” Nova says with a shrug, “but it does the trick…and how.” “So where do you find this stuff?” Ozric asks. Nova pulls the car into the local Kroger. As the pair walk in, all eyes converge on them…a 6’5”, trench-coat-clad clown and a 6’3” bandanna-sporting, long-haired, fully-bearded hippie. They turn onto the pharmaceuticals aisle, and Nova bends down, snatching up two white boxes. “Here we go.” Oz looks down at the boxes in his friend’s hands. “Maximum-strength cough formula?” “Dextromethorphan is the chemical in Robitussin that fucks you up,” Nova explains, “you don’t want the added sinus relief crap…that stuff’s awful for you. Maximum-strength cough is the way to roll. A four-ounce bottle for each of us sounds about right.” “If you say so,” Ozric murmurs indifferently. “I do, indeed,” Nova replies with a nod. Back in their hotel room in Houston (the site of PRIME’s ReVolution 59), Ozric cracks open his bottle. “Ugh…I haven’t drank this stuff since I was a kid…and I thought a spoonful was bad.” “Yeah, it’s pretty wretched stuff,” Nova agrees, “I’d recommend a Coke or something to help wash it down.” “Do we have any?” Ozric asks. Nova reaches into their mini fridge and pulls out two cans. He tosses one to Ozric and raises his bottle in the air. “A toast! To getting curb-stomped by thirteen men and thus tainting not only my own fragile inter-federated reputation, but the reputation also of my representative home fed! Huzzah!” “To getting bent over a turnbuckle and violated by Angelo Deville, after which he’ll probably break my neck in two places and burn my closest relative’s house down,” Ozric replies, “Cheers!” They turn up their bottles. Ozric takes a long pull and yanks the bottle away from his mouth. “Oh, God…” Nova coughs and almost gags as he sets his bottle down on the counter. “Ugh…” “This shit is awful!” Ozric gasps. “Suffer through, boss,” Nova groans, wincing as he grabs his Coke and chugs part of it, “whew…” Ozric takes another pull from his bottle. “I dunno, Nova…” “Dude, if you puke it up, that’s five bucks down the drain…literally,” Nova says to him before taking another pull from his own bottle. “Fair enough,” Oz replies with a gulp, “so how long does it take to kick in?” “Well, after you finish the bottle, you’ll probably feel like crap for about an hour,” Nova explains, “but then you should be money.” “I feel like crap now,” Ozric replies, coughing after another pull from the Tussin bottle. Thirty minutes later… “Nova, you alright?” Ozric asks. “Gimme a sec,” Nova mumbles, bracing himself against the wall of the bathroom with one hand and running the other through his mane, “Ugh…this stuff can fuck with your stomach worse than shrooms sometimes…” “Tell me about it,” Ozric says with a chuckle, “I felt like I was gonna shit myself for about twenty minutes there…” Nova laughs. “If I didn’t think I would vomit, I’d smoke a cigarette.” Another forty-five minutes pass… “Doesn’t the ground feel bouncy?” Nova says, turning to Oz, “it feels like I’m walking on bubbles.” “Yeah,” Oz agrees, “the ground is…um…bubbles…bubbly.” “Exactly!” Nova yells before breaking out in a fit of laughter, “exactly, man! Shit! It’s like…God, what song…” “‘I’m Walking On Sunshine’!” Oz finishes his friend’s thought. “That’s it!” Nova replies quickly, “only…instead of sunshine…” “It’s bubbles!” Oz says, a note of intellectual triumph in his voice. “Holy crap,” Nova says suddenly, his eyes growing wide, “dude…are you reading my mind?” “Um…I dunno,” Oz replies, confused. “Let’s test it,” Nova decides, “I’m gonna think of something, and you tell me what it is.” Nova closes his eyes and squints. Oz stares at him intently for a moment. “What your thinking about is…homicide and discreet body disposal.” “Jesus, no!” Nova recoils at his friend’s morbid response, “I was thinking about, what if guys could, ya know, stick it in the pooper and get each other pregnant, and if they could, what the lovechild of Columbo and Matlock would look like.” “Oh,” Ozric replies, disappointed. “Let’s go to the pool,” Nova says, brushing off their failed telepathy experiment. “Ahh,” Ozric sighs happily as he slides into the heated pool, being careful not to splash water on his face-paint, “I feel…great.” “CAN-OPENER!” Nova shrieks, landing in the water and causing an enormous sonic-boom splash effect that cascades over Ozric. “Dude, chill on the splashes!” Oz yells. “Sorry,” Nova offers weakly, “I forgot about your face-paint…” “You forgot about my face-paint?” Oz repeats incredulously, “how? How is that possible? That’s like me forgetting you have long hair!” “Dude, sorry,” Nova replies softly, “Jesus, call the Angry Police, Ozric Mortimer is radiating negative vibes…” “Sorry, I just have a thing with getting my face wet,” Ozric says apologetically. Nova paddles around the pool for a moment. “Oz, do you think I can win the Royale?” “Oh, my God,” Ozric sighs, “we’re in a heated swimming pool, trying to relax, hyped up on dextro-whatever-the-hell-you-said-it-was, and you wanna talk about wrestling.” “What can I say, man?” Nova says with a shrug, “it’s been on my mind.” Oz tucks his thick black hair back and removes a joint from behind his ear. He puts it in his mouth and lights it, blowing smoke out of his nose. “Man, I know what your problem is…” “What?” Nova asks, “because I feel really unprepared for some reason, and I don’t know if…” “If you’d hold up a sec,” Oz interrupts, “I might tell you. I think your problem is…you’re not thinking about this like a regular wrestling match.” He passes Nova the joint and stands up to his full height in the pool, accentuating his words of wisdom with exaggerated hand motions. “You’re thinking about this like…like…” Oz holds one hand high into the air and the other just above the water. “You’re thinking about this like PTC is way up here, and PRIME is way down here. It’s not, man. It’s just another match. You got your head all fucked up putting this Royale up on a pedestal like it’s the Clash of the Titans. I mean, if these guys were so good, wouldn’t they have been picked by their respective federations for that Seven of the Best thing?” “Hey!” Nova snaps. “Okay, let’s rewind a little bit,” Oz back-pedals, “but seriously, man, you have to go into this match like it ain’t no thing…like it’s just another week in PRIME.” “How do I do that?” Nova asks, exhaling deeply into the air before handing the hooter back to Oz. “Man, you’re six-and-two in PRIME,” Oz says before taking a puff, “that’s nothing to sniff your nose at. Just wrestle these guys like you wrestled Tony Rolo. Wrestle them like you wrestled Razor Blade, and JTF, and me, for Christ’s sake.” “And what if I wrestle them like I wrestled Karina Wolfenden, or Hoyt Williams?” Nova interjects, “which, in case you missed it, was ‘very shittily.’” “You can’t,” Oz replies non-chalantly. “Oh?” Nova cocks an eye-brow, “and why is that, sir?” “Because ‘shittily,’ isn’t a word,” Oz explains, “I typed it into Word once, and a little red squiggle came up underneath it.” “Oh, well, if Microsoft Word says it, it must be true,” Nova says with a roll of his eyes, “I take it you also trust the tabloids and the inter-web, right? Well, fine, I’ll rephrase…what if I fuck up? That blunt enough for ya?” “So what if you do, man?” Oz says, “it won’t be the end of the world. What you have to get into that drugged-up skull of yours is that respect doesn’t come only with victory. It comes with giving your opponents a hell of a fight, and anyone who thinks you’re not gonna give those cats a hell of a fight is an idiot.” Oz hands the joint back to Nova. “I’m going up to the room, man.” “I’ll see you up there,” Nova says with a nod. Ozric returns the nod and climbs out of the pool. Nova lies back against the concrete steps of the shallow end and takes a deep drag from the spliff. He watches the wind whip the smoke around in the air, and thinks about what Oz said, on top of what Rage told him and the words of encouragement from his wife. It looks like the only one who doesn’t believe I can do this…is me. They’re all right, of course. I can do this. I’m as capable as any of the men I’m competing against. It’s just that I’ve never had this many eyes on me at once…all waiting for me to make a wrong step. I know I have a tendency to over-analyze things. Truth be told, the times in my career where I’ve truly excelled, truly raised the bar for myself in terms of competitive performance, I’ve just gone out there and, to quote a ridiculous, embarrassing pop-culture slang phenomenon, “gotten ‘er done.” I guess I just have to do it. … So let’s do it.
