Date: 2004-09-16 "I digress though. Sorry. I do that." "That's okay, Mister Borealis. I like it when you ramble." Yeah, and I like it when I'm not here, and nervous, because I'd like to keep my job. Frankly, I've found that if the company wants you to go to a "councilor" to talk about your problems, that they're convinced that you have definite problems in the first place. Problems that can affect your employment. Nevermind the fact that I don't. That doesn't matter in this room. On this couch. Talking to this condescending bitch. Just sitting here until four o'clock comes to free me, only to bring me right the fuck back here next week. Until she's determined that the mental disorder, which they're convinced I have, is not a danger to myself or the work environment. "Would you like to talk about your mother, Mister Borealis." This is getting irritating. "You have asked...that same question every day. For three sessions now, you have asked that question. Do I want to talk about my mother? Do you magically think that I'm going to change my mind about this? That I'm going to just up and want to talk about her next time?" "I thought maybe you'd have warmed up to me by now, Johnny." "In all honesty, there is no warming up about it. I'd prefer it if you didn't ask again." "Whatever makes you comfortable, Johnny." "And stop calling me Johnny." I hate that fucking name. Johnny. Johnny Borealis...gets so old when everybody in your fucking world calls you that. Johnny. Hey Johnny. Yo, Johnny. Johnny, come here. Come 'ere, Johnny. ...good boy. Good trained little boy. Trained to do what you're told. Jump through burning hoops and roll over. Play dead. Heel. So fucking sick of it. "I thought you said you wanted me to call you Johnny." "I was being sarcastic. I tend to do that a bit." "Were you being sarcastic just now?" "..." I don't even know. She knows it. She knows that I don't know what I am anymore. Sarcastic, sadistic, serious, or sad. Barely knowing who I am, only who I was. I was Johnny at one point. Now...that's lost all appeal. "We can't make any progress if you don't start taking this seriously, Mister Borealis." "Aww...that's terrible." "And if we don't make progress, you keep coming to see me. Week after week after week...and your company will still pay me. I don't see a down side from my perspective. Only from yours, when 3WA finally just fires you, as they're convinced you've got something wrong with you." "I've got nothing wrong with me." I wish I knew that for certain anymore. I honestly don't want anything to be wrong with me whatsoever. I know that I don't think...anything's wrong... "Didn't you say, the other day...that you were afraid something was wrong with you? But that you didn't think there was." ...shit... "I lied." "You lied?" "I compulsively lie. And that's the truth." You don't even know how true that is. I can feel her looking at me quizzically. She's got the eraser of her pencil resting on her lips...her head tilted down just enough that she's looking over her glasses to glance at me. Pushing thirty, wanting to know things about me that I won't tell. And wanting for reasons more than her career. I know she wants me. "You...lie?" "Yes. Sorry." "Why do you lie, Jah-...Johnathan?" That's a good question, Doc. I guess sometimes, there's just things we do. Don't know if it's fun, or helpful, or if it makes me feel good in any way. I suppose it's just like smoking cigarettes. Or being an alcoholic. Or being a sex-a-holic. "I guess...I'm just addicted to it." I'm sure her eyebrow quirks. I want her too. "I mean...it just feels right. Feels complete. Like, if I don't lie...if I don't do it so many times, to so many people...it'll drive me mad. Drive me to the break. Start getting the shakes... I don't really know why I like to lie. I don't even notice that I am lying, sometimes. Sometimes...it's just like I'm telling the truth. And I look back later, and realize that that's not the case. Like it's almost automatic." "I see..." "It's easier. Sometimes. Sometimes it takes a bit of effort. The feeling it gives. The comfort. The control, the fact that I control what you think about. What you think of me." "I see..." You don't see. And neither does she. And the thing she has to wonder about, in the long run, is whether I was lying before... Or am I lying now?
