Goodtime's and badtime's (Part 1)

23 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-23 Training. It used to be a word to me but now it was something I lived. The quiet time in a world known for loud noise. A place that just sort of was a get away from the life outside. Away from the war’s set over sea’s and the bloodshed that it brings. In the dark of a world that I wasn’t sure of, I had to make my home. And after my great victory, I had to make it more then a home. I had to make it my world. Even with the pain that was waiting for me outside I felt that I had gotten stronger. With that victory against Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis, it seemed to almost give me confidence to control even the weakest times that I might encounter in my life. From the frustrations and the grief that my family gives me to the torment and pain that I may put on myself. I feel that this world, this area that I travel and stay in, this training facility, will be the dream land from the reality that I face everyday. As I got off the treadmill, with the sweat running right with me, going down the side of my arms and face, I grabbed the white towel that I had brought from Thomas’s house. It was his lucky towel. A very old teenage mutant ninja turtles towel that he had gotten when he was younger. I couldn’t help but think of the match over and over in my head. They do say a rookie makes many mistakes. For me, I made more then some people make money in a year. As I walked towards the locker room, a voice came over the air. Very smooth and sweet sounding, almost as sweet as peaches and cream. Turning my head around to see Thomas standing right there across the room. Oh his eyes ran to me. His strong stance called for me to come over. He was a fresh look to sore eyes from all the training. I could use a long hug from a friend as I walked over to him, opening my arms up as wide as they would go. “God it’s good to see you.” His body was full of heat. Warm and snuggle like as he squeezed tight around my chest all the way to my back. I said it once and I’ll say it again. The feeling of Thomas’s heart beat going through to mine was the best feeling in the world. I just stood there. Out of breath from all the running that I had put myself through. I could feel muscles that I didn’t even know I had in pain. All down my thighs and legs they felt as if they were set on fire. Like a burning building in flames that only a nice cold shower could cure. “So you have a good workout?” Thomas pulled away from my clutch of his body to wrap his arm around the back of my neck. I think he knew how much I really wanted this but deep down I still felt as if he didn’t trust my judgment. Maybe it was just me. “Yes I did and I know I’m going to need some rub down later tonight. You up for it?” Giggling a little as I squeezed him a little more from the side. I think the happiness of everything new in my life was making me do stuff abnormal and it was showing. As I lead him towards the locker room, the door opened right in front of us, letting out a huge set of steam into my face and Thomas’s. Thomas jumped back, pulling his body in front of me as sort of a shield. A brave man protecting his woman. Maybe I was a little girly girl but at least I knew how to see a strong man that could treat me like a woman and still let me kick ass inside a ring. “So, again. Congratulations on your win and what pain are you going to kick out on to little miss Pandora?” Thomas’s words had a little hint of funniness in them. As if he thought I might be some wonder woman. But I wasn’t even close to that. Though my back was glad it was a smaller person this time. But who would of expected that I’d of won. Not me. But that one just one win. Walking over to my locker, I opened it and pulled out an extra clean towel. Slamming the door shut, me and Thomas began to walk over to the showers. No one was in the room so it was clear for him to be in here. I remember before me and Thomas broke up. We use to spend hours in the shower washing each others back. I took hold of Thomas’s hand as I lead him towards one of the far showers. Pulling him into it, I began to give him a long passionate kiss. His skin felt soft as he began rubbing the bare back skin that I had. Oh god it was a wake up call for me to live life to the fullest. I began unbuttoning his shirt, showing the dark hair that was covering his chest. I could feel the breath that he let out. As both of us let it all out, out of the corner of my ears, I heard a noise. “What was that?” I pushed away from Thomas. Hoping it wasn’t anyone as I felt embarrassed enough that he was there. Looking from behind the white shower curtain that was covering the of us, a woman with long blonde hair came walking in. She looked good even to me. I watched as he let the towel that was covering her body drop to the tile floor. My eyes went wide open as I slowly stepped out of the shower. “Oh!” She jumped back for a second. Grabbing her chest to try and cover her body from my eyes. I was more shocked then her. Not seeing a full woman’s body before, I didn’t know what to do really. I closed my eyes, trying to let her get the towel around her. “I didn’t know anyone was still here.” Her voice was more embarrassed then scared. She was beautiful. Her eyes sort of dances on her body. She walked behind the wall that was separating the showers from the lockers. I grabbed my head slightly moving my hair around as I did. “Well actually I was just leaving.” Reaching back inside the shower to grab Thomas’s hand. Oh I had to get out of there while I could. I pulled Thomas out and ran as fast as I could towards the lockers. I didn’t even look at the girl. I was sure she was going to be think the worst but so would I. Thomas ran for his life as he saw the woman standing right there. I wasn’t one to do stuff like that but I wasn’t living in my normal life anymore. I was more grown up and Thomas was someone I almost tried to impress. Back when I was still in high school, I use to ask to go somewhere and I couldn’t go anywhere alone. But now that I was in the real world, wrestling for a living, I somehow changed. Going out the locker room door, Thomas grabbed my shoulder and twisted me around. Setting his lips right on mine out of no where. I looked up at him in shock again, not wanting to take my eyes off his. But after a few seconds, a buzzing sound came over us. It was Thomas’s cell phone. “Hello?” Thomas asked away, holding the black little cell phone up to his ears while still laughing and staring at me. Quickly changing his smile, Thomas’s lips went from up to down in a second, slowly handing the cell phone towards me. “It’s for you baby.” I took the cell phone with a confused thought going through my head. No one knew I was with Thomas so how could it be for me. Better yet, who could it be? I took the cell phone and held it up to my ear. Waiting for a second for the voice, the four worst words came to my head as I stood there, my other hand being held by Thomas. “God no…”
