Settling the Score: Part One

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-23 There were many advantages, Seymour Almasy had noted, to knowing that life wasn’t an RPG. Among them were finally being competent enough to pass the written and road tests to gain his driver’s license. Sure, he’d had it for over a year now, along with his motorcycle license, but he’d never really indulged. He’d always driven Aerith’s car. Or a rental. In the euphoria of pulling out a tough win over the top wrestler in PRIME, though, he’d decided to splurge a little. Or a lot, as it turned out. He’d shelled out the cash for a Corvette, and then immediately taken the thing over to the world-famous West Coast Customs shop. Once they got over the shock of a 5’7" affluent white boy talking like he’d spent his life in the ghetto (hey, Seymour HAS played FF7. Barrett be all up in yo’ grill, dawg!), they set to work, and Seymour’s ride was soon pimped. He would have gotten his chocobo pimped too, but that would have gotten PETA mad at him AGAIN, and most of those lawsuits are still pending as is. And so it was that Seymour stood outside a Holiday Inn, a duffel bag in his hand, looking at his ride. Painted black and silver, with black velvet seats, a kickin’ sound system, and more odds and ends than most people could imagine, the car was Seymour’s newest toy, and oh, was he enjoying it. "This thing is so freakin’ cool!" he gushed, as if he were a sixteen year old just being given the keys to freedom for the very first time. "I wish I’d have known how awesome driving a cool car was! All sorts of cute girls walk up…that I can’t really talk to because I have a girlfriend…but hey! It’s still cool!" Nice job, Seymour. At any rate, the Final Fantasy gave a last quick check of everything, making sure he had everything he needed for today’s trip. Uncomfortably, he reached under the collar of his mall T-shirt stand made "Cloud x Aerith: Together Forever" shirt, feeling the bulletproof vest beneath. In the aftermath of the attempt on Rich Rollins’ life, PTC officials had sent Kevlar vests to all remaining GTT5 talent who wanted them. Seymour was definitely one of them. "You know, I may hate the Renegade," he mused, shaking his head, "but if nothing else, I can sympathize with the guy. Staring down the barrel of a gun sucks." Come to think of it, it was about this time last year that Seymour was staring down the barrel of said gun, just before dropping the PTC Global Title to the Latch Key Kid. And, while it hadn’t been in January last time around, GTT4s fourth round had been the round where Seymour’s run at the tournament title ended, at the hands of a rookie named Craig Goat. The more he thought about it, the more he decided he REALLY didn’t like this combination of January and GTT Round Four. But what could he do about it? The man who’d tried to shoot him was in jail. The Latch Key Kid had long since lost the PTC Global Title. But there was still Craig Goat. With a smile a mile wide on his face, Seymour uncrumpled a flyer that had been balled up in his pocket, seemingly memorizing the information on it. Nodding, he let the paper fall from his hand, being taken off by the wind. Almasy’s smile only widened as he entered the vehicle. Finally, he would settle something that had been bothering him ever since GTT4. Oh, would it feel good… _______________________________________ In Anytown, U.S.A., it was a typical day, just like any other. Slightly warmer than it usually was at this time of year, but typical nonetheless. People walked the sidewalks, and kids biked around excitedly. The one event distinguishing it from all others (for wrestlers and wrestling fans, anyway), was a fantasy camp being held in the local park. For fans, it was a chance to meet and greet their heroes up close. For budding grapplers, it was a chance to learn from true professionals. Among the men signing autographs and throwing down in the ring were such luminaries as "Mean" Mike Marvel and "Super" Shane Matthews. Local wrestlers who never hit it big, but were still well known for their appearances in nearby gyms and wrestling shows. And then, there was the one man who didn’t look like he belonged. Where the others were big and tough, he was slight and diminutive. Yet it was this man who had the biggest crowd around him, watching him as he taught arm drags and hiptosses, and clamored for his autograph. Leaning against the ropes, he took a promotional photograph from a young fan, and signed it. "Gee, thanks Mister!" the young man exclaimed. His hero reached down and patted him on the head, before looking around him, a bit perplexed at all of this. He’d never asked to be a hero. GTT4 had been a pipe dream of his, that his…girlfriend? Lover? Evil bitch? had dragged him into. But as so often happened in life, the impossible occurred. The rail-thin youngster had begun to win. He beat three competitors, and then went face to face with one of Primetime Central’s deities. Two more wins, and he made the GTT4 finals. While he lost to Doctor Curiosity, his meteoric rise was still unmatched before or since. "Hey, Craig!" he heard a voice call. Turning around, he saw Mike Marvel standing there, smiling down at him. "Show us that move you beat Almasy with!" The man known to the world as Craig Goat gave a small, wry smile. He’d beaten some of the best throughout GTT4, but even if he never stepped in the ring again, he’d be known forever as the rookie who shocked Seymour Almasy in round four. "Yeah!" a few fans clamored. "Show us that move! It was awesome!" Goat seemed to ponder it for a few more seconds. In truth, he barely remembered the move itself, what with all of the hubbub and cameras and noise and celebration that had gone on after his victory. "Go on, Craig," a familiar voice said, from mere feet away. "Show them." The woman standing there looked up at her husband proudly. Sure, she had been his "wife" back then, but only in the course of a ruse. Now, she was his wife legally, in body and in spirit. Back then she had been truly Wretched. Now, it was hard to deny that she was a Lovely creature, indeed. But whether Wretched or Lovely, two facts about her were consistent. She was still a Nurse, and she still loved her Craig. "You know I can’t say no to you, Esme," the upset-artist said, beaming. After taking a few moments to gain his composure, and remember just how the heck he’d shocked the world, he nodded his head. "Alright, everyone," he said, in a low voice. "If I remember correctly, this is how it went." And with Mike’s help, he began re-enacting the night that had changed his life forever. ---------------------------------------- If he closed his eyes, he could still see it happening. Of course, closing his eyes while driving was a terrible idea, but Seymour couldn’t help it. His Corvette sped down the road, the Final Fantasy VII soundtrack blaring at a decibel level that would likely fry Seymour’s eardrums, but he didn’t give one jot. "One night," he muttered, opening up the throttle even more. "One lousy night. Everything was going my way, and then it all went south." That was, in truth, what bothered him above all. It wasn’t as if Craig Goat had dominated him that night. Far from it. Almasy had been in control most of the match, with Craig coming back with flurries of offense here and there, getting some close falls. Seymour shook his head, beginning to sing along as One Winged Angel kicked in. Estuans interius Ira vehementi Estuans interius Ira vehement Roughly translated, the Latin meant "burning inside with violent anger". Pretty accurate, if Seymour was going to be truthful with himself. He hated Craig Goat. Hated that Goat had gone on to beat Jimmy Bong to win the Appalachians Bracket. Hated that he’d beaten Tarrant to get a spot in the finals. And most of all, hated that all of that happened because of him. Because PTC’s "legend" had taken his eyes off the ball, and it had bitten him in the ass. One lousy move. Goat had come up with the perfect move at the perfect time to ruin what had been a perfect tournament for the Final Fantasy. That one move had caused a loss which led to several more, sending Almasy on a tailspin. He couldn’t concentrate on Ember. Or Kimbusa. Or "Avatar" William Gwynn. Not with this shit assailing him. Not with the memory of last year’s failure so clear in his mind. Even so, Seymour smirked. The Invincible, his favorite sword, was sheathed in its scabbard in the trunk of the Corvette. Soon enough, he’d arrive at his destination. And soon enough, Craig Goat would be dealt with. ----------------------------------------------- "I THINK it went something like this. Go ahead Mike, set me up for the piledriver." With a spellbound audience, Craig Goat found himself being doubled over, with his head being pushed between the thighs of Mike Marvel, today playing the part of Seymour Almasy. "There. Seymour was going for Time Compression, that straitjacket piledriver thingy of his. I knew if he hit it, everything would be lost, so I back dropped out, like so--" Goat suddenly straightened, throwing Marvel overhead with a back body drop. Mike smacked his back on the mat, and suddenly, everything seemed like it had that night, what with the cheering fans around the ring and all. Sure, the scale was smaller, but the feel was there. And that feel was all that mattered. Seymour, or Marvel, whoever it was at this point, got up, and swung for Craig’s head with a clothesline. Goat ducked deftly underneath, and from there, instinct kicked in. Goat grabbed his opponent in a reverse neck breaker position, but suddenly dropped to his knees. The effect was similar to an inverted Stunner, and the move had been delivered to perfection. Rolling Marvel over, Craig hooked the leg, cinching it tight. It was different now though, he realized. There was no incredulous Dave Kern on commentary, no Jeff Marx pooh-poohing him the entire match. ONE! No referee, looking at the pinfall incredulously, unable to believe the count he was making. The referee here was the collective mass of kids slapping the mat in unison. TWO! No 20,000 fans, staring at the pinfall in the ring, wondering if maybe, just maybe, Goat would actually win. Most people thought it was just going to be another two count, and then Seymour would get up and put Craig Goat away, like he was supposed to. THREE! And no pandemonium when what was supposed to happen didn’t. Respectful applause erupted from those in attendance, as they looked at the "victorious" Goat. "What was it like," a fan asked, looking at Craig with a new reverence. "Incredible," Nurse Lovely chimed in, a mile-wide smile on her pretty face. "Of course back then I never would have admitted it…" Oh, how things had changed since that day. GTT4 had given Craig Goat the confidence he needed to stand up to Esme. To his family. To do what he’d always wanted. And that meant a moratorium on wrestling and marrying the woman he loved. Craig Goat was now a happily married man who could stand on his own two feet. As such, all eyes now turned to him for an answer. He nodded in response, concurring with Nurse yet again. This time, though, it was by choice. "Absolutely unbelievable," he said, truthfully. "They called it the upset of the year. From what I hear, I can’t imagine Seymour was too pleased about it. He came to XUDF later to try and get revenge…" Goat himself, though, had not felt Almasy’s wrath. The woman who would go on to be his wife did, courtesy of a steel chair from a masked Almasy, who then called himself El Terrible. Hey, it was XUDF. Don’t ask. "You know something," another fan said, looking at Craig quizzically. "I’ve seen that move you just did before." "Really," Goat asked, genuinely curious. "Who did it?" "Seymour Almasy." ------------------------------------------- That fucking neck breaker was nearly the death of him. But it was now part of his arsenal, his setup move, of all things! "I must be a closet masochist," Seymour moaned, continuing to push his high performance vehicle to the absolute limit of its endurance. Full Throttle, the move that he used to set-up his vaunted Cactuar Crossface in GCW, was the move that had ended his dream to become the champion of GTT4. But again, that was all okay. Why, he could see the park a half-mile down the road. All he had to do now was turn into it, drive over to where the wrestling festival was being held, and settle things. So long had he waited for this moment that he could scarcely believe it was here. But now that it was… Seymour Almasy grinned a malevolent grin, and simply raised the volume up to eleven. --------------------------------------------------- Hmm. That was odd, Nurse Lovely thought. She could hear something. Music, most likely. Growing louder and louder. And it wasn’t in English. No, it almost sounded like chanting. In a dead language…Latin. Veni, veni, venias, Ne me mori facias Veni, veni, venias, Ne me mori facias As much as she didn’t want to admit it, she knew those lyrics, and what they meant. Only one man would be listening to this particular song this loudly. "CRAIG!" she called, out to the man in the ring, who was now looking down at his boots. He too heard the music. He too knew what it meant. "I know, Esme," the Goat said, resolving himself for whatever battle lay ahead. "He’s coming." TBC. ------- (Lyrics from One Winged Angel off the FF7 OST.
