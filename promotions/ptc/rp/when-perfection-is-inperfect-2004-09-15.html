When Perfection is Inperfect

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 ::There are days in which you feel like you don’t even want to be there. You feel out of place and could wish for nothing more but to find a corner and just hide in it. Those are the days where you just got to suck it up and get through it because for most of us there are better days ahead. For some people, others think there best days have already past them. That they will never be as good as they used to be. But to that man he still believe he has what it takes somewhere inside of him. But he will never find it by sitting behind a desk doing paper work. No, to find what he once had he must be inside that ring, in front of the thousands of cheering fans and under those bright lights. If he is not there then he is not himself, his whole life has been built on that square circle and anything is alien to him. He somehow must get back to his old self before he self destructs in his last possible big shot.:: ::Scottywood sits at a desk in his office in his new York apartment paint the dashboard of his latest model creation, a Dale Earnhardt Jr. #8 Budweiser 2001 Chevy Monte Carlo. With each brush stroke he sighs to himself, almost wishing he were doing something else. Wishing he was back in the good old days of the XWF where he was the king of xtreme. If there were a match, no matter how insane, he would be in it. But now he has been reduce to building model cars, while still fun in its own way is nothing compared to a hell in a cell inferno or steel cage match, and it is absolutely nowhere even close to being in The Perfect match. As he finishes painting the Testors flat black paint on the dashboard he looks up at his wall and sees the photo of his very first XWF World Heavyweight title win.:: Scottywood: Now those were the days….. ::Suddenly were flashed back to February 7th on XWF’s Tuesday Night Storm. His opponent and Scottywood get up and Scottywood kick him in the mid section and set up for the Scottybomb.:: Shane: Here we go, Scottybomb!!!!!! Ref: 1,2,3, ring the bell Timothy Ferra: The winner of this match and new XWF World Heavyweight champion…..Scottywood!!! Shane: WE HAVE A NEW CHAMPION, the VP Scottywood has carried his dream out and has become champion finally. ::But suddenly the flashback cuts to inside a giant steel and glass dome that NGW calls The Battle Dome. We see John Hitchin kicks Scottywood in the stomach and raises him up into a vertical suplex and the crowd goes crazy. Hitchin moves about a foot over to where that cinderblock lay.:: Shane: No, don't do it their Hitchin. Terrence: Do it Hitchin! Do it! ::The crowd is chanting "Do it, Do it" over and over. A huge smile comes over Hitchin's face as he drops down and Scottywood's head is drilled into the cinderblock and it is crushed as Scottywood body lay out cold on the mat.:: Shane: Nail It Down on a fuckin cinderblock! I don't believe he just did that! Terrence: Unbelievable! What a move by Hitchin! Crowd: Holy Shit, Holy Shit, Holy Shit! Shane: Hitchin crawling over to Scottywood. The blood loss is taking affect on him now too and he hooks the leg on Scottywood as the crowd in the Staple Center is blowing the roof off the place. Owen Simmons drops to his knees for the pinfall count.:: Simmons: 1…….2……3! ring the bell. Shane: Its over! Its over…. Timothy Ferra: Scottywood has been eliminated! And your winner, of the 2004 NGW Battle Dome event and NEW NGW Battle Dome champion. Heading to Xtrememania V for a NGW World Title shot…..JOHN HITCHIN!!!!!!! ::With that we leave the Battle Dome and are back to the New York apartment of Scottywood who is sitting back in his chair and shaking his head.:: ::Some say that was the day that Scottywood passed the torch to John Hitchin, but that couldn’t be farther from the truth. Nobody ever passes the torch in wrestling, they get it stolen from them by someone more deserving. He has tried so many times to beat John Hitchin and have come up short. In The Dome he was so close yet so far away.:: Scottywood: If it was just 2 years earlier I could have beat him. I would have kept him down for the 3 count after that SDT instead of letting him kick out. Then he would of never been able to put he through that cinderblock. It would have been me winning the Battle Dome and getting that Xtrememania V shot at Scott King for that World title. ::Scottywood just sits and stares at the model as he then turns and looks around the office which houses a lot of his XWF/NGW memorabilia. He has hockey sticks, awards, title belts and photos of the days past.:: Scottywood: But it isn’t 2 year ago and I wasn’t able to beat John Hitchin and it was him who faced Scott King and won my World Heavyweight title. Ever since I lost that title to Scott King, I have not been myself, I have not been able to wrestle at the level that I used too. Yes I beat Robb Hardy at Border Line but common anyone could do that. I wouldn’t be surprised if he looses to BYE in the first round, hehehe. ::Scottywood pauses as the PTC GTT4 tournament runs through his mind and he looks down at the half built model that lays on the table in front of him.:: Scottywood: The PTC GTT4 tournament, the biggest tournament in wrestling starts in just a few days and what am I doing? I’m sitting in my apartment building a model car. This could be my last shot at being on top and I am sitting here painting plastic. If I loose first round to Chris Drebbin, this nobody then I will be humiliated. At least in GTT2 I lost to Mike Meyhu, I was able to hold some dignity. But if I loose to this wrestling anomaly then it is over, I’ll never be able to show my face in PTC or NGW again. ::Scottywood gets up from his chair, grabs his car keys and his Dale Jr. hat and walks out of the office door.:: ::So it would seem that a little bit of self-confidence talk has gotten Scottywood to get out of his office but is that enough to change his life? No, he has just masked he true misery will a coat of inspiration, but soon that true feeling will resurface, they will hurt even more then they originally did. It will take a lot more to get rid of these feelings then just a bit of talking.:: ::We cut now to Scottywood inside his Hummer and he is driving down the street of New York City with the tinted windows rolls up as he has gotten tired of crazy fans screaming out there car windows too him. In the past 6 months he has gotten hit by 3 fans as there yelling from there cars, one even asking for his autograph while there going 40 mph. He has been getting sick of heading to the body shop every other month and getting sides replaced. Though it has reassured his confidence that even though his talent has slid, his popularity remains as high as ever. Were now just under a mile away from Madison Square Garden which has become to be known as the unofficial home of NGW. The Hummer pulls up to a small building that on the outside says Dave’s Wrestling School. It was one of the places that helped him in the beginning of his wrestling career. He parks the Hummer and gets out and sees the worn open sign hanging in the window and he smiles.:: Scottywood: I haven’t been here in years. I remember spending hours here working on those suplexes and submission moves as Dave yelled from ringside what I was doing wrong. ::Scottywood opens the door and walks in to the dimly lit gym. We see two men in there early 20s in the ring grappling a man standing at ringside with salt and pepper hair and a cane in his right hand. At the sound of the door opening the two men stop wrestling and the three turn to the door.:: Dave: Hello?….Wait, is that…..No….It is, Mr. Woodson. Now here a face I haven’t seen in a long time. How are you doing? Scottywood: Now bad Dave, not bad at all. How have you been? Dave: I’m as good as I can be. Had my hip replace a year ago but I’m still going strong. Scottywood: That’s great, so who are these two guys in the ring? Dave: The one of the left is Jacob Tyler, he is turning out to be a great high flying talent. The man on my right here is my latest prodigy Michael Weston, he is a superb technical wrestler, not as great as you were, but he is close. Scottywood: Cool…..Hey, I was wondering if it was possible for me to step in the ring with Mr. Weston. Maybe show him a thing or two? Dave: What ya say Mike? Michael: Sure, it would be an honor to step into the ring with a 4 time NGW World champion. Scottywood: hehehe, thanks. ::Scottywood slides into the ring with jeans and a t-shirt on, which for his is his normal wrestling attire. He circles for a few seconds and locks up with Mike who quickly turns it into a arm lock and throws Scottywood to the mat. Scottywood shakes it off and locks up quickly again and Scottywood connects with a knee to the stomach and takes a swing but Mike ducks and connects with a german suplex and Scottywood rolls out of the ring holding his back.:: Dave: You ok Scotty? Scottywood: Ya…..just slipped in the ring. I’ll be right back. ::Scottywood walks away from the ring and walks into the bathroom where he heads straight for the sink and splashes some cold water onto his face. He looks up at the mirror and shakes his head.:: Scottywood: Damn, I just got suplexed by a rookie, by a kid who hasn’t even been in a professional match yet. What the hell is wrong with me? ::Suddenly Dave walks into the bathroom and Scottywood quickly turns around to face him.:: Dave: Scotty, what’s wrong? Scottywood: Nothing, everything is fine. Dave: Bullshit Scotty, I know something is up. Scottywood: Why ya think that? Dave: Because 1, you walked into the women’s bathroom and 2, I know you didn’t “slip” in that ring. Scottywood: This is the women’s bathroom? No wonder it is so clean. Dave: Har, Har. Seriously, why did you really come here? Scottywood: For the crazy drunken party tonight. ::Dave just stares at Scottywood:: Scottywood: Fine……For some reason I just haven’t been myself in the ring since I lost the title to Scott King. I just sit at my like a miserable slob working on my models or doing useless paperwork. Dave: And let me guess, you thought by coming here that I could fix it all for you. ::Scottywood nods his head.:: Dave: Then I question if you have even learned anything from me at all. Didn’t I teach you that nobody else can do stuff for you? You must do it for yourself or else it is meaningless. Scottywood: So what should I do? Dave: First you need to stop trying to duplicate the past. You will never be the same as you were in 2000-2002. You need to find the new Scottywood and make him the best that he can be, not the best he ever was. When you trained here in 98 I knew you had talent. I know your gonna find that spark you need to fight, you just gotta look in the right place. How about you give Rodney a call and let him refresh you on why you got into this business. Scottywood: Hmmmm, haven’t seen Rodney in a while, must have been a good six months to a year. Would be good to catch up on shit and reminisce on the good ole XWF days. Thanks Dave. Dave: No problem, now don’t be such a stranger around here. Scottywood: Defiantly, I need to get some payback on that Mike there for that german I took. Dave: Hehehe, ya. And good luck in that PTC tournament. Show Drebbin how we wrestler New York style. Scottywood: Will do, Will do. ::Dave and Scottywood shake hands as they walk out of the spotless women’s bathroom, which is likely that way since no women has used it in 5 years. The scene fades out and then fades in to Scottywood back in his Hummer driving back to his apartment as that mysterious narrator voice.:: ::So it seems that there might be some light at the end of the tunnel as the visit to David’s Wrestling School wasn’t a total waste. Granted being suplex by an 18-19 year old didn’t boost his confidence, in fact it may have lowered it. But that may have all been washed away with the idea of calling long time friend Rodney McArthur who was known as Ice in XWF. The two of them created XWF, which is today, the PTC member federation called NGW. Ice has popped in once in a while to NGW but never has been able to comeback to a full time role that he once held. During his time in XWF he did a superb job running the federation and even was able to win the XWF World title and hold it for 2 months by beating Revolution X and Tuff Tony. He was also the very first person to win what was then called the XWF Intercontinental title, now known as the NGW American title. He won a 7 man battle royal only to vacate the belt 5 days later. They ran the most successful stable in NGW history called the Extremists, which breed numerous NGW Hall of Famers such as The Magician and The Denouncer Adam Jacobs. But the question that still lies is will this talk with Rodney be able to help him or will it just dig him deeper into a pit of despair, dreaming of being back in the days of XWF.:: ::Scottywood opens the door of his apartment and looks around the place. The TV was left on and almost in some ironic matter the 1990 movie “Misery” is playing on HBO. Scottywood smirks and walks into the living room and picks up the cordless phone and looks down at the television.:: Women: MISERY IS ALIVE, MISERY IS ALIVE! OH, This whole house is going to be full of romance, OOOH, I AM GOING TO PUT ON MY LIBERACE RECORDS! (1) Scottywood: There better not be any Liberace music in this movie or I’m gonna puke. ::Scottywood turns the phone on and dials the familiar number is Rodney. He listen to the phone ring 5 times and the answering machine at Rodney’s place picks up. “Hey its Rodney, obviously I’m out so you can do two things, nothing and like it. So leave a message after the beep.”:: Scottywood: Hey Rodney, its Scottywood, um….give me a call when ya get in. ::Scottywood hangs up the phone and looks back down at the TV.:: Man: Eat it till ya choke, you sick, twisted fuck! (2) Scottywood: Ok……hehehe. Now where the hell is Rodney, figures when I wanna talk with him that he is out somewhere. This just fuckin sucks, errr, my luck has been horrible lately. ::We then hear a knock at the door of Scottywood’s apartment and he turns and walks over to the door. He unlocks it and opens it up.:: Scottywood: Ha! What’s up man? ::Standing in the doorway is none other then the man he just tried and call, former Co-Owner of NGW, Rodney “Ice” McArthur.:: Rodney: Not much, been cool. Scottywood: This is fucked up. I just tried calling you like 30 seconds ago and got your machine and now you just show up….damn. Rodney: Well I got a call from Dave 30 minutes ago and I knew you’d be callin, so I figured I better just stop by instead. He told me your having a little problem in the ring. Scottywood: Figures he’d do that…..and I guess I am having a slight problem. I don't know, it seems that I have past my prime and in that downward spiral. I mean today I got german suplexed by a kid in a wrestling school. And in just a few days I go into the first round of the PTC GTT4 tournament and face some unknown jackass Chris Drebbin. My fear is I’m gonna get upset in round 1 and be humiliated. ::Rodney just laughs and shakes his head for a moment.:: Rodney: Are you listening to yourself? Seriously, you sound like some whinny little bitch. This is not the Scottywood who I created XWF with. The man that created The Perfect match and has risked his life way too many times for ratings in XWF and NGW. Dave wanted me to remind you why you got into this and it wasn’t to win tournaments or impress PTC. The reason we got into this shit is to hear those fans, to here them chanting our names and hanging on our every move. To see them go crazy when we connect with a finisher or when he hit someone with a steal chair. Because no matter how many title were holding or no matter how many tournaments we win, it all means nothing if we can’t get that reaction from the fans whether it be them chanting our name or throw a beer bottle at our heads. ::Scottywood smiles and lightly laughs as Rodney ends his mini speech.:: Scottywood: Now that brings up a memory of a match in Canada, damn beer bottle missed my head by inches, though that’s gonna be a prominent problem when you hand out free beer at a Canadian event, they almost had riots for that stuff….. Rodney: Hahaha, ya, that was a crazy night. But do you understand the rest of what I said. Scottywood: Ya, I do and I think it actually makes sense. I just gotta out there and have fun and not worry about winning or loosing and just worry about putting on a good show for the fans. Rodney: Now your sounding like the 4 time NGW World champion I know. Scottywood: Now I think I’m feeling ready for this match. ::Suddenly we hear Scottywood’s cell phone ringing in his pocket. He takes it out and looks at the caller ID.:: Scottywood: PTC? Hmmm, wonder what they want. ::Scottywood opens the phone and places it up near his ear.:: Scottywood: Hello Voice: Hello Scottywood? Scottywood: Ya? Voice: This is PTC, I’m calling you to notify you that your opponent for your first round match in GTT4, Chris Drebbin, has withdrawn from the tournament. So you will receive a bye into the second round. Scottywood: Are you shittin me? Voice: Um….no Sir, I’m not shittin you. Scottywood: Damn, well thanks. Voice: Your welcome, have a good day Sir. ::Scottywood hangs up the phone and places it back into his pocket as he shakes his head.:: Scottywood: Don’t believe it, just when I’m ready to step back into the ring I get a call that I get my bye back. That really pisses me off, but at least I’m ready for the second round, against whoever I face. Rodney: That does suck, probably knew he was gonna loose so he figured he wouldn’t even try…..Truly pathetic. Scottywood: Indeed……Well since you’re here and since I don’t have to wrestle this week, how about we head out for a few beers. Rodney: Sounds good to me…..but your payin. Scottywood: Sure why not, I just toped my best performance in the GTT. ::Scottywood grabs his keys and him and Ice walk out of the apartment door as the movie “Misery” just comes to an end and the credits start to roll.:: ::So it seems that Scottywood has his confidence back but well have to wait a bit longer to see how he will fare in the ring. For now he will celebrate his advancing to the second round, even though it was due to a withdraw. To him he doesn’t matter how he got there, all that matter is that he is there. So now all that there is left is 95 guys, 95 men and he will be crowned the winner of GTT4……but that is a long time from now and for him he is just going to have to take it one round at a time. So whom will the next round bring? That’s something nobody know, but whoever it is, it will not be an easy match.:: Note: Flashback sequence is from XWF Tuesday Night Storm, orginal airing on 3-7-00 and from NGW Border Line, orginal airing on 3-30-04.
