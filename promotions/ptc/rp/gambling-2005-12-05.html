He had taken around $10,000.00 with him to Las Vegas, he hoped his night would consist of slot machines, roulette tables, a few games of blackjack, and a few games of craps. Bomber wasn’t a gambling man but decided that he would treat himself for a change. He had dressed up all smart, a nice tuxedo, clean shaved, hair styled like nobody else could, a touch of arrogance people would say but Bomber didn’t care, he looked good and that's all that mattered to him. He pulled up outside the hotel, having driven himself, and pulled up the handbrake once at a standstill. Bomber: Not bad, looks like he finally did something right for a change.
