A real church, a Black Priest

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 [The screen opens to the pitch black of the night except for the full moon projecting light over the empty streets. The howling of wolves is faint in the background as the wind blows shaking the leaves of trees. The camera zooms up to a tall historic black building with a huge cross on the top. Graveyards surround the massive building adding onto the eerie feeling of the night. Footsteps are heard as the camera crew begins to walk to the front door of the old church. A hand is pulled out from behind the camera as it slowly knocks on the solid metal door. A wolf howls as the door creaks opening allowing the visitors to enter. ] Voice: In here. [The camera shakes as the crew hears the sound of a deep demonic voice coming from the living room area. They begin walking towards that direction as the hardwood floor underneath their feet creak with each step. Entering the living room you notice several burning candles. The walls are covered with crosses, plaques, and several heads are seen on a mantle piece. A fire is burning in the fireplace as two large black sofas are seen. The camera zooms across each one until it notices a figure sitting Indian style praying on the end of the sofa closest to the fire.] Voice: Welcome to the rest area of the Black Priest. [The instant the voice quit speaking a thunder rumbled outside as the wolves begin to howl louder. The camera crew walked over to the black sofa to reveal the figure of Rzarector. The Black Priest is wearing his black hooded robe, and underneath the hood you see the flamboyant red eyes that are a trademark of the presence of demons. Rzarector hisses as his fangs flash from his mouth. The Godlike Warrior then begins to speak.] Rzarector: The citizens of this community shut down this church long ago. They said the church contained demonic spirits, and that anyone who attended here would burn in Hell upon death. The words of politics eventually scared away everyone except me. I bought this old place about five years ago despite the warnings from fellow neighbors. The environment in this place is not evil, but rather it reflects what good old religion used to be. [Rzarector points out the several old and rusted crosses hanging throughout the room.] Rzarector: There was once a great story about a book called, The Book of Life. The story went that if your name was not in the book upon arrival in Heaven that you would burn in Hell. Well that’s kind of odd considering how Odium’s mother has died recently. Was her name in the book of life? One could only hope so, but seeing how Odium is betting his match on beating me there is no telling what might become of her. Just in case you forgot I am the son of Jesus! What I say goes. Odium, you have messed with the wrong Priest this time. [Rzarector arises from the black sofa. The Black Priest takes a couple of steps to the fireplace and gazes at the mantle piece above it. Staring at the heads Rzarector makes a small hissing sound as he wipes the dust off the name plates.] Rzarector: Here is my collection of wrestlers I have sent to Hell. They were valiant fighters but nothing compared to the strength of the great Rzarector! [The name plates reveal to be names such as Tony Vigilante, Orlando Rivera, Silent, The Great One, and the Leegend Lee Smith.] Rzarector: Those are truly some impressive names. It just seems to me that after being defeated by me they never recovered. I drove those poor fools into retirement just like I shall drive Odium into retirement. Soon he shall join his mother in the pits of Hell. Tony Vigilante was supposed to be something but just another fluke. Orlando Rivera was supposed to be a world champion but I destroyed him. Silent, I carried our team and eventually ran him off. The Great One just couldn’t keep up with me and decided to give up. The Leegend Lee Smith decided that if he couldn’t beat me and be on the top of the FAW that he just didn’t want it at all. [Rzarector looks in disappointment upon the skulls of his former opponents. His red eyes burnt like wildfire at the thought of their demise.] Rzarector: Will Odium become another waste of a wrestling career? Should he just forfeit before this match even starts? Shall he sacrifice his mother’s eternal life on this match? The questions seem obvious that he should in fact just give up. The world will be changing momentum in the GTT4 tournament as the goodness will begin to prevail. [Rzarector walks from the fireplace back over to the black sofa. Instead of sitting down no it he just leans on it with his hands. The camera crew is being silent.] Rzarector: I always valued myself that whenever I performed an interview for Core Wrestling or Primetime Central that I never had to have an interviewer. My personality would be enough to keep the attention of my opponent. I don’t need to answer anyone’s questions because I have enough questions that I have to ask myself every day. [A wolf howling is heard outside once again as rain begins to hit hard on the roof of the old church.] Rzarector: The first question that comes to my mind is, “Will Odium be able to concentrate on our match?” I mean his mother did die. Wouldn’t that serve as a distraction? First off, he is going to have to face someone who has the look of a demon. Then secondly he will have to face the distraction of the fans, and on top of that he will have to worry about his mother’s death! This match will be to my advantage because if he begins taking control I will just say something about his mother to make him mad! Everyone knows that anger does not win a match, but using your brain does! [Rzarector walks over to the fireplace and adds on a couple of logs.] Rzarector: The second question that comes to my mind is how will this inexperienced rookie be able to compare to me? I’ve been at the top, and Odium hasn’t. He doesn’t know what it’s like to be put under pressure. When I left the greatness of FAW to come to Core the pressure still didn’t leave because everyone still expects things from me. That is a price you have to pay though. Yet it isn’t a price that Odium has had to pay. [Rzarector gets done building onto the current fire, and walks over to the plaques on the walls. The Black Priest stares at each one as he looks at the records for holding the titles for so long. Turning back towards the camera Rzarector has a hollow look on his face.] Rzarector: If Odium did win this tournament how is he going to hold onto the belt? This man just shed a tear on national television. Every champion or ex champion knows that to be a good champion you have to be cold hearted. You can’t care about a single thing in the world, and you have to be willing to put your wrestling career ahead of everything else in life. Are you willing for that commitment yet Odium? [Rzarector leaves the plaques on the wall and walks over to a huge statue of Jesus hanging on the cross. He leans down in front of it.] Rzarector: Jesus, through many trials and tribulations I have at last come to prove my dominance in the wrestling world. With your help we can go all the way and defeat everyone that is in our path. The kingdom of God will prove to be the worst enemy among the snakes that crawl throughout this competition. Odium will be first. He has lost his mother, and to lose his wrestling match (the most important thing in his life) will truly lead him to suicide. The dawn of a new day is at hand in Prime Time Central. What once was a place for evil to lurk will now be a place for light to prevail. [Rzarector stands up and kisses the statue of Jesus. He begins walking to his front door as the camera crew follows him.] Rzarector: I want to thank you all for coming on such a short notice. The moon is full, the wolves howl, and the rain is coming down hard. That means it was a perfect time for an interview. In closing I would like to ask Odium to rethink his upcoming match because it could prove to be more lethal to him than his mother dying. I don’t go in for the win; I go in for the kill. There is nothing more that I would want than Odium’s skull planted on my mantle piece! [With that Rzarector opened his front door. The rain bailed in from outside as the lightning made streaks across the sky. The camera crew looked worried because they brought no umbrella, but the Black Priest showed no feelings like a true champion would. He pushed them out in the cold rain and slammed the door behind them. The scene then fades.]
