Date: 2006-10-20 When you think of someone who has driven themselves to complete destruction you either do one of two things. The first is feel sorry for them, they maybe weren’t right in the head. Or earlier circumstances in their life had just gotten to them throughout the years. On the other hand you could just laugh, looking down upon them like common trash. When people see homeless people in the street they normally don’t stop to think about how they could of gotten there. Hell they may have been drunks their whole lives or been hooked on drugs. Not many people think that maybe they survive on the streets for a reason. The later is what the people think of The Real Deal. He thinks otherwise. This wasn’t in the plan! Time: 12:05 am Date: 20th Sept 2006 It is just past midnight, lights in homes are slowly turning off as the minutes pass by. One that light that does not turn off though is The Real Deal’s. He looks up at the street light above his head and nods to it. Acknowledgement is a great thing, even when acknowledging non living things. He looks away from the light and sits down on a couple of cardboard boxes which have been flattened into a rectangle shape, a bed for the night. Real Deal’s home is now an alleyway, a long time it has been since he slept in a proper bed, eaten proper food and had a proper drink. The alleyway is gusty and the cold wind sears across Deal’s rugged skin. Age has grown on him throughout the last year, living on the street not treating him kindly at all. Keeping him warm is a large black fur coat, seemingly stolen as he could never afford anything like it. On his legs a rough pair of jean insulate his thighs Along the left hand side of his face lies a long scar, moving from the bottom of his eye down to the point on his jar. He brings his hand up and stroke it with his index finger, no expression comes across his face. As the wind picks up a large amount of trash sweeps its way down the alleyway, flying all around Deal and into his face. One particular item catches his eye: "Drive. Dedication. The will to survive. They became tradition. WILL YOU? GTT6 IS COMING!" Deal jumps off his ass as if a rocket has been lit beneath him. His eyes widen as they begin to swell. It’s that time again? It has seemed so long. I never thought there would be another one of these. I must enter…I have no choice, I must save myself.
