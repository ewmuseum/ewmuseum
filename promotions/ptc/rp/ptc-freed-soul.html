Freed soul

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[OOC note: It's not that long or great, I have a lot on my mind and couldn't really get to writing like I would have liked to and I may get another rp up, I don't know if not sorry to those I may let down, I just don't have it in me right now to crack out anything great.] When we last seen Kraze he was taking things pretty hard, he lost almost all that was close to him and it showed no signs of letting up, the most recent loss came in the form of Snow who appeared for a short while only to die soon after. Kraze is going threw changes and some he does not know why, like why can he see when something bad will happen but not be able to do anything about it, that really eats him up on the inside because every time that happens it is someone that is close to him or that he cares about in some manner, Kraze also found out that he is indeed related in some way to Hitler, yea that is the one, what does this all mean to him? We don’t know, he seems to be more confused and out of it than anything else, and now he has what could be the match of his career, it is for a title in the what some would call a “super-fed” which had housed many federations that he has been apart of for years now. “My chance to shine” Kraze thought to His self as he heard of the PTC Extreme title division, He thinks that no one is more Extreme than him, he thinks he holds the record for the wrestler with the most hospital visits in one year. Kraze sits on a rock that is a lot bigger than what we can see, the snow piled up around it is what makes it appear that much smaller, the sun barley shines threw the clouds as Kraze remains on the rock, looking to the sky. “Why did all have to die?” Kraze says as he stares into the deep, grey sky, he is talking about all the ones that he has lost over the past few months, no matter who it is they can’t seem to be around long enough for Kraze to think that is is not his fault for their death. The tree’s behind Kraze seem endless as they rise toward the sky only to give way to the deep grey skies which are snow filled, we seem to be either at the mid-point or a higher-point in Lowell, Mass at Fort Hill for this is a site we have seen many times, something that we are now use to seeing because over time with Kraze we have been here so many times it is like a bed time story we never forgot, and we will never forget either. “Snow, Grand pop, Freeze, Mom”, Kraze names off a few people that have passed on in his life time that he takes some, if not full blame for dieing since he seen their death creeping up on them and it was to close to the time that it happened so he was powerless to do anything about it, but he use to care for all life so much to the point that he needed someone or something to blame, something to put the fault on to hate, and that “thing” just happened to be him. “I’ll make it up to you all, I will make you all proud of me, proud to of once knew me.” Kraze continues to talk to the ones that have passed as he rests his face in his hands; his tone gets lower as he continues to speak… “They will all see what I can do, they will all notice that I am as extreme as anyone of them, they will all remember my name when I leave that arena after making sure each and every last one was over the ropes, I promise you all that.” Kraze talks to the dead as if they still live. “Since you all have left I have had nothing but time to think about many things, from why you guys died to why I had something to do with it, you know what I have figured out?” Kraze pauses as if they will reply… “I have figured out that not only did you all have it coming, you all deserved all that you have got!” Kraze starts to laugh, softly then increasingly deeper as he raises his head with a big smile on his face. “Who are you all to sit up there and send your words down to me, who are you to get into my mind after your bodies currently reside in the ground being a maggots main course?” Kraze’s whole domineer changes as he once again looks to the sky. “I am Kraze, a man of no regard for others, I am Kraze a man that once had the balls to get up and go, I am Kraze a man that no longer cares who has passed on, for you are dead and mean nothing more to me, I am living, you are dead, you are nothing.” Kraze smiles as he stands on the rock, he reaches deep into his back pocket revealing a wallet, he removes many pictures from it as he stands on the rock… “Freeze, see you in hell buddy, well if there was a hell, but shit I think it’s really Earth!” Kraze tears the picture of his late tag team partner into pieces as he holds another one up. “Ah, good old Mom…” Kraze stares down at the picture of his late mother as if he does not want to do it… “Remember that time you did not get me that toy truck I wanted?” Kraze talks to the picture of his mother as if it may talk back. “Remember!” Kraze now screams at the picture… “No you don’t do you; I did not expect that you would.” With that Kraze tears the picture in two and tosses it into the snow which covers the ground, the pieces of Freeze’s picture now lye under the pictures of his mother. “Now it is time for the first one to die!” Kraze screams out loud. Kraze pulls a picture of his grandfather out and holds it up, “Yea your up!” Kraze rips the picture of his grandfather in two and tosses it to the ground and it gathers in the small pile forming. “Snow, you slept around, but hell you once were quite a great piece, thanks for the times, but you really suck.” Kraze rips the picture which soon follows in the path of the others. Kraze stares to the sky and mutters a few words, “I am free.” The scene fades leaving Kraze standing on the rock staring to the sky.
