Jesse Jamester Public Access Pimp Joined: 06 Apr 2003 Posts: 18 Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 9:47 am Post subject: Limited Boundaries: Part 1 (GT #1) Limited Boundaries: Part 1 Near the lonely roads of the Southern boarder of Cuba imported ships are being unloaded and reloaded with new shipments of people from Cuba. Only 200 miles away sits Jesse Jamester the Canadian Wrestler who had gone pro at only age 20. His career a shooting success yet somehow he finds himself stuck in Cuba. His destiny now is to find a way back to the U.S. of A. and arrive on time for the Global Title Tournament. Here Jesse the 6’6” 266 pound man with long brown hair, a brown goatee, and the clothes on his back sits on the rugged streets of a busy city. Those who are still in the city are either underground or are living normal lives in a free society. “Damn it! How the hell did I end up here?” Jesse says out loud. His words just scramble with all the people who flow in and out of their houses and city shops. “Whoa…” says Jesse looking at the people. “Hello…” says a child to Jesse. “Hi, do you know where I am?” asks Jesse. “No,” says the child who runs away. “Damn it!!” yells Jesse. He gets u and walks within the crowd searching for someone. But as he comes into a mix he stops dead. A large circle is surrounding a woman dressed in lace who is exposing most of her skin. More of an African decent she has a boa constrictor on her shoulders that is entering her mouth sticking it’s tongue out. “Whoa baby!” says Jesse smiling, “Maybe I’ll stay afterwards.” Jesse stands there watching rubbing his goatee that was brown and is now black with unshaven whiskers. His long time away from the U.S. has led to bad smell and appearance. His hair all dirty from the filthy streets and his clothes a bit torn and ragged as though he were a rat. The woman comes up to Jesse with the snake in his mouth and just as she winks she bites it off and swallows it. Jesse awakens in panic, but he isn’t in the mix of a crowd he’s sitting on the corner of a street in Cuba while the rain comes down. Dirty yes, ragged yes, tired yes, but he has to reach America so he gets up and walks down the lonely streets looking for help. [Back in the U.S.] Jesse’s two friends Bladez and Steve Andrews both sit on a couch watching the news as Bladez turns the channel to MTV. Bladez is a 6’2” 245 lb man with short hair and a small goat that is a stripe on his chin. He has pierced ears and a tattoo on his neck that reads “4Life”. Steve Andrews is 6’4” and weighs 250 lb. He has long black hair and no facial hair. The two are best friends and have been through bad times, good times, and emotional times. “That was the funniest thing we have EVER done to Jesse!” laughs Steve with a high-five to Bladez. “I know… He was so messed after those drugs we put in his drink. I know he’ll get home but what if he knows we did it?” wonders Bladez. “Eh it’s okay man. He has NO clue. Come on, if you were drugged and put on an army airplane then dropped off in Cuba would you know?” asks Steve. “Probably not…” replies Bladez. “Exactly!” says Steve. Bladez changes the channel to PTC. The news reads: “Coming up April 12, 2003, on Sunday is the first ever I-PPV between all organizations that are hosted by PTC. Featuring the Main Event will be a 14 Man Battle Royal. The winner getting the Global Title! Some of those who will be in the match are Mike Meyhu, Tristian Belmont, and Jesse Jamester whom are from Lights Out Wrestling “says a young reporter though Bladez clicks off the power button. “OMG! Were in some deep poop!” says Steve. “Oh yeah!” replies Bladez. The two jumps up and grab there stuff heading out the door to catch the first plane to Cuba. [Back in Cuba] It's a cold night, your typical rainy weather. The streets are semi-empty as an occasional car or two travels down the snowy roads. PTC cameras scan around the area until they notice a figure walking. As the man passes by one of the streetlights we notice that it's Ace. His destination is America; he just walks down the street not paying attention to dead animals or sick children. He cuts a corner and cameras continue to follow. He stops in front of a dark building and reaches into his pocket. He pulls out a phone and dials a number, but no one is reached. Jesse soon finds a garage and walks in. Cameras follow him in as he closes the door, preventing the cold from outside from coming in. It's a dark room and was barely able to see what’s around until the flicking of a switch is heard and numerous lights come on revealing a warehouse. On one side of it we see a Mitsubishi Eclipse and a Toyota Supra. Obviously, some rich guys two cars. He takes his coat off and rests it on one of the chairs inside and then walks into another room. Cameras show him make his way to a part of the wall with a calendar hanging. He grabs a pen that’s near by and puts an X on April 11th. At the time it was around 11:45 PM and Jesse seemed to have wrapped up his day. Cameras zoom in and see Jesse now sitting on a chair with a desk in front of him. He pulls a wallet out of his pocket to reveal a picture. Jesse stares at it for a while and then smiles. After awhile he takes the picture and tucks it into his pocket again. He then grabs the pen again and pulls out a pocket journal he has. He begins to write. ”December 7th, 1941. Everybody knows that day. It was the day Japan surprised everybody and bombed Pearl Harbor. It was known as the day of infamy. Well another day like that is coming. In about no less than 24 hours I face 14 men. This will be the new day on infamy, because I’m going to drop a bomb on all of them. It all ends soon and soon I move on to becoming Global Champ. See the PT C knew what they were doing when they put their best 14 in this match. They knew that I would hurt them like a hunter searching for deer meat. But what they didn’t know is that I'm aiming at ending their 13 superstars in the worst ways. See not only can I take them all out physically, but also I can destroy them mentally. I am a physical and mental part to this sport. Do they know why? Yes, everything I say is true and they know it. The only way you get around here in the PTC is because you sleep, eat, and breathe wrestling, and you do whatever it takes to get around and get your name noticed. If it were I, I’d be ashamed. They can’t earn anything by themselves because they’re worthless. They’re afraid to talk back to me. My words are like a dagger with a jagged edge. Each word stabs them and I break them down. On NEW Era’s PPV, I'll break them down for good, the only thing that will be left of them will be they’re skyscraper sized egos. That’s the only thing people know them for. After our match they’ll know I am for this business as Bush is for taking down Iraq. They will know that they couldn’t stop me in that ring. I plan on becoming the greatest in PTC and there’s nothing anyone can do about that. As soon as the bell rings its go time, and the ass whooping begins. Don’t be scared though Jesse, I'll be sure to take care of myself. They won’t know what hit them when the 3 count goes down. They'll just be laid out on they’re backs wondering why they even went through with the match. I know I’m going to play my cards right, but they, they just going to end up folding. Sweet dreams Jesse. Good luck tomorrow.” With the last letters from Jesse written he sits back in the chair falling asleep. [Airport] Bladez and Steve hurry through the door running up to the counter blabbering out questions. “When’s the first flight?” asks Steve. “For Cuba?” follows Bladez. “We need it quickly,” says Steve. “Real quick!” replies Bladez quickly. “VERY QUICKLY!” say both men. The lady looks down taking her time. The two are very impatience and almost choke her but the risk of going to jail crosses their minds so they stop. The two forget about it and continue standing there waiting. “The first flight will be in 10 minutes. We have a whole flight open almost, would you like to buy a seat?” asks the cashier. “YES!” yells Steve in her face spitting as well. “For 2!” replies Bladez the same way. The lady wipes her face off and gets the two tickets quickly so they won’t talk again. Bladez and Steve hurry to the gate for they’re flight. The two catch it just on time. [Jesse awakes] Jesse awakes after a good nap from a hand tapping him on the shoulder. Jesse looks up and sees a large black man. He falls to the floor and grabs a near by mop. The man laughs and extends a hand. Jesse wonders whether it’s a trick or not. He is lifted up and the two converse. “So you know who I am?” asks Jesse. “Yes I do,” says the man in perfect English. “Wow! I thought only government agents spoke English,” replies Jesse. “No that’s what everyone thinks. Anyway I am Sam…I didn’t think I left the door unlocked but I did. Is there anything I can get you Jesse?” asks Sam. “How do you know my name?” replies Jesse. “Lights Out Wrestling, it’s an important part of this towns interactivity. We all watch it since it’s the best of wrestling and because we get it free, the only channel,” replies Sam. “Well all I want is a bed,” says Jesse. Sam gets Jesse a bed and he falls asleep as Bladez and Steve make there way for him. _________________ Get the f#ck away from this sh#t, or be the b#tch who dies messing with the one and only, true LOW Member, JESSE "THE KID" JAMESTER!
