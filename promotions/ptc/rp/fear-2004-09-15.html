FEAR

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 Fear lies within everyone, whether they’d like to admit it or not. We all fear something at some point. The Technical Spectacle, probably the most egotistical and arrogant character to ever step foot into the squared circle, would be the first to admit that he fears something. But it’s not what you would think of from a normal wrestling star. He doesn’t fear getting injured in the ring, losing friends, losing a title, or even being fired. In fact, in his current position, he could care less about the whole wrestling scene all together. His mind is in Hollywood, and he fears the Failing. Jerms breezed through many others to win the 4 Winds Wrestling Tournament at Maelstrom 3: Legacy to not only become the first ever Four Winds Champion, but he also received a Talesmith Studios Movie Contract. This new movie deal took control over The Technical Spectacle. He devoted all of his time into his movies, while in the process burning many bridges with his current employer the 4 Winds Wrestling organization. The Wrestling scene just wasn’t doing anything for him anymore. He had done enough already and would do anything to bail out of his contract with the 4WW. But he was legally obligated to work two PTC Events: PTC Survival and the forthcoming GTT Tournament as well as the 4WW upcoming pay-per-view: Maelstrom V: Winds of War. After that he was done, out of here forever. But what happened if he failed in Hollywood? He knew he could never go back, he just couldn’t. Not after all that trash he talked about how pathetic his own organization was. Would any other PTC organizations welcome him in with outstretched arms? Of course not, 4WW would blacklist him and the rest would follow. This was it, a live altering decision. He was done with Wrestling and moving into Hollywood forever. Jerms sat there at the end of his bed with his head resting on the palm of his right hand. Sitting in his boxer shorts thinking, calculating he wondered what had he gotten himself into? A large black address book sat on the bed. He reached over and flipped through it till he found the page marked “September 2004”. This page was covered with Events that Jerms was to partake in. Of these dates (which seemed like hundreds to him) there was only one that he cringed at the sight of. The PTC GTT Tournament. Jerms (Muttering): Why did I let them talk me into doing this? Although I want to do good and the get the hell out of this business, I don’t want the damn 4WW basking in my success if I do well! If Jerms ever had his way, which he usually didn’t, he would have elected to be a Free Agent for the Tournament. Why should 4WW share the spotlight, what did it do for him? But since Jerms is still under contract, on paper, with the 4WW he had to be listed as a representative of the 4WW. He continued looking at the date book. He was excited about the upcoming publicity tour for one of his new movies “Mission at Guadalcanal”. Even though the movies Director wanted Jerms to come back on set and re-shoot some scenes, he was still excited. But something caught his eye on the date book and when it did he attempted to look away. He couldn’t help it, that name just kept leaping out at him. Jerms (Reading aloud): “Lionheart” Tyler Babylon, First Round of the GTT Tournament. This angered him. The one and only thing in his book that had anything remotely to do with his new found enemy in wrestling, and he couldn’t keep his eyes off of it. His cell phone rang. He answered it to hear the voice of Dasher Devious, one half of his protection in the 4WW. Jerms (Vacantly): What’s going on Dash? Dasher: Jerms, I’ve been thinking man. Are we really leaving the Wrestling Business? I mean, what happens if we don’t succeed in Hollywood man? Jerms began to seethe. How dare an employee of his question his decisions! Jerms: We won’t fail! I thought you were tough, but really, you’re just a little boy. What are you so afraid of? Dasher: I’m not afraid, I just want to keep my options open that’s all! Jerms: No, you’re living in fear baby. We’re done with the 4WW and Wrestling in general. We just ride this tournament out and then we’re done. Our movies are going to be great trust me. Dasher: Don’t ever tell me I fear something! Jerms: Oh shut the hell up! I’m tired, this is beneath me anyway! Jerms hangs up the phone and flings it across the room in frustration. Tired, he crawls up to the front of his plush king size bed and goes under the covers. Almost instantaneously he falls asleep. The people are real The place is real The FEAR is real Five people have been sent to a place they have never been before to determine if its haunted They record their own experiences there is no film crew they are ALONE. “Voodoo” by Godsmack begins to play as the opening credits for MTV’s Original Program FEAR begins to play. The screen goes black until words begin to scroll onto the screen as an announcer reads it. Announcer: Two years ago MTV brought you FEAR, where five young investigators were sent to a haunted place to investigate supernatural disturbances. Now after a three year hiatus, MTV is proud to bring you Celebrity Fear. The screen goes black again, until a shot comes up of a room that looks like an unfinished basement. Dark stained grey walls with a grey hard floor were the most opulent looking things in the room. Five plain beds with a single white sheet were place throughout the room along with five chairs and a small desk with a computer resting on it. A paint-chipped door is thrust open in the back of the room and five people with potato sacks over their heads enter the room in a single file line with their hands on each other shoulders so as to not get lost from one another. They remove their masks and the five people size each other up. The first three to enter were none other then Jerms himself, and his two twin bodyguards the Devious Brothers. The other men were two recognizable men, David Hasselhoff and Ron Jeremy. There is silence in the room until David speaks first. Hasselhoff: Well guys this should be real spooky! I guess we get started on this computer thing here! Donovan Devious, the much larger of the two brothers cuts in. Donovan: Hold it right there pretty boy, the only person that is going to be calling the shots here is this man (pointing to Jerms). Ron Jeremy: Oh shut the hell up you twit. Dasher: Easy there porn boy, you’re going to get yourself hurt. Through all the bickering, Jerms has managed to slowly make his way over to one of the beds and has fallen asleep. Hasselhoff takes a seat at the desk. Hasselhoff: You guys, I think we got off on the wrong foot. Can we start over? I think you all know who I am right? Ron Jeremy: No. Dasher: No. Donovan: Yea…oh wait…No. Hasselhoff seems to be taken back at this. He thought for sure they would have recognized him, the creator of Baywatch. Hasselhoff: Come on guys, you know me! I was Mitch Buchanan, from Baywatch. Does that ring a bell? Dasher(smiling): You mean Boob Watch! Dasher begins to laugh hysterically until Donovan punches him in the shoulders. Ron Jeremy seems to be perturbed and cuts in. Ron Jeremy: Yea, I know who you are. But (pointing at the twins) who are you two? Donovan: The Devious Brother from one of Prime Time Central’s wrestling feds, the 4WW. Hasselhoff: Oh yea, I’m a big fan of you guys. Ron Jeremy: Pipe down, beach boy. What about your friend over there (pointing to Jerms)? Dasher: That’s the Greatest Technical Wrestler in History! Show some respect. Ron Jeremy: Well who ever he is, he’s apart of this crap too so he better wake up. Donovan goes over to the bed to wake Jerms up. Hasselhoff and Ron Jeremy start up the FEAR program already loaded onto the computer. A flash of information comes up on screen as a narrator reads it. THE RULES OF FEAR: 1.) You MUST follow the instructions given in the investigation of each supernatural hot spot. 2. You MUST use your camera equipment to record your experiences at all times. 3. IF, and only IF you complete both nights of the investigation will you be paid the FIVE THOUSAND dollars for charity. 4. If you decide to quit, you will be paid nothing and another team member must complete the assigned dare before the team can continue. Narrator: You will each be assigned a color. During each mission the assigned color or colors will be attempting the mission while another color will stay behind a be the narrator. Fear is not an option. Jerms has awoken thanks to the Narrators loud voice and he seems angry. He rubs his eyes and squints before getting up and pacing around the room. Jerms: Look at this place, are we in prison here or what? Hasselhoff (standing up): Hi you must be Jerms, I’m David Hasselhoff. Jerms: Who? Hasselhoff: You know, from Baywatch. I’m also very big in Germany. I sang at the Berlin Wall! Jerms: I don’t care, just leave me alone. The attention turns back to the Computer where Dasher sits frantically waving his arms in the air. Dasher: Look the computer is doing something. The Narrator comes back on the screen giving information about the place they are to investigate. They are evidently at a plantation on grounds where a battle from the Civil War took place. Many people believe the Plantation is haunted. Narrator: MISSION 1- Red will navigate. Orange is to investigate the basement of the Main House on the Plantation. Further instructions will be given by the Navigator upon arrival. If Orange fails to complete the mission Orange will be eliminated and another player will have to complete the assigned task. Hasselhoff: Alright, I’m the Navigator. Ron Jeremy: Bad news for you Jerms. Jerms laughs. He’s seen enough and will not subject himself to this humiliating reality show anymore. Despite being a tad bit afraid, it’s more of a pride issue. Jerms: Oh man, you know what. I just remembered it’s my friend’s birthday and I have to meet him for dinner. Looks like I quit gentleman. Devious Brothers: Yea we quit! Hasselhoff: You jerks can’t quit, then it will just be me and Long Dong Silver over here! Come on guys I’m begging you! Jerms and The Devious Brothers grab their belongings and head for the door. When Ron Jeremy stands up. Ron Jeremy: Screw this, I’m not staying here with this idiot alone. I’m out of here too. Hey Jerms, are you in the Movies? Jerms stops suddenly and slowly turns around. Jerms: Yes….why? Ron Jeremy: Oh nothing, I just wanted to know if you wanted to be in one of mine? Jerms(Bug-Eyed): LET’S GO….NOW! MOVE IT! In a mad panic, the three men rush out the door. Ron Jeremy shrugs and heads for the door despite Hasselhoff’s pleading. Narrator: All other players have quit. Purple will have to complete the remaining Missions alone! Hasselhoff: NOOOOOO!!!!! Jerms suddenly awakes from his deep sleep. Jerms: What a horrible dream! The Screen Fades Out. -The End- ***FEAR is a licensed trademark from Music Television. Ron Jeremy and David Hasselhoff were played by actors in the scene and were not the actual person***
