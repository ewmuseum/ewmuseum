The Road to Survival

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-20 The Road to Survival {The scene opens up to an old, abandon dirt road in Des Moines Iowa. It’s cold, dark, and damp on this abandon road. The only thing that is giving it light is the full moon. There is not a soul in sight except for the critters rummaging through the cornfields that surround this road of empty solitude. Up ahead of the road we see a lone traveler setting forth with a goal and purpose in his heart. Cold and hungry the drifter moves on this road with only one thing in mind…….”To be the best!”} {You see this drifter has a long history at being the best in his profession. Some have portrayed him as the perfect gladiator, a physical specimen, and a force to be reckoned with. But there is always a price for that road to glory, and this lone warrior has paid the ultimate price. The lost of loved ones. Who would have known that this profession had so much at stake? But a new competition has arrived, one that is on a much grander scale. I-PPV Survival, Prime Time Central. Where the best around this prestige business of ours gather to compete for one thing….....”To survive!”} 12:35 am, Des Moines Iowa. Deserted road on the outskirts of old man Duncan’s farm. {Our drifter by the name of Jack Jeffery’s aka Ace has been walking for about a half an hour and the fatigue is getting the best of him. Ace looks all around until he finds a clearing in the corn field.} Ace: This looks like a good spot. {Ace pulls out his sleeping bag out of his brown worn out back pack. Ace heads over to the inviting clearing and sets up camp. After all he can’t walk all night and he has a big day tomorrow. Those are his last thoughts of the night before the sleep catches up to him.} 5:40 am {Ace is quickly wakened by the sounds of a monstrous tractor nearby. Startled, Ace grabs his things, but is too late when the tractor is barreling down on him. The driver of the monstrosity stops the tractor and gets out. It was an elderly man in his late 60’s who was farming the corn. It was Old man Duncan himself and he didn’t look to pleased.} Old Man Duncan: Dag nap it! You damn hippies get the hell out of my fields!!! Ace: Listen sir I’m sorry for the intrusion but I’m tired and hungry and I was wondering if I could get a place to stay tonight? Old Man Duncan: What the hell do I look like son? My place is not no fancy Motel Six! Ace: Listen, I’ll be able to pay you………. Old man Duncan: Payment ………Payment! Well that’s a horse of a different color! How much are we talking about here? Ace: One thousand dollars! Old man Duncan: Well golly! But how do I know you’re not some type of hippie freak? Ace: Because sir I’m a wrestler on my way to Prime Time Centrals I-PPV! 7:00 am, Duncan Ranch. {You know they say that the quickest way to a mans heart is through his stomach, and that was the truth in this situation. Ace found himself inside a quant four room house sitting down at the breakfast table in a country style kitchen having a hearty breakfast. Sitting at the table with Ace is Old Man Duncan, his wife and three daughters. They were having Crème of Wheat with white toast and jam.} Mrs. Duncan: Mr. Jefferys, would you like some more coffee? Ace: Sure Ma’am. Thank you kindly. Old man Duncan: So son what team are you on….wait don’t tell me Neon right? God I love those guys with there Monday Night Rush! Or is it UPWA? Don’t see them too much since they are doing monthly specials! Ace: No sir I’m with EPW! Three Daughters: (Laughing and giggling together.) Old man Duncan: I know them! I love those Shadow Riders! Ace: No sir that’s LOW. Old man Duncan: Is it that fed with that President Hyde fellow? Ace: No sir, once again you’re thinking of Neon. Were the fed with such talents as Shawn Singleton, Ricky Summers, and Jackson Kable. And we plan to make a impact in the wrestling world. Old man Duncan: Sounds more like that fed that went out of business BDW! Ace: Ughhhh!!! {Remembrance is to this drifter another form of respect. If his team can go out there and do something special where the fans of PTC would remember….then that would be the ultimate prize wouldn’t it? Team Neon filled with some great competitors and the youngest federation in the PTC fold. But then again will it be UPWA as they are still trying to get noticed as a federation to be reckoned with? Only time will tell as I-PPV draws even closer as we speak.} 12:00 pm, on the main street. {With his stomach and hopes refilled he marches forth sticking his thumb out there to get a ride. On the road to what may be his greatest achievement for EPW to survive in PTC.} {The scene fades to black.}
