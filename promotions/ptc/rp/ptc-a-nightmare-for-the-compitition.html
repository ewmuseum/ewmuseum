A nightmare for the compitition

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens with Noah sitting in his leather chair and watching a commercial for the PTC but we slowly see Noahs eyes slowly closing.He is starting to drift off into a deep dream state a place we like to call the Midnight Nightmare Zone] [Man i dunno what i ate but damn i have some killer heartburn.I must be dreamin again its been a while sicne i have dreamt like this but i was feeling froggy so i decided to jump.I was walkin throught the house but things were dim and none of the lights seemed to work it was odd cause i remember paying the electric bill but still nothing worked.....i was a little confused but i heard a knockin on the door and i went to answer it.I answered the door and there was legendary wrestler Blotch Rhoady man i used to love watching this guy but he died a horrible death years ago as he gagged on a whole chicken leg he swallowed and now that i look closely i can still see it] Rhoady[whispering]Noah Hanson?? Noah:What speak up man??? Rhoady[attempting to clear throat]Noah?? Noah:Can you speak up man??I can hardly hear you?? [Rhoady points to the bone in his throat] Noah:Ohhhh yeah!!! Roady:Noah tonight you will be visited by 3 spirits the spirits of hardcore!! Noah:I already love hardcore wrestling man its in my blood my great grandfather was the very first hardcore wrestler Sween 'The Hardcore Machine' Johanson algend in the business......my uncle Sven 'The Bullrope'Hanson....the first swedish hardcore champion.... Roady:Silence welp!!!![gags and you can see the bone going up and down rhoadys' throat]You will be visited by 3 hardcore legends they will guide you to victory my friend so be prepared.....prepared........[Rhoady vanishes] Noah:Welll that was interesting!!! [Noah tosses and turns in his chair as the nightmare continues] [Well Blotch was truely one of my all time favs but he could not be serious about 3 legends visiting me tonight i mean come really that kinda stuff does not happen in reality....] [Noah sees an odd glow coming grom his kitchen desides to check it out.] [When Noah gets there we see a large man bent over inside Noahs' fridge.] Voice:[speaking in a raspy texan voice]Hey kid ya got anything a little bit tougher than pop tarts or gingerale or bottles of water....i mean kid tell me ya didn't pay for bottles of water pardner?? Noah:Tex Harley???Didn't you die when your wife murdered.....[Tex comes out of the fridge sans ax in head]you......25 years......ago[gulp]??? [All of a sudden i felt real quesy like i was gonna faint] Tex:Hey kid???Kid???? [Tex snaps his fingers in front of Noahs' eyes] [Noah comes to] Tex:Damn kid ya can't handle an ax in someones head and ya call yerself hardcore well yer a little wet behind the ears little man!!!Maybe you should go over to the Hardcore Weinyhut jr??? Noah:What the hell of course i can handle it just never saw it stickin out like that!!![looks at the ax oddly and tries to touch it] Tex:Wold on there hombre ya just can't touch it k!!Besides i am here to teach you something that will make your opponents sqeel like little branded pigs. Noah:Thanx Tex but i am hardcore and i know plenty. Tex:Listen to me kid i was hardcore till the day i died k.And this will help put you over the top kid got it??? [I was wondering what he was talking about and was kinda curious too.So i thought what the hell] Noah:Ok Tex what ya got for me?? [Tex takes off the leather backpack he was wearing and unties it.He opens and pulls out a large roll of barbed wire.] [Tex unwraps it and rolls it around one of his arms] Noah:What in the hell are you doing?? Tex:You wrap this around your arm and deliver a Texas Border Crossin.Not only will it strike fear into there very souls but rip the pretty faces inta silly putty!!Now you try!! [Wow was this guy serious.I decided to try it anyway and wrapped it slowly around my arm but i loved the way it felt the pain the rush of adreline it was like i was empowered by the gods and this barbed wire was the channel.I practiced with a few practice lariats then i turned and thanked Tex for the inspiration but he had faded away......] Tex:[fading voice]Get along little doggy get along...... [Noah wakes up or so he thinks] Noah:Wow that seemed so real!! [Noah looks down and sees his arm is covered with small cuts.Noah gets up and slowly walks to the bathroom to take care of the3 wounds] [Noah bends over the sinkand splashes water in his eyes and on his face.] Noah:That is ridiculous that did not happen....[mock laughter] [Noah splashes more water on his face and then looks in the mirror to see someone standing there....it is Takidsahi Matazaball] Taki:[speaking in Japanese] Noah:Wait a minute if i am dreaming can you at least have sub titles???? [Taki nods] Taki:[subtitled]You need to experince more tradtional hardcore brutality daniel-son!! Noah:Daniel-son??? Taki[shrugs]You need to learn to bring very dangerous and potent weapons to ring young grasshopper!!! Noah:Grasshopper?? [Taki again shrugs his shoulders] Noah:Wait a minute didn't you die earlier this year in one of your hardcore macthes?? [Taki nods] Taki:A true hardcore warrior is willing to do to any lengths to win the match!! Taki:You also must be one with your environment using whatever there is at your disposal to get the job done my young apprentice. Noah:So your saying that just because i am in a hardcore match means using the most destructive weapons and using what's around you as weapons also??? [Noah turns but Taki is slowly fading away] Taki:Good luck young one good luck!!!Man they samuri watch over you!!! [Noah again tosses in his chair as his last visitor arrives.] [A dark cloud of smoke appears and Noah turns to it a large hooded figure steps through.His face cannot be seen and his hands are ghostly white.] Noah:What are you my future?? [figure nods] Noah:So what are we doing?? [The figure points to a door] Noah:Are we going through the door?? [Figure nods] [Noah opens the door and there is othing there] Noah:What does this mean?? Figure:{Deep gravely voice]It means your future is undecided young Noah so use it wellllllll!!! [Noah wakes up in a cold sweat and looks around in shock.The tv is still on and the mexican dinner is sitting on the table] Noah:Damn mexican food always does that to me !!! *end feed*
