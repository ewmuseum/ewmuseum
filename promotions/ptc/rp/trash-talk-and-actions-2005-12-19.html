Trash talk and Actions!

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 The rain falls heavily on the streets of Boston Massachusettes as three police cruisers are located outside a building. A large man is seen yelling at the police officers and pointing at the apartment as the police officers are trying to calm the man. Trying not make him any angrier than he already is. The man turns and waves his hands towards the police officers and walks away with a gleam of sadistic danger in his eyes. He walks forward and turns heading down a sidestreet. Quietly he turns and looks into a hole in the wall bar window and notices the man he was looking for. He enters removing his coat as he turns and grabs the man and begins beating the hell out of the man. Three bouncers try in vain to get the big man to release the smaller man. The big man grabs the first bouncer and slams him against the wall. "Swill eating mackerel this waist of gene space stole my family heirloom. I am going to kill the lice ridden fowl!" The secnd and third bouncers back off at the sudden violent rage as the big man grabs the smaller man again who slips through his grasp running out the door. The big man gets angry suddenly and grabs the closest person top him who happens to be a drunken bargoer. The large man throws the smaller bar patron across the bar slamming into the liquor bottles and he turns taking strides towards the door as the two bouncers come in and grab him again. This time the large man grabs the first bouncer by the throat and hoists him up into the air as he grabs the other one by the head. "I am Jorge Samuelsson you puny pathetic excuse for men have disturbed my rite of revenge against someone who has done my family wrong. You shall pay with unbridled pain. This sunken pig hole you call a tavern is pathetic and will be razed to the ground so I can get my just revenge on the pathetic man who stole my family rune." Slamming the bouncer back first into the bar and then lifting his partner with both hands he slams him down on top of the other and stares holes through people as he dares them to get in his way. He makes his way for the door as a man outside seems to be simply pointing as he speaks. "H He went that way into that building over there!" Samuelsson looks at the man and grins. "Thank you your help has established you to be unharmed." Slowly Samuelsson stalks off towards the building as he enters and looks at the stairwell that leads up. He notices that the stairwells is exit less and more to head straight up into an open area. The man stands across the room and holds a two by four and stairs at Samuelsson and challenges him. "Come on you big Nordic reject. I’ll beat your ass and defeat you as easily as I would any man with a two by four!" Samuelsson looks at the man and holds out his hand. "Give me the rune back and you will leave here with a slight limp!" The man screams and charges Samuelsson with the two by four as the man swings Samuelsson steps back as the piece of lumber swings harmlessly through the air as Samuelsson steps forward driving a fist into the temple of the off balance man. The man crumples to the floor as Samuelsson reaches down and grabs the man and begins to beat him severely and the screams of agony and pain fade as the darkness permeate the scene. **A few Hours later** Samuelsson is seen sitting on a beach as he stares at the rune he had recovered. His anger seemed to have subsided a bit. Yet he still obviously had some agitation still resonating within him. He seemed to stare at the rune in his hand as if it was speaking to him. He slowly stood as he spoke. "Many men and many many more stupid people have tried to discount me. I have stayed my course and path and will not betray who I am. Accept who I am and be done with it. I will cnquer all the unbelievers who mess with me. I dealt with a thief and I do believe the the Arabs have the best ways to handle thiefs. Cutting off their hands was the simplest way of dealing with things. The man who stole this from me was due suffering and torture to that which has never been seen. I did what I needed to do." Opening his other hand that had been clenched the whole time he held four teeth in it. He grinned as he stared at the teeth. "Taking a souvenir with me this evening, I am enjoying crushing this man. He will never forget the person from whom he stole from. I searched this eastern coastline of America and found proof of my family being here. Now I am watching something else begin in front of me. A odds in favorite called Fred Grest who wants a piece of me to move on in GTT5. In my book he is not going to go further than me until the fight is over. I am going to hurt this man severely and choke the life from his body. Who does this man of nothing think he is? Does he believe he will walk into an easy defeat of me? I don’t give a damn who he thinks he is. I don’t care if he is this Brittany Spears left teet. I am tired of the non believers assuming he will defeat me. I am going to teach him a lesson in pain." Turning he places the teeth in a pouch and then turns looking at the ocean as a vessel slowly pulls ashore as he grins. "I defeated the mighty Shaman of the WWA and where has he been. He is all but gone from the WWA now. He shows nothing to me. No man such as he should have ever stayed as long as he did in the WWA. He was the backbone of the federation and I broke it. Is this Fred Grest the backbone of GTT5 nope I want to make it to the end as a victor and win this thing. I want to prove to the world that Jorge Samuelsson should be taken very very seriously." Slowly wading out into the water he pulls himself up and over the edge of the vessel and the crew around him look at him and nod. They turn and begin to use the mighty ores of the vessel to pull the vessel off the beach. Soon the vessel is bouncing up and over waves as Samuelsson stands at the back watching ahead like a mighty stone in a small brook. Standing in the rain and wind as the waves crash around the mighty Viking vessel he stands there unmoving emotionless as his thoughts seem to spill out before him as he speaks. "Fred Grest who are you really? We all know the information provided for us, yet there is so much more you are not telling us isn’t there? I wonder what it is that you’re hiding Grest. Is the popularity and grandeur of this type of match up baffling to you? Do you think that this spotlight is too much? It matters very little to me at this point. I want to move onwards and forward throughout GTT5. You wanted this fight Grest and this fight is something that you shall get." Quietly he leans back against the rudder arm as he steers the vessel through the waters as the waves splash up as the giant sail is pulled into position as the wind catches it and spreads it like an eagle taking flight. He stares upwards again as the clouds seem to be breaking as the occasional star shines through brightly. "Even the blind squirrel gets a nut Grest. You may be established and be the over all favorite in our match up, But your not going to win this match freely. I will fight with all I am and thrust forth the effort seldom seen by mortal men. Crushing you won’t be a privilege nor an honor. It is going to be a purest form of pleasure. The underdog is going to rise and take a bite out of those who predict me at being the lesser man. There is a vast ocean of future for me Grest. You’re just another wave I either have to steer straight into or steer to the side and avoid. I would rather be crushed in the wave rather than steer clear of it. I am not avoiding you I am targeting you." Samuelsson stands as the waves begin to subside as the clouds break and the stars shine through. Samuelsson looks up and smiles. "The path is clear Grest and this time it is straight to you. I am going to fight you unlike any man you have ever faced before. It is time for some good old fashion violence. This time Grest you’re not going to get an easy win against an opponent. If you win you’re going to have to earn it. This time I am waiting for you grest. No more will you wait for your opponents. You will come to me." Samuelsson chuckles and begins to laugh heartedly as he opens his hand looking at the teeth there. "Hope this mans dental bill will not be too bad. I am awaiting the results of other findings and soon the real violence will come in many forms of conquering." The boat drifts slowly off into the night as the stars are the only thing that are brightly focused and shining.
