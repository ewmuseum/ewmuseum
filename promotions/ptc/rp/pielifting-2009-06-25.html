Pielifting Me and Jack Kerouac hung out the other night. Sorry, Jack Kerouac and I, which I fucking hate, because who would ever say “I hung out the other night,” you must imply where you are hanging out and why you’re hanging out, so why can’t the me come before Jack Kerouac, I’m sure he’d appreciate it, anywho we were just chilling right and this dude walks up and asks me what time it is on my watch and I tell the fucker look, man, this ain’t fucking Chicago, ok, you see any cubs here, you’re walking into the den, so the dude just peaces leaving me and Mr. Kerouac, who is confused as all fuck, speed will do that to you, he just starts talking about boy scout box cars, not even boxes, box shaped, and he starts telling me that as a kid he used to have his own, except he never raced it, just kept it on the mantle and shined it every day, I tell him no point in owning a car without taking it for a spin, like having a fucking Ferrari and leaving it in your garage, a fucking waste, who knows who’d appreciate it, but apparently it’s not about that, apparently and I’m not sure if I agree but apparently why bother pleasing the detractors, why take that second when you’re only going to leave alone anyways, when you’re only one drink from Utah, you’re only one from taking that blurry-eyed ride and stepping behind those doors while your mom or aunt or girlfriend is sitting in the lobby only letting one drop? But this is Chicago, and does anyone really know what time it is, Big Ben excluded, Ohio excluded, the states included in this ramble, perhaps you’re confused and that’s ok, I am as well, prescription glasses only work for some, don’t be jealous, jealousy and envy are the enemies, a famous rapper would say that while gripping a chain a cobbler spits on, gotta polish the fucking thing, stops wiping the tracks off his teeth and collects them for the fairy, told him to try and fix the toe but that’s impossible, that thing is torn to all fucks, this is Chicago buy another pair you lazy fuck, but this isn’t Chicago, I see the ocean, no I see a man jumping off a bridge and he’s stuck in the mud and regretting and screaming for God and mom but no one can hear a drowning man as he comes loose and floats into the rocks by Lowell High, kids spit balls from the classrooms. But this is Chicago, and does anyone really know what time it is, Jack Kerouac excluded, Lowell excluded, state pride included, it always is, the ashamed are foreigners or of themselves, and now we’re discussing politics, he champions for anarchy so I throw a brick through a bakery’s window and we turn ravenous, nom nom nom nom, and we turn ravenous, one touches but never again, a slit of light steadies onto my naked body but I’m half way home before it settles, I’m sure he’d appreciate it but his liver failed, thank God I can still hear him, I trade the orange for the jeans and T, catch a bus back, fish out the poor fuck face down, sell him back at home, he became edible through practice and hopelessness.
