History is the beginning!

17 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-17 Within the dark forests of Northeastern Untied States is a quiet hollow. Silently a large man dressed in Viking garb stands concentrating on one spot. He stands it what seems to be an ancient village. Near his feet remain the ruins of one of the old ancient buildings. He nudges one of his feet forward kicking the stone over before he turns and walks towards the ancient once mighty hall which lies in ruins. Stepping over rotten and fallen trees he enters the great hall and looks about. His mind seems to travel to ancient times as he appears to be looking for something, anything that will remind him of his past. Becoming annoyed he kicks a large stone. It hits the wall and opens up a small hole. He walks over and opens it up, reaching in he pulls out a small leather sack. As he begins to pull it open the dried and weathered leather tears like paper and the contents spill on the ground. Looking down he begins rummaging through them and lifts a stone with a Nordic rune upon it. Staring at the ruin a smile spreads across his face. "This rune proves that my ancestor, Ragnar Samuelsson was here. It is with pride that I come to this new land and discover that my ancestors helped forge these lands beginnings. Much like my presence in the GTT5, I went on in the first round past a man named James von Doom who was highly favored over me. I am proud of my victory as all men should be. Now I move forward and face a man named Fred Grest, Could be a very interesting battle indeed." Reaching down he picks up the rest of the contents and then turns to follow a path back through the woods. As the path winds through the darkening hollow a loud rumble and roaring sound is heard. Turning the rune in his hand as he walks the roaring sound becomes even louder. Finally, through some trees and brush the denseness of the hollow breaks and a cliff forms. A mighty outcropping overshoots the Atlantic Ocean. He stops at the edge of the cliff, looking out over the vast vista of the ocean. From here all he can see are the powerful waves beating at the rocks. The ocean fills the rest of his eyes, rolling in its endless cycles out to the horizon. "Beautiful isn’t it? My ancestors must have seen this a million times and every time I see a sight like this I am driven to find out more about my heritage and myself. Makes me wonder sometimes if I am confused. I begin to think that I should have lived in a different time and place, perhaps as a Viking warrior. Then I would not have to fight so hard to show I am a worthy. Now, however, there is little for me to prove anymore so I will just enjoy the fight. I always do." With a sadistic twinkle in his eyes he glances at the nearer ocean as a small sailed boat drifts in the distances. Samuelsson raises his hand to his eyes blocking the sun to try and get a better glimpse at the tiny vessel which seems to be sailing up the coastline towards an unknown port. He walks along the edge of the cliffs, following the boat’s progress. He glances at the rune in his hand and notices that the symbol on the sail is the same. He hurries down the cliff to the beach far below as the sail comes into view clearer. Luckily, it appears to be heading towards him and the beach. He makes his way out into the shallow surf as the men in the boat wave and toss him a rope. He grabs it pulling them ashore the best he can. The men hop out and help him beach the sea vessel. Once the craft has been beached Samuelsson turns, smiling to the men. "Ho there brave sailors why do you have the runic mark of my people on your sails?" The men look at the sails before one nods and steps forward. "Dude, it was a college project and some of us chose that as the coolest looking mark. We felt it meant strength and power for a sea vessel of this type. We chose to sail this boat as part of our vacation to see what it was like to live like a Viking." Samuelsson looks and stares at the symbol and then grins. "Then my family has made a far larger mark on history than just ruins of a town in this region. I am glad you men have given me the proof I have needed. The living proof that Vikings lived and worked throughout these lands. I wish you well of your project. Excuse me, but I have more research to do." With a final friendly wave, Samuelsson turns and heads down the beach. With the ancient village so close, perhaps he would find more signs of his heritage in the current town. Once inside the town, Samuelsson slows his steps, looking over the village. He can see similarities this town has with hundreds of others along coastlines throughout Europe and North America. Towns his family had raided and settled. As he looked around, his eyes lit on the local tavern. With a grin he headed in that direction, thinking that perhaps he could find out some local history from the old timers that would be likely to frequent such an establishment. As he stepped through the doors the aroma of beer and stale cigarettes blasted him in his face. He walked to the bar and ordered an ale. With luck, the older patrons of the bar would not recognize him. With his drink in hand, he turned and looked around the room, marking a small knot of older gentlemen, just the types that could provide him with some local lore. With that thought in mind, he made his way to them. "Excuse me gentlemen, I was wondering if you could tell me about your town. I have been traveling, and your town is very interesting to me.", he said with a smile. The group of men looked at Samuelsson before looking at each other. The oldest of the group spoke up, in the taciturn way that marked all New Englanders "What is it you be wanting to know?" Samuelsson nodded as the men settled back in chairs. A few lit pipes as the bartender walked over. He ordered the men a round of drinks, before taking a seat with them. The oldest one cleared his throat, and took a drink before pinning him with his dark grey eyes. "Our town has been around in one form or another for over 900 years. Rumor was, for a long time that Vikings had originally settled the area. I say was, because a few years ago, one of those nosey professors from the college over in Perth came and started digging around in the forest and along the coast line. He actually found what he claims are Viking relics. Why, the Mayor held a big fancy ribbon cutting when the museum opened to house them." Samuelsson did not interrupt as the man spoke, although he knew his next destination. He sipped his beer as the man talked of other local legends, a treasure supposedly lost by the Vikings that consisted of plunder from many nations, an Indian graveyard that was haunted, nothing much different from the other towns, baring the Viking references. The door opened and he glanced over out of habit. He watched a group of younger men walk into tavern warily. This group would be the one that recognized him. And he did not want to deal with that right now. As the older man paused for a drink, Samuelsson excused himself. "I thank you for your information Sir. Perhaps I will go to the museum and look over the relics that were found. It could prove interesting. Please, accept another drink as my gift for your time and stories." He glanced at the bartender, catching his eye and motioning to the drinks. He laid some money on the table as quickly walked out before any of the younger men could notice him. Once outside he paused for a breath, the fresh air coming as a relief after being inside the stuffy room. He walked through the town slowly as he headed to the museum. Hopefully, it would still be open. Reaching the doors, Samuelsson was prepared to be disappointed. He tried the door, only to find them locked. As he stood there, wondering what he should try next the door opened and a small young lady ran right into him. "Oh excuse me", she said as she turned around. She looked up at him, her eyes widening suddenly as she recognized him. "You..You are…Samuelsson!" Samuelsson hid his displeasure. He thought, knowing he could not deny it. Suddenly, he hit upon an idea. "Yes, I am, and I have come to visit your museum. But, I see, it is closed. And sadly, I leave tonight" The young lady gaped a moment, before shaking her head. "Yes, but, I can make an exception. Especially if you are leaving tonight. And you really want to visit." So saying the young lady turned back around and opened the doors. Samuelsson smiled. Sometimes, being well known did work in his favor, but not generally. He followed the young lady into the museum. "I am really just interested in the Viking items. I understand you have some interesting pieces." The lady nodded and led him in that direction. As she flipped on the light, and the different artifacts were highlighted. Samuelsson looked over the artifacts, taking a step closer. Peering at them through the class cases, his eyes lit upon a small wooden carving. Leaning down, looking closer at the carving he noticed that it had the same rune marking as the stone he had found at the old town site. "Where did he find this carving, do you know? Did he leave any notes?" , Samuelsson looked at the lady expectantly. She nodded and escorted him into a small office. "All his notes are here. Would you like copies?" Samuelsson nods with a smile. As the young lady leaves to make his copies, he simply chuckles. Yes, for once his celebrity status has helped. Once the lady returns with the copies, he takes them and then heads out. "Thank you so much for your assistance."
