Hell from a Hotel

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 (Rocky Adams sits on the end of a bed in his hotel room with his dorky younger brother Melvin on an adjacent bed sitting cross-legged and typing on his laptop as usual. The constant tapping of keys seems to be pushing Rocky over the edge…) TAP-TAP… TAP-TAP-TAP…TAP- Rocky: “What the hell are you doing?” TAP-TAP… TAP-TAP-TAP-TAP… Melvin: “I’m just downloading some footage of your opponent for your cruiserweight match Brother Ethan Knight.” (Melvin spins the laptop round to show Rocky the devastating effects of Ethan’s deadly Cyclone Breaker as the camera zooms in on the fallen opponent.) Melvin: “That could be you brother of mine…” (An uncomfortable silence as Rocky gets up and paces around anxiously. Melvin closes up his laptop and stares at his brother, in admiration in some ways, but also in fear of his well being.) Melvin: “I want you to be careful Ron, this Ethan Knight is a dangerous, evil, sick and twisted man.” (Rocky stops suddenly on hearing Melvin acknowledge him by his real name, Ron. He now knows that Melvin is seriously concerned, nobody has ever tried to reason with Rocky this way before.) Rocky: “You think I can’t be dangerous? You think I cant’ be evil!?! How’s this for evil?” (Rocky starts violently trashing the hotel room, he runs to his bedside and picks up the bedside lamp. He tries to throw it like a javelin, but the cord pulls it back mid-swing onto the bed leaving it undamaged.) (Melvin merely stares at Rocky while he does this. Rocky then picks up the free bathroom soaps and shows Melvin them.) Rocky: “You want evil? What if I were to take these home without telling ANYONE!” (He shoves the soaps aggressively into bag and begins looking for some more activities to prove himself. He paces past the television before stopping and walking back to it.) Rocky: “Ethan Knight is dangerous and evil, well how’s this-“ (Rocky kicks the TV over with a mighty crash cracking the screen. Melvin jumps off his bed and races over to his brother.) Melvin: (Shouting) “NO!!! Frasier was coming on!!!” Rocky: (Shouting emotionally) “Oh God what have I become!?!” (Rocky sits down on his bed holding his head in his hands at the carnage he has created. Melvin sits down beside him to comfort his brother.) Melvin: “It’s okay, you didn’t know.” Rocky: (Whimpering) “One thing is for sure though Melvin, Ethan Knight is gonna receive a lot worse once I step into the ring with him.” (Gaining courage) “I’ll show him that my Tornado Package DDT is a helluva lot more devastating than his Cyclone Breaker!” (Melvin looks on with concern at his brother as he sets the bedside lamp back up on the table. And we fade out.)
