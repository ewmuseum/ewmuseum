Raining Blood-Part 2

23 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-23 There was dark, and nothing else. He was floating in a dark void and nothing else; it was making him go crazy. He felt empty in every way possible, there were no feelings inside, and there was nothing outside either, just the void. It was impossible for him to fathom the all encompassing emptiness that his life had become. Now it was over, he just hoped that this emptiness would be over soon, that everything would be over soon. He didn’t want to feel anymore, he didn’t want to feel the pain. Darkness was suddenly substituted by a bright light. He felt shots of pain through his limp body. Wasn’t this supposed to be the end for him? Why was he feeling pain? Is this the last stage before he completely disintegrated out of this world? Had he been wrong and was this the punishment for not believing in Him? He didn’t know, and he didn’t care, all he could concentrate on now was the pain. ---------- Digital Mortality had tried to kill himself, he had done something he thought would help him keep the pain away, to silence the voices in his head. It would have worked, it would have been ended right there if it wasn’t because of fate or destiny or coincidence… whatever you want to call it. If it hadn’t been because of that his skin would right now be feeling the heat of the flames as his lifeless body got cremated. He didn’t want to be buried; he thought burials were a medieval ceremony that should not be done today. So he wanted his body to be cremated and thrown in the ocean. The only place where he could find calm and isolation. ---------- His eyes opened just a bit, but he couldn’t understand what was happening to him. His brain wasn’t functioning normally and he didn’t understand why. He could hear faint sounds; he tried to move but was unable to. Invisible straps held his hands and legs in place. His mind was starting to work properly, slowly but surely. He could only see the bright light, but it was starting to get less and less intense with every second that passed. He felt the touch of an object in his arm and the sting of something else. He could see white, but it wasn’t a bright light, it was the roof of a room. He saw dark silhouettes over him although he was still groggy and couldn’t distinguish them. He was starting to make out some of the things he was hearing. He heard ‘still doped up ’and‘he’s fading again.’ As the voice said that, he lost consciousness once again, his eyes slowly closing and darkness once again taking over. ---------- Burton had arrived at Digital’s house and knocked a couple of times, when nobody answered he used the spare key that had been given to him. He had news for Digital about the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and about his opponent Joshua Taro Freedom. Digital Mortality hadn’t known that he had been given a second chance and had not prepared for his match. When Burton came into the house and looked all over for him, he decided to check his room. Since the door was open he called Digi’s name and when there was no answer he entered the room. He saw the cardboard box on the floor and the letter on the floor too. When he looked over at the bathroom he was shocked. He stood there for five seconds trying to put together what he was seeing. The first thought had been that he had been murdered. He saw the pile of blood tangled with Digi’s red hair and the thought of a suicide never really occurred to him until he saw the blade on the floor. He ran towards Digital and ripped his own clothes in order to put pressure on the wrists. There was blood everywhere, and he soon found himself covered in blood. He tied Digital’s wrists in a hard knot so that no more blood would flow freely. Next he checked for vital signs and seeing that he felt a faint pulse, decided to carry him to the car and drive to the hospital that was 10 minutes away. ---------- He was slowly regaining his consciousness and he had no idea how much time it had been since he last awoke. There was no darkness now, but endless nightmares of death and blood. He suddenly opened his eyes. He tried to move but wasn’t able to. He looked at his wrists and saw the restraints. He had been making such a ruckus and thrashing so wildly during the last few hours that he had been put in restraints. He moved his head to his side and looked at his surroundings. He knew where he was. Hospital San Pablo. How had he gotten there though? He looked at his hands and saw the scars, the still red cuts on his wrists and he came to the realization of what had happened. He didn’t see anyone for a while, until he looked to the corner of the room and saw the sleeping body of Burton. He had been the one who saved him, and he had stayed all the time with him, but did he want to be alive? He had no idea. He wouldn’t say anything that would upset Burton after he had saved his life. He lied back in his bed and suddenly a giddy nurse walked in. ‘Al fin despierto!’ She said as she walked over to the end of the bed to grab the clipboard and write something that we can only guess. ‘How are you feeling Antonio?’ She asked gazing at him questioningly. ‘Um… I feel kind of dizzy,’ he said as she went over to him and took out his restraints. He quickly brought his hands up to his face to look at his wrists. He couldn’t believe he had done it. He had been so close to death, but not even that worked for him. Burton had woken up and had his hands in his eyes. ‘How are you doing man?’ He asked as he stood up and went towards the bed. The nurse had already left, and Burton wasn’t going to tell Digital that what he had done was stupid. It was no time to preach. Digital needed someone there and that was what Burton was going to do. Digital on the other hand knew that what he had done was stupid. ‘I’m sorry Burt. I wasn’t thinking when I did what I did,’ he said looking at his cuts once again. ‘Let’s not talk about that, I came over to your house to tell you that you had a second chance in the GTT4,’ he told Digital. ‘What? I got another chance? Against who?’ ‘Joshua Taro Freedom from Prime,’ he told him as he got out some papers from his backpack and gave it to him. Digital started to read and spent a while reading and scrutinizing every bit of research done by Burton. When he was done he gave it to Burt. ‘Why are you even reading that? You have to stay and get better,’ Burton said putting away all the papers. ‘Plus, from what I saw of this guy on his promotion, he is an arrogant prick. I don’t think you should see the promo in the state you are in,’ Burt said matter-of-factly. ‘You are not my mom Burt, I appreciate and thank you for saving my life, but I will make the final decision regarding my future in the GTT4 and in fact my career and my life. Put his promo on the television,’ Burt sighed deeply, grabbed a video tape from his bag and put it on the VCR. He pressed play and sat down. Digital looked as Freedom arrogantly told him about things that were personal. He thought he was pretty smart with the mind games but his arrogance and overconfidence was going to be his downfall. He would never be what Digital was, he could never get close to what Digital was, but he insisted on getting personal. ‘Who does he think he is?’ Digital looked at his wrists once more and grabbed what he found next to him, a tray of food that had been there and threw it at the television. It didn’t break, but it hit hard. The cable from the VCR disconnected from the TV and there was static. How did this man have the nerve to say that Digital was glad that Justine had died? He hadn’t done his research well; Digital had just tried to commit suicide. The anger seething from Digital’s eyes told everything to Burton. He was going to wrestle in his state, but there was a bigger problem that needed to be taken care of. Digital needed help, and Burton was going to help his friend. Digital leaned back and closed his eyes. ‘I’m going to fight this guy, I have to. It’s a matter of pride. He dared to talk about Justine that way… nobody does that,’ he said in his most honest tone. He has awakened the monster in me, and he will regret it, everyone else will regret it too. I can’t let him walk over me like that.’ Burton was not going to argue with him, maybe if he found a doctor and told him not to release Burt, no, that wouldn’t work, and he would just escape. It was impossible to stop him when he made his mind on issues like this, he made his decision to take his life and now he was making his decision on the match. Digital wasn’t ready mentally or emotionally for a match like what he was going to go through. He would probably breakdown or snap in the middle of the match. He was going to do it though, if Taro would have not mentioned Justine he might have let him pass on through. He would have just stepped aside and worked with his depression problems. The loss of the woman he loved had thrown him to the ground, but to learn that at the same time he had lost his baby… that had infuriated him. Why hadn’t she told him before? Why couldn’t she call him and tell him that he was going to be a father, she knew he would have gone back to her, she knew how much he wanted to have a kid, how much he wanted to be a father. Could God be playing with him? Was God really that different from the Greek gods? Did he create us so he could have something to amuse himself with? Maybe, maybe there was a God who cared, and if there was, why was he neglecting Digital Mortality? Was it some kind of test? Digital Mortality was nothing but a pawn of a greater being, and if this greater being didn’t really exist, like Digital thought, then he had the worst luck a man could have. ‘Burt, I need your help… I don’t think I can survive by myself,’ Digital told Burton with his face as serious as stone. Burton was taken by surprise. He never expected Digital to tell him that so bluntly and straight to the point. ‘What are friends for man?’ Burton said trying to lighten up the mood but failing at it. There was a somber veil covering the room, anyone that entered could feel the deep sadness inside that little hospital “suite”. Digital felt the blood pumping on his veins, the pain still there, but he knew that he had survived for a reason, maybe that reason was victory, or maybe it was just another way to make him fall once again. Another chance for him to fail. Whichever it was, he was going to tackle it head on, no backing away now. It was time for everyone to know what he could do; it was time for him to show Freedom what he could do. Even though he would still be miserable for the rest of the tournament, he would seek professional help, there had to be someone that could help him with his problems, he knew he was not the first one to lose his loved ones and certainly not the last one either. He had a new mission in life; he had tasted life and tasted death… to him, life tasted better… …at least for a while.
