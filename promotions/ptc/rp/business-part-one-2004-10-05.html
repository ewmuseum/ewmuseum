Business Part One

5 October 2004

Date: 2004-10-05 When he was young he had a fear of the dark. Monsters under the bed, Monsters in the closet, Monsters lurking just out of his range of sight. And every one of these phantom monstrosities was waiting for him to close his eyes. Because that’s when they strike you see. When your guard is finally down they pounce from the shadows and dismember you, eat you alive. And while he always woke up the next morning unscathed it didn’t stop him from dying a thousand times each night, because in his mind the creatures tortured him, committed heinous acts worse then any waking punishment a small child could physically endure. All through the power of his imagination. His mother when not under the influence of the bottle invented silly little songs and games to play to keep the monsters at bay, they always seemed to work too...as long as she was in the room with him, her soft voice and reassuring words were a magic barrier against the creatures of the night, but when she left the shadows would creep out flexing phantom fingers and limbs, shadows that his pitifully small night light couldn’t banish. He spent his waking hours of night praying for daylight. Because daylight made everything real. Shadows didn’t exist in the sunlight, not in the mind of a small child. But life has a strange but persistent way of proving us wrong. And over the course of several years life took the mind of this small child and showed him just how naive he was, showed him that shadows do indeed exist in the daylight, shadows that exist against the sun are the strongest, and standing in them is to feel the absence of the life giving light, and the cold chill that is the life of the shadow itself. Life also taught the small child not even his mother could protect him from the real monsters, because the real monsters didn’t hide in the shadows, the real creatures that could hurt you looked like any man or woman and they could approach you and gaze right into your eyes, they could touch you and it could feel like the touch of any other human being, they could talk to you and sound as real and comforting as any human being. But the monsters existed, they walked the daylight hours looking for the young and unknowing, and the only way to tell these monsters apart from the human beings was to look into their eyes, the young child learned quickly to make brief eye contact and judge a person in a few seconds, most had the warm glow of sunlight contained in their orbs, but there were some...some who lacked that life giving light and gazed at him with the cold chill of the shadows. Warm smiles on their faces, but dead eyes expressing nothing so comforting...their intentions laid bare. The young child learned to avoid these people with the dead eyes, enclosed in the safety of his home, his mother, and his sister his own safety net against the evils of the world, until life abruptly changed once again. One night the young child’s mother crawled into the bottle and never crawled back out. From there things were never the same, life became a downward spiral, much like trying to crawl up a glass wall, nothing solid to grab on to...nothing to help you climb out. So he lashed out at the glass wall, cut himself and bleed, learned many painful lessons. Fear, doubt, and insecurity became his friends. Young Alex Grey was never quite the same after that, the path of the straight and narrow just didn’t seem to work any more, so he wandered off...and up until recently he never looked back. Up until recently... -- Tonight the young child sits a full grown man. Fear, doubt, insecurity are still his constant companions, he’s gone to bed with these three more times then he’s slept with a woman for money, and being well known for his evenings of debauchery and rough play this is indeed a hard thing to surpass. Where he is now he never imagined he’d be, and if he ponders long enough he’d realize he’s not happy with where he is today, the goals he’s accomplished while applaudable have been achieved by underhanded tactics, his sizable bank account was acquired not by hard work but by greed. In retrospect this is not the way he wanted to break into the wrestling business, try as he might he can’t quite place where it all went wrong, though he wagers it was largely the fault of one Martin Smalls. (You run with the devil long enough and he starts to get comfortable with you...soon he’s bunked down with you like an unwelcome house guest and wont leave regardless of what excuses you make) When Martin brought him the contract for GTT4 he’d thought it would be a new beginning for him, a chance to get his hands on Lestatt Knight and end a long running feud, but Knight had been defeated in the first round leaving Alex with nothing but angry promises and threats...and no hated enemy to take them out on. Annihilation had talked a good game, and put up a decent fight but in the end Alex had overcome him, it felt good, got him motivated for the second round... Banner hadn’t shown, whatever his reasons were they were his own, and maybe it was the best choice he could have made, Alex merely coasted through the tourney while others did the hard work, resting he now stood poised on round three facing off against Doctor Curiosity...a man he’d never heard of. Names...Names...a whole long list of damn names that he’d never heard of before, he was out of his element and alone. Rising to his feet Alex decides he needs a breath of fresh air, leaving quietly so as not to wake the sleeping woman in his bed he slips out the door, he can’t remember her name and he’s sure she’ll be gone by the time he comes back...there’s never anyone waiting for him when he comes home. The sun has yet to rise as he walks the empty sidewalks, his breath is a fog escaping from his mouth as he moves slowly, aimlessly without direction, his thoughts moving much in the same way he seems to be condemned to some sort of limbo to live merely In the moment. If only something would snap him out of this god forsaken frame of mind, he’ll never make progress let alone win a tournament match this way. “Alex...” And where would that get him? If he lost the tourney? Martin would most likely cancel his contract...when you couldn’t get any milk out of the cow...well that was usually when the cow stopped grazing in the green pastures and wound up on the plate... “Alex...” Torn from his thoughts he looks around, no one shares the sidewalk with him this early in the morning “Over here Alex...” His attention is drawn to a darkened alleyway “Good...now that I have your attention...” He’s not impressed, he’s had his share of voices whose faces he can’t see, taking a step towards the alley he resolves to bring this face out into the light and ring its owners neck, however as he steps forward he hears the familiar metallic click of a handgun... “I’d prefer it if you kept your distance Alex...most business meetings are conducted at a respectable distance” “Who are you?” he growls There is a moment of silence as the mystery speaker shifts in the alleyway, whether adjusting his aim or relaxing his grip on the trigger Alex isn’t sure. “I’m your best friend Alex” “Name!” Alex says taking another step forward flexing his hands “I want your name!” “Call me friend...” Metal flickers against the dim streetlight as the barrel of an apparently large caliber pistol protrudes from the alleyway “I think that would be the most...healthy choice for the both of us...understand?” He does understand but he doesn’t much care for it, he spits in the direction of the voice and folds his arms, call it his defiant streak rising to the surface. “Alright “Friend” let’s keep the X-Files cloak and dagger bullshit to a minimum...what do you want?” A soft chuckle “Not without your sense of humor I see...but you’re right I despise wasting time when the point can be reached quickly” “Then let’s reach it...friend” he growls again stressing the last word with the deepest of sarcasm “Martin Smalls” the voice says after a moment This time it’s Alex who chuckles “And the point is?” “Martin Smalls” the voice repeats “I can help you get rid of him” “And why...” Alex said slowly “Would I Want to punch my meal ticket?” “Because I can offer you better” Stranger and stranger, Alex had heard the exact same thing from Martin many times. “What if I don’t want better...Friend...what if I tell Martin he’s got competition? Another head hunter trying to bring him down? You’d be up shit creek without a paddle...wouldn’t you?” “You wont” “And what makes you so damn sure” Alex asked “Because one you don’t know who I am, and two you don’t care about protecting Martin...if the light I saw in your eyes when I mentioned getting rid of him is any clue to what you’re thinking then I’d hazard a guess that you’re all ready to sign up” Alex was beginning to have his fill of people who thought they knew him so well, but maybe in this case it wasn’t very far from the truth, if there was a safe way to take Martin out of the picture then just what the hell did he have to lose? Martins whispered words still lingered in his head (You should have gotten me first) Even if it had been a dream it had been the best advice he’d ever received “I think...” The voice continued “That you’re on the edge...and you just need a little push...allow me to push you Alex...hear the truth and let it set you free” From the alleyway Martins voice emanated, obviously a tape-recording “That man is dangerous but he’s not going to harm me...I’m his meal ticket and he knows it, he harms me and his cash flow stops, and he needs his cash...to feed all his dirty little vices that he thinks no one knows about, I’m his dealer you could say...and he’s my dirty little junkie whore” The voice could be no one but Martins and the words strike home in a very personal way. “Do you hear how he thinks of you Alex? A man of your talent deserves more respect...and more power” Alex though unaware has clenched his hands into fists, his nails digging into his palms “What are you offering...” “Once Martin is out of the way I’m offering you half his corporation no strings attached...you can hire someone to run the business for you and sit back and rake in the cash or you can take a more personal position and mold it the way you see fit...either way I’m offering you half the pie” Alex smiled “And I suppose the other half of the pie goes to you then?” “I”m a generous man” the voice said without skipping a beat “But I don’t do this kind of work for Charity Alex” “Point taken...” Alex said “But why should I trust you? You wont even show me your face?” “I will in time” the voice replied “But for right now it would be safer for both of us to remain shall we say anonymous?” “Like to ships passing in the night then” Alex said “So when does this happen?” “Does this mean I have your undivided interest and attention?” Alex shrugged, the thought of disposing of Martin was appealing, he didn’t trust the little runt of a man and he was more then worried that Martin had a contingency plan for removing Alex from the picture if he became too much to handle, sure Alex had always fantasized about simply shooting his employer and washing his hands of the situation...but Martins death would create a stir and Alex would be one of the places the fingers would point first...no this was better...he’d go into this as a partner and reap the rewards that a partner deserved. “You want to kill Martin? Fine by me...you just tell me when and where and I’ll show up with bells on” A soft chuckle came from the alleyway “I knew you’d make the right choice Alex” a small black box slid out of the alleyway and bumped against his foot “Consider this your tools to get the job done...I’ll contact you when I’m ready to move...until then you just wait for me” Bending down Alex picked up the box and opened it, inside it contained a small handgun with a silencer. Rising to his feet Alex felt a chill run down his spine, maybe it was the fear that someone might have heard this little discussion, or maybe it was simply the subtle winds of change that had brought on the shiver. Alex hadn’t seen the face of his new partner but he had the distinct feeling that if he looked into the mans eyes he would see the cold dead orbs of a monster, intentions laid bare with a smile that radiated greed and pleasure at the thought of human suffering. Alex slid the box into his pocket and turned away from the alley, there was really nothing more to say, The monsters of the world that had so terrified him before didn’t seem to faze him anymore...the cold dead eyes held no sway anymore. Not since the day Alex had gazed into the mirror and seen those same eyes gazing back at him. Sometimes the only way to kill a monster was to become one.
