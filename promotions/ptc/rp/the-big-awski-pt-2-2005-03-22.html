The Big AWSki, Pt. 2

22 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-22 Quote of the Day (sign in a field in England) “DO NOT THROW ROCKS AT THIS SIGN” [We open in the living room of AWS Man (also known as Bill). For those who are visiting it for the first time, the room is fairly average for a living room, aside from the hideously neon-striped walls and various paintings hung on said walls, most depicting some type of harm coming to a living emblem of the state of South Carolina. My favorite is the one where North Carolina, Tennessee, and Georgia are giving it a swirly. The furniture of the room consists of a couch in the middle, a recliner or easy chair to either side, all facing a TV and an even more massive porn collection than at the Insane Pres’ office. AWS Man (also known as Bill) is currently sitting dejectedly on the couch, with Pen beside him; the other occupants of the room are the Nude, who has apparently recovered from the ant attack, although he is sitting gingerly in the easy chair; and Harold Hash, AWS Man (also known as Bill)’s friend since his childhood, who was also a wrestler in WMW, where he and the Insane Pres waged a bloody war which involved attempted murder on Hash’s part. That kook. They’ve since made up, and are again the best of friends … whose relationship is based on porn-watching and not much else. Speaking of which, there’s an all threesomes video playing right now, though only the Nude seems to be watching it with much interest. Hash is sharing AWS Man (also known as Bill)’s outrage at the actions of the misinformed thugs.] Hash : That was valued porn. [AWS Man (also known as Bill) sullenly nods, while Hash continues to look for the right words to express himself.] Hash : It was … um … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : It really tied my freakin’ office together. By which I mean it made it bearable to go there. Hash : It was … it was valued! [The scene that’s been playing ends, and the Nude turns to the others.] Nude : What was valued, Hash? Hash : Nude, did you listen to AWS Man (also known as Bill)’s story? Nude : Um … no, I was watching the tape. It was a pretty good scene, weren’t you- Hash : Then you’re out of your element. Nude : My what? Hash : You are out of your element! Now, AWS Man, what I’m saying is this … uh, that was valued porn, and … and… AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, what’s your point? Hash : My what? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : I know it was valued freakin’ porn, so what’s your point?! Hash : What’s MY point?! … Oh yeah. My point is that you’re the President of Wrestling Midwest, you don’t have to take this porn-peeing lying down- Nude : Somebody peed on your porn? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, some freakin’ Chinese poopiepants. Nude : The porn we were just watching? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : No, in my office. Nude : …You have an office? Hash : NUDE, YOU’RE OUT OF YOUR ELEMENT! And AWS Man, the Chinese poopiepants is not who I’m talking about here. This is about showing you’re not one to be trifled with, you’re an important man- also, Chinese poopiepants is not the preferred nomenclature. Call him a chink, please. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : That doesn’t really sound any better… And besides, he is the freakin’ one who peed on my porn. Nude : The poopiepants peed on the porn, Hash. Hash : But he’s not the issue! Who’d you say they were looking for? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh, well they just called me Rutherford; they thought I was this guy because my freakin’ nickname is the Insane Pres. I had Blade do some research on the Intraweb, and it looks like freakin’ they were looking for some other guy that owns a corporation based out of Cleveland, Wayne Rutherford. The corporation deals mainly in electronics, or some boring freakin’ thing like that. And apparently he’s earned the name the freakin’ Insane Pres for his risky business practices and overall strange behavior. Hash : Well, that’s the guy you need to find. You don’t even know who those thugs are, but they were looking for this guy. It’s HIS fault he owed money, and it’s HIS fault they were looking for him and got the wrong guy. Why should his company owe money, and they go out and pee on your porn? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well … um … karma? Nude : Hey, his nickname’s the Insane Pres? That’s your nickname, AWS Man. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well, you know, I was just going to, like, buy some freakin’ more porn instead… Hash : Fuck that. You paid for it, and it’s ruined because this guy owes money all over the place. Go get his porn. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, I guess I… Yeah, I can freakin’ do that! They peed on my freakin’ valued porn. Hash : They PEED on your valued porn… [We cut to the interior of a richly furnished study, lined with plaques and other award-commending memorabilia. The doors swing open and AWS Man (also known as Bill) enters along with a young man, dressed in a suit and tie. The man, Brett, is obviously winding up a tour of the house, one which the Insane Pres clearly had no interest in.] Brett : And finally, here we are at the study. Mr. Rutherford should be in momentarily. Until then, feel free to peruse Mr. Rutherford’s long list of achievements. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Na, that’s really freakin’ OK. Brett : Please, please, don’t be shy! [He takes AWS Man (also known as Bill) by the shoulders and guides him down the wall, pointing out the more noteworthy awards.] Brett : There’s the key to Van Wert, Ohio, given out for a, er, nominal fee… AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh, that must … come in freakin’ handy. Brett : And there’s the Greater Cleveland Area Business Achievers Award, given out – well, not necessarily to those who have achieved a great deal in business, but also those who exemplify a certain, er … perseverance in the face of adversity, yes… AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hey, is that him with freakin’ Fergie?! Brett : Yes, yes, that is Mr. Rutherford with Sarah Ferguson – taken after she was the Duchess of Ferguson, not before. And believe me, photos with that noteworthy a celebrity aren’t easy to come by. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : So what’s with the freakin’ shades? He trying to steal my look? Brett : You, er, don’t wear sunglasses. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : And now I guess I never can, since your freakin’ boss has ruined it for the rest of us! Brett : Mr. Rutherford is, ah, vision-impaired. And yet he’s achieved so much. [The Insane Pres is obviously growing weary of hearing about the other Insane Pres’ accomplishments.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well, I don’t know if I freakin’ mentioned this, but I’m an IWO Grand Slam champion, IWO 2001 Wrestler of the Year and Best Heel, IWO Hall of Fame, freakin’ first Global Title Tournament semi-finalist, 2003 WMW Wrestler of the Year and Best Debut, two-time Great Lakes- Brett : Yes, I believe you mentioned this several times during the tour, and I’m sure you have a lot to be proud of … even though you did it all with the uses of your eyes. So … you know, not really the same thing. [The study’s double doors swing open again, and Mr. Rutherford enters, with the aid of an electronic seeing eye dog – in other words, basically one of those robot vacuum cleaners, programmed to lead blind people around. I don’t know if those really exist, but they should. Brett exits the room as the robot leads Mr. Rutherford over behind his desk, where he takes a seat.] Rutherford : OK, you’re an Insane Pres, I’m a flippin’ Insane Pres, that’s terrific. But I am very busy, sir, as you must be – DON’T FORGET YOUR RAINCOAT! – so let’s get right to the flippin’ point: what can I do for you? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Uhhhh … well, here’s the freakin’ thing: I was just sitting in my office the other day – which is, by the way, bigger than freakin’ yours - (OOC: It’s not, but Rutherford’s blind, so it’s not like he’d know.) – and all of a sudden these two guys burst in and- Rutherford : Yes, yes, you told Brett on the phone, he told me. What’s it got to flippin’ do with me? (Ha ha ha, you’ll never catch me alive, said he.) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : …You freakin’ OK? Rutherford : Never better, except for five seconds ago. Thirty-one flippin’ seconds ago was pretty good too. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Heh heh, yeah, it was for freakin’ me too. OK, so um, these guys were looking for freakin’ you, which is why Wang Chung peed on my porn- Rutherford : Hello! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hi! Rutherford : …Hello! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, I just freakin’ said hi. Rutherford : …Hello! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : (Annoyed) Hey, how’s it going, greetings, salu-freakin’-tations. Can we get back to my porn crisis? Rutherford : Did I urinate on your flippin’ pornography? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : No, not that I freakin’- Rutherford: BOOOOO! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : …Yeah, not that I know. Rutherford : So every time a rug is mitigated upon from here to Pluto – which I’ve flippin’ been to by the way, quite nice – I’m expected to compensate? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : But they were freakin’ looking for you; it was your company that owed the money. Rutherford : I cannot help what my company does behind my flippin’ back! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : But, aren’t you freakin’ President? Rutherford : …Touché. I’m still not going to take responsibility for your problems. I didn’t blame anybody when some flippin’ poopiepants took my eyes during the Great War. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : The Great War? Man … they’re freakin’ right to call you the Insane Pres, you crazy coot. Rutherford : And they’re right to call you a flippin’ bum and a slacker! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Who calls me freakin’ that? … It wasn’t Brett, was it? That guy seemed alright. Rutherford : I cannot help you solve your problems, Mr. Man, only you can. Also, always flippin’ wear clean underwear, even in the bath. [AWS Man (also known as Bill) sighs and stands up.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Freak it. Rutherford : Sure, flip it! That’s your answer to everything! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : I said freak it, you freakin’ crazy! Rutherford : I don’t freakin’ care what you freakin’- wait, which one do I say, freakin’ or flippin’? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Freakin’ flippin’. Rutherford : Oh, OK … Wait, what? [The Insane Pres – the original one, that is – ignores Rutherford and exits the office, while the latter Insane Pres is still trying to figure out which F-bomb he likes to drop. Brett intercepts AWS Man (also known as Bill) as he walks down the hall.] Brett : How was your meeting, Mr. (also known as Bill)? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : OK. Old freaker told me to take any porn in the house. Brett : Oh … Well, I’m not sure what he was referring to, we don’t have any pornography that I’m aware of. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh, really? Freak … well, just give me some of those plaques then. Brett : The plaques? Heh heh, but those are, ah, made out to Mr. Rutherford; it wouldn’t even make sense to give them to you- AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, and he’s freakin’ crazy, right? OK, then, that’s what he said, so give ‘em here. (Points to a vase holding flowers on a nearby table) That would look nice in my office too, he also said I could freakin’ have that. And he definitely wanted me to have at least one coffee table. [We cut once again to AWS Man (also known as Bill)’s living room, where the Insane Pres is the only occupant. He’s got his feet propped up on his brand new coffee table, and is enjoying some of his new “porn.”] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : I don’t know, Pen, this stuff just isn’t freakin’ doing it for me. Although Fergie is kinda freakin’ hot. Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Her, fat? I’m pretty sure I would’ve freakin’ heard something about that. Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh, right, my PTC match. What’s the freakin’ deal with that, again? Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hmmm, sounds kinda interesting … except not really. I don’t get it, everybody freakin’ knows how great I am. Why don’t they just give me another Global shot? I’ve had two already, one more couldn’t hurt. Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well, I think it’s discrimination, against … um, people who wear freakin’ masks all the time, for no apparent reason. First we couldn’t be models, then bus drivers because we freakin’ scare the children, and now I’m being kept out of getting my rightfully undeserved title shots. It’s a freakin’ travesty, I tells ya. Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well, I’ll prove to everybody that while I may have lost twice going for the Global title, I do deserve another shot. Both those first tries were getting my freakin’ feet wet, so to speak … testing the waters. I know that if given another opportunity, I’ll take the belt home to freakin’ WMW. It’d be too valuable to waste. And I could definitely beat the current champion. Is it still Charles Blizzard? Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh, right, I lost to him my first freakin’ shot. Well, what about the guy who beat me last time, Saki? Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Man, that thing changes freakin’ hands faster than Barry Bonds in a pie-eating contest. Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : It doesn’t have to make freakin’ sense, it’s topical. He’s been in the news lately, therefore I’m clever. Anyway, it doesn’t matter who the Global champ is. After I win Seven of the Best, he’s freakin’ going down like a toilet seat. And obviously I’m going to win; I’m not just in the seven best, I’m the best. I have to be the most accomplished entrant in this thing; I even have awards that don’t have anything to do with me, like- [Holds up mirror with “TIME Man of the Year” at the top] – Timmy Man of the freakin’ Year. I rule, everyone else is teh suck. Especially the guy I’m fighting in the first round, LeStanley Pawn. I don’t care how many different movie parodies does, even ones that haven’t freakin’ come out yet, the guy’s a nobody compared to me. He needs to go see a shrink about the dreams he’s been having before getting in the ring with me, because obviously he’s had no time to freakin’ prepare for a legend like myself, and he’s gonna get freakin’ creamed. [The phone rings, and the Insane Pres lets it continue to do so. After a couple rings, Brian Blade walks into the room.] Blade : Aren’t you going to get that? [AWS Man (also known as Bill) glances around the room.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Why, did it freakin’ look like I was going to? Cause I didn’t mean to mislead you. [Blade sighs and answers the phone. He answers after a moment.] Blade : Oh, he’s right here. Hold on a minute, I’ll- [Blade notices AWS Man (also known as Bill) shaking his head vehemently and pointing to the plaques.] Blade : Oh … I’m sorry, he’s actually a little preoccupied at the moment with important, um, office work. What did you need to speak to him about? … Uh huh … Uh huh … Gotcha. OK, I’ll let him know. [Blade hangs up the phone. The Insane Pres continues to flip through the various plaques and other awards he stole.] Blade : Sooo, aren’t you going to ask who that was? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : What? Man, you really have to stop freakin’ jumping to conclusions. I know it didn’t even look like that was going to happen. Blade : Fine, but I’m telling you anyway. It was Brett somebody-or-other. He said not to worry about the stuff that you blatantly stole the other day. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh, good. Cause I freakin’ wasn’t. Blade : Oh, and also that Wayne Rutherford was apparently kidnapped the other day, and Brett needs you to come by the mansion as soon as possible. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hmmm. O-freakin’-K. I’ll run by there after I go to the post office tomorrow … Wait, no, I mean: Wayne Rutherford’s been kidnapped?! DUN DUN freakin’ DUNNNN! [The view fades out on the Insane Pres, doing his best to look stricken, before immediately returning to perusing his stolen items.]
