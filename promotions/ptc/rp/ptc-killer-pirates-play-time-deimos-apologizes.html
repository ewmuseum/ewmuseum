Fatal Four Way Tag Match Roleplaying

13 January 2005

13 January 2005 Play Time! (Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean #1) "Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean" Show Intro [We begin with a typical TV show intro for this one. There's a black bearded Pirate Captain on screen with an eye patch on his left eye.] Captain: Are you ready kids? Kids: Aye aye Captain! Captain: I can't hear you. Kids: Aye aye Captain! ["Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean" them music starts playing.] Captain: OHHHHHHH...Who lives on ship floating on top of the sea? Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Captain: Big and fat and hungry is he! Kids: Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: If no-brained wrestling nonsense be something you wish! Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Captain: Then sleep on the deck and smell like a fish! Kids: Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: Ready? Captain and Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: Cap-Tain Mor-GAAAAANNNNNN!!! Captain: HA HA HA HA !!!! [We end the song with a flute playing for a moment before the "Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean" logo appears on the screen.] [Cut.] * * * [Deimos Gives them Orders] [The scene opens outside in a playground in Las Vegas, Nevada. The wind is blowing and a few clouds slowly stroll overhead like travelers in a distant land. The camera pans downward from the skeleton of a dead tree to the swing set where the "Killer Klown from Outer Space" is laughing and swinging like a little kid. Meanwhile, the camera pans over to show Deimos, LOW and resident psychopathic cult leader, talking with Captain Jack Morgan about the task at hand.] Deimos: [coldly] "You two idiots had better not screw up my plans for PTC domination. I mean, think about it. You guys can beat three of the best teams in the organization in one felt swoop." [Jack raises his hand, almost like a kid answering a question in elementary school. Deimos rolls his eyes.] Deimos: "What is it?" [Jack puts his hand down slowly.] Jack: "Ummmm, mate. I hate to bring it to your attention but there are [holds up four fingers] four teams in the challenge match, love." [Deimos glares at him menacingly and shakes his head.] Deimos: "Jack, you retard. There are three teams plus you and [pointing toward the KKFOS] the clown. There's the Masked Monkeys, the Flying Vergamovs, and A Prophetic Theory. THREE teams, plus you guys." [Jack scratches his head.] Jack: "Oh, right mate. Good point." Deimos: "You two had better make me proud, otherwise they'll be a repeat of last week and I don't think you guys want that now, do you Jack?" [Jack shakes his head.] Jack: "No, mate. We'd be much obliged not to go through that again." [Flashback time. The scene turns staticy as Jack looks up, trying to remember what happened on LOW's Infinite II program. Well, let's help him out, shall we? Roll the footage from Deimos' chaotic little segment to show you, the fans, exactly what they are talking about.] Deimos: "No. Now, it's Deimos. That means "dread" and "panic" in Greek, for those of you here in LOW who don't know. I mean, laughs let's face it. Most of the LOW's fanbase is made up of our world's Mongrel race, so it doesn't surprise me that none of you knew what that meant. I mean, I'm the only person here who actually has teeth and a functioning penis, so I'm not surprised, actually." Deimos, as he wants to be called now, gets a ton of heel heat after that last comment, especially from the men in the audience. He suddenly goes from having a smirk on his face to a look of complete disdain and evilness. Deimos: "I have come here to find a certain person that wrestles here in LOW. A certain person that I once knew in my youth. A person who got all the glory. A person by the name of..." He stops himself and starts shaking his head, almost as if he can't say the name because of all the pain and torment that it has caused him. He stops, gets himself composed, and continues speaking. Deimos: "Well, they know who they are. They know that I know who they are. I know you. You know you. And I know you know that I know you. So that means trouble for pointing toward the camera YOU!" "Killer Klowns from Outer Space" by the Dickies begins playing over the loud speakers and the Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean come out to no reaction at all. One guy is a 6'10, 380 pound guy dressed like a clown and the other is a guy dressed like Captain Jack Sparrow. The clown is waving to the crowd and the pirate guy is looking around like he's drunk as he walks down to the ring. Deimos: "What the hell?" The KPFOC tag team climbs into the ring and the clown starts waving at the crowd. Deimos gives them a look. Deimos: "I thought I told you two idiots to stay in the back with all the other crap that is waiting for me to flush them all down the toilet." Jack grabs the mic from him. Jack: "Garrr...mate, you need to calm down a bit. We just came out here to give you some comic relief." Deimos looks at him angrily before grabbing him and DDT'ing him on the mat. This doesn't appeal to some of the fans and draws some boos. JD: "Now what was that for?" CS: laughs "I love this Deimos guy already! That was great!" The "Killer Klown" goes over to check on Jack. He bends over to see if he's alright. Just as he does, Deimos kicks him low very hard. The clown falls in a heap on the mat and into the fetal position. Deimos grabs the mic off the mat and gives his final thoughts. Deimos: "The only good comedy is Dante you fools." Whatever that means, Deimos throws the mic down and rolls out of the ring. He ignores the fans as he heads to the back. [The flashback ends after that and Jack is shown acting as if a lightbulb went off in his head.] Jack: [having a revelation] “Oooo…sorry ‘bout that, mate. Had a little too much rum.” [Jack has that weird look on his face like he’s drunk and crazy, but, then again, that’s how he usually looks. Deimos seems unimpressed by his futile attempt to make up.] Deimos: “Jack, you guys have a tag team challenge match and we’re standing here [motioning around] in a park so the clown can play on the jungle gym. Now, is that good strategy?” [Jack scratches his head.] Deimos: “No, it isn’t. You guys had best get your act together otherwise this’ll be the biggest disaster since Ben and J-Lo doing movies together. [acting like Vince Lombardi] WE NEED A PLAN YOU RETARDS! This isn’t second grade Physical Education class. This is wrestling. This is the PTC, the biggest federation around, and we’re standing around here, in a park, looking like a bunch of nobodies. And do you know why that is?” [Jack scratches his head again.] Jack: “Because nobody knows who we are?” [Deimos snaps his fingers.] Deimos: “Exactly. We need to show all of these people who don’t know who the hell we are just who the hell we are.” [Jack looks confused.] Deimos: “And you idiots had better win. We need to start Deimos’ plan for PTC domination right now or the message will be forever lost amongst the Chris Storm ego trips and Seymour Almasy leg humpers that have infested the PTC as of late, much like our opponents infest my dreams like the cockroaches that they are. We need to exterminate them permanently and make them suffer the same fate as the planet Alderaan in the original Star Wars. They will be destroyed. Their screams will permeate the air like a sweet morning dew as we stand over their bloodied carcasses, raising our hands in victory.” [Deimos strikes a victorious pose. Just then, the “Killer Klown” comes running over and taps him on the shoulder. Deimos gives him a dirty look as the clown starts pointing at something. Deimos looks over and sees an ice cream truck driving by. The clown taps him on the shoulder.] Deimos: [coldly] “What is it?” KKFOS: [excitedly] “Ashti mabu dama lama lingo!” Jack: [happily, actually figuring something out for once] “He wants ice cream!” [Deimos looks angry.] Deimos: [angrily] “I know.” [Deimos strolls over toward the ice cream truck to stop it. The camera follows him only to turn back to the clown and Jack. Jack taps the clown on the shoulder.] KKFOS: “Fubar?” Jack: “The boss seems very odd here lately. Do you think it’s something we did, mate?” [The clown shakes his head.] Jack: “Thought so. Well, we best be gettin’ back to me ship, mates. We have buried treasure to find!” [Jack walks off-screen, leaving the clown clapping his hands and getting all excited.] KKFOS: “Treasuirie.” [The clown claps his hands and runs off after Jack as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] ---- 19 January 2005 Deimos Apologizes [We open with nothing but darkness. The whole screen is black. Imagine you are in bed, asleep but just about to wake up. Suddenly, you hear a voice from within the darkness.] OSV: "I am the one who makes you tremble before you go to bed." [The voice pauses for a moment as you begin to wake.] OSV: "I am the shadow that hovers over you while you are in a world of dreams. [The screen begins to show a little bit of light. You're a little more awake now.] OSV: "I am the night terror that makes you wake up in a cold sweat." [The screen is a lighter brighter now. You're almost awake. You can see a dark blue faded background with a dark shape in the middle of the screen. The voice continues.] OSV: "I am the one whom you will all come to fear and fear me you shall!" [The screen is finally into a normal, standard color and we can see that the voice is that of Deimos, the "Master" of the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean. For some reason, there is a mural of a beautiful countryside behind him with cute little rabbits and deer painted on it. He has a cold, calculating yet demented look on his face. He is wearing a black leather jacket, a Pink Floyd "Another Brick in the Wall" T-shirt, jeans, and black Vans shoes. He brushes his hair back as he continues speaking.] Deimos: "For I am Deimos and you are all nothing but primordial ooze in my eyes. [slight smile] The very fabric that led to our being. But, I must say, there is something I feel the need to say to you all considering the vapid nature of my cohorts first showcasing on a Main Event level." [Deimos looks down and wipes his nose with wrist for some reason before brushing his hair back again.] Deimos: [solemnly] "I apologize for the very stupid nature of the first program put out by my followers, the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean. You see, they are not what you call "right minded people." They are, in fact, psychotic miscreants just like us all." [A sick smile comes across his face.] Deimos: "I mean, come on. Let's face it. Only psychopaths and sexually depraved miscreants watch professional wrestling anyway. Did you know that most of our audience is inbred, illiterate, welfare receiving white trash trailer folk?" [Deimos has a look of almost disbelief on his face. His acting is impeccable. Yet, he seems somewhat disturbed by the words that he has just uttered, even though he believes them to be true.] Deimos: [motioning] "I know, I know. The truth is hard to hear." [He shakes his head, almost as if he feels sorry for the fans.] Deimos: "But life goes on and there are many truths to find in this waking nightmare that we call life one truth is this. [long, dramatic pause] The Killer Pirates of the Caribbean WILL win the four-way tag match and they WILL conquer the PTC World Tag Team Champions in due time to help begin the Deimos Era here in the PTC." [Deimos has a sick smile on his face.] Deimos: "Seymour Almasy's time has come and passed. It's [points to himself] MY time now. What about me? What about Deimos? Where's my love from the fans? Where's my name scrawled over banners in the crowds? Where's my fan club? [intensely] WHERE'S IT ALL AT? Huh? Where's my love?" [Just then, Deimos feels a tapping on his shoulder. He turns around.] Deimos: [exasperated] "WHAT?!" [The camera pans back to show Captain Jack Morgan standing next to him. Fake cheers are inserted here by my crack editor...uh, well, me, and Jack turns to the camera and winks. Deimos growls.] Deimos: [growling under his breath] "What is it?" [Jack turns back to Deimos.] Jack: "Mate, the clown doesn't want to leave." [Deimos shrugs his shoulders.] Deimos: "So? How is that my problem?" [Jack scratches his head and then thinks he's found the answer.] Jack: "Garrr...matey. Well, it's your problem because if he doesn't leave, then we can't make it to the match. And if we can't make it to the match, then we won't leave here. So, if we leave here, we can make it to the match. But, hence, the clown doesn't want to leave, then it looks like we won't be making it to the match, love." [Deimos looks at him very angrily.] Deimos: "Well, what the hell do you suggest I do then, [mockingly] MATEY?" [Jack has to think to himself for a moment before answering.] Jack: "You'll have to go talk to him because he sure won’t listen to ol’ Jack Morgan. [smiling] He’s having too much fun.” [Deimos growls to himself again.] Deimos: [mad] "Alright! Let's go get the stupid clown and get the hell out of this insane asylum. It's driving me nuts!" [Jack smiles at him.] Jack: "Right mate. You go get him and I'll stand [motioning toward where he's standing] here." [Deimos turns and gives him a weird look. Deimos then grabs him by the hair and drags him off screen. The camera turns and we now see that they are at a carnival.] Jack: "Or not..." [Deimos drags Jack by the hair toward the Ferris wheel as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * Deimos talks the Clown into Leaving [Deimos drags Jack over to the Ferris wheel but stops right in front of a red and white pin stripped tent as the “Killer Klown” comes running towards them. He’s got his evil popcorn shooter and is trying to shoot it at some people behind them but it doesn’t work. He starts pounding on it and hitting it.] KKFOS: “Ashiandi macktu vida pudu!” [Deimos can only shake his head.] Deimos: [thinking something is up] “Clown, have you been turning people into cotton candy again?” [The clown nods his head incessantly and motions for Deimos to walk over past the tent. Deimos and Jack follow and peek around the tent to see three giant cotton candy cocoons with people inside them. The clown’s meals for the week have been made. Deimos shakes his head.] Deimos: “Clown, you have a four-way tag team macth that you should be preparing for. So, I must ask, [suddenly bursting into a rage] WHY THE HELL ARE YOU WORRIED ABOUT YOUR BIG, FAT STOMACH? WHY ARE YOU SO CONCERNED WITH FOOD?!” [The clown shrugs his shoulders.] Jack: “He thinks with his gut, mate. We can win the match using just our swords and clown noses, right clowny?” [The clown nods his head. Deimos is not as enthusiastic.] Deimos: “Guys, how the hell are we going to explain three dead bodies in cotton candy cocoons to the police? Huh? How are we going to do that? We can’t win the match in jail, can we?” Jack: “Uhhh, no?” [Deimos snaps his fingers.] Deimos: “Exactly. That’s my whole point! Now, we need to go to the clown’s circus tent of doom out there [pointing] yonder and hide these bodies before they stink up the joint. Got it? Good. Now let’s roll.” [Deimos pauses for a second.] Deimos: “Wait a minute. [looks around] Where’s “Big” Dick?” [Suddenly, they hear loud groaning and moaning noises. Jack gets all scared.] Jack: “It’s the Night of the Living Dead!” [Deimos slaps him in the back of the head.] Deimos: “It is not! It’s…” [They turn around and see “Big” Dick N. Uranus’ shadow in the tent and he’s having sex with a woman in there. They all look horrified. The situation becomes even more horrific as they look up from watching the two shadows and see a sign on the tent that says “Bearded Lady.”] Jack: [frightened] “JESUS!!!” [The three of them run toward the nearby bathroom tent. He motions toward the KPFOC as they run into the bathroom tent.] Deimos: “Go wash! Go wash!” [As they run into the tent, Deimos wipes his forehead and slinks over to the nearby bench where a beautiful Goth brunette is sitting. He acts like he’s been through a marathon. It’s almost as if he was the one having…well, nevermind. He sits down beside her as she lights one up and he pulls some cigarettes out of his pocket. He puts one in his mouth, pulls out his lighter, and lights it up. He puts his lighter away, takes a puff, and turns to the lady.] Deimos: “Busy night?” [The woman nods as Deimos has a funny smile on his face as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.]
