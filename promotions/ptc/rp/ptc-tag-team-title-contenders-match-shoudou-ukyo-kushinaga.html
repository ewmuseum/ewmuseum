PTC Tag Team Title Contenders Match

16 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shoudou/Ukyo's RP Title: Encouragement :: Opens up inside of a rooftop penthouse that belongs to Charlene Kwon who was the manager and training coach for the 4WW East Wind Champions, Shoudou and Ukyo Kushinaga. In the large living room, Charlene was sitting on comfortable tan leather couch as she was intently watching the East Wind Champions mimicking one another and doing research on their opponents on her silver Apple laptop at the same time. But if you were to take a closer look you would see that both of them are having a game of DDRMAX2: Dance Dance Revolution on the Playstation 2 with the use of the dual dance pads though they weren’t playing instead they was training for their upcoming PTC Tag Team Contender’s Match. :: :: Ukyo soon stops training and stands on the dance pad, sighs out in frustration and slowly her back on the plasma-screen TV as she thrusts her hand down to cause a signal card to slide out from her long baggy sleeve of her dark blue shirt, holding the card tightly in her hand as the card begins to glow a bright violet aura. She sharply turns around to face the TV as she throws the energy card right at the TV. :: :: …The TV instantly explodes when the energy card hit it… :: :: Charlene surprisingly gasped out as she startled by the loud explosion while Shoudou just makes a soft curious sound and curiously looks over to her tag team partner through the dark shadows that hides his face. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ] (frustratingly tone): This is so stupid! We don’t need to train I mean we can easily kick all those teams’ asses blindfolded. :: Charlene emits a soft sigh pass her lips and looks at them with her brown eyes. :: [ Charlene ]: You two did great last time but this time it’s different. You’re not facing 4WW tag teams instead you guys are facing other teams from the PTC community who too wants a shot at the PTC Tag Titles. :: Ukyo just emits a dull sigh and places her hands over her shoulders, looking down at the floor in boredom. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Like you said Charlene, we did great and we’re going to do great this match. Right, Turenn? :: Shoudou, an enigmatic man, who as far as anyone knew so far had the real name of "Turenn", looking over to Ukyo through the shadows. :: [ Turenn ]: Of course, with my skill and yours’ we are going to truly do some damage and those PTC Tag Titles will be ours for the taking. We are both of warrior spirits. I've looked at this community, and there are some that wouldn’t be in a competition of honor. But they will not be a problem. :: Charlene had her eyes halfway closed as she looks at both of them, dully. She slowly closes her laptop to look at them more clearly. :: [ Charlene ]: I’m glad that you two are very confident about winning the match but this confidence alone will not help with this match. Like I said, you two did great…for a team that didn’t even had a single training session. :: She stands up from her seat and looks at both of the champions seriously with her brown eyes as Turenn notices the change of attitude from Charlene since she is so polite and quiet but whenever it comes to a match Charlene changes to a serious strategist. :: [ Charlene ]: Look, your skills are good but good can approve and I want you two to truly, truly ready to face these teams. The two of you are the current 4WW East Wind Champions, but are you a real team? You've not even worked out a team finishing move yet. You need something that matches both of your styles, seeing as how neither one of you is a huge power house, a woman nor a cruiserweight needs to have something special. :: While Charlene was explaining the reasons why they needed to be ready to face this obstacle that awaits them in PTC, Ukyo began to think back on how they got this opportunity in the first place. :: :: …The flashback begins… :: :: Opens up inside of Ukyo’s locker room as her younger brother, Kyo Kushinaga was having both hands over her arm that was hung up in the ropes as she just got finished with her match against the Shadow Ascensions. Ukyo winced once from feeling a very burning sensitive coursing through her favoring arm as he was using his Spirit Energy to heal her arm. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Ow! Shit, that’s too hot! :: Kyo forms a dull expression on his face with hearing his older sister’s words. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: Well, this is what your ass get I mean what made you think that you challenge Dark Angel head on. :: Ukyo closes her eyes and looks modestly. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: He came in the ring and ruined my opportunity of finally showing that stupid bitch, Patti Anderson that she is not the top diva in 4WW. :: Kyo’s brown eyes have turned tiny which caused him to look at her very own sibling much dully than before a sweat drop trickles down from the side of his face. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ] (dully): You really showed him… I mean he was really trembling in his biker boots when you got your arm caught in the ropes. :: Ukyo looks with great embarrassment for a brief moment before looking modestly as she has a faint pink blush across in her cheeks in reaction to way she was feeling. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ] (modestly): I tripped... :: Kyo still kept his hands over his sister’s arm as they was glowing a dim cerulean aura, having both eyes closed as he was looking even more dull than before. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ] (more dully): More like the big bastard has pushed you away from you like you was nothing… :: A single sweat drop trickles down behind the side of her side, looking embarrassing once more for a brief moment before turning her expression to a furious one. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ] (angrily): Well, where the hell were you anyways! We are suppose to family the last time I remembered! [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: Sorry, Tarrant’s funeral ran late and besides I know you could’ve handle Dark Angel it was just that you were too exhausted after contending against Patti and Bullet. :: Ukyo simply nods her head in reply to her brother’s words. Kyo soon stopped healing his sister’s arm and guides his hands away from her arm and stands up from his kneeling position and looks down at his sitting sister curiously. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: How does it feel now? :: Ukyo moves the healed arm around in circle once and looks in delight. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: It feels better… Man, your new powers will kickass. :: Kyo makes a soft amusing sound from hearing his sister’s words. :: :: …Ukyo’s locker room door opens… :: :: Both siblings looks over in the direction of the open door to see that Turenn whose face was in the shadows like always, unless he was wearing his mask, so far the only people he'd actually shown his face to were Ukyo, Kyo, and Charlene. :: :: Kyo gives Turenn a slight stare as he still have fully trusted him yet though he stopped and walks away from the two team. Turenn looks down at the sitting Ukyo with concern though you could not tell since his face was hidden in the dark shadows. :: [ Turenn ]: Are you all right, Ukyo? :: Ukyo nods her head in reply. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Yeah, Kyo healed my arm with his powers. [ Kyo Kushinaga ] (angrily): And where the hell was you when my sister was caught on the ropes…? :: Turenn glances over to Kyo’s voice noticing that he was leaning against the wall with both arms folded over his chest. Kyo was staring at Turenn with slight anger though tranquilly. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: You are supposed to be my sister’s tag team partner after all so I was expecting you to help her. [ Turenn ]: Sorry but I was at the receiving end on a Shadow Ascensions’ attack, specifically a certain half-breed Judas that I just got confirmation from the Childresses that I'll be facing in a few weeks, one on one. :: Kyo still had his doubts about Turenn since no one really knew what he was there for. All they all knew was that he was 'hired' to try to attack Patti Anderson, and in doing so he'd run into her 'knight', Dark Angel in a bad way. :: :: Ukyo notices the tension between both, Turenn and her younger brother, Kyo as she did not want those two to fight so she quickly stood up and looks contently wanting to change the subject. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: I know! Since we’re alright how bout we go to the nearest bar from here and get wasted! :: Both, Kyo and Turenn still staring at one another as Ukyo just forms a modest expression on her face as a couple sweat drops was trickling down from sides of her face. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ] (in thought): Tough room… :: …Ukyo’s locker room door opens once more… :: :: Ukyo, Turenn, and Kyo looked over at the open door to notice a sweating and panting Charlene standing in the open door bent over. :: [ Ukyo, Turenn and Kyo at the same time ]: Are you ok? :: Charlene glances up at them slightly and slowly nods her head once while panting still. Ukyo takes a step forward and looks at Charlene curiously with her dark blue eyes. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ] (curiously): Hey, where did you go anyways I mean we haven’t seen until now. :: Charlene stands up straight and looks at Ukyo with a devilish smirk. :: [ Charlene ]: Busy getting both, you and Turenn an opportunity to get noticed in the PTC Community. :: Kyo makes a soft curious sound though sharpens his eyes in seriousness when hearing Charlene mentioning those three letters as he was very familiar with that community. Ukyo looks in curiosity as Turenn would look in the same way but the shadow was covering his face up. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: PTC… [ Turenn ] (finishing his partner’s sentence): …Community? :: Charlene looks with utter delight about what kind of deal that she made to help get both the East Wind Champion noticed in the PTC World. :: [ Charlene ] (contently): Yup! In a couple of days there will be a PTC Tag Team Title Contenders Match and the winner will get a chance at winning PTC gold. The Tag Team Championship for PTC has been vacated, so they're holding a small invitational match to determine new contenders. The winners will fight the former #1 contenders for the vacated titles. To make things even sweeter, you, Ukyo and Turenn will be representing 4WW in this very match! :: Ukyo looks thrill about the Charlene’s news. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Wow! That is sweet deal! Turenn and I not only kicking ass in 4WW but we will be kicking ass in this community! :: Charlene glances over to Turenn and notices him not looking too thrilled as Ukyo was about hearing the news. :: [ Charlene ]: Aren’t you excited, Turenn? [ Turenn ]: It’s alright I just don’t really care about winning titles. I’m in it for the fighting. :: Charlene having her index finger on her chin as she begins to think a bit and looks up at the ceiling with a thinking gaze. :: [ Charlene ]: Well, I did hear that the teams in this Contenders Match will be challenging. [ Turenn ]: Then I guess you can count me in…I'm still not big on it, but if it'll put a craw into Dark Angel's game, I'm all for it. Not to mention, it'll give us some practice at dealing with large groups. After tonight, we need it. :: Charlene looking in great content from hearing Turenn’s words. :: [ Charlene ]: Great! We will start training right away because I want you two be fresh and ready to go up against these teams and prove in fact that you two are not a force to be reckon with. I even bet that Mr. Kyo will help your training… [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: Count me out of this… :: Charlene looks over to Kyo surprisingly when hearing his words. :: [ Charlene ]: What? :: Both of the East Wind Champions looks over to Kyo curiously. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: I said count me out of this… :: He removes his back from the wall and begins to head over to the wide open door. :: [ Charlene ]: But why Mr. Kyo? :: Kyo stops and stands outside of the door a bit as he was looking down at the floor seriously while resting his elbow on the cold metal doorframe. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: If you two want to wrestle in the Lion’s Den then be my guest but I don’t want to have nothing involve with those incompetents that cannot appreciate the blood and sweat we put in my league. :: Charlene looks in slight concern when listening to hearing Kyo’s words, wondering what gotten into and why he has such beef with the very community. Both, Turenn and Ukyo listened intently while staring at Kyo curiously. :: [ Charlene ]: Mr. Kyo? :: Kyo places his hand in his long trench coat and pulls his trademark circle-orange tint lens from there and puts them on over his twin brown eyes, as he glances over to both the East Wind Champions seriously. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: Here’s a little piece of advice to help you two on this obstacle, out of the teams in this league you two were picked to represent 4 Winds Wrestling and that means you two are in this match to kick ass and show those other second-class leagues that we’re no frickin’ joke. With that said, you better not forget that because every one of our 4WW fans will be rooting for us to win… That’s all I have to say… So good luck and all that other bullshit… :: Kyo shifts his gaze over to Turenn, glaring at him seriously through his orange tint shades. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: Turenn, be sure to have my sister’s back this time or me and you will have a conversation the next time we meet… :: Turenn just looks at Kyo seriously through the shadows while Kyo just tauntingly winks at him to get under Turenn’s skin. Kyo soon leaves the scene as the door slowly closes, leaving both the champions alone with Charlene. Ukyo just closes her eyes and looks modestly as she was having her arms folded behind her head. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ] (modestly): Eh, he is just being his weird self again and besides we don’t need him as long as we have the prefect strategist here. :: Charlene just looks at the door with concern as she was worried about Kyo. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Earth to Charlene… :: Charlene makes a soft surprise sound when hearing Ukyo’s words as she glances over to her. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: You will help us through this, right? :: Charlene was still out of it but she soon realizes what Ukyo just asked her as she nods her head and looks determine. :: [ Charlene ]: So let’s get started… :: …The flashback ends… :: :: Opens back up at Charlene’s large living room as she was continuing her speech as Turenn stands there in slight boredom and lifts his hand up through the dark shadows and emits a soft yawn in reaction from listening to Charlene’s long speech. Ukyo had just tuned Charlene out. :: [ Charlene ]: And that is why it’s important that you two are ready for this match. [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Ok, this is enough talk it is time to train! :: Charlene blinks once and looks at Ukyo curiously as she did not expect Ukyo to be thrilled about training since she was so frustrated last time. Charlene smiles and looks in utter content with a faint blush across her cheeks. :: [ Charlene ] (in thought): I guess my speech really motivated her to train. :: Little does Charlene know that it wasn’t Charlene’s long boring speech that did it instead it was her younger brother’s words of encouragement. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Let’s spar, Turenn… :: Turenn turns around to notice that Ukyo was in her Crane-style stance, glaring at him with fire in her eyes though this causes Turenn to look at her curiously. :: [ Turenn ]: What's gotten into you, Ukyo? :: Ukyo just smirked and looked modestly. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Just remembered why we are doing this. :: Turenn smirks within the shadows and looks at Ukyo tranquilly as he gets in a fighting stances too though he emits a soft startling gasp and soon lowers his guard when noticing something about Ukyo, looking as if he have seen a ghost from his past. Ukyo raises an eyebrow when noticing him staring at him like that as she lowers her guard. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: What? Do I have a zit or something? [ Turenn ]: …Future Shock… :: Charlene looks a soft curious sound when hearing Turenn’s words as Ukyo looks in confusion when hearing Turenn’s words. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: Future Shock? What’s Future Shock? :: Turenn shakes his head and gets back into his stance. :: [ Turenn ]: It’s nothing. Just one of those stray memories I keep having as they slowly return. Let’s get this sparring match started. :: Ukyo cautiously gets back into her fighting stances, looking at Turenn seriously though curiously. :: [ Ukyo Kushinaga ]: If you say so… :: …There is moment of silence throughout the large living room… :: :: Just then, both champions widens their eyes like someone just pulled trigger of the starting gun as they was lunging straight at one another and began fight viciously, wanting to get ready for the competitors in the Contenders Match. Charlene just takes a seat back down on the soft couch and watches them sparring against one another but she soon just remembers the two words that Turenn said. :: [ Charlene ]: Future Shock…? :: She smiles contently. :: [ Charlene ]: Hmm, that’s not a bad name for this team… :: Opens up now at the far distances of Charlene’s penthouse, a serious Kyo Kushinaga was standing on rooftop, looking down at the penthouse, faintly seeing what was going on inside of the penthouse. Kyo smirks from seeing that both, Turenn and Ukyo sparring against one another viciously. :: [ Kyo Kushinaga ]: I guess they might win after all… :: He makes a soft amusing sound in reaction to his own comment before he bends his knees to cause the ground to ripple underneath his feet like water just then he launches up from the air as the solid ground returns normal. Kyo flies up in the air in a “superman” position for a brief moment before blurring out of the afternoon sky. :: :: …The scene fades to black… ::
