Over For Dinner

9 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-09 Jill drove the Rent-A-Car through the streets of Chicago. She had just come from Chicago O’Hare – perhaps the most infuriating airport in the world. Her employer, Dave Harley, had sent her to WWA Headquarters because he had left a sandwich behind earlier that morning. Rather than forget about it – considering Dave Harley is on a rather hefty salary anyway – and let somebody at the office eat it, Dave ordered his Personal Secretary to pick it up before Announcer ‘Fat’ Tony Valentino caught whiff of it. The mayonnaise had turned by the time she arrived back at Chicago. In fact, the sandwich smelt so bad that Airport Security had her searched fearing she was carrying a biological weapon. She didn’t need all this at her age. A few weeks back, she had tried to start a new life for herself. She had eloped to Vegas with an NGW crewmember. But Dave feared it was a plot by Dallas Reeves and hunted her down. She often wished she had fallen under the employment of another member of the WWA roster. Mal Somers for instance. He was now WWA World Champion and, for a long time, was a best friend of Harley’s. But Harley got jealous of his success and quickly turned on him. She very rarely understood the motives of her employer. For instance, she had received a call from him whilst on the flight. He had told her to come around to his house for dinner. Jill had worked for Dave Harley for three years now. The only time she had been ‘invited’ to his house was to unblock a toilet. Bear in mind, Jill was not hired as his personal nursemaid. Her job description involved opening mail, sorting accounts and scheduling meetings. Unsurprisingly, Harley doesn’t pay her for the extra work she puts in. She was now only a few blocks from his house. He had bought a large property on the outskirts of the city soon after the WWA joined Primetime Central – moving networks meant a much larger salary for the WWA stars. Harley had grown up in downtown Chicago. His upbringing was far from privileged, but he had certainly not come from a poor family. Harley’s parents sent him to Law School, but his love for wrestling won the battle over his intelligence. Jill knew that Harley’s parents had once told him wrestling would never get him anywhere. She presumed this was why he enjoyed flaunting his money around town – to rub it in the face of his family. Her car pulled in to the spacious driveway. Jill knew to lock the Rent-A-Car up in the garage. Harley didn’t like ‘normal’ cars sitting in the driveway. He preferred to keep a Hummer and a Porsche in the driveway, so that when people walked past they wondered who lived there. Jill had never seen this happen, but you couldn’t argue with Harley. She stepped in and headed for the huge open plan kitchen. Rain started to fall outside and she was glad to have missed the storm. Glancing at the table, Jill spotted one place set and a few empty plates dotted around the table. Intrigued, she headed to the oven and popped her head inside. The oven was on, but there was nothing cooking. As she gazed in to the open oven, she was startled by a voice. “Jill! There you are!” She almost jumped out of her skin – and almost banged her head climbing out of the oven. She thought of Hansel and Gretel. If this were a fairytale, she’d be roasting right now. The image of Harley booting her in to a preheated oven would probably haunt her as she lay in bed that night. “Mr Harley. Am I to prepare dinner?” She presumed so. Lured over on false pretences. Harley stepped a little closer. “Of course not, Jill. You’re here for dinner!” He led her to the table and sat her down. She felt something cold on her chair and jumped up. “You left a plate on the seat.” “Really?” He sounded a little strange, “I did? How careless of me!” He whipped the plate out from underneath her and she sat. Harley sat himself opposite her and started sharpening his carving knife. “This is very kind of you, Mr Harley. What are we having? A carvery?” “Umm…” Harley glanced around. “Yeah…well…I’m having the carvery anyway.” Jill was confused now. Something in Harley’s eye told her he was up to something. She tried to retrace what had been said in the conversation. Harley was normally so transparent. He was having the carvery…he’d invited her for dinner. No, he’d invited her for dinner. He’d made a point to emphasise that. She looked over at him as he tied a napkin around his neck and licked his lips. “You’re not serious…” “C’mon Jill. Just climb on to the plate. I promise I won’t eat too much of you. The way I see it, you could stand to lose a little weight!” Her jaw dropped open. That was it. Dave Harley had officially gone insane. “What’s the matter? C’mon! It’s for a good cause!” She jumped out of her seat. This time she’d had enough. “What cause exactly?!” Harley put his knife down slowly. “Well, it’s this next PTC match. I’m up against Tarrant. The guy ate his own parents for chrissake! How am I supposed to compete with that?! I know I’m a badass but I’ve never eaten anybody. Just a few pounds of you and I’m sure me and Tarrant would be on even ground!” Jill headed for the door. “Wait! How about just a little leg? Or a finger? Surely you can live without a finger?” But she had already left. She jumped into her car and raced out of the driveway. She wondered if Harley would ever face somebody who paid his staff a generous salary. Perhaps then this job would be worth it!
