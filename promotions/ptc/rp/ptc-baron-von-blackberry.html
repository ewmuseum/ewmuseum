IG 67 RP THREAD

3 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
3 July 2009 Baron von Blackberry in: Sinister Revelation of the Baron! The scene opens up to a sinister laboratory, where mad science happens. In the background are test tubes and beakers lined up on a table, all filled with anonymous but bubbling and potentially haxardous liquids. A cauldron can be seen behind the table. Various mechanical atrocities can be found lined up against the wall, neglected and forgotten thanks to the whims of their creator. The walls were stone, as if this lab was found locked in the deepest dungeon of the most cursed castle in existence. And then there is the individual who's responsible for this scene. He rises up from below the camera's view, a sinister laugh echoing through the halls of his fortress. Two very noticable things are evident from seeing this man. 1) He wears a lab coat. And 2) and perhaps more importantly... he wears a mask. Of a blackberry. This can't possibly end well. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Heeheeheeheeheeheeheehee... SOON! The world will TREMBLE before the chaotic machinations of Baron von Blackberry!" As if on cue, a flash of lightning can be seen, followed closely by the roar of thunder. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "For you see, the idea of an Infinite Gauntlet only appeals to my bid to take over the world! With its power, I could RULE the cosmos for ALL TIME. Heeheeheeheehee! Yes, and with this..." (Blackberry holds up a rather nondescript, cubish-looking device) "...my Hypercube Wrestler Discombobulator... I shall DESTROY my enemies and leave nothing but ashes in my wake! AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" There's a pause as the Baron lets his maniacal laughter drift and little too long, and then he stops suddenly, staring at his Hypercube Wrestler Discombobulator. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ach. I should at least TEST the Hypercube Wrestler Discombobulator before I apply it to my enemies. Who knows what could happen if I tried to use my Hypercube Wrestler Discombobulator before I actually tested it! It could have the opposite effect, creating a polarizing field that might actually give my enemies the talent necessary to defeat me! Or, uh... it could turn them into parmesan cheese. ...I HATE parmesan cheese!" The Baron seems rather enraged, now. Or maybe it's just disgust. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Urrgh! Just thinking of parmesan cheese fills me with rage! What kind of diabolical FOOL would create something so foul, whose texture makes me think of wood and whose taste is FAR too sour for my own, refined tastes! You have soiled the name of frozen pizza for the last time, parmsean cheese! Soon, I will DESTROY you, and those that have birthed your foul existence from the udders of those foul Italian cows! You will RUE the day that your country invented parmesan, Italy! When I gain possession of the Infinite Gauntlet and therefore rule the power of the cosmos, I will turn your entire country into the shape of an ass, so I can promptly kick it right off the Earth!" Now that he's done with that rant, the Baron continues. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ach. Where was I before I was reminded of the foulness? Oh yes." The Baron holds up the Hypercube Wrestler Discombobulator. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Heeheeheeheeheeheeheehee! Behold your DOOM, feeble adversaries vying for the Infinite Gauntlet! For I am Baron von Blackberry, and it is only through my incredible IQ of 275 that it shall be made mine! AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" And now he just keeps laughing, so let's just fade to black now. ---- 4 July 2009 Baron von Blackberry in: Machinations of the Great Beyond! The scene opens up to the Baron again. He's sitting at his workbench, which is loaded with not only wrenches and screwdrivers, but also bizarre tools that include what the Baron refers to as his "Almighty Blackberry Tool". He's messing with a new device, and it should be noted that the "Hypercube Wrestler Discomboulator" is now in a nearby garbage can. It's either a colossal failure, or the Baron got really bored with it. With the Baron, you could never tell. Anyway, the Baron looks up and sees the camera. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ach." He then shoots a glance off-camera. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Why is the camera on?! You KNOW I don't like being filmed when I am performing science! I will have you DESTROYED for this intrusion on my diabolical privacy!" The Baron then sighs and mutters to himself. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ach. It's like the Patriot Act for mad scientists." The Baron sighs and stands up, shaking his fist at the camera. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Now you hear this! I am Baron von Blackberry! I rule my glorious empire from my manor here in the scenic, yet DIABOLICAL nation of Fruitsylvania! You think you can stop me, the Devil Fruit, from taking the Infinite Gauntlet and RULING the cosmos for all time? Perhaps you don't know about the powers of SCIENCE!" He raises his fist, and a bolt of lightning can convieniently be heard. Then he puts down his fist and talks some more. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "While the Hypercube Wrestler Discomboulator turned out to not be such a good idea, mostly because I tested it on one of my legions of test subjects and all it did was give them on a minor rash... I will not give up! I will claim my destiny, and with that destiny comes... the WORLD!" Now that he's done with his little rant, the Baron decides to just launch into his next rant. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "But for now, feeble adversaries, behold the newest incarnation of your DOOM!" The camera pans to the left. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ach, no no no! The other way! The other way!" The camera hastily pans to the right, to reveal a swirling purple vortex located directly in the center of the room. Electricity crackles from the center of the unnatural vortex, white dots (stars, maybe?) seem to rotate around the vortex in a counter-clockwise rotation. This vortex is contained within a spherical device not unlike the sustained fusion reactor that Dr. Otto Octavius from Spider-man 2. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Heeheeheeheehee... behold, my revolutionary Teleportal of the Great Beyond! It reaches across galaxies to help me find the PERFECT minions to assist me in conquering the Infinite Gauntlet and ruling the entirety of the cosmos!" (the Baron seems to ponder this, and now speaks with a lower tone of voice) "Sure, I might bring back a few unwanted things like horrible mutant plagues from outer space or nanomachine gray goo that could wipe out civilization as we know it..." (now he raises his voice to its usual volume "...but this is the name of SCIENCE!" He raises his fist, and a bolt of lightning can convieniently be heard. Then he puts down his fist and pulls out a remote control with a single, red button in the center of it. The Baron grips the antenna on the top of the BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "And now... your DOOM!" He presses the big red button. The vortex crackles a bit... then suddenly disappears. In its place is a pretty young girl with exceedingly long black hair down to her thighs, who wears a white sleeveless top and a rainbow-colored skirt down to her ankles. She carries an empty pot in her left hand, and a confused look on her face. She looks around in the lab, before settling on the lab coat-clad figure standing before her, who cackles maniacally. GIRL: "Um, where am I?" In the foreground, the Baron seems pleased. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Behold, the lair of the genius known as Baron von Blackberry! You have been chosen by the Teleportal of the Great Beyond to serve as one of my personal assistants! Tell me, strange being, where do you come from?" GIRL: "Um... Cleveland." The Baron doesn't even seem to sense anything wrong with this. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "The planet Cleveland! Long have I desired a minion from that end of the galaxy! Yes, your services shall be most useful. Tell me, what kind of superpowers do you possess?" GIRL: "I... what? I don't have any superpowers." The Baron pauses, and seemingly hesitates. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "You... have no superpowers? Well, surely you have a hidden nature. A tail? Maybe some claws? Can you go Super Saijyan?" GIRL: "No, nothing like that." Although it isn't hard to do this to him, the Baron is quite dumbfounded at this turn of events. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "What in the name of Sigmund Freud's mother!? Are all people from your planet as weak as you!? Bah! I shall have the planet of Cleveland destroyed for this!" Angry, the Baron throws the remote control at the device that once contained the Teleportal of the Great Beyond, and he fumes and stomps on the ground in anger, holding his hands over his head. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ach, nein! My experiment is a failure! I worked hard on it, too! My best minions gathered the best materials from the four corners of Fruitsylvania! They even found the rare Frivilim I needed to power the machine! All of it for nothing! Bah! Now I shall have to start over, after I blame everyone else except me for the failure! Bah! Fine! If I can't have an alien space army to do my work in capturing the Infinite Gauntlet for me, then I suppose I'll just have to do it myself!" The girl walks up to the Baron. GIRL: "Um, would you like me to make some coffee?" Suddenly, the Baron's mood seemingly brightens. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Oh, yes. That would be lovely. Make sure there's quite a bit of cream and sugar, though. If it tastes even the least bit bitter, I shall have you destroyed!" Fade to black. ---- 9 July 2009 Baron von Blackberry: A Diabolical Visit To The Tonight Show! The scene opens up to... a studio set. In the background is a night skyline of a city, and in the foreground are a brown desk with a pair of coffee mugs, a stack of papers, and a pencil. There is also a sofa, and on this sofa sit two people. One is a diabolical mad scientist who wears a blackberry mask and a lab coat, and the other is someone who may or may not be a clone of Tom Cruise. We're not sure. Behind the desk sits Jay Leno. Wait, no, I take that back. Behind the desk sits a robot Jay Leno. JAY LENO: "OPERATIONAL SYSTEMS 85%. STATUS NOMINAL. RUNNING MONOLOGUE ROUTINE." Apparently, it's not even *trying* to act like Jay Leno. JAY LENO: "GREETINGS, HUMANS. WITH ME AT THIS TIME IS THE LORD OF ALL CREATION, THE MASTER OF ALL THAT IS SCIENCE, THE DEVIL FRUIT, AND THE MOST DIABOLICAL FRUIT OF THEM ALL... BEEP. GIVE IT UP, FEEBLE HUMANS, FOR MASTER BARON VON BLACKBERRY." The sound of clearly fake applause is heard. The Baron smiles and waves at the "crowd". BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ah, yes, thank you, thank you. It's great to be here." JAY LENO: "RUNNING JOKE PROCESSORS... ERROR. RUNNING BACKUP JOKE PROCESSORS. NOT AS GREAT AS THE RATINGS." Canned laughter, while the Baron just slaps his forehead with the palm of his hand, seemingly frustrated. BARON VON BLACKBERRY (muttering): "Ach, I should have adjusted those processors before we went live." He shakes his head, while the Leno robot continues onward with the interview. JAY LENO: "CLEARLY, YOU ARE THE INDISPUTED RULER OF THE EMPIRE OF FRUITSYLVANIA. HOW DID IT COME TO PASS THAT YOU, THE MIGHTY BARON VON BLACKBERRY, CAME INTO POWER IN THIS GREAT NATION?" BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Heeheeheeheehee... when I was but a small Blackberry, growing up in the foothills of the scenic, yet DIABOLICAL nation of Fruitsylvania, I witnessed countless occasions of severe weakness in the government that I felt that I could take advantage of. They were weak! They were insignificant! They were nothing compared to my genius, so when the time came that I felt that I, the great Baron Von Blackberry, could take over... I did so! Those fools were NO match for my armies of robotic minions! None could oppose my might! I levelled their base of operations, and in its place, I built my DIABOLICAL home in Chateau de Blackberry! And soon... SOON I shall reign over ALL of the world! Nay, all of the COSMOS! AHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" JAY LENO: "BEEP. WHAT WOULD YOU DO WHEN YOU TAKE OVER THE WORLD?" BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "What? Clearly, I would institute policies that would BETTER the world at large. For example, all media outlets would kindly shut up about whether Michael Jackson is a black hero or not. Everyone will swear absolute obidence to me or be destroyed. Everyone will tell everyone else that their leader is both benevolent and kind, or they will be destroyed. This is the kind of society I wish for!" The Tom Cruise clone speaks up. TOM CRUISE: "So, what you really want is a world where nobody has the balls to criticize you?" Baron von Blackberry stares at the clone of Tom Cruise. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "That's strike two." TOM CRUISE: "What the hell was strike one?" BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "That movie you did with the plan to kill the Hitler." TOM CRUISE: "I was just *cloned* from that guy. By *you*." Baron von Blackberry stares at the clone of Tom Cruise. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Leave now, and I might not destroy you right here and now." The clone of Tom Cruise shrugs and walks off-camera. Then Blackberry pulls out a remote control with a red button on it. It looks strangely like the exact same remote control he used in the last promo, but nevertheless, he pushes a button. A scream of a man on fire can be heard, and the clone of Tom Cruise soon runs past the set while on fire before disappearing off-camera. The Baron smells the air. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Mmm. I love the smell of roasted Tom Cruise in the morning." JAY LENO: "BEEP. WHAT DO YOU HAVE TO SAY ABOUT THE ALLEGATIONS THAT YOU ARE A DOCTOR CURIOSITY RIPOFF?" BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Utterly ridiculous. I am the superintelligent dictator of the mighty nation of Fruitsylvania... and he is just a fake doctor with a bad German accent. There is no comparison between the two of us!" JAY LENO: "ARE YOU NOT A DOCTOR?" BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Listen, Herr Leno, if I wanted to be a doctor, I'd just complete my degree at the prestigious Fruitsylvania University. The only reason why I have not is because running a country bent on dominating the world under the iron rule of Baron von Blackberry is exhausting work! I have to make the hard decisions! I have to build the Subsonic Death Machines to level the command centers of my enemies! I have to command the Elimination Squads to exterminate the resistances that crop up against my perfect rule! I have no time to deal with the complications of college life!" The Jay Leno robot ponders this. JAY LENO: "YOU JUST DON'T WANT TO DEAL WITH ANOTHER FRATERNITY INITIATION." BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Bah! So what if they stripped me to my boxers and had me drink beer kegs through funnels, and then I spent half the night bowing before a porcelain throne?! So what if I woke up the next morning covered in shaving cream?! I had them DESTROYED for their insolence! Just as I will destroy you for yours!" And with that, the Baron shoots up from his seat and kicks the Leno robot's head off. Karate-style. Complete with a bizarre "WAAAAAAAH!" sound, like the Baron is, in fact, Bruce Lee. Once he is done with this, the Baron turns to the camera with a sneer. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Interview over! I shall now craft my diabolical plan to claim the Infinite Gauntlet and rule the cosmos! AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" And then we fade to black. Thank God. ---- 15 July 2009 Baron von Blackberry in: Counterattack of the Nefarious! The scene opens up to Baron von Blackberry. He seems to have just woken up, as he's wearing his mask, a bathrobe, fuzzy bunny slippers, and he's carrying a coffee mug. He looks around, confused. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ach. MINIONS! WHERE IS MY MORNING PAPER!? You KNOW that I enjoy reading the funnies in the morning!" The Baron looks around, and sighs. Apparently, the person and thing he's looking for aren't around. So, instead, he decides to do the first logical thing you do in the morning. Check the internet~ A few taps on the keyboard, and the Baron is now amused. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Oh, lolcats. Your insane feline antics do amuse me so. Monorail cat. Simply brilliant. Ho ho ho. When I take over the world and bend it to my will, the second thing I will do is create monorails in the shape of cats. Yes, that is what I shall do." He takes a sip of his coffee and navigates the internet to something else. Something with awful MIDI music. The Baron reacts as anyone would when confronted with such a symphony of sound: He spits his coffee out and quickly clicks away to remove the sound from his delicate, though diabolical ears. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "What... what is this MADNESS? This harsh cacophony resonating in my eardrums! This sinister symphony of suck! It's like... it's like... it's like I'm listening to death metal! Being sung by Gilbert Gottfried! Ach! This sound would drive any sane man into madness! Were I not already insane, I'd have gone insane!" The Baron pauses and considers the last statement. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Wait, am I really insane if I admit to being insane?" Now the Baron pauses and considers *that* statement. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Ach. Whatever. I'll do research on insanity later. In the meantime... that abomination must be eradicated if I am to create a perfect, united, and above all else symmetrical society. Yes, indeed. I shall have to come up with a three point plan to decisively eliminate this... Butcher X." The Baron thinks about it. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "Hm. This shall be tricky. How shall I defeat someone more insane than myself? Reason and logic are incompatable at this stage of the game. My machinations will need to be perfect if I'm going to overcome this devil. So, my first step is to... is to..." The Baron thinks about it, picking up a pencil and scribbling something down on a nearby notepad. However, though he scribbles, his expression becomes more and more frustrated. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "...Ach! Nein, nein, nein. I cannot focus! I keep thinking of that accursed sound! I must find a way to distract myself... something that would bring me pleasure and yet, facilitate my mind and allow me to think of a plan to destroy my adversaries once and for all! But what could do this? What could distract a mind such as my own to such a degree that it could expell that awful aria?" There is a pause. BARON VON BLACKBERRY: "MINIONS! WHERE IN THE NAME OF SCHRODINGER'S PUSSY IS MY MORNING PAPER!?" Yeah, it'll take him a while to come up with a proper plan. Maybe he'll have something the next time we see him. Fade to black.
