Date: 2005-12-10 Outside there is a full moon. My phone had not stopped ringing all night. The same cries and moans from that tired old voice keeps shooting down my ear. My hand began to tremble. "Please don’t do this Steve….I lov-" Love me?, I ask in a petulant tone. "Don’t do this to me" I couldn’t help but smirk, as I turned over that steak I had been slaving over for the past half hour. For one second I lost myself while imagining eating this piece of cow or something. But then I realised I had this break up to deal with. I cannot help you anymore, I say. I cannot be there for you anymore, I say. Goodbye Sarah. Click. Anxious-Avoidant Attachment made me like this (Holmes, J. (1993) John Bowlby and Attachment Theory) Or was it because my mother didn’t love me (Harlow, H. F. & Harlow, M. K. (1969) Effects of various mother-infant relationships on rhesus monkey behaviours. In B. M. Foss (Ed.) Determinants of infant behaviour (Vol.4). London: Methuen.) Either way, I’m a psychologists dream. I move away from the cooker, and look out of my apartment window. For someone who works for one of the most prominent wrestling promotions in the world, my humble abode is a shock to most. I think it makes them realise that most of the money we make goes into travelling expenses, shit like that. A fox wanders into my garden, wandering amiably around. Looking like he has lost a single contact lens. I love observing the world inside my world. In my twenty eight years, I have succumb to many things. Addictions, Relationships, Anger. But in my short time, I realise that everyone in the world has a time where they can finally redeem themselves fully once more. In a few days I will finally have this chance. But for now, steak will have to do. … Drug cabinet is full of my little buddies for the day. Peroxide Blue Bill. Primrose Red John. Two for Breakfast. Five for Lunch. Seven for Dinner. A crème de la crème of a cocktail. "Are you ready?" She ruined my morning ritual. One second, I say. "But we need to go now, we were supposed to be at the hospital at 1:15!" Worry. Worry. Worry. All I ever fucking hear. Down you go Bill. Washed down with a glass of Jack. Followed by John. And of the ultrasound we go. … I was drugged up to my eyeballs but still blind to the damage this was doing. How fucking ironic. And then I met her on the dance floor. She looked angelic And I was the apple that tempted her. I went up to her with my awkward shuffle. Knees together, I looked like a big dunce. Hell, that was better then knowing what I really was. Fancy a drink, I say nervously. "Yea, that would be nice." that voice cut through the noise of the DJ. Made the beats sound like a distant memory. Or was that the drugs kicking in. I don’t know anymore. So what do you drink I whisper in her ear. Such a sweet smell off her hair. I became transfixed on her beauty. So much that she was shouting her answer at me. I think the drugs are definitely kicking in now. … Much of what I know, I find is the truth. Well, most of the time. One of these truths is that women tend to lie. A lot. Another one is that they stab you in the back. Over and over. I stood in front of the mirror, shaving my hair off. As more hair drops to the floor, I can see myself becoming…something. I see my soul in front of me. I can see what I will become one day. Bloodshot eyes and broken self images are one of the less desirable side-effects of the drugs. I know this. She knows this. Sarah sits behind me; looking up with her drained face. Her eyes swelling up with the those damn tears of hers. "Please Steve…why are you doing this to yourself?" Worry Worry Worry. All I ever fucking hear. It’s the only release I have in this shitty life, I snap back. "We will go back to the rehab clinic, we will go back to Morrison…We can-" We can do fucking nothing, I say. Fact is. I don’t want to change. "Steve….I’m begging you." I didn’t see her on her knees, grovelling. "I'll do anything." Liar. "I'll help you through this." Liar. Her sobbing was getting on my nerves now. I couldn’t even concentrate on my shaving anymore. "…I'm pregnant." … A fox wanders into my garden, wandering amiably around. Looking like he has lost a single contact lens. I love observing the world inside my world. In my twenty eight years, I have succumb to many things. Addictions, Relationships, Anger. But in my short time, I realise that everyone in the world has a time where they can finally redeem themselves fully once more. If Sarah stayed, that…thing stayed. How could I do anything in life…if I had that burden with me? I would become 38 in 10 minutes seemingly. 48 in 5. And I would of achieved nothing. Nada. These kind of truths grow inside you like a tumour. I embraced the future. The first thing you need to do. Is forget your past. Goodbye Sarah. Click.
