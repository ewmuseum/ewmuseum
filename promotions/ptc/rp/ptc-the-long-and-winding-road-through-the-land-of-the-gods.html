The Long and Winding Road Through the Land of the Gods

16 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With a flourish, and a few words, the air began to shimmer. The shimmering continued until the very molecules of the area parted and formed a door. Beyond it were nothing but azure and clouds, but DarkStar and Talena both knew exactly what it led to. It was, to use a very basic analogy, it was the highway through the InBetween. To describe it as something as normal and bland as a simple highway doesn’t do it a single bit of justice. Nothing in the InBetween existed on the mortal coil. Humans, animals, extraterrestrials, these things were all living and could not easily pass between the different worlds of the InBetween itself. Some were granted the ability (such as Talena) and others had to master it through years and years of study, although most found it a very unfruitful experience once they had. One would think much knowledge could be attained in a place like this, but they would be wrong. In theory with every realm of Gods, legends, folklore, and fantasy at your fingertips you could learn a lot to make you enviable on any timeline… but that was not the case. What could you learn from a Leprechaun? They are mischievous creatures and anything they taught you would have to be taken with a grain of salt. The Faeries are no better, they are more concerned with prancing then they are about any cosmic changes. The myth that a fairy exists to help a humans life is sorely mistaken, and many mortals have found that out through violent and dangerous means. The worst of all for gathering knowledge would of course be the gods. Whether they are the few that remain strong and powerful or the thousands who are nearly forgotten outside of a few historians and a couple of textbooks, they do not give their information lightly. There is not one creature, mortal or otherwise, who has the ability to be more selfish than a god. DarkStar knew this, and he knew the worst of all was God himself. None of the others really believe he created everything. If you ask Zeus or Odin their opinion of him they would spit, curse, and with a hint of jealously paint you a picture of him being nothing more than in the right place at the right time. They would be one hundred percent correct. The God that the Christians worship was barely a few thousand years old, a mere child to the Egyptians and the Greeks who now fight amongst themselves for the remaining power in their kind. But luck has a lot to do with a lot of things, and this was one of them. DarkStar was not looking forward to having to meet with that bearded twat of a deity again. Once before he met with him and once before it nearly came to blows. DarkStar was stronger than, and in hindsight he should have smacked God a good one just to wake him up, but he didn’t. He let the whole issue drop and he left the Kingdom of Heaven, vowing to never set foot in between it’s decaying gates again. God was cocky. Thanks to his pretentious son and his lucky and well placed holidays, God never had to worry about his power disappearing. Sure, it waned a bit after every tragedy or scandal, but it never made much of a difference. The worst part was that God could care less about his ‘creations’. Although he didn’t actually have anything to do with the birth of the universe, since everybody with half a brain knows creationism is absolute bullshit, he never really bothered to play the part that the mortals gave to him. God sat on his elaborate throne and grew fat off of the worship he was given for no reason. He never did a thing for humanity and never would. His bemused interest in their doings was as far as he would go. If people knew the truth they would be very disappointed, but nobody had the guts to go against him. DarkStar would have, but now would not be a good time to challenge him. “Are you ready, mon amour?” Talena looked him in the eyes lucidly one last time and nodded quickly. They held their hands together tight and took the one step into the newly formed door. The minute they passed into the InBetween the door flickered and closed. They were now walking on nothing. To the uninitiated eye it seemed as if they simply stepped foot before foot through this beautiful, but seemingly unnatural, sky. To them they could see the way. Every single realm that existed was accessible here as long as you knew the true road. The InBetween was limitless and no directions could possibly be given that an outsider could understand, but all who inhabited it knew. To travel to another realm would take centuries without the knowledge of where it lay on the path. Effortlessly you could pass through the small kingdoms of the legends. People like Paul Bunyan and Johnny Appleseed. They were not gods, nor did they have powers, but the people brought them into existence and exist they did. They very rarely, if ever, traveled into the mortal world and even less rarely did they accept visitors into their lands. The urban legends and the folk heroes didn’t live off of power like the Gods did, they were less lucid since most of them existed solely in the minds of grade school children now a days. Johnny Appleseed is said to have his entire realm wallpapered in the best drawings of Kindergarteners. DarkStar wouldn’t know either way, the folk heroes gave him the creeps. After a bit of traveling they reached the realms of the demi-gods. This is where the rest of his kind resided. Not full Gods and not truly immortal, but with powers and legends revolving around them none the less. Many of the realms were abandoned or in large states of decay, but the few that remained were strengthened by the resilience of the stories surrounding them. Hercules for instance. Thanks to Kevin Sorbo he still existed in the minds of enough people to keep his realm strong. For a brief second DarkStar thought about calling in a favor he was owed from long ago pertaining to a situation about Hercules love life (the man didn’t quite have his fathers reproduction talents, but DarkStar knew some lore residing deep in the Earth that would help him with that- a sort of mystical magical viagra) but decided against it. He suffered through serious mood swings that didn’t seem as if they were as much his personality as they were the steroids. He was strong, but not nearly strong enough to help him if something went wrong. In the silence between him and Talena, he felt a pang of sorrow. If this would have happened two or three years ago he would have been able to amass an army of creatures and gods to back him up. He could have sauntered into any number of realms with that cocky smile and charmed the doubts right out of any number of demi-gods heads. Maybe one or two of them wouldn’t be enough, but he had enough friends (and more importantly enough friends owed him favors) to gain a small army of nearly forgotten powerhouses. It wasn’t then, now. It was now now. And now he had to play with the cards he was dealt. After the realms of the demi-gods, they began to walk through the abandoned realms of the lesser gods. At one point long before DarkStar took up the mantle of the Lord of Dreams this area was inhabited by the lesser powerful gods from their own localized versions of mythology. The Norse, Greeks, and Egyptian gods that were not powerful or worshiped enough to control the larger centralized realms that their leaders did were given the equivalent of a fiefdom. This is where Loki and Fenrir once survived and ruled their domains. The once great mead halls were now crumbling, but the ground was still moistened with the blood that was spilt in the name of the Norse. Further on the sound of thunder as the decimated realms of the Greek lesser passed under DarkStar and Talena’s feet. Demeter, Dionysus, and Hestia no longer stayed here, lacking the power of their names to control their realms without sacrificing themselves. The elaborate and self gratifying architecture remained despite not being kept for generations upon generations. DarkStar remembered spending a brief time in Dionysus’s palace on his first journey to the Kingdom of Heaven. It oozed of fertility and the dirt beneath his feet yearned to grow, but nothing would be harvested ever again there. The Romans realm did not even exist any longer. The fall of their culture lead to not only the destruction and scattering of its people, but to the death of their own Gods. Somewhere, maybe, it could be found… but none who DarkStar had spoken with knew its location. The once proud decadent culture of the Romans was the first true casualty to God and his Christian forces. The lesser of the Eastern culture still resided in their worlds. The respectful acknowledgement of what they once were by their people was enough to sustain them in their quiet and reverent existence. Although none had the power of the great Buddha, they did not ask for it. The Asian cultures respected their history and their past and through that respect their many old Gods still lived as the polar opposite of the Roman lore. DarkStar knew not to bother them in their meditations, he knew he never had allies in this area. To the Eastern Gods he was nothing more than an anomaly, they did not need dreams since they had enlightenment. He couldn’t argue and knew that. They quickly left that realm and entered perhaps the saddest of them all in DarkStar’s opinion. It was easy to avoid the wasteland of the Celtic Gods, a few steps through the Egyptian lesser areas and they would have been nearly to the gates of Heaven… but the Egyptians were still strong thanks to the pyramids and the hidden secrets of those great deserts and DarkStar was not willing to risk becoming sport to them. Instead he would suffer his own grief. The Celtic realms were long ago the home of peace and music. This is where the Bards resided. Their magical spell singing kept the realms lush and vibrant. There were very few worlds in the InBetween that could rival the beauty of the Celts, the Irish, and the Welsh. The cross huggers soon moved in and all memory of these joyous Gods were erased. DarkStar had run into one of the few remnants long ago on Earth, and despite her bitterness she was by far the only ‘god’ that DarkStar could truly ever stand. He knew not what had happened to Cerridwen, but her intelligence and beauty would never leave him. If there was any woman he loved other than his own Talena it was her. The green grass had since browned without the songs of the Bards to keep the land in good health. DarkStar remembered what Cerridwen had said about the beauties of this land. How it had looked like the lands of the people who worshiped them in the height of summer. How the trees gave off as fragrant a scent as the flowers did, and most importantly how every single inch of land felt alive. He sorely wished he could have seen it. He turned to look at Talena and say something about how beautiful this land must have been, but he found suddenly she wasn’t standing near him. With a panicked frenzy he looked around the area for her. She could not be left alone long without him to guide her. Her sanity was draining quickly and she was a simple mortal… although blessed with second sight. Before he could call out to her, a womens voice spoke. “If only it could be under less foul motives that we meet again, Drazail.” The voice was not Talena’s, but he recognized it. That scent of honey wine engulfed him and with a smile he turned to the smell “I was not aware one of your many talents was reading minds, Cerridwen. I was just thinking of you.” “I hope the thoughts were flattering.” She moved a few strands of her golden hair out of her face, her lips curved into a smile that’s brightness put the sun to shame. When DarkStar looked back around at the area he realized that the grass was green again. Although green was an understatement of the true color. The slight breeze kept the warmth from getting too warm. The climate was perfect. The entire place felt.. alive. “For you, the thoughts are always flattering.” He hesitated for a second, “I was wondering what had happened to you. The humans can be so cruel with who they worship. A goddess like you should be reaping faith from all ends of the universe. Is all well?” She shrugged. “I cannot complain.” Her accent was music to his ears, it was slight after the generations she spent away from her world, but it was returning. “I was lucky enough to befriend one of the fairies from my time, he was bitter towards the way God has siphoned the power from the rest of us. He helped me return here. This is where I want to live out the remaining time I have.” “Do not speak like that Cerridwen. Have you run out of hope?” “For me?” She paused and than sighed sadly, “Yes. There is no hope for the remaining lesser Gods, especially of my culture. The world does not remember the bards or the spell singers. So it certainly does not remember those of us with less documented histories. I exist now because of students on your world who take classes such as Celtic Mythology in order to have an easy credit on their final grade.” For a second, DarkStar let a sneer cross his face, “Cerridwen, you are fair and just, but please do not slight me by referring to that rock as my world. I may have been born a human but I live as a god.” She seemed to not notice his complaint. “I came here when I felt your presence. Your lover remains in safety. I am the last ruler of this realm and I declare the rules. To her this may only be a second… I have seen ill in your future.” “What ill? I cannot see my life getting any worse than it is right now. I am a ghost without a body and a king without followers. There is few things worse than what I now face.” Cerridwen shook her head, “No. No there is something worse. Much worse. Being forgotten and fading into the mist is nothing compared to the terrible fate that could face you if you continue further. God is not a friendly man, and you know you lack any friends in that realm. Please… Drazail Dream Lord… turn back and figure out another way.” DarkStar laughed, not in a mean or mocking way but of surprised amusement, “In the name of Caibre and Ogma! Since when have you become such a powerful seer. Are you not the goddess of mirth, song, and love? Why give me ill news with no details? Is not the future far out of your domain?” Although he sounded confident and unshaken, he was slightly jarred. It had been years since he saw her, and now she spoke to him as if she feared for his life- she DID fear for his life. He knew the ways of seers, being in love with one for nearly a decade, and the second sight they granted was generally brief and foggy. Even the most clear of visions had to be digested lightly, and could not be told fully for fear of causing irreparable damage to space and time. Although the Gods cared far less about small problems like that than mortals did. “You of all people should know that you cannot have mirth without sadness, song without silence, or love without hatred.” They stood staring at each other as the wind picked up. It was now stronger than a breeze. It ruffled their hair and then through the trees. She spoke softly, “Just like you cannot have dreams without nightmares.” “I understand!” he yelled, he respected Cerridwen, and yes he loved her a little, but he did not need to be lectured on the need for dreaming. Dreams got him into this mess to begin with. If only he would have cast the crown to the floor when he received it. No, he couldn’t think like that. It was far too late for second guessing decisions like that. “I’m sorry.. I did not mean to lash out like that. Please. Tell me what you have seen.” Cerridwen’s eyes showed her true compassion, DarkStar knew she couldn’t tell him exactly what he saw but he wished for some elaboration. “When you reach the gates it will be difficult for you to pass unharmed, when you do pass through the gates what you seek will be given to you.” There was silence. DarkStar broke it with an outburst, “And how is this ill news? I will be granted my humanity! I will be granted the ability to repair my realm! This is news worth celebrating!” “No!” she cut him off, “No. It is not. You will be granted what you seek but it will not be in the way which you seek it. That is all I know. Please turn back Dream Lord.” He shook his head, “I cannot.” Saddened, he turned his back on her, “Cerridwen, I thank you for coming to see me and try to help, but unless you are planning on coming with me to the gates- I must leave you. I grow weaker by the second.” Cerridwen was dismayed, “Then if you will not turn back, stay with me. Stay here with me in what was once my land. Together maybe we can reap enough remaining power to rebuild it, we could make it a haven like the Norse and the Greeks have done. A rallying point for the forgotten.” The light faded slightly in the sky as if a cloud passed over it, the warmth left briefly. DarkStar closed his eyes and pictured what they could do together. She was once a goddess who wielded great power, and he a king of a powerful lore… but he knew he could never leave his lover to rot here. The temptation of Cerridwen was great, but not as great as what he needed to do. “I am tempted, but I cannot. This is not my realm and I do not have the historical documentation that Zeus has to drive my life. No. I must continue. I am sorry.” The light returned and Cerridwen walked towards him. Her bare feet left no prints in the grass as she walked. When she reached him she put her hand on his shoulder. Her bottom lip moved as if she was ready to say something, and it closed again. She was silenced by her own thoughts. Wordlessly she embraced him, kissing him deeply. He took her into his arms and together they shared a brief moment in the beauty of what remained of this oasis. Together they stood, wordlessly. As moments passed Cerridwen finally spoke, “I needed to feel that one more time to truly know what we will both be missing. I fear this is the last time I will see you Drazail, I hope my vision was wrong but in my heart I know it is not. Farewell. We will always have Philadelphia.” A blink. She was gone. Her golden hair, her emerald eyes, her shapely body, gone in a heartbeat. Replaced once again by the desolate remains of the Celtic god’s world. Talena was next to him again. She had never noticed his departure, it had been only a few seconds. He reassuringly squeezed her hand just to remind himself of what he sacrificed peace with Cerridwen for. He felt her blood rush through her veins and felt as if they were one. Peace was an easy sacrifice for love like this. “You seem tired, we have walked long and I have no stars to speak with. Will we reach the gates soon my lover?” Her sanity was hanging by a thread. “Yes, Talena. The gates are accessible to us from here. Part of me wishes to risk the mountain of Olympus and the oceans of the Norse. I know they have much power left, and many of the demi-gods now reside in the kingdoms of Zeus and Odin… but I fear they have run out of patience with me.” They walked a bit more. They had traveled centuries worth of distance in barely a half and hour of time. “Odin and Zeus. Zeus and Odin. They borrow their power from history books and television specials. They house their last brethren in their halls and hope for the death of God but they fear him too much to make an attempt at his house. They are cowards.” Talena hissed the word coward as if it disgusted her, “If they could bury their own hatchets they would have the power between them to do it. They have the armies do they not? Are there not enough Norseman and Greeks to fight?” DarkStar nodded curtly, “Yes, Zeus and Odin use their power to keep their brothers and sisters who lack belief alive. They both sacrifice the weakest to themselves for more power though, they are like aura based cannibals. No. I will certainly not be finding help there… Look.” They had arrived. In front of them was the endless line of souls awaiting their judgment. Somewhere beyond the growing queue were the ivory gates into the Kingdom of God. It was a common misconception that the gates were made of pearl, when in fact they were made from the tusks of a gigantic elephant God had created, when he grew tired of its antics he had it slayed and built into the towering entryway that it was known today. “How will we reach the front, the line continues for longer than memories remain in the pit of your brain.” Talena’s eyes seemed to be focused on something that nobody there could see, this kingdom did not operate under the dream lord’s rules and Talena lacked any semblance of sanity while within its borders. She knew the consequences of following DarkStar on this journey, but now she was crazy enough that she didn’t know she was supposed to have ever known the consequences. “We will reach the front just like anyone else would,” he began to push his way between the souls, “By cutting. Excuse me. Pardon me. Excuse me. Oh you’ve got an eternity to wait, your dead, shut the hell up.” It went this way for what seemed like an entire week, DarkStar and Talena shoved the recently deceased to either side as they made their way to the front of the line. Very rarely one would recognize him, it was a good sign that he conjured up memories that quickly. If all went well he would get his Get Out of the Ethereal Free card from God and be back on Earth within the day. If the climate in the wrestling world was right enough and if he was still close enough to peoples minds that he could jar their memories into remembering him, he could return to his seat of power… it was a long shot. It would be easier for him to simply become a TV star or a Movie star and dictate the people from that position, but it was risky. He didn’t want to invest what little power he had into a fools venture. He was already proven as a wrestler and he would prove himself once more. One more victory and the people will remember, he could use that position to lure them back to the dreaming. To inspire them to care again. Or at least he hoped. Finally, he and Talena passed the first person in line. The elderly man protested slightly but stopped caring. Like DarkStar said, they all had eternity, unlike him. “Hey, you are supposed to stay in-… holy shit.” Peter was startled. He nearly lost his page in the large book sitting in front of him. DarkStar chuckled to himself knowing that Peter still had a semblance of respect for him. “So you remember me?” Peter nodded like a child with ADD drinking a can of coke. He was a hyper angel, hence why he was given the most menial of jobs in this kingdom. “Of course. Holy shit. It took us five years just to clean all the bits of angel brain and blood out of the curtains of the dining hall. HE is not going to be happy. Not at all. Not one bit. No sir.” “Calm down you nitwit. I know he won’t be happy, but I wouldn’t have set foot in this place if it wasn’t important. I come because my realm is failing, and your Lord needs me to contribute power to this operation he has going.” Peter stood up and began to ring a bell that wasn’t visible. “Ok. Ok. But when he asks you forced me to do this. You know his son is still royally pissed right? You slapped him right in the mouth. Christ himself. It takes a lot of balls for you to show up here Dream Lord.” DarkStar bowed dramatically, and very sarcastically. Talena curtsied. “Jesus. He is going to be pissed.” The gates opened without a sound, that is if you didn’t count the obnoxious horns of the angels. They did that every time someone was admitted into heaven without stopping. Their peppiness at this tedious and useless job disgusted DarkStar and always would. He hated when people were happy with a crappy lot at life, it wasn’t right. Especially amongst God’s people. Heaven was an entirely garish place. It resembled a combination of a local carnival and Las Vegas, except without the tact. The joyful cavorting of billions echoed throughout the entire vast world. Its location was not in the clouds above the Earth, it had overpopulated that area long ago, but instead it was the largest area of the InBetween. Power oozed from every tacky neon light. “Do you remember the way my love? It is confusing and I see many colors and many thoughts. I think my head hurts but it might just be from blinking.” He was used to her nonsense like water off a duck he ignored it. “I remember the way, not much has changed, but I can see we will be having an escort to the throne.” Walking towards the pair with a purpose were four angels. Although they looked more like bouncers at a strip club than angels of the typical kind. Standing in the middle of them was a man of average height with long, perfect hair. His goatee was neatly shaven and cut close to the skin. “Jesus Christ.” muttered Talena. “You’ve got a lot of damn nerve, sand merchant.” DarkStar noticed that Christ looked angry, and the tone of his voice solidified the opinion. “Sand merchant? Thank your father that you have that career as a carpenter to fall back on, you would have never made it on your wits alone.” Christ scoffed and started to touch the side of his face nervously, “You think you can strike me, the son of God himself, and walk in here to ask a favor? Then you think you have the right to insult me again?” “This is between me and your father. If I had dealings with you it would only be because I needed a table made, wood worker.” Before he could say another word he felt a blow on the side of his head, one of the burly angels cracked him hard with his fist. He fell to the ground with a thump. His power was failing, that shouldn’t done that much damage. Not one punch. He looked to see one of the other angels with his hand around Talena’s mouth. She bit and scratched but could not free herself. “We shall see how my father reacts to your request after these indignities.” DarkStar panted, his breath was shallow and he felt his conciousness leaving, “Too weak… to make the decision… yourself?” He tried to crawl to his feet, “I always knew… you were… a pussy.” Jesus sneered and sent a swift kick to DarkStar’s chin. The wooden sandal connected hard and DarkStar saw nothing but darkness. In his mind he saw the scenario unfolding. He was not strong enough to defeat God, let alone anything more than a few angels… and Talena. If he tried to fight they would kill her, he couldn’t have that on his head. He would have to beg his way out of this. “Wake up, Drazail.” How long had he been out? He opened his eyes slowly and the light flooded into his retinas. He was in that familiar throne room. The walls and pillars were made of pure gold, the floor was inlayed with gold and paved in pearl and ivory. It was the throne room of God himself. HE sat facing DarkStar. As DarkStar tried to struggle free he realized he was being held down by three of those burly bouncer type angels. Talena was being restrained but by the more traditional type, DarkStar recognized them as a few of the choir that played their harps while the gates opened. He wanted to recruit them to his cause but knew it would be fruitless. “I told you to never set foot in my kingdom again, dreamer.” He was still feeling the effects of the beating, one angel was wrenching his shoulder hard but he ignored it. “It is of utmost importance that I came. I did not foresee being provoked by your son before stepping more than five steps through the gates of your kingdom. I guess this is the courtesy you offer your guests.” God bellowed with laughter. The halls would have shook if physics actually had a reason to exist in Heaven. It was not the joyful laughter of the angels who existed in the kingdom, but a hateful laugh. It cut deep into DarkStar. “You are no guest, you are nothing more than an intruder. It is sad to see a once proud king fall to the level of a common begger. I grow more used to it everyday, more and more of you weak false Gods disappear and become forgotten relics. Some come to me and beg for help, is this no different?” DarkStar struggled, he hated the pompous air of this lucky deity. “I came to ask for help, I will never beg to you. You need me and you know it. Where would heaven be without the mortals ability to dream of entrance into it’s gates? Where would heaven be without mortals nightmares of the horrors of hell? Nowhere. You would have no power if it were not for my realm, and now it decays. I need human form and only you are powerful enough to grant me that.” God stared at DarkStar, or more specifically into DarkStar, and ran his fingers through his beard as if he was contemplating the situation. After what felt like an eternity, he spoke slow and deliberately. “No. You will get no favor from me.” DarkStar thrashed against his captors, “Without me the Dreaming will fall! It is crumbling at it’s foundation even now! Would you watch it’s destruction?” “It will not fall, you pathetic creature. The dream world will not crumble. You are weak and when you are gone I will take over your former kingdom. With the power of the dreaming in my grasp there will be nothing stopping me from destroying the rest of your kind. I will inherit the powers of all and become the only true God.” DarkStar screamed, “You bastard! This has been your plan all along! You act bemused about your alleged creations on Earth but you barter the souls of the Gods in the afterlife against each other in order to become more powerful! You worthless pig dog, you are a disgrace to us all!” One of those holding DarkStar released his arm, presumably to slam his fist into the back of DarkStar’s head. He took the opportunity and maneuvered free of the ones holding him down. He ran at God full speed, screaming every obscenity that popped into his head. With no weapon he stood no chance, but he would try to wrench the head off of his shoulders with only the bare hands at his disposal. He would never have the chance as he was tackled from behind. He and his tackler slid to a stop mere feet in front of God’s throne. “You even try to attack me? Fool. If the rest could not stop me, how could you? A weak and useless God you are now. Consider it a favor that I destroy you and will return your realm to glory.” The angels pinned DarkStar down again, but his anger nearly broke him free. “I will tell them all! I will tell Zeus and Odin! We will unite what is left of our powers and attack this Day-Glo world you’ve vomited out! Then when your power is nearly gone and you will be begging to me for salvation I will tell the world your secrets and while they lose their belief in you I will relish your slow and pitiable death!” God stood now. He was an immeasurable height, at times he would seem to be six foot, and others he would be the size of mountains. These were his laws. “You speak of pitiable death as if you’ve seen it? I will show you pitiable. I will teach you to be disrespectful to me.” God waved his hand, and suddenly DarkStar heard a woman’s screams. He turned his head as much as he could and saw the source. Talena bled from her eyes. It streamed down her face like crimson tears. She clawed at her eye sockets with her nails in a unexplainable effort to tear the pain from her face. She writhed. “She had seen too much, Drazail. Far too much. We will see what depths her insanity drives her with only her second sight to guide her.” God laughed that disgusting laugh once again. He was proud of what he did. DarkStar’s aura grew, he threw off one of the large angels and ripped the throat out of another. The remaining five still held him tight, but now he was on one knee. It looked like he was being forced to genuflect, but there was no holy reverence in his stance. He knew there was nothing he could do for either him or Talena, so he offered her no words of comfort. Instead he looked directly into God’s eyes. “One day I’ll return here, and when I do I’m going to take your head for what you just did… and just to really piss you off, when I slip my blade in between your ribs and let your blood flow to the ground in streams, I’m going to dedicate your death to Odin… you worthless archaic fuck.” God grabbed DarkStar’s face in his hand, he drew him closer to his face in order to hammer his words home. “You will do no such thing. You are dangerous, even in death. So I will give you worse. You pity the angels for their bliss and you seek revenge on me. It will be hard to pity after I take your memory, and it will be even harder to seek revenge…” A light formed in God’s free hand, DarkStar’s eyes widened as his eyes shifted towards it, “…after I take your voice.” The light shot from God’s hand and down DarkStar’s throat. There was a blast and fire shot from the mouth of the dream lord. The angels attempted to hold him down but he was suddenly too powerful, he forced his way too his feet and shattered the skull of one of his captors with a accidental punch. He screamed in pain and in anger, with hatred and disgust, and sorrow and a hundred other emotions he screamed. Then suddenly they stopped. He fell to the ground. His face was charred and burnt from his nose to his chin… his lips looked as if they had been fused together. Talena crawled to him, feeling her way along the ground. She slumped over his motionless body and would have weaped if she could cry tears. Together their consciousness faded. One of the angels who had held Talena in the beginning of this mess walked towards God and bowed. “What shall we do with them now, lord.” God thought for a second, but decided quickly. “Gabriel, I will grant the ex dream lord what he wished… he wanted to be human again? Well now that I have the keys to his realm, his memories of himself, and the very voice he used to inspire riots and revolutions amongst our kind- he is no danger to me. Grant him his humanity and send him back to that disgusting Earth where he came from.” Gabriel nodded, “Yes my lord. As always a brilliant decision.” The angels unceremoniously picked up what remained of DarkStar and Talena and dragged them towards the exit. Somewhere in the Celtic world, Cerridwen began to weap. She felt in her heart that her prophecy had come true, and she knew that the Lord of Dreams had passed from these realms and that what would come next did not bode well for anyone. The blood of old Gods would soon flow. These would be black days ahead.
