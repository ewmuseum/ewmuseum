17 March 2005 "An Old Debt" Character Name: Blind Rage Federation: UWF Old Friends “We should call in the favour from Jerome, uh, Blind Rage.” Mathias said, passing his joint over to his friend Johnson. “For this?” Johnson asked. “It’s worth it.” Mathias replied. They sat in a basement-like room, decked out as a kind or make-shift apartment room. Mathias and Johnson sat upon matching, worn, dark blue couches, watching Television, smoking some pot and drinking some beer. This was the high point of their lives. They were crappy gangsters. “But seriously…” Johnson began. “Look,” Mathias began. “This is the perfect opportunity to get him to help us and this move will put us on the map…” “But what if we need him further down the line, to bust us out of prison or something’…?” Mathias dismissed the comments with his hand. “Look man, you really think Jerome is going to do something illegal for us?” “But he owes us!” “Yeah, but he’s straight now. He ain’t gonna jeopardise that by helping us…” Johnson nodded in response. “Got his number?” Mathias thought about it for a while. His drug-addled brain not working as quickly as perhaps it should. “No.” “Well that’s fucked that then…” Johnson muttered under his breath. Mathias slapped his friends arm. “Shush…their commercial is just about to come on, we’ll get the number off that!” [The ad opens up with the rather sizeable, bright, orange letters ‘Justice Incorporated’, closely followed by their popular (at least to themselves) slogan ‘Justice…for a price’ in fancy joined-up lettering, all of which on the backdrop of a super-imposed ten dollar bill.] Cheesy voice over guy: Justice Incorporated. Justice…for a price! [The ad then cuts to our two loveable superheroes in what appears to be the offices of their headquarters. Warrior is half-sat on a desk and Blind Rage is stood right beside him.] Blind Rage: I’m Blind Rage… Warrior: I’m Warrior… Together: We’re…JUSTICE INC!!!!! [The ad cuts to Blind Rage walking in some undisclosed park on a glorious day.] Blind Rage: You’re having squirrel problems… [The ad cuts to Warrior in the same park kicking a red-furred squirrel up the arse. After doing so he turns to the camera, raises a thumb and produces and incredibly cheesy smile. The ad then cuts to the docks were we see Warrior walking along a pier on a dull and dreary day] Warrior: Or if you’re having pirate problems… [The ad cuts Blind Rage whacking a pirate around the back of the head with a plank of wood. After doing so he looks at the camera, raises a thumb and produces an equally cheesy smile. The ad cuts back to the Justice Inc office. Where we see Blind Rage and Warrior in exactly the same positions as we saw them in before.] Together: Then call Justice Inc TODAY!!!! [A phone numbers appears on screen.] Together: On 1-800-Justice [The ad cuts to, well, another ad] Mathias and Johnson stared at each other for a couple of seconds, before Mathias picked up the receiver on the phone. “When did Jerome turn gay?” Johnson asked. Mathias simply shrugged in reply. Leaving “What do you mean, you’re leaving and you may not be coming back?” Warrior asked, extremely animated indeed. “Who the fuck do you think you are? Scott of the Antarctic?” Blind Rage sighed “Where the hell are you going?” Warrior asked. “I’ve got to pay an old debt…” Blind Rage explained. “Just send a cheque…” Warrior replied. “Not that kind of debt.” “There are other kinds of debt?” Blind Rage sighed…again. “Yes. I owe someone a favour.” “What do they want you to do for them? Use your superhuman strength to help unscrew the lids off their jaws or something?” “They didn’t say.” So let me get this straight,” Warrior began. “They want you to do a favour for them, but they didn’t tell you what it is?” Blind Rage nodded. “That’s about the size of it.” “That’s a big debt you got there…” “Indeed.” “Maybe I should come with you,” Warrior said, “you might need the help…” Blind Rage shook his head. “No. I’m not dragging you into this…” “Why?” Warrior asked. “Last week you dragged me into a shopping mall full of blood thirsty pirates…why is this so different?” “It’s complicated.” “I’m coming with you Rage!” Blind Rage sighed. “Alright. Come on.” Back in the Hood Mathias opened the door to his basement apartment and was propositioned by the rather sizeable image of Blind Rage standing in the door frame. Naturally, his eyes widened and he took a step back. “Damn Jerome,” He said. “You’re getting bigger every day!” Blind Rage grumbled something incoherently and entered the establishment. Warrior was in tears with laughter. “Your real name is Jerome?” Blind Rage sighed. “Shush!” It was at that point that Mathias first caught glimpse of Warrior, who entered the apartment right behind his partner in…uh…goodness. “Johnson,” He yelled. “We got a damn cracker here!” With that Johnson stormed out the bathroom, pulling his pants up as he ran, and grabbed his gun from the coffee table. He ran over to Warrior and jammed it in his face. “Rage…?” Warrior asked, fear spread across his face. “Be cool Mathias,” Blind Rage said. “He’s with me.” “But he’s a damned cracker,” Mathias replied. “With long hair!” “Hey!” Warrior said, the fear completely evaporating. “There’s nothing wrong with my hair!” “Of course not, Fabio.” Johnson added. Warrior shot him a dirty look. “One more word hommie and I’ll cram that gun up your ass!” Johnson turned to Mathias, wanting orders to shoot, but Mathias shook his head. “At least we know which one of you is the bitch now…” Johnson said, lowering the gun. Mathias patted Blind Rage on the back. “Damn, Jerome,” he said. “When did you turn gay?” Blind Rage developed a confused expression. “Gay…?” Warrior’s eyes widened. “Bitch…?” “We saw the ad,” Mathias continued. “We just assumed…” “We thought you were a couple of handbag warriors…” Johnson added. Warrior tapped the gun away from Johnson’s hand and kicked him in the balls. Johnson slumped to the floor in agony. “I’m nobody’s bitch,” He said. “If anything, he’s my bitch.” Blind Rage slapped Warrior around the back of the head, but again forgetting his own strength knocks him half way across the room, stopped only by the wall. “I’m not gay.” Mathias Held up his hands defensively. “Whatever floats ya boat, man!” Blind Rage growled a little, making Johnson and Mathias a little more on edge. “So what is this favour?” Mathias smiled. “Come sit down,” He said. “I’ll tell ya all about it…” TO BE CONTINUED… ---- 20 March 2005 "An Old Debt" Part Two... Wrestler Name: Blind Rage Fed: UWF The Discussion “Well, huh, that sounds reasonable…” Warrior said, with no real conviction. Blind Rage rolled his eyes around in his head and stood from the rather too comfortable dark blue couch. “No it isn’t…” Mathias sighed. “Listen Jerome, we’re not exactly asking you to do anything illegal here!” “I know,” Blind Rage admitted, “but it still feels wrong.” “In fact you’re doing something to clean up the neighbourhood,” Johnson added. “And if it’s well publicised, it could help the business…” Warrior said. “Look,” Mathias began, “you know you owe us a favour. Rather than do something illegal we thought we’d ask you to do something for us that you do for a livin’ anyhow…” “So you want me to go and foil Terry T and his gang, huh?” Blind Rage asked. Mathias nodded. “He’s had it comin’ for a while now…” “And I take it you guys will be there to just step right in and take over their territory…?” Blind Rage asked. “Of course,” Mathias replied. “To be honest with you, Rage,” Warrior said. “I don’t see much of a problem here…” Blind Rage sighed. “Warrior, for a superhero your morals are questionable at best…” “Bollocks!” Warrior dismissed. “So will you do it?” Mathias asked him. Blind Rage grabbed Warrior by the collar of his neck and dragged him toward the door. “Yes,” Blind Rage said, not looking back. “Yes?” Warrior asked. “Then why can’t I stay here and hang with these guys while you go and get the job done?” “Because,” Blind Rage began, “you’re going to help me.” “Bollocks!” Warrior cursed, being dragged out of the basement room. Johnson and Mathias’ beady eyes watched our two heroes leave eagerly. When they were sure that Blind Rage and Warrior were gone, Mathias picked up the phone and dialled a well-memorised number. “Yeah, it’s Mathias. He’s headin’ right for ya now!” Mid-Story Interval Blind Rage knew these streets like the back of his hand, but he hadn’t been around his old neighbourhood for at least seven years. However, he knew exactly where he was going and precisely which roads he would need to take …and the roads that he would need to avoid at all costs. Especially, with Warrior in the van. He could start a fight in the Vatican. “Oh by the way,” Warrior said. “I’ve sighed to you up for a PTC extreme title shot Battle Royale.” “WHAT?” Blind Rage replied, nearly driving the van off the road. “Don’t worry ‘bout it Rage…you’ll do fine.” “You can’t just spring these things on me!” Blind Rage shouted. “Why the hell did you do it anyhow?” “Because it‘s an PTC event. An Interfed event. It will gain us more credibility. More credibility equals more money. More money equals prostitutes…” “…prostitutes equals the Clap.” Blind Rage finished off. “Exactly!” Warrior exclaimed. “Just think of it as a challenge. You get to face off against some of the greatest wrestlers within the Primetime Central, just for the chance of a title shot against the current PTC Extreme Title holder, Shadow…” Blind Rage sighed. “And I don’t suppose I’d be able to get out of this…?” Warrior shook his head. “Nope,” he said, “I put your name down and they accepted you…” “Jesus H. Christ,” Blind Rage cursed. “You’ve really gotta stop doing these things…” “What?” Warrior asked, innocently. “Signing me up to shit without asking my permission…” “Oh, don’t be such a girl…” Blind Rage sighed. “Who the hell is in this thing anyhow?” Warrior fished into the right-hand side pocket of his orange and black tights and pulled out a piece of paper. “Erm, the thirteen confirmed entrants for the PTC Extreme Title Battle Royale are, Shaman…Chris Storm…James von Doom…Kyo Kushinaga…Anthony Palmer…Peter Vetra…Tony Stecchino…Peter Maxwell…Kris CarMicheal…Nova…Kris Krimzon…Wesley Williams and Lyger…” “Er, hang on a second,” Blind Rage began. “You said there were thirteen confirmed wrestlers in this thing…” “Yeah,” Warrior said, “and…?” “You just gave me thirteen names, not including my own.” “And…?” “Well,” Blind Rage began. “If you add my name to the list there would be fourteen names…” Warrior’s face scrunched up in a distinctly confused expression and counted the number of names on the list. “There are thirteen names on here…” “It must just be a thirteen man battle royale, with fourteen men, huh?” Blind Rage asked. “I guess…” “Weird.” “You should be okay thought, Rage.” “How come?” “Who the hell’s gonna be able to tip your fat ass over the top ropes…?” Blind Rage grumbled incoherently and put his foot down on the accelerator. The Double Cross Two Days Earlier… Johnson and Mathias stepped out of the old, rust-ridden, once gold, Lincoln they’d parked haphazardly against the sidewalk, near the entrance way to a local neighbourhood park. They both cautiously made their way into the park, not even trying to hide their low-grade, low-tech, but equally effective Uzi’s. “This is really fucking creepy, bro…” Johnson muttered, following his friend Mathias into the extremely darkened park. “I know what you mean,” Mathias replied, “we should be dead by now.” Johnson glanced at Mathias wide-eyed with fear. “What?” “If this was park was occupied with its usual crowd, then we would be dead…” Johnson stared at him bewildered. “And you still made us come?” Mathias sighed. “Stop being such a pussy.” “A pussy?” Johnson asked in disbelief. “Your going to get us killed be a rival fucking gang…” “He said he’d clear the park,” Mathias said. “And look around. Do you hear any gun fire? You’re still alive aren’t you…?” Johnson nodded his head in response, and muttered something under his breath about not trusting lunatics. They reach the rendezvous point, a large tree located in the north-east section of the park, without any hassle at all. For that reason alone they knew they were in the right place. “What…huh…what do we do to signal his attention…?” Johnson asked. Mathias shrugged. “You already have,” The voice rasped from high in the tree, Mathias and Johnson couldn’t quite pinpoint the location. They were hoods, not Tarzan. “I heard you coming a mile a way…” “Who are you…?” Johnson asked, feeling a little strange talking to, well, a tree. “Pardon?” The voice boomed. “I can’t quite hear you. A damn sparrow is giving me problems…huh…hang on a second…” Before either Mathias or Johnson could speak, one of the top branches began to shake uncontrollably. Feather and leaves were everywhere, which was soon to be followed by “Whooooooaaaaaaaa!” and a fat geek in a squirrel outfit falling fifteen feet to the ground. He landed with a painful thud on the stiff grass. “Bollocks!” the creepy little fat geek cursed. However, when he remembered precisely why he was in the park in the first place, he painfully scrambled back to his feet and struck a pose. Basically, legs apart and both hands on his hips. Mathias and Johnson simply stared at each other, not wanting to believe what was going on. “I am THE SQUIRREL MASTER~!” The guy in the squirrel outfit exclaimed. “And I believe I can be of some service to you…” “How…?” Mathias asked. “You want power and I want to kill Justice Incorporated,” The Squirrel Master began, “I believe we can come to some kind of arrangement…” Mathias and Johnson smiled at each other and continued to listen to what the madman had to say. The Ambush Sprung Now… The clapped out, old, orange VW camper van pulled up outside of their destination, making as much noise as possible under the circumstances. Stealth wasn’t going to be an issue in this job. Blind Rage and Warrior, slid out of the van and made their way to the entrance way to the warehouse. “You sure this is the best way, Rage?” Warrior asked, showing a little concern, even through his mask. “The direct approach has worked well in the past…” Blind Rage replied. “Yeah, but that was against pirates or giant six foot squirrels,” Warrior said. “These guys probably have guns!” “Which is why you’ll be going to the roof to provide me with a little cover…” Warrior sighed. “But what if there’s nothing to take cover behind once you get in there? You’ll be shot!” Blind Rage patted Warrior on the shoulder. “Don’t worry about it…” Warrior sighed. “Right then.” “Now get your ass to the roof and wait for the signal!” Warrior cursed under his breath and entered the alley way next to the warehouse to look for some kind of way to get the roof. Blind Rage stood in front of the door, rolled his head around a few times and did some quick stretches with his shoulders. He then took a step backwards and launched his foot haphazardly at the rather sizeable doors. Wood and bolts flew everywhere. Immediately after doing so, he hid behind the outer wall, expecting a flurry of Uzi fire. However, nothing came. Surprised, he popped his head around the door to see what was going on, but he couldn’t see anything. At all. It was pitch black in the warehouse, no lights on at all and he got the distinct feeling that the lights hadn‘t been on for quite some time. Blind Rage sighed and entered the establishment. At first he tried to look around for some kind of light switch, but his attention was effectively grabbed by a kind of musseled whimpering sound. “Huh, Terry T…” Blind Rage softly muttered. “I’ve come to beat you up…” It was at that point the lights were switched on and it was at that point that Blind Rage realised that he’d walked into a trap. Terry T and his gang were all tied and gagged in the exact centre of the warehouse, just a couple of feet away from Blind Rage and they were surrounded by four giant squirrels. Two red and two grey, if anyone was wondering. “Well, well, Blind Rage,” The familiar rasping voice, with geeky undertones, began, “we meet again.” Blind Rage glanced up at the raised platform to his left and saw the liberally labelled ‘man’ standing against a weak-looking wooden barrier. Blind Rage sighed. “Oh great,” he said, “it’s you.” “YES!” The Squirrel Master exclaimed. “IT IS I THE…oh…shit…whoa…whoooooaaaaaa…” Predictably, the Squirrel Master’s overwhelming weight against the weak wooden barrier was just too much for it to take and so it snapped in two, propelling the Squirrel Master through it and down at least ten feet. He was okay though, much to Blind Rage’s annoyance, he landed on his ample-sized ass. Blind Rage did a double-take when he heard one of the large grey squirrels tutt in frustration and was then forced to attempt hold back a laugh. “You really aren’t that good with heights, are ya?” Blind Rage asked, letting slip a giggle midway through the sentence. The Squirrel Master scrambled to his feet. “I’ll have you know I have super-human agility…” “Bollocks!” Blind Rage exclaimed. “It’s fucking true I tell ya…” Blind Rage sighed. “Well prove it then.” “Er…prove it?” The Squirrel Master asked. “Yeah, do a couple of back flips or something…” “I would,” The Squirrel Master said, “but I’m afraid I’ve not got the right kind of shoes on…” Blind Rage crossed his arms and stared at one of his arch-enemies for a while. However, the Squirrel Master simply ignored Blind Rage’s inquisitive stare and decided to change the subject instead. “I’m assuming that you’re little friend Warrior will be joining us soon…I’d like to begin our festivities…” Blind Rage smiled. “He was watching this whole thing from the sky light in the roof,” he said. “I’m sure he’s managed to call the fuzz…I mean police…by now.” Then Warrior entered through the door, his head down. “First of all, there’s a serious flaw in your plan. How the hell am I supposed to come through the sky light? It’s at least a fifty-foot drop?” Warrior then raised his head and saw the tied up gangsters, the squirrels and the Squirrel Master. “Oh fuckin’ hell not these clowns again…” “Did you call the police?” Blind Rage asked him. “Oh I‘m afraid my telepathic powers are knackered out today due an extremely how curry I had last night so I wasn‘t actually aware that these buffoons were about,” Warrior explained. “Besides, how the hell would I do that? Smoke signal? Morse Code? I don‘t have a mobile phone.” “Ah it seems that police won’t be joining our little party after all then…” The Squirrel Master said. Warrior turned to Blind Rage. “Did he fall off something yet?” Blind Rage nodded and pointed up to the raised platform to his left with broken wooden barrier. “Off that.” Warrior nodded in response. “Ah.” “Enough of this idle chit chat!” The Squirrel Master screamed. “Now you die!” “And precisely how do you intend to kill us?” Blind Rage asked. The Squirrel Master smiled and produced an Uzi, his four giant squirrels did the same. “With these guns…” Blind Rage and Warrior looked at each other and both cursed. “Bollocks!” “Not so fast Squirrel Master!” The voice shouted from the door way. Everyone glanced back to see Johnson and Mathias stood at the doorway, with three other huge African-American gentlemen, all of whom carried some sort of firearm. It was a mixture of Uzi’s and handguns. The Squirrel Master seemed a little bemused by their sudden appearance (if you can believe that). “What are you two fools doing here?” Mathias stepped forward from the crowd. “Well, we’re here to take our power…” The Squirrel Master chuckled geekishly. “Nice one guys…you want a piece of them too?” “We’re going to take a piece of everything…” It was at that point that Johnson and his Crew opened fire. On Terry T and his gang, The Squirrel Master and his giant squirrels and, of course, our two hapless heroes. However, Blind Rage reacted quickly, managing to grab Warrior and get them both out of the way. The noise of the gun fire was deafening, but Johnson and Mathias seemed to focus primarily on the giant squirrels. By now, Terry T and his gang had been exterminated and all but one giant squirrel had been blown away. The Squirrel Master made a run for the exit at the back. “Come on Cecil! Let’s get the hell out of here!” However, only Johnson and Mathias are left standing out of their crew. They watched the Squirrel Master and the sole remaining giant squirrel get away before making their way over to Blind Rage and Warrior. Johnson ran his hand through his badly died hair. It was bland blonde colour that didn’t go all the way and looked completely stupid on him. “Get up!” Warrior and Blind Rage did as they were told and stood. Blind Rage towered above them all. “I’m sorry I have to do this Jerome…” Mathias said, aiming the gun at Blind Rage’s head. Johnson did the same with Warrior. “Any last words, dickhead?” “Yeah,” Warrior said, pointing at the guys hair, “get your roots done you look like a badger!” “Right that’s it!” Johnson exclaimed, Warrior closed his eyes in anticipation. “Hold on a minute gentlemen,” Blind Rage said. “During the fire fight I tapped a small panic button that is built into our Justice Inc uniforms,” Warrior glanced at him completely confused, “the police are already here!” Johnson and Mathias looked at each other. “WHAT…?” They both questioned in unison. Blind Rage looked into the distance, right over the shoulders of Johnson and Mathias. “Come and get ‘em lads!” Johnson and Mathias glanced over their shoulders at the same time, whilst Blind Rage nodded at Warrior. Warrior then knocked the gun out of Johnson’s hand and kicked him in the bollocks, Johnson curled over in foetal position blubbering like a girl. At the same time, Blind Rage crushed the gun in Mathias’ hand (and his hand actually), grabbed him by the collar and hurled him across the room. The impact he had with the opposite wall was sickening in an extremely gratifying way. “You know, Rage,” Warrior said, giving Johnson a sly kick in the guts for good measure, whilst following his friend to the exit, “you could this is very much like what you’ll have to face in that Battle Royale of yours…” Blind Rage glared at his friend, confused. “What their will be two bad ass gangs, a fat geek in a squirrel costume and four giant squirrel’s with Uzi’s…?” “Could be,” Warrior said. “These extreme matches tend to get a little out of control…” Blind Rage stopped in his tracks. “Or do you mean that I’ll have enemies on either side of me in this battle royale and there will be no friends anymore and anyone of them could stab me in the back…” Warrior glared at him, his eyes completely glazed over. It took a couple of seconds for him to realise Blind Rage had actually stopped talking. “Yeah, right…that’s EXACTLY what I meant…” Blind Rage smiled and tapped his friend on the back. His strength propelling him to the floor with a thud. Warrior picked himself up from the floor and brushed himself down with his hands. “Will you quit doing that,” he said, catching up with his tag team partner, nearly slipped on some dark red squirrel blood. “So, uh, when did we get this special buttons in our suites that call the police then?” Blind Rage stopped in his tracks and buried his head into his hands. “Can you SEE any police men…?” End
