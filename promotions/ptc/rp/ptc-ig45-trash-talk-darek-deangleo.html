IG45 Trash Talk Open Invitation thread (all vs Chris Storm)

2 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2 June 2008 The room is filled with egotisitical comments and remarks being slung around like wild gunmen from the West. Each person brings up a good point about the Infinite Guantlet, be it in person or through a video sent into the PrimeTime Central offices. A large cluster fuck of people who have achieved all that they can achieve in the PTC and even unknowns flock to this gathering, all waiting their respective turn to get one cheap shot at Chris Storm. That name again: Chris Fucking Storm. The same man who dominated the PTC in 2004, the same man who actually found his future wife in this business. A man who has more talent in his left index finger than ninety percent of the people blasting him. Yet, still smug and confident, Storm knows full well that despite this being four years later he can still draw the same crowd as before. Almost as if a case of Deja Vu had set in, the Ominous One stands ever vigilant in the far corner of the room. His arms are folded across his chest, his pale blue eyes bright enough to light the entire room. A slightly tilted smirk etched upon his face, the monstrous 6'10" framed man steps out from his isolation and surveys the room with a quick glance around. Instincively, his arms unfold from his chest and find comfort resting squarely on his hips. "Chris Storm has once again risen to the occasion to become a name worth mentioning in the PrimeTime. An accomplishment that so many wish to bestow upon themselves that they are willing to go out of their way to make themselves look like iconclastic infedels." Darek sighs before looking directly into the direction of the new IG Champion. "Christopher, you and I have never met, we have never stood toe to toe; you and I, we have never had the honor of being each others victim. You see back in 2004, I took Seymour Almasy and your lovely fiancee Tessa Windsor to the limits in a match for the ages. Now, again, I rise from the ashes, not to belittle you; but to inform you that not everything is as it seems." The Dark Angel, at one time a dominant specimen in the industry; titls his head to the side and smiles frightneningly at Seymour Almasy. "Speaking of crawling from the wood-works. 'The Final Fantasy' Seymour Almasy has decided to grace us with his awe inspiring, majestry." A slight chuckle of amusement rises into the relatively quiet, but tense air. "Almasy, where the hell have you been all these years? In the past, on two instances you may have gotten the better of me, but that will never happen again; not now, not ever. You see, between yourself and Storm , you two are some of the most decorated Superstars in the PTC however, that doesn't impress me. You've done just about as much as I have in recent memory..." The largest DeAngleo of the family trails off for just a brief moment. "...Nothing." Walking over to the far wall, Darek pauses to run his finger along the window ledge before unlatching the clasps keeping the transparant glass closed in place. With a firm shove, the window opens up and a cold, eerie breeze circulates through the large room. The wind is enough to make several strands of Darek's dark hair, flying through the air like several kites on a perfect day. "In 2008, the PTC has become a shadow of it's former self, but the real story is: The same faces keep accomplishing the same things. Perhaps it's time for someone else to cement a legacy in the PTC, one that will forever linger when the community is gone. One that will stand to symbolize everything I have fought so dilligantly for in my career." Leaning back against the brick wall, Darek continues on. "The time for the PTC to be cleansed of it's sins has come and that time is now." ---- 2 June 2008 "This has degenerated into nothing more than chimpanzees chucking shit at each other. Stupid me, I thought this was a room full of talented Superstars but apparently I'm sadly mistaken. We have the Infinite Guantlet Champion: Chris Storm, doing everything in his human power to absolve the harsh words shot his way from bitter, jealous people." The Dark Angel, Darek DeAngleo pushes himself from the brick wall in the far left corner of the room. Upon further inspection, he looks increasingly aggitated with the commotion before him. If he thought he was going to leave his home in Calgary just to see these animals in this zoo, he would have visited the world's famous zoo in his home town. Years have passed since the Modern Messiah stepped into the PTC to do any sort of battle; be it verbal or physical. A chill rolls down his spine as he contemplates the sheer madness at hand. The last time he had been involved in a shoutting contest like this, he ended up being beaten: twice! In a three way exhibition with the woman of the hour: Tessa Windsor and the other opponent, none other than the most decorated former PTC champion in history: Seymour Almasy. The other match was a another brawl with Almasy and both are matches that Darek relives in his mind every day, over and over. "When I last stepped foot in these parts of the PrimeTime Central, it was more encouraging for people to put up or shut up. I said some things, I went off the handle like many of you are..." he pauses as he reflects on his past. "The difference between me and you animals is simple really; I fought, I backed up my words and while I may have lost the match, I kept my pride and I'm damned sure I earned their respect." Darek glances at Tessa Windsor then Seymour Almasy. "Albeit, we don't really agree on anything, but you I'm sure you can agree with me when I say, at least we put up a show for our words. We fought until the ring was stained red, we waged war until only one of us could stand and in the end, when the dust settled... We were both still alive." Darek marches into the center of the room. He shoots his arms out to the horizons and tilts his head back ever so slightly. "So what's stopping you people from doing the same? Are scarred that your challenge may actually be answered? Why wait for Chris Storm, it seems many of you have unsettled business with each other. I'm not here to blast anyone, I'm just going to sit tightly and watch this ball of yarn unravel." He places his arms neatly across his chest and shoots a look of intensity through the room. "I've played this game, I know what can come from it. Instead of a blatant disdain for these people and their true character, I will let my accomplishments speak for themselves: Competitor on the 4ww Survival Team Competitor in GTT4 that was ultimately defeated by one 'Final Fantasy' The ony Two Time 4ww North Wind Champion The longest ever reigning South Wind Champion, a title defended every week! A two time East Wind Champion The only Projekt Xero Interfederation Champion A multiple federation Hall of Famer There are so many more. I don't need to say such harsh things in regards to Chris Storm or even Seymour Almasy, I knw that when they sit back and look at the people in this room, they at least respect what I bring to the table. And if I'm mistaken and they don't, then perhaps it's time for me to dust off the boots one last time. I'll let my actions speak louder than my words and when the time is right, the Dark Angel will reign a top the PTC - For the first time. Watch and learn children, school is in session." ---- 4 June 2008 Seymour Almasy, the Final Fantasy, a man that many people have come to know as the former face of the PrimeTime Central. A man who normally backs up his heated words with more heated combat. A former multi-time PTC champion, he knows all too well the aspects of what it takes to be the best in this business. How people can sit here and blatantly disregard these achievements is beyond contemplation. Darek DeAngleo knew this man relatively well, he was a villian in person and a monster in the ring. Darek would never deny these facts; he respected Almasy, despite hating the man more than sin itself. He sat back, his large frame leaning against the cool brick wall and allowed his mind to wonder as the recorded footage from Krissy Jasmen and Chuck Ramone plays for the crowded audience. How about James von Doom? This man, if you could refer to him as that, was perhaps the most unusual of all the people in this cascade of stars. Here was a person that could say just about anything and make almost anyone but the target of his mockery laugh; but that didn't impress Darek, he'd never even seen von Doom compete in a ring. He'd only ever heard him run his mouth. But given the opportunity, Darek believed that James von Doom would die defending his words and his beliefs; a noble but vain cause. Smiling as the tape plays, Darek can't help be amused by what he sees and hears on the LCD television at the opposite end of the room. A comedic duo for sure he felt as he watched on, but surely not on the level as the other individuals that they blasphemise. However, who was he to make that judgement in the first place? He knew full well that he couldn't draw a conclusion of their ability sheerly by the imbecilic words they spew from a far distance. Cowards, is all he could think. Crossing his arms neatly in front of him, he just watches as the crowd can't help but laugh at the absurd comments made from afar by two women that Darek knew, many of these stars felt they could destroy in one fell swoop. Just where in the sam-hell did they come from anyway? In all of his time in the PTC, Darek had never encountered a duo quite like this... one that tried so hard to make it known that they loved pet beavers. While the thought sent a shill down Darek's spine, he knew it was a sad attempt to get their names out there... and from he had seen, it was working. Another loud rise comes from the room as the tape ends and James von Doom is more than happy to provide comedic relief of his own when he creates a huge poster displaying the acting extent of the lesbian duo. This brings about another smile on the face of the Dark Angel who finally sees the opportunity to speak. "It's really something how once you mention 'open' and 'trash talk' in the same sentence you can really draw out some of the most unbelievable people. People who hope that they can use this as an opportunity to leech off the sucess of others. People like these girls who won't even show their face." Darek sighs before stepping out of the shadowy confines of the darkened corner. "At least von Doom is here, he may say some stupid shit from time to time but he's here and he's ready for anyone to challenge him. If I were a betting man, I'd bet my life's savings that if I said: 'von Doom, you and me, right now', we'd be finding a fed that would willing to hold the chaos that would surely ensue; even if for one night." He stops a few feet short of Seymour Almasy and just points to him with his right arm. "This man, I know will fight. I was where those girls are (not as a lesbian) but as the leecher. I came to the PTC from 4ww and immediately tried to make a name for myself. I chose Tessa and I chose Seymour and they accepted. Not like today, blasphemy after blasphemy, but no real challenge." Darek walks around to come face to face with the Final Fantasy, "you know I don't like you and I know you don't like me, but I'm sure we can both agree that we miss the simpler days when these issues were settled in the ring, like wrestlers. You and I went through hell, twice. We nearly killed each other and even Tessa in the process, but we fought for what we said and what we believed in." Surely not wanting to be a hypocrite, after a short pause, Darek continues. "With that said, I'm going to shut up and put up. Chris Storm, JA, James von Doom, Tessa Windsor, the beaver loving bitches; I don't care who, if any of you feel the itch to actually stand up for what you have to say, you know where to find me... and the answer is always an astounding: Yes." ---- 5 June 2008 "Jesse Jamester!" The proclamation is made loud and from the familiar corner that the Dark Angel has grown accustomed to. "You and I have something in common, we both beat Patti Anderson to win a title, big fucking deal. You happened to be lucky enough for yours to be the PTC Championship..." A deep, overwhelming sigh follows. "While mine was my first reign as the 4ww North Wind Champion. That's an old accomplishment, but one we both still hold fondly. You were in the right place at the right time and I, well, I was only lucky enough to dethrone her tyrnaical legacy in 4ww." Stepping away from the cold brick wall and into full surroundings of the stars he been lecturing for the last several days. The PTC was more his home now than any federation and he felt the need and desire to stand up for what he believed the PTC stood for: Pride, Honor and Ability. He wanted nothing more than for people to settle their issues once and for all. With Chris Storm becoming the Infinite Guantlet Champion, a barrage of verbal disdain followed in his way. Despite Darek not liking Storm, he respected the man and hoped that a mutual bond of respect would put him into the front running of a possible opportunity to put on the greatest match in PrimeTime future history. Out of the wood works would come former PTC Champions such as Seymour Almasy and Jesse Jamester, jokers like James von Doom and jokes like 'Chuck' Ramone. "Chuck Ramone, you and I really don't see eye to eye on anything; that doesn't surprise me at all. You feel the need to voice, repeatedly your taste in sexuality. Does that make you think you are going to be a more talented fighter? The fact that you prefer to support 'bush' doesn't have any bearing on your ability in the ring. Stop pretending that any of us care that you like women and start hoping that you can hack it in the ring. The PTC isn't your fed's playground, it's a whole new yard. There are bigger dogs, dogs that have waited longer..." His rant ceases for a moment while he takes in a deep breath to calm his emotion. "There are those of us who just want it that much more. To prove my point, I'd love to take you to school and show you exactly what you need to learn to have a chance in the PrimeTime Central." Darek looks at the camera man for a moment and then stares deeply at the lens. "Ramone, if you are afraid, I'm sure we could even make this a tag match. Despite never wanting to say this, I think James von Doom and I should teach you a lesson in humility. You will be humbled by two people who've never amounted to anything in the PTC but desire it so much more than you do." The thought crosses Darek's mind that this whole scene was created for one reason, to challenge Chris Storm. "Storm, I hope you're listening. When I get done with Chucky, you and I are going to have to decide exactly who here deserves a shot at your IG Title. You know deep inside of you that you want to create your legacy in the PTC, why not in style: create a new star, let us wage an epic war that will forever remain in the PTC history books as the greatest IG match of all time. Let me prove myself to you and the world." He smiles slyly. "Again, after I'm done giving the Punk Rock Bitch a lesson she'll never forget." ---- 7 June 2008 "I think Storm should pick me just to piss the rest of you off." ~ Memo from Darek DeAngleo. "P.S.: It would too."
