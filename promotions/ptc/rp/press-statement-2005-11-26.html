Press Statement

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 (the following is an excerpt from an official WWA press statement) "Now to explain my actions regarding Ian Tense. As you may be aware, I defeated Mr Tense for my first WWA Heavyweight Title after he caused severe damage to my face, and since that point, we have not exactly been on the best terms and the WWA management are aware of this, they accept that we will never be the best of friends. The events of the past Meltdown may sound extreme to the casual viewer but if explained thoroughly, I am sure that it will seem less extreme and less aggressive. After his match against Tracer Bullet, which he lost fair and square, Mr Tense approached me in the locker room and was very, well, loud and gruff with me, complaining that I has held him back. In my capacity as road agent, yes it is my job to assist all the talents, but due to personal reasons, I have never tried to interfere with his career, it would not be in the interest of anyone to try to deal with that man. However, after the altercation, I felt it was my duty to act as the peacemaker and I went to the dressing room of Mr Tense to talk to him. He was still suffering from the post loss adrenaline rush and he became more agitated. He grasped at my bags that I carry with me everywhere, and one came loose. Unfortunately, this said bag contained a pet of mine, my rather large and formidable, albeit de-venomised King Cobra, who I have affectionately named "Alice". Alice does not like being disturbed during her sleep and when awoken by an angry wrestler throwing her across the room, she was tetchy to say the least. Her response was to "hood" up and defend herself against the attacker, in this case Mr tense. However, this was not a good thing as Mr Tense suffers from ophidiophobia, which to the layman is a sever fear of snakes. Mr tense, in his panic, started to emit heat and that merely attracted "Alice" to him, his fear acting as a moth to the flame. Now, even though "Alice" has no venom to cause harm, a bite from her could cause serious injury if she masticates on the area in question, is I acted as quick as possible to capture Alice and calm her down, which admittedly does take a few minutes. During this time, Mr Tense was severely agitated and that led to his panic attack that required him to seek medical attention at a local medical centre. I do not hold any malice towards Mr Tense, I do not wish to restrict his career in anyway and I have no intention of following up this incident with anything that could be considered malicious or evil in any sense of the word. I am a man of peace now, I have no desire to fight with anyone, despite what some may believe of me. During my time as an active member of the WWA roster, I was seen as a freak, a loner, one of the weirdest men in the history of the WWA. Yes, I have a penchant for snakes, spiders, rats and all other creatures that people see as disgusting and horrible. Yes, I enjoy the night but as I have explained in the past, I have valid reasons for enjoying these things. I am no weirdo, I am just a simple man with unusual interests, and yes that does include my love of Wicca and the arcane arts. I have shown through my work with the WWA that I love the mystical and the occult, and I am not afraid to educate through my actions. If I have inspired one child to read an actual book on Wicca, then my task is done. Now, you may have seen above, if you are but a casual wrestling fan, that I am not longer a member of the active roster and that is true, I am now "backstage" as a road agent, working behind the curtain to make sure the show goes on. I am backstage for a reason, I can no longer fight safely. During my last match against Havoc, legends of the WWA, I received a severe concussion and for that reason, I can no longer compete day to day within the WWA, and yes that made me sad. One of the things Mr Tense said to me during his rant was that I had removed his application for the GTT5 tournament and replaced it with my own, to further my own vendetta against him. He claimed that I deleted his email application and submitted one of my own in his place, that I would stoop that low just because I hate him. I can categorically state right now that I did not, my application was sent in separately and had nothing to do with Mr Tense, whatever he believes. Mr Tense, this is a personal address to you now. I do not hold any bad feelings towards you and I d not have any reason to want to harm your career. My interests are in making the name of the WWA greatest amongst the other federations in the world. If you think I will hold some sort of personal vendetta against you for some reason, you are wrong, and I am sorely apologetic for the actions of "Alice" and she will now remain at home during my trips. I prayed for you during your time in the hospital and I thanked the Goddess when I heard you were ok. I hope that this now clears up this whole episode, and clears up any problems that we have. I hope that upon our next meeting on WWA grounds, we will get on much better. My name is Kash and Kash is money!
