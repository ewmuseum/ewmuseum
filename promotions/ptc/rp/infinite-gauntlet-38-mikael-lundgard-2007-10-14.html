Infinite Gauntlet 38 - Mikael Lundgard

14 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name? Mikael Lundgard. Occupation? Retired professional wrestler. Previous companies? In America; FUSE, but I’ve also wrestled in my homeland of Denmark, Germany, England, Mexico and Japan. How long were you involved in the wrestling business I still am involved as a road agent in FUSE, but I’ve been wrestling since I was seventeen, so that would be around about ten years. And is your current situation satisfactory? Not completely. You see… I never had that ‘big’ match… That’s something that has bugged me ever since I announced my retirement six months ago. I never stood centre stage at the biggest event in the world and basked in the limelight. Sure, I had my seven days in the sun, I had my universal title reign, I had my triple crown… but I never had ‘that’ match. You know what I’m talking about. The match that solidifies you as a star, the match that makes everyone in the wrestling industry stand up and say “There is a true legend”… I’d like to say that that doesn’t bother me. I’d like to say that I don’t care about the match but I would be lying. I need it. Every time I talk to people about returning to the world of wrestling they all ask the same questions. They all think I need some kind of validation… They all think I’m mad. “Why would you want to return to a world where you are hated?” They ask. And I’ll always answer the same way. I’ll always tell the questioner that I’m just doing my job out there. If you’re working as a waiter in a restaurant and get tipped twenty per cent, it’s your customer’s way of telling you that you’ve done a good job. When it comes to wrestling, if I get a negative reaction from the crowd I know that I have affected them in some way. I’m getting through to them… They pay their hard earned money to show me just how much they hate me… at least that’s what I’d like to think. The truth is that they are booing me because I can say I’m the greatest of all time… but at the end of the day, I’ve never really shown them I can back that up… And that bugs me more than anything in this world I need this match, not only for myself… but for my legacy. The infinite gauntlet will play host to my defining moment.
