Banter of drunkards

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 Croutched over with is arms lain on the bar, the man, only known as Kerry Owens, sits alone at his favorite pub in Dallas. His head, hug low, covered by his long dark red hair and his hands gripped; ever so tightly. Dressed out in pants and a jacket, both denium blue jean, and a pair of brown leather boots; his smashes against the bar, physically demanding a round. The bartender hears the noise made, and responses by pouring a shot of Jack Daniels in a glass for the depressed man. Owens reaches over with his right hand and brings the shot in full graps; fastly tilts his head backwards, while sucking down his shot. Kerry, very slowly, places the shot glass next to numerious other, as he tilts his head back in the previous position. Kerry Owens: Another round. The first words spoken from the tall and shady character, as he makes his announcement for more on going shots. Man: Are you sure? Spoken words from a man behind Kerry, questions his decision, as he leans upon crutches; he is a cripple. The cripple is formal dressed out in a dirty white shirt, underneith a black sports coat, tye dye jeans, and a think cast wrapped on this left leg. Kerry Owens: Yes… Yes, I would. Anything wrong with my choice, Tripp? Reveling the identity of his manager, Tripp Reaper; Kerry turns around and stares eye to eye at the man. The dead and emotionless stare of Owens rivals against the half humored and confused look of Reaper. Tripp Reaper: As my duty for being your manager, I care about your well being. Kerry Owens: My well being? Bah! I do not care, so leave me be. Kerry turns around, back to his sulking position, as earlier; Tripp moves to seating himself beside his managing wrestler. Reaper lays his crutches in the gap of space between him and Owens, as he continues his questioning. Tripp Reaper: Why so tense? You have been doing great in the NWF promotion, and now having a chance to compete against others in other promotions… Wow! Oh… Owens glares his eyes at Reaper, deep in a cold expression. Reaper turns his face to the barkeeper and waves his hand, signaling for a round. Kerry Owens: The world is huge, everyone in this business does not know everyone, I know no not a single being in this upcoming tournement and neither do they know of myself. Tripp Reaper: Besides Eagle, that is true, and you never faced him in the NWF. Reaper takes a sallow of his first glass and slams it down on the counter, as Kerry receives another shot from the barkeeper. Kerry Owens: Not to say I am afraid of any of them, I've taken many famed wrestlers down with ranks high in the NWF and in other promotions. Tournaments are challenging, even harder, when you do not know your oppositions in these upcoming bouts. But challenges are why we are here for, and what we fight for… honor. Tripp and Kerry continue to drink back and forth, only staring into empty space of what is infront of them. Tripp Reaper: So, becoming Tag Team Champion and defeating legendary talent isn't all that you crave? Kerry Owens: You ask that as if you believe others would differ? Ever man, steping into a ring, wants to do it all. They want to win countless of titles, beat the best and have all the bragging rights in the world. Not to say I am nor aren't, but I am only human. So, I must want something beyond gold, beyond some trophy… respect. Tripp Reaper: Respect? Tripp tilts his head, onlooking his management, rubbing the side of his left index against the underlining of his bottom lip. Kerry Owens: I care not to be a part of two promotions at one time, I long only for my current time in the NWF, butthat doesn't mean I want to see whatelse is out there. Three months, I've gone pretty far, but what happens after I've gone too far? Over staying my welcome, by doing all that there is in that promotion? I just want to find out how better or worse it is, outside of NWF, and I don't know nor care of the results. Respect is the only thing that matters, and I give all my respect to those in this tournament. The two men continue their banter, while becoming more intoxicated as the next shot passes down their throats. Barkeeper: Well, that's enough for the both of you. Sorry, but it's closing time. Kerry Owens: Here… Owens pulls out his wallet and pays his tab off, as he makes his stand, and walks out towards the door. Nothing passes his mouth to say to anyone, the barkeeper nor Trpp Reaper. Barkeeper: Your friend has a strong felxiblity, that was some consumtion he took in, even for someone his size. The barkeeper speaks to Tripp Reaper, as Kerry Owens slightly stagers off and exits from the bar.
