Extreme Division Match

17 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Thanks Bondage for the time, sorry about overreacting it's just that I don't like people to fight over a game and since I'm here just for fun I thought that giving up the match would resolve everything. Good luck, well here comes nothing... RP: As the scene fades in we see the number seven on the screen, the screen counts it self down from seven to 2 with a beep following each passing number. After number two we see a pair of blue and white nylon mesh Adidas Rod Lavers on the screen. The camera slowly pans up exposing first a pair of worn blue jeans, then a black "Asesino" shirt, the camera pauses for a moment on the shirt then continues in its upwards journey to reveal The Assassin. The Assassin is leaning against a red lava rock that towers well over his head. He’s eyes shine into the camera, you can tell he’s been thinking, and it appears as if he’s been leaning against the rock for some time. Asesino just stands for a moment before he begins to speak. Asesino: Not as many men as you may think can claim to being a former XWF World Champion. Some because after so many losses they no longer had a desire to be XWF Champion, and some because they simply didn’t have the abilities to win the XWF’s crown jewel. I am one of those men who can proudly say that he has held the XWF World Title, and my opponent this week would probably like to be able to say that as well. Bondage The Sex Fiend wants the Extreme Title, he hasn’t said it with words, but rather with actions. Asesino: You know Bondage I used to get angry when someone tried to challenge me. I didn’t understand why you felt the need to try and challenge me, but that was then, and this is now. Now I laugh when you do such a thing. Everyone knows your a skilled athlete and wrestler, everyone knows you are quite capable of putting up a fight with anyone in this interfed as much as I, but that’s not what you want to do, you don’t want to prove to people that you skilled. You want to . . . come to think of it I don’t know what you want to prove and I’m not sure you do either. Asesino: Bondage although for the past few days you have been my number one priority, there is something else that I have been putting off that I must address, and I guess there is no better time than the present. It seems that I have been booked in an NGW Storm match with a . . . woman. To tell you the truth I’m really not sure which one, Heart Break Chick I think, the name doesn’t really matter. The point is that men have no business wrestling women, period, and to tell you the truth I think that Mr. Scottywood really screwed up with this one. The reason men shouldn’t wrestle women is because men are stronger and tougher by nature, and I don’t want to be responsible for hurting an innocent woman. Instead of attacking this woman verbally right now I’ll simply pose a question. Do you think you can beat Chezina? C.R.? DJ? Canadian Crippler? No? well I’ve beat ‘em all, and that’s just where the list starts. I’m really torn on this one, I’m going to have to do the right thing, whatever that may be. Asesino: It’s times like this that I wish I were Bondage. Then I would make an easy decision and simply beat up this woman for my own sick amusement, something I won't do. All I can promise you is this, I’m not going to let Bondage beat me in the Extreme Division, and I’m certainly not going to let a woman do so either. (Asesino shakes his head and takes a seat in the sand.) Asesino: A woman? I still can’t get over this, I mean come on. Well at least a woman isn’t going to win the Extreme Title, I don’t think they’re letting them compete in that division. Although this division does intrigue me. This division intrigues me because indirectly the outcome of this match is going to affect me. Asesino: Oh the busy life I lead, so much to think about so much to talk about and so many types of voices to beat. I don’t know where I find the time to sack, but then again. I do. The scene fades to a promo hyping the Extreme Division as we wonder if the Assassin stood up and started to play hacky sack. Later... (The scene slowly fades in from blue. We find our selves at the Fish Cove, by the looks of things it is late afternoon, we’re focused in on the ring and we see Asesino bouncing off the ropes back and forth at a tremendous rate. Asesino then spring boards off the middle rope and back flips into the middle of the ring. Asesino is wearing a white t-shirt and black athletic shorts. Asesino walks out of the ring and takes a seat on the apron. Asesino grabs a blue towel and whips his sweat. Asesino throws the towel aside and speaks.) Asesino: Bondage The Sex Fiend. I’m going to be the Extreme Champion, not you. The question is who else will I be facing in the division? Who will I have to compete with to become PTC Extreme Champion? Well, lets go through the list. (Asesino pulls a piece of paper out of his back pocket, it appears he actually has a list. He pulls a thick pair of glasses out as well.) Asesino: Lets see, according to the official PTC Extreme Division program there are 20 superstars involved. (Asesino puts down the list and takes off his glasses.) Asesino: Whoa, I’m going to look really small next to those guys. It’s okay though after I get done with them I will be able to call myself the lightest PTC Extreme Champion in history. Yes, I am still picking myself to win. Sure I’ll be in there with some legends, but let’s look at the facts. I’ve faced two of the men that are going into the division and I’ve beat them both. Asesino: Oh, and Bondage if your mad that I’m looking passed you . . . I don’t care. (The scene fades to black.) Later... (As the scene fades in from black we see Asesino sitting on a lava rock in the dark looking at his black and white TV with the bunny ears on top. Asesino is wearing blue plaid pajama pants, and a Ninja Turtles shirt that goes down to his knees. Asesino looks at the camera.) Asesino: An evil doer like Bondage The Sex Fiend can’t be trusted as Champion. I can be trusted, I will do what’s best for PTC and the fans. With your help I think I can rid the Extreme Division of some evil. Asesino: Oh and there is evil in this division, Like Bondage The Sex Fiend. An evil doer I will bring to justice. If I can't do it for me then for the people. Evil champions are over running the PTC and it’s up to me to stop them. Asesino: Now I’m not saying that I’m going to take it easy on the good doers in the division, but I am saying that I’m asking you to help me unsure a good champion in the Extreme Division. A champion that the fans can cheer and be proud of. A champion that kids can look up to and inspire to be when they grow up. We don’t want thousands of little Sex Fiends running around do we? Asesino: In my mind a Champion has a responsibility to be a good person and fight for what’s right. I don’t know if you feel as strongly as I do about this but it is close to my heart and hopefully everyone elses. Asesino: When I win the PTC Extreme Championship it will be about the fans, it will be about everyone that has supported me, and lastly it will be about me, but only to an extent. All about me is not something I believe in. So when I step in that ring I hope that I walk out winner for the people. (Asesino yawns) Asesino: Now I have to get to sleep, training early tomorrow don’t you know? (Asesino walks a few feet and we see a old mattress on the ground, Asesino lays down. Fade to black.)
