Wash

24 January 2006

Date: 2006-01-24 Note To Self – Don’t EVER let Penny find out what’s in the bo… As a torrent of scalding hot water poured over him from up above, Mal Somers grimaced. He watched as the words he had scrawled on his arm in red marker washed away down the drain. If it was any other occasion he might have thought about the infamous scene from Alfred Hitchcocks’ ‘Psycho’ –but this was no time for reminiscing about classic movies. The knocks at the door grew more incessant, more deliberate. Penny wouldn’t be happy with what was contained within. He wished it was an engagement ring, something he had thought about many times, but had never gotten around to. Kind of like showering as of late, he thought to himself. He wished it was a wad of one hundred dollar bills so he could take her out for dinner, away down the coast for a couple of days. But it was far too late for that. The door burst open with a resounding crash, so much so that even though Mal knew it was coming, he still flinched. Although it was probably more of a pre emptive flinch than anything else. "Who the hell is Kate!" she screamed in the form of a statement instead of a question. Mal sat in the bath, water crashing on him, and hung his head low. Penny held the opened box in one hand and a bundle of letters in the other. Love letters. Mal Somers was generally a good man. He debuted in the World Wrestling Alliance in September 2002 as an evil doer because that was what was called for at the time. But the fans quickly warmed to Somers, the self proclaimed Chairman of MALcoholics Anonymous and he quickly became a fan favourite, his true nature coming through. One bad guy after another made their way up the ranks and Mal was always there to be the customary good guy to take them on, to carry the hopes and dreams of the fans on his shoulders as he took on each new threat. Sometimes he would win, sometimes he would lose, but every time he would emerge with his head held high, a little more popular than before. If only real life was like that. Even though he was a nice guy, he did have his weaknesses. Vices like Happy Meals and Scrabble were acceptable, but what he did was not in any way, shape or form. Mal dared not look up, but under the waterfall of despair he could hear Penny Wise pour her heart out to him. He wished he could say the right thing, but nothing he could say could change what had been done. In September 2004 Mal Somers became the World Wrestling Alliance World Champion and was the envy of many. In addition, he began receiving much more attention than he was accustomed to. Most of it he could shrug off with an autograph or a smile in a photo, but at a bar one night after a show, he met her. Kate. To be honest she wasn’t as drop dead gorgeous as Miss Wise, who could be? In fact, if she were to be tarred with a brush it would be a ‘Plain Jane’ brush. But when he walked into the quiet bar and she turned and looked across at him, it really did feel like one of those corny scenes from the movies. He was drawn across to her, even though there were dozens of other available seats, he sat next to her, and they talked. Talked til the bar closed, and so they continued talking in the parking lot, until he looked at his watch and realised he would have to leave right away if he was to make the next Meltdown show three states away. He gave her his cell number, and she gave him her address. As he drove away he chuckled to himself as he realised that not once the topic of him being a wrestler ever came up. She never called, so a couple of weeks later he wrote to her a letter on a piece of hotel stationary, just asking after her. She replied a few days later to his home address. Mal always remembered that day, seeing the strange letter that was not a bill sticking out of the mailbox like a beacon. He felt his heart race a little as he opened it, and as he read the words within, it broke into a full on sprint. Why did I give her my home address? In hindsight, he liked the excitement. The exitement of the prose contained in the letters, and the smell of the perfume she sprayed on the paper. But most of all, the exitement of being recognised for something other than being a PTC Superstar. He wanted to leap out of the shower and hold Penny and tell her everything was going to be alright. He wanted to tell her the truth, that they had only met that one time in September 2004 and never since. He wanted to tell Penny that he and Kate were really just pen pals. But as he heard his soon to be ex girlfriend scream and shout, asking…demanding ‘Why!?’ he knew that she had managed to read the letters. His thoughts, his feelings, laid out bare and open for Kate to read. Thoughts about his life…Penny’s life. Revelations that he would never dream of sharing with his own girlfriend, but he would put them in written words and mail them across country to this 'other' person. And the way the words were written, a sense of longing and desire that Penny had never come to know from Mal Somers. Until now. And for that reason, no answer would suffice. The harm was done, and he knew once she had poured her heart out, Penny would leave him. But he was not alone, the signs were there, the "holidays" to her parents for weeks at a time. Of course when he rang her parents, Penny was always out…in the shower…fast asleep. And when Mal got his private detective, and former WWA World Champion friend Tracer Bullet to do some research, the evidence did not take a long time to materialise. Penny in the arms of another man, arriving at his house at unusual hours, leaving early in the morning, walking hand in hand in a park, leaving a theatre together. He’d known about it for years, and deep down he knew that she knew. But people can play their part so well, wake up and pour themselves into their clothes, go about their business and not even realise that they are awake until the day is almost over. It took less energy not to argue…not to confront….just to let things be, to let the charade continue. In the end people just want company, and on the nights when Penny was home and Mal reached over and held her cold body, and she flinched, he wondered whether it was a pre-emptive flinch as well. He wanted to say that’s why he had been writing such intimate letters, sending small gifts to a stranger for so long, but it would take less energy for her just to walk away. To feel like the victor – to give her a reason to leave. Eventually, the front door slammed with a hollow thud. Mal Somers looked down at the puddle of water that he sat in. The letters floated around him and the ink began to run and blur. Any other time he would have squeezed out a fart and asked Rookie to join him in a bubble bath. But this wasn’t that time. Mal now knew what his weakness was. He didn't give in to temptation, Penny did, a long time ago. No, his weakness was that he didn't stand up to her a long time ago and sent her packing. As he flicked his wild mop of wet red hair out of his eyes, Mal knew that a lesson had been learned. I wonder if showering will cost me the match? he thought to himself. He shook his head. If he were to lose it would be for more tangible reasons than that. When he stepped through the ropes at GTT5 to face the anti hero Masqerade, the enigmatic Digital Mortality and the mysterious Shanty Jones, he knew he wouldn’t be able to let them wash over him like the now cold water that had left him a sodden mess. He knew that he wouldn't be able to curl up in a ball and let them be the victor. He knew that he would have to stand up to them, fight for all it was worth and in the end, maybe he would truly be the 'good guy' once again.
