Covert Operations (Part 1)

19 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-19 When Dave Harley woke up in the morning, he knew his clothes would be laid out neatly for him. He knew that his breakfast would be sat on the table awaiting his presence and his Spiderman comic book would be sat next to it open at the last page he had read. He knew all this because Jill would make sure that these tasks were completed. Jill was not Harley’s maid - far from it. Jill was Harley’s Personal Assistant. He had hired her during his first few months in the WWA, not too long before X Corp was formed. She was in her late fifties and wore her hair up in a bun. She would glance over Harley’s schedule with a pair of reading glasses perched on the end of her long, thin nose. You might think that expecting your personal secretary to be your maid would be expecting too much. Not if you’re Dave Harley. Jill knew that without completing these jobs for Harley, he’d probably never find his way out of the bathroom in the morning. This particular morning, Harley found his way out of the bathroom and in to the blue jeans and Devil’s Own T Shirt that Jill had prepared for him. He waltzed downstairs and sat at the breakfast table and began feeding his face with the feast Jill had set out. She stood in the kitchen with a black coffee in her hand. She quietly shook her head at the unbelievable manners of her employer. At times t felt more like she was his mother. She had drawn a line, however – if Harley ever asked her to bathe him, she’d be out the door. He slurped his Orange Juice as he looked up at her from his comic. “What?” “With table manners like this, you should write a book on etiquette. I’m sure it would be a bestseller.” Harley always liked Jill’s sarcastic bite. “Hey, when I get around to writing a book it’ll be a smash hit. Just you wait and see!” “Mr Harley, in order to write a book you must first be able to read a book.” She had him there. He crammed the last cupcake into his mouth and stood up. His freshly cleaned Devil’s Own T Shirt was covered in all manner of stains. “I’ve got a job for you today, Jill.” She resisted the temptation to reply with ‘When do you not have a job for me?’ responding instead with a sublime “oh?” “This PTC thing. We’re down to the last 24 out of…what was it? 250?” “Somewhere around the 200 mark.” “Well, I’m facing this Dallas Reeves chump in the next round. I’m sure it’ll be a walk in the park, but I need you to do some recon…reconnas…snooping around for me. I hear he’s a member of this crappy little fed called NGW.” “Next Generation Wrestling?” Jill had heard about it. She’d been a secretary in the wrestling business for a long time. “Whatever. Thing is it’s a mess. They say it’s closing, then it ain’t, then it is, then it’s not. Fact is, I don’t even know if this punk is gonna show up. So I need you to go down to NGW headquarters. Find out what their situation is, but most importantly find out as much as you can about Dallas Reeves.” Well, it was better than the time Dave asked her to spy on Burger King staff and find out what toys were going in the Kids Meals. “I’ll need travel expenses.” This usually put him off sending her. If money had to be spent doing it, Dave was always less enthusiastic. “…OK.” Well that surprised her. An hour later, Jill was stood on a train station platform with a few hundred dollars worth of expenses. She knew she’d have to keep all the receipts from this trip. Dave would want to account for every penny. Meanwhile, Dave sat around in his suburban Chicago home and played Nintendo Gamecube games. He knew he should really be meeting up with his Devil’s Own troops, but something about Super Monkey Ball kept him mesmerised – perhaps it was the uncanny resemblance he had noticed between himself and the monkey. He imagined himself rolling around in the ball and laughed about the amount of damage he’d be able to do. The day past remarkably slowly. After The A Team Marathon, Dave decided to head for bed. Harley fell asleep with visions of Mr T dancing in his head. When he awoke the next morning, Dave knew his clothes would be laid out for him – but they weren’t. He knew his breakfast would be awaiting him on the kitchen table – but it wasn’t. In fact, he barely made his way out of the bathroom. Harley began to wonder if sending Jill on this mission had been such a smart move. After all, he couldn’t make his own Pop Tarts, could he?! He rifled through his jacket pockets and pulled out his cell phone. Harley hated cell phones and the feeling seemed to be more than mutual. Harley dialled Jill’s cell and waited as it rang. And rang. And rang. After almost two minutes, Harley was greeted by an answer phone service. If there was anything Harley hated more than cell phones it was answering machines. “Jill? Jill, are you there? I want my breakfast dammit! Do something! JILL? JILL?!!” No answer. Dave slammed the phone down. He glanced around the room for something to destroy and noticed a little red light flashing on the cabinet…it was his own answering machine. He pressed play… “Mr Harley, this is Jill. Unfortunately I cannot complete my duties here at NGW…In fact, I quit. I’m getting married and I’m on my way to Vegas. Goodbye.” What the hell? Harley had employed Jill for almost three years and she’d never shown any interest in other people. Actually, he’d never spoken to her about her life so he honestly had no idea. He presumed she was a lonely old hag though. But to run off to Vegas and get married?! That was pretty extreme. Suddenly it dawned on Harley. It must be Dallas Reeves. That’s it. He must’ve caught Jill snooping around the NGW headquarters and kidnapped her! Of course, Dave would have to rescue her. And by ‘her’ he means the few hundred bucks he gave to her. Within the hour, Dave was stood in the cold Chicago streets. He couldn’t get a flight to Vegas to intercept them, mainly because he had no idea how to book a flight – that was Jill’s job. He hailed a cab. As it drew to a halt alongside him, Dave dove straight in. “Where to, buddy?” “Vegas.” “Vegas?” “That’s what I said, chumpstain. Las Vegas.” “You realise how much that’ll cost?” Harley sighed. “Listen asshole, do you know who I am?” “Yeah, you’re that wrestler. Craven Moore.” Harley fought back the urge to force feed him the steering wheel. “No. I’m the Extreme Machine Dave Harley. I’m the leader of the Devil’s Own. If you don’t drive me to Vegas right now I’ll invite my troops along for the journey.” That was all the convincing it took. The guy must have seen Devil’s Own’s methods of force on Meltdown. They headed out along Route 66. Dave would foil Reeves plan…and maybe check out the hookers whilst he was there…
