Outline for Dr. Kasidy Williams Drake III

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-10 Tentative Title: "Dr. Kasidy Williams Drake III" Concept: A roleplay with two distinct parts focused on the character of Kasidy Drake and New Eden’s plot. PART I: Tentative Title: "The Two Sides of Tomorrow" Synopsis: One day written from the perspectives of two parallel realities – one in which Drakewerx never collapsed and Kasidy Drake (with wife, Cassidy DeMoore) are happy, and the normal one in which Kasidy Drake, heartbroken over his failures and sins is preparing for his match with Shanty Jones. PART II: Tentative Title: "A Place for Our Unfinished Dreams" Synopsis: Karl is able to sneak into Kasidy Drake’s room in Quaker Manor in the dead of the night and he confronts Kasidy who, in return, reveals the horrible truth – that all of them from Calvin to Llevar and from Karl to himself are all the most dangerous things in the world – the ghosts of incomplete and shattered dreams. Karl tries to convince Kasidy that there’s still enough hope in the both of them to stop the horrible New Eden plot. Outline for Part I * Karl and Julia come to the residence of "Dex," a man that they’ve found to be an old best friend of Kasidy Drake. They want information and after thinking it over, Dex takes them to his friend, Glitch, a world-class hacker. Glitch determines that a large order of fireworks were ordered by Calvin Riley from The Fireworks Company in Sheffield, but the order was discretely cancelled a day later. Another order from The Big Bang Fireworks Company in New Zealand, a company which Calvin Riley owns, secretly shipped an even larger order to the United States about the time that Kasidy Drake was entered into the tournament. Calvin Riley has admittedly promised a "spectacular fireworks display" following Kasidy Drake’s winning of the GTT5 Cup in the finals. The Karl and co. determine that the key to the conspiracy is in the fireworks. After realizing that no one is going to believe them if they tell them (and Glitch would go to jail for all the hacking he’s done,) they realize that it’s up to Karl and Julia. After all, they’re already "dead" and have nothing to loose. Karl goes to break into Quaker Manor and talk to Kasidy whom he is convinced is not a bad person because of the fact that he saved his life. * The second part of the roleplay (and the bulk of it) follows the Kasidy Drake of two different parallel realities: the current one in which he’s preparing for his match with Shanty Jones and visits his cryogenically frozen wife, and the other in which everything turned out … right – Drakewerx never collapsed, and Kasidy Drake is basically living happily ever after. Fill in the blanks. It ends with Kasidy in the bathroom washing his face in both realities. Rather than see their reflections, however, they see each other and realize what they could have been. The GTT Kasidy tries to reach his other self, but the mirror breaks on both sides. Kasidy reflects on how worthless his life has become and cries himself to sleep on the bathroom floor curled up like a dog. Outline for Part II * This roleplay opens with a "montage" of the story of New Eden mostly comprised of the lives of Kasidy and Calvin. The final parts feature Calvin Riley being abducted after being shot in the face during a Global Title match – a match that he’s worked his entire life to earn. He’s abducted, realizes that his dream of becoming a world champion for his dead father has been crushed and "dies" when he is injected with an unknown substance by his captives, including an unknown man who seems to be running things and claims that Calvin is going to complete some kind of plan that has been in the works for thirty-one years. It then shows the scene several years later in which Kasidy realizes that he stabbed his fiancé in the heart without knowing it. His dream of being happy with her is now gone and Calvin, now the evil white-suit wearing billionaire he is today, offers him a spot in the Sovereign Guard, the army of a country that Kasidy’s never heard of. In exchange, Calvin will use his resources to try and save Cassidy. The narration remarks that the two of them are dead and flashes back to the moment in which Kasidy told Karl that he was dead. * The bulk of this roleplay: Kasidy comes to realizing that someone’s trying to break into the manor. None of the other Guardsmen seem to have noticed, and so Kasidy goes out to the yard to find Karl. Kasidy can hear Llevar coming to investigate the strange noises, and he again saves Karl’s life by scaling the side of the manor with him on his back and hiding him in his bedroom until the coast is clear. Karl tries to convince Kasidy not to do whatever it is that he’s planning to do. The two have a conversation in which Kasidy reveals the horrible truth – that they’re all just ghosts of unfinished dreams and that there is nothing left for any of them but to destroy everything else. After hearing Kasidy’s story, however, Karl points out that his "dream" has not necessarily been destroyed yet. Cassidy is still alive – just barely and only on life support, but she’s still alive. As long as she’s still grounded to the land of the living, there’s still hope for Kasidy and he doesn’t have to do what Calvin says. Kasidy agrees to think it over but once Llevar leaves and the coast is clear, he tells Karl to leave and tells him that if he finds him in danger of being killed by the Guard again, he won’t save him.
