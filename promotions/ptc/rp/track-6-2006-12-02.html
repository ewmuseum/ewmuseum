He couldn’t tell a 5-wood from a 9-iron from a cast-iron, but he knew one thing damn well - October was way too fucking cold for the game. Justin Hattoni didn’t seem to share that feeling. The boss inched up to the little twig he called a "tee" and scanned the big long lawn in the distance. He took a few deep breaths to ready himself. Johnny didn’t understand why he didn’t just give the ball a good crack - all this preparation bullshit made no sense at all. Johnny figured it was a lot like pulling off a good hit - sometimes it was best to just roll up your sleeves and smack the fucker in the head. But, again, Johnny Silver didn’t know shit about golf. Justin stretched out his arms and closed his eyes to visualize. Like one of them buddha pricks, thought Johnny. Slowly, Justin lowered the head of his golf club until it was level with the ball. Then he brought it close once, twice, three times, and then… …Four times. Johnny would have told anybody else to hurry the fuck up before he froze his balls off. But Justin was the boss, and nobody talked to the boss like that. Johnny wasn’t even involved in the stupid fucking game - he was just along for the company. Ray Delvecchio was along for company too, but he had decided to play. He was Justin’s right hand man; the pair of them were rarely separated. Most guys in the Hattoni family agreed that facial hair looked unprofessional, but Ray got a pass because he was about a hundred fucking years old. Thwack! Johnny looked over to find the boss’ ball sailing off into the blue until the ball and the blue blended together. It was like the television at the far end of the living room was a window into another universe, and in that universe, her brother-in-law was a god.
