NMPD Unsolved Files - The Firey Convent

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 ::The following is a recovered document that was used in evidence against the self proclaimed Reverend Paul Springwell. The charge was first degree arson with the intent to murder. In 1983, the convent/seminary that Springwell presided over was burned to the ground in a fire. The cause of that fire was found to be chemical in nature, and was deemed accidental. But, suspicions of foul play run amok, as Springwell took out a large insurance policy on the property only six months earlier. The reason Springwell was able to claim the property as his own was due to the fact that it no longer belonged to the Catholic Church. After being excommunicated from the Church in 1981, the former priest bought the convent/seminary and welcomed any that followed his strict way of believing in God. There was only one survivor of the fire besides Reverend Springwell. That survivor is a boy that the reverend had reportedly found two and a half years before as an orphan to the premesis. The boy's name has never been released to New Mexico Police, but the reverend has referred to him as "That Which Should Not Be," "Abomination," "Ragnarok," and simply "The End." Whether this child was somehow in league with the suspected Springwell, if Springwell simply felt pity on him and saved him, or if it all was indeed an accident that he and the boy survived from are yet to be determined. The case is still open, and Springwell is still observed to this day. Below is one of Reverend Paul Springwell's homilies that survived the fire. One of the few things that did. It is believed that through this document his ill intent can be clearly seen.:: My siblings in blood and flesh that live below the Lord and fear Him as we should…I welcome you to the place of those surviving the great purging that the Lord will lay upon this sinful world. We gather here…and we are united. We are joined. We are strong together to worship the Lord and sing His praises. The Lord's children know not pain nor suffering nor hatred nor guilt, for they follow His law and do not commit to actions that would bring them to such base and low views of themselves. They are pure. We are pure. But…we all have been touched at one point or another by the evils of the world. All, that is…save for the boy. Our collective, adopted child. But the rest of us…we are sinners. Born with the sin of our fathers and our father's fathers. Born with the sin of Eve. Born with the sin of Adam. Then, we are washed clean by it. Washed clean by whom, though? Those who sin. Imperfect. Not God. Society and its evils have touched us all! We try to escape that which draws us from the Lord by coming here, to the middle of the desert. Just as Jesus did in his time of trials, we too retreat to the desert, the foresaken land, to pray in peace and give glory to our Lord. …and as with many things…this is ruined by a select few. As was said, we are strong together when gathered in His name. We, as one, are unstoppable. But chip the stone, and the bridge will fall. One rotted apple will cause the rest of the bushel to rot in the same. One without faith…siphons faith from the glorious. …and we must be cleansed. The fires of the Lord will quench us of our thirst for glory. The Lord's wrath shall deliver us from our own damnation if only we embrace Him at the last. When you meet eyes with God, what will your reaction be? Will you cower? Will you plead? For these things He shall cast your soul to the lowest depths of Hell, for He knows why it is that we do so! Rather, look upon His face with unscathed eyes, with an unstained soul, and know that He will spare you His judgement to an eternity of suffering. Know this…and prepare yourselves. Prepare yourselves for the Lord's judgement. Know this when you doubt his plan. Know this when you doubt…his prophets. Know this. And be judged. Sins against God, prepare yourselves. For The End is here.
