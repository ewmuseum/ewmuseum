Date: 2005-03-15 SOMETIME IN THE VERY NEAR FUTURE, PRECISE DATE UNKNOWN: A PROLOUGE [/b I am seated here in this room. It appears to be a tavern. I am seated on what is, by all accounts, a barstool. In front of me is a bottle, exact contents unknown. Next to me (on the right hand side) is a man dressed in a flannel vest, strapped to his back is an axe, on the stool next to him is a moose drinking Guinness, I think, my mind is to addled by The Drugs to known 100% beyond-asoad if there is an actual moose in this actual bar drinking actual Guinness. Addled really isn't the right (write?) description. I've gone well beyond addled at this point, my life is a fog and the fog lamps I paid that shyster at the Ford dealership for have burnt out and the warranty isn't worth the price of the flesh it's printed on. I don't even know how I got to this room that I'm seated in at present. This is the first time I've been actually aware of anything in a number of days, ever since Alice left, I think. Jeez, how long ago was that, anyway? God how I miss her so, the way her brunette curls seemed to burst forth from some point deep in the subterranean layers of her cranium, who knows where the source was, maybe there wasn't one, maybe they simply went forever downward into the infinite pit of her very soul and all We on the surface were able to see was their peak, the tip of the metaphorical ice-berg on the metaphorical ocean that was her endlessly fascinating corporeal presence. Why did she leave me? What did I do? She just disappeared one day and took my (our?) pet duck with her. She left a note saying she was planning to sail the (literal) high seas and farm blueberries on some far-off rock of an island that I'd never before heard of, even though I have extensive schooling in both the geographic arts and all things blueberry, so I think that her story was a fabrication of the highest order, which thought leads me back to the primary question: Why did she leave? My good friend and business associate L. Frederick Flimflam, who just so happens to be seated to my immidiate left in the bar in which I am presently present, has a few theories on the matter that he'd like to share so I think I'll just Pass Tha Mic on over to him. LFF: So but anyway as my friend and associate said, I have a few theories wrt him being abandoned by Alice, they follow: 1.) The letter she wrote is 100% beyond-asoad truthful. This theory has already been disproven the The Ontological Argument For the Disproval of L. Frederick Flimflam's First Theory of Alice's Abandonment, which follows: a.) The letter does not actually exist, it is simply a figment of Flimflam's friend and associates imaginations b.) Something that does not exist cannot be 100% beyond-asoad truthful c.) Therefore, the letter cannot be 100% etc etc 2.) The relationship between Alice and MF&A went south. She blamed him. He was oblivious. She went south. 3.) She never actually existed, at least not in the form that MF&A is remembering her in his present addled (apres-addled, really) state. She was actually The Girl He Fell In Love With IN High School, his One True Love, to put it more concisely. They haven't spoken since graduation when He joined the Army and went to fight in far-off places that know one even knew existed back in Duckfuck, Kansas and She married an ABUSIVE SAILOR. This is the most likely scenario. At this point L. Frederick Flimflam turned into My Father who called me a fuck-up and a disgrace to the family before running out of the room and joining the circus. Unable to believe what I'd just seen I turned and looked across the bar at the bartender who was a large burly man with an anchor tattooed across his not un-large chest. He was absolutely hideous. 'Why ya laughing?' he asked, his mouth breaking into a smile that would turn even the most un-turnable of stomachs. His breath smelled of week old vagina and turpentine. I nearly fell off my stool when it blew across my face at what could best be described as a gale. The only thing that kept me upright was what he had said: Why was I laughing? I wasn't. Was I? It was at this point that I realized that I could no longer feel my face: My body literally seemed to start at the tips of my toes and end at the top of my neck. Oh shit, i though. I can't feel my face. This is most likely not a good sign. I decided I needed to perform a test to make sure my face was even still extant and so but I rocked back slightly on my stool and thrust my face downward into the surface of the bar itself. The smell of fresh blood mingled with the smell of must, rotten pine. The latter smell caused me to experience an unexpected flash back as I was rocketed backwards through the space-time continuum to my childhood, more specifically those moments of my childhood when I would frolic through the pine forest that was located behind my parent’s home. It was really pretty beautiful, that forest with its phallic pine trees that seemed to burst forth from the wet, green earth, pointing upwards towards the heavens in the pale blue skies above. I would frolic through this forest with the very first love of my life, Eleanor G. Krebs; dear sweet Eleanor, [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] her cheeks that turned the colour of fresh apples when she’d blush, her pale blue eyes that seemed not to look at you so much as through you, her laugh; hers was a laugh that could arouse the deepest and coldest pits of the soul. Oh God, it was just so horrible what happened to her… I am now lying face up on the concrete outside. I can hear the faint sounds of paramedics going about their business, of an ambulance siren, but it all sounds so far away… I am lying on my back on a stretcher in the corridor in some hospital in whatever city this is that I’m in. I attempt to reach up and touch my own personal face but my arms are restrained. I cough and little globules of blood arc upwards. Across the hall is another one like me, another one lying prostrate on a gurney not quite sure of where he is or why he’s there. He’s an older gentleman, his teeth are stained yellow, his hands are weathered to the point of nearly appearing worn through; he is balding, slightly. He looks across at me and our eyes momentarily lock and he seems to communicate across the vast expanse between us a very simple inquiry: Hey, guy, so wat’s yer story? Then the voice of a nurse, a human voice, comes and it wakes me and I drown. Fade to black, cue music, roll opening credits. THE AUTOBIOGRAPHY OF WOOF J.
