Amid the chaos...

14 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-14 Complacency - A feeling of contentment or self-satisfaction, especially when coupled with an unawareness of danger, trouble, or controversy. Dictionary definitions. Yeah. I went there. Well, not so much a definition as an accusation, depending on who you talked to in the highly-strung and hotly-tempered business that is pro wrestling. A little re-wording, and you could substitute in the name Karina Wolfenden. Contentment, that can stay. She loved her life, and all those she let be a part of it. She loved everything she did, or she would’t do it…simple enough. Ask her to change anything, and she’d struggle to give you an answer. Well, if you ignore boosting her from an A-cup, or her demand to install most human beings with an off-switch. Self-satisfaction? Sounds fair. Self-centred, guilty. Self-absorbed could also be an accusation, although self-sufficient would be a fairer option. Coupled… although not part of the definition, can be scrapped right away. Doesn’t do relationships, doesn’t do ‘couples’, and most certainly doesn’t do ‘opening up’. Take the last bit however your minds can twist it. Unawareness? A little harsh, maybe. Self-aware, check. Very, very selectively aware when she wants to be, check. Everything she shuts out is deliberate… and plentiful. Danger. She’ll take some of that. The blood simply wasn’t meant to pump around the human body as slowly as it does… so something has to be done to send the pulse racing. Trouble? Been called it plenty of times. Playfully by parents and family, semi-seriously by friends who failed to keep up with her normal lifestyle, and very angrily by her RPW employers. Controversy? We’ll have to see about that one. For now, let’s rewind to Sunday evening. Everything backstage at RUSH Pro Wrestling seemed more fevered when a tournament was on. Not to say that the men and woman that Karina Wolfenden shared a promotion with weren’t normally sharp and at their physical peak... but knowing that there was another prize up for grabs just amplified body language, or increased focus. Angelo Deville, a solid mass of wicked muscle, seemed to have added even more tone to his chiselled body. Piercing blue eyes seemed all the more remarkable, trapeziums seemed to climb just that little bit higher up his wide neck, while his fists seemed to be permanently clenched in expectation of battle. Bruce Shanahan, better known as the "King of the Indies" - Violence Jack, had even managed to add a little more dominance to his proud walk. Tessa Windsor, always full of energy, had found a little more buzz, a little more spring to her step. Coming off the King of the Indies show with the Squared Circle, building up to the GTT, and preparing to take the RUSH Pro show on the road, everyone on the roster walked taller, talked fiercer. Never before had the weight room at the Dale F. Halton Arena, RPW's home, seen so much extra-curricular activity. Rarely used except for weekday evenings, it would carry the odour of stale sweat well into the next week... while the carpet underneath the equipment had nearly been rubbed right through by the frenzied activity of it's user. Runners seemed to never be without a message to deliver, water-coolers always seemed to be dispensing into white paper cones, and road agents barely had a spare second when they weren't passing on the wisdom their careers instilled into them. In the eye of the hurricane, unaffected by the frantic preparations and intense training, was Karina Wolfenden. Calm, content, completely at ease with herself and all around her. "To the curtain in five," one of RPW's production crew bellowed, making sure everyone was where they needed to be ready for the next match. "We switch from promo to ringside in three, two, one..." Sweat soaked the armpits of the energetic workers as the stuffy Carolina night coupled with the lack of air conditioning to turn backstage into a sauna. Removed ties and open collars did nothing to cool people down as they couldn't even find a five second interval to breathe. Perspiration-soaked towels were discarded on corridors, and the bathroom sinks had workers lined up, throwing cupped hands of cold water onto their sticky, salty faces. "Where's Mr Kannon?" a panicked worker then asked, wide, appealing eyes staring all around. "Kannon? Anyone? GHAR!" With slapping paces he jogged down the hall, the back of his grey t-shirt darkened as sweat cascaded down his back. It was like running through a hot shower, the humidity almost washing heated water over everyone's skin, whose pores were beginning to get clogged. "Someone find me Shanahan," RPW spokesperson Matt Jay demanded, his voice echoing down the stone corridors. "He has about three people ready to interview him about King of the Indies and that bloody GTT thing! So track the tattooed loon down, and tell the Forsaken that their match is up in ten!" Cramped corridors became games of human pinball. Shoulders jolted, arms brushed, awkward side-steps let others past while sighing runners threw their arms up in frustration at being slowed down. Packing crates were being wheeled, music being cued, promos being cut, deals being made, workers getting paid. All the activity. All the noise. All the action. All just a minor distraction, occasionally forcing Karina to turn her MP3 player up to keep herself detached from the 'outside world'. Atop a packing crate, hovering around 4 feet in height, Karina had found a spare ring-side mat, and turned it into a makeshift mattress. Resting her feet on a discarded filing cabinet, Karina lay flat on her back, eyes closed behind red lenses, while palms casually drummed thighs in time to the music. All the passers by heard was the tinny residual sound from her earphones, looking with curiosity at the brightly dressed young woman who took the word 'casual' to a whole new level. Complacent, for want of a better word. The rest of the world was something Karina Wolfenden needed regular breaks from. Just existing seemed to tire her. Working out how she felt about something was often confusing enough, let alone having to have the opinions and judgements of others imposed on her. At times a solitary creature, the K-Wolf used her 'zone-outs' to recharge, just as some could choose Yoga. And given her less than golden-tonsilled singing voice, Yoga would have sounded better. "Hold your breath and count to ten," Kari half-hummed, half sung, channelling Placebo's 'English Summer Rain' through her ears, then out her mouth. "Then fall apart and start again..." Amid the chaos, she remained encapsulated in her own calm. The drumming of her palms against her thighs was in an almost sub-conscious sync, as if the music being pumped right into her brain had control over her motor functions. Her only inconvenience was the overly-bright synthetic light that the confines of the Dale F. Halton Arena were drenched in… until, that was, her peace was shattered. “Kannon wanted it built ready for the final segment,” a stern voice commanded, his disclosure being met with grumbles of discontent from the assembled workers. “The floor will need to break, but we don’t want any fans in the aisle seats to see the foam landing pad.” As they continued to converse, Karina turned the volume up just a little at a time, fading the music up while drowning them out. “It needs to be a clean break too, since this won’t be a straight forward bump,” the production and prop chief continued to detail. “Splinters we can handle, but we ain’t getting a femoral artery slashed on any unbroken remains.” The voices grew in volume, Karina reacted and adjusted. She was in perfect equilibrium with her docile environment. “So, let’s get building,” the chief rallied his men with, “we make the switch in 30 minutes.” Although far brighter and keener than her apathetic exterior may have suggested, Karina was too wrapped up in trying to get some rest to think that prop builders and carpenters would actually need tools. The split second the power-drill began it’s skull-splitting whirr, the aggrieved Karina’s eyes shot open. Slowly sitting up straight, Karina’s burning emerald eyes looked over the tops of her red lenses, a glare of pure venom in store for anyone who was unlucky enough to lock onto her eyes. She reached back down into her hoodie’s pocket, but even holding down the volume plus button wouldn’t muster a sound to match that of the deafening machinery. “This… sucks… hard,” she tired moaned, resentful at being dragged kicking and screaming out of her solitary relaxation. “Wanna turn it down a bit?” The moment her words departed through pink lips, they were grabbed by the swirling mechanical volume and ripped to shreds, long before anyone’s ear could take them in for contemplation. “Or maybe move it a bit?” her weary words continued, meeting the same fate as her previous appeal. “Maybe about 200 metres in thatta direction?” Even raising her voice, and adding a more direct tone to it, Karina was always going to fail. Something she never liked doing at the best of times. Oblivious to the scowl of the put-out K-Wolf, the RPW prop team continued work on their section of collapsible stage, while Karina’s aggrieved mind pictured them falling through it. From a great height. “If they’re going to make us stay until last bell,” Karina sighed while rubbing her eyes, never a fan of RPW’s seemingly pointless backstage etiquettes. “Then they could at least try not to shatter our skulls.” Having gotten back home at 5am in the morning, before collapsing into bed and only waking an hour before the wrestlers were meant to be in attendance, Karina was never going to have been the most perky and vibrant star in attendance. Factor in two tough matches in the space of two days, and you had the makings of a weary K-Wolf. After spinning ninety degrees from her sitting position, Karina hopped down from her make-shift bunk, then moped off, leaving the set-builders to get on with their work... away from her poisoned glare. “Where’s the info on Snake Eyes?” one panicked runner appealed to the other, half a rain forest alone having been passed back and forth over the course of the night. “Don’t ask me,” his fellow worker dismissed, rushing past and holding his hands up to rid himself of responsibility. “I’m already snowed under trying to grab some tapes for Shanahan! Just go to their website or something…” “Addy?” the young runner hollered after his colleague with, only to receive a shrug in return, before losing visual. In her usual low-key manner, Karina tried to weave her way though the oncoming traffic. Even looking as if she was sluggish, or methodical, even, Karina seemed to navigate the corridors without any intrusive collisions. “XUW?” the worker then asked of Karina, only getting a blank look in reply. “Any idea what its addy is?” The pale and freckled face of Karina barely moved a muscle, her expression in reply to his inquiry making even blank look an overstatement. “What’s an XUW?” Wolfenden asked in return, showing her ignorance to the wrestling world which played next to no part in her regular life. “Ugh,” the runner grunted, seeing that the pink-fringed woman was of little help to him. “Don’t bother…” Twisting a shoulder inside to avoid jolting against the runner as he stormed past, Karina just let out another lack-of-rest inspired sigh, pulled her all-encompassing hood over her bright hair, and continued on a bearing away from the noise and activity. Backstage etiquette and meaningless roster policy could wait until next week. She wanted sleep. Especially since her flight to Helsinki left at 11am the next morning, and despite her risk-taking lifestyle, she still wasn’t comfortable enough flying to catch some winks above the clouds. Breaking an arm, an ankle, and a wrist couldn’t keep Karina off her snowboard. But a little turbulence on her maiden flight at the age of 9 meant she got little relaxation en route. Like a slalom skier, she wove her path through though human bodies, all interacting with one another, while Karina remained content in her own world, with her own goals. Their meaningless and ineffectual chatter seemed to blur into one long drone as she built up speed. Each body she passed was clammy, skin sheening and dripping, while clothes stuck to salty perspiration. But despite the stifling heat, and minimal air circulation, Karina seemed to be carried on a breeze. Pale skin remained cold and dry, sleek hair avoided any hint of frizz that seemed to effect most in the humidity, and even her layered, almost secretive clothing hung airily off her slender, weaving body. Her own world, her own air conditioning, apparently. "Who did they get?" a curious crew worker asked, finally having a spare moment to check one of the cluttered notice boards that were dotted around the Dale F. Halton Arena. "Wow, Tessa'll have a tough one." "Ah, c'mon," his colleague dismissed, before taking a quenching sip from a bottle of water. "You think they're gonna hold a torch to our guys and girls?" "Pride comes before a fall," the warning rebounded. "Yeah, and 2 comes before 3," the crew member was reminded. "And that's what our Team RUSH Pro'll all get." Throughout the course of the night, crowds had assembled around the boards... especially after someone had scribbled the odds they were taking next to the names. Neither worker heard the steps of Karina Wolfenden as she approached. Light, gliding strides combined with bouncy sneaker soles to give her walk audio-stealth. Where as she didn't really talk to many of the wrestlers in RPW, and could only call two friends, Karina was on first name terms with the ring crew. They were the closest thing she had to a pusher. The men who put her favourite toy together, who erected her extreme trampoline, and who tightly wound the ropes that she'd leap and twist from. "Hey, Frank, Joel," the K-Wolf acknowledged, as polite as she'd been all night. "G'night." Gliding past the duo, Karina remained locked onto beacon of the outside doors, while the duo of crew workers switched their attention away from the GTT debate just long enough to say their goodbyes to Karina's back. "Oh, bye, Kari," one added, before the other chipped in with, "yeah, see ya next week." Having given the board the slightest of glances out of the corner of her eye, at such an angle that it wasn't seen through the red hue of her lenses, Karina quickly absorbed the names. She recognised her fellow RUSH Pro'ers, had no idea who all the other people were... and for the most part, she didn't care. One, however, intrigued her. "Fatbadger..." she mused, on the verge of breaking into a reserved chuckle. "Would be a neat name for a cat..." Not a pondering that Psylocke & Betsy would be too happy about, if they themselves weren't cats, thus incapable of understanding her. Especially from miles away. The line seemed to sum up Karina's take on all things wrestling. Her own lack of ego meaning she had little attention to devote to other's accomplishments or reputations. Fatbadger may have been one of XUW's most decorated stars, just as anyone she ever faced could have a list of accomplishments as long as her patience was short. But to Karina Wolfenden, he was a guy whose name would be a neat thing to name a cat. A lack of respect? No, not in any way. Just a lack of relevance. Finally, Karina turned the corner to where the wrestlers exit warmly welcomed her. Karina didn't even look at the doors, rather through the glass to where the outside world was calling her with its leaf-blowing breeze and alive, crowded paths. Final bell was still a good 45 minutes away, but since she barely interacted with anyone there, it wouldn't be like anyone would miss her... right? Wrong. As the doors swung shut behind the relieved, bounding steps of the K-Wolf, the glass through which Karina viewed her freedom was being pierced by the stare of her 'captor', Christian Alexander Kannon. Her early, and adjudged rude exit was just one on a growing list of things that grated the RUSH Pro Wrestling figurehead. She rarely showed for promotional events, despite being one of RPW's most marketable talents. She withdrew herself from singles action, despite having had a total of three title reigns in RPW. She'd alienated a growing number of wrestlers backstage, while remaining one of it's most popular, 'golden' faces. Not even giving her a grumble or muttered word, the silent Kannon turned, and headed back towards the hub of the Dale F. Halton Arena. Finally having freed herself of the claustrophobic feel that the building's confines gave her, Karina flung her hood back, tilting her chin up and feeling what breeze there was tickle her freckled face. While inside, in confinement, Karina was docile and lethargic, on the outside she seemed to integrate seamlessly into the world. The terrain of winding paths, knee-high walls, and segregating rails was easily traversed by the K-Wolf. A hop, a brisk walk, and a hurdle later, she'd reached the road. Either side of her, pedestrians stood hesitantly back, waiting for a lull in the traffic while teasing an attempt to cross. Having always been able to read the motion of all things around here, all Karina needed was a quick glance from left to right. A confident step off the sidewalk later, and Karina was threading the fine-headed needle of the traffic, leaving it close enough to feel the breeze of the cars as they cut through her trail. Without a glance behind, Karina walked out of RPW's home, and back into her life... needing the boundary to keep them separate. So, whether complacency was an only slightly-off description, twisted compliment, or harsh accusation, it was something that could seemingly always apply to Karina Wolfenden. Maybe she was too complacent to even worry about her complacency, or maybe her complacency just made it easier to ignore any accusations of being complacent. Far from the easiest word to define, its complicated nature seemed to suit Karina Wolfenden. Just as she seemed to suit it.
