Ignorance=Headphones?

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Don’t you just hate it when you’re walking down the road minding your own business getting on with life when someone knocks you? He is a small, spotty screwed up fourteen year old with an attitude problem. His burberry hat sitting proud on his juvenile head you don’t even have to look down to know your about to be meet by the blinding sight of a baby blue hoodie with some trend shifting logo plastered over it like a capitalism bulletin board. His socks run over the blue jogging bottoms he calls comfortable when you question why he wears them, an excuse for a lack of a personality. In a society where individuality is low you really feel like smacking a piece of work like this lad. ‘What ya listening to’ I simply asked him. ‘What’s it to you?’ The little nimwhit grumbled back. K, first the little git bumps into me then he’s rude, it’s just not on. ‘Just asking!’ The chav stepped forward lifting his hat up he rests it in a more acute angle. His head was bald possible a sign that I should be intimidated but the little monkey didn’t know who he was dealing with. ‘Well I’m just asking you to give me three quid.’ He demanded with a certain level of confidence. I sighed relaxing my muscles thinking to myself what to do with this child like creature. Many punishments crossed my mind. Death by choking on his disgusting coloured hat, injury by sweep of the socks or just a cold hard slap to the face would serve a cold serving of justice to this punk and put him in his place. ‘So… Are you gonna (Not using proper English due to his bad education) Or shell I shout out that you just put your hand where your hand shouldn’t be mister.’ Ok I would describe how he said it but no need it made me bloody mad that’s all you need to know. ‘Ya what.’ I muttered in disbelief. ‘You erd…’ ‘I’m going to give you three seconds to turn away and well forget this ever happen son.’ ‘Oh what you gonna do? Hurt me?’ ‘I can smash your teeth out and break your back at the same time.’ At this time another kid had spotted me this kid however was a different kind of guy. Smart dressed, green t-shirt with black stripes, trucker cap, three xXx on his shoulder symbolising his straight edge lifestyle the only one I promote. ‘JAMIE FLETCHER!’ It screamed like I was a member of a boy band. The boy inspected me to check I was real. Pouncing around me like I was wild, rare circus animal he made himself look like a fool but he didn’t mind he lounged forward grasping my elbow I stepped to one side grabbed the little over the top accepted child’s head and throw him 270 degree’s down to the cold pavment slab his back hitting almost wrestler like against the floor. He got back up almost unharmed the little smark turned around. ‘I’ve had training.’ He said to me in a very glee voice almost boasting that he didn’t get hurt. ‘Well train this.’ I barked throwing a very sloppy punch at the guy. He ducked so likely no lawsuit for me today. I turned to face him he winked and ran away happy about his little fight with Jamie Fletcher. The little chav by this time had scampered away like a hare making fast into the distant. Now it was time to do what I had wanted to do and that’s get my ass to the Guildhall because I had a fight tonight. Tonight, it was I and Pyschoduck one on one man to man just like it’s going to be Justin Evers and me in America on Monday. See there’s little difference between the matches because really all what matters is we know I’m going to come out on top. Duck may be bigger, Evers may be bigger, they both might even be smaller. They could be the same weight as me, different heights, same heights, more violent, varied technical skills who knows maybe one of them is just like a jumping Mexican bean and flip like a pancake. Really it doesn’t matter to me. Once I step in that ring I can remember it all, how to fight, how to take someone down before they can even count to three. My technical skills are superior, if given the chance I could break a man’s skull with a chair. If the man is bigger I will use my speed, if he is smaller I will use my power, if he is the same size as me I will make him smaller. If he is larger than me I will have to get violent, if he is smaller than me I will have to play rough, if he is the same size as me will have to play slaps. Some people will say I won’t be able to hack it if I see a chair I will lose it, if I pick up a chair I’ll leave the match. They say I can’t handle hardcore…. Shift to the arena!!! 9.47 that night!!! I’m in the ring. I lay on the mat selling a German suplex. Like a cold fish Pyschoduck scoops me up off of the mat with a waist lock go behind. Lifting my elbow I sloppily drop two stiff shots into the right side of Pyschoducks head. Giving myself enough room to turn I smash Pyschoduck down to the mat with a clothesline to the neck region. Dropping down to my knee’s I cover Pyschoduck while the crowd all stand. One! Two! Just like I knew he would Pyschoduck kicked out. Even no I knew I was booked to win the bout Pyschoduck wouldn’t let the belt go like that he wanted pain, suffering and a classic bout amongst all the other things. I brought him onto his knee’s sitting him in a praying position. I pulled my pad off of my knee throwing it into the crowd before I ran at Pyschoduck I watched them all attempt to catch it pilling on top of each other. I ran at Pyschoduck attempting a shinning wizard but Pyschoduck caught me the monster picked up in a powerbomb position with a quick reversal I managed to hurricanra him down to the mat into a roll up. One… Two… Another kick out by Pyschoduck, feeling the frustration I got to my feet. Just like the greatest of them all I stomped the mat hard with my foot. Like I knew they would the crowd started a great clap shock waving around the arena it spelled the end of Pyschoduck. With my foot high in the air I ran at Pyschoduck but he countered with a corkscrew takedown into a figure four leg lock. The match reaching the thirty minute mark I cried out in pain. Using my hands for leverage I turned the submission on Pyschoduck but with a quick burst of energy he threw his body forward onto the ropes managing to force me to break off the submission. Looking out of the ring I saw the smark from this morning. His eyes where bulging, he started a Jamie Fletcher chant. In fury I rolled out of the ring, I hate it when smarks disrupt the heel and face boundaries. Pulling together all the fluid in my mouth I spat a throat full in the mouth of the young idiot. My mistake I turned there was Pyschoduck flying out of the ring through the middle rope knocking me into the crowd control barrier. This was hardcore as I had gone, I was bleeding from the head, I could feel it, I hated it, holding onto my head the EMT’s came rushing over but Pyschoduck broke them off and threw me into the ring. He grabbed a chair and placed it on me. I couldn’t see him go up top but I knew it was coming so after ten seconds I shimmed to one side accidentally taking the chair with me and Pyschoduck hit the mat hard. By instinct I picked the chair up with me, it came natural to me for some reason. Thrusting the chair high into the air I stood in the middle of the ring waiting for Duck to get up. Banging hard on the mat with the chair I got the crowd behind me, the moment they had waited for Jamie Fletcher was about to get hardcore again. Up he got using the turnbuckle post, lifting the chair high into the air I waited for him to turn. I brought the chair rocketing down with in inches of his head but stopped threw the chair to one swept his feet out from beneath then hit a perfect standing shooting star press. One… Two… Three!!! I couldn’t do it. I’m going to have to overcome this fear to continue in GTT4. Wrestlers will be better than me and I need my cutting edge hardcore attitude to beat them. I’m not as good as Dave Hurst on the mat, Wolfenden in the air or Desolation with power. I must re train myself in hardcore wrestling!!!
