The Equivalent of Poop (IG Promo)

8 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler: Cave Hulk Fed: GLOBAL Things have been weird on the newly created continent of Devonia. The Cave Hulk has felt this weirdness most of all. Now that he is back in his natural habitat, things seem normal rather than the usual chaos that surrounds his life. But things are not always what they seem. The continent of Devonia is as chaotic as anyplace he has ever been in his life. The Kingdom of Rodinia has been attacked by the hellish creatures of the hellish northeastern area of the continent known only as Hell to many. This has turned the entire eastern half of the continent into chaos. And the Hulks are right in the middle of it because they reside in the Hills of Satan region of "Hell," which is right in the middle of the two warring factions. But Cave Hulk is not there. He is on the Devonian Mountain Range. No, this isn't some endurance challenge publicity stunt crap to try and get some attention. Instead, upon hearing news of the war from a band of travelling Desert Nomads that he soon thereafter killed and ate for dinner, he decided to go back to his homeland and help his kind escape. But the road is a rough and trecherous one to undertake. There are many strange and mystical creatures on the mountain road that will have to be dealt with if the time comes to that. Not to mention all the scientist geeks who have travelled to the continent to study all of its lifeforms. Thirty year old men, geeks, who have no idea how to please a woman. Grown men who would rather collect fungus samples than women's phone numbers. Cave Hulk turns around and sees five of the science geeks following him. Cave Hulk turns around and snarls at them. "Nerds," he says with a growl before noticing that one of them is a girl. His eyes widen. She perks her head up and notices his attention. Cave Hulk walks over to her and says, "Wow. Amongst the nerds, I found an actual girl. You have your pick of the litter of guys who have no idea how to please you." She starts laughing as Cave Hulk turns and struts away, waiting for the nerds to catch up as he heads along the mountain trail toward the Hills of Satan. They scamper like flies trying to return to the garbage can before they die. Cave Hulk then senses something with his Hulk powers. He stops and sniffs the air. Then, from out behind a rock, a Giant Poodle leaps out at him! "GARR***" the Poodle yelps out. "You are trespassing on my territory!" The Giant Poodle goes over and pees on one of the science geeks. "AWWWWW!" the geek exclaims in disgust. Cave Hulk smiles. "Well, have you faced anyone whose dumbass handler has lost twelve straight Infinite Gauntlet matches?" he asks with a smirk. The Poodle cocks his head and shakes it as if replying no. "No I haven't," the Poodle says. "But I look forward to pissing on your grave after I bury you in the ground!" "I'd like to see you try Mr. Fluffy!" the Cave Hulk says with a cocky little smile on your face. "You mother fucking non-canine!" the Giant Poodle yells as it froths at the mouth. It's jingly collar shakes as it is engulfed in a rage. "I destroy you like the giant chew toy you are!" The Giant Poodle then charges at the Cave Hulk, but Cave Hulk is ready. He whips out his Club and smashes the Poodle in the head. "Oof!" The Poodle falls to the ground in a heap. Cave Hulk struts over and stands on the Poodle. "Pussies," he says. "Never send a woman to do a man's job." That was an obvious crack at one of his opponents. As many people may or may not know, Cave Hulk is a major sexist. In a Wrestling Observer interview earlier this year, he said that "women are only good for being broken in half by my dick." No truer words have ever been spoken by a single male barbarian since Warren Jeffs took the witness stand two days ago. Cave Hulk continues down the mountain path with his geek followers close behind. The tension in the air is sweltering. It is almost as repugnant as all the other smells, most notably Cave Hulk's fart, which almost kills the geeks the instant the smell hits their noses. The nerds huff, wheeze, and gag as Cave Hulk gets a chuckle out of it. "And they view me as the lesser species," he says with a smirk. "At least I ain't the one with the smell of ass up my nose. BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" Cave Hulk trudges along until they come across another creature blocking the path of the road. "OH MY GOD!" one of the scientist nerds exclaims. "It's...it's...it's..." The true horror of what he's seeing keeps him from getting the words. It is a creature so horrible and ugly, no words can describe how ugly, nasty, and vile this creature truly is. It makes the Cave Hulk look like the sexiest man on Earth, that's how ugly it is. But still, the scientist tries to get the words out but finally succeeds. "IT'S VINCE MCMAHON!" the scientist exclaims in a frightened tone. Cave Hulk looks on in true shock. "What the hell?" he asks. Vince does the McMahon powerwalk of stupidity over to him and pulls down his pants, exposing his ass. "Kiss my ass wrestler! Kiss it!" Vince yells out. Cave Hulk looks petrified beyond belief. "What the hell are you doing you musclebound retard?" he asks in a shocked tone of voice. "Kiss my ass and join the WWE you peasant wrestler!" Vince yells out. Cave Hulk frowns upon hearing this and glares at Vince. "Who you calling a 'peasant wrestler' you fucking idiot?" he asks the billionaire in an angry tone. "Do you realize how stupid your product has become? It's nothing more than your homoerotic, nipple twisting gay wet dream being played out on TV every week so that all of the viewers can tune out over the sheer stupidity of it all." Vince pulls his pants back up. "Looks whose talking 'Mr. Voice of von Doom,'" Vince replies with an angered look on his face. "That retard who you're associated with it comes up with shit so stupid, I wouldn't even air it!" Cave Hulk chuckles. "Squideon has nothing on Jimmy Wayne Chang!" Cave Hulk exclaims, bursting out with hysterical laughter over the Japanese redneck cowboy wrestler set to debut soon on Smackdown. "And TAN ain't got nothing on MVP!" Cave HUlk continues laughing even more, further infuriating the biggest steroid abuser of all the Fortune 500 CEOs. "I'M GONNA KILL YOU!" Vince yells out. He then shoots his hands forward, expecting some WWE pyrotechnics to come out but, that's no happening. He tries it again. "Alacazam!" he yells as he tries to shoot pyro from his fingertips, but nothing comes out. Cave Hulk chuckles to himself. Vince forgot that he is not the Undertaker, Kane, Boogeyman, or God, so he does not have any special WWE powers. Vince gulps as the Cave Hulk strolls over to him casually. "Karma's a bitch, ain't it?" he says before grabbing Vince and throwing him at the Giant Poodle. The Poodle awakens and devours Vince in a bloody feeding frenzy! Jeff Jarret soon jumps out from behind a tree with his guitar. "HA HA HA!" he laughs. "Now TNA is the ultimate wrestling promotion!" Cave Hulk shakes his head. "No it's not," he says. "Yes it is," Jeff replies. "No it's not," Cave Hulk says. "YES IT IS!" Jeff replies angrily. "Then answer me this Mr. King of the Mountain," Cave Hulk says. "Why is it that TNA has gone from innovative wrestling promotion to WCW Lite in less than six months and that you and your friends are the only ones who get booked in the Main Event?" Jarrett snarls and charges at Cave Hulk, trying to hit him with his guitar only to have the Giant Poodle run over and eat him alive in the process! Cave Hulk laughs. "Now I got the stroke, bitch!" The Giant Poodle then runs over to the nearest bush. The Poodle squats and then takes a crap right by the bush. Once done, the Poodle gets up and starts chasing its tail. Cave Hulk then turns to teh nerds and points at the crap behind the bush. "Look!" he exclaims with a devious grin. "Now wrestling encompasses everything from crap to shit." Cave Hulk laughs and then walks off, leaving the nerds dumbfounded. Regardless, the nerds continue to follow him down the mountain trail. Cave Hulk leads them to the end of the trail when a huge black figure hovers over them. "EGAD!" Cave Hulk exclaims. "It's the Black Angel of Death!" The Black Angel of Death stands over them with its arms crossed. "Why do you want to keep the inevitable from happening?" the Black Angel asks him. "Why must the foolish attitude of live and let live be used for everything when it comes to life in general?" Cave Hulk shrugs his shoulders. "How the fuck should I know? I'm only a Hulk." The Black Angel nods its head in agreement. "Yes you are," the Angel replies. "But the end of everything is drawing neigh. Soon, all will be revealed. Soon, the end time will come...for you, all of you." The Angel disappears. Cave Hulk flips off where the Angel just was. "Fucking Jesus freaks," he says in an angry tone. "They always have to ruin everything." Cave Hulk then exits the mountain path with the nerds in tow and then notices something really strange. There's a bright red light shooting into the sky from somewhere off in the distance. It looks like the light from the Luxor in Las Vegas, only red. "What the fuck is that?" Cave Hulk asks, scratching his head. He then shrugs his shoulders, not even giving a crap anymore. "I don't care what it is. I have an Infinite Gauntlet match to lose for the 42nd time." Cave Hulk then walks off the path and into the desert beyond with the nerds. Will he survive? Who knows, but he will lose...that much is for certain. The End.
