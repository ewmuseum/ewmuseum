The Monte Brisas Outlaws: Tag Team Clusterfuck #1

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DJ Affiliated Pimp Joined: 21 Mar 2003 Posts: 3 Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 11:16 am Post subject: The Monte Brisas Outlaws: Tag Team Clusterfuck #1 The scene opens up just outside the arena in Miami. Most superstars have gone home. The car park is near enough empty except for one or two cars. The entrance to the arena door swings open. From behind the door out walks DJ and an unfamiliar face. DJ is wearing a blue shirt and black jeans. He has his bag thrown over his shoulder. The unfamiliar face is talking with DJ. We listen in on the conversation ???: Its ok man. Obviously you will get your hands on them at the pay-per-view. DJ: Yeah I will, But the problem is the PPV is a while away yet. ???: Its gives you longer to get ready for them. DJ: F*ck that! The longer we wait the more hurt the son of a bitches are going to feel. ???: You shouldn’t be talking about them anyway. You were screwed at Anniversary. C.R. pulled an upset win by cheating. He put his feet on the ropes. DJ: Don’t you think I know that. He will pay for his mistakes. Come on Angel hurry up. Just then the door swings open again and out walks Angel holding her neck. She is wearing a white top and a pair of jogging pants. She catches up to the two. DJ: You all right? Angel: Yeah I’m fine. Just my neck is aching a bit ???: At least you didn’t lose though Angel: DJ? Who is this? DJ: An old friend. I saw him the other day and I told him he could come watch a show. ???: Hey I’m Steve, Nice to meet you Angel: (not looking interested) Yeah! DJ: C.R. is going to get what is coming to him. He deserves everything he gets. Yo Steve you got an hotel sorted Steve: Nope. I was just going to look about to see what I could find DJ: There is a couple of rooms going at our hotel. Bit expensive but I’ll lend you it if you don’t got enough Steve: Yeah ok. Stop at the ATM machine and I’ll see what I got. All three of them climb into DJ's Subaru. The camera then opens up in the car. DJ: Buckle up! It’s going to be a crazy ride DJ then switches on the engine and the car moves with lightning speed out of the car park. The camera once again listens in on the conversation inside the car. Steve: So what are you guys up to tonight? Angel: Well I think its back to the hotel, grab a drink and a bite to eat then to the room. It’s been a long night DJ: Yeah same for me too Steve: Damn. I thought you guys would at least make a night of it. Its not every day we come to Miami DJ: Re-phrase that. It isn’t every day you come to Miami. Sit in the hotel bar. Watch some TV. But we are getting food having a drink or two and then we are off to sleep Steve: OK. Pull up there is an ATM machine over there. I hope it takes my card Steve gets out of the car and walks towards the ATM machine. The sign says head left. He turns to the left and disappears behind a bush. Minutes later a man wearing all black crosses the road and heads behind the bush. DJ: That guy looked a bit strange. I’m just going to check on Steve Angel: All right DJ exits the car and creeps up to the bush. Taking a quick look around he tells Angel to phone the police. She does so as DJ slowly disappears behind the bush. A little rough and tumble is heard as Steve comes running round the bush and jumps into the back of the car and ducking down. Angel: Where is DJ? Steve: Ssshhhh he might hear us. Angel now exits the car with the camera following closely behind. We walk behind the bush to see DJ holding down the man that had crossed the road. Police sirens are heard in the background as Steve comes walking around Steve: That will teach you to mess with me Angel: Listen to him. The guy that hid in the back of the car. Steve: I was...errrrr....No....SHUT UP A police car shows up. Two officers exit the vehicle and DJ hands over the man. He is cuffed and placed in the back of the police car. An officer walks over to DJ & Steve Officer: I’m going to need statements from the two of you and possibly the girl. DJ: Not tonight. We will send them to you. Officer: I’m afraid I can’t let you do that. DJ: Why the hell not? Officer: Then that gives you a chance to work on your story. DJ: What the fuck? We didn’t commit any crime. We called you. We stopped it. Why would we want to work on a story? Just then an older looking officer with a granddad mustache walks over Officer 2: That’s fine guys you can go. Fill in these forms and return them to us tomorrow. Officer Johnson do you know who they are? Officer Johnson: No Officer 2: The guy you were giving the lip to is DJ. XWF superstar. The girl he is with is Angel. One of the best females in XWF. And the other guy is.... well i don’t know who the other guy is. Officer Johnson: NO shit. I’m sorry boss. Don’t tell the others Officer 2: (Looks at DJ) Sorry for this. You enjoy your evening now! DJ: Yeah you too. Angel: Can we go now? Steve: Hold up. I still need my money. DJ: Damn it man. OK but this time I’ll take the car around to the machine. He drives to the machine and watches Steve get his money. Steve returns to the car and enters. Steve: I’m a little short. Any chance you could spare me a $100? DJ: A $100 why? Steve: I’m getting pissed tonight. No point drinking and not getting pissed. DJ: You will see what drink does to you one of these days. I have felt it. I was once an alcoholic. The scene then closes as Steve and DJ argue Commercial break. The scene then re-opens outside of The Swipe Hotel in mid Miami. It’s beginning to get dark. The streetlights are now on. The Subaru that DJ was driving pulls into the car park of the hotel and out get DJ, Angel, and Steve. Steve is left carrying all the bags. Obviously he lost the argument. All three strolled into the hotel. The camera follows. A hotel staff member takes the bags from Steve and DJ passes the staff member the keys to his room DJ: I know exactly how much money is in there. The staff member looks embarrassed as everyone around looks. Angel: (laughing) You’re an evil son of a bitch. DJ: I know. Steve heads to the reception desk as DJ and Angel wait. Steve: Man you got a $50. To pay for my room. I only got half DJ: So now you owe me $150. Steve: I’ll pay you back DJ: Yeah you still owe my $13 from school. Steve: Can I have it please? DJ gets out his wallet and hands over another $50. Steve collects his keys and the three make their way into the restaurant. They are taking to a table and they all sit DJ: Man I’m starving. Lets order. DJ raises his arm to catch the attention of a waiter. A waiter is at the table moments later Waiter: (in Italian accent) Can I help you zer sir? DJ: Yeah I would like to order. I’ll have the special of the day. Waiter: What would you like Madam? Angel: I’ll have the same. Steve: Well I might as well have it too. Waiter: OK 3 specials. The waiter walks off. DJ and Angel begin chatting about the night’s actions. Angel: This has been one bad night. First off I lose consciousness and El Locon picks up a so-called victory. C.R. cheats you in a match. Then to top it off, your goof friend Steve here nearly gets robbed DJ: Yeah! Well next week will be different. I assure you of that. Actually after dinner if you don’t mind I’m going to cut a promo as the PTC cameras have followed us from the arena to here. Raven is not present at this moment but he will be at New Era there’s no doubt about that because we are the Future and the Future is NOW. Angel: Yeah ok. Steve: Can I watch you cut a promo. DJ & Angel: NO Steve: OK calm down I was only asking. A different waiter then arrives holding 3 plates and places one each in front of the 3. The scene then fades as all three begin eating. Commercial Break
