Freedom Bound

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 PRELUDE The last time we saw Dani Furher, he had been training with his long time trainer and best friend Rhett Mcnamara. After a single blow that knocked Mcnamara Unconscious , Dani was arrested on charges of manslaughter. Things only got worse though once Dani found out that his attorney was his worst enemy and the same man whom had imprisoned Dani’s own father, a man whom also was destined to have Dani out of the first round of the Global Turnbuckle Tournament by the first round. His actions did not prevail though as Dani continued on with the match against Hoyt Williams despite the odds and walked out with the victory moving on to the second round. Now, recently, Dani was released from prison until his trial date reached. But now that he’s out, only more trouble awaits as he gets set to face the man who pulled out maybe one of if not the biggest upset of round one by defeating Tessa Windsor. Dani had began to reach a point in his life where even he began to doubt himself. [ " Controversy " ] The car skimmed by in a vague impression The Black exterior of the Porsche sped through the looming shadows of the night. Bright Yellow lights illuminated, so as the glowing moon, all wrapping together around the blazing black porsche. Cars rushed by causing a swish sound to occur with every passing moment. The windows to this luxury car were tented a dark shade of black, refusing to let any of beam, nor sight for that matter, to enter the interior of the vehicle. Inside we see a man, skin engaged in a hybrid mix of light Brown and Tan. In Contrast, He sported a Black Sleeveless Muscle Shirt, Black Leather pants, black tennis shoes, and short black hair cropped up in a ponytail formation. The figure by identification is none other than the Apostle himself...Dani Furher. Furher continued down the road, seeming to drive without a single care in the world. By the expression portrayed on his face...he seemed to be focused on reaching a destination unknown to mankind. By the look of his eye...he seemed to be in a state of anger...and disbelief. With the recent controversies taking their toll within his already torn life...its no wonder to some that he doesn't smile on a daily occasion. But this time, he was in a mood that had never before been seen. A Mood that portrayed him as a man who had no friends...but only enemies to call his own. Though at certain extents this theory was fact...it didn't serve as a valid excuse towards these allegations. Nonetheless, his mission, whatever it may have been, resumed as he slowly turned the street corner of Wilson View Lane. His Location...Orange, Texas...a location that didn't serve as much of a home to Furher, but as his current residence. We witness a dark road that furher has just turned into. Nothing but tall jumbled trees surround this dark road, but the headlights of the Porsche illuminate it enough so that it is visible. As Furher is smoothly driving he hits a bump in the road...not just any kind of bump though, this bump seemed to have deformed figures in it. Furher stopped the car and quickly jumped out. He wen't to the front of the car and looked under to see a body laying there. A Dead Corpse. Furher began pulling the body out from under the car.Only the arm laid under the car so it wasn't very hard to do. Furher couldn't believe his eyes as he knelt down beside the body and began to slap it a few times trying to make sure the body was dead. He then placed his hand over its heart and felt no pulse...he knew for sure that t he body was dead, but he also knew that he couldn't have been the person whom killed it. Furher quickly grabbed his cell phone from his back pocket as he quickly dialed " 911 " for help and to report the finding. " Yes...I have found a ded body on Wilson View Lane. No, I did not run it over, I was just driving and saw it in the middle of the road. Thank You. " He hung up the Cell phone and slid it back into his back pocket. He began to pace around for a bit waiting for the police, but as he did, random thoughts ran rapid throughout his head. How was he positive that HE was not the reason for this man's death. How would he handle explaining this to the police. But the most important...could he handle another " Death " case. He had just been released on the supposed death of his trainer Rhett Mcnamara, simply because there was not much evidecence and he could not be sentenced to jail time until the trial, but this would not help his case any. It's not everyday that you deal with two deaths like this...he knew that, and the police knew that. Furher was in a hole...that even himself wasn't sure he could dig out of. [ " Truth " ] Detective Ben Clarington was a fairly hefty man with a pleasurable, solemn face, a bulky walrus mustache, and a new gravity in his gaze and bearing. He was one of if not the top detectives in Philadelphia. He was a sure veteran, being a member of the team for 21 years and counting, during which time he investigated several different murders. Murders that ranged from husband's killing wives, wives killing husbands, and the poor killing one another...all in influenced motives such as money, envy, obsession, and even love. No matter how you put it, Blarington knew murder and its unchanging template. " Guys...next up we've got a pro wrestler who was just charged on the killing of a man by the name of Rhett Mcnamara. We have now found some evidence that he is the killer of the body found in the middle of the road on yesterday evening. Guards...bring him in. " The door in the room opened up as in walked Dani Furher, handcuffed in chains as he sat down in a chair sitting in front a long table. Detective Ben Clarington slowly paced the room back and forth as Dani sat there...nervously, with a guard on his left and his right. He didn't know what to think. He knew that he was innocent on both charges...but the hard part about it was trying to convice the others that he was. " So Furher. I see last time you were here, you were here on a charge of manslaughter to Rhett Mcnamara, and now you're back again...how do you plead your case. " Furher looked deep into the eyes of the Detective without saying a word. He slowly glanced to his right to see a big security guard staring down at him with an evil eye, and then to his left to see the same. It was a nervous state to be in, that was certain...but Furher had become accustomed to these types of things. He now had to dig himself out of yet another hole...one so deep that even he doubted himself. " Simply Put...I didn't do it. " " Hehe...so we've got a smart guy on our hands here. So you're telling me...though the body was found BY you on a dark lonely street, and though your fingerprints were located on the body...that, I'm supposed to believe that you didn't commit the crime? " " Believe what you want to believe...I know for a FACT that I did not do it...and I know also for a fact that truth always prevails...and that is the truth. " " I've delt with guys like you before...guys who think that they are little angels..and every single one of them are in jail as of today because of me. You're nothing special Furher, confess now and save yourself the embarassment and even more difficult punishment. " " I'm not going to confess you something that I didn't do...thats final. " Clarington sighed and shook his head at Furher as if saying that he showed nothing but the sign of a naive and arrogant little brat. Furher had formed a look of anger in his face...a look that defined him as a man who was on the verge of reaching point break. Clarington closed the files lying on the table and then drew them up under his arm as he spoke to Furher. " Very well then...but before you make that your final answer, just remember one thing...your sentencing has two options. The first being life in prison...but that has already been stripped away from you. So that leaves only one option...Death. " A quick smirk shifted on the face of Clarington as he turned his head and began to make his way out of the door. Meanwhile Furher looked at him as if he wanted to jump out of the chair and attack him on the spot, but that would only lead to more problems. Dani dropped his head down as in a state of pure terminal illness. He was so tired of all the drama, that he wanted to kill himself...but he knew deep down inside that he had a legacy to carry forth, and that he had to carry that legacy...no matter what. [ " Black Maill " ] Furher sat in his cell at the Moyamensing Prison, a large manic building at Tenth and Reed Streets, in south Philadelphia.The cell measured in at eight by thirteen feet of white washed walls and stone construction that admitted the relentless heat for which Philadelphia was Infamous for, to enter the cell sticking to Dani and his fellow prisoners like a cloak of moist wool. He did not seemed to be at all disturbed with the feeling of humiliation. If anything, he was joyous of the fact that no one had provided concrete evidence to the killing of the unknown body. But as he sat there, and took a trip down memory lane...his emotions seemed to take even more of a down syndrome. In his Cell was a Oak Wooden table at the far end, as well as a single twin bed. Furher sat at the Table as he turned on the lamp, opened up a notebook and began to jot down some words. He had become a fan of writing as of late and it had become a hobby that he took enjoyment in. But what he wrote was more of something to rid of his soul of the torment...rather than to please others. Im steady trying to find a motive, why I do what i do, freedom aint gettin no closer, no matter how far i go, my car is stolen, no registration, cops partolin, and now they done stop me, And i get locked up He placed the pen down and walked over to the twin bed in where he sat on, cupping his head in the palms of his hands. He began to concentrate on recent events...concentrating on the two murders, as well as the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament where he had advanced to the second round with an upset over a projected winner of the entire tournament in Hoyt Williams. His concentration, though, was interrupted with a banging of the metal bars in his cell. " Hey Furher...you have a visitor" With that being said, Furher looked up to see a police officer opening up the door to his cell. He coudln't believe what he was hearing...a visitor...wanting to visit him? Last time he checked, all of his family were left in Bogota, Columbia..and he had made no friends, noe who would consider visiting him in prison at least. Nonetheless though, he stood and walked out of the cell following the police officer. They continued down the long hallway, passing the cells of other prisoners whom looked on...some even grunting at the two. Finally after a while of walking, they reached a door in which the officer opened and they both stepped inside. It was a small room that was split in two by a Glass Wall. Furher looked into the glass at the other side of the room to see a man of whom he could not identify on first glance. He took a seat in the chair and continued to stare at the man on ther other side, still without a clue in the world to whom he was. " I'm going to give the two of you some privacy, but I will be standing outside of the door. " The police officer then exited the room as Furhed looked at the man whom wore a Black suit, lined by a grey undershirt and black shoes. His hair was thick and slicked back with his goatee carefully groomed. Finally the man spoke up. " Dani...you may not recognize me, and I really don't expect you too..but my name is Don Riley. " " Okay...and why do you wan't to see me ? " " I hear you found a dead body in the middle of the street and was arrested and accused of murder. Well I am here to tell you a little secret that you must promise to keep between the two of us..only. " " And whats that. " "...that body that you found...I killed it. " Dani couldn't believe his ears. Here he was, sitting in a cell serving time over something that he never did when the real guilty party was sitting right there in front of his very eyes, admitting to the crime. He looked like he wanted to shatter the glass and strangle the guy..but he remained calm. " Thanks for telling me...now I an turn you in and get myself out of this mess. " " Not so fast there. We made a promise remember? " " That doesn't mean squat to me right now. " " Well maybe it will once I tell you this. You can go ahead and squeel on me...but if you do, your Daughter who's in college at the university of Wyoming...and your wife who is currently in New York for the National Esquire Convention...they will both come home to see Daddy in the same position as that body that you found....dead. " " You know...a few weeks, maybe even a few days...that would've scared me half to death, but it looks as if I'm going to be killed anyways...so frankly...it doesn't even matter. " " Okay...but what if I told you that your Wife and your Daughter would be joining you...because if you do decide to Squeel, I'll kill them a.. " Furher interrupted the statement by jumping out of his chair and at the window but the construction of the window was built where he could not break it. He banged on the window with fury as The Police Officer rushed into the room and wrestled Furher down to the ground. He then picked up Furher and began to pull and force him out of the door. Before Furher exited though...one last word was heard from Riley.. " Don't Forget what I told you Furher...you squeel...and I'll Kill. " To Be Continued
