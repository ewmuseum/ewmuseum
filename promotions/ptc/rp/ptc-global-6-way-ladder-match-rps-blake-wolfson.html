21 April 2004 15 Years Ago The scene opens up on a bright, sunny day. The blue sky is abundant, and there is hardly a cloud to be seen. The camera pans downward in a vertical motion, revealing a spacious and open countryside. The dirt from the sandy road whooshes past, as the trees in the background sway from side to side, causing a crisp wind to filter through the air. We now pan to the left revealing a large country house dwelling, complete with a large verandah surrounding the front of the house. The door to the house swings open, as a middle-aged man in his forties steps out onto the verandah. He proceeds to walk around the side of the house, as he disappears momentarily, before reappearing with a large blue ladder. The man unfolds the ladder and props it up against the roof of the house, before slowly starting to ascend the rungs all the way to the top. He climbs onto the roof, finally propelling himself up to stand on his own two feet. He turns around, facing the grass in the front yard some twelve feet below. The camera zooms in, as the man closes his eyes and starts to cry uncontrollably, sobbing quite loudly as he does so. The man tries to control himself by taking a deep breath as he makes the sign of the cross, indicating his Christianity. Suddenly a faint voice can be heard, but it's not the voice of the man standing on the roof. Voice: Dad? The man looks down, as the front door to the house swings open. Out steps a young boy of no more than eight years in age, dressed in his red pajamas. Boy: Dad? Are you out here? Father: Go away son! Go back inside! The boy hears the voice as he carefully walks down the stairs to the lawn, as he turns around to see his Dad standing high up on the roof. Boy: What are you doing out there Dad? Father: Son, it's very important that you go inside right now. The man's tone of voice is raised, as he starts to yell in an agitated manner. Father: Do you hear me?! Boy: What are you doing? Father: Something I should have done months ago. Boy: Dad... Father: I love you son. Always remember that. I would do anything for you. Boy: Then get down! Father: I can't do that son. Ever since your mother left... Boy: What are you talking about? We've been fine! We've been coping! Father: Blake... The father looks down at his son, now revealed to be Blake. Father: This is something I have to do. You'll go back to the city to live with your mother and you'll be back with your old friends again. It's the best for everyone. Blake: How could you be so selfish?! GET DOWN!! Blake starts to cry, as tears stream down his young cheeks. Father: I'm not doing anything with you here, so go inside! NOW!! Blake: I'm not mov... Suddenly, Blake's father slips on one of the roof tiles, as he doubles over. He sways his arms backwards to try and stop him from falling over the edge, but it's too late. Blake: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!! In slow motion, Blake's father topples over the edge. He hurtles towards the ground, still swinging his arms as if to try and fly, even though it's a fruitless proposition. Down... Down... Down... Splat. Blake: DAD!! Blake runs over to his Dad, who is stretched out on the ground, with a pool of blood emanating from his mouth. Blake bends over and puts his head on his father's chest, as he starts to sob loudly. Suddenly the screen goes completely black from the outside to the inside. The camera zooms out, revealing a television which has been the focus of the past few minutes. The camera zooms out once more, revealing Blake Wolfson sitting on a couch. Blake: HAH! I love that movie........ The piss-taking Blake Wolfson puts down the remote on the floor, as he smiles from ear to ear. Voice: What crap are you watching now? Blake and the camera turns around, revealing a short balding man with black-rimmed glasses sitting at a large mahogany desk in the corner of the room. Blake: Oh, just some flick. Who would've thought that the main character had the same name as me! I'm telling you, those Hollywood fuckers are trying to imitate me at all costs! Blake grins sheepishly, as he gets to his feet. The camera pans back to the man at the table, who just so happens to be Howard Briggs, Blake Wolfson's personal assistant/lawyer/agent or any other role Blake can think of at that particular time. He shakes his head from side to side, as an ever-present sneer surfaces across his face. Described as a miserable bastard, Howard is ruthless and intractable, which perhaps explains why he aligned himself with Wolfson in the first place. A thunderous knock at the door can be heard, as Blake strides over to the entranceway, peeping through the eye-hole before eventually opening the door. A short Hispanic man dressed in a red uniform is standing in the doorway, with a large tray in front of him. Blake: About friggin' time, I don't pay the hotel a thousand dollars a night to wait half an hour for a simple cup of coffee! The staffer nods, seemingly intimidated by Blake's annoyed demeanor. Blake lifts up the tray, revealing a steaming cup of coffee. He intricately picks up the cup, sipping it carefully, tossing around the liquid inside his mouth to test if it's up to his standards. After a few seconds he spits out the coffee, staining the white shaggy carpet. Blake: What is this?! Staffer: I... uh... coffee? Blake: SILENCE! You insolent fool! This is a double skim cappuccino with a burst of mocha. I asked for a skim cappuccino with a burst of double mocha! What's your name? Staffer: Brian. Blake: Sorry, was that Eduardo? Brian: N... Blake: Right, Eduardo it is. I want you to go back down to the kitchen and get me a skim cappuccino with a burst of double mocha, do you hear me?! Brian: Yes sir. The man promptly wheels the cart out of the room, obviously trying to get out of there as quickly as possible. Blake: And while you're... HEY!! Come back here, I'm not finished demeaning you yet! The door slams shut, as Blake shakes his head in disgust. Blake: Bloody foreigners. Blake smirks at the hypocrisy of it all, considering he too is a foreigner. His Australian accent is still quite noticeable, especially considering he has a penchant for combining two words into one at every given opportunity. He is the self-proclaimed World's Greatest Movie Star, even though he's only been in one Hollywood blockbuster production thus far. Normally known as a short-tempered and demanding person, Blake Wolfson is hated by many, mainly due to his arrogant and self-serving nature. Blake turns to his personal assistant, addressing him in a blunt manner. Blake: This sucks. Howard: What? Blake: The wait. Howard: It's only a cup of coffee, don't be so goddamn impatient. Blake smirks at the irony. Blake: You can talk. But no, I'm not referring to that. I meant the wait for this match... that's what sucks. Do you realize how long I've been waiting for my Global Title shot? Howard takes off his glasses, as he delicately starts to chew one side of the frame. He realizes Blake's tendency to ignore him, so he usually just enters the stupidest of responses to see if he can catch him out. More often than not, it works too. Howard: Red. Blake: Exactly, it's been too long! It's been 64 days, 7 hours and... Blake glances down at his watch. Blake: 53 seconds. Make that 51. 50. 4... well, you get the picture. Not that I'm counting or anything, of course. Blake gets to his feet and walks over to the bay window on the left hand side of the room, staring at the large mountainous valley overlooking the hotel. Howard continues to ruffle his papers, looking busy as always. Blake: You know what really gets me, even more so than the wait? There's a long pause, as Howard is unresponsive. Blake: Thanks for your interest. What gets me is that I heard a rumor that some of my opponents don't think I'm even worthy of this shot! I mean what kind of outrageous accusation is that?! I mean to think of everything that I've achieved over the years. For example, I was the first ever XUW Triple Crown Champion! Howard: Right, but you only won the World Title by cheating... Blake ignores Howard, and this trend continues. Blake: Not to mention that I've won Stable Wars in back-to-back years! Howard: But only because you were a last minute replacement for the Army of Darkness in the last match and you got pinned after two minutes... Blake: Let's not forget that I'm undefeated in Ladder matches, which just so happens to be this month's specialty! Howard: Even though you've only competed in two, one of which was two years ago, and the last one was three days ago and it involved two midgets which you easily disposed of... Blake: Then of course we have to remember my finest achievement, starring in the Hollywood blockbuster "Holy Crap, I Married A Douchebag!" last year with Ben Affleck and Jennifer Lopez! Howard: Despite the fact that you were only in it for thirty seconds and had one line... Blake turns to Howard. Blake: Why is it that you only have something to say when it's to criticize me? Howard: Because it's fun? Blake: Remind me again, who pays your salary? Howard: Bah. Blake: Yeah, that's right, you bah. Howard frowns, as he goes back to his paperwork. A loud knock at the door can be heard again. Blake: Thank God, that must be Eduardo. Blake strides over towards the door, as he sneaks another look through the peep-hole, jumping back alarmingly as he does so. Blake: What the... Blake opens the door, and there stands a figure dressed in a black bed sheet and a purple mask. Figure: RAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAWR! Blake immediately slams the door shut. He turns around to Howard, who once again isn't paying attention. Blake blinks as if he's trying to clear the vision out of his mind, as he turns back to the door and opens it up again. The strange, brooding figure is standing at the doorway again, as Blake just stands there, scratching his head. Figure: Ooooooh boogie boogie! The figure raises his arms as if to scare Wolfson, but Blake cocks his head to the side, looking quite perplexed. Blake: ........ Yes? Figure: Sheesh. The figure takes off his mask, revealing it to be one Scotty Spalzcutoff, who is a familiar face to XUW viewers. Scotty is an interesting specimen; interesting in that he's a complete lunatic. He used to be his former best friend’s interviewer, but after that relationship went sour he latched himself onto Blake like a bad smell. No matter how hard Blake tries to get rid of him by ridiculing him at all costs, Scotty never takes anything to heart. He's a good kid at heart and means well, but is always misunderstood, mainly due to him being a simpleton. Bless his little cotton socks off. Scotty holds out his hands as he smiles from ear to ear. Scotty: SURPRISE!! Blake: What the hell are you doing here? Scotty: I'm renting the room next door, remember? A lightbulb goes off inside of Wolfson's head, as he nods. Blake: Oh, right. But why are you dressed up like that? Scotty: Didn't you recognize me? Blake: If I did, would I be asking why you're dressed up like that? Scotty: Fair point. I'm Minion, the Imp! Blake: Who with the what now? Blake cocks an eyebrow. Scotty: Don't you know anything? This is Hessian's sidekick, Minion the Imp! Hello? Hessian? Freaking monster who could kill you next week? Ring any bells?! Blake: Oh, right, I've heard about him before. Wait... how did you know that?! Scotty: I know everything! Blake: No, really. Scotty: OK, well I stole this costume for Halloween last year and I came across his picture in some wrestling magazine you had, and I put two and two together to make five. Blake: See? That's much more plausible. But I'm impressed Scotty, that means you've done your research. Scotty: Yup... well, you know what they say! There's a long awkward pause, as both Scotty and Blake's eyes dart around the room. Blake: Yes? Scotty: I don't know, I was hoping that you'd take it from there. Blake: ... Anyway, we know who you're dressed up as, but why exactly? Just for fun? Scotty: Oh no, I have a plan! It'll never fail! Blake: The last time you said that I ended up in hospital needing an enema, but that's another story. I'm afraid to say it, but go on. Scotty: No, but this one is foolproof. I use this costume to disguise myself as Minion the Imp, I infiltrate Hessian's headquarters, gain his trust... and then BAM! I'LL KILL HIM!! THE GLOBAL TITLE WILL BE YOURS FOR THE TAKING!! Blake: ................ Blake pauses, as he tries to think about the logistics of this plan. Scotty: No? Blake: I'd say not. Nice try though, retard. Scotty: HEY!! Mom always said that retard wasn't a nice word. I prefer brainiacally challenged. Blake: Brainiacally isn't even a word. Scotty: I rest my case! The scene freezes, as a large block of writing flashes up on the screen. Scotty Spalzcutoff Logic: 1. Oxymoronic; defying natural logic in the first place. 2. Lack of common sense and brain power. 3: Also see: stupidity. The scene returns to normal, as Blake rests his head in the palm of his hands. He just sits there looking absolutely confused, resigning himself to Scotty's absurdity. Scotty: Right, time for phase two of my plan! Blake: Phase two? Blake's voice has just a hint of cynicism in it. Scotty: Yep. Wait right here! Scotty runs out of the room, as Blake glances over at Howard. Howard: Can you just punch him in the face? Because if you don't, I will. Blake: Oh let's just humor him. Somebody has to... Scotty runs back in the room, dragging a ventilator with him. Blake cocks his head to the side once more. Blake: What on earth? Scotty: It's a ventilator! Blake: Thanks, scoop. But why? Scotty: Because next week, you're facing the fittest man alive! ALIVE I SAY!! Blake: Who?! Scotty: Who do you think?! It's Iron Lung! Statistics conclude that Iron Lung's operate 24% more efficiently than the normal human lung, so we need to get you up to tip top cardiovascular shape so that you can compete on his level! He'll be so fit that he'll easily be able to outlast you in your current condition, but a couple of hours on this baby for a few days, and you'll be as fit as him in no time! Blake: Scotty, he... I... uh... no. Scotty: Hm... okay, well how about we go over to Todd Dream's house, wait till he's asleep, and then... wait for it... GET INSIDE HIS DREAMS!! He won't stand a chance then, people are always vulnerable while they're asleep! Blake sits down on the couch, burying his head in his hands once more. Scotty: Or... or... we could go and visit Josh and use his own tactics on him... BY GETTING MOODY! As in angry! As in upset! As in... OOOOH! I GOT IT! We could go and visit Jesse Jamester and make fun of him for having a girl's name and...... man these ideas are just getting worse and worse aren't they? Blake looks at the hyperactive Scotty, as he gets to his feet and calmly places an arm across his right shoulder. Blake: Scotty, I can be frank with you, can't I? Scotty: I thought your name was Blake, but sure, go for it. Blake: You're a twat. Scotty glances down at the floor, looking a little depressed, until he looks right back at Wolfson. Scotty: I've heard that so many times, I'm going to take that as a compliment! Blake: I give up. Scotty, I appreciate your efforts, but I have a really important match to prepare for, and no ventilators or purple masks will be able to help me do that. So I thank you for your thoughts, but I really do have to get going. So if you don't mind... Scotty: Hey, I understand... OH! Wait, I meant to give you something! Scotty reaches into his jacket pocket, as he pulls out a cup of coffee, complete with take-away lid of course. Scotty: Some jibberer named Brian wanted me to give it to you. Blake: Oh, cheers. Blake takes the cup from Scotty as he peels off the lid, before sampling the beverage. Blake shrivels his face up in disgust at the taste. Blake: Bloody hell, now it's cold! Blake glares at Scotty, the obvious cause for its lack of heat. Scotty: It's cold? Pffft... who's ever heard of cold coffee! This hotel should be shut down! Well, on that note... gotta go! Scotty flees out of the room, slamming the door shut behind him, as Blake turns to Howard. Blake: After that, I'm physically and mentally exhausted. Howard: Well that's great to hear, considering the toughest physical challenge you've ever faced is right around the corner. Just another excuse for when you lose, eh? Howard sneers in Wolfson's direction, as Blake knows too well that it wasn't a jibe, but more of a motivational tool. Blake sighs as he turns back to face the window that overlooks the mountainous views, pondering a multitude of thoughts that flow through his mind. Can he do the impossible and win the Global Title, of which no one is picking him to do? If you knew him, you'd know that answer. When everyone is discounting him... that's when he thrives. When his back is to the wall... that's when he's in his element. That's when he's Blake Wolfson. ---- 25 April 2004 The following advertisement was paid for by the Blake Wolfson Party. People are flowing through the lobby of the lavish building at a rapid pace, both business men and business women alike scurrying to different parts of the building. We pan through the marbled floors of the lobby, revealing a waiting room section in the far corner of the building. Lines of plastic chairs are filled with smartly dressed people who are minding their own business, glancing over sheets of paper in a seemingly flustered manner. In the corner of the waiting room are two familiar figures, Blake Wolfson and his personal assistant, Howard Briggs. The pair are impeccably dressed as always, you have to be when you're in the movie industry. Blake is rifling through a set of sheets, looking rather anxious as he does so. Blake: Shit, I lost it. Howard: What now? Blake: My lines, I can't find my bloody lines! Blake's voice is raised, which also raises the ire of many of the people sitting in the waiting room who are trying to patiently concentrate. It's apparent that Blake is at a movie audition, a regular occurrence for him after making his debut acting performance two months ago. Howard rolls his eyes at Blake's tense mood, as he tries to interject himself in-between Wolfson's panic attacks. Howard: Calm the frick down already. You're in a bother. You can own this audition, you know that. Blake: I knew I shouldn't have come here! Blake continues filing through the papers in a bother, as Howard attempts to calm him down once more. Howard: I don’t mean to be rude, but... Blake: Well if you don’t mean to be rude, then don’t be! Howard: Well it’s just that you're definitely not going to get the part with that attitude. Blake: Oh, not this whole spiel again. Y'know, I should never have let you talk me into auditioning for this movie. Howard: You're a movie star, that's what you do. Blake: Yes, I know. But just a week out from the biggest match of my life? Howard: Oh you've been stressing about that stupid match for the last few weeks, you could do with some distraction. Blake: Maybe so, but you only told me about this audition this morning! Howard: Bah, a bit of spontaneity never hurt anyone. Blake continues to find the point that he's looking for in his script, as he ignores Howard. He realizes that Howard is only doing this for the almighty dollar. Blake knows that Howard is a money-grabbing blood-sucking leech, but again, that's why he signed him up to take care of his business affairs in the first place. While Howard tries to get Blake as many acting gigs as possible, it's obvious that Howard doesn't give the same kind of attention and focus to Blake's wrestling career. As far as Howard is concerned, wrestling always comes secondary, and that's evident by him booking Blake in an audition just a few days away from the epic Global Title match. Blake had been thinking about this match for quite some time, after finding out that he'd be one of the six participants battling it out for the biggest prize in Primetime Central. Who wouldn't worry about it? Who wouldn't focus their every breathing minute on it? If any wrestler answered no to the previous questions, they're not in the wrestling business for the right purpose. Instead, they're in it for mediocrity, something which Wolfson doesn't know the meaning of... or rather, something he THINKS he doesn't know the meaning of. Whilst Blake is scrambling through a last minute script revision, an attractive young woman dressed in a white blouse and short black skirt enters the area where the potential actors are seated, glancing down at her clipboard to read off the next name. Woman: Blake Wolfson? Blake glances up directly at the woman with a look of nervousness, as he gets to his feet. Without saying another word, the woman motions for him to follow her, as she turns around and heads down an adjacent corridor. Blake turns to Howard, with a look of resignation surfacing across his face. Blake: I hate you for this. Howard: That's the spirit, go get 'em! Howard flashes a semblance of a grin, as Wolfson walks off in a disjointed manner, following the woman towards the audition room. A door opens in a spacious lounge area, as the woman re-appears and points in the room, motioning for Wolfson to enter. As soon as he does, the woman disappears and shuts the door behind her. Blake smiles half-heartedly as he proceeds to walk over to a panel of three men who are seated behind a black table. He shakes their hands nervously, as they motion for him to take a seat opposite them. Blake takes a look at their nametags as he addresses the trio. Blake: Marty, John, Al... thanks for having me to audition. The middle of the three speaks up, in a low and raspy tone, as if he's smoked too many cigarettes. John: Thanks for coming. John: What on earth are you talking about? How dare you use that language in front of a child! Blake: Huh? Blake blinks, as he now notices that the two people who entered the room are not Hessian or his Imp, but rather a seven foot giant and a dwarf, both of whom are two actors which Wolfson has never seen before in his entire life.
