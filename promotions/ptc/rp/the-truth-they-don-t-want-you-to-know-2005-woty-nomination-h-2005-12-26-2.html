The Truth "They" Don't Want You To Know (2005 WOTY Nomination Hype)

26 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has come to our attention that there are those blind enough to believe that in the measure of achievements for 2005, Karina Wolfenden has Angelo Deville beat. We can see how an argument can be made for that . . . from ignorance. Let’s take this step by step, shall we? Late in her tenure, she became PRIME’s Pulse Champion. Immediately upon entering the federation, Deville was deemed far, far above wrestling for such a low-tier title. Everyone knows he could have won it, or could win it, but why would he bother? That would be like The Rock returning and going after the European Title. Karina: -1 Angelo: 0 She won the Universal Championship. Deville never competed for it. Alright, that would be a point for her . . . except the champion she defeated was Boda. Yeah, that Boda. Yeah. She jobbed the title a whole week later to Killean Sirrajin. Karina: -1 Angelo: 0 She was one (the bigger) half of PRIME’s Tag Team Champions. Deville was one (the bigger) half of PRIME’s Tag Team Champions. Karina: 1 Angelo: 1 She was ranked #1 by The Mark. Deville was ranked #2 for PTC’s Wrestler of the Month; he would have been number one, except The Uber Judge single-handedly made the Infinite Gauntlet something special during that time. The Mark was ignorant – look at the rest of the list; Karina received the “award” because of how much she whored out for promotional appearances and because the compilation method was to take an indiscriminate cross-section of marks and ask their uninformed opinion. Karina: 0.5 Angelo: 1 She won PTC’s Seven of the Best. Deville would have won it, but there was only allowed one participant from each federation, and Karina beat Angelo to the punch in demanding the spot. Still, if you examine the event, The Deville was a major factor in the K-Wolf’s success. Karina: 1 Angelo: 0.5 She won the Dual Halo. It was Deville’s debut match, and he still placed third. Karina: 1 Angelo: 0 She was voted PRIME’s Wrestler of the Year. Deville was a close runner-up in the voting, despite only having been in the federation for a few months. Karina: 1 Angelo: 0.5 She became PTC’s Global Champion. Angelo never got to compete for it. This would be another impressive feat, but she won it from a crippled man she knew beforehand was crippled. Then, before she could have a decent match with it, she forfeited it, then refused to compete for it again. All she wanted it for was to list it as an accomplishment. Show of hands for who thinks The Deville would’ve been that shameful, despicable, and cowardly. Karina: -1 Angelo: 0 She was Wrestler of the Week five times in PRIME. Deville, in far less time, was Wrestler of the Week three times. Karina: 5 Angelo: 3 Karina equalled the PRIME winning streak record of ten consecutive victories. Deville came one match short. Karina’s tenth victory was over Boda. Yeah, that Boda. Yeah. You can’t even pretend that if Deville was handed Boda for number ten, there wouldn’t be a third member tied. Also, Karina was one of Deville’s victims, and the rest read like a who’s who list of PRIME talent, including the then-reigning Universal Champion Hoyt Williams. As a matter of fact, Deville has yet to lose a non-cluster match in PRIME. So if we’re going by pure wrestling match formula, Deville has won sixteen contests in a row. Karina: 1 Angelo: 1 Deville was voted a close runner-up for Character of the Year. Karina? Pfft. The outright winner was Hoyt Williams. Karina: 0 Angelo: 0.5 Karina was known for little more than being an angst-ridden Avril Lavigne ripoff. Deville brought you such classics as “Look at the Dog” and the nickname “Riverdance” for Brandon Youngblood because of how much he tapped, he poisoned Angelus (another Mexican) with a rabid Chihuahua while pretending to merely be mocking Mexican culture through Taco Bell, he . . . shit, just go check out any PRIME show and you can add to the list yourself. Karina: -1 Angelo: 1 Karina was too busy snowboarding to join GTT5. Deville was called out by the reigning Global Champion Rich Rollins, in what has been dubbed a Match of the Year candidate. Despite losing, Deville stole his way back into the tournament. Karina: 0 Angelo: 1 Now that we can look at this clearly, let’s tally up the points… Karina: 7.5 Angelo: 9.5 On accolades alone it's fairly even. Consider intangible qualities and the choice becomes fairly obvious. Add in pwnful campaigning, and, well, it’s not even close.
