IG45 Trash Talk Open Invitation thread (all vs Chris Storm)

7 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wow, just wow... Questo stupisce appena... They say that imitation is the highest form of flattery, but the dude who spat that tripe - he never ran across you fuckin' clowns. Seriously, how long has it been since I stepped foot in a ring around here? Sixty years? I dunno, 'tween kickin' my saggin' chest when I run, and vitamin B-and-vodka shots, my memory's shot to hell. But you kids still act like beatin' a buck n' a quarter kickfightin' cruiser in a wrestlin' match half a decade ago is somethin' worthy of a zipper ripper? First of all, Christian, remember the conditions in which we fought. Out of the game for half a year, busted-ass knee... May as well been dude-checkin' down Cally Bane's drawers; it was a sure thing. Secondly, who gives a damn? What was I ever in this sport? No credible tournament wins, unless ya count that tag team thing I did with Cally and ended up droppin' when I found out what a hose-beast it was. Wait... I did get a little recognition a few times. All those wrassler of the week awards, and that tiny of the year thing... I did shut down the DWO single-handedly, had the CWMC won, but gave it to to the fans to plaster that kissy-faced devil - okay, okay, maybe bragging about beating me is worthy, but that many years ago? C'mon dude! Is there no one else around worth boastin' over in the newer days than me? You're still around - you've definitely beat some bigger names recently, right? Something about a long tag team reign, yadda yadda... Hell, man, I dunno. Grow up? I, for one, kid, congratulate ya on the success. Don't blow it all on someone who ain't comin' back. Anyways, man. In the spirit of old times - blow a goat and go to hell, you blonde-backed ape. I'd tune in to see how well you defend this thing, but I'm too busy being filthy fuckin' rich. Now, what's this I heard about Tess goin' gay? I'm not, but I read this little thing about an experimentin' excuse? .....
