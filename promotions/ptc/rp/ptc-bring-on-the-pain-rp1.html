Bring on the Pain (Rp1)

20 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[No. No one ever thinks about “Insane” Chris West doing anything in this sport, from winning titles here and there. Busting heads wide open for a living. Still no one believes in me, or gives me a chance at anything. Everything I do is about proving people wrong. Ever since I was a Little Kid until now, I’m still doing whatever I can to prove people wrong. Everything I’ve ever done has been meaningless] [The sun is blazing into the camera, as the Camera slowly fades on. As you see a young. About Ten Years old “Insane” Chris West playing Left Field during Lunch at the old Grade School. The Camera starts to pan around showing all the other kids playing baseball. West Just Stands in Left field waiting for the kid at the plate to hit the ball. West knows the ball is coming to him, it always came to him. Get the easy Hit. Hit the ball to West they said. Just like Clock work the ball is hit into Left Field. West goes back, and catches the ball. As West looks into his Glove, and is about to toss the ball back. Everyone stops and says that can’t happen, and give the fucking Kid another at bat] [FUCK YOU. THE BALL WAS IN MY GLOVE. HOW DARE YOU. YOU MOTHER FUCKERS. I’LL SHOW YOU. Truth is Back during Grade School, I was the kid that everyone picked on, I didn’t do anything Back, they know, if I tried I would be the kid being sent home. Should I Blame the kids for there Actions. Fuck, I was the poor kid in a Rich kid school. I Took the Public Bus to school everyday. Riding on the Bus for 90 mins a day isn’t what you would call fun. I was never given a Chance even at the start. The unhappy childhood. But, unlike other kids who get picked on. I didn’t sit at him as an Adult. Writing up Stupid things on my computer going on about what I wanted to do. No, I knew the Anger of being picked on, was going to kill me or some else. I needed to get The Anger out. I knew I needed to prove something to the assholes of my childhood] [A Bunch of Clips of West getting picked on a child. You can see the anger in his head starting to Boil over. Slowly Clips of West Lifting Weights, Running Laps very early in the morning at the local high school he used to go too too. Getting Bigger and Bigger. Clips of West being trained how to Wrestle are being Shown. West being Suplexed, DDTed. Bodyslamed. Being kicked in the head over and over again. As you see West being told, that he’s done, and finish. He Passed Pro Wrestling School, and he’s a real Pro Wrestler now] [You Would think that would be enough. I’ve proved everyone wrong, I can do something. No. I’ve proved nothing, I’m still the useless little shit, I was in grade school, that everyone used to pick on. I’m nothing but, a wannbe. A Pro-Wrestler, God Damnit, you got to be kidding me that say. Wrestling is Fake. Fake. FUCK YOU. YOU KNOW NOTHING. EVERYDAY PEOPLE ARE GETTING HURT, and FUCKED UP. EVERYONE IS GETTING STICHED UP. I’LL SHOW THEM] [Clips of West working his first match. Missing spots. Mistaking dropping the guy he’s working on his head] [Doing nothing right. But, what do you expect I was a Rookie. Its what Rookies do. I Paid the price for fucking up. I Ended up working hardcore matches, back when Hardcore wasn’t a Big thing. Promoters didn’t do it back then. Sure you had small companies. Like ECW doing it, but Damnit, other wants Just wanted the old school. Bring you’re Kids type of Show. But as for Punishment, I was booked in Hardcore Matches. Treated like Shit, but you know what. It Backfired. Turned out I was good at something. I was a great bleeder, but knew how to Survive. I knew I had to do anything I could to get out alive. Even if it means, hurting friends] [Clips of West working in Hardcore Matches. Still being told he nothing and will be Nothing. You still him bleeding, being busted over the head with still. West taking all type of Punishment, but getting up after the matches] [I take a Beating, but gets on ticking. I found away to get my anger out, and prove people wrong. But, I think the truth is. I didn’t need to prove anything to anyone. I Needed to prove something to myself. And I think I have. Sure, I lose matches. I bleed a lot. But I found my place. Blazing new Paths I never knew I could go down. Like this Extreme Division here in the PTC] [All of a Sudden, the Camera Turns on to “Insane” Chris West. He’s sitting in his Locker room inside the 4WW Arena. He’s Wearing a “4WW” T-Shirt, and his Trademarked blood Stained Blue jeans. In his Left Hand, he has a Cold Canadian Beer. As he downs some of it. You Look up close on West. You see his forehead is taped up, and other parts of his Body is also taped up. You would think he was Just went throw a war] Insane: Ohh, Sorry. I know I look like shit. That’s what happens, when with in three weeks you are in a Japanese Death Match, and get the shit beaten out of you, by the Brotherhoods newest Members, Lazerus, and Moses. If, you didn’t know, they are in the PTC Tag Division. God Damn. Sorry Lord. MOTHER FUCKERS. But, don’t worry, that isn’t going to hold me back from winning this damn, Extreme Division Title Match, at the CWMC. [West gets up and slowly limps over to his Barbwire Baseball bat. As he turns around, and walks back to his seat. As he Sits back down] Insane: Yes, I Limp. So Fucking What. I still get the job done, and with My Mr. Trusty at my side. You can beat I get the fucking job done. I don’t even know who is in this match besides Mr. Main Event, and The Irish Bastard. They know me, and I know them. Doesn’t Matter. We all have a common goal, and that’s to make the Big Show. Be One of the Stars that Shine Bright. Showing off for you’re Fed. Making 4WW look good, for signing the talent who had the talent to make it to the big show. I Want to show my boss, I have the talent. And Be thankful for signing me when he did. I Need to prove to myself, that I still got what it takes to fight in the big matches like this. [West Laughs, as he downs some of his Canadian Beer. As the Laugher stops and everything turns Dead Serious, as West gets up and goes over to the camera. He grabs the camera and looks right into it, like he would be looking at anyone in this Match right in the eyes] Insane: Smash me over the head with Glass. Powerbomb me onto Barbwire Boards. Do, what you got to do, but just remember, No Matter what you do, I’ll get up. You got to hope and pray that the three seconds don’t seem like Hours, as the Ref Smashes his hand down to the ground. You got to Pray to GOD HIMSELF, this Match is over quickly, the longer it lasts the better my chances. I’m like the FUCKING Terminator. There is no way to kill me. So, get the job done quick and FUCKING RUN. Cause Bitch, You’re going to feel my RAITH coming down on you faster, then Kobe Bryant raping another Women] [West Laughs as the Camera Fades to Black]
