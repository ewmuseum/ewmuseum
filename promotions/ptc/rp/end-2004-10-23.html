Date: 2004-10-23 "Grrrrrrrrr..." Karina Wolfenden was naturally strong-willed... she had to be to break free of the often stifling pressures that being a member of the Wolfenden family could pile on a person. Her brother, Max, embraced the ambition, and exploited the resources at hand to go into a career more suited to his upbringing. Karina, however, decided there was more glory and pride to be found in becoming her own person. A tough nut to crack, with a frosty shell that deflected all but the most persistent of people, Karina usually had her way... as not many had the patience or will-power to oppose her. But sometimes, her way was more subconscious, setting off a frustrating battle of wills that ravaged her psyche. "Ugh..." she sighed, flinging the empty bottle of sleeping tablets off her bedside table as she remembered she'd consumed the last of them the pervious night. She had been awake since 6am, and only managed to drift off under a chemical lullaby 4 hours before that. For over an hour, she'd fidgeted, trying to get back to sleep, but despite aching eyes, she just couldn't fall back into slumber. The morning sun was glaring through her windows, causing her to squint and aggravatedly throw herself around to face away from the harsh light. "Why did I have to even look at him," Karina then moaned, thumping a pillow down onto her head and holding it here. "Why... why..." The flaw had been found, the shall had been compromised. Karina Wolfenden had been charmed. "I'm an idiot," Karina then critiqued, remembering why she actually enjoyed being single and unattached. As much as she wouldn't let on to Grant, she'd actually had fun the night before. A date was a rare thing for her, especially given that most were scared off after their first conversation with her... which tended to never really go well. He was someone she could just talk to, who took her constant jovial put-downs in the way which they were meant, and wasn't afraid to fire back with some of his own. He challenged her, he made her have to actually think about what she'd say next. He was a frustratingly good match... so good a match that she'd even waived the 'NO WRESTLERS : EVER' rule. Having been stirred by her owner's grumbling, one of her sleek, almost mysterious black cats - Betsy - strode across the single, vast room that made up Karina's apartment. In an effortless bound, the feline hopped onto the bedside table and simple stared at the young woman, who was mostly obscured by the bedclothes. Finally pulling the pillow from her head, Kari turned to Betsy, who hadn't broken her curious stare. "And don't you start," the K-Wolf warned with a sigh, "or I'll start making your meals myself." On some level, Betsy understood the sheer horror of having to endure Karina's 'cooking' every day, and hopped back down, before scurrying back towards the couch. It was one of those times when the K-Wolf was at conflict with herself. Drawn to the alluring young man, while at the same time struggling to maintain that semblance of distance she needed to feel comfortable. It was an occasion where she was reminded that she was still driven by the same urges as everyone else on the planet, no matter how much she separated herself from normality. "Damn you," she jested, pulling the covers up over her face. "Damn, damn, damn..." It was a good job their next meeting would give her plenty of chances to kick and punch him then, wasn't it? He deserved a little punishment for making her attracted to him. . . . "So, what do you have in mind?" a chirpy Grant enquired as he approached Karina, who was already at her favoured kick-bag. "Sauna, maybe?" Not breaking her focus for him, Karina continued through with the sequence she intended, her body and its muscles having already been pre-programmed. Without shifting her eyes from the target, Karina hit a chain of three punches, before flinging herself up into a hard Enziguri-style kick, sending her body crashing down onto the hard floor. Seeing her tall, almost awkward body land flat on its back, Grant's instant reflex was to lunge forward in concern. "Are you alrigh-" Karina wouldn't even give him the satisfaction of asking before she executed a picture-perfect, lightning-fast kip-up. Managing to stop himself just before he collided with the nonchalant K-Wolf, Grant remarked, "I'll take that as a yes." "Ah, you were worried," she teased, where normally it would have been treated as a patronising insult. "That's kinda cute." As his eyes watched her stride confidently past him, Grant was then left with a niggling question. He's justified the concern with the fact that an injured Karina Wolfenden wouldn't quite be as much use to him... but deep down, he knew it was a natural human reaction for a woman he was admittedly starting to enjoy the often testing company of. "What can I say," he remarked in his defence, turning to follow her. "Most people try to land on those things stuck onto the bottom of their legs." "I'm not most people," Kari was quick to remind Grant, looking back over her shoulder at him with a relaxed smile. "I thought a smart guy like you would have worked that one out by now..." Before Grant could answer, Karina turned around and tossed a couple of padded gloves his way, nodding to him to put them on. "So, this is your idea of a second date?" Grant asked, sliding his hands into them, and hoping there was enough padding to stop his hands being pummelled into a mush. "This is only a smalllll part of what you have to do to get a FIRST date," she reminded her eager would-be-suitor. "Last night was just a free meal." Grant couldn't help but feel a little exposed. Having moved into the small matted area that was surrounded by all the floor's equipment and machines, it was as if everyone was watching. When he walked through the other patrons on his way over to the K-Wolf, he'd heard one or two less-than-praising comments levelled her way by the snobbish clientele... so his self-conscious side was already inflamed. Opposite him, hopping on the spot and loosening her body, Karina seemed free of any of his inhibitions. She was used to people looking at her as if she didn't quite fit in... she just called it 'life'. Wearing just a pair of slightly overly baggy black pants and a faded khaki vest, Karina wasn't one of those who bought into the 'gym fashion' philosophy. "Ready?" she half-asked, half-warned as her blonde and pink mane was tied back into a ponytail. With a little reservation, Grant nodded. The night before, he'd managed to talk himself into a guest pass for the fourth round of the GTT, and now he had a shot at becoming the K-Wolf's training partner as well. Two days ago, he could barely see the first rung on the Indy scene's ladder, while now his foot was edging tantalisingly closer to it. Raising his hands, Grant tensed up... probably not the best idea. "Hope you mean it," Kari then quipped, before relaxing back into her kicking stance. Grant was stood there, waiting, expecting something to be flung his way... but instead, Karina just stared right at his padded hands. Deep breaths could just about be made out, despite the fact that Karina hardly filled out her top. Without blinking, Karina's startling emerald eyes just continued to stare, as if she was trying to get a lock on her target. "Are you actually goi-" Grant went to enquire, just as Karina then sprung to life. Going from statue-still to explosively fluid before Grant even had a chance to notice mid-blink, the sweet spot of the K-Wolf's right foot thumped into his right glove, immediately causing him to wince and recoil the hand. Already in the middle of flinging another one to the same hand, Kari checked her motion and returned to her stance. "Relax your hand," she advised, a slight twinkle in her eye at again testing the young man's resolve. "Breaks things less." "Thanks for telling me," he replied, a fake smile across his lips. With hands relaxed, he then stood their as Karina Wolfenden began to unload. After the first few blows, his hands seemed to go numb, to the point where he wasn't even registering the hits. But far from being a dangerous reaction caused by the kicks, it was more psychological. Watching the K-Wolf fling herself with passionate determination into each blow, Grant couldn't help but be impressed. Then, looking away from his hands, Grant saw that Karina was staring right into his wide, deep eyes. "Haven't you ever thought of, I dunno, Yoga, or something?" he asked. "Why would I want to sit around on the floor for hours?" Karina answered, waiting for pauses in her kicks to fit the words in. "I haven't seen that tactic win many matches lately." Not a bad point. "Well, you don't need to worry about winning anymore," Grant assured her with a self-confident smile, "you've go the best training partner a girl could have. Johnny Lexicon won't know what's hit him!" "How's it fun..." Kari went to respond before launching herself into a double rotation Roundhouse Kick, swatting Grant's hand out of the way before pounding it once more as she spun back through, "if they don't remember who knocked them out?" "I'm suddenly starting to feel that I'm not in the safest of places right now..." Grant joked, realising in a hurry that Karina wasn't any softer on a real person than she was on the kick-bags. But even with all the joking and back-and-forth banter, Grant was reminded that there was a reason he was having his palms pummelled by the young Indy starlet. He could see by Karina's body language that this was her element. This was when she was most open. He could almost visualise the harsh, cold exterior start to melt as her body's fluid motion warmed it through. Just like the night before, he could target her with subtle questions to find a footing, then build on that to get his way. "So how come you’re still a free agent?" Grant enquired in his most laid-back way, even starting to move the pads to give Kari a little variation. "I thought somewhere would have snapped you up by now?" Kari's eyes narrowed slightly as she wondered quite where the question came from, but in the end she just put it down to some people's need to make conversation... something she'd never really understood. "Same reason I'm single," Kari then answered, arcing her right leg up then bringing it crashing down onto Grant's left hand with a Crescent Kick. "It takes a lot to get my attention." After flashing him another wink, Karina launched her body into another Roundhouse Kick, only for Grant to tease her by pulling his hand out of the way just before connection. Playing along, Karina went to hit a Snap Kick to the same pad, but before Grant could retreat the trick, she switched her stance and hit a lightning-fast kick to the opposite glove. "Maybe you could throw a few of those offers my way, huh?" Grant then suggested, making sure to add a little touch of humour to his voice. "I don't think they're transferable," Kari shrugged the question off with, her reluctance to even consider it for the briefest of moments grating at Grant. "Raise them up a bit more." "With all those rumours about you being close to the Avalons," Grant then stuck to his line of questioning with, while complying with the K-Wolf's request, "I'd have thought you’d be tearing up the Squared Circle by now." Not entirely keen on the topic of conversation, Karina seemed to pause for the briefest of seconds, before leaping up and flinging her right foot skywards. "It was mentioned," she casually answered upon landing, "but I'm just not in the mood to sign anywhere soon." To someone like Grant, who had worked his ass off, and bumped his body around, for even the smallest of breaks in the wrestling business, the attitude of Karina was always going to be one that rubbed him up the wrong way. He was the first to admit his plan to use her as a footing in the business was hardly moral, and her feelings may get hurt, but her aloof attitude to what was many a person's life's goal angered him. All she had to do was mention his name to someone... was it too much to ask? "Who knows?" Grant pressed, finding it hard to keep his casual and jovial tone. "Maybe you can mention to them that you've found a hot new talent?" But all Karina was focussed on was her own work-out. "Haven't talked to them in a while," she then simply dismissed, not really reacting at all to his questions. Grant needed to stay calm, relax, and get things on a different topic. What he didn't want to do was press to hard, and keep bouncing questions against the same dead-end... but frustration was starting to cloud his judgement. Spinning through into yet another Roundhouse Kick, Karina had the pad pulled from her foot's path. Reacting quickly yet again, she sent a Snap Kick to the opposite pad, only for it too to be yanked quickly away. While she was still slightly off balance, Grant stepped forward and half-heartedly landed an open, padded palm strike... which sent an off-balance Karina tumbling down onto the mats. "They hit back in matches, y'know?" Grant lectured down to her, his smile losing it's jovial look, adopting a more smirking demeanour. Hopping back up, Karina tried to look un-fazed as she dusted herself off and regained her stance. "The Avalons mention anything about try-outs or dark matches?" Grant less-than-subtly enquired as Karina finally landed a tame Snap Kick to his palm. "Dates? Shows? Anything?" "I told you," Karina reiterated, a little concerned when Grant yet again pulled his pads away to thwart her next strike. "I haven't talked to them in a while." "Yeah," Grant kept pressing, putting the pads back in to give Karina another two hits, "but you've got a number, right?" Rather than just fobbing him off with words again, Karina instead remained silent as she chased the pads which Grant was taunting her with more and more. Stepping in closer to make the kick, Grant used Karina's exertion against her by landing another padded hand to her chin, then sweeping the K-Wolf's legs out. "Ugh," she exhaled as she hit the ground unexpectedly. All those around the mats had their attention grabbed by Grant's actions, but guessed it was part of their sparring session. "Is there something bothering you?" a short of patience Karina asked of Grant as she hopped back up. "No, nothing," he grumbled back, holding the pads out in front of him again. "Nothing at all..." With a curious look on her flushed face, Karina launched into another kick, only to have her foot engulfed by the bulky padded hands of Grant, then spun away to send her tumbling onto the mats once more. "Maybe it's time to lose the gloves," a less-than-impressed Karina suggested as she hopped to her feet once again. "Fine by me," Grant hit back, tossing them at her. Rather than try to catch them, Karina just let them bounce off her chest and arm, before they fell to the mats where she'd just risen from. Sensing the tension, a few more of the people working on the machines began to pay attention to the mats, where the duo locked eyes. "If there's something that you want to say," Karina pushed, "now might be the time to say it." Grunting, Grant just motioned for her to continue. Firing off yet another Snap Kick, Karina half expected Grant to hop back, but instead her foot crunched against his upper arm, drawing an immediate wince from her agitated sparring partner. Rather than offering an apology, Karina followed it off with one from her left foot, only for Grant to react and catch the foot. Once more he pushed the foot away, spinning Karina around, but she managed to keep her footing... ...only for Grant to hit a forearm to her left cheekbone that sent her stumbling back a few paces. "Sure there isn't anything you want to say?" the surprised Karina once again asked, rubbing her cheek where she took the impact. "I've already said all I needed to," he hissed back, urging her to take another shot at him, "maybe you just haven't been listening properly." Taking an assertive pace forward, Karina then launched her left leg up into a Crescent Kick, only for it to be switched halfway through into a quick jumping kick to the side of Grant's head... which didn't pack the punch most of her normal offence would. "I didn't realise you were going easy on me all of a sudden?" Grant chuckled with spite in his voice. "Or maybe you just don't think I'm worthy of a proper kick, is that it? Same reason you won't even tell your little wrestling buddies about me to give me a break, huh?" As Grant's voice was raised, more and more surrounding heads turned. "Are you actually going to start making any sense soo-" Karina went to ask, before Grant got a jump on her and scored a tagging, taunting slap to her cheek. "What are you stopping for?" Grant then quizzed, "I'm just getting started." With a relaxing of her defensive stance, Karina indicated that he was about to get his wish. Channelling her whole body into it, Karina went once more for one of her stiff Roundhouse Kicks, only for Grant to just about bob his head down under it, then launch into a spinning forearm that sent Kari staggering back on impact. "Doesn't it bother you at all that you couldn't give a crap about the business when there are probably a 1000 guys trying to get a break?" Grant demanded to know as Karina rubbed her jaw. "A 1000 guys who don't know the Avalons, or the Kannons, or anyone who can get them a break?" "I'm sure there's someone who'll gladly give them a break," Karina revealed as she stepped into a Snap Kick that pummelled Grant's right knee. But as she retracted her foot, Grant ignored the sharp pain by replying with one of his own. Inquisitive stares from the onlookers were then joined by hushed chatter, as the gym's patrons wondered whether it was all part of the training exhibition. Wincing as Grant's foot slapped against her leg, Karina muttered, "I'm starting to find more and more reasons why I shouldn't date wrestlers." "Date?" Grant exclaimed, almost having to stop himself from laughing out loud. "Oh, please... stop acting like you're a catch, for God's sake. You have to be the most up-herself, tiresome bitch I've ever had the misfortune of having to talk to." And that got a few more people listening. "And I think we all know how you got to where you are in the business," he then scoffed, while a seething Kari watched on, statue-still. "Some people actually bother to learn and train, while you get hired to keep the drunk fanboys happy." "Finished?" Karina enquired, fists clenched. "Just how stupid are you, anyway?" Grant then challenged. "Did you actually think I might be interested in you? Some skinny, flat-chested, lanky freak? I don't think so. All I wanted was for you to drop my name to a few people, and you couldn't even do that because you're so far up yourself!" Finally breaking her icy eye-contact with Grant, Karina's eyes scanned the crowd who were drawn to their confrontation, stifling laughter and whispering venomously. "If you thought I was interested in you and your boring, boring ways, then you're delusional," he kept pushing. "Anything to say to that?" It was the reason she'd always resisted getting close to people. With each word he'd hissed her way, Karina hit herself on the inside, reminding her of why she always kept her distance emotionally. She'd been won over way too easily, and had it all thrown back in her face... in public. She'd been played, manipulated, and exploited. And her face was red with brewing anger. "Nothing?" Grant pocked, while the whispers of the crowd swelled in volume. Then, almost too quickly for Grant to respond, Karina flung a right fist at him, only for Grant to read it just in time and grab her forearm out of the air. Growling, Karina flung a furious left his way, only for the same to happen. Then, smirking, Grant stepped onto her toes, pinning Kari's feet down so she couldn't fling a kick his way either. "Nice try," Grant mocked, scoffing at her attempt at retaliation. "Nice nose," she remarked in reply, and before Grant could work out what she meant, Karina thrust her forehead up into his nose, drawing a pained roar as he staggered away, grabbing the crushed feature. Karina's startling green eyes burned with emerald fury as her fists clenched so tightly that her thumbnails turned visibly white. "My nose... my nose..." With all eyes on her after having been shown up and embarrassed in front of the gathered crowd, Karina took a few potent strides towards Grant, looking at the trail of blood that was flowing from behind his hands. "Let me give you something to take the pain away," she then offered, before flinging her right foot up between his legs, causing an instant bloody splutter to be expelled. With a low, gutsy groan, Grant collapsed to his knees, eyes rolled high into their sockets as he resisted the urge to vomit. "The nose was for using me," she seethed, "the balls were for trying to involve my friends." Then, with Grant heaving on his knees in front of her, Karina launched herself into a Roundhouse Kick that slammed the sweet-spot of her foot against his chin, swatting Grant down onto the mats where he then lay motionless. "And that was because I wasn't going to give you the satisfaction of watching me walk away..." Having had Grant try to make her a laughing stock, and with everyone watching thinking that the man who she was with wanted nothing more than a career boost from her, Karina was quick to leave. Having vented the anger she held towards Grant, all she had now were frustration and self-critique for herself... for letting herself be sucked in and charmed by a few loaded compliments. Feeling the eyes on her sweat-drenched back, Karina strode away, head held high, and brimming with verging-on-thunderous hostility... while holding back reluctant tears. Pity the next person to stand before Karina and invite her to unload. It wouldn't be pretty.
