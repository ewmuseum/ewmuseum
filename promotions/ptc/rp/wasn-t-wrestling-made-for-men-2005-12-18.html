Wasn't Wrestling Made For Men?

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 "GTT5, Round Two! Take that, bitches!" The voice of a now familiar person can be heard. The image is blank. Completely white. Where have we found ourselves? It isn’t earth seemingly, even Earth isn’t this blank. It isn’t space neither. The space is black. Maybe high in the sky and these are just clouds? No wait, that isn’t it either. Where the hell are we?! "I’m going to Round Two! Sledge, ME, has done it! Can you believe it!? I’m going to Round Two! How predictable, ofcourse. I knew I could do it, only nobody had faith in me. Either that, or nobody just knew me. Well…they know me now, don’t they? And now I find myself being outnumbered. Everybody once again thinks I can’t do it. Only because of this…Dawn person…" Yeah, that does sound like Sledge’s voice. The image is still blank. Just the voice of Sledge that talks. Where the hell is his voice coming from? Nowhere? Where is nowhere!? "What was his last name again? Dawn…McDonalds? No wait, why the hell should somebody steal the name of a hamburger restaurant? Err…wait, I rememer it now! Yes, it was something with Dawn McFurr…Dawn McFart…Dawn McFarlane! Yes, that was the name! And people call me an idiot only because I can’t remember shit. Puh, I showed them. My knowledge is great! My knowledge is big! My knowledge…demands beer?" Silence strucked in, with Sledge lost in his thoughts, confused by what he just said. He mumbles something unclear to himself. "Now is not the time to think about beer. Now is the time to think about this Dawn McFarlane guy. Who the hell is he anyway? He passed to Round Two just like me, big deal…Many people passed towards Round Two, it ain’t that special! …Or is it? I don’t know. Wait, yes it is, it is special! Dammit, this is GTT5! Passing onwards to the next round IS special! But now what? What do I have to do to continue my winning streak? What can I do to stay in the tournament!?" Sledge starts mumbling about the situation. He has no clue what to do. He tries to think of some kind of strategy. "Dawn McFarlane is a tough opponent. He has made a name for himself. I haven’t. The odds are against me. This sucks! I don’t want to lose! I want to win!" "Sledge?" Just like that, a new voice pops out of nowhere. Now where the hell is that second voice coming from!? "Maybe I should continue training instead of drinking. Maybe I should come up with an evil plan that is so evil, that it shall work! But what kind of plan is evil enough that it will work? "Sledge!?" "Maybe I should kidnap Dawn and throw him off a bridge! Maybe I should do that! Yes! Genius! …But a bit sadistic though. Hmmm…" "SLEDGE!?" "WHAT!?" Silence came in for a moment. The complete blank image makes it even more scary when there is just complete silence. Spooky… "Dude, it’s me. You know me." "No, I don’t. Who are you again?" The other voice sighs. "It’s me, you idiot. I’m your conscience! Don’t you recognize me anymore?" "First of all, I don’t see you, so that already makes the answer a no. Second of all, didn’t I completely drown my conscience a while ago when I started drinking more and more beer?" "You almost did, but you couldn’t get the job done. Maybe that would be better, don’t you agree? Look, I’m here to help you out, since well…I’m your conscience and the smarter one of the two of us. No wait, I’ve always been the brain, DUH! Atleast what is left of it, really, you need to slow down on the beer. In a couple of years you’ll die of too much beer. We don’t want to have that, now do we?" "Well no, but hey, I will die one day, won’t I? Better drink as much beer as I can, eh?" "Not really a smart thing to do. But like I said, I’m here to help you out. So for that, I will prepare you for your match with Dawn McFarlane. Oh yeah, before I forget to tell. One, you are an idiot. Two, Dawn McFarlane isn’t a guy, Dawn is in fact a girl! Why the hell would a guy carry a girly name!?" "Ah, I see. No wonder I found it a stupid name. I was asking that same question to myself over and over again. A guy with the name Dawn. That would be kind of pathetic, hehe. But wait, if it is a girl…YOU ARE TELLING ME THAT A GIRL IS BETTER THEN ME!?" "Well, I never said that. But most people say that Dawn is better then you and that you don’t stand a real chance. But really, I’m here to support you, and I should, since I’m a part of you. So in fact I’m supporting myself…Weird." "I’m not going to get my ass kicked by a girl, dammit! For fuck sake, this is a guys sport! I still don’t understand why girls are in wrestling! They aren’t made for wrestling! …Well, maybe for mud wrestling or for wrestling in the bedroom…But not for professional wrestling!" "So actually, in you eyes, girls aren’t allowed to wrestle professionally? "Exactly! That’s my point." "Well, even when I’m on your side, you are discriminating the opposite sex. Why shouldn’t a woman wrestle? They want to wrestle, so let them do it! It’s their decision to make and not yours." "Come on! Girls can’t wrestle! They suck! Wrestling is made for men and not for girls! They can watch and observe, fine. But wrestle along!? Not in my eyes! Ban all females from wrestling!" "Well Sledge, if it was up to me to say, I would say that you are feeling intimidated by the opposite sex in wrestling. Especially since you realize most people are actually rooting for her instead of you." "On which side were you again!?" "Stop whining for a moment, okay!? Seriously, you have zero confidence in yourself and your ability! Look, people are rooting for her, fine. Let them root for her. But it’s about you! You have to gain some more confidence. Look, maybe you are scared because she has a better reputation then you, but the point is, she is still a female. Even though females can wrestle, males are always better then females in wrestling! The only problem is that she probably will be much more prepared in comparison to you! You can get the job done, just believe that you can do it!" "Sweet, you are on my side after all! Look, I will go out there and do my best, but the fact remains that Dawn McFarlane has a much better reputation then me. That mostly means that she is better, and since people are rooting for her as well, what is left for me?" "Nothing if you continue to whine like a bitch." "Precisely! So let me be and let me continue to drown my fear with alot of beer! Now…where was the exit again? I only see complete blankness. Too much white! My eyes!" Sledge’s conscience sighs once more. Sledge continues to scream about his eyes. "Fine, do whatever you want to do. That’s it, fuck you. You’re on your own now, buddy! I’m outta here!" "You can’t leave! Atleast not until I completely drowned you with beer. But besides that, you are still a part of my body! So you can’t get out of my mind! Neener, neener, neener." "Well, you know Sledge?" "What?" "I was lying. I’m not your conscience. You were right, you drowned all of your conscience a long time ago. You drowned all of it. I was here trying to help you. But noooo, mister doesn’t believe in himself. You are really hopeless. It’s a pity that you throw all of your leftover talent out of the window. A real shame." "Well, atleast I tried, didn’t I? I did make it to Round Two in the GTT5 Tournament. Now take that! HA!" "You suck. Oh, and one more thing." "And that is?" "You are dreaming. You passed out while drinking beer. Damn, you really are an idiot. This entire time you were dreaming and you didn’t even think about it. Haven’t you even noticed that we were talking in a blank area the entire time!? Doesn’t that even frustrate you!? I would be frustrated because of that, but I’m just a made up character. I don’t exist. I’m nothing but an unreal voice that tries to sound smart. Really, the entire time you were talking to a non-exsisting voice and you didn’t even notice it. You even believed me when I said I was your conscience, if I said I was Santa Claus, would you believe me to?" "Probably yes, if I see you standing in front of me and said ho, ho ho!" Sledge starts to chuckle. The other voice sighs again. A very deep sigh this time. "Fuck this, it’s not worth it anymore. I’m out of here. No wait, in fact, YOU are out of here! See you later, moron!" "Is this going to hurt?" Just like that, the image pops open inside Sledge’s living room. Sledge’s eyes pop wide open. He was laying on his couch, but immediatly falls off of it as he wakes up. The clinging of bottles of beer and cans can be heard as Sledge hits the ground with a loud thud. He moans as he is laying on the floor. "Shiiiit…" ------------------------------------------------------------------ Five minutes or so later, Sledge has gotten up from the floor. He is still in his house, which looks really old and dirty as always. The floor is covered with beer cans and bottles. It’s hard to stand anywhere. Sledge is sitting on a chair behind his table. He scratches his hair while he tries to get his mind clear. "Dawn McFarlane…Dawn McFarlane…Damn, what am I, obsessed? Everybody is just rooting for her and I’m already freaking out!? What has gotten into me!? Pressure and stress…Yeah, that’s probably it…" Just then, a knock on the front door can be heard. Sledge looks up and moans. He gets up and walks throught the hall towards the front door. Once again, the person on the other side of the door knocks again. Sledge doesn’t appreciate the low patience of the person. Sledge grabs the door-handle and swings the door open wide. "WHAT!?" "Geez, take it easy!" A female voice answers back. In the doorway, a lovely brunette stands in front of Sledge. The brunette going by the name of Kelly, who is Sledge’s girlfriend, is a bit younger then Sledge, is wearing a dark red winter coat and jeans. She has a cap on her head to cover up from the cold, cold winter. Snow is covering the porch and front yard. "Well, are you going to invite me in or what?" Kelly asked a surprised Sledge. Sledge blinked as he looked at her. "Errr, yeah, come on in. But what are you doing here again?" Kelly entered the house as Sledge slammed the door shut. Kelly walked inside the living room and shook her head while she looked at the dirty walls and the messy floor. "You really should clean up your house." "And you should answer my question." Sledge immediatly fired back. Kelly looked him in the eyes and blinked her eyes. "I just came here to visit. Is that a crime maybe? I didn’t think you were busy anyway, so I thought that I should pop inside and show my face again. We haven’t seen each other a couple of days, so I thought it was time to see you again. Haven’t you missed me?" Sledge hesitated for a moment… "Err, yeah, ofcourse I did! I really, really missed you…" Kelly looked him straight in the eyes. Sledge tried to look at the other direction, but it was like his eyes were being grabbed so that they couldn’t move and had no choice to look back at her straight in the eyes. "You’re lying." Kelly said with a cold voice… "Okay, okay. I didn’t think much about you, but I just had my mind focussed on something else, alright? I will make it up with you later. But for now it’s just that I couldn’t really think much about you. I’m sorry about that…" Kelly walks over to the table and takes off her coat. She hangs it over a chair and sits down on the chair. Sledge walks over and sits down on the opposite side of the table. The two of them stare at each other for a moment as it is silent in the entire house. "Now what could be that important that you didn’t even have the time to think about me for just a moment. Didn’t you think about me just a little bit? Like how I was doing? Where I was at the moment?" "No, I’m sorry. Look, it’s just that I’m under alot of pressure at the moment and that I need to concentrate and get my mind clear. I’m really sorry, I really am, alright?" Sledge said while trying to look Kelly in the eyes without showing fear, but he knew that Kelly was quite disappointed in Sledge. "It’s okay…As long as you keep your promise and make it all up. But now, what is that thing that was so much important then me?" "Well…" Sledge thinks for a moment. "It’s that GTT5 tournament you know about. I made it to Round Two like I told you before. But now it’s just that I’m facing a difficult opponent, not only that, it’s a girl who I’m facing! I’m not sure if I can do it." "Do what?" Kelly wondered. "Fighting a girl. I don’t know if I have it to fight and hurt a girl. That’s why I don’t like it when girls are into a sport that is actually made for men! I have no problem with the fact that I’m in Round Two ofcourse, I do have a problem with my opponent, Dawn McFarlane." "Look, just set your mind on blank and everything will come together. Don’t worry, I think she knows the consequences that wrestling brings. It’s her own fault when she has to face a man in the ring, and so be it. She chose to fight, and a fight she will receive. Us females aren’t that weak as some guys think, do not, DO NOT underestimate us! We know how to take care of ourselves. Look, just pretend she is an enemy, or somebody you really hate and face her with all that anger! You do know that anger makes blind, don’t you?" "I know that very well actually…" Sledge looked down to the table while dwelled in thoughts again, probably old memories. Painful memories… "Good, so don’t concentrate too much about the match. Don’t worry, even though many people are probably rooting for her, because they know her more then they know you. You are still relatively unknown to many people in the wrestling business. But I know you can do it, and I shall support you, ofcourse." "Yeah, I know that. Thanks for that." "No problem, you can always count on me." Kelly smiled as Sledge looked up and smiled back. Kelly got up from the chair and walked over to Sledge. She wrapped her arms around him and she gave him a hug. She kissed him on the top of his head and then put her own head on his. He put one of his hands on her arms. "Remember Sledge, the distance is nothing; It is only the first step that is difficult…"
