The Chronicles of a Magician Pt1

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 The fire cackled in the open fireplace, the room was almost dark only the light of the fire place glowing casting eerie shadows upon the simple décor of the sitting room. A small table was placed in the centre of the room, two deep red high backed chairs positioned at either side, two pictures hung side by side on the fireplace. Both showed the same wrestler a tall man the only difference was one had blue hair and the other had black hair with deep red tips at the ends. The first one on the right showed the blue haired man seemingly suspended in mid hair while clutching a championship belt in his hands. The second one showed the darker haired man with a foreboding look on his face while he stands over the motionless body of another wrestler; again a belt was draped across his shoulder. A greying man enters the room through the double door set in the wall directly facing the fireplace. The man seems to have an air of intelligence about him as he walks towards one of the chairs his dark blue pin striped suit rippling in motion to his bodies’ movements. His deep green eyes set firmly on its goal. "Ah good evening my Mr Greenfield glad you can make it, it has been many years since our last meeting?" A deep voice echoes from one of the chair as a single wrinkling hand appears and gestures to man to have a seat. "It certainly has Andi, what some thirty, forty years since we were last in Four Winds together?" Mr Greenfield says happily as he sits down on the chair across from the man he called Andi. Andi sat there in his deep red lounge robe and matching bottoms smiling to himself his icy stare firmly on the man. Greenfield however took in the wrestler he knew has Andi the Magnificent the years had been good to him that was for sure, his deep black hair was only just showing the first signs of later years, his once short hair was now longer and he supported a classic beard which almost nestled in his lap like some curled up cat. His gaze however was still steely, but at the same time you could see the power waiting to be called upon from behind them. "So Andi why did you want to see me so urgently?" Mr Greenfield asked knowing that cutting to the chase was the only way to go with the Wizard. The magician laughed out loud, "You don’t miss a trick do you Ian," shaking his head the wizard made himself comfortable. "Ok Ian, I know things that I feel the world needs to know and you are the one man I can trust to ensure they get put into print." "I see, lucky I brought my tape recorder along then." Ian fiddles in his pocket and pulls out a small Dictaphone, presses the red record button. Placing it one the table he sits back crosses his legs and settles him self in for what could very well be a long night. The magician grins to himself as he ponders the words carefully. "Well some people believe that at ever decision we make somewhere in another dimension we are facing the same thing and what happens then is one goes one way and the other will take the second route, Now people think this only happens on major decisions and a lot believe its aloud of codswallop. Well I can tell you all now this there both wrong it happens constantly. Like now I have decided to tell you this, but in another dimension running parallel to this one I have decided not to, so another from spits of and forms a new world, even deciding to skip breakfast causes the same thing." Andi coughs and takes a sip of water from the glass that has just magically appeared in his hand before continuing. "So I am going to tell you about these different world’s the first one is very interesting and was the most intriguing. Oh and another small thing, you know when we get a shiver and people say ‘Someone has just walked over out graves’ again there wrong its one of our many selves dieing." Andi pauses dramatically before continuing. "Let me take you now on a journey that will astound you and maybe even baffle you, but I can tell you I saw this with my very own eyes it all begins on a cold December evening in New York…" Snow gently fell from the heavens as a man with combed back black hair stood there looking out of the window, New York was beautiful when covered in snow but now there was something dark and far colder than winter hanging over this great American City. One thing he new he had to make this place safe he had already failed in London; he was not prepared to fail again. Sighing the man in the Blue Jeans and black T-shirt turned round and made his way to the desk that was situated in the room. His eyes narrowed as he struggled with the thoughts of what would happen if he failed again. It was also clear that this person was indeed the Wrestler we now as Andi the Magnificent. A brisk knock at the door sounded bringing Andrew from his reprieve. "Come in and how many times you don’t have to knock." Andi replied in his broad English accent. Four men trundled in and sat down in the four vacant chairs that where dotted around a second desk, in front of each chair was an open laptop. Andrew looked at each man in turn, each man was chosen by the Federal Bureau of Investigation for being the top people in each of the field Andi had asked for when they came to him to head the investigation and here they all where in a disused Warehouse, which was there home until the Investigation was over. Andi looked at the first man sat to his left, now this man irritated him totally especially in the way he dressed, today he wore tight black jeans and a very loud Flannelette shirt, his long blonde hair was receding but this didn’t seem to bother him as he flashed a smile, Andrew shock his head ever so slightly why they had to give him this tall, slightly overweight but solid Aussie he would never know, but credit where credit is due he was fast and a excellent Crime Scene Investigator. Ross F. was his name and science was defiantly his game. The man sat on the left of Ross was intriguing kept himself to himself but when called upon he could use his computer wizardry to recreate a crime in the finest detail and with ever-possible angle covered. The man who stood at a round six foot but sometimes he appeared shortly with the slouch he had from the constant back pain he was in, of course this lead to him being slightly overweight and as always his short brown hair was kept impeccably. Andi liked this man known as RJ S. he was defiantly a man you could count on. Sat across from RJ and clicking furiously on his mouse button was the Criminal Psychologist and he was the very best in his field and like most people with his amount of expertise he had one major flaw, he was very sarcastic but that being said he had defiantly given insights on the man in this case that ten of these so called ‘brainy people’ hadn’t even thought of. He was the shortest member of the group standing at around 5’ 10", he also was completely bald, which probably resulted in his attitude cause he would often remind everyone that he was indeed ‘hung like a moose.’ Andi like the man known as Pete S. even though a lot of the sarcasm was aimed at him he still saw him as a vital member of the crack team. Andrew finally looked at the final man he was there ‘man on the street’, the FBI’s best field agent, he could blend in anywhere he was that good he could easily blend into a woman’s locker room and not be noticed and truth be said he more than likely did and in the process made half of them think that the other half were lesbians, to say this guy liked a joke was a understatement, he would have shot through the ranks of the FBI had he not put a banana cleverly positioned in his pants as he shook the first ladies hand and right on cue the banana seemed to perk up causing her much embarrassment, so here he stood still a field agent he was always ready for action right now he wore a black leather jacket, black t-shirt and black jeans, his hair was covered by a grey hat, or a tea cosy as Pete liked to put it. Andi looked at the man known as Ryan W. and wondered what mischief he was planning. Settling back in his chair he again looked at them and noticed the sombre looks on all their faces and wondered what was up it was still earlier and Andi had not gotten much sleep not that he could remember what he had been doing he awoke a few minutes ago his head resting against the keyboard and a empty bottle of whiskey at the other side. His head hurt slightly but he shrugged it off and asked them the first question. "What’s with the looks guys, someone died?" All four men looked at each other but it was Pete who was the first one to speak and venom was clear in his voice. "Of course someone has died he claimed his twelfth victim last night and we four were the ones that had to go and clear this mess up before the papers get a hold of it and blow this fucking operation out of the water and what where you doing Andi we knocked but there was no answer and the door was locked before you ask why we didn’t wake you." The others looked directly at Andi as the leader of the operation got the ticking of he certainly deserved and boy did he know he deserved that. It all was beginning to get on top of him. "Sorry guys, so what do we know, Ross?" the agent responds quickly looking at the Aussie CSI. Ross looks up almost spilling his coffee as if he was more expecting there to be a argument between the Agent and the Shrink. "Ummm, Well mate it was the same as the other eleven no sign of forced entry, the skin had completely been peeled off the victim leaving the flesh untouched, all apart from the skull with still had every organ just no flesh on it at all almost as if flesh as usual had been burnt off and of course the E that had been carved into the forehead of the skull, again looks to be from the same tool as was used before, I just wish I knew what is was at least then we could narrow our search down, the body is at the morgue under going a autopsy which will reveal nothing as usual but never the less we will get the results in a few hours." Ross took another gulp of coffee as he finished and waited for a response from Andrew. All twelve had died the same way no excessive blood loss at the scene, which had bamboozled the Aussie totally, Andi turned to RJ and looked at him he could tell all this was having an effect on him too. "RJ, do you have anything for me." Andi asked him compassion in his voice. Mr. Stewart looked around and bowed his head as he taped a key on his keyboard as everyone’s screens turned to a 3d rendered model of New York, another key stroke and it changed again to show a more detailed model of a small five by four area of downtown. "So as we know the first murder happened here on 34th street some two months ago until last night when a twelfth murder happened here on West Haven. Now what I did was collaborate all the murders and plotted them into this map and what we get is very interesting, watch." RJ finished a satisfied smile appearing on his face as once again he hits another key on his keyboard. This time the render moves upward to show the same area as before but this time in a plan view then one by one all twelve murders start to appear on the map, and then once all murders are there a line starts to draw and connect each murder showing a clear picture to everyone. "Oh my god he’s forming a giant E," Ross exclaims in amazement, "What a breakthrough." Pete scoffs just before he starts to speak "Yeah well done RJ took your time though, but tell me this cleaver dick there obviously needs to be one more murder before he finishes his E, that final block must have 200 apartments in it." RJ grins "Yeah Peter, I figured that much myself but from what you have told us, our Mr Ember is getting hotter and more volatile and that was proven last night when he committed his last murder some 3 days after the last one. So by going by his need and his habits and a little bit of science I got it down to the exact complex he would need to hit and it’s this one." RJ hit another key and the program set of again and a new circle this time in blue appeared as the program zoomed in it showed a lone house on the street known as Jefferson’s. RJ continued. "This house is the exact house I reckon he will hit next and if he does it will connect the E." Andi pondered this new evidence as the room fell silent, Ember so named because just like a burning ember on a fire that once is got what it needed it accelerated consuming everything it needed. The agent wondered what would happen if he wasn’t stopped before this final murder would it be like it was in London, would he disappear for 10 years before starting again. He knew it couldn’t happen; his mind could not cope with another 10 years of waiting and wondering. He had to work fast. "Pete, when do you reckon he could strike next?" Pete looked at Andi and decided sarcasm wasn’t really needed at this point. "I reckon it will be tonight, it has to be his hunger for the final part will drive him to do it, this could be our only chance of capturing him." Gasps echoed through out the room as they all looked at Pete and saw that for once he wasn’t trying to be sarcastic. Andi shook his head, he had to stop him there was nothing for it. "Ok guys get everything together, Ryan you get down there we need a report on the house within the hour, every possible entrance, everything you can get, when the owners leave what they do everything, get pictures of everyone who goes to the house and send them here." Ryan nodded and was out the door quicker than a startled rabbit. Andrew looked at the rest of the group, "Ok lads we haven’t got much time, get something to eat and everything we need because tonight we go on a stakeout." Morning went into Afternoon and pretty soon evening arrived leaving an empty feeling in the pit of all the teams stomachs. It had been frantic to say the least Ryan had sent excellent surveillance on the house, but all the pictures that came in proved negative as RJ scrawled through every database he could think off, nothing at all out of the ordinary. After a few phone calls he had arranged to have police and SWAT waiting within 2 block of the Springer residence. There was a certain buzz around the team that said everything they had worked on was coming to a head. Just then the phone rang, Andi snarled for they where just about to go. "Hello…Oh hi chief, what can I do for you…But…Ok chief I’ll be there shortly bye." Andrew slammed the phone down hard as he screamed in frustration. "Pete, RJ and Ross get yourselves down to the house and meet Ryan, the Chief wants to see me fucking hell be ready to go in even if I am not there." The darkness was beautiful as he walked to his final destination it’s heart bet faster with the excitement of his final kill, it knew that this would be the most enjoyable one yet, it was all done for a higher purpose, power as always came at a price but unlike this monster some were not willing to make the sacrifices needed. It saw the dim lights of the house approaching, the monsters breathing got faster as it walked within the shadows, even going unnoticed where no shadows were cast. It reached the door finally and passed right through it, these things bore no barrier, to the monster it was just like walking through air. It stalked into the room, a almost human chuckle escaped from its lips as it entered the room and saw the woman sat there watching Wheel of Fortune oblivious as they always where to its coming. Its black gloved hand took grabbed the woman around the throat her long auburn hair resting against him as he pulled her casually from the chair, her blue eyes filled with horror as the pain of the searing started to consume her body. First the blood would start to boil and wilt away until there was just enough blood left to keep its victim alive, and then the skin starting at the head would start to peel from the skull as if someone was peeling a banana. The slow peeling continued the first part of the pink flesh was beginning to show its hair roots for the moment still attached. The monster laughed as he embraced her screams and fears he continued to laugh as her pale skin moved from her face revealing it mushy flesh underneath but still the eyes moved franticly from side to side. Suddenly there was a crash and a splintering of wood quickly followed by flashlights and voice. The monster laughed and wondered what it had forgotten to do, and then it was there, the shield that hid the screams was not up. It knew for that silly mistake it had to die but it was going to take as many as it could with it. "Freeze FBI!" came a gruff voice from the hallway, It turned quickly and threw a ball of fire from its hand directly into the on rushing police man who screamed as he burst in to flame. The monster was about to throw another ball of fire when his head rocked back, it looked in shock open the eyes of the woolly hat wearing young man knelt there smoke drifting from the gun. It felt blood trickle down its face as the world went dark for the last time. "Pete, RJ, Ross get here we got Ember, we got…" His voice trailed of as the lights came on the woman’s screams had finally stopped as she floated there just like all the other twelve victims before her. Ryan’s face turned to shock as he looked at the thing they had called Ember. A tear rolled down his cheek as the other three came into the room. "Strewth mate, it can’t be?" Ross says his face a mask of shock. "It is Ross, Its Andi, he was Ember all along." RJ said as he knelt down and closed the eyelids of their former friend and workmate.
