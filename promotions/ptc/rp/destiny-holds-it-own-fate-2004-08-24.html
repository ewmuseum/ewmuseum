Destiny Holds It Own Fate

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 It is true what they say, that life is a game. A game has a set of rules to abide by while playing. To be successful in a game, one must know the rules and understand the concept. These people play the game who it was made to be and most times they are successful at it. For some it is more than others but generally they all progress in the same direction. While there are others who either can't comprehend it or refuse to accept it these rules and concepts. These people are more often than not unsuccessful when playing the game but there are some instances where a free minded radical will be far better than anyone has every seen. These people have the ability to invade and take control or help steer it in the right direction. Ultimately, I am one of those people and that is my purpose. As times goes by, the game becomes old and monotonous. Repetitiveness settles in a continuous loop, going around and around and around. There exist some people who will never get tired of the game, there are others who would like to escape but simply cannot for one reason or another. The cruel fate of the world? or The cruel beast known as society? "I know the answer to that one!" Sometimes I sit and ponder to myself what things would be like on the other side. But it is hard to envision anything as my mind refuses that passage. I think at this point of this existence, I have dug a hole so deep that it is too late to go back. Well, that is merely how the saying goes. That is not what actually happened with me. There where people digging the hole for me, and every now and then would try to bury me in it. No such luck as yet. Will they ever succeed over me? Could they defeat me? In fact, could anyone beat me? It just depends on what kind of victory you seek. If you mean like a victory engaged inside the square circle, well that itself is no mean feat but that doesn't mean that it can't been done. But victory and defeat extend way beyond the ropes of our stage. They take greater forms in the grand scheme of things. Out there is where the real battle is. "That's not to say that the battle in here isn't as important" Your right! The greatest battles are fought within. Is that inside the ring or inside one's self? I'll leave that up too you. But anyway, what an interesting predicament we have on our hands. This industries best, going head to head, waging a war but I still have got to ask ... what for? Bragging rights just isn't enough these days. Sure, its give people some big heads, but they already where big to begin with. All I can say to these feds that the WWA is opposing, don't not underestimate us. Don't shoot yourself in the foot. If you've followed the Juce than you know that the unknown is where I am from and it is my greatest weapon. But that's right, of course you wouldn't have followed the Juce. No one even knows who I am. No one has given me credit or anything. Just the thing I expected. No, I'm not shocked or disappointed. Tis the nature of this society, to overlook outside greatness. You feds have never looked out of your own windows to see what is happening in the world around you. But the fault is none but your own. Once again, the unknown will become my advantage. "What you don't know ..." Why is my outlook so dark you say. I have been crushed by the ways of modern society. It’s just another way to say "conformist hypocrisy". They left me behind on the outskirts of society where many of us dwell and let our minds run rampant with the thoughts of doom, misery, success and triumph. We may be different, but we are all one and the same. "We are human" It is sad when society gets broken down into categories. From A to Z we stand as we are told, in order, in sequence. The ones that aren't are either put in their place or removed. Simple as that. Is life in a sense nothing but a linear journey with nowhere else to go but straight ahead? Maybe for most of the simpletons, but not for me. My journey is a complex. It will have an end, somewhere beyond the mountain. Once a journey seems to have been completed, another appears and the realisation that your first journey never concluded is realized. Choices have been made and will be made, same goes for sacrifices. Will I ever climb that mountain to see what is on the other side? Of course I will, for that is my path. It is inevitable. But as for how long it will take me and what lies beyond it, such things can only be predicted. There is only one way to find out such a thing ... "When it happens, you'll know."
