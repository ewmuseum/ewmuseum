Mr. Fahrenheit

24 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-24 “Bullseye…” [Kenny Mercury walks onto the UWF tour bus, blowing air over his hand, with his fingers arranged like a pistol… index and thumb extended. Dingus looked over at Kenny from the driver seat.] “Nice match, Kenny.” [Only about an hour ago, Kenny Mercury got a submission victory over Neon's Bullseye.] “Naturally.” [Kenny shot a cocky smile at the UWF road agent.] “It's not his fault… he never had a chance… I was bred for this. I was just born better… there's nothing he could have done to better prepare himself. It was an impossible battle, no matter how hard he worked out, no matter how bad he wanted the win, no matter how much he trained… Nature ran its course… The Shooting Star of Wrestling burned him…” “Right… Kenny, did you see who you face next round?” “I mean, His name was 'Bullseye'… I just don't know how to react to that… I mean… Was he SUPPOSED to suck?” “I don't think so… Are you going to answer my question?” “Another one bites the dust, as Freddie used to say…” “Answer the question!” “What question?” “UGH… Do you know who you're facing next round?” “HA! As if it matters… All shall fall before the Technical Wizard… SHAZAM!” “Dallas Reeves. He's from NGW.” “UGH… Not a fucking Texan… GAWD!” “… He's Canadian.” “Canadia? Even worse! The horde of Canadia is far reaching indeed… Oh man, and NGW? Ew…” “What's wrong with NGW?” “They're just…. So… dirty… Bleh… make sure he showers before I beat him.” “Whatever. Right now, sit down, I've got a surprise for us.” “Surprise? Oh goody goody gumdrops, you fat tub of lard, we're going to Red Lobster, aren't we?!” “No…” “KFC… right… jumbo bucket, and a large gravy… to drink.” “No!” “Oh right, Large Diet Gravy.” “God, no… You'll see.” [A sinister smile ran across Dignus' face. The street lights cut through the dark night and into the bus. The shadow coming off of Dingus' bus driver cap ran down his face, flowing with the contours of his pudgy smile.] “Dingus… You're scaring me… I mean, there's a Taco John's right across the street, that doesn't intrigue you AT ALL?” “Damn it! Just sit down!” “Potato Oles~!!!” “Sit… down….” [Kenny plopped down on the seat behind him. Dingus shifted the bus into gear and it slowly took off, creeping up the street, slowly gaining speed. The bus rambled on through the city lights.] [Kenny Mercury was a character. One that most people should be lucky not to know… or did they? There are Kenny Mercury's of the world everywhere, not as extreme, but every now and then someone with an enflamed Kenny Mercury gland would pop up. A person who was destined for some usually unachievable goal by any practical means and rational. They are 100% certain of their supposed destiny and would step on anyone and everyone to fulfil it. This is Kenny Mercury, and enjoyed the stepping and fulfilling.and the buwai… wahai ..and mmmglaiven.] [The bus rolled to a stop in front of a building with a flashing pink neon light shaped like a woman. It flashed back and forth, making the neon lady dance... to and fro so to speak. To… fro… to… fro. Who says that anyways… they should get German Suplexed out a 10 story window.] “Ye….Haw's???” [Kenny recited what he read off another neon light below the dancing woman.] “Hell yeah, Kenneth! We're gunna see us some titties!” [Indeed, Ye-Haw's was a strip club. Three men throwing back beers walked in. Multicolored lights shot out of the club and into the street in an avalanche of music, cigarette smoke, and drunken good times residue that, you know, gets all in the air and places like these. A green beam hit Kenny in the eyes, then was quickly killed off as the club doors slammed shut. The music could only slightly hummed through the door and walls of Ye-Haw's.] “Excuse me… We?” “Hell yeah, Ken! Bitches in hats and chaps! I can't wait! Come on!” [Dingus put the bus in park and took the keys from the ignition. He tossed his hat on his seat and started on down the stairs of the bus. He stopped as Kenny spoke.] “Dissssss-gusting!” “What?” “You disgust me…” “Kenny? What are you saying?” “I am NOT going in there…” “What's wrong with you? Titties, man! TITTIES~!!!” “Titties schmitties… I'm not going into that place. Just leave me alone, Extra Chunky.” “Be a man.” [At this… Kenny snapped… almost… Kenny actually snapping all the way was bad…. Really bad… but dramatic mini-snap like these happened daily and on nearly every UWF broadcast. He didn't really “snap” per say… just kind of crackled and popped. Kenny lunged forward and grabbed Dingus around the collar with his powerful and skillful hands, they were lethal weapons. He could put a herd water buffalo in a coma for a month with a full nelson. Mercury steamed and got right in Dingus's face, nose to nose and sneered.] “I am a former Universal Wrestling Federation Intercontinental Championship… Former Hardcore Champion, former THREE TIME Television Champion… I won third place in the Prime Time Central Invitational Tournament…I eat nails for breakfast and shit thumbtacks… I bench press Volkswagens. I squat Cadillacs. I can jumprope really… REALLY fuckin' fast… I am the Shooting Star… The Technical Wizard… The Ring Doctor… The VERY DEFINITION OF PRECISION… AND I AM A BIGGER MAN THAN YOU'LL EVER BE OR COULD DREAM OF BEING!!!” [With that, Kenny Mercury flexed with pecs in rapid succession, then shoved Dingus backwards. Dingus tumbled down the bus steps and landed on the sidewalk. Kenny Mercury drilled through the back of Dingus' head with his eyes as Dingus looked back up at Kenny from the sidewalk. Dingus slowly got to his feet, brushed himself off, and with a flip of the bird, headed inside the club, mumbling as he went…] “Psychiotic bastard…” [Dingus disappeared itto Ye-Haw's. Kenny stared at the club for a few moments. He started to calm down, and stepped off the bus. He eyed the strip club some more… He peered in the windows… no, no he didn't, strip clubs don't have windows idiot. He wished they did, though. He wished he could have just gotten a quick glimpse inside as he cursed it.] “Imbeciles…” [Kenny spit.] “Fucking… imbeciles…” [Mercury walked to the front of the bus. He surveyed the city and the streets. He had nothing to do, fuck this town. Mercury used his arms on the hood and hopped up their. He laid back on the front of the bus and put his arms behind his head, buried in those golden locks. They were to die for, honestly.] “Imbeciles…” [Kenny Mercury stared out at the starry sky… Or not. Fucking city… the lights block out the stars. Kenny Mercury looked up at the dead sky. Was Kenny really the better man for not going into the strip club? Did Kenny Mercury have morals? Was he really a good person? Did he respect women? Was he chivalrous?] [Hell no.] [Kenny turned his head and spit off the hood of the bus and onto road.] [He's Kenny Mercury. Kenny Mercury… the shooting star? You know, the technical wizard, yadda yadda yadda definition of blah blah blah… He was Kenny Mercury, and Kenny Mercury didn't have time for women. To Kenny Mercury, women were useless. Stupid, inferior, and useless, did I mention useless? They only got in the way of Kenny's ultimate goal of supremacy… they had nothing to offer Kenny Mercury. Kenny Mercury hated women. There was no point.] [Kenny sighed.] “Stupid bitches..” [Granted Kenny hadn't really had that much experience with them… In years past, when Purple Haze, Kenny's alter ego, was alive and well, he ignored women, too. Not in the same way… Purple Haze was simple, he was good, he was pure, he was a delusional retard. Purple Haze lacked the basic ability to distinguish man from women. But that all changed. That changed when he met Lacuna Debris, the most beautiful woman EVER! But she hated him… because he was a simpleton, a doofus… and she hurt him. Kicked him in the nuts, and whooped the shit out of him. She broke him inside. How could the best thing in the world to him hate and hurt him so much? He snapped… not just snap crackle and pop… but he exploded like he never did before. Kenny Mercury was born and emerged from the grave of Purple Haze. And he hated women… stupid women who only got in his way. Kenny Mercury was above them.] [Kenny laid out on the hood of the bus in the night, shirtless. Mercury was rarely ever seen in anything but his in-ring attire… his shiny black boots, his fingerless martial arts gloves, his black tights with the red ringed planet on the crotch. He loved his gear, and wore it always, it was part of him. Someone else took a liking to it, too.] [A man frolicked up to the bus. Yes, frolicked. He was clad in leather. Black leather chaps, black leather vest, a black leather Harley Davidson cap. His hairy belly protruded out from under the small vest. He had a think black mustache and wore aviator glasses, tinted with no chance of seeing through them. He leaned up on the hood and pushed his glasses down his nose. He looked up and down Kenny's muscularly toned body, mouth and eyes agape.] “Well, looky what we got here… Boy, big fellah… If I told you that you had a sexy body, would you hold it against me?” [Kenny was always a target, an easy one, for homosexual jokes. In UWF, he was constantly under a verbal assault and suspected of being homosexual. Was he in denial? Was he homosexual? Kenny Mercury hated homosexuals… even more than women. Did he hate this man… because he was gay or was he just pissed because he found him rude? I don't know… either way though; he didn't like him one bit.] [Kenny Mercury turned to the man leaning up on the hood of the bus. He turned ninety degrees, sliding across the bus on his wrestling tights to face the man. The man smiled into Kenny's eyes. Kenny chopped off his head.] **CRACK!!** [Kenny sent his black wrestling boot into the man's face, bringing the hard toe under his jaw, sending his head snapping backwards. He flailed backward and landed on the ground. He lay there, motionless. Kenny Mercury turned back around and leaned back on the hood and looked up at the polluted night sky. He spit.] “Faggot.” [The meaning of life? This has been long debated, but once we stop screwing around and get right down to it… its sex. Sex. The one thing that drives people everyday, the continuation of the species, or the satisfaction of the one undying urge. Kenny Mercury was not human. He was not driven by this… he new not love, except for himself. He knew not lust, except for greatness. He knew not pleasure, except for when he had his opponent right where he wanted him…] [Was Kenny Mercury human? He loved neither man nor woman… but he loved being superior… he loved the wrestling ring… he loved his domination over another body. When the “Technical Wizard” cast his spell upon his victim… When Mercury locked that man in a submission hold, it gave him pleasure. Total domination. Mercury was supreme, he was the end and the beginning. He was final. He controlled his opponent… Mercury could control how much pain he could inflict and when… and his victim could not escape. The only remedy that existed was to submit. Kenny was in control. Submission was what he yearned for, and it gave him pleasure. Kenny Mercury wasn't human… or was he? ] [Dallas Reeves… the new apple of Kenny Mercury's eye. Kenny thought of him, this unknown nemesis. He gazed at the stars and thought of Dallas. His slimy tongue ran across his lips… Kenny smirked and bit his bottom lip. He was fantasizing. Visualizing the match, picturing his domination over Dallas Reeves. He would break the man physically, mentally, even spiritually… He would break him until he submitted, forever declaring his inferiority to Mercury. Kenny Mercury was on the prowl, and lusting for Dallas Reeves.] [The black sky was torn in half as a shooting star shot by, ripping through the night. He chuckled to himself.] [Kenny Mercury goes all the way on the first date.] References: ((Snap, Crackle, And Pop (TM Kellogg's Rice Crispies))) ((“Buwai, glaiven” is supposed to be from Professor Frink of Matt Groenig's Simpsons))
