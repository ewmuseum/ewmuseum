Metalingus (Part 1: March Of Mephisto)(ETD Title Match)

20 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chapter One: ADDICTION (AN INTRODUCTION...) My name is Vivica Julian Valentine, I'm 24 years old... and I'm an addict. Not to what you might expect though. Usually this would start some rant about how alcohol or smack ruined my life, but I'm too smart for that shit. I'm addicted to adrenaline. The cheers of the crowd keep my engines going. The feeling I get when I'm soaring through the air. That's what I wake up for in the morning, but everyday it seems harder and harder to convince myself to toss the covers aside. Last time you saw me, I was standing tall and looking my destiny right into the eyes. Eclipsing the legend of one of the most influential people in my career, and doing something my fucked up former lover could never do... winning PTC gold. Then, destiny spit right back into my face. With a single chair shot, the accumulations of my every hope and dream came tumbling down uncontrollably. I looked helplessly into the lights above as my blood, sweat, and tears streamed down my face. Laying on my back, paralyzed in shock and agony at the hands of PTC Public Enemy #1. Things couldn't get any worse right? I thought so too. One by one everything I held dear in my life began to fall. My United States Championship was gone, my GTT6 hopes dashed in the first round, my attempt to go one on one with the "man who made GCW" faded away before I even sniffed him in Jewel in the Crown. Now I spend my time trying to piece everything I have left together and make something out of my life. Destiny turned its back on me and left me alone and distraught, wondering if this is my true calling in life. Maybe after all this time I was wrong? I should probably just turn away and call it a career... but addiction is a hard thing to quit. Just ask anyone as they put another needle in their arm, they know what they're doing. They just can't seem to stop the craving. I can't stop the craving. I'm left at a crossroads, after living the longest couple of months in my entire life. There are so many things I did wrong along the way. Now it's about much more than some silly destiny of gold, it's about taking my life back. A life, which I once loved more than anything. They say that you can't change the past... but I can change my future. I can change the course of the rest of my life in one night. Instead of waiting for destiny to lead me, I'm taking control of my own fate. Even if I have to make a decision that I may regret for the rest of my life. Chapter Two: AGONY "Here you go Markus." The sound of a familiar mother figure in his life resonated through the ears of Markus Eaux D'Artifice. He could almost never go back to listening to things the way they were before the accident. Everything was so crisp and clean, it was almost as if he lived his entire life inside of a bubble. "Thanks..." The Childe of Destiny looked down at the seared slab of meat in front of him. It smelled vile and disgusting. A good whiff of it under his nose made his stomach acids swish back and forth like a storm on the high seas. He looked up at Lia, known to most of the world as Shadow, trying to choke down the nausea while she walked out of the room. As the door closed, leaving him alone once again in the dimly lit room Markus did what he always did. Walked over to the window and looked outside before dumping the scorched, reeking meat two stories down to the earth below, where it was sure to be ravaged by the local wolves in the morning. A painful cough as he slammed the window shut again reminded him of what he had just done, and for how long. Markus tried to get used to it at first, he really did. Yet he still had the taste of a normal man, it seemed. He yearned for a taste of a cheeseburger or some lasagna, it was always his favorite. People took things so much for granted, and Markus would've done so many things over again if he could. That was the catch though. Every time he woke up, he rushed to the mirror hoping it was all just a nightmare. A year had gone by and still no luck, it began to wear down on him... and it was visibly taking its toll. The door opened again, making a beam of light from the hallway cast upon the face of The Childe of Destiny. A silhouette stopped in the doorway, staring back at the slightly blinded Markus. The skin was starting to sink in around his eyes and the one well covered skeletal structure of his face was beginning to become more and more crisp everyday. His collarbones stuck out of his flesh like twin horns, and his shoulder blades like a hunch on his back. "Close the door..." Markus said still squinting away from the light pulling the hood from his jacket over his head. "This isn't normal, is it?" Vivica J. Valentine closed the door behind her, letting Markus grow a little bit more comfortable as they both sat down in a small round table in the corner of the musky room. "Shadow and Virgil... they don't look like you do." "I'm fine." Markus said, his eyes moving away from those of The Black Mariah. "Quit being such a stubborn ass." Vivica retaliated rather quickly; living in the McFarlane Estate with Virgil Ambrosi had put her a little on edge lately. Subtlety wasn't something that she'd ever done well, and constantly being in a psychological struggle certainly didn't help that any. "What would you know about being like this anyway?" Markus stated before coughing uncontrollably. Falling out of the chair onto one knee he tried to regain his composure and get back up to his chair to show Vivica there was nothing wrong. That was hard to do though... when you feel like you're drowning. "I know that wasn't normal either." Vivica said, "We need to get you some help, you need medical attention." "That's a great fucking idea!" Markus said looking up at Valentine from under the black hood, "We'll just get a phone book and look up vampire doctors! It shouldn't be that hard right?" "Hello?" Markus said sarcastically as he pulled himself up to his feet, "I'm dead... do you think that would cause a problem with my diagnosis?" "I get the point." Vivica said with her head in her hands, "I have enough shit to deal with besides your fucking attitude. If you don't want my help... then just starve yourself to death in your little room here and I'll have fun explaining to Lia and Virgil what in the hell happened. You're such a selfish prick sometimes. People in this house give a shit about you, contrary to your beliefs. If you don't care what happens to you, too fucking bad Markus. We do." "Shadow and Virgil, they look at you and they see themselves a long time ago," Vivica said pushing herself up to her feet, looking the bigger Markus Eaux D'Artifice in the eyes. "They were just like you once upon a time. It wasn't something they grew up wanting to be, I'm sure as hell they didn't plan their lives to end up like that. They've accepted it, and I suggest you do the same. Because I don't know what I'd do without you... and I don't plan on finding out anytime soon." The realization of Vivica's words hit Markus like a semi truck. Sitting down on the bed, he looked out the window. He couldn't look her in the eyes anymore, not like this. The Black Mariah tried her best to push down the lump in her throat. It had be a very hard couple of months on her, probably the hardest in her life. Emotionally and mentally unstable, Vivica needed to get away... it was too overwhelming to stay here any longer. "If you go and die on me... you better fucking hope there is no such thing as hell." The Black Mariah choked out the words on the edge of a mental breakdown, "Because if there is... I'm going to find you, and I'm going to beat the shit out of you until they won't let me anymore. That's a promise." Chapter Three: MARCH OF MEPHISTO Sweet home Chicago. At least that's what it used to be. Although it appeared as if Chicago, Illinois hadn't changed at all over the past few months since she left... everything felt different. The crisp winter air reminded her of a much simpler time as she walked the streets. The cold, metal and stone building still towered over her head as her breath tumbled in the breeze until it could no longer be seen. Most people find the cold weather to be annoying, but to The Black Mariah, it was relaxing. It was home. Walking the streets alone was somewhat of a missed memory. A time where there wasn't so much pressure, a time where everything was simpler. There was a down side to the fame that Vivica craved so much. The sight of the baby blue tips of her hair and the black leather bomber jacket was very recognizable to more people than she believed. A walk that she thought would be a chance to get away from it all was quickly turning into a delayed autograph session. Her recent failures had almost made her forget how much she accomplished since the last time she REALLY called Chicago home. It was a necessary evil though... for this is the life that she chose to live. A life of pain and glory, sacrifice and dreams, passion and heartache. A life surrounded by thousands of people and security, instead of the frigid Christmas air and buildings as high as the eye could see. What exactly had gone wrong along the way for her to end up like this? At odds in the very house that she was so excited to be a part of before her first ETD match against Archibald MacGregor. "Archibald..." Vivica still saw the images in her mind. The cheering fans and the flashing lights, the look of heartache as the chair came tumbling down. Her entire body limp and covered with blood. The fans in the crowd disgusted and heartbroken. They thought their lives were over that night... but they felt nothing compared to the pain and agony of The Black Mariah. She didn't just let down a couple million people, she let down herself. She let down PrimeTime Central. Now the entire world viewed her as incompetent... a fresh faced talent not living up to their potential. Yesterday's news, a lost cause. Although a lesson had been learned that night, it appeared as if the world had already given up on The Black Mariah. Loss after loss came following that night, sending her into an endless cycle of desperation and frustration. Ever her own teammates began to lose faith in her. What can you do when every step you take seems to be the next step down towards inevitable failure? Have you ever had the feeling that you're being watched? "Has it been so long that you've forgotten my name?" The feeling of Vivica J. Valentine came true as she spun around towards the alley way that she'd just passed. The sound of footsteps began to resonate from the acoustic chamber the skyline of The Windy City created. As the man stepped under the street light, the deep thoughts of Vivica came to a screeching halt. The look of deep confusion that once crossed her face turned into one of fear. She knew this day would come, the day that the devil came back. After all, the devil never forgets when he is betrayed... "You can call me Christian." The make spoke completely emotionless from behind a pair of dark sunglasses that he wore, even at night, "You used to call me Chris. Although Chris is something that I haven’t gone by in how many months now?" Vivica's frostbitten cheeks and brilliant blue-green eyes just looked back at the man once known as The Midnight Messiah. Dressed in an expensive black suit, complete with vest and tie, over top a white collared dress suit... Christian Zenith didn't look like the same man that Vivica left. He looked a lot more like the man she'd met. He was healthier, and seemingly more in touch with the world around him than the tunnel visioned nervous wreck that spent days locked in his room. His jet black hair glistened under the street lamp as he took a step closer, with the moonlight reflecting off of his sunglasses as he took them off and put them into his jacket. "You don't mind if I invite a little company do you?" Christian Zenith continued to speak to the abnormally mute PTC Infinite Gauntlet Champion. Her response was still silence. From the alley a group of familiar faces began to appear, most notably Jason LeMaster of Global Wrestling bodyguard fame. Along with five or six friends. "We're all friends here right?" Christian spoke with a bit of a smirk as he closed in closer and closer to Vivica. Standing about a couple inches away from her, The Conscious Crisis stopped and looked down at her. His piercing cadet blue eyes looking up and down his former fiance, "We're not here to hurt you Vivica. We saw you around town and thought we'd pay a visit." "I'm not sure if I'm in a visiting mood." Vivica spoke to Christian as their eyes locked, almost as if they were having a stare-off. Miss Valentine broke eye contact and looked away towards the ground before taking a glance at Jason behind Zenith, who waved greetingly. "I understand." Christian backed away a little bit, going closer to the members of The Hunting Grounds that stood behind him. It had been a long time since Vivica had heard or thought about those words. It felt more like a lifetime than just a year, "Why don't you come and stay at the Manor tonight? Your room hasn't been touched since you left Vivica. We left it for you." "I have a home..." Vivica said, trying her best to fight back the anger towards the disappointment that she had looking out into that crowd and not seeing him there for her that night. It was her shining moment in the sun, her big dream was going to be realized and he couldn't even be assed to get out of his chair at home to go and watch her. What a heartless bastard, "If you excuse me, it's getting dark... I should probably be heading that way." "A home?" Zenith questioned Vivica's statement, "Home is where the heart is right? A home is your sanctuary... a home is where you feel immortal, untouchable, like there isn't a care in the world. Is that how you feel at 'home' Viv?" "A place that houses a group of people who are about revolutionizing the industry!" Christian spoke as if it was a monumentous occasion, "As long as revolutionizing the industry comes under the name Shadow... and not Vivica J. Valentine." "Lia didn't ask for anyth..." Vivica verbally spared with The Midnight Messiah before being cut-off again. "She doesn't have to." Christian's cadet blue eyes peered through Vivica's soul, "With one snap of the finger Virgil Ambrosi will do anything Lia damn well pleases. It isn't very hard to see that Dawn is still obsessed with the girl. Where is the stable when you're the one with the problems Viv? They're in the locker room waiting for you... so they can ridicule you and push you down. Break your will so that one day, you'll be just as broken as Virgil is." "You have no grounds to talk do you?" Vivica snarled back. "I do have grounds to talk!" Christian answered, "I let you do whatever you guys wanted to do... every single one of you had free reign. Some of you took it... and some of you didn't. Dawn and Tessa didn't even now what they wanted to do with all of my power at their disposal. So much so that they did nothing but slow us down with their inabilities to decide what they want out of life." "And Lia?" Vivica questioned. "Lia didn't have the patience..." Christian looked back at Vivica, "Shadow was strapped and bound to her title and was so dominating that she couldn't even get a glance at what she really wanted. That's why she jumped ship the second she had the chance... and why all of you went with her. She's a leader, she's decisive, and knows exactly what she wants." Vivica pondered Christian's statements, before he looked into her eyes before saying: "Just like you." The Black Mariah looked back up at The Midnight Messiah who smirked back at her. For the first time she became interested... very interested. "That Archibald MacGregor incident really did a number on you." Christian stated, "Deep down inside you were never really much of a follower. You're a leader Vivica. You inspire, you're strong and you can do anything you set your mind to... regardless at what Virgil Ambrosi has to say. That is why I'm going to give you the opportunity to take your life back Vivica, one more shot at achieving your dream." "What are you getting out of this Christian?" Vivica was quick to question the motives of the slick charismatic business man. "Nothing... really." Zenith responded shrugging his shoulders, "Besides seeing someone I care about succeed. Let's talk more about this in the morning, you look like you're freezing cold. We need to get you inside. Jason?" "Yeah Christian?" Jason LeMaster responded from behind him. "Could you take Miss Valentine to the limousine and get her back to the Manor?" Christian ordered as Jason walked past Christian. "I'm a big girl..." Vivica said, "I can escort myself." "Of course." Christian said as Vivica walked past both him and Jason towards the group of Hunting Grounds members in the alley. As she passed them, they all followed behind towards the black limo that must've been parked in the alley. Once they all disappeared out of sight, an arrogant smirk came across the face of The Midnight Messiah. "It's like taking candy from a baby..."
