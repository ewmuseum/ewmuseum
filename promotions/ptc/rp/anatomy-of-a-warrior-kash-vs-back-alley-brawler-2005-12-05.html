Anatomy of a Warrior (Kash vs. Back Alley Brawler)

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 The auditorium bustles as everyone settles into their worn padded seats. Between picking the foam from the head cushion of one to kicking the support struts of another, people pass the time, waiting for the point of today’s venture. "Come on you lot, settle down." The voice comes from the left hand side of the stage and the voice of reason steps out, up behind the podium. Sporting small glasses perched on the end of his nose and a weird mix of jeans and a casual jacket, he doesn’t look like a typical speaker at a university but then again, given the people in the audience, we are not exactly having a typical afternoon. "Ok, now, let’s see, right, now … come on, settle down, this is a talk, not a rodeo." People eventually settle down, the numerous near silent conversations carry on in hush tones, regardless of the order of silence. "We have a hell of a lot to get through today so let’s get right into it. This is lecture four in the series of "What Makes A Warrior" and to be honest, I think it’s going to be the most interesting. Diving straight in, slide one please!" The slide projector gives its usual distinctive click and the first luminescent slide shines across the room. It shows a promotional photograph of a man known to us all as the former WWA World Champion, Kash. Standing proud with the WWA Championship belt around his waist, we can see a gleam in his eye, one that has been missing from WWA rings as of late "Very quickly, first impressions, come on, shout them out … you, Donna, come on, what do you see?" The professor points to a young bookish girl hiding behind her books. "Come on Donna, what do you see straight off the bat, come on." She grimaces at being singled out, then squeaks an answer of just three words. "Lack of confidence." The professor looks over his glasses at her. "Explain, you know the rules." Donna sits upright, like an idea has washed over her. "Tattoos, piercing, obviously died hair, his body language, it all screams look at the outside, ignore the inside. I would guess he is a very shy man, avoidant personality." The professor smiles. "Slide two please!" Another click, another slide. This one shows Kash at a graveyard, leaning back first against a headstone. He is staring at a stone in his hand, a pensive look to him. "Avoidant personality, a loner, a misfit, I was hoping it would take you a few more tries to get to this slide but it appears Donna has beaten the system. Yes, we can see he is a loner, but look closer, what do you see also in him." Murmurs from the assembled crowd, they do not know quite what to say. "What does he remind you of?" One of the females from the back of the class speaks up. "A … child?" She sounds confused, her words struggling to understand what her mind is seeing. "Yes, a child, what we see here is a man for whom life has come to quickly. You see, when I decided to pick this man for today’s lecture, I figured it was my responsibility to research him as best I could, not just listen to the WWA propaganda they would have you believe about their wrestlers. What I found was, well, disturbing to say the least. This man was literally born onto the streets, an orphan, raised by street dwellers, ramps, hobos, whatever you want to name them, he was raised by them. His father is unknown, his mother died during the back alley labour that gave life to him, the very epitome of the Frankenstein mythos, killing his creator." The assembled body of students start to look a little bored. "Now, we move onto the physical toll that his profession has taken on him. Slide three please." Click! Appearing on the screen is a photo of Kash taken after his loss at Rage in the Cage 2003, after his bloody loss to Damian Thorne. "Now, who can tell me what is wrong with this picture?" People murmur for a while, pondering the image, until one guy speaks up. "His piercing are missing." The professor smiles and nods his head. "His piercings, are missing. Not just missing, torn out from his flesh like a lion would tear out the hide of a buffalo. You see the tattered and torn skin around the wounds, that shows us that he was in a war. He lost, by the way, but this match, this match made him as a hero, it set him on the path to greatness, and all of this from a man with no family, no home, and no confidence in himself. Amusing to think of it that way, that one of the greatest modern day gladiators is a bigger mental screw up than most of the people in this room." A smile crosses a lot of faces, something about this man seems to be striking a chord. "So, where does that leave us, we have found out that this man has no confidence, no self esteem, is a loner and misfit, and a man who was born in a cage of blood and brutality, and this is a hero? Can I have a show of hands, how many of you would class this man as a hero." A couple of people nonchalantly wave their hands, the apathy showing through. "How many of you would believe me if I told you that his next step to becoming a hero was to become the epitome of evil? Now, I hear you ask, how can that be possible? Well, here, this will explain it. Slide four please." Click! Slide four shows Kash laying on the ground, moments after getting smashed in the face with a hammer. "The man with the hammer is Lockdown, the man on the floor is our hero. That one shot changed our subject from a man of light to a man of darkness. Fractured eye socket, broken nose and cheek bone, smashed palate, he took one hell of a beating, but did not let this beat him. He returned, his face covered in this …" Slide five clicks into view and it shows the solid black mask that Kash wore during 2003. "Carved with occult sigils and mystical etchings, this mask was the symbol of all the bad things Kash could do. He used fire, animals, blood, all implements of insane torture. And you know what? He was cheered! The fans loved it! They lapped it up! Nothing would please the more than to see Kash torment some other poor soul with a mystical trick, he was on fire, and it showed, for that was when our hero struck his major goal in wrestling, he defeated the evil to become the champion. Yes, a gold belt doesn’t mean much as a hero, but his deed in capturing the title is the very essence of the hero. But where does that take us from here?" A hand comes up from the back of the auditorium. "Professor, how can a man who practices evil acts be classed as a hero, surely its an oxymoron?" The professor removes is glasses and motions to the boy with the imagination. "How very true, how can you classify Kash as a hero when all he did was evil? How can you justify naming a sociopath, a hero? Well, I believe the answer to this to be very simple. Man cannot be judged on his actions alone, it’s his strength of character that will define us in front of St Peter, not our good or bad actions. Kash may have committed evil against his enemies, but his character was pure and his soul was strong. Kash was not evil, he was just an extreme form of good." Question answered, the professor continues. "Where does that take us from here? Where does that take the man we know and understand as Kash. He has won the title, performed his good deed, what does the world have in store for him now? Well, I have the answer. He went on to defeat everyone, he defeated legends and rookies, he did not back down from anyone and he only lost that title when he was hurt by a man known as Leviathan. In the defence of his title, he was thrown from a ledge through a stack of electrical crates and was nearly electrocuted, but that was not the end for Kash, no no." The professor pauses for breath. "You see, as a true hero should, he did not fall too far behind, he kept his eye on the ball and watched as the WWA went off in a strange new direction, one that would steadily open for the return of the man called Kash, and this return came in a brutal way." Another slide comes into view, this one of a massive steel cage housing many of the WWA stars we know and love. "Rage in the Cage 2004, Kash returned in secret and nearly took the gold, eliminating his friends along the way. He acted as a shining light for the WWA, standing proud for the beliefs they held." A voice interrupts the train of thought that the professor has spent so long getting into. "Why should we believe him as a hero, wrestlers are actors!" A smile crosses the wise face of the professor. "Actors? No. Entertainers, yes. You see, all the men who step into the WWA ring are highly trained athletes who accept the risks to their health when they step between those ropes. Kash knew the risks and he took them every time. He saw his friends fall to injury and began aiming high for the top spot in the company, the Champion!" Another slide crosses onto the screen. This one of a face we only saw briefly, a man named Winter. "For every hero and heroine, there must be a villain. And this man was a villain. His name was Winter and he was responsible for breaking Kash’ neck and putting how out of the game for a long time. This man represented everything Kash stood against, However, he did not succeed totally, for Kash returned to us, returned to the ring and … and … The professor seems uncomfortable and he starts to scratch his face. "Are you ok professor?" The professor starts t talk, despite him fighting valiantly with his face. "I’m fine, I’m fine, just … sorting myself out. The professor turns his back to his class and when they look closer, they see the face of the professor stretched out like a latex mask. The hair hangs down naturally as the man posing as the professor reveals himself. "Now, where were we?" The class is stunned as the man they have spent ages discussing, Kash, stands before them, posing as a professor. "I am sure you are all wondering what I am doing her eon such short notice. Well, it seems I have an announcement to make. You see, I retried from active competition in the WWA to work as a road agent, but right now, I see something I do not like." Kash looks out at all the audience. "You see, I entered myself in the GTT5 tournament, a tournament that means I have the chance to feel the soft impact of bone on flesh again, but it seems I have drawn a bad egg in the first round. You see, Back Alley Brawler, Joe Balboa, whatever he wants to call himself, he can’t claim to be "Back Alley" Kash sucks his gut in. "Back Alley Brawler is what happens when you leave milk in the sun for too long, it turns to crap and that’s what has happened with Mr Balboa. Can anyone here tell me what sort of excuse drugs are? They are no excuse, they are the sign of a diseased mind and a mind like his is so diseased, it is terminal. Balboa likes to think he can impress everyone by slaughtering the English language, but it doesn’t impress me, it sounds like he is talking to a four year old for Pete’s sake!" A smile crosses the evil face of Kash. "Does anyone else here think that Back Alley Brawler is another good idea to call gay love? Anyway, I digress. Back alley Brawler is man who is ready to fall hard at GTT5, but he just does not know it yet." All of a sudden a ruckus is heard from the other side of the auditorium and the real professor appears, stripped to his vest and shorts, tied to a wooden chair. "where do that con man go!!" As they all turn around, they see Kash is gone, all apart from a business card that reads … The Wicca Man is Coming!!
