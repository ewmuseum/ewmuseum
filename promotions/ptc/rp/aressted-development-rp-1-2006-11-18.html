Date: 2006-11-18 _________________ Seattle, P.D. 2:12 P.M. Seattle, WA, USA Nov. 03/06 _________________ "Carnes." An officer calls out. "You've got a guest." The room isn't a typical 'Hollywood' cell. Instead there is a bullet-proof glass window surrounding the entire cell. Brick and concrete form the base of her current home. Rising from the small bed in the corner of the room, Kirsten walks towards the glass, where it meets the door. As she waits for a familiar face or any miracle that could save her from her predicament, her heart sinks as Angelica heads towards her. A look of confusion forms upon her face and she places her right hand against the glass door. "Kirsten." Angelica says as she draws closer to the door. "Are you ok in there?" "What do you think Angelica?" Kirsten says in a huff before turning her back to her friend. "Just who the hell are you Angelica?" Thinking for a second, Angelica smiles. "I'm the same person you saved that night. Remember? I know I can only recall what you've told me, but three men found me in a park on Christmas and brought me to your shelter." "Well then who in God's name is Kiara Leigh Martell?" Kirsten spins back towards Angelica with a look of dismay on her face. Kirsten's words send Angelica into a silent state. Standing blankly, she finally looks into Kirsten's eyes. "I don't know." Kirsten places her hands squarely on her hips. "Do you have a twin sister? Is there something you're not telling me?" "Kirsten you've been the only family I've had and that I can remember for years. At least the only family I've wanted to remember. You know about my step father, I could never go back to that." Angelica says as she approaches the door. "I just don't know what to think. I've met Kiara's parents, even they think I abducted her." Frowning, Kirsten steps closer towards Angelica. "I know what that monster did to you; you ran and never looked back. I can only begin to comprehend what that man put you through." Angelica looks down at her feet, her long brown hair drops in front of her face and a sigh escapes from under her breath. "The doctor says that I have a brain tumour." She finally sputters out, as if hoping to change the subject. "He said that I don't have much to worry about. I haven't had seizures since you found me and the tumour appears to be dormant." "I know." Kirsten crouches to see Angelica's eyes; those lovingly-troubled brown eyes. Eventually the two meet each other's respective gaze. "I found out just before your match with Danielle. I find it hard to believe that they didn't notice this when I took you into the hospital after we found you convulsing in the park." Lifting her head so that Kirsten can rise up, Angelica manages to flash a brief smile. "What were Kiara's parents like?" Immediately anger fills Kirsten's eyes; her blood boiling beneath the surface, now apparent as her face turns bright red. "Why?" Is all she can manage to spit out, hoping to keep her calm. "Well," Angelica begins, even though she can tell Kirsten is clearly angry with the question. "I've just never met my parents, I just wanted to know what they were like." Eventually the anger subsides after a couple seconds pass. Kirsten begins to recall her brief encounter with Kiara's parents; she had attempted to block the encounter, since it wasn't of the friendly variety. Taken back for a moment, Kirsten backs up and sits on the bed in the corner. "Her mother, Mrs. Martell…" Kirsten closes her eyes and soon a stream of tears travel down the gentle curvatures of her face. "You look just like her." "I do?" The question slips from Angelica's mouth. "But how?" Kirsten lifts her head and opens her reddened eyes. "Don't you see? You are Kiara! There is no way that picture and Mrs. Martell's resemblance could be a coincidence; it's just not possible." Angelica tries to get something out in reply, but seems to trip over her own tongue. "You've got to tell me Angelica. Are you Kiara? Only you can clear my name for this damn charge." Kirsten pleads as if only consumed by greed, just wanting to get out of prison. "Wait, that came out wrong. It's just, with all that's happening I don't know how to feel. I've trusted you for the last eleven years, you've been like my sister, my own blood. I need you to tell me if you know anything." Stepping back from the door which separates the two, Angelica bumps into the brick wall behind her. Her face flushed, her eyes cold, she opens her mouth but nothing escapes. Inhaling instead, she closes her eyes and allows her body to slump down to the floor in a crumbling heap. Breaking down just as her friend has, Angelica finally opens her eyes and looks into the face of Kirsten. "I have too much on my mind Kirsten," Angelica begins. "You're the one person in this world that can prove that I am Angelica Dominique. You are the one person that I confided my…" she pauses. "Well, you know, my past." "Angelica," Kirsten starts but is promptly interrupted. "Listen to me now." Angelica says with her eyes flaring. "I've listened to you rant and plead your case. You owe me this much as a friend; as your blood, like you said." Snuggling the grey blanket on the bed around her shoulders, Kirsten just sits back and waits for Angelica to continue. "All I know is that my name is Angelica. I used to suffer from seizure caused from this damn tumour." Wiping the steady stream of tears, Angelica continues. "I don't remember much from my childhood except that I wanted to get away from it. My mother was alright, but she was so wrong for loving that man. You know all this of course." She stops, then takes a moment to gaze into Kirsten’s lovely green eyes. Both are glistening in the light from the swells of water forming at each side. "You rescued me from the pain, you've taught me everything I know with the help of Realm of Darkness wrestling schools. Siefer and you family to me. I need you two more now than ever before; I actually beat Danielle Northsky and I'm now scheduled to meet the Phantom Republican." Almost forgetting the situation completely, Kirsten jumps to her feet. "My God Angie, do you know what this means?" Angelica shoots a look of confusion in Kirsten's general direction. "No…" "You've beat Darek DeAngleo's record in the tournament." Kirsten says with exuberance. "He's going to kill me and perhaps you for that." A hint of sarcasm fills her tone. "It's not about Darek." Angelica snaps back. "This is now. This is me and this man I know absolutely nothing about. I need you to get Siefer out of R4GE as the damn General Manager and actually have him go back to RoD so he can help me get prepared. Since you've been gone, R4GE has consumed his whole life. He's closed the doors to Realm of Darkness Wrestling schools. Even Darek was pissed about that, seeing as he donated the money to start the damn program." Stumbling from her seated position, Kirsten tosses the blanket back onto the bed. "Siefer closed the schools? That asshole." "I need you to talk to him, you're the only one he will listen to." "Why didn't he tell me when he came by earlier?" Kirsten asks. "He thought it would stress you out more, but I had to tell you. I need you, I need him and I need to find out everything I can about The Phantom Republican." Angelica tries not to sound as greedy as Kirsten once had, so quickly she follows up her comments. "Siefer has hired the best attorney for you and bail will be posted as soon as it's set. I've given a full statement to the police telling them everything I've just told you." Angelica trails off for a moment. "They even showed me the picture of Kiara. She looks just like I did, so much so it scarred me. I don't know how to feel about this, but I know you've been my rock for years and I need you now more than ever." _________________ Realm Of Darkness Wrestling School 8:27 A.M. Seattle, WA, USA Nov. 08/06 _________________ It's cold. A thick puff of fog pours out of Angelica's mouth; almost like a cartoon bubble without any words appearing over her head. The multi-colored, red, brown and yellow leafs have already fallen to the frost covered ground. The forecast for this day had called for sunny weather. Shows exactly what the weather man or woman for that matter, knows. Her heels click along the slippery paved sidewalk as she walks up towards the steps of an all too familiar building. Coming to an abrupt halt, Angelica raises her head and looks over the renovated old style building just outside the heart of Seattle's downtown area. Her eyes are immediately drawn to the large neon custom made sign hanging above the door. RoD School of Wrestling. The old brick building seems to be in excellent condition and the location not near as busy as the downtown area just a few blocks away. Still, there are pedestrians continuing about their lives; each of them of them having something different on their agenda and some with the same thing as people pour into restaurants, stores and their vehicles. She seems to stand still for a seemingly endless moment, her vision adjusting to the vehicles passing by. Focused on the hustle and bustle in the streets, Angelica seems to let her consciousness slip away. Her mind has now become subject of its own limitless power. Consumed by this grief, this pain and that God awful stench. Angelica knew all too well where she was going and it was a place she wished to never dwell upon again. Yet her mind seemed to have its own objective, overcoming her conscious effort to awaken from the trance like state. She is whisked away into a world of memory, one where memory seems so much like reality. She is there again, the darkness so black no light can be seen. Then, a faint tinge of soothing yellow light peers beneath the door and wanders into the room. Angelica pulled the sheets up over her head and clenched her pillow tight; she didn't welcome the light or the smell that soon followed. The glimmering silvery metal gives a reflection of deep fear in Angelica's eyes and small beads of sweat on her brow. The creaking sound of the door handle fills the soundless void. Frantic, quick gasps of breath fill Angelica's lungs as she closes her eyes and pretends she's not there. But no matter how hard she tries, the smell gets stronger as the light pours into the room when the door is opened. Wearing just a long white night gown, Angelica is just twelve years old. And yet her innocence has been taken from her by this monster, time and time again. It had come down to this last straw, this one instant. Her hand finds comfort on the wooden handle nestled under her pillow. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] By the time Angelica has realised what has happened, her eyes have begun to focus and she's standing outside the RoD Wrestling School. Shaking her head side to side, she tries to once again suppress the memories of that fated night. On the street beside her a red corvette pulls up and comes to a stop. The door swings open and in the cool morning breeze, Kirsten’s red hair flows freely. She is wearing a business suit with a matching skirt and the cutest red shoes. Closing the door to her car, she pushes the alarm button on her key chain. A high pitched beeping sound emits from the car and Kirsten heads towards Angelica with a smile pressed upon her gentle lips. Nearly slipping as she steps up the curb, Kirsten stretches out and Angelica reaches for her hand to help provide balance. "I'm glad you're out of there." Angelica says immediately after helping Kirsten. "You and me both." Kirsten replies as she regains her balance. "The parents and police insist on you providing them with a blood test. Siefer told me he's fighting that, so he may be late." "Unbelievable. Are they going to continue with the case?" The question is rather rhetorical. "Yes." Kirsten steps up the slippery walk way and heads towards the doors to the RoD Wrestling School. "I can't believe they set my bail at five-hundred thousand dollars. What am I, a serial killer?" Angelica notices the hint of verbal irony and laughs slightly. "It's a good thing I had enough in savings to have you sprung from there. So what's the plan now? Training, video, a case study on my opponent? How am I going to beat this Phantom Republican?" Kirsten walks up the twelve concrete steps leading towards the building, "you're not the only one fighting now Angelica. Between your tournaments, this charge I've incurred and how damn hard it will be to overcome both of these aspects; I expect that neither of us may be successful." Hanging her head, Angelica follows Kirsten up the stairs. "I know, this could be the toughest fight of either of our live and I fear just like you do, that neither of us may survive the outcome. But remembers Kirsten, you're my only family left, I need you. I won't let them put you away. Even if it means I sacrifice my psychological edge in the tournament." "It's simply been a case of bad timing." Kirsten says pushing the door open. "You and I, we're a dying breed. We are meant to fight, we are destined for better things in life." "And if I lose?" Angelica says unsure of what to hear next. "If you lose, we train harder for next year." Angelica nods at Kirsten's comment before following her through the door. "And if you're not around next year?" Kirsten pauses, the whole case flashing through her mind. She couldn't stop thinking that Angelica was really Kiara Martell, but how, why and what were her motives to trick her for so long? "I'll always be here for you Angie." Kirsten says, snapping out of the dream-like state. "This charge will be dropped I know it. I never kidnapped you and I certainly am not responsible for the disappearance of Kiara Martell."
