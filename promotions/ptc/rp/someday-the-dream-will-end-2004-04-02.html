Someday The Dream Will End

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 “It’s tournament time again!” Yes, in the land of Primetime Central, there are but few certainties. One of these certainties is that every so often, a grand tournament shall be held. Much like the fabled knights of yore, warriors from all federations in the PTC come together to compete for pride and honor in these noble battles. The man who was victorious in the last of these is, of course, Seymour Almasy. Victor in the Primetime Invitational Tournament, he held the prize, the PTC Global Title, honorably until losing the championship to Chris Storm. Since that loss, the Final Fantasy has not been heard from. But now, from the fog of obscurity cometh Seymour. Tournament has arisen him from his dormant state. But for how long? --- The bedroom of Aerith and Seymour Almasy is where the Final Fantasy and his wife can be found, and oddly enough, they aren’t even sleeping! What are the odds? Nonetheless, husband and wife stand together, mentally preparing themselves for yet another trial, yet another tribulation… Seymour Almasy: It is time for the armor to be strapped on once again. Aerith Heartilly: Good, Seymour. It’s been so long since you’ve been to battle… Seymour Almasy: Well, you know me, Aerith. I can’t pass up a tournament! Almasy’s voice lacks the power and passion it usually possesses. He stands shirtless, frowning at himself in the mirror, his omnipresent suit of armor lying in a heap on the floor beside him on the carpeted floor. Aerith Heartilly: What’s wrong, Seymour? It is a question Almasy is asking himself as well. He stares almost helplessly into his reflection, greeted by the usual well-toned figure. Lean muscle ripples in his chest, but Seymour somehow feels horribly out of shape. Seymour Almasy: It’s been so long since I’ve competed, Aerith. Aerith Heartilly: I know, but it’s okay. You were upset at losing to Storm…you didn’t even lose to him though! AWS Man’s shoulders were pinned! Seymour Almasy: No excuses, Aerith. I’m not making them…but really that’s digressing. That’s not the problem. As much as losing my championship irks me, my real problem goes deeper than wins and losses. The Final Fantasy continues to look at his reflection, surprised by the quality of his form. No blue bruises dot it, nor do any long, angry gashes rip across it like malevolent rivers. His flesh has healed in his time off, joints no longer creaking with each step. For the first time since Almasy stepped in the Battle Arena, he feels pretty darned good. Seymour Almasy: I’m completely out of the fighting frame of mind, Aerith. Aerith Heartilly: But that’s what this tournament’s for! To get you back in shape! Seymour Almasy: Aerith, you know that’s not why I’m entering it. I take a great deal of pride in my performances in these tournaments. I wouldn’t enter this tournament if I wasn’t intending to win. Aerith Heartilly: But you said it yourself, Seymour…you aren’t exactly in the frame of mind to battle. Seymour Almasy: Yes. That does not mean I am physically out of shape, however. Battling is much like riding a bicycle, once you learn, it is a skill you never forget. I could easily catch an opponent in the Fallen One, or drop him with the One Hit Kill, that is not the problem. My maneuvers are ready to be executed. I simply need to mentally prepare myself for battle. For, without exaggeration, these next few weeks are the most important yet. It is the final comment that worries Aerith. “Most important yet?” Hardly. Seymour had captured the UWF World Heavyweight Title in the past, and fought through the Primetime Invitational Tournament. Six victories later, and Seymour was on top of the mountain, the PTC Global Champion. How could this tournament, a battle for pride, be more important? Looking quizzically at her husband, she decides to verbalize her question. Aerith Heartilly: But, Seymour, you’ve fought for titles almost everywhere you’ve been. Why is this particular tournament, this particular match so important? Seymour tilts his head to one side, understanding where Aerith was coming from. Matt Jackson and Lorenzo Guevera are certainly competitors that could not be taken lightly, but from Aerith’s perspective, they are no Roulettes, no Iron Lungs, or no Chris Storms. The memory of loss tears at Seymour, as the mere mention of the PTC Global Champion’s name often does. After a few seconds of pondering, he has an answer for her. Seymour Almasy: Face it, Aerith, I’m in a slump. Aerith Heartilly: No argument there… She winces, the words coming off far harsher than she intended. Seymour seems to be okay, however. Seymour Almasy: In Global Wrestling I have lost several times. I lost the UWF World Title. I lost the PTC Global Title. Victories in the Arena have not been easy to come by. I need one desperately. As such, this is currently the most important match to me. For this is one that I can win, not one that I already have won. Aerith Heartilly: Oh, I understand now. Seymour smiles, but Aerith is still not convinced. There is something in Seymour’s eyes…something that makes her doubt him in part. Still, she manages to suppress this feeling quite quickly. Seymour had never lied to her before. Why would he pick now to start? Seymour Almasy: Therefore, this battle is absolutely must-win. I must be victorious in order to advance on in this tournament, in search of the title of the greatest cruiserweight for this year in the land of Primetime Central. Almasy’s eyes fill with fire, the fire of a man with absolutely nothing to lose. Seymour Almasy: For now, I can take every risk, every chance I have ever conceived. I do not need to worry about injury. For if I am not victorious in every match, I will not see the next contest. If I am fortunate and skillful enough to win the tournament, well, then I shall be standing atop Gagazet. If not, I will have given it my all. I will have no reason to be ashamed…unless I lose this first contest. Aerith realizes that something is definitely wrong, definitely odd, but she cannot yet place her finger on it. Aerith Heartilly: This is important to you…isn’t it? Seymour Almasy: This tournament means a lot. I was victorious in the last tournament in this land. As such, I have a sort of unofficial crown to defend. If I can win again, I shall go down in the history of this realm for two reasons. I will be the greatest cruiserweight, and the only man ever to win two tournaments in the realm of Primetime Central. Failure is not an option, Aerith. I cannot be defeated. His conviction startles her, almost to the point of being frightening. As strong as he sounds, there is a tone of weakness to his words, a tone of fear in his own right. She has no idea how to respond, save a somewhat pithy phrase. Aerith Heartilly: Of course not, Seymour. No one likes to lose. The words swirl around in Seymour’s head. No one DOES like to lose. Especially not Matt Jackson or Lorenzo Guevera. Two men with far more to gain than Seymour. Two men who, like Chris Storm, just might be hungrier than the Final Fantasy. Seymour Almasy: I know this. And that is why I have to get back in my right mind. I must focus myself on this tournament, focus on victory. You know, it’s amazing how things work, how time changes things. Back in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, I was an unknown who battled his way to a bracket championship. In the Primetime Invitational Tournament I was known, but not considered to be the best. Then, for a while, I was the best. And now, many see me on the decline. They may be correct…but for the next weeks, the next months…they shall see me rise once more. Almasy glances around the walls of the bedroom, trophies and titles hanging proudly in glass cases. The glittering gold inspires him, causing him to stand tall and proud, until his eyes hit upon the other object dominating the room: a painting of himself and Aerith side-by-side. He slumps down slightly, but does so with a smile on his face. Seymour Almasy: I love that portrait, you know. Aerith Heartilly: So do I. Where did you get it again? Seymour Almasy: A fan gave it to me after the match with Storm and AWS Man. He said I looked like I could use some cheering up. He was right. It did cheer me up. More than he could have imagined. It suddenly made that match much less important…kind of put things in perspective, you know? Aerith gives a small squeak of surprise. Rarely does Seymour take losing well. Granted, he doesn’t run screaming about the house either, but he isn’t easily pacified, either. She files away the comment in the back of her mind, along with all of the others she is hearing on this day. The Final Fantasy glances down at his feet, the pile of armor beckoning him, calling his name. He then looks up at Aerith, taking in her familiar, lovely features. His eyes move back and forth, from armor to Aerith, Aerith to armor, before finally reaching down to organize his equipment. Aerith smiles, seeing the familiar confidence returning to Seymour’s eyes. The confidence that she found so gosh-darn attractive. A playful smile comes over her face, as she resists the temptation to pounce him then and there. He didn’t like to be disturbed when putting his armor on, a fact Aerith had learned many times in the past. Aerith Heartilly: Want me to leave so you can have your privacy? Seymour Almasy: No, no, I’ll be fine. Just, no talking. As difficult as it is for you to not speak…I like quiet when preparing for battle. My calm before the storm. Aerith nods her head, almost in shock that she is allowed to stay. It is the first time in a year and a half that Seymour has permitted her to remain with him as he makes final preparations for a major battle. She had seen him dress before, but this was a special occasion. Usually he liked to be by himself for such things, but Aerith wasn’t complaining. Seymour Almasy: Thank you, Aerith. The Final Fantasy smiles, reaching for his leg gauntlets. He could probably do this in his sleep by now. The armor had been strapped on at least a hundred times by now in his career, maybe more. But this time, something is different. As the cold metal of the gauntlet touches Seymour’s shin and ankle, a slight burst of pain attacks the Final Fantasy. He grimaces slightly, remembering the source, a match in which Purple Haze forced him to submit in the UWF, the only time Almasy has ever submitted. Aerith Heartilly: What’s wrong, Seymour? Seymour Almasy: Nothing, nothing sweetie. Almasy steels his expression, hiding the grimace, and the pain goes away. The second gauntlet goes on without incident. As Seymour reaches for his breastplate, however, he grabs at his left arm, pain etched across his face, taking all of his effort not to scream. Aerith immediately rushes over, cradling her husband’s head in her arms. Aerith Heartilly: What happened, Seymour? What’s wrong?! Seymour Almasy: Damn you Roulette! Ahhhhhhh! NO! NO! I WON’T GIVE UP! The wife of the Final Fantasy looks down at Seymour, seeing his eyes squinched tight in agony. It takes Aerith a few seconds to realize what is happening, but the mention of Roulette gives her a slight clue. Aerith Heartilly: Seymour, you’re not in the Battle Arena! You’re in your house! You’ll be fine! Seymour stops screaming, opening his eyes, and looking up at Aerith, an embarrassed expression on his face. He has no explanation for the flashbacks he has experienced, simply chalking them up as reminders of what the Battle Arena brings. He tries to speak, but Aerith pulls him closer, embracing him tightly, worry still written all over her face. Aerith Heartilly: Don’t SCARE me like that! What exactly is going on here, Seymour? A tear falls from Almasy’s eye, as his wife’s worried face makes him feel extremely guilty. Seymour Almasy: Nothing’s wrong, Aer-- Aerith Heartilly: Why are you lying to me? I KNOW something’s wrong. And I would like you to tell me what it is. Seymour attempts to protest, but Aerith’s honest gaze renders him unable to do so. He sputters, but no words come out. After several more efforts, he finally manages to squeak out what he wishes to say. Seymour Almasy: I’ll tell you as soon as I get my armor on, okay? Aerith nods silently, as Seymour picks up the elusive breastplate. The next moments are extremely uncomfortable for both husband and wife, as silence reigns. Almasy manages to get the breastplate on, and attaches the arm gauntlets. As he does so, Aerith moves to take a seat on the end of the bed. Finally dressed, all of Seymour’s pain returns at once, but Almasy is able to control himself this time. Twinges of agony are in his arm, his leg, but soon tear through his body, a reminder of the sacrifices he has made throughout his short yet dangerous career. Just as quickly, they disappear, almost as if to remind Seymour what he is returning to. Aerith Heartilly: Are you ready to tell me now? Seymour Almasy: Alright, alright. I’ll tell you. Almasy reaches out to place a hand on his wife’s shoulder. Due to his slight height of 5’7”, he finds his arm lying nearly parallel to the ground. He feels Aerith move into his touch, cherishing her warmth even through the top of the sleeve of her shirt. Aerith Heartilly: Don’t be afraid, Seymour. You can tell me anything. Seymour Almasy: Well then…ever since the year changed, I haven’t been doing so well in the Battle Arena, as you’ve noticed. I’ve been trying to figure out why. It isn’t as if I’m getting destroyed, but I just keep coming up short. My abilities are still strong, but I don’t seem to be winning. Aerith Heartilly: It’s just a streak of bad luck though! Seymour Almasy: That’s what I thought. What I told myself, anyway. But after I lost to Storm, I realized why I had lost. Say what one will about Storm, but he wanted it. Aerith Heartilly: But you did too! Seymour Almasy: No. I wanted to beat him. I could have cared less about the title. I wanted to defeat Chris Storm. On the other hand, Chris Storm couldn’t have cared less whether he was facing me, or anyone else. All that he cared about was that I had the PTC Global Championship around my waist. He cared about being the best warrior in the world. At that point, I did not….and in all honesty, I still do not. Aerith Heartilly: But…but, why not? You used to care so much! Seymour frowns slightly, having known this would be Aerith’s reaction. Still, he must say what needs to be said. Seymour Almasy: I don’t care anymore because I have achieved what I wish to achieve. The ascent up Mount Gagazet was a wonderful one. But now, I am being forced down the mountain. I do not wish to descend. And that is why I am in this tournament. To see if I can’t get back to the top of that mountain one more time. To stand at the peak of Gagazet, just once more. Aerith begins to understand. Almasy’s words certainly seem to be leading somewhere. “Forced down the mountain.” “Just once more.” The moment that seems to be at hand is one that Aerith had known would come, but one that she had never really thought about. It was worrisome and blissful at the same time. She could do nothing but sit in her spot on the edge of the bed, waiting for the words to be spoken. Seymour Almasy: Aerith, once I am eliminated from this tournament, once it is over, I shall retire from the Battle Arena. The words sounded exactly as she thought they would sound. Completely ridiculous. She knew someday Seymour would have to hang up the armor. She had even thought about what they would do afterwards. She had left college in her senior year in order to help him. With only a few credits left, she could easily go finish, after all, they had saved enough from Seymour’s battles to allow it…but she feels angry at herself at this thought. Somehow, she feels that it is cheapening the moment. Reproaching herself, she looks across at Seymour incredulously, wondering what has brought this about. Aerith Heartilly: But why, Seymour? You love battling! Almasy grins, and nods his head. Seymour Almasy: I do love battling. But there is something I love more than battling. Aerith Heartilly: And that would be? Seymour Almasy: You, Aerith. A familiar flush begins to come over Aerith’s cheeks, turning bright red, and causing her to wrap her arms around her husband’s waist. Aerith Heartilly: Thank you, Seymour. I’m flattered, but I don’t know what that has to do with you giving up the Battle Arena… Seymour looks at his wife. Memories flood back to him, memories of seeing her in a hospital bed, comatose. It is a memory he never wishes to have to relive again. Seymour Almasy: Aerith, if we continue, there is always the chance of either you or I being severely injured. You already have been hospitalized. I cannot allow that to happen to either of us again. Aerith Heartilly: I understand, Seymour. But we can take precautions. If you really want to continue, please do. Seymour Almasy: That’s just it, Aerith. I don’t want to continue. I don’t know what I shall do as a career for the rest of my life, and I have time to think about that, but I do know what I want now. His arms wrap lovingly around his wife as Aerith blushing increases, her own arms tightening around him, clutching at him desperately. Aerith Heartilly: Do you…do you mean it? Seymour Almasy: Yes, Aerith. What I want for the rest of my life is you. No more fighting. No more getting injured. No more having to prepare for opponent after opponent. I want to be able to sit with you on the beach at Costa Del Sol. I want to be able to see the Planet with you. I want to be yours. Forever. The Final Fantasy beams, as Aerith searches desperately for something to say, but words fail her. However, her mouth still works quite well, as she stands up in his grasp, locking her mouth over his in a passionate, deep kiss. Soft moans and whimpers are all the sound that can be heard for several seconds, until both pull back, sucking in air in great, heaving gasps. Seymour Almasy: You are far more important than glory, riches, or fame. You’re everything to me, Aerith. It’s time that I got my priorities in order. I’ve been thinking about this all for a long time. I always knew that, someday, the dream would end… Aerith Heartilly: Not this week, though. She smiles, as Seymour returns the gesture, nodding. Seymour Almasy: No, not this week. And not the next one. Nor the next…nor the next. But for now, I shall take it one week at a time, one match at a time. Jackson, Guevera, you now face a man who is staking his entire reputation, everything he stands for in the Battle Arena, on one single match. One contest, bell to bell. Willing to put everything on the line. Can they defeat such a man, on such an occasion? I say no, Aerith. They can and will bring their best, but I shall triumph. I shall move on. And anyone who thinks otherwise… Aerith Heartilly: Is living in a fantasy world. For an eternal moment, husband and wife look into one another’s eyes, before finally kissing once again, as the touching scene fades slowly
