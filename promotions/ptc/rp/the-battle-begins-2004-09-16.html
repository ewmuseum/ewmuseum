The Battle Begins

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Greed is the desire for material wealth or gain, ignoring the realm of the spiritual. It is also called Avarice or Covetousness. Why you do it: You live in possibly the most pampered, consumerist society since the Roman Empire. Your punishment in Hell will be: You'll be boiled alive in oil. Bear in mind that it's the finest, most luxurious boiling oil that money can buy, but it's still boiling. credit: http://www.deadlysins.com for the definition and other information about it. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Why do I get stuck facing someone who turned her back on the very fed she had held a belt in? Who knows for certain. All that needs to be known is that I, Patti Anderson, will not take this challenge lightly. Jade was a friend, a sister, and above all, a competitor that I would have been thrilled to build a stable with. Instead she used her greed to get what she wanted. Jade used my friendship to assist her in getting the South Wind Title. She knew she couldn't defeat the likes of Featured Attraction without my help. Following that win, Jade used our friendship to push ahead and that was until Corcidon came into the 4WW. That's when the downfall of Jade started. Lacuna Debris was a friend that was brought in by Jade to help become the most dominate female based stable in the 4WW, but like everything else that happens within the walls of the 4WW, a snag happened. Corky used her dominate status and chased both Debris and Jade out of the fed, leaving me to prove to everyone why I am the most dominate female in the federation. I was the one that had to show Corky who the alpha female was. Jade and Lacuna both proved that their greed was greater then what the 4WW has to offer, so they up and left. Now comes the day of judgment is before/upon us, as Jade is to step inside the ring and face me. There will be no holding back, nor will there be any doubt that I will make sure that she doesn't advance any further in this tournament. She proved that the only way she can succeed is to use her greed to attain the heights. Sadly though, it will lead to a prominate position in hell, where she'll be promptly boiled in the finest oil that money can buy. Such a sad ending to someone who used to be the best. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Time: Where I Don't Want To Be Place: Living Room ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Patti Anderson laid on the couch. Her long, bare legs stretched out on the couch. Her upper body covered in one of the many shirts that she packed that belongs to Sean McClancy. A cardboard carton of her sinful delight rested in her hands with a tablespoon sticking out of the top of the carton rested on the coffee table before her, sitting next to that was a box of tissues with a few used one sitting around it. A few toys graced the floor that belonged to the soon to be newest member of the family. An old black and white movie graced the television adding a glow to the darken room. Her hazel eyes lay half closed as red hair lay matted to her face, she seemed half interested in the movie that was before her. Her right hand reached over and grabbed the spoon, and drug it to her mouth. The spoon slid out of her mouth, a nice dome shape left on her spoon. The credits from the movie began to roll as the screen cuts in half as the local news takes their time to highlight their shows. Patti reaches down on the floor searching for the remote. She's not really in the mood to hear about the latest murders and fighting going on all over the world. As her hand grabbed the remote to do some channel surfing a teaser for the news hit about the upcoming Global Title Tournament. Patti rolled her eyes in disgust as she still couldn't figure out why her boss, Ian Chidlress, talked her into enrolling in the tournament. She hated them with a passion, she didn't feel she had to prove herself to others that she was one of the best. She entered the Global Title match the first time thinking she had the chance to prove to the 'good old boys' that a female could hold their little title was to much, so they pretty much jobbed her out to the men that she was facing. That right there was enough to turn her off on the inter fed competitions for a while. Patti sat up as she grabbed for a tissue to battle the allergy attack that was hitting her. The damn cleaning company's dusting policy left something to be desired and she always paid for it when she came home for those short amounts of time. Her hazel eyes stared at the telly as she waited for the news to come on about the Global Title Tournament. "Good Evening. We start off tonight's news with a major announcement about the Global Title Tournament. PTC Owner Argyle has issued the bracket listings for the latest rounds of the Global Title Tournament. I would have to say that the match that has everyone paying close attention is the one involving two of the main Divas of PTC. One being the veteran Jade. The other is the rising star in PTC in the form of Patti Anderson. The fans of the PTC are chomping at the bit to get this one. Patti sits stunned, her hazel eyes staring at the television set. The announcer continues to read the other matches scheduled on the card. Patti couldn't believe her ears that she would be facing her one time friend. "Patti, what's the matter?" A curious voice coming from across the room fills the quiet room. Patti turns her head briefly and sees her main reason for coming back home for a while. Raven, the dark haired little girl that she had come to love. Patti opens her arms to the little girl and beckons her to come over to her. Patti's frown turns into a smile as she stares into the bright blue eyes. She toss ells her hair as she pulls the young girl up onto her lap. The girls night gown bunches up around her knees as she rests her head against Patti's chest, listening to the comforting sounds of her heart. "Patti, what's the matter?" the child asks of her again." "I just found out whom I'm facing in the tournament that Mr. Childress wanted me to enter." "Oh yeah? Who did you draw?" a note of excitement enters Raven's voice. "I drew my old friend Jade." "Jade? Who's that?" Patti looked lovingly into the girl's eyes as she tried to find the right words to explain. "Well Raven, Jade is an old friend of mine. She and I used to be in the 4WW together, but due to Jade getting greedy, she left. Leaving me all alone to do battle with the other females in the federation." Raven pouted as she looked up lovingly at Patti. “She sounds like a mean lady. I hope you beat her up really good.” "I'll tell you what ... I'll beat Jade up to the point that even yer own grandfather wouldn't want anything to do with her." "You promise?" Raven asks with a child like innocence that would make even the hardest of hearts melt. "I promise. Jade's greed will bring about her downfall." "Ok young lady, you need to get to bed and get some sleep if you intend on going back to Japan with me. Grandpapa Minos and Grandpapa Darek will be waiting to see you, as well as yer Aunt Caitlin." Raven wrapped her arms around Patti's neck and kissed her on the cheek. She slipped off of Patti's lap and skipped off to her bedroom. Patti watched the light go on and then off in her bedroom, as the lullaby album Minos had bought for her. Patti leaned back against the couch and smiled. For the first time in her rookie career she felt confidant over facing a veteran. For the first time, greed was seething into Patti's veins as the desire to conquire the one time dominate female of PTC. "So Jade...It has finally come to this. You who were once the dominate female, have now become replaced by the one whom you tried to groom in yer shadow. . While you went off on yer bloody excursion to find yerself, I climbed to the heights of the 4WW and PTC. I claimed the World Title and the tag team title within a matter of months...what did you bloody accomplish?? Nothing. You were forced to the shadows, while I grew stronger and demanded the spotlight. Now it is time for the teacher to be taken to school by her student. Come the end of the week, you will learn what greed can do to one....." Patti looks to the television as the news broadcast comes to an end. The screen quickly flashes to head shots of Patti Anderson and Jade, as the announcer once again reminds the viewers that this is one will be one of the most watched matches. thewitchnineve: Patti stares at the screen as if mesmerized by the site of her friend. Her face goes from stone cold to an evil sadistic smile as she believes in herself. And fade out to a nice pretty purple and black.
