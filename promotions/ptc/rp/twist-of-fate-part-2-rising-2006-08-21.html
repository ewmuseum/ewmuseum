Twist Of Fate (Part 2: Rising)

21 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-21 -------------------------------------------------------------------- Interlude: Who I Am -------------------------------------------------------------------- Have you ever forgotten what loneliness feels like? Have you ever been around people so long that you have to sit down and think about what it is that you're really feeling? That's what I'm going through right now. Loneliness isn't a big deal to most people. A lot of people like being alone, it gives them peace. Not me. I probably shouldn't admit this, but it's probably my biggest fear… dying alone. Looking down at the diamond ring on my floor is a startling reminder of how real my fear is. Some people in GCW call me fearless, but it isn't true. I just fear different things that most people. After all, I've never been the type to be like everyone else. People in GCW even question what I'm doing wrestling. They just don't understand what it's like to be me. All they see is a little girl walking around with vampires. Some girls wanted to be a princess, other little girls wanted to be a doctor. I wanted to be a professional wrestler. I've always loved a good fight. Even as a little girl I used to sit in front of the television and watch people like Ricky "The Dragon" Steamboat. My "sophisticated" mother didn't understand the art and talent behind professional wrestling. I'm not just a wrestler, I'm an artisan. My dream began to take shape at Seven Of The Best last year. I was sitting in the front row, rooting Christian Zenith on hoping to come back to Global Wrestling with something else to stick in their pathetic faces. Something else for people like Eryc Morgan to fail at doing while we succeeded. It wasn't to be, but I did learn something. I found a hero. I found someone who personified everything I wanted to be. Karina Wolfenden showed me that I could be a professional wrestler if I put my mind to it. I didn't have to be big, or strong, or tall… all I had to have was what lies in my chest. All I needed was heart. Now the rumors of Karina's career winding down have sparked my entrance to PrimeTime Central vs. Archibald McGregor. My last chance to step into the ring against someone who showed me that I could be what I wanted to be. Now this Zenith Corp. business stands in my way. This might be my last chance to prove to the world what I'm made of. This might be my chance to show some other girl who may be watching out in the stands that they can achieve their dream. I've always said it's my destiny to be a champion. Torn between the love of my life and my true passion in life. Talk about being stuck between a rock and a hard place. I think I only have once choice to make… and destiny awaits. All sparks must fade. As the sun fades into the horizon, the moon is just beginning to rise. Although not quite as bright even more beautiful and significant. The only light in a world of darkness. Much easier on the eyes, and brilliant in it's own unique way. As one spark fades, I'll rise like a phoenix from it's ashes… and come into my own. -------------------------------------------------------------------- Third Stanza: The Confrontation -------------------------------------------------------------------- Bang! Bang! Bang! The sound of a fist slamming on the large oak door ripped through the tranquil silence of the Zenith Manor. A startled Vivica J. Valentine looked up at the door, it was the first time in nearly an our that she looked at something besides the diamond engagement ring that now lay on the floor in front of her bed. Spinning around, The Black Mariah positioned herself to get off the bed, picking up the ring on the way up and sitting down on the inn table next to her bed. Bang! Bang! Bang! "Time to go!" The sound of Marques' voice echoed on the other side of the door. Just perfect. Christian Zenith couldn't even be bothered to go send off his future wife himself. Rubbing at the dried tears on her face, she slowly made her way towards the door. Opening it she looked into the eyes of the elderly Marques, dressed in his usual professional attire. The servant in his monkey suit. At least Marques got paid quite well for his services. The last time Vivica receive a paycheck of any sort from Zenith Corporation was well before they entered Global Wrestling together. It's hard to take a job seriously that never pays. "Miss Valentine, your plane leaves shortly. We're already running extremely late." Marques stated stuttering a little between words. He could tell there was something off about The Black Mariah. All his years working for the estate he'd never seen her look like this. She was always the one who could put a smile on through everything. "Don't waste your time Marques. I'll go tell Christian myself." Taking a step around Marques she made her way back towards the study. The door to his study was left open just a bit, she could see the glow from the monitor and the shadow of The Conscious Crisis across a far wall. Marques must've asked Christian what the matter was, he wouldn't have let anyone else residing in the building into the room. Stopping in front of the door Vivica placed her hand onto the doorknob. A sense of reluctancy set in as she went to enter the room. After this nothing would ever be the same. Closing her eyes and taking a deep breath, The Black Mariah pushed the door open and entered the room. In front of her was Christian Zenith, still searching everything he could about Tera's presence in Seattle, Washington. Die Erntemashine had become something more than what he'd been accused of during the GTT5 Tournament, it had become an obsession. Just like everything else in his life, he did everything he could to keep him self from the startling revelation of his professional wrestling career. Losing GTT5 and the GW World Championship. He was a failure. "Let her go Christian," Vivica interrupted the silence in the room, "It's over. It's done. Let it rest." Dropping the mouse to his computer, Vivica could see the former leader of Hunting Grounds sink down a bit in his chair. Slowly the computer chair turned around, so Christian was facing the woman he called his future wife. Looking into his eyes, even The Black Mariah, usually jaded by love and fond memories could see it wasn't the same man. His spirit had been broken by successes that have passed him by. It wasn't the decline of Zenith Corporation that had left to his departure from GW back in January as he'd told the media. Vivica could see it in his eyes, even today. It was his acute fear of failure. "I do not think you quite understand the gravity of this situation Vivica." Christian paused, his cold and heartless cadet blue eyes peering back at her in the darkness, "Tera Zenith has surfaced in Seattle, Washington. Her being in Seattle, Washington is not newsworthy. Her surfacing there is, because she's taking money in exchange for injuring people. It's starting all over again. Right now, it's just a wrestling thing… some to quench her thirst so the urges die down. Next she'll be doing small jobs for crime bosses in the area. Once word of her services gets out she'll be running around with Zenith Corporation military grade weaponry just like…" "Just stop it." Vivica interrupted him stepping forward in the room, trying to see him a little bit better, "If she wants to get herself arrested so be it. Wrestling doesn't lead to organized crime. Not everyone immediately makes that jump like you do. There is something seriously wrong with you." "I'm not the one here with the problem," Christian responded, "I'm not the one running around playing little games in wrestling organizations trying to avoid doing her real job. I've heard the way people talk about me when I'm not around. They say I'm obsessed that I've lost it. No one understands. You would've a couple years ago. Not anymore. You've changed Vivica. You've submersed yourself in a false reality trying to escape the real pressures of life. Then you have the audacity to come in here and tell me there's something wrong with ME?" "Submersing myself in a false reality?!" Vivica responded, raising her voice a little bit. "Have you lost your god damned mind Christian? I'm a professional wrestler… the same as you were a year ago. Why can't you just be happy that I've accomplished something and I'm happy doing something for once in my life that doesn't involve me being your little goon." "Is that what the problem is? All you wanted was more responsibility? You sure have a funny way of sho…" Christian was interrupted once again before he finished. "No, I don't want more responsibility Christian. I want to live my own life for once. I left my parents house at 16 because I was sick and tired of being told I have to act a certain way, I have to look a certain way, I have to pursue a certain type of career just to justify my parents need for more. They didn't care what I wanted to be, they didn't give a damn about me. They just wanted to hang my successes on their walls and show it off to their friends." Vivica J. Valentine continued, "You're not any different… either was Jimmy. You take me under your wing and tell me you care about me. Then you send me to go to your fucking dirty work for you while you don't risk you neck. Telling me I have to prove myself time and time again. Trying to groom me into something I never wanted and never asked for. I just wanted companionship. I just wanted to be loved. I never wanted ANY of this shit." "We don't have time for this pity party." Christian said, "Get your bags and get to the airport. You're already late." "Dammit Christian!" Vivica screamed, standing up to The Midnight Messiah for the first time, "Get your head out of your ass and listen to me! I'm not going to Seattle. Get another stupid lackey this time. I'm going to go to the PrimeTime Central event. With or without your permission. I'm going to do what I want to do. If you really care about me you'll come to. There is a ticket laying on table downstairs. If you want to show up, fine. If don't show up, I'll be here first thing the next day to pack my bags and leave for Toronto to stay with Dawn and Lia." The Black Mariah turned around and stormed out of the room, slamming the door shut behind her. Getting out of his chair The Midnight Messiah walked over to the window and looked down at the driveway. A black rental car approached around the winding road. It was Vivica's ride. She was serious this time. She really was going to the PTC Event, and Lia was waiting. -------------------------------------------------------------------- Final Stanza: The Black Mariah Has Arrived -------------------------------------------------------------------- Walking through the hallways, Shadow and Vivica J. Valentine stood side by side in the backstage area. Choosers Of The Slain, it'd been a while since either of them had gone by that name. Together they were a team, making their impact on Global Wrestling by never losing a tag team match while they were together… but tag team championship gold always eluded them. Today is a new day, and a new opportunity. Yet they found themselves walking together as a team. It just seemed right for both of them, whether it be Choosers, Hunting Grounds, or Dies Irae. The Black Mariah and The Black Magdalen always has a special connection lost upon most, but they got it. That's all that really mattered to them. Although their dreams of Tag Team Gold had always been just beyond their grasp, their opportunity tonight was something special. Although they couldn't leave PTC Tag Team Champions… they could walk out PTC Extreme and Global Champions respectively. It was something that Vivica had never really realized until now. They would be the first tag team to hold both PTC singles titles at the same time. While this would just be another feather in the hat to most people, it was so much more to both of them. For years they've both been trapped under the glass ceiling, unable to break out into their own. This was their one chance, this was possibly their last opportunity and neither of them were going to let it slip away. "So this is the big time, huh?" Vivica said looking over at Lia. Shadow looked back at her with a bit of a smile. She'd been here so many times that it was really nothing special to her anymore. The feeling of walking through the doors for the first time had been lost on her years ago, but she remembered her first time. "I guess you could call it that…" Shadow responded, "For me it's just another opportunity. Every one is different and unexpected. I plan on cashing in tonight." "It's destiny." Vivica smiled looking back at her Dies Irae stable leader, "It's just a shame Christian won't be here to see it. You and me, in the middle of that ring… champions. You having the biggest one of them all and me, kicking the hell out of Archibald McGregor. I think for the first time in my career, I'm actually going to put a smile on people's faces." The Black Mariah actually liked the thought of that. It was going to be nice to get away from the constant booing and hissing of the Global Championship Wrestling crowd. She could see it now, standing up on the top rope holding the Extreme Title above her head and the crowd cheering her on. It sent chills up and down her spine just thinking about it. Putting her name up there next to the people who've come before her. Shadow of course stuck out in her mind immediately. Those were footsteps she didn't mind ftlinefollowing in. Looking around she saw the banners hanging from the ceiling of all the former PTC Champions. Dies Irae member Seymour Almost, Rich Rollins, Hessian, Jesse Jamester, The Uber Judge, Emilio Rage, Karina Wolfenden… "Like a phoenix." Vivica said aloud looking up at the Karina banner hanging on the wall next to her with a smile. It was time to rise from the ashes. It was time to break the glass ceiling. It was time to stand up and accept her destiny. Maybe The Black Mariah wasn't so alone After all. She had the dreams and expectations of millions cheering her on.
