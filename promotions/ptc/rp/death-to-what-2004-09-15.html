Death to What?!

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 The ominous grey sky, what exactly do you think of when you look at it? The rain that falls from above, what thoughts run through your head as the drops of water hit you on their way down? The slight chill of it all on a late summer's afternoon, does it make you wish for something better, or do you enjoy the depressing feeling it all brings to you like me? To me, a gloomy day like this puts me in a good mood, but for most, it leaves them wishing for their warm sunny day by the time it's all said and done. It wasn't going to happen today. There I stood, sadistic smile on my face, in the middle of the road letting the rain fall down on me. The cold drops of water sent a chill up and down my spine, and it was quite pleasurable. It rejuvenated the ice cold heart, and at the same time, brought a slight sparkle to my eye. My gaze was directed towards the church, however, and as a car honked at me to move, I did. Walking towards the church the smile remained plastered on my face, and the sparkle in my eye remained. I headed up the steps to the front entrance, and began rubbing my hands together, like I had something planned. Maybe I did, but none the less, I started talking to myself, as I usually do, like there is someone around who is actually going to see me or listen to me. Hell, for all I knew, there could be a camera on my every move, had to make the best of my time. 'So what the hell are you doing here Mr. Lee?' I asked myself as I swung open the double wooden doors to the church. 'Well if I have to come and pay my respects for the passing.' I said this as I walked into the main area. A look of shock popped up as there were tons of people sitting down, a few were still walking by the casket giving their last respects. In the heat of it all, I too decided to follow suit, and walked to the front. 'As it seems in a turn of events, lots of people have showed up to pay their respects to the death here today. Amazing! What the fuck do they think they are doing?!' I couldn't put my finger on it, or at least not yet anyway. I finally made it to the front, and as the last person in line, I began to kneel down in front of the casket, but just began shaking my head. I quickly stood up straight again, and just started spitting into the casket, before even taking a good look. The room went deathly silent, and upon my second inspection, there was actually someone inside. I just smiled, turned around, and glared at all of the people. 'What the hell is he doing in there?' I asked the crowd. They all began questioning each other before one brave soul stood up, like he was taking it upon himself to stick up for this guy. I pointed his way before he spoke. 'He's dead, this is a funeral. Did you expect something else there buddy? What the hell do you think you are trying to pull?' The man asked, and I just smiled. Out of the corner of my eye, though, I saw a couple of largely built men walking my way. I looked over at them, and they paused. I looked back to the crowd. 'I was expecting a funeral, but Jesus, who brought the dead guy in? That's not supposed to be in there!' I finished up my sudden outburst, and walked behind the casket, grabbed the bottom of it with both of my hands, and then flipped it over. The body flopped onto the ground, and the sound that filled that church I don't think I could ever forget. Before too many people tried to come and get me, I searched all through the inside of the casket to look for what was really supposed to be in there. When I didn't find it, I began to get upset. As I looked back out into the crowd I could see quite a few angry faces along with a few female forms lying passed out on the floor. I busted out. 'Alright you worthless fools, where's it at? What are you all really doing here? And why the fuck would you pull something like this? I was supposed to be here at the Second Coming of the Methodist Christ Church for a funeral today, and I never expected this many people, let alone a dead body! Now explain yourselves!' I looked about, waiting for someone to say something before I got jumped. After a few moments someone in the back finally spoke up, and with some sense. 'This is the First Coming of the Methodist Christ Church. The Second Coming is across the street.' I was stunned. A sarcastic smile filled my face, and I was surprised the guy had said it as calmly as he did. While the wave of stunned people continued to fill the room, I left without saying a word, and with haste. I wasn't going to give any of them time to think about it and come after me. So there I was running back through the rain to cross the street. A huge mistake on my part, but then again, I didn't really give a shit. I made my way up the steps to the REAL Second Coming of the Methodist Christ Church, opened the double wooden doors, and made my way into the main area, and like it should have been, was empty, with the exceptions of myself, and the casket up front. The same smile I had on my face a little while ago was back, and I made my way up front. This time I didn't even bother to pretend to kneel down, I just spit on the closed coffin. 'You piece of trash. I don't know why anyone would waste the time and money on you. This whole thing is about you pretty much, but I seem to be the only one who doesn't care about it! I'm doing it for myself, not you. And that's everyone' downfall.' I narrowed my eyes as I reached forward to open up the casket, a noise coming from inside the church startles me for just a minute. I turned my head to see if someone had come in, and I saw nothing, so I turned my attention back to the casket, and started to open it. I had the thing almost open, and then a noise startled me again. This time, however, it was a women pounding on a pane of glass on the second floor of the church. She started screaming at the top of her lungs, and was it annoying as hey! 'Anthony! Anthony! Anthony!' She yelled it quite a few times, and I just couldn't take it anymore. I opened up the casket, and looked up at her. She stopped hitting the glass, and just kind of looked at me with a funny face. I shook my head. We looked at each other for a little while longer; I began to wonder how long it would take her to move. I finally pointed in the direction of the other church. 'I'd be willing to bet you've got the wrong church. Your funeral is across the street. Enjoy!' She just nodded, wiped the tears off of her cheeks, and proceeded to head out of the church. 'Dumb brood.' I said to myself as I finally got to look inside the casket I was expecting to look in earlier. Sure enough, what I was expecting to see was lying there. A shoddy replica of the PTC Global title belt lay in the casket, and I just shook my head as I look at it. I pick it up with my thumb, pointer finger and middle finger, as if disgusted with it. I hold it out to my side and examine it. 'Why are you so wanted? What is it about you that made 180 other people sign up for the chance to wrestle for you? Hell, I'd never do anything for you, I'm doing this for myself, but damn. AS silly piece of gold attached to some worthless government leather? A porn star and a box of condoms would be a better trophy for being PTC global champion.' I shook my head as I tossed the belt over my shoulder. I began to pace around. 'I suppose you've got a good history though, but damn. All these people for something like this? Seems a bit out there to me, but I guess someone he doesn't give a rats ass wouldn't understand. So I'll just think about what I know.' I paused my walking, and took a seat on the organ bench. 'What is it exactly that you do know Mr. Lee?' I was asked, by... myself. 'Well, I do know that the death of many hopes and dreams is where my path in GTT4 is going.' I said in response... to my question. I then realized that I was holding the unworthy to even be called a replica belt over my shoulder like I had pride holding it, and quickly threw it onto the ground. I then continued to question... myself. 'Why are hopes and dreams going to die because of you? And will anything of yours die if you lose in GTT4?' I shook my head at the dumb question, but then realized who had asked it, and decided to just answer... myself. 'Hopes and dream will die because most of these slack jawed limp wrist good for nothing's goal is to get a PTC title shot out of this. But whenever one of them has to face me, the grief that will follow with them after our match up shall be great to watch them go through. They will have lost everything they signed up for. And do you honestly think that I'll care if I lose? I will only lose if I want to, and that will happen when I know there's nobody left who did this for themselves, and not for some piece of gold plated aluminum!' I stood back up, looking at the replica belt as I continued to pace back and fourth. More questions began to pop up, and decided to ask away. 'Well, then what about Sergeant Major Adam Tangle?' The question just happened to role off the tongue, like I gave a damn, but what the hell, eh? 'Two words. Temper, tantrum. I see this guy crying like a baby when he loses. His goal will have been shattered, and he'll go home and sink into a deep depression afterwards. If I cared too much about it, I would have said something myself. And pretty much all these questions are asking the same thing. Let this sink in, and you'll be fine. It's all about me, not the rest of these bastards, nor the dumb ass title shot. Hell, I'm in this as a free agent, not like I can get the shot when I win anyway. So think about that. But now that I've come and paid my disrespect, I think it's time to go. No more questions.' I then began to walk off, but quickly looked around, ran over and grabbed the replica belt off the ground, and started to walk out of the church. As I opened the doors to exit, I heard a voice. 'Who were you talking to?' It startled me, and I turned around to see the woman from earlier standing behind me. I looked around, at the belt over my right shoulder, and then back at her. 'Didn't you see all of those people in there?' She peaked through the door, and then looked back at me with a strange look on her face. I shook my head. 'I'm not crazy!' I yelled at her, and then ran out of the church. The rain had stopped, though, and my good mood was about to run out. The scene fades to black.
