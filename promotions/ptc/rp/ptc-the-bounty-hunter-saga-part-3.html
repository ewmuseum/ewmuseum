The Bounty Hunter Saga (Part 3)

3 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dallas Days Inn Hotel: October 14, 2003, 8:17 pm When he arrived back at the hotel, Myasato immediately went to work. He began to fix himself up. He took to his suit case and removed several Tylenol Threes. He ground them up and placed them in a cup of cold water, before drinking the concoction. The pain in his arm immediately began to fade. He then opened his toiletry case. He reached in and began to sort through the wide variety of things that were in there. Finally, he pulled out a small razor and a pair of medical sized tweezers. He took the razor and heated it up slightly using a lighter that he had pulled from his pocket. He then removed the make-shift bandage that was covering his bullet wound. He then took the razor and made small incisions to open up the skin around the area where the bullet had entered his arm. Next, he took the lighter and began to slightly heat the tips of the tweezers. He then reached inside the wound with the tweezers and began to feel around for the bullet. He followed the bullet’s path directly with the tweezers. He grimaced in pain as he did so, and in pain he clenched his teeth together. He was breathing heavily as he inserted the tweezers further. The bullet hole began to pour out small spurts of blood. He panted in pain, but continued pressing on to find the bullet that was currently wedged inside of his arm. He followed the bullet’s path to his muscle tissue. He pushed the tissue aside and clenched his fist as the pain grew increasingly. He then began to feel around, and felt the familiar feeling of a small piece of metal inside of his arm. He gripped the bullet tightly with the tweezers, making sure he wouldn’t be able to drop it. Then, slowly and surely he began to remove the bullet from his arm. When the torment was over, he dropped the bullet into a small vile of cleaning solution. He would need this tomorrow, he liked to play mind games with his victims before he killed them. Then once the bullet was in the solution he stood, and walked to the bathroom. He swabbed the cut clean, and disinfected the wound. He then bandaged it up, using medical gauze and tape. He breathed a heavy sigh of relief now that the pain was over. He cleaned up all of the small mess that he had made, and then went back to his real objective. He began to plan out a meeting place, he would battle the Souls of the Black Lotus. He would do it with honor, and he would be victorious, he just needed to figure out how. The Nakazuras were going to pay for the pain they had caused him. Jushin Nakazura’s Apartment: October 14, 2003, 9:35 pm “What the fuck was that all about, and who the fuck was that guy?!” demanded Jushin. “I don’t know, but I suggest we try to find out. I suggest we get in contact with our father in Tokyo and see what news he can give us,” answered Ichiro. ”But it will only be early morning in Tokyo,” answered Jushin. “That does not matter, I’m sure our father will be able to understand,” said Ichiro. ”I hope you’re right. And I hope he has some information for us,” said Jushin. With that he walked to the small counter top shelf in his dining area. He picked up the cordless phone that was sitting on the counter and he began to dial the long distance number. He pulled the phone to his ear, and waited as he heard the final few tones ringing through. “Wait!” yelled Ichiro. “What? What do you want?” Jushin asked. “The phone is probably tapped, he knows too much about us already, we can’t take any chances.” answered Ichiro. “Good point,” said Jushin. He looked at Ichiro, unsure of what to do next. That was when his older brother reached into his workout shorts and pulled out a small cellular phone. He opened the flip part of the phone, and began to punch in a number. He tossed it over to his brother, who pressed send, and awaited the answer of the phone. Finally, a voice on the other end of the phone sleepily answered. ”Hello?” “Father?” asked Jushin. “Jushin? Is that you my son? What troubles you enough to call me this early in the morning?” “Sorry father, but it is still night here in Dallas,” answered Jushin. “Awwww yes, how is the world of professional wrestling treating you?” “Very well father. However, there is something that troubles Ichiro and I,” Jushin answered with a slight hint of concern in his voice. ”Yes my son, what is it?” “There is someone here, a bounty hunter of some sort. He is Japanese, but we have no idea who he works for. We only saw him for the first time today, but we assume he has been here a couple days. He tried to attack us today when we were training, we wondered if you had any information that you can share with us,” said Jushin. “Oh my, so the rumors are true.” “What rumors,” asked Jushin. A noticeable sign of confusion and concern took over his face. Ichiro looked concern as he listened and gathered what he could from his little brother’s reactions, and the one half of the conversation that he could hear. “The rumor was that Ishuzu has tried to attack me, by attacking my family. She must have assumed he would kill you, and I would never catch wind of this. But she is horribly mistaken; now I must strike back. However, as for you two, my sons, be careful. Watch your back and trust no one, this assassin has skill, and I am sure you together can defeat him, but separately I am not so sure. Be careful my sons, together we will crush Ishuzu, on separate continents. Call me back, when you have defeated your assassin.” “Yes father, farewell,” said Jushin slowly. “Farewell, my son. Take care, may the Lotus be with you.” With his fathers final words still hanging in the air, Jushin hung up the phone. He tossed the cell back to his brother, and Ichiro shut it and quickly put it back into his pocket. “Well?” asked Ichiro. “He’s an assassin, he has been sent to kill us by Ishuzu!” said Jushin. “Shit! Is he skilled?” inquired Ichiro. “Very assures father. But we must defeat him, as our father is now engaging in a war with Ishuzu on our home front,” answered Jushin. “Alright well let’s go grab some food, and then we can talk about what we are going to do,” said Ichiro. ”Sure, my treat,” answered Jushin. He reached into his shorts searching for his wallet. He felt around, and a look of shock came over his face. “My wallet isn’t here. It was in my suit!” “What else was in your wallet besides money?” asked Ichiro, a growing look of concern on his face. ”Everything! Phone numbers, credit cards, business cards. I think even addresses!” answered Jushin. ”Okay, well get changed I’ll be back in five minutes, we’re going to go out for some dinner, and we need to figure out what the fuck we are going to do, because this guy knows everything about us now. Where we live, where we hang out, who we talk to, who we don’t, EVERYTHING!” said Ichiro. ”You got it five minutes,” answered Jushin. Charlie Chan’s Chinese Dynasty: October 14, 2003 10:45 pm Ichiro and Jushin sat at the table. They were engaged in deep conversation, both of them were looking around nervously, they needed to make sure the assassin wasn’t around. They were concerned and rightfully so, he knew everything. “So, what the fuck are we going to do?” asked Jushin right before taking a small sip of water. ”I suggest we do what our father told us to do,” said Ichiro. ”And what’s that?” snarled Jushin. “We take him out, we beat him at his own game. With him dead he can’t kill us!” answered Ichiro. “I like the sounds of that,” said Jushin. He smiled and took another sip of his water. “How??” Ichiro smiled, and went into great detail about a plan that he had been thinking of. The two brothers continued to talk into the night, until finally their food arrived. They finally had a half decent plan, they would no longer be the hunted, and they would be the hunters. When their food arrived they dug in quickly. They stuffed their faces, they had not eaten in many hours, and this food was a great relief. Jushin Nakazura’s Apartment: October 15, 2003 1:07 am Jushin went to unlock his door, but when he turned the handle the door swung open. He looked shocked, and his brother noticed the expression and walked over away from his own door, and towards his little brother’s. He looked at Jushin inquisitively. “What’s wrong?” he asked. “My door is unlocked, and I’m positive I locked it. It looks like the lock was cut!” said Jushin. Ichiro reached behind his back and pulled out his custom made weapons. He held the two pistols in front of himself as his brother withdrew his own custom made pair of pistols. The two brothers kicked the door open, and rushed in. With guns drawn they checked every inch of the apartment. The apartment was a mess, and in ruins. Whoever had come in was looking hard for something, or trying to send a message. Whatever it was, the Souls of the Black Lotus didn’t like it one bit. They searched the apartment thoroughly but only found one thing. A small note was left on Jushin’s pillow, his bedroom being the only room that was untouched. Jushin was swearing up a storm, obviously frustrated at the thrashing his apartment had received. He looked around searching for signs of answers as Ichiro walked towards the small note on Jushin’s pillow. He picked it up and began to read it out loud. “Hello Souls of the Black Lotus,” he started. “I came to find you to come out and play, but you weren’t home. I thought you were playing hide and seek, so I looked everywhere, but I still couldn’t find you. You guys are good at hiding. Well, I still want to play, and I hope this time you want to play too, because next time, I will do a lot worse. Meet me at the roof top of the Dallas Days Inn Hotel. Be there as the clock is striking midnight, as the fifteenth turns to the sixteenth. Don’t be late! Signed, your friend Myasato.” Jushin continued swearing. “Fucking prick! Enough with the fucking mind games, when we get to the roof, I’m going to make sure he dies a slow and painful death!” he screamed. Ichiro looked down onto the bed, and noticed something that he had not before. He noticed the small thing which had weighted the piece of paper down so it had not blown away. He picked it up, and examined it in the light. He instantly recognized it, it was a bullet; a bullet that had come from his gun. Ichiro could almost hear the assassin’s words teasing him, screaming into his ear. “You got me, but not good enough!” “Now what the fuck are we going to do? This fucks are plan right out the window!” yelled Jushin. “Calm down bro,” said Ichiro. “This may be even better, he must be confident if he thinks he can take us both out in a two-on-one fight. Maybe we can surprise him, and take him out before he does anymore damage. But for right now, let’s get this place picked up, and then we both need some serious rest.” With that Jushin nodded his head, and both he and his brother went about their duties. They tidied up the room enough that it was suitable. Jushin thanked his brother, and then headed off to bed. Ichiro left the room, and locked the door. However, he couldn’t go into his own room yet, he needed to relax a second first. He went to the stairs, and slowly climbed his way to the roof. This always seemed to relax him, he walked onto the roof, and climbed onto the top ledge. He looked down onto the sleeping city. Many of the lights were out, and the lonely shadows walked the streets, the only light illuminating came from the streetlights. Ichiro looked down, and in a way saw his home in Tokyo. He missed it there, he missed everything, he could care less about staying in the United States, but it was too late for that he had a job to do. He took a final glance of the silent, sleeping city. He smiled and looked down, he knew somewhere out there was someone waiting for him. Wanting to kill him. It was a strange power, the ability to take another’s life without thinking twice, and an expensive one to buy. He had never had a problem killing someone, but now that he knew there was someone out there with those same ideas, he felt second thoughts. He turned from the sleeping city, but something told him, that the assassin wasn’t at rest either right now. He couldn’t have been more right. Underground Parking Garage: October 15, 2003 11:47 pm Jushin and Ichiro walked towards the small black sports car. They were dressed in matching black suits, and black sunglasses. They seemed almost emotionless as they walked towards the car. They were almost a scene out of Reservoir Dogs, off to risk their lives for their own survival. They reached the car, and Jushin opened the doors. The two brothers hopped into the car. The whole ride they did not speak to each other, there was nothing that could be said right now to ease the tension. They stared forward thinking, going over the plan in detail. Spread out, get two angles to attack him from, and then strike when they could. They would need to draw the assassin out, they would need to get to him before he got to them. Ichiro sat silently in the passenger side of the car. He checked himself to make sure he had everything prepared properly. He felt his lateral muscles at his sides where his guns were currently resting; safety on and idle. He checked his ammo belt which ran through the belt holes in his suit pants. He felt around, he had about ten clips sat in reserve, he did not want to run out of bullets in this gun fight. Jushin drove silently, he replayed their plan and envisioned how this duel would work out. He kept concentrated on the road, as they were nearly there. He felt his guns and his ammo clips digging into his sides as he drove, but he ignored them. They would be out soon enough. Dallas Days Inn Rooftop: October 15, 2003 11:55 pm ‘They should be here any time now,’ thought Myasato. ‘I just have to prepare.’ He busied himself checking his pistols, making sure he had enough ammo, and spare guns stashed on the back part of the roof. He needed to make sure if for any reason he lost one of his two pistols he would have a spare. He heard the roar of a car’s engine, and then the engine fell silent. That was them, he couldn’t know for sure. But his gut told him it was. He went into hiding, as he awaited the Souls of the Black Lotus’ arrival. Meanwhile The Souls of the Black Lotus, stepped out of their car and they entered the doors leading into the hotel lobby. They walked in sync as they approached the elevator. They took the elevator all the way up to the top floor, from here they had to walk the stairs to the roof. However, they sent the elevator to the roof, as a decoy. They ran up the stairs, and waited silently behind the door. They pulled out their pistols and prepared to do battle. “Ready?” asked Ichiro. ”What if I’m not?” asked Jushin. “Get ready, cause here we go!” said Ichiro as the two brothers heard the ding of the elevator reaching the roof. There was the distinct sound of pistol shots ringing out through the midnight air, they looked up and saw the elevator getting littered with rounds from the assassin. They heard the guns click indicating they were out of ammo, and they ran in through the door. Jushin immediately headed to the far side of the roof. ”FUCK!” screamed Myasato as he realized the elevator was just a decoy. He reloaded quickly, and began to get ready. This battle had already gone too far. ”Jushin?” yelled Ichiro, to his brother across the roof. “What?” came the reply. ”Where is he?” he asked. “Right here,” yelled the assassin’s voice as he popped out and began to shoot at Ichiro; who was ducked behind a few small dumpsters. Jushin peaked his head out, and let off a few blind shots from his pistol. None of them connected but it was enough to scare the assassin. Ichiro stood, and ran quickly away from the dumpsters, hoping that Myasato would not have noticed. He saw the bullet holes, and made a guess as to the approximate whereabouts of the assassin. He ran there, and when he arrived there saw only his brother. The two Nakazuras nearly shot each other, and then stood up questioning where the hell Myasato was. Suddenly a shot rang out, and both Jushin and Ichiro hit the ground. They looked at each other and nodded. Ichiro and Jushin crawled separate ways in an attempt to surround the killer. They saw a small shadow move once they were in position and both brothers fired off several rounds, unsuccessfully. However they yelled to each other, and as was the instruction they both stood up, and attempted to look for Myasato. However, Myasato saw this happen, and also stood. He spread his arms apart, pointing one pistol in the direction of individual members of the Souls of the Black Lotus. He pulled the triggers simultaneously but only heard a clicking. He was out of ammo. The two Nakazuras smiled at him, and then pulled their own triggers only to receive a similar click. All three men sank down into shelter. Jushin and Ichiro dropped their clips and reloaded quickly. Myasato was also reloading when he heard the sounds of clips hitting the ground, he was being slow and sloppy. Myasato quickly reloaded and ran towards where he heard Jushin sitting. He heard the heavy breathing and ran towards the crates, he received fire from Jushin’s brother behind him, but dove over the crates, and engaged in a close battle with Jushin. Both men were lying, and quickly grabbed each others arms. Each man tried to wrestle the other man’s gun away from his own body. Jushin pushed hard, but Myasato struggled quickly, his only hope was to kill Jushin before Ichiro could come over. Jushin finally realized that his brother would not come to his rescue unless he was called. “Ichiro, help me I’ve got him!” Ichiro heard the voice, but then heard several gun shots ring off in the night air. He didn’t know if it was a trap or not but it sounded like his brother was in some trouble, and he had to help if he could. He ran across to behind the stack of crates that he knew his brother was hiding behind. He hopped onto the crate, and saw both his brother and the assassin on their stomachs trying to wrestle each others aim away from themselves. Ichiro hopped onto the crate, and took aim. He fired a shot into Myasato’s leg. The shot connected right into the back of his knee. Myasato screamed in pain, and dropped his gun and reached back to his leg. He let go of Jushin and knew he was defeated. Jushin smiled and stood up, he dusted the dirt off of his suit. Then he walked to the fallen assassin. The bullet wound in the back of his leg disabled him from walking. Jushin stamped the bullet wound, causing Myasato to scream out in pain. Ichiro and Jushin dragged the assassin to the middle of the parking roof. ”This is what happens when you mess with the Nakazura family!” yelled Jushin. ”And don’t think this little stunt went unnoticed we know Ishuzu sent you, and right now our father is taking car of that little problem!” said Ichiro. “NO!!” screamed Myasato. “Yes,” said the brothers as they smiled at the pain they had caused. There was only one final thing left to do. They sturdied him onto his knees, and he sat whispering a prayer silently. The two brothers smiled and took their place behind him. They placed the tips of their pistols diagonally on the back of his head. Before shooting they said a quick prayer to thank the Lotus. “I know you're listening, Come to me in my time of need, when times are tough and those around me bleed. Let not my enemies conquer me, give me strength so I can see. Guide me in this final flight, from all the darkness into light. Through desperate days, through all the haze. Carry me to my success, to my triumph” they said in unison. With that they pulled their triggers simultaneously and two bullets penetrated all the way through their attackers head. The blood spattered all over the pavement on the roof, the two brothers stepped away. They smiled at a job well done, and were happy to be alive. No one messed with them. “So what do you want to do know?” asked Jushin. “Go home and get some rest, we’ve got a match at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup to train for tomorrow!” said Ichiro. Jushin frowned and shook his head. He hung his head low, and laughed slightly. Ichiro began to laugh as well, and patted his brother on the back. The two walked side by side, back down the long flight of stairs. They walked all the way to the bottom and then exited the hotel. They hopped into the small black sports car, which was still sitting in front of the hotel. The Souls of the Black Lotus hopped in, and Jushin roared the engine to life. He smiled and then turned the ignition. He pressed the clutch and shifted the car into gear, then he slammed the gas pedal hard, and the car sped down the road. They headed down the streets southwards back towards their apartments. -End-
