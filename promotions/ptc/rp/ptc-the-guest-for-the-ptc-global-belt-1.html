The Guest for the PTC Global Belt - #1

2 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Guest for the PTC Global Belt - Part 1 - Madison Square Garden Jason Jameson (Free Agent) “I’ll do something productive to get into this infinite gauntlet thing…” thought Jason Jameson as he had embarked on his journey to get back in the good graces of the PTC management. A failed application into the Ideal 4 of PRIME and years in the wilderness has left Jason Jameson well outside the radar of those that picked the qualifiers for an IG shot. Settling himself, he knew what he had to do. Around him, he had maps of America, Canada, USA, Japan, Mexico and less detailed maps of the rest of the world. Anywhere that a PTC fed could have gone, Jason knew its landscape and area. He had compasses to work out distances. Charts detailing the tour stops of every PTC fed, past and present. This was a mission he was ready to complete. But first he had to go to the place that his goal was last seen. By the way, the goal of Jason Jameson… The PTC Global Title, last seen around the waist of Karina Wolfenden in a PRIME ring in Madison Square Garden. And so, the first stop on the tour of former NTICW superstar, obviously enough was Madison Square Garden, New York City, New York. The place of the last public sighting of the PTC Global Title. On the night back on the 25th May 2005, Karina Wolfenden was crowned the Champion after defeating an under par Emilio Rage who was crowned Champion at the Seven of the Best competition, one of the few PTC events to run smoothly. Back then K-Wolf refused to accept the title, stating that she had won the Championship unfairly against a man who was being forced to defend after only weeks ago, being forced to the brink of the death. Most people would agree with K-Wolf upon her decision, but Jason. As he began to do his research on the PTC Global Title, he began to see the immortality that holding the belt went with it. Okay, so no-one was able to defend more than twice, but that just showed the cut-throat style of such a great interfed as Primetime Central. But the money and sponsorship that it brought, would have been just the thing Karina needed. I mean people only remember Hessian for winning the Global Title, sure he may have ended Vampir’s reign of terror twice, but even someone like von Doom character #159 could manage that. Jameson just was dumbfounded that K-Wolf walked off at the end of ReV without the title she had won. It seemed stupid to him to give that up, just because Emilio Rage couldn’t manage what Hessian did. Still as he Googled everything to do with PRIME, asked Jeeves almost every question that could be asked about Emilio Rage and went on Yahoo! to study Primetime Central’s history, Jason was drawn more and more into finding his Championship. However sitting at home in Toronto, he knew that no matter how websites claimed to have the rumoured K-Wolf/Deville video, “The Star” knew that he needed to go Madison Square Garden and look for that belt. The profits from his shoe store was easily removed from the company accounts, the kid that helped Jason runt he store was told to come in early and Jameson was the next plane to New York City in search of probably the most valuable title belt in wrestling. Walking up to Madison Square Garden, Jason was in luck. Tonight the New York Liberty entertain the Connecticut Sun in what some will describe as an enthralling encounter for top class basketball, but even Jason, who is a soccer kind of guy, knows it’s women basketball so it will suck. Paying his ten bucks for one of the shitty seats behind the pillars, Jason got his access to possibly one of the most awe inspiring venues in the world. Why they let women play basketball in here was beyond the out-of-work wrestler, but it guaranteed a small crowd and therefore more room to look around. Stepping into the hallway, the match was to begin within the hour and so Jason set about his plan of looking for clues. He needed something to give an avenue for investigation. An old poster highlighting the subsequent tour dates for the PRIME sports-entertainment juggernaut or maybe an old PRIME t-shirt, and then it hit him. If anything had been left behind, it could only be in a one place… Walking up to the window, Jason felt a little stupid but he knew it was one of his few options. He pulled his jacket onto his shoulders and patted down his t-shirt for dust. He felt like he was about to attend a job interview however he needed to seem convincing. Stepping forward in his dusty jeans, blue t-shirt and black jacket, Jason pressed the bell on the counter and the “Lost and Found” girl approached with a smile. He was a teenager so “Lost and Found” girl may have described her better and when she spoke it was obvious to the Canadian visitor that she was a native of New York. “How can I help you?” she asked, her accent shining through, as she wore dusty jeans and a black “staff” t-shirt. Jason drummed the counter with his fingers, feeling a little stupid about the question he was about to ask. “Do you have any wrestling title belts that have been lost?” asked Jameson, his cheeks quickly going a red glow as he felt so pathetic to ask but it really was his only chance of finding it. “I think I may have one…” replied the girl as she thought. “I’ll just make sure we still have it.” She spun on her heel and disappeared around the corner that leads to the “Lost and Found” stockroom. Jason imagined it as a wonderland of hats, single gloves, scarves and coats, along with a generous helping of bags and umbrellas, but right now he mind was racing. That seemed too simple he thought, waiting for Susan, on her name badge, to return carrying the PTC Global Title. He knew that the PTC management would love him if he returned with the Global Title. He would definitely be given as hot in GTT5 and a go at Emoticon. Jason was already picturing himself getting picked up by a PTC fed after an impressive showing the Infinite Gauntlet. The victory song was playing in his head. Record derailed… Returning around the corner, the girl was carrying a title belt as she slapped it up on the counter but to Jason’s dismay it wasn’t the PTC Global Title. Etched in the gold on the front were the letters, “J…E…W.” The JEW Championship Belt. He knew it from days of PCW shows and the ways in which they tried to degrade it, not that they needed to help the degradation process. They were no queries on Jason’s part that there was no way it was the PTC gold he was searching. “I didn’t think it was that important,” added the girl as she laid the belt out for Jason to study. He had recognised it immediately after watching PCW for many years and knew it was worthless. “It’s worthless,” Jason declared to the young girl, as he flicked the strap of the belt back at the assistant. Jason stuffed his hands back in his pockets, and began to walk away. “Should I throw it in the bin?” the girl called at Jason as he walked off down the corridor. “The bin would be too good for it,” Jameson added, turning the corner of the white corridor. The young girl looked down at the belt and then in one swoop swung from the counter ands back into the store, to lie and rot forever. As he left the “Lost and Found” in his wake, Jameson had his pocket stuffed in his pockets. “What next?” he thought, as he strolled towards his seating area. The game was growing closer and so was the time he had left remaining in the building. He needed to get to the locker rooms that the PRIME superstars would have used, just so he could look around, but which end had they closed off. It was a well known fact that wrestling promotions closed off one end of an arena so that they can build their elaborate entrances, but Jason just wasn’t sure which end they had used for ReVolution 64. “I’ll just have to check every room,” he thought. And then he decided against it. ReVolution 64 had occurred back in May. It was now the 2nd August. If every room in Madison Square Garden hadn’t been checked since then, he really didn’t want to be in the building. Checking every room was a lost cause and “Lost and Found” had proven fruitless. He needed a new plan. … The PRIME tour. There and then Jason constructed a new plan to find the PTC belt. He would go to every arena that PRIME had previously been in and try to find the belt. He would then, check any other promotions that might have used that arena after PRIME. Jason knew a few construction workers. Jason knew that most construction workers will pack up everything they find just so that they don’t get ripped into for forgetting something. The PTC Global Belt had obviously been packed up by a worker that didn’t know any better, and was now lying in a random room in some arena. To find the belt would take a lot of looking, but Jason didn’t mind. GTT5 won’t occur until at least the autumn. Plenty of time to get in the good graces of the PTC bigwigs. For now, he would try to enjoy some women's basketball that had cost him tend dollars. He knew it wouldn't happen, but he prayed for at least a good hotdog.
