A Dangerous Woman

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 “Unnnngh…!” A sound of pain escaped Luna’s lips as she sat split legged between the two saw horses sat upon the floor, only her leg muscles keeping her upper body from falling straight to the floor. Sylus rested his elbows on her shoulders, pressing his weight down upon her, stretching her. She furrowed her brow severely and closed her eyes, trying to keep any other sounds of hurting from escaping her masked lips. She dug her fingernails into her hands, drawing blood. Sylus: “Hurts, huh? I know it does, and I don’t care. This is necessary for you to survive. You have to be limber, quick, and agile at all times. And the more your body becomes use to, the better you will be.” She nodded faintly in response, trying her best to make her mind go elsewhere, and off the thoughts of pain. She drifted off, eyes slits, somewhere deep in her thoughts, fingers of memory trying to grasp this and that as it floated through. She compared her mind to a puzzle, with a lot of pieces missing, so much so you can’t even tell what picture it’s trying to form. But she had a few, small pieces, enough to give her hope for a better understanding in the future. And then she remembered her purpose, and her opponent, Jimmy Bong. She clenched her jaw, ignoring the rivulets of crimson that streaked down her hands to dribble on the floor below. No time for memory recall, no time for trying to piece together the shattered fragments of her past. She had a fight to finish, and to win. Sylus had told her he wanted it more brutal and violent than the last, more dominating. She was still feeling the effects of Rollins punishment upon her skin and bones, but those things would have to be ignored. Bong was a new threat, a new threat that had to be beaten into a bloody, senseless mess in order for her to move on. Sylus: “Five more minutes of this, then move on to the running. Thirty laps, Luna, no less. If you falter or fall, I will beat the will back into you, and you will start over from one, hmmm?” She nodded again, setting firm intentions back on reality. It was pain either way, but pain that pleased Sylus was better than pain that forced his hand. But he knew this as well as she did; she would run those laps without fail, and would continue to torture and test her body until Sylus said otherwise. This was her life now. She had to fight, to win, to be brutal and violent in order to get –peace- back into her life. Such a strange way to go about doing things, but there was no sense in arguing, it was either do it, or die. Literally. Luna (tone strained but controlled): “Thirty laps…I will not fall or falter, Sylus…I will give you thirty laps…and thirty more if you ask of them.” Sylus: “Good girl, very good girl.” He smiled and eased his weight off of her, and it seemed she floated for a moment, as her entire body went numb with the release of pressure. She fought and kept her balance, remaining poised atop the sawhorses. Twenty seconds left and this particular torture would be over, then on to the laps, and on to whatever else Sylus had planned for the day. But she did everything she was told…because the nights belonged to her finally. She always kept her reward in mind…for beating Rollins; she was free to spend time alone in the sweet, chilling air of the outside world, even if it was limited to the rooftop of this worn down warehouse. Sylus: “Time…up, and get those newly stretched legs moving, Luna! Only a few days left before you meet Bong, and I want his ass to –remember- you, got it? Now up! And get moving!” Like a rabbit with its tail on fire, Luna moved quickly off the sawhorses, and as soon as her feet touched the floor, she was off into a full run. If there was one thing good about the next thirty laps, it would give her time to think on just –how- she would make Bong feel her pain. Broken bones? Ruptured skin? Ripping fragments right out of his mind? The thoughts alone were enough to make her smile somewhat behind the mask. But either way she went about it, one thing was certain: Bong would never, -ever-, want to be in the same room with her again. And if possible, probably not the same country. This time, she was going to leave an impression so horrific that even –she- would not want to relive the night in her memory. Sylus: “That’s five, Luna…only twenty five more to go…” She forcefully slowed her breathing; sweat pouring off her like rain in the worst of storms. She thought it was amazing how her body could take all this, and still keep moving. She often wondered if it was some kind of trick that she could take the beatings she had taken and come away with no broken bones, and only minor annoying bruises. She laughed softly behind the mask, feet and legs pumping in a steady rhythm that carried her around the floor. Sylus: “Fifteen…” She sprung into a double front flip, landing on her feet in stride, still running full steam. From the corner of her eye, she could see Sylus smirk, but an approving one, and it made her smile. It felt good to know she was doing right, and doing well. The laps passed by without count, she didn’t care. She would run all day if he wanted her to. Muscles burned, heart pumped, sweat poured, and she felt the odd sensation she was liking it. Did that make sense? Sylus: “And…thirty. Stop.” She came to a halt, feet skidding across the slick surface of the floor for a moment. Instincts told her to bend over, hands on her knees, and catch her breath, but she didn’t. She stood straight up, simply placing her hands on her slender hips and breathing deeply, eyes closed to allow her body to calm, if only for a moment. But Sylus gave no immediate instruction, and it surprised her. She opened her eyes to find him face to face with her, and she had to keep herself from taking an involuntary step back. Sylus: “How do you feel, Luna?” She took a deep breath. Luna: “Doesn’t matter how I feel…I’m ready for whatever you have to throw at me, Sylus.” He chuckled, arching a brow and stepping away from her. She frowned behind the mask…had she said something wrong? Suddenly he clapped his hands, and two men entered from the side door. One was tall, well built, with a mess of blonde curls atop his head. The other was shorter, but just as well built, and sported a bald look, flames and a burning skull tattooed atop his head. Sylus: “This is Michael and Kyle…they are your…playmates, for today.” She nodded, feeling the blood start to boil through her veins. Always it was that she felt that burn when she knew violence was about to happen, the kind of violence that would leave her bleeding, and in pain…but always the play toys got it worse. Her fingers flexed open and closed as the two men approached her, seeming to size her up. Let them, she thought, cause they had no idea what they were about to get themselves into…or maybe she was the one who didn’t know? Did it really matter? Someone was going to get hurt either way. Michael: “Such a little thing…let’s be careful, Kyle, we might break her.” Kyle (chuckling without humor): “So what if we do? Makes it easier to have our way with her afterward.” She smirked behind the mask…seems Sylus always took pleasure in finding the ones that wanted beat her…before taking her. Whatever, she thought, bring it on. Luna: “You like this body? Well come get it, big boys, let Luna introduce herself.” She took a step back and raised her hands, beckoning them forth. Hmmm…they were fast, and skilled. Before she knew it, she had one wrapped around her legs, the other planting a kick dead center in her chest! Splat! On her back on the floor, which is a dangerous place to be. She grunted with the impact, Michael kneeling down beside her and bringing a fist back, THUMP!, right against her skull. She groaned as she felt her eyes cross and Kyle start working on the suit with bruising fingers. So that was the plan…one beat on her while the other got her undressed, not so fast. Sylus: “Stop fooling around, Luna! I didn’t pay these fools two hundred bucks a pop to not get a show! Get with it!” She snarled behind the mask and suddenly brought an elbow up, smashing Mike in the temple. He fell over sideways, cursing, as Luna suddenly sat up, her thumb –disappearing- into the left eye of Kyle! He screamed, a horrific scream that made Luna shiver as she felt the sickening squirt of blood on her hand. She quickly moved her hand away, frightened. Kyle screamed again, clutching at his head as Michael stood, uncaring of his friend as he rushed forward, slamming his knee into the side of Luna’s head! She smacked against the floor again, barely able to keep conscious as Mike mounted her, and started firing backhands across her face…over and over. Sylus chuckled at the bloody mess of Kyle, who was struggling to his feet, while his partner continued to make a bloody mess of Luna. Somewhere inside her, she snapped completely, and as the umpteenth blow came toward her face, she screamed… ~Dream world~ ”Mmmmm…” She slid lower into the tub, the bubbles tickling her nose and making her giggle in a schoolgirl manner. The heat of the water caressed her skin, making her feel lighter than air, and for some odd reason, safer than she had been in a long, long time. The woman, her friend, that dark beauty, sat on the other side of the tub, ticking Luna’s feet playfully to entice yet another girlish giggle, before settling herself. Luna: “It’s amazing…I never knew a bath could be so relaxing. Then again…I’ve never been this tense in all my life either.” Woman: "You've never been in one of my bath's either. The soaps you’re using cost a hundred bucks a pop." The woman spoke in a matter-of-fact tone, sliding one muscled, black gown-covered leg up onto the side of the tub, revealing a silver-laced combat boot. Leaning back and resting her hands behind her as a mane of obsidian locks flowed with the movements, she continued along. Woman: "Besides, you've earned it. Lots of shit going down out there, seems like the safest place to be is around the dangerous one." Luna nodded, trying her best not to succumb to the pleasures of the heated water and the soothing tone of her friend’s voice. Indeed…the world out there was getting crazier by the minute, and with what she had witnessed in the last month, it was a good enough bet that there would be more to come. Lucky she was able to retain her sanity. Luna: “I don’t think you’re dangerous. Well, ok, that was a lie…but you know what I mean. I know you –can- be dangerous, but I don’t think you’re dangerous to me, or a lot of people. You’re just dangerous to those who deserve it. Same as me…” She sighed, brushing her hands up along her bubble slicked skin, closing light-blue eyes slowly. Woman: "Yeah... Except for how I always seem to hurt those I love..." She closed her eyes as she dipped her hand into the soapy water and lifted it, blowing the soap from her long-nailed hands. Brushing the wet fingers through her hair, she suddenly released a light chuckle, before turning to gaze down at Luna. Woman: "But so far, most of it has been a false love. Perhaps some day we'll find what we're both looking for... Perhaps someday, we'll both be complete, and will never again suffer another nightmare? I'd give everything I have away, for just one year of peace..." Luna (The sadness creeping into her voice): “I had what I wanted…I was complete…and it was taken away from me. If the big guy hadn’t shown up when he did…” She shivered despite the heat of the water. She had watched the love of her life die right in front of her eyes, and it was all she could now to keep from crying in front of her friend, though that would have been perfectly all right. Even still…they both had been through so much in their lives, perhaps forcing strength was the better choice? Woman: "He has a habit of doing that, huh?" A dry chuckle escapes Luna's friend's lips as she leans back on the tub's edge and balances herself, reaching up to itch her cheek with a spiked bracelet. Her chuckle soon turns to a sigh as she lowers her arm, and speaks once more. Woman: "All we can do though, is be as strong as we know our hearts are. One day, you two will be reunited; I hate to admit it, but I have a knack for seeing these things. Exile me to Greece and call me the Oracle babe, but I'm stuck with it." Luna (chuckling softly): “I wouldn’t ask you to lose it. But if you’d dare me to be so bold…you and I both know that the big guy loves you, even if he is…well…a freak show gone badass. Who knows? Perhaps the two of you might have that again someday…but, let’s drop all that, and get your ass in here with me. I need to cuddle, and no one cuddles like you.” Luna winked at her friend, offering the most innocent of smiles. A light chuckle emitted from the dark-haired beauty's mouth and she rolled her eyes upwards with pursed lips. Woman: "Hmmm, a cold marble tub siding, or a warm body that doesn't beg for sex from me; a hard choice indeed..." And as she finishes her words, she simply shifts sideways, clothed and all, slipping into the tub with a splash! Coming up out of the water she laughs, sliding up next to Luna and mock-puckering her lips. Luna (Chuckling): “Oh, don’t tempt me…I’ve heard your stories, young lady…” ~Back to Reality~ She opened her eyes with a start, swinging upward defensively and instinctively, coming up with nothing but air. She gasped, the last fragments of the dream fading away, replaced by the sudden and intense pain of reality. Her mind fog cleared, and she remembered Michael sitting atop her…but there was only silence. She glanced down at herself…there was blood, and a lot of it. She scooted back, horrified by the deep crimson stains that matted her clothing, her hair to her face, and seemed to paint the concrete floor. From behind her there was a deep, humor filled chuckle, and she spun, widened eyes locking on Sylus immediately. Sylus: “Enjoy your nap, Luna?” Luna (stuttering): “Wh…what…what happened?” Sylus pushed himself off the wall he was leaning against, coming to kneel down before Luna, raising a hand to pull the hair from her face and stroke her masked cheek softly. She shivered, remembering her own guttural scream before the dreams had invaded her mind again. She was still ill, still able to feel the sickly sensation of her finger entering Kyle’s eye, and she also wondered had they been able to have their way with her? Was all this blood…hers? Sylus: “You are a –very- dangerous woman, Luna…Bong has no idea what’s in store for him, especially if he begins to really hurt you, hmmm?” Luna: “I…I…” Sylus: “Shhhh…I know what you’re thinking. And to answer your question, you won. Let’s just say Michael and Kyle will never bother anyone again…ever.” Luna swallowed…what did he mean by that? Had she…had she killed them? Surely not! She had passed out…hadn’t she? She suddenly felt woozy and light headed, vision threatening to go black once again as Sylus grabbed her and scooped her up into powerful arms. Like a child, she clung to him, unable to do anything more. Sylus: “Very dangerous indeed…come, its time for you to truly rest. Bong awaits you, and you must be prepared to give him an introduction he will not soon forget.” ~Finish~
