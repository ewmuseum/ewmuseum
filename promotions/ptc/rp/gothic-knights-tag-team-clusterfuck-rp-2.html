Shadowmane Public Access Pimp Joined: 10 Feb 2003 Posts: 19 Location: Cirque de Soleil Posted: Fri Apr 11, 2003 5:26 pm Post subject: [Gothic Knights] Tag Team Clusterfuck RP#2 Eddie and Tommy woke up the next morning and did the regular morning routines. After breakfast, they went to retrieve Tommy’s Firebird from the bar. They pulled in and sat there for a moment, looking at the bar. “Tell me,” said Tommy. “Just what gave you the bright idea to enter us in this tag clusterfuck anyway? I’ve just gotten back to 100%… well, 100% for me. The doctor says I might not ever be the same again.” “I think that’s bullshit,” said Eddie. “I’m not letting you quit, Tommy. You worked too hard last time to quit now. Remember how excited you were when we thought we had a contract? I mean, hell, we were about to wrestle in the DWO, a premier league in the PTC.” “Yeah,” said Tommy, smiling. “We were on our way again, but I got injured. I proved that I wasn’t strong enough for this. What makes you think I’m strong enough now?” “Because you have spirit, Tommy,” said Eddie, casting a look at his cousin. “You were the one that didn’t let me give up before. Now I’m repaying you. I’m not letting you give up. Get in your car and meet me at Otterman’s Gym. I set up a workout regimen with Stan. He and his father are going to help us get back in shape. We’re coming out of the gate running hard this time.” Tommy turned and studied Eddie for a moment, then smiled. “Alright Eddie, you’ve talked me into it. I warn you, though, if I get injured again, you’re going to have to fight with Stan. I’m not ruining my life for this.” “Your life won’t be ruined, Tommy,” said Eddie. “You’re going to come out of this in the best shape you’ve ever been in. There’ve been plenty of wrestlers taken out of the action that subsequently returned to the ring. Some of them actually returned and dominated the industry. You’re going to be fine, and with Stan’s help, we will work our way back to the top.” Tommy nodded, then got out of the car, walking over and getting into his own. He sat there thinking over what Eddie had been saying as Eddie drove off. He had wanted this bad when they started looking. He wanted to work his way back up through the ranks, and maybe, one day, be a singles star again. But that was ahead of him… too far ahead of him. What he needed now was to do something, and Eddie was offering him something to do. He fired up his Firebird and peeled out of the Dark Talon, leaving all of the boozing behind, at least for a time anyway. He made his way down the road, thinking the possibilities over. By the time he pulled into Steele’s gym, he had convinced himself that he could accomplish his goals, his injuries notwithstanding. He pulled the car to a stop and shut the engine off, taking a deep breath. This was it. Either he would succeed in this adventure, or he would go down fighting. Eddie was right… Tommy wasn’t a quitter. Eddie had left Tommy to himself. He drove off and went straight to the gym, parking and going in. He looked around, watching some of the students in the ring with Stan. Stan Otterman, whose ring name was “3-Sexy” Stan Steele, was the son of wrestling promoter Ernest “Ernie” Otterman, who had also been a big time grappler in the 50’s 60’s and 70’s. It was Ernie that had trained the four premier wrestlers to come from this school, Fritz and Eddie, Stan and Tommy. Now he would be needed again, only this time, to whip the new tag team into shape. Since Tommy and Eddie had only wrestled together on one occasion before, it would be Ernie and Stan’s job to work their styles together. It would be Eddie and Tommy’s jobs to work together as a team. This always came easy for Eddie with Fritz. He had been battling Fritz since he was a baby. However, Tommy had come along when he was 11. They had their fights, but they weren’t as close as Fritz and Eddie were. They had to break through the differences and figure out what made them, as a team, tick. Ernie would help them. If anyone could do it, Ernie could. The old, grizzled wrestler, with a bushy mustache and white hair walked over and greeted Eddie with a smile. “Hello Eddie,” he said, in a Brooklyn accent. “Welcome back. Stan tells me you signed yourself up for that tag team match at the Primetime Central Pay-Per View. You sure you want to go through with that? I mean if Tommy’s not in good shape, it’s going to be hard.” Eddie nodded. “I know. That’s why we’re coming to you. We’ve not worked together but once. You trained Fritz and me, and we were champions. Tommy and Stan worked together. It’s going to be a tough transition process.” “It won’t be that hard,” said Ernie. “After all, I teach you all pretty much the same style, with different variations. I’ve already come up with some things you can use together. We’ll just have to see what Tommy can do.” They walked over and watched as Stan coached a student in the art of taking a bump properly. Eddie remembered doing this himself. Armdrags, over and over, all day long. This kid would be sore in the morning. He was looking forward to getting back into the ring. He wanted to get the ring rust out as quickly as possible and get down to serious business. Just then, Tommy walked in the door. Tommy looked around, dropping his gym bag to the ground. He took in the sight before him while inhaling the musky, sweaty odor that seemed to emanate from the very walls around him… thirty years of sweat and tears… countless men who have come and gone, some leaving their mark on the wrestling world, others failing utterly… all hitting him like a brick in the face. This was where heroes were made, and demons were vanquished. God knows I need to vanquish some demons, thought Tommy to himself as he picked his bag back up and approached Eddie and Ernie. This was the moment of truth. Was he going to go through with this? Was he ready for the trials that were about to come his way? Only one man could figure that out… Tommy Noble himself. Eddie watched, as Tommy seemed to hesitate at the door. He began wondering if this was the right time to try this. Maybe he was pushing Tommy too hard. Who knew? He was just glad he had saved Tommy from drinking himself into a stupor last night. As Tommy picked up his bag and approached, Eddie smiled. Perhaps there was hope after all. “Let’s do this,” said Tommy, as he approached the two. “I’m not going to hang back. I’ve got to know if this is going to work, and I’ve got to know today.” Ernie smiled. He liked that attitude. “Alrightie,” he said. “Go get yourselves changed and I’ll get Stan to wrap it up with Jimmy over there.” “Just so you know from the start,” began Tommy, “I’m here to make it or break it. Don’t hold back on me. I have to know. I can’t get out there in that arena and go down to an injury that could’ve been foretold right here.” Ernie frowned. He didn’t like it, but if that’s what was necessary, he would do it. “Alright, then we set up the circumstances you’re going to be in. I’ll get all my boys out here, and they’ll all have a go at you, even the students that can barely take a bump. If you have to know, you have to know.” “I do,” said Tommy, turning and walking toward the dressing room. Eddie shrugged at Ernie and followed. A few hours, and several hundred slams later, the two of them were getting on a hot streak. They were getting back into the grove of the mat dance, and were beginning to work together on some of the things Ernie had shown them at the beginning of the session. Tommy’s back had held out this far, and he was feeling like a million bucks. Finally, Ernie called it quits. He had to send his guys to the showers so they could go home, or to work, or wherever else they went when the gym closed. After everyone cleared out, Ernie sat down with Tommy and Eddie and went over strategies, possible gimmicks, and things they could do to impress promoters scouting them the night of the fight. At this point, Tommy just wanted to compete in the one event. He didn’t have designs on running the road for a while. He felt that if they could get a few matches under their belt, to test the waters, they could get the prestige to go wherever they wanted. Eddie, on the other hand, was ready to run the road. He wanted to get back into the lifestyle that he had enjoyed before. He wanted to meet the interesting women, find those out of the way bars, and make a name for himself out of the ring. They argued over their differing points of view for a while, finally deciding that they would have to come back to it later… after the event had taken place. For now, they would continue to practice and train for the big event. _________________ Gothic Knights - Professional Acrobats and confirmed Free Agents. Dilya Love - 100% pure Dominatrix!
