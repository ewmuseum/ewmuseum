PTC — Roleplay

Tag Team Roleplaying

4 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Pajaro de Presa… the Bird of Prey… the Rudo with Attitude-o… proud member of the Masked Monkeys…” “Mommy, why is that masked man standing in a garbage can and talking to himself?” “Shhh, Bobby. Don’t stare. He’s a luchador.” He certainly is. He is Pajaro de Pesa… The Bird of Prey... the Ru- oh, we’ve already been through all of that. Well, he’s standing in a garbage can in a Chicago city park, looking as dignified as a guy in a bird mask knee-deep in trash is able. Shading his eyes with a hand, he looks at the sun. “Hmmm… it’s about that time.” Pajaro de Presa takes a bird call from his pocket and give it a loud toot. Moments later, a cute, white duck comes waddling up. “There you are, Plucky! Things good at home?” “Quack.” “Wonderful! And your little ninos and ninas?” “Quack quack.” “Well, you know how kids are. Ready to go?” “Quack.” The duck flies up and lands on Pajaro’s head. Just then a strong breeze comes up. “Whoah! They don’t call this the Windy City for noting!” Suddenly, a battered magazine smacks Pajaro de Presa in the face. “Ack! What is this?” He pulls the magazine off is face and looks at the cover. “Sweaty Men in Tights Rolling Around Together Illustrated. This is a wrestling magazine! I wonder if I’m in it!” He isn’t. “I’m not? Sure, there’s a twelve page article about Tsunami Oni! What’s the big deal about that guy, Plucky?” “Quack.” “Sure, if you rate success by accomplishing stuff. Hey! Check this out! A tag tournament! Why didn’t I know about this? The Masked Monkeys were UWF tag team champions!” “Quack quack.” “So we lost the belts in our first defense. It was a Pizza Parlour of Pain match! Clearly the deck was stacked against fine Mexican luchadores such as myself and White Tiger! But all that is in the past and I have a hard enogh time remembering what happened five seconds ago! Uh, what were we taking about?” “Quack.” “Oh yes! Gracias, Plucky. Let’s go see the big boss!” His mind (or whatever he has in his skull) made up, Pajaro de Presa ducks into the garbage can and pulls the lid over. Meanwhile, UWF head honcho Richard Hayes is busy interviewing a sweet young thing for a position. What that position will be has yet to be determined. But apparently one of the duties is sitting on the lap of Richard Hayes. “So, Mistee, how are you at taking diction?” “Oh, Mr. Hayes!” “What about following oral directions? And would you call yourself anal-retentive?” “Tee-hee!” Soft music fills the air… "People dancing all in the street See the rhythm in their feet Life is good, wild and sweet Let the music play on Feel it in your heart and feel it in your soul Let the music take control We’re gonna Parti,’ Liming, Fiesta, forever Come on and sing along We’re gonna Parti,’ Liming, Fiesta, forever Come on and sing my song ALL NIGHT LONG" Mistee giggles some more. “I didn’t know you had such a great sound system in here!” Hayes look befuddled. “Uh, I don’t” Richard Hayes and Mistee look towards the music. There in the wastebasket stands Pajaro de Presa, with a guitar in his hands and a duck on his head. He smiles at the pair. “Hiya, Ricardo! Hola, cherry cola! Hayes doesn’t look to happy to see the Rudo with Attitude-. He gives Mistee a gentle swat on the tush. “Why don’t you run along? We’ll finish the interview later.” “OK, Dicky! Oops! I mean, Mr. Hayes! Bit I have cheerleading practice tonight!” “I know.” After Mistee leaves, Hayes turns turns to Pajaro. “All right, you idiot. What are you doing violating the sanctity of this office with Lionel Richie songs?” “Just enhancing the mood, El Jefe!” “And I know I’m going to regret this, but why is there a duck on your head?” “This is Plucky. Say hello to the boss!” “Quack.” Hayes gives the duck a rather insincere smile before turning his attention back to Pajaro. “And what is… Plucky doing here?” “Training! We were gonna use the gym!” “Training? For wrestling?” Pajaro lets out a big belly laugh. “Oh no, boss! A duck wrestling? That would be loco! Nah, Plucky is training for cockfighting!” “Cockfighting?” “Yup.” “Isn’t cockfighting for chickens?” “Don’t me a hater, homes.” “Quack.” Richard Hayes buries his face in his hands. “Fine. Go train with your duck.” “Quack.” A lightbulb appears over Pajaro’s head. Or it would, except there’s a duck in the way. “Hey, boss! Plucky reminded me of something. Check this out!” Pajaro throws the magazine open on the desk. Hayes looks to where the Bird of Prey is pointing. “Body hair removal cream? Thanks, but I’m fine.” “Not that. This!” “PTC Tag tournament. So?” “So why didn’t anybody tell me about this?” “You think I would let you represent the Universal Wrestling Federation? With other federation in attendance?” “But there are chicas in this! I’d get to roll around with chicas! And one’s Asian! “I think she’s with Blaise McClancy.” “Bah, another McClancy! Always hoggin’ the mamacitas! When will the ladies learn that once you’ve had Chicano, the rest is just guano!” “Listen, Pajaro. Don’t take this the wrong way. You’re a complete moron. And you are what we in the business call a jobber. But you’re such an unreliable screw-up that you can’t even be relied on to screw up. You actually won the Television championship. Then you brought White Tiger down to your level. And, for reason known only to the Lord Almighty, the two of you actually managed to become Tag Team champions. But you can’t hold on to titles. All you do is bring down their value. Plus, you just annoy the hell out of me. So if you think I’m going to let you run off somewhere and become the PTC’s problem instead of mine, then… then…” “Yeah boss?” “THEN YOU’RE ABSOLUTELY RIGHT!” Hayes stands up with a big smile on his face. He holds out his hand and Pajaro shakes it heartily. “Gosh! Thanks, boss! C’mon, Plucky! We got training to do!” “Quack!” Pajaro de Presa and Plucky leave the office. Richard Hayes looks very pleased with himself. And when he’s pleased, he wants pleasure. He takes out his cell phone and dials. “Mistee? Dicky, here. I forgot that the interview process require a physical. A very thorough physical. Which I will be conducting personally. Tonight, sound good? No, no. Leave your cheerleading outfit on…”
