Date: 2005-12-05 Universal Wrestling Federation presents… In association with Primetime Central… Directed by Chris the Gardener… Produced by Sam Gordon… KENNY MERCURY is… KENNY MERCURY… in… KENNY MERCURY’S GTT ADVENTURE The lights dim… the curtain opens… the magic begins. Two and a half hours later… Kenny Mercury sits in the theater, surrounded by the select few chosen to view the private screening of the UWF’s newest release. He could not believe what he had just witnessed… the story… the characters… the lights… the music… the dancing… the singing… the plot twist… the sex scene… the MAGIC. It was like nothing he had ever seen before, and most likely, would never see again. "That was…" Kenny spoke, but paused… he was at a loss for words… Simply flabbergasted. "Yes?" Smiled one of the UWF filmmakers. "That was… THE BIGGEST PILE OF BAT GUANO I HAVE EVER SEEN!" The filmmakers smile takes a tack hammer and bashes itself over the cranium several times then swallows a jug of Clorox bleach. It died, and all that remained was a big ole frown. "What?" The filmmaker asked… I guess flabbergast-ism is contagious. "You heard me… that was the WORST movie I have EVER SEEN! I won’t be caught dead being associated with a film like that. I mean, who the fuck was that who played me!? Was that fucking Mark-Paul Gosselaar!?" "Who?" "ZACK FUCKING MORRIS, YOU IMBECILIC MONKEY-GOOSEFUCK! ASK CHRIS STORM" "…" "I… am… appalled. I’m leaving. Fuck you, and fuck your stupid konkey dong movie… Good day sir." Kenny said as he stood up and headed for the door. "But Kenny! Wait—" Kenny spun around, flinging his long blonde hair around his head dramatically. He fumed… but then suddenly regained his composure. Then, as if trying to show the filmmaker what REAL acting is… Kenny opened his mouth… "I said… GOOD DAY… sir." And with that… Mercury stormed out of the theater. "Heinous anus…" Kenny Mercury shuffled down the frigid Chicago streets. The city’s trademark wind tore at Kenny’s flesh, ripping it right off his skull. The city was miserable this time of year, and the worst part was… winter had just begun. The old man’s grip would only get tighter as the months passed, and it was going to suck donkey nuts. "Rod damn that federation… Rod damn it to hell." Kenny muttered as he finally reached his apartment. He fiddled with his keys in the cold. "Fucking useless… I thought we went through this two years ago…" And he was right. Two years had passed from the time Kenny Mercury first took Primetime Central by storm. It was in 2003 that Kenny Mercury ran rampant through out the ranks of the Primetime Central Invitational Tournament, reaching the semi-finals all because of his secret weapon. His secret weapon? No, not his superior technical skills… but the fascination of the PTC fans. They loved the fact that Kenny Mercury was in the UWF… and the UWF annoyed the crap out of Kenny. Kenny hated UWF and PTC fans loved the fact that Kenny was agitated. Well… it’s been three years… and no, nothing has changed. Kenny Mercury laid down in his empty bed. The sheets were cold… just like the city… and the apartment. It suited him. No warmth ever emanated from his man, and all he touched turned to ice. Kenny slipped on his pajamas, which were littered with little logos of a red Saturn-esque ringed planet, and his blindfold… that’s the only way he could fall asleep. Maybe it was because the light bothered him and he needed total darkness… but most like to think it’s because he’d rather not see the person who stabs him to death in his sleep. Kenny didn’t have any friends. "Brandon Youngblood…" Kenny whispered into the black. The next day would bring about perhaps the greatest challenge for Kenny yet… Brandon Youngblood. The GTT would be under way and what experts called one of the most exciting match-ups of the entire first round would come to pass. Kenny Mercury didn’t know fear… his head was too big. But still, this match made him a little nervous. He gazed blankly into the night. What would the next day hold for Mr. Mercury? He wanted answers. "Who?" A voice spoke up from the darkness. Kenny Mercury sprang up from his bed and ripped the blindfold from his eyes. Panic filled him as his eyes darted about the room rapidly. "Who’s there?!" Kenny shouted. "Relax, Kenny… It’s only me… your best friend in the whoooole wide world…" "Who are you?" Kenny quickly leapt from his bed and slammed his fist on the light switch. With a click, light poured into the room. And there was no one. "What the hell was that…" Kenny took a deep breath. "Me." "GYAH!" Kenny jumped back about ten feet… "SHOW YOURSELF!" "It is I, Kenny… the UWF World Heavyweight Championship… your best and only friend…" Kenny cocked his eyebrow… It’s not like things like this had never occurred to him before, if you’ve ever watched a Purple Haze promotion, you’d know what I’m talking about… but Kenny Mercury was skeptical. Kenny methodically made his way back over to his bed. Above the headboard, under glass and upon the wall, hung his UWF World Heavyweight Title belt, made for him after his title reign in the past summer. That summer had faded and changed to winter now… as had that title reign… it was over and existed only in memory and on UWF home video. "You have got to kidding me…" "No. No joke… I am the UWF World Heavyweight Title… and I am here for you." "Here for me? What the hell do you mean? I don’t need anyone’s help! Why are you really here?! And why do you sound like Bobcat Goldthwait!?" "Don’t be silly… I heard you calling out for aid… Your spirit ached for my counsel, whether you knew it or not. And I do NOT sound like Bobcat Goldthwait… You are thinking of Gilbert Godfrey… and thanks a lot… that’s a REALLY big confidence boost… thanks…" "I sense your sarcasm…" "No… really?" "Hey! Stop that! I never knew the UWF World title was such a cynic." "I was held by a fucking chimpanzee… What do you expect!?" "It could have been David Arquette, you know…" "Good point… But anywho! I’m here for you, my son." "Well, I don’t need your help… at all." "Kenny… of all the greats that ever held me… you were always one of my favorites… Now I know you’re a little nervous about this match you’ve got against what’s-his-name…" "What’s-his-name! Ha! He’s the PRIME posterboy… I’m actually considered an UNDERDOG! Can you believe it, ME an underdog? It’s absurd!" "PRIME posterboy? Tony Rolo? Woo boy… Maybe you ARE screwed…" "No, not him." "Ah… Angelo Deville? Yikes…" "No." "Hoyt Williams? Shit… I can’t help you there…" "No, damn it, it’s Brandon Youngblood!" "Who?" "See! You can’t help me!" "BAH! I was only kidding… look Kenny… remember back to the last time you were dubbed the underdog…. Remember back to the PIT… and remember back to the feeling you felt when you upset Tristan Belmont… do you recall that? Do you recall the rush… the pure electricity that came over you, that ran through your veins? Do you remember how GOOD that felt?" Kenny smiled… "Yes…" "This is no different… you said it yourself… Brandon Youngblood is a coward… he beats up poor defenseless women for chrissake." "But I beat women all the time!" Kenny’s voice went up. "Yeah, but you do it for no reason! Just for fun! Youngblood does it because he’s a pussy!" "Oh… right." "He has doubts… he doubts himself… He calls himself mediocre… and a LOSER… He’s a failure… even the very federation that he EMBODIED has cast him out." "So?" "So!? So, the UWF hates you… with a passion… yet you know that they’d NEVER fire you. They need you… you are the very foundation in which that company rests. You are STRONGER than Youngblood. You are more valuable and you are SUPERIOR. This is your chance to prove it. Youngblood has his doubts… but you… you have NEVER been one to come into question when it comes to self confidence. Do you know why?" ‘Because… I’m the technical wizard?" "Yes." "And the shooting star!" "Yes! Yes!" "The submission exhibition… And the definition of precision! I’M KENNY MERCURY!" "YES! YOU ARE!" "I beat Chris Storm at Super Series… I am a UWF Hall of Famer… I made Argyle lick my taint… I made Tristan Belmont wet his panties… I made myself into the best professional wrestler out there." "Prove it… prove it against Brandon Youngblood." "Right… I will… I will… because I am Kenny Mercury… And I read somewhere that I rule." "Good boy." Kenny Mercury hits the lights… and pulled his blindfold back down over his eyes. This match was his; he knew it deep down in his cold, black heart. He was Kenny Mercury, Rod damn it… The next morning, it was time to head to the GTT. Kenny Mercury had woke up extra early, eaten his Wheaties, and filed his fingernails. He liked to eat shavings. Kenny Mercury was perfection… and none of his perfection would be wasted. But he didn’t eat his doo-doo butter, because that’s dirty. Kenny piled onto the UWF tour bus. Mercury stood at the front of the bus and looked down the aisle. His eyes passed on by each row, and the complete retard occupying the seat. Kenny almost vomited… this was probably the most idiotic and, quite simply, insulting bunch of nut-crusts Kenny had been forced to interact with in quite some time. Kenny began to slowly make his way to the back of the bus. He passed Bruce Gordy, the hardcore butcher and Gigantor, the wrestling sasquatch. He passed Jerry ‘Pops’ Kohler, the senile and pant-less referee, and the Terror- Daktyl. He passed Big Ted’s Arm, a severed teddy bear limb that struck fear in the hearts of the entire UWF (yes, it occupied its own seat) and Jay the metrosexual Indian janitor. Yes, he passed them all… and then finally reached the back seat. Two sets of anxious eyes met Kenny’s, looking up at him from either side of the aisle. On one side… El Turtlé, the Mexican lucha libre ninja turtle… and the other, Stee. Kenny looked at El Turtlé… then at Stee… then back to Turtlé… then back at Stee. Kenny shook his head. "Alright, one of you jobber ass cunt bubbles have to get the fuck up." El Turtlé simply looked up at Kenny Mercury, giving Kenny his enormous ninja turtle smile. Stee spit on the ground then flipped Kenny the bird. Stee: shut the fuk up Kenny u cant just wakl in her and make dmands im not geting up for anyon not u not no one cuz im the god the king THE MAN stee 316" Kenny glared at Stee, "You jobber dung ass dickweed… Do you have any idea who I am? I’m Kenny Mercury, a former World champ and in the HALL OF FAME… You have to give your bus seat up to ME… because I superior to you… Jobber." Stee was not happy… Stee: stfu kenny im a person too just cuz u get a bigger push then me dosnt mean ur any better im just as good as u r and i hav rights damn it! im not getting up! Kenny was taken back by this sudden heroic outburst by Stee… and he recoiled for a moment to recollect this thoughts. Okay, now he’s got them. "Die." Kenny reached behind him and grabbed a big slab of beef that Bruce Gordy was talking to. He took the mighty hunk of flesh in his skilled hands and rammed it down Stee’s wrestling tights. With a whistle, he signaled for Gigantor. "Here boy, here Gigantor!" Gigantor, the wrestling yeti man-beast had caught the scent of something tasty… something delicious and with bad grammar and typing habits. Gigantor, with a powerful roar, lunged out to the back of the bus and devoured Stee right then and there. Kenny took his seat on the bus… and they were off… off on the road to the GTT. Brandon Youngblood awaited the arrival of Kenny… and Kenny leaned back in his seat and visualized the match ahead… the sound of the crowd… the smell of the canvass… the sweet nectar of victory… Yes, Brandon Youngblood was in over his head and— What the FUCK!? Suddenly, the entire bus was engulfed by an ultra bright purple light. Kenny and the other passengers are immediately blinded… and that’s the last thing Kenny remembered… Time seemed to not exist… Kenny drifted in and out of consciousness… He danced between dreams and reality… He could not decipher between what was and what was not… the is and is not… a cloud covered his eyes… Kenny look up at a white light, burning straight down into his eyes. Someone stood to the left of his… and there, another one! Someone else on the right… They held some kind of tool or instrument in their hands… but what? And Kenny fell into darkness… The next thing he knew, Kenny was in an empty white room… and he was naked… A grey mist came out through the walls… and once again Kenny blacked out… What was all this? What was happening to him? Kenny Mercury had no answers, he was helpless. He had a tournament to win! The GTT! Brandon Youngblood was waiting for him… He needed to get to the tournament! …the tournament! … the tournament… Kenny Mercury opened his eyes. "THE TOURNAMENT!" He shrieked. "THE TOURNAMENT!" A thunderous voice rang through Kenny’s head. It came from right… through the… Bars? Kenny Mercury was in an enclosed area, sealed off by metallic bars. He ran up to them and grabbed a hold of them. Through his cell… he saw the most wondrous thing… A large pedestal surrounded by at least twenty more of these cells. Within each cell was a collection of the strangest beings he had ever seen… All shapes, sizes, and colors… Each within its own cell. Kenny swallowed hard… "A… Aliens!?" Atop the tall pedestal sat a large green squid-like creature… Its eyes were large and entirely obsidian black. A shirt of long tentacles surrounded the monster, and they jiggled as he spoke through his wide amphibian mouth. "The tournament… Will commense immediately… Once you are in the DANGAH ZONE… there will be no escape, until the dirt is stained with the blood of your enemies… the winners will be sent back to their home world with the knowledge that they have saved their home… And the rest… they shall die knowing that they let down their entire species… and that I will take great pleasure DESTROYING their entire planet! NYAHAHAHA!" "WHAT?! YOU CAN’T DO THIS!" Kenny screamed out. The giant beast turned to Kenny . "And why not?" "Because I have a GTT to win… and if you blew up my planet… then I can’t kick everyone’s ass!" "Nyahaha… FOOL! I am the almighty Thrak-Kah! I do as I please… and it pleases me to see your planets finest warriors do battle to the death! It stimulates me in ways you simple beings can only imagine…" "What?! Why! Why me!? You’ve got the wrong guy!" Kenny desperately appealed. "Are you not Kenny of Mercury? Are you not… The Wizard, Kenny Mercury?" Kenny smiled, he MUST have been the greatest wrestler ever! I mean, even aliens had heard of him,"You’ve… heard of me? Yeah, I’m the technical wizard." "And you are… the shooting star?" Stroke, stroke, stroke… "Yes, of course I am." "And you are the greatest warrior that your planet can produce, correct?" Stroke, stroke—Oh god, I’m gunna squirt! "Of course I am. I am KENNY MERCURY!" "Then I have the right guy. The ULTIMO TOURNAMENT OF GALAXIES shall begin at once." "Wait—No!" "Don’t worry… You shall coupled to a device that will teleport you back to your home world for your precious little GTT… BUT! ANYTHING BUT THAT!" "NYAHAHAHAHA! MAY THE GAMES BEGIN!" Thrak-Kah decreed. And with that, the bottom of the holding cell slid out from under Kenny. He tumbled down into a metallic tube, and was sucked down through it. With each passing moment, he picked up speed. Kenny could not tell where he going nor how fast. Then suddenly, Kenny was shot out of the tube and landed with a thud on solid ground. He slowly staggered to his feet, and brushed himself off, noting that he was curiously wearing his wrestling gear and tights. "GAH! At least Schwarzenegger got a pod…" Our ‘hero’ turned back behind himself to find the contingent of UWF castoff wrestlers and jobbers from the tour bus. This pack of about two dozen misfits were surrounded by a surrealist landscape, filled with twisted black trees and craters. "Great… just great… now I’m stuck with you fuck-knobs…" Kenny shook his head… "Now I know this is a life and death situation… so none of you idiots go getting attached to me… because I’ll kill you just as fast as one of those alien things JUST BECAUSE I CAN! Now I need to STAY ALIVE long enough to beat that cum-quat ass-hat jizzlobbing scrotal-gopher, Brandon Youngblood… and if I’m lucky… long enough to win the whole damn GTT…" "And save earth?" Resident UWF cowboy ring announce Tommy Waters timidly piped. "Yeah… whatever…" "EEEEEEEERRRROOOORRRAK!" Out from behind an alien tree leaps a large human like bat creature. Its eyes red with rage and its claws thirsting for earthling blood. It’s lust drove it mad… and it brought its wrath down upon Kenny Mercury. "Kenny! Here!" Grael Skullcrusher, the jobbing Lord of the Rings Orc cried. Kenny spun around, just as Grael tossed him a steel trident. Kenny caught it, and in one fluid motion, drove the trident deep into the chest of the alien. The creature screamed out in agony for a moment… then went limp. It collapses into a pool of its own entrails and bodily fluids. "Right… Let’s go." Kenny smirked. And they forged onward. "Youngblood… I’m coming…"
