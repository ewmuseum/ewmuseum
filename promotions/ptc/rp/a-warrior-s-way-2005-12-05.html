A Warrior's Way

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 The ultimate measure of a man is not where he stands in moments of comfort and convenience, but where he stands at times of challenge and controversy. -Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. *** RUANNHHRUANNHHRUANNHHRUANNHHRUANNHHRUANNHHRUANNHH~! At first, it was a quick swat with an open hand to silence the incoherently insane screams from a state of the art alarm clock. Solace was found in the peaceful slumber, the hours where he did not have to be cognizant. Those moments had become the only ones worth living for. But as if mirroring the trepid screams that had recently enveloped his life, he could not find the silence he so desperately sought. Eventually, the somber agitation gave way to frantic frustration. Since the futile swats had proven ineffective, he sat up on his bed and grabbed at the clock, only to launch it across the room into the wall. Typical irrationality from Brandon Youngblood. For years his rants would tear asunder any semblance of calm he tried to create, allowing his attitude to make something atrocious out of his personality. Hot headed. Someone who could scream and roar and bring violence upon anyone that even had the slightest hint of dissent from his own view point. Someone who was the complete embodiment of hypocrisy. Fight a drug addict to thrust morals upon him while he would go get high on OxyContin. Scream for wrestling purity and then wield a chair deftly and cheat his way to victory. Plead with ABC officials after an over the top segment involving striking his wife, stating "I'd never really harm her" then months later break her jaw in a verbal dispute in public which not only causes legal recourse, but also signals the end of something he had promised yourself would last forever. Lie to the people about being a National Champion amateur wrestler. Tim Hartung was the baddest motherfucker in the weight division in 1999. Hell, the guy posted an undefeated season that year, and pretty much destroyed him in Big Ten competition. Back to back National Championships were won by Hartung at Minnesota, while Youngblood was busy vying for Second Team All Big Ten. But hey…when your Brandon Youngblood, lying and sawed off anger comes customary. A swig from a bottle of some straight Kentucky bourbon that was on the nightstand. And not even that is safe from the intense anger that he exudes, for it joins the alarm clock on the other side of the room, shattering into pieces. His eyes were blood shot. An overwhelming desire to just turn over and die filled his insides. But there was a lingering sense of something vital. Even when someone is deep in a hole, there is some portion inside that screams to get back on the right path. He picked himself up, grabbing a pair of black gym shorts and nothing else. Over the weeks he'd gained weight. Atrophy works that way. It is the detriment to inactivity. He would surely feel sore, and there was a large possibility that five minutes in he would quit. It hurt, and without the painkillers his superhuman training regimen would be nearly impossible. But for the ninety-nine angels screaming inside for him to just roll over, there was a singular devil quietly whispering for him to push forward. He walked out of his room, hunched over, the burden of his own self made pity nearly causing him to collapse. All he ever wanted was to be unbeatable. The left hand path, the right hand path, it did not matter. Good or evil, it is all a faux notion. Humanity is inherently evil. It is bred; actually welcomed with open arms. Every man has a mean streak, something that will tide them over should they ever need to welcome the primal urges of days long gone. Survival of the fittest. It's a battle that always ends in irreprehensible bloodshed over nothing more than pride. There is no meaning behind it until you give it a warrior's sort of outlook. All that matters in life is pride. To look at it otherwise is laughably complex. Life is a black and white struggle. Grey is created to tide over indecisiveness. His life had been black and white. But as he limped on, the large scale gash on his right calf nearly causing him to fall over in his exhaustion, he started questioning himself. Had the Warrior's Way been the right path? It was all nominal now; there was no way around the past, and most importantly there was no way around the transgressions that led to his exodus. A thick haze of humidity filled the forest, his worn bare feet plodding atop the sodden dirt floor. He limped forward, each step feeling very much like it could be the last. A stark contrast, this was, of not too long ago. His mangy black beard and hair were gone, as were the brilliantly vile red orbs he peered through. The sky was a clouded gray, little droplets of rain falling with such indifference that as it fell it nearly evaporated. His body was a mass of cuts and barely healing wounds mixed in with a dirty appearance of grime and tired sweat. An exhaustion exuded from every pore. He could not even look ahead, instead focusing downward in his irrecoverable shame. The former tanned and focused warrior who had struck fear in every being in the world now wayward, a mass of red and pale flesh. So disgraced was he that the sky could not even open for him. The dojo pants swayed, but even they were in tatters. His Eden had cast him aside. His home, his paradise. No longer did he have to sift through the desert sands in the company of mere mangy wolves. He was taken in by likeminded warriors, honing in on enlightenment and focusing their abilities to reach a level of awareness deemed unattainable without years of arduous toiling. But his intentions were never to be one with the guild. Instead, he used it as a chance to find competition. With the long grass blowing in his face, he rather swing his sword, attempting to cut through the wind coming off the crystal blue ocean than partake in hunting and gathering. While the hunt was always plentiful, he rather slice through the bone and sinew of his fellow companions for what he viewed were their inadequacies. He was a mortar, disrupting the peace to champion what to him was a conceived Spartan existence. He challenged their legends, the one's who the guild looked upon for leadership. And his technically proficient strikes did their damage, wounding without fail anyone who dared step up to his challenge. Not always was he able to stand up after combat, at times losing, but never was he soundly destroyed. Even in the face of the Deville. He would not stand down, choosing instead to charge head on into combat with a man who was practically unbeatable. But when it was finally the Deville who was defeated, the realization to everyone in the camp was that not only was this warrior dangerous, but also relentless in achieving a legacy. A viable threat to all was this tyrant; a man so drunk on his own ego that he could not see the tempers rise from within Eden. Their sneers, their verbal wars, all of it never dawned upon him until he faced the challenge of the Chief. He was to be the Champion, to be the warrior who led them through conquest and victory over all in the region. But the long bearded man of the God's met his relentless charge and soundly defeated him. No longer were his delusions of grandeur a reality. Unable to cope, he challenged the entire guild to fight him. If he could not be their leader, then they could not live. Almost instantly he was ravaged, unable to fight a war from all sides. Beaten near death, bloody and mortally wounded, he was shamed. Exiled and forevermore banished from Paradise. His Valhalla was lost. Even his name was taken from him, forcing him into an existence of mindless wandering. He sought shelter elsewhere, trying to heal the wounds that were given to him as a price for his ambition. Rejection faced him at every turn. It was a struggle to survive. Limping. Wounded. Shamed. There was absolutely no reason to push on. Realization of his own decrypted behavior consumed him. The vision of perfection he sought for so long forever gone from him. Never again would he have a place to call home. His desire of a legacy through blood left him with nothing. The trek was one across the country, to the sandy beaches to the west. There was promise there, and quite possibly he could find some semblance of inner peace. No longer would he draw his sword in combat once arriving there, instead opting to a life of celibacy and worship. Never again would he shame himself through his greed. But each step made the trek even longer. In his condition, it was completely implausible to make it there and swim in the ocean waters, especially with the City of Dis in the distant horizon. He shuddered, baring his teeth and black gums at the thought of sifting through the land of the drifting dead. The sound of a blade cutting through flesh. Until the point of impact, it was without sound. Crimson spilled forth for the gapping wound that extended down the expanse of his spine, ending just at the sheath for his sword. The pain quickly accompanied the cut, but he refused to go down. Fighting back tears and with a pained smirk on his face, he clamped his eyes shut, hunching over. A authoritative kick to the small of his back sent him face first into the muck below. His body was completely sapped. His mind had long since abandoned any desire to continue on. These were surely his final moments. A jump overhead by a barefoot showman. His tights were black, and his flowing blonde hair was done in a ponytail. Landing on his feet like a cat, he twisted and swung his sword like a braggart. His nature was cavalier, the only thing he lacked was a long pointed moustache to twirl as he threw one liners out against his opposition. "DAMN! Nothin' more than a wasted shell. And from the looks of your scars, I thought I was going to bag myself a worthy catch. I'm the definition of precision after all! You'll be so glad when it's over…you'll be able to tell your God that you were slayed by the true shooting star." His arrogance sickened him, but there was absolutely nothing he could do, even if he wanted to. All he could do was close his eyes and hope it was over quickly. The grains of sand were like ice shards, freezing and cutting into his feet. He just could not drive himself to move forward, the awkward feeling of uncertainty hitting him. Could he do this without something inside him to deaden the pain? Would his stamina be what it was before? Before it was just about going out there and doing it, buzzed or not. He did not even know if he wanted this life anymore. He plodded around the garage, the back door open as the powdery crescent moon shined up above. It taunted him. Brandon dedicated his life to wrestling. But being fired, with the people in the media basically destroying him at every turn, with the taint that would forever encompass his life and career , he would rather just sit down with a bottle of whiskey and drink long into the night. The moon laughed at him now, the sand practically sparkling as it did so. The familiar terrain was foreign. He paced around, knowing that his date with Kenny Mercury was set in stone. He could not walk out on the tournament without giving back the small round one salary that he had already spent. He wished he was back in college instead. He'd already gotten in contact with a local community college about admission and was accepted. He was perfectly happy with getting a degree in business and getting as far away from the cursed wrestling ring. He pulled up a chair, leaning into it. He closed his eyes, drifting into unconsciousness. The eyes opened. Kenny Mercury stood there, smirk plastered across his face. And in that moment, he realized what he needed to do. Pushing himself up as best as he could, he grabbed at the handle of his sword. The jewels that had been encrusted in the handle were long gone, but while the exterior appeal was barren the steel was still the same soulless shepherd it had always been. He sneered, his will to survive growing with every second. A growl escaped from his lips, letting the rage that had for so long been his fuel reassert itself. "I'm technically proficient with this…" Mercury trailed off, somewhat uncertain as to why this ragged man was willing to fight tooth and nail for his life. Obviously from looking at him he looked like he did not care for his own well being. But there he was, pointing his blade at Mercury, snarling like an animal. "Technically proficient…" he trailed off once again before doing a blind charge. SHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHN!! The blades met, but Mercury had a power advantage over the wounded opponent. He pushed him backward, forcing him to a knee. Mercury saw a chance, looking to meet him head on with a fatal deathblow. In a flash, the blow was blocked, followed quickly by a hard kick to the midsection. Mercury doubled over instantly, allowing his opponent to nail him with a hard punch across the face. Mercury flopped to the ground, falling face first into a mud puddle. "WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING?!" Mercury screamed, part shocked, part frantic. A wild downward chop followed, but Mercury simply rolled his way out. Kipping up and almost slipping on the slick ground, Mercury was taken off his feet once again by a meticulous strike from the blade of his opponent's sword. Strength and precision were on display. Mercury believed himself to be a technically affluent man with a sword, but never before had he faced someone who combined crisp strikes with pure aggression in such a fashion. Mercury put both his hands on the handle of his blade as his opponent sent down another strike. Quickly, Mercury flung his foot towards the genitals of his opponent, nailing him and causing him to fall to his knees. His supercilious nature would not allow him to lose, not even at his own game. However, his need to cheat further dishonored him and further impacted his image as a technical fighter. Maybe he wasn't the Personification of Perfection. Maybe he was just a mediocre fighter with a flair for the dramatic. In essence a six dressed up as a nine. The opposition continued to rain down strikes, trying to nail him someplace. Mercury tried to scurry his way out. However, in doing so, he allowed himself to be open. A definitive strike reigned upward in a diagonal slash that caused a scream of pain to pour out of Mercury's mouth. "AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! WHAT THE FUCK?!" But before Mercury knew it, the opposition was on top of him, swinging towards his head. Nearly hitting him proved to be a mistake, as Mercury came across and slashed downward across his chest and towards the stomach. A pained expression surfaced instantaneously, but a foot shot right into Mercury, pushing Kenny away from doing any other damage. Quickly, Mercury leapt, jumping over top of his opponent. Brandon instinctively turned quickly, catching Mercury in the center, running him through. The arrogance drained from him just like the skin tone color. Mercury's bottom lip quivered before he spit in the face of his killer. He growled and pulled downward before he cut clean. Mercury screamed as the blood flowed from him like a faucet. Almost instantly, he dropped. As his opposition lay mere feet away from him, screaming in a blood curdling fashion, the adrenaline high crashed and almost instantly he was mortal again. This time, he was hurt more than before. And there was absolutely no recourse to dull the pain that consumed him. However, he could look straight forward. Kenny Mercury had lived a cavalier life. Not only was able to mask his shortcomings with his incessant humor and arrogance, but his charisma had given him a name worth uttering. However, it was the fatal mistake of attacking a wounded animal that truly was dangerous that spelled his doom. A now nameless vagabond who had nothing to live for. A nameless vagabond, who unlike he, had a future. He could feel the salty air blowing against his back in that instant. He picked himself up, limping, licking the crimson off his blade before sheathing it. You truly could not take the animal out of the hunter. Brandon tried to rest but could not. Instead, he wishfully hoped that his heart would stop beating. But as the sun began to ascend over the horizon, something stood out. The sun seemed brighter. More beautiful. The sand dunes over the distance were the most alluring vision he had ever seen. He rose, his mouth opened partially. Was it realization? No. The world does not work in such storybook terms. It was a hint of optimism. Of his insides singing "We shall overcome". The sun, the desert which had its' seductive luster, it all struck a cord that caused some sort of inspiration. It did not matter if he was forgiven of his sins in that moment. What the writers said was of no consequence anymore. His hypocrisy eroded to a basic level of self trust for himself. The sand was still ice cold, but soon that would change. Brandon all but knew that. Hours upon hours spent in the desert heat had brought him through Karina Wolfenden, through Nova, through Dave Gibson, through Angelo Deville. All those battles were epics. The slate was wiped clean, at least in his own mind. Sure, the problems existed, but the abandonment of letting them rule his every thought was enough for now. Angelo Deville disappeared after he lost to Brandon Youngblood. And as the wounded warrior began sprinting towards the horizon, he wondered if Kenny Mercury would do the same. He stopped instantly. Arrogance was not a road he should be traveling down. He knew what Mercury was capable of. A UWF legend, someone who has technical ability and could be a ring general. He understood the importance of the match. The battle against Hoyt Williams proved one thing: take no one for granted. Kenny Mercury was surely having a good time. He was probably on a yacht, or sleeping with supermodels. Brandon’s faced that kind before. Hard work was the only way to go with those kinds of people. Neither man was a necessary favorite, especially with what Brandon had done the last month or so. And as the temperature rose, hard work opened itself to Brandon Youngblood as a means of salvation.
