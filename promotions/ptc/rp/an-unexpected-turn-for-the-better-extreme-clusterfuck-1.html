An Unexpected Turn For The Better (Extreme Clusterfuck #1)

DaveEclipse Network Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 188 Location: Brighton, England Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 3:35 pm Post subject: An Unexpected Turn For The Better (Extreme Clusterfuck #1) An Unexpected Turn For The Better Regrets The chilling breeze brushes over the dusty rubble of a once great city. Zagreb, once Croatia’s greatest city, now only a shell of it’s former self. And the shell has crumbled into a mess of rock. Zagreb is still feeling the effects of the Balkans war of the mid-to-late 90’s. There are still whole areas of Zagreb left just the way it was when it was destroyed in the war. Whole office blocks and building complexes left derelict. And this dusty battleground is now where my next job takes place. I have been handed the job from the Croatian government to seek out a small terrorist group in Croatia known as The Skarj (or The Snake). They are threatening to bomb an area of Zagreb in the next 48 hours and I have been given the task to take out the leader of this group as soon as possible. So here I lie, on a dusty, cold rock. In freezing cold conditions, just waiting for the one moment when this terrorist leader steps out of this building opposite me, so I can take him out. The building opposite me, a warehouse that has been converted on the inside into a terrorist camp, is my sole object of concentration for the next 48 hours. You see I am a mercenary. I don’t follow any particular army from any political group, I work on my own. I get paid a lot of money to eliminate certain large personalities from the world. And I’m very, very good at it. At least I used to be. I haven’t quite been myself since the day I joined Next Generation Wrestling last year. I must admit, I did enjoy myself. I got to perform in front of millions of people, and use my skills in hand-to-hand combat to defeat wrestler after wrestler and earn the respect of many people around the world. I was given a US Title shot. I defeated two very respectable opponents, Apocalypse and Cyrus Skar, in inter-promotional matches. I was considered to be a big star in the making. But then something went terribly wrong. I was losing money by wrestling in such a small promotion and I needed to find another way of earning. And just as I was ready to look for a job as a bouncer, or something similar, I got a phone call. That phone call changed my life. It was from a woman, a very powerful and rich woman, and she offered me a tidy sum if I could stop a hired assassin from eliminating her. So I did, I took her to a safe house, and when the assassin entered the house, I took her out. I thought that would be the end of it, I thought that my career could go on as it was before. But that wasn’t to be. Because of my actions to save this woman, the opposing group, which consisted of a number of terrorist organizations throughout Eastern Europe, added me to their hit list. I was considered more of a commodity for elimination than the rich woman. So my life had to change, forever. I fled the country. I found the first plane out of the country and fled. I realized at that moment, that the only way I could avoid all the chaos of before, is to go back to the life I led before. Go back to the life as a mercenary, the life living a lie. Back to the life pretending all my life to be someone that I’m not, an alias. So I took an alias and through the old media of people I knew before from the business, I got back into mercenary work. This is the first job I have taken since that day. And I am regretting every moment. I really miss the time I spent in wrestling. I really miss it. It was a small stint as a professional wrestler, but in that time I had, I truly felt alive. I felt that it was where I belonged. I had spent a good part of my life living a lie, pretending to be someone I’m not. And in wrestling I was being commended for being who I am. And doing what I want to do. I had the adoration of millions of fans across the globe for being Dave Eclipse, not Red Spider, or The Hornet, or anything like that. I had the adoration for being me, Dave Eclipse. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Lack of Concentration What was that? I swear I saw something move then. Damn it! I missed him! As my mind was lost in wrestling I lost that little bit of concentration and that’s all it takes. I have lost my target. Or did I? It could have been anything, a cat or something like that. I bring my concentration back to the task at hand. I point my rifle to the door of the building and wait for something to happen once again. As I do this a noise is heard behind me, a crackling noise. Footsteps, I’m sure of it. Wait… keep calm for a second. Slowly turn your head, don’t give him a reason to fire. I gradually turn my head around to see who it is behind me. Really calmly and slowly I turn my head and look behind me. It is nothing. I don’t believe it. I’m getting the creeps for no reason. My mind is playing tricks on me. “Stay still… don’t move… take your hands off of your rifle.” The voice is on the opposite side of me. The guy had seen that I was turning my head and circled me the opposite way. I take my hands off of the rifle and onto my head. Then I rise to my feet and back away from the person. He doesn’t look like one of the terrorists. He is wearing a thick grey coat with a woolen collar. He looks really out of sorts here. Not to mention the fact that the guy was speaking English! “So you’re The Hornet then, huh?” the guy says to me in a mocking tone. The guy has a smug grin on his face as he’s just captured me. “Yeah, and you are?” I reply. “You can call me Lieutenant White. I’m part of American Special Forces.” He says, folding his arms. He is looking more and more arrogant by the second, obviously very proud of being in American Special Forces. Actually it’s quite a relief. As much as the guy is annoying me already, he’s not part of the Skarj so I’m not captured. “I saw you there at the top of that building and knew that you’d do something to tamper with my mission, so I thought I’d come up and warn you off.” He says almost as if he expects me to accept his demand. “Warn me off? Mate, I’m not going anywhere.” I tell the guy in a stern voice. “Yeah, that’s right. You’re not going anywhere. You’re staying right here.” I can’t believe this guy. He thinks he can do this by himself? He thinks he’s going to storm into the complex and take out the whole terrorist group? I don’t think so. “Are you mental? You’ll be killed!” “Nope.” He says in a cocky voice, “Up and at them, boys!” As he says that the entire roof of the building gets up and stands to attention. A whole battalion of Special Forces were lying on the roof of this building and they all just stood up, in unison. How come I didn’t notice? “Relax, Hornet, these guys have been here for a while. You managed to walk over them and not even notice.” “What? You mean these guys have been here for over 10 hours without moving?” “Twelve and a half to be precise,” the Lieutenant replies, “it happens you’ve stepped right in the middle of an American Special Forces campaign, a top secret mission of course. The Skarj aren’t the small terrorist group that the Croat leader thinks they are. In fact they are a whole wing of Al-Quaeda.” “Al-Quaeda?” I ask, questioning the Lieutenant. “Yeah, well with the Middle East being bombarded by American troops in the past couple of years and set to continue in the future, the terrorist organization has expanded to Europe, and will use Eastern Europe as their base for the future. Only we have sources inside Al-Quaeda and we knew what was coming.” “So what are you going to do now?” I ask. “Well you may not believe this, but we knew you’d be here. There’s big demand for you back in the States apparently. I know who you are Dave.” “You know my name?!” “Yup, you’re needed in a big tournament back in the States. It’s taking the nation by storm. It’s called The New Era and they want you to compete in the Extreme tournament, being one of PTC’s top superstars. Now we know your situation, Hurst. And we know your reasons for going back into this. And because of it, you have government backing for the time that you compete in the tournament. You will be under the guard of two of the countries best bodyguards leading up to and up until the end of the event. If you follow Sergeant Black over there, he will take you to some transport to take you home.” I’m completely stunned. I can’t believe all of this has been arranged for me to compete in this tournament. It must mean a lot to the country. I follow the Sergeant. He is running to the back of the building. He stops and points out into the sky, a black flickering speck in the distance seems to get bigger and bigger, the breeze from earlier is building even more now, but it’s not from the wind, but from rotors. The black speck is now visibly seen as a helicopter, and it approaches the building and a ladder is dropped from the sky. I climb the ladder, but the Sergeant stays where he is. He is obviously a part of the mission. I climb the ladder and into the helicopter. There are two uniformed soldiers sitting there. They point me to my seat and I sit and belt up. I feel the helicopter’s full force as it turns and flies off away from the building. Looking ahead of me the two soldiers look very serious. “Don’t worry, Mr. Eclipse, you’ll be home soon enough.” One of the soldiers says, trying to reassure me. “I don’t have a home,” I tell the soldier. “Well you’ll be safe soon, sir.” The other soldier says correcting the first. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Return The plane has landed and I’m about to step out of the plane. As the doors open, I look and notice a large crowd has gathered around the aircraft. “What is this?” I ask one of the two soldiers. “Well… now… you’re a celebrity, Mr. Eclipse.” The soldier says with a beaming smile on his face. I’m completely confused by the whole thing. But step off of the plane and walk down the steps in front of the screaming crowd. I notice a small television crew have assembled at the bottom of the stairs and as I step off the last step a female reporter screams into the microphone to supercede the noise of the crowd. “This is Candy Chang reporting at LA airport. These are the scenes that are greeting the return of PTC wrestler Dave Eclipse. This man was captured by a terrorist group in Croatia and has been saved thanks to American Special Forces. We believe Dave has been tortured to give away details of American plans without having any knowledge of them.” Hang on! That’s not true! I go to speak out, but one of the soldiers grabs me by the arm. “Just smile and wave. Don’t make a scene.” The soldier says. Don’t make a scene?! The American Special Forces are getting credit for ‘saving’ me when all they did was get in my way of completing my mission! This is insane, all in the name of propaganda. This is despicable. “This situation is obviously horrible for a human being to go through, and our hearts go out to Dave Eclipse and his family for having to go through this, without ever knowing if he would be able to get out of this alive.” Actually, ‘hearts go out’? This doesn’t seem so bad! I pass the news reporter and wade through the cheering crowd and towards a limousine that is parked nearby. The door is open and I climb in, followed by the two soldiers from before. I climb in and sit. Looking back I notice the two soldiers. “What are you doing?” I ask them. “What do you mean?” They both reply. “Well, I won’t need you two anymore…” “No, I don’t think you understand.” One of them replies, “We are your bodyguards.” What Bodyguards? Oh yes! They are the two bodyguards that are supposed to stop any assassination attempts. “Well… great! I reply. What do we do now?” I ask. “Whatever you want, you get to live as you wish. We will look over you and protect you.” The other bodyguard replies. Well, well, well. Finally, something is going right for me in my life. This is a great change for the better. My dreams are fulfilled and I can live the life the way I want. I get my life as a wrestler back. I can perform in front of millions of fans, I will get gratitude for being who I am, and not for being someone I’m not. I’m fed up of living a lie. I’m fed up of running my whole life and moving from one life-threatening job to another. I want to settle down, I want to have a secure job that pays well and doesn’t have me wondering if I will wake up the next day at all. This is exactly what I’ve always wanted, and it’s handed to me on a plate. All of this in exchange for being propaganda for American Special Forces. Not bad really… _________________ "Winning isn't everything, but losing is nothing." - Dave Eclipse
