Am I The Only Sane One Left? Since the last time I left you I learned that soup is better served hot then warm. You can’t get hot soup anymore because everybody is worried about being sued unless you make it yourself which you only seem to do when you are sick, which ruins the whole damn concept. In this day and age you can’t seem to get anything good anymore. Even the vampires are lame. I watch as the knights of red in their time of distress ride to the center of the battle field fighting not just for dignity but also Johnny Smith’s seventh birthday. Perhaps one days little Johnny Smith will grow up and take the throne of the free world unifying both army’s and creating peace and great prosperity. Today however Johnny doesn’t wish for world peace, but rather an X-box 360. You wouldn’t blame him if you saw the graphics. To the left ride in the army of evil dressed in appropriate black fighting in honor of the throne of Empire. Why do the bad guys always wear black when its one of the least offensive colors? If you really wanted to show evil I think something in teal or a violet would be more vulgar. Oh well fashion was never my faupaw nor was knights and horses. I was born 24 cycles ago in the season of Reagan during a war time that was to say the least cold. The world was on the verge of a great new area as the prince of pop released a great Thrill upon us. He would never rise to the status of king, as he never seemed to have a nose for dominance. Now here I am 24 menstrual cycles later watching this epic battle take place in the name of young Johnny Smith. I watch as the warrior’s line up like particles of cocaine straightened out into a line by the shape edge of a razor. Neither side flinches as they just stare a frightful empty stare into each others eyes. The calm before a battle is almost serene as the entire world stops to focus on what is about to bring death and carnage to the land. Its funny that the blood and death will fertilize and renew life, as something will grow in place of those lost. It’s an amazing cycle that nobody can understand like the permanent press on the washing machine. The two army’s raise their swords as the leader of each army calls for the troops to charge. It feels like slow motion as the two sides head toward each other sure to create a chorus of destruction that will delight the spectators and solve the conflict. I know the outcome of this battle will have little effect on my situation and the world. To be honest I think little Johnny Smith is a brat and I hope his army fails. Yet I also know the good guys always win in the end unless you’re talking about the music business or Hollywood. Then it’s generally the Jews, and the prettiest face that wins. I watched the red princess as she watched on from her sitting booth with worry. He face was that of an angel and her body that of a porn star. Isn’t funny if you make one porn movie you’re a porn star yet you could make a ton of mainstream movies and never achieve the title of movie star. I guess porn actor or actress is the better route to stardom. He boobs hug her medieval dress like a mother hugs her newborn child. She looks elegant despite the war that has commenced in front of her. The fret on the warrior’s faces on the battle field is intense. If it wasn’t for my own power as a second coming, I would worry that this mêlée can spill over to where I was watching. But given who I am and the power I hold no such worry really inflicts me. Its been a rough week and to be honest death and agony and unrelenting malevolence or however the hell Ryan put it would be a vacation compared to having to watch anymore stories of medieval battles, vampires, or what other boring dribble Alcaeus can produce. Are there really enough nerds out there that soak that vampire mumbo jumbo up? There is magic inside of birthday cake that makes it taste better. It’s a secrete God made, sort of his gift to the birthday person. Mother Mary: So did you get that thing done? If I tried the booking agency would bitch and moan about how I’m not respecting the system and then the chairman would step in and bitch some himself. So no, besides his death is a long ways off. Mother Mary: Well drop me off first I have some things I need to get done around the house. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Sitting at the type writer is a sad Duke Williams who types away in his autobiography about the death of his loved cat Guthrie.) His last days were peaceful as he didn’t see his impending doom coming. I remember the day of the events perfectly like they just happened yesterday. I woke up early and heard a song playing in the distance. I got up to find where it was coming from but it ceased before I could find the origin of the melodic tone. Frustrated I found my cleanest dirty shirt and went into the kitchen to have a swig of whiskey and make breakfast. Guthrie was just waking up stretching as he walked toward the table where he would always sit on my newspaper just as I was about to read. His strut was that of a cat who knew he had the world by its neutered testacies. He looked at me with a cynical glaze telling me both that he loved me, but would eat me if I had wings and was smaller then him. As the song lyrics go, “love hurts”. With a mighty leap he made his way to the kitchen table in order to get a better view of what was happening. His little ears rotated like NASA satellites searching for birds, mice, or horny ally cats. His eyes watched as I made a bowl of Captain Crunch cereal. I will never forget the excitement on his face as he watched that cream white milk splash over the grains of sugared wheat. To him this was Shakespeare, Elvis, or Jenna Jameson. He licked his lips telling me that he was interested in having some breakfast of his own. I was irked by his cocky demands but Guthrie much like his name sake of Woody, not Arlo, had a bit of a folksy charm to him that you just couldn’t help but to love. I finished about half of the diabetic delight and passed it off to Guthrie. His little pink tongue darted in and out of the milk like a sewing machine fixing up a hole in somebody’s pants. In many ways Guthrie was like my personal sewing machine, sewing up the hole in my soul. I had been through alcoholism, nine divorces, rehab, world titles, and age. He was the only consist in my life for the past ten years. I loved that cat like a brother he was my rock and I was the guy who fed him. He and I saw the world the same way. Neither of us trusted anybody especially people in suits. Both were annoyed by dogs and hated rats. WE liked to eat and watch TV and when all else failed we enjoyed licking our own balls. Well for me I just settled for masturbation as my tongue was never long enough. Guthrie finished his cereal and turned his head with out looking at me jumping down to meet the day. He didn’t watch me but I watched him as he headed into his litter box and took his morning dump. I often admired the cleanliness of cats as they are always bathing and keeping things in order. I think that’s why cats hate dogs. Cats shit with dignity for themselves and respect of the world around them. Where as a dog just plops down and shits where ever he wants with no regard to those watching him or the mess he is making on property that isn’t theirs. Guthrie covered up his shit with three shovels of gravel. He sang taps in his head and walked away. Cat’s often do things in threes, they are very neurotic like that and Guthrie was no different. I watched as Guthrie jumped up on the window of the trailer and pushed his way through a small hole to go outside and meet the day. With Guthrie gone I cleaned up our bowl washing it while I watched three bats circling above. I pondered to myself if maybe one of them was Alcaeus, or any vampire for that matter. If one of the bat’s was a female bat and a vampire and I made love with it, would that constitute bestiality? The things that make you go hummm. I dried the bowl as I enjoyed another sip of that sweet Tennessee gold. My day was going to be hectic as I was supposed to go to school to teach a class on high economics in the western world. It was a new job I just picked up for some extra drinking scratch and I was enjoying it. Then in a distance I heard the most obscene and horrific sound a cat owner can hear. I heard a loud screech from Guthrie followed by the screaming of a halting car. I know what had happened and clutched my whiskey bottle as I went racing for the door. I remember the warmth of the day hitting my skin reminding me I’m alive. But the site made me wish I wasn’t as lying in the middle of the street was my poor cat Guthrie laying dying. I held the whiskey bottle in my left hand as I watched my other best friend laid out motionless. Then I heard that damn music again leaving me with an eerie feeling. It was indeed the same song that woke me up not more then twenty minutes ago. I looked around and I finally placed the sound, and the source. It was the ice-cream truck which ran over Guthrie. I ran up to him to see if I could save him despite the cool draft I was receiving from the ice-cream truck. The driver apologized up and down but I wasn’t listening. I pet Guthrie on his head and scratched in his favorite spot behind his ear. His eyes opened just a bit to let me know he was still alive. He seemed to tell me that it was ok with the look he gave me. He was going to a better place and I knew it, and so did he. His strength in those final moments I’ll never forget and will take to my own grave. He winked twice with his left eye which was his way of saying that he loved me. I bent down and kissed his forehead just as I noticed the twinkle leave from his green eyes. He was gone and no longer would anybody be sewing up that hole in my soul. I found his favorite spot in the house which was the dyer; I tore it out of the wall. I dug a hole in the ground deep enough to burry the dryer so that he would lie eternally on his favorite warm spot. I wrapped him up in his favorite sweatshirt of mine, and placed it on top of a newspaper so he could forever keep the angels from getting the day’s top headlines. I remember crying with every single shovel of dirt I placed on his lifeless little body. They say a dog is man’s best friend and I agree with that. Dogs automatically bond with their masters but a cat is free of enslavement. He doesn’t allow you to be his master and even though you feed them everyday they still manage to kill a bird now and then and place it on your bed to show you they still can do their fair share and don’t’ really need you. That and to tell you that they love you. No longer will I find the surprise of a bird in my bed or the sarcastic yawn from Guthrie while I tell a story. Even though he’s gone I know I wasn’t given his love in the end. I earned it and that means so much more to me then anything else in this world. I know there is a hillbilly heaven since Hoyt told me so and I hope to see it one day. But I also believe there is a cat heaven with eternal cans of tuna being opened and plenty of papers for the cat to lay on just as somebody wants to read them. If Guthrie were here with me right now I know he would be lying on the keyboard preventing me from typing. I miss you old soul and thank you for being my seamstress.
