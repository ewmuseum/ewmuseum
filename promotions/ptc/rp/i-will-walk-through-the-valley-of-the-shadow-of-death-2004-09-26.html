I will walk through the valley of the shadow of death...

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 (Scene opens as Crigger waltzes into another bar alongside Fullov Shito. Shito becomes distracted by the bright lights coming off the arcade games. Crigger snaps his fingers, Shito pulls out a stool, and the two men sit down.) Crigger: Fullov, the beauty of the whole thing is this. When I say I’m going to do something, I do it. If the people out there don’t believe me, they can ask MacPhereson. I’m on an amazing streak, dude, and it’s not going to end here. Week by week, and little by little, the competitors in this thing will dwindle down. I plan on being in it until the end, and my next stepping-stone is…line. (Mr. Shito [Fullov], reaches deep into this pocket and pulls out a piece of paper. He flattens the paper out on the table trying to smooth out the wrinkles. He then hands it to Crigger.) Crigger: Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis? (Fullov shrugs his shoulders, digs in his pocket, and pulls out two mints. He offers Crigger one.) Crigger: I’ll pass, thanks. Anyway, whatever your name is… (Crigger reads the piece of paper again, and scratches his chin.) Crigger: I’ve about half-assed listened to what this guy has to say, and he’s out there, Shito. I’ve wrestled some freaks before, and you know what? I’ve put them all down courtesy of my Stupor Kick. Jackon, you’re not going to rattle me, but I’m going to rattle you. I’m going to rattle your jaw with my size twelve wrestling boot. You’re going to fall, and it’s going to hurt. You better get your priorities in check, because your time is about up. I’m obviously not of big concern to you, and, hey, that’s cool. I left it all behind me in 1999, and after a four year absence, I came back, and some things had never changed. I was still winning. Quickly I was wearing some gold. I walked into the NGW, and you know what I did? I took an undefeated man with wins in the double digits, and I put a little blemish on that record of his. Nobody else did it! (Three shots are lined up in front of Crigger. Crigger glares at the bartender as if to say, "This is it?" and then looks at the shot individually. He holds the first up to his nose.) Crigger: Here...I smell your fear, Jackson. Don't be afraid. Many, many great men have fallen to me. (Crigger takes the shot, and then smells the next.) Crigger: This shot represents your weaknesses. This is what they call a Fuzzy Navel, or a pussy drink. (Crigger drinks it, smiles all fruity like, and then smells the third.) Crigger: Oh, now that's just fuckin' nasty. Shito, this one's yours. (Shito graciously slurps down the shot and then loudly burps. Crigger laughs.) Crigger: Oh, that’s classic! Ha ha ha. Jackson, I hope just because you’ve made it through the first round, that it’s going to be a cakewalk the rest of the way. I’m going to give you some advice, and I want you to think long and hard about it. Keep your friends close, and your enemies closer. You haven’t done that. In the second round of this tournament, I will be your maker. Is it because I’m more talented? Yes. Is it because I’m an all-rounder and a star? Yes. I mean, you’re great in your own league. You’re probably the big fish in the small pond type, and that’s fantastic. The NGW as I told my first opponent is the big league. You’re the small time, baby. (An hour and many shots later, Crigger notices a man beside him. For the benefit of those at home, all the slurring has been edited and translated.) Man: What in God’s name are you rambling about? You kickin’ Stonewall Jackson’s ass? Well, good. I hate that son of a bitch. Crigger: Who the hell are you? And Stonewall Jackson’s dead! Man: They call me Mr. T. (Crigger slaps Shito on the shoulder and laughs.) Crigger: You ain’t Mr. T. Where’s the mowhawk and gold chains and shit? Mr. T: No, not that Mr. T. I’m Mr. T! Crigger: You can be Mr. Fucking Belvedere, god of sitcom television for all I care. I ain’t impressed. Mr. T: What you drinkin’? Crigger: I drink everything, ‘cept for whatever the hell it was this tubby bitch drank earlier. (Shito pats his belly and grins.) Mr. T: Let’s talk strategy. Crigger: What? Mr. T: You need a strategy to go in and fight Stonewall. Now, here’s where ya start. Crigger: You’re crazy, man. I ain’t fightin’ Stonewall. I’m fighting…line. (Shito reaches deep in his pocket, pulls out the piece of paper, and this time lays down a picture, too.) Crigger: Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis. (Mr. T. picks up the picture and starts tapping it with his finger.) Mr. T: I know that guy. He’s a fruitcake. Crigger: Even nuttier than you? Mr. T: I seen him damn near kill a few people in the ring. He ain’t right, whatever your name is. Crigger: The name’s Oliver Klossoff. Pleasure to meet you. (Mr. T sticks out his hand. Crigger shakes it.) Mr. T: Ah. A foreigner. You been in the country long? Crigger: Jah, tis nice. Mr. T: This is what you do to the sick son of a bitch. You strut and you jive, and then ya… (Mr. T. falls over and passes out.) Crigger: I fear no man. I will walk through the valley of the shadow of death and face my destiny. I’ll emerge, you won’t, Jackson. It only takes three seconds to change a lifetime. (Scene closes as Crigger leaves his tab. He steps over Mr. T. on the floor, and Shito follows him out of the bar. Shito stops a cab, opens the door for Crigger, gets in himself, and they speed away headed to round 2.)
