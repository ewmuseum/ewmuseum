A star is born... maybe.

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 It’s early, very early in the morning. The first fingers of dawn’s light creep into the kitchen of Goat Mansion, just enough to illuminate a pale figure sitting at the scrubbed pine table. Meet Craig Goat. He yawns expansively, shakes out two tablets from a pill box on the table and pops them in his mouth. He lifts his coffee cup to his lips to wash down the bitter tasting pills, but hesitates as it reaches his lips. Not for the first time, he finds himself wondering if he really needs them any more. After all, it has been almost a full year since he last had a panic attack. Is this because the medication he takes so religiously actually works, or can it be attributed to the turns he has taken in his life since then? With an impulsiveness that is not usual for Craig, he spits out the pills and tosses the box in the garbage. He smiles. Craig: I’ve thrown away my crutch, let’s see if I can walk. His happy moment is interrupted by the sounds of an argument from upstairs. Ah, Nurse Wretched and Trixie are awake and at each others throats, again. He grabs his coffee and quickly slips out the door into the garden, eager to avoid referee duties. Concealed behind some dense shrubbery, Craig relaxes and slips into his own thoughts. Has it really been only a year since his life changed so dramatically? It must be. The trees at Shady Pines Sanatorium were just beginning to shed their autumn leaves then, as they are now in the boughs above him. He remembers vividly the moment it began, this metamorphosis. He was sitting curled up in the corner of the therapy room, nervously tapping his fingers in a tattoo against his knees, ever so slightly rocking backwards and forwards when suddenly SHE walked into his life. Nurse Esme Matilda Wretched, a beautiful woman with an air of arrogance and cockiness about her, even though she too was a patient. Craig, a naturally quiet guy anyways, suddenly found himself totally tongue-tied with lust and adoration. This was the moment his life changed irreversibly. Despite the fact that Nurse was clearly insane and claimed to have slaughtered her own mother by ripping out her one remaining eye just the day before, Craig would do everything possible to win this woman’s affections. He knew this would be an insanely impossible task. She was beautiful, he was pasty and thin and lanky. She was a champion wrestler, he was an e-fedding wrestling fan. She was cocky and filled with self importance, he was timid, shy and unsure. She was strong, both physically and mentally, he was an agoraphobic who cried when he stubbed a toe. They were poles apart, and yet he sensed that they would one day be closer to each other than anyone else would ever get. And so began a most twisted wooing. Craig followed her everywhere in the sanatorium and once she discharged herself, he started stalking her. He watched her every movement in an attempt to get to know her better. Like him, Nurse was a solitary figure. She spent her time alone living in a musty locker room in the bowels of the XUDF Arena. He learnt that she had worked as a psychiatric nurse but lost every job she’d ever had due to allegations of serious abuse and neglect of the vulnerable and insane patients in her charge. He discovered rumours of suicides that might possibly have been murders and her tendency to turn medical equipment in to rudimentary implements of torture. Craig knew he should have turned and run as fast and far away from this twisted evil creature… but couldn’t. She intrigued him. He saw, or perhaps imagined he saw, brief flashes of kindness and humanity in her eyes and vowed he would be the one to save her from herself, and maybe in return, she would save him. Craig soon became Nurses Wretcheds apprentice, meal ticket, lacky and eventually friend. She took over his house, alienated all his friends, mocked him, beat him and locked his parents up in the most notorious insane asylum in the country. But he was happy. So happy he unsuccessfully tried to stab her death, faked his own murder and ran away vowing never to see her again. But that was last month. Now he has a new dream and a new girlfriend, Trixie, with whom he plans to run a truck stop (as that was where they first met). He has given up on wrestling, but not on Nurse Esme Matilda Wretched. A scream of pain echoing across the garden shakes Craig from his reverie. His quiet time for the day is officially over. Once blood is drawn, he can no longer ignore the fighting. He makes his way over to the pool where Nurse Wretched and his girlfriend Trixie are rolling on the ground screaming like fishwives and ripping each others hair out by the root. Craig: STOP IT! Both of you! Nurse: She started it. Trixie: I sooo did not! Esme just attacked me for absolutely no reason. Nurse: You used my goddamn shampoo you cheap whore! Trixie: Whore? You’re the one who shags anything in a Luchadore mask. Nurse catches Trixie with a flying elbow, slams her into the pool and holds her head under the water. Nurse: You want to wash your hair? Let me do it for you, bit*h. Craig: Ladies, stop right now or it’s no coffee for you Nurse and no sex for you Trixie… and I know those are both things you can’t live without. The women crawl out of the pool, their now transparent clothing slick against their bodies. A Kodak moment if ever there was one. Nurse smacks Craig across the ear as she stalks back into the house. Nurse: Don’t get so cocky about that Craig, your coffee is instant and judging by the sounds coming from your room last night, so is your sex life. Trixie: When are you going to get her out of your house? I can’t stand her. She only keeps you around for your money. You said you didn’t want to be a wrestler anymore, so why is she still here? We should be working towards our dream honey, a diner won’t build itself! Craig: Pumpkin, I can’t kick her out, she needs me. You saw what happened when I wasn’t here for her. She won’t be able to cope without a strong presence like me in her life. Trixie: You’re sweet, but you definitely aren’t strong! You weigh what? 120 pounds? Craig: I meant strong mentally! Trixie: But you’re still on medication… and you cried at that advert on TV last night. Craig: It was an emotionally moving advert! A strong man is not afraid to show his feelings. Trixie: Whatever. I love you no matter how weak and feeble you are. I just wish you’d have the courage to break free from that evil cow. Craig: I am not weak! I am strong, I am invincible and I could have been the second best wrestler in this country if I hadn’t met you and retired before I even had my first match! Trixie swings around to face Craig, her eyes beginning to water and her bottom lip quivering. In a small hurt voice she asks Craig the question that will make or break them. Trixie: Does that mean you wish you’d never met me? Would you rather I wasn’t here so you could go on to conquer the wrestling world and wallow in the bits of affection that Esme throws you? Craig: Don’t be silly! I love you and I swear I will never set foot in a wrestling ring if that’s what you want. Trixie: Promise? Craig: On my honour. The couple enter the house to find Nurse reading a letter with a look of pure glee on her face. Trixie spits in her direction and disappears upstairs. Craig: Why so happy? Nurse: I have some good news! Ever heard of GTT4? No? It’s an open tournament featuring some of the best wrestlers around. I just received an acceptance letter. This is going to be a fabulous opportunity to win, win, win! Craig: That sounds great, who’s your opponent? Nurse: Jonathan Borealis, some lame rookie from Buffalo. But he’s not my opponent… Surprise! You’re going to have your first real wrestling match in a matter of days. All this training I’ve been doing with you over the last year is about to pay off big time. Craig: Me? I can’t do this! Nurse: Sure you can, I believe in you Craig. I realised while you were away shacking up with your cheap slut that I’ve done wrong by you. You’ve been a good friend, my only friend, and I promised I’d make you the second best wrestler around… and goddamn it, that’s what I’m going to do! Craig: No, I really can’t do this! Trixie would kill me, and so would my opponent, no matter how lame you think he is. Nurse softens her voice and leans closer to Craig, so close he can feel the sweet warmth of her breath move the tiny hairs on his neck. Nurse: What happened to that cocky swagger you had this morning? Where has this fear come from? You know you’re better than that! I believe in you. Craig: Cocky? I’m not cocky… unless you’re talking about sex and then I’m definitely the king of cock. Nurse: Christ! Pass me the bleach, I need to wash my ears out. Don’t ever talk about your sex life with to me! Eurgh, I may just vomit. Please, can we discuss this like adults? Craig: Ok, even if Trixie hadn’t forbidden me from wrestling, I still wouldn’t do this tournament. I know I’m not a wrestler. I’ve never been a proper apprentice to you, more of an assistant. Don’t make me do this Esme, please? Nurse: Before I ever met you, you knew more about wrestling than me! Remember? I got boxing and wrestling confused when I first started in this game. I won my first match without learning a single legal move and I still only know about a handful. How often do I have to tell you that wrestling is about attitude, and you’ve got that in spades. Craig: Yeah, but I’m still not fit enough! You never give me a chance to actually work out. Nurse: Work out? You work out all day! I’ve run you like a slave for the past year, sorry for that by the way, so you may be lean, but your as fit as any wrestler out there. If you can lift a crate of my shoes, then you definitely have the strength to pick someone up and slam them with a suplex. Craig: I still think I’m not smart enough or quick enough. Nurse: You wrote about wrestling in one of those geeky e-feds for years. You know all the moves, you have the instinct. You’ve been able to dodge most of the blows I’ve tried to hit you with when I’m a bit pre-menstrual, so yeah, I’d say you’re quick enough. Craig: Even if all that stuff is true, it still doesn’t change the fact that I promised Trixie I’d forget about wrestling. She’ll never let me do this. Nurse: If she loves you like she says she does, then she’ll let you try out your dream. Would she be happy to see you full of regrets for the rest of your life? This will be the ultimate proof of both your abilities and her love for you. He takes a few moments to contemplate all this and a slow smile starts to spread across his face. Craig: This may be the lack of medication talking, but … I have no proper training. I have no actual experience in the ring. I weigh 120lbs and have never used a gym. I haven’t a clue what my opponent Jonathan Borealis is like. I’ve never even been in a fight before… but I just know I can win this whole damn tournament! But, um… Esme? Can you make sure Trixie doesn’t hurt me when I tell her? Fade out.
