Hype de Magic!

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 You know there’s a funny thing about Magic, you know what that is, Its unpredictable just like me, the funny thing about Fire its unpredictable, but the even funnier thing about Fire is it can be tamed, Starve it of what it needs and it dies, drench it, again it dies. Just like the little Ember even though it can burst back to life, if you stand on it guess what it dies. Can you quench magic like you can fire not a chance, Can Ember quench me, not a chance. I’m bored of talking about Ember let’s talk about something interesting like I don’t know me. Well if you didn’t already know I’m a Magician, not just any ordinary run of the mill illusionist. No, no, no I know real magic, what I do is real not fabricated, no mirrors, no lights, real pure magic. Yeah I know I here you ‘Magic, don’t make me laugh.’ Now you mistake me here I ain’t here to make you laugh, I’m here to make you stand up and take notice. Its simple really magic is all around us; you just need to know who to use it. Now you maybe asking why is the Magnificent one in wrestling? Well that’s easy little man cause I started out in wrestling some six years ago in my native Britain. Came to America joined Primetime Wrestling Academy. Watched people like Red Rock and Paddy O’Shea reach the top then throw it away, is that hunger to be the best, is that the desire we all have to win. Na I don’t think so. Well there this guy in my bracket who everyone out there thinks will walk this bracket yeah Seymour Almasy is his name and you know what I seen more talent on Sparrow, but hey what can you do people are fickle and believe the hype. Not me not by a long shot. You see the thing is I’m better than you all and no it isn’t because I know magic its because I believe it, I believe my hype. I believe in myself, some of you get your kicks in being the one everyone is talking about good for you, just remember I’m behind you watching, waiting, then you will no who I am.
