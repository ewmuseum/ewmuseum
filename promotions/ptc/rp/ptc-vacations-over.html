PTC — Roleplay

Vacation's over

1 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme? What is Extreme? Is it Kraze? Tony Vigilante? Ice? Shadow? Jason Marah? Jason Marah comes close. He comes really close. Why? Because he’s Irish. Everyone knows that the Irish are the toughest sons of bitches on the planet. But Marah is missing on a few things. Such as being the Hardcore Icon. Such as being the “Irish Bastard”. Such as being Sean Michael McClancy. So far Sean has come up just short in gaining his ultimate prize. He sees his higher then his 3 Superior Title wins. He sees this above and beyond his victory over the former Hardcore Icon many years ago, Ace Thunder of the GIWF/UWF infamy. No, this is his biggest achievement he can do. Why? Because he’s been lacking the extreme and hardcore part of his life for some time. Once you have gained a world title level, some think that you’ll never go back to your roots. That you’ll stay in the technical side of things. But sometimes you have to take a step back to your past, and prove yourself the most insane and hardcore bastard in a group. And this is not at a federation level, this is at a level for a group of federations. This is beyond what they have to offer even in the federation he currently resides in, the federation that is owned by his own twin sister and former partner run, 4 Winds Wrestling. While he’s on their roster and payroll, he’s spent the last month since Maelstrom II: Eruption taking a little vacation. His fiancé, Patti Anderson has continued on her path to challenge for the North Wind Title again, while he’s taken some time to do some internal reflection on himself. Just a bit over a month ago, Sean McClancy was dodging a woman who thought she would be his bride, whom he didn’t want. And in the end, his woman had won and he could rest easy. It had been a long time since he had a vacation. But with Patti being needed, he was able to just take some time off and enjoy what he did. We now find him leaning against a ring, a sturdy ring in some back area, actually it’s in one of the basements of the Order’s compound in the Dallas, Texas area. A place that at one time Sean was the Headmaster of, in charge of training the students that would wave the banner of the 4 Winds Academy and the Order of the Hammer in the future. As we find him though, it doesn’t look much like Sean McClancy there. He’s got his fiery red hair still, but it’s not combed or anything. His face is covered with an almost full red beard and mustache combination, from sideburns to chin and all in between. It’s scraggly, and he’s just looking like he’s not worried about shaving during his time off from straight on competition. In fact, he doesn’t look much like Sean McClancy. He’s standing next to the ring here, a pair of tight sweats on, and a towel hung around his shoulders. He appears to be stretching out. A man who looks like a slightly older and more clean shaven version of the Sean McClancy you’re all used to, is standing next to him. Man: Ye know, this would work more if ye were in th’ ring, an’ na’ ‘angin’ ou’ on the side o’ it. He father had a thicker accent then he did. But then again, Sean has toured the world for a few years. But his father was from a small area of Dublin called Dolphin’s Barn. The same place that Sean, his sister and two younger brothers had been born and raised in. Sean: Leave me alone, pop. We went thru this when I ga’ ‘ere. I’m on vacation. Man: Look, yer lookin’ a’ movin’ on an’ getting’ back int’ the competition thing. Ye were afraid o’ actually tryin’ in the 4WW due t’ bein’ related t’ the bosses. Even though Fey would never show ye anthin’ tha’ would look too much favorable. Sean: Yeah, Jaymes contacted me an’ made an offer. Bu’ me mind isn’na made up yet. So, ye really be wantin’ t’ see wha’ I ga’ in there? Man (Well Duncan, his father): Yeah, get yer lazy arse in th’ ring. Ye may be ‘alf me age, bu’ I’ll take ye. Ye’ve ‘ad a bug up yer knickers since I ga’ ‘ere in town, an’ I aim t’ work it ou’. Sean turns around to say something to his father, face to face, but he ends up standing there slack-jawed speechless for the first time in his life…. well second time in his life. Walking into the little gym are two people that he never thought he’d see here. The owners of the 4WW, Geoffrey Ian Childress III and his wife Maeb Fey McClancy-Childress. His best frind, and his sister and fore manager. Now also his boss to a point, when she needs to be. A strange mix you could say. He didn’t know why she was here: business or family? Although he notices that her husband is wearing his usual outdoor combination. Some rock bands t-shirt, jeans and his white snakeskin boots, with his piercings and such in his face and other parts. His sister though, is in her leather wrestling gear that she wears on the few occasions where she gets into the ring, such as just a few months ago for the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. She walks up to him, and hugs him. He’s still a bit shocked, and it takes him a moment to react and return it. Fey: Hi baby brother. It looks like ye need a shave, and maybe a comb. Then again, a pair o’ scissors through tha’ mop would ‘elp out a bit too. She sniffs the air and pulls away from him, crinkling her nose … Fey: and maybe a shower would be in order to. Sean tries to play it tough. If they’re here then they’ve got something for him. Sean: Why are ye two ‘ere? Don’cha ‘ave a Fed to be runnin’? Besides I am on leave until further notice. Remember, I’m on vacation. Ian takes the lead to inform him. Ian: Yea we heard you’ve been slacking off. As of late, Seems to me you need to have a fire lit under your ass again. And seeing as how the PTC just announced that the Extreme Title is having another Contender’s match. Sean: Ah, I don’na fokin’ need tha’. Fathom ga’ it last time, an’ I should’ave been th’ winner. Ian: Can’t deny that. And seeing as how Fathom just lost the belt to one Mike Marvelous… Sean: Wait? Did ye say Fathom lost th’ belt t’ Mike Marvelous? As in prancin’ aroun’ the 4WW, drinkin’ Hot Cocoa Mike Marvelous? Fey: Yer damn straight. Mike is walkin’ aroun’ talkin’ abou’ ‘ow ‘e’s the most extreme of the PTC now. Sean: Fok tha’. Ian: Yup. And Penny sent me a fax saying that they’re booking a 17 man rumble rules match. Extreme rules of course. Those who have been in the division are getting top spots. You’re the last man to enter. Sean: So yer sayin’ tha’ I’m on me way t’ facin’ Mike Marvelous fer the PTC Extreme Title? Fey: Ye could be sayin’ tha. Bu’ there are 16 others in this match. A lot more from PTC then ‘ave been in other events ye’ve taken part in. The likes o’ Kraze, Tony Vigilante, Shadow, na’ t’ mention two ye ‘ave faced comin’ in riogh’ befer ye. Ice an’ Noah Hanson. Sean: Like I’m really worried abou’ tha’. Fey: Well, I’m ‘ere t’ ‘elp ye get ready fer this. Seein’ as ‘ow I can’na be sittin’ on me own arse behind a desk fer too long. Sean: Sittin’? Ye gah’ ‘im t’ chase ye around it a few laps a nigh’ riogh? If na’, then ‘e needs t’ get ‘is priorities straight. Fey: ‘ow nice, tryin’ t’ get me take ou’ of action fer a while? Sean: Yeah? Fey: Well if yer na’ ready, I’m goin’ t’ get limber. Who ye ga’ ‘ere t’day? Sean: Jus’ a few of the newbies from the’ junior classes. A few o’ them are jus’ ou’ of indoctrination. Fey starts to climb into the ring the guys that are in there start to laugh. One of the ‘kids’ really takes the opportunity to rile her up. Kid: Oh come on you got to be kidding me. I’ll crush her. The kid who looks to be maybe seventeen with dark brown hair and what seems to be a heavy Texan accent. He seems to be full of himself and leans against the ropes facing Sean as Fey climbs in. The kid stares at Ian and hawks a wad of spit that just misses Ian’s Albino rattlesnake skin boots. Ian takes a step forward but Sean stops him towards Fey. Fey: Ye know Sean, if ye ‘ad been up t’ par on yer teaching, ‘e would ‘ave better manners. And he would know whom he is talking shite to. Kinda reminds me o’ the others ye’ll be facin’ in tha’ Extreme Rumble. Sean grins and nods his head to the kid. Fey takes her stance and gets ready to take on this rookie. Kid: You know Miss. I don’t want to hurt you. Maybe you should go and get back to the kitchen where you belong. And bring that Freak in here. I’ll take him on any day Sean: Boyo, do ye know who tha’ freak is? Kid: No. Sean: Yer foked. Kid: What? Ian: Ahem, are you a student of the 4 Winds Academy? Kid: Yes. Ian: Which one of the regular academies are you a graduate of? Kid: West Wind, up in Chicago. It’s an old… Ian: I know where it is. I bought it a few years ago. It’s an old training place I used when the GIWF was out of there. Kid: What? Ian: Allow me to introduce myself, my name is Ian Childress. I’m the Keeper of Secrets of the Order and the Patron of the KnightMare Brigade. The kids face goes flush, and he looks to Fey standing in the ring. Sean: Tha’s riogh there. ‘E’s yer boss. An’ tha’ s ‘is wife an me sister, th’ Mistress o’ Secrets. She’s almost as tough as me. I’ll let ‘er take ye ou’. In the meantime, while she does tha, I need t’ take me brother in law an’ talk some other stuff with ‘im really quick. Go a’ead Maeb, get a warm up with ‘im. We’ll be back soon. Fey nods to him, and then she motions the rookie on. Sean reaches up and grabs Ian by the shoulder. Ian nods as they both walk away. As soon as they’re a good distance away, Sean begins to talk. Sean: So why am I doin’ this? Ian: Because you need the chance. I know about Jaymes’ offer. I’ve been in talks with him myself. I’ve actually invested a little money into his little endeavor. You have heard though, right? About the PWW? Sean: No, what? Ian: Blaise is already signed. Sean: Blaise is? Since when? Ian: He told me a few days ago. Jaymes offered him a good amount and wants to get him back on his stride. I’ve given my blessings for it, not that he had a contract with me. But this is for you there Sean. You told me you’d never go for a title in the 4WW. Mainly because you didn’t want people to start up on the internet and that, saying that you only got a title because you’re the bosses brother, or the bosses best friend. It’s time for you to regain the glory you had at one time. And this PTC Extreme Title is something I know you want. Even Jamie himself has said he’s waiting for the day you take that belt and hold it for a while. Sean: I’d like it. I guess I’ve jus’ been a little ou’ of it with all th’ crap tha’ went on in tha’ last fed. I mean, technically I was undefeated as the Superior Champion. I sent them their belt back when we did’na renew our contracts. Ian: Just face it Sean, you should be ready to get back into the swing of things. You’re going in there for the 4WW in spirit, but damnit, go in there for yourself and show those amateurs that they’re not hardcore, they’re not extreme and they’re not going to leave with this. I mean, you’ve entering last. You’re already the number one contender. What else can they do? The only one who could really give you a challenge is entering third, and he’s going to be very tired by the time you get there. Sean: Who is tha’? Ian: One of the men who eliminated your sister in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, Shadow of the XUW. Al Crud’s fed. Sean: Another one o’ the ‘Family’ feds there? I saw ‘im in the cup. Na’ bad. Definatly one I need t’ look ou’ fer. Bu’ if ‘e’s entering third, ‘ed better ‘ope ‘e’s still there when I get there. Ian: Why? Sean: So tha’ a McClancy can make it one t’ one with ‘im. E’s ga’ one up on us. Other then tha, I don’na care who they are. Someone is leavin’ as the’ next one t’ face Mike Marvelous, an’ tha’s me. Marvelous ‘as a big ‘ead as it is, an’ I’m gion’ t’ be the one t’ break it fer ‘im. ‘Ow did ‘e do again’ Fathom? Ian: By the skin of his teeth, by the skin of his teeth. He’d chosen a lion’s den match. Just like our own West Wind Title, it’s challenger picks the stips. So in all honesty, you’d better start thinking just what you’re going to ask for with Mike. Sean: Does it matter? I’m the’ fokin’ ‘ardcore Icon. I’d be ‘alf inclined t’ make it fair fer ‘im an’ let ‘im pick the stips. Bu’ then again, migh’ as well play by th’ rules, wha’ few there are. I guess ye can issue th’ official statement t’ the boys gettin’ in’ the ring fer this. “The Irish Bastard” is comin’ t’ kick their bloody arses an’ claim wha’ is bloody well riogh’fully mine. Ian: I’ll pass the word on. In the meantime, you might want to go save your students for this week from your sister. Sean turns around and looks up into the ring. He sees his sister has the Texan locked in a crucifix arm bar and she’s not letting go. He smiles and shakes his head. Sean: Yeah, riogh’. Let them learn a lesson. I guess I need t’ go get me toy ou’ of storage? Ian: It’s already being delivered. Patti is sending it out here. You just get ready. As soon as I know where you’re going, the jet will be here to get you. I don’t have exact details just yet. Sean: Sounds good t’ me. Jus’ keep me up t’ date, I’ll be there. ~To be continued~
