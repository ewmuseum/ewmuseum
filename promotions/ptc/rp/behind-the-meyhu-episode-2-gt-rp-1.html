We can see who rains down more destruction and death and carnage and blah blah blah. ::The World Champion grabs Jesse by the hair, and smashes his head hard into the outside ringpost, making a sound that all the arena can hear. He then tumbles the Dark Warrior up the rampway, with Lotus following close behind. Meyhu goes to slam Jamester's face into the guardrail.:: Mean Mike Meyhu: You know, thats the first good idea you've come up with in a long time! CRAP! Look what time it is! His friend who smirks, before lifting him carelessly into the air, and dropping him with the Meyhu Meltdown.:: ...The face of Mike Meyhu. Lights Out Wrestling Presents... Disturbed’s “Droppin’ Plates”, the anthem of LOW’s most powerful man is cued as the show comes in to the host, standing behind his pedestal marked “Behind The Meyhu.” “Welcome to this edition of ‘Behind The Meyhu’, I’m Jim Forbes. Tonight on ‘Behind The Meyhu’ we stray away from the main story of the brawler, and instead go in depth with the men and women that have shaped him most, his very own family. Not just his siblings, we’ll look at much of his extended family as well. We’ll see just how each and every one of them influenced him in their own way, from his mother to his deranged cousins. First off, possibly the most influential of all Meyhu‘s on the brawler, his tag team partner, best friend and little brother, The Marvelous One.” ::The fans cheer wildly, and Dark Haze brings up Meyhu the full way, only to grab him by the head again, and thrust him towards the side of the cage. Meyhu skids and stops when he realizes what is going on, then turns and dropkicks Dark Haze. Haze stumbles and falls hard. Although obviously hurt, Haze gets to his feet, walking awkwardly around. He gets to the side of the cage, and turns, but only in time to catch an M-100 from the world champion, with an impact so great that it sends Dark Haze right off the cage!:: Marvelous Matt Meyhu: I will beat him within one inch of his life! ::Marvelous Matt lifts up the woozy Haze, who swings wildly, but can't support his body on his legs, and soon collapses. He makes a desperate attempt up, but Matt pummels him with a clothesline... Matt Meyhu picks up a nearby chair. Leaping over the guardrail, Meyhu brings the chair down hard onto the back of Haze's head. Haze drops like a fly, and does not move. :: Marvelous Matt Meyhu: Nope. And I notice you're trailing into the wrestlers category, so the answer is DEFINETLY... Nope. And since when is Maniac important? ::Meyhu falls back into the corner as the fans start to cheer and chant for Matt. Maniac slowly gets back to his feet as Meyhu starts stomping his foot on the ground. Maniac turns around at the last second, to see the large boot of Matt Meyhu flying straight into his face! Maniac has no time to move and can only endure the pain of the M-100! Maniac drops to the mat with a loud thud. Meyhu falls forwards, landing right on top of his opponent. The ref gets in position for the count, 1...2...3!!:: Marvelous Matt Meyhu: Well, let's just take this one step at a time for now... The ref gets in position for the count, 1...2...3!! “Matt Meyhu has won this match! He won it with that sick move he calls the M-100!” “THE M100! ITS OVER! ITS OVER!” Meyhu covers him, 1.........2........3!!!!! I’m MARVELOUS! The scene focus’s in on Global Championship Wrestling’s biggest star, The Marvelous One Matt Meyhu. He sits in a black leather chair, his eyes hidden behind his dark sunglasses. He has a smile on his picture perfect face as he sits soaking in airtime. Jim Forbes starts. “Ladies and gentlemen, Matt Meyhu.” Matt soaks in some more precious airtime as he basks in his own glory. When he’s finished he leans forward and rests his elbows on his knees and takes a closer look at Jim Forbes, who sits in front of him. “Well?” Matt asks impatiently. Forbes looks a little startled, but quickly collects himself and starts the interview. “Matt Meyhu, you are here because you are quite possibly the most influential Meyhu on our topic of this series, Mike Meyhu. Of course, this episode is going to be based around The Meyhu family as you know, so why don’t you start by giving us some background information on yours-” Forbes is cut off by a sudden movement by Matt Meyhu, which is him leaning back into his chair. “A little information about myself eh? Well I’m the one and only Lord of the Ring, I’m the one and only Marvelous One, former TXW champion, multiple time tag team champion, etcetera.” “I see, and very modest as well.” “You know it.” Matt smiles and squirms a little in his chair, trying to get comfortable. “Well Matt, how do you think you’ve influenced your brother, Mike? Since that is after all what we’re here to talk about.” Forbes questions. Matt takes a thinking pose for a moment before responding. “Well, I think I’ve done a great deal for that kid. I mean, I really got into wrestling before he did so I kind of knew the ropes before Mike entered, although I do admit he somehow wriggled his way into a fairly well known promotion while I was stuck doing independent shows,” Matt goes on, “but still, you have to remember we tagged for awhile and I taught him how to do what he does.” “How do you figure that, seeing as you are more of a technically sound wrestler and Mike is well, The Number One Brawler in the Business. He just goes out there and does basically the opposite of what you do.” Forbes points out. Matt looks a little annoyed with this comment and changes the subject. “In any case, he’s good, I helped him a little bit, and I’m The Marvelous One.” Matt smiles at the camera with his wonderful white teeth and then looks back at Forbes. “Any other questions?” Forbes thinks for a moment before answering, “No, we’re finished for now, I’d like to thank you for sitting down with us tonight Matt.” “My pleasure,” Matt says extending his hand, “Anytime.” The scene fades out as Matt looks back at the camera and fades back to the studio, where the host is standing. “What a guy.. when we return, We’re going to take a quick look at some of The Meyhu’s other relatives from all over.” ....................................... ‘Behind The Meyhu’ returns with Jim Forbes looking up at the camera, shuffling some papers on his LOW pedestal. “Welcome back,” He starts, “as I have found out, the Meyhu family is a very diverse one, and tonight we are going to take a look at some of them. Some have influenced our main subject Mike Meyhu, but this show is ‘Behind The Meyhu’ and we’ve decided to meet some of the family, and now you will get to meet some of them as well.” The scene changes to a fairly good looking young man, probably about twenty years old, sitting in the same leather chair Matt Meyhu was in earlier. Jim Forbes sits opposite of him holding some papers in his hand. “Hello, and thanks for being with us here tonight... Mel.” Forbes begins. “Eh eh, its MAJOR Mel Meyhu.” The Meyhu corrects him. Forbes nods and corrects himself, “Major, Mel Meyhu. Now, it says here Mel that before you got into wrestling, your currently on the independent circuit, correct? It says here that you served our country in the armed forces, and that’s where you get your nickname, Major.” Mel confirms this with a nod of his head. “Correct, I’m Major Mel Meyhu.” The camera leaves Mel Meyhu and changes to a short plump man with a large black mustache. He has rosy cheeks and a friendly looking smile on his face. “Now, what do you by, sir?” Forbes asks the man. “Itsa Me! MARIO!” “Says here you are, ‘Mighty’ Mario Meyhu? And wouldn’t you know it, your currently a wrestler for an Italian promotion.” “Mama mia! Thatsa correct!” “Well, we try to get accurate information. Well, Mighty, why don’t you tell the American fans about yourself a little, I doubt they have seen much Italian wrestling. What’s your finishing move?” “The Head Stomp! I goa off the topa rope and jumpa on their head! MAMA MIA!” Forbes doesn’t look very surprised by this answer, and quickly thanks Mario for his time. The camera shifts again, this time to inside a small store, where a middle eastern man with a long curly black beard stands behind the counter. “Hello, I’m Jim Forbes of Behind The Meyhu, as you know. Why don’t you tell us a little about yourself?” The man speaks with a heavy middle eastern accent, “I... am... Muhammad... Meyhu. I... Come.. from... New York..” “I see, New York, and what do you do in New York? I’m going to go out on a limb here.. do you wrestle?” Muhammad looks very surprised at the apparently correct guess made by Forbes, but answers, “I also drive cabs. Wrestling.. does not bring me the money like I need. I work at the 7-11 as well.” “I see, you sound like a busy guy Muhammad.” Forbes nods understandingly, “Well, thanks for giving us this chance to meet you.” “Thank you, come again!” The scene changes to a neighborhood, where Jim Forbes sits in a lawn chair opposite a large black man with an afro, headphones around his neck blaring music, large jeans and huge untied boots. He sits their nodding his head to the beat of the music as Jim Forbes tries to get his attention. “Alright.. uh.. alright... uh.. Mashaka!” The big man turns his head, “WHAT UP?” Forbes face looks a little relieved as he goes on, “Mashaka, you are a Meyhu, are you not?” “Dat’s right yo! Meyhu comin’ out of South Centro’!” Mashaka confirms. “Fo Sho,” The white host agrees, “now Mashaka, I notice one thing about you that I don’t see in many Meyhu’s, your.. uh.. African American.” “YOU GOTTA PROBLEM ‘CAUSE I’M BLACK!? Listen up cracka, i’ll smack yo head to da floor, scare you make you run out-da door, you thinkin all n*beep*’s do is chores, i tell you I won’t be doin yours, git da hell outta here, go back to ya money, ya cars and yo WHITE W*beep*S! Yah I’m “African American” but Meyhu is all I eva’ bin! I’m wonderin’ why you look so surprised, tell me why I can’t be a n*beep*a Meyhu, and gimme one reason why I shouldn’t HATE YOU!” “Hey hey, lots of hostility there homie, I mean no harm!” Mashaka Meyhu jumps to his feet, knocking the lawn chair he was sitting in over, and starts towards Jim Forbes. Forbes falls out of his chair and starts crawling away backwards from Mashaka, trying to get to his feet. “MAN YOU GET YOUR WHITE A*beep*S OUTTA ‘ERE BEFO’ I BUSTA CAP ON YO CRACKA A*beep*S!” Mashaka reaches into a jacket pocket searching for something as Forbes gets to his feet and the scene fades. A new scene opens with Jim Forbes standing with an average looking man, who stands looking a little awkward next to Forbes. “Hi, this is Jim Forbes and I’m here with Marty Meyhu. Marty, how are you this afternoon.” Forbes asks politely. “Uh.. I’m great....” “Good, good, now, can you tell us a little about yourself. What’s your relation to Mike Meyhu?” “Uh.. that’s a great question Jim, one I’m asking myself right now. You see, I’m in no way related to Mike Meyhu at all, I’m not a wrestler, I’m an accountant, I don’t even watch wrestling, I’ve just heard the name a couple times. I don’t even know why I’m here to tell you the truth.. everyone gets me confused with them...” Forbes stands silent, one eyebrow raised and his tongue pushing against his cheek, confused, slightly embarrassed, and possibly annoyed, among other things. The scene changes to what looks to be an Oriental restaurant, Jim Forbes kneeling at one end of a small table and a physically fit Asian man opposite him. “Hello, Jim Forbes.” “No, I, Miyagawa.” The man points to himself. “Yes, Miyagawa, did I say that right? Meyhu. And I, Jim Forbes.” “Yes. Miyagawa.” “Miyagawa Meyhu? Isn’t that an odd last name for a person of your ethnic background?” “No.” “No huh.. alright then, why don’t you tell us about yourself Miya.” “I, Miyagawa.” “Yes, we’ve established that.” “I, Wrestler. I, Hardcore.” “Awesome.. and wow, I wouldn’t have guessed you were a wrestler..” “I show you move.” “Uh.. heh heh.. no that’s alright, in fact, look at the time! You’ll have to show me them some other time.. nice meeting you!” Forbes gets up quickly as the scene fades. The scene returns to the studio, where Jim Forbes stands shaking his head a little, before looking up to the camera and concluding the show. “I spoke with some more of the Meyhu family, but sadly those interviews will have to be aired at a later time, if at all.... as we have run out of time for this edition of ‘Behind The Meyhu.’ Thanks for watching, and be sure to tune in next week for more in depth looks at the family and life behind the one and only Mike Meyhu. Goodnight.” _________________ GET IT!?
