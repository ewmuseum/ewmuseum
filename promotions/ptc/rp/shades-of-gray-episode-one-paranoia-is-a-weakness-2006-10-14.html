Shades Of Gray: Episode One: Paranoia Is A Weakness

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 Nobody. That's exactly who came to mind when Bobino tried to figure out who could be calling him at his unlisted home phone number, at least from this area code. He stared down at the caller ID as the phone rang in the background. "[detail withheld]: Boston, Massachusetts" That's all his caller ID would tell about this mystery person. Any people Bobino knew in Boston had long since moved, died, or been forgotten about. The skepticism prevented Bobino from answering the ring, as the machine kicked in, and Bobino's voice echoed through the nearly silent room. "I'm out. If what you have to say is important, you'll make me care enough to call you back. Now leave a message, or don't call back." -BEEP!!- There was a small moment of silence as Bobino waited to hear the voice of the mystery caller. "I'm trying to get in touch with Robert. This is Jennifer from Sunny Days Mental Heath Clinic. This is in regards to your brother's recent relea--" The machine switches off in mid sentence, as Bobino lifts the reciever to his ear, pausing before he continues. "What do you mean released?" ---------------------------------------- Most of the time, if you asked any members of the FUSE roster about how Bobino acted backstage, you'd get similar answers from everyone. Bobino was one of the most calm, laid back people you could find, if only for the sole reason he usually felt superior to everyone else. He would enter the arena on time or close to on time, he'd get his assignments, practice his spots, and get ready to perform. The majority of the time, Bobino wouldn't be one to cause a ruckus backstage, or do anything to make others feel uncomfortable. Today was one of the rare exceptions. As the FUSE Roster tried to enter the general locker rooms, they found a two hundred and forty pound obstruction in their way. Bobino was sitting in a steel chair, looking directly at the door, a steel kendo stick gripped tightly in his right hand. Believe me, early in the morning, it's a bit startling to open a door and find an angry Darwinist staring at you with a weapon in his free hand. Most people figured out by the lack of kendo stick hitting them in the face that Bobino wasn't waiting for them. They went about their business, and the day flowed pretty well. Later in the afternoon, people began to worry. The FUSE roster may hate each other's guts, but they care enough to realize when something's wrong with one of their brethren. Bobino never showed up to his practice sessions with John Covel, and he never did report in with wardrobe. He just sat, guarding the entrance to the FUSE lockers. The creepy part isn't even the face that he seemed almost statuesque in his stake out. The creepy part is that he was completely silent, ignoring any comments from those around him. Now, for most people, silence wouldn't be odd, but Bobino isn't exactly what you would call introverted. Usually, he'd join into any conversation that was offered, most likely only to hear his own voice. Again, today was different, he completely disregarded everyone's attempts to converse with him, until one voice got his attention. "What are you waiting for, man?" The deep voice contrasted the rest of the locker room, and immediately alerted Bobino to the presence of the rather large man over his shoulder. "The Living Legend" Majin pulled up a chair next to Bobino, and sat staring at the door with him. Majin and Bobino sat and let the silence linger for a moment. After a small delay, Bobino opened his mouth for the first time in over six hours. "Majin, what would you do if the worst thing you ever did came back to face you? What would you do if something you thought you had taken care of decided to repay you a visit? Better yet, what would you do if you -KNEW- this thing was coming, but you had no idea when, where, or how it was coming?" Majin actually seemed a bit perplexed at Bobino's quandary. He shifts the chair around, as he puts a hand on Bobino's shoulder. He leans in as he finally comes to a conclusion. "Ja'know, I'm not entirely sure what I would do… I'll tell ya what I wouldn't do, though. I wouldn't let it change my day to day life. I wouldn't let the threat change how I live. If I did that, then it would be winning already." Majin rose from his chair, patting his teammate on the back. He opens the door and makes his way out, calling back to Bobino on his way. "Don't stay here too long, we got a match to prepare for." Bobino still sat, gripping the weapon in his good hand. Majin rolled his eyes, trying to figure out how to move his stoic partner. He shook his head as he finally walked off, one last line escaping his mouth as the door slowly swung shut. "Paranoia -IS- a weakness, you know." The steel door snaps shut, as Bobino blinks a few times, realizing the intent of Majin's comments. He looked around the room, and then looked at himself. "What the fuck am I doing?" Bobino stood, and dropped the weapon onto the ground. He looked around again, in an almost frantic way, making sure nobody saw him talking to himself. His hands brush over his face, almost trying to peel the moment off his body. He picks his jacket up and tosses it over his shoulder, as he exits the room, trying to catch up with his partner. As the door again comes to a close, Bobino can be heard chuckling to himself as he leaves. "It's all in my head." The door snaps shut, and we're left staring a the empty locker room. An odd noise can be heard coming from the vacant room. I say odd, because it sounds human, in what should be an empty changing area. Low, dull laughter can be heard, as a gray, gloved hand grips the kendo stick, lifting it out of sight. "Ha… I'm in your head alright…"
