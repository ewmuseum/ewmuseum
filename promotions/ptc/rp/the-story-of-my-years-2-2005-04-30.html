The Story of My Years (2)

30 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-30 ---------4 Years Later-------- Fifteen years old… I was fifteen years old when I was finally introduced to reading. How many kids could read when they were five? Most of them, not myself, I was slowly figuring out the letters at the age of Fifteen. Hiring a tutor was probably the nicest thing Thompson had ever done for me, and that’s not saying much. Finally, after struggling through my first chapter book I sat silently on my cot. When I was younger I’d let my mind wander, to my family back in Mexico… What their life was like, if I truly was better off with Thompson… But it seemed like every time I’d wonder about them Thompson would walk into the room… Every time I let my mind go, something I had never been able to do, he’d ruin it for me. I was a sexually abused teenage boy, this man was a giant. He stood six foot nine, how could I ever stop his urges? How could I overcome his hunger? I had to give in. I had no choice. ----------2 Months Ago---------- I stared at the man who had abused me. The molester. The rapist. The fake. The betrayer. The anger slowly began to boil inside of me… I had trained for years to be a professional wrestler, constantly working, my body was chiseled like rock… I knew I was ready and I approached Thompson. He looked at me and smiled, unzipping his pants. I glared back, the disgust shown by my body posture. I shook my head no. The Devil reached out to wrap his giant hands around my body. It all happened in an instant. My right arm shot out, punching the giant across the mouth. His head snapped to the side as he stumbled backwards… I had stunned him. The rolls had reversed. I was the predator and he was the pray. I leapt off of the chair using it as if it was a rope in a wrestling ring, I shot my arms around Thompson’s neck. When I felt the air rush out of him I felt pleased. The man who had abused me, destroyed me, broken my will and my desires. Crushed my dreams and neglected my purpose in life was now in my grasp. His arms gripped mine, straining to remove them, little did Thompson know a rear naked choke was almost inescapable. The giant took one last gasp as I finally let him go. His breathing resumed, shallow in depth. But they were the thankful breaths of life. I grabbed his feet and slowly brought him over to his chair, propping him up. I went to the garage and grabbed a few extension cords. When I entered the house I began making sure Thompson wouldn’t be able to hurt me when he awoke from his slumber. Wrapping the cords tightly I smiled as I could see his skin turning white… He’d wake up. And I’d stare him directly in the eyes and laugh. I am Toradio Gonzalez… and I am insane. ---------3 Hours Later---------- I looked at the man who tainted and burnt my soul… Staring as I brought my hands up his tired, weary arms. I could see Thompson was growing old, gone was the flowing brown hair, what remained was the shaved scalp. A man who persecuted worse than the skinheads he so resembled do. A flutter of an eye lash and a chuckle from an innocent angel. I glared into the now open eyes of Mr. Thompson, I could see the surprise as he tried to move his arms… realizing the extension cords pulling against his arms… His shocked face… glaring at my resourcefulness, he knew what was going on. I watched as Thompson lowered his head, he thought his life was coming to an end, and I could feel it inside of him, he was almost positive that this was the end of the line. I looked at the man and smiled, my heart filling with laughter. He glared at me confused. “So Thompson… you thought you had me?” I stated glaring at him. “You thought I’d be here with you until death do us part… Your feeble mind let mine grow, this plan is my plan and it’s time I go off and do it… I’m going to wrestle now Thompson” I stated as I began to walk to the door of the cabin. I looked back at him and smiled. “And your going to die,” I stated as I flicked the light switch to the off position. I could hear his soft whimper as I stepped into his Chevy Blazer, I was off to fulfill my destiny. The King of the cruiserweights. ------1 Week Later----- I had won my first ever wrestling match, outside of the people Thompson would bring in and throw at me in training. My red and black mask was still on my face, the pride I felt wearing it was immense. This paycheck was almost five-thousand dollars… for winning one match. I looked into the mirror, into the eyes behind the mask and I knew I had one thing left to do. There was a family living in Mexico who had waited thirteen years for this day… and I knew I had to go see them. My story was far from over.
