And When She Lost

15 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2009-10-15 :And When She Lost: They say man is measured by the successes and failures in their lives... the more successful they are, the more people scream out their name. The more times they fail? Well then they fail alone... it was just the nature of the beast so to speak; human nature at it's finest... living vicariously through the winners, ignoring the losers no matter how well they did prior to the defeat. Katherine knew all too well what it was like to be on top of the world one minute, and drowning in the abyss that was failure the next. the eb and flow that was life for her, like the coming of the tide; she always braved the storms it threw her way. Whether it was at the age of six years old and watching her mother die right before her very eyes, or the slaughter that would follow as her dad stalked the man down and left him as a warning... that was the first time she realized that her life was anything but normal. It was also the night she lost her innocence... dying inside in the process. and when she lost... she lost alone. She never complained about it, just went through the motions; never showing any signs of the bouts of depression that ran rampart through her mind. Even her dreams were overcome by nightmares; no visions of sugarplums dancing in her head... no ponies, no dancing with her father. Just pain, screaming, and blood... her mother's blood. It was why she grew to become what she had despised for so long... the monster within the nightmares, and by doing so... she lost her soul... and when she lost... she lost alone. No one ever said that losing was easy, but they never knew Katherine. Because when she was alone; she was free to be herself; away from the cameras that her career in wrestling had brought her way. Even when she was with her best friends, Katherine put on the mask of being carefree... always the one to crack the jokes in an attempt to mask her pain. It worked for the most part and she was grateful for that. She hated lying to them but she had to; to keep them safe from the maddness inside her. And by doing so she lost her own trust. and when she lost... she lost alone. And now as time had come and gone; Katherine saw her chance to either gain back everything she had lost since she was six years old in the form of the GTT7 or to lose everything else that remained... and she knew all too well what was in store for her... pain and agony would become her friends once more as she made the long treck through all who stood in her way. And should she win, she'll win with them all... and should she lose? She'll lose alone... **** The moon bore down on the body of Katherine Stryfe; her body giving off a glow as she sat on the back porch of her home; her eyes looking out into the darkness in front of her. Her right hand reached for the deck of cigarettes beside her; pulling out the custom zippo her father gave her many years ago with the free one. Sighing to herself, she placed one of the cigarettes into her mouth. [You sure you are ready for this, Kat? I mean it was one thing to go through the extreme tournament on the experts... but this is a whole nother ballgame all together.] Katherine ignored the monster in her head as she lit the cigarette, allowing the carbon to enter into her lungs slowly; like the dragon's flames coursing through her body, Katherine shuddered before the smoke escaped from her nose and mouth before dissappearing into the air. She knew very well what she was getting herself into and wouldn't have accepted this challenge if she didn't believe in herself and her talents as a warrior. [You cannot ignore me all the time, Kat. No matter where you go, no matter what you do, I'm always here. You are going to listen to me sooner or later. This is an impossible challenge and you CANNOT win this! DO YOU HEAR ME?! KATHERINE ANNE BELMONT-STRYFE, YOU ARE GOING TO FAIL!] "That is where you are wrong, Malice..." Katherine spoke silently to herself, closing her eyes as her breaths deepened. She knew full well that the monster inside her was only trying to convience her to drop out of this; but she wasn't about to allow this to happen. She would rather go out fighting then to hang her head low and walk away with the shame of knowing she gave it all away over her own fears. A laugh escaped from her lungs as it echoed off into the vast darkness in front of her. Another drag from her cigarette and Katherine opened her eyes and smiled. "You cannot stop me, Malice. Your words hold no effect this time. I am not doing this for you, or for my family. I am doing this for me and me alone!" [And that is why you are going to fail... fall flat on your face like you did at the extreme tournament. Like you did when you faced the Supreme Machine for your Intensity championship... like you did in your past relationships. It's not all about what you want, little Kat. This world is not all about you! You would be best to remember that when you lose this first round. Because when you lose... you will lose alone.] It was all she could stand to hear, dropping the cigarette on the ground; Katherine stood up from her seat and walked off into the darkness in front of her. The words of her monster still ringing true in her ears. And if she loses this tournament; she won't have her family there to support her, nor will she have the fans by her side. She will walk into the back alone... take that shower of shame... alone; watch the tapes of the defeat... alone. And she wouldn't want it any other way. A sick smile etched on her lips, still walking into the darkness; her destination? Unknown.. but no matter where it might lead her; one thing was for sure. She'd be going... alone.
