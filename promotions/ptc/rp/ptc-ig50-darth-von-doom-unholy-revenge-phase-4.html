Darth von Doom versus Brian Spaes

Unholy Revenge, Phase 4 - Horsemen, Press Conferences, and Death (IG50 Promo #4 of 5) Title: Unholy Revenge, Phase 4 - Horsemen, Press Conferences, and Death (IG50 Promo #4 of 5) Word Count: 1000 Exactly Wrestler: Darth von Doom Inside the Castle of Blood’s dungeon basement laboratory, the Horny Mathematician has brought the rest of the Church leadership down to his lab to show them his latest creation. His assistant Igor has prepped the experiment and is ready to unveil it to the leadership council. Darth von Doom is the first down behind Mathematician followed by Dr. Acula, Wolfen, and C.G. Murder. Once in the laboratory they see something laying on a bed with a white sheet covering it. Murder points to the sheet. Murder: What’s under there? And can it kill people? The Mathematician smiles deviously. Mathematician: Hold on. First I need to show you what I’ve created that goes along with this. Darth can sense a disturbance in the Force. Darth: What do you mean? Mathematician: I’ve been working on creating the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse as you all know. Dr. Acula: So how is your progress coming? The Mathematician smiles and then snaps his fingers. Mathematician: Splendidly. Just then a giant armored red eyed ghoulish looking creature comes walking in. He is armed with two swords and walks over to the Mathematician before stopping. Mathematician: May I present to you the man formerly known as Amnesia who is the first Horseman known as Pestilence. Darth seems very happy. Darth: Nice. He is rather Unholy. Wolfen: *snarl* He smells funny. Suddenly the ground starts shaking as something else walks in behind them. The Church leadership council turns around to see an armored knight-like being. The being has armored plating all over its body and is armed with a giant sword. Murder: What the hell is that thing? Mathematician: That is the second Horseman. He used to be your friend Jon from the NWC. Now he has been transformed in the Horseman War. Darth looks very pleased to see this. Darth: Nice. I finally see you have created something somewhat on par with myself. The Mathematician has a crooked smile on his face at this points and heads over to the covered bed. Mathematician: You haven’t seen anything yet, Darth. Darth: What could be better than this? The Mathematician pulls off the covering and unveils the third Horseman. The being laying on the bed has grayish colored skin and bright blue eyes. It also is wearing dark colored clothes over steel plated armor and circuitry. The being rises up from the bed before standing on the ground to get a better look at the Church leadership that is staring at him. Mathematician: This is the man who used to be Fishboy. Now he is the third Horseman known simply as Famine. Darth: Nice work on this one. Mathematician: Thank you. Famine and the rest of the Horsemen are the finest killing machines on this planet. C.G. Murder then clears his throat. The Mathematician looks over and sees Murder glaring at him but also notices that the Masked Halloween Psycho Killer and the Freaky Ass Ghoul or F.A.G. have walked in. Neither of them look too happy either. Murder: I think me and my friends here don’t seem too happy about what you just said. F.A.G.: Yeah. I am the best murderer in the Church. The Masked Halloween Psycho Killer then pulls out a big kitchen knife and stabs the Freaky Ass Ghoul in the chest, killing him instantly. The body falls to the ground in a heap. Murder: He wasn’t unholy enough for us. What makes you think these Horsemen are? The Mathematician chuckles to himself. Mathematician: Me and Darth have a plan about who the fourth Horseman will be. Wolfen: *snarl* What did you have in mind? Darth: We need to find the Killer Pirates in order to complete Project: Apocalypse. Cow Man suddenly comes running down into the lab. Cow Man: Master Darth! Green Peace is protesting outside the Castle again! Darth growls in frustration. Darth: Stupid idiots. Send the flying monkeys to take care of this. Cow Man: Yes Master! Darth then uses the Force to stop him. Darth: Wait…let’s see how the Horsemen handle them. Darth cackles maniacally as the three Horsemen head up the stairs just as Darth releases Cow Man from his clutches. The Mathematician looks very pleased. * * * Two days later… James the Entertainment Director of the Church of the Unholy is holding a press conference in his hometown of Las Vegas, Nevada to address the group’s recent actions. Most notably the zombie invasion of the United States, the attack on Bodega Bay, and the attack on PTC Headquarters. James stands behind the podium with a smile on his face as he begins to address the matters at hand. James: Hello and thank all of you for being here in beautiful Las Vegas to address the recent actions of my clients the Church of the Unholy. Well let me just say this first. I AM IRON MAN. The reporters look at each other weirdly as James gets a chuckle over their reaction. James: Just kidding. A poor attempt at humor there. Anyway, back to the matter at hand. The Church said they wanted peace and they meant it. I have visual proof of this. Darth, would you please show them what I’m talking about? A collective gasp is let out from the crowd as Darth von Doom walks in holding the severed head of Uzbekistani dictator Islam Karimov. Darth throws the severed head in the air and it lands on the floor before rolling to a stop in front of the podium. James shrugs his shoulders as Darth walks out of the room like nothing happened. James: See? They took care of a ruthless mass murderer just to help bring more peace on Earth. Any questions? * * * At the same time… A remote location in the Yukon Territory of Canada is the landing spot for hundreds of Church transport ships. The ships quickly unload their cargo, which just happens to be zombies! In the woods Canadian hero Slam Boomsdexter watches on while eating some blueberries. (To be continued…)
