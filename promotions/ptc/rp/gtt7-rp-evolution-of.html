Primetime Central Presents: GTT7 Moderate GTT7 a Primetime Central production Home Mobile Rules The Field The Seeds Promos Discussion PTC GTT7 at PTCPedia The Draw at UStream Login Title: Evolution Of Featuring: Steve Harrison Date: 12th Oct 2009 Location: RP Vs Hessian Vs Jamester Prologue Driving in the hills of Virginia near the Shenandoah Valley is a lovely site to behold. Steve Harrison had grown up in Alexandria, Virginia and rarely did he ever spend his time near the trees and wildlife. At this point he was still not enjoying it because he was in a car with Bobby Dean and Simon Marks. He leaned back in the shotgun position looking outside as the wind tossed leaves around.  He hoped he would get swallowed up by the calmness. Steve attempted to forget that he was in a car with people who had made him their personal assistant per a shady contract. Harrison was depressed because of his predicament and in his thoughts of desperation had not buckled his seatbelt. "WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING?' Steve yelled at Bobby Dean as Dean began to lose control of the wheel. The car Bobby was driving began to swerve left to right and then flipped. Steve attempted in vain to grab the overhead handle on the door but it was too late--- "God--please..." Steve whispered softly. CRASH ---Steve had become what your mom warned you about when you did not buckle your seatbelt. The car flipped and he bounced, his fingers cracking against the top of the car and his legs thrashing underneath his body as gravity dissipated. The car finally came to a stop but it was stopped by a head on collision with a tree and Steve Harrison flew, but no voice could be heard--- He flew like a bird let out of his cage but Steve did not have wings and he landed on the ground: injured, bloody, and unresponsive. It all went dark for the lovable loser. Then it all went bright, it was like heaven for Harrison. Chapter 1: Into The Light "Hello?" __hello__hello__hello__hello Echoes. I stood in a never-ending sea of white. I thought I was walking but I never went anywhere, just bright white attempting to swallow me up making it hard to breath. "Steve," I heard suddenly. I turned my head so quick it felt like I no longer had a neck. I saw nothing though and the voice I heard disappeared like the wind. I put my head back and placed my hands on my face, I was tripping out and I could not end it with taking a shower or closing my eyes. When I closed my eyes I saw the same bright whiteness and there was no escape. I started breathing heavily thinking to myself I might panic to death right before I reached some state of Nirvana. "What is going on?" I said quietly but the words again echoed and became louder and louder until I was forced to cover my ears. "It is a choice." I turned again hoping that this voice was not my imagination again. I then came face to face with my father. He was always a menacing looking man but he was smirking at me like he knew a secret. His presence was scary though and my body shook at the site of this man. This man who made my life a living hell when I was growing up and is still haunting me today--- ---Even in death. Death, I thought to myself. This man was dead but here I was looking at his crooked devilish smile making me feel as small as a pea again. "You are dead! This must be one twisted nightmare." My father shrugged and moved his arm across as if he was showing me this incredible scene. "With the right decision this could all end." I looked at him raising my left eyebrow in doubt. "Are you auditioning to be a price of right girl?" "I am dead, don't you remember?" my dead papa said back to me, his ever creepy smile never fading. "What do you want? What are you doing here? What is this place?" I spewed out question after question like a crack fiend looking for that next score. "This does not surprise me." "Huh?" "You asking questions like a child---it does not surprise me." I clamped my jaw shut and began grinding my teeth in anger. This man could even make me pathetic in a freaking dream. "Are you going to answer my questions or continue to degrade me like I am still in high school?" He laughed in my face. "I am not going to do everything for you, Steve. You were always so talented but so sensitive and unwilling to work hard to achieve greatness. Your questions are a way for you to understand everything for yourself without actually doing any work." "DIDN’T WORK HARD!?!" I yelled. My words echoed once again but this time it was so loud it forced me to one knee. I felt like my ears were going to explode but it finally subsided and I stood up. "Sigh. You are proving my point, child. I pushed you hard because you were the one that could reach the perfection needed to bring our family to an echelon not known to common man." "What are you talking about?" He shook his head like he use to when I did not live up to his expectations and chuckled at the confused look painted on my pale face. "I am talking about creating a utopia and our family is the architect to that advancement. I am not immortal, child. I needed someone worthy enough to replace me so I pushed you hard so that you could be the Harrison to bring about the changes." I swear my face became paler after this insanity was spewed out of a supposed ghost. "A cult?" His face twisted suddenly. His arrogant smile faded and was replaced with a look of death. "Watch your mouth. This is no cult, son. This is something our family has worked for since the dark ages. A book handed down through our generations which spell how to succeed and what needs to accomplished." My mouth opened wide in surprise and the whiteness that was swallowing me did not seem as odd anymore. "That sounds insane. I am in a perpetual whiteness speaking to my supposedly dead father who I despise and he is telling me about some cult our family has been part of for ages. I apologize I mean--has led. Do you actually believe that if everything is accomplished in some affront to God manuscript that OUR Utopia will be created?" "Affront to God? Since when did you become a religious person, son?" "Since I just discovered I was born to be the key to some creation hiding behind the facade of a Utopia," I yelled angrily. He moved his hand towards me--- SMACK! I took a step back and fell to one knee holding my face and looked up to see him standing over me. "You were to be the final touch on the grand plan but you were too weak and so your brother took your place. He cannot replace your natural talents though. He is just a yes-man and someone we could put as the face of the company until you woke up and stopped wanting to be loved." I stood up slowly and finished rubbing my face. My face had become red, one side because of being slapped and the other for being angry at all the nonsense I was being fed. "I want respect. You say I am so talented yet all you ever did was insult me so why would I ever care to continue on with my talents. Am I supposed to be the knight in black armor that finally stops caring about everything and ride into a peace treaty signing and cut the heads off of everyone involved?" Without blinking an eye he responded, "only if the treaty does not involve them signing over their allegiance to us." Instead I blinked surprised by how quick a response I received from this callous man. "I was being sarcastic." He slapped my right shoulder, shaking his head like he was about to explain something simple to a child. "It does not matter how you wanted your remark to come out. I am telling you that the grand plan is something that takes priority over love or kindness. You must be hard and able to put any feelings you have on the backburner. Just think about it, Son," he paused looking above---at nothing. "A world order that is not hiding behind Capitalism or Globalization, it has no secrets. It is what it is and is there for everyone to see. Our family will be sitting on thrones, looking down upon the world as we act as GODS.” I rubbed my eyes as they began to feel like they were trying to run out of my head. I looked around wondering if there was anyway to escape this monstrosity of white and insanity. "Is his hell?" I asked as my voice became a whisper. "You sound distraught, Steve. This place CAN be your hell but that is your decision. If everything I am telling you makes you want to hide in a shell like you have all of your life then you can stay." I stood still, sweat began to fight on the top of my forehead to be the first to run down my cheek and fall to the floor. I had nothing smart to say and I had no idea what to do. As I stood wishing that the white would dissipate my father stood staring at me. He tapped his foot faster and faster seemingly becoming more and more impatient with my silence. We had winners as two beads of sweat began running down my left cheek racing to the first to drop and puddle on the floor next to my feet. It did not matter which one fell first. The sound it made that reminded me of a time when I was young. It was a quiet night and I was sitting outside looking at the man-made pond in our backyard and that first drop of rain hit the pond. It felt like that, a time where the smallest noise and the ripples that followed could make me smile. “The pond,” I whispered to myself a small recollection smile came to my face. “The what?” he replied agitated at my stalling. “I was just thinking about that time at our pond when you taught me how to skip rocks. Now you are here teaching me about greed, obedience, and power,” I responded sharply taking a step towards him, my hands beginning to ball up in fists. He pointed at my hands and shook his head, “so a good memory makes you mad, Steve? How about you let go of the past and see the light of the future. You are THAT light, son. You will create a world where YOU can teach everyone how to skip rocks across a pond. All you have to do is want to live, all you have to do is want to survive, and all you have to do is change.” I sighed and finally wiped the sweat from my forehead. “I agree. The past can stay in the past. I am listening to a ghost though so why should I believe anything an apparition is telling me?” “Every generation the head of the household writes everything in the book to a new book. We scribe inside a blank book and then glue the pages onto a real book so as to hide our manuscript,” he said to me blank eyed. “That is why I should I believe you?” I said confused at why he just told me. “No, I am going to tell you where to find it and what you will encounter in doing so.” I unclenched my hands and put my palms down. I placed them each on their respective thighs. I took a deep breath and exhaled slowly thinking a mile a second about what I was going to do. “I will check out this absurd story but not because you are telling me to. I am doing this because I wanted to make a change anyway. I do not want to live my life to be accepted any longer. I do not want to suck up to established people in hopes of being respected or being taught how to succeed. I am aware I need to change and if I have to go on some stupid hunt for some made up grand plan then so be it.” “I guess wrestling was good for you. All of those allies you had were nothing but people wanting to use you. You have finally seen that this world is not about you and the guy next to you. This world is about YOU and what YOU want,” he said with a big smile on his face. He actually looked happy not arrogant—happy. I scoffed, “just tell me where to begin this magical hunt of wonders.” “The book was stolen by Francis,” he said nonchalantly like he knew all along like a caterpillar knows when to create its cocoon that I was going to agree to this. “Your lawyer, that Francis?” I asked my ghostly father. I probably looked quite the sight as this news completely surprised me. “Yes.” Francis was a thorn in my side. The last few years he has been there to tell me I would never get my inheritance from my father unless I succeeded. Of course thinking about that at this juncture is very mind numbing seeing as I am standing across from the person who put that in his will. Francis always seemed like a self serving asshole whose only advice was 'don't fuck up,' and 'you will never get your money.' If I came for him for help it was just brushed off with remarks on how wrestling was for losers or that I was nothing but a failure compared to my brother. He helped my brother humiliate me and I have been cut off by him. "Why does he have it?" "He is a very greedy man. I always knew he was in everything for himself but I never thought he would do this. I might be in it for myself as well but he crossed me and those that cross me do not survive." I smiled. I had a chance to enact revenge on a man who had treated me like I was nothing but a pebble that was in his shoe that was annoying him. I was taken a little aback by the last comment my dad made though. He was pretty much hinting at Francis being killed for his treachery and that is not something I am not very open to doing. I was beginning to feel like my destiny was intertwined with this book and at that point I did not even know if it existed. What I did know was that I had direction and one way or another I would learn something about my family or myself. "So what am I looking for?" "Hamlet by Shakespeare," he told with me glee, seeing his dreams might come to fruition. I chuckled to myself. This master plan was hidden within the confines of a dramatic play with many family dysfunctions---how ironic. "Ok." "Excellent," he said and he walked closer to me. I took a step back but he grabbed me and shook his head with a smile. The next thing surprised me more then talking to a ghost: he hugged me. Then it all went black. End of Chapter 1 Chapter 2: Deception One orange piece of foliage drifted from a tree limb dancing like a flame on a match. It turned and moved and seconds later it had landed right next to white wrinkled hand clutching a glass of white wine. The open ended porch looked upon trees and bushes, no streets could be seen. It was a hidden paradise with a fountain spitting water up to the bright blue sky every ten seconds. The hand brushed the leaf away from his glass and then lifted the glass in the air. "Cheers," he said. He put the glass to his lips and took a reasonably long sip and then licked the last remaining drops from his lips. He placed the drink back down on the table. A woman approached the table in a black night gown. She was tall and slender with long raven black hair. Her eyes pierced the man’s wine glass. It looked like she was attempting to shatter it with her mind. She walked slowly to the table and looked down at the man with disdain in her eyes but they changed quickly when the man looked up at her. His eyes looked hard, like his soul had left his body years ago. She grabbed the glass and lifted it up above her head. The man did not make a move other then brushing off some dust from his right shoulder on his expensive looking button down shirt. The glass dropped and smashed into pieces on the adobo clay floor of the deck and she gave the man a 'what you gonna do about it,' look. He gave her no emotional response. Her smirk soon faded into a grimace that was then followed with her face becoming red. She lifted her hand again and went to slap the man’s face. He made one quick jerk of his left hand and grabbed her by the wrist. She struggled to break free but he gripped her wrist tighter and tighter and then looked up at her eyes. "Foolish." She teared her wrist away and held it. The wrist had become red and would not stop throbbing so she continuec to hold it as if it was about to fall off. "What are you?" "Do not give me your sanctimonious bullshit. You knew who I was when we were married, if this bothers you then you have become soft." She stopped holding her wrist and sighed knowing that she was fighting a losing battle. “I understood being dead was a way for you to escape but now you are going too far.” The man stood up and laughed. He is tall and slender with graying hair that once was brown. You can barely see the brown of his eyes through the darkness he radiates. “I need to get power back. If I need to be even more deceiving to do so then so be it. I have lived in the shadows long enough and all I need is that book back.” She put her hands on her hips, “so you pretend to be a ghost?” He brushed the comment off with a flick of his left index finger, “ours sons are fools. We have one living off his inheritance and running my business into the ground. The other one needed a push. He needed to become the man he was destined to be. Not some failing joke that grasped onto wrestling to get accepted and respected. The accident he was involved in was the opportune time to put my plan into motion…” She interrupted, “the accident you helped CAUSE!” she screamed at him. He shook his head, “that is where you are wrong. I happened t be having him tailed though and when he was sent to the hospital I had some men bring him to a personal physician. I had it all worked out, dear. When he woke up he was in a white room, a close to death coma would be another way of putting it. He choose to live though after our excellent conversation and it only took a small injection to put him back under after our family embrace of course.” “Thurman Harrison, you are a despicable man,” the woman yelled and then stormed off. He sat back down and poured himself another glass of white man, “splendid,” he said. He followed it with a chuckle that is was evil sounding it would make grass turn to dust. “What a fool she has become. I only had these kids to satisfy her and now I am supposed to care if I use them for my own means? That is ignorant. I did not go to all this work to make Steve think I am a ghost with info for her to pull the rug from under me now. When he wakes up from our hug he will get to work and that work will show me amazing dividends. My return will be that of a Phoenix and I will turn anyone who interferes with me to ash. I do not care if it is my finally awakened son or those who put the hit out on me five years ago. That book, ha-ha---Utopia, I am brilliant.” Chapter 3: New Outlook Two Days Later It had been two days since I had awoken in a hospital with the visions of my dream dancing in my head. I left ten minutes after I woke up. It was strange because I did not ask how long I was out or if I had any serious injuries I might have to look out for in the future. I felt fine and other then a few scars on my face I did not see any damage to my body. I left after learning nobody had visited me while I was in the hospital which made my decision all the more easier. I do not care if I become bitter and angry, everyone has brought me to this diving board and I have finally decided to jump off of it. It is time to become my own person and if that means I have to do what I plan on doing I won’t regret it. I walked confidently for once towards the door of Dr. Lawson. She is my psychologist that was helping me with my confidence several months ago and she is quite beautiful if I do say so myself. Since I had gotten out of the hospital I thought it was important to see my doctor and go over a few things that had just happened. I had not seen her since I was forced to become another persons lackey but—but those days are over. I did not even look at the receptionist.  Her vain attempts at stopping me did not work as I opened Dr. Lawson’s door and stormed in. I was expecting a welcome not becoming of a lady but instead--- “Ah he rises from the dead.” ---Instead I got sarcasm. I fake smiled and got down to business. "I need your help." She shrugged her nicely formed shoulders and stood up. "I have not heard from you in over a month. You finally show up showing some new found confidence and think that I can be bossed around all of sudden?" I walked to her where she stood and stared straight into her eyes, "I am not trying to boss you around.  I am telling you that I need help and that I expect you to help me." I watched her eyes dance for a second. She was completely surprised by my tone of voice and actions and to be honest I felt like I was watching someone else say all of this. She smiled at me suddenly and shook her head, her blonde hair bouncing with every slow nod. "What do you need?" I paused because her response might have been exactly what I wanted but I was still extremely surprised. I guess my luck has began to change I thought to myself. I smiled again but this time it was for real, I felt rejuvenated by not being looked over. My comments actually made a mark. “I need someone followed.” “I am a doctor…” I interrupted her right away, “use your husband’s contacts.” She frowned and began thinking to herself. “You are asking a lot,” she finally replied with less confidence then usual in her voice. I nodded and laid my hand on her shoulder, “I need this done. I am not asking as a patient I am asking as a friend.” She rolled her eyes at me and took my hand off of her shoulder, “I will do it but consider our doctor-patient relationship over and our friendship, ha-ha, well—we will see,” she said sarcastically but seemingly very angry at me for me asking her to do this. “We will deal with that later---I suppose,” I said with a small nod. She sighed and sat back down in her chair and crossed her nicely toned legs. "You are going down a path where it would not matter anyway." "What do you mean?" "Where you are going no doctor can help you escape. You say you need someone followed but we all know that after he is done being followed the whole matter will escalate," she said as she began tapping a pen on her left thigh. I walked a few steps away and leaned against the wall. I moved a few inches afterwards because of the proximity of my back to an expensive looking painting. "I have decided that I don’t care anymore. I want to see what is out there and I will not swayed by laws anymore. I will accomplish something in this life be it with my new venture or wrestling when I am finally medically cleared." She stops playing with her pen and stares at me, "yes a new venture that will no doubt lead you to places you will never forget or do things you will never forgive yourself of. I never told you to go down a path like this. I am all for you getting this confidence but now you are going to become a man you use to despise." I looked out her window and watched a dove fly by just missing the first drop of rain. I looked back at Dr. Lawson and nodded slowly. "I have thought of that. But really--who am I kidding?" I paused, "this world is run by those who flaunt money, and with that money they attain power. This power is not something they deserve.  They took it by buying every lawmaker. I am not going to sit back anymore and watch as everything becomes more expensive yet my wage does not rise to accommodate the prices. I grew up in a wealthy family but I was never spoiled and now I have nothing but fleeting beliefs that become more moronic every day I walk upon this planet. It is time for me to take what is rightfully mine and to find out answers I might not want to know. So am I worried about what might become of me in the future? No, I am worried about what would happen to me in the present if I did not change. Maybe I will become the antithesis of greed and selfishness--but at least it is my decision to make. You should know better then anyone seeing as what your husband has done." Dr. Lawson answered quickly going on defensive, "he is working hard to fix all those mistakes now, Steve." I laughed, "Yes, did he go to jail? No. Everything he did made him rich and powerful and one of the greatest wrestlers as well, right? Yes, so now he is trying to atone for past deeds---great. He would not be able to do any of that if he had not become so rich and powerful. Nice try." She shut up after that and thought to herself for several minutes. I stayed vigilant with holding up the wall but I had taken a few seconds out of my time to go mix a drink for myself from her office bar. I took a few sips and watched as she pulled out her phone and began dialing and I listened intently as she spoke to her husband and then a private investigator to put on my case. I continued to take small sips with each sip my smile became bigger and bigger. She hung up the phone and wrote a bunch of info on a sticky pad and handed it to me. It was all I needed to know about the investigator and when I would hear from him first about his job. I thanked Dr. Lawson and was on my way with a jolt in my step and a small buzz from the Crown Royal I had made for myself. I am not a heavy drinker. Either way the next few hours were nerve racking. I waited for that call. I needed this to be a quiet affair when I knew I would not be bothered. It finally came at 11:00PM and I promised the guy his payment would be sent out the following morning. I jumped in my car and drove, my whole body shaking in anticipation. I had no idea what I was going to do. I was sweating... I was thinking... I was prepared, but I had no idea how prepared I would need to be... Drive.
