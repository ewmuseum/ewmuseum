Manipulating The Masses - Vivica J. Valentine (GCW) (IG Round 18)

8 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Step away from the window / Go back to sleep / Safe from pain and truth and choice and other poison devils / See they don't give a fuck about you" -- Maynard James Keenan, "Counting Bodies Like Sheep To The Rhythm Of The Wardrums" Prelude: Insight To A Fear Stricken World "Dies Irae have come, they saw, and they have conquered. Taking an unsuspecting Global Championship Wrestling by storm, the stable known as Dies Irae lead by GCW Crusierweight Champion Shadow has a strangle hold on the championship situation. World Champion Dawn McFarlane, United States Champion Vivica J. Valentine, and Tag Team Champion Virgil Ambrosi round out the stable leaving only the Television Championship untouched in their reign of terror." -- Martin Freedman, Inside Wrestling Source Magazine "The startling revolution that has occured in GCW has shaken the very landscape of the company. The stars and legends of yesterday have been shoved aside in favor of an upstart group of renegade anarchists, bent on shaping GCW into their own image." -- Danny Osterman, World Wrestling Press "Shadow and her band of cult like followers have turned Global Championship Wrestling into an apocalyptic enviornment. With evil firmly in control, this looks as if it may be the end of the GCW we all knew and loved. Something has to be done about this, and rumor is from the inside... a resistance is slowly forming against the bullies formerly of Global Wrestling." -- Scott Jerusi, Weekly Wrestling Review "Rumor has it that recently backstage Dies Irae members have been talking to new talent hires by GCW and scouting their potential to join their forces against the forming GCW resistance." A large man with a dark brown by graying bearing and thick black glasses spoke, an echo accompanied everything he said due to the low income recording equipment furnished by the local public access station, "It may be too little, too late for Dies Irae. Their actions have infuriated the members of the GCW guard, and everyday it seems someone else have had enough of their arrogant actions. With all the titles in their control, their once simple message of armeggedon maybe have sparked a full fledged war. One in which I believe they are ultimately unprepared." With the quick flash of light the room was sent into complete darkness. The sound of a plastic remote landing on the coffee table interrupted the newly found silence. Standing up to her feet she stretched her arms high into the air as a yawn escapes her body. Opening her tealish cerulean colored eyes, she shook her head in disappointment before pulling her blonde hair away from her face. "Everyday another startling development about how evil we are." She said with a hint of sarcasm in her voice, as Vivica often spoke, "What a fucking joke." Proper language was never a strong point for the woman GCW fans knew as The Black Mariah, but then again neither was her concern for their opinions. Another day, another message. It was beginning to grow on her nerves as she walked quietly out of the entertainment room of the expansive estate known as Zenith Manor North. If only they'd ever take the time to listen to what they had to say instead of making quick assumptions based on the actions of their so-called heroes. As she made her way to bed, Vivica J. Valentine wondered what sort of hypocritical preaching she'd hear from the so-called voices of the public when the dawn of the next day arrives. Everyday is an adventure, tomorrow doesn't look to be any different. It's never any different. To do something different would involve change, and change is something today's world seems to fear dearly. Chapter One: Pulling The Strings Looking out from behind the dark tinted windows was like watching the television. Hidden observation of society, how people act when no one was around. Some people may consider it a sick fascination or vouyerism, but for Christian Zenith it was almost like reading. Long ago reading books had lost its entertainment value for him, and Vivica J. Valentine was beginning to find out why. This planet has been shown to her over the past couple months as a sick, sick world. A world where no one can think for themselves, a world where everyone needs someone to hold their hand and tell them what they should do. Self-help books, awareness groups, propaganda, television psychology... the general public ate all of it up. Why can't people come up with their own conclusions on things? Why do people need to be brainwashed by their televisions in which they pay more attention to than the God they hold so dear? The same people that pointed at her and called her a whore, told her she was sick. "Hypocites..." Vivica J. Valentine said out loud as the black limosine came to a stop, "I don't know why everyone can't accept who they are." "That's precisely the problem," Christian said as the door to their limo opened up next to him, letting the sounds of the big city flood their senses, "They're too jaded to know any better." With those words they pulled themselves out of the back of the limo. Sweet Home Chicago. The sun beat down on the duo as they became just two more members of the faceless masses that inhabit the streets. The shadows of the massive towers around them blocked their of the sun as they walked past and Christian Zenith pulled a pair of Black Oakley's out of his jeans pocket and placed them over his cadet blue eyes. Vivica looked around at everyone as they passed, her hands deep inside her pockets. "They won't even give us a fucking chance Chris..." Vivica said as a college aged man walked by her and store at her as he passed, she restrained her temptation to slap him upside the head to finish her sentance, "Since the day we showed up they booed and screamed. Following Seymour Almasy and Michael Stevens like a bunch of sheep... not even paying the slightest bit of attention to what we say." "People have a tendancy to fear what they don't understand." Christian said, stopping himself and Vivica in front of a small electronics store, "They hear you speak of revolution and self-realization. They lost their edge for revolution a long time ago. This is an age of fear, where everyone has to be kept in the dark because if they knew the truth everyone would panic. No one knows the outcome of revolution, no one knows what will be on the other side of self-realization." Behind Christian images on the televisions in the window shows pictures of terrorism on the news. A road side bomb is show exploding as the telecaster explains the casualties and explains the location of the event. Vivica J. Valentine looks around her as people gather to stare at the television and look in shock as the carnage unfolds in front of them. Looking back up at the man once known in Global Wrestling as The Midnight Messiah from under her golden locks, an idea begins to formulate in her head. "If there was just some way to get their attention, and look at me as who I am and not the enemy of their ignorant favorites..." Vivica said, unsure of how she would begind to do such a thing. "Why don't you go out to them?" Christian replied, cold and emotionless as he watched people walk past, "I'm sure there are hundreds of interviewers out there who'd love to get a one-on-one interview with one of the most controversal women in GCW." "All those reporters are the same..." Vivica said, wrinkling her nose a bit thinking all her experiences with the various perverts who call themselves journalists. You'd think they were interviewing her breasts. All they ever did was ask questions about Christian Zenith and what it was like being around Christian Zenith while staring with their mouths open at her chest. "But they listen to those jerkoffs." Christian said as they began to start walking forward again towards their destination, a high dollar restaurant just down the street a block or so away. "Maybe I'll talk to Lia about it..." Vivica said, pondering the circumstances around in her head. "Lia spends every moment of her career trying to get your word out." Zenith replied, looking back over at Vivica who still looked down at the concrete with her hands in her pockets as she walked by his side wearing a black baby tee with a pink LOSTPROPHETS logo across the chest, "You believe in her cause don't you? She's entrusted you to be her protege for some reason right? I think you're perfectly capable of handling this by yourself... if you want it enough." Perhaps their fascination with the television could be used to her advantage. The thing she'd been so aggressive towards working against her could be the very answer to her problems. But would they be ready to listen? Vivica nodded quietly, for once, responding to Christian's questions. As they approached the door to the restaurant a smirk began to grow across the face of The Black Mariah. The television inside was airing Round 17 of PrimeTime Central's Infinite Gauntlet, a series she remembered Christian taking his hand at during his Global Wrestling career. Maybe a fresh start was just what the doctor ordered. A good showing and maybe a little bit of air-time. All the scrambled ideas in her head began to fit together like a puzzle. Suddenly a group of guys opened the door cutting them off and one of them looked over at Vivica in surprise as he walked past. Whispering to his friends he pointed at her before speaking... "You're Vivica Valentine from GCW!" The frat boy said with excitement, "You better watch it, Eva VanLorne is going to kick your ASS." Laughing with his buddies he slapped five and began to walk away. Vivica snarled and went to follow before Christian put his hand in front of the United States Champion. Her greenish blue eyes narrowed as she stood, watching them walk away. Zenith's arm stood between him and the scorning he'd never soon forget, but quickly Vivica regained her senses. "Mindless fucking puppets..." Vivica said under her breath still glaring as they faded into the crowded sidewalk. "Just like you want them." TO BE CONTINUED...
