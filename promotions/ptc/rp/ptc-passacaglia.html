Passacaglia

3 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Mister Drazail?” A sympathetic voice entered his ears. More specifically, a sympathetic but distant voice. It had the sound of someone who had honed the unrivaled talent of faux-caring. But for some reason it made him feel a lot better. He started to run a mental spot checking of his body. His toes still wiggled and his hands still moved, he could still breath and couldn’t feel any obtrusive plastic tubes in his throat aiding him with that ability, he noticed his throat felt like it contained the exact center of a desert, and his eyes felt like they had sand glued to the inside of his eyelids. But he was alive. He opened his eyes slowly, letting the sterile white light flood into his retinas as easily as possible. The woman standing over him wore a nurses uniform, it was impeccably kept. The creases of her skirt stuck out as if they were painstakingly crafted into the material. Her paper red cross hat was almost comical compared to the neatness of the rest of her garb. She was a simple kind of beauty, dark almost caramel colored skin and eyes to match. He wasn’t injured enough to not notice her perfectly crafted legs. “Where am I?” The sound of his own voice startled him almost as much as the obvious cliché of his question. He remembered vividly that bastard of a deity wrenching the very ability to speak from his throat. It scarred his face badly… and Talena… what of his lover? Her sight was ripped from her. If she was forced to survive on only her erratic and incomprehensible second sight she would be driven even further from sanity than before. “You gave us quite a scare. You fell very far, but somehow you are without anything but a few scrapes and bruises. The news crews are going to want to speak with you when they find out about this.” She smiled, it was a cute smile but not overwhelming, “But to answer your question, you are at the Sisters of Divine Mercy hospital in Philadelphia. You were brought in a few hours ago.” The news seemed strange to him, he ignored the chance to ask more questions and also ignored the irony in the phrase ‘divine mercy’, “Was there a woman with me. With dark hair, almost as black as night. And nails that could perform an autopsy on a living person. Please…” The nurse seemed taken aback and saddened, “No. There was no woman injured. Was she a friend? Is there anywhere we could try and contact her?” Yeah, he thought to himself, you could try and contact her somewhere in between insanity and psychosis. “I have to go. I have to find her.” He tried to stand up but found that his arms and legs were strapped to the bed. “What the hell…” A frown formed on the nurses face, “You were combative when you came in. You punched an orderly and called him ‘Gabriel’. Than you tried to eviscerate a doctor. We figured you hit your head during the fall and were worried you would not only kill one of our staff, which would be bad since we are so low of them after the budget cuts, but also end up injuring yourself. We had to tie you down and sedate you.” DarkStar growled, he felt like a tethered animal. He tried once to snap the bonds, but his body was too weak. Either the sedation was still in his blood or his human body was still too feeble to attempt anything, like a baby deer after birth. A few deep breaths later and he was calm. “You don’t understand. I need to find the woman I was with. She isn’t mentally stable and could be in real horrible danger… or whoever around her could be in even worse danger.” The nurse wrote a few lines down on her clipboard and looked back to DarkStar, “We will see what we can do about your wife. For now rest. You’ve obviously had a rough day.” She didn’t know the half of it. “Wait, how did you know she was my wife?” Or maybe she did. “It says so in your patient files. Now shh. Rest Mr. Drazail. In the morning you should be strong enough for us to talk about this more.” With an inward sigh, DarkStar succumbed to her suggestion. He watched how she moved as she left the room and laid his head back onto the rocklike pillow he was assigned. After a few sleepless hours he grew restless. He couldn’t stop worrying about Talena, or the people unlucky enough to run into her. But this turn of events also tugged at his brain stem. How could he be so unfazed. Other than the fact he was strapped to his own bed like a criminal he was otherwise in good shape considering he had just lost a battle with God himself. Sadly, the battle was futile, and looking back at it from where he was standing now- he knew it. At least he made the attempt. Unlike those pathetic cannibal gods lording over what was left of their domains, he made an attempt. He wasn’t going to go out like the rest of them, he wanted to go out like a champ. Although he now had nothing to show for it but confusion and regret. There wasn’t much he could do tied up in a hospital. He couldn’t even feel the weak pulse of his former powers. Was this God’s ultimate plan? To make him neutered and useless as a God, but vital as a human? What better way to punish somebody but to give them back something they cherished broken and shattered. DarkStar was still dangerous, and even the short sighted God seemed smarter than to give him his life back without consequence… power or not. DarkStar tried to wrap his mind around this development, but was interrupted by a voice in the bed across from him. “Dream Lord- I would say you were well met but in a place like this, nobody is well.” He recognized the voice vaguely, it gnawed at the back of his mind but he couldn’t place it. “I am no longer the Dream Lord, I lost that title and I fear I lost my Queen. You are at an advantage though, for you may know who I once was but I do not even know who you are.” The man laughed, it was an unstable laugh. The kind that Talena would release after she read the stars in her own way. “Oh, what a kidder the former Lord of Dreams is- but not as big a kidder as I am. You know me well whether you care to remember it or not. My people long ago dubbed me the maker of mischief and the contriver of all fraud In your tongue and the tongue of the Earth now that mine has gone out of memory, I am called Loki.” That name registered with DarkStar. Loki was a demi-god and the sworn blood brother of the great norse lord Odin and the father of Fenrir the wolf who was destined to destroy none other than Odin himself. “Last I checked you were stuck under the ground with spider venom as your only friend. How was that going for you?” Loki spat. “I expected more from you, dream lord. I come to pay you your earned respects and I get mocked? No wonder you have so few friends left.” He let go of the straps holding him to his bed and walked towards DarkStar. He was in the shape of his true visage but slightly shorter. His long face was accentuated by the auburn beard that followed the cut of his chin. There were scars on his chest from millennia of exposure to a spiders venom. He seemed in good shape otherwise, but as a shape-shifter he could assume any good shape he desired. “I apologize, Loki, but I seem to be in predicament that doesn’t allow me very much courtesy… especially to those who had abandoned me long ago and are more than happy to live out their remaining time in the shadow of the cross bearers.” It was true, of course. The Norse were one of the first to stop paying heed to the Dreaming. All the God’s had their own problems, but of them all the Norse were the most proud and bitter. They still believed they had power, and as DarkStar became powerless they abandoned him and whatever small friendship they had. As he would hear later, Loki was the driving force behind the disrespect. DarkStar didn’t take it personally though, as Loki was the driving force behind most disrespect anywhere. “It is I that should be apologizing, oh former prince of the dreaming. I had you pegged wrong. We all considered you weak, yet here we now have news that you went against God himself with barely your wits and your kingdom behind you? That is a brave thing indeed, even if you were dispatched so simply.” DarkStar struggled against his bonds, he had never liked this trickster- in fact he viewed him as kind of a prick. “Listen Loki, if you came here to rub salt on my wounds than by all means go ahead and make it quick… if you don’t than I will get out of these straps and rip your face off ear to ear.” Loki laughed that mocking, sickening laugh, “You will do no such thing. You have no power. Especially in a place such as this. I did not come to rub salt in anything, however. I came to inform you that your destruction was not taken lightly. In fact, it was viewed as a bit of a wake up call. The remaining forces of Olympus are searching for the brothers who have gone lost, they plan to attack Heaven itself.” This news was a surprise. Had it been that his defeat was enough to wake up the rest of the dieing ancient gods? “How did news travel to you?” With a crooked smile Loki responded, “There is a traitor among God’s chosen. His name I will not speak, but he is faithful to our causes. Of course those that march to the gates of God’s kingdom will be destroyed, but it looks like you will get the war you have wanted from us after all.” The smile grew until it resembled more of a knife wound than a grin. “And all it took was your life.” “I feel fine Loki. Reports of my death have been greatly exaggerated.” He did feel perfectly fine, he would tell the nurse and get released, than find Talena and begin to figure out a way to regain his power. “Does this not all feel a bit strange to you, as a former king of dreams this shouldn’t be so easily accepted. But nay, this is not my room to talk. I have delivered my news and will now be gone, lest I be riven of my abilities in this hell hole. Good bye DarkStar Dream-kindred…” Loki’s body began to shift unnaturally and now he was no more than a hawk flying towards an open window. “I will not be seeing you again.” “Good,” muttered DarkStar under his breath, “I have met many Gods and many demons, but you give me the creeps.” Which brought him right back to his predicament. He still couldn't imagine why God would allow him to live like this. He felt the heat burn his throat and mouth, he could feel Talena's blood drip on him... and just like that he was healed? No. There was something more to this. He began to focus his attention on the leather straps that held him to his bed. As the Dream Lord he could have simply focused his ability on one and snapped it, within seconds he would be free. For now he would have to use mortal abilities. He began to rock his arm back and forth, trying to loosen the straps hold on his wrist. The leather began to loosen, but not before he wore his wrist raw and red. With a groan of effort and pain he slipped his hand out, nearly de-gloving the skin in the process. The rest was easier with his free hand, he began to systematically undo the rest of his bonds until he was free. With a triumphant grin he leaped to his feet, he found his old strength returning to his legs. His body became less weak as he became accustomed to his old human form. The mortality it held was both disturbing and refreshing. DarkStar flexed his arms and legs, trying to work the stiffness out. He began to walk towards the door of his room before something caught his eye. His medical transcript. The nurse said that was how she knew the woman he spoke of was his wife, but if she would have known that little detail she would have known the much more obvious and important detail of his mortality. In the eyes of a mortal filing system, he had been dead since 2003. He picked up the clipboard from the end of his metal framed bed and raised an eyebrow at its findings. There was nothing on it. It was no more than a blank sheet with a few pen marks from when the nurse took her notes. This was beginning to leave him slightly unsettled. What kind of hospital was this? The facts that he ignored began coming to him. DarkStar had grown up in Philadelphia, it was his hometown and the only human city he could relate to. In his 25 years in that city he could never once remember a Sisters of Divine Mercy hospital anywhere near it. He turned on his heels and walked faster towards the door, he would hazard being caught if it meant he could get out sooner. Any risk was worth this. First Loki and now this development? DarkStar was a former God, he knew when things were getting too strange. The door opened with a slight creak, it wasn't loud enough to cause alarm but he travelled as carefully as possible anyway. If there was any attack or attempt on him he had little to defend himself with. He was wearing a thin hospital gown, his back was exposed to enemies in more ways than one. He passed over scalpels and blunt medical instruments simply because if he was overreacting and this was a simple religious hospital, well than he would rather only explain why he broke his bonds and tried to escape than having to explain why he was wielding a deadly weapon. He encountered another strange fact, not only were there no other signs of doctors or nurses, there were no signs of patients. The entire hospital had an eerie quiet to it. There was no steady electric beeping, no bustle of people, nothing. With a hop, he quickened his pace. His stiff legs did him good enough, he partly wished he could attempt the InBetween, but if God had simply made a mistake in returning his voice and memories- well he wouldn't want to expose himself to that bastard so soon. The entire place seemed like a labyrinth, every corner felt like he was turning to a corner he already passed. Each door looked the same, every desk was uniform. The place felt endless. “Next I'll run into a Minotaur,” DarkStar half joked to himself. Now that he was talking to himself he wondered if he had inherited some of Talena's insanity. Oh Talena. He had to find her. Another turn, and his wish was granted. As he bounded past the white clean walls of the white clean hospital, he nearly ran her over. His feet skidded him to a stop and now he was face to face with his lover. She smiled at him. A smile devoid of psychosis. Her smile beamed with happiness, joy, pleasure, emotions that should be foreign to her- ... and her eyes! Her beautiful color changing eyes! They were pristine, still there. No scars or blood adjourned her face. She was as perfect as he remembered. DarkStar's eyes blurred, “Hello lover,” she spoke with a voice more eloquent than he ever imagined, “I missed you terribly. I never thought I would find you here.“ “Nor did I. Nor did I...” He hugged her close. Losing her after all this would have been a bitter pill to swallow. Now he had to deal with that nurse who lied about his wife's whereabouts. “There is something strange going on here Talena. I can feel it in the air.“ She changed the subject, nearly ignoring what he said, “Have you seen outside yet? It's paradise. I didn't think parks like that still existed.“ DarkStar looked at her slightly puzzled, “That isn't important. We need to get out of here. Something doesn't feel right about this place. Something feels... out of place, for a lack of better phrasing.“ She shook her head, “No, this place isn't out of place. It is paradise. Have you seen outside? Come on. I'll show you the waterfall.“ Before he could respond, he noticed something. Her shadow... it didn't fit her. It was taller, longer. Wherever it cast it caused a slight ripple, like pebbles in a pool. He began putting it together. With a lunge he threw his hands around Talena's throat and began choking the life out of her, slamming her head against the white floor of the whiter hospital. Red began to splatter the clean walls. Soon she began to choke out words telling him to stop. A hand fell on DarkStar's shoulder and pulled him off, nearly throwing him across the floor and into a stack of sterile gauze. “Under no circumstances will I allow you to cause harm to anyone under my word of hospitality, Dream Maker. No matter how puckish they may behave.“ The man glanced down disdainfully towards the battered and motionless body of Talena. “Get up Loki. You will gain no sympathy from me.” With his words, the form of Talena morphed away and seemed to melt into the form of Loki. His nose was busted and the back of his head bled copiously, but he was otherwise unharmed. “I was just trying to help the sucker. He doesn't understand yet. Odin forbid that I try to do something nice for once.“ The other man scoffed, he was wearing what appeared to be a very expensive red and black suit. It stood out against the blankness of the rest of the area. “Your opinion on 'nice' is an opinion I will never take, Loki Lie-Smith. I will now ask you to leave. You delivered your message so therefor my word has been fulfilled. I will not join Odin and Zeus on their suicide mission against Heaven, and since the Dream King has lost his crown I would suggest that you not even bother asking him. He has already failed once and would not be able to fail again.“ Loki scoffed at the comment, “He isn't the only one who already failed once. At least he isn't as much of a pussy as you are.“ The man scowled, “Leave before I reeve your soul from your bones and feed it to your Norse pantheon.“ Loki shrugged, he began to fade out but not before chiding the man one more time, “As if you had the power to do so. Whatever.” DarkStar was still reeling from being thrown back, the man stooped down and offered his hand to help the fallen king up. “Hello Drazail, we have to talk.” “Only after you answer some questions...” The man let loose a sinister sounding chuckle, “When you behold who I am, you will not so easily make demands. We have had dealings before.“ DarkStar shook the cobwebs out of his head and got his first good look at the man in the expensive suit. He had shoulder length blonde hair and his features were slightly feminine but were flawless, he was the definition of perfection. As he made eye contact with his deepening blue eyes he realized whose presence he had found himself in and cursed himself. He knew beneath that expensive blazer and designer dress shirt, the man wore two scars where his wings were rent from his back “Long time,” DarkStar forced, “But where would divine mercy be found in the halls of anything you lay your hands upon?“ With that the man snickered, and this time it wasn't sinister. It was warm and forboding, “Why Drazail, you should know that God is not the only one capable of offering divinity and promise. I will answer your questions in due time, and if I still deem it fit I may even offer you the mercy I am prepared to give. So for now you are safe.“ DarkStar was satisfied. He had no other options, without his powers he was stuck here, and he needed the answers of why things ended up this way. So although he was not entirely trusting of the man, he accepted his hand and was helped to his feet. One way or another he would find out the truth, even if he had to accept Lucifer Morningstar's divine mercy to do it.
