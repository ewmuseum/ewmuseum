Could a new brother mean new trouble?

28 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-28 October 17, 2006 1:23 PM Location: Mr. Wachter’s Gym "That’s it, set up the body shot with the left jab!" Mr. Wachter stood outside the ring yelling instructions to Semore Smoke who looked to be sparring with his partner Julius in the ring. Semore Smoke was working on his boxing technique, which if perfected could help Semore become a well-rounded fighter along with his wrestling background. Semore threw out a couple of jabs and then went in for a big right body shot that immediately folded Julius down to the mat. "Nice hit!" Semore took out his mouthpiece and leaned over to help Julius back up off the mat. "Sorry about that Julius, didn’t mean to hit you so hard." Julius looked a bit shaken from the blow but also looked to have taken it well. "Don’t worry about it, you need to go all out in this training session if you hope to win your bracket," Julius said as he tried to catch his breath. "I think it’s about time for us to take a break, these ribs aren’t going to stop hurting anytime soon." Julius wandered over to one of the corners and leaned back against the turnbuckles to try and alleviate the pain some. Meanwhile Semore walked over to the far side of the ring and sat down on the stool that Mr. Wachter placed in the ring. "So, did I tell you that that FBI agent called me about that murder case the other night?" "No, you didn’t kid," Mr. Wachter said as he handed Semore a bottle of water from outside the ring. "What’d they want this time?" Semore took a swig from the water bottle and set it down on the mat next to the stool. "He said that he was flying back here to ask me some more questions. He should be in town today so I wouldn’t be surprised if he paid me a little visit." Mr. Wachter, standing outside of the ring shook his head with every word that Semore spoke. "Kid, you’re standing on thin ice here," Mr. Wachter said, as apparently was thinking about the bloodied shirt he threw out for Semore soon after he returned. "Did you tell them everything that you knew, the truth of the matter?" Semore just sat there for a few seconds taking in the situation as he stared up at the lights of the gym. "You have no idea how much this has been eating away at me and you know better than anyone else that I’m not about to risk going back to jail right now. Especially not for murder, it’s completely bogus… but on the other hand I have to be able to help them out the best I can so I can clear my…" Before Semore could finish his sentence a loud knock was heard coming from the main gym door. "Must be the agent here to ask questions," Semore said as he stood up and climbed out of the ring. "I’ll answer it, you should go take a look at Julius. He doesn’t seem like he’s feeling too well after that last shot." Mr. Wachter just simply nodded his head as he made his way around to the other side of the ring to check on Julius and Semore got out of the ring to answer the door. Semore reached the door and opened it… but to his surprise he didn’t see the agent he expected… "Who are you? And why do you like me?" Semore looked at the man on the other side of the door and his jaw dropped slightly as it was like looking into a mirror. The man on other side had a much different expression he was smiling and even let out a small chuckle. "Simon, I’m your brother!" -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- October 17, 2006 1:35 PM Location: Rocky Mount Police Department "Did the guys up there in Detroit find a match to the DNA we were able to get from Mr. Smoke?" A Rocky Mount homicide captain sat in his office staring across the table at the FBI agent in charge of the Joanna Jones murder investigation. "Forensics found that the DNA matched that of a stray hair follicle we found on the victim. The best part about that evidence is that it places him at the murder as the hair was found in one of the victim’s stab wounds." The police captain sat back and nodded on every word that the FBI agent spoke and followed up a question. "Hmm… Did you guys ever find the murder weapon?" "No," Agent Ryan said as he shook his head. "We think he may have taken it with him when he came back here. Probably packed away in his luggage so it could be checked away and get past some of the extra security measures in the airport. We’re just hoping that too much time hasn’t passed for him to get rid of it. Did you send in for the warrant I requested before I left Detroit?" The captain nodded and handed Agent Ryan a piece of paper from his desk. "One of my guys dropped it off right as you got here, it doesn’t have any set limit as far as search parameters go so you have free range to take whatever you like from that gym. Mr. Smoke is on that last strike so if you find anything that is at all criminal in nature then arrest and him and we’ll trial him for that. I’m sure you would rather get him for murder, but might I remind you that with his record, if you even find an unpaid parking ticket, that’s twenty-five years in prison." Agent Ryan took a hold of the warrant and looked it over to check its authenticity. "Yeah, well you’re right. I’d rather nail him for murder if possible," Agent Ryan said as he looked up at the wall clock in the office. "It’s almost two o’clock, seems like a pretty good time to give our man a visit." The captain nodded and stood up. "It sure does." -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- October 17, 2006 1:45 PM Location: Mr. Wachter’s Gym "So what you’re telling me is that your name is James Deeds and we’re from the same parents and look exactly alike. The kicker in all that is that you were born five years before I was? I’m sorry, but this just sounds like the craziest shit I’ve ever heard." Semore and James whom he met at the door are sitting next to each other on one of the gym’s benches, which are used for spectators at the Saturday Night wrestling matches. "Yeah, it is kind of crazy, but it’s true," James said as he put his arm on Semore’s shoulders draping it around his neck. "Mom and dad had me when they were both in high school and I was put up for adoption. I found out who my biological parents were about fifteen years ago and went on a search for the rest of my biological family. Six years ago I first got a glimpse of you wrestling and I realized that you and I looked a lot like… follow that up with a lot of research plus tracking you down and you get a pretty fun story to tell." Semore shook his head, as he still couldn’t believe much of what his apparent brother was telling him. Semore saw Mr. Wachter approaching out of the corner of his eye and before Wachter could be within full view Semore asked him a question. "Do you think this guy’s really my brother?" "Well, the two of you do look exactly alike," Mr. Wachter said as he reached the two and sat down in a chair that was sitting in front of the bench. "But what I don’t get is if he’s five years older than you then why does he look exactly like you? The two of you aren’t twins you know." Semore nodded his head and looked over to James as if to ask the same thing. "Well that’s where the story gets fun," James said so to answer the unasked question. "I got curious about that myself and I talked to a friend of mine from high school who had become a geneticist and he told me that there is a chance that two siblings could look exactly the same but not be twins as long as they came from the same parents." Semore interjected here with the most obvious of statements. "Yeah, but such a chance has to be very slim to none." "It is," James agreed. "I believe my geneticist friend said that the chance would be the same as three raised to thirty thousand to one. That’s a huge exponential chance, it’d be like winning the lottery a hundred times over." Semore nodded, if only he had ever been that lucky at life to succeed he wouldn’t have needed drugs to try and motivate and move him along in his early years. It appeared that his parents had used up all the luck that any of their kids could have ever wanted just at conception. "Well, since you did all that research I assume you came across the part that said mom and dad were dead," Semore noted as he looked across the gym and saw Julius enter the main hall with his ribs bandaged. James nodded but then shrugged off the comment. "It means nothing now though, parents die before their kids, that’s a fact of life… or at least it should always be a fact of life. At least I finally found you and that’s pretty much all that matters to me right now." "Julius, those ribs all right?" Semore asked as Julius etched closer to the other three individuals in the room. "They should be fine. Mr. Wachter thinks it’s just a bone bruise so I’ll just keep them wrapped up for the time being. Did those cops ever come by here to ask you some more questions?" And as soon as Julius could finish his statement a loud knock was heard from the front gym door followed by the familiar voice of Agent Ryan on the other end. "Mr. Smoke! We’ve got a warrant to search the gym so you might want to open up!" James looked over at Semore as he obviously didn’t know about the legal fiasco that Semore was currently involved in. Semore simply looked over at his newly found brother and shook his head. "Don’t worry about it, this is just something that’ll blow over. I happened to be in the same place around the same time some woman was killed, so they’re going through their standard ‘harass the guy with a criminal record and then find out that he wasn’t involved’ routine." Semore stood up to walk towards the door and waved for Mr. Wachter, James, and Julius to stay put as he could handle the situation. Semore made it to the door and opened it up to see that Agent Ryan hadn’t come alone but had brought the homicide captain along with a few out of uniform personnel who must be crime scene investigators with him as well. "Here’s the warrant for you to look over," Agent Ryan said as he handed Semore the warrant and made his way into the gym. "It gives us free range to search wherever and for whatever we want." Semore read over the warrant and sure enough he didn’t notice any specific restrictions to the warrant so he simply handed it back over to Agent Ryan who immediately scanned the room and noticed the other three individuals. "Can I have everyone please step outside while we begin our search? I promise it won’t take too long so you won’t have to spend too much time outside in the dreary cold rain!" Mr. Wachter, James, and Julius got up from their seated positions and all three walked towards the door and out of the gym so the investigators could do their job. As James walked by the FBI agent, the agent turned and noticed that he looked exactly like Semore Smoke. "Wait. You there, what’s your name?" James stopped while the other two filed out of the gym. Semore stood by at the door to wait for his brother. "James Deeds sir. I just got in town and found my long lost brother." Agent Ryan looked at Semore standing in the doorway and then looked back at the man standing in front of him. "James Deeds huh? That name sounds a bit familiar," Agent Ryan seemed intrigued that the name rung a bell. "Oh yeah! Now I remember, you just got out of jail a few months back didn’t you? I bet crime just run in your family, doesn’t it? Does your ‘brother’ know about your criminal history?" James just stood there shocked that the agent would know anything about him that could be dug up just by hearing his name. It appears that whatever James had done in the past was big enough to warrant a file on him in the FBI. "No… my brother doesn’t know anything," James said in a much lower voice. "But when I get the courage to tell him what I’ve been doing for the last fifteen years, you can bet that he’ll hear everything." The FBI agent nodded as James finished his sentence. "Sure thing there. Well, I’m here to catch your brother, not you. You two might have a lot more in common than you believe." James just turned around and walked out of the gym visibly upset from the last statement made by Agent Ryan. "What was that all about?" Semore asked as James made his way out the front gym door. "Nothing," James said shaking his head. "Just one of those friendly chats that caught me off guard. But, it might be a good time for us to talk since the searching will take them a little while." Semore looked a little confused. "Talk about what?" -_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_-_- October 17, 2006 4:15 PM Location: Outside Mr. Wachter’s Gym / Inside Mr. Wachter’s Gym "Why didn’t you tell me that you had been in prison for fifteen years?" It had been a couple of hours since the investigators showed up to search the gym and they had yet to finish their thorough search. Semore and James had been talking the entire time outside while the investigators searched and James had just reached the end of his long story. "I was afraid you’d shun me since it’s nothing to be proud of, especially when you keep in mind why I was there." Semore shook his head. "You were defending yourself James, there’s nothing wrong with that. The guy was going to kill you and you just had to kill him first to protect yourself. Besides, you did the time and the past is the past. Lord knows I know that from experience." James nodded and hung on every word his brother spoke, as he knew it was the truth. "So, you never told me why these investigators are here." Semore chuckled as he looked around and made sure that no one else outside was in immediate earshot of the conversation. Julius and Mr. Wachter had left an hour earlier to grab something to eat and the two brothers just stayed behind to talk and wait for the investigators to finish. "Well, it’s not as long as your story was but it’s definitely not simple either. About a month ago I was in Detroit and this lady got killed after her and I hung out that day. I of course became one of the prime suspects in the investigation and apparently they’ve found more evidence that links me to her murder." James looked shocked as the words slipped out of Semore’s mouth. "Did you… you know… do it?" "I think the last person I’d need to explain myself to is someone who just got out of jail for murder himself even if it was in self defense," Semore said as he shook his head. "It’s just insane to think that I would be capable of murder after my life finally got back on track." Agent Ryan emerged from the gym and looked to be as giddy as a schoolgirl who just got straight A’s. "Mr. Smoke, can we have a moment with you inside?" Semore looked down at the ground for a moment to think about what the FBI agent might have found to make him so happy. Semore then looked at his brother as he started to walk towards the open gym door. "I’ll be just a second, mind sitting out here by yourself?" James nodded his head without a word and Semore walked through the doorway and into the musky gym. "Maybe you can explain this for us," Agent Ryan said as he held up a zip lock bag with a large knife in it. "We found this upstairs in your storage clos… I mean ‘pad’." "Yeah, it’s my old military issued knife that I kept as a souvenir after my discharge from the army, what about it? I’ve got a permit to own it and since I moved in it’s never left the room." Agent Ryan lowered the bag and stared Semore straight in the eyes. "The forensic guys tested it for blood, and they found traces of blood all over the blade. Ms. Jones was murdered by a weapon that shares a similar cut to the blade we found here, so is there anything that you’d like to say in your defense?" Semore just chuckled, as he couldn’t believe the absurdity of the newest allegation. "Didn’t I just tell you that that knife was military issued? I was in the first gulf war you dumb ass I used that blade against a lot of Iraqi soldiers, so of course it’d test positive for traces of blood." The FBI agent didn’t look amused by the excuse Semore just gave for the traces on the knife. "Either way, we’ll be taking the knife back to see if there are any traces of Ms. Jones’ blood on the knife. Also, can we get the clothes you were wearing that day?" "Sure," Semore said as he nodded his head. "They’re up in my room on the second floor. I’ll just go and grab them." Semore walked towards the stairway in the main gym room and then walked up it towards the door at the top of the stairs. Agent Ryan followed Semore closely to ensure that he didn’t try any funny stuff as Semore walked up the stairs. Semore opened the door to his pad and walked in to find that some of the forensic people were still in there testing all sorts of sharp instruments that he had in his possession. Semore ignored them however and walked over to a mound of clothes that sat next to his bed on the floor. He dug through the clothes and found the pair of pants he was looking for and then found one of his many PCW shirts that he had had in his possession throughout the years. "Here they are," Semore said as he handed the FBI agent his clothes. "They’ve been cleaned since I was in Detroit obviously, but your folks shouldn’t have trouble finding traces on them if there are any. I’m sure you have a security camera image of me from that day caught by the PTC office security cameras so you’ll know that those were in fact the clothes I wore that day." Semore was almost mocking the FBI agent now in his statements, as he obviously knew the process that evidence was gathered and tested for blood evidence and DNA evidence. The FBI agent didn’t let the comments get to him though. He just simply played along with the charade that Semore attempted to display. "We’ll run the tests here locally so I don’t have to be too far away from you Mr. Smoke. Oh, I saw your match against Sledge in the first round of that wrestling tournament. Good luck against John Covel in the second round. Of course, you don’t need luck do you Mr. Smoke?" Semore shook his head and chuckled as he walked towards the door and made his way down the stairs. "Of course I don’t need luck Agent Ryan," Semore continued to walk down the stairs as Agent Ryan followed. "I’ve got that hunger factor that most wrestlers talk about. John wants to be a baseball player, not a wrestler. He could lose this round and go have a successful baseball career playing for some shitty Major League team like the Chicago Cubs, but as for me. This is my chance to shine, my chance to prove that I’ve still got what it takes to participate at a high level." The FBI agent wasn’t surprised with Semore’s response. It appeared that the agent has already read Semore like a book and has grown accustomed to his quirks and mindset. "Well, good luck in that. It’ll be a few days before we can either clear you as a suspect or arrest you. Of course, you already knew that, and I get this odd feeling that even if you are the killer, we’re not going to find any evidence here that directly links you to the murder." Semore turned around and faced the FBI agent head on at the bottom of the stairs and had one of the more serious faces that Semore has ever emotionally shown. "Agent Ryan, you do know me more than you think you do. You wasted your time here this afternoon, but that won’t reflect negative on me, just negative on you. Good luck explaining why you wasted so much time in an investigation against me to your superiors later this week." The last statement was the first statement that had ever caught Agent Ryan off guard in his conversations with Semore Smoke. The FBI agent just stood at the bottom of the stairs with no further remarks as Semore walked towards the front exit of the gym. Once out of the gym Semore looked over at his brother and beckoned him to come close, as he wanted to whisper something to him privately. James obliged and walked over to Semore and Semore leaned into James’ ear. "I’ve got a secret to tell you, and I’ll only tell you because I trust you. It has to do with that murder in Detroit, it also has to do with something else I’ve had planned down the road. Let’s just say I’ve got a new plan that involves you, but it’s not safe to talk about now. Would you love to be richer than you could ever imagine? I bet you would. Well… now that I’ve got your attention, you did ask me if I had killed that young woman up in Detroit earlier, well… I did." TO BE CONTINUED
