IG29 - Climactus (FA)

9 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler: Climactus Fed: FA In the Second von Doom Universe... In the forest of plum trees and parakeets, the sound of footsteps on the leaves on the forst floor can be heard. The crunching sound comes closer and through the underbrush the source of the noise is found. There are four creatures that are making all the noise. They are: Veku, a mysterious karate warrior who is a cross of Eric Cartman and whoever that guy is from Kung Fu: The Legend Continues. He wanders around from place to place, getting into adventures, fighting bad guys, and making as many bastard offspring as he can with the women townsfolk in the villages he saves. So far, he has over 300 children. Cube Man, a weird creature that is the Borg cube from Star Trek with arms and legs. Can only communicate in sign language. Resisting any orders he gives would be futile...unless you have a Twix bar in your hand. Twix is his only weakness. Sir Chippy the Chipmunk, a knight from the chipmunk kingdom of Chipopolis that has joined the Cube Man and Veku on their adventures throughout the realms. Climactus, a giant being who was created at the climax of the last von Doom universe. He is an omnipotent being who is too smart for his own good. He will eat anything that makes him hungry. Our heroes are walking through the forest in search of buried treasure hidden on the sides of Mount Oh-Yeah-Harder-Harder-Yes-Baby-Harder. * * * In the real world... One year old toddler Eric is siting in his crip sucking his thumb asleep when he is awoken by his mom's screaming in the other room. He gets up from his slumber and stands up by the bars at the edge of his crib to listen in and here is what he hears. "Oh-Yeah-Harder-Harder-Yes-Baby-Harder-Fuck-Me-Hard-Cum-All-Over-Me-YES!" Eric sucks his thumb, not knowing what his mother is screaming about now. His father isn't here, nor has he been for many weeks. Eric's mother has had many daddies over to the house but she always just screams at them like this and they leave. 'Could it be because of me?' little baby Eric asks himself in his head. The answer to this question is harder for Eric to think than it should be. That's because he's not alone in his head. There's two people in there. The second part of his is none other than the crazed lunatic evil genius known as James von Doom! When the old von Doom universe was destroyed, the soul of von Doom floated back to Earth and was reborn in little Eric Constantine Waterford. Eric, born to a single mother who is very promiscuous, is not the host body that should have the demented mind of von Doom be fused with his. This is not the environment a lunatic like von Doom needs. He needs a loving and caring environment, not this insanity. But luckily for Eric, the only time von Doom's strange ideas and creations bug him are when he sleeps. But, unbeknownst to Eric or the rest of wrestling world who thought that they were safe from von Doom's insanity, Eric's dreamworld is actually a reality on this Earth. Eric lays back down on his crib and starts sucking his thumb. Soon, he goes back to sleep and the adventure continues right where it left off. * * * Back in the Second von Doom Universe... Our heroes pull out their treasure map and take a look at it. "Infinite Gauntlet?" Sir Chippy the Chipmunk asks, pointing at the map with his little chipmunk claws. "What is that?" Climactus replies, "It is an endurance contest used to see just who in fact is the toughest out of a group of people." "Oh," Chipy replies, a bit dumbfounded. "How do you know that?" "In the old universe," Climactus replies. "The creator lost every single one of the contests he competed in. In fact, his losing streak was the worst in the history of the known universe and all dimensions in it, even the Glorg Galaxy." "Eek!" responds Veku. "Our creator must be a moron." Climactus nods his head. "Yes he is," responds Climactus. "Not a single original idea. I myself am a rip off of a comic book character and Cube Man is a rip off of a..." Climactus stops as an eraser falls from the sky and onto his head. Climactus looks up. "Uh-oh," he says as clouds form and erasers start raining down on them! Our heroes make a run for it! "What the hell is going on?" Veku yells as they start running like maniacs out of dodge. "Our creator is mad at us for making fun of him and now is trying to erase us from existence!" Climactus yells as they all run toward an open meadow just west of their current position. Once they reach the meadow, the eraser rain stops. The four heroes look around, a bit dumbfounded. "Thank the creator," Sir Chippy responds. "We are safe!" Suddenly, a giant light beams down upon them. "Oh bugger," Sir Chippy replies as a giant glazed donut hovers above them. "Dammit!" Climactus exclaims. "It's those bastards from the Krispy Kreme Galaxy!" Suddenly, four Donut Holes with arms and legs armed with filling rifles beam down to the ground and start shooting donut filling at them. This makes Climactus hungry, so he grabs the giant donut UFO and eats it whole! Meanwhile, Cube Man starts doing an interperative dance. The donut holes just stand there and watch in horror, mesmerized by the dancing. They then start mimicing the dance. Cube Man starts slowly doing the dance faster. The donut holes cannot comprehend this. They all start having seizures before suddenly exploding everywhere! Sir Chippy raises his sword in victory. "We have saved Earth yet again my friends!" he says with glee. Climactus looks up toward the heavens. "I sense something more dangerous out there in space," he says. "something that could destroy us all." Cube Man, who can't speak, uses sign language to ask 'what is it?' Climactus replies, "A superintelligence never before encountered." * * * Back in the real world... Little Eric wakes up from his slumber and looks out into the living room through the door from his crib. His mom is just finishing eating a donut while watching a TV show on interprative dance. There are some donut holes on her plate. But they are not alone. In the shadows, there is someone else in the little apartment where they live. It's Darth von Doom, who laughs as he sees the baby. "HA HA HA!" he exclaims. "Soon, I will kidnap the baby and I will use his powers to recreate the new universe in my image! BWAHAHAHAHAHA!" Suddenly, the boy's mother walks over and grabs a hold of his lightsaber. "Oh," she says with a smile. "Nice and hard for me." Darth von Doom turns and glares at her. "Can I touch it?" she asks as she winks at him. Darth von Doom is enraged. "Nobody touches my lightsaber and lives to tell about it!" Darth von Doom growls with anger. Eric's mom seems turned on by this. "So are you going to fuck me to death big boy?" she asks as she licks her lips. Darth von Doom throws his hands up in frustration. "DAMMIT!" he snarls. "I can't even try to create the universe in my own image by stealing a baby with James von Doom's soul in it without something going wrong!" Darth von Doom then turns and storms out of the apartment, slamming the door behind him as he walks off to plan his next move. The end.
