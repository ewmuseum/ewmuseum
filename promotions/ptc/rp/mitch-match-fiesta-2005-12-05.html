Mitch Match Fiesta

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 [The milky white of overhead lighting and the hollow sound of a bare hand slapping canvas fills a darkened gym somewhere in Los Angeles. An ambiguous masked body lies crumbled and limp at an odd angle, as a young, muscular figure stands impatiently above him, stifling a yawn.] Mitch Mitchum: Are there any more? Come on, Biggs. I'm getting sleepy. [The stout, swollen-faced Mr. Biggs glares in Mitch's direction, checks his clipboard full of undercard wrestlers from various regional promotions, and nods his head.] Mr. Biggs: Yeah, we've got a couple more. If you'd shut your goddamn mouth maybe we'd get out of here faster. Remember, the second you get lazy in there, those bastards will tear you apart. [Mitch rolls his eyes and looks at his feet.] Mitch: Yeah, yeah, I know. Sorry, sir. Where's my opponent, then? [A set of double-doors burst open at the far end of the gym and an energetic lucha libre grappler runs full speed towards the ring. The camera pans back and forth between the advancing challenger and a lethargic Mitch, filing his fingernails and glancing every so often at his opponent. By the time the masked man reaches the ring he's moderately winded but no less intense. Screaming taunts in Spanish, he rushes Mitch with a flurry of tumbling manuevers and shadowkicks. Nonchalantly, Mitch raises a spotless black wrestling boot slightly above waist level and waits for the inevitable. The big boot connects with a sickening smack on the luchadore's face and stays elevated even as his opponent's legs give out from under him. Mitch remains with his leg up in the air, still filing his nails without having looked up to see the damage. Mr. Biggs, slaps his forehead and glares at Mitch. Mr. Biggs: Jesus Christ, kid. What the hell are you doing? [Without looking up, Mitch lowers his leg and throws the nail file outside the ring. With a sigh, he picks the limp body of his miniature opponent off the mat and positions him on his shoulders for a powerbomb. But instead of dropping him with a quick downward motion, Mitch arrogantly walks around the ring with the luchador, positioning himself in the corner. After a few moments, he takes off running from the turnbuckle, dropping the masked man on his head in stride. Mr. Biggs turns on impact and pulls a disgusted face.] Mr. Biggs: Alright, alright. Jesus. Cover him and let's get out of here. [Mitch drapes his frame across the limp body and waits for the three-count but it never happens. Looking up, he sees the ref outside the ring chatting up the custodial workers, ignoring the entire match. Without warning, Mitch leaps from his dormant position and slides outside of the ring, picking up a folding chair on his way.] Mr. Biggs: Oh, shit. Mitch, we don't want another lawsuit. Mitch! [Ignoring Mr. Biggs, Mitch cocks back the chair and swings for the fences, decking the janitor in the back of the head.] Mitch: Never take a break during a Mitch match. NEVER! Mr. Biggs: (Under his breath) Oh, shit. [Mitch picks the referee's unconscious body up in one fluid motion and tosses him over the top rope into the ring. Covering his opponent's still-limp body, he slaps the mat three times with the ref's motionless hand and stands up. Mr. Biggs, stunned and enraged, follows Mitch back into the locker room, tearing the roster sheet from the clipboard and crumples it into a ball.] Mr. Biggs: You think I can pay for every stupid outburst that you have, you no-disciplined piece of shit? I've got better things to do than to waste my time mentoring your stupid ass when you don't give a damn about anything, much less me, who has done nothing but… [Mitch suffers him gladly, patting him on the back as they're walking.] Mitch: Aw, fuck it, Biggs. Let's go bowling. FIN
