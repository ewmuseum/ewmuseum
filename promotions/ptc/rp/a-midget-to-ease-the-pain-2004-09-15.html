A Midget to Ease the Pain?

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 “I’ve Dreamt the Red Whale Song Hold the Moment Strong… Then the Moment’s Gone… I Never Can Get Enough. Again Without the Words, Again Without the Someone… Who’ll Afford Me Some… This is Me Hanging On…” :: Tossing and turning in a bed full of dying embers, the heat still infuses a sweat on the brow of the man that vacates the king-size bed. The curtains on the window flow like a disrupted bed of water as a chilled wind makes the sweat feel like ice. Biting his bottom lip, he rolls over, bringing the comforter up higher to cover more of his body. The dreamscape in his head has brought him in further than usual on this night. Tonight it feels all too real…the unforgivable acts…feel all too real once again. The name of the assailant escapes from his lips. :: “Alanna…” :: If he were to be awake at this moment, the name Alanna Rose would create a black cloud over the head of John Hitchin. A black cloud of lies and deceit that she infiltrated John’s life with. Lies and deceit that were going to tear John Hitchin and Dallas Reeves apart. Best friends torn apart by a woman…wouldn’t be the first time in history that it was to happen. And John loved her…he loved Alanna, which made the uncovering of her true nature all that more hurtful. The dreamscape seems to turn into a noisy plain… :: “VERRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRBATIMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM!!!” :: Sitting up in the bed, with his eyes wide, John snaps out of the dream. The cries in his house put a sort of panic into Hitchin’s train of thought. He throws on a pair of track pants, and flies out of his room. He looks to his left, into the den…no one to be found. Then John looks to his left, into his trophy room…no one to be found. Walking down the hall, he can hear the soft sobs of a whimpering human. Walking by a window, a beam of sunlight blinds the 6 foot 9 man. He covers his eyes and stumbles into a wall. :: Hitchin – “Mother fu…just my luck.” :: John shakes his head, then rubs his eyes as he continues his journey to find the noise-maker within his walls. Moving into the living room, John finds the culprit guilty of waking him from a dead sleep. A midget lay on Hitchin’s area rug in the fetal position…with a thumb in his mouth. His thumb of course. John is startled by this little person being in his house, almost falling backwards. :: Hitchin – “What the hell are you doing in my house?!” :: The little man doesn’t move…just whimpers more. Faintly…a word is heard softly escaping his lips. :: “Ver…bat…im…” Hitchin – “What the hell did you just say?” :: The midget shivers. Hitchin is outraged by all of this. He walks over to the midget, and picks him up by the collar of his shirt. The midget’s face is revealed, as Hitchin looks him in the eyes. Tears have dried on the face of the small man. Hitchin just looks confused. A face from his past has returned. :: Hitchin – “Mini Rivid?! What the hell are you doing in m…nevermind that…how the hell did you get in?!” :: Rivid’s face starts to go red. His eyes move, looking up…as if he’s trying to come up with some sort of a made up story. :: Mini Rivid – “I…uh…I came in through the chimney…yes! That’s it! I came in through the chimney!” Hitchin – “Came down th…you just made that u…what is this? I can’t even finish a complete sentence! If you came down the chimney, then how come you don’t have any soot or anything on you…huh?” :: Rivid smiles nervously. :: Mini Rivid – “You keep such a chimney…so clean…yes…clean…” :: Rivid takes his eyes off of Hitchin so that he doesn’t have to try and explain himself more. Hitchin shakes his head in disbelief. :: Hitchin – “First off, I don’t have a chimney…secondly, maybe if you said that you had climbed in through the attic window, it would’ve been a little more believable.” :: Mini’s eyes glow as he smiles. :: Mini Rivid – “Yes! That’s it! That’s how I got in here!.” :: Hitchin just rolls his eyes as he drops Rivid to the ground. :: Hitchin – “Oh please…” Mini Rivid – “Seriously! It is! I just didn’t think you’d believe me, seeing how I’m height-retarded and all.” :: Hitchin walks over towards the kitchen. Mini follows slowly behind him. :: Hitchin – “Honestly…I don’t think I want to hear how you got in here…” :: John opens the fridge and grabs a Molson Canadian beer. Rivid sees this, and is astonished. :: Mini Rivid – “The NGW Champion…drinking at 10 o’clock in the morning?! This can’t be good for public relations!” Hitchin – “Well after finding you in here…I think maybe I should have a few more…just get o…” :: Mini Rivid’s eyes widen as he stops Hitchin in mid-sentence. :: Mini Rivid – “NOOOOOO! Don’t make me go back out there!!!” :: Hitchin takes another big gulp off of his bottle. :: Hitchin – “Not only do I not want to know how you got in…I don’t want to know why you came to me.” Mini Rivid – “There’s an evil hamburger chasing me out there!” Hitchin – “Jesus…I knew I should’ve just left you on the floor weeping…” :: Mini Rivid runs to Hitchin and grabs him by the bottom of his shirt, pleading with John. :: Mini Rivid – “Just don’t let him get me!!!” :: Hitchin looks out of his second-floor window, and down to the street below. There he sees the local burger joint, and a person dressed up as a giant hamburger, promoting their latest offer. :: Hitchin – “An evil hamburger? And just what did this hamburger do?” :: Mini Rivid looks up at Hitchin upset, almost with tears in his eyes again. :: Mini Rivid – “What did he do?! He took my precious Verbatim away from me!!!” Hitchin – “What the eff is a Verbatim?” :: Mini Rivid starts sobbing on Hitchin’s shirt. :: Hitchin – “Fine…I don’t want to know…and whatever, you can stay here ‘til the evil hamburger’s shift is done.” :: Rivid’s eyes light up. :: Mini Rivid – “REALLY?!” Hitchin – “Yes…but…just stay out of my way, I’ve got my own heartbreak to get over, as well as a match to prepare for…” Mini Rivid – “Oh thank you!” :: Rivid wraps his arms around Hitchin’s leg. Hitchin just shakes his head as he finishes his beer. :: Hitchin – “Yeah whatever…I’m not a homo-phobic man…but…just…please stop humping my leg.” :: Mini releases his grasp, and acts like nothing happened. :: Mini Rivid – “Sorry.” :: Rivid opens the fridge. :: Hitchin – “And especially don’t touch any of my food…” :: Rivid closes the fridge, sad. :: Hitchin – “…freak.” :: Hitchin leaves the room, and heads back to his bedroom. He closes the door behind him. Rubbing his eyes, John starts to think about the dream he had. :: Hitchin – “Great…as if getting over the girl that tore my heart out of a chest isn’t enough…I’ve got a big match coming up that I need to get ready for…and I’ve got a runaway midget in my house…just great…this is just going to be fine and dandy.” “CRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRASH!” :: Hitchin closes his eyes as a crash is heard. Rivid is also heard faintly swearing. :: Hitchin – “Damnit…what did he break? I should just go feed him to that damn burger. I need to get my mind off of Alanna…off of what she did to me. I need to get my mind off of that damn midget that is probably going to break everything in my house before he leaves tonight. I need to prepare for my match, I don’t wanna go into the damn thing unprepared. I don’t wanna be knocked out of this damn Golden Turnbuckle before I can get my feet wet.” :: Hitchin looks over to the mirror in his room. His reflection looks back at him. :: Hitchin – “I sure do talk to myself a lot more than I used to.” :: Hitchin just stares at himself, the mirror image seems to be somewhat mocking him with its looks. Then it speaks to him. :: “You do talk to yourself more…you’re lonely. You’re fuckin’ useless. You lost the best thing that EVER happened to you. The GTT…the NGW World Title…your life…it all means nothing now. You’re just a pathetic loser…and that’s all you’ll ever be.” :: Hitchin rubs his eyes, as sweat forms on his face. He shakes his head as he breaks from the small daydream that he was caught in. :: Hitchin – “I really…need to get out of here.” :: John grabs a shirt and socks, putting them on. He reaches over and gets his gear, and heads out of his bedroom. Hitchin moves down the hallway, and surprises Rivid, who quickly puts a fish in his mouth, as he cleans up the water and glass of a broken fish bowl. Hitchin just shakes his head. :: Hitchin – “I’m out of here for a bit…got training to do…don’t burn the house down…” :: Rivid just smiles and nods his head, not wanting to open his mouth. Hitchin just looks at him, and then goes towards the stairs, walking down them. He stops at the front door, and looks up to the railing on the second floor. :: Hitchin – “Oh Mini!” :: The midget walks over to the railing looking down to the door. :: Hitchin – “That’s a baby piranha in your mouth…” :: Rivid’s eyes widen as he spits out at small goldfish. The fish falls all the way from the second floor, in almost slow motion, as the little fella meets his doom…smacking onto the hardwood floor of the foyer. Hitchin looks back up at Rivid, after looking at the fish. :: Hitchin – “You owe me $1.47…ass.” :: Hitchin opens the door, and slams it behind him. Rivid feels bad, and then get an idea, running down the stairs and opening the door. :: Mini Rivid – “Johnny! I’m coming with you!” :: Hitchin is at his car, and just talks to himself. :: Hitchin – “Damnit…why can’t he just leave me be to grieve about my problems?” :: Rivid stands at the door smiling. :: Hitchin – “Fine…but if anyone sees us in public, and asks…you’re just my seeing-eye midget…now get your ass into the car.” “Once Read a Mind ‘til Dawn, Let the Poem Hum, On Emotion’s Rolled Tongue, I Never Yet Had Enough. Are We OK? Are We Open Yet? Again Without the Someone, And this is Me Hanging On… I’m Hanging On… I’m Hanging On… AND THIS IS MY WORLD!” :: The 1978 Corvette speeds down the road. Windows down, and other cars moving out of its way. Hitchin seems focused on getting to where he’s going. Mini Rivid sits next to him in the passenger seat. Bobbing his head to a tune on his mind. He reaches towards the stereo; John hits his hand away. :: Hitchin – “What are you doing?” Mini Rivid – “I was just gonna…” Hitchin – “No, you don’t touch anything in this car. Nothing! You’re lucky I didn’t just strap you to the back axle and just left it all at that.” Mini Rivid – “Why are you getting so angry? What…did you lose someone close to you or something?” :: Hitchin looks at Rivid, and then back to the road without saying anything. Rivid is surprised that he’s actually right for once. :: Mini Rivid – “Oh my god…Hitchin was in love…” Hitchin – “Leave it alone…” Mini Rivid – “The big man with a heart, it’s such a wonderful thing.” :: The come to a red light, Hitchin looks over at Rivid, angry. :: Hitchin – “Listen you slimy little Oompa-Loompa, yes…I lost the best thing that ever happened to me. But she wasn’t right for me, she was trying to tear a friendship apart...she just wanted control. She needed to go. It wasn’t until that I had already won the World Title that I realized what her true intentions were. She was going to try and take control, and move in to become the main shareholder in HitchBomb Industries. A multi-million dollar company that’s only getting bigger…I don’t know why I didn’t see it before…” Mini Rivid – “Hitch…” Hitchin – “…I can see that she was going to control me, and make me miserable…but now that I don’t have her, I’m miserable anyway. So what gives? I should be happy that I didn’t throw away my life like that…but now…but now it’s just a bunch of bullshit! AND I CAN’T TELL IF I WANT HER BACK, OR IF I WANT TO DIE… Mini Rivid – “…Hitchie?” Hitchin – “She was the best thing to ever happen to me…” Mini Rivid – “Hitchin! Green! GO!” :: John looks forward, and steps on it as he flashes his eyes to his rear-view mirror…seeing about a dozen cars backed up behind him. :: Hitchin – “Thanks for letting me know before I pissed anyone off behind me…ass.” Mini Rivid – “Well it’s not my fault! I can’t even see the mirror on the side! I’m a midget remember?!” Hitchin – “So what…now you’re sight-retarded?” Mini Rivid – “Well I never!” Hitchin – “Shut it…we’re here…I got this damn match to train for.” :: The Corvette pulls off of the highway, and into the parking lot of the gym that belongs to none other than Lance Legacy. The car parks in the empty parking lot. Hitchin gets out and Mini follows suit as he does as well. John looks around the parking lot. :: Hitchin – “What the hell is going on here?” :: Hitchin walks up to the doors, they’ve been chained shut by the handles. The sign on the door reads “For Lease”. Hitchin shakes his head. :: Hitchin – “What the hell happened here?” “It’s all gone!!!” :: Hitchin and Mini Rivid are startled as a homeless man emerges from behind a nearby garbage can. :: Mini Rivid – “AHH!!! It’s a shrubbery!” :: The homeless man keeps moving towards them. :: Hitchin – “Who are you? Where’s Legacy?” Homeless Man – “I’m Lance…I’M LAAAAAAAAAAAAAAANCE!” :: The homeless man starts moving a little quicker, less than 15 feet away from Mini Rivid now. Hitchin heads for the car quickly. :: Hitchin – “Just take the midget! Leave me alone!” :: Hitchin jumps in the car and locks the passenger door. Rivid turns around with a look of fright, as John starts the car, and speeds off. :: Mini Rivid – “HITCHIN NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” :: The homeless man becomes smaller and eventually disappears in Hitchin’s rear view mirror. :: Hitchin – “Jesus…what the hell is this all about? This day is just so messed up…I’ve gotta get my head on straight so I can focus on my match against CJ. Fellow NGW’er…but in the end, just the stone that will release this boulder…allowing it to pick up momentum, and destroy anything in its way.” :: Hitchin scratches his nose before taking a corner, a corner right into a huge traffic jam. :: Hitchin – “Well this is just great…” :: John looks out the window, just soaking in the sun in the car. :: “It is great isn’t it? You don’t have the right mindset, and you’re going to lose your first match in years. You’ve got actual competition, and you wear your heart on your sleeve…you’re nothing…Alanna was everything…” :: Hitchin looks up at the rearview mirror. Again, his reflection talks to him. :: “She was the only thing in this world that could truly put a smile on your face…and you just threw her away like she was yesterday’s garbage. You just got rid of everything that you had going for you. It was a fluke for you to defeat Dallas Reeves…he had your number. You’re washed up Hitch…IT’S OVER!” :: Hitchin starts to lose it a little. :: Hitchin – “I’m thirty-six and zero in NGW…I’ve broken the only records that matter in that fed…I’ve no equal there…I’m not washed up…I’m at the top of my game.” “You’re at the top of nothing. The only reason you hold that title around your waist is because you’re afraid to fight Chris Johnson. You’re afraid to fight the man that can talk your heartbreak, and push you right to the point of wanting to kill yourself. So you’d lose your love…and you’d lose your prized possession. You’d be pathetic…wait…you ARE pathetic. You are nothing anymore! You’ve lost it…you lost Alanna, YOU LOST HAPPINESS…YOU LOST PURPOSE!” Hitchin – “SHUT UP!” :: John throws a fist at his rearview mirror, snapping it off. The driver in the car next to him is staring at the situation going on in the white Corvette. Hitchin looks over at him. The driver is just shaking his head. Hitchin rolls down the window. :: Hitchin – “WHAT?!” Driver – “Nothing…” :: Hitchin in a rage, grabs the mirror, and throws it at the man’s car. :: Hitchin – “QUIT STARING AT ME THEN!” :: The driver abruptly looks forward, shocked that this muscular man has just thrown part of their car at his car in anger. The traffic starts to move, as John starts to move towards his secondary destination now that he has managed to ditch Mini Rivid. Speeding down the highway, Hitchin sees his pull off. Going down the exit ramp and towards a smaller road. The ‘Vette moves along the road, making another turn. Hitchin is on his way back towards his house. It comes up on the horizon. But he doesn’t go home, he pulls into a little place that sits across the street from his house. He parks the car and gets out. :: Hitchin – “Burger World…that blasted midget won’t follow me here…not with the evil hamburger protecting the premises.” :: Hitchin chuckles and shakes his head in disbelief. Closing the door, he makes his way into the restaurant. Hitchin orders his usual, and sits down. Starting to unwrap his burger, Hitchin smiles, as his day has finally calmed down. The stress is starting to subside today, but tomorrow will be another day, and the thoughts of his Alanna will come back, and the stress will return. For right now however…John just wants to enjoy this nice…meaty…half-pound…full of grease…hamburg…:: “And you thought you could leave me!” :: Hitchin sighs as he knows the voice. Mini Rivid sits up beside him, all dirty and slightly bruised. Hitchin’s eyes widen. Rivid sits down across from him. :: Mini Rivid – “So you thought you left me high and dry back there with Hobo Dan now didn’t you?” Hitchin – “I…who…what…how?” Mini Rivid – “Remember saying that you were going to strap me to the back axle…” Hitchin – “That’s how you got here?!” Mini Rivid – “No…but that’s not what I’m here to talk about.” :: Hitchin just rolls his eyes, as he takes a bite out of his burger. Chewing it, he just shakes his head, thinking about how much more ridiculous the day could truly become. He looks over at Rivid, who doesn’t have a very impressed look on his face himself. :: Hitchin – “Fine Tattoo…let’s hear it.” Mini Rivid – “We’ve gotta take out that damn greasy fiend that took my…my…” Hitchin – “Your…?” Mini Rivid – “Verrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrbaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaatim!” :: Hitchin rolls his eyes. :: Hitchin – “Not this again…” Mini Rivid – “We have to do this!” Hitchin – “I don’t think so. No, we’re not going to do anything about this. It’s your loss, I’m sorry.” :: Mini Rivid starts to get upset. :: Mini Rivid – “Is that so? Well, how about I go to the police, and tell them how you were mistreating a minority, and threatened to beat up a homeless man?” Hitchin – “What? I didn’t beat up any homeless man! I may mistreat you, but there are good reasons behind that! I’d never beat up a homeless man, let alone touch one!” Mini Rivid – “They won’t know that! And five bucks in my pocket to that man says you did beat him up.” Hitchin – “You wouldn’t…” Mini Rivid – “Try me.” :: Hitchin sighs. :: Hitchin – “Fine…we’ll go figure this entire thing out…AFTER lunch.” :: As he said, Hitchin finished his lunch…while the dirty little man watched him. It was time for them to get up from the table though…it was time for Hitchin to get to the bottom of this “Verbatim” mystery. What is “Verbatim”? Perhaps it’s a prized possession he had while he was growing up…and this mutant hamburger man had taken it. Or perhaps it’s the name that he has given his special place…and the man in the burger outfit was just a sick bastard…either way…Hitchin is going to find out. John and Mini get outside, and look at the Burger World Mascot. :: Mini Rivid – “Now don’t be fooled…he’s quite menacing, not to be taken lightly.” Hitchin – “Yeah…whatever…” :: John walks up to the mascot. :: Hitchin – “Excuse me sir…” :: The mascot ignores Hitchin. :: Hitchin – “I’m talking to you boy!” :: The burger turns. :: Burgerman – “What do you want?!” Hitchin – “I believe you had a run in with, the little person over there…” :: The Burgerman looks over at Mini Rivid, striking fear into Rivid’s heart. Mini runs over behind a garbage can, hiding. :: Burgerman – “Oh yeah…him. He’s walking around dropping CD’s on the ground, and well…I have to walk in bare-feet, cause well…hamburgers don’t wear shoes…” Hitchin – “Excuse me…CD’s…” Burgerman – “That’s right…CD’s…he dropped one, and I stepped on it…almost split my foot right open.” Hitchin – “I see…” :: Hitchin shakes his head and walks back to Mini Rivid. :: Hitchin – “A CD?! It better have had film of the actually shooter of JFK on it.” Mini Rivid – “My VERBATIM!” Hitchin – “Damnit! What the hell is that?!” Mini Rivid – “Um…the…uh…company that…uh…made…” Hitchin – “Are you telling me that it’s some stupid media company?” :: Mini Rivid bows his head. :: Hitchin – “You have got to be shitting me…” :: Hitchin starts to walk away from Rivid, towards his car. Hitchin gets in, and starts it up, driving out of the parking lot, across the road, into his driveway. Mini Rivid yells over to him. :: Mini Rivid – “It was the last one I had!” :: John gets out of his car, locking it, and walking to his door, opening it, and slamming it…leaving Mini Rivid across the road, continuing to yell. :: Mini Rivid – “Hitchin!!! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! LOOK…I…I…I HAVE YOUR $1.47!!! HITCHINNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN!!!” :: Mini Rivid stands there alone…except for the “evil” Burgerman who keeps glaring his big googly eyes at him…those…damn googly eyes. :: That Night :: Hitchin looks into his desk, pulling out his personal journal. A midget had invaded my house, a midget that I’ve known for a long time. The only thing I don’t get is why he came to me…we’ve never gotten along. Maybe there’s something he wants from me…there better be, cause he wasted way too much of my time today. Found out Lance’s gym has closed down for reasons unknown.
