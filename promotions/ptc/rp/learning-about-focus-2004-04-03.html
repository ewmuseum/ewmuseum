Learning about Focus

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-03 For the umpteenth time in the last half an hour Dawn McFarlane made a halfhearted attack at the wooden training pole before her. Her gloved hands danced nimbly between the jutting cylinders that made it as much an exercise in concentration as agility, and howled like a wounded cat as she missed the beat by just a fraction and roughly grated the side of her hand against the unforgiving wooden surface. “Son of a b*^$&!” she screamed, instinctively bringing her hand up to her mouth to suck away the worst of the blood welling up from the mistreated skin. She quickly peeled the fingerless glove off of the offending hand and headed to the small sink set into the corner, running it under cold water more out of habit then anything else. She cups her hands under the clear running stream and splashes the water against her face, gasping as the chill liquid brings a pink tinge to her nose and cheeks. She reaches out to turn off the tap and rests her hands on either side of the porcelain sink, staring at her reflection in the mirror. The woman staring back at her has become a familiar sight to longtime WMW fans, and is only just beginning to make its debut on Primetime TV. Shoulder length black hair hangs just past her shoulders, having grown out somewhat from the more conservative cut she’s used to. The frosted blue bangs currently hang before her face, creating a dripping veil of sweat and water before a pair of startlingly clear emerald green eyes. She swipes her dripping bangs out of her face and turns it to the side, inspecting some invisible spot on one pale cheek. Though flushed her skin is still pale, overtones of her usual alabaster complexion showing through. “Looking for some subtle imperfection the rest of the world can’t see?” Dawn’s head snaps back to face the mirror, the shadowy form of Corvus looming large in the right half, just over her shoulder. “Not exactly. Just…looking.” She turns to face him, letting go of the sink and leaning back against it with her arms folded over her bare midriff. Corvus himself is reclining against the doorframe, arms crossed, looking, for all intents and purposes, like he just stepped out of some gothic backdrop. “Looking for anything in particular? Or just indulging in some narcissism?” Dawn flushes and runs a self-conscious hand through her hair, plastering it back against her scalp. “No…I’m trying to find focus.” “What do you mean?” “I-I’m having trouble getting focused, having trouble getting really motivated here…” Corvus raises that expressive eyebrow, one corner of his mouth quirking up, “Having trouble getting motivated? How hard can it be to get motivated to do well in this tournament? I know you want this Dawn, you haven’t talked about anything else since you got your acceptance fax…what’s really going on?” Dawn turns her side to him, gnawing on her lower lip as she hugs her arms to her belly, “It’s Tessa…Tessa Windsor. I’ve been watching her promos, going over her past stuff and watching some of the footage from her old matches…she really wants this.” “So what? So do you.” “I-I’m not so sure of myself. This is a woman who’s worked her butt off to get where she is, including working past an asshole ex-fiancé and juggling an adopted child…I guess I just feel like…she deserves it.” Corvus shakes his head, pushing himself away from the doorframe and striding across the room towards her, “No. Don’t do this to yourself Dawn, not again. I won’t let you tear yourself apart just because you’re finally putting yourself in a situation where you have to really fight for what you want, I refuse.” Dawn spins on her heel to face him, eyes flashing, “So what if I am?! What have I done that deserves a spot in this tournament huh? What did I ever do that makes me so god dammed special?” Corvus stops at arms length, again folding his arms over his broad chest. “You tried. It’s nothing more complicated then that Dawn. You tried your damndest and you fought your hardest to try and get here…don’t let yourself choke now just because you feel some kinship for the woman you’re about to step into the ring with.” Dawn shakes her head slowly, “I…I just don’t know. I’m confused and it’s making it difficult to focus on what I want to do. I’m ‘this’ close to dropping out altogether, just leaving this to Tessa Windsor and Bob Temp.” Corvus takes another step forward, reaching out to grab one bare arm in his large hand, “Oh no you don’t.” “Hey, you’re hurting me.” “Then use it to get that focus you’re looking for. You say you want to give up just because you’re unsure that you deserve to be here? If you quit and walk away then you defiantly don’t deserve to be here. There are at least a hundred people out there who would kill to be where you are now, and they certainly wouldn’t be thinking of walking out just because their opponent has had it rough. You want proof that you deserve to be here Dawn? Cold, hard proof? Win.” “What?” “The best way for you to prove that you deserve to be here is to win this thing, and win it big. Show all of the skeptics, and all of the critics out there that you’re Dawn McFarlane and you’re serious about being a member of WMW and a member of PTC. Most of all you need to show yourself that you can do it, you need to win this for you. You’ve been down on yourself ever since Darwin’s Revenge and it’s prevented you from really excelling…well this is your chance to break away from that. Don’t tell me you can just walk away.” Dawn drops her gaze, finding some intensely interesting spot on the floor and studying it uncomfortably. “How can I? How can I prove a win out of the best Cruiserweights from all over PTC when I can’t even focus enough to finish a basic routine?” Corvus lets go of her arm, and with one heave of his massive shoulders, drops his dark coat to the ground. He rolls his neck slowly to each side, eliciting a series of nail biting pops and snaps from his protesting vertebrae. “So forget the routine. You want to focus? Want to really get your blood pumping? Then lets spar.” Dawn’s head snaps back up and she regards him incredulously, “You’re not serious are you?” “I’m dead serious. You’ve been trying to get yourself back on track with your martial arts, and I spent quite a lot of time in the orient during my younger years. I think it’s a perfect opportunity to get yourself back on track.” “I don’t know…” “Well you don’t really have a choice.” Corvus explodes forward in a flurry of movement, snapping of a series of fast left and right hands towards Dawn’s arms and torso. She makes a strangled noise and barely brings her arms up, taking several hasty steps back to bring her out of the larger mans superior reach. She rubs a spot on her bare stomach and fixes him with an angry glare, “You hit me!” “That was nothing and you know it. If I’d really been trying to put you out there then you’d be lying on the mat.” She growls something low in the back of the throat and advances on him, launching her own series of strikes at his chest and stomach. He easily blocks the light blows with his forearms, shuffling back slowly to avoid being knocked off balance by her assault. At the last moment she spikes a high crescent kick at his head and he weaves backwards, bending slightly at the lower back and recovering into a lower stance, “See? Now there’s the focus you were looking for.” Dawn drops into her own split legged stance, snapping her arms up and opening the palms so they run perpendicular to the mat below. “Anger isn’t focus at all, it just makes you sloppy.” “Then don’t be angry, just be ready.” She comes at him again, this time starting with a right side ridge hand and following it up with a close elbow. Corvus steps inside the ridge hand and catches it in his left hand, putting his back to her stomach and heaving her up over one shoulder. She hits the mat with a woosh of exiting air and rolls away from him, coughing as she tries to regain her breath. Corvus smirks again and assumes the same stance, beckoning her to him like they’re showing off for millions on the silver screen. She slaps the mat in frustration and comes back to her feet, snapping a trio of kicks at his knee, hip and head as she charges forward. He steps back to avoid the first kick, taking the second on the bone of his hip and catching the instep of her foot as it speeds towards the side of his head. Dawn recovers quickly and spins on her right leg, lashing a left footed enzugiri towards the other side of his face. He ducks his head forward and takes it on the shoulder, leaving her on her back with one leg over his shoulder. “Familiar position?” he smirks. Her jaw hits the floor as she turns a brilliant shade of red, “You, you! Gah!” She sputters out, lashing out a quick left handed jab that catches him on the joint of his chin. As his head snaps back she rolls her left leg up to meet it, catching him between both legs and whirling him down to the mat in a modified head scissors. She takes a deep shuddering breath and glares at the man trapped between her calves, “You’re trying to goad me, make me angry.” “It’s working too. You should be more focused,” he says with a wink, reaching out to tap her lightly on the ass. She shrieks something at him and tries to spike a heel kick down at his head. As soon as the head scissors is released he rolls away, coming up quickly to his feet and reversing direction so fast it clearly catches her off guard. He catches her just above the hip as she gets to her own feet, tackling her bodily to the ground and landing a series of stiff rights to the stomach before she gets her guard up. “If.You.Can’t.Focus.Then.They’re.Going.To.Rip.You.To.Shreds.” he grunts between punches, moving quickly to straddle her midsection. Dawn takes a shot to the side of her head as she plants her hands on the mat behind her, sliding between his legs and driving a solid knee into his lower back. He grunts in pain and jerks forward, allowing her to squirm out the rest of the way and land a light slap on HIS ass. He rounds quickly and she winks at him, “Turn about is fair play.” He grins from ear to ear and rolls up to his feet, settling down into another stance as she draws herself up into a kick boxer’s position. “You know that leaves your leg open.” She winks again, “You gotta catch it first.” He edges forward slowly, feinting at her with his hands to draw her attention away from his feet. As he draws in range he drops low and tries to take her legs out with a spinning sweep kick. At the last moment Dawn flips backwards like a gymnast, snapping both feet into Corvus’ chin and landing on her hands before catapulting herself backwards onto her feet again. Corvus comes back up quickly and spits a globule of blood onto the mat. Her face softens and she lowers her hands, “I’m sorry, I didn’t mean to…” Corvus charges and takes advantage of the her lowered guard to land a solid shot to her gut, following it up quickly with a downward chop to her back as she bends forward. She hits the mat gasping and he follows it up with a big elbow, Dawn only managing to roll away at the last second. She rises into a crouching stance to find him standing with his hands on his hips, waggling one finger at her. “Shouldn’t do that, I TOLD you I wasn’t holding back.” She charges at him, firing a sharp kick at his stomach and trying to capitalize with a spinning back elbow. He blocks the kick smoothly and sweeps her legs out during the spin, catching her like a dancer dipping his partner. “Didn’t think I’d fall for my own trick did you?” She glowers and snaps her head forward, landing a light head butt on the bridge of his nose that causes his eyes to water, and her to hit the mat like a sack of hammers. She rolls in, hitting his knee with a chop block and then snapping a kick into the back of his head when he drops to one knee. She then begins spiking kicks at his head and shoulders from a standing position until he laughingly cries out, “All right, all right!” She backs off with sweat dripping from the point of her nose and regards him curiously as he gets to his feet his large shoulders shaking with laughter. “What’s so funny?” “You, and how quickly you change moods. You still think you want to quit?” She shakes her head, splattering sweat to either side. “No. I’m going to stick this out, one way or the other.” He grins and offers one arm, “Good, now lets go get some ice before we bleed all over your nice carpets.” She slides under his arm and wraps one of her around his waist, the two of them heading out of the training room and towards the kitchen.
