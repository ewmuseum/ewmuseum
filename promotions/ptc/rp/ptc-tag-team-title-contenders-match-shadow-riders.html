PTC — Roleplay

PTC Tag Team Title Contenders Match

17 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Shadow Riders’ RP Title: Return to Greatness (The scene opens in the living room of the plush Miami Beach bachelor pad of the Shadow Riders. In the room Jason Kage lies relaxing on a black leather couch, Playstation 2 controller in hand, as he plays a game of Spider-Man 2. Across from him, Eric Russo sits on a lazy boy recliner as he quietly reads the latest edition of the PWI Insider magazine in much the same attire. Suddenly Russo tosses the magazine onto the glass coffee table in front of him as he exclaims.) Russo: Fu(k!ng dirt sheets! (The camera quickly focuses on a shot of the cover of the magazine and allows us to see “Metallo Meltdown in store for the Shadow Riders?” written in bold black letters under the glossy picture of the Metallo Zord unmasking at LOW’s Pulse. The camera view now focuses back on Russo as he continues.) Russo: Can you believe they actually have the nerve to say we should’ve stayed as the Metallo Zords!? That we actually had a better chance at winning matches as them!? We’re the same friggin’ people! How the hell could we have a better chance at winning matches based on a gimmick!? (Knowing that Russo is about to launch into one of his usual rants about the unfairness of the wrestling business when it comes to him, Kage presses the pause button on his game controller. He then swings his legs of the couch as he pulls himself up to a sitting position.) Kage: You had to know this was coming Eric. We’ve never gotten praise as the Shadow Riders no matter what we’ve done. Did you really think it would change now? It’s not like it matters though. I mean look at what we have done even with all of the naysayers constantly detracting from our abilities. (Russo shakes his head in disagreement as he responds.) Russo: But the past doesn’t matter. This entire business comes down to what we can do here and now. It’s all about how people perceive in the present. Sure we’ve done a lot in the past, but today is today. If people don’t believe that we can go toe to toe with the best teams out there, what’s the point of being a tag team? I’m getting tired of fighting for respect when I know full well it’s never gonna be given. (Kage responds questioningly.) Kage: Are you saying what I think you’re saying? You . . . Eric Russo . . . a founding father of the Shadow Riders, is worried about what other people think. Your questioning your place in this team because of comments made by a dirt sheet? (Russo shakes his head, then responds.) Russo: Hell no, I’m just saying maybe we’ve done all we can do as a tag team. I’m saying that unless we do something to actually make people take note of us and soon, then maybe it’s time we put our tag team career on the shelf for a while. We had a great run as a team, hell one of the greatest runs in history, but maybe it’s time we branch off into some singles stuff. Really give our individual merits a chance to shine. (Kage shakes his head in disagreement as he responds.) Kage: No, I’m not ready to pack this team in just yet. I know we haven’t even come close to reaching our peak. We need at least one more good run. We owe ourselves that much, we have to at least try it before we close this chapter of our careers for good. (Russo slides down in his seat, pessimistic about the future.) Russo: But for what? We’re already the LOW tag team champions for the third time. There’s not much else to do from there except lose the titles purposely just so we can win them back again. Seriously there’s nothing left for us to do anymore. We’ve done it all already. (A sinister grin slides across Kage’s face as he comes up with an idea that he knows for sure Russo will agree to.) Kage: What about the PTC tag team titles? We haven’t done that yet. (Russo perks up in his seat, as he tries to pull Kage away from the idea.) Russo: C’mon, don’t do that to me. Don’t try and pull me back into the team mentality with the idea of going for the PTC tag team titles again. (Sensing Russo’s weakening, Kage pushes the idea a little more.) Kage: C’mon, think about it. Homo/Phobic is gone so we won’t have to deal with their antics again. We don’t have to wrestle like a couple of goodie goodies any more. This time there’s nothing standing in our way. We just have push through the qualifying match that would be nothing more then a formality. Then it’s onto the title match and the Shadow Riders becoming the next PTC tag team champions. (Russo stands up from his seat to look eye to eye with his partner as he responds.) Russo: You know, you really are a bastard pulling something like this. Just when I think I’m out, you pull me back in. (Kage smiles.) Kage: Is that a yes then? Are we PTC title bound? (Russo nods his head in agreement.) Russo: As they always say, third time’s the charm. (Kage and Russo each reach out their hands to shake the others and seal the deal.) Kage: Let’s show these kids what tag team wrestling is all about. (Russo and Kage continue to shake each other’s hand as the scene fades out.) ***********************************LATER*********************************** ~~~{With victory in mind, Russo and Kage set out to where their tag team history began. If they were to truly return to the ring as the Shadow Riders, they would once again have to learn how to wrestle like the Shadow Riders. Over the year that they had spent as the Metallo Zords, Russo and Kage were forced to suppress their killer instinct and penchant for violence. Doing this however came with a price. After so much suppression, the good nature of the Metallo Zords’ in ring style had become second nature to the two Shadow Riders. It would take a lot to bring back the Shadow Riders of old, but Russo and Kage were up to the challenge or so they thought.}~~~ (The scene opens to a daytime shot of a rundown old gym set in the center of a city block littered with bars and what appears to be less then ideal restaurants. At this point, a limo pulls up in front of the decrepit building. The driver of the car quickly gets out and runs around to open the back passenger’s door. Eric Russo steps out of the car saddled with a large duffel bag which is slung over his shoulder. Jason Kage slides out of the car behind him holding a bag of his own. The two men dreadfully look up at the ominous figure of the building from their past where they once spent so much time preparing and perfecting their abilities. As the two men continue to stare up at building, Kage begins speaking.) Kage: It feels kind of weird being back here after all these years. Do you know what I mean? Russo: Yeah, I never thought we would have to come back. Let’s not get all sentimental just yet though, we’re here for a reason. (As Kage nods his head in understanding, he responds to Russo’s statement.) Kage: You’re right, lets go talk to him . . . (With a hard swallow, Russo and Kage enter the building. The inside of the gym is packed wall to wall with different wrestlers honing their skills with different exercises and exhibition matches. The thuds of bodies hitting against the mats and yelling from people perfecting the art of interviewing livens the room. It makes it almost pulse as if it had a life of it’s own. Music with a significantly hard beat blasts through the speakers hanging from the ceiling. The music adds a background sound to the clang of bells signaling the start of the matches, grunting wrestlers giving everything they have to show up their opponent, and the clank of weights. At this point, Russo and Kage spot the man that they are here to see. The head trainer of the gym and mentor of the Shadow Riders, The Snake, stands watching a match take place in one of the many rings in the gym. He wears a baggy black sweat suit with a red trim and a picture of a snake embroidered on the right breast of the jacket. Russo and Kage slowly walk over toward the Snake who is now busy giving his views of the sport’s current state to one of the wrestlers inside the ring. As the Riders move in closer, they begin to pick up the conversation between the Snake and the young wrestler.) Snake: Look here kid . . . I don’t care what anyone else thinks. Any news is good news, if you can’t make the people love you then make them hate you with a passion. As long as you’re getting a reaction out of people then you’re doing something right . . . (Nonchalantly the Riders walk up to their mentor as Russo cuts into the conversation.) Russo: Hey Snake, your two favorite students are back? Happy to see us? (Kage immediately follows up.) Kage: Yeah, it’s good to see you. It’s been awhile. (The Snake slowly turns his head and looks at Russo and Kage. Seemingly uninterested at the presence of his protegees, the Snake responds in his normal gruff voice.) Snake: Did you say something? (A little uneasy now due to his mentors reaction, Russo responds.) Russo: It’s Eric and Jason, we came to see you . . . (Russo is cut off early by his mentor.) Snake: Do you see that I’m in the middle of a conversation here? Well do you? (Slightly backing down, the Riders respond.) Russo and Kage: Uh . . . yeah. (Taking on an even more short tempered tone, the Snake retaliates.) Snake: Good, then just stand there and wait till I’m done before you come and bother me with your asinine questions about if I’m glad to see you and kissing my ass about how good it is to see me. (The Snake turns back to the other wrestler as he continues expressing the views that Russo and Kage interjected themselves in the middle of.) Snake: Now as I was saying . . . the wrestlers in the big time are good, yeah, their real good when it comes to wrestling but their not the best they could be. I’ve trained quite a few big names before they got big in the past but now I have a guy that is gonna leave them all in the dust. I mean you’ve worked with Supernova here in the gym . . . he’s got the stuff it takes to be the best in the business. He has a killer instinct to go along with his ability . . . that is your biggest asset these days kid. The guys up in the big leagues, they’ve lost that mentality. They don’t have the will to fight for what they want anymore. They just sit back and wait for things to fall into their lap. Just follow Supernova’s lead and you’ll do just fine, he’s the one you should be emulating. Well enough of my ramblings, get back to work. (The young wrestler walks off to go continue his workout in the ring as the Snake turns his attention back to the Shadow Riders.) Snake: So are you two just gonna stand there and beat around the bush by asking me stupid questions or are you gonna let me know what’s really on your minds? I know you two are here for a reason since I haven’t heard from you once in over a year. But I’d really like to know what you want because I’m a busy man and I don’t have the time to be wasting it waiting for you two to get to the point. (Perking up and putting on his best show of confidence, Russo responds.) Russo: Well Snake, we came here to see if you would help us get back to our roots. We could really use your help to get us back in the Shadow Riders mentality. (Kage joins in the request.) Kage: Yeah, we are kind of out of practice with being ourselves thanks to that whole Metallo Zord thing we did for the past year. Do you think you could help us? Roberts: Easy enough to answer that request. The answer is no, I got people who need me more right here . . . like Supernova over there. (Russo and Kage turn to get a look at Supernova who is hard at work having an exhibition match in one of the other rings. Supernova looks like a younger, pumped up version of the Shadow Riders with an even worse attitude problem. He takes liberties with his opponent making sure to savor every cry of agony that escapes their body. Still watching the show that Supernova is putting on in the ring, the Snake begins speaking.) Snake: Just look at that kids face. That determination . . . that hunger . . . that killer instinct. Supernova has the drive to become a champion, you two . . . well you two decided to become a joke. A team that will cater to the fans just for some time in the spotlight. Kage: What do you mean cater to the fans just for some spotlight Snake: Look Jason facts are facts. I’m running a training facility to make the stars of tomorrow. I’m in the business of manufacturing the next wave of stars. Plain and simple, Supernova gets my time because he is the future of the wrestling and you two aren’t. I spent enough time training you two already, it’s not my fault you tossed that away to play intergalactic dress up. (The Snake pauses for a moment as he looks over too another young wrestler who is hard at work on the weight bench. He then glances back at Russo and Kage with an unchanging expression as he pulls a silver whistle attached to a long rope from around his neck. The Snake begins to twirl the rope around his fingers as he looks back to the young man finishing his set up on the weights. He begins speaking to the Riders once again.) Snake: Look you two, I’m gonna lay it all out for you the best I can. When I look at you, I can see that you still have the talent. The fact is though that talent can only get you so far. Somewhere along the way you lost the killer instinct that is a mark of my students. You don’t have anything that sets you apart from every other tag team out there anymore. The only thing special about you two these days is your ability to fool people that you seem so proud of. Well my suggestion to you is to enjoy that ability . . . keep what legacy you’ve already built for yourself as a tag team and go your separate ways. But do it now while you can still go out near the top. (Acting as though he did not even hear the Snake’s last comment, Russo tries to cover himself.) Russo: Look Snake, are you gonna help get us back up to what we used to be or not? (Seeing that Russo is trying to brush off his comments, the Snake decides to take a more up front approach.) Snake: Tell me you two . . . you ever think about giving up on the tag team wrestling? (Taken aback by the question, Kage answers.) Kage: Well . . . we . . . uh . . . no. Roberts: Well, maybe it’s about time you two start. (Shrugging their shoulders at the conversation they just had with their mentor, the Riders turn and start towards the front door of the gym realizing that they are not going to get any help from him. As Russo and Kage continue towards the entrance, the Snake leans over to the gyms janitor who approaches him with a mop and pail. The Snake points to the two Shadow Riders as he speaks to the man.) Snake: I’ve trained those kids since they were eighteen . . . they’ve never lived up to their true potential as a tag team. It’s a real shame. (Hearing this comment finally cause the Shadow Rider’s normal confident exteriors to crack. Russo and Kage dejectedly walk away from their past and the scene fades out.) ***********************************LATER*********************************** ~~~{Their hopes of going back to their roots destroyed, the Shadow Riders returned back to Miami. How could they possibly hope to succeed as a vicious tag team with their own mentor turning his back on them saying that they had lost their killer instinct? It was surely not a good sign for future events that were set to unfold. For the first time ever, Russo and Kage were both beginning to question their future as a tag team unit. Would their time as the Metallo Zords, a high point of their tag team careers, actually end up leading the Shadow Riders to their demise?}~~~ (The scene reopens to a shot from the inside of a gentleman’s club along the famed South Beach strip in Miami. There is a slight haze of smoke in the air that is made even more noticeable by the streams of brightly colored lights flashing from the stage. The camera begins to pan around the room until it focuses in on the Shadow Riders who are sitting at a corner table in the club. Now joined by fellow Shadow Rider, Luke Justice, we can see that the three men are having some sort of a conversation. The details of the conversation are however being obscured by the sound of the thumping music playing inside of the club. The camera begins to move in closer without drawing attention to itself. As it does so, we begin to hear bits and pieces of the conversation that is taking place. Russo’s voice is the first that we hear as the camera focuses on the table. He is wearing a short sleeve, black, silk shirt tucked into a pair of black dress pants. A half smoked cigar is hanging from his mouth. As Russo’s lips stop moving, Kage’s begin. Kage is wearing a skin tight, short sleeved, black t-shirt tucked into a pair of black jeans. Luke Justice just stares on and watches as the other two Riders talk. As the camera continues to move in, the conversation finally begins to become audible.) Kage: I can’t believe the Snake just turned his back on us like that. Maybe you were right when you said we should hang it up Eric. (Russo semi-reluctantly nods his head in agreement as he responds.) Russo: I was hoping I was wrong, but if the Snake won’t help us get back what were missing we have no chance of getting anywhere in the PTC tag team division. (Hearing his two friends talking about giving up on what made them famous seems to infuriate Justice who bursts into the conversation.) Justice: That’s bullshit! (The sudden outburst by Justice quickly gets the undivided attention of the other two Riders. He then continues.) Justice: I have watched you two dominate the tag team ranks for the past four years! Seeing what you two could do as a team when you set your minds to it is what made me want to be a Shadow Rider in the first place! I refuse to just sit back and watch you two throw in the towel just because some washed up old has been said that you lost your instinct. You two haven’t lost a damn thing. If you don’t call putting masks on for over a year just to make the fans and every single wrestler in the LOW locker room look like a fool the act of a sick and twisted mind, then I don’t know what is! (Kage cuts in.) Kage: But we’ve come up short every single time we’ve went for the PTC tag team titles. What would make this time any different? (Justice quickly fires back with a response to the question.) Justice: The past two times you were spread too thin. People were afraid of you so you were loaded down with other things to do as a handicap. Think about it, the first time you went after the PTC tag team titles, you became the first men ever to compete twice on the same PTC Pay-Per-View card. You went to the final four of the tag team battle royal on the card and still went out and took Homo/Phobic to their limit. The next time you got a shot, LOW made sure you had to wrestle one match for them to match every one you wrestled for PTC. Is it any wonder you came up short? You two were pulling twice the load of any other team out there and you still took each and every one of them to their limit. (A small smile creeps across Russo’s face as he begins to see the logic in Justice’s statements.) Russo: You know something, you’re right. Every single time we get put to the test in a straight up match, we come out on top. We’ve outlasted most teams to pass through this business and we’ve managed to stay right at the top while doing it. Where are the other teams that people toted as the greatest now? I don’t see Feral anywhere. Homo/Phobic fell apart too. Even the great Q-Factor couldn’t out last us. (Kage begins to get into the same mind set as Russo and Justice as well.) Kage: We can do this. We’re the Shadow Riders and it’s about time we made people remember exactly what that means. It’s about time that they remember that they are all . . . (Knowing what Kage and Justice are waiting to hear, Russo speaks up.) Russo: Nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders. Oh and that’s not ego talking, that’s reality. (As the Shadow Riders order a round of drinks for the table to celebrate their return to the mind set that they thought that they had lost. the scene fades out.) ~~~{With their confidence renewed, the Shadow Riders were primed and set to head for the PTC tag team titles. Would their goal be realized? Could they really recapture their glory as a tag team? Well, that is a story for a different time and a different place but one thing is known for sure . . . life may end, but legends never die.}~~~
