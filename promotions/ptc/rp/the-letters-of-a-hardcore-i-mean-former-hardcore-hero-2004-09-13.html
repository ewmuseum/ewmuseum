{The Letters of a hardcore… I mean former hardcore hero}

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-13 Dear Sarah, Quite frankly Sarah today I was going through the motions of the moment. It's been a day full of twists and turns exciting me more building up the anticipation my heart doesn't desire for my return to wrestling. Never have so many events in my life molded so well together almost like a jigsaw puzzle today has brought me to a conclusion. Come high water, hail or snow I will step back in that ring and I will wrestle again. If ten self-hired men have to carry me down to the ring throw me in and stand by to make sure I kick my oppositions ass then so be it. It all started at nine this morning when I decided to go get the car taxed. I put on my coat because I don't know if they let you have windows in your prison but it's raining cats and dogs down here. Picking up my beanie I headed to the Post Office not evening caring to notice it was a Thursday morning. Turning up at nine sharp there was a queue out the door. Pensioners lining up to receive there weekly money, old men waiting to tax there cars and twenty year old woman banged up with three children and a husband you most likely know in jail stand adjusted waiting for the cashiers to serve them. Feeling slightly out of place I joined the queue between two old men, they seem to know each other giving one another a glance as if I wasn't even standing between them. 'Morning Jim' the man in front of me groaned. He was short and stumpy his eyes seemed to bulge out of his head slightly. He wasn't that old maybe fifty a former pot addict who signed up for war under the influence of drugs and got his leg blown up. He used a couple of crutches to keep him up his right leg cut at the knee. Trying to be self my eyes rolled to the side so I could get a glimpse of this Jim character. This Jim was much older being an expert I'd guess 75. Ok, I lie the dude had a free Television license application form in his hand. He didn't look frail like the other man he looked confident what he had left of hair was slick back with gel. He possessed he walking stick in his right hand and wore a gray suit his suit was clean almost to perfection only the rain outside tarnished it from being immaculate 'Pete old chap, nice to see you again. Where were you last week then?' He replied in an abrupt manor. He had this small English twang to his voice you can only hear in the American movies where they worship the country as if it was a little village or a brother you put make up on. They rabbited on like old woman for about ten minutes until the queue reached the front the man in front of me got served by the same lady as I. When he went to leave however the man dropped an envelope out of his pocket. Seeing it fall I quickly chased after the man but he had gone, losing my place in the queue I walked on down the road in the direction I had seen him go. Right there I wrestled for the first time not in a ring however but with my conscious whether or not to open the envelope and have a look inside. I thought about it for a while pondering then realizing I had nothing to lose I ripped the envelope open, inside was two tickets to South Coast Wrestling’s ‘All Action’ event. I knew where the show was, I knew who was on it hell I’d have wrestled with quite a few of the guys on card actually come to think of it the promoter wanted me on the show but I had chickened out. I shoved the ticket in my pocket as the lady behind the desk barked like a dog for my money due to the huge queue behind them. Ok so I have these tickets I have them in my pocket there’s a show in the evening like hell was I going to go but I did don’t ask why. Maybe it’s because I’m stupid. The closer I got the arena the more I could remember, my brain was finally remembering Sarah, I was seeing the chair, the woman’s face, the wire, the fire even my blood after the chair shot. I could see his face it was distrust he didn’t mean to hit me, he didn’t mean to hurt me I have disliked him all this time for nothing, I guess that’s why I went I knew he was on the card my sub conscious couldn’t take it any longer. After hours of slagging him off to the media I had to confront the heartless son of a gun who I had claimed to do this to me… My Brother!!! The day was starting to age slightly and I decided it was time for some dinner so I headed on out without you Sarah I have been eating takeaway solid for three months. Anyway I was walking down the road when a man bumped into me he was clearly frightened. The guy was shaking with fear as he hit me, I looked down at the distressed little man, and his thick black side parted hair complimenting his bulging eyes. The man stepped back looking up at me in ore. ‘Jamie’ the man muttered… I looked down without recognition of the man my memory blank. My blank stare informed the man I didn’t know who it was. ‘It’s me Mat.’ The man said, if he was a woman you could imagine him opening his hand out wide waiting for a hug by Mat wasn’t he used to be ruthless, he used to cutting edge the man of all men who always got what he wanted with the money, the woman and with his wrestlers. Yes he was a promoter Sarah, the man who got me the break into the game. ‘Oh my god, Mat is that you.’ I replied trying to act shocked. Just before Mat could reply there was the sound of a gun, he looked timid and worried. ‘Here you go Mat meet me tonight at the SCW show.’ I reached into my pocket pulling out one of the two tickets. I pushed it out and handed it towards Mat his eyes looked down on it. ‘Haven’t you heard?’ Replied Mat! ‘What?’ I asked wondering why Mannion wasn’t jumping at the ticket. ‘I went bankrupt and owe a lot of people money Jamie, if I was there a lot of people would have my guts for garters.’ I was shocked, I didn’t show any emotion but Mat did his head dropped knowing the shock I was experiencing. No wonder why Mat Mannion was so timid several 300-pound monsters want his head as a trophy in their homes. However Mat’s hand lunged forward snatching one of the tickets out of my hand. He ran at full pace disappearing into the train station across the road. I watched Mat dodge passed people, then turning around I bumped into a man. I didn’t look up my head was buried in the man’s breast’s. For some reason these breasts felt familiar, I had been here before. My eyes rolled up ever so slowly until they where locked on the man’s eyes he was three inches taller than me, his breath still stunk and his eyes where still spaced out of this world. This guy was the stoner of the wrestling world. The great Pyschoduck was in front of me the tall giant of a hardcore wrestler I shared many a bout with. His hair was still short and black but was a little more faded then all those years ago, tut listen to me it’s only three I make it seem like I’m an old legend coming back out of retirement after fifty years. His words still had little meaning to anyone other than the people who knew him at first I couldn’t understand any of the dribble that spurted from his mouth. His lips where drawn out from all the sucking he had done over the years and I don’t just mean on those joints, if you get me Sarah. ‘Errr a ong rime na z….’ Translated in English… ‘A long time no see.’ After a while he explained to me how tonight he was fighting for the SCW Heavyweight Champion against Mont. He explained he was chasing down Mat because Mat fled the scene five months ago owing him 3k after Mat promised to pay him the money for putting out Mat’s long time nemeses Alan Khan but when the authorities found out three weeks before it was meant to happen Pyshcoduck was finned 10k and before Mat was charged he had ran. Then Pyshcoduck was banned for three months and is now hated worldwide by wrestling fans and people for setting up the end of a fan favorites career for money. Ok so I’m baling now let’s cut to where the real amazement was situated in my day. The clock struck seven and I was tucking into a sweet steak dinner while Eastenders was about come on Television when the ticket almost screamed at me. The wrestling was calling me, like a zombie to blood I was in need. Not even switching off the Television or putting my dinner away I speedily grabbed my shoes and coat making for the door almost forgetting the ticket only to return to the ledge. I arrived at the arena to see the event was half way through the first bout and that the Southampton Guildhall was sold out for 1.500 people. Never before had I seen this in the UK it was a phenomenal atmosphere. What amazed me dramatically where the two workers in the ring neither holder then twenty where doing moves I had never seen. Cutting powerbomb’s into 360-dive sunset flips just watching it made my jaw drop. I almost believed to myself for a moment I couldn’t return to the ring, these youngsters would run rings around me making me look like a fool. The evening went on and just before the main event Mat Mannion crawled into his seat next to me. He wore a luchadore mask to cover his identity from Pyshcoduck who generally had the biggest heat with Mat. The Main event as stated earlier was Mont Vs Pyschoduck for the Heavyweight Title the belt of all belts a Championship I once dreamed to hold. The two wrestlers where fighting a fierce brutal unforgiving battle waging back and forth across the four corners of the arena. After twenty-two minutes the two reached my section of the crowd, three rows back. Looking down at Mat Pyschoduck looks perplexed his eyes remember. He lunges his hand forward around the neck of Mat. Lifting Mat up in complete shock I was him rip the mask off of his face. With Mat held out at arm’s length Pyshcoduck screaming got a whole mouth full of mucus. Hurling it at Mat it landed right between Mat’s eyes. A couple of cop’s come through the crowd pulling Pyshcoduck away but he lunges for Mat nailing him with a upper cut. Out of nowhere Mont connects with a swift battering shinning wizard from behind off a chair going higher then any man I had ever seen execute the move. Down went Pyshcoduck as the referee made the count for the win. Stretchers came flooding through the area Mat was ushered onto the stretcher while he was at an angle only I could see he winked at me. I knew what he meant and it made me happy… Kayfabe! The reason we do it, the reason Mat had taken abuse for the months and Pyschoduck had been hated for that moment when they take trickery of an audience to an all time high. The crowd believed the situation, within minutes smart marks around the world had posted on there internet sites about Mat being an idiot turning up to an event knowing he would get a beating from Pyshcoduck. The referee got on the microphone with an announcement. ‘Ladies and gentlemen, thank you remember next month in the Southampton Guildhall… Mont and Pyschoduck will take on Mat Mannion! Just before he put the microphone down I stormed the ring unannounced, unplanned, in amazement at least half the crowd went crazy. The announcer handed me the microphone. Right there and then off the top of my head I told everyone how Mat had hired me to be his tag team partner next month. This sent them into frenzy. The police then got in the ring taking me out the ring and guiding me to the back. My love was restored from the morning visit to the post office to the inspiring evening watching the packed Southampton crowd chew up the storyline like a dog’s bone. Yours sincerely, Jamie Fletcher ‘Or was this all Kayfabe?
