Fear of Success?

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 Fear of Success? Do we fear our own success, through the paths of life is it so strange that sometimes that what we try for turns out to be the best? In life you can almost guarantee that you will be uncertain many times. Whether this is by what route to take in life, or whether it’s knowing somebody as well as they know you. Sorry how rude of me, my name is Nathaniel, Nathaniel Draven. It had come to my attention that last week my future for the next month was decided without my consent, I placed my soul and spirit into the hands of another person and look where it has gotten me; I am scheduled to face Eza Krazee. I forgot to do it again; I will be competing in the global title tournament sponsored by Primetime Central. This tournament not only has so many big named stars from around the business but it’s also one of the most respected tournaments to date. If you take a gander at the former winners there name is now solid gold through pride and respect, and after I am done with this tournament then maybe I will be one of the people whose names are remembered. I hope that I am one of those names that will turn the heads around but in order to do that I placed my faith in a person I cannot trust, but uncertainty is the key to success this year for me. Last week I was in the prime fighting for a federation called Neon wrestling, that’s been my home for the last month and it was becoming such a great place where I could become a great fighter, it would seem that the future of the company would become uncertain as well, it closed much to my predicament. I found this out yesterday, let me tell you how it ended I am sure you will find it rather funny. Twenty Four hours earlier. I woke up around eight in the morning to the lovely sounds of some singing birds outside of my window, I always like it in my new apartment when I wake because right on the hour these two blue tits come and sit outside of my window and chirp the most beautiful sounding songs that can ever been heard, I often wonder to myself what they are saying to each other or if they are singing. I pulled back the quilt cover and swung by body around ninety degrees before placing my left leg out of my oak bed and onto the floor. As I followed with my right leg I was about to sit up but instead I sighed to myself and yawned. What I thought was going to be just a ordinary day would turn into the last day that I worked for Neon Wrestling. After sitting on my bed staring across the hallway into a mirror where a tired reflection would stare right back at me I finally stood up, the familiar back ache would strike pain once again, I really need to get that pain sorted out, I don’t believe its from the matches I have been having but more the life I have been living over the last couple of years. Inner Thoughts Away from wrestling I had been working in the caves and mines in southern Cyprus where I would work in thirteen hour shifts, I believe its there where I had pulled a muscle in my back. I would get to work and six in the morning only to start work at quarter-past. From onwards to when I left for home it would be digging bricks and rock away, shovelling it into the buckets then taking them elsewhere. This job was pure agony. But I knew as soon as I had made enough money from this job it was the wrestling world that was waiting for me. I remember back then I would stay in a workers hut for a house, I used to wake up after a short sleep on a box folded onto a stone floor, I would be lucky if I got a good hour sleep due to the fact that the hut was cold and falling apart, there was water dripping from nearly everywhere in the hut. No windows made the cold air blow in and the constant hunger for food could keep any man awake for hours. I used to wake just telling myself that maybe next week I would have enough money to get out of this crap hole, but right back in my mind I knew that it wouldn’t be another week, I was looking at a good three more years working in this personal hell, Three more long years before I would get out of it and start what could have been such a long and great career. I often questioned to myself how much work I would have to do before I could really enjoy the life of a pro wrestler, I used to imagine myself walking around in a smart suit at a press conference where people would know my name, heh I used to laugh when I would think people might want my autograph but my thoughts were always interrupted by the horn that echoed around the mines, the horn ended my dream and brought me back to reality that was working in this hell. It was close to three years before I had enough money to leave those caves. I remember the day when I received that pay check that gave me enough, the smile on my face could be seen from a thousand miles away, I was overjoyed and the next day I was on my way to America. On the long flight I would think to myself how it would become reality, all my dreams where now making my life become something more than a mine worker. I would now walk with a new skip in my step; whoever looked at me I looked back at them and nodded, I was a man of importance. When I was working in the mines I read up in a newspaper that a company was looking for trial wrestlers, could this be my chance to get. I thought to myself that I had nothing to lose, this point in my life I was very uncertain on where it would take me, so without looking back I ran into the future hoping anything would hit me in the face, so it was off to this try-out that I would go, this would also be the first time that I would spend some of my money, to some people it was four weeks of pure hell, but after the life I had had for the last three years this was nothing but a stroll in the park. As time would pass I would keep going to these sessions every four weeks, it would get to the point that the trainers saw my desire that they would sign me up, so I signed to Neon Wrestling and I never looked back, it was my home, or so I thought until I woke up on this morning, where not only would I lose my job but I would have no direction on life anymore. End of Inner Thoughts After looking in the mirror for a few moments I blinked and stood up. I like to reminisce sometimes I think it not only tells others about where I have come from but it also makes me smile, to see how far I have come and what I have been through. But my job yesterday was to make my way to Neon headquarters and enter my name into the inter-promotional wrestling event known around the globe as the “GTT” or Golden Title Tournament. I walked across the cold stone floor of my apartment and straight into the bathroom, I always like to wake up by having a shower each morning, I walked straight in and dropped my pants before turning on the tap. This sent up a huge steam trail as the warm water dripped fast from the shower, as the water speeded up it turned into a warm raining sensation. I would spend roughly twenty minutes in the shower washing myself and looking in the mirror at all the bruises that have appeared on my body over the last number of weeks. I have come to conclusion that all the stiff work that has happened over the past weeks matches in Neon it has taken effect on my body, as I slowly rubbed the soap onto my body it would foam and gently massage my wounds, I always get a sharp hurting feeling when I rub it in to hard, but then again my body will get used to the familiar pain after a while. After the twenty minutes I turned off the shower and stepped in front of the mirror in the bathroom, it had steamed up due to condensation so I remember I had to wipe it with a towel which was laid on the chair next to the mirror standing on a table. After the steam had cleared I looked at myself close up, just to see who the real Nathaniel was, and for a brief moment I could see him looking back at me, this man was a powerful man. A man that we should all one day look up to, this man is a born leader and lead he will do. I nodded to the real Juvian and walked out of the bathroom, I walked straight across the hall way and entered my lobby area. I dried myself off with a towel which was laid on the chest of draws to my right. The old chest of draws was left by the old couple who had this house before me, I am sure its made form oak but it has a real nice gothic feel to it, it makes it one of the more pleasant features of this house. I continued to get dressed, it took me about five to ten minutes before I was completely dressed however I don’t dry my hair, I just leave it wet and let it dry naturally. I shaked my head from side to side to make the strands of hair drape over my face before grabbing my jacket and walking out of the room and heading for the stairs. I walked down the Royal Oak stairs and grabbed my car keys from on the table at the bottom. I also grabbed a red apple as I believe that to start a fresh day you should always eat good food, so with my keys and apple in had its perfect for a nice drive towards work, work being Neon Headquarters’. When I started in the business I thought that you only wrestled and got paid, how wrong I could be. I mean you have to show up for the training school to give lessons on the young talent hoping to get a break through, which I do enjoy a awful lot, just to see the desire in those people faces and then to see them smile when they hear that you will be making an appearance is something that I could do forever. Another part of it is the press great and meet, this is where you get to hype up your matches before hand and meet all the fans, we try to stay in character for this but its so hard for me to do so, last year I was just one of them, I was a fan and look at me now I am a Neon Superstar. I packed all my items into the back of my hummer and pulled off into the early morning traffic for another day at the “office”. It was a quite drive early on with not so much traffic I remember tuning my radio into the early morning malice repeat, malice is a radio channel for devoted Wrestling fans, I find it good so I can listen about all the news from around the circuit so I keep up to date. Its hosted by a man whose called Duke Williams, he is a cool guy and maybe one day we might be able to sort out a on air interview hyping Nathaniel Draven. One thing I remembered was listing to the current draw for the Golden Title Tournament, I only wished that the entrance was still open and this was later confirmed after moments more of listing, he said there was still options open and gave a phone number, as soon as I heard that number I was parking the car and getting out my cell phone. It took me around ten minutes to get through and I left all my details, it was later on when I was driving that I heard “New entrant Nathaniel Draven representing Neon has entered”. As soon as that happened I could do nothing but honk the horn on the hummer a several hundred times, people in the street turned to look what was happening and all they saw was a man jumping around in his car honking the horn. I was over the moon, not only had I just ticked off one of my things to do list for today but I had also entered myself in a tournament that could possibly make me famous. I began to think back to Cyprus where I had a chart of the tournament for last years with all the names on it. I remembered keeping up to date and watching all the names finally narrow down. Then I thought now when people do that this year they will see my name, they will see Nathaniel Draven on that chart. As I continued to drive my emotions finally dropped, I carried on listing though as I heard more and more entrants announced from the likes of “Former Prime Time Central champion” Seymour Almasy to Justin Locke. I was still in a awe of disbelief that I had been announced on the radio, for some reason it felt like that all my preparation in Neon of trying to step up to the next level could be seen to the whole world now. There was also a uncertain mood to who I would face, would it be a un-known superstar from a federation not many people know somewhat like myself, or could it be one of theses big named stars hoping to add another title to their belts. I thought to myself who would I rather be drawn against. If I got an unknown person would I be predicted to be the favourite against him or her, but on the downside I wouldn’t be able to do proper research on my opponent, also this unknown star maybe a little gem just waiting to explode somewhat like myself, after thinking about it I decided I wouldn’t like to draw somebody that I don’t know because of that reason. I would like to draw somebody I know, somebody that people will predict to beat me, someone that could take me to my limits, somebody that I can prove to that I can go toe to toe with and still look like a good fighter, its not long to find out who I will face, in fact its just over a hour away, after I have finished at neon I should know who I am going to face and quite frankly I just cannot wait to find out who I will face. I arrived at Neon around half pasted nine on the morning that the draw that would change my life for the next month was taking place. I zoomed right past the guards nodding to them to say hello to them as they was expecting me, I parked the hummer right up and turned the engine off, I sat back and listened to the radio a few more moments before finally turning it off and locking my car up. I walked into the main office and straight up to the sectary, she was called Jenna. Jenna was around twenty years old with soft blue eyes and dark brown hair; she always had a smile to herself and looked smart. “Good morning Mr Draven” she said to me in a soft tone. “They are expecting you in the press conference room, just go ahead and I will buzz them to tell you that you are on your way” she later added whilst smiling at me. “Thank you Jenna” I replied. As I walked down the corridor, I heard over a silent buzz which Jenna was telling the board of directors that I had arrived. My sole purpose for the stop at the offices today was to hype up Neon Wrestling’s stars entering the Global Title Tournament. Neon was hoping that several stars would not only enter but they would also do well for Neon. I believe I red somewhere that Neon would receive a lump some of money for every entrant, then they would receive some more for every competitor that would advance through the rounds. Obviously the more rounds the wrestlers passed through then the bigger the payoff. Its been a well known fact to all that Neon isn’t that rich, the business is hardly full of money, but we are loyal wrestlers and we don’t mind working to cut the costs down, especially me, what I live like now is more than one hundred times better than I had back in Cyprus, just the chance that Neon had given me is more than I could ever have dreamed of. I walked up to the doors where I stood for a few moments, I knew when I opened these doors I would be met with a sea of camera flashes and I had to be in character, which meant that the evil Nathaniel must come into play. I looked up and down then slowly walked into the room, like my expectations I was greeted with many cameras and people shoving microphones into my face, I slowly walked into the room and pushed over a man who was placed there by neon to get some crowd response. I walked up some steps and onto the stage area; it was set out with a long table seated for up to seven people with a glass of water in front of the places and a microphone. There was a banner on the front of the table which red “Neon Represents Global Athletes”. I thought this must have set them back a few ten dollars, but then again I am happy that I can represent them to my fullest. “Ok, I am here now what do you want” I said in a dark tone looking into the audience hoping to trigger a response by one of the many reporters “Nathaniel, what do you think of your chances?” said a reporter in the front row. He was middle aged and looked like he never knew much about myself, Neon or the business. “First off. You call me Mr Draven! Secondly to answer your question…do you think I would be entered in this competition if I thought that I had no chance of winning this? I don’t enter events that take up my personal time to go out in the first round, that would not only be pointless but that would also be embarrassing. And embarrassing isn’t my kind of thing, next question please” I responded with a very sharp tone. “So you aim is to win the Mr Draven?” The man quickly responded. “If you think I stutter please tell me now, not only do I think I can win this title but I can almost guarantee that I will turn some faces by doing it, you see some people maybe uncertain of how I perform in this sport we all love, so I will show them all, I will show them that Nathaniel Draven isn’t a push over, he isn’t a fraud, he is the real deal, and that’s what I will show them” I responded to them again. “Now last question please, you peasants have not earned this much of my time” I like throwing in some of my quotes when I can do so. “How do you feel about your opponent?” Is what they asked next I was puzzled how they knew who I had drawn but I never knew myself, so I had to find out. “Let me ask you a question, how do you feel about him?” I said, reversing the question. “Eza Krazee is a very good superstar, each week at UWF he impresses me” the man responded with. Eza Krazee I have been drawn against. That’s great, I thought to myself… I know of Ezra and I know that I will have to do my utter best so I can beat him. I remember when I first arrived in Neon I went over to watch a UWF show just because it was in the area at the time, and I hate to admit it but that guy can really wrestle, maybe I should have hoped for a guy that I had never heard of instead. But like they all say to become the best you have to face and beat the best, and that’s what I will have to do, I admit its not going to be easy but I am sure that when I train hard enough and when I work my ass off for this fight that at the end of the week I will be ready to beat him. There is no doubt in my mind that I can do it, I just have to show the world that I can do it as well. “Krazee is nothing” I replied to the statement. “He is just another person in the way of Nathaniel Draven’s global title shot, it’s no question that I am gunning that down, not only do I want my Global Title shot, but I also want that trophy to be on my wall in my house, and well is Krazee is in the way then I will have to go Crazy on him and beat his ass down to the mat, you see this is not just me and Eza, but this is now Neon verses UWF, and I know that I wont let my side down, but for now I have other business so that’s all you will get out of me, good day” Is what I said before getting up off my chair and standing up and posing for a few pictures before exiting the room through a door on the left hand side of the stage. I felt to myself that the promotional work went ok, but looking to the future its training for the fight now that I must do. I left the building and went straight into my hummer driving back to the house so I could get my clothes before heading to the gym. If there was one word for what I did yesterday it would be uncertain, because not only did I know who I would be facing at the end of the day, but I did not know that it would be the last day I represented my company as when I got home I found a note attached to my “final” pay check. End of Twenty Four Hour Flashback I vowed to do one thing, and that was to try my hardest in the Global Title Tournament, and that’s what I shall do, Eza Krazee fear your nightmares, because when we meet next week I will turn them into your reality.
