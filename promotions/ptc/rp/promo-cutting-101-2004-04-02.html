Promo Cutting 101

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 Promo Cutting 101 The scene fades back into the infamous home of The New Royal Legion and their Legionnaires, The Howard Johnson Hotel in XUWville. Outside of the hotel, the world’s greatest wrestling fans are camping in the parking lot of the hotel like usual. It is a usual day for them, hanging out hoping to catch a glimpse of their saviour in Bailey. As well as hoping to see the newest Legionnaire – the whiskey warrior, the pungent pugilist, the smelly superstar, the flatulent fighter, the one, the only “The Bastard” Al Crud and last but not least (well, that’s up for debate) the first Legionnaire ever, Larry the Legionnaire. They actually want to see Larry this time as he is making a return to the ring from a one year absence. He is hoping to build on a winning streak of one match, even though technically he lost his last match but Larry doesn’t recognize that match as it was supposed to be versus good ole Jim Ross (JR) not the returning Saij. It’s not as if this return to the ring is some momentous occasion for Larry and the other Legionnaires, Larry never really liked wrestling in the first place. He only ever won one match in his career and that was with help from the New Royal Legion and the Ultra Violents too strangely enough. He is first and foremost a lawyer and a damn good one at that. Sure he lost a major case last year when The New Royal Legion, that’s Bailey and Danyon, took XUW to court over the conspiracy and discriminatory work practices. But the reason why they lost was that they believe the conspirators got to the judge and bribed him. The conspiracy against The New Royal Legion is really that far reaching and in depth. Larry was the first Legionnaire ever. He was the first loyal fan of the New Royal Legion, the self proclaimed greatest tag team in history. During their early years, they were one of the most hated teams in XUW always blaming the fans and the owners of XUW of harbouring a grudge or a conspiracy really against them and that is why they had limited success. But slowly one by one people started to like The New Royal Legion. Their fan base started to grow. Larry was the one who created the “Conspiracy Weekly” newsletter, which is the weekly newsletter that goes out to the Legionnaires to keep them up to date with the on goings of their favourite tag team and there would be other little stories in there about the fake moon landing and the JFK assassination. Slowly people became just as loyal to the New Royal Legion as Larry did and soon the Legionnaires numbers grew from 100 to 1000 to 1 million. Finally, a few months ago they broke the one million members barrier when Al Crud became an honourary Legionnaire. Larry was the driving force behind the creation of the L.O.S.T. Titles, that’s the Legionnaires Only Significant Tag Titles, that was handed to the New Royal Legion because of the unfair treatment they received from XUW and not receiving a tag title shot, which they were promised. After defending the L.O.S.T. Titles successfully a few times, the New Royal Legion decided that XUW was still not respecting them so they decided to go on strike from XUW shows. Larry was the chief negotiator during the negotiations with XUW. But not only did he do that but due to the conditions of the strike Larry was forced to wrestle. This was the short time period where Larry wrestled actual XUW wrestlers. He didn’t wrestle other managers or jobbers, oh no, the XUW brass wouldn’t have had it any other way. They put him in there with some of the best XUW wrestlers there were. His was forced to wrestle the likes of MV Pete – the leader of the Army of Darkness and Sheri amongst others. But Larry did eventually secure a pinfall victory over Kevil. Eventually, the strike was settled and Larry could go back to doing what he did best and that’s being the lawyer to the Legionnaires and the New Royal Legion. But now the time has come for Larry to return to the ring. However, this return to the ring was not of his decision. Since Danyon was still missing and not been seen since mid December when Bailey and Danyon fought each other, they could not enter into the Tag Title Tournament. So Bailey decided that Larry should represent the Legionnaire Family in the Cruiserweight Tournament partly over anger that Larry was withholding the location of Danyon’s whereabouts and partly over that he just felt Legionnaires needed to make an appearance in the PTC. When Larry first learned of his entrance into the tournament he was quite worried seeing that he doesn’t really have any wrestling skills. However, as time went on, Larry changed his mind and started to look forward to entering the tournament and moving on through the rounds. He figured that as time went and the competition got stiffer it would give him time to improve his wrestling skills. And it’s not as if it could get any worse considering that his only move that he has perfected is a head lock. Before, Larry moves on in the tournament and before he even steps into the ring for the first time against Horazon, he has a few of those wrestling nuances to touch up on, one of which is the art of cutting a promo. Inside the Howard Johnson Hotel, up on the 15th floor is located the New Royal Legion Suite, which is home to Bailey, Larry and now Al Crud because of Danyon’s disappearance. Bailey and Al Crud have pulled their two chairs close together and are sitting next to each other and are facing Larry the Legionnaire. Larry is standing in front of them with a piece of paper in his hand. Bailey: Alright, Larry, you’ve had some time now to work on a promo. Let’s see what you go. Cutting a promo is one of the most important things in the wrestling business. Its helps intimidate your opponent and get him really scared of you. It lets him know how serious you are taking him or you can let him know how much of a joke you think he is. But in your case I’d assume that you are trying to show your opponent that you mean business given your lack of experience in the ring and small stature. The whiskey warrior, Al Crud, farts and usually that means he has something to say. Al: This is a cruiserweight tournament, so a lot of these guys are small in size. They aren’t big like you and me. Al pats his beer belly. Al: Of course, some parts of me are bigger than they should be. Back in the old GIWF days this wasn’t as big and I could actually win matches. Bailey: Ah, good point Al. Still, a lot of these guys with the exception of the Cockjockey are bigger than Larry. Most of them are actually wrestlers, while Larry isn’t. So, he still has to try and show that he is serious about this match and try and convince his opponent that he is not a joke. Larry takes a sip of water from a glass that was on the table next to him. Bailey: Alright Larry, your music just hit and you are walking down to the ring. You have the mic in the ring, now let’s hear your opening line. I think you should start out with your catchphrase, the one that will catch their attention right away. Larry: Okay, here goes nothing………. Bailey: That’s not a good line to start out with. Larry: That wasn’t the first line I was starting out with, that was more of a comment than anything. Bailey: I want you to make this a real promo. You can’t go in the ring and say here goes nothing. No one will take you seriously. Now, let’s start over. Go! Larry: My name is Larry………………and I’m a Legionnaire! Bailey clears his throat really loudly. Bailey: Is that it? Is that all you have? That’s not a good catchphrase. Larry: But Al says something very similar. Why can’t I? Bailey looks over at Al who is polishing off another beer. Al: Isn’t that like copyright infringement? Larry: So, I should be taking out the lines in this promo that have Farkin since that’s Saij and Freakin since that’s AWS Man (Also Known As Bill)? Bailey: Definitely. You want to be original, which is why that line about being a Legionnaire doesn’t really work. It works for Al because he says………. Bailey turns to Al and waits for him to say his line but Al being as slow as he is doesn’t catch on right away so there is a bit of a lag time. Eventually he catches on and says his line. Al: My name is Al………..and I’m an ALcoholic. Bailey: See, it works for him as it’s a play on his name. You know “Al” of the ALcoholic part. And all his fans are known as ALcoholics, so it works. You saying you are a Legionnaire is nothing new seeing as your name is Larry the Legionnaire and everyone knows that. Larry: Right. Okay. I get you. Maybe I can say this………..My name is Larry and I have Legionnaires. Larry looks at Bailey whose eyes are quite wide open now. He looks over Al who is opening another beer. Larry: What? That’s original and says that I have Legionnaires. You know as if it’s something to catch to get. I thought it worked. What’s wrong with it? Bailey: Larry, Legionnaires is a disease. It’s a disease you get from drinking stale water. Saying you have Legionnaires is nothing to brag about. You shouldn’t be making light of a potentially harmful disease. Making fun of diseases is never funny. You don’t see people going out there and making fun of cancer or something. You don’t see a wrestler with the name of Adam “InDestructible” Smith calling himself A.I.D.S. now do you? Larry: No. Bailey: Exactly because that’s rude and inconsiderate, especially to those who have actually had Legionnaires disease. We can work on the catchphrase at a later date and time. Let’s move on, wouldn’t you say Al? Al belches really loudly. Bailey: I’ll take that as a yes. Al: uh, sorry. I say we move on. I think having a good one liner in your is always key in your promos. Bailey: Oh yes. Excellent point, Al. Larry, do you have any great one liners or any great lines? Larry: I do Bailey. I’m used to giving great lines because of all the time I’ve spent in court over the years. Bailey: Maybe if you gave some more great lines we would have won that case last year against XUW instead we had to do community service. Larry: Hey, that wasn’t my fault. The conspirators got to the judge. There was no way we should have lost. Bailey: Whatever, get on with it. Larry: Okay. Larry grabs another drink of water before proceeding. Larry: Just like the sun goes down every nite on the horizon, when this match is over I’ll be the sun going down on you. Bailey: What the hell did you just say? Did you even think about what you just said? Larry: I…..uhmmm……yes Bailey: You said you were going down on the guy. Furthermore, you said you were a sun going down. That’s just wrong. That can be taken totally the wrong way as in son with an “o” not a “u”. Larry: Oh yeah. I didn’t even think of that. Bailey: What you should have said was something like “with every horizon there is a sun that sets on it. I’m going to be the one that sets the sun on this horizon for a 1……2………..3.” Larry: Oh, that’s good. Can I use that? Bailey: I’d probably say but there is only one problem with that. Larry: What’s that? Bailey: His name is Horazon, not Horizon. It’s an “A” not an “I”. Larry: It is? Larry goes over and looks at the brackets and sees that it is indeed Horazon vs. Larry the Legionnaire not Horizon. Larry: Well, look at that. It is Horazon. I guess that means my other lines about riding and getting off on the horizon should be taken out. Bailey: I’d say so. I mean you could choose to leave them in but that would be totally unoriginal and I’m sure that Horazon has heard lame jokes about horizon anyways. If there is one thing that Legionnaires are about, it’s originality. Larry: I am original. I’m the original Legionnaire. I was the first. I was the first real fan of the New Royal Legion, while everyone else out there was booing you guys and throwing garbage at you while you claimed that XUW was xenophobic and that Admiral John the former owner of XUW was against you. I was there cheering for you guys. I was there worshipping you guys the day Danyon and you first won the titles. Bailey: There we go. Larry: There what goes? Bailey: A good little bit for a promo, wouldn’t you say so Al. Al farts once more before agreeing. Al: Yeah, that was a good part. I remember I used to be able to deliver lines like that when I was off battling Lew Spectre, hardkore and The Phenomenons in GIWF. That’s what brought me to my greatness. Bailey: He’s right you know Larry. You should go on about how you are the greatest Legionnaire. You were the original. You should talk about how you are “THE” Legionnaire. Even your name says that you are “THE” Legionnaire. You’re not Larry Legionnaire. Oh, no. You are Larry the Legionnaire. That “the” is key. You have to let Horazon and any other competitors know that you are “the” Legionnaire. I don’t think that any of these people really know exactly what the Legionnaires are all about. You have to remember Larry, you will have one million fans cheering you and only you. You will have one million fans, I don’t know if any of these other guys or girls will have that or even know what it’s like to have such a fan base that is so loyal. This is the type of stuff that you have to say if you are going to move on in the tournament and have a chance of winning. I realize that your wrestling skills are not that great or even exist but with the Legionnaires on your side anything is possible. Larry is frantically writing small notes as Bailey finishes up his little pep talk. He looks up finally and at Bailey and Al. Larry: I think I got that all. Anything you want to add Al? Al: Yeah, just go out there and win for the Legionnaires. If Bailey and I can successfully tag together and beat former world champions then you can win this thing. Larry: Thanks for the tips guys. I’m going to go and get back to work on this promo and get back to getting ready for this match. Thanks. Larry looks down at his piece of piece of paper. He mumbles a few things under his breath as he grabs his briefcase and heads for the door. The scene fades out as Larry shuts the door to the suite behind him and Larry goes off and prepares for his first match in a year and his first match in the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament.
