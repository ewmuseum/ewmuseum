Tyrone Grace Public Access Pimp Joined: 27 Feb 2003 Posts: 22 Location: Your mom's house Posted: Fri Apr 11, 2003 11:38 pm Post subject: Human. [Extreme Cluster-fuck RP 2] Everyone is human. In life, there are always possibilities of failing, but no one wants to admit it. Some always say that winning isn't everything, but it really is. There is fake all over the world, and there are only a few of those who separate the facts from the fiction. Life isn't a game, but some live it like they have three "extra men" to play with. This isn't a playstation, rather a journey. Along any journey, you can fall, stumble and witness glory. In life, you can fail or you can succeed, this is determined by you. You hold your own fate, not someone else looking eye to eye with you. Destiny is in control of the beholder, the only question is, will you choose your own? "Damn. When will the lights come back on?" Tyrone Grace sits in the faint light that illuminates his living room. The storm that surged through the Atlanta area knocked out all of the power, and Tyrone Grace was left with flashlights and a battery controlled radio. He listened to some relaxing music, still contemplating his match for PTC. He was still kind of focused, but decided that he would try to stay calm form the moment being. He still had a great view from his house, but there were no city lights, they were all dark. Grace sat on the end of his couch in the reclining chair, drinking some bottled water. Thoughts of his previous match in the DWO rain through his mind. Was it that this PTC situation was on his mind so deeply that he finally lost a match in the DWO? He had been there for nearly five months, and had not lost a single match...until now. To be perfectly honest, he was kind of relieved because he knew it would come sooner or later. He knew that he was human, no superman blood ran through his veins, so he had to lose sooner or later. It was later in this case, but all he knew was that he didn't want this to become a frequent event. How did he allow himself to be beaten? He had confidence, of course, but he wanted to be extra prepared for the I-PPV match. He wanted to make sure he put on a great performance in front of his home-fed crowd, since he would be stepping in front of other federations and fans very soon. He thought about it too much. You know how you meditate on something so long that it begins to take over? You stay up all night thinking about it and going over your plans, but you realize that when you actually get in that situation, you do nothing of what you planned. Tyrone didn't want this to be the situation come PTC's first pay per view. Sure he was confident, hell, he was probably one of the most cocky guys in the DWO, but he didn't want to be overly cocky for this match. He didn't weigh his matches a lot, but when he did, they were very important. This match was of a very high nature, in fact, this was his first interfed match. He'd be facing some of the top, and maybe some of the lowest superstars from many different organizations, and he made sure that he'd be ready. He didn't want to make that same lame mistake that he made in the DWO match. Tyrone realized that he had overlooked his match with Kai Shinobi because he was mainly focused on his match with all of these people in the PTC pay per view. What a mistake. Being the type of person he was, when Tyrone Grace failed, he would try again until he succeeded. Kai Shinobi would be in this match, the extreme cluster-fuck match, and Tyrone would make sure that his presence was felt. Tyrone would make sure that he made everyone know who he was, even if he lost. That was the main goal, to make sure that everyone knew that he left his mark. And how would he do that? Be prepared. That's as simple as it gets. Tyrone learned that when you plan every move and every thought, that you end up with a huge disappointment, which he didn't want. He viewed this match as a cup of water that was half empty. It was his job to fill it up to the rim. Lately, Tyrone had been having dark thoughts when his opponents came to mind. He went to his mother's murderer's grave and just viewed the world like he viewed that one man. He thought about the world being full of liars and thieves, willing to rob any person blind to get success. Grace didn't want to turn out to be that type of person, in fact, he'd rather be a person that went through every match, winning or losing until he got to where he wanted to be. He never thought that he would be in the position that he's in now, possibly adding another notch to his belt with a victory, or loss in this I-PPV match. He didn't know what to think about the world now, he was confused. As Tyrone sat in the dark with a few candles illuminating the area, he felt some form of tranquility. He didn't know why, but his mind became relaxed, and then it hit him. "No matter what, I'm still going to be me. I'm still going to be Tyrone Grace." This, perhaps, was the most logical thing Tyrone Grace said all week. He was right, also, because no matter what, he would be the same man. Win, lose or draw, as long as he tried his absolute best to put on a show and get the "W", he could hold his head high. Tyrone knew that this wasn't the attitude that you could have if you were constantly losing, but he knew that if he wanted to win, he'd have to be able to sacrifice losing. Maybe the situation in the DWO was an improvement to his character. The fans could boo him, and he could put on a show, but in reality, when the ring with broken down and the lights were cut off after the show, he'd still be Tyrone Grace. Maybe he had to lose before he knew what winning "really" meant. He wasn't sure at this very moment, all he knew was that he was getting more and more prepared for this match. He wanted to make sure that when he stood in the ring with all of those other competitors, he had something that set him apart from this rest of this. He was the one man that realized that he wasn't a super hero, super strong body builder or unbeatable man...he was just a man who wanted to win. He was a man who wanted glory, victory and power like the next man, but he didn't have to pretend that he was someone else to achieve that. He's plain old... Tyrone Grace. _________________ "There's a thin line between confidence and cockiness, I have the guts to step over that line." 1/2 DWO Tag Team Champion
