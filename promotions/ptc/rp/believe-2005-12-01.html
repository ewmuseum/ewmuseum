Believe

1 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-01 A man is standing in the middle of his living room. He is wearing a dark blue sweat jacket, khaki pants, and some grayish colored Vans shoes. His short brownish hair has been matted down by water so it isn't sticking up all over the place. His face a sense of sad despair to it as his eyes slowly begin to wander. This man looks around, seemingly not having any idea about what to do next. This is a man searching for a purpose… This is a man who has never lived an ordinary day in his life… This is James von Doom, the current UWF North American Champion and laughing stock of the wrestling community. He has been outcasted and thrown into exile because of his happy demeanor. But, at the moment, he seems to not be the least bit happy at all. In fact, he seems rather sullen and melancholy. For the fire in his heart that once raged for the business he knows and loves is now slowly being extinguished by the laughter of his colleagues. They are nowhere to be seen, but he still hears them. He hears the tormenting comments made on the wrestling forums. "James von Doom's promo was the worst thing I've seen since Troll 2." "James von Doom will never be a Main Event talent anywhere." "James von Doom is nothing more than a comedy wrestler." "James von Doom is a joke, PERIOD." These words echo through his very soul. Feelings of hopelessness and depression suddenly come out in him as he thinks about the very things he's read over the past few weeks. A tear almost forms at the edge of his right eye but he wipes it away before it trickles down his cheek. This is what it feels like to be James von Doom right now. Haunted by words spoken by others and constantly haunted by a past that no one knows about. No one knows what has been going on with him the past few weeks. But with the big tournament starting up, it's all coming to the forefront. James looks around his living room and none of his ever-present sidekicks are around. Or, to put it more precisely, none of them seem to be around at the moment, but there's always at least one or two lurking around his house. Suddenly in the background, a door opens and closes without the sound of a key turning into a lock. James looks in the direction of where the sound came from and sees one of his more amusing associates standing there looking at him funny. The man is wearing a long, white science coat, a pair of shiny old spectacles, has science department regulation black pants and shoes on, and looks kind of like Jerry Lewis. This man is known through the world and the AWC. He is known simply as the Horny Mathematician. James stares at him, mouth agape. "What do you want?" he asks in a sullen voice. The Mathematician smiles at him and replies "I have made an important discovery that will change the world as we know it!" The Mathematician happily skips over to James and puts his arm around him. James turns his head slightly and gives the Mathematician a look that resembles what would happen if one might look over and see an alien milking a cow. "Do you want to know what it is?" the Mathematician asks with a sense of excitement in his voice. James is still a bit befuddled as to why the Mathematician has his arm around James. As he and all of James' close associates know, James is not the kind of person who likes contact of this nature from people. James, however, is not in the mood for this kind of interruption. He needs to brood. "What is it?" James asks in a cold, unenthusiastic voice that would send shivers down a newborn puppy's back. James looks almost feral and psychotic as he impatiently waits for his happy go lucky friend to get to the point. "What's your so-called scientific breakthrough?" "I have discovered very recently the origin of the universe," the Mathematician replies with a giddy sense of joy. He's acting like a schoolgirl on a sugar rush. The Mathematician is never this bubbly unless he's been drinking. James takes a sniff and immediately catches the fragrance of wine coolers. James at that moment knew that what he would soon be hearing would be complete and utter nonsense. But, then again, that was all he ever learned in high school so he might as well put up with it because now, at least he had a friend to talk to. "Friend…" he inaudibly whispers to himself. In a business filled with many associates, he has very few friends. Instead of few, less than five would be a more accurate statement. It seems at times that nobody likes his carefree attitude and out of control behavior. Many have often compared him to a certain Warrior who was just as crazy as he is. CLAP! CLAP! CLAP! James is startled back into reality by the Mathematician, who is clapping in his face to try and get his attention. "You awake buddy?" the Mathematician asks with a sudden sense of concern coming over him for his friend. James nods his head. "I'm all right," he replies. But, alas, he is not alright. He hasn't been alright for weeks. Ever since the memories of his childhood began resurfacing, he hasn't been the same fun loving von Doom that everyone has come to know. Now, he has become a dark, brooding bomb that is just waiting for the chance to unleash a lifetime of fury and pain upon the world. Or, to put it more precise, onto his first victim… In a psychology class he took, he remembered hearing something along the lines of 'we are all products of our environment.' So what environment could create someone as demented as he is? He's a good person, but he hasn't felt like it in a very long time. He was changing as we all do. But, the thing is, he doesn't like what he is changing into. James drifts back into reality for a brief moment and turns to the Mathematician. "So what is the origin of the universe?" he asks the Mathematician with a monotone voice rarely heard from him. No emotion could be detected in any of the words just spoken out of his mouth. He has now become was he has vowed to destroy…just another wrestler with no personality and a set of moves they use over and over in every match. It took three years, but the business had finally broken him. The flame had been almost extinguished. "The origin of the universe is the tip of my penis," the Mathematician says with a sly grin on his face. And with one sentence spoken by one of his friends, the flame once again is ignited. You never know when your life will change. In brief instant, everything you knew and were taught could change. A friend you never knew cared so much could come to your rescue at the drop of a hat. You could be walking down the street and bump into your future wife. One stock deal could make you a millionaire. Or maybe a friend you never thought you had could come in when you are at your weakest and most vulnerable and bring back the life in you by just uttering one sentence. James had it. He finally knew what set him apart from just about every wrestler out there. His sense of humor. But he knows deep down that this is not the time for joking around. It was time to be serious. The biggest tournament of the year, GTT5 was on the horizon, and James had no idea who he would be facing but none of that mattered now. He was back to being his old self, even if just for a short time. "But how could the entire universe originate from that?" he asks with the glimmer of a smile start to appear on his face. The Mathematician has a glimmer in his eye as he is about to start speaking. He looks as if he was a newborn father looking at his first child. But, however, this child could not be seen or touched. This child was just an idea in his mind. A concept theory created by his own mathematical genius. And, like a baby, he treated it as such. "Because of my rampant physical, sexual testing with the female of the species," he says in a serious tone. "I have come to the conclusion that the origin of the universe is the tip of my penis." James raises an eyebrow, almost questioning his judgment letting this idiot into his house. But, then again, all the people he knows for the most part are idiots and he would live a lonely existence without them. "But how do you know this to be fact?" he asks, almost seeming more like a reporter than a person having a casual conversation with a friend. "What proof do you have to back up this theory? What, in your all infinite wisdom, made you come to this conclusion?" The Mathematician seemed more eager now than before, almost like a child trying to prove himself to be better than another child in a sport. He seemed to be on a mission to prove he was right. "I know this to be a fact because this week I have knocked up five women," he replies with a big smile. "And all of them were covered by the rather of the Horny Mathematician. But, to answer your first question, if I have the power to knock up five women between the ages of twenty and thirty-five in under a week, then imagine the possibilities if I continued to have premarital sex outside of my science lab over the entire year. The Earth's population would double!" James has a weird look on his face now. He seems to almost be weirded out by his buddy, who won the AWC quote of the Quarter Award for the first quarter of the AWC's run. "That's preposterous," James replies with a sense of disgust in his voice not heard in some time from a man as outrageous as he is. "We already have a big population problem as we know it. Why would we need a whole bunch of Horny Mathematician spawn running rampant throughout the world?" The Mathematician can only shake his head in disbelief. He replies "Only a fool would think as you do. I mean, think about it. So far, I've only had sex with science geek girls until now. And they all had custom-made spermicide tablets that they took that made them grow facial hair. But now I have had sex with non-bearded women and I love it! It's made me even hornier! I'm on a mission, my friend. A mission to have sex with as many women as possible and create a master race of Horny Mathematicians to take over the world!" James, much like his rather horny friend, has a mission of his own. His mission is simple and to the point. It's been on his mind throughout the entire conversation. All he had to do was wait and receive the fax from PTC management about the tournament brackets. He wanted to see who he was facing. But, instead of doing that, he was listening to his friend talk about having sex with bearded ladies in a science lab. Yeah, there's an image he wanted in his head. Just as he finally got that horrible image of the Shark Woman out of his dreams, now he has to put up with this horror. Then, suddenly, he remembered something. "I wonder if my fax has come in yet?" James wonders aloud, talking to himself. "You hump trees too?" the Mathematician asks him. James gives him a weird look and asks "No. Why? Do you?" The Mathematician shakes his head. "No, I don't," he replies quickly. They look at each other. "What are you talking about?" James asks. The Mathematician replies "What are you talking about?" James shrugs his shoulders and starts walking toward the hallway. Once there, the Mathematician quickly follows him as they walk past a few doors and then go into one on the right side of the hallway. They enter the computer room and James' eyes gaze over to his fax machine. Sure enough, there was a fax lying there, waiting to be read. James smiles deviously and walks over to the fax machine. He picks up the paper and turns it over. "What is it?" the Mathematician asks with intrigue. Being a follower of the wrestling world's female population, he is intrigued to see which femme fatales he will be getting to watch wrestle in scantily clad outfits this year. The Mathematician starts to lick his lips. Dawn McFarlane… Tessa Windsor… Karina Wolfenden… Aimz… The possibilities were nearly limitless. And the Mathematician salivated at the possibility of there being a all female deathmatch where one or both of the competitors both just happened to have a wardrobe malfunction. James, on the other hand, was more focused on seeing who was facing who this year. He could care less about women at the moment, but he has had more than his fair share of lonely nights sleeping alone here lately. He seemingly hadn't had a warm body lying next to his in ages. But none of that mattered now. Now, all that mattered was this…the official GTT5 Bracket draws. "Okay," he says aloud. "Let's see who is in bracket one." James scans over the list of competitors in Bracket One and smiles. "I see Red Rock made the cut this year." The Mathematician smiles. "I love his work." James looks over the list more thoroughly and sees a bunch of names he does not recognize. "Dante? Donovan Midas? Valentine Burst? Devil D. Graves? What the hell kind of name is that?" The Horny Mathematician thinks about that for a second and replies "What kind of name is James von Doom?" James shoots him an angry look and the Mathematician shrugs his shoulders. "What?" However, James does recognize a lot of the wrestlers in this bracket and sees that it will be very tough. "I beat Jagger Vince once or twice in the UWF, but he's good. I hope Drake Coleman goes far. He's a good kid." "Isn't he the guy who beat your pirate buddy in the cruiserweight tournament?" the Mathematician asks. James nods his head and replies "Yes he was. Nice guy and all, but he's got some major players in his bracket. Kash, the Back Alley Brawler, Eva Von Lorne, Triple-X, LeStatt Knight, Cyrus Skar, Jonathon Winters, Jake Hix, Michael Stevens, Fatbadger, Ember, and the legend of legends, Seymour Almasy." James shakes his head almost in disbelief. "This bracket is stacked. I'm so glad I'm not in this bracket." The Mathematician nods his head in agreement. "You're right. Your odds of winning that bracket if you were in it are, if my calculations are correct, two million-to-one. But that's only if my calculations are correct." James can only roll his eyes and begin scanning over the bracket two participants as his horny friend pulls out his calculator and starts punching in numbers to check his calculations on James' odds to win bracket number one. James immediately sees some big names in this bracket, especially ones from the PRIME federation that everyone has been crowing about. "Dawn McFarlane is going to kick some serious ass in this tournament. But damn…" He starts to read out loud the names so the Mathematician can get a full grasp of what major players in the wrestling business are in this bracket. "Get a load of this bracket. Holy crap…it's got (Dawn) McFarlane, Killean Sirrajin, Mal Somers, Vangels Olsig, Shanty Jones, Christian Zenith, Danielle Northsky…" "CORRECT!" James stops reading and looks over to the Mathematician, who is holding up his calculator up in the air. "What?" James asks in an irritable tone, as he is a little mad at being interrupted over something stupid. "My numbers are correct," the Mathematician replies with a pride beaming off of his face. "Those were your odds. And, Danielle Northsky is in the same bracket with Dawn McFarlane?" James nods his head. The Mathematician starts salivating at the mouth and mumbles something like "Bra and panties…" James ignores him and continues where he left off. "As I was saying, this bracket also has Hoyt Williams, The Farmer, Digital Mortality, Kaden Calix, and Shadow, whose a former PTC Extreme Champion." James shakes his head. "I feel sorry for my UWF buddies Axel Action and Sledge." The Mathematician asks "Why?" James replies "For getting put in this bracket of death. I mean, this bracket's loaded even more than the first one was!" The Mathematician starts punching some numbers into his calculator and comes up with James' odds to win that bracket if he were in it. The Mathematician says "James, if you were in Bracket Two, the odds of you winning it would be Five hundred thousand-to-one." A little smirk comes across James' face. He was almost expecting to hear something like that. So, naturally, he replies "Bloody marvelous. I have no chance to win brackets I'm not even in. Oh great. That really makes me feel good. Say, what's my tournament power ranking anyway?" The Mathematician replies "You're officially ranked number one hundred fifteen." James looks over at him and raises an eyebrow. "That low, huh?" he asks in an inquisitive tone. The Mathematician nods his head. James shakes his head and goes back to looking over the fax he received. "Well, I guess that leads me to bracket three," James says with a sense of wonder, just thinking about who will be in this bracket after the last two brackets. He scans over the third bracket and sees some familiar names. A lot of them, actually. "Holy crap…Pete the PG Tips Chimp is back!" James yelps this out in an excited tone. "I love that frickin' monkey!" The Mathematician begins computing some more numbers into his calculator. "The origin of the species is the tip of a monkey's banana," the Mathematician says in a serious tone. James ignores him and continues looking over the list. "John Adams should do good…except for the fact he's facing Pete." James scans over the bracket names some more. "Christian Montiero, Stan 'The Pitfighter' Jones, and Blaise 'Hazard McClancy, oh my." Then, James notices two matches in a row that almost make him crap his pants right then and there. "Oh my God!" James exclaims in an excited tone. "PTC World Champion Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins is facing Angelo Deville! And then, the winner faces either Patty O'Shea or the Uber Judge!" James is almost hyperventilating at this point. The Mathematician replies "Well, upon hearing that, the odds of you winning the tournament went up two whole percentage points." James gives him a look. "Two whole percentage points?" James asks in a questionable tone. "Oh well. Nobody thinks I'm going to win so I guess it doesn't really matter." James looks over the final list of people in the bracket and doesn't see his name. "Peter Vetra, Tony Stecchino, Butterfly Hamada, Virgil Ambrosi, Surreal, Teresa Tomas, and Amy Silveira are in this bracket, but I am not. That bracket is even tougher than the last two! This is crazy! So I guess that means I'm in the hardest bracket of them all. Great…as if I didn't have enough problems at the moment." The Mathematician replies "If you were in that bracket, your odds of winning would be one million-to-one." James seems uninterested in the odds at this point and seems more interested in seeing who his opponent is. Sure enough, he's in the second match of the bracket facing someone from the WWA named Jorge Samuelsson. James can only shake his head and laugh. "Jorge Samuelsson?" he says in a very cold, unimpressed tone. "A seven foot tall Viking with a Mexican name? Give me a break. I can rip this guy to shreds just talking and not having any motions. No moving around and I can beat him. I can be a quadriplegic and do nothing and still go on to round two. But, then again, maybe I'm underestimating him. He did take the Shaman out of wrestling for a couple of months. But Jorge has an even more dangerous animal than the Shaman to worry about…and that animal is James von Doom." James has absolutely no fear of Jorge Samuelsson. He in fact seems more interested in what lies ahead in the tournament's later rounds, especially in his own bracket. James has an sudden sense of anticipation run through his body and he quickly looks over to see who else happens to be in his bracket. One name stands out immediately like a sore thumb. "Emilio Rage," he says aloud. "If I get past round one, I face Emilio Rage. A former PTC Global Champion…a guy who lasted longer in a PRIME ring than anybody…and my opponent if I survive Jorge the Viking." James confidence level is not dismayed yet, but it's starting to quickly fall apart as he looks over the names of the people in his bracket. "Kid Krazie and Ethan Frost are some of my UWF alumni. Going by what I've seen, Frost should win that battle. Fred Grest is one of the more underrated wrestlers out there." But then, something catches James' eye. A match that, to him, will decide his fate. A match that is the catalyst for this entire bracket. A battle that will decide who will win the top half of the Fourth Bracket. "Spartan versus the Legend of Pete…" James utters aloud with a sense of disbelief. "Oh my God. Pete's in this thing and so is Spartan! Just great. Just what I need. IF I survive Emilio, Jorge, Krazie, Frost, and Grest, then I have to deal with one of those two. Great, just great. Exactly what I need." James scans over the bottom half of the bracket and is even more humbled by who he might be facing. "Tessa's in my bracket, too." The Mathematician's eyes light up and his mouth starts to salivate at the very through of the female wrestler Tessa Windsor. "Perky breasts," he says aloud as James looks at him weirdly. "Subtle skin, glowing eyes… (pause) I think I'm becoming horny." James rolls his eyes and looks over the bottom half of his bracket again. "Scottywood, Jimmy Bong, Alec Lancer, Dr. Collin Gilchrist, Saloak, Chris Collins, Tyler O'Neill…" And then, he notices it. Yet another bracket altering super match in his bracket. "Kenny Mercury's facing Brandon Youngblood," James mutters out loud. "Why me? Why do I have to be in the Super Bracket of Doom, huh? Is it just my luck that this happened? Why does my luck always have to be so bad?" But his luck has been bad ever since he could remember, so he guesses this is normal for him. Bad luck seemed to follow him wherever he went, even tournaments. Nothing ever seemed to go his way, no matter how hard he tried. But this time would be different. This time, he had one thing going different than all those times before. This time he had one thing in himself that he didn't have before. He finally found the secret to success. He figured out what he had to do. He had it now. No one could beat him. Why? All he had to do was… BELIEVE.
