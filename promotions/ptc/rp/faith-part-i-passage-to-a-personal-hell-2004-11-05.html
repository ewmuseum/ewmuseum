Date: 2004-11-05 Faith – Part I: Passage to a Personal Hell “I had a great time tonight,” she says with a sincere smile. Even in the shadow of the ground floor of her apartment block, Wendy Sanchez exuberates beauty. With only the orange glow of the streetlights offering any kind of light to the immediate surroundings, it shines off the soft pale skin of her plush cheeks ever enhancing the vision of her special smile of satisfaction. She just radiates happiness. “Me too,” I softly say grinning blissfully, taking in the moment for all its greatness. We stop… no word spoken between us… we just stand there on the entrance to the apartment block, staring into each other’s eyes. That same blissful smile never waning on our faces. I take a step forward towards her, us now standing inches from each other, I can feel her sweet breath as it strokes across my cheek. With our eyes never leaving its gaze on each other’s, I tilt my head forwards and caress her lips with mine. Her arms wrap around my shoulders and neck, her hands feeling through the back of my neck. My arms reach around her waist, pulling her finely toned body pressing against mine. Our lips meet again, my lower lip pulling into hers with her tongue running across it slowly. I gently slide my hand back and forth up her back. Her hands begin to stroke the back of my head as I open my mouth slightly to slide my tongue into her mouth. It presses against hers in an impact of pure wet passion. Both of us let out a moan of pleasure and our tongues slowly caress each other sliding back and forth between each mouth. This is amazing, the most sensual kiss I have ever experienced, and as I start to feel that this should never end, it ends. Wendy slowly retracts her kiss and pulls her head away from mine. We’re both still smiling, but now it’s more because of being bashful and not knowing what to say next! We both chuckle silently at the humour of not knowing what to say, “I better go,” she says. “Yeah, you better get some sleep,” I say almost dejectedly at not being able to share the moment with her any longer, “you’ve got work tomorrow.” “Yeah,” she says, “unlike some people who have the week off!” “Hah!” I say continuing the playful banter, “You’ve seen who my opponent is next, haven’t you? Do you think I’m going to spend my week hanging around?” We both laugh in unison. We both know that there’s no way I’d be taking the week off with the next opponent I have in this tournament. Wendy turns and slides the key into the lock, turning it and walking further into the darkness of her apartment. I can’t even see her now the shadows are too dark, but a slight glimmer from her glistening blue eyes reminds me that she’s still there. “Good night Dave,” she whispers contentedly. “Good night Wendy,” I whisper in reply and with that the door shuts with a sharp wooden clunk, the toll ending our wonderful night. -----=====|=====----- It’s only early evening, but it’s getting dark pretty early this time of the year. We were only out for a meal anyway and we never intended to stay out too late. It’s quite a walk back to the apartment you know, I’m not even too sure I know which way I’m going. I think it’s a left here, down this long street. To be honest they all look the same to me, just a long, narrow road with barely enough room to walk without getting in the way of traffic, not that there’s any traffic anyway. The depths of D.C., the urban heart of the city, where you’d expect at least a little life, but the whole place just seems lifeless. Like a ghost town, only every house looks the same. I don’t know if it’s the shadows being cast across the place, but they all look the same symmetrical; same sized; same grey houses, one after the other. I should have got a taxi back really, but I like the bus. There’s something special about a practically empty bus in the evening. Sitting at the back of the bus with our own personal space, left alone so we can chat without having to worry about other people listening in. She’s great to talk to, Wendy is. We just found the whole night so laid back, not like some dates where you get those long silent uncomfortable pauses, where you don’t know what to say next, that’s probably due to us both being chatterboxes! Ow…! What was that? Something just hit me on the back of the head! Turning my head to see what it is that hit me, all I see is the same thing I’ve been seeing for the last 10 minutes, just a long, empty seemingly never-ending street with endless houses down either side, each looking identical. I’m sure something just hit me though… Ah, it was probably nothing. It was a really nice thing of Wendy to suggest this for tonight. What better way to take my mind off of… you know… my next match… than to go on a date with Wendy to a nice Italian restaurant. Lasagne, red wine, and endless conversation about days passed. It was sheer bliss. Wendy had tagliatelle. I just thought it was a dessert. I always get that flaming dish mixed up with tiramisu! Ow! What the…? That’s it! Who’s throwing that at me! I turn my head really sharply to catch the culprit, but there is nothing behind me, nothing but a long, narrow road. There’s no way someone could have gotten to somewhere to hide that quick, no way. Ouch! What the…? Ow…! My… head… “AAAAAAAAAAAAAGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” A sharp piercing ultra-high pitched noise penetrating my mind and a shock straight down my body paralysing me on the spot. My entire body loses all control, I slowly lean to one side and find myself floating through the air… only I’m not floating. It feels like I’m floating, at least at first, as my semi-conscious haze distorts my thought process, I suddenly realise that I’m not floating… I’m falling… and although it all seems in slow motion, I suddenly hit the floor on my right shoulder, my head snapping over it and hitting the tarmac road with a sickeningly blunt thud. -----=====|=====----- “Daaave…? Oh Daaaaaave…?” Almost melodic but also very mocking, the voice echoes through my head alerting me even in my semi-conscious state. How does he know my name? I don’t recognise the voice at all. The intense heat of fire, the sound of burning wood and a swirling breeze sending a chill across the back of my neck are the next two things. I open my eyes slowly to investigate, especially to find the person that knows my name. It seems I’m sitting with my legs astride in front of me, and my back pressing against a rough brick wall. But it’s still that same street that I have been on for most of the journey home. My eyes dart from side to side rapidly in an attempt to detect the source of the voice. Looking sharply to my left, nothing but an empty endless road with rows of houses on each side. I sharply look right, but same thing, all the way to the horizon. Unless it was someone in one of the houses… I attempt to tilt my body forwards to get to my feet, but I… I can’t move. Looking down at my chest and shoulders, there doesn’t seem to be any kind of restraints holding me down. I just can’t move for some reason. Oh no… This isn’t good… I’m stuck in the middle of nowhere and I can’t move a single muscle in my body. Come on Dave, move for fuck’s sake. I look up my body to my trainers and try desperately hard to move my feet. Even just an inch, something, please… No good, it is like something engulfing my entire body is stopping me from moving anything above my neck. Like a force field is preventing me from moving anything. “Ah… you’re awake I see.” That sound again, to the left of me. I sharply turn my head to the left and scan the immediate surroundings… nothing. “There’s no point in trying to find me, you won’t see me no matter how hard you try.” To the right, I sharply turn my head the other way… nothing. I look up above me, try and turn my head as much as possible to look behind me, but there is only a wall. That was when it hit me… the sudden realisation of the source of the sound. The reason why whenever I looked left, I heard it to the right, and whenever I looked to the right I heard it to the left. The reason why I look all around and can’t see the source of the sound… is because it’s coming from… me. It’s good that you’ve finally realised. I’m sure that there are a thousand and one questions that you want to ask. Please… feel free. Who the hell is this bl- Ah, I had a feeling that would be your first question. And no, I am not a ‘bloke’, as such. I am much, much more than just that. I am Lucifer… I am ‘The Devil’… And more importantly… The Devil lowered his voice for added effect, at a whisper, the words coming from my own mouth, in his own eloquent-yet-fearsome voice, sending chills throughout my body, the sharpness and seriousness of the tone. …Your worst nightmare As much as I don’t want to believe it, it’s far-fetched enough to have someone talk to you by controlling your actions and making you talk to yourself, so compared to that, this is quite a possibility. But something really bothering me now, and a further question that needs answering. What do you want with me? I want nothing with you. In fact, your aura of innocence and ignorance at the evils of the world disgust me. I feel physically sick just being in your presence. You’re putrid. What I want I always gain. And in the whole of existence there will never be a single moment when I would need you to aid me in anything, ever. The problem I have is your next encounter. You see, in winning your last match you have gained much more than you bargained for, much, much, more. You have gained a date with The Deville. But… something that you do not realise, is that in gaining a date with The Deville, you have subsequently also gained a date… with The Devil. And David, I promise you, this will not be the kind of date that you’ve just had with your precious Wendy, I can assure you of that. So you see, it is not what I want with you that is the potent question. The question is, what do I want with your next combatant. Well, I’m not in the mood to acquaint you on such matters. What is more important to you right now is what I am going to do to you to ensure that my colleague progresses to the next stage… well before I am done with you, I will try my damnedest to stop you from competing in this match. But I am not satisfied in just killing you. You’re much too pitiful to deserve such an abrupt end to your miserable life. I am going to torture you, I am going to put you through the worst things you could possibly go through, I am going to break you down, bit by bit, bone by bone, sinew by sinew. Welcome David… to your personal hell. As those last words were spoken, I am alerted to a red-painted tin barrel standing proudly in front of me, with wood protruding from the top and flames flailing wildly from them. For a personal hell, this tin barrel is nothing much. It’s hardly something intimidating, let alone hell. You think too hastily, David. You fail to realise what this small insignificant tin barrel is for. I felt it most fitting that a small insignificant tin barrel would be used to end the life of a small insignificant… duck. Don’t you fucking dare! Or what…? Let me guess, firstly, you’ll swear to God. I have no idea why, but people like you always seem to swear to God when you’re making idle threats. Then what…? You’re going to kill me? Tell me David, with your limited knowledge in… well… anything. How exactly are you planning on killing me? … … Hmm…? That’s what I thought. Of course, after I’ve finished with you, you will be begging me to use this barrel to end your ridiculous plastic duck’s existence, begging me to do it over and over again. There will be no remorse in your voice, no doubts, for what I am going to do to you will be infinitely worse than this. This is trivial in comparison. But I feel like a warm-up. A starter course, as it were. Now, watch carefully. You are about to end your duck’s life. I find myself reaching across my chest to the breast pocket on my coat, carefully pinching the zip and pulling it slowly downwards, these actions are not controlled by me, but by someone else. The Devil is controlling my actions, and I’m just going to have to watch it all happen. There is nothing I can do. And the sick bastard is doing it all as slow as possible, to savour the moment as much as possible. I find it quite pertinent that I am about to end your little friend’s life. There are quite a lot of similarities between you and your plastic counterpart, if you didn’t realise. For one, the small yellow duck is utterly innocent. You are utterly innocent. Then of course there is the fact that this little fellow continues day after day to defy the odds, something that you strive for in every moment of your life. Then of course there is the most important fact of all, it all ends now. Oh no, he’s making me reach into my pocket, pulling out the symbol of everything that I stand for, the object of my very being, and my greatest friend in the whole wide world. Ducky - the small plastic yellow bath toy. And he won’t even give me the dignity of walking over to the tin barrel to place the duck into it. As I sit here with my legs stretched outward in front of me, instead of placing the duck gently into the barrel, he has me take my arm backwards ready to throw him into the fire from this distance. How utterly distasteful, but what more would you expect from an entity of pure evil? I swing my arm forward, letting go of Ducky and leaving him careering into the air. I can barely watch, but a last bit of hope remains that somehow the throw is inaccurate. And then the duck hits the rim of the barrel… But despairingly falls into the barrel rather than away from it. Shrouded by the red tin, I am only left to imagine the scenes of burning plastic only a few feet from where I sit. My precious ducky… is dead. TO BE CONTINUED…
