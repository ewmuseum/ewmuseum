CWMC #2 Homo/Phobic

1 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 29th… no, it's not a Black Lotus promo… and it's obviously none of the other competitors promos either~! The jingle of bells rings out as Tom enters the quaint little shop located in the back streets of Chinatown. The shop is a maze of shelving, all of them cluttered with junk that is cleverly marketed as Souvenirs, Gadgets and Antiques. A tiny white bearded china man approaches, his apparently frail body cloaked in an all devouring robe. Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "Can I help you sir?" Tom: "You certainly can, I'm looking for a gift for my friend… for Halloween." Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "… and you came to a Junk store?" Tom: "Well… I don't like him that much, we found out he was gay last Halloween see, we had a party and he came as a Vampire… much to the horror of our guest his version of 'I want to suck your blood' didn't meet their expectations. That said; it was a great success. Everyone said that it was the scariest darn Halloween party they'd ever been to." Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "I see… then we have many, many things for you?" The wise man grabs a plunger off the side. Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "How about an ancient Evil Chinese Wizard's Wand?" Tom: "Hmm. No, I don't really want to encourage him, goodness knows what he'll use that for." Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "Well, have a look around, anything you see here is for sale." Tom nods as the old man shuffles back behind the counter. Tom browsers along the shelf in front of him. Tom: "Flower pot, no… Home pregnancy test, no… Black Lotus Series One promos on DVD, definitely not…" Tom suddenly stops scanning and looks past his shoulder, towards the back of the shop. Tom: "What was that?" The Chinese man regards him. Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "I didn't hear anything." *THUD* Tom: "There! There it is again!" Tom begins to walk towards the back of the shop. The Shop Keeper moves out from behind the counter and intercepts Tom as he nears a large blanket that conceals something beneath. Beneath the Blanket: "Ouch!" Tom grins, he gazes at the Shop Keeper. Tom: "What is it?" The Chinese man sighs and removes the blanket. A tiny, two-foot tall green creature with a prehensile tail stares up at the two men through a bamboo cage. The creature tries to step to the back of his cage, but is prevented from doing so by his finger that is caught in the lock. Creature: "OW!" The Chinese man reaches to the lock and helps set the creature free. The Creature rubs his sore finger. Creature: "Ouch, ouch, ouch." Tom beams with excited joy. Tom: "What is it? I want it? Can I buy it? Oo-Oo, what is it, is it a Goblin? Bobby loves a good Goblin!" Tom, now smiling even more so, turns to the Shop Keeper for an answer. Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "It's a GremlIMP." Tom: "A GremlIMP?" The old man nods. Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "And it's not for sale!" Tom: "But you said everything was for sale." Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "Not this!" Tom stares at the creature. Tom: "But he has a price sticker on his ear…" The GremlIMP looks at his ear curiously. Tom: "It says five ninety nine." Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "It's not for sale… it is the last of its kind?" The GremlIMP looks saddened. GremlIMP: "Mom?" Tom: "But it has a Buy One Get One Free offer on its other ear!" The Chinese man throws the blanket over the cage again. Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "NOT FOR SALE! Too dangerous!" Tom laughs. Tom: "That little thing! Hehehehe." Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "Either buy the Plunger or get out of my shop!" Tom: "How much do you want? I'll give you an hundred bucks?" The man whacks him with his stick. Tom: "Ow!" Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "Get out!" *WHACK* Tom: "OW!" Stereotypical Wise Chinaman: "Out, OUT, OUT!!! I say!" Tom flees from the shop. October 30th … "Ho-Ho-Ho-Ho-Ho!" The door to Bobby Brain Bangemhard's PTC rented apartment is opened as the joyous Tom walks through the door. Bobby leaps off the bed to greet him. Tom smiles and holds out a large gift-wrapped box. Boozler: "Is that for me? For Halloween?" Tom nods and hands it to Bobby. Gimp enters the scene, his tiny mouth zipper curled up into a sadistic grin. Tom hands Gimp a smaller gift. Gimp: "Mhank mhou mho mhuch!" Tom: "Now don't open that till Halloween Gimp. Bobby?" Bobby has his ear to the gift. Tom: "You'll have to open yours now, it won't keep till Halloween." Bobby eyes bulge almost as large as his tight pink short shorts. Bobby: "Is it a bat!? That'll go great with my vampire outfit" Bobby shakes it vigorously… a few muffled murmurs emanate from within. Tom: "Be careful Bobby!!!" Bobby: "It is a bat!!!" Bobby begins to unwrap the present with all haste, soon enough he is down to an old Oriental looking box. He looks at Tom with eager anticipation, Tom grins back… Bobby goes to open the box, unhinging the catch… suddenly a green creature leaps out. GremlIMP: "Hello!" Bobby leaps back with a start, tossing the box onto the bed. Bobby: "Eeeeeek!" Tom lurches forward towards the box. Bobby: "What the hell is it, sugar?" Tom clambers onto the bed and rights the box. Tom: "It's a GremlIMP, Bobby." Tom reaches inside the box where the GremlIMP rubs its sore head. Tom scoops the tiny creature out and cradles it, the tiny creature's mouth quivers, its claws sinking into the jacket of Tom as it fearfully clings to it. Bobby approaches. Bobby: "Where did you get it?" Tom: "I robbed a wacky Chinaman for it." Bobby leans in. Bobby: "Eww, it's not very pretty is it." The GremlIMP pouts. Tom: "Sure it is… you just have to look past the green, slimy, yellow eyed, lashing poke-your-eye-out-in-a-flash tail, clawed hands and feet exterior." Bobby closes in for a closer examination. Bobby: "Hey! You're right… he is cute." Bobby carefully takes it from Tom's arms and draws the tiny creature into his own cradling embrace, rocking it gently. Bobby: "Awww." Tom grins… result! Tom: "Let's take a photo." Tom dashes over to the dressing table and picks up the Polaroid camera. Tom: "Bobby! GremlIMP!" Bobby and the GremlIMP turn towards the camera. Tom: "Say cheese." The camera flashes! GremlIMP leaps into the air. GremlIMP: "IEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIE!" Tom immediately drops the camera as Bobby tries to calm the GremlIMP. Tom: "Shit!" Bobby: "What's up with him? Doesn't he like his photo being taken, sugar?" Tom: "I forgot… there's some important rules that we have to follow." The GremlIMP returns to quivering within Bobby's embrace. Tom digs through his pocket and plucks out a piece of paper and reads from it. Tom: "The first rule of Fight Club is that you do not talk about Fight Club! The second rule of Fight Club… wait a minute…" Tom inspects the paper, with a shake of his head he turns it around. Tom: "Sorry… wrong rules. The first rule is that he doesn't like bright light… we just saw that, but more IMPortantly sunlight will kill him, so keep him away from sunlight at all costs." The quivering GremlIMP nods. Bobby: "Cool… a vampire GremlIMP, an even better accessory for my costume." Tom: "The second rule is, never get him wet… not even to clean him." Boozler: "So that's what that smell is." GremlIMP: "Hey!" Boozler: "So why can't we wet him?" Tom: "Apparently, if we do, it'll multiply." Boozler: "Really!?" Tom nods. GremlIMP: "Yeah… god really jipped us on that score, sort of takes the fun out of the whole reproduction process when all you've got to look forward to at the end of a good date with a glass of water is a good soaking." Boozler: "Gimp happens to like a date where it ends with a good session of water sports, don't you Gimpypoos?" The leather clad gimp nods. Tom: "But the most important rule… and the Chinese man stressed this just before he lost consciousness, is that we must never, EVER~! Feed it Red Bull after midnight." GremlIMP's tiny ears prick up. Boozler: "Why, sugar?" Tom: "Apparently it turns into a little arsehole." Boozler gasps. Boozler: "Well, we certainly don't want any little arseholes around here, so I'll be very careful of that!" Bobby bites his lip. Bobby: "When you say I can't give him Red Bull after midnight… exactly when does that expire?" Tom: "Sorry?" Bobby: "Well, technically speaking… and I hate to point out a flaw in your plot, but isn't any time technically after Midnight… bar midnight itself?" Tom scratches his head. Tom: "Good point… I guess… ok… Don't feed him Red Bull between zero hundred and one and zero-eight hundred hours, ok?" Bobby nods. Tom: "Oh, and between meals… I don't want him spoiling his lunch." Bobby smiles at his, now, cute GremlIMP. Bobby: "We're gonna have so much fun my little GremlIMPypoos." GremlIMP: "Red Bull?" Bobby looks at Tom. Tom: "Sure, it's not between midnight and eight a.m. or in between a meal… go for it, I bought a case this morning for the little tyke." The scene slowly fades… October 31st 01:00 am - Halloween - OoOoOoOoOooooooo The scene opens back up again several hours later. Bobby and a reluctant Shane Stone sit on the bed, their face illuminated by the flicker of the TV. Bobby tries to nestle into Shane but Shane jabs his elbow into Bobby's ribs. "Hello. Hello." Bobby looks over the edge of the bed as the tiny sweet GremlIMP stands there, staring up with wide eyes. Bobby: "Ohhh… you're so cute. Do you want to come on the bed with Mommy and Daddy?" Shane scowls and tries to climb out of bed, receiving a painful reminder for his efforts as his rope bonds dig into his sore ankles. Bobby picks the tiny GremlIMP up from the floor and places him gently onto the bed. GremlIMP performs a cute little dance. [:fruit:] Bobby smiles. Bobby: "Sooo sweet." GremlIMP stops. GremlIMP: "Red Bull?" Bobby pauses for thought. Bobby: "It's a bit late dear…" GremlIMP points at the red LCD panel of the alarm clock… it flashes 11:50 pm. Bobby: "Hmm… well, even though the clock's seems to have been reading that for an hour now, and you have a pair of Clock-Tampering Wire Cutters in your hand, and me and Shane have just finished watching the Playgirl 12:50 free view… I guess it's still not quite midnight… apparently." Bobby leans towards the bedside table and grabs a can of Red Bull. He offers it GremlIMP. GremlIMP: "Thank you." GremlIMP wastes no time in cracking open the can and immediately, greedily, guzzling it down. Bobby: "Slow down there sugar, you'll give yourself wind." GremlIMP emerges from behind the large can. GremlIMP: "WASSSSSUP?!" Bobby regards it oddly. Bobby: "Watching GremlIMP, having a Bull." GremlIMP: "You're damn friking right you queer ass friking faggot!" GremlIMP tosses the empty can at Shane's head. Shane: "OW!" Bobby: "Hey! Naughty cute GremlIMP!" The GremlIMP grins… his tiny green face contorting into a twisted smile that sets off rows upon rows of needle sharp teeth. Bobby nervously looks at his pink wrist watch… 01:10 am Bobby: "NOOOOOOOO!" GremlIMP: "Yes! Yes!" The panicked Bobby motions to get up, but the tiny GremlIMP kicks Bobby's chest, pinning him back on his cushion. GremlIMP: "Oh no you friking don't god dammit! I've had to spend two friking days with you bunch of benders and I have some things I want to get off my chest, so you just sit there and be quiet duck!" Shane: "Hey, I'm not a bender!" GremlIMP: "Don't even get me started on you jobber boy. You've always slept with men, you've always been a little partial to the old animal friking antics, the only difference now is that you don't get to be on friking top! So shut it, fuck mutt!" Shane purses his lips tightly together. GremlIMP: "First of all… I'm not a friking Goblin, ok?!" Bobby nods. GremlIMP: "Secondly… I'm not cute, not to you, you friking unnatural retards… I'm friking scary, I'm friking terrifying… rarrrrr! Understand god dammit!?!?" Bobby and Shane nod. GremlIMP: "Finally, you never, ever mention my arsehole again… and you certainly don't friking touch it, understand?" Bobby frowns and swats the little GremlIMP with a keen blow to the head, sending the little critter flying off of the bed. Bobby: "Now he's just being silly." A silence descends. From the bed Bobby looks about the room… scanning it for the deranged little GremlIMP. He turns to Shane. Bobby: "He's under the bed isn't he?" Shane shakes his head. Bobby: "Do you think if I look under the bed he'll try to tear my face off?" Shane shakes his head again. Bobby bends over the edge to investigate… Shane maliciously grins. Bobby: "AAARRRRRRGGGGGGHHHHHHH!" Bobby leaps off the bed, the tiny GremlIMP clinging on his head. GremlIMP: "Ieieieieieieieieieieie!" Bobby tries in vain to prise it off as the GremlIMP claws away. Shane: "Kill him! Kill him!!!" Bobby: "I'm trying, sugar!" Shane: "Was I talking to you?" GremlIMP: "Die god dammit, dieieieieieieieieie!" Bobby stumbles onto the floor. GremlIMP is quick to take advantage and yanks Bobby's hair, using it to slam Bobby's head into the floor time after time after time. Shane: "Go GremlIMP, Go GremlIMP, Go GremlIMP!" Bobby desperately, blindly, reaches out… catching hold of one of his pink high-heel Gucci's. Bobby lashes out with the high heel. GremlIMP: "ARGH!" The GremlIMP runs into the wardrobe and slams the door shut. Bobby scrambles to his knees and tightens his grip on the shoe… he looks at the disappointed Shane. Boozler: "Don't worry, sugar, I'm ok." Shane slams his head into the pillow with frustration. Bobby looks back towards the wardrobe. Bobby: "This thing is EVIL~!" Bobby cautiously moves towards the wardrobe… his fingers coil around the handle whilst drawing his weapon high above his head. The door bursts open as the cackling GremlIMP, riding atop a toy robot, leaps out. Bobby falls backwards and onto the bed as the GremlIMP lands upon the windowsill. The GremlIMP stares at the odd couple. GremlIMP: "Hah, ha, ha, ha… see you later you friking queer boys, hah, ha, ha, ha!" The Toy robot smashes through the window, escaping into the night. Bobby: "What a jerk. Do you think we should try and capture them?" Shane sighs. Bobby: "You're right… how much trouble can a GremlIMP and a Robot cause on Halloween?"
