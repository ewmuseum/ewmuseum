Interview….

30 November 2005

Date: 2005-11-30 "Good afternoon. This is your host Chris S. I am here with Stephen Baker also known as SuperNova. Before we start I would just like to say that With the Hype for GTT5 almost over it seems that a certain few superstars have put up a good showing. Anyways enough rambling." Stephen adjusts his position as the camera focuses on him. "Well Stephen as you know GTT5 is a special event in Prime Time Centrals history. Over the many years we have seen such winners as Angelo Deville and Dr Curiosity. I have to say you’re in for a tough ride with this tournament" Smith says with an laugher in his speech. "Well yes as it would seem the odds are very high in me even getting past the first round. But as always I am sure that this Tournament like no other will have some upsets. I just pray that maybe I can be one of those upsets" Stephen says confidently. "To be frank Stephen your opponent has a vast amount of tournament experience where this will be you’re first? Smith pauses for a second before adjusting his pose. "But I ask why there is so much disappointment and disbelief? Surly you must be confident in going into your fist round match? Says smith with a sigh in his voice. "Yes there is but yet as all ways I have to prepare mentally and physically as this will be the match of my life." Says Stephen nervously. As he brushes his hand through his messy hair. Smith then picks up a clip board form down next to him. He begins to flick through the pages rapidly. He suddenly halts as he reads the writing. "I have here some questions form some GTT5 followers. Would it be ok if you answered them?" "Yes I have nothing against answering questions, in fact I find it most enjoyable" Stephen chuckles to himself as he takes a drink of water. "Well this question is from a guy called Alex. A Smug expression arises on Stephens face." He wants to know where you are aiming to get in GTT5." "To be honest I do not think about where I will get to but how I perform if I go out in the first round to Digital Mortality, yes it will be upsetting but that’s what people expect. If I give it my all I can walk away happy, just knowing that I put on a good show for the fans will satisfy me needs." Stephen begins to slouch in his chair, his arms on his knees. Stephen looks into the camera and roles his eyes. It seems Stephen is becoming bored. "Well Stephen, we only have time for one more question. This is from Christian. He wants to know if the recent events with Prime Time central will put a lag on your performance ore boost it?" "To be frank what happened in the past is past, I do not care to think about what happened but what is about to happen. Dwelling In the past is futile. It is the sort of thing that would drive a human crazy." Stephen pauses as he takes another sip of water. "The only thing that is to my concern is Digital Mortality. Yes I am the underdog but that’s how I like it." Stephen finishes his sentence and stands up. He walks off the set. "It would seem we have come to the end of our show. Thanks for watching. This is Chris S. signing off."
