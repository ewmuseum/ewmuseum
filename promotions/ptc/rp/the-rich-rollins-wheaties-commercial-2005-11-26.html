The Rich Rollins Wheaties Commercial

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 [We fade in to your average every day kitchen table, complete with red checkered tablecloth and matching sets of silverware wrapped in neat little solid fabric napkins. Rich Rollins sits alone shoveling spoonfuls of cereal into his mouth, paying little heed to any of the proper mannerisms our mothers have pounded into our heads for generations. Casually looking up, the Renegade grins around a mouthful of milk and soggy whole grain wheat thins.] Renegade: You know what I love most about waking up? No, it’s not a catchy coffee jingle. It takes a bowl full of champion-caliber carbohydrates and fibers to keep a PTC Global Champion going, and Wheaties is, after all, the Breakfast of Champions! [With a dramatic flourish, Rich Rollins turns the Wheaties box on the table to face the camera. On the front panel is a full-size picture of the Renegade, the PTC Global title strapped snug around his waist.] Renegade: Wheaties’ 100% whole grain flakes are hearty and crispy, making them an easy and delicious way to …(he squints off-camera) get more whole grain? Jesus, who writes this shit? Boom Operator: (off-camera) Err… we pulled it off the website. Renegade: It’s a fucking box of Wheaties. You guys have been shilling this shit for decades! I think the public knows how healthy it is at this point. [Jerking to his feet, the Renegade grabs the box of Wheaties and places it center stage, tapping an index finger on his own commercialized face.] Renegade: You need to change up the image, fellas. This is the face of the fuckin’ future! Nobody wants to see some grizzled old prima donna football dickhead chowing down on some cereal he doesn’t even eat. Toss a half empty bottle of Methyl 1-P on the table here and maybe people would buy it. [Sitting back down, Rich Rollins picks his spoon back up and brings a slopping mouthful halfway to his waiting lips before stopping, tossing the camera a devious wink.] Renegade: Stay in school, kids.
