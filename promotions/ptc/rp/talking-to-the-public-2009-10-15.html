New Horizons MaryLynnMay posted @ blogspot.com on January 14th, 2007 So I’ve stopped using livejournal. Sorry LJ. But to be honest? I only started using you because I had a lot of other friends doing the same. I was intrigued by the community. As it died, so did my interest in you. So I’ve signed up for this new service called blogs, or what have you. Computer technology isn’t exactly a specialty of mine. Granted, I’ve been working on the side as a secretary two days a week for four hours a clip, but that’s just word processing and answering phones. Trained chimps could do it. But I left LJ for another reason besides just dropping to a new service. I wanted to have a fresh start. I feel, rejuvenated. I feel, lively, upbeat, happy again. I have passion. I’ve found something new to love. Professional Wrestling. Sure! Sure! You’re gonna laugh, looking at littl’ ol’ 115 pound etherweight over here with the short red curls and the mouse like nose gonna hit you in your face with a string bean like legs and stick like arms swinging away. But hey, listen, I love it. I love getting in the ring, taking a bump or flying through the air. There’s something so freeing. You can put on a whole new personality or just be yourself. You can fixate on a single personality trait and exploit and explore it. And with every match, comes something new to learn. It’s not like law. It’s concrete. You can study it by reading it in a book. There’s something so satisfying about attempting to concur something so… physical. I mean. I never played Field Hockey… Sorry for the delay MaryLynnMay posted @ blogspot.com on February 14th, 2007 It’s Valentine’s Day. It’s the only reason I have time to post on this stupid little blogger service. I’ve just been so busy lately, I had to quit my secretary gig. Three hours a day at the gym and training, another 5 hours studying, plus class? Commuting? It’s killing me. I haven’t talked to Liz in ages, haven’t even seen her in a month and she’s my roommate! I mean, by God! But I’m not having a hard time getting out of bed in the morning anymore. And I don’t mind being alone on Valentine’s Day. Because I’m gonna go for a little run, grab a yogurt, and watch me some Silver & Gold matches. Lindsay Troy is my hero. Holidays! MaryLynnMay posted @ blogspot.com on March 17th, 2007 Wow. It seems I only have the chance to post when schools or the Odessa Dungeon is closed. I just have too much to think, I hardly have the time to put it out on paper anymore. Hell, I have a hard time writing my papers for classes, yet alone pontificate about myself like a pompous jackass. Clitty Twat Bitchenstein or something. Which is, hilariously enough, the name Flyer wanted to give me for my first match up. He said it was a “rib” that most trainers would play on their younger stars. See if they have the desire to work through a, as he put it, shitty gimmick. He says he hardly curses but it seems every other word out of his mouth is a curse word these days. I talked to him. It’s like if he’s not on camera… Perhaps he can join PRIME, curse up a storm with those folks. Oh man. PRIME. I would love to just be in the CROWD one day. Slight Lies! MaryLynnMay posted @ blogspot.com on April 20th, 2007 Yeah. I know. I said that I haven’t had time to write online much, but to be honest, I’ve been writing in a personal black journal these days. It’s much more private, and without being able to read other friend’s thoughts about their lives, I have less inclination to post mine publically for everyone to view. To be honest though, most of my friends are either in my study group or in my wrestling class. I’m just glad my friend Liz is in Medical school and she’s just over on a nearby table. We silently talk across the room and make our jokes. It really helps alleviate the stress. Beyond that, I’ve been hanging out on occasion with Luscious. He’s quite an intriguing individual. A bit timid if you would, but very endearing. Also, there’s Josh and Derek, two of Flyer’s former students. They’re there for a bit of refreshing and reconditioning. Seems they’ve got a big contract with Legacy of Champions on the table. Our beginner’s class ends tomorrow. After that, I up to Intermediate. And then Advanced. Much quicker than this whole Law thing. Should have to pass the bar sometime in the Fall, 2010? 2009 if I’m quick about it. Man. 2009. Wonder where I’ll be then? Graduation Day MaryLynnMay posted @ blogspot.com on April 23rd, 2007 Graduation went well. Luscious and I went out to a comedy club and we saw Edwards/Klein do some stand up. Klein’s really funny. Flyer then came out with a hook from off stage and dragged a kicking and protesting Edwards off stage. Flyer’s been taking an interest in me lately. He said for the intermediate class, he wanted to focus on my flexibility and my center of balance. Focusing on Agility and speed. He knows I’m never going to be able to over power anyone. He’s playing to my strengths. He’s an intelligent teacher. And apparently, he has ties to one Miss Lindsay Troy. Hell, he’s beat her! I called him a liar when he first told me that :-(. Moving to Advanced MaryLynnMay posted @ blogspot.com on September 25th, 2007 Flyer and I have really grown close these last few months. In fact, he’s promised to take me on the road as his star pupil. Flyer tried to join ACW and bring me in there but the place closed after one match of his. He got to face Alias and I was backstage for it. It was a great match, lots of tension in the air, fever pitch. It was like baseball playoffs. And yet still, that promotion can not survive. I wonder if I choose the right profession. I know I haven’t intellectually. The wrestling business is filled with death dismay and destruction. But choosing from the heart? Choosing what I love above what’s safe? Taking a risk for what you want? I can’t ever say that’s a wrong decision. I start in no brand Wrestling soon. It’s going to be a good environment to learn for me. I’m not going to have a wealth of responsibility. And I’ll be able to ease into my role. Most of what I’ll have to do is interfere in matches against my will. That’s pretty simple. I’ve already been given my clipboard weapon. I’m told to say it’s loaded with steel.
