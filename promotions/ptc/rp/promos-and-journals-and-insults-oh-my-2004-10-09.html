Promos and Journals and Insults, Oh my!

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 He was a no one (A zero, zero) Now he's a honcho (He's a hero) Here was a kid with his act down pat From zero to hero in no time flat Zero to hero just like that Zero to Hero? Hi! *Wave.* Did you miss me? I sure did. Considering I only tend to show up when My Hero is filming a promotional piece of some kind, I end up spending a lot of time missing myself. More than you ever want to know. But hey, such is the life of your non-existent semi-dyslexic rotarran. So, given that I wouldn't be here if a JaZon King promo wasn't imminent - even though I'm not looking forward to going back to missing myself - I won't waste your time, and we'll get right to it. But you don't know how hard it is to rambling from keep. We're back in front of our friendly neighbourhood brick wall. Somewhere high above, maybe Peter Parker's doing his thing. Oops. Sorry, I promised I wouldn't ramble. My bad. Now, was I where? Right. Brick wall. It's a simple setting, and in this case, JaZon likes it that way. Keeps the distractions away, and the focus on what he has to say. And in this particular case, since he sat in front of this very same wall last round, it also serves to bring a measure of consistency. Can't go wrong with consistency. Well... That is, unless you're consistently bad. But even if you're always terrible, you can take comfort in the fact that you're reliable, at least. And damn it, there I go again. Focus man, focus! Today, JaZon is standing before the wall, his upper back and shoulders supporting him as he leans against the brick surface. His attire today is fairly plain once again. He is just your average every day 21 year-old, after all. Aside from the fact that he spends his life hopping from arena to arena, and has parents who are the heads of an International Criminal organization, of course. But other than that - oh, and the fact that his former tag team partner is a CIA agent in pursuit of the very organization his parents run - he's just a regular guy. If that's "regular," I don't want to see "strange." But hey, I don't have eyes anyway. So, JaZon's wearing his standard blue jeans on the bottom, with a black hooded sweatshirt, with what appears to be a dragon etched in white across the front. Hold on a second... I know that dragon. It's Trogdor! The Burninator! He was a man, you know. A Dragon-man. So much for that whole not rambling thing, eh? Ah well. JaZon's ready to talk now, so that'll up me shut tib a rof. (JaZon) So, I made it to Round Three. Sure did, buddy! (JaZon) Thanks Saytch, for a solid match. It could have gone either way. But I'm glad it didn't. Smile. (JaZon) Even if it does mean I'm the underdog in my next match. The guy just about nobody knows, and even fewer expect to move on any further. Yep, that's me. Sad, but true. Put up your hand if you think JaZon has a snowball's chance in hell of making it to Round 4. If I had a hand, it'd be up, and it'd probably be one of the only ones. (JaZon) But that's nothing I wasn't prepared for. Hell, I was even looking forward to it. What better way to make a name for myself, to show that I've got what it takes, than to come in the unknown and end up one of the "dark-horses" of the Tournament? To go from zero to hero... Let me use this pause to reiterate that JaZon is already My Hero. (JaZon) And it may just be me, but I think taking Jonathan Sail out of this thing might be what I need to make that transition. Smile. (JaZon) It's funny, though. People around here know just about as much about Jon Sail as they do about me. I've done a little checking, and his name popped up here and there, but never for any extended period of time. There'd be a little hype about him and then he'd fall off the face of the earth. Shrug. (JaZon) But whether he's spent his career in obscurity or not, the guy's clearly good. So good that a lot of people consider him a favourite to go far. So good that his name's been mentioned with the likes of Angelo Deville and Seymour Almasy as potential winners. And Karina Wolfenden, and Doctor Curiosity and everyone else people think could take this thing. (JaZon) So, yeah, I really do think taking our good Mister Sail out of this tournament will open up a lot of eyes. Beating Jonathan Sail is the next step on my path from zero to hero. So I guess that's what I'm going to have to do. Smile. Stop. He's not done, but we're going to take a little bit of a break. *Wave.* I'll be back. Doubt Excerpt from the Journal of Jazon Koenig: September 12, 2004 So it begins. I'm in GTT4, Flinders bracket. From what I've seen and heard, looking into Primetime Central and a few of the competitors, it's going to be exactly what I thought. Huge. Massive. Difficult. The last thing I need right now is doubts, and yet I find myself wondering if I'm just going to end up failing again. Disappointing myself. I'm trying my best to push that to the back of my mind, but I'm still getting that feeling. I know I'm good. I know what I can do in that ring, and I know I can prove myself. But still, here I am, worried that I'm going to flop. I talked to Shadow again today. It's weird to say that, since there are two competitors in the GTT that go by that name. I'm fairly confident that neither of them are my mysterious confidant, even though I've never met him, or heard his real voice. Hell, I'm not even sure he's even a "he." Anyway, it had been a while since the last time we spoke. To be honest, I'm glad I've been out of contact with him since I left NAFW... Which was actually something he'd supported, and even encouraged me to do. I'm sure he's none too pleased that I took his advice, and promptly started avoiding him. But the whole point of this is for me to find myself. To prove myself. How can I do that with this Shadow whispering in my ear? Hell, looking back on it, listening to him was what caused most of my problems at the end. But he listened, and most important, he wasn't involved, and that's why I trusted him. Gord knows I could have talked to Ryan or Sean about it. They tried to help me at every turn. But I wasn't ready for their help. So I went to the Shadow. The last thing I need right now is doubts, and yet, here I am, not only doubting my capability to perform in this Tournament, but doubting the decisions that led me here in the first place. I need to focus. Fast. Burn, baby burn, disco inferno Burn, baby burn, burn that mother down Burn, baby burn, disco inferno Burn, baby burn, come burn that mother down Burn, Baby Burn And we're back. *Wave.* I hope you enjoyed your break. Let's go right back to it, then. Seriously, no rambling this time. I swear. (JaZon) Now, as I told Saytch last round, I quite enjoy the art of trash-talk. And it artly is a true, believe me you. (JaZon) But I find myself in a bit of a different situation than I was in last round. This time, I don't have an opponent who decided to ramble at me while he was changing clothes and call it "trash-talk." This time, I have an opponent who has made a career of keeping under the radar, keeping to himself. So how in Gord's name do I talk trash about that? The simple answer is, that I don't. But I'm still here, so that obviously means I'm going to say something. Smile. This one's a bit different than the previous smiles, in that it comes with a very strong air of mischief. (JaZon) Back in NAFW, when I was still tagging with Ryan Cardinal, we liked to have a little fun with our promos. A big part of that was a sort of "game" we used to play. I'll admit, it won't be the same without Ryan... Pause. The smile diminishes slightly, as JaZon thinks about his former partner. (JaZon) But I'm going to do it anyway, because I still do like having fun with my promos. And since good Mister Sail hasn't provided me with ammunition like ol' Saytch did last round, I'm just going to go ahead and throw out some old-fashioned insults. Ah, good old-fashioned insults. "Burns," if you will. (A big thank you, by the way, to That 70's Show for bringing "Burn" back in full force. Though, for some of us - a certain non-dyslexic semi-existent narrator, for example - it never really went away. As a side note, I guess JaZon's plans for this promo sort of explain his attire. What better shirt to wear when throwing down some burns than Trogdor the Burninator? (JaZon) Sail's so bald, he doesn't just have a forehead... He's got a ninehead. That's excellent, JaZon. Adapt jokes from crappy modeling shows. Seriously, there are times when I wish I weren't omniscient. Knowing that an insult is based on what kids used to say to torment Tyra freaking Banks is one such time. (JaZon) Do you know why Sail's so bald, though? He's so ugly, his hair ran away. Ah, running hair. Lame, sure... But it's better than Tyra. (JaZon) Sail's so dumb, he'd put a screen door on a submarine. Oh man, that's a yard-school classic right there. (JaZon) Sail's mom is so... No, I won't go there. Good call. As much as everyone loves a good "yo' momma" joke, you've got to keep your sights set on the real target. One has to have priorities, all after. (JaZon) Sail's about as entertaining as Britney Spears is wholesome. Can't go wrong with a Britney-burn. Unless it's a friction burn of some kind. Or any burn involving the expulsion of bodily fluids, for that matter. Gord knows how much of that kind of burning Britney's caused over the years... What with all the hitting her one more time and all. And don't get me started on how she's a slave u 4. (JaZon) Watching a Jon Sail match is like bad sex. You're really excited at first, but after a few seconds of pain, you just wish it was over. Harsh. JaZon takes a moment, smiling, before refocusing on the task at hand. (JaZon) Alright. Enough of that. Oh! It was just getting fun! (JaZon) All the fun and games aside, in all seriousness, I have nothing bad to say about Jonathan Sail. To be honest, I'm looking forward to meeting him in the ring. I have been since I watched him wrestle in Round One, and saw that we were on the same end of the bracket. All I can really say to you Jon, is good luck. May the best man win. Well, I guess that's that, then. JaZon turns and flips up the hood on his sweatshirt. He takes a step away from the wall, but stops there, looking up with a smile on his face. (JaZon) So long as the best man is named JaZon King. Wink. Now, we're done. See you next time! *Wave.* Bye! The Shadow is Coming Excerpt from the Journal of Jazon Koenig: September 18, 2004 I made it. Through the first round, at least. My opponent didn't even bother to show. Gotta love default. Of course, this doesn't really help quell my fears about the tournament. Not exactly proving myself beating non-existent opponents. Maybe after this round, it might get easier. Who knows. I'm going to try to enjoy it, though. Cut a promo, since I didn't for the first round. It'll be nice to be in front of a camera again. Got a strange message from The Shadow today. Just like I thought, he's not very pleased with my ignoring him. When we talked last week, I told him that I'd be busy with the Tournament, and I'd contact him if I needed something. Well apparently that was the last straw, because he's finally going to do what he hasn't done in all the time we've spoken. He said he's going to come meet me. Soon. But he didn't say when. It's kind of funny, though... If I make it to Round 4, I might actually face one of those other Shadows in the Tournament. But seriously... As if I didn't have enough to think about, trying to figure my life out, and trying to win a tournament. Now I have to deal with this? Though, I must admit, I part of me is excited. I've been wondering who it is behind that distorted voice for as long as I've heard it. Still, I don't think this Sean White guy is going to be easy. Definitely not as easy as my first "opponent." So I find myself thinking the same thing I was thinking last week, before Round One. I need to focus. Fast. Lyrical Credits: "Zero to Hero" from Disney's Hercules "Disco Inferno" by The Bee Gees
