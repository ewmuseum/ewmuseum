Aspiring for Approval IV - Nightmare

11 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-11 The beat of a heart, thudding and thumping in the background. Somewhere off in the distance. I look, but I see nothing, I just don’t see anything even with my eyes open. It troubles me but I settle in, letting the feelings wash over me and ride the wave. I won’t worry, I won’t feel scared. A shriek comes from a corner of the room that I’m now in, just like the room I started in when I closed my eyes…but not the same as before. The shriek is slowed down to a crawl, a ripple effect right through my ears and brain. I push my hands to my ears but they shatter in spectacular fashion, falling in shards to the floor which is dark crimson. They don’t tinkle and spread themselves all over the floor however. Instead they’re absorbed, making this gloopy noise that sucks them in and follows with a pop. The fact that all those pieces were my hands doesn’t seem to bother me though and that does bother me. I look at my wrists…but I have hands again. The room begins to spin and I twist and turn and turn and twist into a scene of pure horror. A flicker of a murder…one that I never thought could be envisaged. Then darkness again as the floor swallows me up. *** The heart beating, beating at the speed of a decent pace, heightened from the last scene, and this time I have no trouble seeing. In fact no matter how hard I try my eyes just won’t shut, much to the dismay of my mind. For what I stand before is hideous and unthinkable in reality. A muddle and mixture of disgusting and revolting images reverberate through my eyeballs, sapping the fluid from them, drying them up and crusting them over. I try to cry, to shed tears for the images that I fear and despise but no water will come out. It is unthinkable, unimaginable, indescribable… But it’s happening and I can’t believe it. I try to move but shackles force me to hold right in front of it all. To witness it all in front of me. Then nothing. *** The beat of a heat, rapidly ascending to dangerous levels, pounding in my head and pushing me onwards, upwards through the stairs, the unending stairs that spirals out of control and reverses itself on many occasions. It’s like those famous paintings, those famous scenes from Labyrinth…or any number of other references that I could give. What’s important is that something presses me onwards through the maze and changing openings. Something is waiting for me at the end of the path. And I’m going to reach it, one way or the other. I hear the heart beating, beating in a race to reach my goal before I can and at some point I feel it literally fly past me, zooming on ahead and discovering all these new paths along the way that I could never even have fathomed before. Still, the nightmare, or what I now believe to be a nightmare continued. The whizzing of the heart can be heard up ahead of me and I feel my legs pumping faster and faster, trying to catch up. Yet my mind, how I think now is detached, away from the scene in a strange way. I’m scared. And it doesn’t get any easier. The heart pounds harder and closer and I reach it, right in the turn of a road. A fork as the heart stops and ponders where it should go, where it should be. The choices before it are grim and visceral, neither without their taint. On the left I see a trophy, a glistening trophy which sparkles at the merest hint of light. In fact, it seems to be illuminating itself with the light. I scrunch my eyes together to see what it says and there is a simple abbreviation imprinted on it: GTT There was nothing more to the choice than this. Glory, honour and respect was what the trophy held for anyone to endure and reach it. It was the mantle that so many people wished to hold, but only five have had the chance to be bestowed with. It is everything that I want right now…everything that I have ever wanted from wrestling…something that could quench or sate my thirst. I was sure of the choice… But there is still another option. To the right, lay a damp, dark and dull room. There is nothing exciting to the room itself, only its occupants. Sitting in a rocking chair was my partner, Jessica. She looks…beautiful and so happy in the depiction. What is more important than even this though is what she is holding. In her arms lays a baby, a baby so like the two of us that it is impossible to imagine it being otherwise. The moment is…perfect, a moment that she wants. But is it what the heart wants? As I watch the heart choose I scream in agony, the whole surroundings fading into oblivion…as my heart chooses the left… *** Beads of sweat poured down from my forehead, the jolt of something wakening me up in the living room, still alone at home. Now dark and quiet, I sit in the chair, painkillers tossed to the side looking all the world like the biggest junkie. I sighed in disgust at the picture, but did nothing to scold myself for it. I knew it was somebody else that was doing this to me. I stood up violently and felt the surge of pain again in my leg. I let it rush through me, fuel my anger and adrenaline to get me going for what I knew was going to be a long trip. Earlier, before that entire argument that I had had, or the proceeding nightmare, she implored me to give it in, to take off the tournament and reconsider. That’s what had started the argument. Now though I was determined, now I was resolved to see this through and make sure that I came out on top. It was the only way that I could provide for my family, whether or not Jessica would be there. She had to understand how much this meant to me, and, if she did, then we could reach a higher pinnacle than ever before. This was the way, this was the only way I knew how to solve things. Meet them head on. I crept up the stairs to where Jessica laid sleeping. I deftly moved past a couple of obstacles between her and me, then finally reached her. She was so beautiful…I hoped that I could tell her that again someday. I kissed her forehead, seeing her rustle around the bed and smile slightly. I hoped that I would be able to make her smile again someday. Right now I had business to attend to. I had to go and deal with the person who’d dealt me with this blow. Like I’d said before…nothing was going to stand in my way…especially something that was trying to hang onto my coattails. For now, I would step into the nightmare.
