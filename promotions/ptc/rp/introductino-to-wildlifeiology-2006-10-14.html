Introductino to Wildlifeiology

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 As per usual, the screen fades into one of two places, a beaten down shack or a tree house… because the two are environmentally conscious seeing as how neither use electricity. Wildlife, 20-something year old male, 6'0 230 lbs, is standing around wearing his trademark khaki shorts ™ and shirt which reads "Believe in Recyclatude… Ride A Bicyclatude". Wildlife is known as the common everyday wrestler who's true passion is Recycling. But why is he here… WL: Hail you other PTC Fed members… I know that you all are currently wondering one thing and one thing only… why is the oxygen we breathe so dirty whenever we go to a PTC event? Or how come there isn't a recycling bin in my dressing room? Suddenly a door bursts open and out steps an individual standing in the shadows. ???: Or how about… why isn't there a teal colored superhero saving us from evil? Suddenly the man, which is an understatement, steps out to reveal that he is the one and only Captain Planet! Wildlife is not impressed; he just stares at the old-time superhero. CP: The Power And Floor Is Yours! WL: Thanks. PTC will be glad once I've been here a couple of months. XUW is about 200% more environmentally friendly, about 40% of that happened when I became the Improving Cleanliness Champion… so if I were traveling 30 mile per hour in my Eco-Car… where am I? That point is moot… because by the time you see this… I’m not here… Suddenly the screen fades away but then fades back up… to the hallways of the latest XUW Mayhem. Wildlife does not have a match and he most certainly will not be supporting Kidd… not yet at least. He has nothing better to do aside from tour the hallways and peek into a few empty dressing rooms. WL: I am walking back here for a reason… because there was one main reason I joined this PTC thingy, to spread the word, nay the life, of Recyclatude. There are, by the last time I counted, four XUW members in PTC aside from myself. Saij, Doctor Curiosity, DDT, and Axel Action. Although Action has entered under UWF, he is a member of XUW. These four are perhaps the least environmentally friendly wrestlers in XUW. Most superstars have accepted the Recycling Bins that I sent to their locker rooms, yet these four did not. DDT never recycles or throws anything away; he just uses it later in hardcore matches. Saij… well they burn and smoke anything, so Recycling Bins are useless. Doctor Curiosity… well he’s just plain evil. Axel Action… he hasn’t actually earned the right in XUW to have an official Recyclatude Recycling Bin. Wildlife continues to walk, until the camera spots the bare back of a man, a very recognizable man… who is preoccupied. Wildlife notices a near-dead potted plant… Wildlife notices the bare back as Joey Vendetti… Wildlife notices a bottle and recognizes it as Vendetti’s very own water bottle… Wildlife notices and recognizes an opportunity. While Joey is preoccupied with his cell phone, Wildlife pours the bottle of water into potted plants pot, the dry, brown soil turns black. WL: Only in nature can such darkness be a good thing, but if you look at the soil closely, there are little specks of white in there. All the fans see a heel, or darkness if you will, but they don’t see the white specks. Joey Vendetti doesn’t recycle, yet the fans cheer. Same for the Boomer Sooners… hope PTC knows who the all mighty Boomer Sooners are. Wildlife scoffs at this notion… Boomer Sooners are by far the biggest polluters in XUW… he should know, he had to put up with them for all of Stable Wars. He should know about most of these XUW participants anyways, after fighting the SoS with DDT and being in the IC Tourney with Doctor Curiosity… sort of. Wildlife turns around and sees that Vendetti is closing up the phone, so he scampers away before Vendetti notices the water. Wildlife scooches out… but to where? Why in fact the only other place of residence… the beaten down shack. However, it can now not nearly be considered beaten down ever since the Mormons came and planted cotton and cleaned it up… WL: As you can see, I have two places of residence. I know, it seems wasteful, but it’s not. Wildlife opens the door, looks at the thermostat and sees that it’s nearly 98 degrees in here… yet air condition could contain Freon, not gonna happen in a Recyclatude Mansion. Kidd is napping on the beanbag, who wouldn’t in nearly 100 degree weather? WL: Those from UWF or VHW or whatever other polluting feds are watching this… this is Kidd. … That’s about it. Suddenly a large bird head comes through the open window! It looks like an ostrich… but isn’t. It is somehow better… more magnificent… but with less letters. Suddenly EMU makes a screeching noise that sounds eerily like, "Wildlife, what’s the news… scrawk?" WL: EMU, how’s it recycling? Eh, getting ready with Masked Winstrom, not Sean Winstrom, to take the Tag Team titles from The Most Wanteds… although they are some of the least wanted in XUW… hell they wouldn’t be wanted in XUDF, but they are champions. Suddenly EMU makes a screeching noise that sounds eerily like, "I wish we could go to Hong Kong… bu-kaw!" WL: As a matter of fact… we should be going to Hong Kong for this PTC event, you know, spread the word of Recyclatude… GTT6. Suddenly EMU makes a screeching noise that sounds eerily like, "GTT6, is it going to be what we expected… screech?" WL: Sigh, no… it’s going to be a wrestling tournament… yet there is not explanation as to what GTT6 stands for. So, when me, you, Kidd and Captain Planet joined together to think of what it could be… well long story short it doesn’t stand for Green Turnip Turnover. Suddenly Kidd wakes up… Kidd: Wait… *yawns*… so this is not a contest where we recycle turnips? WL: Nope. Kidd: Damn… we have no chance. Wildlife looks around and notices that just about the entire Recyclatude squad is here right now. We’ve got Wildlife, EMU, and Kidd. EMU and Wildlife are a fixture in Recyclatude… but Kidd may be moving on soon. The missing are Captain Planet and Ranger Rick… well, Wildlife wishes he were still Ranger Rick, but now he is a composite of Ranger Rick, Rick Culliford (the Australian Pirate) and Nomrom (The Avenging Mormon… see, Nomrom is Mormon spelled backwards, its witty.). Captain Planet is hopefully staying back at the Tree house of Recyclatude… but of course there is one that will never be forgotten, the deceased Hot Boy Tree. The little sapling that could rap… god rests his tree-soul. Wildlife actually doesn’t much care for the Tag Team Titles; he just wants to advance in the GTT6, whatever the hell it stands for. WL: The GTT6, why do people do it? Some people get endorsements, some people do it to stop themselves from falling into oblivion. People do it for money, people do it for recognition. Xian Phillipse, you’re the typical "boy wonder", no real reason for wrestling… me, I have a reason. I have Earth, I have the ionosphere, I have H and I have two-O (which if you didn’t know is H20). What do you have? Prestige? Show your evil father that he was wrong? Supporting the evil bleaching plants who pour Dioxin into streams? So you can afford a hummer… Kidd: Hummer? WL: The vehicle, the vehicle. Your reasons for wrestling are selfish ones, mine are not. Kidd: Oh, write that down. That’ll be awesome to say against the Most Wanteds. Since your not selfish and you’re a team player… yeah! WL: Yeah, I’ll do that, just give me a second to finish this sermon that fans are falling asleep during… dirty polluting fans. Listen here DDT (which is a horrible pollutant), Mentok, Bobino, Axel Action, John Adams, Jorge Samuelsson, Ember, Dorian Ryan, Peter Vetra, M3, Tigera, and Jeff Harris, this even goes out to you because you dropped out, yet you can still stay home and recycle. Oh yeah, and Xian Phillipse, I just wanted to remind each and every single person one single thing… Win, Lose, or Draw… Save the Rainforest! The screen fades away as Wildlife smiles after delivering his trademark catchphrase. It starts to turn green, but quickly turns to black as Wildlife is ready to make his debut in Primetime Central.
