PTC — Roleplay

John "The Prophet" Adams vs Dan Logan

17 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
17 November 2004 The Chthonic Prophet! ~ "Driven by dæmonic, chthonic Powers." --T.S. Eliot ~ ~ "The chthonic divinity was essentially a god of the regions under the earth; at first of the dark home of the seed, later on of the still darker home of the dead." --C. F. Keary ~ ~ Down deep in the hell that I call my soul is where I fight, a fight where I am the winner taking on poor deluded individuals one by one that in hopes I may find a worthy opponent! Is Dan Logan that worthy adversary? Has he proven himself worthy to be graced by the underground light of “The Prophecy”? A warrior of a not so distant past of LOW has returned to take on The Future, The Prophet. Ahhh yes Dan, it will be a glorious battle between good and evil but the question is who will be the victor? One thing is for certain Dan that I will take you through the brinks of insanity, I will take you through your own Chthonic Hell!! ~ ~ From the ashes of a warrior the underworld he shall come….. ~ Time: 10:02 AM Location: Lucas County Zoo in Ohio- The Toledo Zoo. Date: Wednesday, November 17th 2004 = The day was sunny and bright at the zoo, everybody having a good time enjoying the animals and spending time with the ones they love. As we move closer on this fine Wednesday day we notice the lion’s exhibit, the flamingos bathing at the pound, and the Elephant exhibit. But wait; there is one exhibit that is closed for today, a very dark and dreary exhibit…..the nocturnal animal exhibit, it’s closed all but for one man that has spent a lot of money for his privacy, one man that is set to face an opponent on foreign grounds that man is John “The Prophet” Adams. He is seen admiring the bats in one part of the exhibit when the 5’11’’ 120 pound silver haired beauty Luna SilverMoon, one of Adams disciples walks in. = ~ The harvester of souls has returned to collect the weak….. ~ Luna SilverMoon: Well, it wasn’t easy but I have the information you so desire! = Luna tosses him a file that has Dan Logan’s name on the cover. Adams is seen not really paying much attention to the file but is still fixated on the bat exhibit. = ~ Evils own being the judge and executioner….. ~ John Adams: You know what makes bats such good hunters in the night? Luna SilverMoon: No John I don’t, but maybe…. (Quickly she is interrupted by John Adams.) John Adams: Because Luna they have radar that helps them navigate through the night sky! Funny isn’t it? Luna SilverMoon with a still confused face: What is? John Adams: That a flying rat, that navigates by a simple radar is one of the most feared creatures of the night! Legends were made from this winged creature of the underworld, Dracula, vampires in general. All because of one disease filled flying rodent. Luna SilverMoon: Yeah and is there a point to this? John Adams with a booming voice that can be heard all over the Zoo: EVERYTHING HAS A POINT!!!! You see with Dan Logan, I am the hunter of the underworld, and I will get my prey! As I go to 4WW in this special interfed match I will hunt him down like the dog that he is!! He was unwise to accept my challenge for when we meet I shall show him the true meaning of “The Prophecy”! = How weird Luna thought that a man with only a past to hold on to would affect John Adams in this way. But then again her life was never the same when she aligned her self with “The Prophet”, so much darkness in the chthonic world that he lives in, but it was all worth it if she can get back the one thing that mattered to her the most…..her daughter. = ~ Talking in the self-proclaimed impurities of the world….. ~ John Adams: Now my dear sweet Luna, don’t you have a job to do? Luna SilverMoon: Yes, the will of The Prophecy! John Adams: Then go do it my dear, and be quick about it…..for the best laid plans of mice and men often go astray! = John Adams then concentrates on the bats some more. With a fire and passion in him….he knows, he feels it the time has drawn near. And heaven help whoever stands in his way. = ~ And fill it with its unholy might! ~ John Adams: Yes Dan, the sands in the hour glass are slowly dropping! Your time draws nearer and nearer! Tick…Tock….Tick….Tock! ~And there you have it Dan, my passion and my desire! I will walk into your territory of 4WW and Dan; I fear no evil for I am the one true light in this world of lies and deception! When we meet my friend….yes it will be an epic confrontation that will bend all the rules of sanity, and reality! Not only will you be tested physically but mentally! That is the will of “The Prophecy”!! ~ ~ "The chthonic imagery of Norine's apartment, which..was black as a coalhole and heated by the furnace of the hostess' unslaked desires." --M. McCarthy ~ ~ "Two great and contrasted forms of ritual: the Olympian and the Chthonic, the one a ritual of cheerful character, the other a ritual of gloom, and fostering superstition." ~ ~ Fin ~ ---- 22 November 2004 Chthonic World ~ Dan…Dan…Well what do we have here Dan? You lead an interesting life my friend, and here I thought I lived in a chthonic world. Shall we go over this? Yes I think we shall, you were married to a lady by the name of Emma, such a beautiful lady I hear also…and the two of you had a son by the name of Chris…am I getting warm Dan? Well when the two of you started to have problems, she ended up cheating on you with another gentleman and took your son to Brazil, and that’s where your son met his last days…wasn’t Dan? Do you miss your son Dan, do you miss throwing the ball around on a Sunday afternoon? Is Jenny really doing a good job of filling that empty hole in your life? Now you may ask yourself “How does this bastard know so much of my life?” Well Dan the best is yet to come……. ~ ~ The hour is near….. ~ Time: 12:35 AM Location: Gravesite near Columbus Ohio Date: November 20, 2004 (The scene starts off with only a week away from the big 4WW event. The location is a local gravesite in Columbus, Ohio. The scene around the gravesite is quite dark and dreary, with the night air cold and damp. All of the tombstones in the gravesite were quite elegant for a final resting place. The light of the full moon was illuminating the site just enough for a man that is seen, to navigate through there. The man was searching with a watchful eye for something particular, but what was it? The nocturnal creatures were howling a song of pain and suffering to tell the tale of what these souls had to endure. As the man walks into the light of the full moon, he is revealed to be LOW’s own John “The Prophet” Adams. But why was he here? Why is John Adams ever at a gravesite?) ~ The end draws near….. ~ John Adams: I know it’s around here, somewhere! (With each stone he passed, a new story is revealed. Francis Thorne R.I.P. Born 1945- 1999, David Fredrick R.I.P. Born 1980- 2003, and Chris Logan…..Chris Logan, he found it finally. Luna was right on the money with the location of the grave site. John Adams looked at it with an evil grin….what was he thinking? I mean this is the gravesite of Dan Logan’s kid that passed away from some disease in Brazil while with his mother.) ~ For one man stands before his fate….. ~ John Adams: So, I have found the son of my adversary Dan Logan! So boy how does it feel, how does it feel to have Dan as your father? A man still trying to hold on to a live he once lived….and what for? Is he just one of those old timers still trying to hold on to the past? Is he a man still trying to prove something to the world? Or is the man feeling guilty for the crimes he committed? (The distant sound of thunder can be heard out in the distance as dark clouds can be seen creeping in blanketing the moonlight with its darkness. An Owl can be heard flying overhead to take cover from the coming of the storm.) ~ Against all odds he will fight for a new beginning….. ~ John Adams: Tell me Chris, did your daddy ever love you? I mean if he did, then he would have looked for you, he would have saved you and you would have still been alive today right in your daddy’s arms!! To tell you the truth Chris, I think the man never really loved you at all, no in fact I feel that he thanks the heavens above that he’s not burden by the likes of you! (The dark clouds of an unimaginable sorrow are now overshadowing all of the sky. And the first sign of lightning can now be seen….1….2...followed by the loud booming sound of thunder. The sound is so loud that it scares the birds who were sleeping inside a nearby palm.) ~ To claim a fame that was once lost ….. ~ John Adams: You hear that sound? It is the sound of the coming of the storm! The sound of a new start for your father, confidante the man is that he would be challenging me on his return to the ring! Little does the man know that his return will only mean his own demise! (An unholy down pour of rain starts to fall from the heavens above, blanketing the hollow ground with the sorrow tears of the underworld. The nocturnal animals are all now running for some shelter to get out of the rain. But not John Adams, he has come to say his peace to his advisories son, he must and he will. John Adams looks at the grave with a evil grin still on his face as the rain is covering his body. ) ~ An epic battle has started with the entire Chthonic World….. ~ John Adams: The time has come Chris, your father will never be the same again….I will take the redemption that you seek for your father’s sins! I will take him down to the chthonic world that I call my home, and show him he true meaning of fear!! (John Adams gets off the wet ground still looking at the tombstone. He has vowed to take all of Chris’s anger and frustration of his father, and use it to his own advantage in his match.) ~ With all of the army’s of the underworld to go up against……. ~ John Adams: Don’t worry little Chris, I will seek the revenge of your death by traveling to the Four Winds, and there I will defeat your father!! And I will also prove another thing! That I am a better father to his Son in death….then he ever was with you when you were alive!!! (And with that he leaves the grave site. Renewed by the spirit of self renewal he has vowed to Dan Logan’s dead son that he will make his father pay for letting him go to Brazil. He has vowed to make Chris’s father pay for not being there at nights when he was crying for him, but yet he was not. He has vowed to take the death of a little boy, and avenge his name. He has vowed that Chris’s death will not go unpunished…..) ~ Led by A Chthonic Prophet of a false hope…… ~ (John Adams heads to his bike where he is met with Luna SilverMoon. The long silver haired beauty is still looking like the essence of perfection, even more so wet. She is holding a video camera in her left hand smiling. ) ~ Promising a hope of change, though his evil grows…….. ~ Luna Silvermoon: Well, that’s the most feelings that I have ever seen you express! Did you mean all of that you said? John Adams: Luna….what do you think? What do you think I am doing here Luna? Why do you think I’ve asked you to video tape that escapade? Why?? I will tell you why, it is because of the carnage that I crave, it is because of the suffering in people who do not follow my will that I desire, and it is in the mind of Logan that I will plant the seed of his own demise!! Luna SilverMoon: Yeah well what do you want me to do with this tape? John Adams: Luna even though you have great beauty and skill inside the ring, it is clear to me that brains I not of a strong suit with you, is it? I want you to take the tape and send it straight to Dan! It is my message to him that I know him, I know what type of man he is! And it will also show that I am not a man to be taken likely with!! Now go, before the rain destroys the tape!! (Luna gets into a nearby car, and heads off. John Adams looks on…then slowly looks back towards the direction of Chris Logan’s grave and laughs an evil and sinister laugh that would even make the demons of the underworld shudder with fear. Yes, the end was coming, but will it be John Adams who is the victor? Or will Dan Logan make the comeback of the century into the business he thought he would leave behind? The last scene shown is of John Adams leaving on his motorcycle into the land of the Four Winds, and into battle with the man…..Dan Logan.) ~ The Prophetic Conspiracy’s will, shall not be denied!!! ~ ~ Well Dan, it’s time my friend!! Can you feel it? Can you even taste it? I can, and it will be the darkness of my soul that will snuff out the light of a comeback many have so waited to see!! Dan, my destiny is upon me and my will shall be heard, Jenny knows what I am talking about! Next time you see her, tell her I said hi, ask her what “The Prophecy” means…………….(laughing) Ask her how she knows me Dan…..go on ask her! ~ ~ chthon·ic [ thónnik ] or chtho·ni·an [ thṓnee ən ] adjective of the underworld: relating to the underworld as described in Greek mythology [Late 19th century. Formed from Greek khthōn “earth.”]- Dictionary ~ ~ Fin ~
