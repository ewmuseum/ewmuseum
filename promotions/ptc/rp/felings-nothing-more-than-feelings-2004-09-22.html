Felings... nothing more than feelings

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 For the first time in his life, people are talking about Craig Goat in terms of being a winner instead of laughing behind his back and plaguing him with wedgies. An absolute novice at wrestling, he has defeated his first round opponent, Jonathan Borealis, in the prestigious GTT4 tournament. The players have been culled by half and now he faces a new challenge and a new opponent. One would assume that his first ever victory would push him towards training harder to ensure he is ready to prove to world that he has indeed got the makings of a champion. As ever, one assumes too much… The drapes are violently pulled open allowing the harsh noon light to flood the room. Craig shields his eyes and pulls the covers over his head, enveloping himself further in a pungent cocoon of unwashed sheets and man smells. He grips tightly onto the duvet and struggles to keep it from being ripped from him. He loses. As the duvet flies from the bed, he flips over onto his stomach and buries his head under the pillow. A muffled “G’way!” comes from the general vicinity of his mouth. A sharp pain on the sole of his foot lets him know that his tormentor is not ready to give up just yet. Sighing resignedly, he sits up and glares at Nurse Esme Matilda Wretched. Off key and out of tune, she sings to him, “Wakey wakey, rise and shine or I’ll rip your testicles off!” “Shut up and get out.” “Aw come on Craig! You can’t just lie in bed all day. You’ve got a lot more training to do if you’re going to be in with a chance to win this thing.” In the mood for argument, he replies “That’s fine, I’ll stay here then. I’m not in the tournament anymore.” “What are you talking about! Of course you’re in. Don’t be so bloody stupid!” Craig glares petulantly at Nurse and as he speaks, his voice gets squeaky with emotion. “You don’t understand you stupid bitch! I’m not going to show up for the next one. I’m never wrestling again. So shut the goddamn door on your way out!” “Why?” Nurse asks, but he just ignores her. Seeing she’s not going to get any response, Nurse leaves Craig alone wallow in his thoughts. As much as he is loathe to admit it, her question is valid, and one Craig can’t quite answer yet. All he knows is that something felt wrong in that ring. His wrestling debut was nothing like what he expected and he felt no sense of the achievement he imagined he should as he left the arena victorious. For as long as he can remember, he has fantasised about being a champion. Once, it was just a silly daydream of fame like everyone has, but for the last year, it’s been something he believed might one day come to pass. When Nurse Wretched was at her cruellest towards him, torturing him physically as well as mentally daily, he comforted himself with the thought that it was bringing him closer to his dream. She had always claimed that the only way to make a wrestler was to break him so badly that physical pain was a welcome relief from the anguish and torture of a shredded soul. And break him she did. She convinced him that his own parents hated him and drove him into playing a part in committing them to an archaic asylum, a place that still featured prominently in his nightmares. She turned him against the handful of people close to him and convinced him that he was less of a valued friend and more an object of amusement to them. Nurse Wretched treated him like dirt scraped from the sole of her shoe and he accepted it… he was dirt. He cooked and cleaned and fetched for her. He was her whipping boy every time she was in a vindictive mood. She practised her moves on him until his body was broken, bloody and screaming for mercy or perhaps even a quick death. She used him as a guinea pig for the concoctions she illegally obtained and mixed in a syringe to smuggle into the ring with her, a tool she often used within her aptly named “Clinical Cheating” style of wrestling. As difficult as the last year had been, it did indeed make Craig stronger. Although he would always be subordinate to Nurse, he grew more confident and forceful in his dealings with others. But then she pushed him too far. He confessed his love to her and she laughed at him saying “The stupidest thing you’ve ever done is think I could ever love you. I don’t want to be harsh, but I wouldn’t date you if you were the last man left on Earth. No offence.” With that, she ground down the last morsel of hope he had and drove him over the edge and towards insanity. For surely only temporary insanity could have caused him to try and murder the object of his obsession, the most formidable woman in the world of wrestling? Recently, the medication he has to take each morning and the new woman he holds each night, a dim blonde named Trixie, seem to have given him a new lease of life… a reason for being and something new to find joy in. Wrestling would always be his first love, but now he had even more than he ever dared wish for. So why isn’t he happy? Is this simply the comedown from a massive overload of emotion? But if it is, why can’t he remember feeling the emotions in the first place? Confused and depressed, Craig once more wraps himself in his duvet; hopeful he can sleep and put off confronting this mystery until later… much later, in fact, never if he can get away with it. Unfortunately, Craig isn’t given the choice to procrastinate. Later that afternoon, he squirms uncomfortably on the sofa. It’s normally a very comfortable sofa, but after Craig storming out of the room three times already, Nurse has to tie him down securely. “Ok, now that I have your full attention, why don’t we continue?” “Mummpf.” Nurse giggles, “Oh, guess I got carried away” and rips the duct tape from his mouth. Indignant, he shouts, “You know I’m bloody allergic to glue! I’ll have a huge red mark on my face for weeks now on top of all my other worries. Thanks a freakin' lot!” Nurse does her best to adopt a concerned expression as she asks, “What worries?” but only succeeds in looking constipated. “Ah, fuck it! I’m no good at this feelings and caring crap. Dave, can you take over?” One of the three men standing by the wall steps forward. He is huge and his muscles ripple menacingly under his expensive silk suit. Nurse waves him away impatiently, “Not you, I meant the other Dave!” A second man steps forward, the first may have been huge but this guy is easily 7 feet tall and built like a brick out house. Shaking her head, Nurse puts her hands on her hips and growls, “For God sake! Don’t you realise by now that when I need someone to deal with something that actually involves more than two neurons rubbing together to create a conscious thought, I mean Big Dave?” Little Dave and Medium Dave have the decency to look sheepish as they step backward to make room for their sibling. Big Dave grins at Craig with a mouth full of teeth as big as tombstones, which to give you an idea of his immense size, are actually in proportion to the rest of him. A behemoth of a man, he has a mind to match and a surprisingly sensitive soul. He and his brothers have long been advisors and trainers to Nurse Wretched. Schooled in violence and linked with the shady world of organised crime, they are a formidable trio. Big Dave has always been close to Craig, protecting him from the worst of Nurse’s furies and shoring up his resolve just when Craig most needs it. He may not agree with Nurse’s “break ‘em to make ‘em” school of wrestling, but as long as she does, he will always be there to act as a buffer between them. Quietly, so as to occlude the others from the conversation, he asks Craig. “What’s wrong, and how can we help you? I know you may not believe this, but we only have your best interests in mind. You’ve come such a long way to get into this tournament and it breaks my heart to see you falter at the second hurdle. I know it’s a confusing time for you, you’ve never been the centre of attention before and have always seemed happier on the sidelines, but now that you’ve stepped out of Nurse’s shadow, you have the greatest opportunity ever… you get to see what you’re truly made of! Come on Craig, let me in on what’s up with you? “Dave, I wish I had the answer. I swear I’d tell you if I did. All I do know is that everything seems wrong! I stepped into that ring all fired up and eager to prove my mettle, and for the first few minutes I was on top of the world. I used every trick you’ve ever taught me. I imagined how Nurse would counter each move and attempted to emulate her. I was doing my best… but suddenly I became numb and I’ve been numb ever since. If Borealis wasn’t already so tired out by then, he would have taken me for sure. ” Thoughtful, Big Dave leans in closer, looking deep into Craig’s eyes, almost into his soul “What will it take to get you back in the ring? Is your girlfriend’s disapproval to blame? Wearily, Craig sighs, “I’m never getting back in a ring again. Feeling as I do now, I’d be crushed within seconds. Please let me end my career, however brief it was, on a victory? This has nothing to do with Trixie. She doesn’t like the idea of me being a wrestler, and it hurt her to see me all beat up, but she’s said she’ll let me do this one tournament. She reckoned I’d regret not giving it a shot… but I don’t think even she ever imagined that what I’d most regret is actually entering in the first place. No, this isn’t about Trixie.” “Oh for Christ sake!” Nurse screeches, “Can we stop with the touchy-feely crap now? I’ve told you before, and I’ll say it until someone is blue and bloody in the face; Craig doesn’t respond well to ‘nice’, you’ve got to force him to get him off his lazy arse and start behaving like a real man!” Nurse glares at the three Daves and whispers, “I say we go with Plan B, and as I’m the only one who gets a vote… I guess that’s just what we do.” Big Dave looks horrified and shakes his head apologetically at Craig. Sensing that Plan B is going to be somehow painful for him, Craig sinks into the sofa wishing he could disappear between the cushions to wherever lost change and car keys go. “There is no way I’m letting Craig lose to a slut like Jenna Ryder. Whether he’s ready or not… he will be in that ring. And he will win. My reputation as well as his is at stake here, and no dime store second rate porno star is going to tarnish that!” Laughing, in fact, almost cackling, Nurse turns to leave. But before she does, she addresses the Daves, “I know you don’t like what you’re about to do, but it’s got to be done… and soon.” What is Nurse’s plan for Craig? Indeed, what could be so evil that it makes three hardened criminals, who were once quite involved in making concrete boots a fashion accessory among the about-to-die-horribly, shudder? Is there anything left of Craig to break?
