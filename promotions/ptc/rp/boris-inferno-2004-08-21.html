Boris' Inferno

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-21 I AM THE WAY INTO THE CITY OF WOE I AM THE WAY TO A FORSAKEN PEOPLE I AM THE WAY INTO ETERNAL SORROW SACRED JUSTICE MOVED MY ARCHITECT I WAS RAISED HERE BY DIVINE OMNIPOTENCE PRIMORDIAL LOVE AND ULTIMATE INTELLECT ONLY THOSE ELEMENTS TIME CANNOT WEAR WERE MADE BEFORE ME, AND BEYOND TIME I STAND ABANDON ALL HOPE YE WHO ENTER HERE Some may read the crude and mournful lines above and say, “Yes, I know exactly where that place is and I’ve been down that road many times.” Well, I’m here to say that’s nonsense. Hell. Have you ever wondered what it would be like to travel through hell? Have you ever wondered what it would be like to come face to face with Lucifer himself? I haven’t, and I never will because I’ve witnessed both first hand in my years. I was a mere seventeen years old when I enlisted in the Soviet military during the Cold War. Now, I don’t care what kind of stories are told in the text books of high schools, colleges, or any other organization because the Cold War was not only an arms race. The United States and U.S.S.R. certainly did use their weapons against one another. I was stationed in a future satellite nation of the Soviet Union where I participated in a myriad of skirmishes against the democratic pigs of America. You may here the word skirmish and think, eh, that’s nothing compared to the blood shed in the World Wars or Vietnam. Well, it’s a different story when you see men who you’ve bonded with and seen day after day, fall to the ground holding various wounds as they slowly slip away into darkness. Soon, it went from seeing my friends and acquaintances day after day to seeing the cold and limp bodies of my friends and acquaintances fall before my feet. A glazed over pair of eyes rolling up toward my face as if calling desperately for my help. After my freak explosives accident which permanently scarred and deformed my body, I returned home searching for work. I went from store to store, from business to business, from work shop to work shop, looking far and wide for work. Time after time I was stared at by managers and sometimes my grotesque features even frightened them. No matter how qualified I was for the job I was continuously rejected by all employers. After being so unsuccessful at finding a job I joined the circus. I was put on display as a freak of nature. Every single night we put on a performance I was chained to a cart. I was rolled out in front of a rowdy crowd. I was taunted. I was jeered. I had projectiles flying toward me. I was insulted and degraded. I was humiliated night after night after night. It never seemed to end. Finally, I now am in the professional wrestling business and everything has changed. You see. I said that I’ve come face to face with Satan himself and it’s true. I saw the hatred in the eyes of American soldiers. I saw the prejudice in the eyes of all the employers that rejected me. I saw the disgust in the eyes of the crowd of the circus. The evil within them all was inspired by the Arch demon. However, now the tables have turned. I’ve had my share of being on the receiving end. I’ve frankly had enough of it. Now, in the wrestling business, I go out to the ring night after night after night. Instead of seeing the hatred, the violence, and the disgust in my opponents eyes… I see the FEAR! Once I see the fear, it’s over. I go out to the ring night after night and I devastate. I’m overcome with evil quite often with my weekly episodes of violence. You see, Hell is all about repetition and I’ve lived it my whole life. So, where do I go from here? Mount Saint Hell’s of course. Here I am, in the PTC arena standing in front of the most unforgiving steel structure I’ve ever laid eyes on. Come August twenty-second every member of the survival match and then in the Mount Saint Hell’s match with witness evil first hand from Boris Yeichstag. Match after match, level after level, I will deal my punishment to my opponents, with or without my team mates. Just as a reminder I’ll hang this sign on the entrance to the Mount Saint Hell’s cage: I AM THE WAY INTO THE CITY OF WOE I AM THE WAY TO A FORSAKEN PEOPLE I AM THE WAY INTO ETERNAL SORROW SACRED JUSTICE MOVED MY ARCHITECT I WAS RAISED HERE BY DIVINE OMNIPOTENCE PRIMORDIAL LOVE AND ULTIMATE INTELLECT ONLY THOSE ELEMENTS TIME CANNOT WEAR WERE MADE BEFORE ME, AND BEYOND TIME I STAND ABANDON ALL HOPE YE WHO ENTER HERE
