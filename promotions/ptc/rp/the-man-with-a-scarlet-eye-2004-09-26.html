The Man with a Scarlet Eye

26 September 2004

Date: 2004-09-26 “Farewell, Mr. Riley” Chapter I: “The Man with a Scarlet Eye” By Kyle M. It was such a beautiful day. It was the kind of day that radiated a joy for existence. This joy was then absorbed by and reverberated off of the walls of people’s hearts everywhere. Everyone - even the blind - could not help but see and feel the piercing light of the sun that day because it had never seemed to shine quite this brightly or beautifully before. As the mailmen dropped off the usual plethora of overdue mortgages and water bills it was not accompanied by the usual groans and moans of the recipients, but instead with a smile and a “Good day.” The students of Rileyville University were actually happy to go to school, sit down and learn. Even as Professor Murphy guided his skinny pale body into a room of one hundred and fifty students, his usually grumpy face was giving way to the slightest but most mirthful of grins. “Okay everyone – settle dow...” he began to utter simply out of reflex, but then he stopped himself and gave the entire class a double take. “Ah... already quiet I see.” Les Murphy, who had had to say “settle down” upon entering a class everyday for his entire teaching career felt a bit awkward knowing that, for once, everyone was paying attention to him. His almost-skeletal body moved jerkily as the students of his history class continued to pay attention and trace him with their eyes. When he had been fresh out of NYU, maybe he would have felt at ease with the freedom of silence having just been trained on such a basis, but after thirty-five years, the silence was unnerving. He actually wanted noise. “Why is everyone so quiet,” he muttered in a forceful way as if trying to sound like he wasn’t really concerned, although he was terrified. “You’re usually my noisiest class all day...” Ryan Wilson, who sat in the very back of the room, raised his hand. Ryan Wilson who had been partially responsible for the burning of the school gymnasium was raising his hand! Professor Murphy looked as if he was going to have a heart attack right then and there in the cream-colored lecture hall. “Y-yes Mr. Wilson?” Professor Murphy croaked. “Do you have to go to the bathroom or something?” “Naw, I wanted to answer the question.” Tiny Professor Murphy adjusted his glasses uneasily. “I see... well... you have the floor Mr. Wilson. I guess.” “It’s Sovereign Day,” Wilson said in a “duh!” tone. “You of all people should know that Professor Murphy!” The crowd of student murmured in agreement and poor sixty year old Lester Daniel Murphy felt his skin flush red and a painful lump materialize in his throat. “Who told you about that?” he said quickly, and seeing the confused glances on his students faces, he clarified. “Not about Sovereign Day... that’s a holiday ... who told you that I ‘of all people’ should know about that?” Caroline Mae, one of his brighter students was about six feet tall, had the wrong curves in the wrong places, and was spackled with freckles, bad hair, and an outfit courtesy of the local thrift store. Even she was giving Professor Murphy the “You are SUCH a dork” look that usually she was on the receiving end of. “Because you’re the history teacher?” she answered without raising her hand. “We heard that you’re a real expert on Him.” “Him?” “Him,” several students, some of which had not spoken all semester, uttered. “Calvin Riley...” The tiny professor dropped one of his books. “Oops,” he murmured, his face flushing deep crimson as he picked the book back up. “Yes ... I suppose all of that business did happen in my day... I suppose I could tell you a bit about it without those ruddy textbooks.” Several hushed voices exclaimed “Yes!” under their breath and twice as many hands slapped together in exultation. Professor Murphy smiled uneasily and cleared his throat. “Okay,” he said scratching his hands before clasping his arms behind his back. “Let’s do a little review before I begin to tell you about Mr. ... er... Riley.” Several groans. “Can anyone tell me what type of government we have?” Silence. More groans. A single hand shot into the air. “Yes,” Professor Murphy said, nodding at Ms. Mae. “What type of government do we have?” “A Calvanist Sovereignty, sir,” she said proudly, with her nose in the air. “Very good Ms. Mae,” he said for the thousandth time this semester. “Can you explain to the class what a Calvanist Sovereignty is?” “Of course!” Several scoffs. A little hushed laughter. She ignored it and pressed on. “A Calvanist Sovereignty is a government based on reasoning,” she said proudly. “It’s a global government which was established following the Treaty of Sydney which ended World War III on March 15, 2025 which banned individual governments after warring nations had destroyed approximately one half of the world’s population with Prometheite Bombs.” “Correct again Ms. Mae,” Professor Murphy said with a smile. “Now let’s give someone else a try ... remember everyone: class participation is twenty-five percent of your grade! Now then... who can tell me what a Prometheite Bomb is, and how it succeeded in causing so much death? Yes, Mr. Black.” Fredrick Black, another good student who was very much into the history of wars, cleared his throat and answered. “Prometheite, which is an isotope of the rare element Promethium can upset the stability of normal elements,” he reported. “Similar to how a nuclear bomb releases the zero point energy of plutonium or uranium, Prometheite, when properly used, can cause what is called a Prometheus Effect, which creates a shell of antinuclear energy, disrupting the forces that hold everyday matter together. With the yield set high enough, a Prometheus Effect can completely obliterate large masses of matter, which was evident when the United States, who first developed the device, tested it on our moon.” “And what happened to ‘our moon’ as a result?” “It was destroyed,” Black said simply. “And the debris was pulled into Earth’s orbit, which is how Earth got its rings.” “Right,” Professor Murphy said nodding. “Well done. Now then, historians blame Calvin Riley for the development of the bomb due to his extensive research on the element, but it was an American scientist by the name of Dr. Kasidy Williams Drake who combined Riley’s research with his own, and then another American scientist by the name of Professor Lionel Greene who discovered and then developed a method of utilizing the Prometheus Effect then offering it to President Hillary Clinton in 2013 as a method of defense a year after the war had broken out. “It’s very important that you all remember that neither Calvin Riley nor Kasidy Drake were seeking a method of mass destruction. That was Lionel Greene. Kasidy Drake was trying to develop a formula for a legal steroid void of side-effects called the Prometheus Serum. Now ... does anyone know what Riley was trying to discover?” Dead silence. Both Caroline Mae, and Fredrick Black had their hands raised, but he didn’t want them hogging the class. Both of the students gave a discontented moan and sucked their teeth as Professor Murphy said “No one?” before turning to the rarely used blackboard, and writing something loudly on it’s surface. Tripromethium-Dioxide After scribbling on the board, Murphy turned around and saw that his students were no further enlightened, and just had confused looks etched upon their faces. “Tripromethium-Dioxide,” he said replacing the chalk. “More commonly known as Promethide. When you’re about six years old and your immune system can handle it, you are injected with this stuff.” “What does it do?” Ryan Wilson called out squirming uneasily with the knowledge that he had something in his blood that could blow up a moon. “Why is it there?” The Professor sighed and paused a moment. “Promethide,” he responded. “Acts as a vaccine which stops several archaic diseases – the most noteworthy ones being: cancer, HIV and AIDS, SARS, and Alzheimer’s Disease. The formula was successfully completed by Emperor Calvin Riley in 2012, the year that World War III, or The Last War as it is more commonly known, began. Emperor Riley, who discovered that Promethium Ore was in abundance in his island nation of New Eden was then able to use it to start trade with the United States which forced them to recognize it as a sovereign nation. “Riley then shared the formula with doctors in America who were able to use it to vaccinate children against about seventy-five percent of disease in the world. The United States, however, refused to share the technology with other countries who had suffered a noticeable increase in their diseased numbers – especially sufferers of SARS. As a result the other nations then began to war with the United States in an attempt to force them to share the formula. “The US was getting slaughtered until the mid-point in the war when President Clinton authorized the use of Prometheite Bombs, which wiped out half of the planet’s population – including the majority of their own soldiers who were fighting in the very countries that they were bombing! The global alliance against the US then broke up in the confusion of having the world’s population halved, and the war became strictly chaotic and anarchic. Finally, Emporor Riley, who had managed to keep New Eden neutral all of this time convinced what was left of the world’s ‘leaders’ that the war made no sense anymore as most of the people they were trying to save with the Promethide formula were now dead anyway, along with many extras. “Riley, who felt guilty that his discovery had led to the most gruesome war in history, drew up a plan for a global government called a Calvanist Sovereignty (named after himself). This plan called for a government based on logical reasoning and philosophy rather than needs and desires. With no alternative, the world leaders signed the Calvanist Articles immediately after the Treaty of Sydney in Australia, and for the first time in the history of the known world, a successful global government was established placing some of the greatest thinkers of the time in charge of things instead of politicians. This Calvanist Council, as it was called, included Emperor Riley himself as the chairman. As a result of this government, the world, for the first time ever, experienced world peace... a utopia. Before his untimely death in 2041, the sixty-five year old Emperor Riley wrote in his autobiography ‘From the Squared Circle to the Round World’ this quote: ‘If I were to die tomorrow, I would not shed a single tear in heaven, for I have seen my ideas shape the world, and protect it from it’s own scarlet bloodshed. I would smile.’” The class took in the quote with blank stares. Professor Murphy chuckled, and then took a tattered green book from his desk. “This is ‘The Man with a Scarlet Eye,’ by Kurt Wesley” he said holding up the beaten-up book for the class to stare at. “And historians attribute most of the details that we know of Calvin Riley’s personal life before he became a world leader to this book... oh... yes Mr. Black?” “There’s a rumor going around campus that you uh... met him once. Calvin Riley, I mean.” The professor’s elderly face stretched into a young smile, and his eyes seemed to light up with a light that he had not seen in twenty years. But then, as quickly as it had come, it was gone again. “Yeah,” he said softly. “I met him once.” “Oh? What was he like?” “He was...” The class leaned in a little closer. “He was...” “Yes Professor Murphy?” Caroline Mae said in an eager whisper. “’He was’ what?” But then Murphy gave a quiet “ha!” and opened the old book, which was a mess of highlighted lines and post-it notes. “I’ll answer questions after the reading...” A collective “Awwww!” rang throughout the class, and Murphy gave another laugh (louder this time.) “Just listen,” he said matter-of-factly. “You might learn something...” And so he began to read. - P R E S E N T • D A Y - ”...in other news today, our nation’s capital has been chosen to host the 2004 Global Title Tournament which is sponsored by PTC, a united federation of wrestling promotions. Amongst the hundreds who are traveling to Washington D.C is suspected to be twenty-eight year old Calvin Riley, an NGW Hall of Famer, who was exiled from the country last year for treason and had his citizenship stripped of him. However, Riley’s contract with the tournament provides him staying within the national boundaries for the duration of the games. Back to you Kent...” “What do you mean registration is closed?” I demanded of the stout GTT4 official who chewed his nails callously at my frustration. “I’ve been hitchhiking here from LA for the past two weeks man!” “You should have caught a train, kid,” the man said taking a break from disgracing his fingers. “Maybe you would have gotten here on time.” My eyes were burning and I knew right there and then that I was going to cry at this rate. I’d practically cleaned out my bank account trying to get here from California to participate in the tournament. This was supposed to be my “big break” after all. Of course, I was way too green to actually win the damned thing, or even get relatively far – but I figured if I at least had a good showing in the first round, maybe a promoter would draw up a contract for me. And that would mean that I had a job, which would then mean that I would have money. For once. “Sir,” I croaked feeling my jaw quivering. “You don’t understand... this is supposed to be my big brea-” “Look kid,” the man said authoritaritvely looking me in the eye. “Not only are you a week late, but you’re a round late. Registration closed two weeks ago, and we’re already into round two. If you want to go talk to Mr. Argyle and tell him that you, a rookie that nobody has ever seen before, wants to be squashed into the biggest tournament in wrestling today, then you can be my guest.” “Maybe I will!” “Yeah well... let me know how that one turns out, kid.” I slunk away. I wasn’t going to talk to anyone. I realized that I was stuck here in Washington with no money and no way to get back home to California. Boy, my parents were going to be PO’ed ... Feeling defeated, I sighed and tore myself from the registration booth heading towards a nearby bench where I thought I could at least try to gather my thoughts. My thoughts which were now buzzing around in my head aimlessly. The sun was baking my skin mercilessly, and I thought to look for a water fountain, but I too tired. “What a world,” I grumbled and made my way over to the bench. “What a world, what a world...” I sat down on something soft and warm, and immediately sprung back up. “YAH!” I screamed and tripped over my own feet sending me on a one way trip to the pavement. “Jesus Christ!” My eyes locked upon what looked like a giant velvet bag atop the bench which, to my horror, started to stir. “Oh shit!” I exclaimed as the black mass continued to move. “What the-” And then, the black mass sat up, and revealed itself to be a man dressed in a cloak. A hood was pulled over his head, the shadow of which hid his eyes from me, and he had on one of those black half-masks which covered up his nose and mouth. Due to the fact that he was covered up in this monstrous cloak, and his mask, I could not see much of him other than the darkness he was encompassed in. He turned his head toward me slowly, looked at my frightened body for a moment, and then grabbed a wooden cane from beside the bench, stood up and hobbled away. “Okay,” I muttered to myself and I watched the man hobble to another bench and lay down again. “That wasn’t weird...” Standing up once again, I began another trek towards the bench, but again did not make it. This time, I bumped into a rather brawny black man who was heading down the street with a group of disgruntled friends. His rock-hard mass easily knocked my back as if I were a bullet ricocheting off of titanium. “Sorry,” I muttered feeling a bit disoriented. “I wasn’t paying atte-” But the brawny man would have nothing of it. In a swift liquid movement he had me by the collar and was yelling in my face spewing stray saliva into my eyes and he barked. “DO YOU THINK YOU’RE SOME KIND OF SMARTASS!?” he bellowed. “I OUGHT TO FUCK YOU UP BITCH!” I don’t remember who shoved me. I mean, it would have been physically impossible for the guy that I’d bumped into to have shoved me from behind – especially since he was currently using his hands to lift me a few inches off of the ground by the collar. All I know is that someone in his gang of cohorts who had formed a circle around me shoved me, which caused me to bump into the brawny bellower again. He must not have been bright enough to realize that I couldn’t have done it under my own power with him lifting me up in the air like he was, but regardless, the next thing I knew he was all over me. “Kick his ass!” his friends chanted almost cult-like. “Kick his ass!” I choked out cries for help as the guy pounded me with all of his might. Rights and lefts. Kicks and headbutts. I’m pretty sure he even bit me once or twice. In a matter of ten seconds, I was bloody, bruised, and tears were streaming down my cheek to accompany my shrieks of pain and misery. “Kick his ass! Kick his ass!” Brawny needed no encouragement. My nose already was spurting scarlet syrup and I attributed the stinging that accompanied the stream to it being broken somehow in the fray. “Let him go,” said a voice that was so quiet that I was sure that I had just imagined it. “Now.” The beating stopped momentarily, but I could not see what was going on since my eyes had been severely swollen shut. “Oh yeah!?” I heard Brawny’s voice exclaim toughly. “And just what the fuck are you going to do about it old man!?” “Actually,” the quiet but dangerous voice spoke. “I’m only twenty-eight. So... you’re not going to let him go? Is that your decision?” “Yeah!” A quiet sigh. “Very well,” the quiet voice said. “Then I must do this.” “Do what?” “This.” And then, all of a sudden, there was a sudden explosion of noise in my ears. Oh, how I wished I could see what was going on! There was the sound of screaming, groaning, and moaning, accompanied by quiet grunts. At one point, I felt some soft cloth brush over my nose, and I scrambled backwards from my horizontal base in surprise. The sounds of battle continued for a few more moments and then all of the screaming, groaning, and moaning, degenerated into mass of strictly groaning. What the hell had just happened? I strained against my enlarged eyelids to part my eyes once again and see what was going on, and managed to get them open about a centimeter. I surveyed the scene. Brawny and his cohorts were all lying on the ground groaning and unmoving. And I looked at the cloaked man just in time to see him pick his cane up off of the pavement. Everyone was staring with their mouths wide open. “Here,” the quiet cloaked man said extending a hand over me that I grabbed and was pulled to my feet by. “Come with me. I have some bandages and ice back in my hotel suite...” But the harshness of the day had made me paranoid and I dropped his hand. It fell limply to his side, and he did not flinch. “Sorry,” I said grumpily reaching around for my dropped duffel bag. “I don’t make a habit of following strangers home...” And then something completely unexpected came from the man’s throat – a chuckle. Before I knew what was happening he was pulling the hood of his cloak down as well as his mask. Revealed, was a bearded man with pale skin, and impossibly long blonde braids that he freed from inside of his cloak to reveal that they stretched to his waist. What caught my eye most, however, was his eyes. One was a normal green with white irises but the other... The other was pitch black with a blood red iris that seemed to be able to burn holes straight through me. He extended a leather-gloved hand to me. “Riley,” the man with a scarlet eye verbalized to me. “Calvin Ulysses Riley ... although I’d prefer it if you called me Mr. Riley. I’m participating in GTT4, I’m the emperor of a small country called New Eden, and I like long walks in the rain upon the sands of the beach. Nice to meet you, kid.” He smirked at his own humor, and I shook his hand. “Wesley,” I muttered after hearing that he had made the registration deadline for the tournament, but I hadn’t. “Kurt Wolfgang Wesley. I’m uh... stranded here in D.C for a spell. Maybe I’ll go watch some GTT matches...” Mr. Riley smirked again and nodded. He was leaning heavily on his cane, and I wondered how he’d taken out five or six guys, or how he was in the global title tournament when he couldn’t even walk properly. Man, I wish my eyes had been open during that fight... “Now I’m not a stranger anymore,” he informed me. “Come on. We need to get some ice on that eye...” He walked. I followed.
