IG45 Trash Talk Open Invitation thread (all vs Chris Storm)

1 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ya know, something about these Open Invitational things just doesn’t sit right with me. Probably something to do with the swarm of piss-weak gloryhounds that descends like a swarm of locusts as soon as the word “Championship” is mentioned. Dudes who throw their name into the hat despite their crippling incompetence and overbearing stupidity… a coarse blend of young, dumb never-will-be’s and bitter old has-beens, desperately trying to hold onto the fragments of a career that has long since disappeared down the can… … oh hey Seymour, how’s it going? And look, what a pleasant surprise! Everybody’s favourite mentally-deficient knuckle-dragging sideshow-freak has shown up for the ride as well:- Gabriel Kael… you are “the future”? Gimme a fuckin’ break, kid. 2001 called, they want their gimmick back. “I will break you down”? Bitch, I’ve heard HAMSTERS make noises more threatening than the shit coming out of your mouth. You’re out of your depth, son, just go and crawl back under your rock, it’ll do you the world of good. Anyway, Chris; lets not beat around the bush here. You know me – Andy Murray, The Scottish King of Cool, the most happening dude in GCW and vanquisher of evildoers the world over – and I sure as hell know you, GCW’s tag team champion extraordinaire, a true record breaker and bonafide legend in the making. I don’t have any beef with you Chris, and unlike the other chimps around here I’m not gonna get involved in a lame, shit-slinging “I R DA BEST~!” with you, but I’ve been hearing some mumblings. Lotta people think you can only cut it as a tag team wrestler, and that on your own, you’re hopeless. You know, I’m almost tempted to believe that. Let’s look at the facts, shall we? What’ve you done to earn the right to call yourself Infinite Gauntlet champion? Defeat a half-injured window-licker from the bush league SCCW? That’s not exactly an accomplishment fitting of a man of your supposed stature in this business, is it Chris? You ain’t been tested in this Infinite Gauntlet shindig yet, Chris, and that sure as hell isn’t going to change by facing Granddad Almasy or Union Jack. How about it then, Chris? Andy Murray, GCW’s future leading light, against Chris Storm, one of the most successful competitors in the game today. A chance to prove to these schmucks that you aren’t just a jumped-up tag team wrestler, a chance to show EVERYONE that you can indeed shine without Markus Stone at your side. I ask you to forget about the Gabriel Kaels, James von Dooms and Jackson Rusts of the world, and consider the threat of a REAL contender, ‘cause if you give me so much as HALF a chance, I’ll snatch that title from you in an instant.
