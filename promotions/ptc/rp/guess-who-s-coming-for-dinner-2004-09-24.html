Guess Who's Coming For Dinner?

24 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-24 On October 24th, 1901, Annie Taylor became the first person ever to conquer Niagara Falls and survived the ordeal by going over the falls in a wooden barrel. On April 21st, 1908, Dr. Frederick Cook claimed to be the first human being to reach the North Pole. On May 29th, 1953, Sir Edmund Hilary became the first person to successfully reach the summit of Mount Everest. On May 6th, 1954, Roger Bannister became the first ever human to run a sub 4 minute mile. On April 12th, 1961, Yuri Alekseyevich Gagarin was the first human to ever orbit the earth. On September 18th, 2004, Larry the Legionnaire won his first round match in the GTT4. Should Larry the Legionnaire’s win over Scott King in the first round of the GTT4 be held in the same regard as to the other great accomplishments of the past 100 plus years? If you were to ask any of the Legionnaires in the world they would without hesitation say yes it is a remarkable feat. To the rest of the world they would wonder, why is Larry’s win such a remarkable feat? Larry is without a doubt the worst wrestler ever to compete in the history of the GTT. In his career he has only ever won 2 matches and that was with the help of the New Royal Legion and against lower card wrestlers in XUW. Never before has he ever faced someone as good as his opponent was in the 1st round of the GTT4. Somehow, someway Larry won the match without the help of his two friends. It should also be added that not only did Larry win his first round match by himself he did so without knowledge of the New Royal Legion. Bailey and Danyon (The New Royal Legion) had tried to convince Larry to resign from the tournament out of fear of his safety and believed they had successfully talked Larry into doing so. However, Larry had different plans as he had a little streak of rebelliousness in him for the first time in his life and he went ahead and fought in the tournament behind Bailey and Danyon’s back. Larry the Legionnaire may proclaim that he is the greatest wrestler from Antarctica but that claim should be taken with a grain of salt. For one thing, there has never been another wrestler from Antarctica so by default he is the greatest wrestler there. Also there is that little detail that he isn’t even from Antarctica himself as he is originally from Canada nevermind the part about him being a lawyer and not a trained wrestler. All of those things still add up to him being the worst wrestler in the GTT4. The scene opens up in the now infamous Howard Johnson Hotel in downtown XUWville, which is the home to thousands of the Legionnaires and their heroes the New Royal Legion. Up on the 15th Floor of the Howard Johnson Hotel, is located the New Royal Legion Suite. The suite is a larger than a normal suite in a hotel room. A penthouse would be a better description of it. The room takes up the whole entire 15th floor of the hotel as included in the room are four beds, a normal size kitchen plus the living room area not to mention the extra large bathroom. The four beds are for Bailey, Danyon and Larry plus an extra one for the sometimes visitor “The Bastard” Al Crud of GIWF and UWF fame. In the living room area, Bailey and Danyon are situated near the television lazing about in their recliners looking very relaxed. Slowly, the door opens to their suite and in walks Larry the Legionnaire with his sidekick, Stripes the Penguin, which is a real legitimate penguin that Larry owns so he can have a friend from home. Larry walks into the suite and quickly goes to the fridge in the kitchen area to grab something to drink. Another reason he goes straight to the fridge to try and hide the giant black eye that he received from his first round match from Bailey and Danyon. Larry is wearing the traditional clothing of an Antarctican, a parka along with heavy winter pants even though it never really snows in XUWville. “Hey Larry,” Bailey calls to him. “Can you grab me a bottle of water from the fridge? You’re a bit closer.” “Okay,” Larry responds but his voice is a bit muffled as he doesn’t bother lifting his head out of the fridge. Bailey turns to Danyon, “did he say okay?” “I think so, I wasn’t really paying attention. All I heard was mmmpfh.” All of a sudden a water bottle goes flying over Bailey’s head from the direction of the fridge. The water bottle bounces off the back wall and rolls back in the direction of Bailey’s chair. Bailey looks back at the water bottle rolling on the ground and then looks back at Larry who still has his faced shoved into the fridge. “Did you even look where you tossed that bottle, Larry?” Bailey asks as he gets up and walks over to where the bottle is done rolling around. “You should have been paying attention and then you would have caught that bottle,” Larry replies with his head still stuck in the fridge. “What did you say? I can barely understand you with your head tucked away in the fridge. Didn’t your mom ever teach you good manners?” Larry pulls his head out of the fridge but doesn’t look in Bailey and Danyon’s direction. “My mom…. I don’t have a mom. She died a few years back.” Bailey looks over in Danyon’s direction but Danyon just shrugs as if he didn’t know this fact either. “I’m so sorry to hear that Larry. I didn’t know.” Larry walks over with a piece of fish in his hand to where Stripes is standing. He feeds the small fish to Stripes while keep his back to Bailey and Danyon, as he still trying to hide his black right eye. “Yes,” he replies, “she died of tuberculosis a few years ago. We had a TB outbreak in Antarctica and my mom was one of the unfortunate ones to catch it. She was one of 10 people who caught it and died. Damn near wiped out the population of Antarctica.” “I didn’t know that your mom was Antarctican,” remarked Danyon who seems confused. “Well, yes, she is. Why wouldn’t she be? I’m a full blooded Antarctican.” “Because you are Canadian………I had just…….”Danyon stops and realizes that this is a useless argument. No matter how many times they try to mention to Larry that he really isn’t from Antarctica he denies it and argues that he really is. Sometimes it’s seems as if Larry has made up his whole past to go to with this new persona. If you were to ask Larry about law school and growing up in Canada he would not have any clue what you are talking about. Instead, he would tell of a story about growing up in the cold snow covered lands of the frozen continent. Furthermore, Larry would also tell of the great storm of 1978 when he was eight years old and a big winter storm hit his small Antarctican town causing a power outage and they had to survive 2 months without electricity and in the dark. Danyon looks around the room and notices the silence that has grown over the room since he stopped talking mid sentence. He decides to break the silence. “How ironic would it be if your mom died of Legionnaires disease…….” proving that sometimes it’s better to think before you speak. Bailey shakes his head and chuckles at Danyon’s comment. “I’m sorry to hear that about your mom, Larry. I never knew that. You think you know someone.” “Not a problem Bailey, it never really came up before so I really had to reason to share that information. I’m sure there are a lot of things you don’t know about me,” Larry responds with his back still to them. However, Bailey seems to notice that Larry is avoiding them as he as yet to turn towards them and face them. “Larry, is there something wrong with you?” “No, I’m in perfectly fine. Why do you ask?” “You just seem to be acting a little odd today that’s all. I know that doesn’t mean much with you, since you are odd all the time but since you came into the room I’ve yet to see you turn around to face us.” Larry half turns his head so that they see a profile of his face with his left eye, his good eye, toward them. “Oh, nothing is wrong, I’m just feeding Stripes right now. He’s a hungry penguin.” “Larry,” Bailey says sternly, “look at me for a second.” “I am looking at you,” Larry replies with his head still half turned towards them. “I can see you out of peripheral vision.” Bailey looks over at Danyon now and definitely knows there is something going on now. “No, Larry, I mean turn all the way around and look and Danyon and myself.” Larry realizes that his little charade is up now and he slowly, reluctantly turns towards Bailey and Danyon with his face hanging with a bit of shame. He looks up at Bailey and Danyon with a sad look on his face as he knows that they are going to get mad at him any second now. “How’d you get that black eye?” asks Bailey. “I…..umm……tripped over Stripes yesterday. We were out by the pool and um, I was climbing out of the pool and tripped over him.” Bailey looks in the general direction of Stripes who is standing there beside Larry and as if he knows that somehow they are talking about him, puts his wings over his eyes to cover them. “You tripped over him getting out of the pool? Larry, you and I both know that you don’t like to swim in that water. You claim that it is a hot tub.” “Oh…..” Larry realizes that he is busted now, that the gig is up. He decides that it is time to come clean with Bailey and Danyon and tell them the truth that he did defy their orders and fight in the GTT. After all they are his heroes so he should be honest with them. “To be honest guys, the black eye isn’t from tripping over Stripes, it’s um…from the” Larry decides that it is best to mumble the last part of the sentence, “GTT.” “The what?” Danyon asks. Larry responds by mumbling once more, “the GTT.” Danyon looks over at Bailey who didn’t understand him either. Danyon asks yet again, “What did you say? Is that some Antarctican language you are speaking?” Larry looks up at Bailey and Danyon. He sighs. “It’s from the GTT. I fought in the GTT. I’m sorry.” Danyon straightens up in his chair and looks at Larry, obviously not impressed. “You did what?” “The GTT.” “You still fought in the GTT after we told you not to. You disobeyed our wishes Larry. I must say that I’m disappointed in you. I’m sure that Bailey agrees with me. You could have got injured in there something even more than that hideous looking eye you have right now.” Danyon glances over at Bailey who is obviously disappointed in Larry as well. Bailey stands up and walks towards Larry. Larry stands there with a worried look on his face. Bailey goes right up to Larry’s face and taps his large purple bruising around the eye. Larry cringes in a bit of pain. “That is quite the shiner you have there Larry,” remarks Bailey. “If we put some ice on it tonite that should keep the swelling down but the colour we can’t we do much about. I know how much you like ice so that should be a problem but there is no way to hide that black eye. I’d say maybe a week and it will be gone. I’ve seen worse and I’ve had worse myself although I had to admit that it is up there, Larry. He sure did a number on you. I just hope you learned your lesson that’s all, that you were out of your league in this thing. You’re lucky that a black eye is all you got. At least that’s over with.” Bailey turns around and starts to go back to his seat. Larry watches Bailey walk away, “I did learn my lesson. I learnt that I could hold my own in the ring against them.” Bailey swings back around and looks at Larry quizzically, “are you saying that you…….won?” A large smile comes across Larry’s face, obviously proud that he won his first round match. Danyon stands up from his chair and walks to where Bailey and Larry are standing, “you won? How did you win?” “I pinned him with his shoulders on the mat for a 1, 2, 3,” Larry responds sarcastically. “Seriously Larry, how’d you win? Is there a tape that we can see of this?” Larry stands there thinking for a second, “I don’t think there is a tape because it was an untelevised event. In otherwords, I don’t know how you will be able to see my victory.” Bailey glances over at Danyon before saying something, “that begs the question Larry, how do we know that you aren’t yanking our chain and lying to us. How do we really know that you won? By the look of your face, I’d say you lost.” Larry looks over at Stripes, “he was there. Stripes accompanied me to the ring. He can vouch for it.” Bailey looks at Danyon and rolls his eyes. “He’s a penguin, how is he supposed to tell us that you won.” Bailey pauses for a second and reflects on all of this. “You know what, that’s great you won and all Larry. I think the big thing to me is why did you go and disobey us?” “You know why?” Larry says strongly, as he looks at both Bailey and Danyon in the eyes. “I wanted to prove to you guys that I was the real deal. I wanted to prove just how good of wrestler I am. I wanted to prove that us Antarctican’s can wrestle. You guys doubted me but I never doubted myself for one second. I knew I could go in there and win. Sure I only have a few moves. An arm bar here and a hip toss there but hey I won in the end. I’m out there proving that someone from Antarctica is just as good as anyone else in this tournament.” Danyon looks over at Bailey for a second. Both of them now realize that perhaps they allowed Larry to get away this belief that he is a real wrestler from Antarctica a bit too long now. They realize that it is time to put this joke, as it was funny at first, to an end before Larry gets seriously harmed in the ring. “Larry,” says Danyon, “I think this whole thing with you being a wrestler from Antarctica has gone far enough. I realize you want to wrestle more but you know what, let’s talk to Webster and getting you to wrestle a few matches in XUW or even enrolling in XUDF. That way you can practice in the ring against people more at your level. And then after all of that you can enter tournaments like the GTT, when you are more prepared to wrestle in it.” Larry nods his head in agreement but then he stops and looks over at Stripes who looks back at him with his big penguin eyes. “No, Danyon, I’m sorry. I’m in this tournament to the end. I’m doing this because I want to. I’m taking this thing serious. I even picked out new entrance theme music for when I go to the ring.” “You did?” “Yes, I’m no longer coming out to the National Anthem of Antarctica.” “You’re not?” Danyon asks clearly surprised by all of this. “No, well truth be told, there is no national anthem for Antarctica. We’re a continent not a nation if you want to be technical. Really I should have been coming out to the Continental Anthem of Antarctica.” “Oh. Is their one?” “No. Continents don’t have anthems. North American doesn’t have one.” Bailey scratches his head, “what about Australia? They are a continent but they have an anthem.” Awkward silence comes over the room for about thirty seconds as no one really knows how to answer that. Eventually Danyon breaks the silence, “is Australia considered a continent?” “You’re the one from New Zealand, you should know,” responds Bailey. Danyon nods his head. “Good point.” Awkward silence comes over the room yet again. It’s the kind of silence that happens on the first date when you know that it is not going well and have nothing to say. The three of them stand there in silence looking at each other as for the first time in two years the three of them have nothing to say. Bailey and Danyon are upset with Larry for fighting in the GTT but they both don’t know what to do about it. How can they really get upset at him for disobeying the rules? After all, the New Royal Legion didn’t always have one million Legionnaires cheering them on. They were a thorn in the XUW Administration’s side complaining of conspiracy after conspiracy against them and had been known to cheat a few times before in the past, they didn’t always follow the rules. So, really they can’t say too much to Larry as that would be the pot calling the kettle black. However, the question remains for Bailey and Danyon on what to do with Larry and the tournament. Do they try and force him to resign even though this was unsuccessful the first time and more than likely be unsuccessful in the future as well? Or do they encourage him to continue on in the tournament knowing full well that Larry’s rookie status in the ring could possibly lead to a serious injury? The silence continues on in the room as no one is really sure what to do at this point in time. For the first time in almost a year the New Royal Legion are at odds with Larry. Not since Bailey and Danyon fought against each other last December has there been such a disagreement. However, luckily, the uncomfortable silence in the room is broken by the sound of a key being inserted into the door to the suite. All three of them look at each other as they are all wondering who it would be opening the door. “It must be Al Crud,” says Danyon. “The Bastard” Al Crud, former owner of the UWF was a frequent visitor to the New Royal Legion Suite and is considered an honourary Legionnaire because of his association to them. Bailey and Larry nod in agreement. However, when the door opens up, much to their surprise, Al Crud is not standing there. In his place, is a woman that looks to be in her mid fifties with a touch of grey in her hair. She is standing at the door with a suitcase under each arm and staring back at her are the blank faces of Bailey, Danyon and Larry. “Are you just going to stand there and not help your help your poor old mother with her suitcases,” the lady bellows at them. All three of them stand there with a look of bewilderment on their faces. Finally, Larry is able to stutter something out…..”Mmmmmm…..Mom?” Bailey and Danyon turn to each other all bug eyed and confused. “Mom?” they say simultaneously. “Yes, I’m Larry’s Mom,” she bellows yet again. “Now who is going to come over here and help me with my bags? It was bad enough lugging them all the way here. And what was with all those people out front, chanting and raving like a bunch of mad lunatics. There must be 10,000 people out there in the parking lot. That was hell trying to get through………….” Larry’s mom continues to ramble on while Larry turns and looks at Bailey and Danyon with a look of astonishment on his face. Slowly, that look turns to a worried one, and he gulps, “oh…crap.”
