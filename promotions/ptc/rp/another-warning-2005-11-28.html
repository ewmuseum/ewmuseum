Another Warning

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 Time for a lesson Uber. Try to keep up now. I’ve even italicised the key axioms for you. Bear in mind to pay heed now. You want propaganda? You want me to catalogue all the rationale why you should you be chary of Paddy O’Shea? Sure, I’ll play that game, no problem. I could stand here and natter all day about the achievements, the aspirations and how they’ve been surpassed , how he’s been here before…But I won’t, after all, what’s the point? You won’t listen … and that’s good. Don’t pay attention. It’s good because you won’t see it coming. Too fixated on making your pointless commentaries, talking absolute nonsense about nothing and generally making a fool of yourself, he’ll slip under the net. Under dog? They’re calling him an under dog. Okay, Paddy will be your under dog but when you’re the big name that fell from grace so early on, don’t say I didn’t tell you so. This is your one and only chance to learn. Now he could lower himself to your paradigm, he could be your clown. Hey, let’s make everyone laugh, let’s straighten out every blade of grass in my backyard or let’s act out amusing anecdotes with my partner in crime, whatever he’s called. Yeah Paddy could do that; hell he does it for a living every week when he’s entertaining crowds in PRIME and AWC. But he won’t take it home with me. Unlike you, he doesn’t need their cheap laughs. Because when that last titters gone, and the dust has metaphorically settled, what’s left sums you up to a tie. Silence. He’s not He loves the crowds, lives for them but he doesn’t thrive on them like you. Big dilemma for you then buddy. Please note Uber, they’re the Irishman’s crowd. When they cheer for you, it might as well be an provocation to fall on your ass. When they cheer for him, they see something superior; they see exactly what this tournament is all about. This is quiet ambition, silent confidence. Call it what you will, just remember, they’re not your friends, not your anything. They cheer for you because it’s eight O’clock and they’re quite partial to a whacky stunt. They cheer his name because they want him to win, ascend the ladder so to speak. Just like he did last time… Oh, you didn’t know that? Well let me take you back then. It’s December 2001, the Diamond in the Rough final. The tournament size, structure and even some competitors is quite similar to the GTT you know today. December 2001 people were asking who the hell is he? This Irishman winning a Primetime tournament final? Well Uber, that man was Paddy O’Shea. He’s not the same man addressing you now. The man now is stronger, quicker, more practised. He’s waded through the test of time, cutting it in the top promotions since then. Belts, titles, it’s all pocket change to him buddy. And yet they still stamp him an under dog. Fine, he’ll be their under dog, but remember, He has been here before just like you. Only discrepancy is, when it comes down to tournaments, he’s the one that already has got one up on you. Been there, done that, it’s time to move on. But until you beat me, you’ll still be stuck in square one. Which round did you reach last year, Uber? Sorry mate, you were too busy fumbling and making audios to know there was a match on the horizon. Too busy primming your rose bush, too busy being perfect. You were cocky then, be brash for Paddy. Go on, make it easy for me. He’s got other things to do than waste his time humouring a second rate competitor. Just like December 2001, this is history repeating himself. Come round one, lying on your back, you’ll be asking yourself the same thing. Take heart friend, maybe wrestling wasn’t for you anyway. Stick to what you’re good at. Comedy.
