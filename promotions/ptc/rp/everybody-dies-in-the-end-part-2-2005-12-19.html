Everybody Dies In The End (part 2)

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 12/15 12 Noon Hoyt rolls out of be and is greeted by his half naked wife who is very pregnant. Some guys are into pregnant chicks I am not and neither is Hoyt. Hoyt Williams: Good morning. Mary Williams: You have to speak at the church today get your lazy ass up. Hoyt Williams: I was up all night working on my speech. Mary Williams: Well work your ass out of bed and get ready for the days events Duke is down stairs already. Hoyt Williams: For an alcoholic he sure is punctual. Mary Williams: And consistent the man is already drunk and horny. Hoyt Williams: Everybody has a life’s passion. Mary Williams: Just get ready and get to the show. You still have to prepare for Digital Morality. Hoyt Williams: I can do that in my sleep. While Hoyt fiddles with washing up and preparing for his big day in front of him his father the Malice Man Duke Williams fiddles with the mini-bar in the living room. Duke Williams: People say I’m no good and crazy as a loon cus I get stoned in the morning I get drunk in the afternoon…. Duke sings while mixing up a midday Manhattan in his tuxedo. Duke Williams: Shit we got that meet and greet in like an hour are you going to get down here or not? Duke’s cat Guthrie is sitting on a coffee table listen and observing the actions going on. Tardiness pisses his off but this morning he is tired after a big night of cat nip and pussy companions so instead he just yawns. Duke Williams: You need to be there for the people Hoyt get moving already. Mary Williams: Hoyt’s in the shower and can’t here you. Maybe you should head down to the arena and set up for the meet and greet he’ll be close behind you. Duke Williams: Yea but I don’t want to deal with all those people. People bug me they always talk and smile and all that other bullshit. Mary Williams: You are being paid handsomely to do this. Duke Williams: Well I don’t give a damn about a green back dollar spend it as fast as I can on a Waylon song and a good cigar the only things that I understand lord, that’s all that I understand. Mary Williams: Stop singing Hoyt Axton songs and get your ass over there. Duke Williams: I’m not fighting with the weak like a pregnant women or Digital Morality. Mary Williams: Just get your ass in gear then. A few minutes later Duke is in the back of a cab with his hat Guthrie in his lap. He is heading out to the dome to set up for a meet and greet with a bunch of Hoyt fanatics and Jesus freaks. Not exactly Duke’s kind of crowd but the pay is right and the cat is a fan of Jesus. Duke Williams: You mind if I light a "J" here in your Jew canoe? The cab driver is an older African-American cab driver who has seen just about everything. Cab: You ain’t no DEA agent or any of that shit are you?? Duke Williams: Yes I am a government official with a cat in my lap and a tux on my back. Its government issue. Cab: Shit that’s a good point. Duke rolls Ronnie Reagan in suppository form and lights it up to the chagrin of the cat who opposes pot but loves cat nip. Duke Williams: Ahhhh that hit the spot. You want a hit. Cab: Yea. The cab driver takes a drag off of the crisp herb. Cab: Well I’ll be damned that’s some fine weed for a cracker. Duke Williams: I’m a fine cracker. Man I have to go to the damn church get together and to be honest I’d rather be at home watching the Bears game. Cab: So blow it off. Duke Williams: I can’t I’m a paid speaker and my son is the headliner. Cab: Damn. You know I used to hate going to church with my wife. It was always stand, kneel, stand, kneel, sing, kneel, eat cracker, stand, kneel, sing, sing, shake hands, and leave. My old ass was getting sick of it then I realized something. Duke Williams: You going to lay the jive about Jesus and what he went through on the cross because if you are save it I’ve heard it all before. Cab: Shit no nigger, I was going to tell you about the women. Duke Williams: Now you’re talking my language. Cab: Oh yea where else do old men like us get to see sexy young girls on their knees? Duke Williams: My bedroom? The old cab driver laughs, the cat does not. Cab: Then you one lucky old man and my be you need to thanking God. But if not then look around you take your eyes off the cross and look at some young titty’s and twat. Duke Williams: I just had an awakening! Cab: Now you’re talking. Thank about it young women looking for answers and salvation something tells me you can fill those voids for the young girls. The cab pulls up the dome and some young eighteen year old girl walks in front of the cab. Cab: See. Duke Williams: Amen! What do I owe you. Cab: Another hit on that joint and its free. Guthrie: MEOW! Duke hands over the "J" and the cat smiles where as he’s not a fan of the pot he does enjoy a great deal. Duke lets the cabbie keep the joint as a tip as he exits the taxi. 12/15 1:13pm Back at Hoyt’s penthouse hotel room. Hoyt Williams: Maybe I should just blow this event off. Mary Williams: It’s the biggest sermon of your career. Hoyt Williams: Yea I know but still I think I’m going to blow off the meet and greet and do some prep work on Digital Morality. I don’t know the guy very well so I need to do some background info on him and his style. Maybe watch a tape of two. Mary Williams: Maybe not. He’s from the UWF and Duke went to the UWF and said it’s quality is equivalent to that on dances with stars. You don’t need to worry about this guy you are the PRIME UNIVERSAL CHAMPION and God’s son. He is…Digital slightly better then a regular clock but much less classy. Hoyt Williams: I guess so but I’m not in the mood and the people will see right though me. Mary Williams: You are going now get your ass in gear Mr. Savior. Back at the arena Duke is signing autographs and shaking hands. Duke Williams: Yea thanks for coming out. Lady: I love you and I love Hoyt and Jesus loves all three of us!! Duke Williams: Yes he does. I’m sure he’s sending me a Christmas card right now. Lady: AMEN! Duke Williams: YEA YEA YEA you too. The line is long and never ending mainly of large black women dressed in their best purple Sunday cloths. Duke Williams: Hi how are you? Lady2: Feeling the spirit Duke Williams: I feel him too I got the worse pain in my ass. Lady2: Well I’ll pray for you and your pains. Duke Williams: Hey thanks for coming out. A hot young blonde girl of about twenty-two approaches the table and Duke’s jaw drops. Duke Williams: Wow. Jenny: I’m here looking for answers and salvation! Duke Williams: Well I have plenty of answers and salvation if you would like to meet me in a half an hour for some private prayer work? Jenny: I would LOVE that I have sooooo much to learn from you. Duke Williams: Well ditto then! The young girl walks away and Duke watches her leave as a creepy nondescript man approaches. Donald Oscar Grabowski: Where is Hoyt? Duke Williams: He had to push a turd out of his asshole. He’s in the john taking a Digital Morality. I’ll tell him you were looking for him what’s your name. Donald Oscar Grabowski : Pontius Pilate. Duke stares the strange man in the eyes for a moment as a chill runs down Duke’s spine. Something is rotten in Denmark and it’s more then the Dutch and Duke senses it but can’t place his finger on it. The Malice Man stands up out of his chair towering over Donald getting in his face putting Donald on a bit of alert. Slowly Duke reaches towards the silent alarm button under that table. He doesn’t break eye contact with the man and but backs off instead opting to shake the mans hand. Duke Williams: Well double P I’ll let him know. Thanks again hoss. The man walks away and Duke goes on with the meet and greet. Meanwhile Hoyt walks into the arena and is immediately met by security. Security: Mr. Williams, we have reason to believe your life is in danger and it isn’t safe to be here. Hoyt Williams: Well that’s too bad I guess I’ll have to go home. Mary Williams: Nonsense he is speaking today. Double your efforts and get ready for him to be safe out there. Hoyt Williams: This isn’t a good idea. Mary Williams: It’s not an option canceling. Security: I think you really should consider it. Mary Williams: NOT AN OPTION. Security: Ok we will be on high alert and you should be as well. Mary Williams: We will. Now hurry over to the meet and greet area. Security: I’ll escort him. The arrive and meet Duke who has been busy signing autographs. Security: Mr. Williams we are on high security alert. Have you noticed anything strange. Duke Williams: Nope, nothing. Oh some guy was a bit odd. Security: How? Duke Williams: I can’t explain it I’m sure it was nothing. Security: What did he look like? Duke Williams: I really don’t remember at all. He was here but I don’t remember him. Security: OK call us if you see him again. Duke Williams: You take over Duke I have some place to go. 12/15 2:55pm Duke Williams: Come on Jenny I know just the spot we can study at!! Jenny: Are you sure? Duke Williams: As sure as a cat in a hat. Jenny: Ok then. Duke takes Jenny upstairs as Hoyt prepares to make the stage. Everything is set for the biggest night of his life. AND maybe his last?
