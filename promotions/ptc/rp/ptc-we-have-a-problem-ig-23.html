We Have a Problem (IG 23)

17 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IG 23 Character: “The Fallen One” Nephilim (aka. G. Ian Childress III, VP of Production, GCW) Title: We have a problem... ~Las Vegas, Nevada~ ~Venice Hotel & Casino~ ~Venetian Prima Suite~ ~Executive Meeting Room~ The woman stepped into the hallway, her sharp feminine business suit in a dark color contrasted to her pale skin in a stark hunter green. A flash of Ivory showing at her chest showed that the woman had good taste in her apparel. Her raven silk hair cascaded down to put up quite the striking appearance for what you'd take as a casual audience. The woman was no stranger to the likes of Primetime Central or GCW. She was the leader of the Dies Irae Stable in GCW and the new Primetime Central Global Champion, Lia Vlassy... Shadow. You could say it would be casual thinking that she's the leader of Dies Irae, and that everyone answers to her. But recently she entered into a Faustian like deal with a man who offered to take her to a higher level. A man who leads the organization known as the Order of the Hammer. A man who is known also as the current head behind GCW. Geoffrey Ian Childress III The man that Seymour in his mindless banter in his head has said that Shadow has been mind fucked by. He doesn't understand things, he never could. He doesn't get that not everything revolves around him. He's a problem. He's an Achilles heel to the group. Shadow stepped into the suite after hearing the door click; indicating it was unlocked from the inside. She looked around and caught a flash of red hair over by two children's beds that had been brought up. Fey Childress, Ian's wife and business partner looked over at her and smiled. “E's in th' meetin' room, dear. Waitin' fer ye.” Lia had to think quickly to work her way through Fey's still thick accent from her native Ireland. The woman before her was by all rights a queen, and had been at one time a competitor in the gladiatorial wrestling sport that we all take part in today. Now she had resigned herself to being a wife and mother to a man who had made and broken people in the business. She nodded and then headed towards a plain door that looked to be made of a good oak. She opened it smartly and then let it go behind her. As soon as she entered the room, she saw the man she'd come to meet with. He stood there, shirtless, wearing the hakama style pants he'd been known to where when working out, training students or competing. His stringy black hair was uncombed and laid down to the top of his shoulders. A few beads of sweat remained on his forehead as he looked at her. In more ways then their dress they were diametric opposites. Both had multiple piercings in their bodies, particularly the faces. Ian was covered in tattoos of various types and designs from his travels. But even their species were different. Lia was a vampire, fifth generation from Cain. Ian was a vampyle, seventh generation from Ishram. “I came as soon as I got your message.” She was somewhat out of breathe, having rushed out of her Wynn Hotel suite across the way. She was glad she'd worn flats as she had to take the bridge over Sands Avenue to get over to where Ian and Fey were staying. Ian looked up at her with a glare. She'd taken him on as a sensai, a teacher. And he didn't look too happy. A video was playing on the plasma screen TV mounted on the wall behind him. Shadow winced when she saw what it was. The scene was from the last GCW Worldwide as Seymour Almasy was taking on the newcomer, Ciel. It was in a loop as it showed Ciel locking Almasy into his finisher and Almasy's hand slapping the mat 3 times to signal that he'd given up. He'd GIVEN UP! He spoke only a few words to her. “We have a problem.” She watched as the last fifteen seconds of that match replayed over and over. “You have a problem.” She could only nod. “What are you going to do about it?” If it could physically do so, the color would have drained from her pale face even more. Very few people could make her nervous. She knew this man had power. More then money. That's why she had jumped at his offer to teach her to find herself and to make her a better leader for Dies Irae. “I don't know.” She didn't stutter them, but she felt lacking in saying those words that way. He didn't say anything and let her continue. “I've got to face him in a few weeks. He's got a banked shot for the Global Title. As well as he's made his own aspirations for Dangerous Games pretty clear. If he takes any actions against Dies Irae or the KnightMare Brigade during that, he will be a target. He knows this too.” Ian shock his head. “The man is a virus that has invaded this body. He is a rebel, he refuses to answer to your authority.” She answered him rather quickly. “I've spoken to him on this. He sees Dies Irae as a big collective group of equals. He sees us as something akin to the Knights of the Round Table of fable and legend.” Ian leaned forward, dropping his clenched fists to the table. “The Knights of the Round Table?” She nodded. “The Knights were all equal but one man!” She stepped back a bit. “Do you think Arthur Pendragon actually abdicated his higher position to the Knights? Did Camelot have twelve kings? Or ONE? The others, Dawn, Virgil, Vivica, even that mindless brute, Mayhem have sworn allegiance to your rule as the General behind that army. You worked out the alliance with Blaise and the KnightMare Brigade. You are the chief negotiator. You are the one that calls the shots! I don't see Dawn or Virgil openly challenging you. And they hold positions in your heart that Almasy could never fill. I don't see Vivica, your protégé doesn't balk at your commands! They... they respect you.” Lia nodded again in agreement. She knew it was true. “The only thing he wants is to bed your lover, Dawn, and maybe you too. He gave in because he knew he couldn't win. But why you took him in, that is a loss to me. Why take in the weaker? Why take in one that could not stand up against you in either Global or GCW? Did you feel sorry for him?” She could only crack half a smirk, showing a bit of her left fang on that one. “He's now lost multiple matches, he alone is making Dies Irae look weak. Why? He's supposed to be the one dominating man in all of PTC history. Multiple time champion? He lost to Hoyt Williams of all people to try to get a triple crown. He's now lost face in the challenge you ordered him to do in Brandon Youngblood. And now he wants to try to face you? To bring you into his quest for personal redemption? He lost to CIEL! How can he claim to be so dominating and the best when he taps out to something like that!” She started to look like she was getting a bit pissed off. He was questioning her decisions from the past few months. “What would you have me do about it, kick him out?” Ian smiled, showing his own non-natural cut of teeth. “Ah, there you are. That would be one option, yes. But are there more?” She shook her head. He'd played her so quickly and moved to get her riled up. She could almost hear something laughing, faintly a chuckle in the air. She looked over to a mirror that hung to his side. His phantom, the side she'd not met yet was there and could only be the source of it. “I know when you lead the KnightMare Brigade of old, you were a fan of what you called 'Tough Love', that could also be an option. Seymour is a former multiple time world champion. I think he's just lost his way. If he'd listen, and do as I say then he could reclaim that in GCW. He'd put Youngblood away and then he could claim all that Youngblood thinks he's owed.” Ian's head shook as he took in her words. “So he should be punished. That's what I would do. Almasy was in the Brigade under my brother in law, Sean, in the UWF. He is a brigadier until it's current leader decides to execute. As he's your ally and in Dies Irae, I doubt Blaise would call for that unless you asked for it.” These were things that had remained in remission in Lia's mind for some time. Seymour did have ties to the KB of old under Blaise's cousin, Sean McClancy. It had been short lived due to an injury to the other two members. She knew that Seymour also held a bit of a grudge against Blaise for that, even though he'd not been the one there when it had happened. “Yes, punished. But I don't know how to do it without causing drama within the group. I'm lost on this. If I act against him, I'm the bitch who jumped him. If I tell one of the others, it would still go back to me. We're all equals in his mind until something bad would happen to him. Then as soon as that would happen, it would be a double standard. The captain would be asked to go down with her ship in his eyes.” Ian nodded at this also. “So, would you ask Blaise to offer up some help? This needs to happen soon. He's gotten away with it for too long. He's a dog running around a park without a leash, crapping all over everything you've planted. He must be pulled in before he destroys everything.” Lia turned around, leaving her back to him. “I don't know. The attachment is there. He knows that Blaise would not move unless I asked for it. It's a touchy situation.” She didn't turn back to face him. “I'm sorry, Sensai...” His face became a scowl. She finished her statement. “I'm at a loss here as to how to handle it. It's something that we should not have to deal with.” **SLAM** Lia jumped almost out of her skin. “So you'd let him get away with it for fear of hurting his feelings? Is that how they taught you, Mladshiy Leytenant Vlassy, in the Russian Army? Were you afraid to shoot at the Germans as they tried to take Stalingrad for fear of hurting their feelings?” She turned and could see the blood running down his knuckles from where he'd slammed them down onto the brightly polished table. His eyes had gone darker, yet remained with their ice blue color. The force of darkness that she had heard about from Blaise was there. She had no answer. His choice of words had slammed her in the gut like a sledgehammer. “You should know by now, Lia, that I do not take kindly to people who try to use things to their advantage to break the rules. I don't let that fat fuck Toad do it with the security forces in GCW. I won't let anyone use the feelings of their friends to try to walk all over them for their own personal spotlight. Right now, Seymour Almasy must be brought in and taught that he's got to be a team player or else it can't work out. He had broken the laws you put down for them.” He stood up. “Come here...” He held his hand out to her and she took the few steps over to join him on the other side of the table. He moved her to where she'd be looking at his back though. “Tell me, Lia, what do you see there, on my back?” She took in the whole view. His back was covered with a huge tattoo of a woman. This woman was nude, with large black angels wings coming from her shoulders. She was chained to a rock from her wrist and ankles, splayed out in a very compromising position. The woman's Asian features were unmistakable. She'd been told by Blaise that Ian's first wife, Xían had posed for it. “I see the tattoo, the one of the angel on the rock. She's chained as if given in a sacrifice.” He smiled a bit. “Look... closer...” He reached over and dropped the shade down on the window, blocking the neon lights that shone through from the Las Vegas Strip. Lia's eyes quickly adjusted to the darkness. But as soon as she did, she could pick out something new, something inside...No, something under the coloration of the traditional bamboo needled tattoo. “This tattoo is covering scars. Dozens of them. They're in groups of four to six, as if you had been... whipped.” He looked at the mirror to take his gaze to her eyes. She was already using it herself. “None of us are above the law. They broke two bamboo switches on the that day. But if one thing it did was teach me a respect for the rules put forth before me. This is a lesson that all must eventually face. If not in this life then the next.” Lia stood giving nothing away at that. “However, you are afraid that if you ordered him some tough love, that he would turn on you or question the friendship you have offered him. Those are also admirable traits. Compassion is the leveler of hatred. Which I think is why you are lost here.” She double took at that statement. “Seymour has been an enemy, a thorn in your side for so long, you don't know if you could hold back if you were the jury and executioner in this. You don't know if you could keep yourself from going too far and destroying that which you are trying to foster and build up. You don't know if you could keep that compassion, or that hatred in check. It's too much an on the line thing.” She seemed startled a bit by his new take on it. She watched at first as he started to walk towards the door, back to his suite. “This is why I have a solution to offer you.” She followed him as he opened the door and step in. Fey looked up from her chair as she was still sitting quietly near the two sleeping toddlers. He took a position next to the coffee table, next to the burgundy colored couch and a coffee table that matched the other wood in the room. On top of it was something covered with a small sheet or tarp. “Your possible solution if you'd be interested.” She looked and what ever was under it was about two and a half feet tall with some protrusions coming off the top. She reached down and whisked the cover off revealing something that struck a cord within her. The first time she had met this man was a few months ago in an exhibition match on GCW, where he had taken on the man once known as Moraless and helped him on his way to retirement by destroying him in the ring. Sitting on the table was something she had seen that night. It was a face mask used by him to go to the ring. A mask that covered his identity until he pulled it off before he started to fight. It covered his full head and went down into a chest plate in a fine, but light metal. The visage was a take on old Native American and Japanese Customs of the Demon or Oni Face masks. She knew what the offer was. It was an offer to step up for her. An offer to be her taskmaster to meet out the punishment that was needed for Seymour's lack of teamwork and performance. He was offering the side of him that was a monster that had been one of the greats in the business, back in the PWW and GIWF which were now known collectively as the UWF. He had offered to become the embodiment of her own wrath. He'd offered to once again done the garb of “The Fallen One” Nephilim. She reached out and touched the mask with her hands, caressing the side of it, heading up to the seven horns that came from it's top like a crown. The visage of the demon of the Apocalypse itself as her old training came back to her. “Why would you offer this?” He clasped his hands behind his back. “Every day, I go through the training reports from the five academies that the Order runs. Every day I'm in Dallas, I personally instruct my students into becoming better people, better fighters and those that have it, better leaders. But one thing is, that not everyone can be a leader. Some must learn to be followers. I fear that I know why you find yourself in a rough spot. Almasy has been an enemy for so long, you feel you could not hold restraint. Restraint is something I have to live with every day. Blaise would go too far, I've seen his work before. Especially if he got the impression that Seymour would turn against you again. He's the type that feels that if that's a possibility, then let them go.” Lia nodded in agreement with that. She had respect for Blaise, but he was surely a with me or against me type person. “So as I teach many, I will step into your stead and teach Mr. Almasy about being a good soldier, a good follower.” She looked at the head piece, the mask, again. “Very well, as you have taught me a lesson here. I will agree. Seymour Almasy must also meet the great teacher of the KnightMare Brigade for some lessons. And if he balks at those, then he will give me no choice but to deliver the next ones myself.” Ian acknowledged her decision. The truth of the matter is this: Seymour Almasy has taken it upon himself to put himself above all else in Dies Irae. He only thinks of himself and his own personal goals and accomplishments. While he sits in places like the UWF, a place that once meant something in the world of wrestling, a place that once claimed greats like Lew Spectre, Twizted, Jay Phoenix, Al Crud and Nephilim as their champions, he goes in and thinks his name is the only ticket he needs to rise to the top. While in the meantime he tries to ride the coattails of those he's allied with in GCW to try to rekindle a greatness he once had. But he fails time and again, just like he did at the last World Wide as everyone looked on and saw him tap out to a newcomer to GCW. A man who now can sit there and claim he took the great Seymour Almasy down. But Almasy would have you believe that he's still the man who won the Global Title those few years ago. But lately he's done jack and squat to keep that legend alive. He's the shell of the man he used to be. He feels his name value alone will scare others from taking part in this challenge. There he's wrong. The truth is that he's a tower of inequity to be toppled to the ground. And that is a truth he can't live with. For once again, the “Fallen One” comes forth to teach the valor behind the truth. And we all know, as Seymour will find out: The Truth Hurts.
