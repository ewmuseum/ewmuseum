What is waiting for Koji Sasuke? After days away from everything, he finds himself in the situation that he never wanted to be in. His time is coming, his time is awaiting for everything he wants to do. For everything he wants to take control in. Can he take control of this chance? His chance is here, yet he will be given just one chance of it all. This is the one chance, and he can’t mess up. What if it all goes wrong. What if what he tries fails? He can’t fail, and if he does then his life is over.** ::On a smooth running day of spring, Koji Sasuke questions himself for the situation he finds himself in. Is he prepared for what he has gotten himself into. As the traffic piles up, his time fades. He narrows his eyes, as a stretch black limousine screech’s on the brakes stopping infront of him.
