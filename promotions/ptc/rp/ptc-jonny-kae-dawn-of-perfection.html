The Dawn of Perfection

29 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Immortality: The Perpetuity of Existence. A nice, not too grandiose, definition of a state of being. But how is it attained? Is immortality attained by mere physical existence? or does the Perpetuity of Existence imply something more...something greater? Surely light is immortal, so long as a star exists. In the same context, perhaps one can attain immortality, as long as there is someone to remember...to smile, and reveal "I was there". People over ages have attained such immortality...for better or for worse. Plato; Socrates; Voltaire; Hitler...So, surely, Immortality is not descriminate of action? It must be simply be a bi-product of re-action. The reaction of others creates history. The reaction of the mass creates the words that future generations will hold as given truths. So, here, it is said..."Don't Hate Me Because I'm Better!" The scene opens at the headquarters of Kae Enterprizes in London, England. The scene is focused within one grand, oval room, seemingly at the highest peak of the 120ft building. Satin Curtains drape over the face of the slanted windows; decorating the interior with a lustre only ever achieved through renaiscance beauty. Over the laminated oak flooring, lies a cashmere rug...decorated with contrasting designs...red; gold and black. Next to the rug sits a glass coffee table, upon which a recent edition of "E-Wrestling Times" sits next to a cordless telephone. Two chairs sit beside the table, looking into the center of the room. In one of these chairs, the HSW Evolution Champion, "Hard-Ass" Jonny Kae, sits. He sits, pondering, staring out past the satin curtains, and into the distance, upon the london landscape. One hand rests underneath his chin, holding it in place. The slight wrinkles...tellers of age...appear below his eyes, and severe creases lie over his forehead...tell tell signs of an intensity that before now, must have been disguised so well. But, despite all that...he is still a champion. Still a fighter. Still an athlete, and entrepreneur extraordinaire. Jonny Kae sits up suddenly, and acknowledges the camera. His tone changes, to a brighter, more sincere look upon his face. He sets the HSW Evolution Title over his knee, and lays his hand across it gently. It is placed aside, as Jonny rises to his feet. "You know...as time continues, a man is forced to look back on his life. A man is forced to look back and ponder. This ponderance, is often tragic...and ends in a Macbeth-style self destruct mechanism, which is often inevitable. However...it is not the uniform concept...the uniform reaction. No, there is far more to it than that. On occasion, a man is forced to look back and realize..."I have no regrets". "I have none whatsoever". It is at this time, that you realize, you have attained something special...something meaningful...something Kae. It is, of course, all men's dreams to be remembered...to be respected...and no matter who you are, to be feared. It is the inevitable conclusion of a life's work...to look at the gauged reactions of your peers, and smile at the falibilites they show, that you have managed to extinguish within your own life. I speak from the third person...but then, I know it cannot be true. Not everyone can be like me. Not everyone can be perfect." Jonny Kae turns, and motions for the cameraman to follow. He steps slowly over towards the window, facing over the west of london. He is wearing a white shirt, unbuttoned at the collar, and a pair of black trousers, belted, hanging down over shoeless feet. Jonny stops at the window, and leans on the fireplace nestled beside it. He draws back the satin curtains, and peers over the 120ft drop to the city below. The HQ is located just off of London's prestigeous "Strand". He draws back the curtains for the cameraman also, and offers the view of the aged city. Jonny Kae then returns his attentions to the view, and points his arm out, as if to indicate something "Over there...you see? Kae Enterprizes Stock Market Analysis...KESMA. And there...Kae Enterprizes Sports Network...KESN. And there...Kae Enterprizes Manufacturing Industries headquarters. What you see, are pieces...pieces to a much larger picture. It is all a part of my empire. My empire. An Empire I created. Created from the ashes of modernism, and turned into the capitalist eutopia you see before you. It was a dream...a dream I had when my father, God Rest His soul...passed onto me all assets pertaining to Kae Enterprizes. It was on that day...that Kae Enterprizes became something special. It became my ticket to immortality. Of course, as time goes on, you realize that you want more...and more. And well, this business provided the perfect excuse for me to show my flair, my charm, my ability to destroy all competition. So, to the ICWF...and Kae Enterprizes stepped proudly into the world of Sports Entertainment, on a physical, ground roots level. And now? Now I can surely boast an impressive past...and an ambitious future. 9-time World Heavyweight Champion. Nine times, in just 3 years. Nine time champion. It just rolls off the tongue. But where must it all end? I'll tell you...it never ends. Look down there...look at those maggots...those pathetic neandothols. Some of them surely must work for me. I don't even care. I don't know their faces, nor do I know their ambitions...or their achievments. They are to me, what algae is to a passing Great White...Insignificant. "But, as to this business...where does it end? As I say...it never does. This is applicable to my current situation. There are those who, in this business, doubt the innovator of Kaemenation's ability to bring in the results...but, I tell you this: Age is insignificant. My ability has only been refined with age...like a fine malt whisky. I improve. My technique comes into question, but it devours men like a hurricane passing through a farm. I...the Current, Evolution Champion. The man who was considered the rank outsider. Always, the rank outsider. Never the favourite. But here...here is my chance to attain immortality. My superiority stands out like my impressive headquarters stand out against the backdrop of my imperial hegemony of the business world. This is my time. My Damn Time. And this...this is my ticket to immortality." Jonny Kae stares into the camera for a second. The intensity of his last remarks, forming the tell tale signs above his eyes, returning to a gentle smile. He walks over to his seat, and motions for the cameraman to leave. Kae sits, and returns to a pondering position. Things to think about. Things to contemplate. Immortality. Perpetuity of Existence. Inevitability.
