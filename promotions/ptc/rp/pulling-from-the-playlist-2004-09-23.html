Pulling from the Playlist

23 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-23 Start Program. Open Playlist. Play. Well, who are you? (Who are you? Who, who, who, who? ) I really wanna know (Who are you? Who, who, who, who? ) ’Cause I really wanna know (Who are you? Who, who, who, who? ) Stop. Who Are You? Hi! *Wave.* Allow me to introduce myself. I'm... Well, I don't actually have a name. Truth be told, I don't much so exist. Most of the time by go I "Your non-existent, semi-dyslexic narrator." The point first, I just told you about, in that I don't exist. And the second, well that's something I think you'll have picked up by now, and if not, you will soon. People dnet to catch on to my little speech issues (well, not really "speech," but you know what mean I) rather quickly. I best my try to keep it under control, but sometimes, when I get excited - as excited as someone who doesn't exist can get - things just wrong out come. A sdrawkcab word here, phrase a there jumbled... I also have an unfortunate tendency to ramble. Sorry. So, in a shellnut, it's my job to do a little bit of narration when it comes to a young man by the name of JaZon King. My hero, as I like to call him. Anyway, after successfully advancing to the Second Round of GTT Number Four - thanks to an opponent who was as non-existent as me - it occurred to JaZon that there wasn't so much known about him in these parts, or any parts, for that matter. So, he decided to put together a little promo piece - yes, by Gord, a promo! - to give the PTC and GTT crowds (that's you!) a little introduction. So here we are, in a nice, simple setting. There will be no parks or houses, and there will be no showers or changing of clothes in bedrooms. No, our scene consists entirely of a brick wall. A brick wall, and My Hero. JaZon sits there at the foot of the wall, wearing a pair of blue jeans and a black t-shirt, under a gray windbreaker. He's leaning up against the wall, with his arms folded across his chest and his legs stretched out in front of him, his right ankle crossed over the left. His shoulder-length dark hair is loose, and rustling slightly in the light breeze. All in all he seems pretty darn tableforcom. (JaZon) I am JaZon King. Capital Zee. Historical note: That little quirk came about when JaZon first broke into the business. The promoter he was working for took his given surname, Koenig, Anglicized it to "King," and then emphasized the unique spelling of "Jazon" via capitalization. Clearly, it stuck. (JaZon) I am twenty-one years old. I am German by descent, but have only ever known Chicago as my home. I am an orphan. See that? No? Good. That's a complete lack of emotion on his face. At the mention of his parents, who are in fact not dead, but worse - dead to him - JaZon belies none of the feelings that rage within him. It's a little trick he's picked up over the last couple of years, keeping his emotions from public view. It's not that he doesn't have feelings, of course. He's just controlling them. Kind of like those pointy eared guys on that show. You know, the one with "Up me beam, Scotty" and whatnot. (JaZon) I am a professional wrestler. Obviously. He flashes a quick smile at that. See, I told you he had emotions. (JaZon) I am what you'd call a "high-flyer." I like to take risks. I feed on the energy of the crowd. That he does. But he's not some creepy energy vampire or something... He gives their energy right back to them, in the pure form of exciting action in the ring. Sounds lame, I know, but just watch the kid, and you'll know what I'm talking about. (JaZon) I am on a mission. To prove myself. To find myself. If you managed to tune in to Round One, you'll know that ever since the unexpected return of his parents (and before that, the kidnapping of the woman he loves), JaZon's been a bit out of sorts. (JaZon) But most of all... I am here to win. Lather. Rinse. Repeat. (JaZon) I am JaZon King. Capital Zee. I am here to win. That'll be all for now. But I'll be back. I promise. *Wave.* See you soon! Some Other Beginning's End Excerpts from the journal of Jazon Koenig: July 30, 2004 I did it. I left NAFW. For most of my career, I've been affiliated with the North Atlantic Foundation of Wrestling in some way or another. I was 18 when Cougar pulled me out of the Chicago High School Gym circuit and gave me the chance of a lifetime. It wasn't the big-time yet - just a chance to learn and train in their farm system at Illinois Valley. I never thought it'd change my life as much as it did. But that's... Well, that's another story. It's not that I wanted to leave. My heart is still with the Foundation... In more ways than one... Damn it! No matter what I try to say, it always comes back to her! Let's try this again... It's not that I wanted to leave. I didn't. Things were starting to look up, as far as my in-ring career was concerned. I was getting title shots, people were taking notice. After all my hard work, I was starting to become more than just the random jobber The Judge decided to kick the crap out of a few years ago. The match with Ryan at Revolution... That would have been my chance to catapult myself into the singles ranks. If he'd showed up. But I knew he wouldn't... He had his "other commitments." Some top secret CIA bullshit. Don't ask me how the hell I managed to get a tag team partner who just happened to be a fucking secret agent. Anyway, things there were just getting to... Awkward. With Sean in charge, Krys with Andy, and Ryan off chasing down some international criminals - hell, probably my parents, for Gord's sake - it was just too much. I needed some time on my own. To prove myself. To find myself again. So I did it. I left NAFW. And now, I'm not sure what to do... August 27, 2004 It's been a month. A month since I left NAFW behind. And I've done a whole lot of nothing since then. I haven't proven myself, and I damn sure haven't found myself. I thought the Total J Cup was my shot, but that didn't really work out as I'd hoped. Got to the second round, but that was it. I just wasn't into it, I guess. But today, I saw something. Something that might be what I've been looking for over the past month. GTT4. Primetime Central's Annual Tournament. It's going to be massive, and it's going to have a lot of hype. If I can do well in something like that... This could be it! I've already got my tapes in an envelope. I'm totally applying for this thing. Wish me luck! Last Track. Play. The possibilities Are endless I can do most anything And the impossibilities are beginning-less And meaningless There will be no doubt about it (you are dancing in my crossfire) There will be no doubt about it Stop. Dancing In My Crossfire Hello again! *Wave.* It's nice to see you once again... Even though I don't have eyes. Seriously though, thanks for sticking around. I appreciate it. I hope you'll be glad of your choice to wait around for me to come back (as promised). Of course, this about me isn't. I don't even tsixe. It's about JaZon. In particular, it's about another promo (!!) JaZon shot, specifically for his GTT2, Round 4 match against "Sensational" Sean White, formerly of Neon. Should be fun. And so, we're back at the wall. JaZon's sitting in much the same position as he was in his earlier promo. Of course, that's what happens when you end one promo and start another one immediately afterward. He has unfolded his arms, however, revealing what I can tell you is an NAFW: Can You Handle It? T-Shirt. He might not be active there anymore, and hell, he might never go back, but My Hero's clearly still got the Foundation in him. Good on him, I say. Anyway, how about that promo? Yeah, that sounds good. Arms uncrossed, JaZon leans forward a touch, no longer resting his back on the wall. (JaZon) Sean White. "Sensational" Sean White. Can I call you "Saytch?" Great! Let's chat a bit, before I start. Ever hear of The Bens? You know, Ben Folds, Ben Lee and Ben Kweller? They toured Australia together, and one day, threw together a little EP. Just four tracks, but it's good times. I recommend it. That song you heard before I came back... Yeah, that's The Bens. Moving on. (JaZon) So, listen, Saytch... Turns out, unlike you, I am one to do the trash talk thing. Most enjoyable part of our business, if you ask me. You just can't go wrong with a good old fashioned battle of words. Talking trash, getting into your opponent's head, that'll always be at the core of this game. Along with the obvious physical outlet, the verbal aspect's one of the main reasons I got into this business. Pausing in his speech, JaZon crosses his arms again, and resumes his more comfortable leaning position. (JaZon) I must admit, Saytch, I'm a bit confused. I mean, what gave you the impression that I think I'm extreme? That I'm "hardcore" and a "brawler?" Surely it couldn't have been my "match" first round... Just need to point out that "hardcore," "brawler" and especially "match" were spoken in a rather sarcastic tone, and accompanied by the equally (if not more) sarcastic gesture I like to call "finger quotes." With that, methinks it's time to go into shorthand action mode. This is about JaZon's words, not what he's doing or wearing or taking off or putting on, and in what order. So for the next while, it'll be very simple description of his movements, and maybe some other short random comments from moi. (JaZon) Buddy didn't even show up, so in the immortal words of Homer Simpson, I won thanks to the two sweetest words in the English language... Default. But seriously Saytch... Hardcore? I mean, come on. Does this look like the face of someone who gives and takes chair-shots for jollies? No sir, no hardcore here. Though, ladder matches... Now those are fun. Smile. Wink. Uncross. Lean forward. (JaZon) Anyway... What I'm really confused about is this, Saytch... How in Gord's name do you know I've "never met someone with the technical caliber of 'Sensational' Sean White," when you don't even know where the hell I come from? Lean back. (JaZon) It's a pretty bold assumption. I'm sure you've never heard of them, so I won't name names, but I know a guy or twelve who might have something to say about that. A guy or twelve. I love it. My Hero, I say! Lean forward. (JaZon) All that aside, I've got to comment on your talk of destiny. That I'll meet my destiny. That of defeat. Lean back. Cross. (JaZon) The Gord's honest truth is, you may be right. I may be destined to lose. But it goes both ways, Saytch. Perhaps it's you who's destined to fall in Round Two. The fact of the matter is, neither of us can know for sure. No matter what we say, no matter what we do, there will always be some shred of doubt, one way or the other. Uncross. Stand up. (JaZon) But there's one thing, Saytch, that I know for sure... Inspired by a little bit of The Bens... There will be no doubt about it. You're dancing in my crossfire, and I'm dancing in yours. Now, we'll just have to wait and see who survives. JaZon smiles again, before turning away and leaving us alone with the brick wall. I for one don't like being alone with brick walls, so I'm getting out of here. *Wave.* Bye! End Playlist. Shut Down.
