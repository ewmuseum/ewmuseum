THIS is where ICE AND FNORD will RP!

13 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Bangkok, four months ago…} (We see Fnord sitting at a table looking out at the ocean watching the sunset. He has a drink in hand and a cold look on his face.) [So here I am, sitting in some anonymous bar in Thailand, waiting for something to happen. That’s how the last part of my life ended and the new part began…] (He lifts the drink and takes a few swings.) [Two months of bouncing around the Middle East, Hong Kong, Tangiers, and now here. This was Reeves’ big plan.] (He wipes his brow with one of his gloved hands. The cold of the glove from the ice in the cup gives him mild refreshment.) [Avoid the law, let myself be seen, and above all… Act like I’m waiting for a better offer to come along. Hell, at this point I pretty much am. So, not only am I going to all theses places, but I wrestle too. I have to. It’s what I keep as my cover. 4WW and now PTC are all fools.] (His gloved hands go back down and surround his drink, letting the coolness soak into his hands.) [All this drinking, and I can hardly feel it. I’m still getting used to what my condition has done to me. Realizing how much of the world and it’s effects are filtered through it. I really don’t give rat’s ass what people think of me.] (He looks over and sees a few couples having a grand old time. He doesn’t even give a small smirk.) [But my filter is clogged, maybe forever. Now it’s like a wall between me and the rest of the world. And what worries me is, I’m not sure if I really mind the extra later of separation…] (A voice comes from behind Fnord.) Voice: Can I buy you another one of those, Mr. Williams? (The voice comes from a man looking about the age of 50. He struts over to the table as Fnord turns to look at him.) Man: …or are you drinking alone again tonight? Fnord: I’m just drinking… no preference either way. And my name is Fnord. (Fnord gives a stern and cold stare at the man.) Fnord: How long have you been watching me? Man: You tell me… Fnord: The last three days, picked me up on the bus in the morning. Before that, there was a guy and a girl. They were on me from the airport. (A waitress comes buy and the man says something to her in Spanish.) Man: No hay carga para esta tabla, muchacha, y en el sitio 8 por medianoche. Waitress: Deseo II solamente por favor a usted... (The waitress walks away a little shaken as the man turns back to Fnord.) Man: Well, it appears you’re everything we heard you were, Mr. Williams. My name is Steeleye, and I’m here to offer you an opportunity. (The waitress looks over to the table one more time, before disappearing into the back. Fnord glances at her.) Fnord: What’d you just do? (Steeleye points over to where the waitress went.) Steeleye: What, her? Gave her a little push, that’s all. Fnord: You plan on trying that with me, too? Steeleye: Ah, no… While my door does swing any which way, you’re not exactly my type… And to be perfectly frank, I can’t get a reading on you at all. That’s why it took me so long to approach you in the first place. Fnord: Yeah, that makes sense. Steeleye: Because of your brainpower? Fnord: I prefer to think of it as my disease. (You can tell Fnord is starting to get angered with Steeleye. He grits his teeth and stares into Steeleye’s eyes.) Fnord: Now, what the fuck do you want? (Steeleye just smiles at Fnord.) [And that was how I met Steeleye, The Professor’s main recruiter. A full-time bastard if there ever was one, watching him use his unique abilities made you want to scrub your eyes with a wire brush afterwards… Hmm, I should use a wire brush in my match with Ice.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [So it was no huge surprise how he ended up…] (We see what looks like Steeleye on the ground face down with his pants down around his ankles and blood all around his head on the ground.) [It was only the situation surrounding his death, and the repercussions of it that came as any shock at all.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ {New York, last weekend…} (We are now in a bar with a bunch of costumed freaks standing around socializing.) [While the good guys have their secret headquarters in space and on the moon, those of us on the other side of the fence have an assortment of hangouts…] (One of the costumed idiots is sitting down on a couch with a well-endowed woman in a bikini on both sides of him.) […and an assortment of hanger-ons. Wannabes and groupies. Kids who wish they were bad, and young girls who just need to be close to bad for a few hours at a time.] (We go down a hallway and see a bunch of pictures of some mean looking characters on the wall.) [In the old days, major villains like The Doctor, Hugo Lawrence, and The Deathscythe… They wouldn’t have been caught dead around here. Those guys had class… But the badasses of our modern era spend a considerable part of their lives in underground dumps like this…] (From the door marches in a few of the worst-looking characters around. If there were trouble, they would be involved.) [Because who the fuck doesn’t want to be treated like a rock star? It’s the curse of the 21st century.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (We quickly move up to somewhere above in the same building.) [It was here that Steeleye left his final smear on the world…] (There are four men in the room. Monocide, the one-man killing machine, is sitting down against the wall with his head in his hands. Fnord is standing in the room with his same cold look on his face. A man with huge glasses on, wearing an orange jumpsuit with ‘XXX’ on the chest, is looking very nervous as his hand holding a gun is shaking. His name in fact is, Triple X-Ray. Ray for short.) Ray: Fnord… what the hell’re we gonna do? Fnord: I don’t know… Just let me think. Ray: Blackguards’ll be here any second. I can’t be no part of this… (Fnord turns to Ray and starts to yell at him.) Fnord: You should’ve thought of that before, Ray, you fucking idiot! (The man known as Monocide stands up and looks over at Ray.) Monocide: Get outta here, Ray… This ain’t your fault. (Fnord puts his hand on Ray’s shoulder.) Fnord: Now so fast. We might need him. Monocide: No. They’d eat him alive. Let him go, man… Ray: Thanks… Thanks a lot, Monocide… (Ray turns around and runs out the door. Fnord and Monocide look down at Steeleye for a second. Fnord looks over at Monocide.) Fnord: You’re a real asshole sometimes, you know that? Monocide: Fuck it, Fnord… What’re they gonna do to me? (Suddenly, two men dressed in all black burst through the doorway with guns. These must be The Blackguards. They, of course, point the guns at Monocide and Fnord.) Blackguard 1: What the hell is going on here?! (Both men now see Steeleye lying down on the ground with all the blood around his head and his pants down at his ankles.) Blackguard 2: Jesus fucking Christ in Hell… That’s Steeleye… Blackguard 1: The Professor’s going to shit a brink. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Well, look at that. Someone’s gonna pay for what’s happened. Just like in our match, Ice. Someone is going to pay dearly. Well, I know that I’m not going to be that someone. I know that I am not going to be the one that has to pay. I have entered PTC to represent 4WW. I was at first going to enter the PTC Tag Team ranks before my tag team partner had to leave because of problems. So, my boss, Mr. Ian Childress… he told me that I should go for the PTC Extreme Title. I smiled on that. I am the 4WW West Wind Champion… that is equivalent to a ‘Gimmick Champ’. Let me break it down for you. You throw any gimmick you want at me and I will beat you in it. So, call me what you will, extreme, hardcore, insane, psychotic, I’ll take them all. I’ve been known in my past to do bad things to people. I’ve been known to leave my opponents in a pool of their own blood. You see pain barely has an affect on me. And that little affect that it does have on me is pure pleasure. I prefer to get beaten over the head with a barbwire-covered steel chair. It makes me giggle. So, I joined the PTC Extreme ranks and asked who I had to beat to get ahead. Someone, I don’t even remember who, said Ice. So, I challenged you. I don’t think that you are some easy stepping-stone for me. Hell, I can tell that you are going to give it your all and we are going to have one heck of a match. But I know who is going to win. I am going to win. It’s my destiny to become the PTC Extreme Champion; I can feel that. You aren’t a stepping-stone; you are just in my way. I’m glad that you accepted my challenge. I’m glad that you are trying to be a helper here. Because, whether you like it or not, you are going to help me get to the top.
