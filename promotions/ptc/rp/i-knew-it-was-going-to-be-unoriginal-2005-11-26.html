Date: 2005-11-26 It's overtaking, Obsessed with faking, I'm blind, Looking at my own reflection. What happens… There's nothing changing, When I'm overtaking, The sound of your voice. When a Hell bent father… It might hurt, It might hurt, It might hurt! Tries to take control… Cause I know, I can't let go, Am I just dead and lonely? of his Son's life… I'm stuck and incomplete, Still I know, I must… Brainwashing him… Carry on, carry on, Leave it all behind. Carry on when you're wrong, Am I dying inside? and making him think his dreams have become his own? Close my eyes to remind, How you look when you smile, Carry on, When you're wrong, I'll pretend I’m alright. Obsessed with faking, You know I'm broken inside, Looking at my own reflection. What happens… Am I lost, Walking backwards, To the sound of your voice? When that son… It might hurt, It might hurt, It might hurt! Figures out… Cause I know, I can't let go, Am I just dead and lonely? What that father is doing… I'm stuck and incomplete, Still I know, I must… and decides… Carry on, carry on, Leave it all behind. Carry on when you're wrong, Am I dying inside? He's finally had enough? Close my eyes to remind, How you look when you smile, Carry on, When you're wrong, I'll pretend I’m alright. And what I need, You can't remove, You don't feel the same as I do, Guess I burned myself again. For 23 years… Carry on, carry on, Leave it all behind. Carry on when you're wrong, Am I dying inside? He's put up with it… Close my eyes to remind, How you look when you smile, Carry on, When you're wrong, I'll pretend I’m alright. Wanting to do one thing… Carry on, carry on, It might hurt, It might hurt! But forced to do another… Carry on, carry on, It might hurt, It might hurt! and Now he's decided… Carry on, carry on, It might hurt, It might hurt! To do something about it! Carry on, carry on, It might hurt, It might hurt! Kaden Calix!! Lyrics "Carry On" by Burn Season
