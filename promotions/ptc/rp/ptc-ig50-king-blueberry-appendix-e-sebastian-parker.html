Kenny Mercury versus King Blueberry

(King Blueberry) Appendix E :: Sebastian Parker Appendix E :: Sebastian Parker “With all that out of the way, there’s only one question that I have.” Sebastian Parker was hired by Akane Kawahara, the head of Hawaiian Island Wrestling, a member federation of the National Wrestling Council. He was contracted in the early months of 2002 for the purpose of examining and profiling Jared Sykes, a wrestler soon to be under her charge. Kawahara’s motives had been anything but benevolent, looking instead for a way to dismiss Sykes rather than embrace him into her company. Sebastian now sat in a Starbucks in Cambridge, Massachusetts, sipping a venti soy latte and staring at the man he had at one time been hired to profile. “What’s that?” came the response from Jared, in between sips of an iced caramel macchiato. “You told me how you became this character, but you didn’t quite tell me why.” Behind a pair of silver-rimmed sunglasses Jared raised an eyebrow, the corner of his mouth curling into a half-smile. His relationship with Sebastian was an unusual one. In the strictest sense Sebastian was the doctor and Jared the patient, however the two had formed something of a friendship that extended beyond the normal doctor-patient dynamic. It had started when Sebastian had made the mistake of falling asleep on the way from Boston to Portland, Oregon and his shoes had been filled with crushed Twinkies. “You want me to tell you why I decided it would be better for me to hide myself under a mask?” “In a word, yes.” Jared sighed, not quite sure if the person asking was Sebastian the doctor or Sebastian the friend, either way there was only one answer he could give. “I was hiding,” he said, taking another sip from his drink, “Mostly from myself, if you want the God’s-honest truth.” The response caught Sebastian a little off-guard. He’d always known Jared to be painfully honest, which had become evident to him in their first meeting, but in this case he had expected to have to prod a bit more before Jared would cave. “I don’t know,” Jared continued, “with everything that had gone on in the last few years I wanted a way to get back into things, but at the same time wearing a mask lets me be someone else. If I’m someone else, if I can take even the smallest piece of myself away, than I don’t have to worry about anything that might go wrong, because it won’t be me who’s responsible for it. You know?” For a moment Sebastian did not respond. “I’m not sure if I should be worried,” he said at long last, his gaze fixed on the man across the table, “I’m not sure if I should be concerned about whether you’re doing the right thing. On the one hand it certainly seems as though you’ve made or are making strides towards putting things in the past, but on the other hand it may be that you’re rushing.” “How do you mean, ‘rushing’?” “Jared, you’re about eight months removed from a self-imposed stint in psychiatric hospital where you stayed for two months because of an incident that had nothing to do with you, but which for some reason you couldn’t keep from blaming yourself.” Jared forcibly pushed his drink away from him, letting the plastic cup slide a bit on the table. His hands fell into his lap as he leaned back in his chair. When he sighed he took no measure to hide his frustration. “You have no idea what that was like, Park’. None whatsoever.” “Maybe, but here’s what I do know. I know that you beat yourself up over it, unnecessarily, for a long time. I know that you’re not over it, and knowing you I’m pretty sure that it’s not something you’re just going to forget someday.” Sebastian took a sip of his coffee before continuing. “Look, I’m not trying to be a hardass about this, but I’ve seen what goes on where you are. We have a relationship, a professional one and a personal one, and all I’m saying is that puts me in a position to maybe be a little more concerned about that than most people. And not for nothing, but if what I keep hearing about this Pierce woman is true, than I’m going to need to re-up my blood pressure medication.” Jared chuckled, a move that Sebastian found to be quite interesting given the conversation at hand. “What?” “I wouldn’t be too concerned about it, ‘Park,” Jared smiled as he took of his sunglasses, setting them on the table before him. Bright hazel eyes locked their gaze on Sebastian Parker, “When it comes to Alex Pierce I have a plan.” For Sebastian Parker few thoughts were as scary.
