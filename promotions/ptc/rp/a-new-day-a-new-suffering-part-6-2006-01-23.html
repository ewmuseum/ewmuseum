A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 6

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-23 He could feel the music. He saw and felt the colors of every chord, every note that was played. You could say that at this moment life for him was not black at all, but an assortment of colors that would leave a man colorblind. As he sat there watching the band play, he understood why life existed. It existed to give people moments like this. A moment where everything just seems to click. He smiled, because he knew that not everyone was able to see and feel things like he could. Digital thought about all his misfortunes in the past and he realized that it all led to this moment. This happiness that he had never felt before. Only the thought of living his life with the woman he loved was enough to make him see the world in a different light. Not only that, but his performance on the tournament of tournaments kept getting better and better. He had been the underdog through it all, just like he had been the underdog in life. Christian, the guitarist of the band, put his instrument on the floor and went over towards Digital. He shook his hand. This man was the reason he was able to meet Vero, so Digital would always be grateful to him. Digital hoped that he could repay Christian with something, anything. Digital thought that maybe helping him with his father would be a good place to start. "Congratulations on your victory. That was one hell of a fight," Christian said, smiling. "Thanks, did you all get to watch it?" "Yeah, Burton put it on the big screen here. That was quite a night." That felt good. To know that people still cared about him and his career felt good. After turning thirty-one years old during the week he was outside it was reassuring to know that people cared. It was probably one of the worst birthdays of his life though. He spent a week alone in a strange place. He did not have his girlfriend or his friends near… sure they called, but he had wanted to see them. To feel their presence. "Chris, we really need to talk, please sit down," Digital pointed at the chair next to him. Christian sat down on it and his mood changed immediately as he knew what was up. "Did you see what you father did to Vero? I couldn’t believe it when I saw it, but I almost didn’t go to my match because of it. Do you realize how afraid I was that he would hit her again and that I would not be here to protect her?" Christian sighed. He knew that his father had become a menace to them all. It was only a short time until he decided to put his hands on his mother once again. He would not let that happen though. He would do anything to keep his father away from his mother. "Yes, I saw what he did. I don’t know what can be done; the man is a hazard to everyone’s sanity. I’m sorry that you had to go through all this…" "Don’t worry about it, I would go through hell and back for Vero. I need your help though. I need you to protect her for me. I need you to be my eyes and my fists in case anything happens. I need you to be there for her when I am not here… I couldn’t live with myself if I came back and found her beat up once more." Christian nodded. "I’ll keep a close eye on her. By the way, did she answer you already?" Digital’s eyes lit up. Every single time the subject of his proposal came up, he felt like smiling. "She hasn’t answered me yet… but I saw her face light up like I had never seen it before. I am sure she will say yes." "I hope so. It would be great to have you as a brother-in-law," Christian told Digital. Digital hated waiting for an answer because deep down inside he still had doubts, but on the outside he tried to show everyone that he was confident that she would accept. It was one of those few things that he could just feel. There was not much else he could do but believe and hope. Hope that Veronica would see the same things he saw, that she would feel the same way he felt, that she would want the same things he wanted. "I understand that this is a big decision and the way I asked was a bit awkward and surprising." Before he saw her, he could smell the colors of her perfume. He stood up, waited for her to come through the door at any moment. The air was a rainbow of smells, sounds and colors. His heart felt like it was going to burst out of his chest with expectation. The butterflies inside his stomach were on overdrive. She walked gracefully across the room. He short jet black hair got on her face as always and she had to move it with her hand. She still had small bruises, but they were well hidden under the makeup. Every time Digital saw those bruises it made him want to hit something… or someone. Never her though, never her. His heart sank a little when he saw that her hand was empty. There was no ring on her finger. He would not mention it though, he had told her that he would give her time and that was what he would do. He couldn’t help but sense a shadow coming over everything at that moment. Maybe he had been wrong and she wasn’t ready for it. He would wait for her though. He knew that they had not been going out for that long, but just her presence made everything about him better. His performances had been spectacular against two of the top performers in the world. The tournament had taken its toll though. Digital’s body, even though he tried to hide it, was sore and bruised. Before this he had gone one on one with three opponents and succeeded, but now he had three opponents in the same ring with him, wanting the same thing as him. Victory. They all craved it, for it would cement their performances in one of the biggest events of the year. Everyone wanted to succeed in life, and Digital hoped that his career would finally be fortified as one of the best in the business. Three hungry opponents stood against his goal. He only had to beat two to pass on to the next round, but if he could win the whole thing it would be something to be remembered. Veronica went over to his brother Christian and hugged him. She then moved towards Digital. They embraced and their lips met. Her soft touch was something that Digital had never felt in his life. It felt as if his body was being electrocuted… but in a good way. No matter how old he was, the butterflies kept fluttering around his stomach every time she was there. "I missed you. How are you feeling?" she asked; she had seen the pain he had been in during his match. She only felt bad that she had not been there to pick him up at the airport, but the company she worked for was doing shows on the western part of the island. Her dancing had made her put some things to the back of her mind as she concentrated to get every step right. She could not screw this up. "A little sore, but other than that, happy to see you again," Digital said as he hugged her once more. "Are you ready? Let’s go eat." ---- Dinner had been wonderful. Compared to the last dinner they had together, this one had been great. They spoke about the little break they would both take to be together after her show was over and after the GTT5 was over or Digital was eliminated, hopefully it would be the first one. There was no mention of marriage and no talks of her father. They needed time for themselves. Veronica excused herself to go to the bathroom after dinner was over and the check had been paid. He waited for her at the entrance of the restaurant. Even though this had been a great dinner, he couldn’t shake the sense of foreboding that told him that something was wrong. He couldn’t quite place it, but it scared him. "You are becoming paranoid once again… snap out of it." He had many enemies around the world, but there hadn’t been an attack on him for two years so he didn’t understand what could be the cause of the feeling he had. After a few minutes, Veronica arrived. They walked to the car; Digital opened her door and closed it. He went to the driver’s side and got in. They had planned to go to the bar he owned that night after dinner, but Veronica had something else in mind. Digital didn’t mind, he hadn’t shared the same bed with her for a week or so. The drive to his house was a short one. In less than fifteen minutes they had arrived. The night was clear but the January air was still colder than usual. The house looked weird to him… something was definitely wrong. He walked slowly towards the entrance and motioned to Veronica to stay quiet and close to the car. He finally got to the front door, his knuckles white from the tension of the close fists. It hadn’t killed anyone yet to be ready. He opened the door and slowly turned on the light. "FELICIDADES!" Digital jumped back in surprise as confetti flew through the air towards him and a loud bang was heard. He couldn’t believe it. Everyone was there. Burton, Sheila, Christian, Nick, and his closest friends were all there with pointy birthday hats and weird sound making mouth pieces. Over the frame of a hallway was a banner with Felicidades a nuestro campeón del GTT5 emblazoned across it in a graceful font. He was so used to bad things happening to him at moments in which he felt happy, that if three thugs with guns had jumped him instead of this surprise party, he would not have been surprised. It took him a little while to register what had happened that he stood there mouth open in front of everyone until Veronica came in the door with a camera and took a picture of him. The flash of the camera seemed to wake him up from his trance. He hugged Veronica tightly and gave her another kiss on the lips. Everyone started hooting and laughing. Burton told them to get a room. "I would have to kick you all out of my house first!" There was laughter all over. This really was a big surprise. This had been the first surprise party anyone had ever thrown him, and it felt really good. It felt like people really cared about him. The night continued on with partying, music and alcohol… lots of alcohol. There was a good feeling all around and Digital knew that this night was one of the best of his life. This was probably the best birthday party anyone had ever thrown him and he was with Vero. Everything seemed much better when he was with Vero. A drunken Nick, the bouncer of the bar, went over to Digital and shook his hand. "Happy birthday man… I… you are gonna be so great in the rest of the tournament… I love you man! Give those bastards hell!" Nick hugged Digital hard. Digital could feel his bruises as the big man clutched him like a bear. Nick then saw something more entertaining and moved on. Veronica had been speaking to Burton while the little exchange with Nick had been going on. She looked at Digital and smiled sweetly. They didn’t drink a lot that night, because Digital really wanted to celebrate with Veronica, and he hated the hangovers. At that moment, Burton took the karaoke microphone. "Well my friend, I really hope that you have enjoyed this party. Veronica and I planned this thing because you are one of a kind. Everyone here wishes you a happy birthday and good luck on your next round match up in the tournament. Now Veronica has something she wants to say." She took the microphone from Burton’s hand and smiled at Digital. "As you all might know, Digi asked a very important question a few days ago. At the time, I didn’t feel like I could answer the question. I hadn’t expected it and in order to make the choice that was right for me and for us I needed a little time. Well, I have made my decision." Digital’s heart jumped. Could this be the moment, could this be the time where his life would change forever. He would finally have his soul mate close to him, a part of him. The expectation was killing him. "Yes, Digi… I would love nothing more than to be your wife." All his doubts disappeared at that moment. Gone were all the fears he ever had. All his insecurities were wiped out in his mind. This was the time of his life. He had finally broken his curse. He had the woman he wanted and she would help him go into the ring and destroy his three opponents in the tournament. No one had ever seen Digital perform the way he had, and no one would be able to stop him. Not in his career and not in his life. They embraced to the cheering of the drunken guests. Burton went over to them and congratulated them both. This would be a night to remember, he had been with Digital for a long time and it felt great that he was finally happy. They both glowed that night; he could feel the colors of their feelings floating about him. He silently toasted to their happiness. Digital said his goodbyes to everyone that was still conscious. There was a mess in the living room, but that didn’t matter to him at all, nothing would ruin the night for him. He kissed Veronica like he hadn’t with all the people there. He held her close, caressing her, passing his hand through her hair. He turned off the lights. The stars had aligned just for him on this night and it felt good. It finally felt good to be him.
