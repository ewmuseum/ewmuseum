Darson Digital Genesis Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 609 Location: With Tessa on my Lap Posted: Wed Apr 09, 2003 4:08 am Post subject: Meeting Laguna the Soldier [Extreme #1] In a far away land there is a place called Galbadia. It is just like any other place. A governing force rules Galbadia for the people and by the people. But unbeknownst to the people of Galbadia, there is a level hidden dead within the Galbadia in the military. There men are trained to be lethal killing machines. Trained to blindly follow orders to crush Galbadia’s enemies and increase the wealth of the fat cats of the Galbadia Senate. To enter the Galbadia military you must be above the cut. You must be meaner, tougher, stronger, faster, and better then everyone else. These elite have the killer instinct deep within them. Cold, brutal, and without mercy they strike like a well-oiled machine. They move with stealth and accomplish their objectives without being noticed. Within this finely tuned system of soldiers is one man who stands out among all the rest. Standing at 6 feet and 180 pounds he walks the land doing justice and fighting for the cause of Galbadia. With his machine gun on his hip and two trusted allies by his side, he has no fear. He is the one they call in when things get their worst, when no other man is willing to accept the mission. He is Laguna. “Hi you guys!” Urgh…Laguna…I’m trying to narrate here. Do you mind? “Oh sorry, dude! Go on with your narration thingie uh…yeah!” Sigh. Laguna go study the map I gave you. “The map? Uh well you see I uh… IT WASN’T MY FAULT I SWEAR! Ward lost the map. It wasn’t me!” Laguna… “Yes?” Did you lose the map? “Uh…yeah…” Sigh. “Sowwie” Look, just go make sure the jeep is… “Uh Mr. Narrator, person, sir, dude, guy, uh…” What Laguna? “Well you see….IT’S NOT MY FAULT! Kiros forgot to put on the emergency break and you see what happened was…” Laguna… “Yes?” Did you lose the jeep? “Uh…sort of…” What do you mean sort of? “Well it sort of rolled off a cliff backwards.” Sigh. Laguna please go away. “Ok… ” Now where was I? Oh yes that is right. Galbadia is a land where they survive from the blood of men they never will see nor know. Heroes of the land are ones they would never expect. There is however some exceptions to the rule. Through some technical error one soldier by the name of Laguna has somehow managed to climb through the ranks and be assigned his own unit. Even with Laguna’s odd form of leadership, the unit still by some miracle manages to complete all their missions and have one of the best track records in all the Galbadia military, though they do cost the most. “Hey!” What now Laguna? “Is this the part where you introduce us now?” Yes Laguna. “Kewl! I wanna help!” Uh Laguna… “Yes Mr. Narrator man, dude, person with no body as just a booming voice we hear.” Your tent is on fire. “AAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” Sigh. Now like I was saying before. By some unknown force or reason Laguna was assigned to be in charge of his own unit. Accompanied by Kiros and Ward, Laguna ventures on with deadly mission after deadly mission as the Galbadia military tries to finally get rid of the costly pain in the ass known as Laguna. “Uh…did you say something?” Uh…well…you see…uh…no. “I thought I heard you mention my name.” Uh Laguna…. uh your hair is on fire now. “GGGAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!! IT HURTS!!!!“ Why me? Why? Haven’t I been a good booming voice of narration? Why was I punished by having to narrate for this…. this…MORON! Sigh. Oh well I might as well introduce the bubbling moron. Laguna is a strong willed soldier in the Galbadia military. He fights for the people. He is always willing to help even when it is beyond the call of duty. A kind and gentle soul, he actually detests violence but feels at times it is the only means of securing peace. Along with his specialty made machine gun Laguna will continue to fight in the name of those who can not defend themselves. Kiros; quick tempered but good hearted. He is second in command behind Laguna and is the brains of the group. Kiros always tries to look at a situation with calm and reasoning thought. He is a passionate man and enjoys the finer things in life. He romanticizes the ideals of military life as he looks for the greater good in the world. When around friends he can kick back and enjoy life for as it is. Kiros uses his two katana blades to do his fighting. Ward is strong as an ox and righteous to his cause. Ward stays true to his simple way of life and enjoys the simple pleasures of friendship and nature. Ward is the voice of reasoning between Kiros and Laguna. Wearing only a white t-shirt and baggy army pants with a bandana covering his baldhead, Ward tends not to let much get to him. He carries a large anchor with him to use in combat, being one of the only men able to throw it at his enemy and quickly retrieve it, even with his large size. “OooOOOooo! You made us sound really all badass and shit! But uh can we go ahead and start the story now?” Yes Laguna, you may now start the story. I’m out of here before I have a mental breakdown. “Mental what huh? Any who! I’m going to be narrating for the beginning of the story so sit back, relax, go put on your favorite Daft Punk CD, and ROCK ON GALBADIA! Woo! Uh yeah!” So uh let me get into my narrating voice here. Ok is that good? Yeah there we go. I like SO rock and stuff! Now as I was saying, I’m going to narrate the beginning of the story. So uh…here we go! It all started a long, long time ago in a place called Galbadia. The city was filled with bright lights as night covered over it like a blanket of stars. My unit and I were relaxing at the local hotel listening to the gentle piano music being played on the stage. We had just completed our previous mission successful and were enjoying time off service. Little did we know however as we were enjoying our time off from active military service that we would soon receive the biggest mission of our lives. The air was calm against our skins, feeling a light breeze from the outside being carried in. The hotel dining area had a certain feel about it that brought the soldiers in to relax. Maybe it was the soft sounds coming from the piano or maybe the dim lights that fell upon you allowing one to be lost in his thoughts. For me it was the sight of the beautiful pianist on stage. Her name was Julia. She was like an angel dropped down from the heavens to touch my soul. Ward: Hey Laguna, who don’t you go talk to her this time? Kiros: Yes stop being such a coward and go talk to her. You ogle over the young lady so much you might as well go talk to her. Laguna: Ah come on now guys, I can’t. I mean I’m just a soldier. She’s an angel! As you can tell I’m a little nervous to talk to her. For the past year my unit and I have returned to that hotel dining room and listened to her play the piano after every successful mission. I have in that time developed a crush on her. Although I am too nervous to ever tell her anything like that. Instead we go and listen to her as I admire her from afar. There is even a rumor going around that Julia was the eye of the General of the Galbadia military. But tonight I was going to throw caution to the wind and go for it. I was going to talk to her! Well…after a few drinks anyways. Laguna: I’ll do it! Ward: Do what? Laguna: I’m going to talk to her and ask her to join me for a drink! Kiros: Good job lad! Now go for it. [Laguna rises from the booth they were sitting at and starts walking toward the stage. Julia’s two bodyguards watch Laguna with cautious eyes.] Kiros: Twenty gold coins say Laguna chokes. Ward: I think the guy has the courage to do it. After all he did charge the entire Ester defense unit by himself. Kiros: Only because he got lost in the woods and accidentally stumbled across their base. Ward: Oh yeah… [Julia doesn’t even look up from her piano keys as Laguna makes his way toward the stage through the empty hotel dining room. Just as Laguna gets near her his leg starts to cramp up. Laguna tries to make it farther but his leg soon becomes completely cramped. Failing, Laguna turns around and limps back to the booth where Kiros and Ward are sitting. Wards hands over twenty gold coins to Kiros.] Yeah I failed miserably. I should mention that I am really shy around pretty girls. Whenever I get near one my leg becomes cramped and I can hardly even walk. So I once again chickened out and made myself look like an idiot. I can still remember those bodyguards laughing. But I had the last laugh… [Laguna sits back down to sulk with his apple juice. Kiros and Ward try to cheer him up as the piano music slowly dies down, coming to an end for the night. As Laguna and the crew start to leave one of the bodyguards hands Laguna a note. Laguna unfolds the paper and looks at it.] Dear Sir, Please meet me in my hotel room after this night’s performance is finished. I will be waiting for you. Ask the receptionist ask the desk to give you a key to room 143. Signed, Julia. Laguna: Whoa! DUDE! What do I do now? [Kiros and Ward slap their foreheads.] Ward: Laguna, I think you should go to her hotel room and see what she wants. Laguna: Her hotel room…but what do I do when I get there? [Kiros and Ward groan. They grumble to each other then look back at Laguna.] WHAT?!? Like I said, I’m shy. Hahaha, let’s move forward a little shall we? Yes I did go to meet her in her room. Kiros and Ward decided they would go to the shopping center to restock the camp before the next mission leaving me to see what my host wanted alone. Her room was lovely. Light color paint and a single bed decorated the room. Her window had an amazing view of the night life of Galbadia. There was a small table with two chairs by the window and a desk with the television resting on it by the door. There was a balcony near the window that overlooked the city of Galbadia. As amazing as the room was Julia looked even more amazing. She was still wearing her dress she wore while performing this night. However her hair was let down and was resting on her shoulder and down her back. She was looking simply stunning. Uh oh…I can already feel my leg cramping again. [Laguna looked around the hotel room in wonder. He quickly makes his way to the window and stares at the view. After a few seconds he goes over to the balcony and looks out of it through the glass door. Julia smiles as she watches Laguna run around the room like a puppy exploring a new home. Laguna turns around to go to the television but Julia is right there smiling. Laguna stops and takes a step back surprised.] Julia: Hello. Laguna: Uh…hi! Julia: Mind if I ask you your name? Laguna: I’m… Here is when things started to go downhill for me… [Laguna rotates his upper body and lifts his black shirt up so he can see the back of his pants. He pulls them out and checks his underwear.] Laguna: I’m Fruit of the Galbadia…no wait that isn’t right! I’m Laguna! Yeah that’s my name. Haha, its Laguna. [Julia covers her mouth with her hand as she laughs slightly.] Julia: Are you sure? I’M A MORON! I should have beaten my head against the desk for that. Uh…back to the story. [Laguna laughs with Julia. Hearing Laguna finally start to lighten up Julia starts to laugh even harder. Soon the two are laughing hard, holding their sides. With all the laughter, Laguna completely forgets about being nervous and soon starts to enjoy the night.] It turns out Julia had noticed me staring at her from afar and she actually had to admit that she would stare at me when I wasn’t looking. The night continued on and I soon relaxed. I started to tell Julia about everything that would come to mine. Laguna: Although I’ve been in the military for a long time what I’ve always wanted to be was a journalist. To write about my adventures from around the world and the places and people I’ve seen along my journeys. Writing is my one true love. I continued to ramble on and on to Julia like an idiot that doesn’t know when to shut up. Unbeknownst to me however one of Julia’s bodyguards, whom also were a spy for General Cecil, was right outside the door listening to everything. As he gathered his information he needed he silently slipped away into the night of Galbadia. Little did I know that when General Cecil received the news of my midnight meeting with Julia that it would start the downfall of my unit and my own military career. [At the home of General Cecil, the spy has just returned from the hotel room and given his full report to the General. Cecil turns in his large black chair and faces the spy. Behind him is a giant fireplace with his image above it in full army uniform on a horse, his sword drawn to fight.] General Cecil: This is very interesting. It seems we will have to send Mr. Loire on a mission very far away, one that will require all of his knowledge, skill, and talents to accomplish. This all works favorably for the government of Galbadia. We have been looking for a reason to send that moron on a one way true to hell. Now we will finally get our chance. Evil dude huh? No wonder he’s the villain of the story. He’s picking on poor me just because I’m trying to get my hook up going for me. He’s a stupid mean man. Uh…back to the story. [Cecil dismisses the spy. The spy salutes the General and makes his exit through the richly designed oak double doors. General Cecil turns back in his chair to face the fireplace. He starts to laugh menacingly as black clouds gather around Galbadia. Lighting crackles through the gothic design window.] You see? The guy is creepy! Bad man! [Laguna freezes the screen and starts to scribble on General Cecil with a black marker. He puts horns and a mustache on him with a red marker making a stupid face on him.] Well that is my story. This isn’t the end of it however. There is still much more to come. However I feel I have taken up enough of your time so I will leave you for now. Please join me as I continue the brave tale of a soldier that dared to fight for what he believed in. TO BE CONTINUED… _________________ Tessa: YAY I burped and now i have more room in my tummy!
