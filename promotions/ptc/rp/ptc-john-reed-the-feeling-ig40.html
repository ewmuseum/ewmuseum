"The Feeling" - John Reed November 16th, 2007 The phone on the table let out a low hum and began to vibrate; soon enough “Thunderstruck” was in full blast as the call continued to ring in. After a few seconds a middle aged man walked into the room of the apartment and snatched the phone up. “Hey Dave what’s up?” He asked. The man was clad in a pair of jeans and a brown polo. He cleared his throat and sat down on a nearby sofa, listening to the other end of the line. He nodded and laughed along with some of the comments, catching up with what seemed to be an old buddy. “What? Man did you see the last time I was in a ring? That Iron Man match in FUSE nearly killed me.” He stated. The call continued on; the man giving his friend a chance to speak his piece. Though it was evident the man was in his mid to upper thirties, he had not let the age get to him. His hair was still a nice brown color, free of any grays. He hadn’t let his body go either; his biceps were visible through the sleeves of his shirt. “Well, I’ll do you the favor of considering it. You’re a friend and I’m glad you gave me the heads up. But why would I want to get back into the ring?” He said. He stood back up and wandered over to the table where he had gotten the phone and nodded one final time. “Okay, I’ll think about it and if I feel good enough, I’ll meet you there. Later man.” He finished. Just like that the phone was set back in its original resting place as he turned to exit the room as he had entered. But just as he turns through the door way, a glimmer of light catches something in a glass case on the wall. He backtracked and stood in front of the case, focusing on something. He sighed and stared straight ahead at the gold belt sitting in front of him. It was the GCW World Heavyweight Title, with his name engraved on the bottom. It read “John Reed.” Fade… James Bryan: He’s got him up! John Reed has him set up for the Cradle Tombstone! David Yale: No! This cannot be happening! Reed steadies his man in the piledriver position and then reaches out to wrap his arm around a leg. His fingers interlock as he looks out to the crowd before dropping to his knees and driving the man’s head into the mat. John collapses on his opponent and hooks the leg. Bryan: ONE! TWO! THREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!! HE DID IT! Yale: This sucks! Reed rolls off of the defeated man and buries his face in his hands as the referee receives the championship belt from the time keeper and offers it to him. Andrews: Here is your winner and the NEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWW GCW Heavyweight Champion…..JOHN REEEEEEEEED! Yale: This cannot be true! Nobody beats… Bryan: Believe it! John Reed has defeated The Renegade! John Reed has taken the Heavyweight Championship from the clutches of Rich Rollins! John takes the belt from the referee and looks at it for a moment before pulling himself to his feet. Soon enough he is joined by fellow System X members Luke Hanson and Toad, who lift the new champ onto their shoulders. Fade…. John sighed again as he opened his eyes and allowed a small smirk to cross his face. He dropped his head and nodded; he knew what he needed to do. With that he turned and re-entered the room which he had been just minutes prior. November 17th, 2007 4:30 P.M. CST The sun had almost set as John pulled his car to a stop in front of a small building. To his right sat a white box truck, a few guys were unloading it. John sighed and stepped out of his car, retrieving a sports bag from the backseat before he made his way to the entrance. As soon as he stepped inside the building and saw the ring chills went up his spine. He felt young again, remembering his early days on the independent scene. He stood there, looking at the squared circle for a moment before his concentration was broken by a voice. “You have kept your body in shape my man.” It said. John turned to spot his friend Dave as he walked up. John extended his hand and began to look around as it was accepted. Most of the setup for the show had been completed; all that was left were to finish setting up chairs. “This brings back memories.” John said. Dave motioned for John to follow and the two walked from the building entrance and through the curtain into the “backstage” area. A few of the older guys on the roster were there as the young workers were setting up the ringside area. John recognized a few of the faces from the early days. “Now listen. Mike wants you to work the opener. Don’t get discouraged, he just thinks you’d mesh well with one of their curtain jerkers. The kid isn’t a bad worker by nay means. He’s just starting out.” Dave stated. John kept quiet as Dave walked away to find the worker. John waved to some of the older guys as he walked to an empty chair and set his bag down. He sat and began to remove his gear from the bag as Dave reappeared with a teenager in tow. “John this is Justin Sensible. You’ll be working with him tonight.” Dave introduced. John shook the young man’s hand and returned the introduction as Dave turned to leave the two alone. November 17th, 2007 7:34 P.M. John stood behind the curtain as he listened to “Welcome to the Jungle” play as his opponent made his way to the ring. He jumped in place as it faded and the crowd continued to jeer the youngster. Soon enough the familiar sounds hit the sound system and John took one final deep breath before busting through the curtain. # It’s just one of those days # # When you don’t wanna wake up # # Everything is fucked # # Everybody sucks # The crowd let out a huge cheer as the former star walked towards the ring and made a lap around it. He slid into the ring and raised a hand to the crowd as “Break Stuff” faded. The crowd took a few moments to quiet, allowing the ring introductions to take place. “The following match is the opening contest of tonight’s show and is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first from St. Louis, Missouri Justin Sensible!” It was said. The crowd booed his greatly as he turned and yelled at them, showing a great heel presence that John had to appreciate. The crowd waited in anticipation as John leaned in the corner, relaxing himself mentally. “And his opponent from Chicago, Illinois John Reed!” The crowd cheered loudly again as Reed stepped from the corner and raised his hand into the air once more before the ring announcer spilled from the ring. The referee checked both men for illegal objects and then asked for the bell. It rang, bringing the two men to the center of the ring in a collar and elbow tie up. Reed measured up the two hundred thirty pounder and spun around, grabbing a rear waist lock. Justin applied pressure to John’s hands to release the hold. The youngster spun around grabbed a waist lock of his own before switching to a headlock. John used his power to shove his opponent to the ropes, leapfrogging him when he came back. Justin returned to Reed laying down and hopped over, hitting the ropes once more. When he returned, Reed was up and in mid leap. He landed on Justin’s shoulders and snapped him over with a hurrincanrana. The crowd applauded the spot as John got to his feet and looked towards the curtain, seeing Dave. Dave gave him thumbs up before reed turned back to the youngster. November 17th, 2007 7:49 P.M. CST The crowd was loud as the tried to get behind Reed as Justin collapsed on top of him after the brainbuster. The referee counted but was halted just before the three as John lifted his shoulder. The crowd reacted as if the home town team had just won the Super Bowl. Sensible got to his feet and began to argue with the referee as John slowly got to his feet. Before Reed could take an advantage, Justin turned back to him and sent him into the ropes. John came back and ducked a clothesline, slamming on the brakes. The two men turned simultaneously and john lifted his boot into the gut of the younger man. The crowd buzzed as John shoved him under his legs and lifted him up into a piledriver position. John reached out and locked his hands around a leg before he fell and finished off the cradle tombstone. The crowd cheered wildly as John hooked the leg and received the three count. “Your winner of the match John Reed!” John got to his feet and celebrated only briefly before he spilled outside and walked back through the curtain. Dave stood on the other side to throw his arm around his friend. The two shared a laugh as they walked through a set of doors into the locker room. “How you feeling old man?” Dave asked as a joke. John sat down and took a deep breath as he unlaced his boots. He looked back up to his friend and smiled as widely as he had in a long time before he kicked the footwear off. Dave laughed as he watched his friend try to catch his breath. “I forgot how that felt.” John replied. Dave laid a hand on his shoulder and patted it a couple of times before sitting down in a nearby chair. He knew he had gotten what he wanted out of John. John knew it too. Dave had watched John rise into superstardom in GCW years prior, and never once got jealous. He was happy that one of his friends had made it big. Now that he couldn’t make it in the ring anymore, he wanted to help John gain some of that stardom back. “So are you in? You heard those people out there. They loved you being back in the ring. Just like the FUSE fans did when you wrestled there.” Dave said. John looked at him and nodded slowly. He knew what he had to do. Wrestling is what he loved and wasn’t ready to give it up just yet. Even if John never won another Heavyweight Title or worked for another high profile promotion, winning Infinite Gauntlet would be something to be proud of. “I’m in.” John said with a smile.
