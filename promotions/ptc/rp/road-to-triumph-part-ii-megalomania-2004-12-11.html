Road To Triumph – Part II: Megalomania

11 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-12-11 Road To Triumph – Part II: Megalomania “Who the flaming hell do you think you are?” The words spit from my mouth with malicious intent. I may not be able to get out and physically batter this person, but that doesn’t stop me from spouting as much verbal abuse as I can muster. Over on the other side of the basement, shrouded in the shadows of the scarcely lit room, sitting on the wooden steps with his chin in his hands in thoughtfulness, the doctor sits contemplating his choice of words to answer my ferocious question. He moves to his feet and slowly steps across the floor, his heels clicking hard on the concrete for added dramatic effect. “Who… Am… I…?” the doctor says repeating the question to try and better understand the meaning to give a proper answer, either that or to prolong the answer to make it more dramatic, not that it really matters who this guy is. “Who… Am… I...? Good qvestion.” The doctor stops beside me then leans against the wall. He folds his arms and looks down at me sitting in the seat with a grateful look on his face that I can’t break free of my shackles and do some irreparable damage to him. “My name… ist Doctor… Curiosity…” This man is insane. I thought this man was eccentric, but I never figured he was mentally ill. Well he has kidnapped me, which is mad enough in itself, but it all seems to be stemming from some obsession for Doctor Curiosity. The scraggly coat and foreign accent was obviously not just a coincidence. “You’re not Doctor Curiosity, you idiot.” I inform the deluded freak that stands beside me. “You’re a flaming fraud, that’s a hospital jacket, not a lab coat, your hair is black, not grey. You don’t even sound like him even when you put on that ridiculous voice.” “Shut up! Shut up!” the Doctor calls out as he moves over to me, his face turning beet red in anger. “I am Doctor Curiosity! Zis is ze real Doctor!” “You’re not fooling anybody, pal.” I say continuing my irritancy in my own angry outburst. “You’re nothing but a pathetic little man wanting to play silly games pretending to be somebody you’re not.” “Ahhh!” the doctor cries, pushing away from the wall to distance himself from the torment. The truth hurts, evidently. He paces back and forth frantically across the room, the tapping of his heels on the concrete gaining volume with each step. “You don’t realise vhat it’s like!” the doctor screams in revelation, walking back over to the wall beside me, only this time pressing his head against the wall with his arms wrapped around his head. He’s coming to terms with his delusion, and he doesn’t like it. “That man ist my idol! I haff followed him ever since ze Universal Title Tournament… I always wanted to be just like him. So my vife, Nurse Putrid, found me a perfect lab. I stole a coat from a doctor at ze hospital, so zat I could look ze part, you know?” This man really needs some professional help. With every word that this man speaks even more pity for him seeps through my conscience. “I haff my gadgets… I haff my experiments… It is just like I am ze real Doc C, jah?” I can only nod my head in agreement. This man has me in a really unfortunate position and it wouldn’t make any sense to disagree with him right now, especially with the mood that the guy is in. The doctor moves back from the wall once more, but this time he leans forward so that he’s looking directly at my face with his nose barely inches from mine. He looks into my eyes with sheer ruthless hatred, blood vessels in his eyes surfaced and making his eyes red with rage. He breathes out heavily blowing his disgusting breath into my face. “And zen I heard zat my hero had made it to ze final of ze GTT… After ze UTT I vill not let my idol fail once more, I vill make sure zat ze odds are in his favour zis time…” “By kidnapping me, so I don’t show up to the event.” I say interrupting the doctor in the middle of his speech. “Exactly,” the doctor replies calmly probably realising that his plan is at least bearing fruit, “and my plan ist vorking perfectly, don’t you tzink?” I’m not going to give this freak the privilege of an answer. Instead, I roll my eyes so that the doctor can see me. All it does is elicit a chuckle from him, who is amused by my unenthusiastic response. As if to further highlight the predicament that I’m in, the doctor claps his hands and once again the nurse enters the room, now known to me as Nurse Putrid. She walks slowly into the room with her eyes fixated on mine with a look of sheer frenzy on her face. I don’t know what she has planned for me, but I’m guessing that it’s not particularly pleasant. The huge woman walks right over to my chair and looks down at me, her face riddled with boils and marks, not a pretty sight by any means. I wonder what she wants with me… is she just here to guard me and feels like intimidating me a bit, or whether she’s been given free roam to torture me to her hearts content? Oh, well that’s that answered as she pulls out something from inside the band of her trousers. She bears a wicked smile as she holds the object out in front of her. It’s a very large bullwhip. She lets fly with a test crack of the whip and it makes a mightily loud noise on the concrete floor beside us. Breathing a silent chuckle to herself through her teeth so as to not let her cigarette drop from her mouth, she looks at me, the human punching bag, sitting before her readying for the first onslaught of her whip. Her eyes sparkle as if she’s found gold, reaches over to her lips with her free hand and pulls the cigarette from her mouth after taking a big drag and then dispelling of the smoke in a thick cloud aimed in my direction. “You shouldn’t smoke, ya know,” I inform the nurse breaking the silence, trying to hold back from coughing, “it’s bad for you.” She takes no notice of my words and instead looks to her whip, and then to me once more. With a deep, intimidating voice she savagely utters the words: “Let’s play…” -----=====|=====----- Ha, ha! Look at the weak British idiot, sitting there tied up as a helpless little victim. Aw, poor Davie, there’s nobody here to help you! Boo, hoo, hoo! I tighten my grip on the whip feeling the bumps on the leather handle digging into my hand, give the dumb Englishman a smile and then let fly, swinging my arm over with as much force as I can muster. The whip flies over and cracks directly on his right shoulder. “Aaaaahhhhhh!!!!” the pathetic wimp cries out. I thought you were a wrestler? I thought you were supposed to be used to pain? Hurst’s shoulder glows like a stoplight, its redness bright and spread all over his shoulder area. I pull back my arm and ready for another fling of my mighty bullwhip, I’m going to shred this guy to pieces. “Wait!” a whimper cries from the other side of the room. I turn my head to see the source of the noise. It’s that bitch that Hurst hangs around with. Aw, how sweet, she wants to help her poor defenceless little boyfriend. It makes me sick. “You got a problem, bitch?” I yell sternly at the interfering little slut. She doesn’t reply, instead she looks up to me with a concerned look. I’ve obviously scared her witless, so much for being the hero, huh? That’s it. She’s going to get some now. I walk across the room towards the insubordinate little slag, clench the handle extra tight and bear my arm back ready to launch it forward. “No!” cries Hurst from the other side of the room, “Hit me all you want, just don’t hurt Wendy!” Oh please, spare me the bother because I’m really not that merciful. Not like that lightweight excuse for an evil doctor in the other room. I reach back once more, ready to launch the tirade of lashings to Miss Sanchez… gritting my teeth ready to send a powerful strike her way, leaning forward and: “That’s enough!” Oh great. Our supreme leader has returned… -----=====|=====----- With his eyes following the Nurse’s every movement, Dave sits on his seat, a figure of sheer rage. He doesn’t want the nurse laying a finger on his girlfriend, and he’s incredibly agitated at the fact that he can’t move to do anything about it. Meanwhile though, in the darkest area of the abandoned hospital basement, somebody is completely unaware of what has been unfolding around him. He’s been hearing an awfully loud racket and is wondering what is going on outside. Sitting in the pocket of Exceptional Dave Hurst sits his plastic yellow duck, named Ducky. Ducky is quite content with sitting in the pocket for now, sure it’s dark, but it’s mighty cosy. What the duck doesn’t realise though, is that he is in some serious danger, of which he will shortly find out. “Mister Hurst…“ a foreign sounding voice says from outside of the pocket, a voice which Ducky is unfamiliar with. Curious as to what is happening outside, he listens carefully to what is being said. “There ist sometzing else that I tzink you should be avare of vith my current escapades. I haff plans, Mister Hurst. Big plans. Vone day, very soon, I vill take over ze entire world!” “You’re insane,” Dave replies unenthusiastically, he obviously doesn’t think the doctor is up to such a task. “There ist a fine line, Mister Hurst, betveen insanity and genius. You vill learn zis vone day. Now, observe my chemical warhead!” The duck sits snugly in the pocket, as concerned as he is about the chemical warhead, he doesn’t really know what’s going on anyway. Outside of the pocket, the doctor has pulled a cotton sheet off from over a contraption and revealed a huge rocket, lying on its side and taking up most of the room’s length. The doctor wanders across the rocket, to its base, and reveals a panel, he opens the panel and a raging fire is burning inside of it. “Zis ist a very important feature of zis chemical warhead, zis warhead uses very common chemicals, the kind of chemicals found by burning particular plastics. One of zese plastics you may be very familiar vith, but just haven’t realised… One of zese plastics ist ze same plastic used for a… Small… plastic… yellow… duck…” TO BE CONTINUED…
