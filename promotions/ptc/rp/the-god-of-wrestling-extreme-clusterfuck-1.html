After a few minutes of pause, and a few seconds of laughter from the fans La Envidia Mata finally begins to speak to the house show attendants”...] La Envidia Mata: The beast within my soul is still asleep ... Hell! It don't even matter, the fact of the matter is I’m the man around here, and your the, well, your the boy! I do not give a "flying hoot" as you call it, how tall you are, how small you are, how old you are, how young you are, Quite frankly I’m not interested in what you have to make of it ... have to do about it ... let alone have to say, about it. And you know you talk about LUCK, you say that the only reason I’m Main Event and your not is because of Luck? No, you see the only reason I’m Main Event and your not, is simple, because I’m La Envidia Mata and you’re the Yure! I’m the talent, and your the waste, I’m the Show, and your the Side-show. Yure, you know you have a real cheek to say that the words of which come out of my mouth are utter bullshit, because not only do I speak the truth but I go into detail about the truth, I tell the people what they want to hear.
