[detail withheld] With his pupils dancing back and forth faster than a seismograph in Northridge circa '94, the Illustrious Face-Eater finds it rather difficult to concentrate on one of the many "obstacles" that lay littered in front him. First there's the squad-cars that block any conventional means of escaping through the parking lot. Then there's the riflemen, perched on top of the balconies and low-scale roof-tops that surround him; each one of them waiting with their sights gracing the bright vinyl of Facey's mask. He also can't forget about the ghetto-bird that floats around over-head. Even if the Unfuckable somehow finds a way to make it out of this parking lot alive and with the luxury of a getaway vehicle, the idea of a helicopter chasing him is a little overwhelming to contend with. Escape appears rather unlikely at this angle. However, a lacking libido and the need to compensate for it clouds the Face-Eater's judgment just enough that he still feels that he is in control. After all, he has a Pro Wrestling Champion gripped by the neck with a SMG barrel pressed against his head. He also has a little black boy, imprisoned by his own fear in the hotel room positioned right behind him. Hostages mean leverage, and leverage means possibilities. In a world filled with possibilities, an Illustrious Face-Eater is a very dangerous thing. "PERK-UP THE FUCK-UP AND LISTEN TO MY DESPERATE CRY FOR ATTENTION OR I WILL LEAD-FUCK THIS HOBO'S BRAINS AND CREATE AN ORGASMIC SPLASH OF DEATH UPON ALL OF YOU!" The patrolmen, squatting behind the cover of their cars' extended doors, get a bit nervous as they steady their aim on the crazed madman. They have been trying to keep the situation as calm as they can, but when it comes to an over-zealous and grandiose hostage-taker, keeping the mood calm is actually a lot harder than one would anticipate. Then there's the matter of the hostage himself: Garbage Bag Johnny, still wondering what the fuck is going on. "Dude, this isn’t part of the plan! Granted that there is no plan, but still, I figure something like taking me hostage requires SOME mention---! OWW!" Facey silences the bitching by smacking him with the shaft of the Tec. "That’s because if I would have told you, you would have said ‘no’! NOW UNLESS YOU WANT TO DIE WITH URINE STAINS IN YOUR UNDERWEAR, YOU’LL SHUT YOUR FUCKING LIPS WHEN YOU’RE TALKING TO ME!" "You don’t want to do this!" One of the policeman tries calling out over the PA attached to his vehicle. He stands alongside another cop, higher-ranked, and it appears as if these two are in charge of the situation. "If you do this—it’s over!" "You’re right! IT IS FUCKING OVER! And unless I get a Jetpack, a Harry Potter Invisibility Cloak, six pounds of Everlasting Gobstoppers, a Bye into the GTT6 finals, and safe passage to Canada… I’LL KILL GARBAGE BAG JOHNNY!" Facey observes the lot of people in hopes to catch them squirm in worry over the Zero 2 Hero Champion, but apparently no one cares about the fate of a grizzly man with bad dental hygiene, no matter how famous he is (see: G.G. Allin). So he uses other scare-tactics. "AND I’LL KILL THAT LITTLE BLACK BOY, TOO!" "He’s clearly lost it!" One of the patrolmen screams as the entire squad begins to shift in fear of what the Face-Eater might do. "Son, hang on! We’re going to get you some help!" The Cop says, but through the mob of people Facey notices that the speaker is a little pre-occupied as he drops the microphone. He and his superior appear to have something else on their mind, causing them to argue with each other instead of allowing the one Cop to continue his exchange with the Face-Eater. This causes the Face to get a little jealous. ‘What the fuck is more important than me killing a black kid!!?’ he thinks to himself. "HELP?! I DON’T NEED FUCKING HELP! I NEED A FUCKING JETPACK TO GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE!!! NOW AM I GOING TO PUT A FEW NEW HOLES IN THIS MAN’S HEAD TO FIT MY PENIS INTO? OR ARE WE GOING TO NEGOTIATE!?" The Cop turns from arguing with his boss and says into the mic. "We’re working on it! Please, wait for a moment!" He then drops the microphone, so the Face-Eater cannot hear the exchange, and turns back to his boss. "I can handle this—trust me!" But the Captain is not as faithful as his negotiator. "We can’t have any fuck-ups with this, Sabian. That kid dies, we’ve got a huge fucking scandal on our hands." "There won’t be a fuck-up, sir—PLEASE, let me handle this!" "I’m sorry." He says, shaking his head with crossed arms. "But I’m pulling you." His shoulders, his neck, his brow: they all drop. A bitter taste seeps in with the saliva in his mouth, causing him spit out. "Who’s going to take my place, huh? Who knows how to deal with a psychopath like this?" "Don’t worry. She’s coming." The Captain turns back to the situation whilst lighting a cigarette. At this point in time, he’d take any break and short-cut he can to calm his nerves even if only for a single second. "I’M GETTING FUCKING TIRED OF YOUR LITTLE POW-WOW BACK THERE. YOU GUYS LIKE TELLING SECRETS?!? YOU LIKE PLOTTING MY DEMISE SO THAT YOU CAN THROW ME IN ALCATRAZ LIKE KEVIN BACON IN THAT MOVIE WHERE HE STABS PEOPLE IN THE THROAT WITH A SPOON?!? YOU WANT TO GET STABBED IN THE THROAT WITH A SPOON!?!" The Captain turns to the Cop and asks him, "What the fuck is he talking about?" but the Cop just shrugs his shoulders before picking the microphone back up. "Alright, look man. We’ve got something coming for ya’--." "IS IT POUNDS OF GOBSTOPPERS?! BECAUSE I WAS LYING ABOUT THAT, YOU CAN MAKE THEM SOUR-PUNCH STRAWS INSTEAD!" "Actually, we’re not getting any of that. But don’t worry, pal, someone’s coming who’s going to help you out." Facey’s jaw drops. NO GOBSTOPPERS? NO PUNCH STRAWS?! NO CANDY AT ALL?! The impeding absence of sugar strikes a panic with his current state, and his eyes begin darting around faster than a seismograph in, uh… the Kashmir Earthquake of Pakistan. He glances at the cops again, both on the ground and on the rooftops. He looks the ghetto-bird, circling around in hopes of some high-speed getaway action. He turns to look back at Tyrone Almasy, peering through the glass window of the hotel room with complete and utter disbelief etched in his expression. He examines Garbage Bag Johnny before him, who is mumbling something about trust, friendship, betrayal, and the afterlife. Not surprising, his once-dry pale khaki pants are now completely dark in the crotch and inside legs from saturation. And then, Facey turns to the crowd. He sees, amidst the thick of it all, Nova. WITH A BIG BAG OF POT. Facey’s knees immediately go weak as his eyes fall upon the one substance he loves more than candy. Nova is just finishing rolling a spliff as he goes on examining this fucked-up situation that transpires before him. He has a grin on his face that says "damn, I’m stoned and this is pretty amusing." It’s a look that Facey knows all-too-well, and it causes yet another viable panic attack within the Face-Eater. Nova licks the gum of the paper and seals his joint, a perfectly rolled cylinder, before he sticks one end in his mouth and begins lighting. One of the policemen turns from their post behind their door, noticing the influx of marijuana smoke in his vicinity, and immediately gets a bit perturbed. "YOU CAN’T BE SMOKING THAT!" Nova takes a puff and blows it in the officer’s face, shrugs, and simply says "So?" He takes another drag, this time blowing the smoke in the air above his head, then looks to him and says "Do you not have more important crimes to be taking care of right now?" The policemen curls a lip in disgust then turns back towards the hostage scene, helpless to make such a petty arrest in the face of such dire situations. Nova, however, isn’t surprised at all. Well duh, he is a fucking bad ass. But the Face-Eater can’t help but watch longingly. How he wishes he could be blowing smoke in a helpless police officer’s face (excluding the fact that if it weren’t for this hostage situation, that would not be able to happen). He thinks about how he could be smoking that joint with Nova. The Face-Eater slowly closes his eyes and concentrates on his breathing, taking the necessary steps to battle the nausea that struck him just moments earlier. He thinks about taking Mescaline then going cow-tipping with Garbage Bag Johnny. He feels a spasm in a muscle in his next thigh, though he never learned enough about the body to tell you which one it is. As his eyes slowly flutter open, so does the intensity of the spasm until it becomes a full blown shake. He thinks about kicking "the New Shit" Bobino in the balls. With his breathing so heavy, each exhale from Facey unto Garbage Bag Johnny's shoulder gets stickier. Being wet all over his body would normally annoy the Transatlantic Champion, but in this particular situation he isn't in any position to feel annoyed. He feels Facey's grip around his neck begin to wrench and his own gut begins to tighten in fear. "Uhmmmm can I please maybe get a blow-dryer?" He thinks about that first round match-up with Seymour Almasy. Instantly, the Face-Eater's eyes bolt open and he extends his arm, pointing the gun away from Johnny's head and instead towards the Squadron of police. They all gasp for a moment as they steady their guns; the negotiator and his superior both unsure of how to react. All they had to do was buy some time, which apparently they're incapable of doing. But who can be expected to make magic out of a shit-heap, especially when the last thing that the Face-Eater needs at this point in his venture is time? "ALRIGHT. ALRIGHT. ALL FUCKING RIGHT! I'VE HAD ENOUGH DICKING A-FUCKING-ROUND! WHERE THE FUCK IS MY JETPACK!?" He's too busy overlooking the chaos to take notice to that car spinning out of control as it takes a turn down the street that aligns the hotel. Instead, the Face watches as the cops ignore the car as well, all of them concerned with what he may do next. They are too concerned with each other to notice this outside party entering the fray. "YOU THINK THIS IS A GAME?! YOU THINK I'M FUCKING PLAYING!? ALRIGHT, TIME TO PROVE I'M AS FUCKING SERIOUS AS A HERPE ON A MORMON TABERNACLE CHOIR BOY'S UPPER LIP!" As Facey shoves GBJ's limp figure in front of his own, shoving the barrel of the Tec into the back of his head, the entire police squad murmurs. However the movement of GBJ allows Facey to get a clear view of the crowd before him, especially at the petite brunette that shoves her way past a few of the barrier-creating policemen that stand towards the front. "Adam, what the HELL are you doing?" screams Ellis Nash from behind the restraints of two officers. "You can't be here, miss, this is a dangerous--." "That's my fucking boyfriend!" Even though they clamp her wrists and her waist and try to pull her away, Ellis struggles as Facey just stares speechless at her. The Captain pulls away from the PA system and approaches Ellis and the other officers, ordering them off of her and pointing at the Gold Badge that's hooked to the front of her belt. "It's all right, she's P.D.! I called her up here to talk some sense into this guy." The policemen immediately release her and allow her to recompose herself. While fixing her hair she approaches Adam as closely as she can without passing the barrier of armed officers. Turning away from the scene for a moment, the Captain notices his negotiating cop receiving something on the transmitter, and judging by the look on his face upon hearing it the Captain can't help but deduct that it's important. He quickly runs back over and grips the walkie-talkie out of his officer's hand. "What have you got for me?" "What is this for, Adam?" Ellis asks. The Face-Eater can't muster an answer, instead he just examines the former Frontier Champion of AWC. It has been so long since they last spoken, without even so much as an attempt on his end to establish contact with her. To see her now, and for her to see him, he knows it's painful. He knows that it would have been so much easier for the two to just have forgotten about each other. "Ellis." The Face begins with a stammer. "I don't—." "Don't know what the fuck you're doing? I noticed." Her face becomes flushed with a red tone. It doesn't take a keen observer to notice that she's fighting the tears pent up behind her eyes. Facey's grip on the gun loosens and his arm lowers; no longer concerning a certain aspect of this situation. "I've got a clear shot, sir." "I don't know what is going on with you and me anymore, not to say that I ever did, but you and I both know that this isn't going to work. What are you doing? Trying to kill your best friend? To achieve what end?" Words that strike him with fierce intensity, Facey's posture lowers as he searches desperately for an answer. "I want people to take me seriously." "Well, why the fuck haven't you taken it yet?!" "Take you seriously? Adam, you're a professional wrestler! You piss people off and talk in caps lock and say brash things that no one in their right mind would say if they had even the slightest percent of a functioning brain. You go into hiding for months then return to beat the shit out of the single person I've grown to have relationship with, other than you, and then you disappear again. YOU DO NOT KIDNAP CHILDREN AND HOLD YOUR BEST FRIEND AS A HOSTAGE." "I'm…" The gun in his hand begins shaking, almost raising the question of Facey having tics. "I'm--." "Depressed? Scared? BORED? What is it? Are you having a mid-life crisis a few decades early or something? Or are you really stupid enough to think that something like this would actually be a good idea--?" "I DON'T FUCKING KNOW WHAT I AM!" "Is that a confirmation, sir?" "On second thought, no, the broad might be breaking him…" Ellis folds her arms as if she's not believing a word that's coming out of Adam's mouth. "So you're going to kill people and make a big spectacle out of it? That's not exactly great for your career. Then again, neither is blowing yourself up on PayPerView. Then again, neither is signing up for PTC Events after said explosion." Face-Eater glances around once again. He looks to Tyrone, to Nova, to GBJ and to Ellis. For the life of him, he cannot come up with his next course of action. For the first time in his life, the Face-Eater doesn't know what he should do. "Meh, nevermind. This doesn't look like it's going anywhere… He still in your sights?" "Yes, sir." Ellis notices as the ground troops begin lowering their arms and taking a few steps, a few of them glancing upwards and behind their own position towards the gunmen on the roof. Then she trails their view to notice those gunmen, all of them with their sights pointed towards the Face-Eater. She specifically notices one of them speaking through a communications device. Using that "deductive reasoning-thingy" her C.O.s taught her in Cop Academy, her eyes trail over towards the Captain, standing in the back, ready to issue what could be his final command into the transmitter he holds to his mouth. "Alright. Take the sh--." "Don't take the shot!" Ellis screams, halting the Captain from issuing the command. The anxious rifleman perched atop a balcony speaks hastily into his com. "Sir? Should I take it?" The Captain doesn't answer. Ellis doesn't leave him the time to. She rips the walkie-talkie out of his hand then immediately runs back to Facey. "Listen, Adam, if you don't end this soon they're going to fucking kill you! GIVE IT UP, PLEASE." Now his entire body is trembling. His eyes? They're dancing back faster than a seismograph… you fucking get it now. "Please Adam, for me. Give it up." With tearful eyes, the Face-Eater covers his face and the Tec falls out of his grip and to the ground. Bursting into a painful sob, Facey falls to his knees then hunches over on the ground, creating an extremely pathetic scene that no man ever should partake in. GBJ, suddenly free from his captor's grip, breaks from where he stood through the crowd of police and right to Nova, where he rips the joint from the Rising Star's hand and shoves it in his own mouth. "Yeah. Go right ahead, dude." Nova rolls his eyes. "Dude, I don't know if you were watching, but Facey JUST HAD A GUN TO MY HEAD. I STILL HAVEN'T STOPPED PISSING MY PANTS, SO PLEASE, GIVE ME A FUCKING BREAK." The policemen all begin to surround Facey, most ready to beat the fuck out of him for a few moments before taking him in (after all, they ARE cops) before Ellis begins to fester them off. She approaches her fallen boyfriend, a man she hasn't even talked to in four months, and cradles him in her arms, caressing his head and trying to fend off his tears. "Mr. Dick, you're under arrest for--" "I think he gets it, Scout." Ellis says, looking up from her seat with Facey on the ground. "I've got to read him his rights." The officer insists. With a free arm, she reaches towards the back of her belt and withdraws a set of handcuffs. She calmly clamps them around the wrists of the Face-Eater with his hands behind his back and he doesn't even struggle. No, the Face-Eater can't even muster an elbow into an officer's face to try and escape. All he can do is cry. And the funniest part of it is, he doesn't even know why. Ellis rolls her eyes at the officer, and begins her half-assed rendition of the Miranda Rights. "You're under arrest, Adam. You have the right to remain silent, anything you say will be used against you in court and you know the rest. You have the right to an attorney, but you can afford an expensive one so what's the use there? We're done." Guiding him through the sea of cops, they approach the Captain. Everyone who watches them has a bit of raised eyebrows, unsure exactly of what to do next. "Thanks for calling me; we've been looking for him for a while. I think I can handle him from here." "We'll send an escort vehicle--." "That's not necessary, Captain. Look at him—he's broken. I'll be fine taking him by myself. But thanks." "Well, if you insis--." Ellis continues pushing Adam past the cops and towards her car, making a quick effort to get across the parking lot. She turns around real quick and notices that everyone is still watching her. Each of them are so concerned with their stupid jobs that they no longer consider Ellis Nash driving down the street with this Criminal sitting in the back of her seat. "No, I was glad to see you go at the end of it all. Things have been better since you left." Offended, the Face-Eater twists around on his heels and begins glaring at Jack. "BETTER? Pah! You've been in the slump from hell since I've gone on to bigger and better things… and you know it. When I was around, you were on the top of your game! You know why? Because I dragged you UP there like your mother fucking taskmaster! And now you have the nerve to be disrespectful to me after I offer a few kind words? What kind of a friend is that?!" "Don't call me friend." The intensity of these words go unmatched by any others the Bull speaks. "Jack, that hurts, because I'm really the closest thing to a friend you'll ever know and you cannot deny it. Lucky for you, I'll be around to help you in your time of need; once again." As Jack cocks his eyes in confusion, Facey takes joy in the fact that he's remained one step ahead of his rival once again. "You mean you haven't heard? They drew second round brackets for GTT yesterday and as fate would have it, you've got to make up for your Bye. Because once again, it's you and I." Jack's eyes go wide as he speaks. "One of us will advance, one of us will go home. Don't expect it to be you moving on, Jack, but then again, I wouldn't want you do lose all hope." With a serpent's smile, he changes his tone while saying. "That's not what true friends do." "You're lying." Jack replies, regaining that aura of confidence that once remained thick. "I wish I were. Was hoping for some real competition, like that doorman who's never wrestled before, but I guess you'll have to do. Anyways, I must be off. Assholes to examine and all that stuff." Adam begins his path into the hospital before stopping a single step in to ask. "Don't you think you've got something to be doing too, Jack? Like baby making?" The emphasis of Adam's words imply much more than he is letting on, and Jack swings unsuccessfully for his face. Quickly dodging, he side-steps closer to Jessica and leans in, smelling the strands of her hair that fall loose while touching her lightly on the back. "So good to see you too, Jess. I have missed you. But it looks like we'll be seeing each other a lot sooner than we thought…" With that, Adam leaves and he can't help but feel more than satisfied with the outcome. Jessica O'Grady stays frozen for a moment, whether it is fear or intimidation or some other reaction Adam is unsure of. Though, he is sure that he made an impression; one that wouldn't be forgotten any time soon. If tonight turned out this good, he thinks, I can't wait to see how the rest of the week goes. He walks quickly into the hospital, passing many patients and nurses en route to his destination, then approaches the reception's desk who eagerly asks how she can assist him.
