The Carnival has just begun!

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 Shaman cracks his neck from side to side as he is speaking on the phone, Very angry and in deep conversation. He is rather disturbed at the moment and the yelling gets louder. “What the hell do you mean this is a minor detail?” Shaman shakes his head and rubs his temple. “You told me when you were doing this way back when I joined the WWA that this would be no problem. So here’s the correct information so you idiots can get it right! Listen up because I am only going to say this once. I was born in the Mojave Desert in 1974. Yes that’s in New Mexico stupid.” Rubbing his temples a vein in his forehead pops up obviously from his frustrations. “How do they find you idiots?” Shaman shuffles the phone from one ear to the other. “I don’t really care just get the facts straight guys. You’re playing with my heritage and I don’t..!?! Matt W. did what? He authorized the permanent info? No you fix it now. I will speak with Harrington about it. If Warr has problems contact me! “ Listening as he squeezes the phone a bit. “Look I really don’t give a damn. Do it or your job is going to be in trouble for misrepresentation. Do your job and get it right ok. Fine this conversation is done.” Shaman looks at a stack of papers in front of him. Information he needs on all of his opponents. . He rifles through each one looking quickly for Joey Vendetti. Opening the folder he grins and raises his eyes. “Vendetti you’re an idiot. Telling everyone how I have named myself. Well if you ever did some real research rather than stuff your face with Linguini you might understand a few facts. Number one Native American Bad Ass was a phrase coined and stuck by an announcer named Terry Valentine. Just rest assured your ass will be kicked up one side of the cage and down the other. Your little backyard mafia have no power in the elimination chamber. You’re going to have to contend with four other men, not just myself. Let me give you some advice for a second!” Standing Shaman thumbs the side of his nose as he walks outside and walks over to two cars. One is a 96 Pontiac Sunfire and the one next to it a Mercedes Benz. He looks at both and then chuckles a bit and then looks at a Cadillac and Lincoln Town Car. “You own and drive a 96 Pontiac Sunfire. You’re a pretender. To be even a noteworthy Italian Mobster nonetheless a thug you need to drive a Cadillac or a Lincoln Town car. The higher ups drive a Mercedes Benz and BMW’s you are driving a Pontiac Sunfire. To tell you the truth who is the cheesy one here? After all where do you get off calling the kettle black? You see Vendetti your rants fell on deaf ears. Nobody gives a damn about who you are and how bad you believe yourself to be. Your actions better speak volumes in the ring. If they don’t your going to be the one pushing up daisies! I hope we are in full understanding Capice?” Shaman climbs up onto the Pontiac Sunfire and smiles as he sits in the roof. “ Vendetti maybe this will be just what you will be doing underneath me motionless and prone. Your annoyance will come full circle in the elimination chamber. You will know why I am a very dangerous man. Bring your Spaghetti and Meatballza Italy boy, because when all hell breaks loose you’re going to be on the receiving end of a big stick!” Shaman turns his head looking about and hops down off the Pontiac Sunfire laughing. He turns his gaze towards the hills nearby and at the clouds above them. “Now onto my next opponent, Sean McClancy the Irish stooge. The man who feels insulting me and trying to make me look inferior makes him look intelligent and educated. Well reality check time Sean ol’ boy. You see the reason I grew up outside Death Valley was simple I was raised on a reservation when my mother remarried when I was very young. Then again you wouldn’t know anything about that. I was born in the Mojave Desert in New Mexico. The WWA idiots forgot to make sure my info was correct. Ever heard of the Shinome? No from across the pond we like to call the Atlantic I figure you wouldn’t have. Its not often I get to see a man grasping at so many straws of the past that he looks worse than some of the veterans who refuse to retire. No Sean I won’t go away quietly. You sparked the fire; I am merely pouring gasoline on it. The danger is simply this Sean. You’re shooting for gold. I want to take it from everyone in this match. Will you like what I have to offer in the ring? I don’t care if you do or not. You’re going to get it anyways. It is going to be given in spades." Suddenly lashing out he drives his fist through the windshield of the Mercedes. “Play with me Sean I dare you. Only thing you will get from it is a world of hurt. Yeah I am a big pissed off man. What are you going to do about it? Go cry to your momma? No I won’t let you get to far Sean. Your little world of being better than everyone has come to an end. Wake up and smell the Irish Rose jack ass. I am Shaman not some witch doctor or Magic man. I am Shaman a man who has pride in who he is and what he believes. I am Shaman the man who is going to crush your future for the Extreme Title. Welcome to my damn game boy!” Watching the sun cast its bright haze across the sky as it begins to slowly settle down behind the clouds. He ponders and then turns to look at the cars behind himself. “Kyo Kushinaga a man who likes the mystery of his opponents. I like to know my opponents Kyo the fun is even better when you know who they are. Why do I say these things you begin to think? Its quite simple really. Knowing your opponent causes them to underestimate you. Look at the actions of Sean and Joey. Can you not realize my mere presence is causing them to get into an uproar of verbal attacks? I respect you have chosen to stay silent. You are much like me in some ways willing to allow actions to speak louder than words. You are intriguing Kyo, But very boring as to your prophetic ways. Kyo go preech your monk sermons to those who will listen. I am the man your people have called Gaijin. Do you truly think me to be intimidated as to your thoughts? Mystery is for children and old women. I am going to be straight in your face!” Grinning he turns and begins to walk away speaking. “As for Ice the man has gone as silent as his name sake. The tides are finally turning in the center rides the one man you all will come to fear. That man is Shaman. Fear is never boring.” Stopping he stares at the darkening sky. “Cry without tears.” Closing his eyes he whispers. “See without sight.” Opening his eyes once again he looks about. “Night without day.” Cracking his neck from side to side he then turns his gaze to the horizon. “Everyday is black as the night.” Stepping forward as each step is placed slowly and carefully upon the ground. “Fly without wings.” His eyes watch birds upon the ground as he then looks at his hands. “Feel without touch.” Staring at his right hand he sneers. “Touch without feel.” Staring across the grassy hillside looking at a graveyard he whispers once again. “Life without death, World without end, Sleep without dreams and nothing is quite what it seems.” Turning he gazes up at the darkening skies again. “I want something more than you'll know, Follow myself, and go where you can never go and I see though my eyes are closed.” Looking at the cars that he had left behind he ponders. “I want to see from within.” He begins his steady walk again away from this scene he had begun to paint. “I've been waiting here to be your guide, so come reveal the secrets that you keep inside, No one leaves till the night is done, and the carnival has just begun!” Disappearing beneath the blackening sky a thunderous roar of wind and rain falls as nothing is seen as the camera fades to black.
