Date: 2006-11-18 Yori came to a realization. Walking naked around Antarctica, with only the hollowed out severed head of a sex robot to cover his naughty bits, was not the best plan in the world. That, and fire is really, really hard to make. He bent over a pile of frozen dirt and shaved pubic hair, trying to focus the sun’s rays with a parabolic piece of ice, like he had seen one hungover Sunday morning on a marathon of "Mythbusters." Yori liked "Mythbusters." Of course what he really liked was the saucy redhead on the build team. He also had no idea what in the world a parabola actually looked like, so just used a vaguely concave chunk of ice. He could only hope that the after market hair dye he used on his genitals was highly flammable. A couple hours in, and he couldn’t feel anything below his Prince Albert piercing, and still no sign of even the faintest sparks. This could well be the end of Yori Yakamo, jr, Yori thought to himself. Yori then wondered if he was starting to lose his grip on reality, narrating his own life like some 30’s spy serial in a middling pulp magazine. This was quickly confirmed by the appearance of The Giant Green Rabbit (who may or may not be God) "Rabbit! What are you doing here." "I always show up when you take the green Quaaludes. We’ve been over this." "I don’t remember taking any Quaaludes." "Well, you did lick that green moss under the cold, Antarctic earth for sustenance. I guess it’s close enough." "Hmmm, so none of this is real. I’m not naked and about to die of hypothermia." "No, you’re naked." "Oh. Hey, can you make me some fire." "Well, you are doing a pretty good job, old boy." "What are you talking about." "Oh, you’ll see in about three seconds." Three seconds later, Yori was snapped out of his green moss-induced hallucinations and into the realization that he, indeed, was on fire. "AIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEEE!" Fortunately ROBOYORI’s built in sprinkler system still worked, even without a body, and Yori was able to douse the flames before permanent damage was done. He was still underneath the Earth’s crust, roaming the explosion-tinged wasteland of the former Dinosaur-Man City. He was in a bit of a pickle, and needed some advice. Back to the green moss. Tasty. "Rabbit?" "Yes, my son." "So….how do I get out of this underground Antarctic cave and back to GTT6?" "Don’t you have the YOR-ABBIT?" "Well, I had the YOR-ABBITO. But I doubt it’s cut rate Central American manufacturing could withstand the explosion of a giant electromagnet." "Hmmm, that would be a bit of a problem." "Well, theoretically you could use the electromagnet to realign Earth’s magnetic poles, causing huge amounts of continental drift. That might get you in the neighborhood of the GTT6. Or the GTT6 in the neighborhood of you. All you’ll need is a superheated chamber, a giant electromagnet, and a degree in electrical engineering. Or a writer for a Sci-Fi Original Picture. Either one should work. "Hmmm, how about the severed head of the YORobot, some green moss, and my sexy, sexy brain." "I think you are out of luck. Unless you have a degree in electrical engineering that I was unaware of." "Well, I did win an AV Entertainment Award for Sexiness on the Orient Express. That should count for something." "Not a train engineer, Yori. And no, that doesn’t count. Though it was one of your better movies. I liked the twist ending, where it turns out everyone screwed her." "Way to spoil the movie, Rabbit." "Sorry. To make it up to you, I’ll give you a way out of here." "Really?" "Sure. Whatever." "Hmm, this is a little deus ex machina, isn’t it." "Am I not suitably enough a Bacchanalian figure for you." "No, you’re pretty Bacchanalian." "Good, cause there is gonna be more where that came from." Yori awoke with a start, and saw a Dinosaur-Man standing over him. He was kind of blurry. "What up, my reptilian brother?" "Listen to me fool. You have to act quickly. Only you can save Mike Randalls." "Who?" "Mike Randalls. ‘The Wolf’ Mike Randalls. Got bit by a viper man, sent to the underworld. It happened like twenty minutes ago." "Oh yeah. But he’s dead." "Your powers of deduction are astounding, monkey boy." "Who are you anyway?" "I am Igni." "Oh, you are that Dinosaur-Man gladiator who Randalls killed to earn his freedom after his capture but the Dinosaur-Man overlords in the 1960s." "You can remember that obscure bit of information, but had to be reminded who Mike Randalls was?" "For expository purposes, yes. And anyway, aren’t you supposed to be dead?" "I am quite dead. Randalls stabbed me in the throat with a butter knife." "Yeah, that sounds like him." "But it was not Randalls’ time to die. You threw off the balance of the cosmos with your unfortunate use of the time-traveling dildo. Now you must make amends." "I’ll do whatever it takes to save Randalls." "Good, then follow me." A swirling, shadowy portal opened up and Igni was slowly sucked in, it was vaguely hypnotic. If Yori didn’t know any better, he’d swear he was on drugs. Yori walked into the portal and felt his body slowly disintegrate and reconstitute itself in some dank netherworld. As he followed the ethereal Igni across a stone-tiled floor, he felt a growing unease. "I don’t like this place, Igni." "Nor should you, this is the hall of lost souls." "What’s in it?" "Old files, mostly." They stopped at the edge of a pristine underground river. "Here, I can take you no further. Only death can take you the rest of the way." A spectral figure emerged from the water, standing astern a decrepit looking gondola. His skull-like face had the eyes of a snake, and packs of horseflies flitted about his visage. "Hmmm, I was kinda hoping he would look like Maria Casares." "Keep it down. He’s a little sensitive about his appearance. Good luck, Yori Yakamo, jr. May the fates be with you." "Peace out, Igni." Yori boarded the creepy ass boat of death and took a seat. The boat began to move upstream by unknown and unseen devices. "So, being death, what’s that like?" "….." "Not much for small talk. Okay, I can respect that. You got a Sex Toys Monthly back here. Or People … or… something." "…." "Okay then." The journey, brief though it was, passed in silence, and finally Yori found himself at the foot of a great pyramid. Along the top sat three masked figures. The first one spoke in a thunderous baritone. "Step forward Yori Yakamo, jr." "Sure thing. Just one question, how much should I tip the boat driver?" "A 15-20% gratuity is customary." "Right." Yori did a quick assessment of his financial situation. "It seems my wallet was blown off with my pants in the explosion." "It’s okay, Yori. Death, you may go now." Death spat at Yori’s feet and then began to float off in his boat. "He’s a bit of a sourpuss." "We’d like to fire him, but it’s a Union job." "I gotcha." "Now, state your business with The Lord of The Underworld." "I have come to reclaim the soul of Mike Randalls." There was some shocked murmuring. The second one spoke, in a distinctly androgynous voice. "Randalls, what could you possibly want with him?" "Well, he’s my friend. And I think he knows how to build a fire without lighting his genitals aflame. That would be really helpful. Plus, without him, there is no one to help me fight the Terminating Dildos from the Future." "I see. Well, you must prove yourself worthy to return to the world of the living with one of our souls." "Well, I can build you a mean dildo." The three whispered back and forth. The third spoke, in rich golden tones. "You can’t like, play the lute or something." "A lute? Hmmmm, no….no, I can’t. Maybe one of my Thai Hookers can. But I’d have to look into it." The second chimed in, "How about some nice poetry? We like poetry." "Honestly, all I can do is pretty much build sex toys." The Lords of the Underworld conversed some more. The first let out a hearty sigh. "Very well, proceed." A stone hearth materialized in front of Yori. At its base lay silicon, glass, and various molds. "Awesome. I need about forty five minutes, and some sort of harlot." They spoke now, in unison. "It shall be done." Yori never worked that hard on a dildo in his life. He slaved over the lathe, shaping the perfect model phallus. It was like Michelangelo’s David, if David were endowed with an immense penis. The harlot seemed sufficiently impressed with its functionality, and with its double pronged vibrating forks for anal and clitoral stimulation. It could be removed from, or attached to a strap-on with the touch of a button. And it folded for easy storage. Truly, it was the King of Kin…err, dildos. But would the Lords of the Underworld be suitably impressed with his Hephaestian powers of sex toy craftsmanship. He would have his answer, as the Lords manifested themselves before him once more. "Present your creation, Yori Yakamo, jr." "I call it, the ‘Penis de Milo’" "Hmmm, not the most original name." "Hey, I only had forty-five minutes, and no input from the Thai Hooker marketing team." "Fair enough. We will *ahem* try this out, and return with our answer shortly." And with that, the Lords disappeared. The wait was excruciating. Luckily the harlot had a deck of cards to pass the time. She hustled Yori at Gin Rummy to the tune of twenty pence (whatever the heck that is, Yori thought) before the Lords of the Underworld returned, their robes disheveled and breathing more heavily than before. "Yori….you have passed our test. This dildo is unlike any we have seen before. Truly, you are the greatest sex toy craftsman in the world. We release Randalls to you, on the condition that you do not look back at him as you leave the Underworld. If you do, he will be sucked back down to the depths and lost forever." "That’s kind of a stupid condition." "The Lords of the Underworld have spoken!" With that, the Lords, the pyramid, and the harlot vanished from in front of Yori’s eyes. Yori felt a presence materialize behind him. "RANDALLS?!" Yori started to turn around and was slapped to the ground before he could lay eyes on his friend. "Right, right. Sorry bout that." The gondola strolled back up the river and Randalls and Yori boarded. The boat traveled upstream as Yori was filled with a renewed sense of purpose. Everything would be okay. As he passed through the Hall of Lost Souls though, he could no longer resist the urge to embrace his friend. He turned around to see…. "AHHHH! YOU FOOL!" "Tony Randall?" "Yeah, it[s me. "Where's Randalls?" "Well you know how it is, Yori. The Lords of the Underworld needed Ol’ Mike to run a few errands for them before sending him back. So they sent me in his place. Why did you turn around anyway?" "Well, I just figured there would be a pack of lesbians, waiting to tear me apart. I figured, that would be pretty awesome." "Actually, while the Maenads were from the isle of Lesbos, they were not, necessarily, lesbians." "Oh well." "So what zany adventures do you have in store for us, the new odd couple?" "Well, first I am going to try and build a fire. Then, craft a NEW TIME TRAVELLING DILDO to prevent the future enslavement of mankind by my fully self-aware sex toys, defeat Apeman Hitler, and win the GTT6." "That sounds just keen to me old sport." "Oh man, I already hate you, Tony Randall." TO BE CONTINUED
