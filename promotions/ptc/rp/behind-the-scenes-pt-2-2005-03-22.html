Behind the Scenes PT.2

22 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-22 (PT. 2) *Be very circumspect in the choice of thy company. In the society of thine equals thou shalt enjoy more pleasure; in the society of thy superiors thou shalt find more profit. To be the best in the company is the way to grow worse. Francis Quarles (1592 - 1644)* ~ All that witness my victories and all of my defeats have wondered what it has been to drive me so far. Why do I even bother with it, why haven’t I cracked under the pressure of this business? The answer is so simple; it is my dream and destiny to be where I am today. It is what fuels me and what pushes me to achieve the greatness that I seek. No matter if they are booing me our chanting my name, which is the fuel I need to keep going on with the fight! And I will fight until they all know why I am here and why they call me………The Prophet! ~ 12-14-94 Time: 3:54 PM Location: Culver City/ Los Angeles.CA {John Adams has been working on the house for quite some time know and today is the day. After the three days of hell he had to endure without a lot of food or sleep he has finally reached the day where he was going to apply to Wrestling School. He has finally got the broken sink inside the kitchen fixed, which is when the witch he has come to known as Kathy walks in with such a smug look on her face that it was obvious that she was displeased with his performance.} ~ Pain and suffering…….. ~ Kathy: So what in the hell did you do all day? John Adams: Well I just finished this sink, and did all the paint touchups on the house. Also I finished tiling the bathroom and installed the new carpet…… Kathy (interrupts.): Is that all? You are the laziest person I have ever met! All you do is eat….eat….EAT! John Adams: How can you say that? I have done a lot for you and all I get from you is negativity and insults! {John Adams has always tried to be a calm man even with all of the crap that has gone on in his life from his father to even now. But with her constant pushing and her constant nagging something inside of him just snapped. As if a hungry caged tiger that was just released from his cage upon a crowd of people, John Adams whole expression just changed.} ~ The Beast is set free to feed upon the weak…… ~ John Adams: Who in the hell are you to judge what type of man I am Kathy! I do a lot around here and all you do is complain! I don’t need this crap from you!!! I quit Kathy!! {As he towers over Kathy realizes for the first time how he was actually intimidating someone….she was afraid, it was so clear and obvious as she started to shake and slowly back away from Adams. For the first time in Adams life he felt a type of power…..no not over women but more so bringing out the fear in people man or woman. He fed off of it as it comforted his already tortured soul. With this new found power he finally had found that one thing he was always been wanting……peace. As he stared into Kathy, practically looking into her blacken soul he found out why she acted the way she did. All she really wanted in life was someone to boss around, all she desired was to be in control and she was losing her control over Adams.} ~ Fueled by the desire to consume people’s souls…… ~ Kathy (on her knees begging.): What do you want from me? I’m only human! I pay you and this is how you treat me? I give you a place to stay and this is how you repay me? John Adams: That’s it; I am not going to be put through this any more!! I’m out and you can’t stop me Kathy! {John Adams storms out of the house with Kathy not far behind screaming at the heavens above for John Adams to stop. But Adams would not listen to it……how could he? She was trying to keep him from his dreams and ambitions. And there was nothing in heaven or earth that was going to keep Adams from completing what he has dubbed as his destiny. He finally got to the Wrestling School about three hours worth of walking later, but was it too late? As he went inside the school he could see an arena setup in the back where Wrestlers were practicing there suplexes, irish whips, and most important there falls. He could also see the teachers and trainers being attentive to there students witch pleased Adams very much. He headed up to the front desk which a cute blonde was there answering phone calls.} ~ And cursed to walk as a creation of the damned……. ~ John Adams: Hi, I have an appointment with a Mr. Trent about getting into the school. The Blonde: Name please….. John Adams: My name is John Adams they said that they have a spot for me here. The Blonde: Well Mr. Adams, I regret to inform you that we did have a spot for you here…… John Adams (with a confused look on his face.): What do you mean? The Blonde: Well Mr. Adams it means that we expected you here at a certain time witch was over an hour ago. And you are now replaced with someone who wants it more than you do because of it. {John Adams couldn’t believe his luck…..first he lost his job and home in Culver City, and now his dreams and hopes have been crushed. What was he supposed to do know? Where was he supposed to go? He couldn’t go back to Kathy’s…she was nuts. So the unfortunate outcome that was left for Adams was that now he was homeless. Will he get out of this hole of his?} ~ Nobody will stop him as he unleashes his unholy plan! ~ * Nothing feebler than a man does the earth raise up, of all the things which breathe and move on the earth, for he believes that he will never suffer evil in the future, as long as the gods give him success and he flourishes in his strength; but when the blessed gods bring sorrows too to pass, even these he bears, against his will, with steadfast spirit, for the thoughts of earthly men are like the day which the father of gods and men brings upon them. Homer (800 BC - 700 BC), The Odyssey* *Lost in a sea of no hope, Slowly drowning in my self pity is the only way I can cope, While under the condemn waters of what is my ever growing hate, I try to swim back to the world that has thrown me in here but I find out that it is too late, No where to go, Down is the only place I know, The farther I go being dragged by the hellish demons of hate and sorrow, Oh dear lord all I can hope for is a better tomorrow. ~ John “The Prophet” Adams* ~ To be continued. ~
