IG40 Roleplay Thread

25 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the living room of Chris Storm’s Manhattan condo, the team of Banned & Exiled~! assembled for a very important meeting, one that required even the presences of honorary Banned & Exiled~! member Rikki Roxx. Roxx: ROCK BAND! Strung out across the living room were a guitar, drums, and microphone. Rikki rushed over to the guitar, scooping it up before anyone else had a chance to get to it. Markus decided to go with the drums, leaving his partner Storm with the mic. Picking it up, Chris blew off any dust and slowly brought it to his lips as the game loaded. Start “Wanted Dead or Alive” by Bon Jovi. Storm: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages…prepare to be rocked like you have never been rocked before. Ready your eyes and ears for an experience like none other. On the bass we have the Resident Rocker, Rikki Roxx! On cue the Maestro of Metal played a mute solo, but at least his moves looked smooth. Storm: Slamming the sticks to the sounds of his own beat, my main man, Markus Stone! A quiet pitter-patter bounces from the toy drum set as Markus spun the sticks between his fingers and showed off his skills. Storm: And the Last Great Technical Wrestler, the man who walked 47 miles of barbwire, and pisses fire and gin; CHRIS MOTHER F’N STORM, BIYOTCHES! Roxx: Why does he get the awesome introduction? Storm: Because I’m the one holding the microphone. You chose the guitar, now you have to live with that. Their argument is cut short; however, as Tessa Windsor marched into the living room. In her hand she held a sheet of paper that she held out for all to see. Windsor: What is this crap? The three looked at the sheet of paper, inspecting it for any abnormalities. Storm: A piece of paper? Windsor: It’s an entry form to IG40! Stone: Who’s it for? Windsor: Banned & Exiled~! Stone: Oh cool….wait, what the hell. Windsor: Exactly! And if you didn’t know about it, that means. Her eyes went straight to her fiancés, Chris Storm. Chris looked to Rikki as if somehow he was guilty of this. The strategy didn’t work and only served to add to the awkward silence in the room as the background music from the video game continued to play. Storm: Ok, so I kind of entered Banned & Exiled~! into IG of the forty variety. Windsor: For starters, you do know IG is for singles wrestlers, right? Storm: They said any PTC wrestlers are allowed to enter. Banned & Exiled~! are PTC wrestlers. Stone: What she means is that there are no tag teams in this match. Storm: Well they’ll be at a huge disadvantage then. Tessa had been through this situation with them a million times before, and this was only counting back from when Steven Caldera appointed her their official babysitter. She knew that when they set their mind to something, more importantly, when Chris misunderstood something; there would be no use in trying to change things. She sighed and did the only thing she could; she tried to make things better. Windsor: Fine, if you two are going to do this Infinite Gauntlet deal than you better go hit the gym. Storm: I know Rikki certainly does. Stone: Yeah he got beat down like a little girl against Foster Nackedy. Roxx: I was having an out of body experience. Storm: More like out of gender, ya chick. Windsor: Ok enough jokes and games, time to head to the gym. Tessa shoved the three away from their video games, turning off the television as she passed it by. The three groan, their aspirations of being the greatest rock band ever dashed for the moment. Huddling up outside the New York condo, the trio of Banned & Exiled~! go into planning mode. After a short discussion they break from the huddle and start heading out. Before Chris left, he made sure to give Tessa a peak on the cheek. Hours passed and the sun gently settled in the west, leaving Tessa wondering just where her fiancée was. It had been too long since the three had left for the gym to get ready for their match in the Infinite Gauntlet. Leaning outside the door as she waiting for them, Tessa spotted Markus and Chris coming from down the hallway. She bit her bottom lip as she noticed Chris leaning against Markus as they walked. Storm: You’re…you’re…you’re pretty. Chris smiled sloppily at Tessa, giving her a huge grin. The Petite Pink Diva’s eyes turned sharply to Markus Stone. Windsor: Is my fiancée drunk? Stone: Not exactly. Chris Storm stopped what he was doing, pausing for just a moment as his head tilted downward at Tessa. Storm: BOOBIES~! Windsor: He’s drunk. Stone: Ok so maybe we decided to go to the bar instead of the gym, and MAYBE we had a few to drink. But he’s not really drunk. Moving away from his tag team partner, Chris instead leans against the wall as he started to pee in the hallway. Tessa shook her head in great disappointment. Stone: Wow, uh, ok. You have to believe me, he’s really not drunk. Windsor: Yeah, clearly that is the case. Trying to help her childish fiancée back into the condo, Tessa tossed his arm over her shoulder and did her best to carry his weight. Markus did his part to help hold his weight, though never bothered to help him zip back up his pants. Windsor: You really should have gone to the gym. IG won’t be like all the other matches you two have had. Now you two have as much to win as Playboy has of getting me to pose naked for them. Storm: NAKEDNESS~! Stone: I’m telling you, all he had was just draft root beer. I think he got confused by the name or something. Storm: Ah, sweet, sweet beer; the elixir of the roots. Stone: See. He’s not drunk, he’s just stupid. Tessa looked him over, trying to decide which of the two were more likely to be telling the truth. He smelled of alcohol horribly, but that could have just been an effect from being in the bar. Windsor: I’ll give you that much on him being stupid. But that still doesn’t change the fact that Infinite Gauntlet is soon and neither of you have had any real training for it. Stone: Training, shamining; we’re the world’s greatest tag team. We’ll be fine. Storm: Fine like wine! Woooo! Having enough of his idiocy, Tessa removed his arm from around her shoulder and shoved him back into the hall, where he landed ass first. Storm let out a sharp howl of pain, popping back up to his feet milliseconds later. Shaking her head, Tessa stepped back into the doorframe. Windsor: You two need a strong dose of reality. Storm: What we need is another strong dose of bourbon. With that, Tessa slammed the door in their faces, having had enough of their childish nature. Storm: So now what? Stone: Wanna go buy another copy of Rock Band and bring it over to Rikki’s place? Storm: Sure, but speaking of Rikki, where is he? Stone: I think we left him at the bar after he passed out. I can’t recall. But I’m sure he wouldn’t mind us crashing at his place. It will be cool. Storm: Sweet. ROCK BAND~! And so onward and westward the heroes marched, to their digital rock stardom, carefree of the hours winding down until their Infinite Gauntlet debut. Infinite Gauntlet, prepare to get BANNED & EXILED~!
