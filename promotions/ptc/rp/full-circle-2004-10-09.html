Full circle-!

9 October 2004

Date: 2004-10-09 Don’t you love me? Because I love you. Tell me it’s not true? It isn’t is it? I’m lost well no you’ve lost me. They say I’m obsessive, they say I’m blind they call me insane do you know that. I’m not obsessive I just love you; they tell me you don’t love me. I know you didn’t mean to get with Shaw I’ll forgive you and won’t harm James just be with me, be mine, Rose please. They call me blind. Tell me how much you play me like Jimmy Hendrix does his guitar, like Dave Grohl on the drums or Jack Evans does the crowd. They say how you only want me when I’m winning and how losing my gold made you leave me. I know you wouldn’t do that to me, I know you wouldn’t sell out on me, on us, on everything you came back to me for. Insane there Insane, they can’t see it like I can, like you can where meant for one another where meant to be. You and me that is Rose, soul partners, perfect matches just like the SCW Championship and me. Where like Pea’s and carrots, as Forest Gump would say. You, the gold, and me the perfect match. With you around my waist the title flicked over my shoulder I looked the part felt the part and had you to love the part. I’ll tell you Rose, there wrong and I know your going to prove them wrong for me because you love me and I love you and where a match made in havening… ‘BEEP’… This is the end of the tape… Bye Rose… Love me as I love you… The mad man sat back in the corner of the room leaving the phone alone for a second after running Rose’s tape out clean of an hour in total. Around Jamie lay dirty tissues some with mucus others with blood, Jamie’s arms butchered with stitches after a session of teenager gothic style wrist slitting. Still in the center the room five day’s later like a symbol stands the bottle of alcohol, with a joint on top of it and a cigarette lying across the joint to form a straight edge X representing everything Jamie is about even in the worse of situations. Everyday for the past week Jamie had been leaving messages on Roses machine only for the cold blooded heartless temptress to come home and hit the delete button without consideration before screwing James Shaw all over her dingy one bedroom flat. Ironically a flat Jamie had bought her during their last relationship, Jamie taking pity allowed her to keep the property when they split up after she pleaded with him. You could call Jamie a typical ignorant lover. Reaching Jamie’s lifts the phone off the hook. Like a spring his hand dials the number and rings. There is no answer, Jamie bits his lower lip, and he knows she won’t pick up but still in anticipation he is egger. The phone rings continue less until the answer machine accepts the call it won’t let him record because all the space has already been taken up. The door opens and Jamie looks up. His face red raw with tears and wrists red with blood from his emotional week. There in the door frame stood Jamie’s boss… the charismatic Mathew Mannion, the SCW Owner who went through Kayfabe greatness looked down on Jamie. The distressed star only peered up out of the corner of his eyes to ashamed to expose his face to Mat. ‘Hmmm, get your bag packed!’ Said Mat Mannion with a sense of hastiness. Jamie didn’t reply he wasn’t about to listen to Mat. He’s a state, psycho, out of his mind and can’t be bothered really to do anything. ‘Your going to America.’ Shocked Jamie looks up again. ‘Your match, Eli Smith, it’s Saturday and you’ve got to be there plus your going to be representing team SCW in an Indy challenge Friday night. He’s words fell out his mouth almost as if he had forgot how to speak ‘Who…’ ‘Some girl, some problems about her dad in the past, don’t know really don’t care. Small blonde, twenty one and fly’s a lot.’ Sitting there Jamie thinks for a moment biting his lower lip. He ponders a bit more then looks up at Mat who seems to be waiting on Jamie. Smiling Jamie extends his hand to Mat to get his assistance, Mat pulls Jamie to his feet after a little struggle. On his feet Jamie looks in the middle of the room. The little X sits pretty on the bottle of alcohol in the middle of the room the idolized symbols of this modern world. Showing he is truly better than the modern world Jamie kicks the bottle at the wall. Turning to Mat he has a flare in his eyes, the room atmosphere had rapidly shifted to tense the man looked ready, like a fighter, a tiger and most of all a hardcore hero. Two little shrapnel’s of glass flew back off the wall cutting into Jamie’s chest, which was exposed due to his shirt buttons being undone. ‘Is she hot?’ Stated Jamie cutting the tension straight back down to a bare minimal. Relaxing Mat nods to Jamie. -Viking Hall, USA- Jamie is brushing off his shinny pants getting ready for his six-man match. His arms covered in tape up to his elbows like a warrior covering all his slit wrists marks and self-abuse hints. Throwing his arms forward at full speed like a windmill he jumps up on and down on the spot then does the same actions jumping to his right and left. The door creaks open… ‘Jamie!’ says Mat shutting the door. ‘Changing of plan, new match, one of are team couldn’t make it so your going one on one with the other man in the team. ‘Who?’ ‘Don’t worry, just get out there and have an extreme match for me.’ The tension that surrounded them the day before once again shadowed them. The local booker called Jamie out of the room. ‘No! I won’t!’ stressed Jamie at Mat but Mat knew Jamie would break that, he only knows what he has in store for Jamie. It’s all planned to get Jamie back into the old Jamie make him hardcore again release the beast that was once inside Jamie give the crowd what they wanted. If Jamie broke out then SCW could be huge being Mat Mannion he was about to get Jamie to be violent. Mat Mannion always got what he wanted… ‘Superman’ By Goldfinger played and Jamie Fletcher made his way out threw the curtain in his normal energetic fashion. He was going nuts, shaking hands with everyone in the crowd just like a good wrestler is meant to. Looking up at the ring for the first time Jamie went red in the middle of the ring stood James Shaw. The new SCW World Champion the guy who just five days ago stole Jamie’s girlfriend and in rat fashion screwed her minutes after taking his Championship. Jamie looks at the crowd. None of them know the hidden heat between himself and Shaw who is just grinning in the middle of the ring. Swallowing his pride Jamie jumps into the ring. Despite not knowing the heat the smart crowd in Philadelphia could still feel something between the two men a slight wiff of hatred. The two moved close to another in the centre of the ring before the referee even get’s time to turn around Jamie slaps Shaw in would you could describe as blatant shoot fighting. Shaw in return smashed his boot into Jamie Fletchers rib then grabbing his head threw it down onto his knee then chucked Jamie’s body over so he was back to the mat. Getting up Shaw crashed his knee down into the ribs of Jamie Fletcher again the crowd where eating the pain up like a baby. The two wrestled and did shoot’s on one another for over fifteen minutes when the match started to get violent. One of Jamie’s back hand chop’s broke the fine layer of skin on the breast of James Shaw causing him to bleed slowly the blood only just drawing from the cut. Putting his hand on his breast James Shaw feels his own blood in psychotic fashion he sniffs if own blood then extending his hand wipes it across the chest of Jamie. Lunging forward he smashes Jamie in the stomach with his right foot then brings his right elbow swiftly down knocking Jamie Fletcher almost into tomorrow in rapid succession he throws elbows at Jamie. Taking a rough hold on Jamie’s head James spins him to the ropes slinging him out of the ring like a broken toy. Jamie’s back gets pulverized by the non wrestler friendly hardcore steel crowd barriers of The Viking Hall. On the outside Jamie is flat out unable to get up… James Shaw goes under the ring and pulls out a line off barbwire. Finding a chair James Shaw wraps the barbwire around it with no remorse or pride for Jamie’s problems. Going again under the ring Shaw retrieves a can of PETROL! Going into his tights James Shaw fishes himself a match. Opening the petrol can he covers the chair in the petrol but his match snaps while attempting to light the chair. Looking at the woman near by Shaw grabs a match off her already lit… Jamie looks up and the woman handing the match over is … ROSE!!! Jamie flashes out back that day… I remember now David grabbed the chair from inside the ring. The chair was black it had barbwire loosely tangled around it for maximum effect he looked pumped up the adrenaline was going to allow him to hit me. Pulling a petrol can from underneath the ring he sprinkled the chair with a coating of the extremely hazardous liquid. Looking up I saw him grab a match and lift the chair. The member of the audience wasn’t plain; she wasn’t middle age that was my mind I wanted to think that. The person was Rose, her skin looked beautiful, her hair hung long and sparkly but the witch showed no hesitation in handing the lighter over. They took me away after the match I can remember why my ears hurt so much it wasn’t the holy shit chants I made them up it was the goodbye from Rose… the words where so brutal worse than the chair shot itself, hotter than the fire that turned me blood red and deeper than the barbwire that ripped my skin apart. ‘You’ve been played.’ She said. The chair was burning. Jamie was in position but this time he moved out of the way and he rips James Shaw down onto the chair, his face taking the fall blow. Rose’s face dropped and Jamie turned to her. Launching his hand at her he grabs her by the throat. The whole arena erupts… ‘BURN! BURN! BURN!’ Jamie throws Rose down into her chair… ‘Eli can blame you for the pain!’ Leaving the arena Jamie goes in search of Mat Mannion. The SCW Owner had a lot to answer for; he had a lot to tell Jamie he was a dead man walking… Once Jamie reached Mat Mannion’s VIP room he saw that Mat had gone back to England already on a flight that was leaving in ten minutes time. That made no difference to Jamie all he had to do was it wait revenge would be his but Eli is first. Eli is the top of his list, she’s number one but surely he can’t forget Mat. Will Mat be the bane in Jamie’s concentration over Eli, what will Rose play in there match, will Rose watch. One thing Jamie doesn’t have to worry about is James Shaw because that dudes going to be in hospital for a long time… 1st degree face burns yeh baby consider it a warning… THE HARDCORE HERO IS BACK AND ISN’T AFRIAD TO ABUSE IT!
