Name: Lucas Quinton Title: Star Wars Episode XIV: Why I am Better Than You Part 2: Old News -------- Skip the small stuff. I am here. That is all that matters. * * * Today is the day wherein I put the past behind me. Today is the day I become a new person, a whole person, a sane person. Today is the day I finally ascend. Ascend into the great darkness from the fallen abyss of time and space itself. Today I was going to show the whole world who I really am. I finally get all my crap sorted out. It wasn’t easy though, I had to reassure myself that I wasn’t going to die, that I wasn’t going to plummet into the seventh layer of hell and meet that damn Satan himself. No, I need to hype myself up, to become better than the rest of the people before me, to tell the whole world to fuck them-selves because I was here. I have nothing to lose, nothing to gain. I was going to show them who really matters, who the one that was going to save the day, the superhero, nay, superman. I finally get back to my home, my solace, my resting place, my Valhalla. What do I see when I get there? That fuck Lapidus, he is standing there on my lawn with his dog, there are brown spots all over my lawn. I’m going to kill him. “What…the….fuck?” I listen to him rant all the damn time and he has the gall, the blarney stones, to do this to me? “What do you think you are doing Lapidus? What in god’s name do you think you’re doing? I go away for a while and I come back and I see you and your dog on my lawn doing god knows what? You better have a good explanation for this.” “Well, you see, uhh, hmm, well…” I knew he didn’t have an answer, pretty obvious at this point. Him and his shitty little dog ruining my grass. He must take pleasure in these things, but I certainly don’t and I am going to show him who exactly he is dealing with. “Listen , you god damn bloody fuck. Do you have any idea who I really am? Do you have any idea what kind of shit I had to go through these past couple of weeks? Do you understand? Really? Do you want to know a secret? You are a fat piece of shit, you a nobody, you get you jollies trough shoving that money in everyone’s faces, even though you and I both know you got it illegally. I am in no mood for games or to fuck around with someone like you.” I can’t take it anymore. I am going to crack. He was going to as well. His face drooped so far to floor that he would need them scraped off the floor with a shovel. There are tears watering through his eyes. His mouth gapes open and his eyes bulge out, he tries to express words back to me, but he has lost his voice. He wipes the tears from his face, he completely shut down. I knew how he felt. I knew what he was going through. I really didn’t care that he started crying, because at this point, it is just all for nothing. I know the game, I know how it is played. Could he be lying? Could he be telling the truth? Why should I care about people at this point? * * * …Later… I stare outside the window. I gaze Lapidus and his wife fighting. It just wasn’t his day was it? They were a good distance away, but I could hear them arguing. I don’t care anymore. This depression have left me in a gaze, I couldn’t think straight anymore, I was like a drunk, a stupid drunk that has nothing left, that has any where to go, and the only thing he has left is to end himself. That wasn’t going to happen to me. I am not going to be like my neighbor Lapidus, not like him, not a con man, not a cheat, not a low-life scum bastard who has to cheat his way through the simplest of tasks. I was going to do things my way. * * * This was all old news to me now. Things of the yesterday were dead and gone, and I wouldn’t have it any other way. I could feel the wind beating against my skin in a sense of rhythm, whispering in my ear, telling me things, awaking my eyes to the new age, to the new world. I was going to be reborn. I was going to become a god, a rebel, a made man. There is no more rest for me, only action upon action upon action. My status here is no longer of trivial issues, today was the day I become a shell of my former self. Today I was going to die. Death is the new life, the new warmth, the new rebirth. Forgetting the ways of old, I look through the distance, watching every speckle of dust fly away changing its direction every second. It is time to reveal my plan… * * * Lucas… Lucas Quinton… I can hear it now, my name will echo throughout the ages. My name will become a household stable, a recollection of all things that will be great. I asked everyone before, why am I better than you? And I gave you the answer, because I am better than you. These words were not to be taken lightly. I am better than you, every waking second of every single day. The people who laugh and underestimate me are the ones who will be the first to go. I am now the one in the control box. I am going to be the one who makes the decisions, a dictator, a monarch, a leader, a glorious leader. This is the end of the current era. This is the Quinton era.
