We all live with demons….

2 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-02 It's early on a Friday morning and Reverend Blaze is sitting up in bed staring at the alarm clock as it flashes 12:00 am. Reverend Blaze takes a long drag on his Swisher Sweet and holds it deep in his lungs. As the smoke finally begins to burn his lungs he lets out a bluish white cloud of smoke. Reverend Blaze takes another quick drag of it before putting it out in a nearby ashtray. "These things are gonna kill me." Reverend Blaze thinks out loud. He goes over to the partly opened window and listens to the night sounds that used to put him to sleep as a child growing up in rural Mississippi. But those where thousands of miles away and what seems like a hundred years ago. Now the sounds come from his shelf radio and a soundscapes cd. Oh how he remembers those easier days and how he wishes he could go back to them. But this is 2005 and he can't relive his past, now he needs to concentrate on his future and the GTT5 tournament and his opponent in the first round Twizzy. Although he doesn't know alot about Twizzy he does know that both of them have something in common, demons in their life. Twizzy like Reverend Blaze has seen better days in the wrestling world and Twizzy like Reverend Blaze now struggle to face the world day to day. But in Reverend Blaze's mind that's where the similarities end between the two. Reverend Blaze knows his problems and he is in a daily struggle to control them. Why as a matter of fact its been twelve hours since he had his last drink and now he swears off smoking too. As he stands there looking out the winow the bathroom door opens as the freshly showered hooker steps out with a towel around her. "Where do you want me?" The young girl sheepishly asks. Reverend Blaze is jarred back into reality by her ever so young voice. He turns to her and motions for her to get on the bed. Reverend Blaze stares at her for a long time before finally speaking. "You're eighteen right? I mean I don't want you to think I am some sick child molester. I'm just a lonely guy in the city is all looking for some companionship." Reverend Blaze says this more to convince himself than the young girl. "Does it matter how old I am? I mean you are paying me for the night so-" Reverend Blaze snaps when he hears these words come from her mouth and he leaps over the chair that is in between them. As he reaches her in what seems like a split second the young woman tries to let out a scream but Reverend Blaze's hands are around her neck and cuts off the sound before it can leave her mouth. Reverend Blaze has a half crazed look on his face and tightens his grip around her neck. The young woman begins to struggle but its to no avail, he is much bigger and stronger than she is and her life now is in his hands. Reverend Blaze loosens grip just enough to let the girl get some air. "Yes it matters how old you are! I mean do you think I want to be here? This situation is bad enough for me, I don't want to be here but I can't control myself. Do you understand that? Do you know what it is like to be forced to do things you don't want to do? Now I ask you again, you are eighteen right?" As Reverend Blaze takes his hands away from the young, frightened girls neck she slowly nods her yes. Reverend Blaze let's out a deep sigh and lets the girl sit up. As he slips down by her feet he stares at the ground and begins to speak once again. "I want you to understand something, I want you to understand that if this life we all face demons. Some might be simple ones like a weakness for candy while others are more serious. Some people can control them while others like myself just try to fend them off from day to day. Do you understand that? Do you understand what I mean? I told myself one day that I would beat them and be in total control of my life, but until that time I just have to feed them to keep them at bay. I mean for God's sake I used to be a preacher! Now look at me, in this seedy motel with a young girl like youself. It's not something I want to do, its something I have to do. The smoking, the drinking the sex, I do it all just to keep the demons at bay until I can control them and they can't control me. I don't want to but I have to, do you understand? It's crazy when you think about it but can't think about it to long or else the demons beging to creep to the surface and then they are the ones in contol. But I tell you what starting tomorrow I will be a new man! I will be strong enough to beat these demons and live the life God wanted me to lead. But today I just need a quick fix to quiet their voices and get thru the night, just a quick fix." As Reverend Blaze looks up tears are streaming down his face and the young woman has tears in hers also. But in that split second where these two people just connected in the pain of Reverend Blaze things switched just that fast. Reverend Blaze quickly stands up and pushes her back on the bed and rips off her towel. Reverend Blaze dives into her flesh not like the usual "john" does but like a crazed animal who hasn't eaten in days. This is how Reverend Blaze will control his demons today…. We all have things that control us in one form or another, some people give in to them while others find other outlets. We all have demons in our life but the question is how do you control yours?
