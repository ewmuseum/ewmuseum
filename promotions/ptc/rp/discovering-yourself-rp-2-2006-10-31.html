Discovering Yourself - RP#2

Date: 2006-10-31 _________________ St. Basil's Shelter 12:13 P.M. Vancouver, BC, Canada Dec. 25/95 _________________ A thick white blanket of snow snuggles up against every nook and cranny in the empty street. The tall green light poles are all decorated with different Christmas mythical figures: Santa, Reindeer, and even Frosty, the lovable snow-man. To the east is a large park, but it looks more like a Christmas village at this time of year. Tents roofed with snow, small fires with people gathered near each trying to share the warm toughing kisses of the bright flame. There was a feeling in the air, one exemplified by the holiday spirit. That feeling, the one that lingered amongst these people was anything but the Christmas spirit. It was more like neglect. Society had neglected these people and many of them had begun to neglect themselves. But not all of these people in this camp could be deemed as 'junkies' or 'bums'. Some of them were here without knowing why. Someone of them had lived a good healthy life and then one day woke up in this hell. There was no joy, only sorrow and despair. There would be no exchanging of gifts, say perhaps the odd needle sharing. You know what it feels like when a hundred eyes are watching you. Each one, peering past every other god forsaken event that is happening and planted squarely on you. Being different here is like going to the Middle East and proclaiming George Bush is your God. Each set of eyes seemed to peer right into her soul. Standing next to a large oak tree, the small girl bundled in a large coat keeps her head down. Every now and then she lifts her eyes to meet one of the unwelcoming gazes from the locals. What the hell was she doing here? Tucking her frost-bitten fingers into the pockets of her jacket, she couldn't remember anything that had led up to this point. Thousands of situations played through here mind like a black and white movie, but none of the things she fantasized about were what had truly happened. How did she get here? And more importantly, who was she? These thoughts soon took over the scenes playing through her mind. As she tries harder to recall the events that had brought her to this point, her eyes close and her face crinkles up… "Kiara?" The soft soothing voice filled the crisp afternoon air. The leaves had already changed colors and many were already scattered across the ground. The brilliant earth-tone colors give a warm feeling to a generally cool day. Overhead, the sky is clear; with the exception of a few puffy white clouds floating high above our heads in the gentle breeze. Shining down light mixed with small warming kisses, the sun doesn't appear to be working overtime on this particular day. Yet, standing in the cool afternoon draft, her raven-colored hair dancing with the floating debris; dressed only in a short summer dress with red and pink flowers, Mrs. Martell anxiously checks over both shoulders. Squinting her eyes, she takes a step forward off her cement porch and down the two steps onto the front sidewalk. Leaning forward jus enough to peer around the tall pine tree (the same one that blocked her family room view), she plants her hands firmly on her hips and frowns. Within voice range, the young Kiara is playing at the local church with some of the children who were just leaving the morning session. Waving goodbye to her friends, she stops in her tracks when she hears her mother calling her name. A large smile forms across her face as warm thoughts of her loving mother enter her mind. Skipping across the parking lot, heading to the grass field next to the church, Kiara stops suddenly. Within seconds, her eyes roll into the back of her head and her arms begin to convulse. Soon her whole body seems disjointed like a contortionist. Sweeping herself off her own feet, she smacks the back of her head on the softer grass and not the pavement, luckily. Her body arches and convulses into weird and awkward shapes. Her left arm moving wildly and as it does so it swipes the gold locket around her neck off and into the grass next to her body. …Opening her eyes after finally remembering something for the first time in weeks, she suddenly regrets the decision to force the memory. Blood comes seeping from her nose like an unstoppable crimson river, staining the snow below. Tilting her head back and pulling her hands out of her pockets, she tries in vain to stop the bleeding. Before she can have any second thought, her eyes roll into the back of her head and she falls into the snow. Her body losing control and her mind seemingly slipping away, the seizure comes again. Convulsing in the crimson dyed snow with hundreds of spectators, she is completely unaware of her physical surroundings and consciousness. Instead, she sees something else. A girl, not just any girl, someone that looks remarkably close to her. "Who are you?" "I am who you want to be." "Why do I want to be you?" The girl doesn't move or even crack her mould of emotionless stature. "There is no one else that you can be." Kiara, that was her name. She knew that much from the memory. "I can be me." Now the girl steps closer to Kiara. "And… who are you?" Kiara thinks about it for a second. "My name is Kiar-" She stops and looks deep into the girl's eyes. Their gazes meet and connect for what seems like an eternity but is only a manner of seconds. "My name is Ki-" their eyes still locked. "Angelica." "You and I are now the same. Together we will handle this problem you had." "Problem?" "The Seizures Angelica… the tumour that grows near your cerebellum." Then the girl vanishes. Kiara or Angelica rather is left completely empty once again. Void of any memory. Where she was, who she was, except she held onto one thing… that name… Angelica. Three men stop and set Kiara's body down on a bench across the street from the park. One of them frantically bangs on a nearby door. Within seconds the door swings open and a beautiful young woman steps out into the snow. Her hair is fine auburn and is tucked under a small hat. Her eyes are warm and caring. She looks at the three men and then at the blood they are covered in. At first she imagines the worst, someone had been killed. That was always the worst part about operating a shelter in the heart of Vancouver. Then she notices the blood trailing from the park to the bench just outside her shelter. Now the thought that one of these men may be injured runs through her mind. But her thoughts are soon cut short. "This girl needs help." The man who had banged on the door says while pointing to the bench. Kirsten steps out of the shelter porch and looks on the bench a young, blood covered girl. "Jesus. What happened?" "She looked out of place," the man begins, "I was watching her, she was so young. Too young to here. Had to be a run away." "But the blood?!" Kirsten says hovering over the girl while waving for help from her staff. "She was standing next to that tree," the man points again, "then her nose started to bleed and without any warning…" he pauses. "WHAM! She hit the ground and starting going crazy, all dis-jointed and shit." "A seizure." Kirsten pauses after checking the girl's pulse. "Do me favour, go into the shelter and have them dial 9-1-1." Kirsten nods at one of the men who had helped carry Kiara here and he responds, running into the shelter. "Thank you for helping this girl." Kirsten's warm smile was thank-you enough for the man. "I may be homeless, but I have morals." "I know and I thank you for that." Kirsten scoops the girl into her arms and begins to head to the inside of the shelter. "Come inside, get some food. We'll get this girl some help and tonight you will have Christmas with me." The man nods and the two enter the shelter. "That's appreciated ma'am." Kirsten is about to reply when the girl's eyes open. Kirsten lays Kiara down on a fold out bed and looks at her lovingly. "What's your name sweetie?" No response. "Do you know your name?" Still nothing. Her eyes seem to be soulless. "Who are you?" Kirsten finally asks. Those words seemed to trigger something within her mind. "…K…" A pause. "…Angelica." She finally sputters out. ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ …It is apparent that the seizures that the child has suffered have been from the pressure caused on the cerebellum and the malignant tumour within the astrocytoma glands. It is believed that stemming from this tumour is a perception of a false reality. Under these pretences, the patient has created a world for her to withdraw herself into, one that would sanction her from the consistent seizures. Somehow, part of her brain awoke; dormant for years since child-birth, it is believed that this girl selectively woke up her unconsciousness. Within months, the false reality had become more real to her than her real life. She accepted the past she had created only to stop the consistent seizures… ~+~+~+~+~+~+~+ _________________ Vancouver Regional Hospital 6:21 P.M. Vancouver, BC, Canada Dec. 26/95 _________________ Faint humming sounds are heard from above as the fluorescent lights buzz to light up the room. A clean white-yellow light casts away the shadows in the room. The room is private; next to the bed are two machines, one monitoring her heart beat and the other forcing IV into her arms. In the air, the lingering smell of death fills the hospital. There are many elderly people situated in this wing of the hospital, the whole idea of being here was discouraging enough to the young woman who sat quietly in the corner of the room. Nestled in a soft chair with wooden arms and legs, Kirsten tilts her head to the side and glances at the peacefully resting Angelica. Before long, a man dressed in a long white coat enters the room and immediately heads towards Kirsten. "What do you know about this girl?" He asks while pushing his glasses back up to the top of the bridge of his nose. "Just her first name." Kirsten replies as she rises from her chair. "She told me her name was Angelica." The doctor studies the clip-board in his hands and then looks back into Kirsten's eyes. "We have to list her as a Jane Doe. No medical history, no identification and just a single first name to work with." "I don't know how she ended up at that park, but had those men not helped her, she'd be dead today I fear." Kirsten makes a point to note that to the doctor. Pulling a pen out from the left pocket of his white coat, the doctor jots down a few of Kirsten's words on the paper in front of him. "I'm assuming you're not family or a friend?" "No," Kirsten begins, "but I think that after all she's been through she could use a friend right now." "These tests aren't cheap either. Without the proper coverage, I feel that we may not determine the exact cause of the seizures." The doctor places his clip-board in the basket that is hanging on the door to the room. "I'm assuming it's an epileptic seizure. But I can not be certain without the proper testing." "How much do tests like these typically cost?" The doctor pauses, turning to Angelica and begins checking the equipment next tot the bed. "In the upwards range of tens of thousands of dollars." Kirsten stops for a moment; her mind begins to run rapidly. She couldn't just let this girl lay here, suffering, with no one or nobody to take care of her. So Kirsten reaches into her purse and pulls out her wallet. "I'll cover the girl on my insurance." "Excuse me?" The doctor questions. Kirsten rifles through her wallet and hands the doctor her coverage card. "She needs these tests and if she has no family or friends, then I guess someone has to initiate the first step." "You do understand that you are helping a person that is completely irrelevant to you?" The doctor asks. Kirsten just smiles, "she's not irrelevant to me. She's an innocent young girl on her death bed. She needs me…" Up until this point in Kirsten's life she had been relatively alone. She had graduated high-school only last year and was now spending her free time running a shelter in downtown Vancouver. Studying for child psychology in university, Kirsten had been alone for far too long. Perhaps taking kindness on this soul when it needed it the most would be reward enough for Kirsten. Yet as she peered into the face of the motionless girl in the hospital bed, she knew that this girl needed support, a friend and even a family. That's when Kirsten had the idea. It was vague and unbelievable at first, but as she contemplated the outcomes from this idea, she knew it was what she had to do. Covering the medical costs for Angelica's preliminary examinations, Kirsten thanks the doctor for his support and help. On the vice versa, the doctor thanks Kirsten for her act of generosity. The two stop by the nurse’s desk at the front of the building and Kirsten hands her information to the nurse at the station. Before long, Angelica is listed under the care and protection of one Kirsten Carnes, the only person to even inquire about this girl. After filling out the proper forms, Kirsten stops and looks at the doctor. "Please," she begins, "do not list her as Jane Doe. This girl has a name, let's not take that from her. When she awakens, I know we'll be able to get the information we need from her." The doctor nods and makes note of the name: Angelica. "Thanks doctor. I'll be back in a couple days." _________________ Vancouver Regional Hospital 2:28 A.M. Vancouver, BC, Canada Jan. 02/96 _________________ "Angelica?" "Yes." "If I'm going to be you, how do I forget about who I am?" "You're special, the very source of your seizures, your memory lapses, it will be what secludes your past from you and then you and I will become one." "What if I don't want to forget about my family, my friends?" A slight laugh. "Would you rather suffer and wake up dead at some point in your life?" Now silence. The kind of silence that sends goose-bumps all over your body. "No…" "Then the choice is clear. I can subdue the tumour, make you forget about the past and give you a real future. I am the strength you need in this time of darkness." An awkward moment of silences passes. "Will there be no pain if you and I become one?" "There will always be pain…" "Why?" "Because pain is weakness leaving the body. Soon you will be free of all your weakness. Embrace me, you and I need to be one." The two lock up in an embrace… and then before she knows it, the girl is gone. Her eyes flicker open and begin to wander through the room. She notices the machinery next to her bed, also tubes and IV entering her body. Sitting upward she rubs her eyes with closed fists and smiles at the woman sitting across the room. Within a few seconds, she gathers enough information to know that she had suffered another seizure or at least she figured that much from the vision she just had. Looking down at her white gown, she pushes the blanket away from her body and sets her feet on the cold linoleum floor. Shivers tremble throughout her body as she shuffles feet along the cold floor. Reaching out she grabs Kirsten by the shoulder while she's sleeping in the chair. Shaking Kirsten abruptly, she steps back and watches as Kirsten slowly comes to life. "Angelica?" Kirsten questions. "What are you doing out of bed?" Angelica looks at Kirsten with a look of confusion in her eyes. "Who are you?" Kirsten smiles, "My name is Kirsten; you were brought to my shelter when you had a seizure." "A seizure." Angelica closes her eyes and remembers the conversation with the girl that looked so much like her. "I don't think you'll have to worry about that anymore." Kirsten rises from the chair and places her left hand on Angelica's right shoulder. "How'd you end up in that park all alone?" Angelica tries hard to remember the events leading up to her getting into the park Kirsten is referring to but cannot remember anything but her name. "I don't know. All I know is my name…" "Did you run away?" Kirsten presses. Angelica closes her eyes as she tries even harder to remember the past. Soon steady streams of tears flow down the sides of her face. "I can't remember." Kirsten removes her hand from Angelica's shoulder and uses both of her thumbs to brush away the tears on both sides of Angelica's face. "Don't worry baby. Everything will be alright. You can stay with me if you need to." "Thanks." Angelica looks into Kirsten's loving eyes. "Why do you care about me? You don't even know me?" Kirsten begins to recall her own childhood. "Well…" she begins while reminiscing. "Everyone needs to be loved. You don't remember where you came from or how you even got to that park, I just figured you could use a friend." "What do you do?" Angelica asks. "I'm a University student studying Psychology. But in my spare time, I wrestle." Wrestle? That was the first thought in Angelica's mind. "What do you mean wrestle?" "I need to pay for my tuition and well, wrestling is good money." "Do you think you could teach me?" Kirsten looks into Angelica's eyes. "Anything you want when you get out of here."
