Contemplation

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 ***ROLEPLAY ON*** The scene opens as Johnny “Blaze” Medina, just fresh off of his victory over “The Immortal” Jeremy Page to advance into the second round of the GTT4, is sitting in his apartment in Los Angeles, California. Blaze is, of course, extremely confident about his abilities. However, he also realizes that while there are still about 128 people left in this thing (granted, I haven’t counted everyone who has resigned from the tournament), that with the end of the first round, the pretenders are gone and all that is left are true contenders. While there are favorites to win this tournament, any one of the people, including Blaze, could pull it off… the remaining field is just that damn good. However, what Blaze Medina must be focused on now is the man known as Arch Angel, also known as Michael Staton. 6 feet, 220 pounds, which gives Blaze a four-inch height advantage but they are equal in weight. Like Blaze, Arch Angel has a combination style of technical and highflying techniques, as well as advanced training in special combat tactics outside the realm of wrestling and sport, albeit not quite to the extent at which Blaze has, but enough to where it should make for a damn good match-up that everyone should really look forward to. What is the real “X-factor” in this match, is that Angel has had a broken vertebrae, which had left him partially paralyzed from the chest down for nine months before surgery and a lot of rehab has made it possible for him to return to the ring. Because of this, Arch Angel’s back and neck will be a big target, and will take quite an impact if and when he is hit with the Funky Cold Medina, an Emerald Fusion 2 off of a fairly high jump. Obviously, Blaze is going to want to do what he can to win this match, and is pretty well known for working very stiff and not hesitating to execute a move. However, knowing what his finisher could possibly do to Arch Angel, you have to wonder if he will be a bit hesitant to use it. Plus, since Arch Angel is going to be a father in a couple months, Blaze really does not want to leave Arch Angel unable to provide for his family, yet, at the same time, he needs so badly to have an outstanding showing in this tournament… and losing in the second round is hardly outstanding. The dilemma… the conflict. Blaze: “…well. What to do? I’ve made it to the second round of this global tournament; the tournament to end them all… and now, I face a man who I could possibly leave a vegetable, or some shit. Granted, there’s the danger of that in every match, it comes with the territory, and you know that going in. Still, there’s the normal ‘outside’ chance that one of us in that ring will never walk again, much less wrestle, the chance that you take each time you step inside the squared-circle… but then, there’s those moments when you’re in there with a guy who, at least temporarily, experienced that first hand. Worst yet, any bad bump, and he can be back in that state, only permanently.” Blaze sits there, and contemplates the situation for a second. Blaze: “There’s also the fact that I can’t be gentle with the guy, or worry that I might hurt him, because if I do… I’ll be watching the rest of this tournament right here on this couch. Maybe losing wouldn’t be as bad for me as being carted off would be for Arch Angel, but this is an important time in my career. This is a time for me to prove to the world that I belong in the major leagues of this business, and that I am a major player. I don’t have to win this tournament to do that, although that would be damn sweet… but losing in the second round won’t do anything for me other than make me look average-at-best, and that’s something I can’t go for. We didn’t go for that shit in the Corps, and I damn sure can’t go for that here in this business. So, what all this means is… ” Blaze again takes a deep breath, looks up, then back at the screen with a look of pure sincerity, if that’s even a word… Blaze: “ …Arch Angel, as much as I hope that you remain injury-free, the only way I can guarantee that I won’t severely injure you is if you simply don’t show up. I know better than that, of course. Just like a U.S. Marine has no quit in him or her, neither does a former New York Cop trained in Special Weapons And Tactics (S.W.A.T.). So, just in case things go bad, you had better make all the preparations you need to make, because when I execute the Funky Cold Medina on ya, dropping you straight down on your busted ass dome… I can only pray that after I pin you for the one… two… three… …that you will be able to leave the ring under your own power.” Blaze then takes a sip of some Courvoisier Cognac, and then raises the small glass up. Blaze: “Here’s to our second-round match. One way or another, this match will be one that everyone will be talking about for years to come. Let’s just hope when it’s over, regardless of which one of us leaves with the victory, that we can talk about it fondly, rather than as a moment when someone’s career was ended. But then, what do I know, right? I’m just some ‘bum Marine from the streets of L.A. who runs off at the mouth too much’, right Staton?” Blaze then tips up the cup and drinks down the remaining alcohol. He then wipes his mouth off, and kinda nods at the camera, as if he’s nodding directly at Arch Angel. Fade… …to… …black. ***ROLEPLAY OVER***
