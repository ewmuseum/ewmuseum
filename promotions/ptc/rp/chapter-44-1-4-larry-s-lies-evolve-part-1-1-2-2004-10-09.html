Chapter 44 1/4: Larry's Lies Evolve Part 1 1/2

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 “A snowmobile?” bellows Danyon. “You want us to go all the way to Antarctica on a snowmobile?” Larry pauses for a second before answer, “maybe not all of the way. They don’t have snow in Mexico and Central America so it might be a bit tough to use the snowmobile then. Also, we have to take a boat across the ocean after we get to the end of Chile as it isn’t ice all the way to Antarctica so the snowmobile doesn’t work.” Larry looks around at the three others standing in the room, Bailey, Danyon and Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom, and they all seem very unimpressed with that Larry just had to say. “On second thought,” he continues on, “we could fly there. It would take a lot less time.” Still, the rest of them in the room seem unimpressed with Larry right now. They’ve had enough of his shenanigans and it is about time that the joke of him being from Antarctica ended. “What?” Larry asks as he notices the unimpressed faces of those in the room. “Oh, you are probably right it might be a bit hard to get to Antarctica. Major airlines don’t fly there every day so we might have to wait a few days until we can figure out how to fly there.” Bailey, Danyon and Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom stand in silence staring at Larry while he rambles on. “Although, if we were to get a flight, we’d have to work around my schedule for the GTT as I can’t miss my next match,” Larry continues on oblivious to the rest of them. For the most part he is only talking to himself now and answering his own questions. “Maybe we could go to Washington first for my 3rd round match against Kasper Sky and then we could catch a flight out of Washington to Antarctica. That definitely makes the most sense. I should go and look up the costs of the flights. I’ll probably need 5 flights as I’ll have to make sure that Stripes sits up in the cabin not down below as I know he doesn’t like being down there with all the other animals. Oh, I’m so excited to go to Antarctica as it’s been so long since I’ve been there…….” “Larry!” Danyon interrupts him but to no avail as Larry keeps on talking. “It must be like 8 years since I’ve been there. Yes, I’d say 8 years. I can’t wait to go home. I wonder if Sam is still there. I haven’t talked to Sam since I left. I hope he remembers me, I know I definitely remember him…..” “Larry~!” Danyon bellows and this time catches the Legionnaire’s attention. “Yes, Danyon?” “Shut up.” “Oh…..” “Larry,” Danyon says, “we are not going to Antarctica.” “We’re not? Why not?” “Because it doesn’t make any sense to go all the way down to Antarctica, that’s why Larry,” Danyon says forcefully. “It doesn’t make any sense because you are not from there. It would be a complete waste of time to go down there.” “But ….but…I thought if we went there it would prove that I’m from there,” Larry stutters out. “It would prove to be a big waste of money that’s about it,” Danyon responds. “Larry we’ve said it before and we will say it again. Stop the charade. It’s time for it to stop now before you get injured in the GTT. You are in over your head now and you should pull out of the tournament. There is no shame in doing so. A few people pull out along the way so there is no shame in someone like you doing it, since you are not even a real wrestler in the first place.” Larry nods his head as he listens to Danyon’s speech yet again. He has heard this speech a few times from him since he entered the GTT but he always disregards what he says and continues on in fighting in the tournament. Larry looks at his mom who is standing there quiet, probably for the first time in days that she has been quiet. He then glances at Bailey who is not saying anything either. Finally he looks over at Stripes the Penguin, his best friend, who is standing there with a big penguin smile on his face. Larry rubs his chin with his hand as he scans the room once more. Then all of a sudden he turns and heads to the door and runs out the door. Bailey, Danyon and Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom all remain in the room shocked and confused as to what Larry just did. Stripes the Penguin walks towards the door and opens it up and leaves, following the lead of his best friend. Even more confused now Bailey breaks the silence in the room, “what exactly just happened here? Did he run out on us?” “See what you made my son do? You made him run away,” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom says blaming Bailey and Danyon on Larry running out the door. “Mrs…… Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom, I think you had as much to do with it as we did,” Bailey says trying to defend Danyon and him. “Excuse me but you are the ones who allowed my son to pretend he was from Antarctica for the last 4 months. I don’t see how I’m to blame at all. If you guys didn’t allow him to get away with so much in the first place, things would be different,” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom responds angrily. “What do you mean it’s our fault? All we allowed him to do was have a bit of an imagination. It’s not our fault that he can’t tell the difference between reality and his own dream world. It’s not our fault he doesn’t know his own history,” Bailey says defending his own stance that it wasn’t his fault. “I haven’t seen my son in two years since he started hanging out with you two idiots, so I don’t know how this is my fault.” “Maybe if you had spoke to your son in the past two years he would not have gone off the deep end,” Bailey says matter of factly. “Would you two stop the bickering,” Danyon interrupts them. He is standing over by the window in the New Royal Legion Suite and looking down. “Larry is getting on his snowmobile now. I think we should stop laying blame and maybe do something about this. I can only imagine where he is going.” Bailey and Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom give each other a glaring look before they walk over to the window and look down at Larry the Legionnaire standing by his snowmobile. ------------------- “I’ve had enough of them, Stripes,” Larry says to Stripes the Penguin who is down in the parking lot of the hotel with him. “They are always telling me that I’m not from Antarctica and that I’m not a wrestler. Well, I think I know my own history. I don’t get that confused and not remember people who I meet. I know what I’m doing and I’m going to prove to them yet again Stripes that I can win this GTT.” Larry looks over at Stripes who is standing beside the snowmobile. Larry walks over to the snowmobile’s cab that is attached to it, much like those old motorcycles and cabs this snowmobile has a cab attached to it for passengers. He pulls out two helmets. He places one on Stripes head and does up the chin lock underneath Stripes’ beak. He makes sure the helmet is on correctly and then knocks Stripes on the helmet twice. “They told me I wouldn’t win one match in this thing, saying I’m the worst wrestler entered in this whole tournament. Well, I won two matches. I made it farther than they could ever imagine. I’m not done yet though Stripes. I’m not finished. I’m going to win this thing even if without the help of Bailey and Danyon.” Larry takes the other helmet and puts it on. He makes sure the helmet is on correctly and then knocks his own helmet twice. Larry picks Stripes up and places him in the passenger cab and does up the seatbelt for Stripes. He makes sure that he is all in safe and sound before climbing on the snowmobile himself. “We are going to drive all the way to Washington now Stripes so I can win,” Larry says confidently. Larry turns the key to the snowmobile and starts it up. “Where do you think you are going?” a voice asks. Larry looks confused for a second and looks over at Stripes but then as much as he wishes Stripes could speak he can’t. Larry turns to his left and sees Danyon standing there. “I’m going to Washington, that’s where I’m going. I am going with or without your help Danyon. I didn’t need your help for the first round or the second round and I advanced. I’ve been thinking that I don’t need your help at all,” Larry answers proudly. He turns back to his snowmobile and flips it into reverse. “Wait, wait, wait,” Danyon pleads. Larry keeps the snowmobile in reverse but doesn’t turn it off. “I’m listening.” “Just hear me out,” Danyon says. “I’ve come to realize just how much this tournament means to you Larry and maybe, just maybe, Bailey and I were wrong in telling you to resign from the tournament. You’ve done quite well for yourself so far. You definitely have exceeded my expectations. Maybe there is something to you and wrestling. Heck, I’m sure you learned a few tricks or two from hanging out with Bailey and myself for the past two years so that rubbed off on you.” Larry looks forward but continues to listen to Danyon. “You are in the third round now, Larry and are bound to face a reputable opponent sooner or later. Bailey and I are experienced wrestlers, perhaps we can give you a tip or two in the ring. Help you out with your training so you know how to do more than headlock and armbar.” “I can do a DDT now,” Larry says proudly. Danyon chuckles, “Okay, I’m sure we can teach you to do more than a headlock, an armbar and a DDT.” A small smile grows on the face of Larry underneath the helmet of his. “But what about my mom and the whole not believing I’m from Antarctica thing? Her of all people should remember what it was like to grow up in the cold tundra of Antarctica. I bet she’s been in denial since she left the continent. That’s probably the explanation for it.” “I’m sure we work on your mom, Larry. A deal can be worked out about the whole Antarctica bit too. I mean you’re a wrestler and they always lie about where they are from,” Danyon responds apologetically. “I just don’t to see you or anyone get hurt. Someone is bound to get hurt and I don’t want it to be you. So, why don’t you say you hop off your snowmobile there and come back inside into the Suite?” Larry continues to sit on the snowmobile and thinks about what Danyon just said to him. He looks behind him and sees Bailey and his mom running out of the hotel towards the snowmobile. “Larry, my boy, where on earth do you think you are going?” yells Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom. “Don’t you ever dare run out on me like that again.” “Mrs……er….umm….Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom,” Danyon says as he puts his hand out in front of her telling her to stop. “I think we should all leave Larry alone. He has some thinking to do.” “Thinking? What kind of thinking does he have to do? He better get off that snowmobile right now otherwise he is grounded for life.” “Grounded? How can you ground a 34 year old man?” Bailey asks. “I can ground him if I want to, since he’s my son after all.” Bailey shakes his, “at least we know which side of the family the crazy part comes from.” “What did you say?” demands Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom. “I….” Bailey starts to say but Danyon interrupts him. “I think we should let Larry think for a minute here. I’ve changed my mind and want to support him in this tournament. And I think you two should do the same. Especially, you, Mrs….. Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom.” Danyon looks back at Larry who is sitting on the snowmobile. Larry slowly turns around on the snowmobile and looks forward before taking off his helmet and handing it to Danyon. “I’m going to take you up on that offer Danyon. I’m facing a tough opponent this round. Supposedly this Kasper Sky guy is immortal and can’t die. I bet if he were to spend a winter in Antarctica he’d not survive it and we can see how immortal he is then…….” Larry climbs off of the snowmobile and nods at Danyon and shakes Danyon’s outstretched hand. “It’s a deal Danyon.” “Glad you could come to your senses, Larry,” Danyon says with a big smile on his face, glad that this ordeal is over with. Larry, Danyon, Bailey and Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom all turn and start to make their way back to inside the hotel. They start crossing the parking lot when Bailey stops and looks back towards the snowmobile. “Um….Larry….did you by any chance happen not to turn off the snowmobile?” Bailey asks him. “Oh crap…..” Larry spins around and sees the snowmobile driving backwards straight towards another car parked across the parking lot. “Stripes,” he yells out. Bailey starts chasing the snowmobile across the parking lot hoping to stop it before it runs into another car. But Bailey was too late as the snowmobile crashes into another car. Bailey keeps on running towards the accident as he wants to save Stripes from the accident before an explosion…………. BOOM. Just then an explosion rocks the parking lot as the snowmobile and car exploded into a ball of fire knocking Bailey off his feet and back a few yards. Larry, Danyon and Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom all start running towards the scene of the accident. It seems that Danyon’s words of warning about Larry’s actions and lies being potential harm to someone came to light but never in his wildest imagination did he think that Larry’s lies would put Bailey or Stripes the Penguin in danger. Sirens from fire engines and ambulances can be heard in the distance rushing to the scene……
