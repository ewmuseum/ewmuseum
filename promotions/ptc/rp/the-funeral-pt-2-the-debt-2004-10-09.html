The Funeral Pt.2: THe Debt

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 The Debt ‘Just not fair’ Those were the words I had said as I crouched with my knees on the ground while the raindrops began to fall down to the remnant of the procession. That had been an hour ago. Since then the casket of the fallen mother had been lowered. The tear dried husband and children had long since left to go on with their life, and I had left to go and fulfill the promise I had made. It was raining once again as I began my trek down the slippery road to the place I called home. The long country road gave me plenty of opportunity to think, and that was most certainly what I was doing at that point in time. I had made a promise and I intended to keep it. I thought to never mind the consequences of my actions. Nothing else mattered to me at the moment. I had no friends. The only person who I had ever counted as family was dead, and I had no money. What else was there left to pay a debt with it not with the thing I had thought of. What else could even the score for a life ripped away from all that was good to it? That woman who had just been buried had plenty to live for. She had a family. She had a job. She had money and friends and plenty of people who loved her. On the other hand there was me, the person who survived that fateful accident. I was the person who was alive and well. I was the person who escaped with barely a scratch. I was the person whose small black truck had somehow managed only a dent in a collision with the force of 140 miles per hour. How, I still did not know. The only I knew for sure was that the woman who had it all and should have kept it all was gone. Yet, the person who had nothing at all to live for was still alive and kicking. I was the person who had no family, friends, or money. I was the person who had nothing to live for and she was the one who had EVERYTHING to live for. Why then had fate decided to let me live and take her away? It was just simply and undeniably unfair. It was also something that I planned on replacing. I had to. This was just something that had to be done. This wrong must be righted. It was something that would not go quietly into the night, but I would make sure it would be known. The score would be evened. Even at the price it would cost me. I had no choice. The price was simple. No other would work. There was only one thing I had to offer that could make up the loss of their loved one. There was only one thing I could give to the family whose mother and wife had been ripped away from them much too early. The simple yet ever so elegant price was in fact my life. My life would have to pay the debt where none other could suffice. It was truly as simple as that. I had nothing to live for so I cared not. I was all alone in this world and nothing could change that fact, or so it would seem for the moment I continued my trek down the long dreary country road as the rain continued its abysmal drenching of the area. I had my mission and I was sticking to it. I had only one this to do. It was to get and home and quite simply and unquestionably to end my life. I had a gun. It would suit nicely for this. I would leave a letter apologizing to the family and explain why I had done this. That it was for them that my blood was spilled. It was for them that my life had been ended as retribution for their loss. It would work. I clicked my windshield wipers to their fastest speed. The rain was coming down harder than ever now. I wished it would at least let up a little. Even though I knew it was stupid I decided to continue through the now blinding rain as it straighten even more so. I finally cursed under my breath and decided to stop on the side of the road. I slowly pulled to the side of the road and went a head and put on my emergency light lest some idiot driver end my life before I had the chance too. I put my truck into park and sat there in silence for a few minutes, trying to not think about the upcoming task at hand. After a moment I laughed at myself at the realization that the only thing I had going from me no longer mattered to me any more. The grandest tournament in the land would surely not miss me. My opponent, Muppet Man, would surely not miss the fact that he would get through this round without a fight, unless they decided to stick a dead body into the ring. Now wouldn’t that be something to see. I began to peer out into the surrounding are and noticed something white to the right of my car. I couldn’t tell what it was so I moved to the right side of my truck to get a better view. It was only after a minute of focusing that I realized that I was in fact a white cross. Puzzled, I decided to further investigate. I totally scooted myself over to the side of my truck and opened the creaky door and was instantly drenched by the downpour. I didn’t care as to how wet I was. I would be dead in only a matter of time now, right? I crossed the rain streamed ditch in a single hop and lowered myself level with the cross which I could see was now adorned with rain drenched flowers. ‘To our loving mother and wife. May god rest her soul.’ And then further down it said “Born: Dec 13th 1963/ Died: October 7th 2004.” It was then that it hit me. This was the place This was the place where it had happened. It was this very spot where the woman whose funeral I had just attended had ceased to live I jerked my head towards across the road to the place where I knew I had been, to the place where it had been decided that I should live. Pain rushed through my mind as I began to relive the night. It had started out simple, just a normal drive into town, nothing more. Then it had gone wrong. ’ I took a step up the ditch to try and get a better look at the destruction that I knew lie ahead. The darkness was making it hard for me to see but the remaining headlight of the compact was helping out as much as it could. I walked over to the driver side door from behind to make sure the driver was ok. I knocked on the window to no avail. My view inside was blocked from the fact that the windows were tented, so I went ahead and opened the door. I was amazed to find when I opened the door that no one was in the seat. My first thought was that maybe they got out. Maybe. It was then that I noticed the water pouring through the windshield through a huge bloody hole. I froze up at that moment because I feared what I may find at the front of the car. I slowly shut the driver’s door and proceeded to walk the short length of the car to the front. As I came around the front of it I saw what lie there. It was an image that would haunt my nightmares forever. ’ When it the memory had ended I found myself back on my knees where I had already been that night once before. It was then I was reminded of why I had to do this. Why I had to give up my life. To pay the debt that was owed. Yes, that would be the way it would be. It had to be. I smiled in a way I never had before, much like a maniac even though I was perfectly *sane*. It would be simple I thought once more. I would use the same gun that Richard used to kill himself to kill myself… The same gun Richard used. The same gun the man who I had called father used… It was then that I remembered something else from that night. It was something he had said that I hadn’t thought of much till right then. Though it was something I should have though of long before. ’ You have much left to do. Yet you waste your time. You are better than what you believe yourself to be. You have a purpose in this life. Now you must figure it out. You must forget about me and everything I once stood for.” My purpose. What was my purpose I wondered? Why had Richard, dead for four months, appeared then? It was for my purpose. My destiny… My destiny. Fate, destiny intertwined and considered much the same thing. As IO thought about there in the freezing cold rain it dawned on me. If it was fate and destiny for the message to be given to me on that night then it was also fate and destiny for that woman to be gone from this world as a result of that night. Yes, it now made sense now. It was all coming together. I had purpose in this world and I needed to find it. My purpose was to live on and I knew it. I would have to live on. I would live and repay my debt to the family anyways, not by sacrificing myself but by living for them and helping through their time of need. Yes that was it. I had figured that piece of the puzzle out. I had to live on, and I most assuredly had to win.
