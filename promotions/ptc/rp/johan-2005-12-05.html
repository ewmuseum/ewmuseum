Johan

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 If you heard the name ‘Johan Fredriksen’ would you know exactly who he was? Would you even know Johan was a male name? Probably not when considering the ignorance of all Americans towards the outside world. But if you lived in Denmark, Germany, England, France or Sweden there is a good chance you would know exactly who we are speaking of. Johan was born in Helsinki, Finland. When he was just 5 years old his family emigrated to Denmark. Johan grew up just as any child does. He went to school, he came home, he turned the Television on and stared vacantly at it for hours on end until it was time for bed. He did that with his brothers and sister until his 16th birthday. As with any child Johan enjoyed cartoons and other children’s shows. But most of all Johan loved to watch wrestling. He and his brothers could have watched it for hours on end. Every Monday they would sit themselves down and at 5pm watch the weekly edition of Danish Mainland Wrestling’s Bloodshed and then again on Wednesday for the repeat. Every other day would be full of wrestling each other during the commercials or when there was nothing on Television. As we mentioned, this was all up until Johan’s 17th Birthday. Well, what happened then? It’s simple. Johan decided nightly wrestling with his brothers wasn’t enough. He wanted Daily wrestling with the best the world had to offer. He enlisted in a wrestling school which was famed for one reason and one reason only. Current DMW Heavyweight Champion graduated there. The man known as ‘The Danish Demon’. Rumours were circling that Mikael was off around the world soon. Many people didn’t like that he might be leaving their favourite organisation, but it was said that he would return. Johan was desperate to fill that gap. Training was tough; there was no denying that. On his first day Johan had absolutely no idea what he had gotten himself into. Every minute of the nine-hour endurance test was a gruelling uphill struggle. Johan was stuck in the mind frame that he would start off learning the basics sitting in a classroom watching his trainer point at a blackboard for hours on end. Part of him expected it to be boring until he was sure he knew the basics. Knowing the truth Johan wished the school had taken the more tedious and boring route. He was trained him in the best way his teacher knew, technical style. Of course Johan took to it quickly considering how much in his youth he imitated Mikael Lundgard. Soon Johan was comparable to a swan. Just as the swan seemingly glides across the lake Johan seemed to flow seamlessly in the ring from move to move. Yet also like the swan, just as the legs are kicking hard to propel the swan along, Johan’s mind was working overtime. He had to stay focused on the task in hand, one distraction could throw him off completely. The solution to that problem was simple. ‘Give it time’ It was a cold January evening in 2001 when it happened. Johan was driving home when he turned a corner. In his path was a tanker. The police say the driver was English, he was said to have had a mental lapse and started to drive on the incorrect side of the road. Johan collided with him head on. The scene was not pretty. The car Johan was driving was transformed into a convertible in just a few short seconds. He shattered his jaw, Broke his leg, Broke his pelvis and suffered severe Spinal Injuries. Not to mention the hundreds of cuts and bruises that would scar him for life. Doctors said Johan had a 50/50 chance of ever walking again. Either way though he would never wrestle again. Recovering from the initial shock was one thing but to recover from the physical aspect was another thing altogether. But Johan was determined. He would walk again and he would wrestle again. If he wasn’t in the condition he was there would have been every chance he wouldn’t make it. His physical condition paid off though. First he regained feeling in his legs, the movement in his toes. Soon enough he could move ever so slightly. A year later and he was walking unaided. Against all the doctors requests and advisement he went back to finish his training. Johan was going to do this. He didn’t overcome paralysis for nothing. ~~~~ Sunday April 18th 2004 Skip forwards two years. Johan, now celebrating his 20th year of life is fully recovered. He has signed a contract with DMW for a year. This is no doubt the biggest moment of his young life. Through all the adversity the largest company in his homeland wanted him to come and work for them. Deep down Johan knew he was there for the novelty. He knew he was there to get viewers, as a quick moneymaker. Who wouldn’t want to see the survivor of one of the most high profile crashes ever in Danish history wrestle? All the same, he had made it and nothing could take this feeling of joy away from him. ~~~~ Monday November 15th 2004 4:00pm "One hour until the start of the show! One hour until the start of the show!" Until now he felt like he did before any other match on any other day. But upon hearing the one hour call Johan’s stomach turned violently. It didn’t matter how many matches you’ve wrestled before or how many people you had beaten, when you get to DMW the one hour call feels completely different. Especially when you’re booked against the champion in your debut match. Johan’s hero, the man that had travelled around the world wrestling and gathering a fan base had returned to Denmark and captured the most coveted title in his first match back, had returned. Mikael was now truly a legend known in all parts of Europe and select parts of Asia and America, and Johan was about to meet him face to face. Every single person that could see this match would be watching it. It was an honour to say the least. ~~~~ Monday November 15th 2004 5:40pm Johan’s music faded away as he stood in the ring. 8,000 screaming fans awaited the arrival of their champion. As soon as the first piano note of Timeless hit they all rose to their feet. Nothing could have prepared Johan for the deafening scream. It did nothing but increase as Mikael Lundgard emerged from the curtain. The golden belt around his waist shone brightly in the many lights around the arena. Mikael basked in his glory before making his way to the ring. No one expected this match to last long. Johan exploded from his corner and leapt at Mikael. He caught him off guard and managed to bend him over. In this position he proceeded to hammer away with forearm shots to Mikael’s shoulders. He drives an elbow into the back of Mikael’s head. Mikael gets whipped into the corner of the ring and sandwiched between Johan’s arm and the turnbuckles as Johan runs in with an avalanche splash. Mikael stumbles out of the corner into a fireman carry take down by Johan and then gets an elbow driven into his chest. The fans are stunned to say the least. They had not seen Mikael receive anything this vicious since his return. He was always able to gain the upper hand and keep it for the entire match. Johan was giving this his all. For Johan this was the biggest opportunity he would ever have to prove himself to the DMW fans. Johan climbed the ropes as Mikael lay on the floor. The fans began to chant his name as he looked set to leap from the top. Never in his life had so many people began to root for him. Never in his life had he heard 8,000 strong chanting his name ‘Johan, Johan, Johan’ He grew distracted. His inexperience shone through as he saluted the fans, as he displayed his gratitude for their support, as he thanked them from the bottom of his heart. He should have known better than to take his eyes off of Mikael Lundgard. He should have known better than to become distracted. A DDT brought Johan back down to earth at the same time as driving his head into the canvas. Something went wrong though. Johan grasped his neck as soon as he landed. No rookie could sell that well. This was legitimate. Mikael stood up and shook away the pain from Johan’s early assault. He pulled Johan up by the neck, clearly not the best thing to do in the condition Johan seems to be. Mikael then drives Johan into the mat with a brainbuster. A side headlock was locked in, again Mikael seems to be focused on the neck. Something tells us he knows something is wrong, but it’s not like Mikael to treat another wrestler this way. Is it? A neckbreaker and a pile driver later Mikael locked it in. The Rear Naked Choke. Everyone knew what that meant this match was over. Even if Johan didn’t quit he would in no way be able to stay conscious for long. No one ever had. No one ever will. Johan fought. He didn’t quit. No matter how many times the referee asked him he would answer through gritted teeth ‘no’. His face turned red from trapped blood, lack of oxygen and the intense pain in his neck. Mikael added more and more pressure as the seconds ticked by. To Johan each second felt like a lifetime. A lifetime full of agony, a lifetime that he wished would just pass. Nothing could make Johan give in. Mikael started to turn red from the pressure he was applying. His already bulging muscles grew from the strain. His veins stuck out from his skin as though he was coated in heat shrink and placed under a hair dryer. Mikael began to sweat profusely. No one had lasted this long before. Never had he had to apply so much pressure. Johan started to fade. His eye lids grew heavy as he slipped away into darkness. His hand rose once and it dropped to the mat. It rose a second time and went that same way as the first. The third followed suit. Mikael released his hold on the now unconscious Johan. See? No one breaks out of the rear naked choke. No one. ~~~~ Sunday November 20th 2005 "Recuperation is an impossibility this time around. I have to accept that this is my future. I will not recover this time. I am stuck in this chair for the rest of what can barely be considered a life." A melancholy expression adorn his features, Johan sits opposite. A man once feeling on top of the world. A man who overcame almost everything to get to that stage is now wheelchair bound. Unable to move. Paralysed for life. "There is no 50/50 chance now. I will never walk again. If I was asked two years ago what did I miss more, wrestling or walking I would have said wrestling every time. But until you wake up morning after morning and struggle to get out of bed. When you can’t go to the bathroom on your own. When you know you will never be able to play with your children. You know what the answer should be." A tear forms in Johan’s eye. He would wipe it away but can’t due to fairly obvious reasons. "The one thing I hate the most though, above all the physical problems, above all the hypothetical problems, is the fact that the person that did this to me has never shown a single ounce of remorse. Not once has he ever even mentioned my name." Johan’s stare fixes us in the eyes. Our eyes become transfixed on his, movement is an impossibility right now. "You have to believe me Mr. Williams. Son of God or not, Champion or not, Living Legend or not you cannot take Mikael Lundgard lightly. I beg of you, I am giving you this warning for you to prepare. If you lose focus for one second. If you lose your concentration at all he will take advantage and believe me, The consequences can be life changing." The emotion behind the plea is reflected in Johan’s eyes. Every inch of his face seems worn, he struggles to draw breath as he begins to speak again. "Mr. Williams. Don’t end up like me."
