Into the Abyss Wrestler: Syko Arsonist Juan Santos took the needle and injected the heroin into his blood. Clenching his fists and biting down on the rubber band he used to mark for his veins, Juan began to shiver a bit. The effects of the drug were immediate on him. Juan's leaned back on his Lazy Boy chair and sighed, his eyes nearly rolling back into his head. He was in paradise. Juan picked up the phone and dialed a number. After a moment he answered, "Are you coming in an hour? It's 200 for the hour right? Good." After a few moments of the initial high, Juan leaned forward in his chair and looked at his studio apartment. He tried to stand up, but the effects of the drug were lingering on him and he began to stumble. He tripped over the small coffee table that was in front of him. He cursed God and kicked the table across the room, spilling the empty cans and roaches on the floor. This is how he lived his life these days. He had used up all the money he had earned during his career as a professional wrestler. Once, he lived in suites and mansions, drove expensives cars, took trips to exotic places, and had girls waiting on him left and right. Today, he lives alone. No one ever calls him, or visits him. His only car is a Toyota truck; he lives in a studio. The farthest he ever travels is to the Red Light district for another stash of cocaine or heroin. His once proud fortune now almost reduced to a few tens of thousands. If anyone ever had fallen from grace so much, it was Juan Santos. Where did it all go wrong, Juan wondered as he stared at the bookshelf full of wrestling trophies and accolades. He picked up the trophies and began to read off their titles. No Limits Rumble Winner. Trash Talking Tournament Winner Cruiserweight Tournament Semi-Finalist: Fourth Place Golden Turnbuckle Bracket Finalist Juan held the Cruiserweight trophy in his hands and stared long at it. He then spit on it and through it across the room, smashing the television. "Fucking worthless!" Juan shouted. "All of it, fucking worthless." What have you done to yourself? Juan looked around startled. He turned all around himself, trying to find the voice. "Who's there?" Why did you let yourself get this way? Juan heard a sense of familiarity in the voice, but he couldn't be sure of it. "Who the fuck are you, sister?" He bellowed, looking around feverishly in the small studio apartment. "You have no idea who you are talking to." You got that right. You're not the same man I fell in love with. "What?" What happened to the man I loved? The man who never gave up, who always gave his best? "He got tired of it," Juan answered, just standing in the middle of the room. He knew the drugs made him tipsy, but he was sure the voice he was hearing was real. "He found the cold truth: no one cares about him." That's not true. People still care. "Like who?" Like your mother. She found you after thirty years and you shut her out. Like me. What happened to you, Syko? Juan pulled at his hair and yelled. "You don't fucking know anything! You aren't even real." Aren't you the one who referred to himself as "The One and Only Motherfucking, Syko Arsonist!" What happened to those days? Juan began to say that the voice had no idea what it was talking about. But when Juan turned around in the direction of the front door, he turned a ghostly white at what he saw. There, standing in the entryway, was a beautiful latina lady, with golden tan skin, red auburn hair, and wearing a red dress that was hemmed slightly above the knee. "Who the fuck are you," Juan demanded to know. The lady stepped forward. "You've even forgotten about me." Juan shook his head. The lady walked closer to him and grabbed his hand. When Juan felt it was real, he was scared even more. "This isn't possible. You're dead! You're fucking dead!" "And yet, here I am" the lady answered, "holding your hand and talking to you." Juan pulled his hand away and stepped back. "But I saw them kill you. I saw the knife go into your chest. We buried you, Emilia!" Before Juan Santos had ventured into Primetime Central, when he was known as Syko Arsonist he fought in several indy wrestling federations. Eventually, he was assigned a beautiful woman to work with as a manager to enhance his image. What happened instead was that Syko and the manager, Emilia, fell in love with each other. Their happiness was shortlived, however, when Emilia was murdered. At least, that's how Syko Arsonist remembers it. "I could explain everything," Emilia began. "But why explain the horrible past when we can begin a bright future together." Juan Santos smiled, and it was a genuine smile - the kind of smile he never had since the days he was with Emilia. For the first time in years, he was happy. "Yes...yes!" Emilia then turned away from Juan. There was sadness in her eyes. Juan saw it and immediately grabbed her hand. Emilia wiped a small tear from her eye as she faced away from Juan. "But I don't know if we can do it." "What the hell are you talking about?" Juan pleaded. Emilia looked around and waved her hand around the room, showing Juan what he had reduced his life to. "Look at what's become of you. Look at how you've wasted your life away. You're not the man I fell in love with." Juan yanked her in and held her tight. She struggled to break free. Juan pleaded with her, "You have no idea what it's been like for me knowing you were dead. You have no idea what that pain is. But you're here now. You're here and I can change." Emilia tried to break free, but only managed to reposition herself so that Juan was hugging her tightly from behind. He hand his left arm wrapped around her chest, and his right arm slightly above near her collar bone. For all his money wasting, drug using, and hooker fucking, Juan still kept the physique and strength he had when he was Syko Arsonist. "It's too late for us," Emilia said, almost crying. "It's too late." Juan Santos screamed, sobbing. "No! It's not too late!" Emilia began to cry. "Please just let me go. Let me go." Juan Santos tightened his grip. He did not want to lose the love of his life again. "No!" he yelled, sobbing. "I won't let you go!" "LET ME GO! LET ME GO! LET ME GO!" The voice started out loudly and with each breath grew softer and softer until it was being wheezed out like a struggled cough. The voice was no longer that of Emilia, but of a small brunnette hooker named Lilly. She was only nineteen years old, petite, and was only making her appointment with Juan Santos. Juan Santos continued to tighten his grip. He did not want to lose his love again. But he could not see that it was not Emilia he was holding, it was Lilly. And he could not see that he wasn't hugging her, he was choking her. "You won't leave me right?" Juan said, loosening his grip. Lilly's dead body flopped to the floor, her face blue and her eyes rolled up into her head. Juan stared slightly at the dead girl in front of him, his arms leaving a red mark around her neck like a scarf. "Oh fuck..." he whispered, covering mouth. Juan began to hyperventilate. The drugs had gotten the best of him this time. Juan Santos kneeled down to check for a pulse. But it was a waste of time, the girl was as dead as Disco. Juan started to do some cpr, but no matter what he did he could not bring her back to life. It was one of the courses he failed to take in college. Juan wiped away the tears in his eyes, but new ones were formed. They were partly for this beautiful, innocent girl who was forced into a life she didn't want to have. They were partly for himself. This is how far he had fallen. The drugs, beer, and sex were one thing. But now, he no longer had any control over himself, or his thoughts. He was a danger to himself, always had been. But for the first time, he was a danger to someone else, killed someone else. Juan lifted Lilly up in his arms and repeated that he was sorry for what he had done. He carried Lilly's dead body over to the large kitchen table and laid it down. He searched the kitchen for a garbage bag large enough to fit her body in. In the end, he decided that he would have to wrap her body in different bags. Juan Santos looked out the window. It was past midnight and the rain was pouring down. There was an empty backyard behind his studio apartment and all of the peoples would be asleep. He had enough time to bury Lilly without anyone seeing him. He needed to act quickly. Juan turned back to Lilly, but stopped to look at his reflection in a wall mirror by the window. He saw himself, in his jeans, topless. He saw the body of a failing wrestler, who's muscles were starting to loosen like a rope becoming undone, and the marks and bruises of heroin use. He saw his bloodshot eyes, and the lines of aging and stress under them. He almost didn't recognize himself. "What happened to you?" He whispered, staring at himself. What happened to Syko Arsonist, indeed. What happened to man who walked into Primetime Central with no fear. What happened to the man who won two events, and made a splash in two others? What happened to the man who surprised Inphino Blitz and then took on Angelo Deville in the only PTC vs WT match for the Universal Title? What happened to the one and only motherfucking, Syko Arsonist?! The times changed...that's what happened. What happened to me? The times change, sure they did. But that's not why I've fallen from grace. I fell because I let the world go on with out me. I let names like Clinton Sage, Doctor Curiosity, Garbage Bag Johnny, Nova, the Illustrious Face- Eater, and countless others have their names in the limelight because I couldn't care anymore. I couldn't care less when I let less skilled wrestlers like Jin Akiyara or Sebastian Moraco try to carry the flag on for me. I let the world move on without me, but not anymore. There are many who would never recognize me anymore, and that's perfectly fine. The reality is I'm done trying to win as second best. I'm done trying to live out my life in useless hacks. I'm done with sitting on the sidelines. The world may have forgotten who I am. I'm going to help them remember, one body at a time.
