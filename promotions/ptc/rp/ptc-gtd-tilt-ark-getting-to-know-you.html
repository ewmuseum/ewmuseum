11 January 2005 Tilt (RP #1 vs. Latch Key Kid) Relaxation. Something that Seymour Almasy desperately needed after 2004. He became the co-PTC Wrestler of the Year, and held the Global Title twice. But as the current holder, 2005 looks to be a difficult year. Already a marked man, the first of many would-be assassins would soon fire his bullet. The Latch Key Kid was the highest finisher in PTC during the immense GTT4 tournament, and so he would take dead aim at the Final Fantasy. Almasy didn’t want to have to worry about that yet, however. He was in Las Vegas. The City of Sin was a place near and dear to Seymour’s heart… …But even here, Seymour wasn’t free from problems. Consider Seymour Almasy a man on tilt. ------------ “It’s been so long…” Nighttime on the Las Vegas strip is a spectacular sight, and one that Seymour has been fortunate enough to see many times. The city he once thought to be Final Fantasy VII’s famed Gold Saucer was now his favorite vacation destination. Here, he was an entirely different man. He wore a fine suit, with polished black shoes. He seemed more alive than ever. Seymour Almasy: Some call this place Sin City. If that is true, then please…let the sin begin… Gambling was something that Seymour loved. It was one of the activities he had learned in his video games that actually WAS applicable in real life. Add that to his penchant for watching ESPN coverage of the World Series of Poker, and Seymour had quite a bit of gamble in him. Seymour Almasy: The finer things in life are so much nicer after you’ve gone through Hell first. The statement was made as Seymour looked up at his hotel of choice, the Bellagio. To Seymour, the place was pure paradise. Great restaurants, spacious rooms…and, oh yes, excellent gambling. Today, however, Seymour wasn’t going to be doing much gambling. In fact, he had only one bet to make. He smiled, and quickly entered the building, nodding to the bellboys and other casino personnel who knew him by name. Bellboy: Good evening, Mr. Almasy. Seymour Almasy: To you as well. Seymour continued walking on, heading for an elevator. For now at least, the lure of the casino wasn’t strong enough to pull him towards it. He seemed, however, to have a definite purpose. He punched some buttons on the outside of the elevator, and soon it came whooshing down to greet him. Seymour rested against the wall of the elevator, as it began to head for his floor. Seymour Almasy: 2004 is over, finally. I was the co-Wrestler of the Year in PTC, and yet…I’m not happy. I’ve never been quite this miserable in my life. No personal life makes for a very, very unhappy Seymour. Motivation just isn’t there. And now, I have to defend my Global Title against a man who is VERY motivated, the Latch Key Kid. Ugh… The elevator door opened, and Seymour immediately exited it. Fumbling in his pocket for the room card key, he found it, and opened the door. The room that he entered was nothing short of opulent. It wasn’t a suite, or anything overly ridiculous, but as a room, it was nicer than 90 percent of hotel rooms in the nation. Seymour let the suit coat fall from his body to the bed, as he shook his head. Seymour Almasy: I’ve got to find some reason to care about this match, stat. Because right now, all I want to do is gamble… His eyes shot towards the safe in the corner of his room. Almost automatically, Seymour rose, and walked across the room. His fingers turned the combination, until the safe opened. Inside the safe was Seymour’s PTC Global Championship belt. More importantly at this moment to Seymour, however, was the $500,000 in cash that was ALSO kept in the safe. Seymour Almasy: Mmm…come to papa. Almasy took a nearby canvas bag, and stashed the cash in it. He threw the PTC Global Championship belt over his shoulder out of force of habit, and by the time he had put his suit coat again, he realized that the belt was underneath it. Seymour Almasy: Ah well…it can stay there. I have a wager to make. The Final Fantasy barely shook as he headed downstairs, even knowing full well what he wanted to do. He was going to bet half a million dollars on a single spin of a roulette wheel. On red or black. All he had left to do was figure out which one. Seymour Almasy: Red or black, red or black, red or black. Before he knew it, Seymour was back downstairs. His heart now pounded desperately, as he entered the Bellagio’s main casino area, canvas bag being clung to desperately. In the distance, he spotted the highest of the high limit roulette tables, one that would let him make his bet. He smiled to himself, somewhat wryly. Seymour Almasy: Roulette always has played a major role in my life… Indeed, he had beaten DWO’s vaunted female star to win the Global Title for the first time. That was back when he cared. Right now, however, he wanted to conquer a different type of roulette, the gambling kind. Seymour Almasy: Let’s see if I can win again, shall we? $500,000 was a sizeable sum, even to Seymour. It represented about what he made in a year thanks to his UWF contract and other appearances. Thanks to his wealthy grandfather, it was a bet he could technically afford to make, but it was still a rather stupid thing to do, and in the back of his mind, Seymour knew it. Seymour Almasy: What the hell’s wrong with me? He shook his head. Vice had been a regular in his life ever since Laura left him. He’d taken up alcohol, not seriously, but he had never drank before in his life. Gambling, too, now motivated him. He was sad to note that he now got more of an adrenaline rush out of pushing chips into a pot than stepping into a wrestling ring. Unable to answer his own question, he began to ponder the truly important question: red or black? Seymour Almasy: Red is the color of passion. The color of romance, of all emotion, one could say. Seymour chuckled. With that description, it was pretty obvious which color he would pick. Black was the antithesis of red. The absence of passion, of romance, and of emotion. The color that he most sympathized with. Black was the obvious choice. And yet… Seymour Almasy: Red it is. Despite the turns his life had taken, despite the loss of his wife, Seymour Almasy was an optimist at heart. He couldn’t bring himself to bet on black. He straight out could not. With that knowledge firmly in hand, he took his bag in one hand, and set out for the roulette table, fully prepared to wager half a million dollars on a 50/50 proposition. It was at about this time that fate intervened. ???: Seymour! The Final Fantasy spun around to face the man in front of him. He shrugged, assuming that the man was another fan. Seymour Almasy: Hi there, do you want an autograph. Man: No. I want you to die. Seymour’s eyes went wide as saucers, as the man pulled a gun from his coat. Almost immediately, casino security began to charge the man, as guns in casinos, where much money is won or lost, are serious business. Man: Farewell, Seymour. He pulled the trigger once. It was all he could do, before he was tackled by the security officials. Seymour closed his eyes, and then, nothing. “They lied to me,” he thought, preparing for death to take him. “My heart’s racing. I thought death was supposed to be cold…I’m burning.” Security Officer: Hey! Are you okay? The question jarred Seymour from his thoughts. He opened his eyes, and realized that he felt no pain. Quizzically, he looked around, very confused. Seymour Almasy: Yeah, although I don’t know HOW. I got shot… Seymour looks down at his chest, spotting the hole in his suit jacket. He immediately takes the garment off. As it falls to the ground, Seymour sees the reason for his survival. The PTC Global Championship, still resting on his shoulder over it. And perhaps ironically, a bullet is lodged in the nameplate. “Seymour Almasy” now simply reads “Sey Almasy”. Seymour Almasy: I…I… It is at about this point that Seymour Almasy faints. Security Officer: Someone call an ambulance! He seems okay, just gotta make sure! ~`~`~`~`~`~` Las Vegas Hospital ~`~`~`~`~`~` Slowly, Seymour Almasy became aware of himself once again. He knew that his eyes were closed, but he didn’t want to open them. He kept them closed, while he pondered the turns of today’s events. He’d been shot at by someone he didn’t even know. Had it not been for his habit of putting the PTC Global Championship on his shoulder, he’d be severely injured, if not dead right now. To top it all off, “Sey Almasy” had affected him. See what, exactly? He saw many things now. He’d been lacking a desire to wrestle. It was amazing what nearly dying did for one’s appreciation of his or her employment. He had no idea why the hell that man had tried to kill him. Seymour hadn’t done anything to warrant it. Wild thoughts ran through his head. Was he a friend of Laura’s? He dispelled that thought quickly. Laura might have been hurt, but she wasn’t the type to try and HURT him. As far as Seymour could tell, he was just a psycho. A nut job who thought it’d be a good idea to kill him. The thought made Seymour shudder. All this time, he’d thought he was safe outside of the ring. Sure, inside of it people fought him with fever and force, but outside, he was Seymour Almasy the person. Apparently, that was enough to annoy someone. “Seymour?” Ah yes, a voice. That made him remember that he was technically conscious, and probably in a hospital or something similar. May as well see what she (and from the voice, it was definitely a she) wanted. His eyes fluttered open slowly, and he responded. Seymour Almasy: Yes? Nurse: How are you feeling? The Final Fantasy took stock of his situation. He was lying in a hospital bed. Several members of the casino security that had helped her were there with the nurse. Seymour looked at all of them, and answered as honestly as he could. Seymour Almasy: Well, I’m more than a little bit shaken. Nurse: Understandable. Not many men go through what you did. Seymour Almasy: Does anyone know why he tried to kill me? Casino Officer: He’s a nut, plain and simple. He watches you on TV, and wanted to kill you because “you’re a bad person”. Mentally imbalanced enough that he’ll probably go to a mental institution instead of death row. Seymour Almasy: Jeez… Nurse: If you want, I can get them to leave. They can take a statement from you later if you want to rest. Seymour Almasy: That would be nice, yes. Just want to think for a bit… Campus Officer: Alright then. We’ll be back in a few hours. With that, the officers and the nurse left, leaving Seymour alone in his rather revealing hospital gown. Thankfully, however, a sheet covered his body. He was pleased to note that his $500,000 still remained in the canvas bag, which the security members had kindly brought with him to the hospital, along with the PTC Global Championship with a bullet in it. Seymour Almasy: How the hell am I going to explain this to Primetime Central? Almost as if on cue, his cell phone rang. Reaching into the pants pocket of his jeans, which had been neatly folded and placed on a stool, Seymour pulled out the phone, and could see that PTC was calling. Seymour Almasy: Looks like it’s time to find out… Placing the phone to his ear, Seymour was greeted by a cacophony of worried screams. He did the best he could to answer the deluge of question. Seymour Almasy: Yes, yes, I’m okay…don’t worry about it…yes, I’m okay for the Global Title Match. Tell Latch Key Kid that I’m ready to defend…indeed, the PTC Global Championship belt DOES have a bullet lodged in it…no, don’t worry. I’ve got an idea. I’ll buy us another belt, and I’ll take care of this one. It’ll be good publicity for PTC, and it’ll let me do some good in the world. If you can, get in contact with some Las Vegas area media. I’m going to hold a press conference later today. Okay then. I’ll call if anything else happens. Bye! Gratefully, Seymour clicked the phone off. Seymour Almasy: Those PTC guys worry way too much. Although, I guess I can’t really blame them. “PTC Global Champion Gunned Down In Casino” wouldn’t go over so well. I owe it to a lot of people to do the best I can. I owe it to them, I owe it to the fans, and I owe it to the Latch Key Kid, who busted his ass to get this title shot. Slowly, Seymour sat up, and slides off of the bed. He removed the hospital gown in favor of his jeans and t-shirt, and stretched out, trying to remove the stiffness of lying down for so long. Seymour Almasy: Ah…that’s more like it… The Final Fantasy gathered together his belongings, and headed for the door. He knew that the police officers would be outside. He would give his deposition, and then, he would check himself out of the hospital. After that, with any luck, there would actually be reporters at his press conference… ~`~`~`~`~`~` Outside a Las Vegas Hospital ~`~`~`~`~`~` Deposition given, Seymour Almasy stepped outside the hospital, and was immediately welcomed by a throng of people. Flashbulbs went off, and Seymour blinked. He noted to himself how much more attention one got when he had just nearly been killed. Among the various press were well-wishers, who seemed genuinely happy to see him alive. That was nice, at least. A makeshift podium had been set up at the foot of the hospital steps, and so Seymour set up shop there, taking hold of a microphone. Seymour Almasy: Hi, everyone. You’re probably wondering why I called this press conference. I’ll keep it short and sweet, and then you can ask any questions you want. Reaching into his canvas bag, Seymour pulls out the PTC Global Championship, holding it high for all to see. Seymour Almasy: This is the title belt, the official PTC Global Title that saved my life. Considering it has a bullet hole in it, Primetime Central and I really don’t think it’s appropriate to parade this particular title belt around on television. So, here’s what we’re going to do. At 12:00 AM, midnight tonight, an auction’s going to be created on E-Bay for this title belt. Any and all proceeds from this auction will be donated to the tsunami relief effort, to several accredited charities. The announcement prompted applause from the crowd, which continued for a minute or so. As the applause died down, hands rose in the air. Seymour paused a moment, before pointing to one of them. Reporter #1: John Stark, Las Vegas Sun. Did you suffer any injuries during this ordeal? Seymour Almasy: No, unless you count a couple of bumps and bruises I suffered when I fainted after it. I’m still in shock. Really, I should be dead now, and would be if not for the title. Seymour quickly pointed to another reporter, who seemed to have a follow up question readily in mind. Reporter #2: Mike Wallace, Las Vegas Review-Journal. Why were you wearing your title over your shoulder in a casino, anyway? Seymour laughed. He hadn’t expected that question, and he really couldn’t answer it himself. With a shrug, he did his best. Seymour Almasy: You know, I really don’t know. Force of habit, really. I had it on my shoulder because whenever I pick it up, that’s what I usually do with it. It’s something I’m not even conscious of, as you can probably figure out by the fact that I had my jacket on by the time I realized it. More reporters raised their hands, and Seymour realized that he wasn’t going to have time for a lot more questions. Seymour Almasy: I can take two more questions…you, please. Reporter #3: Peter Johnson, wrestlingrumors.com. Is your match with The Latch Key Kid for the Global Championship still on? Seymour Almasy: Absolutely it is. I’m not injured, just a little bit shaken up. Nothing that’s going to stop me from competing…and who let the wrestling dirt sheet writer in with the newspaper guys? The comment prompts chuckles from everyone, even said dirt sheet writer. One more hand rises, and Seymour points to it. Seymour Almasy: Last one. Reporter #4: Rebecca Williams, KLAS TV. Has this experience made you change anything in your life? Ah, there was the important question. It was something that he had thought about since he woke up in the hospital. After a few more moments of thinking, he nodded, and responded. Seymour Almasy: A bunch of things, actually. First of all, it makes me appreciate my career a bit more. I’ve been kind of down on myself lately, not really enjoying coming down to the ring and doing what I do best, but when you almost die, it tends to put things in perspective. I really love what I do. No matter what I’m doing in the ring, I love putting on a show for the fans. He smiled, but then suddenly grew more serious. Seymour Almasy: At the same time though, it makes me a bit more cautious. I’m a twenty-three year old. Being a wrestler, I’m out there in the public eye, but I’m not like an actor or anything. Didn’t think I’d HAVE anyone crazed enough to try and kill me. Means I’ve got to be more careful, that’s all. Solemnly, Seymour nodded. It seemed as if his mind had once again begun to wander. Seymour Almasy: Thank you all for coming. I really appreciate it. With that, Seymour left the conference site, and began to wander down the sidewalk. He knew that he would have to call a cab to get back to the Bellagio soon, but he had more on his mind. An idea suddenly came to him. One that would help him both personally and professionally. One that would help him be safe…but at the same time, one that would let him do what he loved most about his wrestling career. Have fun. Despite his slight size, he is considered by most to be one of the most dangerous and feared wrestlers in the world. But now, he is afraid. Afraid that he might be attacked randomly by a psychopath again. And so now, the Final Fantasy has undertaken a mission. To find himself some protection. Of course, showman that he is, Almasy isn’t looking for just ANY bodyguards. Over the course of the 24 hours since Almasy had left the hospital, phone calls had been made from the Final Fantasy to dozens of people around the country. They all had a lot in common. They were all fans of professional wrestling. They all knew who Seymour was, and most were fans. They were all very well trained in a fighting art. And they were all…women? Better believe it, bucko. ----- In an undisclosed location that was probably in actuality a school gymnasium, Seymour Almasy stood in the center of a wrestling ring. His PTC Global Championship was nowhere to be found, presumably having been delivered to a safe location for the purposes of the auction. Surrounding the ring were a couple of mats. Seymour nodded slowly to himself, and prepared to begin. Seymour Almasy: Alright. You’re probably wondering why I gathered you all here today. Quickly surveying the crowd, Seymour continues. Seymour Almasy: As you probably know, I was attacked… Suddenly, Seymour realizes something. There are only three women standing before him. He looks down at the prepared speech that is in his hands, and rips it in halves, then quarters. Seymour Almasy: Well, I guess we can skip the speech. Was supposed to be something about how I was going to pick the best three…but we only have three of you here, so I figure I’ll spare us all the sound of my own voice. Mind telling me who you are, where you’re from, and your experience that’s brought you here? The Final Fantasy carefully looked over the three women. It was better than they he had expected. They were all beautiful, and judging by the attire and weaponry some held in their hands, he fully believed that their skills would match. The three looked at one another, until the first spoke. Katie: I’m Katie. 22 years old, graduate of Princeton University. I practice praying mantis kung-fu, among other fighting arts. Despite being all business on this day, Almasy couldn’t help but notice her beauty. She was tall, at least partially Oriental, with striking black hair and catlike grace. Katie: Would you like a brief demonstration? Seymour nodded, as she went into the unique stance that her art entailed, throwing several strikes of all types and varieties. Seymour Almasy: Excellent. Thank you very much. You seem very capable. And now…for you? He gestured to a blonde who stood in the middle. She blushed slightly, but met his gaze and introduced herself. Ashley: I’m Ashley. I’m 25, currently working as a secretary, but in my spare time, I’m a dancer. Pop, jazz, tap…you name it. I’m trying to create a new martial art…it’s not really for practical use, but something blending dancing and fighting. Seymour Almasy: Ah. I’m sure Katie would be able to help you with that. Very nice. Now, for you? The final woman, a brunette, looked quite serious. She stepped forward, and made her pronouncement with pride and a steady voice. Rebecca: Rebecca. 24. I’m an up and coming pro-wrestler. Seymour Almasy: Excellent! Excellent, all of you… The Final Fantasy took a step back, as the three women looked at one another. They knew who Seymour was, but exactly what he had in mind they had no clue. Seymour Almasy: With that out of the way, let’s get down to business. I’m Seymour Almasy. I wrestle for the Universal Wrestling Federation, and hold the Primetime Central Global Championship. As you may or may not know, I was shot at by a crazed fan the other day in Las Vegas. I’ve brought you all here because you’re among the youngest and the brightest at what you do. Now, I’m not asking you to be my literal bodyguards. If I ever think my life is in danger, armed officers will do that. He looked at all three of them, pacing back and forth in the ring as he did so. Seymour Almasy: What I want are bodyguards at UWF and PTC functions. People to have my back, keep fans away from me if they get a bit overzealous. You’d be acting as on air personalities. For some of you, that might be a dream, to be on TV. As far as payment is concerned, I can tell you right now, you’d each be paid $100,000 a year, out of my own pocket. Depending, I might be able to get the UWF to cover food costs, but travel costs will be included. Again, that might appeal to some of you, but the Princeton graduate probably has the potential to make much more than that. Chattering has begun among the three candidates. Seymour senses a bit of nervousness, and does his best to make his final pitch. Seymour Almasy: You’re invited to join me in this not just because you’re great at what you do. I’m doing it also because you’re all fans of mine…or at least your applications convinced me of that. I need friends on the road. I’ve been burning out lately because I really haven’t had anyone to talk to, to confide in. I need coworkers, and I need friends. Soooo, what do you say? The three women looked at one another, and formed a small huddle. Ashley: I’m in. Katie: I’m in. Rebecca: I’m in. So what do we need to do? He was quiet for a few moments, trying to come up with what to say. Nothing elaborate came to mind, so he simply said what he felt. Seymour Almasy: Thank you. Thank you all. This means a lot. Almasy paced a bit more, trying to figure out exactly what it was that he was supposed to do now. They had agreed to his plan. He hadn’t even expected that. Now, he had to figure out exactly what the hell he was supposed to do. Seymour Almasy: Well…the first thing I can ask of you is that you all come with me to my Global Title Match, against the Latch Key Kid. It’s a big moment for me…and I want you all to see me compete live. To get a better understanding of what I do, why I do it, and things of that nature. Before that though, tomorrow, I’ve got a media circus. I have three different appearances to make. And, during the course of that day, each of you will come with me to one of them, so we can get used to one another. Talk a bit, things like that. I apologize in advance for this sounding like an episode of a really bad reality dating show. Three nods come from the three women. Seymour finally relaxes a bit, stepping through the ring ropes, and taking a seat on the apron. Seymour Almasy: It’s not going to be easy, though. I actually, you know, started to do that silly thing called scouting my oppo-- Before he could even finish his thought, Katie spoke up. Katie: The Latch Key Kid. One half of the XUW Tag Team of the Year, the Lunchroom Patrol. Also the XUW Wrestler of the Year, both of those awards voted by his peers in XUW. He’s also their World Champion. Never being what one would call a meticulous preparer for his contests, Seymour was suitably impressed. Seymour Almasy: Alright then. I think we’ll be putting you in charge of scouting my opponents…and, if you don’t mind, you’ll be coming with me on my radio appearance tomorrow. Katie: Certainly. Seymour Almasy: I also have a presentation to make tomorrow. Ashley, would you be willing to come with me to that. Ashley: Absolutely! It’ll be fun! Rebecca: What about me, Seymour? He ponders for a few seconds, before smiling. The answer is quite obvious. Seymour Almasy: You’re the wrestler. There’s a ring right in front of you. I have a huge match this week… The Final Fantasy stepped back between the ropes, and took off his shirt, leaving him in a pair of jeans and shoes in the ring. He bounced off the far side ropes, and looked down at Rebecca. Seymour Almasy: You’ll be helping me train, of course! She froze, and looked at Seymour standing in the middle of the ring, ready to go. Rebecca: But you’re the Global Champion! Seymour Almasy: So? All that means is I’ve got a bigger target on my back… She shrugged, and slid into the ring. Seymour attempted to strike her with an elbow on the way in, but she rolled quickly out of the way, and stood. Almasy rose to one knee, and clapped. Seymour Almasy: Not bad… With the quickness that he had become known for, Seymour snatched Rebecca up in the air. Rather than drive her into the mat, however, he put her back down on her feet. Seymour Almasy: We’ll do legitimate training later on, after we’ve had a chance to get acquainted. Right now though, I think it’s time that we go get something to eat. Ashley: One thing first? He was caught off guard, but nodded. Seymour Almasy: Yes? Ashley: Shouldn’t you give your elite bodyguards a name? Seymour Almasy: You know, I probably should…Actually, I sort of envisioned you three as sort of being like the Gullwings in Final Fantasy X-2. They called themselves YRP among the three of them. Hmmm, Ashley, Rebecca, and Katie….ARK. Perfect! Simple, yet kind of profound sounding… He stood, attempting to exit the ring, but Rebecca almost immediately jumped on his back with a sleeper hold. Her two ARK compatriots quickly followed, and even the Global Champion could not stand up to the mighty dog pile that brought him down. Seymour Almasy: Story of my life, women kick my ass…I swear, some things never change…OUCH! At approximately that moment, Almasy realized that he had, in fact, been kicked in the ass. Seymour Almasy: Whoever it was…I’ll get you back for it later. But can we please go eat? I’m starving! ARK stood, allowing Seymour to rise to his feet. As women can tend to do, they walked together, chatting about their new positions and life in general. This gave Seymour an opportunity to collect himself. He had his three bodyguards. ARK was ready to begin. He had ideas in mind for them, big ideas that would bring him back to the summit of the UWF, and keep him at the summit of PTC. But he was dead tired. His ordeal in Las Vegas had left him frazzled. He had publicity appearances to do that were his duty as the Global Champion. It was becoming tougher and tougher to find time to go train. Whether or not anyone liked it, he was the face of PTC. Out of the ring, he would do everything that the company asked of him. That was the type of person he was, dick in the ring on occasion or not. Seymour shook his head. Now wasn’t the time to worry about it. He needed his food. After that, he would say goodbye to the women for the evening, and retire to more private quarters, where he would be able to worry about a very full day tomorrow. Right now though, he’d settle for a very full belly. (To Be Continued.) ---- 14 January 2005 Getting To Know You (RP #3) Two pro wrestlers, an Asian martial arts expert, and a bubbly secretary. It sounded like the beginning to a juvenile bar joke. But in actuality, it was Seymour Almasy’s solution to his burnout, and his solution to the dozens of wrestlers who wanted to see him go down in defeat. ARK. Ashley, Rebecca, and Katie. Three women, well trained in their arts, and possessing other skills as well. They were personal assistants, bodyguards, and hopefully friends, all at the same time. Today, he would find out more about his assistants, just as they would find out more about him. Above all else though, it was company. And considering the usual monotony of his publicity tours, it was greatly appreciated company. ----- With a look of utter disdain on his face, Seymour Almasy stepped out of a radio station, shaking his head back and forth. He was clad in a pair of dress slacks, with a white dress shirt and tie. His eyes were cast upon the ground as he walked. Finally, however, a pretty Asian woman in a gray business suit placed a hand on his shoulder. It was, of course, Katie, the smartest of the three women comprising Seymour’s ARK. Katie: What’s wrong, Seymour? You did fine. Seymour Almasy: I know, I just hate doing these publicity tours. They’re so mind numbing. Especially the radio appearances. I go on the air, bullshit a little bit about my career, throw out some threatening words to my opponent, and leave. Usually, I’m swarmed by fans when I get out though, so this lack of crazed pre-teen females is an improvement. Katie: I took care of that for you. Made sure the station knew that no one was to be waiting outside. You’ve had a hectic few weeks, and considering your near-death experience, everyone’s seemed willing to accommodate you. Seymour Almasy: Thanks. Want to go grab something to eat? We can go over strategy, logistics, and all of that other stuff I’m not really very good at. Katie: Fine by me…although, you look REALLY tired. Want me to drive? Nodding, Seymour reached into the pocket of his pants, and pulled out the keys to his rent-a-car. He tossed them to his companion, who deftly caught them and unlocked the door. Katie: Hop in. Seymour obliged, climbing in the passenger-side door, as the Princeton graduate with him started up the vehicle, and began driving. Katie: I know a good place a few miles from here. Seymour Almasy: The sooner the better… Katie: Hey, what’s wrong? You seem upset. Seymour Almasy: Nothing’s wr-- Katie: Don’t tell me that. I majored in psychology at Princeton, you’re not going to get something like that by me. Seymour Almasy: I’ve really got to do a less thorough job of picking applicants… He mock-frowned for a few moments, before reverting to his more neutral expression. Seymour Almasy: Anyway, it just gets tiresome sometimes. As much as I love being the best…I hate what comes with it. Katie: Yeah…publicity tours aren’t for everyone. Don’t take this the wrong way, but you don’t exactly seem like a people person. Seymour Almasy: Don’t worry, that’s a more than fair assessment of me. But, if you don’t mind, can we put this conversation on hold? I can take a spin on the couch later, there are more important things to take care of for now. Katie: Yes, like food. With that, she spun the car around into a parking space in the lot of the restaurant, and put the car into park. Seymour Almasy: Yes, like food…and also like explaining exactly what I have envisioned for you all. The pair exited the vehicle, and entered the restaurant. Before Seymour could open his mouth, his companion had everything arranged. They took a seat at a table, and a glass of water was in front of Seymour before he quite knew what was happening. Seymour Almasy: Thanks. Katie: No problem. So, how’d the rest of your day go? Seymour Almasy: Not badly. Got up early to train with Rebecca. For someone with as little experience as she has, she’s quite good. Katie: Was she able to prepare you for the Kid? Seymour Almasy: Not really. The Latch Key Kid wrestles a more technical style than she does, with some big power moves. It was more to keep me in shape than anything. All I can really do is watch film of what he does, and hope I can see it coming in the ring. Katie: What do you really know about him, though? It’s got to be difficult to scout someone you’re not even in the same federation as. Seymour Almasy: It is, but for this match, I’m going to any extreme. He’s from XUW…and ever since I lost to Craig Goat in GTT4, I’ve realized I can’t take any XUWer lightly ever again. The memory made him wince. He was well on his way to winning his bracket in GTT4, well on his way to another of his signature dominating tournament performances, when he had been defeated out of nowhere by a rookie in his fourth match. It was perhaps the most embarrassing moment of his life, and a performance that he never wished to relive again. Seymour Almasy: He’s worked very long and very hard to make it to this match. He started off in the development fed of XUW, made his way up, and has been in XUW ever since. He’s a very strong-willed guy, capable of wrestling as half of a tag team, or in singles. XUW’s rising star for the past year. And, if you want to listen to that idiot DJ, the future PTC Global Champion. Almasy snorted, prompting Katie to giggle slightly. Katie: That guy seemed to have it in for you. Always talking about how you were past your prime, and that a new generation of guys was going to come up… Seymour Almasy: Yeah. Look, there may well BE a new generation of challengers for this title. One of them might well defeat me, but NO ONE tells me I’m past my prime. For cripes sake, I’m not even 25 yet! I don’t even think I’ve REACHED my prime yet. Which is good…because he IS right about a new generation of challengers. The Final Fantasy slumped down in his chair, the conversation only being halted by an approaching waitress. Almasy glanced at his menu for about five seconds before ordering, while Katie gave hers in the crisp, confident tone of voice that seemed to characterize her. Once the waitress left, Seymour continued. Seymour Almasy: Tristan Belmont, Jesse Jamester, Chris Storm, Hessian, Chryste. All of them once top athletes in PTC. All of them at some point considered for contention for this title, or former champions. For the most part, they’re either vanquished, retired, or off in some other far corner of the wrestling universe. Of them, I am the last holdover. As it stands now, PTC has few champions with tried and true experience. GTT4 was won by an outsider, Doctor Curiosity. Myself and Saij are the only two men in PTC standing at the top. But now…the challengers are coming to knock us off. The Latch Key Kid comes with the XUW World Championship, intending to ascend to the snow-capped peak of my sacred Mount Gagazet. Katie: Mount Gagazet? The question perhaps echoed that of most people who don’t know what the fuck Mount Gagazet is, despite this being an impossibility in Seymour’s odd brain. Seymour Almasy: Mount Gagazet is a mountain in Final Fantasy X, the last hurdle that a summoner faces before reaching his or her ultimate goal: the forgotten city of Zanarkand. Everyone knows, whether they like it or not, that I am the best PTC has to offer. The fans in the arena knows it, the wrestlers of PTC know it, and even the only people nerdier than myself know it. Katie: And who would those be? Seymour Almasy: People who spend all day online talking on forums discussing who the best wrestler in PTC is, of course. Wink wink, nudge nudge, and all that rot. At any rate, Katie had been listening patiently, but now, she had a question of her own. Katie: What do you want us to do at the event, though? You told us you want us to come…what will we be doing? Seymour Almasy: Accompanying me to the ring, basically. Acting like managers/valets. Katie: Ah… The waitress returns with their food, and for a few minutes, all is quiet as the sounds of chomping and food consumption take over. Finally, they finish, and cordial after-meal conversation takes place. Seymour Almasy: So…are you glad you decided to do this? Katie: I’m…really not quite sure yet. Seymour Almasy: Why DID you agree to this, anyway? You’re a graduate of one of the finest universities in this country, you could have done a lot better. Katie: Perhaps I could… She gave a small, wry smile, one that caught Almasy off guard. Katie: Then again, you should probably understand. Seymour Almasy: I should? Katie: Of course. It’s all about expectations. Going to Princeton put a lot of expectations on my back…expectations that I probably will fulfill someday, but for now, I want to do what *I* want to do. And for now, that’s work with you. Seymour Almasy: Fine by me. It’s glad to have you aboard. Katie: So…what do you have left to do today? Seymour Almasy: The better question probably would be what DON’T I have left to do today. Katie: That much fun, huh? Almasy chuckled despite himself. There WAS so very much that he had left to do… Seymour Almasy: I have to meet Ashley to present the old shot PTC Global Title to the winner of the auction, which’ll be fun at least. Then, its training, and getting us all onto a plane so we can get ready for the match. Katie: All of this preparation and to do… Seymour Almasy: And all for what will probably be a thirty minute match, tops. I know…but it’s the nature of what I do. All of this work and preparation for something that can end in three seconds. All other sporting events have a minimum length. Wrestling does not. Katie: Sounds stressful. Seymour Almasy: Oh, it is. But for now, I’d rather not think about it. Want to order dessert? Katie: Certainly! And with that, the match temporarily left Seymour’s head. But only temporarily. So much had happened to Seymour. He had so much on his mind. One thing was perfectly clear. Seymour Almasy was a sitting duck. His title, the PTC Global Championship, was ripe for the picking. But could the Latch Key Kid finish the job Craig Goat started? To ask Seymour Almasy, the answer was no. But we just don’t know, now do we? (End)
