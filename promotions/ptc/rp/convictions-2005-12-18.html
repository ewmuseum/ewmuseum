Convictions

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 Blaise was uncomfortable to say the least. He hated these types of things, that was a sure thing. It was time for it to end though. It was time for one thing in his life to come to a closure. He sat there, wearing a suit… yeah, Blaise William Todd McClancy, king of jeans and a t-shirt in a suit. He didn't wear a tie though, the lack of a folder collar made that not needed. It was already torture to be here, he didn't need to make it worse. Sitting in the back of the courtroom, looking as the woman he'd spent a couple of years with stood in front of the judge, awaiting the results of the jury. At least they didn't make him bring the girls into the courtroom for this. That was going to be later one. "Foreman, has the Jury made their decision?" The judge, an older man with a negative hairdo, silver everywhere but his temples, which were still a deep brown, called out. The jury foreman, a middle aged woman who was dressed like she was trying to pickup every man in this room stood up. She straitened her skirt, showing some garter as she did so and then addressed the judge. "Yes, your honor. The jury has made it's decision." The judge tried to hide the fact that he looked, but he was a poor liar. Guess that would make him a good judge. "Very well, if you will read it, please?" The woman unfolded a piece of paper that she had concealed in her hand and then brought it up a bit. "The Jury had decided that in the case of Mrs. Rebecca Morreti and Mr. Francis Morreti. We find the defendants guilty of all charges including: possession of an illegal substance with intent to traffic, obstruction of justice, and bribery of an official of the law. In addition, we have found her guilty on the additional charges brought before the court of child neglect." It was a grand slam. Blaise exhaled as he had been holding his breath while the jury foreman had spoke. Things were changing again… it was a real friggin' roller coaster. "Very well, Foreman. Thank you. In accordance with the Jury's decision, the Municipal Court of Clark County, Nevada finds the defendants guilty on all charges. They will report back here on January 3rd for sentencing!" **BOOM** **BOOM** The gavel struck twice, and it was all over. The woman who had been Blaise's fiance before, Rebecca Fortune, was to be incarcerated for letting her past catch up to her. She swore to him that she'd never stop running, But that wasn't the case. This also meant that his ninety day evaluation as the father of his twin daughters by her, would come to a conclusion. She couldn't raise them from jail, and he'd always been on the proper side of the law. At least as far as they had known. Then again, the activities of a Hunter for the Order of the Hammer aren't exactly recorded for all to see. No one cares if one of the undead, or some lost troll goes away during the night time hours. Blaise got up and walked out of the court room, somber and resolved. As ordered before hand, he made his way down to Judge Johnston's chambers. The judge that was covering his child custody case between him and Becca. He knocked on the door, and entered when he heard the voice ask him to from the other side. Sitting in the room were the older woman of a judge, his two lawyers and two red-haired toddlers. Blaise sighed as he sat down into the chair offered to him. "We just got the dispatch from the balif. I would seem that the jury made their decision. We've been interviewing the girls here now, and we have made our decision." Blaise nodded at her. Everything was beginning to settle in with him. "Mr. McClancy, in this case, you do know that Mrs. Morreti will be loosing full custody of these children, and will not be able to provide for them. You have asked for no support though, and that's fine with the court." Blaise remained silent. "Our decisions borders on making these children wards of the state, or to grant you full custody. Over the last ninety days, you have been in a trial period by my previous decision. Since then you have got your housing situation under control, your job and I might add, done a traditional Yui-No with your future wife, Ukyo when you visited Japan. I see no reason to fault you Mr. McClancy. All we need you to do now, is to sign the paperwork here, and you may take your daughters… home." Blaise's male lawyer stretched out a hand with a pen in it. He took it, and his hand shook as he signed his name on the dotted lines provided on all of the pages that required him to emblazon them. It was done… his daughters were his. //McClancy Residence// //Queens Ridge Housing Community// //Las Vegas, Nevada// Ukyo stood at the counter in her kitchen, working on what appeared to be a cutting board made out of natural wood. She had her gear out for doing sushi, it appeared she was trying to teach someone the basics of crafting this type of food. That person was her new best friend, and neighbor, Danielle Northsky. "Now, all you do once you have everything ready you just put it onto the bamboo sheet here, and then roll it up really tight." The proceeds to act out what she's telling Dani, who has a similar setup in front of her. "And finish by squaring it off just like… this." Her tongue sticks out as she presses down onto the rolled bamboo with the sticky rice and fish inside of it. Dani can't help but start laughing as she watches. "What?" Dani tries to catch herself before she explains. "Nothing, just the look on your face." "Hey, nothing wrong with having a little fun. Just remember, if you're doing anything like this, if it's unagi, Blasie will find it. He's not to much up on a lot of the other stuff. He really hates uni. But unagi and unago he's all for." Dani smiles as she takes a towel to wipe her hands down. "I'll remember that. He and Josh seemed to have the same tastes when we were at the restaurant." Ukyo unrolls her work of art, and smiles. "That's not surprising." She picks up the small, bite-sized morsel and pops it into her mouth. Dani unrolls her, and it looks okay, but it's obviously a bit catty-cornered and malformed. "Damnit!" Ukyo tries to chew and swallow hers quickly as she walks over. "It's okay. Just remember, you need to square it off for this type of roll. Others are usually rounder." Dani still looks displeased with herself. Ukyo smiles though. "It's only the presentation part. How many times did you mess up a hiptoss when you were learning it at the beginning? Or falling for a move? But in the end, it's still all good." Ukyo nods to her, and Dani picks up the roll of rice and takes it into her mouth. As she chews hers down, a ring at the doorbell comes across the inter-com system. "Ah? Who could that be?" She grabs her own towel and wipe sher hands off as she heads through the dining area to the living area and then to the front door. She looks through the little peephole on the large door and then scruntches her nose a bit? "What the hell are they doing here?" Dani walks in from the kitchen just as Ukyo says that. "Who is it?" Ukyo just cracks the door open a bit. "And to what do we owe this?" She backs up and opens the door fully. Dani almost looks like she's going to hyperventilate a bit, all she could get out was one word… "Headmaster?" And she was correct. Two people walked into the living room, both dressed to match their station in the Order. One was a man whom Dani had only met one time before, while training to be a fighter. The other she had only heard about during her modeling stints. Sean McClancy, The Irish Bastard, and Blaise's cousin.. And, his wife, Patti Anderson-McClancy. Sean, the big brutish man, with hair of fire like his cousin nodded in Dani's direction as he used his feet to remove his running shoes and put them in the corner next to the door. Patti doffed her heels and put them next to her husband's. Patti then looked up, adjusting the strap on her dress, and smiled in Dani's direction. Her Australian accent wasn't the slightest in check as she addressed the new person. "Ah, you must be the new chick. Danielle, if I'm not mistaken?" Dani just nodded as she was actually looking in the direction of the man who had run the academy she had trained at. Ukyo caught the vibes going in the room and decided that Sean and Patti had invited themselves in. So she needed to act the hostess. "Okay. Well, Danielle Northsky, please meet Captain Sean Michael McClancy and his wife, Mrs. Patti Anderson-McClancy. Sean you have met before, of course." Dani walked over and Sean put his hand out to take hers in a gentleman like manner. She then shook hands with Patti and tried to move behind Ukyo a bit. "So, what can I do for you?" Sean answered as Patti was looking around at the large house that Blaise had setup once they moved here. "I'm 'ere t' be talkin' t' me cousin. Can ye go pull 'im ou' o' the studio for me?" Sean's thick old country accent came rolling off his tongue. Ukyo visibly winced as she tried to decipher it in her head. It was bad enough that English wasn't her first language, but the barrage of accents she dealt with on a regular basis made it hard to deal with. "He's not here. He got a call earlier from the courts. The thought the jury would make a decision today. I'm hoping that he gets home soon. Please, come on in and make yourself comfortable. I'm teaching Dani how to make proper sushi. You can be out taste testers." Dani moved to open the door to the kitchen for the guests. Sean and Patti moved toward the kitchen. Dani couldn't' take her eyes off of Sean, while Patti seemed interested in Dani. They both took a seat at the dining room table, as Ukyo headed in to get some water going for some tea. Patti finally looked up at Dani and tried to make small talk. "You're the girl who was in FHM a while back, aren't you?" Dani nodded her head. "Yeah, I'm going in a few weeks for a second shoot too. Although this one might end up in a few magazines. But FHM wants to shoot me and a few other wrestling girls who appeared last year. Including you girls who were in the 4WW." Dani nods in both Patti's and Ukyo's direction. "Oh, so you're the one working on getting Ukyo a bit less conservative in front of a camera, huh? It was my bet with her that got her on the cover of ours in Europe." Dani smiles at her. "Yeah, I'm working on trying to get her to pose again. I thought her photos were pretty good. But She's all modest and that. Although I know she's got some pretty screaming stuff that she keeps for only the bedroom." Dani smiles and gets ready to duck any projectiles from the kitchen. However, Ukyo only smiles wide at her. "Patti was the one who got Blaise and I together. She also used to be my shopping partner. Let's just say that we both know what each has in their underwear drawer and maybe other drawers in the bed room." Dani goes scarlet red as Patti chuckles. Ukyo brings out a pot on a tray, with some cups on the side. The tea is served. "So, what are you here for, Sean? And whatever it is, you know his answer will be no." Ukyo didn't like to beat around the bush. As she sat down and poured her own cup of tea, she looked him right in the eyes and blasted him." Sean was in the middle of taking a sip of the tea when she did. He almost spit it out. "What makes ye think I'd be 'ere fer somethin'?" Ukyo looked at him knowingly. "Easy, I know you. I've seen how you work. You may be his cousin, but you're also the president of the federation we left recently. Seeing all the phone calls we've been getting, I'd take you to be the one to use it as an excuse for a family visit." Patti butted in. "Well, we heard about Global. Those tactics aren't good ones. Closing the place to null and void all contracts, and then re-negotiating is pretty much…" Ukyo nodded. "Blaise calls it a 'Vegas Rebuild'. Basically they couldn't afford all the million dollar contracts they had. Just like a lot of the casinos will do. They get rid of all of the union contracts, and everyone's benefits. Then they renegotiate to redo it all for less cost. They figure the desperate or stupid people will be all too happy to take their low-ball offers afterwards. We're neither desperate nor stupid. Most of the casinos who do that end up filing chapter thirteen soon afterwards, anyway." Sean tried to look interested in the explanation, but he wasn't much for visits as it was. However, Patti was all too fine if it suited her. "I see your wearing it?" Patti pulled Ukyo's left hand up and looked at the diamond and emerald ring that she was now wearing. It was the royal engagement ring that matched the one she wore from Sean. She looked at it as Ukyo tried to keep an eye on Sean's body language. Ukyo suddenly smiled though as she saw Blaise's Chevy Avalanche pull up into the driveway, blocked from it's normal parking spot by Patti's car. Couldn't call it Sean's, he'd not had a legal driver's license in years. Ukyo continued to listen to the two guests as she watched Blaise get the two girls out of the truck and head for the side door. "So, do you know how to use it yet?" Ukyo snapped back to Patti's face. Looking at her blemish free, fitness model looks, and her auburn colored hair she tried to make out what had been said and asked. "Do you know how to use the ring yet?" Ukyo suddenly picked it up, remembering the special properties of these rings. The pair was able to be used to spy on their intended mate when he was away from the home. They were to ensure the princes and kings of the Seelie Court in old Ireland didn't break their vows of faith to their intended or their spouse. "Yes, yes, I had a sneak peak a few months ago. It's just now something that I have to get used to wearing. After our Yui-no a few weeks ago, I don't think I've taken it off much." Patti smiled. "Good, good." Ukyo strained her hearing and heard Blaise had entered the house. She knew that his enhanced hearing would be able to pick up everything. "So, again, Sean, what do you want here? You're not one to just up and visit." Sean spoke before his wife could. "Well, if ye guys are free agents, I'd like t' ask ye both t' come back t' th' 4WW. An' maybe extend tha' same offer t' Dani an' th' Brand Brothers." Ukyo shook her head in a double take. The last thing she was expecting. Then she heard the voice from the pantry area that led to the side entrance. "Not bloody well likely!" Blaise walked in, half way still in his jacket. "I left there for a reason, Sean." Sean turned to face his younger cousin. "But the reason why ye left is gone. Kyo isn't there anymore!" Blaise looked like he was getting ready to blow already. Dani scooted her chair away from Sean and closer to Ukyo. "It wasn't just Kyo. It was everything. I was tired of playing second fiddle to people who couldn't lace my boots. I should have been North Wind Champion, by my own merits. I should have been the one facing Kyo, instead of Syckle. I should have been the one who buried that asshole! But instead it was someone who not only didn't get the job done, but fell to some curtain jerker along with him." Sean stood up. "Singleton has my full support as champion riogh' now. I 'ave t' support 'im. I need people like ye though. I'll make ye a promise: come back an' ye ga' Singleton a' Maelstrom IX. I'll sign it riogh' fokin' now." Blaise tossed his jacket onto one of the eight chairs. "Little too late there Sean. I'm not there anymore. I'm not doing it. What would that promise make right there? Aren't you the guy who was afraid that if I got a main event push, people would claim that you had done it only because I was your cousin?" Sean had no answer to that. "C'mon, Sean. What about GTT4? I was set, primed and loaded to win the Pyranese bracket. I could have gone on to face the other three and take the finals. I could have won the GTT4. I was only a couple of matches away. What did you do? 'Blaise, you're facing Tarrant. He's out number one contender. It would look bad if you beat him. Can you do me a favor?' Yeah, I did the job Sean. And look what it got you. Tarrant ended up being a big time loser. In more ways then one. If you would have just let me kick his ass then, and also gain his spot… you'd not be in the problems you are today." Patti tried to pull her husband back to his chair. "C'mon, Blaise. I made some fokin' mistakes! I want t' make them all good. I need people like ye two. I need people like Dani and 'er friends. We're all in th' Order. We're all in the Brigade…" Blaise got a stern look on that last one. "I am the Lieutenant assigned to the KnightMare Brigade: Native American Nations. Do you think your wife would relinquish the KB: Four Winds to me? Do you really think that the 4WW would be ready for me to come in and take what others think they've earned. They'll say I didn't. I have done the job for you so many times, I'm sick of it." Sean wasn't happy. "Yeah, okay, Blaise. Ye jus' go on an fokin' wrestle fer some other promotion then. What indy fed will it be this comin' week? Or 'ow abou' GTT5? Yer fokin' wrestlin' a chimp this week!" Blaise smiled. "I've wrestled worse." "Like what?" Blaise smiles in a wicked way. "Well, there was a guy in Global named Brick. And let me remind you that I've wrestled you before." Sean didn't have a comeback for that one. "And big deal? I'm wrestling a chimpanzee. He's smaller, but has about the same strength. They're a machine built to tear things apart for food and survival. They're not unlike the trolls I took out in that one ladies basement a few weeks ago. Same body design. Just those were eight feet tall, this is a lap animal for some little boy." Sean smiled at him. "An' if ye loose? You'll be a joke. Just like Jamie Shock. What has he done after loosing the UWF world title to that chimp?" "I'm not Shock. I'm not easy. This is an animal. We're all animals, really. But there is one thing that I am that others aren't." Blaise glared as Sean. "I'm a hunter. And hunters find and take their prey. Chimpanzees aren't the king of the jungle. This chimp has to worry about dealing with a predator… in more ways then one. Now, if your speech is done, you've got two choices… visit at family and don't talk business anymore. Or go get on your little private plane and head back to Seattle. But know this, I will never, ever wave your banner, ever again. Got it?" Sean just nodded as Patti held his hand. Ukyo spoke up as she could feel the tension in the room. "Well, now that you've all scared Dani's boobs a size smaller, and ruined her modeling career. I didn't make this tea to be tossed out. Blaise, where are the girls?" Blaise nodded his head in the direction of the back room the girls shared. Ukyo got up and headed into the room, as she passed Blaise she took his hand and turned to him. "What's up?" She whispered to him. Blaise smiled wide at his future wife, lavender colored hair and all. "Congratulations…. you're going to be a mommy. They're ours…" He trailed off as Ukyo finished heading to the back room. Only Blaise caught the lone tear that streamed down her face as she had started to turn away from him. These two, Blaise and Ukyo, had got where they were by standing up for their convictions. And it wasn't a time to stop doing just that.
