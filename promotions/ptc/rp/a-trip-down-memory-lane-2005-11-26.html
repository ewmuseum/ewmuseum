A trip down memory lane

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 It’s just another day below freezing in the Boston area. "I hate this fucking weather," Quite the usually weather for the day after Thanksgiving better known as Black Friday, and unlike many of the crazy people fighting the lines for the best savings, I’m just waking up and fighting the latest drama in my series called, "another hangover." The clock ticks 8:00 am and the big grandfather clock dongs just as my head pounds. "I always said I was going to get ride of that fucking thing." I hear myself say as I grab my head. The mail slot opens slowly and a handful of envelops come through. After they are forced through the door slams shut after it. My head starts ringing again; I can’t help but think every little sound is out to punish me, maybe to teach me a lesson. But I don’t think I will ever learn. After rubbing my head for a moment I slowly stumble towards the door using the wall as an aid as the room is still slowly spinning. I bend over and almost hit the floor but I’m able to stabilize myself before I hit. I pick up the numerous envelops on the floor and make my way back to the kitchen area. I pick up my cup of cranberry juice, which is my only chance of salvation from this hangover; I slowly sip it as I start to flip through the mail. "Bill" Flips to the next one… "Another Bill…" Flips past two or three more… "All I ever fucking get is bill’s, why don’t I get that letter from that guy who says I won a million dollars or something?" At this point I am getting agitated; I flip past two more bills until I come to an envelope with a return address with that of Primetime Central’s headquarters. "Finally something that looks important." In anticipation I slam my cup down on the near by counter with the rest of the bills. "Damn it, that even made my head start to hurt." I pull the nearest chair close by and take a seat. I slowly start to rip the letter up with each second the intensity is building. Slowly I unfold the letter anticipating this is my acceptance into GTT5, the annual tournament to separate the best of the best. "Dear Mr. Vetra, we are glad to say we accept you into the fifth annual Primetime Central GTT tournament. Congratulations." At this point I stop reading as I try and remember back a year ago, I slightly remember it but many of the parts are forgotten, but of those memories that I recall I see myself reacting the same way I did just one year ago. EXCITED… ANXIOUS… NERVOUS… Those are just some of the emotions I’m feeling but not enough to actually cover what I am feeling. I had just graduated from the Killer Kowaski School of Wrestling looking to break into professional wrestling, when I received a letter very similar to this one. The chance of a life time, I had been accepted into GTT4, the fourth annual event but more importantly my professional wrestling debut. Damn how good does it feel to have that behind me; I mean I was young and naive. I remember when I first entered the biz; the biz is what us "professional" wrestlers call the industry if you were one of us you’d know that though. Thought it was the break my career could use, and guess what I was right. I made it past the first round surpassing somebody by the name of, hell I can’t even remember his name I never heard of him again after that match. Then I lost the next round to Sebastian Moraco, what a shame, I haven’t heard much from him either. As a matter of fact, I don’t think he even entered this year. But you know what? My name Peter Vetra has been heard week after week on ReVolution, unlike theirs. So there is only one way to look at it last year was last year, this year is going to be different. How did this start my career? Well I might have been knocked out of round two but the fact of the matter is I signed with PRIME shortly after the tournament and I have been living happily ever since. This year I’m not looking to start my career, this year I’m looking to solidify my name with the rest of the A-lister’s of PTC. Like Alamasy, Oni, Wolfenden, and even that Paddy O’Shea guy, but not that Deville guy, I’d never want to be like him. I’m so close to doing that in PRIME, I mean I’m in the Imperium who is running the federation now it is just the time for me to do it in PTC. I see this as my chance, maybe my only chance to make my name in PTC. GTT5 Is my TIME!
