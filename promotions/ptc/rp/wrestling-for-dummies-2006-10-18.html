Wrestling for Dummies

18 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-18 Somewhere in the southwestern United States, lies a road. There is nothing particularly remarkable about this road. The pavement is black, the lines are yellow, and it is as straight as Wilt Chamberlain could have ever hoped to be. Surrounding this road is an endless sea of browns with a smattering of green. The road continues on over the unseen horizon, the heat from the day giving off a wavy mirage. From out of this heat induced phenomena a small, dark blip is seen. It grows slightly larger as it gets closer, revealing the outline of an automobile. As the car gets closer, it becomes apparent that it’s a green car. An old green car, it has a loud and rumbling engine. It tears across the otherwise peaceful desert, its engine a low pitched howl. This green Plymouth Fury, with her faded paint, rust spots, and worn tires seemed almost to blend in with the bleak environment that it was currently driving through. Sitting in the driver’s seat, Jason Payne draped one arm out the window, while steering with the other. Wearing black sunglasses, his shoulder length brown hair whips around in the wind as he drives down the road. To his right in the passenger seat is a man with a similar hairstyle, though his is jet black. He also wears black shades, though he is visibly shorter than Jason Payne. The man known as "The Wolf" Chris McMillan looks out the window at the desert landscape, his mind in deep thought. McMillan – "So have you heard who you’re facing in this GTT thing?" Jason turned to look at Chris for a moment before looking back towards the road. Payne - "Yeah, some guy by the name of AgentDash. He’s from AWC." McMillan nodded still looking out the window. Reaching up with his right hand, he scratched his chin for a moment. McMillan – "Let me guess, he’s a secret agent with a closet full of skeletons?" Payne just smiled as he glanced out his window for a moment. Payne – "Yeah something like that. Another gimmick wrestler who only weighs about a buck ninety. This kid has to jump around in the shower in order for the water to hit him." Both Chris and Jason share a laugh over that statement. Chris reaches down in the floorboard, pulling out a wrestling magazine and flips it open. McMillan – "Have any idea what you’re going to do to promote this match?" Payne sat silent for a moment before answering. Payne – "I haven’t decided yet, but I’m pretty sure some type of pop culture reference is going to get dragged through the mud in the process." McMillan laughed the way that only McMillan could, that low, boisterous laugh. A smile across his face, he continued to flip through the wrestling magazine. McMillan – "Dude, when you had that match against Yori Yakamo Jr., and you were spoofing Star Wars and said something about, "Move the ship out of the dildo fields so I can cut a clear promo!". That was seriously some funny shit man." Payne smiled, thinking back to that promo, keeping his attention on the road. Chris flipped the pages in the magazine. Coming to something that grabbed his attention, he pulled the magazine closer to his face. McMillan – "Hey Jay, there’s a write up in this magazine on GTT6. It has a breakdown of the brackets." Payne looked over at his old tag partner and arched an eyebrow. Payne – "What does it say about my bracket?" McMillan took a moment to look through the article. McMillan – "Says your bracket is the most heavily contested bracket and whoever comes out of it could be the favorite to take the whole thing." Payne nodded, agreeing with what the article said. Keeping his attention fixed on the road, he spoke again. Payne – "Does it make any predictions?" McMillan looked through the article some more. McMillan – Eh…the prediction out of the magazine lists Nova as its pick. Then the got some boxes on the side that have some fan’s opinions from magazine’s LiveJournal community." Payne – "Oh yeah? What do those say?" McMillan – "Uh…well the first one says that as long as anyone but Jason Payne comes out of that bracket they’ll be happy. Jason Payne is a no talent dick who can’t hack it in the big leagues of wrestling." Payne looked over at McMillan with an incredulous look on his face Payne – "Who the fuck said that?" McMillan pulled the magazine closer to his face in order to read the small print. McMillan – "I don’t know. Someone with the internet name ‘Morbius72’." Payne gave his partner the most quizzical of looks before turning back to the road. Payne – "The guy’s name is Morbius? Isn’t that the big bald black dude from The Matrix who was Cowboy Curtis on Pee-Wee’s Playhouse?" McMillan lowered the magazine and cast a disgusted look over at Payne, and slid over from Payne as far as the car would allow. McMillan – "No dude that was Morpheus. And I’m more than slightly perturbed that you watched Pee-Wee’s Playhouse and knew that." Payne shrugged and looked over at Chris. Payne – "A man has to know his pop culture references in this business Chris. If that upsets you, well, too bad I guess. What else do those internet things say? Anything else that mentions me?" McMillan turned back to the magazine and combed through the article again. McMillan – "Yeah there is one here from someone who calls himself ‘Spanky’ that writes, ‘The Pooh bracket is the most hotly contested bracket in GTT this year. That being said, out of all the great match ups to look forward to, the one I can’t wait for is the one that sends that piece of garbage Jason Payne out of the tournament. He has no business being in the same bracket as Killean Sirrajin.’" Payne’s face turned into a façade of indignant repulsiveness as McMillan read the excerpt. Payne – "I’m gonna kill all those bastards." McMillan looked over at Payne as if he were crazy. McMillan – "Dude. These people think that because they belong to a wrestling forum that they all of a sudden they know all there is to know about the wrestling business. Most of them are just skinny little kids with pink hair who like to think that the societal rules that govern our way of life are fictitious and don’t apply to them. Let it go man." Payne’s face was a mask of grim determination. Payne slammed his foot onto the accelerator and the Fury roared in response as Payne flew down the highway. Payne – "I can’t Chris. A man has to do what a man has to do." From overhead, sped up footage of the green Plymouth Fury streaking across the desert is shown. From there, we see a fingerless gloved hand reach out and push a doorbell. SFX: DING DONG From behind we see the backs of Jason Payne and Chris McMillan standing in front of the door to an average middle class house that could be anywhere in the United States. The locks on the door are heard clicking, and the door opens to reveal a girl in her late teens to early twenties with dark purple hair and black lipstick on. Payne pulls a huge pad of paper up and looks at the text. Payne – "Good morning, do you post as ‘Morbius72’ on LiveJournal?" The girl obviously bewildered looks at both men. Morbius72 – "Yes. I co-mod a wrestling community on LiveJournal. Why?" Payne hands the huge stack of papers to McMillan, who points to a line of text. Payne – "Did you post on LiveJournal that ‘Jason Payne is a no talent dick who can’t hack it in the big leagues of wrestling.’?" Morbius72 looks between both men again. Confused, she slowly nods her head. Morbius72 – "Yeah, I said that." Payne looks over at Chris with a smile on his face before looking back at ‘Morbius’. Payne – "Thought so." Payne reaches into his pants and pulls out an automatic pistol and points it at the girl. The girl screams and curls up on the ground in terror before Payne smirks and begins to put the pistol away. Payne – "Just kidding, it’s not real. But this is!" Payne kicks the girl in the side of the head, sending her reeling off the porch. McMillan joins in with Payne as they begin to kick the hell out of this girl. Another sped up shot of the green Fury speeding across the desert is shown. This time, it is going in the opposite direction of the previous shot. Then we see Payne and McMillan in front of another house. A kid with pink hair answers the door. Payne – "Do you post to the wrestling community on LiveJournal as ‘Spanky’?" Spanky - "Yeah, so?" Payne – "Did you say that Jason Payne ‘He has no business being in the same bracket as Killean Sirrajin.’?" Spanky breaks into a slight chuckle. Spanky – "Yeah…I remember saying that." Payne grabs a metal hook by the porch, swinging it and catching the pink haired punk right in the mouth, sending him to the floor. Him and McMillan then proceed to start kicking and hitting him repeatedly. Another shot of the Fury speeding across the desert is seen, this time heading back the original direction. Then we see Payne and McMillan walking up towards a house. McMillan has the huge stack of paper in his arms. As they walk up towards the house, another duo is seen walking up towards the same house at the exact same time. Both sets of guys get to the house at the same time, and the other pair is revealed as being Jay and Silent Bob. Payne looks them and then back to McMillan. Payne – "Do you believe this? It’s Jay and Silent Bob. What the hell are you two guys doing here?" Jay looks at Bob for a moment before addressing Payne. Jay – "We’re here to beat the living shit out of some little bitch-ass cocksmoker who wants to call us names on the fucking Internet. Yous?" Payne looks at McMillan with a surprised look before looking at Jay. Payne – "Holy shit! That’s why we’re here too! What a small world this is! I wonder which one of us should go first?" Jay looks at Bob and the huge stack of papers he’s carrying, and back to Payne. Jay – "Well, we’d like to get our hands on this little bitch if you don’t mind. We got our mother fucking movie premiere to get to and we gots all these names to get through. Then after that, I’s gotsta get home and get on LiveJournal myself because GTT6 is coming up, and I gotsta make my picks for myself and my tubby hunk of man love here." Payne looks at McMillan and arches an eyebrow. Payne – "Oh yeah? Me too. Who are you picking by the way?" Jay – "I don’t know man. Anyone but that little Payne faggot. His little bitch-ass couldn’t wrestle his way out of a wet paper sack." Jay began to cackle as Bob laughed with him, nodding in agreement. Payne looked over at McMillan, who only nodded. McMillan moved to punch Bob in the face, but before he could, Payne produced the pistol from his pants and plugged two shots each into Jay and Silent Bob. McMillan jumped back, obviously stunned. McMillan – "Holy shit dude! You just killed Jay and Silent Bob!" Payne looked at the smoking barrel of the gun, and then over at McMillan. Payne – "Snootchie bootchies, biatch." The next shot we see is of the green Plymouth Fury speeding across the desert into the sunset as we FTB.
