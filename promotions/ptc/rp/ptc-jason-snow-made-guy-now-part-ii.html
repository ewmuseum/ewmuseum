IG 32: I'm a Made Guy Now - Part II

2 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Freddy “Grab-Ass” Grobossi At the end of the day, nobody knew shit because that’s the way the bosses wanted it - all the way up to Nicholas Ramone, the head of the family. You don’t question a guy like that. Maybe they didn’t trust us to keep our mouths shut - I don’t know. But now that I think about it... the shovels - they were in the backseat, between me and Mikey. Jason Snow There were four of us in the car, on our way out to dig a hole in the woods. Five of us, I guess, if you want to count Dante Gasperoni. But he was in the trunk, dismembered, and wrapped in garbage bags. I was driving the car, but it wasn’t mine. The four of us met at a body shop run by some of the higher up wise-guys and picked it up. An old Cutless that would blend into traffic. Dante’s body was already hacked up by the guys there to make for easier carrying. I wasn’t used to jobs like this - normally it was other guys cleaning up my mess. Fernando Alissandro: “Alright, plates are clean. Drive out in the woods somewhere and get this thing done smart. I don’t want some fucking lovebirds finding a God damn hand sticking out of the ground on their morning walk.” Dino Avalano: “Order of operations: You get there, you dig the fucking hole, you take the body out, and you get your asses back here. That way, if someone comes walking along, they see you digging a hole, but they don’t see no dead body. And I want all four of you digging - even you, Mikey, “made” or not. The sooner you’re able to leave the scene the better. But whatever happens, you don’t take the body out until the hole’s been dug. That’s how guys get caught. Capice?" Fernando Alissandro: "Alright, Jason - I’m giving you this cellular phone. There’s one number on the speed dial, and that’s my number. You run into any problems - anything at all - and you use this phone to dial me. Mikey’s the button-man, so he’s in charge. Noah and Freddy are along for the man power. Your job is to carry the phone and make the call if need be. Alright? Now get the fuck out of here." Noah Sosiari See, what happened was, Mikey Hattoni (who was in the backseat) shot this girl down at a little card joint he ran. Some cold blooded shit, I guess. Girl was just fifteen or sixteen years old. And in front of a room full of witnesses to boot. I mean, everyone in the car had killed a guy or two, but a kid? That was some sick shit. Nobody in our thing endorsed that. Least of all Dante Gasperoni. See, the way things run in our thing is like this: At the top you’ve got your boss. The Don. He’s got his underboss and his consiliere and yada-yada. And under him, he’s got his team of captains, or caporegime in Italian. Under each Capo, you’ve got your soldiers. Button-men. Made guys. Now, in our thing, when you get made, that means you’re in the club. Nobody touches you no more. Mikey Hattoni was a made guy. So anyway, Mikey Hattoni was a button-man and his Capo was Dante Gasperoni. Dante heard about the shit with the girl, and he fucking lost it. And I mean lost it. Big bald fucker showed up at Mikey’s house and slapped the piss out of him right in front of his girl. Mikey still had the bruises on his face now. But remember what I said? When you get made, nobody can touch you. Not even a Capo like Dante. He could have whacked him, but you don’t put your hands on a made guy. The rules are very clear on this - it’s zero tolerance, and it’s a hanging offense. Nobody told us who whacked Dante or how they did it. The fewer people that knew that, the better. Mikey Hattoni Fat fucker should have known better, that’s the way I see it. You never put your hands on a made guy. Those are the rules and they’re there for a reason. Otherwise, what would be the sense in getting made? Anyway, the fat fucker was in the trunk now, cut up into pieces to make moving him a little quicker. I was in the backseat next to the shovels - fucking things should have been in the trunk. I took a flask out of my jacket and took a long, hard swig. “You want to cut that out?” Snow’s eyes snapped on me in the rearview mirror. He was driving. “Last thing we need is to get pulled over because you can’t stay sober for five fucking minutes.” “And you’re one to talk, Snow?” I leaned forward. “You drink like a fucking fish - everybody and their fucking mother knows that.” “Not when there are shovels in the back seat and a body in the trunk,” the son of a bitch hissed at me. “Put it away.” “Hey, fuck you!” I snapped at him. “Don’t forget where you’re at on the pecking order, alright blondie? I’m the guy in charge of this fucking thing and what I say goes, cocksucker!” I wasn’t about to be bossed around by some mick. I gave his mirror a hard look for good measure, and then added: “I’m a fucking made guy now, so just shut the fuck up and drive the fucking car.” Freddy “Grab-Ass” Grobossi Now a month ago, before he got made, Mikey Hattoni would never have talked to Jason Snow like that. I couldn’t believe he was doing it now. I mean, yeah, Snow was a mick, which automatically put him on a level a little lower than the rest of us, but he was also about the most dangerous motherfucker any of us had ever met. That’s what kept him alive all this time running with the Italians. And normally, when a guy talked like that to Snow, he wound up drinking his supper through a straw. But Mikey was made, and the whole reason we were driving was because of what happened the last time someone put their hands on him. Anyway, the mood obviously needed to be lightened. Jason Snow “So I hear you got a couple of matches coming up, Snow?” Freddy meant well, but I always hated when the guys talked about wrestling. It was embarrassing, because I constantly had to put up with the fucking jokes about the persona. Cracks about leather pants and orgasms. It was getting pretty fucking old, but at that point, I needed something to take my mind off tearing Mikey Hattoni’s throat out. “A couple of them,” I replied, never taking my eyes off the rearview mirror. The cocksucker was still drinking. I knew it was night time, and no cop could really see him back there - but still. There was a fucking body in the trunk. “That Ember guy, right?” “Jay Phoenix,” I corrected him. “Among others.” “Didn’t you beat him once?” Noah put in from the passenger seat. “Twice.” “Right,” he nodded. “So where’s he been?” Freddy asked from the back seat. “I mean, he used to show up in PRIME, right? But I ain’t seen him there in a while.” “Pissed off that they called him a fag in the papers or something,” I shrugged. “A fag!?” Freddy exploded, laughing. “They got you wrestling a fag? In them leather pants of yours? Shit...” His face became more serious. “He ain’t really a fag is he?” I shrugged again. “Better be careful, Snow,” Mikey was getting in on it now. “Fucker might get in the ring and then the ‘mere sight of you’ will make him blow his load all over your leather-clad ass.” They all got a good laugh at that. Noah Sosiari If Mikey didn’t shut his fucking mouth we were going to end up digging another hole for him. And then when the bosses found out, we’d be back again next week to dig one for Snow. I don’t mind telling you that it wasn’t a comfortable situation, despite the laughter. And worse yet, there were rumors that Snow actually knew the girl that Mikey killed, and wanted his head for it already. Some people even said the mick was banging the girl. On top of all this, Snow’s temper was fucking legendary. That made the situation all the more volatile. All the while, my mind kept drifting to Dante... I’d always had a soft spot for the guy, but I guess that’s how our business goes, right? You don’t bury your enemies - you bury your friends. Freddy “Grab-Ass” Grobossi So we got to the edge of the city and started looking for a nice dirt road. Snow wasn’t talking much anymore, but despite that, I think both Noah and I knew that he was getting pretty fucking close to slapping Mikey around a little bit. Not that I blamed him - Mikey could be an annoying little cocksucker, and he was a junkie to boot. Still, I didn’t want to be back here burying Snow this time next week for putting his hands on a made guy the way Dante did. It wasn’t fifteen minutes before we found one that was overgrown and out of the way. It went pretty deep into the woods, so we all got out of the car, grabbed shovels, and made the difficult trek down a short hill next to the road. There were some branches and leaves and shit that had fallen down there, so we figured it’d make some good cover for the grave. It didn’t look like nobody’d been out here in years. “Lets start digging,” said Jason, and he shed his jacket behind him. We all obliged, except Mikey, who seemed determined to be a fucking jack ass all night. He leaned against a half-rooted tree and pulled his flask out of his pocket. Snow gave him a hard eye, but he was smart about it and he kept his mouth shut. He started digging, and then me and Noah followed suit. Mikey - the fucking prick - he didn’t move a muscle. Mikey Hattoni What the fuck should I have dug for? I was a fucking made guy, not like the rest of these clowns. The way I saw it, Fernando and Dino could go fuck themselves. The job needed a button-man to make sure the shit went down smooth, and that’s what I did. Besides, the mick was a professional wrestler - I figured he liked working out and shit like that. Freddie “Grabb-Ass” Grobossi So we were digging the hole - no help from Mikey. He just sat there drinking and snorting coke and doing whatever the fuck else he felt like. I could have dealt with that, but this fucker had a mouth on him - always had. I never understood why it was they made him in the first place. “Should have never laid his hands on me like he did,” Mikey said quietly, but just loud enough for us to hear over the scraping of our shovels. He was smoking a cigarette and leaning on a tree, beneath its branches, hugging his body loosely and painted in sifted moonlight. He glanced back up the hill to where we’d left the car. “He knew the rules as well as anybody - better. Fucking Captain,” he sneered. I kept glancing over at Snow, who seemed to be taking his frustrations out on the ground. Every time he stuck the shovel in, it was a fair bit deeper than when me or Noah did. And if I had to guess, I’d say he was probably visualizing himself sticking the thing in Mikey’s chest. Everybody knew about the mick’s temper. “Just keep quiet,” I whispered to him, winning a quick glance. “We’ll be done here in a few minutes and we can pack him up in the car and get the fuck out of here.” The mick didn’t respond. He just kept digging. “But he’s always had a reputation for shit like that,” Mikey went on, and ironically, the moonlight caught one of his black eyes perfectly at that moment. “Fucking temper. Having a temper in this business?” Over my shoulder, I saw him staring directly at Snow as he clacked his tongue. “That’s a good way to get yourself killed.” “Cocksucker,” I muttered to myself, wishing the drunk prick would just keep his fucking mouth shut. The last thing I needed was to be explaining to one of the higher-ups how the one made guy on our trip didn’t come back with us. But to his credit, Snow kept his head down and kept digging away, sticking his shovel deeper and deeper into the ground each time. “Stupid cocksucker,” Mikey slurred loudly this time, looking back up to the car again. “Fucking bald-headed motherfucking prick! Laying his hands on me!? On fucking me!? I’m a fucking made guy! You don’t never lay your fucking hands on a-” “Will you please just shut the fuck up!?” I looked over at Snow, but he was still digging away. The explosion had come from the opposite side of me - Noah Sosiari. He speared his shovel down into our hole and turned to glare at Mikey Hattoni, wiping the sweat from his brow, and then he boomed: “For fuck’s sake, Mikey - if I have to fucking listen to this shit for another God damn minute, it’s going to be me laying my hands on you! And I’ll take the punishment just to put me out of my motherfucking misery!” Mikey took a step forward, carelessly tossing his cigarette to the side. My first thought was “evidence,” but maybe that was just paranoia, because in all likelihood the ground would freeze a time or two with the seasons before anyone ever found the body. “You know who you’re talking to, coc-” “I said shut the fuck up!” Noah flared. “Now pick up a motherfucking shovel and stop standing there like a fucking asshole!” Mikey Hattoni Can you believe this asshole? He was standing there, talking to me like I’m some kind of fucking jerk-off? And him so fucking green to the business that he pissed grass. I should have slapped the shit out of the little cocksucker right there, and I was about to, but that God damn mick interrupted me. “Enough!” he barked. “Both of you!” He grabbed Noah’s shovel in the opposite hand as his own and started marching back up the hill. “It’s deep enough. Lets get this over with and get the fuck out of here.” Jason Snow By the time we lugged the shovels back to the top of the hill, they were still going at it. Everything in me wanted to plant Mikey Hattoni in the grave right alongside Dante, but that wasn’t an option. No matter how fucking obnoxious he was, he still had the right of it - he was made, and I couldn’t touch him. Not without considerable risk to myself, and perhaps my family. That thought was the only thing keeping the junkie fucker on the right side of the ground. “This ain’t over between me and you, motherfucker, you here me?” Mikey wheezed at Noah, lagging behind the rest of us. He was out of breath. “This shit ain’t settled.” “Go to hell, Mikey,” Noah lazily snapped. I dug the keys out of my pocket and popped the trunk. Freddy “Grab-Ass” Grobossi It was the damndest thing - the trunk was empty. And I mean empty! Weren’t even any garbage bags in it. No blood neither. Nothing. We all kind of stopped in our tracks, staring into the thing until, finally, Snow exploded. “For fuck’s sake!” he boomed. “They must have sent us in the wrong fucking car!” “H-oly shit...” Noah said slowly, and then turned to the other guys. “Imagine the surprise Mrs. Alissandro’s going to get when she goes out to pick up her Monday morning groceries tomorrow!” It wasn’t even a joke. “This is real fucking bad,” I added. “Do you think anyone saw us at the body shop tonight?” “Lets get the fuck out of here,” Noah started for the passenger door of the car. “We’ve got to find out what the fuck’s going on before somebody gets busted.” “Nobody’s getting fucking busted,” Mikey scowled. “We lost... a fucking... BODY!” Noah boomed, taking a step toward him. “I told you once,” Mikey stepped into Noah’s face, “you don’t talk to me like that.” “That right?” “Fucking right.” “For the love of God, shut the fuck up!” it was Snow’s turn to explode, and his tone was so threatening that we all fell instantly silent. “Just calm down - I’m going to call Fernando and figure out what the hell’s going on here.” Noah Sosiari Man, I was going out of my fucking mind. I don’t know what you do for a living, but in my business, losing track of a motherfucking body is about as bad as shit gets. I could only hope that Fernando knew what was going on back in the city, but I don’t mind admitting I was shaking in my boots at that moment. Snow pulled out the cell-phone that Fernando had given him and found the speed dial. It seemed like an eternity went by in the time it took someone to pick up, but finally they did, and Snow began tearing into them. “Look, I don’t know what the fuck’s going on here, Fernando, but we just popped the trunk and-” His voice cut off mid-sentence. You know, it was hard to tell by the moonlight, but I swear to fuck the guy turned white as a ghost. “What?” He said into the phone, his voice shaken. I thought I was about to piss my pants. “You’re sure?” he said, and his eyes turned up at us. “What the fuck is he saying!?” I blurted. But Snow didn’t answer me. Instead, he sighed deep, and then his face hardened. And when he spoke to Fernando again, on the other end of the line, his voice was calm. “Alright.” Jason Snow The voice on the other end of the phone wasn’t Fernando Alissandro’s; it was Dante Gasperoni’s. I recognized it instantly. Calmly, I reached into the back of my jeans, pulled out my nine-millimeter, and fired a single round through Mikey Hattoni’s forehead. Just like that. Just that easy. He never saw it coming. I kind of regret that part. Freddy “Grab-Ass” Grobossi “Get him in the hole,” said Snow, nodding down the hill as he put his gun back. I think both Noah and I froze, unsure of what the fuck we should do. Mikey Hattoni, a made guy, just had his brains splattered all over the God damn forest. And here was the mick, calm as a fucking monk. You know, I still can’t be sure if we didn’t react because we were afraid of him, or maybe because we were kind of relieved that we wouldn’t have to drive home with Mr. “I’m a made guy.” Anyway, as it turned out, there was never really any hit on Dante Gasperoni. The big bald fucker was still as alive as ever. The real hit was on Mikey the whole time. We found out later that the higher-up wise guys got tipped off from a man on the inside that a witness had come forward after Mikey shot that girl, and the Feds were building a case against him. On top of that, Mikey was about the biggest fucking junkie in the city, and the Feds flipped junkies as easy as your old lady flips pancakes. So I guess that’s just the way it goes in our thing, you know? Guys get clipped. And they get clipped for all sorts of shit. Ain’t like we work at the candy store or the fucking DMV. We knew the deal when we signed up. So we started down the hill to bury Mikey Hattoni, the made guy. And it wasn’t even that hard - his body rolled halfway down on its own.
