Pain is always (Part 2)

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 In twenty four hours, my life continued. Facing the man that I had dreamed about just hours before. But I wasn’t thinking about the match. No. I was thinking about my life. “Why was I still going on?” The very question that I kept asking myself. Out of everything that had happened, I kept asking myself if I was supposed to be going on. My family tried first to get me to not go through with this dream. Next it was my mother who begged me to stop dreaming of a dream that wasn’t go in any shape that she could think of. Then it was my father. Not even the death of him could stop me…or it seemed. I could only think of the dream. Seeing my fathers face, filled with fear, did something to me. I couldn’t get over what it might mean and that scared me. My life was complete before all this. Before Thomas, before wrestling, and before high school. I was little and I didn’t even know how to decide on what to drink. Now I was deciding on my life. I stood in the bathroom. Holding a knife that I had gotten from the kitchen. My eyes, wearing a dark shade of color all the way around them as I held the knife in both my hands. In my heart it felt like my life wasn’t mine. I felt like my soul was dead and the death of my father only brought the anger that I had closer to the surface. I stood my ground, holding the blade towards my chest. A small tear rolled down my cheek as I lowered my head. I tried pressing it in but it felt hard. Like someone was pulling my hand away. I didn’t know what to do. I felt scared. Scared of failure and most of all, scared of being alone. I didn’t want the fame. I could care less about the money or the power that might be brought from wrestling. I only wanted to die. “Elia…” I head the voice. I turned my head to the door, forgetting about the blade that I was still holding. As I turned, the blade slid across the other hand that was holding the spot on my chest. A sudden cold feeling rushed throughout my body and regret took over. “AWEE!” I screamed out as I feel back against the door. The red colored blood rushed down my hand and to the white floor that I stood tall on. What did I do? I reached over, grabbing the towel that was hanging on the wall and wrapped it around the cut. I could still heard Thomas coming up the stairs. What was I to tell him. I didn’t want him to see me this way. I placed the knife on the counter and turned on the bathroom tub. The blood was everywhere and the towel was covered. I felt dizzy. Almost seeing double as I sat on the toilet. Leaning back against the seat support, I looked up at the ceiling. I felt like passing out but I knew I had to stay awake. The pain form the cut was starting to set in. Not the felling of suicide wasn’t the path I wanted. “Where are you Elia?” Thomas’s voice sounded concerned. And he had a right to be. My dream scared him even more then it did me. I laid there. Still as I could be as the feeling of passing out came closer. The towel was more red then white. My eyes felt heavy and the sound of the water drowned the other noises in the room. I didn’t want to go out like this anymore but I missed my father. I missed the life I used to have and would give anything up for it. My eyes slowly went closed, going out, and almost crossing over. ---------------------- The pain in my hand was gone. I stood tall. Standing in a white room that I had no idea how it got there. I couldn’t feel anything. No pain, no regret, nothing. I turned round from the wall. Still wearing everything that I was. Where was I? That was the question that I asked myself. I must be dreaming but why was I dreaming here. As I turned, I saw the form of a man. His face was familiar and it gave me a happy feeling. A set of dark brown eyes locked on to mine and for once, I felt nothing else but an overwhelming balance of happiness. I knew the man. His name was the reason I was here on this earth and the reason that I tried to comment suicide. He was my father and this time he wasn’t scared. He stood next to the very tree that was in my dream earlier. I couldn’t understand what was going on but I knew that there must be a reason. “Elia,” My father said. His voice was still strong. Not the voice that he had as he passed away, trying to say my name but unsuccessful. I walked forward. Walking right towards him. This place wasn’t like anywhere I had every felt in. No hate was present. No feeling’s but of what you make. He seemed to be happy now. He wasn’t the same as he was when he lived. I could feel that and I knew were I was. I was dreaming and maybe out of all the pain and aggravation that I felt, maybe god granted me the last chance to say goodbye. “Father,” I screamed out as I got to him. I wanted to reach out and pull him in but as I did I got nothing. My hands went right through. Like he was a ghost even in the dream world. But to just see his face made me feel happy. “Why do you feel pain Elia?” My father’s question surprised me. It was as if I didn’t expect him to know I was in pain. But what do I say? Do I tell him that it is him that I feel pain? Do I lie? I lowered my head for a second as I thought. “I need you back father. I need you around. I can’t live without…” Before I could get those words out, my father smiled. Placing his hands on my shoulders and pulling me closer. I was surprised. Shocked that I could feel him. I held him tight. Got it felt good to feel his warmth. I didn’t want to leave. I wanted to stay here with him forever. But as I did, the white color that the room was in added an extra one. The very same hand that I had cut turned a bright red. What was going on? I pulled away from my father. Looking down at my hand I noticed that the hand was solid red. The pain that I had felt was starting to come back. My happiness feeling soon changed to a scared feeling as I looked up at my father. “What the..” He was gone. His body hand vanished and the room soon began to change. The white walled place was brought to the red color that my covered hand was. I soon felt scared as I turned and ran away. Why was I running? I could not go anywhere but it was the only thing that being scared made me do. --------------------- “ELIA!” My eyes shot open. Looking up at Thomas who held me in his arms. I was awake and the pain in my hand was all through out my body. I had been dreaming but what did it mean I could not see. I looked at my hand, wondering what I had done as I kept thinking to myself. Did I want to die or did I want to live? The question was mine and mine alone. As closed my eyes as Thomas held on to me as tight as he could. I knew the pain would never go away. I knew it was forever. Pain always is.
