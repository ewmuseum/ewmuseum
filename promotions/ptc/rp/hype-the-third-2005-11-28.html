Hype the Third

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 To put it mildly, it’s been a bad year since the last GTT tournament for me. Let’s see, where do we begin? Well, I suppose we can start with losing to Craig Goat. Scrawny little thing, just like me. Next to no professional wrestling experience. And yet, he pinned my shoulders to the mat for a three count. Hey, just proves that anything can happen. The sort of upset that makes things interesting. And hell, that’s what it did. I thought it was a fluke at first. Shrugged it off, and regrouped to make my attempt at defending my Cruiserweight Tournament crown. And it happened again. Another defeat to an unknown. In the first round, no less. But hey, wrestling’s not all there is in life, right? I’m not Chris Storm, after all. So of course, the bottom choice that moment to drop off. Since CW2…oh God. I’ve discovered that vampires are in fact very real creatures. The woman I loved more than anything fell for one. I went to GCW in the hopes of a new start, only to run into an enemy from my past who wanted to settle the score. I went to PTC’s Super Series with the hopes of becoming the Global Champion or the Extreme Champion. Neither of those happened. But you know what? I didn’t lay down. I’ve pulled myself back to my feet one more time, because I’ve got so much to go on for. Soon, I take my chance to become PTC’s first ever Triple Crown Champion against Hoyt Williams in the Cloister of Trials. Love has given me another chance, in the form of Tessa Windsor. Hey, if you thought she had special powers before, just imagine what she can do with me managing her career. Among other things. But I digress. Equally importantly, though, is GTT5. My chance at redemption. Some people look at me and ask why I feel I need it. I’ve got two PTC tournament victories, they tell me. Back to back. They wonder what else I have left to prove. The answer to that is "everything". In this sport, if you rest on your laurels, you have no reason to compete. You’ve won a world title? Congratulations. There are others to win. Become PTC Global Champion? Great! Now hold onto it. Won a tournament? Awesome, now try to win another. That’s the way I look at it. You always have to set goals for yourself. Always have to reinvent yourself. Seymour Almasy today is a wrestler. But he’s much, much more than that. He’s also a manager. A loving boyfriend. An owner of one of the largest collections of PTC action figures in the world. (Oh, I finally found that Angelo Deville figure. FUCKING SWEET LIKENESS!) And with skill, determination, and a little bit of luck, Seymour Almasy will once again be a tournament champion. Now, if you’ll excuse me, I have to go. Tessa wants to go shopping. … First person to call me a bitch is getting dropped on their fucking head.
