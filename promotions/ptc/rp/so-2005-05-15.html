So?

15 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-05-15 When signing her life away to the world of wrestling, there were quite a few things Tessa Windsor never expected. She never expected to live a life of luxury, and of course she was right on that behalf. Her three-bedroom estate back in Chatham was hardly anything deviant from your usual family home. Her modes of transportation differed none from your typical American mothers. There was the silver Ford Windstar for days out on the town with the kids, and a Honda Civic in a matching color for nights out with Chris. The only things that set her apart from your average mother of two were her age and her profession. It's difficult enough to raise two kids who had two constant people in their lives, their mother and their father. Christa and Evan lacked that sort of luxury due to road schedules and the rocky relationship between Tessa and Chris. As of now, the two hadn't seen their adopted mother for almost ten days. In their little minds it almost seemed as though Tessa just stopped caring., but that was far from the actual truth. For two years now Tessa had woken up every morning, regretting the fact that she could never be half the mother everyone expected her to be. Yeah, she had the money needed to provide the children with a decent lifestyle; yeah, she had all the love in the world for those children, but what she didn't have was the time or experience. The last thing she ever expected at age twenty-one was to mother not one, but two children. Yet she had to be; it's what her brother requested before his twenty-eight years of inexperience had been tragically ended. After all she'd been through, it almost seemed appallingly wrong for someone to go and add to her misery, yet things only snowballed for the diva of WIW and PCW fame. She'd been engaged, almost married, wrongly fully married, and dumped, all in the course of the year that followed. Then to top things off, her performance in the ring started to slip, and the critics started to dig into her credibility. After all of that, it seemed perfectly logical for Tessa to want to quit, which is exactly what she did, until recently. Now, she regretted taking the bait, tossed to her by her good friend Dee. Sure, she was happy about managing to defeat Joshua Freedom and RAD to make it to the third round of the annual Primetime Central Cruiserweight Tournament, but what she wasn't happy about is the turmoil it managed to bring about. Right about now she could have been home curled up in the comfort of her own home with her children and their adoptive father, enjoying the rare moment of absolute happiness. Instead, she found herself tucked inside the royal blue sheets of yet another one of her associates' guest rooms, wondering why she felt the urge to right the wrongs of her former (quite against her own will) husband, Gaz Green. "Rise and shine," the cheery voice of Dee, one of Tessa's closest friends and mentors, proclaimed as she popped up like a sprouting plant from the twin bed beside the one Tessa had crashed in, with a sudden burst of energy that Tessa only wished she had this early in the morning. Instead, Tessa found her baby blues struggling to brush off the weight of sleep as Dee silenced the alarm beside the two, while the lighting of the clock now changed the time to 5:01."Five more minutes, please?" "Do you know what could happen in five minutes?" harassed the dainty blonde in a devilish manner, as she slightly cracked open the blinds. Her current personality fit her ruby-colored lips just as well as it did the red silk that wrapped around her contrasting colored skin, serving as nightgown. Right about now, she was more of a devil to Tessa than the little Green cruiserweight who in her mind had gotten her into this whole mess. "Of course I do," Tessa replied in a muffled tone as she latched onto one of the cotton stuffed pillows beneath her and found a common alternative use for it, a light shield. "But unless you want me locked away for life, I suggest another five." "Please, you wouldn't hurt a fly," Dee remarked as she yanked back the foot end of the royal blue comforter that Tessa had nestled under, causing an ear-piercing shriek to erupt from Tessa's lungs as the crisp morning air collided with her bare arms. "Fine, you've got me beat." Tessa surrendered, then climbed out of bed to fetch her hooded pink fleece to not only fight the cold, but to conceal her disheveled brunette locks. "Good, now hit the shower," Dee commanded, as she went back to work on Tessa's bed, only this time actually covering the bed, rather than stripping it of it's sheets. "The earlier we get it over with, the better." "Says you," Tessa argued back, as she rummaged through her duffle bag in search of what to wear. Not that it mattered anyways, but it something to do to pass the time needed to fully wake up. "Just get going." Dee commanded once more, "I want to get this over as much as you, so we can get out of here." "In that case, I'll be out in twenty," Tessa replied, as she ditched the mess of clothes she'd left beside her bag, and instead made her way to the bathroom. Sure, Champ (as they called him) was a great associate in the business, and neither minded the ritzy lifestyle his house offered, but quite frankly neither of them felt all too comfortable around the guy, knowing that he'd expect them to return the favor for one night of luxury. "Ten," Dee haggled, treasuring a chance to get out of here before Champ woke up and they'd be expected to carry out his orders. "You've got it." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ By the time Dee and Tessa had gotten out of Champ's oversized estate, the sun had fully risen and there was a mild amount of life in the form of churchgoers, an awkward sight in the city of Las Vegas, the city of sin, despite it being a Sunday morning. Dressed in their Sunday best, none of them seemed like they belonged in a city such as this, where scantily clad women were a tourist attraction. Then again, it wasn't as if Tessa felt like she belonged here either, despite her being recognized as another one of those bare-all women, even if she'd never done such a thing. "You know Dee, maybe we should just forget this whole thing and just book it." Those were the first words that fell out of Tessa's mouth as she fixed her eyes on the brick building that housed her one time husband. Initially, Dee had thought to agree with Tessa. There wasn't anything in this for her, aside from a night in Vegas, and even that wasn't anything special. Then it crossed her mind that this wasn't about her, but Tessa. It was about taking the potential Tessa had to be an outstanding individual who fought her own battles, rather than a puppet on strings held by others. "I'm sure Chris would love to hear that story." "What do you mean?" The mention of her beloved partner managed to spark a bit of interest back into the eyes and mind of Tessa. "I'm sure he'd love to hear about how you took off on him and kids for a week, just to back away from what you promised to do on your time away," Dee rallied in attempts to raise Tessa's spirits some, without tearing into her too much at the same time. "Yeah, you've got a point," Tessa responded as she drug the pair of stripped pink Adidas Superstar sneakers that covered her feet through the piles of dust that had collected on the polluted sidewalks of the city. "That's more like it." Dee grinned as she started to walk in the opposite direction of her smaller colleague, which seemed to catch Tessa by surprise. Stopping dead in her tracks, Tessa shouted over towards the bubbly blonde who had gotten her this far, hoping it pulled Dee back in her direction. "Aren't you coming with me?" "You can do this on your own," was Dee's response, as she plopped herself on a nearby bench, littered with both current and previous editions of the local newspaper "I'll be waiting right here for you when you finish." "Some help you are," muttered Tessa under her breath, in a manner that made it difficult to distinguish whether it was her being sarcastic, or quite angered. Dee, who was probably about as nervous as Tessa at moment, brushed off the fact that she'd just deserted her friend in her time of need and instead did the only thing she could think to do: cheer Tessa on. "Go get 'em, girl." Wasting not another second, Tessa inhaled a large portion of air containing god only knew what, and hoped for the best, all while expecting the worse. Fortunately, or unfortunately as some including Tessa might have saw it, Tessa had found just the door she was looking for, without needing to go any further than past he lobby and to her first left. Now, it was time to take care of business. Well, here it goes, Tessa thought as she reached out to the large wooden structure with the number one (something that probably only added to the enormous ego of the Prince of Pranks) painted in black. Rapping on the door with her small fist three times, she hoped the entire process would be this quick and painless. As expected, there was sign of life on the other side of the door, as the wooden floor underneath the elegant carpeting creaked slightly. Within a matter of seconds Tessa was greeted by the one person she'd hoped she'd never have to deal with again after this. With the door half open, Gaz poked his head through the door, seemingly unaware of just who was standing before him as the clock stuck seven. "What is it you could want?" started the Prince of Pranks who had quite obviously had a late night out, judging by the bags under his eyes, who only stood no more than four inches taller than Tessa. "We need to talk," was the only thing that managed to survive the trip from Tessa's brain, to her glossy pink lips. Still, the annoyance in her voice rang out in his mind, and instantly he made the connection in his mind as to just who he was dealing with at this early hour. "OH SWEETCHEEKS, LONG TIME NO SEE!" Though the excitement in his voice sounded like it could hardly be contained, Tessa knew his only reason for doing this was to elicit some kind of dramatic response from her, which would only set her up for further harassment. "And let's hope it stays that way after this," Tessa cut in, lacking the same sort of enthusiasm carried by her one-time husband. "Playing hard to get, are we?" retorted the Emerald Menace with a chuckle as he leaned his five-foot-nine frame against the edge of the doorway. "Hardly," replied Tessa in disgust, giving him the exact kind of answer he needed to continue on with his tormenting. Jumping on the opportunity to win yet another battle of wits with the woman who he'd unintentionally married, Gaz went ahead and threw out the expected response. "Then why is it you've come crawling back this time my darling? Is 'The Storm' not living up to his expectations in the sack?" "That's not for you to worry about," she responded in a surprisingly calm nature for someone in the presence of their worse nightmare come true. "I knew it! I'm right!" Gaz continued on, generating a slight roll from Tessa's baby blues. "If that will keep you quiet for more than a second, than yes you're right." Tessa gave in and sighed as she angled her slender frame against the wall parallel to Gaz's doorway in attempts to get comfortable as she waited to win her minute of time to speak her mind. "That's what I thought," Gaz proclaimed as he held his head of spiky of green hair high. "Now, what can I do for you today my beloved ex?" "I want you to pull those tapes immediately, and finally admit that those rumors that you've gone spreading are false," Tessa demanded, fully expecting him to know what she was taking about, though she had little proof that it was actually him, other than the fact that it sounded likes something he'd do. "Tapes?" inquired the much more successful half of the very short-lived tag team, Team Green. "You mean you've gone and pulled a Paris Hilton with our honeymoon and didn't let me know about it?" “Don’t play stupid with me. You know what I’m talking about.” Snapped back Tessa, finding little humor in him even suggesting that the two laid in the same bed, let alone pulled a Hilton. “Do you think I’d lie about this sort of thing?” Questioned the Prince of Pranks, actually seemingly half serious for once, if only because he had better things to deal with than her nagging. “I mean come on now, when have I ever denied something of this sort?” He had quite a point, a point that was good enough for Tessa to bring back to Dawn in her reasoning for not putting up more of a fight. With that pointed out, Tessa had to give the worse possible kind of response to her nemesis. “You’re right there.” “Of course I am.” Grinned the Jester, as he glided his hand through his hair as if this were reason enough to Tessa to break down and go “LIKE OMUHGAWD GAZERETH, I LOVE YOU! HAVE MY BABY!” But, it wasn’t, though Gaz would probably say otherwise. Instead, it only thing it left her wondering one thing. If it wasn’t Gaz, then who was it?
