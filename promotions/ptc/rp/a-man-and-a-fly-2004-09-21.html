A Man and a Fly

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 September 20th, 2004 Houston Texas The Evers Mansion 6 PM Eastern Standard time As I sit here, watching a trailer for the soon to be hit horror movie “Saw”, I can’t help but notice this horrible buzzing sound that is coming from the right side of my computer desk. The trailer ends and I spin my chair so that I am facing the right side of said computer chair. There it is, in all it’s glory. Complete with a set of wings that are, at the moment, motionless; it’s two little hands are rubbing together. I can only think back to what my third grade teacher Mr. Watson told us that one day during science class. “Now, boys and girls, there is something important that you should know about the behavior of flies. He started off saying. You have to understand that Mr. Watson was a robust man with a fading hair line and he tended to sweat profusely when lecturing for too long. Believe me, despite only teaching third graders, this guy loved to lecture. “Whenever a fly lands, even if only for a few seconds, it tends to excrete waste. So, having said this, if is for your own health that if a fly should happen to land on your food, you dispose of it in a proper trash receptacle.” It was until this very moment that I ever actually decided to heed his advice. Despite the fact that the lean turkey breast with a touch of mayonnaise and a slice of cheese was less than halfway eaten, I knew that I had to just let it go. None of us really want to eat a sandwich that is covered in fly, er, crap. At that very moment I knew that I was going to seek revenge on that fly. I loved that sandwich, I was really enjoying it since I hadn’t gotten a chance to eat that entire day. I had been thinking about a fax I had received earlier in the day. 3 hours earlier I sat there going over some paper work for Wrestling Midwest. I was the Vice President and in having that position within the company, I had some responsibilities that I had to take care of on a day to day basis. One of my tasks was to monitor of talent to see who deserved to move up the ranks and what not. Suddenly, my fax machine began to go crazy, it spat out a cover page and a few adjoining pages before coming to a sudden quiet hault. I wheeled over to the machine to see what exactly was the cause of all the ruckus. I had gotten a fax from AWS Man, my stable mate and the President of Wrestling Midwest. He was reminding me that some time during my busy schedule I had to make sure to cut a promo for the GTT4 tournament that I had entered myself into. “Man, I’ve been so busy with Wrestling Midwest that I almost forgot about GTT4. I guess it didn’t help that I was one of the fortunate ones who got a first round bye. I would have rather wrestled in the first round so that entering the tournament and being accepted would have been fresh in my mind. Speaking of freshness, this could be a fresh start for me in the PTC world. So far I have been involved in three PTC related events. The first one was the Chris Williams Memorial Cup in which I didn’t fare that well. I was eliminated relatively early when my fued with Noah Hanson bubbled over. Next up was my match with Kenji Lee. Kenji was from Primetime Central member federation Global Wrestling. He was looking for a good challenge and I was more than happy to step up to the plate. I handily defeated him, he joined Wrestling Midwest and the WMW slash GW saga was launched. Global Wrestling came out on top of that battle and I was rather disappointed that Wrestling Midwest lost the main event that I was a part of.” Alright, back to that damned insect. 3 Hours and 5 Minutes Later I decided that I should dispose of the sandwich right away in hopes that without the presence of food in the room, the fly might leave me alone to finish my internet endeavors. After that I was set to do some sort of promo for my match with Jamie Fletcher. Just as planned, I got out of my computer chair and walked the paper plate that was the house to my poop riddled sandwich to the garbage. I flipped it upside down and watched any reminder of my tasty snack go plummeting to its un-eventful death. Can a sandwich really have an un-eventful death? Well, I’m used to Spatulas who are deputy Presidents, so don’t mind me. “Now that I got rid of that stupid fly and that sandwich that would have been oh so delicious, sans the excrement, I can get back to what I was doing.” I know that they say it’s not healthy to talk to yourself, but the sound of my own voice helps me feel safe and secure. What originally seemed like a full proof plan was starting to unravel very quickly. I was trying to check my email to see if Wrestling Midwest had any new recruits that I might want to fly to Cleveland and check out when suddenly that horrible buzzing came back. This time, it was ten fold. I guess that fly just wanted to ruin my afternoon. It wouldn’t take a whole lot to do that, I have a very violent temper. I very quickly slammed my hand right where the fly had just been when I glanced over. I yelped in pain and turned my hand over so that my palm was facing up. Not only did I find out, to my dismay, that the fly had gotten away; but my palm was absolutely red and throbbing with pain. I tried to go about my business of checking my email, acting as if nothing had happened. There it was again, this time it seemed like it was resting on my ear lobe. This particular insect sounded like a car sitting at the entrance to a tunnel, revving it’s engine through a PA System. The constant BZZZZZZZZZZZ BZZZZZZZZZ BZZZ sound. I began to go insane, or at least what I think insane would be if I ever went there. My eyes get a blood shot red color, I begin to tug at my six inch long brown hair. The worst thing in the world is trying to concentrate on the task at hand and having a fly constantly buzzing in your ear. It’s quite distracting. Finally, realizing that I can’t handle another minute of this, I grab the fax that was sent to me earlier. Rolling it up every so carefully, I stalk my prey. This fly is going to wish it never met me. Well, it won’t wish anything because it is going to be nothing more than the splatter remains of what once was an annoying pest. That, of course, is very similar to what I do to my opponents in Wrestling Midwest every show that is broadcast. Not that I would be the type to brag or anything. I went into this big dramatic over my head wind up before sending this rolled up piece of fax paper crashing down on this unsuspecting fly. I knew that there was no way this sucker survived the piece of paper since it was a lot wider than my hand and I was able to send it down with a lot more force. Victory, it was sweet, I had tasted it before. I lifted up the piece of fax paper expecting it to be covered in little blotches of blood, the ultimate sign of victory. I let out a loud howl when at the precise moment that I lifted up the paper, the fly began to buzz again. What the heck was a guy to do? I can’t even describe how much louder it seemed to buzz this time. It felt like it was right in my ear drum, just eating away at my sense of hearing. If I didn’t end this soon, I was going to go deaf. This was the only thing that seemed to make sense in this day of madness. At long last, the one thing that could keep my sanity and my ability to hear what went on around me hit me. I opened up a filing cabinet and begin to rifle through my CD collection. I pulled out one that I wanted to listen to, The Battle of Los Angeles by Rage Against the Machine. Surely, with head phones on there was no way this fly could possibly invade my sanity. It’s amazing how the most obvious things are oblivious to us for the longest time, thus is one of the flaws of the human race I guess. September 21st, 2004 Cleveland Ohio Wrestling Midwest’s Studio B 24 hours after the fly incident; 6 PM local time On the way here, I had picked my brain on what I was going to do for my promo against Jamie Fletcher. I wanted to make a good impression, this tournament was a great opportunity for anyone. Even if I didn’t come close to winning, I would get rewarded for being the last Wrestling Midwest person in the tournament. When it all came down to it, I decided that it wasn’t how I presented my case on why I was going to hand this guy his ass. Instead, it was what I said that was going to have the most dramatic psychological effect on this guy. If there was any chance of me causing this guy to ‘shake in his boots’, I figured that I should do it in a simplistic yet familiar setting. No chance of guerilla flies or other distractions that could knock me off my A-game. I walked into Studio B as I have done a thousand times. My friend Jim whom I had just got a job in the talent relations department walked up to me to see if I needed anything. “Justin, hey man, how was your flight?” Are the first words out of his mouth. “Jim, good to see you are fitting in nicely around here. My flight was ok. I had a little incident with this fly from hell yesterday. Other than that, I’m doing ok. I’m ready to get this show on the road so that I can say what I have to say about Jamie Fletcher.” Jim directed me to the backstage area of the WMW show with Benny Tyler. For those of you out there who have never seen the WMW show, which is probably the vast majority, I will explain it to you briefly. Benny Tyler is a staff member of Wrestling Midwest, he has a show for WMW wrestlers to go on and talk about their match. He does a little Q and A and things usually develop from there. He’s like mixture between all the late night talk show hosts, but much cooler. The director counts down from five and when he gets to two I can hear my theme song “When Worlds Collide” by Powerman 5000 booming through the studio When I hear all the boos from my adoring fans, I know that it’s my cue to head out into the studio. Benny gets up out of his chair to greet me like he has probably at least twenty times since I came to Wrestling Midwest in June 2003. We do the usual hand shake thing and Benny motions for me to sit in a chair next to his desk. “Welcome back, Justin.” Benny says. I reply with “It’s good to be back as usual Benny.” He gives me a half smile and then gets a little more of a serious look on his face. “Now, Justin, you are preparing for your second round GTT4 match. This could be a good opportunity for you to really show what you are made of. You can shut up all the critics who think you will be nothing more than a low mid-carder when it comes to ‘the big time. What do you have to say to this?” Benny brought up a good question, did I think that I could make it in a tournament with the enormous variety and amount that a tournament like GTT4 held? I sure was going to try as I was about to tell Benny. “You know Benny, everyone has always counted me down my entire career. I’m used to it by now. I don’t have to tell you what I have done in the past because you know. I don’t have to regurgitate it for all these people, because they know. I don’t have to bore Jamie or anyone else with it because I know that they don’t care. I have proven over the course of the last few years that when people doubt me, I rise to the occasion. I don’t care what the stage is. It can be a house show or the main event of the biggest pay-per-view ever. I go in the same way, knowing that I will wrestle my best and more times than not come out on top. This Jamie Fletcher is new to me, I have never wrestled against him. I have seen him cut one promo and that’s it. At the same time, he probably knows very little about me too. I’m just this guy from that little federation Wrestling Midwest that everyone looks at and laughs. That’s fine with me though, most of us boys at WMW prefer it that way. It’s easier to catch our opponents off guard.” “Well put Justin, based on the one promo that most of us saw Jamie cut for the first round of this tournament, we saw that he is a master of the mind games. Him and some of his co-workers pulled off this whole bag of trickery against some fans. You are one of the masters of psychology here in WMW as you have proven time and time again. What impact do you think the weapon of psychology will play in this match?” “Well Benny, I will admit that was a great rouse that those fans were victim to at that show. I don’t know if you could pull that off as easily on Wrestling Midwest fans, the English seem to be kind of gullible. When you think about it though, wrestling plays a huge part in a wrestling match. Even if you aren’t aware of the importance of psychology and the fact that it can affect the outcome of a match, every wrestler still uses it at every time. When I think about this match with Jamie, I know that getting a psychological advantage at the outset of the match is going to decide the winner. Whoever can get into the other persons head the sooner that bell rings, that is who will have the upper hand.” “In this type of situation, do you stray from your game plan or do the same things you always do?” I knew Benny was going to ask me this type of question, it was just the way he was. That’s one of the reasons I decided to do this interview with him, I knew exactly what he was going to say and how I could use it to my advantage in getting my point across to Jamie. I knew this was going to be the last question before Benny said it was time to wrap things up, just watch. “Well Benny, that is a question that you have asked me a thousand times in a thousand different situations. I will use pretty much the same strategy I always use. There will be one exception. I will see what I can get my hands on as far as tapes of this Jamie guy wrestling. If I notice things in my arsenal that can counter things that he likes to do, then I will capitalize on that. You see Benny, I’m in this tournament for the fun. I want to see where I match up against some of the best guys in the wrestling world. If I lose to Jamie, I just continue to dominate in Wrestling Midwest. However, Jamie, don’t think I’m going to go down without a fight. There is no way that will ever happen. When you are starring up at those shiny ring lights wondering what went wrong, just remember this. I want you to swallow your pride and Wear Your Grudge Like A Crown. “Thank you for your time Justin, thank you to all the viewers for viewing in as well. This has been Benny Tyler hosting the WMW show with Benny Tyler. Good night.” As Benny finishes his farewell, the scene fades to black with ominous buzzing heard in the background.
