Happy Holidays from James von Doom James von Doom opened his eyes in the epicenter of destruction. His studio apartment had fallen victim to the tornadoes known as laziness and disorganization. Von Doom clutched his head while slowly rising, another night of sleeping on the hard unforgiving surface of a Jack Daniels bottle didn't do his ailing neck any favors. There wasn't one hint of evidence that beneath the layer of filth laid a respectable parquet wooden floor. The three week old Domino's pizza boxes he used for cover during the cold New York nights fell off his back when he reached a fully erect position. A pounding at his door woke the dilapidated beast who lived in a constant loop of hard times for the last five years. After the last major incident of home invasion in a neighborhood occupied by gangsters, von Doom was given no choice but to pack up and move into the only complex which would accept him and his reputation, Imitation Apartments. Stepping over twenty inch piles of garbage and two homeless individuals, von Doom maneuvered his way to the front door and opened it. Who would want to see such a pathetic human being in his natural habitat? “RENT.” The man who gave him the chance to be in a habitat at all. He is short in height but packs one of the most devastating clotheslines ever seen in wrestling. He rolls with two utter imbeciles known as Beef and El Janito and sometimes hangs out in the wrestling organization known as PRIME. He speaks one word at a time with a voice so deep that Barry White used to cry himself to sleep at night. Steve the Rambling Midget (Landlord) Communist. “Hey man, how's it going?” “RENT.” “Oh...yeah....about that, I don't have it today. Give me until next Friday.” “RENT.” “Can't help you out, sorry.” “DEATH.” Steve took a small step forward and clotheslined von Doom in the only place that his reach would allow, the package. The sheer force of the blow made Joe Rogan leap from his chair many miles and away and shout, “WWWWWWWWWWWOW” for a reason that befuddled his ignorant house guests. Von Doom fell to the floor in agony and slipped quickly into unconsciousness. Thirty Minutes Later With an icepack made of ice and paper towels stuffed in his five day old boxers, von Doom stepped out into the dimly lit hallway of the basement. He elected to stay underground for three reasons. One, because of his financial instability, two because people heckled him at every conceivable turn whenever he was visible in a public setting, and three because he is James von Doom. Due to these unfortunate set of circumstances, von Doom believed bad things came in threes. Evidence of the aforementioned manifested last week when three little kids robbed him immediately after von Doom received his welfare check. They proceeded to beat him to a pulp and fled before anybody cared enough to stop the onslaught. Even the people who tended to his wounds ransacked his pockets to see if any change was left before helping von Doom up. Walking up the stairs into the lobby of Imitation Apartments, von Doom held some commonality amongst his fellow tenets. Everybody who occupied the building held the same name as a famous professional wrestler. The cast of characters ranged from Angelo Deville occupying the penthouse to Xavier Kannon cramming the elevators each morning explaining the work of God to anybody who would listen (only visitors were foolish enough to travel in the boxes of doom) to Caesar 'Nova' Vega walking around at four in the morning chock full of tattoos emphasizing the anti-drug straight edge lifestyle. Adjusting his icepack, von Doom took a key from the disgusted counterman with a horrific case of sexual impotence, Jason Snow, and gingerly walked toward the mail room. Finding his box quickly, von Doom turned the key and opened the door, only to immediately recoil in horror and fling a mousetrap across the room. Swearing underneath his breath, von Doom moved the icepack from his package to his finger, while reaching inside with his other hand and retrieving a lone letter. He was surprised to get any kind of mail other than death threats, which were promptly dumped at his front door in bags three feet high on a weekly basis. The letter was addressed from the offices of the PTC, and von Doom instantly smelled rejection. He then moved his nose down to the icepack and disregarded the previous notion. Using his free hand to open the letter, von Doom tossed the envelope in the trashcan and was elated to find out news of his acceptance into IG 54. Finally, he would get to show his prowess on a grand stage and potentially get to become a professional champion, something that would evaporate the sully that had surrounded his name ever since he could remember. Perhaps, he could even get a little respect. Without warning, a bucket of fish heads came down over his head and sent his entire body into shivers. The man responsible for the act patted the bucket three times and gave a drastically altered, yet still identifiable grin of <=) Angelo Deville: Happy Holidays, motherfucker. Thirty Minutes Later Capable of sensing his putrid smell even behind closed doors, all of the occupants vacated the gym in advance so James von Doom could get a workout in. The only comfort he received from the bucket of fish heads was dumping a pot of cold water over his naked body before finding an old boom box and walking down to the gym. This gym was the cream of the crop in terms of apartment gym establishments, one that would flatter even a Gold's Gym member. Unfortunately, James von Doom is a completely out of shape 205 pounds who doesn't have much power, speed, or ability in any facet of training. Flipping on his boom box, von Doom set the treadmill for a relatively low speed and began to work up a sweat, which he was able to maintain for four minutes while Lil Wayne played. After the song concluded, von Doom heard the introduction to a song that was made famous by one Rocky Balboa, Eye of the Tiger. He became inspired and set the treadmill to a higher speed, a running speed. Once the guitar kicked in, the sounds made were rhythmically in sync with von Doom slipping on the treadmill, his face colliding with the belt, and his body being launched backwards into the weights, which all fell indiscriminately around him. Thirty Minutes Later With a throbbing headache, James von Doom woke up handling a new icepack, made of the same materials, in an attempt to decrease the swelling on his forehead. His options were running poignantly thin. There was no way he could go out and request the services of a public trainer due to his notoriety. He already experimented with the gym and epic failed to the point where he now owed about two thousand dollars in damages on top of the rent. He didn't have the money to go out and purchase workout videos that would be sufficient to train for a grueling match. So goes another chapter in the Greek tragedy of James von Doom's life. Still groggy, von Doom walked over to the television and hit the 'Power' button. Much to his delight, one of his favorites movies was playing on the 13 inch television that he had somehow managed to keep, Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back. Trotting back over to the bed, von Doom wiped his hand across the bed sheets to remove all the crumbs from his dinner of two day old pizza. He locked eyes with his idol, Darth Vader. The character had appealed to von Doom ever since his forced isolation five years ago. Having a closet littered with paraphernalia related to the character, von Doom always fantasized about one day becoming the legend. To von Doom, Darth Vader is a fearless and intimidating figure who could ruthless slaughter any foe who dared step in his path. Everybody be damned who crossed someone with such an aura and such strength. Suddenly, an idea hit the troubled young man, one so crazy that even von Doom thought it might work. Stuck in such a bind, there wasn't another legitimate avenue available. Winning the IG event was something he had to do. He needed the respect. He needed the accolades. He needed the money. He needed to improve his life. The decision was made. He walked into his closet and shut the door behind him. Three Minutes Later The man once known as James von Doom now appeared in full Darth Vader gear. Confidently, he walked over to the bathroom mirror and triumphantly stood with arms at his hips. James von Doom: From here forth, I am no longer James von Doom... but instead... Darth von Doom. Von Doom rushed out of the room and back into the closet for the final piece to the puzzle, his own personal lightsaber. A broom. Now, von Doom was ready to conquer the world. The first mission...
