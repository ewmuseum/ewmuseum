The New Guy

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-21 There are normally only a select few occasions as to why someone would wear a finely tailored Italian suit. The suit is more specifically known as an armani suit, the finest that money can buy. Someone wearing an armani suit could be going to a wedding, or maybe they could go to a major corporation's business meeting. Someone wearing this suit could even be attending a funeral or a burial, but not Joey Vendetti. No... Joey Vendetti is going out for dinner at a fine Italian restaraunt. How terribly stereotypical it seems that our Mafioso friend Joseph Marciano Vendetti is finding his way to an Italian restaraunt while wearing a finely tailored armani suit. What's next? Making an offer someone can't refuse? Placing a pair of concrete shoes on a man in debt? Either way, the scene opens with a white 2004 Pontiac Sunfire speeding down a street with what seems like hundreds of small shops on each side. The engine of the sunfire hums as it passes the pedestrians along the way and each person on the street gives the car a different look. Most looks that are seen on the faces of these people though, are looks of fear. Why would someone fear a white 2004 Pontiac Sunfire? Well the answer to this question is quite simple really. In this neighborhood, a nice car on a Sunday usually means that there is a special meeting of sorts that is about to take place. The people in this neighborhood know especially if several nice cars are seen driving around, that something is about to go down. This neighborhood is the Bensonhurst section of Brooklyn, New York, a section riddled with organized criminal activity. For years America's organized crime families have met here in Bensonhurst to discuss strategic plans for taking over their respective markets, anywhere from laundering to prostitution. It seems no surprise then, that the locals of Bensonhurst seem to have a growing sense of dread as the Sunfire speeds down the street. Soon, the car begins to slow down as it turns its signal on and makes a right turn into a rather large parking lot. A few other random cars are seen in the lot, the most notable of which is a black stretch limousine and the Lincoln LS that is parked next to it. The white 2004 Sunfire pulls into the parking space next to the Lincoln LS and parks with the passenger side door facing the Lincoln. Soon, the car shuts off and the driver's side door swings open, allowing Joey Vendetti to step out into the dingy air produced by a factory somewhere near Bensonhurst. As Joey shuts the door, we see that he is still dressed impeccably in his black armani suit with the lengthy red tie that is now tucked deep into his jacket. As mentioned before, the day is Sunday, near the middle of the day at this point. Joey had gotten dressed in the locker room that was provided for him at the PTC arena, and of course was not impressed with said locker room at all. How could he be? For Joey's whole life he has been used to receiving the very best treatment, mostly for who his father was. Geopetto Joseph Chonci Vendetti was Joey's father and the head of the Vendetti family, the "Don" as it were. Geopetto always made sure that Joey and his eleven brothers got the very best. They lived in a mansion, they wore the finest clothes money could buy, and each of them would receive a BMW on their sixteenth birthday. Even Joey's locker room that he used in his stint in XUW's developmental federation, XUDF, was the finest locker room in the whole building and in the XUDF's short history. Joey was a daddy's boy, to say the least. But the reason that Joey is now in a less than attractive locker room in XUW, and an even less attractive locker room at Primetime Central, is because of who his father was. Yes, Joey's father is long dead. Geopetto Vendetti died in February of 2003, shortly after Joey defended his XUDF United States Championship for the first time against rival Chushy. He died of a heart attack that was a result of the massive amounts of steroids Geopetto was using to stay in peak condition at his tender age. It was a special kind of steroid not found anywhere else on the market.... After all, if the families of the mafia could dominate any respective market, then there was no doubt as to which market the Vendettis dominated. The drug market. A million stories lie behind the reason that the head of the Vendetti family tested his own product, but those million stories are for another time. As Joey takes a few steps away from his sunfire, the camera pans up from the back of Joey's head to reveal the name of the restaraunt in which Joey will be dining. The main board on the top of the restaraunt reads: Pagliachi's Ah, Pagliachi's, the only place in town that the locals can scarcely afford to eat. For it to just be a place to grab a pizza, the place is quite expensive. Generally, the locals just don't eat there. Speculation on this fact is not needed as Joey enters the restaraunt, and nearly everyone in there is dressed just as nice as Joey is. Joey grins as he walks past a table near the door but suddenly his body is jarred violently and spun around by a rather short and stocky fellow with tan skin and a thick black goatee. His black hair dots his head here and there, but for the most part he's cleanshaven on his chrome dome. Joey's eyes pop open as the man stares angrily at him. Man: Vendetti! You don't nevuh come in heuh and pay y'bill. It's stackin' Joe. Joey looks, with angry eyes, back at the man who has grabbed him by his shoulders with a crushing grip. Joey: Yeah well I gotta job later this week that should get me some dough, I'll get back to ya. The two of them stare at each other with steely-eyed gazes momentarily.... Until both of them burst into a hearty fit of laughter. Hahahahahahaha! Rocko, it's been a while paisan, how you been? Rocko: I'm doin' great Joey, just great, the Mrs. decided we'd be movin' soon, but I getta keep dis joint. It's a nice place, locals don'like it much... Not sure why really... But Joey, why you here? You ain't been around f'near... a year or two! Rocko Pagliachi, owner of Pagliachi's. Rocko is the stereotypical Italian, usually wearing blue jeans, a wife beater t-shirt and a leather jacket, but today while he works in his fine dining establishment, he's wearing a tuxedo with a jacket, and a grossly sharp blue tie. Rocko is the type of man who will give you the shirt of his back, but will take yours just as fast. He's a kind hearted manager, yet at the same time a black hearted gangster. Besides, what better place than a restaraunt to run a laundering scandal..... Erm... to uh.... sell spaghetti and many other Italian delights! Rocko stares at Joey and smiles for a moment, awaiting his answer. Joey: Rocko, I'm... I'm actually meeting someone in here believe it or not. Rocko with an air of disappointment: Joey... not one of those meetings right? Joey sighs. Joey! I thought you was done with that stuff! Joey: I was Rocko, but now I got some stuff I gotta do. I got some money I gotta make. Rocko: What are you talkin' about Joey? Joey: Just don' worry bout it Rocko. You got a reservation for two under the name "Raimondo"? Rocko: Yeah, I got that, that you Joey? Joey: Yeah, meetin' Venchenzo here. Wanna go ahead and seat me? Rocko: Yeah... HEY TONY! Get ova 'ere! A moment later, a young man, probably about eighteen years of age, scuttles up to Rocko and Joey. He's wearing the standard uniform of Pagliachi's pizza; black pants, a white button up shirt, a red vest, and a black tie. He clumsily approaches them, his shoulder-length black hair bouncing about, and stumbles as he approaches. Rocko smacks him over the back of the head and scowls at him. Tony ya clumsy little mook! Stop foolin' around! *he turns to Joey and smirks* It's 'is first day Joey, don't be too sore at 'im. Joey: I know what it's like to be the new guy. But I want my seat now! Tony: Yes sir. Joey and Rocko howl with laughter for a moment until Joey follows Tony through the maze of tables, to a booth in the back where Joey is promptly seated. Tony grabs two menus and two sets of silverware and lays everything out on the table before laying the napkin out on Joey's lap. Joey smirks and looks up at the kid, as he clumsily tries to pull his order book out and struggles even more to remove the cap from the pen. A few of the other Pagliachi waiters walk by and chuckle at their comrade as he struggles and Joey pays little attention to the painful look in the eyes of Tony. A moment later, Tony is ready to take the order. Hi, I'm Tony Riccolini and I'm gonna be your server tonight. Our special is baked Manicotti and and our salad of the day is the Sicilian Salad, with Mussolettuce. Joey chuckles. What can I get for you? Joey: I just need a drink, get me some sweet tea and get it here quick. I don't care if it is your first day. Tony: Yes sir. Tony scuttles away, and not ten steps from the table, one of his fellow waiters passes him and Tony trips and falls headlong over his friend's foot. Patrons all over the restaraunt burst into laughter as Tony helps himself to his feet. It seemed to Joey, at that moment, that an aweful lot of effort was being put into torturing the new guy, simpy because he was the new guy. Those people have no idea as to whether or not this boy can handle the job. These people judge this kid as if they know him, but they don't, so the only reason they have to ridicule him is because he's new. Tony stands with a look of disdain on his face and tries to shake it off as he walks away. A moment later, the camera pans back to Joey as he sits still in his booth, watching on as the young man is ridiculed. A few moments go by until Tony returns to the table with a Joey's drink in his hand. Joey looks up at him and nods towards the table. Tony stutters and places the drink down on the table. Tony: Can I... Can I g-.. Can-C-Can I... Oh God I'm sorry Mr. Vendetti. Joey: Look, just get me some bread sticks while I wait... Tony stands, trying to write it down on the pad. Just get the frigg'n order! Do it now! And stop lettin' those guys pick on ya, you're lookin' pathetic. Stand up for yourself or no one's ever gonna respect you. Tony nods and scuttles off to get Joey some breadsticks. Joey sighs and straightens his tie up a bit as the soft music plays over Pagliachi's Pizza and Italian Eatery. He sits back to relax, no doubt thinking about the elimination chamber match for the Primetime Central Extreme Division Title. Joey doesn't have a doubt in his mind, he's walking out with this belt. He's going to walk into the Elimination Chamber as the new guy, and whether he is set to wrestle first, or whether the One Man Mafia is released into the match after a few minutes, he will be triumphant. Joey is positively sure of this. As of yet, Joey has only heard from two of his opponents; Shaman and Kyo Kushinaga. Perhaps the most mysterious of these two men is Kyo Kushinaga. A man who knows next to nothing at all about who Joseph Marciano Vendetti is, yet still believes that he is a "second-class wrestler from the same league as DDT". Joey can see it in Kyo's face, he doesn't take Joey seriously. But that's fine with Joey. Joey thought about Kyo for a while, and wondered why Kyo decided he was going to subject himself to the kind of torture that will befall him in the Elimination Chamber match by letting the words fly about Joey. Joey wondered these things.... only for a moment. You see, to Joey it doesn't matter. Kyo Kushinaga is just another man who will say "I'm going to beat Joey, he sucks." But Joey has dealt with this before, and he knows how to overcome it. Joey knows that Kyo Kushinaga is no threat... His words are not powerful enough. Shaman is another interesting character. He has been dubbed the "Native American Badass" by a select few fans in the home federation that he competes in, the WWA. Almost, it seemed that Shaman was just the stereotypical wrestler. He wrestles under little nicknames given to him by the fans that mean nothing at all, and don't come from any sort of accomplishment. It's just another catchy name for the fans to call him. But Joey couldn't rightly complain about Shaman having a horrible nickname. The fans gave Joey quite a few when he was their pet. It's true. At one time or another, Joey Vendetti's name was chanted throughout XUDF arenas and even once echoed in the XUW arena. Joey was called "The One Man Mafia" and of course "The Italian Stallion". Joey never asked for these nicknames, and only when he was a pet of the fans, did he offer the names up for a cheap pop or two. It seems to Joey, that Shaman is doing just that, and that he must be just that, a tool of the fans. But Joey only recently learned how much of a mistake it was for him to ever care what those fans thought about him. They have no bearing on his life, and nothing they can say will effect him anymore. Joey is too focused. Joey is too extreme. As these thoughts pass through Joey's head, Tony approaches again, this time leading a large man with brown wavy hair who's wearing a nicer suit even than Joey's. It's a navy blue tint with a black undershirt and a silver tie. Tony seats the man, who Joey eyes suspiciously from across the table. Tony: What can I get you to drink sir? Man: What're you new? I want your best bottle of red in the house, and for your sake, make it a good year. Tony: Yes sir. Tony jogs away from the table, leaving Joey alone with the mystery man who is now seated across from him in the booth. Joey looks up at him and takes a sip of his tea. Joey sets it on the table and then glares at the man. Joey: I thought I was meeting Venchenzo Raimondo here. Man: Yeah well Venchenzo's busy, I'm one of his associates. The name's Nick Beducello. I'm here on behalf of the Raimondo Loan Company. Joey: Yeah, I gather. Now where my stuff? Nick looks around to make sure no one is about before he speaks. Nick: Venchenzo has asked me to inquire as to why this information is needed. Joey pounding a hand on the table: What does that matter? I delivered my best friend's body in that trunk to your boss and I want what he said I'd get in return, and I want it now! Nick: Ssssh, don't make such a scene! You're gonna let everyone here know you're with us, and as far as these people know we're just having dinner. Joey: Shut it Beducello, I want my information now. I have to have it, don't you understand!? I have to have it! Nick: Look, if you don't stop making a scene I'm taking your info and leaving. Joey sighs, trying to calm himself down, and runs a hand over his close-shaven head. A moment later Joey's breath steadies and Nick nods. That's better. Now why are you in such a hurry? You know the procedure, you know this is the way I gotta do it. Joey: Yeah I know. It's just... I need that information. Dave Kipfer has been such a pain in my ass. I have to get rid of him, I have to ruin his life. This here isn't business Nick, it's personal. Beyond that, I got this Elimination Chamber thing to do for Primetime Central. I'm representing XUW. Nick: I heard. I also heard DDT was in there too. Joey: Yeah, but DDT hardly represents XUW. He's dangerous when he needs to be, but here, he doesn't need to be. DDT, if he's smart, will just sit back and accept the fact that I'm gonna win that belt. Nick: Heh, Venchenzo's put money on you again Joey. Joey: Thanks. Nick: Don't let it stress you. I heard your little rant about words bein' more powerful than fists. You know that's shit Joey. That's absolutely stupid. Joey: No it's not. You say one word about a guy and it gets under his skin and that word is all he can think about. The trick is not to let it bother you. If you're focused on the word how can you focus on the match? Nick: Right. So... I'm not real into wrestling, what's an Elimination Chamber? Joey: It's a cage. Nick: Just like the old school cage matches? Joey: No Nick, not like the old-school cage matches. This one's gonna be different. There are four chambers, one at each corner post with one guy in each, and two guys in the ring. Those two guys fight, and after a short time limit, someone else gets out. Nick: So you'll find out whether you're one of the first two guys or like the fourth one right? Joey: No, there's no way to know, that's the way I like it. I could be the first guy in, or the last guy in, or anywhere in between, it's great. I like to think that I've got a bit of an edge here. Hardly any of these guys have thought to show their faces, I'm wondering if they'll even be around for this match. It looks like though, that we might have a few empty chambers. Nick: Or maybe a few taken hospital rooms? With a grin... Joey: That too. Joey and Nick start to talk again, but Tony approaches with a basket of breadsticks and sets them down on the table, along with a small saucer of dip. Joey and Nick both stop their conversation to look up at Tony as he draws the pad out of his pocket and pulls a pen out as well. Tony: So what can I get for you guys? Joey: I'm gonna have the baked Manicotti. Nick: And I'll have the Shrimp Linguini. Joey: And make it snappy kid, you took forever with those frigg'n bread sticks. Tony: Yes sir. Tony finishes writing the orders down and turns, preparing to walk away from the two guys, but this time he only gets about five steps before another waiter bumps his shoulder as they pass each other. Tony drops his pad and is forced to stoop over and pick it up. Joey sighs and looks at Nick, as Nick returns the gaze. Joey: You know, that kid's gonna go off on those guys one day. Nick: Forget 'em, he's just a frigg'n kid. Joey: You know what I'd do, I'd get both'a those guys a new pair of shoes. Nick: That's nice of ya. Joey: Concrete shoes Nick. Nick: Oh. Joey: They think that cause he's new, he'll put up with it. Those guys are makin' the same mistake these PTC guys are. I'm Joseph Marciano Vendetti for God's sakes, not some nothing or some has-been. I've been around the block a few times, more times than I'd like to admit. And these guys don't bother me with their little words, hell the indian guy can't even pronounce my name right. Nick: Yeah? Joey: It's Marciano, not Macciano. What the hell? Worst yet, he was talkin' about wantin' all his opponents to know that he knows them by name. This mook doesn't even know my name! Nick: No kiddin'? Joey: No! And this Japanese guy, he's all ninja one minute, then he's all "Let's not be ninjas for a minute." You know, a real ninja is a ninja all the time, or so I heard. Nick: I thought ninjas weren't around no more. Joey: I guess they are. Nick: Maybe he ain't a ninja. Joey: Maybe not. Nick: So the only two you heard from are morons, and the other guy you know is DDT and you say he ain't no threat? Joey: That's right. That's why I had time to come pick up some lunch here. I'm not discounting DDT, I just know that if I've beat 'em a million times, I could pull out one more win anytime I wanted. I can't believe I let him sit over here and misrepresent XUW like this. Nick: Well you can't really represent yet Joey, you're the new guy. Joey: Whatever Nick, quick gettin' off the subject, where's my information? Nick: Joey, ok listen.... They got a file full of your info. I don't know who got it, I don't know how they got it, all I know is they got it. Got it? Joey: Got it. Nick: So here's how it breaks down. You know this Dave Kipfer guy you're so bent outta shape about? Did Joey know Dave Kipfer? What a waste of a few seconds of Nick Beducello's life. Why did he even have to ask that question. Until recently, Joey's major enemies had been outside the wrestling world, but now there's Dave Kipfer. Dave, a man who decided to put his nose in Joey's business and stop Joey and his crew from dismembering Saij, the current PTC Global Champion. Ever since then, Dave has done nothing but ruin Joey's life, so Joey just wanted to do the same, that's all this is about. Joey knows that his normal means of getting rid of someone, in the ring, will not work. He knows already that he can almost match Dave Kipfer, but he's not ready yet. So how do you get ready to face someone better than you? You break them down of course. You have to beat them before they get to the ring. And how do you do that? With words... and sometimes with a little help from a friendly neighborhood mafioso. Joey: Yeah I know 'em. Nick: Well, it turns out... Nick pulls a manilla folder out of his jacket and plops it on the table. He's about to speak again but... Another interruption, how classic. Tony approaches the table with a look of complete rage on his face. Joey looks up at Tony and smiles as he approaches. Joey: What's up Tony? Tony: I... Tony sighs with anger. Joey: What is it? Tony: I... I lost my book, so I need to take your orders again. Nick sighs heavily but Joey looks behind Tony as his eyes get wide. Joey: You know what I like about being the new guy Nick? Nick: Huh? Two of the other waiters approach Tony and flick him behind both his ears. The two of them burst into laughter. Waiter1: Awwww, poor Tony, are you gonna cry on us Tony? Waiter2: Time to call little Tony's mommy! Just go home we don't need you here. Not even half a second after the word "here" escaped the second waiter's mouth passed before Tony was throwing wild punches at both of them, causing restaraunt patrons to stand up all over the place. Soon, both boys are bleeding out of their noses and mouths as a smug grin crosses Joey's face. He grabs the folder off the table and stands up. He tosses a few dollar bills from his pocket onto the table and looks down at Nick. Joey: I like being the new guy because... they'll never expect what happens. Joey grins as he takes the folder out of the restaraunt. Nick sits still in shock as Tony continues to pulverize his enemies.... Much in the same way that Joey will do to his enemies in the Elimination Chamber. The scene fades slowly...
