Date: 2009-10-22 Two massive slabs of humanity the perfect example of the American beast; hairy, greasy, oversized, and obnoxious wrap tape on their wrists and tell jokes boisterously. This is not the bowels of the Democratic Party although close, it is the locker room of a wrestling federation. I have returned for reasons even my biblical brain can’t understand yet. Its been a wild journy that is strangly coming together. All I was told was the progress of man was dependent on my arrival back into the wrestling world. My messiah like body and wit for some reason only know to God and his booker friends in the sky needed the Second Coming to arise. Actually it’s about my forth return but this one is officially being marketed as the Second Coming because it sounds right and McDonalds wants to create a marketing campaign around it. That’s how you know you’re a damn fine America. It’s not easy knowing all and seeing all; sure masturbation is an ease, but watching old women shit is no pleasure. Power like words are only valuable to those who know how to use them. Thankfully for me I’m perfect, and not only do I know how to use them I know how to manipulate them which is really a golden ray of sunshine beaming from the eyes of Aphrodite while Johnny Cash sings, “Damn Your Eyes”. Twist’em till they sense only to me because I want to give you all of me. My father calls me son. My God calls me Savoir. The fans call me an asshole. My mother calls me no more. My doctor calls me schizophrenic. “I never been nobody's idol, But at least I got a title, And I Take Alot Of Pride In What I Am.” –Merle Haggard. THE HOYT WILLIAMS STORY: I TAKE A LOT OF PRIDE IN WHAT I AM!!! It started with rain as all great stories do. The bible started with creation and that is really hard to top. The world has been trying forever to write better, but damn whoever wrote that, shot for the skies and captured and captivated culture forever. Rain isn’t creation but rain is the closet we can get to actual five sense imagery as everybody has experienced it, damned it, enjoyed it, smelt it, felt it, and even prayed or danced for it. Rain is faith a mystical cleansing that seems too weird to be real. The closest we get to magic or tragic as it falls. I was sitting in my office in Chicago watching the rain fall waiting for my agent to call. I’ve been waiting a lot lately; waiting for change, waiting for hope, waiting for my God damn coffee, waiting for death, waiting for work, waiting to stop waiting. If life really is worth the wait I’ll be a very wealthy man. Aww here comes my wife with my coffee which is the hottest thing in the room as our relationship has been very cold lately. She just handed me the coffee and was gone before I could thank her. Oh well. At this point I was set to close up shop, and go back to the heavens. Step on that rainbow and cross that bridge between the life of suffering to the life of eternal perfection. Say my goodbyes and ascend to the skies for my rightful place as the Second Coming. Drugs came easy for me after a wrestling injury that left a permanent pain in my lower back. Not the kind of pain found in old Negro blues songs, or the pains of dead dogs but the actual feeling of my flesh being torn over and over again. I was a good man but I needed pain pills to get back to work. I needed to work. Work was my obsession that kept me from my other less sane obsessions. As long as I’m in control I’m ok. Plus wrestling is what I love. So I needed to do drugs to work and this is how cycles begins –for me it was the beginning of the end. Heroine and pot are my favorites depending if I want to be around people or not. Today was a Heroine day and that left me in a hole that only a call from my FUCKING agent could break. The numbness is all I need now as life is nothing more than a quest for pleasure. Most take a noble road; fall in love, marry. Not me I took the HIGH horse. Like I said it started as a need, but all things that start if you nurture them they should progress because if it starts it ends and all that’s left is the journey. What the hell am I rambling about? Sorry. The agent never called because after I left wrestling I moved on to Hollywood. I became a huge star and a huge drug addict. Now nobody will touch me and rumors of my sanity are starting to circulate like the world spinning madly out of control. TMZ is the sin of our generation as it’s an abuse of God’s ultimate gift to you. God gave you his most powerful of tools and you technojunkies fix on it. Judgment is no joke. Judgment is God’s ultimate power and one of our most unique gifts. At some point in your life you have even judged God. How can a God allow wars? A great flood? God damn! How amazing is it that the greatest tool of God causes his own destruction. Judgment is a bitch. The day ended with me asleep in my chair just drifting off to the patter of the rain. The door swung open waking me up as I was ready to give my wife a tongue lashing; sexual or otherwise whichever the situation called for. But it wasn’t my instead it was the most perfect angel I have ever seen. Things are starting to look up, mainly my penis. Being who I am I have seen my share of angels so believe me when I tell ya this girl is hot! It’s always the eyes that gets me first as they are the center of the soul. Her deep blue eyes were the perfect complement to her dark black hair. She was the patron saint of wrestling valets our Lady of Gaga. Which means here presence was sent by God himself a heavy concept for mortal man. Something was up. “I’m not going back to wrestle,” I greeted her as she shock the rain off her perfect 5’6, slim, top heavy frame. “I don’t gave a hot damn what the old man in the sky thinks.” I was in a fighting mood as I was due for another tie down and shot. “So unless you’re here for no limits sex turn around and float away,” I snared hoping she would vanish. “Well, the rails are washed out north of town We gotta head for higher ground We can't come back till the water comes down, Five feet high and risin'” —J.R. Cash She didn’t leave instead she spent the next four hours preaching about Armageddon and the great flood that would result from these rains unless I agreed to return to wrestling. I could tell she was lying and had no idea why God sent her to get me back into the ring but I was enjoying her yarns. I smoked a thinking man’s cigarette and before I knew it the passion of her words overcame me and I jumped on her and we fucked in the unholiest of ways before shooting up and fading away with the night. I licked my thumb like a bad ass in Kerouac novel as I rolled off her as the sun shinned down upon us in the American splendor of morning. The phone rang, “RING RING” I shouted back like a mad man as I dashed to answer it. People in poverty don’t get early morning phone calls which made me wish for a simpler life of toil and low wages. “You WOKE ME UP,” I shouted into the phone. “Jesus ummhumm JESUS!! JESUSSSSS.OH JEEESUSUS. Ok, fuck, Jesus, Ok. Bye.” “Who was that,” asked Our Lady of Gaga. “Jesus. He’s back and he’s lost near Wrigley Field. He said he was stuck at some place called the Gay mart,” I responded. “Jesus! Off to the gay mart,” she shouted with the sexist of eyes and great sense of adventure. I went to grab my cell phone but I noticed it was down to the last bar from all the calls from my wife made to it the night before. I couldn’t handle it so I threw the phone as hard as I could against the wall watching it explode like a firework on Independent’s Day, the most patriotic of all the holidays. These were not the most patriotic of times so it was nice to watch such a gregarious display of patriotic destruction that I almost saluted it but time was of the matter. “Knowing Chicago traffic and ‘Boy’s Town’ the way I do,” I tugged tightly on my flannel buttoning it up, “ I demanded we fly”. Our lady of Gaga was a bit hesitant to pull her angel wings out and who could blame her with rain still in the forecast and an antichristian sentiment sweeping the one nation under God. But Jesus was in Jeopardy and something wasn’t right with any of these happenings. If 2012 is the end maybe this really is the ending. Yikes man I needed a fix. We flew high higher than most and faster than the rest we were atoms and nothing more specs of humanity flashing through an online world that is ignorant to the beauty and magic happening all around it. It was China Town past Greek town past little Italy past Buck town and the hippies past Lincoln Park and the yuppies right there to Wrigleyville and Boy’s Town. I hated Boy’s Town and not because of the gays. I found them to be fairly pleasant and interesting sinners. No it was just the tackiness of the rainbows, oh and most of all Cubs fans that made me bitter and anger. Cub’s fans are the epitome of all of the seven original sins. Gaga and I landed and nobody noticed but who could blame them they were all drunk. To walk the streets of hell is the walk the streets of wrigleyville a consistent state where every drunken frat boy and sorority girl walked zoombied on never ending binge drink. Snarling frat boys looking for fights stare you down until a chill rushes your spine. This was the cocaine district and you could see it in the eyes sleep was long a memory. We bee lined it for the Gay Mart on Halsted street as me and the Angel walked hand and hand like young carefree lovers. What was Jesus going to say after he discovered I’ve defiled another angel? I got problems and even Christ knows it. This was not the time for our brotherly dispute. Something was up, something scary, something not just with me but the world. I needed answers and he was the deity I needed to chit chat with. We arrived like the Ghostbusters making an entrance into the Gay Mart both for theatrics and egos as we demanded to speak with Jesus. I almost punched the fruit who suggested we try church. This was a very serious situation if Jesus is killed the trinity is broken and every code starts breaking down. Computer Code, Language, to Structure. The world crumbles. Why did Jesus return anyway? My head was spinning like an out of control car on ice. Our Lady of Gaga continued a line of questioning as I slipped outside and shot up again. The feeling of confusion and responsibility went away and so did I as I closed my eyes. It was a banjo party with Steve Goodman and Steve Martin and it was magnificent. I wore a rhinestone suit of white as a bevy of black girls were all over me. I had ants in my pants so I jumped into the pool which turned out to be a shark tank. I didn’t want to swim I just wanted to die but now in the face of death I wanted to live. I started swimming away from the sharks franticly but I couldn’t get away. I didn’t need to, the sharks were just chilling. When in Rome do as the Romans! Except this time don’t kill Jesus. Jesus. Where is he? I was slipping away from the task at hand. I woke up as Our lady of Gaga handed me a dragon flower tea. “What did I miss,” I asked. “Just the beginning of the end honey,” she responded as I slipped back off to sleep. Ahhh darkness again. I got up and found out that Jesus was seen taken away by gritty men in work attire. We had very little in the way of clues and the gravity of the situation was intensifying in my own fried head. Our lady of Gaga was on the holy hot line to heaven to try and get a heavenly tracking device placed on Jesus. Nobody was answering. Our prayers went unheard. I felt like one of the general population for the first time in a long time and finally felt the horror of the world. Does heaven even exist anymore? Perhaps Christ came down here not to help but rather to hide and escape the very destruction of heaven. If heaven is gone then I am nothing more than just a man with the same cruel fate as everybody else. Perhaps the rain and floods are a result of who ever destroyed heaven now doing the same over earth. I would drown and simply cease to exist. Dust to dust. How unfair. Angels like snowmen are much underappreciated and don’t last long enough. Just as the human body can’t survive in heaven (the reason for death) the heavenly body can’t survive on Earth for very long. Our Lady of Gaga was a melting snow man in a world full of global warming. We looked at each other with a sadness that was much deeper than tears as we both knew her fate. She knew mine as well as we both were slowly dying each in our own separate ways. I wanted to give up and I think she did to as we both embarrassed and laid in bed cuddling and watching the rain. Being a savior I don’t do love stories I do principled tales and if I was going down I was going down fighting. To be honest I didn’t know what I was fighting for. Heaven could still very well exist, and Jesus maybe is just on vacation. But the reason didn’t matter as much as the fight did. I needed a reason to focus and get back into control because my grip on reality was slipping fast. I threw my drugs into the trash and told Our Lady of Gaga that war was about to be commence. Wild eyed like John McClain in a Die Hard movie nothing was going to stop me from taking back heaven or destroying hell in the process. War is the necessity of progress so mote it be. A Richard Nixon candy jar head that sits on my desk opens the hidden compartment where I keep my gun. Tricky Dicky as I like to call it. A pistol hand crafted by God himself custom gripped to my specifications. The gun has unlimited bullets that could pierce both the living and the dead. I put it in my belt as I told Gaga to stay put. It was too cold to ride my Harley on a normal day but this was anything but. I needed the wind in my hair. Plus now know suspecting I maybe mortal the added fear of death made the ride that much more enjoyable. The roar of the engine and the vibration through my soul I was ready for whatever the evil had in store for me. I filled my gas tank up twice as I just kept riding down the highway with no destination in sight. Jesus and I were two very different people and I had no idea where to even begin to search for him. So I just rode. The majesty of the dusk and the dawn in the desert sky is the perfect reminder of what heaven was and I needed that. I was sober and inspired and it was wonderful. I stopped somewhere in Arizona for M&M’s, rum, and gas. When I reached into my pocket I found a flyer our Lady of Gaga must have placed there. It was for a wrestling tournament called GTT7. Naturally it was right in the direction I was now heading. I didn’t know what it was but I was certain of one thing. It was a message from God. He was still alive, but greatly weakened. Now I had purpose and direction and I knew GTT 7 round one is where I would find Jesus. I hope it’s not too late for him or me.
