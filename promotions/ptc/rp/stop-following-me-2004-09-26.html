Stop Following Me...

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Fall is an interesting time of year. One of Sapphire's favorites when she was a child. A time when you could play outside all day in the relative warmth and the fallen leaves, and then come inside at night and sleep curled up close to your mother. Fall was much more than that, though. An end. The end of the warm weather. The end of some animals' time to be awake and roaming. Fall was here again, and once again, the end. September 26, 2004 12:57 PM The lights in the hotel room were off. Silence pervaded every inch of the large and luxurious suite. The only intrusion into this solace was a glimmer of light trying desperately to force its way through the curtains that had been drawn so tightly the night before, in preperation for this attempted intrusion. Suddenly, a new intrusion enters the fray. The buzzing of an alarm clock was added to the quiet din of the traffic outside of the hotel. This intrusion seemed planned, as the bed in the back of the room began to stir. An arm reached out and made contact with the source of the noise, and it was gone as quickly as it had come on. The stirring didn't end, however, as a figure crawled out from under the covers and flipped the light switch near the bed. Standing in the light now was a gorgeous woman with her long and curly black hair falling down her back. Sapphire was wearing only a t-shirt, and a pair of black lace boy short style underwear. A yawn escaped her as she stretched her arms to their full length. "Good morning." The voice was met with a shrill scream as Sapphire realized that she wasn't alone in the room. Sitting in a couch in the half-living-room-half-dining room adjacent to her bed were the two men who had ben her "bodyguards" for the past few years. Both men were quite taken aback by the sight of the young woman nearly naked. Who wouldn't be? As the young woman realized who it was that had sullied her privacy were, she became rather indignant. "Jesus Christ, what the fuck?" she asked rhetorically as she made her way to the foot of the bed, and retrieved more clothing. Both men watched intently as she bent over to pull a pair of shorts from her suitcase that was placed in front of her bed. Sapphire was aware she was being watched, but it was something she'd grown accustomed to in her former line of work. 'Former line of work...' Sapphire thought to herself, 'Yeah, right. Current and constant line of work is a more accurate description.' Shaking out the thoughts of failure, Sapphire collected her thoughts and turned back to both men once she had pulled the shorts up to her waist, and rolled them down so that her stomach peeked out from beneath the t-shirt she was wearing. "I could have you both arrested, you know?" One of the men smiled warmly at her, as though she couldn't possibly be any dumber, "This is our job, honey... and I'd say that in your position... as it were... you wouldn't want to get the police involved." The young former prostitute was beyond the point of aggravation with these men, but she was also beyond the point of even considering bringing the police into the situation. He would just have her killed. She knew that better than any of them believed she did. Her bluff had been called. However, there was one issue that was bothering her about their presence. "I thought He was cutting me off for this little venture of mine," Sapphire stated calmly, "What are you two doing here if that was true." Sapphire had always thought that leaving the flyer for this tournament behind on his desk was a plant. She figured that He saw a chance for some exposure, and some free money if Sapphire had gone here and made an impact. Nothing more, and nothing less. "We're not here for you. In fact, coming here was just a favor that we did for Him," the second man, who seemed to be infatuated with Sapphire, stated with a smirk. "You're not here just to watch me? What the hell are you really here for, then?" asked Sapphire with a hint of fear in her voice. "We're here to do our jobs. The ones we had before you ever showed up on his doorstep begging for work," the first man said ominously, "Watching out for Him." Sapphire's blue eyes exploded to twice the size that they once were, and her mouth dropped slightly, "You're telling me that He's... here...?" Both men stood up from where they were seated, and made their way towards the door as the infatuated man winked at her, "We've already said too much. Listen, He'd like to speak with you sometime soon. Say... in a day or so. Don't worry, though, we'll contact you." One of the two opened the door and both of them smiled warmly to her. However, she wasn't even close to paying attention to those two at this point. Her eyes were fixed on the wall in front of her. Thoughts raced through her mind, trying to find a reason for Him to come to this city as this time, not even knowing that she was involved in this tournament. She'd made a point to not tell Him where she was going. So how would He find out? How did He know? Why was He here? There's only one reason. "It can't be..." Sapphire rushed to her bags to find the list of participants that she'd been sent by Primetime Central. She yanked it out of the side pocket, and glared at the sheet. Her gorgeous royal eyes scanned the names quickly, and stopped short when she saw something she hadn't expected. "Holy... fuck..."
