Good Morning Beautiful

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 My life. My destiny. Everything I stand for. About to be thrown away. For two pills and a potential 4 or 5 hours of a close-to pain-free life? I hadn’t slept for two days since what happened. I remained in that hotel room, withering away in the pain from all my new gashes, thinking about shit. My thoughts and I were just hanging out, thinking about what would and could happen if I did give in. "Well, what do you think?" I was on the brink of insanity, the pain twisting and wrenching my mind until I could no longer think straight without talking to myself. This began last night, lying in this bed staring at the ceiling. In an attempt to draw my mind away from my back I made pretend someone else was there. Mind you, this wasn’t HIM I was thinking about…it wasn’t the normal hallucination of the past that had haunted me and tormented me. This was someone else. Someone familiar. "Well, Drake darling, you’re in a sticky situation, eh?" She was whispering sweet pleasantries into my air, licking my lobes with her breath. I sat on the edge of the bed and I could feel her sitting behind me, tormenting me by pressing against me ever so slightly. "Yeah. You could say that again…um…uh…" "It’s Charlene, baby. Couldn’t you remember my voice?" Her. The lady of my life before the war. I had only saw her once after my accident. She returns…now? "Why’d you leave, Charlene?" I remember the day vividly. I was lying in the hospital bed, the full body cast keeping me from moving. I was encased in pain, my mind warping and twisting. Why I was awake then was beyond me. Suddenly, though, there she was…Charlene. She had that long blonde hair and those blue eyes that just screamed at me. Her skin was soft and smooth, complimenting those beautiful lips well. And…that body… But she wasn’t there to screw me into a piece of wood…she was there to break my heart. Essentially, she told me that she found someone else to be with while I was in Vietnam. Since I was in the state I was in, she believed it’d be easier to tell me and then run away…but it wasn’t easy for me. That was the moment of nothingness. I stopped thinking. I stopped being. I wasn’t me anymore after she told me she loved me not. "Oh Drake honey, don’t you worry about that. I’m here now…ain’t that enough?" Here she was now, though, sitting behind me and being with me, loving me again. I didn’t care if this was part of my imagination or not…it soothed me. It kept me going. It kept me breathing. "I guess. Well, Charlene, I’m stuck. I got these pills and a bunch of pain…what should I do?" I was like a kid around her, speaking out and acting like an 8 year old rich kid. I was polite as ever, not trying to hurt her and keep her around longer. It was pathetic. "Why, Drake baby, you know you’re in pain, right? Those damn mugger really shook you up, din’t they?" The pain throbbed for a moment right then, reminding me it was still there. I winced for a moment and keeled over, clenching my gums. My thoughts were muddled, and a flurry of insanity swept through. "Yeah, they did…but…but I got…" "Got what, honey?" "I got these pills." Silence. I could feel her vibrating with pleasure from those words. She felt like someone else at that moment, but I knew it was her. She was back. "Drake darling, you gotta take ‘em." "Really?" "Why yes! I mean, you can’t go on living yer life in the pain you been feeling! That just ain’t natural! You need SOME help!" I sat there, staring at the floor, thinking about what to do. On one hand, I wasn’t raised to be a junky. I didn’t live the life of a pill popper, going for the next best pleasure. But on the other, she was right…I did need some help. The pain was destroying and, in my profession, pain was definitely a hinderance. I finally came to my decision. Without turning around to face her, I stood up and walked to the bathroom, slamming the door behind me. I could hear her rush to the door, putting her ear against it. "Drake honey, what’s wrong? You angry?" I shook my head. She wouldn’t understand. I loved her, but I had to do this alone. I decided to try these pills, give it one night…just to see what it was like. I mean, I couldn’t get anymore fucked up…what could these pills do? "Drake darling, please! Tell me what you’re doing!" "I’m taking them." "Taking what?" "Them." I took out the pills and placed them on the sink, making sure to close it up so they wouldn’t fall down the drain. I looked up into the mirror and sighed at my grotesque look—bloody, dirty bandages gripping to my skin, my black eyes looking emptier than ever, my mouth full of gums and broken teeth. I was a monster, a freak…so what will these two pills do? "You don’t even know how to take those right, do ya?" "Of course I do." Like I’ve said before, I’ve been living among the low life ever since I got back from ‘Nam. I’ve seen it all. I’ve lived in the heroin houses of Peabody, MA., and I know the proper way to do a couple doses of pills. "Then how do you do it, darling?" "Don’t worry about it." She didn’t need to know something so vile. All it involved was something to smash up the pills and a straw. You took all the pills the same way, right into the nose. Oxycontin, Codeine, Ritalin, Aterol…the best way to take any of those was by making a line and sniffing it up. I started grinding up the two pills by putting them in a plastic baggie and smashing them with my fist. I pounded the pills again and again, reducing them to a fine powder. They were perfect, the most beautiful way to do it. Slowly, I laid out the dust across the sink, carefully making sure not to spill any into the actual sink itself. Once it was in a pile, I spread it out into a thin line, ready to sniff. "Drake darling? I hope you’re ok honey…" "Just leave me be for now. I’ll be out in a second, honey." It was now go-time. I grabbed my straw and held onto it, staring down at the powder. Pausing for a moment, I had my life flash before my eyes. Was this all worth it? I remembered my days in high school, being the star in everything. I remembered my time in Vietnam, building up my accolades in the field. I remembered the accident, the flames engulfing my body. I remembered recovering, being alone for years. I remembered fighting in Asia, killing unknown warrior after unknown warrior. I remembered returning here, only to be a slum dweller, nothing more than your common rat. I just thought about things…and all I could do was say good morning to the beautiful new future, and good bye to my old world… *SNIFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF* -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Here I am…sitting…existing…being…what the fuck?…groovy?…no…this can’t…bughhh… "Hellllllooooooo Draaaakeeeeee…" Who the…who gives a fuck who it is…you know?…jus…fuck it… "Yoooooou ain’t looking so hottt Drake…you living right? Ha…ha…ha…" Fucking a…who the fuck is this?…is that?…what about Charlene?… "What…you think I’d…leave you, Draaake?" "No…man…no way…" "Maaaan?…What’s wrong with you? You can’t….trip off Oxyyyyyyy….Ha…ha…ha…" Maybe I…lost too much blood…maybe this was my insanity…maybe who gives a fuck?…I feel nothing… "You sure are smiling….Draaaake…you feeeeeling good?" "Yeeeeeeeah." Heh…my normal hallucination…treating me good…heh… "So this…was a goooood idea, right Draaaake?" He was talking…but I was too enamored…yeah, enamored…by just…the nothinggggggness…of everything, man… "Uhhhh, yeah, sure it was." "Gooood, good. You think you can straightennnn all this fuckingggg slow-mo-bullll-shit out before Clyde, though?" What? "You just imaginingggggg this…ain’t no such thing as Oxy-Sloooooow Mo, fool!" What? I snapped into consciousness, breaking from the present to where I am now. But I was still pain free. My mind was playing tricks on me, rendering everything slow. It was a beautiful thing, though…I had never been so happy in my life… "See? Ain’t this better?" And I was still the happiest I had ever been. I felt nothing. I felt like a real human being…no feelings, no emotions except happiness, not a care in the world. I sat there, grooving, listening to my self preach to me. "Why’d you get all slow-mo? You want more out of the experience." "No, no, this is great…I love this…I guess…" "Well, heh, I did that to you, no worries." "Wait, what?" "I made it slow, Drakey boy. I made everything groovy. I wanted to show you what drugs like these could do. They could make your life better, Drake! Make you exist!" "Well, I guess." "Don’t you want to experience shit like that from now on?" I sat there, losing interest in what he was saying and become more in love with how I was feeling. It was something I never wanted to end. I couldn’t end. I wouldn’t let it end. "Don’t let this end, please." He stood there, intrigued at what I said. He crept over to me, crouching besides me and putting a hand on my knee. He was sinister…but he was me. "Oh? You like it, eh? What happened to being pure? What happened to the soldier? Is that all bukakke now?" I was silent. He was right…I had abandoned all morals. But I didn’t care. This feeling! It was amazing! Everything had changed! "I just…want…to keep on feeling this way…" He stood up, thinking to himself. I could tell he was trying to see how he could torture me…but that thought was in the back of my mind. I wanted more! I needed more! "Well, Drakey, you and I don’t have that much money, you know? At least not to keep this habit running." And that was true. At 70 bucks a pop, I couldn’t afford to pop pill after pill. "But…there is an alternative." There is? "Another drug that works VERYYYY similarly to Oxycontin, my friend." Another? "It’s heroin." "WHAT?!" "What’s the matter, Drakey?" "I’M NOT A HEROIN JUNKY!" "But it’s a lot cheaper! Same type of feeling, too!" "BULLSHIT!" "For the same type of feeling, you could be paying $15!" Silence. 15 dollars for this type of feeling? Wow. What a deal. What an offer. I’d be pain free…Clyde Walkins or anyone else wouldn’t stand a chance! I turned to see him walking away, out of the room. "Hey, wait! One last thing!" He turned, a perplexed look draped across his face. "Where did Charlene go?" He began laughing hysterically, almost keeling over. Once he recovered, he stood straight up and struck a pose a woman would. "Oh, Drake darling, don’t you worry about me, honey!" The voice was hers, but the body was his. That rat. Oh well. As long as I could feel this way…oh Lord please help my find a way… ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- "Good morning beautiful. Good bye world." --The The
