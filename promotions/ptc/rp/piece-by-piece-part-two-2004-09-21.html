Piece by Piece: Part Two

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 The journey to tournament glory had begun again. In round one, Seymour Almasy had reduced Ash to exactly that. Round two now loomed on the horizon. But Seymour wasn’t concerned with Darek DeAngeleo at the moment. Earlier, he had vowed that he would live. And that was exactly what he intended to do tonight… --------- “Great job, Seymour!” The PTC Global Champion walked back through the curtain, sweat pouring from his body, the PTC Global Title dangling from his shoulder. He had been victorious yet again in a tournament. Seymour Almasy: Thanks, Dave. As Almasy passed through the back, he continued to receive and accept the accolades that others gave him, in the form of kind words and pats on the back. Part of him was happy, yet part of him wanted to just be alone for a few minutes. After working his way through the gauntlet of people, he made it to his dressing room, and entered. Seymour Almasy: Finally. He surveyed the room that Primetime Central had made his own. It was large enough to have been his bedroom, with amenities such as a private bathroom, a microwave, and various other goodies. Usually, Almasy celebrated a victory with iced apple cider. However, the bottle of liquid that rested in the ice bucket on a table in the corner wasn’t fruit juice. Instead, it was a bottle of Bacardi 151. Seymour Almasy: It’s nice to be alone again. Ash gave it a good effort…but in the end, he found out what everyone does. That I truly am as good as advertised. He grinned goofily as he said it, picturing himself in front of a PTC camera. Pulling the leg pieces of his armor off, the Final Fantasy allowed his body to relax. There was no need to be tense. His night in the Arena was over. Seymour Almasy: But my night is only beginning. It’s time for me to have some real fun. Wordlessly, PTC’s Global Champion stripped bare, and grabbed a towel from his duffel bag, which rested by the bathroom’s door. The door shut, and it didn’t take long for the sound of water running to be audible. Seymour was noted in the locker room of the Universal Wrestling Federation for the unfortunate habit of being a shower singer. Thankfully, perhaps, tonight he wasn’t singing any of the numerous RPG songs he had committed to heart, but was rather talking to himself. Seymour Almasy: It’s so relaxing in here. I can wash away all of the sweat…all of the blood…and all traces of the Final Fantasy. Once I get out of this shower, I’m just Seymour Almasy again. The sound of shampoo squirting out of its bottle was music to Almasy’s ears. As his hands worked the thick liquid through his shoulder length hair, Seymour could feel the white dye dropping out of it, splashing against the shower floor. Moments later, Almasy stepped out of the shower, and wrapped a towel around his waist. Seymour Almasy: Much better. The bathroom door opened, and Almasy stepped out, his naturally blonde tresses flowing behind him. From his bag he pulled a tight black t-shirt, a pair of jeans, and blue boxers. He worked at dressing himself, sliding the boxers on, the jeans over that, and then slipping the t-shirt on over his wet hair and torso. Seymour Almasy: Hmmm…I wonder if I pass. Almasy crossed the room to the door, which had a mirror on it. He almost shook his head in disbelief at the sight. He looked…. Seymour Almasy: Normal. I look like any other twenty-three year old, about to go out on the town. Perfect. The tight t-shirt clung to his body, showing off the muscular abdomen that had so excited Aya and his ex-wife in the past. He grinned, before looking across the room at the bottle of liquor. Seymour Almasy: Come to papa…you know, I always hear about people doing this. Drinking before they go out, so they’re nice and buzzed when they hit the club. They call it pre-gaming. Heh…doesn’t make the most sense to me, but if everyone else thinks it’s a good idea, who the hell am I to disagree? Peer pressure at its finest. Almasy wanted to be a ‘normal young man’. He dressed the part. And now, he would drink the part. Opening the bottle somewhat clumsily, he downed a small quantity of the drink. He sputtered slightly, but recovered quickly. Seymour Almasy: Damnation…why is my heart pounding like this? I shouldn’t be nervous. It’s not the first time I’ve ever had alcohol. Well, it’s the first time I’m drinking for the sole purpose of getting plastered, but still… Shrugging the nerves off, Almasy poured more of the liquor down his throat. Wiping his lips contentedly, he put the bottle down, beginning to run through the events of the night. Seymour Almasy: Violence Jack, Vendetti, DeAngeleo…all through to round two. As expected. However, this bracket shuffling wasn’t so kind to me. The King of the Indies stays at the other end of the bracket, but DeAngeleo and Vendetti are now directly in my path. DeAngeleo, unfortunately for him, is now next in my path. The bottle is once again raised, and more rum flows from the neck of the bottle into Seymour’s mouth. Over half of the contents of the bottle work their way into Almasy’s bloodstream, and Seymour simply sits, waiting for the alcohol to take its effect. Seymour Almasy: We’re no strangers to one another. Few months ago, Four Winds Wrestling thought it would be a good idea to show off the size of their dicks to everyone in Primetime Central. Tessa Windsor and I called DeAngeleo on it, and so we had a match, for charity. As much as it pains me to admit it, Tessa Windsor prevailed in that contest. But what this DOES mean is that Dark Angel, Darek DeAngeleo…you and I have a score to settle. An unfamiliar warm feeling slowly begins to fill Almasy. It seems to crawl throughout his body, and he knows that the alcohol is just barely beginning to take effect. Seymour Almasy: You see, Darek…I don’t take kindly to those who like to walk around and proclaim their greatness anymore. You don’t see me do that. You don’t see Violence Jack do that. Hell, you don’t even see that arrogant asshole Angelo Deville do that…most of the time, anyway. Those who have true skill have others who speak of their glory. I don’t have to tell people I’m the best. People already say it, think it, and know it as gospel. Almasy reaches for the bottle, and after a few attempts, manages to get hold of it. He attempts to drink a bit more, but the alcohol pours onto the Final Fantasy’s cheek. Seymour giggles, before putting the bottle down firmly. Seymour Almasy: Shit…this stuff works a lot better and faster than beers do. What the hell was I thinking about anyw--oh yeah, DeAngeleo. Darek’s one of the few men in Primetime Central who has stepped in the ring with me, and not been defeated at my hand. That’s gonna change. Oh yeah, that’s gonna change… He says no more on the subject. The alcohol has taken effect. The buzz hits Almasy like a clothesline, practically knocking the subject of the GTT out of his head, which was fine by him anyway. Tonight was for partying, not worrying. Seymour Almasy: It’s time to PAAAAAAARRRRRTTAAAAAAYYY!!! Standing pretty easily, despite the alcohol in his system, Almasy exits his dressing room, forgetting all of his personal belongings. Such things aren’t even a concern to Seymour at this point. Despite being buzzed, though, he is in pretty good command of his faculties. Mouthy, but in control of his faculties. Seymour Almasy: Hey guys! Ya wanna go out tonight? The technicians that Seymour speaks to shake their heads no. Almasy shrugs, as if to say ‘their loss’, before practically kicking the door to the outside open, and stepping out into the familiar nighttime sky. Seymour Almasy: Now, to go find a club. This is a hot city, shouldn’t take me too long… No sooner than the words escape his lips does he find the place. The Gaia, a club renowned for its unique rainforest theme, frozen drinks, and beautiful women. Seymour’s eyes light up like diamonds as he gazes at it. Seymour Almasy: Perfect.. Almasy reaches into his pocket, pulling out two bills of unseen denomination to pay the cover charge…and a third bill to make up for the fact that his name probably isn’t on the list. Seymour Almasy: Take it…the name’s Seymour Almasy. The bouncer looks over the first two bills, and then the third. He stares at Almasy for a moment, and then back to the third bill. Bouncer: Go ahead. Seymour steps through the door. Although alcohol is supposed to dull the senses, Almasy almost feels as if they are heightened. The music coming from the club seems louder, and the heady scents of perfume and feminine bodies mix together to form a tantalizing combination. Seymour Almasy: Oh, Yevon… It is an environment that Seymour had never before seen. Mock trees were everywhere, as very real parrots and other jungle birds rested on perches throughout the club. Seymour Almasy: I’ve been missing out! Gleefully, the Final Fantasy walks over to the “treetop” bar, looking at the mixing concoctions. He takes a seat at a stool, giving his order to the bartender. Seymour Almasy: Pina colada, and keep them coming. The middle-aged bartender laughs, until Almasy plops down a fifty-dollar bill on the table. Suddenly, all is serious. Bartender: You sure? Our coladas are known for having the most kick out of any of them in the city. Seymour Almasy: Excellent. Keep them coming. Wordlessly, the bartender turns to prepare the drink. Almasy smiles, letting his eyes scan the dance floor, and any of dozens of beautiful, well-dressed women. He turns back, to find his drink placed on the bar. Seymour Almasy: Now, this is the life. This is something that movie star Gordo should be doing, not me… The Final Fantasy sipped the colada, and was immediately overpowered. Seymour Almasy: Shit, bartender wasn’t lying. Mmm… He continues to sip away at his drink, enjoying the coconut and pineapple flavors of the beverage, until he begins to hear a distinct clicking behind him. Seymour Almasy: Well, I guess the alcohol’s going to my head. Now I’m hearing things. Almasy shakes his head, as if to try to get rid of the odd clicking, and continues drinking. The noise persists, however, and Seymour is now quite sure that it’s not in his head. Seymour Almasy: Hmm? He is even more sure when he feels a tap on his shoulder. Slowly, he swivels around in his stool, and discovers that a woman is standing there. Woman: Hello. Even despite his inexperience and the woman’s formal tone, it is obvious to Seymour that the girl is inebriated, and far more than he is. Seymour Almasy: Hi there. What can I do for you? Woman: Oh, nothing… She blushed slightly as Seymour began to scan her. She was certainly pretty, with short, silky-brown hair, a beautiful dress that left very little to the imagination, and the source of the clicking, a pair of two-inch heels. Seymour Almasy: Let me guess…you’d like me to buy you a drink. Woman: Oh, wow, that would be great, actually… Of course it would, he thought. Somewhere back in high school, he’d vowed never to let a pretty face and an awesome body sway him. Ah well…back in high school a woman like this wouldn’t have given him the time of day. Seymour Almasy: Another colada, barkeep. For the lady. Put it on my tab. Bartender: Will do. After shooting a wink to Seymour, the tender headed off to go prepare a second frozen drink. Almasy couldn’t help but laugh at the gesture, but his attention was soon recaptured. Woman: What do you do? Ah, his favorite question. The one that usually repulsed people. Seymour Almasy: Professional wrestler, actually. …Well, it was a better answer than “RPG Character Trying To Save The World From Evil Menace”, but not by much for most people. This woman, however, didn’t seem to be upset. Woman: Cool. I used to watch. I’m sorta in between jobs right now. Trying to find an accounting position somewhere. Good. Intelligent company. Seymour may not have been able to tell a chicken from a chocobo, but he was an intelligent, if not street smart, human being. Dumb people were a turn off. This woman seemed to be anything but, in all areas. Bartender: Drink’s ready. Almasy reached over to hand the lady her drink, but he soon found her sitting comfortably in his lap, taking the drink herself. Woman: I can get my own drinks, thank you. She gave a haughty sort of giggle, centering herself on Seymour’s lap, and reclining back against him while she took sips in between giggling fits. Seymour Almasy: Alright then. I’m certainly not complaining. So, why did you come over to see little old me? She doesn’t respond in words, at first, settling her head back onto his shoulder, and looking up at him mischievously. Finally, after a few more moments of making him wait, she responds. Woman: Because, you lucky guy you, I happen to think that you’re the most handsome out of all of them, and I have come to ask you to dance. It was all Seymour could do to not shake his head. She was tanked. No, beyond tanked. She had to have been drinking for HOURS before he had shown up. Still, there was something charming about her. It might have just been her chest, but still… Seymour Almasy: Sure. You’re drunker than I am, and I may be the most uncoordinated human being on the planet, but I really don’t care. Let’s go. The comment prompted laughter, as she stood, and grabbed Seymour by the wrist, dragging him out to the fringe of the dance floor. A loud techno song began to boom throughout the Gaia, and Almasy stood confused, hands ready to accept her in his arms for a waltz, or perhaps a tango. What he got, of course, was the woman turned around, grinding against him. We can forgive Seymour for not being familiar with the ways of bumping and grinding, can’t we? That wasn’t to say he wasn’t a quick learner though. After a few moments, he was into the swing of things. He laughed as the woman spun around, placing her hands on his shoulders. Woman: You…don’t spend much time in the clubs, do you? Seymour Almasy: That could be said to be something of an understatement. Usually I’m too tired after a match to go out. Woman: Well, I’m certainly glad you came out tonight… He looks at her. She looks at him. They kiss. It’s certainly not unusual, especially considering the wild amounts of making out and groping that pass for ‘dancing’ at this club. Seymour Almasy: As am I… The woman giggles again, a sure sign of too much alcohol, or perhaps too many drugs, Seymour doesn’t know. Nor does he especially care. Besides, after fucking a prostitute, what’s the worst shit this one could be involved in, anyway? Once again, she practically leads him by the hand, this time back to the bar. Seymour Almasy: My legal tender still good? Bartender: Barely. Enough for one more each. Seymour Almasy: In that case, my good man, give us the most ludicrous drink you have. Bartender: Ah, something that’ll ensure you two end up next to each other in the morning? The bartender gives Almasy another wink, and both Seymour and his companion respond with goofy nods. Bartender: Well, here’s a little something I like to call the Minus Thirty. So named because once you’ve had one, it’ll make you think any of our more portly women are thirty pounds lighter. You two won’t be having that problem though…so enjoy yourselves! Laughing to himself, the bartender prepares to mix together the Minus Thirties. The woman reassumes her familiar spot in Seymour’s lap, this time facing him, looking directly into his eyes. Woman: You know, you’re the first guy I’ve ever met here who seems to know what the hell he wants to do with himself. Seems to have a goal. I’ve seen doctors, lawyers…they’re all so boring. All have already met their goal for themselves, so they come here to drink and hit on women. It’s sad really… Seymour Almasy: Some goal I have. I’m trying to advance on in a pro wrestling tournament. Woman: At least it’s something! I have a lot of things I want to do in my life. I want to see the world! I want to help people. Everyone just seems so…complacent at times. Seymour Almasy: Well, if I want to move on in this tournament, I can’t be complacent. Y’see, the guy I’m fighting happens to be fucking gigantic. Almost seven feet and over three hundred pounds of mother fucker trying to steal my spotlight. You can see how little I am. If somebody that size catches up to me, I’m not especially long for this world. I’ve got to stick and move. I’ve got to wait for him to do something stupid, and make him pay for it. I’ve gotta--aw, fuck, I’m talking too much. It’s the alcohol, I’m-- The woman cut him off with another playful kiss, which was over before Seymour quite knew what was happening. What he DID know was that the woman’s body felt like utter heaven, and after spending the earlier portion of his evening arguing with a prostitute and wrestling a tough opponent, there wasn’t anywhere he’d rather be right now than in this club, in this woman’s arms. Woman: Don’t worry ‘bout it. It’s nice to have someone to talk to. Bartender: Drinks are on, you two. Indeed, the two Minus Thirties sit in commemorative Gaia nightclub cups. Almasy opens his mouth to question, but the bartender cuts him off. Bartenders: Cups are on me…so long as the tip’s on you. Seymour reaches into his pocket for another bill, but yet again, he is cut off. This time, it is by a hand on his shoulder, as his companion throws a twenty down on the bar. Woman: I’ll take care of it. Seymour Almasy: Thanks… She clambered off of his lap, and back to the stool next to the Final Fantasy. Almasy felt a pang of sadness that she had left, but the suggestion that followed made up for it. Woman: Want to come back to my place? Gee, let Seymour think a little bit… Seymour Almasy: Absolutely. Taking their frozen drinks with them, the two rose in tandem. Hand in hand, they made their way to the exit. Even while sipping his drink, and looking at his beautiful companion, he took in the atmosphere of the building. It was something that he never wanted to forget. Woman: We’re going to have fun tonight… The words were whispered into his ear, making Seymour shiver so much in anticipation that he almost dropped his drink. Casually, the two sauntered out of the Gaia, now linking arms like a pair of middle school lovers. Almasy took yet another sip, sadly realizing that he probably wouldn’t even remember this encounter in the morning. Ah well… …or would he? Life has an interesting way of annihilating our expectations, doesn’t it? (To Be Continued.)
