IG 33 - Trash Talk Here!

30 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jericoholic Anonymous presents: PTC Infinite Gauntlet #33 - The World vs. Jason Snow "You know what, Jason, if a few swipes across the upper lip constitutes a Dirty Sanchez, then what you have on your face should be called the Grizzly Adams." Fade into a rented soundstage in San Diego, CA, home of A1E's weekly edition of Warfare. JA stands in the foreground, dressed his ring gear, a JA lucha-mask logo t-shirt and of course, the World Famous Lucha Mask of Considerable Style®. Behind him stands his fianceé and valet, the Lovely Lollipop, dressed in a floral print sun-dress and high heels. Behind her is a backdrop that says "Infinite Gauntlet - Fuck Jason Snow!" JA: Seriously man, do you even look in the mirror when you get up in the morning? You look like you just tossed Kathy Bates' salad after a midnight run to Taco Bell. I know wrestling's not as big as it used to be, but if some poser Marine type can hawk sandwiches with Jared, then I'm sure you could score a Schick endoresment. I'm afraid to even slap you across the face. And that hair... Jesus, combined with the twelve-year-old looking peach fuzz on your mug, you look like you should be in Abercrombie and Fitch's Macho Catalogue, giving the shirtless, oiled-up token minority a backrub in the middle of the woods. Lolli: But, like, I like Abercrombie and Fitch 'n stuff. JA: And I forgive you for that. Lollipop sticks her tongue out at JA, and the Anglo Luchador just shrugs it off. JA: Aaannyway, I mean, this industry has seen its fair share of smacked asses. Only Stamford is worried about hiring the pretty boys. But you, man, you take the freaking cake. Now it'd be one thing if you were a... *snort* beautiful human being underneath that façade, but you reveal that you have the personality of a smacked ass literally twenty-five times in size. I mean, women have orgasms at the sight of you? Babe, do you have orgasms when you look at Jason Snow? Lolli: Like no. He, like, makes me want to file a sexual harrassment suit and whatever. JA: There you go. You either make Kathy Bates and her type orgasm at the sight of you, which would explain the fecal beard, or you're just so delusional you make Howard Hughes look grounded and logical in comparison. In fact, I wonder, do you and Chainz sit around and jerk each other off while you watch her nude scene in About Schmidt on loop while he surfs rotten-dot-com on the 'Net? I think I may be onto something. And then there's "Oh my greatness." What the fuck is this, Nineteen-forty-seven? Are you gonna bust out the "Gee golly willicker" next and claim that Cammryn Manheim has "nice gams?" You can't be a real person. You have to be some twelve-year-old with that growth syndrome, you know, the one that Robin Williams had in Jack? That's gotta be you. I'm convinced that I'm fighting an adolescent here. Well, multiple adolescents if you count Sonny Silver, who admitted he stares at men dressing in locker rooms. Hey, Sons, you're the one making gay jokes? Christ, now I know why Lindz hung out with you in the eff-Dubya-oh... you were the Will to her Grace. And then, you have Rook Black, or The Über-Judge, or whoever the fuck went before me. I've seen more rousing rounds of paint drying. The only person in the world who could make Brandon Youngblood or Jean Rabesque look enthralling... my advice to you is to get the hell out of here and cease making yourself look foolish. That is, unless your stated goal is to bore everyone to sleep and sneak out with the Eye-Gee Title when everyone's nestled all snug in their beds with visions of Nitro Girls dancing in their heads. Then by all means, keep up the good work. I won't need to refill my prescription of Lunesta and I can throw away my Best of Aye-Jay Cirrus on the Stick Dee-Vee-Dees. But back to Snow, man, seriously, your time as double Champion is about up, because I'll be damned if I let some shit-faced adolescent giant beat me, the Anglo Luchador, in the middle of any ring. It's about time that Snow days around here ended and the reign of Jericoholic Anonymous begins. So do me a favor, and wipe the crap off your face, because when you hand that strap and ten pounds of gold over to me, I want you to look presentable. Fade to the PTC logo.
