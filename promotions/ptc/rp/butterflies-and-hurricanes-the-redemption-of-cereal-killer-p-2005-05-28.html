Butterflies and Hurricanes - The Redemption of Cereal Killer - Part Final: "Your time is now"

28 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-05-28 Taken from his world and thrust into one of silence, Cereal Killer sat rocking on the floor of the Sitting Room of Darkness. Every time it seemed like there might be a voice, a noise to help him back to the existence he had known and loved, it turned out to be nothing but the creak of a door, or the rustle of papers in the wind. Living without the voices seemed like an impossible task. He could think straight, for sure, but there was no spark of invention. There was nothing in his mind other than one thread, one track, a single path leading him to the final of the tournament. He continued to rock back and forth, but there was no escape from the silence. "How long has he been like that?" asked Sirius Smythe. "For a couple of hours now, just as you said he would be," was the reply from The Ripper. "Do you think it is time to show him the way forwards?" "Yes.The Ripper shall show him how to proceed, and how to win." Any noise would have been good, any sound to distract him from the deafening silence. He wanted to speak, to make a noise of his own, something to break the monotony, but he was unable to move save for the rocking motion. His knees were up by his chest, with two arms clutching them as tight as they could. He looked a million miles away from being the finalist of a large wrestling tournament, let alone the champion. A sound! A noise! Blessed relief came in the form of footsteps and speech, even muffled behind a heavy wooden door. Even if it was the people who had done this to him, he would welcome them with open arms if they would converse with him, and rid him of the noiseless room that tormented him. The handle turned, and Cereal Killer's face lit up. A wide smile embraced the room, just waiting for someone on whom it could bestow itself. The first things that CK saw were the booted feet of his long-time nemesis. His gaze the travelled upwards, over his black trousers, just waiting for the authoratative voice to say something, to say anything. ¡§The Ripper wonders how you are feeling at the moment.¡¨ Cereal Killer chuckled. For many people this situation would be normal. Indeed, almost everyone would have been comfortable with the silence, if not perhaps at ease with the drugging and captivity. For CK it was intolerable. All his life he¡¦d had his voices for company. They¡¦d never been succesfully treated, and he was happy with the way he had lived. Until now. ¡§Feel? I¡¦m not sure how to feel any more. I¡¦m alone. I¡¦m all alone.¡¨ ¡§Draw strength from it! Grow within yourself, find that single-minded determination. You have to be able to break your opponents both mentally and physically. You must see their weaknesses and bring the in to sharp focus. Without that strength, you will never win another match.¡¨ ¡§Then let me lose. Let me back to the way I was,¡¨ pleaded Cereal Killer. ¡§Not when you have come so far. Can you not feel the strength within you? All the voices, the disability, the were just holding you back from your potential. You had an illness, a disease, and it has been cured,at least for the time being¡K¡¨ The Ripper let the final words hang in the air, like thin tendrils of not smoke, but hope. CK stood up and in his mind he grabbed for them. "I can go back?" "You would be a fool to do so. Power lies within your own head now, and not the delusions of yesteryear. To take that backwards step would be imbecilic. To take that backwards step would cost you the tournament. With focus, with the darkness at your side, you will win. With your illness, you might not." "But I might still win?" "It is within the realms of possibility, just like you might win the lottery ten times in a row. The odds are infinitesimally small, but they do exist." "Whereas if I play your game…" "Do not be fooled into thinking that this is a game. This is very real indeed. You will not come across anything more real than this in your entire pathetic existence. What you have now is more than just a clear head. What you have lost is far less than what you have gained. Just use your mind to help you instead of hiding in one small corner of it. You have been granted something that few people ever experience." Cereal Killer listened, and turned inside himself. He could think, and the thoughts were indeed there, bold and bright… but there was something more. He could visualise things so well, he could see everything in crystal clarity and it all made sense. Calculations came to him easily, and it was as if he could see things before they even really happened. "What have you done to me?" asked CK. "The Ripper has freed you." CK shivered. This felt like something new, something powerful. "Do you feel the power coursing through your veins? It will fade if you do not continue treating your illness, your deficiency. Soon everything will go back to how it was, and you will miss the silence, you will miss living at the centre of your own being, perfectly balanced in every way." "They test… they test you for drugs though." "Not for these drugs. Nobody could argue against you using the prescription drugs to help control your mind, and the others… the others are not ones that the authorities are aware of. You will be safe if you continue the treatment. You will be safe, and you will be a champion. Think of the change in your fortunes. Remember how it felt to be loved… to be feared." Cereal Killer's mind was in turmoil,and for the first time it had nothing to do with the voices. Instead he was thinking of the past, and how he had loved being a champion. He had craved the success time after time, but in the end his fear of failure had brought about his downfall. He stopped caring and stopped trying to win, so that when the titles were history he could say that he had planned it that way, just like the boy dumped before the prom, saying he had never wanted to take his former girlfriend anyway. "You can become a destructive force, just as you envied The Ripper in the past. You can tear through these two people, this Windsor and this MacGregor. You can subjugate them to your will and break them. You can be the master of all that you survey. Think of the way you could turn them inside out, and make each one of them squeal in pain and terror. Think of what you can become. Think of what you have become." CK remembered the arenas that he had shared with The Ripper. It was true that he had envied the colossus of a man, destroying all who stood before him on the road to becoming the champion, and first man to be placed in to the XUW Hall of Fame. It was not just his record that CK appreciated and desired to emulate, but the way he approached his opponents, knowing with absolute certainty that he could bend them to his will. CK thought back, and remembered the faces of people who had to fight The Ripper. He remembered the brave ones who stood up to him, and how they all, ultimately fell. Even those who got the best of him one time, would find themselves defeated in the long run. The Ripper played a long game, and CK reflected that sometimes he thought it best to give people what they wanted, in order to take it away later on, to devastating effect. This was not one of those times, though. Now The Ripper wanted, or needed, obediance from Cereal Killer. Frank thought back to those faces, especially the faces of the weaker ones, the men who knew from the moment they saw their name up against The Ripper that they were defeated. He thought of the tears that would fall from their faces, or the way youcould see behind blank eyes that their hope was slipping from their grasp at a speed they would never comprehend. He thought back,and saw all their faces… and her face too.Valerie Rose Dubonnet, a woman who never existed, but a woman that had made Cereal Killer feel more alive than any time he had spent in the ring. He thought of the way she had wept as he looked into her eyes, and the way that hope had slipped from her. "No." The way she had died, in his mind, was terrible. Hope had faded, to be replaced only with darkness. "That's all this is. It's a lack of hope." "Hope? The Ripper does not need hope. He deals in absolutes. If you wish to have power, little one, you need to follow.In time you can become a leader, but never in that time will you need an emotion as base and vile as hope. It is the recourse of weaklings who cannot control their own destiny." "Then why do you need me? Why, if you control your own destiny?" The Ripper laughed, and when he did it was an atrocious sound, full of death and darkness. "Do not fool yourself. The Ripper controlsyour destiny, no matter how you try to deny it. Now accept your position and that you can do nothing to change it." "Why though? Why me?" "Throughout all of this, you still fail to see the one truth. You fail to realise that you have been asking the wrong question the entire time. You should not ask why, for it is the call of one who is lost. You should accept that somethings happen, and move on.You get hung up on these questions, when really the only one you should have ever had to ask is the simplest one of all-" Cereal Killer looked at The Ripper, desperate to hear how he could change his situation. "The question you should have asked, is 'Why not me?', but youavoided it because it would take you to the fact you dare not acknowledge. That there is no reason why it should not be you that The Ripper chose for this task. Destiny has crafted you and now Fate will finish the job. Come with The Ripper." He turned to leave the room, but CK didn't move. "No," said CK. "What?" "No. I don't want power, and I stopped lusting for gold and glory long ago, when I retired. Sure, when you entered me for this tournament I thought I'd make a go of it, but I didn't want to do it to bring glory to the darkness, or to usher in any stupid era of terror that you might want. I was just trying to better myself, or provide for Valerie. Now I can't do that, so I'll just walk out that door and try to win it as myself… for myself." "Do not be a fool, little one. You need…" "I don't need you! I survived for years without you!" "Who brought you food? Who made sure you were kept alive and out of care homes?" "So you're my fucking mother now? Big dark bastard wants the best for me? No! I'm leaving, and you know what? You can't stop me. I don't know why, and I don't care why, but you need me to win that tournament. So, maybe like this I could win it easily, but I don't want to. I'm not doing anything for you ever again. I'm going to walk out of the door here and not look back, and you'll have to let me do things my own way. I'll win this with my own personality, with my voices, or I'll not win it at all. You can't harm me, Ripper. You can't stop me at all." The Ripper was silent, apparently trying to think of a way to counter CK's threat. "No, don't try to stop me. I am leaving and I'm leaving now. You can extract your revenge on me after I wrestle in the final. Until then, leave me the fuck alone!" He stepped through the door and slammed it behind him, leaving The Ripper, for once, lost for words. ------------------------------------------------- And so it was that Frank, the wrestler better known as Cereal Killer, was redeeemed. He left behind the Domicile of Darkness and its master, The Ripper. He left behind the drugs that had promised to cure him, and even stepping through the portal brought some murmur of delight from the back of his mind. He would wrestle, and he would do all he could to win, but it would be on his own terms. MacGregor and Windsor be damned, he was going to be the champion.
