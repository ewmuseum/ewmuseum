Bedknobs and broomsticks

22 October 2004

Date: 2004-10-22 Craig paused for a moment on the doorstep of Goat Mansion to take a deep breath. Why had he done it? Why had he gone to visit his parents? Given that nothing ever went according to plan in his life, shouldn’t he have expected something like this to happen? “What are you waiting for? Open the door. I’ve waited a year to come home and I’ll not wait a moment longer.” Craig dug about in his pockets for the keys and came up empty handed. “I forgot my keys. But don’t worry, Nurse will let us in.” A look of concern crossed his mother’s face. “A nurse? Have you been ill? Oh my poor child! You’ve never been very healthy have you? I blame your fathers poor genetic input.” As she spoke, she prodded her husband in the chest with a bony finger. “I haven’t been ill Mum, Nurse…” …Opened the door and shrieked at Craig, “Where the HELL have you been! Dave and I waited for hours in the gym for you. I won’t tolerate you screwing up the training timetable I sweated so hard over… all for YOUR benefit, remember?” A resounding slap to her face quietened Nurse. Shocked, she looked down to see a small elderly woman wearing a scowl. “How dare you speak to Craig like that, you witch! Now stand aside and let me in or I’ll set my husband on you.” Mr. Goat shrugged at his wife’s words and offered Nurse a timid smile. Speechless for once, she stood aside and with a sudden chill in her spine, realised just who these people were. Nurse was thankful that she had been so careful not to let her face show on the day she abducted the couple. She followed Mrs. Goat into the kitchen where she was meticulously checking all the cupboards and surfaces for signs of dirt and poor housekeeping. “I hope you find everything as good as you left it. Craig may have his faults, but cleaning isn’t one of them.” Nurse smiled sweetly. Mrs. Goat was shocked at the idea of her son, her sickly, agoraphobic son, prancing about the house with a feather duster in hand. “I don’t know who you are, but I assure you, my son would never stoop to such menial tasks! I assumed you were his maid.” Just as Mrs. Goat had found it difficult to imagine Craig in that role, Nurse found it impossible to picture herself in an apron (unless it was attached to a wrestling ring), and shuddered. “I apologise, I haven’t introduced myself properly. I’m Nurse Wretched. I don’t work for Craig, it’s pretty much the other way around.” Mrs. Goat dismissed this idea too with a derisive snort. “My boy… working? I think not! He’d never cope with something like a job. I won’t put up with that. Consider this his resignation. And as he no longer works for you, there’s no place for you here. Please leave.” Craig entered the room just in time to hear the last of this. Even though he had in the past tried to cut off all ties from Nurse, evict her from his house and even attempt to kill her, for some reason, he couldn’t stand the idea of losing her now. He desperately tried to think of something to stop his mother sending her away. But as usual, Nurse had a plan. “Mrs. Goat, please let me finish what I was saying. I am not just Craig’s boss, I’m also his… wife.” Mrs. Goat was the first to break the uncomfortable silence that followed Nurse’s declaration. “Well, I can’t say I’d have picked someone as… old as you for Craig’s wife. I always believed gold diggers to be younger than their prey.” Craig’s father ambled into the room, the garage door opener and stereo remote tucked safely under his arm. He cast a sad gaze over the trio. “I smell an argument. I’ll be watching TV until it’s over.” With a sigh, he wandered through to the lounge, and muttered, “I wish I was back at the loony bin… it was so peaceful there.” Craig placed himself bodily between the two women. Glancing from one to the other, he warned them, “If you start fighting, I’ll leave and never come back… I mean it. You are the most important people in my life and I know that if you give each other a chance, you’ll learn to love each other, just as I do. Please try to get along… please?” Almost in stereo, Nurse and Mrs. Goat burst into fits of laughter. His mother patted the top of his head patronisingly, “Run along to bed dear, the grown-ups need to talk. Your wife will be up to you later, maybe.” Nurse nodded her agreement, and outvoted, Craig left them slug it out, or whatever it was that women do when their territory is threatened. Unfortunately for lovers of violence the women did what all women in these situations do; they made a nice cup of tea, sat down to have a nice little chat and endeavoured to out-compliment each other. All the threats and injuries were delivered through the smallest of actions, the merest hint of sarcasm and a change in their tone of voice so subtle that only other women and dogs could hear them. Eventually, after many nice biscuits, an uneasy truce was declared – for now at least. Craig lay stiff on the very edge of the mattress. His muscles ached with the effort of keeping his body from rolling the last centimetre out of the bed and onto the floor. His heart ached from knowing that the one he craved so much lay so close that he felt her warm breath as it gently moved the short hairs on the back of his neck. Sensing his discomfort, Nurse said, “If you like I could sneak back to my own room, we don’t need to keep this man and wife ruse up twenty four seven, just when your parents are around.” Craig couldn’t believe that Nurse was putting his comfort ahead of her own. Was she ill? “No, you’ve got to stay here, Mum will be in to check on me soon. She always checks on me a few times during the night. I don’t think she’s yet realised that the risk of cot death decreases once you’ve actually graduated from a cot.” He inhaled deeply and caught the familiar scent that was Nurse; Poison perfume coupled with the sweet smell of the Irish whiskey she so liked, with a faint trace of her laundry detergent and toothpaste. Combined together, it was the best thing Craig had ever smelt. Why couldn’t this have been under different circumstances? He had always wondered what it would be like to lie beside Nurse, make love to her, fall asleep with her and for her to be the first thing he’d see when he opened his eyes in the morning. He’d just about gotten used to the idea that it would never happen when it did… except for the making love part of course. Frustrated, he punched his pillow into shape, screwed his eyes closed and willed himself to stop thinking such ridiculous things and get some sleep. Nurse could feel the tension radiating from Craig. She knew how hard this must be for him… and wondered if it was indeed making him hard? She took a perverse pleasure in his torment. It felt good to be wanted so desperately, and this was one of the main reasons Nurse had kept him around for so long. Every time he stared at her with his puppy-like adoration, Nurse felt her ego grow. She had total control over Craig. She had cajoled, coerced and compelled him to do those things which she knew he would find hardest to do, starting with the committal of his parents. In the rare moments where Nurse felt the slightest tinge of guilt about her mistreatment of Craig, she justified it to herself by saying that it would make him stronger in the end – that it was all for his benefit; but deep down, she knew this wasn’t what drove her. It was the feeling of power that she had become addicted to that guided her actions. Just as it was the reflected glory she knew she would gain that had prompted her to enter Craig in GTT4. She didn’t care what happened to him, she only cared about what other people would think of her as his trainer and manager. Even so, he’d surprised her by coming this far. Maybe her cruelty had indeed made him strong enough to tough it out with the big boys? What irony if the vague excuses she used to justify torture were more fact than fiction. The door of the bedroom slowly creaked open. The dim light from the hallway showed Mrs. Goat’s silhouette filling the portal. She paused there for a minute or two, waiting for her eyes to adjust to the darkness. She could just about make out the shape of the two figures under the covers. She noticed a gap between their bodies, a substantial space. No cuddling? Perhaps this marriage was in trouble already. She hoped so. She exited the room with a smile. “She’ll be back again in a couple of hours. I’m sorry about this Esme. Maybe we should come clean, tell them the truth about us.” Nurse Wretched moved a little closer to Craig and whispered in his ear, “Do you really think your mother would let me stay if she knew the truth? It was hard enough convincing her that I belonged here after I told her I was your wife!” “I meant what I said earlier, I don’t want you two fighting. You need to work out a plan to stay out of her way as much as possible.” Nurse frowned. Craig seemed to be more intimidated by his mother than he was by her! Up until that moment, she had assumed that Craig would take her side in any argument against the old battleaxe. “Craig, tell me a bit about what your mother was like when you were growing up? Perhaps if I understand her better I can get along with her more.” Nurse was being nice again! What was she up to? Perhaps I’m being too hard on her, thought Craig. It’s possible, just possible that she actually likes me! “Well, I guess Mum has always been strict, you know? Everything always had to be done to her standards. One of my earliest memories is of her showing me exactly where all my toys should go. There was a box for everything and they were marked very precisely. If I didn’t do it right, she’d get this disappointed look on her face that could just break my heart. Dad and I did everything possible not to disappoint her.” “So she was cruel?” “Oh no! Not really. She was… Mum, and Mum knows best? I don’t think I’m presenting the best picture of her. The important thing is that no matter what, I love her.” “Craig, you helped to lock her away in an asylum!” Frustrated, Craig rolled over so that he was facing Nurse. “So what! I kidnapped and tortured you. It doesn’t mean that I don’t…” Craig’s brain caught up with his mouth and stopped just as he was about to utter the ‘L’ word. Nurse lay silent, her mind turning over all that Craig had said, and more importantly, hadn’t said. So, he loved his mother and he loved her. That he loved her wasn’t news. There had been a time when he had said it to her everyday, almost begging for a positive response from her. But the idea that he might love his mother more was disturbing. Soon, he’d have to choose between the two and Nurse didn’t want to be the one to lose. She needed Craig, needed his support, his blind obedience and his family’s fortune. There was only one thing she could do to make sure he would make the right choice. Craig’s body tingled all over as Nurse pressed close to him. Was she naked? His hand felt her bare thigh and confirmed it. With a jerk, he snatched it away from her warm flesh. “I’m dreaming!” “No, you’re not dreaming” whispered Nurse as she began to kiss shoulder. “This feels right. Make love to me Craig.” A rush of emotions raced through him. His entire body shook with pleasure. Just as he was about to reach out and reciprocate her caresses, he froze. His mother. She was in the next room! She’d hear everything! Craig’s amour quickly turned to fear. He couldn’t do this, not now, not with the possibility of his mother walking in. Mustering ever ounce of will power in his body, Craig dragged himself out of the bed. “I’m sorry” he said as he pulled down a spare blanket from on top of the wardrobe and made a nest for himself on the floor. The door slowly creaked open…
