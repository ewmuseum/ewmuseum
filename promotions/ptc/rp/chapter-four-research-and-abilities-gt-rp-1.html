Donny Diamond Emotionless Monster Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 59 Location: My own personal Hell. Posted: Thu Apr 10, 2003 10:33 pm Post subject: Chapter Four: Research and Abilities [GT RP #1] Chapter Four: Research and Abilities This is the time for legends to be born, and for someone to reach that immortal status. Here is where the path of fifteen different competitors all meet up at the center of the crossroads. A head of them is the path that only one can take it. Some may call it the Mount Olympus of this business, because whoever makes it past the other fourteen individuals will be able to walk as one with Gods. Though the one allowed will not win this honor by the flip of a coin, all the contestants will be placed inside of a ring for one match, where their fate is to be decided by the spinning of a wheel. Only one can walk this road, and soon we shall see who this sole survivor is. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~ ~`~ Los Angeles, California. Donny Diamond’s estate ~`~ | ~`~ April 9, 2003. 3:54 PM ~`~ ~†~ A large home can be seen, styled in a medieval gothic sense. Parked in front of the home are a few different types of cars, and a black motorcycle with red and purple flames running along it. On the front porch is where we see him, Donny Diamond, dressed in his normal wrestling attire. Beside him is a lovely woman, dressed in a long black silk evening dress. She is leaning against the shoulder of Donny; obviously this is a picture from the past. Suddenly the sound of a door closing is heard as footsteps follow it. There we see him, dressed much like he was in the picture. He removes his trench coat, as the sound of footsteps can once again be heard, coming beside Donny is a guy. Standing a little under the height of Donny, he has shoulder length red hair with black highlights down the back, and his frame is nicely built. Donny hangs his black trench coat on the wall, and we can see he is wearing a sleeveless black DWO shirt under it. As he walks across the room he moves his arms back and forth so that the muscles can loosen up. His friend, Spirrow Nugachi, watches him as he lays down on the bench and grabs the barbell over his head to start pressing it up, and down. ~†~ Spirrow Nugachi: Aw dude, what is up with that? Are you getting old, or something on me? Man if you call that a work out something is seriously wrong with you. No wonder you seem to find yourself more on the defensive side of things in your matches nowadays. I remember when you use to be able to match biceps with me, now you probably couldn’t even keep up with me in one of my light workouts. Donny Diamond: Yet for some reason you still wonder why you are always getting your ass kicked in those street fights of yours. Ever stopped for a moment to think about how your fighting style can be improved? Just because you’re the strongest doesn’t mean you will always win. It’s about stamina, technique, and endurance. Spirrow Nugachi: Yeah, yeah. All of that mumble jumble you’re preaching about is good for when you’re still fighting in the minors. When you make it to the big time, if you don’t have any power behind that punch your ass will be taken down for the count. [Donny continues his work out by lifting the weights up and down as sweat can be seen forming across his pale forehead. Taking a seat on the bench beside him, Spirrow lies down and grabs the other set. Matching the pace of Donny, he starts lifting it up, and down as if he was doing it with ease.] Spirrow Nugachi: See, this is what I’m talking about dude. This pace of yours is to easy man, how you expect to get some guns if you keep doing this weak shiz? Man, how you expect to win this Global Title match if you can’t even match what I can do with these weights? Donny Diamond: Ah, so you think in a wrestling match I got no chance to win because I am not matching with your weight lifting skills? Easy way to see who’s right Spi, lets end the talk and do it right here. Let’s see if the big bad beach boy has what it takes to keep me down. [After pressing the weights up from his chest one final time Donny places them back onto the rack over his head as Spirrow does the same. Together they get up, and walk into the center of the room where they lock up into an amateur style grapple. Spirrow showcases his power as he pushes Donny down onto the floor with ease. As Donny gets back to his feet Spirrow punches him in the ribs a couple of times, then grabs him around the neck and tosses him across the room.] Donny’s Thoughts: Keep focused, and look for him to make a mistake. He’s relying strictly on his strength, and is not using his mind to achieve victory. Just wait for him to make a mistake, and take it from there. [Again he watches for Donny to climb to his feet, he then meets him from behind with a forearm onto the back of his neck. Following this he hooks Donny around the waist then pulls back tossing Donny into the air for a release germen suplex. Donny lands on his feet then quickly catches Spirrow from behind in a dragon sleeper. Using his strength he grabs Donny’s arm then forces him to undo the hold, then nails Donny with an elbow to the ribs. Spirrow quickly turns around and goes for a big clothesline; Donny grabs his arm and gets behind him to hook him in another dragon sleeper. He reaches back and clubs Donny in the back of the neck, but Donny doesn’t release the hold, but instead he tightens it, which forces Spirrow to drop down onto his knees. Donny gets his leg in front of Spirrow’s body then falls back keeping the dragon sleeper intact while also hooking him in a body scissors. Not wanting to give up, Spirrow continues to search for a way to escape, but Donny has it locked in so tight that Spirrow’s arms are nearly useless so he taps out on the floor.] Spirrow Nugachi {Barely able to speak}: I… I give… [After showing who was superior, Donny releases the dragon sleeper. He climbs up to his feet, as Spirrow remains on the ground holding his throat while coughing due to the lack of oxygen. Spirrow climbs to all four, before Donny grabs him around the arm, and pulls him back up to his feet.] Donny Diamond: I told you its not just about power. It’s about technique, something you might start to learn about since I just beat you down. Spirrow Nugachi [still coughing a bit]: No. I was just having a bad day… You could never match wits with me on a normal day. Reme… [Cough] …Remember, I just got over a case of the flu. That’s the only reason you were able to take me down. Donny Diamond: Make up an excuse if it will make you feel better. The facts are that I just made you tap out. A win is a win no matter what. [Spirrow slowly tilts his head up while still coughing and holding his throat obviously still feeling the affects from the dragon sleeper. Light enters the room from the stairwell as the door opens, the closes again just as quick. Coming down the stairs is an elderly man dressed in the most extravagant tuxedo one could buy. With his arms by his by his side he casually walks up towards Donny, and his company.] Donny Diamond: What is it Alfred? Alfred: Excuse me sir, but you told me to give you notice as to when your special files arrived. Donny Diamond: Good. You may be excused Alfred. Alfred: Thank you sir. [Alfred bows before his employer, then walks back up the stairs and exits through the door.] Spirrow Nugachi: Dude that is like so cool. Donny Diamond: What is? Spirrow Nugachi: The penguin. You have him eating out of your hands dude. I like so want one like that, how much did it cost you to have him trained to do tricks like that? Donny Diamond: Alfred? He’s not animal. Alfred is a well-mannered butler that knows his place, during his working hours. As a part of his job he did as I asked and notified me of my stuff arriving. Now if you will excuse me, I got some things to do up in my room. [Not really caring if Spirrow makes a comment or not, Donny starts walking up the stairs. Putting his hand on the doorknob he turns it, opens the door then walks through it leaving it open for Spirrow to leave when he is prepared. He makes his way towards the stairs where Alfred is waiting with a glass of water in hand; he takes it and nods as he drinks it while walking up the stairs. Turning to his left after reaching the top of the stairs he opens the door to his room, and walks in. This room has to be the most elegant of all the rooms in the estate, as far as bedrooms go. He notices the small envelope placed on his desk, he walks towards the wooden desk to take a seat in the small chair in the front of it.] [Turning to face his laptop on the desk, he presses the power button and the screen comes to life. As Windows XP starts up he reaches to the package on his left, ripping the top apart he opens it. Reaching inside he finds what he was looking for, a compact disc, after the startup program loads he pops the disc into the drive. A video comes up on the screen, and we see a very fuzzy picture with a voice to match.] “The payment was very helpful, and I hope you feel that your money was well spent.” Donny Diamond: You would think for the price I paid, the video would be of good quality. [Ignoring the lame mpeg video he “X’s” it off, then follows the menus on the page to look at the documents inside. It starts to load up, and we get to see the list of wrestlers enclosed within.] “Kaiowas, The Real Deal, Mike Meyhu, Jesse Jamester, Tristan Belmont, Shaman, Nick Adams, Damian Ryan, DVD, Jomary, Scott Curtis, Adam Brown, “The Pride of Japan” Samba Tatenaka, Michael “Spitfire” Stevens.” “Please make your selection as to which wrestler you would like to preview first.” [Using the track pad mouse on his laptop, he clicks on the link the leads to information of Kaiowas. A voice can be heard on the speakers as it is now reading out the information contained about Kaiowas.] “Kaiowas, The Tribal Warrior. Born in Brazil, and was once a follower to the God, Pultra. Now the leader of a tribe called the Ktulu. His finishing maneuver is “Tribal Punishment”, a Pump-handle slam into tombstone piledriver. For the last two months Kaiowas has been the undefeated DWO World Heavy Weight Champion.” Donny Diamond: How about you tell me something I don’t already know? [As the document on Kaiowas comes to an end, he moves the mouse over the next name, and proceeds to click on the next name on the list.] “The Real Deal, master of technique inside of a ring. Born in Houston, Texas. Recently he has gotten over a life threatening, and career-threatening case of paralyzes due to his arch-nemesis Donny Diamond. His finishing maneuver is the “Flight of the Phoenix”, a top rope leg drop. Currently he is both the DWO Extreme, and Intercontinental Champion.” [Donny nods slightly as the voice finishes to read on The Real Deal’s bio. He goes to click the next name on the list, Mike Meyhu. Right after doing so a big red “X” appears on the screen as the voice comes back on.] “The information you are looking for could not be found. No matter how much money you spend, you will not get the information you need to achieve an easy victory. If victory is what you seek, then you will prove yourself inside of the ring like all fourteen other competitors. Thank you for doing business with us.” [The CD-ROM opens up to eject the disc. He takes it out, closes the drive, and then breaks the CD in half before tossing it into the trash. Quickly he turns the computer off, and then takes a walk towards the sliding glass door in his room. Sliding it opens he then walks through, but everything starts spinning in front of him. Our scenery is now very cavernous, flames shoot out in front of him as he quickly leaps forward in a diving fashion to dodge the flames. He lands on his knees as he slides across the mixture of dirt and stone, with his head turned to the side he looks to see where the flames came from.] [There it stands with its piercing blue eyes staring straight at its victim. A lion, but not an ordinary one, this one is engulfed with flames as it’s fur coat, and a blue ball of flame is perched on the tip of its tail as green flames cuff its neck as the mane. It roars and more flames shoot out towards Donny as he rolls forward to avoid being burned to a crisp. Donny looks up towards a tall stone pillar rising up from the flames where a throne can be seen on top, his father sitting within. Spikes rise up from the lava around the small circular island enclosed with lava as well. He remains still in the middle of the isle as the flaming beast slowly walks circles around him.] Donny Diamond: Thanks for the kind welcome father. I see you have yet another test for me to pass? [Climbing back up to his feet he looks to the beast that dashes towards him. Side stepping the beast it quickly whips back around lashing its tail as it shoots out more flames with an intense “Rar!” He does a standing back flip to dodge both attacks, as he reaches under his coat in midair to pull out his small sword. The beast lunges forward tackling Donny down onto the ground, quickly he slams the blade into the chest cavity of the beast, but it passes through like a hot knife against a stick of butter. Using his legs he flips the beast off from him, and onto the ground as dust rises into the air.] [Backed against the spike wall the flaming creature creeps slowly towards him, quickly he unleashes another funnel of flames; Donny slides under them across the dirt and picks up a stone as he turns around. Just as the beast turns around Diamond tosses the stone, the mouth of this beast opens and the stone flies right in. It starts coughing as it chokes on the stone. Beginning to grow in size Donny turns around grabbing his sword from the ground and looking up towards the pillar as he covers his face. Soon after the beast implodes and flames fly everywhere almost in a super nova type fashion. Slowly the spikes descend into the lava from which they came, as the beast is no more, and neither is the threat.] Donny Diamond: Your beast is no more, and neither is your trial for me to finish. I did as you asked of me, now tell me why am I here? You at least owe me an answer since you nearly had me killed. [Coming from behind the throne is Sorrow dressed in the same fashion as always. Placing his hands out in front of himself he slowly raises them up as Donny rises up from the ground. Slowly Donny is pulled towards the pillar, he nods to Sorrow as he is placed before his father.] Satan: Welcome back home my son. Excuse me for intruding in your studies, but I wanted to see exactly how focused you have become. Sorrow tells me your little get together with each other went well. However I also see that you’re having some trouble obtaining information on your opponents. Donny Diamond: Indeed the chatter between Sorrow, and myself was very informative. He showed me a trick, or two as for as keeping focused is concerned. Which is how I was able to achieve victory over your little test just now. As for me obtaining information on my competitors, I hit a little snag. Easy enough to overcome however, and besides you should be having some new visitors very soon. Satan: It is always nice to see how much your appreciated when one has a son as grand as you. I can tell you this though; over the next few days’ things will begin to get very difficult for you. Donny Diamond: Life is difficult father. Any trials that come forth is just a part of living, you would have not let me be reborn as your son, and with these gifts if you doubted my abilities to survive. As for the future the only thing that concerns me there is going to that arena being well prepared for the unknowns that await me inside. Satan: That is exactly what you should do. Prepare yourself for what you do not know, that way you will be the one prepared for what ever it is awaiting you. Donny Diamond: This is my chance to reside by you for all eternity. Each of us that are stepping into the ring know that this is not only a chance to win a Championship, but a chance to be remembered forever. Immortality is what we have a chance to obtain father, and I will not let it escape my grasp. I got my entire mind focused on winning this match, and when I do father my reach to immortality will be dedicated to you. _________________ DWO Record: 9-5-0 PTC Record: 1-0 -I only mark for those deserving, and you know who you are.-
