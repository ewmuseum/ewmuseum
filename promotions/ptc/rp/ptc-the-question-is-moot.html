The Question is Moot

30 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
moot - (müt) Unsettled; undecided; not necessary to be decided It is said stupid questions deserve stupid answers.....but do they? But what if the question doesn’t even deserve that? What if it’s like the person asking how the weather is as he stands outside, or the women asking how many pieces of chicken come in the 4 piece meal? What if the question is so meaningless, and without reason, it makes your head throb, knowing someone used what brainpower they had to formulate it? You’ve heard the questions asked before, and this week, I’ve heard one over and over again. I’ve listened to it come in many forms, from the mouths of all. They no sooner asked it, only to answer in there own way. They asked to make us, their foes, think. They asked, as if to make others believe they knew....but really, they’d never ask if they really knew. If they knew, there’d be no need to waste breath they’ll need later. In case you were wondering about the question.... “What is Extreme?” Since everyone else answered, so will I... ________________________________ It’s cold. The man we see before us stands in the cold and snow despite the elements. He makes fists with his toes, thinking perhaps they’ve frozen and fell off hours ago. His face isn’t rosy, but blood red, causing his blue eyes to contrast even more so against his red skin, and dark hair that hangs down in frosty strands over his face and ears. He stares at a set of five headstones. Four of them...the new graves of his family. He moved them here for reasons still unknown. He told him to do it, so he did. He never questioned, he just did, and things were better because of it. The fifth stone sits off a bit from the others. The snow makes it impossible to read, as the snow as seamed to cover it from view. But it’s this fifth stone he stares at. He walks over to it, and brushed the snow away from it. Damien David Teri 1976 - DDT stares at his own headstone. He stands, and begins to walk towards a large mansion. Its appearance is at first, of an abandoned house, weather beaten and in a state of disrepair. Looks can be deceiving as this one is. The closer we come, less worn it becomes. The rear porch that from afar looked ready to fall down, now looks as it may have just been built. DDT walks up the stairs, and finds his way to a large wicker chair that sits there. The wind and snow blow furiously outside, yet no snow falls on the porch. DDT sits, and picks up the glass the sits on the table next to it. Bourbon.....his favorite. He drinks it quickly, and puts the glass down on the table. He sits back in the chair, and stares into the winter storm ahead of him..... The winter is by far the fiercest of enemies man can face. Wind, water, temperature........sometimes all, sometimes one, but all at their worst.........when extreme. DDT reaches into the cabinet under the table, and pulls out a bottle of Makers Mark. He pours another glass, and returns the bottle back to the cabinet. When the weather gets this way, the average Joe Shmoe runs for the shelters. He turns the heat up, and stays inside until it is safe, until the extremities they so fear are no longer a factor. They wait as long as it takes, only braving them when necessary....work, shopping, taking out the trash. DDT sips his whiskey. He swirls it around his glass, staring into it as he does so. I suppose at this point I’m supposed to relate this to the upcoming Extreme Title Contenders Rumble I’m in. I’m supposed to create an elaborate simile of the weather to my ability to fight within “extreme elements.” I could do that..... But it’s a moot point. To sit here and tell you about why I think I can win is irrelevant. Seventeen people, all different shapes, sexes, and sizes will enter at random times. We’ll fight till there’s one man standing, with no rules other then when you’re out....you’re out. It’s organized chaos. It’s madness..... DDT smiles. It’s awesome. DDT puts the glass down. He stands up and walks in the doorway next to his chair, and into the old mansion. The inside of this place is old...very old. A layer of dust covers the walls, but as DDT opens the door, a gust of wind follows him through, blowing it from the hall. He takes a left, then a right, then another right, then down a staircase, walking through a maze that only he and a few others could navigate with such ease. He passes doorways, leading to rooms even he’s never seen, yet never has to, and probably never will. He stop at one large door, and walks through. The room is huge, the ceiling much higher then it should be for the house, yet it is. DDT stands in the doorway and looks across the room, and there sits a wrestling ring. He walks over to it, and runs his hand over the apron. This ring has seen extreme. I once had friends who would stand toe to toe with me in this very ring. We’d spill one another’s blood, not just for training and perfection of our craft, but sometimes just for fun. DDT hops into the ring. He walks over to a corner, and climbs it, perching himself on the turnbuckle. He rests his head in his hands. Ray broke my nose with a chair....Sidhe and I busted The Ripper wide open....then he used the same chair to do the same to us. Even Kat got angry at me once, and stabbed me in the thigh, just because I smacked her in the ass with a towel instead of hitting her with a 2x4. I miss those days...... DDT sighs.....but shakes his head, clearing the memories away. I can't really relate those days to this match. So why think about them? I tell you why...because I want to. I want to remember what passion I felt for being in this ring. I want to remember the days when I craved spilling the blood of another person, even at the risk of my own well being. I didn’t care. It made me alive. It made me what I needed to be. It made me an icon.... DDT reaches behind his head, and feels a scar. A scar that he’ll hold dear, and perhaps even fear a bit. It’s what kept him out of the ring for two months. The plate that is there now is a constant reminder that he was hurt by the one thing he loved. What I think extreme is......it’s a moot point. I can tell you being thrown from the top of a twenty foot cage or using C4 is extreme. I can tell you picking up an announcer and using him as a weapon is extreme. I could even tell you being put through a table on to a concrete floor, having your skull cracked and needing a plate to fix it, and then going after not one, but three...heh....FOUR of the men responsible is extreme. But you may not agree. I don’t think being tied to a cross is all that extreme, yet Jason Marah and his “teacher” found it useful. Somehow it will prepare you...yet I know I personally won’t have a cross handy. And if I did, I wouldn’t tie you to it....I’d more then likely do it properly. I’m not that convinced that Lacunia is extreme. Her parents died a horrible death. So did mine. They burned. Mine too. She got millions of dollars. I got put in an Asylum and blamed for their death. You see my point? Moraless? Extreme? Jade? Vertigo? DDT shakes his head. I could go on and on about all of you. Even you Shadow...and I’ve watched what you call extreme up close. But in the end, it’s all a bunch of crap. What is extreme? Who fucking cares? I’m not going into that ring to prove to anyone that I’m extreme, hardcore, ultra violent, or whatever name you want to put to it. I give a rats ass about any of the things any of you have done in the past, or will do in the future. I don’t care if you’re a woman, and man, or a transsexual. It makes no difference to me whether you walk into that ring first or last or whether you walk out at all. What I want is blood. I want to feel it running over my hands as I choke the life out of you. I want to feel it splatter on my face as I bash yours in. I even want to feel my own blood run from my veins onto the canvas. I want bones to break, sinew to tear, tendons to snap. I want the screams of pain and anguish to be louder then the cheers of the masses, who’s bloodlust may be greater then our own. DDT is breathing heavy now. Sweat is beginning to form on his brow. But most of all....I want to smell the fear of each and every one of you. You can’t deny it’s there. I feel it to! I feel the panic as my lifeblood slips away drop by drop. As I cry out, I wonder if it will be my last breath, my final words, all in unmistakable pain and anguish. You feel it too....and you all love it. Maybe more then you realize, and maybe less then you’re prepared to deal with. DDT wipes his forehead. I’ve seen death. I’ve watched the people closest to me die in flame, in a mass of twisted metal, and recently, figuratively in their own self loathing. Each time, it’s taken me closer to my own end. So much so....I know where my final place of rest lies. I’ve dug my own grave...and I accept that one day, I’ll be sent there. That day may some soon. It could be one of you who do it. DDT scratches his chin. It could come down to me and one of you. We could stand toe to toe, face to face, with everything on the line. You may have my neck in your arms, squeezing it, ceasing the flow of air to my lungs, denying my brain precious oxygen. As I struggle, everything you want is there before your eyes...the glory, the fame, the right to be someone. As you dream this, you realize that the only way this can be achieved is to end my struggle. To clamp down harder, and ultimately, end my life. Would you do it? Could you? DDT smiles. It would be a large price to pay...and may even be considered “Extreme”. DDT hops down from the turnbuckle, and begins to walk to the door. I know you're all will to be as extreme as possible to win this. You'll pull out all the stops. Nothing can stop you..... can it? As DDT walks through the door, his last words echo through the gym. Now there's a question worth asking.
