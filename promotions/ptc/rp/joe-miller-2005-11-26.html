Date: 2005-11-26 The camera comes into focus after some difficulty, the sounds of a monkey can be heard and after a moment more of shaky picture we realize that it's a monkey behind the camera, the monkey makes a few more chittering noises and the speaks perfect English dominated heavily by a British accent "Alright Mister Miller you may proceed when ready" "Alright thanks Ben…Hello GTT5….I uh…I guess this is a message for all you folks back there living in the past…for a uh…" he looks down at the paper in his hands "For a uh Stan "The Pit Fighter Jones" Uh Hi Stan…I’m Joe Miller and I’m coming to you from the future…The Association of Journalistic Flying Monkeys has been kind enough to loan me a camera and an operator…say hi Ben" The monkey speaks from off screen. "Hi Ben" "Erm…ok good enough, Now Stan I’ve studied up on wrastlers….er Wrestlers of the time period and I’ve discovered that…" Joe is cut off by Ben. "Mister Miller you’re going to have to hold on…the red light thingy is blinking…I think I’m out of Batteries…one moment please" Joe suffers this in relative silence muttering under his breath once "Stupid flying Monkeys…" "What was that!?" "Erm nothing…nothing….are we uh…rolling again?" "….Yes" "Alright thanks Ben..now uh Mister Jones…I’ve studied up on wrestling promotions of your time period and it’s quite different from here in the year six thousand…a lot more verbal, it seems that you wrestling matches back then were a lot of talk…so I find myself forced to comply with the time period and uh…"Trash Talk…so without further ado I will…" "Uh Mister Miller? The red light is blinking again…hold on…those must not have been fresh batteries" "Are you serious!" Joe Miller Declares unhappily "For crying out loud! I’m trying to conduct a promo here…can I get another Monkey in here?" "HEY! Look asshole I went to Yale! TO YALE!!! So you sit your ass down and stop your complaining!" Ben the tech monkey sputters and screeches and Joe wisely quiets down, after a few more minutes of fiddling the picture comes back into focus and the monkey speaks, a certain terseness detected in his tone. "Go ahead Mister Miller…" "Thank you…ok…see Stan I’ve compiled some trash talk with which to heap verbal abuse on you and hopefully demoralize you to the point of making a mistake in the ring…so without ado….ummm….I’m going to kick you in the balls….urm…Your mother was a whore….you’re hung like a…like a….a wha…oh a flea! Yes! Your genitals are the exact proportion of a fleas! Ummm and last but certainly not least….your ah…your mother dresses you funny" Suddenly Ben the tech monkey speaks "Uh Mister Miller…that pesky red light is blinking again I think I need to…" But Ben is cut off as Joe stand up and throws his papers up in the air in frustration. "That’s it! I’ve HAD IT! I can’t work under these conditions! Yale? You went to Yale? YALE MY ASS…You stupid flying monkey!! I mean you ride around on a jet pack fer crying out loud…how can you be so blasted stupid!!!" The sounds of an angry monkey can be heard and the camera begins to shake violently, Joes eyes widen. "Hey! Hey what are you…what are you doing? Hey! Hey that’s gross you put that down! Don’t you throw that! NO!" A sudden wet and quite brown splotch appears on the wall next to Joe who ducks and dives out of the way his face a mask of revulsion. "You little bastard!! That was disgusting! When I get my hands on you I’ll…." The camera slumps downward as the sounds of a uber pissed off monkey can be heard rampaging through the room, screams and yells and furniture being destroyed with reckless abandon can be heard but thankfully no picture but the floor greets our eyes, every now and then a wet splat punctuates the angry monkey screeches until finally judging from Joes cry of revulsion….one connects. "AHH! OH GOD!!! You …YOU HIT ME!! ARGH! YOU LITTLE BASTARD!! My…MY MOUTH WAS OPEN!! MY MOUTH WAS OPEN!!!" The screen wavers for a moment and then fades to static, Joe can be heard in the background retching until mercifully….all is black. Guess those batteries were duds after all.
