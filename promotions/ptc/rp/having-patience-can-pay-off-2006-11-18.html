Having Patience Can Pay Off

18 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-18 "What else? What else is there?!" Killean exclaimed as he sat at the bedside of his best friend. He was holding Dave's warm yet weak hand in his own trying to squeeze some life back into it. Killean had been there for a couple hours now, staying by Dave’s side. The doctor had visited the room earlier to talk about the heart attack. It was not as severe as originally thought. After some tests, Dave’s circulation turned out to be excellent and there was no need for bypass surgery as originally expected. He remembered word for word what the doctor said as he flashed a bright smile. "The circulation is excellent. The blood pressure is a little high but that can be corrected. The exact cause of the attack is still under review but we have deemed stress to be the main factor. We’ll keep you posted if we find anything else on the tests." Stress… as the doctor read off his notes, Killean silently wondered if he would be next. What would it be to push him over the edge? His current lack of activity in PRIME? The situation with Charity? What about Mary and Matt in what seemed like a disagreement on helping him? Or could it be GTT6 and his upcoming match against Atlantic Wrestling Club member AgentDash? "No…" Scolding his negative thoughts, he tried to get himself out of that mindset. It took exponential amounts of stress to trigger something like a heart attack, especially in someone of Killean’s age. Vegas was older than him, but was quite possibly in equivalent shape. But then what was it that caused Vegas to be under such stress? Only one thing came to Sirrajin’s mind. Killean had a working exemption to attend wrestling shows. However, since the supposed "crimes" took place at his own business, he was prohibited from attending his duties there. Therefore, Vegas had to pick up the slack and take on his duties as well. It surely would be enough to stress someone out. The driver who Killean punished for his drug pushing was gone from the company and the position had still not been filled. Some loads had to be pawned off to other companies and the existing drivers were already working overtime. Killean constantly wished he could be there to help pick up the slack and possibly do a couple runs himself. However, the tether that was around his ankle told him he could just stay home where they felt he belonged. So here he was, at his best friend’s bed side and the room being patrolled by another police officer. More than ever, they feared him as a flight risk. So when he tried to get up and go outside to make a phone call, you can bet someone had a problem with that. "Do you really need to follow me around like I’m a child? I do have GPS on my ass… ankle if you wanna be technical." He was being outwardly sarcastic but in a way he was kind of being honest as well. "Standard procedure I can assure you." The heavy set black man said. The tag on his uniform said Dixon. "Officer Dixon I presume?" The officer nodded in response. "I’m just stepping outside to check my voicemail from my phone. You can accompany me if you like, but please don’t stick to me like a friggin’ spider monkey." Hey, Killean was just being honest again. This guy was hanging around him like he had a donut in his coat pocket. But unfortunately for Killean, his night was about to get a lot worse. "Where’s the handcuffs?" One nurse blurted out from her position behind one of the nurses tables. Killean immediately stopped and turned, noticing the middle aged half blonde half grey haired woman staring him down. She must either have known who he was or was told by a colleague. That or it was kind of made obvious with the ever tiresome police escorts. "What was that?" Killean asked as he made a few steps over to the desk. "I asked where the handcuffs were. Who knows? Perhaps you might haul off on one of us next you savage." Mighty strong words from a mighty small, older lady. Officer Dixon took a couple steps to his right and stopped Killean from approaching the counter closer. "Who are you to fuckin’ judge me?!" His voice contorted as he cursed and pointed at the woman. She pointed right back at him. "He should be in jail, not walking around in public!" She responded as he backed away from the officer’s grasp. Instead of raising his voice anymore, he just smiled and tipped his head to the nurse. "I apologize for raising my voice Ma’am." He was now calm and waved to the nurse as he walked away. "After all, you’d know about people who’ve been arrested for violent crimes wouldn’t you? Your husband is a regular down at the precinct for beating your frigid ass." A few inaudible words came from the nurse’s mouth after he finished his comments. The officer accompanying him actually broke a smile at the comment as well. That’s what he needed to do. He needed to start making positives for himself out of bad and possibly explosive situations. Sure the nurse probably wasn’t happy with his comments, but he made himself feel good. With a hearty laugh, he could actually feel some of the stress leaving him and his shoulders beginning to lighten up. There wouldn’t always be nurses around to badmouth him and berate him so his challenge would be to find the best way to stay positive. His next situation would actually slap him in the face. Outside, as he dialed the numbers to access his telephone systems voicemail, Officer Dixon lit up a smoke and exhaled a large amount. "You know those things will kill you…" He said matter of factly, knowing that the officer would be unaware that Killean had smoked again only a week earlier. "So can this cold weather, but we live with it." Dixon replied in short time after taking another puff from the cancerous bad habit. Killean found that he had one message and pressed ‘1’ to play it. Voice: Hi Killean, its Mary here… He stood silently, stewing in his own thoughts. She had taken a rather long pause and then she stumbled over her words. Two words he distinguished were couldn’t and no. With a whisk of air heard on the message, she brought the phone down, ending the call and whatever hopes Killean had of having Mary on his side to help him out. It was a setback certainly. Killean had enough of those to endure in this already long quest to try and salvage his life and make sense of some things. He didn’t hang his head however. Instead, after realizing that Mary wasn’t going to be able to help him, he held his head high and put the phone back in his pocket after turning it off. He nodded to Officer Dixon who put his cigarette into the nearby ashtray. Dixon then accompanied The Supreme Machine inside the hospital once more so he could be with his friend. ~o~ Monday, November 13, 2006 The last couple days had gone by in a flash it seemed. Killean was constantly at the hospital with Dave Vegas. Since the day of the heart attack, Vegas had become totally conscious and was now mostly aware of his surroundings. He would be going home today and Killean was just now walking back in the door of his loft from his final visit. He helped Dave get some things ready to go and after Vegas was on his way in a taxi; Killean was immediately accompanied back to his own home via dog walker… police officer. As he flopped onto the couch, he kicked off his shoes and turned on the television. As soon as the screen came to life, the phone rang. Sighing due to just wanting to be lazy, he moves along the couch and fetches the cordless phone from its perch. His eyes gaze over the small screen on the phone to check just who is calling. "Mary and Matt W." Another audible sigh. He really didn’t want to answer the phone to find out more about why Mary couldn’t help him with Charity. Matt probably came home, ripped life a new asshole with a tantrum about how much he hates Killean and then drank a Mountain Dew and packed for ReVolution 111. Killean was only slated to appear for his JITC Match against Adam and then he was ordered to pack up to return home. He would then get ready to attend his match for GTT6 against AgentDash scheduled for the weekend. He only had two more rings before the answering service would have picked up, so he needed to make a decision. He could answer the phone and have his ear gabbed off about why Mary is faithful to her husband… or he could be an asshole and let it ring to the answering machine. What to do, what to do… His kind and relatively exhausted heart thought the first option would work so he pressed the button on the phone to answer. Killean: Hello? Mary: Hi Killean. Really, did anyone expect Matt W. to call Killean? No? Didn’t think so. Killean: How are you Mary? Mary: I’m good, I have Georgie here on my lap was I talk to you. I’m sure she would say hi to her Uncle Killean if she knew it was you. How are you doing? Color him confused. He hadn’t heard the words Uncle and Killean in the same sentence referring to the same person ever since the problems started between himself and Matt. Before Georgie was even born, Killean was selected to be an honorary Uncle to her and he happily accepted the offer. But why did Mary say that now? He shrugged it off, not wanting to look too deep into the possible slip from Mary that she may be ashamed of. Killean: Well, be sure to give her a quick hug for me. I’m doing alright for now. Been dealing with Dave being in the hospital. Mary: Oh, I’m sorry, I had no idea he was there. Killean: Yeah, he finally got let out today. Had a minor heart attack and the docs thought it was from stress. Mary: Oh, well be sure to wish him well from us. Us? Was Matt there too? Usually he could tell if Matt was around Mary when he talked to her because she really watched what she said. She would stumble, stop talking all together and even hang up the phone, much like last Wednesday. But Matt was not there Wednesday, he was ReVolution 110. But once again, he was not willing to dig any deeper into a possible word slip from Mary. Mary: Anyways Killean, the reason I called was regarding Charity. W… errrr I found out some information about where exactly she is and when they arrived there. Ok, now he was really confused. First Mary told him the other day that she couldn’t help and now all of a sudden she had some information? How? Why? Killean: Really? How did you find out? Mary: It’s just… I… awwww heck, I’m just gonna tell you exactly what’s been going on here. She paused as she cleared her throat. Killean could hear her moving around in the background, possibly trying to settle into the couch to tell Killean exactly what was on her mind. He waited with the patience of a man who had an incurable disease, wondering just when he would be taken into the light. Mary: Killean, when I told you the other day that we couldn’t help, I unknowingly lied to you about it. Killean: Unknowingly? What do you mean? Mary: Matt told me that we couldn’t help. Turns out that all along ever since I told him that they were possibly in the area you described, Matt has been looking into it in secrecy. He was the one that brought this information back to me. He told me not to tell you that it was him but I can’t lie to you… I can’t lie to anyone. You know that. The only thing running through Killean’s mind at that moment was reaching down to pick up his jaw that hit the floor at breakneck speed. Matt was HELPING him in secrecy? Matt W.? The guy who wrapped a chair around his head was now offering help? Perhaps Mr. Ward isn’t so Inhuman after all. Killean: Ma… M… MATT?! He went out and did me a favor? Mary: I have no clue what his motives were, but I know that he hasn’t stopped talking about it since he went to check. He said the place seemed creepy and for Matt to find anything creepy is rare. It had to be bad. He said that when he confronted the people on the large piece of property and asked for someone named Charity; the guys eyes lit up like he had just been kicked in the shin and said there was no one there by that name and that he was trespassing. Killean: Nothing happened I hope… Mary: No, there was no physicality. They kept asking him to leave and he noticed a rather large building in the distance. He said that when he asked then about it, the three men turned and moved towards him. One of them had a shovel. So to avoid any other problems, he left. But he could tell just by the reaction of the man that Charity was indeed there. He actually stayed a little further from the property which is accessible by a dead end road. He noticed a few vehicles driving down there and one of the girls in the back looked like Charity. He immediately came back and told me what he found out. That was the first I even knew of him doing this for you. He originally told me he was seeing some old clients again. What could Killean really think at that moment? He had just found out that his sworn nemesis was helping him behind everyone’s back. This wasn’t just any la-dee-da situation either that could be taken with a grain of salt. This specific issue struck a chord deep inside Killean’s heart and he couldn’t believe for one second that Matt could cue up the heart to assist him in any way. Maybe he didn’t give Matt enough credit. There was much more history behind their relationship that any wrestling fan could ever know. They were friends, almost best of friends between the time that Killean showed up in PRIME and before Matt showed up on the same stage. Either way, much of the animosity for Matt was fading as he thought about it because there was no way it could be untrue. Mary just said she couldn’t lie and she was right. Killean had never known her to lie and this was just another situation that she had to be telling the truth. She would not dare pull at his heartstrings, she just isn’t like that. He knew he had to attend ReVolution 111 to meet Adam in the JITC, but now he had another reason to be there. He knew that Mary had told him not to say anything, but other ideas came to mind. ~o~ Wednesday, November 17, 2006 PRIME Presents ReVolution 111 The fans in the arena were growing ever impatient with the growing tensions in the ring and that was voiced by their constant loud nature. Tchu was out there trying to help Alexander Kannon from ending up like his Uncle Xavier but he was fighting a losing fight. What part of it was a shoot or a work, he didn’t really know. But Killean knew that something had to be done and this was the perfect moment to take action. He kind of knew that Tchu would be drawn to any heinous activities Fuck You had planned and sure enough, it was all going down in the ring as he ran to stand in front of the curtain. It was the only thing keeping him out of view of the PRIME fans and the individuals in the ring. He had caught the action on the monitor and knew from the beginning that Matt was playing right into their hands by approaching the ring. He would be murdered if someone didn’t make their way out to assist him. The plan was set earlier in the day. Killean made a phone call to none other than the Devil’s Don himself. Deville was pleased to hear that Killean was intent on joining their little group to rid them of their only obstacle after young Kannon was out of the way. He got along with Deville in the past but he knew that Deville never thought twice about him otherwise. With the information he found out about Matt helping him, he could not stand idly by and watch as the man who helped him got ground into the ring canvas. Matt was tough, but Fuck You was just too much for any one man. So he lured Deville right into his trap and now it was about to unfold just as he had planned. He didn’t want to trick Angelo because he respected him, but he couldn’t not help Matt. As he noticed on the screen to his left that Matt had slipped into the ring and the four members of Fuck You were converging to end his very existence, he knew it was now time to swerve the world once again. He would make it seem like he was to help them destroy Matt W. No one would be the wiser, especially Angelo Deville and perhaps even Matt himself. He signaled to the music and pyrotechnics crew stage side to start his music and he heard the fans gasp in surprise and fire boos towards the curtain as they awaited his appearance. Microphone in hand and trademark heel type smirk on his face, he stepped through the curtain. The fans got louder with their heat and the action in the ring stopped. All eyes were set on him. He didn’t care what management thought. He didn’t care what anyone thought. He knew he was going out there without proper authorization but it wasn’t an issue to him. Screw the storytelling and screw the angles and written script. He was issuing his own rewrite and for once he was going to swerve the world for all the right reasons. He was going to swerve the world to return a favor to an old friend…
