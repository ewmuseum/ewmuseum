Nobody Does It Worser - Episode 3

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 The scene opens up in the locker room of the Boomer Sooners. Saij is standing by himself, his trademark goofy grin spread across his face. Saij: HUZZAH~! and greetings, all you Fark-O-Maniacs! Saij here! I just wanted to send out a message to that motherfarkin’ son of a monkey’s uncle, The Masked Winstrom! I hope you like whoop-ass, ya farkin’ bastard, cuz I’m gonna open a can of it when we face off in our round two match at GTT5! So get ready for Fark-O-Mania to run wild all over… At that moment, fellow Boomer Sooners Dave Kipfer and (freshly-crowned XUW World Champion) Fatbadger step in. Dave: Uh, Saij? You’re not facing Masked Winstrom at GTT5. Saij: Huh? I’m not? Fatbadger: Yeah dude. You’re like, not even IN the tournament and stuff. Saij: Fark! Why the hell did I think I was facing Masked Winstrom at GTT5 then? Dave: I dunno. It’s a shame you’re not though. That match would’ve been awesome. Fatbadger: Yeah, but it’s not happening, so now GTT5 is gonna suck worse than a match between Jimmy Bong and Kenny Mercury. Saij: Wow. That’s pretty bad. Ah well, let’s go drink some Code Red or something. HUZZAH~! They all start to leave. Saij: Hey, let’s play a game! Raise your hand if you’ve been on the PTC Top Ten list! Dave and Saij both raise their hands. Fatbadger cries. The scene fades to black. --- MAYBE ENTERPRISES, TRAINING FACILITY – CHICAGO THAT AFTERNOON AS THE SUN SETS OVER THE FAIR CITY OF CHICAGO O’CLOCK "Half past five and it’s already dark out… I hate that about the winter sometimes. Anyway, this is the place, sir." "About time, my moustache is nearly frozen solid." Chicago certainly looked like something out of a Christmas card in the daylight, but the ever-blackening sky was quite adept at dampening such warm spirits. A stinging cold punctuated the wind this evening. The gloomy air felt quite like it had been infected by death’s spectre. Dark buildings and a sparse array of streetlamps did little to quell the sense of foreboding in this particular section of town. One would certainly have to be surprised to find any signs of life here. As it happened, one would find such a surprise upon closer inspection. Two men were approaching what seemed to be an abandoned storehouse, tucked away between other high-rise structures, hidden from the city’s populace. No windows, no signs of life were to be found anywhere in its frigid brick walls. The only way in was a locked steel door facing the street. A small dot of red light at the front entrance served as the only indication that the building was of any importance—it marked the location of a card scanner. One of the men—a pale, bespectacled blond forty-something—dug into his coat pocket and procured a key card with the unmistakable Maybe Enterprises corporate logo printed on front. He swiped the card once through the scanner, and the light switched from red to green. With a click and a groan, the steel door slowly creaked open. Fluorescent light spilled out into the unrelenting darkness, welcoming the pale blond man and his companion—an older white-haired gentleman with a thick frosty moustache. "Are these security measures really necessary?" the older man asked. "O-Of course," replied the blond man a bit nervously. "This facility is where our top-secret athletic training program is being put to its final test before wide-scale implementation commences. We can’t have our competitors skulking about and nicking our ideas, now can we?" "Hmph. No, I suppose not." "Now please come in, we’d best not dally… our guard gets antsy sometimes." They stepped through the threshold, escaping the bone-chilling night air. The door clanged shut behind them. Now they stood before another steel door. To the right of it was a console with a handprint scanner and a microphone. On the left was a window, reminiscent of a ticket booth. Behind the window was a security guard with the bill of his hat pulled down over his face. He was asleep. "Crack security staff you have here, doctor," the older man chortled sarcastically. "Er, yes… indeed," the blond man muttered, embarrassed. He marched up to the window, looking fully intent on banging his fist on the glass in a fit of rage. Instead, he just tapped politely. "I-I beg your pardon," he spoke timidly, "but please wake up. You’re sleeping on the job and that is against company r-regulations." The security guard stirred just a little bit, not even bothering to look up. "Mmmm… what… no, you idiot…" he mumbled, then fell back to sleep. "Oh… I-I see," the blond man replied, taken aback. "W-Well fine then. I imagine your supervisor will give you a poor review during the annual end-of-year evaluations for this. Have a g-good night." The older man rolled his eyes. "Honestly Kent, grow a backbone, would you? I’m here to see this training program in action, not to watch you shrivel up like a phallus in ice water." "I-I’m just a lab scientist, not a… not a… well, never mind," the blond man said meekly. "I’ll let us in, hold on one second…" He walked up to the door and placed his hand on the scanner. At the same time, he spoke his name into the microphone. "Dr. Dennis Kent, Research & Development, Maybe Enterprises Sports Division." At once, the steel door slid open. The lifeless, monotone voice of the computer spouted back, "Welcome, Dr. Dennis Kent. You may proceed. Have a nice day." Dr. Kent and the older gentleman walked through and found themselves inside a state-of-the-art fitness center. Fluorescent lights hung from the ceiling to illuminate the facility. The far wall across from them was lined entirely with mirrors. The walls along the floor were lined with a myriad weight-lifting machines and equipment. A staircase in the corner led up to a second level, a balcony that ran along the entire perimeter of the wall. Treadmills, exercise bikes, and stair-masters were to be found there. The centerpiece of it all, however, was a wrestling ring set up in the middle of the floor. "No damnit, no!" barked an angry voice from the ring. "You stupid son of a bitch, can’t you get anything through that thick skull of yours!? Now get the hell out of those ropes and try again!" The orders came from the mouth of a tall, muscular man. He must have served in the military, the older man figured, for his hair was trimmed to precision in an army buzz-cut, and he wore an olive wifebeater with camouflage pants and boots. The target of his ire was a young man tangled upside-down in the ropes. He was wearing a tuxedo. It was Jimmy Bong. "You know," Bong said with a smile, "it’s rather silly that I can’t seem to get into the ring without getting stuck in these ropes. Six career tournament matches, honestly! You’d think after all of that, I’d have figured this out by now! Good heavens, I must be a complete moron!" "You’re a complete JACKASS, solider!" the military man grunted. "Now quit talkin’ and get ready to go again!" As Bong worked himself out of the ropes, Kent approached the ring with his companion. The sight of Bong tangled in the ropes unnerved him even more. "Erm, w-well," he gulped. "This is Jimmy Bong, our, erm… test subject… as you can see. Hoo boy." "Not exactly impressive, is he?" the older man snorted. "This p-program takes time," Kent replied. "At any rate, our latest findings show that his strength has increased ten-fold already." "Really? I find that difficult to believe, but that sounds impressive." "It’s quite true, although… w-well, he couldn’t even tear a piece of paper in half when he got here, so that’s still not a very significant increase." "Hm… what about that other man?" "That is Gordon Gloucester, formerly a sergeant in the United States army. Has several years of wrestling experience too, I believe. He was hired recently by Maybe Enterprises to assist in the project. I-I don’t know much about him besides that." The man in the army garb, Gloucester, looked contemptibly at Kent. "Hey!" he shouted. "What the HELL are you doing here, Kent? This is supposed to be a private training session!" "It’s not that private," Bong interjected, almost right-side up now, "with that Erik Lang fellow constantly poking his head in and out all afternoon." "Did I give you permission to talk!?" Gloucester snarled. "Well no," Bong said, "But in all fairness, you didn’t give me permission to be silent, either." Gloucester’s eyes went wide. He walked up to Bong, who pulled his foot from the ropes and was finally free, standing on the apron. "What…" he whispered, "…did you say to me?" "Oh, terribly sorry, I suppose in my awkward position I didn’t project my voice loudly enough," Bong started to explain nicely, "but what I said was…" "I think I distinctly heard you ask me to CASTRATE YOU WITH A WOODEN SPOON!" "Oh. Well, that’s not exactly what I…" "IF YOU EVER MOUTH OFF TO ME AGAIN, I WILL FASTEN YOUR BALLS IN A VICE AND LOCK YOU IN A GARAGE WITH THE CAR RUNNING!!!" There was a moment of dead silence. "Now hang on," said Bong incredulously. "How on earth are you going to castrate me with a wooden spoon and then lock my testicles in a vice? Your threats are completely incompatible with one another! Not to mention they sound like something someone’s girlfriend might say…" "Just… just SHUT UP!" Gloucester said finally. He couldn’t believe what a genuine idiot Jimmy Bong was. Dozens of soldiers under his command in the army would have pissed their pants by now. Unfortunately for Gloucester, he was dealing with a man too stupid to know fear. The security door opened once again. Bong looked in that direction and groaned disdainfully as Erik Lang sauntered into the room, looking his usual quirky and jovial self. "And HOW is our warrior doing?" he asked gleefully. "I do hope the training is going well!" "It’s going about as well as the Osama hunt," griped Gloucester. "Seriously, this guy is the dumbest piece of shit I’ve ever been in a ring with. He’s the only man I know who would actually get smarter from a lobotomy." Lang clasped his hands in delight. "EXCELLENT~! That’s exactly the Jimmy Bong I want the whole world to see! A man who stands up to danger because he’s too thick to know he’s in danger! A man who will fight to the death for… THE FORCES OF STUPID~!" Bong and Gloucester groaned. They apparently had at least one thing they agreed upon: Lang was a bigger nutcase than even Bong was. The older gentlemen who had accompanied Dr. Kent into the facility looked dumbfounded. "Forces of stupid?" he repeated. "What the hell are you talking about? Kent, who is this man?" "I should ask the same about you!" Lang shot back, eyes narrowing. "Who are you? Where did you come from? ARE YOU… PURE EVIL!?" "N-no!" Kent half-shouted (for that was as best as he could shout). "Th-this man is Mr. Xavier Pendable, one of Professor Maybe’s investors!" Bong’s eyebrows lifted. "Pendable?" he asked himself. "Where have I heard that name… oh yes, of course! In the office earlier today!" He jumped off the ring apron and made his way to the old man, shaking his hand when he got close enough. "Pleasure to meet you, Mr. Pendable!" Pendable smiled. "You too, Mr. Bong." "Please, you can call me Jimmy," Bong smiled back. "Very well then, Jimmy! And in return, you may call me Xavier, or just ‘X’ for short as my friends do." "Hm," Bong considered. "‘X’ Pendable? Why, that’s not an obviously foreboding name at all!" "Uh, no," Pendable replied a bit hesitantly, "I don’t suppose it is." "EXCUSE me," Lang interrupted, getting impatient. "But just WHAT are you doing here, Mr. Mandible?" "That’s Pendable," he corrected indignantly. "But anyway… as an investor in Maybe Enterprises, and in particular a significant financial backer for this project," Pendable motioned to Bong and to the wrestling ring, "I simply wanted to see first-hand how my money was being put to use. It was difficult getting access to this place from Professor Maybe, but Dr. Kent was gracious enough to bring me along for a guided tour." "Feh, guided tour," grumbled Gloucester. "This is supposed to be a Goddamn PRIVATE training session! Why can’t you jackasses get that through your heads!? Bong here can’t even get himself through the ropes, so how the hell am I supposed to get him ready to face a technical mastermind like Kenny Mercury if we keep being interrupted!?" "Bah, who cares about whether or not he has any training?" Lang scoffed. "Everyone knows that to get anywhere in wrestling, you need to have the right IMAGE. And I have something here that will help improve Mr. Bong’s image A HUNDRED THOUSAND-FOLD~!" "Do you have to end all your speeches with the tilde-exclamation point combo?" Bong asked. "Breaking the fourth wall does NOT help your image, you know!" Lang retorted. "You know, telling me not to break the fourth wall is also breaking the fourth wall, in and of itself," Bong shot back. "Touché," said Lang. "Anyway, I have something for you… something that EVERY wrestler needs! A VALET~!" "Really?" asked Bong, his face lighting up. "Oh, goody! I was hoping I’d get a token female companion sooner or later! Where is she?" "Why… right here, of course!" Lang declared, throwing his arms into the air. Everybody else looked around, but saw nobody. "Uh, M-Mr. Lang?" Kent spoke up after a moment. "Th-there’s nobody around." "Oh, I’m sorry!" Lang apologized bashfully. "How rude of me! I’ve been standing in front of her this entire time!" He stepped aside, to reveal… A FEMALE MIDGET! She was as beautiful in body as she was big in stature—which is to say she was pretty damn ugly. "Everyone, meet Samantha ‘Sweetie’ Shortcakes!" came Lang’s proud proclamation. "She will accompany Jimmy Bong to all of his matches from now on!" Nobody quite knew what to say. They couldn’t help but stare at the grotesque dwarf-sized diva… you know, in the same way people can’t help but stare at a horrible car accident. A moment later, Gloucester burst out laughing. "My prostate is bigger than that chick!" he guffawed. "Goodness, you should really have your prostate checked then," replied Bong. Gloucester looked about ready to plant his foot up Bong’s ass, when Pendable chimed in. "So, uh," he started, "does she… you know, speak?" "OF COURSE ME SPEAK," Sweetie grunted loudly. Everyone nearly jumped out of their skins at the sound of her voice, which was something akin to Barry White with a head cold—only a ten times louder. "You’ll have to forgive her," Lang sighed with a woeful expression on his face. "The poor girl has the verbal capacity of a caveman, and she has some sort of disorder that prevents her from speaking with any kind of vocal inflection. THAT IS TO SAY, SHE CAN ONLY SPEAK VERY LOUDLY, LIKE THIS~!" Bong hung his head in despair. "Why are all the women I work with freaks?" he asked himself sadly. Sweetie overheard him. "YOU NO LIKE SWEETIE?" she asked angrily. "YOU GO TO HELL!" She proceeded to kick Bong in the shin. "OW! MY SHIN!" Bong wailed, hopping up and down on one foot while grabbing at the tender area. "That’s the second bloody time today!" "Oh yes, I forgot!" Lang smiled. "Sweetie is also a distant relative of Carol, Professor Maybe’s receptionist!" "How… charming," Bong spouted through gritted teeth. "Ahem," came the gruff sound of Pendable’s voice. "Jimmy, I would like to have a word with you for a moment… in private," he finished, looking at everyone else. "B-But sir," Kent tried to intervene, "P-Professor Maybe s-said that under n-no circumstances were you t-to…" "I don’t care what Stuart said!" Pendable barked. "It’s my money he’s using, so I’m damn well entitled! Jimmy, over here if you please." He started walking to the far side of the room, next to the mirrors along the wall. Bong hobbled over to join him, away from all the others. "Jimmy," Pendable began with a sigh. "It is with great personal risk that I’ve come to see you this evening." "I’ll say, with that walking miniature death machine wandering about," Bong grumbled, shooting a sideways glance back at his new valet. "No, no, that’s not what I meant," Pendable half-chuckled. "What I mean is… well, how shall I put this… I have reason to believe that this ‘training program’ you’re undergoing isn’t exactly all it appears to be." "Really?" asked Bong, cocking an eyebrow in surprise. "How so?" "I don’t know exactly," Pendable continued, "but I have the feeling that foul play is afoot. Have you noticed anything particularly strange about your daily regimen?" Bong paused for a moment to think. It was a miracle his head didn’t explode. "Well," he said finally, "not really. The workouts seem just like any other ordinary workout, to be honest. Same soreness afterward and everything," he rubbed his left arm. "I’m sort of wondering when the ‘innovative’ part of this program will come into play, because quite frankly nothing about this seems ‘innovative’ to me at all." "And yet your strength has increased more rapidly than it should under a normal training program?" "It has, actually!" Bong smiled with excitement. "Look what I can do now…" He reached underneath a bench a pulled out a Chicago telephone book. "I can rip this thing in half, watch!" Bong gripped the phone book tightly, and attempted to rend it in twain. After about two full minutes, he gave up, exhausted. "What the…?" he asked himself, confounded. "Oh wait, I did that wrong. I can rip it in half, just not all at once. Here, look!" He tore out a page, and ripped it in half with ease. "That’s… um… remarkable," said Pendable. "But anyway, I wanted to warn you, Jimmy. If I’m right about this training program, and there’s more to Professor Maybe’s plans than we know, then we might be in danger." "Danger you say? I do hope it’s not mortal danger. Out of all the dangers there are in the world, that’s by far and away my least favorite." "I’m afraid it may very well be." "Fiddlesticks," Bong sighed with disappointment. "Why can’t I go through just one wrestling tournament without being in mortal danger? Oh well, I’ll just have to do my best, I suppose." "There’s an important meeting in a few days with all of Maybe’s investors," Pendable continued. "Rumor has it that he’s going to disclose certain details about this project… and I’m going to be there. I’ll find out what I can and hopefully get some answers before Stuart becomes suspicious of me." "Hm, right then," said Bong. "I certainly hope nothing horrible happens to you in the meantime." "HEY!" boomed Gloucester, who had finally left the ring and walked over to Bong and Pendable. "I’ve had enough waitin’ around! We need to get back to training, PDQ!" "Mr. Pendable, w-we really n-need to get going," Kent stuttered as he approached them as well. "Wait a minute, all of you!" exclaimed Lang over all of them. "Mr. Bong needs to get better acquainted with his new valet before we can continue with anything! They must work together, so that Kenny Mercury might fall to THE FORCES OF STUPID~!" "ME LONELY SO ME JOIN GROUP TOO," said Sweetie, walking over right behind Lang. "Now see here!" Pendable started, walking over to the others. "I’m in the middle of a very important conversation with this young man!" Bong just stood watching, his back to the mirrored wall. The other five continued to argue in front of him over what was going to happen next. Then the lights went out. Everyone fell silent. … "AAAAAAA-CHOO!" Bong sneezed. BANG! SHATTER! A second later, the lights came back on. Jimmy Bong, doubled over after sneezing, righted himself up. He looked down at the ground, and his eyes went wide with shock. "Horrors!" he exclaimed. "Xavier ‘X’ Pendable is… DEAD!" A moment of silence passed. "What? No I’m not! I’m standing right here!" Pendable burst out indignantly. Bong turned to his left. Pendable was indeed standing right there, looking quite healthy and not in the least bit dead. "Well that was unexpected," Bong mused. "What with your name being a painfully obvious foreshadowing device and all." "Happens all the time," Pendable shrugged. "What I would be more concerned about though is the hole in the wall right behind your head." Bong spun around. One of the mirrors on the wall had been completely shattered, and there was a smoldering bullet-shaped hole. Whoever fired the gun had just narrowly missed him by inches. "Blimey," he whispered to himself. "Someone just tried to kill me. Wow, what are the odds of me sneezing at exactly the same moment a bullet gets shot at my head? Plus it was rather silly of whoever it was to turn off the lights and then shoot at me, but still." Bong turned back. Five people stood in silent shock before him, lined up from left to right like suspects: Xavier "X" Pendable, Erik Lang, Samantha "Sweetie" Shortcakes, Dr. Dennis Kent, and Sgt. Gordon Gloucester. They all looked at each other, not certain what to say. One thing, however, was certain: Someone had just tried to murder Jimmy Bong. BUT WHO!?
