Global Title Contenders Match

27 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There is Always a Reason (He sat there in the black leather recliner like it was a fashion accessory rather than an item of furniture. It was worn down and faded in parts, kind of like he was, but overall it was solid and reliable. Again, kind of like him. He was asleep, albeit in a very light sleep, leaning back against the leather upholstery which usually provided a comfortable haven for him in these troubled times. Immediately recognisable was the unkempt mop of long red hair, splayed out over the headrest like paint thrown on a canvas. His face was dominated by a tatty ginger beard containing some clues as to what he may have recently eaten. His muscular torso was covered in a black T-Shirt which exclaimed "Laying the CRAXXT Down!" as if everyone should know what it meant. He wore a pair of ripped blue denim shorts that had not likely seen a washing machine for weeks. Last of all, but certainly not least, his bare feet dangled over the footrest of the recliner. His left foot was as it should be, naked, dangling. His right foot however, was another story all together. Around the ankle was a round silver object, with two red lights which flashed every five seconds or so, interchangeably. All of a sudden his body twitched, an unconscious reaction to something occuring in his dreamlike state. A flutter of the eyelids, and he was awake. He lurched forward, returning the footrest to a vertical place. Across the room from him stood two men, replete in black suits and black ties, dark sunglasses, almost clones of one another. The man on the right spoke first. "Good morning Mal." The red haired gent stood bolt upright and went to walk towards the two men, but after the first step he stopped, and looked at his right foot. "What the hell is this?" "Somers, it's for your security..." replied the man on the left. "...and the public at large." added the other. "You bastards!" Mal lurched at both men but then thought the better of it. He stood on the spot, one hand on his hip, and began stroking on his mottled beard. Resigned, he sat back in the chair, and motioned for the two men to sit. In sync, both men shood their heads indicating the negative. "Agent Rooney, when is this going to end?" asked Mal. "When you confess to the murder of your parents" he replied. The other suited man held his hand up, cutting Rooney off. His name badge shimmered as the light caught it, and read "Agent Barr." He spoke. "Mal, we have new information that I believe is going to end these investigations very soon..." Somers gulped as the words came out. "...one way or the other. Until then, you wil be under house arrest, and as you know, the only time you can leave your home is for work." "Yeah that ballet crap you do with big sweaty men in tights" added Agent Rooney. Somers rolled his eyes. He had been through this process many times over the past several months. But the ankle movement detector was a sign, to him at least, that things were not moving in the direction he had hoped. Ever since his nemesis in the World Wrestling Alliance, Tracer Bullet had accused him of the murder of his parents, Somers had been under house arrest, while investigations had taken place. He was only free to leave the house when he was working, so the invitation to compete in the PTC Global Title Challengers match was very exciting to him, in more ways than one. A smile appeared on his face. "You know, it's less than a week now til my big match?" "Unless we find the evidence you have been looking for" replied Agent Rooney, "then you'll be locked up for good." The smile evaporated. These Agents were sure able to get under the skin of Mal Somers, perhaps more so than anyone before. But then again, a lot was at stake. His career, his livelihood, his freedom, all hinged on the outcomes of the investigation. At first, Mal had assumed it was a ploy by Tracer Bullet, private eye, to get under Mal's skin just enough for Tracer to defeat Mal and wrest the World Wrestling Alliance World Title from his grasp. Perhaps it was, but Mal withstood the onslaught and defeated Bullet twice in as many weeks. But the stigma, the longer terms effects remained. Indeed there were talks amongst the PTC brass that an accused felon should not take art in such a prestigious tournament. Common sense, however, prevailed, and the old adage of "innocent until proven guilty" was adhered to. "Who really thinks you are going to win this match anyways?" sneered Agent Rooney. "I do!" replied Agent Barr, who, being a wrestling fan, had a bit of a soft spot for Somers. "Well, at least he may come second?" "I think I can do OK" said Mal, probably the least convinced of the three. His whole life, Mal always had a reason for fighting. When he inherited his parents gold mining business, he fought to keep it going. When the companies board staged a mutiny and fired Somers, making him a scapegoat, he came back and beat a lot of them up. He then fought to get into the World Wrestling Alliance, fighting the whole time to work his way up. He, alongside his then Extreme Corporation stablemate Dave Harley, fought the evil Cell in an epic feud. He fought young Eddie Van Dorn because Van Dorn had stolen Mal's beloved Rookie. He fought Odium because of the same thing. He recently faught Tracer Bullet because of the accusations that were laid against him. As Rookie once told Mal, Mal has always had a reason for fighting. This PTC matchup was a different story. What was the reason? Why did Mal need to fight? "Why the hell do you talk to a bloody defunct Rookie Title belt?" spat Agent Rooney. "Why do you hate me so much?" replied Mal. There was no answer. Rooney was as silent as Rookie, the retired Rookie Title belt that Mal believes he has a friendship with. One morning, Mal woke up and decided to stick black woolen hair on the belt, as well as coloured macaroni facial features. Ever since then, Mal is rarely seen without the beloved belt draped over his shoulder, talking away to it. It is behaviour that has garnered Somers a reputation as being one of the oddest characters the WWA has seen, but also one of their most popular. "It must be a bit disconcerting when your only friend is an inanimate object?" asked Agent Barr. "What, kind of like your partner here." replied Somers, staring daggers at Rooney. But truth be told, it was of some concern to Somers. Recently, two of his closest friends had abandonded X-Corp for the side of evil. Leaving him virtually alone with only Rookie to console him. It was a double edged sword, being voted the 2004 WWA Face of the Year, one of the most popular people, but in reality, one of the most lonely. "Look guys, I'm amped about this upcoming match. Sure, it gets me out of the house, but it also gets me doing what I love best. Getting into the squared circle with the best this game has to offer." Somers lazed back in his chair, arms behind his head. "You've got Nick Worrell from Core. He's the cW Ringmaster and that has to count for a lot. Like me, he had a PTC Top 10 win late last year. He's their longest serving roster member, I guess kinda like Shaman from WWA, he's a guy who is always going to be at the top of the division." Somers rubbed his mottled beard some more, deep in thought. "Then you've got Emilio Estevez in Prime. Now, I never knew that the star of classics like Young Guns and Men At Work was also a wrestler, but there you go." Somers seemed distracted, he turned and picked up Rookie from a side table, holding him close to his ear. He nodded, contemplating. "Emilio Rage? Uh, OK. Now why would he change his name from Estevez? He... Somers listened to the belt again. "They aren't the same person? Now you tell me! Fine, from what I hear he is a fine exponent of wrestling and a real contender. I'll have to be wary of him." "Especially since you called him Emilio Estevez" added Agent Barr. Agent Rooney uncharacteristically chuckled, and Mal frowned. There was one man left, a man that he was a little more familar with, having seen his recent appearance on PTC Television. "So, that leaves Justin Evers from Wrestling Mid West. He's their current Great lakes Champion you know?" Agents Barr and Rooney nodded, Rooney feigning interest and Barr somewhat less so. "I saw him on television and he sure looks like he is in pretty good shape. Only thing is, and this kind of proves my point.... because we have no reason to fight, like him stealing Rookie, or sleeping with my girlfriend, or even spilling coffee on me, it is hard to get out there and say how much he wants to beat me to a pulp. You know? Its not like I am going to beat him for the honor of being the Great Lakes Champion." Somers shuffled in his seat a little, trying to think of the right words to say. "So that's why I didn't even garner a mention in his viginette...there is no reason why he should." "Maybe he's petrified of you, and he dare not speak your name for fear of crapping his pants when you face him!" said Rooney, sarcastically. "Yeah good one!" Somers said, shaking his head in annoyance. "He...wait...hold on...". Somers conferred with his beloved Rookie. "I think you may be onto something there Rooney. It's been staring me in the face this whole time. There is a reason to fight, there always is. It just depends on the way you look at it. If I step into the ring with Evers, Estev...I mean Rage, and Worrell, without some reason to fight, then I'm as dead as Rooney's marriage." Rooney seethed, Barr laughed, on the inside. "But think about it chaps...who else from the WWA has moved on to PTC greatness? Who else has held the prestigious Global Title in their grasp?" Barr, Rooney and Rookie were all silent on the issue. "Exactly. Imagine if I came second, or even first in this match. I win a Global Title shot against either Seymour Almasy or the Latch Key Kid! I'd be one step closer to going where no WWA'er had gone before. Most importantly, if I get into that situation, and actually win the Title, then I wil have accomplished what that ass wipe Dave Harley tried and failed at. Becoming the Global Title Champion!" Somers looked pleased, the Agents less so. Barr spoke first. "You're getting a little ahead of yourself there Mal". "True, but you have to aim high. I am going to walk into that ring with the goal in mind. Take on the best the PTC has to offer, and put the World Wrestling Alliance on the map! And hopefully, one day, rub Dave Harley's ugly face in it! Let's go celebrate my great idea!" With that, Somers, with renewed vigour, jumped to his feet, draped Rookie over his shoulder and went to the front door. Twisting the handle, he shouted out aloud. "Let's party like its nineteen ninety nine!" The door opened, and he steped out of his apartment. Suddenly, the pockets of Agents Barr and Rooney began beeping. They sprang to their feet, drawing their weapons. "You wont be going anywhere if you don't get back inside!" Sheepishly, Somers stuck his head back in the doorway. "Heh, sorry guys, no hard feelings hey?" "As you said Mal, there is always a reason to fight. I can think of a million reasons right now" said Agent Rooney. Resigned, Mal came back in and shut the door, sitting back on his seat with a thud. The PTC Global Title Contenders match couldn't come soon enough, for a multitude of reasons.
