Sacrificial Benefits

Date: 2004-08-12 _______________ ::[Introduction]:: _______________ It has come down to basic survival. A fight to be the one dominant force that remains, there are no friends, there are no allies, only blood and a relationship bound by blood. These beings, men, women, gods, immortals, demons, monsters, they came to present the gift of war. Each nation came under their banner with the intention to destroy the opposition. Each individual that had been drawn to battle believed they were going to be the warrior, the one who stands strong and leads their brothers and sisters to victory. But it would take an entire team effort, sacrifice, dedication, will and the desire to suceed. There were no conscripts amoung the ranks of these four man armies. Each of them knew full well the consequences of loss, but the sweet taste of victory was enough to lure the finest fighters from each land. This war would begin to write history for a new future, one that will see a shift of power arise from the forgotten lands. The once weak, will arise from a firey blaze and step out in full force like the great pheonix; their presence will commit awe amoungst the spectators, their power will invoke fear into the souls of their enemies. On this eve, the warriors will stand on a field of battle and fight to define their legacy for the next generation. The war was not about territorial gain, it was not about persecution, it was a matter of pride. These beings have chosen to march, and march they shall, to victory or slaughter. The fate of entire nation rests firmly on the shoulder's of these four men: Fnord, a proud member of the KnightMare Brigade. He began his rise to stardom when he had been inducted into the well known guild. Before long Fnord had found himself main eventing against the likes of Jerms and Patricia Anderson. Though Dark Angel liked to hold claim that he had famed Fnord in the Four Winds by hand chosing him to join the Brigade, Darek knew that Fnord himself had the talent to make it to the top eventually. Their friendship and alleagence was soon ended when Dark Angel, when either for the sincere reason of saving his grand daughter or for his lustful desire to be with Patt, left the Brigade to join the Shadows Ascension. Jerms, a wretched little puke. The man was enough to make people sick, his blatantly cocky, egotistical, asshole approach to everything made you want to reach over and slap the hell out of him. Yet, there was something behind that mouth, and that was his ability to back up the words he spewed. Somehow, The Technical Spectical had become a household name, and now he would be a fighter for the Four Winds. In a match in which submissions and pinfalls can score the only win, Jerms would prove to be a great asset to a team of already unstable foundation. Mike Biddle, a man that at one time fought at the side of Jerms, Sean Summers, and Todd Dream in the Featured Attraction, he needed little or no introduction. At one time known by the now famous name, Mike Marvelous, Biddle was forced into retirement but has come back under a contractual clause. Soon Biddle will be bringing an end to a treacherous fued with Jerms to determine the number one contender to the North Wind Title, that alone deemed Biddle worthy of fighting for the Winds. Then of course there was Darek DeAngleo, the Angel with one thought on his mind: Redemption. He wanted nothing more than to storm onto the field of battle and destroy those who had sinned against him. The members of the UWF and 4ww KnightMare Brigade, specifically Blaise McClancy, Sean McClancy, Corporal Punishment and Fnord. His anger had to be subdued, fighting the temptation to lash out, Darek knew their time would come but for now he must learn to co exist with his new found allies. The choas had only begun there, Jerms and Dark Angel had been long time enemies. Could Darek bury the hatred between a dead demon and the Technical Spectical? It was true that Darek had chosen to once again follow the light of God, and under this divine presence he wanted to forgive Jerms and to move on. But Darek also knew that Jerms was manipulative, smart, and would stop at nothing to get what he wanted. Could he know trust this snake, the vile lies and untold misfortunes that Jerms had spun in the past were too many and caused far too many problems. This was another man that Darek wished he could reach over and strangle the life out of, yet now he found himself relying on not one, but two of his worst nemesis. Fate had been cruel to many, Darek knew this was the hand that had been dealt to him and he had to learn to work with it... either bluff or take the pot. ___________________ ::[Darek's Thoughts]:: ___________________ Unbelieveable. That's exactly what this whole situation has built itself up to be. From the fall of the tyrant, my former demon... Dark Angel to the outrageous choices of a maniacal madman. Ian Childress had to be of his fucking rocker to even contemplate such a team. The very thought of working with Jerms and Fnord was enough to make my stomach turn. I have begun to preach the good word and live my life through God's example, for this was the path I had once chosen long before I became consumed by hatred and darkness. Through this practise, I would like to believe that I could find it within my heart or now cleansed soul to forgive Fnord and Jerms for our tainted history and move on. Infact, I know I am capable of giving them such a distinctive honor, yet I always dwell on the thought that these two men would give anything to be the best, and that meant destroying friendships to do so. Jerms had done so to Biddle and Fnord to myself, now it was more of a matter of trust than forgiveness, could I trust any of these men to truly have my back? Of all the worthy applicants that would give their blood and last breathe for the Four Winds, Childress had to choose Biddle, Fnord and Jerms. The lies, the deceit, the pain and brutally agonizing suffering that we have endlessly inflicted upon each other over the past couple months, it was enough to justify our hatred for each other... not form us into a team. Childress has gone and crossed the line setting me into a war with men that I'm not even sure can define the word honor. Though, something tells me, if I hold up my end of the bargain, they're corrupt greed will help secure a victory for our great homeland. Perhaps, Ian wasn't insane at all, but more brilliant than anything, teaming mortal enemies with each other? I am often reminded of a saying, "Keep your friends close and your enemies even closer." It goes without saying, that there is not one person on this team that I can trust... but each of them has my respect. Together, we are now bound to each other, we must work together, and even before then, we must learn to comprehend that by the word 'we', it refers to the entire group and not one superstar individual. Individually we can achieve great things within our homeland, together we can take the entire PrimeTime... There was something rotten about this whole situation that had been approaching now. The war for Survival would have pitted men and women from NOW, Core , RPW and our very own Four Winds. Yet, for reasons unknown or unforseen, RPW has decided to withdraw. They've tucked their tales between their legs and went running for the hills. To me, now it was only a matter of time before the others soon followed. Of all the men, demons, gods and other oddities that would march out into battle carrying their flag, it was the RPW that I had looked forward to facing the most. A land inwhich warriors are bred by a daily basis, it would have been a distinct honor to be a part of a legendary team to over run the Rush Pro organization. Of course, that can never happen now. Instead, my thoughts now find themselves dwelling up the members of my other opposition. Quite honestly, whenever my thoughts moved onto NOW, they just brushed past and kept going. I couldn't really forsee a probable threat from such an antidisestablished organization. Their union shall fall and when it does, I'll be there to judge each of their souls for the sins they have commited. There isn't a day that passes when I don't question the purpose of this war, if not to gain territory, then what for? To prove that you are better than the rest? However, despite my pessimistic view of fighting for no reason, this will soon be reason enough... The Four Winds has been a laughing stock of the PrimeTime since even before I wandered into that great land. Knowing this, I couldn't be more motivated to show the world just exactly how much of a threat the violent Winds can be. We will walk into the war with our head held high, our pride and dignity on our sleave and when it commences, we will fight to defend what each of us has worked hard to create... our home. For me this about the legacy that I wish to leave, searching for answers from God, I beleive the best example I can make of this situation is to turn those who oppose us, into Martyrs for the living world to see and understand. There will be none who can master the Four Winds, there will be none who can master Darek DeAngleo and there will only be the righteous judging of the damned. By God's will, I shall purify these beings for their sins. That word had taken on a whole new meaning for me, Purify or Cleanse, to me they were common daily words and now they stand to mean so much more. My redemption, my trails will bring me into the choir of Angels, as a watcher I will bring about a great change into this forsaken planet. There will be those who have sinned and done so for their entire lives, but now, before my will and the judgement of the Almighty, they shall fall to their knees genuflexing before God in worship and praise. I will purify the PrimeTime, starting with this war, starting with Core and NOW. This will be more than a battle, more than a war, it will become my mission. A mission inwhich I must put my faith into the hands of God and Ian Childress, God's choice for me is to walk this path and Ian Childress was the man who laid it. Am I walking the Green Mile? I doubt that, Childress is a business man, he wouldn't send his men to death... Unlike Patricia Anderson... That name made a lump swell in my throat, one so big I had to force it back down. The woman stole the heart of a misguided, possessed Darek DeAngelo, she played Dark Angel for the fool he was and in the end she brought about his final downfall. I will no longer attatch myself to the names Dark Angel or Patti Anderson. This woman led Dark Angel away from his allies and brothers in the KnightMare Brigade but threatning to adopt my grand daughter, it was hard but I had to fight to get through to Dark Angel that Raven was important to our existence and when he realised it, he went with Patricia. She brought him to his knew home, the Shadows Ascension, where Dark Angel would be reunited with Raven but soon, Patti had entangled the demonic me into a love triangle. She led Bullet and Dark Angel into a slaughter, then went to the back to get some good love from her man Sean. In the meantime, Dark Angel is being caddle prodded, kicked, spit on, power bombed. Next to Patricia lies my hatred for those who commited that act. I do owe them one thing, because of their assault, I have seen the light. My path has changed and now I walk towards the white light of salvation. Yet, I still feel the compelling urge to seek vengeance for that tortured soul I once was, Dark Angel. Indeed he was evil, perhaps like a maniacal dictator, but he too had his limits and this was an outrage. Bringing together the bands of the KnightMare Brigade from the UWF and the Four Winds to destroy one being... none of them could do it on their own. Not Blaise, not Sean, not Patti, not Fnord, not Ukyo, not Corporal Punishment... none of them, but together, they accomplished what no other could. They killed the man... The myth... But they will never kill the LEGEND that is Dark Angel. His foundation for a dynasty and a legacy for future generations has been cemented and now I shall make him a martyr for his efforts. His kingdom will be glorified by the grace and love of God's light, within the Four Winds there will be peace and tranquility. Soon a new era will dawn for a dying age, but first we must survive the Armegeddon. _____________ ::[The Story]:: _____________ The sun had begun to peek through the white fluff of cloud that now obscured it from shining down to the planet. Rays of light made their way to the earth, adding warmth and light to the day and just like the one before it, today was a blessing, a miracle, a new day to relive the wrongs that one had lived. A new day was a new beginning, the sun now penetraded the clouds and shone down, it's warm touch spread across the land, peering into windows, nooks and crannies. The light passed through the glass and straight onto the face of a man. Moving his hands upward to his face, they clench into fists as he slowly rubs them across his eyes before squinting out his window. It was day already but it felt as though he had just fell asleep, shaking his head, he leaned to his right and moved his feet to hang off the bed, bending his knees his bare feet touch the cool hardwood floor. Several thousand bumps form on his arms and move up to his spine, then back down. Shaking it off, he glances at his clock and graons. Shifting his body to the left, he reaches over and turns the alarm off. Again, he couldn't even sleep to the alarm of his wake up time. It had been a week of sleeplessness and now at six fifty, the sun had already beckoned him to awaken. The new day had come and now he must prepare for what it would have in store. Rising from his sitting position on the bed, he moves over to his open second stroy window and stares out at the morning that he had been given today. His eyes make their way past the Pengrowth Saddledome and over to the enormous Tower that was an obvious sign of the city. Leaning a little, he continues to take in the morning beauty before closing the curtains and turning back to face center in his room. Reaching to his nightstand he grabs the remote and pushes power. "...ay we're expecting a high 23 and tonight a low of 6, with a chance of light showers throughout the city. That's it for todays five day weather forcast. In sports news today, Brian Sutter GM and head coach of the Calgary Flames noted that his goaltender Mika Kiprusoff would be going into arbitration for his contra-" The screen flickers and the channel changes. Darek watches on as a commercial finishes and the television program returns. Blinking at the attempt of humour, he stares at the animated feature, he understands the attempt at the humour but feels displeased with their shameless effort to promote such filth. "Lois, you vile wretched whore of a mother..." Again the channel changes, this time the television shows the green numbers changing in the right hand corner or the set. The channel comes to a complete stop on 21. Darek moves over to his dresser opening the top left drawer, removing socks and boxers, he makes his way over to the closet, glancing again at the television. "The PrimeTime Central community announced their card for the Upcoming IPPV between it's member federations earlier this week. Amoungst the matches are a Extreme Title match to determine the now vacant title. This match will feature six extreme combatants, squaring off in an ellemination chamber. Amoung them is the current number one contender, Kyo Kushinaga of the 4ww." "That's right Ron, it's also boasting a huge main event in the form of many former champions. These former champions will be facing off against the current Global champion Saij, in what promises to be a battle of epic proportions. Jesse Jamester and Seymour Almasy will have the right to fight for the Global title and I can bet that neither of these men have wanted that title more." "They both appear to be in the best shape of their careers, but Saij has seemingly broke the curse of the Global title, can he retain against these two mammoth stars?" Darek sets the remote on the dresser and smirks at the thought of Seymour Almasy fighting for the Global Title. Moving his eyes away from the TV, he finds himself looking at his reflection with a sly smile on his face. It was only a matter of time before he would be stepping into the same battle arena as men like Saij, Jamester and Almasy, and Darek felt he could be the one to define the words, Global Champion. First, he had to prove himself worthy. Working under such circumstances, he knew that this match coming up between the Four Winds, NOW and Core was going to be his opportunity to prove just he is what has always claimed to be, a God amoungst men. Staring into his own cold blue eyes, the smile soon fades out and he continues to take in the information of the PTC IPPV on the ESPN wrestling channel. His ears seemed to stand on end when the mention of match six had come. He knew this was going to be his fated destiny, a path that he chose to walk. Though he had walked through the valley of death to get here, the redemption and sweet salvation would be worth it in the end. Darek turned his face back to the television, and then walked over towards the far corner of his room stopping for a brief moment to catch the word on his match. Ron nodded at his co-anchor and coninued on, "The sixth match of the evening is scheduled as a no disqualifactions, no countout, submissions and pinfall victory only. After Rush Pro Wrestling and the PrimeTime Central parted ways, that left a huge void with the talent in this match." "Not necessarily, there is now an opportunity for a new league to emerge as a big factor in the PrimeTime rankings. You can bet that each respective organization, NOW, Core and the 4ww is dealing with their teams accordingly. Each one chosen with the intent to survive not only this grueling match, but perhaps the next match, the Survival Finale." "True, Match six will be the final qualifying match for the final Survival meeting, meaning whomever takes it will be get little or no rest before they are out competing again." "Remember, the final Match is Mount Saint Hell's and should promise the best team to finally emerge from the ranks of the PrimeTime-" The screen seemed to shut down and blackness now covered the set. Darek walked past the television and pushed the power button as he made his way towards the bathroom in the far corner of his main suite bedroom. Stopping at a shelving unit, he reaches in and soon music is heard playing in the room. Flipping through a small CD binder, he ejects the disc tray and inserts a new disc. Closing the binder he walks over to the washroom and opens the door. Tossing the socks and boxers onto the counter by the shower, Darek opened a closet beside the washroom and pulled out a white Armani suit, a black silk shirt and a white silk tie. Placing his wardrobe on the counter, he reached into the shower and turned on the water. Flipping the switch at the base of the handles, the shower came to life spurting water into the marble tub. He already was shirtless, it was just a matter of removing the jogging pants he wore to bed last night and stepping into the hot water. Even that task proved to be difficult this morning. Ding Dong The doorbell echoed throughout the empty house and into Darek's room. He lifted his head and walked back out of the washroom, across the room over to the door. Opening it he stepped out of his room and began down the long hallway before the windinging wooden staircase that would take him to the main floor. Marching down the corridor, he approached the staircase and jogged down them with relative ease stopping at the main floor. Turning to his left he began down another hallway that would lead him to the main door and porch. Along the walls were various photos and pictures of Dark Angel in the GulfSouth, Global Championship and Four Winds Wrestling. Passing them he stops at the large double, oak doors and unhinges the main lock pushing the door open. Looking to the outside Darek saw his grand daughter standing at the front of the porch, a large grin on her face. Having to take another glance after a long blink, Darek assures himself this vision is true. Stepping to the outside he look out into the driveway where a vehicle stays, engine running and a figure behind the faintly tinted windows. The midnight blue Mazda Miata purred for a moment and then the engine fell silent. The door cracked open and the foot of Patti Anderson stepped onto the driveway. Crouching under the low roof of her vehicle, she soon reveals herself to Darek. They hadn't stood face to face since she counted him to a victory over Blaise McClancy. She was beautiful, a creature of God that defined grace and class, but underneath that lied a demon. Darek vowed to never be taken in by her games and now he stood before her in a testament to his word. Raising his eyes to the woman who destroyed and freed him, but more importantly it was the act of betrayal that hurt him the most. Anger flared within his cold blue gaze and Patricia raised her sleek sunglasses above her eyes, she smiled and nodded at Darek, as though nothing had ever happened. Inside her, she knew what had happened and her version of the truth was much different than the truth that Darek spoke of. Yet Darek would hear nothing of it, he believed Patti was behind the knightMare Brigade. "Why doncha put some clothes on mate, ya look like shit?" she threw at him. It was the first words they had spoken and that was what she had to say. Darek was expecting her first words to be 'I'm sorry', boy was he wrong. His face even told Patti that he had been expecting something else. Finally looking away from her, Darek reaches down and lifts Raven into his arms. "Well, albeit, t'is a lil' early to be out and be out. Did I interrupt you?" Darek turned away from Patti and opened the large oak door to his home, setting Raven inside he sent her off to play and told her he would be with her in a moment. Raven disappeared down the hallway and Darek swang back towards Patti. His eyes met hers and she slowly began to approach the porch. "Are you kidding me?" he shot blankly, "the last time we looked at each other, I was just about to be on the receiving end of a beatdown from your fiancee-" Patti interupted him, "I'm not 'ere to discus that. I'm 'ere to let you see Raven, she misses you. But remember Darek, she's in our foster care, if you try anything you'll regret it." The words cut like a knife but Darek accepted that as the truth, "I know." Looking away from Patti he continues on, "why are you doing this?" "I'm a member of the Four Winds Family and you represent them in a breat battle." Darek looked back into Patricia's eyes confused, "I thought seeing her would help ease your mind and help you focus on what matters the most right now. A victory for the Four Winds." Nearly speachless, it was hard to believe this woman was possessed by evil, but this was her cunning way. "Thank you." Darek managed to utter before stepping back towards his door. "I guess this means you'll be back to get her then?" "When the time comes. For now you do what you have to do." She pulled her shades back over her eyes and stepped towards her small Japanese sports car. Slipping inside, it wasn't long before the engine was reving and the vehicle had come back to life. Slowly it backed out of the driveway and turned right along the street curb coming to a sudden stop. The tinted black window rolled down smoothly and Patti stuck her head out the window. "Doncha ya dare think this means you and I are by any means finished discussing certain issues, Darek." Darek nods, nearly obdiently, but then snaps out of a trance like state and walks into his home. Patti throws her vehicle into gear and races off down the street. _____________ ::[The Moral]:: _____________ In order to achieve the common good for a great nation, each individual must sacrifice and learn to coexist. This war is not about selfish pride, it is about the pride of an entire land. There will be everyone from your home to back you up and yet these are the same people you call brother, sister, ally, enemy, sinner. Each of them places their faith in us, the warriors to achieve our objective, become the most dominat force within the PrimeTime Central community. But to be the best, the individual must become a cohesive unit, a well oiled machine that thinks with one mind and fights with one desire. When this happens, the Four Winds will march into the field of battle, fight and then be victorious. To achieve this, there will be pain, sacrifice and we the warriors will have to give our blood... our souls... "To be the best, you have to beat the best" and that is exactly what the Four Winds intends on doing... this is OUR nightmare.
