Sunday Service

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Life is a divine mystery. Why we are here? What is our purpose? What happens when it’s all over? Who decides these things? For some, the answer is made up of scientific answers. Evolution of single cell organisms, The Big Bang Theory, some even with the belief we came from somewhere else in the universe. Everything can be explained. Yet for most, it doesn’t take lab testing, DNA samples of monkeys, or unpronounceable theories. These people have faith. Belief in Jesus, God, Allah, Buddha...whichever one of the many you choose to worship. You choose to believe that this power is behind your meaning. You answer all those questions with a silent prayer, or a robust rejoice. God is your answer for everything It’s a beautiful Sunday morning. The sun shines brightly through the wispy white clouds that are scattered in the blue sky. The sun seems to shine even brighter on a small church just outside an Air Force base. The sounds from within it are joyous. From around the corner, a white Infinity Coupe slowly moves down the street, and parks outside the church. Inside, the Pastor, dressed in robes of red and gold, leads the congregation in song. The choir sings, the organ blares. Everyone’s faces are as bright as the daylight that cascades through the stained glass windows. As the song comes to an end, the Pastor stands at the pulpit, and speaks to his people... Pastor : Brothers and sisters....Jesus is with you.....The Holy Spirit is in you.....and The Lord loves you!! They respond randomly with “Amen” and “Hallelujahs“ Pastor : As we come together today in the holy love of Jesus, must learn to understand why he gave himself for us. Why he was nailed to the tree. Why he sacrificed his mortal self, so that we could be with him in his Father’s kingdom. More shouts of praise, as the organ player chimes in with a few joyful notes of her own. Pastor : We must come to understand ourselves as sinners my brothers and sisters, sinners who NEED to ask the LORD for forgiveness. To pray to JESUS and ask him to light our dark paths with his goodness! Can I get an Amen? They all break into song again. All the while they never notice the strange pair that have entered the church. They stand at the back, watching. The song ends, and the Pastor takes the floor again. Pastor : So it’s today my children, I ask....who among you wish to testify? Who among you wish to confess among your own, your sins, and ask Jesus for his forgiveness, and guidance through the dark forest of sin!? A voice responds. I do! The whole of the church turns to see this voice who has spoken. The first individual they see, is a large man, who towers head and shoulders above all of them. He wears a indistinct light blue blazer, which clings tightly to his broad shoulders, and hangs open over his muscular chest. A plain white shirt, and blue pants complete his Sunday best. His long blond hair hangs loosely over his shoulders. But his hair and his outfit are not his most distinguishable characteristic...it is his face. Handsome, yet plain, his nose, jaw, and cheeks show his age to be somewhere in his late 20’s early 30’s, but his pale blue eyes reveal a much younger, even childlike innocence. With his dominating physical appearance, it is easy to understand why the real source of the voice was not him, but the diminutive man who stands to his right. Standing not even to his hip, a small man, wearing an old gray linen suit, holding a fedora of the same color in his hand, who smiles as the faces cease to gawk at the giant to his left. Instead, they begin to gawk at the older gentleman, who’s face tells a very different story then that of the other. His eyes, also blue, are more gray like his hair, or what is left of it. The lines on his face tell of a life that is without innocence. The Pastor walks to the duo. Pastor : What is you name my brother? George : George sir... George DeClement The man speaks with a slight accent...French it seems...yet has a very good command of the English language. Pastor : And who is your companion? The Pastor goes to ask the large man, but he’s stopped by the small man. George : He’s my son...Martin Alexandre. He doesn’t speak...the Lord has chosen for him to praise him other ways. Pastor : Well, Brother George...what sins do you wish to confess to. George : The sin of pride. Pastor : Pride you say? One of the seven deadly sins...a very serious sin. George : Yes sir...My son and I are both guilty of it. Pastor : Tell you story George...tell us all. He hands the microphone to the George, who hands his hat to Martin, who steps back, as his father walks forward, and faces the people. George : I am a sinner. I’ve wrongfully, and knowingly proclaimed my son to be “ The Magnificent”. I’ve done so night after night, to crowd of people after crowd of people. I’ve done so to make others believe he is as I call him. To do so is wrong...and I ask the Lord for forgiveness...for me and my boy! Shouts of forgiveness comes from the congregation. George : I also ask for forgiveness for another. A man who does not claim to be Magnificent....but calls himself Odium. Odium a word meaning hatred.....disgust for all and everything. He calls himself this knowingly, and willingly. I wish forgiveness from the Lord for him as well. More shouts of forgiveness. George : I ask this in the name of Jesus, Mother Mary, and The Lord above. The pastor comes forward and lays a hand on his shoulder. Pastor : The Lord forgives you my brother for your sins... George falls to his knees, and begins to weep. The congrgation breaks into song once, more, and George gleefully sings along. The service ends, and the people begin to file out. As they do, they stop and speak with the Pastor, who says goodbye to each and every one who walks through his archway. The last to leave is George, who is without his son Martin. The Pastor smiles as he and the small man shake hands. Pastor : Brother George....might I inquire where you and your boy are from? George : I was born here Father, but grew up in France, that’s where My wife and I adopted Martin. Pastor : Ah...adoption. George : Yes...I get that often. Most find it strange such a large child is the natural offspring of such a small man. Pastor : Only small on the outside brother... George : I like to try Pastor. Pastor : Have you moved here to Alexandria? George pauses at the mention of the city name. George : Alas....no. We are a part of a traveling show here Father....at the Air Base. A small wrestling federation that is performing here tonight. Pastor : Ah.....your boy is a wrestler. George : Yes....Lex le Magnifique. Pastor : Alex the Magnificent...the pride you spoke of. George : Yes...A sin that keeps us fed. I ask for forgiveness every day from God for it, even though it is he who sent us down this path. Pastor : God told you to sin? George : No.....God spoke to me. He told me to spread his message to the masses. At that moment, this small company was holding tryouts. Martin had the ability, and we needed a means to live, as well as a means to spread the message of Jesus. I try my hardest to do both. Pastor : But is you continue to commit this sin of pride, how can you expect forgiveness. George : Because I know God... George nod to the Pastor and walks off. Martin waits for him in the car. They drive off..... Much later.....after the show at the Air Base, George sits at a desk in their small hotel room. Martin sleeps in one of the small beds. His legs hang off, but he sleeps quietly. He fought three men...and defeated them all. Who they were means nothing. George writes in his journal...... Martin once again, impress the impressionable. I delivered Your message to the masses again, and I believe they listened. Martin’s success has led to a larger stage...The GTT. Wrestlers from all around will come, all to sin the sin of pride. They all wish to be called the best. I have entered Martin, not to seek his glory or my own, but or Your glory. It doesn’t matter who we must face, whether it’s a man who knows nothing of us like Odium, or any of the others. They will hear Your words, they will know Your love, and if necessary, will feel the wrath that You have allowed us to deliver to the non-believers. It doesn’t matter what a lowly Pastor of a church thinks, what matters is You. I pray for our success Lord, a success that will be for the greater glory of Your kingdom.... Amen. George make a sign of the cross, and shuts off the light....
