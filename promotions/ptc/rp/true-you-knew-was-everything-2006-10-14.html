True you knew was everything…

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 Reality. It is, in its broadest terms, reality is everything that is real…everything that exists. On a stricter sense of the philosophical concept of reality, there are levels: phenomenological, truth, axiom, and fact. Phenomenological reality usually is based on the experiences of one person, and can often be so unique it can never be agreed upon, or be experienced by anyone again. It is a very spiritual level of reality. It’s a reality based on what one believes it to be…to see only what they want to see. Truth is formed when more then one individual share similar experiences, and come to a consensus on its interpretation. Truths can vary from group to group, such as religions, and the belief or non belief in God. Axiom is self evident realities, which a givens, and are the basis for further concepts. Fact is a reality based on elemental principal. "The sun rises in the east" is fact, no matter where you are from. Fact can however, be proven otherwise. It was fact that the earth was flat, and that the earth was the center of the universe. We now know it was incorrect, but imagine what we will know tomorrow… DDT’s apartment is a fucking wreck. He smiles to himself as he thinks about how it mirrors his career as of late and his life on the whole. He’s still getting his chances. He and The Fallen Fae just recently gave their shot at reviving the Tag Team division, but alas, their reign as champions will go down as, uneventful, and unmemorable. Now, he gets another chance at gold in the biggest cluster fuck of a match ever conceived. To top it all off, was XUW commissioners DJ and Sev asking him to participate in GTT6. DDT was familiar with PTC, having been a part of the Extreme title division some time ago. He bored quickly of it. The ideals of "Extreme" were individuals who were extreme outside the ring, but suddenly were the next Chris Benoit or Kurt Angle in it. They wanted to be wrestlers, and many of them didn’t have the stomach for blood. They talked the talk, but never walked the walk. The DDT at that time had no time for that. The DDT at that time… Going back… Even as he says the words in his head, he is forced, maybe for the first time in a very long time, how different a man he is today, then when he first came to XUW. His early days, back in what many call the "Forbidden Letters" days as they fought for their name, let alone their identity, DDT was just like the fledgling fed…know one knew what he was capable of. He was a throw in almost every show. Then came the fad in wrestling known as "Hardcore". Everyone was doing it, so naturally, XUW did it as well. This is where DDT made his mark. The feuds between Raven X, Cereal Killer, Rob the Throb, and himself spawned some of the first legendary battles in XUW history. The title passed between most of them, DDT getting the best of the time, holding it for almost 2 months. This was a great time for XUW, and for DDT. Just when it was at its peak however, XUW came crumbling down. The people at the top had lost control. They had stripped the World Champion of his title, and the other champions were left with no contenders because of severe backstage politics. Ownership was ready to close shop. Just as it was about to die, it was sold, and with the sale came new life, and DDT’s life would change forever. Tournaments were held to determine the number one contenders for the titles, and a separate 8 man tournament was held for the vacant World Title. Every one of the singles champions were in the mix, as well as the last two champions of record. This left 4 open spots for the new management to fill. As the brackets were announced, DDT found himself left off the Hardcore title hunt. This puzzled him. Then the Submission title bracket came out…and again, no DDT. It was around this time DDT came to meet a man who would become a large part of his life. He gave him a sense of belonging, something he hadn’t had since his very, very short childhood… His childhood… Going back further… Damien David Teri was born October the 22nd, 1976. He had an older brother and a younger sister, but his memories of them a faint. He remembers bits of his mother and father, remembering more of his father each time DDT himself looks in the mirror. However, so much of that time is locked away in his mind. Officially, it was ruled arson, and Damien D. Teri was accused of burning down his home and killing his family at the tender age of 11 years old. When the fire department found him, he was sitting in the middle of their family room in a catatonic state. He was sent to an institution for "troubled youths", in hope that they could get him out of his state and perhaps get answers to the terrible tragedy. Alas…all that happened was the birth of a monster. Damien became DDT because of that place. The "treatment" he received was, at best, cruel. Shock therapy for hours at a time, solitary confinement, and abuse from other patients caused an already fragile psyche to fragment. Now, there was Damien Teri, a scared little boy who didn’t know what was going on, and DDT, the hate and fear born into a persona to protect him. He took over, and the little boy was buried deep…safe from harm… Going forward… Discouraged and angry, DDT sat alone in the XUW arena. Back then, they wrestled most shows there, only the big Pay –Per-Views going elsewhere. He’s yet to be placed in the tournaments, and as XUW marched forward to Reborn, he was an outsider looking in. As he sat in the ring, a voice, which was quiet, yet carried in the empty facility spoke out… Voice : They don’t understand you. DDT looked, and as if he had been there all along, stood Sirius Smythe, lawyer and patriarch to the Army of Darkness. DDT: What do you want? Sirius: The same as you DDT…the same as you. I look at you, and I see talent wasted. I see a man who can be a force in this place…a man who can shape this place into what he and others like him want it to be. DDT: If you’re talking about your "Army"……there isn’t many in it who I’d consider like me. Sirius: No, not all of them…just one. DDT knew he spoke of The Ripper. Until recent, he had been a quiet force, much like DDT. He began to rise before the management fallout, but his impact loomed. He was almost guaranteed a spot in the World Title Tournament… Sirius: Join us DDT. DDT: Why? Sirius: Because we can help you become something more then a broken man. We can replace the family you lost with a power you can never know alone. DDT: No…why me? Sirius: I’ve been watching you a long time…and believe me, when the master plan comes full circle, the earth shall tremble at the mention of your name. You…and the Army of Darkness… And so…with a nod, DDT agreed. A day later, he was the last entrant in the World Title Tournament. He did change the XUW. While he didn’t win the World Title that night, he did fight in the main event, and under that spotlight, he joined the AoD. That night, he found strength, power, and a sense of being… Back to the now… Now, much of that is gone. The Ripper is dead, Raven and Pete gone, and Sidhe Nihm has slipped back into being Fae. The Army that was his family was gone, and he was feeling lost without it. The only peace he found was that it was he himself who was feeling it. The time in the institution had created an alter ego…a kind of darkness within him. He also had felt at times, the pull of fear…the child who still missed his family, and had so many questions. As of late, neither had control of him, or for that matter, had been a "dominant" force to his conscious mind. The fact he was aware of the multiple personas was in itself, amazing. No, he was feeling quite normal. Regardless of his professional troubles, he was perhaps, for the first time ever, at peace. A knock on the door interrupts his daydreaming thoughts. He looks out the window and sees a UPS truck. Must bee the paperwork for the GTT. PTC needed updated signatures for whatever reason. He opens the door. A short woman, mid twenties stands in the doorway in her brown uniform. UPS woman: Damien Teri? DDT: Yes. She looks up at him from her clipboard. She goes pale for a second as she stares at DDT. She looks as if she’s seen a ghost. UPS woman: Um….sign here… DDT signs. The woman continues to study his face. DDT looks at here DDT : Something wrong? UPS woman: You look familiar is all. DDT: Not a pro wrestling fan are you? UPS woman: Isn’t that fake? DDT smiles. DDT: As fake as the scars on my face. UPS woman: Sorry… DDT: Don’t be. You didn’t hit me with…well, I’m not sure what scar was from what hit anymore… UPS woman: Sounds like a dangerous job. DDT: It’s a living. UPS woman: How does one get started in something like wrestling? DDT pauses. He thinks. When, and why, did he become a wrestler? The answer never comes. A long pause is interrupted by the UPS woman, who is becoming increasingly uncomfortable. UPS woman: I’m sure these papers are some kind of insurances for your job. DDT : Something like that. DDT reads the GTT contracts… DDT: Mentok the Mindtaker? UPS woman : Isn’t he a cartoon character? DDT: Wrestling has it’s share as well. This guy isn’t even a wrestler…he has some guys dressed as mummies or something like that fight for him… UPS woman: I’m confused… DDT: Yeah, UWF wrestlers tend to do that to people. The fear of the woman has changed to quizzical confusion DDT: UWF. It’s the federation Mentok wrestles in…for now anyway. They’re shutting down. UPS woman: So you’ll be out of work soon? DDT: No, the XUW has no such problems. UPS woman: Sounds interesting… DDT: Yeah… DDT signs the clipboard and hands it back to the woman. DDT: Thanks. UPS woman: Have a good day. She turns to walk away, then stops and turns back. UPS woman: What’s your name? DDT: In the ring they call me DDT. UPS woman: No…what’s your real name? DDT pauses. DDT: Damien…Damien Teri. UPS woman: Nice to meet you Damien…my name is Elizabeth. They share a smile, and DDT closes the door. Nice girl…pretty too. Yet, DDT didn’t find her attractive. As he pondered the young woman, he thinks again, when and how did he become a wrestlers…and why? Outside, Elizabeth sits down in her truck. She hold her hands at ten and two on the wheel, and stares blankly out into the street. A tear runs down her cheek, as she mumbles to herself… Elizabeth: Freddy…
