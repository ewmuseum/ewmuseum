The doubt of the non belivers.

7 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-07 ~ There is no security on this earth, there is only opportunity. General Douglas MacArthur (1880 - 1964) ~ ~ Opportunity is missed by most people because it is dressed in overalls and looks like work. Thomas A. Edison (1847 - 1931) ~ ~ Some people have asked me if I was surprised that I have made it this far already in the tournament…..and how does it feel to be the long shot to win round three let alone the whole thing. Well to let your curiosity be at ease I will tell you how it feels…..I love it! I crave all of the best that PTC has to offer! You may see trouble, where as I see opportunity! Opportunity to show each and every one in the world that I John “The Prophet” Adams is a force to be reckoned with! ~ ~ Enter the night that will blind your eyes…. ~ Date: 10-7-04 Time: 2:52 PM Location: Hollywood California- Location of the old EPW Headquarters. ::::: The atmosphere out today is nice and sunny with a slight wind blowing in at a nice 5 mph from the west. The place is Hollywood, where dreams are made, and where dreams are broken. This is the place where at one time a company stood and shined like no others. This is the place where one man a little over a year ago, with the determination to live out his dream was given his opportunity. This is the place where EPW Headquarters used to stand, “The Evolution of Professional Wrestling!” now just a parking lot where it once stood. Under the leadership of people like Richard Rush and John Adams it flourished like no other even though it was only for a short time. But with many things in life, all good things must come to an end. And for this man, John “The Prophet” Adams that was a saying he knew all to well. We can see John Adams walking around the parking lot as if he was paying his last respects to a loved one who has passed away. And why not, he loved this business with all of his heart and soul. Whatever the business asked of him he did it. He bled for it, some say he would have died for it. And today he is here to pay his final respects to the company who gave him his start, EPW. ::::: ~ The things that once were should be left to ashes….. ~ John Adams thinking to himself: 9 long years I have spent in this business. And 8 of them were in the Indies. And where has it gotten me as of late? Fame, fortune, and the satisfaction that I’m doing my all to make this business the best it can be? It didn’t serve me well in EPW….After a few months of running the company it went bankrupt! But that’s in the past now and a new battle field is laid before me…..LOW, the pinnacle of my career. The one place where I will leave my mark on this world! But there is also GTT4, the mountain of excellence. A place where the best of PTC get together to battle it out! ::::: John Adams continues to walk around the parking lot to reminisce of old times long ago. A few of the local homeless are seen near by pushing what are now there homes. All the blood, sweat and tears he put in over the course of his career will be put to the test in GTT4. First there was -Tessa Windsor-, a beautiful woman who had a wonderful career in RPW and the same with PTC. She was one of the few to ever beat Seymour Almasy(UWF). Then there was Dani Fuher, who a young wrestler was trying to make a name for him in this business. All who have fallen and felt his wrath. Now its round 3 in the Pyrenees Bracket and the opponent is none other and a legend in his own right Blaize "Hazard" McClancy of 4WW. ::::: ~ A battle field of fighting gladiators battling one another…….. ~ John Adams who continues to think to himself: Blaize "Hazard" McClancy, one of 4WW’s elite, in there long line of talent. The cruiserweight wonder himself, who tends to be quite the Prophet himself…………Damn I hate this seen, I need a drink! ::::: John Adams heads over to his Harley Davidson Bike and heads to the local bar. ::::: “LANCERS, HOME OF THE STARS.” ::::: Kind of a well known place in Hollywood where the gimmick of the bar is that the stars of Hollywood have drinks there. Gimmicks, one thing wrestlers in this business pride themselves on. They take pride in knowing that there gimmick that they portray on TV is the most entertaining, not for John Adams. John Adams doesn’t do this for the fans, he doesn’t do this for the ratings, and he definitely doesn’t do this for the money. This is the very life blood that keeps him going, this is the very lifeblood that gets him up in the morning to go in that very ring and defeat his opponent. This is his life; this is the very essence of John Adams and what he hungers for. And from the looks of the body’s he left so far in this competition he has been starving for a very long time. The general mood inside the bar is hectic as Game 2 of the playoffs are on and the score is tied between LA and St. Louis. The thick stench of smoke is filled in the air followed by the sweet aroma of Alcohol. Everybody in the bar is watching the game to see if LA can tie it up in the series. Nobody is playing at the pool tables or shooting darts. John Adams sits at the bar. ::::: ~ The doubt of the ignorant shall be lifted…… ~ John Adams: Hey barkeep! Give me a beer! ::::: No answer from the barkeep as it is seen that he is glued to the TV. ::::: John Adams: Hey Barkeep, give me a damn beer or I’ll fucking rearrange your damn face! ::::: Finally the bartender pulls himself away from the TV and serves John Adams a beer. The patrons of the bar start to talk to each other as a commercial comes on. ::::: Patron #1: Damn, St. Louis is playing a more solid game then LA! Patron #2: Oh yeah, the Dodgers are just taking there time! You’ll see there going to make a major comeback here! Patron #3: You want to make a bet on it? Patron #2: Hell yeah, I’ll put $20 dollars on it! Patron #4: Hey you guy’s who do you think is going to win GTT4 Round 3? Patron #3: Unstoppable Eugene! Patron #2: My man Seymour! Patron #1: I feel John Adams! ::::: The room was silent as they all stare at the man in confusion. ::::: Patron #4 Laughing: I agree with you that the man is good! But come on I think it was all just pure luck to get this far! Patron #3: Hell yeah, and his luck is about to run out! He’s facing Blaize "Hazard" McClancy of 4WW! There is no way that he could ever stop the man! ::::: John Adams leaves after he finishes his beer. Did he feel that they were right? Did he feel that the man from the Four Winds was going to beat him? Hell no, why should he care what a couple of drunk red necks thought. He is going into GTT4 facing Blaize "Hazard" McClancy of 4WW and coming out of there going into round 4. As John Adams gets on his bike he thinks to himself about the past, present, and future of his career. After a moment of pondering he comes up with the only solution………::::: ~ As “The Prophecy” will set your mind free! ~ John Adams thinking to himself: The winds are about to change!! ~ We must believe in luck. For how else can we explain the success of those we don't like? Jean Cocteau (1889 - 1963) ~ ~ When love and skill work together, expect a masterpiece. John Ruskin (1819 - 1900) ~ ~ The skill of writing is to create a context in which other people can think. Edwin Schlossberg ~ ~ That is the passion I feel for this business, that is my love! Some may say that I am a sick individual, and also some may say that my priorities are never straight! Well I say fuck them! This is my life long passion, and at GTT4 I John “The Prophet” Adams will show you Blaize, I will show you the true meaning of pain, suffering, my passion as I deliver unto you “The Prophecy”! ~ ~FIN~
