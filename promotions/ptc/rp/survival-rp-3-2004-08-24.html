Survival RP#3

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 Reason for third RP: [detail withheld] RP: [[detail withheld]] [On this fairly quiet residential street at the top of a hill, maybe 10 miles west from Manhattan there is a house narrow enough to be a row house, but not connected to the adjoining houses. It's a tall, narrow house, oddly enough matching the build of the people who live here. There is a Volvo sedan in the driveway; a Volvo station wagon is usually kept in front of the house, but is out somewhere. Both of these refrigerator-like vehicles are the same ugly metallic color, which I am convinced must be listed in the dealer's literature as "Noncommittal Ugly Silvery-Kinda-Beige-like." The bottom half of the house is white; the top half is red. There's a white fence and a little grass in front, and some brick steps leading into the house. Four teenage guys sit on the steps, laughing their asses off at comments that don't make sense to you because these people apparently talk only in inside jokes. The camera turns and looks down the street.] [Nicky Jam and the mysterious Man that is always around him walk toward the camera. They're talking about where to shoot their next promo.] Nicky Jam: Why are we in New Jersey? Man: I'm sure a lot of people ask themselves that all the time. Nicky Jam: How many? Man: Depends. Nicky Jam: On what? Man: On how many people there are in New Jersey. Nicky Jam: Why do we need to sit on steps to do this? Man: People liked the promo where we were sitting on steps. So I want to try sitting on steps. So keep your eyes peeled for nice sitting steps. Nicky Jam: How about those? [Yep, the ones that have four people sitting on them. We see them again. One, who lives here, has a square head and a buzz cut that accentuates its squareness. Another has mutton chops and apparently has forgotten how to use a razor. Speaking of which, the third has weary, sunken eyes and a neck beard. That's right, his face is clean, but he has a neck beard. And last but not least is a starving-artist-looking guy with insane-looking eyes and a goatee.] [Nicky Jam approaches them.] Nicky Jam: Can we use these steps to shoot a wrestling promo? Starving artist: Go right ahead. OW! [Buzz cut just slapped him] Buzz cut: Well I guess so, just make sure you clear out before my mom gets home, she wouldn't be too happy about it. Mutton chops: Yeah, believe me, you don't want to piss his mom off, it doesn't take much, that's for damn sure. She's been on my back for 3 weeks because she wants some prom photos of his sister and me. Jesus Christ what a pain in the ass? [Buzz cut is offended by this, he knows it's true but is still offended.] Nicky Jam: Uh, guys, I need to use the stairs. Yes or no? Buzz cut: Yeah but make it quick. Nicky Jam: I will. [Pause. Nicky Jam then looks at Mutton Chops suspiciously] Nicky Jam: You went out with your friend's sister? That's pathetic. Mutton Chops: I see no problem with it. Nicky Jam: Sorry. I was just jealous, I guess, I mean I don't have any friends who have family members that would go out with me. [Pause] Nicky Jam: Or any friends, for that matter. Man: Son, are we gonna accomplish something here today or not? Nicky Jam: Sorry. Yeah. Let's talk about this weeks upcoming PTC Survival pay per view! Man: Why certainly. Nicky Jam: I am in a survival elimination tag match as I team with my brother Chivo, Limp and Boris Yeichstag to take on the best wrestlers from NEON and EPW. Man: That you are. Nicky Jam: For the chance to get to the final match against the best feds from PTC, no less. Man: That you are. Nicky Jam: Hmm, leading my team to victory would be nice. Man: It would. Nicky Jam: See now this is when I would talk about how fast I have risen in the ranks and files of the UPWA, but well, I won't. I signed with PTC before really checking out the talent level and past history of the company. I don't know why I did that actually. The commute is a pain in the ass. I don't even like the winters around here. Why I picked it is beyond me, actually. It's like some outside force talked me into it. [The mysterious Man rubs his hands sinisterly] Nicky Jam: But I'm there for keeps and damn it I want to get some recognition, I'm both the UPWA Tag Team and US Champion. Man: Yes, and it's very important that you keep winning titles as soon as possible, my friend. I have big things planned. Nicky Jam: Like what? Man: You'll see someday. Just keep talking. Nicky Jam: Well ok. I own 2 titles, but deep down I want a bigger title. Because see when you win a title, it's easier to make friends. And maybe one of those friends will have a sister who will go out with me. I can only hope. Man: Son, if you win enough, I can almost guarantee that people will respect you, or at least pretend to, or at the very least, not run away from you for no particular reason. Nicky Jam: Sounds exciting and so UPWA like. Man: It is. Well that is, if you don't have any friends. Which you don't. So yes, it is. Nicky Jam: Many men stand between me and something that will make people respect me. That might sound like not much of a goal, but think about what it's been like to be me all these years. Anything that will make people at least pretend not to hate me, that's priceless to me. Other guys might be trying to build careers, and so am I. But damn it I'm trying to lead Team UPWA to victory so I can use it to buy people's affection! Mutton Chops: That's a great way to live your life, schmuck. Starving artist: Yeah, you f*cking overachieving bastard. Sunken eyes: You're like Kiczek for Christ sakes. [Another reference no one understands, nor should they. These people are really lousy promo presences, they can't even curb the inside jokes for a minute.] Nicky Jam: What are you talking about? Man: Ignore them, son. You know a man is no more or less than the sum of his concrete accolades. You keep those belts and you'll be more of a man than you are now. [Mutton chops begins to object but is interrupted as a refrigerator on wheels, oh wait, I mean the previously-absent Volvo wagon that I mentioned earlier, rolls up. Buzz cut's mom and sister get out. Mom is relatively short and eminently middle-aged looking. Sister is looking uncomfortable, which is what she does best, unbeknownst to you of course. Mutton chops goes into the house for a second and emerges with an envelope, which he hands to Buzz Cut's sister. We don't hear what they say to each other. Buzz cut's mom quickly takes the envelope and inspects the photos she had been pestering Mutton Chops for. She grunts approval before glancing at the steps where Nicky Jam is sitting.] Mom: Ryan, who are those two men sitting on my steps? Buzz Cut: Er,they're shooting a wrestling promo. I told them they could use our steps. Mom: OUR steps? No. MY steps. And I say get the hell off them. Nicky Jam: Yeah, I'm really scared of a short middle-aged woman who drives a refrigerator, er, Volvo. Mom: Don't make me use force, pal. Nicky Jam: Look lady, I asked if I could sit here, your son said yes. That's it. I'll be gone in a few minutes. [Buzz cut's mom puts on her most self-righteous face and charges Nicky Jam, who leaps to his feet. They tie up and Nicky Jam suplexes her onto the steps. She's not getting up too fast. Then Nicky Jam climbs the wrought iron railing, which bends under his weight, and does a huge moonsault. He then picks her up, unconscious, and carries her back to the car, setting her back in the driver's seat. Then he returns to the steps to applause from everyone else.] Nicky Jam: Er, hopefully she won't remember what happened when she wakes up. Man: Don't worry about it. Now if you can beat up on pro wrestlers who are in better shape than you like you beat up on that much-smaller-than-you-are middle aged woman, you'll be just fine. Nicky Jam: Eh. Shouldn't be any harder than that was. Mutton chops: I'd think that sounds funny, but he's right, when she gets that self-righteous look in her eyes she could probably lift a city bus if there was someone under it that she could lay guilt on. [Buzz cut's sister nods agreement. Buzz cut rolls his eyes. He knows it's true, he just can't bring himself to admit either of them is right.] Nicky Jam: Well it's all in a day's work for Nicky "The Pride of Puerto Rico" Jam. Man: The Pride of Puerto Rico? Nicky Jam: Legend.. maybe? Man: We'll talk about it. [No they won't.] Nicky Jam: Bye, everyone. [Nicky Jam and the Man walk away. Mutton chops begins making fun of the composition of the photos from the envelope as Buzz cut's sister looks back and forth from him to photos and looks either slightly amused or rather disapproving, it's really hard to tell.] Mutton chops: Look at this cheap looking gold background. Jesus it's like we're on the set of the Gong Show. I also like the way they accentuated the fact that I missed a spot shaving, so it looks like I have a bug crawling out of my mouth. [He looks over at the car and sees Buzz Cut's mom, who inexplicably hates him, just starting to wake up and smiles. I think we might be seeing the first actual fan of Nicky Jam.] [Fade out]
