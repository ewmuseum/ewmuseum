Family reunion…and plans unveiled…

1 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-10-01 “Ahh…Chicago” he thought to himself as he stepped out onto the sidewalk, outside the airport. The flight had been a good one. The flight attendants had catered well for him, and he told himself he would have to fly like that again. Flying private jet was all well and good, but there was something slightly more thrilling about being able to converse with those uniform clad angels, who hurriedly catered for one’s every need. Yes, he decided…will have to do it again. The rain pattered softly on the concrete grey of the Chicago sidewalk. He mused that each drop told a story of its descent from the parent cloud. What things they had seen…what mysteries they had uncovered. They almost played a melody as they splattered onto the top of a metallic covered dustbin just across from him. No man could have produced such natural harmony of sound. It was as if each drop communicated with its predecessor, in an eternal waltz of melody. But of course, after all, they were just raindrops…and he…he was just a man. Out of the corner of his eye, Jonny Kae noticed the black limo, waiting patiently at the second exit to the airport. He let out a gentle sigh, and decided he’d better make himself known to the nameless driver. Grabbing his bags, he slowly stumbled forwards towards the black stallion of metal…and, dropping his bags before the door, tapped on the driver’s window. How insolent that he be reading a newspaper! Hadn’t they told him whom he was picking up? Hadn’t they told him how important his guest would be? A swell of rage ran up Jonny Kae’s spine. The window rolled down slowly, and a pair of eyes peered out from beyond the newspaper. Kae’s eyes caught the mans’, who simply sat there, staring… Man: “Yes?” Jonny Kae was shocked. Had they not even bothered to explain to the man that the most handsome and beautiful person to walk out of the airport would be Jonny Kae? Hadn’t they even given him a picture? Such a lack of effort… Jonny Kae: “Well? Are you going to open that damned door for me, or do I have to do it myself?” For a moment, the driver sat, digesting the words of Jonny Kae. Then, it seemed like a look of realization came over his face. Then one of contemplation, and finally one of shock. Had he just questioned the most ruthless corporate boss in the United States? Oh God…Quickly, he fumbled with the paper, folding it coarsely, and dumping it on the seat next to him. He hurriedly opened the door, rushing to get out of the car. In all the rush, his hat fell to the floor. He leaned over and picked it up, before standing almost at attention before his boss. Man: “Sir…yes…Sir. Sorry. I…I didn’t realise it was you…can I..Should I…” Jonny Kae frowned. Who was this mumbling, fumbling little man they’d sent? Jonny Kae: “What’s your name?” The man thought for a moment. Man: “D…Derrick Sir. Yeah. Derrick Moses Manhattan.” Jonny Kae took in the man’s figure. He was in his mid forties, of African descent. He was slightly overweight, but there was wisdom in his eyes. This obviously was not where he’d seen himself as a bright eyed young boy, looking up at the stars. How sad…yet how very American. Jonny Kae: “Ok Derrick. Don’t worry about it. Just put my bags in the trunk, and we’ll get going.” And with that, Jonny Kae was back in Chicago, Illinois. ********************** How glorious to be back, he thought. How wonderful. The decadence with which the building was decorated. He had done that. He remembered having done that. It was not so long ago that Michael Kae had been London’s guest, and he Chicago’s. But now…now they were back together…a family again. A rush of excitement filled him. This was the time he would get to reunite the once infamous Kae Brothers. But for now…just take in the grandeur…home. As he walked down the ground floor hallway, flanked either side by ageing portraits. The Kae family’s legacy to their heirs, sons and daughters. He wondered how long it would be before his picture finally joined theirs. He mused he would scowl in his portrait…treating visitors to his gentle distain. This caused a soft giggle to escape his lips. How funny would it be, this hallway of proper faces, looking pompously yet honourably at all onlookers…and then Jonny Kae, scowling at the viewer. Yes. He would do that. And play some awfully deprived rap song at his funeral. How that would anger the family. How hilarious. Yes… His nostrils were suddenly filled with her scent. It was a perfume of Camomile extracts and the slight tint of Chrysanthemum. Was there Jasmine in there too? He wasn’t sure. Could never tell with her. How long it had been though. He remembered dancing with her in the old gardens, back in Basingstoke Hall. The sound of the violins playing over the deep, yet gracious, violin cello. Like the sound of running water. Some Vivaldi String Quartet movement, it escaped him now. But how they had danced. Her dress flowing so gracefully, like a rare feather caught in a very slight breeze, swaying this way and that. His hands on her side and in hers…as they moved so effortlessly over the recently polished floor. He was so happy then…so happy. That was before all of this. Before the entry into the ICWF. Before he had been so corrupted by power, and the thirst for more. She had taught him that. But then, he had taught himself how to become too greedy. Her leaving was a blow to his heart…but he would never have told her. Never, that is…until now. This was the smell of an angel…the smell of Mother. His pace quickened as he made his way down the hallway. The faces lost their lustre as he continued. Why bother to look at them, when round the corner is the face he had been waiting to see for years. The scent grew stronger, and then words…he could hear her speaking! Something about re-paving the path in the garden…about replanting a wayward rose bush. But what she said wasn’t important…it was her voice, and that’s what mattered. Finally, he turned the corner where the scent was strongest. He turned to his left…and there…at the end of the hallway…in that room. She stood there, dressed in a purple cut off top, with a white suit jacket, of silk…and matching silk trousers. Her hair…her blond hair…now slightly soiled by the appearance of grey strands was tied back into a lovely bow atop her head. Still, she bore no wrinkles. Not any he could see from here…from this distance. Her face was a perfect portrait of what it had been a decade ago. She didn’t age. Only her hair…but that didn’t matter. It was his mother…Samantha. The very name made him swirl with a passion for seeing his own flesh and blood. Only now did he realise how very wrong he’d been. How much he’d missed her. So much lost time…run to her… He was tempted to run, but stayed himself…anxious not to appear over-excited. He slowly stepped towards the room. Suddenly, as her head turned in distain at something, their eyes caught one another’s. Her frown disappeared almost instantly, and was replaced by one of shock. Then a smile crossed her lips. But it wasn’t a smile of true happiness…it was more a deliberate smile. A calculated smile. Oh mother, you always were one to mask your true emotions. She beckoned with her hand, for him to come forward. He stayed at the steady pace, head held high, just as she’d always taught him. He stepped into the room, and her scent was almost overpowering…but he refused to let her control him like that. She turned back, whipping some order to one of the young attendants, before turning back to her son…Jonny Kae. She wrapped her hand around his arm, and looked up into his eyes. Samantha Kae: “How was your flight darling?” His heart sank. There were no fond greetings. No great hug. No tears. He had wanted there to be tears…by all rights she should cry! Damn it, he wanted her to cry. Jonny Kae: “Not bad thanks Mother. How are you?” Samantha’s eyes searched him, looking at his every detail. Her fingers leaned forward, adjusting his tie. He knew he had tied it perfectly…but if she wanted to adjust it, who was he to try to stop her? Samantha Kae: “Will be much better once we sort this bloody place out.” Was this all she had to say to him? How was your flight? This was such a disappointment. But then, maybe he had expected too much of her. He had wounded her deeply…he knew that. So, maybe she just couldn’t open to him. Jonny Kae nodded at his Mother, and took off his jacket. He placed it delicately on a chair beside a mahogany desk. He knew that desk…so many a night had he sat there, watching the world move past in its youthful decadence. But what caught his eye now wasn’t the view, nor the desk itself…but the picture. That had been the last time they had been a true family. He, Michael and his Mother, Samantha Kae. That was almost four years ago now. Had it really been that long? Of course it had. But now…now they were back together, weren’t they? Samantha Kae: “Jonathan. What on earth have you done to your hair?” He forgot he had cut it. She would obviously still notice a thing like that. Jonny Kae: “Yes. It needed trimming Mother. Besides, where I’m going with my career, I think it will be better to be the image of refinement.” A smirk crossed his lips. He knew that would hurt her, and he enjoyed that. It was almost getting back at her for her heartless greeting. Had she not missed these last four years? Did they mean nothing? He honestly didn’t care that much anymore. Oh well…more important things to think about now. Samantha Kae glared at her son. She felt the spite in his words, but she forgave him. She didn’t know what to say to the grown man she had left four years hence. So what, he had hurt her…she was his Mother. All pains are unimportant when pressed to a relationship like that…and besides, if Michael could forgive him, so could she. But, in some ways, she was proud of the man her son was. She had taught him well. He would never be cheated…never be manipulated. He was the manipulator, and that was exactly what she had wanted him to be. Strong, and full of the same ruthless intelligence that had made the family countless fortunes. Samantha Kae: “Michael tells me you’re some big shot now. Evolution Champion huh? Sorry to say, it’s nothing compared to your former glory…don’t kid yourself Jonathan.” Her words would hurt him…she was sure of it. But, it was just a game wasn’t it? A game of words they had played since he was old enough to spite her. She said them almost without urgency, picking up the very picture she had seen her son’s eyes caress. Yes…those were good times. And now? Now it was time to rebuild the empire… Jonny Kae: “Well, Mother, difference is…this was done all by Jonny Kae. You had nothing to do with this…and that makes it all the more sweeter. Your lessons have found their way to your son old woman…and now he seeks to use them diligently in the pursuit of glory.” He said this sarcastically, with a glimmer of triumph in his eyes. Samantha looked up, and she found it almost impossible to withhold a chuckle. Oh, this was too good. She let a smile find her lips, but quickly suppressed it. Samantha Kae: “Oh, and I suppose you have this unfounded belief that you are anything like as great as your father was?” Oh, she knew that would hit the spot. When he had died, William Spencer Kae had told his son not to be anything like him. He had been ashamed of his life…his ruthlessness. But then, he had always been a fool. She was the real power behind the family. William had always known it, and so had Jonny. But none the less, she knew he had some regard for his father, and that Jonny had been let down by his father’s apologetic remarks pertaining to his life…a life which Jonny had sought to emulate. Jonny Kae: “Oh Mother. How long you have been in the shadows. If you were to have seen me, you would know I have by far exceeded that old man’s expectations. His tragedy is my vengeance on the world…oh, and my wallet isn’t quite empty. Have you watched the news recently woman? I believe the Kae Enterprises Sports Network is running rather smoothly. And, of course, we have sizable shares in almost every facet of corporate life. My father, to me, was merely a speck in an ocean where I am a tsunami.” She again couldn’t suppress a smile. He was perfect! She had taught him so well! Oh, what a son she had…what a son indeed. Oh, to hell with it… Michael Kae watched as he saw his Mother throw her arms around his brother, Jonny. He would be lying if he didn’t say a hint of jealousy coarsen through his veins. Jonny Kae had always been his mother’s favourite. He had seen that well before the two of them betrayed him in ICWF. But it was still clear…even though after four years, he had wounded her deeply, she still held Jonny Kae in such high regard. Michael had tried so hard to be worthy of her adulations…but it had always been Jonny who got the praise…who got the applause. He was always the one who did everything first. Every title in professional wrestling bar one, Jonny had won before he had. That one was the cruiserweight title of VWF, and that had only been because Jonny was too heavy to compete in the division. Michael suppressed a scowl, but still looked on with envious eyes. Jonny Kae: “Oh Mother…how I’ve missed you.” Samantha Kae: “Shush Jonathan. Give me this time in silence. I wish to behold you for the man you truly are. So proud…we were all so proud of you…still are.” Pain shot through Michael’s spine. She had never told him that. She had never told him she was proud of him. But that didn’t matter now. They were together, and he supposed he should be thankful for that. But to hell with happiness…he despised his brother, and loved him dearly at the same time. Such a tangled web we weave. Who had written that? Didn’t matter…it was so true… Samantha Kae: “Oh, I’m so happy. My two boys back together. I watched Global Domination from the country house in Paris. I’ve always watched you Jonny. But it was so fulfilling to see that stunt you pulled to retain the Evolution title. Everything seemed so perfect…until that Deszaras fellow showed up. And now, you mean to face him?” She wiped a tear from her eye…she felt vulnerable when she cried…but then, she was allowed to after four years, wasn’t she? Jonny Kae nodded at her remark. Jonny Kae: “I’m afraid that me and that resident scytzophrenic have a few things to sort out Mother. One being his consistent interference in my damn business…the other being the strap he wears around his waist. This Evolution title isn’t enough anymore…I must have more. You know me Mother. I’ve always been a world champion. Seeing someone else on that throne is simply intolerable. I will not stand for it…” She smiled. Oh, those words were music to her painfully deprived ears. She loved her son. Jonny Kae was the promise of her future…she had put all her hopes in him, and he hadn’t disappointed. The corporate empire was flourishing…far better than her husband had ever achieved (though she dare not admit that), but where her true pride came…was in Wrestling. It was where Jonny could show his true character, and be renowned and applauded. In the corporate world, all applause was muffled by speculation…but here…here it was all so simple. You win, you get respect…and he had not lost in HSW yet. And she would refuse to let him lose now… Michael looked on still with oppressive eyes. He knew what his Mother thought. She held Jonny in such high regard. What about him? He had helped with the British Corporate affairs. He had taken on the responsibility of the entire British Market single handedly. He would have taken the world stage, but his brother got that as the older of the two. How he regretted popping out of Samantha’s womb just four years after…it was such a foul trick of nature. To give such predominance to one created first…and to leave the scraps from the proverbial table of life to the second. Jonny took the time to look over at his brother. Something troubled him. Jonny decided to break off his Mother’s embrace, and stepped up to his brother. He held his hand out in a gesture of handshake. Michael hesitated, looking in Jonny’s eyes, but eventually took the hand. They shook, and Jonny pulled Michael into his embrace. He closed his eyes…the Kae Brothers…back together again. So marvellous. He turned his head, and whispered into his brother’s ear. Jonny Kae: “We fooled them all brother…we fooled them all…” ******************* It was approaching midnight, and they had talked for hours. Reminiscing about everything…childhood…their father, Samantha’s husband…old successes in the Wrestling industry…the Corporate Empire. Jonny Kae’s head was filled with images of what had occurred between this great family. His mind shot back to when the three of them ruled the roost in the ICWF. They had been such powers…so great. But then, it had become boring. Hostile takeovers always are when there’s nothing more to takeover…it ceases being hostile. Jonny looked into his brother’s eyes, as he explained diligently his past 2 years of running the British side of Kae Enterprises affairs. Amazing…that he had received paperwork, acquisitions…from his brother, and not even realised it. He may have signed the very papers his brother had designed…how extraordinary. But just looking into his face, he saw his brother had evolved too. He was no longer the young, easily impressionable boy he had once been. The weight of responsibility had made a real man out of him. Jonny was proud… But then the conversation turned to the immediate future. Samantha Kae: “Deszaras…” The very name made Jonny scowl with disgust. That reprobate was top of the food chain in the HSW…but he didn’t give a damn. As far as he was concerned, Jonny Kae was the most prolific star there…and he was going to show it. Samantha Kae: “What are we going to do about Deszaras…” Jonny looked over at Michael, to see if his brother wanted to contribute first. His brother returned his gaze, and saw what Jonny was thinking. He nodded, and turned to his mother. Michael Kae: “Well…quite frankly, I was a little insulted when that little confused kabuki doll attacked me at Global Domination. I mean, that was our time Mother. Our reuniting in the Wrestling world…he spoilt it. And well, I’d very much like to see him embarrassed in some form.” Jonny smiled. Jonny Kae: “Come now Michael. The man cannot be embarrassed any more than he embarrasses himself. Talking to mirrors? Exchanging ideas with the shadows? The man belongs in a psychiatric ward as much as he belongs in the wrestling ring. But, I would of course suggest strip him of everything that he feels creates this humanity…this power of thought for him.” Samantha nodded. Her two sons were so good at scheming…plotting…he really didn’t need to add anything anymore…but she decided to. Samantha Kae: “Well, at Super Series, perhaps Jonny Kae can provide the first blow to the man’s pride by defeating him in front of such an avid audience. He will be disrespected in public…and I think that will be a good place to start from.” How ruthless his mother was…this was only the beginning he thought. What plans had she in store for the Resident Evil upon the return of HSW? Samantha Kae: “He is without his little brother in arms, Kris Carmicheal now. Oh yes, I’ve watched how that pathetic Covenant has frolicked around in its perceived dominance. But now he is alone. His supposed friend KvK seems so uninterested in everything, I think we can eliminate him from any equation we draw. So…you defeat him at Super Series, and we shall go from there.” Jonny nodded. He looked over at Michael, to see if he had any input. Michael had a questioning look on his face, and Jonny decided to remain quiet, to see what his former tag team partner had to say. Michael Kae: “Well, beating this man won’t be easy Mother. And even if we do…embarrassing him might just make him rather angry. A cornered lion is more dangerous than one left out to roam the plain. What did you have in mind to weather such a storm?” Samantha smiled at her son’s inquisition. She had plans…she always had plans. They weren’t finalised…but she was prepared for every eventuality. Samantha Kae: “Well, my inquisitive son…rest assured that things will be set in motion that will change everything. Upon our return to HSW, I am sure one way or another, we will get your brother a world title shot. Somehow, we will ensure that Jonny takes the title from Deszaras. Strip him of yet another facility with which he can grace his humanity. Defeating a man who is so unaccustomed to defeat, is like reintroducing a five-year clean heroin addict to an injection of that which he seeks to escape from. It will either destroy him…or it will enamour him to re-evaluation. Either way, we will have the advantage. When he is vulnerable…we shall not relent. Everything about this man, we need to dissolve. Get him to his very core essence…and all you will have is a withering child looking for somewhere to fit in. And that, my children…is exactly where we want this man. At that point, anything you do or say will have a profound effect. We can break this man…we can break him…and enjoy doing it.” Oh, she was so malevolent. So absolutely Machiavellian. It was so perfectly ruthless, as he had always known her to be. The old Kick a man when he’s down routine…such a score to play in front of an orchestra of lies, deceit, and eventual power. So great…so lovely. Samantha Kae: “And from that base…we shall rebuild our empire. Headstrong Wrestling isn’t going to stand as it’s always stood. It’s not going to be so infelicitously narrow and straight. We’re going to take away the fabric of stone which this mountain is made…and replace it with sand…and watch it fall to pieces. And then…when we see this supposed empire of wrestling prowess at its knees…it will be up to Kae Enterprises to rebuild it in our own image. My sons, we are here to create, not destroy…but then, that’s the purpose of creative destruction.” Ah…the classic rules of hostile takeovers. His father had taught her well, Jonny mused. He wasn’t sure how Michael would play along with this…did he still possess the same moral fabric that had hindered him from greatness before? It had hurt Jonny in retrospect to betray his brother…but given the same circumstances, he would have done it again. Such a cruel lesson to learn…but a necessary one. Must defend against Naivety at all costs… Jonny Kae: “Well, of course…Kae Enterprises is the goal. But we must move one step at a time Mother. Wearing down one such as Deszaras isn’t as simple as it sounds. There’s a lot more to him than there has been to others we’ve dissolved. He’s not a Roderick Moon…or a Radical Jazz…or a Cisco Kid. He’s Deszaras. His very essence is self-destruction…but he takes it out on the world. We simply need to channel that destruction inwards. I would suggest that implosion might be key to crippling this man’s pride.” Samantha knew her son’s words to hold truth. She nodded, and wiped her lips with her palm. Samantha Kae: “Well, we’ll take it all one step at a time. But, Jonny, when you stand in that ring and face him this coming week…make sure your eyes tell him you’re plan. Make sure he knows what you seek to do, and that there is nothing he can do about it. Rage might be our ally…consume him, and we can take advantage. But, alas, it’s late boys. This old woman isn’t quite the woman she used to be…and needs sleep. We shall talk more in the morning…I bid you all a fond evening. Night Night boys.” But she was the woman she’d always been. Her mind worked so diligently to find any route to her goals. She was a portrait of intensity when it came to her plotting. And he knew that things were to pick up once again for the Kae family. Michael also stood, and left the room, bidding Jonny a farewell. He almost didn’t hear Michael’s words, but had mustered enough thought to acknowledge them, and reciprocate. He sat now, facing the window…watching as the street lamps covered the street in their manufactured blanket. The sun rose in the morning…as it has done for centuries…new days…new beginnings…new pride.
