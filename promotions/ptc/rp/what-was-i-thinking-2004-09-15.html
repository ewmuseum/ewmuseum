What was I thinking?

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 Forgive me world for I know not what I do, is it greed that made me join GTT4? Or is it the love and respect to want to represent my federation? Is it the will inside me to want to fight, to face a challenge that I have never seen before? It's not everyday something like this comes along its not everyday, you get a chance like this to go against greets from other federations to put your skill against them and see where you size up its crazy the amount of talent that has come into this tournament. Its crazy the amount of people that have joined this. Its crazy the amounts of hype its gotten and no matter the outcome I am apart of it, I will make history, I will win GTT4. It’s not only my goal but it’s my destiny. GTT4 my road to greatness. (The scene takes you to a shot of WMW superstar Fury standing on top of a hill in the middle of the night. He is sporting a White Hoodie and dark blue jeans with light blue stitching on them. He is looking out at the stars and the sky, the camera moves around and focuses on his face he looks to be at peace; suddenly he shakes his head.) A few days ago I was perfectly fine, I could wake up every morning and take my normal jog without a problem I was at peace with myself. I could get up go to work at WMW I would know what was going to happen that day. It would all be the norm I would go in figure out who I was going to face, and then I would prepare for my match. Then the other day it hit me I have something bigger to worry about I joined one of the biggest tournaments in the history of wrestling. (Fury smacks his head.) Did I really join the tournament? A tournament that consists of some odd 200 or more superstars all looking for a chance to prove themselves all looking to make a name for themselves. Hell maybe I am dreaming maybe I just thought this whole thing up. Maybe one night I was sitting on my couch and I was drinking heavily and I thought up this big tournament. Well not so much the tournament me joining the tournament. (Fury smiles and nods.) Yeah that's it this is all just a figment of my imagination a drunken dream that I actually think I can hang with some of the people in the PTC. I haven't exactly had the best track record in PTC events hell tournaments to be exact. Like I was saying it’s probably a dream, just something I thought up cause everyone else was thinking about it. (Fury reaches in his pocket and pulls out a black and mild cigar and as he does so alittle white paper falls out of his pocket he puts the black in his mouth and bends over and picks up the paper, he un folds it and looks at it. At the sight of the paper his eyes bulge out of his head. He smacks his head again and rubs his eyes.) I guess its not a drunk dream this is the memo I was sent confirming that I joined GTT4, I don't believe this what the hell was I thinking? Or was I even thinking at all. Am I out of my mind? I just don't believe that I would join something so big I mean I have other things going on. (Fury crumbles up the paper and throws it down the hill.) Damn it Fury what the hell are you thinking? Why would you join GTT4? Is it for redemption? But redemption for what. (Fury sits down on the hill and crosses his arms over his legs he puts his head down then looks up at the stars again.) This isn't me. I have never questioned the ability that I have not until now; its amazing how your nerves and something like this can get to you. This is big this could be my big chance and I don't want to fuck this up I can't second-guess myself all I can do is push forward. Focus on energy on winning this thing. (Fury finally decides to light his cigar he takes a puff and lets it out slowly.) My focus has been lacking lately with everything that is going on in the WMW, Matrix the bastard that he is took a bat to my manager he busted her open leaving her with 10 stitches and a concussion and she is still suffering from that. That’s just another thing I have to worry about if I bring Tasha to ringside with me will she be hurt? Is something going to happen to her. (Fury grabs puts his hands over his eyes he takes a deep breath and.) FUCK? Why did I do this what was I thinking, maybe there is still time to back out maybe I can just walk away and no one will even know the difference. If I do back out I could be known as a coward but I don't care I can't go through with this shit. (Suddenly Fury's phone rings he looks at it and frowns.) Hello.Oh hey Tash what’s up? You want me to meet you. Ok where at? All right I'll be there in about 15 minutes. Ok I'll see you then bye. (Fury closes his phone and shakes his head.) This is going to be the perfect time to tell her that if I am going to go through with this that she needs to stay in back I can't risk her getting hurt again not now not ever, but I wonder what she wanted to talk to me about. She didn't sound the same. (Fury finally starts his decent off the hill and the scene ends) ___Love a weakness or a strength? ______ (The scene reopens to a car turning into a driveway the car turns off its engine and the door swings open. Out steps a foot the driver stands up and the camera traces from the legs up and it turns out to be Fury, he puts his keys in his pocket and locks his door, He starts to walk up to the house but you can tell he is clearly nervous cause he is fiddling his hands, he stops at the door and raises his hand to knock on the door when he stops.) Maybe I should just turn around right now and save Tasha and myself the trouble, just avoid this conflict something is giving me a weird feeling in my gut. (Fury turns around sharply to his left knocking over a plant that is on the porch when suddenly the door springs and there stands Tasha in her night gown, Fury looks at her up and down and lets out an embarrassed grin, Tasha just smiles.) Tasha: I didn't think you would be coming. Fury: I. um was running alittle late that’s all. (Tasha nods and waves Fury to come in, he reluctantly walks in her house he looks around at the various artworks on the wall and many pictures of Tasha and Fury from when they went to school together.) I just can't get rid of this feeling in my gut, it’s not a bad feeling but it’s not a good feeling. I just need to shrug it off and just go with the flow, yeah that’s right I'll just go with the flow. Tasha: You can have a seat anywhere you like. (Fury just nods and walks over to a near by chair, he sits down and folds his hands and starts to rock back and forth.) Tasha: Dee if I didn't know any better I would say your nervous. Fury: Me nervous no why would I be nervous? I have nothing to be nervous about. I'm in total shock right now I have no idea why I am so nervous, I've never had a problem telling someone that I didn't want them at ringside with me before so why would I have a problem now? Why the hell should I care? So to hell with it the cats out the bag I just need to go for it and tell her. Fury and Tasha: I have something to talk to you about!!! (Fury and Tasha look at each other then laugh and smile, Tasha begins to blush as Fury just sits in his chair playing with his hands.) Fury: Tasha you go first mine isn't really all that important. Tasha: Oh no I'm sure you have alot on your mind with the GTT4. Fury: Well I... (Tasha stares at Fury waiting for him to spit the words out, Fury takes a deep breath and swallows slowly.) Fury: Ah Tash I don't want you to come to the ring with me at the tournament. I don't want anything to happen to you like it did with Matrix I couldn't handle it if anything... (Suddenly Tasha lunges forward and kisses Fury smack on the lips she pulls away, still holding his left check and staring into his eyes.) Tasha: Fury I love you, I've had these feelings growing inside me for sometime now and I just could find the right time to tell you. (Fury stares at Tasha in Disbelief, he blinks a few times) Tasha: Fury did you hear me? This can't be happening I didn't just hear what I think I heard did I; Tasha told me she loves me. No it can't be, this isn't happening right now. Fury: Yeah I like you to Tash you know I do we have been good friends for sometime. (Fury stands up he looks around the room trying to avoid eye contact with Tasha.) Tasha: Is that all you have to say is that you like me too. I'm still in shock what else am I supposed to say, I can't believe she just said that after I got her hurt on Burnout I can still see the stitches on her head. Fury: Tasha I just can't do this right... (Tasha stands up and looks into Fury's eyes for a moment then leans in and kisses him again, Fury tries to fight it at first but then his hands find the sole of her back and he embraces her, they kiss for awhile and Fury finally pulls away.) Fury: I...Um have something I need to do, we can talk about this more later. I have to get outta here I need to think. Something told me she had feelings for me but I didn't know it was this strong I have feelings for her too but I'm not ready to. (Fury turns to walk away but Tasha grabs his hand.) Tasha: No don't leave no tonight. (Tasha grabs Fury's hand and leads him to the bedroom and the scene ends.) ____A few hours later_______ (The scene reopens to the bedroom the camera pans around until it finds the bed and Fury and Tasha are laying there, Tasha is laying on Fury's arm sound asleep, Fury opens his eyes and looks over at Tasha, he slowly and gently moves his arm out from under her head, he looks at Tasha kisses her forward then smacks himself in the head, realizing that he is standing there in nothing but his boxers he finds his clothes and puts them on. He walks over to the near by table takes pen out of his pocket, finds a piece of paper and writes Tasha alittle note he looks at her again and then walks out the room.) What am I doing? What the hell was I thinking I told myself I would not get romantically involved with another manager and look what happened. Stupid, Stupid, Stupid. (Fury smacks himself in the head he finds his shoes puts them on and heads out the door shutting it quietly behind him, he walks to his chair unlocks it and hopes in, he puts his key in the ignition puts his hand on the wheel, then his head quickly follows as he lays his head on the wheel.) Damn it Fury you need to focus. You need to remember you have Clayton Wright in the first round of the tournament. (Fury bangs his head against the steering wheel.) Damn it the tournament but I can't get those words out of my head "Fury I love you, I've had these feelings growing inside me for sometime now and I just could find the right time to tell you." but I have to I need to focus. Clayton right is the thing I need to be focusing on. (Fury finally starts his car and begins his drive, he takes one look back at the house and a smile comes across his face.) You know I never thought in a million years that I would get up the nerve to join a tournament as big as this 200 or more wrestlers joined this thing. People from all over the PTC have come to represent its federation. (Fury stops at a stoplight) The first thing I thought when I heard about this tournament was there is no way in hell I am going to join this thing. All the hype it got all the press it’s been unbelievable; it was truly a sight to see. As it went on I seen the people joining up people that I have thought about facing. Josh, Tessa Windsor, Seymour Almasy hell even my partner Noah Hanson joined so I had to put my name out there I had to give it a try. (Fury presses the gas and goes on his way again.) My first experience in a PTC tournament wasn't exactly the best it was the crusierweight tournament. I thought I would do good hell make it out of the second round but I was beaten taken out right away, and to my disbelief I had to sit there and watch everyone else give it ago, then Crossfire came around and team WMW lost. So now I see myself a few more months down the line in the biggest tournament of all time. If there were ever a time for me to shine if there was ever a time for me to be on top of my game it would be now. (Fury takes a left at the next light and stops his car infront of a hill, he gets out and walks to the hill he starts walking up it.) My opponent is a free agent by the name of Clayton Wright and to my luck I know nothing about the guy. His past history titles he has won federations he has competed in and that leaves me at a loss I don't know if I am going against a heavyweight, a crusierweight or a brawler. You can pretty much say I am going into this match blind and there is nothing I can do about it. (Fury makes his way up the hill and takes a seat he props his knees up then folds his arms over them.) Clayton this tournament as you know is big, it can be the vocal point for alot of superstars man can say they are in it to represent there federations to show what they have to bring to the table and with my that is partly true I do want to bring some honor and respect to my federation but more importantly Clayton I am in this for me. I am in this to win this. I want to be someone that is known around here. (Fury lies back in the grass and folds his arms behind his head.) Even if I only make it past the first round I want people to remember my name, more importantly I want to send a message that Fury has arrived. Clayton its round one the stakes are high and I plan on winning. I feel kinda sorry for you man I really do cause when its all over with you are going to head to the back and realize that you just got beat by someone you have never heard of a black horse an underdog if you will. (Fury takes a deep breath then clears his throat.) I won't lie though I will be just as shocked as anyone if I make it past the first round it will truly be something that will go up there in my list of accomplishments. Its all about who wants it more Clayton and I want it so bad I can taste it. I need this and I am going to get it why? CAUSE THE BOOK OF FURY SAYS SO. (Fury stands up and begins to walk back to his car and the scene) FADES TO BLACK Theme: Disbelif- a not believing in: Lack of belief. Ending Statement: To believe one can do something is the first step to greatness, at first it was strange I didn't really believe I joined the tournament I may have been drunk who knows I found the memo and I started at it in disbelief not really wanting to repeat my past failures. Then it hit me its time to make up and show the world what you are truly made of it's time for Fury to really arrive GTT4 first round Clayton Wright is the first that stands in my way, I will do all I can to make it past him and meet the next challenge. I want this, I need this and I will achieve it. It’s just a matter of time.
