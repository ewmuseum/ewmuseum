I'm Shocked and Appauled

15 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-15 [The Scene Opens, and quite bizarrely, if you haven’t been watching TV in the past week it would seem odd, we’re watching a Dumpster in the middle of the day. The rusty yellow skip staying in focus for several seconds. People walk past quite contented and motor vehicles can be heard passing behind the camera, horns beeping. One of the people passing the dumpster tosses a wrapper of a candy bar into it, quite unaware of what’s lurking within] [The scene cuts, now we can see a sleeping Smokey Wead, the sun shining on his face, dead to the world, in the metaphorical sense. A candy wrapper bounces off his head causing him to screw up his face and make it into the almost dream state of partial awareness, that type of sleep that makes you hallucinate slightly, before he starts top open his eyes and hope that it is all a dream. He rolls over, making an assortment of crunching noises, and as the days morning light filters in through his semi closed eyes the first thing he sees in front of his face is a used Condom] [For a few seconds it takes a little while for the sight to sink in, whether it is from the sudden brightness of the sun light, or just that his vision is still hazy from last night, or even if he’s still in pain from the fall, either way he was bound to notice it eventually] [The woman walking past had no idea what was going on] [As she walked past, completely oblivious to all these events, she is in the wrong place at the right time. A scream is heard from the dumpster she’s walking past as a man covered in trash vaults out from the skip and lands right in front of her. She screams. The Man looks at her and he screams again. She screams louder one more time before reaching for her shoulder strap bag and clonking the man over the head. The surprising weight in the handbag is enough to cause the man, once hitting the side of the dumpster to fall back into it again, in a spectacular cartwheel. She dusts her self down, lets out an indignant high pitched squeak and carries on] [Back in the skip Smokey shakes his groggy head free of the new cobwebs and takes his bearings again. Looking around he sees he’s back in the dumpster and clambers out slowly. Once on the pavement, he then dusts himself free of the assorted garbage that accumulated on him overnight. Finally ridding himself of all the junk he can see on himself, he realises an annoyance in the back of his neck, he reaches back and pulls out an entire raw liver. Smokey shudders and throws the liver away and turns to walk back to his apartment] [The Liver sails through the air, landing on the face of a passing cyclist, almost as content as Smokey was with having it about his person, the man tries to rid himself of it, this action distracts him from the road, causing him to career into a hot dog vender on the other side of the street. The hot dog stand tips over, spilling all the wieners over the sidewalk. The sudden meat up for grabs attracts the keen nose of an Alsatian dog, which makes a lunge at the hot dogs, taking its owner with it. Unfortunately, the Woman lets go of the pram she was holding, being taken aback by the sudden jerk by her pet. The pram now moves slowly down the sidewalks shallow gradient and out into the road. The driver of a red sports car, thankfully notices the new obstacle in the road and violently swerves it, crossing the road and running over a fire hydrant causing an impressive water display] [Smokey watches these events mouth hanging looser than Michael Jackson’s nose, utterly gob smacked] [He then thrusts his hands into his pockets, and whistles innocently, walking past the accident] [He looks puzzled for a moment, and we can see him patting his legs from inside the pocket, then his hip pocket, then his shirt, even though it doesn’t have any pockets. He then looks back at the dumpster and dives back in, strewing rubbish out of both sides of the yellow skip. He then emerges, with a sigh of relief holding a see through bag, containing several roll ups. He kisses the packet; happy to have it back, soon wishing that he hadn’t after a rather strange taste enters his mouth] [He hops out of the dumpster humming some tune or other to himself and starts to walk away, but his progress is hindered once again] Voice: Here! I saw that [Smokey cringes and turns round slowly, now facing a tramp, with a shopping trolley filed to the brim with various “things”] Tramp: I work this block; you’re taking what’s mine by agreement Smokey: No no no, I don’t think you understand, these are mine [He waves the pouch of roll ups at the tramp, but this doesn’t seem to make much difference] Tramp: Oh, wise guy eh? [The Tramp rummages in his trolley and comes back out holding a rolled up magazine, he bats in his free hand as if it were a baseball bat, it may not sound very threatening, but that magazine has staples in it god damn it. Smokey lets out a small whimper as the Tramp walks towards him] [The Tramp lifts up the magazine and begins beating the now cowering Smokey about the head with the magazine, Smokey holding up his hands in a poor attempt to deflect the blows] Smokey: Ow stop it, ow, ow, quit it, ow [Smokey turns on the spot and runs, crouched over round a corner, the Tramp in hot pursuit, still hitting him around the head] Tramp: Give up? Give up? Gimme the garbage Smokey: I haven’t got any garbage, it’s my cigarettes Tramp: Course it is, hand it over [He stops hitting Smokey, who’s now slumped against a wall, and holds out his hand patiently. Smokey looks up cautiously, checking to see that he has indeed stopped the beat down. He looks up to the tramp now standing above him, but looks left when he hears some yelling, the tramp just keeps his eye’s fixed on Smokey] Smokey: Hey, Isn’t that your trolley? Tramp: What? [He turns round in time to see a gang of teenagers running round the corner laughing and joking with his trolley of things. The Tramp drops the magazine and chases after the yobs] Tramp: Here! COME BACK WITH MY STUFF! [Now the tramp also runs out of sight around the corner, pursuing a new quarry. Smokey simply looks down at the magazine in the new hubbub. It’s the latest PTC monthly, containing special coverage of the Cruiserweight tournament, Smokey picks it up and flicks through the pages, the scene fades out on that image] [The scene fades back in; we can now see Smokey has finally managed to get back up to his apartment, although we don’t know how long. He’s pacing back and forth across an open area of the room, Bubba, his tag partner, is now holding the magazine scanning it. His fez cocked in a quizzical position, as in its only being held on his head by his raised eyebrows, the man in the suit, Smokey’s tournament liaison that he still hasn’t bothered to learn the name of yet is sitting in a chair shaking his head] Smokey: I can’t believe it, I Just can’t believe it…. Do you believe it? [He turns to the man in the suit, who, after realising that he’s asking him the question, promptly shakes his head] Smokey: I just don’t believe it… Read it back to me again Bubba [Silence] Smokey: Bubba? Bubba: Eeeerrr [Smokey turns round and fixes his eyes on Bubba, still sitting staring at the magazine] Smokey: Bubba… It helps if the magazine isn’t upside down [His face growing red Bubba turns it the right way up] Smokey: Now hand it to what’s his face [Bubba Closes the magazine and tosses it to the man in the suit, and then sits back feeling like a fool, of either XUW or UWF fame] Smokey: I just don’t believe it [The man in the suit opens the magazine and looks through it] Smokey: The nerve, the utmost gall of them, I’m going to write a letter, I mean it, their not being let off with this Man: You mean the bit where they say you didn’t deserve to win against Kirk Urban Smokey: No not that bit Man: The bit where they call you a Weed of a man? Smokey: No not that either Man: You have no chance against Jimmy Diablo? Smokey: No Man: you give the UWF a bad name? Smokey: No Man: What then? Smokey: They said I was a Capricorn, when I’m an Aries, all that other stuff is true enough Man: What? That’s all your mad about? This entire article about you laughs you off as a joke that got a lucky break! Smokey: You know how unlucky it is for someone to get your star sign wrong? Man: No…. Smokey: Neither do I…. but it can hardly be good luck can it [Smokey paces about a bit, obviously worked up and worried] Man: Your being ridiculous Smokey: Am I? Ok, ok, I have a solution [He turns to Bubba, rubbing both hands together] Smokey: Get the laxatives; I’m getting my rabbit’s foot back [Bubba coughs and splutters at the suggestion, where as the man in the suit screws up in his face in disgust] Man: I’m sure that’s a bit extreme, maybe you could try something else… Smokey: Maybe your right…. Ok, how’s this, find twenty black cats… and then we can shoo them over my path Man: Isn’t that bad luck? Bubba: Mmm, Other White Meat Smokey: I don’t know… Ok, maybe that wont work… but I’ve got to do something to help me Man: you could go to the Gym and train a bit Smokey: Please, let’s be more realistic, no need to go to extremes… Bubba…. Do you still have that longhaired wig? Man: You can’t possibly think Bubba could be passed off as you do you? Smokey: Worked before [The man in the suit gives Smokey an icy glare, while Smokey is completely oblivious to why] Man: You’d be disqualified for being over the weight limit Smokey: so? Man: We’d fire you for that, nice little clause in your contract [Smokey pauses for a minute fixing an icy glare of his own on the man in the suit, who is now smiling jovially] Smokey: I hate you Man: Thanks [Suddenly a deathly silence creeps through the room, everyone being quiet and motionless] [Smokey flops out on the nearest thing available… Bubba] Smokey: Ok, you win; we’ll go to the gym Man: That’s the spirit of a fighter Smokey: Don’t be ridiculous; it’s just to get you off my back [The Scene Fades, as you don’t want to see Bubba in a Gym, no seriously, you don’t… we’ll how about this comparative scene] [Scene opens on a plate full of orange jelly on a washing machine with the concrete weight removed. The scene stays in for a few minutes before fading back out again] [Darkness]
