Testing Times...[GT rp2, rp 3/6 The Final Saga]

Kaiowas Cable Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 52 Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 5:07 pm Post subject: Testing Times...[GT rp2, rp 3/6 The Final Saga] Where? Exactly.... Where? Where on earth.... or elsewhere, was Kaiowas? And how.... how did he get here? Fuzzy hazy memories of little and nothing are all that flow through the neural networking of his brain... And no matter where he was..... What exactly was "here"? The familiar doubt and confusion sets in quickly as Kaiowas sits where he is. Not exactly fear yet.... It hardly ever comes to that.... but the lack of confidence propagates itself through his feelings. Trying as he had done so many times, Kaiowas struggles to bring to the surface of his mind any of his short term memory... Again his brain flops in failure and the now numbing pain shoots through his legs and arms. It felt like something close to the pain of extreme exhaustion... the sort caused by a huge build of of lactic acid in the muscles. For some reason or for some sort of mental blockage Kaiowas could not kick his mind into gear and remember the important things.... Like why the hell he was sitting in this "place", if it could be even given the honour of being called a place.... Kaiowas himself felt trapped... Usually a feeling very foreign to the huge Brazilian... And as they say, never corner a rat. That usually applied to Kaiowas, the rebellious streak in him breaking against advantages and using the only power of the underdog, surprise, to succeed. But right now it just wasn't possible to even start any sort of escape. He felt drowsy and drugged, to the point where lifting his arms resulted in them falling back to the cold blank surface he sits on within seconds. His body just wouldn't respond.... just like his short term memory. Everything seemed to have fused out and fallen into a dirty heap of mixed emotions, scraps of broken memories and random surges of short term energy. Nothing seemed to last long enough in this place to actually be of any use. Kaiowas' morale was at an extreme low, his mind beginning to worry about the things he had held for certain in this coming week. He has two very important matches in the Primetime Central event: New Era... It should be the least important thing on his mind right now, but he cannot stop relishing the thought of the chance to destroy so many names in one night... Then he realises he is stuck here and there is no way he can fight in his matches for the DWO and Global Titles. The power and fame spurs him on in the short spurts of energy but he just cannot pull or push himself far enough to actually make any real progress to escaping here.... Here? What was here? Well there isn't much to say about a prison cell bathed in the sweet black light of night. By touch, the floors were made from blocks of stone hewed from quarries and so were the walls. A few feet away lay the open cell door. So demoralising was that sight... How can he not reach it and get out, make his way back to the village to recover? Why is it so impossible to crawl just feet to escape? Kaiowas wasn't even chained to anything.... It was a sort of physical and mental demoralisation tactic that his imprisoners were employing to break him down. Slowly it was working. Everything combining to become one great force push back his boundaries of confidence and bursting through the effects of his Tribal mental conditioning exercises. His head pounded and his heart thumped. From time to time everything hurt, then everything stopped... The teasing would never end and it was getting to him.... No-one visited him, no-one fed him or gave him water yet he never became thirsty or hungry in the slightest. In fact he seemed to need nothing at all. This prison confused his mind, the jumbled set up and lax imprisoners seemed illogical and nonsensical to him. Why wasn't someone torturing him? Or even just making sure he was still in his cell and that he wouldn't escape from under their noses.... That is what Kaiowas would do.... That is what Kaiowas did at his post as jailor back in the Amazon with the Ktulu. Why? Why wasn't he chained? Why wasn't he kicked and beaten? Why wasn't he questioned continually? Why was he even in this cell.... Something seemed to stop him even remember his capture and his capturers. The only clue he had was that his ears were bleeding when he awoke here and his head hurt like it had been slammed against a brick wall many times. Something began to occur to Kaiowas, something awoke in his mind.... not memories, not shards of his short term... But his thought process kicked into gear and his brain began to formulate as much as it could. It rushed through possibilities and all sorts of information that he had gleamed from his surroundings were all thrown into the mix. Finally his mind offered up the thought like a gift to the gods. The first thing he had acheived in the cell. On that very positive note Kaiowas realised that the thought was extremely depressing.... They didn't need to keep him here.... Kaiowas' total lack of energy or confidence, will to move and his body's incapability to do anything bar surviving kept him here.... Kaiowas was failing in his escape because his body stopped him. Not because of the high security and not because of any sort of person keeping him here. His imprisoners were probably laughing at him and at their treatment of him. They were so confident that they could leave the prison door wide open without fear of their prisoner making any sort of escape act. They were so confident in the power of this fact that they did not need to physically destroy him or mentally break him down. They knew that this situation would do that too.... Or maybe it was the voices they relied upon.... *** Leader? Lord? I see no Lord before me... No reverent man, no honourable respectable man.... I see no Lord! *** *** A Leader? Where is the courageous never say die leader? Where has he gone to? Has he run to protect his Tribe? All I see before me is a miserable tramp! *** Kaiowas: (feebly) No! [Kaiowas lies now in the corner of the cell, his arms surrounding his legs pulling them closer to his chest in a futile act of defense from the bodiless voices that sound throughout the empty cell. Looking around him in despair, Kaiowas cannot find the source of their whining, disconcerting insulting tones. Kaiowas looks like an utter wreck... His face sweats anew as the voices lambaste him once more, his dreadlocks, normally pure and clean, lie dirty and ruffled as they fall from his scalp. His body is covered in mud and muck, his skin showing bruises and little scars that healed from cuts of battle. A huge nick out of his shoulder has healed badly into a pure pink scar, blemishing his chestnut brown skin even further. Acting like a scared boy, Kaiowas tries to pull himself as far away from the voices as possible....] *** This one is a warrior you know! *** *** Warrior? Noble, strong, confident men who destroy the enemy with ease and who protect their comrades with as much valour as they can muster? *** *** Yes one of those....... I don't see it in him though! All I can say is that from what I see we have a coward! Yes! A coward who lets his friends die for him! *** Kaiowas: No! Lecood.... That was not my fault! No! [Lecood? Quickly a memory of the man who had given his life for the Tribe lately flickers into his mind. This time it doesn't hurt him but it does not help his physical condition...] *** Lecood? An old man? You let an old man save your life? You must be even more pathetic than I thought! And then you let him die!! *** *** What a coward you are! You are such a useless warrior, such a useless leader.... You do not deserve the Ktulu! They are blind to the fact! You are not worthy to even be their slave! How do you believe that you can take on a god *** *** A god? "He" is going to fight a god! That is insulting to the god! This wretch couldn't land a blow on a slow moving cow! *** Kaiowas: Pultra is no god! No....! [Pultra.... Another memory rolls into his mind's eye. The evil manipulative powerful fake god known as Pultra. Kaiowas was meant to be destroying him, leading the last offensive in the long and bloody campaign that started after Kaiowas turned against his former master and vowed to kill the impostor god who had held control over his Tribe, the Ktulu, for generations. The strength seemed to begin to flow back into him slowly, but so did the pain and this advancement did him no good...] *** Feisty eh? I thought you were brain dead, you must just be dead meat then! How can you ever come to challenge this "fake god" of yours? You have nothing! Look at yourself! A quivering wreck in an open prison cell! *** *** You can't even escape when the door is open! How do you expect to even scrape a fallen Angel? You even fear our voices! You let us break you down! How could you ever be able to destroy an angel*** [Kaiowas has pulled himself into more of a sitting position, feeling slightly more confident with the advantage of his fast returning memory... Sliding up against the wall for support he tries to battle back verbally against the hollow voices...] Kaiowas: How would I kill Pultra? How would I end his vile existence? I would take my bladed staff and skewer it through his body armour reaching down into the depths of his chest cavity wrenching his heart free of his body and ending his life with one twist of the serrated blades! That is how I would destroy him! [Finding more strength Kaiowas pulls himself to his feet and throws back his hair. Flexing his muscles he looks forwards once more....] *** Show us.... *** *** Yes! Show us! *** [Suddenly in front of Kaiowas his huge wooden Tribal bladed Staff materialises. It shines a white light and Kaiowas gasps... Slowly inching his hand towards the handle, being very cautious in case of any treachery he closed in on the grasp.] *** Show us your skills little warrior! *** [Grasping the handle with one hand and then both he pulls it from its floating position in the air and strikes into his offensive pre set battle position, with the long staff held horizontally in order to be able to strike with both ends and to fed off any blows in a one hundred and eighty degree radius.] Kaiowas: I'll show you! [Suddenly the white light that emanated from the staff begins to run up both of Kaiowas' arms enveloping his body slowly. Kaiowas gasps as he looks at it and soon his whole body is an image of shining white light silhouetting his body. A whining noise like that of an engine starts up and gets faster and faster....] *** FLASH *** Where now? [Where now had Kaiowas been transported? All is black and dark. He can still feel the tough grain of his bladed staff in his hands and he knows that he still rests in his offensive pose. His sense of smell is giving him warnings as the slight breeze brings odours of foul and brooding things. His ears also are sending him signals of danger. He still could not see anything at all but the rest of his senses were just strong enough to make up for the absence of sight. Strangely Kaiowas now felt none of the fatigue that he felt five minutes ago in that cell. The lactic acid build up had leeched away and the tiredness had fallen from him. Yet he still could not place any details about his capture or anything relevant to his capture.] *** ROARRGGHHH *** Kaiowas: (quietly) Here we go... [All at once the lights flicker on and Kaiowas is in awe of what greets his eyes. All around him is a huge collosseum. The seats all lie empty, not a person spectating this event. Where Kaiowas stands is typically sandy ground, the arena part of the huge building. Around him all the gates lie shut and locked. Horrifyingly in front of him stands the most disgusting and foul abomination that Kaiowas has ever set eyes on in his life, and it is readying to charge him!] Kaiowas: (quietly and confidently) Let us begin.... [The huge mess of blood, flesh and bone opens it's maw further as it releases its bone chilling bellow. The constitution of the whole beast seems to be demon like. It's skin is a thick type of chainmail scales all overlapping forming a great defense from blades and slices. It's long thin pointed face is brought to a fine sharp teeth filled mouth. It's arms are covered in hair and ripple with sick looking muscles. It stands in total at ten feet tall and it's pointed tail pokes out from behind it. At the minute it levels itself by crouching and builds up ready to charge!] Kaiowas: OOOOWWEEEEEEEEERRRRRRRRRRRR!!! *** ROOOOAAAARGGHHHH!!! *** [Kaiowas seizes the early advantage by moving first and he charges forward towards the beast, who in response moves off milliseconds later. The speed of the huge demon is also extremely surprising for its size. It's agility is also very high as it darts toward Kaiowas, feinting this way and that trying to throw Kaiowas off the game of chicken. Kaiowas has no intention of changing direction and levels his staff, the sharp serrated blades of Lecood glinting and pointing towards the beast before him. They rapidly close on each other. At the last second Kaiowas leaps, visibly rethinking his approach in mid air as he turns side on, swinging the first blow towards the temple are of the demons head....] Kaiowas: RROOOOOAAAAAARRRRGGGGHHHH!! *** THWACK *** [Kaiowas failed! The demon had thought on the move and swayed sidewards raising his right arm to swat the man nearly half his size away. Kaiowas hits the ground running and is closing in on his large enemy once more. This time he uses his Staff as a personal lever, like a pole vault, launching him into the air once more. Holding on to the weapon as he flies, he brings it to bear in a foul swoop and fury of left and right blows to the face and shoulders of the demon.] *** CLUNK, THWACK, CLUNK *** [Kaiowas' blows were all deflected once again. The demon had raised his hand to cover his face and the scales on his shoulder had done the rest of the work. The beast had survived once more, without even a scratch! Kaiowas however, had flown over the shoulder of the beast as he had hurriedly made the flurry of strikes. Kaiowas hits the ground at speed and skids back a few yards before toppling and rolling to a crouch position.] Kaiowas: What the? [Now the Demon was on the attack, after patiently discarding any attempt by Kaiowas to land any sort of blow or strike. Smiling evilly it lifts a huge demon handful of sand from the dusty ground and watches the grains fall away from his grasp. Kaiowas sits watching, panting, fearing that the sands of time may have finally fallen away prematurely in his life and that he may be ended right here and now... Everything he had achieved and had set out to achieve would be lost in one duel with a huge red bloodthirsty demon....] Demon: Little Warrior..... You are puny! [With that the demon pounced, flinging himself into the air and landing feet from Kaiowas, throwing a clawed hand at Kaiowas. Kaiowas was quick to fend it off but the strike left three huge notches on his large wooden staff. The next blow came from left field and even though Kaiowas could not get his staff around to block in time, his instincts forced his to bounce backwards and he only sustained small scratches from the long clawed hand that reached out at him.] Kaiowas: Demon... you will perish... [Sweat now freely rolling off Kaiowas' brow, his unclothed upper body glistened, the various tattoos shining with their black green ink. His dreadlocks fly and fall all mirroring the movements of his head with a slight delay. The battle is intensifying and the Demon has Kaiowas still on the defensive, but the Tribal Warrior is keeping him well at bay with the long reach of the bladed Tribal Staff. Blocking and parrying the strong and swift forearm crushing blow attempts by the demon is tiring Kaiowas slowly but surely. Kaiowas attempts a quick whirlwind flurry of slices after countering the last blow of a sequence to buy himself some time. Spinning and twirling nimbly, he twists the staff in a honed flight, attempting to strike the demon as quickly and as strongly as possible while getting Kaiowas out of the way of the huge beast at the same time. The whirling blade strikes once, twice and three times making no dent on the scales of the beast but Kaiowas puts at least ten feet between them giving him time to regroup.] Kaiowas: (mockingly) Demon! You are too slow! Ha! [Striking a heroic pose Kaiowas recomposes himself for the next drive of attacks and parries, while the demon makes the ground between the two combatants. Kaiowas notes that the beast is picking up speed and momentum, and it reaches him making a tackle dive at him. Kaiowas ducks under the tackle and points the blade of the spear upwards, forcing it in through the scales as the demon flies over his crouched form. The blade actually pierces the armour like skin and a green liquid flows forth. Retracting the weapon Kaiowas rolls back away from the beast again, giving both fighters a chance to clear their heads. The demon roars in pain but the pain is short lived as it is soon up on its feet and advancing on Kaiowas again... Kaiowas himself raises his staff and finally holds his ground this time. Guessing at the beasts first move Kaiowas ducks right and plants a sweet blow to the kneecap on its left leg. The crunch is audible and the beast buckles, roaring wildly from its maw.] Kaiowas: Demon you have failed! [Kaiowas is moving again, towards the prone figure of the great demon and is soon upon him, ripping and tearing at its flesh with both ends of the staff, in alternate blows and slices. The more Kaiowas weakens the demon, the thinner and less resilient the skin becomes. Soon Kaiowas' face is flecked with the beast's green blood and the roars of the felled giant are inescapable. Suddenly it heaves Kaiowas off it and with one mighty movement it knocks Kaiowas onto his back. Kaiowas hits the ground hard and has the wind knocked out of him. He struggles to get up but finds a foot on his bare chest. The demon presses down hard, applying pressure and force to Kaiowas' chest. Kaiowas' arms go limp and his staff rolls away in the dust...] Demon: Foolhardy Human! [Reaching down with his claws the demon prepares to rip Kaiowas' head apart in the rage and fury of the heat of battle. Green blood drips onto Kaiowas from the severely wounded Demon, but the wounds seems to not have slowed him at all. The dark shadow of the demon falls over Kaiowas as his death approaches....] _________________ DWO World Champion and Most Improved 2002, PTC no1 january 8th and 14th and no7 too!
