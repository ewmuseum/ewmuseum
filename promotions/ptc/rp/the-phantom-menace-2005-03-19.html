The Phantom Menace

19 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-19 Only the sound of my boots crashing down on the hard floor was heard as I marched through the dark palace. There were numerous statues scattered amongst the shadows, almost as if they were hiding from my very presence. Most of the details of my surroundings were hidden, not that it mattered too much to me. I knew where my destination was, and the Emperor was waiting for my arrival. It had been some time since I was sent away to search out the planet that we were to overtake next. This Empire was ever growing, and they so close to completing their quest to rule the galaxy. The last of the rebellion would soon crumble by their hand. Victory was oh so close, and with this next conquest, the War could finally come to an end. Only The Emperor's short sightedness almost ruined him. He gave his top spots ... the generals or his Armies, to those who could barely tie my own boot lace. Now he knew the error of his ways. He may not wish to admit it, but he knows he needs me ... and in a way I needed him. Before I knew it I had reached two large, heavy doors with all kinds of foreign designs and inscriptions. I pulled one arm out from beneath my cloak and pushed the doors open. Behind those doors was the one I had come to see. He was seated in his throne, which was shrouded in darkness, with only the light flickering of a few candles around him. From this distance I couldn’t make out much more than his outline, but as I grew closer, more of his face became recognizable. He as well was wearing a cloak, with only a bit more than half of his face being uncovered. His skin, which had begun wrinkling at a rapid pace, was very pale, so pale that even in darkness it could be seen, like moon light. As old as his face looked, his empty eyes told a story of even longer proportion. It was almost as if I were staring straight through him, and not at him. I got only a few paces from him, and yet he still remained motionless, almost as if he were in a deep sleep, but I knew better. I pulled my hood off, letting my long dark hair flow behind me. More what seemed like an eternity, I stood there in front of that throne, and watched and waited. I knew what he was waiting for, and he knew he wouldn’t get that satisfaction. After awhile, a very low chuckle came from his voice. His laugh seemed so far away, as if it came from somewhere else, almost ancient. “Still haven’t learned your manners Knight ?” “Sorry Emperor Caldera, they seem to miss me everytime.” The Emperor adjusts a bit in his seat, a seat which he has almost seemed to be part of every since he came into power. His dark eyes meet my Icy blues in a lock, perhaps a lock of acknowledgment, respect or even somewhere … hatred. “So Knight, you have been gone for almost four years … I assume you found what you were looking for.” “ and more Emperor … I learned the things that I never could from you.” The Emperor took in a deep breath, holding it for a brief moment before exhaling and turning his gaze away again. Staring off into the darkness, as if searching for something he continued on. “The Empire was young then Knight … I was young then. Yes I know that by the look of myself now, it would seem that I was always this way. Things aren’t always as they appear Knight … far from it in fact. I gave you the foundation of what you were to become …” “NO ! I built who I am Emperor. You did not lead me to the dark side, I paved my own damn road. You can take credit for a lot in this galaxy … almost wiping out the Rebellion, Destroying Sidious and STILL having these pathetic creatures follow you, making this empire larger than it ever could be … but you will NOT take credit for creating me.” “How dare YOU interrupt me? Knight it seems your time away has made you feel some sort of invincibility. You will learn your place, that I will be sure of.” “Give me a break Caldera … I have battled an Army with nothing but my skills with my light saber. You do not frighten me, not now, not four years ago … NEVER.” The Emperor’s gaze snapped to me, and slowly he pushed himself to his feet. I could almost feel the pain in his body as he raised, almost from the dead. It was probably his first movement of this extent in ages. Never one to back down … I stood up to the Emperor. He inched closer to me, stepping from the throne, and just inches from me. I could smell the death that just flowed from his body … his entire being. “Defiance Knight … it was what always defined you. No matter the great ones I had by my side, Darth Jhub, Octavian, Dread, Mayhem … they never quite had that darkness in their soul that you possess.” “There’s a problem with this though Caldera … I never have stood at your side. I have always fought for one person and one person only.” “This is correct, but now is the time for you to stand up and take your place. The Empire needs you … needs your strength and your skill.” “What do I get out of this Caldera ? I know you want me to finish the Rebellion, you want me to give you last few worlds that you do not have in this war, but what about me?” “You Knight … will get it all. When it is my time to pass, you will control this Empire, and you will rule this world of darkness.” I turned away from the Emperor, now thinking about this offer he has handed me. I didn’t need much convincing nor much thought. I spun back around, with a grin on my face. “Where do we begin Emperor ?” A devious smile spread across his face now, as he turned and went back to his throne. He sat, cracking all of his bones as he sat down. He placed a long, thing finger under his chin as if in deep thought. “There are but SEVEN worlds to be conquered now Knight. You shall become the best of the seven. I have struck a deal with the Rebellion … a deal to have the greatest each has to offer, battle it out. Dark and Light Jedi alike will find their way to the World of Earth. A committee has been formed … Prime Time Central, is what they have called themselves. They will over see the affairs.” “And you?” “I know you will handle it … you have beaten an Army before remember?” “I grow tired of this … just tell me who I must kill first.” “To be apart of the Best of Seven, you must defeat a man from AWS. I know very little about him … you will have to do the research on your own.” “Pretty vague description there don’t you think ?” “It’s all he goes by … do you really need more ? Hes a Jedi and he uses a Light Saber, deal with the rest.” “Fine Caldera … I will single handedly end this war. This man, this AWS Man will fall to my feet, and then I will proceed to destroying the rest.” “Very good Knight … In Five Days you shall arrive on Earth, I expect great results.” “Enjoy the show Emperor.” “Good journey … Darth Knight.” With a slight bow of my head and a smirk I covered my head with my hood, and turned away from the Emperor leaving him at his throne, alone once more. After years of me sharpening my skills, he called again … which was no surprise to me. He allowed his best to go once before, he needed me this time. He had no one else worthy of going into battle with the Rebellion and actually winning. I had never been to this planet Earth, but I was sure that it would be quite an experience. I could play Caldera’s game for now, destroy his enemies and what not. For now it would be all simplicity … but if he really thinks that I will allow him to simply bask in my glory, then he has another thing coming. This was merely step one, in my quest to receive the ultimate prize. Winning this war would mean being the greatest Jedi … Light or Dark to journey through the Galaxy. In these next few days I did have somethings to look out for, namely this Man of AWS. I would find all I can, and soon our battle shall go down, and I will stand victorious. Things certainly have changed over the years, but all to the good. I am focused and more disciplined than ever before … heading into battle this time just feels right. Everything will fall in to place, that I have no doubt about. For now … The Battle of the Seven best awaits … “BOOOM !” Lightning streaked across the sky, lighting up my entire bedroom, waking me up instantly. I remained laying there in my bed of silk sheets, and just looked from side to side. Everything kind of came back to me all at once. After a short time, I forced myself to sit up in bed. I attempted to rub the sleep from my eyes, but it was of no use. In a sudden rush, my dream came back to me … and I laughed out loud. My imagination certainly runs away with me sometime, with wrestling and obviously a bit too much Star Wars watching going on. I spun in bed, allowing my feet to pant themselves on the carpet below. After standing I groggily walk my way to the bathroom door, then flick on the light. Of course the light temporarily blinds me, as everything is just one flash of red and yellow. My eyes adjust not too long after that, and I find myself staring at myself in the mirror. I simply stare for awhile, watching myself … wondering if I knew exactly what was digging around in my mind anyways. Did I really see myself or my situation as “STAR WARS” material ? Nah of course not, we would have to accomplish quite a feat. I have slept lightly since this thing has been cranking things up. I know Caldera chose me over the rest, but the pressure is alot. It is up to me to respresent the face of GCW as wel as my own personal pride. Do I have what it takes to destroy any and all challengers ? I guess the day of reckoning will soon ... very soon, be coming full circle. All answers shall be questioned.
