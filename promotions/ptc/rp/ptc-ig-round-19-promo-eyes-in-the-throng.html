IG Round 19 Promo - Eyes In The Throng

29 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jake Hix Roung 19 Promo Eyes In The Throng Nightclub The lights swirled around the dance club briefly touching the young flesh like a lover’s kiss before moving on as the music continued pumping out from the many speakers. He didn’t want to be here in the club, but his personal opinions didn’t end up meaning a whole lot sometimes. He was a public figure, and sometimes that meant living up to the persona he had built for himself years before. Tonight, Pitt had arranged the meeting with the Japanese marketing executives. The topic at hand was some kind of crazy energy drink, and they were considering using him as a spokesman. Despite his attempts to wriggle out of the meeting, here he was surrounded by the target audience downing the straight shot of liquor to chase away the lingering doubts in his mind. Jake Hix had more pressing matters to worry about, but for now he needed to be the Big Dawg. The concerns of his personal life and family struggles had to be put aside to handle his business life. Jake motioned for another shot of tequila, wanting to enjoy the smooth burn of the Patron before the guests arrived. A powerful hand clapped down on his right shoulder, almost making him spill his shot. Jake looked into the mirror behind the bar and saw his brother’s smiling face. “They here already?” “Waiting upstairs in one of the private rooms. They seem very interested in discussing this idea with you. The old fans and the new fans all seem willing to see you behind something like this.” Pitt took the empty seat next to Jake at the bar. “You seem in a dour mood.” “Dour? Who the fuck talks like that Pitt?” Jake downed his shot. “I could say dreary, dismal or dark if you prefer, but there’s an artistry in the word dour that I enjoy. Now what is wrong with you?” Jake dropped the shot glass onto the bar, letting it bounce and spin before slipping behind the bar and falling into one of the trays of glasses. He turned, an empty soul staring into his brother’s confident gaze, “How can you ask me that?” Pitt threw his arm over Jake’s shoulder; closing the space between them so only they shared this moment. “You have to forget all that Jake. You’re out and about, and the eyes of the world are on you. The eyes of your enemies will be judging you, but you also have fans eyes here. I just need you to put aside everything and be the Big Dawg for these men. That’s the only choice, you have to be the man for everyone’s sake.” “I don’t know if I’m that guy anymore. I don’t know who I am anymore Pitt.” Jake hung his head for a second, and then felt the eyes and glances. He looked in the mirror, not catching the looks in action, but wondering which ones he had just missed. He wouldn’t give them the satisfaction, not now. Without thinking about it he had already flipped the switch. He had become so adept at being different people he was beginning to forget the lines of demarcation between his own personalities. Jake threw Pitt’s arm off and raised his arms into the air. The suit jacket crinkled up as he stretched out his massive form, “Let’s go make some money!” Jake straightened his suit jacket, and then ran his hands over his face to revive himself before smoothing his hair back. Presentation would be important to these men. They expected the consummate man on the scene for this meeting, the dangerous man they had seen in the tapes that would have been reviewed. Pitt stood up, “That’s what I wanted to hear. Come on.” They walked across the club floor, smiling back at the women giving them the second and third glances. “Now, they’re here to party and talk. They’ve brought a lot of women with them like they always do. You need to establish yourself right off the bat. Don’t worry about offending these men; they are just the marketing side of this opportunity. You need to exceed their preconceived notions and expectations.” Pitt explained as they climbed the stairs. “Be the bad ass.” “Sure, if you can still find that somewhere inside that tequila soaked brain” Pitt teased, enjoying getting under his brother’s skin. Pitt and Jake approached the opaque sliding glass doors. They nodded at the wide set man standing watch on the outside. He dutifully opened the door for them, and they stepped inside. The closing of the door insulated them from almost all of the sound outside. The V.I.P. Room was filled with trendy black furniture with red accents that matched the color scheme of the entire club. Lounging around with open bottles of champagne and liquor were many women and three men. The three Japanese executives were all dressed alike in black suits, white ties and what seemed like a standard skinny black tie. Jake gave each of them the once over, looking for the subtle signs of the leader amongst these men. Normally, you would look to the center where a man could be the center of attention. He had learned many years before that often the leader would be aside in order to observe the exchange without dominating the scene. Subtle clues revealed themselves. The man in the middle cast a furtive glance over to the man on the right, and two of the women were being overly courteous to him. The man on the left seemed unwilling to look up at Jake at all, intimidated by his arrival. The other women in the room, seven in total, were listening or visiting, but those two were more engaged with that man. The man took the time to look up from his special treatment, not confronting Jake with his casual glimpse but ascertaining who he and what he was in that short moment between breaths. Without waiting, Jake walked over to the man on the right. “Kenji Yamazaki, you’re the man who is so interested in meeting me.” Kenji slowly pushed away the young blonde’s arm holding the champagne glass with two fingertips. He rose from the couch, tucking his tie behind his jacket as he buttoned it up. Jake was even larger in person, and he found himself put off looking up at a man a foot taller and almost wider than he and his companions put together. “How did you know?” A wizened voice asked. “Surely you don’t think the Big Dawg can’t recognize the leader of the pack?” Jake leaned down, bringing his face right in front of Kenji’s so the man’s black eyes were suddenly in the spotlight of Jake’s fierce emeralds. “Or are you trying to say something about me?” The already quiet room became still. The merriment seemed to cease as though an invisible force sucked the joy out of the space. Jake let it hang in the air uncomfortably a little longer than necessary before a deep, roaring laugh came rushing out of his belly. He grabbed Kenji by both shoulders and shook him roughly as everyone else began forcing their own laugh. “Come on Kenji, you need to relax a little bit. I’m just messing with you. How do you like the club my brother picked out?” Jake asked as he observed the man closely. Kenji shook off the sudden loss of face as a cultural misunderstanding as he laughed a little, “I like the club, especially the women. They are…friendly.” “Hey, any place we go to always finds its share of friendly women.” Jake offered his hand to the blonde who had been feeding sips of champagne to Kenji. She stood with his help, and then he pulled her close his arm wrapping around her back. “I see you got started without me, but let’s have a little taste of my home. Let’s get some Patron all around. Pitt, let’s get a couple of bottles up here.” “Good idea.” Pitt stepped out to let the guard know to place an order for them. “So, who’re your friends?” Jake looked at the other two suits. Kenji nodded at him, “This is my director of media Tsutomu Shimura and my director of brand management Yuzo Kato.” Jake gave each of them a little head bow, “Well boys, you seem to know how to welcome a man. Let’s get a little warm companion ship and get to know each other.” Jake took the blonde with him as he sat down on one of the open couches, forcing the men to rearrange to get a better view at him. He called a redhead over with the curl of his fingers, and she joined him on his open side. “Aren’t you a lovely young thing.” “My name in Sarah.” “Of course it is.” Jake pushed a few strands of hair away from her face, his lingering gaze forcing her to look down and away. They sat, mingling and mixing for a time before the bottles of the Patron arrived. Just as the shots were poured Jake’s cell phone rang. He took it out and looked down at the number, “Gentlemen, if you’ll excuse me for a moment.” The three men nodded. Jake answered, “Hello? Oh, hey. This isn’t the best time for…okay. What happened? So what does that mean for me? Can you call me back when you know something? Thanks.” “Nothing wrong I hope?” Kenji asked. Jake shook his head, “Nothing with me. You know I had a match against Alcaeus and Penance.” “Right, such attention has not escaped us. Your recent exploits and branching out are part of the reason we wanted to have this discussion with you.” Tsutomu added. “Well, apparently Alcaeus has some personal problems. His head up his hemorrhoid infested ass, some lover’s spat or something bad enough to get the old hook. So he’s out, and now they don’t know about the match.” Jake shook his head in disbelief as he downed the shot of Patron. Kenji was annoyed, “This is not good. We wanted to build on such exposure. “Hey, this a Greek tragedy for all of us. They’ll work it out and let me know what’s…” Jake was cut off as his cell phone rang again. He pulled it out, “One sec guys. This is Jake. Yeah, so what did they decide? Hey, that works for me. Good riddance to bad disease ridden rubbish as far as I’m concerned.” Kenji waited expectantly as Jake snapped his cell phone closed, “Good news?” “Well, Alcaeus is definitely gone. Looks like me and Penance will tear into it.” Jake poured himself another shot. “How about a drink to that?” Everyone picked up their shot glass. They all clinked the glasses together and then downed the strong tequila. Many of the women and young Yuzo seemed to struggle with the shot. Pitt reached over and playfully slapped Yuzo in the belly, “That fire will help put a little hair on your chest Yuzo my boy.” Through watery eyes Yuzo smiled at Pitt, “No, this is great stuff. Top notch.” Jake’s phone rang once again. He pulled it back out, “They just don’t give up. I apologize Kenji.” “No, let’s see what they are doing now.” Kenji laughed. “Perhaps they will have more gossip on Alcaeus.” There was laughter as Jake answered his phone, “Hello? And I care because? Look, you tell them that I don’t care who gets lined up on the other side of me. I just want someone to lay out in front of the entire world. Bring Lamen on as far as I’m concerned and anyone else they want to throw my way. Hell, any chance to knock someone from Prime down a peg or two is a community service as far as I’m concerned.” Jake hung up. He held down the button, powering the phone down. “I think we’ve had enough interruptions tonight gentlemen, and you deserve some attention, and these ladies deserve our full attention.” Jake stroked Sarah’s thigh for emphasis. “So did I hear it will be you versus Penance and Lamen?” Tsutomu asked. “Looks that way.” Jake’s other hand was hanging over the blonde’s shoulder, the caress moving closer and closer down to her enhance bosom in front of these men. “For me, that just means the chance to make two more people my bitches.” ‘You know, this is the kind of attitude the manufacturer we represent is looking for Jake.” Kenji began to slip some business in, “He needs someone who can grab the attention of the young men and women, and portray the strength and…and…cockiness?” Pitt laughed, “Yes, cockiness.” “Good. We need that kind of person, and we think you could be the man to fit the bill so to speak.” Kenji poured them each another shot of Patron. He pushed the shot glass over to Jake as he raised his own, “So what do you think?” Jake picked up the offering, “What kind of time are we talking about me spending in Japan on this?” “A couple of weeks a year, maybe three at most to do some promotional work.” Yuzo explained. He tilted his glass toward Kenji, “Let’s talk through the specifics tomorrow, but it sounds like something I could support. Tonight is not about details, tonight is about getting to know each other, binding as it were. So let’s drink to that and let our attorney’s work out the details while we work through our hangovers!” Kenji nodded enthusiastically, “HAI!” Everyone joined in, “HAI!!!” Everyone grabbed a shot of Patron or a glass of champagne and drank. Jake tossed his shot glass over his head as he stood, “Gentlemen, why don’t you let these ladies show you how well they move on the dance floor while I talk to these two ladies in private for a moment?” Kenji, Tsutomu and Yuzo all stood up. They nodded enthusiastically as the women escorted them out of the V.I.P. Room. Jake asked his two ladies to wait while he stepped outside with Pitt for a moment. They leaned against he railing as they watched their hosts invade the dance floor. “Those guys may inadvertently hurt some people on that dance floor.” Pitt laughed as Yazo spun around and almost fell. “See what I mean?” “So, did I pass the test?” Jake stared off into the throng of people with a blank expression covering his face. Pitt elbowed him playfully, “You gave them exactly what they wanted.” “You know, there’s all sorts of people looking up at us here in the V.I.P. thinking how lucky we are. They look up here jealously at what we have, or they want to come join us.” Jake forced a smile as he spoke to cover the dread. “Of course, we both know at least one set of baby blues is looking at us in a completely different way. They’re watching us to see what we’re doing and reporting back. So how do I ignore that Pitt?” “You don’t, but you don’t let it control you. I always said the one place you can take out all of your frustration and aggression is the ring. Take all of this and just funnel it into the match.” Pitt looked at his brother, “Go take the girls back home, and I’ll keep them entertained. Forget about all this for a night.” Pitt walked away, determined to make sure the three men remembered how much fun a night out with Jake Hix could be, even if he wasn’t there. Jake returned to the room, opening the door to reveal Sarah nuzzling up to the blonde with her lips lightly grazing her neck. They both looked up at him, inviting him to join him with the smoldering gaze in their eyes. Jake watched as Sarah’s hand traced around the curve of the other woman’s breast. He didn’t even know their names. They weren’t anyone to him. They would simply be another notch on his bedpost that the watching eyes could run back and tell their masters about. He wasn’t looking for a relationship, someone to spend time with or a sympathetic shoulder. They were just flesh to his eyes and sustenance to his mouth, ready to be completely devoured and consumed. Everything else had been taken away from him, but for a brief instant he would find bliss in the oldest form. “What do you ladies say we take this back to my hotel room?” Jake’s hand reached out to each of them, and they both accepted. Each pressed their bodies into his, and he could feel the heat and softness of them beneath their thin clothes. Jake escorted them out of the club, getting the thumbs up sign from the executives who would no doubt be fulfilling their own fantasies with the women. Jake knew the truth was these were likely paid professionals there to ensure he was cared for in every way to show the businessmen’s appreciation. Professionals were the only way for him to feel safe anymore because the last great love of his life had been murdered because they knew it would hurt him. He could find distraction and salvation this evening… For a few hours… From the cloak of the throng of swaying bodies, a man watched as Jake left the club. He cut his dance with the frail feminine frame in the short skit short as he followed Hix out to the valet parking area. He remained unnoticed as he crossed to the self-park, an area a man of supposed self-importance like Jake would never look. He had a job to do, and he would not disappoint his masters. The declaration had been clear. Jake Hix was not to have any peace, and if he did it would be turned against him. He followed the limousine closely, wondering how best to handle this sudden development.
