The Past, The present, What about the Future?

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 El Serpenti stood on the deck of his ship. The sun was out and Serpenti was dressed in a pair of white loose fitting cotton pants, tan sandals and, a white mask. This mask was open at the top allowing his long black hair to fall across his back and shoulders. Bowling slightly in the noon breeze. The Ship, La Rose Azul was its name. A ship Serpenti had been working on building for a year now. Serpenti looked around at the almost finished ship, anticipation to cast off on its first voyage. Serpenti closed his eyes and relaxed as the spray of the sea hit his masked face. Once there was a time he would have felt the water on his face. Long ago before he become El Serpenti. Serpenti took a deep sigh as he sat down on some wooden crates. Slowly sipping on his drink of Mango and banana juice, Serpenti smiled at the memories of his child hood. His parents. Living on the ocean. Slowly his smile and happy memories turned to a frown as they merged and flowed into sad memories. “What be on your mind captain” Said talon Eagleclaw coming up from below deck. The two had become close friends over the recent months. “Talon me amigo, did I ever tell you about my past. And how I came to where this mask?” Serpenti asked with his eyes still closed. Talon dropped the large rolls of ropes and took a rest leaning against the main mast. “No. That’s something about you I do not know. Never thought to ask you about it to be honest.” Talon said pulling a large cup of water out of a large barrel. “Ahh.. Well let me take you back to when I was a small boy. I love the sea, I grew up living on the ocean. My mother taught me my schooling. My father was the captain of a Ship. They were treasure hunters much like my self. My father was from Spain and my Mother From Mexico. The two met as my father was on a expedition in Mexico looking for some Inca treasure in South America. I remember the joy and happiness they had together. They were so happy together, deeply in love after years.” Serpent says in a almost trance like state. “Much like you and Miss Kitty?” Talon asks causing Serpenti to snap out of his trance. “Si. I believe the lady has stolen what many have never been able to steel from this Pirate. The Kitty has me heart Amigo.” Serpenti says giving a thin happy smile. After few more sips on his juice and he begins again. “Anyways. When I was but a teen boy My mother and father left to land… they never returned from the jungles of Africa. I searched for weeks but could find no trace of them. It was as if they vanished. All I had was a promise I gave to my mother and a ship my father has owned. My mother always made me promise. If anything happened to them I would make way to Mexico. To live with my uncle.” Serpenti said with a small frown. “I thought you and your uncle are close?” Talon says confused. “We Are amigo. But I hated the thought of living on land. Not feeling the water spay across my face or the smell of the cool crisp sea air. I did not know anyone; never had I met my mother’s family. But I had nothing to fear. I arrived and was greeted with open arms. My uncle I come to find out was a warrior of the ring. A Luchador. He was El Grande Serpenti. Thus for My name when he retired. Although I missed the sea, and vowed one day to find out what happened to my mother and father, I enjoyed my time with my uncle and family. MY uncle taught me a lot. Combined with the fighting my father had taught me, I learned the art of Lucha, harnessed my inner lucha sprit. When My Uncle retired. I honored him by becoming El Serpenti De La Muerte.” Serpenti said. “I see. Very noble of you to honor your uncle by taking his name but making your own as well” Talon said with a nod. “Gracias Amigo” Serpenti said as he stood up to look at the horizon. “Did you ever find out what happened to your parents?” Talon asked. “Sadly that is a no. I was close once, but….” Serpenti said taking a deep sigh. Serpenti shook his head. “Talon. I have only truly loved only two women in my life with a passion. After the first. I never thought I would feel that way again.” Serpenti said. “Well I believe I know Kitty to be the newest flame.” Talon said with a smile. “Si. She is very intoxicating. She has sparked that feeling in me I though long dead.” Serpenti said with a smile coming across his face. “So, Who was the first?” Talon asked “The first… Carmen Santiago Better known as La Máscara Carmesí. She to was a Luchador. And we fell in love as I was training with my uncle. When I set out to go find my Parents, She came with me. Our adventures were many and we sought many a treasure from my father’s books. I was hoping to find some clue to their location. Sadly however the gold lust over took Carmon. She loved the life of a pirate more then the life of a luchador. He Lucha sprit turned dark and foul, and she began to live up to her name, She would leave a crimson trail wherever we went, unbeknownst to me. When I learned of this I confronted her. But I was too late. She has fallen in love with inflicting Pain. I should have known… When she fought in the ring.. She went that small bit further to cause un-needed pain to her opponents. It seamed as if the more pain and suffering she caused, the happier she was. It would place her in a euphoric state.” Serpenti said sadly. “I am sorry Serpenti” Talon said with a frown. “We were at sea, when I confronted her about her Sadistic Bloodlust. Reviled for her true self, She and the rest of my crew turned on me. Said I was not a real Pirate. I fought the woman I loved but the odds were against me. The crew over took me and made me walk the plank of my fathers ship. I washed ashore on a small island in the Caribbean.” Serpenti said with sadness in his eyes. Serpenti stands up and tosses his glass shattering it across the deck. “And it has taken me this long to build another ship!” Serpenti shouts his anger rising. A man dressed in a postal workers uniform walks onto the deck of the ship interrupting the conversation between the two friends. “Is there a El Serpenti onboard? “ The postal worker asks. “Si That would be me Amigo. What can I do for you?” Serpenti asks. “I have two letters for you Sir. Please sign here stating delivery has been made.” The worker says handing Serpenti an Electronic notepad. Serpenti signs his name and the Worker hands him two large envelopes. “Gracias. Have a nice day Senior” Serpenti says holding up the envelopes. The worker nods and leaves the way he came. “What is it Serpenti?” Talon asks One envelope has the PTC Logo on the front the other has a skull and cross bones. Serpenti opens the first with the PTC logo and reads out loud. “Dear El Serpenti, He have processed your application and have chosen you to compete in this years GTT 4 Tournament. Your reservation in Las Vegas has been made. Inform the Marriage hotel management you are with the PTC GTT 4 Tournament and your name and you shall be shown your room. Your opponent for round one is a man by the name of Dusk. Good luck in round one and the Tournament. PTC Director Argyle!” Serpenti exclaims. “YES!!!” Talon shouts. Serpenti smiles and the two friends give each other a high five. “I am in! This is my chance to fight some of the best in the world outside Global!” Serpenti says very happy. “And the other” Talon asks. Serpenti looks at the other envelope and opens it. Pulling out another note. As he reads it his face changes from smiling to very somber. “Looks like King Willy has sent me where to meet him to finish our trade.” Serpenti says. “Serpenti why is this map so important?” Talon asks. “It is the treasure my father and mother was looking for when they disappeared.” Serpenti says as he turns and heads to the ramp leading off the ship. “Be ready to go to Vegas when I return.” Serpenti says calling over his shoulder “WAIT!” Talon shouts stopping Serpenti. “Si Amigo?” Serpenti questions talon with a tilt of his head “What happened to Carmon? Did you ever catch up to her?” Talon asks. “Ahh.. NO. But her name has grown and has come to be feared on the sea. I am sure one day my path will cross with that sadistic bitch once more. When it does I will be ready to exact my vengeance. Now get our stuff together as I go finish this with King Willy” Serpenti says as he turns and leaves. Serpenti stands in-front of a small shop in downtown Dallas. The shops single window had various items placed on shelves. Skulls of different designs, what appeared to be shrunken heads, Jars containing items from different colored sand to frogs. Two very large wood carved statues of strange looking spearmen with masks sate on each side of the door. The sign above the door read Mystic Domain. Serpenti looks down at the paper and nodded to his self. This was the correct address the men who worked for King Willy. The Jamaican Voodoo high priest who had part of a map Serpenti needed. Not to mention leader of his own cult and crime syndicate. Serpenti pulls out the small box containing the ancient Indian talisman that housed the sprit of a long dead Indian warrior. Serpenti quickly places the box back under his trench coat and opened the door. As he enters the hairs on the back of his neck rise, and he defiantly has the feeling of being watched. With a deep breath to calm his nerves and senses he steps into the smoke laden shop. The recognizable smell of Marijuana, mixed with the scents of at least a dozen different incense fragrances. As the door closes the sound of a bell chime lets the proprietor of this establishment know someone has entered. The room Serpenti stands in is filled with barrels holding bamboo stalks, to wooden canes to, rolled floor rugs, crates and wooden cages filled the rest. In the cages were Birds of different species, to Rabbits, and a few Chickens here and there. A shelf to his right held candles of all shapes, sizes, and colors. “What can I do for you” The fractured voice of an old woman said from behind a mountain of books and crates. Slowly a round and short old woman waddles into view. Her eyes a milky white look right at Serpenti. “Well are you going to stand there in silence, or are you going to speak up? “ The old woman asks in a demanding voice. “I am sorry my lady, I was just caught up in the wears of your store. Interesting stuff.” Serpenti says apologetically. “No you were not. Looking at mine eyes you were. I see you even though you may think not. I know why you have come here Snake man.” The old woman said in her fractured voice. Cackling to her self as she waddled right up to Serpenti. “You come to see the king. He waits for you in the basement Snake Man.” The old woman says with a smile showing her missing toothed mouth. The old woman looks Serpenti in his eyes with a intensity that makes Serpenti have to pull away. Feeling as if the woman had been looking into his very mind. Serpenti shakes his head trying to clear his thoughts. “Where is the entrance to the basement? “ Serpenti asks as he rubs his eyes. The old woman raised a pudgy arm and pointed to a red and gold beaded curtain. “Gracias.” Serpenti says and heads to the red and gold beaded curtain. The old woman smiles and waddles back to her mountain of books and crates, once again. sorting and placing things into different piles. Serpenti steps though the beaded curtain. A lone torch lights up part of the darkened stairway. Past the glow of the torch light the place is an unnatural pitch black. Serpenti picks up the torch and heads down the stairs. Silently thinking he would have rather meet him in the ally way again. Serpenti walks for what seams a endless amount of time. “How far under the city am I” Serpenti wonders to his self. Soon Serpenti stands in front of a large iron door. No handles or knobs to be seen to open the door with. A single empty torch receptacle is to the left of the door. Serpenti shrugs and places the torch into the receptacle. As he does the sound of grating mettle on stone fills the air as the large door cracks in half and opens inward. Serpenti steps into a large cavern. A shimmering waterfall splashes down into a large glittering pool before heading off in a small river. Serpenti looks around to find the source of the light. Crystals cover the ceiling, glowing inwardly and giving off there light. “Beautiful is it not? “ Comes the raspy voice of King Willy, giving Serpenti a start. Serpenti looks to his right and swore he was alone not just a scant for moment’s prior. Where did he come from? Serpenti shudders. “Magic” Serpenti thinks to his self “Si, It is a beautiful place.” Serpenti responds. “Strange how such a place of beauty can be right under the filth that is above.” King Willy muses. “Si senior Willy. But I am sure you did not ask me here to show me this beautiful place.” Serpenti says turning to face King Willy. “Ahh, strait to the point. Very well Snake Man. You are correct. I know you have the Talisman I have been wanting. You completed a task many had failed to do. Therefore I have the piece of the map you seek.” King Willy says with a hiss. “At last!” Serpenti thinks to his self. “Now I only needed one more piece.” The two men traded items with satisfaction. “That piece has been handed down for generations Snake man. I now must give to you the clue to the next piece. Seek the one who would be king, but is denied. Seek the wielder of the king of swords. For it is not in this world that the last piece lays waiting and resting.” King Willy recites with his eyes close. “Gracias your majesty.” Serpenti says with a bow. King Willy responds with a nod of his head. “I hope you find what you find that which you seek the most Snake Man.” King Willy says shaking Serpenti’s hand. “Gracias you Majesty.” Serpenti says and turns and walks back out of the door. King Willy watches as the torchlight vanishes up the staircase. Out from behind a rock outcropping steps a female in a red mask and a red leather body suit. “The deed is done.” King Willy says. “Good. Come my little Serpent. Time to play once more. And you will suffer for turning on me!” The Masked woman says
