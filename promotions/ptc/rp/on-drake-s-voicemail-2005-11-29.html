On Drake's Voicemail

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 "I've dislocated my left shoulder twice, I've had stitches in three places on my head and broken my nose once. I've got two fake teeth and seven crowns, my jaw has been broken and wired together. My neck and back have both been broken and the discs fused, my right knee has been fractured and I have an ingrown nail on my left big toe. I tend to put myself out for a big move, and I'm a little sloppy with my left. Took an enzuguiri kick to the ear last year and haven't heard the same out of it again. Two broken ribs, three concussions, whip lash twice, pulled a hamstring once and broke my pinky on Toxic's forehead last month. When I win Drake, I don't want you saying you were surprised, screwed, uninformed, or underestimating. You only get one round to impress the hell out of these people, so deal with your shit and bring your best. I don't need to know who you are or how you fight, I walk into this match same as every other. This could be my last, and you could be the best. One hundred and twenty seven other people want the same thing I need, Drake. And right now the only one that matters is you."
