Your Opinion Here

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 Opinions. One of the words that while everyone wants to deny ever having one on certain topics, it is unfortunate that everyone does have one. It doesn’t matter what the topic is. It can be political. It can be in sports. It can be in the weather, or it can just be some other random topic that people talk about. Everyone has an opinion in one way or another. And it is that opinion that people express, out and out, through and through. Some people don’t understand that the opinion is something that should be kept to them. Some people don’t understand that an opinion that not everyone wants to hear. An opinion can be your best friend, and also be your worst enemy. It all depends on how you use it, and for what reason, but it is your opinion, and that opinion should be respected by everyone, no matter whom. Opinions are what wars are started on. Opinions are what relationships are made of. Opinions are everywhere. You can run, you can hide, but no matter what you do, you will never get away from opinions. (The scene opens up with “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee standing along side of a news stand. He is wearing a UWF shirt, as well as the hat. He is examining the different magazines and newspapers at the stand in front of him. Those are setting there in front of him. The news stares back at him, but Kazee continues to look through the different headlines that are there. It is a beautiful day in this bustling metropolitan of whatever name you should choose it to be.) Kazee: This world is filled with one thing, and that one thing is what people think about one thing or the other. Whether it is wrong or right. Whether they have proof to back it up or not. Everyone has something they believe in. Even though what they believe in causes so much trouble in the world. I mean, look at some of these headlines……. (The camera looks down at one of the papers in Kazee’s hands. The headline reads “POWELL CLAIMS GENOCIDE, SUDAN CLAIMS FOUL.”) Here is a clear cut case of what I am talking about. What we have here is a situation in which the Sudanese are having an internal battle amongst themselves. If anyone has been following it, there are several opinions as to what is behind it. According to Powell, it’s genocide on a group of people. Others say it is religions. Sudan claims that everything is under control and it is an internal affair……. Truth of the matter is is that a group of nomads are trying to get areas for their livestock to graze and drink. They are imposing on traditional farmer lands. The Sudanese government basically gave the nomads arms to drive the farmers off their lands. Of course, once again, this is my opinion, but it’s got more facts than the others do. (Kazee picks up another newspaper. This time the headline reads “PUTIN CALLS US TERRORIST.”) Is the United States really a terrorist organization? Well, at least in Putin’s opinion they are. Sure, he can try and make links. He has done a good job of it. The US on the other hand is calling on Putin to launch and investigation into what happened at the school in Chechnya as far as the over run and the killing of the children. They want stricter laws and regulations for the……. Once again, my opinion, and probably closer to the truth is that everyone has problems with terrorism. Every country has probably even had relationships with terrorist at one time or another. And every country has problem with security. I mean are we really naïve to believe that every country doesn’t have problems with Security? If we didn’t, then would September 11 have taken place or Oklahoma City either? Highly doubtful, but someone would have you believe otherwise. But opinions don’t stop in politics. I mean lets look at sports shall we? Everyone has a different opinion in different sports. (Kazee picks up a sporting magazine and flips through the different section. He comes to a section entitled “Preseason Football Predictions.”) Look here for example. One person has the Eagles and the Colts in the Super bowl, another the Pats and the Packers. And yet another that I actually have to question has the Vikings and the Chiefs in there. Obviously, everyone think different here. Everyone think that different teams are going to win…… The question is who is going to win. Who is going to make it to the big dance? Everyone thinks they know. Everyone thinks they have it all worked out. The X’s and O’s don’t even matter to them. They have it all right there in their head and they know exactly what is going to happen……. But we won’t know till January. We won’t know until the game is here. Whether it is the Patriots and the Eagles or it be the Lions and the Bengals, nobody can guess with one hundred percent accuracy as to who is going to make it to the Super Bowl. After all, that is why they play the game, isn’t it? And even the world of professional wrestling has its opinionated people. Even the world which so many consider being fake, which in and of itself is an opinion. Some of those magazines are so freaking stupid in their opinions. Even though I don’t like calling anyone’s opinion stupid, look at this one right here. (Kazee picks up a magazine. On the cover there is a tag line for an interview with the Karicatures of Kazee. Kazee starts flipping through the pages till he comes to the page of which it is on. On the page, there are three pictures. Fizbane the Fabulous, Mr. Meaner, and “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee are all right there. The headline reads “Will Three Ever Become One Again?”) I don’t know why people don’t understand that I am not Mr. Meaner. I don’t know why people don’t understand that I am not Fizbane the Fabulous. I don’t know why everyone thinks we are one and the same. See, this is me. I am “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. I take things seriously. And I believe in simple things….. Mr. Meaner is a total jack ass. Not even close to who or what I am. When will people ever see that I could never be Mr. Meaner, or at least this incarnation of him? Whoever this guy is is stealing my old gimmick. Yeah, he’s been successful, but he is not me…… And Fizbane the Fabulous. A party animal. A man who believes more about dancing and having fun then studying and training. I don’t think this guy has ever taken a day serious in his life. And hell, how could this guy be me. I hate to do all those acrobatics that he does. Not even close to me…… But here is something that oughtta be amusing in this article. An association with all three of them responding... How about I read some of these to you. (Kazee folds the paper back and starts to read it out loud for anyone who is seemingly listening.) Topic: “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee Kazee: Sportsman Meaner: Goody Two Shoes Fizbane: Square Topic: Mr. Meaner Kazee: Jack ass Meaner: The Best ever Fizbane: Meaner than a junkyard dog, badder than ol’ King Kong Topic: Fizbane the Fabulous Kazee: Nice guy, needs some focus Meaner: Hot woman, nerdy guy Fizbane: Party Animal ALL…. NIGHT…. LONG!!!! Topic: Patti Anderson Kazee: Love to meet her personally and professionally Meaner: Nothing more than a slut. Fizbane: Anyone say Fizzy Express (Kazee puts the magazine down and just stares at it. He starts shaking his head.) Kazee: How could anyone believe that all three of them are me? I mean, some people buy into this conspiracy theory and all that. And it gets totally ridiculous. If all three of them were me, there is no way I would’ve even been able to even think after the Regal Rumble, let alone compete the next week against The Communist….. But I do have one more thing I’d like to look in that magazine for. I see they have GTT4 prediction. Let’s see what they think about my match. (Kazee flips through the magazine again and start reading it. He has a rather surprised look on his face, but accepting.) Here is a little shocker. They have me and Mr. Draven pretty even on the prediction. Who would’ve thought? I’ve been so use in my life to being the underdog that being in a position where people expect me to win is unfamiliar ground for me. Being picked so evenly with my opponents is something that I’m not use to…… Well, Mr. Draven, it’s a match where neither of us knows each other very well, other than what we’ve seen on tape or in person, whatever the case may be. The so called experts are of the opinion that we are pretty even down the middle. They don’t see either of us having a clear cut advantage and are of the opinion this will be an even match……. I’m of the opinion that I’m against a great athlete in this match. I’m of the opinion that this match is going to truly test me in several ways. I’m of the opinion that I am going to, as always, give 110% and no matter what, I am going to do what needs to be done. The fans are going to get a treat, I have a feeling. And the fans are of the opinion, I can state with all certainty, that wherever I am, they will see a great match….. The GTT4 is looking for a blockbuster in the first round. They’ve got several. But here’s an opinion for you all. Here is a sleeper match for you all. “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee vs. Nathanial Draven is your sleeper match for the first round. And some experts even agree with my opinion. In the end, all the opinions, they won’t matter, for the better man will come out on top. And whoever that may be will be making a big impact on the GTT4…… Good Luck, Mr. Draven. Good Luck. (Kazee takes a $20 bill out of his wallet, grabs a few wrestling magazines and walks away to read some of the other so-called expert’s opinions as the scene fades to black.)
