This Gift Sucks [IG-70 Jadian Bridden]

12 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jadian Bridden stood in front of the magazine rack, thumbing through the latest edition of Lowrider magazine and admiring more than the extravagant paint jobs. Apparently the racks that hold the hydraulic pumps and extra batteries aren’t the only ones they emphasize in the magazine, because the sexy latina with straight black hair looks like she shopped for a bra in the juniors section. While you won’t be seeing the two time LiveWire champion showing up to Temptation hitting a rolling three wheel, the man has been a car enthusiast since he was knee high to a grasshopper. He can still remember waking up at four in the morning to make sure everything was loaded into the back of his father’s Chevy truck, then driving over and parking in line behind twenty to thirty candied and flaked out classics, the majority of them being Impalas and G Body style vehicles. Like the car enthusiasts, Jay took pride in the fact that he had done most of the work on the 1967 Schwinn frame he had found at a garage sale for fifty dollars. There’s just something about accomplishing things that has always been a goal of Jadian’s, and he has continued that thought process in Sin City Championship Wrestling. Having held the LiveWire title on two occasions, and now having the opportunity to wrestle the Gateway title from the grasp of Sin City’s very own Napoleon Dynamite, Jadian doesn’t plan on stopping anytime soon. “What are you looking at,” Julissa Minorez asked when she walked up behind him with a large bottle of Fiji water in her hand. The Mexican Minx took a glance over his shoulder, her eyes focused on the huge set of breasts that look as if they were photographed in 3-D. “Now she looks good enough to eat.” Jadian cracked a smile, and if you listened close enough you would have heard a slight chuckle. “Of course,” Julissa continued before Jadian could say anything. “It would probably cost you more than a Big Mac at Mickey D’s for her to let you do it.” Now that made Jay laugh, and as he closed the magazine and placed it back on to the shelf, he allowed himself to enjoy the moment. He shoved his hands into the pockets of his oversized Dallas Cowboys jacket, something he picked up when they got back to San Antonio and realized it was freaking cold. It wasn’t that cold when he visited America’s hat, at least he didn’t think so, but it could have been that he was expecting it to be a bit cool. “So are you saying you’re a cheap lay, or just that you know me well enough not to expect me to pay thirty dollars for a piece of meat and a baked potato?” “I’ll take ‘How Cheap is Jadian’ for two hundred, Alex.” she answered with a smirk. She too was bundled up for a change, though she chose to be stylish about it with a full length leather coat that reaches about mid-calf. “Laugh it up,” Jadian replied. “You’re still the one who put out for a cheeseburger and a small order of fries.” “Yeah, yeah,” Juli rolled her eyes. “I’m pretty sure you know by now that I would’ve slept with you anyway.” “I know,” Jadian said with a chuckle. “But I like bringing it up.” “Jerk.” “Yes ma’am.” “I got us a Malibu,” Julissa tossed Jay a set of keys. “I still don’t know why we’re not just picking up your car from the parking lot.” “Because as much as I keep telling you that following Lane down this road is the wrong thing to do, you have your mind set on not listening to me.” “You’re worried about nothing,” Jay scoffed at the idea of being afraid of Pierce. “She’s probably too busy tickling Amy’s clit with her nose to be trying to kill us.” “And that’s exactly why we’re not driving my car,” Julissa shook her head. “You keep focusing on what she’s doing with Aimz, completely forgetting what this woman is capable of doing to people that betray her.” “You still don’t get it do you?” Jadian stretched his arms out and spun around in a circle very slowly. “You’re free, Juli.” The original LiveWire champion blinked, then folded her arms across her chest. Abover her chest would also work here, since you could rest a case of beer on her rack. “Enjoy the freedom of not having to answer to a woman that people have been saying isn’t thinking with her head, because it is a rare gift that not many people have had the opportunity of feeling.” How could she make him understand that Alexandra Pierce is not a woman that takes kindly to people turning on her. Granted, she is the one who actually made the decision to turn her back on what they had worked so hard to achieve. Much like Jay, that isn’t the way she sees what happened in Ottawa. Desade may have acted out of character when she delivered the Spider’s Kiss to Lane Stevens, but there is no denying the fact that Pierce is very dangerous. The decision to follow Desade or Lane Stevens isn’t one to be taken lightly, especially when you take into account the fact that both have the capability of causing far more than bodily harm to someone that chose to oppose them. There really wasn’t a right or wrong answer to this dilemma, so maybe she should take Jadian’s advice and just enjoy this gift…Even if she didn’t feel free.
