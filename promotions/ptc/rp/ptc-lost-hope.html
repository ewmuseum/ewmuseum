Lost Hope The thunder outside of his hotel room crackled with such ferocity that it stirred Craig from the half sleep that he was in. His soft red eyes blinked feverishly as he stared at the beige ceiling above him. Part of him wished that he had died in his sleep and another part of him toyed with the idea of just killing himself right there and then. Yet, he couldn’t bring himself to do it, no matter how hard he tried. Each time he had tried over the past week, he had failed miserably. Normally, a person who accomplished everything he put his mind to, Craig was having difficulty in ending his own life. A life that he felt wasn’t worth living anymore. A life he had forfeited. The only problem was that life hadn’t given up on him. He slowly rolled out of the bed he was sleeping in and looked at the rain which was peppering the night. His eyes burned from the lack of sleep and even though his body told him to rest, Craig fought the urge and got up only to walk to the window. Below him, the city was very much alive. Yet, how could a city be so alive when one of its citizens was feeling the life being sucked out of him. No longer did he feel like he had a purpose to live. Everything he knew had been a damn lie. A fib. A fable. What he thought he knew were all a web of lies that he had been told for another person’s ambitions. He had been toyed with because of information he once had, information that he couldn’t get back even if he tried his hardest. In all reality, it was information that Craig wanted no part of. Craig turned from the window and walked through his lavish hotel room that he had been staying in for the past two weeks. His feet softly echoed throughout the room as he entered into his dark bathroom and he turned on the sink faucet. The warm water splashed against the porcelain and all Craig could do was look at it. For so long, he had been lost in his thoughts, trying to find out who he was. Now, he was lost in his thoughts for a completely different reason. The thoughts he once had were shattered and just illusions created for him. Now, he didn’t know what to think. He didn’t know what to do. Everything he held to be true was gone and there was no one left for him in this world. He had gotten a fragment of his memories back and he foolishly wished that he could give them back. If he had a choice to return to the fictional world he had been in just two weeks ago, regardless of how bad it had been before, or have his memories back, he would without question return his memories for that feeling of life rushing through his veins. It had been two weeks when he stood inside of a temple in Monte Visto and had been given some of his memories back. He had been given back a fragment of his memories because of the life he had taken. The life of Wayne Arthur Silsbee. Little did Craig know that it was at his own doing that he had received those memories back. It was part of some cruel game that Craig had created for himself years past, or so he had been told. At one time, he had created a place where he could store his memories. Yet, it had been destroyed, courtesy of the same woman who had destroyed his life. It was then that Craig knew he had to safeguard his memories and if he ever lost them, he would have to suffer because of it. The death of Silsbee had been one of his tests and brought back violent images and memories of the life he once had. The memories he had retrieved were those of the woman he thought of as his wife. Jocelyn. It was in these memories that he found out what Jocelyn had truly done over the years to him instead of the memories she tried to implant in his brain. She had killed his true wife, his friends, and most importantly, his daughter. The daughter he thought was alive. All hope had vanquished and was replaced with pure anger and rage. That was why he had to kill her. He went to her hotel room and killed her. Violently. The blood stained his hands, but he didn’t care then and didn’t care now. Except for the fact that he wished he could be living with those same blinders on and filled with ignorance. His life had been horrible before and he knew it hadn’t gotten any better, nor would it get any better in the future. With nothing to live for, he had tried bitterly to kill himself, but each attempt failed. Drugs, alcohol, slashing his wrists, nothing had worked for him. He thought it was some cruel joke being played on him by life. The past four months had been horrible, he thought to himself. Coming back to the world with no memories, being stabbed, being framed for the murder of a woman he barely knew, and having to search for his memories. But, this had been worse. The past two weeks had been a trip to the darkest depths of hell. He just wished it was all over. Then, as he stared at the flowing water, a blinding pain hit him right between his eyes. He stumbled backwards as all he could see was white in front of him, as if someone had locked him inside of a room painted in all white. He grabbed at his face, trying to find the source of the pain, but it was no use. The pain was winning fast and he thought that this was his ticket out. As he felt his consciousness starting to leave him, he embraced it. Unfortunately, it wasn’t death. It was much worse. --- September 1, 1997 Children’s Hospital Los Angeles “Craig…,” a voice echoed from behind him with such passion it caused Craig to shudder. Outside, the sun was out and bright, but yet the wind howled loudly. Today was going to be the best day of his life and he could feel that happiness in his heart. As he turned around, he saw his wife lying in the white hospital bed. He was amazed by her beauty even though he saw her everyday. Her long, blonde hair was tied up in a ponytail and you could see the pain etched her in face as the sweat beads raced down her cheeks. Twenty hours she had been in labor and she had been amazingly strong. He walked over to her and held her hand as she smiled back at him. He loved her brown eyes and saw the strength and love in them that had made him fall in love with her. “I’m here, Sam, trust me, I’m here for you,” he told her softly, wanting to give her his strength. More than anything, he wanted to take her place and let him take the pain while she stood there, supporting him. He hated to see her in any kind of pain and agony, and he knew this was the worse and the best kind all wrapped up into one. As he glanced to the entryway, he kept seeing nurses and doctors go back and forth in the hall outside of their room. He looked back at her and smiled at her once again, trying to keep her calm. It had been a little while since he had seen a doctor checking on his wife, but he kept his fears buried, knowing that she had the same fears. Then, his wife started to groan and her eyes immediately closed without as much as a warning. Her breathing quickly turned shallow and he knew something was wrong. His eyes suddenly darted to the doorway, hoping to find someone coming in to check on his wife, but nobody was there. He gripped her hand harder, thinking it was just labor pains, and just lent her his strength to help cope with the pain she was going through. While holding her hand and just getting in rhythm with her, he could tell that her breathing was becoming irregular. Her eyes slowly opened and she looked at him with so much worry in her eyes that it scared him. “Can you get a doctor in here?” she asked of him, almost hesitant to even ask that as if she knew something was so drastically wrong that she was scared of the outcome. He just simply nodded his head as he rushed over to the door to see if he could get some help. After twenty hours of being labor, he thought that they would’ve done something to help induce the labor, but they had stalled for as long as they could have hoped. As he walked to the door, a nurse noticed him and walked over to him. “Everything okay, Mr. Maloof?” the nurse asked. Craig immediately knew something was wrong by the tone in her voice. “Where’s the doctor? What is wrong with my wife? She’s in there in a considerable amount of pain!” he told her with urgency in his voice and for a brief second, he could see some fear on the woman’s face. Instantly, he could feel his body take over as he knew something was wrong. She didn’t say anything as she walked away and tapped a button on the wall. She then paged their doctor and moments later, the doctor was walking up towards him. Before he reached him though, he paused to talk to the nurse. After they were done talking, she walked into the room to check on his wife. “Mr. Maloof, mind if I talk to you?” he asked of Craig and the two walked a few feet away from his room. “What’s wrong, doctor?” Craig asked, rather concerned at this point. “We’re having some difficulty locating the baby in the ultrasounds. We’ve been looking at them and running blood tests, but not seeing anything to this point. We’re concern--,” the doctor began to say, but he was quickly interrupted by a piercing scream, one that was coming from his wife. Craig quickly turned around and raced for his room where he saw a mixture of pain and agony on his wife’s face. The doctor rushed in right behind him and so did several nurses. Samantha was sitting up in her bed and looked to be breathing hard as she was unsure of what the problem was. “Oh God, it HURTS!” she yelled out and she looked at Craig, looking for him to give him a reason. Yet, he was clueless, and all he could do was grab her left hand, letting her know that he was there for her no matter what happened. After a moment though, she was silent and as he looked at her, he noticed that she had passed out from the pain. Fear struck him and he was becoming defensive by the second, wanting to protect his wife at all costs. He looked at the doctors who were checking her out. “What happened to her? What the hell is wrong with her?! Do something!” he yelled at them, but there was only silence in the room as the doctor checked her out to see what happened. Meanwhile, a nurse was examining her uterus, hoping to find something. It was when she recoiled out of fear that he knew something horrible was wrong. The doctor looked around and noticed blood on the nurse’s hands. Samantha was bleeding out of her uterus. “Get OR one ready, we’re coming up right away. Let them know,” he said with such urgency that Craig didn’t like it one bit. They started to unhook Samantha from the various tools they had hooked her up to and started to wheel her out of the room. He started to follow her, but the doctor tried to grab him. He was unsuccessful though as he ripped himself away from the doctor’s grip and followed them. “Mr. Maloof!” the doctor yelled out as Craig raced down the hall with his wife, not caring one bit if he wasn’t allowed inside of the operating room. He wanted to be with her. He needed to be with her. Security guards started to approach him as they reached the elevator, but he just pushed them out of the way. He was determined to be with her. “Stay away from me! THAT IS MY WIFE!” he yelled at everyone! People not even involved with the case were looking at him with fear, but he didn’t care in the least bit. More security guards started to approach him, needing him to calm down, but he just ignored them even as they grabbed at him and tried to pull him away. Yet, he was strong and resolute about his desire to be by his wife’s side. As the security guards pulled him away, he tried his best to fight them, but deep in his mind he knew that he couldn’t be there. Her life was in their hands, no matter how much he didn’t like it. There was nothing he could do at this point. Craig walked away from the security guards as a nurse walked up to him with the doctor by her side. He looked at them with a glare that he wanted to strangle the life out of them. Yet, he restrained himself as he paced back and forth, wondering why the hell they were right before him instead of trying to save his wife. “You need to let us do our job, Craig. Your wife is very ill right now and things, well, I’m trying to be optimistic, but they don’t look good at all. But, we’re going to try our best, we promise you that,” the doctor told him before he walked away and headed up another elevator that was waiting on him. Craig just stood there for a moment before the nurse took him to the waiting room. With each step he took, he felt the anger building in his chest. He felt hopeless and he didn’t like the feeling one bit. As he entered the waiting room, the pain in his heart exploded. “No… no! I need to be with my wife! Don’t you people fucking understand that?!” he yelled so loud at the nurse that everyone looked at him uneasily. He was a time bomb just waiting to explode, but he didn’t care. The way Craig felt, helpless and scared, was something that he hadn’t experienced in his life, and he didn’t like the feeling at all. The nurse pointed to a chair for him to sit in while he waited, no longer being tolerant of his abusive style. Craig looked at the chair and contemplated throwing it at her, but he fought with all of his strength to calm himself down. He knew that Sam needed him, but she didn’t need him acting in this way. The minutes ticked away slowly and he felt the agony as he watched the clock. His right foot kept tapping as he was rather impatient with the waiting. Every now and then, he had to resist the urge at leaving the waiting room, fighting whatever security guards were between him and his wife, and find out what was going on with her. Yet, the only the thing keeping him here was that he knew Sam needed him to be strong, but he couldn’t possibly be strong when she was upstairs somewhere possibly dying. Slowly, he found his head buried into his hands, praying that she would be alright. He wasn’t a religious man, but in this moment in time, he would do anything it took to save his wife. Hours passed as Craig waited on an update on his wife. In the waiting room, he could see people looking worried about their own loved ones. Yet, Craig could care less about any of them. Then, he heard the door opening and as he looked up, he saw it was his doctor being followed by a nurse. In her arms was a small, beautiful baby. His baby. Craig could only stand up and look at her as they brought her to him. They placed him in his arms and he looked down, he felt like all was right in the world. He had a child, but then the reality struck him. He looked up at the doctor, wanting, no, needing an update on his wife. The doctor just smiled at him, calming Craig instantly. “She’s okay. She’s in recovery and you’ll be able to see her soon. Your wife, she has amazing strength. The damage done to her, no other person would’ve survived. We’ll talk about it some more, but for now, we wanted you to see your daughter,” he told Craig and joy washed over him as he knew all was right. His family was still intact and they were stronger for it. He knew that Sam would be able to fight whatever had happened and as he looked at his daughter, he knew that she would inherit her same traits. “Hey Sarah, I’m your daddy,” he said, glee in his voice. He was a father and he couldn’t be prouder. --- His eyes fluttered opened with any warning at all. As Craig’s eyes scanned the room, he felt like he had been hit by a train once, then it stopped, reversed, and hit him again. His mind was pounding with an intense pain that he had felt before. The last time he had received some of his memories, the mere pain from them inserting themselves into his memory was enough to almost kill him. This time, the pain was much worse and he feared what would happen if other memories started coming back him without a moment’s notice. What made it even worse though was that it was of his daughter. He wasn’t sure though why the memories were coming back. It simply baffled him. As he pulled himself up though, he knew he couldn’t go through it again. His heart ached in such a way that he wished he was already dead. Instead, he had to look himself in the face and realize how much he had failed. He knew there was nothing he could do to save those that he loved. He just wanted a way out at this point. Craig couldn’t deal with having such a heavy heart for fear it would shatter into a million more pieces then it already had. With the water still running, he looked at himself in the mirror, and heard the birds chirping. For others, the world was a place they loved to be in. For him, it was hell. He wanted an exit from this hell, and he only knew one way to do it. He had to end his life. To Be Continued [No, seriously, it'll be continued and finished in the Extreme Title match RP. :)]
