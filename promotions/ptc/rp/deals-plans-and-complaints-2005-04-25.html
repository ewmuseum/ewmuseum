Deals, plans, and complaints

25 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-25 Things don't get much more complicated in a week’s time, then they did for me last week. I went from being on a personal high, having won my first match in the CW 2 tournament, to just a couple of short days later being on one of the worst lows of my entire life. You'd think that having moved on in a tournament filled with so much talent, that even the great Seymour Almasy was unable to advance, that there would have been little that could have brought me down. Senor, like myself, gringo would have been dead wrong. Instead of being able to celebrate my huge victory on PRIME's weekly event, ReVolution, I was forced to not only wrestle a second match, in just a couple of days, but I was forced to take that match against the Satanic monster himself…Hessian. Is that anyway to treat the man who is trying to do PRIME proud and win a tournament as prestigious as CW 2? I should say it wouldn't be, essa, and yet that is exactly what I found myself doing. Here I am a man of barely six feet in height and just over the two hundred pound mark and Hin See finds that it is fitting for me to face off against his self proclaimed "plan" to destroy Vampir and the Dark Age. Where is the logic, or for that matter, justice in that? Don't strain to hard trying to think about it gringo's cause the answer is simple… There is none. No reason at all that I should have been used as the quote, lamb lead to the slaughter. Senor Hin See could have chosen anyone he liked to face off against Hessian. Baltasar would have been more than happy to get in the ring and take out the legend that is Hessian. Instead Hin See decided to give me what he was calling a "warm up" match for my further involvement in the tournament. BULLSHIT! Why don't he just take a knife, stab it directly into the middle of my back, severe my spine and save me the trouble? Warm up match, my well tanned Mexican ass! I am not a fool. I can see right through the racism and can easily read between the lines. That match was nothing more than an attempt to take me out. To keep me from being able to compete against Cereal Killer in the second round, and thus forcing me to forfeit my spot in the tournament. In response to that I have a very clear answer for Senor Hin See. Sópleme ramera! I will not take this kind of travesty lying down, I can promise that racist swine that much. Of course that wasn't the end of my wonderful week, and I use the word wonderful in the most insincere form I possibly can. See my problems of course couldn't just end there. No… Having the big, twisted, disgruntled freak of nature Hessian kick my ass all over the ring wasn’t enough for the current management of PRIME. They had to let their jealousy and hatred boil over even further. Los Diablos, the greatest tag team of all time, should right now be PRIME tag team champions. Once more they were cheated out of their God given right to hold those precious titles by a cowardly act of one Angelo Deville. I know what your thinking…he paid the ref off to count out the wrong man, once more, just like both Karina and him have already done before. But this time, my gringo fanatics, you'd be wrong once more. This time he went to an even lower level. He pulled out even a tactic that I would have never believed him cowardly enough to pull. He had Los Diablos deported back to Mexico, on BOGUS charges! Yes, I know gringo, calm down and take a few deep breaths. I feel your pain just as much as you do at this point. But being angry about it will get you no where other than the hospital with high blood pressure, and I urge you not to give Deville the pleasure of such things. Deported. Removed from the country of the United States where they have absolutely every right to be, just like that yellow shit himself, Deville. All this in order to not have to defend the titles, which aren't even rightfully his in the first place, against the true, tag team champions of PRIME…Los Diablos. Fear not my Diablos fanatics there will come a day when Deville regrets the fact that his mother kept him at birth, instead of doing him a favor and drowning him. For now this isn't the time or place to worry about such matters. Right now is the time for you all to focus on my winning in the second round of the tournament and continuing the long and proud tradition of Mexican wrestlers everywhere. Ariba!!!! <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> The light through the cafe window was shining brightly onto the skin of Enrique Morales. His naturally brown skin taking in the calming warmth of the bright sun that could almost always be found high in the San Antonio skyline. As he sat in his booth, waiting on the arrival of Jacob Cross, he flipped through a wrestling magazine. Stopping every now and again to raise his head just enough to see over the top of the magazine, and examine whichever patron had caught his attention. "Damn gringo. He is always late." Morales muttered to himself as he flipped further into the contents of the magazine. The magazine what the official one of PRIME, the first issue no less, and Morales was just now getting the chance to look it over. As his eyes darted back and forth across the pages, quickly scanning the thoughts of those who wrote for the magazine, he was unable to notice that the man he was waiting for had finally arrived. "Since I said ten o'clock, and I'm here at ten o'clock, I would hardly say that is late." The sound of Jacob's voice was able to startle Enrique as he quickly spun around in his seat to face his newfound associate. The eyes of Senor Morales simply became slim slits, as he convied his displeasure with Cross through a leering glare. "Where I come from Senor Cross, when you someone asks you to be some where, you show them some respect and get there early, specially when it concerns business. I'd think a man like yourself, Cross, would understand that." Morales shot toward Cross as the man that helped build PRIME in its early days, sat down on the seat across the table from Morales. Cross simply smirked as he ran a hand over his neatly trimmed, black goatee. "Yes. Well in your country they also believe that being poor is an honor. I take what I know from Mexico with a grain of salt." The retort caused Morales to shake his head with anger and his naturally tanned face to turn a light shade of red. Cross simply sat back in his seat, folding his arms over his chest, allowing them to rest against the fabric of his classy dress shirt, made of silk. “Senor Cross,” Morales began before he was cut off by Cross, who placed a hand in the air, and shook his head. "Please. The formality stage is long over Enrique. You may call me God if you like, but if not then you can simply refer to me as Jacob." Another devious smile formed over the lips of Mr. Cross. "If Hoyt heard you speak in such a way we'd be picking you up off the concrete right now." Morales replied with a deep sigh. "Yes. As absolutely devastating as that sounds, Hoyt is of no issue at this moment. We're not here to throw names of people back and forth at one another. It's time to talk serious business, Enrique." "Good Senor…I mean Jacob. I have been thinking a great deal about your offer and I am prepared to listen to what you have to say. But first I must know whats in it for you?" The question caused Enrique to give Jacob a concerned look. It was one thing to trust a gringo, something that Enrique rarely did anyway, but it was a totally different subject to trust one that could be as dirty and manipulative as Mr. Cross had proven to be in his career. "Enrique, why you cut me to the quick! Can’t a concerned American reach out and help his fellow man when he can clearly see there is great injustice being thrown in their way?” Cross questioned with a look of absolute shock on his face, just barely enough to hide the arrogant grin behind it. "Not when that American gringo is you. Trusting that your doing this out of your kindness would be as stupid as believing that Deville is a sincere man when he says things aren't personal. Only a fool would believe it, and I'm no fool." By now Enrique was more or less tired of the games. He knew that Jacob wanted something and he knew that he wasn't going to go any further with this deal until he knew what it was. “You are wise beyond your years JB, ” the mocking tone of the nickname that Cross referred to caused Enrique to shutter, “and here is the deal. You see I have a little issue I need to get around in PRIME in order to get back into the company and your going to be the man to fix that for me." "Issue? What sort of issue would that be Senor?” The look in Enrique's eyes conveyed that he was more than interested in this subject. "Well, you see, the good man that I am when I left PRIME I did a big favor for a friend of mine. You might have heard of him, and who he used to be isn’t important, but I lost this silly Loser Leaves PRIME forever thing in order to help him out on my way out of the company. I was leaving anyway, and honestly at the time had no intent to return, so I did him and essentially PRIME, a favor by allowing him to beat me in such a match." Cross explained with a twinkle in his eye. "I don't see how this pertains to me gringo. It's not my fault that you didn't look further into the future than a couple of weeks. What can I do that will erase the fact that you so stupidly put your PRIME career on the line for another, who might I add, probably would never have done the same for you?" Enrique questioned, his gaze, never leaving the bright blue stare of Jacob Cross. "Maybe he wouldn't have. That isn't the issue here, essa, as my getting back into PRIME is. How you can help is very simple. In order to get my foot back in the door, all I need to do is become an associate, or an agent as it were, to one of the current members of the company. That associate, my friend, is going to be you and your tag team Los Diablos." "HA!" Enrique laughed out loud as he slammed a fist down on the table and just shook his head, "And just what the hell makes you think I want to do that, gringo? Why in the world does Los Diablos, or myself for that matter, need you to be our agent?" "You know that little deportation thing that just happened last week, well with me as your agent I could keep things like that from happening. We haven't all the time in the world here man. If you want my help in this tournament and beyond we've only got a short time to prepare you. I'll need an answer now." Cross replied, a hint of desperation in his voice. It wouldn't be noticeable to anyone who was an amature to this business, but Enrique had been around long enough to know how to catch such things. Cross clearly needed him, and in all truth, Enrique felt that he needed the guidance and knowledge that Cross had for him as well. "Well, gringo, I think we're going to either make a very good team…" Morales began with a grin before raising an eyebrow and shaking his head, "or we'll wind up killing one another. Either way…it's a deal." Morales reached across the table and shook the hand of his new associate, or agent, as they both stood up and smiled. "Good. You won't regret this any time soon." Cross remarked as he turned and walked away from Morales, toward the exit. As he did Morales simply stared at him before mumbling to himself once more. "Some how, gringo, I highly doubt that."
