Meditation For Medication

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 You haven’t experienced pure comfort unless you’ve sat beside me on a quiet day. Listening to the sounds of the permanent things around us, letting it take control of your spirit. Letting it clear your mind. Taking a deep breath, your lungs collapse from the chill in the wind. Your hair dances around on a hollow stage, just the way I like it. Pulling a blade of grass up from deep inside the earth’s soil, I wonder if Team Global will ever get a long for this night of such importance. Think about it, if it doesn’t ruin your comfort that is. You have Josh, the reigning Global Wrestling World Champion, for over 100 days now, teaming with my friend and brother in Manifest Destiny, Leinad Soma. I never thought this would happen. Merely a week ago, we battled it out in the ring. With kicks, punches, and suplexes, we break each other down. We strive for success and we thirst for each other’s blood. Well, maybe Leinad more then others. We want each other to roll around in a world of torture, it’s our pleasure to do so. Every night, it isn’t surprising to see a pint of blood painted on this canvass we wrestle on. To most, it is disgusting and heartless, but to us…it is our masterpiece. Letting the blade of grass go, it twirls around in the wind…the beginning of a journey we all take each and every day when we open our eyes in the morning. Not knowing where this world, this wind, will take us. I sure hope Primetime Central knew what they were doing by having GW in this match. We take things seriously…and most of the time, it gets ugly. I hope they understand what will happen if Team Global comes together. In a tiny ring, there will be mass destruction and havoc. Chaos, Wow! Such a powerful word, we must live up to it’s name. If there is such a way to prepare for a night like this, I suggest you do so. I don’t know if you are religion or not, but if are, I suggest praying. Closing your eyes, bowing your head, and whispering words in hopes of coming true. Or maybe you should carry your good luck charm around in your pocket for the next week. Or…or wear your lucky underwear for Survival. Whatever it is, do it. There is no time like the present. The wind blows my hair to the side, over my shoulder. I turn my face towards the wind and take a deep breath. I squint my eyes, trying to push out a tear to freeze up on my cheek. What a crazy world we live in. I wonder if there is any other like it. Without thinking too hard, I want you to think about it. Is there another world that people can make a living off of cleaning up the filth of the world? Whether it be your local garbage man, or a member of the police force. Or, what about, people who make profit from selling their bodies for sexual encounters. If they make such profit, that means that their bodies don’t mean a lot to them to begin with. A world where children are photographed in the nude, a world where people are innocently killed, this…this is the world we live in. A world where models show us how to dress, scantily clad, then later complain because they are an object of lust. But it is alright with us. We accept such a thing. When things like this come up, we then like to think about butterflies and flowers. Think about something great, to take your mind off of the horrible. I suppose this is the reason why I’m here today, sitting beside this stream of forgiveness that runs beside a land of greed. People say that you should face your problems instead of running away from them. But what if the problems you face will never change? If they will only get worse as time moves on? What if you are outnumbered so much, your opinion will never be heard? What happens when nobody will even give you the time of day, to listen to what you have to say? This stream, it hears my confessions. Because nobody else will… I sigh, because I’m saddened at the sick world we live in. What a sick world we put up with… Footsteps are heard, it’s Leinad Soma…and other people. Without even turning… Without my knowledge… I already know it’s Leinad. Such a monster can be heard from miles away… ”I brought you some company.” I continue to face the stream and speak… ”Perhaps this isn’t the proper time for company.” Without looking, I know that Leinad nods… Sometimes, I know him better then he knows himself… ”Sorry Cherish. I thought he would want to see you.” I turn my head around, jumping up at the same time and run towards her with a big smile upon my face. I haven’t seen her in a week, I’ve been out here the entire time, excluding myself from the rest of the world. I lift her up in my arms and twirl her around…she looks, angelic. If you seen the both of us, walking down the street, you’d think that we are the worst couple in the world. But then, I’d question your relationship, criticizing you, putting you down, and making you a believer. It’s just too damn easy. I look over at Leinad, who stands beside his…well, they haven’t discussed that yet, Cassandra. I would say that they are together, but the one time, I seen Cassandra with a whip in hand, standing over a bloodied Leinad Soma. People shouldn’t wonder about the relationship I have with Cherish, so I won’t wonder about theirs. Whatever floats your boat, is the proper term. I put Cherish down on her graceful feet… ”How did you get here?” ”Master Soma brought us.” I nod my head and smile towards Leinad… ”Well, why don’t you join me then?” I wave over Leinad, Cherish and Cassandra to the streamside… ”No, Cassandra and I will be dealing with our own things. We’ll leave you two alone.” I nod and take Cherish’s hand and guide her to the spot where I was seated. She kneels down and sits on her ankles. She is wearing a gray Sage Sabbot hooded sweatshirt and a pair of khakis. Her hair, tied in a ponytail except for the few strands that blow in the wind in orbit around her head. Her eyes, like diamonds, but more precious. I am so lucky to have a woman like her… ”Thank you for coming.” She smiles… ”Any time off that Master Soma gives me is amazing, let alone spending it with you.” I feel like a schoolboy with a schoolboy crush… ”Stop, you’re going to make me blush.” Even Sage Sabbot has a weak side… ”So, what are you doing out here by yourself?” ”A lot of thinking.” ”About what?” I hesitate before I tell her… ”Survival is almost here. I’m just wondering if Leinad and Josh can get together for one night and we can show Primetime Central what GW is all about.” ”I talked with Master Soma inside the limousine about it.” ”You did?” She nods her head… ”Yeah, and he said he is going to put their differences aside for one night and one night only.” ”Good, hopefully Train is having the same talk with Josh right now about it.” ”Let’s just hope so.” I stand to my feet, still holding Cherish’s hand, I help her to her feet… ”I’ve been coming to this place for guidance for years now.” ”Who guides you?” ”…myself. You’d be surprised by everything you learn from yourself when you are in a place like this. A place that clears your mind…” I touch my finger, gently, on her head… ”…your heart.” I put finger over her heart… ”…and your soul.” I put my arm around her and start to walk with her… ”Let me show you around…” She nods. Her hair warms my neck as it hangs down on me… We walk along the streamside, stepping over stones that have heard every confession I’ve said since I was a child. We skip stones across the stream and make it to the other side. We reach the beginning of a forest and I step inside, pushing back branches so Cherish can walk safely in… Down a small path, and up a little trail, we reach a garden full of fruits and majesty… Cherish gasps at the site. ”Sage, this is beautiful.” I take her hand… ”And that is exactly the way I feel when I look into your eyes…” She blushes, it works like a charm. She leans over and kisses me on the cheek. ”You’re too cute.” I smile… ”Follow me…” I run up towards a couple trees, while Cherish follows behind me, laughing the entire way. ”Trees full of fresh apples and pears.” ”Wow! Can I have one?” By the time she finishes the sentence, I already have pulled down a red apple from the branch and placed it in her hands. She takes a big bite of the apple as the juice squirts out on the ground. She chews it a couple times, then swallows… ”This is the best apple I have ever tasted in my entire life.” ”I agree.” She takes another bite, then holds it in front of my mouth so I can take a bite. I take a little nibble of it, too much fruit can cause a bellyache and that’s the last thing I need right now. ”We have to come here again together when we have enough time.” I nod my head in agreement… ”Sounds like a great idea. But for now, I must finish my meditation and relaxation techniques.” ”Okay, I’ll be waiting by the limo.” I nod and give her a kiss… She turns away, with apple in hand and skips until I can’t see her anymore. I am left in awe of her. I grab an apple down from the branch and I hold it in my hand. I take a small bite, like I used to when I was a child. I take a deep breath, you don’t understand how amazing this feels. A small ladybug crawls on my thumbnail and I put my index finger underneath it and lift it up in front of my eyes… ”Hello, ladybug. I suppose this is a good sign having you around right before the Interfed Pay Per View. People say that you bring luck to whoever holds you in his hands… I squeeze my fingers together, pinching it to death. It’s wings smother in my very fingers… ”Too bad Team Global will not be needing it.” I lift my fingers apart and the dead ladybug falls to the grass, a new burial for it. I smirk, such a sadistic and nefarious smirk. That’s right, the old Sage Sabbot is back once again…
