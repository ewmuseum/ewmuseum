The Pieces Are Coming Together

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 \ | / It had just about turned to 7 pm and the sun had slowly faded its way into obscurity for the evening. Wraith was sleeping soundly until the sound of the phone had woken him up. His eyes shot open as reached across the nightstand to pick up the phone. Normally his phone never rang or at least he didn’t hear it. Perhaps he was sleeping lightly this evening. \ | / “Hello? Thank you, I appreciate your calling. I’m just lying here in bed actually... I know that isn’t the thing to do on your birthday but you know my habits and that is just the way things always work. I should probably see if I have any mail or anything like that. I’m surprised that my brother has not woken me up yet. He usually does but I don’t blame him for having things going on in his head like he does. Right, I know I shouldn’t make excuses for him but he’s the only brother that I have...or well at least recognize as being such. He’s never treated me unfairly even when we have had our disagreements. I saw how he treated Shadow and Epitome but he is vengeful when people do him an injustice or he feels they have anyway. Bare with me a second here, I am going to take the phone with my downstairs to see if there’s anything going on yet.” \ | / Wraith slips out of bed and doesn’t bother putting any other clothes on then what he was wearing. The black shorts and white tank top that he wore to sleep was all he had on but it wasn’t anything he had to worry about even if there was still one female around in the house, which was Slayer’s girlfriend, Lauren. There was a good sized pile of mail there addressed just for him. Most of them were birthday cards but there were a few packages that friends had sent and a few more from fans. It seemed like even though he wasn’t one of those mega stars he still had a really good fan base. He corresponded with some of his fans over the internet, a few of the cooler ones that he’d met on the road. Wraith found a letter opener on the desk in the office room that was communal to everyone who wanted to use it. There was another office that his brother had but it was under lock and key to anyone other than him. The Preternatural went through the cards rather quickly just to see who they were from. \ | / “I sure had a lot of cards and a quite a few packages this year. The fans are the best. I don’t know how they got this address but maybe Corvus had them forwarded from the PO Box that I have in Minnesota. It’s probably going to take me all night to get through this. I can’t wait to see what you bought me. You know I hate surprises! I think after this whole Survival thing is over I’m coming home for good. I have been thinking a lot about it since I came back to Cleveland and I was much happier when I wasn’t on the road so much. I’m just not happy with wrestling anymore. It’s just lost on me. I wanted to show everyone what I had but sometimes when you are pushing more than what you have you tend to only hold people back. I hate to say that after this I’m going to finish out my remaining contract with WMW and then fade off into the dark night. I know that is what you were hoping to hear anyway. I want to concentrate on my music career. It’s just time to move on.” \ | / It was evident that now Wraith was talking to his girlfriend but he wasn’t trying to make it too obvious for those watching at home. He tried to mask how he talked as the home of Corvus was rigged with cameras all over the place and had patched feeds to all the different wrestling organizations such as WMW and PTC. He stood up and scooped up the pile of mail and gifts and found a bag to put them in to carry them back upstairs with. Once he was back in his room he set the bag on the bed and laid back down to wind down his conversation so he can get cleaned up as he was probably going to go out someplace just to get out. \ | / “Sweetheart, hang on a second will you? I have a call on the other line. I’ll chase them off quick, ok? *hitting the flash button* Wraith here...oh hey, I was wondering where you were at. I didn’t see you when I went downstairs and your door was open. Oh, yeah...I can meet you at the club in about an hour. I just have to take a quick shower and finish talking to my girl. I’ll see you in a while, thanks man. See you then, bye. *hits the flash button for the second time* Sorry hon. That was my brother calling to tell me that he wanted me to come to a club that’s not far from here. So I should probably get ready to go and meet him. Did you want me to call you when I get back in? It probably is going to be a bit late so I’ll just call you when I get up. Alright, I’ll talk to you tomorrow night. I do you as well. Sleep well when you get there darling. Goodnight.” \ | / Wraith turns the phone off and puts it back on the charger and looks to the mail and shrugs a bit figuring that he’d be able to get back to it later when he came back in. He walks into the bathroom and closes the door behind him and turns the water on to start his shower. \ | / ~~~~ \ | / Wraith is now fully dressed and ready to go meet his brother at the club. He is wearing a short-sleeved button up black shirt, black cargo pants with a chain attached on the side down to the black Vans’ shoes that adorn his feet. He figured this club wasn’t going to require him to dress fancy so he didn’t. One change that one might notice directly is that the color of his hair is different. It’s now white with black and red colored streaks within it. He checks it out in the mirror to make sure that it didn’t look ridiculous and heads out of the bedroom. After making sure he had his cell phone and wallet with him, he locked up the house and exited through the garage. His bike had been moved inside while he was sleeping or had been borrowed by someone. He didn’t care either way he wasn’t a jerk about his possessions. Now on the bike, he hits the garage door button and waits until it lifts up before starting the bike up. Moments later he is speeding out of the garage heading towards the club. The feed is cut as the camera can only see just a little ways past the driveway. Time passes and we now find Wraith pulling up into the parking lot of the club where his brother was waiting for him. The building looked like an abandoned factory and it resembled something out of one of the Blade movies. It was a totally gothic oriented club. There were bouncers standing at the door who were letting people in this evening that had exclusive invites. There was a line nearly a mile long as Wraith cut through some of them and was chuckling to himself as they were yelling obscenities at him. Some of the women were trying to attach themselves to him just to try and get into the club. Wraith gave them his name and was let right in much to the irritation of those who had been waiting in the line for over several hours already. Of course when people know who you are or you have relatives that have positions of power as he did then things were a little easier. That was a perk he didn’t mind. Even as thoughts or retirement loomed through his head, the thought of stardom still trickled its way into his head. Now that he was inside he moved through the crowd of people until he met up with his brother Corvus, who was sitting at a table towards the back of the club in the darkest part of it smoking a Djarum black clove. Of course most people thought he did it because it was trendy among the goth crowd but Corvus smoked them because he enjoyed them and had discovered them much earlier than when they started becoming mainstream. “It’s about time you showed up, have a seat.” Corvus said as he flicked an ash into the tray and motioned the bar girl to head over to their table. He slide over the pack of cloves towards his brother and offered him one. “Take one if you want one.” Wraith slid the pack back to him. “Not right now, it’s too early for me. I haven’t been up that long.” The bar girl approaches the table and stands there waiting for an order. “I’ll have a wyne.” Corvus signals that he wants one as well and the bar girl walks off to go fill their drink order. “Happy birthday kid, I don’t have your present with me here but I’ll make sure you get it before we get back to the house and turn in for the night. I knew you were coming back here otherwise I would have gotten us into the box for the Twins game. They were playing the Indian’s tonight. It’s funny how things like that catch our attention.” {Wraith nodded and as cool as that sounded to him whether it was as a gift or just as something for the two of them to do just to hang out it wouldn’t have mattered to him. It wasn’t like the two of them were real close all the time, it was more of that they were all the other really had when everyone else seemed to crap on them.} “That would have been cool. I really wasn’t expecting anything special so I’m not worried about it. I need to start getting ready for this Survival match that is coming up. I’m hearing the competition is going to be brutal. I’m feeling like I’m on my last legs here bro and if I don’t make a good showing there then I’m not going to be able to do it anywhere.” “Well, sometimes it takes longer for others. You and I we are both unique it is just taking people longer to see that you are one of those guys who are going to be on the cutting edge of this sport. You have abilities and moves that stun just about anyone. Just because you are in a rough period don’t give up. Screw the critics if you have read something about yourself that was negative. I know just exactly what you are afraid of. Get that out of your system and out of your head. Once you do then other people will realize it too.” {Wraith concentrated on what his brother was saying as the bar girl brought their drinks. Of course it was going to go on Corvus’ tab which equaled out to he wouldn’t have to pay a dime since he knew the owner of the club more than likely. He had connections everywhere and that is something Wraith admired even though he wouldn’t admit to it. The main message he was getting out of what Corvus said was that he had to stop thinking that he was in the shadows of his brother. Only then could he really make anything work for himself. Wraith guzzled down the drink and set the glass down.} “You know what big brother? You’re right...you are damn right! I’m going to wrestle harder and fly higher than I ever have before. I’m not content with people saying that I am nothing and that I will never be as good as you. They may have one thing right, I may not be as good as you but that is because we are completely different people with totally different styles. I can’t use my back as an excuse anymore either. It is better than it has ever been and I’ll prove that when I step in that ring to represent Team WMW with the rest of the gang. I know that most of them hate you but that can’t be put on me either. If they think it is then it is their childish acts that are going to force us to lose the match. Especially that Matrix guy because not one person that I have spoken to has seen or heard from him at all. I’m not about to be disqualified because someone has a stick up their behind. He better get motivated or he’s going to find me on his doorstep with a 2 x 4 ready to whip him like a bad dog who has urinated on the carpet!” {Corvus was amused at his brother’s comments but also was good to see that he was getting some of that fire back in him that he used to have back in the days when they were working for the American Wrestling Alliance. It has been ages since that place was operational but if Wraith would have really put that aggression that he had just now into things, it would have been easy for him to have gone toe to toe with anyone and competed for any championship he wanted to. The WMW was not any different. Sure there were all different levels of talent but on any given day or at any given time just about anyone can come out of seemingly nowhere and be a contender. Corvus hated to see his brother get defeated by comments of how he was in a business that he just couldn’t be successful in. Wraith could be anything he wanted and that was what was going to be put in his head even if it had to be beaten out of him. The music that was playing in the club stopped and the DJ that worked there got onto one of the stages where they had musicians perform. The guy had a microphone in his hand and made sure he had the attention of everyone in the house before he spoke.} DJ: If I could have your attention. I’ve just been told that we have some very special guests here at the club tonight. They are from Cleveland’s very own Wrestling Midwest promotion. I have also been told that one of them is also celebrating a birthday this evening. We’d like to welcome Wraith and Corvus here to the club this evening and I know that this is supposed to be a night where you would just relax but Wraith I’ve heard that you are quite a talented musician. If you and your brother wouldn’t mind, I’d like for you to share with us some of those talents with the rest of us. C’mon up guys! {Wraith looked at his brother wondering if he had set this up but the look on Corvus’ face seemed one of absolute surprise. It was always easy to tell if the big man had something under his sleeve. Maybe the bar girl had overheard them talking and knew who they were and talked to the manager. Anything was possible at this point. The DJ kept encouraging them to step up on stage and even brought a sort of spotlight on them to get the crowd involved as well. Wraith stood up finally and motioned for his brother to follow him as the crowd parted for them with ease so that they would be able to get to the stage relatively without hassle. The two men collaborated as to what they possibly were going to play and since they didn’t have a full band backing them up the opportunity to play an acoustic song seemed to be the best. With their luck there was an acoustic guitar there for Corvus to play. That seemed a little more coincidence then Wraith would have liked but perhaps this was one of those tests his brother had for him. Wraith grabbed a chair to sit next to his brother who was tuning it so that it sounded right for what they agreed to play.} “Normally it is usually the big man here that does the vocalization but I guess we’re sort of switching things around tonight. Must be because I’m just another year older and I’m getting a chance to do my thing. I guess you never really realize how nervous you are until you see a ton of people anticipating your next move. It’s kind of the same thing in the wrestling ring. I’m not sure how all familiar you guys are with wrestling but I’m going to play an acoustic version of the song that I’m going to be coming out to the ring to very soon. It’s an oldie but a goody so here it all goes. This one’s called “Bullet With Butterfly Wings” hope you enjoy.” {The younger girls in the crowd cheer as the opening riffs are played and Wraith pulls off his shirt. He gets an even bigger reaction from the females then. Everyone is looking like they are enjoying the music as it is being played. The song lasts several minutes but once it is over the entire audience gives them a round of applause but it seems like more of them are cheering for Wraith then they are Corvus which in this instance is what he was hoping for. Wraith picks up his shirt and nods to everyone and he and his brother make their way off of the stage and go back to their seats.} “They really were into you up there kid; you did a damn good job. Maybe I should let you sing a little more often instead of hogging up the spotlight as I’m often said to do. I was really impressed with you. You have a lot of talent not only in the ring but off of it. If I were you, I wouldn’t give up just yet.” {Corvus motioned for the bar girl who was standing nearby to get them a refill on their drinks. He didn’t plan on staying much longer as he still needed to give his brother his birthday gift even though now technically his birthday was all but over.} “You set all this up didn’t you? Crazy jerk...I should make you go outside and throw down in the parking lot. Then again I don’t feel like getting my a$$ kicked. I’ve had a pretty good day so far. I really should thank you.” Corvus pulls out another clove and puts it into his mouth; lights it and exhales before making a reply. “No need to thank me, they were cheering for you. That was the whole point of that. I wanted you to realize that you had a lot going for you. That Survival match you’ve got is the first step. Go out there and give it your best, don’t worry about those on your team until you need to. I’m not exactly keen on teamwork these days but I think these guys realize this thing is going to be bigger than any petty grudge or lame excuse they have. Your time is starting brother; you just have to seize it.” {Wraith ran a hand through his newly changed hair and realized that Corvus was right. It was all starting to come into place now. He knew what he needed to do to make things work in his favor. There was just no other way to it than to keep preaching that this was the “Year of the Wraith” and not just be words but reality.} “You’re right; I’m going to make this work no matter what. I may have slipped up a little before but that was then. That was before I let that crazy being that’s been locked up inside of me out. The “Wylde Child” of Wrestling Midwest is going to do it his way and that is to help the team do what they have to do to achieve victory. Because the three W’s are going to quickly motivate everyone that is around me. *putting up one finger* Working hard, *puts up a second finger* Wrestling hard, *puts up a third finger forming the W that has become sort of his insignia* and Winning hard. That’s what’s going to happen when we face the guys from LOW, WWA, and PRIME. They may not have heard of WMW but once this is all over they are going to certainly FEEL the WMW!” \ | / Wraith grinned as their drinks were brought to them. He confidently took his glass and held it up. “To the W” he said as he clanked glasses with Corvus before chugging it down but not spilling even one drop down his chin as he tilted his head back to drink it. This was definitely a sure sign of him being more fired up then he ever has before. He had goals and he was going to set them one way or another. \ | / “I think we better get going kid, I’d had for the little ladies to try and mob you tonight. I don’t think your girlfriend would appreciate that. Let’s roll out of here.” “I’m ready to get out of here. I think there’s been enough excitement for me for one night. I have some gifts fans sent me to open when I get home anyway. Meet you back there?” “Let’s see who can get there faster.” Corvus grins as he slowly starts to edge towards the end of the booth. “I’ll give you your present when we get there. I’m sure you are going to absolutely love it.” He makes it look like he’s egging Wraith on to race him but it was more like a joke than anything. ~~~~ \ | / Once he was back home, Wraith plopped himself down on the leather couch in the front room. He waited for his brother to come back as he went to go get the gift that he had gotten. After several moments Corvus reappeared with a rolling cart with something that looked close to a half sized refrigerator. It was wrapped so that what was inside wasn’t easily figured out. Corvus didn’t say another word and just let Wraith explore for himself. He got up from the couch and walked over to it and touched it to see if it was solid or not which it was. He figured what the heck and just tore into the paper to see what was under it. After the gift wrap was all scattered across the floor at his feet there stood a glass case with a guitar inside. Wraith looked over to Corvus and nodded his appreciation but didn’t know there was more to it. \ | / “This is a really cool guitar man but I don’t understand why you have it in this glass case. You could have just given it to me without being all dramatic about it.” “Well there’s a reason why it is in the case goofball. It’s because it really isn’t meant to be played as it belonged to someone you consider to be one of your idols. I had a really hard time trying to find this but it was well worth the price as you are the only brother I have.” “Well who did it belong to? Don’t leave me hanging like that.” “It belonged to Kurt Cobain. These things aren’t just lying around so finding it was very tough but when the money is right people are willing to ask how high when you tell them that they need to jump.” \ | / Wraith’s jaw could have dropped to his feet if he had not kept himself in check. It was one of the best gifts he had ever been given. His brother usually got him the coolest stuff but this topped them all. He didn’t even want to touch the case to put fingerprints on it. After the initial awe subsided, Wraith was able to speak again. \ | / “I really don’t know what to say other then thank you. This is very cool...you have no idea how much I appreciate it.” “I think I have an idea on just how you can show your appreciation. You go out there and promote WMW with the best of your ability and then I’ll see your gratitude. All you needed was a little motivation...a little push. Sometimes that is what we all need to get steered into the right direction. I think I’m going to help you get this upstairs and then I’m going to call it a day. What do you say bro?” “I was hoping that I wasn’t going to have all the fun carting this up there. This is one of those times that I kind of wished I had stuck with the first floor. I like all the space I have up there though so I’m definitely not going to complain.” \ | / Wraith and Corvus then work to get the glass case and guitar upstairs without hindrance. He finds room for it in his music room so that whenever he is in there he can look at it and get a little inspiration from it. Corvus and Wraith embrace in a brotherly hug before the big man exits the room and heads back downstairs to his chambers. Wraith looks back at the guitar one more time before he goes back to his room. He crashes down on the bed and looks at the ceiling where all of the events of the day. All in all he had a good birthday and he also gained more confidence in himself towards this match for the PTC. There may or may not be a good chance of WMW winning or going very far but at least he knew that he’d be able to help them if they were able to get something going and possibly shock the world by working as a united front. \ | /
