The Nomad in Detroit...ad

16 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-16 A gloomy cloud wins the battle against the sun and hides it. The cloud brings with it a haze, a thick and humid afternoon, with nothing to do because while it's hot outside, there is no intention due to the discouraging storm rolling in. Among the people sitting inside their homes, Current Core Wrestling Tag Team Champions Lag Sverige. Not much has been seen of these two in recent weeks because Matt Lanier has decided to revamp the tag team division, ultimately choosing that the titles will no longer be active but remain in the Swedish Tag team's hands. Adoph's mind has been attracted to the ever popular gaming system, Playstation 2. While Bjorn has managed to keep him occupied buying games left and right, Adoph has been hooked on one and only one game, the seemingly never ending series of football games in Madden 2005. He now sits on a blue suede couch with a PS2 controller in his hand and his eyes glued to the television. Wearing the orange football jersey of the Cincinnati Bengals, Adoph refuses to take his eyes away from the TV sitting in the middle of a black entertainment center, even after Bjorn calls him from another room. After one more call Bjorn enters the room and stands looking at Adoph as he finally pauses it and looks up at Bjorn, who is sporting a tight blue shirt and fitting jeans. "What Bjorn, I just got a 3rd down conversion and there is only 2 minutes left in the game" Adoph answers Bjorn's call only after Bjorn had to act. "I know you have been enjoying the game and what not but, don't you think it's time you hit the gym or something? We have been out of the ring for a bit and while it may not have been that long it feels a lot longer and now we have a shot at winning another title." Bjorn gives Adoph a reason for interrupting the supposedly ever-important game. "I'll do it later. It's no big deal, I mean you said it yourself, Matt Lanier doesn't really like us and he is really only giving us a shot because we have some stupid clause or something. I mean can't you win, I know that it is a tag team match but all you have to do is pin and I can just help you a little bit. I really don't like wrestling that much anymore." Adoph replies to Bjorn as he yawns and puts down the controller realizing that this game is going to take longer than he first thought. "You don't like wrestling anymore..?" Bjorn asks in almost disbelief, "You mean to tell me that I brought you to America almost 13 years ago to wrestle and now you don't feel like wrestling or you don't want to wrestle. That's too damn bad man, you signed the contract, you made a commitment and like it or not you are going to have to finish this out." Bjorn strikes back already getting a little frustrated with Adoph and his reasoning. "I know, I know, but I like this football. Back home they never had this, I mean I think I saw it once but man this game is great. Just look at the rest you get and the tackling and the blocking and hitting and man I just love this game. And last night I saw the Falcons play the Ravens and apparently last year Michael Vick, he's like the best quarterback in the league, was injured by the same team in the preseason just like this so he didn't play a lot but the Falcons lost because of it... And tonight the...” Adoph begins to start to get away from the point but before anymore can be said Bjorn slows him down and then shuts him up by just putting up his hand. "Look..." Bjorn says as he sits down in an armchair adjacent to the Adoph, "I know you may like the game, and you can go out and join a local league with a bunch of guys playing flag football or whatever, just don't go overboard with this. It's eventually going to die down and you're going to get sick of it and want to move onto something else and I can't go on like that so I'm ending this right now. You're going to wrestle, we're going to beat Cassidy and Worrell and after that we can come back home, and I'll take the win and you can keep having fun with your video games but don't go over with it... understand?" Bjorn says as he rises from the chair and heads out of the room not really waiting for an answer from the bamboozled sibling, Adoph Nilsson. Before Adoph can even reflect on all of what Bjorn told him, Bjorn re-enters the room and walks right back over the chair he sat only seconds ago, except this time he remains standing. "Yeah and I hope you haven't forgotten about that Primetime Central I-PPV? I mean you were picked for the team and you have been slacking with that. I haven't seen you talking to anyone else on the team or even trying to get in touch with a ride to get to the arena. Adoph you really need to put your ass in gear because this is getting a little out of control, especially with this whole, Madden/football thing you have going on. So put football out of your mind and get yourself a ride to the arena because I won't be able to make it until a little later on in the show and you will need to be their at the start of that show. I'm going to be going out for a few so when I walk back into this house you better have a ride man, because I am going to be held responsible for your stupidity and your screwing over of an enter wrestling federation slash multi-million dollar company." Bjorn puts some emphasis on the last sentence but Adoph still stares ahead at the game with his head in his hands looking board out of his mind. Bjorn doesn't say anymore to Adoph, but instead walks out the door and leaves Adoph to himself. Around this time, Adoph feels bad, he can't help that he wants to move onto something new but he has left his brother with no life but wrestling, and without that he has nothing but Adoph himself. He was inspired the speech that was meant to almost discourage Adoph, had inspired him and he had made a decision, he was going to get that ride. "I have made a decision," Adoph says as he stands up and points his finger into the air, "I will get that ride!" Adoph takes a few steps and then tries to dive up over the armchair in which Bjorn was sitting early, but doesn't make it and ends up knocking down the coffee table and the armchair itself. Adoph quickly recovers and then grabs the phone that once rested on the coffee table and lifts it up looking face down at the keypad. "Hmmmm... A number, I need a number to dial." Adoph speaks to himself staring down at the phone still. "Oh, Oh OH! I got one. Eight, Six, Seven, Five, Three, 0, Ni-eine!" Adoph finishes dialing and then holds the phone up to his ear. After a few seconds Adoph grins and then begins to speak with the other person on the other line. "Hi yeah can you give me a ride... No, no I don't want to call back or try again later... Ummm... No I refuse to. Can you just give me a ride? Please?! I mean my brother is really mad at me and I need a ride to this big I-PPV for wrestling... Are you a wrestling fan? " 10 Minutes Later "...fine then it's settled, I give you a ride on Monday and then you give me one the rest of the week, great! Wait what? That's new, usually you just repeat yourself, what do you mean ninjas? No, I didn't order any ninjas!" Before Adoph can finish his sentence the front door is busted down and six men wearing black robes with ninja masks and red sashes rush in and begin preparing for a large encounter. "HIIIIIIII YA!" One ninja shouts at the top of his lungs as he rushes towards Adoph. Adoph does some horrible jump spinning kick, or at least what looked like one, and the ninja gets collapsed by the giant that is Adoph. Adoph gets up and then tries to duplicate the actions of the ninjas when they first entered the house. "You don't want to mess with me... I watched Karate Kid II, only a few hours ago. So if you're going bring it... BRING IT!" Adoph gets serious and then runs straight at the group of ninjas but manages to miss them all and head straight out the door. Adoph continues running until he reaches the street where he stops and looks around. Adoph begins dusting off his hands like he just took out the garbage and then looks into the camera. "Ninja moves complements Mr. Miagi's Ninja Doju and Chinese Take out... on the corner of Ash and Maple Streets. Learn to take them out, with your chopsticks, Mr. Miagi's Ninja Doju and Chinese Take out." Adoph looks at the camera one more time and then heads up the street and in a few seconds an old woman comes walking towards Adoph. "I need a ride," Adoph says to the old lady who mistakes it for a sexual inneundo. "You need to get your mouth washed out with soap... That's what you need youngster." The old woman says as she is slow but determined not to use anyone else's help as a mode of transportation. "What? I don't get what you said... Bjorn says I have ADD." Adoph walks with her as he waits for an explaination being confused by the old lady's confusion. "ADD!? What are you speaking Greek?!" The old lady questions as she stands erect and then in a full sprint runs away from Adoph. "ASSHOLE!" she shouts as she makes her way a few feet from Adoph and the stops and hunches over once again to begin walking in here very feetle position. Adoph is stunned for a moment and then shakes it off continuing walking in the same direction in which he once was. Adoph doesn't say much and doesn't encounter much until he trips over a waist high banner. He manages to stand back up and gain some composure as he looks at the banner in which he tripped over. It's a bright orange banner that reads in white text, "Athens 2004". Adoph stares at it for another moment and then looks around. In the distance he can see some ruins of a tall building with columns. He spins around to see a statue of a man throwing a diskess. He walks around a little more and then finds a man wearing a bright aqua blue t-shirt, khaki shorts and a white fisherman hat holding a clipboard. "Hey... umm... where am I?" Adoph asks the man who has been reading his clipboard since Adoph spotted him. "You've got to be kidding me right? You're in Athens, man... The Olympics... does that ring a bell?" The man laughs to himself but Adoph doesn't seem to budge at the fact that he has gone from Detroit, Michigan, USA to Athens, Greece. Adoph continues to inquire about this last answer. "Do these, 'Olympics', have a bus to the stadium?" Adoph asks putting his hand up on his chin and rubbing his beard. "Yeah but the next one is in five hours, unless you're a worker and you get one in about the next fifteen minutes." The man doesn't look up from his clipboard as he continues to answer Adoph's questions. "Well I don't have five hours... but fifteen minutes... that seems like this time. So these workers, you are one?" Adoph continues to question the man as he relaxes his body a bit knowing that he may be standing in the same position for a bit. "Yeah, I'm not wearing this stupid ass outfit for no apparent reason." The man says stating the obvious about the outfit. "Well I hate to do this but..." Adoph takes a deep breath and then chops the worker in the neck. Within seconds men in black suits holding bats and various guns surround Adoph. "TERRORIST!" The men shout and then begin kicking Adoph in the shins and as Adoph falls the men begin to swing the bats and shoot at Adoph on the ground. The men dispand but not before they all toss down some grenades at the ground where Adoph once fell. Adoph stands up wearing a white turbans around his head and camos with a long gray beard and a sign around his neck that reads, 'Hi, My Name Is Osama'. After Adoph looks down and takes off the outfit he heads off the screen... "Hey mister wake up... you're on my puppy mister!" A little child's voice is heard as the screen opens up and a little kid holding a lease whining about someone sleeping on their dog... that someone is Adoph Nilsson. Adoph gets up and rubs his eyes as he towers over the kid and the flattened tea cup poodle. Adoph looks around and then at the child and the dog. "So you mean it was all a dream... I'm not in Athens! I'm in DETROIT! NOT ATHENS!" Adoph jumps up and down and then runs off screen only to return a few seconds later and talk to the kid again. "Dogs weren't meant to be that small by the way!" Adoph runs back off screen but this tim ethe camera follows him as again he runs straight into a sign. The same thing happens like the last time Adoph tripped over a sign but this time, Adoph turns back to read the sign... The sign reads, "Joe Louis Arena". Adoph smiles at the sign and then stands up again and looks down the street. He looks at the huge parking lot and then back down the street again. Adoph notices the out of date Christmas lights still on the house with a plastic Santa and then speaks to himself. "Hey that's my house!" Adoph shout as he runs, around the sign this time, back to his house, which is only no more than 15 feet down the road. Adoph runs and takes a turn into the driveway of the house with its Christmas lights still up as 6 men, wearing black ninja suits and their ninja masks in their hands, leave the house. Dol... PHIN
