Interlude - Middle of Middles

20 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-20 Present Day Campfire in the graveyard [You still find yourself sitting by the campfire with the man that has told a very interesting story. You watch as he continues to talk,] Syckle: When I awoke I found that I was a man of 20 years of age. I was located in the town of Vertigo Heights, North Carolina. I had slept for over a hundred years. I sat up in bed and looked around what was my room and found that it was spacious. I got up and found the door and that was when I met my new “parents”. There names were Maria and Thomas Roberts. I learned quickly that I wasn’t the most important part of their life and I never was. That will come in later. But for now I can tell you that I was also greeted by a little bouncing baby blonde girl. Her name was Shallimar. To speed up this story I will tell you nothing of importance until six years later. Within those six years I had a falling out with my parents and I left, I had one bad relationship with a woman named Cassandra that would lead me on a quest a few years down the road, and I had entered into the world of Professional wrestling, all the while behind the curtain hunting and killing demons. When I stepped foot into wrestling I entered into an upstart federation called the Halo Wrestling Federation. The federation owner Jonathan Saint was an old friend and called upon me for a meeting about the federation. * * * HWF Offices A few years ago [William and Jonathan Saint sit across from each other on opposite sides of a desk. In front of William sits a contract for the HWF. Jonathan talks to William,] Saint: William I could really use your help around here? William: I’m not much of a wrestler. Saint: You don’t need to be. I need your help for another reason. William: What are you talking about? Saint: This federation is a front. It is just for show. William: A front for what? Saint: There are a few of us here, namely myself and another that are like you. We hunt demons and keep the world safe from evil. William: Are you serious? Saint: I’m dead serious. Just sign on the dotted line. [William signs on the dotted line,] William: I’m probably going to regret this. * * * Present Day Campfire in the graveyard [Syckle is now standing and walking around the fire and you,] Syckle: Those two years in the HWF culminated with a rivalry with our enemy federation the Hellion Wrestling Alliance. That federation was made of demons that took human form and was run by a man known only as Diabolique. Diabolique had the power to bring others under his thrall and they would do as he commands without question. I fell under that thrall. I was commanded to take out everyone of the HWF roster one by one. I did as I was commanded until the final battle. The last member of the HWF roster was “Super Six” Jeff McCartney. If I was to beat Jeff the HWF would die and the world would be invaded by demons. Through all of this I was troubled with nightmares of a jackal headed man that was telling me to resist the thrall, * * * Syckle’s mind A few years ago [Syckle stands before a man in a golden jackal head mask. The man’s voice echoes all around the black void that they are standing in,] Anubis: William Roberts, you are stronger then this demon. You have to dig down deep and unleash the power inside. Unlock what you know is there. Syckle: What are you talking about and who are you? Anubis: You will come to know who I am in time. You have had the memories. You have come to think of them as nightmares. They are not nightmares they are visions. I cannot tell you of what they are visions of. Syckle: Thanks for the vagueness. Anubis: Vagueness is all I can give you right now but you know that Diabolique is wrong and you cannot deny your calling. Syckle: So how do I break his hold over me? Anubis: You have the power. Use it. [The man known as a Anubis fades.] * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle had stopped and looked up at the sky,] Syckle: Somewhere within me I found the power to fight back and I challenged Diabolique to a fight. The fight was a brutal one but it was I that came out on top and I put an end to his madness. A few days after the final fight I received a call from Dante Lucero. Dante was a friend from a long time ago. I went to school with him and his sister, Lourdes Lucero. Dante and I talked for a few minutes catching up on old times. It was then that he asked me if I had any plans for the next few months. I told him that I hadn’t thought that far ahead. Dante then asked me if I would like to come and stay with him and in sister in Ireland. I thought about it for a few seconds and then I agreed. I really needed a break from the demon world and I needed to heal. The next few days I spent getting thing prepared and packed to go to Ireland. Once my affairs were in order I powered up the private jet and arrived in Ireland a few hours later. As I stepped out onto the tarmac I was greeted by Dante, * * * Emerald Stone, Ireland A few years ago [William steps out on to the tarmac as jet winds down. He is greeted by a man that stands his height and has white hair and wore a red open button shirt and red leather pants. Dante Lucero extends his hand and William takes it. William slings his duffel bag over his shoulder as they walk to Dante’s car. William opens the trunk of the car and throws his bag in then slams it. He opens the passenger side door and gets in. Dante does the same on the driver side door and then starts up to car. William admires the lush green rolling hills and then he turns to Dante,] William: So why the call out of the blue, Dante? Dante: To be completely honest William, Lourdes asked for you. William: Why? Dante: She won’t tell me. She says that she will only talk to you. William: You don’t sound worried. Dante: Lourdes, has been acting strange as of late. William: How so? Dante: Every night just around midnight Lourdes starts screaming. William: Is she having nightmares? Dante: Yes but it’s not that kind of screaming. This is a constant, steady, high-pitched, blood curdling, wake the dead type of scream. William: What else? Dante: As I said there were her nightmares. She told me that they are full of horrible images of death, murder, and maiming. William: Anything else? Dante: I’m sure there are things that I haven’t seen yet but I’m sure you’ll see everything. William: Hooray. * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle has now started to pace around the fire again,] Syckle: Dante was right. My first night in Lucero Keep which turned out to be a castle that sat high up on a rolling hill. Lourdes was there when we arrived but she said all of two words to me. That night I experienced first hand what Dante was talking about. Around midnight I heard the screaming. It echoed throughout the castle. I followed the sound and came to a large wooden door. From under the door I saw a bright blue glow. As the final stroke of twelve happened the screaming and the blue glow stopped. I had my hand on the doorknob when everything stopped. I decided against opening the door and went back to my room. It went that way for the next few days. The screaming and glow would happen until the final stroke of midnight. On that Sunday night I was able to open the door. * * * Lourdes Room A few years ago [William opens the door to Lourdes’ room. There standing over her bed is a long figure in a ragged blue dress. The creature could best be described as a woman that was hideously deformed,] William: A Banshee! Lourdes: HELP ME! [William ran up to the Banshee and grabbed it by the neck and threw it across the room. The Banshee hit the wall but got up and screamed as it attacked William. William was slammed up against the wall. The Banshee pulled him up and slammed him into another wall and then through a dresser. William gets up and hits the Banshee with a fist. Lourdes has now gotten out of bed and starts to attack the Banshee. She is quickly dispatched into a nightstand. Dante comes into the room brandishing a gun. He takes aim and hits the Banshee. The Banshee it hit and screams. Dante is blasted back with a blue ball of energy. William stands up and starts to glow green. He sends a green lightning bolt toward the specter. The Banshee is blasted back against the wall. William throws another lightning bolt and the banshee is knocked to the floor. William’s eyes then grow a bright green as he speaks in voice that wasn’t his own,] William: DEMON, YOU SHALL TORMENT THIS HOUSE AND THIS FAMILY NO MORE! YOU ARE HERE BY TO BE JUDGED BY THE KEEPER OF THE BALANCE. [William closes his eyes and then he opens them,] William: YOU HAVE BEEN JUDGED GUILTY WITH THE CRIME OF TORMENT. YOU ARE TO BE BANISHED TO THE HERE AFTER AND SUFFER YOUR SECOND DEATH! [William places his hand in front of him and a black void opens up. The Banshee is sucked into the void and then the black hole closes up. As William stops glowing green both Lourdes and Dante look at him from the corner of the room. He looks at them and then around the room,] William: What the hell happened here? Why are you two bleeding? Dante: I think the real question is what just happened here? William: What are you talking about? Dante: You just whooped the hell out of a banshee, you glowed green, spoke in some voice that wasn’t you, and then opened a hole to god knows where. William: I have no idea what you are talking about. I think I must have been sleepwalking because I feel like I just woke up. If I caused this I’ll help clean it up. [Dante went to say something but Lourdes clasped his arm and shook her head,] Lourdes: No more he doesn’t know anything went on. Leave it at that. Dante: Why? Lourdes: I’ll tell you later. * * * Present day Campfire in the Cemetery [Syckle had stopped at the grave of Lourdes,] Syckle: I had come to find out later that Lourdes was an emissary of Isis sent to earth to keep tabs on me. Lourdes and I eventually fell in love and were married. But not long before the wedding I had to take care of something. It had come to my attention that while I was in Ireland with Lourdes. My father had turned abusive toward my six year old sister Shallimar. He went out of her way to hit her any chance that he got. One night while I was dining out I got a call from my friend who works at Vertigo Heights General Hospital that my sister had been brought in after being severely beaten by my father when he found out that Shallimar wasn’t his child. My mother had an affair with a man named Ethan Raine and wound up pregnant. My mother had my sister and passed her off as my fathers. The details of how he found out aren’t important. What was important was that he put her in a coma. I told Lourdes what happen and we flew back to the states to be with my sister. I stepped into my sister’s hospital room and found her hooked up to a life support system. All I could think about was revenge and what I was going to do to my father when I got my hands on him. * * * Shallimar’s hospital room A few years ago [William paces back and forth as Lourdes looks on from where she was sitting. William stopped pacing and hit the wall,] William: Grrrr! I am going to lay into my father like he did Shallimar. No child harmed this way. I am going to kill my father. Lourdes you stay here. I’ll be back. [Lourdes stood up and grabbed William’s arm,] Lourdes: No you can do that. Solving violence with violence will do nothing. There are other ways to get at your father. William: No there isn’t at least not in these parts. My family owns most of this town. My father is a messiah to this city. He is untouchable at least when come to legalities. No an eye for an eye is the only thing that he will learn from. Lourdes: William you don’t understand you could possibly kill him by touching him. Osiris and Isis won’t be happy with you. [William looked confused,] William: What are you talking about? Osiris and Isis? Lourdes: Fine William just go. Do what you must. [William gave Lourdes a confused look but then took off.] * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle still gazes at Lourdes grave,] Syckle: Lourdes was right. Violence can’t be solved with violence. But I never got the chance to find that out. While I was racing toward my family’s estate with nothing on my mind but ripping my father’s head off his shoulders I didn’t see the curve before it was too late. My car went sky high landed on an embankment and then flipped and rolled down the hill coming to rest next to a rock. The ambulance came about five minutes after my crash and pronounced me dead. Some how I had been looking on as they moved my body to the ambulance dragging me up the hill and then taking off. I was left at the crash site. That was when I saw him. The man that I saw when I seemed to sleep walk. * * * Crash site A few years ago [William looks on as a man who had an aura of green around him walked his way. The man carried a large gold jackal head mask under his arm. The man had long flowing red lochs of hair that blew in the wind. William asked the man a question,] William: Who are you? Anubis: I am a friend that has intervened on your behalf before you made a big mistake. William: Nice job. You killed me. [The man laughed,] Anubis: You aren’t dead William. William: Wrong Red. The paramedic said I was. Anubis: For all intents and purposes your body gives the appearance you are dead. Consider what this an out of body experience. William: Okay then why are you here? Anubis: I am here to give you advice. William: On what? Anubis: Sit and we shall talk. [Anubis gestures to two large rocks. Anubis and William sit,] Anubis: I can see that you have questions burning inside to ask. You have until sunrise to ask me all the questions you want. Sunrise occurs in one hour so ask your questions but I will tell you now there are some that I cannot answer. Not now anyway. So ask your questions. William: Who are you? Anubis: My name is Anubis. William: Why is it every time that I sleep I see you? Anubis: That is one of the questions that I cannot answer. William: Okay, then why is it there are times that I wake up and find myself in a totally different place other then my bed and around there is destruction? Anubis: Suffice it to say you are doing your duty. That’s all I can say. William: Fine. If I’m not dead then what am I? Anubis: You are in limbo. Your body is dead but your soul is still alive. At day break I shall return you to your body. William: You said you had some advice for me. Anubis: Yes. Do not go after your mother and father. There fate has already been put in motion. I know that you want an eye for an eye but I am telling you not to go after them. [William looks to his left and see the sun start to break over the mountains. He looks back to face Anubis but finds him gone. Then he finds himself being pulled back to his body.] * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle has knelt in front of the grave and cleared away the snow at the base of the grave,] Syckle: I found out what Anubis meant about my parents. They died the next night in a bad car accident. Shallimar was in a coma for three years. She awoke after I came back from finding my first daughter Allure in Egypt. Allure was my daughter from the relationship I had with Cassandra Cassidy. The woman that I said would come up later earlier in my story. Cassidy and I were a fly by night pair. When she left she forgot to mention that she was pregnant. I got a call from the Egyptian authorities telling me that I had to come and pick up my daughter or she would become a ward of the state. They had told me that her mother had been gun down in the middle of the street and Allure was found trying to plug her mother’s bullet wounds with her little fingers. I came and got her and then brought her back to my family’s estate. But I also came back to find that my sister was awake and Lourdes who was now my wife was pregnant with my second child Jillian. I also had started back with a new federation named the Gulfsouth Wrestling Alliance. A year later Jillian was born and then after another year my whole world was taken away from me. * * * GWA Arena A few years ago [Syckle had just finished a great night that culminated with successful defense of the GWA World Title. Syckle heard his cell phone ring. He picked it up and answered it,] Syckle: Hello? [Detective Jennifer Lee was on the other end of the line. She gave Syckle the bad news,] Lee: Yes Mr. Roberts this is detective Jennifer Lee from the Vertigo Heights PD. I hate to tell you this but I’m afraid that your wife and three children were killed in a fire earlier tonight. [Syckle stopped everything he was doing. He felt like he was going to die. He hung up the phone and took off for the nearest airport.] * * * Present Day Campfire in the Cemetery [Syckle now looks up to the moon,] Syckle: I went back to Vertigo Heights and identified the bodies of Shallimar, Lourdes, Allure, and Jillian. I then took off and spent the next four years searching for the man that took away my family. Over those four years Vertigo Heights came to know me as Syckle. The man that showed up when the innocent need vindication. Some of the cops came to hate me but others sought out my help when cases became too much for them to handle. But I had found that the serial arsonist was back in town. I hunted him down and beat the hell out of him. I was just about to give him the final blow when everything stopped in a freeze frame. Once again I was looking at my body and knew what it meant. * * * Warehouse District Alley A few years ago [Syckle turns to see Anubis leaning up against a wall. Anubis looks around and smiles,] Anubis: So we meet again William. Syckle: Go away Anubis. I am busy at the moment. Anubis: No your beating of the man is over. Syckle: What makes you think you can stop me? Anubis: Simple, I have all the answers to the questions that plague you. You have waited long enough for answers. But it is your choice. Answers or your life. Syckle: What will happen to him? Anubis: He will live out the rest of his life in jail and I’ll make sure he lives with the pain that he has caused you for the rest of his life but you must leave now. Syckle: Fine take him. Where is it that I need to go? Anubis: I’ll send you there. [Syckle was transported away.] * * * To be concluded…
