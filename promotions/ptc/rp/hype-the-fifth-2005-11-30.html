Date: 2005-11-30 25 People You Don’t Want to Face. 25. Yourself. When it comes right down to it, in any tournament, your greatest enemy is not the man standing across the ring from you, be he Angelo Deville, Rich Rollins, Craig Goat, or even Steve "Milkman" Carnage. Your greatest enemy is yourself. Your doubts, your fears, your insecurities. We’ve seen them all come to play over the course of this hype period, in the form of the various techniques and strategies. Some have chosen to call little attention to themselves, hoping to become quiet dark horses in the tournament. Others use pictures and video to seize the public’s attention, to feed upon the spotlight and use its golden glory to propel them to PTC immortality. We have won tournament matches. We have lost tournament matches. We take that experience with us into each subsequent tournament. It guides us, it shapes us. And when we are alone, preparing for the most important match of our lives, we all must face ourselves. Truly, we are the most dangerous opponent. Because, if we can’t beat ourselves, if we can’t master our psyches and steel our wills, how can we be expected to beat a man or woman who does the same? ----------------------------------------------------------- "Yevon," said Seymour Almasy, "X"ing out of the Primetime Central Forums browser window, "how melodramatic." Heaving a sigh, the Final Fantasy rose, heading off to the kitchen to fix himself a pre-workout snack. As he did so, however, he had a thought. "Hey, what the fuck happened to seven through twenty-four?"
