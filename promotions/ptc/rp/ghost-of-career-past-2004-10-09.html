This wasn't a moment that anyone would ever have expected, especially in the heart of New York City. Though there were many cities that carried the monicer, only NYC could really live up to it's reputation as the city that never sleeps. Even now, for the rest of the world, the heart of Time Square was bustling with life. The sun shined down boldly from the sky above the city, it's glimmer off the millions of panes of glass only adding to the glow of the city from the millions of lights sparkling from the glowing billboards and gigantic video screens. Thousands people crowding the space, traversing the concrete spill ways on foot or only slightly faster by car. As always the city was alive. For everyone else anyway. For Train the city stood frozen in time, if only for a short moment. First the city froze. Then they all disappeared. Everyone other human being walking in the heart of the city with millions stacked on top of eachother became non existent. All except her. It had be a rather uneventful day save this freeze frame moment in the heart of Metropolis. The Giovanni Rims people could have chosen to be assholes about the change over in Train's career as he and Global Wrestling unamicably parted ways. Instead they proved they were in it for the long hall with Train, calling him back to NYC for a new round of promo shots for the next round of print adds. Last time Train posed with the models, the biggest rims the company could provide and the GW Platinum Title slung over his shoulder. The tag line.... "Two ways you can roll with Platinum..." at the top of the ad. "Be smart... Buy Giovanni Platinum." at the bottom. There was no Platinum Title to pose with this time, but that was alright. The provided a wide array of platinum chains, watches and bracelets to make up for any lack of bling. Train just added those to his own diamond chain and Rolex, mixing them with his Timberlands, black jeans and chisled upper body displayed prominently under a gleaming wife beater. All he had to do was stand behind one of the big 24" chrome rims and cut his coldest look straight at the camera. Again they'd come up with the concept to wrap itself around Train, his mind easily seeing it as they described what they were trying to do. Behind him GW Arena over his right shoulder and WMW Arena over his left. From the front door of GW's Arena a line of rims growing larger and larger as they stretch forward diagonally, meeting up with the rim in front of Train. A line of rims growing smaller and smaller as they stretch diagonally back towards the front of WMW Arena. Now the tag line embraced his transition. "Trains and Giovanni Platinum...." it would say at the top... "MADE TO ROLL." it would state plainly at the bottom. The pages of XXL, Vibe and The Source would be graced by the ads by the end of the month. The whole thing hadn't taken more than two hours to get in and knock out. That left Train with a day to kill in the Big Apple. Hell, it was New York. No doubt he could find something to do. Something to occupy his mind. He knew it for a fact. If only he'd known the world was so fucking small... Train's quest to sniff out something to do is what lead him to Times Square. There was nothing but things to do. He figured he couldn't have been out and about for more than an hour or so when he spotted her. Most of his excursion had been spent signing autographs and posing for pictures for a plethora of tourists and natives alike. It felt good to get love from the folks on the street. It let him know what he did stuck with people. Let him know he was leaving an impression in the business...he was adding up to something. But none of that added up to this... Train had stopped to take another set of pictures with another set of fans when he spyed his first glimpse of her. At any rate Train had his arms over the shoulders of the two young boys who'd come up and asked him for a picture. He should have been spying the camera as the boys' father, obviously the consumate tourist, snapped the picture. Instead her auburn flashed like a beacon as it swayed in the breeze, a red flag and caught his attention. Train didn't even see the flash of the camera. For a moment all he could see was a small group of women across the busy street. The stood with their stylishly covered backs to him, staring into the display window of a jewelry shop. All three clamored and pointed at the jewelry, obviously layinging their claim to the diamonds like he and his friends had done when they were children, pointing at expensive vehicles that passed through their neighborhood claiming "That's my car." But the only one that really stood out was the one in the middle. Her redhair blazed in the light that shined down from the sky and, even at this distance, he could see he milky white skin had a healthy glow to it. He body was tall and slender, but with enough curves to make any man take notices. Of course that wasn't really what caught him. Her shape... the way she stood...held herself...there was just somethign about her that was just so... so... Familiar. What was it that gave him the sense that he'd seen her somewhere before? A damn good question considering he couldnt' even see her face. But that changed quickly. The small clique turned from the display of sparklers and scanned up and down the street, obviously trying to choose their next destination. And that's when it happened. That's when the world slowed to a crawl. The question of familiarity was no longer a question. How did he know her? No.... How could he forget her? The two boys and their father thanked him for being kind enough to stop for them, but he hadn't heard a word of it. Instinctualy Train gave a polite nod, but he never even looked back. The flow of traffic was slow, so the trip across the street, against the light was slightly perilous, but agility was a trait he'd worked hard on cultivating. He dipped and dodged through the slow to moderate traffic with realative ease. By the time he reached the other side of the street her crew had shuffled of down the street. Throw the crowd it was hard to keep an eye on them, but he managed to spot them. Train dipped and dodged again, though it was harder in cramped quaters. He let loose a few sorry's and excuse me's as he bumped people out of his way via his bulky frame. Train didn't really know why he was chasing her. Didn't understand why he was so anxious to hear her voice. Fact is he hadn't devoted any significant thought to her in a couple of years. But still...whatever the motivation. He knew he had to see her. "So... Christopher called last night." said the redhead to the tall brunette to her left and the short, but curvy blonde on right. "Oh God," said the blonde in an exasperated huff. "Please tell me you are not going to put yourself through this drama again. You got divorced for a reason. You can't just keep letting him play these games with you everytime he's feeling lonely are smart enough to realize he made a mistake." "I know we got divorced for a reason," said the redhead. "It's not like I didn't have anything to do with it." "Gab... come on." the brunette interjects, cutting off her friend in a tone that's about as frusterated as the others. "How long are you gonna let him keep beating you up emotionally about something you did years ago? And then letting him come sniffing around whenever he feels lonely?!" "Thank you!" yells the blonde, accepting the other's back up on their shared opinion. "Guys...." the redhead chimes in. "He tried. He really did. It's just hard. He knows she's not his and it's hard. It would be for anybody." "Not anybody." states the brunette plainly. "He's not mine and I love her to death." "Just because you're a model doesn't mean you have to play dumb. You know exactly what I mean." "Touchy, touchy..." says the brunette with a slight chuckle. "I'm just saying. He only pays attention to you when it's convenient for him. You deserve better. Brianne deserves better." "Yeah... she does.... Alright, forget I mentioned it. Where do you guys wanna go for lunch?" "Where ever it is...." begins a deep, husky, yet slightly winded, voice rumbling from behind the tight knit group. All three women, somehow knowing, even amongst all the noise and conversations around them, turn to see who's speaking to them. The relative confusion on all of their faces is quickly replaced by a new look for the red head. A bit of shock... confusion...with just a sprinkle joy thrown in for extra flavor. He deep green eyes glimmer as they lock right onto his. Train just grins at her. "It's on me." Train says. "Oh my God......." marvels the redhead. "Tony?" Train responds, before taking a deep breath, trying to figure out where to go next. "How you doing Gabby?" "Wait..." says the lanky brunette, turning her glance from Train to her friend, reading the shock written all over her face. "Tony? You mean.... THE Tony?" She knows she should likely repsond to the question, but stands in silence, unable to muster a word. "Gabrielle?" asks the blonde, on the surface politely inquiring about an answer, but underneath demanding an answer. "Ummmm..... yeah, ahhh... Brandy, Angela.............. This is Anthony James. Most people... Most people just call him Train." Gabrielle says, her faces still reading some shock, but a growing, dumbfounded sense of happiness sneaking in somewhere along the line. "Tony.... This is Brand and Angela." she says of the blonde and brunette respectively. "Nice to meet yall." he says briefly addressing him, it being perfectly obvious they aren't of any real interest to him. "It's been a long time." he says, refocusing on what's important. "Yeah. Yeah it has." "Ok," Brandy says quickly leaping into the conversation. "Well it's obvious you guys have a lot of catching up to do so, we're just gonna head on." "Wait, wait guys," Gabby says. "This is our day out." "Gabby," Angela says not allowing anymore room for argument. "We can postpone for a day or two. "You two...you guys have a lot to catch up on." "But...." "Byyyyyyyye...." Brandy says, pulling Angela along gently as they walk away, quickly disappearing into the crowd. The city is still alive and bustling, but thee is a silence that dominates everything else as Tony and Gabrielle turn back to eachother. Their eyes meet. Neither has an idea what to do, but luckily it's instinctual...their smiles can't be controled. ====== ~ ===== ~ ===== ~ ===== ~ ===== It was a complete blur. If either of them had cared to ask the questions there wouldn't have been much in the way of answers how they'd wound up here. There was some small talk and chuckles as they traversed the streets of NYC, but it's hard to say who suggested they head back to Train's hotel room first. It had been years, but there was no denying the chemistry was still there the way it had been back in wIw... which means there's no real telling who put who's hand on who first. Quite frankly, it wasn't important to anyone in the room now, especially Train. All he knew was that it was good to be here again. Despite the passage of time it was still a familiar scene. His clothes and hers, strewn haphazardly across the room as passion lead them to strip eachother down to their souls. Now the only thing Train cared to focus on was her. Her nails scraping across his back... the feel of her breath on his skin as she moaned. The headboard banged out a beat as steady as any professional drummer could produce. She was soft and warm. She was comforting. She was a reminder. A reminder that he could be the underdog and still come out on top. She was a reminder that he'd amounted to something even since the begining. Her moans were his battle cry. The last few weeks had been daunting...she reminded him how much he loved to fight. ====== ~ ===== ~ ===== ~ ===== ~ ===== She was always angelic when she slept. He'd known that long before tonight. Long before now as sat watching her from the plush chair where he lounged now, just watching her silently. By all accounts it's never something he should have known, but he had. wIw.... World Internet Wrestling. Even though it wasn't Train had long thought of it as his first promotion. The first one where his work was seen before a few drunk jackasses in some high school gym or half delapidated national armory. He'd tried those a time or two, but why the hell would the make an serious habit of it. $25 bucks a night if your lucky. Barely enough to cover gas and a value meal for the local burger joint. That was a joke when he could earn a thousand times that in a night. But when wIw had come calling it was different. They had a television show. A loyal audience. A respected name according to all the wrestling websites he'd investigated them on. Stars that made real livings. Who earned real respect. They may have just been a blip on the national radar, but they were on the radar none the less. In his story he'd transformed it into his first promotion because it was the first one that amounted to shit. Train knew it and he'd taken full advantage. The established stars of course wanted to deny him any momentum, but that wasn't going to happen. Train had been too damn hungry to allow that. To those cats a tough fight was getting beaten bad enough to need a stretcher to leave the arena. Train didn't come up in an arena. Stretchers weren't shit. Now bodybags? Yeah...those were something. That was what it meant to be in a tough fight. They did to the hurt real bad. Train had done to the death. They had nothing for him. Most of them anyway. Every promotion had a top dog. An alpha male that could take the heat from anyone. It was the same with the streets. Back in Chicago for a long time that had been Train. It wasn't like that that in wIw though. There it had been a man that even had what it too to intimidate Train. Most times Train would have been embarassed to admit that to anyone under normal cirmucstances. But then... 7'0"... 355 pounds... You can describe someone like that in a lot of ways, but normal wasn't one of them. The man was a true to life monster. Or he could be when he felt like it. But most times? Most times he spent most of his effort, not on destroying opponents, but playing it up to the crowd. The intimidation factor was never completely eroded, but the fact was watching the man work...the intimidation was there...but the respect was not. The man was everything Train vowed he wouldn't be when he came into the business. He was unreal. And not in that, showstopper way. He was unreal in that everything he was, everything he tried to accomplish... it wasn't him. It was all motivated by some pussy ass desire to be liked. Not to say he didn't put on a show. Black and silver face paint... black leather wrestling gear with the big black duster... repelling in from the rafters. There was no doubt the fans loved it. Hell they should have...it was all formula. He puffed his chest out like he was the original, but there was more than one cat doing that same shit at the time. Even his name...the stage name that he just about insisted anyone and everyone call him in and out of the arena...there was nothing original about it. Just a nifty name to put on posters and t-shirts. The big, bad avenger from above... the dark one... Arch Angel. Train figured the loser had made everyone suffer, watching him kiss as much ass as possible, but there was one person who suffered more. There had always been this beautiful woman he'd kept with him. She was supposed to be his fiancee. She had the rock to prove it, but pretty much everyone knew she was part of the show. Everyone knew she meant more to him as a prop for the show and his "character" than as a person. That's why Train had made it a point to get to know her. They say the way to a man's heart was through his stomach, but when its' a prideful man? The quickest way is through his bitch. And that's all she'd meant to Train. A quick way into the champ's head. At first anyway. But, somewhere along the way...some way, some how... the gorgeous red head with the sparkling green eyes had gotten to him. In a way that made it worse. Every man has see a woman with a man that didn't deserve them. Every man has taken a turn down "If she was my girl" road. It was a long ugly trip though. Because every man knew, even though she cared about you, she was still stuck on him. Train knew that. But, as much as he hated it, he didn't let himself wallow in it. He did what he'd intended to do. It was an elaborate scheme. In the end Train and Gabrielle had spent a week alone in the Bahamas after staging a "kidnapping" where he dragged her away after drugging her. It had been a war long before that, but it turned into World War III after that. But in the end it was just like Train knew he would be. He was pissed...to pissed to think straight. Instead of being professional he made it personal. That was a mistake. The Angel had clouded judgement. Instead of just trying to beat him, he tried to hurt Train. That's how he lost the wIw Heavyweight Title to Train. The promotion didn't last much past that...financial problems and the normal reason a promotions goes under. But Train went out on top. And he considered it one of the biggest victories of his career. Not taking the title. On any given night anyone could have done that under the right circumstance. No, no, no... he took more than the title. Train hated the bastard and he managed to take his most prescious possesion he had... His pride. After moving on in the GTT4 tournament Train had come to believe it was a hell of a small world. Jerry, Train's agent, hadn't been with him back in the wIw days so he'd been damn confused when Train had raised such a stink about know the stats of the newest incarnation of Arch Angel he was to meet. For a moment he thought it was the restarting of a war. But, unless the man had shrunk a foot and gotten sick enough to lose about a 150 pounds of muscle, the war was going to remain ice cold. But still... stomping another Arch Angel... it was going to be a fun week in a tournament that, up to this point, had been strictly business. "An ass whuppin' for old times sake." Train chuckled softly to himself. Suddenly there was movement and the sound of rustling sheets brought him back to the future. Gabrielle sat up a little slanty eyed, using her arm to hold the sheets close to cover herself. "Hey." Train said. "Hey," Gabby responded softly, the hint of a grin on her lips reminding him of the old days. "What time is it?" "5:30." he stated plainly after glancing at the digital clock across the room. "Oh wow... I gotta go." Gabby quickly slid to the edge of the bed nearest the door, and more importantly, the biggest pile of clothes. She pulled the sheet along with her to keep herself covered as she tip toes her way towards her garments. "You gotta go?" Train says, playfully expressing his disappointment. "But we didn't even have a chance to talk yet. I wanted to catch up a little. I mean what are you even doing nowadays? How you been staying so damn fine?" "hahaha... Actually I told you all that. I'm designing fashion now. Just opened up a new store with the two girls I introduced you too. And I do yoga and pilates which is how I maintain my girlish figure." Gabrielle quickly gathers up the vast majority of her clothes and trapeses into the bathroom closing the door. Train stands and takes up a spot against the wall, directly across for bathroom. "So what you rushing off for?" "I... I just got something important I have to do, that's all. The life of a business woman, you know?" "So I ain't got to worry about Archie swining in through the window trying to kick my ass do I?" "haha, very funny. Christoper has long since retired from the repelling business. Wrestling too. He was never really the same after you took the belt from him. Besides that.... I dont' see much of him anymore." "Good for you. Bout time you saw the light on that punk. You deserved better." "Maybe Tony...that doesn't mean I still don't care." "God damn girl. Please don't tell you you're still..." "Tony, don't worry about me. If you want to help me, save the advice and dig my shoes out from underneath the bed." "Aight, aight..." Train trudges back over to the bed in the semi dark room. As he passes the night stand his elbow brushes past Gabby's purse. It's doesn't take much to send it flying and it's contents spilling out onto the floor. "Damn it" Train says to the inconvenience. He flips on the nearby lamp to provide the light he needs to retrieve the contents. All of it seems pretty standard for a girls purse. Some random coins... several tubes of lipstick... a compact... a couple of tampons that he'd normally rather die than deal with...nothing that stood out. Nothing save for one thing. It was a picture... a little girl. She looked mischevious in her little red dress and grin. She was quite as a button really, but it seemed obvious she'd be traveling in Gabby's purse. The girl was dark olive complected with insanely curly hair. It was perfectly obvious she was bi-racial. Train flipped over the picture and read the words "Brianne at 4." Hmmm.... Must've been a friends kid or something. It was a reasonable assumption... Only... right at that moment.... he could have sworn... He'd seen that little girl before. But where in the world would he have... Suddenly Train snaps to as the bathroom door swings open and Gabrielle emerges. Train quickly stuffs all the contents, save the photo, back into the purse and makes a quick swipe under the bed, skillfuly retrieving her shoes as she'd asked. He holds them up for her as she quickly crosses the room and takes a seat on the edge of the bed. "Thanks." she says, quickly taking them and siding the stylish high heel boots into place. She stands and Train hands up the purse as well. "So it ain't gonna be another 5 years till I see you again, right?" Train asks as he stands to meet her. "I hope not." she says with a warm smile. "I missed you." "Of course you did." he responds with a chuckle that only brings him a playful punch in the arm as a response. She pushes herself up onto her tiptoes as she leans in. Her lips slowly caress his for just a moment. "You be good Tony." "I'll think about it." he says with a grin. "Yeah right." she says with a smile. Train can't do much more than watch as she turns and heads towards the door. Gabrielle quickly grips the knob and swings the door into the room, stepping out into the hallway. She turns back for just a moment...pausing their eyes meet... They both hope this won't be the last time they see eachother...but somehow knows it will be. She shuts the door behind her and Train settles back into a seat on the edge of the bed. He opens his hand and the picture reappears. It's even beyond himself why he kept it. It was rather stupid to think it meant anything. There's no way he'd ever seen this child. It must have just been someone that looked like h.... That was it.... Looks like her.... Train leaps to his feet and scrambles back to his jeans that lay in the center of the floor. It only takes a moment to retrieve his cell phone. He flips it open and connects to the net...connecting to the site where he stores some of his important photos. If they're there they can never get lost. Pictures of belts and matches and friends...and family. Train nimbly uses the arrow buttons on the phones and navigates the photos of his family. He goes back...back...back in time. Back to when he was just a child. Back to just before his brother died. Back to when his sister Joanne was a young girl herself. It only took one picture from that era to understand what he recognized. It took only one picture to rock him to the core... Now he knew where he knew the little girl from. Joanne... Brianne. They were damn near twins. How in the hell was that possible? Hell...it was almost like they were relat..... "Oh shit."
