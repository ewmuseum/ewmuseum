Go Team VIAGRA : Vacancy or How the Bears Won -- Act 1

11 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-11 TONY DAVIS(As the Narrator): Previously, on what you didn’t but should have seen on Go Team VIAGRA… INT. HOTEL BEDROOM – MORNING [Tony Davis wakes up underneath his sheets. The sunlight outside shines in through the nearest window, focused directly where Tony’s eyes are. The sudden burst of light forces Tony to wince and rub his eyes for a few moments before he can take a chance to see his surroundings. And even that doesn’t help him, as he quickly puts on his best confused look.] TONY DAVIS: Where am I? [Tony looks over to his left, where he sees a young brunette woman, the one from the bar that he was trying to hook Flyer up with, lying next to him. He looks off to the side for a moment and then lightly nudges her while looking at the camera.] TONY DAVIS: Hey… who are you? EXT. STREETS – EVENING [Tony Davis has gone on to chase a power walking Flyer down through the streets of Hong Kong. The bright lights give them a rainbow-esq tint all over their body. It’s like Vegas, if Vegas were foreign.] TONY DAVIS: Jack. HIGH FLYER: I don’t care Tony. Find your own way home. TONY DAVIS: I didn’t steal her from you, honest. I didn’t! [Flyer sighs reluctantly and turns around to face Tony, stopping in his tracks. Tony almost bumps into Flyer, and then backs off slightly.] HIGH FLYER: You’re still a child Tony. You’re like six years old. TONY DAVIS: I wasn’t the one who said we should buy a hamster and a rocket launcher and "see what happens." HIGH FLYER: That was Poser. INT. GAME STATION – NIGHT [Five Eddie Scott Posers are seen playing Dance Dance Revolution, in a team vs. team type contest. Each one looks just like the other, except they’ve each adopted a color system it seems. Remember, Eddie Poser has already died twice on screen and has made mention of having quintuplet brothers who aren’t actually brothers but clones. On the other side of the DDR war are the locals of the area, including the one small girl who had beaten him earlier that day. He is currently sweating up a storm on the machine.] POSER: Hey, you’re pretty good at this. Let’s you and me buy some hamsters… I promise it’ll be explosi-VO. EXT. STREETS – EVENING [Flyer throws his arms up in the air and begins to walk away once more.] HIGH FLYER: We’re done Tony. TONY DAVIS: Fly… HIGH FLYER: DONE! **END OF PREVIOUSLY ON* -- FADE IN – INT. CONFESSIONAL BOOTH – UNKNOWN TIME OF DAY – UNKNOWN PLACE [Taking a drag off of his uncharacteristic cigarette, he tosses the remainder to his side in ignorance. He looks slightly worn, a bag under one of his eyes, which is strange even to him. High Flyer squints his eyes as if he had just woken up, and takes in a deep inhale.] HIGH FLYER: Listen. Kid. Sammy… [Flyer adjusts himself in his chair and looks directly into the camera.] HIGH FLYER: I’m going to kill you. [Shrug] And if that video tape bites me in the ass when I actually kill you and goes into evidence in a court of law, well then so be it. [Pause] I’ve had a lot of time to think lately, traveling on my own and battling this strange case of insomnia. [Flyer winces and rubs his eyes.] HIGH FLYER: I sure hope they aren’t related. Like… estranged cousins who accidentally marry. [Flyer pauses to get back on track.] You’re an overachiever, y’know? And overachiever’s are really annoying. You know they’re gonna lose, cuz they’re OVER achieving, but you can never exactly tell when. And honestly, that scares me, which is why I’m going to have to kill you. Just to be safe, if I wanna keep goin’ on this merry go round of ptc thrill rides, I’m gonna have to disembowel you with a spork or send "man eating" poodles wearing sweaters of dynamite on you. [Flyer pauses.] HIGH FLYER: Some would call that excessive. But I grew up on Looney Tunes cartoons and they played music on rib cages. Which, hello, so talented. Like any of you could even do that on a real xylophone… [RUN OPENING CREDITS] EXT. BEACH – PORTUGAL – DAYBREAK [It was an amazing sunset, bright colors lit up the sky slowly gaining ground on covering the entire nightscape. High Flyer stood on the oceans edge, looking out into the sea as he took a drag from his cigarette. Flyer then sighed, lowering his shoulders as he did.] HIGH FLYER: This is so cliché. The lonely man thinking by the ocean. I wanted to try it once… It doesn’t work. [Flyer turned away and walked back up to a main road. An old beat up Chevy Lebaron rested on the edge, and he quickly climbed inside. Panning out revealed two other people in the car. One of them is covered by a large cardboard box, cowering in near fear. The other, sitting shotgun, dressed in large bright yellow clothling, monocle sitting over his right eye socket. This man was EDWARDS: So, are we here to get you and Tony talking again or are we here to get you ready for Sammy Brown? HIGH FLYER: C’mon. I’d be ready for Sammy Brown before Sammy Brown was Sammy Brown. [Edwards hits a number on his phone, and places it next to his ear.] HIGH FLYER: Who are you calling? EDWARDS: [to phone] TONY! HIGH FLYER: [scolding] I will turn this car around! KLEIN: We’re in park. [Flyer grudgingly turns on the car and switches from park to drive.] HIGH FLYER: Why do I hate every one of my friends? EDWARDS: Yeah, you heard that? Tony said fuck you man. And I gotta second it. Klein? KLEIN: Just keep that cell phone away from me. EDWARDS: Ooooh! It’s the evil cell phone, owned once by Satan for a day before stolen from him by Houdini, who then crossed it back over to the material world in a Penn & Teller trick. [Edwards, while saying all this, waves the cell phone in front of Klein’s face. Klein immediately throws his box back down over his head and begins to literally shiver.] KLEIN: They’re just libertarians. EDWARDS: Yeah, that makes them evil. HIGH FLYER: I’m a libertarian. EDWARDS: And you’ve said yourself you’re quite the evil man. HIGH FLYER: When did I say that? EDWARDS: Was it before or after you blinded Poison Ivy. Oh wait, no, it was after you killed the second coming of Christ. HIGH FLYER: Deacon wasn’t dead. I just injured his shoulder or something. EDWARDS: Still. Evil. [Edwards flips down the visor above his head and begins to check himself out in the mirror. Also in the reflextion is Klein, still sitting in the backseat with a box over his head.] EDWARDS: I don’t even know why you didn’t pick us to be apart of your little road trip fiasco. I mean, it’s on television, this is a television show, and you don’t pick the future star of the zeros? I just don’t get you. HIGH FLYER: Calling yourself the star of the zeros won’t garner you much Hollywood attention. EDWARDS: That’s why I rented every billboard in the Los Angeles area. [Edwards immediately looks away.] EDWARDS: On an unrelated note, here’s your credit card back. HIGH FLYER: … I hate my life. EXT. HONG KONG STREETS – DUSK [All five Eddie Scott Poser’s exit the Game Station looking quite ruffled. Bloodshot and dreary, the main/original Edward Poser raised a cell phone up to view.] POSER: Huh. Twelve missed calls. [Poser hits a button.] POSER: I don’t want to go on Instant messenger. Take me to voicemail. [Poser does nothing, but then begins to yell in the phone.] POSER: VOICEMAIL. [Nothing happens. Poser gets angry and throws the phone against the nearest wall. He sighs in frustration, and then extends his hand out.] POSER: Okay, one of you clones should give me your phone. So I can check my messages. Alright? [One of them does so, and Poser begins to press a few buttons, after a few moments of seemingly being unable to even hold the phone in his hand, he tosses it once again into the wall, shattering it.] POSER: Alright, number three, gimme your phone. RED POSER: I thought I was number three. POSER: You’re number four. GREEN POSER: What number am I? POSER: Two. Mostly because I like Green the best. BLUE POSER: But uhmm… I was the second created by you. I look the most like your image. POSER: Which is why you’re five. I can’t have people confusing me for a clone of me. I mean, that’s just simple linguistics. YELLOW POSER: I’m scared. POSER: Of course you are, I tainted you with the color yellow. YELLOW POSER: Hide me. POSER: You know… [The original Eddie Scott Poser looks out of the city landscape.] POSER: We really shouldn’t be here anymore. Jack’s gotta be heading toward Portugal and I can’t get us out here with nothing but Jack Harmen credit cards and Edward Poser state identification. RED POSER: You have an ID card?! POSER: Yes. Because I am not a clone. You stupid stupid clone. [Just then, the three remaining Poser’s phones ring, and they instantly reach in and answer.] YELLOW/BLUE/GREEN POSER: Y’llo? [They each cover their phone piece and talk to the remaining two.] YELLOW/BLUE/GREEN POSER: It’s Tony… We’re WHAT?! POSER: Hey, one of me at a time. Yellow, you’re most scared, so you’re out of the picture. Because y’know, you’ll over react to everything. Blue? Yeah, you’re sad, so we can’t have emotions clogging the line of dialogue. Green? Tell me what’s what. [The real Poser places his hands on top of the Green Poser’s shoulders.] POSER: You are my favorite me. GREEN POSER: … Jack left. [All four of the other Poser’s look to their immediate left.] GREEN POSER: He’s gone man! HE’S GONE! POSER: … He… He left us here in Hong Kong? … [Poser pauses, and then his eyes go wide.] POSER: ALL OF THE POSERS ARE GONNA DIE!!! [Eddie sobs and falls to his knees, crying into his hands. Yellow Poser places his hand on Eddie’s shoulder, who quickly swats it away.]
