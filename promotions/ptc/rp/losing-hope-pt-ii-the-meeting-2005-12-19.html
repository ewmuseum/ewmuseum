Losing Hope, Pt. II: The Meeting

19 December 2005

Date: 2005-12-19 University of Southern California Athletic Department Athletic Dept. All scholarships will be withdrawn and money will be returned to the athletic department. You will not be able to return to the football program nor any other athletic program at this university for the remainder of your tenure at this university. This expulsion is the result of your violation of the NCAA student-athlete drug policy. If you consider that correct procedures have not been followed in this case or an unfair decision has been made, you may appeal in accordance with the procedures. You may wish to contact the regional office on the above phone number to discuss the appeal procedures. If you need an interpreter, please advise the regional office. Yours sincerely, [name withheld], Athletic Director ************* Prior to his landing in Columbus, Brian had called and arranged for a limousine to pick him up at the airport. After all, he had the money and the Hyatt Regency was one of the classiest hotels in the downtown area. If this meeting was to hold any significance, he would have to present an intimidating and dominant stature to his, shall we say, clients? He was pleased to find his driver waiting for him on such short notice when he arrived at Port Columbus International. One thing was still nagging at the back of Brian’s brain and he tried to repress it as best as he could and that was the fact that this had been the second time in the past few months that he had received a call from Columbus. He tried to focus on his near future but was consistently interrupted by thoughts of his father. The phone call in the early hours of the morning he received from the Riverside Methodist Hospital. Your father, James, has suffered a severe stroke. We need you here as soon as possible. The hospital Brian had passed many times visiting his Ohio relatives, was only five minutes north from the center of downtown Columbus. His father had died there days later and he had not been there for him. Instead he had returned to the south to participate in an insignificant wrestling match. Brian broke down that night. Returning to reality, Brian brushed aside any lingering thoughts of his father and attempted to calm his nerves by opening a bottle of wine in the back of the limousine and pouring himself a drink. He brushed off his recently cleaned Versace suit that he’d managed to slip into in an airport bathroom in a very James Bond-ish style. Seeing his reflection in the tinted glass, he couldn’t help but admire how dashing he looked and he slid on his trademark Prada sunglasses. The airport was a good fifteen or twenty minutes away from the city so Brian decided to pour himself another glass of wine in a second glass, and he sank back into the comfortable leather seat. Before he could place himself into the perfect position, his cellular began to ring. Half expecting it to be the mysterious man from the day before, Brian prepared his game face, but when he pulled out the phone he realized that the call was coming from the Primetime Central Head Office. They must be calling about my match. Brian flicked the phone open without hesitation. "Arson speaking." "Hello, Brian, this is Scott Jerkinson from PTC to inform you on your second round match." "I figured as much. Who you guys throwing at me now? O’Shea? You gonna let me at that skinny Irishman? I’ll knock the hops right outta him. Or maybe Deville? I could see you bastards trying to get me outta this thing early." "Well, actually Mr. Arson, Deville’s no longer in your bracket, he was defeated by Rich Rollins last round." "He WHAT?" "But don’t worry, he’s back in it, since he received one of the two play-in spots given out." "That son of bitch. Figures he’d get that kind of treatment. Second chances are for pussies!" He said snidely. "So are you going to tell me who I’m facing or not?" "Well, yes, I’m trying to get to it. You’re second round match up is with Doreen Pepperpot, winner of the Pepperpot/Hamada match." Brian spit out a mouthful of wine and began to choke. "Mr. Arson are you all right?" The Primetime Central spokesperson asked rather concerned. "Hold on," Brian gasped, "you mean to," he continued to choke, "you mean to tell me I’m facing that fat bitch?" Brian managed to control his gagging. "I think I just threw up in my mouth." "I’m sorry to hear that. And yes, you are facing Ms. Pepperpot. The match takes place on December 20, and if you have any questions feel free to call us. I wish I could chat longer but I have more confirmation calls to make, goodbye sir and good luck." Brian couldn’t believe his luck. A girl for the next round, and an amateur at that. The thought delighted him, yet at the same time he was repulsed by the fact that he’d have lay his hands on the mammoth woman. He never understood why they let these ladies into the tournament in the first place, considering their poor performances in the past, but judging by the looks of this Pepperpot gal, he wasn’t quite whether or not this was actually a woman at all. Well, that’s a load off my shoulders, he thought. Or maybe not. It was approaching eight-thirty and Brian believed nine o’clock was a reasonable time to conduct this unscheduled meeting. He once again started to consider what the purposes of the meeting could possibly be. Sure, he had been taken into custody and had his name and face broadcast all over the nation, but that was over a month ago now. Not only that, but the entire drug network that Brian was involved was based on the West Coast and never dealt with anything past the Colorado River. What does he want? He pondered. I’m sure it’s nothing. Soon, Brian would find out that it was far from nothing that this unknown caller was after. ************* University of Southern California School telephone number: 213-###-#### Regional office telephone number: 213-###-#### James K. Arson Brian Arson Dear Mr. James Arson, This is to inform you that today the school board of the University of Southern California has decided to expel your child from this school. This decision has been taken consistent with the procedures of the Department of Education and Training for suspension and expulsion from a particular school and this school’s discipline code. Your child will be expelled from this school only. Should you wish to enroll your child in another school, it is the responsibility of you and your child to arrange that enrollment. The decision to expel your child from this school has been made after his violation of school code regarding the use, possession, and intent to distribute illegal narcotics within school grounds. This expulsion may result in legal action should the authorities deem it appropriate. In accordance to school code for this type of violation, your child will also be banned for life from the University of Southern California campus. Should your child fail to comply, legal action will be brought forth. Your child will not be able to return to this school. I have previously provided a copy of the school discipline code and the Department’s procedures document on suspension and expulsion. I again draw your attention to the sections of the procedures on expulsion and appeals. If you consider that correct procedures have not been followed in this case or an unfair decision has been made, you may appeal in accordance with the procedures. You may wish to contact the regional office on the above phone number to discuss the appeal procedures. If you need an interpreter, please advise the regional office. Yours sincerely, President [name withheld] ************* The limousine driver opened the door for Brian, and he stepped out in front of the Hyatt Regency plaza feeling a little buzzed. The night life in the Columbus area was certainly buzzing with activity, especially in the famous Brewery District, established by the Germans in the 1800s. It had been a collection of family-owned breweries for decades, and they had just finished renovating it a few years ago with vintage buildings in order to pay homage to the German immigrants. The area was one of Brian’s favorites, and he smiled as he walked toward the lobby doors of the Hyatt Regency. Inside, the incredible lobby wasn’t too crowded as a few people were just checking in, while others sat by the blazing fireplace. In the center of the lounge was a giant Christmas tree dressed with a beautiful arrangement of ornaments. The hotel had a very modern feel to it while at the same time maintaining a rather classy atmosphere. Brian reached into his pocket and pulled out a slip of paper. Room 657. He folded it back up and slipped it into his pocket and headed toward the elevators. He was the only person around as he pressed the button to go up. He waited no more than five seconds when one of the four elevator doors opened. Two couples, dressed up for a night on the town, stepped out from the inside. The two women were gorgeous and smiled at Brian as they passed. Nodding to the group, Brian returned their smiles with one of his own and slid by them into the open elevator. Level 6, going up! Brian stepped out of the elevator and headed down the deserted hallway. There was not a single sound coming from any rooms, as if the whole floor had been abandoned and he was left feeling a bit spooked. Finally, he stumbled upon room 657 and rather than knocking, he placed the side of his head to the door. Silence. He hesitated before knocking and pulled the slip of paper out of his pocket once again to make sure he was at the right room. Sure enough it read room 657 and Brian just shrugged, approaching the door once again. But before he could knock, the door swung open and a giant of a man stood in front of him. He stood toe-to-toe with Brian, something most average men could not achieve, with arms the size of tree trunks, and no neck that Brian was aware of. "We’ve been expecting you," he grunted and turned back into the suite. The first room was practically pitch black, the only light coming from a crack in the door of the next room over. The large man opened the door and motioned for Brian to go in before him. He silently obeyed and stepped into the lit room where a man was sitting in a cushioned chair, going over a multitude of papers. This man had dark greasy hair with a matching mustache. Brian could tell from the way he was sitting that the man could be no taller than six feet, but his rough looking face presented an intimidation that Brian was familiar with in his dealings with very powerful men. The man looked up as Brian entered the room and smiled. "Ah, Mr. Arson," he said and Brian immediately recognized the voice as the one from yesterday’s call. "I was worried your arrogance would get the best of you and we’d miss you!" "Yes, well, I guess you’re not the best judge of character then," Brian grinned sarcastically. The man laughed and motioned for his partner to leave the room. The beast of a man left quietly, shutting the door behind him. "I’m assuming there’s many questions that you have for me. The foremost being, who am I?" He paused for a moment, grabbing a glass sitting beside him and took a drink. "Well, my name is Mikael Solokov, and I am going to offer you a little proposition." The man paused, making sure he had Brian’s complete attention. "You see, I have only lived in the states for about five years now, but I am very familiar with you and your… business… on the West Coast, and it interests me. So much so that I’ve decided to take it over for you." "Ha," Brian laughed. "Impossible. You could never buy it off me, I wouldn’t have it." "Oh, Mr. Arson, how naive you are. This is non-negotiable, in fact, whether or not you’ll allow it has nothing to do with this meeting. You see, you are the first person in your ‘organization’ to know any of this. Everything’s been planned out and negotiated. All of you on the West Coast are now completely out of the equation as I plan on spreading outward across the country with your network." "That’s impossible! We’ll stop you; your men would be taken care of before you had the chance to make a dime, assuming you’re not bluffing in the first place!" Mikael released a deep laugh that frightened Brian. "And that is where you come in handy, my friend! You will make sure your men stay out of this affair or I’ll have you arrested immediately." He pulled a manila folder out from beneath the loads of papers he held in his hand and placed them on the coffee table in front of him so that it faced Brian. Brian looked down at the folder quizzically, unsure what to make of it. "This folder here can make or break you, Brian," Mikael said. "Well, maybe just break you." He opened the folder and Brian gazed down at its contents. His eyes widened and jaw dropped instantly. The manila folder contained a plethora of photographs. Photographs of himself. Incriminating images involving him and well-known Central American drug lords as well as men that were already being held in the American prison system. Brian reached down and flipped through the photographs, each one slowly spelling out his untimely demise. "But…" Brian began. "Where… where did these come from?" "Remarkable, isn’t it? You’d think someone with such a well-known face as yourself would be a bit more careful with who he befriends." Mikael grinned sadistically and grabbed the folder, putting it back underneath the papers and setting them aside. "How does it feel to be on the other end of the chain, Brian? You’re finished. But look at the bright side, at least you can lead that pathetic little life of yours without having to worry about the authorities… or can you?" "You son of a bitch!" Brian rose from his seat and jumped across the table to attack Mikael. Just as he was about to punch the man, the large body guard came bursting into the room and grabbed him from behind. He struggled to get out of the man’s tight grip as Mikael rose from his seat, but the massive arms wouldn’t budge. Mikael drew a gun from the back of his pants and pointed it at Brian who immediately stopped struggling. "Oh, Mr. Arson. I’m sorry, I really am, but make this easy on yourself. Just walk away. Walk away, and you’ll have nothing to worry about." The big man led Brian out of the room and back to the front door. Mikael opened the front door of the room for him and the beast threw Brian into the wall across the hall. Mikael lifted his gun and pointed it at Brian as he began to turn around. "Just go, Brian. It’s over." The door slammed shut, leaving Brian alone in the deserted hallway once again. He was in complete and utter shock, unsure of what to do. He began to walk down the hallway and stopped at the next door, punching it in a fit of rage. "FUCK!" How could he have been so stupid? How could he have allowed this to happen? This must be karma, he thought. I DON’T BELIEVE IN KARMA! What was he going to do? He had just been cut off from his only source of income. He was in big trouble, real big trouble. Everyone he worked with, all those underneath him; he had compromised all of their lives by being careless. How was he going to explain this to everyone? They’d kill him. No longer was he important. They’d kill him. He simply wouldn’t tell them. They’d find out soon enough, but it would buy him some time. This isn’t how it’s supposed to be! He took the elevator down to the lobby and exited through the doors from which he’d entered. Head down with his overcoat on, he headed toward the river. Where he was going, he didn’t know, just so long as it was away from here. Something about this city… something about it just spelled doom for the wreck of a wrestler. It’s time to end this…
