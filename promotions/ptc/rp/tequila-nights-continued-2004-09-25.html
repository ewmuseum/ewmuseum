Tequila Nights Continued

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 “So there I was, bound and gagged being held for ransom by three of these bad ass banditos.” “How much did they want?” The elderly man asks. “Five million, and every day they didn’t get it I was gonna lose a finger.” “Did you pay?” The boy asks. “Let me tell the story, I’m getting to that part. So there they were, standing around like they were waiting for something, then out of the blue one says…” ”When we gonna start taking fingers?” A bald Mexican says with a grin. His powerful arms corded with thick muscles, twitching every time he turned over the twelve inch knife in his hand. “Dun touch heem.” The apparent leader chastised, his accent much heavier. Sweat glistened off the man’s brow, and his fingers twitched nervously every so often, as if fiddling with the triggers of imaginary guns. The third member of the posse, a slobbish looking man, his belly barely constrained by his dirty muscle shirt. Gripping a rifle in his left hand he parts the thin curtains covering a small window, the only window to be seen in the shack they used as a base of operations. The man said something spanish and lumbered towards the weapon cache as the door swung open. The seductive temptress that had lead Johnny into this situation to begin with stepped inside and closed the door behind her. She’d traded the skirt and dirty shirt for a pair of black pants and a leather jacket, she said something else Johnny couldn’t understand and backed towards the corner of the shack nearest where Johnny was restrained. Both hands behind his back and held firmly in place by thick rope that chaffed his skin, a filthy kerchief strapped across his mouth to keep him silent. Two thin trails of blood ran the length of his face, temple to chin, from the blow he’d taken earlier in the night. With heavy eyes he feigned disorientation and watched all three of his captors collect weapons, whatever was happening it was bound to be bad. The bald monster took a sawed off shotgun in his thick hands, but not before passing a pair of ranger long barrels to his leader. The clicks resounded as weapons were locked and loaded, and then a knock. A voice in spanish calls from the other side. And then again when none of the men inside respond, more urgent this time. Finally the leader responded and the door opened, he sounded apprehensive and shifted his weight from foot to foot. At the sight of the new comer, Johnny perked up to full senses; his wrists turning and working against his bonds. The sheriff of sorts took one look at Johnny and said something in spanish, he seemed to grow angry as the leader of the men responded. “I can’t believe you try to cut me out. This was my score!” The sheriff shouted and stormed towards Lexicon. “This sac of shit was my payday mutherfuckers!” “We took it first essay!” The leader raises both of his guns. “The federali was in on it?” The same boy asks. “That’s what I said” Johnny replies, wiping a cloth across his head and fanning himself with a magazine. “I was as surprised as you are.” “I’m not suprised homes.” All five of the people around him give a chuckle. Johnny looks at them incredulously and snickers. “Anyways, as I was saying. This was a tense situation, but this ‘federali’ was cool as ice, a grin on his face and his hands half raised. They started arguing in spanish, still gibberish to me of course, and then…” BOOM The door of the shack flies open and slams against the wall, a shot is fired and another, the lumbering mountain of flesh hits the wall and wails. The barrel of the shotgun spits its ammunition out the door, its wielder shouting and kicking over a table. Johnny franticly shuffles his chair as the federali suddenly ducks behind him for cover. The double pistol bearing leader fires off shot after shot out the door, his partner ducking behind a table and cocking his gun when another volley from outside slams into his shoulder and throws him to the ground. The leader turns his guns in Johnny’s direction and fires a shot, barely missing his head. Eyes wide with horror Johnny struggles even more and tips his chair over, as he falls the federali has his gun trained and fires a single shot. Head snapping back; the leader stands shocked for a second until his knees give out and he crumbles. Silence suddenly reigned in the wake of such chaos, the officer hesitated before creeping out from behind the fallen Johnny. A floor board creaked. A bush shuffled outside. The federali looked around and then stood to his full height and turned his gaze on Johnny and the cowering woman “Just like that eh?” “Just like that. If I had blinked I would have missed it. It seemed that everyone was dead except for the three of us. A trained officer, a bound and gagged wrestler, and the bitch that started the whole ordeal. I seen a glint in his eyes right then, these two knew each other, and the way they sounded when they started talking. Talking lead to arguing until they stood face to face and shouted…” By a stroke of luck, Johnny felt the ropes on his legs and gone slack. A leg broken in the fall allowed Lexicon to struggle first his left, then right leg. The man and woman argued on in ignorance of the wrestler as he turned over onto his knee’s and head, and shimmied into a crouched position. Hunched over with the chair still bound to his back, Johnny turned and charged in a backwards rush. Slamming into the federali and driving him into the wall, the chair shattered on impact. As the ropes around his shoulders and arms went loose, Johnny brushed them off and pulled the gag from his mouth. After reviewing his handiwork he searched the unconscious cops pockets and then laughed out loud in triumph. Raising the keys in the air. BANG! The shotgun muzzle exploded, shattering the calm and throwing Johnny’s heart into his throat. Diving out the door he scrambled into the night as another round tore the side of the door into tiny shards. The splinters showered around Johnny as he scrambled to his feet and ran, paying a passing moment to spy a dead deputy in the grass to his left. The white of the cops car made it easy to see by moonlight, crashing into the side Johnny jiggled the handle and found it unlocked. Pulling the door open he scurried behind the wheel, his hands shaking as he tried to find the key for the ignition in the dark. The passenger door opened and a form slid into the chair. “Get out.” Johnny snapped. “Take me wit you.” The woman replied. “I still didn’t know her name at that part, but it was just then that the right key slid into place and I turned the engine over. Again she insisted I take her, and when the shotgun round slammed into the back of the car I wasn’t in any mood to argue. I slammed on the gas and got the hell out of there as fast as I could.” “Thas some story amigo.” The old man commented. “Oh it’s not over yet. Call me a sucker for pretty faces, but I felt it was my duty as a man to at least drive her to the next town. We drove for a good couple hours and it wasn’t until the sun had started coming up that we came across a small town… The car pulled into the gas station and rolled up next to a pump. The desert sun already warming the air, preparing itself for the afternoon blaze, created a peaceful haze over the quiet little hamlet. A single old man rocked back and forth in a chair from across the porch, his face so wrinkled it was hard to tell if he watched Johnny and the woman climb out of the car or not. Regardless of what the old man saw, Johnny circled around to the pump and started filling the vehicle in silence. The woman crossed her arms and leaned against the door, looking at her feet and maybe summoning the courage to say what she was about to say. “Take me to America…” Johnny glanced at her and then looked away. “Fuck no.” “If you don’t, they will kill me.” “Your bed sister. You made it, now you lie in it. I’m leaving you here.” Again silence took over the pair, Johnny watched the meter roll over, but something cast a shadow over his expression. Perhaps the knowledge that his apathy would cost a life. He’s been the source of one life already in his past, could his conscience handle another? “I’m sorry…” She says. Johnny doesn’t respond at first, the gas handle clicks and he retracts it. “What’s your name?” He finally asks, putting the pump back on it’s hook. The woman smiles a bit, hope suddenly touching her eyes. “Maria.” She responds “Well Maria, do you have any money?” He face falls slightly. “N.. no.” “Then I suggest you get in the car.” Johnny walks back around the car as a Mexican walks out of his station. He calls something in spanish and Johnny bolts for his door as Maria shuts her own, reaching over and turning the key as Johnny settles into his seat and slams his own door. The engine already started Johnny pulls it into drive and floors the pedal. The car speeds out onto the road, leaving an angry man to wave his fists and curse. Maria laughs, turning around in her chair to look back at the man as Johnny maneuvers the vehicle out onto the highway one again. “I’m starving.” He comments. “What’s this.” Maria mutters and reaches into the backseat. “What did you find?” Johnny asks as she slips back into a sitting position, a leather wallet in his hands. “Must be that federali’s.” Maria says, opening it and pulling out a stash of bills. Maria grins and then laughs again. “So where’s this Maria now?” The Mexicans laugh a bit. “There’s no Maria.” One of the men says and waves his hand at Johnny. “Good story though.” The old man says. Johnny absorbs the feedback with a grin and a chuckle, still fanning himself languidly. “I thought the whole was over then too, but isn’t just my luck…” ”There’s somebody behind us.” “What?” Maria looks out the back as Johnny checks his mirrors. “He’s coming up fast.” Maria says and slides back into her seat, tugging on the seat belt. With a determined mask on his face Johnny presses down on the gas. The engine rumbles and the approach of the car behind them slows. It looked like an off white sedan, but it proved to have a powerful engine under the hood as it sped up even faster and pulled into the other lane. Eyes to turning to watch the car as it comes up beside them. Maria gasps. Johnny hits the brakes. The federali fires his shot a second too late. The tires squeal and smoke erupts behind Johnny’s getaway car. Their pursuer going on ahead and then hitting him brakes too. They pause, both cars stopped. As if measuring the other. Tires suddenly turn, squeal and catch. The vehicles lurch forward and then charge, both hands on the wheel Maria screams and covers herself. The gap closes fast, closer and closer death rocket before his very eyes, but Johnny held true. The other car swerves out of the way. Johnny lets out a cry of victory, Maria takes a breath and leaps over to the look out the window as the other car crashes into the ditch, smoke erupting into the air. “You almost got us killed.” Maria gasps. “Almost only counts in horse shoes babe.” Johnny looks at her with a grin. “Let’s get out of this fuckin country, I’ve got a match to win.” “Is this all true?” The elderly man asks. “It sounded good.” Johnny smiles and climbs to his feet. “Bah, I knew ye was full of shit.” The audience chuckles again as Johnny walks towards the exit. A shadowy form approaches from outside, and a moment later a gorgeous spanish girl, dressed in a thin pink dress steps through the door. “The bus is here Johnny.” Maria cocks her head to the side as Johnny steps up and kisses her quickly. Together they step back out into the sun and disappear.
