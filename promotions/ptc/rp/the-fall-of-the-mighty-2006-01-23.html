The Fall of the Mighty

Date: 2006-01-23 The Fall of the Mighty Number 6: What do you want? Number 2: Information. Number 6: Whose side are you on? Number 2: That would be telling. We want information. Number 6: You won’t get it! Number 2: By hook or by crook…we will. -The Prisoner ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Now, before anything else, time for penance. The cell is dark and windowless. It isn’t dark to the point where there is uninterrupted blackness, but it’s close. If you strain your eyes, you can just about see the outline of your hand, but you got to be squinting damn hard, I can tell you that. It’s dank and it’s gloomy, specifically made to send you round the bend. And even the strongest of minds has fallen to it’s wrath. The discrepancy, the loneliness…and the most daunting fact of all is undoubtedly that, though you can’t see the walls, they’re always watching you… With visibility very much reduced, other senses are heightened. Hearing isn’t one because there’s nothing; absolutely nothing. But touch, yes, that most certainly is intensified, and the cold cold concrete underneath just feels that much colder. The room wants you to be uncomfortable and uneasy; it wants you to be suspicious of the fact it may split in the middle and swallow you whole. But it won’t give you any indication, because it won’t speak a word. Did I say there’s no sound at all? Sorry, what I meant to say was there was no sound save the scratch scratch scratch as stone scrapes stone. Paddy lies on the floor of this asylum, not knowing day from night and not able to sleep. There is no sense in this place, just the bitter realization that soon they will come for their prisoner…and soon, they’ll take the things he stores in his head, the only thing that is truly the property of Paddy O’Shea. Scratch scratch scratch But in the meantime, he’ll just keep on writing. Where he is? He wishes he knew, they blindfolded him after he was taken off the plane. He was escorted to a car and taken away to here…into the darkness, where no one sees what they’re doing and no one hears what they say. So fresh from the wrestling match he had bargained for himself, there was no glory in his win, not when you know, as soon as the referee counts three, that your old way of life is about to disappear. Men of entered, stuttering freaks have walked back out. If they could even walk at all. They want answers, they want to know why his jacket was found near the site of a bomb explosion, they want to know why he did it, who sent him in. And if Paddy has no answers for these questions, hell, anything will do. And why? Because they’ll be revered as the men who brought down a terrorist Thing is, in the lonely cold of night, he’d swear that black was white, if they just let him lie down… And close his eyes… Right now, he’s a spider in their open palm. Comply with what they want, fuck the truth and all that, and they’ll put him in a jar as a pet, they’ll let him off easy…but dare try and escape from that palm, and they’ll stand on you, goddamnit. They’ll hurt you because if you’re not feeding the system, you’re an enemy, and woe be tide those who stand in the way. Paddy pauses from his writing for a second to wipe a tear from his eye. He’s scared, genuinely terrified and the big, bad boxer would have no qualms with telling you that. He can’t remember what he’s wearing now, it’s been so lonely and long, but if he keeps writing, maybe all this will be over. Maybe. Be a man Paddy, you swore you’d be strong "Aye can’t," he whispers. "Jesus Christ, please let this be over…" Then he’s writing again, allowing his hand to write of one thing while his mind thinks of another…why is the most obvious question. Why do they hound him? Did he actually plant a bomb and wasn’t aware of it? Is he a random that can be their scapegoat? Or is he the scapegoat, they want specifically sent down? Remember ‘88 Paddy… "No…" Remember it Paddy. Remember the IRA that was your IRA. Remember your bombs, maybe this wasn’t your bomb but there was others. "Aye didn’t bomb people, aye bombed soldiers who brutalized us without compassion. We took back what was ours, what they weren’t willing t’ give. Aye never bombed innocent people…aye was a son of Eire, fightin’ for Eire." Eighteen years Paddy,,,but they finally got you. For one thing or another, the perpetrators get pinned to the wall Paddy shot up to his knees and punched the wall so suddenly, his knuckles bust with a thin squelch. He felt no pain. "AYE DID MY DUTY, THE THINGS AYE HAD T’ DO! NOT THIS, NOT WHEN AYE’D WALKED AWAY FROM IT!" Paddy’s shouts cut right through the air like an arrow and it echoed several times. Then he fell back onto his side breathing heavily and frantically as he exhaled with a shudder. He felt his cheeks blaze red with tears. "Aye don’ deserve this…please…aye don’t deserve this, aye’m sorry." Then another noise enters the picture. The sound of faint footsteps and Paddy’s eyes bulge in fear as his ears detect them. He crawls into the corner, forgetting the writing, feeling his way around a bed he almost forgot was there, and then huddles into a ball. The footsteps grow louder and Paddy’s breathing becomes more distinct as he rocks back and forth. He has to rock for fear of being completely apeshit. "No…please no." The door is suddenly thrown open and the light that enters the room blinds Paddy as he holds a hand up to distinguish who is there. The man before him, is unflinching for what seems like five minutes. As Paddy’s eyesight begins to adapt to the conditions, he sees the man before him is dressed in a suit. An indicator he is one of their specialist’s at this sort of thing. He seems to be smiling at one side of his mouth as his eyes scan the room, taking in the writing that is all over the floor and three quarters of the way up the walls, a desperate scramble to cover as much of the room as possible. In the darkness, many sentences have overlapped but the message is still there. He loved nutbars, they didn’t break so easily, it meant he got to punch the fucker around for a lot longer. The man whispers the sentence that is recurring all around him. "I am sorry……yes boy…you will be…you will be." Paddy rocks forward as he feels his eyes slide out from their sockets further, the image of that dastardly face, burning into the back of his head. The man only stares and stares and stares…then Paddy breaks. "AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! AHHHHHHHHH! AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!" The walls dance with the sound, throwing it back in Paddy’s face. The man’s smile widens into a full grin as he slowly closes the door. Slowly cutting out the beam of light. Back to the dark…where no one sees what they do. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Man cries, in the early morning. Woken by the sound of a gun." -Christy Moore ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Paddy." Danny O’Shea bolted upright in his bed. The piss stained duvet that had been tucked around his neck was now philandering around his waist, and began to ripple and snake as his chest flared in and out. He felt out of breath, as if the room had been a vacuum and he was sucking the last few morsels of air into his tar stained lungs. Danny wheezed horribly, and rolled onto his back in a slow motion for fear his back would cave in on itself. He was a broken man, what with years of deteriorating physical condition, a certain product of the cigarette and alcohol habits. He was broken on the inside too. His children and wife had long since abandoned him leaving him with the conscience that had only spawned with their departure. And it was difficult. Look at this feckin’ kitchen women! he heard himself say. IT’S LIKE A FECKIN’ PIGSTY! He thus proceeded to beckon to his wife to come over for a nice little chat with his knuckles. And his children, don’t even start on the children. Whether it was pinching baby Shane for crying too loud or putting young Patrick through a glass pane in the caravan window for looking at him in the wrong way, he had been brutal. It was bound to happen. The day his wife found blood in her urine from all the kidney shots. The day she woke up from her daze of fourteen years of abuse. The day she packed her suitcase. The day she took the remaining O’Shea brood living with her to her sister’s in England, leaving the unsuspecting Danny in the bar, drinking to whatever could be drunk to. Oh what twisted logic it was that his conscience was born into the world after they left. For a while he searched for them, phoning anyone who would listen or know but as the realization of the things he had done finally struck a nerve, he realized the cruelest thing he could do was to ever discover where they lived. And so he went back to a life absent of family. Wake up, eat, drink, sleep. A life less ordinary, no. A life absent of inflicting abuse, yes. He would leave them to their lives, hoping beyond hope that they might somehow see in themselves there was a possibility he was a different man. He was the one being abused now and there wasn’t a moment that passed by where he thanked the Lord in heaven for making him hurt everyday. At least that meant he was on penance. Only today, something was different. From what dream he had been revitalized, he did not know; he did not remember faces nor could he recall even the rough concept. It was with a sense that could not be classified as seeing or hearing, that something came and spoke to him. It was like a deep echo that reverberated instead of spoke. It was the face of his son in everything he saw. Danny is staring now at a picture on the far wall. Even in the murky haze that shrouded the room, he could see it clearly in his mind. It was his son, it was Paddy O’Shea. And as the reverberation corseted again through his mind, he shuddered as unadulterated dread washed over him. His son, Paddy, was in the most extreme pain of his life. Problem is solved by solution and this was answered like clockwork. Despite his promise to stay away, his son needed him whether he knew it or not. And by Jesus he was going to help him. Danny rose from the bed, a damp sweat still clinging to him, in a trance. Picking at a heap of clothes that was nestled between a pizza box and a broken glass, he extracted the driest attire and proceeded to pull them on ruggedly, his feet lifting from the floor like Velcro as the sticky carpet begged him to stay. Where do you think you’re going? Stop where you stand, it’s useless to even think like that. How DARE you even consider the idea of going looking for the son you brutalized. Do you think he wants to see you? NO! Just stay here, it’s not like you can go on a mission drunk anyway Danny didn’t blink. "Aye don’ need beer." As Danny hopped about on one foot, skillfully trying to pull a sock on while avoiding the mini puddle of some brown shit you can keep the hell away from me, a soft rumble ran through his feet. AHAHAHAHAHA! Thirty five years, non stop, and you just stop. Yeah, yeah, keep on kidding yourself. You’ll reach the end of the road and run out of steam. Then you’ll come crawling back to me! Danny paused for a second then found himself whispering. "Aye’ve forgotten when was the last time I seen him". He smiled softly. "It’ll be nice t’ see him again." The carpet was growing frustrated, he was determined, he was going. He couldn’t just walk away, just like that. He was supposed to be indoctrinated into the generic way of thinking - the ALCO way of thinking. Just stop now. You don’t even know where to find him. You’re only wasting valuable drinking time, that liver isn’t going to crawl out of your ass on it’s own! While sitting on the edge of the bed, Danny started to pull on a pair of tattered loafers, his eyes now affixed once again on the picture of his son. Instinct…he would find him by instinct, do you believe in things like that, things science scoffs at? Or do you have faith in what you can’t see because conveniently, everything works out just fine? Danny was the former, now he’s the latter. Go figure. Danny jolted up from the bed, suddenly and with such force that the bed banged against the wall. He had to go, for his son, for the lost times. He had to do this. Even if he hated him, at least he wouldn’t be alone in what ever he was facing. Unless of course he was too late. Danny tried not to think about that scenario and quickly made an exit for the room. No! Wait! Stay for a drink at least, something to get your engine going before your journey. Danny slowed down to a stop and bowed his head. Just one last drink? He was ready to give the whole thing up in that instance, alcoholism working against him on the side of cowardice. Then he recognized it, he recognized the offer as a ply to keep him there. He would drink, and before long it’s sleepy time and it’s the next day and his son has been blown away like dust in the wind. Danny closed his eyelids as he spoke, saying words he never thought he would say. "Not this time chief…aye’ll see ye again." His head down, his shoulders thrust forward, Danny walked through the archway of his bedroom slamming the door and subsequently putting a lid on that stupid voice. He traveled the few meters to the caravan door, opened it without pause…and stepped out. The dreary morning that had previously been indicated, was no more. Now there was sun in the sky and a twitter in the air. His shadow stretched along the pathway leading up to the small country road that would take him into the village. He stood for a moment, merely sucking in the air with glee. He had done it…he had put family before beer, it wouldn’t bind him again, so help him God. "Aye’ve woken up Paddy," he said. "And aye’m comin’ son…aye swear aye am." Train his mind whispered and that was good enough for him. He took the first step and then the second. All were traveling in the direction of his future. The thing was, Danny wasn’t fifty metres down the road when the ring of a phone came from his caravan. Danny didn’t hear it and walked on. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Comon…comon ye bastard." Paddy foot taps the ground frantically. Pick up, he’s telling the phone, the phone is deaf to his pleas. The line cuts into a steady drone yet he holds the phone, trying to postpone the inevitable. The receiver is then removed from his hand and put back on the phone and suddenly he’s back facing them all. He sits on a wooden chair and everywhere there are eyes staring at him. Four men stand against the walls to his left and right, one at the door and two across the table from him. The room smells of piss, the walls are suspiciously brown. Misery, it was with that that this room was created. Paddy fumbles nervously with his thighs and he rubs them slowly, rocking back and forth, back and forth. He looks at the people around him, arms crossed, steely gazes. You will give us what we want we say they say. Paddy looks at the two men in front of him. The one to the left, his strawberry blond hair, wears a frown. His eyes are piercing right into the back of his head. The one on the right is also sickeningly calm, his black hair and beard also covering an inalien cold glare. And still they just stare, and still Paddy looks away, risks a glance, looks away, rubs his thighs, rocks back and forth, and looks at them again. Staring until Paddy thinks he’s going to explode. "Aye didn’t do anything…aye didn’t do anything," he whimpers, as he looks up once again. "Ye have t’ let me go, aye didn’t do anything". And still they stare. "Nothing…really, aye did nothing, aye was at a friends house when it happened, aye didn’t do anything, ye have t’ let me go. It’s illegal, ye can’t", Paddy sniffs and rubs his nose. The buzzing in his muscles and joints increase as he continues to rock in short jerky arcs. He had to rock, the tingling in his muscles demanded he do so because that meant he wasn’t staring like one of these sick bastards. This meant he was fucking different, he was innocent! "You fucking…terrorist," the blond haired man said, venom being spat. His mouth drew into a tight round shape. Paddy said nothing, his words had fallen on death ears. The man continued to sit and stare but the bubbling of anger in his face was inhuman. "You….you fucking scumbag…" He spoke so softly. Paddy closed his eyes, and tried to think of other things and beat the interrogation. There was no windows, looking at a wall wasn’t going to do him favors, so he had to think. He had to pray. Hail Mary, full of Grace "You want to see the people you killed from the bomb, I’ll fucking show you!" the thick English accent said to him, infiltrating his thoughts. His eyes still closed, he felt a fist slam against his temple, he groaned but kept his eyes closed and tried to keep focused. He had to beat this. But already his head was throbbing. Two hands on each side grabbed his arms and held them apart while another pulled his hair and another slapped his cheek. Bless Art Thou Amongst Women "Fucking look at what you’ve caused!" He felt his eyelids being pull apart and the first thing he saw was a decapitated body, bedding a pile of rubble. "No! Aye didn’t do it," Paddy screamed. They wouldn’t believe him. The man with the beard flicked a page over revealing another picture. It was of a child strung over a telephone box, dried blood covered half of her face. And Blessed Is The Fruit Of Thy Womb, Jesus "You murderer, you killed these people," a voice from his right screamed. Somebody grabbed his crotch and squeezed hard. Paddy let his bladder go. "Aye didn’t do it ye fucker, let me go." They didn’t like that. Punch from the right and left sandwiched his head and he felt himself losing consciousness. They didn’t like that and so started to tackle his stomach. There was two holding his arms, one holding his hair back, two holding pictures and screaming at him, and three hitting him where they could. He couldn’t move, where would he run. "Look at this you bastard!" They forced a picture of a Father holding a dead baby in his arms. He was crying and so was Paddy. "It wasn’t me," he moaned with a dry whisper. God hates a liar they decided and drilled a baton into the top of his foot so hard he thought he would throw up. Another pulled his ear whilst punching him in the neck where it hurt the most. And he continued to scream. Holy Mary Mother of God "Jesus. Jesus Christ! Aye didn’t do this!" Old blondie lost the plot at that stage as he head butted Paddy in the face. Paddy felt his nose crunch. Someone hit him in the foot again with a baton before he was thrown to the floor and boots were planted into his gut. Pray for us sinners Paddy scrambled into the corner holding the back of his head in the best way he could. The boots rained in and he dared not look up for fear that would be the last thing he would see through those eyes. "Murderer! We’ll crucify you for this" Now and at the hour of our death Paddy felt his body give way and his arms slowly dropped to his sides, so badly beaten that his face and arms wouldn’t be the same for weeks. Then he was kicked square on the forehead forcing his head back into the wall. That’s when he slowly faded to black. Amen That was just a taster he heard them say, as he drifted away. They have him for three days. By Jesus, three fucking days. By hook or by crook… ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ We all got crosses to bear baby…the question is, are you going to pick yourself up when you fall? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
