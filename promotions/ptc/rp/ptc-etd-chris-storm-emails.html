You would never expect Karina Wolfenden to have a natural hair color. Moreover, you would never expect to see Chris Storm in a hardcore, technical void, match. Expect the unexpected. No, Deville is not wearing women’s underwear. He might, after all no one has ever asked if he does after all, but no, I digress. Chris Storm is competing for Primetime Central’s Extreme title. The Last Great Technical Wrestler is about to go hardcore and I was there to witness it. The day seemed as ordinary as any other did. Sun, birds, people, all did as they do in life. The sun shined, the birds sang, and the people passed me by absorbed in their own existence. The gentle rays of the sun brushed against the earth warming it despite the bitter winds that blew through the skyscrapers and office buildings. At a comfortable fifty-six degrees, it was a much-welcomed change from the snowfalls of recent memory. More so, when you are homeless as I have found myself. I am indeed homeless. I am the man on the corner you pass by each morning trying not to make eye contact with. You pull your children away from me like I am some diseased animal. But I do not fault you, I sadly was just like you before I found myself in this state of misery. Now I have no home and no family. It has been so long since someone has called me by my birth name that I have almost forgotten it. Those who know me simply refer to me as Arizona. Feeling rather bored on this sunny and windy day I ventured off to speak with my good friend Chris at his New York apartment. If anyone in this world could keep life from being too repetitive, it was Storm. There was just something in his personality that made him ever changing, never able to simply sit still and take in life. Instead, he was always too busy trying to grab the horns and fight. Maybe that was just the way he was. Allow me for a moment to talk about my good companion Chris Storm. An odd character is he. It is almost as if he lives in a completely different world from the rest of society. A world filled with energy and power, ever moving and ever changing. If one were to try to dissect his mind to figure out just how he thought I wager it would take a lifetime of research and you would be no closer to the answer than you were a moon’s time ago. Approaching the building where he resided I greeted the old grey haired door attendant. Mr. Jones smiled and kindly opened the door. Waves of cool crisp air rushed into the streets, wrapping my body as they went. The apartment complex was far from the most lavished of New York City but the rustic feel was a more than welcome change from the bitter conditions of the streets. Moments later, I found myself at his door on the third floor. After enough months of friendship, I felt comfortable enough to let myself in through the unlocked door. I was welcomed, as usual, with his all too well known voice. “Bitch! Get the fuck out of my apartment. There isn’t enough liquor in the world to get me drunk enough to sleep with you so you must have broken in. Now get the fuck out before I call the god damn cops!” Not surprisingly, a young woman, maybe in her mid twenties, came hurrying down the hallway and past me out the door. A bundle of clothes in her folded arms was tucked over her chest. The sounds of footsteps approached. Turning my head, I spied Chris walking toward the kitchen adorned in only his white smilie face boxers. Taking a moment to scratch himself where I would have rather not seen him scratch, he looked over and finally noticed me. Sadly, that still did not cause him to stop scratching himself there. “So who was that?” I asked with my eyes turned away. Chris shrugged his muscular shoulders and sat down at the kitchen table. “How should I know?” he stated with little care. I pulled out a chair and joined him at the table. Chris reached across the table and grabbed the box of cereal, Trix his favorite brand. With a light smile I replied “I just thought you would at least learn her name before you slept with her.” Chris merely grunted and reached back grabbing a bowl. “When has something stupid like that ever stopped me before?” It should not be surprised to learn that Chris’ love life left much to be desired. To say he was a Romeo to the fair Juliets would be a bit of a stretch. “Oh right I forgot you don’t save manners” I chimed. Some would be offended by such a comment but with an individual such as Storm, that was actually one of the nicer comments he receives. Milk flowed onto the dried fruit shaped cereal, filling the bowl until it nearly spilled over. “Damn right, manners are for pussies and hippies.” He sounded proud when he shouted it out as bits of chewed up mush flying from his mouth. Chris placed a sheet of paper on the table for my viewing, a flier of an upcoming PTC event. It seemed Primetime Central was going to be holding an Extreme Battle Royale. Sixteen men would battle it out for the right to face off against Shadow of WMW for the Extreme championship, bypassing the ladder system normally used to choice contenders. Chris took no more than one glance at the flier before lifting his bowl and tipping it back letting the milk and remaining cereal to slide down his throat. Placing the now empty bowl back on the table, he wiped away the milk mustache “I’ve entered myself into this little shebang. You’re looking at the next Extreme champion of PTC.” “You’re retired,” I reminded him, “besides since when have you been a hardcore wrestler? I thought you were strictly technical.” This was exactly what I meant when I said the man was ever changing. One moment he would spout out about how he was the greatest technical wrestler alive and the next enter into a hardcore match. Placing the bowl in the sit Chris looked over his shoulder at me. “When have I not had a plan?” My loyal friend of many years had a point; he was never one to be stumped by a problem. “My UWF contract was bought out but with a clause. If either Hayes or I have an idea for me I’ll work a few shows for the UWF. Therefore, I can represent UWF in the Battle Royale. Not that I will be proud to boost their banner, but when one needs to travel across the mountains one may need to ride a donkey.” Indeed, it looked as if he had found the loophole. However, that was merely the first part of the equation. “That still doesn’t explain how ‘Mr. Suplexes You’ would enter himself into a hardcore match where technical wrestling is thrown out the window.” A spark in his ocean blue eyes, like a drifter seeing that saving ship through the mist, “that is exactly right!” he exclaimed. Chris placed his index finger on the flyer over the pictured wrestler’s eye. “These sorts of events are completely devoid of any form of technical wrestling right?” “Yeah…” I started to speak but he interrupted me. “And I am single handedly the greatest technical wrestler of all time, right?” He leaned in further toward my face as he awaited the answer. Truth be told I had no idea where this all was leading to but regardless it seemed to make perfect sense in his odd mind. “Well I…” “Exactly!” he shouted before I could finish what I was going to say. “That is why I must bring technical wrestling to that garbage wrestling! If I can win the belt, think of the matches those talent void hacks would have to compete it. Ironman matches, submission matches, even better yet an Ultimate Submission match!” He spoke with such enthusiasm, each word soaked with meaning. Looking up to him as he spoke I could not help but smile. He maybe an asshole and he maybe one of the most annoying wrestlers on the planet but one could never say Chris Storm was not a man of passion. Every activity he threw himself into he put forth one hundred percent of him into it. I rose from my chair. “So now you are some type of hardcore savior?” I asked him with a hint of sarcasm. The sarcasm was not aimed toward him but rather toward the very idea of hardcore wrestling needing a savior in the first place. Chris glances out the window to the hustling city below. Without turning back to me he continued on his train of thought. “Extreme wrestling is a disease. It is the cancer of wrestling. Countless talents shorten their careers and lives just to hit each other with a chair. However, once I win the title I will get rid of Primetime’s Extreme division and save them. So yeah…” he looked over his shoulder revealing his sly grin “I am their savior, whether they like it or not.” That was Chris Storm, the self-proclaiming savior of hardcore wrestling. It made me wonder if the likes of Peter Maxwell or Peter Vetra even wanted him as a savior. Most likely not but that would not stop Chris Storm from trying anyways. I sized up Chris Storm. He was still dressed in only his white boxer shorts. His black hair with the blonde top and spikes stood up perfectly, one could not tell if he had just spent hours on his hair or just woke up. At six foot four and two hundred-fifty pounds, Chris was large enough to take on even Blind Rage in the Royale. But against the likes of Wesley Williams, Kris CarMicheal, and Tony Stecchino, Chris Storm would easily stand out like a sore thumb. “You know,” I said as I started to prepare my own breakfast. “If you are going to do this you may want to think about changing your looks a bit.” Opening the refrigerator I pulled out two eggs, which carefully tucked between my fingers, and a slice of ham. Chris placed a hand on the counter and leaned on it, staring at me blankly he asked, “what the hell are you talking about? What is wrong with my look? Is my hair messed up?” Franticly, as if it was on fire, Chris ran his fingers through his hair making sure each strand of gold was in perfect condition. I chuckled to myself over his public display of femininity. Placing the skillet on the stove, I cracked the eggs and placed the yolks beside the ham in the skillet. The fire crackled to life. “When you compete you dress as if you are in some high school gym getting ready to wrestle for state, certainly not suitable for a hardcore match. Your flesh would get ripped to shards.” Shivering at the idea Chris clearly did not fancy the idea. He was a man of great narcissism if one could not tell by now. He valued his physical looks at such a high price that it warped his personality in such an animated way. Quite useful for a laugh I might say though. The sweet aroma of eggs and ham filled the apartment dancing gleefully under my nose. When you are forced to find the majority of your meals from inside a dumpster even something so common as eggs and ham becomes a rare delicacy. Chris lifted his nose to the ceiling and gave out a snort. “I was a former WIW Hardcore champion if you remember correctly. I can handle this garbage.” Flipping the eggs and ham, I looked to him. “Do you not think many of the others have been Hardcore or Extreme champions of their own leagues? Looking over the competitors list though some may be unknown to you but, I can assure you this, there is a list of former champions from various leagues in this royale.” “And what does that have to do with anything?” Chris bellowed. “In case you forgot Arizona, hardcore wrestling is nothing more than a pathetic attempt by untalented idiots to be successful. Look at that Extreme Championship Wrestling organization. They buckled under the real wrestling organization.” Indeed the man had a point. Looking through a realistic point of view one had to admit hardcore wrestling has had little success against serious competition. ECW fell to the mighty WWE. Even the WWE itself could not sustain a hardcore division. That to hell as the people cheered for the likes of true wrestlers. Chris, in his own mind, was one of these true wrestlers. Sliding the eggs and ham from the skillet to a plate, I sat back down at the table to enjoy my first warm meal in two full days. “I tell you what Chris. If you are serious about being in this Extreme Battle Royale than I have someone in mind that will help you prepare for it.” Chris looked at me with a raised eyebrow. “And just who would that be?” “I have more than enough planning to do for now. I will be back tomorrow and you will see. Deal?” I extended my hand out to him. Chris cautiously accepted and shook my hand. “We’re going to make you a hardcore legend Chris.” The very mention of the word ‘legend” had Chris smiling as he rubbed his bare chin. “This is money in the bank. Money in the fucking bank.” Though he was not the best educated of the crowd the man still quite possibly had enough talent to pull this out. Most definitely, his unique style of technical wrestling and vast knowledge of the suplex would play a major factory in the outcome of this match. However, for now I had preparations to make. Give me a week and that man will be the next Hardcore Icon. Looking down at his eyes, I saw a compassion there that I must have missed in the past few years. On the other hand, maybe it never was there before. I pulled my vision away from him, feeling to ashamed to even look him in the eyes now. “Look at me” I said holding my arms out to the side allowing him to get a good view of my full figure. “I am a bum, I live on the street! My meals come from a dumpster. Why would someone like her ever love someone like me? However, you…you have it all. You are talented, handsome, and well off in life.” “That’s why I did all this. The second I found out you wanted to be in the Extreme division I formulated how to get you two together.” My plans were revealed. It had been a set up from the word go. “I knew you would not change anything about yourself. You are too full of pride to ever conform to any stereotype of hardcore. Nevertheless, I knew it would be my best chance to reunite you two.” Both my hands fell on his shoulders. “You love her and always have. It did not matter if it was when she was married to Gaz or when you two fought backstage in Rush Pro. Looking in your eyes now I would say you love her more now than you ever have before, more so than when you first laid eyes on her. Moreover, she loves you too! She never stopped.” There was a loud gasp. Chris and I both looked to the bedroom doorway where Tessa stood. The clothes that were once in her hands now laid on the floor in a small pile. I did not know just how long she had been there or how much she had heard. Chris opened his mouth to speak, to say something to her, but like a scared rabbit, she quickly dashed past us and went out the door into the hall. I could not blame her for her reaction. This was a sudden load for her to carry. Not only did she have the admitted love of the man she had always treasured but also a man she knew little to nothing about outright confessing his love for her. To think what went through her head I could only imagine. Chris stood there like a statue, frozen in time. I slapped him on the back of the shoulder awakening him from his gargoyle slumber. “What are you waiting for? Go to her!” I ordered him pointing toward the direction she fled in. Chris gave me a nod and hurried after her. Within moments, the door shut and I was left alone in Chris Storm’s apartment. I do not know what happened between the two after Chris left after Tessa. Maybe he caught up with her and confessed his love once more. The two reunited in a passionate kiss living happily ever after. Maybe he never caught up with her. She fled to quickly leaving nothing but dust for him. Here with my thoughts alone in this world I stood. This or that did not affect me. My bones would turn to dust in time and my memory slowly fades from this world. On the other hand, just maybe a piece of Chris Storm would endure against the erosion of time and survive. If he won this Battle Royale, he would move on to face Shadow. His legacy would grow and spread. I cannot live forever. I know and accept this. Chris, however, has the chance. He must reach up and seize it. As for me, the pizza shop just a few blocks from here should be dumping their leftovers in a few hours. I may just give them a visit. Leave the Schmoopies antics to Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor.
