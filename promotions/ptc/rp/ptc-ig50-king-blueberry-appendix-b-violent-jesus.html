Darth von Doom versus King Blueberry

4 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(King Blueberry) Appendix B :: "Violent Jesus" Appendix B :: "Violent Jesus" Anyone who thinks that I’m an unusual guy needs to take a serious look at some of the company I keep. I don’t have the same kind of relationship with Lloyd Sullivan that Merv’ does, but nonetheless I’d say we get along okay, or about as okay as you’d expect to get along with a half-crazy oaf who looks like Jesus and acts like, well, Jesus at age 4. The story with Lloyd is that he used to work for a software company writing code in a cubicle all day. From what I gather he was a religious guy, but not psycho-religious or anything. I guess his company was going through some layoffs and Lloyd was one of the poor folks who got the axe. When he went home that afternoon he caught his wife cheating on him with one of the neighbors, and his brain just sort of went kablooey and replaced itself with a potato. Personally, I don’t blame him. While I haven’t had that whole wife-bangs-the-guy-next-door experience, I’m pretty sure that if I did I’d want my brain to become a starchy vegetable; preferably a yam. Yams rule. We sort of bumped into Lloyd by accident on a run through California in the late months of 1999, and he’s been with us ever since, traveling with us from city to city and living at Merv’s when not on the road. We call him “Violent Jesus”, mostly because he looks like Jesus, is known to burst into the occasional tirade, and is awesome when it comes to breaking shit. One time he also transformed water into Kool-Aid. It seemed enough like a miracle to us. It’s because of their weird bond that when it came time to let Lloyd know what the plan was (and that I’d be dressing like a blueberry) that Merv’ did most of the talking. If you think explaining “King Blueberry” to a rational person was tough, try watching a legit genius explain it to a legit potato-brain. “Lloyd, do you remember when we used to travel from town to town and put on shows in front of everybody?” Mervin was always great at this sort of thing, explaining it like you would to a child without being too condescending. “Eugene wants to dance on Broadway!” Here’s the thing about Lloyd: ever since a tour through Minnesota he never spoke for himself as himself, if that makes any sense. See, there was one afternoon when we kind of lost him out in public, and he wandered out into the city. I guess he got his hands on a toy rabbit, a plush yellow one from one of the shops, and he started carrying it around with him. He said it was his herald, or something. Then he went and named it Eugene; started hanging it on a rope around his neck like Flava Flav. Ever since then it’s always been “Eugene wants this,” or “Eugene wants that.” And on the rare occasion when he’s not speaking for Eugene he refers to himself as “The Me”. It’s seriously fucking bizarre. Oh, and Eugene never just asks for anything, he barks demands at you like you owe ‘m money or something. “Lloyd, we’re going to be going on some trips again, like before,” he continued. I just sat back and let the master work. Lloyd was confused at first, or about as confused as someone in his state could get. To put it another way Lloyd was to confusion what Spinal Tap albums are to the color black. I’m not sure what Mervin told Lloyd about my situation. Even if he did, I’m not sure that Lloyd would really be able to understand it, or realize what it all meant under the surface. What I do know is that he stared at me for an awfully long time before he said anything else, and it kind of creeped me out, more than a little. I’ve never told anyone this, but there are days – not many, but a few – when I’m convinced that this is all an masterfully crafted act, and that V-to-the-J knows a lot more than he’s letting on. “Eugene can come, too?” And then sometimes I’m snapped right back into thinking we could make quite the French fries from whatever’s in his head. Merv’ was the one who gave him the news, which got Lloyd all worked up. He started flapping his hands the same way I did when I was little, which got me laughing a bit since my mother always told me not to do that because people would think I was retarded. Thus far I’d been holding on to a little surprise, but hadn’t told either of them yet. I figured this was probably the best time to drop the hammer. “Just so you guys know,” I said, as calmly as I possibly could, “there’s a catch to this.” It was here that Mervin’s brow furrowed. I think he was expecting something like this – he’d known me too long not to, but he was most likely holding out hope. I only wish I was holding a camera, because the reaction was priceless. Lloyd seemed to love the idea, but Mervin, well, if this was Star Trek and he had a phaser it’d have been set to ‘murderyouintheface’. I have no idea why he ever thought that I’d be the only one in this group who’d be dressing like a blueberry.
