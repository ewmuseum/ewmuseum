Old Flames Never Die

16 April 2004

Date: 2004-04-16 You’re going about this all wrong No time to weep No time to despair in the past I’m the one you have to worry about Not some poor girl who you marred So quit living in the past you damned Fool The present is what you need to worry about I’m what you need to worry about A young man in his twenties walked into the Coliseum where the second round of the Cruiserweight Tournament will be held. He wore a black Nike sweat suit with white sneakers. He walked towards the locker area with great ambition in his stride. A few people called to him, telling him he can’t go where he is going. Yet he ignored them. He didn’t care what they think. In his heart he knows he belongs here. In his heart he knows he can fight in the tournament. “Sir!” a gruff voiced called to the kid. “Only those who are registered to fight can go into that area.” The kid was Jack. Jack Brian from Del Fuego Gym. Ignoring Syko’s request to stay away from the tournament, Jack bought it upon himself to try to get into the event himself. He was so sure he could have his big break here. “I want to fight in the tournament.” Jack replied back, trying to sound tough. The source of the gruff voice was a large African American man. “Nah, the sign-ups have been over for weeks now bro…” he told Jack matter-of-factly. “You’re too late. If you want autographs, you can buy your tickets like the rest of the world.” Jack sighed in disgust. He didn’t want to believe it was over this easy. “Hold on there!” a voice called out from the distance. Jack turned to see another large burly man. This one was Caucasian and rather hefty. He looked to be someone rich and important sporting a suit and a large black suitcase at his side. The man crept closer examining Jack. “So, you want to fight huh?” he asked. Jack nodded with great determination in his actions. “You know, I don’t think it will be such a bad idea.” The Caucasian told the African American man. “He’s too late.” The disagreeing argument came. Jack let out a sigh of disgust. “You people are shitting me. You have a bunch of skinny guys in there trying to out-pin each other. You call them fighters? I could easily beat anyone of them. In fact, I’d be willing to be on it. I’m not the number one student in Del Fuego Gym for nothing.” The African American man just shook his head and walked away. The other stayed having caught Jack’s attention. “My name is Marc.” He introduced himself. “For now that’s all you need to know. Oh yeah, and I can get you into the tournament. But tell me first, where is this Del Fuego Gym?” Jack only heard on this Marc had to say. “No, you tell me how I can get into this tournament.” Marc smiled slyly. “Are you sure? These fighters are tougher than any you have ever seen and more likely ever faced. Just because they look like they can start fires, or appear in X-games, or carry a big sword, or give Buffy a run for her money in demon hunting, or even start their own cult, it doesn’t mean that they aren’t tough fighters.” “Yes, I know all that…” Jack said. “Just tell me how you can get me into the tournament.” Marc motioned for Jack to follow him into the restricted area. As they walked side by side into the locker rooms and fighting arena, Marc explained himself to Jack. “I can’t get you in with a red carpet. You’ll have to do a try out match first.” Jack just nodded in approval. “Well here we are…” Marc announced point to a man stretching out in the far corner. “He’s wanting to get into the tournament as well. In fact, he says the same things you say. So…you ready?” “You betcha old man…” Jack responded grinding his fist into his palm. “I’m going to make a fool out of all these skinny pretenders. Now I just have to beat him and I’m in?” “That’s the plan.” Marc answered. “Now tell me about this Del Fuego Gym…” <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Boring. Endless boredom. Syko slouched in his Nissan Skyline waiting for the traffic light to turn his way. Next to him on the passenger’s seat, was a take out order from Subways. Syko dipped his hand into the bag and plastic wrapper and took out half a foot long chicken breast sandwich. He took a large bite out of it, indulging himself in the taste it bought to his lips, the satisfaction it bought to his stomach. The light turned green, and Syko stepped on the gas. Screech! Syko quickly pulled on the brakes as two cars seem to come at his from both directions. One car, a dark blue Ford Mustang, came from his left and crashed into the front hood of his car causing it to spin in place for half a circle length. The other car seemed to turn just in the nick of time. Yet, it wasn’t entirely successful as it bumped the rear end of Syko’s Nissan Skyline. The tiring screeching had bought up a lot of dust off the ground. In his car, Syko gripped his wheel. He was lucky that he was wearing a seat belt. However, there were no serious damages or injuries, just a scare. Syko breathed deeply. What the hell were they thinking, he thought. Outside, the two vehicles that had put a scare on each other came to a standstill. Both doors open and both drivers walked out to determine their damages. There were a few dented bumpers, but nothing too big. The driver of the Mustang was a man who looked like Steven Segal. He walked over to Syko’s car and knocked on his window. Syko rolled down his window; his gaze averted from the Mustang driver. “Are you okay man?” He asked. Syko shook his head. “What the fuck were you two doing?” Unbeknownst to Syko, the Mustang driver motioned for the other man to drive off. Obviously they knew each other, and obviously this wasn’t an accident. “I’m sorry Syko…” “Excuse me?” Syko asked turning to face the Steven Segal wannabe. “How do you know my name?” The man cleared his throat. “Aye, I recognized who you were.” Syko unlocked his seatbelt, opened the door, and stepped out. He dusted the crumbs of his sandwich off his black jeans. “How exactly did you recognize me? I’ve been here for half a minute.” “I…” but the man couldn’t finish his words. Syko instantly knew that something was up. He grabbed the man by his throat and forced him down to his knees. The man tried desperately to set himself free. In the end, he was freed. However, he was not freed by his own accord. He was freed because something else had caught Syko’s attention. Steven Segal II rose up to his feet and saw that a dozen vehicles had suddenly appeared lined up around them. Syko stepped back, taking extreme caution. The man on the floor, smiled a bit…thinking he was saved. Bang! A bullet sped through the air and found the temple of Steven Segal II. Syko watched in awe as the man dropped lifeless to the concrete floor in front of him. Syko then looked up to see the man who shot him. He was a bald man with a solemn look on his face. “The guy was a dumbfuck…” the man said nonchalantly as he approached Syko. “He had simple orders.” The man said as he stepped over the lifeless body. He looked up at Syko, scanning him from head to toe. “All he had to do was to deliver you a little message and be done. But no…he had to make things so dramatic.” “Who are you?” Syko snarled. “No one.” He said. “I’m just here fixing this dead punk’s mistakes.” “Like hell you are…” Syko responded spitting on the floor, just an inch shy of the man’s boots. He didn’t take that too kindly. “You’re talking to me all wrong,” he said. “Do it one more time and I’ll smash your face with an iron.” “What is it you want?” “It’s really simple really. I don’t see why we have to tell you. There’s a little event that’s going on right now. We all know about that.” “Then you would know that I’m not interesting in moving on. If you people have bets to keep, too bad.” “It’s not that simple. You’ve been picked to win against The Fool. You have no choice but to win. The Fool is a joke of a wrestler. It would be blasphemy for you to lose.” “Not interested.” “That’s what she thought you would say…” “She?” “Our boss. The trade off is this…you win, and everything goes fine. You lose, and everyone in your Gym dies in an accidental fire.” “Who are you?” “I’m just a messenger…” <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Jacob blew his whistle and the group of loud students at the Del Fuego Gym became silent for a moment. “Has anyone seen Jack?” Jacob asked. “His mother’s here.” Amanda, that over ambitious blonde girl, walked up to Mr. Jacob. “No sir, I haven’t seen him. Actually we haven’t seen him all day. He was talking about wanting to fight in some tournament, and that’s the last we heard of him.” Jacob shook his head. “No, that tournament is already in progress. There’s no way he can enter now, so he should be back by now.” Amanda shrugged. “I don’t know sir.” “What’s going on?” a soft voice asked. It was a woman in her forties. She was dressed rather attractively. “Where is Jack?” Jacobs shook his head. “He’s not here madam. I’m sure he’s okay though. He’s a very strong kid. My guess is, he just decided to run out and hang out with his friends. He’s fine, I guarantee you.” Jack’s mother rolled her eyes. “That kid. I’m going to drill him a new ear when he gets home.” She scowled. “I told him to always call me and tell me where he is. I can’t be looking over him and his brothers all at the same time by myself.” A couple of the students began to chuckle. They couldn’t believe that someone who acted as tough as Jack did was a momma’s boy. “Oh I bet he is…” The sentence was interrupted by a group of policemen walking into the gym. The students all dropped what they were doing and followed the steps of the policemen. The one in front, a man in his 40’s with a beard, approached Jacob. “Are you Mr. Del Fuego, or Syko…?” he asked not sure if he was reading what he had written down correctly. “Are you the owner of this gym?” Jacob shook his head. “No sir. I’m in charge of admissions and registration for the gym. I run things on the administrative level here, sort of speaking. Mr. Del Fuego is not in today. I’m running the gym in his place, so what can I do for you?” The officer cleared his throat. “It’s not an easy thing to say this. Are you close with any of the students here?” Jacob nodded. “Yes, we all know each other very well.” “Okay, I have some news regarding one Jack Brian.” The officer continued. “Would you by any chance be able to locate someone in his family for us?” “What’s wrong with Jack?!” Jack’s mom asked frantically, fear striking her heart. “Excuse me madam…” the officer said. “Who are you?” She reached into her purse desperately trying to find her identification card. “I’m his mother!” “I’m terribly sorry madam. Your son’s been shot at the Coliseum. He’s being taken to Bellevue Hospital right now, and his condition is critical.” A loud scream of despair and denial came from Jack’s mother. How do you tell someone his or her son is going to die? Amanda gasped and soon she started to cry. The other students looked around at each other, each not knowing what to do or react. “Madam, come with me.” The officer said. Jack’s mother followed the policemen outside. So did Amanda and the other students. Jacob on the other hand…his good hand…ran to his office. No, he wasn’t hiding. He was running to get in contact with Syko and let him know what was going on. He opened his door in a rush and ran inside. He didn’t get too far. Inside the room, slouching on his chair, eating his donut, drinking his milk, was the man who cut off his fingers. Marc. “What are you doing here?” Jacob asked. “I’m just here to give you a little reminder about your debts.” Marc answered. “Have you kept Syko fighting?” “Y-y-yes” Jacob answered hoping that Marc wouldn’t realize what an obvious lie that was. “Liar.” Nevermind. “I know for a fact that Syko doesn’t want to go face the Fool. It’s a shame really. The Fool doesn’t seem to be very impressive of a fighter. It could have been an easy win for him.” “What are you going to do now? Cut my other fingers off?” Marc spit on the desk and shook his head. “No, I said I was here to give you a reminder. It’s already happened. Now Syko has a reminder too…” “What are you talking about?” “Let’s just say that you are not alone in your gambling habits…” “I don’t get it…” “Your good student, Jack, was so convinced of himself. So much in fact that he bet he could win any match. The poor kid couldn’t even beat one guy…let alone three or four.” Marc explained smiling a disgusting smile. “You bastard…” “Oh it gets better,” Marc assured him. “The poor kid had no idea what was going on. So I made like the good Samaritan and put things into perspective. You should have seen his eyes lit up when I mentioned your debts and Syko’s role in the tournament. I bet he was pretty disappointed in you.” “Bastard!” Jacob screamed out. “What did you do?!” Marc scoffed. “Do you really have to ask? Don’t worry about it old man. He’s not going to talk to anyone anymore. We made sure of it.” With that said, Marc threw a plastic bag on the table. In that bag was a human tongue. Jacob grabbed his stomach, trying to control himself from barfing. For the most part, he was successful. However, he still felt nausea. “Jacob, we need to talk right now!” Jacob turned to see Syko storm into the room. Talk about being in the wrong place, at the wrong time. “I want to know everything that has been going on….” Syko asked of Jacob. “Oh you mean to say that he hasn’t told you about his enormous gambling debt?” Marc chimed in. “Who are you?” Syko asked. “I’m the one who has all the answers.” Marc introduced himself. “Now go ahead Jacob. Tell him about the deal you made. Tell him about the money you owe us.” Jacob was frozen silent with fear and embarrassment. Syko stared at Jacob waiting for an answer. But there was none. “Speak up you mute son of a bitch!” Syko snarled at Jacob. “What the hell did you do?” Jacob shook his head. “I’m sorry Syko. All I wanted to do was win some easy money. So I went to Miasma Casino and tried to win a few bucks. But I didn’t and I kept falling into the hole. I lost all my life savings, and …and…” “And now you were using me to pay off some gambling debts?” Syko scowled. Syko felt like hitting something, but he contained his rage. “There’s more,” Marc insisted. “Tell him about your new debts.” “I…I…” Jacob stuttered. “I told them I would get you to keep fighting, or I’d pay half a million grand, or lose my wife and daughter. I don’t want to lose my wife and daughter! Please tell them you’ll fight. You can win the tournament! Please tell them you’ll win!” Syko was at a lost for words. Marc stood up from his seat and walked towards the door. He patted Syko on the shoulder. “I’ll leave you two lovebirds to catch up on things.” He said as he opened the door. He then crept closer to Syko’s ear and whispered into his ear. “I’m sorry about your car.” Marc slammed the door shut, and Syko looked back. It was crystal clear now, at least most of it was for him. Syko smirked a bit and then turned back to gaze at Jacob who was distraught. “What are you going to do now?” “I dunno.” “You want me to face this Fool?” “Yes…” Jacob answered lowly. Then he remembered what he was going to say to him earlier. “Syko! There’s something you have to know about…” “Really?” Syko exclaimed sarcastically. “You think?” “No, I mean Jack’s been shot.” Jacob explained. “It wasn’t an accident either!” Syko turned and opened the door. “Where is he?!” Jacob followed behind him. “He was shot at the Coliseum.” The same arena the second round of the Cruiserweight Tournament will be held. <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Jacob and Syko arrived at the Coliseum, but they were too late to see Jacob being sent off in an ambulance to Bellevue hospital. They both asked the police officers around what had happened. What they learned was that Jack was found by the janitor in the stall of the men’s room in the 2nd floor. The janitor thought it was a drunk, until he turned him over and saw the bullet wounds in his stomach. As it happens, right now the second round of the Cruiserweight Tournament seems to be postponed a day. If not indefinitely… However, the people running the tournament are too self-righteous to let anything stop it. As Syko and Jacob prepare to leave the Coliseum to visit Jack in the hospital, an unexpected visitor approaches them. It’s the man whom confronted Syko earlier. “What are you doing here?” Syko asked of him. He laughed. “The name’s Guile, punk.” He answered. “Is that how you always greet people?” “Who is that?” Jacob asked. Guile pushed Jacob down to the floor. “You just worry about getting us the money old man…” “What do you want?” Syko asked. “You want my answer?” “I already have your answer.” Guile said. “I told you what was going to happen if you didn’t do what u were told. I’m sure you know what happens to Jacob here as well. But it’s not me who’s taking your answers.” “Who is?” “….she is…” A pair of black boots appears from around the corner. Slowly…the gaze trails up her black boots and over her smooth silky-like legs. She wears a midnight black miniskirt with a matching top. Her lips are ruby red in color, and her eyes are baby blue. She is very attractive, and her hair…it’s a euphoric form of auburn. No…more like crimson tide. Guile cleared his throat. “Please allow me to introduce to you… Karla…” Syko and Karla stared at each other. To them, there was no other person in the room. Karla walked towards Syko, smirking at him. She then leaned over and kissed Syko on the cheek. “It’s so good to see you again, big boy.” She whispered into his ear. Karla turned around and waved goodbye to Syko and Jacob. “I do trust that this time, we can cooperate?” She turned to Guile. “Come on Guile, let’s go.” Syko watched Karla leave his presence. His stare didn’t leave from her presence at all. He stood there like a statue until Jacob pulled at his arm. “Who is she?” “Just an old memory…” “Syko…I understand if you don’t want to fight…” Syko hushed Jacob up. “No, forget about it. Who am I facing again?” “The Fool, Ian Fetzer, He’s an anomaly of a fighter. He’s tall and skinny. Yet, he’s very successful, just a bit awkward to understand. He’s part of a federation known as the XUW. Doesn’t seem to be too much of a hassle. Are you going to fight him?” “…yes…and I’ll win too.” No more toys No more jokes No more time No more games No more hypnosis Give it up You’ve already lost You’re a damned Fool, if you think you have a chance.
