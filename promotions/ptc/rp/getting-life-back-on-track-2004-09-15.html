Getting Life Back On Track

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 The great pain that is life. The rain pelting against my apartment window was inducing me further and further into the sleep my body longed for so desperately but my mind was frightened of like no other. For when I would shut my eyes and fall down that path to the sweetness of sleep my mind would replay event in my mind that I did not wish to remember. My mind would unconsciously replay the very thing that drives me and at the same time stops me from completing my goals in life. The simplest of things: memories. I pushed into the arms of my light blue recliner to help myself up from the cozy seat where I had begun to nod off. The pain afterward would be too much for me to gain those precious few moments of sleep. Sleep could wait as long as I needed it too. At least that's what I told myself. In truth sleep had already waited three days, and it would have been a week had it not been for five minutes of pain and pleasure that I yielded to in a moment of weakness. I stood up, but my feet immediately began to ache from the weight of my body. I took a couple of wobbly steps before my body regained its balance. I had been attempting to rest my mind and body without sleep, though it has yet to help in the least. My body was able to rest it self from the daily rigors of life but my mind had become mush if even that. My head pounded with a headache that longed for sleep to relieve it, but all I could give in reply to it was Tylenol and caffeine, neither of which it enjoyed. I reached down and grabbed the remote control from the small wooden table next my recliner and proceeded to turn the TV off that had been assaulting my head with its vicious attacks of sound and light. I tossed the remote into the recliner and turn to my right and look at the window where nothing but rain and clouds resided in my view. I could see the lightning in the distance and knew that the storm would only get worse. It always got worse in the end, though it never mattered. I reached up and grabbed the long white cord and gently tugged on it to bring the blinds crashing down to block my view of the misery that lie outside waiting for me to venture into for some reason or another. I turned and walked across my small apartment living room, ignoring the various item of trash that littered the floor such as food wrappers and caffeine loaded soda pop cans that had collected over the last week since the dreams returned. I began to walk down my small hallway as I my feet begin to adjust to the weight of my body and begin to ache less and less. The scent of coffee several hours old reaches my nose as I am walking down the hall and it begins to wake me up from my stupor. This is a good thing I thought to myself. The more awake I am the less chance of me falling asleep at some inopportune moment. That was what I feared the most at the time. I could have been threatened with death and still I would have not even blinked twice if it meant an end to the torment of my dreams. I rounded the corner of my hall and come face to face with my dingy kitchen space with its flickering yellow light and unwashed dishes piling in the sink. I looked past the loud refrigerator on my left to where the old coffee maker sat resting in wait for me to take refuge from my slumber with its caffeine loaded liquid. I sighed as I began to walk into my kitchen and immediately felt the cold yellowish white tile meet my feet with its icy cold grip. I take a few steps over to the coffee pot and reach to the sink next to it for a semi-clean coffee cup. This would surely wake me up a little bit more, at least that's what I hoped. I poured the steaming coffee and took in its stale aroma that still aroused my senses. I turned and started to walk over to my table and along the way flipped the light switch to illuminate the table of newspapers and magazines. I sat down in the creaky wooden chair and looked at the top of the newspapers that I had yet to look at. Sitting on top was the entertainment section and in the lower right of the page was a column about the Global Title Tournament of which I was entered in. It was time I returned to the world of wrestling. I guessed that this would be as good as a time as any. Maybe some federation would look at my talents and reward me with a job. Lord knew it had been a while since my last one and the unemployment checks were about to run out. I quickly tired of the newspaper even before I had read any of it. I sighed and stood up as the chair below me creaked once more as the weight upon it was lifted. I started to head over to the bathroom where I planned to take a shower. Lord knew that I needed it. As I walked back down the hall to wear my bathroom was I ran my fingers through my grungy hair and felt the oil that had accumulated in it in the three or four days since I had last taken a shower. I became disgusted by the oil and jerked my hand away from my hair and attempted to wipe it off on the wall. As I did I crossed a picture hanging there on the wall. The picture had been taken four or so years before, not long before my eventual split with the man who I treated like a father, Richard White. I looked at the picture of me and Richard sitting there next to his new house in eastern Oklahoma. He was proud of it almost as much as he was proud of me. At least that's what I had thought. It would be soon after that I would learn the truth behind the reason he cared for me so. It wasn't because I was like a son to him as he had said many times. No, it was because I helped to increase the size of his wallet and nothing more. I would go on to leave that place and never try to look back, though I would eventually be led back there by fate. Fate had a plan for me when I returned there. Though I still know not what it intended to teach me barely 4 months ago when I returned and when Richard had decided to... I didn't want to think about it as anger for what Richard had done quickly swelled up inside of me. I ripped the picture down from the wall and smashed it against the wall, cutting my hand in the process. I tried not to think about the events of that day those months ago when I arrived at Richard's house for the first time in years, and for the last time forever. Contempt and hatred is what I felt for the man who had been like a father to me. He was man who had found me deep in my jungle hell of a home from where I knew of no one else. He had been the man who had trained me to be what I am today, an entertainment incentive meant to attract the masses for money. Although today I did it in other ways than he had wanted. I slumped my head against the wall where the picture had been. The contempt and anger that had been swelling inside of me had dispersed and all that was left was the sad child that I really was in the end. I begin to sob lightly as my eyes began to tear up. As much as I hated the man, I also missed him. He had been a driving force in my life, regardless of the fact that he had betrayed me. I straightened myself upright and wiped away my tears with my uninjured hand. I began to walk once again down the hall and entered the bathroom as I switched on the light switch to my left leaving a streak of scarlet blood on it. I turned on the water faucet and rinsed off my bloody hand. The cut didn't look to bad and wouldn't affect me very much in my upcoming tournament match. Though if I didn't some how rest I would be not be much of opposition to my opponent, Larry. I flicked my hands a few times towards the tile floor to get some of the water off before drying them with a near by blue cotton towel. As I finished this I wiped my face with it and looked up into the all-knowing mirror. In it I saw ragged image of myself, my dark long blonde hair oily and messed up. The dark lines I saw under my dim blue eyes showed how tired I really was. I wiped my face once more and blinked a few times. I went to take a step towards the bathtub to start the shower when something I didn't expect happened. Before I could stop myself I was falling to the floor, a product of my foot meeting with the slick wet tile. I tried to stop myself but to no avail and then I felt nothing. Darkness enveloped me as I felt as if I was falling and never would stop. It was then that the thing I feared began. I could feel my heart pounding through my chest as if it were going to rupture my skin and send me into a state of shock. My companion, nothing more than a stuffed bear, though I feel that he would have surely suffocated if he had been a person. My grip on him was only but scarce portion of my worries. My legs pressed against the seat to my front, my body rigid in anticipation. I hear a strange voice from above, nothing but garbled noises reach my ears as I chance a look out the window, I'm all alone now, my mother is nowhere to be seen. I hear a thunderous roar coming from the outside, I fear of what may be out their waiting. Suddenly, I am thrust into the back of my seat, not able to move a muscle in my six year old body. The pressure seems to be almost too much for me to bear as the landscapes outside of my window launch themselves out of my sight behind me. I feel my body begin to elevate as the view to the outside world changes and revolves to an angle as it flashes by, and then just as suddenly as it all began, my frantic journey of motion and confusion comes to an end as my body begins to feel light. The feeling returns to my nerves, my grip loosens as the ground from whence I came slips farther out of view, cars begin to look like ants and then nothing at all. I see nothing but scores of shades of green and crystal like water reflecting the sun until I am even above the clouds themselves to the point that the sky above has darkened to the likes of night time almost. Then a thought suddenly enters my mind. I begin to think that maybe, just maybe flying isn't so bad after all and that perhaps the living in the jungle with my parent would be fun. My outlook on life increased dramatically from that point on, regardless of my young age. I was going to with my parents again in just a few short hours. ~ I had been walking around the dense and foggy forest, leading myself to nowhere. Not even caring how to get back. I had a knack for being over confident even at that age. After a while I noticed that I did not recognize the types of leaves that were hanging off the branches of the lush trees or the bugs that I was coming across. I being the type of kid I was did not worry about such things, a normal kid would have probably turned back in fear of getting too far and not being able to find his way home. Even further I trekked into the unknown tropic wilderness, listening to the wild animals sing their songs of joy, or of victory, and even there final screams of defeat. I finally got to a part of the jungle that seemed nothing like the rest, being all dark with dead trees and plants. Frankly the place scared me. It was then that I got my wits about me and decided to go back. I did have to get back before my parents woke up after all. It was after I had decided to turn back that I then noticed something following closely behind me making only soft patter for its footsteps. I had no idea what it could have been, but I knew that I didn't like it. I quickened my pace after that, fearing of what might be if I did not. Unfortunately after I quickened my pace so did it. Soon thereafter fear totally seized my little body. My blonde hair getting in my face as I ran as fast as my little legs could carry me, though as it were to seem, not fast enough. After what seemed like hours of running through the woods I finally could not run any longer so I sat down and did the only thing a seven year old could think of, cry. I put my head down into my pudgy little hands. What would I do? What was that thing following me? Where were my mom and dad when I needed them? A light rain had spread over the forest when I finally stopped and crying to see the world around me. How I wish I had looked up. Because when I looked up I was looking into one the most horrible thing I have ever seen; two glaring red eyes staring right back into my pale blue ones. To say fear swept over my body would be a vast dryness in the eyes of truth. I was up in a second running for all I was worth, not caring where I was going. Just running, running to wherever my feet took me. To get away from that hideous spotted beast that even now was giving me chase. I finally began to hear a familiar sound, running water. I figured that if I made it that far then I would be safe, right. I ran even faster then, tearing strips of my beige clothing away from my arms and legs as I went. The sound of water was increasing, but as was the sound of my pursuer catching up to me. Then I saw about 15 feet away, was a small river. I quivered in joy, yet then at that very moment as I was just feet away from the safe haven, all went dark. ~ I had been running all morning in search of our camp. Hoping to find my mom and dad there waiting for me, over joined that I had finally returned. Yet when I found the camp I was less than pleased to find what lie there. There was my mom laying there, her back facing me, not moving, not even breathing. Deep gashes scarring the flesh of her once fair skin, blood now dried in her beautiful blonde hair. I trembled to the very depths of my soul as I saw her. I slowly drew nearer and nearer to her. Until at last I reached my hand out and touched with delicacy what had once been my mother, nothing but cold, dead tissue now. I turned her over to see my mothers beautiful eyes once again, yet all I saw was a pale blue, empty look in them then. It was too much for me to bear. I turned away not wanting to see and began to look around the hut for where I feared I would find my also deceased father. Yet when I found him, only shards of bloody clothes and a single dismembered hand were left. ~ The room had turned suddenly cold, or maybe it was me. "Michael", began Richard, "like you, is a failure". Then Richard did something I would never forget for the rest of my life. Richard turned his gun and pointed it directly at Michael. Then his muscles tightened and a short loud blast emitted from the gun and a hole appeared in the area of Michael's heart. Michael looked down at his chest where he had just been shot and then dropped to the floor. "That is what I do to failures." Richard said as he turned his head back to face me. He then started rolling in his wheel chair over towards where I was standing. As he neared me he pointed the gun directly at me until it was pointing directly into my stomach. "The difference between you and him, however. Is that it is not your fault that you are a failure." Richard then looked up at me as a tear rolled out of his left eye. "I'm sorry boy." Richard said, and then with that said I heard a loud shot go off once again, and a sharp pain start to emit from my stomach. Was this what it felt like to be shot? If it was then it didn't feel as bad as I thought it would. It was then that I looked down and realized that the pain I had felt was the recoil of the gun as it shot strait into Richard's chest. I couldn't believe it. Richard had just shot himself right in front of me. My former mentor and the man who was once like a father to me had just shot and killed one man and then turned the gun upon him own self. I had no idea what I would do as I sat there with Richard's lifeless head in my hands as he began to slump down. I screamed as my eyes opened to the view of my bathroom ceiling. A great pain now emitted from my head. I was very nauseous as I came to, trying to remember where I was. Though the only thing I could remember were the dreams of my memories that I had just had. They were there fresh in my mind and the pains of them were even greater than that of my now bruised head. Something had to be done. Something would be done, and it would have to start now. This would be my opportunity. The tournament, it would get my mind off of all of these things. It would allow me to rest. Although rest was what I needed the most, though my true rest would have no choice but to wait. This was my opportunity to turn my life around. It was my chance to make everything click once again. So yes it would have to now start. It would have to start with the GTT. It would have to start with my opponent Phil Botwin. It was time for me to do the two things I do best, train and win. It would start here. It would not end here.
