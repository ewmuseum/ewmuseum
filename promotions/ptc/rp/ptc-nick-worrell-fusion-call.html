PTC — Roleplay

Global Title Contenders Match

31 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up in Pennsylvania, it’s a clear day, the sun covered only slightly by misty clouds. The camera pans down to a car, not any ordinary car, a hot pink Volkswagen Beetle, Nick Worrell’s ride of choice. His “whip” if you will. It is parked outside a building which has a large sign sporting the name in big green letters “P-T-C”. This is his belief on what PTC is, he doesn’t know better, as well all know he’s a bit clueless about things. This is attributed to a life changing accident that sent Nick into depression, it also makes him lose some short term memory, hence his handy “Fred Fusion” notebook and oversized pencil to keep notes on his life. He looks up at the large orange-bricked building and walks in through the motorized glass-sliding doors.] Nick: Hi, I’m Nick Worrell-Fusion, I’m here for my PTC Global Title number 1 contenders match! [There is a younger lady sitting at the receptionist desk, she has a simple ponytail and large glasses covering deep brown eyes. She looks at Nick puzzled.] Receptionist: Excuse me? Nick: My match, Mr. Lanier called me earlier and explained that I have a PTC Global Title number 1 contenders match, I need to talk to the people running it. Receptionist. I think you’re mistaken, Sir. Nick: Oh no, I’m not, I even recorded it to make sure, I have a tendency to forget things like this, but Daddy would never forgive me if I forgot something of this magnitude, so I took initiative to find this PTC. Receptionist: We called YOU? About a PTC Global Title match? Nick: Yes ma’am. Receptionist: What is the PTC Global Title? Nick: Truthfully, I have no clue, that’s why I’m here, to find out. Here listen to the call. [Nick pulls out his fancy cellphone his Dad bought him, who is a confessed text message-a-holic. He needs to keep in touch with his son so he bought him the newest technology. Nick goes through the cellphone browser and pulls up the recorded phone conversation between himself and Matthew Lanier, owner of Core Wrestling.] Nick: Ah here it is! Nick: Howdy! Matt: Hello Nicholas. It’s Mr. Lanier, your boss. Nick: Uncle Mattie! You don’t need to be so formal you know. Matt: Uncle? Mattie? Nick: Dad told me you liked to be called that. Matt: He did, did he? Well I don’t have time to talk about my names, I have big news. Nick: You know Daddy doesn’t like me talking on the phone by myself, he says it’s the Fred Fusion rule! Matt: Don’t worry, you can talk to “Uncle Mattie” anytime you want. Listen here Nicky, PTC called us earlier. You know PTC right? Nick: Of course! (you can hear the uncertainty in his voice) PTC, who doesn’t know PTC! Matt: Anyways, the Global title, a title that is considered to be one of the most renown in the world is going to be on the line in a month, and PTC needs a few contenders for it. Nick: What does that have to do with me? Matt: After careful consideration, we have chosen you of the list PTC gave us. We want you to represent Core in that match. Nick: ARE YOU NUTS? Matt: No sir, someone in that state of mind could not run a successful company like Core Wrestling. Nick: Uncie Matt, you know as well as anyone, I am the worst wrestler in Core. Why not send Munson or Artemis for god sakes. Matt: Artemis? ARTEMIS? We want to win that title, not make a fool of ourselves. We both know that as Pride champion you are more than capable of handling yourself in the ring, and as of late there is no doubting that you are indeed the hottest wrestler in Core. Nick: Hmm Daddy’s gotten to you. Filling that bald head of yours with that Nick Worrell: Champion rubbish. I am not a rubbish, the Ringmaster trophy, the Pride title, it was all luck. Matt: The whole basis of Ringmaster is to negate the luck involved in matches, you can’t have luck through 4 matches Nick. We chose you because of your consistency, your dedication and experience. Nick: What about my stupidness, clumsiness, and blackness, was this all taken into consideration as well? Matt: Not really, we saw more strengths than weaknesses. Now Nick, you’re the longest active wrestler in Core, we have faith in your to put out your best effort and show the PTC what Core has going for ourselves. Nick: I don’t know Unc, this is all happening so fast. Usually Daddy processes my thoughts for me and makes my decisions. Matt: Well I’ll call him then. Just find out who your facing and you will be fine, I’m sure of it. Nick: I think you’re making a huge mistake Uncie Lanny, but you know best. Matt: And Nick. Good luck. Nick: I’m going to need it [The call ends as Nick flips his cellphone down, ending the playback. The receptionist looks at him like he’s crazy.] Nick: I told you, you guys called Mr. Lanier, and he relayed the message to me. It’s that simple, all I want is some info about this title and who I’m facing. Receptionist: Sir, we are the PTC, Pennsylvania Turnpike Commision, we currently operates permanent variable message signs at strategic locations along the Pennsylvania Turnpike. These programmable signs are operational 24 hours a day and are controlled from the Commission’s Operations Control Center in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. Future plans call for the installation of additional permanent variable message signs to be located at other key points throughout the Turnpike system. Nick: Zuh! [As the receptionist finishes her explanation the door opens and there stands Core Hall-of-Famer and father to Nick Worrell, Fred Fusion. He walks in and strikes a pose to the delight of .. well nobody, except for Nick of course who jumps up and down clapping for his father. Fred walks over to the desk] Fred: I’m sorry Miss. My son here, and yes he’s black and he’s my son, he’s a little bit confused today on what’s happening. Receptionist: I’d say, he was going on about a title shot or something. Is he on drugs? You know you should spank him, my Bubba get’s one everytime he gives me a wrong look, you should try it. Fred: I will take it into consideration, but it’s not that easy to spank a 250 pound man. Receptionist: I’ll give you that, anyways, do you guys have any questions about the turnpike that I can actually help you with? Fred: No Ma’am. We’ll be on our way. [Nick and Fred wave goodbye to the kind lady and walk out to the parking lot. Fred puts his hands on Nick’s shoulders and looks into his eyes] Fred: Now son, I know you want to impress me, but taking initiative is not the way to do it. Remember what happened last time you tried that? [The camera shows a short clip of Nick getting chased by tigers in a cage of a large zoo.] Nick: You’ve always told me to get to know my opponent and his nickname was tiger for god’s sake. Can’t I ever do anything right? I just want to know who I’m facing so I can leave them off my will when they kill me, I don’t want to know about a fish that turns. Fred: A fish? What are you rambling on about Nicky. Nick: I don’t know to tell you the truth. Fred: Well I have good news and bad news. Nick: Good news first please. Fred: Well, I know who your facing for the Global title match Nick: Hurray! And the bad news? Fred: I know who your facing for the Global title match. Nick: They’re the same thing! Fred: Yes, but these guys your facing, let me tell you Nick, they aren’t pushovers. These guys are some of the very best in their respective organizations! Nick: I don’t know why Uncie Matt put me to represent Core, why not you, why not Munson, why not Gwynn.. Fred: Well, I’m retired, Munson’s probably eating some animal raw at this moment and you beat Gwynn, you were the logical choice. Nick: Logical eh? Fred: Don’t get Canadian on me Nicholas Worrell-Fusion, what did I tell you about using that dirty Canadian slang! Nick: I’m sorry! Fred: That’s better, hug? [They embrace on the sidewalk of the Pennsylvania Turnpike Commission building and they both jump in Nick’s “Betsy” as he likes to call it. They head off to a nearby restaurant] Fred: Also, the fact that you’ve wrestled more matches than anyone in Core helps, I mean you have the big fight experience. And you have the championship experience! Speaking of championships… [Fred pulls out Nick’s Core Wrestling PRIDE title out from a duffel bag strapped over his shoulder.] Fred: I thought I told you to wear this wherever you go, everyone will know who you are. Nick: I tried, I went to the mall and these little boys started throwing rocks at me saying I stole William Gwynn’s title. I know it was a lucky win but I didn’t steal it! Fred: There there, we need to forget about William Gwynn, we need to forget about Core opponents for the time being, right now you need to concentrate on 3 men. Emilio Rage, Justin Evers and Mal Somers. Nick: Eek! Fred: What? Nick: The guy’s name is Emilio there’s no way I can beat him. Fred: How do you figure, you’ve beaten guys named Flip, William, Smiley and Daniel. Nick: My uncle told me, never to mess with guys named Emilio, Jesus, or Big Black Bubba! Fred: I see, well then just call him Rage. Nick: That’s not too comforting Dad. What about those other guys, how good are they. Fred: They’re good, like I said, these guys are some of the best in PTC, that’s why they are challenging for this title. [Nick is swerving through the lanes zipping by cars in his little Beetle, he pulls into a local Indian restaurant parking lot, they get out and Fred straps on Nick’s PRIDE title around his waist and wipes it clean for him.] Fred: There, that’s how you need to look. Like you’re a champion, being a champion isn’t enough Nicky, you need to look like one, act like one and smell like one too. Nick: What if I don’t deserve this championship? Fred: How many times do I have to tell you that you deserve it, you went through one of the most grueling, brutal and destructive matches in Core history just last week, and now your going to challenge for the chance to be a contender for the Primetime Central Global title! Nick: I..I guess. I will agree to whatever you say to me Pops, you are my confidence, you believe in these things that my gut feeling tells me aren’t true, but your arguments are compelling enough to make me believe them. If you think I can beat those guys, I will do my best to beat them and get a shot at that title. Fred: You will, I’ve been looking at some of these guys and they don’t even know that you’ve won your Pride title yet, they probably think Ringmaster is all you’ve accomplished. Hah! They don’t know what’s going to hit them. [They walk into the restaurant which is pretty much deserted, the aroma of Indian cooking spices fill the air. A young pretty chocolate skinned girl comes up with two menus.] Waitress: Please follow me, Sir, is that a new style of belt to hold up your pants? Nick: Oh, you’re talking to me? Fred: It’s a world championship dear, it shows he’s Core Wrestling’s PRIDE and Joy. Waitress: I see (She looks at Nick with an interested eye as she leads them to their table. ] Fred: As I was saying, these guy’s have done a lot in their fed’s some are up and comers like Rage, the other two are veterans and big time players. Yet, personally, I think that your biggest threat is Rage, the kid has potential . Just like you did when I first saw you. Nick: So your saying they’re all better than me. Fred: You really need to stop that, I mean it’s been going on long enough, don’t you think that you should finally realize that you aren’t a jobber? Nick: No, if I feel like I’m a jobber, then trust me, I’m a jobber, and I am a jobber. Fred: Ok ok, stop saying you’re a jobber, it’s annoying. The fact remains, this is one of the biggest matches you’ve been in. And from what I hear you just need to be one of the last two men standing to get a shot at that Global title, but don’t settle for second Nicky. Nick: Why not? It’s good enough for me, I definitely don’t deserve first. Fred: Because, it’s simple, if you beat that last man, you go in to face Latch Key Kid knowing you’ve already pinned or submitted the other guy in the ring with you. That confidence factor is highly under-rated. [The waitress returns and looks at Nick admiringly while she brings out the pad for their order.] Waitress: What can I get for our “champ” today (she winks at him) Fred: We’ll have some Chicken Biriyani, Chicken Tika and some Curried potatoes and peas please. Waitress: Is that it? Fred: Also, I’d like for you to stop staring at my son, he doesn’t have time for women, lady. He has time for his PRIDE title and his future Global title. Waitress: I..I..I wasn’t doing.. anything.. Fred: Yeah yeah, save it for Apu, I know what your upto, I have a wife you know, I know all the tricks in the book. Even the one on the last page. [The waitress stands back shocked] Fred: Yes I know about the last page, so don’t try anything like that around here, just get us our food and we’ll be on our way. Oh! Can we have some lemon iced tea as well? Nick: With blue crazy straws! Fred: Yeah! [They give “five” to each other as the waitress walks away confused] Fred: That’s how you have to handle yourself Nicky, like a champion. These people will try to arrange a marriage and you will end up with some one toothed girl from India named Popeitha as your wife. You need to find a girl that I like and your Mommy will approve of. Nick: Like you said, if you don’t think I’m ready for a wife or anything like that, then I’m not. Your always right. Fred: And I’m right about you winning. I need to give you some pointers, Justin Evers, the guy from Wrestling Midwest, hasn’t been in a big time match for a while, you have that going for you right now. You’re coming off a huge win, you have the momentum going into this match Nicky, take advantage of that, just keep steam rolling forward, you can’t give guys like these a chance to take advantage of you! Nick: I’ll do what I can Daddy, I’ll do what you say and hope it’s enough. I mean, I’d love to show the world how great Core is, yet I feel like I’m not the person, but if Lanier chose me then he chose me and I can’t let him down. I can’t let Core down. This match is not only for you, it’s for all the guys who’ve I’ve ever faced, for all those guys in Core who know we’re the best. Fred: That’s the attitude! Damn, why can’t I get you to think like that for one of your Core matches! Nick: Who knows. Some things are worth fighting for. Fred: Oh also! Emilion Rage, he has other things on his mind, they have a big tag team title tournament going on there and he’s right in the middle of it, with him in the tag match mindset, you need to jump on him early and keep him down for as long as possible, because I know he will be a factor later on whether we want him to be or not. Nick: What about Mal Somers, what do we do with him? Fred: He’s the X-factor, I don’t know what’s going to happen with him. Just make sure he doesn’t get going because it’s going to be hard to slow him down. Just keep up the pressure, you might be a veteran in the ring but at your age, you have all the stamina in the world. Nick: I see, and has Emilio or any of these characters said anything so far? Fred: Yeah, but I don’t want to talk about it. (Fred face becomes flushed and he lies his chin in his hands) Nick: What is it? Fred: They were saying all this stupidness about how I baby you and how someone like you will never make it in PTC Nick: You baby ME? You raise me harder than any parent ever could. [Fred pulls out a napkin from his pocket and holds it to Nick’s nose.] Fred: Blow. [Nick blows his nose and Fred wipes it up and throws the napkin away.] Nick: Wow that’s better, I needed that. Fred: I’m your father, I knew you needed that. Just like I know you need to get a shot at that title and you will. You will have a chance to become Core’s first PTC Champion. Do you know what this could do for us Nicky? Nick: Get us loads and loads of Ice CREAM!? (Nick becomes almost giddy with excitement) Fred: Maybe, but this could make us one of the most recognized feds in PTC, and that alone is worth it. We will not give up without a fight right?! Nick YES SIR! Fred: APU! Where’s our food. [As the waiters and waitresses bring them their food Nick begins to this to himself] The Global Title, one thing that has eluded a Core superstar since it’s creation. Can it be mine? Will I get a shot at it? Personally, I don’t think so, I don’t think I’m worthy, I don’t think I’m skilled enough to compete with the best Primetime Central has to offer, yet with my Dad’s support, with Matthew Lanier’s support how can I think the way I do? I have a chance to bring Pride to Core like this title brings good fortune to me. It’s funny how I went from nothing, absolutely nothing in Core, just a former Classic and Intrepid champion into Ringmaster, Pride title and main eventer in the matter of a few months. And there’s one reason I have to thank for it, my father. He’s the catalyst to my success, and once he’s in my corner, nothing can go wrong. Every wrestler loses, it’s the percentage of times you do, and the way you lose that show the wrestler you really are. And I haven’t lost much in the past few months, I’m not going to deny it. I can’t, the record shows, I’ve lost to Jason Hughes, possibly the absolute best in Core history and William Gwynn in a cage. I haven’t lost to anyone else. I’ve avenged my loss to Gwynn, I’ve beaten other top guns in Core, hell, I beat a top gun in GCW as well. Yet after all this, I feel like it’s luck. Well for Core’s sake, for Daddy’s sake and for my well being, I hope this luck continues, because it’s the only thing that could possibly save me [Fade]
