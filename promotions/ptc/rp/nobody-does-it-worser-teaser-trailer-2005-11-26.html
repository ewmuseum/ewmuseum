Nobody Does It Worser - Teaser Trailer

Date: 2005-11-26 CLICK HERE FOR AUDIO TRAILER~! NOTE: The following is the script of the audio trailer, linked above. It is only intended to allow the listener to follow along with what is being said. Listen to the audio trailer instead of just reading this script. If you do not, YOU ARE A LOSER~! -------------------- Voice-Over: This holiday season, the world of professional wrestling will be turned on its head once again… and once again, the ENTIRE world will find itself in need of a hero… a hero who has the ability to save us all from CERTAIN DOOM! Bong: Oh, uh… are you talking about me? Voice-Over: No. For the love of God, not you. Bong: Oh. Voice-Over: But you're the only choice we have. Bong: I am? Oh, goody! Voice-Over: For years, he has served the British government… Bong: Look at me! I'm serving the British government! Tra-la-la, dee-diddly-do! Voice-Over: And last year, at GTT4, he was assigned to save the world… Bong: My goodness, THAT was a mistake! If I were in charge, I'd rather send a mentally challenged rock to save the world than me! Sadly though, our mentally challenged rock was killed in the line of duty… Voice-Over: And like the rest, it was an assignment that he failed… Bong: Oh well, you can't win them all I guess. Unless you're Dr. Curiosity. Voice-Over: He was fired from his job… Bong: Fired!? But why!? I mean, just because I failed at saving the world… and, well, everything else I've ever done in life… Voice-Over: He has no wrestling knowledge whatsoever… Bong: Turnbuckle? Oh, I love turnbuckle! It's my favorite kind of bread! Voice-Over: You uh… you're thinking of pumpernickel. Bong: Pumpernickel? That's preposterous! Everyone knows that a pumpernickel is what you carve at Halloween to make jackalopes! Voice-Over: And when it comes to GTT5… NOBODY DOES IT WORSER than… Bong: Bong. Jimmy Bong. *bang* AGH! Voice-Over: Coming soon. Bong: Who the bloody hell shot me!? Voice-Over: I did. Bong: Well… well that wasn't very nice!
