IG 30 - Cassandra Paragon: Mission One

31 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRESENT My name is Cassandra Paragon. I am a fugitive from my own time, known as the What Came After. PAST I've been here for a year now, trying to prevent your future from following the same path that mine did. I have been tasked with investigating, infiltrating and destroying a secret society known as the 'Dark Cowl'. Historical studies conducted in the What Came After will place these people at the heart of the so-called accidents that tore the Earth apart and made the majority of it uninhabitable. When I was sent here it was with names of people, places and incidents that I have been methodically following, trying to unmask the true face of the Dark Cowl. These assignments have taken me through the worlds of business, politics, drugs, and some areas that even I have failed to understand. To explain, I had better start at the beginning. My first case, the first investigation I had to complete after arriving in Kansas in 2006 was on a man named Casper Horowitz. You'll never have heard of this man, though he will very probably have impacted on your life in some small way. I knew that he worked on the fourth floor of a specific building in the center of Houston, so after taking some time to adjust myself to the differences between the time I was living in and the What Came After, I sought him out. The first thing I noticed, aside from my initial culture shock at seeing vegetation and buildings more than twenty years old, was the sheer amount of people. Stepping into a building and then downwards into pedestrianized walkways beneath the city that the residents of Houston call 'the tunnels', I was shocked to see first tens and then hundreds of people. All were clothed well, and most looked healthy as they hustled and bustled in and out of the various eateries located beneath the office buildings. Some were even overweight, something that will be almost unheard of in the What Came After, and reserved purely for the leaders of warlike enclaves. In a time of such want, being obese without a genetic permit for it can be a treasonable offense. Casper Horowitz worked for a small company that took up just one floor of one of the smaller office buildings. It was supposed to be a firm that specialized in graphic design for marketing and commercial purposes, but that was just a front for their real mandate. Behind their facade, the real purpose of Viralcom Design was as servants of public health. An odd dichotomy, but one that made sense once you understood the way in which they served their purpose. I took the elevator up to the fourth floor and presented myself to reception. I was wearing a gray suit and pants, with my burgundy hair cut into a bob. It was slightly unusual attire for me, but my training with the enclave's military had prepared me to be adaptable at all times. I spoke to the receptionist with the accent I would be using for my investigation, that of someone from California. I naturally spoke with an accent derived from the combination of people I grew up with; a strange clash of the Midwest and Russian, but had learned to mimic many accents that existed before What Came After. The receptionist buzzed me through easily enough. I had arranged an appointment with a different member of Casper's team; the one who dealt with genuine inquiries as to Viralcom's services. All I really needed was access to the offices without a fuss, allowing me to slip in and then, when the job was done, slip out again. “Yes Patrick, I'll see you shortly,” said a voice through the door, hanging up his telephone before he asked me in. The room I entered was the same as could be fund across the globe. Four dull walls, a small window to the city outside and a desk with a tidy amount of paperwork on it. “Ah, Ms Fletcher, I'm so pleased to see you,” gushed Jeremy Swann, greeting me with the pseudonym I had used. “And I you,” was my response, giving him a smile and a slightly flirtatious look. It never hurts to put a man at ease. Our conversation went as smoothly as I could have expected, with my questioning always met by a service that would live up to some, but not all, of my apparent desires. Mister Swann had evidently been practicing the art of just failing to land business deals for quite some time. After all, there would be no point taking on a client when your real purpose in life was something at odds with your advertising. “It's a shame that we can't be of use to you,” said Swann, with a conciliatory nod of the head. “Please think of us if you need anything that's more in our line of work though.” “Of course I will,” I replied. “Would you like me to see you to the door?” “No, I can make it back, but thanks for the offer.” “Don't forget to hand your pass back to the receptionist.” “I will,” I said, and with that I was out of the door. It had been the best part of an hour, but I knew that it was not time that had been wasted. Down a corridor, a right turn and the quick opening of a door took me to where I needed to be; in the office of Casper Horowitz. My gun was out before he could even move, and a dart containing Rhapsodize was in his neck before an utterance came from his mouth. He was unconscious before I even closed the door behind me, thanking the cosmos for non-open plan offices. PRESENT I have a match ahead of me, against a man named John Covel. He does not know that we are linked at a remove, but we are. In another time or place we would be allies, both of us looking towards Clinton Sage with a view to destruction. Our motives, though, could not be more different. Covel is an honorable man trying to bring dignity and skill to his chosen profession. Clinton Sage to him is a barrier to being the best, someone who he needs to defeat to prove to himself and the world that Sage can be stopped. To me, Clinton Sage represents something else entirely. He's merely a part of my next assignment. I have to interrogate each and every member of the professional wrestling fraternity that could be involved with The Dark Cowl. It seems strange and somewhat farcical that I am searching for the end of the world within such confines, but I cannot change the way reality occurs. This business has far more to it than meets the eye. People are involved in all sorts of chicanery and suspicious behavior; from cult leaders through underworld hitmen to those who even claim access to the paranormal. Most of the former and all of the latter are fakers, of that I am sure, but my assignment files read like some kind of Hall of Fame. Violence Jack, Clinton Sage, Angelo Deville, Shadow, Jason Snow... it went on and on; all of those were connected to organized crime, or worse. All could be in the pay of The Dark Cowl, whether unwittingly or by design. So to see two of those names next to one another, in one single match? It was as if Fate had decided to lend the righteous a hand. In no other circumstances would I be able to interrogate these men with such freedom, allowed access to them, with people not caring if I harmed them in the process. Of course, there would be no way for me to use my drugs to aid success, but I am very, very well trained. I can be exceptionally persuasive when I want to be. PAST Rhapsodize is a truth drug, but one that works a little differently from our more comment varieties such as Truth or Facts. During my first assignment I was wary of using too much of my extremely limited supply of the latter, and so Rhap was my drug of choice. Quite how it works is something for the chemists of the What Came After, but I knew its effects. Initial unconsciousness comes around almost in the blink of an eye, but it is soon followed by the user, or the victim entering a hypnagogic state. This is the period between being awake and falling asleep. It is a dream-like experience, but the drug enables the brain, in fact it compels the brain to create speech. An agent like myself can then, as I did with Mister Horowitz, lead them along a path of questioning where the brain will supply the mouth with words which the 'patient' genuinely believes. The only problem with this as an interrogation method is that the brain refuses to stop speaking, and will go on at length about whatever subject is at hand, hence the name Rhapsodize. “Mister Horowitz,” I intoned. “Tell me about your job.” “I work for Viralcom as a graphic desi...” “Your real job,” I said. You can interrupt people when under the influence of Rhap, but it really helps not to do it often. “Sheesh, I never get to talk about my real job. Jeremy's so pushy about the rules. I can see why, but just for once I'd like to talk about what I do. I take pride in my work! I'm good at it.” “You can tell this to me.” “We're the good guys. We're the protectors of America's health... hell, even the world's health! By that, I mean the world that's open for viral marketing and meme-shifts. Oh, you're probably wondering what a meme-shift is, or even what a meme is in the fist place. Well, I'll tell you...” I knew precisely what a meme was, but it was important to let him tell his own story. “A meme is like an idea, but one that passes itself on to others, who'll then pass it on again. It's like a virus... a real one I mean, but it's not bad for you. Apart from if it is... but we don't do bad memes. We leave that up to the others. I don't want to confuse you though, so just think of it as an idea. Like if you get an email that says no human being can lick their own elbow, you'll try to do it. You'll see that it's true and pass it on to others. You'll carry that meme, the cultural memory equivalent of a gene, and pass it on to others. You get a bunch of memes to play off one another and you can build societies, religions, whole ways that people think. Hell, just look at global warming, we're doing a great job there.” “How so?” I asked, with a good idea of not just where this conversation was heading, but also the next one. “Well, it's common sense to recycle and reuse things, to reduce waste, save money and care for the environment. The world has a limited amount of resources, so just making that extra bit of effort makes sense, no? You'd think that, but did anyone but the fucking hippies care when we tried to push through initiatives on environmental issues? Oh hell no. Everyone just figured that they were going to be fine, and that some poor schmuck in Cambodia or Sri Lanka or Ethiopia or somewhere else they'd never been to would get the shaft. Just keep on cutting down those trees and burning that oil... so we tried at first, my predecessors, to tell everyone that the oil would eventually run out. Did it work? Well, I'm sure you can guess...” Horowitz took a deep breath, and it occurred to me that I wasn't even sure he had breathed all through that little tirade. “Nobody did anything! So it was our job to change all of that. We faked a few science reports, we bribed a few people, sent out emails and commissioned television shows. The media is very, very fickle and loves stories of doom and gloom. We took some regular science that was accepted and put it in to action. Next thing you know, the world and his wife all know about global warming, and they're scared! Hurricane Katrina was a godsend for us! We had a hand in making that movie, 'The Day After Tomorrow', and now everyone is awash with humanitarian joy about trying to save the planet. The ends justify the means. There's no great global cataclysm on the horizon, but it's all about keeping the populace worried just enough. We don't want to spark riots, but a little fear is healthy. It lets you control people. Of course, the others don't want us doing that, so they're firing back and destroying the credibility of some of our reports. It's a meme-war. Everyone talks about these things at water coolers and in bars. Our ideas gestate and take on a living form. We influence the masses, the countless millions around the globe, those plugged in to our network of suggestion.” As someone who previously had been quite literally 'plugged in' to a network, I had to smile. It was almost time to ask him the question I had come here for, but he had to talk just a little longer. “It's not just global warming though. Take public health... you know how bad Coke is for you, right? Of course you do. Sunny D too? You've read the emails, you know about how they use these things as industrial acids and preservatives and how you need to drink 8 glasses of water every day to keep your body healthy?” Casper laughed to himself. In his dreamlike state he was obviously enjoying the feeling of power. It's fiction! We made it all up! Go read any sensible journal and you'll find it's all a lie... but what a lie it is! It propagates itself memetically, through word of mouth, emails, you name it. But it's a good thing... it encourages people to drink water instead of a sugary, carcinogen-laden 'treat'. This is how the world works... but of course the others try to stop us, as always...” “These others... who are they?” I asked. “If we're the good guys, they're the bad ones,” he said, his voice dropping lower and quieter. “We're the light, and they're the dark.” FUTURE In the What Came After I'll spend most of my life training for missions such as these. The enclave I'll live in, one of the few large centers of population that will be left in America after the destruction, will pay for my every need as I become the embodiment of everything that they need in an agent. Well, they will not pay as such, because currency as a whole will not exist within our enclave. Each will receive from the council as according to their needs. The irony of America's largest remaining enclave surviving on a system not dissimilar to Communism will be an irony that does not go unnoticed. We will adopt this system because of our size. Even the largest enclave will comprise just a couple of thousand people, and most will not make it into triple digits. Those will be the simple facts of life in the What Came After, the demented goal of The Dark Cowl. We will not know why they were involved in the lead-up to the so-called accidental detonation of a Concussion Bomb that will tear an enormous hole in the Western United States and send shockwaves and tsunamis all across the globe. All we will know is that the What Came After should not happen to everyone. The scientists who will send me back will be far more informed on the subject than I ever will be, but my actions in what to me is the past can serve to ensure that more realities face up to a future, and not a What Came After. PAST “...they're the dark.” As I heard the words I had been waiting for, I heard an office down the hallway open and a man start walking towards our location. I flattened myself against the wall and hoped that whoever was coming past would think Casper was merely on the phone. I wished that I had hit him with a shot of Brevity and not just Rhap, but I had decided to preserve my supply of drugs, and it was not something I could change. Instead, I readied the gun again and switched the ammunition to Sleep. I didn't want to use it, but I would. I had killed before then, and I would certainly not hesitate to do it again to prevent my mission from being compromised. The footsteps approached, passed us by, and then continued on down the hall, my actions shielded by a thin, but useful wood and frosted glass door. As all this transpired, Casper continued to babble onwards about his work, and I had to redirect him towards the other side, the ones opposed to his activities. “They work around here too. They always counter against everything we do, and they spread bad memes. Promiscuity, greed, violence, all of those kinds of thing.” “And they're government funded too?” I asked. “We think so. They're certainly on an even footing with us with regards to resources. I don't know so much about it though. Jeremy knows about that. I've not had any direct contact with the others, only...” “Does Jeremy know any of the other side personally?” “Yeah,” mumbled Casper, after shaking his head in annoyance. My interruptions were starting to break up his hypnagogic state, and he wouldn't stay under for much longer. “He has lunch with him sometimes. A guy named Patrick Hart. I've never actually seen him though...” His voice trailed off, and within seconds he was slumped back in his chair, deeply asleep. The Rhapsodize had worn off, but deposited him as it always does into a steady state of unconsciousness. I knew all that I needed to know, so after retrieving the dart and dosing him with a little Blank, I left the office and walked out. He'd come to in an hour or so, and would have no memory of anything other than pleasantly falling asleep in the office... well, assuming that none of his superiors caught him slacking off his job. I exited the building, confusing the receptionist somewhat, and made my way to the street just in time to catch sight of Swann turning around a corner at the end of the block. I thought back to my overheard phone conversation, and followed him to the restaurant where he was to meet 'Patrick'. PRESENT Physical combat holds no fear for me. I am smaller than most of the male wrestlers I come up against, but I am by no means intimidated by this. I know that I have greater speed and agility than any of them could hope to achieve, and a very precise knowledge of where and how hard you need to strike a man to cause significant damage. My training has helped keep me alive against the odds, and my wits have kept my identity, if not my existence, secret. Well, my wits and Facial Technology, but that's a whole other story. The wrestling, the competition, the fight; I relish it. I will not pull back at all, and I will be able to defeat whoever is in front of me on any given night. It's just the exposure, the audience that makes me scared; knowing that one day The Dark Cowl might recognize me for who I truly am, and then my days will be numbered. But until then... PAST Jeremy Swann entered a bar and was shown to a table. I waited for a couple of minutes and then followed him in, taking a seat at the bar, just far enough away to be inconspicuous, but close enough to keep an eye on him, and anyone he was to meet. I didn't have to wait long. Scant seconds later a man in a sharp black suit arrived, and I knew on instinct alone that this was the subject of my first assignment. His eyes passed over me as they looked at angles, at faces and at exit points. I smiled inwardly, reflecting nothing on my face as I mentally noted that this man wielded far more power than anyone I had yet encountered since my journey began. They ordered lunch and talked. I lip-read as best I could across the room, but everything seemed to be fairly innocuous. It made sense; the business of subverting the world, or destroying it, was hardly likely to be a hot topic of conversation in a bar. I nursed two soft drinks for an hour and a half before their meeting ended. Some business had been conducted, but unless they were being careful and talking in oblique language or code, there was nothing I could glean from having observed it. There was only one thing to do, and that was take a closer look. Tailing Patrick to where his vehicle was parked proved to be trickier than when I had followed Swann. This man took a somewhat circuitous route, occasionally even doubling back and visiting places which he had already passed. He didn't ever look lost, but often glanced around seemingly at random. It was almost like he wanted to be followed. I can't believe he did, especially considering what was to happen next, but the thought troubled me. What it did not prevent were my actions that followed. I grew closer to him as I sensed that he was losing concentration and returning to where he would either drive off from or be picked up. The sparse scattering of people around the entrance to the car parks made seeming innocent harder, but having been raised in the enclaves where people themselves are a rarity, it becomes an important skill to be able to avoid detection. As I placed my hand on his right shoulder I felt him tense and spin around, only for my fingertips to crash into his throat, before I applied a small dose of Sleep. He was out, and I was appropriating his car in no time. Wary of any tracking devices that could have been present, I drove it only as far as my own car, and then switched. The police found his body five days later. He'd been lying in the undergrowth near a park for two days, but the three before it had not been pleasant for either of us... for him especially. I must say that I was impressed by his dedication to the cause. He didn't crack even a jot under any pressure I put him under, at least not until I brought out my little pharmacy and doped him up. Even then, The Dark Cowl had placed some neural blockages in his mind that I was unable to get past. I learned little of use with regards to unmasking further agents of theirs, and the only information I got about his co-workers was that they were not in on 'the big picture'. In fact, beyond the existence of The Dark Cowl and a working example of their prowess with neural blocking, I only really learned one thing. But it was enough to make my blood run cold. They didn't know exactly where or when... ...but they had known I was coming.
