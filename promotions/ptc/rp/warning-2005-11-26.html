Date: 2005-11-26 You walk into a pitch black room. You’re hands are not even visible in front of your face. You stumble and fumble around like a drunk searching for a toilet. The smell is familiar, but horrendous. Bam! You’re foot connects with something on the floor and you plunge to the floor. You run your hand along the floor frantically, trying to find anything to help you. It finally reaches something. It’s small and feels like metal. You fiddle with it hastily to discover it is a Zippo lighter. It takes a couple of tries, but it finally lights. A glimmer of hope brought to your dark world. Then, you remember your fall and carefully move the lighter around, hoping to discover where you are. There, to your left, lies a shoe. You quickly check your feet to find it is not one of your own. You step closer to the shoe and see that there is a leg attached to it. Scared, you run the lighter up from the pant leg. You hit the thigh, the belt, the abdomen… All the while fear grows in your, as the amount of crimson stained into the clothing grows larger and larger. You finally reach the shoulders. You cry out in horror. You have found his throat sliced and a well-placed shuriken sunk into his forehead, holding an envelope firmly to it. Your name is written on the envelope and after taking a moment to regain your wits, you pull the shuriken from the man’s cranium. Despite the urge to vomit up everything from the last ten years of your life, you quickly open the envelope to find a letter. Hoping to find out any clues about your current situation, you begin to read: "I see you have found my friend, James. He thought he could hide from me like you did, but as you can see, I found him. You were warned about running and you didn’t listen to me. That was a big mistake, botard. Therefore, you find yourself in this room with a corpse and very little hope of surviving. Running is pointless with me. Be it the bathroom, your child’s birthday party, the confessional at your local church, or even the abandoned warehouse where you tried to hide, I will find you. Most times when you least expect it. Along with taxes and death, it is inescapable. As you sit in this room, you find yourself with two choices. You could face death like a warrior and fight for your life or you could just let me kill you and save yourself from the agony of defeat. No matter what, you die. With honor or not, it is your choice to make. Be warned, though. That lighter you hold has a very short supply of fuel. When it goes out, your time is up. Choose wisely." You grip the paper tightly in your hand, crumpling it up. You wave the lighter frantically in all directions hoping to see anything. Fear overpowers the pain rushing to your brain from your thumb, as the heated metal burns your flesh. You begin to panic with the shrinking of the flame. It is just about out, as you rise to your feet. Terror etched on your face while the flame lessens to barely anything. Behind you’re a black mask and a pair of jade eyes comes into sight just as the world goes black once more. The sicken sound of metal slicing through flesh and the gurgling of blood echo through the room. Silence follows and then, the room is lit up by flames radiating from a nearby wall. The last thing you see is the phrase "X3" written in flames above the rotting corpse of James. Two down, one hundred twenty-seven to go.
