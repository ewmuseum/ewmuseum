“Where One Ends… Another Begins” Featuring: Itachi Kobayoshi Written By: [name withheld] Word Count: 995 That morning was hard on all of them. Gai was marching in to meet the man that killed his mother, and Itachi and Kisame were standing by, and watching Gai make a horrid mistake. The three woke up, and didn't speak another word of Sarah Solah, or how she died. All of them had too much on their minds already. They packed their belongings, the few they had brought, and made way for the Tokyo Correctional Facility. As they entered the building, and found their way to the visitor's area, Itachi stuck close to Gai, knowing he wasn't ready, but couldn't be stopped. Gai stepped to the reception area, and requested a visitation. “Alright, you're just in time for visiting hours... who are you here to see?” The guard ask through a microphone from behind a Plexiglas window. It was at this moment that it dawned on Gai that he had forgotten to find out the man's name. He turned to Itachi, who took a deep breath before stepping next to him. “We're here to see Ryan Solah.” Itachi said, his voice ever stoic. Gai's eyes widened as he looked up at Itachi. “His name is Solah? Why didn't you tell me? Why did you let me do this?” The guard looked through his paperwork before interrupting Gai's questions. “I found him, we'll be out to escort you to see him in just a few minutes.” Itachi led Gai back down to a seat, where he could barely look at him, feeling the pain in his eyes. “Itachi-san... Answer me.” Itachi slowly turned and looked at Gai, and said nothing. For the first time he could remember, Itachi, a man who always chose his words carefully, couldn't find any for this moment. Luckily for him, the door opened and a guard signaled for them to follow. The four men walked past many cells, and were finally let into the monitored visiting area, used strictly for dangerous felons. Gai sat between Itachi and Kisame, while an armed guard stood in the back of the room. Gai stopped asking Itachi, knowing he wouldn't get an answer at the moment. He just sat and waited, scared to see who would walk through the door. As the door slid open, a tall, slender white man walked through the door wearing an orange jumpsuit. He was cuffed and the wrists and ankles, and sat across the table. He didn't say anything, instead waiting for Gai to speak. After a long period of silence, Ryan Solah finally began to get annoyed. “Ok, you here for some report or newspaper? What do you want from me?” Itachi stopped Gai, and answered for him. “Ryan... I'd like you to meet your son. Gai... this is your Father, Ryan.” “You're kidding me... That's not him... why would anybody bring him here if it were him?” Ryan asked, completely in disbelief. “I wanted to come here... I wanted to confront the man that killed Mom... I wanted to tell him what he took from me... I didn't know that it was you... Nobody told me.” Gai said without lifting his head. “We tried to stop him, but he said this was his journey... which makes sense. He met his mother, now he's met you.” Itachi explains to Gai's father. “Listen, if what you say is true... then you're the only one that can help... I didn't kill Sarah... someone made me rob that store, and when the clerk tried to grab my gun... it fired... I didn't want to do any of that... but I needed money, for the baby... for you.” Ryan began to tear up a bit as he recounted the events of that day. “I borrowed a lot of money to pay for the doctor's appointments, and the people I borrowed it from wanted it back. They made me rob that store as part of the payment, or they were going to kill Sarah... I think they wanted us gone.” Ryan's voice began to tremble as he remembered everything. “Why would anybody want you two gone?” Gai asked the obvious question, that everyone was wondering. “We moved to Tokyo from Boston... and I had a lot of gambling debt back in America... I think they wanted revenge for me skipping town on their debt. I think this was their payback.” Ryan said with certainty in his voice. “Alright, visitation is up, time to go Solah.” The guard said as he began to pull on the chains binding Ryan's hands. “Please... help him figure out what happened, Please help me.” Ryan pleaded as he was being escorted from the room, wanting just a few more minutes. “I'll find them, Dad... I'll figure out who did this.” Gai suddenly yelled as the door was closing behind Ryan Solah. ~*~ The group was walking back after leaving the prison. There was a long time where nobody said anything, just quietly walking, trying to take everything in. “You alright, Gai?” Itachi finally asked. “I think I'll be alright... It's better to know the truth, then live in a lie. I'm going to be able to deal with all of this.” Gai's voice was weak, he was holding back his emotions. Itachi remembered when he was young, and first started talking like that, first started holding everything back to stay strong. “You serious about helping your father?” Kisame asked, keeping in step behind Gai. “I just need to know the truth... if he didn't do it, I need to know who did.” Gai said stoicly. “Well, what's the next step in your journey?” Itachi asked. “I know why you did this... you wanted me to have a reason to go to America, besides living off of your dreams. Now I have that. We're going to America, to claim Primetime Central's Championship... and to find the truth.” Gai's words end the conversation. None of them spoke again until they were home, and began planning for their next journey.
