Thinking

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 Thinking is defined as the act or process of thinking; cogitation. Thinking is what happens when synapses in your brain create electrical charges and allow you to process complex thought. But you will not have to do this for GTT5. Oh no. Instead, you already have it flagged who you think is going to win… Angelo Deville… Hoyt Williams… Seymour Almasy… Or perhaps the Legend of Pete. But there is a diamond in the ruff and you, without having to think, no this already. There is a wrestler who is waiting in the shadows, biding his time to come out into the spotlight. From out of the darkness, he will rise to the top of the wrestling world and take over…but you already know this. You already know his name, too. Without thinking, you blurt it out already. I don't even have to say it because you've already said it for me. His name is… "James von Doom."
