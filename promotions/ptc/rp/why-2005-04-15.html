Why?

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 Why? A resounding question that it is. One word can become the most powerful question that anyone could ever ask. Why? Why is the sky blue? Why is the earth round? Why is the ocean salty? Why would someone put so much faith in things they can't see? Why? Why? Why? Some questions can be answered quickly? Who? Just give a name. When? Give a time. How? Give an action. What? Just an extension of Who. But that one word... why... conjures up some things that sometimes can't be explained, such as the behaviour of men. Why do men compete? Why do they love? Why do they bother? Or how about... Why would a man who has everything he's ever wanted... jump at the sudden chance to get back into the ring to prove himself once more? He's got a lovely wife. She's given him two lovely children. He's got his own federation and movie production company, and thus has a lot of money. He's the head of a secret oganization that's bent on ensuring that when the world goes... he'll be the one sitting on the top of the trash pile that remains and claim it as his own. But he's getting into the ring once more. This man is a former champion of many federations. His name is whispered in two of PTC's own federations, for different reasons. He's the former GIWF World Champion, the federation that would later become the UWF. His history there speaks for itself. And none there say his name outload for fear of what could happen. His stable, the Knightmare Brigade has been there a few times before. His hand reaches out when it needs to, and strikes those that need to be. He's used such people as his brother in law, Sean McClancy, Cpl. Punishment and Penny Preest to do his bidding and make sure things get taken care of. He's the owner of the umbrella company that provides the 4WW with it's share of the pie. No one there stands up to him. He is the ultimate power when it comes down to it. He runs the place, through such as those he has hired to do so in his name. But even those like the afformentioned Mr. McClancy, his brother in law and life long friend, know better then to question his judgement. For the results of doing so would be very bad. Let's not forget all the other aspects of this business that he's reached out and touched in many ways. The biggest in training such greats as Draven Lox, Ritoru Joufu and Blaise McClancy. But... the question still remains... Why? Fortuntatly not many in this tournement would remember his reign on the top. Where he's shown how ruthless and downright uncaring he can be. He's put a few out of this sport, including the brother in law of Jay Phoenix, Johnny Blaze. So, after all that he's done, we still ask... Why? Why? That's a good question you know? “Yes, I know it is.” Geoffrey Ian Childress III, Ian to his friends and family, answered... himself. A man who had gone by many names, especially those in the ring, and even more outside the ring. He's known as: “The Fallen One” Nephilim: The name that he made his wrestling fame and fortune with. Impulse: The name that he served for his first few months in the PWW as, when others didn't know whom he was. Tetsuo Oni: The “Iron Demon”, the first name he wrestled under when he started out in Okinawa and Southern Japan. The Keeper of Secrets: The title he holds within the Order of the Hammer, his apocalyptic bent organization that he's taken over in the last 5 years. And... The Wrath: A name derived from a little hijacker that sits in his head, a being that was once known as Suriel. A internal conscience of sort that has full and complete contempt for others not like him. A man who now sits in a dark room, on a large highbacked chair. A chair with a red velvet cover for it's seat and backing. Plush in it's cusioning, this chair is where he holds court as the Keeper of Secrets for the Order of the Hammer. A man who at one time, breaks many stereotypes and expectations. You know he's got money and power, so you'd expect a clean cut and fashionable man. He sits there, almost reclining sideways in the large chair, his leg over the left arm. His chin on his fist, he sits and looks to be in contemplation. He wears his usual dress for this type of duty, a white shirt that looks like it would be fitting at a Renaissance Festival, the yeoman look with lacings going up the chest to the collar. His pants are tight, and soft leather. A cloak sits over his shoulders, the brooches that hold it in place holding his rank of station badges. Around his neck sits a medallion that carries the family crest of the family that was once known as “Ishramson”, a family that he's related to on his mother's side. All the while dressed as he was in a medievil times convention, the other adornments belay a more dark future then past. His unkempt hair, shoulder blade length in a rich black with red and gray streaks appearing out of no where, dyed in at a random placing. The metal in his face with it's multiple piercings shows his want for pain and the fact that he really doens't care. A look at his naked body whould show an affinity for all types of things that dealt with pain in his own body with the traditional japaneses style bamboo needle tattoos that still hold their tone in his skin. However, as much as he's into self torture and denial, Ian had never been a man for the hardcore follery that filled wrestling at the time he was in his championship prime. He's always been one for a more strategic, and trained approach. A purist in the martial arts that he leaned as a child. He never even liked really leaving the mat, relying on himself and not his environment to give him the advantage. No Excuses, no crutches. Why are you going to do it, my brother? Why bother with their meager tournements and chances at glory. You've never seeked glory for yourself. “Because there are some who have forgotten what it's like. There are some who don't know me anymore.” Is it about fame? You've had it. It's a hollow thing. “No, it's about control. The natives grow restless. They forget who their true master is. They forget that I'm the one who's been in charge for a time. I've lost some of the control I used to have. It's time to regain it.” He nods to himself, the dialogue going on soley in his head as he watches through the muted light of only a few candles in the sconces of this old Spanish Abbey. He watches as some of his underlings scurry across the marble floor. You know what they're saying though? It's not right for one of your station to lower yourself to this. “My station? I'm a warrior, always have been. I've got to do it for myself and thus do it for the Order. Even do it for the federation that I've put into Sean's hands. They forget that it's not his to own, but to run. And they forget that if things were going the way I didn't want them to... I'd swoop in and put them back. They forget their place though, law suits, attempting to take over what is not theirs. I must make an example of those who would attempt to stand before me to those in the 4WW. My father always said that the military leadership way was to: 'never tell someone to do something you'd not do yourself.' None of them signed up for this. None of them care about the 4WW anymore. So in their absence I'll show them just what it's about. Not a fucking one of them is a team player anymore. And after I do this... it'll be time to remind them just who the boss is. And when one of them does the inevitable and attempts to challenge me, in the back of their head they'll remember me.” Are you ready for this? “You better damn well believe it.” You know that even those here in the abby talk about you and this tourney now. They don't know what to say. Their leader is taking himself into the front lines. But some dont' realize you've been there before. Would you like to hear them? “Yes.” Ian's ice blue eyes track across the room, and pickup on two men who stand lighing a few extra candles. In his mind he hears the high pitched voice of a younger man speaking. “I can't belive the Keeper is going to do that? He's going to fight others to get himself known in the wrestling world? I know he owns the 4WW with the Lady, but why?” Another, deeper voice tunes in. “He's a former big named champion. This won't be anything new. He still trains people at the 4 Winds Academy in Texas. He'll show them how it's done. He's never really left.” The higher voice returns. “But he's not been in the ring for 2 years in a competitive standpoint. And the last match he ever had, he put a man into a coma. Johnny Bl...” “We don't say that name around here. He's not to be spoken of. Just know that he is being taken care of by The Keeper and that all is well with him. However, he has paid for his crimes in accordance with our words and writings.” Ian chouldn't help but smile at that. Rules he had never made, but ones he didn't care too much about. They were certainly more loyal then those in the 4WW, like Todd Dream and Luscious Lady Luck. Then again Todd Dream had been Johnny Blaze's good friend through out a good part of his career, and had been even his stable mate when Johnny Blaze was put out of commission, possibly permantly. Again he listened in. “I still don't get it. Why risk himself in this way? If he wins, great. But if he looses... he risks many things. The others are looking for any sign of weakness in him. They'll pounce in a heart beat and go after his position again.” “No, his blood relation to Lord Ishram itself has been proven. He's solidified his position. Don't worry about such things. Worry about doing your duties and making sure that when the day comes, you're ready to fight with the Order against all of our enemies. No matter if their human, or not.” “I will brother, I will. But still, I don't quite understand one thing... Why?” That question again. Why? Why? Why? “I'll tell you why!” The two men jumped, the younger one dropped the lit candle in his hands. He scrambled to reclaim it before his tan robed caught on fire. Both men turned and looked towards the darkness that was the location of the voice. “I'll tell you why. But the answer may confuse you just as much as it would if you didn't have it. There are many reasons why.” “Yes My Lord, tell us your answers and we'll spread them to the others if they ask.” They knew their position. Then again they didn't want to face his other side either. “The first reason is simple. How can others respect me if they don't know what I'm capable of. How can others fear me if they have never seen what they should fear. How can othes respect me and what I've done if it's history instead of current events?” They nodded their heads. “And after hearing you talk about the last time I was in the ring...” They looked shocked, for their words has been nothing more then whispers. As they visibly shake, Ian... no... Nephilim came to a full upright position in the seat. He then leaned forward. Anyone else would have sworn that the blue of his eyes almost glowed in the dim lights, and that it appears to be a huge set of black feathered wings coming from his shoulders... but the darkness can play tricks on people. These two had heard the rumors of the being that Lord Ishram, had implanted inside of him. A man possessed. Possessed by something that at one time had laid cities to waste as the whim of a god who had forgotten what it was like to be a father. But they shook their heads and all returned to normal, just a silouette of a man sitting in the dark looking at them. “Johnny Blaze was too long ago. I'm a warrior. I must be prepared at all times. I can not lead you all in the final days if I've grown soft and can't do the job anymore. I do this for the Order as much as I do it for me. I do this to show the 4WW whom to respect just as much as I show the idiots in the Black Lodge who wish to rule where I do. But soon, soon there will be something more then this. Soon there will be something called the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. I must be ready for that. I must be ready to reclaim something from my past. The one thing that I never was able to claim before.” They still watched him, intently. As if they were prophets taking down the words from an angel of the Lord, himself. “But most of all, for when I walk into the Memorial for the man once known as Kain Threat... I must be prepared. I bring myself out of what some would call hiding behind a desk for one main reason...” The younger one swalloed a load of spit down in a loud gulp as Ian leaned back in his chair. “More then anything, I give you the biggest reason as being... I'm presenting myseslf as nothing more then...” He pauses, looking at them. The younger one starts to shake, having never spoken to anyone as high up in the Order, let alone something that ona whim by their laws could destroy his life in many, many ways. “... Bait.” “Sir?” The older man spoke out, the younger one too scared to do so. “Yes?” “Bait, sir?” “Yes, Bait. Bait for a man who would know me by many names, including being able to call me by my common name. I come out for him. Seeking him. And in the end, I will find him or he will prove, finally to the world, that he is a coward. In the meantime, I face off against many others, of no consequence. Does me no harm. But the bait is there. I only need to reel in the catch when it bites.” At that, Ian folds his hands together and sits them on top of his chest. The two take the signal and scamper off out of the large room. Nephilim, “The Fallen One” smiles as they go. Half a world away, at the home of the Order of the Hammer in the United States. A little town on the outskirts of Dallas, Texas. We find a woman who's very important to Ian Childress in the mansion that the two own and share there when needing to take care of Order business. She's louning on a large sofa with two other women, her nannys for the two children that she bore last year while flying back from a 4WW show in Japan. Byran and Breanna Childress lay in their cribs just a short distance in the background, the sounds of babies sucking on bottles escapes their lips. The three ladies are sitting there, drinking Tea. The two nannys, provided by the Seelie Court of Ireland to take care of children that are the future of them, as much as they are for the Order, are in their uniforms. While Fey, herself, is wearing what would ammount to a silk and lace teddy and a overcoat of gossamer and lace. All three appear to be enjoying a bit of tea as they talk quietly, so as to not interrupt the young ones. A knock comes to the door of her chambers. “It's open. Ye can come in.” The door opens and one of the acolytes from the Church the Order uses over here comes in, a man who this week is assigned to duties not unlike a butler to a normal mansion-like household. “You'll excuse the interruption madam. But I could not help it. For you have some mail from the company that's marked as urgent and a telegram from Mr. Childress.” Fey, originally annoyed at the interruption, perks up at the mention of her husband sending her a telegram. She puts her tea down. “Well, won'cha be bringin' it over t' me. I can'na be readin' it from there.” The Lady of Secrets' accent rolling off her tongue drives the man to bring the stack of mail to her. She first opens the telegram, that being the most important to her. She scans it over, a smile coming to her face. She then puts it down and opens up the letter from PrimeTime Central, sent via her brother's offices for the 4WW in Seatttle. She scans it over, and smiles even more. “Aye, this is good. Will you ladies excuse me?” The two answer her in unison. “Aye, Mistress. We've got the youngin's until you get back.” Fey stands up and slides her slippers over her feet as she walks out of the room. Since she's in her house, she doens't bother changing, and she heads out into the hallway then down the stairs to the first floor. She walks diligently towards a single room when she gets to the bottom and produces a key from behind the painting next to the door, on the wall. She unlocks the door and then enters, turning on the low level lights. She surveys the room, a room full of many pictures and a few championship title belts all bearing either her name or the name of her husband. Belts from all over the world. However, one object sits in the center of the room, under a glass case. An object that looks like it would be some soft of helmet or breastplate, maybe both. The visage on it though, is one of some sort of demonic or diabolic being, encased in what would be steele or iron. She looks at it, and breathes in heavily. The last time this was used was the match that put Johnny blaze out of action. It was the first match she had fully acted as his valet and was a week before he had taken her as his second wife. It was also two nights after Xían, his first wife had taken her own life. She walks up and stares at it. “It's time fer ye t' be unleashed once again. Even if it's only fer a few nigh's. Ye've been locked up way t' fokin' long.” As that, we fade out. Cut on the dotted line, or so. .......................
