Date: 2005-11-28 Clark Engvold November 28, 2005 Mr. Gibson’s 5th Grade Class Civics GTT 5: Stecchino vs. Vetra - Round 1 Why would in god’s green earth would anyone ever want to hype up a match between Tony Stecchino of GW fame and Peter Vetra of PRIME. Word on the street’s is that both men have no real shot in hell at winning the thing, why would either of them try. In this essay I hope to show that one of these men do have a shot at winning GTT 5 and the man I am picking to win is none other than Tony Stecchino. Don’t mind the spelling I’m a fourth grader and I hate grammar. Oh wait fourth grade was last year. Now politics are going to play a big role in this tournament and everyone knows that politics make the wrestling world go around. Could Ken Partridge Jr. been champion in Global Wrestling if he had not gotten that stain on Bubba’s bright blue dress. Hell no and people would have noticed something like that on top of it. Dark blue and crusty, rest of the dress light blue and silky. But back to what I was saying in the first place. Jesus was Jewish and all you stupid Christians need to realize that and hop on to the winning team. Hell can’t hold anymore Catholics or Republicans. End rant on religion. More on that later. Any way, in PTC there seems to be a big band wagon for people known as Jack, Deville, Almasy, etc. In they eyes of the public they can do no wrong. They could cut a promo anally destroying a flamingo and killing manatee with a cube of butter and it would be the greatest promo in the history of the world. I beg to differ. I would find that appalling and really self serving. They could do that on their own time. I watch wrestling to watch wrestling, not hour long promos that have nothing to do with it. The game has changed, the sport is different everywhere you look and Charles Barkley is the greatest personality on TV. I can’t for his new show Hangin’ With Mr. Barkley costarring Mark Curry and Bob Saget. Hopefully that new Dave Coulier series will work out. Is Mr. Wizard still alive? Where was I? Oh yes… Tony Stecchino can be declared insane by most, but an innovator none the less. Peter Vetra not so much. There are a few people who if they died this moment no one would care or rejoice. Peter Vetra is one of those people, people. Now I am not wishing for Peter Vetra to die, just to disappear for a very long while and possibly never to return. If he was buried in the everglades under a layer or two of swamp moss then that would be just fine with me. If Peter Vetra can’t satisfy a woman, what in the world makes anyone think that he can satisfy a wrestling fan, especially an internet wrestling fan. We are the bitches, stupidest people on the face of the planet, outside Lutherans and Methodists. Actually Scientologists make me cringe, nice little story they believe, I’m happy they believe nonsense. I could sit here and type and type and type on how good Tony Stecchino is, but he is no where near the top darling on the list of internet favorites. Why do you may ask? Well he disappears every now and then, but he has interests outside of wrestling, rumor has it he is trying to break into Hollywood, which is fine and dandy, we need a good legit wrestler that has talent to make the full jump over. I can’t blame the guy, can you? Sure he gets arrested for murder and gets off, then drug possession and then the fake murder of his wife and then his divorce that drove him insane and then he teamed with Maverick Reno and then Reno shot him and everyone thought he was dead. EXCEPT FOR ME! Tony Stecchino will always be in my heart of hearts for as long as I live until death do me part or until some one better comes along that I can hop on their band wagon. I won’t be getting on that bandwagon of Almasy, Deville and Dr. Curiosity and the such because eventually people will get tired of them, just like I am and see they are really no different than the janitor at school who likes to poop in all the stall during recess. Everyone must do it at one point in their lives. They are just normal people. Tony Stecchino is amazing and he deserves the respect he deserves and has earned in the long, long years he has put into this business. How many in GTT5 can say they held a World Title for 18 months straight defending it at least once a month and holding on to it at all costs. Please will they stand up and show themselves. For important title wins and longest reigns there is no one that is better than Tony Stecchino, bar none case closed. In closing I would like to say Tony Stecchino is probably the greatest wrestler of our generation and many young kids can look up to him as a great role model.
