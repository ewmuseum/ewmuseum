PTC — Roleplay

Tag Team Challenge Roleplaying

17 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My partner Noah isn't the only one that has been over worked and tired. I have had press conferences here and there. A match in WWW that he has already mentioned. I just recently one the WMW championship again that alone brings a burden down on my shoulders. Sure I love the fact I am the champion once again. But everything that comes with the title can be allot. In WMW I have to worry about the all in battle royal a match that will determine who will face me for the WMW Greatlakes title and on the other hand I have to worry about the PTC tag challenge match. That will put Noah and I (Soul Purpose against the team of the Killer Pirates of the Outter Caribbean. I know what your thinking what kinda name is that? Well I can't tell you but all I know is that when the time comes to face them Noah and I will be ready. We want to make a name for ourselves and the WMW and now is the perfect chance. (The scene takes us. To St. Paul, Mn in the personal gym of WMW superstar Fury. He is standing in front of a punching bag nailing it with left and rights. A few kicks here and a few kicks there. He stops as he is breathing really heavy and trying to catch his breath.) God I have been working myself to the bone lately, first my match with Shadow on Burnout took me to my limits and then some. Then in the same night I faced Justin Evers for the WMW title. My body is sore, my knee is driving me crazy but yet here I am still training my ass off. (Fury takes one more deep breath then strikes the bag once more.) Everyone close to me keeps telling me that I need to think about taking a break. I hear them a break would be nice but for some reason I just can't seem to bring myself to taking that needed time off. Something is keeping me from hanging up my boots for a week or two. (Fury just shrugs his shoulders as he takes a step back then lunges forward striking the bag hard with a swift kick.) Something is telling me that I need to keep moving I have to keep doing something. Is there something I am running from? Is there something that I have done recently or something that has happened recently that I am hiding from? Am I unsure of something? (Fury stops once again and sits there to think. The sweat pouring down his face.) I know it's not the fact that I won the Greatlakes title I welcome that challenge. It's not cause of the fact that I joined another federation besides WMW. Everyone needs to fine-tune their skills more every now and then. What the hell have I done recently that is making me keep myself busy? (Fury punches the punching bag.) I just can't put my finger on it. What the hell have I done recently other then asking Tasha to marry me? (Fury not realizing that punching bag was in full swing gets hit by the punching bag and it staggers him backwards a bit. He sits there and shakes his head.) I think I know what it is now. The fact that I asked Tasha to marry me is still playing in the back of my mind. It was the right thing for me to do. I love Tasha with all my heart, So why is it distracting me so damn much? (Suddenly footsteps can be heard.) Voice: Is it cause your getting married to the most beautiful women on this planet? (The voice brings a smile to Fury's face as he turns around and is met by a blonde beauty. His fiancee and manager Tasha. Tasha slowly walks up to him puts her arms around Fury's neck and plants a kiss on his lips.) Tasha: Gross you’re all sweaty. Fury: I can't help it. I was training. Or atleast trying to. Tasha: What do you mean trying to? Fury: I keep getting distracted. Something is playing with my focus. (Tasha frowns.) Tasha: Your over working yourself. (Fury laughs.) Fury: No I'm not. I've been going through the same work schedule as always nothing more nothing less. Tasha: Riight.Hon you have taken on allot more since you have become the Commissioner of the Wrestling Midwest. Then you went and took on another wrestling job. Your over worked. {{She could be right but I'm not going to tell her that. I can handle everything I'm perfectly fine.}} Fury: Nah really I'm fine. I just can't focus that's all. (Tasha rolls her eyes at Fury's weak attempt to try and convince her that he is handling the heavy workload that he has on himself.) Tasha: You know when I think about it, You and Noah are allot alike. Fury: What are you talking about? How did Noah get dragged into this conversation? Tasha: Well cause of the simple fact that you’re both hard headed and don't like to admit when you’re being over worked. Maybe its just men in general you guys always have to be hardheaded bastards. Fury: I love you you know that? Tasha: Well I love you to but that still doesn't change the fact that you’re being hard headed. You and Noah both are. Fury can you honestly tell me when the last time we actually got to sit down and have a talk with Noah, Kristina about something other then wrestling and business? (Fury sits there for a moment he is clearly trying to think of a time but is drawing a blank. He smiles finally like he got something.) Fury: Last week actually. We all got together at Noah's home and had a nice talk. (Tasha frowns) Tasha: Yeah about wrestling and the upcoming all in battle royal. And the day before that we talked about this big challenge tag match you two have against those pirate fools. {{Ok I see her point there really hasn't been anytime for relaxing but I can't help that.}} Tasha: Your just taking on way too much. You’re not going to have time to heal right. (Fury kisses Tasha on the lips once again.) Fury: I’ll be all right besides it's not that, which I am worried about. We still have to plan our wedding and all that good stuff. Tasha: Noah has to plan his wedding to but you guys won't take the time to sit down with your ladies and get things done. (Fury frowns.) Fury: You know I always have time to get things done. Tasha: When it comes to wrestling yeah of course you do. When it comes to this wedding stuff and alone time together you have been kinda well you know. Distant. Are you getting cold feet? (Fury sighs as he looks into Tasha's eyes. He isn't exactly pissed off but more disappointed then anything at the fact that she thinks he would be getting cold feet.) Fury: Tasha I asked you to marry me for a reason. I love you and I want to spend the rest of my life with you. You have been there for me when other people haven't. Tasha: Then why have you been drowning yourself in work? Everytime I bring up the wedding you kinda step around the subject. Fury: Cause Tash last time I helped with wedding stuff look what happened. (Tasha suddenly comes to realize that Fury has a fear of messing up this wedding too. For those that don't know Fury was to be married to a lady by the name of Lisa Smith she was Fury's manager at the beginning of his WMW career but cheated on him with his cousin and rival Justin Evers. Fury was fully into that wedding helped whenever he could. The time he could have used for training he used to help plan the wedding. He doesn't want the same thing to happen in this case. Cause you know what they always say history is bound to repeat itself.) Tasha: You have to trust me that nothing is going to happen if you help with this wedding. You asked me to marry you. So there for you have to help me plan this thing or I'll pull my hair out. (Fury smiles.) Fury: Yeah I'll help but I'm not jumping in to it face first. Besides it’s a women's instinct to be better at these things anyway. Tasha: That is true men are such boneheads. Fury: Hey I'm not a bonehead. Tasha: Well ok you’re not a complete bonehead. Atleast not yet. But if you take a few more chair shots anytime soon I wouldn't doubt it. (Fury suddenly lifts Tasha up over his shoulder and she starts hitting him.) Tasha: Let me down bonehead. Fury: Not until you take it back. Tasha: I’m not taking back anything. (Fury begins to spin around in a circle.) Tasha: Ok, Ok I take it back. (Fury stops spinning and puts Tasha down and she falls forward to his chest.) Fury: That’s exactly what I thought. Tasha: You’re an ass sometime you know that? Fury: I know. (Tasha and Fury both laugh as they walk away from the punching bag towards a bench that holds a bottle of water and the WMW Greatlakes title. Fury sits on the bench first and Tasha sits on his lap. She puts the Greatlakes title over her lap.) Tasha: So champ you think Soul Purpose will be ready for this match? Fury: I’m sure Noah is ready. I don't know if I am ready yet. Tasha: Why’s that? {{I know nothing about these guys. The only thing I know about them is that they are from LOW atleast I think they are.}} Fury: I know nothing about them. You know how much I hate going into match’s blind. Tasha: Yeah I know but there really isn't much you can do about it unless you can get ahold of some of their matches. Fury: I don't think I can handle something like that after watching their latest promo. (Tasha smiles.) Tasha: I thought it was cute it was kinda like a kids show on PBS or something. {{Cute my ass it damn near made me gag. These guys made a challenge and if they are as serious as Noah and I are about getting a shot at the PTC Tag Straps then they are going to have to do a whole lot better then that. If not Noah and I will tear through them.}} Fury: Cute...Yeah sure but cute won't win them a chance at the PTC Tag titles. Hell cute won't get them known at all. They can be as cute as they want but it won't help them win this match. Tasha: Ok maybe cute was the wrong word. Entertaining then. Fury: Try more like sad and pathetic. These guys are sitting out there "traveling'" on a boat when Noah and I are here getting ready for this match. {{That's one of the things I can't stand when someone makes a challenge you would think that they would take the match seriously. That they would be doing anything and everything they can to prepare for it. Now I don't know if traveling on their pirate ship is the way they train. Noah and I on the other hand like to do things the hard way. Spare in the ring take bumps and work each other over a bit. Do some homework on our opponents. That right there will give us the edge in this match.}} Tasha: Does it bug you that bad baby? (Fury nods) Fury: Yeah it does. It bugs the hell outta me, but there is nothing I can do until Noah and I meet them in the ring. We have something to prove and if we have to make an explain out of these two then we will it's as simple as that. These guys made the challenge we accepted it. Now the only thing for us to do now is.... Tasha: Win. (Fury nods) Fury: Listen I want to get dressed and take a shower I don't want to sit here stinking all day and all night. So you feel like waiting for me? (Tasha kiss Fury on the lips then gets up off his lap and sets the Greatlakes title on his shoulder.) Tasha: You shower up and all that good stuff I'm going to go across the street and look around. Fury: Ok I'll be out in a few. Tasha: Or you could come out all.... Fury: Don’t even say it. I'll come to the store when I am done. (Tasha kisses Fury on the cheek then heads out the door. Fury grabs his bag and heads towards the showers.) I've been in two PTC events so far. I competed in the Cruiserweight tournament and then I competed in GTT4. Everytime I competed I have had a great time. I got to face different talent from different federations in the PTC. Those two times I took place in those tournaments I never expected to win I just wanted to see how far I could go. I just wanted to see if I could make it past the first round. This time this match against the Pirates I'm not in this thing to see how far I can go. (Fury looks intensely into the camera as a smirk comes on his face.) I'm not in this thing to see how long it will take me to be knocked out. This time it's different Noah and I are in this thing to put not only ourselves on the map but the WMW as well. First we face the Pirates from LOW then we will face whoever steps to the plate next. Then when it is all said and done we will have our shot at the PTC Tag Team Titles and we will meet the champion’s head on and we will win. Cause It's Our Purpose.... OUR SOUL PURPOSE. (Fury makes his way to the shower room and the scene) FADES TO BLACK
