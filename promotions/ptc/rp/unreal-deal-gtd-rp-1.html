Unreal Deal [GTD RP #1]

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Real Deal Public Access Pimp Joined: 10 Feb 2003 Posts: 30 Location: Over at SurReal's finishing off my Jack D! Posted: Fri Apr 11, 2003 7:32 pm Post subject: Unreal Deal [GTD RP #1] [The scene opened in the large room we were in previously. The Real Deal still had his eyes closed and was thinking single thoughts to himself] **One Step** **One Step Closer** **One Step Closer To My Fate** **I have never felt this way in my life before. I have a feeling of extreme emptiness. I don’t know how to deal with it. Here I am. Lying on the ground, a prime target for Axe and his men, but what do I do? I stay here. It is as if I have no more feeling in my body or in my mind. I don’t care if many of his guards came walking in here right now. They would feel my wrath like none other** [At this point a chilling breeze passed over him. His hair moved slightly but he himself stayed completely still] **The breeze is calming. It is cold but that helps to calm down the burning desire within me** ^^Meanwhile^^ [We see two guards standing in front of a half circular metallic desk. Sitting behind it is Axe] Guard #1: Sir, Jinn was unable to take out The Real Deal. He did succeed in however in killing his wife Atlantis. Axe: What’s the status of Jinn. Guard #2: Dead Sir. [Axe smashed his fist off the table making the guards jumps backwards] Axe: Useless! Guard #1: What should we do? Axe: FIND THE REAL DEAL…NOW!!! Guards: YES SIR! [The guards then turned around to face the opposite direction and walked out of the room. Axe held his head with his right hand and sighed] Axe: You will never win Real Deal! [His voice echoed throughout the room. We then went back to The Real Deal who was still lying on his back] **I really should move to avoid confrontation but hell if there’s anything I’ve loved over the years…it’s confrontation** [He then started searching his right pocket and brought out the item that was given to him by his wife Atlantis. It gleamed brightly from the light coming from the ceiling. The Real Deal opened his eyes for the first time and looked straight into its centre. Suddenly a glimmer of silver light shot across the eyes of The Real Deal. He blinked for a second and sat up] **Magic…** [Real Deal then put his right hand out with his palm facing up and set the object in its centre. He then spoke softly] Real Deal: Exhale desire… [A small beam of light appeared from the small orb and engulfed the face of The Real Deal. The light then expanded outwards and showed the two guards who were talking to Axe earlier moving towards the room. The Real Deal then immediately stood up and moved to the far end of the room. He stood with the orb in between both his hands. Watching the door because he knew the guards would emerge at any time. They were heavily armed with rifles and were obviously going in for the kill. This didn’t seem to phase Real Deal though, who was only equipped with a pistol and a small dagger. His eyes widened as he saw in the orb that they had reached the door. He could hear them speak inside his mind] **Guard #1: We go in, drill him and that will be it!** **Guard #2: Don’t get cocky now Mike. Anything can happen with this guy.** **Guard #1: Don’t worry about it.** [We saw a picture of them close up. The first guard was typing in a number to open in the door. He typed 4327 and the door opened. The two men stormed the room; they opened fire as soon as they entered the room…though The Real Deal was nowhere to be found] Guard #1: Where’d he go?! Guard #2: I dunno. He couldn’t have got by us. Guard #1: You take the right hand side of the room. I’ll take the left. Guard #2: Gotcha! [The two starting searching the room but their mission to find The Real Deal wasn’t successful. They looked at each other strangely before the first guard noticed something in the corner] Guard #1: Wait here. [The first guard walked towards the far right hand corner not really knowing what to expect] **Caught between the visions of hate** **Can’t breathe under this weight of existence** [The guard kneeled down in the corner and picked up the object he found] Guard #1: I found something! Guard #2: What is it? Guard #1: It looks like some sort of sphere. [The guard started walking towards his friend holding the orb of The Real Deal’s up] Guard #1: This must have been The Real Deal’s. Guard #2: I don’t care if it’s his. I just want to know where he is. [Wherever The Real Deal was…he was thinking to himself. Thinking about the guards] **Soon you will know where I am. Then again, you’ll only know for a number of seconds before your untimely death. You can bring your whole army and still not defeat me. You cannot defeat the magic** Guard #2: Uhhh Mick… Mick: Yea? Guard #2: Look at the orb. [Mick turned the sphere around and it was glowing pure red. He suddenly started to scream with pain as the orb got brighter and brighter until it was as white as snow. Mick then dropped it and it smashed into about eight different pieces] Guard #2: Good one Mick. What we going to do now? Mick: What the hell are you talking about Dave? Look at my hand! That thing starting burning it. Dave: Always a cry baby Dave. Mick: Shut up and lets find Real Deal! [A voice then echoed out throughout the room] Voice: If something is all around you…why search for it? [Mick and Dave looked around in shock. They didn’t know what to think] Dave: Mick…we gotta get out of here. Mick: Why? Dave: Look at the orb. [Mick looked down at the broken orb. Each piece was transforming into a sharp object like the dagger The Real Deal had before. Dave started to run towards the door but didn’t make it very far before being stabbed in the back of the head, neck, arms and legs with each of the individual pieces of the orb. He dropped to the ground immediately and Mick ran over to him] Mick: Dave? DAVE! [Mick held his head. He looked like he was going insane. He couldn’t believe what he had just seen] Voice: One of you out of the way… [The sharp orb daggers moved themselves out of the bloodied flesh of Dave apart from the one that had went through his head. They rose up into the air and joined together once more to reform the orb, which then fell to the ground. Mick looked straight at it. His body paralysed. The orb became a bright blue colour. We then saw a light emerge once more towards the ceiling] Voice: You have been around far too long my friend. How could you have ever survived…look at you, you can’t move can you? [Mick tried to get the words out of his mouth but he just couldn’t, he was finding it hard to take a breath never mind speak] [The light then transformed itself in a shape of a human figure. It then became evident that it was The Real Deal. As the light materialised we could see a wide evil grin on his face. Mick’s eyes opened wide as he saw a fist coming towards him. With no problem, The Real Deal’s fist went right through Mick’s head. As The Real Deal fully reappeared his checked out the damage he had done and then pulled his fist back out. He held it up and shook off any entrails that were left. He then turned to look at the door] **They think they can defeat me…FOOLS! They will all perish by my hand and I will stand tall in this land** [The door slid open and fifteen plus guards entered the room] Lead Guard: Time’s up Deal! Real Deal: Indeed it is…For You! [We then saw The Real Deal shatter into thousands, if not millions of pieces. They shot towards the guards at a skin ripping speed. The guards looked on in horror as they moved closer. The screen then faded to black] [End] _________________ You will become one with the mat! - The Real Deal. #7 PTC - Week Beginning 20th January
