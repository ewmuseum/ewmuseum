Date: 2005-03-20 THE PROFESSOR Alas poor woof woof, I knew him well. He was a man of infinite jest. Oh well, he was a man like any other and, like all men, death was bound to come to him at some point. But did it really have to be so soon? Such a shame, such a shame. Oh but sitting here and dwelling on the death itself isn't going to accomplish anything. Let me tell you a story about woof, it takes place when he was a pupil of mine. It was the summer of 1996. I was starting my tenth year at **** and I was beginning to grow tired of the whole university teaching scene. You see, Alastair MacEntyre, my closest friend and a really great professor, had died earlier that year in a really horrific boating accident. It seems that while water-skiing with his lovely wife, Virginia, Alastair's ski rope had gotten tangled in the engine and so he got off his skis and swam over there to check it out and as he was doing this he somehow lost his footing and went ass-over-teakettle into the propeller itself, scattering bits of him all over the side of their luxury yacht. Now, when Virginia explained this to me I found it to be, from a physical aspect, a nearly impossible tale. She assured me that it most definitely happened that way and my inability to imagine it obv. came from my lack of schooling in the field of physics. She said that Henry Q. Flossbringer, a tenured Physics Prof at the University, could probably explain it to me if I asked nice enough and gave him a bottle of cognac for his troubles. I said no that's all right. She told me that the funeral was going to be in two weeks and that afterwards they were going to cremate him and scatter his ashes over Nixon's grave. Alastair always loved Nixon. Anyway, it was during this not altogether easy part of my life that I first encountered the boy who would be woof. He came to me fresh out of one of the top boarding schools in the country, his mind an open sponge waiting to be filled with all the knowledge of many things, of fools and kings &c. So but anyway it was my first day of class. I was teaching a course on Postmodern Criticism in the Pre-Post-Mid-Post-Feminist-Post-Industrial Age that semester entitled 'Postmodern Criticism in the Pre-Post-Mid-Post-Feminist-Post-Industrial Age 101' and I was really looking forward to it as it was something I was deeply fascinated by that that point. Anyway, one of my students that semster was one woof J. woof. Now, and here's the funny part, on the first day of class as I was reading off the class list I got to woof woof's name and I stopped and I looked at the class and said to them 'Is this somebody's idea of a joke?' and they just all looked at me like I was crazy and didn't know just what the fuck I was talking about and so I decided to shed some light on the matter 'Who wrote "woof J. woof" on my class list! I demand to know!' and then as I was standing up there with smoke probably coming out of my ears because I was so mad woof woof raised his hand and said 'Excuse me, Sir, but my rwaly actual honest name is woof J. woof. It's not joke.'And I was just flustered and the class had a good laugh at my expense! That crazy woof woof! He dropped my class the next day and that was the first and last time I actually had personal interaction with him. SORRY ABOUT THAT, THAT PERSON RESPONSIBLE HAS BEEN SACKED. WOOF WOOF IS NOT DEAD. REVOLUTION SERVED WITH A SIDE OF PANCAKES The dismembered torso of woof woof's 6th Grade Homeroom teacher has somehow found it's way into his, woof woof's, recent dreams. It always shows up at the same time during the same dream which dream begins with woof woof walking down a silent and empty hallway. ON his head he is wearing a party hat and across his chest is a sash that says 'WORLD's BEST MOM.' He is not wearing pants. On his feet are those deck shoes worn by rich east-coasters on their yachts. He is also wearing a pair of thick black rimmed glasses and a Groucho Marx moustache. He walks nearly the lenghth of the hall but when he comes to the fifteenth door on the right he enters the room. Inside is his First (But Not One-And-Only True) Love, Eleanor. She is lying prostrate on a surgeons table with her legs spread open. Sans a sash that says, 'WORLD'S BEST DAD' she is completely naked. As woof woof approaches her she sits up and looks at him and silently mouths the words 'You Are Loved' before reaching down to her crotch and turning on a faucet that has replaced her more womanly parts. From this faucet issues forth a stream of pink liquid that immidiately begins to fill the room. woof begins to attempt to swim through it to Eleanor but whenever he gets close she places a knee-high leather boot-clad foot on his forehead and pushes him under as she hums the hook from 'In da Club' While under the pink liquid woof can clearly see his 6th grade homeroom teacher. She is giving him the thumbs up whilst fellating Jimmy Stewart. woof manages to fight to the surface. The room is no longer a room but an ocean and Eleanor is no longer a girl but an island. woof woof extends his hand for her but the tide is too strong and he's washed out to sea. He wakes up sweaty and screaming. THE FALL OF WESTERN CIVILIZATION WILL COME NOT WITH A BANG BUT A WHIMPER That was the title of an essay by Alastair MacEntyre, composed just days before his (supposed) death. In it he states that western civilization is actually a sock puppet controlled by a monkey in a lab coat. This was clearly meant as a joke but has, in recent times, been adopted by many university humanities departments as a truly credibly theory. Noam Chomsky recently wrote an essay saying that it't not a monkey in a lab coat that controlls the sock puppet but actually multi-national oil cartels. Chomsky could not be reached for comment at this time. After Chomsky's essay, entitled 'America Is The Cause for All the World's Troubles but I Don't Hate it, Honest,' was released it was answered by an essay from leading conservative intellectualy Rush Limgaugh which essay said that the monkey in the lab coat was actually the Liberal Media. Limbaugh's essay, entitled 'Excellence in Essay Writing,' is now being scrolled across Fox News's 'News Ticker' 24/7. It is not known at this time what any of this has to do with woof woof other than the fact that a few people have put forward the theory that he, woof woof, is actually the monkey in the lab coat.
