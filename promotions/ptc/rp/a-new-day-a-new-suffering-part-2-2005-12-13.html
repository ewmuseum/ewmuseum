A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 2

13 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-13 Guilt, that is what he felt as he walked through the streets of Old San Juan. The blue paving stones that the Spaniards placed through the streets hundreds of years before were still there, silent. The blood that had been spilled on them throughout the years turned blue to hide the passion with which it was spilled. People destroyed over their religious beliefs. He knew that religion was an important part of the Spanish society that conquered the small island of Puerto Rico, but he never could understand it. They told him while he was growing up that he had to believe, or else he was going to hell. He never believed them. It all seemed too far-fetched to be true. A lot of people believed though, and he didn’t mind, but he got tired of those few that tried to "guide" him into the light. Those few fanatics that even their own Jesus would be ashamed of. These were the thoughts passing through Digital’s mind as he walked in front of what in Puerto Rico is called the Iglesia de la Fuente de Agua Viva or the Church of the Living Water Fountain or whatever the hell that means. It was funny, because the leader of this church had just bought two mansions worth over one million dollars each. Was every religious leader such a hypocrite? Digital had no idea, but he knew that most of the people who called themselves religious or quote the Bible every couple of words are the least religious of them all. It is not about knowing the dogmas, it is about living them. Digital knew that most people did not live them. It was also funny that most people didn’t think a person that did not believe in their God could be happy. They couldn’t conceive of a man that is good without having to believe in their God. Digital thought he was a pretty good guy. He had made some wrong decisions in his life, but he had gone through it with his chin up and changed his ways. As he entered the church or congregation building as they like to call it, his stomach kept churning. He knew this wasn’t a place he wanted to be, he could feel the air around him compressing. To be truthful, he hated it. He couldn’t stand being in that place because he knew what would follow. The people making "holier-than-thou" speeches in front of awe-struck listeners that had nothing better to do with their money than to give it to their supposed leader. Digital couldn’t understand it. This place seemed more like a club than a church. There was a room with refreshments, a room with a pool table and a big screen TV, an indoor pool for the kids… the unfortunate kids who are not given their chance to choose. The young people obligated by the masses to pick a side just because they have already done so and ostracizing those who do not. He opened the door to the main chamber and was greeted by a fellow with a wide smile. In his hands he held what Digital assumed were the prayers of the day, the announcements of upcoming events or a list of merchandise that they were selling in their gift shop. Merchandising God like they merchandise any other character created by human imagination. "No, that’s okay. I’m just here to speak to Veronica’s father." "Oh, okay. He will be finished with the first part of the mass in a few minutes. You can sit down there if you want." The man with the papers aimed his finger towards the last chair in the back of the chamber. They wouldn’t want some guy to come in here and start any trouble. Nobody liked the people from the Living Water Fountain. Not even the Evangelical groups that they represented. They were the worst of the worst to the other religious groups on the island. Even the Mitas, a Puerto Rican created religion, hated their guts and with reason. Anyone who in their creed has "We believe in the duty of paying tithes and giving offerings to indentify ourselves as sons of God," is definitely taking advantage of the ignorant. Digital watched as the preacher spoke about commitments and about what it meant to really be part of the congregation. As he said these words he watched the new people and pierced their hearts with his gaze. That was one of the things that he had learned in all his time as a preacher for the church. The other was to play with words and use his tone of voice to convince people that what he said was right. Digital couldn’t believe anything he said now. After what had happened in his house, after what he had done to his family and these people standing here could in no way know. "The operative design of our mission is not limited to conduct modification but to the involution of a new conscience." Digital was tired of this. He couldn’t listen one more moment as the preacher used the power of the tone of voice to catch the listener’s soul and never let go of it. "Vayan con Dios y en paz." He finished the gathering with the usual. The preacher had already seen Digital sitting there and was not at all surprised. There was one member missing in this meeting they would have. Veronica had not arrived yet. She was supposed to have been there already, but he guessed that she had run on a little traffic. The streets of Old San Juan could get very congested in the afternoon. Digital stood up and respectfully waited for the preacher to finish shaking hands with his parishioners. The preacher, dressed in a nice grey suit walked towards Digital while shaking hands with those in the way. Digital had called him the night before to tell him that he would be passing that day with Vero. "How are you, Antonio? Did you enjoy the service? I think you can learn something from it." "Frankly, I tire of all these Christian platitudes." Alberto, the preacher and Vero’s father, chuckles at the comment but in his heart he knows that some day Antonio would come to realize that there was no real living without something to believe in. There is no living without God according to him. "When you reach my age, you will realize how important it is to believe in God and in his son Jesus Christ." "I would appreciate it if you didn’t preach to me, sir. I live my life according to what I think is right and not what God tells me. I do not need to come to some congregation and pay royalties so that I can be forgiven. How can you not see that your leader is corrupt? Stealing the money of these ignorant people in order to get mansions for himself and his family? You must really be blind." Alberto had heard that argument a lot of times and he still couldn’t believe what had been done was really that bad. That money was used to show their leader that they were all willing to give up their material things in order to follow God. "Why do you speak to me with such disrespect? My wife has poisoned my children against me and turned you against me as well. I knew she had something up her sleeve to take me out of her life and get my money." The more he spoke to this man, the more he reminded him of his opponent in the tournament. This man was so blinded by religion that his whole life did not just revolved around it but needed it, desired it to survive. Religion was one of his vices. It was his cocain. It was his Valium. When you come right down to it, his opponent and Vero's father were exactly the same, except that Hoyt had found a way to release his anger on the ring, this preacher didn't. Hoyt Williams thought that God was on his side. Digital did not need God to beat his opponents, he just needed himself. Digital shook his head silently, listening to the ravings of a drunk who couldn’t remember what had really happened. He was listening to a man who had long since forgotten who he really was, a man of his God, a preacher who beat his family up. Delusions are what he was having, the alcohol had fried his brain. Digital knew what that was like. "Do you have any idea of what you are doing to your children? How Vero came to me because she didn’t dare to talk to you alone? Do you realize that you are destroying your own family! Did you listen to yourself right now? Do you actually believe that your wife is poisoning your children against you? Think about what you just said, it is ludicrous." Alberto shook his head. He thought that he was right. "You have no idea what is really going on. You are living in this world that your mind has created and not in the reality. Don’t you see that? Puñeta! Can’t you realize what you are doing to your family?" "You have no idea what has been going on! You stand there judging me as an evil man, but I am not the one who fights for a living. I am not the man who let his child die!" This was a blow to the gut. He couldn’t believe that trying to help this man because he was the father of the woman he loved that he would get blamed for what happened two years ago. Digital could not respond, feelings of both anger and sadness at the memory of Justine and their unborn child were just too strong. At that moment, Vero arrived. She heard what her father said and saw their faces. One was red with anger the other was in a state of shock. She was surprised at what her father had become. He had once been a preacher for the church, explaining to others who God was and now he was no more than a shell of the man she remembered. She went over to Digital and hugged him, knowing that her touch would calm the waters inside of him. Digital had a very dark past, but he had learned to keep it deep down. Veronica knew of this past, it was one of the things they both talked about when they started going out. The death of the woman he loved and his unborn baby had plunged Digital into an all-time low. He tried to commit suicide after that, but his friend Burton had found him before he had died. "How could you say something like that, father?" "I said it because it was true! If he had been in his house with his woman, nothing would have happened to her. He left her to fight someplace or other and left them high and dry! Do you call yourself a man after that?" Digital moved Vero out of the way carefully. He had had enough. "You throw out accusations like you have any idea what really happened. You don’t know the streets that you are walking with those comments, but I agree with something, I was too quick to start making accusations. This kind of discussion is not the real reason why we are here. We wanted to know your side of the story. What do you remember, and how we could help you, but you just seem to get the worst out of me with your ‘holier-than-thou’ attitude." "Please, stop this fighting! I just want to fix my family, not separate it even more. Please dad, tell us what you remember." Vero, always the voice of reason started the conversation how it should have been done. Digital knew that he had been in the wrong to start arguing with her father, but he seemed to be able to press the right buttons. "Well, what I remember from that day is that I was trying to have a discussion with your mother and all of a sudden your brother was trying to kill me. He attacked me right in front of you!" That was the moment where Digital and Veronica knew that he needed psychological help. That was the moment they realized that this man was not in his right state of mind, they had to take him to a psychologist. They didn’t understand though, why had he started to drink so much. Digital thought about the tournament. He didn't want to leave Vero to fend for herself. It wouldn't be fair. It wasn't right. She had been there for him when he needed her, and he was going to be there for her. He was going to do everything in his power to help. Even if it meant no showing the tournament. She wouldn't let him do that though. She was too stubborn and she cared too much for him to let him throw out his future just like that. It was hard, being torn by the two, but in the end he knew that if things got worse, he would give it all for her. He loved Veronica. She was his only vice. She was his cocain. She was his Valium. Alberto saw the faces of Digital and Veronica and knew suddenly that he was wrong, and that he needed help. It was honestly all that he could remember, he would swear on God to it. "Why, God? Why?" He put his hands on his face to cover up his shame. He didn’t want his daughter to see him like this and worst of all, how would all his parishioners feel when they heard about this? What would they do? They would never listen to him again; no one would listen to him again. He would lose it all. Pride is a very strong element in human relations. "You can’t tell this to anyone, Vero. I… I don’t know what I might do if I lose this place." "What? You are talking about your family here. We are more important than this place! You have always put this place over us, your faith over us. This once, think of us first! Do you know what this has done to mom?" His demeanor changed with the mention of his wife. In all of this he still blames her for everything. Why? "Don’t mention that scorpion woman to me! She has poisoned all of you against me. She always wanted to leave me and now this is his way, he waited for me to snap so that she could use it against me in court! I hate her! I…hate…I love her… la amo." He broke down, he was crying now in full view of both of them, he had never done that. He had never cried in front of his children, not even when his mother died. The walls were slowly crumbling down. Digital could see it; it had happened to him as well, he could feel it in Alberto. They needed to find out why at the mention of her he broke down. Digital thought that it was much easier to ask. "Why do you hate and love her at the same time?" "She… she has been cheating on me…" Surprise. Digital knew how it felt. The paranoia, the suspicion, it was impossible to live like that, so he hid in a bottle to cover the pain and the fear. He hid his feelings until they just burst out. In some it was tears, self-mutilation or even suicide, in him it was aggression. That is what happened that day. All his frustrations mounted up and made him do something that he knew he would always regret but it didn’t matter because he had lost all ways of reasoning. So his family had to take the brunt of it. Veronica had never been so unhappy in her life, watching her parents like this. It must be very hard for anyone to watch their parents acting like this, but the one person that would get the biggest impact was Vero’s little sister, who had fallen unconscious for a few hours and now had a scar on her head to remind her of the time her father had almost killed her. Not only would she carry that scar, but wound would be much deeper. In her heart.
