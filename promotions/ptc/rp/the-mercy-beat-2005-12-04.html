Date: 2005-12-04 The dark room only showed a shadow of a man sitting, shaking ferociously. Strewn across the table in front is blood and drugs, the bag of heroin ripped open and made into a mess. He makes no notice of this, though, staring into space, a lone tear dropping down his cheek. "Oh what a night this is gonna be. The world can sleep without me." From below the table the man pulls out a handgun. It shines in the darkness, and as it pressed against the man’s temple, his face is revealed. He is covered in dirty bandages, but his dirty, blank eyes are piercing. The hammer is pulled back. And now, Drake’s eyes close. What has happened to our hero? ---------------------------------------- Born in 1944, Drake Coleman was raised in Winthrop, a suburb of Boston. There, he was an All-American in Football, Basketball, and Baseball. If you looked up the term "Ideal Young Man" in the dictionary, you’d see Drake’s slicked back black hair and square-jaw smile there, glancing one right back at you. He was gorgeous, as well, and in fact engaged to his high school sweetheart, Charlene. Following the Coleman family tradition, though, he enlisted in 1962 and, soon after, sent off to fight in Vietnam. Things were going well there, as Drake became the hero. Numerous times he was cited for saving his fellow soldiers in war. It was rumored that he was being used on war posters as the ideal soldier. One day in 1964, though, everything changed… ---------------------------------------- *BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!* "Holy fuck! Air raids! Jesus, take cover!" "Sarge is gone, so is Jenkins!" "We’re doomed!" *SHHHHHHUUUUUUUUU* "DRAKE, WATCH OUT!" ---------------------------------------- Drake was burned, badly. From head to toe he was covered in 3rd degree burns. Somehow, though, he lived, and was taken back to the medical infirmary. From there, he spent years stuck in a full body cast. During these years, Drake spent most of his time with his thoughts. Quickly, his brain deteriorated into a mass of anger and angst, pissed off at everything. Why didn’t they just finish me off?, was a common question to himself, as every day was a day in pain. Soon, he grow scarily independent and antisocial, not speaking with anyone. Finally, they removed the casts and began the rehab. Through out his years of not moving, Drake gained back much of his strength and mobility. Unfortunately, though, his skin stayed charred black and scarred. He looked like a walking burn victim. So, in order to protect the skin, the military issued Drake with a lifetime supply of Band-Aid+, a type of bandage that was super strong as well as resistant to fire, wear, etc. Once Drake was strong enough to support himself in the real world, the military let him go and, quite frankly, forgot about him. Veterans wouldn’t believe he was part of the war, and current soldiers (and most people) shied away from him to begin with. From there on, Drake was on his own. As most people refused to hire him for obvious reasons, Drake needed to find a way to support himself. Realizing that he could use his pent of anger and aggression to his advantage, he quickly learned the martial arts and made his way over to Asia where he fought in an underground death match league. Years of this really brought him into insanity. Every night was the same: He’d step in the ring, kill some unworthy opponent, and then vanish in the darkness. He gained the nick name "The Bonfire", as the fans noticed he was covered in burns. He assumed that, since he was so aggressive, that he was born from one, and since the name has stuck. Now, Drake just roams the world, looking for something to keep him alive. Every once in a while he’ll wrestle in a small promotion, but he’s done with the spotlight. It was all too much, almost bringing him to death. The foot was down: No more prime-time wrestling. Until one day… ---------------------------------------- *RINNNNGGGG* The room was bright, the sun shining into it. The off color walls and puke-stained carpet shone in the sunlight, casting a sense of putrid homeliness. The bed in the center of the room had springs poking out, looking as uncomfortable as ever. The sun glimmered off these, adding a nice touch to it all. Lying on the bed was Drake Coleman, facing the ceiling with his arms and legs spread out. His eyes were wide open, black as coal and blank as a new sheet of paper. The only garments he had on were the bandages, stained in blood and only-God-knows-what, covering him from head to toe like a mummy. If anyone came in the room and just saw him there, they’d swear he was dead. But from lifelessness, his hand shot out and fumbled for the phone, the rest of his body staying as still as possible. He grabbed onto the receiver, pulling it off its resting place before it could ring again. Slowly he dragged it through the air, seemingly trying to be careful of anything that might get in its way. "Hello?" "Hi, this is Ted from PTC Headquarters…how are you doing today, Mr. Coleman?" What the fuck?, was the first thought Drake had, confused as ever. PTC lost touch with him ever since he lost in the Cruiserweight Tournament. What did they want now? "What is it?" Drake hoped his rudeness and abruptness would scare whoever the hell "Ted" was away. "Well, um, Drake, we’d like to inform you that you made it past the drawings for the first round of the GTT5 lottery picking, and you’re scheduled to face Johnny Lexicon, and…" "Wait, what? GTT5? Johnny Lexicon? A MATCH? I didn’t sign up for this!" Drake was furious. How could someone do this for him? Is there really this little respect for him? Drake had already realized that he had little respect…but now THIS?! "Um, well, you see, we have this contract here saying you’ll fight…a friend of yours brought it in…he left an address too…kind of weird, but…" "What’s the address?" "Um…23 West Haven St., um, Mr, Coleman, we have a few—" *Click* Drake was now in a world of confusion. What was GTT5? Who is Johnny Lexicon? And, most importantly, who signed this for him? With all these questions in mind, Drake began dressing himself, throwing on a pair of black pants over his legs and a long black t-shirt as well. On top of that he pulled on his long black boots and draped his black trench coat over his shoulders. Finally placing his black hat and sunglasses on his head, he was complete. He turned into what he always wants to be: blank. Nothing. Walking down the stairs and onto the street, he had forgotten that he cannot hide in the shadows during the day. The black was to force away attention, for attention has only brought Drake pain in the past. So, now, there he was, sticking out like a sore thumb for the entire world to see. Trudging through the crowded sidewalk, Drake heard and saw everything. Every glare, every "What’s that on his skin", ever giggle was heard. After years of this, though, Drake was hardened. He could not listen to it while, at the same time, know it was happening. After so many years of pain, this still got to him. Finally, Drake arrived to the address, which just seemed like a door in the wall. As he reached out to open the door it creaked inwards, inviting him in. He pushed it open and noticed a long set of stairs leading up to a darkened room. So, of course, Drake climbed up them. As he pushed up on each step, he could hear laughing grow stronger and stronger. It sounded familiar. "Draaaaaaake….heh heh heh…come on now! Few more steps!" And when he reached the top, Drake saw him. "What, thought I left you for good?" the man said. ---------------------------------------- A few months earlier "To obtain sanity, Drake, you have to execute yourself. You have to finish the job the explosion never managed to do. You don’t have the guts for that though. You’ve become a softy. Sure, now you’re angry, a brute, whatever. On the inside, though, there’s still me, that hope that maybe, eventually, everything will go back to normal. Soon, it will be both me and Charlene, taunting you, telling you to become sane again. You’ve resisted it up to now…lets just see how long you can last." He removed his hand from my shoulder, shaking his head at me. A tear fell down my face as he started walking away. He headed towards the exit, and now the bones of Charlene were gone too. I was left with myself, with the man behind the desk staring at me, a look of pity in his eyes. "Hey man, I’ve said your name a good 10 times now. You gotta shut up and get back to your room or GET OUT!" He was now yelling, his mouth wide open when he said that last part. "I’m…I’m sorry, but…did you happen to see a young guy here with a girl while I was down here…at all?" I was hopeful that, maybe, just maybe, that wasn’t my insanity. That maybe killing myself won’t be necessary to become me again. That maybe I can go through life happy and content. "What in the…you were the LAST man to come in here for HOURS now! Now, get back to your room or GET OUT!" I quickly turned away from the man after he said that, shaking my head. ---------------------------------------- Drake remembered this man, for it was himself. Earlier, this man told him to kill himself to get rid of the sanity and to meet up with his wife, Charlene, again. Of course, Drake didn’t, and since then the man stopped coming back to him. He had thought he had overcome his insanity. He thought wrong. "I’m not killing myself." Drake became defensive, trying to scare the man away. "Kill yourself? Shit, I’m over that. Don’t worry, buddy! Besides, I like living with you! It’s fun!" Silence. "What, nothing? No hello?" "What do you want?" Drake’s tone was stern, his glare stabbing into the man. It looked just like he did before the war…the square jar, the slicked back hair…everything. Drake could have SWORN he had overcome this hallucination… "Oh, Drake" the man said, walking towards Drake with a smile on his face. "You make me laugh. No really, I’m laughing. Here I am, helping you by signing you up for this tournament…" "You couldn’t have! You’re in my head! That’s impossible!" "Or is it? How do you know? Maybe I took over your body? And then, maybe, I signed you up, having full control? Do you remember every moment of the past few weeks?" Drake had to admit to himself that he did not. That was the way life went now-a-days, though; a day would go by and it’d be like every other. Quickly Drake would lose himself, blacking out from the monotony and boredom. Drake could’ve ran for president and not remember. "Ok, now that you’re done speaking out of your ass, Drake, let me continue. You see, I knew you’d love this. You have been out of it for so long that you NEED this, actually. You live off competition. Your life is based on one causing pain to others, you know that right?" Silence. "And don’t think I haven’t seen you in those indy leagues, killing those poor kids! Big tough guy you are! So, after a couple of those matches, I said to myself, Self, why don’t you just sign up Drake for the biggest of all bigs? And, guess what? Voila! First round match against Johnny Lexicon! Excited?" "No. I’m throwing this match. Goodbye." Drake turned to walk out. He thought by turning his back on the man that he’d be gone. But before he could even take a step, there was the man, standing there smiling. "Drake, are you kidding me? I’m in your mind, buddy! I’m wherever YOU are! Save yourself some problems and, hey, just hear me out, ok?" Reluctantly, Drake didn’t move, keeping his glare on the man. "Now that you’re fighting, you’re going to be feeling pain again. You know it, I know it. You’re gonna be bruised, and even though you are this big tough guy, you’ll be in tons of pain. And, hey, guess what? I’m going to be there too, you know." The man flashed a big grin, to which Drake made no reaction. "So, I have a suggestion. No, in fact, I have a solution! Want to hear it?" "Do I have an option?" Drake dryly responded, keeping his glare focused. "Well, I guess not. Ok, hold out your hand." Nothing. "Ok, Drake, I said hold out your hand…not stand there like a fucking loser, ok?" Drake slowly put forward his arm, opening his palm facing up. The man reached into his pockets and pulled out a couple pills, dropping them into Drake’s hand. Drake examined them closer. "What are these?" "They’re race cars…what do you think they are? Painkillers, baby! That’s oxycontin! MmmmmMMMM! Tasty!" Drake wanted to throw them into the ground, but the idea intrigued him. Ever since the accident Drake has lived in a world of pain. He always refused shit like this, preferring a couple ibuprofen. His better side told him not to accept them, though. "No way, uh uh, sorry." "Whaddya mean ‘uh uh, sorry’. You’re the one who bought them! Shit, you even used YOUR money! 140 bucks for those babies!" Plopping to the ground, Drake held onto the pills with one hand and dug his face into the other. He was ashamed of himself, hating how he could let this man buy these drugs. Never, ever, ever would Drake use this shit. But now…since they WERE there… "So, c’mon now, buddy! Put those in your pocket, bring them back to your shit-hole hotel, and try ‘em out! Fuck, I’ll make a deal with you…you do this, and I won’t bother you for ONE week! You can’t say that isn’t a GREAT offer, can you?" Drake paused, thinking about the situation. The instant release from the pain is tempting, so much that, in fact, it’s a world he’s willing to pursue. But the fact that they’re drugs…Drake has seen this before in ‘Nam. He has seen little shit like this lead to much bigger things. "I’ll think about it." Drake said, sighing heavily. He pulled himself off the ground and headed out the door, to the laughing of the man. In a moment, Drake instantly changed his mind. No way would he use drugs! "You son of a bitch, I’ll never—" But once Drake turned around, there was nothing. He was gone, just like that. And all that was left was Drake Coleman, a tournament, and two pills. Oh what a night this is gonna be. ---------------------------------------- "Oh what a night this is gonna be.
