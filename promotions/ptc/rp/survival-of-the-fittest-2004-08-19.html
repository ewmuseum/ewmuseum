Survival of the Fittest

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-19 (Sgt. Clint Slade glances up at the intense sun, his hand raised to his eyebrows, shading his gaze. Crow’s feet sprout from the sergeant’s eyes like wild brush. Slade licks his lower lip and a few bristly words slip off his tongue.) Sgt. Slade: Nine Thirty. No later than 9:35. (The camera pulls back, revealing two men standing attentively in front of Sgt. Slade: Desolation and Agony, collectively known as the Pain Syndicate. Like Sgt. Slade, the WWA Tag Team Champions are dressed in army fatigues.) Desolation: You can tell time by looking at the sun? That’s wicked cool. (Slade steps up into Des’s face, their noses nearly touching.) Sgt. Slade: Did I give you permission to speak, private?! Agony: Heh, heh. He said private. (Agony giggles and Sgt. Slade jaunts over to him. The sergeant’s breath parches Agony’s eyeballs.) Sgt. Slade: And just what are YOU here for? Did you come to send off your girlfriend, here. [nodding towards Des]… Agony: I heard Joe was going to Survival training, and I was all like “That’s where the party’s at.” Besides, [patting his abs] the old A-train could use a little tune-up… Sgt. Slade: …that was a rhetorical question, private! Desolation: Private. Ha! That is funny. [turns his head downwards] Fall in, “private”. A real soldier stands “erect” and remains “firm” under pressure. Agony: A tough soldier is a good soldier. No “bones” about it… Sgt. Slade: ENOUGH! (Des and Agony straighten up and stare straight ahead.) Sgt. Slade: You are here to prepare for war. Your enemies: WMW, PRIME, LOW, and the rest of Primetime Central. They may look like you. They may talk like you. They may dress like you. But, soldiers, they may as well be North Korea, Vietnam, and Nazi Germany. This is not Happy Fun Time… Desolation: Ah, snap. I knew I shouldn’t have brought my pogs. (Sgt. Slade glares at Des, but doesn’t reprimand him for his wisecrack and continues to address his company.) Sgt. Slade: We are here to get down to the basics. These training exercises will prime you for battle in every conceivable way. When it’s game time, boys, you will be prepared. Understood? (A short, silent pause) Desolation: Should we applaud, or something? I don’t know how these things usually work. Agony: I don’t know, but I saw someone do this in this old 1940s film. (Agony starts to do a Hitler salute, but Des grabs his arm before he can complete the gesture. The scene fades to the middle of a field. Again, Desolation and Agony stand side by side, watching Sgt. Slade alertly.) Sgt. Slade: Half of all the world’s wars are fought here. (Slade points to his cranium) Desolation: Yeah, and but all of the world’s wars end in France. Agony: ZING! Sgt. Slade: Mental training is just as important as any physical training. At Survival, you will be on your own. You won’t be able to trust anybody. Not even your teammates. Even the fans will be against you. You will need mental toughness if you want to have any chance of standing victorious. That’s why I’ve brought you here. Agony: An empty field? Desolation: Oh no! I’ve seen stuff like this on the Forensic Files. I’ll bet you have a van full of duct tape, rope, and trash bags, don’t you! Sgt. Slade: This isn’t just any field. This is a mine field. There’s no better way to test a person’s mental toughness than to put their lives in mortal danger. You have three minutes to exit the field before all the mines go off simultaneously. If you make one false step… Desolation: …and we’re drinking the rest of our meals through straws. Sgt. Slade: Precisely. But, to show you how easy it is, I’ll walk through it myself, (Slade walks away from the Pain Syndicate, traversing the mine field in a zig-zag fashion. When he’s done, he is about fifteen feet away from TPS. He turns towards them.) Sgt. Slade: Oh, and boys. Don’t bother following my footprints. (Slade picks up a bucket sitting at his feet and hurls its contents across the mine field, washing away the imprints of his boots.) Desolation: Gulp. Agony: Double gulp. (Desolation takes one step forward and winces. Nothing happens. He takes another step, this one more sideways than forward. Again, nothing happens. Agony begins to follow his brother, making sure to step directly onto Des’s footprints in the dirt.) Agony: Easy does it… take your time… Desolation: That’s easy for you to say. I’m the one that’s going to catch all the shrapnel. (Des takes a few more awkward steps, his jaunting figure almost doing a bizarre jig.) Agony: Just a little further… (Des takes one more cautious step. Without warning, there’s a clicking sound and a disk shaped metal object pops out of the ground.) Desolation: Oh shi… (The mine explodes in a white flash. Everything is silent for a moment and the air is filled with smoke. The stillness is broken by a bellowing laugh) Sgt. Slade: Har, har, har. You guys are too much. (The smoke begins to clear. Desolation and Agony stand hugging each other with their eyes closed, covered in white powder. Slade rubs his index finger across Des’s cheek and then licks the powder off of it.) Sgt. Slade: Powdered Sugar. You didn’t really think I was going to use real mines, did you? Har, har, har. (Slade walks away. Desolation and Agony finally open their eyes. They realize they are still hugging each other and they immediately release. They brush themselves off.) Desolation: I knew that. Agony: Didn’t fool me for a second. (The scene fades.) Continued in Part 2...
