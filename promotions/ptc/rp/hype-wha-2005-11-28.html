Hype? Wha?

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 Hello, my name is John. Parks, that is. Black gold. Texas tea. You may know me as the guy who just won that jobber battle royal over at PRIME this past week, or maybe even as the guy who holds a win over Hoyt Williams as of the week prior. And if you’re real up on your wrestling knowledge, you’ll even know me as the first guy in 360˚ Wrestling to beat Tarrant, no-show or not. Hell, I might even have been the first to beat him, period. Who knows. All I do know is that I rock, and now you know it too. So, now I guess I should get on to the hyping, and not just the unfounded bragging. Oh, how I do love the unfounded bragging. Makes me feel like a big man to lord things over people that only came to be through mysterious fluke or present from Jesus. Nonetheless, the time for being more of a man than you has come to an end, replaced only with the time to hype myself to all high hell and back. Without further ado, I shall… COMMENCE THE HYPING~! … Yeah… You know, this would be a hell of a lot easier if my only high points in this game didn’t involve beating people via no-shows and other tragic mishaps. The best I can brag about is how much better I am than someone who can’t be assed to roleplay against me. That’s my selling point. I’m the guy who’s not worth the effort of sitting down and typing out sixteen or seventeen words for the win. I’m the guy who will then treat that aforementioned win the same as if I just got AIDS the very day they discovered a cure. And I’m the guy who will subsequently shame you out of e-wrestling entirely. Ask Phil, he hasn’t been the same since I interfered with myself on the top of the CN Tower while shouting out, "I beat Tarrant!" over and over again in orgasmic delight amidst cries of horror and intense vomiting. And you could be next. So maybe I don’t have any hype. Maybe I have nothing at all to hype. But I will tell you something, and it’s something everyone in this tournament needs to remember. I have nothing better to do, and I will spend entire days on end writing out useless crap just to beat you through sheer volume. And that’s only if you actually show. You see, you might not think I’m worth it. I don’t blame you. Not many people think I’m worth five minutes of writing. So you might think the same, and figure me posting a roleplay will do enough in your favor that you won’t actually have to write anything. But what if that isn’t the case? What if I don’t completely suck, just for one night…and my roleplay counts? What if I beat you like I did to so many others, and drive you out of the hobby through complete assholery? What if? But moving on to a touch of seriousness now, I’d like to take a moment to prepare those of you who’ve never read my work. I figure it’s the least I can do for the poor souls who will have to sit through probably two thousand or more words of mindless drivel, and then switch over from Knight’s to read mine when they’re done. HIYO! … Not funny, huh? Didn’t think so, but one can always try. Anyway, if you’ve never read my work before, I suggest doing so at least a little before roleplaying starts here. Because, honestly, you’re gonna need to adjust a little. My writing style is out there, I constantly break the fourth, fifth, and sixth walls, and I have no discernable technique whatsoever. That, and my blatant disregard for reality is only bested by the Milk Man’s finishing move. It all makes for a mish-mash of fun and excitement. And maybe even some erotic fan fiction every now and again. If you have previously read my work, I’m sorry. That’s all I can say. But I’m getting bored of writing, as you are probably of reading, so I will end this…thing…now. To everyone in this tourney: good luck. I hope it’s as much of a blast for everyone the whole way through as it has been just starting up. And I hope I can at least make a few people laugh or even just think they’ve read the wrongest thing in existence. If I can do that just once, my job shall be finished. So, in conclusion: I don’t have any hype, but what I do have is heart. And poor graphic skills. See you in round two. Even if I have to kill someone to get there…-eyes [name withheld].-
