Hitting home, Hitting Digi

23 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-23 Of all things to love and be thankful for in central park Joshua was thankful for the birds. Nothing and no one could spread the word of live and death faster and with less bias then them. There singing was music to peoples ears, unknown that there voices spoke of such happiness and horror. One story in particular had caught Joshua’s attention and for the first time the music he heard from the birds brought two sweet and joyful tear drops from his eyes. Joshua could not contain his joy as they sang there hearts out spreading the word of life. It had taken Joshua two days of listening to there songs to hear what he was after, to hear the story of Digital morality. One those sweet words had reach him though the wait felt more like a gleeful payment of which Joshua would gladly repeat. “Funny how weak men do nothing but strengthen those already strong” he told himself as the birds began to sing a different tune. Joshua started to stroll towards his home just a few short minutes away from the park. His already confident stride given a boost that almost had him skipping past people whose lives clearly depressed there souls into almost nothing. As he walked he could help but wonder, how easy life would be now Digital Morality was in the state he was. “Do I even continue to train” he asked himself before quickly stopping that line of thought. “Don’t slip up Joshua, not this time work harder not easier” he told himself though the words fitting more his father than Joshua. The weaker his opponent the over confident Joshua became and the less he prepared. It was a response that had cost him victories in the past and become a sore point Joshua refused to admit to in the company of others. “Not this time, this time we truly set an example.” He mumbled as a hobo sort change from him. Walking past the drunken old man Joshua couldn’t help but stop and turn back and look at him. After smelling he was down wind of the man who had not washed for clearly months Joshua walked by once more, wanting to keep his displeasure to a minimal but his findings to there up most. The old man saw him watching him and came once more looking for what he claimed was ‘spare change’ Joshua had been quick to argue one doesn’t have any spare change because life cost to much to claim such riches. However had then taken $40 from his wallet and given it to the old man, the payment for services rendered though not know by the man. In watching him beg Joshua couldn’t help but rehear the sweet words once more, retelling the story of Digital Morality and his timely stupidity and weakness. The old man hadn’t cared; Joshua was sure that for $40 dollars the man would have just about done anything he had cared for, but just standing there was more than enough. The drunk had started off towards a place Joshua hoped he never got to see. It was a place men like him know exist but ignore “why depress ourselves, there life’s” he told himself as justification for his ignorance. With his wondering over and an answer to the question brought forth by seeing the old man Joshua continued for home. His matches were coming and even with news of such magnitude easing up would be a mistake Joshua would not repeat. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -<>- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Joshua: “Hello Digital” Joshua was looking directing into a camera his eyes glowing with pleasure. Joshua looked like a man pleased with his chances, and looked like he would take even more pleasure in hurting his opponent. Joshua: “Digi, I fear we may have gotten off to a bad start, the wrong foot so to speak. You know I think we, you and I should get together for a chat man to man.” The smirk on Joshua’s face signalled his lack of realness, the offer no more than a set up for an onslaught of which Joshua would take nothing but pleasure in giving. Joshua: “Come on Digi, you and I together in a nice family bar sitting down to some cool fresh beers. You know the kind, the type of beer that makes you want to leap up and sing because it that good. I know this bar, a great place whole families go together to enjoy the festivities. You and I could meet there among the men and women with there children.” Joshua paused for a second knowing he had started to poke at some of the sore points of Digitals. Joshua: “We could even bring our own families, I don’t have any children myself but I do have a lovely woman who I plan on marring. Think about Digital, there we would be, you and I sitting down to ice cold, imported beer so strong, so tasty the hobo’s outside feel all warm and fuzzy just smelling it. What do you say? You think we can sit down together and have a beer? I know you want one? All that pain you’re going through, all that hurt, why suffer needlessly? Just have yourself a few cold drinks to take the end off. Come on how bad could it be? You’re a strong guy; you can handle just a couple of beers, right?” Joshua’s coldness could be felt threw the lens, his ever growing insensitivity towards others present once more. Joshua: “On second thoughts maybe drinking isn’t the answer, maybe its best you just sit down. Yeah that’s it, you should just step back from it all sit down and talk it over with that loved one. You know that one true love you can always count on to be there, to listen because only they truly understand you. I know whenever I have a problem I cannot fix alone, I can simply turn to my love, my Jessica and talk to her about it. She’s so perfect, always ready to listen, always ready to help and understand. I know am getting all mushy and love struck but it you have someone to love, then why waste time? I’m sure you are the same Digital, if you have that special someone the little things people judge you on don’t matter, right? All that matters is that you are with the one that you love, and the one that loves you.” Joshua is almost chuckling to himself, he knew about Digital’s loss, knew it all to well but making it hurt that little bit more was a pleasure Joshua enjoyed to much to pass up. Joshua: “When you think about it Digi, I mean how much does what we do really matter? I mean so what if we’re the champion of the world somewhere, without that one love, the special someone to share it with, you’re really just some guy with a belt. Yeah life would be pretty pointless if I ever lost Jessica; she’s the reasoning to my actions, the one that no matter what stands beside me, my soul mate. Love; funny how love conquers everything, no man, women or child can stop love taking over them. I know you understand that Digi, without someone to love we are just empty shells walking around aimlessly. Do you have someone to love Digital?” It was a question Joshua knew the answer to, one Joshua knew would cut deep into Digital morality. Joshua’s intension were clear, he wanted this contest won before either stepped foot inside a ring. Joshua: “Of course you don’t, who would really love a pathetic piece of waste like you” The smirk had gone and the gleeful chuckling ended in the space of a sentence. A coldness crept into Joshua tone like a thief through an open window. Joshua: “What women would want a drunk like you Digital? You so weak, so needy that any women would find herself pulled down to your level. Is that the type of man you are? Are you the type that demoralises and tortures women for his own sadistic pleasure? Love Digital, love is about growth, about improving each other a people. Love isn’t about driving your woman into the bed of another man, it’s not about turning her into a whore so she can knock herself up and tell you its yours. Digital, come on know, drinking yourself stupid because of your so called love? Sobering up to prove yourself? Are you really that weak? Are you that pathetic of a man that this things I say are true? Can you even start to deny my words?” Joshua paused for the briefest of moments as if allowing a response, while he was alone and only silence filled the pause it felt somehow needed. Or Digital, or are you so weak that as you stand there looking at yourself in the mirror. Looking at yourself with thoughts of ending it all, are you deep down thanking god she’s gone? I ask because you know full well that gone you don’t have to sit there with her, gone you don’t have to be strong, you don’t have to bring up somebody else’s child. Deep down you know you like it this way, you like the fact you no longer have someone, its easier isn’t it? You like it easier don’t you? You like it like this because you’re weak, because you’re not strong enough to handle love. Its that why you drank? Did you want to love her but saw the work, saw the responsibility? Crumbling into a bottle is so much easier isn’t it? Sitting there drinking yourself stupid telling yourself that sooner or later she will just leave you. That’s safer then opening up, safer and easier then being honest and trusting someone, right? I mean come on now it takes strength, pride and really work to be with someone. Love isn’t all walks in the park and Sunday afternoon’s in bed together.” For a second time the smirk returned, however the coldness in Joshua’s tone remained at a cutting level as he spoke. His words meant to hurt Digital Morality, meant to open up the deepest, and most painful wounds he could ever hope to find. Joshua: “Where you glad when she died? I mean deep down? All of a sudden all the responsibility was gone, there was no more pressure, no one else to look after. I mean look at yourself, she dies and you straighten yourself out, are we to believe you cared about her? Come on now Digital, you have to admit that with her you were a wreck. The minute she’s gone and you put yourself on a path that will improve you way of life. Are these the actions of a man upset? I’m sure there are people that would disagree, but it sounds like you are almost overjoyed at being alone, is that why you fell guilty? Is that why you dream of death?” - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -<>- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Arriving home Joshua was meet with kiss, Jessica had missed him and though he had been gone less then three hours being missed felt good. Joshua responded with the almost time warner ‘missed you too’ but though cheesy as it would sound to outsiders he had. With everything Joshua had in his live, he knew losing Jessica would be live dying a death of such horror and pain only to wake up and have to live through that same death again and again. Joshua was grateful for the life he had, but he couldn’t help but think he earned every single bit of it. Joshua knew he had been working hard all his life, knew he missed out on a similar childhood to others, so getting everything he wanted now was just. Jessica continued her greeting by kicking the door shut behind him and pulling him towards the bedroom. Joshua gazed into her eyes, completely absorbed in her as she was him. A brief moment came and went where Joshua wondered if Digital Morality ever had anything close to this, but wonder was all Joshua did. Joshua soon lost all train of thought as he and Jessica headed into the bedroom, love was a beauty thing, and Joshua was thankful for his. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -<>- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Joshua: “Do you really think you’re sad? You know its because you happy, don’t you? A sad person doesn’t clean himself up; a sad person doesn’t take a life changing decision and stick to it. Be honest with yourself, you got to be alone, no woman you don’t truly love adding to your problems. You had time to yourself, saw she had ruined your life and so sorted yourself out. Now doing this meant one thing, you blamed her for your drinking, your depression. The second you were without her, you quit drinking.” Joshua was looking for the kill blow now, looking to destroy Digital Morality emotionally before having to facing him face to face. Joshua: “Ok, ok maybe am being harsh? Maybe but I doubt it, I merely speak the truth and if that’s harsh for you then maybe its because you deserve to be hurt. The blunt and painful truth is this, you were so unhappy with your life, so upset with who you were with. That when you realised your pain, you sort, acted and drove your ‘love’ away. You drowned your pain in alcohol and continued to truant the ‘love’ you told yourself that you cared for. So much did you hurt this woman, so much that when she died you know she felt relief. She was free of you, free of you false love and free to be peaceful. Deny it if you can, if you even want to try blind yourself from your problem. But as you lay bleeding those few thoughts of her passing through your mind hoping the pain stops remember when you wake, when you regain your clarity you still have to come face me, and once I’ve done beating you, all that will be left is the truth, that your mess of a life is so because you and you alone were to weak to stop it being anything but.” - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -<>- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Waking with the evening sun coming through the windows and Jessica lying huddled up next to him. Joshua felt blessed, all his years of had work had gotten him to this point and reaping the rewards felt better then he ever imagined it would. Laying there looking over his good life it became clear why people hated him. Joshua had everything people ever wanted, he was making large amount of money doing a job he was better at then anyone else. Joshua had a beautiful woman who was head over heels in love with him, and who he loved. Nothing else matter, nothing else could top his personal and professional victories. It suddenly hit him, Joshua had everything Digital Morality had lost. Joshua had the happy life, he had the love of a woman, the loyalty. Joshua knew Digital would hate him, Joshua knew that the second they meet Digital would want him dead, dead not because of the things he said and felt about him. Joshua knew he would want him dead because he had everything he wanted.
