So I'm sorry and I hope you can see the words now and enjoy.} Entry Title: A New Aide ==| Prologue |== Extreme… There are a couple of meanings for this simply word. In a small portable Webster Dictionary I found, the word extreme is an adjective and it means greatly exceeding; going far beyond the bounds of moderation. However, this is the intelligential meaning for this word as this is not the true real meaning well...the wrestling meaning. You see when the word “extreme” is used in the wrestling world it usually means ladders, chairs, tables, any kinds of cages and any other things that can tear a body apart. This is the real meaning that us extremist know and love… When I first started my wrestling career I didn’t even know what being extreme was all about that is until I had my first extreme match at a certain 4 Winds’ PPV that would change my life forever. Ever since that fateful night I have been driven to push my body to their limits in every extreme match I get booked in. This brings me to my first match at a PTC I-PPV… Not too long ago there was a certain match at a most recent 4 Winds’ PPV where the winner of the match would become the #1 Contender for the PTC Extreme Title. Now I don’t have to tell you all who won this match, know do I? Ok, for those of you have been living under a rock…I, Kyo Kushinaga better known as the Deathscythe Outlaw has defeated and survived this match against 4 other wrestlers, three of which was from my league and one from some no name league. After winning this match with ease I was anxiously hoping that I could face the Extreme Champion, Moraless who was able to retain the title after his second defense breaking the title’s curse. A person who can break a curse like that is a worthy challenge in my eyes but apparently something has come up causing him to vacate the title but oh well it would’ve been a great and thrilling bout between me and him…then I would’ve caused him great physical pain… With the title being vacated, the most extreme match that has probably ever happened in PTC history is going to happen and the match is the Extreme Chamber which has 6 extremist including myself thriving for this prestigious title of Extremehood. Hmm, that sounded a little bit on the cheesy side but hey these are my thoughts and not yours’…so there! Anyways, the so unlucky victims that will be facing me are… DDT, an extremist from XUW who has gone up against Moraless not too long ago but he couldn’t get the job done so he is back again to win the title when the title doesn’t even have a holder. Out of the bunch, I would have to say that he has to be desperate of us who wants the win title badly since his lost last time. Joey Vendetti, an extremist who too is from XUW, I don’t really know anything about him except that he had a couple of matches in the Extreme Division struggling his way to the top but since it was taking him so long to get to the top he decided that signing up for this Invitational Extreme Chamber match, thinking this would speed things up and even hoping to get to the top this way. He will be only doing this in vain which is quite pathetic but hey he’s nothing more than a second class wrestler from the same league that DDT is in. Shaman, an extremist from a league that has been running for 9 to maybe 10 years and this league is the great WWA. I have to hand to the WWA I mean this is only league I know that has been around for a long time while the most leagues usually fades away in the shadows. Back to Shaman, I have no clue about who this guy really is as he is a complete mystery which could make this more interesting. I usually like it when I go into matches blind. It keeps me on my toes. You see I enjoy the unexpected it’s more thrilling this way rather than knowing who you are facing, don’t you think? Moving on to ICE who is an extremist from my league, 4WW who in fact use to be in Global Wrestling and use to be their Heavyweight Champion until being defeated by Josh. I’m guessing this former top dog is missing the taste of gold and wants to get his reputation back about being a strong champion. Damn, he will be truly disappointed once he gets his ass eliminated. Finally, the last extremist is quite the celebrity from the old days of 4 Winds and now at the Universe Wrestling Federation and this person from who I am speaking of is no other than “The Irish Bastard” himself, Sean McClancy. I don’t really know much about this Irish Bastard except that he is no stranger to alcohol and that his relatives have planted cameras in his hotel room at one time and taped a certain someone getting busy on a certain 4 Winds Champions which I have to say that I made quite a profit on when selling those tapes. It is a damn shame that two of my special customers had to get a brutal beat down by an “amateur porn star” but oh well at least I know that I got his attention. I also found out that he was a former Extreme Champion who was defeated by the one who had vacated his title and now he is seeking to get it back. This should prove fun especially when I eliminate Sean, Patricia Anderson’s fiancé I know she will be spitting mad and that’s a sight I would enjoy to see. What? You want to know about me…? There is nothing quite special for you guys to know about me except that I will become the new Extreme Champion and that my fellow extremist shall be warn because I’m a force that they have never faced in their worthless careers. I just hope you all have a very common blood type because lots of blood is going to be shed.
