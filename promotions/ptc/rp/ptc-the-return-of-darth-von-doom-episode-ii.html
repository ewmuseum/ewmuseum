Wrestler: Darth von Doom (FA) Promo Title: The Return of Darth von Doom, Episode II: Attack of the Giant Penis Creature and the Three Tittied Demon Monster --- Somewhere in the Sahara Desert… An incident has just occurred. I, Darth von Doom, just destroyed a giant robot of unknown origin sent to either capture me or attack the Unwanted, the group of exiles that I am currently traveling with. The power… The power inside of me… Is too much for any person to bear. After I unleashed my energy attack onto the giant robot that attacked me and my friends, I felt different. It almost feels like the whole experience awakened me to the fact that I am a creature of darkness. The reason I am alive is to create mass carnage and death. However I do not feel like that is my purpose now. The purpose of my existence now is unknown to me. I have to figure it out now. Luckily I have this group to help me to discover a purpose. This eclectic group has made me one of their own. Some of them are actually enemies of mine from my former life. This group of exiles includes the werewolf Wolfen, the assassin Blue Jay, the female experiment Nebula, the former superhero Ash, the three Killer Pirates, and our leader the mysterious Bishop. As I lay on my knees on the sand, the group comes over to me. We all look over at the sand where the robot who attacked us once stood only to notice that the sand is now turned to glass in that area. Bishop looks down at the sand as Blue Jay and Nebula go over to investigate what happened. Bishop walks over to me as I look up at him. “What…what am I?” I ask, looking to him for an answer to the one question that has been bugging me ever since I had my flashback episode. “You are a living weapon,” he replies. His answer scares me more than any horror movie ever could. However, as he continues, the more scary it becomes. “You were created by a man named the Horny Mathematician. He saved you after your death and in doing so unleashed the Mitichlorians that you had inside of you lying dormant. After you were severely injured in battle, he patched you up into the cyborg you are today. You were a practitioner of the Dark Side of the Force.” This makes me raise an eyebrow. “What exactly is the Force?” I ask, a bit confused as to what he is telling me. “The Force surrounds all living beings,” he replies. “It’s what gives you your powers. You are the most dangerous being on this planet and probably one of the five or ten most dangerous beings in the universe. You have committed acts of mass murder and genocide the likes of which the world hasn’t seen before.” “That is why your power must be kept under control by you and not used for nefarious means by other people. You were used to by two or three evil organizations in the past to try and take over the world or destroy the world. This is why they want you back. They sent a robot here to try and recapture you. They clearly need you for something big. The only thing is, which organization is behind this act.” This gets the wheels turning in my head. Bishop makes a good point. If someone is after me, which organization would be the one that would be coming after me? There have been a few. Church of the Unholy might be at the top of the list. They have been wanting revenge for the evil, fallen elder god known as Tioneb since before I was a member of their organization. They also have a wide assortment of monsters, goons, and other assorted horrors at their disposal. The Circus of Doom is another possibility. I was a founding member of that organization with the Horny Mathematician. In fact, I killed most of my associates upon joining them and attacking the Temple on the Island of Misfit Wrestlers. Most of the rest of them were killed when my time machine experiment went awry and almost killed everyone on the planet Earth. They quickly fell apart after we joined up with the Dictator of Russia to try and take over the world. That really worked out well. Speaking of the Dictator, he formed the Axis of Evil and they tried to take over after the main Circus leadership was arrested for committing war crimes. It all fell apart after that. Then the Circus of the Unholy formed when the two organizations formed up and have been together. Maybe that is the culprit of this act of aggression. Which one is it? Only time will tell. After awhile, the group decides to leave our current base of operations and head for Casablanca. We leave shortly thereafter and head out into the desert toward the northwest. I know that they will find us and try to recapture me to commit acts of unholy evil. I must be ready for their next move. * * * Somewhere in America… Inside the secret lair of the organization behind the attack, a man quickly runs into the command center of the whole place. There he finds the leader of the whole operation sitting in a chair watching various things on numerous television monitors as the man begins to speak. “Sir,” the man says. The other man in the chair, the leader of the whole operation, turns around and is revealed to be a red skinned demon with horns on his head. His name is Belial and he is now the leader of the Church of the Unholy organization since Darth von Doom left. “Yes,” he snarls out. “What is it?” “Darth von Doom destroyed the robot sentry we sent out to find him,” the man quickly replies. Belial doesn’t look too concerned. In fact, he seems happy about what he has just heard. “Perfect,” Belial says with a devious grin. The man looks confused by this reaction. “Why are you happy, sir?” he asks. “I just told you that Darth destroyed the robot that we sent to get him. Why would that make you happy?” “Because I planned on him destroying it,” Belial responds with a grin. “The whole idea behind the robot was this. To get von Doom to use his Force powers on it and destroy. We need to capture him and get him back to using his true potential. We need evil Darth back. He is the key to us taking over the world.” “We need Darth alive. I have sent in a team to get him. Get the rest of the Council together. We need to plot out our course for the future. If we pull this off, then the world will be ours for the taking. If not, then this will be another bad loss for us.” “I only hope our team succeeds. HAIL TIONEB!” Belial says to the man, giving the Unholy salute to him. “HAIL TIONEB!” he replies, doing the same salute before walking out of the control room to go find the Council to inform them of the meeting. * * * On the border of Morocco… As our group wanders through the desolate Sahara Desert on our way to Casablanca, I start to think about what lies ahead. I’m on uncharted waters at this point. I can’t remember the last time that I fought on the side of good for anything, let alone the fate of the world. This is something that seems very odd to me. On the side of evil, that is where I once stood. Now I’m on a mission to face the same people that I used to work with less than a year ago. Bishop has the idea to use me as bait to lure them into a trap. This idea will involve us capturing one of the enemy and interrogating them for information with using the threat of me doing the interrogation as a weapon to get what we need to know. He is of the opinion that they are scared of me and that is why they want to recapture me. He may be right but then again, they may want me dead. We won’t know until they make their next move. The group is very quiet as we travel to the city. Well, besides the three Killer Pirates who won’t shut up. Everyone else just walks along, not really saying too much other than asking about directions and how long it will take us to arrive. The storm is coming and we all know that the battle ahead will not be a pretty one. We must be ready, otherwise we face annihilation and that is not an option. * * * Las Vegas, Nevada… Somewhere in Las Vegas, a man is wandering down a dark alley with a gun. Screams are heard followed by gunfire. The man has just killed a woman in cold blood. Instead of a normal reaction of either being scared or showing anger, he starts laughing hysterically over this. Just then, some sirens are heard in the distance. The man turns and runs down an alleyway. He continues going through the endless maze of buildings before eventually reaching a parking lot. However, someone is waiting for him. A few people, in fact. “We were sent by Belial,” the man says to him although he does not look like a man for some reason. “He sent us to give you this.” The man has a second man walk over and hand the man a big manila envelope. “In that envelope are coordinates and maps that will lead you to Darth von Doom and the Unwanted,” he continues. “As well as your money for the job.” The man who received the envelope smiles. “Been waiting for this,” he says. “Tell your boss the job will be done.” “Thank you, Anarchy,” the man replies back as the men all go back to the big black van they came in and get inside before driving off into the distance. Looking at the envelope, Anarchy has a big grin on his face. “Finally some real fun,” he says to himself before walking away from the scene. * * * Casablanca, Morocco… We arrive in the slums outside of the main city and slowly make out way into town through a busy bazaar. People come from seemingly everywhere just to take a look at us. We’re an awesome sight to these people. Almost all of them have never seen freaks and mutants like us before in their lives except for maybe in the rare comic book that gets sold in the city’s book shops. They especially marvel at myself, Wolfen, and the three Killer Pirates which to them are like something they’d see at the local cinema in some sort of movie by a certain director named James who is related to me. Little kids walk over to touch us. Their faces light up with excitement after they touch us. I feel like a rock star this point. Heck, we all do. It’s a great feeling. As we make our way through the crowd, it almost appears as if they are worshipping us like Gods of some kind. It’s a very weird situation, at least for me it is. But everyone in the group seems to be taking it kindly. To me they seem as if none of them is used to getting good treatment by people. Their reactions appear to be a bit bewildered actually now that I glance over again. However, upon further inspection, some of the people seem out of place. The fact that they are wearing all black makes them stand out from the locals. I sense a disturbance in the Force as well. Something is about to happen. I grab Bishop by the air to stop him from walking any further. He looks at me, a bit bewildered. He looks at my face for a moment and then suddenly realizes that something is up. “What is it?” he asks me. I motion my head toward the crowd of people and he looks over. “See those guys in the black?” I ask, pointing at them in the crowd as Bishop looks over. The other members of our group see what is going on and stop walking. “Yeah,” he responds. “They’re working for the enemy,” I reply with confidence. “They stand out too much and they give off bad vibes. I can sense disturbances in the Force and they give off major ones. I believe they work for the Unholy Circus or whatever their organization is calling themselves now days. I expect an ambush to be imminent.” “Imminent?” he mumbles out just as a loud rumbling shakes the ground beneath our feet. A giant shadow then shades the entire area like a mountain appearing out of thin air in the middle of empty plain somewhere. Everyone looks up and above us we see three giant robots looking down upon us. The people all around us start screaming and running in all directions as the robots pull out weapons on us. The weapons appear to be some sort of plasma weapons technology. This has the Horny Mathematician written all over it. Only he would experiment with such technology, going against the Berlin Accords passed by the United Nations after I almost destroyed the world with my own experiments with plasma energy. The robots then start firing on our position as we take evasive maneuvers. BOOM! BAM! BOOM! Explosions rock the area as we get out our weapons to try and fight off the robots. The Killer Pirates have guns and start blasting away like kids playing the old video game Duck Hunt to little or no success. Bishop and Wolfen have taken up hiding behind a small stand in the bazaar and are also firing shots at the robots but having limited success at creating actual damage. Nebula and Blue Jay are taking on the goons in black that I noticed earlier. Just then, I remember something on my utility belt. Looking down at my belt I see a metallic cylindrical shaped object. It is my Sith weapon of choice, the Lightsaber. Reaching down I pull it off my utility belt and hold it my hand. Haven’t used this weapon in awhile and I inspect it. As I do this, however, a large plasma blast hits the ground in my vicinity. Looking up, the robots have their guns trained on my position. Using the Force, I push them back and ignite my light saber. I leap into the air and off of some of the stands that are in the bazaar area. Leaping off of a food stand using a Force Jump, I fly toward the first robot and dodge it’s punching attack. Dodging in mid-air, I twist around and cut off the robot’s head. After doing this, I use the falling head to jump off of and slice the second robot in half from the chest down. The giant monstrosity falls to the ground in a heap. Pieces of it destroy nearby stores and apartment complexes with ease. The third giant robot looks down at me with its gun trained right on me. Apparently they want me dead and not alive. So the plasma blasts from the big gun on his right hand that are fired in my direction doesn’t surprise me at all. I block the shots using the Force before feeling a rage inside of me. I then notice that my skin is glowing red. An energy grows inside of me. I feel the dragon is about to be unleashed. A giant red bubble appears around me again just like the desert when the first robot attack. The energy burst is about to explode. “ARRRRRRRGGGGGHHHHHHH!!!!!” The energy inside of me is soon unleashed as Bishop gets the rest of the group to a safe distance. The red energy wave I unleash destroys the robot, disintegrating it and all the buildings in a small area where I am at in an instant. After the shockwave is unleashed, it quickly subsides once it reaches it’s maximum distance. Just as quickly as it was released, it ends. I catch my breath and look around at the destruction that I have just done. The rest of the group comes out from hiding to survey the scene. As they come back outside, out of nowhere a big breasted blonde bimbo woman dressed in black appears with a big breasted, horned red demon creature at her side that is floating in the air above the ground. I recognize the woman immediately as she cackles like the evil satanic witch that she is. “Brandi Blowjob,” I mutter out, shaking my head as I do so. And no, not that head. That head is for sex but this is a time of war. “AXEL Action’s little tramp.” “I’m not a tramp,” she snarls out before smiling deviously at me. “But I am here to deal with you Darth and all your little friends. I have with me this demon and this creature.” She motions behind her and out appears a giant penis creature from the deepest, darkest depths of Hell. Brandi cackles as she motions to her two monsters. “They have been sent from the furthest reaches of Hell itself just to destroy you!” she exclaims with demonic glee. She then motions for the creatures to attack me. “ATTACK!” Just then the big breasted demon creature and the giant penis monster creature then attack me and the rest of the Unwanted! Everyone ducks for cover from the fireballs that the demon woman is shooting at us and the acid sperm that the giant penis monster is shooting at us. I use the Force to block some of these attacks right back at them. This pisses off the demon woman. The penis monster, however, goes from attacking us to turning its attention to Brandi. She screams in terror as it goes over and attacks her snatch Manga style much to our shock! We cover our eyes and ignore it while we attack the big breasted demon woman from Hell that is shooting fireballs at us. I use the Force to deflect her shots away from us while Blue Jay and Nebula blast at her with rifles from the side as the Killer Pirates quickly run over to join them. The demon fights us off with her flames as I pull out my light saber. Force Leaping through the air, I fly at the unholy beast and dodge three fireballs she hurls at me before chopping off her head! Her head rolls on the ground toward the rest of the Unwanted as I float down to the ground using the Force before landing on my feet. Bishop looks down at the head before we get distracted by loud, moaning noises. Looking over toward the origin of the noises, we see the penis creature ramming itself into Brandi’s snatch and soon kills her with the dreaded Deathgasm that has killed many old men in the past. After Brandi’s body falls to the ground in a heap, the evil penis monster then turns in our direction. It almost smiles at us after his face turn only moments earlier. However it instead attacks us in a swerve heel turn. This pisses me off. The creature lunges at the Killer Pirates who punch at it. Then the penis monster notices Nebula, who is a female. It targets her vagina and goes in for the Manga Deathgasm kill just like it did to Brandi Blowjob. The creature is blocked by Blue Jay, who fights it off with a sword as I ignite my light saber once more. “Enough games,” I growl out in a very annoyed tone. Force Leaping over the monstrosity, I land right in front of it as it is trying to attack Nebula. Without hesitation, I behead the monster. It falls in a limp heap to the ground as blood covers the street. Shutting off my light saber, I look around at everyone. “Ok, we just say some real freaky stuff right now,” I say to them. “But we’d best get on the move before we see some even freakier stuff if they decide to ambush us once again.” The Killer Pirates agree with me quickly, with Captain Jack Morgan simply stating, “He’s right, mates. There be some crazy creations in that Church of his.” “Agreed,” Bishop responds. “I have seen some of them myself. We need to go now before back-up arrives.” The Unwanted then quickly run into the city away from all the damage that was just caused in the battle against the demonic entities and giant robots. We head deep into the city and hide out in a seedy looking apartment building until nightfall. We know this won’t keep them from finding us, but at least it’ll give them the slip until we can figure out what to do next. Our future may depend on it. * * * Somewhere in America… Back in the mysterious organization’s control center, Belial looks at the television screens in the room, watching various evil deeds being committed as the man from before walks back inside the room. As he continues to watch the screens, the man walks into the room to inform the demon Belial the news of what just happened. “The woman and the two demons failed in Casablanca,” he says as Belial clinches his fist in anger. “The robots also failed in their mission. Darth is still on the loose.” “DAMN!” Belial exclaims loudly. “They tried to kill, not capture. And they ruined our plans! THEY RUINED THEM!” “Now we go into plan B. Anarchy has been contracted by our organization through the Hades Corporation and been sent in to capture Darth and kill the others. He will not fail like the others failed. He must succeed, otherwise our plan will not have a shot at succeeding.” The man nods his head and walks out of the room as Belial contemplates the events of the day as he turns back to watch the screens. The world is ending soon and he must complete the preparations for this event. He must, otherwise Lord Tioneb will not be pleased that would make him even more of a target than Darth is now.
