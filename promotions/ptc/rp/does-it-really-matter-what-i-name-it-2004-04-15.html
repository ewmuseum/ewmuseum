Does it really matter what I name it?

15 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-15 Can you hear it? Can your ears register the screams of a soul being crushed within by the ideals of science and pagan philosophers? I can hear them clear as day. It is like surround sound blaring in my head. Millions upon millions think they can only believe what their senses pick up or what scientists tell them are facts. Then, another large mass of humanity foolishly pray to false profits or live by the teachings of a glorified heretic cult leader. Stupid fleshbags… Have they no faith? Are their minds so closed that they truly believe that creatures, like demons and angels, cannot exist? I know one such being that ignores his inner faith. I heard him speak down about religion and faith as if it is a disease and then, he believes that demons walk the Earth. Seymour Almasy is that hypocritical man. His microscopic mind is so clouded by psychosis that he cannot escape the fiction that has jailed his faith. Let him ramble about philosophers like Frederich Nietzsche and the fictitious creations of a Japanese video game creator. Let him think his soul is safe from my grasp and in a way, I think it is. He need not worry of me tearing his spirit from that battered body because with his pagan ways, he shall be going to hell all by himself. How ironic that the “hero who will save the world” will be spending eternity in the land where evil reigns supreme. That, my friends, is truly comical. I sit comfortably upon a white leather sofa with my shapely blond servant, Dinah, alongside me. Across from us is a pair of wholesome all-American people. Their names are John and Diane. They have brought me to their home to speak with their son, Jeremy. It seems that the young man has been neglecting church, his religious duties, and his schoolwork. His parents fear that he could be heading down a very dark path to sin and various other troublesome things. They called the correct person to set him straight, have they not? No one knows the life of sin better than the good Father Pietro Geist. “I shall do my best,” I softly inform the couple, as I rise to my feet with a gentle grin. “That is all we ask for, Father,” the dark-haired father replies with a handshake. “We know that you can help our Jeremy.” I leave the parents in the well-kept living room and Dinah is hot on my heels, as we creep up the staircase to the second floor of the home. Bible-thumpers are such simple folk. They bought the entire “I’m a holy and good priest” shtick, hook, line and sinker. Now, their child shall pay the price for their stupidity. I notice how the entire house is immaculate. There is not the tiniest particle of dirt, dust, or pet dander in the entire area and just like any god-fearing family would have, there are various crucifixes, pictures, and statues in all the rooms and hallways. The house seems perfect in every way, until the sound of loud rock music can be increasingly heard with each step towards the end of the hallway. There is a large wooden door separating us from the music and a large Marilyn Manson poster covers the upper portion of the door. Interesting how the poster reads, “Antichrist Superstar.” One can surmise that the poster was not a gift from his clean-cut parents. My female companion opens said door and we come across a twenty-something year old boy at the monitor of a computer. His room is engulfed in posters of various bands, most of which are not very Christianity friendly, and the floor is covered in grimy clothes. It boggles my mind how this child can be the flesh and blood of the duo downstairs. Although, I do imagine that this is what my room would look like, if I was a twenty-year old child. “Pardon me,” I say in my normal monotone voice and get no response. “Pardon me.” The music is nearly deafening in this room and that is most likely the reason he cannot hear the words escaping from my lips. I quickly solve the problem with a snap of my fingers and his stereo shuts off. Jeremy quickly turns around and finds me standing before him. He squints his eyes, as he tries to decipher why I am here, but he just gives up and takes the easier way. “Is there something I can help you with, padre?” He asks sternly. “I do not believe so,” I reply and Dinah shuts the door behind her, as she leaves upon seeing the motion to do so. “However, your parents believe that there is something I can do for you. They tell me that you have been not doing things you should be. For example: you are not attending church anymore.” “Look,” he responds firmly, after a sigh and rolling of his eyes. “I just do not want to deal with that stuff anymore. I am old enough to know what is and isn’t right for me and church doesn’t seem to be the great thing it was when I was younger. I am sorry, if that offends you, but that is how I feel and neither you nor my parents can change it.” “Who said that I am here to change your feelings or beliefs?” I ask and clear some debris off his bed, so I may sit on the edge. “What other reason would you be here?” Jeremy replies and places a baseball cap on backwards over his shaggy brown hair. “To offer you something else,” I answer. “It is true that your parents invited me here to bring you back into the fold, but I have been enlightened lately to something new and I think you may be interested in it. There are no strings attached and no harsh guidelines to follow. Care to hear about it?” “I’m listening, Father,” he says to me and hunches over in his chair. That is all I need, young man. I just need to be heard to “convert” you to the ways of my lord and you will be. Well, you will be or you shall find out what spontaneous human combustion is all about. Let us see what choice you will make. “My new church is quite different than the one your parents attend,” I explain with some energy. “It is not even close to being as strict as theirs. We do not preach how you should follow by some rules that were made to keep order back in the ancient days of Israel nor do we tell you to spend every waking our at church praying and paying homage to the lord above. Spending your whole life praying out of fear of being sent to hell is lunacy. The lord did not have that in mind when he allowed man to create religion. We preach that you should live how you wish and do what brings you joy. It is as if you are trying to create your own tiny piece of heaven on Earth in preparation for when you go there. There is no hell. There is no eternity of torture in the flames. That is balderdash created to keep the Neanderthals in line. Are you one of the primates? Of course you are not. Humanity has grown far superior in intelligence over the centuries, so those regulations have become obsolete. What I am giving you is a chance to take your first step towards heaven. You shall even receive a gift from my lord upon ‘converting’. What more can you ask for?” “What kind of gift?” he quizzes me with a skeptical look. “Anything your heart desires,” I answer quickly. “There is nothing outside of my lord’s power.” “That’s bullshit,” he enlightens me. “He won’t do some things.” “I believe a display of his power is in order,” I announce to the young man. “What would you like to see?” “Jessica Alba in a schoolgirl outfit on my bed,” he says with a confident smirk. “As you wish,” I say with a devilish grin. You all know the drill. My fingers snap and the magnificent form of the actress, Jessica Alba, appears laying on the youth’s bed. A miniscule plaid skirt barely covers her white panties and a white blouse is tied closed around her ample chest. Her silky brown hair is parted into a pair of pigtails with red bows at the base of each pigtail. Her feet are locked snuggly in a pair of leather shoes with large gold buckles over top a pair of white socks that come up to her knees. She smiles seductively at Jeremy and his jaw drops to the floor. The young man’s dirty digits protrude out of a hooded black sweatshirt and he reaches out to touch the beauty. Just before his fingertips can even graze the woman’s well-tanned body, I snap my fingers and she vanishes with a puff of black smoke. “Feel the bed,” I instruct him. “It is warm from where she was laying.” Jeremy follows my instructions and runs his hand along the comforter covering his place of rest. He looks at his hand and then, to me with amazement in his eyes. That look is a sign that I am nearing the completion of my objective. “But… But… How?” He stumbled into the question. “It isn’t possible.” “Anything is possible with the power of my lord,” I smile and adjust the leather glove on my right hand. “You see, the lord blessed me with the ability to give people rewards for changing over into this new faith. What you saw there is a small sample. Ask and ye shall receive.” “I want to be a powerful warrior like one of the characters from my favorite video game,” he quickly exclaims and leaps over to me. “What game and will you become a member of the Church of Judas?” I ask and glare at him sternly. “Only by doing that shall your wish come true.” “I pledge myself to it,” he wastes no time in answering. “The game is Final Fantasy VII.” “Then it is done…” I say softly and extend my hand to the young man. The very instant Jeremy’s hand grasps my own to shake it; a surge of energy explodes from my hand into the young man like bolts of lighting, which sends his head flying off his head. His eyes light up and the energy makes his hairs stand up on end. The room if filled with a light blue, almost white glow and just as suddenly as it began, the burst of energy ceased. “What no flashy earth-shattering soul reaping?” you ask? I have noticed that too many of my opponents have realized that Pietro is just the shell in which I fill the vacancy. Therefore, I decided to be less dramatic to keep my presence a secret. I doubt it will work though. It feels like there is a camera following me and everyone can see what I am doing. Moving right along, I hand the young man back his Fox Racing baseball cap, as he tries to regain his breath. “My whole body tingles with heat,” he utters between breaths and I nod in agreement. “How do I know that I have the power?” “Much like a gun, point and fire,” I inform him and take a step away from him. The Jeremy glares at a dart board hanging on the opposite wall of his room. His eyes are engulfed by the same light blue glow from before and with a grunt, he sends a burst of nearly white energy streaking towards the dart board. Upon impact, the dart board explodes, sending pieces of wood and metal in all directions. The newest member of the Church of Judas looks to his glowing hands with pure joy and a small smile comes over my face. We have ourselves another satisfied customer and I head towards the door. Jeremy begins to conjure a small reptilian creature, as I step through the doorway. I know what you are thinking, “Why did I take this young man’s soul, if he was on the highway to hell already?” Well, in such an environment as this, one cannot be sure that his parent’s religious beliefs would not effect his way of thinking once more. Therefore, I made it a sure thing that his soul will be joining the Irish pub owner in the flames. Better to be safe than sorry. It is time to deal with his god-fearing parents. John and Diane look up at me from the bottom of the staircase with worry in their eyes. Dinah dusts some debris off of the shoulder of my jacket, so the pair would not discover it, just before I approach them. “How did it go?” Diane questions me with a slight tremble in her voice. “Do not fret, dear woman,” I say softly and cup her cheek. “Your offspring will be just fine.” “Bless you, Father Geist,” John grins and places his arm around his wife, as I try not to show any distaste for the comment. “Now, please join us for dinner. It is the least we can do.” “My apologies, but I must be going,” I announce and continue to the front door. “I have an appointment with a wrestler and his wife.”
