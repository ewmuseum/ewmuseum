[The Shadow Riders] The Getaway pt. 2 (TTT Finals RP 2)

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Shadow Riders Affiliated Pimp Joined: 08 Apr 2003 Posts: 4 Location: Over here now. Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 9:08 am Post subject: [The Shadow Riders] The Getaway pt. 2 (TTT Finals RP 2) (The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states . . . "The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.". The scene opens to a shot of a quiet suburban street that looks just like any other. Suddenly, the peacefulness of the street is shattered as the sound of screeching tires cuts through the air. Barreling around the corner comes the Shadow Riders in their blood soaked Cadillac. The car speeds down the street and quickly turns into one of the many driveways along the street. Once in the driveway, the Cadillac pulls into the open garage of an average looking house with a well trimmed vivacious lawn and a white picketed fence. Inside of the garage, the car’s motor cuts off as the door slides down concealing the car. The scene now fades out only to re-open in a matter of seconds. This time however, we are taken to a shot from inside of the house’s bathroom. Inside this bathroom, “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage is bent over the sink busily trying his best to get the bloody chunks of brain matter and skull off of his hands. As he does this, “Superstar” Eric Russo stands behind him waiting for his turn at the sink. As he continues to wash his hands, Kage begins to speak.) Kage: “We have to be extremely careful with this little predicament that you got us into. Mike already isn’t too high on us being here and one wrong move from you or me will more then likely put him over the edge. Not to mention the fact that Mike doesn’t think things through too much and he might just end up trying to throw us out of his house if we get him pissed and that might totally screw our chances for New Era. (Thinking about the possibility of Roberts acting in such a way, Russo begins to question Kage.) Russo: “What are we gonna do if he kicks us out? I mean coming here was pretty much our only option. What are we gonna go take on Homo/Phobic with a blood covered car and a headless body in the back seat!?” (Reassuringly, Kage responds.) Kage: “No . . . nothing like that. I don’t plan on leaving this house until we get done what we have to do. I’m just saying that I would really appreciate it if you tried to make sure that it didn’t come to that. I mean Mike is supposedly one of our friends and you don’t just come over to your friends house waving a gun around and laying down the law. It’s just not a cool thing to do.” (As Kage finishes, he reaches over and dries his hands off on a small hand towel hanging next to the sink on a small gold ring. He then moves away from the sink as Russo now moves in to wash his hands off as well. As he does this, the conversation between the two men continues when Russo begins speaking as he washes his hands.) Russo: “Just tell him not to act like he used too. He looked pretty freaked out when he took a look more freaked out then the UWF's roster when we took out Q-Factor. I for one really don’t feel like dealing with him when he’s all freaked out and panicky. It’s just something that I know will end up getting on my nerves if he keeps doing it. That’s why we always used to end up fighting when he was on the road with us. Kage: “Man, look at it from his perspective. Mike just had two guys that he hasn’t seen for the past two months show up in his garage at eight o’clock in the morning with a headless body! What, do you think this kind of thing happens to people everyday!? Mike is a normal guy, he wasn’t expecting something like this to happen while he was eating breakfast! He’s doing us a big favor, don’t forget that.” (As Kage finishes speaking, Russo reaches out and turns off the water. He then grabs hold of a white towel and begins to dry his hands off as he responds.) Russo: “I’m not forgetting that . . . but if him doing a favor for us means that I’m gonna have to take his bullshit again, I’d just as soon tell him to go to hell and just add one more body to the equation.” (With a look of complete disgust on his face, Kage responds.) Kage: “What the hell is wrong with you!? You shot Johnny to get us into this mess and now you wanna shoot Mike too!?” Russo: “No . . . I’m just saying.” Kage: “Well stop just saying! You’re kinda starting to freak me out!” Russo: “Damn! I’m sorry . . . I didn’t think you were gonna be all sensitive about it and shit.” (As Russo finishes drying his hands he hangs the towel back up. As he does this, Kage notices that Russo has managed to turn the immaculate white towel into a blood stained mess. Seeing this Kage exclaims.) Kage: “Are you insane!? What the hell did you just do to his towel!? You ruined it!” (Nonchalantly Russo responds.) Russo: “Chill out, I just dried my hands off a little.” Kage: “You’re supposed to clean them first! You don’t just wipe them on a clean white towel!” Russo: “You saw me wash my hands off first.” Kage: “No . . . I saw you run your hands under the water. I didn’t see you actually wash them.” Russo: “I did wash them though . . . it’s not my fault that blood is a pain in the ass to get off! It’s not like I was purposely trying to make a mess or something. Maybe if he had better soap instead of that cheap ass store brand crap I could have done a better job with it.” Kage: “Don’t even try and use an excuse like that! I used the exact same soap that you did and my towel didn’t look like a fucking tampon when I was done with it now did it!?” (Kage does his best to compose himself then continues.) Kage: “Look, let’s just forget about it . . . we can deal with this one little screw up, ok? I’m just trying to tell you that it’s shit like this that is gonna end up causing a problem with Mike. If Mike came in here right now and saw is towel like that, he would go into one of his fits. You know how he can get when it comes to neatness. So, all I’m asking is that you just try and think before you act in the future while we’re here. I don’t feel like having to deal with Mike just as much as you don’t. Now please don’t take that the wrong way . . . I’m not trying to tell you what to do or anything like that. I’m just asking that you try and show just a little consideration and not put me in a position like that . . . ok?” (In a totally calm and relaxed voice, Russo responds.) Russo: “See, all you have to do is ask me nice like that. It’s no problem, I’ll try my best to think everything through before I act on it. You just keep Mike calm and off my back and there won’t be any problems. Can you do that for me?” (Relieved Kage responds.) Kage: “I’ll do my best . . .” (With a nod to each other, Russo and Kage exit the bathroom and head towards the kitchen of the house. Once there, they are greeted by Mike Roberts. Mike is a young man who appears to be somewhere in his mid to late 20's and is still dressed in his bathrobe. Mike, being hospitable, pours three cups of coffee for his two guests and himself. Kage is the first to pick up a cup and take a sip from it. Wanting to complement Mike in anyway he can to keep the situation smooth, Kage then speaks up.) Kage: “Wow Mike . . . this coffee is really good. Do you know how long it’s been since I had a good cup of coffee in the morning? Hell do you know how long it’s been since I’ve even been up this early in the morning? I mean damn man, me and Eric would have been happy with even some of that tar that you used to try and pass off as coffee. But no, you don’t mess around anymore. You have the good shit that you have to buy in the little one pot in a package deals. It’s really good . . . what flavor is it? I can taste a hint of almond.” (Not buying the false front that Kage is putting up, Mike responds.) Mike: “Jason would you just knock it off for a little while? It’s starting to get a little annoying and that’s pissing me off.” Kage: “Pissing you off!? What did I do?” (With his voice slightly cracking showing signs of an forthcoming outburst, Mike answers.) Mike: “Look, I know you two so you can just drop all of the false fronts that you’re putting up to hide behind. I don’t need you guys to complement me on my coffee or anything like that. I know my coffee is good, I drink the stuff every morning. Would I be drinking it if it was bad? I don’t think so. I had to spend months drinking that instant coffee that they served in the home because of you two but that’s another story. The story I’m more interested in right now is the one about why I have a dead body with half a head in my garage. That’s the story I really wanna know about right now. It’s that story that’s causing me a world of unneeded stress and I don’t like it. Avoiding stress is one of the main reasons I no longer work for you guys but you two don’t seem to understand that now do you considering you’re here in my house right now. (Caught slightly off guard by the response, Kage tries to smooth things over.) Kage: “But Mike . . .” (Kage is cut off as Mike continues.) Mike: “No . . . Jason. It’s my turn to talk now. I wanna ask you question since you seem to have so many answers ok?” Kage: “Ok . . .” Mike: “When you were pulling into my garage, did you see a flashing neon sign that read something like “Mike’s Body Disposal” or maybe one that said “Mike’s Dead Body Hideaway”?” (Doing his best to try and calm Mike down, Kage begins to plead again.) Kage: “C’mon Mike . . .” (Once again Kage is cut off by Mike.) Mike: “Answer my question Jason before you get me really mad at our little situation here. When you pulled into my garage did you or did you not see any signs that may have lead you to believe that my house was a place to stash dead bodies? Well did you?” (Swallowing hard and realizing that he is going to have to play along with the line of questioning, Kage responds.) Kage: “No Mike . . . I can’t say that I did see any signs like that.” Mike: “And do you know why there aren’t signs like that out in front of my new house in this new neighborhood?” Kage: “Why’s that Mike?” Mike: “Because my house is not a place for you two to hide dead bodies until you can think of something else to do with them!” (Kage makes another attempt to quell Mike’s outburst.) Kage: “Mike . . . man.” (Kage’s mention of his name only causes Mike to pick up the pace.) Mike: “Woah there Jason . . . I’m not through talking yet so just shut up.” (The backbone that Mike is showing seems to throw Kage slightly off guard as Mike continues.) Mike: “Now, you two have to understand that if my doctors show up here for their weekly checkup visit only to find me helping two other guys to dispose of a body, I’m gonna be sent back to the nut house for good. There’s not gonna be any discussions, no counseling, no trial hearing from a judge . . . I’m just gonna be thrown directly into the home so they can commit me. Do you know what a serious damper something like that will have on the peaceful future that I have planned for myself? I don’t wanna go back there ever again Jason do you understand that? The last time I was in there, I wondered if they were ever gonna let me out on my own again. I mean I wanna help you two out and all . . . I really do. However, I’m not about to have my freedom snatched away from me to do it.” Kage: “Mike you kno . . .” (Kage is cut off in mid word by Roberts who continues with his rant.) Mike: “I don’t wanna hear it Jason. I mean c’mon you’re not gonna talk your way out of this with me. I know you guys too well for that. There’s absolutely nothing you can do or say to me that is gonna make me forget that I never wanna go back in a mental home again. Now my doctor usually checks in on me when she gets done her shift at the home. That means that she’ll be here to see me in less then an hour and a half. In that time, I suggest that you guys figure out exactly what you’re gonna do and exactly how you’re gonna do it so you can hurry up and get out of my house. That’s all I’m asking of you two.” (With a sigh of relief that Mike’s rant is finally over, Kage calmly responds.) Kage: “That’s all we wanted Mike. We don’t wanna cause you any unneeded worries over us. All we want to do is use the phone and make a few calls so we can get out of your hair. The faster we make those calls, the faster we leave.” Mike: “Why didn’t you just say that to me in the first place instead of sitting here all calm drinking my coffee and chit-chatting? Do you know how much time you wasted with that horse crap? I can’t take this with you two . . . just go make your calls. There’s a phone down the hall in the bedroom.” (Kage crosses the kitchen as he starts down the hall towards the bedroom. As he does this, he calls back to Mike over his shoulder.) Kage: “Thank you for this Mike, you’re a good friend too us. We’ll remember this if you ever need anything from us.” (Mike drops his head shaking it from side to side as he speaks aloud to himself.) Mike: “Sure . . . that’s right . . . I’m a good friend. I’m a real good friend. I’m a good friend, a nice guy, a soon to be a psychiatrically committed nice guy but oh well.” (At this point Mike looks up to see Russo staring at him with a smile on his face.) Mike: “What are you smiling at?” Russo: “Nothing man, I just didn’t think you were actually gonna help us out. Thanks for this . . . seriously, thanks.” (Russo and Mike share a slight laugh as Mike responds.) Mike: “Don’t mention it. I may not be happy about it but I won’t ever not help you guys out. My dad would rip me a new one if I landed his two prize students in jail.” **MEANWHILE** (The scene opens in the dining room of none other then Joey Deniro. Deniro sits at a large dining table dressed in a silken robe. He is eating a large breakfast as he talks on the phone.) Joey: “Let’s just say for argument’s sake that the doctor shows up at the for the daily check in. What do you think she’ll do given the present situation?” (Joey pauses as though listening to someone on the other end of the line.) Joey: “No, you really think she’ll fucking freak out asshole? That’s not the type of answer I’m looking for from you. I don’t know exactly how bad the situation is because I’m not there to fucking see it now am I? What I’m asking you is how big of a freak out is the situation is going to warrant . . . a big one or a little one?” (The scene splits to show Kage pacing around Mike’s bedroom with the phone pressed to his ear while still holding a shot of Joey in the other half of the screen. Kage begins to respond.) Kage: “Look Joey, I really appreciate your help in this but you have to understand just how explosive this situation can become at almost any time. If this doctor shows up here after a long shift at the hospital to check on her reformed patient and finds Mike and two guys that she has never seen before moving the package around in the kitchen, there’s no telling what she’s bound to do. I for one am not in the mood to find out either if you hear what I’m saying to you.” Joey: “Ok, ok. Let’s just say for instance that things get a little too messy for a quick clean-up. Do you think we might have to take out a few more packages then originally expected?” Kage: “Well there’s always the chance that something like that can happen but it’s not a likely scenario.” Joey: “And tell me, why is that?” Kage: “Well you know me and Eric, we’re gonna do whatever we have too to get ourselves out of this situation. However it’s not ever gonna come down to that because by that time you’re gonna have this all cleared up for us. You’re gonna do that because we are not only delivering the package that you wanted but we’re also gonna put up a nice little friendship fund in your name. On the other hand if I end up having to clear up a situation with one of my good friends and his doctor because you couldn’t get the job done in time, I’m not gonna be a very friendly person in the future. That’s something neither of us want because it’s a lose-lose situation.” Joey: “I understand all of this Jason. I just like to make sure I have back up plans in case something falls through. Everything I do relies on the what if’s and that’s why I’m still here. It’s all about having backups.” Kage: “No Joey, you’re not understanding me. There are no what if’s in this situation so I don’t wanna hear about something that doesn’t exist. What I wanna hear from you is that I don’t have to worry about anything. I wanna hear that you have this entire situation under control and everything will be all cleared up by the deadline. I want you to tell me to go sit in the kitchen, have a cup of coffee and kick back while I wait for the cleaners, which should be here any minute now.” Joey: “You have nothing to worry about Jason. I have this entire situation under control and everything will be all cleared up by the deadline. So, go sit in the kitchen, have a cup of coffee and kick back while you wait for Calestro, who should be there any minute now. Does that sound good to you?” Kage: “Calestro is coming here to help us out?” Joey: “Oh I see you’re happy and calm now. Feel better or something?” Kage: “Yeah . . . a whole hell of a lot better. Why didn’t you just say that in the first place?” (The scene cuts to a shot inside of a dark, damp, cellar looking through a doorway into the main area. Int that area we see a man fastened to a wooden chair with ropes. The man is being worked over by a couple of well dressed men. The camera pans to the right to reveal a man standing in front of a small desk, phone in hand with his back to us. We can only guess that this is Calestro. We can also see that Calestro has a small piece of paper on which he is jotting down details as they are relayed to him over the phone. Calestro begins speaking into the phone.) Calestro: “So, is the doctor easily freaked out?” (Calestro pauses.) Calestro: “What time is she expected?” (He jots down the time.) Calestro: “Give me the names of all the major players again. I wanna make sure I have them all written down.” (He begins writing down the names as he reads them off.) Calestro: “Jason . . .” (Finally we get a look at the paper he’s jotting notes down on. It reads:) 3492 Ruskin Lane Escondido 1 body (headless) Blood covered, shot up car Jason (long hair) Calestro: “ . . . Eric . . . Mike . . . and we don’t know the doctor’s name. Ok, got it.” (Calestro jots down the last few notes.) Eric (cocky) Mike (his house) Doctor (10: 30) Calestro: “Expect a call from me around 11:30 either way. The place is about thirty minutes away from me right now. I’ll be there in ten.” (Calestro hangs up the phone never revealing his face to us as the scene fades out.) **TEN MINUTES LATER** (A car comes whipping down the quiet suburban street on which Mike’s home is located. The car speeds down the street abruptly stopping directly in front of Mike’s house. The front door of the car swings open as Calestro steps out. He approaches the front door of the house. As he reaches the door, Calestro extends his finger and presses down on the door bell of the house. The sound of the doorbell can be heard ringing in the house. The door is quickly opened by Mike Roberts. Roberts steps back startled by the physical stature of the man. Calestro glances down at his pad of paper then back up at Mike as he begins to speak.) Calestro: “You must be Mike the owner of this lovely piece of property. Am I right?” Mike: “Yes, you are.” (Calestro extends his hand as he introduces himself.) Calestro: “Mr. Deniro sent me, you can call me Calestro. I am here to lend my cleaning services to you.” (Mike responds as he grabs Calestro’s hand and shakes it.) Mike: “That’s good to hear, we’ve got quite a mess.” Calestro: “So I’ve been informed. May I get to work now?” Mike: “Be my guest.” (Mike and Calestro begin walking towards the dining room. As they do so, Calestro looks towards Mike and begins to speak.) Calestro: “I would also like to express to you the gratitude on the part of my good friend Mr. Deniro for your cooperation in this situation. Let me just say to you that Mr. Deniro’s gratitude does not come easy but it is definitely worth having.” (As Mike and Calestro enter the dining room, Russo and Kage stand up to greet them.) Calestro: “Let me guess, you’re Jason; and if I’m right on that, you must be Eric. Now, let me get straight to the point of what we’re all doing here. If I understood what I was told about the situation, we are on a truncated timetable here are we not?” (Mike pipes up.) Mike: “Yes.” Calestro: “You’re doctor . . .” (Calestro glances down at his note paper taking a quick look at the information as he continues seamlessly.) Calestro: “ . . . will be dropping by at 10:30. Is that right?” Mike: “Yes it is.” Calestro: “I was also informed that if the doctor were to get here to find us here at the same time, that she would not find it too acceptable.” Mike: “No she won’t like that one bit and neither would I.” Calestro: “Fine then, that will give us about an hour to hurry up and get our jobs done. Now this is possible but only if you follow my every direction no matter what. We have to do this clean and quick so pay attention. Now there’s a headless body in a car out in the garage right? Well let me see it so I can see what we’re gonna be dealing with.” (With that the picture fades out as the screen once again fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... "The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.". The screen now fades to black.) _________________ "You are nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders." - "Superstar" Eric Russo
