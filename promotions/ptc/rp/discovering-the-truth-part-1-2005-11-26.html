Discovering The Truth! (Part 1)

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 The sudden chilling feeling of a grave site is shown to your eyes. The darkness, evil rage is plunged into the air, wiggling through out the area as the camera pans out. "Hello…" The voice is familiar. That of a young girl that we all know. A child almost like. Panning through out the grave’s that sit in front of us. It was a feeling of death. A feeling of lost souls that were laying in their final resting place, taken away from the world that they lay in. "The dead. People that no longer matter to this world. People that no longer matter to the one’s that loved them…" Cold air began to race along the grassy noel that each headstone sat on. Blowing the leaves off of the heavy branches that swung in the air. The moon, hanging way over the eyes of us. Shining through out the hundreds of dead bodies that were resting six feet under the ground. "Elia Smith. The girl that you see with your eyes but if you look deep down inside you will see someone different. Someone that isn’t supposed to be there." Discovering the shadow effect of a woman standing next to a grave. Her hands, crossed together at her chest which stood out wide in the black leather outfit she wore. My eyes, glowing in a light blue, different then the dark red that they sometimes are. Coming in closer, watching the fog as it floats by. "Appearances can be different on the inside from the outside. Not everything you see on the outside is the same as the inside. Right now you see a feeble young girl who can’t hurt a fly. One that screams when the sight of blood comes. One that’s as weak as a child. But on the inside, you can see someone on a mission. Someone that is strong with rage, revenge, and most of all…evil." Uncrossing my arms, watching as the camera comes in closer and closer, passing by grave after grave, one of a young girl who was only sex years old when she died. "Jimmy Bong. You signed up just like me for the Global Turnbuckle Tournament Five. Expecting to face Elia Smith. A female. A weak pathetic female who with the slightest pain taps out with so much force, screaming as loud as she can. But what you will soon figure out Mr. Bong is that what you see isn’t what you get." That is right. What you see isn’t what you get. The camera, the very lens that stands in front of me, stands there as the fog pours by. "Mr. Bong, right next to me is a grave. A grave that you will soon come to see as reality. It’s not to scare you. It’s not to make you believe in my words. It’s to open up your mind and realize that sometimes, no matter how you fight it, faith always comes back around. You see, soon I will have my hands on the Cross of Vinaca. The same cross that will solidify the death of yours truly. The cross of life. The cross of death. My life source that I need." Sighing, taking a deep breath before stepping back from the tree that I stood by. Caressing the gander of my chest with every step backwards. "Mr. Bong, death isn’t a tragedy to me. It’s a resurrection of my life. So get ready, ready for me…" The fog came rolling in. Covering my body like a wave of water or birds, swarming the area till I was no longer in sight. All you could see was the very name that was carved into the headstone that read, Elia Smith. "Reamond…"
