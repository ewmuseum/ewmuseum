Chapter Five: Revisit (GT RP #2)

Donny Diamond Emotionless Monster Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 59 Location: My own personal Hell. Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 7:59 pm Post subject: Chapter Five: Revisit (GT RP #2) Chapter Five: Revisit Before one goes into a match of this magnitude there are a few things that one must do. Have a few drinks with some old friends, spend a night on the town with your special someone, or chill out with the family in front of the tube. Why? That is what people do before they head into something where their life is put at risk. Their life is not just at risk because of the unknown stipulations of this match; it’s at risk because of the stakes. When something as big as this Championship is on the line, people will do anything it takes to win. This is a chance you may only get once in your career so of course one would do anything to win. If you say otherwise then before you step into the ring ask yourself what this match really means to you. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~ †During life there comes a time A time when you must shut your mouth And listen to what everyone is telling you Sometimes you must put your pride aside† ~`~ Hell ~`~ | ~`~ Time stands still in Hell ~`~ ~¤~ The large castle can once again be seen, as bubbles can be heard popping in the see of lava surrounding it. Standing before the two shadow guards is Donny Diamond, dressed in his normal attire as the winds of the underworld blow the back of his coat around. They do not even move a muscle, as they know exactly who he is, after a moment of staring them down for no real reason he walks into his father’s home. He walks down the center hallway, and notices his father no to be around. Alongside the throne of his father’s servants sits and points towards the dinning area as his finger shakes nervously. Nodding towards the servant he makes his way down the dinning hall, and into the dinning area as he notices his father, and Sorrow sitting at the table enjoying a nice dinnertime meal. ~¤~ †If you don’t want to be the victim Then you must set yourself in order This is not the time to showcase your strength On this hour you must prove everything to yourself† Donny Diamond: I see you have grown a custom to having dinner without me. Satan: Ah, welcome my son. Sorry we could not wait for you, after all it is a very long journey from that pillar to my kingdom. Donny Diamond: Indeed it is, but it was you that put my on the pillar in the first place. Why not use the teleportation powers of Sorrow to have me arrive with you both at the palace? Satan: What would be the fun in that my son? Sorrow: Besides one often finds lot of information as he wonders alone in his mind. In order to achieve the true foundation of universal knowledge one must also conquer all of the questions he holds within. Donny Diamond: What makes you think I have questions about myself? Who are you to say anything about me, Sorrow? You don’t know anything about me, other than what my father has told you. Satan: Take a seat my son, and we will see. [Hearing his father’s words, he pulls out a chair from the table. As he goes to take a seat in the chair a silver plate appears before him. On it is different selections from the different dinning tables around. He grabs hold of the chicken leg on his plate and brings it up towards his mouth where he takes a big bite out of the flesh and chews away.] †This is a chance for only the best The best of the best The fittest of the fit So I take a look and wonder as to why some of you were selected† Satan: So my son, you seem to think you have no questions about yourself? Of course you do, because you are human. All humans question themselves, and when that happens you are left to ask yourself questions about what you really are. Sorrow: Even you do this Donny. For instance, since the day you killed your father and you were sent to spend some time in prison you had questions. You wondered as to if you really did do something you would eventually regret. That alone is an example of you questioning yourself. Donny Diamond: I do not question my reasons for killing that man. He was never a father to me; the man sitting at the end of that table is my only father. Satan: Perhaps, but listen to the words he has spoken before you pass judgment. Never did he say you questioned the murder of your father for your own purposes, there is always many different angles in which you must look at things Donny. Sorrow: Now the words we have all spoken has gotten you to think a bit. This is the questions that all souls ask before they are to stand in front of the pearly gates. No one is asking you to go back on your choices; we’re just showing you ways to gain the advantage over your competitors during your two matches. If you can get your mind focused completely on your assault, and do not question yourself once then the victory is yours for the taking. Donny Diamond: Then if it is not choices for myself I should be asking, what is it I should be questioning? Please old wise seer that has the knowledge of all shed some light on my feeble human mind. Sorrow: Ah, sarcasm how nice. Every choice you make does not only affect you, but it can also affect others. One word answers all of those questions you have about the murder of your father. Ron. [Everything starts to spin, and the scenery slowly turns from a mix of many colors into one solid color of red. The pace of the spinning quickens and soon everything goes from red to black, and the underworld is no longer where he is. Once again he is back in the bedroom of his home, sitting on his bed. Getting up from his bed he walks towards his dresser in the far left corner. On top of it is a letter, rolled up and sealed with the Diamond family emblem in wax. He breaks the seal then unrolls the parchment; he takes a seat on the bed where he then reads it.] †This is no time to give the rookies a chance A chance to prove their worth This is where legends are to be made Not for new stars to be born† ~`~ Donny Diamond’s Estate ~`~ | ~`~ April 12, 2003. 2:08 PM. ~`~ “After everything my brother, and myself have gone through I must say I have learned a lot about him. Even though he has done some harsh things in his days, he has changed some since we have came back together as a family. A part from our stepbrother Neo, I am the only family he has left. So he may have done something as horrible as killing our father, for that I forgive him, because I know he will never tarnish the new relationship we have.” [He looks down at the scroll for a moment, before he crumples it up inside of his hand then tosses it into the trash. For a moment he thinks, then he gets up from his bed and opens the door to his bedroom. Climbing down the stairs he grabs his trench coat from Alfred who is waiting at the front doors opening them with his arm out stretched for where the coat was a moment ago. Without giving it a second thought he slips the coat on, and walks down the stairs leading out the front of his estate. Walking to the side of his stairs, he gets on his motorcycle as Alfred tosses they keys to him from the porch.] †So make yourself wonder as to why you’re here Then ask yourself if you really deserve this opportunity Now figure it out as to if you doubt yourself Then ask yourself if your really worth this honor† [Placing the key into the hole he starts it up, engines booming he speeds off making the circle towards the gates. Zooming onto the highway he speeds down it as the wind forces his coat to whip around like a weapon of mass destruction. Time passes quickly and he is seen pulling up into the parking lot of a nearby hospital. Stopping the engine he takes the keys out, and places them in his pants pocket as he starts walking towards the entrance. Doors slide open as they do in all hospitals and he is greeted with a gust of bone chilling air while walking inside to see the mass amounts of people walking back, and forth to various quarters and sectors of the facility. He makes his way towards the administration desk for he can get some instructions for his destination.] Donny Diamond: Could one of you please tell me where to find ICU? Administrator: Yes sir we would be glad to help you, but… Donny Diamond: Good then stop all of this useless chatting, and give me some damn directions. Administrator: Excuse me sir, but visitors are not allowed to go there without authorization from a hospital physician. Donny Diamond: Who said anything about me going there? Get your facts straight lady. I asked for some directions, I never said I would be going there. See I got to deliver some supplies to the laundry department back there, and they told me it was by ICU. Administrator: Very well sir. However the laundry department is not near ICU. Donny Diamond: Look I got my orders ok? Stop giving me the runaround and give me the directions to ICU. Administrator: Go down that hall, take the fourth left you come across and hit the elevators. Take them to the second floor, then take a right go down the hall and take the second right and you will see it down that hall on your left hand side. †One chance Is what you’re given So take this time to wonder Are you really worth it† [Quickly he walks down the hallway the administrator pointed towards. He follows her directions, as he comes across the elevators. The door slides open and he steps in as he can feel the eyes of everyone with him scanning him closely. After what seemed like eternity it finally opens again and he follows the directions once more till he comes upon the door to ICU. Opening the door he walks inside, immediately at the sight of him some of the nurses, both female and male, rush towards him.] Nurse #1: You are not aloud here. Donny Diamond: Yeah, yeah I heard all of this from the bimbo out at the front desk. Nurse #2: Please leave now before we are forced to call security up here. Donny Diamond: Now why does everyone have to go and make things complicated? Nurse #3: Visitors are not allowed in this quarter of the hospital please leave now! Nurse #2: That’s it I’m calling security now. Donny Diamond: Come on now why does everyone have to be so hasty? Are you going to deny someone a visitor just because of some stupid rule? Livin’ up a bit guys, rules are made to be broken. [Before the male nurse Donny grabs him around the back of the shirt and pulls him back.] Donny Diamond: See I said there was no need for some trouble to be started. I will not bother anyone, and believe me this person is most likely looking forward to my little visit. So if you wish not to have this colleague of yours become a patient I suggest you follow me. [With the nurse still in his grasp he walks into a room, a very small room, and makes motion for the others to follow. They do as told and he steps out closing the door behind him, and then locks it from the outside. Glancing over to the marker board with all of the patients written on it he sees “205 Ron Diamond”. A devilish smile crosses over his face for a moment to give us a slight sign showing us in most ways he is still human. Down the hallway he comes until he arrives at room 205 where he pulls open the curtain and steps in.] †Look at all of the different members of your family Ask them what are the big things they accomplished Now once again go and ask yourself Do I really deserve this honor† [There he lies, in a hospital gown and his face entirely wrapped in bandages. Perhaps out of a sign of respect to his burned up brother he removes his black sunglasses then takes a seat beside Ron. He removes one of his gloves and places his open palm across the forehead of Ron. Once more the scenery changes and we are inside of a place where the sky is red with flames raining down as Donny stands on a small floating island the is covered with a stone top, and enclosed with a steel cage like look. Before him stands a very normal Ron Diamond, and they stand there staring eye to eye.] Ron Diamond: So after what you did to me, you still dared to show your face? Not only did you dare show your face, but also you decided to enter my mind. Donny Diamond: I found your letter. Ron Diamond: My letter? Who really gives a damn brother, after what you did to me I will never forgive you. Right here I vow to never forgive you for torturing me like this, in fact I will do to you what you continue to do to our father. Donny Diamond: So this is what it’s all about Ron? Our father? Let me tell you a thing or two about our father Ron. He never loved you Ron, he just used that as a way to get at me. Our father knew how much it would kill me to show love towards my twin brother when I was the one he hated most. Ron Diamond: Why does everything have to revolve around you Donny? You know for the past couple of months when we were together, I really believed you had changed Donny. [After saying those words, Ron lunges towards Donny. He sidesteps his brother, and Ron slides past him. Slowly he turns back to face his brother eye-to-eye across the small cage.] †You may have those little fights With special people in your life But you never know how much you love them Until it is to late† Donny Diamond: That’s the problem with you Ron; you’re always looking for someone to change. This is real life brother, people cannot simply change who they are overnight. You have to understand as well that maybe some people do not want to change because they have come to like and understand as to whom they are. Ron Diamond: You cannot possibly sit there, and tell me you are proud and comfortable with all of the decisions you have made so far in your life. Somehow I know that not to be true Donny. Yeah you may be the bastard that killed our father, but you paid your time for doing that crime. There is no need for you to feel like you must stay as this beast that everyone thinks you are you can become the man I know you can be. Donny Diamond: Never do you listen Ron. I live with the things I did during my life, and I do it proudly because I did those things for a reason and I will not look down on the actions I have made. I made the death of our father for a reason; I stand by that reason Ron because I still feel very strongly about it. Ron Diamond: No brother that is just your mind playing tricks on you. I will not believe that you are proud of yourself for killing our father. Donny Diamond: I am proud of it Ron because it makes up a very vital part of me. If I was given the opportunity of doing it again I would, since I believe it was the right thing for me to do. His death was not just done out of hate, I also did it to protect you. If I didn’t then he would of continued to hurt you through me Ron, can you not see this? Ron Diamond: Why do you continue to try and lie to me? My father would of never used me like a pawn in a game of chess, just so that he could find a way to hurt you. He loved me Donny, you just killed him because you were jealous of the affection he was showing towards me. [Once again Ron lunges towards Donny, however instead of sidestepping he remains still and grabs Ron around the neck. Using his strength he pushes Ron back and presses him up against the cage. He lifts Ron into the air up against the cage wall, choking him.] †Lots of things can happen In such a little amount of time So keep them close Because you never know when they can be taken away† Ron Diamond: Go on Donny and kill me just like you did to our father. You came here not to make amends with me after you burned my face; you came here to finish what you started. Deep down inside you know as long as I’m alive then a part of our father will forever live. Donny Diamond: If ending your life is what I wanted to do then I would have done it a long time ago. I know that as long as you live a part of him remains; maybe that is why I keep you around, because I know you loved him even though your love for him is blinding you from seeing the truth. Remember that Ron, because one day when I’m not here to save you then you will die because you are to blind to see the truth. Ron Diamond: Maybe I’m blind brother, but at least I’m not hiding who I really am. So which of us is worse off? Me for being blind or you for hiding the truth from yourself? Donny Diamond: I’m not hiding the truth Ron; I’m just trying to give you a glimpse into reality. I got something big going down for the PTC Ron. If I make it out alive, I will make my return as Global Champion, then I will come back to pay you another visit. Until then brother. [Releasing his grip on the neck of his twin brother, Ron slides back down as his feet touch the ground again. Donny turns around from facing his brother, and he walks slowly towards the other side of the caged floating island. Ron keeps his eyes focused on Donny as he slowly starts to fade away. Back by the bedside of his brother he opens his eyes, then removes his hand from the bandaged forehead is was resting on. Sliding his glove back on, he also puts back on his black sunglasses as he gets up from his seat still looking down at his sleeping burned up brother.] †This is something to look forward to Everyone may doubt in your ability Because some don’t even believe you to be worthy Worthy of this spot† Donny Diamond: Soon brother I shall return, and once I do it will be known as the new Diamond legacy. Be prepared for something you do not understand, and something you will be forced to accept. ~¤~ Without taking another look at his brother he walks out of the small room closing the curtain back after doing so. The nurses inside of the room can be heard trying to kick the door open as Donny walks right past them. All noise then suddenly stops as a beeping noise can be heard coming from the heart monitor Ron was hooked up too. Right after hearing the beep a steady noise can be heard to notify the nurses of heart failure. Everything around him turns an eerie gray, and slowly he turns to the side where he sees a large black shadow. ~¤~ †Remember to put your best effort forth This is not the night for stars to be born Legends will be made Your name will be forever remembered Do yourself proud and do not fail This chance will never be revisited by you† _________________ DWO Record: 9-5-0 PTC Record: 1-0 -I only mark for those deserving, and you know who you are.-
