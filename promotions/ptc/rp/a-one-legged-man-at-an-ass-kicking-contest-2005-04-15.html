Not to mention cars traveling through the city and lights shining from office buildings where frantic men and women pull all-nighters. And it wasn’t stormy either. It was more like misty. Or drizzly. Yeah, it was a well-lit and drizzly night in San Francisco. Lights of all the colors of the rainbow are reflected on the sheen of the streets, like a concrete Pollack. And in the landscape of luminance rises an edifice whose darkness came from more than any lack of light from within. No, the OmniNational Industries building seemed to emit shadows from somewhere deep with in empty halls. And darkest of all was the top floor, high above the earth, cold and joyless like a shadow of heaven. In the depths of this space lurks the Devil that rules this structure… Tsunami Oni. He sits in the shadows, devoid of the white facepaint and small horns he typically affects. His eyes are a brooding black, no longer hidden by glowing green lenses. He is dresses in a black silk robe and loose pants, the shine from the exquisite material visible even in this gloomy environment, the sole illumination come from the moon shining a window, half-hidden by clouds. The eyes of Oni stare at nothing as he twirls a cane tipped with the head of a silver dragon.
