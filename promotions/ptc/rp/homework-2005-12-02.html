Homework

2 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-02 It’s a cold miserable day that no human being should have to be stuck out in. Its about 12 degrees below outside and oh yeah it’s SNOWING!!! How hard? Hard enough that you’d think Sailor Mercury hit you with one of the spikes from the "Mercury Ice Storm Blast" its that cold! The streets of New York City are all but deserted as you take a look around and note maybe one or two brave souls but nothing truly inspiring to look at as the street you ended up walking on had been made almost unworkable by the majority of the ice that was covering the sidewalk! As you walk past the "Virgin Megastore" you look up ahead in the blinding snow and note a house about 3 blocks up from your current position and decide that, seeing as how you don’t want to be a casualty to the cold, you walk the extra distance towards that house and see if anyone is home… Meanwhile in that very house… "No No No Michiru! You have to be careful when you hit a move like that hun! You can hurt someone bad if you miss the move that you just tried…here let me show you." The young man in that house says he climbs the turnbuckle and shows Michiru the proper way that the move is performed before letting her try it again. This goes on for a good 20 minutes as Michiru was finally getting the hang of the move that the young man showed her and was relieved when that part of training was over…after all they had been going at that one move for 45 minutes! Oh well if Josh Matthews had a hard time doing it then you understand her situation right? All the while this was going on another young lady was getting lunch together for everyone that lived in the complex when she heard a knock at the door and being the lady that she was she didn’t keep whomever was at that door waiting long. The young lady opened the door and saw you standing their shivering from the cold and hurriedly bring you into the house and out of the cold. Not knowing what else to do the young lady calls for someone else to come upstairs and help her with you. Another lady comes from the spiral staircase and behind her is apparently the head of the household but you don’t know that yet. Currently your thoughts are with getting warm and not catching hypothermia or some other really nasty affliction associated with walking in the mess that you were out in only a little while ago! As the young lady comes down with the other young man in the house you began to speak to the first young lady while trying to keep your mind off of the cold and praying that you can at least stay the night in the house. "Thanks" you say to the first before introducing yourself to young lady sitting next to you and as you look her up and down and the first thing you notice about her is the orange mane of hair on her head and her sparkling blue eyes that are usually very much cold and calculating at times are shining with concern for your well being and that in a way makes you smile. "Don’t worry about it. We were taught well by our leader that if anyone comes here that needs help we are to give it and right now you look like you really need it! Would you like a sandwich? I was just in the middle of making lunch for the rest of the house when you came." The lady asks you and you nod your head with an affirmative before she gets up and you get a better look at the rest of her outfit. A white turtleneck with a pair of Arizona Blue Jeans and white socks, which while simple in nature fit her to a tee and you can’t help but smile as you get ready to speak again. "By the way I didn’t catch your name. I told you mine so its only fair that you tell me yours right?" You ask the young lady and she nods before getting the lettuce, tomato and the tuna fish out for the sandwich she was going to make you. "My name is Juri Arisugawa. I live here with the rest of the people in the house and my…"friend" is Ms. Tina Davis whom I partner with up on occasion in the wrestling ring. Joshua Curtis is the leader of a faction that he calls the ‘Army of Old School’ and he taught us almost everything that he knows about the business of Pro Wrestling. He’s got a friend of his in Brad Cummings training our newest wrestler in Michiru Kaiou for a match she has coming up in a really big tournament coming up and she’s kind of nervous about it. I know the feeling because when I wrestled in a ‘last woman standing’ match for the Women’s championship I was really nervous but I think Joshua was more nervous than I was because it was my first match ever and I hadn’t been tested before like I was going to back then. I’m glad I won the match and I’m glad that I got to be the first woman to win the World Title for him. THAT was an honor." "It was even more son an honor being the manager of the "Beautiful Leopard" on that night because she went through more in that match than I could have possibly asked for from ANYONE! She was so busted up from the match that I almost wonder if we’ll get to see her wrestle again in the ring…oh where are my manners? My name is Joshua Curtis and I’m the head of the household that you are currently in. I see that you met Juri already and this young lady next to me is Tina Davis whom was my first ever International Champion in the days of ‘House of Pain’ wrestling and that was a momentous occasion for us to be sure. I was so happy then that I almost thought nothing could top it until Juri won the Women’s title and then that became my crowning achievement as a manage." Joshua explained at which point Brad came up with Michiru and as soon as she noticed you she stopped what she was doing and greeted you politely before speaking. "So you were the one that Juri got out of the cold hmm? Juri’s such a sweetheart and is someone I’m glad to call a friend of mine." Michiru said as she took a seat right next to you whom by now have sufficiently thawed out and was munching happily on the sandwich that Juri had given you but stopped long enough to speak with the newest superstar in the camp of "Old School". "Everyone here has been quite nice Michiru and I’m glad I decided to come here when I did or God knows what would have happened! I do have to ask you one thing though: Are you nervous about your upcoming match against Archer in Round one? It sounds like an uphill battle with you being the new kid and he sounds like he’s been around the block a couple of times. I’d be nervous if it were me in my first match against a seasoned vet but I guess if you have the same kind of success that Juri had or even Tina then that’s a plus right?" You ask Michiru and she nods before speaking. "Its always a challenge when you come into a situation that you never experienced before but then again I also performed in concerts and to me this is like one big concert performance with another person involved. I look at Archer kind of like my partner in this duet and we are going to make some beautiful music in the ring the likes of which have never been seen and may never be seen again because God only knows if we’ll cross paths again after this match you know?" Michiru asks you and you nod as you take the last bite of the sandwich and walk over to the kitchen to rinse the plate out before speaking again. "I understand completely Michiru and I know you have an affection for one Tessa Windsor but the problem is to get to her you have to make it to the finals and to do that you have to make it past Archer first! I’d love to see the match between you and Tessa as I know the rest of your faction would but the problem is you have to take it one match at a time in this business right?" You ask Michiru and she laughs before speaking again. "Spoken like a veteran. It’s true that I want to face Tessa yes it is, BUT I also know that standing in my way of getting to that match is Archer and don’t think that I have overlooked him at all because that just doesn’t happen here. We know how to approach things like this and its something that has helped us in the past be successful. Well listen darling I must go get a shower and get ready for tonight. We’re supposed to be having company but witht the snowstorm that hit us I don’t know if they are coming. You are more than welcome to stay until the snowstorm blows over if you’d like…after all its not like us to throw someone back out in that mess that’s going on outside." Michiru said and you took the offer willingly as you went upstairs and got a change of clothes in the room shared by Joshua and his boyfriend Zack Johnson. As we leave you seem to be enjoying yourself with the company as it DID come and the one thing that came out of it for you was you made some new friends and even better allies! Au Revoir J
