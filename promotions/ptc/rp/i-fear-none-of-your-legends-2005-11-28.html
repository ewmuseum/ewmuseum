I Fear None of Your Legends

28 November 2005

Date: 2005-11-28 "Gwynn's impressive … " Langsuyar (PrimeTimeCentral.net) "Gwynn all the way because he simply PWNS anyone … " BraveHeartFan (PrimeTimeCentral.net) " … irreplaceable talent … " [name withheld] (CoreWrestling.net) For several days I've been watching, listening, reading these multimedia compositions of the supposed greatest wrestlers of today. They hail from XUW, UWF, PRIME, fWo, and other infinite acronyms for mediocrity. They sit around and deliberate as to who the most impressive is, as to who they expect to win this tournament. I have heard so many names getting tossed around. One I haven't heard is "The Avatar" William Gwynn. Why would this be? I have come up with only one solution. My counterparts and these fans, simply are not familiar with the greatness of William GWynn. They have not seen the man who has beaten PRIME's Diamond in the Rough 2004 four times. They have not seen the man who held Core Wrestling's Legacy Championship for a record length of time. They've not seen the man who is only weeks away from being crowned Core Wrestling's Ringmaster 2005. You've not seen me, folks. That's the only solution. For if you had seen me, if you knew what I was capable of, this bracket would melt in respect. This tournament would collapse upon itself and be only a vehicle for my furthur glory. But it shall nonetheless. Hark! I ride into this tournament with only endless pride and glory ahead. Recognize me now! Recognize this power! This Earth-Shaking Potential! It is upon you now. You know now what you must fear. You know now what you must tremble before. The British Bomber cannot stand in my way, he's dead. Who dares to step forth next? Who dares? I fear none of your legends. "The Avatar" William Gwynn Here's the audio. This time the outcome will be different. BELEE' this hype or ELSE …
