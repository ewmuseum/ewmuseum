A Little Help Part 1

7 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-07 What had he done, he had tried to end his life, to end the suffering so he could be what? Back together with Justine and his unborn baby? Or turned into a cold cadaver that would turn into dust? He didn’t believe in heaven or hell, so what good ending his life would bring him? He had no idea, but it seemed like the right thing to do at the moment. It’s funny how now that Justine is gone, he uses her as inspiration. He needs to talk to himself about her so that he can go out there and do what he used to do. He didn’t need the pain that came with her name to get up and fight. Now he was just a fragment of what he used to be. It was time for him to do what he was supposed to do, take what had fallen apart, put it together and go out there and win this once a year tournament. It was right there for him, and he just had to reach his arm over and grab it. He tasted victory after his match with Joshua Taro Freedom, whose strategy was to thrash Digital and Justine. That did not sit well with Mortality and he went out there and looked like he used to. He was strong, agile and most of all, smart. You don’t see that Digital lately. It is pretty frustrating that he needs something like the death of Justine and his unborn child to fuel his hatred for what he had become, and to fuel his determination in the ring. Nobody is really sure if Digital can take any more of this mental punishment that he puts himself through. Those deaths hit him pretty hard, and there was nothing to show us that he would ever get over them. He tried to kill himself in his desperation and his depression. He was looking down at his shoes as a distant voice spoke. He didn’t know if the voice was directed at him, or at his surroundings, but he really didn’t care. He was in his own little world right in this moment, and there was nothing that mattered. Digital looked around trying to take in his surroundings, he stared blankly at the white walls and the small windows that let the light of the sun give the room a sort of glow. He noticed then that he was not alone. He looked right in front of himself and saw a woman sitting there talking. He couldn’t hear her words, but she looked very professional. He looked to his right and saw a teenage girl with long black hair sitting there listening to what the strange woman was saying. To his left a boy of about twenty looked blankly to the woman, in his eyes Digital saw the sadness and the desperation of a young man who had lost something and was now searching for it. There were more people in the room. There were faces that Digital did not know, faces with pain and sadness in their eyes. Most of them looked very young, probably all of them were teenagers, but why were they here? Digital was pretty lost in his own world that he had completely forgotten. After attempting suicide he promised Burton that he would seek help, but how could these kids help him. It was just not possible; they looked more lost than himself. He sat straight and finally heard the woman talking. ‘Now, as some of you may have noticed, we have here Antonio Vargas, or someone you know as Digital Mortality from the Universal Wrestling Federation.’ One of the kid’s eyes lit up in recognition and amazement, and then he frowned. ‘He can’t help us… he has everything,’ the boy said looking back down to the floor. Digital the anger he was feeling for life and at the guts of this little boy who dared to say that he had everything. ‘He is here because he tried to do the same thing you did José,’ the woman’s voice was soothing. ‘Why would he want to kill himself? He is rich and famous,’ the boy said not understanding what Digital had gone through, not understanding that Digital had lost everything in that car accident Justine had. ‘She died… not only that, but my unborn son died in her womb…’ Digital told the little group in a very low voice. He held his tears inside of himself, he didn’t want to seem like a weak person in front of these little kids, but they all knew he was weak. He was there wasn’t he? That already told them how weak he had been, because they also understood that. Burton would never understand it, would never understand why he tried to kill himself, but these little kids did understand. The girl to his right was Juliana; she had tried to kill herself by swallowing over 20 pills of god knows what. Why did she do it? The reason we all think about it, because she felt there was nothing in this world for her, like Digital had felt that day he burrowed so deeply to find his veins. The day the blood had stained his already red hair and turned it into a crimson mess. Digital had to pick himself up, he needed help, and he was going to try and find it with these kids, because they were the ones that had made him famous, they were the ones that looked up to him, but how could they look up to a man like Digital? A man so torn between life and death. Was he ready to face the music? Was he ready to put himself to the test? He had a lot to learn from these kids, even if he didn’t understand it yet. He would eventually understand it; he would know that his time to shine was still up. He was in a tournament that would reward him for his success, and success he has had so far. He defeated Taro Freedom easily and he would now face a man that had been picked by some to win the whole tournament, a man that like Digital got eliminated in the first round and was then given a second chance… for him, it was a second chance for a second chance. He needed this to get in the Olympics at amateur wrestling. Nobody yet understood how winning a professional wrestling tournament would help him in his dream, but nevertheless, he was here and he was good. Could Digital up his game even more? Could he put aside his problems, put them at least on hold, and try to defeat his opponent, try to move up in this prestigious tournament? If he goes far enough, he might even get another chance at the Global Title, but in his current state, he could never defeat any of the big names. The Seymours, the Dr. Curiosities, the Wolfendens… and the Devilles of wrestling. They were all still in the tournament, and he would never be able to get to them if he wasn’t himself. ‘Juliana, can you explain to us what you were feeling at the moment you tried to end your life?’ She asked in the way she had asked hundreds of other kids, in that same tone she had used with everyone that she knew had a problem. The girl looked up and at the faces of all around her, she seemed scared and uncomfortable. ‘Um… I… life sucked. I hate high school, I hate my life, and I especially detest my father. He didn’t have the right to touch me…’ She said as the tears started flowing down her cheeks. ‘You can cry all you want to Juliana, doesn’t worry about it, we all understand how you feel,’ the woman told the girl. She then looked at a silent eighteen year-old called Lawrence. The boy was playing with his long black hair and also with his beard sometimes. He had his wrists bandaged the same as Digital. ‘Look at me… I am nothing, no one. No one gives a damn about me! Why should I even be here? Why? So that I can keep on suffering. You realize I’m 18 years old and I have never had someone that told me that she loved me. I haven’t had a girlfriend or even a first kiss! How about when you think you at least have someone that has been there with you through all your life and in the end it was nothing. Nothing! Is it fair? Of course not. I was picked to suffer! I was selected so that my life was a living hell, and I will die the same way I am right now, nothing to anyone…’ Digital was distraught after hearing what this boy had to say. He had had his love, he had a woman that was bearing his child, and he had felt her love. This poor kid hadn’t felt the love of someone; he hadn’t seen love in the eyes of his soul mate like Digital had when she was alive. He had felt warm every time she would look at him; he had loved every time she pressed against him, when her lips pressed against his. That was life, and this poor kid had no idea. Digital’s face had lost the lines of hatred for life and of frustration and had been replaced by pity. He looked at the kids face and saw the pain and hatred in his eyes, much the same way as he saw himself when he looked at the mirror. How could I have been so selfish? At least I found my love, I did lose it, but I can say that I had it. What so many people never find, what people search for their entire lives, I had it. Why don’t I stop being so selfish, why don’t I just keep going with my life and win this tournament in Justine’s name, and if I don’t win it, at least I will be happy that I tried my hardest, that I gave it my all. I am sure that is what she would have wanted. I guess I was wrong, I guess I still have a lot to learn from these kids. Could I be ready for the fight against Moraco though? Will I be at the top of my game, physically and emotionally? I guess that depends on my resolve. His eyes were lit after a long time of hollowness, his face completely changed. He had understood his calling now, his mission. He would go out there and win, not for himself, but for Justine, and most of all, for those who had been less fortunate than him. Like that boy, the kid that had never even been kissed. He needed to show the world that people had the power to move on. That you would not lose everything if you lost your loved one, because she would want you to succeed, to move on to better things. Burton, who was looking and listening in the far corner of the room saw the new expression in Digital’s face, saw his new resolve and stood up. The woman stopped talking as she saw Digital suddenly stand up, Digital burrowed in his pocket and found an elastic band, he took the red hair that had covered his face a few hours ago and tied it up into a ponytail. All the kids were now looking at Digital, waiting to see what he was going to do, expecting him to jump out a window, or even kill the woman talking, but he went toward Burton and shook his hand. He turned back and went to the kid, Lawrence. He shook his hand too. Held it firmly and looked him straight in the eye. ‘Thanks, thank you for helping me understand that the world does not revolve around me, that what happened to me could happen to anyone, and that what happened to me before the accident, doesn’t happen to everyone. Please, if there is something… anything I could do for you, give me a call,’ he said as he handed him a card taken out of his wallet. He turned away and started walking towards the door. ‘Good luck everyone,’ he said as the door closed behind him. He had just been saved by someone who he didn’t think would have been able to help him. Those kids had made him understand that he had been lucky. What he had with Justine didn’t happen to everyone. True love was rare, and he had found it. Like I said before, the Holy Grail of life. A soul mate. Now it was time for him to show that he is everything everyone says he is. It is time to show Sebastian Moraco, and the rest of the people watching the tournament that Digital Mortality is a force to be reckoned with. For her, he would do anything…
