Trigger Effect: Chapter One

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 When the cell phone on her nightstand began to ring, Eva VanLorne went from soundly sleeping to fully alert in an instant. The fact that is was 2:00 a.m. only heightened the instinctive response. After all, the only time the phone rang this late was when there was bad news or an emergency. Shuffling quickly to an upright position, she swipes unruly strands of blond from her eyes. Flipping the phone open she simply says," Erik?" It wasn't Erik's voice she heard. "Eva? It's Delilah. I am outside your door. I didn't want to knock and scare you." Immediately Eva could feel a sense of dread crawling over her body. Why does Delilah have her twin's phone? " What's wrong Delilah? Where is Erik?" " Come open the door Eva. We need to talk." Eva's heart began to pound as she flipped the phone shut and scrambled out of her bed. The sense of urgency she detected in her brother's fiancé's voice had her moving quicker than normal. Bare feet stumbled across her floors as she hurried through the darkness and unlocked the door throwing it open. She had a million questions ready to be hurled at her soon to be sister in law but one look at Delilah's face and her heart fell through the floor. " Oh god..is he.." Delilah said nothing. She merely came forward and wrapped her arms around Eva's neck hugging her with a pent up emotion. Eva instinctively returned the embrace staring blankly at the far wall as she fought for the will not to break down. Finally Delilah pulls away wiping away a tear with shuttering breath. " I'm sorry Eva. I should have been more honest with him. I should've stayed with him. This is my fault." The woman was stammering and it was quickly getting on Eva's last raw nerve. " Out with it woman! What happened!?" The silence of death seemed to linger on the air as Delilah merely stood there shaking her head ever so slightly. It was obvious she was looking for the right words. " Erik is…." Eva grabs her arms and gives her a quick shake. " Erik is what!" " He is missing Eva. We don't know if he is dead or not. " The floor seemed to sink from beneath Eva's feet at those words. Her entire world was threatening to cave in as she stood there staring at Delilah trying to soak it in. Then it dawned on her. "We?" Delilah stepped aside as two rather large older men came into view. They had been there the entire time and Eva had not noticed. Erik would have been disappointed in her for such an oversight. In their past it could have gotten her killed. Delilah gestured for them to enter the room suddenly all business. " Secure the room." Eva blinked staggering back to give them room as she looked at Delilah for answers. " I will tell you all you need to know as soon as I know the area is secure." Secure? Area? What was all this? Eva didn't fight the gentle yet firm grip on her arm as Delilah led her to the couch and urged her to sit down. As the men moved about and asked her questions concerning the house she either numbly nodded or shook her head. After a few moments all was marked as clear and Delilah took Eva's shaking hands within her own. " What I am about to tell you can go no further than this room. It is a matter of national security, and privileged information." Security? Privileged? " When I met Erik in Brazil, I was there on assignment. I work for the NSA. " As soon as those words hit Eva's ear her hands ripped free from Delilah's as if the touch suddenly burned. She stared at the woman with every accusation that flooded her angry and distraught mind. " I know it is hard to take Eva. Trust me, I never wanted to keep it from you..and especially not Erik, but this is my job and keeping it hidden is part of it." " So why now? Why are you telling me this now?" " Because we fear those who did this to Erik will be after you next." Her heart skipped a beat. Blue eyes widened with a shocked realization. After all the work she had done to leave her dangerous lifestyle of the past behind, it was coming back to haunt her and this woman was the cause. Her voice took on a grim tone filled with the anger she felt. " What happened to Erik?" " We believe he was taken by a group called Anarchist Unlimited. This group is led by a man by the name of Deitri Petrov. He is a former.." Eva was no longer listening. Delilah didn't need to tell her who Deitri Petrov was. She knew all too well and with that knowledge she knew that Delilah might not be the cause in all of this after. A catalyst, perhaps, but not the cause. " He is a former colonel in the Russian Army who went AWOL after he and a group of his men were left to die in Romania while on a mission to recover some nuclear technology stolen a few weeks earlier in route to Prague. Of those men, he was the only one to survive. Bitter at his government's easy dismissal of the lives of himself and his men he formed a terrorist group called Anarchist Unlimited in the year 2000. Since then they have been connected to terrorist acts in Russia, France, England, Bosnia, Germany and even Japan." Delilah's jaw simply snap shut as Eva gave her the information she was supposed to be giving Eva. With a stuttering breath Eva sprang off the couch and began to rug her arms in attempt to ward off the sudden chill overcoming her. " How did you know all that?" Eva turned and stared Delilah directly in the eye. " Because I was Deitri's lover for four months." This time is was Delilah's turn to have the ground beneath her shift suddenly. " You knew him?" " Erik and I both did,"Eva murmured as she walked to the far wall and leaned against it," We were hired by someone to get close to Deitri in hopes of reclaiming some art he had in his possession. It was set up for me to meet him in a club and work my way into his good graces and his bed… So you see this might not have anything to do with you." Delilah lurched up and cut her off. " Wait a second..you and Erik worked for the government?" Eva scowled and looked at Delilah over her shoulder. " No, we worked for a private party." " I see," Delilah paused for moment before continuing on," That might explain a few things. It definitely helps to piece together the puzzle a bit. Either way we have to get you someplace safe. I have a car waiting to take you to a safe house you have a few minutes to pa-" " I am not going anywhere." She couldn't run. Running always led to more running. She had built a new life and she would be god damned if she would let anyone take that away from her now. Her life was too public to be able to simply vanish without a trace and not have questions arise. She was a champion. She was a sex symbol. She had a tournament in a few days. No, she had to stay visible, besides if she was visible then she could draw them out. She hated the idea, yet, she knew there was only one way to skin this cat so to speak. " Eva, I can't protect you he-" " I don't want you to protect me Delilah. I want you to use me as bait." The two men give not so subtle coughs of disagreement as Delilah sighed. " Eva I can't do that. My superiors would never allow me to use a civilian.." " I don't give a good god damn what they approve or disapprove of. Too much red tape for my liking. If my brother is out there and he is still alive, then I will do whatever it takes to get him out and back home safely," she turned pinning the woman with a determined glare," You can either work with me..or I can work alone, either way I am not going anywhere. I have too much going on to just disappear and I know my rights. So..what's it going to be?" Delilah shook her head and threw her arms up in defeat. " Officially I can't let you do this…." Eva's chin titled upwards defiantly ready to argue this into the ground if need be. " However," Delilah looks at the two men, each nodding to the other," unofficially I want him back just as much as you do." Delilah stares at both the men in turn as she reaches into her pocket and pulls out a small black wallet tossing it to the nearest one. " I am going on vacation boys." They didn't argue. They knew better than to argue with the woman apparently. Simply nodding they took the wallet and took their leave of the premises. As the door shut behind them Delilah turned to Eva. " So where do we begin?" " We begin by making a few phone calls. If Deitri is after me, as you believe, there can only be two reasons. One, he wants Erik and I to do a job for him and isn't willing to take no for an answer. Or two….he is still angry over the job we managed to pull on him which nearly got him killed. Either way if I make the right calls to the right people, he will find out and come looking for me." " I don't know if that is wise. That will bring him here." " Precisely," Eva grins devilishly," that is exactly what I want him to do. The only thing we have to do is be ready." Delilah looked at Eva in a different light now. She actually looked as if she had found a respect she didn't hold for the diva earlier. Eva ignored her as she picked up the phone and began dialing numbers. The first few calls were failures, but around the fifth she got the bite she was looking for. After a few moments of rapid, heated conversation she hung the phone up and looked at her new partner in crime. " I hope you are ready for this. Erik and I don't do things the way others do. If you want to help me get him back. You are going to have to play this game differently than the way you were trained to do." " I can do that. So…what do we do now?" Eva shrugged nonchalant. " Now? Now we wa-" BANG! The window in the west side of the room suddenly shattered inward as gunfire sounded off in the distance. Both of the woman hit the deck, flattening themselves against the floor as bullets continued to fly overheard. Glass rained down as the barrage seemed to come from everywhere. Silently Eva was cursing herself, she should have known if he wanted her he wouldn't be far behind Delilah. Her ears were ringing as she crawled on her belly towards Delilah and barked the orders to follow her. Keeping their heads low, the pair shimmied to the kitchen. Here Eva took the risk of getting hit by getting to her feet and slamming the switch which would open the pantry and ultimately lead Delilah and her to a temporary safe haven. The sound of a sizzled whistle caught her ears forcing the woman to turn and watch as the assailants began to set fire to one of her precious homes. There was no time to be angry now, only the short opportunity to get to safety. Falling back to her stomach she leads Delilah into the pantry. Inside she flips open a trap door and urges the woman inside following closely behind. As her feet hit the floor below her head rose listening to the sound of tiny explosions as her house erupted into a blazing inferno above. Already smoke was beginning to seep downwards causing her to cough a bit as she and Delilah both looked for a way out. " Are there any windows down here!?" Eva shook her head. " No, I didn't want animals or moisture getting in here and ruining my collection!" " Well what now then?" " I don't know! I have a walk in cooler over there, but I dont know if there will be enough air for us to wait the fire out or if we can even get out!" The sound of haunted groaning came from above as the foundation pillars began to quake beneath the weight of the weakening structure. Both sets of eyes looked upward each thinking the same thing. Blue eyes met green ones. Eva looked around the cellar frantically. There had to be a way out she just needed to think. SNAP! CREEEEEEAAAAAAKKK! BOOM! Above the northwest side of the house was already collapsing. As it came crashing down the roof of the cellar in that area caved in a bit bringing in a new funnel of smoke. They had to get out. They had to find a way, but how? If they didn't they would die of smoke inhalation..or worse. They were trapped….like rats.
