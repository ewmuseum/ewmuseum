Who?

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Wait, who are we talking about again? Armaan. Who? Armaan! Who the hell’s that? Come on, dude, he’s that Indian guy. Used to wrestle in India, now he’s over here and stuff. … God’s sake man, the guy’s always on TV. Let me think, what’s he done recently? Oh, oh, he’s the last ever EWA champion. Ever! Uh huh. I don’t actually know what the EWA is, mate. What?! Where you been the last few years? EWA. European Wrestling Alliance. Used to be development fed for the WWA. You’ve heard of the WWA, right? Yeah… Yeah. It was a development fed for that. So, he won a title that was for rookies. Nothing special. Dude, dude, you’re missing your point. They did a whole reunion and stuff, and he won. This whole rumble-type thing, and he came out the winner. Bu- And, before that rumble match, he had a TLC match. Won that flippin’ thing too! Pretty impressive. Damn right! So, is that it? Nothing else? Of course not. He’s always on WWA TV nowadays. He went through this whole gauntlet to prove he was worthy of the Television title. Did he win the belt? Well, n- See, you keep missing the point! So, why were we talking about him? Because he’s in that GTT5. The what? GTT5! Golden Turnbuckle Tournament! Never heard of it. Yeah, well it’s some tournament where all the best wrestlers compete. And he’s in it. Oh…okay. Don’t you wanna know what the tournament is about and stuff? Not really. Well, Armaan’s gonna win it. Okay. And he’ll be the best wrestler in the world. Okay. Umm…will you say anything to suggest you’re remotely interested? Is that guy Craig Miles in it?
