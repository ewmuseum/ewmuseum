TTD - Illuminati -v- Lunchroom Patrol

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open in the Lunchroom Patrol locker room. The Latch Key Kid is sitting in front of a table covered with videotapes and notepads. In the middle of the table sits a television set with a VCR next to it. The screen shows nothing but static snow, and LKK is feverishly scratching his head. He picks up a pencil and chews on the eraser end of it, then makes a few notations on a sheet of paper. He picks up a tape and checks out the sticker on it, then inserts it in the VCR. He hits play, and the snow fades away. We see Frank, the Lunchroom Patrol’s would-be philosophical advisor and everybody’s favorite coffeehouse owner, appear on the screen. I remember the first time Matt walked into my coffeehouse. He looked like a deer lost in headlights at first. I happened to glance up from the counter as he walked in, and I mused to myself about how out of place he looked. There was something different about him, you know? I couldn’t figure out, but it was there. And then he opened his mouth, and that southern accent came spilling out. I’ve always said that I can pick them out, you know, from growing up there myself, and I pinpointed his as being from Florida. Of course I was right. But that’s neither here nor there. Listen, I know what you’re doing, Brad, and I appreciate it. I’m sure that in time, Matt will as well. So let’s go. You want my thoughts on Matt’s current situation. This, uhm, how do they say, crisis of conscience. We all go through it, Brad, it’s nothing to be worried about. But then again, I guess you’re no stranger to that yourself, so it’s not you we need to worry about. So then, how to approach Matt with how you feel? Pretty much in this exact way. Lectures won’t work, you’ve already tried that, we all have. A cup of coffee used to pave the way for a heart-to-heart. Put a few lattes on the table, and that’s it. Remember how it used to be, back in the day? I miss that at times. Don’t you, Brad? Okay, okay, I’m sorry. Well, the way to do this is to make Matt realize that there really are people out there who care about him, whether he wants to believe it or not. There’s a good person inside, and there’s a good person outside. The trick is to reconcile Matt and Doughboy once again. Now, I’m sure you realize that things will most likely never be the same again, but that’s okay. That just gives you more to work with. There’s a lot out there, Brad, and Matt needs to experience it. It’s time for him to admit to himself that he wants to find what’s out there. Matt, listen to me. Pretend you’re sitting here, at the usual table, with a cup of latte in front of you. Listen to your friend Frank. You are a good person, and you do great things for people. The world is better for having you in it, Matt Dixon. Don’t be afraid of Doughboy and what he represents, and don’t be afraid of admitting that you don’t want that anymore. Nobody can tell you how to live your life, Matt, but they sure can help you get there. That Frank…I knew he’d pull through for me…for Matt. He always knows what to do at times like this. He’s right, though. The only way to get Doughboy back is to make Matt understand the importance Doughboy plays in everybody’s life. Sure, it may just be a character, but it’s a character that makes people feel good about themselves. And that’s something he can be proud of. Hmmm…what next…ah, yes, here we go. Nicole. Latch sticks another tape in the VCR, and Nicole appears on the screen. You know, we met in Florida so many years ago. It’s been so long, that I can’t even recall exactly how we met. But there we were, in Florida, and our paths crossed. From then on, they were forever entwined. I always had a thing for Matt, but he always seemed to shy away from getting too serious. I never really figured out why, but that’s okay. The important thing is that Matt is where he is right now, at this point in time. He’s at the top of XUW, and aside from his success in the ring, he’s made a lot of people happy. He’s touched a lot of lives. The thing about Matt is, he’s too humble to admit that. He says he created Doughboy because of what the fans wanted? Doughboy is just a façade, a phony? Well, he’s wrong. Sure, he may have created Doughboy to give the fans what they wanted, but that only shows that he cares about them. He cared back then and he still does. He’s never turned his back on the fans, and he’s never turned his back on his friends. To me, that says a lot more than any title belts ever could. Doughboy may be a tough façade, but underneath it, there’s a real person. You don’t believe me? Just think about it this way. This inner clash he’s going through right now…why do you think he’s making such a big deal out of it? It’s because he cares that much. He wouldn’t be tearing himself up inside if he didn’t care at least somewhat about this. He cares about it because he’s a truly genuine person. And he cares about other people. If he truly did not care about the fans, he’d just cast Doughboy aside without a second thought. But you’ve seen firsthand the trouble he’s having with that. Heck, he couldn’t even properly terrorize the Fool. No, Matt’s not ready to ditch Doughboy. And he’ll never be ready. Doughboy will be back, and he’ll be a better person for all of this. You’ll see. I hope so, Nicole, I really do. For Matt’s sake, for your sake, for my sake, for the fans’ sake. All I’ve wanted since this whole thing started was for Matt to come to terms with this Doughboy thing. There’s no need for him to suffer this much over it. He sees his friends around him, he knows we’re here for him, but for some reason, it doesn’t seem to be enough. When I was going through my own down period, when I was battling my own demons, Matt didn’t turn his back on me. He stuck it out, he pulled me back in, and he’s probably the biggest reason I was able to snap out of that. He knows what it’s like to have a friend go through hell and back with you, because he’s been that friend. If he won’t listen to his friends, maybe he’ll listen to his opponents. This tape should work fine. Latch ejects Nicole’s tape, and inserts a third tape into the VCR. He hits play, and after a moment, everybody’s favorite asshole, Blake Wolfson, appears on the screen. You promised this wouldn’t take too long. You swore. Now what the hell is that you want? Oh, Doughboy. You know I have more important to things to concern myself with, don’t you? Like, oh, I don’t know…my upcoming Global Title match? A few minutes? Sure, I’ve got a few minutes to spend for that little turd. After all the beatings I’ve laid on him in the past, the least I can do is talk about this little inner spat that’s tormenting him. You want my honest thoughts? He’s being a pussy. There, I said it. A real man wouldn’t care what a few fickle fans think about him. A real wrestler wouldn’t care who he has to hurt to get to the top. Turn your back on a few people, burn a few bridges, step on a few toes? Welcome to my world, Matt. I remember the first match Hodgo and I ever had against you two. And it seems like your little shit of a partner hasn’t started to mature since then. Did that beating do nothing? Did you learn nothing from that lesson? What about the second one? The third one? Something nice, you say? My mom always used to say, if you don’t have anything nice to say, don’t say anything at all. Now bugger off, I really don’t give a shit about you or Doughboy. Blake stands up and starts to walk off, as LKK hits the stop button. Hmm. Maybe that one won’t be the best to use. But then again, he did say a few things that could be taken positively. Maybe he’s right about not caring what a few fans think about you. If Matt’s really that wrapped up in who he might hurt by discarding Doughboy, if he’s really that worried about casting off this mask that he feels was created by the fans, then what’s wrong with that? Isn’t the whole point of this for Matt to be what he really wants to be, what he knows he should be? Matt’s a person, but he’s a good person. It doesn’t matter what Doughboy does, or who Doughboy makes happy, or how many belts Doughboy wins. What matters is that Doughboy is a good person, a good friend, and a good tag partner. Besides, Doughboy was never so strong of a persona that he completely buried Matt inside. Matt’s always been there, and he’s always been present, Doughboy or no Doughboy. Nicole’s right. He is a genuine person, and that shows through in the amount of thought he’s put into this whole thing. In that same way, Matt’s good points shine through in Doughboy. That’s all Doughboy is, really. He’s just Matt’s good qualities manifesting themselves in a visible manner. Matt may think he created Doughboy because of what the fans wanted, but that’s wrong. He created Doughboy because he is Doughboy. Maybe not all of him is Doughboy, but the good points are, the most important points, and that’s what he has to realize. I guess that’s why I’m compiling this video tribute to him. If I can get him to see how people really view him, then that might go a long way towards getting him to come back around. Hopefully I’ll have this finished by next week. Now, if only I could find the tape of our victory over DarkSeid and the Shadow. I’m sure that would motivate – Just then, the door to the Lunchroom Patrol locker room opens. LKK spins around, and sees Doughboy walking in. He attempts to hide the videotapes and notepads, but only succeeds in creating a suspicious amount of rustling. Dough: What exactly is going on in here? LKK: Nothing. Nothing at all. Why…uhm…why do you think something’s going on? Dough: Because you’re fidgeting around like a little kid hopped up on too much chocolate and sugar. What’s in that bag you’ve got there? LKK: What bag? This bag? You mean, my backpack? There’s nothing in it. I mean, there’s something, but it’s not really that impor – Dough: Of course it is. It must be that important if you’re trying to hide it from me so carefully. Now are you going to tell me what’s in there? LKK: Nope, sorry. Dough: Have you been watching that Illuminati promo again? You’re acting an awful lot like they were at the end, there. Are you sure you’re not up to anything. LKK: No, man…nothing at all. Just, chill. Dough: Right. Look, it really doesn’t matter to me. Whatever you were doing in here before I came in, it doesn’t matter now and it will never matter. But you do remember that we have a match coming up against Illuminati, right? This is no time to slack off and start engaging in leisure activities. LKK: I realize that, and trust me, nothing like that going on here. But I wouldn’t go so far as to say that this will never matter, because it will. You’ll see, in good time. Never say never, Dough. Dough: Matt. LKK: Right, silly me. How could I have forgotten. Don’t worry, Matt. My head will be in this thing. Dough: Good. It better be. Dough heads to his locker and drops his duffel bag in it. LKK looks back at the pile of videotapes on the table, and wipes the sweat from his forehead and takes a sigh of relief.
