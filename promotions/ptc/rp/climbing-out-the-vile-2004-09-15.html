Climbing out the Vile

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 You have no options— A dream you awake from and you look down at your palms--damp and disgusting while sweat begins dripping from your brow. How the hell could you let that dream slip through your grasp? How could you stand by and do nothing about it? Why do you have no pride and thus go back to your old ways? I had objectives. I had goals to achieve my objectives and yet it would seem I did not define them well enough. One little shove from some fools had an atomic bomb effect on myself. Pathetic. I was going to leave everything behind me while I entered an obviously flawed yet famous tournament. I can look back and wince as tights are pulled and referees are paid under the table to make the ‘correct’ call. Wrestle God was a disappointment not only for me but also for everyone involved. That was going to be my entrance into the main stream and instead I was left with a sour taste in my mouth. Screwed and tossed towards the wolves which gladly took me back down to where I slum. A disrespected nobody! A dreamless schmuck cheered as the Wolf King as I returned to Mexico. They had no idea that I planned to never come back or that I was humiliated at the highest level of this business. I was enthralled with their devotion but I was still stuck in a lose-lose situation. I was still going to be unhappy. All my return meant was that I had given up. I had tossed my dreams aside along with my son and flew back to Mexico where I would once again use my wrestling as an alibi to export drugs from Mexico into America. What a wonderful role model I am. No options? I said I didn’t when I returned but the truth is I could have done whatever I wanted. I was free from the numerous cartels and yet I walked with my shoulders slumped right back into their gun wielding arms. “Ah back again.” Yup. “I knew you couldn’t leave the familia.” Well… “There is no hope for you as an American wrestler.” yea. “Do not be so sad.” Sigh—sigh—sigh—snort! What a sewer I reside in. I had always said I would never do the drugs I trafficked yet there I was taking lines like I was a member of the Dallas Cowboys. “No amontone el coque” “Get your own coke if you think I am hoarding it, Jose,” I said like a fiending bastard. That was a typical day for me. I was a shell of a man and I did nothing to solve my problems. I disrespected myself daily. Ruining my body and making excuses like a child had become something you could dot down in a calendar. Often times in life people treat you how they perceive you. If someone sees you as a confident decisive person they will have to respect you. If you walk with your head down and low mumbles it is easy to see that you can be pushed around for the others amusement. In this business though it isn’t about amusement it is about the bottom line, the cash, and the big yacht filled with bikini…. nix that nude girls. Though I am sure Juan laughed when I came back and he decided it was fitting to cut my pay by a third. Oh what a great welcome back. ~~~^~~~ “Gonzalo, it is great to have you back,” Juan said with his usual coy smile. His black hair with mousse making him look like an extra from West Side Story stayed in the same place as usual. His doctor whitened teeth blinding me like a bad reminder to everything I had come back to. I sighed and shrugged at his coy comment, “not great to be back,” I said. “Drugs without your plane is like the great Phoenix resurrecting from urine, Gonzalo,” Juan said with a laugh following his dirty ignorant joke. “Do you even care that my dreams have been shot?” I asked knowing the answer but I could no longer hold back my anger. Juan looked down at a shipment of coke and paused. He took his wallet out and tossed all his cash on the table in front of him. He looked up at me and began laughing. “You bring this and you bring me back this,” Juan said as he pointed from the coke to the money. “I do not care if you are happy. You are not worthy of my respect and you never will. You are a bitch that I order to do my bidding so shut up or take your ass to a trailer park because that’s the only place you could afford,” he said amused. I laughed uneasily and my shoulders slumped, “it is probably not a smart business move to insult the person who delivers your ILLEGAL paraphernalia,” I said some how finding an ounce of pride. Juan laughed, “is that a threat, you has-been?” he asked. I shook my head, “no. It is the truth, boss,” I said. He nodded, “I am aware but we both know that you have the back bone of an amoeba,” he replied. I sighed and pushed my hair from my eyes. It was hot outside Juan’s extravagant house, as he did not trust his help coming into his house. It didn’t help that he was just standing outside with a drink in his hand in only a pair of shorts while I was wearing an expensive suit that all people have to wear when they visit his house. He was power hungry and making us sweat was just a bonus but especially me since I had come back after I had quit. “A little respect is all I ask for as an employee, Juan,” I said softly. Juan looked at me and took his time replying as he enjoyed watched the sweat begin to show through my suit. “And you will get very little, Gonzalo. I don’t need you and I could just kill you for even showing your face again. The little respect you are getting is that I am allowing you to return. So accept that and cool yourself off—you look to be sweating,” he finished with a laugh. I waved my hands towards my face hoping that the little air generated by it would cool it off. Of course it did very little and I looked back at Juan. “You are very observant,” I replied. Juan waved my sarcasm off and then began looking serious. “I may as well throw you back into the business quickly,” he said. “I hope you have you flying legs ready because tomorrow you are heading to New York with a shipment,” he said. I nodded slowly, “I suppose I have to be.” “Exactly.” ------- Would you like some coffee as well? Ha. I was nothing but a talking monkey to Juan that did his bidding—and I did. I delivered what needed to be delivered getting deeper and deeper into a dark hole that had me passing out on my floor toppling on top of old pizza boxes and dirty clothes. For two months since I came back in shame I had been their little errand boy. I was laughed at and spit towards by the greedy bastards who ran the drug ring. I was nothing but a spineless fool to them and it irked me more and more each day. I did nothing to stop their disrespecting glances and even if I did muster the balls those balls would probably no longer be attached. It was monotonous depression. It was the same thing every day. Taking orders and saying ‘yes sir,’ like I had no free will. Every day ended with my nose in a pile of Colombian gold with a wrinkled five-dollar bill shaking in my hand as it sticks up my reddened nose. If it wasn’t someone else taking me down a notch it was myself. I was just a pile of nothing. I took up space and slept while I did not work. I had forgotten my goals in life and my son who I had promised I was going to get back. I wasn’t worthy of having a son and I wasn’t worthy of breathing at this point. I did nothing for society but help the cesspool of the world make money and stay out of jail while they insulted me and cut my pay. “Aye, Gonzalo when is your next match, hombre?’ Jose would ask me EVERY DAY. I would just wave him off and laugh half-heartedly, “When Juan gives me some time off,” I would say EVERY DAY. “You can’t let those bastardos lead your life for you hombre,” Jose said to me one drug induced night. It had been a long week with me going back and forth from Mexico to New York four times. I had used my agents at several small airports to go around with relative ease. Nobody bothered me and if they tried my military ID usually got them off my back. My dad was good for something still I would think disrespecting a man who had done everything he could for me. That evening I had gotten back to Mexico City and went straight to the bar where Jose and I had several drinks, following them up with several lines of coke. It was another wasted night in another wasted week in another wasted month. “This coming from a man who drives the limo’s,” I said sarcastically yet very quickly as the coke had quickened my step. Cocaine is known for increasing your dopamine in your brain. If you have an education of a teenager you will not understand what that means. To boil it down coke gives a happy feeling by increasing your levels of dopamine. This is a scientific fact but when it comes to me it does nothing but make me happy for five minutes. The rest of the time I just pace thinking of ways out of this mess. Jose nodded, “chico, I ain’t got any other options. You at least have something you can do,” he said. I shrugged and then shook my head, “I have nothing,” I said. “You are the Wolf King!” Jose yelled towards me angrily. I rubbed my nose a few times and scoffed at Jose’s words. “That means I lose pointless matches in crappy rings in front of only five hundred drunken Mexicans,” I said. Jose sighed and took a gulp of his bottle of tequila. “You can still do it, hombre.” “What are you trying to say, Jose?” I said annoyed at his constant pushing’s. “You can stop this garbage and do what you WANT to do, hombre. You can wrestle and leave for good,” he said enthusiastically. I sighed, “I can also be murdered by slow torture by those we work for if I do what I WANT,: I said depressingly. “You cant let what they say about you affect who you think you are. I need you to do this because then you can help me finally escape. You are my anchor and I want to be hauled up. I need you and you need to leave or we will both be dead anyway. This business is as volatile as the stock market and lets just say a big decrease in tech stocks is like a tech being put towards our heads,” Jose said emotionally putting every fiber of his being into his speech. I just stood there staring at him with my mouth opened in complete surprise from his emotional words. “I—I am washed up, Jose,” I said softly. Jose shook his head angrily, “shut up you half-gringo dummy!” he yelled. “WELL WHAT THE HELL DO YOU WANT ME TO DO?” I yelled back at him so loud I almost strained my vocal cords. “I want you to stand the fuck up and be a man. Stop disrespecting everything you stand for by becoming a waste of space. Go back to the states and become that wrestler you always promised you would become,” Jose said. I laughed uncontrollably and just shook my head, “that dream was shot down by ineptness of certain referees against that overrated, marble mouthed fool Noah Hanson,” I said angrily still bitter about that certain match. Jose nodded, “if you can get over that there is one last chance,” he said. I rolled my eyes, “how so?” I responded. “The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament!” He exclaimed happily. “Another place to be ignored and disrespected because I do not have a known name? I am not sure that is a great last chance,” I said disinterested. “It is YOUR last CHANCE,” Jose said angrily. I nodded and sighed, “Fuck it,” I said. I wiped my face as I thought and I smiled, “may as well die doing something worthwhile huh?” I finished. ------- To make a long story short, I am on the run. I left the business to again try to make something of this wrestling career. I am hiding out in a basement of some drab run down house where my companions exist of rats and cockroaches. It is worth it though because I did not enter the GTT to allow rats and cockroaches let me down. If you can assume one thing assume that all those before me our enemies to me. I have been disrespected in this career from when I first started to several months ago when I finally came to the states. There is an evolutionary scale here. They say if it does occur it takes millions of generations for a trait to stick. Well I have decided to skip the theory and come at everyone the way they have always come at me: with disrespect. No longer will I allow people to scan my name and continue going without a second though. No longer will I allow them to laugh and say I have no chance at beating some awful performer like Kash. I swear there is a ‘Kash’ in every federation and I wrestled mainly in Mexico. To be disrespected by someone like that is like having your ball hairs plucked by Charles Manson. The disrespect is going to flip flop like election year propaganda and I will toss you non believers into a vat of acid. My opponent seems to think I am no threat because his TV doesn’t get Mexican wrestling shows. He thinks his clichés and getting back up on the horse crap is going to get him an easy win because he is facing some guy named Gonzalo Rey. That stuff is going to stop. I am sick of being a second thought. I am sick of being an easy win and I am sick of facing morons who do not have an intelligent thought in their cerebellum. I DISRESPECT Kash, and this is the way it is going to be now. I am facing a man who was a mainstay and supposed great from one of the least talented fed I have ever seen. This place is so boring and pointless that people like Kash some how become good wrestlers. I could toss one of these huge New York rats into the ring and it would at least defeat Dave Harley and he is better then Kash. If you want to try and bury me by ignoring words and disrespect just remember I am use to it but I certainly do not accept it. I have been through too much nonsense to let this continue hence you all have made me change. I am disrespected daily but now all of you will be disrespected. I do not care if you have a fan base and get verbal blow jobs daily like Angelo Deville or if you continually choke in tournaments though everyone says you are going to do well like Tessa Windsor. It is that simple you all can bite me. I have to prove a lot and I have a lot to lose right now but what I can gain is a lot more important to me. I have climbed out from the vile and I am clean once again with only success as an addiction. I am coming for more then a tournament…I am coming for my son and I am coming to gain that R-E-S-P-E-C-T.
