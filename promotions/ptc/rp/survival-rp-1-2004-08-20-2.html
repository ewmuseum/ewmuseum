Date: 2004-08-20 ::Six O'clock After Noon:: "Scott, what's up?" "Dude, you coming to our room or what? We've got booze, women, grub, booze, music, women and Ian. Say, bud, did I mention the booze and women?" "Oh yeah? I've got my old manager in here. She's lounging. But, f*ck it, I'll be down in a few." "Wicked, dude. Jett, Bill and I will be waiting. Man! Ian's making an ASS of himself!" "Word. I'll be down." The problem with people is that they're weak. The problem with people is that they believe they're so much more than what they really are. Egotists. Take Toronto for example. The city I've lived in for as far back as I can remember. Poster-city for the Canadian commercial caravan. In some cases, economy starts and ends in Toronto for this country. It's great. Everyone in Toronto thinks they're hot. Everyone thinks that they're "the sh*t". Cairo of Canada. Enter: the plagues. Sars. Mad cow. I'm expecting Lake Ontario to turn red in a week. I'm expecting the locusts and the death of the first-borns. "The sh*t" hit the fan. The problem with people is that their weaknesses are the exact thing that provokes their downfalls. The problem with people is that they don't even notice their downfalls. I grab a pack of cigarettes from my table. Rei's sitting down eating a pita and falafel. Obviously, she didn't take my suggestion into consideration. Or if she did, she didn't consider it for long. I grab my keycard. "Where are you going?" And I walk out the door. ::Seven O'clock After Noon:: "Chivo... I love you." "Easy, dude. How many of those Zimas have you had?" Ian, or Void. A Super Talented Dude and one of my friends in the UPWA locker room. He counts his fingers, and then lifts up seven of them. "Three!" He's not the roundest apple. "Okay, dude. Sit down before you hurt yourself." "Check me out. These Canadian chicks are eeeeaaaasy." Ian turns around only to get in Jimmy's face. Jimmy smacks him across his head. "B*tch, why don't you drink some man drinks?" "Zim-zim Zima!" "Pansy. And stop making out with my wine bottle! That's going to help me get my groove on. I don't want your f*cking slobber all over it. Damn, Ian!" Jimmy give me Coors. "I was surprised they sold this stuff in Canada. And here I was thinking we were going to drink p*ss all night." "Yeah, you'd be surprised. Between you guys, I had to get used to this American beer. I must say, it's something." "Ah, you're a natural!" The thing I hate the most is that everyone thinks I care. What I really hate is that people think I'll be just like them. That I'll enjoy the same things, speak the same way, wear the same clothes, generally act alike. My style is unique because I make sure to be unique. It's one of those things about me. I can't be like anyone, or I'll lose it. The way I see it is that life is all about living. And you can't live through anyone else. That's why I live it my way. Avirez thinks I'm poo. Get new material. Well sh*t. He's got me completely read. At least he knew one thing about me - I am smarter than The Corporation. If I were to be like Avirez, I'd be thinking about my opponents constantly. I'd be taking them apart. Spelling mistake by grammar error. I'd be watching for openings to make fun of and swear at them. Sorry, I'm like no other. And the only three words that come to mind when I think of Avirez are: Sick my Duck. ::Eleven O'beers After Shots:: Bill winked at us. Scott and Jimmy looked at me, and we all knew something was up. Ian was passed out against the toilet in the washroom. Hopefully he'll learn his lesson. Never ask a girl to watch her take a leak. So Bill walks up to one of the horizontally challenged girls in the room, and asks her to dance. This can't be good. The two walked to the "dance floor" which is pretty much the living room minus the table, which they folded up and put in the kitchen. He lined her up in front of a mirror, and looked at her in the face. He grabbed her chin, and turned her to look at the mirror. "You see that?" he said, referring to the two of them holding each other. "Remember it." Bill let go, and walked away. Scott, Jett and I giggled, and Bill gave us a funny face. The chick wasn't too pleased. Obviously. You can't exactly have a preconceived notion of someone. Anyone. It's impossible. So, I figure, don't preconceive anything. Stop taking sh*t at face value, and get into the dirt. I can be like Limp and guess that no one else is as good as I am. I can state that I'm the best without understanding my competition. I can do anything these guys can do better. Right, Rey? I've won just as many tournaments as you have. And still they don't know my name. I don't care. I already stated why I'm here. To be UPWA World Champion. Let's dance. ::Too O'clark Altar Shorts:: Ian comes out of a room where he's been making out with the same chick Bill was "dancing" with. "Duuude, check Ian out." Jimmy laughed. The smile on his face was worthy of a picture. Bill coughed some of his food out, "Aw man. Not with her!" "C'mon guys." I had to make some sense out of this. "It's Ian. He'd f*ck a pile of rocks if he knew there was a snake inside." Scott smiled, and before taking another shot, said, "He wouldn't care if the snake was even there." ::Passed Out:: It's just like sex and videogames all over again. Stop being so ignorant. Stop being so stupid. I mean, this is Chivo you're dealing with at Survival. Sick my duck.
