Garbage Bag Johnny WILL win GTT6: Part One

19 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-19 Back before I was old enough to know any better, my dad took me outside one day to show me how to fight. He was small for a man, but at the time, I feared him more than any man alive. He held his palms out and after I threw the most powerful haymaker my stringy right arm could muster, he grabbed me by the wrist and reformed my fist so that the punch would have more knuckle in it, and I wouldn’t break my thumb. It was one of those nights again, and his lessons weren’t exactly coming in handy. I had kind of gotten myself into this one, though. I was in a bit of a rut at the time. I was out of work, and I was running out of money. Still, I had my optimism. As long as I kept a positive outlook on things, everything would be fine. So I kept pretending everything was alright, and after about a week of sleeping all day and spending nights trying to recount the last human interaction I had, I figured it was about time to get out of the apartment. I was feeling good that night, although that could have easily been the pills. I was feeling so good that while completely cognizant of my malnourished wallet, I was set on going to one of the city’s many overpriced bars. I settled on one of those fake Irish numbers; don’t much remember the name. All I know is that the fluorescent sign had clovers to bookend the pub name. Inside, it was the same as any other bar I’d seen. The blonde bartender was a viral vacuum of attention and tips from the line of drunkards traced across the bar stools. The tables were mostly full as well, and the ones that weren’t were accounted for by the men throwing darts or shooting pool, or that one son of a bitch avidly slamming his fists at the Golden Tee arcade. As an astute economist, I surveyed the scene for cost-effective methods of obtaining beverages. Women were the most charitable when it came to ignorant donation to the spendthrift drinker. Most of them were quickly drunk and prone to misplacing their drinks. The others were quite content with pretending to be just as drunk and purposely placed drinks away in unprotected ranges. The more revealing the outfits, the easier it was to swipe a misplaced drink. The men with them were almost as charitable. They paid more attention to the women than the drinks and played constant mathematical games in order to calculate how drunk the women would have to be to go home with them as well as how sober they would have to stay to take advantage of these calculations. The problem with the men was that if they saw you grabbing their stray drinks, they’d try to act offended about it. The payoff, however, made it worth the risk as their drinks were much stronger. The risk, however, is a tricky thing to deal with when the odds turn against you. I had started off successfully enough, scavenging drinks I knew would go unfinished, and once I had liquored up enough gall to start picking off fuller glasses and stronger spirits, my judgment was being guided more by bravado than rationale. "You must have some pretty big balls to drink something that ain’t yours." My fingers slowly unwrapped from around the glass of straight whiskey as I placed it back on the table in front of me. I turned around and found myself face to face with a pair of impressively chiseled pectorals that flexed out from behind a shirt two sizes too small. When it hit me that I technically wasn’t face to face with anything, I swallowed hard; so hard that I didn’t even taste the traces of whiskey still clinging to my taste buds. I failed to choke out an appropriate apology in time. I was too busy thinking about how this freaking mutant was simultaneously twice my size and meticulously groomed. Part of me was stuck marveling at the evenness of his goatee, consistently two millimeters thick along the whole perimeter of his mouth. "Why don’t we go somewhere without so many witnesses? Then we’ll see who has the bigger pair, huh?" I was a bit startled at first, not quite sure of what was being implied here. That inkling of insecurity was escalated into an alarming blare of sirens as I felt his thick fingers knuckle around my crotch. He lifted me off the ground with one hand and carried me back into the restrooms. He slammed me into the side of one of the stalls, releasing his Herculean grip. I slid down to my feet, nearly hyperventilating. He pressed me against the outer wall of the stall with one arm; the other was reared back with a balled fist at the end. "Now look here, you piece of shit! I have the biggest balls in this place, and nobody but nobody pulls any funny stuff with me and gets away with it." As my eyes followed the bulging veins from the end of his sleeve all the way to his premonitory fist, I realized that I had no time to sit and listen to him lecture and taunt me. I had to act fast, or I was going to get the holy hell beat out of me in front of a restroom full of pissing drunkards. It wouldn’t have been the first time. As I swiftly aimed a kick right between my assailant’s legs, I felt my foot sink into a wide gulch between his testicles. He certainly was a man of his word. It felt like my foot was wedged in between a pair of watermelons carved from their rinds. Seemingly unfazed, he laughed- just stood there laughing like I’d just barely tickled his scrotum with a feather or something. I was out of ideas. "You must be really hurting for a hurting, huh?" His clenched fist reared back, and I saw his fist approaching in slow-motion. This was mainly because he was attempting punch me very slowly as the girth of his massive arms made movement a chore. And before he could slug me with his astronomically sluggish hook, the door of the stall behind me swung open, and out from the stall emerged my savior, holding a beaconing, wooden plunger. The wooden handle snapped in two upon connecting with the bulbous head of the man with large testicles, and immediately, I saw the consciousness drain from his eyes right before he fell into a heap in front of me. "Jesus Christ! Can’t a man pinch a loaf in peace anymore?" The dissimilitude was amazing. He stood there wielding the remaining half of the plunger of modest stature and without a protruding muscle on his whole person. His back was draped beneath a fluttering cape and his face was hidden behind a luchador’s mask that looked inflated as if his head had exploded and been replaced by a masked balloon – as unfathomably ridiculous as that situation might be. He turned to exit the door without washing his hands, but as he stood in the doorway, something clicked inside that balloon of his, and he turned towards me. His neck tilted back and forth as he studied my face, examining it in uncomfortable detail while decisions turned behind his mask’s eyeholes. "You," he pointed. "Come with me." I was a bit uneasy as I followed the masked man out of the bathroom, but I figured I owed it to him, and besides, I really had no motive to stay in the bathroom anyway. He stopped at the bar where there were two open seats. "What you drinking, buddy?" "Whatever I can get my hands on." The masked man laughed before ordering two mugs of whatever was on tap. While the bartender filled the order, he turned his attention back to my face. He stared at it with the awe of an explorer who had just discovered the grail. "Something in my beard?" I asked, not quite knowing what to make of the situation. "No," he responded. "It’s your face. You’ve got the exact look I’m going for." I had no idea what he meant by that. He was locked in such a gaze that he didn’t even notice the bartender set down our drinks. I could hear the salubrious desire bubbling from within the mask. He eyed me like an island castaway eyes an imaginary steak. "What would you say if I told you I had a job for you? There’s a lot of money in it." "What are you- some kind of modeling agent?" "You could say I’m an agent of sorts, yes." I had never tried my hand at modeling before, but I considered myself to be a handsome man; more handsome than most, anyway. Still, the offer was tempting, and I had turned from being uneasily harried by implications of homosexual attraction to being flattered by the spontaneous opportunity to showcase my natural beauty. I tried to play it coy. "Can I have a night to mull it over?" "By all means," he assured me. "Sleep on it, and if you decide you want to know more, I’ll be here waiting tomorrow night." I finished my drink, now regaining my buzz after being unwillingly sobered out of it by the earlier confrontation, and generally thanked the masked man for intervening in the bathroom, buying me a beer, or offering me a job based on my aesthetic superiority. He stopped me before I left. "Hold up for a second. I didn’t get your name." "It’s Johnny," I bluntly stated. "I’m Adam Dick. It’s good to meet you." *** I woke up early on my own accord, desperately needing to leak out the old bladder. It looked halfway decent outside, but the chilled air in my apartment hadn’t quite caught up to the sunbathed lukewarm of the October temperature outside. Still with sleep encrusted in the corners of my eyes, I reached, by habit, for my pipe and lighter. I set my mind right, and then I got up to go to the bathroom. It was an uneventful trip, but I found some importance in it when I couldn’t go back to sleep. I figured some medicinal aid would help me. There was no point in being up at this hour. I reached for the sandwich bag on the nightstand, right next to my pipe and lighter, but lo and behold, the sack was empty. "Shit," I seethed through my morning breath. I tossed the empty bag on the coat of dirty clothes and miscellaneous junk that formed a protective dermis over the hardwood floors and picked out a light jacket, a pack of cigarettes, and my lighter. Flipping a cigarette in my mouth that was lit before I got out the front door, I walked down the tree lined side streets I was getting close. I could smell the mixture of hemp and body odor in the air, and I was spotting more and more houses as the block became more residential deeper in. There it was. I could tell the house used to be blue at one point, but now it was worn a bit gray. It had a large porch that was half walled in, like some sort of hippie fortress. Two of the strung out douches were asleep in a hammock that swung from hooks on the porch. A few more of them were passed out in various places along the wood planks of the porches foundation. Right in front of me, one of the misguided fools was covered in paint, lying in the knee high grass. I stepped over the hemp shoes-wearing son of a bitch; I almost stepped on him because the grass had grown so wily. I should have stepped on him. Instead, I stepped up very abrasively onto the wooden porch. The hippies on the hammock woke up, craning their necks, but not moving. They were sunken into their hammock, and were at the will of its sea-like swing. "What’s going on, man?" he greeted me, not the least bit offended that I woke him up. That hippie bastard! He greeted me like we were old pals. That non-confrontational, pacifist son of a bitch! "Nova here?" I asked, trying to be as brief as humanly possible. The female in the hammock pointed towards the door. The screen door was unlocked and the door behind it was wide open. I wiped my shoes on the welcome mat, afraid to take them off because of all the germs that hitched in on the bottoms of bare feet. I heard the bubbling of inhaling indulgence shake up the sound waves from the living room. Nova was sitting on the couch without pants. Cartoons glowed brightly on the television screen in front of the couch as he juggled a bong and a bag of cheese puffs. He was a strange character. I never took him for a hippie himself, but he paid the bills on the house with money from the bags he sold, and he let whatever free spirited waste of skin stay rent free. Some of them contributed to the bills, many of them simply in the form of their continued patronage to his entrepreneurship. I hit a rough spot once, and because of my loyalty as a customer, Nova was more than happy to offer me a spot, and I was more than happy to turn it down. I couldn’t stand to be around all of those hippies. I much preferred to pass the time in lobbies and all night diners until I was back on my feet. Now, Nova was laughing at something in the cartoon. He still hadn’t noticed me there. "Nova?" His skeleton seemed to jump in his skin. He turned towards me while trying to catch his breath. "Shit, Johnny. Don’t sneak up on me like that. Here. Have a seat." I sat on the couch next to Nova and stared forward at whatever cartoon he was watching. I didn’t recognize it, but it seemed colorful enough. He didn’t seem too vested in the plot anyway. As soon as I sat down, Nova popped right up and started walking out of the room. "I’ll go get some more shit real quick. It’s already cashed. You want a bag to take home with you?" "Yeah," I agreed frankly. Who was I to argue? "How much do you want?" "Just a quarter, man." I sat there on the couch while Nova ran back to his room. The urge to help myself to some cheese puffs was irresistible. I knew that Nova wouldn’t have minded. He’d have even offered them to me. All alone on someone else’s couch, though, there’s a certain unspoken breech that occurs when you take another man’s cheese puffs without asking. He would have never known if it wasn’t for the damning orange crumbs that stained my fingers, my clothes, and everything else I touched. When he came back, I could tell he knew just what I’d been up to. He didn’t say anything, but I could tell he knew. He had a sad look in his eyes. "This is some pretty good stuff," Nova said, back on the couch now as he packed a small nugget into the slider. "It’s usually one hundred, but I’ll drop ten off for you." I spoke with great reserve. "I’m a little short, man. Can you spot me on this one?" "Johnny, I spotted you on the last bag. You still owe me ninety on that." "I’ll have it soon," I defended. "I was at the bar last night, and this dude had a job offer for me. I’m meeting him tonight. I’m going to accept it, and then I’ll have your money." "Oh, yeah?" "Yeah. I just need you to front this bag until he pays me, and I’ll have your money right away." Nova seemed to be mulling it over as he drew in the tube of smoke. He passed me the glassware as he held his breath in and exhaled as soon as I flicked the wheel of the lighter. "I guess I can spare it," he conceded. "What kind of work will you be doing?" "Modeling." Nova coughed a little bit in his throat, but I was almost positive that it was just residue from the last hit. I proceeded. "Yeah, I ran into this agent at the bar, and he said I had the perfect look." Nova interjected quite rashly. "Are you sure you didn’t hallucinate this like the time you thought you saw Dracula at Starbucks?" "He was ordering a grande café au lait with skim." "Yeah, but didn’t you say this happened in the middle of the afternoon? In broad daylight?" "He was wearing Ray Bans." "Look, man. There’s no way that you could be a model." "Why not?" I was quite offended. "Look at you!" he apparently exclaimed, his gesticulations growing more severe as the whites of his eyes tinted redder and redder. "You’ve got long, stringy hair and a beard that hasn’t been trimmed in months. You look ridiculous!" Ignoring the harsh reality of his accusations, I held steadfast to my convictions as I took one last rip before awkwardly trying to stand. "You’ll see, man. You’ll see when my face is plastered over all the magazine racks, or when I’m in the commercials advertising an assortment of crèmes and lotions." "I’ll see, alright," Nova said sarcastically as he tossed me the bag on credit. "I better get out of here, then. I have to look sharp when I meet with Adam. I don’t want him to change his mind." "Who’s Adam?" asked Nova, although I could tell by the frequency and force at which he was swallowing that he might have had some inkling of a clue. "Adam Dick," I stated flatly. "That’s my agent." With his neurons firing slower than normal, I was out of the door before he could issue a word of warning. Apparently, some time back, a guy in a mask screwed Nova over in one of his dealings. The way I looked at it, though, it could have been any guy in any mask. Nova was probably just paranoid. The son of a bitch gets that way when he’s high. *** On the way to the bar, shortly before arriving, I passed by a beggar. He looked old, but all beggars looked old. You never saw any of them that looked young. I was wearing my best clothes, so he must have mistakenly taken me for someone with money. He stood up from a cement stoop, waving a Styrofoam cup in front of me. "Can you spare a dollar so I can get some coffee?" "Sorry," I mechanically lied. It’s important to have a politeness ingrained into your demeanor when dealing with the homeless. You never knew which one of these bums would be a stickler for manners. He turned as I passed him and started hanging onto my good sleeve. I was a bit annoyed with his dirty hands all over my good sleeve and all. I felt like slapping him. "Are you sure? Check your wallet. Sometimes miraculous things appear." What a hopeless lunatic! He had the worst begging pitch I had ever heard. This tactless rube deserved to be homeless. I dropped my fabricated pity. "Maybe a dollar miraculously appeared in your wallet." I felt a bit bad about my unchecked barb. He looked down and bit his bottom lip in shame. He knew he was a pretty unskilled beggar, and he was probably ashamed, but he had to keep at it. I tried to snake my way out of the altercation, hoping that he’d save his lines for the next passerby. "Look, I’ve got to get going. You’re messing up my sleeve." "Where are you going?" he asked. My rage swelled and settled like lava. It felt like lava, anyway, or what I would imagine lava would feel like. It didn’t concern him, regardless, but there was no harm in being vague if it would emancipate my sleeve from his clutches "I’m going to meet someone; a friend." "Ah," he breathed. "You must have a dollar if you’re rich enough to have friends." I didn’t know what he meant by that. I figured it was either illogical banter or redundant adage. Either way, I was merely concerned with the extrication of my damned sleeve. I forfeited rational conversation and looked him squarely in the eye. I could’ve slapped him- him and his sad bag eyes. "Let go of my sleeve." "But-" "Let go." It was no time to reserve my firmness. He let go of the sleeve, and I brushed off the spot where he had touched me and kept on walking. I could see Adam there, earlier even than promised. He was smoking something, and from the way he defiantly rocked back and forth, I knew what it was. The approaching, dissipating cloud confirmed it. He flicked the roach off the other way as he saw me coming, and then he extended his arm towards me for a handshake. "Johnny! You’re looking sharp. Shall we?" He released his sturdy handshake grip and led me by the shoulder away from the bar and down the sidewalk in the direction I was heading. I was confused and a bit apprehensive. We came to an alley, and rape whistles were going off in my head. As a beautiful man, I oftentimes had to be wary of glib strangers and their rectal ambitions. My fears were somewhat abated when we passed the first alley, though I knew we’d pass by more alleys. There were always alleys. "So when are we going to talk about my modeling career?" I was getting impatient. "Yeah, sure," Adam said, obviously not paying attention to my question. "We’ll get to that eventually. First, I’ve got to show you something." Dick walked on ahead, and I had to speed up to follow him. The bars and businesses and strip malls began to die out, slowly, giving way to free standing shops and random or abandoned convenience marts. The ground ahead began to decline behind a tall fence with wire spiraling across the top. There were train tracks on the other side, but they looked like they hadn’t been used for quite some time. They were brown and rusted and not sleek like the rails I was used to seeing. "Give me a boost," he said, and I intertwined my fingers so I could hoist him up by one foot. He carefully scaled the fence and landed on the other side, beckoning me to follow. "Hurry up, Johnny. We can’t let anyone see us trespassing." "How am I supposed to get over?" I asked with a shrug. I could have left right there, and he wouldn’t have caught me. I didn’t know what this wall all about. For a while, I thought I should have left, but I didn’t, and things worked out anyway. "Just climb the damn fence and pull yourself over!" He seemed agitated, and I was a bit agitated too, but I had already walked far enough. The cross-work of the fence formed diamond shaped holes that were just large enough for the toes of my shoes to fit up against. With some measure of difficulty I managed to climb up the fence. My body scratched and grated over the top and I fell ungracefully. I stood up and looked around at the emptiness of it all. "I guess this is kind of scenic. I could model something rough and rugged here, alright; maybe some men’s outerwear?" "Outerwear? What the hell are you talking about?" "I thought this was about modeling." "Modeling? That’s retarded. Who the hell ever said anything about modeling?" He could see I was confused and immediately changed his tone to restructure his explanation. "Look, just sit down here and watch for a second." He pointed out to the bottom of the decline on the other side of the tracks as we knelt in the grass. It was some kind of empty lot; save for two bums circling each other with balled fists as they threw wild punches and tried not to trip on their own feet. "You see those two bums fighting there?" "Yeah." "Yeah, well they fight like this most nights. They bet the change they get from begging on these fights. Whoever wins takes it all." "What a way to live," I said sarcastically. Dick picked up on my tone. "Are you kidding me? If a bum knows how to fight, he can live more comfortably than you or me. There’s a killing to be made in this. I’ve got a plan in the works. I’m just ironing out the kinks to see how much I can profit from this. You know how to fight?" "I can fight alright." "Good. These bums should be no problem for you. We could make some money if we plan this right. You want to make some money?" "Well, sure." I remembered the money I owed Nova. I didn’t want to fight a bunch of bums for it, though. "Adam, you ever fight a bum?" "Of course not! I don’t have the face for it. I need someone who can convincingly play a homeless man. You might have to beg for change for a while to build up your rep within the homeless community. I’ll take care of everything else. We’ll split everything down the middle. You cool with that?" I was somewhere between disappointment and optimism at the opportunity. I could’ve said no, but I didn’t. I had nothing else going on, and I was sick of doing nothing. It was about time I did something about it. "Yeah, I’m cool." "Great," Adam said, placing a hand on my shoulder. "This is going to take off. If we can keep these fights on the down low, there are people in certain social spheres who would pay big money to watch and wager on these fights. We’ll be sitting pretty in no time. How does that sound to you?" "It sounds good," I said, somewhat blankly. I was bored enough to take him up on almost anything, though, so long as I could put my faith in someone else’s underhanded schemes instead of my own. "It sounds real good." Down in the lot, one of the bums had knocked the other to the ground with a lucky fist. He was quick on top of his opponent and started biting the back of his head. The man on the bottom submitted, and the victor took the mound of beggarly earnings. He ran off, hooting and hollering. The loser sat on the ground, having grabbed his paper bag full of malt liquor. His face looked contorted when he drank it as the fight had soured his nectar.
