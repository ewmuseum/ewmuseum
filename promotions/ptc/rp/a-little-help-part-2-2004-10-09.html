A Little Help Part 2

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 It had been a bumpy ride for the man known as Digital Mortality. These past few months had been something that he wished he could forget, but he wouldn’t and he knew that. That is the reason why he is trying his hardest to move on in this tournament, the Global Turnbuckle Tournament was the most prestigious he had ever been in. The quality of the tournament could be known by just reading the names of the people in it. Wrestlers he had grown to be fan of. Wrestlers who had had a big impact on Digital’s career, his inspirations. People he looked up to, like Jay Phoenix were in this thing, and he might have to face him if they both continue on their winning ways. But before that could ever happen, he had to go through Sebastian Moraco, a very talented amateur wrestler, who craved victory as much as he did. Digital was still sad about what had happened, but he had learned to channel that negative energy that had made him go crazy for a moment and put it in his wrestling abilities, and in his resolve to win. It was important to himself, and to those still around him that he succeed, but those left wouldn’t care if he lost, because they would know he tried his hardest, he gave his blood, sweat and tears for it. What people like Burton didn’t want though, was to see him getting himself drunk and crawling through the gutters. He was better than that, and everyone knew it. He knew it, but the pain had made him do things he would have never done. He always told himself that no matter how rough things got he would never try to kill himself, but he did just that. The sky was blue and the birds were chirping today. The sea looked so beautiful to anyone that passed it by, the beach was not full today, because it was a workday today. There were a couple of young students from the nearby university trying to relax and take a time out from the hardships of school. Digital had known what it was like, going to University and feeling lost. He changed majors three times. Going from Computer Engineering to Psychology and ending up in Political Sciences, just like his father he had wanted to be a politician. In his first year of law school though, he felt lost. He didn’t feel like it was for him. That is when he found wrestling. He was a nobody, like everyone who starts the business and doesn’t have anyone else in their family in that line of work. His family didn’t like the idea at first. They didn’t want their son going out there and putting his body on the line day in and day out. He knew what was in store for him, he knew that he had to work hard to get to the top, and after two years of being a no one, he reached the top, but he wouldn’t have done it without the support of Justine and Burton. Digital had met Burton when they were teenagers in high school. Since then they had become best friends, and nothing could come between them. Every time Burton had a problem Antonio was there and vice versa. They were inseparable, they were like brothers. They could confide in one another and forget what the world thought of them. Then one day Burton announced that his family was moving to Chicago. ----- A young Digital sat on a stool with a glass of whiskey on the rocks in his hand. He took a sip and fixed his short black hair with his left hand. "Burt, what's up, man? You're acting so weird today," Digital told him with a laugh. "Don't tell me your girl left you again." Burton tried to smile, but he couldn’t, he had to tell his best friend that he would probably never be able to see him again in a long time. 'My family is moving to Chicago…' he told Digital, looking down, not being able to look at his eyes. 'About time! Now we can do whatever we want here man! No parents to bug you,' Digital said with enthusiasm, not catching the point. 'I mean… me and my family are moving.' Digital, who was in the middle of a sip from his whiskey, almost choked. 'What? You are leaving me here by myself?' was the only thing Digital could ask. 'It isn't my choice…' Burton tried to explain. 'Whatever,' said Digital, standing up and throwing a twenty dollar bill on the table. 'Let me explain!' cried Burton, but Digital was already gone. ----- Digital was never a man of patience, he was always known for his strong character and his short temper. They didn’t see each other until years later, when Digital was hired by a small wrestling federation based in Chicago called the UWF. Burton was a reporter for a small local magazine and was told to cover this new guy called Digital Mortality, to his surprise, it was his old friend. From that moment on, they were inseparable once again. They became best friends once again, and helped each other succeed. Burton was soon editor of the magazine with all the help Digital gave him, and Digi was on the front page of almost every issue, making him a household name all over Chicago. Digital, started to train Burton so that one day he could make his debut in the wrestling ring one day. Now, if it hadn’t been for Burton, Digital Mortality would not be breathing the nice smell of salt water in the beach. He would not have been able to look forward to his next match. He still had his doubts about how he would perform against such a formidable opponent as Sebastian Moraco, but he knew he would do his best. It was just a matter of being confidence, but even with his new resolve, he was far from being a hundred percent. He was far from being the Digital Mortality that everyone was expecting to see, but he would give it his best. He would show them all that he was worthy of the second chance he had been given in the tournament and in life. Digital took the glasses from the little table he had set up in the sand and put them on. He needed to look at the ocean. It was the only place where he could feel good; it was the only place that knew all his secrets. Digital’s relationship with the ocean was just like his relationship with Justine, although he didn’t really know if the sea loved him. The only thing he knew is that he loved the sea, but during his hard times just recently he had forgotten about his relationship with the sea and abandoned it. The sea reminded him so much of Justine. Burton walked toward him with two glasses of orange juice since Digital had stopped drinking. He gave Digital his glass and sat down in the chair beside him. ‘Ahh… it’s been a long time since I saw this beach so clean and beautiful. The last few weeks it has been very dirty.’ Digital thought that the beach had a connection with him, that it knew when Digital was sad, that it knew when something was wrong, it was as if his feelings were reflected on the waves. He drank from his orange juice and relaxed on his chair. He closed his eyes for a few minutes when a ball dropped right at his feet. A girl and a guy went running towards the ball and stopped as soon as they saw it had hit a man. Digital grabbed the ball and looked over at the two college students. They were saying I’m sorry as much as they could. Digital smiled and tossed them the ball. They smiled back and said thank you. When they got back to their game the guy kissed the girl. It made Digital sad, it reminded him of the great times he had spent with Justine, but he knew that those days were gone, only a memory now. He understood that and he could now move on. ‘So Digi, how are you feeling man? You think that focus group thing really helped ya?’ Burton asked putting his glass down on the table. ‘Yeah, I’m sure it did. That kid made me realize that there are some people that are worse than I am, people who have never felt the love of another human being, some not even their parents. I realized I was being selfish, I realized that, and I think I can now move on,’ Digital answered him honestly. ‘You helped me too man… thanks to you I am still breathing this air, thanks to you I can drink this juice, and if I win my next match, it will be thanks to you.’ Is it possible that a man go from being everything that he wanted, having everything that he ever wanted, fame, fortune, love… to lose it all, only to regain it once again? I think it is possible, and I think Digital is a prime example of this. Could he fall once again into depression? Possibly. In this world anything can happen, good or bad. Digital is not the only one to lose his wife and child, and he won’t be the last. It is a fact of life. People die, and most of the time there is no way you can help them. Sometimes you just have to let them go gracefully. I think Justine can now go. Digital wanted to believe in heaven, and believe that there was a paradise and salvation, only because he wanted to believe that she was up there and she wasn’t suffering, or worst of all she was only dust. He tried to lie to himself, but it was to no avail. He knew that the only way he would remember her was in his mind, and he would do everything to keep her there for the rest of his life, even if it was painful to him. He knew that he could always turn to the sea for remembrance. The sea would be his middleman between him and Justine. She would always be there, right in his backyard, a couple of steps away. Whenever he needed her, he knew she would always be there. Both of them, the sea and Justine would always be there. He took of his black shirt and put it down in the little table. He started to walk towards the beach and he felt the breeze of air flow through his long red hair. It had been so long since he got into the water at the beach, even though it was right behind his house. His smile got even larger as he touched the water. Soon he was up to his shoulders in the beach. He relaxed, the warm water flowing through his legs as he swam father and father away. He was now floating in the water face up; he closed his eyes and fell asleep. He knew that the she would never drown him. He saw her then, in his dreams. She was wearing a beautiful blue dress, her long black hair flowing with the wind, she started to walk toward the beach and he started to swim towards the shore, closer to her. When he reached her, she grabbed his hand and smiled. She put in his hand a necklace. She kissed his hand and hugged him. When Digital tried to hug her, she disappeared, she turned into sand. The only thing left in his hand, was a necklace he had given her on her anniversary. He woke up, he was still in the water, but in his hand was the necklace he had given her. Digital smiled and went underwater. She would always be there for him. Justine and the sea, now one and the same.
