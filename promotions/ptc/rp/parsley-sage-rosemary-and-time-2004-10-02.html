Parsley, Sage, Rosemary, and TIME~!

2 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-02 Ah, the sweet scent of victory. Tsunami Oni... the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea, the Vivaldi of Violence, the One and Oni, the Shogun of Air and Darkness, the Lord High Evilutionary, the Grand Poobah of Red Hot Monkey Love... wait, that last one is just for the bedroom. Anyhoo, Tsunami Oni completely devastated his opponents in the first two rounds of GTT4. It was almost like they weren't even there. Like they didn't show up. What's this? A little gloating on the part of Tsunami Oni, hmmm? Poor sportsmanship, maybe? Perhaps, but we are talking about a guy who once won a match by holding the ropes, pulling the tights, and having his valet hold his opponent's leg down all at the same time. And this was after clocking the schmuck with a cane. So poo-poo on you! Victory does smell sweet, but Oni does not at the moment. One of the advantages of being an insanely wealthy superstar is having a gym inside the headquarters of your corporation, OmniNational Industries, located in beautiful San Francisco. All that money from the T-shirts (one for every nickname), the action figures (all new editions since he unmasked), bath toys (spews evil blue bubbles), the cereal (Oni-O's is an important part of a complete breakfast), and uhhhh... other sources (like blackmailing the United States with the launch codes to her own nukes- that'll teach Dubya not to leave briefcases in bathrooms). After an intense cardio session, Tsunami Oni heads up to his luxurious penthouse, soon to be featured in an upcoming episode of MTV's "Cribs." The Vivaldi of Violence is taking his next opponent very seriously. Tsunami Oni plops down on a divan and clicks on the television. It's a tape of Shadow's Round 2 victory over Shaman, a capable foe. As he watches, the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea talks to himself. Hey, he used to be a supervillain. Some habits are hard to break. Oni: Ah, Shadow. You are impressive. And by "impressive" I mean "I wouldn't kick you out of bed for eating crackers." A bit on the gloomy side, though. Sure, I know things are tough these days for you. No more "Friends." No more "Sex & the City." But just rent the DVDs, pick up some Ben & Jerry's, and have a swell time! But Round 3 won't be a swell time for you, sultry Shadow. You have this misfortune of being in between Tsunami Oni and something Tsunami Oni wants. That's like getting between a mama bear and her cub! Damn... is that me smelling like that? Remembering he is a stinky little devil, Tsunami Oni clicks the TV. He strolls into the bathroom and gets out of his sweaty gym clothes. Hey! His pubes are blue, too! Whodathunkit? Oni steps into his antique bathtub, complete with feets, and turns on the shower. The hot water cascades down his broad shoulders and perfect pecs, his washboard abs and rock hard glutes. Hey, just a little something for the ladies... and some of the guys. As Oni washes the sweat away, he notice a bottle marked "Tea Tree Oil." He picks it and opens it. Taking a whiff of it, a look of sadness creeps onto his face. Oni: Ah, Shiatsu... looks like you left something behind. God, this brings back memories. You... me... this bathtub. The time it got so hot in here, the fire alarm went off. Memories. Oni stares at the bottle, thinking of the woman. The woman he shared so much of his life with. The woman he loved more than anything. The woman who left him for his nemesis. The woman that Tsunami Oni does not blame, for he blames himself for driving her away with his lifestyle of ceaseless violence. The memories stir something in the heart of the Devil. And that's not the only body part that stirs. But Oni leaves that one alone. He has this horrible fear that his knees would buckle and he would knock himself out on the edge of the tub. The EMTs would show up and figure out what he was doing and sell the story to the tabloids, yadda yadda yadda. Or worse, he could end up in the Darwin Awards. So Oni decides to wash his hair instead. Putting the tea tree oil down, he grabs his shampoo. It's blue! As he lathers, Oni feels a tingly sensation. Not just on his scalp, but all over. Oni: What the hell? There better not be toxic waste in the water! I'll write my Congressman! Wait, I'm not American. Then I'll... ahhhhh! My eyes! Unfortunately, the tingling distracted Oni and he got shampoo in his eyes. It could happen. As he struggles to get it out, the sensation intensifies. After several seconds, the feeling fades. Tsunami Oni notices he doesn't feel the shower anymore. Oni: Damn it, did Shiatsu forget to pay the utilities before skipping out? Breaking my heart is one thing, but that's just rude. My freakin' money... Tsunami Oni manages to get the shampoo out of his eyes. A few blinks and his sight is restored. Looking around, he realizes why the shower stopped... he's not in his bathroom anymore. Instead he is in a large room with tiled walls and floor and no windows. Well, that kinda sounds like a bathroom, but this is different. No toilet for one thing. You really need a toilet in a bathroom. It's a lot cleaner that way. But there is a massive metal object looking a lot like a laser cannon pointed right at the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. Oni: That... doesn't look good. Maybe I'll go back to having shampoo in my eyes. Suddenly, Tsunami Oni hears the sound of a throat clearing. He turns and sees a man in a white lab coat bearing a striking resemblance to Albert Einstein. Except he's a midget. Because midgets are cool. And he's not alone. There's a tall, busty, blonde girl in a nurse's uniform with him. Because tall, busty, blonde girls in nurse's uniforms are even cooler than midgets. Now a busty, blonde midget in a nurse's uniform... that would be money. Midget: Greetinks, Herr Oni. I am Doktor Feinteim und this my assistant, Freda. Freda: Hello, Herr Oni. Oni: What the hell do you want? Doktor Feinteim: Ach! Such anger! Such hostility! Oni: Well, how would you like to open your eyes and have something long, hard, and massive pointed at you? Freda stares up and down at Tsunami Oni a wistful smile on her face. Freda: I vould tink I died und gone to heaven. Oni follows her gaze and remembers that's standing there in his birthday suit. He gives her a smile back. Freda blushes and giggles. The Doktor looks annoyed. Doktor Feinteim: Freda! Calm yourself! Freda: My apologies, Herr Doktor. It's just... Doktor Feinteim: I understand, my dear. Tsunami Oni looks confused. Which is understandable is you think about it. Buck naked. Weird room. People with funky accents talking about his package. Oni: What? A hottie like like you hasn't seen a stud muffin like me naked before? Granted, there aren't any QUITE as studly as me, but some come close. And quicker. Doktor Feinteim: Oh, there are plenty of attractive men in der vurld. It's just... Oni: Just what? She got crabs? Doktor Feinteim: No. No. It's just... every man in der vurld has a teeny tiny penis. Oni: What? That's not true. You're a doktor. Hell, You've had to have seen porn sometime. And you're a midget. What about "God's practical joke?" The midget scientist looks very depressed. Heartbreaking, really. No one likes to see a sad midget. Doktor Feinteim: Dat vas true once. But den a lesbian terrorist group called der Elektra Complex set loose der Puny Penis Plague. In an attempt to make der men und der vemmin more equal, dis bio-engineered plague reduced der size of every penis on der Earth to dat of der clitoris! Oni: What? That small? Freda: Jah... Freda's eyes water ever so slightly. Oni: Even you, doktor? Doktor Feinteim: Ja... Now the doktor's eyes water. Oni: Wait a minute, but the magic wand here is still a perfect ten? Doktor Feinteim: Dat is because you ver born centuries before der Puny Penis Plague! Oni: Centuries? Doktor Feinteim: Ja, Herr Oni. Velcome to der year 2969! [Paused for dramatic effect.] Oni: What? Doktor Feinteim: Ve have summoned you from der past for save der future! [Dayum, this is some dramatic shiznit!] Oni: What? Doktor Feinteim: You see der big laser cannon lookink thingamabob? Oni: It's kinda hard to miss. Doktor Feinteim: Dat is der Temporal Discomboobulator! Vith dat, I can bend der laws of Gott himself und summon objects, creatures from beyond der veil of time itself! Oni: Does he always talk like that? Feda: Ja. He took a class for it. "The Von Doom Gide to Public Speakink." Doktor Feinteim: Vurth every penny. Oni: I take it there's a point around here somewhere. Doktor Feinteim: Oh, ja... Der vurld of 2969 needs you, Herr Oni. A horrible monster is destroying der city of Schwarzeneggerburgh! Oni: Schwarzeneggerburgh? Freda: In your time, it vas Washington, DC. Den after President Schwarzenegger lead der United States to victory over Canada, it vas renamed Schwarzeneggerburgh. Doktor Feinteim: Ja, und ve developed dese stereotypical accents in his honor, too. Oni: Why did the US go to war with Canada? Freda: Vell, in 2009, Avril Lavigne and Chad Kroeger put out an album of Rush covers. Dat much bad music vas to much for Herr President und he nuked 'em! Oni: Damn, I'm gonna have to wait five years to be rid of Avril and Nickelback? Doktor Feinteim: Afraid so, but you're just in time to save 2969! Oni: Uh, great. One thing, though... Doktor Feinteim: Ja, Herr Oni? Oni: GET ME SOME FREAKIN' CLOTHES! Freda: Do ve gotta? Oni: Ja! I mean, yes! Damn, you got me doing it now, too! Doktor Feinteim: Ve have clothes ready for you, Herr Oni. Freda vill show you. Freda: Just valk down der hall, Herr Oni. Oni: Shouldn't you go first? Freda: No, I tink you should. Oni: OK... So Tsunami Oni walks down the hall with Freda following, gaze glued to his posterior. Her eyes follow every bounce. Oni: Am I walking the right way? Freda: Oh, ja.. ja! Bouncy bouncy bouncy... Freda directs the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea into a room off to the side. He sees a sleeveless bodysuit in there. Oni: That's my old costume! I don't wear that anymore! Freda: Vell, dat vas der one at der wax museum. Oni: Well, I'm not wearing the horned mask. It looked like a propeller hat. Stupid monkey-boy tailor... last time I get a janitor to make my outfits... Grumbling about midgets and monkeys, Tsunami Oni begins dressing. He notices Freda is watching, with one hand in her blouse. Oni: Uh, a little privacy. Freda: I've been staring at you naked since you got here. Oni: Good point. Finished putting on his old outfit, the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea checks himself out in a mirror. Oni: Yeesh, this puppy's tight. Doesn't leave much to the imagination. Not that you need to imagine. You know, Freda, you look familiar... Freda: Ja, dat's because I am a direct descendant of UWF interviewer und commentator, Freda Schtupp. Oni: No kidding? Freda: Ja, in fact, I am Freda Schtupp XXX. Oni: I knew your ancestor well. So did Shiatsu. Freda: I know. I read her diaries. Dat's vhy I insisted Herr Doktor summon you. See if der legend... measured up. Und dat's a big "ja." Oni: There was another choice? Freda: Vell, he was impressed vith Herr Almasy's bunny-slaying prowess. But I pointed out you have experience vith giant monsters, like blowing up der Loch Ness Monster und der massive snail you beat in Tokyo. Oni: You mean Es-KARRRG-o? I was hoping no one would remember that... especially me. Freda: Vhy not? You killed a monstrous mammoth terrozink Tokyo just usink an oversized novelty salt shaker! Oni: Listen to what you just said. It was stupid. I'm supposed to be a serious competitor now. Big tournaments. Title shots. Getting the love of my life back. Then you yutzes whisk me into the future in my birthday suit. And you remind me about Es-KARRRG-o! A giant FREAKIN' snail! It woulda taken him until...oh, THE END OF THE FREAKIN" UNIVERSE, to destroy Tokyo! He was a SNAIL! GAH! I wanna go home! Freda's eyes are watering. She rushes over to Tsunami Oni and grabs his arm. Freda: Help us, Oni-wan! You're our only hope! Freda: Help us, Oni-wan! You're our only hope! Freda: Help us, Oni-wan! You're our only hope! Freda: Help us, Oni-wan! You're our only hope! Freda: Help us, Oni-wan! You're our only hope! Freda: Help us, Oni-wan! You're our only hope! Freda: Help us, Oni-wan! You're our only hope! Oni: What the hell? The Vivaldi of Violence yanks his arm out of the grasp of Freda Schtupp XXX. They stare at one another, confused. Freda: Gott in himmel! I vas caught in some kind of time loop! Ve must take you to Herr Doktor! Oni: Couldn't we just keep this our little secret? Freda: No! To Herr Doktor! Freda and Tsunami Oni return to the lab, where Doktor Feinteim stares at a chalkboard of cryptic formulas. Could be the Grand Unified Theory. Could be the recipe for spam. Could go either way. As Freda tells Doktor Feinteim what happened, Tsunami Oni stands behind her mouthing "she's nuts" and making the international gesture for "loose screw." But it's to no avail. Doktor Feinteim: Interestink... hop up onto der table, Herr Oni. Oni: I got a bad feeling about this... Doktor Feinteim: Don't be a girly-man! I am a doktor! Still apprehensive, Tsunami Oni none the less hops up onto the table. Doktor Feinteim subjects Oni to a physical exam that really doesn't need to be described. After several uncomfortable moments, the Doktor is finished. Doktor Feinteim: I am finished! Oni: All done. Doktor Feinteim: Oh, ja... Oni: Then could you stop holding my testicles? Doktor Feinteim: Oops. Sorry, Herr Oni. Oni: You're still holding my testicles. Doktor Feinteim: So I am! Silly me! Oni: Let go of my testicles. Now. Doktor Feinteim: Fine, fine. Mein gott, no respect for science... Freda: So what is it, Herr Doktor? Doktor Feinteim: Vhen ve brought Herr Oni into der year 2969 vith der Temporal Discomboobulator, he has apparently acquired veird powers over time itself! Oni: What the fudge? Doktor Feinteim: Herr Oni, you can cause a loop in time with physical contact. I call it... der Mega-Rerun Touch~! Oni: That's a pretty freakin' stupid name. Freda: That's amazink, Herr Doktor! Doktor Feinteim: I suspected something like dis vould happen. Tsunami Oni stares at the midget scientist. Oni: Wait, if you suspected this would happen and it did, why the overly-thorough physical? Doktor Feinteim: Vell... Oni: You just wanted to hold my testicles, right? Doktor Feinteim: Maybe.. Oni: Damn it! Can't I see a doctor without getting molested? My rectum still clenches up everytime I see a lab coat. You know, I'm going to do a little scientific experiment of my own. Tsunami Oni leans down and flicks Doktor Feinteim in the forehead with his finger. THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! THWACK~! Doktor Feinteim: Ach! The midget scientist falls to the floor, a Looney Toons-esque bump on his forehead with stars orbiting. The Vivaldi of Violence stares at his fingers with a fiendish grin on his face. Oni: I could get to like this. Freda: Herr Oni! Ve don't have much time! You must fight der monster! Oni: Oh, yeah. Gotta save Schwarzeneggerburgh. So where is this mofo at? Freda: No far from here! You can see der creature outside der lab. Tsunami Oni and the buxom blonde run out the lab door. The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea is amazed at what he sees. It really is a city of the future. Flying cars. Holographic billboards. Robot butlers. Randomly tentacled aliens. Seven story gold statue of the Terminator. It would be a beautiful sight if not for the wreckage. And fires. And mangled bodies. And that statue is kind of of tacky. Oni: Wow. It's like a cross between "The Jetsons" and Columbus after the Ohio State-Michigan game! So where's this monster at? Freda: Dere, Herr Oni! Dere! Following Freda's frightened finger (oooo... alliteration!), Tsunami Oni sees the culprit behind the mayhem. There, amongst piles of smashed flying cars and demolished instant sex-change booths, is a massive dinosaur! The blast from the past lets out a mighty roar and stomps on the debris! Oni: Sweet mamba jamma! A freakin' dinosaur! Wait a minute... This wouldn't have something to do with Doktor Feinteim and his Temporal Discomboobulator, would it? Freda look a tad nervous. Freda: Errr... vat make you tink dat? Oni: Well, there's the huge dinosaur shaped hole in the side of the lab. Freda: Oh, ja.. dat. Now dat I tink about it, maybe Herr Doktor does have sometink to do vith dis. Oni: Spiffy keen. Wait a minute... is that dinosaur wearing something? Staring at the prehistoric beast, Tsunami Oni spots a fringed leather vest on it. Looking at the dino's head reveals a huge Stetson hat on its monstrous noggin. And the feet stomping away at the carnage are wearing cowboy boots complete with spurs. Oni: Slap my cheeks and call me Rosey! The dinosaur lunges at a fleeing bystander and chomps him! The poor yutz is quickly chewed up and swallowed! The dinosaur throws his head back in triumph! Dinosaur: YEEEEEEEEEEEEEE-HA! Yippee-ki-yay! Y'all best not mess with the toughest varmint west of the Jurassic... CHRONOSAURUS TEX~!!! Oni: Things just got REALLY stupid... Chronosaurus Tex continues on his path of destruction, destroying property and munching citizenry, singing "Don't Fence Me In" the whole time. Hey, you kids can sing along at home if you like! Just follow the bouncing ball! DISCLAIMER: There is no bouncing ball. "Oh, give me land, lots of land under starry skies above," A mammoth cowboy boot turns a slow moving citizen of Schwarzeneggerburgh into something red and squishy. "Don't fence me in! Let me ride through the wide open country that I love," An enormous green tail smashes into the First National Clone Bank, sending concrete and frozen embryos plummeting several stories to the ground. "Don't fence me in! Let me be by myself in the evenin' breeze, And listen to the murmur of the cottonwood trees, Send me off forever but I ask you please," Jurassic jaws crush a flying police car into itty bitty bits. "Don't fence me in!" Boy, those Cole Porter songs are really timeless. Speaking of timeless (ooooo... what a segueway!), as Tsunami Oni stares at prehistoric outlaw, Freda looks at the Vivaldi of Violence with eyes wet with tears. Freda: Herr Oni, vill you be able to save Schwarzeneggerburgh from der wrath of Chronosaurus Tex? Oni: Just answer me one thing. Freda: Ja, Herr Oni? Oni: You still got Jagermeister in 2969?
