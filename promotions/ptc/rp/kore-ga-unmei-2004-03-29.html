Kore ga unmei...

29 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-29 A warrior born of heaven. The scene opens to a ring in an old gym. Normally, one might find a struggling prizefighter here, or perhaps a judoka in training, but not this day. Today this setting appears quite strange, considering who stands in this ring. In one corner is Kenzo, a young man of no more than 22 years. His presence does not make this scene strange at all, but the one who stands across from him most certainly does. That man is Raijin, the Shinto god of Lightning. Once a kami, he quickly grew tired of his life in what he calls Tengoku, the domain of heaven. He chose to become a human in order to test himself, to face the best warriors of this realm, and in this tournament, he has found the greatest gathering of the strongest of all of Earth’s fighters. The scene shifts to Raijin’s face, hidden behind a mask that reveals only his mouth and his eyes. The mask is not a disguise to hide his identity as some might believe, but rather a greater representation of his true soul. He closes his eyes for a moment and lets his head drop. He takes a deep breath, opens his eyes, and brings his gaze to meet the camera. His eyes begin to glow with a supernatural electric blue light... his divine soul shining through his human form. Raijin: For centuries I have stood in heaven looking down upon this world, living a life separated from all mortal pleasures and afflictions, unable to be harmed but by the mightiest of gods, taking in the delights of the land where the kami dwell. Now I have come to this world to be one of you, and I find as a man, I am as vulnerable as any other. The intricacies of being human have escaped me thus far. I have yet to feel love, hatred, happiness or pain. I have yet to feel the surge of adrenaline within my veins, or the tranquil bliss of slumber. I have yet to feel what it means to truly live as one of this plane. The camera pans around to Kenzo, uncertainty in his eyes. He has no idea why he was called here, or what Raijin has in mind for him. The only reason he agreed to show up was that something happened to him that he could not ignore... the man standing in front of him, the man called Raijin, appeared to him as he slept the night prior to this meeting. Seeing the man that had appeared in his dreams, he could not refuse this encounter. Raijin: You, my earthly friend... Kenzo, you shall teach me. Kenzo: Me? What have I got to do with you? Why have you called me here? Raijin: You are the only soul upon this planet that knows who I am. My life has not been revealed to my opponents nor anyone else save for you. What they have seen of me can scarcely scratch the surface of my existence. I came to you in a dream, advising you of my impending arrival, choosing you for your unwavering allegiance to the gods. You shall be my guide, for I am blind to the realities of being human. Kenzo looks at Raijin, absolutely perplexed by this request. Kenzo: But what can I do? Raijin spreads his arms wide and scans the entire gym. Raijin: Look around you. We stand in a ring, do we not? You need not wonder why now... to begin, you shall do what this place was made for... I want you to strike me. Kenzo is taken aback. Kenzo: Strike you? Raijin cocks his head to one side. Raijin: Is that not what I said? Kenzo nods. Kenzo: W...well yes, but I... Raijin looks straight into Kenzo’s eyes. Raijin: You what? You cannot strike one of the gods that you had so revered all your life? You have no reason to fear, my friend... I shall not retaliate. I will not strike back. I simply want to feel what it is like to be human, to be susceptible to pain and injury. To know what it will feel like to be hurt in combat as will most certainly happen. So I say once again... hit me with all your might. Kenzo steps back, hesitant to do what he has been told. He shakes it off, steps up to Raijin, rears back and swings with all his power, connecting squarely with Raijin’s jaw. He reels from the impact, looking almost dumbfounded at the force directed toward him. He steps back up to Kenzo. Raijin: Again. Kenzo waves him off and shakes his head. Kenzo: No... I can’t... Raijin clenches his fists and stomps the mat. Raijin: Again!! This time there is no hesitation, as Kenzo floors him with a strong right. Raijin falls flat on his back, and blood trickles from the corner of his mouth. He sits up. Kenzo: Uhh... Raijin rubs the back of his hand across his blood-flecked lips and gazes down at the crimson streaks upon it. He looks over to Kenzo, a wide grin spreading across his face. He chuckles softly and nods. Raijin: Ooh... so this... this is what you call pain... Kenzo inches backward, even though he was assured he would not be retaliated against, simply because of the wicked look in Raijin’s eyes. Raijin climbs to his feet and clenches his hands into tight fists. Raijin: Haha... I like it... this pain is invigorating! After centuries of feeling nothing, this is a shock, and a surprise... a welcome one, I might add. I see I shall quite like this mortal form. It feels good to really live! I shall enjoy the punishment inflicted upon me by my opponents... but no doubt, I shall enjoy inflicting it to them more! Raijin’s seemingly newfound fondness for violence startles Kenzo at first because of the zeal he shows for it, but is not so much of a surprise as he has quickly come to accept Raijin as being more than ordinary. Raijin: I came to Earth for one reason, and one reason alone... to engage in decisive combat with the greatest champions in existence in order to prove myself as worthy of my status, and to honor Ama-Terasu-O-Mi-Kami, Sun Goddess and ruler of the heavens. I have found those champions here, and I have seen them, and I now know that they truly are to be feared and respected. Kenzo climbs from the ring leaving Raijin alone. Raijin only glances at him for a instant before returning his focus to the impending affairs. Raijin: I will not step down though, nor will I retreat, for I have one advantage over them, one thing that sets me apart from them. You see, the problems that plague mankind I have never experienced. The fear and sadness that inhibits them and the anger that seethes inside them keeps them from truly grasping the realities of their situation. They cannot completely focus on the task at hand... their mind will not be entirely in the battle. I, on the other hand, have nothing to keep me from concentrating all of my energies on this. Raijin once again looks at the streaks of now-dry blood on the back of his hand. He smiles, tasting the sanguine fluid on his mouth. Raijin: The blood that runs through me may be the blood of all people, but the soul that inhabits my form is the fearless soul of a kami, an undefeatable spirit and the source of all my power and courage. Though my flesh might be torn, my bones shattered, and my blood spilled, my soul shall never die, and so even if I do not win, I will not be defeated. All light in the gym fades save for that which shines above Raijin. He breathes slowly and deeply, collecting his thoughts and calming his spirit. Raijin: I only ask one thing of my adversaries... Coricidon... and James Edwards. Those who climb too high are often burned by the sun... will this be their fate, or will they find the strength to survive the ascent to the height that I stand? He raises his bloodstained fist into the air. Raijin: Ganbarimasu... ikuzo! Raijin stands motionless, without a breath, without blinking. The scene slowly goes out of focus and fades to black around him. Now a warrior of earth.
