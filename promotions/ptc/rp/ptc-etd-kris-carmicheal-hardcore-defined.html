20 March 2005 Hardcore defined by difference... Character: Kris CarMicheal Fed: HSW Self-confidence. Many posses this trait as they gloat and talk of themselves as if they are the world. But are they? Are they all as good as they say? As good as they believe? Could they all be the best? That thought dwindles about my brain as I make my walk along one of the dark damp streets of Detroit Michigan. The air crisp today as it’s not hot enough to wear just a shirt but not cold enough to wear my trench coat. I settle with a long-sleeve shirt and it appears to be holding its own against the minor chill. Back to those thoughts, I go as I see all the names. Everyone claims to be different under their personas but they are wrong. They’re all the same as the name is the separating factor. They all dream of the same thing and all need the same thing. Attention, titles, and supremacy are those aspirations, few find them. They all see themselves as nothing but perfect. As the top of the line. As the best of the best. But not me. I need not tell myself I am the best, it wouldn’t be true. For everyone, there is always one better. Always one stronger, and always one quicker. For me, I look upon myself as the one the others look upon. They all see me. My face painted in it’s black and white paint, the spiked accessories I adorn, and the chains and straps that hang about my ensemble. They see me and think nothing of myself. They see me and feel I am nothing but low-life trash. That trait there is possesed by all those that believe they will overcome all. The trait of ignorance. They see me as a threat and think that their petty looks and crooked callings will bring me down. Too bad I don’t go down without a fight. Losing my train of thought, I breeze past a business man. His nice black jacket rests over his body as does his freshly ironed pants. His hair is styled back as he seems to be on his way to a job interview. He glances upon me as he looks in disgust. The look he shows is nothing I haven’t seen, it’s what I have grown to expect. As he continues, I do the same. We walk separate ways now as I pass an elderly woman. Appearing to be in her mid seventies, she gives that same look. That look of hate and anguish that she believes is taking away from my heart. But how could that be, my heart ticks on in its hateful manner just as it had before. As she leaves me, I look ahead. The local Deli is merely a few blocks away and I could use something to subdue my hunger. I pass by many of the same characters on my way to this Deli and all seem to have that same feeling of pure hatred. Ignoring each one, I find myself looking upon the large wooded sign. Across it are the letters reading; “Big Dave’s Deli”. What a name I think to myself as I wonder what this Big Dave is like. As I walk through the double doors of the Deli, I look across to see a fairly big room. Along the walls rest shelves and shelves of food products one can buy. Cheeses, meats, and many other toppings seem to be for sale as I look out to see the bar looking section. The line stretches for a little bit until stopping at a slightly obese man with his fat hanging over his belt. The site of this man only tells me of all the norms like him. All the overweight fools that look alike in every single case. I take this time to make my way to that bar. Behind it, I can now see three people. One is a man looking as lean as can be. His hair hangs over his face as if he is a skateboarder of some sort. Across his chest is a blue O’Neil tee that tells me he surfs now. Next to this surf bum, there is a woman. She looks to be of the age of seventeen or eighteen as her face is very young and her body is quite petit. She prepares a turkey sub at the moment as a man tells her to hurry it up. Next to her is a man with a white apron across his large chest. The apron is spread with various stains, as it seems unwashed for quite some time. He wipes his nose across his hand as he then wipes that on the apron. ‘What a pig, just like that guy in line’ I think to myself. I quit my looking about the employees as I make my way to the line. A gap rests between a set of people so I make my way in. I can tell they know I jumped in but what will they do? “What are you doing you little rat? Get to the end of the line or better yet…back to the sewer you came from.” Says a man that has misjudged me at first glance. I look upon this man as he stands upright. His eyes meet mine as I look into him. I can see from his style that he belongs to a lot of money and that he doesn’t seem to like me. By my looks is my first guess because I have yet to speak with the dense gentleman. I now look away from him and up to the sandwich list. Roast Beef, Ham, BLT, and many other choices spread across the board as I contemplate just what I want. As I do so though, my thoughts are ruptured. The pesky voice of the rich man cuts into my ears. “ I said get out of here you little too-bit punk! I was in this line before you so move away and let me up! It’s not like you have any money to buy a sandwich anyways!” I laugh at this arrogant gentleman as he can’t seem to find the humor. I continues to laugh as he asks what is going on. As I begin to descend in the laughter, I start with my first words to this text-book norm. “Well well well, lookie what we got here. Just another predictable snob? Yes. You really want me to go back to my sewer? Back to the place I spawned? If so, you were wrong. I came from the same home as you would have. I came from the same life you had. When I looked upon it though, guess what I saw…” I say as I wait for an answer. He shakes his head and I continue. “I saw the life you lead today. I saw myself thinking just like you. Where are those thoughts going to get you? Everyone thinks of them, everyone knows them. Except me! When it comes to thoughts, I have my own. The kind that the others are unaware of and the kind that they are scared of.” The man looks upon me laughing now. “So you think you were born in a twelve bedroom house like me, father and mother, and two siblings?” “I said nothing of that, I said I lived that same lifestyle. I said I was born in a house like yours. If I lived as you did, I would be just like everyone else in this world. In this rotting hell hole of a world. I would have ended up looking down on one of my kind and thinking of reasons to why they are nothing but scum in this world. Why would a life like that be so fabulous? Why would many do anything for a life of fame, fortune, and glamour? All it brings about is classic norm syndrome.” I reply as I look about the menu again. After deciding a roast beef and Swiss sub will do, I make my way to the front. The moans and complaints of that man echo still as I ignore the foolish bastard. I look at the name tag of the obese worker and it reads; ‘Big Dave’. He smiles at me as he wipes his nose with his apron. The utter disgust of this man is nothing but degrading. Almost as degrading as the competition I have been set against. “What woul’ ya’ like Mista’ gothic boy?” Asks Big Dave as he snorts in laughter at his question. “You think I could get someone other then a complete and total fat ass to serve me?” I ask as he seems offended by my question. “What is tha’ mista’ tiny man? I’m not’a fat ass.” Replies the denying Dave. I laugh about him as I ask for either surfer boy or maybe even the young girl. He says no so I walk over to one. The surf boy is first I find as I begin to speak with him. “Listen hang ten, I need a roast beef and Swiss sub and I want you to make it. So can ya’ get on that?” “Um dude, I can’t really do that. Ya’ see man, Big Dave is like…my boss and what he says…goes. Sorry dude.” Says the man in a genuine surfers voice. I ignore him now as I look to the young woman. She smiles at me as I motion for her to come to the front. She does so as I ask the same question. “I’m sorry but you’re not really my type and I only serve…” “Ha ha ha, of course I’m not your type! If I was, I would have to die! When it comes to your type, I show nothing but bitterness. So little girl, I’ll say just what I have to all the other norms in this vast world…I ain’t leaving. Learn to live with it!” With that, I walk to the front again. The man who had bitched about me earlier seems to be ordering from Dave so I push him out of the way. I look at Dave again and take a deep breath. “Dave, I’ll take my sandwich from you.” Dave now does the laughing as he looks about me. “I hav teh’ abilitay ta’ refuse service ta’ anyone who I sey’ fit. An’ I sey’ ya’ fit. Good day kid.” I smile about the overweight man as he does the same. The man I had shoved now comes up and pushes me. I look at him now and send a shot to his midsection as if in the ring. He drops to his knee and I kick him across the face. I take a knife now from the counter and get to the ground. The screams and gasps I hear are all over now. I place the knife about his neck as I poke him slightly. “As much as the others may think, I am as hardcore as the rest of ‘em. And when it comes down to the encounter, I find no need to hold back. So Mister whatever…any last words?” He whimpers in fear as I push the knife down a little bit. “I…want…my…my…my mommy!!” I laugh at the fool as I let him go. He lies on his back in shock as everyone can see his pant front has been wetted. I point the knife at him again and drop it next to his head. He cries as I leave him on the ground. As I exit I utter the words; “Hardcore is defined by difference. A different style than all the others. I am that different style than all the others. I am that guy that stands out. When it comes to hardcore, I’m merely invincible…” With that I take my leave through those doors again. The scene now fades out showing nothing but a black screen. ---- 24 March 2005 Crazy Day... Character: Kris CarMicheal Fed: HSW Hardcore. Many have been apart of this word as they have found out what it truly means. With utter disregard to one’s body, a skilled one of this field must live by this. It’s not easy…if it were, everyone would take part in it. Over the years, men and women have all claimed of being one of the top icons, the biggest names to come from the hardcore lifestyle. How could this be so? How could such a mass of superstars be the best? The answer comes with not much thought…they aren’t. Many believe as they look in the mirror they are the real deal. The man that is going to show the world he is the best. With that glance to such a false hope, they later recognize what they have gotten themselves into. The pain and torture that has been brought upon them is unbarring as they look for whatever way out there possibly is. There is no escape though. No way to leave the world they had thought they could live. Life becomes hell. In this hell, nothing but suffer comes. The chairs, tables, thumbtacks, knifes, wood slabs, and even glasses echo in ones thoughts as they become a persons nightmare. A nightmare that lasts forever. ++++++++++Not Hardcore?++++++++++ There I rest, on my way to the next Shocker we go. Odis rests at my side, as he seems to have been taken to the realm of dreams. The realm where nothing is felt or heard, only thought. I feel almost as if I’m ready to join him but I battle through. The bouncing about of that car was unbelievable…I never would have thought a car could find so many pot holes. Driving this vehicle was a man of Arab style. A turban rests about his head as his face matches that of a very tan gentleman. His dialect gives away his race just as much. Looking about his card on the driver side sun blocker, I see his ID card. Muhammad Patel is the Taxi drivers name as he has apparently been in the business for about three years. I couldn’t see how anyone could stay around such a job for that long but who knows. He now takes a right turn down a back road as he begins humming some sort of Arab beat. “You seem to look like someone I know sir…” Says the driver as he looks about me in his mirror. I show no emotions to the comment as I look away. I spot a couple walking down the street at the moment as he still thinks of me. I can tell he knows, he just wants me to say it. “You’re that Defect kid, right?” Asks the man in his dialect. I nod slowly as I wonder now what he wants. Possibly an autograph? No. I know of his kind. Not the Arab kind but of his broader kind. He is just like the others. The ones that don’t seem to understand myself and others like me. “Kris CarMicheal I am…what do ya’ want?” He smiles thinking he has achieved something. I frown upon that smile and look off again. I can see the sites of many buildings and many more people. The people gather about in a circle as if a fight has begun. I look off from that as the man continues. “You’re in that Extreme Title battle…right?” I nod again as he smiles. His ugly yellow teeth shine to me in his overhead mirror as I look upon Odis. He still rests out cold as if he was drugged. “So you’re a wrestling fan I guess?” asks I toward the smiling man. “Yes yes, very right. I actually am a big fan of Primetime Central and have watched many of their matches. This should be a good one. I mean, big names in that pool. How did you manage to get in?” I shrug at that comment as I look to see an Elephant in the seat next to him. It looks as if it is a small figurine that a kid would play with. I glance back to the driver and begin to answer. “I was invited. You didn’t mean that in a bad way…did you?” He laughs as I can smell his breath. The scent of raw onions is caught in a back draft and I try as hard as possible to fight the fumes. They find my nose though and I’m stuck in a storm of odor. “Of course I did. Look at you…a measly little wannabe hardcore kid. I got my bets you’re gone first.” I hold my temper back at this moment as he laughs upon me again. My hand makes its way to my pocket though. It rummages about as I feel the figure of a chain. I slowly drag it out now as I hold it below the driver’s eye site. “So I’m not hardcore, am I?” “Look at you… a little face paint and you think you’re amazing.” He replies as I tighten my grip on that chain. The links make a clanking noise as I raise it. I can see the driver’s eyes as he has yet to see me. He continues with his laugh until I wrap it around his throat. The car makes a swerve as it comes to a quick halt. Odis’s head makes a run to the seat in front as he wakes up in a bang. The driver squeals in agony as I let go. The door is soon opened by me and I make it to the Arab man’s side. I rip his door open and get close. He clenches his neck in pain as a bloody river runs on his white shirt. “Not hardcore? Am I nothing but a punk kid? Not anymore. You watch that match as you had said and you’ll see. You’ll see me as I take whatever I can, use it however I need it, and walk away with a shot at the newest champion. Don’t ever underestimate!” With that, Odis exits. He looks at me holding his head as he can’t seem to find where he’s at. I motion for him to follow as I leave the helpless man hanging from his cab. His lesson has been learnt for the day…what a good student he was. ++++++++++Jimmy’s Hot Dogs and Chili ++++++++++ As we make our trip down that road, we pass various men and women. Some in their elder state as others have yet to enter puberty. Families are around just as well as their children run around the area. I look to Odis now; he still holds his head as it throbs continuously. He apparently found out where we were but still looks unknowing. “Man…what happened?” Asks my dear friend as we walk down a sidewalk. I laugh at that as I roll down my long sleeves. The chill I find is uncomfortable but is ended once my sleeves are fully extended. We continue as I start to answer his question. “Nothing much…he just misjudged me.” “I know what happens there. He say you weren’t hardcore enough for an Extreme battle?” asks Odis as we pass a delivery truck. I nod to that and see he knows me quite well. As we go further and further from the scene the Taxi was left, we spot a roadside vendor. The stand has a sign across the top that reads; “Jimmy’s Dogs and Chili”. Looking away from that sign though, I see the man who runs it. He has a slender figure that shows his lack of exercise. Across his nose is a pair of glasses and under it is a long mustache. Over his body is an apron that reads Jimmy’s dogs in a blue bubble lettering. The thoughts of food are pleasant so I make my way with Odis to see Jimmy. As we approach, Jimmy starts to yell around. “Hot Dogs and Chili, you know you want it!” He was right. As I stop in front of him, he seems to think he persuaded me and Odis to come. I now see the finer details of the small cart. A hot dog cooker rests on the right of me and a big pot lies on a hotplate. Jimmy smiles to each of us and starts his talking. “What would you like sir?” I think to myself for a minute before making my mind. “I’ll take…” says I before being cut off. “I’ll take two hotdogs and a cup of chili.” I turn to see the man that had said that and notice it’s a different man. He shows a look of disgust toward me as I show the same to him. “I was here first jack-ass!” I say as he laughs upon me. “Kid, go back to your mommy. Maybe she can tell you Halloween is over.” Says the man. I smile at him with the comment. “Funny guy are we? You sound like someone I’ve talked to before. Right…you sound like all the others. Just like the rest of this world, you have a problem with me. Before I say a word, you have a problem. Well guess what…when a problem occurs, something must be done to extinguish it. What do you suppose we do?” The man smiles more and just balls out laughing. “Just as I thought…don’t worry, I’ll answer this for you.” With that, I send him headfirst into the stand. The chili pours over and Jimmy jumps about. Odis grabs the man’s cup and his hotdogs. He stands over his head and force feeds the hot dogs to him. As he goes to drop the chili, I grab it. “You see, unlike you all think, I am hardcore.” I lean forward now and pour the steaming chili across his chest. He squirms and winces at the pain as I can’t seem to not laugh. As it all empties, I look for another source of pain. I find the cart as good enough and drop it over his head. The man lies still now. His breath is harder then before and his screams have ceased. I can tell he has found the pain I have felt, this is satisfying. ++++++++++Weapons++++++++++ After leaving the scene we had caused, we find our way to a back ally. It’s unoccupied and there seems to be a set of old rusted benches. We walk to them and make ourselves comfortable. The rusted steel is quite hard but good enough for our liking. I take a glance about this new scene to see many weapon like figures. Directly across from me rests a large dumpster. It rests as rusted as the bench we sit upon. The sheer size of it intimidates foes. This sight could be a possible weapon in this extreme title battle royal. Possibly to throw someone into or even to slam the doors on. In all sense, this weapon is painful to the punch. Next to that lies a set of box crates. Another possible weapon that could strike fear into the eyes of all the competition. The moves I could perform through it, the people I could hit with them. In all seriousness, they could nearly break a person down. I hope we have those for our match. The object that really catches my eye is a steel old bat. I’ve used them very often and they always seem to work. In the end, they always seem to send the finishing punch. I know there will be a bat there; I just can’t wait to get my hands on one. “You see ‘em to ‘eh?” Says Odis referring to the weapons. I nod as I think about more uses for other choices. We sit and relax until I hear the sounds of the sirens. They would scare the normal ones but not me. I am not a normal one. I can hear them get closer and closer until finally they pass by the ally. Their sirens scream at me but don’t see us. I can see Odis wondering what would happen if we got caught. I stand now and motion for him to follow. We exit through another street and walk down the road again. That is until I find another cab. I hold my hand up and he comes to a stop. As his window rolls down, I look about him. Another Arab man looks at me as I tell him where we’re off to. He knows and I jump in followed closely by Odis. “Off to my title shot we go.” Fade…
