Darth von Doom versus King Blueberry

4 October 2008

Unholy Revenge, Phase 5 - Slam and The Fourth Horseman (IG50 Promo #5 of 5) Title: Unholy Revenge, Phase 5 - Slam and The Fourth Horseman (IG50 Promo #5 of 5) Word Count: 1000 exactly Wrestler: Darth von Doom In the Yukon Territory of Canada… After the Church transport ships unloaded the zombies into the Yukon territory, another batch of transport ships arrived during the night and dropped off the Ninjas of Doom to oversee the operation. The plan is to send in more zombies from the north while the armed forces are still dealing with the zombies that came in from Mexico in the southern border of the US. However, there is one factor that the Church did not factor into their equation… SLAM BOOMSDEXTER. In the woods as all of this has been happening throughout the night hours Slam has stood watching. He’s kept quiet with only his flask of alcohol and his blueberries to keep him company and to help him drown away his sorrows. His loyal sidekick Joey has been sleeping the whole night and has no idea what’s going on. Joey: Boomsdexter…zzzzz…Boomsdexter… Slam turns back to see that the zombies are heading south towards the United States border. The Ninjas are leading the way. Slam continues to watch until they are all gone but deep down he knows that in the morning the police will be calling the Slam Boomsdexter hotline and that he will be there to save the day yet again. But, for now, he hides in the shadows until the morning when the first light comes. * * * In the Caribbean… The port city of Tortuga has been bustling with activity for a few days now with ships fleeing there to fill up on supplies in a panic over the impending war that seems to be starting between the Church of the Unholy and all nations of the world. However three pirates have had no idea what’s been going on. Their names are Captain Jack Morgan, the Killer Klown from Outer Space, and Cave Hulk. Instead they’ve been getting drunk on their boat the Black Nemo III at the docks for about the past three weeks since returning from the Black Ocean of space upon reaching the end of the universe. Now they are asleep after a full day spent drinking rum. However they are awakened by the sounds of what seems to be the marching of soldiers. Jack: What the…? Just then they hear footsteps on the deck of their boat and then coming down the stairs to the crew quarters. They three grab their weapons and get to their feet quickly just as someone familiar walks down into their quarters. It’s Darth von Doom and he’s already ignited his light saber. A midget wearing all white and holding plates known as the Midget of Pain is with him as well. Jack: Darth?! But you’re…you’re… Darth: Dead? I think not. You three thought you killed me in space. Now it’s my turn. Just then a speck of dust lands on a plate the midget is holding. MOP: YOU! YOU GOT DIRT ON MY PLATE! The Midget starts throwing plates at them as Jack pulls out the light saber he got in space and starts fighting with Darth. Ninjas of Doom come running down and start battling the Hulk and Klown. Darth uses the Force Push to knock Jack into the wall. The MOP then throws a plate that smashes into the head of the Klown and knocks him down. Darth then goes over and picks up the clown. Darth turns to the Ninjas. Darth: Keep them busy. The Mathematician needs this one. Darth then turns off his light saber and carries the clown off the ship with the Midget of Pain in tow as the Hulk and Jack continue to battle the Ninjas. A few minutes later, after the battle is over, Jack and the Hulk look for the clown but can’t find him anywhere. He’s long gone by this point. Darth accomplished his abduction mission and the clown is on his way to the Mathematician’s lab for experimentation. * * * The next morning… That morning Joey wakes up from a good night’s rest to see Slam Boomsdexter standing near him trying to drink out of an empty flask. Joey: Wha…what’s going on? Slam: While you were sleeping, a zombie army came through here. Joey: A zombie army?! Slam: Yeah. And of course this happens right when my favorite wrestler King Blueberry is going to be on SCCW TV tonight. We’d better get going to the town. I have a feeling the police will be calling the Slam Boomsdexter Hotline very soon. They quickly run over to the Boomsdextermobile and get in. Slam starts it up and the old crappy truck lets out a familiar sound as they drive off. BOOOOMMMMSSSSDDDDEEEEXXXXTTTERRRRR… Slam drives the Boomsdextermobile to the nearest town which is Haines Junction. The town is silent when they arrive and most of the buildings are damaged. Groans are heard in the distance. Slam: Houston, we have a problem… * * * The next night… At the Castle of Blood, inside the Horny Mathematician’s dungeon basement lab the Mathematician and his lab assistant Igor have finished the modifications necessary to complete the final transformation of the Killer Klown into the Fourth Horseman of the apocalypse. The Mathematician cackles and points to the being formerly known as the Klown. Mathematician: He is ready. The Horseman of Death is ready to help bring about the end of the world. Only through total Death can there be peace… Death rises and looks like an even more psychotic version of his former self. However, instead of a clown costume he is wearing black armor with claws and spikes on the hands as well as on the shoulder pads. He also is now armed with a scythe and his face is different as well. The gnarled, shark like teeth are still there but his eyes are now charcoal black and his skin is now grayish in color with red markings on his cheeks that are unholy symbols for the word death. Death rises and is ready to fulfill his mission of peace through death.
