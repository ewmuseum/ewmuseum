In All Its Forms...

14 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-14 [As a house show in New Orleans is going on, the crowd is buzzing about the upcoming GTT4 matches that will see the world’s greatest role-player crowned. Just as the third match of the show is about to begin, "Not Listening" by Papa Roach begins playing and the crowd pops huge as the video screen is illuminated with the words… BLACK CHERRY Black Cherry pops up in the middle of the crowd on the floor level. Cherry looks around the crowd with a smile on her face and a can of beer in her right hand. She pops the beer open, pours it down her throat, and then spits it out around her all over the fans, who start cheering even louder. Cherry drops down and makes her way to ringside, stepping over the steel guardrail. Cherry looks into the ring with an expression of not being impressed written all over her face. Cherry then walks around the ring, pulling another can of beer out of her back right pocket. Even though we know Cherry is somewhat hardcore, this sort of behaviour is unseen. Cherry stands up on a chair and leans against the steel guardrail with her right foot as she pops open the can of beer, looking into the ring, still not looking impressed by the two male unknowns that are in the ring, ready to wrestle. Cherry chugs down the can of beer, and then tosses the can into the crowd, where it is fought over by several hardcore GTT fans. Cherry gets down, and then walks up the steel ring steps and gets into the ring. She points to the entryway, signalling that it is time for the two wrestlers to leave, but they refuse to do so. Cherry looks out into the crowd with a slightly angry, slightly surprised look on her face. Cherry goes from looking into the crowd to punching both of the guys right between their eyes. She picks up one of them and throws him over the top rope to the arena floor. She stands the other one up, kicks him in the stomach, and then gives him the Cherry Drop. Cherry rolls the unknown out of the ring and when she gets back to her feet, the ring announcer tosses a microphone into the ring and right into her right hand. The music begins to fade and the crowd starts to settle down after going nuts and cheering louder than they had all night, at the sight of Black Cherry laying a beating on the two unknown wrestlers. Cherry motions for the crowd to settle down with her arms, and then begins to speak.] Black Cherry: Well, well, it is good to be in a party-central like GTT4, because I am going to turn this damn tournament upside down and throw the biggest keggar any of you voodoo-practicing bastards have ever seen! [The crowd pops huge for that statement and start chanting "Cherry! Cherry! Cherry! Cherry! Cherry!”] Black Cherry: Now, you all know that I’m pretty rowdy, but I thought I’d kick off the GTT4 festivities by showing you a slightly different, or should I say, extended side of my persona. You all know I love an occasional bottle of Jack Daniels and drag of a cigarette, but I just thought I’d show you. I mean, there’s another side to my angle here, though. I mean, I’m fighting a guy, something I’m not amazingly used to. Little punks at the gym that tell me I’m too head-strong are no problem, but these guys are a lot bigger than me, and I’ll have to have my game on if I want to not only walk out of it as the victor, not only walk out of it in good condition, but walk out of it ready to fight in Round 2. So, I’m trying to get into the mindset of the average, or not so average male. I need to think, eat, sleep, and shit like a man if I want to succeed in not only this world, but in this tournament especially. It’s likely that a lot of my opponents will be guys other than this one, so I’m going to need to get under their skin, realise their flaws and exploit it for my own personal gain. I can play the psychological game, and I can play it a damn sight better than you, or any of the other punks, male OR female, can in this entire tournament. Yep, I’m talking about all 200-odd of them – they will all fall by my hand. Anyway, back to the point at hand. If I lose this match, I’m letting down every female out there who wants to prove that the girls can run with the guys. If I lose this match, I’m letting down everyone who has ever fought for the independence and equality of females. Mind, I’m not going soft on you now, I still only give a damn about one person, and that’s me. Looking out for number one is what’s got me as far as I am right now, and it’ll damn sure continue to make me go far in this world, and more importantly to me, in the wrestling world. I mean, this whole thing is about the jealousy of males. They’re jealous that nowadays, the girls CAN run with the guys, they can run as fast, they can throw as far, the list goes on, but perhaps the most prudent to this situation, we can wrestle as damn good also! [Some of the female audience members give Cherry perhaps the cheapest pop ever dealt in the wrestling world.] Black Cherry: The men of this world are getting scared and damn near intimidated to their wits end that the ladies are closing the gap of the male-to-female supremacy that sadly exists in this world. Dave-whatever your name is, I don’t blame you for being the way you are, it’s practically built into the genes of every man in the entire world, to want to be the dominant species on the planet. However, I’ve got news for you – the women are taking over. Like I said, the gap is being bridged every day, and a victory over you would make the step to equality that much closer. And if the other ladies in this tournament set the same example, well, then it’s all the better for us. Now a lot of people might be wondering, just how in the hell did in this tournament that supposedly houses the best people in the business when I’ve only recently made my return to the wrestling world in the DU. That is a question that deserves to be asked since I didn't win any kind of official number one contender match. But just a few weeks ago, in front of over seventy thousand people, I won the most violent, most insanely hardcore match the DU has ever seen. A match that was so hardcore, and so big in stature that it earned me superstar status. [The fans in the arena begin clapping and cheering for Cherry.] Black Cherry: I don't know how or why anyone would even allow you to enter this tournament. This isn't some title that is defended twenty four seven and used as a comedic joke. This is a serious piece of hardware that is fought for in the most extreme matches in the world, with the best of the best fighting for it. But I guess that Davey-boy doesn't hold it in that much high regard. So I guess it is up to me to show the world just how prestigious this tournament is and how big of a deal it is for me to win it. This tournament is going to see some of the most extreme battles fought over it and I am going to be the one to kick start this legacy, and kick start it in the right direction before you can take it and shit on it just like you've done with every other thing you've ever touched in your career. I'm not about to let you take a tournament and a title that is truly established and turn it into a fourth rate title, because it has been held by a fourth rate wrestler like you. So Dave, get ready to learn what the meaning of hardcore is. Get ready to learn what real hardcore wrestling is like. Get ready to learn what being in an extreme match is like. Get ready for your first time at the dance because Black Cherry is going to take you on a ride that has a one hundred percent casualty rate. At GTT4, there's no way out, and there will be no return and when all is said and done, one more time it is going to be Black Cherry getting one hand raised, with a title being placed one step closer in the other. And you can take that to the bank! [Cherry drops the mic and as "Not Listening" by Papa Roach comes back on and the crowd pops huge for The Alpha Female once again. Cherry goes to each side of the ring and points into the crowd to get them to cheer even louder and to see which side of the arena is cheering the loudest. The right side of the arena is the side that she sees is cheering louder than the others, so she gets out of the ring, hops over the steel guardrail, and exits the arena through the right side of the arena. Cherry stops about halfway out and then finally leaves the arena with some of the fans following her out to a local bar.]
