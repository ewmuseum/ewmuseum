A new path, thanks to a strange guide.

14 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-14 “Where are you?” Serpenti thought as he stared into the bottle of tequila. Kitty Mac had vanished in the night a few months back. About the same time Jackson Namenlos Duisturnis vanished from sight… Since that time he has not been the same… Until now… Another drink trying to flush the thought out of his mind. “I can not. IT is wrong.” Serpenti slurred. Serpenti sat at the bar located in a small Mexican restaurant and cantina. From the looks of it its was in the middle of the busy hours. Serpenti’s plate sat there barely touched but the bottle of tequila sat there half empty. A old man with a black and gray goatee walked over. “Is every thing okay sir?” The man said. Strangely the people were not shocked of the fact that Serpenti was wearing his ever present mask.. This one however was finless and a solid dark green color with two black snakes on the side of the face. Serpenti rolled his head to look at the old man. “Yes… Well No… Well I don’t know. Let me ask you this amigo. Have you ever been in love with a woman that you could not touch?” Serpenti asked. “Hummm. Let me think… Umm no. “ Said the old man nodding his head “Well I…. No worries. How much do I owe?” Serpenti asked as he staggered to his feet. “It will be thirty dollars Sir.” Said the old man handing Serpenti his bill. Serpenti held up a hand and then laid down a fifty-dollar bill and started staggering away. “Sir.. your change!” The old man called out. “Keep it amigo… I have plenty.” Serpenti called back as he walked out the door. Thunder sounded in the distance as the wind started to blow… However Serpenti did not pay the signs of a looming storm any signs. Serpenti staggered away from the cantina lost in thought, slowly heading to his destination… The beach. The long pier stood visible still as the sun began to set behind the ocean… And at the end of the pie was a large old styled ship. Serpenti had spent a lot of money rebuilding that ship… And this was El dragón tres del mar’s (The sea dragon: three’s) first voyage. Serpenti sat down on the pier and slowly took another drink of the tequila. Trying to place the sinful thought’s in his head to the back of his mind. All he wanted to do was lock them away and toss the key to Davie Jones’s locker. But to no avail. “Gaspar you stupid fool. She is married… and not to just any man, but to a fellow Luchador. A man who trust you. Just forget it!” A familiar voice said to Serpenti… Serpenti rubbed his eyes…. But to no avail… standing in front of Serpenti stood a monkey. Serpenti looked at the bottle in his hand and dropped it into the sand. “Okay… A mucho tequila para mí. (To much tequila for me.) Serpenti said rubbing his eyes again. However the eye rubbing did not cause the small white and light blue monkey to vanish… Instead the monkey just laughed. “Ohh I see… You think I am created by that bottle you consumed…. HAHAHAH! That’s funny” The monkey said still laughing. Soon the laughter stopped and the monkey looked back at Serpenti. “ NO! Sorry I am not going away that easy” “Okay.. but there is no way your real…. “ Serpenti said. Quickly getting a slap to the face… “Ouch!! Why did you do that?” Serpenti asked. “Because I needed to show you I am real… At least to you I am… Why I got sent to you I have no clue.” The monkey said. Serpenti’s mouth opened and closed in disbelief of what was happening. Serpenti had seen many strange days in his travels around the world… but never a talking monkey. “Excuse me. But you cannot be real. Monkeys do not go about talking.“ Serpenti said forcing his self to sober up. The monkey picked up a stick and walked about and then pointed the stick at Serpenti. “Well your are right. Normal monkeys don’t go around talking. In-fact that can not talk at all.. Except those damn chimpanzees and a few apes. But that does not matter. For I Eulb Yeknom, I am a sprit guide and some one assigned me to you. I told them that you would fare better with a lizard snake or a dragon.. But no you get me.” The Monkey Eulb Yeknom said tapping Serpenti’s head with the stick. “I see… I think. So who has assigned you to be my sprit guide?” Serpenti asked holding his head as the onset of to much tequila started taking effect. “That I can not say.” Eulb Yeknom said shaking his head no. “But why. What harm can there be If I knew who sent you.” Serpenti asked sounding very confused. Eulb Yeknom snapped his fingers and a brown leather books with green and red text across the front saying; A Sprit’s guide to sprit guiding, appeared in front of him… The book its self was bigger then Eulb his self. Eulb opened the book up with a bit of difficulty and taped the first page with his stick. “Page one… DO NOT REVEAL THE OUR IDENTITY!!! YOUR BOSSES!” Eulb said out loud slamming the stick on the page. Eulb snapped his fingers again and the book disappeared. “But..” Serpenti started “NO! I can’t do it Gaspar. If I did… I would be fired and sent off into the void. Not a good place to spend my retirement.” Eulb Yeknom said crossing his furry little arms across his chest to make the point clear. “Okay. If you are my guide… then guide me. How can I not be true to my heart? What should I do?” Serpenti asked as he stood up. “ Because right now I am lost.” Serpenti said kicking some sand with his boot. “I know. But Juan trusts you. If you truly do feel you are in love with this Teresa. Then you will wait. Her mask will be revealed when she is ready to reveal it.” Eulb said with a nod of his head. “What are you talking about… She does not have a mask” Serpenti shouted down ad Eulb. “Every one has masks Serpenti. Not all are as visible as the one you have on, but they are there. If you care, just be a friend right now. They need that right now more then anything.” Eulb said nodding while scratching his chin. “I see. There is a point to your wisdom… Sadly I feel like the fabled Lancelot. Doomed to love a woman I can never touch.” Serpenti said with a sigh of defeat. “Captain Serpenti… The ship is ready to leave. A storm is approaching and we must leave and head back to American if we plan on getting there in time.” Sounded the voice of Serpenti’s first mate. A burley man built like a stone. His Asian features very apparent as his thin moustache and goatee hung down past his chin. “Very good Jago. We shall leave this moment.” Serpenti said stepping onto the pier. “Sir. Who were you talking to, if I may ask? ” Jago said. “I was talking to Eulb Yeknom the monkey.” Serpenti said spinning around finding nothing in the spot Eulb was standing. “Monkey? I see. Very well my captain.” Jago said unsure… Jago quickly headed to the ship muttering about being drunk during a storm was not a good thing. Serpenti stood there looking in disbelief.. “Could I have really been… “ Serpenti started to say before the thunder boom ended all thoughts. Serpenti headed to the ship with haste as the sails began to raise filling with air. Serpenti stepped onto the ship as the wooden ramp was pulled up. Serpenti turned looking back at the beach as the ship pulled out of the harbor. Serpenti shook his head and then headed to his cabin. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Serpenti sat at his desk. Lying in front of him was the official invitation to the Primetime Central CW2 tournament. Dear Mr. Serpenti, It is my greatest satisfaction to inform you that you have been selected top participate with some of the greatest cruiserweights from around the world. Congratulations. The event will be held in different locations around the world as well. You will be informed as to the location of your match up in a later letter. Your opponent however can be revealed to you. You shall be facing Skylar Kelly, from WMW. Once again congratulations and good luck. Sincerely PTC chairman Argyle Von Smackdown. Serpenti folded the latter and placed it back into the golden envelope it arrived in. Serpenti took a long sip of his Strawberry-Banana cream juice and leaned back in his chair with a smile and a sigh.. “Finally” Serpenti said slowly. This would be his second chance to prove his self to those out side of Global’s walls. A chance he desired so much.. Serpenti’s musings ended abruptly as the fax machine began again as a message came in…. Training Meeting: La Herencia de Herederos invite you to meet them at Global Gym in the Global Arena and Casino in Las Vegas. This meeting is for training to prepare for the six-man tag team match set to happen on Evolution. Meeting time eight o’clock a.m. tomorrow Noi Tsunami Serpenti looked at the paper and nodded… Still curious to the monkey he had encountered in Mexico two days ago. Serpenti shook his head to clear his thought as he picked up the phone and waited…. “Hello amigo Eagleclaw!. It is El Serpenti… Si. I need information…. Si. The information I need is files and studies on a Skylar Kelly, from promotion called Wrestling Midwest. Si! Gracias. Serpenti hung up the phone and stood up and walked onto the balcony… Setting under the ocean was a magnificent sunset. Serpenti bowed his head in silence as the last rays of sunlight vanished. Once again the fax machine began printing…. Serpenti walked over to the paper as he came out of the fax machine. Everything about Skylar Kelly that could be found was printed on the sheet including a picture of his self. Serpenti ripped the last print out off and smiled. He slowly turned walked out of the library of his home reading as he did. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Serpenti climbed into the waiting RV. The driver climbed in following him. Followed by Jago Tamura. “Where to boss?” Asked the driver as he took his seat. “We are going to Las Vegas… James.” Serpenti said heading to the back. James nodded and turned on the engine as Serpenti laid back in his bed in the back. Jago sat next to Serpenti’s door keeping a watchful eye out. The RV. Pulled off and out of the California home of El Serpenti. Serpenti slowly drifted off to sleep… dreaming of the days of his youth… the days he spent with his parents… And for the first night in a long night he slept in peace. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
