The life and death of a martyr…

4 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-04 CODED: Bold-Axel's speech Italics-Action and Narration Underline-Pedestrian's speech The scene opens up in a park in the middle of Chicago, Illinois, home of the UWF. It's raining 'cats and dogs' out, and there is nobody roaming the streets, due to it being 1:30 in the morning. The camera pans around a bit, revealing a man sitting on the bench in the middle of the park. This man is hunched over, staring at the palms of his hands. His jet black hair is hanging down, water dripping from the tips of it. Rainwater can be seen rolling off of his leather trenchcoat and onto the ground, puddling at his feet. He speaks almost as if he's psychic and knows the camera is there. Life… God's way of kicking you square in the balls. Just when you think things couldn't get any worse, they do. Just when you think the odds couldn't be anymore against you, they are. Just when you think you couldn't be any lower on the 'totem pole', guess what… You are. You see, life has a way of kicking people when they're down. Especially my life. When I was nine, I lost my parents due to a bank robbery gone awry. They were at the bank, trying to withdraw some money from their bank account so that they could buy groceries wtih which to feed myself and my brother, Ace. All of a sudden, a couple of idiots ran into the bank, pointing their high-caliber handguns and threatening to kill anyone who didn't comply with their orders and give all of their valuables and money. Due to my parents believing that they had to do whatever it took to feed us, they refused to give their money to the robbers. Those robbers murdered them in cold blood. It was hard to cope with. My brother, Ace, had to go to school, work a job, and support me until I was old enough to fend for myself. That's when we began training ourselves to become professional wrestlers. Professional wrestling was something that he and I had always wanted to do. We wanted to break into the business together. Seeing as how he was bigger and stronger than me, he was going to be the technician of our tag team, whereas I was going to be the high-flier. He chuckles a bit. It turns out that not only was I a better technician than he was, but I was also a better high-flier than he was. So he took up the 'Power' style of wrestling. We trained for six years, and once we had perfected our craft, we made our way to a company known as OWA, or Omega Wrestling Alliance, as it's known by most. It was owned and operated by Karl Zarn. He later sold it over to his cousin, 'Shotgun' Stoney Wilde, who at one point was a sort of 'mentor' to me. That's another story for another time, though. Ace and I wrestled for several different organizations. We finally came across a federation known as UWF. Ace and I watched and scouted for a few weeks, looking at all the competition. Ace decided to wrestle somewhere else, because he claimed the UWF's antics were too 'silly' for him. At that point, we went our separate ways. That's all I'm going to say about that, though. Fast forward three years into the future. Ace and I were wrestling as a tag team on the indy circuit. As you all know, we were known as Action Packed. Anyhow, one of the rookies on the other team attempted to plant Ace with a Brainbuster. He botched it and murdered my brother. Axel sighs a little as he speaks again. This is where I deem it necessary to speak of death. Many people think of death as a release. A savior, of sorts. I look at it as God's way of telling you that he's better than you and that he can do whatever the Hell he wants to you. It's his way of torturing us like ants under a magnifying glass. That's my take on it, and I'm sure most of you won't agree with me, but you know, I couldn't care less. The dictionary defines 'death' as the act of dying, the termination of life, the state of being dead, the cause of dying, a manner of dying. It also defines 'death' as a personification of the destroyer of life, usually represented as a skeleton holding a scythe. When Ace died, a part of me died with him. That's why I am who I am today. Axel pulls out a dagger from the pocket of his trenchcoat. He digs the blade of it into the palm of his hand and drags it across, making a gash in it. Blood begins to pour from the cut in his hand and fall into the puddle of water at his feet. The water turns red. Blood, the one thing that all living beings, human and animal alike, share in common. Contrasted to all of our differences, blood is the one thing that is the same in all of us. It doesn't matter who you are. It doesn't matter if you're white, black, Asian, Hispanic or mixed, it's the same in all of us. Some blood is just darker than others, because some hearts are as cold as ice and black as night. He holds his hand out so that the camera can focus on the palm of it. The blood is still pouring out. You see this blood? This blood that is now dripping from the palm of my hand is the same blood that I bleed night in and night out for each and every one of you. You people need a savior. You people need someone who will bleed for you… Someone who will die for you. I am just the man to do that. Unlike some people around here, I'll put my body, my health, and my life on the line just to win this tournament. I'll give everything I've got until I've got nothing left to give, and it's all for the fans. I doubt that my opponent for my Round 1 match would do that. No, I KNOW that Kaden Calix can't and won't do that. I have nothing to lose, but everything to gain. I'm like El Diablo, Beelzebub, Lucifer, Satan, or whatever the Hell you want to call him. You can lock me away, block me out, pray for me to leave you alone, throw crucifixes and Holy Water at me, but I keep coming back. I will persevere, and I will be persistant. You cannot stop me. You will never be able to. I may not draw on the power of demons and fallen angels, but I do draw on something more important. I draw on the energy and support of these fans. These fans are the lifeblood of professional wrestling. Without them, this business would be nothing. Without them, I would be nothing. A lot of the guys in this tournament are nothing either way, so that really doesn't matter. At that moment, some guy who is dumb enough to be walking in the rain at this time of the morning walks straight through the camera's view. Axel calls out to him. Hey, you! Come here. I've got some questions to ask you. The man turns and looks at Axel. Who, me? There's no one else here, is there? If you answer that, you're an idiot, because it's a rhetorical question. Sorry, what are your questions? Do you follow professional wrestling? Yes, I do. Why do you ask? Axel lifts his head for the first time in this promo. Do you know who I am? Holy shit!!! You're Axel Action! Yes, I am. Have you been following GTT5? Yes, I have. Your opponent, Kaden Calix, doesn't stand a chance against you. I've been watching you compete for the last five years, and I must say you're one of my favorite wrestlers. Thank you, but that's not what I wanted to ask you. I wanted to ask if you knew who Kaden Calix is, because I sure don't. Well, he's relatively unknown to me also, but I do know that he's twenty-three and still lives with his mother. They both burst out laughing hysterically. After a couple minutes, Axel regains his composure and speaks again. That's hilarious. You're joking, right? Not at all, my friend, not at all. Well, that's all I wanted to know. Okay. Can I have your autograph? I have both a pen and paper in my pocket. Sure. What is your name? It's Jeff. At that time, Axel signs his autograph onto the sheet of paper. It reads, "To Jeff, my # 1 fan. From, Axel Action". He hands it to the guy and the guy thanks him furiously. Axel looks back into the camera as the man walks off. You know, you can tell a lot about a guy based on his current living conditions. It appears that Kaden Calix is a 'momma's boy'. He's twenty-three years old and still living with his mom. I can't really make fun of him, though, because at least he still has a mom. That's beside the point, though. Calix, in a short time, you and I will be squaring off in that ring to determine who moves on to round two in GTT. I'm going to tell you something right now. I won't hesitate to hurt myself in order to hurt you. I won't hesitate to shatter your mind, body, and spirit just to move on in this tournament. In closing, I leave you with this short, literary masterpiece. THE INITIATE Which way is the right path, as I stand upon this chaotic crossroad of hate… How many ways are there to roam on this dark and damned road of Fate… "There are many ways, my son, to find where the souls of Demons remain… But it takes only one second of despair and of doubt until at last, your soul, they will gain… Inherit these lands, these things, these dreams that are yours, forever, to adore… For there is no life, in the depths of chaos, my son, for you to explore…" -C. Vincent Metzen [FADE…]
