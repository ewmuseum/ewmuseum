No Price Too High: Part I

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 I walked through the muck of an abandoned house and made it to the arena. People whispered to each other asking each other whom is this guy wrestling Kash. I just muttered angrily to myself about getting the respect I deserve. I came through for myself though, and that is what matters. I took my opportunity and finally made something of it with a sound victory. I had been isolated for a few days but there was only one thing on my mind: success. It did not matter what it had do with because it is a broad statement that I wished to use in every aspect of my deteriorating life. I had to drink that elixir and gain years back on my life. I had one thing I had to do. It had been bothering me for a long time. I had to get my son back from that whorish mother of his. That bitch that stole him from me, that bitch whose family got me into the drug trafficking business, that bitch whose family would love to put a slug deep within the temple of my skull. I married into a psycho Italian family who had dreams of being the next Gotti’s. The vultures told me that to be allowed in the family I had to play my part. I had to use my plane expertise to get them the drugs they wanted so they could ruin the youths on the New York streets. I did it… It was the beginning of the end and after a mere two years our marriage was over but not before she conceived a son. MY son, the child that I hoped would end all the nonsense. Alas, it made me ‘soft,’ to them and when I wasn’t doing their bidding I was looking after Angelo Rey while the bitch was snorting up anything she could steal from her family. That is how it all started but unfortunately, when I was finally cut loose I did not let every line be cut. I was an impressionable young man and I never realized that I had been used until I was so far in, that my only work experience was organized crime. It is a sad and annoying story to think back on but I have only myself to blame. Now it is time to resurrect MY life. It is mine! It is not the cartels, it is not my former families, and it is not some hyped up uninspiring wrestlers. Disregard me all you want----I am use to it like children are use to the feel of Michael Jackson’s hand on their unmentionables. It is time to rule. ==== I counted the cash in my briefcase and laughed softly to myself. It was quite the profit I had made on the drug deal. Juan had given me a lot of drugs to import into New York and I did it. I did the last job unfortunately for Juan Carlos I did not return. Instead I had over five hundred thousand dollars in a brown briefcase with a number lock on it. It was a brilliant rouse if not extremely dangerous but to get out from under them I had to give them a black eye. I had to show them I was not scared and that the money I took was now MINE. “And some left---for a house,” I said seriously. My first purchase was a black 2004 Mercedes E55 AMG. It put me back about 85,000 dollars with all the extras I put in it. I felt like a new man driving towards the home of my ex-wife and my son. This was retribution and I was going to live it up on the down low. I am not ignorant. I realize they will come after me, but for now I want to enjoy seeing my son for the first time in years. --- “Here I am.” KNOCK-KNOCK-KNOCK Gonzalo Rey stands still putting his hand down to his side as he waits for his knock to be answered. Gonzalo is extremely nervous as he continually taps his foot on the concrete porch before the door. It had been four years since he had seen his son since his ex-wife would not let him anywhere near him. It also did not help matters that her family would have loved to take him down so it was dangerous and near impossible for him to visit. Last time Rey saw his son, his son was three years old and all Rey could think of is how much things could change in four years especially since his son was now seven. “Who is it?” a small voice said sounding worried. Rey took a step forward and smiled to himself, “hey, Angelo?” he asked cautiously. “How did you know that?” the voice responds. Gonzalo nods and tries fixing his hair, “I have not seen you in a long time but I know my sons voice when I hear it,” Gonzalo says excited. The door cracks a little and Angelo looks out of the crack and looks up at the excited Gonzalo Rey. Gonzalo smiles and waves a little and pulls out a toy from his behind his back and Angelo looks up at Rey and then throws the door open. He runs out and hugs Gonzalo and then punches him a few times as tears stream down his face. “THEY TOLD ME YOU WERE DEAD!” he screams. Gonzalo tosses the old toy to the ground and hugs his son back and pats him on the back trying to make him calm down. “They lied, son,” Gonzalo says angrily. He wipes his grimace away and smiles, “but do not worry because I am back and I have never-ever forgotten about you. I do not want to make up excuses for why I haven’t visited but just know that every day all I can think about is getting you back,” Gonzalo says. Angelo looks up at his father and sniffs ending his tears. “Where have you been?” he asks. Gonzalo sighs, “in a bad place, son. When you are older I will explain everything to you but I doubt we have a lot of time to talk when I see your mothers car is in the driveway,” Rey says quickly. Angelo nods and points to his Justice League T-shirt, “you like my shirt, dad? Batman is the coolest,” he says. Gonzalo nods, “I was always a Batman fan also,” he says. “More importantly though…where is your mom?” he asks. Angelo shrugs, “she is sleeping. I went in and she was sleeping and she had chalk dust next to her and I don’t like chalk dust so I did not bother and then you knocked and now I am happy,” he says like only a kid can. Gonzalo takes in all the info and laughs, “and?” he says with a laugh. “I am glad you don’t like chalk dust, son because I would like it if you would stay away from it forever,” Gonzalo says knowing that the chalk dust is his ex-wife’s coke, her being an addict and all. “What if I have to write on a chalk board?” his son asks. Gonzalo nods and wipes some nervous sweat from his brow, “just don’t play with your moms, ok? Promise me!” he says. Angelo looks down sadly. “What’s wrong?” Gonzalo asks. “You are leaving again…aren’t you?” he asks. Gonzalo shakes his head, “no, no. I don’t want to leave, Angelo. I want you to come with me and never come back here again but that is your decision and nothing is easy,” Gonzalo says seriously as he rubs the top of Angelo’s head. “Leave mom?” he asks. “Yea,” Gonzalo responds. “I know it isn’t hard but she and her family won’t let me be a part of your life so this is all I have left to do or can do,” he says. Angelo leans against the door to the house and thinks to him self, as he stares from inside his house to the car parked outside that Gonzalo came in. “Where would we go?” Angelo asks confused at what is going on. Gonzalo nods and begins scratching a cut over his let eye and then grimaces, “we will find our own slice of heaven in this hypocritical world,” he says seriously. Angelo’s eyes get wide, “you’re bleeding!” he says surprised. Gonzalo moves his hand in front of his face and watches as a drop of blood drips from it. He shakes his hand and then grabs a handkerchief from his back pocket and puts it on the cut over his eye and holds it there hoping to stop the bleeding. “It is just a little injury from the job,” he says with a laugh. “What were you doing?” his son says curiously. Gonzalo takes the handkerchief off and pats his son the back, “I was wrestling,” he says. “Wow,” Angelo responds. “You wrestle like Violence Jack and Angelo Deville?” he asks amazed. Gonzalo pauses and thinks to him self, “well…kind of,” he says. His son looks confused up at him, “what do you mean?” he asks. Gonzalo sighs and then smiles trying to not show his disappointment in never really being a famous and successful wrestler. “Well I am wrestling in a worldwide tournament with them but I am not considered a successful and accomplished wrestler,” he says. “Why not?” Gonzalo clears his throat and looks around not only worried about being seen but also becoming flustered with questions of his success. “My career has been in Mexico and thus the American public could care less about me, no matter if I can do things people could only imagine of. I never was much a star in Mexico either. I loved what I did though and just because I wasn’t winning titles every night, I was putting on a good show,” Gonzalo finishes with pride. “You are in a tournament now!” Angelo says joyfully. Gonzalo laughs, “yea and I am going to prove to everyone that your dad is no push over,” he says. “Can I come?” his son asks. Gonzalo nods, “of course but there is no going back,” he responds seriously. Angelo thinks again and then slumps his shoulders, “can I say bye to mom?” he asks. Gonzalo looks around and then at his watch. He takes a deep breath and begins thinking about how to respond to a valid question. It isn’t that he doesn’t think it is the right thing to do but it will also get the finger pointing directly at him real quickly. “You can write a note but no talking to her because then the lies about me being dead will become a reality, son,” he says forcefully. His son nods, “ok,” he says quickly. “Well hurry up and grab some clothes and things you will need but do not worry if you forget anything because I can buy you new stuff if need be,” Gonzalo says seriously. Angelo turns around and runs back into his house to get the essentials and write his note to his mother. Gonzalo watches with pride in his eyes as his he smiles at his son running hard to get everything he needs. He looks behind him and watches as a few cars drive by but none of them are threats to him and he just turns back around and begins to fidget with his shirt as he continues to wait. His patience is running thin at this time but it is only because of the danger he is. It is all worth it to have his son back though. “Even if I don’t survive all of this, this one moment has made it all worthwhile,” Gonzalo whispers to himself. Fade === They call me the assassin… I have been cordially invited to defeat every member of WWA in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Sorry I did not RSVP but I think there are more important things then respecting “Flying Brian Calvin. For instance, I had to brush my teeth—two times today and that isn’t counting the two hours I took to nap between escaping with my son and driving out of New York. This is not about being accepted anymore. This is my new business and in this business if you are disrespected as often as I am--you get sweet revenge. The odds makers seem to think that I am once again the underdog. I am not a compulsive gambler so I have yet to bet against me yet that seems to be the ‘safest,’ bet. Bah. It is my job to offer bitch slaps to everyone who doubts me. It is that simple and trust me doing it is even simpler against you ingrates. I see peons banging at my door proclaiming they will defeat me with weak on liners and promo’s with training as the subject. Did I happen upon a time warp when I was flying into America last week? Well damn…pay me sluts because you obviously do not see this amazing discovery of mine. Ha. One line insults like ha are far more engrossing then a WWA promo(period). I really do not care what federation my opponent is from but it would seem I am constantly fed these guys. That is fine because every person in this tourney is an enemy of mine. Your fed? Meaningless to me. I wrestle without prejudice. Your name? Meaningless to me. I mutilate without prejudice. Your hype? Quiet to me. I murder without prejudice. I do not give a’ flying’ fuck about any of those because I am not intimidated. I do not know if you people realize but I am rather confident that I am better then you all. Brian Calvin is no different. He is just a man a lot like me other then he is a dyslexic hillbilly with no chance at beating me. I guess we don’t have that much in common after all. He is the future of the WWA—I on the other hand might not have a future past next week. I guess he has a security blanket; then again, it is hard to feel safe when you have made two powerfully insane enemies in the course of two days. This is an edgier Gonzalo Rey. I have found a backbone and I am no longer taking shit from anyone. If you think the things I say are insulting, too bad I don’t care and I think you should look into getting a harder shell for your crumbling ego. Do not fret if in a cognitive state of a dream that your dream turns into a nightmare with my hand bludgeoning into a bloody mess. It is just a future occurrence you can look up and be like “damn, déjà vu.” I will gain your goddamn respect and in the process use your defeats as my stepping stones. Now ponder my truthful words and leave the hell alone, morons. Gonzalo Rey > Brian Calvin= No freaking doubt. End
