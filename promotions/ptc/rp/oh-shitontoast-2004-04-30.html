Oh Shitontoast

30 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-30 1 ‘Aaaargh,’ she yelped at the darkness that covered her like a well made cape, showing little pieces of electricity licking at her body from each side, up and down. Her fists were clenched in bright orbs of blue as she strained to keep the spark flowing from left to right. Another effort stressed grunt cleared the humid, sweaty air right down the centre for the tiniest of moments before she fell face first to the ground. “Damn…it,” her second came out with an exhale of breath and a mighty kick to the leg rest of her white sofa, an emphasis at the very base of anger. Her knees bent downwards as though a great weight had disembarked upon her person, one that she was just barely holding. Her wrists came up from her hips and were held out adjacently in front of her increasingly bright body. Her frustration grew as her body dimmed down back to normal human capacity, her knees met with the ground in a kneeling position. All this tied in to her inability to control her emotions. If she couldn’t even handle a flimsy light generation mojo with a demon’s heart imbedded into stone, how could she ever Hope to beat the likes of Syko Arsonist to make it through to the next round of the Primetime Central Tournament? A popping sound, similar to that of a broken light bulb, signified the end of her tether. Her hopes had diminished with greater worries for her friend; one pretty hate machine to be precise. It’s all good and well trying to run a body on hatred, but when that’s all you’re living on, you’re not going to experience the pleasures of life that Carys was quickly beginning to forget Hope for. The only one mercy she is granted is to take that aggression and cleanse her soul every week, inside and outside of the roped ring. She wondered - with deep longing embedded in her crystal blue eyes – what it would be like to go out and party, perhaps even to let lose and get drunk? She conjectured about the possibility of having less training fighting demons, or shooting people good old ‘Il Mafioso’ deemed unsuitable to see another dawn on this miserable earth, and more time pressing weights with her back flat against the bench, alas things don’t always work out that way. Yet a certain thing is always expected of her. She was expected to give it her all like every other person who wrestled for small amounts of cash, in the hope that one day – perhaps in the distant future – she would be able to make it big, extra fights were just a fast track for her, she guessed as she folded her arms in her own private hissy fit. 2 It hadn’t been much of a sleep for him anyway, his body writhing for what seemed like an eternity, even upon the comfort ridden sheets that covered Carys’ bed. The yelp from Carys in the front room had been the nail in the coffin for that nights sleep, and he used the term lightly. His body weight shifted from a rested position, to one that had him perched n the side of the wrought iron bed. His hands clasped the mattress with frustration, things had moved from too hot, to too cold, to just plain sweaty. His hand went up to his face to shield the tired jade eyes that filled the hollows of his sockets. He wearily looked at the gap in the door that looked like it was lit by a fluorescent light. His eyes soon adjusted and he gawked, bemusement screwing up his face. He looked through that tiny gap that had found itself between door and frame to see Carys sitting in frustration upon the floor, knees tucked under the rest of her body with slight wonder. She had been this way since she got back from Chryste’s the week before. Yeah, that was when he had realised how insecure Carys was about the demon powers she tames; he didn’t need his telepathic gifts to tell him, either. Sure she was worried, deeply doubting herself as she prepared to face a seasoned veteran as she was little more than a rookie. But you’d have to be a blind incompetent fool not to understand that most of her problems. Inside and outside of the ring all spiralled down to the fact that she felt she had no life anymore. Everything consumed time that she should have been taking out to get trashed, fucked off of her face on assorted beers, drugs and living up the typical life. Josh turned around and was about to give up on waking up, before he heard Carys fight back to her feet, stressing under her breath- amusing how she still didn’t want to wake him, her fists banged together, reacting like a negative and positive battery cable…spark erupted across the room. “You’re going about it all wrong,” he told her from behind the door, deciding that sleep on a night like this one simply wasn’t going to happen. 3 “Sorry to wake ya there, little’un,” she muttered as she sat back down on the sofa, the way her body was burning up with electricity only minutes before it wouldn’t have been an unreasonable assumption that there would be scorch marks on the clear white of the furniture, yet there were none…and Carys really could have cared less anyway. She span on the plush leather seat to face the boy who was barely awake and padding furiously at his sleepy eyes with the heels of his hands. “Now,” she patted the seat next to the boy invitingly, “what are you talking about?” The kid did as was suggested and sprawled across the sofa, resting his back lazily upon the bottom cushion of the sofa. “Well,” he started matter-of-factly, the way he meant to go on no less, “when you’re using your gifts you’re supposed to relax, find a good feeling inside of you and hold onto it.” While he spoke he adjusted Carys’ legs and arms into a meditating pose with surprising ease. She scanned him through the side of her eyes as he carried on in his tired tone, sparing a yawn here and there. “You got that thought?” he smiled, flashing his white teeth, when Carys returned a simple nod. “Who’s Tara Reid?” Carys felt a warm tingle of blood spread through her cheeks as she tried to shake it off. “What did I tell you about that? Y’know I can’t do this.” Carys went to get up from the seat but Josh placed a reassuring hand upon hers. “No, I don’t even know if I should have these powers – or gifts, whatever – I killed despicable creatures, that’s a gift to the world. I don’t want these damned terrible-” “-Stop!” Josh shook his head. “If you weren’t meant to have them – TAME them – then you wouldn’t. But as it stands, as I see you…you are, so quit moanin’.” Carys growled and straightened the posture in her back, her back popped and her eyes closed; she hated that sound, her head shook, fatigue apparently going with it as her back came back into place. “Okay, feel the spot where your power emanates from,” he subconsciously reassured her by adding his own emphasis on ‘your’ as it passed through his Hispanic lips. “Now breathe from it, with it…keep your mind on the happiness.” Josh nodded as her power began to seep. Not in the form of electricity, not in the form of her other elementally captured magic – one which she had even less control over – water. What started off as a growl finished in: “Goddammit!” she fell back down onto the couch. “You had it then. What happened?” josh looked more worried than irritated, not that was an easy task, but his eyes never left Carys. The seventeen sensation slammed her fists into her knees, “I can’t stop thinking about this fuck-err stupid match that comes up this week, Syko Arsonist is god like in the ring compared to me, the rookie. I don’t have any serious competition under my belt, just the goddamned seventeen year old chick that had enough gusto to take the Eclipse Title from Karina Wolfenden.” “You hacked a clean run out in DWO…8-0-0 I do believe.” Josh had no actual idea his self, but he remembered going across it when scanning her memories. “Yeah, beginner’s luck, I need to get some real wind under my sales before that match, and I can’t even fuck-err can’t even FOCUS!” Interrupted mid sentence, Carys slammed her hand down upon the handle of the busily bleeping phone and brought it up to her ear, retardedly up side down. “Dammit….yeah. Who is it calling at this time at night?” Her voice was clear, and her fingers idol enough to slam down on the loudspeaker button, so needless to say when she shouted, someone got an ear full of angry seventeen year old. "You know me, Carys." A calm, and handsome man's voice spoke from the other side, it's accent rich of Italian. "It's me, Jackie Rizzo." “Eh?” she looked down at her hands, like they held the answer, the person’s name certainly rang a bell with her, but she just couldn’t think why…then it hit her, “oh! OH! No, no, no! Not right now Jackie, this is a bad time. Bad, bad, bad, bad, bad, bad time! Your timing sucks, SUCKS!” "Heh..." Jackie retorted over the phone. "Sucks or not, I'm pretty sure it's fuckin' important, or I'd probably not be calling anyways. Ya figure that too kiddo?" “I figure you’re being a patronising mmphmmph.” The last part would have been fucker, but Josh had his hand in front of her mouth in time for her not to have her head cut off. “Fine, why don’t you just spill it; I’ll do what I can. As always.” There was a slight pause from the other end. And then following what seemed like a frustrated sigh, Jackie responded. "I want you to intercept something for me... a shipment being sent to Chryssie. Pay's great, more than you'll make in that pissy wrestling gig in a week. It'll only take an hour or two, if you're as good as Don Cortolli says you are. Can you do it?" “This shipment, would it be the powdered white stuff that isn’t snow?” Carys questioned, she’d already tossed about five thousand dollars worth of that crap from her bedroom window last week, and she didn’t want to have to be blamed for doing it again; not to her face at least. "You either learn quickly, or you're the one who made it snow outside her window..." Jackie's voice had an heir of amusement to it. "That's exactly what I'm talking about, Carys. Rico tipped me off on a big case of it coming in for her. She gets it through some bitch named Scrie down at this Goth club called Darkening." There was another cautious slight pause. "It's in Milano." “Both, and you and your Dons need to keep their ears to the ground long enough to learn I’m lookin’ after a sprog,” she gave a playful ruffle of his long matted hair and went back to the conversation, “now.” Carys sighed and checked her finger nails; painted clearly but with enough paint on it to stop her chewing the shit outta them. “Is this the kinda thing that needs them painting on the alley’s when I’m done?” "Well..." Jackie responded in a calm manner. "Chrys and Scrie, they've known each other a while, and this Scrie, she's connected; so I don't advise it. But hey, if it's her life or yours, you make the right fuckin' decision yeah?" “I’m no fuckin’ retard, you can be damned sure that she’s going to be hit hard whatever the cost. Oh,” Carys had almost pressed the hang up button, “…Jackie, make sure you transfer the cash to my trust fund, I don’t wanna go blowin’ all my cash now, do I?” Across the phone, the deep laugh of the youngest Don was heard, before he eventually spoke. "Will do, Carys, oh, and no need for Chrys to find out that I'm looking after her best interests, okay? I'm sure she'd be right pissed at us both for this one, but I'm not gonna have my bride unable to even stand up at the wedding." “Of course, I don’t want her near that shit anymore than you do.” Carys stifled a giggle back with her hand, as she stared at her ten year old buddy mocking Jackie’s laughter, “I’ll confirm things in a few hours, then.” Carys slammed her hand into her head as she realised he didn’t have any true idea of how she was really going to get there. "Good... I look forward to good news. As far as what to do with the shipment...feed it to some fish." He paused momentarily, before continuing. "Oh, and if you need a lift, get a hold of Juno the Fixer, he's quick." “Y’know it’d be a lot better business if I dropped it at Juno’s and let him find a retailer, you’d get a hefty return.” Carys turned to look over the back of the sofa, the kid had flashed out of her sight the way he often did, lord only knows why. “I’ll find my own way there, trust me.” She eventually answered, as Josh flickered back into her sight, fully dressed and backpack donned. “Well kid, looks like C’s got some baby sittin’ to do,” Josh furrowed his brows, like they had been dragged down the middle of his face by a loose piece of skin. He’d been lookin forward to the possibility of getting a little fight. "Ha! She's gonna love that!" Jackie responded in an amused manner. "Good luck kid, don't do anything to get you wasted." And with that, a click was heard, Jackie ended the call. 4 “Semi’s…” she patted her waist, welcoming the distraction of doing a job for Jackie, now she didn’t think about having to fight the man who once faced Angelo Deville in the ring, the same man who won the No Limits Battle Royal, and there she was, thinking about him again. “Pistol’s,” she patted the chest of her black trim leather jacket, and the right foot of her high boots. There was nothing quite like a good ambush to take your mind off a man who squared up solidly with many a fed’s best fighters and wrestlers. That’s what she was going to do, attack mercilessly, whether Jackie would approve or not. “Knives,” while she was knelt down on the floor she checked her remaining boot, the handle of the knife sticking out enough for an easy retrieval. Of course there was a plural to her speech, and the second would be the one that lay in the lining of her right forearm, it would be her saviour in the snap of a wrist. Another shake of the blood in her head to chase away the Cruiser Tournament and she was ready. Josh still looked at her like she was clinically insane. “Okay, talk me through this…one last time?” Josh did, and soon Carys was floating, brought into that position by her power. Carys opened her eyes and looked at the kid. “Race ya,” she winked before flashing out in a fit of electricity. Josh followed suit with his flickering of light. 5 We soon found ourselves amidst the dark of night at the hillsides of Italy. The small town of Desio, located within Milano. Inside the halls of a large estate, that one would recognize as the third and smallest estate of Chrystal Fellini. And speak of the devil... She slowly walked through the halls, wavering in her steps... Intoxicated... Her body was loosely draped with a thin, black gown of silk, and her baggy eyes were half shut... She was slowly degrading day by day. She was suddenly scared into shape however, as Josh, from out of nowhere, suddenly appeared in a flash of light! "MOTHER FUCK!" Chryste suddenly shouted, almost startling Josh as bad as it did her! She tumbled back to her backside after she stumbled, missing the sudden appearance by Carys as well. Carys erupted into the room in a fit of laughter and electricity, her grin enough to send shivers up the spine of the Italian beauty, who was still recovering from the shock. Carys’ eyes met with her friends apologetically, as though she searched for a white streak, one of fear and shock. “Sorry C gotta ditch the kid on ya.” Carys shrugged, she needed to keep the conversation short enough so that she didn’t go into her worries over her upcoming match, or more importantly the prevention of the shipment that was due in under three hours; if she was fast enough she knew that she could probably stop it before it got on the road, minimizing the risk almost ten fold. "God damn it..." Chryste muttered, she was obviously a bit lost. Breathing heavily, she slowly stood, and wavered a bit, before she shook her head and continued. "I'm... I'm not a babysitter, I fuckin' hate kids... I'm in no shape anyways..." Carys nodded her head and flipped an index finger and thumb into the denim pocket of her leather jacket and pulled a small jar. “I figured as much,” she muttered after flicking a strand of blue hair from her eyes. The jar went up to Chryste’s nose, smelling salts no doubt, very potent, and sobering without a single doubt. Chryste groaned, and slapped the bottle away; her lips were twisted into a scowl. "I'm not fuckin' high, Car... I..I just can't sleep... It's too painful." She then turned her attention to Joshua, as her frown remained. "Why do you need me to watch him?" “Err…yeah…apparently they don’t let kids in nightclubs. So…will ya?” she sighed, one that was filled with diluted glee. She put her balled up fists around the straps of her black back pack. Chryste sighed, and weakly propped herself up against the wall. "Sure, go get some Lucky Charms, or a beer or something and watch the toon channel kid." She absently waved her hand at Josh and groaned once again, as she held her other hand over her stomach. "You hafta get him by morning though, or he'll be left here. I gotta go see a doctor; and no! I'm not pregnant!" Carys smiled and propped the handles of the back pack straight back onto her shoulders, “Thanks C; I’ll return the favour when you have one.” She spread a teasing grin on her face before tracing a blue lined top lip on her cheek. Chryste just flopped her hand and slumped a bit, before she shrugged and responded. "Whatever, he best not give me any trouble, or I'll take you up on that favour for stress relief." She weakly smiled and wiggled her brow at Carys, before turning her gaze down to Joshua again. "Afraid of getting lost? All the halls lead to fun places, go find out." Joshua looked over to Carys, seeming overwhelmed by the Pretty Hate Machine. Carys patted the little guy on the head, before she slipped a green bill inside his pocket, “I should be back, otherwise just wait for me at home.” Before she had taken her hand off his head she was gone, leaving most of his hair in a static frizz. His jade eyes changed from the dry air in front of him to the hot eighteen year old Italian chick. “So…beer?” Chryste just shrugged, and pushed from the wall. "Big sliding glass cooler full in the basement... Touch my guitar while you're down there though, and I'll kill you." She weakly spoke as she started on her slow trek back down the hall. “You want one?” his hand already flashed, telling her that he wasn’t going to take the scenic route for the beer. "Vodka" was her reply as she continued to walk, and used the wall as her prop. Josh nodded and slid his eyeballs from left to right, like he was searching for it; it came to him…without much wait. 6 “It’s here, Scrie,” The man who called through the door closed it after him with a creak that resonated through the deck of cards the – fiery red haired – woman shuffled in her hands. She smiled through the thick cloud of cigar smoke that lingered around her mouth. Her voice was naturally raspy, how you’d imagine a snake would speak no doubt, and slithered over her lips as she looked up to the larger coal haired, if a little chased with grey hairs of age. “Tool up, Cain. I don’t need to tell you there’s more than three dons sending in people to prevent this shipment.” A grunt and a nod was his notice of affirmation. There’s something interesting that Carys didn’t know. The other two ‘teams’ – more than one person – had been eliminated from Scrie’s competition. She was expecting the third sometime during transport, the six men that were following her were all she thought she needed. But, luckily for our seventeen sensation, she had the element of surprise running for her. The third party was meant to be just that; a ‘party’. Carys was one person sent in to tackle a shipment for her friend’s husband to be. Her best friend was going to be full of the crap if she didn’t prevent it, so she’d die before it wasn’t taken elsewhere. “Matthew,” she grunted from behind the door, she was technically locked in the building and her uncomfortable ness was transparent in her voice, “let me out so we can get this thing moving.” A heavy dead bolt screeched like a miserable cat that’d had its tail trampled on by a bus. “Dammit,” she hissed to herself before the sun of midday pierced her hazel – bloodshot, but hazel – eyes. “Scrie, something is wrong…I feel it.” The red haired lady pulled the tinted shades from her collar and placed it over her eyes; she dismissed him with the heel of her hand, rather abrasively. “Anyone would suspect this was your first time Matthew.” She teased with a grin that looked like it didn’t belong on her face, seemed as out of place as a machine gun in a priests hands. “What in the living hell is that?” she looked to the horizon, smoke screeched from the hot tarmac making the van look malformed and twisted. “TODAY THE WARNING CAME IN THE FLOODS!” Oh great, this was her master plan. To go in with guns blazing, the UPN truck with the boot full of cocaine. She could have gone and left everything perfectly okay, apart from disgruntled fuckers, she could deal with that though. But then there’d be another deal another day, and if Carys didn’t get this out of the way she’d be going into this match blinder than Ray Charles in the dark, and a veteran like Syko Arsonist wasn’t to be treated like that. ‘BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!’ “Has this guy been snorting our shit?” Scrie muttered to herself, the blacked out windows didn’t show the fact that there was a seventeen year old girl at the wheel who barely had her driving license. The van screeched to a halt, on top of four of the men who had accompanied Scrie on her journey. Scrie had barely been pushed out of the way by the morose looking Matthew. Of course those who had been caught under the wheels had died almost instantly, and Carys was waiting patiently behind the door, a little amount of blood making a sick kind of bandana across her forehead. Cain almost ripped the door from its hinges and got a magazine of bullets from her left semi pumped up his arm; the final finding home in the right eye socket of the henchmen. Carys loaded up another magazine, and made extra careful to take out its twin from her jacket, she tried her best to get over the pain in her hands, the burning sensation of twisting her wrist slamming the van through its total gears. ‘It’ll heal for Sunday,’ was all that was on her mind as she rolled onto tarmac. “One shin!” she screamed, like she was tallying the number of bullets she used more carefully with the second object of her affection. It snapped in half and brought him down to the ground, the girl with the red thigh high leather boots was released from his grip as he fell face first to the tarmac, his yelps of pain muffled by the hot surface. Those pained yelps were ended with a second shot; coming faster – by milliseconds – she’d like to believe, than her finisher would on Sunday, god speed. “One fuckin’ face.” Carys felt herself being peeled off the ground by her long midnight blue and blonde hair, there was no struggle in the grip that held her there. “Meddlesome whore, who sent you?” Her free hand - yeah Carys had been picked up with one arm, a downside to being a scrawny fuck – slammed into Carys’ kidney. Well, on the coming Sunday Carys would face a legend in her game of choice. A legend in her eyes at the very least. Today she would have to see Syko Arsonist holding her up like the proverbial rag doll, and quit the playing about. And so there he was, as she had wished, going full motion towards a swing into the wall. Carys used her wits, her speed and vitality of youth to kick back from the wall, to swing inside out against the pain of her head and slam the bottom of her foot into the nose – whose nose no longer mattered. A pop of a neck and squelch of bloody sneakers later, and Carys was on her feet, in pain. But still standing, just the way it would end on Sunday. Now it was Saturday morning, less than a day to go. Boy oh boy she needed to get out of there, her sigh emulated. ‘ZAP!’ Well, that sound was different than usual. For a reason. 7 “Oh Jesus,” she giggled, hanging in the shade of the alley, looking on at the men fighting in the dirt streets with their cowboy hats on. “Did I land in the wild west?” she snorted to herself, loudly and very obnoxiously, but that was soon cured as quickly as a bad ailment, when she saw the ladies with their parasols and long dresses, and the guns holstered on every man in sight. She WAS in the wild west. “Oh shitontoast…” -To Be Continued.
