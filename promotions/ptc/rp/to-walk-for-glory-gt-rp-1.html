Nick Adams Public Access Pimp Joined: 08 Feb 2003 Posts: 21 Posted: Thu Apr 10, 2003 5:13 am Post subject: To Walk For Glory [GT RP #1] (The scene is dreary. It's almost like a dungeon. Of course, it obvioudly isn't. If it were, there would be stone on the walls. Instead, the room is surrounded by sheet metal. It is the kind that is spray painted black so as to make escape seem even more hopeless, especially when the lights go out. Ironically enough, there is a slight form of illumination coming from the ceiling. It shines into one corner. It looks oddly shaped, since the bottom of the corner is quite bumpy. Due to that one large bump, the room looks as though the walls were somewhat formed to fit the landscape in places. Suddenly, a soft, quiet voice echoes throughout the room.) Voice: They say that glory can be achieved in many ways. Quite honestly, I believe that. After all, Einstein will go down in history as a genius, the man who made the atomic era. But did he ever participate in a war? No, he did not. His theories were used in the Manhattan project, but he never partcipated in wartime activities. I could list many more examples in detail, gaining glory and prestige for what they have done. Nimitz, Hirohito, Nobel, Locke, Rousseau...but they are just names. What made all these people famous was that they did not just think about their words. They acted. (The voice sighs, and the bump in the corner, which is becoming less and less believable as part of the wall, but for the moment, is somewhat immaterial, slightly shifts.) Voice: Of course, actions ALWAYS speak louder than words. So, here's how I'm going to act. (The bump suddenly rises, and a head and hands are revealed. Apparently, the voice is coming from the man. He is wearing long black jeans, black shoes, a long-sleeved black shirt, and a black cap. He has a sort of clean-cut look to him, though at first glance, he seems to be a shorter man.) Man: I'm not going to win the Global Title. That's right, PTC, I won't go out for your title. I'll participate in the match. I'll kick some serious hide. But when it's all said and done, I won't take your title. Quite honestly, if I need to go outside of the World Wrestling Alliance to get a title, then I have become less of a man than I truly am capable of becoming. So, I will participate. I will defeat some people. But in the end, unless somebody really pisses me off, I won't be the man making the final pin. Bet your money on that. (The man takes a couple steps forward.) Man: You wanna know why I am choosing to let somebody else have the Global title? I'll tell you why. I'm not winning the title because of three things: one, I didn't leave journalism and go into wrestling just so that I would enter a federation and then slave myself to a group that only holds Pay-Per-Views that don't really affect my progression in the federation in which I am a part. Two, I have my sights set on a title that carries a little more weight with me: the WWA United States title. So your title is little more than another ornament to place on my walls while I chase a dream. And thirdly, I am not going to take the Global Title at New Era because I am Nicholas Adams, and I would rather entertain my federation's fans than fight people that I won't see outside of this pay-per-view. (Nicholas Adams takes off his hat, revealing wild brownish-blonde hair. He drops the cap to the floor before removing his shirt and showing a very toned body.) Adams: Don't like it? Too bad. (FADE OUT.)[/url] _________________ Name's Nick. Now, as for who you are: Who cares?
