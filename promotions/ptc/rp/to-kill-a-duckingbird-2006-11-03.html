To Kill A Duckingbird

3 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-03 How did this happen? Saij wondered that very question, over and over and over. He sat there and wondered. He sat there on that cold iron bench and wondered. How did we get here? He knew the answer anyway, but the shock of being led away in handcuffs in front of a public crowd had numbed his mind. The spectacle of it all was short-lived, however; now he was engaged in a dragged-out waiting game. Oh, the waiting. There was not much else he could do in his current situation. He was locked in a grimy cell inside the local police station. In his first few minutes there, Saij had ticked off a mental list of what he could do to pass the time before being released. The options were few and largely unappealing: sit; stand; think; any combination of the above. He also could have conversed with the other prisoner sharing his cell, but there was nothing he cared to say to him… not more than he already had, anyway. Anyway, what to do? He’d already spent a good deal of the evening sitting, and thinking was historically not his best asset. Standing it was. It seemed all right at first, but after the second hour had passed the staggering ennui had more than taken its toll. At that point, sitting down seemed a thrill by comparison. The only places he could sit were the bench or the small toilet in the corner. His nose wrinkled in disgust at the thought of using that toilet—not just because the place reeked like hell as it was, but more so because of the utter lack of privacy. Truth be told, Saij really needed to take a leak, but he was holding it in with all he could muster. There was no way in hell he was going to relieve himself with his cellmate watching. Not with that damn duck watching. Not with Plucky the Cockfighting Duck, seated on the other side of the bench from him, watching. -~-~- Only seventy-two hours prior, Saij was riding the biggest high he’d had in months. Finally, after countless weeks in exile, drudging through the indy circuits and wrestling no-name half-talents… performing in filthy backwater Podunks where nobody knew his Goddamn name… finally, he was back in the spotlight. Finally, he’d gotten a quality fix. No more of the cheap shit; he was back in action in PTC. The crowds, the lights, the competition, the glory—after going so long without them, the rush was as intense as it had been the very first time he’d set foot in a big-league ring. Now he was no longer welcome in XUW, but it didn’t matter. At that moment, nothing else mattered. It was as if he’d been born anew; Saij was sporting a fresh haircut and had shaved for the first time in weeks. The bags under his eyes still lingered, but had diminished. His trademark goofy grin was back. The color had returned to his face. Indeed, he was starting to look like the Saij of old once again, as he sat in the locker room toweling himself off after his hard-fought victory over Samuel Stevens. His little daughter, Leanne, was digging through her daddy’s duffel bag and happily throwing his stuff all over the place. It was a mess. Saij didn’t care—he wasn’t even paying attention, really. His gaze seemed to travel beyond the room; he looked off into an unknown distance with an utter sense of self-satisfaction. He was euphoric. Orgasmic. Nothing, it seemed, could bring him down. Yes, it seemed nothing could bring him down—not even the young clerk from the tournament office who stopped by to drop off a packet for Saij, containing his information for round two. Saij’s excitement grew as he picked up the envelope and looked at it. Surely, the name of his next opponent had to be inside. Another challenge, another fix. His addiction would be satisfied once more. Unable to wait any longer, he tore the envelope open. He sifted through travel vouchers, hotel information, itinerary for public appearances, until he found what he was looking for. With baited breath, he drew the slip of paper out and closed his eyes. This was like Christmas! Who was he going to get? Jason Snow? Garbage Bag Johnny? Surely someone who would give him a huge challenge… He opened his eyes. His jaw dropped. His face reddened with anger. The paper said: "Plucky the Cockfighting Duck." Buzzkill. "What… the… FARK!?" Saij yelled, crumpling the paper up into a ball and throwing it across the room. "A duck? A DAMN DUCK!? What the fark kinda challenge is that!?" he kicked over a chair on the last word, sending it sailing into the wall. What did I ever do to hurt YOU? it surely must have wondered. "ARGH!" Saij screamed, pounding his fist into the wall, then wincing from the pain of doing so. He was seething. He paced back and forth furiously. Leanne watched and wondered why her daddy was so mad, and why he was muttering so many "no-no" words. At last, with a defeated sigh, Saij slumped onto the floor in a far corner of the room. A chill ran down his spine, and he pulled his legs to his chest as he began to shiver. Like a slap in the face, the knowledge that he was fighting a… a… a stupid duck in the next round had so cruelly snapped him back to reality. He was crashing. Withdrawal was already kicking in. "I didn’t sign up for this," he mumbled quickly under exasperated breath. "I want a real wrestler, not some stupid farkin’ water fowl!" Saij retreated from the world into his thoughts. This was ridiculous. How long was it going to take to pin a duck, anyway? Five seconds? Ten seconds, maybe? Christ, all he had to do was sit on the damn thing, and it’d be over. No way was this match going get him that sense of Nirvana he often got from defying overwhelming odds, that high for which he was so desperately starved. There had to be some way around it. Perhaps he could demand another opponent? No, PTC was very strict once the matches had been set. He had no chance of getting to face anybody else. Well, a slim chance maybe… but only if… "Only if I killed him," Saij half-chuckled to himself. His head shot up. "Wait a second!" he exclaimed. "Kill Plucky? Hey, technically that’s not illegal… he’s only a farkin’ duck after all. Why not? I’ll do it!" Saij smiled, but it wasn’t his usual goofy grin. It was a smidge on the demented side. Appropriately enough, at that same moment, Leanne had managed to fish her father’s iPod out of his bag; it was playing "I’m Going Slightly Mad" by Queen. "C’mon Leanne!" Saij said cheerfully, leaping to his feet. "Help daddy pack up his stuff, we’re going home! I have a date with a duck!" A dead duck at that. -~-~- Saij and Plucky glared at each other as they sat silently in their cell. Pajaro de Paso was standing just outside, conversing with one of the officers and trying to clear everything up. That was going to be a tall order, though—things happened so fast that nobody was really sure exactly what had happened. But Saij was certain he knew, and he didn’t agree with the story that the Rudo with Attitude-o was telling. "That’s not what happened," he finally grumbled indignantly. "Quack!" Plucky said. "I am not a big fat liar! Your friend there is!" "Quack." "Friend, amigo, whatever! It’s all the same!" "Quack quack, quack!" "No, I don’t have anything against the fine citizens of Mexico." "Quack?" "Or ducks." "Quack quack quack!" "Fine, I’m sorry we got in a fight! But for fark’s sake, your fri—amigo, I mean—could at least tell the right story!" "Quack?" "No he’s not! He’s not telling the right story at all! You know damn well what really happened! We didn’t meet at a bar, we met at a fancy restaurant…" -~-~ It was a quiet evening at the All Seasons Bistro, a cozy little place with soft lighting, candles, paintings on the walls, everything to create the ideal romantic atmosphere. The place was perfect for a date, or an anniversary… or for murdering a duck. Saij sat impatiently at a table in the corner, fidgeting about and checking his watch frequently. Every so often, he reached into his jacket and felt around to make sure the weapon he was carrying was still there. It always was, and he had no real reason to believe otherwise. Still, it was better to be safe than sorry—he couldn’t afford to lose it. It was the instrument of Plucky’s doom, carefully and specifically hand-picked for the sole purpose of annihilating a cockfighting duck. Finally, Saij’s guests had arrived. Plucky waddled into the restaurant, clad in a fancy top hat and monocle. Pajaro followed closely behind, clad in his traditional luchador garb. The other patrons stared in wonder. "Hey look! A retarded luchador!" "I’ve never seen one of those up close before!" "What about that duck with the hat and monocle?" "That’s nothing special. I see those every day at the Duck Evening Wear Emporium down at the mall. Wholly unremarkable." "Yeah, you’re right. But a retarded luchador! Hot dog! Wait’ll Grandma hears about this!" "Grandma’s deaf." "Oh yeah." "And she’s been dead for ten years." "Oh… yeah." Saij smiled and waved the pair over to his table. "Huzzah! Plucky! And uh… other guy with Plucky!" he greeted warmly. "Welcome, and thanks for joining me! I was starting to get worried you guys wouldn’t show up." "Quack." "Ah, heavy traffic you say? Fair enough. But you placed your order online before coming, right?" "Quack, quack!" "Ah good. Then we won’t have to wait much longer. Yes, we won’t have to wait much longer indeed… MUWAHAHAHAHA!" Saij roared with maniacal laughter, and rubbed his hands together with evil glee. Plucky and Pajaro looked at him worriedly. Saij noticed their stares and, clearing his throat, hastily changed the subject. "So uh, you guys want anything off the wine list?" he asked. "I didn’t get anything. I never drink alcohol. Ever. Certainly not several wine spritzers in one sitting. No sir, the very idea of that is preposterous. Preposterous I say! But you guys go ahead and get anything you want. I’ll just sit here and enjoy my water, which I always, always drink. Not alcohol." Saij took a sip from his glass. "Yeah, so why exactly did you invite us out for dinner again?" Pajaro finally spoke up. "Why not?" Saij replied. "After all, it’s not everyday that the One-Man Army of Farkness gets the… uh… privilege, of fighting a duck, especially one as farkin’ tough as Plucky. I just wanted to express my admiration for my opponent, and treat him to a good meal as my way of saying, ‘may the best man win!’ Or best duck, whichever happens first." "Quack!" said Plucky, as the waiter brought him a glass of tequila. The fine-feathered fowl raised it with his wing, to toast the gracious host. "Why thank you," said Saij, clinking glasses with Plucky and taking another sip of water. "Bueno," Pajaro said cheerfully as another waiter brought him a Corona. "See, amigo?" he elbowed Plucky in the side, "and you were worried that he was secretly planning to kill you! What a loco idea!" Overtaken with horrific surprise, Saij jerked his head aside and spewed his water on another waiter, who happened to be carrying a big tray of food over his head. He consequently stumbled backwards, lost his balance, and sent the food flying into an elderly couple sitting in the corner. (As luck would have it, smearing each other with food happened to be a secret fetish for the both of them, but they were too shy to let the other know, and they hadn’t had decent sex in years. They started making out like horny teenagers, reviving their once dead and empty marriage.) "Uh, yeah," Saij laughed nervously, setting his glass back down. "That’s a silly thing to think." He wiped his clammy brow, and once again tried to steer the conversation away from certain evil plans that involved killing certain cockfighting ducks. "So hey," he said. "You guys wanna hear a joke? Alright, so a naked blonde walks into a bar with a duck under one arm, and two-foot salami under the other. The bartender says…" "Quack!" "Oh. Uh, sorry… I didn’t realize that was going to offend you. Guess I should forget the other joke about the duck and the bartender who’s gonna nail his beak shut, huh?" "QUACK!" "Alright, alright! I’m farkin’ sorry already! Sheesh…" "Wasn’t it a poodle instead of a duck?" Pajaro asked. "I dunno," Saij shrugged. "I never actually saw The Breakfast Club. Oh good, here’s our food!" Their waiter placed a big tray of food on a stand next to the table, and served the dishes one-by-one. "Here’s your order, sir," the waiter said with a pleasant smile. He set a covered dish in front of Saij, and removed the cover with a flourish… "Duck confit!" Pajaro looked shocked, and his jaw hung open. Plucky was outraged. "QUACK! QUACK QUACK QUACK QUACK!" "Oh, uh… oops," Saij grinned, rubbing the back of his neck. "Wow, talk about awkward. Um… hey, honest mistake, right?" "QUACK QUACK QUACK!" "Geez, don’t get your feathers all ruffled. Is it really so bad that I…" "And here’s your dinner sir," the waiter continued, setting Plucky’s dish in front of him. "I do hope you enjoy it. And, as you requested, we flavored it with some extra… …sage." Saij’s face darkened with fury. "YOU SON OF A BITCH!" he shouted. "Quack! QUACK!" He leapt across the table at Plucky, causing it to spill over on its side! Food and feathers went flying everywhere! Saij and Plucky rolled around on the floor, each trying to overpower the other. Saij punched Plucky in the stomach! "QUACK!" Plucky bit Saij’s hand! "FARK!" Saij twisted Plucky’s ankle! "QUACK!" Plucky chopped Saij across the chest! "FARK!" Back and forth they went! Chairs flew all over the place! Tables were turned over! A toilet was thrown at some point! The patrons and staff all fled for the exits to avoid the melee, as the screams of pain could be heard with every blow: "QUACK!" "FARK!" "QUACK!" "FARK!" "QUACK!" "FARK!" "QUACK!" "FARK!" They sprung up from the floor, Saij to his feet, and Plucky beating his wings enough to be floating at eye level with his attacker. They charged each other and butted heads at the same time! "QUARK!" Saij stumbled back, and Plucky landed on the ground, looking dizzy. After taking a moment to regain his bearings, Saij saw that now was his chance to destroy Plucky once and for all. So, he reached into his pocket, and drew out the weapon that would signal Plucky’s end… -~-~- At this point, it would be prudent to inform the reader that Saij is by no means a conventional thinker. In fact, he’s what one might call an idiot. Nonetheless, he lives life by a set of fascinating rules, one of which is the following: "Every situation has a completely illogical explanation, which can only be deduced through the process of Unreasonable Reasoning." In this case, Saij thought long and hard about how best to destroy Plucky. He reasoned (unreasonably) that destroying something is essentially the same thing as canceling out its existence. And how might something be cancelled out? By confronting it with its polar opposite, of course! With that in mind, Saij made a trip to an adult sex toy store, to find the one item he was positive would end Plucky’s existence… -~-~- "Alright Plucky," Saij grinned evilly as he pulled out his weapon. "Prepare to meet your doom, at the hands of my… …DUCKFIGHTING COCK!" Saij brandished a phallus-shaped object of considerable size, and pointed it straight at Plucky. Plucky just stood and stared curiously, not quite knowing what to make of the situation. It was just as well, because he had no time to react… as Saij threw it directly at his head! It struck him across the face! And bounced off harmlessly. Saij was flabbergasted to see that Plucky was still standing there, alive and well and generally existing. "DAMNIT!" he shouted. "My brilliant plan to kill Plucky has been foiled by the stupid forces of logic and real life! Argh! Well, it’s a good thing I had a Plan B." He reached underneath his seat, and pulled out a hunting rifle (which any sane person will tell you he really ought to have used in the first place). He took careful aim at Plucky, who started to fly away. Pajaro leapt out of nowhere, and grabbed the barrel of Saij’s rifle, pointing it away from his avian amigo! "Hey hombre, wait a second!" he shouted. "Don’t you point that thing at Plucky! Besides, it’s not duck season, it’s rabbit season! Saij rolled his eyes. "Damnit man, I’m not doing that bit again! Get away!" He shoved Pajaro aside then looked around the room for Plucky. He spotted the hapless duck, darting through the air, back and forth across the room, quacking loudly. Plucky was flying left-to-right, right-to-left, directly in front of a large painting: it was a meadow, with tall grass, a single tree on the left, and generally plenty of bright blue sky. It looked oddly familiar. Saij took careful aim, and fired. BLAM! He missed. BLAM! He missed again. BLAM! A third time he missed. Plucky flew away. "Fark," he growled to himself. "I knew I should have brought more than three rounds!" Suddenly, a dog sprung out of nowhere, pointing and laughing at him. "Shut up, you stupid farkin’ dog!" Saij shouted. He threw the rifle in its direction and struck it over the head. The dog fell unconscious. Saij pushed and shoved his way through the panicked crowd, looking around wildly for his nemesis. He shook his fist and swore to the heavens: "I’ll get you, Plucky the Cockfighting Duck! I’ll get you if it’s the last thing I ever do!" Then the police arrived. -~-~- "And that’s what really happened." "Quack." "Alright, so you weren’t actually wearing a top hat and a monocle. But hey, you’d look pretty cool in them." "Quack." "Alright, so maybe I had a few drinks." "Quack." "And maybe it really was at a bar instead of a fancy restaurant. But I didn’t try to kill you!" "Quack." "Okay, so I did try to kill you. But you know something?" Saij scooted closer to Plucky, and carefully placed an arm around his shoulder. Plucky seemed nervous at first, but there was something different about the way Saij was addressing him this time. It was gentle. Friendly. Music seemed to waft through the air… like that cheesy "we had some tough times, but we still love each other" music from the end of every episode of Full House. "I learned a lot today," Saij continued. "I learned that maybe wrestling a duck won’t be as bad as I thought it would. I mean, you really have a lot of fight in you, man. I thought you weren’t gonna be enough of a challenge for me, which in a roundabout way is pretty much why I tried to kill you…" "Quack." "Yeah, I know that makes no sense. But hey, you know what? I was wrong. After that farkin’ awesome brawl we had, I think you’ll be a great challenge for me. And when we meet in the ring, we’re gonna farkin’ tear the house down, you hear me? I guess what I’m trying to say is… I’m sorry. Can you ever forgive me, Plucky?" "Quack quack." "What!? Fine! Screw you too, then!" Saij folded his arms and angrily turned away from Plucky. A moment later, the cell door opened. "Out. All of you get out of my sight or I’ll bust out the rubber hoses." -~-~- Not long after Saij and Plucky had left the police station, a woman arrived in the front lobby. The officer at the front desk greeted her warmly. "Hey Brenda. What brings you by here so late?" Brenda Clark, a slender woman in her mid-thirties, sighed and lifted her briefcase slightly. It was heavy. She always had plenty of paperwork with her. "I have a hot date," she replied sarcastically. "With a database anyway. Got a new case on my desk a few days ago, and I’m having trouble finding the guy’s address." "Ah, more of that child welfare stuff, huh?" "Yeah," Brenda sighed, brushing a stray lock of red hair away from her eyes. "And lucky me, I get all the crazies. This new case I’ve got, it’s this guy—professional wrestler, from what I understand—who insists on taking his kid everywhere with him, even to his shows. Not a healthy environment for raising a child." "Heh, now that’s something," the officer said, bemused. "We just had a wrestler here in the slammer." Brenda raised an eyebrow. "Really," she said slowly. She opened her briefcase and quickly pulled out a folder, then leafed through its contents. "His name didn’t happen to be…" she took a moment to find his name. "Jeffries, did it? Scott Jeffries?" "Scott Jeffries? Oh, you mean Saij?" "That’s his moniker as I understand, yes." "Yeah, that was him! Let him out not more than half an hour ago." Brenda couldn’t believe her luck. She wasn’t going to be stuck in front of a computer screen tonight after all. Instead, it looked like she might be taking another innocent child out of an irresponsible father’s hands and putting her into foster care. "You happen to have his address?" she asked, smiling. -~-~- Special thanks to Thommy for agreeing to doing some collaboration this round!
