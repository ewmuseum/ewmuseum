Preparing for a war!!

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-09 The world was once thought to be flat. There were beings during this time that believed the world was made at the gods bidding and sailed the seas. They were feared as far south as the Arabian nations. They were feared by many nations and individuals. The barbarian tribes of the east never came to the northmens countries and they were only felled by one mighty force. Christianity crushed the viking people and the heritage they had left behind. They were swallowed up by the mighty tide called Christianity until one man stood up and faced them all. The room is filled with dark shadows only broken by a huge fire burning in the center. Upon the walls there is a mural of viking gods and victories. Staring solidly at the fire is Jorge Samuelsson. Seemingly at peace with himself over past events as he holds the WWA Tv Title across his lap. Slowly he raises his eyes from the fire and looks around and then brings them down to his title. Breathing out a deep sigh as he looks at it he then pulls it to the side and drops it on the floor. "Mighty are my people and I am one of the mightiest and the mightiest of this current time. Phobos has brought up a dangerous fact. The truth is things should have been done a certainw ay. Yet the WWA has been delivered the message I was sent to make known. The Norse are back. Phobos is merely Greek and forgets that the beliefs of the gods differ as much as the religions on this mudball called earth. My people call it Midgard as I am closer to Asgard rather than dwell among the non believers." Lifting a log he tosses it into the fire. Reachign down once again he lifts a large iron pole and pokes at the fire stoking it as the flames burn brighter. Shaking his head a moment he then wipes his forehead looking at the fire. "GTT5 round three is upon me and I am facing Alec Lancer a man who is favored above me again, much like many others. Yet I am going to continue to open their eyes. I will work hard to make this a mighty victory for all my ancestors and what they fought for. I stand her ready to defend everything I believe in forget who says what. If it is a fight they want I will give it to them." Slowly he pulls a book from his side. He slowly opens the book and thumbs through it looking for something. He sneers slightly as he looks at the title laying next to him and he then turns and flips the pages of the book as he stops and looks up. "What? Did you not think a Viking warrior could not read? Feh pathetic ingrates it surprises me how some of you made it past the middle ages." Shaking his head and flips a few pages and then stops. Using his thumb he passes down a few lines on the page and then grins as he raises his eyes to the fire. "Perhaps I will give you a little mythology lesson so it will give you a bit of better understanding of why I am going to keep fighting and win this damnable match up." Clearing his throat he leans back and smiles evilly. "Odin the father of the gods also known as Woden in the Gemranic states. He a son in Thor god of thunder and the Storm and adopted a son in Loki child of a half giant. Loki is the trickster and the Father of Fenrir the Wold and the goddess of the dead Hel. Loki’s biggest downfall with the gods was his murder of Balduer where he killed him with the wood of a mistletoe tree or so the story is told. Loki’s children are lead by him to bring about Ragnarok. The descendants of the gods chose to leave a tale of the gods to protect the descendants and the children of the aesir. My secret I still hold for the cildren of the aesir were given gifts of boon and tey shall lead the Vikings back to glory." Smiling he closes the book and turns and brushes his hand across the title and frowns. "Not the glory I was hoping for , But it is a start. Even those less fortunate beings like the plebeians who vote against me in the GTT5 I will prove you all wrong. PTC has to prove one thing to me. Are they ready for me to fight hard and run through its ranks? I am a warrior a Viking warrior and nobody and I mean nobody is going to stop me from doing what I do best. What is it I do best? I hurt people I put out legends. I am sure you people are familiar with Sun Tzu’s the art of war? I hope your not as stupid as you are lead to be." Cracking his neck from side to side Samuelsson looks around and then grins. "You know I am going to only mention four of them since this is round three. I kind of borrowed this idea from Shaman a man that made it to round three last year and got his ass handed to him by a female. She was quite good. Then earlier this year I destroyed him as I made my way into the ring and made my presence known in the WWA. Tell you what I will give you my own interpretations on the first four." Chuckling with a disturbing sound. He then leans forward staring dangerously close to the fire. "Sun Tzu said: The art of war is of vital importance to the State. Yes it is vital to the state for I am the man who brings it to the house everytime I enter the ring. I slap my opponents down and attack them again and again as they scream in agony and terror. The little kids crying next to the security barriers as I crush their heroes and in the end it is all merely fuel for my soul fire. I enjoy watching them cry and the fans boo me because it is a fuel for my need for pain. I cause it and you have no choice but to accept the pain as it is delivered to you." Laughing loudly as he waves his hand into the fire over and over. It seems that a disturbingly aggressive nature of this man is becoming very distinct. "It is a matter of life and death, a road either to safety or to ruin. Hence it is a subject of inquiry which can on no account be neglected. No War cannot be neglected nor ignored. I am the fury that is released upon the unsuspecting masses as I crush those who cross me beneath my bootheal. I will slam my mighty fist down upon the bridge of all noses as I watch the blood spurt and drip upon the ground and pool up underneath the feet of the worlds conqueror." Raising his arm he looks at his huge arms and grin as he bends them and turns looking at the fire once again. "The art of war, then, is governed by five constant factors, to be taken into account in one's deliberations, when seeking to determine the conditions obtaining in the field. Yes simple facts training to the fullest of your abilities and then push yourself beyond them. The burn should get beyond the ability to stand it. Fight the good fight and give reverence to the Vikings who have paved the path before you." Reaching down he grabs the WWA Tv Title and lifts it up pointing to it and then turns his head nodding. "These are: (1) The Moral Law; (2) Heaven; (3) Earth; (4) The Commander; (5) Method and discipline. Maybe perhaps I was mistaken about the comments I previously stated. Then again they mean a lot to me. Magnus Samuelson the huge man and Former Worlds Strongest man. He paved the way for me to come the the United States of America. He became a champion around the world and became a house hold name here. How about another Viking who thrives on Viking Power! Sven Karlsen paved another path for me and my own. He worked hard yet there is something these two men as Viking warriors did not have. They were not children of the aesir. They worked hard yet achieved the goals set before them by who they are and the naysayers and the doubters were proven wrong." Standing up he stands before the fire. His face becomes that of rage as he stares at the fire. His hands spread forward palms outwards towards the blazing flames. "My path lies before me, in the ring against Alec Lancer. Someone who thinks I am an easy mark. I heard all the talk from you to the others Lancer. You are thinking me an easy win. I don’t care what you think little man. I am going to break you and make sure that you will become a laced boil who shall never cross my path again. You failed to think about a few minor details. First you mus6t remember that I am the man you are facing not the little girls from the catholic school down the road. Go make their panties wet on your own time not on mine. Bad mouth me all you want boy and in the end you will be spewing nothing but blood out of that little pie hole. Tis time let the women weep and the men vomit at the sight of your battered body laying upon the ground. You shall know pains never imagined at my hands." Reaching down he grabs a large log and begins slamming the log down into the fire as embers burn and scatter everywhere bit by bit piece by pice as he raises the log as it is on fire now as he holds it like a war club. "I am tired of being under estimated my anger will thrive for tomorrow I am going to hunt you down and make sure you learn the newest piece of the puzzle. I am going to show you the way of the Viking. I will show you the way to Valhalla as they shut the door in your face. The child of the aesir is angry Alec Lancer. Go tell your pretty little friends that you won’t be seen until you have healed. I am going to find every little piece of your life and make my mark on it. It is time you learned that someone else has finally stepped forward and looks for some payback for transgressions unknown. You and I dance very soon Lancer nothing is going to change what is going to happen to you in the ring. If I win I win, but if you win it will be by the skin of your teeth little man." Throwing the log into the fire Samuelsson sits down as he lifts the WWA Tv Title and lays it across his legs as he looks down at it and his emotions seem to melt away as he becomes an expressionless being once again. His eyes going back to stare at the fire from that whence he began.
