IG45 Trash Talk Open Invitation thread (all vs Chris Storm)

31 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
31 May 2008 Well, well. Here we are at last, the long awaited first apearance of Gabriel Kael in the world of Primetime Central. Consider your virginity to true greatness lost, PTC, much like you, Chris Storm, will soon consider your Infinite Gauntlet title lost. I was waiting for the right time to show up here, the right time to begin my dominance in this sport, and what better party for me to show up at than one where I can take out one of the most overrated, overpaid, and over the hill so-called "stars" of wrestling lore? Face it, Storm, you may not know me, but you fear me. You talk about the past, about Almasy and Rust, and drop names like Angelo Deville, but one thing you should be thinking about is that Deville is DEAD, and that guys like you and Seymour live so far in the past that you apparently still think it is the late 90's. Back when PCW was alive and guys like you still mattered. But there is a force in this world that rules all, Chris, and that force is Time. Time has a way of sneaking up on us, it is very, very tricky, and it played the greatest trick it has on you. It snuck up behind you, slowly yet surely, and when you thought that you were safe, it passed you by. And when it did, it whispered, ever so gently, in your ear. Guess what, buddy? You've been passed, and you no longer matter. You probably tried to ignore Time's whisper, or maybe you convinced yourself that it was a figment of your senile imagination, but it was there, nonetheless. The men who were the "big dogs" of the PTC yard are barely standing anymore. I think that most of them were smart enough to bail when they felt the whisper from Time in their ears that they were being passed, that their spotlight was shining on somebody else. Those big dogs that are left, guys like you, are the ones who sit in their little dog houses and eat the soft, mushy food while the future big dogs are out chasing down fresh meat. The future, the guys out hunting, are the ones like me, the guys biding their time, waiting. And then, when the old curs become too weak to fight back, they rip out their throats. Chris Storm, I am the future. And you, my friend, are about to learn that the hard way. So by all means, keep dropping names that you remember from yesteryear, when you were important, and keep looking at those GCW tag team titles and trying to convince yourself that tag wrestling is still important. Do whatever you need to do to feel as good about yourself as possible, Chris, that's what I hope you are doing. Because soon, the hunting dog is going to come and tear out your throat, and in the afterlife of a once great career you will wonder where all of the past glory you loved so much went. And then you'll remember that little whisper in your ear, and you'll realize what I already know. You are finished, and my time is just beginning. ---- 1 June 2008 Ah, Rust and von Doom. How did I guess that you two would come up with something to say. Tell me, do you merely speak to hear the sound of your own voice? Because those words mean nothing to me. It would be in the best interest of both of you to not try to place me into stereotypical "dark gimmicks" as you so wrongly put it. As with much else about this world, you have much to learn about me, and learn you will over the coming years. I may not be on your radar, but I assure you that soon, I will be. Rust, enjoy the popcorn. I'm sure it will be nice to have something to munch on while I realign our friend, Mr. Storm's spine for him. The truth is that I could give less of a shit about what you, von Doom, Curt Cunningham, Chris Storm, or any other fossil still kicking around here thinks. Von Doom can call himself The Present as much as he cares to, and everybody will, I'm sure, have a lovely time with talking big words. You poor saps can't back up big words like I can, you don't have a shot at following up on the shit you've been talking. Let's face it, this contest is decided already, we're all just putting on a show. At the end of the day, I'm walking away with the Infinite Gauntlet title. But I digress, I will not waste any more breath on the fools rapidly falling into the distance in my rearview mirror. Chris, I see you still have not had anything to say. How disappointing, really, considering that you had a lot to say not so long ago. Afraid, perhaps? Maybe even you are realizing that an oncoming tractor trailer is about to run right over you and leave you a proverbial smear on the ground? I was planning on taking the high road here, but I'm afraid my base nature has won out, and I shall now be taking liberties as I break you down mentally before I inevitably break you down physically. I'm curious about the most recent goings on in Global Championship Wrestling, with your beloved Miss Windsor and one LeStatt Knight. Tell me, does she merely flit about as she pleases between members of that roster? Are you seriously so inadequate sexually that you cannot satisfy her enough to keep her faithful to you? I pity you, Storm. Truly, I do. Perhaps when Tessa is finished with polishing the knob of LeStatt, she will move on to your butt buddy Marcus Stone? Or someone else on the roster, hmm? Jay Terror? Octavian? Maybe, though I shudder to think of this, even Clyde Fox. And as much as a little girl on girl action between Tessa and Vivca J Valentine would be fun, I think that Vivica would have enough gray matter between her ears to not touch something that has been violated by so many... Hey von Doom is referring to himself as "The Violator" these days, perhaps he's had his way with her too. There's an idea for you, Chris! It's perfect! Almasy was flapping his gums a little earlier about you having a built in out should you lose your Infinite Gauntlet title to him, but you have an even better one for when you lose said title to me. You can just blame it on the fact that you got a scorching case of herpes from Tessa! If you actually do manage to get back into her pants (unlikely, I know, seeing as you apparently can't satisfy her) you may actually end up with the real thing! There you go, Chris. All of your coming problems are solved. Your pride will heal from the loss of this Championship, and barring complications related to the multitude of sexually transmitted infections you may or may not be suffering from, you are free to walk away from wrestling into a quiet retirement full of antibiotics, memories of your glory days, and a burning sensation in your crotch that never seems to go away. I hope you enjoy it, Guru of Gonorrhea. ---- 1 June 2008 I look forward to you trying to "fix things" Storm. Honestly, I do. I want nothing more than for you to come gunning for me with everything you have, including your oh-so-frightening "German Suplex O'Doom" and watch me get right back up to finish what I've already started, that being inevitably breaking you down. Multitasking is an enviable trait, but most people with slightly more intelligence than the average chimpanzee can walk and chew gun at the same time. The fact that you can "handle business" while holding the IG crowd? That holds no more weight with me. So you are slightly more advanced than a primate, or at the very least somebody stricken with Down's syndrome. You don't impress me. You don't intimidate me. So bring it on, take care of business. "Fix" things and shut me up about your precious little HIV Spreading Machine. I welcome it, in fact I want nothing more. Come on Chris, you can't beat me, and you know it. ---- 1 June 2008 Ow, my pride. Seriously, I don't think I'll ever recover from you guys. I... Wow... Words can't express how badly you douchebags haven't humbled me at all. You honestly think that I give two squirts of donkey piss what you broken down hasbeens think? Von Doom you want to talk about EPW and what's been happening with Pierre? Watch, just watch, the next episode of Riot, because you will see what happens when people piss me off. I'm thinking maybe you should step between those ropes, James, and back up what you are talking about, but maybe you can't, maybe you just don't have the balls, hmm? Think about who you are fucking around with before you keep thinking things up with that defective brain of yours. Andy Murray... wow there's a name nobody gives a shit about. You've done what now? Oh right, you were in AWC, the most overrated fed in the history of this business, and maybe you were good there, but to be honest anybody who knew the proper way to suck off the management was successful in AWC. "2001 called, they want their gimmick back?" Wow that's original. You ain't even worth my time, you Scottish King of... oh wait, there's nothing to add here. You are below shit, hell you aren;t even worth the sweat that will be dripping off of me when I crush the dreams of Chris Storm and anybody else that they put in front of me. And at running the risk of "wasting everyone's time" I'm going to spend just a moment on you, Jackson Rust. What rock did you crawl out from under? Seriously who the fuck are you? I would like to suggest something to you, crawl back under said rock before you get hurt, because you can't run with any of the others in this little contest, hell even "I'm the 19,9999999th guy to have fucked Tessa Windsor" Chris Storm and "I Violated Axel Action" James von Doom are above you. So do me and everybody else a favor here, pumpkin, and crawl back under that rock, go back to the mediocrity that is threatening to swallow you whole, and get out of my face. Storm could beat you with his hands behind his back, and he couldn't take me on my worst day. Chris. Murray asked you to forget me. Do you honestly think that is possible? I'm making my mark in this business, and nobody will forget Gabriel Kael. Soon, very soon, a new chapter will be written when the Infinite Gauntlet finds a new owner and a hasbeen finds his way to retirement. Maybe they will throw you a big party at GCW when you finally acknowledge that this is the end. Maybe you will pry that mouth of yours off of Marcus Stone's pork sword long enough to attend it, and maybe, just maybe, I'll let you walk in there under your own power. So forget these morons, forget the von Dooms, Rusts, and Murrays of this world. They can't carry you to the greatest match of your long career like I can, and they for sure cannot bring you crashing down into the painful embrace of reality like I can. You want to face me. You need to face me. If for no other reason than to have one last great match before you finally realize that you no longer have it.
