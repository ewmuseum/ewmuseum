Prelude to Premonitions

Date: 2004-09-16 “For never can true reconcilement grow, where wounds of deadly hate have pierced so deep.” She’d seen those words time and time again in her mind, written in many a tongue many known and unknown to her. But she still understood, that phrase transcended translation; each time the phrase came across her mind’s eye she scanned over it in frenzy. ‘For (In the most general sense, indicating that in consideration of, in view of, or with reference to, which anything is done or takes place) never (not ever; at no time in the past or future) can (the ability of action)…’ Each word held its own definition, and the meaning echoed in the back of her mind as she went through each word one by one in the construction of that phrase. She knew where those words were from, and she knew who they were child to. Her own wounds could never be healed, because the blade that had pierced her heart was one of lies and deception. Everything she knew had been a lie, the only time she’s known love… and it has been nothing but a façade from the first day she met him. Her time was spent much of lying there, strapped down by her limps, arms above her head, ankles and wrists bound to the posts of the bed. She could feel, during the times she had senses durable enough to work, that she was under cover for most of the time, a blindfold tied around her eyes to give her nothing but darkness for the most part. Underneath the covers, she was naked. She would lay there, blurting out incessant words and phrases like a severe Turret’s patient. Her voice would echo off the walls, and sometimes she would answer her own questions thinking someone else asked her. At times she would be joined by that man… but she never thought of him as a man anymore. He didn’t exist in her mind, only a figment of her delusion. He was her imaginary friend… her imaginary rapist. Only he could free her from her ties, and only he could remove her from the bed. It was only during these moment of freedom was she abused and torn asunder by him. A living doll used for his sadistic fucking sessions, there was no passion or love in his wild penetrations. Only lust, like a beast seeking to scratch an itch he can’t reach. Her orifices would burn from fluids his and hers. But every time she was placed back in bed, she was cleaned and doctored. There was no caution in her use; they didn’t care if she cried or screamed or felt any pain at all. All they seemed to care about… is if she were still alive when everything was over and done with. Night after night her head would shake and quake with maddening images, things that would drive a newborn babe to animalistic ferocity. Gruesome and revolting displays, violence, no… ultraviolence, with no regard for law or morality; these things made her stomach tie itself in knots. But she could not wretch. She had nothing to wretch to begin with. She was skin and bones… barely living off of the pills and unknown bits forced into her mouth. Sometimes she could taste meat; other times vegetables, rice and grains. Never a sweet taste touched her lips. But she knew of the pills that were placed on her tongue, that were stroked down her throat to make sure she didn’t hide and spit. They knew she would eat the food prepared for her, but the pills must be taken, or else the needles came. She would get the needles anyways every few days. She could never count, because the pain blinded her senses. But she always knew there was one needle that would pierce her vein and take from her the little life she had left. When she woke up, she was always bandaged, and doped up on anesthetic. Those were her only true moments when she felt at bliss… And that’s when the dreams would come to her. Nasty images, racing back and forth in her brain like a Power Point presentation on caffeine, she could only watch and allow them to saturate into her soul. Sometimes her eyes would reach across the world into the states, where vile acts were committed right before her eyes. In Charleston there were floggings of those different from them, those who craved sexual alternation. She felt each of their minds as she was private audience to these things. Their minds were filled with rage and confusion, mental partitions jailing any form of understanding and acceptance. And she, she could look into the mind of that man being bloodied and broken… and she could feel fear. She could hear his whys, his pleases, his stops, his begs, his Gods and his Christs. But she could do nothing, nor anyone else. He was a victim of his lifestyle, one that was only accepted on popular culture, but never in reality. She’s felt that fear in her life, she’s said the same whys and don’ts and no’s that he has. But as she had learned herself through experience… words hold no muscle against actual flesh and bone. When you are weak… the strong prey upon you; the weak can pray, but they hold no strength at all. Dr Mengelev would look down on her with instrument ready to pierce her flesh, screams and the repugnant odor of charred flesh wafting through her nose. Other times placed her in situations drawing tighter to her nerves than others; the Rape of Nanking forced into her mind as a toy to numerous soldiers, what was supposed to be her family slaughtered right before her eyes, blood splattering into her face as the men took her body as entertainment roughly. Her life would flash right before her eyes, though none of the scenes looked the least bit familiar to her. It was another’s life seen through her own eyes… and all she could see is the faces of frightened people praying and crying as the high pitched whine of jet engines propelled them into man made monoliths. Across the ocean men were pleased with themselves, laughing and smiling satisfied with mass murder. Each and every time, she heard the same exact questions and pleas. ‘Why are you doing this?’ ‘Please stop!’ ‘Don’t!’ 'Jesus Christ in Heaven above please help me…' The cries for mercy swirled about her mind, echoing off of walls that seemed to imprison her. She stood there naked in the single spotlight as the voices filled her ears, hammering on her eardrums with an African tribal beat. There was so much sorrow… so much despair… and she could do nothing… nothing but curl up into a tiny ball in that circle of light. Everything was thrust upon her heart at once, fear, sadness… she was scared for her life. The knocking at the door wouldn’t stop; she found herself in the corner of the dark room rocking back and forth, her nude back pressed against the cold stone wall. Again and again she voiced her wish, hoping and praying it would come true. Her voice would hiss through in a whisper at first, but as the knocking progressed it did as well, raising slowly and crackling through strains of sobs. ‘I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t knock. wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I Knock. don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna KNOCK. die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t KNOCK!! wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die. I don’t wanna die.’ Leaving manners at the door itself, whoever knocked pushed the door open, and as light flooded into her dark cube she screamed out clutching handfuls of her black hair madly. Light filled everything around her, and soon a construct of darkness became one of light all around her. Her skin seemed to glow with an unearthly light, hair shimmering. The light somehow made her feel at ease; light always the representation of peace and goodness in this world. She opened her eyes slowly and looked around at the pure plain surrounding her, and they closed back with a sigh of relief. She leaned back to lie facing up, and allowed the silence to set her nerves steady. “How can you live in a world like this?” That voice… it was the same voice that had haunted her for years. Night after night, each and every dream; she could not escape it. She could not fight its will; she could not disagree with it as much as she tried. In the end, that voice was her master. She sat up quickly, eyes darting around the snow white universe for the owner of that voice, an action always left useless. Her heartbeat began to grow faster, breaths rising higher and higher into her throat as he spoke to her. “You have seen the world as it is; a place of torment and affliction. A place where acceptance is foreign and the bond between parent and child resides at their pelvises, this world is ruled by desire and lust, a greed for sin. And you have some desire… some will… to live in this world?” Her eyes would slowly fall downward, ashamed that she even had a pining to be amongst a civilization that would do such things to themselves… kill, steal, rape, enslave, inflict pain upon, control, lie, and manipulate… she felt mortified even more that she allowed herself to be tool of all those things. “You don’t have to live in a world like this, full of the unjust and corrupted… you know that, right?” With head nodding, she knew what she had to do. Each time she was presented with that question it always came in different form. There was a knife one time, another a pistol, and once time a noose suspended in midair. This time, it would be by her own hands. Her hands grasped at her throat, the windpipe firmly closed shut. With her valve closed it would only be a matter of time before everything would get foggy. A cloud would float over her brain, and her vital organs would slowly shut down. Her back arched as she began to lean back, accepting the fate grabbing at her. Sometimes it felt as if she had no control in the matter, but then there were times when she believed that it was her body acting by instruction of her subconscious. No one forced her to take the knife to her wrists, no one put a gun to her head to put a gun to her head. It was her own doing that caused her to gently nuzzle the noose around her neck. Maybe she hated herself, and maybe that voice was the embodiment of her self-loathing. Maybe she wanted to die, to escape the pains of her physical self and the torture endured in the mental plane. She’s been called every derogatory name known to man, ever racial slur spanning all the races of the world, and they all had maximum impact on her psyche. Everything made her want to die, even breathing. And so… she was down to her last, when… “Wait.” He always stopped her right at Death’s door; the right moment with the Grim Reaper himself would place his cold clutch on the doorknob and begin to twist. But that voice… that voice proved to be the doorstop keeping him out. Her eyes fluttered open weakly as she listened to the voice echo around her. “Why should you die? You’ve done nothing wrong. You’re a victim of the world itself. Other people’s sins made you into what you are today, gave you all the pain you feel right now. It’s not just for you to die because you can’t deal with the pain of the world… because if you die, it won’t mean anything. You will just be another life lost at the hands of life itself. No one will care, no one will think twice about you, and everyone will continue to live on in sin.” Reason pried her hands away from her throat, pushing her arms down to her sides. Slowly she sat up, her hair grazing across her spine with her head tilted back looking upwards. “But… there is something you can do… to set things right.” She nodded slowly. She understood. Why should she die when she’s lived a good life? Everyone else has caused her trouble, everyone else made her cry. It was her against the world, and in order for her to survive she would have to fight just as evil and vile as the world itself. “Yes… you know what must be done. In order for you to have a peaceful, happy life… everyone must die. When you are alone, then you will be at peace. No one to touch you, no one to call you names, no one to make you cry. Only you… and your thoughts…” She picked herself up off the ground slowly and stood there with her eyes lowered. She was a good girl, but it was this voice that made her think bad things. Maybe she was one of the bad people that made up the world… or maybe she indeed was nothing but a product of the twisted system. She couldn’t decide, because she had no solid grounds of debate. All she was definite on… was that she wanted to be left alone. And if the people had to pay… it was nothing but a small price to pay. “Good,” the voice knew she was on board, and he sounded pleased. “Now… you must tell me what you see in your dreams. Tell me if your dreams come true, little one.” In an instant the girl found herself back in that darkened room, the spotlight upon her yet again. This time there was no fear of persecution, but there was a sense of duty heavy in her heart. Dressed in a flowing black gown sheer and delicate, she stood barefoot before a white Victorian vanity dresser, the mirror stretched out wide before her like a windowpane. She gracefully rested on the bench set before the mirror, hands gripping the curls of the bench’s ridges as she leaned forward. Her dreams… her desires… her fantasies of a world without humans… it could be possible, but there were events that must play out before her dreams could be accomplished. The mirror fogged up from the inside as she watched, and soon it began to dissipate, images coming into clarity before her eyes as La Boheme serenaded her. News reports of earthquakes, typhoons, and volcanic eruptions plaguing Japan, were signs of the end times. There was a force at hand that was causing Japan to be battered by the elements, a force that opposed the elements themselves. People were dying as the earth tried to fight against this unknown assailant, but they seemed to be losing the war. One news article was centered around the natural disasters… a mysterious man found off the coast of Japan refusing to speak to officials. All that they can go on is the Korean label on his shirt. Faces came across in the reflection, those familiar and unfamiliar to her. In a home somewhere in Japan twins of opposing genders stood in the doorway of a bedroom, where they watched behind the back of an older man, their father. The man salted with gray in his slicked back hair knelt before an altar with a woman’s picture framed on the shelf. The Shinto altar had incense burning in honor of her, the man’s head lowered in reverence. The twins looked at each other, saddened by the sight before them. They knew why their father held so much grief in his heart… and they knew the man that was responsible for his pain. They vowed that if they ever saw the man, they’d take his life in exchange for their mother’s. Elsewhere, the bond between old and young wasn’t as strong, but was instead hanging by the thinnest of threads. “Who taught you how to shoot, boy??” Gunfire blazed through the docks as two men threw lead at each other with intent to kill. An older looking man with black hair and a black suit knelt down behind a crate to reload his gun, glancing over his shoulder. “I hope you’re ready to see your mother, boy, because I’ve had enough of you here and now!” He checked the chamber to make sure everything was loaded, and then he pulled back the hammer before standing up. He aimed, but there was no one around. No bullets flying in his direction, nothing. “You know what your problem is, don’t you boy?” he said, one hand reaching up to wipe over his beard. “Not enough spankings as a child. If I whipped your ass a bit more you’d have come out with some discipline instead of growing up the snot nosed brat you are now!” The insults continued on, the man growing frustrated with no one in sight. He wanted this man dead, his hatred was as rapid fire as the gun in his hand. Those tired eyes of his darted around the docks, but found nothing but crates, piers, and the night sky. Footsteps began to ratter fast behind him, and by the time he turned around, the other man was already upon him. There was nothing he could do; the gun knocked from his hands and a solid fist implanted into his jaw. Down he went to one knee, and after shaking off the blow he glared up at the hulk looming over him. He was a burly Japanese man, wild eyes and shaggy hair matching suit hanging down around his face. His breathing was categorized more as heavy growls, the anger no long an emotion he felt, but the nucleus of his existence. The man gripped his fists tightly, watching the older man kneel there frozen. This was it, the moment the two of them had dreamed about. This was the point where one would live… and the other would die. Smoothing back his hair, Raizo Tenzan began to grin wickedly at the old man, a slow chuckle breaking past pearly whites. In a flowing motion he pulled the old man up by the collar of his shirt, whipping out a Desert Eagle and pointing it to the temple. He drew close to him, smiling the entire time with malice written in his eyes and in his mind. “When you get to hell, old man,” he hissed carefully, “make sure to look up and say hi to mother for me.” In the sound of a gunshot everything faded from sight, and moved along to another scene as Italian lyrics were wailed overhead. The gun was in another person’s hand now… the identity revealed as the brand name BLITZ could be read on the holder’s wrist. Inphino Blitz stood there, blood leaking down the side of his head and streaking from the corner of his lips, his entire body looked like it was the center of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Weakly he looked ahead, but amidst his fatigue was an anger in his eyes only rivaled to one man… and that was Akiyama. But as the scene shifted around, it was not Akiyama who forced such animosity in the Black Dragon Master. Instead… it was the one person Inphino cared for, trusted, and even loved… Mina de Margo, the other person who stood near the base of that volcano, was on the other end of that gun… and she had the fear of God in her. In a white holding cell deep within one of Japan’s secure holding facilities, a tired man sat on a white cot. His shirt and blue jeans were dingy, his red hair near brick color from grime and grit. He sat there staring ahead at the white wall across from him, not blinking, not moving… only thinking. There was one thing on his mind that persecuted him day in and day out… and it was life itself. The life given to him… and the man responsible for that life… He scratched at the Korean tag on his shirt slowly, glancing down at it before looking back up at the wall. A woman scorned… Soteria stood leaning against the railing of an apartment balcony overlooking Tokyo. The dark maiden was always clad in black PVC pants with heels and a black bra, her long flowing raven hair sweeping against her lower back. Those dark eyes of hers were lowered to the nightlights. Lately, she’d been craving attention from one man… the man she had and now had lost. He had betrayed her, left her and her friends for what he called GOOD. What was good in this world? Good and evil was always a matter of opinion rather than definition. He was the only man she had ever loved… and if she couldn’t have him… no one would have him… The photo of Inphino Blitz was crumpled in her hand, released to plummet to its demise. The girl watched all this, the music beginning to come to a close. There was one last scene to unfold before her, a blurry sight that made her lean in and squint to view. A man lived by his ethics; there were things that he refused to do anymore. Redemption from the sins of a past life, but in these drastic times, he found himself going back into his old habits. And he found himself sinning yet again with his blade. The katana was embedded into the man’s thick chest. Inphino gritted his teeth as he pulled a slick saber out of Akiyama’s chest. Blood ran down onto the steel gangplank, drops sneaking past the metal mesh and sizzling into the molten lava below. Akiyama looked down at his chest, a look of disbelief even on his own eyes. With the beast stunned Inphino snapped across his face with a hard roundhouse kick, coming around with his back facing Akiyama and backflipping towards him. His feet kicked Akiyama dead in his face, and the seven footer teetered back over the edge towards the molten lava. Inphino is known for his graceful balance and agility… but in light of the recent battle, even he could not keep his footing. He went over as well, two yells singing out their doom.
