Date: 2005-11-27 The pulsing flash of 12:00 reflects off of a dirty face at the same time that the sunlight battles its way through damaged window blinds. The twin sized bed, with a pillowcase-less pillow and only one comforter, partially covers a naked man who lies upon a mattress; mattress of who’s sheets have been pushed off the bed and now lie scuffled with dirty clothes on the ground. The face resembles that of PRIME superstar Tyler O’Neill, and it’s a sight to get a little humor out of. His mouth is cocked open and a streamline of drool is followed down his pillow to his chin which sits in nothing but a saliva puddle. Mmm. A television remains on across the room and the sound of the anchorman is heard… "…it’s 2:37 in the afternoon and it’s a beautiful autumn afternoon in San Diego. Our top story, ‘Was Brock E. the Reason Why?’ That story later today." A grunt reveals itself from the sleeping O’Neill and he begins to shift in his bed and spins around to lie on his back. After a short moment, he rubs his eyes and then opens them. Staring at the ceiling for a moment, he sits up and begins to make his way out of bed. Sitting on the edge of his bed, he plants his feet on the carpeted floor; kicking away a Cherry Coke can and a condom wrapper. Tyler always did play his cards right. Getting out of bed, he reaches his arms up straight and stretches his bare body while exposing a loud yawn. He begins to make his way to the door, but before stepping out into the dim hall of his apartment, he takes a look at the calendar hanging on the wall where he focuses on the large black WORKOUT 6AM written across the days remaining in November. Tyler shrugs and makes his way out the door while distinguishingly scratching his balls. Tyler always did aim for professionalism. A short way down the not so long hallway, Tyler walks into another room and soon switches on the light revealing heaven; heavily recognized as the bathroom. He stands tall and proud in front of the porcelain king, and the sound of urine plunging into the depths of gold tinted water is soon heard. Mounting his hand on the wall in front of him and letting out another yawn, Tyler continues to take care of a favored morning tradition. The sound begins to soften and soon Tyler shakes it three times. Tyler wasn’t playing with himself. Not even bothering to flush, nor turn off the light, Tyler makes his way out of the bathroom and back into his bedroom where he collapses back on his bed. He pulls the comforter over him and closes his eyes. Tyler O’Neill: Yea, he’s ready.
