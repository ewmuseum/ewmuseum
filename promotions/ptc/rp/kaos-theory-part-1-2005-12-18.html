Kaos Theory Part 1

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 Broken. Unstable. A shell. There was no other way of putting it; clear and simply he was falling apart. His mental illness was taking hold of him and driving him to new levels of despair he previously thought were unattainable. He could cope with the sleepless nights, the tormenting nightmares, but the constant physical battle against his new personality was taking its toll. Cyrus felt unbelievably drained by this raging war inside his head. Yet, somehow he had managed to keep his mind focused when in the ring. It was the only thing keeping him from a total breakdown. His social life was non-existent; as was his love life. Cyrus had no family left and no friends. For the first time in his adult years; he was truly scared. Not just that he would lose complete control of his body and become his own prisoner. But because he knew that Kaos was unadulterated filth. He seemed to have no weakness almost like a machine in his ways. Cyrus went to sleep worrying about what new problems Kaos would bring to the morning. His life was now made up of the odd moment of tranquillity, where he could be at ease even if for only a few hours. The rest of the time was spent battling a seemingly relentless foe. Constantly struggling against Kaos to retain control of his own functions, Cyrus found himself blacking out as to the happenings of the physical world. Kaos enjoyed having full control over Cyrus’s body and used it to satisfy his desires, his only motivation being that of creating misery for everyone around him to experience. A trip to the shops endeared scrabbling through scores of angry faces, people baying for Cyrus’s blood unknowingly hating a different man. Cyrus was refused service at many places and forced to change his habits, shopping in places even the scum of society would be embarrassed about. He felt like an outsider in his own home town. He’d finally risen from the shadows to be noticed by his own people, yet not for who he was. After he’d exited his teenage years, Cyrus was introduced to ‘The Voice’. It seemed that a few years of abuse that he’d suffered hadn’t just been pushed away to the back of his mind; it’d been stored then multiplied by the feelings of loneliness he’d then felt. Cyrus often wondered if he’d been a better person had he not witnessed his fathers suicide. Would he have felt more in touch with the world? Rather than feeling like the outcast he had become? ‘The Voice’ had never been able to fully take over for long periods of time. Unlike Kaos it had to settle for being heard and letting’s its opinions out into the world. Usually this was enough for the manipulating monstrosity, though sometimes it felt unfulfilled living in the backseat of Cyrus’s life. So every now and then it’d take matters into its own hands, teasing and twisting, playing with Cyrus’s emotions as well as his life. ‘The Voice’ and Cyrus had tried to live together as one, but ‘The Voice’ had betrayed Cyrus’s trust. Hammering another nail into Cyrus’s already shut emotional coffin. Cyrus had tried to form relationships before and had succeeded, yet each time it’d ended in disaster. Always the one he’d loved being snatched from him in some cruel twist of fate. So Cyrus went alone, the only way he’d always known. Cyrus had nearly always been a one-man team, but he had never felt so loathed by every human around him. Some people didn’t agree with his morals but diversity played host as a key factor in modern society. People got along even if they were slightly different. Now because of his other side Cyrus was hated by all. It wasn’t going to be long before a spark ignited the mighty explosion that was brewing. Somebody was lighting a torch to place at Cyrus’s feet. The flame had gone out the first time, they weren’t going to let it be extinguished the second. ------ The same touch of the cold harsh floor stroked his face. How he was boring of this tiresome performance Kaos was making him rehearse again and again. Each time his blurred eyes focused he wondered where he’d awake. Wondered what destruction would be around him, what consequences he’d have to face. What he and Kaos did not know was that the real show was only just about to start. Cyrus managed to find some energy left in his weary body as he rose to his shaky legs. Trembling he could feel his muscles aching under fatigue. His body felt as if it’d been inside a washing machine on the fastest spin-cycle possible. His own mind buzzing with pain deafening him to his surroundings. The realization suddenly kicked in. The familiar chair in the corner, the comfy blue leather suite all the Nip/Tuck videos scattered across the floor. The fragrance that greeted Cyrus’s nostrils was not a pleasant one. Faeces and urine hung in the air, Cyrus sensed that although he hadn’t left the house under Kaos’s influence, he’d done something he would never want to remember. The laminated flooring was wet as Cyrus walked round his living room towards the stench, which seemed to be coming from the kitchen. He had to hold his breath as he stepped into the seemingly unknown. Ironic how his own kitchen contained something he could never have fathomed possible. Cyrus nearly wretched at the site in front of him. A girl of about twenty one of Asian origin lay before him, a bloodied mess. Her dark hair was matted to her head covered in a crimson and white mixture. Cyrus tried so hard to keep the acidic bile in his stomach as his eyes gazed over the situation. So many thoughts ran through his head, his mind was ticking over at a million miles per hour. ‘I can’t have done this?’ ‘Who is she?’ ‘What was she doing in my house?’ ‘What if somebody’s phoned the Police?’ ‘Is she dead?’ ‘Shit I hope he didn’t kill her.’ ‘Should I phone an ambulance?’ ‘Damn, that smell is putrid.’ After a few moments Cyrus tried to clear his head and think logically. He needed to phone an ambulance whether he was responsible or not. He went to move out of the kitchen when a hand grabbed his leg. ‘Please, help me.’ A muffled plea of help escaped her lips as Cyrus stared wide-eyed at the young woman. Her accent was clearly from Manchester so Cyrus presumed she was local. He gently picked her up in his arms and carried her frail, broken body into his living room and placed her on his expensive blue leather sofa. She slowly opened her eyes and Cyrus found himself looking directly into an abyss, a sea of white that never ended. The poor girl was blind; Cyrus took a step back in shock and took a moment to collect his thoughts again. ‘She’s blind, why was she here?’ ‘How did she get here?’ ‘What the F**K am I going to do?’ The shrill sound of the telephone ringing brought Cyrus back to the real world and made him make a choice. He thought whether or not he should ignore it, yet he was intrigued as to who it could be. He never received any incoming calls and couldn’t imagine who it could have been. ‘Hello’ ‘Hello?’ ‘F**k this!’ The phone fragmented into tiny pieces of wires and circuitry as Cyrus slammed it back onto the desk. ‘I don’t have time to play any games now!’ Screaming at himself more than anyone else. The girl jumped as Cyrus raised his voice, which brought him back to her, the problem. "I think it’s time you did something Cyrus, rather than just letting that poor girl lie their, dirtying our sofa with her filth." The Voice was joking with Cyrus even given the seriousness of the situation. "I suggest you take her up to the bathroom and give her a shower. Kaos made an awful mess didn’t he?" The Voice let out a snigger after this remark, obviously very pleased with itself. ‘I’m disgusted to share myself with the both of you, I may take some things to the extreme, but this is going beyond anything I’d ever even imagine doing. This is just sick.’ Cyrus let out a tear as the reality of the situation started to creep in. He genuinely felt sorry for the girl lying there, she didn’t deserve to be a part of this. "She’s a hooker Cyrus. She got paid, so it’s not like Kaos robbed her." Again the voice let out a wry laugh as it probed deeper into Cyrus’s emotional pain. "Just throw her outside; her pimp said we could do whatever we liked." Suddenly it dawned on Cyrus. The Voice was able to witness what Kaos was doing, it wasn’t kept in the dark like he was. ‘Tell me, is this the truth? Are you a prostitute?’ Cyrus tried to speak calmly so that he didn’t scare the girl anymore than she already had been. "Yes, I am. Please no more Sir. I can’t take anymore beatings like that, my body is my living. I can’t, please Sir. I can’t…" She began crying, tears staining her reddened cheeks even more; burning and stinging her delicate face. Cyrus had to look away as he felt his emotions taking over. He started to remember what it was like to feel for somebody, to care for them and to want them to be alright. He phoned for an ambulance and didn’t think of the consequences. He decided he’d act on impulse for a change. He’d been thinking too much recently. ‘Why not?’ he thought. He had nothing to lose anyway. ----- Cyrus rode in the back of the ambulance with the paramedics tightly grasping the girl’s hand, constantly reassuring her. It was a long, tiresome ride, especially for a man who hadn’t been sleeping well. They arrived at the hospital within half an hour and Cyrus jumped out of the cramped space and stretched his legs. He followed the medical team into the hospital and was told to report to reception. He knew he couldn’t do that. He knew he’d have to leave her on her own. He’d asked her back at his house if it was ok to leave her. She agreed and said that worse had happened before. Cyrus couldn’t imagine anything worse than what had happened to her by his hand. He felt sick just thinking about it. His breathing finally slowed down as he realized that this episode was over, yet he knew that another one would soon begin. Kaos was unrelenting in his actions and wouldn’t let up for a second. He knew that as each day went by, Cyrus was becoming weaker and weaker. It wouldn’t be long before he wasn’t able to regain control. Cyrus prayed every night and hoped that, that day wouldn’t come. He took in a breath of fresh air from outside the hospital and let it cleanse his lungs of the disgusting stale atmosphere he’d had to endure before. He needed to think of a way to weaken Kaos, but he really did seem like an immovable object in Cyrus’s quest to sanity. Though his mind was still not clear, still not focused. What if the semen in the girl’s hair was analysed? What if she called the Police and pressed charges against him? It was too late now. He’d walked away. Walked out of her life and hopefully would never see her again. Another consequence he had to live with. Another choice he’d made without thinking, why did he keep doing this? Why did he keep putting himself under this much pressure? He hadn’t been at his best mentally for months and yet still he insisted on competing, still he insisted on putting his physical health on the line. And for what? A chance at a little prize money? A little taste of stardom? Cyrus didn’t need any more cash, a clause in his Lights Out Wrestling contract meant that when the federation closed he got a payout. Money wasn’t a problem anymore. As for fame, he’d never really yearned to be famous, never yearned to be a Deville or a Rollins’. All Cyrus Skar wanted to be was normal. And that was something that he feared he would never be. So why enter a tournament below his best with nothing to gain? Because it was the only thing keeping Cyrus sane. It was the last part of his life that actually meant something to him. Competing in that squared circle, no matter how tough it was on him, was everything he needed. It made him focus, even if only for a short period. His mental illness faded, for the only time in his life he was starting to feel normal. Then the deafening striking of the bell, the end of the match and the end of normality. Cyrus needed the fight, needed to wrestle. However clichéd it may have seemed, it was his life. For now Cyrus was actually relaxed, Kaos’s latest escapade was over and he could start to think ahead to his match against Ember, possibly the toughest match of his entire career. He couldn’t let the problems in his personal life distract him. He decided he’d not stress himself out anymore by participating in the cow herding that was ‘Public Transport’ instead he’d have a brisk walk home. The exercise would do him good. He entered his house with a sigh of relief and was greeted by the awful stench he’d almost forgotten about. He wandered back into the kitchen and started to run a sink of hot water, he’d have to try and clean this up tonight before it got any worse. His eyes drifted away from the sink and towards the toaster, stopping on a cream piece of paper. Cyrus, looking bewildered meandered through the muck and fluid on the floor and picked up the note. ‘The explosion’s been brewing Cyrus. All it needs is a little spark. The drugs didn’t work and that was a shame. It was a nice idea poorly executed. But don’t worry Cyrus. There’s still going to be a lot of fireworks. I hope you’ll enjoy them.’ Cyrus tried to make a run for it but it was too late, before he knew what was happening he was out cold on the floor.
