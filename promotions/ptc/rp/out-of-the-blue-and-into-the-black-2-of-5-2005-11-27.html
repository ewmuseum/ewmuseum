Out of the blue, and into the black (2 of 5)

Date: 2005-11-27 Great. I'll be known as "the paw-print guy". There's plenty of cursing in the above space, but I find it less crass to let people insert their own expletives, since we all have our levels. One man's cunt is another's… operation gone horribly wrong. Hurriedly scrambling back on track… Before pen even begins long-distance flirtation with paper, long before their first nib-poking embrace, I've had my application stamped with… considering he doesn't leave the apartment, I don't want to know. What I do know, is that breathing through my mouth would be a good idea until it dries, then I can get with the scraping. Maybe they'll have piles. Of the in-tray variety, that is. There can be one for ex-GTT winners, maybe throw the ex-Global champions into it as well. Then a "banned and exiled" pile. Yup, gotta have one of them. And "other", of course, where would the world be without an unassuming heading to dump all the other crap under. And lastly, shoved behind even the banned pile, can be the short stack of those who couldn't complete the simple task of not making a mess on their entrance form. At least I can sleep safely knowing mine won't be the only one, and will have a good bunch of coffee-mug rings to keep it company. He looks proud of himself. Or proud of me. Or he wants me to die. Or I need to feed him. Or he resents the fact that I dare to sit in the chair he was eying up. As you can tell, I'm not one of those who can read their pet's signals. And now it's a narrowed look of 'I bet he's got a monologue going on'. Fucking sly things. His name is Jones. Yes, just Jones. No 'Mister', since he'd hate me for that, and so would I. And yes, he's named after the cat from Alien. Technically, he's Jones VI, but I found counting pets within their names rather morbid. That, and I think he'd probably appreciate not knowing that 5 felines had been and gone before him. The instant panic-espresso hit of paranoia would only get in the way of sleeping, or his favoured pastime of licking himself. I'm sure that's what you wanted to know. You probably know as much about me as you did a few minutes ago, huh? Probably even less, as I decide to sedate your imagination. Take it as a blessing, because odds are, you don't want to get to know me. It's not that I'm a bad person, well, not 'bad' bad. I haven't killed a guy or pushed a handicapped person over… anywhere outside my head. Just take my word for it. The further you are away from me, the less hassle there'll be for you. Try not to be intrigued. Honestly. It's not that interesting… Well, I'd better leave that one up to you when it all hits the fan. No, I don't plan to hit a fan. Just think of it like this: I. Don't. Matter. Unlike some, I've come to terms with this. I'm a nobody, I'm peripheral. If GTT5 was only 64 guys, I'd be swapped over to the "yeah, right, bye-bye" pile. I can pretty much bet that upon hearing my name, people will ask "who?". It's not an insult, it's to be expected. I'm not going to start putting my fist through the drywall because people pick me to lose, and I'll preserve my dignity by not rambling on about "oh, nobody picks me, haha, I'll lose, oh well, haha, my face hurts from gluing this smile in place haha ha ha ha". Out of the blue. What I do will not be for attention, no matter how some will inevitably read it. Think of it as my last stand. Into the black.
