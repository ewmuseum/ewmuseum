Partial Stress

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 The rain pounces down on a black limousine as it pushes through the beginnings of Hurricane Frances in Florida. The limo has it’s lights on and is driving very slowly down the two lane highway. The road is deserted and there is only a sheet of rain as far as the eye can see. The limo pushes on through the rain and the two inches of standing water on the road. The swamps on both sides of the road are flooded, so naturally the water has spilt on to the road. The driver squints as he looks over the dash and into the abyss that is in front of him. The rain is coming down so hard and fast and thick, its almost like a fog. Visibility is only about twenty feet. Sitting in the back are Tony Stecchino and Stevie Nickels. Stecchino looks out the window with an excited expression on his face. Nickels shows a bit more worry. Stecchino has a white T-shirt and some surf shorts on. Nickels is decked out in a black on black suit. Might be better that way, the stain wont show if he wets himself. The driver is having a hard time controlling the car in the sustained fifty mile per hour winds and the hydroplaning the car is doing over the standing water. Nickels looks worried, but the driver is going through intense hell in the front seat. He has never driven in anything like this. Though if he survives, the ten thousand dollars in which he was paid will come in handy, but if crashes, he knows he will die one way or another. Nickels takes his right hand and slaps Tony on the left arm to get his attention. Stecchino who seems to be entranced looking into the storm comes to and looks back over at Stevie. Stecchino: What’s up? Nickels licks his lips, looks out the window and bites his lower lip. Nickels: This! Nickels very adamant in his answer as he lifts both shoulders and points out both windows. Nickels: Why the hell did you drag out into a storm like this? Stecchino looks over at Stevie with a bit of disdain and looks back out into the storm. He closes his eyes. Stecchino: I thought that if we came down into the storm that maybe, for some ungodly reason… We might find Melissa on our old estate down here. Stecchino opens his eyes and looks back over and Stevie. Who’s look of fear then changes to a look of sorrow. He looks down at the floor of the limo, sorry that he over reacted, he should have known that Tony was down about this whole thing. He has said a few things here and there. Stecchino: Our anniversary is this Friday night and well… He pauses and thinks over his words. When he gets choked up he tends to mix up his words and things don’t come out as clear as he would like them to. Then he as to explain them and he usually puts his foot in his mouth and things are just well… Complicated. Stecchino: I just miss her dude, I mean you see what I have become with our her. A monster, I absolutely hate who I have become Stevie. I don’t wanna be this power hungry killer anymore. I need stability, I need something pure in my life. I need her. Tony looks out the window for a second, then he quickly looks down at the floor and then back outside, before finally settling on the floor again. He slowly looks at Stevie. Stecchino: She completes me, she was everything good in my life. I am nothing with out her.
