No Pity

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme Rumble (Stevie Vertigo RP #2) **The scene opens up to a shot of Stevie Vertigo, sitting in a smoke-filled room. The room is dark, and also hazy. Despite the darkness, we can see that Stevie is wearing some sort of white suit, and his huffing back a cigar, thus the smokey air.** **We can vaguely see what appears to be a pool table in the background, leading us to believe that Stevie may be sitting in one of his billiard rooms. Stevie lets out one more billowing puff of cigar smoke, before proceeding to speak...** - STEVIE - Almost time for the Extreme Rumble, those involved are pleading their cases, pleading to the powers that be, and to the fans, that they are the real deal. All these ambitious Extreme championship hopefuls, piling on their bullshit, spewing out pity-stricken statement, after pity-stricken statement. All of these guys, ICE, Moraless, Tony Vigilante, they're all going into the Battle Royal with the mind set of "I'm the true Extreme icon," and they are all going in planning on spill all the blood possible on their way to the win. **Stevie stands up, and begins to walk about the room, clinching the cigar between his index and middle fingers on his right hand. Stevie takes another puff, and continues talking...** - STEVIE - However, it seems as though every single one of these guys is out of their element. Sure, they may have been doing this longer than your's truly, and sure, they may talk a big game, but you can see that they're nervous. With every quivering syllable that exits their mouth, with every sorry stare in their eyes, you can see it. You can see that they are afraid of what lies in wait for them in a few weeks time. You can SEE the desperate attempts at covering their fear. Each one of them spout at the mouth, how they are the REAL extreme stars of Prime Time Central, but you can feel it in your bones, every viewer out their, every redneck, every pee-on in a trailor, gathered around their glowing screens of radiation, watching these men of the PTC Extreme Championship Division, tell them how "they're the best," and that "THEY are the epitomy of HARD-CORE." **Stevie reaches into a nearby desk, and slowly pulls out what appears to be a hand gun. Stevie also pulls out a cloth, and begins to clean the firearm...** - STEVIE - Stories of hard times, stories of making their family proud, stories of wars, and crime, yet for some reason, despite all of the apparent agony, and wondering, these stories still do not compare. The stories do not compare to the real struggle. These stories to not compare to the real emotion that runs through all of the hell holes of the world, the gutters, the ghettos. The stories of the men of the PTC Extreme Title division are stories not of hate, or brutality, they're stories of building images, monikers, if you will. Boys, your stories are fake, or if not, they are contrived. **Stevie begins walking out of the room, but not before loading the fire arm in his hand, and clicking it, ensuring the deadly weapon is in position to kill. Stevie then puts his hand containing the weapon to his side, and takes the cigar out of the clinch of his teeth, allowing for him to let more billowing cigar smoke pour from his mouth...** - STEVIE - Just because the blood that runs through my veins are bluer than any socialite walking the planet, does not in any way, shape or form mean that I haven't lived, and experienced, at the same time, some of the most horrifying crimes, or acts of brutality known to man. Acts way worse than any Vigilante can think of, than and Kraze can cause. My whole life has been spent with "business" being taken care of all around me, all the time. I was raised in a house where slime balls were taken care of, and a house where there were no exceptions, whether you were a poor musician, or a wealthy record producer, if you were in the way of something that my dad, and his associates wanted, you were dealt with. **Stevie continues walking down a hallway, and continues to hold the gone at his side in his right hand, and the cigar being passed back and forth between his hand, and his mouth...** - STEVIE - And now, I see all of these "blood thirsty," "violent" men, getting prepared to go into a violent, hostile battle with not so much as an idea of the brutality that lies on the other side for them. I'm not talking about the plastic attack of Moraless, or the desire to be the next hot star that lies in Ickwa Hamunya, I'm talking about the next level of violence that lies in yours truly. I'm talking about the merciless, unbias killing mind of a man that that at birth, was baptized in the water of a corporate society, I'm talking about Stevie Vertigo. This rumble is all mine. That number one spot is all mine. And that Extreme championship, soon enough, will be all mine. **Stevie reaches a door, and begins to open it...** - STEVIE - I'm not sugar coating the truth. Nor am I padding my "image." I am simply letting everyone out there in on what will be the grim reality. I want the Extreme title, of course I do, but I also have to get rid of something that has followed me around my whole career. I must get rid of the assumption that I'm the rich kid that has never had to fight for anything in his life. **Stevie finally opens the door to the room, and proceeds to walk inside. Once inside, we see a man sitting in a chair. The only light in the room dangles over the roped and taped man in the chair, and gleems off of the nervously-sweaty forehead of the frightened man. Stevie begins to circle the man, while at the same time, showing off the hand gun in his hand...** - STEVIE - This is the connotation that drives me. Every poor kid that sees the Rolex on my wrist, and makes a smart ass comment about "never had to work a day for that," or every athlete that steps into the same ring as me, and takes me for a soft, rich boy, these are the people that drive me. I've never gotten pity, from anyone. When my uncle Jack gunned down on Santa Monica boulevard, I never shed a tear, because I knew that someone else in my family, someone I looked up, was going to be killed by a gun next, but then I get all of these pity-cases, talking about their hard times, talking about how they've been through hell, and back to get to this rumble. Fuck your sorrow. Fuck your pity. And Fuck you false sense of reality. I've lived around death, I've lived around fear, I get opposed with a situation, I face it, I don't sit around, bitching and moaning about it, and in this rumble, I'm going to deal with all of these cases of "poor me" syndrome, in the most violent, unthinkable ways. **Stevie bends over beside the man in the chair, almost as if to talk in his hear. While Stevie talks to the camera, he still looks at the man...** - STEVIE - There are going to be tragedies in this match, and that is going to lead to more sorrow, and THAT is going to lead to more casualties in my way. Extreme is not a word to be defined, it's a way to deal with the PTC's charity cases, and deal with them is exactly what I'm going to do, at the PTC's Extreme Rumble. **Stevie stands up straight, and points the gun into the man's cheek...** - STEVIE - Dealing with problems. DEALING with problems! I have... (now back in the man's face) a problem! And it doesn't make me happy, when these problems won't go away, so I have to make them! **Stevie cocks the gun, and we hear a gun blast...** BOOM!! **But as the blast goes off, the picture turns to dark.**
