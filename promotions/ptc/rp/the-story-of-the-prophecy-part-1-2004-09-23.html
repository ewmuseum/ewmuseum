The Story of The Prophecy (Part 1)!

23 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-23 {Dream lofty dreams, as you dream, so shall you become. Your vision is the promise of what you one day shall be: your ideal is the prophecy of what you shall at last unveil.} ~James Allen~ {Think of how many religions attempt to validate themselves with prophecy. Think of how many people rely on these prophecies, however vague, however unfulfilled, to support or prop up their beliefs. Yet has there ever been a religion with the prophetic accuracy and reliability of science?} ~Carl Sagan (1934 - 1996) ~ ~To truly understand ones drive and determination in life, one must go to the beginning where it all started. One must see how a person’s destiny takes shape in life. For me it happened in the very beginning since birth. For me “The Prophecy” of John Adams started when I first opened my eyes unto this crazy sometimes mixed up world of ours. But unlike others who may fall prey to the trials and tribulations of everyday life as they become mindless sheep to survive, I on the other hand decided to evolve. Evolve in my life not to survive, not to be another mindless sheep, and certainly not to have a 9 to 5 job just to live my life wanting to be like everybody else! No I decided to evolve past the means of normal man and take control of my life, I decided when I saw the destiny laid before me not to have any type of authority preordain the way I live! I decided to live my life by one thing and one thing alone……..”The Prophecy”! ~ 10-31-76 Time: 5:30 PM Location: Riverside General Hospital Paternity Ward -----In the depths of the cold dark hospital near the paternity ward where new life begins, our attention is drawn to a beautiful young woman being carted off in a wheelchair. The screams of the young woman could be heard all the way to the morgue. And why not, on this day of all days on all hallows eve, this young lady is about to give birth. The nurse carting her off to prep her for delivery, but wait there’s the nurse and the lady about to give birth, but where is the father? This thought is carried with her as everybody in the ward watches her being checked in and carted off. ----- Head Nurse: Name? Lady: Lorraineee! Loraine Adams! Head Nurse: Great and your insurance carrier’s are? Loraine Adams: Just get me in there now! Nurse pushing her: Yes she is already 10 centimeters dilated and her water has broke! Head Nurse: How far apart are the contractions? Nurse pushing her: 2 minutes apart! Head Nurse: And the father? Nurse pushing her: Mr. Frank Adams will not be here because of a previous engagement at work. Head Nurse: Alright wheel her in to room 24! -----The Nurse pushes Loraine into the room with a sadden look for her situation. Just think a husband that would rather work than see his own child enter into this world. But enough of that, with new life about to be born he continues to wheel her into the room. 6 hours and 29 minutes it took in labor, a minute before the witching hour of all hollows eve before Loraine and Frank’s son was born unto this world. But what was next for the child was nothing but a constant struggle to belong in the chaos we call life. ----- Head Doctor: So Mrs. Adams have you thought of a name for your child? Loraine Adams: Yes, since this is a child of God’s I have decided on the name John, after one of Jesus most favorite Apostles John. 10-31-81 Time: 6:35 PM Location: The Adams Mansion in La Sierra Riverside. -----Evening time in this beautiful 10 room estate the stars are bright and conditions outside on this all hallows eve could not have been better. Children can be seen outside going to house to house dressed as demons, monsters, and there favorite hero with the goal to obtain the most candy for there greedy little hearts. The atmosphere outside is perfect without a care in the world, but then inside the Adams mansion there is another story. Inside the Adams Mansion there should be joy and happiness for the young John Adams has just turned 5 years old, but there is no sign of a party of any kind. No balloons, cake, friends for him to play with, and no presents at all. But there wasn’t any surprise for it happened year after year. And as we go inside the majestic but lifeless building we see in the very spacious Victorian style dining room a young John Adams sitting by himself on what is supposed to be his special day. ----- Young John Adams: Happy Birthday to me! Happy Birthday to me! Happy Birthday to John! Happy Birthday to me! -----The loneliness in his heart weighs so much that he begins to cry. But out of the darkness from the hallway comes John Adams mother Loraine. Dressed in her bathrobe holding a cupcake with a single candle in it she walks in. ----- Loraine Adams: Oh John I’m so sorry, but don’t you cry my son. Everything is going to be ok. I brought you a surprise….look! I know it’s not a birthday cake but your father refuse to bye one for you because you didn’t pick up your toys. -----As John Adams mother comes in to the dining room, her sons thick bruises are more noticeable located on his arm and face from his father’s idea of punishment for his child. ----- Loraine Adams: Honey I’m sorry, but you know your father is trying to get that deal to have his company build the county Mental Hospital. So your father has been a little stressed out lately. But dry your tears my darling and make a whish. -----Her son blows out his candle with a look of happiness from his mother. ----- Young John Adams: Mommy, I want to tell you what I wished! Loraine Adams: But son if you do it won’t come true. Young John Adams: But mommy it’s a whish for the both of us! I wished for me and you to live happily ever after, oh and that daddy would die a horrible death! -----Just then John’s father walks into the dining room also dressed in a bathrobe. With a look of evil and hatred in his eyes you know he heard every word his son made. ----- Frank Adams: Why you little shit!! Don’t I provide for you and your mother? Don’t I provide a wonderful place for you to live? And this is how you repay me? ----- He begins to walk towards his son with the intent to hurt him. But Loraine steps in-between his path. ----- Loraine Adams: Stop Frank! Stop your scaring him! Frank Adams: Loraine will you just get the hell out of the way! You stupid bitch! You’re always talking his side over mine! Don’t you know that if you continue to treat him like this, all it’s going to do is make him soft!! But who cares right Loraine! Who cares that my slut of a wife would rather take the side of her pansy ass son over her husband’s right! Well Loraine I’ll make you care you stupid bitch! -----A husband and father are supposed to be a caring, loving, and nurturing influence for his wife and kids. But this is not the case, as Frank Adams like a man possessed begins to hit his wife over and over again in the face. The young John Adams cowers in fear over to one of the corners of the room crying his eyes out. ----- Young John Adams: H.Hap..Happy Birthday to me…….. (SOB.) (To be continued!)
