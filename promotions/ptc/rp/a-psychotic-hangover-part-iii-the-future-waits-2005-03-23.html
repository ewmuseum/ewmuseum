A Psychotic Hangover, Part III: The Future Waits

23 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-23 My vision was blurred, damn near gone, but my hearing was just fine. I could barely make out the two figures carrying me down the hallway, but I could hear their footsteps as they were dragging me along. I felt out of it, almost as if I was hammered off of my ass. They had come into my room to tell me about "exercise time" and I was expecting some free time to stretch my legs in a gym, or outside, but that wasn't the case. The two muscle heads, Franklin and Robert, had returned a few moments later with a nurse who proceeded to stick me, and give me something that damn near knocked me out. I had wished it had, because this blurry vision and drunken feeling just wasn't doing it for me. It wasn't long before the footsteps stopped, and the sounds of keys being rattled around were heard, followed shortly by the sound of a door opening. The door to my room that is. The two did away with me as quickly as possible, and once again, I had been tossed onto the hard floor, and yes, yet again I felt nothing. With my blurry vision, though, I was unable to see if my head bouncing off the floor had busted me open this time or not. I did, somehow, managed to crawl up onto my bed to rest. Evidentially I had a meeting with the head doctor or something today. I needed to rest so this shit would wear off. Didn't need to be acting drunk in a situation that could ultimately mean I was going to get out of this place. I had bigger and better things to be doing. No longer than a couple seconds after I closed my eyes, I heard a noise. A noise of something hitting my window. When I opened my eyes, however, I was no longer in the room at the mental institute. I was in my bedroom back at home. It was also no longer light outside, but instead it was nighttime. I thought to myself for a moment before throwing the covers off of me. Could this have been all a dream? Seemed to be the question I was asking myself a lot. I sat on the edge of my bed now and looked at the window, as if to wait for the sound to happen again. Sure enough it did. I got up, walked to the window, and pulled back the curtains. There on my front lawn was the figure of a woman, the woman that worked at Spencer's Drug Store downtown. Lia. She was throwing rocks at my window, an act usually only done where there are more than one story to a house. I opened up my window and poked my head out into the warm night air. 'What are you doing?' I asked her as she dropped the remainder of the pebbles onto the ground. She didn't say anything, but instead giggled like any girl in love would as she ran to the window. 'You weren't answering the door when I rang the doorbell, and you didn't answer the phone, so I figured I'd let the little girl in me come out and play for a while.' With that, she planted a kiss on me. One I had never experienced in my life... at least not one I had remembered anyway. She finally detached her lips from mine, and removed her tongue from my mouth long enough to let me breathe. My eyes widened and I looked at her with a look of surprise. 'What was that for?' I asked. I wasn't sure if that was the best thing to ask or not, but she looked a little disappointed. 'Well isn't that what boyfriends and girlfriends do when they greet each other? We have been dating for a while now, and we've been saying hi like that for quite some time as well.' She said as she looked at me with an eyebrow raised. 'Are you feeling ok?' She asked, her face changing to a look of concern. 'I feel fine. I just...' I didn't allow myself to finish. I didn't want her freaking out when I told her I didn't remember that we were going out, if that was actually the case. 'Yeah, I'm fine. Just kind of tired. Confused too. Life seems to be throwing all kinds of shit my way the last few days. I don't know. Maybe I'm trying to concentrate too hard too. I've got a big opportunity right now, and I'd sure enough like to grab it.' I ended my ranting right there, I didn't need to just go on and on about it. I'm sure if we've been dating for a while now, she's already heard it time and time again. Her hand was now on my forehead, checking to see if I was warm or not I suppose. 'Well you aren't warm or anything, so I guess you're ok. And don't worry too much about Seven of the Best. You always do better when you aren't worried about it. You saw what happened in your match with Drake Coleman. You worried once, just a little bit that the IWL World Title curse might actually affect the outcome of the match, and sure enough, it got to you enough that you lost. So yeah, just don't worry about it.' She smiled when she was done, and gazed into my eyes, and I caught myself staring back into hers. 'Come here.' She said as she leaned forward a little looking for another kiss. I wasted no time giving her what she was expecting as I leaned in as well. Our lips met again, as far as I knew, only for the second time, but it was just as sweet as the first time. Or it was until the following words came. 'Is he kissing the glass?' I was unsure who it was, because Lia's and my lips were locked, and my eyes were shut tight as this magical moment continued. 'I think so. He probably hasn't been laid in years.' I heard, coming from a different voice this time. I quickly opened my eyes. Sure enough, it was no longer night time, and I was no longer at my house back in Lost Hills. There were Franklin and Robert, standing there with shit eating grins on their faces. I could feel my face turn red, but the expression that came upon it was not one of embarrassment. 'So do you guys not have to fucking knock or what?' I asked with a stern tone. 'We did you crazy bastard. But we don't have to wait for you to answer to enter the room. Whether or not you are making out with the window or not. But anyway, it's time for your meeting. Doc says it won't take very long, so let’s go.' With that, a nurse walks in with a syringe in hand. My sight had come back now, and I straightened up quickly when I seen her walk in. 'Yeah... NO!' I exclaimed. 'If I'm going to actually talk to this guy, I don't need to be on any fucking drugs. Get that thing away from me!' With that the nurse turned around and walked away. I figured it wouldn't be that easy, but it was. Both Franklin and Robert motioned for me to "come along" then, and so I did. Get this over with, and hopefully get out of here today. That was my goal anyway. I followed the two for a while, watching them make sure I was still following them every few seconds or so. It took a while to navigate through all the hallways and stuff to finally get to the big guy's office. Robert, the bald twin, walked inside the office to let him know I was there. I kind of looked around a bit and checked out the name on the door while I waited to meet this guy. "Dr. McCain” Sounds like a douche bag I thought. Robert then poked his head out the door and nodded, signaling for me to enter. Franklin went in first, and I followed. As I entered the fairly decent sized office, I was surprised to see not a man sitting at the desk, but a woman. The little name plate sitting on the front of the desk read "Dr. Jacqulyn McCain." I was certain a smile came across my face immediately when my eyes met with the attractive middle aged woman. She stood up and extended a hand. 'Welcome Steven, I've been waiting to meet you.' She greeted, as if talking to any normal human being. Treating me like I deserved to be treated. 'Glad to actually meet you too.' I said in return. I didn't waste time though, and as soon as we all were seated, I began. 'Now look, I don't know who called and told you all I needed to be here, or why I needed to be here, but I don't honestly think I need to be here. I've been "this way" or however you want to put it for the last eight, nine years of my life with no problems. Yes I admitted myself to Southwind in Kansas a year ago, but that was dumb of me. And I just think...' She stopped me where I was, in the middle of my long winded rambling rant. 'Steven, I didn't bring you here to question you being here. Nor did I bring you in here so I could listen to you beg to be released. From what I've heard about you since you showed up, I've come to the conclusion that, even though you may act out on certain occasions, everyone does from time to time. And your attack on Dr. Hornung, well, to be honest, I would have done the same thing. He no longer works for us. So as far as you being here, you are being released… Today.' She then stopped. It seemed too damn good to be true. 'Are you...?' I didn't even finish before she put up a hand, and nodded her head. She then handed me a few pieces of paper. 'All that I ask of you in return is that you attend sessions with my set of hand-selected doctors, and myself from time to time, every two weeks. Just to sit down and maybe discuss anything on your mind, maybe try a few methods to see if we can't cure you of anything that may be wrong.' She paused, and I signed the areas that were marked for my signature. I handed her back the pieces of paper. 'I also ask that you allow us to provide you with two volunteer escorts for the next few months as you have just agreed to let us to by signing your name to these papers. These escorts will merely keep you out of rough situations, and the likes.' She said as I though about how this two escorts thing was not in my mind at all, but my stupid ass had already signed the papers. I guess it's the price I have to pay to get the Hell out of here. 'Who are these two volunteer escorts going to be?' I asked. She then smiled as she looked at the two muscle heads sitting on either side of me. Franklin and Robert both stood up, looking down at me with smiles on their faces. 'Our resident twin bodies here. Franklin and Robert Alexander.' I just shook my head. I looked at her with a worried look on my face. 'Are you serious? I mean, they've treated me like shit since I've been here. If they do that to me outside, there's no telling what I might end up doing to these two. And are they going to have to follow me around, even at work?' I asked. She smiled at me. 'They are only doing their job. As far as outside these gates go, you are not a patient of this facility, but instead a personal patient of mine. They are only around to make sure you don't do anything drastic. And as far as your work goes... Well for one, that's why I am releasing you. You have a steady profession. But two, it's a profession that shouldn't mind you having to have them escort you around, and it may even help you out, who knows.' She said as she smiled at me. 'Professional Wrestler.' They both laughed, as they looked at me with smart ass looks. I just let out a heavy sigh. 'Look at it this way Mr. Lee. You could be stuck in here for the next two years like initially planned. So if you've got too many problems with these arrangements, we can always go back to plan A. But other than all that, you are free to go. All your paperwork is done, and you can walk out the door with these two whenever.' She said, and I was out of my seat before she could change her mind. The, now known, Alexander twins followed me closely as I walked towards any exit I could find. First, though, I needed to use the restroom. 'Hey, I need a bathroom before we get out of here.' Franklin pointed down the hall a ways. 'Thanks.' They followed me to the door, but waited outside as I did my duty. As I was washing my hands the thought entered my mind again that I was still clueless as to who called me in. I still had no clue, and I knew it was just going to eat away at me until I figured it out. I cupped my hands together and let them fill with water before splashing it onto my face. I reached for the paper towels shortly after splashing my face, and then turning the water off. I dried my face off and looked back into the mirror. The facial expression that I saw next was not mine though. Nor was the person I was looking at in the mirror me either. No, this was in the other person sharing my body with me that I've come to know and call Markus. He was taunting me, as if to say something. It was shortly after his expression faded back out to let mine come back through that I realized. 'It was you wasn't it you little bitch?!' I blurted out as I let my right first hit the glass of the mirror. 'Don't you run away from me Markus! I've got some things I need to get off my chest, and they all have to do with you!' I went into a rage. Hoping he would come back so I could take care of him once and for all. Something I had been unable to do for the last eight years of my life. I began tearing things apart inside the bathroom, and obviously the twins heard me from outside the door. They quickly came in and seen what I was doing. They tackled me, and began dragging me out of the bathroom. 'Steven you dumb ass, calm down! You want to get re-admitted before getting out of here! Save it all for later, would you?!' Robert said as the two drug me out the nearest exit. Well, the mystery as to what had actually happened in the last week was solved. My more evil than me alter ego had turned me in, I remembered, or assume I remembered, that me and Lia were dating, or at least something was going on between us. Though I don't remember anything before the last week of my life, but I've come to accept that the past is the past, and that I need to worry about not caring about the future, like I always have done. Me, Myself, and I is all I needed to care about really. Winning Seven of the Best? If I do, I do, if I don't, it's no skin off my back. I still know what kind of talent I have, whether or not some fan loved jerk-off wins or not. I still know that the fans bring down us good wrestlers to make way for the ones they love because they have no real talent at all. But I'll stop worrying about that now. As far as my future goes… I've got to attend stupid ass sessions once every two weeks to tell strangers who I don't care about, and who I'd really like to kick the shit out of. Fun! I also have these two large bastards following me around everywhere I go. As far as they go, they better not fuck around with me, or I'll have to do something that WILL send me back to the nut house! But as far as their first actions go by pulling me out of my rage and getting me out before I was thrown into a padded room, I think we'll be just fine together. Or so I hope. The future waits. The scene fades to black.
