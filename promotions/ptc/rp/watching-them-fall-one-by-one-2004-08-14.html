Watching Them Fall...One By One

14 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-14 It was always night in the dream. And he was running. It felt as if he were chasing after someone, but he knew that couldn't be right. At least at some point in the sequence he knew that. Running. Hunting. The maze of low-lying vines and foliage that surrounded him tore brutishly at his arms and face as he ran. Dead, dry leaves crunched under bare feet as he sprinted through he underbrush, his gaze never leaving that figure just up ahead, who was crashing clumsily through the waist-high growth. The figure ahead was so dim and distant, but he knew he had to reach it in order to be sated. He knew that if he could only reach the man screaming, cursing, falling up ahead he could- We could feast, you and I, we could- -he could find the end to this dream, and maybe then the dreams would stop. And then the jungle itself materialized in front of him, simply looming up out of the gray nether world of the dream. He always stopped when that happened, his feet suddenly reluctant, their passage through the maze and the darkness slowed by an unbearable pressure he didn't understand. Still screaming an anguished howl, the man-figure ahead never hesitated, but bowled headlong into the jungle. It seems to swallow him up, in a black throat of leaf and vine. His heart dropped as the man-figure disappeared from sight, suddenly realizing the dream would end again, without truly finishing. Again it- His scent. His scent is still on the wind. We can smell him. Find him. Taste him. He burst into motion, now a blur along the dusty village path, and careened into the sheer jungle wall, passing through as branches grabbed at his hair like reaching, skeletal fingers. He stopped once inside the shell of the jungle, his bare feet digging into the soft soil of the jungle floor. The air seemed the press in on him. He could hear the tree-snapping, crashing, crying sounds ahead. He smelled the air, and smelled sweat, urine and fear. He followed the smell, running full tilt on the uneven jungle floor, sometimes on two legs it seemed, other times...on four. If he could only reach the man-figure, he knew that the dream would end. The riddle would be solved. He bounded over fallen trees, the slight sound of his bare feet on the cluttered path never disturbing the soft, surrounding stillness of the night. He could hear the man-figure up ahead. Close. Halted. The man was in a cramped oval opening in the jungle, thick walls of vines seeming to naturally weave together to create a sort of living fence around the obviously frightened prey man. He watched the man spin in circles, wide-eyed and whispering a steady stream of curses as he searched for a way out of the dense thicket. Now he would get his answers, he thought in the blanket void of the dream. Now he would see the end. He is ours. The prey is ours. It is time. It is time. He called out to the man., but all that emanated from his mouth was a low, throaty growl. The man screamed, searching the underbrush for the origin of the sound, his sense of direction clouded by his utter, blinding fear. Now. It is now. He felt his lower muscles go taught, his legs feeling more like tightened springs than normal limbs. He was ready to pounce, he thought. “I am ready to pounce and I love it.” His lips pulled back from his teeth in a mindless sneer, a thin line of drool spilling from his lips to the soft vegetation below. The low growl began again, back in his throat and then begin to build in short, panting barks. He felt himself go just then, as if his mind went on autopilot. The idea of being outside oneself came to mind, but that wasn't it either...he was still there, he just let something else take the wheel. His barks became louder, harsher, more intense as his breath quickened. The man-figure could hear him now, but that was fine. He couldn't run. The man-figure backpedaled away from the sound, his eyes wide. He tripped over a root poking out of the earth like a rotten tooth and sat down hard. His legs still kicked into the mud, as if trying to fend away whatever he couldn’t see, but could hear getting closer. Whatever it was that he could sense hunched behind the bushes. "It's me. I am behind the bushes." The figure screamed. The creature pounced. +++++ "Nnnnnnng" Kevin Thompson half-yelled as his large frame shifted brutally amongst the bed and suddenly, his eyes burst open and the mix of sweat and tears rolled down the corner of his eyes. He looked around wildly at his surroundings and determined that he found himself at an old shack. Though what was he doing here, as it was long ago since he had been here before. Just how did he get here and what is going on? It was he who has been dreaming… His eyes forced shut and he could not open them. His body shook and his lips began to tremor. It was the dream again. As his mind delivered him further and further into the abyss, he woke up with another scream caught just behind his lips. He still didn't understand it at all, but it was recurring more frequently now. More vivid. More real. "I was only sleeping…it was only a dream…a dream of--" Kevin stops in mid sentence as he is confused as to why he finds himself where he is now. He looks into the darkness of the scene and raises from bed. "How did I get here? Why am I here? I don’t remember coming here…I don’t live here anymore…" He speaks profoundly to himself as if it was the blackness who he was talking to. Kevin continued to look around the old shack, nothing had changed. All was in the same state as it was when he left it many years ago. Suddenly, The New Revolution gets for the first time in a long time, a smile upon his face, though it quickly turns to a frown. He jumps to his feet in the pitch-black night and crossed the small space to the cramped bathroom. Leaving fresh, muddy footprints across the creaking floorboards of the old shack… BOOM!! The beast was startled out of his sleep from what to have been another nightmare and the sounds of loud screaming and pounding feet filled the ears of The New Revolution. A whooping alarm was blaring in the tiny space, and he smelled smoke. He ran over to the bars of what seemed to be a cell that he was confined in and tried to see what was happening, but all he could see was moving figures in the dim haze. A guard in a ratty tan uniform ran by coughing hoarsely, and the beast thought he'd caught a coppery scent of blood as the man passed. He gripped the bars and looked around the tiny jailhouse, though the surrounding environment did not question Kevin as he did not take the time to figure out how he got here, but what he was thinking about was how to get out. He had lost track of time in here, but knew he had seen the moon through the barred window at least twice since he was caught and unceremoniously thrown into this cell. “Smoke…fire…I must escape…” Panic was quickly building up inside The New Revolution. He pulled at the bars a little bit more but he did not stand a chance against the hardened and cold steel...the bars did not even budge. The beast barked out into the smoke-filled corridor, and was answered only by a far-away screaming. He was alone… ...again. He paced around the small cell as smoke swirled about the ceiling, and settled nervously into the straw nest he had built for himself. He remembered the night they took him...it was dark, the beast was prowling around the outskirts of Ashdod, looking for food. He remembered chasing someone or chasing something. He remembered the trap. The men with their beating-sticks and ropes chasing him down, tying him down. He recalled biting a hand hard enough to draw blood, and then a startled curse and a bright flash of pain. He was knocked out cold for quite sometime and the dynamic fears that haunted him in his sleep seemed endless, until he woke up here, away from the forest and caged like a rat. Now, the smoke seemed to be lowering from the ceiling, as if the room was flooding in reverse, and the beast lowered his head into the straw nest as he mewled and whined to himself. He scratched into the hard stone floor with broken fingernails, but soon saw that there was no way out there. He felt his bladder let go. “I must escape…I must run. Dig…Flee…and run…” There was a noise at the door, and The New Revolution instantly cowered. Was it the men again? Was it those same men who have seemed to put Kevin Thompson in a state of confusion, lost in what seemed to be a life-fulfilled nightmare? Even in the fire, were they opening the door to beat him again? The bruises on his back and legs had just begun to fade to a purplish hue on his wounded and dirty skin. No it wasn’t the guard. It was someone else rattling the key in the door...someone familiar. "Jesus Christ, Kev...what has become of you?" Jayson Mecca asked. The beast backed further into the corner, away from the voice that was hoarse with smoke. Glowing embers floated in the air in the small cell. "No time for that now, Kev" Jay who has been Kevin’s long time best friend, said as he pushed the cell open. "This whole fucking country is crazy! Someone firebombed the jail. I was able to sneak in when everyone started to panic, let’s get you out of here." The New Revolution sprinted past him and away from the fire, through the open cell door and out of the unknown jailhouse. The area is in complete destruction. What seems to be a protest could easily be mistaken as a war. Trees were burning and people were fighting. Complete chaos filled the black night and Kevin finds himself running with no where to go. To make things worse, he doesn't know where he is, how he got here, or where Jay went. He soon runs and finds himself in a familiar environment, the forest. As he glances back, the sight, sound, nor the smell of fire and chaos can be recognized. There are trees surrounding him and some of the trees have very distinct red markings on them. Not any ordinary X markings, but markings such as WWA, WMW, and one that reads PRIME. The New Revolution pauses for the moment because these symbols remind him of something. He took the time to think deep within his mind what it all meant and suddenly, his eyes unclenched and he smiled. As if he knew it was there, Kevin reached down on the ground under a pile of leaves and grabs an axe with a special kind of blade on it. It seems relatively unrealistic, but that doesn’t bother Annihilation. Without hesitation, The New Revolution begins to swing at the trees and with one swipe each, the trees come crumbling down one by one. He begins unleashing his fury on all the trees…WWA, WMW, PRIME, GW, EPW, 4WW, XUW, UPWA, Neon, UWF, Core, and NGW. The New Revolution takes time to catch his breath until he reaches the biggest tree of them all, and that tree is marked with three letters…an L…an O…and a W. This time, Annihilation does not begin to swing, but instead, places the axe on his leather belt and begins to climb the tree. With brute strength and endurance he climbs, and climbs, and climbs. After a short while and what seemed to be eternity he reaches the top of the tree. Stationing himself on the top branch and holding onto it with one hand, he reaches for the axe. He pulls out the axe quickly and raises it in the air and releases a horrific, yet energetic roar into the black of the night…he finds himself above all of the crumbled trees that he took out and also at the top of LOW. Nothing can stop him now. +++++ …And his eyes open to a familiar scene inside the bedroom of Kevin's apartment. The sun shines in and the morning breeze brushes upon The New Revolution's face. He wipes his face with his hands and rolls out of bed. A small smirk completes his usual façade and he mutters a quick few words before making his way to the bathroom across the hall. “Another boring day…*sigh*…” [The scene fades out]
