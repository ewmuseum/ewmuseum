Problem Solving (GW RP #3)

12 April 2003

Silent Wind Affiliated Pimp Joined: 08 Apr 2003 Posts: 6 Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 8:08 am Post subject: Problem Solving (GW RP #3) I drove the panel van into the lower parking deck of the high-rise building that ICO Enterprises occupied, taking my ticket to enter, spiraling downward and downward, as if I were going to the center of the earth itself. It was a seemingly impossible task. The CEO, Trent McAthry, stays locked up in his office all the live long day. Inside the office, security cameras watch everything that happens. No one goes in or out without an appointment. Even then, each appointment is thoroughly checked out by security. If something goes down, or is McAthry presses the magical red button mounted on top of his desk, it takes security fifteen seconds to arrive at his office, cocked, locked, and ready to rock. But, behind every dark cloud, a ray of sunshine. When not being summoned my McAthry, or searching his appointments, the security guards wait patiently in their little security station, watching the screens with a live feed to McAthry’s office. In the event of a power outage through, the office goes to lock down. No way in nor no way out. I did mention a ray of sunshine, didn’t I? Well, here it is. The one way in and one way out, in the event of a lockdown is through….ventilation shafts. Said shaft goes from the office, with multiple exit opportunities, the best being the one at the end of the line, dropping down in front of the stairwell. I park the panel van into a space, exiting the vehicle. I move around to the back of the van, opening the rear doors. Sitting in the cargo space, I stare at The Pinch. A Pinch is a rather simple device. It creates an Electro-Magnetic Burst, which kills all electrical for approximately one minute. Let me say that again. The Pinch will kill all things electrical for one minute. The Pinch will create a one-minute lockdown. One minute of privacy with Trent McAthry. Next to the pinch, I have a nice little bomb, to add into all of the confusion. Granted, the pinch will kill things electrical, but everything will be restored after one minute. Both the detonator’s for The Pinch and the bomb are wired to a cell phone. Dial one number, instant power outage. Dial the other, instant explosion. God, I love technology. Making sure everything is in place, I lock the van up and shut the doors. Passing by the mirror of the van, I give myself the once over. Black Armani Business suit with a red shirt and black tie. I wore a long black wig, the hair pulled back into a tight ponytail. My face held a nice tan complexion, a small black goatee glued to my chin. I smile a wicked smile, heading towards the elevators. The ride up to the top floor was a bore, thirty flights up with nothing to do but hum along with dreary elevator music. It surprises me that businessmen don’t have mental breakdowns from this. I reach the top floor and step out of the elevator, taking note of the air duct opening two feet away from the elevator doors, next to the door leading to the stairwell. Quickly, I cross the long hallway, passing by cubicle after cubicle, hearing the mindless drones on the phone. At the end of the hallway, I see a secretary sitting behind an oaken desk. Behind her, the tall oak doors of Trent McAthry’s office. May I help you? Yes, Ma’am. My name is Sebastian Caine, and I have an appointment with Mr. McAthry. Yes sir, Mr. McAthry is waiting for you. I nod almost absent mindedly at her as she reaches across her desk, pressing a red button. However, as Mr. McAthry requires of all of his visitors, security will have to search you before you may see him. I nod at her again, giving a polite smile as the guards emerge from their station. Yes, Ma’am, I was told to expect that. The guards preformed a very thorough search, but came up with nothing on me expect a silver ballpoint pen, my cell phone, and my keys. In my briefcase, they discovered nothing but folders with papers tucked inside. They gave their nod to the secretary, who pressed another button, opening the oaken doors behind her. Mr. McArthy will see you now. I walk past the secretary’s desk to the doors, pulling on open and stepping inside. The room was dark, the only light seeming to come from the window behind the large maple stained desk. Trent McArthy rose from behind the desk. His gray hair was long and thinning on top, providing a view of his scalp. He had a weathered face, taunt against his skull. He offered his hand. I crossed the large office in a slight run, accepting his hand and shaking it. Mr. Caine… His voice came out gravely, a rough mix of old again and pure hardass. What can I do for you today? Well, sir, my company is in dire need… I reached my hand into my right pocket, feeling my cell phone. Unless we are able to come up with a substantial amount of funding, all will be lost for us. I dial the number to The Pinch, slowly. He nods in understanding. And this….this is where I come in… My thumb hovers over the ‘send’ button. Yes sir. Very well. Let me see your company’s portfolio. He said this with a slight sigh, his wheel turning on how and why to turn this offer down. I raise my briefcase to his desk, sitting it down, while jamming my thumb onto the send button. Seconds later, the lights go out. What the hell?!? NOW! I leap up and over his desk, while pulling out my silver ballpoint pen. I jam it quickly into his throat, puncturing his jugular. Blood pours out as I pull the pen away, thrusting it into his right eye, sending it all the way into his brain. His body collapses to the floor. Forty-five seconds left. To the vent now. I leap up, grasping the grating and pulling it down. Thirty Seconds. I can hear them beating on the door, calling out. I leap again. Twenty-five seconds. I pull myself up into the shaft. Twenty seconds. I start to wiggle my way through the tight duct. I slid quickly, making fast time. I see the last grating, marking my escape. I pull it up into the vent quickly and quietly, dropping down with a cat-like grace. As I rise from my landing, the lights come back on. I hear a scream as I open the door to the stairwell, shutting it behind me. I remove my left shoe, popping the heel off, revealing a hidden hollowed out portion of the shoe. From this compartment, I remove a tiny bomb, which I turn on. A green light smiles up at me. I attach the bomb to the door, turning on a motion detector. If someone opened the door, a small explosion would let me know. I started to run down the stairs, taking them three or four at a time. I was halfway down when I heard the explosion. So much for, “After Him!” I laugh to myself. At the bottom of the stairwell, I take a moment to rest and collect myself. I pull the long black haired wig from my head, tossing it away, using my own skull-cropped hair. I opened the door, stepping out into the lobby of the building. Taking the cell phone from my pocket, I dial the number for bomb in the panel van. As I reach the glass doors, leading to the outside sidewalk, I press the send button. I don’t hear anything, but can feel the ground shaking a bit. Walking outside, I feel the warmth of the sunlight hit my face. I couldn’t help but smile. Problem solving can be so much fun sometimes. As well as profitable. But, proving the impossible is possible, that’s the real kicker. I walk to the curb of the street, waving my arm, calling for a taxi. As the taxi pulls away, I watch as the police and fire trucks arrive. FADE _________________ Sleep child... Sleep the eternal sleep... Sleep the forever sleep...
