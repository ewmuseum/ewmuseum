The odds...

13 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-13 One step closer that is what I am. I am one step closer to gaining the attention that I strive for. However, the pathway to my goal becomes more troublesome with each step that I take. The first round was rather simple. I overcame the odds and found glorious victory. The second round looks to be quite a different situation all together. Gone are the likes of Blaise McClancy and Syxx. Before me stands a man who could be deemed champion. My opponent is a man who has found victory in tournaments before and should by no means be taken lightly for he could live up to his billing. I could taste sour defeat at his very hands. I should be worried, yet I am not. For I am an unstoppable juggernaut racing down the road to my goal and no man, myth, or legend shall halt my stampede down the highway to hell. Yes, I am confident. No truer words can ever be spoken. Perhaps, I am too confident in my own abilities. However, I will not allow myself to fail in this quest. My scheme requires that I obtain the attention of the world, so I may spread my message and send the largest horde of souls to hell that Lucifer has ever seen! Then the almighty lord of hell shall shower me with the praises that I deserve! That day will come. Oh yes. That day will come. So it is written, so it shall come to pass. ----- Something can be said for the good in this world. I have come face to face with it more times than I could care to remember and have seen it in many forms. Therefore, I can comment on it very truthfully. Warriors of the light are loyal to the cause to the point of being zealots. Those who are pure of heart will not budge or flinch when in the face of darkness. Their loyalty is unweaving and without limits. Such can be said for their faith. Whatever occurs happens for a reason and they believe their lord is infallible, which is true because if the lord was ever wrong, the universe would cease to exist. That is the one law that can never be changed or amended. God is always right and they will fight on forever to prove it so. Their will and determination knows no bounds. With the very last ounce of energy in their wretched bodies, they will continue to fight in his name and for the side of good. Sometimes that trait leads them to victory and other times, defeat. Yet, they will accept the outcome with open arms because their lord wished it so. Demons like me do not have such traits. We believe in the phrase, “Those who fight and run away, live to see another day.” We pick our spots and will not risk ourselves unless it is required. Trust me. The time where it is required does happen more than one would think… It is a peaceful spring day and I sit peacefully atop a condemned apartment complex while gathering my thoughts on what lays ahead for me. My blonde servant, Dinah, examines the various coffin-stuffers scrambling below us like ants around an anthill. There are people going to work, eating lunch, playing with their children, walking the family pet, among other things. Each with the possibility of having their souls ripped from their weak bodies. Dinah’s blue eyes race from person to person, as she tries to find me the next soul to be cast down into the flames. A gust of wind streaks past us, which hits me in the back and causes Dinah’s skirt to flutter upward. Clouds begin to circle above me and my associate looks to me with a hint of fear in her eyes. “Why fear?” You ask? She has gotten the same sensation that I have and it is nothing new to us. We are about to be visited by someone not of this realm and more often than not, it is not someone on our side. “Prepare yourself for anything, child,” I instruct Dinah, as I rise up to my feet. Dinah sprints next to me and gazes up at the heavens. I too look towards the heavens and watch the clouds part like the red sea. The splitting allows a sparkling ray of golden sunshine to escape and hit the roof of the building before our very feet. This means only one thing. We could be in for quite an eventful afternoon, as no demon ever comes to Earth in a ray of sunlight. Logic is correct once more and the pair of us watches winged creatures coast down out of the sky. Just my luck, they are heading in our direction. “Belial,” a voice thunders through the surrounding area, making me raise a brow in interest, as I have not heard my given name in quite some time. “Your time in this realm has come to an end! So says the lord!” Archangels always have a flair for the dramatic. It is like they think their time in this realm must be like something out of a bad Shakespearian play. It is as if all of heaven is watching them on a big screen television or something. I am just surprised that they do not break into a long monologue midway through a battle. Yes, that is what this duo is here for. They did not bless us with their presence for tea and crumpets. I can assure you of that. They wish to send me back into the flames before I can even compete against Almasy. I have only engaged in combat with one angel since taking this body and I barely made it out of that meeting with my head still intact. “The lord has no jurisdiction over me,” I enlighten the white-haired angel and his brunette ally. “He lost that right when he condemned me to remain in the flames for all eternity!” The angels land softly upon the rooftop and their feather-covered wings slip behind them comfortably. The voice came from the white haired angel named, Sangrariel. He is the guardian of the portals of Heaven. He is the one who decides who enters heaven and who enters the flames. On a side note, he is one of the highest ranking knights in Heaven, so I must have angered someone important. They do not send out the big dogs unless you really push some buttons. The brown-haired, female angel is Ariel. She is the great angel of new beginnings in life, which leads me to believe that they have come to banish my soul from this flesh bag and let Pietro’s own soul reclaim it. This is not adding up, however. They would not send the top guns out to retake just any soul. Father Geist may be more important than first believed. Therefore, I think I shall hold onto this mortal coil for a bit longer… “No one is outside of the lord’s will,” Sangrariel announces and draws his lengthy katana-like sword. Fire explodes from the palm of my hand and a broad sword appears in my grasp, as the fire diminishes. I slide slowly into a combat ready stance, as my angelic opponent does the same. Ariel runs up to her ally and looks up at him with a big smile. “You can destroy him and save the soul within,” she tells Sangrariel with a surprisingly young and perky voice. “I believe in you.” “I shall not fail the lord again,” he replies firmly. “Just watch my back incase his unholy accomplice decides to enter the fray.” That is interesting. Archangels do not usually need moral support form anyone. Their confidence levels are forever at their peak, but it appears as if the guardian of the portals may have had a poor outing last go around. That would be the only explanation that I could devise for why Ariel is accompanying him. I may have to just pry a little to get it out of him and once I do, his destruction will be at hand. Until then, physical combat is going to be my workout for today and you know how demons love violence. “I am here, master,” Dinah growls, as she glares harshly at the pair of angels. “Wait for my word, child,” I instruct her through gritted teeth. The metal from Sangrariel’s sword sends a reflection of light across my chest and I break towards him suddenly with my sword clenched tightly. The sound of metal against metal echoes through the area, as the white-haired being blocks the strong overhead strike I send his way. I push with all of my power in an attempt to break his defense, but he shrugs me off. I stagger back a few paces and regain my stance. We stare harshly at one another and begin to circle. “You cannot match my power, hell spawn,” he announces with a small smirk creeping onto his face. “True and most would pick you as the favorite in this duel,” I reply back with no emotion at all. “However, power is easily overcome by intellect, so beware the underdog.” That is it, mindless follower. Build that confidence back up, so I may crush it when I find the weakness that lurks inside you. I can smell the doubt in the back of your mind. Just give me an opening to learn and exploit it… “We shall see,” he responds and races in my direction while roaring like an angry lion. His speed is incredible. It is hard to keep up, but I do and deflect every attack he sends my way. He tries a wild slice with the intent to amputate my head from the rest of my body and I narrowly avoid it by ducking. The savage swing leaves him off-balance and I pop up to an upright position in time to deliver a punch squarely in his jaw with my right hand that sends Sangrariel stumbling back a few steps. I keep up the offensive and unleash my own barrage of strikes, which he defends without fail. My series of attacks ends with a backhanded slice with my sword that he blocks with his gigantic katana. The strike has a lot of power behind it and he is forced to use two hands to keep his defense intact. The defense leaves him open for a left punch and I deliver it soundly to the bridge of his nose. I watch him backpedal even more and look up at me with pure hatred in his eyes, as he drops to one knee. “I shall not fail again,” he grows to himself and wipes a trickle of crimson life from his nose. It is time to see, if I am correct about things. Tactic number one: bluff. “What is the dilemma, Sangrariel?” I ask in a mocking tone. “Cannot seem to get back to your old fighting form after you last defeat?” “I… I… I do not know what you are talking about,” he stutters in reply. “I have never lost in battle.” “Admit the truth, archangel,” I continue on. “I know how you were defeated by a brother of mine.” “Blasphemy,” he exclaims with a combination of fear and shock growing within his eyes. “I am undefeatable.” “Word travels fast in the flames,” I inform him with a miniscule smirk creeping onto my face. “All of hell knows how the great guardian of the portals was defeated at the hands of the unholy.” “It was not fair,” he hollers at me with some anger in his voice, as he rises back to his feet “There were three of them. I could not defeat them all at once.” That is precisely what I thought. “Your excuses are pathetic and meaningless,” I answer callously and point my sword at him. The male angel’s roar is heard once more and he charges at me like an enraged bull. I try to stand my ground, but it is in vein, as I am forced to take several steps back when defending myself from his onslaught. His power seems to have grown twofold, but his control and focus are dwindling. It is like he is no longer a highly-trained warrior but a savage beast. Each attack that I block sends a shockwave through my body, as his strength is far superior to my own. However, I find an opening when he goes for a vicious overhead blow. I just avoid the strike, but the front of my uniform is not as lucky. The razor sharp blade opens a hole in my uniform like a hot knife through butter. It is easily fixable with my powers, but the offensive motion was too close for comfort. The power unleashed in the strike causes Sangrariel to over swing, leaving me an open chance blast a small fireball directly into his face. The archangel screams in agony and drops his sword, so he may grasp his sizzling face. Almasy better pray in thanks to the lord that I will battle at his level because I could make him a choice of either original recipe or extra crispy. “Sangrariel,” Ariel cries out in horror and she goes to comfort him. Unfortunately for the female angel, my blue-eyed servant cuts her off. Ariel moans loudly, as she sends a punch at Dinah. Making it look rather easy, Dinah simultaneously deflects the strike with her right hand and socks the angel in the jaw with a solid left hand. That is all she wrote for Ariel, as the brown-haired being drops to the ground like a stone. Dinah cannot help but chuckle, as she stands over the fallen angel. As you can tell, not all angels are created equal. Sangrariel is a born and bred warrior who was built to battle for eons on end. However, Ariel is a lover, not a fighter. Therefore, the worst psychic could have seen that outcome ahead of time since Dinah has been blessed with a few other talents other than her beauty by my hand. I could not have my lone servant and confidant be a worthless little girl who is repeatedly dismantled by any opposing force, could I? Now, I shall return to the business at hand. “Dinah, secure his weapon,” I command without taking my focus off of the singed archangel. I catch sight of a lone eye peering at me through the white-haired being’s fingers. His pull away from his face quickly to display his mangled face to the world. His rage is now out of control and he explodes into a flurry of strikes with at full extension, only to be crippled by the intense pain of his right wing being severed from his holy body. He reaches around and runs his hand over the portion of his back where his feathery appendage used to reside. All he finds is a river of blood flowing out of his body. His crimson-covered hand trembles in shock, as he stares at it with wide eyes. This gives me far more than enough time to make the same amputation to his other wing. Now, this game is over. Archangels with no wings are humans. They are just as mortal as any fleshbag walking this realm and as easy to kill as a newborn baby. Another over-hyped warrior meets his doom at my hands. “Let my words forever ring in your ears, as you pass on to the next realm. Beware the underdog,” my voice booms. And just like that, it is over. The tip of my blade is driven viciously through his Adam’s apple, ending his short mortal life in the blink of an eye. I watch his crimson life drain out of his neck, as I remove my weapon from his corpse and kick him to the ground. I hope all of heaven was watching. I want this to be a lesson to all that may stand in my path. I will not falter and I will not fail. My goals will be accomplished, come hell or high water. Seymour Almasy, prepare to come face to face with your own inevitable "game over".
