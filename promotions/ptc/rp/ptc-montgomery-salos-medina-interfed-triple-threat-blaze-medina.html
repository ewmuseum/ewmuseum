Montgomery/Salos/Medina Interfed Triple Threat

17 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***ROLEPLAY ON*** The scene opens on a cliff, overlooking the city of Los Angeles. It's night time, and the city lights glow throughout, almost making the city look beautiful. However, things aren't always as they seem in the City of Angels. In the sky, there always seems to be a helicopter. Maybe it's a police helicopter, also known as the 'Ghetto Bird', looking for a murder, or a robber, or some moron driving dangerously throughout the city. Of course, it could also be a news helicopter, some reporter covering the story of a murder, or a robber, or whatever they can to make a buck of someone else's misfortune. Also, if you listen close enough, you can hear it... gunfire, ringing throughout the city. This is the sound that disturbs the man known as Blaze, Jonathan Medina. Blaze grew up on these streets, dealing with looters, robbers, jackers, dealers, and all kinds of unsavory people everyday. He slept in the alleys, or on the benches at bus stops. Rarely, he would be able to stay in a shelter. Then, he went on to join the Marine Corps. But although the surroundings and the people were diffrent, the sound was still the same... rat-a-tat-tat The sound of gunfire. Now, this sound, while being all to familiar, still bothers Blaze. The camera shows Blaze, as he walks up to the edge of that cliff, overlooking the city. Blaze: "Same shit, different day. Some damn gangster wannabes, or some psycho lunatics, making life difficult for everyone else. Oh well, what can you do? Go on with your life. That's all you can do. You know, it's funny... I fought hard for 17 years to get out of this shit... only to come back to it. Sure, I'm a different guy now. I'm wiser, stronger, and tougher... but the sounds of those weapons firing, it still bugs me. You might be wondering why I'm here, looking over this city. Well, this is really one of the only places in L.A. where a person can get away from the noise, and overcrowding. It gives me a chance to gather my thoughts. Thoughts like, how I'm going to defeat Jake Salos and Mike Montgomery." Blaze grins a bit. Blaze: "A triple-threat match. Some good competition, to be sure. I'm looking forward to it, but I dunno if you guys should be looking forward to what is in-store. You see, although I'm the xUw Submission Champion... this isn't a Submission match, so that allows me another whole avenue to use to defeat you. I'm not going to say some dumb shit, like I'm the only reason there is to watch this match, or how I can't be defeated by a couple of guys from the bush leagues... there's no reason to say that. Everyone knows where xUw stands, but the fact is, I don't agree to matches outside of xUw with just anyone. If I thought you two weren't worthy to be in the same ring as me, I wouldn't be talking about it right now... because I wouldn't have gotten involved. That said, make no mistake that I am planning on leaving this match as the winner, and I know you two are planning the same. But only one of us can win this match, and I see no reason why it shouldn't be me. Mike Montgomery, you're the one who issued the challenge. Open to anyone. That takes some balls. I'll admit, I have some respect for ya, but that's not going to stop me from breaking you down and leaving you out. Whether it be by submission, with the Silent Assault (Spinning Headscissor Takedown into a Figure-Four Chokeout) , or any one of the various submission moves I have... or by pinfall, using any move from my extensive arsenal... you will remember the night when you fall in defeat. Jake Salos... I don't know much of anything about you, except that you're going to have to work very hard to prove that you are on my level. I'm not trying to insult you, or anything, I'm just stating fact. You might be impressive over in cW, but when you step into that ring with me, you are going to need all the help you can get, and all I'm going to need is one Funky Cold Medina (Jumping Emerald Fusion 2) to bring an end to your night. And when it's all over, two men will leave that ring, defeated at the hands of the better man. It's possible that I could be one of those two, but highly unlikely. Train hard... and pray that I have an off night. ...dismissed." The scene ends as Blaze, with a sly grin on his face, turns back to look at the city skyline of Los Angeles. Fade... ...to... ...black. ***ROLEPLAY OVER***
