Darth von Doom versus Itachi Kobayoshi

6 October 2008

“The Longest Journey Begins With the Smallest Step” Featuring: Itachi Kobayoshi Written By: [name withheld] Word Count: 816 “So... wait, shouldn't we be heading for the airport? We have that Primetime Central thing to get to, don't we?” Gai questions as he tries to keep in step with Itachi. “Not everything is linear. We will be claiming that championship, but we have a bit of unfinished business before we leave town, or rather, you do.” Itachi quipped with a slight grin on his face as he walked up to the door of what looks to be a long abandoned home. “I don't understand, all I cared about was finding you again, proving those that claimed you were dead wrong. Now you say I still have unfinished business? What else could I have possibly forgotten?” Gai's obvious confusion to the situation causing him to get more heated with his mentor. Without responding to Gai, Itachi finally achieves his goal, and snaps the lock off the door, allowing him to slide the door open. “Welcome back home, Gai.” The pair walk through the door, and Gai seems taken aback as he looks at the dust covering most of their old possessions. “I haven't been here since you... since they told me you were gone.” Itachi smiled, brushing off an old kneeling mat and kneeling down, trying to bask in the faded glory of his one-time home. “Well, that makes two of us... I've been nowhere near Osaka, mainly hiding in Kyoto, knowing most of the people I had angered would not be looking there for me once the word of my demise had spread.” “But... but why never tell me, Itachi-san? I've lived the past five years, being told the two people in this country that gave me a chance... the two people that didn't judge me for being a Gaijin... They told me you were killed, Itachi-san... why let me live through that?” Gai's eyes watered a bit as he tried to hold his emotions back and hide them from Itachi. “Gai... We gave you a chance, because we always wanted what was best for you. We took you in to try and right the wrongs of your life... and I wasn't going to endanger you by letting you know the truth. If you knew I was alive... they would've known. I couldn't do that to you.” Itachi barely moves while speaking, trying to focus and meditate while guiding Gai. Gai looked at Itachi, trying to calm and focus, and left the matter at hand be for the moment, while Itachi slipped into meditation. ~*~ Later that evening, as Itachi entered the dining room, he investigated the house, much of the dust and clutter had been cleaned, and it was beginning to look as if people lived there again. Gai brought a bowl of noodles accompanied by rice to the table, and took a spot adjacent to Itachi. “I couldn't rest, so I tried to tidy up a bit. I know we won't be here long... but it reminds me of old times.” Gai smiled as he began to eat. “You did quite a job, but you will rest tonight, you've got quite the journey ahead of you before we leave for America.” Itachi reassured his protege. “Itachi-san... you never did tell me what buisness we have left in Osaka before departing to save the Infinite Gauntlet Championship.” Gai had stopped eating, trying to get the information from his mentor. Itachi puts down his food, and looks towards Gai. He thinks for a moment, before wiping his mouth and standing up. “Follow me, Gai.” Itachi walks briskly, as Gai hurries to keep in step. They walk through towards the living area of their home, where Itachi picks up a picture frame from a shelf. He wipes the dust off, and we can see an image of three men in front of a back drop that read “FUSE”. The larger man to the left was Itachi Kobayoshi in his wrestling gear. In the middle, looking much smaller than he was compared to the two large men behind him was Gai, a large smile plastered across his face. The third man wore similar wrestling gear to Itachi, and was just barely smaller than him. “How do you feel when you try to remember Kisame?” Itachi questioned him. Gai just stared at the photo, remembering their time in PTC and FUSE wrestling. “I feel good. He's a part of some of my best memories, along with you, Itachi-san. I'ms ad that we lost him... but I'm glad that I knew him.” Itachi smiles at Gai's response. “That's a very mature and intelligent way to veiw life and death, Gai. I'm proud of you.” Gai smiled as he held the photo, still thinking of old times. “As am I, Gai.” A slightly deeper voice echoed from the entrance way. Gai spun his head around, and saw the man from the picture, Kisame standing before him.
