IG 67 RP THREAD

16 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: We find the biggest Swedish slaughterhouse standing in front of a PRIME backdrop, black and white, just the way they like it in some random setting. Perhaps a living room? Perhaps the last Revolution? Who knows? We do know it is recent because over the shoulder of this blond, Viking Quest extra look-a-like is toting one half of the PRIME Tag Team Title belts.) RIPPA: PRIMEates! PRIMEttes! Anyone else with the wrestling package on DirecTV... Thank you for coming. I, as many of you know, am Veljumin Rippa. I wrestle for PRIME. I am one half of the team Wicked Ways...the Champions of these waters. I am only clearing this up because in weeks past I have been mistaken for my hero, Thor. I am, sad to say, not the Hammer God that I do wish very much I could be. Thank you for the kind gestures, however. (He adjusts the belt over his shoulder. The camera rattles a bit and he stops, looking just beyond it to glare at whoever is working it.) RIPPA: Another reason I want those of you watching and listening to know my name and know it well. Ruby tells me that, even though PRIME has told us to stay away from other companies in the past, we should get some exposure. Get our names... out there. Which is why I'll be entering myself into the Infinite Gauntlet 67 on July 25th. RIPPA: Not to be a sour puss, but really? It took you guys that long to invite me? all you had to do was call up PRIME and say we want some talent, not Tony Gamble. You ask for a "little help" and well, I guess that's what you get. (He snickers at his own joke. In Sweden, all jokes are funny.) RIPPA: And then, when I look down the list even more I see we hav-BOOMMTHUDTHUDTHUDFFFFTFTFTTTFFFTTTFFFFFTTTTT (The boom mic comes tumbling down on top of Rippa. A man with long, red hair comes out form behind the camera to snag the mic and help the big Swede up.) RIPPA: Crap, Scraps! CUT! (CUTTO: Veljumin Rippa sits in front of a posh and pristine fireplace somewhere in the foothills of Sweden. He is wearing a cool and comfy smoking jacket made of the finest kitten skins in South America. They are exquisite. He takes a puff on a pipe to show how relaxing it is to be here, in Sweden, and smoking pipe. A swig from the glass of fine Swedish water shows you that his thirst is now quenched, and may begin this shooting gallery.) RIPPA: I look down the list of this roster and what do I see? (ZOOMIN: Rippa's eyes, blazing with disappointment.) RIPPA: I see a blueberry von blackberry parfait with a side of freak pudding. Is that what the once prestigious Infinite Gauntlet has become? A fruity tooty freakshow? (CUTTO: Rippa strolls along a wooden deck that seems to have many beautiful women enjoying themselves in the hot tub. Enjoying themselves in the naughty way.) RIPPA: Rest your fears, my friends. I am here to make sure that won't happen. Be it some hunchback no name or even the Vitamin C and K rich produce down the line, The Swedish King of Clobbering People is going to remind people exactly why fruit, and freaks, eventually...go bad.
