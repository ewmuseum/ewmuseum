Last of his kind.

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 A battle. Two men. Only one can continue on. Who will be the one to persevere? Who will go on to higher glory? That would be the one who can put aside his differences. The one who can put aside his thoughts of others and concentrate on the task at hand. The one who can come into the ring and fight the battle with no distractions. Those all make up the one thing that would make a winner in this. There must be a winner to the battle. There must be a hero in the story. Every battle is just that, a chapter in the story of someone’s life. In the case of this ‘business’, in the case of this sport, you have your heros and your villians. But in the end, the hero is the one who is supposed to win. But what truly describes a winner? What truly describes a hero? To most, a winner, a man who handles the challenges in front of him would be declared not only the winner, but the hero. Out of all of the definitions that you find for those words… one trait defines them all. A person who has distinguished valor… fortitude in suffering… undeniable courage. Courage… There are some that would say that courage is the lack of fear. There are others that say that courage is the ability to tackle your fears and strive through them. Is courage the lack of fear or is it the ability to overcome it? If you go along those lines, then a hero, a winner is the man who is not…. a coward. There is online one type of hero that can be had in the story. One winner to be declared. But there are many types of cowards. There are those that show their addiction to their fear in many ways. Some run and hide from it all. Some lash out and try to show that they’re better then what they fear. Some become useless. Some try to make others feel that they are useless. In the first round of the GTT4, you will see a battle between one man who is a coward and the man who has proven himself to be a hero in the face of adversity. One of these men, NEW’s “Cash” Caleb Asher: a man who’s recently shown his true colors when he has been in the face of adversity, a bigot and all around jackass. The other, 4WW’s Blaise McClancy: a man who’s come from a wrestling family that has recently recovered from a severe knee injury and has now risen to claim not one, but two titles at one time. A man who not a little more then a year ago had failed in his first tournement experience since that knee injury when he fought in the Universal Title Tournement. Not too long ago, he also failed in PTC’s own Cruiser Weight Tourney. However, despite his failures, he still strives to return to his one time place of honor in the wrestling sport. A former world champion… The fisrt and only ever eWe Legacy Tourney winner… But now it’s time for him to finally turn his luck around. After coming out of his medical leave to return to wrestling, in his cousin’s own federation, his found himself holding onto the 4WW’s East Wind and South Wind Titles. The tag team and midcard level titles for those not familiar with 4 Winds Wrestling. And after the other failures in his life, he’s found that by sticking to it, but not succuming to his fears, he’s found a new life with his current tag team partner, in and out of the ring, Ukyo Kushinaga. While his upcoming opponent can spout off insults and use racial slanders to try to ‘bully’ his opponents into beliving that he’s superior, Blaise will just have to show him. But then again, words are not a means of hurting someone physically. Mr. Asher, do you know what it is you are getting invovled in? Do you realize just who it is you’re facing? You’re facing a man who’s fighting through all of his doubts and fears, overcoming them, and in the end, he will once again be on top of the world in his own right. Blaise McClancy, also known as “Hazard”, is the last of his kind. He is the last active wrestling member of a family of wrestlers. His father was a wrestler. His uncle was a wrestler, his cousins were all wrestlers. But with Fey’s pregnancy, Sean’s retirement and his other cousins taking time to help both of them… he’s the last one. No, he’s not a beer swililng, cursing, and Enlish-langauage-masacring Irishman like them. But what he is can’t be denied. He’s a McClancy through and through. And they have no time for cowardice. Japan Kanto Region Tokyo Prefecture Downtown Tokyo Grand Palace Hotel Princess Suite Yes, the Princess Suite is where we go off to to find the hero here. The second largest suite in the hotel, but perfect for the trio that currently wrestles under the name of FutureShock to share and not be in each others way. In the bedroom, with it’s king sized bed, have been staying the actual wrestlers and tag team partners, Ukyo Kushinaga and Blaise McClancy. Out in the living area, sleeping on the couch has been their manager, the now few weeks into her 18th year, Charlene Kwon. We find that it’s during the day, the windows are open to let some of the sunshine in. A nice day out, scattered clouds and the low 80’s on the farenheit scale shows that they probably won’t be staying inside for long. In fact, Ukyo has not decided to stay inside at all. She’s out on the balcony in a pair of small shorts and a babydoll t-shirt, looking out towads the city, specifically in the direction of the Tokyo Dome, where in just a short while she and Blaise will be fighting later on. They’ll be in a match to defend their Tag Team Titles, or more properly the East Wind Titles. Charlene, still in her silk pajamas, appears to be running behind a bit as she sits on the couch that has been serving as her bed for about a week now, typing away on the keyboard of her new tablet pc that Blaise bought her for her birthday. Next to that small computer, lays a large manilla envelope. “Well, did he tell you anything about it?” Charlene almost yells, so as to ensure that Ukyo hears her. “He mentioned it in passing. I think he signed up for it before you were fully signed as his manager. You’ve got to remember…” “No, the official details on it were not released until after he was revealed to be the one under the mask as Shoudou. I’m going to litterally kick his ass if he doesn’t have a good enough excuse for this. I’m supposed to be his manager now, right?” Charlene just kept looking at the screen, as if trying to absorb everything she could see. “Take it up with him if you will. He should be back in a little bit from his ‘meeting’ with that woman again. I’m still not sure about this offer to join with this Order of the Hammer that he belongs to. But his cousin seemed to be very sincere in her offer on it.” Charlene didn’t bother looking up at her on that. She’d actually been in talks with some of the other members of the Order for some time. She’d decided that if they were going to work together with Blaise McClancy and his leg of the KnightMare Brigade, she’d learn more about the organization that was actually secretly behind both that stable and the 4WW as a whole. “Holy crap!” Charlene suddenly blurted out. This brought Ukyo into the living room area to check on her friend and manager. “What is it?” Ukyo asked. “Jay Phoenix has confirmed that he’s coming out of retirement and will be taking part in this tournement as a free agent.” “I thought he was still under 4WW contract?” “He is. At least according to the fact that he’s been getting a paycheck for his medical. That’s part of the reason why his own fed couldn’t happen. Ian Childress, basically still owns his sou. I guess…” “Blaise won’t be too happy on hearing about that. He’s had some strong words about his old teacher, since the PWW failed to open. That was part of the reason for that witch leaving him.” That comment got Charlene to look up at Ukyo. “I don’t think he’s complaining aobut not being with super-bitch Becca anymore. Other then she’s got custody of his two girls. I have a feeling he thinks he traded up.” Not even a knock on the door, it just opens and Blaise enters, looking a bit tired in his face. He openly yawns as he sees the two girls over by the couch area. His eyes narrow as they both look up at him with this “you’re going to get it from Charlene” look. The man, looking something like what you’d get if Jay Phoenix and Fey McClancy were the ones who had children, instead of Fey and Ian Childress, just gives them an equal return. “What did I do now?” His red hair, cropped short to his skull, red goatee and green eyes show the Irish half of his heritage. They’re traits he shares with his infamous cousins from his father’s bloodline. However the broad nose, high cheekbones and darker skintone come from his mother’s side. She is a member of the Paiute Nation from the Valley of Fire reservation in Nevada; the same place that Blaise was born. “Like you don’t know? Mr. ‘Enter-a-tournement-without-talking-to-Charlene-first’ McClancy.” Charlene answered. “Oh, I guess they sent me my confirmation packet. They said they’d fill me in on my first round opponet to get to know him by this coming weekend.” “Yes, someone named “Cash” Caleb Asher from NEW. Here’s your packet.” Charlene holds it up as Blaise walks over and takes it from her. “So, with a name like that, let me guess. Rookie, silver spoon in his mouth, and a general asshole?” “An asshole so much that the FCC has apparently threatened to fine NEW is he doesn’t stop using some racial slurs.” “Really? Wonder how he’ll take the fact that his first opponent is a ‘dirty injun’ then?” “Hmm, recently been fueding with NEW’s TV Champion, Tsuyoshi Tanaka, and he’s been pretty vocal on what he thinks about the Asian races there.” Ukyo’s face went to a scowl there. Blaise pulled out a promotional picture of the man from the envelope. “So, his first opponent will be a dirty injun, who’s dating a, what’s he use? Probably Jap, and is managed by whatever word he uses for a Chinese woman. He’s about to have a coniption when he sees his packet.” Blasie holds up the glossy picture. “Gawd, this guy looks like a closet…” “Don’t say it.” “No, look at him. I bet he’s Canadian, huh? He just has that look. Kinda like Chris West and Lee Satima. That… aire about them.” “Born in New Brunswick, but recently moved to be your neighbor. He lives in South Las Vegas.” “What the bloody hell? Little fucker probalby lives in the area near or in Spanish Trails. Can he wrestle?” “He’s classified as a submissionist, but his finishers are both either aeriel or impact moves. I can’t quite place him.” “Man, this guy is just one of those ones that are trying to compensate for something, ins’t he?” Charlene leans back and looks at Blaise. She knows he’s not a rookie in the industry. He’s had a couple of the best trainers: Ian “Nephilim” Childress, Sean “The Irish Bastard” McClancy and Jaymes Phoenix. He was on his way to having a history like theirs, but his was still being written. He’d already one a tournement a few years back, when he was the SCWF Superior Champion. He was the only man to ever win a tourney in that name. But then he had his in ring accident while facing his cousin, breaking his knee. A year and a half in rehabilitation and he’d returned to the ring. Charlene had to admit it though, he was no slouch when it came to his ring work. In fact, that was his whole thing. That was what made him “Hazard”. He was a wrestler, and nothing else. He didn’t have a gimmick or façade to hide behind. He wasn’t afraid to put his true self forward. Ukyo chimed in as Charlene looked on. “What do you mean?” “You take some of thsee guys, who never had to work a day in their life and you put them in a school, teach them and then toss their ass into the ring. Next thing you know, they think their god’s gift to the universe, let alone the sport. In the meantime, they’re stining up the place. This guy here seems to have a few shortcomings as it is. And anyone who would live in Vegas is a fuckwad anyway.” “What? You’re from…” “No, and when we get back to the states, you’re going to the Valley to meet my folks anyway. I’m from the Valley of Fire, a large place of desert and big rocks about a half an hour north of the shit hole that is ‘Lost Vegas’. It’s a place for wage slaves and losers trying to compensate for their shortcomings by hanging out at parties and gambling their lives away. If I would borrow some of this guy’s schtick… the reason why the Natives use bingo halls and casinos… it’s the white man’s firewater. It’s bad enough this guys obvioulsy has a fear of non-caucasians as it is.” “Uhm… he likes to insult and berate them.” “Yeah, prime candidate. He’s afraid of them. He’s xenophobic. He makes them seem smaller to him by tearing them down. Same principle as the school bully picking on the little smart guys wearing glasses. He’s so afraid of the other races in this world, he just up and puts them down.” “Yeah, I remember those days.” Charlene had been one of those kids wearing the glasses and being picked on. She could relate quite a bit. “Yeah, but in the end, what happens? The school bully is still stupid and the geeky kid with glasses becomes his boss. This guy, his nickname is “Cash” what’s his catch phrase?” Charlene smiles. “You can bank on it.” “Oh my gawd. What is up with that? “ “He’s also quoted as saying that even if you can beat him, he can buy you and you’ll still be his bitch.” “Puh-lease. He’s not got enough money for that. I wonder if I can call Argyle and see if he can give me a fucking challenge? This is almost depressing.” He pulls out some of the paperwork in the envelope and flips through it. He also drops a DVD onto the table. “Submissionist? But uses a suplex or a slam, hell looks like most of his stuff is simple throws… damn, how can he call himself a submissionist. I bet if he got locked into a triangle lancer he’s scream like a little girl. I’ll give him this, his profile looks like someone who wants as little contact with his opponent. He’s afraid of pain. If he can grab them and throw them, he’ll not have to deal with their possible return or counter. He’s a man of many fears. And I intend to make him face them. Who else is in the tourney. ” Charlene leans forward and looks at the screen on her small pc. “Hmm, Craig Carter droppped out…” “DAMNIT!” “What?” Ukyo perked up. “I wanted a rematch with him. That’s the guy who punked me out of that Universal Title Tourney.” “Uhm, Angelo Deville, Seymour Almasy, Dr. Curiosity, Jay Phoenix…” “What? Okay, a list of bastards as far as I’m concerned. I guess I’ve got a little trip to make come once we’re done at this event. You two don’t need to show up if you don’t want to. I’m just going to walk in there and show this guy how a real wrestler works, and then roll on out. You two can head on to Seattle if you want to. When I get back we can go take a trip down to Nevada and who knows, maybe I’ll stop by the Spanish Trails community in Vegas and piss on his doorstep too. To think, I was a little worried about trying something like this so soon after coming back. But if this is what I’ll have to face to start with, I’m not going to be worrying about a damn thing. Let’s go get some lunch. Other then that, I’m not going to worry about this putz until after we’ve taken care of the Ozz Brothers and Kanton. He’s just not worth it until then.” He picks up the DVD and slides it back into the envelope, as well as the papers and photos. Yes, we can say that after failing in the first round of his last two tournement appearances, Blaise should be afraid of getting into another one so soon after coming back to the mainstream of wrestling. But how would he ever get anywhere if he didn’t face that and just jump right back in there? Could Caleb Asher face his fear of non-caucasians? Could he face the fear of good, clean hard work that he will need to do here? Can he face his obvious fear of pain? Or will he try to continue the façade and try to hide what he truly is? You live in Vegas now Mr. Asher. You like to gamble? You like to take a risk? That’s what Hazard is all about. Just remember one rule from “Lost Vegas”. Don’t play at a table you can’t make the maximum bet at, and never, ever bet against the house. Your poker face isn’t all that good. You’re as see through as a showgirl’s skirt. Just remember, you have every reason to fear what is to come. You’re up against the last McClancy. And a coward, he is not.
