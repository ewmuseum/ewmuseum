Nobody Does It Worser - Episode 4

5 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-05 GARFELD’S SECRET FIENDISH VILLA – YUCATAN PENINSULA, MEXICO FOUR YEARS AGO ONE PARTICULARLY EVIL NIGHT O’CLOCK "Welcome, Mr. Bong. This is indeed a pleasant surprise, although sadly your entrance rather lacked style." Luminescence of a full moon glinted off the waxen, hairless scalp of Johannes Frederick Sebastian Billy-Bob Garfeld, the World’s Most Fiendish Fiend™ (and part-time hairbrush salesman). He stood at balcony’s edge, overlooking a dusty courtyard in the middle of his secret fiendish villa. About a dozen armed guards flanked Garfeld on either side, their rifles aimed squarely at his arch nemesis—Jimmy Bong, Agent Double-Oh-Nothing. The tuxedo-clad British spy had just landed inside Garfeld’s evil residence, floating down on a parachute that bore the Union Jack. Unfortunately, he became caught on a flagpole erected in the courtyard’s center. He was tangled, helpless, and looked quite silly indeed, that everlasting dunderhead. "I’m not here to impress you with my skydiving abilities, Garfeld!" Bong yelled back. "I’m here to impress you with my… uh… my fiendish-plot-foiling… abilities! Yes, that’s right! And I’ll do it by putting an end to your diabolical scheme to blow up the moon!" "What?" asked a puzzled Garfeld. "What in the blazes are you talking about, Mr. Bong? I’m not planning to blow up the moon! I’m not planning to blow up anything! I’m here on vacation, you stupid twit!" "AHA!" Bong exclaimed triumphantly. "My fiendish-plot-foiling abilities have succeeded, then!" Garfeld rolled his eyes. "Honestly Mr. Bong, how can you be so thick? And to think you work for British Intelligence… or should I say, British Incompetence? BUWAHAHAHAHA!" The guards also chortled and guffawed, like the mindless yes-men that they were. "SILENCE!" bellowed Garfeld with a raised hand. The brown-nosed knee slapping ceased immediately. "Well in any case," Bong continued, "You’re still wanted for an entire laundry list of crimes! Terrorism, murder, kidnapping, extortion, conspiracy, smuggling… jaywalking, loitering… we won’t stand for it anymore! Your days of crossing in the middle of the street and late nights of hanging out in front of public buildings are through, through I say! Now everybody drop your weapons and put your hands over your heads, you’re all under arrest!" He quickly reached into his jacket and pulled out his weapon, aiming it at Garfeld and his goons. "Very amusing, Mr. Bong," sneered Garfeld, "but I highly doubt you’ll be able to do us much harm with a… boomerang." Bong looked at his hand. He was indeed pointing a boomerang at them. "Damnation!" Bong cursed. "Why the hell did I barter away my Walther for a souvenir boomerang at the Sydney airport!? But still, you’d best beware, Garfeld! I can throw this thing pretty hard!" "Forgive me for not quaking in fear," came Garfeld’s mocking reply. "Anyway, I think we’ve dispensed enough with the obligatory salutations. Why don’t we entertain ourselves for a while? Gentlemen, please help Mr. Bong down." Four of the guards briefly retreated into the villa, and then re-emerged at the courtyard’s ground level. After a few minutes they freed Bong from his embarrassing entanglement and had him back on his feet. The British secret agent dusted himself off, looking warily at the guards surrounding him with their guns at the ready. "Let’s have some fun with Mr. Bong," Garfeld announced with a smile. "Bring… him out." There was a collective gasp of fear from the guards. Bong simply cocked a curious, quizzical eyebrow up at his archenemy. An old portcullis hidden in the shadows along the far wall began to ascend. All eyes were transfixed as its steel teeth rose out of the earth, clanking and creaking open like a prison cell. With a final lurch, it groaned to a stop. The gateway stood open, a less-than-grand foyer into what seemed nothing short of total darkness. A moment later, the echoing of footsteps broke the trepid silence. Someone was emerging from the gate. "¡DIABLO! ¡NO ES HUMANO, NO ES HUMANO!" yelled one of the guards standing on the ground. He dropped his gun and fled, flailing his arms and screaming like a frightened schoolgirl. Bong watched the guard clamor up the wall and dropping over to the other side. The screams ceased a split second later. "Dear me, I think that poor fellow forgot about the very spiky rocks that surround this place," Bong said quietly to himself. Then he turned back to the gate. A gargantuan figure now loomed in the shadows. "Come on out," Garfeld barked. "Come on out and play with Mr. Bong… …Ears!" "Cripes, not him," whimpered Bong. He looked up warily at the giant man creeping slowly toward him from the darkness, and braced himself to fight… --- MAYBE ENTERPRISES, SECURITY CENTER – CHICAGO THE DAY AFTER BONG TRIUMPHED OVER MERCURY…OH, AND A FEW DAYS MORE AFTER SOMEONE TRIED TO MURDER HIM AS WELL. YEAH, THAT’S PROBABLY IMPORTANT TOO. A QUARTER ‘TIL FIFTEEN MINUTES FROM NOW Jimmy Bong hadn’t been in a place quite like the hi-tech nerve center of Maybe Enterprises security since his tenure with OHMCS (especially ever since the one time he ran amok in their central server room with a garden hose and was consequently banned from going anywhere within ten meters of any kind of electrical equipment in the greater London area). His awestruck gaze fixated upon the flashing checkerboard array of screens that climbed twenty feet, nearly reaching the ceiling of the darkened room. A trio of hawk-eyed security guards sat vigilantly at its foot, keeping a close watch over Professor Maybe’s small empire. After remembering himself and self-consciously closing his agape jaw, Bong scanned what must have been thirty-five, forty monitors at least. He recognized a few places—the entrance lobby on the ground floor (the same floor they were currently on), the receptionist desk on the seventieth floor (where Carol, he noticed, was chasing off some poor girl scouts with a baseball bat while screaming her lungs out), and the training facility across town (which didn’t really require any parenthetical description, but the other two monitors each got some so it was only fair). It was the events that occurred at said training facility a few days prior that had brought him here, along with the CEO of Maybe Enterprises himself. Always a busy man, Professor Maybe had been particularly preoccupied as of late with the trial run of what he proudly called an "innovative workout regimen" that would revolutionize the fields of athletic training and sports medicine—a trial run with Jimmy Bong as the proverbial guinea pig and GTT5 as the testing field. So far, the regimen seemed to be working. Before Bong had started, he was a twig who couldn’t even tear a sheet of paper in half. But now, he could most certainly tear several sheets of paper in half with incredible ease. His strength was increasing at a remarkable pace. His muscles were… well, not growing at all. He was still a twig. But nonetheless, he now had two impressive victories in GTT5 under his belt. ("It’s really odd," he commented after defeating Mercury, "that by defeating Elia Smith in the first round I effectively saved the world from a horrible demon, yet by defeating Kenny Mercury in the second round I’ve doomed the world to destruction by aliens. Maybe by defeating Chandler Tsonda in round three I’ll save the world from… uh… ill-tempered candlestick makers.") Maybe did not seem particularly overjoyed to be out of his office. He practically never stopped by the security wing. Indeed, such visits could only be made possible by extreme circumstances. The attempted murder of his precious "guinea pig," it turned out, was extreme enough. "Play it back again," he mumbled to one of the guards at the console. The guard simply nodded once without looking up, and rewound the tape that was being displayed on a bottom-row screen. "Jimmy, take a look at this," the CEO beckoned. Bong lowered his gaze and watched along with Maybe as the tape played, sans audio. The screen flickered to life with an image of the training facility. Bong was shown speaking with Xavier "X" Pendable, one of Maybe’s financial backers. "This was taken a few days ago," Maybe explained. "We’ll play it up to just a moment before the attempt on your life occurred. See if you notice anything that might help us out." "Right," Bong nodded. They watched the monitor carefully. Bong and Pendable continued their conversation a few moments more. Bong’s trainer, Sgt. Gordon Gloucester, then joined them (looking rather pissed as always). He was followed closely by Dr. Dennis Kent of the Maybe Enterprises Sports Division. Right behind Kent came Bong’s eccentric publicist and image consultant, Erik Lang. Last but not least was Bong’s valet, Samantha "Sweetie" Shortcakes—a midget who was anything but sweet, with the complexion of a beaten horse and the orating skills of a caveman. "Alright, freeze it here," Maybe said finally. The guard paused the tape. Maybe and Bong took a minute to study the frame. "Nothing out of the ordinary," Maybe uttered thoughtfully, half to himself, half to Bong. "Strange. You would think that if one of them knew the lights were about to go out, that person would be getting ready to reach for their gun." "Hm, yes. It was definitely one of them though, right?" asked Bong. "Has to be," Maybe continued. "We’ve examined film from every camera inside and outside the building from that night. Nobody entered or exited around the time of the attack—that much is certain. So yes, it was definitely one of the people inside, without a doubt." "Offhand I say it was Sweetie," Bong grumbled. "She has the disposition of a harpy covered in sulfur." Maybe let out a chuckle. "Certainly an interesting choice for a valet," he snickered. "Interesting? She’s a bloody nuisance!" Bong spouted indignantly. "Last night when I went out to the ring to face Mercury, she walked out with me, right? Well, she kept saying ‘MERCURY IS PLANET, JIMMY NO CAN FIGHT PLANET’ over and over again. It was really getting on my nerves, so I finally told her to put a sock in it. Then just my luck, she took me literally and grabbed onto my leg, trying to get to my sock! That tenacious little she-devil wouldn’t let go!" Maybe looked astounded. "So you fought Mercury with a midget clinging your leg? And won?" he asked. "For all you or anyone else knows I did," said Bong under his breath, glancing around. "Amazing, Jimmy! Well done! Although I’ll agree with you that Sweetie’s behavior was a bit… well, strange." "Strange? That’s not even the half of it!" Bong scoffed. "She didn’t let go after the match ended. In fact, she hasn’t let go at all!" Bong and Maybe both looked down. Sweetie indeed still had her hideous little midget arms wrapped firmly around Bong’s right leg. "Sweetie!" growled Maybe. "Get out of here at once, and for God’s sake take a shower, you look filthy!" "SWEETIE ALREADY TAKE SHOWER," Sweetie grunted as she relinquished Bong’s leg. "SHOWER WITH JIMMY. AND SWEETIE NOT DIRTY, SWEETIE JUST HAVE MISHAPEN FACE LIKE VIETNAMESE WHORE." She turned around and stomped out of the room. Bong abashedly rubbed the scruff of his neck as Maybe gave him a questioning stare. "Well, she was clamped to my leg!" he sputtered in his defense. "What was I supposed to do? I was afraid that if I even dared touch her, she’d bite off a very delicate part of my body! You know… like a toe, or maybe my kneecap or something. The whole affair was very awkward." A moment later, Sweetie stomped back in and went right up to Bong. "AND THIS FOR CALLING SWEETIE HARPY COVERED IN SULFUR." She kicked him hard in the shin. "OW!" Bong wailed, hopping up and down while cradling his leg. Sweetie spun around and stomped out of the room once more. "I hate running gags," he gritted through his teeth. Maybe just shook his head and turned back to the guard. "Play the rest of the tape," he said. The video of the training facility resumed from where they had left off. They watched the lights go out, leaving everyone in total darkness for a few moments before flickering back on. A mirror on the wall behind Bong was shattered as it had been a few days ago, and everyone was looking around in shock and confusion. Bong mouthed his declaration that Pendable had been killed, followed a second later by Pendable’s insistence that he wasn’t dead at all. (Truth be told, he did have a point.) "Alright, that’s enough," Maybe sighed. "Well Jimmy, I’m afraid we don’t have much to go on at the moment. We have five suspects, and the only other lead we have is the bullet found in the wall." "Which the police are examining, I take it?" Bong asked. "Erm, not exactly," Maybe answered tentatively. "We have our own ballistics experts working here at Maybe Enterprises. In uh… instances like these, shall we say… I prefer to take care of problems on my own. Anyway, don’t worry about it. Given time, we’ll trace the bullet to the gun it was fired from, and from there we’ll figure out who the culprit is." "Well that’s a relief," Bong smiled. "All I have to do in the meantime is avoid being killed! And given the fact that I spend most of my time with everyone on our list of suspects—every single day, for that matter—that shouldn’t be terribly difficult." He paused for a moment. Then his smile faded. "Wait a minute. That’s not a relief. Why, that’s not a relief at all! I’m doomed! DOOMED!" he cried. Maybe chortled softly and clapped a reassuring hand on Bong’s shoulder. "Don’t fret, Jimmy," he said. "I’ve taken measures to make sure you are well-protected." Bong’s face lit up. "Oh goody! So you’ve fired everyone to make sure none of them can get near me?" "No." "Excell—wait, what!?" Bong was shocked. Did Maybe really just say "no?" "Jimmy, please understand," Maybe said quickly, seeing Bong’s stupified reaction. "You’re right, letting my staff go was the very first course of action I considered, naturally. But we have to look at the big picture. If I fired Gloucester, we wouldn’t have any time to find you a new trainer! Then who would help get you ready for your bout with Chandler Tsonda?" "Matthew Perry?" asked Bong. Maybe gave Bong an odd look, then continued. "And as for Erik Lang, I can’t take him out of play either. He’s spearheading our ‘Forces of Stupid’ marketing campaign that’s going to put you on the map! In fact we just put out our first shipment of Jimmy Bong t-shirts, look!" He unbuttoned his suit coat to reveal a black t-shirt underneath, bearing the phrase "JIMMY BONG: BEWARE THE FORCES OF STUPID~!" in white letters. "That’s… um… splendid," Bong said, not sounding at all convinced of the shirt’s splendor. He didn’t particularly care for Erik Lang’s line of thinking. In fact, he was certain that Lang was completely mental—and when Jimmy Bong thinks somebody else is mental, that’s saying something. "Exactly," Maybe smiled. "So Lang needs to stay, otherwise our whole marketing campaign is down the tubes. The same goes for Dr. Kent. He’s had a huge hand in the development of this project and is a key figure in the Sports Division. It wouldn’t be wise at this point to just let him go. He could easily find work with one of my competitors and share valuable information about the products we’re developing here. That would be fatally detrimental to Maybe Enterprises." "Well yes, I suppose my life is hardly important compared to your business," Bong said earnestly. "Then there’s Pendable," Maybe’s eyes narrowed as he thought. "Hmm… I know he was eager to visit the facility. He wouldn’t stop pestering me for security clearance. Why, though? What the hell was he doing there? Jimmy, what did he say to you?" Bong had been afraid that question would come up. Maybe was unaware of the reason Pendable had gone to speak with him that night. According to Pendable, there apparently was more to Maybe’s "training regimen" than met the eye. He seemed to believe it was part of something bigger, something more sinister. Bong couldn’t let Maybe know what Pendable had said, especially if his hunch was accurate—even he wasn’t that stupid. On the other hand, Pendable didn’t have any proof to reinforce the veracity of his claim… "Jimmy?" Maybe asked again, growing impatient. Jimmy Bong was neither smart nor slick—but he thought up a lie, and he thought it up quick! "He uh… he came to give me the tragic news that my poor grandmum had been killed in a horrible rocking chair accident!" he sniffled. Maybe scratched his head. "I thought you said your grandmother died when you were just a boy." "Grandmum was very stubborn," Bong replied quickly. "I see." A frown came over Maybe’s face. He was thinking, which made Bong nervous. "Well, no matter," he said finally. Bong let out a sigh of relief. "But Jimmy, I think you’d best avoid Pendable at all costs from now on." Bong found this statement intriguing. Maybe didn’t want him going near Pendable? "Why?" he asked curiously. "Because he’s the most likely of our suspects to have a motive," replied Maybe. "Consider the others—for example, if Gloucester wanted to kill you, he could easily have done it by now." "Well, he does genuinely despise me," Bong shrugged. "Just like nearly everyone else I’ve known. In fact, I nearly drowned once when I was three, and my father cursed the doctor who gave me CPR." Maybe shot another confused glance at Bong before going on. "Yes, but Gloucester just doesn’t strike me as the type who would pull a sneak attack like that." "Oh, so you’re saying he’d murder me to my face! Well that’s awfully considerate of him," Bong quipped with moronic delight. "Erik Lang isn’t very likely to have a motive either," Maybe continued. "After all, you’re the focus of the biggest marketing campaign he’s put together in years! It wouldn’t make any sense for him to want you dead." "Nothing involving me ever seems to make sense," Bong chuckled. "Plus you’ve seen Dr. Kent, right? Brilliant mind, but he completely lacks self-confidence. He doesn’t have the fortitude to kill a fly, let alone a human being. I also doubt he would jeopardize the program. He’s spent too many years helping to develop it to sabotage it now. That leaves Pendable." "What about Sweetie? What if she’s the one who wants me dead? You can at least fire her, right?" Maybe laughed. "Jimmy, I’m sure if she wanted you dead, she’d have killed you last night while she was clinging to your leg." "Well perhaps she was trying to kill me by annoying me to death!" Bong protested. "Sorry Jimmy, I’m not firing her," Maybe said sternly. "If Lang thinks she’ll be an asset, then that’s good enough for me. He hasn’t steered this company wrong yet." "Just bloody great," Bong grumbled. "So as I said, that leaves Pendable. He’s hinted for weeks now that he wants to pull his money out of this project. He can’t, though—he signed a contract. So, he’s legally bound to fund this project until it is either completed or discontinued." "And a surefire way to make sure it’s discontinued would be to kill me!" Bong exclaimed. "Why, that’s brilliant! That’s the greatest reason I’ve ever heard for someone wanting to kill me! Usually it’s daft things like, ‘You’re my hated archenemy,’ or ‘You annoy the hell out of me,’ or ‘Because I’m bored, that’s why!’ But to be killed for money… now that makes me feel useful!" "What the hell are you talking about Jimmy?" Maybe asked, quite confounded. "You know, I’m beginning to notice you have a habit of saying the most outlandish things. It makes me wonder if you’re not mentally disturbed." "I’ve been wondering that myself for years," said Bong. "Well, let’s not worry about that," Maybe sighed. "Anyway, that’s why I believe Pendable is the one who wants you killed. As far as I can tell, he’s the only one who would benefit from your death. Unfortunately I can’t act until I have proof. Plus, there’s no guarantee that any of our other suspects are innocent—there might be some facts we don’t have. But since I can’t afford to replace any of them at this point, I’ve enlisted the help of someone who will ensure your safety. Jimmy, I’ve hired you a bodyguard." "How delightful!" Bong smiled. "Who?" "Well, I don’t know much about him. He’s freelance, and worked mainly for some large organization for several years until recently. I’ve heard he’s quite good—and he’d better be, for what I’m paying for him." "Excellent," said Bong, glancing idly back up at the wall of monitors. "I’m sure that…" He froze. His eyes were locked upon the monitor displaying the ground entrance lobby. A tall behemoth of a man had just stuffed his way through the revolving door at the front. Bong recognized him immediately. He’d crossed paths with the man several times before. One of them was four years ago in Mexico. Another was just over one year ago in Atlantic City. It was the same man who tried to kill him and Majorly Shafted during the GTT4 tournament. It was Ears. "Horrors!" Bong exclaimed. "It’s Ears! What’s that evil bastard doing here!? I must stop him before he does something fiendish!" With that, he dashed out of the security room. "Jimmy, wait!" cried Maybe, running after him. It was just a quick sprint down the hall before Bong reached the lobby. He scanned the area and spotted the giant Ears standing in the middle of the room. Ears wasn’t hard to miss, of course—oily black hair topped his seven foot frame, and he was usually clad in a white dress shirt with dark brown slacks and suspenders. His most distinguishing feature, however, was his oversized ears. They were made of solid steel. "You there! Ears!" Bong shouted. Ears turned and looked at Bong. Bong wasn’t sure if Ears ever spoke or not—he only ever communicated with a menacing smile. In any case was communicating toward Bong at that very moment. "Oh bollocks," Bong gulped. "Maybe getting the attention of a nearly indestructible mountain-sized assassin wasn’t the best idea I’ve ever had. Well, no turning back now. BLEARGH!" Bong rushed at Ears, and threw a punch… into his right ear! CLANG! "OW!" Bong shook his hand painfully. He wound up again, and launched another fist… into Ears’ left ear! CLANG! "AGH!" he cried again. "Bloody hell, that hurts! Why isn’t this working!? You would think his ears would be his weak spot!" Ears just shook his head at Bong’s complete stupidity, then reached down and grabbed him by the throat. He hoisted the former British secret agent off his feet, suspending him several inches off the ground. Bong kicked and struggled, gasping for air. "EARS! STOP!" someone shouted. Ears and Bong looked in the direction of the voice to see Professor Maybe running toward them. "Ears, put him down!" Maybe ordered. "You’re supposed to protect him, not kill him!" Bong’s eyes went wide. What in the world was Maybe talking about? He stared into the face of Ears, wondering whether or not he was actually going to obey. The giant simply smiled once more and then released Bong, who fell to the ground in a heap. "That’s better," said Maybe. "In the future, Ears, please remember what I’m paying you for, alright?" Bong slowly got back to his feet, coughing and gasping for air. "W-Wait a second!" he spat. "Are you saying that… that Ears is… my bodyguard!?" "Of course," said Maybe. "Why, Jimmy? Is that a problem?" Bong couldn’t believe it. Was it a problem? Oh no, it wasn’t a problem at all. Someone tried to kill him, so it was only natural that he should be placed under the protection of… someone that tried to kill him. Of course, there was no way Maybe could have known about Bong’s past encounters with Ears. The fact that he had once been a British spy was not exactly information Bong wanted made public—particularly if Pendable’s suspicions were right and Maybe was really up to something that didn’t exactly qualify as legal. "No," said Bong after a moment. "No problem at all." "Good, glad we’ve settled that," said Maybe, looking at his watch. "Well, I’m afraid I have other business to attend to, Jimmy… and so do you, of course! Another round to prepare for, right? Don’t worry, Ears is under strict orders to remain with you at all times. I don’t think anybody will dare try to hurt you as long as he’s around." Yeah, because he’ll probably do it before anybody else gets a chance, Bong thought to himself. "That’s good to know," he said, heading for the door. "Come on Ears, let’s be off." Ears smiled again, which made Bong feel quite unnerved. Maybe waved to them as he moved toward the elevators. "Try not to die!" he shouted after Bong with a joking tone. He turned his back, and muttered darkly under his breath. "…not until after the tournament is over, at least." He stepped onto an elevator and turned back around to watch Bong and Ears leave the building. An evil smile crept over his face as the doors slid shut.
