TTD - Illuminati -v- The Corporation

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I can't believe it.... We lost.... And in the first week no less.... AND TO TWO GUYS BY THE NAMES OF INVINCIBLE GABE AND SCOTT RAMPAGE! Am I unjustified or am I allowed to be angry over this disappointment? I thought we had it in the bag....the clear cut victory.... I guess I was wrong. I guess I should have remembered the age old adage: When you assume, it makes an 'ASS' out of 'U' and 'ME'. A loss in the first week doesn't do much for the old self esteem, and it doesn't bust open anyone's preconceived notions of the "Black Sheep" PTC family....the mVw. I guess we’ve got to step it up…show Invincibleness that their win was a fluke, my disappointment shall be short lived, for this week, I will take care of business….and I will guarantee the win…AT ALL COSTS! [The camera opens up in the plush hotel suite that serves as Jayson Kenny’s temporary home, which also doubles as the official I L L U M I N A T I headquarters. The two official members of the newly formed group are stretched out in various forms on two different couches just listening to the silence, brooding within themselves. The third unofficial member of I L L U M I N A T I, Kellan Jeffries, their lawyer is off pitter pattering around the suite….probably trying to coax some “classy” young lady into his bed, or off doing some drugs. Both the men are dressed very casual, “Your Divinity” Jayson Kenny is clad in black mesh basketball shorts, and a black tee shirt, his bald head gleams in the light and a cigarette dangles from his lips, his eyes have a certain glazed look to them, as if he’s some where….just not here. “Your Saving Grace” Rhys Cartel is adorned in khaki shorts, a white tee shirt, white ankle socks, and black ADIDAS flip flops. His short, stubbly, hair cut needed a shape-up and his beard was growing gruff, but at this point he really could care less. What he was preoccupied with however was the perplexing conundrum of how in the hell they had lost to the team of two bastards named Invincible Gabe and Scott Rampage? As Rhys Cartel sits in silence he retreats deeper and deeper into his mind…..] What the f**k man. How in the hell did we lose to those two asses. I can’t believe it…this ha s to be some kind of conspiracy. I L L U M I N A T I is one of the most dominating forces to ever walk the Earth, we weren’t supposed to lose. The plan was supposed to be that we creep through the PTC Tag Tournament undefeated, and stay that way until we won those damn titles. But no, it wasn’t to be. Maybe we underestimated those two a little more then we should have. Maybe we should have never underestimated them in the first place. Maybe this whole thing was my fault. Maybe I shouldn’t have picked Kenny as my partner. I mean, I had been looking at the guy for awhile, I knew I wanted to form a stable with the guy even before he rose to his glory, I have an eye for these things and I’m telling ya this guy had all the promise in the world. Then he goes and hits his f**kin wife. He has to go and fuck up my whole master plan just because he can’t keep his hands to himself when his wifey tells’em that she’s splittin and takin the kid too. I say fuck the bitch, I mean if shit got a little hot and she bounced on me, then it only proves that she never wanted to be with me n the first place. I don’t get all strung up over hoes. As for the kid….I guess I’d be hurt too, but not enough to lay the smack down on the treacherous hoe. And even though he was knee deep in shit creek regarding all this controversy. I still wanted to team with the guy. I don’t have a clue why. But I just knew that we’d be a dominating force. So I send my personal lawyer, Mr. Kellan Jeffries out there to get him outta all this drama. To set the man free so that my master plan could go to work. I never told him who was financing this mission, I felt that if he knew who was helping him out, he’d become too proud and refuse my offer….and we couldn’t have that. I was patient with the kid, I helped him out of jams but he STILL after all this couldn’t keep his hands to himself, this time he attacked that bastard Shawn Johnson and injured him badly. I mean I can understand wanting to bash the kids skull in, but come on, show a little self control. After the attack he landed himself in jail again. There wasn’t much I could do for him, but eventually the charges were dropped and after a couple days he was released from jail. I finally had to tell him who was saving his ass so he couldn’t jeopardize my plan any longer. And finally at the biggest mVw Pay Per View, I L L U M I N A TI debuted. Everything fell into place. Our first plan of action was to show the world that our team, is the best team, and what better place to do that then the biggest tag team tournament around? The PTC Tag Titles were ours for the taking and it looked as if we were home free….until last week. Last week we lost to INVINCIBLENESS! How could we ever lose to those two? That’s like me losing to a one legged wrestler….IT’S IMPOSSIBLE! But I guess last week, the world tilted on it’s axis and the impossible became the very possible. It wasn’t all bad, it got us focused, it got us really ready for this damn tourney. I’ll chalk the first loss up to being inexperienced in tag team work, the next loss will be unacceptable. I’m confident that that won’t happen. The tag teams of PTC have awakened a beast, they’ve awakened a sleeping giant. It’s time for everyone to see that we’re not just all talk. [Rhys is jerked out of his thoughts suddenly by the voice of his new tag team partner and friend Jayson Kenny.] Jayson Kenny: Did you hear me? Rhys Cartel: What? Oh, um, no, sorry, just thinking. Jayson Kenny: I said that last week was complete bull. Rhys Cartel: You’re telling me? We should have never lost to Invincibleness! Jayson Kenny: I blame myself. Rhys Cartel: WHAT!? WHY!? Jayson Kenny: I dunno, I just feel it’s my fault. Rhys Cartel: Nonsense. Think about it, we just formed this thing a couple weeks ago and in fact that match was out first as a tag team. Invincibleness may not have been together for a long time, but they’ve probably been together for a considerable amount of time, they’ve taken their fair share of losses, but they overcame it. We’ve just got to get our chemistry right ya know what I mean? It’s no big thing man and nobody should be blaming anyone. Jayson Kenny: I’m f**kin pissed man. First I get kicked in the mouth by Seth Adams, then after I kick Shawn Johnson’s lousy carcass across the ring and take my damn United States title he finds the strength to call those two cronies of his, Sean Thomas and Suicidal, out to kick my ass three on one,a dn you’re no where to be found! Then on top of all that shit, we lose to Invincibleness in the first week of the PTC Tag Tournament, this is not shaping up to be a good month man. . .at all. Rhys Cartel: Whoa, settle down man. We got all the bases covered. TAJ set me up with Sean Thomas this week for Malice. He gave you Shawn Johnson, and Seth Adams and Shawn Johnson are duking it out. We basically got retribution on all fronts man. Jayson Kenny: But the PTC sh. . . Rhys Cartel: (cuts him off) C’mon man chill. Invincibleness did a good thing for us. They shoved their feet so far up our asses that they’ve awakened us. We thought we’d just waltz right through the tournament and they showed us that, that wasn’t to be. They’ve awakened a beast, man. The monster that is I L L U M I N A T I has been shaken awake and we can truly show all these PTC punks that we’re the most dominant team in the league. Ya know what I’m sayin? Jayson Kenny: Yeah, I guess you’re right. Rhys Cartel: Who do we got this week anyway? Jayson Kenny: (chuckles) Some bastards named The Corporation. But it doesn’t really matter does it? We’re gonna crush these dildos. [Rhys chuckles aloud at his partners eagerness, which wasn’t apparent a few minutes ago.] Rhys Cartel: I guess it really doesn’t, but I guess we should do a little homework on these guys. Jayson Kenny: They’re from Four Winds Wrestling if that helps any. The tag team is made up of, and this is a horrible name, “Canadian Perfect” Chris Wrestling, and some Spanish guy La Envida Mata or some shit. They’re a fairly new tag team so there’s not much info on’em. Rhys Cartel: (chuckles) Looks like some one has been doing their homework. Jayson Kenny: (shrugs) What can I say? After last weeks loss I figured we’d need to be a little more prepared. Even though there’s not much on these guys I think we can beat’em. This is their first PTC tag tournament match, and they’re probably taking it a little more lightly, kinda like we did against INVINCIBLENESS! I’m thinking, that they’re thinking that they can just scoot on by with a win. . .easy. Little do they know that, I L L U M I N A T I has truly awakened and that we’re out for blood. Rhys Cartel: That’s the spirit my friend. Nobody can stop us now. Not the Souls of the Black Lotus, not The Lunchroom Patrol, not the Metallo Zords, NOBODY! We’ve gotta let the world know that we’re not gonna take any more shit, from anybody. I L L U M I N A T I is God’s gift to PTC, and EVERYONE, will realize that. [The camera focuses in on a T.V. showing last weeks loss to INVINCIBLENESS!, then it FADES OUT!] It’s our time to shine. We’re not gonna take that loss laying down. The Corporation has got a rude awakening coming, if they think that Cartel & Kenny are gonna just lay down for them. It’s time for us to get respect. . . And time for the mVw to get the respect we deserve. . . It’s time. . . It’s the only shot we’ve got. . . . And you best believe we’re gonna take advantage of it. . . . I L L U M I N A T I “The Past, The Present, The Future”
