Guilt Hey Sebastian, I think it’s probably safe to say that I’ve lost whatever handle I had on the way things have been going. In the past few weeks, maybe even months, it’s spiraled so out of control that at times I’m not quite sure who the fuck I’m supposed to be. I know you know this already, but you’ve always said that me putting it out there would help me get over it, so… yeah. When the National Wrestling Council closed its doors, a result of her and everything she did, I didn’t really have anywhere to go. So, I ended up making a deal in Japan, hopped on a plane, and was fortunate enough to hook up with a guy there named Kageyama. He took me under his wing, showed me how things went down over there, and more or less made me a member of his family. On October 13, 2005 we were sparring, I lost my grip during a series of moves, and he took the fall which ultimately ended his career. Partial paralysis as a result of the injury, an injury that I caused, left him incapable of wrestling. On April 30, 2007 he died from complications following a heart attack. While I know the two aren’t related, the one thing that I never got over was that his last few years on earth were spent removed from doing what he loved because I fucked up. I didn’t cause his heart condition, but I could never get over thinking that what happened because of me helped break it. The only thing worse is that I did it again, only this time someone might actually die as a result of what I did, and as a result of who I am. My best friend lies in a hospital bed because of her, because of how I interacted with her, what I thought I could do, and what should have been a foregone conclusion. Right now he’s in a coma, “recovering” from an operation to fix what the doctors call a subcranial hematoma. Apparently, Merv’ is bleeding into his brain. I was supposed to be his best friend, but the only reason he was anywhere near the building is because I brought him. Maybe it wouldn’t matter. Maybe they’d have got to him at home anyway, but even then it would have been my fault. If I don’t push the issue, if I leave well enough alone then none of this happens. Everyone goes on with their life, and no one gets hurt. To be honest, I don’t know if he’s going to wake up, or what he’ll be like when he does. I may well have killed him, and even if I didn’t… I have ruined two lives, Park’. Two people, two families, are worse for having known me, and having been foolish enough to think that I was someone they wanted in their life. I can’t let there be a third. At this point you know me better than anyone I can talk to, which is why I’m sending this to you. I’m putting it in your hands to sort things out and tie up any loose ends. I guess there’s only one thing left for me to say. My name is Jared Andrew Sykes. I was born in Salem, Massachusetts on December 14, 1978. Two families have been ruined because of me, and by the time you read this there will be an unfortunate third. Tell my mom that I’m sorry. See ya’, J 11/30/08
