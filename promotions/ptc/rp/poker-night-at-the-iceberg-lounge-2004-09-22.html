Poker Night at the Iceberg Lounge

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 In a town named Gotham, the coolest hotspot is a swinging joint called the Iceberg Lounge. Almost any night the place is packed with movers and shakers, wanna-bes and never-weres. But tonight is different. The owner, Oswald Cobblepot, is throwing a private party with a very exclusive guest list. One of the names on that list pulls up to the curb in a midnight blue PT Cruiser. Stepping out of the car, Tsunami Oni glances up at the building with eyes behind dark glasses, in spite of the late hour. Dressed in a snakeskin suit and ribbed black t-shirt, Oni checks his blue hair in his reflection in the driver side window . He gives himself a small grin and walks toward the building. As the Vivaldi of Violence approaches the entrance to the Iceberg Lounge, he is stopped by the doorman, a burly Asian man in a tuxedo and bowler hat. He puts a hand up to stop Tsunami Oni. Oni: Oddjob, it's me, Oni. I'm on the list. I've been here a mess of times. Oddjob stares at Tsunami Oni, no recognition in his eyes. Oni: Yeah, I know don't look like I did last time I was here. I ditched the mask. But it's me and I wanna make some cash, so just let me pass. Tsunami Oni attempts to walk around Oddjob, but the doorman blocks his path. Oni: I don't believe this. Look, you used to let Harry Osborn in here when he was underage. Just because he ran around a couple times in his daddy's Goblin jammies doesn't make him a player. And here you are giving ME a hard time? I'm Tsunami Oni! You know, instigator of the Evilutionary War, founder of N.O.G.O.O.D? Ring any bells? Crossing his arms, Oddjob simply glares at the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. Oni: I can prove it. See, I still got horns. Tsunami Oni points to the small prosthetic devil horns on his forehead. But Oddjob's expression doesn't change. Oni's, however, is getting more and more exasperated. Oni: Oddjob, you can trust me. I'm Tsunami Oni. You know me. You've checked out Shiatsu's ass every time we would show up. Oddjob raises an eyebrow. Oni: No, she's not here and I really don't want to get into it. So just let me in. You're pissing me off. Oddjob remains steadfast. Oni: Look, it's not like I got a library card with "Tsunami Oni" written on it. There's no way to prove it to you. Except like... THIS! Tsunami Oni cuts loose a blast of EVIL BLUE MIST ~ into the eyes of Oddjob! As the doorman staggers in blinding pain, Oni grabs the bowler hat from his head and clocks him with it. There's a loud KLANG~! and Oddjob slumps to the ground. Tsunami Oni drops the bowler onto his unmoving body and steps inside the Iceberg Lounge. As Tsunami Oni passes through the door, a pudgy man with a pointy nose waddles up to him. Dressed in a tux, he carries an umbrella even though he's inside. He looks through a monocle at Tsunami Oni and the unconscious Oddjob. Oni: I told him I was on the list, Cobblepot. Cobblepot: Wah! Wah! I guess he had it coming. But if I get any Girls Scouts or Jehovah's Witnesses knocking on my door, you got to deal with them! Wah! Oni: You got it. Cobblepot: Now come on. We've already played two hands. Walk this way. As Oswald Cobblepot waddles off like a penguin, Tsunami Oni follows, imitating his distinct gait. Cobblepot spins and swats him with his umbrella. Cobblepot: Now cut that out! Oni: Sorry, Ozzie. The unusual-looking pair walk through the empty ballroom, the dim light and white tablecloths give the room a haunted mood. Cobblepot leads Oni to the VIP room. There a motley looking group are gathered around a green felt table with cards in their hands. A white haired man in a suit looks up and give Oni a nod of recognition. White haired man: Hello, Tsunami. Sorry, but we started another hand. Care for a beer? Oni: Thanks, Maggie. Maggie: Only you can get away with calling the mutant master of magnetism that... Tsu. Maggie points to a can of Sapporo and it floats into the air towards Tsunami Oni, who takes it. A flick of the white haired mutant's wrist and the can pops open itself. A short bald man with a scar dressed in a Nehru jacket is annoyed. Bald man: I hate when you do that! It screws up my PDA! Maggie: Like you know anyone, Ernst. Ernst: I know lots of people! I was head of SPECTRE! A stunning woman with black hair and mesmerizing green eyes snickers while petting a Siamese cat. Woman: Rawr! And I thought I was supposed to be catty... Ernst: You be quiet, Selina! You don't have people coming up to you asking where Mini-Me is or demanding you to say "one... million... dollars!" I swear if I ever get my hands on that Mike Meyers yutz I'll show him a REAL shark with freakin' laser beams coming out of its forehead! Oni: OK... cut him off... Selina: Here, Tsunami. You can sit next to me. Just let me move my purrrrrrse. An armored man in a green tunic and cape shakes his head. Armored man: Again with the purring. So trite. Selina shoots the man in metal an evil look. Selina: Listen, Vic. Just because you and your Latverian trouser snake are stuck in that sardine can, that's no reason to purrrrrsecute me! Vic: No speaks to Doom like that, especially a glorified street whore like you! Oswald Cobblepot begins waving his hands. Cobblepot: Wah! Wah! People! We're supposed to be professionals here, not children! Selina: Sorry, Ozzie. Vic, I'm sure you have a very nice Latverian trouser snake. Vic: Thank you, Selina. And I meant "glorified street whore" in the best possible light. Cobblepot: Great. Now back to the cards. Oswald Cobblepot takes a seat and the hand continues. After many turns of betting, raising, and folding, everyone who's still in shows their hand. Selina has the worst hand. Sighing, she takes her top off, revealing a black lace bra and a pair better than anything in poker. Selina: I still don't understand why you guys play for cash while I'm playing strip poker! Oswald: House rules! Wah! Wah! The winner seems to like it! Indeed, a huge slug-like creature is licking what passes for his lips with a disgusting tongue as reptilian eyes ogle Selina. Slug-thing: Booba shakka lakka! Om bonga slaggalagga! Selina: There is no chance in hell you'll ever get me in a gold bikini and slave collar, Hutt! The Hutt: Oog banga shoo moolookooo! Selina: Hey, I was out of my mind that night on cosmopolitans and you know it, purrrrrvert! The Hutt: Heh heh heh... A new hand is dealt. As the players look their hands over, Maggie looks up at Tsunami Oni. Maggie: So, Tsu, how goes things? Oni: Can't complain. Can't complain at all. I'm in Round 2 of the GTT4. I have my shot at regaining the UWF World championship at Live and Loud. My father wishes me to join the family business. Maggie: What would that be? Electronics? Banking? Oni: Yakuza. Maggie: A fine organization. I'm sure you'll excel. I must say I'm pleased to see father and son in business together. It broke my heart when Pietro and Wanda left my Brotherhood to associate with that archer and Yankee Doodle on steroids. Oni: Yeah, things are going pretty well. The sound of snickering cuts through the air. Ernst is looking very amused. Ernst: Really? Is that why dear sweet Shiatsu isn't with you tonight? There is the slightest tension on Tsunami Oni's face. His jaw clenches briefly at the bald man's question. Oni: She's still suffering from headaches. Seymour Almasy gave her a concussion last month and she hasn't recovered completely. Ernst has a fiendish smirk on his face. Ernst: Oh really, Mir. Oni? From what my sources tell me, there is a bit of... friction between the two of you. And I'm not talking about the kind of friction that occurs when Tab A goes into Slot B! With a snarl, Tsunami Oni throws down his card and rises, trembling with anger. Oni: Listen, Blowjob... Ernst: That's Blofeld! Oni: Shut your damn mouth! If you want to get personal, we can get personal. Why don't I tell everyone how you got that scar by your eye? Ernst: In battle! I got it in battle! From Bond! Oni: You lying sack. You were thirteen and at boarding school. You were peeking into the showers got caught, so someone threw a pencil at you. Selina looks at Ernst in mock surprise. Selina: Ernst, I thought you were the purrrfect gentleman! Ernst: Lies! It's all lies! Oni: Oh, and it gets better. He was peeking into the BOY'S shower! Ernst: I got the wrong window! It was an honest mistake! Everyone at the table is chuckling at the bald man's expense. Ernst is fuming as he points an angry finger at Tsunami Oni. Ernst: I don't have to take this from you, Oni! You ripped me off all the time! Orbiting laser cannon? That was me! Stealing nukes for blackmail? Me! Secret organization with an acronym for a name? All me! And just look at you now. Mr. Goody-two-shoes as guys like Almasy and Montgomery show you how evil is REALLY done! Why Cobblepot still lets you in here is beyond me! The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea stares coldly at Ernst Blofeld. Oni: Because he just loves watching me piss you off, you Uncle Fester-looking bitch! The tension is thick in the air. Oswald Cobblepot thumps his umbrella on the table. Cobblepot: Gentlemen! Wah! Wah! This is supposed to be a friendly game! If you can't settle down, than settle it outside! Oni: Maybe we should. Just then, a middle-aged woman in a fur coat comes rushing in. One half of her hair is white while the other is black. She looks very agitated. Cobblepot: Cruella! Cruella: Oswald! I can't believe you didn't invite me! Cobblepot: How did you get in here? Cruella: Your doorman was passed out at his post. Probably drunk, knowing the sort of ruffians you employ, Oswald! Cobblepot shoots Tsunami Oni a glare that would curdle milk. Cobblepot: This is your fault, you know. Oni simply shrugs as Cruella looks confused. Cruella: What? You don't want me here, Oswald? Oswald: It's just that this game is for a certain type. And you're not that type. Cruella: Supervillains, right? Well, I'm a supervillain! Cobblepot: Cruella... Cruella: I am! I got that whole back and white motif! I got a sinister name! I even have a song! Cobblepot: Cruella, you fight puppies. Cruella: So? Magnus over there fights a bunch of freaks who've never held a real job in their lives! Maggie nod his head in agreement. Maggie: It's true. Whenever Logan gives "best at what I do" line, I wonder what it is exactly that he does! Does he have any vocational training? Does wandering around and beating people up have a 401k? And you know, for a group that wants humanity to accept them, they really should get out of that mansion more often. Oh, and don't get me started on that name. When a woman that looks like RuPaul's protege comes up to you and says she's an X-man, try not to laugh! Cruella: See? Magnus agrees with me! He thinks you should let me in your little party! Maggie: No, Cruella. You fight puppies. You're a schmuck. Oswald Cobblepot stands up and puts his arm around Cruella's waist while having his umbrella tucked under the other. Cobblepot: Cruella, why don't we step out of the room and discuss this? Wah! Cruella: Sure, Oswald. You know, I have no idea why you never return my calls, We'd be perfect together. Penguins are black and white, you know. Cobblepot: Wah! Wah! I know. After giving, Tsunami Oni another nasty look, Oswald Cobblepot leads Cruella out of the VIP room. Oni turns to Ernst. Oni: Look, that's somebody that's really pathetic. Why don't we agree we're both bad-asses and leave it at that. Ernst: I agree. Truce. Suddenly, there's the sound of a gunshot from somewhere in the Iceberg Lounge! A woman screams and then another shot is fired! A few moments late, Oswald Cobblepot waddles back into the VIP room, the tip of his umbrella smoking. He resumes his seat at the table. Cobblepot: Well, I don't think we'll have to worry about that nuisance again. Wah! Wah! So, Tsunami, you were saying you were in some kind of tournament? Oni: Uh, yeah. It's this big wrestling tournament. There are guys from federations all over the place competing. Quite a few free agents too. There's a lot of competition, but a whole lot of glory. Maggie: And who is your distinguished competition? Oni: Someone named Bladwin. Selina perks up at this. Selina: Baldwin? I love Baldwins! Alec, Stephen, Billy... even Daniel. Oni: Err, that's Bladwin, not Baldwin. Selina: Oh. Nevermind. Oni: He's got a weird first name too. Volker. Hey, Ernst. You know a bunch of languages. Any idea what it means? Ernst thoughtfully rubs his chin. Ernst: Hmmm... Volker... I believe that it is Bulgarian for "he who possesses carnal knowledge of underage weasels." Or something like that. Oni: Whatever. The smart money is on me to slap him around like a red-headed stepchild. Vic: Wrestling...hmph! That is a sport of peasants! Peasants! Maggie: Vic, you started out as a peasant. Vic: I didn't say I didn't like it. Tsunami Oni turns to the man in metal. Oni: Hey, Vic... is it true that you got to tap Sue Storm's fine ass? Vic: Well, in an alternate future, yes. Oni: Wow, she dumped a guy with stretchable parts for you. Way to go, Doctor D! Vic and Tsunami Oni high-five like frat boys. Selina shakes her head and looks at her cards. She sighs and throws them down. Selina: I fold. Oni: Wait a minute... Selina: You're not supposed to look at my cards after I fold! Oni: You had a full house! Why did you fold? Selina: I'm an exhibitionist. So sue me. That said, Selina unzips her skirt and removes it, revealing a black lace thong and garter belt. As she leans forward to toss her skirt onto the table, Oni leans back to catch the view. Suddenly, Selina looks worried. Selina: Oh my God! Cobblepot: Wah! Don't tell me Aunt Flo just paid you a visit! Selina: No! My cat! I haven't seen my cat since that ruckus with that Cruella purrrrson! Maggie raises an eyebrow. Maggie: This isn't some feeble attempt to have you ask if we've seen your pussy, is it? Selina: No, I really can't find my cat! Maggie: Good, because that would be trite even for this bunch. Selina is on all fours looking for her cat. The men are looking upwards and thanking the Lord Almighty for this moment. Selina: Cleo! Where are you? Finding nothing, Selina stands back up. Selina: Look, none of you guys saw anything? Tsunami Oni, Vic, Maggie, Ozzie, and Ernst all shake their heads and answer "no." Selina: Jabba, what about you? The Hutt just shakes his head quickly. Selina: Jabba... Just then, Jabba lets out a massive belch. A huge furball flies from his mouth and onto the felt table. Cobblepot: Wah! You're going to pay to have that cleaned, Hutt! Selina is heaving as her rage grows. The male villains really dig the enraged heaving. Selina: You... SLUG! YOU ATE CLEO! Jabba: Boo shabba labba! Selina: I don't care if she was the best tasting pussy you ever had! Maggie: Damn, we were so close to avoiding something that. Selina: I'm gonna scratch your eyes out! Jabba: Shamma lamma ding dong! As Selina lunges for the Hutt, he starts to glow. Before his scantily-clad assailant can reach him, Jabba fades from view. Ernst: He beamed up! Maggie: I thought it was a different bunch that did that... Selina is left alone in her fury... and lacy underwear. Selina: Rawr! I can't stay here! I'm going home! Oni: Um, like that? Do you want my jacket? Selina: Nah, it'll be easier to get a taxi like this. As Selina storms out, the male villains longingly watch her leave. Cobblepot: Wah! That damn Hutt! Ruined all my plans. Well, let's just keep playing poker. The remaining bad guys resume play. After a few hands, Oni has gathered quite a bit in winnings while the others have lost quite a bit. On the final hand, it comes down to Ernst and Tsunami Oni. Ernst: I don't have any more cash on me. Do you take IOUs? Oni: Considering how many times you've faked your death, hell no. Ernst: How about this DVD of Pussy Galore and her lesbian flying aces in the shower? Auric gave it me for Christmas. Maggie: Not again with that word... Oni: Sure, that'll cover the bet. Ernst grins and lays his cards down. Oni: A straight! Not bad, Ernst. Just not as good as the four ladies in my hand! Ernst: Damn! Good thing I made copies of that. Cobblepot: Wah! Well, gents. It's been fun, but time to call it a night. That freak in the bat costume will be here soon. He acts spooky. I pretend to be scared. I give him info and he goes out and beats up my competition for me. This is a lot sweeter set up than when I tried stealing Faberge eggs. Wah! Maggie: All right, Ozzie. Hey, Tsu... good luck in your tournament. Vic: Yes, the Lord of Latveria wishes you good fortune in your battles as well. Oni: Thanks, guys. Hey, no hard feelings, Ernst? Ernst: How could I ever stay mad at you? Now that Mike Meyers yutz, he shall pay! Oni: I know, Ernst. I know. Cobblepot: You don't have to go home but you can't stay here! Wah! Oni: All right! We're going! Tsunami Oni leaves the VIP room. As he heads toward the door of the Iceberg Lounge, he sees Oddjob has regained his senses. Oni: You going to remember me next time? The doorman stares at the Vivaldi of Violence and says nothing. Oni: Yeah, I thought so. Stepping outside, Tsunami Oni looks at the DVD he won from Ernst. He thinks about how much Shiatsu would love watch it. He stares into the night, wondering where she is right now. Is she thinking about him? Does she feel as empty as he does right now? Or does she feel hate for him, caused by years of putting her life on hold while he put her in harm's way? Or what's even worse, maybe she doesn't think about him at all. He is so lost in these thoughts, he almost doesn't notice the woman stepping from the shadows. Selina: Purrrrhaps I need your jacket after all. Tsunami Oni gives her a small smile and tosses his snakeskin jacket to her. Selina catches it and drapes it around her shoulders. Oni: What're you still doing here? Selina: Waiting for you. I know you're not looking forward to going home alone. Oni: Well, I got to catch a flight to San Francisco first. Selina: Why not come home with me? There's always flights tomorrow. She raises an eyebrow as she gives the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea a smoldering look. Oni: I know pretty much every man on the planet will hate me for this, but I have to decline. Whatever is going on with Shiatsu and me, I can't say it's over. Maybe this is all my fault, but I really think we can be back to how we were. Selina: Don't let the boys hear you talk like that, you romantic fool. Oni: I'm serious here. Selina: I know, and that's what makes you so appealing. Well, if I can't have you, I'll guess I'll to be content with wishing for the best for you. Oni: Thanks. Selina. Selina: Look, a taxi. A cab pulls up to the curb. Tsunami Oni holds the door open and Selina slides in. Selina: Last chance? Oni: I don't believe there's any such thing as last chances. Selina: Good night, Tsunami. Oni: Good night, Selina. The taxi pulls away and Tsunami Oni walks to his rented car. After getting in, he realizes he left the DVD of Pussy Galore in his jacket that's now being worn by Selina Kyle. He laughs at the serendipity of the situation. Then Tsunami Oni starts the car and drives off into the Gotham night.
