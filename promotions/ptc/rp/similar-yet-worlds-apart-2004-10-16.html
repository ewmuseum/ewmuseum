Similar, Yet Worlds Apart

16 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-16 [A hushed silence descends upon the room as the red light on top of the camera is illuminated – they’re recording. Stood in front of the camera is Dave Harley. He looks remarkably confident, having successfully fought his way in to the fourth round of the PTC GTT4 Tournament. The banner behind him reads World Wrestling Alliance – the federation that in recent months Harley has dominated as leader of the Devil’s Own. His troops mill around off camera – Leviathan, The Pain Syndicate, Mr Darkness. Harley grins at the camera and removes his sunglasses as he begins to speak.] Harley: Black Cherry, Robb Hardy and now Salaok…these are the morons who have already fallen at the hands of the Extreme Machine Dave Harley. It’s Round Four and this time I’m up against Dallas Reeves. Well I’m not gonna stand here and rip you apart, Dallas…not yet anyways. You see, believe it or not we have quite a lot in common. [Dave glances at his Devil’s Own followers, most notably at Leviathan. It was Leviathan who helped Dave orchestrate his takeover of Devil’s Own – a faction that belonged to Ian Tense for a long time. Tense made the mistake of choosing Leviathan to represent him in a match in which his ownership of Devil’s Own was on the line. Leviathan laid himself down in the centre of the ring and allowed Harley to claim the deciding pinfall. Leviathan gives Dave a slight nod. He knows where Dave’s going with this.] Harley: You were trained in the Hansen House up in Ottawa. I trained in the Carnage Family Gym in Detroit. We both have quite a pedigree there. During your stay in X2K, you formed an elite stable with your old enemy turned comrade. Well, when I arrived in the WWA I formed an elite stable – X Corp – with my old enemy turned comrade, Mal Somers. Wow, talk about coincidence! You have a hulking man-beast named Beastor as your bodyguard. Well I have the Green Eyed Monster Leviathan watching my back. [Leviathan smiles at his comrade. They had once waged war against each other. It was the dominance of Leviathan and his partner Lockdown that brought Dave Harley and Mal Somers together. X Corp fought endlessly against them for months. A mutual respect is now held between Dave and Leviathan.] Harley: You have close ties to NGW World Champion John Hitchin. Well, you all know how close my ties with WWA World Champion Mal Somers are. [Harley almost laughs as he thinks about it. Harley turned on his former stablemate shortly after Mal won the World Title. Dave took over as leader of Devil’s Own and publicly humiliated the new World Champion on WWA Meltdown. To further aggravate Mal, he recently obtained a restraining order, which will prevent Mal Somers from getting his hands on Dave for 90 days. Dave was especially proud of that one.] Harley: But best of all Dallas, you’re considered to be one of the brightest and most popular stars in NGW today. Well guess what Dallas? I am the most popular star in the WWA. Take a look at the latest PTC rankings. I’ve been Number Two for two weeks in a row! Where’s the World Champion Mal Somers? His star power faded soon after he won that World Title. [The bitterness is clear in Harley’s body language. His fists clench and his teeth grind as he talks about Mal. It is this jealousy that has fuelled Dave’s antics in the past few weeks. It is this bitterness that has led to conflict with WWA legend Craven Moore. Harley’s envy has placed a huge target on the back of the Extreme Machine. Luckily for him, he stays close to his Devil’s Own troops at all times.] Harley: But we do have one major difference, Dallas. Can you guess what it is? Do you have any idea? The difference is that I traded my popularity in for power. I rejected the fans adulations. Why? To become the most powerful and most dangerous force the WWA has ever seen. That’s what you need to do to be on top, Reeves. Seize any opportunity that comes your way. I saw a way out of X Corp, a way out of the shadow of that piece of sh*t World Champion and I took it. And now here I stand before you. It’s Round Four of the GTT4 tournament. Only one of us can go through to the finals of the Pyrenees Bracket and I’ll be damned if it’s gonna be you. [Harley glances at the clock on the wall. His time is almost up. Besides, he’s starting to get bored of belittling this guy.] Harley: Think about what I said, Reeves. We may be similar, but we’re worlds apart. Next week, you’ll understand the power behind Harley 435. [Dave Harley relaxes a little. He feels confident that he’s given his opponent food for thought. He notices that the red light is still on and quizzically looks in to the camera.] Harley: Is this still on? The promo’s finished. Turn the damn camera off! [There is a light sound of snoring. It doesn’t take Dave long to identify the cameraman as the culprit. With a glance to Leviathan, the Green Eyed Monster is happy to oblige. He walks casually over and picks the cameraman up by the neck. His green contact lenses almost glow with rage as he carries the startled cameraman over to Dave Harley.] Harley: Good morning Mr Cameraman, I hope you slept well. Cameraman: Huh? Wha? I-I-I’m sorry, I didn’t get much sleep last night! Harley: Oh yeah? Well take a nap when I’m not paying you to do it! Did you hear any of my interview? Cameraman: Oh, um…yeah, yeah! Harley: Really? Pretty cool wasn’t it? Cameraman: I-I-It was awesome! Leviathan: I especially enjoyed the part where you destroyed that effigy of Dallas Reeves. [To the Cameraman] Don’t you agree? Cameraman: Yeah…th-th-that was my favourite part! Harley: UH UH! Wrong answer! Levi, could you turn that camera off for me? Leviathan: It would be a pleasure. [Leviathan flexes his muscles and with ease hurls the cameraman across the stage floor straight in to the camera. There is a spark and the camera crashes to the floor and smashes. A roll of film rolls across the concrete, stopping at Dave’s feet.] Harley: We’re using a Video Camera?! This is the digital age! Leviathan: Presumably Matthew W. traded the WWA’s Digital Cameras for cheap spirits. [Harley reaches in to his jacket pocket and pulls out a Dictaphone. He clicks record as he lifts it to his lips.] Harley: Harley Note; Kill Matt W.…or pee in his Whisky, whatever’s easiest. [Harley storms out of the studio, leaving the cameraman unconscious on the stage floor. Leviathan and The Pain Syndicate follow. Mr Darkness picks up the unravelled videotape, rolls it up and places it in his briefcase before joining his comrades.]
