"I seen this oppurtunity and acted on impulse"

14 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-14 *Fred Grest is seen wearing one of his WMW shirts that read "Fred Grest... I am The Show." The shirt is making reference to "The Fred Grest Show" that he does. If I explained I probably just confuse you so why bother. He is seen playing a Madden 2005 on the PS2. The screen shows Carson Palmer throwing the ball and he hits Chad Johnson who just burned Charles Woodson for a touchdown. Fred Grest's reaction to this is...........* Fred Grest- SON OFA BITCH! *Fred Grest throws his controller at the wall as you hear a knock on the door. Fred Grest answers the door and sees his best friend Owen walks in.* Fred Grest- Hey Owen... whats up? Owen- I met a huge fan of yours today. Fred Grest- Not suprising I have a huge fan base now since joining WMW. Owen- No, this kid has followed your career since WECW. He has every promo you've cut and every damn match you've been in. Fred Grest- Every match? Owen- Yeah, even the ones where you sucked. *Grest points to the camera and says....* Fred Grest- See that camera we're in the middle of a promo now. You can't tell my opponents I've had a bad match. Owen- Man, those things pop up in the weirdest places. Fred Grest- Yeah one guy in this tournament had one right after he had sex with this Japanesse chick. Owen- You sure it was a promo and not a porno. Fred Grest- I don't have too watch those anymore after all I am with Valkyrie now. Owen- Keywords in that sentence would be "don't have too" Fred Grest- Shut up Owen. Owen- Anyway, the kid knew who i was from your promos and wants to meet you. Fred Grest- Sure I'll meet the kid. Owen- He's out in the car right now but I should warn you. Fred Grest- About what? Owen- He has turret syndrome. Fred Grest- Oh well bring him in. Owen- Alright. *Owens walks out to the car and brings back a 13 year old kid wearing an old Fred Grest shirt that reads "Fred Grest The Extreme One". This is from his days in a very small fed in WECW where he competed in his 1st offical match and won the opening bout on the debut show. The match happened to be for the Hardcore Title where he started calling "The Extreme One". Fred Grest shakes the kid's hand who introduces himself.* Kid- Hello Fred Grest im Paul Neumann. Fred Grest- Nice to meet you Paul. Paul- I think its great that your in GTT4 you should do very well. Fred Grest- I know I will do well ultimately my goal just like everyone else is to win it. Paul- You have another WMW wrestler in your bracket Jakob Mayhem and FUCKING DUMBASS BITCH! Fred Grest- Its alright I am well aware who is in here from WMW. Paul- Yes, I know your an underdog but soon they'll know who that Fred Grest is THE MOTHER FUCKING best Fred Grest. *Fred Grest laughs at what Paul said wheather if it was intentional or not he may have made his catchphrase better.* Fred Grest- You may have just improved my catch phrase. *Paul smirks since he was just complimented by his hero.* Fred Grest- So you are my biggest fan? Paul- Yes I am I have followed your short career since I 1st saw you. I am just glad you are finally getting a chance in the bigger promotion like WMW. This may be the 1st promotion that may actually stay afloat even with AWS Man now in charge. Fred Grest- Yeah but WMW Isn't what this is about its me. I know that sounds cocky of me but I have a chance to prove myself here starting with Zach Johns. Do you know anything about him? Paul- Never heard of him but if decided to join this I am sure he is A DAMN COCK SMOKER WHO BLOWS HIS DAD'S DICK! Fred Grest- Wow, thats pretty incestful. Paul- Yeah, It is. Fred Grest- So do you want to hear a Fred Grest promo in person. Paul- You bet...........YOUR SHITTY FUCKING ASS I WOULD! *Fred Grest shakes his head knowning that he doesnt mean what he says.* Owen- Yeah, Im sure we could all use a nap about now. Paul- Fuck off Owen. Fred Grest- Somehow I think he met to say that. *Young Paul smirks and nods yes.* Owen- Shut up Freddy. *Fred Grest flashes his cocky smirk and starts his promo. The camera is directly focused on him now.* Fred Grest- Its funny my theme song is Step Up and yet that is exactly what I am doing. My entire career I have said I was the best and given little chance to prove it. I've been in some horribly ran feds that shut down shortly after they started. Now that I am in WMW a PTC fed I am more then confident that WMW will be around for awhile. I am starting to make a name for myself there and but I want every promotion to know the name Fred Grest. I want Prime to know who I am. I want Core to know who I am. I want WWA to know who I am. I want every single fed here to know the name Fred Grest. For money? No, thats not it. For glory? Nope! For the titles? Ofcourse not. While I want all those things thats not why I entered this. I want just like everyone else to be the best. I am willing to go through single person in PTC to do just that. While I may not win GTT4 I won't go down without a fight. Nobody knows who I am and I like it that way. The select few that do know me have me labeled a underdog which I do not mind at all. To be honest I am glad I am. *Fred Grest stops for a moment thinking about what this could do for him.* Fred Grest- You see when I found out about this tournament I had my doubts. I didn't think I stand a chance and maybe I don't. Now you are thinking why did I sign up for this then. I acted for the moment. I seen this oppurtunity and acted on impulse... *Suddenly confetti and ballons fall directly above Fred, Owen, and Paul. Oh and its alot way too much even as a siern goes off.* Owen- What the hell was that?! Fred Grest- Beats me. Paul- For some reason I think this was MOTHER FUCKING PLANNED! *Fred Grest shrugs his shoulders.* Fred Grest- Maybe... Owen- Anyway go on. Fred Grest- As I was saying before I acted on impulse... *All 3 look around too see if its going to happen again and nothing does.* Fred Grest- That impulse was to sign this and see what I could do. There is no shame in losing in something like this. I am up against the very bets from PTC promotions and guys who were stuck in smaller organizations like me. I am sure Zach Johns is a guy just waiting to show us what he can do as so many others are. I am sure there are plenty of guys here that are just like me taking an opportunity and making the most of this. This could very well be something to remember. We could seea huge upset from someone..... someone like me...... maybe even me. If I was a betting man I wouldn't count me out. Yeah I know Seymour Almasy is the favorite but does tat really mean anything anymore. The greatest upsets are the ones few if anyone expects. You've heard of the Miracle on ice well if someone like me were to win this it would the Miracle between the ropes. Yeah I would say normally you've mest with the rest now mess with the best because there isnt anybody like Fred Grest However not this time..... all I have to say is good luck to all. *The scene fades to black.*
