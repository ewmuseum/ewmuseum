(2) Getting back in

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 The streets of Washington D.C. are usually filled with patriotic tourists gawking at the sites of their nation. They stand at the Lincoln Memorial looking at the great president carved in stone, they stand silent and in awe at the man who lead us through a civil war. These same people then walk over to the Washington Monument and stare calmly into the reflecting pool, commemorating the man who lead us to our independence. From that spot they drift ever so quietly over to Arlington National Cemetery and walk to the eternal flame of John Fitzgerald Kennedy. They venture over to the grave of the unknown soldier and watch silently as the men march back and forth all day, changing the guard every forty-five minutes. They go to the capital building and stare in wonderment, and they walk over to the White House and wonder if our great leader is inside doing foreign policy. Yes this city is full of amazing things by day. By night though, it becomes a totally different animal. The scum begins to creep out slowly, hiding away from the day light. They begin their walks daily at about this time, like the vampires of old they slowly sneak out and begin their business. The up right well to do citizens of Washington D.C. know when the street lights kick on, it’s time for them to go inside, the drug dealers, the gangs, the contract killers. All in our nations capital, men who rank high on our most wanted lists freely roam the streets. At this same time, all the other small time sleazebags know when to go back to their holes. They understand when a big time somebody comes walking down the road. A man like Token Weed. He walked down a small alley in Washington D.C. his chrome forty-five strapped tightly under his leather coat, the small time guys knew who he was. The hippest newest drug dealer on the D.C. streets, word had already spread he was dealing the mayor his fix of crack. This alley lead the king of the scum down from the pits of darkness into the light of a bustling bar district. He grinned, he had a big sell to make tonight, to bring the greatest fighter of all time back from the dead in this tournament. That was the way a man like Token Weed perceived himself, of course he had been beaten before. But the key thing is, there has never been a decisive win. No one had ever totally cleaned this mans clock, he was not Oscar Delahoya in the ninth round with Bernard Hopkins. This man was a 27 year old KING. He was untouchable, invincible, a god among men. This god among men was going to use his persuasive powers to wiggle his way back. Back onto your television sets, back into your home. The GTT was being aired on Pay Per View, all hours of the day, multiple rings in one giant stadium. Token Weed wanted back into one of those multiple rings. He wanted to be in front of people. This time though, he wanted to maim the mother fucker in there with him. Not many people could pull something amazing off though, they couldn’t wiggle their way back into a tournament like this with out… Well that’d take something special. Something like an addiction. Token grinned as his head filled with thoughts, he walked down through the bustling bar area in Washington D.C. The small time dealers would make their deals on the street, right here and they’d all be ‘big ballers.’ A man like Token Weed doesn’t have to flaunt what he has and what he makes like all of these insecure fuck wads. He doesn’t need to brag his way to the top, he knows where he stands. The chrome .45 just makes sure the others know. Token’s smile widened as he came to a medium sized office building. ‘Primetime Central Promotions’ read the sign outside of the walk way. He reached into his leather jacket producing a large bag, he approached the glass double door and pressed the buzzer. ‘Yes’ an elderly woman said as Token stood outside calmly waiting. ‘Tell Mr. Argyle one of his money pits is here to visit him,’ Token said rather cheerily. ‘Mr. Argyle isn’t accepting appointments right now,’ the woman said. Token began to grow impatient. ‘Tell Mr. Argyle I have his money for him. I also brought over some freshly made candy from my wife,’ Token stated, he was impatient but still keeping his charade of being happy on. The woman rang back over the intercom system. ‘Mr. Argyle said he’d like to see you immediately Mr. Weed. Please enter in the left door, hang a right, third door on the left.’ The woman stated dryly. She had been at this post for nearly two hours, the endless amount of wrestlers coming in and out was starting to make her hate this job. The man at the door on the other hand entered through the two glass doors and stood silent looking around the building, in the middle sat the PTC symbol. The different federation flags hung ornamentally from a second level balcony, very much like the United Nations. Token began to walk forwards, he could hear the click of his shoes off of the white marble. The money the PTC had made over the years was no secret to anyone, but to Token he couldn’t believe he hadn’t found out about this customer until now. He walked passed the cold white marble pillars that held the balcony above up, as he walked down the hallway making it a point to count the doors. He approached the third door, it was ornately carved. Token glanced at the wood, he was sure it was oak. The calmness of the building wasn’t going to negate Token’s street smarts though. He slowly opened the door, his chrome .45 glared into the darkness of the room. It all happened in a flash, Token felt a large arm wrap around his neck, another set of arms came out of the darkness and grabbed his fire arm. The gun quickly left his hand as he put his arms into the air to surrender. Token grinned slyly as the lights flashed on and Argyle sat silently glaring across a glass desk. The two men walked over and sat Token down in a hard wooden chair. ‘So boy, what do you have for me?’ Argyle stated like a small child in a toy store. ‘Hey boy, how about I get my fucking gun back before I put four holes in your head. With my foot.’ Token stated as he glared across the table. These two men weren’t exactly buddies by any means. ‘Anyway, what do you want for it?’ Argyle asked slyly as he glared across the table. That’s when the blue haired man began to smile. ‘I want back in.’ Token stated bluntly as Argyle looked at him oddly. ‘What?’ Argyle asked as he continued looking at him, could he be serious? ‘I know you hired some of the dip shits from the indies to fill some of your spots. They never showed and you have a few play in spots remaining.’ Token stated. His smile grew wider as he had ended that sentence. Argyle shook his head in a no. ‘I don’t mix business with pleasure Token, so how much money?’ Argyle stated as he pulled out his wallet. ‘I don’t want money. I want back in, period. No questions asked. If you have a problem with it, fuck off. I’ll take my coke and sell it to some one who will give me what I want for it. Then I’ll throw in the fact I’ll have every other coke dealer in D.C. knocked off. Is that ok with you fuck stick?’ Token said glaring at Argyle. Argyle shook his head once again. ‘Token you don’t have half the power you say you do.’ Argyle stated angrily. ‘You will not deny me, you will not step into my way again.’ He said, he was infuriated, the hair on the back of his neck had slowly began to stand. That’s when Argyle’s jaw dropped. Token instantly stood up and pulled his leather bomber jacket open. Strapped to him were explosives that wrapped all the way around. ‘They aren’t armed’ Argyle said as Token smiled, taking off the jacket and throwing it on the table. ‘No they aren’t, but about 7,000 more like them are. Inside of the arena you rented out under your insurance dip shit. So you have a choice, take my drugs and put me back in. Or get the fuck out of my face and be a poor as mother fucker that can’t buy it off me if he wanted to.’ Token stated as he slowly pulled out the large bag of cocaine. Argyle slumped his head to the ground. ‘Okay Token… you win. See you tomorrow.’ The deal was done, he could never sweep the building for all of those explosives in 24 hours. It was impossible, and now he knew why Token Weed would glare on in his eyes. The King of The Scum.
