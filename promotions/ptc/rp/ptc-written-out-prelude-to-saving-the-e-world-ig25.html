Written Out: Prelude to Saving the E-World (IG25)

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nova stands across the ring from the Last Son of Lucha Libre, the anticipatory roar of the crowd sending lightning bolts of adrenaline rushing up and down his spine. The bell sounds and the Rising Star rushes forward, bummed in the knowledge that Damien Cruz is about four times as fast as he is. BAM BAM BAM!! Backwards stumbles the Rising Star, hands flying up to shield his face as Cruz flings foot after foot upside his head. He bounces back off the ropes and catches the Latin Assassin in the cradle position before flinging him overhead with a release suplex. Cruz rolls with it, and both men spring to their feet and collide in a lock-up. Suddenly Nova’s eyes widen as the cheering crowd becomes visible through his opponent’s body. Slackening out of shock, Nova allows the now-translucent Cruz to shove him to the mat. Cruz leaps forward, extending an elbow, but his image by now is so transparent that as he connects with the Rising Star, he vanishes altogether, leaving only Nova lying in the ring. The crowds are silenced and vanish as well into complete blackness. ------*~*~*------ November 24th, 2006... Nova takes a drag of his cigarette, the end of the butt lighting up a bright orange in the relative darkness of the second-story office in his old house. He puts it out in the ashtray next to the computer and pushes his glasses back up his nose as his eyes dart across the monitor screen, trying to make sense of what he sees. But he can’t make any sense of it at all. “K-Wolf has resigned. AgentDash gets a free pass to Round 3.” “William Gwynn has resigned. Yori Yakamo Jr. gets a free pass to Round 3.” “Almasy is out. Sirrajin gets a free pass to Round 3.” The headlines assault him. Gwynn, out. Almasy, out. K-Wolf, out…the rest of his bracket, emptied. For the Rising Star, it doesn’t add up. William Gwynn he can’t vouch for. But Almasy? Nova knows him fairly well, from Craig Miles’ New Frontier Wrestling. Hell, Nova got the Final Fantasy stoned for the first time backstage at the ULTRATITLE playoffs and watched him cackle with glee as he experienced FFVII’s Chocobo ranching system in a whole new way. The show hasn’t aired yet, but the segment is sure to be an instant classic for the legions of Almasy’s fanatically fanbase. There’s just no way Seymour decided to just throw in the towel and let this one go, not when everyone knows that it’s probably his last campaign in the game. Not when he received a play-in back into GTT6 after getting, quite literally, flattened by GCW’s Kimbusa. Where did he go? The same goes for the K-Wolf. Why would she bow out of the biggest tournament of the year, when she almost captured the tournament championship in GTT4? This would be just the victory Wolfenden needed to capture the public eye again. As with Almasy, rumors were circulating that GTT6 was the K-Wolf’s proverbial last dance. Nova slides a joint out from behind his ear and sparks it, leaning back in the computer chair to turn on the air ionizer stationed next to the desk. Maybe there’s nothing to see here. Maybe Nova’s just stoned and in the mood for a good, old-fashioned conspiracy theory. But maybe, just maybe, there’s more to this than anyone realizes. Doesn’t anyone else find this a little strange? ------*~*~*------ PTC Headquarters, the Earth’s Core Nova steps inside the glass elevator and pushes the Core 4 button. The doors close, and the elevator shoots downward. Nova is awestruck as expanses of cavern become visible to him, molten waterfalls glowing and flowing amidst the blackened rocks. Ding! “WELCOME. TO. THE FOURTH. CORE. FLOOR. ALL. THINGS. G. T. T. SIX.” Nova steps out onto smooth marble and looks around. The walls are marble as well, bare except for the same Salvador Dali on the left and right side of the hallway, “La Tentation de St. Antoine.” A single marble desk sits at the end of the hallway. Water runs down the wall behind the desk, backlit in orange, and a skinny bald man in a black suit stands behind the desk, absently tapping a pen against his forearm. At the sight of Nova he stands up straight and grins broadly. “HE-LLOOOOO…,” the man says in a high-pitched voice as Nova walks up to the desk. “Hi,” Nova says, a serious expression on his face, “are you the, uh, GTT6 guy?” “There’s a whole staff to the tournament,” the man replies condescendingly, “but I’m on staff today. How may I help you?” “I want to inquire about the tournament drop-outs in Pooh Bracket,” Nova says, solemnity in his tone. “What’s there to inquire about?” the man asks, cocking an eyebrow. “Seymour Almasy, Karina Wolfenden, and William Gwynn dropped out of GTT6. That is all. Nova and Damien Cruz will compete for the remaining third-round spot.” “I know, I’m Nova!” Nova exclaims, “And you don’t think there’s anything strange about what’s happened to Pooh Bracket?” “They dropped out,” the man reiterates, his smile fading slightly, “that is all.” “I don’t think that’s al-” Nova begins, but the man cuts him off. “You have only completed one promotional video of the two required to compete in the second round, no?” the man asks, his grin returning, “that’s not very…competitive of you, is it?” “There’s time yet,” Nova replies defensively. “Is there?” the man asks, grabbing hold of the digital clock on his desk and turning it around to face Nova. 8: 34 “So?” Nova says with an indifferent snort. “The deadline’s not until midnight.” Still cackling, the man behind the desk grabs his computer monitor and turns it around as well, the screen illuminating Nova’s face, which becomes immediately horror-stricken. Round 2B Finishes 0 days 0 hours 0 minutes “No…,” Nova says, backing away from the desk, “something is fucked-up here.” *Pointing a finger at the man* “SOMETHING IS FUCKED UP HERE!” He turns and begins running down the hallway as the man behind the desk howls with laughter. “THEY DROPPED OUT!! AH-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA!!! THEY DROPPED OUT!! HAHAHAHA!!! THEY DROPPED OUT!!” ------*~*~*------ That night, the House Formerly Known as Vega, the Same Desk-Chair We Began the Promo in… Nova sits in front of the computer, visible only by the light of the monitor and the ember of his cigarette. His eyes rest on the PTC forums page, a source of online communication in the business. I’ve gotta be careful. If today was any indication, this whole Disappearing Act thing going around GTT6 might be bigger than I at first imagined...after all, I was silenced for inquiring after the missing persons, kicked out of the bracket I was gonna win and the tournament I was gonna make my mark in… The Rising Star shakes his head bitterly. He opens a link to a news article titled “Seymour Almasy…Dead?” The fact that someone else finds these disappearances unsettling is at least a step in the right direction…but somehow I don’t think this is true. I think…yeah, I think this is propaganda, meant to divert people like me from truth…but what is the truth? He takes a drag from his cigarette before putting it out and giving his glasses a push back up his nose. I need to find someone else out there who realizes something’s wrong…but I have to be careful how I go about this. He pulls up a new topic screen and types “Pooh Bracket’s Gettin’ Kinda Lonely…” as the title, before typing “It's really weird how Almasy, the K-Wolf, and Gwynn have all dropped out of Pooh Bracket. Anyone else find this particularly strange?” Harmless. Nova posts it and begins playing Solitaire, occasionally refreshing to see if he’s nabbed any replies. But he’s a tired fella, and soon passes out. He wakes up some time later, darkness still present outside the office window (as well as rain from a hefty ongoing storm). He rubs his eyes and looks up at the screen. A reply! Yahoo! Deutschland | Mail, Wetter, Suche, Politik, Nachrichten, Finanzen, Sport & Videos “I’ve noticed that something quite specious is going on within that bracket. Perhaps we should discuss this further over a bowl of not cereal,” is the covert reply from Garbage Bag Johnny, shown to be posted around three hours ago. BAM BAM BAM!! Three firm knocks at the front door startle Nova, who gets up and jogs out of the room and into the hallway. He opens the door, and a soaking wet GBJ practically falls into his arms, panting from loss of breath. He looks up at the Rising Star, eyes wild as beads of water drip off his face. “IG 25!” he gasps. “There’s still time!” STAY TUNED FOR THE NEXT INSTALLMENT IN THE “WRITTEN OUT” SERIES FROM YOUR FRIEND AND MINE, NOVA!
