Yeah, There's No Pictures So Don't Bother

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 NOTE: The following conversation comes from the archives at Enders & Friends, a manufacturer of many products that make their way into homes all over the world. It was recorded for the security of the customer. Agent #315, Mary Calhoun: Hello, Enders & Friends, how may I help you? Customer #71387: My toaster won’t work. I have a hankering for a toasted bagel with cream cheese and my old toaster shits itself. So I guy buy this toaster made by your swindling corporation and it- Calhoun: I’m sorry to hear that, sir. If you can explain to me the exact happenings of your situation, I will do the best I can to serve you. Customer #71387: The happenings of my situation are that this toaster sucks so much cock it’s got a place reserve at a street corner near you. Calhoun: Sir, we won’t get anywhere if you don’t calm down. Now tell me what the exact problem you’re having is with the toaster. Customer #71387: I plug it in and it doesn’t turn on. It just sits there doing jack-all on the counter. Calhoun: Have you checked all the fuses in your residence? Customer #71387: Well, I don’t live in a residence, I can afford to live in a very expensive apartment. But yes, I asked to have the fuses checked and they’re all fine, so you’re oh-for-one. Calhoun: Is it possible that the outlet isn’t working? Customer #71387: No, I didn’t want to take the four-second effort of plugging something else into the very same outlet and just decided to call you so you could tell me to do it. Calhoun: Ummm…okay. Customer #71387: That means I already checked. Calhoun: Now, could you read me the model number that’s on the back of the toaster? Customer #71387: A-D-F-1-1-1-2-0. Calhoun: Alright, I’ll just check on something for a moment. - Customer #71387: Well, what’s going on? Calhoun: I checked the model number and they’re not one of the models that have been involved in any type of recall. Have you made sure all the toaster’s settings are on their default? Customer #71387: Yes, I haven’t changed a goddamn thing. Calhoun: I’m not really sure what to say, Mister… Customer #71387: I’ll give you a hint as to what to say- "I’m an unhelpful cunt." Calhoun: There is NO need for that… Customer #71387: Mr. Tsonda. That’s who I am. And yes, there’s a complete need for it. Were you or were you not an unhelpful cunt? Did you or did you not alleviate my problems? Calhoun: Well I did everything in my power- Customer #71387: Well go get some more power. You didn’t do enough! Calhoun: There’s no reason for that tone! Customer #71387: So whenever I buy a kitchen appliance from Enders and all his two-faced Friends, I should just assume it won’t work. I get your meaning. Calhoun: I mean that what you’ve experienced is an anomaly. Customer #71387: What I’ve experienced is a shitty toaster and shitty resolution of a problem! Calhoun: I’m certain that if you take the toaster back to the fine retailer that sold it to you, you’ll be able to get a full refund. Customer #71387: So it’s unreasonable to expect to get anything that actually works from your crooks? I should just give up and ask for my money back now? Calhoun: No, sir, I do suggest that you ask for the same product, however, if you- Customer #71387: Well, I won’t, so don’t bother. Calhoun: I’m sorry to hear that, sir. Is there anything else I can help you with today? Customer #71387: No, but you’re not allowed to hang up on a customer, so I think you’ll be my ear for a second here, missy. You know, I’m pretty fucking important. I’m competing in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. And- Calhoun: Sir, I’m going to hang up. Customer #71387: Consider your bluff called. You’re not allowed to hang up on a customer. In fact, you have to hear me out. So I’m competing in this huge tournament, except no one remembers how fucking fantastic I was back in PWC and I just got into PRIME so some of them are dumb enough to be expecting a win by that Luther.. Ry…Rx…the one with the R-last name. But let me tell you something about Chandler Tsonda. I don’t ever give up, I don’t lay down for anybody, and I damn sure don’t take no for an answer. Wait, are you still there? Oh fuckbean. Conversation terminated.
