(His)tory of Blood - Part Two

21 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-21 **Eryc pauses for a moment and looks over to the table to see that his bottle of water is now empty. He gets up and walks out of sight but returns a short time later with another bottle of water and presumably has had a bite to eat because he’s been sitting in the same chair for hours speaking about his past.** Ok, where was I? Oh yes, I was going to discuss my return to Francia which at the time of my return was renamed France. I first traveled to where I was born to see if I could find any information about my family, my brother and his wife and of course my son but only a few people recalled that we were ever there. It was stated that after my disappearance, my son was raised for a brief time by my brother and wife until my brother was ordered into the army to fight against the Vikings in which he died in battle. My sister-in-law left the area with my son and there was no contact with them after that; I can only imagine that she raised him to adulthood and remarried after my brother’s death or remained a widow until her final days. Knowing the information I was looking for, I left to go see if Audric was still haunting around where he had taught me but his manor was empty and the winery had been dormant for a half a decade according to some sources I talked to. I did not and still do not know if Audric is still alive but even before I became mortal again I didn’t feel his presence anywhere which gives me the suggestion that he met his final death between the time after we parted and before I returned. It’s such a sad fact that a good man and teacher did not manage to live as long as he should have but even still I am indebted to him for guiding me in the ways I needed because I’m sure that even today he would still be proud of me and my accomplishments. **Eryc knew he had come a long way since then but this was only merely the beginning of his adventures as plenty more events have taken place; especially in the second part of the millennia of his existence. The horrors that plague him to this day are just barely scraping the surface of his memory as he recalls them rather vividly and anyone with a lesser will would have ended their lives long ago; it is a wonder how even the vilest creature the likes of which Corvus was could still have a human side.** My desire to see more of Europe (along with the rest of the world) grew as I traveled north where I found my way to England and settled in to an area that was right outside of London. The area was rather quiet and nobody came snooping around and to them Alexander Rook was nothing more then a well to do sculptor who kept to himself and that is the way I preferred it. Every now and again I would go to a social function to woo some ladies and who wouldn’t when I had the charm of a Casanova and the looks to match? It was more like gathering up a black book of young nubile ladies to keep my meal card punched. Being that I was a sculptor, I would be able to lure women to my estate to see the collection of art that I had done which in ways some of the individual pieces were erotic which captivated these ladies to try and talk me into doing a sculpture of them (whether it be clothed or in the nude). This was my way in and could feed on them (however, I rarely did kill my victims but made sure they would not remember their experience.) Most of them were repeat liaisons in which I knew what I was dealing with as far as the cleanliness of the women. I liked to make sure that those I drank from were disease free and with outbreaks of different things (like the Black Death aka Bubonic Plague), you could never be too careful in those times. Disease would be the least of my troubles though. One evening as I was walking back to my estate I came across a creature unlike any that I had ever seen (besides my own kind). It was a large furry looking hound with extremely long teeth and built like a tank. The creature I speak of (which I later found out through a friend) was called a Garou, which would be referred to in modern times as a werewolf. This one happened to be in crinos form (think wolf man on steroids) and was very pissed off about something. (Which I would learn might be because the Garou and vampires mix about as well as oil and water.) I was unprepared for this and even though I got a few licks in, the creature nearly handed me my ass…literally. He tore shreds into me which left me very weakened and close to the point of meeting my final death. When there are unknown entities in the world you have to be prepared just in case and I knew one day I would get into a fight with another vampire or a creature such as the Garou and I was prepared for it. The odds of coming across a creature such as this were rare I thought and go figure it was a Werewolf of London (more like nearby London but what does it matter). Is it any coincidence there is a song called Werewolves of London? In any event I still find it amusing. Now I’m going off track; I’m sorry, it’s rare that I am able to make a joke. I was decimated very thoroughly by this Garou and thought that my end was immanent but in my mind recalled a practice Audric had told me about torpor, which is a recuperative like coma for vampires. With this in mind, I previously had made a secret room in the basement of the estate so that I would not be found or disturbed by mortals and then placed myself in this sleep like state. I slept for at least a quarter of a century to heal all the wounds I sustained and what woke me up was my thirst and nothing could sate it unless I arose from my tomb and butchered anything I could get my hands on…be it a man, woman, child, or an entire family. I had to regain my energy to use my powers. When I left my estate it was the same as I had left it minus a few of the sculpts I did which didn’t mean anything to me unlike if furniture or the paints I accumulated over the years…some of them were priceless and if I were to sell them today would make several fortunes worth of money. Some of that has been lost along the way but a lot of it has been stashed in places that only myself and a few close associates could recover it if need be; you can never be too careful in any time period. Despite that fact, I needed to feed. I remained covered in the shadows while I roamed about looking for the best spot in which I might be able to find what I was looking for and much to my chagrin the only thing that might have several people inside would be the church I was fast approaching. Normally godless creatures would fear to tread upon holy ground but sensing something different about it begged me to investigate it further. Inside the church a dozen or more bodies were soaked in blood and a couple of them had been mutilated. I thought it might have been the work of the loathsome Garou I encountered a century prior but no… My magnified sense of smell picked up something else, a vampire, one that was of my kind: a toreador. What I encountered next was one of the most disturbing sights I may have ever encountered. The vampire that I was seeking out (and the jerk who took the food out of my mouth) was a priest himself in his full get-up. His name was James Black, whom later I would call Deacon Black. James’ family was living in the church but it was invaded by the least likely of all vampires I would have expected: my maker, Helena. Helena first killed James’ wife and then slowly tortured him by weakening him enough to render him helpless to fight against her; she taunted him as she took the lives of his two daughters and proceeded to give him the same choice as I was given. In the same fashion she abandoned him as she did me and that made us brothers in a way and it would give me the opportunity to right wrongs that had been done to me by taking up the role of mentor as Audric Du Chateau did with me. James and I burned the bodies just in case Helena had made a mistake (which was highly possible). He then knew at that moment his life as he knew it was over and a part of me inside, the one you are seeing before you now held compassion for his plight. In time when there is so much of it for you, you tend to forget things: what it is like to enjoy a real meal, the comforts of a woman, and the emotions your mortal soul provide for you. Those were things lost to me but I could see them deteriorate in James’ and it pained me to see it…the beauty of innocence stripped right out from under him. We both had our chance to choose death but something deeper within wanted to find out what was waiting in the wings on the other side. With James I was never selfish with my teachings of our clan’s ways but some vampires might withhold things in fear that their childer (or offspring) would slay them for more power and is the reason why the elders of the remaining vampire families remain in hiding until they are ready to devour the world. James and I traveled together for about a century and a half before I turned him loose into the world as Audric did with me. We knew that we would see one another again somewhere in the years and centuries to come and welcomed it; I can safely say that no other person has looked out for me as I have for them then with James. He remained in England for some time longer but I decided to explore the emerald isles of Ireland. I would place myself being there around 1889 give or take a year. It was there where I became acquainted with a writer of whom I had heard was doing research on vampires despite the fact that all they had ever been were fictional tales people told and not reality. Bram Stoker was rather intellectual about some of our kind but all of what he knew was based on hearsay and that is why when we came into contact and I began helping him write his epic novel, Dracula, I steered him more from the truth so it would continue to only be half truths. You’ll find that in every single vampire story you ever read. Most things are consistent but there will always be one or two differences that make it harder to see the whole picture that these creatures do exist in the world. Stoker never considered that I was a vampire even though I fit the description of the main character of his story which of course he embellished with minor differences here and there and I wasn’t going to stop him, I thought it was a brilliant piece of literature. When the book was published in 1897, I took a gamble and traveled to the Middle Eastern lands that I had heard were rather dangerous. I was fearless back then. It was more of Corvus making decisions then I was. He was in control. Saudi Arabia was where I found myself next and it was also where I became even more of a monster then I was before. The clan of vampires which inhabited there were unique and deadly assassins called the Assamites; you might still see some of them in modern times associating with the likes of Al Qaeda and other terrorist groups. (Any of them with the right amount of money says enough.) I was in the wrong place at the wrong time when I encountered them and instead of killing me they captured me and held me prisoner while torturing me all the while just to get their rocks off. My only question was why hadn’t they killed me for intruding? Dozen’s of other vampires had spoken of this clan and the ones that hadn’t made any deals with them did not have anything good to say about them…not one solitary word. Soon it would become clear to me what their intentions were: they were preparing me to become one of their newest assassins. The name of Corvus had journeyed even to this particular part of the country after competing with Vlad the Impaler and enjoying pillow talk with Elizabeth Bathory. **The thoughts of the torture he endured while in the Middle East haunted him as every single drop of blood they took from him and every little singe of his skin from taunting him with fire and sunlight echoed in the eyes of his mind. What made things even worse it was not the torture they put him through but more of what they forced him to do after they broke him down after he was tortured This was where the true evil beginnings of Corvus began.** I was not the only one being tortured at the time. They had a traitor among them who for some reason was giving out their plans to rival clans in order to thwart the leader of the Assamites in hopes that he could take over for the group once the other man was no longer trusted but the plan failed horribly. His punishment was to be final death in which I was to drink all of his blood and take all of his inherent abilities in an act called Diablerie (it is frowned upon in vampire society and in many cases the penalty is death if caught). In place of the traitor that I killed I became a high ranking member of the small branch of the assassins which was unheard of before in the clans history as they normally did not allow outsiders of different ethnicities other than Arabs’ into the clan but after my joining they began to look outside of the Middle East as well for potential assassins. As time passed I accumulated many kills and not only counting the victims I bled to death with feeding but also those I was chosen to kill as targets. Some of them were even popular world leaders which I cannot indulge the name of for safety purposes. Closing in on the middle 1900’s, I began to see the things I was doing at the time were not who I was or wanted to be even though the evil inside me made a good enough argument for the reasons why these methods were necessary. Maybe they were necessary but I didn’t want it anymore…I wanted simplicity. I began to plan my escape but did not know exactly how to and this is when I knew that I would need the aid of an old friend…James Black. With the ability to summon anyone by thinking of them, I beckoned James to come to Saudi Arabia to fake my demise. A month and a half later he appeared and with a surprise I would never have expected…my brother. To make this easy I am simply going to refer to him as what I know him to be now: as Ash. My brother was near death in a battle against the Vikings when one of our own brought him over into unlife; I would never to have expected two members of the same family would later reunite as vampires. James and Ash were able to convince the assassins they had killed me and all of us then stowed away on an oil tanker which its destination was the Americas, which I have never had the chance to explore. The Great Depression was beginning to bloom which meant that I would have to cull some of the hidden money I stowed away in addition to what I had on my person (which was not much) at the time. Prices of real estate were very low in this time and allowed me to get a nice little studio in which I could take up painting to create pieces with the intention of selling later when the economy picked back up (if it were ever to pick up). In the early 1960’s I began to spread out into other artistic genres other than those involving art; I started to see what kind of effect I could get with my voice and mind you it wasn’t the type that would make the Daughters of Cacophany proud (a small bloodline of vampires whose voice in song could render any male vampire or not to do their bidding or explode their ear drums) but it was at least something. Despite the low economy people still made their way to the opera houses which I became an unknown star under the identity of Julian Cassadine, which began my interest in the performing arts and would later help me in my current environment. Regrettably I was only able to stay in New York for ten years without people growing suspicious of me barely aging or rather not aging at all; it was time to take leave and maybe see how places I visited progressed since I was there last. The best choice for me personally was Japan because I admired them and I’m sure their culture had only improved with time. Returning there proved to be a pivotal moment in my life; one that has shaped me to be the person I am today. **Eryc’s tone was of fondness whenever he spoke of his time in the orient; it was the one true place he felt like he could be at peace with both himself and the beast inside of him. It was a place where he learned the meaning of loyalty, honor, and dedication to not only yourself but others around you and that is the greatest gift that he has ever received what would be well over a thousand year life span.** I kept my business discreet when I first arrived in the sense that I did not intend to make waves with any of the vampires that inhabited the island. The old saying that you should never reach into another man’s cookie jar without asking would be the appropriate thing to say in regards to how eastern kindred thought of their western counterparts. One night as I was walking around on a sight seeing expedition, I encountered a mugging in progress with two thieves trying to rob an old lady of her purse which I stepped in to break up and nearly crippled them which caught the attention of a wrestling promoter who witnessed the incident. Shohei Baba, otherwise known as Giant Baba was interested in using my size and stature in his wrestling organization which at first I refused but he offered me a good amount to take care of my living expenses. The only setback was that I was an American and I could only take part in attractions that were held at night. Both complications were easily dealt with (I would only work on night shows and I would wear a mask); all I needed was to come up with a name to perform under. One of the men who worked under Baba made the mistake of coming into my hotel room and accidentally opened the curtains which sent rays of light against my skin nearly starting me on fire and if you’ve ever been set on fire (and even if you haven’t), it’s not very enjoyable. My true nature was uncovered and I could have killed him but in making this discovery, he came up with the moniker I would use in the ring and could do so for as long as I chose to stay under a mask. It was at that moment I became known as Akuma (the meaning of the word devil in Japanese). It was a term I was familiar with from my first time being there with the Arashikage clan. Working for All Japan Pro Wrestling was a blessing as it allowed me to take out my frustrations on anyone who was foolish to get in my way and I was being paid like I was a king which is coincidental that in a previous life I had been the son of one. The foreign style of fighting is much tougher then in the states and anyone who has come to the land of the rising sun would be able to tell you the same. The punches and kicks are stiff but when you cannot feel pain you have an advantage. While I was there I also trained with several other types of martial artists to make myself a better fighter all around under the belief it would only help me in the future (which have paid dividends for me). I stayed under the hood (mask) for roughly fifteen years without anyone knowing the difference. It was an unspoken myth that there had been several different men to go under the name Akuma while wearing the mask but I could say for a fact that I was the only person to ever wear it. My role in AJPW was very mediocre and over the years I had grown tired of professional wrestling and wanted to go explore a career in music but the current genre in the 80’s was unappealing to me. MTV began to grow popular and that swayed me a little bit but it wasn’t until my brother called me from the States to encourage me to come back to consider putting together a musical group (band). The contract I held with AJPW was about to expire and decided to go back to catch up with the man who I grew up with but spent so many years apart from after I became a vampire; I was going to make sure we were inseparable. He was residing in California of all places. (Cali is one of the states with the most sun other then maybe Arizona and Florida and for a vampire the thought of it was insane but it was worth it for the night life.) I let it be known to my co-workers in AJPW that I wouldn’t be renewing my working agreement when it was over because family matters at home needed my tending to and that was all I needed to say and they said if I ever wanted to come back that I’d be welcome. Was I going to go back? I was not sure of anything at that time but I did want to work for Baba again if the opportunity came but sadly he passed away before I could ever come back to Japan for an extended period of time. I was ready for the next phase of my emergence into popular culture. **Placing his hands together, Eryc reflected for a moment on the information he was about to convey to those who would eventually view this "interview" if that is what you want to consider it as. An expose or documentary might be a little more appropriate for what this is.** When I arrived in California I knew nothing about what it would take to be a part of a band and had not even picked up an instrument other then say a violin several hundred years ago and the two are completely different in their use. If I had any say as to what part I was going to play it was going to be the vocalist because I knew that physically I slightly more attractive then my brother and I was more of the leader of the family when we were young. My proposal was not met with an objection as we began to study various instruments and with our advanced abilities were able to learn them by ourselves rather quickly but the fact remained that we did not want it to be us alone…we needed at least two more members. It would be dangerous to place an ad in a newspaper and have a mortal answer them because temptation would be too great to have one of our fellow band members for breakfast (or dinner whichever you prefer). Luckily there were a few places which we were able to ask around that brought us two other vampires who had not been around quite as long as we had been but it was more their talent we were after. That is how I met one of the many loves of my life, Emily. Emily was one of the few that showed up when she had heard the whispers about the group being formed and wanted to be involved. She was a rather classy women but her head has not always been in the right place which at times made me wonder if she was Malkavian (F.Y.I. those are the crazy vampires you don’t want to try and debate with.) I’d be almost certain that she was one of the founders of the gothic movement with the way she dressed and to this day one of the few vampires out there who have a gazillion piercings’ (I’m exaggerating but that’s because I can). Never the less I had encountered someone who I might have fallen in love with even though vampires technically are not emotional enough to have these feelings (however there is a debate that toreador’s remain the closest to possessing mortal emotion). I’m not prolong the story because basically we were nearly "married" in a mortal ceremony but she wandered off with a mortal and it basically ended our romantic relationship but we still remained business partners within the band. The name we chose for the band was Opaque Oblivion just because it seemed to fit who we were. We didn’t pretend to be vampires pretending to be vampires like you might see in an Anne Rice novel. This was something we wanted to be genuine and that’s exactly what it was. This was in a time where we had to compete with bands like Nirvana, Pearl Jam, Alice in Chains and all of the other grunge rock bands of the era but we happened to outlast quite a few of them before we finally split up several years ago. In that time we amassed several platinum albums and sold out venues all across the world, however, living the style of a musician was not easy and as you see in many publications or newscasts…someone is always doing something they shouldn’t. **What Eryc intends to say next is not the hardest thing he’s ever had to face but it is still one of those blemishes of his life that he could never repeat ever again. Fame and fortune was never his intention at the beginning it was thrust upon him and pressure builds leading into demons interacting with demons to create a catastrophe inside his shattered core.** I never expected to admit this but I was the a-typical rock star…partying all the time and all that one needed to do back then was find narcotics to give themselves a rush like no other and for a vampire drugs are no good when they are in their natural form…they have to be through the blood of someone who has ingested them. Naturally we fed on groupies who were addicts and junkies which in turn made us crave more for their blood which became a total mess. Several of us almost decided to take a walk into the sun to bake like a potato but luckily we were able to help each other out before we destroyed ourselves. It was also during this time I was contacted by an agency who knew about my homicidal past which would lead to them offering me a job as a contracted killer. The pay scale enticed me and they gave me an opt-out option if I decided I no longer wanted to. This worked out well because I could do the hits while I was not in the recording studio or out on the road (but if the contracts were on someone in a place I was going to be I took the job). I could never have been happier. Of course nothing lasts forever even for a creature of the night and it would only build up to where I wanted to explode and I knew that was not good for me or for those around me and so I severed my ties with the agency I did wet work for and took a hiatus from the band to pursue a new idea I had been drumming around in my head. I decided to become a wrestling promoter. At this time there was way too much going on in the US with wrestling so I decided to take my idea elsewhere and see how it would go and it lead me to Canada, a place where wrestling is almost put on a pedestal (and some of the stars from the country are as well). I garnered interest in my project and was able to get it off the ground in 1999. The promotion was called Independent Championship Wrestling (ICW) which I thought was clever as I envisioned it to be a place that was independent of what you would see regularly from other the mainstream organizations. The mistake I made was partnering up with another American named Johnny Veins…he turned out to be a selfish prick who tried to undermine me at every corner until everyone that was working for ICW became alienated and left. I had to close up production but I vowed that I would not give up the idea of becoming a successful promoter as my first mentor, Shohei Baba, did in Japan all those years ago. What I decided I was not going to do in the first place was where I should have started and going back to the United States and giving it a shot was my next intentions. My new dream was to become the American Wrestling Alliance (AWA) which I based out of Seattle, WA. This was where most of the band members were at the time and I thought it would be easier to handle things there then I would away from them just in case Opaque Oblivion decided to return to the music industry. The AWA was a breeding ground for up and coming stars; some of which are still active today that included: The Rossi Brothers, Noah Hanson, Slayer, and one name that is familiar to wrestling fans across the country is Shadow. Not many would be privy to this information but it just so happens that Shadow was actually my thought to be long dead wife, Lia Vlaszy. **For reason only known to him, it was evident that talking about Lia or Shadow as she is now known makes him rather uncomfortable but being a part of his past is not something that can be taken away even if he chose to. They were bound together but there is even more to the story then Eryc could possibly tell at this point as secrets of his past are still unraveling to this very day.** Our relationship was one of hate at first but eventually we began to become acquainted again but like all romances do it fizzled and faded and after an argument she left my employ (only to come back again some time later). We never really allowed ourselves the chance to talk about what happened until a year or so later (which I will detail shortly) and that made it difficult for us to move on either alone or together as it were. The itch to perform again so I stepped out from behind the scenes and used the name I had been given by Lady Bathory to my advantage to become one of the most vile and hated characters at the time: Corvus. As Corvus I managed to do great things with guys like Don Rossi, who was one of the few people I found worthy enough as an opponent and he defeated me to win his first and only World Championship with the company. It was his dedication I admired and would seek him out again in the future to do business with. For three years I was able to keep the AWA going but I was losing interest and everyone else involved was as well…which meant it was time again to move on. I didn’t feel like walking away from fighting because I enjoyed every detail of it and came to the conclusion that I didn’t have to be a successful promoter to kick someone’s ass in week in and week out. This is when I received an offer from Wrestling Midwest out of Cleveland. What I intend to say about WMW will be kept brief. My time there was tumultuous at best but I will say that the duration of time spent there except for at the end were some of my most enjoyable memories until recently (which I shall explain in further detail as well). Coincidentally I was involved in several contract hits while I was in Cleveland (one of them involving a pedophile teacher whom I gave an acid facial). My partner in crime was a man named Epitome, who teamed up with me in the ring now and then along with my brother who tagged along as his former alter ego, Wraith. Lia and I were reunited once again but we were on and off so I felt it was best that I move on and try to find someone else to occupy my time with. Enter Dawn McFarlane. I met her through a contest WMW was holding where participants were locked up in certain stores in a shopping mall and each week (like Survivor on television). Being locked up with someone of the opposite sex is a great way to get to know someone both emotionally and physically and it seemed that was the case between Dawn and I…she needed someone to protect her and I was the right person to do it. Dawn was having problems with a vampire who was obsessed with her (I recall that being the story anyway) and being that I knew how to handle creatures of that nature I handled it the best way I could: with physicality. Marcus (the vampire giving Dawn trouble) was not much of a fan of mine after I smacked him around a bit and sent him packing but he learned the lesson not to mess with Dawn again. What we had was good enough to be described as a December-May romance as it was basically doomed from the start. I should have figured that with her mental instability at times along with the viciousness of Corvus would not mix well. I had high hopes then that I would end up being able to stay with her for a long time but it wasn’t meant to be and after I parted ways with WMW we rarely spoke even when she came to work for the same company that I landed in. Global Wrestling (GW or now known as just Global) seemed to have high hopes for the character of Corvus and gave me a lot of perks that I didn’t ask for in my contact but who could argue it if they were throwing it at my feet? The efforts I was putting in to being an impact player were enjoyable until the proverbial rug came out from under my feet when I failed in defeating the World Champion (which was Salaok Davis at the time) in a match and from there all went downhill. **His bad attitude towards things in general was not doing him any favors with Global at the time; Eryc knows that this was not the end of this rollercoaster…it was actually the beginning. The supernatural element that surrounded Global started to creep its way towards him with the likes of other vampires such as Shadow and Leinad Soma but they were not as much as factor as the brother of the man named Sid, who was the brother of Global grapplers Jake Hix and the Texas Wrecking Crew.** I inadvertently became involved with Jake Hix when I was in the midst of tracking down vampires whom were holding information pertaining to my past. We ran into one another in the underground tunnels of one of the hotels on the Las Vegas strip where he was looking for a book that held information that would help him defeat the dark entity of Sid. In the meantime my sire Helena had once again graced her presence back into my life as a nightclub owner going under the name of Rhianna de Vorace. At first I was not sure of her intentions but after a while I realized that she wanted to make amends for abandoning me (and James Black as well). Her charms were too difficult to deny and we started spending more time together. It seems like a lot of the time that I’m floating between different women and that is true but I have learned that I am not the best at trying to maintain relationships (which is why they almost always fail). Several strange murders took place in Las Vegas which because of the character I portrayed in Global immediately gained the police department’s attention. I was also spotted at the scene as well which did not make a good case for me. I was even taken in for questioning and was followed around by this female detective named Van Buren. It took a little while but I finally was able to rid myself of her constant badgering and waiting for me to slip up. I have to admit that I was involved in one of the murders but it was completely by accident that I killed her (I even stopped drinking from live victims and had connections to blood banks) but I discovered later on she was a prostitute who didn’t have any family; it was unlikely that anyone would miss her. **Eryc exhaled hard as he places his face in his hands and massages lightly not wanting to have to cover the six to seven months of his life. The roads of life (or unlife as the case might be for him) were meant to be bumpy and he was completely aware of it but that didn’t change the fact that he would be placed in a situation he was unprepared for…the miracle of life.** Getting back to Jake Hix, I offered my hand to help him with his fight against Sid which was a lesser of two evils. I enjoyed the world the way it was despite its frailty and I would be damned if someone was going to mess it up for me. Just when we were making progress, Sid sent some of his cronies to New Orleans where we were investigating information and a brawl started to take place between a dozen of them (maybe more) against the two of us. We were doing well until one of those assholes stuck me with a syringe full of something…it rendered me unconscious pretty quick and fell off of a railing down into a garbage can below and did not wake up… until the next morning. My stomach was in agony as I made it to the bathroom and threw up all the blood that was in my system from the previous nights feeding and slowly I began to feel my heart beating, my lungs expanding and contracting, and the temperature of my skin increase to the point where I was actually warm again for the first time in a millennium. I was amazed not to mention shocked at the revelation but it would mean that I would have to make a lot of changes in my lifestyle habits and how I handled my profession as a fighter. Making a return to Global was not the best idea in the world because I was no longer the man I once was. Corvus was fast becoming a memory (but in time would make sure that I knew he would always be a part of me). My frustrations with coping with my new life did not mix well with the professional problems I was having with Global and their reluctance to place me in higher profile fights and needless to say I was ready to explode (and I did). What happened next was not a shock. I quit my job with Global and went into hiding from everyone (and I mean everyone). My brother, my friends, and my agent did not know where I was and I preferred it that way and figured the time I had to myself could be spent with figuring out what I was going to do with myself now that I was just an average ordinary guy. My time was spent holed up in a penthouse I bought in New York. I was learning to cope with emotions that were bombarding me after all the years where I didn’t feel any remorse over my actions and I needed to find a way to atone for them even if I knew that I would never make up for every single one. Good deeds go unpunished or so it has been said. I thought maybe if could try and fight the elements of darkness with the darkness that was within me then maybe the world would be a better place but the question was where I should start? The most corrupt place I could think of was Las Vegas and Global but I was definitely not ready to go back there and even if I was they would not likely take me back if I tried but even if they did I would need to be in top fighting shape. Preparation for me was a large factor because my body went through alterations to be a living, breathing, organism and I needed to treat it that way. It was a good thing that I remembered all that I had learned overseas on how to keep my mind focused through meditation and my body in great conditioning through martial arts combined with weight training and eating correctly. I no longer would smoke any kind of cigarettes (like Corvus did in the past). Impurities in the body would only hurt me and not allow me to live a full, long, life that I expect to unless unforeseen circumstances change my plans. **With the invisible war that is raging on between the vampires and also the one he tried to help Jake Hix with against his brother Sid the world was in peril and it was only a matter of time before one of the dark factions won and left the world decimated and broken. Guarantees of survival were slim for him because he was sitting in the middle of the fence. Once a vampire now a mortal but would it last? Even if Eryc wanted to stay how he is (and he does) there is no such assurance that he will not revert back to his old self.** Once I felt that I was physically and mentally ready to get back into the fighting arena I found myself a small company to test my skills out and did fairly well for myself and found something even more worth while then competing… I met a woman and after a short time of us being together we began to fall in love with one another. Kristine was a college student who was going to the University of Michigan studying to be a doctor of neurology and she was a vision of beauty with her auburn hair and emerald green eyes. Every inch of her had my senses reeling the night we met at the bar and I couldn’t leave that night without getting her number and of course I did before she returned back to commiserating with the friends she came with. I called her and we set up a date and even after the first one I could tell that it was going to further into something special with the way she treated me…like I was normal. She didn’t know anything about my past other then the things I told her (obviously all made up of course) and even pretended not to know who I (a musician/professional fighter) was just to not seem like she was trying to get something out of me. All of the pain and suffering I held inside went away when I was with her and I didn’t even think about who I had been or all of the evil deeds I had done. Redemption seemed possible for someone who didn’t deserve it. There are times though when you think everything is too good to be true and this was no different. I would learn that no matter how much you love someone…in the end they let you down. Krissy and I were enjoying every minute of our time together and I even agreed to go with her to Canada to spend the Christmas holiday with her family. It was a very awkward time but at the same time it was exciting for me because these were things I never did before. We ate an excellent dinner and exchanged gifts like a normal family would do. Little did I know that my happiness was going to start to crumble before my very eyes in the next few days; it all began before we even left to return back to Michigan. I was given a pair of ice skates as a gift because it was known that I was a very fanatic hockey fan (Krissy and I went to a Red Wings game a month or two before) and I decided to try them out because there was a huge lake out behind the cabin we were staying in. As I was skating I didn’t notice there was a thick tree branch that had blown out onto the ice and it was caught up in my skate and I took a dive. The impact must have been very hard or the ice was thin in the area I hit because I fell through the ice and was trapped under it. **Being in the frozen waters unable to breathe gave Eryc the shivers recalling how it went down; it was definitely something that he would not recommend to anyone nor would he prefer that it happened again.** I felt my life slipping before me and all of the things I had wondered about for the future were in my mind…you know the "what ifs" that people think about. What would it have been like if I would have married Krissy and we had a family and then when they grew up had a family of their own? Luckily for me Krissy’s father saw me go down into the water and rushed to rescue me and performed CPR on me when I could not breathe and was able to get my heart to begin beating again but I was still unconscious and fell into a small coma in which I was rushed to the hospital by helicopter. The battle for my life was definitely not over. While in the coma I was pulled into a world my mind created where the remnants of my past started to play themselves out in front of me and the tour guide was none other then Corvus, who manifested himself into a second personality after I became human again. This was not the first time we had encountered one another in a state such as this. I was having dreams for a month or so in which Corvus had managed to free himself as the dominant personality and showed me what the foreseeable future would hold. His intentions were to murder Krissy as the first thing he would do to torture me. He was also insistent that my relationship with her was not what it seemed and that she was hiding something from me but I was not about to believe anything he had to say because the lies he was capable of were ones I knew well. In the end I grabbed a torch and threatened to light him on fire and somehow that propelled me back into my normal reality and strangely I woke up a day later in the hospital; I was in better shape then the doctors could have ever imagined. Krissy had stayed by my side the entire time which proved to me that she was the one that I was going to spend the rest of my life and grow old together with. Spontaneously I proposed marriage to her and she accepted even though I did not have a ring, but I would have one sent overnight and would be there in the morning to give to her. Hospitals were not my cup of tea so I checked myself out as soon as possible and if there would be complications I would just see someone in the US if there was a problem because I did not want to run the risk of them finding something in my blood that related to my former vampirism. I slept in the rental car all the way back home because it was advised that we did not fly with the injuries I sustained from the accident. Krissy talked me into staying with her in Michigan even though I had never actually seen her apartment because we always spent time out or were at the hotel that I was renting weekly to come back and forth between Michigan (where I was wrestling) and New York (where I was working on new music). When we arrived I immediately went to sleep because of the medication I was taking and didn’t wake up again until the middle of the night when I needed a glass of water. I was walking back to the bedroom when I bumped the lamp table with Krissy’s purse on it and spilled the contents out onto the floor and quickly tried to put everything back before I woke her up but in the process discovered a shocking piece of information. Inside her purse was a wallet in which there was a badge and ID stating she was an agent with the F.B.I. It was apparent in my mind that she was lying to me the whole time about being a college student and used it as a cover to get close to me because I was being investigated still for the murders that took place in Las Vegas. I put everything the way it was and staggered to the couch and passed out until the next day and woke up to find that Krissy was gone supposedly running an errand to get books for her classes. While she was gone I checked my phone messages from the last few weeks and one of them came from the Global management office stating they were interested in giving me another chance and if I was interested I should call them back. Having been lied to by someone I thought I could trust gave me the motivation to grow the grapefruits to own up to my past actions and call Global to work out an agreement. I called and talked to Brandi Sights, who was the second in command to Bubba McCoy (the owner of Global) and asked me if I could come to Japan where they were holding a PPV to make arrangements for me to return to action. For me there was no other choice and I told her that I would be there to meet with Bubba and after the conversation ended I quickly packed up my bags and called a cab to come and pick me up to take me to the airport. As I was ready to walk out the door Krissy came home and I told her that I knew she was lying to me about who she was and this resulted in a small argument with me ending the engagement and walking out the door to try and secure a new future for myself. 2006 was already starting out with a bang it seemed. **Even though he was still not supposed to fly, Eryc disobeyed the doctor’s orders in order to go to Japan to meet with Global’s management. His intentions were to make sure that he cleaned up the rest of the bad elements that were tarnishing the company and also to find answers from the one person who could give them to him: Jake Hix.** While I was waiting in the hallway to be called in to talk with Bubba McCoy I was surprised to see my brother stepping out of the makeshift office before I was called in. Management explained to me that the best way to ease me back into things was for me to team with Ash and was the condition of me being allowed to return; it didn’t leave me with much of a choice but to agree since it was my only way to find out some things I was interested in knowing, I had to accept. Ash and I had not spoken to one another since I disappeared despite his best attempts at trying to communicate with me but I couldn’t see him and I getting along with no longer being a vampire; I was potentially the enemy to him or any of the others who still walked the night with all that I knew about them. We did our best to get along and named our new team Projekt Mayhem but even a shot at the tag titles would not be enough for us to get past our differences which prompted Ash to ask Global to opt him out of his contract. What did that mean for me? I was there on the condition that I teamed with my brother and nothing else. The answer was that because I was showing improvement not only with the skills I had but also how I conducted myself around the company that I would be given a new singles contract and would see that in time if I continued on the same pace that I could even be one of the top stars. This was not something I was concerned with more then I was finding out if the stuff I was injected with was permanent or not and why it was done and in order to do that I would need to get in touch with Jake Hix. At the time I was in no position to interact with the World Champion as I was not anywhere near his level so I had to come up with a plan and I did…climb the Global ladder and get him to realize that I was there and that I was not going to go away. It has taken me several months and in that time I have won the Platinum Championship and the International Championship but still Jake Hix and his brothers (who make up The Syndicate) are giving me a rough time trying to get answers and have attacked me when I press the issue. They are hiding something and I would certainly like to know what it is; I guess I will just have to hit them right where they live and take more and more of their spotlight away until they concede what I want to know. I have found myself in the company of a man who has had a dark past just like mine and we are bound together by the darkness it seems (even though it was discovered that I murdered his bastard old man when I was still a vampire). The two of us have vowed to continue the mission I started and change the course of Global by taking down the Hix family and making not only Global safe from evil but also the entire world…even if we have to do it one small step at a time. As good as it all sounds there’s definitely more to this then meets the eye as it seems someone out there is trying to get my attention. James has found his way back into my life around the same time that I learned someone murdered my maker who came to me in a dream to try and encourage me to stay the course that I have been going even if a few bumps in the road might try to stray from it. I have since made amends with Krissy who has faithfully returned to my side as we worked things out and renewed our engagement but I grow wary that with all of the things going on that her safety is questionable and I will do what I must to protect her especially now that I’ve come across someone with information on my past that wants to blackmail me to prevent what they know from reaching her. This is something I cannot allow. I have every reason to believe that the person who killed Rhianna has something to do with blackmailing me and I intend to get to the bottom of it even if I have to do it alone. **Eryc looks at the recording time and realizes he only has a little bit left before it cuts off and he has covered all of what he feels has been important in his life.** Now that you have a better idea of who I am you may understand in the future that I did what I needed to do to save the world from creatures you never knew existed. It is a long shot that this video will ever see the light of day with the media being heavily controlled by these vampires and those they have power over but I have a feeling that this will get leaked somehow, someday…and if it does I will most likely be dead as I said at the beginning. In summary I believe what you have seen will tell you that despite all the evils in the world and no matter how hard you feel you have fallen…there is always a way to overcome any odds. Heroes are still out there…you just have to believe. **Eryc stops the video camera and stands up from his chair to walk over to it to lock it up so that no one can get their hands on it until the time is ready and while the world is not yet prepared for what is going to happen, they may know sooner then later what is happening whether he has anything to do with it or not.** - - - - Eryc stepped outside of his office after recording his biography and saw that Krissy was already packed to go with him to where the PPV for PTC was going to be held. He was very surprised at her eagerness to travel with him as her interest in his fighting was usually non-existent. "Is everything alright dear?" He nodded as saw that she put most of his belongings together for him as well except for a couple things she didn’t know that he needed to have with him. "I’m good." Eryc said with a smile. It was a long shot that this Extreme Title Division match would go in his favor but it would at least get him noticed by people outside of Global. Vivica Valentine was a person he was familiar with being that she was an associate of a former Global personality, Christian Zenith, who was someone that Eryc didn’t personally care much for (for reasons of his own) and it would be good for him to show well against her to send a message out to Zenith who would surely be watching. Krissy approaches him and places her arms around him, keeping him closer to her. "I know you’ll do well; you have the right motivation to do whatever you want with this." Her belief in him was a sure sign that he’d make a competition out of this; it was his destiny to be the guy to stop Archibald McGreggor, a PTC outsider from keeping the belt…it was the reason the PPV was even being held. "You know…you just might be right." He squeezed her in his arms as he looked off out into the NYC skyline as he peered out his penthouse window. "is you…"
