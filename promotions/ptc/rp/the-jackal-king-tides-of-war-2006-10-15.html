The Jackal King: Tides of War

15 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-15 Prologue. Saturated by filth, that was the way Jesse felt these past few days. Seeming as if the world around him had been uplifted from it’s normal, and dropped off somewhere far, far, off in another galaxy. Though, in reality, it was just the tide of the winds in which swayed his thoughts. In recent weeks, Jesse had been tackled by the confusion of his own mind, being pulled into an abyss of unnatural perplexities. It was an obstacle to carry on day-to-day tasks, even the simplest. Words flowed through his unconsciousness, throwing him off routines and dazing his focus. Questions began to arise, even though Jesse knew the name in which they generated from: Anubis, he did not take kind actions in letting the voice in his head rule him. Searching through libraries, online, and in books he borrowed from fellow wrestlers, of history and different cultures, Jesse found a source. The name Anubis was that of an ancient Egyptian ruler, one who ruled Amenti, the underworld. Anubis’ train of thought flowed at night the deepest, sometimes spoken out-loud as Jesse dreamt. Catching some of the words as he slipped in and out of REM states, Jesse pieced it together after not to long, and started to record Anubis’ thoughts. Anubis had entered Jesse through weakness. From years of heartache, distraught, and loneliness, a void in Jesse was left open, and became the gate for that of Anubis to come. Through the thoughts Jesse recorded, a tale of Anubis’ life was revealed, outlining his childhood, adolescents, and the span of time in which he became known as Anubis. Now though, Anubis was to return to life, and wanted to find the person that murdered him millions of years ago. Seeking a warrior, one who could be tested only by battle, Anubis forced Jesse to join the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, and forced him in thought to become the vessel of his unmerciful onslaught until he found that certain one. Of course, the person who did it would not be so foolish to just say it, for they were controlled just as Jesse is, through the mind. Their would be a collision of unnatural power, something of ancient strength, that will bring both souls to the squared circle, only to have them battle out their hatred until one ceased to go on, giving in, and then giving up to the other’s strength. Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Edition Six, was and is the only place where Anubis could figure a warrior would travel to summon the strength of his fallen. Anubis would not let this go unnoticed, and so, here he stands, in the entity of Jesse Jamester’s body, waiting to dismantle the one who has caused him a lifetime of suffering as he deals with the dead. And so, it begins… Part One. Journey of the Hunter Sun set dimly on the counter top in the apartment of Jesse Jamester’s. Blazing down in an orange spectacle of arrays, it reached out vividly and caught the eyes of all resulting in a short blindness. Due to this, squinting occurred as Jesse sat at the counter top, reading the newspaper, his eyes hurt, blazing red from smoke that came from the cigarette in his right hand. Surrendering the paper to the counter, Jesse closes his eyes, the navy blue disappears behind his eyelids, a pale complexion washed over the warrior, making it easy to identify him. Thinking of his life, he concerns himself little of the thoughts that Anubis thinks, focusing on the task at hand, the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. However much he thinks of it, he cannot let down his previous accomplishments, his winning the Global Title back in March of 2004, almost two years ago. His dreams of being the best had come true, yet fled just as quickly. For Jesse, it was not a matter of just being called the best, but proving it, to know you were by defending the title with honor, pride, and dignity. A noble cause, yes, but one that came with consequence. Attention. Pressure. Stress. Doubt. All lingering thoughts that took coarse in his mind when he was champion. It proved too much for him, a feat he could not overcome, and so, he fell victim to Hessian a month later after defending the title. Till this day, that thought roams his mind, and he itches for a chance to come back and win the strap that he once called his. Easier said than done, yet the task at hand would prove a challenge that he was willing to face, with failure not being an option. Much had changed over the coarse of two years, the warrior within him had grown, and he was more sensible to knowing the pressures he would encompass. The growing tension would subside after each round, but it’d come back as soon as he knew his next opponent, only to repeat in sequence until his fate of winning or losing was determined. Last time was labeled his fifteen minutes of fame, however, it felt more like five… this time, it’d be different, and the name in which he carried with pride, would once again stand at the pinnacle of professional wrestling as the best. "Just watch," mutters Jesse unsuspectingly with his eyes closed. Part Two. Engulfed with dreams BURRRRRRRRP! The unruly sound of male testosterone fills the air, with a stench of beer on its breath. Feeling the surrounding, a taste of the sweet winter night is felt as Jesse and his cousin Jakob sit on the back balcony of his apartment, staring up at the stars and crescent moon. Dressed depressingly, sporting black jeans and a shirt, hair pulled back in a ponytail, a beer in hand, Jesse smiles as he laughs at the joke Jakob just said. "Hahaha, always a prankster! Wow, can’t believe you did that… that’s crazy, and she just walked away?" replies Jesse to Jakob. "Yeah, she just walked away!" laughs Jakob hoarsely. His large proportion sat uncomfortably in the chair, but he was taken away from this unease with a beer, or multiple beers from the look of the pile he has next to him. Stuttering with his next words, Jesse grows impatient, and finally blurts them out, "Do you believe in fate?" Taken back by the question Jakob just glares down, and then looks at Jesse, "Yeah, I do." A brief pause surrounds the two. Beginning to feel uncomfortable, Jesse goes to say something, but is cut off with an extended response from Jakob. "I figure it like this. We are put here, placed on earth, and we live our lives. Through the coarse of our life, we accomplish many things and finally we find our true purpose for being here. That though, comes after a long time of searching. That to me is our fate…" Smiling, Jakob raises his beer to Jesse, "To the things that will come, and for the memories we’ll have, let’s hope to live… for as long as we are to, and enjoy every second of it!" They toast, and then down the beer, releasing the pressure of serious talk and bullshitting about their everyday lives. Happy to be with his cousin, Jesse was beginning to feel that maybe, the tournament, was his way of finding his fate… just maybe. Part Three. Gathering hope Sitting on his floor doing crunches, Jesse counts. Forty-five… Forty-six… Forty-seven… Forty-eight… Forty-nine… FIFTY. A huff subsides, and he sits up, staring at the floor with sweat running down his face. He looks at the clock above him on the wall, noticing the time: 11:39 PM, Friday Night. He cracks his neck, and rolls over on his thigh, placing his knee down, and standing up. From the doorway, a note falls through the mail hole. Looking at it questionably, Jesse walks over, and notices that it has no return address. Opening the left corner from the back, he peels away at the edge of the envelope, and opens it up. Inside, is a letter, folded three times to the middle. Unfolding, Jesse begins to read, and his eyes open up wide. Dear Jesse, This has been a long time coming. I have been with you from the time you started your training as a warrior, and I have seen you grow. In the past four years, we have been distant, as the fall of Lights Out Wrestling had done such. However, I am writing to you now, to tell you something I feel you need to hear, before continuing to reach for your dreams as you once did in my squared circle. Everything that is ahead of you, all the paths that you shall walk, will take time and effort. Do not give up on them, and do not fear their results. Take on each with an open mind and the thought of succeeding. When this is done, you will be where you want to be… at the top. Take care, Trigger P.S. I don’t like to wish good luck, it’s like a curse I feel, so best wishes. With that, Jesse slumps on the couch, and all doubt, pressure, and thoughts that lingered fall from mind, leaving only the task at hand: The Tournament. "Believe… believe… I will be your guidance, just be patient and let me control," whispers Anubis
