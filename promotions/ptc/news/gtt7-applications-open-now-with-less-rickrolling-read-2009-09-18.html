GTT7 Applications Open! (now with less Rickrolling) **Read**

18 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://gtt7.primetimecentral.net/application.php Notes Fill out the form and hit submit. Easy, right? Almost.... 1. Everyone wanting to enter must use the application form. This includes holders of guaranteed spots and seeds. No exceptions. 2. When asked for your forum name, use your regular forum name, not some incognito one you signed up for to hide who you are. I take this seriously. Applications may go missing. You have been warned. 3. When you enter your forum name, you must also select the Lookup Name button and pick your name from the list, even if you have typed it correctly. 4. After you have submitted, the next page will give you your login name and remind you of your password. Write these down or save them in a text file o something. You will need them to get into the tournament site to post your RP's. When the site goes live, you will be asked to test them. Do it then and there, because "My login didn't work" will not be accepted as a valid excuse for a late post (we have computer clock enforced strict deadlines for these events - one nanosecond late past the deadline and you = fail). 5. No email validation this time around. (the tradeoff for that is no ability to change your own password if you lose it). 6. If you submit twice or want to change your application, send me a PM. I don't have much spare time to be changing stuff around, so please consider carefully before you hit submit. 7. Any problems with the form, send me a PM or reply here. Other Info Apps will be open for approximately 2 weeks, although this may be reduced to 1 week if we hit the maximum number of applicants that we are prepared to work with sooner rather than later. We aren't telling you what that number is, so don't ask. In short, don't screw around if you do intend to apply. Apps will be accepted in this order: 1. Guaranteed spots. 2. People who donated charity prize money. 3. Seeds. 4. Order of application (first in best dressed if you don't have a seeded position, didn't donate or win a guaranteed spot) 5. Other. 6. Pets of Other. 7. Lice/nits/fleas on the pets of Other. 8. People who haven't filled out the Getting to Know You thread. 9. People who remain banned from PTC. *List subject to editing if omissions are noticed. I can live with people donating money getting a higher priority if there aren't enough spots to go around, as the intention here is clearly to do something good, not to sell anything for a profit. If you have a problem with that, please deal with it quietly in your own home. Its not something that needs to be over-analyzed, or even discussed in any detail at all. The starting field will be announced by Pete, at a time and in a format of Pete's choosing. Pete may at his discretion add any variables he wishes (e.g. theme words, hype rounds.. whatever). Should be fun! If you don't want to enter GTT7 but would like to help judge, please contact Pete via PM and offer your services. The more judges, the faster each round will go. Please volunteer, as we're too busy judging to chase other people to judge. You get the idea. Thanks for reading!
