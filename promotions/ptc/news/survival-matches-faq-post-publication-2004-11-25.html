Survival matches FAQ - post publication

25 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Q. Where is the final match. A. It doesn't exist yet. Q. What? Why not? A. Anyone want to help me finish judging it? 30 odd RP's for you to read and rank by fed. Some of the best qualified judges who come around PTC have turned that particular privalege down, when Survival was still a hot potato (as opposed to being stone cold as it is now). Q. Okay, I don't personally want to do that because nobody cares anymore and I actually want to play Halo 2, but I'm in PRIME/XUW/4WW/ex Neon, and I RP'd, so I want to know who won overall, so how long is it going to take from now? A. Beats me. It may never happen. I can categorically say it won't happen before 2005 unless someone wants to step in and do it, as Pete and I can't. Q. That's pretty crappy. A. *stifles a laugh* Yes, yes indeed it is. Q. So, I read the matches, and none of my moves were used and in my opinion character was treated pretty shabbily. A. Welcome to the answer as to why the matches didn't go up two months ago when I had them. I had hoped to have them audited against people's bios and what they are supposed to be doing, and edited as necessary. Unfortunately that never came to pass, so you have them as is, submitted by the author, without any editing whatsoever. The original intention was to do that, have transitions, and have the final, all on time. It didn't happen. I've already explained why in other threads. Q. Okay, I'm over it. I must say though that you guys are unreliable, disrespectful assholes and I wish I'd never spent the time RP'ing for your stupid event. A. Point taken. Q. I'm still reading this and haven't broken out in fits of laughter. I want to RP against people, and e-fedding is pretty much dead elsewhere except for that Fwrestling.com place and maybe WFWA, and I don't like them. If I forgive you for this massive fuckup and take GTT4 not also collapsing in a heap as a sign of your good intentions, What can you give me to do? A. For now, the divisions will be going into rebuild phase, and that's it. There are drawing board level plans for future events, however this one still stings like hell so there's no screaming hurry to repeat it, lateness and all. Argyle: Q. Okay, I'm over it. I must say though that you guys are unreliable, disrespectful assholes and I wish I'd never spent the time RP'ing for your stupid event. A. Point taken. Unreliable? Sure. Disrespectful? Possible. Assholes? Definately. By the way, if you're taking this FAQ as anything more than informational, and a bit lighthearted, don't respond. At all. You will earn yourself a vacation. Survival is shit. We all know it. Don't beat the dead horse. Please. Posted on the PTC forum by Argyle and Pete.
