BRACKET 1 Match 1 Seymour Almasy (GW) v Lorenzo Guevera (NGW) v Matt Jackson (RPW) Match 2 Blaise "Hazard" McClancy (FA) v Adam Syxx (FA) v Pietro Geist (FA) Match 3 Primetime (WMW) v The Fallen Fae (XUW) v Christian Crawford (RPW) Match 4 Horazon (FAW) v Larry The Legionnaire (XUW) BRACKET 2 Match 5 Carys (RPW) v Fury (WMW) v Armaan (WWA) Match 6 Jay Phoenix (4WW) v Max Roswell (GW) Match 7 Syko Arsonist (FA) v Angel (FA) v Alex Caine (FA) Match 8 Robb Hardy (NGW) v Justin Walker (WWA) v The Fool Ian Fetzer (XUW) BRACKET 3 Match 9 Jason Starr (NGW) v The Cockjockey (XUW) Match 10 Coricidon (4WW) v James Edwards (RPW) v Raijin (FA) Match 11 Gordon "Denrol" Korth (WWA) v The Blue Coral (4WW) v Thai Trang (UWF) Match 12 Ethan Knight (RPW) v Rocky Adams (WWA) BRACKET 4 Match 13 Smokey Wead (UWF) v Kirk Urban (WWA) Match 14 Emilio Dervante (LOW) v Marcel Celestini (FA) v Jimmy Diablo (FA) Match 15 Karina Wolfenden (RPW) v Limp (UPWA) Match 16 Tessa Windsor (RPW) v Dawn McFarlaine (WMW) v Robert Theodore Temporary aka Bob The Temp (XUW) For those that are interested, this was the selection method: 1. Randomise the Free Agents using Microsoft Excel and put them in triple threat matches. 2. Randomise players who are members of member feds using Microsoft Excel. Do some minor re-shuffling to make fed representation as even as possible across brackets. So, there are no seeds, or favourites, but some people lucked in with not getting a triple threat match, purely by chance. Good luck all :) CW1 brackets
