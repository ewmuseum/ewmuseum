Round 2 Bracket 2 Results

20 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Match 3 Carys (RPW) d Jay Phoenix (4WW) I don't know what happened with Jay's first RP, but he didn't manage to make it back and post it or the second one :( Match 4 Syko Arsonist (FA) v The Fool Ian Fetzer (XUW) Judge 1 The Fool, RP 1 Good RP on the whole, though pretty surprising to me since it was my understanding that “The Fool” Ian Feltzer was a comedic character, and this was anything but. Even without knowing too much about the Fool character, it was easy to see his emotion and really feel for the character. I’d be lying if I said I wasn’t a little disappointed at the style, simply because I was looking forward to reading one of the Fool’s famed hilarious RPs, but it was still a very good piece of writing and made me care about this character, something that most first RPs I read from someone do not do. Pros: Good emotional depth for the character, and I think that the quest to regain past glory is something that most of us can relate to, in some way or another. Cons: A little too “thinky” for the first half of it; it’s just the Fool’s thoughts for a little too long of a stretch of time. But I dunno, maybe I just have ADD. Syko, RP 1 I liked this one a little more than the Fool’s first. It just had a more interesting story, I guess. At first when I thought Jacob really had lost his fingers in a grinder accident, I thought that was a really odd thing to have happen and treat it so casually, but then the flashback was good stuff. It’s rare for a RPer to create sympathy for a character on bad terms with his own wrestler, but Syko does it and does it nicely. Pros: Very entertaining, kept me interested in the plot throughout more so than the Fool’s did. Cons: I’m not all too sure that I like the situation that Jacob will be further hurt or possibly killed if Syko doesn’t win his match. It almost seems like putting pressure on the judges to have Syko win unless we want this guy to die. I’m sure that wasn’t Syko’s intention at all, but it still kinda feels like if I pick against him, I’ll be killing off this character, which I don’t like. Also, just a little thing that bothered me: How exactly is a wall “apathetic”? Well, technically I guess all walls would be apathetic, but it still doesn’t make any sense to call them that. The Fool, RP 2 This one was definitely a lot more like the type of style that I was expecting from the Fool. It was funny and entertaining throughout, and had the Fool up to the wacky antics that I assume are a lot more typical for him. But I really don’t know about the ending. So is the Fool going to be going into the tournament match thinking that he’s Syko Arsonist? How will he wrestle as somebody that he’s not familiar with? This RP just leaves too many unanswered questions. Pros: Funny, and the hypnotist was played off to a tee. Cons: The ending. There just seems no possible way the Fool could win in his state of mind, and that definitely hurts him from a judging standpoint. Syko, RP 2 I don’t really know why Syko is spending so much time on this Jack guy, and I don’t especially like it. What’s the point of all this? At first it seemed to be it was going to prove that the people in the tournament are really good, but we already knew that. Then with him getting shot, it just didn’t seem to have a lot of purpose. Maybe if it was someone close to Syko, or just if Jack had been somewhat likeable; but Syko seemed to hardly know him, plus the guy was a complete jackass, so him getting shot really doesn’t mean much to me, and doesn’t seem like it would mean all that much to Syko. It just seems there would be better ways for the mob people, or whoever they are, to send a message to him. The part with Syko was good for the most part, though. Pro: I like the line ripped off from Christopher Walken in Joe Dirt.  Cons: I’ve already said how I don’t like what Syko’s doing with Jack. Also, this is a fairly minor detail, but the car crash part wasn’t too realistic. Having just recently been in a crash very similar to the one described in this scene, I know the damage is a lot more serious than that if the car that hit him was going at enough speed to spin him 180 degrees. A LOT more, and Syko would have been slammed into his airbag with enough force to daze him for at least a few moments. I know that’s really not that important, but it still bothered me to see an accident trivialized like that. My precious car is a wreck because basically the same thing happened to me, although of course my case was an accident. Final Decision: This one simply came down to who has the advantage going into the match. Syko now has motivation to win the match, whereas the Fool thinks that he’s Syko … which I guess would give him just as much motivation, but would really hamper him overall. I pick Syko. Judge 2 Originality takes it. Syko's first roleplay, where he gets "Found out" was one of the most original stories I've read in e-w. They were a pleasure to read. Nothing wrong with Fool's, just didn't have the edge that Syko did. Judge 3 Syko has a really mechanical style as evident by the dialogue between characters, his characterization of the teacher/student relationship was nothing too original, but the back story of the "mob" involvement adds to his storyline heading towards the match. However I was left bewildered on whether he wants to be a face or not, I'm guessing he is a face to play up to being the savior of his gym. Could have done a better job on detailing some of the characters in the roleplays to emphasize their importance... "20 year old blond" I know she may seem like a throw away character but it doesn't really allow the reader to get into the roleplay from the onset and loses their attention. As for the fool, the dialogue worked well between the characters but in his first roleplay he slowed down the roleplay with a lot of needless detail about the beach and the symbolism between it and his character. As well it too some time establishing himself as the underdog face in his first roleplay, he really needs to work on the pacing but the characters in his roleplay were enjoyable. The Fool did a better job of capturing his character and keeping my attention for his roleplays, though his mechanics could still use a little work. The Fool def. Syko Arsonist Winner of Match 4 Syko Arsonist defeats The Fool, 2 votes to 1 CW1 brackets
