Round 3 Results - Kanga

23 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE PHANTOM REPUBLICAN V ANGELICA DOMINIQUE Judge 1 ‘The Fallen Angel’ Angelica Dominique Arrested Development This roleplay was a good effort, but for me anyway the technical word for it is ‘meh’. When I was reading through it, I couldn’t help but drift off a few times and get a little bit bored. The writing is good and the descriptive paragraphs are sound, but it’s the format that I didn’t like. For most of the RP, it was just two people talking to each other, which can get fairly boring easily. As well as this, I hadn’t read your stuff before and I had no idea reading the story what was going on, apart from a couple of mentions of kidnap, and how somebody was being accused of it or something. But it was a good story once I understood it. The Phantom Republican RP #1: The Politics Of Family When comparing with Dominique’s, it was a little bit, if at all, better than his. Again the writing and description were sound, good enough, but the story didn’t particularly grab me, and there was barely a story to it. Even though I’m sure it wasn’t the case, it was one of those RPs that had a hint of “this was only written because it had to be” about it. Again just a personal argument, I’ve never been a fan of interview RPs that are just speech and descriptions of the person’s reactions between speaking. However, the end part was written well. RP #2: GOP, The College Years A much better RP, a good read and a vast improvement on the other one. This was written in a story style, which is my personal preference, and the writing throughout was excellent. I did like Gordon’s opposing emotions, having him stuck between euphoria, pity and guilt. If you do more RPs like this one in the tournament, you could definitely get through the next round and even further in my opinion. Pick: The Phantom Republican Judge 2 Solid match up. Dominique had a fairly intriguing story that I think could turn into something very well done but it's clear by the end of the one promo that is posted that it's being rushed which unfortunately hurts the overall of the piece. Still a good, solid, read that I enjoyed. TPR was pretty solid throughout both of his pieces. A little choppy here and there but nothing to bad and I really enjoyed them both. They had me interested and were quite entertaining. A good job. Dominique quite possibly could have pulled this one out if not for the first one seemingly rushed and had the writer had time to put into a second piece. Pretty good showing, none the less, and congrats for making it as far as you did. In this case though I've got to go with TPR for the win. Pick: The Phantom Republican Judge 3 Angelica Dominique Roleplay 1 Pros - Good ambience. There's good writing here. Unfortunately, I can't get into the piece. Cons - Extremely difficult to get into. At this stage, you've got to make sure you bring the readers in with the rest of the stories you're telling. I felt a little lost. - Not a technically sound piece. - Paragraphs are extremely blocky in the second half of the piece. Phantom Republican Roleplay 1 Pros - Technically, good. At this stage, you just have to make sure you continuall put out pieces like that because it starts to become lazy if you don't. - Good story. Loved the ending with Gordon's mother. Stuff like that is the essence of your pieces. Show that a little bit more. Cons - Break up the dialouge a little more. Easier to read at the end then the beginning. - The political talk, while interesting to me, could bore other readers. So, I would keep it interesting and short. Roleplay 2 Pros - Great story. I was hooked the entire time. - The protest were great. - Great dialouge and great ending. Cons - Last piece I found no erros and this time I found several errors. Need to watch those. Pick: The Phantom Republican Result - The Phantom Republican d Angelica Dominique, 3 votes to 0 KIMBUSA V THE SENTINEL Judge 1 Sentinel 1 I think proof reading is always good, but especially on your opening line. A simple error such as 'life' and 'live' on the first line almost feels like it'll set the tone (note - after reading the whole thing it's almost the only error, which is amusing and sad). Okay... an interesting scene to open, though I'm very quickly confused about what the hell is going on. What was the vial for? In terms of procreation I mean... IVF is about extracting eggs and sperm and fusing them in a lab environment... what's this vial full of liquid to do? Sounds like a frikkin' potion... Whilst revealing the time gap in the segment with Cassidy and Karla fills in how old the kid would be, I'd have preferred to know during the actual scene. Until she swore I thought the child was very young. Okay, the rest flew by, in a good way. Very good writing and an enjoyable plot. I'm interested in the Second Vita project and the plants, and their relevance to what is going to happen... but I have a few more questions. What's in the box? What did really happen between Wolfgang's wife and Karla? How old is Wolfgang? Why must it remain secret at all costs that he is The Sentinel? Hopefully some of these get answered next roleplay. It's a good sign that I do want to know. Kimbusa Good writing style, and a gift for description; giving details without it seeming unnatural or boring. Really enjoyable style of writing. I'm a newcomer to Kimbusa, and he's reintroduced very well and with care. There's definite depth to him. I read to the end without ever having trouble or needing to stop and go over things again. In fact, in pure writing terms it's probably the best I've read thus far in GTT (though by no means have I read all, or even most, of the Rps). It's also a great character development piece. It hints at the past and gives just enough for you to fill in the gaps. But? Yeah, there's always one of them. There's not enough storyline going on for my liking. It really is a lovely piece, but I would have preferred something to actually be happening beyond Kimbusa becoming assured in his own being. That said, I still really enjoyed reading it. Sentinel 2 A very interesting roleplay. I ran through it pretty quickly, and am interested in the story. Very few of my questions from the first one were answered, and I still think that some of them are oversights rather than suspense and mystery, but on the whole I didn't mind, as it went further into the 'darkness' associated with Karla. She's an interesting character, and a good evil one. I do so like a good evil character, and she fits the bill perfectly. Torturing kids and killing them? Brilliant! Now that's how to put someone over as evil! If I was to have any criticisms regarding this they would be as follows. Firstly, I think the storyline has a few flaws. Some are probably because I didn't read the first few 'chapters' of this, but that's the job of the writer to fill in for me. Some are probably due to oversights, but they are fairly few and far between, so I can suspend my disbelief and roll with it. The second problem is that, really, this is mostly not about The Sentinel... and I know nothing about why this character of his is so important, and I definitely did want to know. I liked the ending with the two little paragraphs; one showing Wolfgang and the other his daughter. I liked how it showed him prioritising his new life, in a way, over his daughter. I just wasn't given a reason as to why this Sentinel lark is so important to him. Those flaws aside though, a couple of very, very entertaining pieces. Summary: Despite the 2:1 ratio, this was a close choice. Not a tough one perhaps, but one that deserves explanation. Kimbusa's writing is definitely the better of the two, no doubt. His characterisation was great, and it flowed well. Sentinel's story was definitely the better of the two, and the more entertaining. I just feel that Sentinel's story overcomes the writing prowess of Kimbusa's handler. Had there been a second Kimbusa roleplay which specifically moved on an intriguing plot then he would have taken it. As it was, the character development of the sumo giant was not enough on its own to overcome two very entertaining and compelling pieces from Sentinel. Both did an excellent job and the one who progresses will be a tough act to knock out. Pick: Sentinel If I was to offer one piece of advice for Sentinel, it would be to have a greater degree of involvement in what the whole 'Sentinel' character is about and its importance to Wolfgang. Other than that... keep up the good work! Judge 2 In parts of the Sentinel's RPs, the narrative was choppy, disjointed and relied too heavily on repeating itself. Kimbusa's was structured much better and, overall, told the better story. Pick: Kimbusa. Judge 3 Basically, we're looking at some motivation issues. Not much more needs to be said about that. Sentinel pretty clearly offered us more to take interest in with his story, putting him far enough ahead. Pick: Sentinel Result The Sentinel d Kimbusa, 2 votes to 1 SUMMARY - The Phantom Republican d Angelica Dominique, 3 votes to 0 - The Sentinel d Kimbusa, 2 votes to 1 BRACKET FINAL - The Phantom Republican v The Sentinel BRACKET JUDGES Dean Craig Vista Ben Lindz Andy Kanga
