Job vacancy: Assistant to the Tournament Director

28 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm not in any way naive enough to think that I can do all of this on my own, as I also have a lot of work to do to help towards finishing out Survival, as well as real life committments. I need help with some of the menial things, like collecting and uploading all the headshots for the website, that sort of thing. So, you need to be tech savvy - i.e. can handle an FTP client, copying and saving images and that sort of thing, WITHOUT me having to train you how to do it. The person who wants this job will be privvy to the applications before the brackets are released, so I want someone trustworthy - that cannot be stressed enough, if you blab stuff, you'll ruin things that I don't want ruined. Oh, and you can't be a player in the tournament. If you're interested in helping out towards making GTT4 a success, drop me a line via PM. Thanks.
