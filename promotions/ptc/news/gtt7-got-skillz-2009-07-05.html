GTT7 - got skillz?

5 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For those of you who don't know, GTT is PTC's premier event, making a comeback in September after a 3 year hiatus. It usually has its own website and ideally should have some cool bells and whistles :) Do you have a skill that you are willing and able to contribute to the production of GTT7? Are you able to donate some time over the next 6-8 weeks? We are looking for help with some multimedia specialist things like videos, graphics, music and the like. If you have something up your sleeve that you think might be useful and you'd like to be in on making GTT7 happen, please contact me or Pete via PM with what you can do. Disclaimers: - Confidentiality please. Blabbing spoils these things. - We are not looking for offers without one or more skills required to produce an event. "I'll help, what do you want me to do?" isn't what we are after here. - Offers from people with good intentions but actually no time to spare are not helpful. We have previously been kept hanging on until way past the last minute. It blows deadlines and impacts on everyone involved - so thanks, but not helpful, please be realistic and don't commit to anything you can't do, on time. - This isn't a call for judges. That will come in good time. If you have non-specific questions, ask here. If you have specific questions that would give away any potential goodies, please ask them in private via PM.
