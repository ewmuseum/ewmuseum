GTT Website are go! (some things you need to read)

2 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://gtt7.primetimecentral.net The site itself focuses less on "glitzy" presentation and more on function - i.e. everything is there, works correctly and you can find things quickly. Its actually the first time I've used a canned template to put things in from out of the backstage script. For those of you with mobile devices, the mobile script I wrote for PRIME's site has been adapted to this site. I've tweaked the font size CSS so that Iphone users can actually read the text, but haven't been able to try it out on other devices yet. If it is unusable, let me know :) You must do this - ASAP 1. If your character is listed in the field list, please try your login, and if you get into the admin panel, update the fields in your bio that appear on the website. If there are other fields in there that aren't on the website, you don't need to worry about those. If your character is not listed in the field list, you will have to wait until during/after the draw until it does before your login will work. If you can't log in and your character is on the site, please let me know. 2. Fill in the bio fields that appear on the site (basic stats and bio field). Please note that these fields appear on all GTT sites that your character appears in - so keep it fairly generic. Characters that have appeared in previous GTT's will have the old bio in there. Please update it. 3. If you have been in previous PTC events with websites, you should see all your previous characters in the character selector. You can fill in the bios for these characters if you feel so motivated. Please don't post anything with them on the GTT7 site. I haven't restricted that, and don't want to spend the time doing so.... so just don't do it. If you have other characters in previous PTC events with websites and they aren't in the dropdown, let me know and I can add them to your login account. If you only have one character that has been in a PTC event, you won't see a dropdown, so this doesn't concern you.
