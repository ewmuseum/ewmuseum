In the spirit of moving on with things.... another GTT6 nugget for you (GTD spoiler)

28 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PTC has a new Global Champion - Seymour Almasy. Mr. Almasy has declared that GTT6 will be the final outing for him with respect to PTC... so that leaves us with a small quandry. Vacate the Global Title, or make him defend it? I'm going to use my 51% majority and make him defend it... in GTT6. How will this work, I hear you ask? Good question! Every PTC member that Seymour Almasy faces in GTT6 will be challenging for the PTC Global Title. You beat him, he's out, you become champion. But won't that mean the winner of GTT6 will be the Global Champion also? Not unless Almasy wins it, or the person that beats him wins it. If after defeating Almasy, the new Global Champion gets beaten, the title is not on the line, but whoever does it will get first shot as soon as the dust settles from the GTT6 final. How will his opponents be decided? Your best bet is to hope you end up in Almasy's bracket, or be his Round 1 choice. After that, you need him to make the finals along with you. Can't do it any other way than that. What if some schmuck gets a match with him? How is that fair? Life is full of surprises. If that schmuck beats Almasy, he will have been good enough to do so, thus deserves to be the next Global Champion. Can Free Agents get in on this? Ummmmm no. Questions?
