Round 2 Bracket 4 Results inside

20 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Smokey Wead (UWF) v Jimmy Diablo (FA) Judge 1 SMOKEY RP#1 I thought it was a good start to the series. It was well-written, sets up for Smokey’s battle for soberness with help from his friend. There were a few spelling and grammatical errors, but nothing to really put me off. Overall, was a good start to the series. SMOKEY RP#2 HA, HA, HA! Some very funny moments in this rp that had me chuckling and people in my house wondering what’s wrong with me. The whole liver accident situation and the Star Sign was classic, then trying to put Bubba in the tournament. Very funny, not sure if it’s enough to win the match though, we’ll see. DIABLO RP#1 Er… okay… so it’s a roleplay set in the future… for the present… I’m confused on how that works, but of course that’s not going to take away from a good rp, so we’ll see where this goes. HA, HA, HA! I love when that happens. Something really ridiculous happens and it’s ended with someone saying “What the hell does this have to do with the match though?” almost a message to judges like me who are probably thinking about the same thing at that point. Okay, reading through the roleplay I was completely clueless until the last couple of paragraphs what the whole thing had to do with the match. So I take it 8 years before is talking about when it was Jimmy’s match. Obviously Jimmy doesn’t die in this whole thing then. Will be interesting to see how it all goes, and if the strange futuristic history lesson has any bearing on the series, or is just useless filler. DIABLO RP#2 Okay, now I’m confused. She’s the widow of Jimmy Diablo, and talking to Jimmy Diablo, who is alive and well? So there is more than one Jimmy Diablo I take it… :S Oh, so Jimmy Diablo isn’t ACTUALLY Jimmy Diablo? Okay, fine. After reading the 2nd I’ve got to say I’m quite disappointed with this series, it was long-winded in parts and wasn’t particularly eventful. OVERALL Overall I was looking for something in either of the two series that set them above the other, and found nothing. Smokey’s series was funny, but offered little storyline. Diablo’s offered storyline, but dragged on in parts and was largely uneventful. Neither wrestler concentrated on the match too much, other than mentioning it a couple of times. In the end, the only thing I could base the series on was who I’d want to see more of in the tournament. And for that I picked Diablo, hoping that he’d take my comments and offer some more meat to his storyline in his next series in the next round. Judge 2 It started to get a little clunky in the dialogue part of it. Pros: Pretty funny stuff. Cons: The stuff towards the end really didn’t help any in convincing me that Smokey could win this match. Diablo, RP 1 It gets off to a pretty weird start with the scene in the future that seemingly has nothing to do with anything. From a writing point of view, I don’t like the fact that the story starts in Jimbo’s head, then immediately switches to Jimmy’s; if it’s told from a close third point of view to Jimmy, then it should be that way from the start. I think the description for Mexico City about breathing the air being like smoking two and a half packs of cigarettes a day to be a little much. I’m pretty sure that’s not even possible, and it didn’t seem like it was meant to be a hyperbolic statement. And I don’t even get that part at the end. Uh … is Diablo going to die soon? I hope that’ll be explained well in the next RP. Pros: The writing is quite good, and keeps me engaged. Cons: The lecture part of the RP feels like I’m reading a narrative part of a novel, not a history lesson on something that actually happened. People just wouldn’t speak that way about something that had happened in their past, as if it’s all entirely new information; for example, after giving the new UN name, the teacher wouldn’t say, “the former United Nations,” just like when we talk about Russia today, we don’t then parenthetically say “the former Soviet Union.” And I don’t really even see the point of that part, unless it was a dream, which it doesn’t seem to have been. Diablo, RP 2 Maybe I’m missing something here, but why is this called Shard #8 when we were just on 2? Oh well. At the beginning of the RP, how is there a table separating Jimmy and Jocelyn as soon as she opens the door? Another thing that makes little sense to me. I also don’t think it makes a lot of sense to call the police “oxymoronic,” at least not unless you say how they did something that was an oxymoron. Little gripes aside, I now finally get the Jimmy Diablo thing, although I was confused as hell for the first half of the RP. Guess that’s my fault for not reading his first round RPs. And the ending of this RP just confuses me again further. Who is this guy impersonating Jimmy Diablo, and was he the one that killed him? Because I thought that Rikyu guy was. Maybe these are questions I’m supposed to be asking, or maybe it’s just my fault for not reading the first round RPs, but this just seems to leave to many open questions for a RP series that might not even be continued. Pros: Answers some questions I had from before. Cons: Opens more up. Final Decision: This decision’s kinda hard since the guys’ styles were so different. But Jimmy’s were much better written, and I think that’s going to be the deciding factor for me. Jimmy Diablo gets this one. Judge 3 Pick = Jimmy Diablo. Apologies for no time to do any comment worthwhile. Winner Jimmy Diablo (FA) defeats Smokey Wead (UWF), 3 votes to 0. Karina Wolfenden (RPW) v Dawn McFarlaine (WMW) Judge 1 No brainer here, K-Wolf wins with 2 RP's to 1. I won't list point by point what was good about each RP, but I will say that K-Wolf's were very good as expected, and McFarlaine's RP was solid. Had McFarlaine posted another really good one to make the count even, then the match could have been very close... but due to numbers alone, K-Wolf advances. Judge 2 Karina Wolfenden - Karina was awesome here in this match. It was long, but the story was so intriguing that it continued to draw me to read. Dawn McFarlaine - The story for her was equally as good as Karina's. Everything I look for was there and the story was maybe just a little bit better than Wolfenden's. Winner: Karina Wolfenden, solely because she gave me more to read than Dawn with the 2-1 RP. Judge 3 Karina Wolfenden #1 Part One - You Were At Peace -- Pros: Matt delivers again. We know this man's writing. Nuff said. Cons: Not much in the line of cons on this one. From what I can gleen here, continuing a storyline in RPW and the tourney as a whole. So keeps everything going. Karina Wolfenden #2 Part Two - The Odd Couple. -- Pros: Again, great work. Cons: For a K-Wolf rp, it was obviously and admittedly rushed to get it out before deadline, and comapred to the first one, it showed. Wait, I got a real con on both: Bad Grammer in the rp title. Immediatly followed a period to end the title with a comma. Bad Mat, bad. Dawn McFarlaine #1 The Vicious Circles of Life, tear Me Up Inside -- Why didn't she do a second one? Why? Pros: Surprising in the first round with a solid defeat of her opponents. And in this round? Could have been a contender again. Solid roleplay. Doesn't over write above and beyond the character. Doen'st try to down you in thinking they've got an oxford dictionary sitting there, yet there is good command of what is being written here. Some good work here. Where is Dawn's name in WMW's list for the GTD contendership? cons: There was only one? Yeah. While K-Wolf did manage to get out a second rp, it could have been very, very close if there had been one more from Dawn. Good show none-the-less. The pick: K-Wolf Winner Karina Wolfenden (RPW) defeats Dawn McFarlaine (WMW), 3 votes to 0. CW1 brackets
