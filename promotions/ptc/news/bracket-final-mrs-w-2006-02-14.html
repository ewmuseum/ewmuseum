Bracket Final: Mrs W.!

14 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
KIMBUSA V SEYMOUR ALMASY Judge 1 Damnit. Damnit. Damnit. Damnit. Damnit. Damnit. Damnit. You bastards. I hate you. I'm a big fan of the both of you, and you both have made this incredibly hard to call. You fricking bastards. Both of your stories were incredible, and the writing was fantastic. In my opinion you both deserve to be in the final four. Unfortunately, I can only pick one of you to move on, and I really wish I could put both of you through. Seymour's story was very compelling and he played some major cards this round. First his grandfather dies, and then Laura shoots herself at the end. This is a major turning point for Seymour's character, as two of the most influential people in his life are now gone (I'm assuming Laura is dead based on the ending of the third RP). So Seymour is now at an impasse... what will happen to him next? How will he react to losing the people he's cared about? What of his relationship with Tessa, given that he was willing to give everything up to go back to Laura? Kimbusa's story was equally fantastic. He pulls off a rather great feat in that he tells much of the story from Caldera's perspective rather than Kimbusa's, yet it still all ties together very nicely. Great description and very fleshed-out, I would even go so far as to say that this story is superior to the one Seymour told... which is saying something because Seymour has put together an excellent series throughout this tournament. If Kimbusa had done a third RP that was on the same level as the two he posted, I would give him this match. But it's his two against Seymour's three, so the decision is painfully close. Both left me wanting to know more about each character. Both were entertaining and a great read. I felt Kimbusa's had more substance than Seymour's did. Seymour's was more of an emotional roller coaster than Kimbusa's. I could justify either of you winning. But, here's what it comes down to. Seymour made some drastic changes that his character now has to deal with. He has brought this character to yet another turning point where he must re-define himself and by my estimation go in a new direction that he hasn't gone before. Kimbusa's story has been very well thought-out and very insightful into his character's history. But I sense that the future has vast potential for Seymour. That is what compels me to favor Seymour as the victor of this match. As I said, it was incredibly close. My mind changed several times after I finished reading, until I could finally settle on a concrete reason to pick one of you over the other. I applaud both of you for your efforts, and congrautlate you on a job well done. PICK: Seymour Almasy d. Kimbusa Judge 2 Kimbusa: Loose Ends (Part One): It starts of pretty slow, but you're able to keep me reading with only one minor break. The story is good, even if the piece seemed far too long for it. Writing gets a 9/10, great there. The abrupt ending keeps me hooked for part II. Final Confession (Part Two).: Great self contained story. I really liked this one a lot, much more than the first. When it comes to E-W stories, Part II should be better and thats the case with this. Giving Kimbusa a real reason to win drives this one far in my mind. Everything falls together and makes sense, great work. Seymour Almasy: Birth: Your writing is so, Refreshing? If that makes sense. You seem to do your own thing, and thats commendable. Your style suits you, and it shows greatly in this. Solid work, nothing else I can say really. Life: Good work connecting the first two parts seemlessly. This is turning out to be the closest match I've judged so far. The chemistry between Seymour and his Ex is great, and the ending really hooks me for Part III. If the 3rd part is as good as the 2nd, it may be the deciding factor for me and this match! Death: I differentiate an RP from a Good piece of writing when the 'chore' factor is eliminated, and I absolutely enjoy myself in reading. Kudos for this, definately the best piece with the most shocking ending I've read. The Decision: I took a break from judging after read Kimbusa's two and Seymour's first two. It was deadlocked at that time, I really had no idea who to pick. But Seymour's 3rd really did it for me, thats the kind of work you expect in a brackett final. I'm a huge Kimbusa Mark (Which may give up my identity) but I can't award it to him. Pick: Seymour Almasy Judge 3 Seymour Almasy RP 1: Birth Very good entrance, I thought, definitely set the standard for the rest of your roleplay. Good grammar as well, I struggled to find any mistakes with punctuation, so that made it a lot easier to read than a mistake filled RP. The next part was also good; I think you had the right amount of description throughout. To me, one of the worst things you can do in a RP is it over or under-describe. I also like the way it relates to the real relationship between superstars in USA or England, and how they are even bigger over in Japan. Basically, I like the way it relates to real life, if you get what I mean. I didn’t like the next part as much as I liked the first part. I’m not sure why, but to me it just seemed as though it was a bit detached from the rest of the RP, a bit random. The writing was fine, as good as the other parts; the problem was that it was just detached. However, the next part did go back on track with the whole “Fame in Japan” idea, and I liked this part a lot, it certainly made up for the last part in my opinion. I do like that you actually mentioned Kimbusa in the RP, because some people don’t do that and I think the opponent should always be given some mention. I definitely like the way it left somewhere for you to go in your next RP. RP 2: Life Again, I like the way that the entrance is written well, and sets a good thing to focus on and a good standard to write to for the rest of the RP. I have to admit I do like the format you use; where you cut the RP into different sections. In the next section, I thought it was good to see you use the last RP to write this section and RP, with the death of Cid Wilson on his mind. I thought the reporter was a nice touch, again it sort of related with real life, not fantasy, which is something I like in RPs. I thought the last section was pretty good. It was excellent writing, and also left the options wide open for the third RP, but to my disappointment in this RP there wasn’t much mention of Kimbusa, which I thought let it down a bit. RP 3: Death The opening section was a lot longer, and to even my surprise I liked this even more. I liked the way you left something to do with Laura for later, and I like the way you demonstrated how close he was with Cid and how you left what you were going to do for the next section hanging in the air. Excellent start. When I realised you had done a large section on Cid, I was saying Thank God because I was glad the whole RP was about Laura. I also liked the whole idea of Cid leaving a note for Seymour. I also loved how at the end of the section the reader then knew you had made a decision and were intent on keeping it, but left it so that we didn’t know what it was. On to the penultimate section… This section was the best written section, the best story of the entire three roleplays and the most exciting section. However, it had no mention of Kimbusa whatsoever. This let it down a little bit, but I am just hoping the final section mentions GTT5… Hooray! It does. This third RP was the best of the lot, and even though some might get sick of it, I think the whole “winning for somebody you love” thing is an excellent storyline. The writing was good, and I was very impressed. Overall: Very impressed by your three roleplays. Kimbusa will have to bring out the big guns to beat you. So let’s see. Kimbusa RP 1: Loose Ends (Part One) Throughout this roleplay, I think you made a mistake of being over descriptive all the way through. By the time I was a third of the way down, I was like, “Okay, I’m getting bored now.” However, the writing was rather good, and as far as I can see you made no big mistakes with grammar, punctuation and your use of English. Overall, not a bad roleplay, and the ending did leave somewhere to go for the second RP, which is something I like in a RP. RP 2: The Final Confession (Part Two) To me, this RP was a lot better than your first RP. Your first one seemed to lack movement, it seemed as if it wasn’t going anywhere. However, this one was the opposite, and I thought it was an excellent RP. It still had the same amount of description as the first one, but inside these bits of description were some, once again, excellent writing and excellent grammar. I loved the story about the roots of Kimbusa, about how he was an accidental killer from sumo wrestling, and in a way this could have been intended to scare Seymour Almasy. It was an excellent, original storyline that I didn’t see coming as his history. I thought this second RP definitely made up for what I thought was a poor first one, and so makes it quite close to decide who gets the victory. Pick: Very close, but I’m going to give it to Seymour Almasy because I just think he outdid Kimbusa this round. Judge 4 Seymour Amasy I have never read anything by Seymour or Kimbusa before this round. That can be a hinderance or helpful. Seymour's RPs made it neither. To put it bluntly, they rocked. I can see why everyone enjoys putting lips around Sean's member, because he can really tell a story. The way Seymour was introduced, how his back story was told well, the way he and Laura's history was done...yeah. Excellent. The only thing that annoyed me was that the 3rd RP was slow in the middle...but, even then, it wasn't. So, yeah, badass. Kimbusa Like I stated before, I didn't read up on either Kimbusa or Almasy. Almasy made this work. Kimbusa...not so much. I felt lost reading parts of the RP. Explanations of things would've been nice. You can't count on every judge reading every RP from previous rounds. Being lost also helped fuel the fact that, at times, your RP...dragggggged. And I'm talking, during-the-RP-I-get-bored-with-it-and-take-a-dump dragged. Which isn't good. Almasy kept me on my seat during RPs...you didn't. Ouch. The second RP was not-good-killer in this sense. It took me forever to realize Kurokame was Kimbusa (that would explain the password!). I had a feeling it was...but it would've been nice if you maybe mentioned once that Kimbusa went by Kurokame. But, EVEN IF YOU DID THAT, the beginning of the second RP...DRAGGGGED. I almost took a nap while reading it. Thankfully, you concluded it in such a way where I didn't think it was hopeless. ANd, the first RP was decent, maybe good enough to beat Almasy in a 1 RP v 1 RP competition. But... Verdict ...maybe you wouldn't have. While Almasy held my attention with an awesome story, and an even better ending, Kimbusa's RPs left me in the dark. While the first RP was good (The entertainment district part was cool), the rest was kind of sucky. It could've been great!...if you didn't assume that I was an avid Kimbusa fan this whole tournament. Sean didn't assume I was an Almasy fan. He made a story that was easy to get into, and the fact that I want another RP in a good way means something. Pick: Almasy with the win! RESULT Seymour Almasy d Kimbusa, 4 votes to 0 Seymour Almasy is the Mrs. W. Bracket Champion. The Bounty on Seymour Almasy lives another round. Congratulations to both players! Brackets
