Bracket Final Results - Rabbit

29 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EMBER V STEVE WATSON - CPA Judge 1 Steve Watson: The style is a quick read with just enough action and detail to balance the dialogue. However, after reading through I am not very concerned about the story. I think the blackmail sex/love thing was great, but then it is immediately broken by the agents arrival. I just did not get into the story as much. Ember: The first story to get the new mask is crafted beautifully. The second piece does a great job explaining the situation, but the back and forth style did take something away for me. Overall, Ember has a more memorable and engaging story. Pick: Ember Judge 2 Due to unfortunate circumstances, I'll have to make this short*. Both sets of promos were awesome, better than most I've seen in this contest. Unfortunatly, Watson's story flowed much more smoothly, and kept me interested throughout the entire thing. Ember's left me a bit confused, and Because of this, I'l have to give this one to Watson - CPA. Pick: Steve Watson - CPA. * Hey, my electricity is out at the moment. I can give you a summerized pick. Within a day or so, I can get you the full breakdown, promo by promo off my computer. Sorry. Judge 3 I read both of these guys last round, so am looking forwards to this. Watson 1 Pros: - I love to see a good, intriguing storyline that actually involves the tournament to such a degree. They're a rarity these days, but this proves that they can be done and done well - Pulled me in to the story quickly and well. I think that someone entirely unfamiliar with your work might have been troubled, but this actually filled in the blanks you had skipped over the previous round, leaving me with more of a feel for it all - A good pace to the story, and the way you flipped between the different times was flawless, and done exactly when you wanted it to be done Cons are harder, as there is nothing that stops this being very good. But I will try, as this is a very high level of competition. Cons: - Characterization. The whole thing is very good, but I'm not feeling a massive connection to our protagonist. I'm involved in his struggle, and I do love the story... I'd just love to feel closer to Steve Watson the character. At the moment, were he to fail or be killed or something, then it'd be a shame, but I wouldn't be genuinely upset. That little emotional hook is what this really needs. Ember 1 Pros: - In the first few lines there's an immediate lyrical depth that captures you without seeming pretentious. - Having an entire roleplay encompass just one scene without ever growing tired, seeming too short or too long or making mis-steps is just phenomenal - There's not too much storyline in this, but it hits at the end, but throughout the whole is a thread of tension that holds perfectly Cons are harder... much harder Cons: - Occasional proof-reading mistakes - Occasional lapses into a slight degree of going over the top in description. This only happened once, maybe twice during the entire roleplay though, so it's a minor fault and pales in comparison to what you have done in positives with the language... just a warning to stay the right side of the line Watson 2 Pros: - This has a great conversational tone to it, like the narrator is really telling a story. It touches on the consciousness of cast members, but retains its impartiality. A good example is how it says the journey felt like hours but probably took far less time. It's a tiny thing, but it creates such a great feel to the whole piece - Buwahahahahahaha! I laughed out loud at the Agent Dash reference(s). I normally hate intertwined stuff, but a simple burn can works wonders. - Good story. It's an effective intriguing mystery that doesn't tend to rely on any schlock horror tactics. - Good ending, with a brief flicker of hope that Hainesworth might not be so bad extinguished with the wiping of the drives. - I also like that nobody is squeaky clean in all this. Everyone seems to have their bad sides (aside from Watson). He's playing a lot of people at once whilst maintaining his own, almost bumbling character. Cons: - Weren't Steve's family taken away by the bad guys? How can the 'cops' simply send by a car? He should have worked that out earlier, and surely Chad is lying to him? - It didn't go as far as I had hoped it might. The wiping of the hard drive is good, and the hints of how far the whole thing spreads is good too, but I wanted a little more. Ember 2 Pros: - Good intro again... brings you in from a completely different angle... and then it's intimated that Phoenix was gay??? Hmmm... .... .... ... ... ... figures! (Bah! Refuted later... but I have my own opinions...) - Useful recap to bring in the newer readers and refresh the memories of the previous ones. - And then the hint as to Ember's identity... interesting... - The section about the mother started a little slowly, to tell the truth, but in the end was excellent. Cons: - The excellence of the scene with the mother is heavily compromised by it not being Ember as the baby, which would have been thematically wonderful. - I don't buy the transformation as part of him being hit on the head by a sign. WAAAAAY too corny. - Terrible ending. It was too convoluted, worked too hard to lead us one way and then the other, and then finished with the oldest trick in the book, one which has been used several times in this GTT alone. “Oh, there goes a gun... tune in next week to find out who died or didn't die!” Bah! Overall: Both had a lot to offer here. Watson has the more down to earth style of writing, but a good plot that is well paced and full of interest. Ember has a more, shall we say, 'flowery' vocabulary that can veer between fantastic and overplayed. He also has a great storyline, but again one that veers between genius and lunacy. I can't procrastinate this one though. I've got Ember as the clear winner here. His first roleplay just totally blew me away. It's my favorite one that I've read from GTT thus far. His second had a lot of unnecessary parts to it, but was still good and showed flashes of inspiration. Watson didn't do a thing wrong, and often at this stage you get two guys with grade 'A' work, but you have to choose, and in terms of characterization, emotion and sheer writing prowess, Ember just impressed me that little bit more than Watson. Congrats to the runner up here (who knows, he might win the other votes and get through anyway, and if he does I wouldn't complain as he was great). Watson, you provided a great story, but sometimes it just ain't your day. Ember. Congratulations. Field. Overlook this man at your peril. Pick: Ember Result Ember d Steve Watson - CPA, 2 votes to 1 Match Judges Pitt Andy Bob(ino) Ember advances to the Semi Final Round. Matchup TBA. Rabbit
