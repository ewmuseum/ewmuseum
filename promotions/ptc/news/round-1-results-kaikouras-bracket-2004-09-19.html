Round 1 Results: Kaikouras Bracket

19 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These are results, they are not Round 2 Brackets. Those will be announced after all the results are up. No-shows and Double-no-shows are not noted. If you haven't read the judging thread, do it now: http://community.primetimecentral.net/showthread.php?t=9955 Jay Phoenix d Desolation (2 votes to 0) to advance Dave Hurst d Kyo Kushinaga (1 vote) to advance Sapphire advances. Tex “One Gun” Chattanooga advances. Moraless advances. Fred Grest advances. Odium d Lex le Magnifique (2 votes to 0) to advance. Angelo Deville d Snake Eyes (2 votes to 0) to advance. Rzarector d Jakob Mayhem (1 vote) to advance. Arch Angel advances. Blaze Medina advances. Train advances. John Crigger d Drayton McPhereson (2 votes to 1) to advance. Eddie Benoit advances. Fury advances. Judges Comments Jay Phoenix vs Desolation Desolation (8.9): I liked the RP and the historical references. (That must of sucked to loose that way.) I think the embarrasing moments could've continued but there's no bad points in ending it early. Jay Phoenix (9.0): Rick James? Anyway, the RP kind of went no where for me. I know roleplays can't be about the match all the time, but this was waaaay out there. Pick: Phoenix Both competitors did a good job with the theme word. Phoenix - what an epic! 8000 words. The longest roleplay I had to read. I give you props for having very few flaws in your work, and for keeping your writing and story line constant. Desolation, your work was well thought out and while I thought Phoenix wrote too much, I didn't think you developed your storyline/character enough. I'm giving the win to Phoenix. EDH vs Kyo Kushinaga Dave Hurst vs Kyo Kushinaga Dave, you forgot to add Priscilla, Queen of the desert to your list of credits, ha. There was times that I thought that you pushed just a little too hard, and I wasn't sure you captured the theme of 'shyness' … your character was maybe more insecure than shy. Kyo, I found your work hard to read, it was full of errors in your English: "Kyo stares forward as he squint eyes halfway having seriousness in them" and in some parts just didn't make sense to me at all, I assumed that you where writing as someone who either does not have English as a first language, or to emulate someone who doesn't however, even if it is a gimmick to write that way, your writing still needs to be understood by the reader. I give the win to Dave. Chattanooga Either very very smart, or very very green. Lex le Magnifique vs Odium Lex: His RP I didnt see where his word fit into the whole thing, It almost looked like he had wanted a deadly sin word instaead. Funny thing is he does mention Odiums word in his RP. But again I got the fact that he is a very relious character but thats all I got from this RP. Not sure what to say here, It seemed that Odium had a better grasp on his key word, then Lex did. Even being a long ass RP I didn’t mind it to much Odium keep it flowing well and it didn’t feel like a task reading his Rp. Pick: Odium Lex (8.4): Grammatical errors plagued the roleplay. No real mentioning of the match or tournament was made until the end. Still a good builder roleplay. I hope that I can see it build to something else. Odium (9.1): Though long winded, the main focus was the match and that was a big plus for the RP. The consistent attacks on Lex's pride was great as well. Great RP and I hope that I can see it happen once again. (Of course as another RP.) Pick: Odium Snake Eyes vs Angelo Deville Angelo Deville Vs. Snake Eyes Angelo Deville: A solid RP, great from beginning to end. Proved why he's one of the best right here. Snake Eyes: Was good, but didn't keep up with Angelo. Just an unlucky draw for him in the first round, he could have done so much better against just about anyone else. Pick: Angelo Deville I read Snake Eyes first, and thought it was pretty good, good enough to win him major titles. Definitely good enough to compete in this match. Then I read Devilles, which while well written, for the most part felt too constant, even bland. The smooth writing overcame that though, and I had them level pegging most of the way. Then, like the early part in Devilles story, he turned the tables with his last paragraph and blew Snake Eyes away by using his theme word to full effect, which left me nodding in appreciation. Pick: Angelo Deville Rzarector vs Jacob Mayhem Mayhem, your roleplay was very short, and while you spoke of 'wanting' things, I didn't think it necessarily met the theme; greed. In my opinion it didn't developed your character much. Rzarector, Your roleplay was a bit too "Mordecai" for my tastes, however it was well written, it developed your character and it used the theme well. Rzarector, I'm giving you the win. Train Train: Spelling killed the 9.2/9.3 I wanted to give. Long wided but it stayed focused on his GW happenings and his past. I can't find anything wrong other than the few spelling mistakes that stuttered me a little bit. Drayton McPhereson vs John Crigger Will have to go with Drayton on this one, It seemed that again the word was the undoing of the man. Crigger’s RP just didn’t seem to have anything to really do about the whole show. Granted I think it was one of the shortest ones I had to read Pick: Drayton McPhereson D.M (9): Great RP. Character reminds me of myself in some aspects. Didn't focus too heavily on the match or the tourney but I felt that wasn't a bad thing in this case. (Wasn't a good thing, just wasn't bad.) John Crigger (9.1): I like this guys cockyness. Reminds of a combination Jericho/Orton. Touched up on his history and I love the fed nationalism he showed as well. Winner: Crigger (But McPhereson deserves a standing O after the match.) McPhereson vs Crigger Both pieces were well written, however McPhereson's work didn't draw me in as much as Crigger's - which while much shorter than McPhereson's - was funny and had a good pace to it that made it much easier to read. Both roleplays had an element of trash talk and both used the theme word well, Crigger also employed a nicely set up 'storyline'. Where I can't say the same of McPhereson - his work was more of a monologue to the camera, that could have been directed at anyone - I almost expected him to bring out a skull and launch into "Alas, poor Yorick! I knew him…". McPhereson's work in my opinion just wasn't as memorable. Crigger the win. Kaikouras
