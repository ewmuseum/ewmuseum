Round 3 Results: Kaikouras Bracket

13 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dave Hurst d Jay Phoenix (3 votes to 0) to advance. Odium d Fred Grest (2 votes to 1) to advance. Train d Arch Angel (2 votes to 0) to advance. Angelo Deville d Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis (2 votes to 0) to advance. Round 4 Matchups Dave Hurst v Odium Train v Angelo Deville Judging Comments Dave Hurst v Phoenix Jay Phoenix: I enjoyed the way Phoenix’s first RP started the same way as Hurst’s. It really showed the connection that both men have with each other going into the match. It highlighted the way each one was taking their own approach to the match while still dealing with it in the same way. Phoenix’s second RP really delved into his character’s personality which was extremely nice. He also nicely blended in some of the history of his character and reasons for being in the tournament. Dave Hurst: Right from the start I enjoyed Hurst’s work. The way he described the feeling of the kitbag strap digging into his shoulder had me actually feeling it myself. The RP was then launched into a great story of self doubt that really caught my attention. This happened so much so that by then of the first RP, I couldn’t wait to start on the second to find out what was going to happen with Ducky. The second RP wasn’t a letdown of those expectations either. Bringing the story full circle very nicely and ending on a high note. The only comment that I can make is that these Rps were exceptional. The Result: I really enjoyed the Rps from both Phoenix and Hurst and wish there was a way I could see both of them move on. In the end though I can only pick one. So I’ll give the nod to Dave Hurst. Both Hurst and Phoenix had equally good writing. Both had a great story and really showed what their character had to offer. In the end though, Hurst’s story just called to me a little more and just barely edged out Phoenix. Pick: Dave Hurst J. Phoenix (9.2) A few grammar problems. But not Many. Really Confused at some parts (kind of figured it out.) Good RP towards the end but it was long winded... J. Phoenix [2] (9.001) Punctuation problems returned but ehh it's too common now. Definently one of the most long winded roleplays I have read to date. The bar scene helped the score. Dave Hurst - (9.2) Long winded, but effective. With the scene changes, I thought that I was reading a movie script or some sort of play. Dave Hurst [2] - (9.2) Like the intro scene. Best one that I seen yet in this tourney. Pretty cool concept. "I'm a loser." Not like anyone else's roleplay's I've seen to date that always say "I'm a winner"... "I've done blah blah and held blah title for blah blah times." Pretty original. Pick: Dave Hurst Jay Phoenix: The roleplays were well written, but I felt like they were longer than they needed to be. He seemed to write in places where he just felt like writing. However, the quality of writing was very high. Though I did find them to be boring in quite a few places. Dave Hurst: Original as always, I mean absolutely superb. Maybe I'll get slated for this but I really enjoyed his roleplays. They made me smile and were well written. Quality of writing is not always the winning factor, a superbly written piece of work that bores you to death is not as good as a very well written piece that entertains you thoroughly. Jay produced some good writing as always, but Dave gets my vote on orginality, I really did enjoy reading his roleplays and they are my favourites from everything I've read this round. Pick: Dave Hurst Odium v Fred Grest Odium’s 1st- Well written dream sequence with the tale of Odium trying to fight sleep to avoid these nightmares that keep plaguing him. Odium’s 2nd- Another well written sequence that really helps the reader understand just what Odium is going through with these dreams, especially at the end when it mentions he was surprised his eyes even opened. Fred’s 1st- Lots of humor involved in a roleplay that focuses on his show being cancelled which made for a decent read, but it was littered with technical errors that really dragged the quality of it down. Fred’s 2nd- I enjoyed this one more than the first, but once again there were a high amount of technical errors. Think it could have been a little better if there was a little more detail put in as to their surroundings. Rather than just focusing on the dialogue, and Fred not being able to drink. Pick: Odium These two both wrote well with their own styles making it a real choice between two styles of writing. I simply prefer something that does more then 'talk' your way through an rp. Odium wins for his inclusion of ambience and scenery throughout his roleplays. Pick: Odium Odium: Really enjoyed both of your roleplays, well structured, had a good flow. Fred Grest: I liked the old schoolness to your roleplays, good flow, well written. Pick: Fred Grest Arch Angel v Train Angel-The roleplay seemed a bit rushed. No real introduction to the characters, it just jumps into the scene. Then all of a sudden something occurred which makes the doctor come to the conclusion that she isn’t going to make it. And then she’s dead and he turns into a monster. Just seems too generic for me. Would have liked to have seen a better build up to this event. Not just him strolling in and then finding out two seconds later that she’s going to die, when she seems perfectly healthy with no sign of this being unlike any other birth. Train- Found the dialogue at the beginning to be very confusing. It just jumps in without telling you who is saying what so that the reader has no clue what is going and what the relationship between the people conversing is, as well as how many people conversing there are. Who is Jerry? Who is Tony? Is one of these guys Train, because it seems like it, but there really isn’t any clear cut answer because both of them seem to be asking the questions a person going into the match would be asking. The first part and the second part really don’t seem to be related at all, rather the second bit seems to be added on for lengths sake. The plot in the second half though was certainly a lot more solid than in the first bit. Pick: Train Arch Angel Over all this RP wasn't horrible but I don't think it was the best he could of done either. It seems like it was written hastily and he needed to take a little more time with it. It does set up the mood for this match up. Train A few spelling errors were noticable but nothing huge. The story flowed well and kept on a subject. A blast from the past and it leads to a little more at the end. It is a good RP nice Read. Over all the discription was pretty good I could almost picture the setting of the whole RP. Pick: Train Angelo Deville v Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis Jackson’s 1st- Very strongly written, but towards the end when things start to focus on Jackson, things really started to get clumped up. I highly doubt a fearful man would be speaking full paragraphs to himself. Jackson’s 2nd- Wasn’t fan of how suddenly the scene cut off, without any evidence of what was going to happen next. Especially how it skips to the next bit with Brian Davis, who doesn’t really seem to play any relevance in this bit besides him coming across the bodies of the Denucci family. All together I wished it could have been a bit more original instead of piecing together bits and pieces to movies such as Hannibal and Phone Booth in. Loved the tie in to the first roleplay though at the end. Deville’s 1st- Just plain awesome. Great story all around. Looking forward to see what happens with this “bond” between Satan and Deville, through the voices. Deville’s 2nd- Another great piece. It’s really got me interested as to what going to happen next, especially with the contrasting thoughts of the devil, that just make him all the more evil. Pick: Angelo Deville --- Is it just me or did I fall into a gang-fight when reading these rps? Every rp was either somebody getting shot or a conversation with Satan. These are two TREMENDOUS writers and both were simply amazing in the detail they put into their rps. Deville's work was simply better though, longer, more entertaining, more catching, more ambience, better scenery, better use of the full idea. I can tell you from judging Deville's work in the GTT3 that I consider Jackson to be the NEXT Angelo Deville, but he's just not there yet. Angelo Deville is one step closer to a second GTT trophy as he gets the win in a superb showing from both. Pick: Angelo Deville Kaikouras
