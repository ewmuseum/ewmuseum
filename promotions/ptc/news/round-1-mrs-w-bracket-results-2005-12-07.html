Round 1: Mrs W. Bracket Results

7 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. JAKE HIX v MICHAEL STEVENS Thanksgiving Feast by Michael Stevens I started to cringe part way through this, it was all too perfect, quite sickening really. By half way through, I firmly believed it was going to be some sucky horrible romance novel rubbish. I had only just started to wonder if you were jerking my chain and it was a dream sequence when you changed direction and made it so, so great timing there. This is exactly the reason I gave you the nod in IG Round 14 - because I thought you might do something good with the guardian angel. Thanks for not disappointing! I’m definitely wanting more of where this is going. The choices we make, the pain we live with by Jake Hix A mighty fine piece of writing. However, I have to say, and this applies to anything you've done here at PTC thus far, is that the flow is too consistent. That is, there's nothing here that incites any significant emotional response change - this is the real thing that a writer needs in their arsenal, in order to be able to reach out and grab his/her readers by their crotch, and twist. One example, it was crystal clear that Enzo was going to be popped, so when it did happen, it came as no surprise. All it raised out of me was "meh". You have the words, you have solid characters and good storylines, its just that one element that you need to incorporate that is the difference between what you have now and wherever you want to go with this game (See: Rich Rollins - two years ago he was in exactly the same position from what I read of his work). In fact, if you master it, you could conceivably be going a lot further than this game, because your style would easily adapt to larger works if you choose to do that. Result: Michael Stevens d Jake Hix Absolutely nothing in it… almost. Stevens had the missing ingredient I refer to in HIx’s work, in that he took me right down to the depths of cringe, then brought me right back up again, with perfect timing. I’d be very afraid of Hix though, as he could well be the next Rollins in time. 2. JOHNNY LEXICON V DRAKE COLEMAN The Mercy Beat by Drake Coleman Easy to read. Understandable. Identifiable plot. Sells the schizophrenia quite well. Patchy in the middle, and a little underdone with the burns and the time in the body cast. A couple of things that were touched on were not explained, such as what actually happened to Charlene. A bit of liberal borrowing from Stephen King's The Dead Zone, minus the fortune telling, would have really given this an extra boost, for example Charlene going off and marrying someone else while he was in the cast. I don't know how you've covered that previously, but a paragraph on it here would have been the icing it needed. Winners Never Quit by Johnny Lexicon I get what you are trying to do. I was right into it for the first three days worth, but then… the wheels fell off. By the time I finished day 5, it was all pretty much lost on me and I just wanted it to finish. Perhaps too diverse an approach to cover your idea? Result: Coleman defeats Lexicon Lexicon’s was actually a technically superior piece in the first three days, but after that I couldn’t get into it at all… Overall, not much in it at all really. Coleman will need to step up several notches to advance to Round 3. 3. ANDI THE MAGNIFICENT V EMBER The Chronicles of a Magician, Pt1 by Andi the Magnificent I can not stress how important spelling and punctuation are. Proof reading is as important as story-line. Your story-line had real potential, but I don't think that you pulled it off. Magic by Ember Ember, I enjoyed your story-line, I thought it was well written and I'm looking forward to seeing more. Result: Ember the win 4. VALENTINE BURST V CYRUS SKAR The Past: Part 1 Sick, Sad and Delusional by Valentine Burst I dunno. I found it a bit like an episode of Seinfeld. Nothing really happened. However, unlike Seinfeld, I couldn't connect. The Drugs Don’t Work by Cyrus Skar I found my attention drifting at points, but on the whole I found yours to be the better written work. Result: Cyrus Skar d Valentine Burst All in all the average vs the average. Skar needs to significantly elevate his game for Round 2. 5. CLYDE WALKINS V LESTATT KNIGHT The Cell: Zero by LeStatt Knight Roberto, I suggest a proof reader. Your punctuation and spelling need work and you often switch tense in the middle of a sentence. Whatever enjoyment I got from your RP was undone by the annoyance factor of repeated bad punctuation and swapping of tense. The SPAM of roleplays by Clyde Walkins Walkins started off slow and left me with some images that I didn't really want stuck in my head, but the center -- I discovered --was full of chocolaty goodness. Result: Walkins the win. 6. JONATHON WINTERS V SEYMOUR ALMASY When a legend rolls into town by Jonathon Winters I went out to the yard and covered my donkey's bollocks before reading your post. My donkey was pretty safe. Not that I didn't like your writing. I did. Very much so. It was just so. damned. long. Prologue: Change by Seymour Almasy I groaned a bit when I realised this was a dream-sequence, but I have to say you pulled it off with style. Result: Seymour Almasy d Jonathon Winters This was actually the best match-up I read this round. I'm giving Almasy the win, but if I could put em both through. I would. 7. REVEREND BLAZE V TWIZZY Junkhead - Chapter 1: "Rock N' Roll" by Twizzy I have just one question: Spit or swallow? (on second thoughts, don't answer that!) What next though? Why do I want to see this man again in Round 2? We all live with Demons… by Reverend Billy Blaze Had an easily identifiable purpose, and served it well enough. That’s a good thing. However, in keeping it simple, it felt a bit too generic. You are experienced enough at the game to have been able to add a touch more depth here and still keep it as simple as this. Result: Twizzy defeats Reverend Billy Blaze Not much in it at all. Twizzy did a slightly more convincing job of taking his character down to new depths than Blaze did of demonstrating that his character was down and was in no danger of finding the inner strength to come back up again. Neither writer gave me much in the way of wanting to read more next round. 8. KASH V BACK ALLEY BRAWLER Anatomy of a Warrior by Kash Strip away the setting, and you have character history and some trash talk. A quite engaging character history presentation (no pun intended), and distinctly average trash talk to round out. You do write very well, and if you’d found another way to finish this one off – from right before the point where Kash unmasks, I’d have had a lot more trouble picking the match. Back Alley Brawler I like the character, I like the writing style, I enjoyed the story as much as I did your Infinite Gauntlet piece. Result: Back Alley Brawler d Kash 9. RAGNAROK V FATBADGER Act of Faith by Ragnarok I haven't studied the bible, and tend to switch channel when evangelists take center stage. So, I'm at a bit of a disadvantage here, because its hard to recognize how original or not some of the expressions within this piece are. Aside from that small reservation, I liked it, a lot. You clearly established the pecking order of your characters quite well, and the hypocrisy and deception of the situation your main character is in. All that was missing there was an obscure homage to one of the greatest evangelical tricksters of the 20th century - "Forgive me father, for I have sinned against you!", but you would have had to credit that one :D I particularly liked how the supernatural opening sequence turning out to be simply the misguided perception of the Reverend having a smoke. Nice touch. Nothing to tickle me for next round though. The Road to Losing … SPECTACULARLY!! GTT5 Style Part 1 by Fatbadger To those familiar with fatbadger and the Boomer Sooners, this is undoubtedly a hoot. To those not familiar with them, I suspect it'd be like the "what the?" you get after having a low-flying plane go right over your head. I'm in the first category, so got quite a few good belly laughs out of it. Really funny stuff! Great job with the other Sooners :) Same as Ragnarok - I want to know now what you have for me in Round 2? Also, take heed, you may not be so lucky next time with having judges that are familiar with your group. Result: Fatbadger d Ragnarok This for me is one of those ones where I don't want to eliminate either guy, because I equally liked both pieces, but for different reasons. As pathetic and lame as this sounds, I’ve literally taken a coin out of my pocket and flipped it. Tails - fatbadger wins. 10. THE BRITISH BOMBER V THE AVATAR WILLIAM GWYNN Gambling by The British Bomber This was easy to read. It’s a shame it didn’t actually do much. Put a purposeful storyline in there and stay away from the clichés (e.g. the entire sequence with the elevator attendant) and you would likely have advanced. I like the way you sell Bomber not being all he thinks he is in the eyes of the people he encounters, that's a nice touch. All the Wrong Moves: Gloria by Avatar William Gwynn You had me hook, line and sinker with this, up until you went back to the present time at the end. Truthfully, that bit is best deleted if you keep a personal archive. Result: Avatar William Gwynn d The British Bomber 11. XIN XIN XIONG V EVA VANLORNE Trigger Effect: Chapter 1 by Eva VanLorne Fast paced, easy to read, efficiently structured, correct spelling and words, cliffhanger ending of sorts, doesn't require that I know a whole lot about the history of Eva to be able to follow it. All good stuff. If I had to say anything negative, it would be that it is a little too "plastic Charlies Angels fluff TV" for my liking. That’s not a bad thing though in the grand scheme of things. Lust: My problem with bitches by Xin Xin Xiong Normally I don't like protracted combat scenes in RP's. but for you I'll make an exception. I'm extremely impressed with the level of detail and the way you retained focus from start to finish. I didn't lose interest at any point during reading, props for that. You have the ability to portray this guy as a believable no-selling monster or someone with human frailties, depending on the mood. The plot itself is a little like a garden variety Bruce Lee movie, but your characters thoughts and commentary gave it a unique flavor. I’m guessing by the title that you’ve got long term plans. A good thing, because you’ll get a chance to use the next one. Result: Xin Xin Xiong d Eva VanLorne I read Van Lorne’s first, and though then that she’d be almost impossible to beat. Xiong managed to do so, and in retrospect, fairly easily. Props to both writers for entertaining pieces. SUMMARY - Michael Stevens d Jake Hix - Drake Coleman d Johnny Lexicon - Ember d Andi the Magnificent - Cyrus Skar d Valentine Burst - Clyde Walkins d LeStatt Knight - Seymour Almasy d Jonathon Winters - Twizzy d Reverend Blaze - Back Alley Brawler d Kash - Fatbadger d Ragnarok - Avatar William Gwynn d The British Bomber - Xin Xin Xiong d Eva VanLorne Brackets
