Bracket Final: Mrs S.!

14 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CHANDLER TSONDA V SALAOK Judge 1 Just a few brief comments before I give you guys my pick for this match. First, congratulations to the both of you on making it to the final round of a bracket that saw the likes of Deville, Spartan, Mercury, and many other talented handlers. You should both be very proud for making it this far. This is an interesting decision because on the one hand, Salaok got the full 3 RPs up. On the other hand, I thought Tsonda had the better quality. So which wins out here? Was Tsonda's quality enough to make up for having posted one less RP? To be honest, I thought both sets of RPs were lacking a bit in substance. The premises of both stories were simple enough: Salaok continued to focus on his character's past, his memories of his wife and child, and his issues with his brother. Tsonda seemed mainly focused on his de facto conquest of Deville in round four, while keeping in mind that he still had Salaok to deal with in order to move on. They were good stories, but in reading them I just had this sense of, "this is what they are, and that's it." I dunno. In any case, I particularly liked how Tsonda interspersed symbolism and allegory with the goings-on in his RPs. It worked very well and was complemented his story nicely. I thought Tsonda's writing was much more polished than Salaok's. Salaok, you have great talent. What you should work on is more carefully proof-reading your work, because there were several misspellings that unfortunately detracted from my overall enjoyment. But that's only a suggestion for improvement, and not the sole basis for my decision. Overall, I just liked Tsonda's stuff better, to the point where I believe he makes up for the lower numbers. Therefore, I give Tsonda the win. PICK: Chandler Tsonda d. Salaok Judge 2 Salaok- "Forgotten Love" First thing that I noticed when I read this rp was grammar. There were some bad grammar mistakes, sentences that were hard to understand and some that didn't make sense unless you kept reading. I know that everyone has mistakes on their rps, but when the competition is as far along as this one is, things like grammar can really take away from a good rp. I don't normally care much for grammar and tense, but on this piece it seems that it was somewhat rushed into being posted and never really got properly proof-read. I think you did a pretty good job in introducing the challenge that is ahead of Salaok. Although for such an emotive it was lacking descriptions of emotions. I don't know how it feels like to lose a pregnant wife so I want you to make me feel it. I didn't feel anything until the scene with the son. I thought that it was beautifully done and probably the best part of this piece. The first part of the piece I don't think should have been added in there as it just seems like a quick way to mention the tournament. It was not well done as it set up a mood for the piece which you completely changed after that part. This was not bad piece, but it wasn't a great one either. I thought you could have worked on the grammar and the mentioning of the tournament a little more making this piece seem a bit rushed. Oh, and when I read this piece, one word came to mind... "Highlander"! Chandler Tsonda- "Welcome To The Distraction" Very nice piece. No mistakes grammar-wise that detracted from the reading or that I actually cared about. You have a very nice grasp of the language with a few words that made me go 'wha?' but never actually going over the top. I enjoyed the parallel story of the king/prince and Chandler. This is a nice example of subtle "mentioning" of the match. The way he compared himself to the new man trying to get to the top was very good. I liked how he had Angelo as the king who finally got dethroned in last round but the king knowing that before long someone else would lead a "revolt" to dethrone him. The parts of the piece with him and Shari I thought could have used some more dialogue as I really wanted to get to know Shari. I only found out that she is a sexy woman with an atittude and a soft spot for celebs. I'm hoping you will revisit this character in later pieces since I think she is very interesting. Overall great piece. Salaok- "One chance at life" I gotta say, you took all of the things that I had a gripe with in the first rp and you fixed them all. Good job. There are no glaring mistakes this time and if there were, I was too into the story that I didn't notice them. You did good with the mentioning of the tourney and the opponent as it didn't feel rushed or awkward at all. This part of the story was much more interesting. The going back into the room to find clues was a pretty strong scene with the dead kid and the strong image of the crow outside the window. The pain of the betrayer being his brother was done well. I can't find many things wrong with this piece. Great job. Salaok- "In the time of need..." I have to say, I am dissapointed. The last two pieces were good but this one just falls down with all the momentum from your other pieces. It starts of with a dream sequence which was very well written and filled with emotion but it was just that, a dream sequence. After that he out of nowhere goes to her sister, who he had never even mentioned before. I thought this was the time for you to explore the hatred between Johnathon and Salaok but none of that is done. This feels like a third rp for the sake of a third rp. The ending was very weird as it felt like a 'To be Continued...' ending but it actually leaves you there hanging without any closure at all. It doesn't make me want to keep reading. Chandler Tsonda- "Welcome To The Obsession" This was an interesting piece. Again, any mistakes made I didn't care for since the story kept me so enthralled. I really liked this piece. It was fast paced(the switching between scenes) and interesting. The fact of the obsession Tsonda has with Deville is clearly shown and in a good way. It is interesting that in the first piece there was the story of the prince who overthrew the last king symbolizing his victory over Deville and now he literally does the same by taking over his mansion and his assets. The fact that we 'may' have seen Deville interrogating the man in there was a nice touch. Not much I can say that could make this better. Impressive. Pick: Chandler Tsonda wins. I picked Tsonda because of the overall consistency of his rps. Both are talented guys and they have shown it throughout the tournament but I think that Tsonda just had the upper hand in this one. Great story and I look forward to reading more of it. Salaok, great job to you as well. RESULT Chandler Tsonda d Salaok, 2 votes to 0 Chandler Tsonda is the Mrs S. Bracket Champion! Congrats and props to both gentlemen. Brackets
