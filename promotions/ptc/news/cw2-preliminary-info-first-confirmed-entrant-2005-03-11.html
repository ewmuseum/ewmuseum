CW2 Preliminary Info, First Confirmed Entrant!

11 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some random info: - CW2 - PTC's Second Annual Cruiserweight Singles Elimination Tournament. - Signups for CW2 will commence not long after I've done the work needed to get the RP board working on the 7OTB Website, sometime over the next 12-15 hours. - Signups will be anonymous. - The tournament will commence a not long after 7OTB wraps up (NOT BEFORE! I will hold it up if SOTB should for any reason run late.). - Characters entering must have a maximum weight of 225lbs. - There will be 32 places. Each PTC member fed is guaranteed a minimum of one spot should one or more members of that fed enter. I will not be doing the random Round 1 triple threats as we did last year, if you don't apply on time and get in, then I would be directing you to HSW's tournament, which will also be happening soon. So, if you intend to sign up, don't go out for long periods of time over the weekend, and miss out. It won't necessarily be first in best dressed, but it will be a deciding factor should there need to be one. - A limited number of Free Agents will be allowed to compete. Approximately 10, no more than that. - Round 1 will have a 1 RP limit, Rounds 2 through to the final round will have a 2 RP limit, and the final round will have a three RP limit. Each round will go for 6-7 days, with judging as quickly as possible after that. - The first confirmed entrant is CW1 winner, Seymour Almasy. Sean has confirmed that he will take a place in this year's event. So, in reality, there are 31 spots left. - Yes, its been around 4 months since GTT4 finished, and its actually a few weeks later than CW1 started this time last year. Time flies! - All CW1 RP's can be found in the archive forum down at the bottom of these forums. CW2 RP's will be done on the website.
