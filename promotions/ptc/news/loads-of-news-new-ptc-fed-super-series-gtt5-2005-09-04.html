Loads of News! (New PTC Fed, Super Series, GTT5)

4 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
4WW bows out As RJ announced on the General forum, 4WW have stepped out of PTC for the time being in order to focus on restructure. I wish them the best with that and hope that it won't be too long before they are happy with things again. RJ remains here on staff as an admin. Welcome to AWC PTC's newest member fed is AWC. AWC's owner, Pierre H., is a regular of these forums and has patiently waited for a spot for his fed. Check them out here: http://awc.supremepixels.com and please make them welcome. Some time ago I put in the "6 months open" rule. While AWC haven't been open 6 months yet, they have their shit together sufficiently enough to demonstrate that they can make it on their own, and thus aren't in danger of becoming the next one month wonder fed. Thus, the 6 month rule is waivered in this case. PTC Titles - Global Title has been vacant since Karina Wolfenden won it and vacated it. - With the departure of Nova's handler, the Extreme Title has joined the Global Title in being vacant. - Killer Pirates from the Outer Carribean are no longer active in PRIME, so the PTC Tag Titles are also vacant. Which brings me to.... Super Series I-PPV Super Series I will run immediately prior to GTT5 signups opening - that is, GTT5 signups will open a few days after the SS results are posted. RP'ing is expected to start in two weeks time and last for 7-10 days. The confirmed matches are as follows: Main Event - PTC Global and Extreme Title Match Scavenger Hunt Match for the vacant PTC Global and Extreme Titles Eligible Entrants in this match: - Karina Wolfenden (PRIME) - most recent PTC Global Champion - The Uber Judge (XUW) - June Top 10 #1 Place Getter - Hoyt Williams (PRIME) - July Top 10 #1 Place Getter - ??? (???) - August Top 10 #1 Place Getter - All Infinite Gauntlet Match Winners currently in PTC feds with the character they won with. (Emoticon - Global, Rich Rollins - GCW, The Mime - HSW) - Any former PTC Global or Extreme Champions currently in PTC feds with the same character who never received their rematch. - As The Uber Judge qualified twice as well as winning a guaranteed match, he will enter the match with the Extreme Title Belt in his possession, and will need to leave the match with it in order to keep it. - In Summary: Wolfenden vs Uber Judge vs Williams vs ??? vs Emoticon vs Rich Rollins vs The Mime vs ??. PTC Tag Team Championship - TBD. XUW vs PRIME - SURVIVAL BRAGGING RIGHTS 8 Man Tag Elimination Match Team XUW: Cereal Killer, woof woof, Scotty Spalzcutoff and Saij. vs Team PRIME: TBA. Global/Extreme Title Qualifier Matches Each member fed is invited to submit a match between two roster members. The winner of each match will compete in a cluster match at Super Series 2 against the PTC Global and Extreme Champions (to be held first quarter 2006) where both titles will be on the line. PRIME and XUW are exempt from this, as all team members of the winning team* of the Survival Bragging Rights match will compete in the cluster match. * Except for no-showers. WWA vs WWA TBA UWF vs UWF TBA Global vs Global TBA HSW vs HSW TBA GCW vs GCW TBA AWC vs AWC TBA If you are interested in representing your fed in one of the fed vs fed matches, contact your fed head, as I will be booking the matches based on what they say. All matches will have a 2 RP limit. All places in matches are subject to confirmation - i.e. if you see your name here and want to be in the match, contact me via PM to confirm your spot. If you don't contact me within 7 days then your spot is forfeit. Judging and Match Writing - Volunteers needed. August Top 10 Nominations for August Top 10 are now open and need to be in by end of Saturday September 10. They come to me from your fed head, so go and nag them to put your name in. As stated, the #1 placegetter in the August Top 10 will be offered a spot in the scavenger hunt match. Posted on the PTC forum by Argyle.
