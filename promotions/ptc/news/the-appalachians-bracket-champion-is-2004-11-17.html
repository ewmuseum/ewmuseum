The Appalachians Bracket Champion is....

17 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Craig Goat d Jimmy Bong, 5 votes to 0. Craig Goat will face Tarrant in the semi finals. Judges Comments At this stage it gets too close to call. And this is a classic bracket final. Both had strong stories, but I preferred the characterisation in the Goat pieces. Sorry to Bong, who wrote in my opinion almost equally impressive stuff. Pick: Goat Goats 1st- Well written , with plenty of information about the character and his past, concerning him and Nurse Wretched. Some of the dialogue got a bit dry during the argument, but it was quickly redeemed by the comedy that followed shortly after. Goats 2nd- Great continuation of the story of Goat and Nurse Wretched, and with Goats mother added in, it just made it all the more entertaining. Perfect combination of just about everything needed for a great roleplay. Bong’s 1st- Well written and entertaining for sure, but at times I think the humor was pushed a little too much. Bong’s 2nd- Just as the first, it was definitely entertaining, especially the ending , but as I said before I just feel the humor was pushed a little too hard at parts. Pick: Craig Goat Craig Goat RP 1 – A great roleplay that caught me from the start. The plot was worked out very well. I liked just about everything about this roleplay, since it had all it needed. An all but perfect relationship, with a complicated twist added to it. Also, I strongly believe you need to talk about wrestling and they also did that. The relevance was there, not too strong, but nice and subtle. 9/10 Craig Goat RP 2 – The story picks up where the first roleplay ended. I really was into the manipulating Nurse Wretched. The story was once again very good. This roleplay had the total package. Although I didn’t like sudden parts it was still pretty good overall. Unfortunately, there was no end to this story. 8.8/10 Jimmy Bong RP 1 – In my humble a character needs to be believable. If your character is a wrestler, fine. If your character is an agent for whatever organisation he’s working for, fine. But you can’t combine both. It’s an unbelievable combination. Not to forget to mention that both jobs require a lot of time and travelling. I just didn’t like it. If I put that aside the roleplay was fairly nice, but nothing too special. You can write well, Jimmy Bong, but you have to make more of a believable character. That does not mean your character has to be for real, because a character like Angelo Deville isn’t for real either but is believable. A bit more relevance wouldn’t hurt you either too. 7.3/10 Jimmy Bong RP 2 – It went from unbelievable to even more unbelievable. The plot was nice, but nothing special. The characters were superficial, once more nothing too special. Basically, you have great writing skills, but your characters aren’t half as good as good as your writing is. You can’t seem to think of a good character. The plots are fairly decent. But your main problem really is the character. You’re giving me the feeling he’s Superman. He’d been having two jobs as well, living a double life. The whole world sees his face and only he is believing that nobody identifies Clark Kent as Superman. This really hurt you, and your plots weren’t that good, but mostly your character. 7.0/10 Pick – Craig Goat, without any discussion. Sorry for Jimmy Bong who is a tremendous writer but my vote goes to Goat. If I were you I’d make my character a little bit more believable and you’d do even better than you are doing now. Good luck to both in the future. Both had entertaining and fun stories, both were enjoyable. But the sense of growth and the fun turns of events in Craig Goat's story really stood out on this one. The image of Nurse Wretched scouring herself in the shower trying to get the icky feelings off of her was absolutely vivid which helped contribute to my pick. Pick: Craig Goat This was a tough one. Goat and Bong's work both impressed me. Goat's work was more concise, but I appreciated the elaborate plot of Bong's RPs. I liked that Goat's focus was far more on the GTT itself, as after all, these are wrestlers competing in what's probably for both of them the biggest opportunities of their careers. I believe Goat was mistaken in some of his comments to Bong, though, as I'm pretty sure that Bong has wrestled before the tournament, as I think I recall him from the cruiserweight tournament earlier this year. If that was Bong that I'm remembering, he's improved ten-fold from then, which is all the more impressive. In the end, I have to give the slight edge to Craig Goat. Though Bong's RPs were very funny, by the middle of the second they were starting to grow just a bit tedious, whereas I think Goat had pacing and length down to a perfect tee, and I was at all times interested in what was going to happen next. Also, as I already mentioned, I liked the greater relevance to the tournament in Goat's RPs. Pick: Craig Goat Appalacians
