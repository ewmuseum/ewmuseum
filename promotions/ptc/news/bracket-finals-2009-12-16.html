WHEN Bracket Finals start THURSDAY, December 17th and run until SUNDAY January 3rd, 2010. WHAT You will have 2 weeks to post 2 RPs. There are no sandbagging limits and there are no rules that you have to post one on one day and one on another. The minimum RP count is 2 and the maximum is 2. In other words, you MUST post 2.
