Round 2 Results - Piglet

31 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SEMORE SMOKE v JOHN COVEL Judge 1 Semore Smoke: Both pieces were all over the place with too many things going on, which distracted me from the narrative. Also, the progression of the plot had me stupified as to how these events would happen in real life. I think I've watched enough Law and Order episodes to realize that there's no way any police department would wish a potential murder suspect luck in his upcoming wrestling matches. I know Ice T wouldn't, because he did New Jack Hustler. John Covel: The flashbacks to the minor-league incidents were detailed much better than Smoke's flashbacks to the PTC HQ conference, but I'm left wondering why Covel didn't have charges pressed against him and why the Sergeant and the victim were so nice to him. Also, the initial set-up of the meeting with Selig and the MLB reps felt rushed, with not enough hoops for Covel to jump through to get what he wanted. It all seemed rather easy, with more telling than there was showing. Nonetheless, Covel moves on with the more compelling story. Pick: Covel Judge 2 Semore Smoke: RP #1 - Decent roleplay. It furthered the story you were building in round one (Yes I actually did take the time to read it in order to understand where you were going with the two this round) and that is a good thing. The roleplay really bogged down, and lost a lot of flavor, for me when you got to the middle of it where he was explaining the whole time with Ms. Jones to them. It just came off very hokey and completely unbelievable. I’m no expert on the situations of being interrogated for murder but some how I doubt that if you left the information about going to her apartment out and they knew it, then you turned around and said it was only to protect these two people you’ve never met....yeah I don’t see them simply letting you walk out the door ten seconds later. It just came off...I don’t know pretty weak to be honest. Still over all the roleplay was solid. Not a bad read but something you’d certainly have to improve a great deal upon as the tournament progresses if you’re able to move on. John Covel: Rp #1 - Pretty solid roleplay. It is an interesting story with Covel attempting to get back into baseball after what he did to an umpire. I’m not sure how those things work out, the votes and stuff, but none the less it was pretty interesting to see how it was all played out. I especially liked the ending where you leave the reader wondering whether he is in or not, and this forces a read of your second roleplay. Pretty good job. There was the typical spelling and grammar errors you find in any roleplays but nothing that sorely stuck out. Semore Smoke: RP #2 - I’m sorry man but I just couldn’t get into this thing at all. You jumped around from thing to thing, and you just had so many things that didn’t make sense in the least. If they found a weapon that they believed would be a murder weapon Smoke would be arrested on the spot, not wished good luck in a tournament. Further more, even if he was out on bail, there is NO WAY he’d be allowed to fly around and wrestle with him being the primary suspect in a murder investigation. Sure this might sound like nitpicking and silly but it honestly takes away from the whole story for me. The writing was solid, very few spelling errors or grammar issues that left me re-reading a sentence more than once, but the over arching story is just messed up with these inconsistencies. Not to mention, even if he knows he is innocent, Smoke isn’t going to be NEARLY as calm as you make him appear to be with this hanging over his head. It simply just doesn’t work well for me. Other things, such as the ending, really helped to make it very hard to get into this roleplay. So he did it, or said he did, but why would he instantly trust this man he has supposedly never met? Why would he try and include him? None of this makes any sense to me and just further clouds the roleplay for me. Sorry man but it seems like you just didn’t plan your work out very well. You seem to have a knack for writing, and probably could have pulled this story off much better with a little more thought and planning. This story, to me anyway, comes off very rushed and as if you didn’t really take into consideration much of anything while writing it. Sorry if it sounds like I’m being to harsh, as I really don’t want it to seem that way, but this was something you could have done much better with and I’m hoping to see you improve upon this by taking such things into consideration in the future. John Covel: Rp #2 - Honestly not quite as good as your first piece but still fairly solid none the less. I really enjoyed the first half or so where you went back and showed some of the things that Covel went through directly after the attack, his conversations, and what not. Pretty solid piece. I enjoyed it. This one could have been a lot closer for me than it was but in the end I just felt like Covel did a much better job of telling his story, keeping me interested, and making me want to see more of it. Smoke appears to be a guy who has some talent but just needs to take more time polishing up his stories and working on them. Good luck to Covel in the next round. Pick: Covel Judge 3 Semore Smoke: RP #1 Nothing bores me more than an interrogation scene. Usually in these, nothing is happening but mediocre dialogue, and I hate to say it, but Smoke's lives up to this. The Agents seem really dull, and Smoke's tone of voice carries a pseudo tough guy attitude that makes me roll my eyes. Something you need to work on is how you start your sentences. In the first flashback (haven't finished the RP yet, still reading), pretty much EVERY sentence starts out with "Semore did this...Joanna did that...He did this...". It's hard to read. RP #2 The people in this RP are all out of bad-acting school. Ever seen "They Live"? Imagine those guys acting in this. This is some terribly stale dialogue that is mixed with horrible stereotypes. Nothing interesting is happening, and the characters are either too-knowing or not enough. Like this line: "Can I have everyone please step outside while we begin our search? I promise it won’t take too long so you won’t have to spend too much time outside in the dreary cold rain!" You need to think of a fresh idea...not "Getting tried for murder, but not really" mixed with "blue-collar working wrestling" and "family member with shame over self crime, but still lovable because it wasn't his fault." John Covel RP# 1 You write fluidly, which works well. I feel actual compassion for Covel. Though I don't know his crime (it hasn't been revealed yet up to this point where I am), I am expecting that he didn't do it. With simplicity and directness you illustrate some that seems so real and yet, surprisingly, really fresh in e-fedding. Even though I'm sure this has been done, it feels like it hasn't, because John has that human touch to him. And then the crime is revealed. I love it. Now, I don't mean it like that, but the way it was scripted...emotions getting the better of him. This RP is, so far, one of the best I've read, and you barely (if at all) mentioned wrestling. If there is anything I have to criticize...well...that'd be tough. It flowed really well, everything was as long as it needed to be, and it seemed every fact was relevant to the story. I had a fun time reading it, and good job. Rp #2 Good idea to keep me hanging. You already have me keeping on reading. I liked where it went, the first part, but I hope you explain why the ref dropped the charges. Seems kind of odd and Deus Ex Machina-esque on a certain level. This is an excellent dynamic you're working with: the young man who's too immature for his own good, but looks at himself as the adult. You capture it well through the narrative...the line that leads up to the talk was written pretty well. A great way to conclude. I love how all the emotion builds up to him being banned and then climaxes with the inevitable "yes". While your RPs dont even come close to wrestling, I don't care. They were awesome. Lots of fun to read, and I can tell that good things will come out of it...as long as this doesn't lead to burnout. Pick: Covel Judge 4 Semore Smoke: In both RP's the descriptive writing was jerky, filled with needless detail and repetition of words. Much of it didn't really add to the story, and seemed to be there just for the sake of it. Some sections would be better if condensed a little. On a positive note, you write excellent dialogue. I wonder if maybe you're more used to script style rps? John Covel: Read through your first RP without stopping to take notes at all. The story flowed really well and hooked me early on. Good insight into the character and your writing shows emotional depth. In order to progress much further, you really need to relate your story more to wrestling and GTT in particular. The issue of relevance has brought down more than a few greats in this tournaments past. In fact, had your opponent been just a little stronger, I would be voting against you this round. Semore Smokes story was a little bland and horrifically implausable (I'm more than willing to suspend belief when an story calls for it, but in the context of your RP and setting, you went too far). Your dialogue is very strong, but the rest of your writing needs a lot of work. It could be improved with some mentoring. John Covel's was a well written and interesting piece. Could go far if the issue of relevence is sorted out. Pick: John Covel Result John Covel d Semore Smoke, 4 votes to 0 SUMMARY - John Covel d Semore Smoke, 4 votes to 0 - Clinton Sage d Salaok via no-show - Jacob McKail d Trashcan Man via no-show - Violence Jack enjoyed a bye.
