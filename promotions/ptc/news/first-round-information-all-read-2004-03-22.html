Maximum Number of Roleplays per player per round: 2 Roleplaying starts: This coming Saturday. Countdown Clock Link Cutoff time for First Roleplay: Friday week. Countdown Clock Link Roleplaying ends: Saturday week. Countdown Clock Link Countdown clock runs on server time. I have no idea what this is, but there's plenty of time between start and end time for two roleplays. If someone wishes to translate that to something that US-based people understand, feel free. No Sandbagging - see first RP cutoff time below. No linking RP's. Post them here. I don't have any objection to them being archived in your bio in your home fed after the round is over. At this point in time, no round 2 playins. There was enough time to sign up before now. With the amount of triple threats and confirmations, hopefully none will be needed. If there happens to be a double/triple no-show for a match, then the person on the other side of the match is getting a bye into the subsequent round. Anyone wishing to surrender their spot in the tourney can do so by PM'ing me. If you are in a triple threat match you will not be replaced. If you are in a singles match, I will replace you with someone from a triple threat match in your bracket, providing your withdrawal is done longer than 24 hours prior to starting roleplaying.
