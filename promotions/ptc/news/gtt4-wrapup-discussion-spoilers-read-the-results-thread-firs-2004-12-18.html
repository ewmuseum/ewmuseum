GTT4 Wrapup / Discussion (spoilers - read the results thread first)

18 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First Prize: As winner GTT4, Andy is to nominate a charity of his choice to Buddy D., who has generously offered to donate $US100.00 to this charity in Andy's name. Second Prize: As highest place member of a PTC fed with the character entering the tournament, Latch Key Kid (Mike) will square off against PTC Global Champion Seymour Almasy (Sean) at a later date for a shot at the Global Title. Third Prize: Shadow and Dave Harley earned shots at the Extreme Title. Fourth Prize: Andy needs to come up with a Poser description for Dr. Curiosity and provide it to me. One of PTC's premier poser artists will be rendering the images and splash screen. (he can out himself if he wishes :D ). Made it all worthwhile :) Mini-Stats Total Roleplays over all rounds (including Tex Chattanooga): 429 Round 1 No-shows: 42 So, four months later, its all said and done for another year. PTC's next tournament will be a small scale event similar to the Cruiserweight Tournament earlier this year. Look for GTT5 in the second half of 2005!
