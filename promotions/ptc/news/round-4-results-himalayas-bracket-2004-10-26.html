Round 4 Results: Himalayas Bracket

26 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Latch Key Kid advances. Karina Wolfenden d Johnny Lexicon (3 votes to 0) to advance. Bracket Final match: Latch Key Kid vs Karina Wolfenden Judging Comments Latch Key Kid ugh he got fired!! Poor kid.. I am glad to see he is still in this after all these rounds. It is fun to read his refreshing rps. Like I said they are fun to read and makes my job as a judge enjoyable. Only one truly visible mistake towards the end were he says Persia is from XUW instead of Uwf but other then that it was a easy read. I would have liked to see one more RP out of Latch Key kid to keep the bar raised. It is good to see that he is going to go to the next level of the GTT4. If anything he can hold his head up high as he has made lemonade out of the lemons that were handed to him in the first round. Lexicon vs Wolfenden Karina Wolfenden- Beginning Great RP, lots of description in there. I also liked the way some of the words in there were emphasized, all in all a great RP. Karina Wolfenden- Middle Another great piece by Karina, still liked the idea of emphasizing certain words in the RP to actually get a feel for what the characters in the RP are feeling. Johnny Lexicon- The Warrior's Soul This is a great piece of work by Lexicon even though I felt he used to many big words that was drowning out the RP. And he emphasized more on something else then his opponent. Johnny Lexicon- The Warrior's Heart Once again very detailed which is good and very good Character emphasis. But not enough talk about his opponent or GTT4. Pick: Karina Wolfenden K-wolf RP1 Now here is a girl that has watched the movie how to lose a guy in 10 days and took it to heart. you really have to cheer for the guy that after watching her about kill the punching bag still asks her out on a date or at least tries to . IN the end you knew she would at least have to try to give him a shot. maybe men need to read this more and take notes on what to do. Just remember now a day mostly being this persistent can get your butt shot or tossed in jail for harassment. Good job on setting the bar with the first RP. it will give Johnny Lexicon something to work for. RP2 your realize this guy is going to be a slight sleaze bag from the get go. using K-wolf for his own career. Thank the gods she is smarter then that. and she is the ultimate ice queen, again I think she has watched the movie and taken to heart. again setting that bar just a little higher for Lexicon to try to met and beat. This is her game to win or lose. Rp3 again shows why K-wolf is still in the GTT4. The guy gets handed his butt and learns a hard lesson is trying to use a woman. There is not greater wrath then a woman scorned. As soon as she figured out pass the hope that it wasn't what she was thinking it is. She showed him that she isn't a stepping stone. Most women will like that she totally kicked his butt in the end and put him in his place. Again she raising that bar. Johnny Lexicon RP1 Good solid rp but honestly not my favorite RP of this round that I have read. He reaches for the bar that K-wolf set and almost meets it. He shows that the heart of the warrior is strong and the fight will be a good one. RP2 Interesting to say the lease. One more RP would of maybe put him even with K-wolf he did a good try but this is more of a do type of thing. There is more to this then the thin line of what he paints it to be. Pick: K-wolf K-Wolf: Overall, a great 3 roleplay series. All of which in this series had continued from one another with a constant flow involved with them. The emotions of each tied together nicely, though the titles were bland, not that it makes a difference, great writing overall. Johnny Lexicon: Two roleplays produced, each of which had great use of his writing talents, each one well written. The themes with one another had worked well and showed a great amount of talent in what he did. The odd grammar error I believe I caught, other then that great job. Pick: Karina Wolfenden I'd say this because the characters three roleplays beat the two of Lexicon, both very good at what they wrote. KW had the edge with the great flow of the series, where I felt like there was no conclusion with Lexicon. Good job to you both! Himalayas
