Round 2 Results: Appalacians Bracket

30 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seymour Almasy d Darek DeAngleo (3 votes to 0) to advance Joey Vendetti advances. Craig Goat d Jenna Ryder (2 votes to 0) to advance Brian Calvin d Gonzalo Rey (2 votes to 1) to advance Fnord d El Serpenti (2 votes to 0) to advance Steven Caldera advances. Jimmy Bong d Luna Morbid (2 votes to 1) to advance Jamar Gordo advances. Judges comments will be edited in later. I have them at home in my spreadsheet. Round 3 Appalacians bracket matches: Seymour Almasy v Joey Vendetti Craig Goat v Brian Calvin Fnord v Steven Caldera Jimmy Bong v Jamar Gordo Round 2 Judging Comments Almasy vs DeAngleo Almasy*Well it's obvious why he's the PTC Champ. Not only extremely well written, but fast paced as well, which is always good. Nice balance of description and dialogue in both rps, especially the second one. DeAngleo*God knows that I'm a huuuuge description whore, so both rps were very enjoyable. The only thing holding it back was that it had tendencies to drag at some points and some noticeable spelling errors. Pick: Almasy Comment: This contest went down to pure mechanics to determine the winner, as both competitors had great works, but also suffered from different grammatical problems. Almasy constantly shifted from past to present tense, while Darek had many spelling errors throughout his work. While I struggled to decide which was worse, the spelling errors are easier to be fixed, and show that that the writer did not proofread the work and thus probably rushed through it. Because of this, Seymour defeats Darek by a hair. Pick: Seymour Almasy Third Judge comments TBA, winner Almasy Joey Vendetti Vendetti 1: Great concept, roleplaying as fans at an XUW show! Very neat idea indeed. Well written with a good flow and tempo. I enjoyed reading this piece a lot. Vendetti could go far. Good luck, I'll be reading. Jenna Ryder vs Craig Goat Jenna 1: Er, what happened here? Seemed to me like a hardcore erotic story with Goat's name tossed in there. Well written yes, but a little too graphic. The comparisons with Ms. Jameson automatically sprung to mind, and aside from the hair colour/length, well. Use your imaginations. Jenna 2: Well, less hardcore... But this fella certainly knows his porn. Well written again, but this had zero tournament relevance. This isn't a "Write your way through a porn stars career", it's an e-wrestling tournament. Goat 1: Good transition to the second round, he acknowledges the fact that he won a match (Which is extremely rare for him!) and gives views on his next opponent. Not quite as good as round 1's piece IMO, but still a solid roleplay. Goat 2: Well written with subtle moments of comedy genius. The roleplay displays a lot of different levels to the character, and he clearly has a lot more sides to offer. Ones I am looking forward to reading. My Pick: Craig Goat - His roleplaying style is an entertaining read, and the character is clearly multi-dimensional, and I want to see more of him. Jenna's was, well, porn. And as much as I like porn, wasn't good enough to beat Goat. Ryder*All I can say is....interesting. In a good way. Anytime you can include a line such as, "Fuck him like you mean it!" in an rp, it will always grab attention. Good length, good pacing, both were very entertaining in my opinion. Goat*I liked his writing style better than Jenna's and both his rps were also very well paced. Perhaps not as interesting as Jenna's, but then again, I am a guy, lol. Still, his rps were just as entertaining, making this my toughest decision of the round. Winner: Goat Gonzalo Rey vs Brian Calvin Rey’s RPs: Very well done with the various scenes in each rp. It was like a nice half dozen small rps in one. The ambience was good and the length seemed sound. I don’t really have any tips besides just keep doing stuff like this. I really liked your style. Calvin’s RPs: I also really liked your style Calvin and thought that had you been competing in most other matches I judged that you would have won. However, this is just one of those instances where a judge just prefers an rp over another equally good one. You had a very solid ambience all the way through, your second rp being superb story wise. I personally wish both you and Rey could move on but since only one can I have to go with Rey. Sorry. Pick: Gonzalo Rey Gonzalo’s 1st roleplay- Very confusing introduction with very simply dialogue. I had to read over it a few times to understand., but this line still left me very confused. “I had been isolated for a few days but there was only one thing on my mind: success. It did not matter what it had do with because it is a broad statement that I wished to use in every aspect of my deteriorating life.” Is success the broad statement? As I read on it began to jump around more. It jumps from the talk of getting victory to him needing to get his son back without any real connection. A few of the sentences were broken up into their own individual paragraphs when I feel they could have been placed together and formed one. Don’t really see why they were broken up. One thing I did like though is the introduction to the characters history for those who aren’t familiar to him. As I read on, the writing started to get a bit stronger and made more sense. I feel the second part could have started off a bit stronger with more description pertaining to his surroundings. I’m also not big on “a lot” as a detail as everyone has their own opinion as to what “a lot” is, especially when it’s concerning two things like money and drugs. I feel there wasn’t much to offer in terms of dialogue. You can only say “he says” so many times before it starts to get dull. Try spicing it up with some detail as to how the person said it and what they did while they said it. The writing started to get choppy and confusing again after the son‘s meeting with his father, mainly in the part where they spoke of the mother’s “chalk dust”. I also noticed that the detail was very bland, with simple descriptions such as “sadly”. There are so many ways a person could look when they are sad, it’s hard to picture just how Angelo looks. I feel putting some detail in there about Angelo’s body language could help intrigue the reader some in the future. Especially since it seems there wasn’t the slightest hint of Gonzalo being upset besides him wiping some sweat from his brow, yet somehow Angelo concluded that his father was upset. It really hurt the quality of the roleplay since once again I had to go back and read to understand just what was going on since it seemed all of sudden they skipped over to talking about Angelo taking off with his father and then to wrestling. The self promotion at the end I felt it could have gone without and been a whole separate roleplay. It seems to be added on there just for lengths purpose. Though it was decently written, I just feel it didn’t add much to the roleplay besides a few pages. I felt the ending of the father/son scene probably would have made a more fit ending for this bit than the self promotion at the end. Also noticed this was part one, but it seemed from the second roleplay there was nothing to follow it. Gonzalo’s 2nd roleplay- The introduction was a bit stronger this time around, but I feel the story behind the family didn’t need to be told again. Just like before though things were a bit jumpy. It literally jumped from the mention of how drug obsessed his wife’s family is, to the GTT, with like zero connection. Though it was sort of like what the roleplay was based around, distractions I’m just going to assume that one was intentional and I applaud the writer for that. It puts the reader right into his shoes. Once again though I feel the first bit was enough for a roleplay, especially since it seems like the following parts seem to be there just to add on length. I liked the story, I just don’t feel it belongs in the same roleplay as the first part. I feel the rest following the first half belonged in a roleplay of it’s own. As far as quality is concerned I feel this roleplay could use some of the same improvements the first one could, as far as dialogue and detail. I also don’t why some of the things he said to his son were italicized, yet others weren’t, since the italicized bits didn’t seem to have any more significance over the unitalicized bits. Calvin’s 1st roleplay- The introduction wasn’t too strong, given it started with the simple stuff. However, the introduction to the character was awesome. It lets you know what others know about him, and what they don’t. Then it got into real depth and focused in on one of Calvin’s biggest weaknesses, his alcoholism. The flashback was amazingly written, and was transitioned into and out of well. The ending was a bit rushed though, and though it has yet to be continued, I felt it could have ended a little bit more smooth if the last line had been eliminated, or at least explained why a shadow of a person could make him say such a thing. Calvin’s 2nd roleplay- Smooth transition over from the first roleplay to the second. One thing I didn’t understand though is why Brian was thinking something like ‘Brian was alone in his home with a damn maniac…’ to himself. However, as serious as this roleplay was intended to be, it also squeezed in the occasional humor line, such as … Intruder – 2 Calvin – 0 This made for an enjoyable read. The bit where he found out who the intruder was, was also enjoyable and very well written out. It explains just how he knows the guy, which people sometimes tend to leave out. I also love how certain words were emphasized to get the point of this guy barging in across. It certainly added to the drama of things, while hooking me in. The ending to it all was great, though once again I’m still confused as to why the ending line was there, though it’s a strong line I just don’t understand why Calvin is thinking this after all he just went through., which really didn’t seem to relate to the line besides the alcoholism. Pick: Brian Calvin Both good writers, you can tell they have quality and experience. Howevever, I chose Calvin cause his story was more intresting, and just a better read. There were alot of things that just contradicted Rey's theory .. he goes on about how his wife's family wanted to be the next gotti's and sell drugs and what not and had him do it because of his knowledge of being able to fly a plane and what not ... But then the character he wrote as was portrayed just as much as bad guy as well ... but then said how he needed to get his son back from his wife .... Kinda like Depp from Blow wanting to get his daughter back from his ex-wife. Never the less, in both cases, the son should go to CPS :P Anyhoo, a more intresting read, Calvin won. Both showed they knew what they were doing, so it just came down to the better read. Winner = Calvin. Fnord vs El Serpenti Fnord 1: Powerful and emotive, and long. But well written enough to keep me interested. But I got to the end and it was like, 2 completely different pieces of writing. The last few paragraphs were no match for the opening story. And they were very loosely linked. I'm not sure how relevant the bulk of it was. It was mostly a well written story (VERY well written), with a roleplay attached to the end. Well written though. Fnord 2: Were there theme words in this round? Was it "destiny"? I found this piece incredibly hard to read, and the word destiny has lost all meaning to me. I saw it too many times in a piece I just felt uncaptivated by. Where was the flair and panash of the sleekly written majority of the first roleplay? Serpenti 1: Decent enough. It ambled along nicely, but was without a real purpose from what I could gather. It flowed well, and I feel no worse for reading it, but no better either. Fairly average, will need to improve to advance. Serpenti 2: Again, decent enough. Fairly choppy in a lot of places and things switched around a lot. It dragged a bit longer then I'd hoped. Decent, but nothing great. My Pick: Tough call, but I go with Fnord. Only for the brilliance of the story section in roleplay one. He'll need to improve the consistency if he wants to move further in this tournament. Serpenti*The pacing of these rps seem to get better and better as I go, and this was no exception as well. Interesting character, few spelling mistakes other than the intentional ones. Two more very good rps, no real big complaint Fnord*Very good pacing. Descriptions were brief when they had to be, which is a very good thing. I also enjoyed Wesley's little rant at the end of the second rp. Another very hard decision here. Winner: Fnord Jimmy Bong vs Luna Morbid Comments: This was a tough one. Jimmy Bong definitely has great comedic writing ability, while Luna has mastered the visual aspects of writing. However, ultimately it seemed that Luna was a bit more focused on the tournament itself in her content, while Bong started to stray off subjectly and tried to fill his second roleplay with as many jokes as he could... and then mention Luna merely twice. Pick: Luna Morbid Luna Morbid Review A Dangerous Woman The roleplays with characters training and such don't really do to much for me, seeing as it's just being used by seemingly everybody and isn't all that unique. On the other side though it was good for the scenario it was in. The fight with the two guys was cool. The somewhat out of body thing with the bath scene was nice, showed more personality for the character. This roleplay was pretty good. Didn't shock me, but it was good. Training Day The senses in this promo are greatly described. I could feel the rain as I read it. Or maybe my arms fell asleep I don't know lol. I can see the relationship, or should I say devotion, that Luna has towards Sylus is very, very deep in this roleplay. You always have your character training though don't you? lol. Anyway, not a bad roleplay, I think you weren't complete into it emotionally, but it was good. Not spectacular, but good. Jimmy Bong Review(Winner) Certificate to Harm Parts I and II Well, this match is going to be the most difficult to judge. I mean you have your great humour and Luna has the whole death ninja thing going on. Humour versus seriousness is one of the hardest things to choose over in my opinion. I have to give the win to Bong though, because I feel his stories have more potential than Luna's in the tournament. Another reason is to because I believe that there was actually method to your madness and wasn't just spontaneously written. Pick: Jimmy Bong Both writers produced good quality work with good character development. The only thing that worried me is that while both wrote in a creative way, neither really had the terribly original story-lines. In fact, Bong's was similar to Dr Curiosity's work last round, I am not by any means suggesting the work was plagiarised, however. I thought Bong's work was the more original of the two. A good close match up. Bong the win. Pick: Bong Appalacians
