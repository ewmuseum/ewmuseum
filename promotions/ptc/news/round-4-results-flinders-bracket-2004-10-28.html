Round 4 Results: Flinders Bracket

28 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kasper Sky d Token Weed (2 votes to 1) to advance. Shadow d Jonathan Sail (3 votes to 0) to advance. Bracket final matchup: Kasper Sky vs Shadow Judging Comments Sky vs Weed Kasper Sky's work was fantastic; not INCREDIBLY long so my attention was held throughout the whole thing, and both of his rps were very well written. Token Weed's one rp was decent, but I expected much more. Kasper Sky in a landslide. Pick: Kasper Sky Kasper Sky v. Token Weed KASPER SKY Title: Eternal: The End Author: Kasper Sky Date: 10 - 21 - 04 Content: 8/10 Entertainment: 8.5/10 Relevance: 8.5/10 Setting and Description: 9/10 Deductions: 0 ________________________________ (Total Marks) 34/40 (Total Base %) 85% Explanation: The author does welll in keeping suspense as a main part of the mood and tone which is a nice surprise. People tend to fill their Promos with a lot of descritpion but seem to forget that the description can help creat a certain mood. The intro to the ro had that suspense to it, which i thought was well done. All in all a good RP here. Title: Eternal: The End, 2 Author: Kasper Sky Date: 10 - 23 - 04 Content: 9/10 Entertainment: 9.5/10 Relevance: 8.5/10 Setting and Description: 9/10 Deductions: 0 ________________________________ (Total Marks) 36/40 (Total Base %) 90% Explanation: The emotion was great, it worked well with the suspenseful tone created at the beginning once again by the author. I felt that this promo may have been a little rushed, but it did not suffer in any way. It read well from the start to finish and I really enjoyed it. TOKEN WEED Title: A Meeting With The Mob Author: Token Weed Date: 10 - 22 - 04 Content: 9/10 Entertainment: 9.5/10 Relevance: 8/10 Setting and Description: 9/10 Deductions: 0 ________________________________ (Total Marks) 35.5/40 (Total Base %) 88.75% Explanation: Great description from beginning to end. I really liked the way he played the thug/gang bangger theme, but with a twist. The ball, the story, everything down to the californian way of talking was pretty darn good. I was hooked at the start and thought it was a great ride. Pick: Token Weed (88.75% average) over Kasper Sky (87.5% average) ~Token wins based on the percentiles, but my hat to Kasper for doing one hell of a job here. Kasper Sky -Vs- Token Weed Sky 1: Eternal: The End Pretty decently told. He seemed to be almost trying too hard in some places, and over-described a number of plot moments that could have done with moving the story along instead of slowing it down. Decent enough, but didn't inspire me greatly. Sky 2: Much nicer than the first piece. Flowed a lot better and wow! What a killer hook at the end... (No pun intended!) Enjoyed this piece a lot more than the first. And Am itching to read the following piece. Weed 1: Well this was... different. Superb opening I thought it was going to go somewhere really interesting. I was left disappointed, as it turned into a barrage of swearing, gangsters and drugs with no real direction or purpose. Overall: Weed's was well written, but didn't seem to go anywhere or show me anything of any interest about the character other than he's a drug dealing gangster. Seemed a little one dimensional. Sky showed a lot more character depth in his pieces and they served a purpose. He made me care about what happens in the next roleplay, while Weed's I'd probably set the VCR. Pick: Kasper Sky Sail vs Shadow One of the closest matches that I have had to judge, maybe ever. Both handlers know they've written very good rps, with fantastic ideas, and this is a tough decision. I picked Shadow by a hair, only because I really enjoyed her rps and she managed to get one more great one up. That showed commitment, and, as the quality was about equal in my eyes, that pushed her over the edge. Pick: Shadow JOHNATHAN SAIL Title: Questions, Questions, Questions... The Dialogue between the two seemed a little forced at parts but all in all flowed very smoothly and sounded completely realistic. The author has a way of keeping the reader hooked line for line. Title: L'horreur Sympathique Author: Shadow Date: 10 - 22 - 04 Content: 9.5/10 Entertainment: 8/10 Relevance: 8.5/10 Setting and Description: 8.5/10 Deductions: 0 ________________________________ (Total Marks) 34.5/40 (Total Base %) 86.25% Explanation: Loved the story being told, but felt it was carried out longer than it needed to be in some places. Title: All The World's a Stage Author: Shadow Date: 10 - 23 - 04 Content: 9/10 Entertainment: 8.5/10 Relevance: 8.5/10 Setting and Description: 10/10 Deductions: 0 ________________________________ (Total Marks) 36/40 (Total Base %) 90% Explanation: Bravo, amazing finish to the series of RPs, man that's the best part about judging these series RP's is the ending payoff and the author smacks dab on with this one. Just go read it. Pick: Shadow (88.333% average) over Johnathan Sail (88.125% average) ~ The extra RP helps give Shadow a more dominant win. Sail's handler didn't make best use of the 10 days RP time and chose drinking over competing on the last day, allowing Shadow's handler to get the jump and go through. Three a piece and this might have been different, but then again it might not. I'm sorry I won't get to see where Sail's story ultimately ends up, but the quick and the dead is the way of this game. Pick: Shadow Flinders
