Would you like a CHALLENGE for GTT6?

9 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In talking with [name withheld], I've decided to push the boundaries to see exactly what a person will do for a GTT6 spot. Therefore, [name withheld] has given me the ability to give away ONE (1) Guaranteed entry into GTT6, no questions asked. But, there's a catch... (isn't there always?) THE CONTEST For those of you that have played before and want a challenge to those of you that never have, but have always been interested in entering, I'm going to make your dream come true. I'm going to give you a spot in GTT6, no questions asked. But, you have to do something for me. You're not going to enter as a character of your choosing. Instead, you're going to allow me to determine the character you use three (3) days before GTT6 starts. That's right. You're going to enter into the tournament blind. You will have the rough idea of a character, and it will be up to you to fill in the details of said character. Understand, this gimmick will not be an all encapsulating gimmick. There will be plenty of room for your own ideas, and the character. THE DETAILS To enter, you will come up with a biography for Sammy Brown, a career midcarder who keeps a dayjob as a New York City doorman. He's 6'3, 290lbs, and in his early 40's. 500 words or less, please. You will post said biography in this thread. From there, I will decide ONE person to be Pete's GTT6 Guaranteed Entry. Three days before the scheduled and confirmed start for GTT6, you will recieve a PM from me containing your character information. You will not reveal it before the tournament to anyone. Doing so will force you out of GTT6, and the spot will be given via random /ask. In the event you win GTT6, and/or would like to pursue the character in a fed, I have no problem with that. The moment the PM is sent, the character becomes your character to do with as you please. In the event you win this competition and then no-show GTT6, you will not be allowed to enter a PTC competition ever again. Period. And if you're within a three day's walk from Cleveland, Ohio, I'll murder you myself. If you're farther away than that, and somehow, someway need a kidney someday, and I'm the only person that matches, you can forget it, bastard! In the event I don't deliver within the three day window, you're free to use your own character. Also, if I need a kidney, feel free to taunt me by peeing on me, saying "REMEMBER THAT, MOTHERFUCKER!? REMEMBER HOW THAT FELT? DO YOU?! WELL FUCK YOU!" Competition starts now. Competition ends... eventually. Aaaand begin. No questions. Just write.
