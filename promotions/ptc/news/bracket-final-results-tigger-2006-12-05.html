Bracket Final Results - Tigger

5 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JASON SNOW V IBLIS Judge 1 Iblis: Strong story, and the style it's presented in is refreshing. There's really not much to say besides Good Work. I was hooked throughout the entire thing, and read both pieces in one sitting because of it. Great. Jason Snow: Been a fan of your writing since the start of GTT6. Haven't posted a bad RP yet, and this round was no exception. Compelling work with these, I'm interested in what's going to happen to Anthony in the next round. Pick: Jason Snow. Judge 2 Jason Snow 1 Solid piece of writing. I thought it had too much focus on the supporting characters, and I didn't get to see much of Snow himself. Not sure what the purpose of the song lyrics in the middle was, really. The bits with Dugaze were my favorite, but I also enjoyed the reminiscing to when Raven was alive. Very evocative moments there. Overall I felt it could have said more. Other than Dugaze, nothing much really happened, and I'm still not a hundred percent sure where he fits in. Iblis 1 Very well written, though there is a slight flair for the melodramatic, and occasional run on sentences that could have done with shortening. An interesting story, but how it ties in with GTT and all that I do not know. I'll be interested to see where this goes in the second part. Iblis 2 To be honest, I was not really a fan of most of this. The story was interesting enough the first time around, but to retell the exact same tale twice just bored me, as very little new was added to what the son had seen. As such, most of this was just filler for me, with the only interest at the end, with what happened to the father's body, and then the connection to the descendant who I guess is wrestling. I'll tip my hat; I think this is the first wrestling 'zombie' we've had, and it's a good ending, and yes, you're a fine writer, but repeating the same story over has hurt my opinion of this pair of roleplays. Jason Snow 2 You have a lot in common with your opponent, in that whilst writing very well, and bringing me into a world, you've left me with a load of misgivings. Again, the story was so much about Dugaze and the criminals that I was left wondering if you just didn't really have any story left for Snow, and so wrote about someone else entirely. Apart from dropping his name on occasion, there was no real link given during the roleplays between Dugaze's lot and Snow's domestic worries. As such, the more interesting parts were punctuated by me asking to myself, 'So what?'. I think the scene with Alyssa was overdone. I liked what you tried to do, but having such a long list of body parts made it seem more clinical and scholarly to list all the parts, instead of the passion and intensity of emotion that you were (I expect) aiming for. And, overall, it dragged a little. Decision. After the second from Iblis, I thought he was out of the running, but the second from Snow did more to alienate him from me than I could have expected. So much storyline going on and all we've got from the man himself is that he still loves his dead wife, which was kinda easy to spot early on. I don't mean to be cruel or unkind to the two writers, because they're both clearly excellent in terms of dialogue, narration and setting up some interesting characters. I'd say that Iblis had the more memorable story, and it is certainly something I that I think could exist and do very well outside of the confines of GTT and e-wrestling. Snow's, whilst not hitting the same high notes as Iblis, had more consistency and didn't shoot itself in the foot by writing the same roleplay twice. So, a tricky decision. In the end though I'll give it to Jason Snow. I would much rather his storyline start to touch upon, y'know, the actual character entered into the tournament, but there's a lot of classy writing there (such as the opening to the first roleplay). If you can just rein in the focus and make his life a little more interesting, and perhaps link things up a little more (or just make the links clearer) with the rest of the world you've created then I'd enjoy it a more than I did. Well played to Iblis though. A very interesting character history, and had the second had more new information or settings then you'd definitely have taken it on my card. Pick – Jason Snow (by a hair) Result Jason Snow d Iblis, 2 votes to 0 BRACKET JUDGES Max Andy Apologies for limited amount of judges, however... there were originally five asked to judge, but three had to drop their assignment for various valid reasons, and rather than wait another couple of days replacing them, I'm going with this. Tigger Bracket Champion Jason Snow Tigger
