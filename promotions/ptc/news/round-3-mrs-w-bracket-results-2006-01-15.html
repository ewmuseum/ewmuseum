Round 3: Mrs W. Bracket Results

15 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MICHAEL STEVENS V KIMBUSA Judge 1 Stevens: On the Run: Part 1 Surprise surprise, Stevens has managed to do it again. The story as whole was a good read despite a few errors here and there as far as grammar and spelling goes. The constant struggle to live or die throughout his series is like being on a roller coaster ride from hell, complete exhilarating and stomach churning. The proverbial fall from the top into the bottom of the barrel is an old over-used story, but he managed to bring a refreshing angle to it. He is consistent with the whole storyline from beginning to end and seems focused on his goal. Bonus for him. However, at the same time, it almost feels like he missed the mark in this one..almost. For the first time I found myself not as excited to get to the next leg of the journey as I have been in the past. Overall: Good story. Consistent with the series as it has been thus far. A few key errors made as far as spelling and grammar. I suggest closer proofreading in the future. Kimbusa: Closure Okay so you have this sumo wrestler dude who is actually a family member in the Yakuza on his first assignment. Two obnoxious 'back-ups' and one side kick who looks none to pleased to have him there. I thought I had all the elements of this story figured out as I read along, I was wrong. In all honesty, I thought the woman in red was is on it. When she flirted with him the way she did, all I could think was..there goes his gun. I can not say I was surprised by the quality of the story, I expected nothing less. I can say I was surprised by how it flipped the channel. I liked it. It was fluid, the description had a way of thrusting the vision into your head remniscent of Stevens style. You can tell these two guys work closely in other circumstances. Overall: The tale was great. You really felt the main character. I was surprised at the horrid typos in there in a coupld of places. The handler obviously didn't proofread. I can not stress it enough- proof read!! Grammar was on the spot as far as -I- could tell. Pick: Kimbusa d Stevens This was not an easy decision by any means as I am a fan of both of these guys. Both of these men are fierce competitors and excellent storytellers. I had to sit back and mull over each story, the description, the tale, the unfolding, all of it. In the end I had to give it to Kimbusa..but only just barely. My reasoning is that in the end, he simply left me wanting more, more than Stevens did this time. Stevens did well, but I think he just barely missed the mark this time. Good show, Stevens. You should be proud of yourself. I was very excited to judge this match and was surprised to find some huge similiarities it the two roleplays. Both dealt with the filthiness of underworld... as if it was a topic word for th round. Each person gave their own spin on it and these two heavyweights of the GCW, Phyl and Ben, did a great job. Judge 2 Michael Stevens Stevens' roleplay was a powerful piece. I especially liked two parts of it in particular... One being his social commentary on the homeless, which I found to be very well done. The other being the "death" of Michael Stevens. I was so ready to see what Spitfire was going to witness after death, but then he was dragged back into hell, which was his life. The "death" of Stevens was very well written, while the social commentary was very inciteful. There were some grammar problems in the roleplay... such as using "their" when "there" was appropriate. There were also some comma problems, which were sometimes annoying. Commas used when semi-colons should have been, for instance. Also, I sometimes found myself wondering when it was going to end, as the piece dragged in places. Also, there was a question that ended with a peroid. However, these are only minor problems, and I feel that Phyl produced a very good roleplay. Kimbusa This entire roleplay was a delight to read, start to finish. In an interesting contrast to Stevens' rp, Kimbusa was the beneficiary in the underworld, while Stevens was the victim. It was a great battle between the powerful and the powerless. The only grammarical errors were two spelling mistakes, which happened to fall in the same sentence. This rp was beautifully crafted, and I feel that Ben is a stronger writer. He exhibited a better use of language. By reading the roleplay, I truly got the feeling that Kimbusa was a real MONSTER. In the beginning of the story, Kimbusa is nervous and unsure of himself, but by the end, he is more confident, and ready to unleash himself on the underworld as an unrestrained colossus. I very much liked that Ben tied the story into the tournament at the end of the roleplay, something the Stevens rp lacked. I actually felt like I was Steven Caldera and had just been told Kimbusa's story. An excellent roleplay from top to bottom. Pick: Kimbusa. Kimbusa's roleplay grabbed my attention and kept it for the entire thing. It was very well written from a technical standpoint, and I really got a better read on the Kimbusa character than I thought I did with Stevens. The end of th rp was a nice touch, relating back to the tournament after a riveting story. Phyl has nothing to be ashamed of, but I just feel Kimbusa edged out Stevens. Kimbusa d Michael Stevens 2 votes to 0 EMBER V DARKSTAR Judge 1 Ember, I really enjoyed both your RP's. I thought that they had a nice pace to them, and they are easy to read. My one gripe would be that I felt that I had been dropped into the middle of a story-line, like I'd picked up a book missing it's first chapter. I didn't have too much of a problem picking up the thread, in fact I was tempted to go back and read your earlier stuff. Darkstar, it's a bad sign when I read your RP before reading your opponents work, then when I go to write my comments, I can't recall anything about your RP. I had to go back and look over it again to refresh my memory. I looked back at your posting history and noticed that thus far you've gotten through GTT5 on only one RP, I felt that you needed to step it up more. Pick: Ember Judge 2 The Curtain Call Saga: Part II, In Which the End Begins Well, quite short and didn't really get very far with explaining anything. I'm guessing you had planned to follow this up but since you didn't post anything more, you never got the chance to. A shame because it was an intriguing piece. Light and Darkness Nice RP and shows some good progression, getting into the mindset quite well, illustrating strains on him while at the same time showing his drive for the GTT. Very well written and a good story. Darkness and Light Another good RP and while it was written well, I was slightly disappointed in that you didn't venture into your own question towards the beginning. Perhaps in a future RP? Sadly this wasn't as close as it should've been. Darkstar's bit was so short that you can't really glean anything from it that could justify it getting anywhere near Ember's two solid RPs. Better RPs of this quality will be needed for Round 4. Pick: Ember Ember d Darkstar 2 votes to 0 SEYMOUR ALMASY V CLYDE WALKINS Judge 1 Almasy: Family Matters: Part one The Final Fantasy really surprised me here. Though I have always loved his stuff, the sense of true realism was never quite there. It is his gimmick, so that was to be expected. To see his real name, the hard realism of his young adulthood...all I can say is: Good job. I really enjoyed this. I liked the fact he gave us a glimpse into his past, his -real- past and showed the disfunctional side of his family. The change from defeatist attitude to hopeful to shock and disgust was well played out. In the end he did the job and left me wanting to read part two. Almasy: Family Matters: Part Two So we start off with a feud between mother and son, okay, that is typical one would think. Almasy's character is clearly not dealing with his past well, also..typical. To have his 'righteous' way of life and heroic stance jabbed, gutted and brought down by his mother in a dark shadow of ( through perspective) truth..not so typical and rather enjoyable to read. So now we have gone through doubt, hope, shock, anger, shock, doubt. What's next on this coaster ride? Ah yes...hope. Like the first one I enjoyed the story as a whole. The interaction between mother and son was priceless. In the end, though, the journal entry almost felt like a cop out? It just felt like a big let down after that terrific build up to me. Walkins: The Finalest Fantasy Of Them All: Disc 2 Okay, let me start off with the negatives first. Can we say movie montage rip off much? I saw X men, Spiderman, Aliens, MIB ( I think?), Tuxedo( Jackie Chan) and a few others in there not to mention Final Fantasy( check title..who knew!?). It is lloooooonng, like really long and that made it hard to get into. To be honest this did not bode well from my stand point. But, then, I started really reading it and found that despite the blatant movie elements it was a damn fine read. It was entertaining, well put together and all around a funny story. I was a bit dissapointed at the sudden loss of Heather being a part of it about a third of the way through or there abouts. Walkins: The Finalest Fantasy Of Them All: Disc 3 Again..negatives first. Quality over quanity. Length can be a turn off for some people. Now...positives. I liked how it jumped right in there from where the last one left off. Cod piece....heh. Congradulations. I will never be able to play FFVII again without going into fits of hysterical laughter. Walkin did well to bring in several elements of the game, Cait Sith and his..depositing of fortune, the Coral Prison, Cactar..etc etc. This second installment as actually more entertaining than the first, despite the lack of Heather. Pick: Walkins over Almasy This was an extremely difficult decision. I honestly feel both men deserve to move on but, alas, there can only be one. I had to sleep on this decision before finally giving it to Walkins. It came down to the second rps and how I felt at the end. Almasy's kind of left me feeling ..eh about how he ended it while Clyde had me wanting more. Both men had one stronger rp and one weaker one. You both did a fantastic job. Thanks for the good reads. Judge 2 The Finalest Fantasy Of Them All - Disc 2 - by Clyde Walkins I'll be honest and say I don't really 'get' this piece at all. Everytime a story started to unfold, your characters would do something unexplained. Whilst the writing was good, the only part of the RP I could get a grip on was that it had something to do with the Final Fantasy games, which I know nothing about. Hopefully the next one sheds some light on this tale The Finalest Fantasy Of Them All - Disc 3 - by Clyde Walkins After reading this, I'm still pretty lost on what you were trying to do. Granted it did have me laughing, but it was far too random to make any sense to me. Family Matters: Part One - by Seymour Almasy This one started off a little slow, giving props to The Brawler for the tough match in round two, but soon evolved into a great emotion-filled tale. You did a fantastic job of building your character, and the ending really hooked me into reading the next part. Family Matters: Part Two - by Seymour Almasy You did a great job playing off the last one, and built a bit of suspense near the end, with the mother being paid off for breaking Seymour down. Not much more to say, really, Great Job. Pick: Seymour Almasy Had I more of an undertanding for Clyde's RPs this might have been closer, but I'm afraid Almasy takes it easily. Judge 3 TBA Pick: Almasy Seymour Almasy d Clyde Walkins 2 votes to 1 RED ROCK V WILLIAM GWYNN I noticed a few minor, minor spelling mistakes in both writer's work, but nothing major. I really enjoyed this match-up. It was probably the most even of all the work I've read/judged so far. Red Rock. I enjoyed your RP's - but I felt that they where a bit 'light weight' especially considering that nothing really happened in the 2nd RP, and yet you still ended it with a ".... to be continued..." Still I enjoyed the humour, it felt a bit like an episode of 'the Young Ones' -- typical British humour, maybe? "Avatar" William Gwynn -- nicely written! You almost lost me part way though the crystal ball reading -- I had to backtrack a little at one point -- but other than that, you had me eating out of the palm of your hand. Over all, I found your work to have more depth, I'm giving you the win. Pick: William Gwynn Note: Second judge no-showed, so I had to go with one judge. ROUND 4 MATCHUP Kimbusa v Ember v Seymour Almasy v William Gwynn Two highest placed advance to bracket final. Brackets
