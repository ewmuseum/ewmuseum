Hype The First: Planting Seeds. Ooops.

9 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I can't make GTT suffer, just because [detail withheld] I need something to do that isn't just me sitting on the couch or at work. So, the title says it all: This year's GTT will result in 16 seeds in 4 brackets battling it out to see who's cuisine reigns supreme. So, imagine GTT like Wimbledon: 16 seeds, the rest fighting to unseat those seeds. So, to help me determine seeds... 1. Submit what YOU think is your best roleplay. It does NOT have to be original, though you certainly can make it an original roleplay, though there will NOT be bonus points for it. You have one week, regardless if you're entering or not. You can enter and psyche out the field (Mat), you can enter even if you'll be laughed at (Steve Carnage), you can enter even if you're not sure you can make it (Tony, who has enough talent to write as well as anyone in this game) 2. Joe? Aaron? von Doom? Craig? Ben Van I.? You're already guaranteed a seeding. But, maybe you'd like to be the #1 seed. Without entering, you cannot be. Maybe 4's good enough for you. Maybe you suck and should shut the fuck up and enter anyway, since the worst you can do is be the first people seeded in PTC history. (btw: Does it piss you PRIMEates off that 4/5 spots went to SCCW wrestlers? Are you pansy asses slipping off the radar, and you're not the best anymore? BECAUSE IT SURE FUCKING LOOKS LIKE IT.) 3. You have a week (that's 9/17/09) to enter. No extensions. 4. You can only enter once. Linking is fine. Copy/paste is fine. (linking is better). 5. Being seeded does NOT guarantee you a spot in GTT. Once apps open, you all still have to apply to claim your spot. A seed can't be given away, or handed to someone else. DO YOU WANT TO HELP JUDGE THIS? Good. Because you will. Help shape PTC history by submitting (AFTER 9/17 until 9/24) your TOP 16. If you DON'T, you leave it up to me and [name withheld] and Andy. If you DO, you HELP me and [name withheld] and Andy. If not, no sweat. Just don't bitch when Sean's the #2 seed because we're woefully out of touch with reality. DO YOU WANT TO RECRUIT PEOPLE FOR THIS WHO AREN'T NORMAL PTC POSTERS? Good. Because if you do, ask them to introduce themselves in the Intro forum. The person with the most people recruited will get themselves a guaranteed entry into GTT7. And, yes, [name withheld] is eligible... at a 4:1 ratio. Every 4 Tom gets counts for 1, because he's got the influence. SO GO FORTH, PTC. Entertain me while [detail withheld]. Show us good writing. And remember: HAVE FUN.
