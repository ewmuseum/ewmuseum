Round 1 judging progress update

7 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thanks to the judges who have chipped in so far and delivered promptly. Their help has been invaluable. We should see one bracket up in the next couple of hours, if all goes well, and probably one a day after that - also if all goes well (although I may have a rest on Friday night and cuddle up and watch a video or something instead, as this has been quite taxing). FYI - Each Round 1 match will have only one judge, due to lack of available resources - i.e. the people who usually volunteer are all playing this year. I won't take people who are playing and have not been eliminated, I do not believe in that at all - to the point where I"m giving up an awful lot of sleep rather than looking at doing that ;) Smiley: I don't mean to sound like I'm asking for too much, but I would really, really appreciate it if a judge could throw my comments my way during the judging of the next bracket. It's always a benefit for all of the players to feel some kind of direction in what the judges are looking for as it relates to their own personal style, and considering the fact that I did spend an hour writing a ten page roleplay, I really would like someone to glance over it and tell me something. We all know how few people even visit the Writing Showcase board and those people, for the most part, aren't the judges anyway. I know the juges are stretched thin and pressed for time, but it's not my fault my opponent didn't have the decency to roleplay or even apologize. If not, I perfectly understand there not being the means to judge an 'extra' roleplay, but if a judge has a spare ten or fifteen minutes sometime, their opinions to me would be invaluable in me brainstorming how I should shape my next installment of my roleplay series in the next round in which my opponent will have had some counseling and I will have not. Its a competition. You won your match by no show. If you get something on the feedback forum, you'll have to be happy with that. (read that as by the time I'm done I'll have lost count of the number of work I've read, and the other judges are doing set amounts, so... no.)
