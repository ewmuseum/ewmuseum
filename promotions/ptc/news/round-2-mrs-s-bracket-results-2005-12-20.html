Round 2: Mrs S. Bracket Results

20 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DAMIEN RYAN V SHANTY JONES Poor Damien Ryan. Everyone thinks he walked into a gun fight with a toothpick, and had no chance at winning this match against the GTT4 Champion's handler. Did he have a chance? Since his second piece was a few minutes late, I only read the first, and completely disregarded the second, so, my thoughts below are only on that piece. First, the introduction was somewhat out of place with the rest of the story. Breaking EW Kayfabe has been done to death. It was nice to see a bit of history of the handler, but, coupled with the rest of the story, it was sort of a "meh" experience. As far as the story...I've seen it before. Countless times. Wrestler does wrong, gets arrested and disrespected. Kudos for using the "Don't you know who I am?" line twice in the same story. All in all, Ryan's first (and only counted effort) was simply average. And then we come to Shanty Jones. Honestly, I'm not digging the style here. I know the breaks are all from different perspectives, but, it makes it rather hard to actually get into it. The cameos and appearances from the Doctor add some comedy to the story, and I can see why he's been brought in. The second story clinched it for me. It was an absolutely brilliant mix of story and trash talking without actually trash talking. Again, the Doctor adds to the story, rather than just making a cameo to rub it in everyone's face that Andy won GTT4. Granted, that's one of the reasons, but, it's not simply on display for the world to see the entire time. So, Damien Ryan, to answer your question, "Who's Damien Ryan?" I can give you this answer: The man who was eliminated in Round 2 by Shanty Jones. Winner: Shanty Jones MASQUERADE V KADEN CALIX "The river of crimson that was gushing from her head was more than any person should be able to bear witnessing." This sentence sets the tone for the entire story. It's difficult to read, and difficult to get into. It jumped around more than a Mexican Jumping Bean on a tequila filled rampage. From beating a woman, to fighting with mom and dad to the Olympics? And with no explaination on why the girl was laying on the floor, bleeding at the end of the first, I'm going to assume that it'll be a mystery for the rest of our natural lives. The second story was broken up with italics, words from Kaden's past catching up to him, or playing out at convenient times. And once again, another contrived storyline about a plane crash someone was supposed to be on. You, good writer of said character are quite possibly leading GTT5 in the use of overly horrible cliches, as well as dual use of the "fade to black". Honestly, I didn't care much for Kaden. There was absolutely nothing endearing in the story here about him that would make me feel that way. It was all just sort of...meh. As far as "Masquerade", who decided he (Masq, you may be a she, so please don't be offended by use of the pronoun 'he'. k? thx.) was going to just post once, and hope that will be enough to move him on. Was it? We start with a letter. Oh boy. I love getting mail! Actually, a letter like this, I wouldn't mind. It was wonderfully written with fantastic imagery. Getting into the story, it's well written as well. No contrived imagery, no crazy descriptions of things that aren't going to matter much later on, leaving me (the reader) to fill in the blanks. B. Chaney is sort of like one of those "we make your wishes come true" TV shows. He rescues people from horrible situations, and help them get their lives together. I could have done without the "my friend, my brother, my uncle's cousin's hairdresser's dog's breeder's monkey" comments after every sentence almost, but, all and all, I really enjoyed it. So, should Calix take it for doing two mediocre, average stories, or should Masquerade continue his unlikely advance through this tournament on the back of his mediocre effort, but spectacular story? I've got to give it to Masquerade. True, Calix did post two. But, if I own two Chevys, does that make me better than the guy that owns one Porsche? In every aspect, Masq outshone Calix, and he advances. Winner: Masquerade KASIDY DRAKE V CHRISTIAN ZENITH The Music Man! HA! Just had to work that in here. Truely, after reading some of the older works of Kyle, I didn't expect something like this out of him. After Riley and The Music man earlier in the career, I can honestly say that I really didn't expect the writing to be this good. Kudos, for picking it up, Kyle. The first part of the story was excellent. Other handlers probably would have gone the route of killing the woman after she did something for him. I like that he simply took what she had to offer. The second part of story one falls off a bit from the first. Look, I realize people want to see some sort of relevance to a tournament, or a match, or something, but this isn't 1998 anymore. We've evolved the game to where it isn't just about the match at hand, or what's happening. It's about the character. If you can't build a connection to the character, no one's going to care. I would be much more intrigued at the story if it didn't have much of anything to do with wrestling, especially the sparring section at the beginning of the second part. The second story starts off with a bit about Calvin Riley, the aforementioned person who I believe is the benefactor of the entire organization. We're back with Karl, and he's eating like a pig. And it looks like Karl's going to try and be a hero. Kasidy's entry into GTT isn't to win. Instead they're trying to set up some diabolical machine that no one's ever going to believe them about, and so, it's only up to them to stop. And now, it's gone from a nice, mild, good story, to a Hollywood production. The Eraser, no less, one of the worst Arnold movies. the best part is the fact that Kasidy, when being told about Zenith says that he sounds like a soap opera script, never realizing he's in the Eraser 2: Electric Kaboomaloo. And we end with a twist and a hook for round 3. Will it be enough? You know, Zenith, everything will be fine if you decide not to use italics, just once. Italics are used for emphasis, not to just make stuff look slanty and cool. Just something to think about. You know. For next time. Please. Well, we've moved from one movie script to another. Whereas Drake was making his own version of Eraser, and Zenith making his own version of...some piece of crap. Come on. Do we have to have these contrived peices that make wrestlers chase assassins? Seriously. They're fucking wrestlers. And then, in the middle of a fist fight on a roof of an icy building, Zenith actually pulls out the "Global World Champion isn't a fucking joke" line? What the fuck? This is a painful read, made even more painful by the unconvincing imagery. Not only is it ridiculous in the USA to have it happen that one person gets his head blown off standing right next to you, but this isn't the first time it's happened? And Zenith doesn't think he's a bullet magnet? I'm surprised the cops guns weren't bursting out ready to blow him the hell away. The best part of the first story? The end, until it says "To Be Continued." I almost cried. The line of the second RP is amazing in it's ignorance. "Maybe some guy who doesn't know who he is and joined the tournament to find out his identity. Or perhaps a big, dark, and brooding mysterious guy who drinks blood and 'strikes fear into the heart of everyone who crosses his path.' I really do love this place… everyone has to make up some silly story to make people think they're something special. They can never even come up with their own make-up story. Just recycling the myths they heard from the people before them, hoping it'll work again and strike horror into the minds of the ignorant." Perhaps Miss Valentine is speaking candidly about her protoge. On the positive side, both RPs are filled with good visuals, even if the storyline is bit blahse. I just found it hard to care about either of the characters Zenith wrote about, or his situation. And this one ends "To Be Continued" as well. Will it be? No. Today, the story of Christian Zenith ends. Drake told the better story, had more endearing characters, and pulled off the upset. Winner: Kasidy Drake DAWN MCFARLANE V SLEDGE Uncharacteristically, there are a few glaring mistakes in McFarlane's first piece. Dawn's working out a problem by punching a bag repeatedly. If I had access, I'd probably do the same thing. Alex tries to get Dawn to talk, and is first met with resistance, but then, opens up about some drinking problems related to WMW, and other stuff. Honestly, if I was in WMW, I'd probably have a drinking problem too. I'd take whatever it is I could to make the pain of being around so many suckbags go away. But, that's beside the point here. And we learn Marcus is a vampire. Right. The conversational point of the story is a bit slow. It seems a tad forced, but, at the same time, not exactly all that orignal. Almost like an Episode of "Charmed" (not that I watch that show...). And now, we're going to go kill a vampire. *sigh*. WE HAVE BOOBS! That's definately a point in the favor of McFarlane. And then we have fangs which totally kills the boobs for me. I'm indeed a sad panda now. The rest of the story was just sort of meh. I'm not sure if Dawn's in a slump, but, this wasn't one of her best works that I've read. It was fairly marginal, honestly. Trash Talk, leading to something else. Oh boy, Sledge. Welcome to 1998, buddy. We not only get treated to Sledge, but also his conscience. Wonderful. And the basis of the story? Girls can't wrestle. And that shares something with his storyline here: It's kind of a crappy opinion to match a fairly boring story and storyline. Give this to McFarlane. It was better on every level. Sorry Sledge. I just couldn't get into it. Winner: Dawn McFarlane ARCH ANGEL V VANGELUS OLSIG Wow. The first eWrestling character to die of Avian Flu. Sort of sad. I hope it won't be transmitted by reading the story. I don't feel like dying any time soon. Strangely, I liked this story. Some idiot didn't kill this girl, she simply died because of an environmental factor beyond her control. Unless she had an affair with Donald Duck or something, but, who could blame her there. It was simple, straightforward, and put together well. I didn't realize until after I read Angel's that Olsig no-showed, so, congrats, Angel. You get comments too, lucky boy. Arch Angel advances to Round 3 ARCHER V KILLEAN SIRRAJIN Man, this is two heavyweights. Sirrajin, an up and comer in PTC, and Archer, handled by Mat, someone who's made it to the top with two characters, and possibly working on a third. Sirrajin's first story was something that I'd never seen before. I hope he knows that he could find the bitch, and have her worthless ass thrown in jail for child endangerment. I mean, who knows if Sirrajin would even come home or something? This story? I'm saying it now: The best of GTT5. Period. It was heartwarming, and proved that people don't need to do shit like being a vampire hunting space cowboy from Planet Q-34 on a galactic mission from their king to assassinate people who stand near Christian Zenith. Archer's single peice was fantastic too. I like how his dialogue is almost as if the narrative voice is telling the story, and not allowing it to fall into the hands of the individual people involved. But, all in all, I have to give it to Sirrajin. The different side of the story, knowing how violent Killean can be in the ring was touching to see the direct opposite. Archer kicked some serious ass, and I think, if there's one available, he should get himself a playin. Winner: Killean Sirrajin HOYT WILLIAMS V DIGITAL MORTALITY Judge 1 Hoyt never seems to amaze me, and today, he did just that. he told the same story from two different sides. The man who is/was going to kill Hoyt, and the man himself. Everytime I think he can't get much better, he does. As far as Digi, his story was just sort of...there. It wasn't spectacular, and it wasn't superbly memorable. It was just written, and pretty much forgotten in a matter of moments. Still, it was well written, and it flowed well. Against Hoyt though, it simply wasn't enough as Hoyt takes the W, and forces Digi to the sidelines. Pick: Hoyt Williams Judge 2 Mrs. S. Bracket: Hoyt vs Digital Digital Mortality: A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 2 A powerful story of family drama...the force of will between a disillusioned believer and a non believer in this rp is strong and intriguing. I enjoyed the honesty of human thought and emotion in this however, I found myself struggling to finish it in the end due to the sheer length. I reread it and found that the length was necessary to get the point across. All in all great story, decent read. Part 3: I was pleased that Digital didn't drop the ball in this thrid installment of his story. It was as long as the first one, yet I didn't find it as difficult to get through. My only complaint is that there were places where he made unecessary motions in the story. He walks down the hall, he opens the door, he takes off his coat...etc etc, all in one paragraph and each a sentence of their own. I would suggest trying to find a way to get the point across to your reader without so much detail on the 'little' things. They tend to detract from the story. Hoyt Williams: Everybody Dies in the End ( Part 1) This is a surprisingly interesting twist on faith. To me, the symbolism of Satan vs God and his son was thick in this with the assassin and Hoyt. Over all the story flowed well and kept me interested, despite the rather unpleasant scene in the bathroom. ( Thanks for the visual!) My only suggestion here would be closer proofreading as I did notice a few spelling errors and punctuation errors. Good job by the handler here. Part 2: Okay, I loved the first one. I am not loving this second one. The style of dialogue read like a playscript and with this much dialogue it really took away from the story as a whole for me. About half way into I realized it was the same story from a different point of view. This can be good, but in this case I got a serious case of deja vu half way through. The same words were used for the same scene practically. It just didn't do it for me this time. Pick: Digital Mortality When I read the first stories I honestly thought this was going to be a difficult call to make, but when I read the second rps it became easier. Hoyt is talented, of that there is no doubt, however in this particular set of pieces the second really just didn't move me. Digital takes the match. Judge 3 Hoyt Williams Part One: Loved the story. Loved the way that you took a new spin on faith and how the story played out. As usual with Al he is able to keep me wanting more and more every time I read his work. Looking forward to the second piece. Digi Part One: Excellent story on the family here. Very moving piece that kept me reading. Some might have called it long but I thought it was perfect for what you were doing. It flowed really well and kept me reading the whole way. Excellent job. Leaving me with a very tough call through the first rps. Hoyt Williams Part Two: Another really great piece of writing that had me intrested. Not quite as good as the first part due to an over use of dialogue that some what slows down the story a bit. Then again the first was so well done it would be hard to follow up with a piece just as good. Over all still a very good read. A few spelling errors but I'm not a nazi on that sort of thing. Nothing in there that made it hard to follow or read through on one sitting. Digi Part Two: Another excellent read by Mr. Digi. Picking up and not slowing down, to any great extent, from his first roleplay. Once again it kept me reading, intrested, and focused on the story rather than the length which can be a problem if the story isn't there. This one was. I really enjoyed the two pieces. Decision: Of course the two of you had to make this an extremely tough call for me. I really loved both stories and found myself reading through them both with out the slightest bit of boredom or saying "Man when will this end". In the end I had to go with Digi simply on the merit that his second piece was really just as strong as his first in my eyes and unfortunately for Hoyt his second fell just a little short of the excellent first piece of writing. No shame here for Hoyt as he put up an excellent fight that against some of the other writing in this round would have earned him a move into the third round. Pick: Digi Winner: Digital Mortality SUMMARY - Shanty Jones d Damien Ryan - Masquerade d Kaden Calix - Kasidy Drake d Christian Zenith - Dawn McFarlane d Sledge - Killean Sirrajin d Archer - Digital Mortality d Hoyt Williams Brackets
