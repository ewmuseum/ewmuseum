Mechanics of The Draw

11 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Looking at potential ways to deliver the draw. We had a blast doing radio shows with GTT's 4 through 6, and a failed attempt at webcamming in GTT5. This is in two parts: Random Draw: Fans: Is being able to see a random draw with your own eyes via webcam supremely important to you, or do you have enough trust in one of us to do it via radio or some other medium? Techheads: Technology that we should use for the draw. What websites/services have you used? Discuss. Selected Draw: What is your opinion on allowing some people to select the draw based on their opinions on what would be good matches? Should it be a draft ala GTT6 or a committee selecting matches and being able to switch things around before publishing? What is your opinion on some sort of seeding system? What is your opinion on raffling prizes to people to allow them to select their opponents? (we did this with GTT5 - Andrew won and picked Jeff, and I'd say that that process probably contributed to how he acted out later on with the whole dog and pony show thing). Brackets
