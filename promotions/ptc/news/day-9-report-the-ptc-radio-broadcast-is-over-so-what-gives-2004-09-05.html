Day 9 Report: The PTC Radio broadcast is over, so what gives?

5 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Matchups for Tournament Favorites After a huge edition of PTC Radio where the entire draw for GTT4 was released by GTT4 Tournament Director Argyle (aka the father's-day celebrating, swearing, partially drunken sex-fiend), it has been revealed that joint GTT4 favourites Seymour Almasy (PTC Global Champion) and Angelo Deville (GTT3 winner) have both drawn Round 1 matches, missing out on the handful of byes available. Deville takes on Snake Eyes (FA) in the Kaikouras bracket, while Almasy takes on Ash (FA). Other matches of note include: - XUW's fatbadger taking on Karina Wolfenden (FA) - Inphino Blitz to take on Sebastian Monaco (both FA) - One time PTC Global Title challenger, 4WW's Patti Anderson, to take on Jade (FA) - Another one time PTC Global Title challenger, XUW's Blake Wolfson, taking on Officer Stone (FA) - XUW's The Shadow taking on Token Weed (FA) - XUW's Doughboy vs Violence Jack (FA) Replays of the bracket announcement broadcast can be caught at odd times during the day by checking the GTT4 discussion forum, and the full brackets will be published with the Day 10 report tomorrow. Sean McClancy withdraws from GTT4, retires from active competition Former PTC Extreme Champion "The Irish Bastard" Sean McClancy has succumbed to a number of serious neck and shoulder injuries and been forced to withdraw from GTT4, and is in fact retiring after final matches with Moraless in UWF and for the Extreme Title match at PTC's Survival event. PTC staff acknowledge McClancy's reign as a fighting Extreme Champion would like to wish McClancy well in his future endeavours. Replacing McClancy in the Appalacians bracket is one of McClancy's Survival opponents, XUW's Joey Vendetti. Vendetti is one of the lucky few to receive a bye directly into Round 2. Rumor Killers PTC Management must at this point in time declare false all rumors regarding the entry of "The Virgin" Byron Calver, PCW Hall of Famer Meanstreak and The Phantom Booker into GTT4. "I encouraged all three of them to sign up, as they would have drawn great ratings for this contest, especially Calver," declared GTT4 tournament director Argyle. "However, it wasn't meant to be this time around." Bye confirmations Just for something different, before the full brackets are posted tomorrow, the following individuals are confirmed as having byes into Round 2: - Emperor Riley (NGW) - Justin Evers (WMW) - Erica Lopez (FA) - Jenna Ryder (FA) There are more, but divulging them now would ruin the suspense, no?
