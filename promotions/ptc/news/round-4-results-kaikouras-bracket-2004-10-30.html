Round 4 Results: Kaikouras Bracket

30 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dave Hurst d Odium (3 votes to 0) to advance Angelo Deville d Train (2 votes to 0) to advance (third judges comments and pick may be added at a later time) Bracket final matchup: Dave Hurst v Angelo Deville Judging Comments Odium v Hurst Odium: All three of his roleplays were well written, had a good usage of length and got to the point fairly well. The last one sort of had me confused when he was talking about the story and such, didn't quite catch on to the point of it, though it was well written and the emotions were shown well. The first two were the best I'd say, and the third one not seeming to fit in quite as much to me, great series either way however. EDH: Had three solid roleplays also himself. Each of which had good flow with one another, and had a point behind each that were fairly similiar. I liked how his final one was sort of related as to the style Odium had, yet it was portraying back on his past. Good usage of the flashback, I thought overall the three had a great use of length, words, and emotions collberated together in a hell of a series. Pick: EDH Dave Hurst versus Odium Note: I didn’t read all of your past roleplays. I read some of both. Dave Hurst RP1: The first roleplay started off nice with a few jokes. However, in my opinion there was a bit too much description. The part with Wendy and Ducky was also nice to see. He kept the subject on the pain, which is good in my opinion. With a very long flashback about his past (match) with Jay Phoenix. In the end, there was a lot of symbolism when he forgot about the pain and gave the plant some water. Very good written roleplay: 8.8/10 RP2: Once again, a great beginning. But it went wrong with the doctor. I think we all know he’s referring to Doc. C here. To top it all off, bringing a plant to the hospital isn’t funny; it’s ridiculous. The bit at the psychiatrist was also not funny. It only made me think Dave Hurst was crazy. It all made no sense at all. Suddenly, the roleplay gets a lot better. The part where he placed his bet was well done, with a nice reference to Odium. The quest of saving the plant finally ends with a tip. The end was solid, with once again a nice reference to the tournament. It isn’t as good as the first one, and I’ll be nice here: 7.7/10 RP3: By far, the best. I really liked the symbolism here and although there were some mistakes (how can a 15 year old guy look into the future and know he’s going to call himself exceptional?) it was very good. A nice, short and simple roleplay: 9.0/10 Total: 25.5/30 Odium RP1: The story was very nice. Both men tried to outsmart each other and nobody really seemed to have done so. It has been plotted out well, and I think he will build on this roleplay. I liked the relevance with his home fed and GTT 4, but also with wrestling in general. Much focus wrestling, which I think is a must in e-fedding: 8.5 RP2: I am impressed with this roleplay. Odium succeeded in making a good trash talk roleplay without letting it get the upper hand. Unfortunately, it had not much to do with his first roleplay, which I think is a shame. Still, I believe this roleplay was as good as his first one: 8.5 RP3: His final roleplay gave us some insight in his past. I wasn’t really impressed by this roleplay for numerous reasons. The end was really strange, almost unbelievable. Also, the end of the rp, after the flashback, was unnecessary. It did make some relevance to the tournament, but it wasn’t necessary in my opinion. Unfortunately you couldn’t keep up the high level of your past two roleplays: 7.5 Total: 24.5 A short note: I felt that both did a great job and it was very close. Good luck to both in the future. Pick: Exceptional Dave Hurst Sorry it took me so long: Rp1 Odium's first Rp was strong on Description. Over all not a hard read but it seems a little strange to me. Rp2 Dark but then again that is the way his whole character is. The babble seems to be the same babble you see from others nothing new. I would like to see him maybe come up with something stronger for the next RP. Both RP1 and RP2 were a shorter length Rp which isn't a bad thing for the judges but you expect more in the shorter Rps. RP3 Though he would step it up more then he did. Over all not a bad RP but it seems he tries to use the type cast thing to much. RP1 The ducky is a little strange... but it works. Here you see that the character even though it is something on a computer screen could be a real person. He give the kudos to Jay, I haven't seen many people do that. And it shows the bigger person. He knows that He could be in Jay's place and Jay could be in his. It was a good read and really shows that one RP can make a difference in what we see. RP2 what seems to be the problem? Hurst your a NUT ... But a fun nut to read about. I like this RP, the poor plant I hope it survives!!! It shows his mind isn't totally focused on one thing or on one person. He isn't obsessing with this oppoint making the RP a fun easy read. RP3 Ok I think he sums up what people try to say to these people that keep going back to their history to show what they are instead of what they are now. Strong opening to this RP and hits the nail on the head to begin with. But then goes into a memory of his child hood. Kinda weird but I guess that explains why he is the way he is. The plant Survived!!!! YEA!!! Pick: Dave Hurst Train v Deville Angelo Deville vs Train Angelo- What can I say? Solid story-telling, great interaction between The Deville and The Devil, awesome roleplays. I normally don't like long roleplays, but I have to say that these always kept me wanting to come back for more. The whole thing about the Devil taking over Angelo's body was entertaining and when he tried to move his body when the devil was sleeping kept me thinking of what a struggle that must be like. I gotta give Angelo props here for a job well done. Train-I was really impressed with Train's rps this round. I started reading and was hooked all the way till the end, where the lack of a last rp made me feel like I was being cheated... we never knew if the girl was really his, we didn't get to see how the mother would react except that she was surprised. Like I said, the roleplays really impressed me and made me want to see how the story would go, but unfortunately DeVille's story was more complete and more rewarding after you read it. Props to both competitors, I really enjoyed reading both of your work but I gotta pick a winner and that has to be Angelo Deville. Pick: Angelo Deville Angelo Deville(FA) vs Train(GW) Word on the street was that Deville had this one in the bag. Not having read either writers work, I expected it to be an easy call, however I'm happy to say that wasn't the case at all. Train wrote really well, his storyline read well and built nicely. Deville has a fantastic style of writing; he has control over the words and really paints a picture. Train's writing was good, but it felt like he'd worked hard at it, where as Deville's work felt like it came easily to him. I must say I wasn't all that impressed with Deville's first roleplay, I found it much harder to 'get into' than his other writing, and trash talk doesn't tweak me in the right places. As Uncle Junior would say, Train was a (bleep) hair away from owing the match, and Deville was that hair. :-D Kaikouras
