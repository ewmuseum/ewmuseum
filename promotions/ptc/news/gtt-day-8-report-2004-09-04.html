GTT - Day 8 Report

4 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Right-side of each Bracket to be announced on PTC Radio in < 12 Hours! With only 18 vacant spaces left, GTT4 signups close at approximately 2.00am UTC. Refer to timeanddate.com to work out what that is for you! At this time, visit the PTC Radio Website and tune in to the bracket announcement broadcast. Listeners will need Apple Quicktime, Winamp or equivalent program capable of playing streaming Shoutcast broadcasts. Statistics...zzzz..zzzz WWA has taken a handy lead in the number of applicants that are representing it in GTT4. Equal second are 4WW and UWF, while Neon is third, followed closely by XUW. Figures today released for the GTT4 website shows that it has been getting a massive amount of hits, totalling almost 50% that of the entire PTC forum community. GTT4 administrators are very pleased with the popularity of the site. There is no one individual that is known to have competed in all of the previous GTT's. A greater than 50% majority of people competing in GTT4 have not previously competed in any PTC tournament. At least 10 individuals competing in GTT4 also competed in GTT1. At least 10 individuals competing in GTT4 also competed in GTT2. At least 15 individuals competing in GTT4 also competed in GTT3. At least 30 indivuals competing in GTT4 have previously competed in a Roleplay God tournament. At least 10 individuals competing in GTT4 also competed in PTC's Primetime Invitational tournament last year. At least 15 individuals competing in GTT4 also competed in PTC's Cruiserweight Tournament earlier this year. At least 15 individuals competing in GTT4 also competed in previous Universal Title tournaments. At least 5 indiviuals competing in GTT4 also competed in PCW's 64 person Diamond in the Rough tournament back in 2001. At least 10 individuals competing in GTT4 also competed in RPCommunity's Chris Williams Memorial Cup last year. Confirmations! In the tradition of milking the suspense for all it is worth, here are some more confirmations: Arefiev (GW) Jamie Fletcher (FA) Pandora (FA) Horazon (FA) Doughboy (XUW) Kash (WWA) Joshua Dunn (UWF) Falcon Mega (FA) These people, along with everyone else, will have to wait until the Radio broadcast to find out who they are facing.
