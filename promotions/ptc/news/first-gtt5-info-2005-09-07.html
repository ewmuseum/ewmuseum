First GTT5 info!

7 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- 128 entrants, unless I get over 300 applicants, then I'll consider bumping it up to a larger number. - Intention is to have 64 spots for members of PTC feds, and 64 spots for Free Agents (handlers with no character in a PTC fed). - Webcast 1: Entrants without guaranteed spots will be put into a hat and drawn on a webcast. This will determine who will be in the tournament. That means, there's no great advantage to being the first applicant... but the application period will only be one week, so procrastination time will be limited. I'll either work out how to cast it live (preferable) or record it and offer it for download. - Webcast 2: First Round Opponents, with the exception of Rich Rollins and his chosen opponent. - Round 1 - 1 RP limit. - Will there be Round 1 theme words like with GTT4? Probably not, but never say never. If I do decide to have them, you'll have to suck it up and deal with it :D - Something else in mind to replace theme words? Absolutely. - Prizes: As my sig says, one of the winners prizes will be a custom wrestler site, designed by someone as yet unknown to me and coded by me complete with scripting. - When? Signups for the remaining spots will start a day or two after the SS results are posted. (learning lessons from Survival here) - People who no-showed previous tournaments without notifying me and/or your opponent before the RP time period expired - If I remembered that you did that, then your chances of getting into this are slim to none. - Plagiarists need not apply. - Hey, expiry deadlines for IG are pretty lax... will it be the same for GTT5? Hell no... if you are 1 nanosecond late posting your RP, it won't count. That's all the server needs to bump you over to the next date. - So, what's the big suprise to replace theme words? Not telling, but the clue is that it involves hype... and lots of it.
