Round 1 Results: Rockies Bracket

18 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These are results, they are not Round 2 Brackets. Those will be announced after all the results are up. No-shows and Double-no-shows are not noted. If you haven't read the judging thread, do it now: http://community.primetimecentral.net/showthread.php?t=9955 Muppet Man d Justin Vivazi Locke (1 vote) to advance Caitlin Fairchild advances. Alex Grey (aka Payne) d Annihilation (1 vote) to advance Dr. Curiosity d Grandad English (1 vote) to advance Johnathon Banner d The Latch Key Kid (1 vote) to advance Justin Evers advances. Jamie Fletcher advances. Elia Smith d Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis (GW) (1 vote) to advance Pandora advances. Peter Vetra advances. Jade d Patti Anderson (2 votes to 1) to advance Scottywood advances. Horazon d Phil Botwin (2 votes to 0) to advance Joshua Taro Freedom d Façade (1 vote) to advance Judging Comments Muppet Man v Locke Two nicely written roleplays - both are well structured and free of flaws. Locke's piece while nicely written didn't really go anywhere, it had great potential but I think it fell a little flat. Not a particularly good use of his theme - Stubbornness. Muppet - go climb your mountain - you've got your win. Annihilation v Alex Grey (aka Payne) Annihilation, did well on his RP the show of what would bring joy to some. I kinda got lost in his RP on where he was truely trying to take us. It wasnt a bad RP. I would have to go with Payne on this one. Both men did well on their Rps but even with the gross Dream sceene his RP showed that he really wanted this one. His intrest perked up and he showed his Enthusiasm once he found ther is a chance for his Revenge if he gets to face LaStat Knight in the ring. Pick: Payne Dr. Curiosity vs Granddad Ian English Dr. Curiosity vs Grandad George English Dr Curiosity produced an incredibly long roleplay and he also had what I thought would be a tricky theme - love. However much to my surprise he kept me entertained the whole way. It's obvious that George English has also put an enormous amount of effort into his roleplay, maybe too much effort, as it wasn't always easy to read, in fact I struggled to get to the end of his 6,000 word epic. There were pockets of greatness in English's work. However he was simply outclassed by Dr Curiosity. Dr Curiosity's writing was well structured, It was laugh out loud good, it was a masterpiece filled with classic gems of writing. I am very, very impressed. Dr Curiosity wins. Banner vs Latch Key Kid I wish I could take both here for the win but I guess I would have to go with Banner on this one. Latch Key Kid did a great job with the rp also so it is almost unfair to have to pick one winner out of these two; I wish he could also go on to the next round. Banner showed what mos twould think that a roster might act like if a new Champoin was all of a sudden tossed into a Tournament like this. You could have choked on the feelings that were shown in the locker room. latch Key kid was a fun RP to read like I siad I wish I could pick both to win, but going to have to go with one or theother, latch Key Kid is a man after my own heart. a Coffee freak, Love it and it was cute. He faces the world thru the eyes of a kid. wondering what is always behind that door and if the grass is greener on the other side of the fence. Pick: Banner Smith vs Namenios-Duisternis ackson Namenlos-Duisternis Elia Smith: This one came down to mainly who stuck more to the theme of the RP. Both had themes that could be easily represented and I feel that Elia Smith did the best job at representing the “Insecurity” theme in his RP. Jackson: This guy has talent, but I didn’t see the feeling of “luck” in his RP. [name withheld] is right when he says that these weeds the weak out, not saying that Jackson is weak, he was just not at the top of his game. Two really great RPers came at it here with great stuff, just one guy I felt did better then the other. Pick: Elia Smith Patti Anderson vs Jade Patti: Another good rp but I just couldn't get into it. Good descriptions, but I feel the conversation she had didn't add anything to it. Jade: Despite being very, very, extremely, ridiculously long, she put a lot of effort into it and her writing was also very good. My pick: Jade, but it was close. Patti Anderson: Bit of an English error at the start, but I'll forgive her because the rest of it is gripping stuff. The inclusion of the young character "Raven" is enough to bring a tear to your eye, but in a twisted way throw your emotions the opposite direction as Patti discusses her revenge on Jade. In a word: Awesome. Jade: I'm sorry, but I just couldn't get into this one after reading the Patti RP. The content is good, but it seems long and drawn out needlessly. Patti only gets a mention in the final eighth of the RP whereas Patti mentions Jade all over the show and provides an insightful view of their history and future. Winner: Patti Anderson. Two good RPs, if Jade's was cut short and a bit more mention of her opponent then it would have been a tight call. Patti: Positives: "And fade out to a nice pretty purple and black." Nice. : Negatives: Some sentences don't totally make sense For example: "Now comes the day of judgment is before/upon us, as Jade is to step inside the ring and face me" And "Patti Anderson laid on the couch." Not a particularly developed story line. Jade: Positives: Very strong beginning. Well developed character/s Good flow. Negatives: "stretching and relaxing over her buxom that could only be described as ample." Her what? Can something stretch and relax over my buxom? No. I think not. It doesn't make sense. I thought Jade's story-line was better developed and the writing flowed better, there where parts that could have been cut, but on a whole it was just that little bit more polished. Winner: Jade. Suggestion for both writers - better proof reading. Horazon vs Phil Botwin Botwin: I've read a lot of his stuff and he's an extremely gifted writer. Very very good stuff, good descriptions, but too much of a trash talk. Horazon: Too put it simply, it's the best I've read so far. The atmosphere, the style, everything was superb. Closest thing I've read to perfect, I have no complaints My pick: Horazon...too bad because Botwin would have beaten out a lot of other first rounders. Phil Botwin: Good RP, but I don't expect to see a bad one from anyone in this contest. Was a little long for my taste though and I lost interest half way through. Horazon: A series of events is not really a compelling RP IMO, but it did well enough as I reached towards the middle and latter half. The story shows basically an underdog instinct to the tournament and of not wanting to be a failure, I like that. Good job. Pick: Horazon Freedom vs Facade JT Freedom: It helped that I liked his character, as I found this roleplay to be very enjoyable. A few grammar mistakes here and there, but that's just me being picky. It was also perfect length I thought. Facade: Very good writer who does excellent monologue. But that's all I felt this was, a monologue. Not enough descriptions of his surroundings, despite being a good read. My pick: J.T. Freedom Rockies
