Round 3 Results: Rockies Bracket

13 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Muppet Man d Horazon (2 votes to 0) to advance Dr. Curiosity d Alex Grey (2 votes to 0) to advance Elia Smith d Jamie Fletcher (2 votes to 0) to advance Sebastian Moraco d Digital Mortality (2 votes to 1) to advance Round 4 matchups: Muppet Man v Dr. Curiosity Elia Smith v Sebastian Moraco Judging Comments Muppet Man vs Horazon I found Muppet Man's work to be the more enjoyable read out of the two. Mixed some humor with some seriousness and character development and kept the stories moving. Horazon's RP's were sluggish, too repetitive and weren't proof-read. Not to say that Muppet Man's work was error-free, but using 'too' and 'to' incorrectly and omitting words in the middle of sentences leaving us to 'fill in the blank' so to speak is something too easily fixed to be overlooked. Pick: Muppet Man Muppets 1st- Great combination of both drama and comedy. It really allowed the reader to see Muppet Man full circle. Great character development, and all together a great read. Muppet 2nd- Smooth transition from the first to the second. With the thoughts from Muppet Man I would have liked to see some sort of description as to where he was or what he was doing while he was thinking that. Something that wouldn’t kill the suspense, but at least provide the reader with a visual image. However, even without the description the thoughts played a strong role in the roleplay and were very well written. Horazon’s 1st- Nice introduction to the piece. There were a few technical errors that really stood out though. I found the whole “I did this” “I did that” type bits to get quite boring after awhile. I felt it could have used either more dialogue during those bits, or even a flashback where Horazon is pondering all this stuff in his mind after the murder. I feel that all the I stuff subtracted from the emotion this roleplay was intended to have since it didn’t really focus on his thoughts alone, rather what he did or was doing. Horazon’s 2nd- Better than the first, but the writing was a bit choppy. There were several parts I had to read several times over just to understand what was going on. Definite improvement from the first though since there were less I’s and more of the thought put in. Pick: Muppet Man Dr. Curiosity vs Alex Grey Doctor Curiosity- Awesome series that was just plain well written. Loved the interactions between Colette and DC, especially in the second roleplay when DC was trying to cover up murder with divorce. Alex Grey- Another well written roleplay with great symbolism when it comes to monsters and people who abuse your trust, my only issue is that there wasn’t anything to follow it up. I loved the theme of trust vs. mistrust when it comes to people Alex is involved in, but it was left hanging with no second roleplay to follow it up. I really felt that a second roleplay really could have helped this be an even closer match than this already has proven to be. Pick: Doctor Curiosity Alex Grey (Payne) v. Pick: Doctor Curiosity (85.625% average) over Alex Grey (80% average) Elia Smith vs Jamie Fletcher Elia Smith vs Jamie Fletcher Elia Smith’s roleplayes…where in one word, short. Very tense, and well written however. In the lack of length that he’s laid out, he’s able to put forth a very commanding and intense situation. Everyone in the world’s got an inner pain to deal with, and if someone says they don’t then they’re in denial. Elia’s pain is nicely portrayed, and I was able to give a damn about the entire situation. Elia’s a guy with problems, but who isn’t really? Fantastic imagery throughout the entire read. Short, but solid. (7.7/7.8) Pretty gutsy going in and trying something different by writing the first roleplay in a diary-type form. Admirable, but in the end, unrewarding. Even with the note on top explaining how it went, it just didn’t connect together as well as it should have. Finding myself lost, and re-reading parts took away from the overall appeal, and interest of the first roleplay. The second roleplay goes back to a novel type, and the writer is definitely stronger this way. His inner struggles are more apparent, and I’m able to stay in the middle of things without having to go back and re-read it. Better effort second time around. (6.8/7.6) In a face off that could’ve been much closer…my pick = Elia Smith Pick: Elia Smith Smith -Vs- Fletcher Smith: Decently written story suffering from a number of typos and grammatical problems. I can see what he was trying to portray, but the emotion didn;t quite grip me as much in his pieces as I had been in other matches. Seemed a little jumpy and kind of all over the place. The second piece especially. Add that with the fact that it seemed to be a roleplay with a couple of lines tossed in to fit his current story with the tournament (And not brilliantly either). I had no idea who this guy was and was offered no kind of explanation, and had to assume the backstory. Smith didn't really impress me. Fletcher: If I had any clue what was going on in the first roleplay, I'd be able to comment further. The beginning and end seemed to make sense, the whole middle section was just... random. Too difficult to follow for my eyes. The second one was easier to follow, but again, didn't really set the world alight either. Fletcher didn't maintain my interest even slightly during the second piece. To my disappointment, Smith picks up the win. Uninspired by Fletcher, but just irrelevant from Smith. Smiths better writing wins it, but I will be very surprised to see him advance much further. Pick: Elia Smith Sebastian Moraco vs Digital Mortality Moraco’s first RP was very delicately sewn together. It could’ve fallen through at anytime, but he didn’t miss a beat with his writing, thus creating a very enjoyable read. Flowing through very smoothly into the second roleplay, Sebastian just keeps it coming on strong. Adding a great background story to go along with his drive to continue on in the GTT. An extremely confident Sebastian Moraco could very well be destined for great things. Provide a great read, and a solid rp…THAT’S “what love can do.” (8.4/8.5) Continuing on with the underlying subject of love, Digital Morality takes a much darker and different take on the subject. Implementing self-loathing and suicidal tendencies. The support group was a very nice touch, as a resolution was placed. Moving onto his second roleplay…reflection plays out, in life, and in relationships, adding a lighter side to things, while still keeping the darkness lingering. Great outing, and thoroughly enjoyable. (8.7/8.4) Very close matchup indeed, but there can be only one. Pick: Digital Morality Moraco: 2 pieces which linked nicely with each other had me gripped. Massively empathised with the character, whom I've never read before, but provided a wonderful insight into during the early phases of piece 1. He is telling the story of a pure athlete trying to make it, and fight for his way back to the top of the amateur wrestling world by taking part in GTT4. The desire, beliefe and attitude were all there, and made these roleplays a pleasure to read from some of the others I've judged in previous rounds. Very different, down to earth approach, but very effective. Tended to over describe and drag on a little, but all credit to the man. Great reading. Mortality: The first piece was emotionally enthralling. A real rollercoaster. But I've never read a Mortality roleplay before, and no nothing about the character. It was head first in. I can only assume that his girlfriend (Whilst pregnant) had died, and Mortality had attempted suicide? Assuming I knew that already, a great piece. But I didn't know it. The second piece lacked in my opinion. It seemed to be more of an interim piece working up to the next one. And It didn;t really follow on from the second... The guy was his best friend, Mortality just tried to top himself and all there was about it was an off the cuff remark? Didn't add up to me. My pick: Has to be Moraco. Consistency, and a good story really won through for me in the end. Good luck to him in the rest of the tournament. Pick: Moraco Digital Mortality vs: Sebastian Moraco I found this a very difficult match to judge, both writers have worked hard and produced very polished pieces. However, that being said, I found that the love story that Mortality has woven though his work lapsed into cheesy in some places, which gave Moraco’s work an advantage, where in some places Mortality was cheesy, Moraco had shining moments that hooked me – one being him walking into the GTT4 gym, I thought that was a nice touch. Pick: Moraco. Rockies
