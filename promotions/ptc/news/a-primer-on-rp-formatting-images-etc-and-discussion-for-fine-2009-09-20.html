A primer on RP formatting, images etc (and discussion for fine-tuning as necessary)

20 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Your GTT RP's will be posted into a custom version of my Backstage Script. The previous script used for GTT's, CW2 etc (the one with the blue background and gray text in the login panel) has been discontinued. Content Each RP requires a title, location and the content. Avoid using long words with no spaces (e.g. a very long aaaaaaaargh!). The script has a 50 character limit on such things... it might be a bit buggy with respect to compatibility with TinyMCE (see below), I'm still testing. Editor The RP editor uses a Wysiwyg mechanism called TinyMCE. This gives you a reasonable choice of formatting controls, and will hopefully handle special characters a lot better than previously (e.g. angled quote marks, em-dashes etc). Controls will be added or removed as needed. For those of you who like to do things at a more granular level, TinyMCE has a code editor. These are the HTML tags that the script will not reject: Writing offline or in other packages If you write offline in Microsoft Word then paste into the Interface, there is a "Paste as Microsoft Word" button on the toolbar that you must use. If you don't do this, a whole lot of hidden garbage gets pasted in that trips up the "biggest word" counter and will block you from posting. Editing after posting You may edit your RP during the round, up to the expiry time, upon which the ability to make edits for that RP will be disabled. The scripting keeps a time stamp of the first time you posted it, and the last time you edited it. After you have posted, please review your post in the site itself and correct anything that is breaking the site layout. I have some experimental scripts in place to close tags and the like, but extra wide images and that sort of thing are not being blocked. Images Use sparingly, and keep them smaller than the space allowed in the GTT site (I can't tell you what that is yet). I may provide the ability to upload images so that you have somewhere easy to host them... depending on how much time I have to code it. If I don't get around to it, there's loads of free image hosting sites out there like Photobucket. Banned from use No background music. Nothing using a script tag will be permitted. No tables. No BBCode formatting (the kind you use in forums). The previous script supported this, this one does not, and will not. Feedback/suggestions/questions welcome, as there is still plenty of time to refine the system.
