Round 3 Results: Appalacians Bracket

14 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seymour Almasy advances. Craig Goat d Flyin' Brian Calvin (2 votes to 1) to advance. Steven Caldera d Fnord (2 votes to 0) to advance. Jimmy Bong d Jamar Gordo (2 votes to 1) to advance. Round 4 matches: Seymour Almasy v Craig Goat Steven Caldera v Jimmy Bong Judges Comments Calvin vs Goat First off comments for Craig Goat Well, I didn't like either of his roleplays. The seemed to jump right into the middle of an on-going story with no way of actually giving the slightest recap as to what had happened previous. One paragraph would have been nice documenting some things that the writing left unclear. As far as the actual quality of the writing goes, it was average at best. A varied vocabulary was evident, but only every now and again and sentence construction lacked imagination. Both roleplays seemed to drag on and made me cringe when it was obvious the writer was trying to make me laugh. However, the stories were slightly thought out and their did seem to be a point to the roleplays. They weren't just 'knocked up' quickly, although the grammar suggests otherwise in some places. Keep an eye on that because it harms the flow of the roleplay. Overall, two fairly well written pieces of work, with far too much speech. The prose just seemed like actions most of the times...which made for pretty bland reading. Comments for Brian Calvin I liked the whole theme for his first roleplay. Very well done, he started off with a negative and turned it into a positive with brilliant descriptive paragraphs really setting the scene and a good use of adjectives made this an enjoyable read. However, the conversation at the end wasn't broken up enough and people spoke way too much. Just ask yourself this question. When you talk with someone do you talk non-stop for a minute or so before letting them speak? Though, this is only a little problem, the trash talk at the end spoiled the roleplay for me and hurt the flow of it, but overall it was a good piece of writing. I liked the second roleplay, it told more of a story than the first and stuck to it better, the writer included a small section of trash talk at the end which didn't hurt the flow of the second roleplay. General Comments Craig Goat's work was definately geared towards a very given direction, though it seemed to rely on the side-characters too much in my opinion. It dragged on and didn't really entertain or excite me, it seemed he was trying to write something to make me laugh and instead it made me cringe. The quality of writing was good, but I'm sure he could have made it better. Brian Calvin's work impressed me, he took two styles and put them into one roleplay. In the first part of his roleplays he'd tell the story and at the end he'd wrap them up with some trash talk to make them very relevent. A nice way of working things. However, I'm not saying that this was the deciding factor. Calvin's writing was of a far higher standard than Goat's and was very well constructed. It is for this reason that he is my pick. Pick: Brian Calvin Craig Goat RP1 OVer all the first RP is strong. The twist of the girlfriend laving with her captor is a bit strange expecially when she thinks it is a female. twisted but twisted is unforgetable. RP2 A little stronger this 2nd RP shows what type of tiwsted people he comes from. The whole mind control thing I guess is a good twist. 'Flyin' Brian Calvin Rp1 Can he do it is the question raised for this. Is his age and beaten body going to be a factor? He does something that most of us would love to do go to someone that is older and wiser to get a stright answer. Good Rp it was a good read and I like how he is showing the fatuge on the wrestler after 2 rounds of the GTT4 and not hiding that he is human after all. makes the RP more believeable. Rp2 THis RP is a little more strange. It seems to quick to me. he wnats a manger he goes to do something for wife and ends up hiring the guy... all to easy and simple a little more meat to the RP would of been nice. Pick: Craig Goat Craig goat, a drag queen named Davina and a hypnotherapist who makes his wrestler-subject gay and a nurse totured with a dildo – ha – one of the most original roleplays I’ve had the pleasure of reading so far! Flyin’ Brian, I liked the premise of your first roleplay, the ‘younger’ man leaning a lesson over a game of chess, however I was confused by your second, I had expected more of an association between the two. Pick: Craig Goat Steven Caldera vs Fnord While Fnord's was entertaining, I just found myself drawn into Caldera's more. Very tough match to call. Pick: Caldera Steven Caldera: Very well written work, some of the best prose I've read in a long time. The writing had a good flow to it and was very well balanced between dialogue and descriptions. Fnord: Good description helps make a piece of writing stand out, however too much description can sometimes bore the reader. Unfortunately there wasn't enough description to really make me feel as though I was involved in the roleplays, I just happened to be reading them. I had to really try hard to vizualize what was going on. However, the story within the roleplays kept me entertained, though I'm not a fan of lots of dialogue, maybe cut down on the dialogue and get some more prose in there. Pick: Caldera, for having a very good balance of prose and dialogue and an entertaining story. Jamar Gordo v Jimmy Bong Bong's rps seemed to use the length he wrote at as a positive while Gordo was unable to complete what could have been a good rp with quality work in his second attempt. Bong's rps were solid, not spectacular but enough to garner a victory. Jimmy Bong: I liked the whole from Russia with Love gimmicky thing, and for once it did make smile. It's very unusual for a roleplay to make me smile so take this as a big achievement. However, I don't usually like comedy roleplays, though I liked the 'this is like a poorly written spy novel' line quite a lot. Can't really knock the writing as it was very well written. Mr Bong definately deserves his spot in Round 3. Pick: Jimmy Bong Jamar Gordo: I liked the ending of the first roleplay. A nice cliff hanger to keep us wanting more. I also enjoyed reading the writing as you put a lot of effort into it and it shows. However, the beginning of the first roleplay dragged on a lot and bored me a little, but thankfully it picked up towards the end. The second was much better and was all action and adventure which I feel does help his case. I enjoyed reading the second one thoroughly. What a close match, definately the closest one I've had to call up to now. Even though the first Jamar roleplay bored me a little in the beginning and I feel he dragged it on more than was need, the action towards the end, the cliff-hanger and the action in the second roleplay coupled with his good writing skills put him just slightly in the lead although it was a brilliant showing from Mr. Jimmy Bong and he should not be disappointed. Pick: Jamar Gordo Bong. Bwhaahaahaaaaaaaaaaaaa…. Loved it. I especially enjoyed your review of your Round Two match with Luna. Good job. Gordo – your 2nd role play wasn’t as polished as your first, and that gave Bong the advantage of consistency. There are spots in your work that just aren’t all that easy to read – maybe it was because you rushed the role play, but Bong has totally outclassed you this round. Pick: Jimmy Bong Appalacians
