GTT - Day 7 Report

3 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phoenix takes it right to Almasy "You may be the GTT4 poster child, but if you weren't the current PTC Global Champion you'd be as good as yesterday's garbage, just like you were before Survival!" is just one of the countless broadside shots fired today at PTC Global Champion by GTT1 runner up, Jay Phoenix. As the two engaged in an impromptu pissing contest during a scheduled autographed signing, both raised suprisingly long and impressive line of scalps. "Where were you when I defeated the likes of Phoenix Rose, Bubba McCoy, Ivan Stanislav and AWS Man?", Phoenix demanded of Almasy, who counted on his fingers as he returned fire with an equally impressive list. "Karina Wolfenden, Moraless, Dave Harley, Angelus Sorrow, Roulette, Fathom, Kenny Mercury, Syko Arsonist...". The two talked each other down well into the afternoon, leaving a long line of open-mouthed autograph hunters standing in line waiting for quite a long time for one to gain the verbal upper hand and break the impasse, so that they could finally get their autographs. Sky calls out Deville Accomplished tournament performer, Kasper Sky, today called out GTT3 champion Angelo Deville in an unsanctioned impromptu press conference of his own. Sky ran over and ran down Deville's career, declaring that the former Universal Champion was an enigma that belonged in the past with all the other retired relics of yesteryear. Deville has yet to respond to Sky, and his place has yet to be confirmed in the GTT4 lineup. Also active today on the GTT4 promotional circuit was former FAW superstar Jamie Fletcher, LOW's Annihilation, Prime's Joshua Taro Freedom, 4WW's Kris Kanton, Arch Angel, Falcon Mega, UWF's The Renegade Rich Rollins, NGW's Scottywood, XUW's Blaze Medina, Craig Goat, Neon's Hot Mask, Exceptional Dave Hurst, NGW's John Hitchin, John Winters and WWA's Shaman. Did you say confirmations? Drayton McPhereson (FA), Eddie "The Pitbull" Benoit (FA) (no relation) and Fury (WMW) close out the left side of the Kaikouras bracket. Shaman (WWA), Rob Williams (NGW), Shadow (WMW), Jake Salos (Core), Sean White (Neon), Volker Bladwin (FA), LeStatt Knight (FA), and Chris Sanchez (4WW) close out the left side of the Flinders Bracket. Right-side confirmations! GTT4 Tournament Director Argyle unexpectedly released twelve more confirmations today, however their brackets and opponents are not yet available. The lucky twelve individuals are: - Phil Tarrant (FA) - Hirokazu (FA) - The Shadow (XUW) - Kyo Kushinaga (4WW) - Jade (FA) - "Loose Cannon" James von Doom (FA) - El Serpenti (GW) - Desolation (WWA) - Jamar Gordo (FA) - Itagaki Freeze (GW) - Black Cherry (FA) - Blake Wolfson (XUW) After a slow application day, there remains a shade under 20 spots left before the full 192 spots are filled. Signups are expected to close prior to the right bracket announcements on the special edition of PTC Radio.
