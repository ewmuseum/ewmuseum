Semi Final Results!

2 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RICH "THE RENEGADE" ROLLINS V "THE FINAL FANTASY" SEYMOUR ALMASY Judge 1 Rich Rollins: The Sun Is Burning: Part II: Lucky enough for you, I've been reading your work for the past few rounds and it allows me to fully understand whats going on in the story. The advancement of the stalker and the mistress really makes this one a good read, as all your others were. Almasy's appearence at the end provides an Uber-Hook to read the finale! The Sun Is Burning: Finale: 'The Paddy Wagon, Rollins thought without a single shred of humor, how fucking cute.' I nearly cried reading that line. It, along with the remainder of the RP and the finish, make it one of the best RP's of the tournament. Seymour Almasy: Weapon of Choice: I: I'd anticipated this peice since the ending of your bracket final RP. It edges out Rollins first RP in my mind, what with all the revelations; giving you a slight advantage so far. But can you maintain it? we shall see. Weapon of Choice: II: Let me say, there's nothing wrong with this piece. I found the opener boring, and the fact that Seymour left his bag empty wouldn't have given him the opportunity to smuggle a gun. How did he get past Laguardia security with it? Also the fact that there was no suspence. Since you and Rollins infused your stories I knew what'd happen. Decision: Difficult choice, two great wordsmiths duking it out for a place in the finals. While Almasy wrote the best piece of the round in my mind.. he also wrote the worst. Rollins stayed consistent, and his two were good enough to get the nod. Pick: Rich Rollins Judge 2 GOOD. GOD. This is probably the most incredible match I've had the pleasure of reading this entire tournament. And I'm especially glad that I've been following both of you since the first round because the events that occured in your RPs had that much more meaning. I really can't say I found anything scathingly wrong with either set. Great story-telling, compelling drama, jaw-dropping plot twists, and utter, utter chaos. Even better was the collaboration between the two of you. This is a fantastic example of what a late-round GTT match should be. The lives of both Rollins and Almasy are unraveling, spiraling out of control. I was left wondering what the hell else can happen to these guys. Unfortunately, we only get to find out about one of them, for the final round against Andy. And Andy should take notice because whoever goes through to face him in the final is riding on all the momentum in the world. He's going to have a hell of a time defending his GTT crown. There's no denying that this is a painful decision, to send one of you on to the final while the other is forced to bow out. You've both done incredible work all tournament long. You've both pulled off some incredible victories, and have earned the honor of being your respective bracket champions. Given the calibre of the field this year, that is quite an accomplishment. I'm honored to be judging this match, I just want to say that before I make my pick. After much thinking, I've decided that I'm going to award this match to the man whose story really came to a head this round. He's been building up to this point, and hit a dramatic climax that left me amazed. That man is Rich Rollins. Andrew, congratulations on telling an incredible story. If you go through to the last round, I can't wait to see how everything resolves. Sean, congratulations to you as well for another amazing series with Seymour Almasy. I would really love to see what happens with him as well, and to know what you had in mind for the final round, but I don't think [name withheld] would let me split my vote. I don't have anything else to say. What else is there? Pick: Rollins d. Almasy Judge 3 First off, I'd like to pre-empt everything with this: Fuck you Rich Rollins and Seymour Almasy for making my job pretty much fucking impossible. It's hard enough that I possible read one of the most enthralling storylines since I've started e-fedding. What's worse is that both were written so well for the characters. In Rollins RPs, we saw the main story progress. We could see the motives for everything, why they happened. Essentially, the perfect way to read the RPs would've been Rollins first, then Seymour. The reason for this is because...Seymour's story was like a side story. Now, that is not criticism...this is just me commenting on how brilliantly this whole thing was written. I vision this whole thing as some kind of Quentin Tarantino movie with worse dialogue (Sorry, but Tarantino dialogue is the best, bar none) and wreslters. The whole thing was sadistic, heart wrenching, and wow. The suicide topic was really chilling as well. On one hand, Rollins captured the lack of emotion suicides. The Renegade character seems to feel no remorse. And then, Seymour Almasy lays his heart out, trying to kill himself...AND NOTHING. Picking a winner in this is like having twins and being forced to kill one: you have to try to see which one will benefit in the future. Both RPs were TOO GOOD to judge on how they are now; I just can't pick a clear favorite. What I'm turning to is what I'll most look forward to reading. With Rollins, we have the whole arrested thing, the grieving over O'Doyle, and all that. That's bound to be good. With Almasy, we have the whole confessing thing, the grieving over his life, and all that. That's bound to be good. CRAP. If I HAD to choose one, though...it would be Rollins. Why? Because he writes the anti-hero almost to PERFECTION. The little remorse he has, the lying, the backstabbing, the betrayal, the constant renegade emotions, trying to weasel his way out of shit...I don't how you write Rich in terms of Face or Heel, but trying to make this man a Face would do injustice to the character. Seymour, I am taking nothing away from your RPs. I love the character and everything about him. But I have just been made a true Cyber Mark for Rich Rollins. "I'm sorry...that I didn't kill you instead" or whatever the line was...GOLDEN. Pick: Rich Rollins, by barely a millimeter RESULT Rich Rollins d Seymour Almasy, 3 votes to 0 Rollins advances to the final of GTT5, and will meet Shanty Jones for all the marbles. Mr. Rollins may nominate a charity of his choice, as he has claimed the bounty on Almasy. Congratulations to both men. No shame for Sean here. At this stage, Seymour Almasy and Chandler Tsonda have picked up guaranteed Extreme Title shots. If Rollins wins GTT5, they will instead collect Global Title shots. (Shanty Jones is ineligible, although Andy has one in the bank anyway). Finals
