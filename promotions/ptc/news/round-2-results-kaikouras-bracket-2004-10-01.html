Round 2 Results: Kaikouras Bracket

1 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jay Phoenix d Tex "One Gun" Chattanooga to advance. Dave Hurst d Sapphire (2 votes to 1) to advance. Fred Grest advances. Odium d Rzarector (2 votes to 0) to advance. Arch Angel d Blaze Medina (2 votes to 1) to advance. Train d Eddie "the Pitbull" Benoit (2 votes to 0, Benoit DQ'd for linking) to advance. Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis d John Crigger (2 votes to 0) to advance. Angelo Deville d Fury (2 votes to 0) to advance. Kaikouras Round 3 Matches: Jay Phoenix vs Dave Hurst Fred Grest vs Odium Arch Angel vs Train Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis vs Angelo Deville Judging Comments Dave Hurst v Sapphire Dave Hurst: Really solid RP series for the second round. Caught me into it and I read with complete interest. Has a strong character and story. Sapphire: Wow, everything I just said about Dave can be transfered here pretty much. Great series and great match up between these two, both have what it takes to actually win this thing, but only one can win this round. After further review it was still pretty much a coin flip between these two. Great stuff, but I just feel more compelled with Sapphire's work and his character, so I'll pick Sapphire. Great read from both though. Pick: Saphire Dave had some genuinely funny moments in his roleplays and got me giggling a few times. While it wasn’t conventional and it wasn’t the usual, he did warp the boundaries of what a ‘roleplay’ should be about. He took it into that whacked out dream state that many try but many also fail, and it made it an enjoyable read. His narration wasn’t bad either. While it wasn’t something I would have posted in a tournament, he must have disarmed any connotations about his character through humour. Sapphire’s first roleplay blew me away. It was a powerful read from the word go, and while it had me enthralled with the story and the reasons, the second never got off the mat. It was a big letdown, leaving me with many questions and things I wanted to see more of, and because of this, it was quite a letdown. Nowhere near long enough. As the handler didn’t rp for round one, this one goes to Dave Hurst. Pick: Dave Hurst Sapphire, your first role-play was great; however I was disappointed by the 2nd of the two. It seemed rushed and the end left me confused, I thought maybe I'd missed something someplace? Dave, I'm glad you've developed your character better this round. You're writing is funny, original and flows well. I give the win to Dave because I felt it was more consistent. Pick: Dave Hurst Fred Grest Good RP, but the dialogue seemed short and detatched IMO. Too much ambiance and too little dialogue means very little story development. Good RP, but try and add more speaking to the RP, it just didn't seem too compelling to read and I found myself asking myself at the end "What was this for?". Odium vs Rzarector Commentary: I've read the goth gimmick, I've done the goth gimmick, and I have to be honest here, I didn't feel Rzarector's was all that creative a gimmick. Using the death of his "mother," I thought Odium's handler did a much better job of introducing the character to me, and it seemed to be a much more developed character. Rector, while well written (but please proof-read!!), perhaps should've focussed on letting the reader get to know his character a bit more and less on trash talking for his match. Congrats to Odium if he wins, this was a close match. Pick: Odium A guy who sounds like a rapper against a guy who sounds like a drug, this match looks good already. Rzarector opened strongly, but as soon as we hit for the ‘scene rolling in’ or the ‘camera panning around’ I knew these were shallow roleplays. Despite the length, Rza didn’t quite carry them as well as he could have, going on in parts that seemed to take forever to say what a few sentences could easily have said. Still, his roleplay about God was an interesting read, even though I’ve seen a lot of these darkside wrestlers come and go. 6.7/10 Odium was a case of quality beats quantity. He didn’t have to go on forever with things such as scene or describing exactly what was where in the room or in the setting. He left a bit to the imagination which I believe works better than telling people how many salad forks are in the third drawer from the top. Powerful stuff, and the only letdown was that his length suffered slightly. He should have put more into it because they were shorter than Rza’s, again, due to unnecessary padding by the former. Odium also changed tense a few times which was a bit of a letdown, but we all do it. 7/10 Pick: Odium. Blaze Medina vs Arch Angel Arch Angel: -What I enjoyed most about this rp. The way you blended past and present. You gave me an interesting story while keeping the current focus on wrestling. -What I did not enjoy about this rp. I could not always see the events of the past effecting his current life and view of the world. He seemed almost two different people. Granted this could have been the point but the delivery was poor in doing so if that is the case. Blaze Medina: -What I enjoyed most about this rp. The interactions between characters. -What I did not enjoy about this rp. It grew repetitive at times. Not so much that you repeated words but thoughts dragged on much longer than needed to be. Focus more on the idea of telling the reader what needs to be told and stopping before it turns into filler. More pages does not equal a better rp. Granted both rps were still very solid efforts and you should be very proud of your work. :To both, you both put worth a great effort and put heart and soul into the roleplays. Win or lose both should see this match as a challenge and chance to test yourself. In the end however it was the hard decision of this judge to award Arch Angel the match. Pick: Arch Angel With Arch Angel, the dream sequences were nice and revealing of the character. Good description in both RPs, but mediocre dialogue, little storyline, and little to do with the match. For Medina, there wasn't much overall story between the RPs either, but the dialouge was smooth and entertaining, even if some of the extra characters in the second RP were just annoying. Angel's RPs had me skimming after a while, though, while Blaze kept me entertained, so he gets my vote. PIck: Blaze Medina Arch Angel: First one... a bit generic and enclosed... the dream ending doesn't give me any specific reason to want to read the second. Take it a bit further and leave it on a more defined cliffhanger ending. I liked the opening of the second. At first I thought it was just going to be you putting yourself over with a fawning fan, but the Colin Farrell turnaround made me smile. Great descriptive work when Michael went into the living room. I want more of this. Ending when stepping off the plane and into the interview is a fizzer. Medina: For RP1, an OOC tournament summary and some IC trash talk might get you out of Round 1, but its not really roleplay. You may as well be talking to a mirror in the bathroom while brushing your hair. Adding some characters and a generic setting to RP 2 didn't improve the situation much. The support characters didn't actually add much value to the piece, and neither did the rundown of the other matches and title defences you have ahead of you. Best advice I can give you is to spend some time check out some of the tournament favorites. They incorporate mini-stories into their work, rather than shallow conversations. They get a lot of mileage out of this, you get to know who they are and what they are about, rather than scant facts tossed in (e.g. Medina was a marine... so? Tell me more...). Pick: Arch Angel, specifically for the descriptive work in RP2. Do more of that, much more of that, and you will go further. Steer well clear of the interviews and plane sequences, they blew. Train vs Benoit Train: A nice RP, great character and story development. Kept me interested, nothing much more to say. Eddie Benoit: First off, I'm not a fan of RPs being linked out. I don't know if there is a rule against it in this tournament, but there have been in other tournaments, including previous GTT's if I remember correctly. The RP was way too short for the story to seem compelling, I know that size doesn't matter, but when story developement needs to be done, some work has to be put into it. The HTML was too hard for me to read through, the dark red on the black background really hurt my eyes to read. I had to highlight it just so I could read it. Pick: Train. Train: Couple of careless typos. Try and use less words/sentences to get your ideas across. Either that or have some dialogue instead of a constant stream of identically sized paragraphs. It makes the mind wander. Eddie Benoit: Linking RP's instead of posting them on the site is against the rules, so you lose anyway. I read your piece though. You're just starting out, the best thing you can do if you see yourself still playing this game in a few years time is read some of the work of other players in this tournament who make it to the bracket finals and beyond, and take note of their style. Use that to expand beyond what you are doing now in GWA and you will own that place hands down. Lose the name though. Even a well-worn name like Gravedigger is better than a WWE-derived name. Pick: Train. John Crigger vs Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis Jackson’s 1st Roleplay- Strongly worded introduction. Not a big fan of how it seems to skip around though. It starts with a strong focus on being alone, then jumps to the mention of a woman, an abusive father, and loneliness, without any sort of connection between the three. Again it skips, without any real transition. It seems to be from past to present, or from mind to the world, but I can’t be really sure on that, because then it goes back to mind/past and skips over to a bar. The wrestling scene though didn’t seem to have much to do with anything, besides introducing the girl. But since she was already mentioned, it seemed kind of pointless. The bar scene was very solidly written and put the tie in of the woman/father/loneliness, but left me a little confused as to how Jackson is feeling. Is he mad or hurt? And why the hell was he going to kill the bartender. Overall the writing was real strong, but the transitions could have gone a little smoother. Jackson’s 2nd Roleplay- Strong introduction. The transitions have me confused once again though. It seems as though we go into Jackson’s head, but it seems like he’s narrating this story to himself, which doesn’t seem necessary unless he’s trying to make some sort of decision. Another thing I’m not a fan of is the continuous “…” . Seems unnecessary to have it at the end of almost every other line unless there is going to be something that’s a surprise, or an actual pause in speech. This piece was very well written but still left me confused as to why Jackson was in the confession box. I got that in the end he felt he was called there to help Billy, but seeing as though he wasn’t expecting Billy there, what originally drew him there? Great character development bit, though I don’t understand the connection between the first and second roleplay. Crigger’s Roleplay- Introduction was weak. It starts off with Crigger, and a man the reader may or may not be familiar with. For those who aren’t, one who happens to be me, there is zero explanation or detail as to who this guy is. It mentions them walking into “another” bar. But being this is the beginning of the roleplay, it’s hard to understand how they could be going into “another”, unless this is the next part of a series that wasn‘t mentioned. The dialogue was hard to follow. The conversation just starts out of nowhere, and with a line that seems to make no sense. The beauty of the whole thing? What’s “the whole thing?”. It jumps around horribly to where the reader has to look back several times and still may not understand still what’s going on. Plus I find it hardly believable that winning one match is an amazing streak. It continues and only gets more confusing. He says he’s listened to what Jackson has had to say, but what did Jackson say to him? After reading both lines I saw nothing directed towards Crigger. All together I found this to be a very difficult read that jumped around way too much. Winner: Jackson Jackson, WTF!? I felt like I had tripped over and somehow landed in a badly written romance novel. You go through, but only because Crigger wasn't up to the same standard that got him through the last round. Pick: Jackson Fury vs Angelo Deville Fury: I don't see many straight script formats anymore, so this was a pleasure to read because of that. I was a long term fan of scritp style back in the day. Novel is compelling, but as a reader, script is always easier to read. Good character and story development, will be a tough choice. Angelo Deville: Deville just wowed me, I've read his work before, but this one was off the charts, really some of the best work of his I've read. The character is rock solid and so is the RP. The style is something unique and interesting. I'll have to look at both RPs again to see who my pick is. Pick: Angelo Deville, it came down to Deville's RP just being more compelling and Fury having a few grammar mistakes. Fury: Kudos to you for showing up and having a crack at Deville, when players with bigger names than you are resigning in defeat rather than losing a bit more sleep and making the effort to put up a piece against other tournament favourites. I like that, its the right attitude to have and you do WMW proud. Now comes the hard bit, your RP though is pretty much a rundown of what's going on for you in WMW, and a lightweight conversation about GTT4. Doesn't bring much to the table in terms of an interesting plot or anything that makes me want to read more from you next round, so I can't put you through. Deville: Did you get the word abstruse from a thesaurus? I had to look it up! Parts of this would be perfect for a match against Unstoppable Eugene. Totally love the interaction with Satan, and am hoping you actually do a torture scene at some stage with this type of dynamic going. The ending to this is amazing! I tell people to do cliffhanger transitions, it doesn't get much more cliffhanger than this. Kaikouras
