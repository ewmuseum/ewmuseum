How The Seeds Were Planted

28 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, a Mommy and a Daddy who love each other very much... Wait, wrong talk. Here's what I did: 1) I cleared my schedule. I wanted to digest each story in it's entirety. No skipping, no moving past parts, no "not finishing", nothing. It took three days after work from around 8PM to 1AM just to read everything. Explains the recent Twitter silence to some, I'd reckon. 2) Due to my terrible memory of late, I wrote everything I liked and didn't like in a notebook. (more on that later) I read each story in the order of posting, meaning Jake was first, Kyle was last. 3) Each story was given the determination listed in a previous thread: NO-- People who weren't getting seeded. DOUBTFUL -- People who are unlikely to be seeded, but still considered. POSSIBLE -- People who could be seeded with help. PROBABLE; BUMPABLE -- People who HAD to be in the discussion longer, when it hit 16. PROBABLE; LAST RESORT BUMP -- People who could only be bumped if 15 other people wrote way, way better. DEFINITE -- People who were not being bumped at any cost. 4) Once I had the 27 POSSIBLE and above seeds, I went to work. a) I pitted all the D!!! against each other. I got my top 3 that way. b) I pitted all the PR, LR against each other. I filled in #4-10 that way. c) I pitted the P,B against each other and filled the top 16. d) I slept on it. e) I woke up and reread my notes. f) I forgot entirely about what I'd done, and reranked EVERYTHING again, without prejudice. g) My top 12 stayed the same. They were then set in stone. h) I took into account my guaranteed seedings and ranked my top 16. Two were already in the top 16, and one was on the cusp (Aaron was ranked very highly, possibly already in. Can't remember) i) I took a nap. j) I ignored the rankings. k) I reread the rest of the RPs fighting for the last spots. l) Determined it was Voss. m) Sighed contentedly. CRITERIA 1) Story. a) Did it pull me in? Did I identify with it? Was it readable and enjoyable? b) How was the prose? Was it well written? c) Spelling and grammar alright? (Not as important here) 2) Character a) Was the character static? Did he/she develop? Was the character one I could identify with? b) How did the character develop? Was I shown not told? Did my imagination get a workout? 3) Je Nais Seis Quoi? a) Was there an X Factor? Was there an intangible that didn't fit another category? Was there something there that was undeniable? b) Was there something there that cost someone spots? And, that's it. I came up with my top 16, and while I wavered a bit between 13-20 (minus the guarantees) I'm happy with what I have. The Notebook I'm very likely to send it to the eventual winner. I'm very likely to, after the tournament, post everything inside of it so you can get a glimpse into my thought process. I will not reveal it before hand, as I need to go into the tournament with no bias toward any person or RP, and strictly judge the story at hand. That may show some sort of bias, and that should be eliminated. Questions?
