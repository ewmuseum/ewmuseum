Round 1 Results: Pyrenees Bracket

18 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These are results, they are not Round 2 Brackets. Those will be announced after all the results are up. No-shows and Double-no-shows are not noted. If you haven't read the judging thread, do it now: http://community.primetimecentral.net/showthread.php?t=9955 Unstoppable Eugene advances. Noah Hanson advances. Kenny Mercury advances. Dave Harley d Black Cherry (1 vote) to advance. John “The Prophet” Adams d Tessa Windsor (2 votes to 0) to advance. Salaok advances. Dani Furher d Hoyt Williams (1 vote) to advance. Josh d James von Doom (1 vote) to advance. Eric Lopez advances. Robb Hardy advances. The Shadow d Token Weed (2 votes to 0) to advance. Blaise McClancy advances. Dallas Reeves d Scott Vyper (1 vote) to advance. Nathaniel Draven d Ezra Kazee (1 vote) to advance. Tarrant d Khaled (1 vote) to advance. Judging comments: Unstoppable Eugene: To this day I still do not understand the point of the quotes. They never play a part of the rp and seem to be there to get a laugh. Problem is it's someone else's joke, not original material. Character relationships are poor. Good mention of PTC and the tournament however. Nice jokes but never anything to make me laugh too hard. Better work than I expected however. Dave Harley vs Black Cherry * Harley’s RP: I’ve seen his work on many occasions and this was not his best work. I feel it got his point across and tied in both his feelings on the GTT to his own status in WWA at the moment. Vengeance was about as perfect a concept for him to write as could be, given his current story in WWA he had a huge amount of room to maneuver storyline-wise. No ambience and a little short but it did the job for round one and gave him a victory. * Cherry’s RP: This was, in my opinion, a very sloppily thought up RP. It was nothing more then an in-ring promo and an entrance copied from The Sandman of ECW. I was extremely disappointed when I saw that the use of ‘jealousy’ was of course directed at her status of being female. I honestly feel that the handler could have used a number of other things rather than the old male vs female routine that is seen in every inter-gender match. Pick: Dave Harley Tessa Windsor Vs. John Adams Tessa Windsor: Good RP, wasn't as good as it could have been, but a solid effort first round. John Adams: I like this guy's style. I'm not a big fan of excessive HTML use, but I looked past that and read the story and it really got me, and that doesn't happen too often. He'll be a person to contend with down the line if this was just an introductory. I expect his work to get more interesting down the line. Pick: John Adams --- T.Windsor (FA) vs J.Adams (LOW) – Winner (Adams) --- * Windsor’s RP: I liked how this rp built around Windsor’s need for something more than what she had. How, through all of her victories and successes she still wasn’t where she felt she could be. I was in no way impressed by the use of ambience or setting in this rp as frankly there wasn’t any. The thought process that Tessa went through was solid; the use of her “mistrust” word I felt was poor. It seemed that for every good quality in her RP there was another quality that the RP lacked. She had something involving ‘regret’ that she bolded, obviously wanting to express something, but nothing really came about from it which made me question her reasons for drawing attention to the word. She is a very good writer, no doubt, but this was not enough to give her a victory. * Adams’ RP: His overall style was one that I rarely see. His look of the rp made it really come off the page and intrigued me before I even began to read it. I wasn’t thrilled by a little lacking in the use of his word ‘intolerance’ as I feel that is a word that could be used across a wide scope of characters and situations and he could have delved deeper at points and used that topic a bit more. His flashback involving his father was short yet very strong and to the point. His use of ambience especially in the beginning was very good and overall was what made me give him the win over Windsor. I feel that if you can make a reader see what you want them to see and feel what you are trying to express then your work has done its job. His work did that and while I would have liked to see maybe a little more length and detail to it, he said what he wanted to say and expressed his characters’ feelings very well and overall I just enjoyed his RP’s look and his use of ambience. He earned his win. Pick: John Adams Hoyt Williams vs Dani Furher Williams’ RP: A very well-written, almost biographical account of William’s life. Wasn’t much outside of the history of Hoyt Williams. I wasn’t very impressed with the lack of use of “remorse” as most rps I’ve read in GTT4 were able to actually use their words to an extent. Williams really didn’t do that. Was not very impressed with the racist comments in the rp, but whatever, it wasn’t much and it built Williams’ character a bit. Overall an ok, but not overly-impressive Roleplay. * Furher’s RP: This rp lacked something…I just don’t know what. It had good use of ambience and used the word very well…passiveness. A very tough word to be dealt but it was used well. I liked how the writer switched between a thought-process rp and a flashback-type rp. It gave me a hint of the character in a way that was different then Williams’ version of just telling me his biography. I really liked the ending and that was one of the main things about the rp that won me over. Very well done. Pick: Furher Josh vs James von Doom * Moody’s RP: I really enjoyed this work. I felt that the setting was perfect for his topic of loneliness. His use of a first-person told story was good and his use of ambience was extremely good at the beginning and allowed me to connect with the setting. The story was well told about loss and about Moody’s loneliness. The font choice was not my favorite and I felt there were points where Moody went either to deep or not deep enough. However, overall it was very well written and Moody is very deserving of this match victory. * von Doom’s RP: This RP was based mostly around von Doom’s obsession with becoming known. However, the way he wrote the rp, with von Doom’s comments and then a mocking response by some ‘narrator’, took away from his rp. Very little ambience was used, and even less relevant ambience, which was a disappointment to me as I read his work. My feeling is that there was not enough effort put into the rp to stand on its own in a 1-rp limit setting. Pick: Josh Token Weed vs The Shadow Token Weed: Wow! Nice RP, nice introductory, I know the character already, but it was nice to throw a little bit more at me. The Shadow: Another great RP. These two had a close first round, I'm still trying to figure out which should really get the pick. I knew practically nothing about The Shadow and now I know enough to follow him through the tournament, that's a great start. Pick: I'd have to go with The Shadow, but it was extremely close IMO. * Weed’s RP: Arguably the best opening to an rp that I’ve read in this tournament. This was just extremely well thought out and made me want to continue reading. Weed’s comments about genocide, tyranny and his desire to be even more hated were all very solidly written. He let me down a bit in the story portion and I just felt that it was not as good overall as Shadow’s was. If he had put a little more work into the story, a little more detail and something more at the end then it really would have won him this match in what was the closest match of this bracket for me to judge. * Shadow’s RP: This was the best use of ambience in the entire bracket. I could literally see everything that Shadow was writing out and the rp simply flowed from one point to another. He didn’t try to over exceed the needed detail nor did he leave me wondering about something. He left me wanting to read more of his work and while his initial opening was not as solid as Weed’s, his overall rp was a little bit better and he deserves the win. Pick: The Shadow Dallas Reeves vs Scott Vyper Reeves’ RP: Not much to say here other than that the rp entertained me and I look forward to reading more of Reeves. I was impressed with the story that Reeves told and the ambience was ok. Grammar was solid as compared to Vyper’s and just overall I felt that Reeves did the most he could with the amount of length he used. It was a god overall rp and one worthy of a round one victory. * Vyper’s RP: The first thing that struck me here was the noticeable grammatical errors in parts of the rp. Nothing big, but those things stick with me. The character’s conversation with the Fetish character did not impress me or make me want to read more. The eventual promo of sorts done by the Vyper character was good but not enough to beat a comparatively flawless rp by Reeves. Pick: Reeves Nathaniel Draven vs Ezra Kazee * Draven’s RP: I really enjoyed this work. Grammar was solid and just overall I felt that Draven did the most he could with the amount of length he used. I enjoyed the way he used a first-person approach to his rp and allowed the reader in the head of his character. There was not much ambience used but that is already tough to ask for in a first-person setting. I liked his story portion at the end with the speech at the podium. Overall, very well done and deserved of the win. * Kazee’s RP: Not much to say about this one other than that it was very short compared to what could have been done with it. If I wanted to read a paper then I would have bought one. I don’t want to read an rp about a guy reading a newspaper. The use of the word “opinionated” was used far too literally and overall it just wasn’t something that intrigued me. Pick: Draven Khaled vs Tarrant Khaled’s RP: A solid effort on Khaled’s part. The opening part regarding the match did not seem very relevant after I’d read the whole thing. The ambience seemed choppy and the rp was shorter than it could have been as I felt there was more that could have been told. It was an ok rp but not one I would choose to stand on its own in a one-rp battle with someone like Tarrant. * Tarrant’s RP: Not much needs to be said here. He had an amazing use of ambience. He had the perfect length for the rp. He told me what I wanted to know about his character. He inserted the GTT4 into his rp, which I know is not easy for some people who do a more character-developing type of rp despite the match recognition being necessary in a tournament of this caliber. Tarrant had all of that and he won the match in convincing fashion. Pick: Tarrant Pyrenees
