Judging -- Open Call

29 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I need judges. So far, I have [name withheld], Ben M. and myself. I need people who can -- in the first round -- help take some of the load off our shoulders. So, with that said, here's what is expected of those who would like to judge the first round: SHORTFORM JUDGING We're not looking for indepth analysis. We're looking for MAYBE a paragraph that gives constructive feedback. That's it, that's all. YOU WILL NOT BE ANONYMOUS. Your name will be attached to the match after RP time is over, and will be revealed, too. No more Judge 1, Judge 2. HOWEVER, that doesn't give someone with an axe to grind because they lost a close decision time to go ballistic. If that happens, said member will be lifebanned. You will not compromise the integrity of this tournament, because you feel like measuring dicks. ON THE OTHER HAND, YOU WILL NOT BE ANONYMOUS We're adults. 95% of the people here know each other. Please keep your comments as a judge out of a public forum. Prediction threads are fine, so long as you don't go overboard gushing on a particular person. Keep it short so that the haters can't use any bias (perceived or otherwise) against you. Don't let your words be used against you, please. I don't want to fight trolls. BE RESPECTFUL. The days of "this is the worst pile of shit I have ever read and you should cut your hands off so you don't type any of this garbage ever again" are over. Brutal honesty is not the name of the game. Constructive feedback is key and gushing compliments are encouraged. If the RP is a big piece of shit, keep that in private when the participant asks you why he didn't win. So, in the FIRST ROUND who's going to help judge? I'm only looking for first rounders, so, please don't reply that you'll help when/if knocked out. We'll get to that later. Thanks. JUDGES: Peter S. Argyle. Ben M. Tom [name withheld]. Pierre H.
