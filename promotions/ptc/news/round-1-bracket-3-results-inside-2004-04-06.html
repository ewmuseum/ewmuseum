Round 1 Bracket 3 Results inside

6 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have two remaining judges holding up Brackets 2 and 4 results. If they don't get their stuff in by this time tomorrow I'll find alternatives. Both have experienced computer difficulties. Shit happens unfortunately. Round 2 Roleplaying will commence this weekend, so we can get back in sync with full weeks. There will be less matches of course, and more judges this time around (hopefully!). Anyway, putting Bracket 3 out of their misery ;) Bracket 3 Match 9: Jason Starr (NGW) v The Cockjockey (XUW) The Cockjockey (XUW) wins by default after Jason Starr no-showed. Match 10: Coricidon (4WW) v James Edwards (RPW) v Raijin (FA) Coricidon (4WW) 2 votes defeated Raijin (FA) 1 vote and James Edwards (RPW) Judge 1 Edwards: Essentially, the first roleplay served as Background for the second. it introduced the character, ran through his history, explained what he was doing right now, and then set up the kicker. The Ex girlfriend re-appearing was something I was glad to see, as without it, I couldn't see a purpose to the piece. As soon as she appeared, it all made sense. Then came the second. It really worked for me because I enjoy stories of closure and redemption. James saw his ex girlfriend, feelings came back, she opened her mouth, and Edwards realised that he was glad she left. It was a great emotion series, and look forward to reading more. Coricidon: Well, HLA Sells! Once I got over the fact I thought I wa about to read a story out of a lesbian porn mag (Don't get me wrong, thats no bad thing!) I was suddenly snapped out by Coricidon battling her emotions, and snapping at this woman, all for saying she loves her. Showed a great portion of her personality. The roleplay was emotionally charged, and I enjoyed it. The second roleplay was EXCELLENT. Although the first portion dragged a little, once she lef tthe gym, it all took off. The news stories about the 19 year old Teen Suicide.... you KNEW it was Sky, and I found myself waiting on the edge of my seat to Coricidon discovering this. Superbly written. I HAVE to read the next one. Raijin: The first piece, much like the others, served as a good introduction to the character. Unfortunately, it didn;t serve much more than that. Yes, it showed that he is now a human and can experience pain, but what else? I couldn;t find myself getting into this much. The other thing that I noticed was that this god seemed remarkably english in his mannerisms and such. I don;t know, just couldn;t understand it. The second was MUCH better than the first. The comparisons he was making with faith and what not and his situation were great. And really enjoyed reading it. My pick for the winner goes for Coricidon. I just LOVED reading it, showed some great emotion, and more importantly, left me right on the edge of my seat for the next instalment. Judge 2 I picked Raijin to win for a couple of reasons. The introduction of his character was far superior to the others, and you were able to get a greater sense of who Raijin was, where as at times I was confused as to what Coricidon and Edwards were talking about. Coricidon and Edwards both used a similar story of love in their RP's, and didn't focus as much on the match as Raijin did. Raijin also used their story of love against them in his second RP, saying how he'd exploit their weaknesses, something which the other two never talked about. Raijin's character was very interesting, as he is a spirit from heaven who's come to earth for the purpose of experiencing life. Normally that would seem complete fantasy, but the character and RP's were written a way in which he was able to pull it off very effectively. The other two wrote good RP's too, but I just thought that Raijin's character and story were more interesting. Judge 3 James Edwards First RP, light and fluffy, consisted a bit of Edwards’ history. History is okay in small doses, this one dragged out way too long for little return for the amount of words, and at the end, despite the potential for future RP set up with the two women, I was left with a feeling of “is that it?”. Second RP – I started off thinking that the first paragraph was making me think too hard about things I really didn’t want to be thinking about this time of night, but then it yielded a contender for quote of the round – “At the end of the day we are all loves bitch”. I read the rest of it, it was easy to read, flowed well. I’ll say at this point that Edwards is a good writer, a welcome addition to any fed on the net’s roster. I’ll also say that I don’t go for this Mills and Boon Love Actually type of stuff in this context, so I can’t look at him seriously to advance here, because the idea of reading more of it in later rounds is not particularly appealing. Coricidon First RP, anything but light and fluffy. This piece was very focused, with no lulls. Rather than going into a full history, some interesting elements of Coricidon’s character were introduced to us, in a most captivating way. This to me is a great example of a roleplay written for a tournament outside of the author’s home federation. A self contained story that doesn’t regurgitate past achievements or attempt to describe someone’s life story compacted into a few pages in order to introduce them, and yet gave enough to hook my interest in the character, so that I want to read the next roleplay. The other two are going to have had to have worked very hard and very smart to do something better than this. Second RP. I’m a bit apprehensive going into this because of comments made about Edwards work. The first one dealt with love, but was powerful enough in its presentation that I didn’t feel like I was watching a chick flick. I read the second one and am left wondering what will happen when Coricidon, who’s just gone through a major personal development process, finds out what has obviously gone down, as anything other than Sky topping herself is not possible. The possibility for further character development in the next set is very good, with analyzing and implementing the stages of grief. Raijin First RP. Damn me, another great character introduction with no re-hashing of history but in your face character development from ground up. I love it! No obvious weaknesses here that are worth pointing out, and we are treated to great use of descriptive vocabulary, without overdoing it. Second RP. The written word throughout is again flawless, however I didn’t get the same buzz from this one as I did the last one. Its like its expanding on concepts introduced in the first one which could have actually been in the first one, without actually adding much in the way of new ideas or direction, then reacting to what the other two have produced. I’m a fan of paying attention to your opponent in a tournament, but I felt the implementation here was a bit rushed and not in keeping with the style of the first RP. The words match the overall style, but the meaning doesn’t, it reminds me too much of elementary level trash talk, where you just throw yoru opponents weaknesses back at them in a too obvious manner. Making a pick here is extremely difficult. The choice is between Raijin and Coricidon. I think Raijin is a marginally better writer, at least in this series, but I want to read Coricidon’s next set more than I do Raijin’s, because he hasn’t left me anywhere to go at the end of his second, whereas the potential Coricidon’s next set could be absolutely blistering now that its gotten past the danger of becoming the next Meg Ryan film. With that in mind, Coricidon gets the nod. Match 11: Gordon "Denrol" Korth (WWA) v The Blue Coral (4WW) v Thai Trang (UWF) No contest due to all three no-showing. Apologies to Korth's handler because I forgot to post the reminder/warning about the missing bios like I said I would. Match 12: Ethan Knight (RPW) v Rocky Adams (WWA) Ethan Knight (RPW) d Rocky Adams (WWA) 2 votes to 0. Judge 1 MY vote in this match swayed toward Ethan Knight. A pretty even matchup overall. Despite putting out more roleplays, I found that Adams' roleplays didn't grip me to read on to the extent that Knight's did. Adams' did however show a development of his character's feelings going into the match, which i liked. In my honest opinion I feel Ethan Knight deserves to move forward in the tournament as he has demonstrated his potential and shown an interesting side to his character. It would be nice to see more than 1 RP from him for the next round. Judge 2 Rocky Adams Both of his roleplays being trash talk without a great deal of depth shows that he didn’t research the abilities of the handler of his opponent very well and attempt to counter them. If the bio wasn’t enough, he was only a PM away to ask for more information. Nothing wrong with his work as such for what it is, however without depth its not going to compete very well in a competition like this. Kudos to him for jumping in and having a go at it though. His written word isn’t bad at all, in time he’ll develop the ability to play this aspect of the game well enough to get further than the first round. Ethan Knight Testify! Some random trash talk in the midst of some pretty decent preachyness. Writing effective preachy is a hard skill to master, it requires some background and experience. Without knowing the handler too well, I would say he’s either spent a bit of time in church or watched quite a few movies, because he sold me on this one. Can he keep it up in future rounds and maintain the originality? My pick goes to Ethan Knight. CW1 brackets
