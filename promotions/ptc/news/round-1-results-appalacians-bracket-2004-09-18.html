Round 1 Results: Appalacians Bracket

18 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These are results, they are not Round 2 Brackets. Those will be announced after all the results are up. No-shows and Double-no-shows are not noted. If you haven't read the judging thread, do it now: http://community.primetimecentral.net/showthread.php?t=9955 Seymour Almasy d Ash (2 votes to 0) to advance. Misfit d Korey Drock (1 vote) to advance. Craig Goat d Jonathan Borealis (1 vote) to advance. Jenna Ryder advances. Gonzalo Rey d Kash (1 vote) to advance. Darek DeAngleo d Chaplin Graves (1 vote) to advance. Flyin’ Brian Calvin avances. Joey Vendetti advances. El Serpenti advances. John Hitchin advances. Olf Magnus Svensson advances. Jimmy Bong d Mike Montgomery (2 votes to 0) to advance. Luna Morbid d “The Renegade” Rich Rollins (2 votes to 0) to advance. Jamar Gordo advances. Steven Caldera d Triumph Frost (1 vote) to advance. Violence Jack advances. Judging Comments: Seymour Almasy versus Ash Almasy (9.4): Great RP. Theme stuck in well here, like the "life as a video game bit". Great job with the Aya bit. Not quite a 10 in my book but it comes close. Ash (8.8): Offensiveness is a word... (a degree of being offensive). Just wasn't used here in the apporpriate manner. The theme should've been used spariginly, not as the "Word of the Day". Pick: Almasy Ash went down here. Almasy wins this one and shows why he’s PTC Global Champ. DESPITE THIS PART I, TO BE CONTINUED STUFF. I think the arrogance works in their favour? Pick: Almasy Misfit vs Korey Drock Misfit: Roleplay was good. I liked the intro and the road parable, and the course he took to get there. It was a nice way of letting us know about the history of him without boring the reader with details about a fed he’s never heard of or people he’s never seen. The roleplay was tasteful and didn’t go too far. However I don’t think he hammered the evilness point home quit enough. He was confident and this was a positive. However I do think there is a thing as too much description, and Misfit detailed things too much. We all know what a church looks like, he didn’t need to spend 4 paragraphs on it. I found myself skipping and leaping this section. As for his ‘to be continued…’ I’ll be the judge of that ;-) 7.5 / 10 Korey Drock: This roleplay just dragged on and on and on in a monotone voice, that almost had me falling off my chair in sleep. It was good, and it was lengthy, but he didn’t really have any high or low points. It was just a long number of huge blocks of text. Ambition never really made any high pinnacle point, because he just sort of kept speaking like a teletext machine. Should have broken the etxt up, maybe used some CAPITALS or exclamation points!! Maybe asked some questions? You see how it makes a difference. 6.7 / 10 Winner: Misfit. Craig Goat vs Jonathan Borealis While both men made great use of the theme words, Goat's interested me a lot more. I want to know what happens next to this quirky character, while I didn;t learn as much about Borealis. Gonzalo Rey vs Kash Gonzalo Rey Vs Kash Kash: Started off nicely, but his roleplay was let down by the brackets. It’s just a formatting thing I know but they just make it read like a script. There’s no need for them, I’ve even let one of my guys know that in this tourney. Seemed a little bit over the top with the smashing of the window and the hammering of the car, from frustration. Didn’t fit the theme very well, but hey, he gave it a solid shot. 6.5 / 10 G Rey: Another cokehead. What the fuck is it with these wrestlers doing drugs? Still, his had meaning. He portrayed an edgier, seedier side of life in his roleplay and he actually gave some backstory as well which was nice to see. He tackled disrespect well, being the drug barons whipping boy and for this he gets a higher grade. Gozalo Rey seems a more intense and deeper character than Kash and while I’d love to see more of that chica in the kinky shit from Kash, he’s going to take an early shower. G Rey: 7/10 Winner: G Rey Darek DeAngleo vs Chaplin Graves Chaplin Graves V Derek Deangelo Chaplin: Not a bad roleplay. I liked the description he gave, let me decide what it looked like for myself, without going in so far as to describing how many rivets hold the floorplate to the external wall. The guy seems like he’s got talent, just unsure of how to really bring it out just yet. A few spelling mistakes let him down, as did a few too many/too quickly repeated words, but he’s not bad. And the colour fucked him over too. 6.5 / 10 Derek: This roleplay was long. And confusing to an outsider. Be that as it may, he put in a lot of effort. This whole talk of dark angels and demons in wrestling as been done to death. How many people can have these voices in their head, how many of these people are pychoes who somehow manage to lead a real, normal life AND find the time to garner hot chicks? A bit unbelievable but if this is his character I respect it. Nice kind of twist towards the end, I think. Not sure if he tapped on the lust angle well enough, I was expecting blowjobs in nightclub toilets. Still, he won this one. And what is with the to be continued? Cocky bastards!! ;-D 7/10 Winner: Derek Deangelo Brian Calvin Brian: I can almost relate to some of the stuff he had here, and for that reason this roleplay hit home a bit more than it was probably meant to. The guy who goes out after being forced from the nest, makes a success of himself and then returns to the promised land. I liked his style as well, almost a jovial approach to the rolepaly. Earned him good points with me. Brian Calvin (9.4): I liked this roleplay... I mean I really like this roleplay. Lost me in the beginning but the involvement of the family and the way how Brian bullshitted his parrents were classic. The RP kept me waiting for how he was going to break the news to his family and their reaction (which there wasn't really any but still it was a great point to earn.) El Serpenti El Serpenti (9.1) Great RP. I liked the intro to El Serpenti's character and background. And (not to start another conspiracy theory) maybe the Voodoo Lady casted some magic on Dusk... And I now want to see more of this roleplay especially after the introduction of the lady at the end. Score would've been more but I kind of had to take away for the no accent marks. (Yes I know this isn't Spanish class but there is a big difference between Yes and If... I had to take away for something after all!) Good luck in the next round(s). Jimmy Bong vs Mike Montgomery Mike Montgomery Vs. Jimmy Bong Mike Montgomery: A good RP, but I found it to be your normal introductory RP. I find that intro RPs are very stereotypical a lot of times and follow the same basic rules, this one was no different then most that I’ve read. Jimmy Bong: Really original stuff here. I’m a fan of comedy and maybe that’s why I thought it was great. But the character is funny, interesting, and can go a long ways. Reminds me a lot of other similar characters in the past that I’ve read. Nice and refreshing. Pick: Jimmy Bong Mike: His roleplay was not bad I guess, but then again not amazing either. It was sort of typical run of the mill stuff, and even though his theme was nonchalance, it was still a bit sort of ‘degrade your opponent by trash talking’ style of thing. This guy is a World Champion and I know a lotta people will be riding their bets with him, but I wouldn’t write the other guy off just yet. Bong: He won. Winner: Bong Rich Rollins vs Luna Morbid Morbid used the theme word better, and I found myself enthralled in the reading of the piece. Rollins was good, but not good enough. Rich Rollins versus Luna Morbid (MY GAWD THIS WAS TOUGH TO CALL) Rollins (8.9) Liked the nightmare sequence and how it related to the match. The expansion of the "not fighting woman" fact could've been more though. (Grade booster right there.) Luna Morbid (9.0). It was a good RP, but I kind of found the whole "do this or I'll hurt you/kill you bit was getting old.. Toward the end a few points were deducted for the non clarity in the pronous. (Like I knew who 'he' was but I didn't know whether 'she' was you or someone else.) Winner: Morbid (But Rollins should take a bow) Jamar Gordo Probably one of the most interesting roleplays I have ever read in my seven years of roleplaying. Needless to say it matches your theme to the letter. This is a roleplay I definently wish to see a Part II down the line somewhere. Steven Caldera vs Triumph Frost Winner: Caldera. The other guy used a camera. Its WWE shit that needs to be canned, because it’s unimaginative. Appalacians
