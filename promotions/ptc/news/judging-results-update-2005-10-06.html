6 October 2005 About a third of the judges have their picks in so far. Judging for the two large matches has been finalised. I will be writing the main event on the weekend. So if all other judging comes through and match writers meet their target, we should be seeing results early next week. Anyone who wants to submit spots to me to appear in the main event, feel free. I've already gotten some great suggestions so far from some of the participants, no reason that anyone else can't contribute if they don't want to. Anyone contributing a spot will be credited :) 10 October 2005 Update: - One singles match received (ironically, one of the no-contested matches). - One singles match completely judged.
