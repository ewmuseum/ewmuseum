HIGH FLYER V SAMMY BROWN Judge 1 While I really dig High Flyer, the roleplays contrasted each other so sharply, it came down to what I enjoyed more. And while I tend to go for comedy over almost anything else, I really, really, really enjoyed Brown's effort this round. Pick: Sammy Brown Judge 2 Sammy Brown- RP #1 I'm very lost right now. Who the hell is Bradley Stevenson? I'm hoping this gets clarified. Upon reading further, this is a very interesting dynamic. Involving another character...was Stevens in the tournament? Reginald Jenkins? You have to explain these things, even if it is a quick little thing! Right now, I'm completely lost in this RP. Now I'm back in it...this is probably the worst judging ever, but I have to say, you lead on for a while and not explain a thing, but then it all comes into light. I'm not too sure what I think about this... Now, finally, it seems that something I'm truly lost on. What was Brown's history? Why is it so controversial? But, on the other hand, I do like how you're writing on the past rounds, so it doesn't leave an awkward dynamic for the judge. You're telling a story that doesn't make me want to see where you're going, but it make me want to see how you got to there, which is a good thing. I don't care how you're going to accomplish the next round now, I want to see how you got to this round. (Does that make sense?) Overall, a "meh" RP with a "good" dynamic. RP #2 Your narrative is boring. Nothing really innovative, you're just telling it how it is. No flare, nada. Now I'm not too sure why Bradley is in so much trouble...you didn't make it super clear in the first RP if they caught him for fixing the match or not. Oh, he gambled? Ok, there it is again...see first RP. "He needs to win this tournament." UGH. It's amazing how many story lines make the tournament a source of reliability. High Flyer- RP #1 I'm not even that far, but this RP is so zany and easy to read that he keeps me going, even though I'm a bit lost. Who are all these larrys? Regardless, insanity is good if it's entertaining. That was previously on! Groovy. Let's get this installment going. "HIGH FLYER: I’m going to kill you. [Shrug] And if that video tape bites me in the ass when I actually kill you and goes into evidence in a court of law, well then so be it." Awesome. While I'm not sure of ANYTHING that's really happening at all, it's all fucking hillarious. The entire insanity of the situation works well with the concept of the character, and, while I do have a bit of an understanding (I lied, ok! I LIED!), the confusion just makes it all the better. A fun read, without a doubt. RP #2 You make skimming an impossibility. Just thought I'd toss that out there. "TONY DAVIS: You know any famous Carl’s? Now that’s just gonna bug me…" Hillarious Your RP was just like the first...that's a good thing. High Flyer wins, hands down. A hillarious piece that kept my laughing the whole time, while Sammy Brown's made me frustrated. Result Sammy Brown d High Flyer, 2 votes to 1 JACK MURPHY V VANGELUS OLSIG Judge 1 Jack Murphy Aspiring for Approval III- Hard Times It took me a bit to mull over exactly what it was that I liked about this piece. I think the best thing about it is that the first person is used to good effect. In my opinion, it's really hard to pull off first person without coming off very awkward on some points; this piece actually seems to produce an authentic voice for Murphy that I find very believable. Other than that, the sense of pacing is also good, with the story unwinding at an even keel over the piece. Anything that I would consider a con is something that stacks up mostly in the details- for instance, there are points where the dialogue seems a little stiff because the language is so formal, and also, despite knowing that the story is continued, the ending just seems a little bit pushed, with a real 'tune in next time for ...' feeling that takes away a little bit from the realism. Overall, an enjoyable piece, though I look to the second for this round for an improvement. Aspiring for Approval IV- Nightmare I'm not quite sure that I get the same clear sense of character voice here that I did in the first piece. It's a bit of an odd piece, the way that the nightmare scenery is evoked sort of halfway, and the details left for the reader to imagine. At first I wasn't sure I liked it, but then I'll admit, the first scene of the piece had me hooked in, very interested. The choice scene has me a bit puzzled, but only in the sense that I'm not sure whether it's supposed to be shocking that Murphy chose wrestling, as it seemed fairly obvious to me from the point that there was a choice that that was the one he would make. I have an appreciation for the character's attitude, however, even if it isn't completely comprehensible to me why he would be so upset about choosing wrestling when in the last RP, he was talking about that being what he loved above all else. The last scene adds a nice bit of tension between the two ideals that I think will play out interestingly through the rest of the story. Vangelus Olsig Trial Series: The Great Scopes Scandal (3) Hm. I think it's a fairly clever device to orient someone who might not have any clue what's going on here through a journalist. It worked for me, at least, since I've never read any of your work with Olsig before, and might have otherwise been thinking 'wait, what the hell is going on here?' had I read it without this first scene. The language of the journalist seems a little dated, but that actually gives it an interesting little flair, I think. The rest of the story for this first piece is good, and definitely interesting, though there are places where I think the style fails it a little bit. There are some instances where the language just gets tangled up for me (this being an example of that: "She clinched her fist tightly, desperately trying to restrain herself from unleashing a path of rage upon the man in which she'd been led to believe she loved."); it sort of keeps me from getting a complete read on the scene, but what saves it is that the dialogue is good, and still shows me all the tension that scene needs to have some fire. The last scene I like, but I'm not entirely sure that the format of it is effective for me as a reader. It is an effective cliffhanger, at least, that makes me wonder what I can expect in your second. Trial Series- The Great Scopes Scandal (4) The split story works a little better in this one, I think, and I can't exactly put my finger on why, other than maybe because this RP starts off with that sort of construction, so there's ample time to get used to it before everything comes together. The story in this one is very good, and worked pretty tightly so that everything gets explained and I'm not left hanging about things in the end. That said, I'm a bit confused about just how simple it seems to tie all this up. Obviously, you're going in a different direction from here, but it's almost like I'm wanting to see loose ends from this, how it affects his life from here on out. I'm left hanging on a detail here too- what was the deal with the journalist in the first piece if he's not going to appear again? The way it was written, it really sounded like he was going to play a role later on, and I just feel like there's a knot here that hasn't been completely tied. As far as the end goes, I'm not too sure how I feel about the inclusion of the GTT after all that high personal drama- it's almost like it's a letdown to get that deep into a story, and then cap it off with something that's been a marginal concern most of the time, even if it is a lead-in to future story. These two stories are worlds apart, and it's *very* hard for me to compare them in any meaningful way. You've got two stories that focus on intensely personal issues, and that's about where the similarity ends. The difference, I suppose, comes in the actual content of the story. With Murphy, though as I've said I like what is written, and the voice it creates for the character, I feel like the story is fairly predictable. As far as Olsig goes, there's more depth for me, more to think about and hang on to- yet at the same time, I don't get as clear a look into his head, as tight a grip on his character. The strengths of these two pieces pretty much balance each other out. In the end, that basically leaves me with the choice of which piece engages me more as a reader. Coming down to that, I'll put down a vote for Olsig. Pick: Vangelus Olsig Judge 2 Murphy: Your first was definatly the better of the two. I like the content you've chosen and I really like that you make no bones about the fact that Murphy is trying to pin any number of his troubles everywhere but himself. I find that kind of honest character appraisal is very uncommon in most RP's today, and it's a refreshing change. That said I think your second RP was vague to the point of being almost pointless. Although I got that he was going through a bad trip, you failed to describe any of the horrible things he was seeing or even the simplest idea of what it might be to tempt your reader. Olsig: An interesting premise as a whole, although I admit I found the twists and turns a little confusing even though (on your advice) I read the two pieces prior to these. The character emotion here seemed a little flat in places, going from fairly rich development between Olsig and his bride-to-be to almost cliched between Elisa and Angie or Olsig and Essex. Having the story wrapped up so quickly also seemed a little jarring, giving the impression that you were rushed to finish or just didn't stretch it out as much as you might have otherwise. Final Call: I've actually struggled with this one for quite a while. Murphy had one excellent piece and one not so excellent piece, while Olsig had two solid pieces that just just weren't as enjoyable. In the end I'm going to give the nod to Murphy. His story captured me a little more, even with the lackluster second piece. Pick: I'm calling for Muphy d. Olsig Judge 3 Murphy: Liked the first one, quite a bit: classic wrestlers tale that very few do, so it was refreshing, albeit a bit cliche with the girlfriend not liking what they're doing and everything like that. The second one no so much. It didn't really add to the story and was kinda lost on me as to why it would have been done at all, unless it was something that as sewn up fast to get it posted at the earliest opportunity towards deadline. Still good, but it just didn't fit in all that well with the first one. Olsig: The story is okay for me. It's got a few twists and turns in it that make it enjoyable. Although against Murphy's it should have not held up as well in the tourney. However Murphy's second lost a lot of steam and Olsig kept his going. In the end, it's which one made me thing they wanted it more... And that would be Olsig. Pick: Vangelus Olsig RESULT Vangelus Olsig d Jack Murphy, 2 votes to 1 SUMMARY - Sammy Brown d High Flyer, 2 votes to 1 - Vangelus Olsig d Jack Murphy, 2 votes to 1 BRACKET JUDGES RJ Craig Pete Sam Kat James BRACKET FINAL Vangelus Olsig v Sammy Brown Eeyore
