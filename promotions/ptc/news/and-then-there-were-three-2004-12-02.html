GTT4 — News archive

and then there were three....

2 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dr. Curiosity has taken the remaining spot, alongside Exceptional Dave Hurst and Craig Goat, in the final round of GTT4 by defeating The Latch Key Kid of XUW. In a disappointing turn of events, Hurst and Goat won their matches by forfeit over Kasper Sky and Tarrant, respectively. All three competitors have claimed some impressive scalps during the course of GTT4: Craig Goat has defeated Jonathan Borealis, Jenna Ryder (handled by the very accomplished Rob K, of Tyler Nelson fame), Flyin' Bryan Calvin, current PTC Global Champion Seymour Almasy, Jimmy Bong (handled by Saij, former PTC Global Champion), with Tarrant's no-show being the only free ride. - Dave Hurst took out 4WW's Kyo Kushinaga, Sapphire, GTT1 finalist Jay Phoenix, Odium, GTT3 winner and former Universal champion Angelo Deville, with his only free ride being Kasper Sky's no-show. Dr. Curiosity is the current fWo Cruiserweight Champion, and has been a finalist in previous Universal Title tournaments. One of Andy's other characters, Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy, rode bareback everyone else in sight to victory at PTC Survival :) To reach the final of GTT4, he took out Grandad George English, Jade, Alex Beige ah sorry Gray, Sebastian Moraco (character created by the handler of Skyo Arsonist, who has challenged for the Universal Title and took out PTC's 2003 Trash Talk tournament. All three have the ability to take the prize and tell Buddy where to send the prize check to. Only one of them will. Will Andy miss out on sex for a year if he beats Craig Goat? Will Dave Hurst miss out on sex for a year if he beats Craig Goat and Dr. Curiosity? Stay tuned, GTT4 is where its at! And now... Final Round information! RP Time Starts: Now! RP Time Ends: 11.59pm Monday 13th December, Server time. RP Limits: 3 Minimum, 5 Maximum. Sandbagging: One RP on the last and second last days. No limits on other days.
