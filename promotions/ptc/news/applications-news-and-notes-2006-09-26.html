Applications news and notes

26 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At the moment, we are about 16 short of the number that I want to start with. This is taking into account people who haven't validated yet, however this will need to be done before your application can be considered, so if you haven't validated, you need to sort this out quickly, or miss out. That goes to all of you who used hotmail accounts and thought "She'll be right.". She won't be right, you need to use another mail account and reapply. If we can get the necessary number of people by Saturday night, we will be doing the live broadcast of "who's in". At the current rate, without doing much active advertising, word of mouth is doing the job and we are getting quite a few new faces in, and some faces from many years ago. Its looking really exciting :) We still have to figure out the finer points of how that is going to work so no specific info yet, but if it goes how we want it to, it'll be well worth tuning in and listening to. One nugget for you: This year, because for some reason I have a fetish for the number 6, there will be 6 brackets. Make of that what you will.
