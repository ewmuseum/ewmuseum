GTT - Day 4 Report

31 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former PTC Global Champions conspicuous by their absence As yet, no other former PTC Global champions have been confirmed and added to any of the four brackets. Enquiries regarding whether PTC was planning suprise Round 1 matchups involving such individuals as Hessian, Jesse Jamester, Chris Storm and even Donny Diamond failed to yield any new information as to whether any of these stellar performers have even applied for a spot this year's tournament. When specifically asked about Saij, tournament director Argyle responded with this: "Saij was a fine Global Champion, however a final ruling is yet to be made on whether he is allowed to compete in future PTC events, and this is not expected to happen until after the Survival event airs. I will say though at this point in time that it will be an extremely unlikely set of circumstances to see his name up on the list of confirmations for GTT4. The good news is that the same however cannot be said about Angelo Deville, former Universal Champion". When questioned further regarding whether Deville had entered the tournament, the tournament director remained tight-lipped. Long time PTC members will recall that the Universal Championship was once considered PTC's highest honor, when it was shared between PTC and the WO group of federations. Applications still pouring in Although there have been well in excess of 128 applications, aspiring competitiors still continue to fill out the form and try their luck. It is still undecided whether the tournament will be expanded to 256 players at this point in time. Confirmed Entrants fatbadger (XUW), "Detective" Kraig Carter (FA), Nick Persia (UWF), Cyrus Skar (LOW) and Lori Equinox (WWA) join the Himalayas Bracket. Munson (Core), Kayden Paulton (FA), Patti Anderson (4WW), Officer Stone (FA) and Scottywood (NGW) join the Rockies Bracket. Robb Hardy (4WW), Token Weed (FA), Blaise "Hazard" McClancy (4WW) and Dallas Reeves (NGW) join the Pyrenees Bracket. Jenna Ryder (FA), Gonzalo Rey (FA), Darek DeAngelo (4WW), 'Flyin' Brian Calvin (WWA), Sean McClancy (UWF), Dusk (FA), John Hitchin (NGW), Olf Magnus Svensson (FA), Jimmy Bong (FA) and "The Renegade" Rich Rollins (UWF) join Seymour Almasy and others in the Appalachians Bracket. Rumored Bracket Shuffling? Once again our loose-lipped tournament director let slip in casual conversation today that at some point during the tournament there would be a spot of "inter-bracket shuffling". What does this mean?
