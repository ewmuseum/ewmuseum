Some news! (New Fed, Site Policy Change, SS/GTT5 News)

28 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. New Member Fed! WeWA returns to PTC after their last stint here three or so years ago. Peeps who've been around since then would remember WeWA's fed head, Mr [name withheld], doing a stint being co-president of PTC along with Steven Caldera and [name withheld]. He produced some memorable PTC I-PPV events and was famous for his appearances on PTC Radio back in the day when it was a regular feature on Fancast. Creative differences with PTC's then-owner Ron saw him move on to a similar role in Fedwars. Fun days :D Please make Mr [name withheld] and any of the WeWA guys who choose to venture over here for a chat feel welcome. 2. Site Policy Change PTC's main website has long been limited to use by PTC member feds. Given that its activity of late would make a three-toad sloth look like a 100 metre olympic gold medal winner, I've thrown open the gates to allow affiliate feds news posting privs as well. So, if your fed currently has a link exchange deal with PTC, feel free to hit me up for a news posting account that you can use to promote your fed's warez. This was to have been a new feature in the new PTC site, but Christmas is coming quicker than it given the amount of overtime I've been getting from work this year. 3. SS / GTT5 news! I have as of five minutes ago, received the last outstanding match for SS. So, all going well, we will finally see results up this weekend! As usual, I will take the knowledge I learn from this event into the next one, and attempt to improve things. Some things that caused the delay couldn't be helped, life is like that sometimes. Thanks to everyone for your patience. GTT5 applications will open a couple of days after the SS results go up. Given that it is starting a month later than I originally wanted, it will probably span over Christmas. If that happens there will be a break for the Christmas / New Year week, or a ridiculously long RP period for that round. Posted on the PTC forum by Argyle.
