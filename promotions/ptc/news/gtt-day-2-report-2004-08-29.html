GTT - Day 2 Report.

29 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PTC HQ Vandalized! Police were called for the second time in 24 hours when the outside of PTC Headquarters was vandalised with anti-Seymour Almasy propaganda. GTT4 Tournament Director, Argyle, had this to say: "Someone really has a hardon for taking on AND taking out Seymour in this tournament. It's really quite disturbing. I mean, look at all these posters picturing him in the line of fire. Rest assured, this situation will be dealt with. I've already called the cleaning company to take care of this mess, and Seymour will be able to compete without the addded pressure of worrying about what some severely sociopathic freak might do while his back is turned!" 100 App mark passed! The number of applications for GTT4 is now over 100. WWA, 4WW and UWF are now level pegging with the largest number of representatives from PTC federations. Non-member feds having prospective representatives now include NEW, TSC, NAFW, CAL (BWwA), NWC, LoC, OCW, DWS and XiPW, although we can't find evidence of XiPW actually existing. The number of unrepresented free agents submitting applications continues to skyrocket. 8 Additional confirmed entrants! PTC Management would like to confirm that the following individuals have been confirmed to compete in GTT4: - Digital Mortality (UWF), Kris Kanton (4WW) and Jerms (4WW) will be appearing in the Himalayas bracket. - Kenny Mercury (UWF), Noah Hanson (WMW) and Unstoppable Eugene (FA - formerly of PCW) will be appearing in the Pyrenees Bracket. - Caitlin Fairchild (Divas Unleashed) and Annihilation have joined Muppet Man (WWA) in the Rockies bracket.
