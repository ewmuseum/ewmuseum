Q. What if I/my opponent don't/doesn't post hype?

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nick Persia: My opponent (Scottywood) is banned from PTC. Do I still need to post everything (hype, rp's etc)? Or is he still allowed to compete? No he isn't. Yes he is. I warned him in a PM when he applied that if the Hate using stolen posers bizzo spilled out onto the forums at any time during the tournament that I'd boot him over the fence, so yes, he is out. Slight amendment, it didn't spill out onto the forums, so technically he didn't break the requirement there, however having seen the PM from him regarding the stolen posers, his ban stays, so he's still out. I did specificallly mention to him that I was iffy on his application because he was currently in a dispute with one of the administrators here. All he had to do was have a quick look through is site and delete the images - he's known about this for a long time. But instead he left them and did nothing, and there were several PM's sent to his host, hosting company and server company to try and get it done because he didn't do it... so him getting pissy over a sig is hypocritical at this point in time, under the circumstances. Anyway, if you want to know anything else, feel free to ask me via PM. GTT5 isn't about Hate wrestling or arguments between PTC staff and Hate staff.
