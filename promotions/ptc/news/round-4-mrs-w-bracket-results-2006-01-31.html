Round 4: Mrs W. Bracket Results

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EMBER V KIMBUSA V SEYMOUR ALMASY Judge 1 Alrighty. I'd like to say first that it's a shame Gwynn had to resign from the tournament. He had some good stuff going in previous rounds and would have done well here. However, the other three contestants put forth good efforts and made this a tough bout to call. So, here are some thoughts on each player's work: Ember Opening your RPs with a story and a nursery rhyme respectively served as a useful device, particularly since they contained concepts that later showed up as the RPs went on. You tell a good story relating to Ember's past, and the writing is solid. Probably my main issue with your work is believability. The idea of Ember being a spirit (in lieu of a better term) "wearing" another person's body was a hard buy for me, to be honest. When it comes to e-wrestling, I'm admittedly not a big fan of characters that come down from heaven/emerge from hell/posess a human body/etc. and end up competing in a wrestling tournament. It just doesn't seem terribly real to me. Don't get me wrong, I don't mind stretching reality, but this is really kinda reaching. If this were written as a seperate piece outside of the e-wrestling genre, then I wouldn't be nearly so anal about that sort of thing. Also, in your second RP, I felt like Carole believed Ember a bit too easily about Raven (in spite of the fact that she did hear her). That's a smaller example. But hey, it was good work, and you're obviously talented, no question. A lot of this was just a tough sell for me. Seymour Almasy Using last year's loss to Craig Goat as your story for this round was quite a brilliant move, and probably somewhat risky I might add. Someone unfamiliar with the characters of Goat and Nurse, or without knowledge of GTT4, might not have found your work this round as meaningful. Lucky for you, I'm not one of those people. Way to be ballsy! The story was simple enough, and as with Ember it was written well. In a way it almost seemed too simple. By that I mean I had this sense that the story was slightly lacking in substance. Seymour needs to settle his past, Seymour goes to see Goat, Seymour apologizes, everything is fine, throw in a recount of the GTT4 match in there somewhere... that's sort of what it felt like to me. I can't really put my finger on it. At any rate, if I had to take issue with one thing, that would be it. And it wasn't really that major. You wrote the characters of Goat and Nurse well, and did them justice. The writing flowed nicely, and it was an easy read. Plus the Final Fantasy references were entertaining as always. I even knew what the Latin from "One-Winged Angel" meant without you giving the translation. Yes, I am a nerd. Well done, sir. Kimbusa Like Seymour and Ember, this work was written very well. Great story. There was plenty of character development, and more of Kimbusa's past is revealed. The bits where Kimbusa was poking around in the casino (or at least hoping to poke around, if you get my drift) seemed to drag a bit, but didn't detract too much from my enjoyment. Shifting the focus to Steven Caldera in the second RP was an interesting move, and again a potentially risky one since Kimbusa was not physically present... but you pulled it off nicely. I did have some more "believability" issues in these RPs, as I did with Ember's work. I understand that Kimbusa's reputation preceded him and struck fear into the hearts of the bodyguards, but c'mon... Zaccanelli is running the Tokyo underground and he doesn't even have his own gun? He wasn't afraid of Kimbusa, so by all accounts he should've capped his sumo ass himself. Also, I found Caldera actually traveling to Tokyo because of Kimbusa's story to be a bit of a stretch as well. Ultimately I buy it, because it's a feasible occurence. It just seemed a bit extreme to me. On the other hand, maybe Kimbusa is just that damn good of a storyteller. Hey, next time have Kimbusa go tell Bill Gates a story that'll make him send me a billion dollars. That'd be awesome! Anyway, that's just kinda nitpicky, really. And I'm being nitpicky because your RPs were great. I enjoyed them thoroughly. Decision Well, after several days of trepidation, I'm going to put all minds at ease and announce that my vote for... fourth place goes to "Avatar" William Gwynn! What a shocker! Hehe, alright, seriously now. My third place vote is for Ember. He ran into some very tough competition with veterans like Seymour and Kimbusa in this round, and unfortunately he didn't do enough to take either one of them down. Mostly it was the whole "believability" factor that I described earlier. I just wasn't buying Ember's character. That's what swung it for me. Second and first places were much harder to determine. On the one hand, I found Seymour's RPs to be more entertaining and easier to read. On the other hand, I thought Kimbusa's story was more fleshed out, and had some great development. It was a close call, but my second place vote goes to... ... ... ...Seymour Almasy. Which means Kimbusa takes first place honors for this match. It was close, but ultimately Kimbusa's strong story compelled me to put him ahead of Seymour. If the other judges vote likewise and put these two through to the next round, I'd advise Sean to step up his game a bit more. I'm sure he's planning to anyway though. I'm looking forward to a bracket final of Kimbusa vs. Almasy if that's what it does indeed come to. Summary 1st: Kimbusa 2nd: Seymour Almasy 3rd: Ember Judge 2 Kimbusa This is the first time I've read any of Ben's work. I just never got around to it before. I can now see why he's done so well for himself in these things so consistently, and why he keeps coming back for more despite being otherwise retired from handling. I liked the first piece more than the second. There's something about the Caldera character, both when you write for him at least in this instance, and even when Rollins incorporates him (which has no bearing here) that screams "support character". After reading the first RP and wanting to see how Kimbusa handled suddenly being in charge of a whole lot of things, it was a bit of a let down to then be served with Caldera's hunt for him and his inner self. Technically nothing wrong with it, and I didn't dislike it, I was just expecting something else. Almasy Props to you Sean, a great choice of subject material for this round. Risky for a number of reasons, but you need to dig deep and do exactly that to get anywhere at this stage of the game. Execution not bad at all. The issue I have with it is that I expected more when Goat and Almasy met eye to eye. The slap from Nurse was dramatic.. but to polish this a bead of sweat or some other indication of hesitation or pause on Almasy's part before extending his hand would have built it up a bit more. The leadup to the meeting was fine, I didn't know what to expect, especially with the sword, but that's why the pregnant pause and clear indication of Almasy's decision at that point in time - because up to now it had been ambiguous - was important. Ember Also the first time I've read Ember's work this year. A nice lot of depth in there, which is how I like it, however, you make me work too hard to read relatively unimportant things by burying them in description. Challenging me to think is great, I like that, but if half way through I take 20% and say mouth wateringly great, and throw 30% away as fluff, then you're not doing it right - at least for me. Tough, tough choice. 1. Kimbusa 2. Ember 3. Almasy 1 was a lock. Kimbusa's first piece drew me in above all others. 2 and 3 was a hard choice. Very hard. After much changing of minds, I'm marking Almasy down to third because of what I've already said about wanting more at the moment of meeting and when Almasy made his choice not to do harm to Goat but instead to make peace with him. As I said, something as simple as a bead of sweat trickling down his forehead before making the move to extend his hand would have done the trick, and made all the difference. Fantastic work by all three players. Judge 3 Firstly, I want to say that going into the judging I knew that I was the tie-break judge, and that my points wouldn't change the number one slot, but that it was a tie between 2nd and 3rd. Kimbusa, I'm not going to give detailed comments, and my opinion doesn't really matter in your case, but I do want to say that while I've not read much of your work so far, but I'm really glad I got to read this one! Good work very solid RPs. Almasy, each time I've had the pleasure of reading your work I comment on just how easy it is to read. It always has a great hook and always draws the reader in. I really enjoyed the story in these two peices, I liked the lead into the confontation with Craig Goat, and the way the music was woven throughout (was it/is it your entrance music? if it is, it's a really nice touch). My only niggle was that after the building of the tension up to the point of the meeting, I felt that there was almost a cop-out after the meeting itself. Part of me wants to believe it was intentional, but part of me doesn't. Ember, phew! Good writing (as always) but there are times that the storyline got a bit complicated. I judged your work last round and I felt that it was lucky that I did because otherwise I could easily have become lost. There are places that I thought that your work was overly discriptive, which added another level of complication. Overall, I find your writing to be well paced and absorbing. Since my judging for Kimbusa doesn't matter (he wins no matter what I decide) I'm down to picking between Almasy and Ember. Almasy, I always feel as if writing is so easy for you, to read your work is almost no effort at all. I'm sure it's skill more than anything else, but it makes your work deceptive. On the other hand, I find Embers writing complicated, it has teeth and grit and it feels like you put alot of work into it. It really comes down to story line. Truth is, I liked Almasy's better. 1. Kimbusa 2. Almasy 3. Ember RESULT Lowest score -> Highest value Kimbusa - 3 points *Match Winner Almasy - 7 points Ember - 8 points Kimbusa and Seymour Almasy advance to the Mrs W. bracket final.
