aka Round 6. Matchups: Shanty Jones v Chandler Tsonda Seymour Almasy v Rich "The Renegade" Rollins 2 RP limit, 2 RP minimum (in other words, do two, I want a level playing field folks!) One bounty is going down... which will it be? In fact, only one bounty is a lock for the finals! Andy is one match away from three tournament finals in a row. Sean is majorly out for pride and redemption, but does he have anything left after last round? Will our Global Champion confirm that he's the man, or does a two time former Global Champion have what it takes to put him in his place? Will Will complete a fairytale run and take a spot in the final that we all thought would go to Jeff? Stay tuned...
