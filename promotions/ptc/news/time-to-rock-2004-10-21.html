Time to Rock.

21 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings. Most of you know me as the jerk that's pretty much had Survival held up for the past two months. I do sincerely apologize for that. Time during Survival was at a premium, but a few unexpected things have lead to the demise of the event. I'm currently holding in my EMail box 6 of the 7 events, and the 7th is well underway, and looking to be fantastic. I'm hoping that it will be a main event worth waiting for, and I hope that the members that did make it to the final will appreciate the time that went into it. Argyle and I are extremely sorry that we've let you down during the time of this event. We both knew that our time was going to be at a premium leading up to the time after Survival, and yet, we went through with it anyways, knowing full well that things could get ugly. And by all accounts they have. I'd like to thank each person that participated in Survival. You all did a heck of a job, and worked your tails off for your home fed. That will not go unnoticed. I'd like to thank Jack, Jay Phoenix, Jaime, Josh and RJ for busting their nuts to write 6 great matches. These men are a true credit to PTC, as well as their individual feds. If I were to start (another), I'd want each and everyone of them involved in some capacity. A special thanks to Jay Phoenix for writing a match that was originally left for dead by it's original match writer, and doing a bang up job of it. Thank you, Jay. It's appreciated more than you know. And so, the event itself will begin to be posted each day, starting at the VERY LATEST next EDIT TO CLARIFY November 1, 2004 Monday Afternoon. Each day at 5:30PM EST, another match will be added on the main site, and each will be linked to the forums in the General Discussion forum, until the Main Event is posted on Saturday. THe first two matches will be the ETD and the GTD so that RJ and Jack can continue on however they need to do. Both will be posted on Monday. Then, Tuesday through Friday, the qualifying matches will be posted, and on Friday the final teams will be announced. Saturday, the final match will be posted, and the winner of the First, and more than likely last, PTC Survival will be crowned. As a thank you to all of you that participated, Argyle and I would like to give each and every person that RPed a custom member title. If we had money between the two of us, we'd love to give a thank you gift, but, since we're not as well off as we'd like to be, we're going to have to do this instead. If you RPed, please reply below, and give your custom member title you'd like. All I ask is that it's family oriented, or at least, moderately clean. None of the 7 dirty words if you will. It can be about your character, about you, about a view of life, whatever. Again, I apologize for the lateness of the event. It was never my intent to hold things up like this. -Pete Posted on the PTC forum by Pete.
