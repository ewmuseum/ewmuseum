GTT - Day 6 Report

2 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Possible final Resolution to incomplete GTT2 tournament? Today's GTT4 press release from tournament director Argyle included something completely unexpected. "It is well known and has long been a thorn in the side of Primetime Central that for well documented reasons, our GTT2 tournament, held under previous management reigemes, never finished. White Mexican, Hessian, Seth Masters and Akiyama were all cheated out of a chance to go down in history. There's not a lot I can do to rectify that now, however I would like to formally apologise to all four semi finalists on behalf of everyone at Primetime Central who was responsible for letting them down, myself included." "Additionally, as a token gesture of respect, if ALL FOUR were to show interest in competing in GTT4, I am prepared to offer each of them a bye into the second round, as a professional courtesy." When it was pointed out that two of the four gentlemen in question had since retired from the sport and had been out of the public eye for quite some time, Argyle was quick to change the subject and declined to answer any further questions on the matter. Almasy responds to threats! When questioned during a GTT4 promotional radio interview about the rampant bribery attempts and intimidation tactics used by many competitors to secure a first round spot against him, the PTC Global Champion stated simply, "Lot of people eager to lose, ya?" Almasy is scheduled for similar radio interviews all week. PTC 2003 Wrestler of the Year not entering GTT4? Attempts to contact PTC 2003 Wrestler of the Year, Chryste (Chrystal Fellini), for an interview have thusfar been unsuccessful. At this point in time it is feared that if Fellini does not make her presence felt soon, she will likely miss the cut, with only 20 available spaces left in the tournament. Confirmed Entrants Lex le Magnifique (FA), Snake Eyes (FA), Rzarector (Core), Arch Angel (FA), Blaze Medina (XUW) and Train (GW) join the already talent-laden Kaikouras Bracket. Venneman Pendragon (GW) and Tsunami Oni (UWF) join the breakout Flinders Bracket.
