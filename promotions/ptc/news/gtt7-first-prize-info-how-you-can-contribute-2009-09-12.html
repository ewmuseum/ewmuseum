GTT7 First Prize info - how you can contribute!

12 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This year we are again doing the charity donation prize - i.e. the winner gets to nominate a charity that a sum of money will be sent to. Previous iterations of that system had a few flaws - the major one being that a handful of people's intended generosity was bigger than their wallets when it came time to make good on their promise. So... this time around, it'd be sweet for a larger group of people to kick in just a few bucks each, thus making it a group donation, with real money already collected. Appropriate proof of the transaction will of course be posted. So.. for the next few weeks, you will see the donation bar at the top of the forum home page. If you've got some spare change in your bank account, please consider putting it towards a good cause. It might be cancer, animal welfare or something similar, whatever takes the fancy of the eventual winner. If/when we get to $100, I'll reset it and collect some for the runner up to make a similar choice with, and so on. You can donate anonymously, or if you choose to reveal your identity during the transaction, your name will be listed permanently on the financial contributors page. Please consider :) *edit 1* 16 September: $100 has been raised for the winner to donate to the charity of their choice. We will now aim for an additional $50 for the runner up to do the same. Thanks everyone!
