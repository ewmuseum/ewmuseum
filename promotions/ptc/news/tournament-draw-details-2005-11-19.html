Round 1 placements will be drawn in about 13 hours from now... 12.00pm Eastern Daylight Savings Time (Australia). If that is meaningless to you... http://timeanddate.com/worldclock/custom.html?continent= look up Melbourne, and compare it to where you are. The draw will either be on Webcam http://gtt5cam.primetimecentral.net or PTC Radio. http://radio.primetimecentral.net Webcam will be attempted but will likely dumped if the lag makes it a worthless exercise. With webcam, I won't be doing audio. The webcam interface has its own built in chat proggy. I've emailed the last two unvalidated applications... should they fail to achieve this by draw time, they will not be able to compete, under the Tex Chatanooga Rule. If the draw is on PTC Radio, I "might" take calls during the show - depending on if I have time to set up the second PC needed to do the calls between now and then. If I don't, you'll have to be content with my dulcet tones, and any MP3 recordings that people want to send in for me to play during the show. Brackets
