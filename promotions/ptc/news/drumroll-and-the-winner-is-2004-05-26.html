Drumroll..... and the winner is....

26 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Judge 1: Oooh, only one rp apiece. They put it down to the wire. K-Wolf Otherworld, RP 1 vs Almasy Seymour Almasy Final Fantasy I, RP for Finals (good luck to Mat) Or more aptly End Game. Okay, I'm going to have to grade these together. As these are two of the best written for the show. What's good: Both are amazing rp's. Where K-Wolf experimented with a multiscene entry that tried to loose the reader, then reel them back in, then loose them again... like an angler fighting that big fish.... it was amazing. Seymour went for the final rp that he may very well be writing for this character, and cutting all the loose ends short, and taking ti all down to... one more match... then freedome. The bad: Nothing really bad here, but while K-Wolfs was very, very good.... writing wise, they are dead even as far as I'm concerned, we run into the fact that Seymour concentrated on the match, the predicatment and the ending of the storyline that he's running right now. All was material that would make it just a little bit better then the montage of viginettes that were strung together nicely for K-Wolf's. Unfortunatly a full on story told, to a series of viginettes and scenes strung together, the full story would have to be on top. Final Decision a la me: While either one of these people deserve to win, based on the rp's posted, Seymour Almasy will not be putting over the new guard, but will be retiring with his head held high. But a note: it's a just barely on that. K-Wolf experimented from what I saw compared to the other rounds, and a bit less experimentation and a bit more of going with what you know works would have turned that tide the other way, easily. Judge 2: Cruiserweight Final Judging. Seymour Almasy: Final Fantasy I What we are given here is a long series of dialogue, focusing on the future of Seymour and Laura. It focuses well on the match and the worries and emotion of the characters, proving to be a far different role-play to the one that I envisaged when reading the semi final rp’s. The way that it addressed Seymour’s doubts was great, he still maintained some characteristics of the old Seymour, references to the FF games again and Boco his ostrich great stuff. However it felt a bit too drawn out, there was no action so to speak apart from Almasy attempting to close his suitcase, as a whole it was just one huge piece of dialogue which really did not do it for me. It felt like so much more could have been done, as opposed to what was said. In essence there was just something missing from the roleplay, this is supposed to be his final battle before retirement, yet I really didn’t see any emotion. I know that at the end he said that he was going out with a whimper, the way the new Seymour wanted to, that’s fair enough, but I still wasn’t allowed to engage with the character due to a lack of emotion showing. Karina Wolfenden: Otherworld. There is only one word to describe this. Immense. From the second I began reading I was totally engrossed in the story, literally unable to pull myself away from the writing. The image that was painted with every action and description allowed myself to actually think I was there. Everything in the piece had a purpose. This is no lie but I have read books that were less adequate than this role-play was. The ending was fantastic and actually gave me the shudder up the spine when you finally realise it all coming together. I’m going to stop here. Winner: Karina Wolfenden. Seymour is a fantastic writer, but Karina pulled something special out here a truly fantastic piece. the type of which I have never seen before. It truly was unparalleled. Judge 3: Karina Wolfenden - "Otherworld" This was a good RP. It was very different. I liked the uniqueness of the format, it was complicated and hard to follow at times, but overall I thought it was an interesting piece. There were times when I simply had to scratch my head as I was a bit confused as to what was going on, but after re-reading it a few times, it all clicked. I liked the change in points of view back to Karina's family, it's the basis of who she is and Mat did that well. The writing, as expected, was superb, and everything flowed pretty well even in chapter form. The only thing I was a little disappointed with was a lack of relevance for the match itself. I'm not talking about trash talking or anything like that, but apart from the last few sentences, the match was hardly referred to at all. The significance of the match was mentioned in passing, but just not enough for my liking. Seymour Almasy - "Final Fantasy I" Very enjoyable RP. It was very different to Mat's in the format and presentation, yet just as effective. I loved Seymour's storyline. The veteran warrior going into battle for the final time. The way in which he spoke about the importance of the match was great, it made us realize that not only does he want to win the match, but he NEEDS to win the match to go out on top like a hero. I liked the continued theme of how he had just discovered his true identity and how it's going to effect him in his life, I thought that was dealt with well and realistically. I liked how he addressed that this is not just the end, but merely another beginning for himself. I couldn't really find a fault with this RP at all. The Call After much thought, I have to give this one to Seymour Almasy. I just thought that Seymour conveyed that he, the character, wanted to win more. I'm not just voting for him because this is his last match and therefore would obviously have the more emotional storyline, but the way in which Seymour conveyed his emotions regarding the match was just more superior to K-Wolf's, I thought. For example, Seymour used comparisons between himself and Wolfenden and how they were similar, which was a nice touch and it just reinforced that Seymour was completely focused on the match, where as I just didn't get that same feeling with Wolfenden. Both stories were wrapped up well and it was a tough call, but for mine Seymour Almasy wins the tournament. Congratulations to both men for getting this far. Winner of the First Annual PTC Cruiserweight Tournament (2004) is... Seymour Almasy, 2 votes to 1. Congratulations to Sean for taking this sucker home and to Mat for doing the right thing each and every round, and thank you goes to all people who put any sort of effort in to this, be it judges or players. While the final few rounds may have been scant in hype and therefore interest from many people despite it containing some stacked matches, this is the first tournament, or pretty much any event in living memory for me in PTC where there has been great sportsmanship from start to finish, and naff all in the way of party poopers spoiling it for everyone else. I know some of the judges comments didn't go down too well with some people in earlier rounds, however part of the game is getting any kind of feedback like that and being able to choose to take it with a grain of salt if you don't agree with it, and I'm pleased to see that people were able to do that with good grace. This leaves me very willing to invest a lot of time and effort into PTC's big annual tourney later on this year, and I'll go into it with a good reminder of what works and what doesn't in things like this.
