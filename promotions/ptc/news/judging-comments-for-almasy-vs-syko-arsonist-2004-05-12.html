Judging comments for Almasy vs Syko Arsonist

12 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some mighty fine analysis provided by all three judges. Thanks to you all for your time and effort! Judge 1 Seymour Almasy rp#1 Silence before the storm. Nice role-play, liked the way in which it showed a number of different emotions and points of view, such as Aerith’s decision to tell Seymour. Especially liked the part where Seymour appeared to be normal then went off on the whole barrier materia route. I wasn’t too keen on the parts where Almasy was talking to himself after leaving the bath, it just seemed to be there to pad things out although it was saved somewhat by the brilliant use of juxtaposition right at the end showing Almasy once more focusing on his match whereas Aerith was just pushing herself to do what she had to do. The role-play left me wanting to know what happens next and was a great time to end the role-play. I loved the comparison between Syko and Mt Gazaget also as I side note. Syko Arsonist rp#1 The greatest Weapon Great opening first paragraph I love the way it just went straight in there bamn, giving a nice insight into the mind of Syko whilst at the same time showing a segment of his history too. Nice. What is hindering me with this rp at the moment is that there are quite a number of grammatical and spelling mistakes, really throwing me off. The jumping of time throughout the piece whilst it did allow an insight into the past and allowed you to progress through the role-play didn’t seem to be executed that well. The story as a whole began really well, I enjoyed the story of the casino and the intensity was great you could tell the panic that must have been going through Karla’s mind. Yet then it seemed to just digress. I think the problem here was trying to do too much in one role-play with it seemingly just being pieced together. Seymour Almasy #2 00:00 Another great opening seems to be a common thing with these rp’s, almost expected some sort of crazy theme music after the announcement of zero hour. Jack Daniels as elixir, nothing short of inspired it really is. I was sort of expecting more here, I don’t feel that the reaction that his world was not real was real enough. There was definitely something lacking he just seemed a bit frightened. Where as I would have expected some sort of huge reaction, his world had basically just been torn apart. I’m sorry but I found the part about Aerith’s mum being a wedding planner far too cheesy and as though it was just thrown in to give a foundation for your final rp’s should you progress that far. The resolution at the end was good though, I liked the idea that a sort of compromise had been made that he could still live the fantasy in the ring, there was something really good about that yet at the same time the reaction seemed a little too perfect, not sure if this is building up to something later on but we’ll see. I guess. Syko #2 The search of a lifetime Picks up exactly where the last one finished, such as in Seymour’s 2nd. This is showing a lot of raw human emotion, with a reaction that would be deemed a lot more realistic refusing to believe the truth. Once again though there are some spelling mistakes within the piece and the part with the inner thoughts just seemed a bit too rambled for my liking. Something was lacking towards the end of the rp the talking between Syko and Jacob did very little to keep me entertained in the role-play, finding myself being forced to read it as opposed to wanting to read it. Final Decision: A tough match to call, both competitors are headed towards their respective retirements and it shows with the determination within both of them. I actually feel quite bad that one of them has to actually lose as I would like to see the endings to both of the stories and hope that the loser does actually bother to write the finale of the piece. In the end though I give the win to Seymour Almasy by a whisker, the grammatical errors in Syko’s rp’s and the fact that something felt amiss in the final rp are the main factors in my influence. Winner: Seymour Almasy. Judge 2 Seymour Almasy RP #1 - "Silence Before The Storm" Pros: - Anticipation. Seymour built up the surprise very well, by mentioning it at the start of the RP, forgetting about it during the middle and then using it as bait to read the 2nd RP. The suspense was very well built up. - Writing style. Good mix between dialogue, thoughts and description. Everything flowed really well, and there weren't any major errors. - The importance of the match. Seymour has built this tournament up from day one about how not only does he want to win, but it's pretty much the bane of his existance at the moment, because it's how he needs to go out on top. Cons: - The surprise. Even though we don't know what it is yet, no real reason was given as to why Aerith was about to reveal his secret. But I'm grading this as I read it, so if it's addressed in the 2nd RP then that's great. - Love story. I realize that with the characters involved it had to be about Seymour and Aerith's relationship, but for my liking it was focused on just a bit too much in this RP. Overall: Really good RP to start off. Well written and it addressed the significance of the match at hand, while it still made you want to read the continuation to see what the outcome is. Syko Arsonist RP #1 - "Destiny 1: The Greatest Weapon" Pros: - Reference to the match. Syko made some really good points about the match, including Almasy's previous successes. I also really liked the bit where he said that Almasy had already won because he had the love of Aerith Heartily, and how that was the "Greatest Weapon". - It was entertaining. There were some holes in the story as I'll mention below, but overall there was a lot going on from the intro to the bit with Karla to the flashback and eventually to the end where it was revealed who his mother was. It kept the reader interested. - Fluency. It was mentioned last week that Syko always switched from scene to scene and normally that would be quite hard to follow, but somehow even though there are about five different scenes and settings in this RP, it still flows well, and Syko is able to convey all the different time settings effectively. Cons: - There were quite a few errors concerning grammar, but none really big enough to take away from the enjoyment or easyness to read. - Storyline. While it was entertaining, there were a few a few bad bits in the storyline just like how his adoption was brought up and addressed matter-of-factly like it was a done deal. Plus I just kinda have a thing against guns in RP's, but that's only a personal thing and it wasn't really used so I can't complain too much. Overall: Overall it was very good. It was both hit and miss at times, but there was a lot of intriguing bits like his thoughts on Seymour, and while the finding his biological mother storyline is pretty stock standard, I'm still looking forward to reading the conclusion. Seymour Almasy RP #2 - "00:00" Pros: - How it was revealed that he wasn't really Seymour Almasy. Some clever parts in this RP dealing with the Elixer, the Firaga, etc. It had some very funny moments and not intentionally on Seymour (the character's) behalf. Even though I may not agree with parts of the storyline as I'll get into below, his character development was excellent. I think he covered every little point in relation to how his identity had been changed and how it affected him, such as his name, Aerith's name, his healing powers, etc. - How Seymour's change will reflect on the match. I like how he drawed comparisons saying that he's still a warrior and that the ring is still his arena of battle. Like I said about his first RP, the importance of the match was still emphasised in this one as well. - Writing style. Seymour has an excellent style and it conveyed his emotions pretty well. Everything flowed and he kept it interesting. Cons: - The surprise. Don't get me wrong, it's a great angle to do in a tournament, especially nearing such a critical stage. But I have to question the way in which it was done, as I mentioned earlier. The announcement just came out of nowhere. I realise that Aerith had been contemplating such a move for a while now, but why? Why was this the final straw? Why the revelation now? To that extent we were left out in the dark, which was a bit disappointing. - The way in which Seymour just took the news straight away like duck to water and seemingly accepted it. I know he didn't believe it at first, but you'd think that he'd put up more of a fight and just not believe it at all, considering almost his entire life up until this point had been a lie. It's almost as if he just shrugged and said "Oh well". Overall: - Excellent RP. There were a few minor storyline holes that I mentioned above, but all in all, it was a great read. The way in which he conveyed his story was great, and the relation to the match was done very cleverly. I'd say at this stage that it was better than his first, and I think it tied up the storyline quite well. Syko Arsonist RP #2 - Destiny 2: The Search of a Lifetime Pros: - The handling of the revelation that he met his biological mother. The way that he rejected her initial announcement was quite realistic, as he denied that it was even a possibility until the very end of the RP. The way he came around to wanting a DNA test was done very cleverly, as it was built up to a crescendo at the end. - The way in which he related the storyline to the match. I'm a sucker for relevance, and Syko was able to tie in his storyline and the match superbly, because there were so many similarities. Cons: - Besides from a few minor errors, I don't think I could pick one fault from this RP. Overall: This was an awesome RP, much better than his first, and it probably just shaded Almasy's second for RP of the match. It's almost a shame that I can only think of two pros for this RP, but those are the two main areas that just stood out in my mind, and everything that he did well in his first RP, he did well in his second RP as well. The Call I have re-read these RP's numerous times, and I've been stewing about this decision on and off all day. It's by far the closest match I've ever had to judge, and I'll just say that it's been a pleasure to judge a match of this standard. So, the analysis. I actually feel bad about giving so many cons for this match, because I think I've actually been too overly critical, trying to find faults with both writers in order to find a winner. I probably haven't complimented both men enough, as their high standard of writing is pretty much a given. Technically, I think Seymour is a better writer. His story flows a bit better and he is able to paint a more effective picture for his stories. On the story side of things, both men's stories were very interesting. I'm guessing that this is probably the most powerful series of RP's that Seymour has ever done. Making the decision to reveal to Almasy that he's not who he thinks he is not a decision to take lightly. It was done in an entertaining manner, but it has to be said that I was a little disappointed at how it played out. Like I said earlier, Seymour took the news with shock at first, but after a few minutes he seemed like he was back to normal even though he had just been told that his entire life had been a lie. On the other hand... at first I was worried that Syko's story would be quite repetitive as a "finding your long lost mother" series has been done before... but the way he played out his emotions was done superbly, and was more realistic than Seymour's reaction. He didn't believe the truth about his mother in the entire RP, yet right at the end there was an ounce of doubt in his mind, so you could really see the change of heart develop. As for the match itself, this is where it gets tricky. Sometimes when I'm judging a match if I'm at a deadlock, I look at which character has stated that they need the win. In this case, that doesn't help. Both men have made it quite clear in their RP's that this is their last hurrah, and both men want to go out on top. They've each shown a great amount of desperation for wanting to win the match, which makes it extremely hard as I really am going to hate picking against one or the other, because either way, the character ends for one of them. Decisions, decisions, decisions... Okay... after a great deliberation, I'll have to say that my pick is... Syko Arsonist. I just think that his story was played out a bit better than Seymour's, and it also left a little question at the end of the RP as to what would happen in the future, which makes the reader a little more intrigued to keep on reading. So on that note, I have to go with Syko. Seymour's were great RP's though, and whoever wins in the end would be fully deserving of the victory. Congratulations to both men for getting this far. Judge 3 Almasy v Arsonist Almasy #1 Silence before the Storm is an appropriate title for this RP, because it focuses on Aerith’s, or should I say Laura’s inability to tell Seymour what she knows she must eventually tell him. The characterisation in this is just perfect, and makes the RP exceedingly readable. Whilst nothing dramatic happens, and essentially the same jokes are brought up here that were used previously, it still somehow works very well. The materia joke was very well placed, and the whole thing is just paced so well, illustrating the differences between reality and fantasy that allow Seymour to continue to live as he does. The tournament was well involved and the general story remains original and written very well, from the stories of Seymour’s past battles, to the greatest one he might have to face in the future… the battle against reality. It’s a great first RP because it really sets it up for one hell of a climax… and the end of an era. Arsonist #1 Syko Arsonist writes very well, and his use of language is almost always smooth, with a good balance of dialogue and description, both of which are done very well. We’re on very familiar ground as his story continues, the gym being burnt down, and Syko knowing who it was that committed the crime. There’s a slight feeling of melodrama to it all, as Syko gets interrupted as he tries to commit murder to the person who has turned ‘arsonist’ on him. Then we get an addition to the story, of Syko as a baby and the revelation of his true mother, who happens to find him and drop the bombshell. I’m not sure if it’s having watched Star Wars several times, having seen the same storyline done before, or knowing nothing about the Marissa character before the brief interlude… but this ending doesn’t really have the cliff-hanger impact that I think it was going for. Nevertheless, it’s a very well written RP, and at the moment it’s very close between the competitors. Almasy #2 So, if that was the silence, then this must be the storm. The way Aerith/Laura tells Seymour of his predicament was done in a very blunt way, more-so than I was expecting. The reactions, though, are priceless, as Seymour seeks to defend the fantasy world he believes he lives in. I think that after the initial resistance he gives in too quickly though, and his words and actions seem to be slightly out of synchronisation with the narrative, in that he doesn’t appear to be having as much trouble adjusting as is suggested. Maybe this is due to his unwavering faith in his wife and her love, but either way it stood out slightly to me as something that wasn’t absolutely spot on. The rest of the RP is a joy though. It ties together Seymour’s past with his present, and point to his future. Life won’t be the same, but he can still be the bastion of goodness and light, fighting the allure of the villains and perhaps winning the Cruiserweight tournament. I’m really not sure where things will go after this, should Seymour advance, as it will be strange to see Almasy without him being deluded… but perhaps there will be just as much mileage in the gimmick with him adjusting to the real world around him. All in all, a great couple of RPs. Arsonist #2 Syko’s got to pull off something very good indeed to take this one, but he’s definitely capable of it, so let’s have a look. We begin with Syko’s alleged mother and a detective trying to convince Syko that he is in fact her son. The detective produces evidence of it, but Syko is understandably sceptical of the whole deal. This storyline is broken up with Syko’s reflections on his own career, and what it will all mean to him. It is interesting that both characters are trying to win this big prize to be remembered as a winner, and that they have progressed this far to meet one another. Alas, only one can make it to the final, and even then they are far from guaranteed an ultimate victory. The woman is preying on Syko’s mind though, and it is having a profound effect on him. He has endured through tough times before, but he is potentially undergoing a change here with regards to his life… much like Seymour again. We end on Syko agreeing to take the DNA test, and the likelihood of finding out that the woman is his real mother (unless there is a twist yet to come). It would be very interesting to see if Syko’s demeanour would alter knowing she told the truth, or if his convictions would stay in place about not needing to see her or be interested in her despite this news. All in all, a very good RP. Decision time: Both men left me wanting to read more, and to see where their story goes next. Both men involved their opponent and the tournament. Both men wrote very well, both descriptively and with regards to the spoken word. Both sets of RPs entertained me a great deal. As such, this is a great semi-final to the tournament, and both men have shown they deserve to be here. We need a winner though. Seymour Almasy brought to a head pretty much everything that he has been going through since the character was created (though I personally have only seen his recent history). This was a bold move, but it worked very well. It’s an interesting and very original twist on the theme of self-discovery, and it fits the characters perfectly. I think this gives him the advantage over Syko Arsonist, as his storyline was a little less ‘new’ to me, and was something I had seen done before. There was also a slight feeling that the ‘mother’ storyline was too conveniently placed. It’s something that can happen in the world of e-feds, character lurching from one life-altering crisis to the next… and then the next… and then the next. Sometimes it can seem like reaching, that just as one external catastrophe has been dealt with, another springs up, as if the character were cursed. None of the dilemmas and problems are directly of Syko’s making, and so it all seems a little contrived when placed next to the internal conflicts of the Seymour Almasy storyline. This is an edge for Seymour, and that edge turns in to a victory from the simple fact that I did enjoy his work a little more than Syko’s. I just thought it hung together slightly better, the writing just slotted together slightly better and I wanted to read a third Seymour RP more than a third Syko one. No disrespect to Syko at all, and any ‘criticisms’ found here are me really going out of my way to find fault with two sets of excellent RPs. Syko is very, very good and one of the very best in PTC. Seymour Almasy though, on this showing, was just one rung higher, and as such he progresses through to the finals. My pick to win – Seymour Almasy.
