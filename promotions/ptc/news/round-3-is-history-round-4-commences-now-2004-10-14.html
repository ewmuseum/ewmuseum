Round 3 is history! Round 4 commences.... NOW!

14 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Congratulations to the Round 3 winners, who will now play on into Round 4, with one exception. Johnny Lexicon finally reclaims his spot in GTT4 by taking the place of George English (Prime), who resigned after posting his work due to [detail withheld]. English was defeated in Round 1 by Dr. Curiosity, received a playin into Round 2 and subsequently defeated Joe Black, one of the tournament favourites, then Tony Aliso in Round 3. PTC would like to wish English's handler [detail withheld]. Round 4 details: Roleplay Period starts: Now Roleplay Period ends: 11.59pm Saturday 23rd October. Roleplay Limit: 3RP's maximum (9 days, don't waste them. Earlier GTT's were 3 from the first round, so no whining!) Anti Sandbagging limit is in effect: One RP per day on the final two days. No late RP's will be accepted for overtime or for the sandbagging rules.
