Round 2 Results: Rockies Bracket

1 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Muppet Man d Scottywood (2 votes to 0) to advance. Horazon d Caitlin Fairchild (2 votes to 0) to advance. Alex Grey advances. Dr. Curiosity advances. Jamie Fletcher d Justin Evers (2 votes to 1) to advance. Elia Smith advances. Sebastian Moraco advances. Digital Mortality d Joshua Taro Freedom (2 votes to 0) to advance. Round 3 matches: Muppet Man vs Horazon Alex Grey vs Dr. Curiosity Jamie Fletcher vs Elia Smith Sebastian Moraco vs Digital Mortality Judges Comments Muppet Man v Scottywood Muppet Man: RP - Loved the humour... The whole RP was unique and just had a satisfying flow to it. There was a few things I didn't quite understand but they were minor. Scottywood: RP - Had good moments. I liked the fact he actually discussed his match a bit, the way a RP match should be. Length suffered a bit. Quality was good though. Pick: Muppet Man Reason: Over all I felt more interested with Muppet Man's roleplay then I did with Scottywood. Both RPers are good so the choice wasn't easy. I just felt that Muppet made more of an attempt to win then Scottywood did. Muppet Man/A) In depth roleplay. Like the situation that happened in the beginning. Also the closing paraphs kicked --- in the end. No real problems to complain about, well done. (Note you may want to explain upon any reference to other promotions, not everybody (like me) know who you are taking about. Same goes for other events. Plus I'm no Brit, but £1000 sounds like a frigging lot of money.) Muppet Man/B) Great start. Your gimmick was shown really well in this one. Sort of bored me out with the dialog a bit but not too much. Scottywood) The title alone got my attention. The RP itself was also good but a second one would've made this an even more interesting bout and possibly an upset. Pick - Muppet Man Horazon vs Caitlin Fairchild Caitlin Fairchild Review Another Day at the Gym Very good visuals. I don't know if it's because I've seen gyms such as that, but it was excellent opening images. Yet, the promo was very, I guess you could say forced. I mean it's very bland. Then when you switched from talking about Horazon, then all of a sudden switched to talking about seeing your father again? It just didn't do anything for me at all. Good visuals, but overall it was a poor read. Never Gonna Stop Starts off good. I think you actually showed what you can do in this promo. The talk about your characters past were very good, and this roleplay you didn't force feed your opponent to me which made it much better in my opinion. The ending was very nice and I liked how you didn't really make her break her thought, that made it a great promo in my opinion. This one is much better than your other one. Horazon Review *Winner* Death and Life Part I Wow. I have to say that is like a perfect roleplay in my mind. The whole time standing still thing during the car wreck, man, I was like actually there. I have to say you set me in the perfect mindset for that promo at the beginning and I can't wait to read part two. Death and Life Part II Talked about a near death experience. I'm sure everyone has thought about something like that happening to them at some point in their life, a dead realitive or friend coming back from the beyond to tell them something. That was the single most best example of how an event like that would come to be and what would happen in it. I just can't praise this role play even more it was that good. Pick: Horazon Horazon told the better story of the two, which was enjoyable to read and looks good within the next few rounds. Pick: Horazon Alex Grey Terminating the Project The promo started off so great, it looked like it was going to be the best read I have had in a long while, personally I didn't mind the cursing, but you might want to hold back a little bit on that in the future. The RP was above average, then it kind of wandered out when the whole ordeal with you getting shot happened.... that was kind of odd. I think you had this promo planned out perfectly in your mind, but you didn't feel like going in great detail on how the later half of the situation came about. I liked it. Not one of the best RP's out there, but definately a good one. While it Lasts Ugh. I really didn't want to see another roleplay related to that first one. The style of this one really didn't do anything for me. The roleplay seemed kind of choppy and it didn't give me a good feel, or set me in a decent flow. The only thing that I really like about your roleplays is how you insert what he's thinking between dialogue. I can't help but feel that you've written better pieces. Dr. Curiosity Pure... pwnage. Justin Evers vs Jamie Fletcher Justin Evers: RP - Slightly educational, felt sad about the sandwich. The promo was pretty decent, nothing too out of the ordinary though. The thing that saved it was it's realisticness. People can somewhat relate to this promo on a homework/home business level. The interview was good but not anything new. Jamie Fletcher: RP 1 - Good humor, grammatical errors were at a low. I always loved a good drunken english story. Obviously this guy did some WMW scouting to get some RP material which is a plus. I got lost a couple of times though, parts of the RP really didn't make sense. All in all though it was good. RP 2 - This was an average roleplay. Nothing much else I can say about it, I liked the first RP alot better. It seemed alot more creative then this one. Winner: Jamie Fletcher Reason: If Justin Evers would have shot out another RP, he could have won this one. However 2 Jamie Fletcher RPs do indeed overtake 1 Justin Evers RP. So Jamie advances. 1.LOL even though it jumped it was funny, the fly gave us the reason for the jumping. Othe then that great job keep it going. 2.no 2nd RP Jamie Fletcher 1.The first RP isn’t super strong, but it is a good one, He needs to bring more in the next levels of this completion. 2. In the eyes of the wrestler and in the mind of that person, this RP is a little stronger then the first I would tell him to keep working at it and don’t give up. Winner: Jamie Fletcher Third judge comment TBA Elia Smith 1. What can you say not a bad RP at all being unfamiliar with the character I got a little idea what she is like. Gotta say good luck with the GTT4 to the handler. 2.The Second RP shows a little more to what could come from the handler if faced with a stronger opponent in a latter match up. I have to say good job and keep it up. Joshua Taro Freedom vs Digital Mortality Joshua Taro Freedom: RP 1 - Little errors but the RP was rather boring. He dragged out this tough guy persona for alil too long and this hatred between him and his father didn't help things. It could have been alot worse, but at the same time it also could have been better. RP 2 - Length was good, the core story of the RP was there. Alot of grammatical errors though. Had to double back on a couple of lines to see where he was trying to go. The style was unique but I felt he kind of dragged it out a little too long. Better from the first though. Digital Mortality: RP 1 - Length was good and there was almost no grammatical errors. The RP was deep and kept me interested. The ending left me wanting more, which is the exact reaction anyone should get from reading a RP. RP 2 - Not as good as the first one, but enough to keep me interested. I liked the conclusion and felt satisfied when I finished reading it. Good work all in all. Pick: Digital Mortality. Reason: I didn't have to struggle to finish Digital Mortality's RP like I did with JT Freedom's. There was more creativity with Digi as well. I give props to both men though, both had a pretty good showing. Awesome writings here, very great overall on both of the writings the people in the match up did, however there must be one winner and my choice has to go with who I feel has the better work over all and that was Digital Mortality. Both competitors should feel very proud of themselfs because it was very close and the writings were flawless to me. Pick: Digital Mortality Rockies
