Round 3 / Bracket Final Results!

4 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bracket 1 Seymour Almasy (GW) d Horazon (FAW) Bracket 2 Carys (RPW) vs Skyo Arsonist (FA) Syko Arsonist: RP #1 - "Burn Baby, Burn: Forgive and Forget Pros: - Nice introduction. Syko effectively summarized his time in the tournament thus far, and gave the reader a good, concise description of his current feelings and his opponent. - The storyline. I really digged the continuing storyline of how Syko was in the tournament to pay off a debt, and now that he's done that, he's continuing to be in the tournament for himself. It makes sense, and it was conveyed effectively. - Writing style. This is one of my first opportunities to read Syko's work, and I enjoyed it a lot. There was a lot of switching back and forth between different scenes, but everything was constructed smartly in that it wasn't hard to follow where the story was at all. Cons: - Content. There were a few spelling, grammar and tense errors, but on the whole nothing that took away from the overall enjoyment of the RP. - The only real criticism I have with Syko's RP is that it ended abruptly. I presume he was planning to continue the storyline with another RP, but never got round to it, which was a shame as I was looking forward to seeing where he was headed. Carys: RP #1 - "Oh Shitontoast" Pros: - Writing. Carys has an exceptional knack of making the reader visualise exactly what they're reading, down to the very last detail. - Build-up. Carys built this RP up well. At first it started kinda slow, but then it got going and it hit a good rhythm before the big crescendo. It's all about building interest and Carys did this well. Cons: - The match. It just felt to me that the character of Carys was taking the match secondary (which I guess had to be expected when you're a demon hunter, but still, some importance for it would've been nice), as it was hardly mentioned and apart from a name drop here and there, so there was no real relevance to the match and no reasons explained as to why she'd win the match. - The abrupt finish. See the comments about Syko. It's hard when you're expecting to do a 2 RP series but only get one up, because more often than not the first RP is just left hanging there with no conclusion whatsoever. The Call They were both very good RP's, but with the abrupt finish of both of them with no conclusion, it made them very hard to judge. In the end though, my vote goes to Syko Arsonist. Carys was good and I think technically was the better writer, but there just seemed to be something special missing in the RP. Syko's had a more interesting story I thought, and his RP left me wanting to read his second more than Carys. It was a close match though and both writers can be proud with their efforts. Bracket 3 Ethan Knight (RPW) d The Cockjockey (XUW) Bracket 4 Karina Wolfenden (RPW) vs Jimmy Diablo (FA) Jimmy Diablo Vindication: Piecing Together the Mirror, Shards #4-7 Pros: Good writing style. Good writing period. Lots of story here. And quite a bit of character development. You can get into it, for the most part. The concept of doing the two separate scenes really rocked. Gave it a cinematic feel to it. Cons: Not too many. Very good writing. Only thing to really complain about is that it was to be continued and it wasn’t. Another shortcoming was that it wasn’t much on the actual wrestling aspect. But it was interesting all the same. Final Judgment: I liked it. Stellar piece of work. Karina Wolfenden Preparation is The Key., 1/2 vs. Diablo Pros: Excellent writing here. Even better then the last round of writing for K-Wolf. There is nothing here that is really wrong. Character development, scenery descriptions, and in general good writing. Cons: Again, not many in this rp. The only thing *I* personally have an issue with is the mentioning of a few real life wrestlers right at the beginning and one line in the near end of the roleplay. But that’s a pet peeve of mine. Other then that, it was very good. A good challenge for Diablo’s first rp. Karina Wolfenden A Change Of Scenery, 2/2 vs. Diablo Pros: Damn… even better then the first one in my eyes that I’ve read. Sky must have sparked something in the K-Wolf. This one showed some of what K-Wolf really has to go through in her life, and shows interaction with her family. Now, admittedly, not very familiar with this character, I really liked it. As of reading both, I have to say… too bad Diablo didn’t get his second one up. Cons: There weren’t many, if any at all. I really liked it. Final Judgment: 2 great works from K-Wolf Overall Judgment: Kinda bad when you have a bracket that produces 3 works of great writing, but one misses getting up #4. Against almost anyone else, the one that Diablo did would have been enough to overcome two. But in this case, the law of quantity has to come in again. Winner: Karina Wolfenden. CW1 brackets
