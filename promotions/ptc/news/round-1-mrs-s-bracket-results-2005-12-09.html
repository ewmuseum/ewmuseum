Round 1: Mrs S. Bracket Results

9 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SAM WOLACK V DR. COLIN GILCHRIST Project Eos: The Making of a Mad Man by Dr. Colin Gilchrist The first half is too similar to your IG pieces, where hillbilly crowd with pitchforks / journalists / government lackey comes in, gets told some info, then killed. It was exceptionally good the first time, but the second and now third time going to the well are wearing thin. Andy had this syndrome problem too with Dr. Curiosity during GTT4, where the writing was formulaic. Several people I know of picked up on it, and he fully knew it and successfully covered it up with comedy and witty dialogue. You haven't used comedy, so you need to find another method to make this character work if you are going to go with the murders each time. It would have been better without the first reference to GTT5, where Gilchrists' reasons for entering it were to top up his finances to an insanely large amount again... those few paragraphs were a bit silly, and appeared to be designed to appeal to the relevance crowd than having any legitimate use. While it gives meaning to the title of the RP, the relationship recanting felt like filler... no real purpose to having it there, as it didn’t add anything to the plot – there should be some symmetry or connection, instead of bundling in two concurrent stories in with no interrelation at all. It didn't tease for next round, and it didn't add anything significant to this round. Sure, its great stuff to put in there, but you need tofollow it through. For example, complement the (slightly cheesy) ending with the basement murder and also have Gilchrist’s wife issue him with unexpected divorce papers… or something like that, to tie present and past tangents together neatly. I quite like the premise of having the retrograded marine fight in Gilchrists place under a mask. That's introducing something new to the story, and is exactly what I was looking for from you. Haunted by Sam Wolack Well written. A particularly good line to have Wolack reliving over and over again, it has just the right amount of “haunt” to it. Result: Wolack over Gilchrist I agonized over this for quite some time. I was hoping for Gilchrist to bust out something epic like with his first IG piece, after missing the mark with his second. Voting him out feels like I’m punishing him for failing to live up to my expectations, because while he didn’t, this piece was still very good. Wolack’s was also very good, although heavily reliant on the "He didn't make it either" mantra to give it substance. Without that, it would have been very, very ordinary, and wouldn't have got the win. JAMES VON DOOM V JORGE SAMUELSSON Believe by James von Doom This started out well, and I quite liked the Horny Mathematician cheering von Doom up. Then.... and one of my pet peeves with competitive tournament matches is this … you put 5-6 pages worth of detailed tournament analysis into a roleplay. It doesn't matter how well you write it or how many clever quips you put in it, it is painful to read it, because its already on the discussion forum written up by so many other people. The advice and then understanding! by Jorge Samuelsson That was interesting. Viking trash talk. Some pretty good stuff in there. Needs a bit more focus on a storyline for future rounds though. Result: Jorge Samuelsson d James von Doom I quite liked the first half of von Doom’s RP. It was quite a lot more readable to me than a few of his earlier Infinite Gauntlet works. I wish he’d chosen something else for the second half though. He's a lot closer to being able to win a match now here than he was before. CRAIG MILES V ARMAAN The Legend of the Silver Sunglasses: "Promises and Pills -- An Introduction to Craig's Acid-Based Quest of Darwinism" by Cocky Craig Miles I've been told by many people over a long time that you know what you are doing. I can certainly perceive and appreciate the intention behind it and the work put into this, but the finished product is too "randomly scattered" for my personal taste. I completely lost touch with what I was supposed to be thinking about on a regular basis. Writing this way is a hit or miss proposition depending on who the reader is on any given day. For example, anyone from XUW circa 2003 will tell you that PTC hates comedy writing, whereas anyone from XUW circa 2004 will say the exact opposite. Contemplation by Armaan This is very sweet. You just gained a casual fan. Armaan over Cocky Craig Miles It is quite tricky to judge this one because of the completely different styles. Armaan's focussed approach takes it over Miles' party-like atmosphere. CHANDLER TSONDA ADVANCES VIA DQ As Crazy as is necessary by Chandler Tsonda Wow! In the first 2 paragraphs you had me chuckling at the fun-poking. And the stuff about "Single Personality Syndrome" had me instantly interested. I kept on reading, and the first few paragraphs had me chuckling along. I liked the way he was writing exactly what everyone thinks in those situations. However, I found myself losing interest, as there was seemingly no direction. Then I read the link between the two birthdays, and was hoping we'd end up somewhere to do with that, but the build up didn't come off, and the ending seemed somewhat anticlimactic. Judging comments for Tsonda's opponent withheld. KID KRAZIE V ETHAN FROST Introducing Kid Krazie by Kid Krazie The intro didn't exactly set the sparks flying, and the setting of talking to a psychiatrist was pretty cliché. As I was reading along I found myself chuckling along. There were some funny moments in there that’s for sure. It was well written, but a lot of the material I had seen done before and I found myself not really getting into it and the general feel of it was pretty bland. Its not UWF vs HATE, its Frost vs Krazie by Ethan Frost Read extremely well. It flowed nicely and I enjoyed reading it. I am a big fan of roleplays being about wrestling. While this was nothing spectacular story wise, I enjoyed the OOC/IC connotations he had associated, the odd witty quip, and the underlying story. Result: Ethan Frost d. Kid Krazie Krazie's was nothing special. It was an average roleplay. Frosts was good, well written, but lacked something. Can't quite put my finger on it though. Good enough to advance though. SALAOK V CHRIS COLLINS Self Doubt Pt 1 by Chris Collins I liked the weasel theme, the way you wove it into your work was a nice touch. But other than the weasel, nothing stood out to me, nothing good, nothing bad. Once Opened, Some things can’t be shut by Salaok I enjoyed the details in your work, it flowed well and was nicely paced. Result: Salaok d Chris Collins I'm giving Salaok the win. SPARTAN V LEGEND OF PETE Nicolae Romanescu 1931-2005. Father and Friend by Legend of Pete This piece felt quite warm and fuzzy to read, almost like watching the pilot of an anticipated new spinoff series where you know the faces in it, but the setting and support characters are new and shiny. Nice work! Core by Spartan I quite liked your Sage character from Infinite Gauntlet, and am pleased that you deviated slightly from normal practise and brought him along for the ride with your new character. A couple of hints as to who he is but not enough to make it a disappointment when we do eventually find out - very clever. Hey asshole, give me back the damned book! Result: Spartan d Legend of Pete No downer at all for Pete, he provided an entertaining read that would have advanced through other opponents without a problem, and he easily had the potential to knock over this one too, with a bit more content. Ryan successfully managed to avoid the element he knows I didn't like about some of his previous writing, and it definately shows. He's successfully building nicely on his IG work with Sage, but not relying on familiarity with it to make this work. BIG BILLY BLACKWELL V EMILIO RAGE Big Billy Rides Again by Big Billy Blackwell Interesting piece. I liked the way it spliced between the dialogue and the flashbacks, providing some vital insight into the character, which might I say is a very good one. I really enjoyed the cowboy stories. Didn;t fully grip me, but finished feeling pleased I'd read something good. Rough Times, Hombre. Rough Times by Emilio Rage This bound along at a fantastic pace. Sneaky witticisms and one of the funniest things I've read for a while (Getting released then re-arrested for weed - Wished it had finished there for a great comedy ending, but wouldn't have helped Emilio get to his GTT5 match!) While not some of his best work, it was certainly a very good piece that had me very entertained. Result: Emilio Rage d. Big Billy Blackwell This was a pretty tight call. Both men had an excellent showing and I enjoyed reading both. But I have to give the nod to Rage, whose storyline through the piece had me trying to read the next line before I'd finished the one I was on. KENNY MERCURY V BRANDON YOUNGBLOOD A Warrior's Way by Brandon Youngblood An interesting opening, while nothing grabbed me. It was essentially a dream sequence with some "Live action" cut in. Loaded with Metaphor it linked Youngblood's ongoing storyline with GTT and Mercury nicely. Kudos for that. By the end of it I realised I'd read a very good piece of writing. Shooting Stars by Kenny Mercury I really, really liked this. I found myself chuckling along with the story, and while its not the first time I've seen a wrestler talking to a belt, found it very funny. Was it wrong to laugh at the lines about beating women up? Class. I was absolutely entertained throughout the reading of this. Kenny Mercury d. Brandon Youngblood Well, this was one of the matches of the tournament, and a tough pick. Both styles were wildly different, and both had their good points. There were very, very few points to note. Out of about 11 matches I've judged, this is the joint tightest. I just have to go with the one I enjoyed the most. Youngblood could have gone far, but Kenny Mercury really pulled it off for me. JIMMY BONG V ELIA SMITH Nobody does it Worser by Jimmy Bong Classic Bong, all the way. The audio hypes helped add so much more to the RP reading experience by giving me a sample of Bong’s voice pattern the way you intend it. It adds so much to the character having heard those. Quite a few belly laughs. A Past. A Future. Now A Present…(Part 1) by Elia Smith Buddy, this liberally craps all over your IG work from the earlier rounds, from a great height. Everything that irked me about your writing style has been given due attention. Only a couple of typos and incorrect words this time compared to dozens last time ;) It really makes a huge difference. Your hypes also gave the RP added meaning. Result: Jimmy Bong d Elia Smith Damn. This is another one of these opposing style matches where both pieces absolutely deserve to go through. I slept on this for two nights, right up until I started typing this paragraph into the forums. As close and difficult to choose as Fatbadger and Ragnarok were for me, and a few others as well. I literally feel like the penultimate e-heel for putting Smith out under the circumstances, but I have to pick someone, and won't let myself be influenced by anything other than what's on the RP pages :( All I can say is that I don't connect to Elia quite as well as I do to Bong. That's all I can give you for a reason. SUMMARY - Sam Wolack d Dr. Colin Gilchrist - Jorge Samuelsson d James von Doom - Armaan d Craig Miles - Chandler Tsonda advances - Ethan Frost d Kid Krazie - Salaok d Chris Collins - Spartan d Legend of Pete - Emilio Rage d Big Billy Blackwell - Kenny Mercury d Brandon Youngblood - Jimmy Bong d Elia Smith Brackets
