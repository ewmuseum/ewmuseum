Round 3 Results: Himalayas Bracket

12 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Latch Key Kid advances. Nick Persia d Mr. Darkness (2 votes to 1) to advance. George English d Tony Aliso (2 votes to 1). ** Karina Wolfenden d Ichiro Nakazura (2 votes to 1) to advance. **Johnny Lexicon takes George English's place in Round 4 due to English resigning after posting his roleplay. Judging comments: Latch Key Kid The usual. We’ve all got a usual don’t we? Breaking apart from a team, or grouping can be crippling to a wrestler that may not be used to being on his or her own. The support from the other group members is nicely shown, as the roleplay is a nice, lighthearted affair. Which works. If it were otherwise, it’d most likely have crashed and burned. Good mention of the opponent too, not going too overboard on the situation, yet playing it nicely into the advice given to the “Kid.” The rejoining of the members at the end of the second roleplay was to be expected, almost as to say “Avengers Assemble!” Good efforts, nothing groundbreaking though. (8.0/7.8) Mr. Darkness v Nick Persia Dark’s 2nd- A ton of symbolism but I felt it was a little short for how much it was built up in the beginning. I would have liked maybe a scene with the lions doing battle, or maybe actually doing something, rather than just getting details on each. Didn’t seem to be much plot and I felt it probably could have actually been better if it was straight forward with the characters, rather than just comparing one lion to Nick and another to Dark. Persia’s 2nd- Nice introduction into the story. However, there were a few technical errors that really stood out to me, such as a chuckle appearing on the child’s face. Call me stupid if I’m wrong, but isn’t a chuckle a form of laughter, not a facial expression. A few grammatical errors as well. The plot just as before wasn’t anything too interesting. Just Nick and his nephew discussing the obvious. Would have liked it more if the interaction took place somewhere away from the ring. While it gives Nick those “adult” qualities that are great with his nephew, it just seemed like the same old thing I’ve read before with every other character I’ve ever seen with children. Pick: Mr. Darkness Mr. Darkness, short and to the point. The first roleplay was a great discussion piece. The dialogue was rich, and kept an uneasy tone about it. Making Mr. Darkness indeed…dark. Few grammatical errors, nothing major. The second roleplay then gives the downfall of a great first roleplay. The comparison between the lions, and the upcoming battle that Darkness was going to be in, was a good idea, but it just fell flat. More commenting from Darkness himself could’ve helped this out greatly, or something…it just didn’t appeal as well as it could’ve. (7.8/7.3) “Where are we going?” That’s the perfect quote for this. I felt as if this could’ve gone so many places, but just…didn’t. It was very nicely paced, and the mentioning of his opponent was an added bonus, however it set up to become so much more than it ended up being. Being split into two parts was almost unnecessary as it could’ve been told as one roleplay without dragging along. The way the family is brought together throughout the roleplay shows for a great combined effort, and Persia pulls it off nicely. A few grammatical errors, but nothing taking away from his story. Short, not very effective, but brings warmth. (7.8/7.7) A good bout between the two of them overall. Pick: Nick Persia Both had good roleplays, but Persia had more of a thread running between his two roleplays. I feel that Mr. Darkness rushed his second roleplay and oversimplified it, which ultimately hurt him in the long run. I also liked the subject matter of Persia’s, particularly the second roleplay. It was nice to see a positivist, happy roleplay between uncle and nephew instead of the typical, foreboding, dreary roleplays that seem to be commonplace in the hobby. Pick: Nick Persia. Tony Aliso vs George English Both players wrote good RP's. Aliso's RP's were good and told a nice story, but they seemed to drag on a bit. Perhaps it was the mood of the storyline? In any event I found them to be well-written with some decent character development, but I couldn't get interested in them. English, on the other hand, had a well-written RP that held my interest with a good story and a better idea of what the character is about. If I gave a numerical score to Aliso's RP's and averaged them, the number would be lower than English's one....albeit by a slim margin, so I give the victory to him. I know English had to withdraw from the tourney, but I won't vote against him because of that. He submitted good work and won this round in my eyes. Pick: English Aliso’s 1st- Great description, and a very original storyline. Absolutely loved the human qualities given to him. Even though he’s got the GTT coming up, he shows just like any other normal person he has other things on his mind, in this case a relationship with a woman he’s found himself bound to, but who is now dead. Aliso’s 2nd- Continued well from the first. Wish there was more development that explained the relationship between the brothers, other than it just mentioning that Pete was jealous. Pretty nice ending to it all though. Grandad’s RP- Like the story of how George got involved in the tournament and how he originally planned to foil the plans of those who entered him in, and how it didn’t work out in the end. Loved how the history of the character was worked into this piece, but I feel it was dragged out a bit and got a bit dull after awhile. Pick: Aliso Tony Aliso vs “Grandad” George English Aliso showed some brilliant storytelling at times, although the pacing seemed a little off. At times when it looked like it was going to pick up, and the ball was going to start rolling, someone hit the slow motion button. Don’t get me wrong, detail is great, just as long as it doesn’t take away from the feel of the story. The Becky character came and went too quickly, but attention was paid to developing her character, that’s a plus. In the conclusion of the story, I believe there wasn’t enough of a conflict between the brothers to bring a secure enough of an ending to accommodate the resolution. Could’ve been a blockbuster second half to the story. (8.1/7.6) As for English…what a crazy…adventurous old man. His passing back and forth through memories and the present day really gave a sense of understanding what he was all about. The seriousness that’s represented in the writing is loose enough to be able to take the entire experience tongue-in-cheek. Keeping good attention to detail, the paranoia induced in this tough old man, who’s worrying about a rebirth in Communism coming right from the Magic Kingdom, was enough to belt out some good chuckles. If it weren’t for its randomness, and its incompleteness, the roleplay would’ve been pure gold. Being forged from silver isn’t half-bad though. Pick: Karina Wolfenden (90% average) over Ichiro Nakazura (88.125% average) Karina Wolfenden vs Ichiro Nakazura Karina Wolfenden? Nothing short of pure brilliance. Great feel to the roleplay, didn’t go off track and bore me at anytime. Nice detail, and an easy going flow to the story allowed me to step into the story, and see what it was to be Karina. Was able to easily identify with her train of thought at times, and from the first, to the second rp, was able to easily keep up with what was going on. By the end of the second roleplay, when the work world is put behind her, Karina is finally free mentally from the day…something that EVERYONE can relate to. Great work, extremely solid. (9.3/9.5) Ichiro Nakazura…definitely not someone to be taken lightly. He came into his first roleplay with guns blazing. Letting the reader step into Ichiro’s life, and to share in to situations in which he’s involved with. What makes it different for Ichiro…we give a damn about his situations! And they’re situations that are entirely believable and that some of us may have even gone through ourselves. Nice doctor mini-cliffhanger at the end of the first RP. Roleplay number two was like hitting a 3-point buzzer beater. The doctor’s office, although done before, was done brilliantly. The roadblock that was seemingly put up in Ichiro’s way, was crushed by his drive to want to be #1…with a bullet. Difficult to top…very difficult to top indeed. (9.3/9.6) Probably the most enjoyment I’ve ever had reading roleplays, both writers got better as the stories went on. Pick: Ichiro Nakazura Nakazura – can I see your doctors report please?
