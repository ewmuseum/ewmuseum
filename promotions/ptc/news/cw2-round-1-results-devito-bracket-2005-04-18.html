CW2 Round 1 Results - Devito Bracket

18 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cereal Killer (XUW) d Chris Collins (Core) 2 votes to 0. Drake Coleman (IWLE) d Captain Jack Morgan (LOW) 2 votes to 0 Enrique Moraless (PRIME) d Fred Grest (FA) 2 votes to 0 Elia Smith (Global) d Tyler O'Neill. Devito Bracket Round 2 matchups: Cereal Killer (XUW) v Enrique Moraless (PRIME) Elia Smith (Global) v Drake Coleman (IWLE) (zing~!) Judging Comments: Cereal Killer v Chris Collins Judge 1: Chris Collins: Great read, to say the least. The roleplay's format reminded me of the format a movie would use, where it would show one scene, followed by a scene cut to a new scene, followed by another scene cut, etc. I've never really read many pieces like this; and I must say, the format really intrigues me. It seemed like the "cuts" in the story were well placed, and made the "scene switch" just in time before it could have gotten stale. Only flaw I found with this story, and I suppose it's more of a personal issue, was I thought there could have been a deeper thought process as to how Collins came upon the revelation that was his father's motivation through reverse psychology. I would've liked it better to have seen Collins run this thought through his head more sophisticatedly before he became overwhelmed with guilt. However, I liked the ending, and it definitely left me with wanting to know more about Collins and to see if he shall confront his father or not. Cereal Killer: Another great read, no doubt. This roleplay was very well written and I loved every second of reading it. The introduction was great, as it gave me perfect insight into the kind of person Cereal Killer was (seeing as how I had no knowledge of him whatsoever). After reading these roleplays, I'm beginning to get the feel that this movie-eqsue format in eWrestling is a popular trend nowadays. I wouldn't know as I'm an avid card reader, not a roleplay reader. Anyhow, the beginning of the roleplay was definitely enthralling, forcing me to continue my reading. I loved how it gave insight into the history of CK. I'm very intrigued by history; so, naturally, that earns extra points in my eyes. Definitely one of the better reads of the tournament, if I may say so. I felt like CK's story was overall more refreshing and entertaining. I simply enjoyed it more so than I did Collins. Not to take anything from Collins, as he produced an almost equally great roleplay in my eyes, but I feel CK's was better. Pick: Cereal Killer Judge 2: No surprise here, more so if you read both roleplays. Cereal Killer to round two. Now I'm not saying Chris Collins did not impress, as I enjoyed his roleplay greatly. I remember his work in Rage and XUCWSAGFD or whatever Mason ran and this was leaps and bounds greater in every way. But Jack showed great mastery of his character and writing. I was surprised by how well he knew his character and made the entire roleplay flow. Keep it up and he'll be the second to win back to back tournaments here in PTC. Pick: Cereal Killer Drake Coleman (IWLE) v Captain Jack Morgan (LOW) Judge 1: Drake Coleman: "The Violence of Truth" was a very well written roleplay in my mind. It gave background information on the character which is a personal plus for me, as I don't have any knowledge of the character whatsoever. The recurring dreams (or shall I say nightmares?) intrigues me to say the least. This roleplay has definitely left me wanting to read more on the Drake Coleman character. His "antihero" persona is interesting as well. This is credited to when Coleman told the random caller, "I don't even want this title, you can have it." Captain Jack Morgan: "Not a Pirate?" was definitely an interesting read as well. I've never read anything on either of the three characters mentioned (Morgan, Killer Klown, and von Doom), but I got a good feel for them in this roleplay. The style was definitely different. Not used to the playwrighting format. Nothing wrong with that, however. I felt the overall story was pretty good. von Doom claiming Morgan wasn't a pirate with Morgan feeling inclined to prove his friend wrong. What happened with that, anyhow? Morgan never proved he was a true a pirate. von Doom, apparently an assassin of some secret organization, ordered to murder his friend was a great dilemma to add. Personally, I would have liked to see more thought process from von Doom on this. Though, that would take away from Morgan and make the roleplay more centered around von Doom. And that wouldn't be good. I had a better feel for Coleman more so than I did Morgan. I felt like Morgan's roleplay was centered around von Doom more so than Morgan, as it seemed von Doom was the main protagonist in the roleplay with him dealing with the bigger delimma. Both roleplays were nice, I just felt Coleman had the overall better ground story and focused more on the character that is participating in this tournament. Pick: Drake Coleman. Judge 2: Captain Jack Morgan Pros - So funny, had me laughing the whole way through. Nice little story too about not being a pirate and it was good how the story went on from there. The roleplay had relevance about the tournament, and it was always in the correct tense from what I noticed anyway, well written. Length was well done and it didn’t seem to short or too long. Ended very well and still funny. Cons - Only cons I could really see were a couple of spelling errors but nothing too serious. Another thing I suppose is the clown guy as even though he is funny I think it’d be nice to know exactly what he’s saying sometimes. I know you showed it by talking with Jack at points but at others I missed it, no biggy on that though. Drake Coleman Pros - Great roleplay from start to finish, it was relevant to the tournament and still gave me a deep insight into a character I have never seen before. The roleplay was almost dark, the sense of disgrace that Drake was feeling was damn evident from the get go and drew me in as the story went on. Not once did I have to stop reading during the roleplay and I got from start to finish in no time at all. Cons - The only con I can really find is one spelling error throughout the whole thing I feel. The tenses were good as they were split up into different sections and nothing stuck me as odd or irregular. Overall Two great roleplays, one funny and one serious but I must give this one to Drake Coleman as I enjoyed his just a little bit more. No discredit to Jack Morgan as his talent was showed as well in this round. Pick - Drake Coleman Fred Grest vs Enrique Moraless Judge 1: Fred Grest Pros - The roleplay has meaning behind it which I like, it relates well to the tournament and the wrestling business as I whole and I think that is important in this competition. Another pro is that the writer is not afraid to show the background of his character and his weaknesses. A lot of people would probably come into this tournament purely confident and trying to show their superstar is unstoppable with the way he acts. I also liked the length of the roleplay, it wasn’t too long or too short and I feel it ended on the right note. Cons - Some small spelling errors throughout the roleplay which got me thinking exactly what was he saying at that part. There was also a word missing in one of the paragraphs and thankfully I was able to determine what it should have been. That kind of broke the flow of it a little in the fact that I had to stop and re-read a part. Some tenses were also wrong but nothing too major, only part that really confused me was the start. It kind of goes from 3rd person into first. Morales Pros - I really liked the intro, it really set out exactly what Morales is going for in the tournament and was well written. This roleplay also outlines well with the tournament and with the industry. I also like the introduction of the American guy as it spices things up a little especially when they trade insults. I also like the attitudes shown by all three characters, they are worked well by the writer, their expressions tie in well with what is going on. Cons - Near enough the same as the Grest roleplay. A number of small spelling mistakes and two or 3 missing words in this confused me a little but I got round them. I think a little more care could have been taken to ensure that these problems did not occur. In my eyes the roleplay was a little too long for what it was, it kind of started to drag at the end but before that I really liked it. Overall This is a tough one as these roleplays are very even in my eyes but if must make a decision I will have to go for Morales based on the fact that along with his relations to the tournament and wrestling in general he told an underlying story which could develop into something special during the course of the tournament. I know Fred did the same but I feel Morales’ will keep people coming back to see what is going to happen. Pick: Enrique Moraless Judge 2: Comments to be edited in. Pick: Enrique Moraless CW2 brackets
