Results are up!

6 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://ss.primetimecentral.net/index.php Hit the Results link from there. Some navigational tips: - The results script (something I've been tinkering with for the last 8 or 9 months when I have a spare minute) supports full view or chapter view (looking at the matches individually). - Full view can be scrolled through - if you want to go to a paricular match, go to the credits and hit the name and it'll take you back to the top of that match. - Chapter view can be navigated with the Next and Previous buttons, or using the credits, which are also linked, similarly to the full view. Judging: - Thanks to the judges (in no particular order) * Johnny Lexicon * Peter Vetra * Andy * Reyes * RJ * Sammie * Buddy * Leigh S. * Jay Phoenix * Me Each of the contested matches had three judges - and only one match had a clean sweep. Matchwriting: See the credits for who did what. Some really nice work! Matchwriting Standins: Props to both Pete and Jay for filling in when other people bunked on writing their matches. Pete especially for taking the ME, which was a huge task. With everything going on I simply couldn't get the job done no matter how hard I tried (see below). Jay took on not only judging for the ME but one singles match and heavy editing of the tag elimination match. Congratulations: - To the fed that decisively won the Survival Bragging Rights match :D Read the results if you want to know who it is.... I enjoyed everyone's work for that match, thank you. - To the two individuals who claimed the vacant PTC singles titles. The three judges all came up with different winners, so it was a very close match indeed. I could have justifiably given the titles to several of you. Miscellaneous: Kasper Sky traded me the two matches that nobody volunteered to write for a spot in GTT5 (yeah, that makes me a whore, but you gotta do what you gotta do to get things done, and two written matches would have shown me that he was actually committed enough), but only came up with one of them, so he doesn't get it (especially after seeing him hover the other thread and not saying anything after Jeff busted him for playing poker instead of doing the second match).
