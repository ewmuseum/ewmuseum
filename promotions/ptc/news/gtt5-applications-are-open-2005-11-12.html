GTT5 Applications are open!

12 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://gtt5.primetimecentral.net/application.php Come one, come all (almost), tell your friends. The application form will be open for 7 days. The draw of who gets in and the initial matches will be on day 8, on an edition of PTC Radio / PTC Webcast (If I can get that side of it working). As usual, there will be some unique side orders thrown in here and there to make things interesting. Round 1 in particular has something that will require a little more than a roleplay being slapped up on the site with 5 minutes to go before deadline. Guaranteed spot holders need to fill in the form. Failure to do so before the bracket draw will result in you losing your spot to someone else who did fill in the form. As the fine print on the form says, you must use a valid email address, or your application will not be even looked at. When submitting your form, you will receive an email with a link to click - pretty much like validating a forum account. If you have difficulties with this, submit your application again with a different email account. I will not be manually validating any accounts - so if you screw this up, you won't get in. Simple. And.... Meet the used CD Spindle Cover 'o Doom, or as I call it, Randomizer 2005. At the moment, it has a few odd computer screws in it. But, in 8 days time, they will be displaced and it will have the names of all the accepted applicants in it, waiting to be drawn out. Questions, problems with the form etc, post here.
