GTT6 Final Round Details!

7 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Holiday break is over! Jason Snow v Violence Jack v Ember All three had to fight through 90 odd other people to get here. All three want to add a GTT to their CV. After all, a GTT Tourney Win > a UT Reign >>>> RPG anything, right? Jay - Ember's been to a GTT final before with Jay Phoenix.... but that was all the way back in GTT1. Will he be able to fire up and go one better? Justin - Violence Jack has been the preferred pick of many people for years now. This time, no unexpected blankings have stopped him early. Now there's only Snow and Ember in his way. David - Who the hell is Jason Snow, I hear you ask? Well..... In his trophy cabinet you'll find, amongst a bunch of poker chips and other things, four PCW Heavyweight Championship reigns. The best I could do was one, and that was only for like 5 days. So... slouch? I think not. GTT6 - The Final Round - 3 RPs, no more, no less. - Starts: Now. - Finishes. End of Friday, January 19th. - No Sandbagging - you know how that works by now. - Jason Snow has a 1000 word limit... or not. Good luck gentlemen.
