Round 4 Results: Appalacians Bracket

28 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Craig Goat d Seymour Almasy (2 votes to 1) to advance. Jimmy Bong d Steven Caldera (2 votes to 1) to advance. Bracket final matchup: Craig Goat vs Jimmy Bong Judging Comments Seymour Almasy v Craig Goat Seymour Almasy: all roleplays. I summarized this all into one coherent mess so that the story can be explained and why my reasons where as such. As much as the roleplays were good and told a very compelling storyline i believe the opponent should have been more of the focus rather than a short blurb about them. Although the opponent was mentioned in the beginning the story became a bit vacant heading in a different direction than the one that was expected. Good, But seemed to be heading elsewhere. Craig Goat: Mentioned the GTT4 event a couple of times. Curious as to what you were tryng to achieve here. Competition or merely self gain in character. The stories meshed, But i felt it lacked in the over all competition aspect against an opponent. Should have concentrated more on that aspect. Vote: Seymour Almasy on story of roleplay alone. Craig Goat’s story eclipses Seymour’s. It could be a passing of the torch of sorts I guess, but what we’re seeing is Goat’s rise. His story is that of young man coming into himself in spite of the manipulations of Wretched. There’s a strong sense of subtle strength in the character that’s growing, and that sets him apart from Seymour. While all of Seymour’s work is solid, his story of his attempt at growth feels a little less… noble? Having defined himself from rpgs previously, Seymour is trying to define himself as a normal human being. Where Craig’s secret struggle for his identity is against the manipulating relationships in his life (e.g. Wretched, Mum), Seymour’s struggle for his identity seems to be pushing him towards his estranged wife showing a kind of dependency on his “others”. In this battle to reclaim personal identity, Craig Goat appears to be stronger. Looking at the characters specifically, Seymour seems to be a little wishy washy with what he wants and desires, unsure of himself, locked within his comfort zones. While this isn’t necessarily a bad characteristic, how we’re told about it seems a little awkward. Goat’s tiny struggle against the people manipulating him feels like a special secret, revealed to the reader through subtext, but believably hidden from the other manipulative characters. Pick: Craig Goat. Seymour Almasy vs. Craig Goat Before I start, note that I've never read any of either competitor's work before this Round. Thus the comments are based only on your RPs for Round 4. Seymour Almasy RP 1: I like the intro, setting the tone for the RP to come. I like how you use nicknames, accomplishments and descriptions instead of repeating pronouns and names over and over (i.e. "The Final Fantasy," "the UWF North American Champion," "The elderly man"). I also like how your narration - particularly after Seymour left the restaurant - flowed with the dialogue. It wasn't too difficult to jump into the story part-way through, which is also good. You told a bit about Seymour's history and his relationships, while still building up to the next stage. RP 2: Again, intro sets the tone. If this is a regular pattern in your work, I like it. Good follow-up to the stage that was set in the first piece. Solid description and narration, and the dialogue was awkward, in a situation where that's exactly what it should have been. Unimportant side note: Laura Winters turned into Laura Almasy part-way through. Freudian slip? RP 3: Thumbs up to the intro, once more. A good conclusion to this round's work, tying everything together from the first two, and extending upon it. Yet, you've still left a lot to work through, should you advance, with Laura's potential pregnancy, and the potential encounter between Laura and Ashley still floating up in the air. Craig Goat RP 1: Retold the history of Goat - both recent and past - to set up the eventual reunion with the parents. Made it absolutely simple to jump in as a new reader. The whole scenario at the hospital is rather absurd, but it was written in such a way that it seemed normal, as opposed to over-the-top. It could very easily have gone the other way, but it didn't, to your credit. RP 2: Continuing from where you left off in the first piece, with Ma and Pop Goat returning home to find an evil Nurse living in their house. The dialogue was great, and I particularly liked how Mrs. Goat and Nurse Wretched treated Craig similarly, which says a lot about why Craig would put up with and fall in love with Nurse. Great scene in the bedroom, with Craig almost getting what he's long wanted, but not being able to take hold of it. RP 3: Craig recalling Nurse's explanation of what she'd done to Laura Almasy was brilliant. Here he is, thinking about a situation that so perfectly paralleled what had just happened to him, and yet he doesn't realize it, blinded by the faith that there is some shred of decency within Wretched. That is, until - as you so aptly put it - the bubble burst later on. The fact that it tied in to your opponent's work was a nice bonus. Craig's relationship with Mother Goat continues to be hilarious, and a solid parallel to his relationship with Nurse (from what I've read, at least). At the same time, through this piece and the last, the relationship between Mr. and Mrs. Goat also parallels that of Craig and Nurse, culminating in the perfect father-son conversation to close. Conclusion All six RPs were well written, and enjoyable. A close and difficult decision. While I would have liked to see a bit more on GTT4 from Maria - and that's something that Sean did well - Maria's third RP puts her ahead for me. All of the great parallels, the most tournament relevant material of her RPs this round, combined with humour and emotion in a solidly written package. Pick: Craig Goat Jimmy Bong v Steven Caldera The Big Shiny Sight Receptacle - Part I= One of the best RP’s I have read so far, yes it was a story but I liked it a lot. The Big Shiny Sight Receptacle - Part II= The continuation of this RP is great reminds me of a spoof on Mission Impossible. The Big Shiny Sight Receptacle - Part III= Great close, and I love the fact that he’s cover is a wrestler in GTT4. Trouble Brewing= Very good description, a lot of content, but I felt that so far Bongs RP’s are just flowing together much more. The Question= A real good insight to the mindset of Steven Caldera, very nicely put together. The Answer= Still nice description, but location is everything and to have it in the office again one has to wonder does this guy ever get out? Pick: Jimmy Bong Steven Caldera vs Jimmy Bong Steven Caldera: Not to sound too critical here i think this roleplays seemed a bit off point. The roleplays were good, But seemed to not concentrate on the opponent. Need to mention him a few times to show and keep interest in the roleplays. You'll lose reader interest if it goes beyond point of the roleplays. The point should have been your opponent not your current situation. You can lead into current events later or somewhere during the rp. Jimmy Bong: Touched some bases with his opponent, But not enough for the current taste of the roleplays. The match should have been a bit of interest rather then the current happenings. The roleplays were very good, but seemed to stray away from the facts at hand. The match against his opponent. Since both opponents began in the roleplay area i decide to choose on strength of roleplays and I felt Steven Caldera told a better story. Concentrated a bit on himslf for more of an insight of the character. Pick: Steven Caldera At this stage of the competition I had expected that quality work was a given. However the quality of the work by these two writers still impressed me. I’m giving the win to the writer whose work kept me glued to all three of his roleplays and whose work left me looking for more. I can’t wait to read his 5th round work (even if I’m not judging it, you can be sure I’ll be reading it.) Caldera’s work whilst very good, just didn’t keep me as stuck to it as Bongs, I found my mind wandering off in parts. Pick: Jimmy Bong. Appalacians
