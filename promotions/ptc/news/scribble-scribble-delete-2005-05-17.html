Several ironies spring to mind: - I had well over a dozen judges ready and waiting... whereas usually I'm never getting enough judges. *scratches head* - GTT2 and RPG4 had the judges and/or administrators fall over and abandon things when the going was good. Now we have the poster child tournament for mass falling over by players. *shakes head sadly* - We went from the single best showing tourney in PTC's history to the single worst round I have ever seen in any tournament, in the space of 10 days. *scratches head again* This is where we are at: - Pete resigned during the round. - Ryan advances due to Pete resigning. - Willow no-showed without a word so is out (and need not bother applying for GTT5 as I've seen him on the forums during the week several times). - Skylar is out also. Having thought long and hard about it, I'm not accepting that to advance. That's the way I've always worked, if your opponent resigns you are off the hook, but if he doesn't and you don't roleplay either, then its a double no-show. I don't subscribe to the post three lines of anything to pass idea. That is, if Skylar knew about the no-show and I didn't then that is bad, and if Skylar didn't know about the no-show and posted that, then its as good as a resignation. - Cereal Killer d Tsunami Oni. - Tessa Windsor d Drake Coleman. Final Round Matchup: - A.Magregor v C.Killer v T.Windsor *credit to the great Mike K. for the thread title.
