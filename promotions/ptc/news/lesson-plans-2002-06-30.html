Lesson Plans

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The beginners’ lesson plan: twenty-two graded tasks, in any order, then a final test. Task 1: Getting to Know Poser Assignment: Your task is to read the “Familarize Yourself with the Workspace -White Mexican” Tutorial and answer the following questions. List the 12 different Display OptionsExplain Box Tracking in your own words.What does Depth Queuing do?Explain to someone with no poser experience how to make the shirt Blue.How does one make the figures head exactly twice its size?How do I change it so the background is pink?Where do I find the camera controls?In your own words explain what the Smooth Shaded display is.To make the figure point straight into the camera I would use what tool?I would move the mouse in what direction to have him point at me? Task 2: Conforming Clothes The assignment has not been preserved. Task 3: Adding Hair Assignment: Your task is to submit a P4 image that has hair added to it’s head. A front, side and back image must be included. When submitting make sure to explain how you got the hair on the figure in your own words. Task 4: Lighting The assignment has not been preserved. Task 5: Adding Textures Assignment: Your task is to submit an image of a P4 figure with a skin texture applied. He is not to wear any clothes and please turn genitalia off. When submitting make sure to explain how you added the textures in your own words. Task 6: Importing a Background Assignment: Your task is to successfully import a background of your making into poser and render an image. The figures in the image must be P4 and the background must have your posting name on it somewhere. When submitting make sure to explain how you were able to add the background in your own words. Task 7: Import a .3ds file or .obj file Assignment: Your task is to successfully import a .3ds or .obj file into poser and create a unique image. When submitting make sure to explain how you imported the file in your own words. Task 8: Parenting a Prop to a figure Assignment: Your task is to create a series of four images showing that you have parented a prop. You should use the animation option and render as images instead of a movie. You may parent any prop as long as you can prove that it will stay on the figure when moved. An easy place to show that is in the hands. Task 9: Adding Tattoos Assignment: Your task is to create an image that shows a tattoo added to a P4 figure. You must also include a clipping from the skin texture where the tattoo was added. Do not post the whole skin texture. Make sure to explain how you added the tattoo in your post. Task 10: Loading Morph Targets Assignment: Your task is to create a P4 image that shows a morph has been applied. You are free to choose what morph but make sure to explain what you did to add the morph in your own words. Task 11: Create a Shirt texture from scratch The assignment has not been preserved. Task 12: Create a unique standing pose Assignment: Your task to take a P4 figure and pose him in a unique way. Do not use a preset pose you won’t learn anything that way. Explain to us what method you used to pose the figure and how you went about doing it. Task 13: Adding Scars The assignment has not been preserved. Task 14: Adding a second character Assignment: Your task is to create an image showing two P4 characters in the same image. Explain how you got the second character in the scene and how you made the image. Task 15: Post Work Basics Assignment: Your task is to take this image and touch it up to look as closely related to this one as possible. Make sure to explain in detail the steps you took to make the image. IMAGES WILL BE ADDED SHORTLY Task 16: Post Work Blood Assignment: Your task is to take an image of a P4 figure and add blood to it. Explain in detail what you did to make the image. Task 17: Enhancing Hair with Post Work Assignment: Your task is to take an image and make the hair look more realistic. Explain in detail what you did and why you did it. Also make sure to post the original and then the post work. Task 18: Create a Wrestling Pose with two figures Assignment: Your task is to create a wrestling scene with two figures. Make sure you include shots from at least two angles and explain how you made the poses. Task 19: Add Tights Assignment: Your task is to edit a body texture and make a pair of tights for a P4 figure. Make sure to show a front, back and side view of the figure showing that there is no seems. Explain how you went about making the tights. Task 20: Post Work Lighting Assignment: Your task is to take an image and enhance the lighting. You may enhance it in whatever way you feel fit but make sure to post the original and the enhanced version as well as explaining what you did and why. Task 21: Create a Group Image Assignment: Your task is to create a group image of four or more figures. You may create the image anyway you feel fit as long as four figures are seen. Explain how you made the image and why. Task 22: Create a unique image combining seven or more of the previous tasks The assignment has not been preserved. Final Test When you have all the tasks complete you will be contacted and a time for your final test will be scheduled. It will be a combination of a written and graphical test.
