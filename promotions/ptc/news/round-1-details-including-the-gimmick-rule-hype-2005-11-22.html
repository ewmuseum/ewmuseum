Round 1 Details, including The Gimmick Rule: Hype

22 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is pretty much a transcript from the radio show, with a few nuances added: - Round 1 has a 1 RP limit, no word limit. - No theme words being handed out for use, as was with GTT4. Last year for GTT4, I did all the hyping. Almasy particularly copped it ad nauseum. This year, you are going to do it. How that will works is: - Before Roleplaying starts there will be a five day "hype period". The roleplay period will be five days after that (10 days total). - During this five days, you must post from a minimum of one hype on the GTT5 site to a full maximum of five, but only one per day. - Hypes will have a word limit - 1000 words (a couple over is okay, but not many). - The purpose of posting these hypes is to get your character over, BAMN. You can use written words, you can post images (you need to provide hosting for them, and you need to make sure they don't break my site layout!). - For content, you can trash talk, conduct media interviews, post weblogs, hype the match, hype your character, have a mock debate (co-operative with your opponent)... basically anything you can think of that will make your character get noticed and can be posted on the site (usual no-porn rules apply :D ). - The hype post does not have to directly relate to the RP (but smart hype will add value to the RP). - Judges will be encouraged to read the hypes... your hypes will be linked at the top of your RP's once they are posted. Officially, the hype is to be considered "Support Material" - i.e. non-essential reading. The trick is, the better you do, the more people will talk about your work on the boards, the more people are aware of your presence in GTT5. - You may consider five to be too much, you may consider one to be not enough... how many hypes you do up to your limit of five is a gamble you take. Judges may not read all five if you do five uninteresting ones. Remember, this is to provide a bit of fun that is something unique to this tournament. If you consider yourself any sort of writer, then doing this should be a piece of cake. If you find it difficult, then its probably good that you are challenged so early, because later rounds will be far more challenging than this with just the RP contests. From my point, it will also filter out people who don't really want to be here and signed up with their eyes bigger than their typing fingers. People who do want to be here will work harder, and make it work in their favor. Questions? Hypes are posted on the GTT5 site, much the same mechanics as posting a roleplay. If you are in so many feds and such a small thing as this is an issue (small as in 1000 words = 1 page), then is signing up in a tournament the right thing for you to be doing? (read between the lines there - one of the purposes for this is to weed such people out early on instead of having semi final no-shows because people are too busy in their four home feds all of a sudden... kapishke?) Why do you care what happens to other people? This is every person for themself ;) The actual amount of work involved will be not much different to Round 2, which will be a 2 RP limit, give or take a few hundred words if someone maxes out their full five. If 1 RP in 5 days is pushing it, then what would 2 in 7 or 8 be like? You can use this to give some background on your character that otherwise wouldn't make sense in the RP. A short story on one element of how he came to be, that sort of thing. Plenty of scope. Hype period will start this Friday night US time, and run for ~5 days... upon which the RP period will immediately start after that, which will also go for ~5 days. 10 days total, you have to do something in both spans of 5 days. I've had a couple of PM's / Emails with queries on whether certain things are allowed for Hype... The main thing is, if it isn't illegal and won't get me thrown off the server or break the GTT5 site layout, then pretty much anything is possible, if you stick to within the 1000 word limit. It is only a one round thing. Unless when Round 1 is over people love it and want it to continue... and then if it were to do so it would not add to the projected workload. Up to 1000... although if people post 1 word just to qualify it'll probably not count and disappear (see: CW2 Semis.)
