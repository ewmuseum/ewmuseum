Dr. Curiosity d Muppet Man (3 votes to 0) to advance Sebastian Moraco d Elia Smith (2 votes to 1) to advance Bracket final matchup: Dr. Curiosity vs Sebastian Moraco Judging Comments Curiosity v Muppet Man Muppet Man: Muppet Man’s Rps were well written with very few spelling mistakes. I really enjoyed the comedy that was infused into the Rps. It was a nice change of pace from some of the more serious styled Rps that I have read Dr. Curiosity: Another great set of Rps. Dr. Curiosity had a perfect blend of humor and story which made his Rps an extremely enjoyable read. Something special is going to be needed to top these. Pick: Dr. Curiosity, Muppet man gave it his all and had nothing to be ashamed of with his efforts in this match. In the end though, Dr. Curiosity just came out on top with a better story. The Muppet man showed us a person with the most miserable of luck, someone struggling with self doubt, and a person who has learned to set aside anger. The Muppet man is trying to live, trying to find happiness and preserve it when he finds it, and having a hell of a time of it. While the slapstick, particularly with the title belt was quite funny, it wasn’t laugh out loud funny. The Doctor showed us someone who was experiencing remarkably similar things, a desperate struggle for happiness and a trunk full of baggage complicating things. But the Doctor is doing something about it, and it really seems to make the character shine. The events, antics, and snappy one liners are used to deliver a good deal of laughter. The Doctor’s behavior with his wife, what with pretending to be seduced and deliberately shoving his ex down the stone steps, is positively devious enough to almost be evil. Comparing the characters side by side, the Muppet Man seems like someone more worthy of respect, but the Doctor resonates with the vibe of the ruthless cheater, someone who takes advantage of all the circumstances available and comes out ahead. Both sets of work were a great deal of fun, but I had more fun with Doctor Curiosity. Pick: Doctor Curiosity. The Chronicles of Muppet VI – Insurmountable- A good RP with a lot of description and content, the whole thing flowed quite nicely. This RP was great, never knew how absent minded the doctor was…………loved the Disney Device. What Lies Beneath- Some of the funniest stuff I have ever read………… All three flowed together quite nicely and he also had mention of his opponent in GTT4. Great Stuff.
