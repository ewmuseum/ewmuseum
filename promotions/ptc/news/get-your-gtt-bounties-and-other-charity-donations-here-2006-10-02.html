Cash Prizes - Charity Donations: 1. $50.00 donation to a charity to be nominated by whoever knocks out Dr. Curiosity. (Anonymous) - offered before we knew Andy signed up :P 2. $25.00 donation to The United Way on behalf of whoever knocks out Seymour Almasy. (Dean) 3. $10.00 to the charity of his choice for each round that Sean progresses with Seymour Almasy (Mat W.). 4. $25.00 donation to The United Way on behalf of whoever wins GTT6. (Dean) 5. $50.00 to the charity of the choice of the person who drew the eventual winner in their bracket on PTC Radio (me). 6. $50.00 to the charity of the choice of the person that reaches furthest who has never been in a GTT before. (Jamie Fletcher) Non-Cash Prizes - Handler can win - A TEAM Championship of Champions Shot for the winner of GTT6 (transferrable down to the 6th place finisher) - Tom H. - A guaranteed spot for both the winner of GTT6 and the runner-up in the 2007 TEAM Tournament of Champions (Non-transferrable) - Tom H. - A guaranteed shot for the TEAM Challenge Championship (details coming soon!) for the runner-up of GTT6 on the turf of their home fed! (transferrable to 12th place) - Tom H. - If the man who takes home the bacon is from the greatest city in the world...Chicago, IL, USA. I will buy them a Moo & Oink hat, Moo & Oink BBQ sprinkle, and a pound of Moo & Oink frozen chitlins. (Al L.) - The Sean W. "White & Nerdy" Bounty: In the vein of Al L.'s unclaimed bounty last year, I will put a tangible item on the line. He or she who claims the bounty shall recieve a new copy of Final Fantasy X. Should they have this, a copy of Final Fantasy X-2, Xenosaga I, Kingdom Hearts I, or Star Ocean: Till the End of Time can be worked out. Should you be a non-RPG playing Philistine, I SUPPOSE I can get you an ROH DVD... How to get the bounty, you ask? Two ways: 1.) If Kimbusa defeats Seymour Almasy in round one, beat that sumo handling bastard Ben like he owes you money. 2.) If Almasy beats Kimbusa, the bounty will go on Almasy's head.
