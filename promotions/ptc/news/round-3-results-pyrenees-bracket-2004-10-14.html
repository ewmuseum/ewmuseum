Round 3 Results: Pyrenees Bracket

14 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dallas Reeves d Unstoppable Eugene~! (2 votes to 0) to advance. Dave Harley d Salaok (2 votes to 1) to advance. Tarrant advanced. Blaise "Hazard" McClancy d John Adams (2 votes to 1) to advance. Round 4 matches: Dallas Reeves vs Dave Harley Blaise McClancy vs Tarrant Judges Comments Dallas Reeves v Unstoppable Eugene~! Reeves had this match won after his first rp, the fact that he posted a second only proved it. Eugene's humor was ok but got annoying quickly. Contrary to the beginning of his rp...it had no point. Reeves RPs were insightful to the point of the tournament and well-written throughout. He deserves this victory. Pick: Reeves Unstoppable Eugene: Liked your work. Good solid roleplay. Dallas Reeves: I enjoyed both your roleplays, very sound and solid all the way through. I also went to high school with a guy with the same name. Though that has nothing to do with my decision. Pick: Dallas Reeves Salaok v Dave Harley me down. With the twist ending that Harley pulled, it made me feel as though everything I had read up until that point was just filler. Salaok: Salaok did a great job of with his two Rps by having a nice blend of story and working mention of his opponent into it. I can say that I really did enjoy these two Rps and will be following his work next round as well. The Result: I’m choosing Salaok here. While Dave Harley’s RP wasn’t a bad read, it can’t compete with Salaok’s ability to introduce me to his character and let me know why he’s in the GTT. Pick: Salaok Dave Harley - Excellent RP with a rather crappy ending. The creative writing -v- trash talk theme in the RP was nice and put well with tongue-in-cheek the whole time, but somehow it felt like two or three RPs at once ... a serious and creative narration RP, one of the best I've read lately that still focuses entirely on wrestling, which read great ... mocking the very style he was using, which might have been the entire point but only seemed to poke it's head out of the first in a couple of places, with the 'Scooby-Doo Murder Mystery' twist and the end ... and a final mocking of anything and everything with an ending that made little sense but still made me smile. I'm not usually a fan of the naration/story-telling, but Harley did it really well and used wrestling in it extremely well ... but after he turned it towards a murder mystery/parody it seemed to lose a lot of steam, and the end of the 'story' was poorly written even if he did save it with the very end. Still a really entertaining RP, though. Saloak #1: Nice narration, but not a particularly entertaining RP. Not much of a story at all to keep me reading, just a long series of Saloak's feelings and motivations that just ended up blending together. I can't say anything particularly interested me. Well written, certainly, but not entertaining at all. Saloak #2: Exponentially better than the first RP ... just far and away better and more entertaining. Blending good writing with a great look at the tournament had me hooked for the first half of it. The explanation for all of the drop-outs before the rounds had even really begun was even great, worked really well with the character, and was creative enough to make me smile. After the narration zipped back to the present it seemed to lose a bit, though, and stopped holding my attention. Still a great RP overall. Overall: Honestly, I'm just going to ignore Saloak's first RP, because I didn't enjoy it at all and it'd just end up hurting the second RP if I factored it in. Putting Harley's RP up against Saloak's first is an extremely tough decision, since I thought they were both great RPs. Harley's was entertaining throughout the entire RP, though, while there was a decent chunk of Saloak's that lost my attention enough to skip on a bit. Really close match, but I'm going with Dave Harley. Pick: Dave Harley Harley only wrote one roleplay while Salaok wrote two, generally that should give Salaok the advantage, but in this case, I’m not really sure it does. I always go for quality over quantity, and I don’t think Salaok has the quality or consistency in this case. Dave pulled out a good twist at the end, I’m giving him the win. Pick: Dave Harley Blaise McClancy v John Adams Adams took a smaller, more compact approach while McClancy used long, verbose roleplays. However, the large roleplays of McClancy were riddled with spelling errors, to the point where he misspelled his own character’s name!! Adams’ roleplays seemed more of a package deal, and though shorter, they got their points across. McClancy’s roleplays seemed long just to be long… and the lack of revision showed a lack of willingness to take that small extra step, which would have possibly one him the round. Pick: Adams McClancy told the stronger story, and was better written. Adams tended to jump around a bit, drag on in some description, and made it far less enjoyable to read. Pick: Blaise McClancy Blaise McCancy: I enjoyed the storyline and it even mixed in the aspects of what was going on currently with his opponent as well as mentioning his opponent. The flow of the rp's went very well and i enjoyed them very much. Adams: The story was good for a quick rp. Seemed to be lacking with more depth. Seemed a bit generic with the bar scene involved. Even though enjoyable for the read needed more. Pick: Blaise McClancy Pyrenees
