This needs to be done (concerns Andy and Will)

26 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Will: Tough luck for me, I s'pose. I was hoping you would be on last night to answer whether we were still doing a one day-limit, but alas, them's the breaks. It's only dissapointing because I had the RP basically finished at the deadline, I just didn't think that deadline applied to this round so I went a little longer than I would've otherwise. I realize it's a moot point, though, and I picked a bad week to get reamed by life. Good luck to Andy. Honestly, it never occurred to me that people would think it was other than normal, unless I said that it was other than normal, given how militant I've been about rules and times and things. I relaxed a bit on the notifications because by now I expected you guys to know what the score was and when things had to be done by :( Not going to happen. That type of change to the rules requires code changes to the posting script. So, you could also find and quote me saying that the script is coded to physically stop people from posting more than once on a calendar day. I would have said something if I'd done that :) Yes, I could have written it again, but I didn't think I had to, these guys have all seen several others be DQ'd posting mere seconds after the time expired, with repeated warnings about posting incomplete work rather than being late. It was a pretty big thing to take for granted. He had 10 days to ask if he wasn't sure. So did Sean, Andy and the other Andy. All famous for deadline hugging. They knew what to do, and they did it. No PM's from any of them asking for clarification.
