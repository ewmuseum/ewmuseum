PTC Survival Update 2 - June 15

14 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
14 June 2004 Date of event: August 22nd Roleplay Period Start: 12.01am MDT, August 1 Roleplay Period End: 11.59pm MDT, August 15 An event website will be provided, complete with custom Backstage Script. All players will use this to roleplay and maintain biographies. Each Handler will only be able to play in one match, this includes the survivor match. Card Main Event: PTC Global Title Defence: Elimination Match Chris Storm (RPW) vs Seymour Almasy (UWF) vs Jesse Jamester (GW) vs Global Champion No Former Global Champion in this match lost the title by being pinned or submitting. This will now be rectified. (swapping the order of the July and August GTD matches, Hessian will also be in this match if he drops the strap this week). Match under regular GTD rules, three roleplays each. PTC Survivor Match: SCAVENGER HUNT. - Each team of four is looking for a nominated item (TBA) - Working as a team they have to be first to bring it to the ring. - If any team member gets pinned or submit at any time through out the match while holding the item, their item becomes fair game for other teams - Upon any member being pinned or submitting, the entire team goes to the penalty box for five minutes. - If multiple teams are in the penalty box, any team member being pinned in the penalty box causes the entire team to be completely eliminated. - One team per fed, or feds may request permission to combine to make one team. - All PTC member feds who have signed up for this must submit a team of four players to Pete by first week of July. PTC Extreme Title Defence: - Champion, Challenger and match gimmick TBA. PTC Tag Team Title Defence: - Champions, Challengers and match gimmick TBA. Karina Wolfenden (RPW) vs Dawn McFarlane (WMW) - Last Woman Standing match. - 1 RP Limit. Additonal matches TBA. Note: If you want one, state your case to Pete and myself via PM as to why the match is worthy of being on a PTC I-PPV (i.e. don't come to us with some random match, suggest one that will be able to tell a good story based on an established need for the match to take place), and be prepared to find a match writer and three impartial judges. Match Writing and Judging assignments and match writing guidelines including commentary team members will be advised closer to the event. That is all for now. NEW MATCH ADDED! Tessa Windsor (RPW) vs. Patti Anderson (4WW) Gimmick: Regular Match - Pinfall, Submission. No DQ, no Countout. MUST BE A WINNER. RP Limit: 1RP. 15 June 2004 STIPULATION ADDED TO MAIN EVENT - the match is now an elimination match. Posted on the PTC forum by Argyle and Pete.
