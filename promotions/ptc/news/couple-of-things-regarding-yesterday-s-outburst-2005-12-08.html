Couple of things regarding yesterday's outburst

8 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- By my count there were 33 matches in Round 1 of GTT4 that had one judge. A far smaller amount had 2, and the 2 judge that got split votes had a third (more often than not, either me or the GTT5 first round draw typist o'doom, Davena). A public record of these figures exists here: http://gtt4.primetimecentral.net/index2.php or by searching the GTT4 forums in the archive down at the bottom of the forums. Therefore, the only measurable decline between this year and last year is in fact exactly 64 less people applying for a spot (or being offered a spot if they did apply). Taking a look around the game in general and comparing 15 member feds in PTC this time last year to 10 now and the volume of traffic here in general, I'm still quite chuffed with the level of interest. - I judged the Coleman vs Lexicon match. Cute cliches aside, my judging comments indicated quite clearly which parts of Lexicon's RP that I liked, and the parts that I didn't. Last time I looked, I was entitled to my subjective opinion on these things. I don't see any disrespect in there towards him. - I don't socialise with either Coleman or Lexicon, the only time I talk to either of them these days is either on these forums or in the occasional PM during tournament time. Hasn't happened so far this tournament. So... no bias there, eh? - I in no way said Coleman's was a work of art and blew Lexicons away. It was far from it. At this point in time, I'll go as far as saying that if Lexicon had stopped after Day 3, I'd probably have given him the match. - I in no way used words like "Lexicon's RP sucked balls, so does Lexicon, and while I'm at it GCW can also suck a big fat one as well for hiring a reject like him in the first place", or anything similar to that in my judging comments. - I distinctly remember bending over backwards to help out Lexicon last year when correcting a mistake I made in making new round brackets, giving him a free bye and even creating a virtual bracket for him until a playin was needed to get him back into a regular bracket. I could just as easily have told him that life sucks and to deal with my mistake. So, he hasn't done too badly at all out of things here. - I also distinctly remember Lexicon chucking a major tantrum last year after losing to Mat. Pattern? Looks like it. Now, onto the point of my post: I happen to not like the idea of having the forum filled up with such threads as this every time someone disagrees with a judging decision. It detracts from the tournament, and is why I don't reveal who the judges are, or allow them to indicate which matches they judged. If every one of them had to justify their picks down to the nth degree every time someone disagreed, I'd have trouble finding any judges. This is a competition, half the field will be wiped out by the time judging is finished. Some of the people being wiped out could clean the floor with some of those that remain in. Deal with it. I also don't like getting huge PM's from judges ranting on getting no respect from players and refusing to judge anymore, I've had several of them over the years. Therefore, your options about complaining about judges in public are slim to none. If I decide to allow a thread on discussion on the topic, you'll know about it. I had one open before GTT5 started inviting people to give advice to the judges. I'll freely admit that was more for the benefit of the players, so that they could get some understanding about the nature of subjectivity and individual people, and that there is no set formula to writing or picking a winner, because everybody is different. If people abuse this and vent or accuse or whatever, the usual procedure is to suspend them until such a time as they sack up and apologise to whoever they punked out. There's actually a very long list of people Mr. Lexicon punked out in his numerous posts on the matter, including the entire field of those playing in the tournament. Fortunately the mods removed his posts and stopped people jumping all over him. They were actually forced to suspend him when he kept going. So... if he chooses not to do what he very very deep down knows to be the right thing, I have no problem with that, as the judge he decided to bitch out was me, and I'm not going to refuse to myself to judge. Although, I have to say that after the last five days solid reading and doing nothing else, and looking at the increasing pile of dishes in the sink waiting for me, doing the dishes is gradually starting to become an attractive proposition ;) I am however not going to put up with any more of his bullshit on my forums. Or anybody elses.
