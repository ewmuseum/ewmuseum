BRACKET ONE -- NAME ANNOUNCEMENT (WITH FIRST MATCHUP)

24 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In GTT4, we settled on mountains, each member trying to climb to the top. In GTT5, we settled on mothers, the winner conquering our familial rights. In GTT6, we settled on the creatures of A.A. Milne, because fuck it, that's why. And now, in GTT7, we've decided to get a little more serious, a bit more refined and a bit more... well, whatever. He may not actually have ever existed, but, folks attribute more than 33 plays to him, as well as writing a whole bunch of sonnets during his lifetime. Without further adieu, I'd like to announce the first bracket name for GTT7: He was CONTROVERSIAL~! (LOL! LOL! LOL! LOLLLLLLL! SPLASH~!) for his time, and it's said that if you utter the name of The Scottish Play while you're in the midst of rehearsing for it, you'll release incredible bad luck. Your first bracket... BRACKET WILLIAM SHAKESPEARE! if you could imagine me saying that very Chairman Kaga style, that'd be swell. thx.
