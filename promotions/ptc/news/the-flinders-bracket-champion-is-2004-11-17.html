The Flinders Bracket champion is....

17 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kasper Sky d Shadow, 3 votes to 1. (I haven't heard from the other two judges I had lined up so I'm not going to wait anymore as the result is determined.) Kasper Sky advances to the Semi finals to face the winner of Hurst v Deville. Judges Comments Kasper Sky vs. Shadow For the sake of this match and getting this done, I'll review it as an overall. This was a 2 (Sky) to 3 (Shadow) Rping match for the record. Kasper Sky: 2 rp's, that were great. Very good detail and characters. Another person who's showing why they got this far already in the tourney. Only notes that I can put here are the ones that everyone else seems to get in there. Why put To be continued on a tourney rp, when it may not be continued if you don't continue on to the next round? And of course the violence in the rp's. But for this one I can forget/forgive the death and that with the entire basis for the character and what the story is about. Overall: Very good, and potentially one that would show that 2 rp's can beat 3 if done right. We'll see. Shadow: Okay, I'll admit it, never read any of this Shadow Character. The name kept me away until I was asked to read it to judge it. And now I kick myself in my backside repeatedly. I say this upon reading the word Ravnos and getting locked into this whole series. Yes, I know what that is and what the rest of it pertains to. I'm marking out severly for the tourney. We've had one who followed the Cthulu Mythos in his rp's and now we have one that is as much a fan of V:TM as I am. However, this could be a disavantage if I were to find that she didn't do it justice. That was not the case. Very well written in the style for the game. And it made me want to go to WMW when I get the time and read some of her other work. How anyone can call her an underdog, I don't know. Overall: Very nice. I enjoyed reading this series. Final Decision: 2 rp's to 3. All very well done. But, 2 very good rp's don't beat 3 very good rp's. If Kasper had mustered up a 3rd one he might have been able to pull it through in this case. But while we've had the argument before, I don't think his two beat her three. And I'm also very admant that if you make it this far in a tourney, and you're wanting to win, you don't cut yourself short on either quality, or quantity. Shadow, with an “upset”. Pick: Shadow Some really mind blowing stuff, both deserve to be here by the quality pieces produced. However, my pick goes with Sky because I found myself enthralled in the story, awaiting the next chapter. Shadow wrote 3, but Sky's hooked me in more which is why I have to give him the win. Pick: Kasper Sky Kasper Sky RP 1 – A nice story with a lot of description, but also a lot of relevance (mostly a pep talk to himself). A really unusual roleplay, but it worked and worked well. Very original concept in my opinion. The character development was good. You could also clearly see the mind set Kasper was in. Good job, Kasper Sky. 9.0/10 Kasper Sky RP 2 – The same style roleplay although it was a lot shorter than the first roleplay. It was once again a good roleplay. I think you had a third roleplay planned, because the story was not finished yet. 8.5/10 Shadow RP 1 – Very intriguing story. It was great to read that Shadow was still thinking about WMW. I got a great insight in the mind of Shadow, which is also great. Shadow really proved why she is one of the best in GTT 4, in my opinion. 8.0/10 Shadow RP 2 – No mention at all about her match or wrestling in general. The continuation of the story was good, but not great. Overall, it’s not as good as the first one. Still, it’s really good. But not exceptional. 7.5/10 Shadow RP 3 - Luckily, I saw some relevance again. This third roleplay was by far the best of the three. Good writing and a good story make this one of the best roleplays of the tournament, if not out there, 8.5/10 Two overall better roleplays from Kasper Sky against three roleplays from a hard working Shadow. This really is a dilemma for me as a judge. I really doubted a long time about this, but I decided to give my vote to Kasper Sky. His original style of roleplaying was the deciding factor for me. Your effort is much appreciated though, Shadow. Pick – Kasper Sky (Very, very close) Shadow “Everything I Touch, I Break” This is a very good RP that extends from the past involvement in the GTT. Works with has happened in the past and in someways points to the future. There is some great description here and it goes very indepth into Shadow’s emotions so we know how she feels. My one negative is that this RP is long, almost too long in that it just seems to go on and left me wondering where exactly we are going. It was good but the length took something away for me. “Until Dawn” This was another good RP from Shadow. It was a good read that flipped back and forth between two stories then built up to the end scene where they are fleeing the estate. Once they escaped, it concluded nicely as it set up for the future as they continue to flee from the Den. The next phase in the relationship between Gideon and Shadow occurred as they kissed. Still, I did however find it a bit long for my liking but I was able to remain more focused on this RP than the first one. Excellent work nonetheless. “Mystery of the Whisper” This RP started where the last one ended having The Den find that they had escaped the Estate and realizing that they didn’t do the job. The RP flashes over to Norway where Shadow and Gideon have decided to hide for the time being. Here she is able to refocus back on the GTT and back on her opponent, Kasper Sky. It was nice to see that she was able to put so much focus on the tournament in this last RP. She had some good description and a good storyline here as well with her inner thoughts and inner debate. Another solid RP from Shadow. Kasper Sky “Eternal: The End (Bracket Finals RP 1)” This is a really interesting RP and flows much like the previous Kasper Sky RPs. I really enjoyed reading this one as it furthered the storyline of Kasper and his past. I find the twists and turns in his story keep me wanting more and it was the same in this case where it was found he killed Athena’s dad. He leaves it with me wanting more in the end. “Eternal: The End (Bracket Finals RP 2)” This 2nd RP from Kasper Sky was another solid RP. I really enjoyed it as the story of his unknown past becomes more and more twisted. He is able to work in the GTT here and remember that he is in the tournament, much like the first one where he puts in a section as well about it. But the best part of this is the storytelling and the way he goes about it. I really enjoyed it. Conclusion: This is a really, really tough call to make. I can make cases for either of them winning and also make cases for each of them losing. Shadow was great in her description and story and getting across the emotions of Shadow, however, at times I thought it was a bit longwinded and found myself wondering when it was going to end. I have nothing against long RPs but I just couldn’t keep my interest in them at times. While Shadow’s were long, Kasper Sky’s were shorter. However they were short and sweet and to the point. He was able to progress his storyline very nicely, keeping with the twists that he has done throughout the tournament. Good use of the language and description throughout his 2 RPs. But the problem is that he only did 2 RPs. I so wish that he had done a third. Still at this point in time when I’m writing this up and trying to decide who to pick I am going to go with Kasper Sky. I have a feeling I’ll be in the minority here but in the end I really enjoyed Kasper Sky’s RPs this round much more. Yes, he only did 2 while Shadow did 3 but the rules only state a minimum of 2 and a max of 3. It’s a tough call to make but in the end I was left wanting to read the next one from Kasper Sky more than Shadow. To reiterate, I’m picking Kasper Sky. Pick: Kasper Sky Flinders
