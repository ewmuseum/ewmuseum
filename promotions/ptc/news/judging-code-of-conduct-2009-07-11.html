Judging "Code of Conduct"

11 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Previously, the judging has been almost a "come as you are" proposition, based on the fact that people are volunteering to do it, and it takes an assload of time and effort for the volumes we are talking about here. I'd like to have things a little more structured this time around, and am here for feedback before we set things in stone. Some of your more coherent and sensible statements may end up becoming rules... so treat this seriously. [name withheld]'s golden rule: Remember that GTT is a tournament and not RP school so should not be treated as a one stop learning shop... you are playing to compete... On with the questions: Players This is your chance to say what you want out of GTT judging. It may or may not make a real difference, but no harm no foul. Q. What is the minimum amount of feedback from a judge that you would expect to see to justify their decision? Q. Forgetting the concept of minimum, what do you personally actually want in an ideal world? Q. Do you usually judge when you have been eliminated from a tournament? Why/why not? Q. Do you want to know who your judges are when their comments are posted? Q. SHOULD you know who your judges are when their comments are posted? Q. Should other current players be allowed to judge your match? Caveats? Q. Anything else you want to say? Previous Judges This is your chance to educate people on how much effort it actually takes to judge a match and what it means to give feedback to a certain level. Q. How much time/effort do you put into judging on average? Think 1 on 1 match with 2 RP's each. Q. What sort of feedback do you give? (what's your style?) Q. What is the maximum effort that you are prepared to put into judging? Q. Should your identity be revealed or kept quiet? Q. Anything else you want to say? Scoring Systems There was a thread in the GTT4 forum on a match being decided after the judge averaged a score between 2 RP's vs the score from 1 RP. At the time, "Come as you are" rules dictated that this was okay if the judge decided to do that. This is your chance to provide feedback on such things in a way that will help formulate some official rules/guidelines. Q. Thoughts on different types of scoring systems? Basically whatever comes into your head when I say "scoring system". Q. True/false. Scoring systems should not be allowed. Q. True/false. Scoring is okay, but averaging should not be allowed. Anything else that you think is relevant, shoot. We want something relatively open and transparent that people will have faith in and not undermine if things don't go their way. You can help form that by responding.
