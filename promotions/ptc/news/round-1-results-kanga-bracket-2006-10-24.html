Round 1 Results - Kanga Bracket

24 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DANIELLE NORTHSKY V SSL Judge 1 SSL This piece was very average for me. By the end, I was actually kind of happy it was done because I wasn't compelled by the overall story. I think you can write, but the story just wasn't compelling. SSL is crazy, and I think there's a ton of potential there, but I don't care about the doctor looking to become rich off of using SSL for his own greed. Nothing made me want to read further. Danielle Northsky I was hoping for more and I got a piece that was on about the same level as SSL. Technical wise, there are some errors here that make the entire piece feel a little clunky. I thought Danielle's story was alright, but it didn't grab me like I thought it would. I like the idea of an Indian heritage, however, the story just kind of left me kind of blah, and I was hoping for something that would make me want to read more into it. I think the writing is good and it has potential, but the overall piece is average. This was a weird decision because neither piece grabbed me. In the end, I chose Danielle Northsky as her story was a little more interesting then SSL's. Pick: Danielle Northsky Judge 2 Danielle Northsky: I found the first portion of the promo, which gave an overview of Danielle's wrestling history and alliegence's, to be overlong and perhaps unnessessary. It was slow getting to know the main characters, but they were well portrayed and interesting dynamics. The second half of this promo was the stronger and makes me think it could develop into a good story. SSL: Well written and I very much enjoyed Dr. Sorrow's character. However there simply wasn't enough insight into SSL himself. In future tournaments a deeper introduction of the main character could see you go further. While I really liked much of SSL's promo, I feel Danielle Northsky's was the stronger of the two. Pick: Danielle Northsky. Judge 3 DN: The roleplay took some getting into but once I had gotten into the main chunk of the roleplay I was very impressed. A great character was portrayed in this roleplay which did a lot to establish DN to the reader. SSL: This roleplay followed the similar pattern to his opposition. However this roleplay was a lot more systematic and easily broken down into sections. I was disappointed however with how rushed it felt and how little attention to detail the roleplay showed. Pick: Danielle Northsky SSL just didn't hit the note at times and his roleplay lacked in the detail and research that DN undertook so perfectly. DN also displayed a much higher level of knowledge in her character. Result Danielle Northsky d SSL, 3 votes to 0 HOYT WILLIAMS V JOSHUA KOSIDLO Judge 1 Joshua Kosidlo Overall, technically sound piece. The story itself was solid, and I felt like it was a solid intro. Nothing spectacular, but it got the job done. Enjoyed the very beginning with the letter to Hoyt. Hoyt Williams The biggest problem with this piece are the glaring errors littered throughout the piece. It distracts from the piece when you've got misspelt words, using me instead of I, and using the wrong word (to should be too in this piece). The story though is a good one, and made me laugh throughout. I liked the ending as you don't know which road will lead to where the baby is. Solid. This one is not an easy pick to make. With Kosidlo, his piece is technically sound with a solid story to go around it. With Williams, you have a somewhat technically sound piece with a good story to go around it. Where do you lay the points down on, the story, the technicallity, or the overall piece? With this one, I have to go with Joshua Kosidlo because of the fact that his was concise while Williams was a little more all over the place. While humorous, it did take a way somewhat from the overall story. Pick: Joshua Kosidlo Judge 2 Joshua Kosidlo: I enjoyed the air of mystery surrounding Joshua and the hints of things to come with the Mission and his lycanthropy. Dialogue was very realistic and the promo flowed well, except for one or two minor inconsistencies. Despite the story being mysterious, you still had everything in context through references to your opponent. Hoyt Williams: It was written from the point of view of Hoyt's father and while he's a great character, I didn't really get a good sense of Hoyt himself. This counts against you I'm afraid as the person in this tournament is Hoyt. Funny and well written for the first portion. However it seemed to lose focus some point halfway through and meandered through unnecessary banter and randomness until the last few paragraphs about the kidnapping. I was left with very little sense of what's going on. I enjoyued portions of it and almost wished the rest of it would be as good. This is a very tough decision as both had strong elements and were good jumping off points for a tounament such as this. However, Hoyt lost me halfway through and was unable to get me back whereas Joshua kept me interested and wanting more all the way through. Pick: Joshua Kosidlo Result Joshua Kosidlo d Hoyt Williams, 2 votes to 0 SEYMOUR ALMASY V KIMBUSA - PTC GLOBAL TITLE MATCHUP Judge 1 There's no question that both these pieces were written well. Even though the plot progressions shared similar elements, the coincidences didn't detract from my readings of them and they left a strong impression regardless. In the end, however, Kimbusa's work was much more engaging. The character motivations and desires were crafted more effectively without becoming too prosaic and the supporting characters were just written better. Pick: Kimbusa Judge 2 Kimbusa. Along with story content, the characters' depth carried this through. Pick: Kimbusa Judge 3 For all that was riding on this, I have to say point blank that I felt let down by both writers, and under the circumstances I refuse to be nice about it and blow smoke up your asses. Both of you are old enough to take what I'm about to say without crying like pubescent girls who had their barbie dolls burned with a magnifying glass by their mean older brother, so I'm just going to say it... while trying not to grandstand - which I believe is the true intent of the new judging rules and their limitations. Kimbusa's tale of depression and finding his inner anger/motivation failed to get any sort of rise out of me from start to finish. All the description and adjectives in the world couldn't hide the bland and frequently used plotline. I have to say that its almost like the plot too closely followed reality with respect to the writers muse, but didn't match the return of it in the end. I've seen far, far better from Kimbusa, so I know its in there somewhere. Almasy, for someone wanting to go out with a bang, looked like doing just that for the first half, as I was quite drawn in, but shortly after he got tossed out of the GCW building, it returned to the same old same old cliche'd bleh that you can find in the work of any other player not truly committed to advancing or believing in themself enough to come up with something unique and inspiring, minus the blatant spelling mistakes and bad grammar. Same, I've seen far, far better from Almasy. Pick: Almasy, because I was interested for the first half. Maybe I expect too much, and maybe I shouldn't expect from you the level of work you both put out in GTT5 against each other and others, but dammit guys... this was one of those once in a lifetime money shot matchups that so few people get to partake in :( Result - Kimbusa d Seymour Almasy, 2 votes to 1 SHADOW v PURPLE HAZE Judge 1 Purple Haze v Shadow Purple Haze- A very well written comedic roleplay which served a great purpose, following an easily readable pattern. Purple Haze seems like a well thought out character smartly introduced to get around PTC rules. Shadow- This was a very well written roleplay and was extremely well detailed with an excellent flow. The story that was being conveyed could be followed without much hassle. However there was isolated periods where the roleplay became wooden. Judgment In favor of Purple Haze, had me laughing the whole time. Was a very good comedic roleplay. Shadow's roleplay was a good dramatic piece but Purple Haze displayed unparalleled skills in knowing his genre, that is not to take anything away from Shadow who put up the closest of fights and would be a shoe in, for a play in. Judge 2 Killer first round match. Haze's piece started off kinda slow, but things picked up and turned hilarious with the appearance of Mercury. The Q&A with the guy in the van was pure gold. In fact, maybe it's just because I'm juvenile, but everything to do with Mercury was gold. The THC for blood bit, too. Shadow provided some of the finest writing I've read in this game, with divine (almost Mercury-like) imagery. It suffered from a lack of tangible progress, and at times the pity-party was a bit much, but the theme was brilliantly executed. Different methods, equal merit... Essentially, my decision comes down to the fact that I enjoyed Haze's piece more, until Haze actually showed up. The idea behind the character rules, but the presentation was severely lacking, especially the closing speech (reverse psychology?). Haze left me feeling hosed. Mainly because of that, though not to take away from Shadow's fantastic effort, I choose Shadow to win. But... Just for the record, I'm pretty sure Kenny Mercury would've taken it. Pick: Shadow Judge 3 Purple Haze: Too light. Written when stoned perhaps? Funny moments, for example the "Pinter-eque" comedy of the repo men silently removing all the furnature including the chairs. Shadow: A little too over-written in parts, thus becoming a bit stiff, but the better crafted of the two. Pick: Shadow Result Shadow d Purple Haze, 2 votes to 1 SUMMARY - Danielle Northsky d SSL, 3 votes to 0 - Joshua Kosidlo d Hoyt Williams, 2 votes to 0 - Kimbusa d Seymour Almasy, 2 votes to 1. New PTC Global Champion. - Shadow d Purple Haze, 2 votes to 1. - 'The Fallen Angel' Angelica Dominique d Alizé via forfeit - Brian Arson d Vivica J. Valentine via forfeit - Peter Lenex missed his flight and Tessa Windsor's taxi from the airport got a flat tyre.
