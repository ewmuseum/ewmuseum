The Pyrenees Bracket Champion is....

16 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tarrant d Dave Harley, 4 votes to 1. Tarrant now advances to the GTT4 Semi Finals face the winner of Craig Goat and Jimmy Bong in the Appalacians. Dave Harley – “False Idol” Normally I don’t worry too much about quantity in a roleplay, but this needed more to it. This was by no means a bad roleplay, but I will touch on a few problems I had with it. First, it wasn’t presented well. I don’t knock off points for aesthetics, but I might suggest a slight tweaking of the layout. The use of brackets to frame the narration is distracting; you don’t need to frame the non-speaking parts, just leave them alone. Also, there were a few slight word editing errors, which also detracts from the roleplay. In terms of content, the roleplay alluded to WWA storylines too much. I have no problem with using your character’s exploits elsewhere to frame who he is, but we really weren’t given anything new to work with. I’ll be honest, I haven’t read much of your work previously, so all I really know about Harley is what I get in this first RP. You by no means have to start from scratch every round to explain to a new reader who you are. But I don’t know much about what you’re doing in this tournament. All we were given was a few lines of Harley standing at an altar preaching his own greatness. In fact, there was never any mention of an audience in the church; I got the impression he was speaking to an empty room, or maybe just to the organ player. I’d suggest a little more description to start off the roleplay. Start off slower, give yourself time to set up a scene. There needs to be more motion, too. Harley just stood there the entire time. There’s nothing wrong with him preaching from the book of Harley, but a good speaker is dynamic. The space between the dialogue was primarily used to put Harley over further rather than give him life. The RP had some strengths. Here’s a character I found myself curious about, only to find the RP had ended before it got started. I liked the fact that he has an entire book dedicated to his own greatness. You simply need to accentuate everything that’s going on. You should be less concerned with putting Harley over and more concerned with his dynamic motives. Harley needs some depth. He needs to do something more than just stand and talk about how he’s going to win his match. I don’t want to simply be told what Harley is thinking, I want to see it. I want to see him doing things, interacting with people and his environment. Overall, this is a good core, but it needs something extra. “Over For Dinner” An improvement over the first one, for sure. I liked what you did with the setup of the roleplay. We got some insight into a character, we got some interaction between characters and settings. Unfortunately, I felt deprived again by the end. A great setup was cut prematurely short in the end. Ultimately I liked what was said, but there just simply wasn’t enough of it. This time around I got to know a few things about Harley. I like what I saw; it wasn’t a biopic of Harley’s life, but there were some little details thrown in to give us an idea of who we’re reading about. Also, I did find myself sympathizing with his secretary a bit. In the end, this series suffers from lack of quantity; again, volume of content isn’t a huge priority, but when you have something going you need to go with it and give us a whole scene, rather than a nice setup to a few lines of payoff. A well written roleplay, but this series could have greatly benefited from a third segment. Tarrant- “Fresh off the Press” This roleplay opens with a nice little explanation of who Tarrant is. I like that he set up this series with an overview of who he is and where he’s going. The roleplay then evolves into a pretty good insight into Tarrant’s mind. The first person narration is unique and well used. I find many times people who use first person lose a bit of honesty, but you made good use of it. The roleplay was well laid out, the format was pleasing to look at and easy to read. I noticed one or two misuses of words, but overall it seemed well done. My only complaint about it is simply a lack of real action. There was some good conflict between Tarrant and his wife, and the article he reads adds to the interest. But overall, he doesn’t do much. As this is only the first roleplay in the series, there’s time to make up for it, but it would be nice to open up the scene a bit. I guess what I’m getting at is there needed to be a few more lines written to explain his surroundings. The action itself was well written, but I had a hard time visualizing him in motion because I didn’t know anything about his surroundings. “A Trip to the Doctor’s” Another well written roleplay. I liked the layout, the structure at the beginning of the roleplay was well conceived, if not perfectly executed. However, I found myself losing a bit of interest in this one. I found Tarrant’s predicament amusing, and at one point did indeed laugh out loud, but when I realized it wasn’t going anywhere I lost interest. Again, well laid out but lacking real description. I like to know what’s around a character. I know it’s tedious, but I really need to be able to see the doctor’s office in my head to feel like Tarrant’s alive. This roleplay didn’t give me much. It was well written, don’t get me wrong. But it didn’t give me anything new; I didn’t get anything new from Tarrant, other than he’s a nice guy that cares about his fans. Unfortunately, I feel this roleplay did more to sell Tarrant as a weathered middle age man in need of an enema instead of a feared, talented wrestler. I can appreciate the honesty in this, however, and the fact that he’s so human is a plus. However, I find it hard to believe he’d really be able to go a distance in a tournament filled with hungry twenty three year old wrestlers. At this point in the series, the roleplays are well written but in need of a jolt. I have a feeling this roleplay is really just setting up the third, which is fine, but if you don’t plan on really giving us something new then you should just lump the transition and the payoff into one bigger roleplay. “An Uncertain Future” Yeah, I thought this might happen. This roleplay is the best of the three, and the bombshell of this roleplay was well written. Again I find myself bothered by a lack of description, but ultimately Tarrant’s predicament draws my attention away from it. I didn’t feel like I got to see enough of how Tarrant handles this news; I would have loved to follow him to the gym and hear what he’s thinking. That would have really put us over the top with it. To see how Tarrant plans to come to terms with his diagnosis and still prepare himself for his match would have been a great touch, but instead I find myself wondering what he’s thinking. Perhaps that was intended, but I’d still rather see his problem play out a bit more before the match. Despite the loss of this opportunity, I think this series was well written. I’m giving the overall nod to Tarrant. Harley’s roleplays were good, and there’s no doubt he earned his place in the bracket finals. But Tarrant’s roleplays simply gave me more. I feel like I got to know Tarrant better, I know what kind of guy he is. Were I a betting man, looking at the cocky Harley versus the rickety old Tarrant, Harley would be the smart money. Of course, that’s not what this is about, but I would suggest to Tarrant that we get to see some of his strengths come to light in the face of his weakness. A good showing from Harley, and good luck to Tarrant. PICK: Tarrant False Idol- Was a good RP, had a lot of talk about his Experiences with GTT4, but could have used more description of Harleytarian Church. Over For Dinner- Good RP, had a lot more description in there especially the part about the mayonnaise, great RP it separated itself from the previous one but was a great read. Fresh off the Press- There was more talk about Blaise McClancy then Harley but all in all a good read. A Trip to the Doctors- A little more talk on Harley and nice transition from the last RP. An Uncertain Future. – A good ending it was really heart felt. And a lot more on his opponent Harley, really a good read. Pick- Tarrant To begin with, Harley did two RPs to Tarrant's three. Generally, I find that someone doing less than the minimum's going to have less of a shot, but we'll have to read the RPs to see, now won't we. Harley's work was solid, if unspectacular, and a little short as well. I thought the first of the two was superior, and in the second, the ancedote between him and Jill about consuming her was amusing as well. Harley also has a tight focus on what he's doing. This has helped him in the tournament defeat people who are somewhat, shall we say, less focused on the proceedings in their work. Now, onto Tarrant. Tarrant has the advantage in quantity, with three Rps. I like the diary beginnings to each RP, but think it would have almost worked better if they comprised one RP. To me, the diaries were better writing than the rest of the pieces. Tarrant's work also has grammatical and punctuational lapses in odd places, which usually isn't a big deal, but in a close match, can cost you. To his credit, he does also focus on the tournament. Both men benefitted from a bracket without any of the "big names", and the winner will definitely have to step up in order to defeat Jimmy Bong or Craig Goat. But with that said, the 3-2 RP edge coupled with Tarrant's style and storytelling ability is too much for the Extreme Machine. Pick: Tarrant. I never thought that I'd become a member of the grammar police, but the run on sentences in Tarrant's work kind of struck me. Further, the cancer really didn't win me over. Consider it a bad break for Tarrant's handler that this judge actually [detail withheld]. It's also a case where I'm aware that prostate cancer in men is very treatable, almost to the point of being comparatively tame held up to other potential cancers. So that particular scene really just ripped me right out of the reading. Comparatively, Harley's work had a good sense of entertainment what with Dave Harley being more and more ridiculous. I liked the bracketed use of description, and was particularly fond of the sermon. Pick: Harley Dave Harley “False Idol” The promo by Harley was a trash talking promo. Not much to this other than restating what he has been able to do so far in the tournament outlasting all the other WWA wrestlers making it to the bracket finals. However, that was all to it. It was a typical trash talking promo. It was decent but nothing that blew me away, which at this stage in the tournament I’m looking for. “Over for Dinner” This promo is a decent start for Harley, focusing on something that Tarrant did in the past. He wants to eat his assistant because Tarrant ate his parents in the past. But that’s all the promo has. Not much more to it. No real character development or some sort of storyline that continued on from previous rounds. Not even dealing with what Tarrant has done in this tournament at all or dealing with what Tarrant has said during this tournament, just pulling something off his bio about eating his parents. Tarrant: “Fresh off The Press” I really enjoy the little diary entries in the beginning that bring the reader up to speed on what is going through Tarrant’s mind. The whole dropping of gimmicks is a nice storyline and with the first person narrative we get to see the changes from his perspective. I am looking forward to see where the gimmick drop is going and how Tarrant will deal with it. “A Trip to the Doctors” This RP started off a little slow but it built up with the suspense of what was happening in the Doctor’s office. The part about the children going to the tournament was nice in that it showed the new, nice side of Tarrant. The question left me wondering what the Doctor’s results will be and what happened in there. This was a good middle RP in that it filled in the storyline and set up for the future. “An Uncertain Future” This is an interesting twist, with him having cancer. Now this could put a wrestler at a disadvantage in the ring if it became too serious and harmful. However at this point, it doesn’t seem to be that big of deal seeing as he believes that it is in its early stages and hasn’t affected him yet. Also, cancer and diseases have been done before as angles and stories in efedding so this isn’t the most original storyline. On the plus side I like the building of this new character of Chris Tarrant. I like the insight that we get into him and know what he is thinking about. Conclusion: Both of these handlers have done a great job in making it this far in the tournament. However, only one of them will go on to the next round. And this wrestler in my opinion is Tarrant. I enjoyed his RPs much more than Harley’s. He brought something new to the tournament with his dropping of gimmick and breaking of kayfabe. While the cancer bit has been done before and could potentially harm his wrestler in the tournament in the long run if the disease spreads rampantly at this point in time it can be overlooked. His character progressed and developed over the three RPs with a continuing storyline. Harley with his two RPs did not do this. I felt that his RPs were fairly generic in the sense that they could have been written for WWA, especially the trash talking one. While there is nothing wrong with a trash talk RP, it just has to bring more to the table at this point in time, this far into the tournament. So, in conclusion once again, my pick is Tarrant. Pick: Tarrant Pyrenees
