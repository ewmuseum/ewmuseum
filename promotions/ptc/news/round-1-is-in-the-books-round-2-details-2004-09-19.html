Round 1 is in the books! Round 2 details.

19 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All Round 1 Results can be found in the Results page. Uber-thanks again to the Round 1 judges for being super-fast with their picks and helping wrap up the judging period in under 48 hours. Round 2 Brackets have been posted! Cast your eyes over to the left of this page to see who's facing whom. Start time: September 22 12.01am (Server time). Check out the count-down clock at the top-right of screen to see when this is for you. Tip: Start writing now, it doesn't matter when you post it. End time: September 26 11:59pm (Server time). Tip 1: That's 6 days, including the day before the ability to post roleplays is enabled. Start now! Tip 2: If you have them and you are happy with them, don't sit on them, post them ASAP. This cuts down a lot on the judging time, which is good for everybody involved. RP Limit: 2 Anti-sandbagging rule is in effect. This means, only one RP can be posted on the final day. A second one posted on the final day will not be counted It doesn't matter when you do the first one before that. Judging: If you would like to judge a match or six, drop Argyle a PM on the PTC forums. Because some judging will take place over weekdays, the judges are being requested to provide their picks within 72 hours of Roleplaying period closing, instead of the 48 given in Round 1. Theme words: Are not in use for Round 2, however may return in some form for a later round. Bracket Shuffling There will be no further bracket shuffling. Positioning within brackets now will be held for the remainder of the tournament.
