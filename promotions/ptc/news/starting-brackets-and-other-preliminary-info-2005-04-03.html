Starting Brackets and other preliminary info

3 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Round 1 will start on the coming weekend, exact time TBA. I need the time between now and then to get the website online. The following brackets are preliminary. What that means is that if the people listed do not respond to the confirmation thread by the time stated in there, then they are out and will be replaced. If they are a member of a PTC fed0, they will be replaced by a member of a PTC fed. If they are a free agent, they will be replaced by a free agent. Additionally, playins for Round no-shows will be offered to members of PTC feds who applied but didn't get in. See beneath the brackets for a list of those.
