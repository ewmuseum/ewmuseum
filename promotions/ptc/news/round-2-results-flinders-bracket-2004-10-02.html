Round 2 Results: Flinders Bracket

2 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kasper Sky d Tony Stecchino (2 votes to 1) to advance Larry the Legionnaire d Cyrus Skar (2 votes to 1) to advance Falcon Mega advances. Token Weed d Christopher Shackleton (2 votes to 1) to advance Shadow d Shaman (2 votes to 0) to advance Tsunami Oni advances Jazon King d Sean White (2 votes to 1) to advance Jonathan Sail d Iron Lung (2 votes to 1) to advance Round 3 matchups Kasper Sky v Larry the Legionnaire Falcon Mega v Token Weed Shadow v Tsunami Oni Jazon King v Jonathan Sail Judges Comments Krasper Sky Vs. Tony Stecchino Krasper Sky: Has a pretty unique style, but I really don't like the combining of chapters. Call me old fashioned, but I prefer each chapter to be an individual RP. It always throws the story around if otherwise. With that being said the chapter system seemed a little too short IMO, but as noted, the size doesn't matter, just a simple observation of mine. All in all, nice style, nice character, but the story jumped with the chapter development in the single RP and it just didn't seem compelling to me at times. Tony Stecchino: Nice RP series going on here, you and Sky have a similar writing style, not similar RPing style, but writing seems similar, in how you convey your text to the reader. Interesting character, but way over used IMO. Then again, what character today isn't way overused? So that isn't too much of a deciding factor I believe. The story devleloped well and I read through with interest. Pick: Tony Stecchino, Sky came close though. Great to see Stecchino showing up with serious intention to take this one out after his recent misfortune, when he could easily have rolled over at the mere mention of his opponent's name and the regard others hold him in. This is very close, so very close. Sky takes it in that I think his writing is that little bit more focussed, but that's personal preference rather than anything substantial that I can point out. Either of you are more than capable of making at least the bracket finals with this type of material, but don't get too complacent or wrapped up in yourselves because there are guys in the other brackets who would at this point in time take you both to school because they are playing the game with the intent to win far better, and lets face it, that's what we are here for. You should both know who they are, and should both be following their progress with as much attention as you are giving to your own opponents. Pick: Sky Sky, well written, good flow, Tony's work was also well thought out, but I think Sky's flowed better. I'm not sure the love story angle worked so well for you Tony. It seemed a bit forced, maybe even a bit over the top - honestly, searching for your love in the face of a hurricane is a bit… well, naff. And the abusive mother angle also seems over-done. Tight match up, but I'm going with Sky for the win. Pick: Kasper Sky Larry the Legionnaire v Cyrus Skar Larry*I enjoyed this roleplay for two reason. One, it was an easy and very enjoyable read (especially the opener, which I found funny). Two, I liked the character a lot. Good stuff.* Cyrus*Very well written, but it seemed very familiar to me. The beginning was very good, but then it kind of dragged. I just felt it needed a little more in the end though.* Pick: Larry Larry the Legionnaire RP1: oh good Lord, it seems that he is like a 6 year old kid the stories keep getting bigger and bigger.... and well it bites him in the butt. over all it was a good RP Fun to read. Cyrus Skar RP1: scary look into someone I really didnt know. great discription good flow to the RP. good lenght. Pick: Skar Cyrus Skar vs Larry the Legionnaire Larry, I liked the fact that you referred to your round one win, however you didn't mention your round 2 opponent at all. Your writing style it good, it had a good flow, it's easy to read and it's entertaining. Good job. Skar, your work was also well written, but you didn't mention GTT4, your opponent or wrestling at all in your role play. Larry's work has the edge because it flowed better and kept a good pace through-out. Pick: Larry. Falcon Mega Falcon Mega: Not that bad. The story is way over-used though with people giving basically a pep talk about their next round after "wrestling" their last round opponent, in this case 48 hours after it. But it's enough to catch my eye and read through it. It was a good RP, could have done a little better in story development like I said, but is a good RP none the less. Token Weed v Christopher Shackleton Shackleton*Nice little poem in the beginning. The main thing I liked was the style of writing, using the first person rp. Good rp altogether. Token*Good descriptions, he set the scene up pretty well. Another good rp, although I thought it was missing that extra something it needed to send this one over the top. Winner: Shackleton Shackleton: Its written well, it gives me a bit of the character's background to chew on, but it doesn't take me anywhere that I want to go. I know you can do far better than this with depth of story, but in a tournament you have to convince me of that here and now, not with what I know of you previously. Weed: I've not gotten into the way most other players have addressed the tournament. The way you've done it here though is good, very good. To say that it is too short is not quite right, to say that it is missing something to give it extra oomph is probably closer. I'm going against the grain here and giving it to Weed, because there was a distinct high in his story, whereas Shackletons remained flat and word for word took a lot more effort to read. Pick: Token Weed Is it possible to pick a draw? I thought not. Weeds is short and the ending is shallow but the descriptions in the first half is good enough to make me want to read more from the author. Shackletons is a solid RP and deeper than Weeds because it is longer and has more going on but doesn't stand out in any one area for me. A little spark anywhere in it would have given you the win as Weed left himself wide open here. Pick: Weed Shaman v Shadow Shaman: Solid RP's. Nothing spectacular, and it was a bit repetitive, but it was a good trash talking series of RP's. A few spelling and grammartical errors, but nothing that took away from the RP's as a whole. Shadow: Very well written series. She countered Shaman's words very well, and did it in an interesting manner, varying up things a bit. The 2nd RP focused on absolution was perhaps a bit over the top, but it was entertaining and made a good point in the end. Pick: Shadow. Her RP's were just a bit more interesting than Shaman's, and they flowed much better as well. Shaman put in a good effort however. Shaman*Both rps were good. Nice to see a match in the middle of an rp sometimes, I enjoyed the style it was written in. Little too much trash talk at the end of the second one, but it was pretty good for what it was. Shadow*I liked her style a little bit better and it's always nice to see a good stabbing, lol. Seriously though, both rps gave it's own distinct feel, something which is hard to pull off. Winner: Shadow (it was extremely close) Jazon King v Sean White JaZon King: Really strange RP. Had a rambling introduction and then a bit of information about the wrestler himself that didn't really go into much depth at all. Nothing focused on his opponent or the GTT whatsoever. Sean White: Good RP, very well written. It set the scene effectively although there wasn't as much substance to it as there could've been. Pick: White. There was more description and detail than in King's, and White's was constructed very well. King 1: Certainly entertaining. Couldnt help but chuckle at the narrator, who provided great comedic relief. Decent enough piece with some funny moments. Nothing outstanding though. King 2: No Second Roleplay White 1: Decent enough. Plodded along nicely, and was pretty well written but I was not inspired enough to shout it from the rooftops. It was just another roleplay. It had a cliffhanger towards the end, but I can't say it intrigued me. White 2: No Second Roleplay My Pick: Both fairly mediocre, this is difficult to call but I'm giving this to King. He made me laugh. Although he'll need to put the effort in to get much further. JaZon King: Bizarre organization in the RP, but surprisingly extremely entertaining. The narration, journal entry, dialogue, and the way it all fit together worked out really well. Not much I really saw wrong with it ... not the best RP I've ever read, but it was very solid, and the most entertaining one I've read so far in this tournament. Sean White: Not a bad RP from White ... good description, and a decent story carrying it all together. Only real problem that jumped out at me was it just wasn't all that entertaining. Nothing in the RP made me want to keep reading, and I just ended up skimming through the chunks of description on my way through it. White's RP was decent, but I thought King's was very well done, and easily enough to win. Pick: King Iron Lung v Jonathan Sail Jonathan Sail Vs. Iron Lung Jonathan Sail: Really solid RPs, I like the character and he dragged me in from beginning to end with his story. A really interesting mix of style and really has what it takes. Really will be tough to contend with. Iron Lung: OMG. This is a hard choice. But it's round 2, what did I expect? Another solid two RPs from this pairing. Wow. This guy is just interesting, I couldn't turn away because I wanted to know more. Pick: Almost a coin toss really, either man could have won, but I'll have to go with Jonathan Sail on this one. Solid effort from both. Sail: After reading several trash talk RP's this evening, a story is a nice change of pace. Consistent... no major peaks or troughs. Ending leaves me wanting more, especially with your choice of mark. If you're playing recognition tactics with that, its quite sneaky... I like it. Technically flawless. I see novel writing in your future. Iron Lung: Others might, but I don't like this type of opening analysis of the tournament and your part in it. It adds zero value beyond being an opening for the sake of having an opening. As far as your writing goes, you are good... very good... RP2 intro is much better than the first, even though it still has a relatively light and fluffy point to it. I liked both of these better than I liked your work for the Global Title shot a while back. With a slightly kinder draw you could have waltzed into the bracket finals without much fanfare, and perhaps beyond. You've been trumped by Sail here though. His writing style is more focussed, and his story is more intense, and left me wanting far more. Pick: Sail Iron Lung a insominac's dream lol with the Doze away.. good RP fun read flow was good and it made it not a chore to judge. Rp2n second though I dont want any of that. Good RP funny as he just seems to barly make it from spot to spot you expect him to fall over at any moment. John Sail Rp1: OK, flowed good was a easy read. Rp2: wasnt as good as Rp 1 but again focused on where he was not what he is doing. Good RP again a look into a past of what made the man. good RP good discription and a good read.
