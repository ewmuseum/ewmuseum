GTT - Day 11 Report

7 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Final Brackets announced! Applications Closed! Yesterday saw posting of the final brackets for Round 1 of GTT4. Changes to the preliminary brackets released on PTC Radio included the withdrawals of Munson (cW) due to scheduling conflicts and Blitz Bomber due to application irregularities, as well as several announced byes instead going to late applicants. Applications for GTT4 are now officially closed, and it is no longer possible to get a spot in Round 1. PTC Management would like remind all competitors that they should have received their site login details and Round 1 theme words, and if they haven't then they should register any difficulties HERE. PTC management would also like to wish all competitors good luck in the tournament. GTT4 Preview Edition of PTC Radio this weekend! Two broadcasts of PTC Radio will be run this weekend, one to suit USA residents, hosted by The Malice Man Duke Williams, and one to suit UK residents, hosted by GTT Tournament Director Argyle. It has been revealed that "first dibs" on any second round playin spots will be up for grabs during the broadcasts. Bone up on your PTC trivia and familiarise yourself with some of the promotional work for PTC Survival if you intend to play for this prize. Click to discuss... Round 1 Theme Word exchange game! GTT Tournament Director Argyle has released details of a somewhat risky game where GTT4 entrants that are unhappy with their theme word can exchange them with another players. There is, however, an almighty catch to this process, one that will particularly appeal to those that partake in gambling. Click for further information... Reminder on time schedule! GTT4 will go ahead as scheduled, as indicated in the About section of this website. Round 1 Roleplaying will start on Sunday 12th September, and span five days. This site will have countdown clocks posted in order to eliminate any potential timezone confusion. Reminder on Biography posting! Competitors without links to an online biography in their tournament biography should post a biography HERE as soon as possible, in the interest of fair play. Finally... Keep an eye out on the GTT4 discussion forum for all the up-to-the-minute news and rumors. PTC Forums - GTT4 Discussion
