Kasper Sky d Larry the Legionairre (2 votes to 1) to advance Token Weed advances Shadow d Tsunami Oni (3 votes to 0) to advance Jonathan Sail d Jazon King (2 votes to 1) to advance Round 4 Matches: Kasper Sky v Token Weed Shadow v Jonathan Sail Judges Comments Kasper Sky v Larry the Legionairre Kasper (9.1) I felt this RP was gragged on and boring at times, but I did like the storytelling evident here. If the roleplay focused more on present day rather than past events, it would've scored a bit more. Still a pretty solid RP. (Also, the "Legionnaires" crack was funny.) Grammar and punctuation was off by a little bit. Larry (9.3) I'm forgetting, is this the guy that's from "Antartica"? Anyway, minus a few grammatical errors here and there, this roleplay was still lengthy but against was was an entertaining read because of Larry's mom "sonning" him. Other than that nothing else to really comment on. Pick: Larry the Legionairre Larry The Legionnaire: The roleplay and story were good. The placing and interaction between the characters was good as well. I enjoyed the aspect you addressed your opponent directly and played with the oddities going on in the here and now. Kasper Sky: I enjoyed the History lesson between the two. It was fun and fluent. Addressing the issues of the past as well as addressing the comments of your opponent and staying true to your character. I had to choose one i go with Kasper Sky for the in depth character detail and history. Pick: Kasper Sky Larry the Legionnaire: RP1: A good strong RP even for a character that is a nutter. Fun read nice flow. His friends cant seem to support him but yet they do. It is like telling a child to go play in the street and it does. Something is BOUND to happen. And it isn’t going to be good. RP2 What can I say, the poor critter….. He has made up his mind to keep trying and not giving up. Sooner or later maybe he will get a life or get a clue but it is funny to read. So until then keep going. Kasper Sky Rp1 Good story a heavy character with a strange unforgiving background. The Rp was glimpes of what has happen vs. what is happeing now. Rp2 Again a flash to cover the years that he was in a coma and unlike most he didn’t come out with all his memories. Pieces and parts of his mind and who he is. Got to say that Larry gave him a close run fo rhis money I like them both but will go with : Pick: Kasper Sky Tsunami Oni v Shadow Oni’s 1st- Hilarious stuff, though some parts of the roleplay could have used more detail in certain areas. Oni’s 2nd- I absolutely loved the song bit at the beginning, just plain hilarious. Better than the first, in my opinion, just for the song. Shadow’s 1st- Loved the story and how her history tied into it all. Wish some of the bigger clumps had been broken up though, made it difficult to read at times. Dragged on a bit at times, but all together it was a pretty good read. Shadow’s 2nd- Found this more enjoyable than the first, simply because I felt it was easier to get into Shadows mind, especially during the interactions with Amir. I do feel though that some of details in that part could have been expanded to make the read a bit more interesting for the reader besides the simple stuff such as someone saying something in one way, without any body language. There was great detail on body language and how things were said right before this bit, which would have been great if continued into this scene. Great piece of work though. Pick: Shadow Tsunami Oni: I love this guys work. He did a very nice job. Keep doing what you are doing. Shadow: Very good style, very good flow. I loved this work too, I am a new fan. This one is too close to call, a very tough desicion. Pick: Shadow Tsunami Oni (9) Okay the pubes being mentioned... need not be mentioned (And hey you don't need to blackmail Bush, just mention WMD and Iraq and see what he says ) God's pratical joke (?) Anyway... This roleplay was like the twilight zone gone horribly screwed. What the F?! If there's an ending... (wait there is.)... Great job none the less. Had to take away something for the not focusing on the match bit but it wasn't much. Tsunami Oni [2] (9.01) Again this is another OMG moment in this tourney for me as a judge. No kind of match relevance but funny as f'ing hell though... And then there's the singing intro bit... too good. Shadow 9.2 I like this RP. Good read. A bit on the length side but wasn't much of a problem reading through... over and over again... Then the whole underdog thing also was a plus. Shadow [2] 9.0 Good RP... Just a bit out there for me. Pick: Shadow. Jonathan Sail v Jazon King King’s - Thought the narrator giving commentary through the entire first bit was quite creative, but got annoying after awhile. If that was intended, I’ll give props for that. If not, try and cut down the amount of narration by this guy, and let the promo focus in on Jason if they are both going to be involved. This is supposed to be about Jason after all, not the narrator. So as long as they are both involved, focus more on Jason, rather than the narrator. Second bit I liked simply because it really allowed the reader to get familiar with I think the third bit and first bit could have worked just fine together, without the break, and instead had the second bit at the end. Loved the fourth part, though like the second I’d prefer it to be a separate roleplay. Big props though for the creativity though as far as showing that King isn’t all about trash talk, that he too is an actual human away from the ring, and not just one of those guys that stay in character all the time. Sail’s -Great story with great character development. Loved how it allowed the reader to get familiar with Sail through his own perspective, as well as others perspectives. Another thing that really draws me towards this character is the honesty, which makes him unlike the rest who claim every match is important, where as though Sail is straight out honest with mentioning that the only matches that will matter are the “big ones” such as debuts, title matches, and his retirement. Pick: Jonathan Sail Jazon King: I really enjoyed this roleplay, the favorite out of all those that I read. I was entertained all the way through. Jonathan Sail: A really good piece of work I really enjoyed it, in a few spots it kinda dragged on a bit. Pick: Jazon King Jonathan Sail (9.4) Nice will rounded RP. A bit too lengthy but I felt that the RP could've been split into two RP.s I like the office scene earlier on and I also like how the whole RP fit together. Jazon King (9.35) I like this roleplay. Not lengthy at all. Told the story of Jazon in the GTT4 tourney. It's not enough to win the match, but it's still a good RP.) Pick: Jonathan Sail (barely) Flinders
