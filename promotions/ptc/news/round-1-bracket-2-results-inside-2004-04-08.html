Round 1 Bracket 2 Results inside

8 April 2004

Bracket 2 Match 5: Carys (RPW) defeated Fury (WMW) and Armaan (WWA) three votes to none. Judge 1 A great showing from Carys and Armaan here, both rp's from them were solid but in the end I felt Cary's was the better rp. Fury did not have a good showing due to OOC issues thus taking him out of the running. Judge 2 Carys - The Roleplay was good as far as the story was concerned. However, when it came to the tournament I was very disappointed due to the lack of it. Mixing in stuff from the "home" federation was unique, but the story is what kept me reading this one. Fury - This was horrible for me to read, the dude mentioned doing a promo inside of a ring. I've never known a federation to own one arena, and allow their wrestlers to go in-and-out at will. The intro (first paragraph) was really good, but there was no story at all. His form of trash-talking was decent, but this is a PTC Tournament you should be pushing yourself to do your best. Armaan - Armaan had a very nice work there, the story was fun to read. He mentioned everything I look forward to judge in Roleplays, but he also hit a place I HATE to read. When mentioning Carys, he mentioned she was a woman. Still not a problem, but the way he did it totally left a bad taste in my mouth. I know this is wrestling, but no matter how tough you make your wrestler to be you have to remember, a woman can still take you to school. Winner - Carys wins this match hands down without much competition. Her Roleplay was much better, even if the mentioning of this tournament was a little lackluster. The story was more enjoyable, and the development of her Character for this tournament was very well-done. Good job Westley. Judge 3 Fury A token roleplay, as indicated in the OOC comment at the bottom. I don’t get why people need to bring the my fed is being overlooked gripe into a their writing. Nonetheless, props for turning up and hope you have another opportunity to do your best for WMW in future events. Armann Good grammar and spelling – check. Smooth flow – check. Talked about tournament and opponents – check. Some character development or introduction – Armaan it seems is softcock and possibly a sexist pig at the same time, so check. Story with any sort of depth or hidden meaning that makes me nod with appreciation… not found. Carys This was a little too racy for me, making it me work harder to focus on its meaning, but I got more of an appreciation for elements of it than I did Armaan and Fury’s work. The ending was a little abrupt. Is Carys perhaps looking in a mirror? I’m being rushed away to do something else so can’t go into more detail. I’ll simplify my pick to Carys worked harder in the trenches to get a reaction, so takes this one. Match 6: Jay Phoenix (RPW) defeated Max Roswell (GW) by default due to Roswell no-showing. Match 7: Syko Arsonist (FA) defeated Angel (FA) 2 votes to 0 * Alex Caine no-showed Judge 1 Syko #1- Wow he did a great job. Then again I expected him to do a great job on his roleplays. There was a few weak spots, he's not perfect after all. But his opponents will have to impress me to take this match. Pros-Strong storyline that made me interested in the whole rp. He broke it up so it was easy to follow. Nice flowing roleplay. Cons-STOP GOINGS SYKO WALKED....SYKO TURNED....SKYO LEFT! Really you did it twice and its just annoying. I see no purpose for it at all. Oh the only other real thing is make the other character Jacobs more solid. He seems more background than a driving force. I don't know anything about him other than he's an unskilled poor laborer who needs money. Angel #1- I needed something strong to defeat Syko. This was not it. A great effort by Angel but I wasn't seeing this beating him. Hopefully the second works out the problems that were in the first. Pros- You told us everything about your character. It was WAY too much in my opinion. We don't need the entire life story here. Jacob was an interesting character. He wasn't the main character though. I found myself much more interested in Jacob than Angel. If Jacob was the wrestler entered instead of Angel it would have helped but here I felt it hurt. Cons- Sorry the pros had so many cons in it. You did good stuff but you went overboard and hurt yourself. Angel seemed like a side character for Jacob. The rp was WAY too long. I know size doesn't matter but when it's boring me to the point were I need to take breaks so I can finish it, it's too long. The reason I found it was boring is because you used way too much detail. You OVER described stuff. Say what you need to say and leave it at that. I'm always told if it doesn't help the rp, don't include it. You had alot of that. Syko #2- You didn't need to improve on much from the first rp. In the second rp you came on just as strong. Pros- You included your match and opponents in nicely. Alex was not mentioned much which since he didn't rp that was not much of a problem. You had a good storyline that was well wrote. Cons- You repeat yourself a lot. Reduce that because its pointless. Avoid using the same word over and over again. Basicly same problems as last time. Try getting someone honest to look over your rps before you post Angel #2- Much better than your first. I was actually able to sit through this one with less breaks. I still had to take two breaks to actually finish it though. Pros- Vast improvement. We got to see Angel as the main character. Showed background and addressed your opponents and what they said nicely. Cons- You have too much description in your roleplays. It takes forever to get to the actual rp through all it. You bog down your rp too much. I have trouble finishing the rp without taking breaks. There is also a problem with repeating yourself. You use the same words over and over instead of changing it up. Winner: Syko Arsonist. I enjoyed his rps much more than Angel's. His was much easier to read. He had me more interested. Judge 2 Very good RPs from both Angel and Syko, which is exactly what I expected, sadly. There's not a good deal of criticism that can be brought against either of the RPers, so it comes down to just blatant nit-picking to choose a winner out of either of these two. Syko's, as strong as his RPs always are, did irritate me a bit in the use of the generic emotional plot devices, especially in the first to justify Syko's entrance into the tournament. They're not bad, just sort of contrived for that typical emotional response, which I've never been a fan of. Angel's first RP was a strong introduction to the character for anyone who wasn't familiar with her. At the same time, the RP could have had the same impact and been much more interesting if it was a quarter of the length. Somewhere along the line, it doesn't matter how well written an RP may be, it takes a lot to justify something that long and keep it from becoming tedious and boring. Angel's first definitely crossed that line; her first RP was more informative and enjoying than Syko's, but the length hurt it more than anything else. Syko's second RP was much stronger than his first. The exploration of his unwillingness to begin wrestling again, his hiding from the business with his gym, and everything else that goes into it, it was an extremely good RP. Angel's second RP was more along the lines of what I disliked about Syko's, namely with the typical formulas that are so rampant in wrestling. It lacked a lot of what made the first one interesting, and seemed a lot more formulaic for the character; typical dark, mysterious character, filling a roll I've seen many others takeover. It's not written poorly, but it lacks the originality and interest Syko's had. Angel's first RP beats Syko's, but Syko's second is strong enough to win the match. Match 8: Ian "The Fool" Fetzer (XUW) defeated Robb Hardy (NGW) and Justin Walker (WWA) 2 votes to 0 Judge 1 "The Fool" takes it, in rather decisive fashoin. Walker and Hardy decided to use the typical RP fare with beating up/threatening women, heroic measures, and didn't really tell much of a story. I don't really feel I know or even care about each character. Fetzer, however, provided background, and we leared straight from the giddy-up, why Fetzer wanted to get involved in the first place. For that, he advances. Judge 2 Robb Hardy First RP: Gangland shooting, family reunions, bigamy/adultery, government conspiracy theories, then a reappearance of TJ Codair, who I believe is another character belonging to Hardy’s handler, from EPW. How much can you pack into one roleplay? With all this helter skelter, it felt to me like you ran out of steam half way through and added Codair’s presence to wish Hardy good luck was thrown in to pad things out. I say that because with all these reunions, there was no teasers for anything that anyone would be doing in future promos, and what you did have seemed patchy. Justin Walker First RP: Deep personal loss, alcoholism, vowing over his mother’s grave to do one better than her. Second RP: Focused on the match, yelling at a woman whom he had trysts with the night before. You’re fine with technical, only a couple of typos. Where you lack is depth, both in character and storyline. My advice to you is to read of some of the other tournament work to see what you’re competing against, and expand your roleplay ideas beyond one time point and one mood that you focused on this time, and then come back next time and give it another shot. Ian Fetzer I would be insulting this man by making any of my usual comments, he has things well under control. The Fool takes it decisively. CW1 brackets
