Round 3: Mrs S. Bracket Results

15 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
KILLEAN SIRRAJIN Judge 1 I walked into this judging not really familiar with either the writers or their characters. I was surprised, for various reasons… Killean Sirrajin Sirrajin wrote two good RPs, but I felt they could’ve been better. The first RP showed a nice sense of family togetherness. At times it seemed to drag along, and I literally yawned at one point, the story sort of boring me. When it really go my attention was when they were really appreciating Desiree. That had a nice touch to it, put a sensitive side to a stereotypically commonly insensitive job. The second was interesting. With the first two scenarios, I kept on asking “What the fuck? Why would he just straight up kill Sirrajin?!” But then, I like how it came to a conclusion at the end, wrapped things up. The RPs were good…but he flooded with too much un needed detail and descriptions of emotions. Digital Mortality Two interestingly contrasting RPs. The first one really captured the despair Digi was possessing. Burying the ring was a nice touch, and attempting to put it all aside was good as well. You could see that Digi was trying to hide basic emotions. The second one was a nice touch, as it showed a stronger side and an emotional side to Digi. As the first one was decent, this one was great. Really enjoyed it. The cliff hanger factor was awesome. The Verdict I think my feelings for the RPs can be judged like this: Killean: 1st: Decent 2nd: Good Digi: 1st: Decent 2nd: Great (as it leaves me wanting another RP) My choice: Digi with the win. The only thing I’d have to say for Killean is to leave out some of the un needed details and shit. Like, it’s nice that everyone is all lovey-dovey, but move along with the story. It seemed like in the first RP you did a lot of emotional filler, which leaves me bored. Judge 2 Killean Sirrajin vs. Digital Mortality The Killean Chronicles: Holiday Feelings Interesting RP, this had a nice sentimental feeling. It was a bit lacking in not addressing the upcoming match once but I'm guessing the follow up is responsible for that. It was nice to see him being changed by the daughter he has to take care of, however I think you could have added more emotional attachment into it and emphasised the effect Desiree has had. The Killean Chronicles: A Father's Decisions This was quite good, I'll be honest in saying I haven't seen the scenario idea before in an RP. this was pretty damn good and kept me engaged but again it was lacking any real mention of the match. Perhaps it was on purpose due to his concerns with his daughter but it was a little odd to me. A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 4 Good, good piece here. Nice, short and simple, this ticked all the boxes and left me looking for more. It shows you don't have to write massive amounts to show what talent you've got and Digi has it in spades. A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 5 Another good one and very sel-centred around the story at hand. Didn't deviate from it and it paid off from that take. Very well written and thought out, and just like Killean the only thing I can say is that there was a lack of comment towards their match. So two guys who are able to look at the bigger picture. In a way this seemed very appropriate as if it had've been one very focused and one distant, it might have been easier to judge. As it is, I'm swayed by Killean's ones because they seem fresher to me than most ideas. Digi's ones were great and there is no shame in roleplaying the way he has. Pick: Killean Sirrajin Judge 3 Killean Sirrajin: The first roleplay, I found myself losing interest in parts and really wondered what you were trying to achieve with it. Killean the family man? Well yeah, but Killean the man who's going to kick Digital Mortality's ass? I wasn't as convinced. Maybe it gives me a little insight into the character, but unfortunately I felt myself not caring. Very well written, although lots of big unbroken paragraphs didn't help how easy it was to read. I really don't know what to expect from the second. And I must admit I'm in for a shock. The second showed me why you should be here. The multiple scenario's is something I personally haven't seen in a roleplay before. It worked brilliantly and followed on from your first roleplay very well. This is where maybe the first doesn't do a lot on it's own, but with the second, it shows me a hell of a lot of talent. Very impressed with these two roleplays side by side. Digital Mortality What can I say? Both of these men deserve to win this, both of them I can see have put so much effort in, I wish I hadn't been given this match to judge to be honest, after reading each of their roleplays. The first from Digi, well, it blew me away to be honest. The story was gripping, it was well paced and had me wanting more right after the first roleplay. The second was no disappoint and I find myself not wanting, but 'needing' to know if she'll marry. I felt so attatched to the characters I was disappointed when the second roleplay finished. However, a big thing I found with each persons work was the unbroken paragraphs made them a little harder to read. Each to their own, but I need to pick a fault with your work or you're both going to get big headed. Overall: Well you guys suck. This has been one of the hardest decisions in my entire life. Killean's roleplay were two very different works that flowed beautifully into one another and Digi's was two great parts of a story that I thought were done superbly. I think, due to a slightly better consitency I'll have to go with Digi. Both roleplays were excellent and I really couldn't fault them. If anything Killean's first let him down just a little bit, as I said before I felt my interest wavering in places. Props to both writers, I wish I could put you both through. Pick: Digital Mortality. Digital Mortality d Killean Sirrajin 2 votes to 1 MASQUERADE V DAWN MCFARLANE Judge 1 Dawn McFarlane I liked the first RP a lot. It opened up the true emotions held for Lia and how Dawn is still clinging. I then loved the walk she had with Dawn…really, when will she be ready? The second one was interesting. Marcus comes and takes Dawn away…that rat! He then beats the shit out of her…that rat! It disappointed me how she reacted to him… "You fucking bastard! DO you honestly think I would miss you?! Are you so fucking twisted that you can’t see I loathe everything about you? You raped me in my GODDAMN BEDROOM! I can’t wait for the day you shuffle off and fucking DIE!" The dude raped her. That makes it sound like he cheated on her. That annoyed me. Overall, two great RPs. Masquerade One RP, but an amazing one. After finishing, the first thought that came to my head was “This could be a blockbuster movie”. Really gripping dialogue…I love the Masquerade character. It would’ve been nice for you to describe the shady folks in the room…maybe be like “They were assassins, they were rapists, they were blah blah blah”. The thing is, though, the whole thing was awesome. The Verdict Both were excellent. Even though Dawn had two, Masquerades left me wanting another…and another…so awesome! So, ultimately, I choose Masquerade to win. Judge 2 Masquerade: I found it very hard to get into your roleplay, your writing style was impeccable, but the jumps in the roleplay confused me a little. A very well written piece of work, but it seemed to lack something, it felt incomplete. Maybe there was a second to come? I'm not sure. However, I loved the first section and feel that even though it did seem to lack that little something, it was a very well accomplished piece of work. I can't take much away from this, even with it's sense of that little thing I can't quite put my finger on, it's still impressed me very much so. Dawn McFarlane: Two roleplays to Masquerades one, however, quantity isn't always better than quality. The first one took me in right from the start. It was an interesting story, broken up a little with the letter which helped make it a pleasant read. The second roleplay, didn't capture my attention as much as the first. It just seemed more of the same. Overall I did enjoy both roleplays and I'm finding it tough deciding a winner. Overall: The fact that Masquerade only did one has made this a very hard choice. That one was a very good piece of work, but McFarlane's showing was also worthy of going through to the next round. However, I did enjoy reading Dawn's but Masquerade's work left me wanting so much more. I was filled with such a sense of intrigue and wonder it really did make me think 'Wow.' Dawn wasn't bad in this round. Masquerade was just very good. Pick: Masquerade. Masquerade d Dawn McFarlane 2 votes to 0 ROUND 4 MATCHUP Mal Somers v Digital Mortality v Masquerade v Shanty Jones Two highest placed advance to bracket final. Brackets
