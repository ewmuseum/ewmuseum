Super Series preliminary info

23 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I gots me the Super Series site layout today, it looks pretty schmick! Super Series will be an I-PPV event. RP'ing will be first part of August, card publication shortly before the end of August. Preliminary info for the event: I want each member fed to provide a matchup between two of its members - that is, you guys get two of your members to have a match on a PTC event, they roleplay here for the event, and the RP's count for this event only. I would be asking the community first for volunteer match writers, however if not enough are forthcoming you may be asked to help out for your fed's match, with plenty of notice. The match provided by your fed can be a title match or not, up to you based on availability and willingness of your members to participate. The intent is to showcase the best possible from each fed. You guys know who that is amongst your own members. Title matches would go further up the card as a gesture of respect to whoever volunteers to put one in, but other than that I plan to do a draw of the card order on webcam/PTC Radio close to the event, so that card positioning is clearly luck of the draw. I haven't spoken to Andy about this yet, but it may also be a good time to reintroduce the PTC Global Title (or its replacement). So if that goes down there may be something similar to Seven of the Best where the winners get put in another match against each other (no additional RP's). Either way, on top of the showcasing your fed's aspect, I'd be wanting some competing against other fed's aspect. We are in the middle of discussing something else unique and interesting for the Global Title reintroduction, but the two ideas might be able to be compatible. Additional matches for the event would be an Extreme Title match if possible, the pirate boat match for tag teams, and a feature attraction match between the winner of this Round of Infinite Gauntlet and someone else yet to be decided. So, consider yourselves informed. I'll annoy you closer to the time as far as actually booking the matches goes. I'd like to announce them over time in order to build up interest, instead of dumping them on the public at the last minute. Posted on the PTC forum by Argyle.
