BRACKET FINALS -- Call For Judges.

28 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm looking for the following: 1. Disregarding everything you've read previously. The character, the fed they're involved in, everything. Don't take the story into regard as part of a bigger picture, only what's presented in these two stories. 2. Don't be nice. Be constructive, but, don't be nice for niceness' sake. At this stage, these people are going to be likely judged on grammatical errors, word counts, characterization, dialogue and the like. As such, those 4 that advance as Bracket Champions will likely need to pull out all the stops to ensure that they do. 3. Read every word. 4. If you dislike what you've read so much that you don't want to/can't judge it, please PM me. Tell me you don't want to judge anymore, no strings attached, no harm, no blood, no foul. 5. Your deadline will be the Sunday following week. I will slow release the results over a few hours and get on with the next round. PLEASE be sure to budget your time over that week. Read as you go, print it and walk on your treadmill, whatever. Who's interested and what match(es)? I'd like to have 5 per, if possible. I cannot judge the Impulse v. Stevens match, and will not involve myself in the decision. Pete R. bought me some beer last week, and I don't want anything pointing toward it being a possible impropriety on our behalf. He is persona au gratin in my eyes at the moment as far as this tournament goes, and yet the love in my heart burns with the intensity of a thousand suns. Seriously though, I wouldn't want it to soil a win, so, I'm not judging it.
