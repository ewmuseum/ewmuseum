Round 1 Results: Flinders Bracket

18 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These are results, they are not Round 2 Brackets. Those will be announced after all the results are up. No-shows and Double-no-shows are not noted. If you haven't read the judging thread, do it now: http://community.primetimecentral.net/showthread.php?t=9955 Kasper Sky d Hell Itself (1 vote) Johnathan Sail d Itagaki Freeze (1 vote) Falcon Mega d Luke Chadbourne (1 vote) Larry the Legionnaire advances. Tony Stecchino advances. Iron Lung d Venneman Pendragon (1 vote) Tsunami Oni advances. Shaman d Reginald Atkins (1 vote) S.White d R.Nox (1 vote) Shadow d Raguel (1 vote) Christopher Shackleton advances. Volker Bladwin advances. El d LeStat Knight (1 vote) Jazon King advances. Judging Comments Kasper Sky vs Hell Itself Win is Sky's both men just wrote four rps and sandwiched em together but HI didn't seem to have cohesion or anything beyond trash talking Itagaki Freeze vs Johnathan Sail Freeze. Ah. Yeah. OK. I have to say the whole backward dealie was a gimmick that just didn't work for me. Sail's work had a good flow, it was strong, it had nice details and (maybe more importantly) it made sense. Sail wins. Luke Chadbourne vs Falcon Mega Chadbourne: nothing pulled at me and made me wanna keep going. On top of that his writing felt fragmented... Falcon Mega: given that he writes like this the rest of the tourney he could easily be in the finals.. One point one goal one emotion those thing stood out and then surrounding that was everything needed to keep me involve.. good description and better telling of emotion Pick: Falcon Mega Larry the Legionnaire Larry: Great RP. Nice introduction, Liked how the introduction was made to GTT4 entry, shocking the bejesus out of everyone. Loved the Antarctica bit. Iron Lung vs Venneman Pendragon Theme worked better for Lung. VenPen did well, but, not as well as Lung. Shaman vs Reg Atkins I would have to go with Shamen, I had a hard time seeing where Atkin’s Theme word fit ito his rp. Not that I minded the look into what brings the man to be the way he is. I think he was trying to show us why he doesnt trust but then again I am not sure where the dead cat comes into the whole thing.... Shamen’s RP was long and hard to read but it was a good RP. He stuck with his word the entire RP, this was a improvement from the EDT match rps that he did for Survival as I had read those also. And HE was really Relaxing!!! Shadow vs Raguel I will have to go with Shadow on this one, The RP left me wanting to know more about her. And she showed what most of the handlers don’t show, the fact that the characters in their real feds still have matches to deal with. Not that Reguel didn’t do badly in his Rp; Personally I think that Shadow did a much better job showing me what her character was thinking. I think I am going to have to go read some more of her stuff, so I know this character better. Reguel's dark persona was intresting to read. But I dont think he showed as much as Shadow did with her Rp. He kept mentioning the blood like liquid on the hands and what not but again I couldnt get into the character as much as I did Shadow. Pick: Shadow White vs Nox This is pretty much even stevens as far as quality goes. Similar length, similar style. Both easy to read, both about the same depth as the other, both about the same use of their theme word. I had to look further afield to pick a winner. Sean White takes it by virtue of a few careless grammatical and spelling errors from Nox. That's all there was between them, nothing more, nothing less. Pick: Sean White El vs LeStatt Knight Lestatt Knight - Decent stroytelling that was interesting. Technical errors were very few which is quite an imrpovement for him. Very good ambience. Problems were that it did not really deal with the theme he had listed (skepticism) nor did he mention his opponent in any notable way. Flashbacks were also over dramatic and unrealistic at parts. El- I really dislike this gimmick and this character. That being said, the RP was very well written. Only one or two technical errors. I found the fable in the middle of the RP very intriguing, and it really pulled up what was otherwise a very over the top and uninteresting RP. I also found the supporting characters very lifeless and annoying. Winner: El - I hope she (I assume the writer is a woman) can make the story more interesting. It was a tough choice as I don't want to read another El RP if she continues with the same trend of characters but I thought her writing was stronger. Flinders
