CW2 wrapup discussion

4 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another tournament over with, bring on GTT5 (in September/October). Most people paying attention would have realised that I essentially threw out the traditional PTC tournament rule book with this one, with the aim of keeping things interesting, in a limited sphere. - The brackets were an illusion other than limiting who people's second round opponents were. That was intended from the start. It amused me though how the discussions on Troyer bracket unfolded. - The removal of caps and limits of any kind on the last round. - Not protecting any "name players" from facing any other "name players" at any stage of it. (i.e. sticking with the randomizer as much as possible, only avoiding same fed vs same fed matches, and making the Sean vs Ryan matchup because after them creating their own hype on the boards I didn't want to risk either of them jobbing to someone else and not seeing the matchup). - The average of 10 days per RP period, staying with 2 per round. Which ended up doing absolutely nothing towards stopping the mass burn out in Round 3. It could possibly be related to the previous point, because the people who had burned out had had to fight every step of the way to get that far, so in that sense it backfired big time. - Props to the Judges (in no order, and could have done one or 10 matches). Noah Hanson Kyle (Smiley) Shadow (Kat) Matt W. Jeff W. Jerrod Ken Kaze Warren B Rob W. Mat W. Ross 3 Nova Dave (of the Doooblin variety) Pierre H. Ryan (Emilio Rage) Loz (Digimon) Wayne (Kraze) James D. (Dawn M) Peter Vetra Jay Phoenix Sean W. Ryan K (Flying Vergomovs) Brock E. Hopefully I haven't left anyone out. Other than the late last round, which was due to the split decision needing the third judge, who disappeared on me, this has been by far the most stress-free tournament to run with respect to judges. Nobody judged a match while they were playing, and we still had enough to cover everything. It was great! Up until Round 3, the lack of no-shows was unprecedented in anything like this that has been done here. With burnout, we perhaps know what the problem was, but not really what a good solution to it is that doesn't involve seeding or otherwise mollycoddling. In one way I think it is good to show people who otherwise might be intimidated to join something like this that everyone is actually human. At the moment, I'm thinking this will be the last secondary singles event for PTC, and will just stick to GTTx as the main singles tournament. Next year we could do something else, assuming everyone hasn't moved on to drinking at hooters and there's still a market for this sort of thing :D So.. random discussion is welcome. Props on the things you liked welcome. Constructive criticism welcome. Props on anyone's RP's that you liked (that is after all why we are doing this, right?) Bitching about judges or seizing on one comment that didn't suit your tastes and whining about it will not be looked upon favorably. You've seen the list, these are all good people, and your peers, so deserved the utmost respect for stepping up and doing the work it took to make it happen.
