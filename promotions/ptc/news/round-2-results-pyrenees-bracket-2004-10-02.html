Round 2 Results: Pyrenees Bracket

2 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Unstoppable Eugene d Desolation (2 votes to 1) to advance Dallas Reeves d Kenny Mercury (2 votes to 1) to advance (Reeves 2nd RP not counted due to late posting) Dave Harley d Robb Hardy (2 votes to 0) to advance Salaok d Noah Hanson (2 votes to 1) to advance Tarrant advances The Shadow advances Blaise McClancy advances John Adams d Dani Furher (2 votes to 1) to advance Round 3 matchups Unstoppable Eugene v Dallas Reeves Dave Harley v Salaok Tarrant v The Shadow Blaise McClancy v John Adams Judges comments Unstoppable Eugene v Desolation Eugene’s RP: Hilarious, I was rolling as I read this. Good luck in the next round. Desolation’s RP: I’ll just say this…I didn’t ‘get’ it. I read it. I re-read it. I read it a third time. I still don’t get it. Pick: Eugene Wow, uhm, how can I say this nicely? Heh, Well I read Desolations first and I'm thinking ok I'm sure this Eugene cat has won, then I read Eugene and instead of it being a close call cause they were good rps it was completely ass backwards. They both have alot of room to improve, Eugene almost seems a rip off of Eugene from WWE and the rp was really pointless. Desolation was on topic with GTT and all but his ranting about the subject were so easily to be looked at as OOC , he would say how no one expected him to beat Jay Phoenix and all this other stuff, he coulda made it feel more in character by stressing the wrestling involved in the match up itself... if there was no match up, then make shit up ... Jay didnt expect me to reverse Blankedy blank move and him with my X finisher. anyways, if these guys plan to win a round 3 match they better step it up ... Pick: Desolation Was Desolation's a roleplay or a rant? Eugene's mommy gets her wish. Pick: Eugene Mercury v Reeves Mercury: Really good RP. Had some very funny laugh out loud moments during it, it kept me entertained throughout. Great rationale at the end about how he didn't care for sex and how he tied that in with the match. Everything just clicked and flowed really nicely. Reeves: Like Mercury, a good RP. It too had some very funny moments, including the customary Nicklebad quip. Also, I really liked the end about Mercury, and how he tied that together. I thought from a technical standpoint, this was written better than Mercury's. This was a really tough call, as it usually is when there's only 1 RP to read. Both were really close, as they were both well written and were both entertaining. In the end though, I think Mercury had more depth to his character where as I think the side characters in Reeves' story took away from his character and the emphasis that should've been placed on him. Reeves' talking about the match was just a bit too generic for my liking, where as Mercury varied it up a bit. So on that point, Mercury wins. Pick: Mercury. Mercury: Developed his match and character well throughout the story. Had some good character development going on. Great for size and quality. Dallas Reeves: He too developed well around the match up, good size and quality. By far the closest match I've judged so far. Pick: Dallas Reeves Mercury’s RP: Did not like the way the rp started, thought it swept the reader in to quickly without any buildup. The ambience was very solid but the conversations were extremely choppy and I did not enjoy reading through it at parts. The last few paragraphs were good though if you get to the point where you need to add in homosexual jokes you’ve pretty much failed in the rp. Not a terrible rp but you should have done a second one. Reeves’ RP: Was a big fan of the prelude he put in his rp, it’s a solid way to bring the reader up to date on the events that have occurred without needlessly searching through past rps. His ambience, as in the last round was very solid and his conversation pieces, while choppy were good. I liked the consistent dialect he used for ‘Remy’ and felt that the music was perfect for the rp. I also think that he ended the rp at the right time and made great use of the length with quality writing all through. Very well done and deserved of the win. Pick: Dallas Reeves Dave Harley v Robb Hardy Harley’s RP: Extremely short…in most cases I know an rp is going to suffer if its as short as this one is. However, Harley seemed to use every available letter to pack his rp with stuff that intrigued me. From the ‘Harley Notes’ to a strong opening sentence and the relationship between the passengers. It was not a great rp but it worked and one of the things I really stress, using the length of the rp to get your full idea across, was met. Hardy’s RP: First issue I had was the link for a previous rp. If I need to go back to another rp to understand what you are trying to speak on then that’s bad. You should be able to bring the reader up to speed quickly and easily in each rp and not make him go searching through past works to understand what you are trying to show. The rp was fair, nothing spectacular and I felt that you were lacking in ambience after the fifth paragraph. I really would have liked to see maybe description of facial expressions between Hardy and Smokey as the conversation seemed to lag without any more outside references. Not a bad rp, but I think it could have been done better. Pick: Dave Harley Harley: Short but sweet roleplay, got to the point, showed what he wanted to get done. Overall good quality. Didn't get lost in interest once! Robb Hardy: Had a good story, but I found myself starting to lose interest in some parts. He needs to build it a little better, possible not have such huge paragraphs of text. Pick: Harley Salaok v Noah Hanson Salaok: My first comment, you are an amazing writing. My second comment, you are NOT a wrestler. I was left wondering why you wrestler or even bother to show up to the tournament. How are you going to wrestle is you are trying to stay low brow after the murder? You write beautiful but you are in the wrong hobby. If I was a creative writing teacher I would give you an A easy. Looking at this as someone who is suppose to be writing a wrestler, you did a poor job. Salaok is NOT a wrestler he is a hitman. N.Hanson: While you are a very good writer you did not meet Salaok's level of talent. However you made me see Hanson is a wrestler and actually wants to win this match. He has heart and drive. You entertained me and left me satisfied. But be glad Salaok decided to forget about even being a wrestler and wanted to write his action hitman story instead of a rp for a wrestling tournament. Winner: N.Hanson. Yes people will complain Salaok should win since he was the better writer. But you must realise Salaok has no place in this tournament. By his roleplay the tournament is the very last thing on his mind. If he would have said "I have to go to the outside of the country to the GTT4 and wrestle Noah so people will think I was never here" instead of saying "I'm going to my casino so people won't think I'm here" then I would have easily given him the match. Salaoks 1st roleplay- One thing I like about this roleplay is how it’s a continuation of the story from before. It’s nice to see that things wont change just because this is a different round and new opponent.It would have been nice to see some sort of explanation of went on though, for those just starting to read. The roleplay gives you some background of the character which fills you in on what sort of guy this is and what his aspirations and weaknesses are. The description was great, but sometimes dragged out. Had trouble understanding just what a Harvester was, my best guess is it‘s sorta like the mafia. However, from what you learn about Salaok in this bit, you can use to apply to him in the ring through symbolism. I’ve gathered he wants to prove himself through this tournament and on his own at that. Noah’s roleplay- Nice introduction. Some technical errors, but those are easily overlookable by the ability to put yourself in Hanson’s shoes. It is clear that going into this thing, Noah is determined. One of thing he wants to prove is that big names can be beaten. In the hotel scene, the technical errors began to get a bit overwhelming. I had to re read things sometimes to understand just what I was reading. Great character development involved in it though. Informs you on just who Noah Hanson, the person, is. The strip club scene seemed to be there just to make the roleplay longer. It didn’t really add any sort of important aspects to anything, just two guys hanging out. For a GTT roleplay it seemed a little too focused on WMW. Salaoks 2nd roleplay- Though I’m not usually a big fan of defining words from the dictionary, this one actually seemed to tie in well to the roleplay, simply because it was tied off that Salaok felt that his father fit none of those definitions. Still no clue what either the lair or a harvester is. But just as before, it was really strongly written. There was a bit of a problem with tenses so I’m not sure if this is meant to have been in the past or the present, I’m just assuming the present. Didn’t really feel that the whole process of killing his father and concealing the evidence needed to be explained as it was. I feel it would have been better if it was explained as it happened, rather than “next he had do this, and then he had to do that.”. It was still a strongly written piece of work nonetheless. Winner: I feel that even though Noah only put out one piece of work as opposed to two, this was a really close match, almost neck and neck. However the second roleplay put out by Salaok gave him the extra he needed to take the win , so I’ll give Salaok the nod. The symbolism in his first roleplay was incredible, and his writing was strong throughout. And while Noah made it clear that he was focused on this match, the lack of a second roleplay simply hurt him as Salaok put out two extremely well written pieces of work. Hanson: While he's putting in more about the tournament than Salaok, this piece comes across as a token effort. Its easy to read, quite well written for what it is, but what it is isn't very deep or thought provoking. Couple of funny spots aren't enough to bring it above ordinary, which I know you are capable of doing having read some of your previous work. Salaok: Continued from the previous round, this leaps right in where the other left off, which is a mistake in that I haven't read or didn't judge the last round, so have no clue what you're on about until way too long into it. Solid, but not breathtaking, I'll take your Round 3 match if it is given to me, but would not be begging for it above all others. If Hanson had done two, or at least provided more than a very generic monalogue and a trip to a night spot, then I'd have looked at him seriously, but as it is now, you've put far more into this and therefore should be rewarded as such. Pick: Salaok. Dani Furher v John Adams are doing something very very wrong if you want to win tournament matches. Similar level of spelling mistakes and incorrect words used as Furher, but shorter more direct and concise paragraphs make it far easier to read. Pick: Adams, because of the easier to read factor. The Story of the Prophecy Part I Nice quotes. I like how you added your own after them all, it added a nice feature to it. I generally like how big events in a characters history get drawn out, but sometimes I don’t. The birth of John Adams was one of those things I was glad I didn’t have to read in detail about! “I know it’s not a birthday cake but your father refuse to bye one for you because you didn’t pick up your toys.” , talk about horrible. I think the whole father getting pissed thing was a little rushed, but it was good nevertheless. Can’t wait to read part two. The Story of the Prophecy Part II This is like one of those roleplays that reminds me of classic movies with just the bad guy against good guy plots, I like it. He got a crucifix for his birthday? Odd indeed, but I’m assuming it’s one of those neckalaces type deals. I like how the whole murder got summed up real fast. It made me think of those movies you see that end in a flash, then it has that text come across the screen that keeps you thinking for a while and makes you question things. This promo was awesome man, I mean I LITERALLY saw all of it, and for some reason Pearl Jam’s “Jeremy” popped into my head while I was reading it. Look up the lyrics and you’ll see why if you don’t know the song already. Awesome promo man, just pure awesome. Dani Furhur Freedom Bound The beginning of the promo made me think of like a freeway full of moving vehicles, then you said, “The windows to this luxury car were tented a dark shade of black, refusing to let any of beam, nor sight for that matter, to enter the interior of the vehicle. Inside we see a man, skin engaged in a hybrid mix of light Brown and Tan” I thought we couldn’t see inside though. When he found the body on the road I didn’t really have a feel of what your character was thinking, because that’s an event that should bring out every emotion in a person and I just wasn’t seeing that. The court room scene was very will written. Just on a side note, I am seeing a few spelling errors here and there. It was an alright promo overall. Torture Good visuals in the start. I got a good feel for the setting in the prison. Basically, the promo was good, I liked it alot, I mean the whole story of the lamp going out and stuff was interesting and the satan thing was ok, call me crazy but it sounded like he was trying to help you. The promo was good and all, but not enough to make up for the first one. I have to give the win to John Adams on this one. Pick: Adams Pyrenees
