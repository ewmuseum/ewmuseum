PTC Survival Update #3 - General Info.

2 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Where will Survival be held? This is to be decided. It will be in a large arena. A VERY Large Arena. Who is the commentary team? Due to them not having jobs, and essentially being free range characters now, the former commentary team from PCW will be the commentators for the event. The following will contain information how to write each character for use on the show. Dave Kern - Classic Wrestling Play by Play Man. Please, don't think of him as Jim Ross or Michael Cole. If anything, he's a Gordon Solie type. He's very knowledgeable about every aspect of wrestling, and eats, lives and breathes it. He's not afraid to make excited calls, but again, he's not going to do a Jim Ross-esque call like "STUNNER! STUNNER! STUNNER! STUNNER! BY GOD ALMIGHTY! STUNNER!" Jeff Marx - Classic Color Commentator. Again, don't think of him as a sort of Jerry Lawler. He's more of the Bobby Heenan/Curt Hennig type. He will not like any part of anything going on, and does this for the paycheck. He hates faces with a passion, and doesn't much care for heels either. He doesn't care for the current state of wrestling, and would much rather see a match from the 1950's style of slow action, instead of the fast paced action of today. Argyle - Classic Third Chair. He's the IC Hype man. He'll be hyping up the Main Event coming up, as well as biographical information of all the characters, their involvement in PTC events, how they've spent their careers, etc. Other Crew: 'Sir' Mat W.: Ring announcer. Stealing from IRL, like I tend to do, one of PTC's most outspoken people will be our ring announcer. He's announcing each member on their way to the ring, as well as the stipulation for each match, etc. Mat will be using his "Sir Mat" Persona as part of each match, meaning that he'll be rather condescending towards everyone, passing off each accomplishment like they mean nothing. Please make sure that any outside spots result with him getting his brains bashed in with a steel chair. Often. Duke Williams: Backstage Interviewer The Malice Man will bring his own unique style to the show. I'd ask that if you're going to use Duke as the person interviewing you, that you get with Al, and talk to him to get a few questions that Duke would indeed ask. AIM: [name removed] Joe Global: Backstage Interviewer He's just your regular, basic, run of the mill interviewer. He'll ask the easy questions, won't be confrentational, etc. Think of him as like a Todd Grisham-type. How will the event be written? Depsite my preference for novel style, I think that a script style will be easier for each person to write. Therefore, we're going to do a combination of the two. The following is a perfect example of how this event should be written: Wrestler A backed Wrestler B into a corner, and unleashed a flurry of fists to his head, knocking B silly. A pulled B from the corner, and irish whipped him into the opposite turnbuckle before following him in with a big splash. B came out of the corner, and fell to the ground after swinging at the air, like Ric Flair. Kern: Excellent series of moves from A there! Marx: He used a closed fist! This isn't boxing! Argyle: A comes from a fighting background. He uses his striking moves to set up his finisher, a deadly lateral press! Marx: So what? Punching is for boxing! Not Wrestling! A rolled B over, and went for the cover. 1... 2... Kickout by B! Kern: Almost a victory by B here in the opening minutes of this match! Wrestler A picked B up by the hair, pulling B to a vertical base. He lifted an arm, and ducked under B, lifting him for a suplex, and carrying him over in a classic vertical suplex. A stood afterward, and put a heavy boot to the top of B's head. A walked confidently to the ropes and climbed to the top turnbuckle. Argyle: He's setting him up for the 360º Guillotene Leg Drop! Kern: If he hits this, this match is over! Marx: THe match is already over. No one cares! Booo! Booo! Punchy McPuncherson should have been DQed minutes ago! Wrestler A jumps off the top, and hits the leg drop across the throat of B. He reaches down and hooks B's leg. Kern: THERE IT IS! THE LATERAL PRESS! THE MOST FEARED MOVE IN WRESTLING HISTORY! Fans: ONE! TWO! THREE! Angles You're welcome to play off any angles on TV. For instance: If there's a certain team you want to call out, do so. If there's a certain match you'd like to set up with a backstage interview, do so. If you're friends with people on other teams, feel free to do something with them. Understand though, that you need to make each team come out on top with your comments. There should be no backstage attacks, PERIOD. The threat that it could happen is one thing. The actual attack itself? Not allowed. Deadlines Deadline to name your team for Survival Main Event: July 29th at 12AM EST Beginning of Roleplaying: August 1st, 12:01AM EST End of Roleplaying: August 15th, 11:59AM EST - With two weeks, there will be ABSOLUTELY NO EXTENTIONS. Don't even bother asking. You will not get it. Judging Deadline: August 17th, 11:59PM EST - This will be so matchwriters can get a winner for each match ASAP, so they can finish their matches. Segment Deadline: August 20th, 11:59PM EST - There will be no extensions, and this can be in as early as possible. Match Writing Deadline: August 21st 11:59PM EST - Again, no extensions. You can start your matches as early as possible, with the exception of the Main Events. You'll simply need to add in a winner and finish the match by this point. Show Posting: August 22nd at 11AM EST - If your match is late, you will not be a part of the show, regardless. The matchwriter will not be welcomed to participate in future PTC events. If you're asking to write a match for this show, it should be your #1 priority to get it in on time. The show will be posted as the day goes on, with each match and segments between each match being posted throughout the day. For instance: Opening Segment: Posted at 11AM. Second Segment: Posted at 12PM. Third Segment: Posted at 1PM. Fourth Segment: Posted at 2PM Fifth Segment: Posted at 3PM Sixth Segment: Posted at 4PM Main Event Finale: Posted at 5PM. This way, there's more reason to read more and more of the show. Clever, eh? I thought so. These deadlines will be STRICTLY adhered to. Questions? Comments? Post Below!
