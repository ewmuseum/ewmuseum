Beginner: Simple Backrounds - Jay Sylo

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A plain background that does not look plain: a flood fill, brush strokes and a motion blur. Now there are ways to make a simple back ground by just flood filling the image using the paint bucket but here I will teach you to make a back ground that doesn't look too plain and also has a stylish appeal. -Now to practice open a 300x300 image (pixels); resolution: 72.000; Back ground color: Transparent; Image type: 16.7 million colors (24 bit). -Now once your image is open click on the paint bucket and set your color plain (the little black arrow on the texture area to the right of your screen is what your looking at, click it and change it to a whole color if it's not already like that.) Go ahead and choose any color you want...if you would like to follow a long I chose a light blue. -Now flood fill the image. Simple enough right? Ok here comes the next step. -Click on the paint brush. At the top on your bar (if you have opened it if not click view>>toolbars>> and select most of em.) there is a little box with a blue bar on top, white back ground and what appears to be a rectangle and a little rail road track. Click that and it brings up the tool options menu. -Set the size of your brush to 3; hardness: 0, Opacity: 100, Step: 25; Density; 100; build up brush: NOT CHECKED. -Now pick a color of your choosing, me I'm using white and begin to make a diagonal motion from left to right (top corner to bottom corner) until you have marked all over the image (remember to leave some of the color showing) -Now go to Effects>>Blur>>Motion blur. -After the box pops open you want it to look a little something like this... Now if you have done it right you should have something that sorta looks like this.... Simple for beginners yet not bad looking. Enjoy!
