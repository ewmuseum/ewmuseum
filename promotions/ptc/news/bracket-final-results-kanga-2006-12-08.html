Bracket Final Results - Kanga

8 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE SENTINEL V THE PHANTOM REPUBLICAN Judge 1 Alright I want to start off by saying that I thought all four pieces for this round were very well written and I enjoyed reading each and every one of them. It was a very tough call but I am going to have to go with The Phantom Republican for the following reasons. Just note that The Sentinel did a wonderful job and really made this a very tough decision. Phantom Republican- Great job with both of your pieces. I really enjoyed seeing the reaction of Gordon to the news that his father was gay and the second piece where he learned a lesson about how sometimes when you speak up, even if the person you're dealing with knows your right, that it has possible consequences. I enjoyed it and found myself geninunely engrossed in the story. Pick: The Phantom Republican Judge 2 4 very well written pieces. It's easy to see how both of you have gotten to this stage of the tournament. Sentinel: I like Wolfgangs character, you explore deep issues with him and portray his anguish in a realistic and meaningful way. Not as impressed with the 2 female characters however. Came across as a bit 2 dimensional in these. Story started off well, but I had a lot of trouble with the idea that Abigail would give her child up to a woman she hated. Phantom Republican: Enjoyed your easy going style of writing and dialogue. You kept me entertained throughout and provided a few laughs. Got a great sense of what Gordon is all about from his reactions to the various situations you put him in. I was very impressed with your first RP was a little disappointed that second turned out to be a completely different story to the first. Pick: Phantom Republican. Judge 3 Sentinel 1 The first scene is good, if a little melodramatic. We get to see a lot about Wolfgang and his motivations. It also draws us into the second section with his daughter. The dialogue in that part was a little forced and didn't seem to flow that naturally... but then again it's not a 'natural' scene, I guess. The line about the coldness being in Karla's heart was a little corny. I like the idea, but it would probably have been best introduced with a couple of lines instead of the little throw-away that made me groan a little. The scene as a whole with the guard didn't play out as well as I'd hoped either. The characters both seemed a little flat, and not as rounded as I know at least one of them is. I read the previous round for Sentinel, and enjoyed it a lot, but this doesn't seem to be quite as good. The last scene saves it though, as the emotions kick back in and Karla ensnares Angela. That was definitely the best bit of the roleplay. Phantom Republican 1 This reads very, very easily. There are occasional slips with words, but overall it just flows excellently and really gets me involved quickly. TPR comes across as quite the character straight from the very start and the light humour throughout it works very well with me. The bit about robbers cracked a proper smile from me. I am confused by how old Gordon is supposed to be. I thought he was very young, hence the college thing mentioned earlier, but then he says he hadn't seen his Dad for 15 years, since his High School Graduation, which would place him a lot older than that. Colour me confused. A nice ending though. It's a pleasant little story that I enjoyed reading. Not many people write the light hearted emotional comedy stuff these days, and I think that when you get a well written piece of it, such as this, it's a real refreshing change. Phantom Republican 2 Starts off a little slow, but I like how Gordon is being introduced more and more, with information about all sorts of sides of his personality. He comes across like a naïve little dynamo; easily led, but once you point him in the direction you want, look at him go! As it goes on, I dunno, I'm just not buying into this piece as much as I did the previous one. It's nicely written and all, but I don't think it has the necessary 'oomph' for me. I could tell that he was going to get an F from miles away, and wanted to see his reaction and what the teacher would say, and maybe even a retort from Gordon, but it just stopped as I was finally getting interested. As I said though, it was nicely written and you have a good style that differs from a lot of writing out there, and I like that. Sentinel 2 I like how things are gathering pace now. We definitely seem to be on track for a crescendo. Unfortunately, there are a bunch of things that just don't sit quite right with me. Angela seems to accept that Karla is her mother very easily, and won't even listen to her father. I know she's a rebellious teenager and she distrusts Wolfgang, but to suddenly grow attached to someone who is essentially a stranger in such a way strikes an off chord. Wouldn't Angela even suspect she's being lied to? The flashback scene fills in the blanks for those unfamiliar with the story, which I like, and reminds me of what's going on. I think Abigail (careful when choosing character names that are similar, on at least one occasion you said Abigail when you meant Angela) gives in too easily to Karla about giving up the baby, and Karla's rant about how Abi isn't even a real woman seemed a little melodramatic. Also, Wolfgang looks uber-dumb when he admits that his wife was incapable of having children, and doesn't think it is important, even when his daughter is stood in front of him, asking who her mother really is... ummm, Wolfgang, she's asking you who her mother is, you fool! But enough of my nit-picking on plot devices. The writing of the exchanges is very good, and the twists and turns towards the end are also very good. Angela overcomes one of my above misgivings, proving me wrong in a good way by getting pissed with both of them and running off on her own. Karla wins, in a way, and we're left wondering what will happen in the next installment of the story. And, I do want to know, which is always a good sign. DECISION TIME: The Phantom Republican had two solid pieces, though one was (IMO) clearly better than the other. The Sentinel offered a very different style of writing, and veered from moments of excellence to at times leaving me feeling that there wasn't quite enough depth or realism... humanity maybe to some of the characters in the world. We're given glimpses of why people are acting like they did, but not enough for my liking. I'd like to maybe have heard a bit more about who Cassidy is, and what the purpose of the Seconda Vita malarky is (here it focused only on it being used to lure Angela, whereas I am sure it is a lot more). The Sentinel, though, offers me a greater incentive to put him through based on wondering what happens next. For all the misgivings I have with it, I really do want to see what happens to Karla, Angela and Wolfgang (probably in that order, too). TPR was almost writing parables or similar, moral lessons in the world of a young masked Republican. The Superman of the Senate, if you will. So, it's a very close decision, and both have done very well, and would be worthy champions of the bracket. I'm going to go with The Phantom Republican for the win. I think his style offers something different in a good way, and I think that his easy tone won me over just a little more than the drama of The Sentinel's offering. Good writing, peoples! Pick – The Phantom Republican. Result The Phantom Republican d The Sentinel, 3 votes to 0 Bracket Judges Dean Maria Andy Bracket Champion The Phantom Republican Kanga
