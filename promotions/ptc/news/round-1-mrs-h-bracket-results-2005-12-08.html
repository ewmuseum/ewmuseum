Round 1: Mrs H. Bracket Results

8 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BRIAN ARSON V CHRISTIAN MONTIERO This can’t be the end by Brian Arson: I've never read Arson before, and I wish I had. The opening made me laugh in a strange kind of way. I really liked the thought of him retreating to a family scenario, and it allowed for plenty of interesting things to happen, which they did! The way he interweaved the plot driven story with his thoughts on the situations and tournament was also very nicely done. Very nice piece of writing indeed. We’re off to Tampa Bay! by Christian Montiero: Again, I've never really read Montiero. I liked the intro, and I found myself chuckling along to some of the events taking place. Particularly enjoyed the thought of the russian running out with the keys with a half naked bum behind him! While the story was nice, the bit towards the end seemed like it was tacked on though. My only criticism in an otherwise fine piece. Result: Brian Arson d. Christian Montiero Tough choice, both men could go through, but I empathised more with Arson's, and because of that I found it a more enjoyable read. Hard luck to Montiero though, you could have beaten a great deal of people with a better draw. SEAN GALEN V JAYCE BRADLEY 'Last of a dying breed' by Sean Galen It started off with a bunch of action, a shoot out! A great start and it had me wanting to hear more. He didn't arise the winner, which you don't see happen too often in written stories. It transitions to a bar scene which confused me that there was no answer to what originally happened, however he did state it was just the beginning of the tale. There was a little too much left open ended by the end of the bar scene however, and it didn't seem to tell me much. 'Just Another Night' by Jayce Bradley The beginning seemed to be a little spotty, hard to tell what was going on completely. But once the story started it had me pretty interested. He told a brief background of the character and his entourage, Braun. He showed the characters wrestling and real life sides, though the story could've used a little more historic information. Result: Jayce Bradley d Sean Galen He wrote enough to get by this round, and hopefully picks up the pieces to continue on his reign to the next round. THE UBER JUDGE V PADDY O'SHEA Being the Man that you are by Paddy O'Shea A bit of a rollercoaster, this short story took me through all kinds of emotions. It had comedy and drama. It was poignant at key times, and the level of description within the backstory was excellent. I really found myself wanting to see what happens, along many different sub-plots. There was one section (The very detailed description of how to get to his home town) which was a little unnecessary, but other than that I thoroughly enjoyed it. The whole piece really geared around Paddy's complete over-powering feeling that this was his last chance saloon, and he displayed it well. Well done. The Three and One by The Uber Judge A very thought provoking and interesting piece of writing. Raised many points and really made me feel like the Uber Judge did have this incredible power of persuasion simply by reading it. The way he wrapped it altogether too was very nice. I really enjoyed the way he highlighted some of society's "Flaws" shall we say, and it all culminated nicely. Result: Paddy O'Shea d. Uber Judge Two nice pieces, both totally different. But Paddy's takes it for me. I found myself gripped in the plot, while with UJ's whilst it was very well written, I found a little difficult to follow. BUTTERFLY HAMADA V DOREEN PEPPERPOT The Test of the Curse by Butterfly Hamada: Very nice piece. About 3/4s through I wondered where the relevance was. But towards the end, it came together. A story where two separate events are bound together by destiny. Nice stuff, and I'm looking forward to the next installment. Size Matters by Doreen Pepperpot: Refreshing to have a female wrestler who isn't a barbie doll clone, this roleplay really showed the kind of emotional struggles that Doreen as to go through. Its the emotion drew me into the story and really made me care for the character. Result: Doreen Pepperpot d. Butterfly Hamada Pepperpots touching tale was a refreshing read. PETE THE PG TIPS CHIMP V JOHN THE PROPHET ADAMS In Search of a Chimp by Pete the PG Tips Chimp Bwhahahaha... Pete, I'll get back to you when I'm done laughing. Colour me well impressed. GTT5 and Monkey Business! by John "the Prophet" Adams John, there is nothing wrong with your piece, but I'm sorry to have to tell you, you got out written by a chimp. Where your piece was solid, his shone. Result: Pete the PG Tips Chimp the win. A talented writer, I hope the chimp can maintain this standard throughout the competition and not get lazy or run out of ideas. DALLAS REEVES V MITCH MITCHUM Baaaaad Beastor by Dallas Reeves A lightweight tale of getting the band back together. Mitch Match Fiesta I don't really get the point of this. Is there one? Dallas Reeves d Mitch Mitchum I'm not familiar with Mitchum. I know Reeves has written loads better than this. Best thing I can say here is don't deadline hug next round, or you will very likely get squashed... hard. EVAN JACKSON V BUDDY KINGFISHER Flight by Evan Jackson Easy to read. Social commentary and referencing your opponent without resorting to out and out trash talking. It may be well written, but it does nothing at all to tickle me for the next round. What GTT means by Buddy Kingfisher This is perfectly fine, for what it is....but what it is is just like an in-ring, but without the ring and the dumb blonde interviewer holding a mic in front of your face and prompting you with questions. Evan Jackson d Buddy Kingfisher Jackson takes this one with a better choice of material. Not much in it when it comes to the actual writing. JACK MURPHY V SURREAL SurReal- The way of the Samurai An excellent written piece which flowed very well except for one place where there was a disagreement in words which made me read the same sentence two or three times and reword it in my head. The piece was a tad bit short for my likings but had the substance it needed. SurReal brought some history and where he had been in the last months since he was last seen in this piece. I enjoyed this piece but saw it more as a character development piece then a full blown competition piece. Still very enjoyable to read. Jack Murphy - For Better or Worse A very interesting piece where the author explored an option I have not seen often in which the wrestler is going after another career idea. He was looking to expand his career from wrestling to acting like many professionals are currently doing. The piece overall had exceptional description in fact even some places went a little over board on some description that was not needed to further the piece. This left Jack with something to continue on with leaving me wanting more but not leaving me with questions. Result: Jack Murphy d SurReal I found both pieces very well written but decided to choose Jack Murphy, I found his piece to be a little more thought provoking and interesting while SurReal seemed to be a character development piece. The only thing I want to tell Jack is that in future rounds pay attention to description as sometimes he goes over board on subjects that doesn't matter to the overall piece. Great job by both guys. MAGGOT V VIRGIL AMBROSI Deep in Thought by Maggot I quite like your writing style, but I have to say that I don't know how its going to stack up against something with more depth. It seems a bit odd that you'd get in so early with something short and with not a lot of oomph, in a one shot round. The style of this RP seems more suited to an angle fed, where you would post shorter RP's to complement an incard segment. The Search Begins by Virgil Ambrosi Much advantage gained by the fact that I read Shadow's SS work, so know exactly what's going on here. Having done that, this makes perfect sense, and is quite engaging, whereas without that I would have been in trouble finding a reason to care about it, because it would have been quite meaningless, much like picking up a novel off the shelf and reading one of the middle chapters first. This is the thing that may come back to bite you in later rounds, depending on which judges you get and how you play it. Result: Ambrosi d Maggot AMY SILVEIRA V JASON WARR My Return to Hell by Amy Silveira: Interesting scenario - A girl being forced into wrestling by her father, so that was completely unexpected right off the bat. It was nicely written, even if in stages I wanted to scream "For fuck sake, tell him where to stick it!" But I guess that was kind of the point. Good intro to the character(s) and highlighted the backstory well. One criticism is she was decidedly english for an american, if that makes sense, but other then that, I enjoyed it. Prom Night by Jason Warr: A fantastic self contained story which contained so much emotion, linked everything together well and had me gripped throughout. I loved the way the look in the eyes related to that story. I just really, thoroughly enjoyed it. Result: Jason Warr d Amy Silveira Silveira's was good. Warr's was great. Really enjoyed it. ANGELO DEVILLE V RICH ROLLINS Conscience by Angelo Deville One particular statement, “A parlor trick to amuse the masses”, reminded me very strongly of wanting to strangle both you and Mat with a rusty piece of barbed wire all that long time ago for whoring out the brand spanking new Universal Title on that angled match in PCW. Anyway, it almost distracted me from what you were leading up to. I get leery when I bump into people using other people’s retired characters, as too many people have done it because they don’t have anything else in the tank at the time, myself included at one point. Props to you for completely rewriting the rule book on that one. A divine tragedy by Rich Rollins I didn’t think you’d ever top “that” Infinite Gauntlet RP… but my elevated heart rate and light headedness from holding my breath tell me otherwise. When I say to people I want more depth in what they are writing, this is it, in spades. Winner: Rich Rollins d Angelo Deville Jeff showed us again why he is and will always be the man, and leaves with no shame in this loss whatsoever. His rendition of doubt over the legitimacy of his place in the business leading into giving the middle finger to tradition (and the poor starving kiddies) was simply beautiful. Under the circumstances, posting so far before the deadline showed a great deal of courage and faith in his own ability. Andy should feel very proud of this effort. I feel quite humble that it was posted in my tournament. Thank you. Props to both gents in a match worthy of being the final round. If both of you are amenable and Andy still has the PTC Global Title after his current commitments with it, I’d be very interested in holding a rematch, this time with the title on the line as you both wanted to for this match. Consider this one the feeling out process, if you will.
