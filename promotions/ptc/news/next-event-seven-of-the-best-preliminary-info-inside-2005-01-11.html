Next Event: Seven of the Best - preliminary info inside

11 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rather than wallow in self pity over Survival's dismal failure any longer, its time for me move on with things for 2005. Seven of the Best... designed when we had 14 feds :D - The idea is that each fed nominate one representative. - That person gets paired in a singles match against another fed's rep, the matchups are decided by me. - The RP limit for the matches will be three, per usual Global Title rules. - There will be written matches, assuming I can get volunteers at the time. They are a lot smaller than Survival, so it shouldn't be an organisational nightmare of a problem. - Three judges per match, no long drawn out comments, its a competition, not a feedback party. I'll be looking at judges who put in for GTT4 on a regular basis. No judging of people in the same fed or known friends. - The winners of the seven matches go on to compete in a battle royal. I am undecided yet as to whether this needs a futher roleplaying period or whether it will be lumped in with the initial lot. - The winner of the battle royal receives a future global title shot against the current champion. Ideally this will be the March Global Title match - the February one is already locked and loaded to go once the Almasy vs LKK match is out of the way. In essence, it is making the nominations process of last year a public thing, instead of having a bunch of faceless people read and evaluate stuff that nobody can see what they evaluated and nobody knows who did what, so its all of course a huge conspiracy ;) Which is not to say that nominations won't return in future, but trying different things is good, no? The event will have a relatively simple website, I'll pretty much use the same scripting as I used for GTT4, although its had a lot of work done to it since then. Current ETA for this will be February, so this is notice to consider which of your guys would be suitable to garner your feds much publicity by being victorious :D Consider that date locked in stone when you see it on the public announcements forum, not before. When I ask, I'll need a quick response, rather than dragging it out for weeks waiting, so if you talk to members on AIM, ask them now rather than in a month's time. If we don't have 14 feds at the time, then it'll be 6OTB... or something that makes the numbers fit. It's no biggie. Questions, comments, feedback welcome. Cheers! Posted on the PTC forum by Argyle.
