CW2 Round 1 Results - Baker Bracket

19 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alexander Willow (FA) d Juri Hyobanshi (FA) 2 votes to 1 El Serpenti (Global) d Skylar Kelly (WMW) 2 votes to 0 Tsunami Oni (UWF) d Constantino Zergolov (FA) 3 votes to 0 Javier Mendez d Horazon Round 2 matchups Alexander Willow (FA) v Javier Mendez (FA) El Serpenti (Global) v Tsunami Oni (UWF) Judging Comments Willow v Hyobanshi Judge 1 Alexander Willow OVERALL: It’s clear that you are a very good writer, and I enjoyed this piece, yet it seemed more like a set-up to something bigger and better rather than a stand-alone story. Too many scene changes led to a feeling that the writing had been somewhat rushed, and each scene seemed to focus a little too strongly on reaching its cliffhanger in the smallest possible number of words. - A few bits that didn’t quite seem to make sense – ‘He had promised. Now he was going to keep it’ – I know what you were getting at here, but you didn’t phrase it quite as well as you should have, and this interrupted the excellent rhythm your writing was getting into early on. Strong writing, but you did seem to descend into a sea of confused clichés – reminding me to an extent of my own writing. The use of metaphor is good, but tone it down a touch. Brilliant use of language with the death in the opening – using the word ‘beautiful’ to describe a suicidal shot to the head is not at all typical, and you pulled it off very well indeed. Spelling, grammar and punctuation generally very good, although there were a few errors. Good characterisation with a variety of different characters. Strong set-up with a number of different scenes. Unfortunately, it felt too fast-moving – more like a TV show than a piece of writing. I would have preferred fewer, longer scenes. The story seemed ever so slightly rushed. Usually in such a story, one would see at least some investigation of some sort taking place before the breakthrough, which you hit upon too suddenly. I appreciate that this was a long piece of writing ‘as is’, but you could have done with some more preamble to the breakthrough. The scene of the meeting between Alexander and Christina was very strong indeed. Juri Hyobanshi OVERALL: I really enjoyed your portrayal of a school community, with an outsider seemingly dogged by terror of some kind of monster. Although I felt you introduced too many new characters in too short a time, your characterisation was very good and your style of writing exemplary. The ending was a little abrupt, but sufficient to achieve its end. - Good detail in your description of seemingly minor things made your writing feel complete. ‘Miles of street rushed from underneath his treaded soles’ – very good imagery here. Unfortunate repetitions of certain words – while these words were still appropriate, I generally regard it as poor to use the same word twice in a sentence of creative writing. In particular I am referring to: ‘In his panic shopping bags were knocked from hands, held hands broken, and people knocked to the ground like unbalanced pins’ Good characterisation, as for Willow. Spelling, grammar and punctuation – practically perfect. On a few occasions, you should really have used a semi-colon rather than a comma, but I can’t fault you here. Strong portrayal of the group of students. I’m afraid I have to turn my nose up at your constant use of capital letters within Kawaii’s speech. I understand what you were trying to do here, yet you took it too far. And capital letters annoy me... ‘an intellectual tone’ I’m sorry, but I am not sure such a tone exists. You should have described this further, rather than assuming that the reader shares your idea of what an ‘intellectual tone’ sounds like. The short character introductions on the right hand side helped differentiate between the many characters you used. Without them, I would have been lost. I think the number of characters was just a little too high. Excellent ideas came across in your italicised paragraphs – I am greatly intrigued. Good portrayal of danger and fear in the latter part of the piece. The decision WHY did I get such a tough match? This is one of the hardest decisions I’ve made in e-wrestling... but I’m going with Juri Hyobanshi. Pick: Juri Hyobanshi Judge 2 Juri’s handler is obviously a great writer. The story was very interesting and kept me reading until the end, but it was way too confusing. Too many characters, too many changes in style and a convoluted although interesting story made it lose some of it’s shine. I know that this is one of the biggest discussions around e-wrestling, but unless I am missing something, I didn’t see Juri in the rp at all. This is a wrestling tournament and Juri Hyobanshi entered it, but I didn’t see Juri at all in the rp nor mention of her. Maybe he was waiting to give another role on the series to Juri, but I felt her absence was a deterrent to the rp. Willow’s rp was very entertaining. He gave us background on the character(I had never read him before), he explained why his character was wrestling and still was able to create an interesting story that was not hard to read. I loved the way the pace of the rp seemed, the scenes seemed to fly and make this rp more suspenseful. Great rps by both handlers. I would love to see both of you move on to the next round, but this cannot happen. This was a very hard match to judge. In the end I decided that Willow’s rp was just a little bit more entertaining and not as convoluted with information as Juri’s rp. I felt that the absence of Juri in the rp made the rp seem a little less important. In this case I prefer the fast paced action to the slow paced story with too many characters to remember. Pick: Alexander Willow Judge 3 Willow v Hyobanshi - Rage Alexander Willow- A well written and entertaining roleplay. Spelling and grammar are important for me, and Willow nailed it. The story, it seems, lost me at some parts, and myabe it was a little too short...but the action, dialogue and overall feel is very appealing. Juri Hyobanshi- First of all, "When someone writes a story and stops it before it’s completion, the world that story exists in ends," is a great line. lol. Anyway, the roleplay was enjoyable. Well-written, some good sequences. However, I am confused by the storyline. The proression was quirky and the point of the roleplay -besides to entertain- is beyond me. Too many points where I was like, "what just happened?" or "how does this effect the plot?" There where too many transitions from one small section to the next, a little awkward to read. So...I pick Willow...narrowly. I can see, however, how it could easily go either way. Easily. Pick: Alexander Willow El Serpenti v Skylar Kelly Judge 1 El Serpenti- The story begins with Serpenti feeling a broken soul, monetary wealth does not seem to be the problem or the issue, alas it is a woman. (We can all relate with that.) Ooh a nice use of sympathetic weather syndrome with the rolling thunder and the wind rising, it would have been nicer without telling us of the impending doom, allowing us to make our own decision. A little more care with spelling could have been useful, I don’t think there will be many ships at the end of a pie J. As the story continued it started to take on a distinctive Mulan type feel, at the moment I’m not sure if this is a good thing. Other than the Pie thing the piece was well written although I felt that something more could have been used to link the separate scenes together, they never really had a link between them, resulting in the feel of the rp becoming disjointed however it was still entertaining and gave an interesting insight into a new character (for me as a reader) as well as explaining his feelings and having a distinctive human feeling about him, which is something I look for, a connection. Skylar Kelly- Ah a nice little quote to begin with, I like this sort of thing, it sets up roleplay’s quite nicely. We open with a nice internal monologue going over the things, which were playing on his mind, interlinking it with the opening quote. A few spelling errors which caused re-reading at first. As the rp progressed I felt that I lacked substance and did not make itself stand out. The grammar also degraded through the rp, making it a lot harder to read. El Serpenti's rp simply wasn’t as one dimensional as I felt Kelly’s was which was a very standard looking into camera talking promo. Pick: El Serpenti Judge 2 El Serpenti- Short, quirky, cute. I enjoyed it. A basic storyline, a few twists, and overall decently written. A few grammatical problems. For example, these sentences which certainly are deserving of a "?" or two at the end: “But why. What harm can there be If I knew who sent you.” Nonetheless, solid. Skylar Kelly- Cliche. Right off the bat, quoting philosophy...which is fine if you expand on it, but otherwise...er. Though it wasn't set in an arena, it felt like an in-ring promo. Too much trash talk -and by that I mean overused and redundant trashtalk. Needs to have an actual plot and purpose. Pick: El Serpenti Tsunami Oni vs Constantino Zergolov Tsunami Oni OVERALL: An entertaining, albeit somewhat hectic, read. The non-stop comedy was spoiled a little by a large number of typos – spell-check and proof-read, please. - Very strong narrative with a personal feel. Various lapses between past and present tenses did annoy me. Spelling, punctuation and grammar were quite good – one or two lapses; ‘hear’ instead of ‘here’ at one point almost made me projectile-vomit but other than that you were OK. Frequent typos would have been eliminated if you had proof-read your work properly. Some parts made me laugh out loud – ‘“Shhh… you’re breaking kayfabe,” Oni warns.’ For all your comedic strengths, you appear less able with handling emotion. You didn’t quite capture the reader with Oni’s seemingly-monotonic paragraph about how wrestling is ‘his life’, and did nothing to distance your character from the dozens of others who use the same approach. There didn’t appear to be a reason for Shiatsu being about to cry; not that the reader could understand, anyway. The ‘Thug Roc/Thug roC’ scenario made me chuckle – I won’t penalise you for your BLATANT rip-off of my very own ‘Woof Woof/woof woof’ debate... Constantino Zergolov OVERALL: While well-written, the roleplay ended far too abruptly. The character development was limited and unsustained. We didn’t find out how Constantino got a spot, nor how he knew it was against Tsunami Oni. It seemed like you simply ran out of time with this roleplay and were planning to write a lot more. - ‘another wise monotone apartment’ Well, this made me chuckle... unfortunately, I highly doubt that it was intentional. While your writing skills were generally good, you seemed to overuse certain words, such as the verb ‘to question’, which made frequent appearances in the opening portion of the piece. The tension between Constantino and Igor over Igor’s singles accolades was worked well. Pick: Tsunami Oni Judge 2 What can I say? I was a little disappointed with both rps. I have read other stuff from Oni and I have to say that this rp was a bit lacking. Oni reminded me of whiny heel Edge and that is not good. I liked the whole idea of his friends fighting for him but I think that for the next rps we need more Oni and less everyone else. Oni rules the show and should be in in the forefront of the rps. Zegolov’s rp was very disappointing. I felt like the story of the tag team in which one member overshadows the other is too clichéd and didn’t make me feel anything special for Zergolov. It seemed rushed and done only for the sake of no showing. I was looking forward to this one, but I guess I’ll have to wait for something better next time. This one goes to Oni simply because his rp was a little bit more entertaining. Pick: Tsunami Oni Judge 3 Tsunami Oni- To begin with reading this I was somewhat disappointed the rp, seemed to be all over the place, until the end when it all came together and I realised exactly how intelligent a writer Oni’s handler actually is. What appeared to be lack luster actually developed into something quite special. An excellent story is already in the works. Zergolov- This was a nice mix of personal feeling and dialogue, at times the description could have been better but it still flowed nicely. The general story line though was somewhat clichéd and has been done a number of times, a tag partner wanting to get out of the shadow of the other one. This one was a lot closer than I would have initially thought at receiving the judging. Zergolov’s story was well written and solid, however Oni had an extremely entertaining and inventive story, which still remained true to the tournament. It involved personal strife and a quest for retribution Pick:- Tsunami Oni. Mendez Since Horazon no showed and I still read Javier’s rp I decided to write something about it. Okay, simply put, this was a wonderful rp. It was probably my favorite out of the ones I have read so far. I could follow the storyline flawlessly and I felt that there were no dull moments in it. Huge props to you man and I look forward to reading your next rps. CW2 brackets
