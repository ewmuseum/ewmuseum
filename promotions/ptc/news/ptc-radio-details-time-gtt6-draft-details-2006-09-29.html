PTC Radio Details: Time, GTT6 Draft details

29 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, I said "draft". Radio time 7.00pm Saturday Night PST. 2.00am UTC/GMT Sunday morning. 12.00pm Sunday Lunch Time AEST. Radio Details http://radio.primetimecentral.net When the time comes, click on the dialup or broadband links, whichever suits. You will need one of the software programs listed. I prefer Apple Quicktime, as I've found it is the least laggy. If you've not listened to streaming radio before and your computer doesn't know what a .pls file is, save it to your local PC and run it with your chosen audio program directly. There is sometimes a lag between the dialup and the broadband links, and a lag between different people. If you are in chat and you read stuff before you hear it and this bothers you, I can't do anything about that, you'll have to leave the chat room and just listen, or suck it up. Chat Details Join in the official chat on the PTC Community Forums chat room. The Draft The first phase of this year's GTT draw will be determined by the use of a draft mechanism. The drafters will be Pete, RJ and Lindz. The draft MC will be me. They will each be responsible for populating two brackets in GTT6, one name at a time. They will be able to talk a bit about what they know about that character, or read some info off the application if they don't know anything. We wouldn't want the draft to be over with in 10 minutes, would we? Each bracket will have 16 entrants. Okay, I know who the two PTC admins are, but who's this Lindz person? Lindz posts here as "QueenOfTheRing". We have a large number of applicants from the FWrestling/CSWA neck of the woods this year. Lindz is providing them with representation, as there's a fair chance that the PTC guys in the draft would be less than familiar with a lot of them. Thanks to her for helping out with that. Won't that put them all in the one bracket? Maybe. Have to wait and see. Just sit back and enjoy it rather than worrying about these things. The Matchups The matchups will be determined by the randomizer after the draft has been completed, and announced on the GTT6 website. The Guaranteed Spots? Those with guaranteed spots will be drafted first. Everyone else from the total application pool will be drafted after that. We have more than 96 applications now, so some will miss out. Order of application does not matter. But what of the people in fed EXUWFA? We are new and the drafters don't know us. This is true. Get on the intro forum and introduce yourself, and respond to some of the threads in the GTT6 forum, and get your name out there. How can I do that if you guys haven't validated my new forum signup? I'll validate all the outstanding signups when I'm done making this post. After that, its up to you. What of the Global Title Shots? At this current time, Seymour Almasy has elected for his fate to be determined by the randomizer, rather than picking anyone, as he won the right to do in IG. He will be in the draft, so one of the three drafters will have some control over who gets the Global Title shot in Round 1. Be nice to them, and be nice to Sean if you want to get him to change his mind. He takes flowers and cash as bribes, but will probably pick you for the shot if you agree to write his next 6 months fed work for him so he can focus on PTC competition (BURN***) Its Radio, will you do callers? Probably not during the draft, but I'm willing to take some afterwards. I'm also willing to play prerecorded adverts from PTC member feds for their forthcoming events, hypes for your characters and whatnot. If you can throw something together in MP3 format and get it to me before air time, I'll likely play it. Anything else? There's been a lot of discussion about judging recently. I'll be talking about that, and disclosing some of the reforms we have in mind aimed at restoring confidence in how we do things and to help keep GTT6 running as smoothly as possible. Will you play that clip Pete did making fun of Noah Hanson again? You betcha. What did you forget to mention that you are now editing in? 1. I'll also be revealing what the pre-Round 1 no-show eliminator will be. Last year's was the hype stuff. Any other questions?
