Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Huge thanks to all the Round 1 judges for their work. This could have dragged on for several more days, but everyone put in and delivered way before time. Round 2 brackets will be up in a couple of hours. I have some things to attend to at home first and need to give the people I've invited to be Round 2 playins a bit more time to respond before I allocate the last few spots. For the ones that got invited, its the quick and the dead I'm afraid, if you're too late getting back to me, such is life. Speaking of Playins, there are 8 spots, and I've invited 8 people to fill those spots, however if they decline or don't reply by the time I'm done with what I need to do at home, I'll look at taking others from anyone who replies to this thread and expresses interest in one of the spots. No promises, no guarantees, just the possiblity. Brackets
