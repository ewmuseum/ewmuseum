Forthcoming Cruiserweight Tournament

13 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Due to significant enough interest in the signup thread, this tournament will go ahead. I will close off signups this time next week. Signups here: http://forums.primetimecentral.net/index.p...?showtopic=6243 Known facts: - The final size of the tournament will depend on two things - number of applicants and number of available judges. - Maximum weight per entrant is 225 pounds. If your wrestler is a bit over that, make him/her go on a diet :nasty: (credit: Jack) - Two roleplays per round, any style. - Second round playins will be allowed where both opponents no-showed for the first round. Preference will be given to those who lost their match on a split decision. If there are more of those than there are places, then I will select at random from those. If there are more places than those, then I will select at random from the straight sets losers, with preference given to those who posted both roleplays. If that doesn't cover it, then I'll work out additional means of selecting playins then. - Prize: At the very minimum one of those forum award trophys. If the turnout is good and the tournament was well received, then I'll splash out and buy the winner a custom poser set as well. If it bombs then I'm not spending money. The tricky thing is picking the poser artist, because there are so many good ones to choose from who hang out here, perhaps I'll let the winner decide that. - Sandbagging is not allowed. Your first roleplay must be posted a minium 24 hours before your second. Not 18 or 12 or anything else that falls outside this figure. Unknown facts: - Precise start date. Estimate is 2-3 weeks. - Precise Roleplay deadline. It will be on the weekends of each round. I'll look into getting some sort of count-down clock mechism put up somewhere so that people don't have to niggle over time zones on the last day. There will be two clocks - one for first Roleplay, one for Second, to help enforce the sandbagging rule. Judges: - I need volunteers for judges. Please send me an e-mail at [email removed] to register your interest in doing this. You will be required to read the roleplays, select a winner and write a paragraph why you made your selection. Depending upon the ultimate size of each bracket, you will have 2 or 3 days to do this. If you are involved in rating a fed in the ratings program, I suggest not volunteering at this point unless you have a lot of time on your hands, as the Ratings will be debuting very soon. - If you are playing, you cannot judge. If you are eliminated, you are welcome and encouraged to volunteer to judge subsequent rounds (think of it as someone judged your match, so give a little back to the system). Finals: - I am happy for a fed to host the final round as a match, similar to the way that the Divisions are currently running. Would need a matchwriter and a host fed for that. Preference will be given to a member fed who has not recently hosted any PTC match, however if nobody else wants to do it then by all means. If you are a fed head and want to do this, send me a PM complete with the name and contact details of your matchwriter. I have certain hype expectations, so only volunteer if you are prepared to write a few news posts here and there to help promote this, your fed will benefit also. Non-Member fed entrants: - If you aren't a member of a PTC fed, you are allowed to compete, however you may find yourself in triple threat match in the first round with other non-member players in order to fit you in. Non-member players will be the first to be bumped if the numbers don't quite fit, with preference given on a first in first served basis. What will stop this from going ahead: - Not enough judges signing up. FAQ: Q. Will the winner of this end up being the first CTD champion? A. No, however you never know. I'm not sacrificing the stability of the other divisions to make this one work. I'm even leery of taking judges away from the challenge matches at this point, however there's enough interest in this to run it so I'm prepared to give it a go. Questions? If anything needs to be added, I'll edit this post.
