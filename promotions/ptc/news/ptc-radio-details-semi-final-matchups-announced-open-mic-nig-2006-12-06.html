Radio time 8.00pm Saturday Night - New York, Miami... UTC-5 1.00am UTC/GMT Sunday morning. 12.00pm Sunday Lunch Time AEST (UTC+11) Radio Link Details http://radio.primetimecentral.net or at the bottom of the forums. When the time comes, click on the dialup or broadband links, whichever suits. You will need one of the software programs listed. I prefer Apple Quicktime, as I've found it is the least laggy. If you've not listened to streaming radio before and your computer doesn't know what a .pls file is, save it to your local PC and run it with your chosen audio program directly. There is sometimes a lag between the dialup and the broadband links, and a lag between different people. If you are in chat and you read stuff before you hear it and this bothers you, I can't do anything about that, you'll have to leave the chat room and just listen, or suck it up. Chat Details Join in the official chat on the PTC Community Forums chat room. Topics for Discussion - The semi final matchups for the 6 bracket finalists / semi finalists. - Hopefully some of the Semi Finalists can actually call in and talk some smack. - On-air voting for PTC WOTY 2006 by callers. Go crazy with your reasons. Entertain me and the votes will count. - Open Mic night - trash talk your current feud buddy. - Take parting shots at Almasy on his way out the door. - If you are in GCW, tell Rollins why he sucks. - If you are in XUW, tell everyone else why they suck. - If you are in UWF, say goodbye and thanks for all the fish. You get the idea. See out 2006 with a bang. More as I think of them Toons - If you want something played, send it to me and I'll play it. Try for stuff we haven't heard evey previous radio show. For example, Pete sent me Time Warp from Rocky Horror last time. - Richard Cheese please. Sound Bytes Last time you heard just about every banned person be made fun of, because someone took the time to actually make the sound bytes. If you are into audio stuff, make your own sound bytes and I'll play them. Promotional tools... poke fun at people... the works.
