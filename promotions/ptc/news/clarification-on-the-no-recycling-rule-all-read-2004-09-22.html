Clarification on the no recycling rule (ALL READ)

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the GTT4 Rule Book: No Recycling: All Roleplays posted must be original for this event. This means that they are not to have already been used in your home fed. If after the event your fed head allows you to use them there, that is between you and the fed head. There has been some quite liberal interpretations of this rule, particularly the "after the event" bit of it, from people who should know better, or at least have asked. GTT4 in its entirity is what I mean by "the event", I don't think there's any other reasonable interpretation of that. Thus, "after the event" means when GTT4 is finished. So, what it means is that you are writing your material for GTT4. While you are in GTT4, and while GTT4 is still running it is not to be used elsewhere. Period. The winner and high placers of this tournament expect to be and will be known as among the best in the hobby for a long time to come. Don't water that perception down by adding a recycler tag to it, it'd be just as bad as the GTT1 winner being seen as a plagiarist rather than the winner of a 128 man tournament. I'm a nice guy, so I'll allow the group of people in Round 2 who liberally interpreted this rule by using their Round 1 Roleplays for matches in their home feds after posting them on the GTT4 boards to continue in the tournament, but there's no second chances, for anyone. You get sprung, you're out. To close out one last loophole at least within PTC boundaries, people found to be disregarding this rule after they are eliminated and posting stuff in their feds will be barred from future PTC events. As a condition of membership, Fed heads are also required to abide by PTC rules, so don't fuck around splitting hairs, just don't do it. Wait until after the tournament is over. Finally, if you disagree with this rule and have no intention of honoring it, please resign now.
