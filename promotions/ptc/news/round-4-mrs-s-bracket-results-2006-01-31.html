Round 4: Mrs S. Bracket Results

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAL SOMERS V DIGITAL MORTALITY V MASQUERADE V SHANTY JONES Judge 1 Digital Mortality To say I'm disappointed this round is very on-the-mark. First of all, you say "she" and "he" and use pronouns WAY too much. In a paragraph, saying "she" a trillion times is just straight up blech. Change things up, use a name. Next, the flow of it is disrupted by changing thoughts, over and over. Now, I know Digi is truly in love with Veronica and all. The story is good...but the big problem is that I honeslty don't care about future roleplays. You ended so that I didn't want you to continue the story, but rather that I was wondering how you could continue it. And, I'm sorry to say, that doesn't work. Especially not in GTT5. I know you're a good writer, and I know you can do better than this. An off week? Something like that? Shanty Jones Now, I haven't bothered to read your other RPs. My thinking is that a writer shouldn't assume that a judge knows everything about the character or whatever, no matter how big your name is. So, at first, I was confused. Why did this dude get killed? What was Shanty thinking? Was this explained in past RPs? I didn't want to stop, though, so I kept on reading...even though it draggggged. And not only that, but you jumped around from one idea to another too much. This can be done right, but you need to do it more fluidly. The thing is, though, once the first RP ended, I said to myself "Oh, okay, cool". I completely disregarded all the things that annoyed me and got sucked in. I also liked how the second RP directly continued from the first. I wasn't left trying to remember what happened between now and a past RP. It was nice to have that refresher. This whole RP was just plain cool. I liked it a lot...I definately can see how you won last year. Even though the ending of the second RP was a little weak in comparison to the climax of it, I'd like to see where it goes. Also, the little note at the end about Goodman intrigues me. Not that this final note on your RP will affect how I judge your RP, but you don't need to cite passages from the bible. I know your intentions were good, but I think it can be pretty obvious that they were from there. Saying that the passages from the bible was, to me, like saying "You must live under a rock and not even know the slightest about biblical style of writing." Don't. Note: The quoting is in the rules - no exceptions. It protects everybody involved from anything to do with plagiarism accusations, so he did the right thing. Aside from that, you're right :D Mal Somers First off, you had some punctuation problems. Sentences too long, etc. Just because they're commas doesn't mean making two sentences one, or killing a sentence by adding a confusing fragmentation. But it wasn't a problem, just something that made me stop and kill the flow of the story at a couple points. Now, the first RP...looking back onto the notes I took while reading it, one was "What's the point?" The whole thing seemed kind of just rambling, blah blah blah. Up until the final paragraphs, I didn't give a shit. In my mind, I had "4. Mal Somers" ready. That all changed, though. Once the second RP started, I was very intrigued. Love letters? Even though you are so in love? My God! Now, the whole thing played out, but then...things happened way too fast. It seemed like you must've been writing for a long time, and then by the end, you said to yourself "Yeah, yeah, yeah, ok, wrapping it up." I did like the RPs, though...I just wished that you maybe put a little more into them. Masquerade First off, you have an amazing grasp for the english language. Are you plagiarising someone else?! :P No, I kid, you have amazing talent with the language. *Waits for everyone to say "HOW DARE YOU ACCUSE OF PLAGIARISING BLAH BLAH BLAH.." :S* Now, onto the RPs. The story is very interesting, but it just seems crazy. It is as if you had this idea before the tournament, and then said "and I guess I'll throw in that I'm a wrestler, here and there". This would be a good movie, as I stated when I judged last round's RP, as it is very enticing. The thing, though, is while in the last round I had no doubt that you were the best of the wrestlers, I do not share the same sentiments now. And the thing that makes me feel this way is the reference to the actual wrestling. I remember looking in the judging advice thing at the start of this tournament that someone said "Judges don't like it when you don't talk about a match". This time, though, it seemed tacked on. Like, you had all this written out, and then you said to yourself "Oh shit, right, wrestling!" and just added some bullshit. That took a LOT away from the one RP. The Final Verdict: Shany Jones stole the #1, and Digi unfortunately sank to #4. Those are my two SURE choices. The #2 and the #3, though, are quite different. Mal Somers and Masquerade had two very different RPs. But I felt that there were on the same calibar for different reasons. The wrong thing for me to do right now is to give it to Mal JUST because he posted 2 RPs. That'd be like saying a long book is better than a shorter one just because it is long. That is not the case. Ultimately, I give #2 to Masquerade, simply because I want to see where this whole story goes more than I want to see where Mal's goes. You had a good RP, Mal, but Masquerade left me wanting a little bit more. So, in conclusion: 1. Shanty Jones 2. Masquerade 3. Mal Somers 4. Digital Mortality Judge 2 Masquerade Well when you look at the roleplays posted and you see a competitor has only posted one when the limit is two you usually sigh, I know I do. However, this isn't the case in regards to Masquerade. The one roleplay posted was long enough to keep up with the quantity others had written but just not seperated. Now, at such a huge ammount of text I had to say I was a bit overwhelmed. However, thinking about it, if it was two roleplays I probably wouldn't have been. Yet, I did not find it hard to get into, neither did I find it a chore to read to the end. I really do buy into the mystery of the character and I loved the story in the roleplay. The quality of writing from Masquerade scares me. The quality of Masquerade's syntax and lexis amazed me. An excellent piece of work. Digital Mortality Now, I've been lucky to have read some of Digi's work in a previous round as was impressed by the high standard he set himself. I was however, worried that he would eventually burn out. Unfortunately for his opponents he doesn't show any signs of weakening yet. His first roleplay gripped me, maybe not as much as Masquerade's, but it was a very entertaining piece. Finding out Digi's going to get married was nice and the happy ending seemed deserved. I really felt happy for him. I don't feel the second had my attention as much as the first, but, it was still well written and still had that nice flow his work seems to be following at the moment. He may have won a different group, unfortunately Masquerade is on fire right now. Shanty Jones This bracket, I must say has been very hard to judge. It was hard in my mind to pick out the top two and even then in which order. It's nice that all four of the competitors have made this a hard job for me. It's what the GTT is all about. Anyway, I've not read anything of Shanty's before but I was pleasantly surprised when I did start reading. A very interesting storyline which was well written. Between Shanty and Digi I really couldn't pick out any weaknesses at all. Both were good solid pieces of writing and both writers entertained and interested me. I didn't feel as connected with Shanty though, whereas I really cared what was going on in Digi's life and that's why Digi just got the nod. Mal Sommers Now Mal's got nothing to be ashamed of either, again, this was a writer who's work I had not read before, so I didn't know quite what to expect. It wasn't that his roleplays were bad, or even just below par. His writing was of a high standard and it wasn't a bore to read. I can only put two people through and although Masquerade almost inspired me to go write a roleplay myself, the other three in this group really gave me a hard time deciding who to put second. I can honestly say that if Mal had been in a different group things might have gone the other way, but when it gets down to this few people, some spectacular pieces have started to come out of the woodwork. All though this may be of little worth to Mal Sommers and Shanty Jones, I will be keeping an eye on them in the future as they both provided enjoyable work. Masquerade 1 Digital Mortality 2 Shanty Jones 3 Mal Somers 4 RESULT Lowest score = greatest value Masquerade - 3 points *match winner Shanty Jones - 4 points Digital Mortality - 6 points Mal Somers - 7 points Masquerade and Shanty Jones advance to the Mrs S.
