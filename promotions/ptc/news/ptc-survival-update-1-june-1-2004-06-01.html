PTC Survival Update 1 - June 1

1 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1 June 2004 Pete is the event co-ordinator/overseer/card compiler/man with a big whip/possibly webmaster for the event subsite. As of now, we have a grand total of five match writers and four judges who've generously volunteered their services. Not the usual guys either, all are new or almost new to contributing in like this, to my knowledge. Props to them! This number needs to increase in order for this event to be possible. The current idea for the main event is to have the GTD Former Champions Rematch match appear on the card instead of being a touring match, which places the time for this at late July / early August. Jack is to organise match writers and judges, and can arrange for judging comments as have become standard for the divisions if he wishes to. The Extreme and Tag Titles will be defended, with preselection of challengers and organising matchwriters/judges up to the division heads, RJ and Ryan. XUW, LOW, 4WW, RPW, CORE, UWF, GW, NGW and PRIME have registered places for players in a Surviror Series style matchup/series of matchups. WMW, UPWA and BDW are also welcome to play if they wish (thread on fed heads forum for this), as well as any new feds that come in or return in the next 4 weeks, give or take a week. The possibility of other matches is being looked at. There is obviously a demand for them, looking at the I-PPV Match wish list thread in the General forum. For them to happen, it will go something like this - if you want to compete in a singles match, one competitor in the match will be required to write a different match, and the other competitor to judge another match. That makes it like Ratings will be - user pays. Agreement to that does not guarantee you a spot - the final card composition will be at the sole discretion of Pete and myself. Not everyone who wants one will be able to have a match. More info as it becomes available. BDW and WMW positions in the Survivor matchup are confirmed. Thanks to the new volunteers. I'll add you all to the list, and someone will be in touch with you when the time comes. We are now at 6 matchwriters and 11 judges. Keep 'em coming :) 5 June 2004 Thanks all. You will be contacted when the time comes. Update: 12 Judges 6 Matchwriters Another large update is in the works. Posted on the PTC forum by Argyle.
