Round 1 Results - Rabbit Bracket

17 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AXEL ACTION V 'THE NEW SHIT' BOBINO Judge 1 The New Shit Bobino: An interesting introductory role play to what should make a very interesting saga. While simplistic the role play was well written with only minor errors. This allowed the writer to pitch the story with great ease despite a slow start. Axel Acton: Traditional idea which was well dealt with. The role play should strong characteristics of Acton with well placed humor and strong character will power. Both role plays were entertaining and good reads but broad and brief in content. I however have decided that “New Shit” Bobino just pips Axel Acton to the post. Pick: Bobino Judge 2 Nice pair of introductory pieces. Action brings the motivation to win the tournament as a final testament to – and as the standard-bearer of – the UWF, while Bobino set the stage for future hijinks. Putting aside the ever-annoying, ever-present “mysterious insane bastard returns***” storyline (this was only the third roleplay I read for the tournament, but the second instance of this), the exploration was great and I enjoyed reading it. Majin just sitting down beside Bobino made me chuckle, and I can’t begin to describe how pleased I was when the possibility that the badguy was in Bobino’s head was shattered. The conclusion, while simplistic, worked well. Overall… Very easy to fall into, provided a good feeling out of the character, and more importantly, did it as a build-up to the storyline. My problem is that the storyline inspires little more than a “Great, another wrestling guy with a psychotic brother/sister/aunt/dog/voice-in-his-head out to kill him.” On the other side of the coin, Axel taking the opportunity to champion his dying fed for one last hurrah got me going. It’s specific to him and the current times as dictated by the world… Like, for Bobino, the storyline could be anyone, wrestler or not, at any time, whereas for Action it’s specific, nurtured by outside phenomena, and topical. After the letter, and with the exception of some of Axel’s words later on, the presentation left a bit to be desired. Mocking trashtalk and its reputation is all fine and well, but if you’re going to include it afterward, it better stand out. It better make me laugh, or make your opponent cringe in a way that I as a reader know he’s cringing. The issue really isn’t whether or not trashtalking is done, but how it’s done. Overall… Great premise. The letter and the opening of Axel’s address really had me pulling for him. You made it very easy to relate to Axel, and to get emotionally involved with him. Unfortunately, where it strayed from that it became generic, and the humour of a grown man watching Teen Titans/Teletubbies/Sailor Moon scratches my funny bone instead of tickling it. So I’m left weighing a generic idea with good presentation beside a good idea with mainly generic presentation. And with the latter, the idea, while good, has very short legs. All that’s really been promised is a continuation of living room interviews and passionate cries for the UWF. With that looming in the back of my mind, I’m not sure who should win. At the risk of seeming to contradict myself, Axel showed the capacity to act on the fly and adapt, while I get the impression Bobino’s locked into a storyline that won’t change regardless of what happens. Bobino was the better read, and will likely be the better read if he advances, but does it have more merit? I’m not sure it does. In the end, all things considered... Axel had a grand idea and a few good moments, but the effort was diluted by the shallow humour and almost commonplace diatribe. Bobino had a questionable idea that was enriched by subtle humour and effective presentation. Bobino wins, with the tipping point being that he did more, and is poised to do more. - Pick: Bobino Result 'The New Shit' Bobino d Axel Action, 2 votes to 0 MENTOK THE MINDTAKER VS DDT Judge 1 Mentok: I find it hard to give any sort of feedback here, without slipping into the persona that gave me the original PTC Asshole Admin moniker. The work probably would have been clearer if it were done script style. The "novel" format simply doesn't work well with the character, and the constant dialogue. Mentok is a minor character that provides comic relief on Harvey Birdman. Making him the focus of an entire story is difficult, and in this case, is rather poorly done. DDT: Good story. I wasn't familiar with DDT, but, it was nice to get a little glimpse into his psyche and what got him to the present. The read was a bit difficult with the switching from past, to further past to now, but, I suppose that's just personal preference. Pick: DDT over Mentok. I wasn't enthused with the Mentok piece in the slightest. Head to head, this was a pretty easy result. DDT did just enough to ensure that he moves on, and Mentok did just enough to ensure that he wasn't going to get even close. Sorry, Mentok. Another time, another character maybe. It just wasn't your event. Judge 2 DDT Some awkward grammar. Gimmick not original, making RP not very interesting. Suggest additional proofreading. Mentok The RP is funny...but goes NOWHERE. It has no focus at all, it seems, and it's just rambling. It's good enough in the first round, but it really isn't that high quality. Humor can win the first round, don't depend on it for the second. Pick: Mentok gets my vote. Judge 3 Is Mentok the Mindtaker a real cartoon character? If so, are you allowed to do that? Not knowing the answers to those questions, lets continue. I actually enjoyed Mentok's random flipping in and out of characters, and cheesy humour, but then, it's late, and maybe it's because I'm tired. :) DDT, the first paragraph is meant to grab the reader by the balls and draw them in, instead, your's made my head spin. It did get better, but not good enough, I'm giving Mentok the win. Unless of course Mentok is a real cartoon characater, and if that is against the rules (and I'm leaving that up to someone other than me to decide), then he can get out. Pick: Mentok Result Mentok the Mindtaker d DDT, 2 votes to 1 Note, regarding Judge 3's comment: The subject of whether the Mentok character should be allowed in GTT at all has been discussed on the admin board, with opinions split all over the place. I'm allowing it because it wasn't picked up before RP'ing had been done and there is some precedent for Mentok in previous PTC events in an era where we allowed characters called Ronald McDonald in events, but won't be in future events, to keep things simple. Abbreviate that to one got through to the keeper past the Aussie. EMBER v "DEADPOOL" DORIAN RYAN Judge 1 Ember: I loved it. Great detail, great description, great flow. I thought this was an excellent “bring up to date” while not stail and slow. Very good roleplay. Ryan: Again a good piece. It didn't go into great detail but provided the reader with sufficient knowledge and insight into the character. It also gave the reader a good introduction to what Ryan has been up to in his wrestling career. Ember has written a wonderful role play which in my judgment out did Ryan. Ryan's role play was good in it's own right but in a battle of writers on this occasion Ember shone through. Pick: Ember Judge 2 Very contrastive writing styles, but you both stayed on point and put out enjoyable reads. Ember’s prose was more eloquent. Ryan had a stronger message. The blossoming of Ember’s story leaves me feeling déjà vu, simply because this isn’t shaping up as the most original or mysterious plot, but the sublime presentation and the exploration of the characters goes a long way in making up for that. The only minor technical issue I had was the lack of variance in sentence structure for the narration. More specifically, with few exceptions, all the sentences are incredibly long, which makes it harder for the reader to become immersed and easily follow. While there’s absolutely nothing wrong with elaborate sentences, those that are succinct are essential to the equation. They lend impact to both. Mind you, that’s just well-intentioned nitpicking, as you obviously have an exceptional grasp of language and wield it deftly. The rhetoric gnawed at times, but not to the point where the underlying meaning was lost. Besides, I prefer the effort occasionally slipping into rhetoric than no effort whatsoever. Overall… The story on its own doesn’t appeal to me all that much, but the style may persuade me to want to continue reading. For Dorian Ryan, as I read through, I didn’t know what you were up to. It wasn’t until the end that the attack dawned on me. Very nicely done. Subtlety can go a long way. Some of the conversation comes off really flaky, like in the bar, and with the exception of that one scene Dorian almost seems a spectator to his own life. Maybe he just has a stoic dignity about him, I don’t know. Which is basically my point – despite his unending presence, I don’t know much at all about him. Is it all singularly focused on nailing Ember? Overall… It’s a sweet assassination piece, but very little else. And… I’m giving the nod to Ember. I’m very tempted to choose Dorian Ryan, as I was uber-pleased with the intent, but… In the face of Ember’s superior presentation of character, personality, and style, not to mention making me smirk and smile a few times, Dorian’s sublime focus falls a bit short. Pick: Ember Result Ember d Dorian Ryan, 2 votes to 0 JOHN "THE PROPHET" ADAMS V JORGE SAMUELSSON Judge 1 Adams story contained more developing story, which was enough to make the difference. Pick: Adams - very narrowly Judge 2 Ahhhh… Mom n’ Pop gun shops versus phantom tarot readers on airplanes. The undead take on Nordic Gods. Brain-eating against sleep-groping. Brings a tear to my eye. Despite the fact that the Prophet’s roleplay made three out of five with the evil murderous whomever, he went all out and made it the lesser of the absurd so it didn’t really hurt him. The piece has not even a passing acquaintanceship with reality, and you work it well. Nice, easy-going piece. Even with the deadly serious you weren’t serious at all, and that was charming. Samuelsson also made reality his bitch, though in less extraordinary ways. I thought putting Samuelsson over as a tough guy by having him spit on and rough up the steward was pretty useless, but I liked the poem and the roleplay as a whole. Overall… Similar styles, strengths, and shortcomings boils it down to whose presentation and comedy entertained me more. I’ll take the conflicted dopey Viking over the anti-hero with the sorcerous evil twin. Pick: Samuelsson Judge 3 Adams started a story, whereas it just kind of felt like a day in the life of Samuelsson. While that isn't a bad thing, it didn't lead me to want to read more Samuelsson work. And while Adams' story was not my cup of tea, gimmick and storywise, it certainly was enough to earn him a pass onto the next round. Pick: Adams Result - John "The Prophet" Adams d Jorge Samuelsson, 2 votes to 1 M3 VS TIGERA Judge 1 The use of language and dialogue for both stories were very good, but the subtle nuances of Tigera's presentation made things just a little more fascinating. Pick: Tigera Judge 2 M3: This was an exciting read. Very good knowledge of your wrestlers background displayed and much thought had gone into carefully executing a story in this roleplay that moved along in unison to a well structured introduction to the goals, aims and life of M3. Tigera Complex. It could be put at times a little to complex. However at other times hints of something special. Overall this match for an interesting read. Maybe a little more concentration could be spent on reviewing the very smart issues you deal with rather then speaking about so many. Sometimes the flow of the roleplay would be disrupted by quick flashbacks. A very tough battle. Two good roleplays, two very different styles. I am going to have to pick Tigera to pick up the win. While a narrow victory, Tigera put forth a great effort in the First Round. Pick: Tigera Result Tigera d M3, 2 votes to 0 WILDLIFE V XIAN PHILLIPSE Judge 1 Some strong use of dialogue and some interesting characters put this in favor of Phillipse. Pick: Phillipse Judge 2 Wildlife - Assumed history and characters (Kidd) - Descriptive paragraphs don't flow well. Xian Phillipse - Builds well for the future. Pick: Phillipse Result Xian Phillipse d Wildlife, 2 votes to 0 SUMMARY - 'The New Shit' Bobino d Axel Action, 2 votes to 0 - Mentok the Mindtaker d DDT, 2 votes to 1 - Ember d Dorian Ryan, 2 votes to 0 - John "The Prophet" Adams d Jorge Samuelsson, 2 votes to 1 - Tigera d M3, 2 votes to 0 - Xian Phillipse d Wildlife, 2 votes to 0 - Peter Vetra d Jeff Harris via forfeit.
