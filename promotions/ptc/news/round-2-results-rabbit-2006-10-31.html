Round 2 Results - Rabbit

31 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"THE NEW SHIT" BOBINO V STEVE WATSON - CPA Judge 1 Steve Watson: “Tickets” - I couldn't help but feel a little disappointed at the end of this roleplay. The roleplay felt very standard, ordinary and didn't really achieve anything other then in a few sentences. While it had great intentions of setting Steve Watson's thoughts out to the reader it could have done so to much greater effect. This could have been improved if the writer had put more into either the establishment of Steve Watson's mental state, the significance of the tickets or a more dramatic response to the end announcement. This roleplay attempted to briefly touch on a series of issues arising from the playin of Steve Watson but could have been done to greater effect. “Time” - This is a better effort. The scope of the roleplay is more concentrated then in “Tickets” this does wonders for the quality of writing attributed to the major themes of writing especially as all the small storylines gradually intertwine through the roleplay. Then the classic stick is thrown into the clogs at the roleplay to add another problem for Steve Watson, who isn't having the best of times lately. "The New Shit" Bobino: “Beyond control” - A well thought out development roleplay. The second in the series. As a stand alone roleplay it wasn't grand but certainly not bad. The flow of the writing was a strong positive of the roleplay but the roleplay lacked in great substance and the simplicity of the piece dragged it down. As a great writer you should be writing far better roleplays then this piece. If you take in context of the series then it adds perfectly to the previous rounds roleplay. In the process of this match that however will not be taken into account during judgment. “Title not provided” - This roleplay was divided into two sections. The 1st section dealt with Bobino and his doctor and then Krisko and his own. I thought the roleplay did it's job but nothing more. The introduction of Krisko could have been done much better. At times Krisko came across as pure evil his “sadistic grin” coupled with the rp's deadly last words “Soon enough... soon enough” but during other portions of the introduction this graphic portayal of evil felt less significant the paragraph about why Krisko was allowed to watch FUSE forced the reader to infer from the writers implications that the Krisko's calmness was a result of his desire to inflict revenge. This was quite sloppy. Four decent roleplays. The four roleplays balanced well and neither of the two competitors ran away with the match which was a disappointment on both guys behalf. After reading one another's roleplays both of them can believe an extra 10% would have won them a match. If this was a General Election this result would be one via a hung parliament. The New Shit Bobino pips Steve Watson past the post. His build up, writing style along with the mystery of Krisko mean his roleplays just out do Steve Watson's ticket. Pick: Bobino Judge 2 The initial Bobino offering felt as though it was rushed, particularly compared to the quality of his follow up piece. Watson's consistency, the slow build of a believable yet elaborate story helped make the difference in his favor in spite of some other things being nearly at an equal level. Pick: Watson Judge 3 "The New Shit" Bobino Roleplay #1 - First off, let me say that this is a solid outing. I thought the story was extremely short for the second round of GTT6, and that actually hurt you because of the fact there was just so little here in terms of story and events. You've got a good grasp on writing, but you just give so little that it's not enough to really whet my appetite. It felt like things were going to start going somewhere, and it just ended with the beginning all over again. I thought the ending with the tears were good, but I wanted more. That's a good thing, but it should be I got a good story to go along with it, and I got the smallest fraction of a story. Technically, there are some errors here. Really, just run it through a spell checker because I didn't even have to sit there and make sure everything was correct because the spell checker in Word did it for me. *detailed examples of grammatical transgressions and subsequent lecture removed* You can write, and you have some moments, but this is not a winning piece. I hope roleplay #2 is better. Roleplay #2 - Once again, you write well, but this is not GTT worthy. I'm sorry if you think it's harsh, but the story here is nearly non-existant. Is there even a story here? It just feels like a mix of random scenes you stuck together and tossed together for a roleplay. There's the slimmest of connections from the first piece to this piece, and there's no referring back to it at all. There's no picking up from the tears, but it now seems Bobino is in a clinic, watching FUSE shows. Where's the story? What're you trying to accomplish here? Why do you want to be in GTT6? What does your character want? That's interesting. None of this is interesting. I read it and I'm bored. Once again, you have a good grasp on writing, and I don't think your skills are being questioned here. It's the story you're trying to tell that just isn't working, and isn't the least bit compelling. *more on the grammatical and spelling deficiencies removed* Steve Waton - CPA: Roleplay #1 Coming into this, I wondered if the hype behind this was going to live up or if I would be sitting here wondering why the hype was so big for you. Turns out the hype lives up to you as you're a great writer, as your opponent is. The difference here is that your story is ten times better, and you don't make the mistakes he makes. Outside of one fragment sentence at the end of the piece, I didn't spot any other errors that took me out of the piece. I did wonder about the date of this piece as it mentioned the Manning Bowl and things weren't exactly clear for me. Sorry, that did take me out of the piece, but not for long. I love the story you're telling, and I'm interested in where this goes. It's a very a simple tale, and you do a good job of meshing the wrestling world with your work world, though the wrestling world is obviously on the heavier side this time around. I also felt like you did a great job of balancing your description and action, keeping the piece moving. At the end of the piece, I was left wanting more. It wasn't a long piece, and was barely longer then Bobino's. The way it hurt Bobino though doesn't hurt you here as I felt like I got enough story here to whet my appetite, but just a little bit. I need more story really, and in the later rounds you're going to have to get it if you move out of this round. Just be prepared for that. I know some guys aren't the kind to write long roleplays, but if you bring the story along with it then you're going to do fine. That's something I really looked for, and while you did a good job of getting me intrigued, in the long run, you're going to need more story. Roleplay #2 Another good piece here, and just as good as the previous one. You have a few more errors here, primarily fragments, that need to be fixed. They just stick out, that's all. Not going to go in-depth on this piece here as I touched upon a lot of the things in the last roleplay, but you did good here again. You did many of the same things, telling a good story, and leaving just enough to keep you interested. I think you brisked over the interrogation/interview with the detective, but it may not end up being important at all. However, that is something I would've liked to see you handle. You pick up the story right from the end of the first roleplay, and it just flows. It almost feels like you wrote one long roleplay, and split it in half honestly. I liked the ending and how you've really grounded it in reality with how Steve has to work and still have his GTT6 Round Two match. Good stuff. Just watch out for those minor errors. This one is no contest. Steve Watson - CPA. Just disapointed in Bobino as at this stage in the game you start to expect something more. Watson, you're probably going to have to step it up next round as this tournament is just going to continue to get tougher and tougher. - Pick: Steve Watson - CPA Judge 4 Watson comes back hungrier, and fixes minor errors from last round and advances. Pick: Watson Result - Steve Watson - CPA d "The New Shit" Bobino, 3 votes to 1 XIAN PHILLIPSE V PETER VETRA Judge 1 In a case where "showing" and not "telling" made a significant difference in how accessable the stories were, XP's work was stronger by means of dialogue and the use of the characters voice and pov to color the story in a believable fashion. Pick: Xian Phillipse Judge 2 Peter Vetra There were a lot of different ideas presented, but the pieces were more like a jumbled hodge-podge of fragments thrown against the wall to see what would stick rather than a concise, neat, flowing piece that really developed one or two strong points into a story. There was way too much kayfabe being broken for my taste and the depression angle has been done to death. Xian Phillipse Nice relating of current goings-on back to the inevitable task of facing Vetra in the ring. The social commentary within the pieces was a nice touch, but almost started to wander into becoming a bit overdone as the readings progressed. Both pieces were solid, even if the ending of the second piece was expected. Pick: Phillipse Judge 3 Xian Phillipse: “Virgin”- “Wrap it before you tap it” is an awesome line. As I read the beginning of this roleplay I am being exposed to a masterclass of classy, egotistical character driven work. The writing clearly gives a reader an impression of the bizarre life XP lives and is a splendid read. The writer had a strong grasp over the comedy used in the earlier section of the roleplay. Similar application of the drama would have made this roleplay even stronger. “Funeralis” - A superb roleplay. Engrossing, thrilling and well built up. I must say from the moment XP and Bizzy held hands I was waiting for the inevitable. This is not to knock predictability because it was well delivered. Two great efforts. Peter Vetra: “The Truth Behind my lies” - This is an insightful piece which lacks great flow but the lack of flow is made up in hard substance. The writer shows something few do in this day and age a crystal clear understanding of his character. While greater attention to the flow of the piece would have improved the roleplay it is hard to pick many faults in this Vetra effort. “Revenge will be mine” - Second Roleplays have a habbit of being a letdown and I couldn't help feel this one adhered to the rule. While the 1st roleplay showed substantial command the second roleplay seemed rushed and hadn't had the great thought process that had been established in the first roleplay. I will have to give the win to XP with two cracking roleplays. They seemed to outclass Peter Vetra's in this round. Petere Vetra just didn't seem to keep the pace up in his second roleplay unlike XP who fielded a second roleplay which some may argue eclipsed his 1st. Pick: Phillpse Judge 4 Peter Vetra: The Truth behind my Lies... The first part of the RP was pretty cluttered in my opinion. I had some trouble picking up what you were trying to say, and you repeated yourself far too much. The 2nd part was far better, but was it enough? Revenge will be mine by Peter Vetra: Again, far too cluttered. Any entertainment value this could've had was suffocated by it. What's this group? You don't explain it at all. The RP start with you beating on some guy, the last one left us with him just getting the news of his father's murder. There's no connection between the two, at all, and I was totally lost here. Xian Phillipse: Virgin A bit hard to follow at times, but a techincally sound piece. Funeralis Could've used a little more info on the characters.. Pick: Xian Phillipse Judge 5 Xian Phillipse: Excellent opening. A good mix of humour, trash talk and dialogue. Xian comes across exactly as he's supposed to; an immature cocky teenager with a lot to learn. Poorly structured sentences let you down in places. Parts of it, such as talking about the ex-girlfriend, seemed unneccessary and I'm not sure what it was supposed to add to the story. Over all, quite good, but I think you need to give more depth and purpose to your character. Your plot is weak and needs to be fleshed out a little. Peter Vetra: Littered with spelling mistakes, bad punctuation and jarring grammatical errors which just got worse as the RPs progressed. Please have someone proof read before you post! Story is strong though and you portray Vetra's anguish and indecision well. However, it just didn't hook me and this may be partly because I had to reread parts twice to make sense of it. This was a very tough decision to make. Vetra had the stronger storyline, but Philipse's was the more entertaining and better written. Pick: Xian Phillipse Result Xian Phillpse d Peter Vetra, 5 votes to 0 EMBER V JOHN 'THE PROPHET' ADAMS Judge 1 John Adams: Lost Sister (PT.2) I take it you explained the goings on of this RP in the first round, which I didn't read. Lost Sister (PT.3) Much of the same in this one, more things that went unexplained. The writing itself isn't bad, however.. Ember: The Ballad of two - part two I was happy with this piece. It gave me just enough hook to go onto the second right away, nothing to nitpick from me, man.. The Ballad of two - part Three by Ember: No complaints for this piece either. I liked how it played off the last, and the ending certainly makes me want to read more Pick: Ember Judge 2 Ember: #1: Pretty good start. I was hooked on it pretty quickly and it never seemed to lose my attention. Solid work. #2: I really dig your description in your pieces. You’re able to make it absolutely easy to feel like I’m actually there and seeing these scenes as your character is. Good solid work once more as the story continued to impress me in this second piece as well. Adams: #1: Good story. I am genuinely interested in what is going on with this whole thing. The spelling errors and such weren’t bad at all, at least that I noticed, and therefor the piece flowed very well for me. #2: Another good job Mr. Adams. I enjoyed the roleplay as much as I enjoyed the first one. You have made this a very tough decision to make between your story and the fact that it isn’t slowed down by large amounts of filler and such. It flows nicely once more and made reading the roleplay very quick and easy. Good work. This one is actually quite a tough call.
