GTT - Day 5 Report - HUGE NEWS DAY!

1 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GTT#3 WINNER AND RUNNER-UP IN VERBAL CONFRONTATION! Today, during a GTT4 promotional function, GTT3 winner Angelo Deville and runner up Inphino Blitz exchanged several heated words. Numerous security guards acted quickly to seperate the pair before blows were exchanged, however the two continued to posture and stare each other down while being led away. Blitz hurled deathly insults at Deville, which Deville countered with a thick loogie and a slow finger across the throat motion. No word as to whether the two again encountered each other before the function drew to a close. Tournament director Argyle still refused to confirm whether Deville would actually be in the tournament, however punters took Deville's presence at the function as a sure sign, and started taking odds on which bracket he would appear in and who his first round opponent would be. Deville defeated Blitz in the final round of GTT3 in one of the closest battles in PTC history. Punters have speculated that the two could easily see each other in a rematch, depending on where Deville is placed in the tournament. TOURNAMENT EXPANSION CONFIRMED - 192 PLAYERS! TWO NEW BRACKETS ADDED! TRIPLE THREAT FINAL ROUND! Tournament Director Argyle confirmed in his daily press release that the final number of competitors in GTT4 would in fact be 192. "We have so far overshot the 128 player application mark that it would be counter-productive for us to have such a large number of rejected applicants walking around with sad faces. Expanding to 192 allows an extra 64 players in, without overtaxing our available management resources. Judging will be a problem, however we'll be asking many people to pitch in and help where they can, so it should all come good." "This expansion has allowed us to accept such players as Kasper Sky and Jay Phoenix. Both of those men would have missed out completely if we had stuck to 128. Both of them have proven track records at tournaments, so are the sort of competitors that we want here." "With respect to the strange number, we did have to make some fundamental structural changes to the tournament to accomodate the extra players. The key to the change is that the final round match will now be a triple threat match, between the winners of the bracket finalist elimination round." "At this point in time, we have about 30 more available places in the tournament. Applications close in 3 to 4 days, after that the only way to get in will be to apply for a playin and hope that there is a need for one." FIRST ROUND OPPONENTS TO BE ANNOUNCED ON PTC RADIO! In an exciting development, this weekend will see a special edition of PTC Radio, with regular host Duke Williams and Tournament Director Argyle. During the course of this special show, all first round opponents will be revealed. The time and date of the broadcast is yet to be confirmed at this point in time. It is expected that the GTT4 site will not display the names for a full 24 hours after the show. CONFIRMED ENTRANTS Tony Aliso (Neon) and Ichiro Nakazura (GW) round out the first half of the Himalayas Bracket. Phil Botwin (Neon) and Joshua Taro Freedom (PRIME) round out the first half of the Rockies Bracket. Nathaniel Draven (Neon) and Khaled (WWA) round out the first half of the Pyrenees Bracket. Matrix (WMW), Steven Caldera (FA) and Violence Jack (FA) make up the remainder of the first half of the Appalachians Bracket. The new Kaikouras bracket sees Jay Phoenix (FA), Exceptional Dave Hurst (FA), Adam Myers (GW), Fnord (4WW), Moraless (FA), Fathom (FA) and Fred Grest (WMW). Finally, the Flinders bracket sees Kasper Sky (FA), Jonathan Sail (FA), Luke Chadbourne (4WW), Larry the Legionairre (XUW), Adolph Nilsson (Core) and Tony Stecchino (Neon). The Almasy saga No update at this time on the investigations into vandalism and bribery relating to Almasy. We will keep you posted.
