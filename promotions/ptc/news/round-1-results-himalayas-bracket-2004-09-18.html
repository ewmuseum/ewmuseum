Round 1 Results: Himalayas Bracket

18 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These are results, they are not Round 2 Brackets. Those will be announced after all the results are up. No-shows and Double-no-shows are not noted. If you haven't read the judging thread, do it now: http://community.primetimecentral.net/showthread.php?t=9955 Kris Kanton advances. Hirokazu advances. Jerms advances. Inphino Blitz defeated Sebastian Moraco (2 votes to 1) to advance Joe Black defeated “Avatar” Willam Gwynn (1 vote) to advance Mr. Darkness advances SSL defeated Sergeant Major Adam Tangle (1 vote) to advance Superstar Eric Russo advances Karina Wolfenden defeated fatbadger (2 votes to 0) to advance Arefiev advances Nick Persia advances John Winters defeated Cyrus Skar (2 votes to 1) to advance Johnny Lexicon d Lori Equinox (1 vote) Tony Aliso defeated Zane Ozz (2 votes to 1) to advance Ichiro Nakazura d Hank Cobb (1 vote) Judging Comments Hirokazu Digital Morality gave Hirokazu this win because he no showed, though Hirokazu is lucky to even get the win. A lackluster attempt at a roleplay where at the end he admits to have ‘not wanted to finish it’ as well as saying he’ll ‘probably lose anyway’ almost doomed him. He should find Digital Morality and thank him for giving him the win. Jerms Babylon didn’t show, so this was easy as well. I liked how he used the MTV show FEAR as well, very creative. I forgot all about that show. Also, the cameo’s by Hasselhoff and Ron Jeremy especially… what more could someone ask for? Inphino Blitz Vs. Sebastian Moraco Inphino Blitz: Solid RP from a solid character. It’s very easy for Blitz to portray his character with certain moods that captures an audience, and he dragged me in from start to beginning, nice read. Sebastian Moraco: Another nice read, I enjoy this character as well, but there were a few grammatical flaws that I noticed, and I found myself having to be more or less real picky to choose a winner for this one. Really close match from two really good RPers, I wish Sebastian luck where ever else he may go. Pick: Inphino Blitz Note that this was read with a completely open mind, no matter how big of a douchebag I think Marq is. I simply think that Moraco did more with his theme to establish being an unknown than Blitz did with his theme already being known. Pick: Moraco I. Blitz -VERY LONG RP. It however did not bore me to tears as I had expected. Very interesting though repetitive. How many different ways did you plan on explaining the same thing? All in all one of the best rps I've seen this round. S. Moraco -Seen better from the handler and looked like they had trouble with this rp. Too many questions were left unanswered for my taste. What was the conspiracy? What was the assistant coaches motives? What is the nature of the relation between the best friend's GF and Moraco. Seems to be more than meets the eye. In the end it came down to who gave me more of what i wanted to see. I wanted a full story with reason and ideas. While Blitz rp was pure irony of the way the handlers seems to feel (i.e. the handler is in fact doing it for glory despite his little whiny front) The winner is still Inphino Blitz. Pick: Inphino Blitz Joe Black vs Avatar William Gwynn Black- Wow is the best word for this rp. I enjoyed reading it and never found myself complaining. Avatar- Started off strong and fell off at the end. You had me caught up but when it switched to in ring and tt I stopped caring Winner: Joe Black Mr. Darkness -Mr. Darkness you tried too hard to fit the theme word in and thus made it seem ackward at times. The rp seemed to be mostly stuff already seen in WWA and there was hardly any mention of GTT or your opponent until the very last line. You need to change things up if you wish to advance to round three. SSL vs Tangle SSL- Pretty good, but you'll have to do much better if you want to advance. Focus more on telling a story than talking about the tournament. Tangle- Too short. You told little and left me wanting more, which in this case was a bad thing. Had you continued and made it to a more well developed rp instead of just the past then it could have easily beat SSL. Winner: SSL but barely. Wolfenden vs fatbadger Fatbadger: The RP was ok, but lacked a lot from the start. It was more or less your normal boring introduction and I was looking for something unique and creative. Also, please don't use the lime green against a light background again, it killed my eyes to read the text with such a bright focus on it. Karina: Solid RP, no real flaws, great introductory that followed the GTT rules perfectly. Pick: Karina Wolfenden Ahhgh! Fatbadgers font color nearly blinded me! Both roleplays were very well written, with Fatbadger causing me to laugh audibly while Karina’s roleplay synthesized the theme with the character masterfully. Ultimately, my vote goes for Karina. Throughout the piece the reader is taken through the complexity of not just Wolfenden, but of the word ‘complacent’ as well. The ending, also, is just fantastic. Pick: Karina Wolfenden Skar vs Winters Both wrote well. I liked Skar's more. Pick: Skar Cyrus Skar (9.0): Great RP, it wasn't really going to well with me in this particular time... There were times in which the roleplay rather bored me more, like there was nothing to read on about... just that story. This is one of the tougher one's to grade. But I deducted .2 because the theme wasn't in sight of me. John Winters (9.2): Another great RP, theme fit in very well and used appropriately. Character was intro'ed in the right way... I just felt this one clicked more. Pick: Winters An extremely close match, in fact it almost came down to a coin toss. Both writers did an extremely good job and there were very few flaws in either roleplay. I'm giving it to Winters however. I thought his use of the theme was better - his storyline was slightly better in my opinion (maybe sex sells, after all). With this kind of standard in the competition, "the odds look good", indeed. Pick: Winters Equinox vs Lexicon Equinox vs Lexicon - Not what I expected out of an established PTC star like WiG. Lexicon wrote better than expected. Aliso vs Ozz Aliso worked his theme better. Pick: Aliso T.Aliso -You focused more on trash talk than actual story. Problem is the talk was in such huge clusters that it became boring and leaving me not caring. Z. Ozz -Too short and little detail hurt this rp. You made good use of your word but overall I still did not find myself enjoying this roleplay. Pick: Z. Ozz. Enjoyment was the key here. I could finish his rp and not feel I had to force myself to continue to read. Ozz: Well structured, good writing. Nice use of the theme word, however, I didn't think the roleplay went far enough, it showed a good background to the characters, but didn't develop it at all. Aliso: also well structured, good writing. Slightly clichéd in spots but there are some moments that shine. The attention to details and Aliso's character development swing it for me. Aliso gets the win. Nakazura vs Cobb I.Nakazura(GW) vs H.Cobb(LOW) Two very different roleplays. Cobb has almost produced a trash talking TV ad for himself where Nakazura has written an almost too clichéd dream sequence. However Nakazura handled his theme well and his storyline - while clichéd - was solid and well structured. I did enjoy Cobb's writing style, and in other circumstances I probably would have thought his the better piece of writing, but it was, in my opinion, too short and didn't go anywhere or address his theme well. That being said, I'm giving it to Nakazura. Himalayas
