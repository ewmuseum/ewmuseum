High Flyer Biography Biography High Flyer is an eccentric, prankster, hopeless romantic with some slight psychological problems which hamper his personal life and allow him to excel inside of the wrestling ring. His thought process is nothing like any one else in the world, routinely discussing global politics before talking about Howard the Duck without losing a step. He has a hero complex, which puts himself in situations that give him the air of "in over your head." Examples: 2000 : FWF invades the IWO. With inside information, Flyer attempts to stop this by his lonesome only to hit the wrong guy with a sledgehammer. 2003: aWc. Flyer joins and plays the hero to the crowd, but the crowd absolutely detests him, driving him into fits of anger and rage that turn his character from the warm hated "superstar" to an almost bi-polar "hanging on a string" personality he currently abides by. 2002: WWR. Although made as a half hour detective comedy show, Flyer cast himself in the lead, as the 'hero' of the piece. 2003-on. fWo. When Eron left the Rogue stable, Flyer was casted in to the role of leader. Throughout this stage, he slowly isolated himself from his teammates, trying to 'fix' everything. When he got himself into a conflict with Kellen Kinkade, he could not beat him by his normal "hero" tactics and had to resort to crossing that invisible line. Since then, his only remaining friends turned on him and he wound up alone and divorced, bitter and angry. Entering 2005, he put all his problems squarely onto the shoulder of Craig Miles and attempted to "exact retribution." Failing, Flyer fell back on what he knew, playing the hero to the crowd and taking the Cruiserweight strap off of Doctor Curiosity. But it was revealed later in the year that it was all a disguise for Flyer to enact his master plan, injure the upper-main event cluster of superstars and give himself the World Heavyweight Championship. He's now crossed that line. Everyone knows he's crossed that line. So… just how dangerous can he be? http://www.fwowrestling.com/roster/cgi/bio.cgi?highflye
