It opened in July 2001 and closed in February 2008. History of XUW XUW Championships Major XUW Titles * XUW World Title * XUW Intercontinental Title * XUW Tag Team Titles Minor XUW Titles * XUW Television Title * XUW Submission Title * XUW Hardcore Title * XUW North American Title (formerly XUDF North American Title) * XUDF United States Title * Other Past XUW Titles The "Big Three" Events Stable Wars, the Deadly Draw, and the Halloween Battle Royal were referred to collectively as the "big three" annual events in XUW. Stable Wars began in 2002 as an eight-team tournament that took place in one night at XUW's annual Big Brawl pay-per-view. The first two rounds were usually sudden death contests where the first team to score a pinfall won the match; the final was traditionally a four-on-four elimination match. The members of the winning team received title shots of their choosing. The Deadly Draw tournament, established in 2003, was a tournament consisting of 16 tag teams formed by 32 entrants. The teams were determined by a random draw that traditionally took place via a live webcast. The tournament then played out until one tag team remained; the two members of the surviving team then faced each other one-on-one to determine the tournament winner. The Deadly Draw champion received a shot at the World Title in the main event of Reborn. The Halloween Battle Royal first appeared in 2002. Every member of the XUW roster dressed up in a Halloween costume for the match, and their identities were typically not revealed until they were eliminated. The winner of the Halloween Battle Royal traditionally received a World Title shot at a future date. Mayhem Mayhem was XUW's weekly flagship program. It aired regularly on Monday nights from 2001-2006, then began airing on Tuesday nights in 2007 and ran until XUW's close. A total of 222 episodes of Mayhem were broadcast. Vortex Vortex debuted in November 2001 and aired on Thursday nights. It regularly featured the top tier of XUW's talent much in the same vein as Mayhem did. Vortex was cancelled in 2004 after nearly three years on the air. XPosure XPosure was a weekly Sunday program that mainly showcased the lower-card talent of XUW. It began airing in 2002, then was cancelled in mid-2003. It was later resurrected as XUDF XPosure, a Saturday night program that featured the up-and-coming talent of XUDF. Pay-Per-View XUW broadcast many different pay-per-view shows throughout its run. Beginning in 2003, six shows were regularly broadcast on pay-per-view each year: * Reborn - XUW's flagship pay-per-view. Aired in spring of each year from 2002-2007. Split into two shows and billed as "Reborn" and "Reborn Again" in 2006 and 2007. * Cause & Effect - Featured the tag semifinals, tag final, and singles final of the annual Deadly Draw tournament. Aired in the first quarter of each year from 2003-2007. * Big Brawl - Featured the annual Stable Wars tournament. Aired in summer of each year from 2002-2006. * Anniversary Show - Aired near or at the end of August from 2003-2006. The 6th Annivesary Show, XUW's final show, was broadcast on February 18, 2008. * Road Rage - Annual mid-autumn pay-per-view, originally an annual summer pay-per-view before the creation of the Anniversary Show. Aired each August from 2001-2003, and each October from 2004-2006. * December 2 Dismember - The last XUW show of each calendar year before holiday break. Aired every December from 2001-2006. XUW also held an annual November pay-per-view in its earlier years, under the banner of "Double Trouble." * Double Trouble - Original Double Trouble show, aired in November 2001. * More Double Trouble - Aired in November 2002. * Triple Trouble - Aired in November 2003, featured a card consisting heavily of triple threat matches. A number of one-shot pay-per-view shows have also been held throughout the years: * Copyright Infringement - Aired in October 2001. * X-Terminated - Aired in January 2002. * Annihilation - Aired in April 2002. * Sudden Impact - Aired in May 2002. * Liberation - Aired in July 2002. * Dingos & Darkness - Aired in September 2002. * Unknown Factors - Aired in October 2002. * Power & Prestige - Aired in April 2003. * Stamped Authority - Aired in June 2003. * Violent Times - Aired in July 2003. * Farkness & Darkness - Aired in September 2003. * Code Red - Aired in May 2004. * My First Big Wrestling Show - The 2005 edition of Cause & Effect, re-named by then-commissioner Webster Junior.
