RULES The rules of PTC are simple. Joining PTC Please answer the following statements with true or false. My fed is open. My fed is not involved in other interfeds. I fully plan to post regular updates and contribute in the forums. If you answered true to all of these questions, then PTC is for you. Participation PTC is a user driven site. Fedheads and players alike should post news, rumors, columns, etc. and become involved in the forums. At its core, we are a community. If your intention in PTC is only to help your fed, without contributing to the site or community, then this is not the place for you. Wrestler Contracts In PTC there is a simple "contract" rule. Wrestlers are bound to their feds until released by their fed heads. Furthermore, Wrestlers may only wrestle in one PTC fed at a time. Handlers may play different characters in different feds. Trash Talking All trash talk goes on the Trashtalk board. It must be in character and targetted at a specific individual or small group of individuals. Recruiting Free Agents We have a PTC Free Agent forum. If you are a PTC affiliate, you may recruit from this forum, by responding to potential free agent posts. If you wish to advertise for players, you should do so on the ew24-7 advertising forum. Free agents are only signed by your fed once they agree to a contract and appear on a card. An application from a wrestler does not imply they are signed by your fed. Posting to the Main Site Proofread your posts before posting. You may post three posts per section per day. If you need to post more, see a member of the council. Do not advertise looking for wrestlers or staff on the main site. Use the ew24-7 advertising forums to do that. All the categories of the main site are self-explainatory. Top 50 Wrestlers Top 100 Efeds FAQ's Rules FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS Q: What is Primetime Central? A: PTC is a user driven news site which allows fed owners and wrestlers to promote themselves their feds and themselves, while interacting with other fans of the hobby and competing in interfed events. Q: Who owns Primetime Central? A: A superior race of super intelligent monkeys own Primetime Central. Q: How can my fed join PTC? A: Click the join link and fill out the application. Please read the rules section prior to sending your application. Q: I am an ewrestler. How many PTC feds can I be in at one time? A: Each CHARACTER is limited to one PTC fed. You may, however, create multiple characters, which would allow you to become involved in numerous feds. Q: Are there any icky wrestler contracts in PTC? A: Yes and no. We use a simple rule that a wrestler is under contract with a fed until he and the fed head can negotiate a release. If no release can be negotiated, then the wrestler may petition the PTC Council for his/her release. Q: Is this stuff copyrighted? A: Absolutely. Do not steal any images, roleplays or anything else and try to use it on your site. More FAQ's to be added. Also see the rules, as many answers to questions of interest are located there.
