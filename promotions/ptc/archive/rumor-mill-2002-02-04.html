PTC Rumor Mill, January to February 2002

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The Rumor Mill page of Primetime Central’s own site (rumourmill.shtml) as the Wayback Machine captured it: the ten rumor items of 30 January to 4 February 2002. The items are written as gossip about federations and their wrestlers, several of them as jokes, and are served as written. The page’s sidebar lists (newswire, rumor mill, columns, ratings, top ten) are carried by the February 2002 columns page and are not repeated; credits that name a person read the first name and last initial. The page’s navigation and banner are not carried. The capture’s own date is not recorded; the document date is its latest item.
WWA Talking to Undisclosed Wrestlers... 2/04/02 WWA has been in contract with 3 men, All of these men are under classified Information. Mr.Jones Had this to say. "WWA is Making it big time. Wrestlers Leave all the better. There is Stars from all over PTC wanting in the WWA. But the Three im talking about Will be huge trust me." More Information as the contracts are signed or Released ZDW Rumors Report #1 2/04/02 ZDW had its first show but with a different roster than what they had planned. Why is this you ask? We've heard two things going around the locker-room and we'll tell you both the rumors. The roster has been suspected of housing a drug-lord kind of chain and have been shipping out some coccaine and crystal method. They have been busted by officials in ZDW and released of their contracts. The other rumor flying around is the roster wouldn't co-operate with management and were just told to leave the arena. Which is true? We have no idea. But we also have word for their next card that next card they will have 5 more people debut along with the other 5 who were on Sunday night be there. But who are the new stars of ZDW? Yous will just have to find out and listen to the rumors and rants. ZDW GWF possible signing? 2/03/02 Reliable sources say that controversial young superstar, Wes Martin, is in talks with GWF head, Eric Lansheer. According to rumours PTC owner, The Phantom Booker, voided a GWF contract Wes had recently signed pointing out that Mr. Martin was currently a PWA student and not a free agent. The same source says that days later, Martin requested an official release from the PWA from PCW superstar and PWA head, El Diablo. When questioned, Eric Lansheer's gardener was simply quoted by saying, "HEY! Stay off my lawn!" Source: Global Wrestling Federation Playboy Johnny Davis and Jesse Harte 2/02/02 the word is in the back of EPW is that playboy Johnny Davis ,and Jesse Harte are makeing a stable! Now it's just a rumour ,but anything thing can happen in EPW so keep your eyes open! Source: EPW Zero Degrees.. A rumor approaching 2/01/02 There is a rumor going around that Zero Degrees Wrestling is airing a PPV coming Saturday Night or Sunday Morning. This rumor hasn't been confirmed true. Also some confirmed rumors are that 3 former Zero Degrees Champions are returning on the show. Tommy Crimson(ZDW 1), Tony Vigilante(ZDW 2), & Dilly Dawg(ZDW2) are supposed to be returning on the scene. Dilly Dawg is just coming off some knee surgery from an injury in IOW and Tony Vigilante is coming from IOW on a hot streak. Also expect a former ZDW star, High Roller to appear at the show and a member from TFZ, Risk to appear. Another rumor floating around is that Andy Vahamaa currently is not the owner anymore, but it is actually his brother Aj Weston who re-owns ZDW again. We'll just have to wait and see either Saturday, Sunday, or some other day. Zero Degrees Wrestling PCW a Problem? 2/01/02 Cities from all the sites PCW has held its pay per views, weekly cards, and other shows have been showing a greater amount of impatience after a PCW show. Signs show that after a PCW show in their town, the town increases in people reporting to the police station for running a red right, or in severe instances, a car accident. But even after an extensive study from the government of China, Japan and all the other Asian countries PCW has visited, they do not have a cause for this. A Chinese officer, replied by saying this. "You people annoy! You people can't leave good people of China alone! Leave us alone! Stop annoying! STOP IT!!!" More news will be brought along as this investigation of impatience. OSW reaches a new LoW? 1/31/02 Some recent reports have been saying that another former LoW superstar might sign up for PCW's biggest rival. Current OSW superstar formerly of LoW, Great Dragon, claims he was the best in LoW, and it doesn't matter who it. This person might well be revealed on the first edition of Slam! Two time former PCW Cruiserweight Champion, Jake Keeton, is also headed to OSW, according to a published report on the online wrestling sheet, Wrestlingtattler.com. "Despite the fact he is lazy, has a drinking problems and that homosexual episode in the PCW lockerroom, we feel Jake lends us a bit of credibility," said an anonymous source 'deep within OSW' according to the report. "I mean, what's not to love?" Source: Old School Wrestling Zero Was appearantly caught spying 1/31/02 Zero was Appearantly Peeping at WWA World Champion Angie while she was at her home on Wednesday. When He found out her Man Ricky Rage was there they were off to the Races. No reports have led this to be true yet. Source: Jacob Q. - GWF Rolf and Sylo Splitting Up? 1/30/02 After a few weeks of campaigning for President, Rolf the Monkey is said to be gaining a few jealous friends... most importantly, Jay Sylo. When asked on the situation between himself and his manager, all Sylo could say was "I'm a wrestler, not a Politician and so is Rolf.. get over it." When Rolf was asked about it, his main defense was, "Squeak! Squeak! Squeaky, squeak, SQUEAKY!" With that type of response, how can you doubt it? More on this as it comes... Source: Jacob Q. - GWF
