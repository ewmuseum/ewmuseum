Armaan Biography Biography Armaan is the first-ever Indian wrestler to step into the WWA rings. Having come from an established wrestling background in India, Armaan has begun a journey in American professional wrestling to showcase his skills. Armaan has not won an official WWA championship belt, but has the privelige of being crowned the very last EWA champion, the EWA being the WWA's former development fed. After winning the title off Rocky Adams in a TLC match also involved Mr Darkness, the Indian again entered the ring and retained the title miraculously in the brutal Pain Games rumble. Since his return to the ring after a year's hiatus, Armaan has not looked back. Armaan proved to then-Television champion Freddie Arson, as well as the rest of the viewing world, that he is a worthy challenger after withstanding a gauntlet to obtain a TV title shot. Though the Indian ultimately failed in his attempts to win the belt, his angle and culminating ladder match with Freddie Arson was heavily praised. Since then, Armaan has never looked back. A great showing in the Independence Day Rumble in his comeback match was followe shortly by a semi-final exit from the Extreme title tournament, after a gruelling match-up with fellow GTT5 competitor Jonathan Winters. Armaan is now out to showcase his talents to the wider wrestling world, and bring more recognition to not only his own individual talents, but to place the WWA on the map in the process.
