Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The channel pages of Primetime Central’s own site as the Wayback Machine captured them in early June 2002: the TV Guide of the twelve channels (index2.shtml; the same guide heads every channel page), the top ten of the LCW Mercury channel (7.shtml), and the sidebar every channel page shares (14.shtml, 7.shtml and 2.shtml read alike): four dated headline lists, the latest on 3 June 2002, then the top ten wrestlers for the week ending 2 June under the PTC Champion. The XCWF channel profile of 14.shtml is carried by the XCWF profile already on this wing, and the Channel 2 page holds only the sidebar. The ticker script, the images that head each list and the navigation are not carried; the FAQ and Rules pages that carry the same sidebar are served on this wing already.
