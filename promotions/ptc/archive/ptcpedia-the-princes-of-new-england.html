The team, consisting of Connor O'Reily and Simon Knox, are a duo of wrestlers who are proud of the sports heritage of New England, particularly Boston. They were the youngest Tag Team Champions in company history, before their sudden departure from the promotion. History Signing to PRIME shortly after the Culture Shock pay-per-view, the Princes of New England quickly made an impact in more than a few ways. Between their vicious promos against pretty much everybody and their memorable debut at UltraViolence (coming out during the tag title match with a nigh-army of cameramen), the Princes have seemingly made enemies of everyone in the promotion. The team suffered several setbacks in the beginning, however, losing their debut match against Lindsay Troy and Wade Elliott and going on to lose an important tag title match to Team V.I.A.G.R.A. at Colossus V. However, the team began to roar back and during PRIME's three week New England tour, the previously-heel Princes of New England suddenly became one of the most massively popular teams in the company. They went on to defeat VIAGRA at Revolution 173 to finally win the PRIME Tag Team Titles, the final show in the New England tour. They lost the titles back to VIAGRA at Great American Nightmare admidst a sudden departure from the company.
