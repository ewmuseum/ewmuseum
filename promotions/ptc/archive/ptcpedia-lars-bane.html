He has three brothers and a cousin who also wrestle. He frequently teams with his older brother, Fritz Bane. Growing Up Wrestling Career Rising Star Wrestling Lars debuted at the inaugural Pride and Honor show for Rising Star Wrestling against Jason Storm. He was disqualified due to a low blow. He lodged a complaint with management about the conduct of the referee, but his appeal fell on deaf ears. He wrestles as a heel.
