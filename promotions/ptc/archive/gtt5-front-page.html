What this is (written in 2026) The GTT5 site’s front page as it stood late in the tournament: a results log from Round 1 to the semi-finals, the bounties and the additional prize, and the welcome text. It carries no date; its newest entry is the semi-final results, which the organiser’s forum post dates to 2 March 2006, and it does not yet have the final. No capture of the page is held. The page’s navigation and the markup of its headings are not reproduced; its text is.

Bounties! - Whoever eliminates Shanty Jones (handled by GTT4's winner, Andy H.) from GTT5 will be able to nominate a $US50.00 donation to the charity of their choice. - Whoever eliminates Rich Rollins from GTT5 will also be able to nominate a $US50.00 donation to the charity of their choice. - Whoever eliminates Angelo Deville from GTT5 will also be able to nominate a $US50.00 donation to the charity of their choice. (donation courtesy Mat W.) - Whoever eliminates Seymour Almasy from GTT5 will also be able to nominate a $US50.00 donation to the charity of their choice. - If a Chicago resident wins the tournament, they will receive a $20.00 voucher to the Moo and Oink restaurant, courtesy of Al L. If any of the individuals with bounties on them win GTT5, the applicable bounty prize will instead be passed on to runner up. If they are runner up, then they will get to nominate the charity themself (as in, $200 is enough for the winner!). This is in addition to the winner prize of donations made by Buddy D. and Rob W. A personal thank you from me to Buddy, Rob and the two anonymous doners for their kind gesture. Welcome to the official website for the latest installment of PTC's annual open invitation tournament, GTT5. Applications are now closed and the brackets have been announced. However if you missed out, feel free to follow the action by visiting this website every day during the course of the tournament. If you have been accepted into the tournament, please ensure that your login works, as failed logins will not be accepted as an excuse for late roleplays. If you can't get your login and password to work, visit your player bio by clicking on your name on the brackets page, and hit the "Resend Login Info" link. It will be forwarded to the original email address used by you when you applied. As has been discussed here on the PTC forums, Round 1 will span two time periods - Hype and Roleplay. It is important that you read and understand the rules on this, as failure to submit hypes according to the rules will be considered a no-show, and disqualify you from further participation in the tournament. Once the hype period commences, the hypes will be located right here, for all to see. Roleplays will be located on the roleplay board, and from the headshot shortcut bar on the right, once it starts to get populated. Enjoy the tournament!
