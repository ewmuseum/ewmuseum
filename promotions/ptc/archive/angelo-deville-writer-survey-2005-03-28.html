E-Wrestling Personal Information Survey Part 1

28 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Your Name Jeff Your Past Character(s)(including promotions) Technically, I don't have any genuine past characters. All of them were Deville playing different roles. >=) Pump, The Purple-Headed Warrior (WWF [one match], WEEF) - My first stab at ewrestling, this gem ripped off Steiner, The Rock, and a bit of Val Venis to create a genetically ridiculous shit-talking innuendo machine. I try to keep parts of it alive in Deville today. >=) The Dread Pirate Roberts (RPW) - Deville wanted to play a face for the sake of the inevitable turn and the unspoken nyahs that would result. The spoken ones, too. I also wanted to play a pirate, inspiring a fear of pirates in Mat that still fills me with pride. I was actually going to run it as an original non-comedic gimmick at first, but Pete and Mat mocked me too much, so at the last minute I decided to ripoff one of my favourite movies. The Shepherd (Face Off) - Deville wanted to win the tournament because he wasn't allowed to win the tournament. Your Current Character(s) (including promotions) Angelo Deville (PRIME, NFW) - >=) El Cocko Steelo~!, with Pete as El Cocko Erecto~!, Los Cocko Doomo~! (UWF) - If I had died before these characters saw the light of day I could not have died a happy man. Now I can. >=) Other E-Wrestling Contributions (i.e. EW-News Columnist) I'm here for atmosphere. >=) Approximate E-Wrestling Experience in Years First wrote a roleplay in 2000. I don't have five years of direct experience, though... More like two-and-a-half. How did you get your e-wrestling start (include your character, promotion, reason, etc.) In an IRC chatroom. It wasn't actually efedding, but these guys had designed bots to act as referees, then we'd register characters with them and fight it out using a combination of move selection and dice rolling. My character was CrossEyed Cyclops, who pwned. Anyhow, after about half an hour of that this girl linked me to an efed, explained the premise to me, and I was writing my first promo later that night. For the WWF (War War Federation) as Pump, against . . . DUN DUN DUN . . . GONG! The Undertaker. >=) What has been the most important factor influencing your decision to join a particular promotion? (i.e. someone you know recommended it) Back in the day I'd just swing by the roster page, take note of whom were the champions, then spend some time reading roleplays. If the majority of the roleplays didn't interest or entertain me, or if I figured in my infinite egotism that the challenge wasn't there, I'd move on. If the roleplayers were good, I'd go check out the rules and the on-air product, then email the owner. For the most part that's still how I operate, but now I know so many people and there're actual communities instead of Top 100 lists, so the process has changed in little ways. What I put into the game and take out of it is different now, too, so I tend to look at the people behind the characters a little more than the characters themselves. Which one (or two) people in e-wrestling have most positively influenced your path in this hobby? Why? Pete - He's been with me for years, outdoing me, forcing me to outdo him, helping me, keeping me grounded when needed, riling me up when needed... >=) Pete just rules, and I had the honour of experiencing it from the inside. Still do from time to time. >=) Dan - Taught me some of the finer things about running an ewrestling storyline and sorta took me under his wing as my fedhead in FSW. We don't talk a lot anymore because he's too busy looking like Chavo, but between his example, his friendship, and his brutal honesty, he's definitely been a positive influence. >=) Mat - Another guy that helped by example, his roleplaying was the biggest reason I initially joined this community. It didn't take long for me to come to respect damn near everything else about him, and the damn near is only there because he was a vegetarian. Mat's like the pinnacle of what an efedder should strive to be. Technically, all of my friends have positively influenced my path in the hobby in some way, as have the majority of the people I've worked with and am currently working with. The PWA and PTC in general as well. Without them I doubt I'd still be in the game. What do you consider your greatest e-wrestling storyline? Why? Angelo Deville and Jason O'Neil / Peter Maxwell It started in mid-'02 and has spanned five federations (though most of the planning died as just that). By popular conjecture it defined the beginning of the rebirth of FSW, saw Deville pretend to murder O'Neil in PWC (spawning the Deville / Magnum Wolf feud), and experienced a symbolic climax when O'Neil literally killed Deville. We've more than hinted that they've been in cahoots the entire time, including with the murder (though O'Neil didn't buy Deville's guarantee of the result), but there's always been an underlying distrust -- or corruption -- in their alliance, and it's come to violence and real scheming more than once. This feud/angle really helped me define and develop Deville, and it was the foundation for countless other storylines and angles. Someday, somewhere, it'll pick up again. >=) Individual storyline-wise my favourite is The Deville making a deal with The Devil. It's usually so cliched and executed so pathetically, yet so pwnerful in concept, that I had to explore it. Plus it affected a bunch of other storylines, heightening some while bringing a satisfying close to others (like Salvatore). I'll probably return to it in the near future, as there's one final avenue I want to pursue. What do you consider your greatest e-wrestling match? Why? Results-wise or in a competitive sense? In a competitive sense I'll say Deville v. Kriegman. This match was so long in coming (OOC) and we hyped the hell out of it (IC and OOC >=), making people care about it despite the fact that the characters' main motivation for winning was simple pride, being the guy to knock the other off. We actually made it seem like it was a long time coming in-character. To this day I still want to argue that it was. I think it's because Dan and I cared about it so much. There was a respect and an excitement and . . . It was just really fun. It went right down to the wire with a tie-breaking judge, and when the dust settled Kriegman became the second-ever Universal Champion and Deville experienced his first competitive loss in PTC. Results-wise there are way too many to choose from. What do you consider your overall greatest piece of writing in e-wrestling? Why? Hmmm. I dunno. I've written a lot of great pieces. >=) Seriously, I have no clue. Trying to think about it, none really stand head and shoulders above the rest, and whenever I start to settle on one another pops up. I'll just wuss out and say I have like 20 favourites, then spare you their listing. >=) Next to your home promotion, which promotion do you currently consider to be your favorite (if you follow any other promotions)? Why? I don't routinely follow any federation I'm not involved in, but ACW's shows have entertained me the few times I've pulled one up. PTC I follow more on an interfed level than a fed-by-fed basis. I check out the odd show, but for the most part my "extracurricular" reading is of the roleplaying variety. If you had the opportunity to work with up to five people you have never worked with before, who would they be? Why? (Note: You cannot choose someone who has ever worked in the same promotion with you) Ever? Shit. ... Your note be damned. I'm just going to list people I want to work with, but never have. >=) As Deville Tsunami Oni - He brazenly flouts the fact that he has more nicknames than Deville, and that's somewhere you don't wanna go. >=) I just like the character and think we could do cool things. We'd play well off one another. For those who want to bring up the fact that Oni and Deville have worked together, when Oni misted Deville twice in that cluster tag, I say to thee pfft. I had very little to nothing to do with that, thus I don't count it. Doctor Curiosity - I don't even have to explain. >=) Violence Jack - Deville could out-evil him. >=) Token Weed - He always pitches me crazy ideas but they're never any good. >=) Nah... It's your ass, Brock, and you know it. >=) Everyone Who Claims Deville's Ducking Them - I'm only a PM away. >=) Holy fucking smiley army... Anyhow. As Los Cocko Doomo~! Los Diablos - So Petis and I could show Dean what shattering Mexican stereotypes is really about. >=) Each And Every Last One Of Your Moms - Because LCD~! is crude like that, vato. >=)
