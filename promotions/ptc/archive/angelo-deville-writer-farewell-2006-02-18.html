So...

18 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I had this plan. Actually, I had about a dozen, accounting for different scenarios. Not a one of them, believe it or not, involved "screwing over" anyone, and some of them mutated in spite of intention. Others, especially the main, evolved as if by divine intervention. I've known for over a year that GTT5 would be the final resting place of Angelo Deville. I'd also known from the week Andrew won the right to name his first opponent in the tournament that my round one match was etched in stone. To save fellating Andrew too much, suffice it to say I also knew I was likely going down. Part of it was because of how hard Andrew owns, part of it was because of how masterfully I knew he'd play me, and part of it was because, perhaps pseudo-prophetically, my storyline was designed to start off slowly, to confuse people and make them underestimate what I was doing and where I was planning to take it. It struck me as ingenious (still does, honestly) to contrast the hype with the sort of bland monotony that would make my opponents think "Sweet. He really is overrated." (You might think that's a low tactic, and I won't argue at length with you, but... too many people write only what they think they need to write to survive, and it has always been my philosophy to exploit any and every weakness possible.) That storyline -- that plan -- was the amnesia schtick and the insanity to follow. The insanity was going to rule, btw, but that's neither here nor there. Up through the hype period I was sold on it, and with the opportunity to actually earn a playin, I knew it would help my plan to lose to Rollins (there would be an initial period of "Deville's going to be on a rampage!", but after continuing to halfass it for another round -- employing the "only what I need to survive" tactic I just badmouthed -- the tune would change). In fact, I foreshadowed it at the end of my hype video, where it may have struck some people as odd that it was Deville hunting Rollins rather than the other way around. Anyhow, instead of going through with the plan, on the day roleplaying began I decided it wouldn't do to job to Andrew after all, for two reasons: first, I knew he'd wanted the match since we first started talking way back when he was in the PWA, the feeling became mutual as time passed, and I respect(ed) him too much to cheat him; second, "Fuck you, bitch. You wanna call me out? >=)". I thought to own him with a one-off, counter-epic piece. Win or lose the plan would still go forward, starting the next round... If I lost, people would think I'd fallen off... If I won, people would think I'd burned myself out. As everyone knows, Andrew came, saw, and pwned, and I lost. I was happy with my piece, but moreso for my decision to initially abandon the plan once we discovered there would only be one (two) playin spots. Still, I always hedge my bets when I can, so I read the better part of every contested roleplay and tried to determine whom the judges would advance, in case I didn't. I won the playin spot, and so, with a minor re-write, the plan chugged along unhindered. All along a major part of the plan was to disappear from the ooc community, however, the reaction to it was the one thing I failed to anticipate. Maybe it's because I no-showed my PRIME match during that hiatus (that was never my intention, btw... I had a sweet roleplay in mind for that match, but then I pulled a Jeff and put off writing it until the last night [off-topic... remember when pulling a Jeff entailed roleplaying within an hour of the period beginning?], then screwed that over by pulling a Ryan [not Hooters, but as good as]). That was the first match I ever no-showed, and I still feel bad about it, but... Despite it, it never occurred to me that my disappearance would lead people to jump to the conclusion that I was dead or in jail, and I certainly never fathomed that anyone -- especially Ryan -- would think Ryan had a bye. Egocentric as I am, I figured people knew me well enough to expect I was up to something. Plus, I'd just done all of my WotY campaigning, so I wanted a little break. I went a while without even checking any forums, up until about an hour before the sandbagging deadline. I'd been planning to post two roleplays all along, and I had my first one written (it was only the first and second scenes of what I posted, and the second scene ranted in a completely different direction), with the third scene of what I posted in my mind as a stand-alone, much-deeper-explored, heart-wrenching piece. Then I saw all the shit on the forums. A lot of it pissed me off, especially Ryan pretending he just threw something together for the hell of it when he knew I knew he had a roleplay written in advance from the round before, but mostly... I don't really know how to explain it... I felt hollow. The whole thing felt hollow. I knew how people were going to view the situation. Perceived as a cheater, I felt cheated. I decided I'd job to Ryan and make the disappearance permanent. I rewrote the second scene from the "No, seriously. Fuck you." part on, turning it into an ooc rant of obnoxious doom I was sure would offend any judge who read it. For the third scene, instead of getting introspective and exploring how Deville accepted the chanting and why, I channelled instead what I was feeling and tried to end it with the reader wondering whether or not I'd really just called him an asshole. I won't get deep into it, but... Actually, I won't get into it at all. Suffice it to say I only checked the forums after that out of morbid curiosity and a need for finality. If judging even happened, I expected it to be a reaming. A bunch of people PMed and emailed me with various worries for my sanity... Those made me smile (especially Andrew's, which for the most part hit the intent dead on), but at the same time they made the hollowness echo. How could I possibly explain it? Before long the results were posted, and as I started reading through the decision it immediately dawned on me that the words belonged to Ben. That was like being kicked in the nuts, because I've craved his Uber-Judgement ever since I first witnessed an example in my email inbox for the UTT all those years ago. He's the only person I will freely admit knows more about writing and interprets it better than I do. I think my initial response to his first uber-judgement was "Holy shit... You're even smarter than I am." (don't fear the ego; feed it) Anyhow, from then on, every time I competed in a match with published comments I yearned for them to be his... Yearned for a dissection beyond what I myself could do. Wondering what sort of child-molester I must've been in a past life to have the realization of that desire coincide with the only time I've ever tried to throw a match, I bitterly read on, saddened I'd let down a mentor. But Ben gave me the benefit of the doubt, focusing on the merit of the presentation and feeling rather than chomping down on the bait of shallow animosity I littered throughout. That was enough to revitalize me, to fill the emptiness with hope. I embraced my plan anew, altering it slightly to comment on the situation (I wrote half of my first roleplay for the following round, opening with a letter from a friend of mine who had the unfortunate burden of nonchalantly informing the community of my death and begging for kind words of remembrance >=), and logged onto AIM incognito to squash the still-persevering nonsense about something unfortunate having happened to me to prevent me from coming around. I wavered day to day... One moment I was gung-ho to compete, and the next I didn't see the point. I felt guilty for my supposed "screwing over" of Ryan, I was still pissed over the reaction, and worse, I felt guilty for feeling pissed. A part of me even went so far as to try to convince myself that it was unfair to the competition for me to continue. So it was that on the night of the sandbagging deadline, as I sat down to finish my first piece for the fourway, I contemplated my dilemma. I could continue on under the stigma of the people who'd already made up their mind that after all these years I spontaneously transformed into an ignoble, amoral, cheating bastard, and perhaps in time try to make them see they had me wrong... or, I could hang up my boots, retiring as I simultaneously was named PTC WotY for 2005, defeated Ryan's ever-trashtalking ass one roleplay to one roleplay, received Uber-Judgement, and told the world to piss off. Say whatever you want, but you can't deny that's a hell of a way to retire. That's exactly what I was thinking when epiphany struck. The hollowness wasn't something newly foisted onto me. In fact, external emptiness as I was perceiving it doesn't even exist. As hard as I've been denying it for the better part of a year, the hole is in me, of me. I've succeeded in filling it from time to time (especially with Dean, Mat, Chris, and Sky in PRIME), but even while I enjoyed it, it was a battle. It shouldn't have been -- it shouldn't be -- but it was and is. Realizing that, realizing why, it was obvious to me the time had come to move on. And so, this explanation notwithstanding, I have. Ewrestling for me has been for the most part a wonderful experience. I've met some really cool people, and we engaged friendships and creative alliances purely unique to this hobby. We achieved an intimacy and brotherhood rarely experienced outside of this space, building and honestly critiquing, growing, pushing ourselves and one another to perpetual improvement. I'll always cherish that, and the people that made it possible. I'm not going to specifically name people and give props or whathaveyou, one, because I've always preferred this sort of thing in private, and two, because you know who you are. Likewise for the people who suck, the monkeys I wouldn't piss on if they were burning in the desert; you assholes know who you are, too. Anyhow... I'm going to chop this now... I've been debating whether or not to write it for about a month now (uber-retiring and disappearing all in one is cool, but I owe some of you more than that), and if you made it through to this point, there you have it. I'd have done this sooner, and I'd have come around to chat, but I knew I needed to distance myself, to prevent myself from filling the hole with another charade. There's so much more I could say -- perhaps that I should say -- but I won't. Instead... I'm sorry, Argyle, for no-showing your tournament. I wasn't intending to waste your time, nor was I attempting to frustrate you with my disappearance (or my subsequent pwnery of Ryan >=), but I know that I did, so I'm sorry. You're one of the few I have infinite respect for. I'm sorry, Sebs, for bailing on PRIME. I'm sorry to the people I've reamed over the years for no good reason, especially you, Jay. And I'm sorry to the participants of the two matches I no-showed, even if some of you considered it a favour. >=) Oh, and I'm sorry to you, Pete, for only mentioning you once in my farewell, and that only to point out that I'm younger, more handsome, and hung much lower than you. >=) For my friends: I'll catch you on AIM. For the people who decided to write me off as an asshole over how they interpreted my plan without even trying to ask me about it, those who talked mad shit just because I wasn't around, those who flew or rallied to the banner proclaiming me a bastard: go fuck yourself. For the rest of you: kick ass, have fun, and always, always, always remember... this fucking smiley is mine. >=) o/ PS - I made this whole thing up. The real reason I left when and how I did was to spite Mat by forcing him to donate money on Will's behalf. >=) PPS - Stop being ashamed of being in this hobby, for fuck's sake. It's cool, it's fun, and hell, I was involved for almost six years... That last bit by itself is enough to name it a noble endeavour. The next time you're feeling down about it, remember me, and this: http://thedeville.angrypete.net/muahahaha.mp3 >=)
