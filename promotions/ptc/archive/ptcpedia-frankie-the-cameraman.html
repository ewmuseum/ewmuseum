He is most well known for being the only 6 time Untamed champion in Next Generation Wrestling. He currently uses the moniker of "The Frankie". Early Life TBA Career Next Generation Wrestling N/A Hate N/A Return to Next Generation Wrestling N/A Encore Entertainment N/A Car Accident and Coma During the months after the closure of NGW and during his stints in Global, WMW and Encore Scottywood battled with an addiction to alcohol which many believed hurt his performance in the ring, as he would routinely come to the ring buzzed if not drunk. After Encore closed Scotty's condition continued to deteriorate, as he had nothing to do all day but drink and play video games. Scotty's wake up call came on April 12th at 2:30 in the morning when he was driving back from the bar with friend Frankie the Cameraman and Scotty swerved to miss what he thought was a deer, which was a stop sign and clipped a NYPD cruiser and rolled his car 3 times before it crashed into the display window of a local hardware store. Placed under arrest for DUI and sent to the hospital for what would luckily only be a broken right forearm, bruised ribs and kneed and several cuts and lacerations. Frankie was taken to the hospital unconscious and diagnosed with a severe concussion and was in a coma. Scotty was released 2 days later and sent to Riker’s Island where he spent 2 weeks before he was released and the charges dropped due to Scotty’s high priced lawyers, which was rumored to have been paid for by Damon Alexander. His Lawyers arguing that the police did not issue Scotty his Miranda rights when he was arrested. After being released from prison he gave up alcohol and started his training again and got himself to a trim 265 pounds. Though with Frankie still in the hospital there was a heavy amount of guilt on his shoulders and he started to slip into a dark state of mind which started to scare many close to him considering his already pre existing enjoyment for violence and anarchy. He started shutting out everyone from his life and for a good month was hardly even seen from. A wide range of rumors started to spread from he was playing internet poker and making 6 figures to he was planning the return of NGW, and one website even said he was “The Batman”. While he was making money during his month of isolation in his New York apartment no one is sure how the money was coming in, but many think Scotty is getting paid by Damon Alexander for a job he did while still in Encore, though if this is true it is being done off the books. High Octane Wrestling Also during these months, Frankie the Cameraman came out of his coma, but things were no longer the same. Scottywood thought Frankie was nothing but a distraction to him, holding him back. Frankie tried to patch things up, until Scottywood snapped in a Chicago McDonalds and SDTed him onto the floor, leaving him in a pool of blood. Frankie was dianosied with another concussion and started to suffer from amnesia. Not knowing who attacked him, Frankie searched, but due to alleged pay offs by Damon Alexander, no evidence was found. He got a job in HOW as a cameraman, which lead to his memory coming back after suffering a chair shot while being the cameraman for the House of Pain match at ICONIC. Since then Frankie has been a thorn in Scottywood's side, vowing to get payback. Frankie appears on a TV show on the newly relaunched High Octane Television Network, and it is entitled H! True Octane Story. The show has been known to reveal shocking "facts" about fellow HOW superstars.
