Mike Wade is a former professional wrestler from Waterford, ireland. Wade is probably best known from his time in Atlantic Wrestling Club (AWC) where he was the first person ever to hold three titles at the same time. He was inducted in the AWC Roll of Legends in June 2006. Starting Out Mike started out in the Liverpool (UK) based wrestling promotion DIWA back in January 2000 where he wrestled as "Mr Showtime". Soon after making his debut he wrestled under the ring name "TEE KAY". During his time in DIWA and finding his feet in the business he met his longtime companion and manager, the personality disordered midget "Mickey Moore". Mike and Mickey Wherever Mike Wade has gone. From DIWA to PRIME, from NTICW to AWC Mickey Moore has joined him. Mickey claims to be a 532 year old leprechaun from Ireland. Mickey is in fact Michael Bigg from Horsham, PA and is a 2 foot 5 inch midget.
