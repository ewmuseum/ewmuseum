Match History Deville’s career match record is severely under-reported due to there being no archive available for his first two ventures into the wrestling world, with War War Federation (he wrestled one match there against the Undertaker) and World Extreme E-Fed (where he retired titles, was a grand slam champion, and wore three belts – including the Heavyweight Championship of the World – when he had to leave the company to serve a prison term.) What we do have a fairly accurate accounting of is what came afterward, with the return of Deville to the wrestling world in a quaint little place called WIW, well before it became a part of PTC. He stayed there for two weeks, capturing the Television Title and a shot at World Gold, before embarking for the Primetime Wrestling Academy.
