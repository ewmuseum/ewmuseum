What this is The index of the Primetime Central forums as the Wayback Machine captured it in February 2002, the one Ikonboard page of the PTC forums that was captured open: the boards of PCW, the PTC tournament and the member federations, each with its description, who led it, its counts of topics and replies and its last post (the latest is 18 February 2002), then who was online and the board statistics. The posters’ and leaders’ names print as handles; those built on a real name read the first name and last initial; a registered character’s billing stands. The page’s navigation, icons and member links are not carried. The capture’s own date is not recorded; the document date is its latest post.
