SCCW LiveWire Title

captured 25 Jan 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sin City Championship Wrestling's tertiary championship, currently held by Baron von Blackberry, who won the title at Temptation: Devil's Advocate on September 1st, 2009. This is his first reign. A wrestler with four successful title defenses of the LiveWire Championship is granted a shot at the SCCW Gateway Title. * Livewire Match Rules * Matches have a ten minute time limit. * There are three rope breaks allowed per wrestler, per match. * Disqualifications are strictly enforced and the title CAN AND WILL change hangs on one. If the champion is disqualified, he loses his title.
