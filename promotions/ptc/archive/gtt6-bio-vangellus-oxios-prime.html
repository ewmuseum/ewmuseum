Vangellus Oxios Biography Biography Vangellus Oxios was a nerd when he was younger. Well, not necessarily a nerd, since he wasn't the greatest in the acedemics department. More just geekish looking. He was tall and gangly, didn't really look all that good, and was way too goofy for his own good. That didn't help anything. When he graduated high school, he wanted to make a fresh start for himself. He left home and took fighting training. Boxing, kickboxing, marginal MMA… though he was still rather lanky, he made it work for him. What he would learn was that his entertaining brand of humor would delight the fans and frustrate his opponents. So he would go on to amass a respectable winning record in stand-up striking contests. He avoided grappling contests since that was not his forte. By becoming a public figure, he gained himself a great number of endorsement deals. He was quickly becoming one of the most marketable men in the world, and he had hundreds of products with his name and/or face attached to them. Seeking a new challenge, Oxios entered the world of professional wrestling early in 2004. Seeing it as a growing market, one where his face was not as well known, he wanted to jump in at the ground floor. Though he was some weeks late, he still made it 'early' in the history of Rush Pro Wrestling in Charlotte, North Carolina. Vangellus Oxios would debut against El Diablo in a #1 contendership match to the Heavyweight title. Although he didn't win, he still entertained and showed off his own special brand of combat. As a gimmick, he would list the names of moves based on product endorsements, and perform the moves related to those names throughout the course of a match. This would continue for a little while. Oxios had a losing record to start in RPW, but never lost his stride or his smile. And the fans had only good things to say about him. Even if he wasn't winning matches, he was still consistently one of the highlights of the show with his antics. Eventually, he would begin to amass wins, defeating the likes of Chris Storm, Hessian, Chris Collins, and so forth. His hard work paid off with a title win, capturing the Eclipse Title from indy sensation Gideon Poe in a three-way match against Aimz. The federation would close prior to Vangellus losing the title. Van took a year off from wrestling, during which time no one really knows what happened to him. But when he returned to wrestling along with Gabriel Brown (the former Invincible Gabe of RPW) to form a tag team known as Teh Pwn, everyone knew that they were going to be in for a treat. It didn't turn out that way, mostly as Brown didn't want to be a team wrestler anymore. But Oxios didn't let that stop him; he went to Gabriel's old partner Scott "Rampage" Matthews and turned them into Teh Greatness. Though Oxios's partner has changed, his dedication to the team has not, and he has reaffirmed it by capturing the Tag Team Titles twice, and vowing to revitalize the division and strike out into singles wrestling as well.
