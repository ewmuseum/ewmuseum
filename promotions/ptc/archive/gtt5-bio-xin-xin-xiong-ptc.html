Xin Xin Xiong Biography Biography Born and bred in West Point, NY until the age of 13. At that age, he was sent to Japan to study ninjitsu under his grandfather, Naito Futeki, as he was the last in a long line of masters in their clan. While in Japan, he worked as a janitor and member of the ring crew for the wrestling promotion Toryumon to pay the bills since his grandfather was as he put it "a lazy bum" and didn't work. One day, he and one Nobuyoshi Nakamura (a.k.a. the famous Dragon Kid) got into a heated debate and X3 took out the masked fighter. This caught the attention of Ultimo Dragon who started to train the young ninja in the ways of puroresu. The upstart showed amazing prowless in the art of human chess and developed a strategy based off his favorite wrestler, Keiji Mutoh. In no time at all, Xiong had become a master at both ninjitsu and puroresu and things looked to be going well for him. That was until his father became deathly ill with brain cancer. He rushed back to America just in time to see his father one last time. He remained with his mother, paying the bills by working as a bouncer in between wrestling on the independent circuit. Now, he steps into the ring in the big times, looking to show the world what a little mixture of old school martial arts and new school wrestling can do. *Key facts* - Is half Chinese (Father) and half Japanese (Mother). - The lone remaining member of his family's clan (Mother's side). Here is a good history on ninjitsu: http://www.bujinkan.co.za/H1.html - Has a vertical leap of 40" second only to Spud Webb's 42" in the world record standings. - Only uses his poison mist (red or green)to surprise a foe or as a last ditch effort to survive. - The ladies love him to the point that he has tons and tons groupies. - He wears face paint under his mask, so his true face is still hidden should his mask be removed. - His body is covered in various tattoos. Most importantly his Black Widow tattoos which symbolize each master of rival ninja clans that he has defeated. -He has no problem running his mouth to anyone, man, woman, or child, and will any chance he gets. -Has a proactive stance on dealing with heels and isn't liked much by faces either, as he sees them as just waiting victims. -Will show a little supernatural aspect to him in tourney rps.
