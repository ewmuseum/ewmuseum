PRIME Alias Title

captured 19 Aug 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The PRIME Alias Championship was a lower tier Championship, dubbed the gimmick championship. The wrestler who had possession of the Championship would rename the belt by another name. The Championship was dissolved on ReVolution 129.
