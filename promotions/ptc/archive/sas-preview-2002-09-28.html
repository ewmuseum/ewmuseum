UWF: Sunday Afternoon Supremacy (Preview)

28 Sep 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SAS Card THC Title Match The Plague (c) -v- Jay Phoenix This is the main event of the card. The match came about after Jay Phoenix made his shocking return at Defining Moment by aligning himself with Lew Spectre. Plague who is The THC Champion, took the opportunity to issue a challenge to Phoenix to get back at Lew and to prove that he can go toe to toe with a multi-time World Heavyweight Champion. Jay Phoenix on the other hand thinks The PLague is a no one and when the collide at Supremacy on man will walk out proving that he is as good as they say they are Nine Man Tag Team Match Digital Mortality & Local NVA Company -v- Tsunami Oni & Thunder & Lightning -v- The Legends & Slasher Thsi match is a preview of Live and Loud 2, which will see Tsunami Oni going against Digital Mortality for The IC Title. This feud has been going beyond the limits. Tsunami and Thunder & Lightning formed the alliance of The Storm Front to end The Communist Regime in The UWF. The Iron Curtian has been a dominate stable since Stable Wars a few months back. The NVA have held The Tag Titles multiple times, and Digital Mortality is goiong on his third month as Champion. The Legends have been thrown into the Tag Title scene, and now that they have brought back Slasher, this new force is looking to show that they could truely be Legends. Television Title Match Purple Haze (c) -v- Killer Dude Purple Haze is looking to be an impressive superstar in The UWF, gaining The TV Title in no time, defeating the Number 2 ranked man on The PTC "The Final Fantasy" Seymour Almasy a week ago. That was a very impressive victory and has already makes Purple Haze a big time player in The UWF. As for Killer Dude, he has been a bit of a dissapointment as of late, dispite being a two time TV Champion, not to mention the first ever UWF TV Champ, Killer Dude seemed to have lost his way. And now with a chance to be a three time champ, who knows if this will end his slump. SW Title Tournament -- 1st Round (First Blood Match by Starr's Request) Misfit -v- Kevin Starr The Shoot Wrestling Title Tournament, is looking impressive so far. And this match will be no different. Kevin Starr, a former Shoot Wrestling Champ, who was screwed by The Legends, is looking to get revenge on them and regain his title. But he will have to get through Misfit, who has been going up and up here in The UWF. Thsi will be a classic and a first Blood Match at that. Supremacy will definatly be a show you will not want to miss, and with a week, to Live and Loud 2, anything can and will happen Source: UWF: The Place to Be
