Global Title Tourney Rolls Forward 1/27/02 80 participants, with representatives from all but one PTC affiliate, put their names on the dotted line in the first 72 hours of signups for the Primetime Central Global Title Contenders Tourney, set to kick off on Saturday, February 16. Highlights of the signees are GWF Hall of Famer Justin Marks, CCWers Andrew "Ace" Hawk and Kensuke Suzahara, NTICW superstar Jason Jameson, BMW dynamo Kid Krazie, WWCF worker Tyler Davidson, PCW veteran Miyagawa, ProWC's Commander, Ghetto Thug of WWA, Johnny Reno of XCWF, Jesse Harte of EPW and PWA's Jake Jones. PTC officials expect a hard fought tourney and anyone can win it, though the winner will have no easy time actually securing the belt from PTC Global Champion, Vampir Nosferatu. For a complete list of the signees thus far, or to sign up for the tourney, click the link below! Source: Sign up for the PTC Global Title Tourney! Have some pride in your fed! Primetime Central Relaunches! 1/22/02 PTC is back and with eleven top notch organizations involved, is stronger than ever. Returning to PTC are the Global Wrestling Federation, New True Internet Championship Wrestling, the Primetime Wrestling Academy, Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, and of course, host federation Primetime Championship Wrestling. Incoming feds include the rapidly rising Big Money Wrestling, the Elite Wrestling Alliance, Pro Championship Wrestling, Xtreme Championship Wrestling Federation, World Wide Chaos Federation and Old School Wrestling. With top stars like WWCF's Tyler Davidson, OSW's Great Dragon, BMW's Donomite and Kid Krazie, EWA's Tony Vigilante, ProCW's Carl Cash, XCWF's Shawn Boyd, GWF's Jay Sylo, NTICW's Semore Smoke, CCW's Blayze and Jason McAllister, PCW's Tyler Nelson and Kid Wonder and PWA's Drek Redaxe things are sure to boil over quickly in the battle to attain PTC's ultimate individual honor, the Primetime Central Global Title belt. Obtaining the belt will not be easy, however, as PTC officials have announced that 128 men will battle for the right to be named the #1 Contender for the PTC Global Title, which is still in the cold, pale, hands of PCW's Vampir Nosferatu, who never lost the belt prior to PTC's relaunch. Be sure to check out the forums to chat with wrestlers and fans, talk a little trash, or showcase your talents. No matter what your level, goals or preferences are, PTC has something for everyone!
