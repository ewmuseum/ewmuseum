Peter Lenex Biography Biography Lenex has had a very detailed history, dating back to 2001 when he made his in-ring debut. He has been going strong since then, and has managed to rack up an impressive record, riddled with title reigns here and there. Now, he wrestlers for the PWX and in his spare time, he tests his hand in tournaments. Currently, he considers himself the God of War, and prides himself on being a true fighting machine. His career has had many peaks and valleys, but the moment that he called himself the God of War is one that can be considered a peak. Overall, Lenex has been apart of a few federations. They include: UWO: Ultimate Wrestling Organization (Hall of Fame) ACW: American Championship Wrestling IWF: International Wrestling Federation JWF: Joyfire Wrestling Federation CSWA: Championship Wrestling Association ECFW: Extreme Championship Fatal Wrestling UCWF: Ultimate Championship Wrestling Federation PWX: Pro Wrestling X Career Record: 109-42-15
