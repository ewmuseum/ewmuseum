New-Found File:nftcpedia.png Type | Hybrid Founded | Headquarters | New York City, New York Key people | Darcsyde, Corvus, Jackson Programming | Elite Combat, Zero Tolerance Website | New-Found New-Found (NF) is an active federation that is part of the Primetime Community. ReVolution was once the weekly show of PRIME. The air date varied until the product was moved to HBO and now the date of air is varied anywhere from 9 to 13 days apart. To date, ReVolution has been televised 227 times. History New-Found came to be from New-Found Wrestling, originally running nearly six years ago. The federation has been through many sets of hands and even worked as Global's developmental territory under the ownership of Joshua Kosidlo. Currently, New-Found is being run at a high end independent level, with talent from all over the market. New-Found Television Elite Combat Main Article: Elite Combat Elite Combat is a bi-weekly show aired on MSG at 10pm EST. The show is an hour long. Zero Tolerance Main Article: Zero Tolerance A weekly webshow aired on nfw-online featuring a match or two along with the a talk show The Last Laugh hosted by Corvus. Pay-Per-View Broadcasts Pay-Per-View events are the culmination of the month's biggest rivalries, and sometimes the beginning of new ones. New-Found hosts a series of PPV Events through out the year. No-Holds Barred Main Article: No-Holds Barred No-Holds Barred is a night where every match is fought under No-Holds Barred rules. The event was created to pay homage to former PTC fed Global. The very first No-Holds Barred aired April 26th 2009 and was highlighted by a major grudge match pitting two arch nemeses Joshua Kosidlo and Corvus against one another, with the stipulation that if Corvus won the match he would be entitled to one wish. Corvus cashed in his one wish when Joaquin Pierre was unable to compete in the New-Found Heavyweight Championship match due to lack of medical clearance and would end up defeating then champion Darcsyde to capture his first New-Found title. Retribution Main Article: Retribution Retribution aired July 12th 2009 on PPV. The event was headlined by a Steel Cage Match for the New-Found Heavyweight Championship. The contest saw Darcsyde take on the reigning champion Corvus. The event also saw the onset of a New-Found Tag Team Division when Sean Molson and Jacque Noreault defeated Morbyd Theology. Retribution also led to the birth of a new championship, or a revision of one. As a result of the popularity of the Death Match for the New-Found Television Championship the title went under some changes. Dark Ages Main Article: Dark Ages Dark Ages is set to air live on PPV from Lawrence, Kansas in the Allen Fieldhouse on August 30th 2009.
