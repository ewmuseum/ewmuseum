PTC: New Era — Pre-Show

19 April 2003 (announced card)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PTC I-PPV -- "New Era" Pre-Show On April 19th, 2003, PTC will host a Pre-Show event in the buildup to the New Era Card. Events include: PTC Baseball - Two teams of the standard number of players. There will be 3 innings consisting of 4 outs. Most anything goes. There is a keg at every base...if you make it the base you have to drink a cup of beer. Pirates Of The Sylicone Valley (Jackson Rust, Chryste) -v- The Stacked and Relaxed Ass Kickers (Tyrone Grace & Annihilation Drinking Contest - Shot for shot, drink by drink. Each wrestler takes a drink then another then another...try to hold in your liquor or dont pass out or else you loose! Drinkers Include: Chryste, Jackson Rust, Dallas Reeves, Callisto Bane, Roulette, Jack O'Lantern Freestyle Competition - You think that you know the rhymes that can win over a crowd? Well put your mouth to the mic and lets see what you are really made of. Rappers Include: Chryste, "The Violence" Vince Jones, Roulette, Inphino Blitz Best Finisher - Every wrestler has a finsher, the move that really showcases the wrestler at its best. But when you put the best against the best, which finisher truely does what it name says? Wrestlers Participating: Chryste, Julius Corbin, Donny Diamond, Shane Robertson, "The Violence" Vince Jones PTC Football - Now that NFL is over...PTC is proud to present to you its first football game. Weapons are linned against the sidelines, and the only rules are those that Trigger himself will make. What does this mean for the game and how it is played? Team 1: Annihilation, Tyrone Grace, Lord Monstar -v- Team 2: Surge Mock That Bitch Contest - Having a feud with an annoying opponent? Feel that someone is way too overrated? Well lets see how you portray them in this competition where the more insulting and true you are, the better you will be. Mockers Include: Chryste Mocked: Storm +Tessa Mini-Golf Havoc - What's more relaxing than playing a nice round of MiniGolf, than playing it with your rival? Do you think that you can survive with your enemy and still sink a hole in one at the windmill? Participants Include: Shane Stone & The Bum Boozler (Homo/Phobic), Dan Logan and Cyrus Skar Tug of War - Interfed matches prove which wrestler from which fed is better, but when ALL the wrestlers are against ALL the wrestlers in another fed, then who truely is the better fed? Biggest Bump Contest - Falling is fear that many people have, even though its the part when you stop that hurts the most. How high can you can you go and fall...only to get up again and fall from somewhere higher? Fallers Include: Jackson Rust Knowledge Knockout - The physical part of wrestling is a must, but so is the knowledge. So with all fighting aside, which wrestler is really the strongest mentally? Contestants Include: Julius Corbin, Dallas Reeves, Fathom, Cyrus Skar, Shane Robertson, Donny Diamond, "The Violence" Vince Jones Would YOU like to participate in the New Era Pre-Show?
