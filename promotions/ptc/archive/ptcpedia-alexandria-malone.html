She is the younger sister of Joey Malone, and is romantically linked to Steve Knox. __TOC__ =Early History= Alexandria was trained by Joey Malone's longtime rival, Daniel Phillips. After her training, she was sent to the Squared Circle. =the Squared Circle= Alexandria made her debut for the Squared Circle in March of 2007. After a strong debut against her mentor, Phillips, she made the semi-finals of the annual "Tournament of Commons", losing to Simon Knox of the Princes of New England. She fought primarilly for the Evolutionary Championship, until finally winning the championship from Inoue Doi in early 2008. She later dropped the championship back to Doi. Afterwards, she engaged in a feud with Daniel Phillips that was violent and vengeful, as Phillips wanted to make an example of his former student and wanted to end her career like he ended her brothers. The feud between the two culminated in July in a career match, which Alexandria barely won. A month later, Alexandria won the Clash Royale and now is in line for a shot at Coral Avalon and the Championship. In late October, Alexandria defeated Avalon for the Championship. =PRIME= Nationally, Alexandria was a member of the Blue Rogues when they came to PRIME. The highlight of her time in PRIME came when she defeated Karina Wolfenden for the unsanction championship given to her by Sonny Silver, the Undisputed Amy Dumas Memorial Jizzbang Champion. She held the championship for over a year before she ultimately returned the championship to Silver at Colossus V. =Legion= Alexandria is part of Legion, and is a member of Jonathan Wilson's team. =New Frontier Wrestling= Recently, Alexandria has joined the home promotion of her boyfriend, Steve Knox.
