This is how it went. Alexis: How did you come about getting into the GW? KFX: Well, I had a job as a hot dog vendor in front of a building where Overload was to be held two weeks ago. What happened was, Bubba was never satisfied with the topping. For five minutes: "More relish, less, tiny bit more, wait, too much. Next, the same thing with the relish...and finally I just beat the crap out of him, and he decided I was of the fighting caliber. Then I told him I was in NTICW before it closed down previously, and he added another digit to my salary. Alexis: Since your arrival, you have been very vocal, and have started getting a good fan following, what do you see for KFX this year and in the Future of GW? KFX: Well, I see any endorsement adds in the future...from Gatorade to Talcum powder, I'll be a commercial icon. My manager, MC Sour Monkey, hopes to start a political career, and yes, being president of GW kind of counts, and I hope to make that happen. Alexis: What is your take on the History of GW, and its transition from GWF to GW over this last month? KFX: Well, there were so many checks I signed to the GWF's bill, that once it became GW I spent an hour erasing F's. And the history is nothing but fake deaths...really a drag. Alexis: Your biggest rival is? And why? KFX: My biggest rival? Mike Storm or The Josh. Mike Storm because I've already beaten him for a TV title shot, and now I'll beat him for the Cruiserweight championship, he's useful to be feuding with. The Josh because he was chosen to go to PCW over me, yet I just beat him, and I need to continue to beat him for bragging rights. Alexis: Your toughest opponent is? And why? KFX: 'Amazing' Mike Storm, because of all my opponents, he comes up first alphabetically. Alexis: Most favorite opponent is? And why? KFX: Well, all my opponents are swell, they really are...gee golly...I'm dripping with sarcasm. Alexis: If you had a chance to face one man in the GW, or Primetime Central, who would it be? And Why? KFX: If I had a chance to face one man in PTC, it would be...The Multiple Man. The Multiple Man vs. The Man of 1,000 Gimmicks. Because our gimmicks are nearly the same... No Kayfabe breaking, no fine print. Alexis: What can we expect to see out of the KFX, in and out of the ring? KFX: In the ring, you can see wrestling matches. Out of the ring, pubicity stunts and quite possibly a run for office. Alexis: What would you say to fans out there that want to be like KFX? KFX: Start getting taller and stronger, change your name, and find a latino friend named MC Sour Monkey. Alexis: What other interest besides facing some one in the ring, do you have? KFX: Lacrosse, Baseball, soccer, money, rolling in money, earning money, spending money, and ladies. Alexis: What's the favorite place for you to go to just relax and unwind? KFX: I'm yet to ind a place with mini golf, TV, Hammocks, and an animal petting zone. So a completely relaxing place is just a dream...for now. Alexis: Who do you think will be the first Grand Slam Champion? KFX: TLM Jay Robson. He's very close, and he doesn't need to get any of the titles I'm getting. Alexis: If you could pick a Quote What would it be,? and Why,? and by who? KFX: "Hey, we don't know what they do at that lab! Maybe they experiment on them by making them sleep with other, good-looking monkeys! We don't know! Hey, stay out of the this you mangy little chimp!" which was said by Jay in 'Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back', because it shows that there is always a good side to things. Alexis: Best Moment in the GW thus far? KFX: Beating the man who was chosen to go to PCW instead of me, 'The Josh' Joshua Kosidlo. Alexis: Worst moment? KFX: When I didn't when the Clash of the Titans 3 Royal Rumble. Alexis: What's your most embarrassing moment, in or out of the ring? KFX: My debut in NTICW, where after the match I ran off in a daze yelling "Butterflies!" Alexis: What are your Thoughts on the New Talent in the GW? And WHY? KFX:I think I am the dominating newcomer, but there are some big faces out there, and in Mike Storm's case, big masks, and it seems like GW is signing some decent talent. Alexis: OK, Quickies. Alexis: Beer or Wine? KFX: Beer. Alexis: Favorite Color? KFX: Midnight Blue. Alexis: Favorite band? KFX: Anytime Method Man and Redman get together. Alexis: Blonde or Bruette or Red Head? KFX: Brunette. Alexis: Favorite Food? KFX: Pizza. Alexis: Coke or Dr. Pepper? KFX: Dr. Pepper. Alexis: Favorite Sport other than Wrestling? KFX: Soccer, non-American football, futbol, fusbol... Alexis: OK any last words for the GW fans?? KFX: MC Sour Monkey for President! Alexis: And that was my moment with the FX Man. Mimic was all set to go to XCWF Spring Break-Out on Sunday to face Al Magz for the XCWF World Championship. This sudden accident has only set him back from his chance. After winning the Circuit at Total HaVok II, Mimic was on a free pass to SBO on April 28th. For this accident to happen is a shame and has upset all the XCWF locker room. Although Mimic has never been a big fan favorite, the fans and the other guys in the back appreciate all he has done and never wanted to see this happen. Perhaps one of the most sentimental over this is Al Magz, who was actually looking forward to the match at Spring Break-Out on Sunday. Al Magz did assure everyone though that there will be a Mimic vs. Al Magz match in the future. For the title, not for the title, it wouldn't matter to Al Magz. He would just like to give Mimic the match he deserves. With this happening and the match being canceled, Al Magz watches SBO from the sidelines as he will watch some of the XCWF's rising stars hit the ring. Mimic on the other hand watches from the safety of his bed in the Miami Regional Hospital as arrangements were made for Pay-Per-View to get through to his room.
