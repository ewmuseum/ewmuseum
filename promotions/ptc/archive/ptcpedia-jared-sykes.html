In Wrestling * Finishers and Signature Moves * Lightning Helix - Anaconda cross * Omega 13 - Inverted fisherman buster/Impact Driver * Theme Music * Save Me - Killswitch Engage * Noted Rivalries * Wyatt Connors * Lane Stevens * The Dead Man's Hand Championships and Accomplishments Sykes is the only 'Grand Slam Champion' in SCCW history, meaning he is the only man to have held all four of the company's major titles. SCCW LiveWire Title : September 8, 2008 - October 6, 2008 (28 days) SCCW Strength in Numbers Title (w/Charley Crisp) : November 7, 2008 - April 1, 2009 (144 days) SCCW Gateway Title : September 27, 2009 - January 18, 2010 (115 days) SCCW Universal Title : July 5, 2010 - Present He is also the longest reigning Infinite Gauntlet champion in PTC history, defeating five opponents over a ten round span. He has two additional victories in the competition, though neither were for the Infinite Gauntlet itself. (7 wins total) In 2009, Sykes won his way to the semifinals of GTT7's Faulkner bracket, before being eliminated by Impulse in the third round.
