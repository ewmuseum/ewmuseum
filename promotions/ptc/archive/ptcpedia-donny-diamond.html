Donny was adopted by an elderly family who lived in Los Angelas; around his 18th birthday his adopted family was killed in a car accident. Following the news Donny began the search for his brother Ron; and the two of them met up in a small wrestling promotion where he learned to wrestle. The two of them had a small push and became Tag Team Champions, but the federation soon closed and the two brothers were seperated once again. Donny found another federation where he became a decent singles competitor where he also won several singles championships. He then got married to a woman and the two of them had a child; his wife would also start to manage his wrestling career. Seeming to be at the top of his game he became World Champion of that promotion until the federation closed as well. Taking a break from wrestling for a while to heal some nagging injuries problems began to surface with his wife, and tragedy struck where his daughter was killed in a car accident as well. His wife blamed it all on him and hit with a divorce where she took all of his money, leaving him with nothing but sorrow. After a few months passed he would leave Los Angelas and go on the search for his father who he found in Chicago. The two of them got to know each other and ironically his father owned a small wrestling company where he allowed Donny to wrestle under the alias "Star". After making a name for himself over the next few months it was decided that "Star" would be given a chance to become Champion where the match was planned for him to pick up the win. Before the Pay-Per-View could take place Donny would overhear a meeting between his father and other members of management where they wanted "Star" to unknowningly job the match. Not wanting to have anything to do with messing up the company, he unwillingly jobbed. Reflecting back on his life it seemed that everything always messed up for Donny just after getting started. Following the Pay-Per-View Donny talked his father into taking a walk outside with him. Once outside the two of them went into an alley where Donny had a complete mental breakdown and struck his father from behind. After beating on his father for a bit, he stuffed him into a dumpster and set it on fire. Before leaving the alley, he picked up his father's pair of black sunglasses. Back inside the building Donny went into his father's office where he saw a new contract made out to him with the name Star erased; allowing him to wrestle as his real name Donny Diamond. When the body of his father was found an investigation took place, but no evidence could place Donny as murderer and the investigation was soon closed. Returning to his home in Los Angelas, all the pain began making Donny go insane and soon forced him to commit suicide. On his last breath a hooded figure appeared offering him a deal. He knew of all the pain and sufferring Donny had to live through, and because of that the deal was simple. In exchange for his soul, Donny would be given unlimited powers to make all his dreams come true, while at the same time making all those in his way feel unimaginable pain. With his new found powers Donny Diamond was signed to a federation called the DWO (Dominant Wrestling Organization) where he firmly planted his name into the company with some amazing feuds. Months passed and it was announced that Donny Diamond and Kaiowas were selected to compete in the PTC PPV for a chance to represent DWO and become PTC Global Champion, and on the same card the two would fight each other in an Asylum match for the DWO Championship. On that night Donny Diamond would defeat Kaiowas to become DWO Champion and defeat all other opponents to become PTC Global Champion. For months Donny Diamond would be on the top of his game until some political influences in the PTC stripped him of the PTC Global Championship, making him (to this very day) the only undefeated PTC Global Champion. Back in the DWO his feud with Kaiowas would soon come to an end as he would lose the title, due to the debut of two new DWO signee's, Damian and Roulette. The three of them would have one of the most talked about feuds in DWO history that would force Donny's soul to be cleansed, swaying him away from everything that is evil. Upon the cleansing of his soul, his evil counterpart would be trapped in another dimension until Damian found a way to free it. While in the feud with Roulette and Damian, Donny would also be forced to enter this dimension to do battle with himself, resulting in a battle with the devil himself. Months passed and DWO was soon closed, but the three of them would never be seen again. No one really knows what happened to Damian, nor Roulette, and as for Donny... Some say the final battle caused him to end his life, while others say he still lurks in the shadows, only time will tell I guess. Donny Diamond has recently been revealed as the person behind the attacks on SurReal and Equinox in GCW. Making his return to the federation after a 5yr. break. Dominant Wrestling Organization ...
