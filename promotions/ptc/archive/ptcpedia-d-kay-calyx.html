D-Kay Calyx

captured 7 Dec 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
D-Kay Calyx (born Darren Carter) is a former professional wrestler hailing from Chicago, Illinois, who used the Calyx-O Drop (suplex to sit-out facebuster) as a finishing move. A graduate of the Primetime Wrestling Academy, D-Kay was originally called up to PCW2, before being called up to the major leagues of Primetime Championship Wrestling following his impressive showing in the PCW vs PCW2 match at Gold & Glory (during which he eliminated Howard Porter after smashing him over the head with a cinder block). D-Kay was primarily a singles wrestler, however, his career will be remembered for his tag team run with Duzza, and for his membership in The Wild Deuces (with Boda, Tim Walden and Red Rock). After leaving PCW, D-Kay surfaced in New True Internet Championship Wresling (NTICW) for a few months, before a suspected mental breakdown resulted in his disappearance from active duty. D-Kay returned after 6 months out, helping his good friend Joey Flash to a win in a 6-man ladder match, before jumping ship to RUSH, where his career eventually fizzled out. D-Kay is now retired from wrestling and owns his own record store in Chicago, as well as DJ'ing at a number of local club nights.
