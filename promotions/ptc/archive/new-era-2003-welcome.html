PTC: New Era — Welcome

undated (the event site's own front page; built 7 April 2003)

Welcome to the Home of PTC's "New Era" I-PPV On April 20th, for the first time in nearly ten months, Primetime Central will be host to a card unparrelled in the world of wrestling. Representing over 25 promotions, some of the greatest wrestlers in the world will gather in Las Vegas for one very simple reason: Silly, Pointless, and Always Entertaining Annihilation of one another. For the first time since Vampir Nosferatu defeated Jay Phoenix in late 2001, the PTC Global Title will finally find a home in a massive fifteen man, invitation-only title match. Terry Woods will finally step out of semi-retirement to defend his Extreme Championship against the challenger to his title, Maelgrim. Finally bringing the PTC Tag Team Tournament to an end after six months, the UWF's legendary odd-couple pairing of Shane Stone and the Bum Boozler will take on the legendary LOW tag team of the Shadow Riders. And so many more matches, it'd make you cry like a baby. Within these walls you will find all neccessary information regarding the New Era PPV, the Pre-Show, and all wrestlers and federations participating in the event.
