3 July 2002 [XCWF] PPV Ratings, Research and more! This past Sunday the XCWF showed Untitled (Sub name: B.o.D. Hostile Takeover, which was cut). Eight great matches in one night of action. All the XCWF's titles on the line, including the huge Unification match up to combine the United States and Intercontinental Championships! Here are some numbers for the PPV: Live Attendance: 21,532 (Near Sell-Out with added seating) PPV Buy-Rate: 2.9 News reporters caught up to Tom Mason following the PPV Event, trying to see what his take was on the PPV and all the figures: "I am pleased to say the least. I knew that this wasn't our best PPV. I didn't intend for it to be. What was important was that we had enough fan based interest and hype put into this event that made it a success regardless of big match stipulations. That's what this event was all about, a straight up night of wrestling. Nothing flashy on our part like usual." A study was conducted by XCWF researchers to get feedback from fans on what they thought of the night's highs and lows. Out of a 100 fans asked this was the generated feedback: How many were surprised to see Tom Mason back in control? 31 How many didn't see Mimic being fired on the horizon?: 85 How many were ulitimately shocked by Mark Estep and Phil McCann sidding together as heels and attacking Al Magz? 97 Those were the biggest three spots of the night next to other matches and title changes. Figures on those were barely brought to attention. The next XCWF PPV Event will be XCWF UncoNS2XionL from the MCI Center in Washington DC. The event will be holding the end to the PTC sponsored REBEL Tournament and crowning of the PTC Extreme Champion. That will air July 21st 2002 at 8 ET / 5 PT. More information on that card will follow.
