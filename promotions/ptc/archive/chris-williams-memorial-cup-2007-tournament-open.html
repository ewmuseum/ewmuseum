Chris Williams Memorial Cup 07 - tournament open

With thanks to Argyle for letting this become the first ever (and hopefully regular) PTC Indy Event :) Chris Williams Memorial Cup May 4th 1999 Chris Williams, the handler of a character known as Coldblooded Kain Threat, was killed in an automobile accident. Each year the people whose live's he touched through efedding gather together in an ever increasing circle of friends to honour his memory with a single elimination wrestling tournament. Each year the CWMC takes on a different guise. In the past it has been a round robin style tournament, it has been abracketed, single elimination tournament, and it has even been a three matches (battle royal, guantlet and one-on-one final) one night PPV type deal. Whatever format it takes it is a pure RP tournament based around a very worthy concept! The current Chris Williams Memorial Cup champion is none other than former PTC Global Champion and 2004 PTC (co) Wrestler of the Year - Saij!!! The 2007 edition of this tournament is now open - and is open to ALL e-fedders that want to take part. We are going to go back to basics with this one so the tournament will be a simple 64 man, single elimination, tournament. The earlier rounds will have 1 maximum of 1 RP, the middle rounds 2 rps and the final round a maximum of 3 rps (1 rp being the minimum). If you would like to be a part of this then all you have to do is let me know ... you can do that by replying to this very post or by emailing me at [detail withheld].
