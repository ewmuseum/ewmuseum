PTC News: June 2004

24 Jun – 26 Jun 2004 (4 posts)

24 June 2004 WMW: End of June Contract Updates, Fighters taking time off, Reil removed from writing staff Jeff Hardkore has been released from his per show deal. The front office just could not justify keeping him on with his inconsistencies on and off screen and his lack of a work ethic. Steve Carnage has been signed to a developmental contract which will last until August 1st, 2004. He's will earn $5,000 over that period. Christopher Roberts, The Rossi Brothers, Josh Briskout, Scott Vyper and Wone's contracts have officially expired. No word if the company will be picking up any of these contracts. AWS Man, Fury, and Justin Ever's contracts expire on August 1st. Although all three are held very highly in the company, and seen as three of the thoughest fighters in the business, no word on if they will be returning to the company. Kenji Lee and Orange Ninja's developmental deals expire in early August as well and both are expected to sign into the company. Lee, however, has stated that he may see who else has interest in him. Jayson Reil has been removed from the writing staff. Justin Evers and Corvus are expected to take some time off after Crossfire. Evers may return by Hardkore Hell. Corvus is only expected out for 2 weeks. Source: Wrestling Midwest === 24 June 2004 GW plays trick on WMW, Reil taken off Crossfire, Caragias signed Reports today have been confirmed by WMW Spokesperson Benny Tyler that indeed Chris Caragias has agreed to make a return to the squared circle. Chris Caragias signed the month long development deal earlier today to what he says is a trial period, ironing out small nagging injuries, getting into better shape and getting rid of ring rust. The former 360 Entertainment Wrestler hasn't made a huge impact in PTC yet, having a short stint in LOW, but he looks to have a solid fed under him now. Caragias was quoted saying "...The Charisma is back! WMW is his first stop" as he signed the contract. He was signed to a developmental deal. No word on how long his stay will be. Jayson Reil has made his first public statement since disapearing mysteriously a few weeks ago. And after a stay in the hospital, he'll need even more time to heal the injuries aquired from a car accident. Wrestling Midwest wishes him the best of luck in his time of healing. With Reil injured, he was taken off Crossfire. No word if he will be replaced in the match against Deacon, or if the match will be taken off the card. More on Crossfire. Wrestling Midwest is being criticized by critics lately because of their non-promotion of the interfed event. However, sources say that the reason is because they were not involved in the creation of promotional flyers and banners, and once it was time to promote the event, GW possessed all promotional tools. Looks like GW has tried to pull another fast one on WMW. Source: Wrestling Midwest === 25 June 2004 WMW Show w/ Benny Tyler: Corvus Benny Tyler: Corvus, it's a pleasure to have this opportunity. Hello everyone. I'm Benny Tyler and this the WMW Show. Not the 1 hour extended version. Today, we just have one interview, and it's with current Great Lakes Champion, Corvus. Once again, thank you for joining. Corvus: No problem...I'm sure it is all your pleasure. Benny Tyler: Now, let's cut right to the chase. Blood Factory, you defeated a great collection of competitors, in what some are deeming as the greatest match in Wrestling Midwest history. AND, best of all, I'd assume, you won the GLC. What is your reaction? Corvus: My reaction is that I worked very hard to get to where I am at right now. I could say a million different things about how bad my opponents wrestled but they did not make it easy for me to waltz out of the ring with the GLC. They should all be proud that they were defeated by the best in WMW, period. Benny Tyler: And with that win, came another in the books. You were named to the PTC Top 10, coming in at #9. How does it feel, first of all, to be named to this list, and what do you think this means for not only your credibility, but the credibility of Wrestling Midwest? Corvus: It could have been any number of guys on that list but PTC saw something special when they reviewed the video tapes sent in by Brad Johnson of my work. Whether I was #1 or #9, I still helped put WMW on the map and I will continue to lead this current generation of superstars as it has been called upon me to do so. I was not picked, I was CHOSEN. Benny Tyler: Speaking of that. Rumors are flying around that WMW's Vampire might be leaving the company to find competition elsewhere. Is there any truth to that? Corvus: You must read a lot on the internet son...I don't blame you for being technologically inclined to do so in today's age but nothing is ever certain that is for sure. If I decided one day to show up somewhere other than Wrestling Midwest, my presence would be felt in whatever capacity it was. Right now I have no aspirations of leaving Wrestling Midwest. I am their champion and their leader...I may be crazy but not stupid. Benny Tyler: Well, that's a relief. That was definatly the answer I was hoping for. How do you think you are percieved now, now that you have topped WMW? I mean, when you first came in, it seemed like you were playing second fiddle in the Shoot Division fueding with The Lion. A few months later, you've taken over. Corvus: *chuckling* Benny, this would be a perfect case of what you might call an elaborate plan. Obviously there was not much of a match between myself and Lion. I could have devoured him whole without any remorse. I knew that I needed something to get myself in the door and to get noticed. Winning the Shoot Challenge was that exact way. It was a stepping stone in a grander scheme of mine. Benny Tyler: Well, once again, you were placed on a pedistal, but turned down the offer to defend WMW in the War Games match? Why did you choose to decline the offer and fight with the Kindred? Don't you think defending WMW's pride and chance for recognition is a more important match? And, now without you there, what do you think of WMW's chances at defeating GW? Corvus: Well...first things first Benny. I chose to decline and decided to choose my allies over teaming with a bunch of backstabbing lepers. I'm sure you know quite well as everyone else that I wouldn't urinate on any one of Team WMW if they were on fire. I care nothing about this war between the two organizations because it has nothing to do with me. However, there is one personal situation I have to take care of. The anomally known as Leinad Soma...he has pretty bold claims about me from what I have heard. I decided to take care of my own matters before WMW's. If they fail...then they can come to me for help to take care of the situation. Benny Tyler: Now that you've won the GLC, what is your next step? What is your next big program? Any fueds lined up? Corvus: Well obviously there is nothing above being the GLC, but I'm not going to rest my hat there. I plan on continuing to keep on working as hard as I have been to show that I am the right direction for Wrestling Midwest and to make sure Brad Johnson knows that I am worth every bit of the investment that has been put upon me or will be. As for upcoming battles, I am sure that AWS Man is next in line. He and I are not exactly strangers to one another. Though just like Justin Evers, I plan to make him just another name in the win column for the Dark Prince. Benny Tyler: Well, to close off the interview, is there anything else you want to say, any message that needs to be sent to either an individual, a group of fighters in the locker room, or in this case, a fed like GW? Corvus: This goes out to Wrestling Midwest and Global Wrestling. I want you to take a good look at this face because what you see here is wrestling at its best. I fear nothing and there is not a damn thing in this world that I can't achieve when I get it into my head. The Great Lakes Championship is proof positive of that. Also to those group of clowns that jumped me after Blood Factory...remember every blow because you are going to endure it ten fold and that is not a threat... it's a PROMISE. Welcome to the new dark age of wrestling...enjoy your slow descent in HELL! Benny Tyler: Thank you for your time. It was a pleasure speaking with the new Great Lakes Champion. And for Wrestling Midwest, Corvus, and the WMW Show, I am Benny Tyler. Good night. Source: Wrestling Midwest === 26 June 2004 UPWA Destructicity PPV Comeback Announced! Once one man from a team gives up, submits or is knocked out, that entire team will be forced to leave and another team will enter. When another of the contestants submits, gives up or is knocked out, that team will be froced to leave. Finally, when the last two teams are in the ring, the UPWA Heavyweight title will be lowered to 20 ft. above the ring. The contestant to retrieve the title will be pronounced the winner and the new UPWA Heavyweight Champion, as well as receiving the International Warfare Trophy and $500,000. ALSO...
