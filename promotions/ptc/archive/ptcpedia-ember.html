Jay Phoenix - the Eternal Flame Aliases Inferno Crimson Ghost III Aguila de Flagero Judgement Ember Bio Entrance (current) Announcer … from Flagstaff, Arizona, weighing in at 200lbs, standing 5'11", this is the HOTTEST star in wrestling … JAY PHOENIX!! [A haunting guitar melody plays over the PA system as on the VideoTron flames start to appear, curling up to completely cover the blackness of the screen. A drumbeat fills that air as words, sung in a powerful, pure voice can be heard mingling with the melody as a bass driven counter melody joins with an electric guitar to complete the music. ] "Day by day, watching you disappear Wishing that you were still here beside me On my own, swimming against the tide There's nobody on my side but your memory Then I'll rise, right before your eyes On wings that fill the sky Like a Phoenix rising Like a Phoenix rising Wings of fire, tearing into the night Screaming into the light of another day Carry me out of the hurricane Into the smoke and flame and we'll fly away And I'll rise, right before their eyes On wings that fill the sky Like a Phoenix rising Like a Phoenix rising Higher, higher hear the thunder roar from above Fire, fire, fire make me whole And I'll rise, right before their eyes On wings that fill the sky Like a Phoenix rising Like a Phoenix rising Wings of fire, tearing into the night And we'll fly away ... " [As the music fades away, the flames on the VideoTron die down, leaving the screen completely black again … until suddenly with an explosion of light and noise the flames reappear, this time in the easily distinguishable form of a Phoenix that fills the whole screen. On either side of the ramp way two bursts of flame leap out, sending to fan shaped flares of fire across the entrance, through which a silhouetted figure walks through as on the screen, superimposed on the mystical flame bird symbol, two words appear …JAY PHOENIX] [The crowd are on the feet, cheering and screaming as the realisation of just who it is hits them. Phoenix stands on the ramp way, the flame still bright behind him, as the music still plays. He is dressed in a black leather look body suit, flame motifs running up the side of both legs, meeting at the thigh where they join together to form almost a belt of fire. The arms are cut off at the shoulders exposing muscular biceps, the left one covered with a glistening tattoo of a stylised Phoenix in flight surrounding by bands of gold and red flames. Black and red elbow pads are met by taping that covers his forearms and wrists. A silver necklace hangs around his neck, falling down to his chest, and when the light catches it at the right angle it can be seen to be a disc containing thin interlocking strands, a bright purple amethyst at it's centre … a dreamcatcher. A smile plays across Phoenix's face as he pans his attention around the arena before slowly walking down towards the ring. When he gets to the ring, Phoenix, with a quick move, jumps to the ring apron and climbs the turnbuckle, holding an arm up to the fans, which resume their cheering in response, before vaulting over the ropes to land in the centre of the ring.] History Move set Phoenix's wrestling style is best summed as a daredevil. He likes to mix high-flying risky moves, with fast-paced technical wrestling, with some martial arts on the side. Strikes Basic forearms Basic elbow smashes Open-handed palm strikes Knife-edge chops Dropkick Rolling kick Roundhouse kicks to legs, body, and head. Shining Wizards of all sorts and sizes. Jumping knee, HHH style. Missile dropkick Somersault dropkick Flying forearm smash Submissions Cross armbreaker Fujiwara armbar Triangle choke Texas Cloverleaf Figure Four Leglock Sharpshooter Anklelock Grapevine Anklelock Other Moves Huracanranas of all sorts. DDT Double-Arm DDT Senton Tiger Suplex Hold Russian Legsweep Tornado DDT Super Frankensteiner Diamond Cutter Diving shoulderbarge Victory roll (springboard from outside) Various martial arts moves Signature Moves Calm before the storm - sleeperhold/legscissored armbar submission, held till either a tap out or the opponent goes to sleep. Thunderstorm – twisting corckscrew moonsault from the top rope Electric Chair – Hurricinrana from the top-rope Lights Out - side thrust kick to the face Burn Out - Mysterio’s 619 Fade Out – Sharpshooter Rare Moves (Rare move) A small flash / ball of flame is sent from Phoenix’s left hand to the face of the opponent. Finishing Moves From the ashes Short arm clothesline into two round kicks first to the stomach and then to the face. As opponents falls to the canvas Ember runs up the turnbuckle and without pausing performs a shooting star press. Phoenix Rising To setup the finishing move, a butterfly powerbomb (pedigree set up … both arms behind back while head is lowered, into a short power-bomb,if the opponent is too big, then just the pedigree is used instead of the powerbomb) leaving them on the mat. After the set up move, Phoenix springboards from the middle of the top rope, into a somersault leg drop, landing across the opponents chest. Federations Titles & Accolades Jay Phoenix has the honour of being the most decorated wrestler in PTC history as far as top level titles go holding World level titles in six different federations. PWW - Professional World Wrestling (grand slam champion) (PTC Federation) PWW Television Champion PWW European Champion PWW Intercontinental Champion PWW World Champion (two time) PWW Tag Team Champion PWW Master of the Mat PWW Face of the Year / Feud of the Year / Handler of the Year PWW Hall of Fame GIWF- Global Internet Wrestling Federation / UWF - Universal Wrestling Federation (grand slam champion) (PTC Federation) GIWF Television Champion (two time) GIWF (T)he (H)ardcore (C)hampionship GIWF Intercontinental Champion GIWF North American Champion UWF (formerly GIWF) World Champion GIWF Feud of the Year (co-holder) UWF Hall of Fame GZW- Ground Zero Wrestling (PTC federation) Inaugural GZW World Champion RUSH (PTC Federation) World Champion 4WW - 4 Winds Wrestling (PTC Federation) North Wind Champion (World) 4WW Hall of Fame WWA - World Wrestling Alliance (PTC federation) WWA World Champion CSWA - Championship Wrestling Association Presidential Champion GDW- Global Domination Wrestling GDW Western Hemisphere Champion IOWF- International Online Wrestling Federation IOWF International Champion Tournaments Chris Williams Memorial Cup Winner PWW - Master of the Mat Winner RPGod 4 (tournament cancelled before end) WWA - Embrace the Power finalist PTC - Seven fo the Best entrant PTC - CW1 entrant PRIME - Jewel in the Crown entrant GTT Jay Phoenix is the only wrestler to have taken part in all six GTTs, and is the only wrestler to have reached the final in two.
