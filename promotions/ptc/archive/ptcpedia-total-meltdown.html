Total Meltdown

captured 29 Jan 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Total Meltdown, is the current name given to annual supershows of the World Wrestling Alliance, these supershows have occasionally been known under other names over the years. Total Meltdown has featured some of the most spectacular non pay per view matches in WWA history. History Total Meltdown 2004 [1] Staples Center, Los Angeles The Mysterious GAROU defeated Tommy Starr via schoolboy roll-up in a Hair vs Mask match. The Chuckster and Steven Murphy defeated Khaled and Liam Jenson in a TV League Ladder match. Deimos defeated Tracer Bullet and Nicholas Adams in a Three Way Elimination match. Wes Sanders defeated Leviathan in a Last Man Standing match. Denrol defeated Vik Ventressa via Super Evil Killer. Grunge won the WWA World Heavyweight Championship over Damian Thorne via G-Sharp. Faultline 2005 [2] Staples Center, Los Angeles Joshua Taro Freedom won the WWA Television Championship over Lawrence Lei, Dave Hurst, Bonafide, Showtime, Odium, Nytro, Tommy Turner, Bionica and The League of Goodness in a Battle Royal. Joey Edwards retained the WWA Extreme Championship over Silence via Joey-Lectrified. KaOs (Komodo Dragon and Pyro) defeated The Stockbrokers (Alec Lancer and DJ Wallstreet) via Dragon Drop. Khaled defeated Hell Itself in a No Disqualification match. Matt Warr defeated Shaman via Interference and Belt Shot. Brian Calvin retained the United States Championship over Grunge and Chance in a Triple Threat match. Mal Somers defeated Tracer Bullet via Somers-Assault in a Steel Cage match. Jack Griffiths won the Embrace The Power tournament over Ember via The Lights Out. Dave Harley retained the WWA World Heavyweight Championship over Craven Moore in a 2 out of 3 falls match . Total Meltdown 2006 [3] American Airways Arena, Miami Belial Khan and Tommy Starr defeated The Devil Dogs via Shooting Starr Press. Nicholas Adams defeated Ramone via The Thunder Lock. Jack Griffiths defeated Dave Gibson via Crimson Skies. Tracer Bullet defeated Denrol in a Bakers Dozen match. The Detonators (TNT and Dynamite) won the WWA World Tag Team Championship over Havoc (Deimos and Phobos) via Big Bang. Khaled defeated Mal Somers in a No Holds Barred match. RITC: The Journey Begins 2007 [4] Joe Louis Arena, Detroit Nahum Tate defeated Aaron Sasaaki, Cobi Deekins, Ryacard, and Leonardo Sugi in a Breakout Battle Royal. Mal Somers, EVD and Rookie defeated Jamal Diggs, Tye Diggs and Mister LED in a Six Man Elimination Tag Team match. The Rainmaker won the WWA Television Championship over Christopher Dumont via Shooting Star Press. Elements of Opposition (Ry and Tarin Ballard) defeated Tempered Steel (Anvil and Hammer) via Misdirection. Chris MacBeth retained the United States Championship over Jack Griffiths and Christian Heights in a Triple Threat match. Richard Cranium defeated Jevon White in a TLC match. Kade Williams retained the WWA World Heavyweight Championship over Armaan via Inevitability. Total Meltdown 2008 [5] American Airlines Arena, Dallas Komodo Dragon won the WWA Television Championship over Paul Sterling via Dragon Drop. American Eagle MK II defeated Kharon via Running Powerbomb. Azrael defeated Andy McMirra and Mighty Joe via The Blackout in a Triple Threat match. Weapons Of Mass Destruction (Mick Vain and Vic Vicious) won the WWA World Tag Team Championship over Dave Harley in a Handicap match. Eddie Van Dorn defeated Proteus via The Dropout. Total Meltdown 2009 [6] Madison Square Garden, New York City Trendkiller defeated Christopher Dumont, High Rise and Denrol via Southern Comfort in a Four Corner Survival match. Seth Copeland retained the United States Championship over Eddie Van Dorn via The Endgame in a Falls Count Anywhere match. Shaman defeated Lucas Black and "The Frontman" Richie Stone via countout in a Triple Threat match. Jaymz Watkins won the WWA World Heavyweight Championship over Ry Ballard via the Killdozer.
