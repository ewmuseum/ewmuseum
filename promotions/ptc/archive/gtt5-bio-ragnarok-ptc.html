Ragnarok Biography Biography Real Name: Unknown Height: 6' 9" Weight: 385lbs Percent Bodyfat: 8% Birthplace: Unknown, grew up in Silver Creek Seminary, New Mexico Birthdate: Unknown, believed to be around 30 years old Appearance/Style: Ragnarok is a beast. He is a weapon, and that is all. Standing at six foot nine inches and weighing in at a massive 385lbs, Ragnarok is built for fighting and not much else. He's not a tactician, he's not a martial artist, he's not even that hardcore. He's all about raw power and not hitting the mat easily. He's got black, greasy hair that goes down past his shoulders. His eyes are a dull yellow, much the color of piss. Ethnic-wise, Ragnarok has little or no recollection of his parents or his past, but DNA testing has shown that he has traces of Seneca, Samoan, and Scottish blood in him. By no means is he considered attractive. To augment a horribly large, ugly nose and a cro-magnan man type forehead and jawline, Ragnarok has covered himself in scars and tattoos. He's got a large burn mark on the left side of his face, the outline of a Gaelic Cross across his entire back, three simple straight lines on his chest (one for tension, one for relaxation, and one for where the two meet), and seven small circular knife wounds (on each leg, each arm, chest, back, and near his forehead) that represent the seven deadly sins. History: As stated, Ragnarok does not know much of his youth and isn't very smart. In fact, he has almost no individual thought of his own. All of his direction, all of his thinking, is done by his manager, Reverand Paul Springwell. To see more about Paul Springwell, see below. But as far as Ragnarok goes, he listens to everything his managers says as thought it were the Gospel truth. That's because Springwell has brainwashed Ragnarok to a fanatical, cultist sect of Christianity all his life. Ragnarok believes himself to be an Angel of Death, the instrument of God to cleanse the Earth of sinners and anyone unworthy of the Lord's plan. Manager: Reverand Paul Springwell Manager's Bio: Paul Springwell is the voice and controller of Ragnarok. To be blunt, Springwell is a madman. He is a preacher in his fifties who is small and appears physically weak, but who has a terribly powerful charisma and ability to manipulate people. Springwell was the head priest of the Catholic Seminary in Silver Creek, New Mexico. He was in charge of several nuns and priests in training. Over the years, Springwell grew more and more strict in being Orthadox and went to the brink of maddness. Over time, Springwell believed that human beings were a disease, nearly every one of them a scourge to God's name and the Catholic Church eventually excommunicated him for his beliefs. For this, Springwell made his own fanatical sect of Christianity that included many from the Seminary and Convent. In years before, Springwell found an abandoned child, in the 4 - 6 year old range, at the door to the Seminary. He saw this child as a sign from God and did not name him, believing that he could not name such a thing. Eventually, Paul Springwell believed his own cult to be too sick to survive. One night, he locked up the Seminary and Convent and burned it to the ground. Miraculously, the boy that he took in and called his son managed to escape the fire with only moderate burns. He believed this to be yet another sign from God that this boy would be the tool by which the curse of humanity would be cleansed. He named the boy Ragnarok, also calling him The End, and set him to extreme physical labor and training which made the already large boy a beast of muscle. Now, Springwell has turned to find a new way to get God's message out. He believes that if people see the raw power that is Ragnarok, they will either repent their evil ways or live in fear of a vengeful God once again. If one can compare Ragnarok to a bomb, Springwell is definitely the maker of the bomb, the silo it's stored in, the launch sequence, and the activator of the detonator.
