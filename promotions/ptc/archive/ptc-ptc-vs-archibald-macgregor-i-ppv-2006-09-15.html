PTC vs Archibald MacGregor I-PPV ---- PTC AND THE CASE AGAINST ARCHIBALD MACGREGGOR In March, a travesty was committed. Hoyt Williams, then Extreme Division Champion, entered the ring against Archibald MacGregor, the first ever free agent to fight for a PTC Title under PTC rules. Despite his best efforts, Williams lost, and for the first time ever, a non-PTC member held one of it's biggest titles. For his part, MacGregor has been less than apologetic, challenging all comers for his belt. Can Karina Wolfenden win the triple crown? Will Vivica Valentine live up to her destiny? Can the unsung wrestlers like Eryc Morgan, Damien Cruz and "The New Shit" Bobino move to the level of one of PTC's elite? Or, will Archibald MacGregor rip his pants down, and once again, collectively moon PTC? Also: The Global Title is up for grabs as newly crowned champion XUW's own the Uber Judge takes on GCW's Shadow for PTC's biggest belt. And, for the first time in over two years, the PTC Tag Team Titles are ready to be unvacated as Untite and The Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean square off! Things are about to get interesting! But, until then, here's an ad for AWC.
