UWF: Sunday Afternoon Supremacy (Recap)

29 Sep 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What a great afternoon of wrestling fisplayed by The UWF, the card opened up with a First Blood Match to see who would be going on to the next round of The Shoot Wrestling Title Tournament. The Match was between Kevin Starr and Misfit. The first big chance for either man to draw blood came with Kevin Starr and Misfit both holding chairs when Kevin Starr went for The Wish Upon a Starr, he nailed it but Misfit was holding a chair that was blocking him from getting any real damage caused. No blood was seen, the match continued. There were a lot of other chair shots that didn't cause blood, misfit was basically getting the most damage of the night. And it came down to one final swing of a steel chair by Kevin Starr that blasted misfit in the face busting him open and putting an end to the match. With that victory, Kevin Starr will go against Joshua Dunn in the next round of The Shoot Wrestling Tournament. Following that was an interview with The Devil From The Deep Blue Sea himself, Tsunami, with his lovely valet Shiatsu. He went to to talk about Live and Loud 2 and how he is looking forward to getting his hands on Digital Mortality and becoming The Supreme Commander of The Secen Continents, and how he and The Storm Front are finally going to end Communism in The UWF. Following that interview we were ready for our next match of the night, it was Purple Haze, The UWF TV Champion going against Killer Dude. The Match was pretty much dominated by Purple Haze, and in the end it was Purple Haze coming away with the victory after making Killer Dude tap with The Spell-Binder. The next match was The Nine Man Tag Match, involving The Iron Curtain (Digital Mortality and the reigning UWF Tag Team Champions, The Local NVA Company) vs. The Storm Front (Tsunami and Thunder &Lightning) vs. The Role Models (The Legends and Slasher). The match was an all out war, action both inside and outside the ring. Slasher and Digital Mortality started off the match, Digital Mortality went for an early Digitalizer on Slasher but it was blocked by Slasher. Slasher was in for a while in that match facing Duc Chu Song for a while and than facing Chris Thunder, before finally getting the tag to Lance Rose of The Legends. Rose and Thunder fought for a while showing off some impressive moves, capping it all off with a devastating Superplex by Thunder that sent Rose crashing to the mat. Thunder went for the pin but it was broken up by Slasher. The Legends than double team Thunder until he tags out to Thai Trang of The NVA Company. Luke runs away and tags out to Tsunami. Tsunami Oni nails a leg flip pin but only manages a two count. Tsunami hit The Evil Blue Mist on Trang, after that all hell broke loose. The Legends bring in a table, Slasher enters with a chair with nails all over it. Trang gets nailed with the chair and falls to the mat bleeding from the face. The insanity didn't end there, a lot more damage was inflicted in that match, bodies everywhere, destruction all over the arena untill the end came with Digital Mortality getting Tsunami Oni in an inside cradle and gets the one, two, three, and The Iron Curtain leave victorious. Following the match The Plague came out of nowhere and attacked Slasher from behind. The Plague got busted open by a fire extinguisher. After that a table was set up and Plague sent Slasher crashing through it with a splash while holding a steel chair. They ended up fighting up the ramp. The Plague was going to throw Slasher off of the ramp, but Slasher blocked it. The yended up fighting backstage a bit until Security broke them up. Plague was furious and actually challenge Slasher to an Exploding Ring Barbed Wire Death Match at Live and Loud 2. The Main Event was next, with Plague defending his THC Title against Jay Phoenix. This match was going to be great. It started off with both men exchanging punches. Phoenix went for a suplex but it was blocked by Plague who nailed Phoenix with a neckbreaker. He than locks Phoenix in a Backbow Submission. So far the match started off on the technical side. It seemed Plague wanted to show Phoenix that he wasn't just Hardcore. But that didn't last too long, after Phoenix resisted The Backbow Submission, Plague left the ring grabbing a ladder, a trash can. Plague went to nail Phoenix with the trash can but Phoenix ducked and nailed a release german suplex that folded Plague right on his head. Phoenix than nailed a northern lights suplex, but Plague kicked out at two. Phoenix nails a devastating dropkick sending Plague flying to the mat, but again Plague kicked out after two. Phoenix was doing a superb job of alienating The Plague from his weapons. Both men were on the top turnbuckle fighting. Jay Phoenix puts one of The Plague's arms over his back, and grabs his leg, and nailed The Plague with a fishermans suplex from the top rope. But Phoenix couldn't make the cover. He finally makes it after a few seconds but the Plague managed to kick out after two and seven eighths. After that disply, Plague grabs from under the ring his trusty sledgehammer and goes after Phoenix. Phoenix somehow uses his skills of wrestling to take Plague down with a flying headscissors sending the sledgehammer flying out of PLagues hands and Plague falling to the mat. Phoenix had the hammer in his hands, but throws it away instead of ending The Plague. He than nails Plague with a belly to belly. After another failed pin attempt by Phoenix they head outside the ring. Suddenly Slasher emerges and attacks The Plague. Slasher and Plague battle up the ramp as Phoenix just stands and watches. Slasher rams Plague into the steel support beems and than kicks Plague right in the groin. They than head backstage and Slasher sends Plague into a pile of chairs. Slasher accepted the challenge for The Exploding Ring Barbed Wire match at Live and Loud 2. The ycontinue the battle backstage and Slasher nailed Plague with a coffee pot. Back in the ring Phoenix tells the ref to hold his hand up in victory. Plague and Slasher than head outside adn battle in the parking lot. Slasher throws Plague's head right through a car window, slicing Plague wide open. Plague hangs there as Slasher heads to a nearby car and starts it driving right toward Plague. Plague gets out of the way and Slasher goes crashing right into the other car. After a big powerbomb onto the car windshield by Plague, they both fall off the hood and lie bloody and motionless for a minute. After a few punches being exchanged both men notice a sledgehammer out on the ground, they both make a dash for it and the scene fades out. When they get back tot he lot, Slasher is found unconcious with the sledgehammer lying next to his body and blood soaking the ground next to him. Back in the ring we see Phoenix standing in the ring with his hands held high in victory, when The UWF World Champion, Danny Jaye, emerges and spears Jay Phoenix. Jaye nails Phoenix with some powerful blows to the face. Haye gets up giving Phoenix enough time to exit the ring and head as far away from The World Champ as possible. The show ends with Jaye in the ring alone holding The World Heavyweight Title yelling at Phoenix who just continues to walk away. Source: UWF: Come on, you know you like it
