Thank You! === 11 May 2003 [GW] The Wade Report First off let me introduce my self to every one, MY name is Jack Wade. For a while I have been a part time writer for GW. Once employed by GW[F] As part of there magazine staff. Now I have been rehired to right my thoughts for GW online Magazine / website. Deciding a starting point for this column has been sort of difficult, But I have found a way to start. WRESTELCADE! GWS big PPV of the year. This night the big talk was the showdown of Silent Wind and Quartz, and the Hell in a cell showdown between long time foes Brick and the co owner of GW Salaok. But that’s not all Wrestlecade held. Acting Co Owner Logan Creed had reinstated the Primestar title that Salaok had disbanded a month prior.. We also saw a very heated confrontation between Jesse Briggs and Ely Braxton. A Cruiser Weight battle royal for Contendership to the title, and an International title match putting five top names in this big time match. This night we witnessed a one night six man tournament. High Fave’s to win this tournament in my opinion were Roz Blayze and Deacon Holmes.. The names in this were all recent arrivals to GW, Max Roswell put out a great showing for his self, as did Blayze and Holmes, Holmes moving to the finals of this tournament. But in the end The monster Leviathan won the title, I don’t think we have seen the end of the rivalry Holmes and Leviathan have had over the past month. After Leviathans rise to championship we moved to the Braxton Eight Ball and Briggs match. This one was set up do to the unbelievable turn of character by Jesse Briggs. Braxton and Briggs for those who don’t know are long time friends of Bubba McCoy. Briggs turned on his friend when he walked out on Brick, Logan Creed and Braxton leaving them to be beaten at the hands of the Nevermore [Salaok’s stable in GW] Briggs would prove how tough he was and how down right men he was when he would win this one, After the match the Nevermore again attacked Braxton, Gunner Addison would then come to ring side to aid Braxton. She seemingly had gotten into Briggs’s head and got him to join her, But he stabbed her in the back, or should I say Batted her… Briggs’s is heading down a path and I don’t like the look of its ending. Then came the Cruiserweight match… A match once again not going to the fave, BJP in this case, BJP Roz Blayze Max Roswell and Akio Hakura all put out a lot of effort into this match and it showed. This match was elimination and a spot fest mostly. BJP with the upper hand got eliminated when unrepentantly Salomon Vation would show up distracting BJP… Blayze would then advance to the end and gain number one Contendership to Sage Sabbot’s title.. a very big win here for Roz After this two long time rivals fought each other in ‘Harvester of Pain’ Mark Kullypso and ‘TLM’ Jay Robson… Both these guys took this match and showed a lot of people not to count them out of the title scene, Jay in this one got the best and showed a form of his self that we have not seen in a long time. Great to see Jay focused once more. Jay in this outing added the Platinum title to his long list of titles. We saw one of the most down right travesties in GW to date when Current International Champion Tragic Hero walked away with the titles… I for one say Dynasty had this match won. He had taken out Chris Camero early on. He was the definite favorite in this match, and yet he lost. Part of this has to be the attack from Leviathan… and the subsequent assault from Quartz saving Tragic Hero… I say it was a travesty… Others agree with me. But after this we witness Brick finally getting his hands on Salaok. [For those that don’t know the Nevermore in a whole have terrorized Bricks family for the past year] Brick put the hurt on Salaok and picked up a very gratifying win here… This match was full of the interference we have come to expect from Nevermore members… But Brick pulled though all of it, This match lived up to the hype around it and I am happy about that. We now get down to the battle of father and Son. This fight took place on top of a building across from the arena in Huston… The end result was one I have never seen before. The building was rigged and Silent Wind in a attempt to end his fathers life, blew the sucker up! Nothing left there save rubble. Quartz survived however.. Silent Wind. None know of his condition.. Great Pay Per View all in all. With the look at Wrestlecade out of the way I will be on my way out. Next time I will review the current happenings in GW and talk about new faces signed. In my first official GW column finnished this is Jack Wade signing out…. Please forgive me for any adult language...but hey...I need this stuff. Thank you for time Dess.... Dess: No problem at all. Rebnik: Okay, now before we get into Matrix, I want to get one question out the way. Sub-Beast: is he still a threat, and why or why not? Dess: Well, I supposed that's up to him... If he decides to pursue me and the Great Lakes title, then yes, he is a threat... But... If he stays in the Tag Division, then no, I don't believe he is. But I do believe somewhere down the road he'll seek revenge and I say, just bring it. Rebnik: Would he be capable of beating you? Its obvious that he is fond of using cheats and interference to his advantage...but fair and square...is he a threat? Dess: Well, we've yet to have completely fair match, so I can't really decide. But say if we had a Cage match or something along the lines of that, then yes, I do believe he is capable of beating me... But not as capable as I am of beating him. Rebnik: Okay, damn cocky as usual. But I must admit, as cocky as you are...you never seem to lie about what you plan on accoplishing. Dess: That's exactly correct! Sure, I may be cocky, but! I've got the title around my waist... So maybe being cocky isn't all too bad. I think Matrix and I were around the same time... But my cocky attitude got me more shots, people thought I couldn't back it up. But to their surprise, I could. So why I was challenging everyone, abusing the Wrestlers backstage, you know the whole deal... Whilst Matrix was still quite unknown, working his way to the top. But it payed off, as he got his shot against me. I don't think he was quite prepared for the match as I knew he had the talent, yet I still pulled off the win. Rebnik: Yes, that was quite a brutal fight. In all of the hell in a cell matchs I have seen, the two of you in the ring made the best. Matrix had talent, and endurance, but somehow you beat the jobber. How did you prepare for him, or did you at all? Dess: I can't really remember. I think it's because I had already been in a couple 'brutal' title matches before whereas this was probably one of Matrix' first. So I was probably more experienced... But I still trained hard for the match, I was damned if I was gonna lost those titles so quickly Rebnik: Well, one thing to point out was Matrix was a fan-favorite. This could be shocking to somebody who has never seen UHW, as now in WMW he is the most hated man in the federation. Dess: Yeah and I was quite the opposite in UHW as well, I was the heel, whereas in WMW I am now the face. But I think Matrix has changed alot since our first matches in UHW. Since then, he's become more 'sure' of himself, more tougher, more smarter and overall just a more evil, better Wrestler. Maybe he chooses to turn his back on his friends and his fans because of a good reason. But I doubt it. I think Matrix wants the Gold, and he want's it bad. He'll do what it takes to take it from me, that is the true reason Matrix 'turned'. Either that, or he was watching some 'old school' Dess promos, hahaha. Rebnik: Yes, you were the spitting image of a modern Matrix bad then. But back into the time-line, Matrix trained for a solid month to get another shot at you...and he got that shot easily by defeating a Jeames Kolonok for a number one contership for that Hell in a Cell Title. So at Homocidal Holloween, you stepped into that cell with him for the second time Dess: Yeah I watched his promos and he was training... Hard. But once again, I came out on top. I knew Matrix had an 'untapped' potential and I believe that after that night, he began to get better and better very quickly. After I won that match, I kept on acting cocky thinking that was the end of Matrix, but I was wrong... Rebnik: Yes...and I believe you were so worried about Matrix being back on top, that it ended up you decided getting rid of the belts were the best thing to do. So you had a 'shoulder injury' and vacated the belts, only for Matrix to take the belts later that night. After that, he challenged you again...only this time he was the defending champ. I can't say much other than the fact that the match was a reality check for me. I started realising that someday I was gonna get my ass kicked, which happened at Total Wreckage... And that maybe being so cocky wasn't such an 'ideal' approach to Wrestling. Rebnik: Well...I hate to say it, but it has done Matrix a whole lot better then it did you. I give Matrix all credit for that match, it was awesome, and I think that after people saw the match they must've thought: "Oh wow, Matrix made that match, that's cool" so his popularity shot up. I think he deserved it, at the time... Rebnik: But then something changed. After that match you both rocketed the singles division. You, Dess, went onto to win your first main event pay per view, while Matrix won the King of the Deathmatch. Dess: Yeah, that was awesome. I was proud of the both of us... Rebnik: Though then Matrix vanished to another fed as commishiner, in which he brought you from UHW to GAW. UHW wasn't looking to 'alive' so I figured i'd have enough time to join Matrix in GAW. After alot of 'evil act's' we then went to MWF, after that died, GAW became WMW... Here's where I did my best... In WMW I sorta though that if I am going to be a recognised Wrestler in WMW, then I'd better start to stop cheating... I told Matrix this, but he didn't listen... So he payed the priced. Rebnik: Yes, at this point you went your seperate ways, and thats where you come now. And Matrix being the hometown favourite, I wouldn't be surprised if he tries harder than he ever has to pull of the win. But i'll say this, it ain't gonna happen. Rebnik: I have heard that before, from both you and Matrix. Well, Dess, in the end no matter which you look at it, this will not end. Whether you win or he does, the legacy wil live one until one of you finally quits...and Matrix is not known for quitting, and neither are you. Either way, Dess...this fued cannot end. Source: wmw === 18 May 2003 WMW: "Week in Review" by Benny Tyler Week in Review by Benny Tyler Welcome to another edition of Week in Review. Alot of happenings this week. Alot of things to talk about. Friday Night Riot is up and was a huge success. Donovan Merlot is the new Commissioner. New signings every day. Lets see what happened this week. Friday Night Riot ------------------- Another classic edition of Riot off the air, however this time, we finally started to run some dark matches. Dracore, the man who lost his debut match against Tami Weber in a 'contract match', later signed with the company despite, and was looking for win #1. Alas, another newcomer, Wolf, stopped him from achieving that goal. Wolf and other new guy, the DT, will be facing off at the PPV in a Dark Match, to get the chance to be promoted to the main roster. Every match that went down was a basic push for the PPV. Umbrage came out and attacked Fury, and made the challenge for the Midwest Championship. Matrix defeated Kyle Keenan and challenged Dess to a Blood Factory match. Jeff Hardkore defeated Noah Hanson, but was subsequently stripped of the title and handed the new Cruiserweight Title by the new Commissioner, Donovan Merlot. And what is up with Merlot being named Commissioner. Its like going from one extreme to another. Goody goody, to pure evil. But then again...two extremes,...one looked good, and thus far, the other has done good. Merlot stripping Hardkore of the title was one of the highest rated segments on the show, right behind the match itself, and the main event. After that, Starblazer and The Xtreme Rebel had thier rematch, and this time around, Scheffert came out winner, and got shot into the PPV, right in the title race, while Starblazer has been given a dark match. Better luck next time, Starblazer. Star is a great talent, and hopefully, he will pick up his game for next week. And the Tag Team Title match...the Main Event. The highest rated match of the night. I don't even think Sub-Beast cared about winning the match. For the first half of the match, he had Dess on the outside. Both were a bloody mess by the time the match ended, but we saw some good moves, and even saw Volcano and Archangel battle to see who was the biggest man. At the PPV, they get to have a one on one match. Front Office Bits ------------------ WMW has been in a signing fury, this week, signing a total of 6 new wrestlers. And, Johnson is saying there will be more, making one believe that he is preparing for PTC to sign WMW to a cable contract. It didn't take long, thats for sure, but the move is very deserved, on WMW's side. We've worked hard to create good concepts, and everyone has been working hard to put a good product out there. WMW signed a few new column writers, or atleast will be this week. I can't wait to read what our new writers have to say. Reb's Perspective is proving to be a really awesome column, and I hope he keeps it up. It has been released now that WMW was once again offered to merge with another fed, but once again, turned the offer down. Its like, fed heads are seeing the fed growing and succeeding and want to push it down. So they offer to have WMW join them, either hoping that we can do the same for thier fed, or they'll have atleast one less competitor. But, alas, WE have one less competitor. WMW signed a short-term contract with another, smaller interfed promotion called Newfound Wrestling. No word on when the contract will official begin and the two companies will start doing business together. PTC Happenings ------------------ Primetime Central will be holding an interfed tournament next month. Currently, no WMW superstars have been officially added to the tourny, but it is speculated that WMW will send atleast 5 over. Brad Johnson will be taking part in the organization and booking of the event. Also, PTC sent WMW a warning, during the week. It was a cease and desist order to stop recruiting off of the PTC Free Agent pool. Public Access federations do not have permission to recruit through this method. WMW sent out a memo to all of its employees to make sure that the action stopped. Who to look for ------------------ Johnny Blayze has really opened my eyes. He's got a lot of heart, and is very consistant, both with his promos and in the ring. Its hard to say what happened, but the transition over this last month is really pushing him into the spotlight. The DT, currently is the top guy on the Developmental Roster. He'll be making his debut on the main roster after the PPV. He's definatly superstar quality. He's got the superstar look, and attitude in the ring.
