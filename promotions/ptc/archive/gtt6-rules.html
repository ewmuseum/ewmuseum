GTT6 rules

2006

What this is (written in 2026) The GTT6 tournament site’s own rules page, with the judging panel as named at the time of writing, as the donor’s database holds it (lot 0159). The page names no date: it says the tournament was expected to run from early October to mid December 2006 and lists the first ten judges the 24 September 2006 judging-panel post names. No capture of the page is held. The page’s navigation and the markup of its headings are not reproduced; its text is.
Rules What: PTC's GTT6 is a singles elimination roleplaying tournament. When: The tournament is expected to run from early October through to mid December, 2006. Who: GTT6 is an open invitation tournament. That means anyone can enter, not just members of PTC feds. The tournament director is Argyle, PTC's owner. How: Registered entrants compete in the tournament by roleplaying against their nominated opponent. The roleplays are judged by peers of both entrants, and the entrant deemed to have posted superior work moves on to the next round. Why: GTT6 is provided by PTC for e-fedding enthusiasts to have fun competing against each other in matches they would not otherwise experience. An offshoot of that is for the possiblity of member feds being able to sign "Free Agent" players. An entrant is classed as a Free Agent if they are not a member of a PTC member fed. That being said, there is no expectation on any of the Free Agent entrants to sign with a PTC fed after playing in GTT. If you like what you see in any of the PTC member feds, feel free to negotiate and join. If not and you are just here for the tournament, enjoy yourself on us. Cool… so how many people are going to be in this thing? GTT6 is limited to 96 entrants. Damn, that's a lot of people… so how much work am I going to have to do each round? What's the RP Limit? That will vary each round. The first round will have a 1RP limit. The main reason for this is to weed out the people who aren't serious about playing, without overly taxing the people who are serious about playing. Subsequent rounds will see increases in the RP limit, at the discretion of the tournament director. It is envisaged that Round 2 will be a 2 RP limit, while the final round will be a 4 or 5 RP limit, with the rounds in between then in between that somewhere. Did you just say it could be 5 RP's in the final round? Why so many? Why not three like usual? A RP limit is a maximum, not a minimum. It is expected that come time for the final round, the players may well be quite spent of ideas. The extra is, just in case they aren't, to give them all the latitude in the world to be able to prove who is THE BEST in the game. That and that we don't want any old greasemonkey deciding where Buddy's hundred bucks goes to. How long is each Roleplay period? Round 1 will be for six days. Other rounds will vary depending on the RP limit and how many weekend days in each. So, what are the RULES? One Character per Handler: Handling two characters in this tournament takes away a spot from someone else. Anyone found handling multiple characters will have each character expelled from the tournament. Plagiarism: Anyone found to be stealing the work of others and posting it in this event will be banned from participating in any future PTC events, for life. No Recycling: All Roleplays posted must be original for this event. This means that they are not to have already been used in your home fed. They may be archived in your home fed after you have finished participating in the tournament, not before. They are not to be used for competative purposes in your home fed, period. If you are found doing this, you will be barred from future PTC events. Quoting: If you use snippets of other people's work, or quote song lyrics, or anything else that you didn't personally write, you MUST quote your reference. Linking: All Roleplays must be posted on the GTT6 website using a username and password determined during the application process. Roleplays linked and hosted elsewhere will not be considered during judging. Deadline Extensions: Are extremely unlikely to be granted, and definitely not when it is not something that is our fault. Time zone differences will not be accepted as an excuse. There will be countdown clocks posted on this website to determine the precise ending of the roleplay period relative to where the player is in the world. Login troubles will not be accepted as an excuse. It is the responsibility of the player to test the login and password combination given to them at the earliest possible time, even if they don't intend to roleplay at that point. The short of it is that the Tournament Director will be extremely unwilling to hold up the entire tournament for the benefit of one person, even if they are in fact being shafted by this not being done. Think Star Trek II - The needs of the many outweigh the needs of the few. Roleplay Content: Once Roleplays have been posted on this website, they will remain available for viewing on the site archive once the event is over. Future requests by the author to remove material will be carried out at the sole discretion of PTC management. What's the catch? I'm glad you asked. The tournament director reserves the right to shuffle any or all brackets at any time during the tournament. Holy shit! I really don't like change. I want things to be nice and predictable. I want to know who I'm facing 8 rounds in advance, and I want to research them all right now. Don't do this to me! Did you ever watch a couple of series' of Big Brother? Notice how they introduce new things or change their routine each time to keep things interesting? Well, if some of these things are introduced during the course of the tournament, think of it like that. In other words, don't be a wuss, and don't overlook your opponents and book yourself into the finals before roleplaying even starts. So, how come Free Agents can't take the Global or Extreme Title shots? These titles are reserved for members of PTC feds. They are a way for contenders to get their work read by others outside of their home fed. Also, PTC is an interfed, set up for the benefit of its member feds, so it would be a bit silly if a Free Agent held any of its titles. But I used to be in a PTC fed, I'm just not in one now. I've posted on the PTC forums for YEARS! Surely that must count for something, right? Surely you can make an exception for me? No. If you're not in a PTC fed at the time of entering the tournament, then you can't have the title shots. You're also not permitted to claim that part of the tournament prize pool if you happen to get signed to one half way through the tournament. You'll have to be content with bragging rights and the other prizes. Okay. Hey, I have to ask, what's with the names for each bracket? "Sometimes, if you stand on the bottom rail of a bridge and lean over to watch the river slipping slowly away beneath you, you will suddenly know everything there is to be known." ~ Winnie the Pooh Oh, one last question, what's with the extra questions on the application form? You mean the no-show thing? Well, if there are more applicants than there are spaces, PTC management needs to determine the best way to work out which applications to reject. People with a history of no-showing PTC events may find themselves first in line to be rejected. GTT6 judging panel These people are required to make an attempt to reasonably justify their pick. Nothing more, nothing less. They are not required to give you detailed feedback or tips. They aren't your English teacher or your older brother or sister. Their job is to pick the winner of a match, and they should be able to do that without being molested afterwards by people with sour grapes. They potentially have a lot of work to do to meet their judging commitments, so can't be expected to give hugely detailed comments. Comments are an added bonus, not a right. With getting the names of these people, I'm also laying down the law up front - if anyone harrasses any of them in private and I find out about it, there will be ejections, bans and suspensions in response. These people give up their free time to do the work necessary to bring results. If something they say doesn't float your boat and you feel that whining, complaining or otherwise blustering is the only way to respond, that's tough titties for you, end of story. Openly slanderous general comments such as "fraud", "bias" and the like will not be tolerated. I have no sense of humour about this, so I suggest you don't even make jokes about it. At the time of writing, the judging panel includes: Argyle (PTC admin) RJ S. (PTC admin) Pete S. (PTC admin) Ben M. (The Uber Judge) Jeff W. (Angelo Deville) Pitt (Jake Hix) Maria L. (Craig Goat, Nurse Wretched) Sam L. (Drake Coleman) Andrew D. (Langsuyar) Jamie Fletcher. As the tournament goes on and people are eliminated, the judging panel changes. Eliminated people step up and help out, and people who judged previous rounds have to attend to other commitments, and therefore the judging panel is subject to change. Anything else?
