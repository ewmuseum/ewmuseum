4 August 2004 PRIME :: Family Problems For Boda Despite a successful midweek houseshow in Tampa Bay, the fans were left disappointed by one notable absence from the line-up. With the event being so close to his hometown, Floridian PRIME-ates where looking forward to seeing former OSW superstar Boda in action, however he was not in attendance. In fact, the Big Bad Boda Daddy has not featured in a PRIME ring since Colossus last month. At first, his absence was thought to be the result of injuries he sustained during his brutal encounter with Kasper Sky, but now sources are suggesting that the Orlando resident is going through some "personal family problems". At the moment there are no further details other than the rumoured involvement of his two daughters. Unless any further information is released, this remains speculative at best however Boda is expected to remain on authorised leave until this situation is resolved. Source: PRIME === 5 August 2004 cW Bids Farewell to FitzPatrick BEAUMONT, TX - August 4, 2004 – As of August 2, 2004, Core Wrestling has been without one the presence of one of its most influential personalities on camera and off camera. After the August 1st, pay per view, Eugene “Gene” FitzPatrick turned in his resignation to the Core Wrestling Board of Directors. On camera, FitzPatrick was the Commissioner of Smash. Off of camera, FitzPatrick was the CEO of the company. Lanier said that he was glad that FitzPatrick decided to retire from his company instead of another company and that Core Wrestling has suffered an irreplaceable loss by FitzPatrick retiring from Core Wrestling. “Fitz and I talked about the possibility of this happening,” Lanier stated, “Fitz has decided his time is up in the world of professional wrestling, so he has retired. I’m glad he chose to retire from Core Wrestling instead of some other promotion because it has been a privilege to work with FitzPatrick and I would be angry if he was to have left us early on to work for someone else. Core Wrestling has suffered a hard, irreplaceable loss with the resignation of Gene.” FitzPatrick was headed back to London, England, after turning in his resignation. He said he wanted to get home and spend time with his family. His daughter, Eliza FitzPatrick, will remain on the commentary staff of Core Wrestling along with Taylor Snyder. “Daddy will be missed,” Eliza said, “We played out that father daughter feud well, but I love my dad and I’m glad he’s finally getting able to go home and rest. I’ll miss him around here, but he’ll always be a part of Core Wrestling.” “Having Eliza around,” Lanier also stated, “Will be like having a piece of Fitz around here too.” Core Wrestling wishes [name withheld], or Eugene FitzPatrick as the Core Wrestling fans new him as, the best of luck in his new retired lifestyle. He will be missed and never forgotten around Core Wrestling. Source: corewrestling.net
