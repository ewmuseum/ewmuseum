The Elite Championship is a professional wrestling world championship that is independent of any federation. Competed over primarily by Primetime Central Affiliated feds, the recent Inaugural Tournament saw Steve Knox of NFW become the first ever Elite Champion. What is the Elite Championship? The Elite Championship was created as an independent touring title after the PTC Unified Title was retired. Its purposes is to provide competition and to attract attention to the federations and competitors associated with it. It allowed for a string to be drawn from fed to fed to increase readership and allow others to become aware of the competitors from these federations. It also promotes a friendliness between the different feds as they work together to create stories and develop feuds that will later turn into matches on a variety of different events. The Mission Statement The idea behind the Elite Championship is to continue to unify the PTC community under one title that will allow for healthy interfed competition and continue to build relationships around the community. We pick up the strands of the PTC Unified Title and move on from it with the goal of creating a title that is competitive and honored throughout the community while respecting the past of PTC and working towards enriching that history. Respect is the name of the game while competition is what motivates us.
