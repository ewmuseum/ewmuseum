SCCW Television Temptation Main Article: Temptation Temptation is the flagship broadcast of SCCW, airing semi-weekly on Cinemax, and hosted by Dave Gibson and Eugene Ware. SIN on SPIKE Main Article: SIN on SPIKE SIN on SPIKE marks SCCW's halfway point between Pay Per View broadcasts, and showcases PPV Quality Matches on Basic Cable. Pay-Per-View Broadcasts SCCW features several pay-per-view events throughout the calendar year. Snake Eyes Main Article: Snake Eyes This event, held March 31, 2008 at the Miramar Marine Corps Air Station, featured what many have labeled as the PTC Match of the Year, the SCCW Universal Title cage match between Jonathan Rhine and Aimz. All In Main Article: All In This event took place in August '08 and featured the first-ever Heaven & Hell Match for an on-demand shot at the SCCW Universal Title, won by Desade, then posing as Charlotte Ramone. Defiance II Main Article: Defiance II This event took place in December '08 and featured Lance Marshall challenging Aimz for the SCCW Universal Championship.
