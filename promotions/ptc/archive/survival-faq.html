What this is (written in 2026) The PTC Survival event site’s own FAQ page (the headshot resizing, how the event would be written, the rule on angles), as the donor’s database holds it (lot 0159). The page names no date; Survival was a 2004 event. The writing-style section repeats the wording of the organiser’s third Survival update, served on this site. No capture of the page is held. The page’s navigation and the markup of its headings are not reproduced; its text is.
Why did my headshot get changed some? Aesthetics. The default headshot size is 100x100 pixels. Some of the headshots provided were not proportional to this so had some edges chopped off. Others were much larger than this so had a smaller subsection cut out of them, as squishing them down to the 100x100 size made them look like crap. How will the event be written? Depsite my preference for novel style, I think that a script style will be easier for each person to write. Therefore, we're going to do a combination of the two. The following is a perfect example of how this event should be written: Wrestler A backed Wrestler B into a corner, and unleashed a flurry of fists to his head, knocking B silly. A pulled B from the corner, and irish whipped him into the opposite turnbuckle before following him in with a big splash. B came out of the corner, and fell to the ground after swinging at the air, like Ric Flair. Kern: Excellent series of moves from A there! Marx: He used a closed fist! This isn't boxing! Argyle: A comes from a fighting background. He uses his striking moves to set up his finisher, a deadly lateral press! Marx: So what? Punching is for boxing! Not Wrestling! A rolled B over, and went for the cover. 1... 2... Kickout by B! Kern: Almost a victory by B here in the opening minutes of this match! Wrestler A picked B up by the hair, pulling B to a vertical base. He lifted an arm, and ducked under B, lifting him for a suplex, and carrying him over in a classic vertical suplex. A stood afterward, and put a heavy boot to the top of B's head. A walked confidently to the ropes and climbed to the top turnbuckle. Argyle: He's setting him up for the 360º Guillotene Leg Drop! Kern: If he hits this, this match is over! Marx: THe match is already over. No one cares! Booo! Booo! Punchy McPuncherson should have been DQed minutes ago! Wrestler A jumps off the top, and hits the leg drop across the throat of B. He reaches down and hooks B's leg. Kern: THERE IT IS! THE LATERAL PRESS! THE MOST FEARED MOVE IN WRESTLING HISTORY! Fans: ONE! TWO! THREE! Angles You're welcome to play off any angles on TV. For instance: If there's a certain team you want to call out, do so. If there's a certain match you'd like to set up with a backstage interview, do so. If you're friends with people on other teams, feel free to do something with them. Understand though, that you need to make each team come out on top with your comments. There should be no backstage attacks, PERIOD. The threat that it could happen is one thing. The actual attack itself? Not allowed.
