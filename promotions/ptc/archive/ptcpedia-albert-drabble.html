Drabble grew up on the streets of West Yorkshire, England. Not much is known about Drabble, except that he has a strong connection with his close friend Julia. Nobody knows where he aquired his wrestling skill, but one thing that has been revealed since his debut, is that he is one of the most pleasantly surprising wrestlers for his size that you're ever likely to see. WWA Road Agent Gordon Korth spotted him at an Independent show in Las Vegas in November of 2008, and signed him to a try out contract. After his debut, his contract was renegotiated and he is now on a contract worth six figures. Early Life All that is known about Albert Drabble is that he was raised on the streets of West Yorkshire and that he has had a tough life. He has a very close bond with a former prostitute named Julia, who has joined him in the WWA as his valet. Drabble suffers from serious bouts of bi-polar disorder and depression and has suffered chronic insomnia for years, he is also said to be borderline suicidal. Nobody knows where he aquired his wreslting skill, but one thing that has been revealed since his debut, is that is one of the most plesantly surpising wrestlers for his size that you'll ever see. WWA Road Agent Gordon Korth spotted him at an Independent show in Las Vegas in November of 2008, and signed him to a try out contract. Korth also signed fellow WWA newcomer, Elvis Hunt, to a contract on that night. Career World Wrestling Alliance Albert Drabble and Julia made their WWA debut on the February 23 edition of Meltdown, to defeat KC James. Two weeks later Drabble lost in a fourway match to decide the number one contender to the Television Championship at Revolution, to exciting newcomer Brandon Eagle, when Drabble became engaged in a brawl at ringside with Michael Cross.
