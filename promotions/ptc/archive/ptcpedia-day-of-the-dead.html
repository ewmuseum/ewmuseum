Day Of The Dead, is the fall spectacular of the World Wrestling Alliance, traditionally occuring around Halloween. Day Of The Dead has featured some classic match-ups and that tradition will only continue into the future. History Day Of The Dead 2004 [1] Azteca Stadium, Mexico City Vain & Vicious (Mick Vain and Vic Vicious) & The Devil Dogs (Adam Tangle and William Johnson) defeated the League of Goodness (Great Animated Hero and Stereotypical Anime Sidekick) and KaOs (Komodo Dragon and Pyro) via pinfall in a Way to Greatness match. "Bonafide" James Curzon won the Intercontinental Championship over Khaled via The Rapier Splash in a Steel Cage match. Craven Moore and Dave Harley won the WWA World Tag Team Championship over The Pain Syndicate (Desolation and Agony) via pinfall. JuceMystA defeated Leviathan in a Green Room match via Jucifier.
