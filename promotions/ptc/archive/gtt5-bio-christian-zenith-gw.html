Christian Zenith Biography Biography Christian Ulrich Zenith was born in Rammstein, Germany… the son of a German business man and his wife Sylvia, who was a homemaker. Christian only has one sibling, Tera Zenith. Supposibly, she is dead now… as is the rest of his family. After moving to the United States, Christian was living the American Dream… spoiled by the riches of his father. Not only did he learn from best tutors money could buy, his vast knowledge of Vale Tudo was taught to him by the greastest Vale Tudo fighters in the world. It was amazing what the almighty dollar can do to the life of a German Boy. A boring life as Vice President under his father's rule as a teenager drove Christian to the streets in rebellion of his silver spooned ways. His money and power combined with his hard nosed business skills turned Christian Zenith into a legend on the streets of Chicago. Not anymore though. After the assassination attempt on his life, Christian Zenith has since left the underground scene for life much better suited for someone of his nature. The roped octagon of Global Wrestling. Now he is also the Owner & CEO of his late father's Fortune 500 Insurance company. Well, at least to the general public it's known as an insurance company. Behind the scenes, Zenith Corporation really makes its money as the #1 producer of weaponry for the United States Military. There is nothing Christian Zenith enjoys more than the battling of the minds, a psychological battle to show his superiority to all men. Being the best is his motivation for going on, he will settle for nothing less than dominating his adversaries both mentally and physically in the ring. Christian Zenith is very intelligent and a shrud business man. Although he is fairly skilled in the ring, his mental attributes is his strong point. Other Feds Global
