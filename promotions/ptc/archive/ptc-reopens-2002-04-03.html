Primetime Central Reopens, 3 April 2002

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The main announcement page of Primetime Central’s own site (main.shtml), as the Wayback Machine captured it: the notice of 3 April 2002 that Primetime Central Television was ready to broadcast, with the twelve-channel map (four channels of PTC’s own, eight for its network federations) and the federations left off it. The page’s banner image and the channel logos are not carried.
Primetime Central Reopens 4/03/02 Primetime Central Officals were proud to announce that Primetime Central Television is ready to broadcast now that the eight networks are signed to long time broadcast deals and each will have a premiere fed represent them in weekly rankings. Primetime Central Television will broadcast for the first time on Saturday night right after PTC Malice goes off air. From that point on aside from any unforeseen circumstances PTC TV will be on air 24/7 and provide twelve jam packed channels for the serious wrestling fan. At this time PTC TV will offer subscribers twelve channels with four channels available only on PTC TV and eight networks that have been picked up from their normal viewing area and assigned a channel on PTC TV. In the future subscribers could see more channels added but PTC TV officals were more then pleased with the release of only 12. Noticible in their abscence are PCW, OSW, NTICW, XWA, RUSH and PWA. PTC TV decided that they would not move these popular feds from their regular broadcast channels and they will not be part of PTC TV at this time. Here is a run down of PTC TV as it is currently. Channel 1 will be occupied by the News Network and will be the channel to get up to date news from the eight network feds and PTC's smaller affiliated feds. Channel 2 otherwise known as the Central Ranking Channel. Here PTC Fans can check up on the latest PTC Wrestler rankings and Ratings. Channel 3 is being asigned as a 24 hour Infomercial Channel where the PTC affiliate feds will fill the airwaves with ads for thier feds. Channel 4 will host the Primetime Network. This is the station all will want to tune into as all major PTC events will be broadcasted here starting off with PTC's first I-PPV April 14th. Channels 5 through 12 will host PTC's eight network federations which will also broadcast in their own region. For the real wrestling fan PTC TV is the only way to go but for the casual fan you are bound to be able to find one or two of these feds broadcasted on your local cable packages. Below is a little background on just where the stations originate from. Channel 5 the American Sports Channel will proudly bring you Global Wrestling Federation. A long time member of the PTC this fed should be one to watch as it gets some primetime exposure on of a America's most widely watched Sports Channels. Channel 6 and the West Coast Network will bring exciting West Coast style wrestling of The Wrestling Aliance to the western coast of North America. Channel 7 and the Great Northwest Network signed the only Canadian based federation of the eight network feds and Canuck Heat Zone will heat up the homes of the Canadian wrestling fans. Channel 8 and the International Sports Network will host Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. Aside from PTC TV CCW shall be seen in many European homes providing a very entertaing alternative to "football" or better known as soccer to Americans. Channel 9 has been assigned to the ACCESS network a large satelite based broadcasting corporation and perhaps the only PTC Network that would fit with Big Money Wrestling. The two will team to broadcast internationally to anywhere a satelite is located. Channel 10 and Great Lakes Television will proudly present LCW-Mercury to viewers in the Great Lakes area. Channel 11 and The EEL Network which is being hyped as China's answer to FOX has added Furiously Addictive Wrestling to it's lineup and will provide the Asian area with it's share of American Stlye Wrestling. Channel 12 The Wrestling Channel will host the attractive looking federation LCW-Evo, which will be picked up by many local cable companies in North America. The rest of the feds associated with PTC can apply to join because just like real TV shows get cancelled if they don't get the job done. Waiting lists will form and affiliations will continue to be made.
