Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Primetime Central? PTC is a user driven news site with a loose interfed attached. Feds post their own news and hype their own stars. Fans, players and fed heads can visit our forums to chat about the hobby, look for new feds, web design help or free agents. At its core, PTC is a community above all other things. Q: Who owns this dump? Primetime Central is owned by a group of super intelligent monkeys. These monkeys work hard under the supervision of the all-powerful and mysterious Digital Warlord, who has been known to crush all those who oppose him. Q: What’s with all the channels and how do I view them? A: Think of PTC TV as real television, there’s a remote which is located in the lower right hand channel of the screen and all you do is push the button you want to view. Each channel has specific programming on it be one of the network feds, a news channel or any of our other channels to come in the future. Q: How does PTC work? A: PTC is set up and monitored but it works because feds, primarily the network feds with posting privileges keep it up to date with daily posts. Q: What type of things can I find on PTC TV? A: Currently you can find news on the network feds and some closer affiliated feds. For the network feds their channel will show you some background information on the feds, their news postings, up to three recent shows, the top ten wrestlers, current champions and wrestler spotlights. There is also an infomercial channel were 88x31’s of PTC affiliates will go. Q: How do I become an affiliate? A: Simply click on the join option above and fill in the required information and put a link to PTC on your site. Affiliates are not network feds. (See below) Q: How do I become a Network Fed? A: Network feds are normally feds that have been around for a while and have a solid player base as well as a reliable staff. If you become an affiliate and prove to be worthy of a network spot you could either get picked up on a new channel or replace another fed that isn’t up to standards. Q: Will PCW, OSW, NTICW, RUSH, XWA, or PWA be apart of PTC? A: For some I-PPV and tournaments members of these feds MAY participate depending on the event but for the most part the answer is NO. PCW, OSW, NTICW, RUSH, XWA, and PWA will not be involved in any of the PTC Channels, or rankings or any other correlation to PTC TV. Q: Is this stuff copyrighted? Yes, by either PTC or the affiliated fed that posts the news. Don't steal, borrow, or reproduce content here without our permission. Q: How do I contact the monkeys or Digital Warlord? You can email us at [detail withheld], or drop us a line on our forums. Q: I want to join PTC, but my fed isn't open. Will my fed be considered? No, it will not. You should show that you have an active player base and secure staff prior to applying. We will, however, consider giving you a lesser affiliation, which gives you posting privs to our "other news section." Q: It burns when I pee. What should I do? See your family physician immediately. Q: Can my fed join PTC if we are enrolled in another interfed? It depends. We have healthy working relationships with some other interfeds, and not so friendly relationships with others. So, as stated, it will depend on who you are currently involved with. Note, we will consider giving feds in any interfed posting privilages on our "Other Efed News" section, assuming that the news is related to their fed and not advertising another interfed in any way, shape or form. Q: Can you guys make me poser images? No, we really can't. Use the advertising forum to find poser artists.
