(Stanislav gingerly limps to his feet, and immediately manages to lock a bearhug in on the HIT!) Jeff Marx- THIS IS ALL! TRAPEZOID’S TAPPING! LOOK AT HIM! Nate Dumas- He’s not tapping yet! But it doesn’t matter, because Riggs is going to call for the damn bell anyway! (Riggs reaches back to call for the bell, but a resurgent Leo Micelli gets up and takes Riggs down!) Jeff Marx- IVAN HAS BEEN SHAFTED OUT OF A VICTORY BY THAT IDIOT OFFICIAL! Nate Dumas- HE’S THE MAN IN CHARGE! (Micelli goes over to Ivan, and breaks the bearhug with a low blow.) Jeff Marx- THE REFEREE CAN’T ATTACK A COMPETITOR! (Trapezoid walks over to Riggs, and picks him up in an inverted crucifix. Turning to face the announce position, HIT flips him into a sitout Tombstone piledriver.) Nate Dumas- PERPINDICULAR BISECTOR ON SPEEDY RIGGS! HE’S OUT, THANK GOD! Mr. Batee- CAN YOU SEE HOW WELL HIT IS HANDLING RED ARMY INTERFERENCE? Nate Dumas- Absolutely. Although, if it wasn’t for Ivan’s own mistake, he’d have had the pin after that Iron Curtain when there was no referee in the ring! (Trapezoid turns around, as Ivan latches the bearhug back on, and turns it into a belly to belly suplex, crushing the HIT to the canvas.) Jeff Marx- The Soviet Machine is destroying Trapezoid! (Ivan lifts the HIT back up, and takes him right back down with a sambo suplex. A grinning Stanislav drops a hard elbow into the Trapezoid’s ribs, then makes the cover.) Nate Dumas- The big elbow from Ivan! That could be it! 1…… (The Red Army rises at ringside, clapping their hands.) 2…… (Ruslan nervously clasps his hands, awaiting the third handclap. Alas, it is not to be, as the Trapezoid manages to get the shoulder up.) Nate Dumas- Trapezoid kicks away again! Jeff Marx- It doesn’t matter. Ivan is still in control! (Stanislav smiles, going over to the corner with the table in it. He climbs with Trapezoid to the top rope, lifting him into position for a powerbomb off the top.) Nate Dumas- Ivan’s going to end it again! (Trapezoid manages to counter, and pulls Ivan’s leg out, crotching him on the top rope. Ivan turns to climb back down, facing the cage, but is caught by Trapezoid. HIT crosses Ivan’s arms..) Nate Dumas- LOGARITMIC FUNCTION, COMING UP! Jeff Marx- NO WAY IN HELL! Nate Dumas- He has the leverage! All he has to do is drag Ivan off the top! (HIT manages it, putting Ivan through the table with his crossarm thunder fire powerbomb. Still, Ivan sits up.) Nate Dumas- CRAP! HE JUST WENT THROUGH A TABLE!! (Alexei Ruslan signals quickly to Tom Walczak. The powerhouse manages to ascend the steel cage, and climbs in. As Stanislav shakes himself out, Walczak charges the Trapezoid, but is met quickly with a Triangle Trapplex. Stanislav goes back to work on the HIT with hard body shots. Walczak gets back up, and signals for the Polish Powerbomb, but Ivan waives him off.) Jeff Marx- The hell? Nate Dumas- Ivan’s getting Walczak out! He wants to do this one by himself! (As Walczak begins climbing over the top, Ivan picks HIT up, and flings him headfirst over the top rope, into the steel cage. Trapezoid gracelessly crashes into the ground. Walczak, Krieg, and the Two Round Faced Women leave, as Ivan waves farewell to his comrades.) Nate Dumas- TRAPEZOID’S GETTING OBLITERATED! (As he lies on the concrete, The HIT removes his mask, revealing his familiar face, as blood trickles down his face from a wound on the top of his head, matting his now shortened hair with blood.) Jeff Marx- Ivan has painted the Trapezoid red! THE COLOR OF COMMUNISM!! (Slowly, The HIT rises and rolls back into the ring. Ivan grabs Trapezoid by his bleeding face, and lifts him into the air, nearly beheading him with THE IRON CURTAIN!) Nate Dumas- THE IRON CURTAIN! SECOND ONE OF THE MATCH! Jeff Marx- And the referee’s not unconscious!! (Ivan drops down, hooking the Trapezoid’s leg, as the count goes down. 1….. (Loyal Communist Party Member Wang cheers from his spot on the floor, still dazed from HIT’s attack.) 2….. (Ruslan and Tempest prepare to cheer..) 3……………..Or not. (HIT’s shoulder slips out at the last split second. The crowd is decidedly split at this point, as the HIT supporters begin a chant of “HIT! HIT!” Ivan looks at him incredously, as Ivan turns to get the chair from earlier. He takes the weapon, and rises it overhead, as HIT crawls, like a gasping fish, towards something.) Nate Dumas- STANISLAV STALKING THE HIT WITH THAT CHAIR! (Stanislav rears back to strike the death blow. However, HIT manages to get the object he was crawling for, and nails Stanislav in the gut with his oversized barbed wire yardstick. Ivan stoically endures the pain, as the HIT pulls the weapon away, with chunks of suspender and flesh stuck to it.) Jeff Marx- Ivan stands! (Trapezoid winds up, and smashes Ivan full in the face with the weapon, dropping the Russian Bear. Ivan’s proud face slowly begins to bleed from a half-dozen tiny puncture wounds.) Jeff Marx- NYEEEEEEETTTTTTTT!!! Nate Dumas- Stanislav’s been busted open! (Trapezoid sees his chance. He steels himself, preparing for what he perceives to be his last chance to win the match. Stanislav rises, as The HIT bends the massive Ivan over, and applies a pumphandle.) Nate Dumas- He’s going for Parallel Bases! Jeff Marx- There is no way he can lift Ivan!! (Trapezoid attempts to lift Ivan once…twice…..and finally gets him up on the third try! He pulls Ivan to about a 60-degree angle with the ground, and drops him with PARALLEL BASES!) Nate Dumas- HOLY CRAP! TRAPEZOID SCORES PARALLEL BASES!! (HIT uses the last of his energy to crawl over and cover Ivan.) 1………… 2……… (As Jeff Marx nearly begins to cry at ringside, Ivan shoots a shoulder off the canvas. His kickout has none of the authority of his earlier ones, but it prevents him from losing nonetheless. To prevent Ivan from getting up, HIT rises, and quickly leaps to the top, nailing Ivan with a high hang time moonsault.) Nate Dumas- THE Y-AXIS TRANSFORMATION! But HIT’s not covering… (Trapezoid gets up off of Ivan, and climbs out of the ring. He looks up the twenty foot steel structure, and begins to climb..) Mr. Batee- CAN YOU SEE THAT THE HIT IS ASCENDING THE STEEL CAGE! (Trapezoid smiles, as he reaches the apex of the structure. He holds his arms out for balance, and prepares to jump.) Nate Dumas- THIS IS IT! TRAPEZOID’S TAKING THE MATCH IN HIS HANDS! He has to be going for his finisher here! Jeff Marx- GET UP STANISLAV! (The HIT leaps, into a Swanton. This is held briefly before he rotates through with a 450. Just before impact, he turns the move into a frog splash. The impact from twenty feet in the air jars Stanislav’s body. The HIT flies about four feet in the air after striking, and falls off of Ivan.) Nate Dumas- FOUR-THIRDS PI TIMES RADIUS CUBED! Trapezoid’s got it won! (HIT inches over to Ivan’s downed body..and finally gets a hand over Ivan’s chest.) Jeff Marx- NYET! COME ON IVAN!! 1…. Nate Dumas- NO WAY! NO ONE KICKS OUT OF THAT! 2…. Mr. Batee- EVEN I CAN SEE IT’S OVER!! (With an incredible surge, Ivan presses The HIT off of him!) Nate Dumas- Unbelievable! Ivan has got to be superhuman! Jeff Marx- HE IS BEYOND SUPERHUMAN! HE IS COMMUNIST! (Stanislav slowly gets on his feet. As a flabbergasted Trapezoid looks on, Ivan applies a front facelock, and begins to lift the HIT in the air.) Jeff Marx- RED SCARE! DO IT, COMRADE! (Ivan releases the HIT at the apex of the suplex. Unfortunately, Ivan’s power is such that the Trapezoid flips in midair, and goes flying into the cage. Unbelievably, he grabs onto the bars, and springs off with a twisting cross body. Ivan, however, even more unbelievably catches him, and executes a fall away slam.) Nate Dumas- I cannot believe what I just saw! Jeff Marx- I can! Stanislav is dominating the matchup again! (Stanislav places the steel chair on the HIT’s face, and delivers a legdrop, crushing the weapon into the HIT’s bloodied cranium. As he pulls the chair away, we can see HIT’s face is practically coated with the red liquid. Stanislav covers, not hooking a leg this time.) 1…… 2….. (The HIT again kicks out. Ivan’s incredulousness seems to have faded, as he realizes he is in a war. On the outside, Ruslan decides that it is time to intervene, and he climbs the cage. Ivan tells Ruslan to return, but the wily military man pays no heed. He drops into the cage, with a pair of handcuffs, and swings for the fences. Unfortunately, the HIT ducks, and lifts Ruslan onto his shoulder in pumphandle position…) Nate Dumas- Watch this…. (Trapezoid holds the pumphandle, driving Ruslan headfirst to the mat with an Emerald Froison, in a move he calls simply Parallel Bases II.) Jeff Marx- THERE WILL BE HELL TO PAY FOR THIS! (Ivan charges, but HIT ducks. Stanislav hits the ropes as the HIT pounces, grabbing Ruslan’s handcuffs, and using them to bind Ivan to the ropes.) Jeff Marx- Someone stop this! Nate Dumas- Ivan’s in deep trouble! Trapezoid’s going off the deep end! (Trapezoid takes the barbed wire yardstick, and delivers another shot to Ivan’s skull. He follows with a second and a third, a sadistic look in his eyes.) Mr. Batee- CAN YOU SEE THE HIT’S LOST IT! (The Trapezoid has gone from normal to psychotic. The crowd does not know what to make of it, as the Ivan supporters grow more vocal. As Stanislav takes blow after blow, the final conscious person at ringside decides to make her move.) Nate Dumas- Tempest is getting out of her chair! Jeff Marx- Thank LENIN! Someone is going to try and stop this! Nate Dumas- I don’t think this is the best idea….HIT’s past track record indicates that he has no qualms about hitting a woman.. (Tempest begins climbing, quickly ascending the cage. She drops the last few feet on her descent, and rolls into the ring, stepping in front of Stanislav as The HIT readies another shot.) Nate Dumas- TRAPEZOID’S GOING TO DECAPITATE HER! (He doesn’t. Instead, he throws the weapon out of the ring, and backs up to center ring.) Jeff Marx- The hell? Nate Dumas- HIT wants a fight! And it looks like Tempest is willing to oblige…damn..this is getting serious.. (The two circle, intent concentration written on the faces of both.) Nate Dumas- This is pride here, plain and simple. Both from the premiere federations of their beginnings….HIT from IWO, Tempest from fWo..and that being the fed still in the limelight today. The two feds had a rivalry for fans stemming for years. Whether or not either will admit it, there IS bad blood here… (Trapezoid seems exhausted, yet he makes the first move, a right hand that is blocked by Tempest. She fires back with a sharp knife hand chop, following it with a second and a third.) Nate Dumas- Tempest unloading on the Trapezoid! (She ducks a Trapezoidal right, and catches him with a spinning heel kick. Tempest grins, and climbs to the top rope, as a wobbly Trapezoid rises. She leaps off the top rope, planting the HIT with the infamous System Crash.) Jeff Marx- DA! DA! DAAAA!!!! Nate Dumas- TRAPEZOID’S BEEN KNOCKED OUT! But Ivan’s tied in the ropes! (Thinking quickly, Tempest drags the HIT’s body over to where Ivan is tied. Ivan lifts a massive boot, as Tempest slides HIT under it. Ivan presses his military boot onto the HIT’s chest.) Jeff Marx- COVER! 1…. Nate Dumas- You’ve got to be kidding! 2…. (Trapezoid manages to shoot his shoulder up. Tempest looks surprised, but she goes back to attack the Trapezoid. He shoots a quick go-behind, and nails her with a lightning-quick German Suplex.) Nate Dumas- SINE! (Trapezoid rolls through again….delivering a Tiger Suplex.) Nate Dumas- COSINE! (HIT rolls through once more, dragging Tempest to his feet, delivering Tangent, a Tiger Suplex ’85. Sensing the end is near, the Trapezoid calls for Parallel Bases. An enraged Ivan has had enough.) Nate Dumas- OH MY GOD! Jeff Marx- PRAPORCHIK!! (Rearing back, Stanislav SNAPS the ring cables! He stalks towards the Trapezoid, as Tempest flees, destroying the Sultan of Sides with the third Iron Curtain of the contest.) Jeff Marx- Okay, THAT’S THE THIRD IRON CURTAIN OF THE GODDAMNED MATCH! THIS….IS…..ALL! 1……. 2…… ………………………………….. (As the referee’s hand comes within an eyelash of three, the HIT weakly throws his right shoulder off the canvas.) Nate Dumas- TRAPEZOID LIVES! Jeff Marx- THIS IS IMPOSSIBLE! (Ivan’s shock fades quickly, as he sets the Trapezoid for a Red Scare. HIT slips behind, however, and locks in the Extraneous Solution!) Nate Dumas- He’s got it applied dead center!! (Trapezoid rips and tears away at Ivan’s neck and shoulder. Slowly, the Russian Bear drops to the ground. At this, the HIT releases the Extraneous Solution, and changes to the Word Problem, using Ivan’s massive arms to choke him out.) Jeff Marx- IVAN WILL NEVER TAP!!! (Indeed, Ivan seems to be doing his best to fight out of the move, but finally, the lack of breath becomes too much, and he ceases struggling.) Nate Dumas- Ivan’s passed out! Trapezoid choked Stanislav into unconsciousness! (Spotting the timekeeper in the confines of the cage, The HIT walks over, and snatches Ivan’s Order of Lenin Medallion from him, and places it around his own neck, and begins ascending the cage.) Jeff Marx- THIS IS BLASPHEMY! (The HIT finally stops, perched atop the cage. He extends his arms, and then leaps off. His body rotates backwards, completing the necessary rotation for a shooting star press in the first ten feet of falling. Unbelievably, he continues rotating AGAIN, and crashes onto the body of Stanislav in a move so impossible to conceive or execute that it could only be called….) Mr. Batee- CALCULUS! Nate Dumas- DOUBLE BACKFLIP SHOOTING STAR PRESS! That is the most insane thing I have ever seen! TRAPEZOID COVERS! 1…. (He reaches back, hooking one of Ivan’s massive legs.) 2…. Jeff Marx- SOMEONE GET RUSLAN AWAKE! WANG! SPEEDY! TEMPEST! ANYONE! (Marx’s pleading though, on this day, is not enough.) 3. (Ivan’s final words to the HIT can he heard, as he removes the medallion from around the Trapezoid’s neck.) Ivan Stanislav. I am sorry. But, it is as you capitalists say…business is business, da? (Ivan and Tempest walk down the aisle, embracing again at the top of the entryway. Slowly, The HIT recovers, as he climbs the turnbuckle, the crowd cheering both men as the show goes off the air.)
