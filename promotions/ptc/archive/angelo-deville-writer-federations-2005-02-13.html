Say something positive about....

13 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Primetime Wrestling Academy - (PWA) - A feedback forum that worked, though grudgingly at times. It helped me refine my roleplaying quite a bit, and in turn provided the opportunity to give constructive criticism to those who genuinely wanted it. It's also what introduced me to the concept of "community ewrestling", and was the sole deciding factor for installing AIM on my computer. I met a lot of great people there, and the short time I spent there was one of the most fun I've had in this hobby. Primetime Championship Wrestling (PCW) - The fed that made me join this community, and the launching pad for many of the great talents we've had and still have to this day. New True Internet Championship Wrestling (NTICW) - Insanely different (I was only around for one incarnation). Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment (PURE) - Gave all those people with unpronounceable names a place to feel at home. >=) Pro Wrestling Circuit (PWC) - It spawned Angiegol~! RUSH - Had a nice little rivalry with FSW, which I'm pained to see was left off this list. RUSH Pro Wrestling (RPW) - Like a retirement home for many of the old WOers. Great angles, great roster, and the sexiest damn fedhead the e-world has ever seen. >=) Underground Brawlers Club (UBC) - Would have been the birthplace of Los Cocko Doomo~! Sports Entertainment Live (SE-Live) - Before my time, I think. Wrestlingtattler/Wrestleonline (WT/WO) - I was ranked number one for the last rankings it ever had! whoo~! Old School Wrestling (OSW) - Hosted some of the best angles in the history of ever. EWPlanet (EWP) - Cimon~! ew-news - Great hype machine. Five Star Wrestling (FSW) - The place that r0x0red everyone's b0x0rs. Where I met and became friends with Petis, Dan, Justin, Mike, and many other people I respect and admire. We dominated GTT3. >=)
