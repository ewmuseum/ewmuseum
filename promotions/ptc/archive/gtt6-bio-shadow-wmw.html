Shadow Biography Biography Shadow is a bit hard to classify- a dark-horse surprise in GTT4, she has been hailed along with Dawn McFarlane as 'the only good thing to come out of WMW.' Dominance has been a theme of her career- typically, when Shadow holds a belt, she holds on to it for a *long* time. Yet, incomprehensibly, despite all her work, she has never been given a shot at a World Title. She has never had much luck in actually *winning* a tournament. Much to her great frustration, 'the big one' has always eluded her in the ring. But… the winds may be changing for her. Shadow, already a former PTC Extreme Champion, earned a Golden Ticket at Super Series 1 that awarded her a shot at the big prize- the PTC Global Title. After waiting for her shot until she was about to pop, Shadow came out with a strong showing against the Uber Judge, and walked away with the big prize. It was a sweet, sweet reward after a long sequence of doubts- Shadow can't ever make it big, her gimmick is ridiculous, she can't cash in when the chips are down, she's a good bit player, but not an Ophelia or a Desdemona. That's all changed now. And GTT6, PTC- they're looking like totally different ballgames for her. Other Feds GCW
