Roleplay Limits Extreme Title Match: - NO RP LIMIT Global Title Match: - 3 RP Limit Tag Title Match - 2 RP Limit per team - Sandbagging Rule for all matches: Only one RP on the final day. Judging: The judges will be selected from the PTC community by the event director and will not have any known bias towards either of the competitors in the match they are judging. Plagiarism: Anyone found to be stealing the work of others and posting it in this event will be banned from participating in any future PTC events, for life, and likely be treated like a leper by the majority of the community. Putting it simply, be kind to your fed and don't do it. Recycling: All Roleplays posted must be written for this event. This means that they are not to have already been used in your home fed or anywhere else. Additionally, they are to remain exclusive to this event, and may only be posted elsewhere for archival purposes. They are not to count towards matches in your home feds. Anyone sprung doing this will be barred from future PTC events, and any fed knowingly allowing this risks being kicked out of PTC. Quoting: If you use snippets of other people's work, or quote song lyrics, or anything else that you didn't personally write, you MUST quote your reference. Linking: All Roleplays must be posted on this website using a username and password provided by PTC Management. Roleplays linked and hosted elsewhere will not be considered during judging. Deadline Extensions: Are extremely unlikely to be granted. No-shows: We all get busy, these things happen. However, if a player accepts a position in this event and then no-shows without notifying both their opponent and PTC management that it is going to happen before the last day, then that player may be barred from competing in future PTC events. Its all about respect people. Roleplay Content: Once Roleplays have been posted on this website, they will remain available for viewing on the site archive once the event is over. Future requests by the author to remove material will be carried out at the sole discretion of PTC management.
