James Curzon First Appearance | Latest Appearance WWA | WWA Federations | World Wrestling Alliance Age | 29 Birthplace | Southampton, England Finisher | The Flintlock Wrestling Style | Cheating Technician Championships | WWA Intercontinental Champion WWA GenX Champion Handler | [name withheld] James Curzon (born May 2nd, 1980) is an English professional wrestler who currently wrestles for the World Wrestling Alliance, a former member of the Primetime Central circuit. Curzon is a cunning and devioius cheater. He is also a total coward and is paranoid about everything. He's arrogant and boastful about his skills, which due to his intense cowardice he rarely puts into use. "Bonafide" is a WWA veteran, who made his debut for the company in 2003. Curzon left the WWA in 2005 to work the International circuit, wrestling in Mexico, Canada, Germany, Japan, China, Italy and his native Great Britain. He re-signed with the World Wrestling Alliance in September of 2008 and made his on screen return with an attack on Nick Adams, he was accompanied by two more returning superstars in Harley Trapper and Johnny Shrapnel, collectively the three men are known as "Trouble". Early Life James Curzon was brought up in a family with a rich naval tradition. The Curzon's had been officers at sea for 7 generations and James had to follow the family tradition. He joined the Royal Navy but because of his cowardly nature he managed to get a cushy desk job. He saw out his commission living it up in London. He went on to study at Oxford University, he passed the course by bribing tutors and copying other people's essays. His parent's always paid for whatever he wanted and after he left Oxford his father bought him a WWA contract. Career World Wrestling Alliance "The Admiral" James Curzon arrived in the WWA expecting to be an instant success. However his arrogant attitude irritated everyone he met and Curzon was assaulted backstage on a number of occassions. James' natural cowardliness now combined with extreme paranoia. Curzon was convinced everyone was out to get him and that there was a conspiracy to hold him back. However Curzon made an ally in Showtime. The two men teamed up and, with help from Curzon's personal referee Silas Fagin, managed to get a few victories. Showtime mentored Curzon in the wrestling arts but James' was becoming more and more paranoid. Eventually he turned on his only friend and struck out on his own. Curzon decided a name change was in order, firstly as an alias to fool the Conspiracy's minions and secondly to remind everyone that he has "championship credentials" and is a "bonafide winner". James Curzon became Bonafide. Bonafide is still totally paranoid but now has developed a nasty temper. If anyone calls him a coward he flys into a rage and assaults the perpetrator. Curzon left the WWA in 2005 to work the International circuit, wrestling in Mexico, Canada, Germany, Japan, China, Italy and his native Great Britain. He re-signed with the World Wrestling Alliance in September of 2008 and made his on screen return with an attack on Nick Adams, he was accompanied by two more returning superstars in Harley Trapper and Johnny Shrapnel, collectively the three men are known as "Trouble".
