Source: FAW === 11 April 2004 Lanier Releases Statement Concerning mVw and PTC Radio BEAUMONT, TX - Sunday, April 11, 2004 – Upon his arrival after a meeting with FOX executives concerning the future of Melee returning to Core Wrestling, Matthew Lanier came in to find a memo informing him that Gene Fitzpatrick has accepted a challenge on behalf of himself and Lanier to compete in a match at an inter-promotional event with mVw in early July. The match accepted by Fitzpatrick was Matthew Lanier and Gene Fitzpatrick versus Taj Escobar and Phoenix Rose. Lanier called a quick press conference for all available press consultants to release a statement regarding the challenge. Below is a transcript of the conference. “Thanks you ladies and gentlemen for coming out so late for this gathering. I know it is past the normal operations hours for most people here in Beaumont, but I deemed this situation important enough to gather you all hear. Please save all questions until I am finished, and I will be glad to answer a few before returning to my home. Many of you heard on Primetime Central Radio during the brief segment recapping Second Chances and giving a heads up on Smash, that I had a challenge to issue to Taj Escobar and Maximum Velocity Wrestling. Someone from the cW offices has leaked information about the challenge and somehow word got to Escobar what I was going to issue. I was planning on issuing a challenge to the mVw to do a joint show on July 5, 2004, where both Malice and Smash would be combined and we would have a whole night of inter-promotional matches. Unfortunately, that information has been leaked before I could make the announcement at Smash tomorrow night. The challenge is accurate, but as of right now, no guarantees I will be willing to go forth with the idea considering recent developments. As you also all heard on Primetime Central Radio, a challenge was issued by mVw CEO Taj Escobar and new mVw hire and former Core Wrestling Continental Champion Phoenix Rose. Rose and Escobar challenge Gene Fitzpatrick and me upon hearing the leaked rumors of me challenging them to an inter-promotional event. I am here tonight to stand down from the challenge and say that I do not accept even though someone has already formally accepted for us. I have more important things to tend to for that night if the night even goes forth. As for mVw and their recent propaganda, I am saying right now, cW will have no further involvement in working up our own propaganda to dissuade viewers from watching mVw. I will not stoop myself down even further than what I have involving myself in these childish antics. No are there any questions? Q: Do you mind that you may be considered a coward for not accepting the challenge set forth by Escobar and Rose? A: I have no worries about being called whatever. It will not affect me. I will continue to work day in and day out working to make cW the best product available in the professional wrestling world. Q: What will you do if mVw continue their propaganda efforts against Core Wrestling? A: I will treat mVw like a child for their childish antics. Ignore it, and it will go away. Q: How does this affect the relationship between Core Wrestling and Primetime Central? A: I do not blame or hold PTC at fault for what has occurred on their radio show. I know Dave Hurst would have not placed anything on air that he knew was fictional. Had I been contacted before hand by whoever accepted the challenge on the air, I would have gotten this all straightened out beforehand. Thanks you for time. No more questions. End of conference. Doctor’s today saw FAW World Heavyweight Champion Apocalypse’s right leg today. Doctor’s Looked it over and have said that he has a slight tear in the right knee that might need surgery. The tear happened last night on Panic when he took on fellow FAW member Sniper for the FAW World Title. We caught up with Apocalypse today and ask him what he thought about the doctor report and if it would keep him away form in the ring? “Not a chance. I just got this title and I’m not letting it go. Hurt leg or not, broken neck or not, it doesn’t matter. I will not sit on the sidelines while all the action with 4WW happens. There will be a time when I let it heal but right now isn’t the time.” No comment’s have been made from the offices but more will come with this story. === 24 April 2004 [PRIME] ReVolution XXX Details Released Leaving behind a water drenched arena in Auburn, ReVolution heads to Little Rock in Arkansas for it's thirteeth edition. Yes, it's XXX but there's no Vin Diesel to be seen. Instead, the fans have a mouth watering Main Event consisting of five ingedients... Ignatius Lisieux, Tony Rolo, the Trail 2 Glory, the Global Title and a ladder! Having defeated the dreaded Global Title Curse, Lisieux now has to face his biggest rival in PRIME, but with the odds in his favor. English agreed... keen to get Motoki out of his office seeing as how he had just stolen the coffee maker from the co-owner's new suite. Indeed this Wednesday could turn out to be a great night for Motoki and his co-horts, especially since his group is gauranteed at least one representative in the T2G Final as Adam faces Black Angel. On paper the semi-final match seems great, but insiders are fearing a whitewash should one of the stablemates elect to lay down for the other. Meanwhile there's the small issue of a vacant Intense Title to address, plus Taun Pham is rumored to be hatching some sort of plan for End Game in order to restore the balance of power in PRIME. Plus your usual selection of PRIME talent including Gods Champion Hoyt Williams and a debut for "Sinister" Steven Lee.
