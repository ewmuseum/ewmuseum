Archibald MacGregor Biography Biography Name: Archibald MacGregor Height: 6' Weight: 223lbs Age: 30 Resides: Oxford, Pennsylvania Entrance Music: "Family System" by Chevelle Wrestling Style: Submission Finisher: "Garote" -- Triangle Choke Background: Everyone who watches Archibald Allen MacGregor wrestle in the squared circle, even his most hated opponents, can't deny the grasp of the sport that he has. Like liquid inside the ring, he manages to defend his opponents offense while drowning his opponent with a variety of his own offensive moves. If only his life outside of the squared circle came so easily, so simple and perfect. After the passing of his parents, most recently his mother 4 years ago, Archibald has drowned away his sorrows with alcohol. He would have stopped wrestling years ago, if it weren't for the mental anguish his father put him through. If it weren't for the name that appears on his birth certificate: Archibald. The drive to be great at something, to honor the fallen.
