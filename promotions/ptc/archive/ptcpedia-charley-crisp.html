Biography Charley Crisp is a self-admitted sexual deviant. It is common knowledge amongst most professional wrestling experts of his previous career that he starred, directed, and produced several adult films, mostly in the area of [BDSM] and fetish genres. Despite this odd character trait, Crisp is very well-received by wrestling fans due to his mannerisms of dancing during his entrances and comedic assaults on SCCW/PTC heels. Crisp is also known for his staple hairstyle, a large Afro which has been dubbed "The Fro That You Know" by many fans. Career Holy Cow Championship Wrestling A very small promotion holding bi-weekly shows at the [Cow Palace], Crisp's wrestling career started under the moniker of Mart Shan on May 15, 2007. Sin City Championship Wrestling Crisp made his in-ring debut April 11, 2008 on Temptation against Jacque Noreault, Jay Draven, and Charlotte Ramone in a fatal four-way match, winning by pinfall. This earned him the opportunity to compete in a double-elimination tournament to crown a new LiveWire Champion, where he was eventually eliminated by Bishop in the semi-finals. Jabs have been traded by Crisp and GCW star Chris Bagwell. After a promo shot by Crisp for SIN on SPIKE featuring a marionette parody of Bagwell, an attack orchestrated by him on Temptation prompted Charley to make various allegations of Bagwell being a closet [Zoophiliac]. Crisp believes that it was Bagwell who was behind the Great Pot Brownie Fiasco of 2008. Crisp took a brief hiatus after the All In pay-per-view, and returned to action on the 9/28/08 episode of Temptation, vowing to help put an end to the Dead Man's Hand. On 10/6/08's episode, he secured the LiveWire Championship after defeating ally Jared Sykes in a questionable manner due to the interference of Jadian Bridden. After striking up a common bond after their match, Sykes and Crisp began to cut comedic interviews and were eventually forged together to form the Kings of Pop, and the two successfully defeated Phillip Kennedy and Jaidan Bridden for the Strength In Numbers Championships on the 11/8/08 broadcast of Temptation. Personal Life Originally from the Los Angeles area, Charley Crisp now currently resides in Las Vegas. In information derived from backstage apparel and candid interviews, Crisp is a huge sports fan, notably of the [Los Angeles Clippers], the [Los Angeles Kings] and the [Los Angeles Dodgers]. A standout free safety in his high school sports career, Crisp "claims no allegiance" to any football team, but admits to attending a few [Trojans] games when he has the chance. Crisp is currently single and "ready to mingle" by his own accord. It is widely speculated that he is a frequent customer of various escort services around the nation.
