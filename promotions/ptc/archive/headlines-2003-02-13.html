PTC Headlines, February 2003

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The Headlines page of Primetime Central’s own site (ptc.wrestleonline.org) as the Wayback Machine captured it on 13 February 2003: the three items it carried, dated 10 February 2003 (the PTC v WrestleOnline Universal Title match signed), 3 February 2003 (the sale of Primetime Central to two long-time members, the move of its forums and its new address) and 25 December 2002 (the “No Limits” Battle Royale announced). The page’s navigation and site banner are not carried.
Primetime Central Takes On WrestleOnline.. 2/10/03 The match has been signed, and the time is now.. Syko Arsonist, Primetime Central's No Limits Rumble winner, will lace his boots up and seize his opportunity at WrestleOnline's Angelo Deville, for the PTC/WO Universal Title. This will be the first PTC defense for Angelo Deville, and it is a match both communities are looking forward to viewing. Roleplaying for the match has begun, and will continue until Sunday, February 16th on the Universal Title division forums. Primetime Central wishes Syko Arsonist the best of luck, and would like to thank WrestleOnline for allowing the match to happen. New Owners For PTC! 2/03/03 It's official: Primetime Central has been purchased. It will no longer be owned by WrestleOnline or by Ringcrew Entertainment, but will continue to be affiliated. The new owners? Terry Woods and Trigger, two long-time members of PTC. They are both committed to devoting their time and energy into making PTC better than ever. Over the next few weeks, many changes will be happening. If you are viewing this message, then you know that the PTC URL has been changed. Also, we have moved our community forums to http://www.lgm1.com/ptcforums. The rules will remain the same, and the structure of this great community will remain the same. In reality, the people running it will remain the same: you. We at PTC ask for your continued support throughout these upcoming changes, because without you, Primetime Central isn't primetime at all. We thank you greatly! PTC Announces "No Limits" Battle Royale Event 12/25/02 Opportunity knocks for all PTC federations and their grapplers to shine, in the first ever "No Limits" Battle Royale, which will take place on January 18th, 2003. The rules are simple. There is no limit to the number of competitors. There is no limit to the amount of material the wrestlers can put out before the match. There are NO LIMITS!!! PTC officials will be checking the workrates of those involved, and the feds who work hard will move up, and feds who slack, may lose their positions. NO FED is SAFE!! Additionally, the winning wrestler will be named the #1 Contender for the PTC Global Title! Signups are going on right now, but then end on the 3rd of January!
