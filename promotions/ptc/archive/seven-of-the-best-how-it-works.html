Seven of the Best: how it works

What this is (written in 2026) The Seven of the Best event site’s “how it works” page, as the donor’s database holds it (lot 0159). The page itself carries no date; it describes the nominations stage, so it stood in March 2005 when the site went up (the organiser’s 5 March 2005 post, served on this site, announces the site and the nomination window). No capture of the page is held. The page’s navigation and the markup of its headings are not reproduced; its text is.
Primetime Central brings to you Seven of the Best. The purpose of Seven of the Best is to provide a unique and exciting event which will showcase the PTC Global Title contendership process on a wider scale than the contendership matches that we are used to, and to provide an alternative to the "behind closed doors" selection process that nobody not in the inner sanctum seems to know anything about before the challengers are announced. How it works is each member federation has been given the opportunity to enter a single player of their choosing, whether it be through fed heads direct choice, contendership matches or whatever. That player is at random entered into a one-fall match against a player from one of the other PTC member feds, and those two will battle it out for a place in the final match. The final match will consist of an over-the-top battle royal, with the last entrant remaining in the ring being named the Number 1 Contendter for the PTC Global Title. The runner-up will receive a future Extreme Title shot. should they choose to accept it.
