LeStatt Knight Biography Biography LeStatt Knight has been through alot for a 26 year old man. He lost a wife and child when he was 18 and a brother to murder when he was 20. These events have forced him to grow up much faster than he originally intended to. He keeps many emotions locked up inside except anger and hatred. He has slowly began to appreciate what wrestling is, especially from being the GCW World Champion. It is starting to be fun to him, giving life a purpose once again. In the ring he uses his extraordinary strength and resiliency along with his martial arts background to form a quite unique in-ring style. Once in a match he had his arm ripped from its socket, but somehow popped it back into place to go on and still win the match. He has turned intoa dominate force in GCW this time around, holding the US title for 105 days and is well over 100 days with the World Title (and counting). The fans and many wrestlers back stage hate him, but he has made sure that they will respect him because that is what is most important to him. Other Feds GCW
