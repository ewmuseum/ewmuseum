Javier Mendez Biography Biography Age: 18 Location: New York City, New York (Alphabet City) Ethnicity: Columbian Height: 5’10 Weight: 178 lbs Gimmick: Modern Day Urban Existentialist to make it sound nice. Pretty much he doesn’t care about anything. All he does is live for the moment and what ever feels good. If you’ve ever read the French Novel "The Stranger", I kind of created the character after Mersault. What he has to do with wrestling: He’ll eventually get into it for money purposes. He’ll never hold a grudge, he won’t find any meaning in what he’s doing, and he’ll never seek any fame. He’ll just do it to get it done and nothing more. Past fed experience: PWA, PCW, PCW2, NTICW, EW, PURE, OSW, The Ring, and so many other experiments gone bad. Fighting Style: Mendez has a brutal fighting tactic. He lacks any real adrenaline or fighting emotion and will usually not even start the fight until he is first hit. Once hit though Javier will burst into rage. He holds many of his feelings inside usually and in the ring he lets everything out through dirty tactics and unimaginable moves of brutality. Mendez holds no grudges though and never really hates anybody because he doesn’t care enough to. Common Moves: There are no names for any of Javier’s moves because he is simply doesn’t care enough too. - He will headbutt his opponent right in the chest and then as the opponent looks downward he drives his head up at his opponents jaw in a quick snap action. - When Javier sees his opponent weak he’ll plant there head between his two knees and slowly force them together while hardly driving his fist into his opponents temple. - Javier will go into minute long bursts. During this he will take his opponents head and bash it against the mat over and over for his entire adrenaline rush. - Javier also uses a rake to the eyes commonly and will sometimes try and do it to a submission. - Javier will usually finish his opponents by trying to hang them. He’ll rip the top rope from all the turnbuckles and then use it as a rope where he’ll tie a noose. He then hangs the rope from the turnbuckle to the bottom mat and actually tries to hang his opponent into submission. Usually doesn’t work with much taller wrestlers. Short History: Javier was born in Alphabet City and has lived there to this very day. He’s never had any steady friends and has never cared much about anything. Other then a few fights and few encounters with drug dealers and prostitutes, nothing extraordinary has ever happened to him. He lives with his mother, sister, and uncle in a studio apartment. He works in a nearby Keyfood Supermarket where he usually quietly observes all the people he checks out. Javier deep down wants to live in the city and get away from the depressive environment he is now in, but he knows he never will.
