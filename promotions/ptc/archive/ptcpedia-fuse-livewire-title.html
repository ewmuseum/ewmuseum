FUSE Livewire Title

captured 7 Mar 2008

The Livewire Title is FUSE's tertiary title. The current champion is Jason Cruise, who won it by defeating El Diablo in a match at FUSE's PPV, War in Washington. * Fun Facts * No wrestler has ever held the Livewire Title twice. * John Covel is the only former Livewire Champion to go on and hold the Universal Title * Covel also holds the record for the longest Livewire Title reign at 84 days followed by Lane Stevens (76), and Tanaka (73). * Livewire Match Rules * Matches have a ten minute time limit. * There are three rope breaks allowed per wrestler, per match. So basically, no match will have more than 6 rope breaks. * DQ's are strickly enforced and the title CAN AND WILL change hangs on a DQ. If the champion is DQed, he loses his title. * If a competitor intentionally throws his opponent from the ring or leaves it on his own he will be disqualified. * Livewire Title History * Jason Cruise def.
