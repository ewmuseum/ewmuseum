28 June 2019 Last fed you were in, how long ago: The last thing I did in eW was eight years ago, towards the end of GCW. "The Itch" had hit me again, so I brought Rich Rollins back from the dead (he faked his death, of course) and came back as an admin. I had a blast helping GBJ run the fed for a while, but I hadn't ever run a fed before and got burned out. It led to some really creative and fun projects though, and was probably my favorite run in any efed I ever had. It also led to me making this for one of our last PPVs: Shockwave 2011 Intro Last RP match you had, who won: Triumph Frost and Jay Terror vs. Andy Murray and Rich Rollins, at Shockwave '11 (on THE MOON~!). It was a Last Man Standing match, and Terror and Frost kicked our ass. :LOL: Last title you held: The GCW Tag-Team Championship with LeStatt Knight (held posthumously after Rollins "died") Last person to send you a PM before PTC closed: Chris~! from UWF Last thing you posted about here before PTC closed: Ye Olde Service Announcement, if my posting history is to be believed lol For those who are no longer with us, did you have a match with Sean, if so, where abouts and who won it? This came as a huge shock to me today. I don't know how I missed the news, but I am heartbroken to hear about Sean's passing. We had a rivalry that lasted almost a decade, and it blossomed into one of the best friendships I ever made in this community. When I first started in the UWF after GTT3, Seymour Almasy was THE guy I wanted to impress. Sean was IT, he was the guy I knew I could do great things with, and over the course of many years we did exactly that. There's so much history between our characters, but my favorite match was our Mount Gagazete Deathmatch from NC-17 III A close runner-up was our match from the Infinite Gauntlet, when Emoticon beat Rich Rollins. It was the time he finally beat me. :) Rest in peace, Sean. You were taken way too goddamn soon. [detail withheld] ---- 1 July 2019 Last fed you were in, how long ago: As Lindz stated, I kinda came back to a rp fed called CWF during a recruiting drive. She asked real nice, and for whatever reason, I figured why the heck not. Before that, my last run was in GCW near the end of all things. Last RP match you had, who won: Won some match that actually had Al on the other side, oddly enough. It was some three way tag where the winning team would move on to a three way match for a mid level title shot at the next PPV, and this was last week. Before that? My last match in GCW, against Ryuji Kamigawa (Sean) in the quarters of the Rampage 2011 tournament. Last title you held: The SCCW Gateway Title, I think? That sounds about right. Last person to send you a PM before PTC closed: Jay Phoenix, who sent a bunch of people a pm about a creative writing construct he was housing with Lee Best, I believe. It was a bunch of handlers who wanted to write but sought out the competitive structure within eW. It lasted for a round or two, had Josh and Nate do some podcasts, and it was interesting. Last thing you posted about here before PTC closed: Apparently this: http://community.primetimecentral.net/index.php?threads/sccw-national-organization-of-women-on-the-richmond-incident-and-protests-in-memphis.45891/ But....I don't think so. Had to be something during the end of PTC and all that. For those who are no longer with us, did you have a match with Sean, if so, where abouts and who won it? Sean and I knew each other from around the turn of the century in the old IWO days, which was before I think PCW was a glint in anyone's eyes, at least when [name withheld] (sp?) was getting IWO banner ads on Scoopswrestling, which was part of IGN (utterly ridiculous to think what could get money thrown at it during the height of the dot com bubble), and he rose to prominence there before I did. I think, by the time the IWO run was over, we'd gone our separate paths, with Sean going to PCW/PTC, and me going to the angle feds and the EWPlanet equation. Sean became quite the legend, and given general competitive fire, I remember myself and other contemporaries just laughing at this notion because "we were better than Sean was", whatever the hell that was worth. We occupied different spaces, and to be honest, I give Sean credit for doing what he did, because he got to where he did because of his own hard work and talent. After growing tired of not having direct control over my position on cards and in the angle community, I made the jump to PTC and PRIME via recruitment from Mat. Sean, I don't remember where he was exactly at the time, but he was all over the interfed, and was totally a damn king maker and titan. Our paths began crossing quite a bit after that, in matches as well as in real life. That makes things between the two of us interesting, but unlike Andrew up above, I can't exactly say it was all for the best. I beat him every time we ever faced off except one time, during some interfed match up, where Pete took a massive dump on both of our rps, of which I don't think either of us took too kindly, but hey, oh well. At that point, the way Sean was remembered was as a precursor to the super talents of Jeff, Mat, and others, and was a sort of relic that didn't have the writerly capacity to compete at the highest level. In truth, Sean was strident in his idealogy of what he would write and what the hobby should be, and that clashed with the shifting tides of what was considered to be winning material in our competitive environment. That being said, even in saying that, Sean was to prominence in a lot of places, had substantial runs, and was well liked by 'generations' of talents in the community. He made a mark as Seymour Almasy, did a fantastic job in SCCW as Phillip Kennedy, he was always plugging away and doing well. Hell, me and him were going to be involved in a hellacious angle feud in Jolt that fizzled out because I had one of my many burnouts. I took him for granted, and I took for granted the challenge he would present. My last PTC match was with him, and it isn't a happy memory. We faced off in the GCW Rampage Tournament, and mentally, I was fried to an absolute crisp. Sean worked his butt off on his piece with all intents to smash me. Me? I wrote something that was a little more sedated than my usual work, all in a relatively short time, and posted. I gave subpar effort. I still managed to beat him. That...did not sit well with him. He threw a fit behind the scenes, as did Joe, and what they said got back to me. That I was given a pass through bias. It made me quit. In fact, for the most part, I haven't really taken back to writing like I had since that day. If I am not mistaken, a few years later, Sean apologized to me, because he knew I was really raw about the whole thing. But that is a lifetime ago. I got the news of his passing a year ago, driving from Lansing Michigan to Philadelphia in one shot, avoiding the toll roads, reading a message Katie posted about it when I was taking a break to grab a smoke. I'd been friends with him on Facebook, and knew he was doing good things in his life, and it just shocked the hell out of me. Like, I was sitting on a bench, shaking, just not understanding what all could have happened. I still don't know, and that's fine, as his family and wife deserve their privacy in this matter. But Sean Williams was living his life and things were going so well, and for it to just end like it did, I would have never expected he would have left us so soon. At the end of the day, he was just a good dude and was instrumental in the foundation of this place. I'm glad for the competition we had, the conversations, and all that came with it. Hopefully he's enjoying wrestling and rpgs from the best seat in the house. You're missed, dude. Last thing you thought about relating to ew before the "lets put the forums post back online" post went up on Facebook: Probably "shit, I have to get an rp up and only have an hour". Beyond that, I have a lot of thoughts about the folks and contemporaries here. Given that we have a forum again, maybe I'll post about it, because it's been stuff on my mind for years and years. Handlers you miss hanging out with: I mean, I have a bunch on my FB feed. I'm a super hard person to pin down and talk to. Is it a cop out if I say I miss most everyone? I know we are all in different places in our lives, but there's a big part of me that wishes the person I am now would have been the guy in PTC and the like all those years ago. I really lost contact with [name withheld], and I wonder what he's up to, how he's doing. Handlers you still talk to: Lindz definitely. Katie messages me from time to time. I texted Pete a few months ago thinking he was another Pete I knew, and never responded to his text once I realized I'd messed up! [name withheld] messages me from time to time but I'm shitty at responding to him. Ryan has messaged me from time to time as well. Ever meet any handlers? Al and John. Something significant that happened in your life in the last 8 years that made you happy: Eight years? Well Jesus. That's a lifetime ago. And many lifetimes ago as well. I'm just enjoying life now.
