1 February 2003 *XWF* Special Stipulation Match At next Sunday's XWF PPV Penalty we will see Chezina face off against Rage in a Special Stipulation Match. There are alot of options to which type of match will be selected for this big match. But one think is for sure and that is that knowing the XWF they will come up big at the PPV. === 2 February 2003 *XWF* Saturday Night Brawl This past Saturday Night Brawl was held in Daytona Beach, Florida and it was another packed arena. The opening match was a Lion's Den Match between El Locon and Canadian Crippler. For a big guy El Locon showed good submission abilities but at the end the submission expert Canadian Crippler nailed El Locon with the Crippler Kick followed by the Crippler Tamer and made El Locon tap out to win the match. Then at the backstage area we saw Zero Lionheart been approach by a kid that asks for his autograph. Lionheart gives it to him but gets hit by a 2x4 by the Xtreme Champion C.R. The second match was between Xtreme Champion C.R. and newcomer Rage in a Non-Title Match. This 2 really gave the people a great wrestling match but at the end C.R. went for a punch but Rage pushed him into the corner and when C.R. turned around he got a kick to the mid section followed by The Sacrifice and Rage scores the upset win. Finally, it was time for the big main event in which Chezina was facing his brother and XWF Champion Chivo and Big Dave Kaos in a Triple Threath Match. At one point The Rodriguez Brothers were going at it while Kaos was outside the ring. Kaos started to get up but the lights went out and when they came back he was out and we saw Canadian Crippler running through the crowd. Chezina then took care of his brother inside and went outside grabbed Kaos into the apron and nailed him with a Cobra Clutch Slam on top of 2 tables that were stacked earlier in the match. As soon as he entered the ring Chezina was speared by Chivo who instead of going for the Chivo Stunner he decided to put his brother on the top rope and he went for a Chivo Stunner from the top rope but Chezina nailed him twice on the gut turned him around and nailed The Chezina Bottom from the top rope to win the match. Then at the back Big Shot was talking to Shane one of the XWF interviewers and suddenly he was attacked by the US Champion DJ with a trash can. Then through him into the door. DJ placed the trash can in Big Shot's face, then went to the end of the hallway and started to run after Big Shot and nailed him with a dropkick to the trashcan on the face of Big Shot. DJ then took a casket and placed Big Shot in it and tried to ignite the casket but luckily R-Mac saved the day. DJ and R-Mac started to brawl as the show faded out. === 2 February 2003 _-_NGW_-_ New CEO?
