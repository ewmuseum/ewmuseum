Need a match/theme people

24 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OK, some of you may be aware that I run something called the Chris Williams Memorial Cup each year. This is an efed tournament in memory of the HANDLER and not the character, who was killed in a car crash. The first three years were both straight up RP contests (3 max) and the tournament took the form of a single elimination bout similar to King of the Ring. The fourth year was different in that it was held between four feds, each of which had a fatal four way to decide which of their wrestlers went to the final match, another fatal fourway. Last years was a triple match threat ... rumble match to winnow the entrants down to eight, then a guantlet match to winnow it down to the last two (which ended up being three LoL) and finally a cage match to crown the winner. Each year there have also been fed and ifed title/grudge matches. This years CWMC is running late, but it IS going to happen ... real life and health stuff have calmed down enough that I can concentrate on getting things organised again. Only problem is that I am completely blank in terms of what to actually do for the tournament part of the card this year. So, feel free to leave some ideas for me (please be aware that if your idea is for a match that you DON'T want someone else to use then don't leave it here! LoL) and you will even get credited on card for it if it is used :)
