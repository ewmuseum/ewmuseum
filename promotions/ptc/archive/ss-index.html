UWF vs UWF Kenny Mercury vs Chris Storm Special Guest Referee - Argyle Global vs Global Master of the Mat Rematch Jake Hix vs. Dawn McFarlane HSW vs HSW HSW World Champion Deszaras vs. HSW Evolution Champion Jonny Kae It all started on Shocker before Live From Death’s Door. Forced to team in a tag match, Jonny Kae and Deszaras faced off against David Dace and Switchblade in early May. Jonny Kae took advantage of Deszaras’ finisher, picking up the three count on Dace. Not surprisingly, Deszaras took exception to this, and there has since never been any love loss between the World Champion and the relative newcomer. Onto Atrocity, and Jonny Kae defeats Siren in a one on one matchup for the Evolution Title. Siren had come out in support of Deszaras, looking to take matters into her own hands for the world champion. However, coming up just short, Siren’s attempted defence of the man she looked up to did not end as planned. Since Atrocity, the relations of HSW’s World and Evolution Champions has continued its descent. Jonny Kae hit a nerve with the world champion in an interview after the July Pay Per View, leading to an assault by the world champion, getting his point across. These little altercations continued right through to the late August Pay Per View, Global Domination. Deszaras was taken down with a shot from a Baseball bat with the initials MK. At the Pay Per View, it turned out that this bat belonged to none other than Jonny Kae’s brother, and former tag team partner, Michael Kae. Deszaras got his revenge at the Pay Per View, taking Michael Kae out of the Evolution Title match, and taking out Jonny Kae in the process. Now, on one of the biggest stages in the wrestling world, these two ego-driven champions face off for the first time in singles contest. Both have something to lose (Jonny Kae’s unbeaten record; Deszaras his vengeance) and both look to settle a score that, some may argue, has gone on too long unanswered.
