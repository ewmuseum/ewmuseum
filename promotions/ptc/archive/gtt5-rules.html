GTT5 rules

2005

Rules What: PTC's GTT5 is a singles elimination roleplaying tournament. When: The tournament is expected to run November 2005 through to late January 2006. Who: GTT5 is an open invitation tournament. That means anyone can enter, not just members of PTC feds. The tournament director is Argyle, PTC's owner. How: Registered entrants compete in the tournament by roleplaying against their nominated opponent. The roleplays are judged by peers of both entrants, and the entrant deemed to have posted superior work moves on to the next round. Why: GTT5 is provided by PTC for e-fedding enthusiasts to have fun competing against each other in matches they would not otherwise experience. An offshoot of that is for the possiblity of member feds being able to sign "Free Agent" players. An entrant is classed as a Free Agent if they are not a member of a PTC member fed. That being said, there is no expectation on any of the Free Agent entrants to sign with a PTC fed after playing in GTT5. If you like what you see in any of the PTC member feds, feel free to negotiate and join. If not and you are just here for the tournament, enjoy yourself on us. Get to the good part, what are the prizes for the winner? There are a number of prizes for the winner. 1. The winner is invited to nominate a charity of their choice, upon which long time two PTC members - Buddy D. and Rob W. - will each donate the sum of $US100.00 (one hundred dollars) to that charity in the name of the winner. PTC management would like to thank both gentlemen for this extremely kind gesture. This is Buddy's second time doing this, he also did it for GTT4. It is Rob's first time. 2. The highest placed member of a PTC Federation will receive a future shot at the PTC Global Championship. 3. The second highest placed member of a PTC Federation will receive a future shot at the PTC Extreme Championship. 4. The winner will receive a personal website for their wrestler, with free hosting and one year's registration of a domain name of his/her choice. 5. In addition to the personal website, the winner is also able to claim an additional hosting package from rpcommunity.com for another site, courtesy of Jay Phoenix. Should the winners of the Global and Extreme title shots decline to take them, they will be passed down to the next highest finishing player. Other prizes may be added at the discretion of the tournament director. Cool... so how many people are going to be in this thing? GTT5 is limited to 128 entrants. Damn, that's a lot of people... so how much work am I going to have to do each round? What's the RP Limit? That will vary each round. The first round will have a 1RP limit. The main reason for this is to weed out the people who aren't serious about playing, without overly taxing the people who are serious about playing. Subsequent rounds will see increases in the RP limit, at the discretion of the tournament director. It is envisaged that Round 2 will be a 2 RP limit, while the final round will be a 4 or 5 RP limit, with the rounds in between then in between that somewhere. Did you just say it could be 5 RP's in the final round? Why so many? Why not three like usual? A RP limit is a maximum, not a minimum. It is expected that come time for the final round, the players may well be quite spent of ideas. The extra is, just in case they aren't, to give them all the latitude in the world to be able to prove who is THE BEST in the game. That and that we don't want any old greasemonkey deciding where Buddy's hundred bucks goes to. How long is each Roleplay period? Round 1 will be five days, after the five day hype period (as described on the GTT5 discussion forum). Any rounds with a 3RP limit or greater will be seven days. It is expected that two weekend days will be included in a full roleplay period. So, what are the RULES? One Character per Handler: Handling two characters in this tournament takes away a spot from someone else. Anyone found handling multiple characters will have each character expelled from the tournament. Plagiarism: Anyone found to be stealing the work of others and posting it in this event will be banned from participating in any future PTC events, for life. No Recycling: All Roleplays posted must be original for this event. This means that they are not to have already been used in your home fed. They may be archived in your home fed after you have finished participating in the tournament, not before. They are not to be used for competative purposes in your home fed, period. If you are found doing this, you will be barred from future PTC events. Quoting: If you use snippets of other people's work, or quote song lyrics, or anything else that you didn't personally write, you MUST quote your reference. Linking: All Roleplays must be posted on the GTT5 website using a username and password provided by PTC Management. Roleplays linked and hosted elsewhere will not be considered during judging. Deadline Extensions: Are extremely unlikely to be granted. Time zone differences will not be accepted as an excuse. There will be countdown clocks posted on this website to determine the precise ending of the roleplay period relative to where the player is in the world. Login troubles will not be accepted as an excuse. It is the responsibility of the player to test the login and password combination given to them at the earliest possible time, even if they don't intend to roleplay at that point. The short of it is that the Tournament Director will be extremely unwilling to hold up the entire tournament for the benefit of one person, even if they are in fact being shafted by this not being done. Think Star Trek II - The needs of the many outweigh the needs of the few. What's the catch? I'm glad you asked. The tournament director reserves the right to shuffle any or all brackets at any time during the tournament. Holy shit! I really don't like change. I want things to be nice and predictable. I want to know who I'm facing 8 rounds in advance, and I want to research them all right now. Don't do this to me! Did you ever watch a couple of series' of Big Brother? Notice how they introduce new things or change their routine each time to keep things interesting? Well, if some of these things are introduced during the course of the tournament, think of it like that. In other words, don't be a wuss, and don't overlook your opponents and book yourself into the finals before roleplaying even starts. So, how come Free Agents can't take the Global or Extreme Title shots? These titles are reserved for members of PTC feds. They are a way for contenders to get their work read by others outside of their home fed. Also, PTC is an interfed, set up for the benefit of its member feds, so it would be a bit silly if a Free Agent held any of its titles. But I used to be in a PTC fed, I'm just not in one now. I've posted on the PTC forums for YEARS! Surely that must count for something, right? Surely you can make an exception for me? No. If you're not in a PTC fed at the time of entering the tournament, then you can't have the title shots. You're also not permitted to claim that part of the tournament prize pool if you happen to get signed to one half way through the tournament. You'll have to be content with bragging rights and the other prizes. Okay. Hey, I have to ask, what's with the names for each bracket? Its a joke, son. Oh, one last question, what's with the extra questions on the application form? You mean the no-show thing? Well, if there are more applicants than there are spaces, PTC management needs to determine the best way to work out which applications to reject. People with a history of no-showing PTC events may find themselves first in line to be rejected.
