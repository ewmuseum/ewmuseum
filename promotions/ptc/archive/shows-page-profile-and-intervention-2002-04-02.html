PTC Shows Page, April 2002: a Federation Profile and the PTC Intervention

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Two parts of the Shows page of Primetime Central’s own site (shows.shtml) as the Wayback Machine captured it, in the page’s order: the federation profile that opens the page, headed NGW on the page, whose pay-per-views and whose history beneath it (it begins “Big Money Wrestling officially opened its doors”) are those of Big Money Wrestling, and the PTC Intervention sign-up of 2 April 2002 (the card of matches for the interfederation pay-per-view of 14 April). The page’s other parts (the profiles of LCW Mercury, FAW and LCW Evo, the rules and the FAQ) are carried by pages of this wing already and are not repeated. One messenger name the sign-up prints is marked as withheld. The capture’s own date is not recorded; the document date is the sign-up’s.
NGW Open Since: September, 2001 Member of PTC Since: January, 2002 Fed Style: Roleplay Roster Size: 29 Weekly Shows: Tuesday SellOut, Friday FallOut PPV Names: December 16-Corrupt Agenda January 27-!Trial and Error! March 31-Blood, Sweat n' Tears May 26-ReVival 2002 July 28-Money, Power, Respect Current Champions: World Champion-Reaper Million Dollar Champion-ICE Cash Money Champion-Jeremiah Wilson Blood Money Champion-Donomite Top Dollar Champion-Joker Tag Team Champions-Mark Espinosa/JerseySyko Recent Signings: Eric Shawn, Juggernaut, JerseySyko, "Maximum Perfection" Leo Bravardo, Jiru Numato, Brad Douglas, Abel History Big Money Wrestling officially opened its doors in the early summer of 2001. While not the greatest fed around Big Money Wrestling did have a very solid and active roster. All was going well and Big Money Wrestling well on it's way to being at the same level it is now, but due to unfortunate circumstances. Tycoon had to close it down for a few monthes. Big Money Wrestling re-opened its doors in September of 2001, it started off under the ownership of Tycoon and Don. Soon Tycoon was able to get many of his old wrestlers from the first Big Money Wrestling to come back. Soon their was a roster large enough to begin operations. Tycoon one day contacted Louis Miranda and asked him about his days in wrestling, Louis had been out for about 2 months. Tycoon was able to talk Louis into coming back to wrestling and head his creative team. Louis agreed and soon the team formed a co-ownership amongst Don, Tycoon and Louis. The shows went on as normal with everything going good, soon Louis grew tired of the work load falling on him and decided that it would be best to move on. Louis left for three weeks. He kept in contact with Tycoon and Donomite. Tycoon then took a hiatus and left Big Money Wrestling out on a limb. Donomite stayed around while Tycoon took time off and Donomite gathered help to keep Big Money afloat. After seeing the support that Big Money was recieving. Tycoon decided to resurface and become a part of Big Money Wrestling as he did not want to see Big Money Wrestling go down like that. Tycoon and Donomite then begin working sick hours to bring Big Money Wrestling to a respectable level. They slowly started to build up Big Money Wrestling with help from quality staff members. A new design was unvieled and people were slowly becoming interested in Big Money Wrestling again. Tycoon and Donomite realized that they were lacking in the creative department, so Tycoon approached Louis Miranda to head there Main Event scene. Louis finally decided that he really wanted to be part of the growing Big Money Wrestling and came back in late November. A PPV had been planned and hyped called Corrupt Agenda. The show went off great under Louis Miranda and the creative team. Scott Phillips became the first ever World Champion. After winning the title Scott Philiips began to slag off and not work at his best and was soon dethroned as World Champion on December 25 by Ragio Versace. Ragio retained this belt for almost two weeks finally being fired on national television by Louis Miranda. The title was vacated and Risk met an up and coming Jeremiah Wilson at !Trial and Error! to find out who would become the next World Champion. Axel would surprise the world that night and would help Risk capture the title. Risk held the belt for three weeks. He was found doing drugs and stealing from other wrestlers in other promotions so he was fired on national television. The World Title was once again vacated. On March 31st, 2002 Reaper defeated Donomite to become the fouth Big Money Wrestling Champion.
