James von Doom

captured 2 Oct 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Von Doom has had a long career in wrestling and has competed in various organizations. Most fans and wrestlers consider him to be somewhat crazy or eccentric. He has recently developed a bizarre strategy of making himself appear weak to his opponents even though he is quite a cunning fighter and match strategist. He will feign weakness, throw matches to make his rivals think he is weak and not a threat to them, he will lie and make bogus alliances with people, and often lures his opponents and allies into a false sense of security while simultaneously convincing them to turn on their allies and close friends. He makes complex webs of false alliances while only having a true alliance to a small people. He was the first in wrestling to intentionally lose matches and was seen as weak by his fellow wrestlers. He also bizarrely seeks to be hated by the other wrestlers, bookers, and other wrestling personalitis. He uses lies and deception after admitting to his fellow wrestlers that he would do so, lies about conversations and alliances and even lies about past experiences in his life. His straight talking attitude toward other wrestlers has made him a security risk as well. Regardless, James is considered one of the most devious wrestlers in history. Career Overall Record: Singles: 70-54-4 Tag Team: 22-12-0 PPV: 21-14-1 Main Event: 14-10-0 Cluster: 10-17 Total: 102-83-5 Career Championship History: TEAM Free For All Championship, TEAM Lethal Lottery Tag Team Championship (with Otaku), GLOBAL Platinum Championship x3 (2 as James von Doom and 1 as Darth von Doom), LOW Realm Championship (as Deimos), UWF Insert-Name-Here Championship, UWF North American Championship, UWF Television Championship, UWF Tag Team Championship (with Killer Dude), UWF Jobber Champion, GHW Undisputed Championship, GHW Television Championship, GHW Hardcore Championship, GHW International Championship, GHW World Tag Team Championship (with Jon Collingwood), FEW World Championship x2, FEW Tag Team Championship x2 (with Misty Mountains and Fast Eddie), FEW North American Championship, IWA World Championship, WAPW Global Championship, WAPW Weapons Championship, XFnW Hardcore Championship x2, XFnW World Tag Team Championship (with Jon Collingwood), USWF2k4 World Championship, USWF 2k4 Crossova Championship, USWF2k4 Patriotic Championship, USWF 2k4 International Champion, KEF Intercontinental Championship, XFW World Championship, XFW Suicide Championship, WWE Intercontinental Championship, HWA Cruiserweight Championship, and CCW Prime Time Championship - Total: 37 Von Doom explains his 'bizarre' new strategy: "However, you do bring up a good point. I think everyone hates me. Well, that's not what I want. I want everyone to hate me. That's part of my strategy. What happens to the guy who everybody hates and nobody thinks can win? Are you going to be more worried about him or more worried about someone like yourself? Think about it... *grins deviously*" Von Doom explaining his new mindset going into GTT: "I'm just the instigating villain that everybody hates. That's all I am. I think that I should be the forefront of people's thoughts of all times and I feel that I can be at the forefront of their thoughts better as a bad person than as a good person. To put it in this perspective. Can anyone take pleasure in watching a group of people tear each other apart verbally, psychologically, and emotionally? Well I can and I've been doing it every day since this started up."
