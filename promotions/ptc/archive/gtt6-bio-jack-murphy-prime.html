Jack Murphy Biography Biography Jack Murphy is a man who has lived and toiled for this industry for the past twenty years. Time has not been kind to his body and to say he's a run down is a slight understatement. In the preceeding 4-5 months, AWC has seen a gradual degeneration of the body and mind of a man who has put too much into a career that is ready to break him. This could be his final event, this could be final straw, but for Jack, there is no other way that he can conceivably continue. To reject such an opportunity doesn't even come into his mind. It's not like he doesn't have anything else in life that is waiting for him. He has a loving girlfriend, a budding gym which he hopes to turn into a wrestling and a generous account of money which could let him live comfortably for a couple of years while he got on track with another life. This is wrestling though, and it has always been the only thing that has ever mattered to Jack. There is no other option. This is what will be his past, present and future. Other Feds AWC
