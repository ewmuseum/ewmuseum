Pay-Per-Views PRIME runs five Pay-Per-Views each year: Culture Shock in March, UltraViolence in May, Colossus in August, Great American Nightmare in October and King of Kings in December. Culture Shock Main Article: Culture Shock Held in March, features the Dual Halo for the Golden Ticket, and the Jewel in the Crown winner getting his title shot. UltraViolence Main Article: UltraViolence UltraViolence is the second Pay-Per-View of the calendar year, usually held in the month of May. It features a hardcore atmosphere with a lot of matches contested under No Disqualification rules or some sort of other violent stipulation.
