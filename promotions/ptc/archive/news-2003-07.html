9 July 2003 [Plague Fighting] Friday Night Frenzy Friday Night Frenzy - 7/11/03 ----------- On Saturday, July 5th, Plague Fighting announced that an edition of Friday Night Frenzy would indeed take place on 7/11/03. This will be Plague Fighting's third event, but it will be their first event coming out of the downtime which they experienced. They've recruited many new fighters, whom they thought would be capable of heating things up... ...They were right. In only a few days, fighters have exchanged some not-so friendly words to say the least. The card is as follows... Skull vs. Fire This match is new recruit vs. new recruit. There is a lot riding on this match, as attention has primarilly been focused on the new recruits as of late. Shaun Francis vs. Twiztid Canadian Championship Another example of a match where new recruits will go against one another. This match is for the Canadian Championship. Both Shaun "minority" Francis, and Twiztid have been contributing to the heat which has built up around Plague Fighting in recent days. Shaun "minority" Francis, has also exchanged some not-so friendly words with heavyweight/world heavyweight contender, Vinn. Star Angelique vs. Impact Tornado Championship Yet another new recruit vs. new recruit match. Both of these fighters have been very quiet as of late, but their skills will speak for them this Friday as they take on one another in this Tornado Championship match. Marshall "The Sadistic Pineapple" Edgars vs. Kerosene Confederate Championship This is perhaps one of the most focused on, and anticipated matches of them all. As Marshall Edgars, the man disguised as a pineapple, will take on redneck Kerosene for the Confederate Championship. Kerosene has made a mockery of Marshall "The Sadistic Pineapple Edgars" in recent days. Marshall Edgars has dealt with his own "problems", thus he has not responded to Kerosene's remarks, but come this Friday, jokes aside, these two will battle it out for the Confederate Championship. Many have underestimated the toughness, and fighting abillity of Marshall Edgars, this Friday is his chance to prove that he can indeed fight. Vinn vs. Distance Plague Championship Vinn, has made a large impression in Plague Fighting thus far, expressing his biggotted views towards Minority, and his opponent in this match Distance. this match is for the most prestigeous item in Plague Fighting, the Plague Championship. These two men have perhaps created the most heat in Plague Fighting thus far, but this Friday it all boils over when these two men meet face to face. ---------------- Gabe Carter has also assured fans, that this Friday will be an exciting and memorable card. Coming from Gabe, it was apparent that he has something up his sleeve. We'll have to wait and see... ------------ Source: Plague Fighting === 10 July 2003 [LOW] Rumored Return In an amazing turn of events today at Evangelica Dominitas Hospital, former Xtreme Champion "The Vigilante" Luke Justice has come out of a 4 month coma with little possibility of mental deterioration. Justice went into this coma after dropping his Harley Davidson motorcycle outside of Chicago, IL. He was rushed to Edwards Hospital in a Chicago suburb where it was discovered that he would be paralyzed from the waist down due to nerve and spinal damage. After transfer to Evangelica, he slipped into the coma that put him out of the spotlight indefinitely. He is scheduled for rehabilitation starting next week with some of the top therapists in the world. Luke's stellar carreer has been dotted by numerous title reigns in several wrestling companies around the world. His most recent reign was as the LOW Xtreme Champion while he was with the now defunct Shadow Riders. Our thoughts and prayers are with him and his family during these trying times in what will certainly be a slow and difficult transition. === 11 July 2003 [Plague Fighting] Frenzy 4 (7/18/03) Date scheduled: 7/18/03 Venue: Pepsi Arena, Albany, New York The Northeastern tour continues as we visit the Pepsi Arena in Albany New York. Brandi Sights Reporting Source: Global Wrestling === 26 July 2003 TS: Koch Leaves? "Koch Leaves TS?" As reported by Eric Van Eden Leaving on bad terms is even a light way to put this. The Ex-Chairman of Twilight Slams and now the Leader of the Creative Department Rob K has just flipped the territorial bird on new owners Matt Johnson and Juan Martinez. The arguments over the past couple of days have pushed Koch away completely, and quite frankly, he’s not happy about it. In an exclusive interview with me, here were his comments: “TS can go fuck themselves. I put my heart and soul into this organization, and when I stepped down as Chairman, I thought it was for the better. But now I realize that all I’ve done is handed it to a pair of immature independent in idealism twats. We can’t agree on anything, they don’t listen to my constructive criticism, and they’ve basically led me to revolt against step. It’s a sad day in our history that I have to leave something that I’ve worked so much on, and I blame myself for letting the company slip through my hands. I’ve led it to die now, but ask me if I give a shit.” What’s the future of the relations between TS and Rob K? Will Rob K invent a new and even better federation in the near future with the help of David “DJ” Hedley? And will Matt Johnson and Juan Martinez face the music before their hottest commodities leave the lackeys? Stay tuned for more information as it develops! === 26 July 2003 [GW] Shawn Fox in GW?
