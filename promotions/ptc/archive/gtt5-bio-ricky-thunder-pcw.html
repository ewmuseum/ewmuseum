Ricky Thunder Biography Biography Ricky was born into a wrestling family…kind of. Well not really, it was just his brother before him, and he didnt start wrestling until after Ricky was born. But they were friends with a guy who was born into a wrestling family…Kind of. Ricky's older brother paved the way for the Thunder name in CCW, where Ricky got his start. Ricky was 10 when his brother was taken from him by cancer. On the same fateful night, his parents were also taken from him in a car accident. Ricky was "adopted" and raised by the Aitkins family, who took him in as one of their own. Sean, was the family friend who took Ricky under his wing, and taught him the ropes of wrestling. Ricky learned much from Sean. Sean was a CCW Hardcore, Tag Team, Trans-Atlantic, Independant, and Rising Star champion. He was also the firs to have a PPV named for him. Ricky was in good care. After many years of practice and training Ricky, joined the ranks of CCW, where he won the Tag Team titles with The Supreme Machine (Sean) as well as the Independant, and Cruiserweight titles. SuMa soon disappeared. No word as to where he went, but Ricky decided it was time to move on in his own life as well. Ricky left CCW to join PCW. In his debut match he was a cluster brawl, where he beat out 29 others, and lost only to Miyagawa. Ricky hearing rumours backstage that he was "screwed" for being new…took exception and left PCW shortly after. Ricky decided his wrestling days were over. One day Ricky is contacted by a friend he made while in CCW, in one Michael Stevens. Stevens relays the word about the wrestling federation of GCW. Ricky declines, stating his wrestling days are over. After thinking it through for a few more days, Ricky decides he is going to give it another run. Ricky is ready to prove to the world he is just as good as he ever was, if not better. Other Feds GCW
