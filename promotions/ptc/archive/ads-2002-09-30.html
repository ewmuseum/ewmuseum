Source: Dominant Wrestling Online Animosity Wrestling needs you to fill in our Board of Directors 9/21/02 The Board of Directors has been installed to ensure an honest, and fair management of AW. This is an advanced staff organization. All business shall be brought before the board weekly, and voted on. Majority will rule. Listed below is a complete list of all positions available on the Board of Directors. Chairman of the Board This is the most important role of all. The chairman acts as President, the founder, the owner, and the leader of the board, and oversees all operations. In the case of an emergency, the chairman reserves the power to pass a motion without majority vote. This power may only be used in times of an emergency. The Chairman is in charge of making sure each and every Director of their board is doing their job. Also, any OOC out of AW, such as interfeds, PTC, ect., will all be handle by The Chairman and the board of Operations. The Chairman will select a team of trustworthy executives to help make decisions on emergency acts or tour promotions. The Chairman has the final word or any decision or problem involved with AW. Director of Operations The Director of Operations acts as Vice-President, but cannot pass motions without majority approval. In the case that the chairman leaves unexpectedly, the Director of Operations will assume control until the board can vote on a permanent replacement. While the Chariman works on other promotions, The Operations take over the federation and makes sure everything is running correctly. When the chairman returns, the operations may work on other promotions or help out in areas that need helping on the board. There shall be several of these in the board. They will all share the name Co-Director of Operations. They are one team together. Most of the members of this team will be people who helped build AW. Tthese people will be the ones that gets AW handed to them if the Chairman should resign. If somehow, help is needed in the Operations, then the staff team of The Chairman will help out. Director of Television A commonly-used term for this position would be Commisioner. The Director of Television is in charge of booking the cards, scheduling events, writing results, grading roleplays, checking over the forums to make sure no rules are being broken, and controling the In character staff. Members of the News Section should help when writing segments in a show, but it is not required. The team should also work together and create storylines for the shows. This is the most busy section of all, so he will select a big team to work under him, such as bookers, writers, etc. His team must be approved by the board. Director of Graphics Graphics is a broad division, including website design, graphics, PPV images, Poser, etc. Therefore, this division is allowed two representatives on the board. These board members will have equal say. They will select a team to work under them, such as Poser artists, website designers, etc. Their team must be approved by the board. Director of News Being heard in the e-fedding world is a vital part of any successful federation. The Director of News will be in charge of promoting AW through PTC News, as well as any other sites the board deems necessary. He will also be in charge of writing columns, show previews, show reviews, rumors, and news segments for the AW site. Also, they can write segments in a show for AW. That is not required. He will select a team to work under him, such as segment writers, column writers, etc. His team must be approved by the board. Director of Talent If there is one thing that kills a federation, it is the steady flow of talent...or lack thereof. The Director of Talent will be in charge of recruiting new wrestlers and staff members from every place imagineable. Any new recruits that he has deemed worthy to hire will come before the board, and be voted on. Also, he is to help other AW superstars become the best that they can be by working with them to meet the AW's expectations. He should have great judgement to hire someone into AW. He will also make reccomendations to release a certain superstar if the talent isn't where it needs to be. He will select a team to work under him, such as talent scouts, etc. His team must be approved by the board. Director of Finances AW's money system can be complicated at times, and requires constant tweeking. The Director of Finances will be in charge of dealing out money, setting contracts, adding new benefits, etc. He is mainly here to keep up with important buissiness information and records such as Archives, best roleplays, ect. for AW. Anything important that AW has, The Finances will have a copy in case something should happen to the original. He will select a team to work under him. His team must be approved by the board.
