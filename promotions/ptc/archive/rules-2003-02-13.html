PTC Rules, February 2003 edition

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The Rules page of Primetime Central’s own site (ptc.wrestleonline.org) as the Wayback Machine captured it on 13 February 2003, the edition that sets out the four tiers of federation (Affiliate, Public Access, Cable and Network). The page’s side navigation and site banner are not carried.
The rules of PTC are simple. Joining PTC Please answer the following statements with true or false. My fed is open. My fed is not involved in other interfeds. I fully plan to post regular updates and contribute in the forums. If you answered true to all of these questions, then PTC is for you. Participation PTC is a user driven site. Fedheads and players alike should post news, rumors, columns, etc. and become involved in the forums. At its core, we are a community. If your intention in PTC is only to help your fed, without contributing to the site or community, then this is not the place for you. Wrestler Contracts In PTC there is a simple "contract" rule. Wrestlers are bound to their feds until released by their fed heads. Furthermore, Wrestlers may only wrestle in one PTC fed at a time. Handlers may play different characters in different feds. Trash Talking All trash talk goes on the Trashtalk board. It must be in character and targetted at a specific individual or small group of individuals. Recruiting Free Agents We have a PTC Free Agent forum. If you are a PTC Network/Cable affiliate, you may recruit from this forum, by responding to potential free agent posts. If you wish to advertise for players, you should do so on the PTC advertising forum. Free agents are only signed by your fed once they agree to a contract and appear on a card. An application from a wrestler does not imply they are signed by your fed. Posting to the Main Site Proofread your posts before posting. You may post three posts per section per day. If you need to post more, see a member of the council. Do not advertise looking for wrestlers or staff on the main site. Use the PTC advertising forums to do that. All the categories of the main site are self-explanatory. * Affiliate Federations are link-exchange only. They are not involved with PTC events, titles, etc.. * Public Access Federations are the federations that are either relatively new to PTC, federations starting up, or federations that are struggling. These federations are allowed to participate in PTC events, hold PTC titles, use the PTC Fedheads website, and use the PTC main site for fed promotion. Their shows are not rated in PTC ratings, their wrestlers are not listed in the PTC top ten, and their main site additions are not rated as extras. They are not allowed to use the PTC free agents forum either, as they are expected to build up a roster and produce shows by themselves at first, as we are not here to just give you a great federation. * Cable Federations are federations that are producing shows, and are starting to make that climb torwards being a great e-federation. They get everything Public Access Federations recieve, they are allowed to submit top ten choices, their extras are rated, and they are allowed use of the PTC free agents forum. * Network federations are the cream of the crop. All of these federations have been running consistently for a great deal of time, and have proved to be federations truly worth the time and effort that was put into them. PTC puts most of it's effort into promotion of network feds, as their weekly shows get rated, and they get maximum ranking priviledges. - This community lives through interaction. You will find people on the forums at all times of day, you will find people in the chat rooms most of the daytime, and we are constantly keeping the communication lines open between all parties. If you would like to join the forums, you are more then welcome to join. We'd love to have you.. Thanks. Primetime Central.
