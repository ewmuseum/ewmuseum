Richie Stone First Appearance | Latest Appearance WWA | WWA Federations | World Wrestling Alliance Age | 25 Birthplace | Fresno, California [image: Image:Usa.png]' Finisher | Charisma Cutter Wrestling Style | Grappler/Martial Artist Championships | WWA United States Champion Handler | [name withheld] Richie Stone (born March 6th, 1984) is an American professional wrestler who currently wrestles for the World Wrestling Alliance, a former member of the Primetime Central circuit. Stone is a cocky and arrogant young man. He was born into immense wealth. His father is the founder of StoneCorp International, one of the worlds largest energy companies. He has never wanted for anything and at 24 years of age was a junior executive in his father's company and has travelled the world. He has always had a penchant for combat, having the time to train in Muay Thai, and amateur and professional wrestling. He has no other family to speak of except for his Mother and Father and he lives a very opulent and decadent life. He enjoys drinking, smoking and women, and has plentiful amounts of all three. Stone has friends in high places and seemingly endless cash flow due to his high paying job outside of wrestling and his high yield swiss bank accounts, along with his trust fund. He is self educated and very intelligent, not religious in any means of the word and believes himself to be perfect. Ruthless to no end, he will do anything necessary to succeed when he wants to. Early Life As a child, Richie was a typical privileged child. He was given things many people only dream of, including training in Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-jitsu, Boxing and Wrestling, by the best trainers and was moulded into the athlete he is today. These days, Stone has undergone a personality change, and believes himself to be a Rockstar. He has severed all ties with his family, and is now living only for himself and his fiancee Yasmine Beckridge. He has a budding Rock career and has a hit single off his album; Stone Cold Superiority....the song is titled "I'm awesome, you're not". Career World Wrestling Alliance In late 2006, after Richie had won titles for both boxing and Brazillian Jiu-Jitsu he met an executive from the WWA. The man was impressed with Richie's performances and other achievements and proceeded to ask the future "Frontman" if he was interested in a career in professional wrestling. After a sizable contract had been drawn up and an intense training regimen set in place. Richie spent the next two years applying what he already knew from martial arts and boxing to competing in the squared circle. He then went on to make an impressive debut in the WWA, at Global Warfare 2008 in Paris, France to much hype and praise. He went on to win the match and steadily rise up the WWA ladder into the main event picture. As of late, Richie has been setting his "Corporate America" mindset aside and has been doing some soul searching to find himself. He is currently putting his Stonecorp responsibilities on his employees underneath him for raised pay so that he can completely focus on developing his own presence in the WWA. In January of 2009, Richie captured his first WWA singles title when he won the United States Championship at Birthday Bash X by busting open Trendkiller. Not only did he win gold but he is slowly winning over the hearts of the WWA fans with his new personality, flashy and dynamic in ring style, and his newfound respect for the WWA faithful. He then went on to retain his title in a war with Seth Copeland the following Meltdown in a "match of the year" candidate. Richie Stone competed in Ultimate Glory II for the World Heavyweight Championship at Revolution 2009, but was eliminated by his nemesis Copeland after The Endgame off the ring apron onto the concrete floor. Stone would go on and defend his US title against Denrol on the April 20th edition of Meltdown, in a no disqualification match. Stone and Denrol beat each other to a pulp, but in the end, it was Copeland who cost his rival the US title, when he attacked and nailed Stone with a vicious Endgame, spiking him on the top of his head, and dragging a groggy Denrol on top to become the new United States Champion. After the match Copeland said he did so to remove "distractions" from Richie's life, the US title being the first.
