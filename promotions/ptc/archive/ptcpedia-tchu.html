Tchu Handled by Matt Ward [[Image:|190px|center]] First Appearance | Latest Appearance New 4 Order | PRIME Federations | N4O, tU (the Underground), R4W, PRIME Age | 31 Birthplace | Dayton, OH Finisher | The Downfall Wrestling Style | Technician/Brawler Championships | N4O Tag Team Titles tU Immortality Title R4W World Heavyweight Title PTC Infinite Gauntlet Title PTC Unified Title PRIME Tag Team Titles PRIME Universal Title (x2) [[:Category:|Images]] Tchu (real name Matt Ward, born Aug 19, 1976) is a professional wrestler most well known for his stints in the 4w owned N4O and R4W, and most recently in PRIME. His often emotionless, machine like, demeanor... combined with his well-known aggression and talent... has earned him nicknames such as The Inhuman Being and PRIME's Wrecking Ball. Physical Characteristics Height: 6’2” Weight: 244lbs Trademark Moves The Downfall – Tchu’s legendary finishing maneuver. A ‘Sleeping Neckbreaker’. Locking his opponent in a sleeper hold, Tchu quickly snaps to the mat, driving the back of his opponent’s skull into the canvas. The move can be hit on anyone at any time, often coming from out of nowhere to finish the opponent. The move has become synonymous with The Inhuman Being, and is rarely ever kicked-out of. FTW – Tchu’s submission finisher of choice. The judo-choke hold known as the Katahajime. Inhuman Treatment – Occasionally used to finish off superstars under 300lbs, this move sees Tchu hoist his opponent onto his shoulders in a fireman’s carry, then push them up and out (in an F5 motion). As the opponent spins loose, Tchu keeps his arm hooked across their chest and shoulder and slams down with a Rock Bottom type finish. Awards/Accomplishments * N40 Tag Champion * tU Immortality Champion * R4W World Heavyweight Champion * PRIME Universal Champion (x2) * PRIME Tag Champion * PRIME’s 2005 Jewel in the Crown Tournament Champion * PRIME’s 2006 Dual Halo Runner-Up * PRIME’s 2007 Dual Halo Winner * PRIME’s 2006 Wrestler of the Year * PRIME’s 2006 Co-Match of the Year (Vs Killean Sirrajin) * PRIME’s 2006 Feud of the Year (Vs Killean Sirrajin) * PTC Infinite Gauntlet Champion * PTC Unified Champion * ToC Finalists (finished 2nd Place behind Nova) New 4 Order In October of 2001, 4w Enterprises unleashed to the wrestling world the N4O. Present from its first Asylum Show till the final days of its existence (culminating in a spot in the Elite Eight match at the final PPV, Brutal Tactics), Tchu was a staple of the federation. Random Facts & Trivia * Tchu once held three different tag team championships at the same time (N40, C4W, XUPW all w/Sephiroth) * Tchu’s first Universal Title reign lasted only about 20 days. His second was more than 100 days longer. * Tchu started his PRIME career 9-0. One shy of the PRIME record undefeated streak. * In 2006, Tchu finished as the winner in the Feud of the Year category (Vs. Sirrajin) in the PRIME Year End Awards. He also finished runner-up in the same category (Vs. The World). * Tchu main-evented Colossus III in Tampa, FL in a match 4 years in the making, going one on one against his nemesis Killean Sirrajin. The match (held outdoors at Raymond James Stadium) ended under a steadily falling rain. * Tchu successfully defended his Universal Title at The Great American Nightmare in October of 2006 by defeating PRIME Hall of Famers Vangelus Olsig and Nova in a triple threat. (Tchu prefers not to talk about those following matches with Nova) * In 2007, Tchu defeated over 50 other superstars to win the Dual Halo. He eliminated six superstars: Violence Jack, Angelo Deville, Jason Snow, GBJ, Karina Wolfenden, and Danny Ferguson. A literal PTC legends-esque list. * His average Halo finish: 1.5 (First and a half place) * Tchu is the ‘winningest’ IG Champion ever, defeating 19 foes over the course of 3 rounds, before dropping the title in a triple threat. * Due to unforseen events surrounding the PTC 2006 Year End Awards, Tchu considers himself the 2006 Wrestler of the Year ( very much disputing any such claims by one Doc C.) * Tchu doesn’t like Clinton Sage
