Jake Hix Biography Biography The Hix Family ranks as one of the greatest Texas wrestling families of all time. Along with the Von Erichs and the Funks, they created a standard of excellence in the business. The path to greatness was started by "Big" John Hix and Pitt Hix in regional promotions as they competed against state legends like Wild Bill Reed and John "Hawk" Peters. While John eventually retired, Pitt Hix went on to an amazing career he parlayed into business ventures in retirement. After John and Pitt, three more brothers would come along. Jake Hix was the middle brother, and Sid and Seth Hix were twin brothers who found success in Japan as a tag team until an injury forced Sid to retire and Seth to find prominence as a singles wrestler. Middle children are often the ignored children. They can be neglected and slighted, but Jake was raised in a loving household where no such neglect occurred. However, when it came to being a professional wrestler Jake was forced to live in the shadow of his older brother Pitt. Pitt Hix had come to prominence in the SAWF during a time of unparalleled success. Competing against legends like Rick Slamm, TMADP, Silas Creel, Versatu, Flash Redux and "Sensational" Stephen Reed he became a legend as a Two Time World Heavyweight Champion, Extreme Champion and Tag Team Champion. All of these facts weighed heavily on a young man coming up through the ranks of the rookies in the league. Jake Hix was a promising prospect with all the right physical attributes, a great character and plenty of potential. Unfortunately, Jake was also a lazy wrestler who felt that he deserved success. He found his first few months in the league difficult, faced with tough losses and lacking drive. The SAWF Management Team had established a new league, the RAWF for younger stars. The decision was made to send Jake to the league to work on his skills, and they asked Pitt to let his brother know. The meeting just highlighted all the frustration Jake was feeling. Pitt became the man ruining his career, keeping him down, and so Jake decided to become even bigger than his brother. In the RAWF, Jake became the top face for the entire league. He was beloved, with great merchandising sales and fans chanting his name wherever he went. He found the success he had been looking for all those years. The SAWF Management Team was impressed with Jake's performance, and he was brought back to the SAWF full time. Jake was popular, more popular and loved than he had ever been before. Fans chanted "Big Dawg! Big Dawg! Big Dawg!" every time he entered the arena. He was satisfied, but then he began to notice all sorts of differences. He was cheered, but whenever Pitt entered the arena he was cheered for so much it was deafening. No matter what he accomplished, he would always be compared to his big brother. Jake took a different path. Jake became the biggest Heel he could. He forced people to look at him differently because he was now nothing like his big brother. Jake was the walking evil, a man on a mission. He took on everyone with a simple motto, "I'll take you down, break you down and make you my bitch!" In his career he captured singles glory, tag team glory and even became a Hall Of Famer. Once Pitt retired, he didn't stop. Continuing on creating his own legacy like no one had seen. Setting Billy Starr on fire, retiring Dylan Knight, surviving the monster Burns and in the end gaining the respect of the world. Hated, he received a standing ovation none the less in his retirement match. He left respected.. But then… Retirement sucked! Jake began pursuing his business interests, but most had been set-up by his management to run without him for the most part. He continued training himself and new wrestlers as part of the Hix Family Wrestling Academy. He also began studying traditional wrestling, Brazilian Jujitsu and kickboxing. He began to improve his traditional fighting skills while training others. The Hix Family Wrestling School shut-down for unknown reasons. Jake began showing up on the wrestling scene more and more as a guest announcer, trainer and celebrity. He then decided to pursue a new direction, out of nowhere coming to the Dark Horse Saloon in his hometown of Dallas, Texas and winning the 64 Man High Stakes Tournament. Jake accepted the contract offer, and now is has joined GLOBAL. Other Feds Global
