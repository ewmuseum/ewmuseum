PTC Wrestler of the Year - 2005! Stage 1 enclosed

9 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I don’t expect to win, but it’s fun to be nominated, even through self-nomination, so I’m putting forward Angelo Deville. >=) Debuted at PRIME’s Culture Shock in the fabled Dual Halo, in which he outlasted every member of the roster save Emilio Rage (the runner-up, who broke the all-time PRIME record for time spent in a match) and Karina Wolfenden (the winner). Compiled a record of 15-2, with both of those two losses suffered in cluster matches. Won nine consecutive matches, one victory shy of tying the PRIME winning streak record, before finishing second in the Tropical Turmoil main event. Matches included in that streak: - Defeated Los Diablos in a Mexican prison to retain the Tag Team Titles; Deville ended the night by inciting a prison break riot (which inspired a hit television series) and turning on his tag team partner, Justin Williamson, leaving him locked in a cell with their vanquished foes. - Defeated Justin Williamson (formerly known as The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, handled by Sean, of Seymour Almasy/Emoticon fame) in the grudge match that followed their dropping of the titles. - Defeated Magnum Wolf, effectively slamming the door on his Ideal 4 bid. - Defeated fellow nominee Brandon Youngblood in a Submission match. - Defeated Xavier Kannon in The Million Dollar Match. - Defeated The Covenant (Violence Jack, anyone?) in a handicap match. - Defeated fellow nominee Karina Wolfenden at Colossus. - Defeated PRIME Universal Champion Hoyt Williams, Vengelus Olsig, and Kyle “Velocity” Lamen in a fourway lumberjack match. - Teamed up with Nova to own fellow nominee Brandon Youngblood and Aimz. Pinned fellow nominee Emilio Rage in a triple threat match that also included Brandon Youngblood, in the follow-up for the runners up in the Dual Halo, dubbed the Dual Halo Aftermath match. Pinned fellow nominee “Irish” Danny Ferguson in an Irish bar fight. By dreaming up a pwnerful drinking song. You heard me. >=) Another loser in that Irish bar fight was the esteemed Paddy O’Shea. Submitted Intense Champion Vangelus Olsig (again) in a non-title match. Poisoned Angelus by siccing a rabid dog on him. Made “Look at the Dog” famous. >=) Was responsible for Jon Kano actually rocking, albeit only for a second, as the result of a nuclear blast. Seriously. No, seriously. Got in fellow nominee Karina Wolfenden’s pants. Consequently turned her into an obsessive wreck. >=) Suckered Argyle into sporting The Mark of The Deville on at least one occasion. >=) Went on a drunken rampage with Nova, setting the wheels in motion for alcohol getting banned backstage at all PRIME events. Opened a secret bar and casino backstage to spite the new prohibition on alcohol. Subsequently banished the Olsen twins to GCW. Was the guy responsible for fellow nominee Emilio Rage fucking Lindsay Lohan. >=) Introduced nuclear weapons to PRIME television. Hired Hoyt Williams to pray against fellow nominee Brandon Youngblood. The instant almost-miracle almost killed him. A mere two months later, Youngblood was fired. >=) Won PRIME’s Tag Team Titles. Voted runner-up for Roleplayer of the Year 2004-2005 (behind Karina Wolfenden), despite only being in the federation for a few months. Voted runner-up for Character of the Year 2004-2005 (behind Hoyt Williams), despite only being in the federation for a few months. PRIME Wrestler of the Night on three separate occasions; runner-up for the award too many times to count (Dillon stole this line from my bio, so while he used it before me in this thread, I still feel right using it). PTC #2 Wrestler of the Month for June 2005 (the only month he was nominated under the present communist system >=), ranked only behind the Uber Judge, whom ruled by virtue of his incomparable Infinite Gauntlet streak. Inspired the reigning Global Champion, Rich Rollins, to take on the role of challenger in GTT5. Barely lost the match. Advanced to round two anyhow due to unbridled pwnery. >=) And, blatantly, just to spite Argyle . . . won Face Off. >=)
