Poser University: mission, rules and FAQ

c. June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is (written in 2026) Poser University was a school for the Poser artists who made the 3D character pictures many feds used, opened in June 2002 by an artist of Primetime Central’s community. This is its own mission statement, rules and questions and answers, from the donor’s database; no capture of the page is held. It shows how the community taught Poser: tutorials, lesson plans, a request board and free textures. The page names no date of its own. The markup of its headings is not reproduced; its text is.
Mission Statement The primary goal is to provide help for aspiring artists to improve their skills. We strive to have a wide selection of tutorials as well as step by step lesson plans to help beginers learn the basics of Poser. We offer the opertunity once an artist has reached an elite level to help them find paying work with White Mexican’s own production team but no artist is required to accept. Rules and Regulations The rules are pretty straight forward. Any violation of the rules will result in a warning and if further violated you will be banned. Trouble makers are not tolerated. PoserU is a friendly place, treat everyone with respect. Only respond with helpful comments. Comments such as "You suck" and "That sucks" will not be tolerated. It's okay to not like someone elses work but state reasons why in a helpful mannor. No recruitment of Poser Artists on the forums. Violaters will be warned once and banned if repeated. Have fun, learn and improve. You are not to IM the Teachers or Principles. If they IM you that is fine or if they tell you to, otherwise don't hassle the staff through IM's. If you need to talk to staff use the board messanger. Frequently Asked Questions What is Poser University? PoserU is a place where anybody no matter what level they are on can come and get help about Poser. What does PoserU have that other sites don't? The two main differences with PoserU are... 1. Our primary goal is to help the beginner become an advanced user. 2. Not only do we have tutorials but we also have lesson plans that students will complete and progress. Can I recruit an artist using your forums? Absolutely not. PoserU is not to be a place of recruitment but a place of learning. Students do not need the worry about a dozen requests for posers. Can I have a poser of my character made? Yes and no. There will be a board set up for poser requests where you may post a description. If a student chooses you to practice on then it will be made but you can not hound students to make one for you. Who runs PoserU? The White Mexican is the man that gets the final say in things although fell free to check the staff profiles for information on some of the other staffers. Can I profit from PoserU? Actually you can. In todays efed world there is a lot of money to be had doing posers. The better you are the more you can make. If you stick around long enough I may even ask you to join my team of artists and hook you up with jobs. What can I use your freebies for? Anything you want as long as it doesn't result in selling or redistributing them. As long as this site is open direct anyone wanting them to come here and get them from the source.
