Seven of the Best rules

c. March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is (written in 2026) The Seven of the Best event site’s own rules page, as the donor’s database holds it (lot 0159). The page names the roleplay period as 11.59pm Saturday 12 March to 11.59pm Wednesday 22 March (2005), so it stood in March 2005. No capture of the page is held. The page’s navigation and the markup of its headings are not reproduced; its text is.
Roleplay Limits 3 RP's per player. One RP on the last day. The Three RP's will be used to determine who advances AND who wins the final matchup. Roleplay Time Period 11.59pm Saturday 12th March to 11.59pm Wednesday 22 March. Judging and Results expected within 5-7 days after this. Judging Judging comments will be minimal if any. This is a roleplay competition, as would take place in your home fed, where the reward for writing your roleplays is participation in a match on an official PTC event. The judges will be selected from the PTC community by the event director and will not have any known bias towards either of the competitors in the match they are judging. For the one on one matches, there will be three judges per match unless not enough volunteers are found to do this. For the main event, there will be three judges. Plagiarism: Anyone found to be stealing the work of others and posting it in this event will be banned from participating in any future PTC events, for life. Recycling: All Roleplays posted must be written for this event. This means that they are not to have already been used in your home fed or anywhere else. Additionally, they are to remain exclusive to this event, and may only be posted elsewhere for archival purposes. Quoting: If you use snippets of other people's work, or quote song lyrics, or anything else that you didn't personally write, you MUST quote your reference. Linking: All Roleplays must be posted on this website using a username and password provided by PTC Management. Roleplays linked and hosted elsewhere will not be considered during judging. Deadline Extensions: Are extremely unlikely to be granted, as the Roleplay period is quite generous. No-shows: We all get busy, these things happen. However, if a player accepts a position in this event on behalf of their fed and then no-shows without notifying both their opponent and PTC management that it is going to happen before the last day, then that player will be forever banned from competing in future PTC events. Its all about respect people. Substitutes: The announced card is final once the RP period starts. Roleplay Content: Once Roleplays have been posted on this website, they will remain available for viewing on the site archive once the event is over. Future requests by the author to remove material will be carried out at the sole discretion of PTC management.
