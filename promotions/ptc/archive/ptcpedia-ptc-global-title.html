PTC Global Title

captured 19 Aug 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now defunct after being merged with the PTC Extreme Title to create the PTC Unified Championship, the PTC Global Title was, for several years, the premier title in Primetime Central. Title History * Vampir Nosferatu * Vampir won the first ever GTT to be crowded the first PTC Global Champion. * Vampir defeated Jay Phoenix in the finals at Primetime Championship Wrestling's Friday Fusion on November 23rd, 2001 * He would later be stripped of the title. * Obsidian * Obsidian, from WeWA won the belt on May 19, 2002 at Night of Champions. The belt would later be retired after Obsidian suddenly departed from PTC. * Obsidian defended his belt against Omega Fro in FAW shortly after winning it. The exact date is unknown. At the time the belt was referred to as the PTC World title. * Donny Diamond * Won a 15 Man Elimination Match at a PTC PPV for the vacated title on 4-22-03. * The last five by order of elimination were DVD (XWF), Jesse Jamester (LOW), the Real Deal (DWO), Mike Meyhu (LOW), and Donny Diamond (DWO). Donny was named the winner and crowd the newly restated Global Champion. * Donny lost the belt when losing in the first round of the PIT in July of 2003. The belt was declared vacant and the winner of the tournament would be the new champion. * Seymour Almasy * Seymour's first reign as Global Champion came on July 2003 and lasted until March 2004 where he would be defeated by Chris Storm. Seymour won a 64-Man PTC Invitational Tourney for the belt. * On October 31st, 2003 Seymour successfully defended his title against Angelus Sorrow. * On February 8th, 2004 Seymour Almasy successfully defended his title against Dave Harley of WWA in a Best 2 out of 3 falls match on RPW's Adrenaline program. Chris Storm attacked Seymour Almasy with the Global title after the match. * Following the match the head of the PTC Global Title division stepped down and was replaced. * Chris Storm * Chris Storm defeated Seymour Almasy and AWS Man (Also Known as Bill) on March 2004. * He would later lose the belt to Jesse Jamester during a fatal fourway match on April 4, 2004. * Many credit this time as a renewal in interest in the PTC Global Title, which saw it's first ever stint of regular title defenses. * Jesse Jamester * Jesse defeated Apocalypse (FAW), Patti Anderson (4WW), and © Chris Storm (RPW) at XUW's Reborn on April 4, 2004. * Hessian * Hessian defeated Blake Wolfson (XUW), Iron Lung (LOW), Josh (GW), Todd Dream (4WW), and © Jesse Jamester (GW) in a Six-Way Ladder Match for PTC Global Title on LOW Realm 50 on May 5, 2004. * Following the match the head of the PTC Global title division stepped down and was replaced. * Saij * Saij defeated Digital Mortality (UWF) and © Hessian (RPW) in a Three-Way Match for PTC Global Title. * Saij successfully defended the Global title against Aiden DeLaCroix and AWS Man (aka Bill) on 08-02-2004. He would lose the belt just six days later. * Seymour Almasy (2) * Seymour won the belt back from Saij in what was supposed to be a rematch of all the previous former Global Champions at PTC Survivor on 08-08-2004. Only Saij and Jesse Jamester showed up for the match. * The Latch Key Kid * Latch Key Kid defeated Seymour Almasy on LOW television on 01-24-2005. * Emilio Rage * Emilio Rage defeats Mal Somers (WWA), Nick Worrell (Core), and © Latch Key Kid (XUW) at PTC's Seven of the Best event on 04-02-2005 * Karina Wolfenden * Karina vacated the title after defeated Emilio Rage on 5/25/2005. * The night is referred to as one of the worst nights of the Global title's history since Vampir's win. * Rich "The Renegade" Rollins * Rich Rollins won the Global Title at PTC's Super Series in a Scavenger Hunt Match for the vacant PTC Global and Extreme Titles on 02-14-2006. * Uber Judge * Defeated Rich Rollins on 06-04-2006 for the title. * Shadow * Shadow defeated Uber Judge by KO at PTC vs. Archibald MacGregor on 09-15-2006. * The match was one of the fastest very, with Shadow defeated Uber Judge in less than two minutes. * Seymour Almasy (3) * Seymour defeated Shadow on the eve of GTT6 on FUSE's Killzone on 09-28-2006, making the first person to ever win the belt three times. * Chandler Tsonda of PRIME was scheduled to be a part of the match but had to withdraw last minute for personal reasons. * Kimbusa * Kimbusa defeated Seymour Almasy on 10-24-2006 in the first round of the GTT6 tournament as part of the Kanga bracket. * In the next round of the tournament, Kimbusa defended the title against Shadow and retained the belt. * It is the second time ever the belt was defended during a GTT event, the first to not be in the finals. * Clinton Sage * Clinton Sage defeated © Kimbusa, Angelo Deville, and Doctor Curiosity on FUSE's Killzone 30 on 02-22-2007. * Jason Snow1 * Jason Snow defeated Jayce Bradley, Dusk, "The New Shit" Bobino and © Clinton Sage on 04-30-2007 1At this point, the Global Title was unified with the Extreme Title. Trivia 17 different superstars have held the PTC Global Title. Only 4 have ever successfully defended the belt after winning it: Obsidian, Seymour Almasy, Saij, and Kimbusa.
