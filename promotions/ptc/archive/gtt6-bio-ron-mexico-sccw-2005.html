Ron Mexico Biography Biography Basically dude is a yuppy, salesman with the coin to his name but can't use it because his parents have now become tired of him using his money on gambling on Texas Hold'em. So now he's forced to take on a real job to support himself and his lifestyle. Though Mexico wasn't his original name, he later changed it feeling his career in wrestling and Sales would just look better on a business card than his name which was Perseus Andrews III. After reading a story in the paper about Michael Vick and his exploits with women felt he had to immortalize it. That added to the frustration of his parents who he had been gravy-training off their estate for years was finally kicked out of the house in Martha's Vineyard and asked to get a life for himself. So with nothing else but his mobile sales job and wrestling to put his time in and get a little bank was forced to actually take his work seriously. Which was something he had never had the pressure of being forced to do. Let alone a wrestling move. After FUSE saw him and signed him onto a short term deal he decided to take the role seriously. Though his work as a salesman was just as important to him as FUSE so he might get back at his parents proving there was more to Mexico than superficiality and a life’s futility. Other Feds FUSE
