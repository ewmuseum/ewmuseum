Ash Real Name: Ash Plato Alter Ego: Judge Height: 6'1 Weight: 225 lbs. Entrance Music: Faces by Orgy Hometown: Las Vegas Valley Metroplex Political Views: a self described "Free Thinker that the government cannot control with their lies or propaganda" Occupation: Visual Artist, Political Activist, Vigilante Favorite Musical Artists: Tool, A Perfect Circle, Staind, Isis, the Red Sparrowes, Pelican, Smashing Pumpkins, Radiohead, Coldplay, Nirvana, People in Planes, Interpol, The Killers, A Girl Called Eddy, Orgy, Incubus, the Bravery, The Shins Biography Born and raised in Las Vegas, Ash grew up in a wild climate. Las Vegas was and still is out of control. The multinational gaming conglomerates, mega corporations, roving gangs of thugs, and utter lawlessness of the citizenry made it a harsh environment for any child to grow up in. During his tumultuous teenage years, Ash found release in the arts. His sculptures, paintings, and writings were a release for him. However he withdrew and became more anti-social then he was in the past. After graduating from high school, it was all too much for Ash. He got into political activism and may be considered an anarchist with his actions. The crime rate in his neighborhood was out of control as well and someone close to him died. For months he was distraught and depressed, having no direction, but Ash was inspired by the movie Dark Knight and at nights he becomes the vigilante anti-hero known as Judge. In his all black attire, Judge judges the criminals he catches by either leaving them for the cops (minor crimes) or by killing them (more severe crimes). However he is not a hero at all. He has been known to take the money or other items that the criminals have stolen to keep for himself. The cops are not happy at all over his actions so he is also a wanted criminal for the murders he has caused from his "judging." Promo History I II [1] Allies and Enemies Allies: Various friends and associates in the daytime as Ash Enemies: Rick Roll - Master criminal who "Rick Rolls" his victims. He plays off like a sympathatic person or crime victim before doing so. Is a top priority criminal for the Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department. Is revealed to be Ash's best friend Todd in I. First appearance: I Pilgrim - A giant, psychotic Native American male prison escapee in a Pilgrim outfit who "harvests" his victims bodies. Uses hatchets, axes, and other assorted weapons on his victims. First appearance: II [2] Amnesia - A sociopathic lunnatic who has no idea who he is. Violent, has homicidal tendencies, and is basically against everyone both good and bad. If they have a plan, he will try to make it blow up in their face and create chaos and anarchy. May or may not be pro wrestler and Vegas resident James von Doom. Rumors would lean toward the negative on that.
