Argyle’s notes on the card

Argyle wrote these notes into his copy of the show in 2026. They are not part of the 2005 text below, so they are set out here, each under the heading of the part it was written into, word for word.

Jonathon Winters versus Xian Phillipse. WWA showcase: Jonathon Winters vs Xian Phillipse. Non-title: if Winters won, he would get a later shot at Phillipse's title, if Phillipse still held it. Xian Phillipse posted no roleplay.

Kenny Mercury vs Chris Storm. UWF showcase: Kenny Mercury vs Chris Storm, with Argyle as special guest referee.

Jake Hix vs. Dawn McFarlane. Global showcase: Jake Hix vs Dawn McFarlane, a Master of the Mat rematch.

Deszaras vs Jonny Kae. HSW showcase: HSW World Champion Deszaras vs HSW Evolution Champion Jonny Kae, their first singles match.

Trey Tremendous vs Eva VanLorne. GCW showcase: Trey Tremendous vs Eva VanLorne, an ACWF match: Asphalt, Chains, Wrenches and Forklifts.

Paddy O'Shea vs Hate. AWC showcase: Paddy O'Shea vs Hate, a Hardcore Lumberjack match. Hate posted no roleplay.

XUW vs PRIME - Survival Bragging Rights. XUW vs PRIME for Survival bragging rights, an eight-man tag elimination match: Team XUW (Cereal Killer, woof woof, Scotty Spalzcutoff and Saij) vs Team PRIME (Kyle Lamen, Aimz, Alex Ross and Dave Gibson). All four winners would be eligible for the Global and Extreme Title match at the first event of the next year. Kyle Lamen posted no roleplay.

Scavenger Hunt Match for the vacant PTC Global and Extreme Titles. Main event: a scavenger hunt at Harvard University for the vacant PTC Global and Extreme Titles. The first to find a belt and hand it to a PTC official won it. Karina Wolfenden (PRIME) and Seymour Almasy (GCW), tethered together; Rich Rollins (GCW), whose GTT5 pick-your-opponent prize went to anyone who pinned or knocked him out; Hoyt Williams (PRIME); The Uber Judge (XUW), carrying the Extreme Title in; Shadow (Global); Michael Stevens (GCW); Brandon Youngblood (PRIME). Karina Wolfenden posted no roleplay.
