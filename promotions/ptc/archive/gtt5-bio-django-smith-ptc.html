Django Smith Biography Biography Tell you a bit? All right. Take the dysfunctional family archetype and then twist it a bit. The protagonist here is Django Smith. He's about thirty, give or take a year. Well, he's a delusional guy who thinks anything bad that happens is a blatant conspiracy against him. He's subtly paranoid. These are defense mechanisms--along with building and believing elaborate stories his mind makes. Defending what? Well, we'll find out eventually. Now let's delve into the rest of the family. Dad? He's dead. Car accident. That same car accident left both Django's uncle and grandfather in a state of mental retardation. Uncle thinks he's John Wayne, pilgrim, and the grandfather goes in and out of Elvis and fictitious characters only he knows about. Mom? She's in a wheelchair, thanks in part to a nice tumble. She also dislikes Django a whole bunch for spending most of his college money on wrestling shit--training and shit. Sister and brother are sort of normal, but they've got their own quirks, too. With the cast and the situations that may arise from them, I can have a bunch of fun jerking the story from atmospheric cynicism to lighthearted goofiness.
