24 April 2003 UCWF Weekly Welcome to UCWF Weekly. I am J.L. Scoopz. This week:Views And Ramblings Thie Weeks News, Rumors, and Rants from a man who says the new generation of the UCWF is upon us all!!!! -Sorry about missing the last few weeks UCWF fans. My life has been non stop madness the past few weeks. Its really hard being the head of talent for the UCWF, did you all know that? We have just been signing so much talent as of late and been developing new stuff...its just nuts! Enough about me though...onto the shiznat! -Like I said above...the new generation is upon us all and the major question is who is the leader of this generation? It comes down to two people: Johnny Johnson and The Notorious Don Joe Giovanni. Personally, I think Johnny is more old school than new school. He comes out week after week and rags on his oponents with his words and somehow manages to back them up in the ring. With The Don, he takes the bull by its horns and leads by force. The Don has my pick, but hey, I've been known to be wrong every once in a while. -Is Ace Of Spades back? It sure seems like it with his ass kicking he gave to Blaze. That Crossover was one of the most amazing and insane moves I have ever seen in my life. Mad props to Ace. -The Krazy-Wacko Connection just got what might be the biggest win of their lives this past week when they won the UCWF Tag Team Titles for the like.....7th or 8th time by beating The Leathel Injection AKA The Comptons. Clean win or not, it was still huge and really establishes them as the top team in the business. -The Comptons are simply described by one word: AMAZING. I love Linken...I mean he's my bro and all, but this week I just can't see him getting past Jason Comtpon. This guy is a future champion! -Poor KO Kid! The guy comes back, gets cheated out of a win, and gets hurt in the process. I admire him for finishing the match though even though he was already hurt. KO has semi-severely broken bones in his foot. He will be out on the shelf 4-6 weeks recovering from this injury. We wish him nothing but the best! -What's up with The Fox? The past few weeks the guy has just not been on his game at all. I hope he can get things together soon. This former IC, Extreme, and Universal champion still has alot left to do in UCWF! -Rich Bitch was looking very good in the ring Friday. I wonder what he can do againest the youngster IC Champion on Sunday? -Speaking of Simmons, isn't that kid impressive as hell?! He's 2-0 here thus far in UCWF and giving new meaning to the Intercontinental Title. I see another future world champ on our hands. -Flashing Thunder is also impressive. He is undefeated as well and showing alot of guys what being Extreme is all about. This week is his true test though. If he can beat Ace Of Spades, he establishs himself as a true athlete here. -Forever we will be hollow...forever we will be...forever we will be hollow...HOLLOW AGAIN! Man, I love that song! I can't wait to see those guys live at the PPV! -To end it this week...Get ready for a shocker to end all shockers Sunday. I've been in talks with people here and there and shits gonna hit the fan Sunday! Until Next Time....... J.L. Scoopz
