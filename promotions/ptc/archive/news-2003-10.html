4 October 2003 ::EPW:: Cracking Up?, Is It All Too Much? It was reported today that Dante Locks was seen checking himself out of a ward at his local hospital in Russells Hall. He stormed out of the ward claiming he was fine, but passers by seemed to think differently... "He was crazy, he threw a tray off the table shattering all that was on it. He was swearing profoundly, i'd never heard anything like it." We all know that his recent split from his fiance of 2 years Stace has caused a large amount of personal problems, psychological damage being one of the main problems. "Psychological trauma can cause many more problems than are let on. Breakdowns, being one of the main threats. Carrying on like he is pretending nothing happened will not heal the wound that has been opened.. he needs to get in touch with his sensitive side before it all builds up inside of him.... thus causing the breakdown." A comment there by one of the analysts at the hosptial concerned for Dante's welfare. We weren't able to catch up with Dante for a comment as he soon sped off in his BMW Z3 weaving in and out of traffic... Rumour has it that CJ is encouraging his wild behaviour, and could become a threat to both himself and Dante. Will this escapade continue, or will CJ realise that Dante needs to hela before he gets back into the swing of things? Reporting back later with any up to date reports. === 27 October 2003 cW Rising Star: Alex Caine Name: Alex Birthdate: 8/9/81 Height: 5'10 Weight: 226 lbs. Reason: Teaming with Morpheus Omega on his arrival, Alex Caine already looks to be a force in cW. The two men make a tag team called Twisted Reality. Titles are a possiblity for these two. === 27 October 2003 cW: Battle of Britain This past Sunday on PPV Core Wrestling presented its fifth PPV entitled Battle of Britain. The event sold out the Wembley Arena and was packed full of great action. In the opening bout, five men squared off in an over-the-top battle royal. These five me were new cW talent making their first Core Wrestling PPV appearance. After a huge spear from Alex Caine, Damien Wilde was the first to be eliminated, followed by a double team from Caine and Morpheus Omega to eliminate Skill Willie with a shoulder toss. This left the tag team Twisted Reality in the ring with Ted Masters. Omega in desperation takes both men down and then hurriedly eliminates Caine with a desperation shoulder toss. Omega and Masters were left in the ring. Masters went on to win the battle royal with assistance from his valet, Cynthia. This isn’t over between Twisted Reality and Ted Masters. If I were him, I’d watch his back. Backstage, Skill Willie was a little upset over his loss. He feels he could have won, but things happened like they did for a reason. He decided it’s time for him to start doing whatever it takes to get ahead in Core Wrestling. Could this lead to a new side of Willie that no one has ever seen before? The show went back to the ring where two of the baddest women in Core competed for the Core Wrestling Siren Championships. A little bit into the match after a missed moonsault by Artemis, the clothes started to come off. Artemis was stripped down to her bare essentials, but still managed to retain her title. Another day and another time for Alinie Fanco to win the Siren Championship. Backstage, former two time Legacy Champion Jantz Spalding received a letter from Sidarian’s ex-girlfriend, Sara Richards. Jantz was a little confused about the letter and the confusion wasn’t needed before his big match tonight. Back to the ring, Simon Krask was set to take on Mike Banks. Krask has been avoiding Banks since their brutal barbwire bat match on Extreme Smash 27. Krask came to the ring first looking to get this over with, but Banks played a mind game of his own with a little magic trick he taught himself. He tricked Krask into thinking he wasn’t coming out, and then came up from behind with a steel chair nearly taking Krask’s head off. The match had some huge spots in it with Banks being whipped into a bare turnbuckle. Krask ended up pulling off the victory even after the vicious attack at the beginning of the match. Backstage again, challenger for the Classic Championship, Jason Hughes had a spot interview with Alura. Basically Alura wanted to know what was going on in Hughes’ mind with the contract to his telecommunication company Sunfire Tel-Com on the line as well as the Classic Championship in this winner takes all match up. He sent out a strong message to his opponent, Alex Seeves with the interview. Back in the ring, the Core Wrestling Tag Team Champions were set to defend their titles against The Breed. Kyleman and Super Shawn, the Great British Heroes were doing well against The Breed. The Breed took early advantage against the obvious fan favorites, but in the end, the GBH came out on top after a disqualifying chair shot from Dorian Black. The GBH continue their reign as the Core Wrestling Tag Team Champions, but backstage, you better believe Twisted Reality was looking on with wishful thinking about the tag gold. The show then went to Jantz locker room were he was sitting down reading the letter he received earlier. It was from his former companion, Tara. Looks like a redeveloping relationship with Tara as well as the fans are in the near future with Jantz Spalding. Spalding had a moment to reflect why he was here and how he got here. Back to the ring again for one of the most anticipated match ups. Jason Hughes, CEO of Hughes Inc. and current holder of the contract to Sunfire Tel-Com squared against Core Wrestling Classic Champion, Alex Seeves. Both the contract and the Classic Championship were on the line in the match. Both men looked strong coming in, but a mistake by Hughes almost cost him. Seeves exploited a fear of Hughes but it then back fired on him when Hughes hit a huge shoulder tackle from the top rope. Hughes went on to hit his finisher and pin Seeves retaining his company and walking away with the Classic Championship. Right after that match, Sidarian took on Jack Diamond in a Lose Leaves Town match for the Icon Championship. Both men were punished in this match each hitting their finishers two and three times but still they both would kick out. The match went back and forth repeatedly and it was Sidarian who finally put Jack Diamond away winning the Icon Title and then shaking hands with Diamond leaving him in the ring to say his goodbyes as he has to leave Core. Former cW Owner, Matthew Lanier came from ringside seats and expressed his feelings to Jack and then allowed Jack the opportunity to address the fans one last time. He last match of the night was the Fatal Four Way set up by both Eliza FitzPatrick and Eugene FitzPatrick. Quinn Masters, AWOL and Jantz Spalding all wanted a piece of Daniel Adam’s Legacy Championship. The match was a great one and by the end of it all, Jantz Spalding walked away the fans’ champion winning the Legacy Championship for the third time. Daniel Adams then turns his back on Eugene FitzPatrick after being fired, but Adams shoved in his face an Iron Clad Contract signed moments before the match in case this happened. Adams is now a thorn in the thigh of Eugene FitzPatrick in this epic war raging on. At the closing of the show, Eliza was on the phone with the same man she has been for the past few weeks. This time, an interesting surprise when the man walked through her door. The repeated phone calls were to see which side of the table this new star would play on. Would he be in the power FitzPatrick Administration looking to totally dominate Core Wrestling or on the rebellious faction of cW stars led by Eugene’s daughter, Eliza. The man made his decision after slamming Eliza down hard in her office. The show ended and the battle was decided. Both sides gained a little, but lost much. No total figure has been given yet, but the estimated calculations is this that event brought in nearly 40.1 million U.S. dollars or roughly 24 million pounds. This amount is pre-expenses, but even when the expenses are paid, this will be marked as the highest grossing event for Core Wrestling to date. “I told you before, cW is expanding,” Eliza FitzPatrick commented during a post Battle of Britain Press Conference this morning. Apart from the record gross of any Core Wrestling event, Battle of Britain marked a turning point in the war between Eliza and Eugene FitzPatrick. Both parties received a huge blow sometime during the night, but it was Eugene’s Administration that took the biggest pitfall of them all. Daniel Adams lost the Legacy Championship to Jantz Spalding in the Fatal Fourway and then brutally attack Eugene leaving the Administration after expressing to Eugene that Eliza signed him to an Iron Clad Contract prior to the Fatal Four Way in case if the situation did escalate to what it did. “Adams made the biggest mistake of his life when he crossed me,” Eugene FitzPatrick said addressing the situation with Daniel Adams. The blow that Eliza FitzPatrick’s team of rebels took on Battle of Britain was the new addition to the FitzPatrick Administration. Eliza was slammed hard on the floor of her office by newest Administration member, Patrick Powers (a.k.a. P2). That is the explanation for the neck brace on Eliza during the press conference. Patrick Powers looks to be a strong force that may be driving the Administration into complete dominance in Core Wrestling. With P2 and the tag team Twisted Reality at FitzPatrick’s beckoning call, things do not look good for the Rebels. Creative Control in Core Wrestling looks to keep up the high ratings and high revenue with its second edition of Melee and the 31 episode of Smash all this week. Smash 31 marks the beginning of the Ringmaster competition. This week and next week is set with Round One matches. All the new talent introduced in the pre-PPV wave of new talent will be entered in the tournament. This is a great way for them to make a name for themselves in Core Wrestling. Also, on the cW.net, a new banner is advertising a new minor league to Core Wrestling set to be opening soon. Gulf Coast Wrestling set in the hometown area of former cW Owner, Matthew Lanier, looks to be the training grounds for prospective Core Wrestling superstars. It will also be used as a recuperation league for those injured in Core Wrestling looking to return, but need the ring rust cleared. If you missed Battle of Britain, it will be replayed on Tuesday and Thursday nights on Pay Per View. Source: corewrestling.net === 30 October 2003 [WMW] Epitome upset with his current push? After a short hiatus from action, Epitome returns this Halloween at El Dia de los Muertos to defend his Ace Superior Championship against Lucha Libre. It was unclear as to why Epitome was being held out of action, but reportedly he isn't happy with the way things are being handled backstage with him in and out of character. Rumor has it that if things don't clear up soon, Epitome may be looking for a different place of employment. Source: WMW
