PTC: New Era — Tyrone Grace Segment

20 April 2003 — the MGM Grand, Las Vegas, Nevada
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Drip. Drop. Drip. Drop. As the scene fades in, you see Tyrone Grace lacing up his boots in a secluded area of the arena. Steam rises from the floor, and pipes surround him, but he continues to prepare. He felt the need to speak before he went to the ring, a need to let some things off his chest, so he decided to do it alone, in a place like the boiler room. His face sweats from the humid air that filles the room, but he doesn't seem to mind. He spoke aloud, and as every word dropped from his mouth, you could hear the echo of the small, crowded room. "When you finally get the chance to prove your ability to the world, you have to stop and think to yourself; Is this a dream? This life has tossed me a lot of challenges, but challenges of a nature that turn into awards when I use my ability to take hold of them. This is what this whole pay per view is about. Several people from several federations and organizations that gather to showcase their abilities, respectively. Some are here because they were pushed, given a chance and really have no talent. Some are here because they worked their asses off and deserve to be here, so maybe I'll be able to separate those two classes." "How crazy is this!?! You're going to put some of the top extremists from some of the top feds in one damn ring? This is truly a place and time to showcase ability. Who knows, maybe I won't win this match. Maybe I've done all this preparing and thinking for nothing. This all has to do with fate ladies and gentlemen. I wouldn't be here right now if I didn't think that I could actually win this match. I would be here if I didn't believe in myself, but I do. I believe that I will use my God given talents to win this match." "Some may doubt me, some may say that some bigger name has a better chance of winning than Tyrone Grace has, but all I know is that I will walk down to that ring in moments to showcase my abilities. There is no gimmick tonight, Tyrone Grace is a man that is going to be what he TRULY is...Perfection. There is no room for error tonight, the prize at hand is too grand. So, for the DWO, for all the Tyrone Grace fans...this isn't your night. This isn't the night where Tyrone Grace represents you. This night is for me to showcase my skills, and I'm going to do it with my natural ability. No games, to tricks...it's either time to put up or shut up. So, before I walk down to the ring, just be true to yourselves because you all know that Tyrone Grace will be the last man standing tonight, God willing. Tyrone Grace exits the boiler room, leaving the camera with the same faint sound that the room gives. Drip. Drop. Drip. Drop.
