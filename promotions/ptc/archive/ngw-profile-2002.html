Member Profile: NGW

NGW Facts Fed Name: NGW-Next Generation Wrestling Web adress: http://ngw.ewhosting.com Roster Size: 32 Recent signings: Angelus Nosferatu, Laetitia Casta, Mad Dawg Munn, Zabier Weekly Card: Tuesday Night Storm Next PPV: Xtreme Havoc (August 4th, 2002) Current Champions NGW World Title: Reggie Rivid NGW AMerican Title: Time Bomb NGW Transatlantic Title: Ty Sanders NGW Xtreme Title: Panzer NGW Untamed Title: Axle NGW Tag Team Title: John Hitchin + Scott King Current Staff Co-Owner: Scottywood Co-Owner: Ice Vice President: Time Bomb Chairman: The Denouncer Commish: John Hitchin Head of Storylines: Calvin Riley DWR: Assistant DWR: NGW's Top 5 Wrestlers 7/21-7/27 1. Chris Toms 2. Zabier 3. The Denouncer 4. Axle 5. Ty Sanders NGW History The NGW opened January 25th, 2002 under the name XWF-Xtreme Wrestling Federation. The fed was owned by 2 men who both came from the IWF, which had just recently shut down. These two men were Ice and Scottywood. Now the XWF was a novice fed and was the first fed either one of them had owned. They had a simple web site made on angelfire with an unoriginal name, title belts and loads of real wrestlers. The XWF was based on hardcore wrestling and insane innovative matches. One of the first signs on this was at the XWF’s first ever PPV Out Cold. The match was Unlimited member Prime Time vs. XWF Vice President Scottywood. The match wasn’t for a title but just a grudge match. The match ended with a moment that will be etched in ever XWF fan’s minds forever. The moment was Scottywood driving a Zamboni off a loading dock onto a car which Prime Time was in. Prime Time was lucky to say the least because the Zamboni landed on the section of the car he wasn’t in. If it had to would have spelled certain death. 2 months later the XWF held it’s second PPV, Wrestlemania that has come to be known as Xtrememania I. Despite the unoriginal name the XWF proceeded to hold a huge PPV match. A few weeks earlier, Ice, Scottywood’s best friend stabbed him in the back and joined enemy Revolution X, now known as Calvin Riley. This prompted the match of Scottywood, X-Rated and Excalibur vs. Revolution X, Prime Time and Ice for all the titles in the Extremists and Unlimited Stable, which was almost every title in the fed. The match started out face paced and exciting but then something happened that changed things in the XWF forever. Ice and RX’s best friend prime Time turned on RX and joined Scottywood in the Extremists costing RX the match and the titles. After this match the great Unlimited empire began to crumble. The Extremists proved that they were the most dominant force in the XWF. After that the XWF had great moments like Stable Warz 2002, crowning of the KOTR, which then lead to an incident that almost killed the XWF. It was around late October-early November when VP Scottywood got in a heated argument with co-owner of his chat wrestling fed ICF Icpnumber1. He has stolen the ICF site from Scottywood and refused to give it back. Now according to speculation ICPnumber1 reported the XWF site to angelfire for violation that the XWF didn’t violate. The XWF believed that he had connections inside anglefire and had the site shutdown for no reason. This left the NGW without a web site to operate it’s fed with. Now at this point many federations would crumble but the XWF stood tall and rebuild a web site on freeservers. This web site lead the XWF into the year 2001 where the XWF debuted “The Perfect Match” at Out Cold 2001 and crowned Brian Hardy Royal Rumble Champion at Border Line. At Xtrememania II the main event was set as Revolution X (Calvin Riley) vs. XWF President and World Champion Ice. The match was hard fought but in the end Revolution X came up short of claiming his 2nd XWF World Title. After Xtrememania 2 the XWF hit a low, low point having only 6 cards in a 2 month span of time and then not having a single card for the next 3 and a half months due to the XWF rebuilding it’s site on a new server because of bandwidth problems which freeservers. The XWF tried to make a comeback but it was like trying to revive a dead animal, impossible. At this time Revolution X was working on a new e-fed called DSW: Downwards Spiral Wrestling. He offered Scottywood a deal to take in the XWF wrestlers into his fed. Scottywood agreed but many things had to be iron out. Scottywood wanted to keep the XWF name alive in the fed and bring over the XWF title belts. But the talks did no go well, soon both sides stopped talks and Scottywood made a decision that months latter would look back on and be happy he made. He decided to restart the XWF, by himself, without RX and the DSW. The irony of this all was that the fed about to die, the XWF, managed to get back on their feet and keep going. While the fed, which was about to help, the XWF collapsed and never got off its feet. This now brought the NGW into the year 2002 and it’s 2nd anniversary. The XWF held it’s big PPV Out Cold with Scottywood vs. Hollywood Jeremy Davis for the XWF World Title with special guest ref Tony Weston. The title had been vacated after The Phantom Booker told Scottywood that world Champion Jason snow was a rip of the PCW Jason Snow. Now Scottywood had the match won until former XWF wrestler Triple H, now under the name Jack Parkman returned to the XWF and screwed Scottywood out of the match. Hollywood Jeremy Davis won the XWF World Title for the 2nd time in his career. At this time the NGW was now a member of the PTC-Prime Time Central thanks to Calvin Riley who told Scottywood about this great interfed promotion. Now it was February 24th, 2002. XWF’s Border Line, XWF Owner Scottywood promised a “Evolution” Well that promise was carried through and the XWF unveiled its name change to NGW-Next Generation Wrestling and a brand new web site. Also that night Bloody Roar won the 30 man Royal Rumble. After that the NGW began to gain steam and picked up great talent like The Charm, Time Bomb, Reggie Rivid, Total Karnage, Big Pete and others. Then weeks later the PTC was looking for a new fed to become a network federation. So the NGW, XCWF and DWO started to bid for that spot, started to bid for channel 9. Then it came down to PTC Malice on Sunday with The Phantom Booker, where he would announce you won the channel. Now The Phantom Booker lets feds call in and give one more reason why they deserved the fed. Scottywood decided to call in and live on the air The Booker gave the NGW and XCWF a network spot on PTC TV. Now the NGW has reached the big times after more then 2 years of hard work. Their next challenge is to create a new and better site for the NGW and bring the NGW out of last place in the PTC rating. Now after recieving the network deal the NGW has started to grow and sign more known talent like Steve Slash, John Hitchin, Axle, Scott King and more to come. NGW's orginal co-owner Ice now has returned to the fed and has plans to dominate the World Title divison. Now with the PTC remerging bigger and better the NGW has taken channel 10 and is ready for a new era in the PTC.
