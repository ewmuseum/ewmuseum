Called In The End, it was highlighed by three enormous main events. It was set to be four hours long, and in fact ran over to an extra half hour to the delight of the fans. Some major moments are highlighted below. In the first of the main events, former teammates Jason Kage and Eric Russo competed head to head in an 'old school match.' LOW being a company that thrives on extreme stipulations, this was an incredible change of pace, but the matchup did not disappoint. However if fans thought that they had seen the last of the Shadow Riders for the evening, then they were gravely mistaken. Next, we saw what some viewed to be the biggest World Championship defense yet, as Tristan Belmont took on Jesse Jamester. Jamester, fresh from the fWo's Survivor competition, was ready to break into the spotlight of LOW and show the world that Belmont's Warrior ways were outdated and useless. Did he get the opportunity? Finally, too much was riding on the line when Mike Meyhu fought Trigger. It was set in stone that the winner would take full ownership of Lights Out Wrestling, while the loser would never be seen in LOW again. It was the last match of the night, but of course there wouldn't be a clear ending... The show was jam-packed with quality entertainment, that included the crowning of a new Aftermath Champion, new Tag Team Champions, and a new Xtreme Champion, as well as the number one contender for the Realm Championship which will be decided in the coming week. It was a night of major surprises and debuts, including the return of first World Champion CADE, as well as former champions from FAW and 360WE. It was a night of major impacts, including the trial-matches of four free agents, competing for a spot on the LOW roster. It was a night that we will never forget... And it is a night that you should not miss!
