Clothesline using top rope Championship History: As James von Doom - GLOBAL Platinum Championship x2, UWF North American Championship, UWF Tag Team Championship (with Killer Dude), UWF Television Championship, UWF Insert-Name-Here Championship, UWF Jobber Championship As Darth von Doom - GLOBAL Platinum Championship As Deimos - LOW Realm Championship As Manager: managing Killer Pirates - PTC Tag Team Champinship, LOW Tag Team Championship x2, GLOBAL Tag Team Championship; managing "Big" Dick N. Uranus - UWF Television Championship History Biography: In Japan, James von Doom became VON DOOM and this changed his career. He became a star again. At first he wrestled as the mysterious Nimbus, a masked wrestler who hailed from the Oort Cloud. Upon unmasking, VON DOOM went undefeated. He earned the nickname 'Arctic Wolf' due to his cold blooded demeanor during matches. Also changed to a more fast-paced, spot filled, up-tempo style. Came back to America three months later. He damn near started a riot at GLOBAL's Dark Rituals PPV where he and other von Doom related wrestlers fought fans in the crowd. On GLOBAL Underground in early December he showed up and openly mocked everyone and made an open challenge to all PTC people who 'kick ass' to fight him while officially calling himself VON DOOM for the first time outside of Japan. He then signed with FUSE and made his debut, claiming he was going to 'destroy' the heroes of the fans due to their mistreatment of him upon his return to America. Character Traits: On the mic is very comedic and pokes fun at his opponent(s) and/or rival(s) with trash talk and comedy, however is the opposite in the ring. Once the bell sounds, he is ruthless. His recent interviews have drawn comparisons to movie characters Ricky Bobby from 'Talladega Nights' and Chazz Michael Michaels from 'Blades of Glory,' both played by actor Will Farrell. Feels at one with nature and is at peace when in natural surroundings. Hates Las Vegas but recently relocated to West Yellowstone, Montana. However, he is stuck living in the turbulent city still during the harsh winter months that Yellowstone experiences. It also helps with his travel schedule as well. Is well liked in Japan. Has a core fan base that loves him to death but is admittedly one of those wrestlers that you either really love or really hate. Known to be a comedian backstage, but also has a reputation for being a loose cannon, for sometimes being out of control, and for doing shoots. Overall Show/Match Strategy: VON DOOM will do anything to make himself appear weak to his opponents even though he is quite a cunning fighter and match strategist. He will feign weakness, throw matches to make his rivals think he is weak and not a threat to them, he will lie and make bogus alliances with people, lying, and often lures his opponents and allies into a false sense of security while simultaneously convincing them to turn on their allies and close friends. He makes complex webs of false alliances while only having a true alliance to a small people. He was the first wrestler known to intentionally lose competitions and was seen as weak by his fellow wrestlers. He also bizarrely seeks to be hated by the other wrestlers. He uses lies and deception after admitting to his fellow wrestlers that he would do so, lied about conversations and alliances and even lies about past experiences in his life. His straight talking attitude toward other wrestlers has made him a security risk as well. Regardless, VON DOOM is considered one of the most devious wrestlers in history.
