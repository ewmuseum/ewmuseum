BoneCrusher BoneCrusher (Born Bogard Xavier Crew on October 23, 1967 in Los Angeles, California) began his professional wrestling career on December 31, 1988 in the All-Star Wrestling Alliance in California after being spotted while playing junior college basketball (In feal life, the character was created in 1988 for a backyard fed called the ASWA). His speed and agility for such a large man (or for anyone of any size) impressed many. His imposing figure struck fear into the hearts of opponents. His fighting spirit (BoneCrusher has never tapped out or verbally submitted in his 19-year career) and drive to succeed has earned him the respect of even his harshest critics. BoneCrusher is a five-time ASWA World Heavyweight Champion, ASWA InterContinental Heavyweight Champion, ASWA World Tag-Team Champion, WCEW eXtreme Tag-Team Champion, xUw Television Champion, and xUw InterContinental Champion. BoneCrusher is a black-belt in different martial arts disciplines, including Karate, Dux-Ryu Ninjitsu, and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. BoneCrusher has played semi-professional basketball in the ABA and USBL, holds an amateur boxing record of 5-0, and holds professional records of 2-0 in boxing, 2-1 in kickboxing, and 5-1 in mixed martial arts. BoneCrusher has competed in the Golden Turnbuckles Tournament, and has even served as a Commissioner in the National Wrestling Federation. He runs a gym in Los Angeles that trains people in boxing, MMA, and professional wrestling. Two prospects from that gym have been successful in getting into the business, including Slick Nick McGrath and BoneCrusher's own nephew, "Corporal Punishment" Jonathan Medina (also known as Beret and Johnny "Blaze" Medina). Other wrestlers who were already in the business have went to train at BoneCrusher's gym as well, such as Lieutenant Ronin BlackKnight and former XUW World Champion and PTC Global Champion Saij. BoneCrusher is currently competing in Xtreme ULTIMO Wrestling. A high-altitude, high-impact Flying Elbowdrop from the top rope. It can still score some three-counts, but has become an alternate finisher. * F.E.A.R. Factor - Bo'Cru puts his opponent on the top turnbuckle, then climbs up. From here, he lifts his opponents, leaps off with amazing altitude and strength, and hits a devastating Dominator from the top rope, doing severe damage to the head and upper torso region. Has been known to cause opponent to bleed. Can be done through tables, onto chairs, and onto (or into) ladders. Introduced in XUW Gimmick For years, BoneCrusher has been the street-fighter from south-central Los Angeles. This is because the big man of Irish, Italian, Mexican and Native American descent actually grew up in south-central and had to fight to survive. Now though, he is a veteran of the ring, and multiple-time champion in many organizations. His combination of frightening power, incredible speed, agility and athleticism, "never quit" fighting spirit and expertise in martial arts and technical wrestling has made this seven-foot, three-hundred-plus pounder one of the most feared and respected professional wrestlers in the world. Outside the ring, BoneCrusher has become known as a consummate professional, and has been known to help younger talent improve their in-ring skill.
