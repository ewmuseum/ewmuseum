A lot of these men were then given jobs by the occupational forces to learn more about how to build such a strong and dominant military and what weaponry secrets these men and women might have. Walter Wilhelm Fleischer was such a man. The United States was such a suitor. The rest of Walter's life was spent working with the United States military as an engineer, and weapons expert. He passed his knowledge onto his son, Ulrich Matthias Fleischer who would use this knowledge to start the foundation of an empire. An empire that would be known as Zenith Corporation. Named Zenith after expecting nothing less than the pennicle of technological advancements, he immediately started working directly with the United States Air Force. On the grounds of this Ramstein Air Base a child was born on July 12, 1982. His name was Christian Ulrich Fleischer. The heir to the Zenith Corporation throne. Christian grew up with all the luxury's a family could have. A modestly large house in outskirts of Kaiserslautern, a couple of Mercedes-Benz automobiles in the garage, the best education money could buy. At the age of 12, due to the suggestion of the military Zenith Corporation and their employees were moved to a skyscraper in Chicago, Illinois as to better serve the country. A move which changed the life of Christian Zenith, bringing him to the land of opportunity. Young Christian thrived in the new home. Quickly becoming one of the more popular kids at school, and at home. Ulrich began to groom his son to be a great business man, sending him to a year round school for future businessmen. At the age of 14 Ulrich already had his young son running minor operations in the company. At age 17 Christian's charisma, absolute brilliant business sense, and intelligence had earned him Vice President of the Company with a unanimous from the board of directors. The business world came easy to the saavy young prodigy. He needed something more... he needed a challenge. It was at this time that Christian turned to the streets of his new hometown. His wealth and influence gathered the weak and fragile leeches to his side. His charm and charisma won over most of the more prominant members of the underground. He became almost worshipped as a God on the streets. His hands were in nearly every shady activity occuring throughout the city. As his wealth and influence continued to grow, so did his desire to be something more. In just a few weeks Zenith Corporation would be his. The shocking and horrific murder-suicide of his mother and father left the whole city in mourning for days. His companies line of work coming under heavy scrutiny. In two short days Christian Fleischer was named President of the company, quickly setting off a wildfire of alligations pointing at Christian as the ringmaster of a murderous coup d'etat cover-up. With no evidence and the police seemingly locked in to their version of the events, no indictment ever occured. Yet under his lead Zenith Corporation didn't take much time shedding the taint of corruption off of it's name. It flourished from just a military weapons manufacturer to also be one of the most recognized and respected insurance and health care providers in the country. Christian was a multi-millionare, and his company broke into the Fortune 500 just a year after he took the helm. Yes, these things came easy to Christian Fleischer... who legally changed his name to Christian Ulrich Zenith a few years after the passing of his parents. He still wanted something more. He missed the adrenaline he'd get from the crowds of followers in the underground. He missed the thrill of competition. A late night spent flipping through channels on the television provided the answer Christian had been looking for. Professional fighting. The crowds, the combat, the fame. It had everything he'd been dying for. A local indie fed wouldn't be good enough for the likes of him. He'd get private lessons from the best wrestling and vale tudo instructors that money could buy. After over three years of training, Christian marketed himself to various wrestling and fight promotions around the United States. Most just laughed in his face, thinking of it as nothing more than a silly publicity stunt for Zenith Corpotation... completely unknowing of how good he'd become. After beginning to think the inferior minds of the world had shot down the opportunity of a lifetime, Christian recieved a phone call from GLOBAL Wrestling. An offer that was soon accepted without much hestitation. It was then that the wealthy businessman shocked the world by proving his skills as a world class performer. Winning various titles along the way before being inducted into the GLOBAL Wrestling Hall of Fame. After being at the top for so long, Christian became bored with the lack of competition in the ranks of GLOBAL and retired from the ring into relatively obscurity from the pro wrestling world. When people speak of the career of Christian Zenith, it usually turns into a debate of what ifs. What if Christian Zenith faced a PRIME roster stacked with opponets such as Angelo Deville? What if Christian Zenith faced a GCW roster stacked with opponets such as Rich "The Renegade" Rollins? While his merit cannot be denied, many think Christian left the sport far before his time. Luckily for those people, while running his business he never really stopped honing his skills. One day it's almost inevitable that he will once again step foot inside the squared circle... that is... for the right proposition. He is an unparrelled businessman after all. His skills come with a cost. With an offer that can't be refused, it's almost certain that Christian will make an even deeper mark into the history of professional wrestling. Either inside the ring, or out of it. Category: Characters
