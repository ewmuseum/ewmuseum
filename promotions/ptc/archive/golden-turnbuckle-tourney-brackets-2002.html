Golden Turnbuckle Tourney: Brackets & Points System

25 Jul – 3 Aug 2002

Golden Turnbuckle Tourney Brackets to be Announced on Sunday ew24-7 Radio 8/03/02 ew24-7's Sunday edition will air at Noon EST/5 pm UK with the Phantom Booker... ...oh yeah, and the pairings for the opening round of the big 256 man Golden Turnbuckle Tourney as well. So be there foo! Source: ew24-7 Radio Golden Turnbuckle Tourney Fed Rating System? 7/27/02 PTC Officials announced today that they will impliment a point based system for the Golden Turnbuckle Tourney, and the top point gaining organization will be given a special team trophy. Additionally, the top Cable Affiliates in the tourney could find themselves promoted to Network status. "We want to reward those up and coming feds who make a strong showing in the tourney with increased air time on a national level," said PTC CEO Harry Balczak. "The system is solid and should insure great battles." According to Balczak, the point based system will work as follows. Wrestlers competing in the first round earn one point for their fed. Wrestlers winning in round one gain a point for the fed. Wrestlers winning in round two gain two points for the fed. Wrestlers winning in round three and beyond gain one additional point per victory per round. Points are culumative. For example, if Johnny Qtips of WWR enters the tourney and wins in the first and second round, before being eliminated in the third, he will earn one point for entering, one point for his win in round one and two points for his win in round two, to earn a total of four points for his fed. Note, that wrestlers who sign up and fail to show for round one will cause their team to LOSE three points. All fed heads are encouraged to get as many wrestlers as possible in the tournament to ensure their federation has a shot at the Golden Turnbuckle Team Tourney! Source: Signup for the Golden Turnbuckle Tourney! Golden Turnbuckle Tourney Signups! 7/25/02 PTC officials announced that signups for the much-talked-about Golden Turnbuckle Tourney have opened up early Thursday morning. "After all the hype, floods and everything else, we are ready to roll," said PTC CEO Harry Balczak. "The Golden Turnbuckle will be a massive tourney, putting all others to shame." Balczak said he expected 128 to 256 competitors. Signups end on Thursday, August 1st, and first round tourney brackets will be announced in a special edition of ew24-7 Radio at 10 pm Friday evening, August 2nd. All fed heads should encourage their wrestlers to get involved! Do you have what it takes to hoist the Golden Turnbuckle over your head and be called the best there is?
