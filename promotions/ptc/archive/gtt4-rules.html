GTT4 rules

2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is (written in 2026) The GTT4 tournament site’s own rules and prizes page (what, when, who, prizes, roleplay limits, the rules, and the “Fun Facts about GTT” list), as the donor’s database holds it (lot 0159). The page itself carries no date: it names Round 1 starting on Sunday 12 September, which is 2004, so it stood while sign-ups ran, in late August or early September 2004. No capture of the page is held. The page’s navigation and the markup of its headings are not reproduced; its text is.
What: PTC's GTT4 is a singles elimination roleplaying tournament. When: Signups are expected to run for 7-10 days. Round 1 Roleplaying will start on Sunday 12th September. Who: GTT4 is an open invitation tournament. That means anyone can enter, not just members of PTC feds. Pretty neat, huh? The tournament director is Argyle, PTC's owner. How: Registered entrants compete in the tournament by roleplaying against their nominated opponent. The roleplays are judged by peers of both entrants, and the entrant deemed to have posted superior work moves on to the next round. Why: GTT4 is provided by PTC for e-fedding enthusiasts to have fun competing against each other in matches they would not otherwise experience. An offshoot of that is for the possiblity of member feds being able to sign "Free Agent" players. An entrant is classed as a Free Agent if they are not a member of a PTC member fed. That being said, there is no expectation on any of the Free Agent entrants to sign with a PTC fed after playing in GTT4. If you like what you see in any of the PTC member feds, feel free to negotiate and join. If not and you are just here for the tournament, enjoy yourself on us. Get to the good part, what are the prizes for the winner? There are a number of prizes for the winner. 1. The winner is invited to nominate a charity of their choice, upon which long time PTC member Buddy D. will donate the sum of $US100.00 (one hundred dollars) to that charity in the name of the winner. PTC management would like to thank Buddy D. for this extremely kind gesture. 2. The highest placed member of a PTC Federation will receive a future shot at the PTC Global Championship. 3. The second highest placed member of a PTC Federation will receive a future shot at the PTC Extreme Championship. 4. The winner will receive a poser image pack, which they will be able to be used in their home federation, and which will be displayed in the PTC image gallery and on a splash page acknowledging the victory which will go into the rotation on the primetimecentral.net main page. Should the winners of the Global and Extreme title shots decline to take them, they will be passed down to the next highest finishing player. Other prizes may be added at the discretion of the tournament director. Cool... so how many people are going to be in this thing? That is the million dollar question. It can be confirmed now that the number will be a multiple of 2. That means, depending on the number of applications... it could be 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 or even 256, just like GTT3. PTC management would be comfortable with 128. However, if the number of applicants exceeds 256 by a significant amount, then the tournament will be run at that size providing enough judges volunteer. Damn, that's a lot of people... so how much work am I going to have to do each round? What's the RP Limit? That will vary each round. The first round will have a 1RP limit. The main reason for this is to weed out the people who aren't serious about playing, without overly taxing the people who are serious about playing. Subsequent rounds will see increases in the RP limit, at the discretion of the tournament director. It is envisaged that Round 2 will be a 2 RP limit, while the final round will be a 4 or 5 RP limit, with the rounds in between then in between that somewhere. Did you just say it could be 5 RP's in the final round? Why so many? Why not three like usual? A RP limit is a maximum, not a minimum. It is expected that come time for the final round, the players may well be quite spent of ideas. The extra is, just in case they aren't, to give them all the latitude in the world to be able to prove who is THE BEST in the game. That and that we don't want any old greasemonkey deciding where Buddy's hundred bucks goes to. Sounds complicated... but I'll go with the flow. So, how long is each Roleplay period? Round 1 will be five days. Any rounds with a 3RP limit or greater will be seven days. It is expected that two weekend days will be included in a full roleplay period. Okay, this still sounds like fun, I'm in baby! But first, what are the RULES? One Character per Handler: Handling two characters in this tournament takes away a spot from someone else. Anyone found handling multiple characters will have each character expelled from the tournament. Plagiarism: Anyone found to be stealing the work of others and posting it in this event will be banned from participating in any future PTC events, for life. No Recycling: All Roleplays posted must be original for this event. This means that they are not to have already been used in your home fed. If after the event your fed head allows you to use them there, that is between you and the fed head. Quoting: If you use snippets of other people's work, or quote song lyrics, or anything else that you didn't personally write, you MUST quote your reference. Linking: All Roleplays must be posted on the GTT4 website using a username and password provided by PTC Management. Roleplays linked and hosted elsewhere will not be considered during judging. Deadline Extensions: Are extremely unlikely to be granted. Time zone differences will not be accepted as an excuse. There will be countdown clocks posted on this website to determine the precise ending of the roleplay period relative to where the player is in the world. Login troubles will not be accepted as an excuse. It is the responsibility of the player to test the login and password combination given to them at the earliest possible time, even if they don't intend to roleplay at that point. The short of it is that the Tournament Director will be extremely unwilling to hold up the entire tournament for the benefit of one person, even if they are in fact being shafted by this not being done. Think Star Trek II - The needs of the many outweigh the needs of the few. Okay, I get all that... there has to be a catch, right? There's always a catch. I'm glad you asked. The tournament director reserves the right to: - shuffle any or all brackets at any time during the tournament. - provide the players with subject material or themes to acknowledge or address in their writing. Holy shit! I really don't like change. I want things to be nice and predictable. I want to know who I'm facing 8 rounds in advance, and I want to research them all right now. Don't do this to me! Did you ever watch a couple of series' of Big Brother? Notice how they introduce new things or change their routine each time to keep things interesting? Well, if some of these things are introduced during the course of the tournament, think of it like that. You sure drive a hard bargain! Uh huh. So, how come Free Agents can't take the Global or Extreme Title shots? These titles are reserved for members of PTC feds. They are a way for contenders to get their work read by others outside of their home fed. Also, PTC is an interfed, set up for the benefit of its member feds, so it would be a bit silly if a Free Agent held any of its titles. But I used to be in a PTC fed, I'm just not in one now. I've posted on the PTC forums for YEARS! Surely that must count for something, right? Surely you can make an exception for me? No. If you're not in a PTC fed at the time of entering the tournament, then you can't have the title shots. You're also not permitted to claim that part of the tournament prize pool if you happen to get signed to one half way through the tournament. You'll have to be content with bragging rights and the other prizes. Okay. Hey, I have to ask, what's with the mountain names for each bracket? Roleplay God 4 used North, South, East and West, so instead of copying them we went with something that matched the images on the layout. Pretty neat, huh? Oh, one last question, what's with the extra questions on the application form? You mean the no-show thing? Well, if there are more applicants than there are spaces, PTC management needs to determine the best way to work out which applications to reject. People with a history of no-showing PTC events may find themselves first in line to be rejected. Okay, I lied, one more question, how to I apply? Hit the Application button. If you can't find it, that means applications have closed and you're shit out of luck! Playins will be granted at the sole discretion of the tournament director if a player has no opponent due to double no-shows in the previous round.
