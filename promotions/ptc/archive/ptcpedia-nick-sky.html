Early Life Nick Bedford was born in Rhode Island with his mother Gena and his father Abdul. They were a poor family who lived in a apartments with no room at all. In 1988 Nick's parents, Gena and Abdul got divorce and left little Nicky is stress. At age ten in 1993, Nick Sky left his parents due to thir drug problems and left them a note saying: "Mom, Dad today I have to leave to leave you and your boyfriend do to your legal issues." Federations Nick Sky was in Ultimate Wrestling Association, Global High Flying Hardcore Wrestling, Ring of Honor, and the Pro Wrestling Acrobats. He was proudly loved by the fans and held the World Title and the secondary and third best titles in each federations. He also held the tag teams championships in each fed multiple times. He is currently in the Dream Wrestling Federation's Minor League. Career: Legacy Nick Sky made his wrestling debut in 2003 after meeting Ring of Honor's chairman Cary Silken. He was a big time superstar there and worked there for 2 years after retiring after being arrested for the being accused of having marijuana. He was in prison for 10 months after being bought out by Cary Silken. ROH: Ring of Honor After making it through his tough life in 2003, at age twenty Nick Sky caught along with Ring of Honor's chairman Cary Silken. In Ring of Honor Nick Sky met ROH World Champion George Broadway and they became tight friends. But 2 months later Nick Sky won a battle royal to become Number 1 Contender for the ROH World Championship. At the time Nick Sky and George Broadway were ROH Tag Team Championships. At Ring of Honor Pay Per View "Take No Prisoners" Nick Sky and George Broadway had to face off. In result of the match Nick Sky threw George Broadway 40 feet above down to the concrete floor. George Broadway's career was over after the match was called off to forfeit due to George Broadway's fall. The truth is George Broadway severely broke two of his legs. George and Nick's friendship fell apart. Nick still goes to visit George but George will never forget what Nick Sky did to him. 1 year later Nick Sky one the ROH Pure Championship and held both the ROH World and Pure Championships. He held both of those titles until his arrest late December after officers found marijuana plants in his house's back yard. Nick Sky pleas guilty after stating "that I don't use it, I just sell it." Still ROH great champion and their so called "Legacy's" career was all over due to nonsmart choices.
