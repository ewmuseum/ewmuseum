James von Doom Character List

captured 7 Mar 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the updated character list. Decided to only put currently active and/or living characters on here. Most of the rest of the characters are complete crap (except for The Broom, of course). So enjoy the madness that is the James von Doom universe. Good Guys Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean Captain Jack Morgan In the first chapter of the Black Ocean, Jack kills James Vaga on a small mountain on Illitharia in the first scene and obtains the Port Key to Hell. Then he and his crew decide that the best course of action is to find the Port Key located in Devonia that leads off world and take the Portal off planet. They want to do this to become the greatest pirates of all time. Once they leave Illitharia, they head to the Devonian port of Mammatus. The Mammatons are soon attacked by the Nephilim, causing the pirate and his crew to head south to avoid the battle. The only problem is Datth von Doom and his Ninja Army are waiting for them there. After killing all the Ninjas and evading Darth von Doom, the pirates escaped into the Devonian Northern Plains where they survived a Dire Wolf attack. They then ended up in the Hills of Satan where Wolfen met up with them and they ended up at the village of the Hulks. Shaman Hulk then told them of a prophecy about three men who would journey through the stars and reach the end of the universe. After this, the Pirates ended up in the Devonian Badlands where they survived a Giant Land Crab attack by killing the beast. They then end up in Goblin Valley where they fight off four Goblin warriors before witnessing the Knights of Rodhi attacking the Goblin army, thus giving them a better chance of sneaking into Rodhi at nightfall. The three pirates then are led by Jack into the Petrified Forest. There they witness a Hades Corporation employee surveying but decide to move on rather than investigate. After surviving walking through desert baking under the unforgiving sun, the pirates finally rest upon reaching the grassy coastal area only to have Hulk save Jack from being attacked by Zombie von Doom whom he kills. After killing Zombie von Doom, the Pirates waited until nighfall to sneak past the fortress of Omega Centauri and into the Kingdom of Rodhi. Upon sneaking past the Knightly guards, the pirate stole a small pouch of coins and the priates were safe until the clown broke wind. When the Knights heard this, they chased the pirates into the grasslands of Rodhi until they reached the Golden Throne. After the pirates escaped from the throne after a quick battle with the pirates, the Evil Ones circled the pirates. The clown used his white magic powers to help them escape to the Golden Stairs just as the Knights arrived and got into a skirmish with the Evil Ones. After escaping from the Evil Ones, the Pirates ended up running through the Valley of the Gods before finding the Port Key leading Off-World. Then they survived a quick skirmish with the Evil Ones before running to Oberon and leaving the planet through the portal. After exiting through the portal, they ended up in Metal City on the planet Ore. Once there, they were soon taken to the Mayor's office and he let them leave the domed city without incident. Upon leaving, they ended up in the dreaded Rusted Lands where they ended up in the ancient ruins of the planet's Great War. There they slayed a humanoid cult and the dragon they worshipped before finding the second Port Key and soon leaving the planet through the portal. Killer Klown from Outer Space In the first chapter of the Black Ocean, the clown and his fellow pirate Cave Hulk go with their captain Jack Morgan to Illitharia where Jack kills James von Doom and gets his Port Key to Hell. Jack then comes up with a plan to use portals and their Port Keys to sail the Black Ocean of the universe. Clown and Hulk agree and the crew heads to Devonia where the port key and portal on Earth are located. There, Hulk's good relations with the Mammatons proves useful until the Nephilim attack the port city of Mammatus! The three pirates then try to sneak south only to be confronted by Nephilim but ultimately saved by Mammaton soldiers. Thier Allies Wolfen Wolfen was a college student in Slovakia before he was hiking one night and bitten by a wolf. The next day he woke up and was a werewolf. He was ostracized by his family and exiled from the mountain hamlet in which he lived. Now, he is a monster on the prowl in the dreaded Hills of Satan. His best friend is the Cave Hulk and they hate everybody. After trying to use his new stalking technique, he is caught by Hulk and Klown. Wolfen then joins the Pirates as they journey through the Hills of Satan and to the Hulk village. There, he leaves the Pirates and goes back into the wilderness from whence he came. Will appear on Future Earth. Knights of Lagoon The Knights of Lagoon were forced to retreat from their home planet and go into hiding here after the Birds of Prey swarmed their kingdom at the behest of the Piscean Empire and plunged the once peaceful realm into chaos. After jettisoning from the planet in an escape pod, the Knights landed on Earth next to a wrestling show. There, they were followed inside by the Birds of Prey and survived only by pretending to be professional wrestlers. They did such a good job, however, that they got hired to do it full time. But only time will tell if the Birds of Prey and the Piscean Empire are done with their attacks or if this is just the beginning. The Hulk Family The Hulk, member of the Hulk Family, is really Adam who is one of James von Doom's best friends, they greeted the Pirates upon their arrival at the Hulk village. Shaman Hulk then told them of a prophecy of three men who would travel the stars before the Pirates went to bed and left the village the following morning. Darth von Doom Darth has been plotting his revenge for months and now has tracked the Pirates to Mammatus, there he and his Ninja Army confront the Pirates for one possibly final battle, after losing said battle he kills the zombies of his dead ninjas and then makes a deal with the Dark Man to stop the Killer Pirates from completely their quest of sailing the entire Black Ocean of the universe. Darth then corners the Pirates in Mammatus but they kill all of his Ninjas and defeat him before he can kill Jack Morgan. After waking up from unconciousness, he is attacked by the zombies of his Ninja Army whom he quickly kills. Then he is confronted by the Dark Man and he makes a deal with him to stop the Pirates.
