Joe Smallze — RIP, by Hardcore Hoefel

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Soulja Smallz was known over the entire internet as one of the greatest competitors to ever roleplay. In an extremely unfortunate incident, Soulja Smallz passed away on April 22nd, 2002. He died due to a drug overdose. The drug was methadone, which he took and April 20th, 2002....and his body never fully recovered. He ended up in the hospital on the 20th, his system was cleaned, and he was released. He fell asleep early in the morning on April 21st, never to regain conciousness. Aside from what the e-wrestling world knows of Soulja Smallz, I know a lot more. I am his cousin, known to you all as Hardcore Hoefel. My real name is [name withheld] (handler of FAW wrestler Hardcore Hoefel), and Soulja's real name was Joe Smallze. He was my cousin, both in character and in real life. We grew up together in New Jersey, and lived a city apart. We fought each other numerous times, as most family members do....but never held any grudges. His family moved him out of Jersey City when he was 13, in hopes of getting him away from the drugs and violence of our neighborhood. Unfortunately, he found the same situation waiting for him in Holiday, Florida. I guess wherever you go, the drugs....the violence.....you can find it. He got into more trouble in Florida than I think he would've living in Jersey with me. Law enforcement's a lot stricter by me, compared to hardly ever seeing a cop down by him. My cousin Joe Smallze liked to live the fast live....girls, drugs....you all know the life I'm talking about. The same life a lot of us have lived and some are still living. The lifestyle that has claimed a lot of family members, friends, and people we were close to....and at some points almost claimed our own. Most of us were lucky enough to live passed it.....Joe wasn't as lucky. Me and him used to get completely trashed all the time when we would meet up. Acid....ecstasy....weed.....a whole bunch of different drugs used to be in our daily routine when we'd visit each other. Some of my favorite memories of me and my cousin are some of our drug stories. But those same fun times is what eventually turned Joe Smallze into another statistic of today's society. That's what many look at his case as.....he was just another statistic of what happens when teenagers get too carried away with drugs. He overdosed on a drug called methadone, which is a prescription drug....but he took it illegally. I refuse to say he was stupid or be mad at him.....I really can't, seeing as how I've done most of the same dumb things that he did. I made it out alive, he didn't. His e-wrestling career was a pretty long and elustrious one. He e-wrestled on and off for a a little over 6 years, and was the one who got me started in e-fedding. He started his career in the NAWA and ended his career in Furiously Addictive Wrestling. He's fought many of the net's best roleplayers (many of whom are now retired), and has beaten most of them. He lost very few matches, and the one's he did lose were because he would be too fucked up to roleplay (something I've been guilty of numerous times as well). He was a strong competitor, and we followed each other throughout our careers. In August of 1996 we both created the interfederational stable The Legends....which came to be known as one of the strongest forces in e-wrestling history. In the beginning, Smallz carried most of the load. I was very new, and he took me under his wing. He helped me with my gimmick, with the quality and the length of my roleplays.....basically helped me become the best roleplayer I possibly could. Everyone who crossed path's with Soulja Smallz was left with a lasting impression of him. For the most part it seems that everyone either loved him or hated him. He just wasn't the type of person who would sit quietly in the background and wait his turn....he was always in the mist of things, always in the spotlight. That's probably why he won so many titles in his career.....he never wanted to wait his turn. He would always push and push until he got what he wanted....and never settled for anything less than his best. He was definately the closest person to me in e-wrestling and in real life. We teamed up countless numbers of times, and never lost a tag team match. He had just come out of retirement not even two months ago, and then his life was cut short. In his last match he was victorious against former-FAW Hardcore Champion Xander. He was scheduled to go against FAW's European Champion Skipster in a match to determine not only who would be FAW's first T.V. Champion, but also who would walk away with the European strap as well. But now FAW will never have a Television Champion, that title has been retired in the name of Soulja Smallze.....before ever entering anyone's hands. His final stable was The Legends.....the members were him, Hardcore Hoefel (myself), and Baz.....both Skipster and The Omega Fro were set to join us, but when Soulja died so did the Legends. He had a great life, and lived it to the fullest. Whether it was e-wrestling or real life, Joe Smallze always wanted to have the most fun he could in the shortest amount of time. So I guess you could say the thing that ended his life, is also a big part of what made him so great. Below are some thoughts and comments shared by some who knew Joe Smallze aka Soulja Smallz...... FAW European Champion, Skipster; I knew Joe for a short time, but from what I did know about him, he was a great guy and he will be missed in FAW as in the real world. I talked to him a few times online and worked with him in an angle. Joe was a great wrestler and always will be, even if he is gone. His body may be gone now, but his soul lives here in FAW forever. Joe was a great man, at least from what I knew about him and he will be forever missed. I know Joe will always be looking down on us, watching over us, and when his cousin gets online and checks things in FAW, Joe will be right behind him checking up on us. Joe will live in everyone of us, Joe is a great loss to the e-fedding world but he will never be forgotten. FAW International Champion, Lone $tar; Every body gotta go, but whay tha fuck in had to be that nigga Smallze? All tha times he took his falls, he still stood strong, let tha nigga be remembered by soul, close to heart, how long will dis mourn me? We know you'll be here fo us homie, rest in peace. In closing, it's obvious that Joe Smallze was not only a great roleplayer, but a great cousin, great friend....jus' an all-around great person. So this is what I ask of you.....if you pray, keep him in your prayers. If you smoke weed, spark a blunt in his memory. If you like to hit the bottles, pour out a lil' liquor next time your drinking for him. But whatever you do, do not forget the man, the myth, the LEGEND.....Soulja Smallz. LEGENDS LIVE FOREVA'....
