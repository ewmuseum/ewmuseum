Jay Draven First Appearance | Latest Appearance MSW Old School Saturday | SCCW SIN on SPIKE Federations | NWC(Various Affiliates) FUSE SCCW Age | Birthplace | Denver, Colorado Finisher | "Spinebuster" Wrestling Style | Power Technician Championships | MSW Tag Team Champions (w/ Michael Durant) HIW Shipwreck Champion (Inaugural) NWC North American Champion Handler | JJ Jason Ryan (Born Jason David Ryan in Denver, Colorado) is a professional wrestler and former manager best known for his time in the National Wrestling Council where he was also known as Preacher and Jay Draven. He is currently under contract with SCCW as Jay Draven and was a former member of FUSE as Jay Draven, JD Cross and Preacher. Career History Return to SCCW Ryan would make his return to wrestling as a member of the Sin City Championship Wrestling roster when he appeared in front of a capacity crowd at the SCCW show Snake Eyes. He followed this up with a victory over "Big Stack" Phillip Kennedy in his first match in over a year. He would then start a rivalry/partnership with "The Punk Rock Pixie" Charlotte Ramone that included teaming together at SIN on SPIKE in a match against Andre Jensen and Sameer al-Jezuri.
