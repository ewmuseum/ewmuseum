He is known for his obsession with video games to the point of making near-constant references to them, particularly Zelda and other Nintendo games. The Codemaster is way sexier and in no way as dumb in the face as Seymour Almasy. Early Life The Codemaster was born the third son of 1960s-70s-era wrestler Curtis "the Hammer" Brown, a pioneer of African-Americans in the "sport" of professional wrestling. Though he has little interest in becoming a wrestler, he eventually learned the craft on a bet from his older brothers to prove that he could be more successful than them. fans Wrestling organization Making his debut in the national scene as "the Codemaster", Brown started as a manager for the Fuse Bros., a tag team similar to the Mario Bros. He eventually grew frustrated with them and left to form a brief tag team with Ken War, before emerging as a singles competitor. After losing a tough ladder match to Craig Miles, the Codemaster surprised and defeated Zero to win the fWo Hardcore Title. The Codemaster held the title for three months, including a narrow title retention against "Nasty" Nick Brandish at Cyberslam VI, the Codemaster lost his championship to Lindsay Troy and then disappeared from fWo television for nearly a year. When he returned, the Codemaster first set his sights on Craig Miles in the effort of settling his score with him. Although he defeated Miles in a ladder match after striking Miles in the head with a flaming Keyblade, Miles held up Codemaster with a fake gun and made the Codemaster reevaluate himself. During this time, the Codemaster entered a feud with his cultural rival, the internet smark Keith Scott Zimmerman, over Zimmerman's Internet Title. The Codemaster scored consecutive victories against Zimmerman at Meltdown and the Wednesday Night Warfare afterwards, though severely cheated the second time around. The Codemaster's sudden championship loss to Alex Creed drove Codemaster over the brink, but then he found an unexpected new ally in Coral Avalon and assaulted Sonny Silver, leading to a tag title match between the newly formed "Blue Rogues" and the team of Silver and Lindsay Troy, which the Rogues won. Soon after, the Blue Rogues dominated the fWo's tag scene until the fWo's closure after a riot. Somewhere in that, they lost the tag titles to High Flyer and Rana Venenosa, too. I guess. PRIME After a long hiatus from wrestling, the Codemaster reemerged in PRIME with Avalon, reforming the Blue Rogues. By this point, the Rogues had more than just its core team; now including Mega Job, the Falk Brothers, and Alexandria Malone. The Rogues immediately challenged Danny Ferguson and Lindsay Troy for the PRIME Tag Team Titles, but came up short in their first encounter at Ultraviolence 2007, only to win the championships in a Tag Title battle royale the next week. After months of successful defenses, however, the Codemaster and Coral Avalon lost the championships to the team of Vangelus Olsig and Machavelli St. Romani. The team splintered soon afterwards. The Codemaster returned alone to PRIME, wearing a visor-like mask, and claimed Coral Avalon wouldn't ever be back in PRIME. Since then, the Codemaster has been a very rarely active character, only reemerging recently as a quasi-lackey of Nova.
