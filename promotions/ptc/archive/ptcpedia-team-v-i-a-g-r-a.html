Laurel, NJ Finisher | the Natural High Wrestling Style | Technical Luchador Technical Powerhouse Spotfest Monkey/Striker Championships | PRIME Tag Team Titles*2 IWO World Tag Team Championship² jOlt World Tag Team Championship² tSC Tag Team Championship Handler | [name withheld] In 2000, High Flyer & Tony Davis would form Team VIAGRA. High Flyer sold snow, Tony Davis sold nads, so why not put them together. A little known fact though, was that Rob Kestler also was apart of the group, turning Team VIAGRA into a rarely used Freebirds tandem. After Rob Kestler's turn against the group in October of 2000, Team VIAGRA simply remained High Flyer and Tony Davis for years, until Mary-Lynn Mayweather's entrance into the group in 2007. Since then, Mary-Lynn has helped Viagra in only one match, at PRIME's 2008 King of Kings event. Still, Mayweather is considered a full member of Team VIAGRA, and as such, she is considered a champion, in accordance with the Freebird Rule.
