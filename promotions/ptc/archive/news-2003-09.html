14 September 2003 -[PWF]- Show Results: 09/12/03 DARK MATCH Tag Team Match [Frank Castle/Mercenary vs. Bulldozer/Renegade] Summary: This match started off a little soft as both teams were felling each other out. Mercenary got tagged in and seemed to turn it up a notch with a few impacting moves. Later on in the match Renegade took the upper hand when Frank Castle went to reach for a tag and Mercenary blew him off. Renegade took advantage as the Mercenary left the ringside area leaving his supposive partner to fend for himself. Renegade drops Frank Castle with a power bomb and tagged in his partner. Bulldozer comes off the top rope with his patented "Wreckin' Ball" double leg drop and gets the tag win over an abandoned Frank Castle. Outcome: Renegade & Bulldozer, Pinfall. Match Length: 12 minutes, 24 seconds. FRIDAY SHOCKWAVE Show Segment: The started when you see Grim Reaper enter the arena with his belt and gym bag. Singles Match [X-Con vs. Doug Delicious] (Primetime Championship) Summary: The Champion starts off the match by winning the chain wrestling with a hammerlock. He taunts the crowd as X-Con reverses the hold until Doug Delicious works his way to the ropes. The match gets more exciting 7 minutes later when Doug Delicious is being rocked by seemingly endless offensive assault by the X-Con. The X-Con sets Doug Delicious up for his "The Chair" Death Valley Driver finisher. He lifts him up on his shoulders, but HeartBreaker comes running down the aisle way as the referee calls for the bell. X-Con is now being out numbered until his tag team partner The Hangman enters the ring and knocks down HeartBreaker and Doug Delicious. Then if that wasn't enough, out comes Boogie Knight enters the packed ring and helps out his fellow teammates as they dismantle the Demolitionz. Boogie Knight and Doug Delicious sets up a table in the middle of the ring and HeartBreaker hits the "HeartBreak Hotel" Frontface Power Bomb on X-Con throw the table. Outcome: No Contest. Match Length: 10 minutes, 45 seconds. Show Segment: No Limit is scene backstage getting coffee until Mr. Reliant walks up and grabs a cup. He says to No Limit, "It's a shame!" No Limit responds, "'Scuse me?" and he says, "Wasted talent!" as he hands No Limit his management office card. No Limit gives him a confused look as Mr. Reliant walks out of the room. Special Challenge [Tony Scarface vs. Johnny Rage] Summary: Tony Scarface came out after the commercial break, ranting and raving on why he hasn't been on television yet. He then challenges anyone in the back to a fight! Out comes Johnny Rage as he gets on the house microphone and had this to say, "You come out here and complain, but you have that the reason why you aren't on the television programs is because you suck!" Tony Scarface gets upset by the remarks and attacks an awaiting Johnny Rage who ducks out of the way by the attempted clothesline. Tony Scarface is being tossed around from pillar to post as Johnny Rage starts to feel it. Side note, Johnny Rage is not in wrestling gear! Tony Scarface seems to be about done, until he hits Johnny Rage with an obvious low blow as the referee calls for the bell. Tony Scarface hits the "Swimming with the Fishes" Vertebreaker on to a dazed Johnny Rage as he is left laying in the ring. Outcome: Johnny Rage, Disqualification. Match Length: 4 minutes, 23 seconds. Show Segment: Grim Reaper was interviewed in the backstage area. Before he could answer the question, "Do you think you can retain your belt tonight?" He was rudly interrupted by Mark Larmen. He grabs the microphone as he very clearly states that he will not interfere in tonight's main event. He says that he will be in the box suite viewing the rest of the night. He smiles as he walks away. Triad Match [The Prototype vs. Wraith vs. Sudden Death] (United States Championship) Summary: This match started off with The Prototype standing in the corner watching both Sudden Death and Wraith tear each other apart. He waits for his opening as he begins to attack Sudden Death from behind with breataking blows to the lower back region. He works in to a headlock as he is whipped in to the ropes. He comes off ducking Sudden Death's clothesline, but is connected with Wraith's big boot. Sudden Death and Wraith get back in to it as Prototype is regaining his whits. Fast forward a few moments, The Prototype has the advantage in the match as he continues the figure four leglock on Sudden Death. Sudden Death after 2 minutes of pure pain and agony finally reaches the bottom rope. The Prototype is forced to let go after the 4-count. He drags Sudden Death in to the middle of the ring. He turns to lock in the hold, but is met with Wraith's decapitating Fierce Clothesline. The Prototype falls backwards in to the ropes and bounces back facedown. Wraith flips him over and attempts a pinfall victory. The Prototype being aware, effortless lays his foot across the bottom rope as he saves the match. Sudden Death gets up as Wraith comes over and tries for another clothesline, but he sees it coming and catches Wraith on the return with a clothesline of his own. A few minutes have passed and The Prototype is still trying to regain his strength as he lays in the corner. Wraith is being set up for Sudden Death's finisher. Wraith is whipped in to the corner, comes walking out and ducks Sudden Death's Big Boot attempt. Sudden Death turns around and is whaled by Wraith's Fierce Clothesline. He gets up and is instantly knocked out as the echo of The Prototype's "Precision Kick" is heard throughout the PWF Complex. He falls on to Wraith and gets the win. Outcome: The Prototype, Pinfall. Match Length: 14 minutes, 07 seconds. Show Segment: As the show comes back from commercials, we see The Prototype talking backstage to the reporter gloating on his United States title win a few minutes ago. Out comes Sudden Death and complains how he was just screwed out of the belt. The Prototype explains that those are the rules of the match, the champion doesn't have to be pinned to lose the belt. Sudden Death has fumes coming from his head as he is hot. He attempts to take a swing as The Prototype, but he ducks out of the way and Prototype shoves Sudden Death in to the mesh fencing backdrop of the interviewing section. Both men roll around until PWF officials break up the fight. The camera cuts back to the ringside announcer's booth as they hype up the upcoming title defense and what brought on the challenge. The show takes it's last commercial break. Singles Match [Innovator vs. Grim Reaper] (PWF World Championship) Summary: Grim Reaper comes out to the ringside area with the prestigious PWF World title around on his shoulder. He looks up at the sky box and sees Mark Larmen waving back down at him. Grim Reaper finally gets in the ring and Innovator walks in his face talking smack. Grim Reaper smiles as he shoves Innovator back with his huge hand. Innovator comes back and gets in Reaper's face once again. The Reaper tries to shove him this time, but Innovator hits him in the ribs as he takes control quickly with swift shots to the ribs. Grim Reaper clutches his side as Innovator takes a series of cheap shots. He turns around to taunt the crowd and when he turns back once again to face the Grim Reaper, he gets laid out by a huge boot to the face. Grim Reaper nods his head in approval as he waits for him to get back up and drops him down with repeated clotheslines. Innovator gets up again and Grim Reaper scoop slams him straight to the mat. He falls down and begins to wrench Innovator's leg. Innovator screams out in horror as he squirms his way to the ropes. Grim Reaper takes his time as Innovator regains his vertical base. He corners him and gives Innovator a series of lefts and rights. The referee tries to break-in to stop the closed fist punching. As he distracts Reaper, Innovator hits an illegal low blow. The Grim Reaper falls down to his knees as Innovator starts to delivers the boots to him. Grim Reaper gets up by the help of the nearby ropes and Innovator kicks his legs out from under him. Seemingly for the last few minutes in the match, Innovator has grounded the big man with repeated submission hold attempts. Grim Reaper struggles and he kicks out of the modified side leglock with force as it throws Innovator through the ropes. Grim Reaper gets to his vertical base before Innovator. Innovator walks over to the Reaper as he hits Innovator back with a right hand shot to the face. It stuns him, but he comes back and kicks him in the gut. Innovator tries for another kick, but Grim Reaper dodges it and hits Innovator with a back hand and sends Innovator to the mat. He gets up and Grim Reaper knocks him down again with another stiff chop. He waits for Innovator to get up again and Reaper clothesline him to the mat. He follows the Innovator as he crawls to the closest corner. He tries to low blow Grim Reaper again, but he catches his foot and he shakes his head in disgust. While holding his foot, he hits him with a knockout back elbow shot that sends Innovator in to the corner. Grim Reaper leans in the corner and whips him to the far corner and closes in with stiff clothesline and Innovator's legs fling high in the air. Grim Reaper sends him in to the previous corner and Innovator comes walking out. He grabs him by his throat as the initial clench echoes through the building. Grim Reaper nods as he balls his left hand and points down as he lifts Innovator high in the air and drops him with an impacting Chokeslam. He gives the sign for his "Point of No Return" and kicks Innovator in the gut. He gives the signal once more as he lifts Innovator high in the air and out from behind, Mark Larmen hits Grim Reaper in the back with a crutch. The referee calls for the bell as Mark Larmen destroys the crutch on Grim Reaper's back. He grabs the microphone and says, "If you paid attention to detail, you would've noticed that, that wasn't me in the suite. I never entered that suite tonight and you fell for it. You thought I was telling the truth and now your clinching your back in pain and you ask WHY? Why? Because I can!" Innovator gets up via the ropes and continues to kicks a fallen Grim Reaper. Mark Larmen drops the mic as he joins Innovator. Both men double team the PWF World Champion until The Assassin holding a steel folding chair connects the back of the Innovator and swings wildly in Mark Larmen's direction, but he leaps through the ropes. The Assassin stands over the Reaper as he holds Mark Larmen and Innovator at bay. Outcome: Grim Reaper, Disqualification. Match Length: 18 minutes, 57 seconds. Show Segment: The show ends when The Assassin makes a challenge for next week in the direction of Innovator. Innovator nods as it seems to be verbally set by both men.
