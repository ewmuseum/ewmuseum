Huffman Handled by Sam G [[Image:{{{Image}}}|190px|center]] First Appearance | Latest Appearance VPW | XUW Federations | XUW Age | 20 Birthplace | Unknown Finisher | The Victory Lap Wrestling Style | High Flyer/Risk Taker Championships | XUW Television Championship (x1) [[:Category:{{{Images}}}|Images]] Huffman (born Ulises Theodore Huffman, on February 23rd 1987) is an English professional wrestler currently contracted to XUW. Although the place of birth for the character is billed as being unknown, it is widely recognised that he is an Englishman and was in fact born just outside of London. He is currently the XUW Television Champion and is undefeated in singles competition, successfully defending his title on four occasions. Early Career Huffman began his early career as an 18 year old in a small American promotion called VPW. The firm soon folded due to financial irregularities and Huffman found himself out of work. After spending two years on the indy circuit in his native England, he signed a professional contract with XUW in May 2007. XUW Signed in 2007, Huffman made an immediate impact on his debut at Reborn 2007, winning the Television Championship against Stone. His high-risk style and natural showmanship made him in instant favourite with the XUW fans. Wrestling Persona Huffman is portrayed as a hyperactive and charismatic superhero, peeling away on his own mis-adventures with his faithful car, The Huffmobile.
