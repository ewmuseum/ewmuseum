King Blueberry First Appearance | Latest Appearance SCCW Temptation June 13, 2008 | PRIME/SCCW Cataclysm September 19, 2008 Federations | SCCW Age | 29 (born 12/14/1978) Birthplace | Currently Unknown Finisher | The Jack LaLanne Driver Wrestling Style | Japanese Light Heavyweight Championships | SCCW LiveWire Title, Infinite Gauntlet Handler | [name withheld] King Blueberry (born December 14, 1978) is an American professional wrestler currently working for Sin City Championship Wrestling (SCCW). He is well versed in the Japanese light heavyweight style, and is commonly seen with his associates Blueberry Mask 14 and Blueberry Dragon, both of whom are also noted for wearing masks. It is rumored that these men, known collectively as The Legion of Blue, have a notable history in the now-defunct National Wrestling Council (NWC), but to date no information has surfaced to substantiate these claims. Career Sin City Championship Wrestling (2008-Present) King Blueberry appeared on the June 13, 2008 episode of SCCW Temptation defeating En Fuego. During the broadcast he debuted a short, pre-taped vignette proclaiming the merits of Cybernetic Abraham Lincoln With Nunchuks as a means to keep farmland safe from ninjas. On the June 22, 2008 episode of SCCW Temptation King Blueberry defeated both Simon King and Austin King in a "King of Kings Match". King Blueberry also appeared during the broadcast performing a poorly rewritten cover of "Blue Suede Shoes". For the July 20, 2008 episode of SCCW Temptation it was announced that King Blueberry would challenge a mystery opponent for the chance qualify for the Heaven & Hell Match at All In. With rumors abound, it was revealed that King Blueberry's opponent would not only be a mystery oppponent, but also Jadian Bridden. The king was victorious, winning via the Jack LaLanne Driver. After the match Hunter Sabuani made his return, attacking Bridden. Win/Loss Record King Blueberry currently boasts a 6-2 win/loss record, with only one loss in his home territory of SCCW coming at the hands of Wyatt Connors, number one contender to the Elite Championship in the Heaven & Hell Match. The second loss came at the hands of Prime's Kaiser Vashaun at Cataclysm. (This is widely regarded as one of the worst wrestler entrances on record.) * Noted Rivalries * Jadian Bridden (June 2008 - Current) * The Dead Man's Hand (June 2008 - Current) Championships and Accomplishments On the September 8, 2008 edition of Temptation King Blueberry teamed with Josh to take on champion Charlotte Ramone and Jadian Bridden. The rules of this match stated that whichever wrestler made the winning pinfall would be the champion. King Blueberry won the match, and the title, when he pinned Bridden following the debut of a new move, the Rolling Ripcord Cutter. As a result of this win King Blueberry is scheduled to wrestle the PRIME Intense Champion, Kaiser Vashaun at Cataclysm. On October 15, 2008 King Blueberry competed alongside 5 other wrestlers for the PTC Infinite Gauntlet, ultimately winning. He is the 23rd person to hold this honor.
