Garbage Bag Johnny Biography Biography Garbage Bag Johnny is not homeless, though oftentimes, people mistakenly identify him as a hobo. Rather, GBJ was a once intelligent boy who somewhere along the road, turned into a perenially intoxicated man. Often, the victim of his own hallucinations, Garbage Bag Johnny has little short term memory, lots of detatchment from the real world, a pretentious wordiness, and an ass kicking streak that catapulted him from Zero to Hero to AWC Transatlantic Champion. In addition to this, GBJ also really likes pudding. He wears beekeepers uniforms because he likes the material and style, although he cares not for bees. GBJ's spot in GTT6 is guaranteed. This is a lucky event for him because he would've been too absentminded to do anything more than claim his spot. In the ring, Garbage Bag Johnny is very new, but very skilled. Trained in the art of drunken brawling in parking lots and basements, GBJ's transition into the ring was remarkably smooth. In AWC, Garbage Bag started off with an impressive 11 match win streak that included his success in Zero II Hero, granting him a title shot at Coast to Coast. With the East/West roster split, Garbage Bag's contendership became complicated when the AWC Crown, held by MIA Adam Dick, and the AWC Belt, which Pierce Lavelle regained for the third time after a series of battle royals, were viewed as separate entities. GBJ's contendership was solely for the crown, making his contendership only half as legitimate as it was in the first place. On a Fresh!West special, though, Garbage Bag Johnny claimed #1 contendership to the belt as well, defeating 7 other competitors in an epic battle at Ruby's Surf City Diner. With both contenderships for Coast II Coast and an event that seemingly called for GBJ to wrestle in London and New Jersey on the same night, arrangements were made for a triple threat match at the Bermuda Islands. From there, GBJ defeated Pierce Lavelle and Adam Dick, uniting the belt and the crown, and winning the main event at the biggest show in AWC history while defeating two AWC legends in the process.
