"All F'n That" Jeff Harris Biography Biography Jeff Harris has just come off of an eight month prison sentence after he was wrongly accused of killing Bloodbox owner Simon Caine. While Jeff did actually intend to kill Caine he was unsuccessful as Caine had been seen walking around London, England looking to re-open his promotion. Before Bloodbox Jeff was in hiding after losing a seven year feud with his step brother Neu Generation. Jeff suffered extreme internal injuries and had died for several minutes before he was revived. Jeff paid the doctors off to tell the rest of his family and friends that his death was permanent, he did it so he could just slip away somewhere, grow old and die alone as he felt dead inside after losing the feud he had fought so hard to win. He was only forced out of hiding when Neu Generation entered Bloodbox only to punish and torture his son Shawn Harris, since Jeff ran him off he had found glory after winning the World Championship while feuding with the owner Simon Caine, trying to make him respect the wrestling business. Since being in jail he was released for one night under heavy guard to a reunion show for an old promotion he once worked for, he won the triple threat match against his closest friends Adam Masterson and Theo Solomon and went back to jail for several more months until he was released and signed to Fantasy Wrestling Federation. --- Sorry if that all seems a little jumbled Other Feds GCW
