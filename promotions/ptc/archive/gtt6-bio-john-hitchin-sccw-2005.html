John Hitchin Biography Biography In the late 90's, the indy wrestling craze had erupted all over Canada. John Hitchin vowed that he was not going to be left out of it, even if he was supposed to put his body at risk. Breaking onto the scene, in 1997, John put up losing numbers across the board, not being able to find any respect or recognition. But he kept at it, and eventually broke through in 2000, when he captured his first World Title with the DWA (Dark World Alliance). This brought the attention he was looking for as he signed on with former PTC fed, NGW (Next Generation Wrestling). In NGW, Hitchin took the bull by the horns and gave it the proverbial piledriver as he captured every title the federation had to offer, never losing any of them. Instead he just gave them up as he won the next. Eventually the NGW closed down, and Hitchin left there with a 53-0 record. Life beyond this federation, has resulted in a desk job for the 6'9" man. Day in…and day out, he's crammed into a suit…and he sweats about numbers instead of bodyslams. He hopes that coming out of retirement, and entering the sixth installment of the GTT as a member of FUSE, will revive his career, and bring back the glory days he once lived in. Other Feds FUSE
