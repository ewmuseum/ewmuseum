Elvis Hunt First Appearance | Latest Appearance JPWC | WWA Federations | Japan Pro-Wrestling Company World Wrestling Alliance Age | Unknown Birthplace | Las Vegas, Nevada [image: Image:Usa.png]' Finisher | Rockstar Headache Wrestling Style | Techno Brawler Championships | WWA Television Champion Handler | CJ Woods Elvis Hunt is an American professional wrestler who until recently wrestled for the World Wrestling Alliance, a former member of the Primetime Central circuit. Elvis was once a high-flyer who was a huge star in Japan, but after a chronic knee injury, Hunt ate and drank himself out of shape and gave up his dream. He is now a bitter, angry, rude and vulgar man and does/says whatever the Hell he wants. He's trying to get back on top of his A-game but some bad habits he has been unable to shake. He is still a formidable competitor despite the exterior, he is considered an innovator of offence, but also has aspects of a traditional old school wrestler, like Dusty Rhodes or Greg Valentine, still with a hint of the high-flyer he once was. WWA Road Agent Gordon Korth spotted him at an Independent show in Las Vegas in November of 2008, and signed him to a try out contract. He made his debut at Birthday Bash in his first competition in the company, and left the massive event as the WWA Television Champion. Hunt lost his Television Championship to Andy McMirra two weeks later, and upon failing to regain his title in a three way rematch at Revolution, he asked for a release from his contract and the WWA obliged, releasing the charismatic star on good terms. Early Life Elvis Hunt was a wrestling prodigy. As soon as he was old enough to compete he flew across to Japan to be the biggest thing since sliced bread. Unfortunately for Hunt he did his knee within three months of signing with the promotion Japan Pro-Wrestling Company. He was side-lined in Japan for 9 months while he healed, in the time he was lonely and ate and drank a Hell of a lot putting him out of shape. His Visa was cancelled and he moved back to Las Vegas and worked as a card dealer. His life falling into a hole, a down on his luck ex-class mate from high school found Elvis and convinced him that he should wrestle again and he should be his agent. Having agreed to Louis James' pitch Elvis Hunt threw himself back into the fray, despite being 30-40 pounds heavier than he should be. WWA Road Agent Gordon Korth spotted him at an Independent show in Las Vegas in November of 2008, and signed him to a try out contract. Career World Wrestling Alliance Elvis made his WWA debut at Birthday Bash X in his first competition in the company, and left the massive event as the WWA Television Champion. Hunt would later lose his Television Championship to a flawless Andy McMirra on the March 10th edition of Meltdown. Elvis Hunt gained a rematch for the TV title in a three way match, also involving Brandon Eagle at Revolution, but after failing to outlast the rookie Eagle, who left the event as the new Television Champion, Hunt requested a release from his contract days later, based on personal grounds, and the WWA obliged, releasing the charismatic star on good terms.
