Infinite Gauntlet

captured 18 Apr 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Infinite Gauntlet is one of the premiere events that occurs in Primetime Central. It's an ongoing series of challenges that tests the competitors involved. The current Infinite Gauntlet Champion is David Danny Morrison. History Infinite Gauntlet began in 2005, prior to GTT5. It ran on a weekly basis. It is notable in PTC history for being first avenue that Free Agents had to able to compete in PTC sanctioned matches.
