3 January 2004 [RPW] Sunday Adrenaline Live at the Halton Arena! 12/21/03 === 3 January 2004 [RPW] Police Statement Released & Poe Travels To Canada for Extensive Training? Police Statement Released. Six days after Adrenaline 7 RPW officials were in attendance as a Police Statement was revealed regarding the brutal assault that happened on the grounds of the Halton Arena. The Police Chief had this to say: “At first it was suspected that this was perhaps a fight between two rowdy fans that went way too far, however after five days of intensive care in North Carolina’s premiere Hospital, Lenoir Memorial Hospital, it was revealed that there was more to meet the eye with this case. The assaulted party was so badly beaten we could now only barely make out whom the RPW superstar was. He is none other than Jackson Rust.” A clearly bemused and distraught Rush Mason had this to say on the matter: “The repercussions of such a vicious attack, that has up to now kept Jackson bed ridden, are going to shake the foundations of this bond that has been developed over the past eight weeks. When I find out just who has done this there will be pink slips flying about. This is not acceptable. There is CCTV littering the entire arena, so much so there’s little chance that anybody is going to get away with such a mean spirited attack. All the evidence we have gathered right now are pieces of metal caught in wood work, presumably from a chase, and a tire iron strewn with blood. We already have some heavy suspicions and those shall be investigated within the next few days.” More from the RPW News Team as we get it will be made available. Poe Travels To Canada for Extensive Training? The RPW superstar, who as of late has been reportedly dealing with problems ranging from anonymous harassing phone calls to returning to the wrestling ring and making a bid for the world title while attempting to overthrow former friend now bitter rival “The Alpha Solution” Pete Maxwell, had finally decided to take the week off. Although going on a leisurely retreat to the coast was the last thing on The Shade’s mind. Instead, he decided to check himself into the Kombat Arts Training Academy in Mississauga, Ontario Canada.It is here that Gideon is currently honing his skills in everything from Muay Thai to Greco Roman wrestling. We managed to get in touch with Gideon’s vocalist and close friend who proved to be an inspiration when helping him recover from his short term paralysis, Trent Scott. “Well, it’s no secret that Gid’s has been doing a lot more training than usual. He’s been clocking into that gym they have at that promotions H.Q at 9am and clocking out at close to 9 or 10pm in the nights’ He’s been going on a much more restricted diet and he’s even doing Yoga again. Yoga for Christ’s sakes! I told him if he starts getting into all that Feng Shway or whatever you call it bullshit and starts moving around shit in his house, I’m going come down on him like a ton of bricks. The last thing I wanna start getting told when I go round to his is to step away from the broom closet cause it’s blocking his aura or whatever the shit they’re into is.” Trent said with a hint of sarcasm in his face “But yeah, he’s not into that stuff. He’s just been doing a lot of extra extensive training for obvious reasons” When asked what these reasons were, Trent continued: “Well put it this way, it’s not down to the cause of the paralysis. Short term paralysis stops you from moving, simple as that. It doesn’t turn you into a vegetable, the worst effects he suffered, aside from the damn obvious, was weight loss. I swear, forget these fat club shows. These people need to get short term paralysis, that hospital food makes to lose weight quicker than a 14 year old kid takes to jack off to the lingerie section of the Sears Catalogue.” When we asked Trent what the other reasons were for Gideon’s extra extensive training: “Well I thought that would have been obvious. As soon as the guy got the all clear from Rush Mason, and let me tell you his jaw dug a dent in the floor when he seen Gideon walk into his office, to return to the ring, he immediately went for Maxwell.” Trent then took a moment to chuckle to himself, before continuing to say “You know Karma came and kicked that son of a bitch in the ass quicker than any of us could have imagined. Maxwell spent so much time slithering his way into that main event scene and trying to filch that world title, that when Gideon returned and went straight for Maxwell, Gideon himself got tossed into that main event scene. Some people say it’s bullshit” Trent shrugged “ But hey, the wrestling industry’s a lot like the music industry. You do what you can to get what you want without selling out too much. Gideon’s done that, and hasn’t sold his soul to anyone. He means it when he says he’s done playing the nice guy. Always the start maker, never the star. That was him, not anymore. “ “That’s why he’s gone to Canada. Now that he’s in the main event scene he feels he has to turn things up 20 notches. He’s gone there to hone his skills, and come back prepared to go back into that war zone.
