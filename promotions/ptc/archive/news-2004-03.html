20 March 2004 4WW Presents Maelstrom III: Legacy The 4WW Presents: Maelstrom III: Legacy At the Grand Olympic Arena Los Angeles, CA March 28th, 2004 Who will climb the mountain? Main Event #1 North Wind Title Match "The Savior" Todd Dream [C] vs. "The Darling of the 4WW" Patti Anderson Special Guest Referee: Jaymes Phoenix It's been a long time coming. Patti Anderson gets her official second shot at Todd Dream. Both of her last two times in the ring with him have ended up with someone else interfering? Can Patti get a fair shake or will she be destined to have every odd stacked against her. Can the third time be the charm? Or will Dream once again prove that he's the best in the 4WW for a reason? Dream actually announced that he'd helped her out against the invading threat of FAW's Apocalypse on the last StormFront. Now, the following has changed. After long deliberation Fey McClancy has decided that a regular 4WW referee can't handle this. And with the announcement that Jay Phoenix is still under 4WW contract and will be Todd Dream's tag team partner in the upcoming PTC Tag Team Tourney, it's been decided that the honorable man that he is would be the best to give this one a fair shake and to keep it all in the ring. Main Event #2 4 Winds Elimination Match The 4 Winners of the Qualifying matches. The winner will leave with the 4 Winds Wrestling Title $100,000 dollars bonus money, a movie contract with TaleSmith productions And a guaranteed shot at the 4WW Title of their choice. Who will be the new top dog? Who will take 15 others to their representative limits? 4 men or women will get to this spot. One will leave with a check, a contract and a belt that is the ultimate 4WW trophy for the next 6 months. And not to mention could leave with a guaranteed shot at whatever 4WW Title they'd like to get their hands on. West Wind Title Match Fnord [C] vs. The Masked Avenger *Gimmick to be challenged by Masked Avenger The Masked Avenger is here, and he's after all of the evil doers who follow the Childresses in their attempt at wrestling world domination. Is it true that there is someone with more of a stick up their ass then Jay Phoenix when it comes to how Evil Ian Childress is? OR is he just a plant? It really doen'st matter right now. What does matter is that he earned a shot at one Fnord on the last Tempest and he's yet to make his challenge as the challenger for this match. What will be the gimmick selected? Is Fnord ready for a battle of this magnitude? **Card will be updated once the gimmick chosen is known** East Wind Title Match Featured Attraction [C] vs. Perfect Pleasure The East Wind Titles recently changed hands and gave the boys of Featured Attraction a total of 5 belts between them. Two of them are Jerms and Mike Marvelous. And the only other full time tag team on the roster after Featured Attraction was done with them all is getting their shot at possible greatness. Featured Attraction have been a corner stone of the 4WW for months now, and it's only time before these two men get their shot at the big time. What will mean more to them? The tourney? Or these belts? No one can discout Slade and Bullet in their own right. The two have proven their loyalty and worth to the 4WW roster. This one could be the sleeper of the night. Keep your eyes open. Fatal 4 Winds Qualifier match #4 4 randomly drawn contenders The last batch of 4 will compete here for their shot at the Tourney Final and the prizes at the end. South Wind Title Match Josh Starwind vs. Winner of the SWT Contender's Open Josh Starwind has proven himself again and again on the roster. But rumors are flying about with him being connected to some other member of the 4WW roster. He'll not know whom his opponent will be here, but it could very well be such people as Sean Summers, Bullet, Jerms, Fnord as the mainstays. Or maybe newcomers like Chris Wrestling or "SuperStar" Eddie B. will come out to challenge him... who will want to take part in the SWT Open and get a shot at the Death Scythe Outlaw. Fatal 4 Winds Qualifier match #3 4 randomly drawn contenders The third batch of 4 will be competeing here for their shot at the ending co-main event and the 4 Winds Prizes. Tempest Title Match "Simply Stunning" Sean Summers [C] vs. "Canadian Perfect" Chris Wrestling It's been coming for a few weeks now. Chris Wrestling, Sean Summers. Tempest Title. Chris Wrestling is a hungry newcomer here who targeted Summers even before he'd gotten a match. Then he took on and grabbed the opportunity in his first match to secure a shot at him at Maelstrom III. Will Chris Wrestling be the first one to put a chink in Featured Attractions Armor? Fatal 4 Winds Qualifier match #2 4 randomly drawn contenders The second match in the Fatal 4 Winds Tourney will take place here. Who will it be? SWT Contenders Open Paticipants TBD Unlike some federations who don't know how to do an 'OPEN Invitational' type Match, the 4WW does. Any and all interested can come out to the ring and enter at any time before the bell rings! The winner will go on to face Josh Starwind for the South Wind Title later on in the night. Fatal 4 Winds Qualifier match #1 4 randomly drawn contenders The 4WW will start off Maesltrom III: Legacy with the begining of the 4 Winds Tourney. Who will be in each match? Who will take the final prize? Tune in and find out. Confirmed members participating in the 4 Winds Tourney: Mike Marvelous, Jerms, Bullet, Sean Summers, Chris Wrestling, Fnord, The Masked Avenger, “Superstar” Eddie B. and many more! Who could return? Who could show up? Only time wili tell here. === 26 March 2004 WMW - Dess to retire after Survivor The new rumor going around the backstage area is that former Great Lakes Champion Dess is planning on retiring after Survivor. Originally, it had been noted that he may be leaving WMW to finish his career in nWc. However, now that nWc has announced it's closure, expect Dess to announce his retirement after the Survivor PPV. Source: Wrestling Midwest === 26 March 2004 WMW - New signings for Fuel [3] We'd like to announce the signing of 7 new competitors, including 1 tag team. Wraith, Big Red, Hell Seeker, Ska, Slayer, and the Alaskan Predators have been added to the roster and signed to Fuel. Ace and Wolf were given a second tryout match. Sean Quinn and Chris Steel have been released. Source: Wrestling Midwest
