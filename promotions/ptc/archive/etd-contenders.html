ELOcalc.zip Rules for using ELOcalc: Select the Age of Mythology setting, then put your points into OWN TEAM. Put your opponents points into OPPONENT TEAM. Select the winner (Own or Opponent) under the winner dropdown menu. Click on Calculate and it will show you the way the scores would change. Special stipulation for multiple person matches: The total points that could be won facing all opponents will be totaled and then averaged. Example: Triple threat match Contenders have 1200, 1100 and 1000 points total. Contender 2 (1100 points) wins the match. Contender 1 looses: would have lost 32 points. divided/averaged to 16 point loss. Contender 3 looses: would have lost 18 points. Looses 9 instead. Contender 2, the winner gains those points to total a gain of 25 points. Special rules will be made on a case by case basis dealing with battle royales or other types of elimination matches (usually to make this fair, a bank of points will be setup if the standings need a shakeup. Rules to be posted if needed) No Showing a match will result in a double penalty to loss of points! Winner will still gain the points he would have gotten from winning the match normally. Those who do not show any activity after a 6 week period will be passed on the division by others waiting to get in. There is a running limit of 20 members at a time. Self suspension will freeze your points and allow you to bypass the above ruling. Being suspended for inactivity will reset your points to the baseline 1000 upon return. Competing in other PTC divisions: The ETD is a voluntary basis. However, you may be restricted by the other division heads. The holder of the ETD can not compete for any other titles. Contenders may, but they risk being a voluntary suspension for the period of the contendership, and if they hold another division title, they will be considered a voluntary suspension. Currently members of the Tag Team Division have an exemption for the previous ruling. This may change as the status of that division changes. Character information for the Offices of the Extreme Title Division. Currently the in-character head of the Extreme Division is Penelope Dawn Preest. She has been a wrestling manager for a long time, having managed and valeted such wrestling greats as the GIWF's Ace Thunder, The GIWF Hall of Fame winning tag team, Tropical Storm. She is the actual sister of the late (GIWF) Maury "Twizted" Preest and Jamie Stenson-Preest (Ace Thunder's real name). She knows Hardcore after growing up with her two half brothers. She has also wrestled a few matches in her time. Penny, or Ecstasy as she was called, is, to put it bluntly, not into men in the slightest. She is a tall (just shy of 6 feet tall) and statuesque blonde haired and blue eyed woman. She will usually appear at events promoting the Extreme Title and other PTC cases.
