10 May 2002 [LCW-evo] Joe Black on Dr. David Chambers The evo champion talks to stuffed bear. Remarkably pathetic isn’t it? But welcome to the state of wrestling as it is today. Psychopaths come at us from every direction. A normal individual, and by that I mean someone who does not communicate with inanimate objects, think they are God (more than usual), or display symptoms of MPD, comes along as rarely as I get a piece of ass anymore. But on the other hand, normal is boring. Normal is predictable. Normal is not what people want to see. So bring on the psychos is what everyone yells. The more the merrier. So allow me to introduce to you, LCW-evo’s premier psychopath and evo Champion, Dr. David Chambers. Doctor David Chambers first came our way 3/27/01 where he held an interview for us spewing some nonsense about having seen everything. His hair was a mess, he was wearing hospital scrubs, and carrying around a teddy bear. He’s definitely come a long way, but the bear hasn’t gone anywhere. It was at the Suicide PPV on 4/08/01, after Doc just won an elimination match to determine contendership for the LCW’s Universal Title (Third tier title, ranking below the National Title) that we were revealed just how wacky the Doc was. The bear talked. Of course it didn’t actually talk. But Doc would rant and rave like a standard lunatic making no sense, and then finally, he would raise the bear up in front of his face. Then the bear “talked” to us with the calm and composed voice of a psychoanalyst. The brains behind the operation were revealed. 100%, Grade A Crackpot. However, the bear was pretty damn rational even if the Doctor was completely out of it. It was clearly the bear who was carrying the Doctor around, which explained how someone with barely the means to communicate with other people could have possibly negotiated his way into LCW. If I could meet the individual responsible for that, I’d smack him upside the head. The Doctor, who tended to change his last name for every match, Dr. Flagg, Dr. Boyd etc (but not even once to Dr. Doom to my dismay), continued to wrestle matches and tended to win more often than not. Not unlike a wild animal, he possessed a ferocious fighting skill and tended to only be slowed by the most devastating of blows. The loony did indeed whup some ass. But eventually a method was revealed to generate the madness. The Doctor was a psychotherapist in his life previous to going absolutely ape-shit. And the bear was guiding Doc through therapy. After each pleasant explosion of violence, displayed for all to see on LCW, the Doctor became more lucid and less prone to drool everywhere. And the bear began to bring the Doctor back to himself. Which has got to be the absolute stupidest logic I’ve ever heard of in my entire damn life. But what the hell do I know? Common sense has absolutely no place in the same thinking as psychoanalysis. But it happened that way. And if that was the intention, it seemed to work. Doctor eventually remembered his name. And eventually was able to communicate with people, and he did behave in a more or less like a normal individual. But a very quiet, shy sort of guy, who tended to let the bear do the talking and thinking for him more often than not. But eventually it all led to finding out what the Doctor’s damn problem was. The Doctor had a wife and a daughter. What’s important to note about the girl is that she had a similar stuffed bear who would often tell her family what “Bear” was thinking and feeling as a means to express herself. “Bear says that he doesn’t want Cap’n Crunch for breakfast. He wants Lucky Charms!” One evening, Doctor Chambers home was broken into by- care to guess? A loony, very good. Whose identity is still somewhat mysterious. He subdued the family, and, pardon me but there’s no gentle way to talk about this, killed both Doctor Chambers’ wife and daughter. And it destroyed him. He broke into the psychiatric hospital where he worked and stole a teddy bear from a patient there before vanishing into the woods for three years. But in spite of all this, he still had a distinctly rational mind, and a stuffed bear. But most importantly, he had a drive to continue living. Without a doubt, Doctor Chambers life was wrecked. He lost everything, and he began to lose himself. And what makes the Doctor special is the fact that this sort of thing kills people. It drives them to suicide. It drives them to shut the world out and waste away. Yet the Doctor remained. He evolved if you will. He returned from his regressed state of subhuman mindlessness. Many people consider him to be a joke. After all, the evo champion carries around a stuffed bear. Some go as far to question why this man is even a wrestler. The Doctor is what he is, and that’s all the explanation you really need. Because it’s about strength. It’s the strength to keep living and regain his life and memory that was the Doctor’s greatest challenge, not any man he’s faced in the squared circle. And someone who possesses that kind of strength can excel at any damned thing that they choose to do with themselves. Because the challenges of his life will never surpass his absolute darkest hour. No one can kill his wife and daughter again. More so than any craziness that the Doctor experienced while he was still a raving loony, he displayed such resistance because all the suplexes in the world will feel like pecks on the cheek to someone who is in that much agony. And that’s not all that unique. We have here the standard psychopaths in the wrestling world who all claim the same thing. “You can’t hurt me since I’ve been to hell and back and I bought this t-shirt while I was there” and other such loads of crap. But the Doctor never talked about it. I don’t think the bear even talked about it. You made the connection to what made this loony tick after you knew why. And while the standard psychos seem to be proud of their alleged intimate relationship with pain, the Doctor made it clear that it was NOT worth it. And that if he had a choice, he’d damn sure keep his wife and daughter in the land of the living. The Doctor possessed the strength to come back to us. The strength to heal. And a great number of people were probably relieved. They didn’t have to fight that animal. They now faced this mild mannered alter ego of the mindless lunatic who could clearly feel pain like everyone else. Cake walk, right? Wrong. The Doctor kept winning. He had his friends and tutors of course. He had the bear still. But more so he had the capacity to improve. Drawing from the amazing struggle just to be able to remember who he was and what happened to him, he had the ability and the strength to get better and better. Doctor David Chambers defeated Dean Wallace to win contender status on Evo’s Impulse 1.2. On impulse 1.5 he won the title from Duel in a hardly gratifying victory by DQ under special stipulations. It just goes to show that special stipulations always lead to something stupid happening. At Dawning, Doctor David Chambers defended his title against Dean Wallace, drawing a submission with Anesthesia after distinctly popping Wallace’s shoulder out of joint with the hold. Which is pretty brutal, but the guy should have tapped. And what will start to scare the remainder of the evo roster, as well as the wrestling world, is the fact that Doctor David Chambers isn’t stopping anytime soon. Doctor David Chambers has yet to fail to surpass himself. His strength, the stuff that allowed him to crawl back to the land of the living from his personal hell has made him like a god among men. And he’s getting better. The Doctor will no doubt continue to be the evo champion, ceaselessly winning title defense after title defense. He’ll continue to display that he’s made of a more durable material than most men. And if there’s anyone on the evo roster who can take his title from him, I don’t know who it is. Joe Black, evo commentator. Source: LCW evo === 11 May 2002 The First Ever SMASH Hardcore Champ most hardcore defense ever! Source: www.dominantwrestlingonline.com === 18 May 2002 Mercury: Who's who? There are sixteen active wrestlers in Mercury. Who are they? What are they involved with? The Oblivion: The primary heel stable, which has caused headaches for the entire federation since its beginning nearly two and a half months ago. Its members are... Gol- Mercury champion, and one of LCW's rock-solid veterans. Has every skill one could ever want in a champion, as well as a few not many people wish he had. Magnum Wolf- At times he seems to be the de facto leader of the group. Magnum is certainly the most outspoken, and is the focal point of all Oblivion opposition. Former North American champion. Hollow- In a past career he was a nigh-invulnerable LCW champion. Now, he's undergone a serious change, both with his outlook and his incredibly harsh wrestling style. As the group's enforcer, he doesn't care about the glory, only the combat. Rush- Recently returned to Mercury after vanishing. He's fought EEI for much of his career, and now he's doing it alongside the rest of Oblivion. Killjoy- Not much is known about this newcomer, besides the fact that his past is linked with EEI. EEI: A reformed, somewhat reluctant face stable. Going into Suicide they possessed all the gold in Mercury, but can that last given Oblivion's desires? Dirge- North American champion and one of EEI's founding fathers. He's undergone a serious change from what was once seen as a comedy act, and today is one of the toughest competitors in the world. Pariah- The newest member, the most recent Mercury champion, and one of the most determined anti-Oblivion wrestlers in the company. At times his behavior is erratic, but don't mistake that for weakness. Vincent Picasso- Perhaps the most consistently successful wrestlers in 2002, Picasso has nearly singlehandedly dominated the tag team division; he's been one-half of the tag team champions for an amazing three and a half months! Brimstone- Dirge's best friend and the foundation upon which the stable is formed. Johnny Glamour- A member of the Elites team along with Picasso. Unaffiliated wrestlers: Geoffrey Slate- Of late, Gol's greatest rival. Paranoid at the prospect of being screwed by Oblivion any more than he has, Slate wants nothing more than to drive a stake into the stable's heart, while at the same time gaining the Mercury title. Harbinger- At first, it seemed that he was the only 'big name' in Mercury. After an incredibly long reign as champion, however, he was quickly overtaken by nearly half the roster, and now he fights to establish himself once more, both in Mercury and in the PTC. Robert Young- While he's been around just as long as Gol, Robert was only rarely taken seriously. Today he's focused, and determined to defeat the best that Mercury has to offer. Jin Akujin- A rising star in the company. Jin doesn't have much flash, but his unmatched athleticism and incredible fighting spirit have made him a crowd favorite following two outstanding matches with Harbinger. Inferno- Also a rising star. After ending the career of veteran Blaze McCoy, Inferno is ready to make a name for himself in the company. "Iron" Jack Isiah- Recently debuted, but also the oldest wrestler in Mercury, Jack just might be the 'oldest' rookie sensation around. === 20 May 2002 [Shockwave] Kliq's Korner The first edition of Kliq's Korner is now posted at Shockwave Wrestling. Source: Excite Pro Wrestling === 31 May 2002 The Informant THE INFORMANT #1 "Hello everyone I am the FAW informant and I'm here to give you my first column of the month. Many changes happened last Sunday on the May Day Massacre: No Laughing Matter Pay Per View. So lets start. Handicap Match Syd & Sabatash vs. Baz This match had a lot of hype thrown into it with Baz bullying Syd after his promotion from FAWA. Sabatash stepped in on Syd's behalf and as his trainer. The weeks past and Baz continued to pick on Syd with Sabatash continuing to step in until the match was signed. The match itself was a high key affair good crowd reaction and I liked the offense shown by Syd. If pushed right he could become a household name in FAW. Baz showed me something in how much he has improved since his return at Vendetta, he has become more evil and more uncaring towards his fellow man. However Sabatash and Syd won this match via pinfall but what shocked me was the aftermath of the match when Syd turned on his teacher beating him into the mat. Rating: 7/10 Womans Championship Match Courtney Charisma Carter vs Mystery Oppoent The highlight of this match was thinking who would be the mystery opponent, smart money was on Jean Deas but to everyone's shock Tanya V came out to challenge Triple C. The match was very short and basically just a catfight. A mistake by Triple C allowing Tanya V to score a upset pinfall to become new FAW Womans Champion. Rating: 3/10 Hardcore Battle Royal This match came after Xander ran in after a Hardcore Title match between Apocalypse and Violator. Xander attacked the newly crowned champion resurrecting the forgotten 24/7 rule however he couldn't get Apocalypse pinned. From Xander's lead this provoked Violator, Trent Hunter and Chimera also attempting to become the Hardcore Champion. On an episode of Panic Xander pinned Apocalypse to become champion but then lost it twenty seconds later to the man he just pinned. Xander took out his anger by tossing a tin of Spam at Apocalypse missing and hitting President JLC. after this JLC booked a fifteen minute battle royal and after the match the twenty four seven rule would disappear from FAW forever. This was one of the funniest matches of the night and one of the most confusing. The humor factor is something FAW has lacked as of late but this match made up for it, it had everything blood, sweat, midgets. The match itself had fifteen title changes with Xander, Violator and even FAW interviewer James Harris pick up the title. But at the end of the night a pinfall as the buzzer sounded allowed Violator to become the FAW Hardcore Champion for a fifth time. Rating: 8/10 Lone $tar vs. Kevyn Vernon A match that pitted newcomer Kevyn Vernon against former International Champion Lone $tar. This match was the most unexpected of the night. Signed on Panic just pays before it gave neither man much time to prepare. The match went back and forth with both men pulling off some incredible moves but after a reversal Vernon scored the win and a great boost to his start in FAW. Rating: 6/10 Tag Team Turmoil Shifty & Joe La Fontaine vs. Mass Murders vs. FWO © vs. Bryan Statis & Hardcore Hoefel vs. Chris Thunder & Prodigy When you have five teams all going for one goal, the FAW Tag Team Champions you will have turmoil and thats exactly what we got in this match. The self proclaimed coolest team in FAW were first to the ring and critics had Shifty and Joe La Fontaine as the favorites. There opponents were the former FAW Tag Team Champions Mass Murderers. Shifty showed a great bit of agility early on in the match hand springing off the ropes. Black Ghost and Nightmare King proved they were better, bigger and badder than they were before. The match went back and forth until The Murderers hit a double face slam on Shifty and Nightmare King picked up the win for his team. After the match Shifty and Joe La Fontaine took out there aggression on The Murderers and eventually got them counted out. Bryan Statis and Hardcore Hoefel were next in followed by the Champions Neo and 9th Wonder Of Foam, the FWO. The match was back and forth and Statis and Hoefel isolated Foamy in the corner and took advantage of this. After Foamy tagged in Neo, Neo took control and used some high powered moves to score a victory from his team. The newly formed team of Chris Thunder and Prodigy looked promising and if this team get a few wins under there belt they could be in line for a Tag Team Title run but May Day Massacre was not going to be there day as Prodigy was covered by Foamy. Rating: 7/10 Stipulation Match Jim "JLC" Phoenix vs. HaHaKlown The return of FAW legend HaHaKlown at Breaking Point made every FAW fans hair stand on end. HaHaKlown sets his target on President JLC and after weeks of bickering between the two the match was signed. But there was stipulations. If HaHaKlown had won JLC would've been gone from FAW and the presidency of FAW would be decided in the main event of the evening, however if JLC won HaHaKlown was fired from FAW and JLC would still be in control of FAW. However it was not to be Jim's day and after a well worked match that sent pulses racing JLC lost to the ledged closing a page on the man that was Jim Phoenix. However JLC wasn't going to out without a fight and after numerous near falls HaHaKlown upset the world and for the first time that night the arena was silent! Jim Phoenix made sure he said goodbye to the people who made him the man, the ledged and the icon of FAW. Rating: 10/10 Six Man Tag Team Match New School vs. Old School This match came around between a bitter rivalry between Spawn and Tony Vigilante. But then British Bomber pushed himslef into the match as a self proclaimed leader of Old School. Bomber teamed with Spawn and Skipster to take on Tony Vigilante, Shawn Herrera and Kyle Kilbane. Then add the value of the winner takes the company and you've got a great main event. But on paper the event looked spectacular but it was far from. With the heat between Courtney and Tony before the match we all new how it would end with a good old screw job. Tony Vigilante takes control of FAW and we are at his helm. The match did have its good spots and before Tony was confronted by Courtney I thought the power struggle between Spawn and Bomber would push Spawn over the edge and he would return to the hell master he once was. With New School gaining the victory over Old we can only wonder what next months Pay Per View will hold. Injury's With the Pay Per View just over on the EEL network four FAW superstars suffered minor injuries. Baz suffered mild bruising to his ankle but isn't expected to miss any T.V time. Shifty suffered minor head knocks but isn't expected to miss any T.V time. Xander suffered 2 broken ribs but isn't expected to miss any TV time. Violator pulled his hamstring and is expected to be on Distortion. Chad Winters & Fox The reason Chad Winters was backstage last night at May Day Massacre is he was there to promote his new fitness video "Be The Chad". Chad Winters signed a 2 year deal with the Fox network and will be appearing in an episode of Futurama later this year. Panic Reviewed Panic the show now in it's new timeslot of Wednesday on the EEL Network. The revamped Panic set looked amazing and the new logo added to the occasion of change. The titentron showed us clips from the main event at May Day Massacre and then we were joined for an interview with Tony Vigilante and new school which got major heat from the crowd. After a brief commercial and a backstage skit between Xander and Shifty we went into the first match. Chimera went into the match seeking revenge on the blind attack from the past pay per view. The match went back and forth until New School came out and threw there weight around by firstly costing Chimera the match then firing him. After this the event went to a commercial and then came back to a backstage skit between Xander and shifty which saw Xander stealing Shifty's IN Championship. The skit was the funniest thing either man has done since arriving in FAW nearly five months ago. We were then given two backstage skits by Mass Murderers and then Lone Star. Hour two kicked off with Shifty taking on Trent McCall in a non-title match. Shifty won the match via the Shift Slam then heading backstage in pursuit of Xander. New School then made a mockery of a tribute video to JLC before there match against Skipster and the returning Omega Fro and the newly reunited friends put a dampener on New School's victory party by winning the tag team match. Tony Vigilante then addressed the FAW which the fans didn't approve of and heavy JLC chants began too rise. The main event of the night saw Spawn force Tony V to count the three on Bomber and this leads to more friction between New and Old school.
