Impulse Biography Biography Another biography, another day at the office. This is as meaningless as press conferences, photo shoots, and interviews with people who call themselves journalists. At the end of the day, the only information that gets passed on is that which the conductor agrees with. They're called 'editors' for a reason, you know. Regardless, my name is Randall Knox, but you'd know me as Impulse. If you know me at all. I'm what's known as an 'unseeded' wrestler in this tournament, which, based on the initial predictions, is somewhere around an untouchable caste in traditional Indian culture. You look at me and see what you want to see. Some will see an unseeded enigma going up against a big bad number four, and immediately decide not to remember my name, since I won't be here long enough to warrant committing it to memory. That's fine - I am not the message. Some of you will see a man who started at the very bottom of Craig Miles' New Frontier Wrestling, built a foundation on guts, talent, and perseverance and eventually broke out of the pack as the face of the Wrestling Revolution. Even before I had a face. Some of you will remember who won the first ever Sears Tower match for the vacant Television Championship less than a year after debuting with a three month losing streak. Some of you will see a non - factor in the FWO. None of you have the proper perspective to see the full scope of the message. But that's alright. I have all the time in the world.
