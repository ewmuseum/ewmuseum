Ryan Ruckus Biography Biography It's a story we've all heard before. R2 grew up knowing he would be in entertainment, and from a very young age, took a spark to pro wrestling. He began training a couple months before his 18th birthday and quickly became the most fan-friendly star on his local circuit. The recognition and backstage demeanor he carried was enough to get him a try out in N.E.W. where he spent the better part of the year as a fun loving face. But the fun was not to last. As fate would have it, the owner of NEW was a petty slob, bathed in nepotism for his personal friends and pet projects. This didn't sit too well with the idealistic Ruckus, and he took up the banner of revolutionary and peoples champion, secretly aligning himself with power players over the next year. Thus the birth of the greatest stable ever, Something Wicked. But that was only the beginning. The formation of his undefeated stable was just the catalyst to a much larger plan, and finally, Ruckus manuevered himself into a match for control of what was now EWW. He emerged from the match victorious, sentthe evil owner packing and birthed EWW into the Roughkut hall of fame winning: Sin City Wrestling. Having achieved all he could hope forand more, Ruckus handed the reigns of SCW over to his long-time friend and tag partner, "the Professor" Doug E. Fresh, so that he could go pursue his dream of Hollywood stardom. During his hiatus, he helmed a major motion picture throughRogue studios, penned a best-selling memoir, "Face-Pop Culture," and married his longterm girlfriend, Karen (Foxy) Fox. But then he caught wind that the absolute power of running SCW hadgone to Doug's head. And R2 new he'd have to once again return to put things right. And that's whathe did, retaking Sin City Wrestling by storm, and capturing every championship title the company had to offer on his way up the ranks to dethrone Doug's pet champion, M.D.K. Ruckus now sits at the top of the mountain, a final bastion of hope between the fans and the underhanded Professor. And he will stay there for as long as it takes for people to once again believe in heroes. Even if they are a "tweener" in demeanor. Other Feds Sin City Wrestling, SCW
