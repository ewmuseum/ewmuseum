Mr. Black The Story So Far June 24, 2002 A long time ago, or what seems like a long time ago, three men raised a dying wrestling federation to heights unimaginable when they started. Those three men worked hard and long hours, putting out the best product they could. They often clashed, and two were poised for war when the third disappeared. Slowly, the other two stepped back and the resurgence was over. Fast forward three months to June 01, 2002. An anonymous piece of mail, received by the three, would be the catalyst for what is now known as Project LoW. The letter alluded to large profits should the three find some way to work together again. It was signed by a Mr. Black. The details were sketchy but the three put their differences aside and forged a new beginning. In one short month this elite group of talent spawned the LoW and are poised to bring it to the top. What are these large profits the letter eluded to, and just who is Mr. Black? July 18, 2002 The Lords of Wrestling kicked off Project LoW with a bang. Mr. Black made sure that Pedi, Petapaw, and Cellrock knew exactly who the boss was, is, and forever will be. Though not in attendance, Mr. Black sent his lawyer, Cedric Irvine, to Chicago to oversee the first LoW production. To open the show Irvine informed the trio that their staff, that they personally hired, was now terminated...effective immediately. In the ring Dark Angel, Jaguar-F, Hardcore Hoefel, Harry Manback, and Protocol all advanced to the Alpha Title tournament, to be held in ten days at Battle for the Belts. July 30,th 2002 With Battle for the Belts in the books we can recount, and get a further grasp of what's going on. Maybe even shed some light on some rather touchy issues. First and foremost, although some may argue, a congratulations are in order for one 'Suicidal' Harry Manback. He shocked the World by taking home the LoW Alpha Title. Secondly another congratualtions is in order for one Precious. She shocked the World, maybe even more so than Manback, by taking home the Omega Title. Other key happenings: Rage, a direct hire by Mr. Black, debuted and stired things up. He stated that Mr. Black's intentions were to have Hoefel the champion and he was sent there to see it through. He even brought with him some muscle, taking the form of the Rossi Brothers. Cellrock and company were less then pleased to see him, and even less pleased that Rage would sign the Rossi's, who Cellrock was already in heavy negotiations with. Seeing the Rossi's with Rage was enough to make Cellrock go off on a tirade. He refused to let Hoefel win the title, just to spite Rage and Mr. Black. So he had his friend, 'Big Mighty', Thor challenge Hoefel for a match later in the night. With the help of Chris Pedi, and his debuting tag team Minimum Wage, Hoefel got passed Thor. This further stirred the pot and set the ground work for an explosive finish. In the main event there was to be a three way to decided the champion. 'Primetime' Rob Lewis, 'Suicidal' Harry Manback, and Hardcore Hoefel battled for nearly a half hour. In the end Cellrock and Thor made sure Hoefel didn't win. When Rage saw the outcome he stormed the ring, with the Rossi's, and all hell broke loose. Hoefel eventually got his revenge, cleaning house in impressive fashion. Then, in perhaps the biggest swirve of the night, the 'Franchise' Matt Helms showed up. He had lots to say, but never made clear weather he can legally be here, or if he was still under EPW contract. With lines being drawn, and sides being taken, where Helms figures in is still a mystery. Cellrock has faced off against Rage and Mr. Black, where does that leave Petapaw and Pedi? Hoefel is free, can he win back the fans...and does he even care? With Precious taking home a title, will this ignite a flame in women around the world? To be continued. . . ::LoW::
