Rules, Ratings & Rankings

RULES OF PTC 1. A character may not be in two PTC feds at one time. 2. Contracts are simple. Once a character is in a PTC fed, he is bound to that fed until he receives his release from the head of said fed. Disputes are mediated by the super intelligent monkeys who staff PTC. 3. Follow the rules posted for each of the forums. Breaking the rules results in immediate banning. 4. In Character is In Character Out of Character is Out of Character – know the difference RULES OF SITE POSTINGS 1. NO OOC No breaking Kayfabe or whatever you call it (Only exception is in special situations, Staff will decide this) 2. No one line Join our fed stuff, be creative when posting, Want members be creative. [This ties in with #1 rule, Think about it, We ask Fed members to not break Character in Rping same here, Think of it as a different aspect of Rping] 3. Add your feds name in the post, [Examples: [BBB] or BBB’s Weekly Wrap-up] not doing so will result in the post not counting for ratings. 4. Make sure your post is in the right place, Check the site after posting, If its in the Wrong place move it, This will also count against ratings if not done, Or cause posting limits. We want to present an organized format. 5. IN Recent Shows, a) Post Show Info: Post a teaser about the card with a link to the card. [It’s more interesting, and counts more then a one line Results are up click here post] b) Pre show Info: Again a Teaser or a run down on what is expected, Hype the show and your stars. It generates more interest then posting the card line up. [For Networks: Doing this in a very creative and good way will gain extra Rating points!!!] Again these are not rules we are making just one person to do. It’s for everyone. A more organized site helps promote the PTC site better and not only will you’re fed but also PTC as a whole grow. RULES OF RATINGS 1. Each Main Page news submission is worth a point (limit 1 per day). 2. Each column submitted is worth an additional point. 3. PTC Radio and Board activity will be measured subjectively. 4. Each federation's Primary Show will be evaluated and rated by PTC for quality and entertainment values. 5. Once the final votes and tally is made, an even division of the scores will be made to bring them into a range between 0 and 5. *** Note *** Please undestand that this methodology is in it's initial phases, and may be modified to better suit the community in the future. RULES OF RANKINGS 1. Each Fed-head will submit 2 wrestlers for the weekly top ten, first and second choice clearly annotated to [detail withheld] no later than 8PM CST Saturday. Failure to do so will result in a federation having no wrestlers listed in the top ten. 2. PTC Staff will review the submissions and rank them according to the following criteria: 3. PTC Presence: Activity on PTC boards (General Convo, TT, Poser and Graphic, etc...) In regards to presence, we mean "meaningful presence". This is not an excuse to meaninglessly spam. 4. In-Fed Impact: The involvement and impact of an individual wrestler on their respective federation. 5. PTC Radio Presence: Duke Williams does PTC a great service with Malice, and those that support him will be rewarded. PTC Radio is a great way to make yourself and your federation household names. Seize the opportunity. 6. PTC Top Ten Rankings will be updated every Monday. *** Note *** Please undestand that this methodology is in it's initial phases, and may be modified to better suit the community in the future. * More to be added or taken away as needed *
