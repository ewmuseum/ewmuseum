Peter Vetra Biography Biography The history of Peter Vetra is not very well known. The only things that have been known about Peter Vetra are those of which that have been established from his time on television. When Peter Vetra emerged onto the wrestling with PRIME back in 2003 he was a self proclaimed member of the Imperium, a secretive scientology type group that had been banned for many previous years. After playing that for a couple of months Peter had been revealed to the world as a fraud, someone who had not been a member of this infamous group but actually someone who knew very little of this group. This is the point in his career everything started going down hill, more and more matches we lost, Peter started settling in to depression. Something he thought he would never be able to shake until he met the girl. There is always some girl that can make you feel happy again, Well Peter had found her with Melissa, the Starbucks employee that he had to fight so hard to even get a date with. It was not long before the relationship was flourishing and Peter and Melissa were in love. Everything was going great, Peter had a love interest, Peter’s wrestling career was starting on the up swing now; this is of course after being revealed to the world as a fraud. But with every good story there has to be a bad right? Well here comes the bad part for Peter, after a party one night Melissa and Peter got into a fight which resulted in Peter getting arrested and charged with Domestic Assault and Battery. Now the two of them were no longer aloud to see speak or even breath near each other and Peter was being torn apart. After the trial he was let go on his will and was slapped with a restraining order and probation for six months. Peter did not speak with Melissa that whole time but was in chronic depression using wrestling as his outlet, the only problem with that was he was not getting anywhere in his career. But once the order was lifted Melissa tried contacting him, he tried contacting her, it went back and forth the two of them seeing each other for rendez vous in the middle of nights and what not, but knowing their relationship would never go anywhere. Then one night she pulled her psycho bitch antics again and told him to never speak with her again. This sent Peter into another crazed depression, the only thing he loved in his life was gone then it came back and now it was gone again, Peter did not know what he was going to do. One day in the back room of PRIME he ran into IFE Adam Dick, they started smoking marijuana and this is what seemed to bring Peter happiness… this seemed to be the only thing. The only problem was it wasn’t always enough, Peter found himself looking for stronger harder substances and started messing around with mushrooms, at this point it was obvious what was going on and the head honchos at PRIME decided to release Peter Vetra. Now Peter is back on track to a degree, mushrooms are something he stills messes around with once and a while, occasionally he will smoke but he is still very interested in drinking. But the difference is now he can go to work and get his job done seeing as before he was having a huge problem. Peter is trying to get his footing back on the right track with his wrestling career, and to do that he knows he has to be clean. Other Feds GCW
