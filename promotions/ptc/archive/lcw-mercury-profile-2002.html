Member Profile: LCW Mercury

undated (a standing page; site capture window c. 2002)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(note: more information on LCW can be found in the profile of our sister federation, LCW Evo) LCW Mercury is a roleplay-based e-federation, with a heavy emphasis on quality of both posts and shows, as well as consistency and professionalism. The booking staff produces shows which stack up with any e-federation in existence, and as such we are looking for posters of the same level. We have a respect for, and a positive relationship with, all of the handlers. If you're ready to compete at the highest level with the best possible results, then Mercury is the last federation you'll ever need to join. For those who merely wish to watch, shows are reliable, logical, and matches are richly detailed. Specifics: -The Makeup: Mercury opened in September 2001, and traces its e-fed roots to LCW (November 2000), EHW (May 2000), and the MMWF (February 2000). While few names remain from the first incarnation, each progressive era has seen new talents join. Out of the current roster of 14, at least 4 debuted in each of the last three phases of the federation. This has created an interesting dynamic, with a solid core of veterans and a group of highly motivated newcomers. There hasn't been any real life conflict between players in at least a year thanks in part to the relaxed, respectful environment. -The Posting: We expect one post per show, and an average of two to three will be needed for a serious push. We're willing to work around schedules to an extent, so long as we're given advance notice. -The Shows: Each match is original, and written by hand, with a description of each move. Matches are realistic, technical, dynamic and entertaining. Storylines are designed around maximum freedom for the handlers, and are also done with an attempt to grow the character. Pay-per-views are loaded with long, intense matches that both tell a story and advance (or finish) storylines. -The Rest: We encourage you to compare our product to that of any other federation. We believe that the gifted and dedicated among you will appreciate our effort, and can take advantage by applying to join the roster, or just by reading the Impulse and pay-per-view shows.
