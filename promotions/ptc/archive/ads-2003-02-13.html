Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The Ads page of Primetime Central’s own site (ptc.wrestleonline.org) as the Wayback Machine captured it on 13 February 2003: the ten recruitment and opening notices federations posted there between 30 January and 11 February 2003. The messenger names and e-mail addresses that two notices print as contact details are marked as withheld; credits that name a person read the first name and last initial. The page’s navigation and site banner, and the links under each notice, are not carried.
World Wrestling Revolution Applications Still Open 2/11/03 The World Wrestling Wrestling Revolution is still accepting all applications, Some have been sent in and are being reviewed, more would be great. Get them sent in as soon as you can while the going is still good, We're looking to re-build arround some great talent while keeping the great talent we have causing a ferocious mix of Revolutionary superstars. Source: Dave T. {NTICW} Applications Opening 2/09/03 NTICW has started recruiting wrestlers to build the federation back into the best on the market and are only wanting serious athletes inquiring for a spot on the roster. It has been clearly stated that NTICW won't have any slackers along for the free ride with hope or chance they become someone off their money or time... At this time, members are being invited into the federation and NTICW will be reviewing any applications received, but is still not definite just anyone will receive a place on the limited roster. Ceo Crossett claimed earlier to our sources "The problem with a lot of federations these days, they are trying to rapidly expand and they push talent out the doors by bringing in another. NTICW plans to work with their selective roster and build them into the most loved or hated wrestlers imagined. Though the wrestlers will work for us, NTICW will also work for them in return with the mutual respect!" This is unofficially quoted, but from the best of sources... Its early to tell for sure, but from the sounds of things the spots on the roster will fill quickly and if serious it would be best not to wait regardless if you are the best or even a hot rookie trying to make a name for him or herself. We will keep you updated about the names signed on the dotted line and also rumor has it that applications may be closed after one week of having them open and remain invite only.
