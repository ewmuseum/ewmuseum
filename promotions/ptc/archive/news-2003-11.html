8 November 2003 UPWA: What is wrestling Globalisation? The question I'm considering today is, 'What exactly globalisation in wrestling?' Now some may think this means getting a big money investment, a big star to carry the company on his back, and lots of advertisement and television time. But, I believe it takes much more than that. It need the right people running it, a high work ethic, a locker room full of guys who are willing to bust their asses in the ring to make a good show, a solid fanbase to support the wrestlers, a good television slot to show of angles, or even just a monthly large event to give the fans something to remember. Unfortunately, the UPWA has hardly any of these. By the skin of our teeth we got a deal with the PTC, our wrestlers phone in their performances on several occasions, we hardly run weekly shows, and when we do run shows, we don't even have more than 4 matches. And, I think it takes a bit of an idiot to run a whole company all on his own. The UPWA really lacks the big umph, which makes us all wonder why we get ratings even above the thousand mark. This is all harsh to say though, as there are those few devoted roster members who stick by the boss and make his time easier. It is really hard to make suggestion about storylines and feuds when you don't even know whose going to turn up at the next event. The UPWA is a federation that really needs to be bigger to thrive and survive. It has the layout, the dedication of the people running it, and all it really needs are some new wrestlers, who would be willing to perform to a level the UPWA needs to make it in the big leagues. So far, the UPWA has got it's highest attendance as 17,255 at it's first comeback pay per view in June, and since it has only held shows in small arenas where they can tape their 45 minute television show. Not very big league, eh? Well, hopefully, things are about to change, and times are about to look up, and I believe that in a year, the UPWA will either be one of the top rated federations, or just dead. Take your pick, and thanks. PJ London === 11 November 2003 -NGW- NGW goes Primetime! Late last night NGW and PTC officials released a statement saying that NGW has returned to the Prime Time Central TV Network. NGW had left PTC earlier this year shortly after ownership of PTC changed hands from The Phantom Booker to former Light Outs Wrestling Owner Trigger. NGW made it over to at that time Revolution Studios run by Munny McMahon. Munny soon left RS himself to what seems to be his retirement from the sport and eventually leaving the control/ownership of RS to AW owner Jack. RS which was renamed Revolution Wrestling Network went into a downward spiral. NGW went into a downward spiral of their own when ownership of their domain was threatened to be pulled. NGW decided to close its door for a short time and regroup. NGW stayed closed for 2 months giving the federation a complete makeover in every aspect. New Site, almost complete new roster, addition of new staff and a renewed will to run the federation. At this time RWN had hit near rock bottom and no longer considered NGW a member. So NGW abandoned RWN and began to hang out around PTC again which was under ownership of Argyle. It seemed like only a matter of time before NGW made a return as a PTC member federation, and yesterday that suspicion became truth as NGW officially returned to PTC. Source: NGW === 11 November 2003 [WWA] Federal Investigation The Federal Trade Commission has begun an investigation into the WWA fiances. They were tipped off late last week by an "Unknown Source", but given the WWA's history, it really could be anyone that has ever held a grudge against back office staff. And that is a lot. The WWA, Owned by Mark Harrington has been in the control of Matthew Warr for the past 2 years, executively and financially, and this shocking rumor has shaken the very foundations of the 7 year old wrestling federation. It has been widely considered that since Warr took over the job as acting CEO 25 months ago that the WWA has enjoyed a "Golden Era" with a whole new generation of top stars breaking through the "Glass ceiling" which many were considered to be held back by. However, the exemplary record and meticulous tax records submitted in the past 7 years will now be researched, reviewed and rectified by Government officials, and if the WWA IS deemed to have broken the federal law, then they will be prosecuted. The FTC sent in their team to commence a full financial audit on Wednesday, and the investigation is expected to take several weeks. The findings and reprucussions will of course be seen here first. Ringmaster also marks the end of one of the members of former Core Wrestling Tag Team Champions.  Dark Hearts had to take a tremendous leave for a few months after their reign as Tag Team Champions.  Nos then returned nearly a month and a half ago claiming that he was the better of the two members of Dark Hearts.  Fenix and Phranq Qeq then returned only to hear the claims and accusations made by Nos.  Fenix then issued a challenge to Nos for a Losers Leaves match at Ringmaster and Nos eagerly agreed. After the contracts with the Great British Heroes ran up, Core Wrestling decided against renewing them.  That of course leaves the Tag Team Championship vacated.  Two newer teams in Core Wrestling are thrown into a match together for the Tag Team Titles.  Both of which have earned their way in Core Wrestling thus far, and both of which look to make the tag team title run one to remember.  From NEW, Gideon Fontaine and Neil Fracas take on Morpheus Omega and Damien Alexander in what looks to be an explosive bout. As ordered by the owner of Core Wrestling, Sidarian, upon losing in the Gauntlet Match to be in the Ringmaster finale has to defend his title against an eager Simon Krask.  Krask recently returned to Core Wrestling and continued to add wins to his record.  Carrying the team of him and Halihandro around is no easy task, and he would like nothing more than to win singles gold instead of dragging Halihandro around all the time.  Sidarian hasn't had a title defense since defeating Jack Diamond at Battle of Britain and is looking forward to proofing that he is more than just a champion on paper. The Legacy Championship is also on the line at Ringmaster with the much anticipated rematch between Daniel Adams and Jantz Spalding.  Spalding has held the title three times now and Daniel Adams has held it once.  Adams looks to reclaim it as Spalding looks to retain it.  Spalding has once again reassured himself that the fans is why he's here in Core Wrestling and that everything he does is for the fans.  Will Spalding and the fans emerge victorious? And finally, the moment every Core Wrestling superstar, especially the two involved, have fought and waited for during the last month.  A Ringmaster Competition comes to a close in a Sixty Minute Iron Man Match between two men who one their respected gauntlet matches the past week on Smash.  Both have earned the right to be in this match and the winner will definitely have earned the right to be called the Core Wrestling Ringmaster.  Skill Willie and his submission skills go up against Jason Hughes and his submission techniques.  It's going to be one long hour for both men.
