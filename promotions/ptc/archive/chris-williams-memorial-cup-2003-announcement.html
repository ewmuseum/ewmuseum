Chris Williams Memorial Cup

25 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok guys, after speaking to the powers that be, I am VERY pleased to announce that the fourth annual Chris Williams Memorial Cup is going ahead, and that the PTC will be heavily involved! The CWMC is an IC memorial show for an OOC handler ... Chris Williams handled a wrestler called Kain Threat ... who was killed in a car accident in 1999. We have held a card in his memory each year since then, and each time it has got bigger and better. ... this year is shaping up to eclipse all the others! The card itself will have two themes: First one is the tournament proper, which will have three parts, a 64 man battle royal, an 8 man elimination match, and finally a 1 on 1 match to crown the CWMC Champion ... a title that is held until next year when the winner crowns the next one. The second theme will be 'specialty' matches. This is where it should get interesting :) The PTC titles will be on the line (actually the Global and Tag will definately be on the line, I haven't heard from the Extreme head yet ... but Ross and Ryan are ok with it so hopefully he will be too) The number one contender for the Global title will also be decided here. If feds want a specialty/grudge/title match to be included on the card they just need to contact me. A cruiserwieght match is a suggestion too. Basically this is going to be a BIG card. ... but it is also one that is going to be a success!!!! There is a PRIZE for the tournament winner as well ... an autographed piece of WWE memorabillia signed by the WWE star of the winner's choice (as long as they are currently active!) There will be a poser with the CWMC title. ... and there will be the knowledge that the first ifed type event that the PTC is involved with worked out great (I can see the future!!!) At the moment I am hoping to offer 32 places to the PTC feds themselves, and it would be up to them to nominate people to enter the tourney. The other 32 places will be for free agents and non-PTC feds. The PTC title matches obviously have to be for PTC wrestlers only, but the speciality matches (like cruiser) are completely open. The date for the card is going to be the 9th November 2003 ... and signups will open soon (after the Trash Talk tourney is open so as to not steal their thunder) The site for the CWMC is already up and running: Chris Williams Memorial Cup ... as are the boards where the majority of information will be held, and RPing will take place: RPCommunity forums Oh, the team behind this is me and the Phenomenal Ryan mostly, but there is already a lot of offers of help for judging and writing (more always appreciated) and to ensure that EVERYTHING is done fairly, Argyle has agreed to be an impartial adjudicator for this and in the case of any queries about judging, he will have the final say. Updates as they happen! J
