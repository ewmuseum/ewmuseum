24 January 2003 *XWF* Tuesday Xtreme Tuesday Xtreme was a very hyped event because it featured so many things like the finals of the US Title Tournament and finally DVD and Chivo would have to face each other in a 6 men tag team match. The opening match was the final match in the US Title Tournament in which DJ made history by becoming the first ever XWF Us Champion when he defeated Canadian Crippler with his finishing move the DJ Splash. The second bout was a 4 way dance between P.R., Limp Bizkit, El Locon and Enishi. The match was an elimination match. The match quickly turned into a brawl as El Locon and Limp Bizkit took it to the outside leaving Enishi with P.R. inside. El Locon snapped and nailed Limp Bizkit with a steel chair getting himself disqualified. El Locon also nailed Enishi with the chair and then proceeded to chokeslam Limp Bizkit threw the announce table. He then rolled both men into the ring and P.R. took quick advantage by pinning Limp Bizkit eliminating him. He then covered Enishi but Enishi was not done for. Enishi bounce back and won the match. After the match Asesino who was doing commentary congratulated his tournament partner Enishi and then carried with Corporate friend Limp Bizkit to the back. The third match was a Triple Threath Match between Koko B., Luciffer and Big Shot. This one went all over the arena and since it was hardcore rules everything was allowed. The match finally ended in the bar where Koko B. nailed the Death Koko Driver on Big Shot through the pool table for the win. The main event was another battle in the war between The Corporation and The Rodriguez Brothers as Asesino, Raven and Corporate Champion DVD were representing The Corporation facing World Champion Chivo, Chezina and Nicky Jam. This match was almost total domination for The Corporation as the used teamwork to get rid of every member of the Rodriguez Brothers. At the end Asesino got the Asesino Unprettier on Chivo. From Chicago, IL === 27 January 2003 {{TS}} Saturday Night Two The second Saturday Night kicked off at the Hartford Civic Center, and it was a night filled with surprises. The night started with an amusing interview with “The Man of Funk” Terrance Williams, in which he complained about a soar neck on behalf of the chokeslam last week by Demon Stalker. We then swept right into the action with the opening matchup. The lovely Alison Saint James took on Outsider, with harsh results. Outsider’s “Done Deal” (Torture Rack to an F5) brought down Alison, and when referee Ken Chapel put down the pin victory, it was all but over for Alison. When Outsider left the ring after staring down the broken body of Alison, a loud shriek came into the audience. Gothic Greta came out with a broken armed straight jacket, and she immediately began pounding on Saint James. Gothic Greta nailed her “Black Light” (Impact DDT) to Alison before setting her up and executing the “Flesh Bite” (Shooting Star Press). She was then detained into the jacket once more. Ken Chapel escorted her out of the ring by tossing her up on his shoulder. Alison had to seek medical attention and was diagnosed with a mild concussion later on in the night. The cameras then faded to President Koch who stated to TS reporter Brian Carone that his surprise for the night would have to remain a secret for a little bit longer. We would soon find out what (or who) the secret was by the end of the program. The next matchup pitted “The Man of Funk” Terrance Williams and Marcus Arelius. Both men had an advantage in this matchup, and Terrance had the chance to put it away with “The Funk Effect” (Full forced kick to the jaw). Marcus saw it coming and diagnosed Williams with his “The Felony” (Torturer Crab). Williams refused to give up and somehow got to the ropes, forcing Marcus to break the hold. Suddenly Yeahboy’s music played and Marcus was distracted (Marcus injured Yeahboy’s hamstring using the same “Felony” move last week). The distraction was just enough for Williams to regain his position and seek “The Funk Effect” once more to get the three count. Yeahboy did not even come out but both men promised to repay him in their own way. It was now time for the introduction by President Koch. This introduction was the introduction of the brand new Vice President, Matt Johnson. Johnson promised to make Twilight Slams a better place to wrestle and said that next week in his first official debut as V.P. he would introduce a new wrestler to the TS roster. The main event was between the monstrous Crazy Killer and Yeahboy. Surprisingly, there was not any interference by Marcus Arelius in this matchup. The word was that Marcus had already left the building, but that did not seem to matter much to Yeahboy. He almost pulled off the upset, kicking out of two powerful moves by Killer. However, Killer was just too much for Yeahboy and a “Crazy Bomb” (Top Rope Powerbomb) put Yeahboy to waste. Overall, it was an excellent night and I sure cannot wait to see TS Saturday Night live this Saturday from Mohegan Sun Hotel and Casino in Uncasville, Connecticut! The second bout was between the Xtreme Champion C.R. and the US Champion DJ in a non title match but very important since both men had been on fire during the past couple of matches. DJ went for the cocky cover but only got a 2. DJ got upset with the ref which was enough for C.R. to roll him up and score the big win. The next match was scheduled to be Zero Lionheart making his debut against Koko B. but things went differently as The Corporation destroyed Koko B. allowing Zero Lionheart to pick up his first win in XWF without breaking a sweat. The cameras went to the backstage area as the XWF World Champion Chivo conducted a promo in which he told DVD that he has a match next week against Chivo's brother Chezina for the Corporate Title and wished DVD good luck. Then he said that since Enishi has been hanging alot with The Corporation recently he challenged Enishi to a non title match next week to send DVD a message. The next bout was an I Quit Match between P.R. and Asesino which have had a long rivalry since their CWF days. The match resembled more of a hardcore match than a submission match. At the end Asesino got his arms tangled on the ropes. P.R. took advantage and nailed Asesino with multiple chairshots in the head and then put Asesino in a modified boston crab with Asesino's arms still on the ropes. Asesino had no choice but to say I Quit. The main event featured a brother vs brother match between Chezina and Nicky Jam. Even as brothers this 2 guys took to each other during the whole match. New Years Resolution Results === 29 January 2003 {{TS}}: Saturday from Uncasville This Saturday Night will mark the first call to duty of the newly appointed Vice President Matt Johnson. He stated last week that this Saturday he would debut a brand new wrestler into the Twilight Slams community. Could it be Samba Tatenaka? The Japanese hybrid was supposed to enter at the “Hell in Your House” pay-per-view. We would have to wait and find out live from Uncasville, Connecticut on Saturday Night! The opening bout will clash two evenly recorded men with one loss and one win. The opponents are “The Man of Funk” Terrance Williams and the Outsider. Look for these two to lock it up multiple times in the future. These two do not really have a hate towards each other yet, but we can always provide some fuel for the fire. Terrance recently came off an upset victory against Marcus Arelius thanks to the help of Yeahboy, and Outsider recently came off a victory against Alison Saint James. This will be the first time one-on-one between Terrance and Outsider. The other time these two met, it was in the ring with the monstrous Crazy Killer who picked up the victory with the interference of Demon Stalker. The second match will have a new fighter in Benjamin taking on the slow starting Yeahboy. Look for the rookie Benjamin to try to pick up his first Twilight Slams win, and look for Yeahboy to get off his losing streak. Our main event will feature Crazy Killer taking on Marcus Arelius. Crazy Killer has certainly made a huge impact so far in Twilight Slams, and he is undefeated to boot! Marcus plans to use his Greek God power to try to throw Killer into the pits of Hades! This match will also feature a special guest commentator, and we still do not have any word as to who it may be. Look for both of these men to put on one hell of a show. So be sure to catch all of the action live from Mohegan Sun Hotel and Casino in Uncasville, Connecticut this Saturday Night live on public access television from Twilight Slams! We'll keep you posted... === 31 January 2003 [FAW] Jon The Bomb 'Out of It'? The latest rumour emerging from the FAW Headquarters is that Jon The Bomb, recently returned wrestler who is highly documented as the first ever FAW member - was involved in a serious bust-up with his client Lee Smith yesterday night. Many FAW fans will know Jon manages Smith on a part time basis - nothing more and nothing less. Rumour has it that the two were seen brawling at a bar in Lee's hometown - Hollywood. Policemen apparently had to be called to the scene and Lee was taken to hospital. Several supposed witnessed have come forward and confirmed the story to the FAW Press Office, but on asking Lisa Foster (FAW President) didn't seem to know anything about the incident. FAW Fans are thought to have reacted angrily to the rumour with many saying that Bomb should never have returned to FAW in the first place - and that he is now the Hulk Hogan of Furiously Addictive Wrestling. A press conference on the matter is expected at 19:00 GMT tonight. More FAW related news as we get it. Source: Furiously Addictive Wrestling === 31 January 2003 ~ WWA ~ Calgary Under Attack? There have been reports recently of a man wandering around Calgary causing general ruckus and aggravation. The man was later identified as WWA Wrestler, Shaman. Shaman, who has won every title available to him in the WWA has for some reason begun random attacks on citizens of Calgary. Shaman is believed to be in this state after he was viciously attacked by The Cell on the most recent Fiesta. Since then, his attitude has gotten lower and lower. Local police arrested him, but he was soon released as there was little evidence against him. Shaman will be fighting for the WWA TV Title at RITC VI Against Nicholas Adams, Grunge, and the current Champion, Christyan Payne.
