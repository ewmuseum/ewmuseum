PTC: New Era — the Card

20 April 2003 (announced card)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PTC Presents: "New Era" Pay Per View Live, From the MGM Grand in Las Vegas, Nevada -- April 20th, 2003 PTC Global Title Match Elimination Match featuring fifteen "invitation-only" members of the GT roster. Participants: Mike Meyhu (LOW), Jesse Jamester (LOW), Tristan Belmont (LOW), Donny Diamond (DWO), Kaiowas (DWO), The Real Deal (DWO), Shaman (WWA), Nick Adams (WWA), Damian Ryan (WWA), DVD (XWF), Jomary (XWF), Scott Curtis (PWE), Adam Brown (PWE), "The Pride of Japan" Samba Tatenaka (TS), Michael "Spitfire" Stevens (CCW) "Wheel of Misfortune Match" -- Roulette Rules [See the GTD Forums.] PTC Extreme Title Match Terry Woods (c) (FA) vs. Maelgrim (FA) The Death Trap: The ring is suspended 30 feet in the air by titanium wire attached to each turnbuckle. Turnbuckle pads are ripped off and replaced by steel balls which are covered with spikes, the ropes are replaced by barbed-wire, and the ring is ripped of a mat and replaced with solid steel sheeting. Inside the ring are weapons, every sort of weapon. Under the ring is a pit of broken glass, doused in gasoline and lit on fire. The only way to win is by fatality, knock out, or to toss your opponent over. PTC Tag Team Titles Match -- Tag Team Title Tournament Finals Homo/Phobic (EWA/UWF) vs. Shadow Riders (LOW) Standard Tag Team Match. DWO World Title Match Kaoiwas (c) -v- Donny Diamond Asylum Match - Both competitors are placed inside of the custom built three story DWO Asylum. Inside a torture house awaits them, equiped with anything and everything you would find inside of an asylum.. Including the blood thirsty, insane patients and medical labs. Hell, for sure. FAW World Title Match 9th Wonder of Foam (c) -v- Rzarector -v- Xander No Time Limit. Tables, Ladders, & Chairs Match. Special Trash Talk Challenge Match Genesis (DWO) -v- Ace Masters (FA) Information to be decided by those involved XWF World Title Match C.R.
