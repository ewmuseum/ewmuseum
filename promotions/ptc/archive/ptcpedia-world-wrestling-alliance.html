World Wrestling Alliance Active/Inactive | Years Open Active | 13+ RP/Angle | RP Fedhead | Josh, Joe PPV's a year | 2 (plus 2 special Underground's) Weekly TV Show | Underground (Webcast) Number of Titles | 2 Website | World Wrestling Alliance WWA Forums The World Wrestling Alliance is currently an active federation that was part of the Primetime Central for many years, under the original ownership of Mark Harrington; the company has also been under the control of [name withheld], Matthew Warr and Phil Flaws. They are currently being handled by [name withheld] and Joseph Stock. Since it's return the WWA now runs a weekly webcast called Underground and looks to run 2 pay per view's which are; Retribution and the annual spectacular Rage In The Cage. Note: There are two special treats in that two events that had once been a PPV's in the old WWA, the Bourbon Street Brawl and the Independence Day Rumble. They have now been relegated to a special broadcast's of Undeground. So now WWA Fanatics can view these epic treats for free at wwa-online.com. The company currently has two major titles; WWA Championship which was formally known as the WWA World Heavyweight Championship, and the WWA United States Championship. The WWA went out of business in June 2009 and has been closed since then. New ownership has purchased the assets of the fed, including a number of contracts with time remaining on them. But being out of sight means being out of mind. Drastic cuts have occurred and the WWA is really starting from scratch. This means things will start out with an indy fed feeling. Think ECW in the late 90s, or ROH/DragonGate today. Instead of sold-out stadiums, we’ll be wrestling in convention centers and arenas that only holding a few thousand. The remaining fans are our die-hards, the ones who live and breathe WWA. Rage In The Cage XII will see the return of the WWAtron and an all new set design. We can handle some lightening effects, but a Hell in a Cell or Buried Alive match is not in the budget. Of course as time goes on, we should rebuild our audience and get moving up the ladder.
