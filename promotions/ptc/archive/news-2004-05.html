6 May 2004 The Draft Has Been Issued BEAUMONT, TX – May 6, 2004 – At a press conference today, Matthew Lanier addressed all of the free agents and independent talent that are roaming around looking for a place to call home. Lanier announced a new contest to be held for Core Wrestling to give one person the opportunity to win a roster spot in Core Wrestling. Lanier called the contest “The Draft.” At the beginning of the conference, Lanier referred to the last issued United States Draft: “On June 30th, 1973, the United States government inducted it's last draftee since the draft was issued earlier that year. It's now the year 2004. It's been 31 years since the draft was last used, but no more. Core Wrestling is calling all the able and willing bodies that want to be a part of a federation that is ever growing to sign up and volunteer and be a part of The Draft!” Lanier went on to explain the competition the best he could without giving away too much of ideas and design behind it. He did allude to the using of judges to help run the contest and that sixteen participants will be chosen from all the applications that come in for the contest. Lanier and his staff looked excited about the upcoming contest and have high hopes in its success over the summer as well as a continuation into the future of Core Wrestling as an annual event. Lanier ended the conference by saying: “So get ready, The Draft has been issued.” Source: The Draft === 7 May 2004 Apocalypse's future in question During Static two weeks ago, Apocalypse, one of BDW's greatest talents, got some horrific news. His girlfriend was hit by a drunk driver while leaving a bar and is now in ICU. She is severely injured and Apocalypse remains at her side constantly. He only leaves to wrestle his match and returns right after. He won his last match, but this emotional strain can only be held in for so long. Many believe he cannot continue to be a mainstay in BDW while attending to Haley at the same time. Only time will show how strong Apocalypse really is. === 8 May 2004 Jarone Signs to BDW: Earlier this week BreakDown Wrestling Co-Owner Jefferey met with Primetime Central veteran JJ "3J" Jarone. Jarone recently was released from jail on Drug Trafficing charges. Co-Owner Jefferey is very pleased with this most recent signing, and feels eager to get this star in the ring. Jarone is set to debut this Sunday night on Static against Kuff McSlade in a singles bout. This Sunday will be Jarones return to the ring in over 4 months. PTC writers caught up with JJ "3J" Jarone Tuesday evening to get his thoughts on this signing and he had this to say. "I'm feeling pretty good right now. This will most likely be super change in my career of wrestling. Coming off of Global Wrestling and into Breakdown Wrestling. I plan to give all I can to help BreakDown Wrestling become perfection at it's finest." After being asked if there could be any other pending charges JJ Jarone simply said no. The bigger question lately has been how will JJ Jarone perform in the ring after taking so many months off. It has been told when JJ Jarone comes back to the ring after an injury he is at a 85% rate of winning his return match. "We promote a good community," said WMW Owner Brad Johnson. "We also like to keep everything very open, however when a superstar has a problem with something we have done or said, we expect them to come to us personally to address the issue." Earlier in the week, Vice President Justin Evers released his bi-weekly Top 20, which had placed Primetime at number 16. "Primetime was upset about his placement," said Johnson, "and felt he was being isolated and pushed down for some reason. It was an issue that could have been easily resolved through one on one conversations, but Primetime decided to take it upon himself to make the issue public. And after being asked 3 times to cease and desist the flames and verbal attacks, we decided it was in the company's best interest to send him home to think about his actions." Garvin said, "Everyone has a mean streak. Everyone has a personal goal that they set for themselves, and when you don't meet it, you get frustrated. So, I sympathise with PT, but he was warned and didn't stop, and the first rule in being in business is that you don't attack those in authority." Primetime is expected to return in 2 weeks. ----- Directly after his match at High Stakes, Dr. Thug was released. "To survive within a company, you need to develop a good raport with those around you," said Johnson. "You also need to realize the boundries that are set down by society. Free speech has a broad acceptence, but when you take advantage of it to create controversary and to harrass those around you, it forces those in authority to make a decision." Dr. Thug used offensive language to express his dislikes, which caused a mass reaction within the community. It was taken to the Board of Directors, and after a week of deliberation and looking at the policies and procedures, it was deemed inappropriate and fell outside what was good. "We hate to release a fighter who is active and who works hard, but when push came to shove, we knew it was in our best interest to let him go," said Johnson. ----- Former GWer Kenji Lee and former UHWer The Painted Assassin have been hired and placed on the upcoming cards.
