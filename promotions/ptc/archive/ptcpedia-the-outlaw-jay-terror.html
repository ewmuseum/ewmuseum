Born Jamison Bryan Talley on September 9th, 1983, Jay has gone from drug addicted small-federation loser to high class championship star within the ranks of GCW. His career is far from over and if he has anything to say about it, he'll be smoking the entire time. It's his signiture thing anyways. Full History Jamison was born into a family where wrestling was life. His grandfather was a wrestler (not the best by a long shot but he was still a fighter in the ring), his father was a well known superstar that traveled the globe, and now as you’d expect-they hoped for the youngest Talley to continue the legacy. As he was younger, Jamison was always the kind of kid to find trouble. He liked to test people’s words and see how far he could go without overstepping the lines. That held true as he grew older…the main difference however was he would learn that going a bit further wasn’t the worst thing in the world. His father attempted to train his son on many occasions but his own performing got in the way causing Jamison to care little about the business. Instead, he had fun by running around town with his gang of friends making mischief wherever they could. Between him and five others, they created a group which they called ‘The Bandstand Bandits’ (Mostly because the center of town was a giant bandstand). The six kids, now eighteen and younger, wore masks to hide themselves in the night as they created various raids on different points in the town. From small theft to bullying the kids made a name for themselves quick as a group to fear. Jamison would stay with the bunch until he was nineteen when he left the position as lead of the group (Or ‘Top Terror’ as they called it). His reasoning was simple; his father had finally retired and was dead-set on training his son. He was banned from leaving the house until he finished four hours of practice a day. The rules continued to build up causing Jamison to get enough. He decided that he would fight but it wouldn’t be for his father’s glory or even under his father’s name. At twenty one, Jamison moved out and found himself a small wrestling promotion in Concord (CWA-Concord Wrestling Academy). With the little he remembered from his dad he created his own style by finding dumb luck early on. He ignored most training and found drugs to be his path to success. For a good year he experimented with multiple drugs and doing so brought him to a point where he wanted to give up on life. His wrestling career had already turned to muck after two years and he was ready to leave for good. Luckily, someone found him on the side of the road and recognized him from a house show. That someone went by the name of Daniel Franco. He had a background in managing wrestlers but that was a long while ago. As he looked over the incapacitated kid though he felt something that made him want to take the kid under his wing. He woke him up and found him some food and soon after proposed a deal with him. His terms were simple, all Jamison had to do was fight his heart out and he’d receive a place to stay and food to keep him going. Daniel didn’t want pay but would rather test and see if he could complete this unique experiment. The duo moved to Pennsylvania to get to Daniel’s home and there he enrolled Jamison in another small promotion local to the area. He did what Daniel asked with many arguments along the way. It was in his nature to rebel against rules and authority and Daniel saw this as an advantage in the ring even if it became a nuisance outside of it. Jamison, wrestling under the moniker Jay Terror, was ready to move out and start to get deeper in Daniel’s experiment. His first target soon became Global Championship Wrestling (GCW), which was a long-time member of the Primetime Central circuit. In his first in-ring encounter, his lack of experience and overall stately way of stretching his abilities proved to be his downfall after he suffered a closely contested loss to Jayce Bradley. The match was part of the first round of Warpath tournament which would ensure him a shot for the GCW World Title if he advanced. In his following match, he took on Damon Aldair on WorldWide 66. He showed his prowess in the ring and proved that his wrestling was in the same league as his mouth spoke him to be. Through this time, Daniel and him had obvious dessent among one another but that is only natural when a manager wants to push their superstar onto the map. In his third match, the rebellious smoking nut took on Jimmy Thunder in a GCW Television Title match. Steven Caldera, GCW owner, noticed what he had to offer and placed him in the early title match to see what he had in him. Luckily, Jay took advantage of the situation and buckled down to win the championship and a load of surprise along the way. He surpassed Brock Sheperd in a match for his title and moved forward to Warpath, the pay per view, where he'd get a shot at combining both the GCW Vanguard Title and GCW Television Title. At the same time, a lovely girl by the name of Heather appeared in his life. Her gorgeous looks and matching personality hooked Jay in immediately. The one thing about her though was she acted as if something was going on with her and Daniel Franco when Jay was absent. Jay would go into Warpath and fight Katie O'Claire, another talented star on the rise. The GCW Vanguard Title and GCW Television Title were up for unification and Jay would take the victory and combine the gold in a dominating fashion putting O'Clare out on an injury. A little after his win his father passed due to a heart attack Jay felt was his fault. A meeting with him a day before ended in a tragic way and the night after his father was dead. With this in mind, a new stimulant for his career erupted and took Jay to the next level in his mindset. He became more fierce and wanted to do more. Franco would enter him into the Chris Williams Memorial Cup Tournament where his first round opponent ended up being Phil Atken of FUSE. Jay would go on to advance in the tournament while splitting ties with his manager for a slight reprieve. While round two of the tournament began, Franco found his way into Jay's life again and won himself a spot back on Jay's side again with shaky terms. His next opponent would be Tsunami Oni, a legend, where he would lose unfortunately. The loss came as a huge surprise to Jay seeing that he felt that he was meant to win the tournament. Nevertheless, Jay moved on and began to focus on more things GCW related. He began to abuse his powers in terms of booking matches between Jesse Ramey and Paddy O'Shea by making them wrestle each other blindfolded and do other things for his amusement. In the mix of things, he would defend his Television title for the fourth time, this time against newcomer Brad Jackson, where he would wind up being disqualified. It turned out that Shawn Porter had something to prove to Jackson and he attacked him making Jay lose the match while still holding onto his title. Nevertheless, he moved on to Tsunami and there he squared off with Paddy O'Shea in an attempt to end their heated feud. Jay won that match as well but for his efforts he turned around to find that none other then Tyson DeBough came out and took the title claiming it was his. Now Jay struggles to gain back his title while avoiding the establishment. With high hopes of doing just that in a tag team match with DeBough against Banned & Exiled~!, he was unfortunately unable to touch his partner in any way other than tagging him from bell to bell. In the end of the match though Jay would be tricked and pinned ending with DeBough getting the best of him. The following week, Jay made his way into Tyson's life once more as he attacked Tyson with a ladder while keeping the orders Caldera made still ok. He would learn later, though, that he would enter number one in the Dangerous Games match and face Tyson in a ladder match at DG. At Dangerous Games, Jay would unfortunately wind up losing the title in the match and seeing Tyson walk off as the true champion. For the Dangerous Games match itself though, Jay went on to eliminate five people (the most in the match) and land in the final four out of the thirty competitors. LeStatt Knight soured his run though leaving Jay out of the action for the finals. When it came to Devil's Night, Jay found a friend in Jordan White (who had recently left the establishment) and they tagged together in a first blood match against Knight and "The Renegade" Rich Rollins. Jay and Jordan surprisingly score a victory after a quick save by Jay resulting in him busting Knight's face open. Just after, however, Knight took the fight right back to him and Terror was left lying on the mat in a puddle of his own blood. As he continued on his quest to take down LeStatt, he wound up scoring a match with the icon at the biggest pay per view of the year: NC17. Before that though, the mic talks and pranks continued which even ended up with Jay smoking a cigarette Knight laced with PCP. At the big show, Terror wound up drawing the short straw as he fell to Knight in a very close match which was later dubbed the match of the year. As it turned out though, he made his way past and into the running for the world title of Vivica J. Valentine. Jay pushed on after NC-17 and moved into the Round Robin tournament with the likes of Lia Ambrosi, LeStatt Knight, Jordan White, and Rich Rollins vieing against him for a shot at Vivica J. Valentine's freshly won world title. In his first match against LeStatt Knight, he avenged his loss at NC-17 by beating the hall of famer with his own move (The Knightfall) and picking up a win. In his second bout, Jay squared off with Rich Rollins where he found his second win to be in the form of a ladder match. Their bandanna's were on the line and thanks to Terror's win, Rich Rollins officially lost his rag. He moved forward in the round robin as he squared off with Lia Ambrosi which ended up with...
