Also well-known for memorable year-long stints in both The Squared Circle as its World Champion and as a former tag team champion in the Fans Wrestling Organization with Lindsay Troy as Silver and GOLD. He is infamous throughout the wrestling industry for being braggadocious, volatile, highly confrontational and opinionated about the wrestling industry as a whole, but widely respected by most fans for his exceptional use of brutal strikes, vicious suplexes, and undeniable charisma.
