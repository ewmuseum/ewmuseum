Birthplace | Belmont, England Finisher | Oblivion Bomb Wrestling Style | Power House Brawler Championships | GCW Tag Champion, 2X TV Title, GCW World Champion Handler | Bryan Images Bryan Mayhem (real name, Bryan O'Neil) is an English professional wrestler best known for his hardcore lifestyle and The Belmont House of Torture Match, which is considered by many to be one of the most sadistic matches ever. He is currently at war with Vivica Valentine in GCW. Highlights include physically decimating her at Tsunami, and attempting to set her on fire. This led to an Inferno Match at Dangerous Games, a match Bryan Mayhem emerged victorious after burning Vivica's arm. Early Life Bryan O'Neil is the oldest of three. The middle child being the now Legendary Dark Messiah Jason O'Neil for FUSE. They came from an abused background. It was his brother Jason who broke him into the wrestling business, landing him a job with EHWF where he went 35-1, his only loss coming to Jason O'Neil. Youngest brother whose name is unknown disappeared at a young age because of their abusive father. Mayhem's father use to beat Jason relentlessly until Bryan beat him, and Anton disappeared. Bryan left Belmont and was not seen for years. Jason became a world renowned wrestler and found Bryan a minister in the church of Satan and coaxed Bryan into the wrestling world. Bryan and Jason than wrestling as the name JNightmare, and together they dominated the federation. Bryan established a record of 35-1, and held almost every title except the World Title. He became famous with his Belmont House of Torture match with the now legendary Grim Reaper. Its premise was both men would be locked inside Bryan's home for one half hour, and at the strike of the clock the winner must exit. This match was the premise for a 60 minutes special on violence in wrestling, and became the most sick and gruesome match ever. Bryan would later end the careers of two wrestlers names Syko, and Reed in the very same match. Bryan became very jealous of his younger brother's fame and popularity and would later turn on him. After the departure of president Walnut, and new and younger man named Riggs took over the fed and granted Bryan his well deserved title shot, against his very own brother in a street fight. Bryan overcame Jason and won the title, or so he thought. Riggs claimed the match was never 'legally' sanctioned and thus stripped Mayhem of his title. Mayhem went berserk and attacked Riggs, breaking 4 ribs, and his shoulder. Bryan was forever banned from the fed. After realizing this, the PCW in its desire for ratings for it was young signed the Lunatic to a lucrative deal making him an instant millionaire. However, Bryan's interest in the dark occult became his main focus on life and it turned him into a raving lunatic. He was set to wrestle a big name in PCW at the time named Ricks. During a night on the town with his girlfriend, Mayhem jumped Ricks and apprehended his girlfriend, forcing her to live in a cage for well over a week. This disgusting act got Bryan fired. Bryan would disappear for a few years until his forgiving brother brought him back into the light and brought him into Caldera Enterprises. Like always Bryan was known for his power and display of evil in the ring before he left again without saying a word. He became somewhat of a legend because of his out of the ring antics. His overall record was an astonishing 51-6. He has returned to GCW to gain the only thing hes never achieved. A world title. GCW Bryan Mayhem is one of the original members of the Global Championship Wrestling, enjoying a successful career with the company. He is a former Tag Team Champion, a two time Television Champion, and a former World Champion. He currently works with Shaman as Oblivion. He holds two records with GCW, both with regards to the Television championship. He ties Dan Rivero and Zippy for most title reigns with two, and he also ties with Jordan White for the longest title reign at 91 days. His first title reign came when Mike Savige, anxious to continue his amazing streak of successful defenses, issued an open challenge for the TV title. Seeking to make an impact worthy of a spot in the Devil's Night main event, Bryan Mayhem answered and soundly defeated the dominant Savige to win his first GCW singles title. After shocking the world (and himself) at Devil's Night by burying Bryan Mayhem alive, Chrissy-Poo found himself the target of the demented monster. Reborn as Bryan O'Neil, the big man faced Chrissy at NC-17 in a ladder match for the title. But Chrissy shocked him again, grabbing the belt and becoming the new TV champion, reborn himself as the dynamic Chris Kannon. His second title reign would come ending Jordan's record title reign. Mayhem would go on to hold the title again until he was defeated by his later partner Shaman. His World title reign came against Ambrosi, who held the title for some time, garnering more and more respect as one of the world's most talented wrestlers. However, GCW veteran Bryan Mayhem, after six years with the company, won a battle royal on WorldWide to earn his first ever GCW Title shot. The two met in Las Vegas at GCW's supershow High Stakes in an intense match, with Lia throwing everything she had at the monster Mayhem. But Mayhem's size was too big an obstacle to overcome. Mayhem finally manhandled Lia into a pinfall, and after many years of waiting, finally capturing the biggest prize in the game. Ambrosi quickly declared her intention to obtain a rematch with Mayhem, but Mayhem continues his dominance of her for several weeks. Mayhem tore through the competition on a wildly successful and dominant reign, before finally meeting Lia once again at Warpath. Again Mayhem dominated the match, but Lia proved herself worthy of the shot by mounting a successful attack. Eventually Lia managed to topple the big man with an insane slam off a ladder, and three counts later she won back her World Heavyweight Title. Bryan Mayhem has been with GCW from the beginning (brief absence for about a year) and is a future Hall of Famer. After having a taste of the gold he has vowed to win the World Title again. He has joined forced with Shaman and formed the most destructive force called Oblivion.
