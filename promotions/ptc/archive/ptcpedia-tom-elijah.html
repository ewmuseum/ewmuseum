Elijah is known well throughout independent promotions for competing in creative mixes of deathmatches, hardcore matches, and brawls. Along with this, he has been the subject of much controversy as he has become infamous for being stiff with opponents, no-showing events when he feels like it, and showing up to events possibly inebriated. As of October 2009, he has signed on with J. Leslie Voss' Awesome and Win Entertainment. His plans are unclear at this time, but what can be said for certain is that his intentions can not be good.
