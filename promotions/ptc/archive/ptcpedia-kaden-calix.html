Calix has, within the previous year or two, begun to rise to a fame that many before him has achieved. Originally from Athens, Greece, his wish of coming to America to wrestle was granted to him by a willing wrestling trainer/promoter/sponsor/and former superstar, Justin Michaels. Calix's first venture into the American wrestling scene was in Primetime Central's GTT5 tournament. Shortly after losing out of the GTT5 tournament, Calix was picked up by, and signed a contract with, Global, where he stayed until the final days of the federation. Kaden has just recently signed a contract for GCW through some very unorthodox recruiting by Steven Caldera, and he will be attending weekly events, while still finding himself locked up inside of a mental institute in Wichita, Kansas the rest of the week.
