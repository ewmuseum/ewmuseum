Mount Saint Hell's comes to PTC with a fiery bang as the Survival team Main Event! The Match: There are 5 levels to Mt St Hell's. Each level stands 10 feet off the ground, for a total of 4 levels of steel. Each level has a 45° angled Ramp to the next level. The first level is floor level, at 50' x 50'. The second is 40'x40'. The third, is 30'x 30'. The fourth, 20'x20', and the final is a 10'x10' platform. The ramps are opposing each other on the diagram, and the final level does not have a ramp to it. Each team must work like a team to make it to the top. If one member of CORE is kicked to the bottom, the other members of CORE cannot advance another level until their partner catches up with them. They're welcome to go down and help him as well. The winner is the first TEAM to make it to the top of the Mountain. + Match 8 - Main Event + Primetime Central Global Title Match - Former Champions Rematch Saij (XUW) (c) defends against Jesse Jamester (GW) and Seymour Almasy (UWF) Site Built by apdesign studios.
