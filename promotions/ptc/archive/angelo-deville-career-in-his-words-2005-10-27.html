Tell us about your character

27 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Angelo Deville Alignment: He's gone from consummate heel to a tweener with sometimes crippling bouts of conscience... Arguably a face now. Key Wins: In order of occurrence... The Undertaker~!, in his first-ever match, in WarWarFederation (WWF~!), after stealing from The Rock and advising The Deadman do certain things with his 33-pound head. >=) Sir Turnip, in WEEF. This guy was the first person in EW I ever looked up to. He wrote my first match and entertained the hell out of me with it, to this day he's one of the best roleplayers I've ever seen, and, yeah, it just kicked ass when I got to face him. Soulfire, in WEEF, World Heavyweight Championship match. Deville won the title by powerbombing him off the roof of Madison Square Garden. Deville's first -- and only -- World Title. He never lost it. Azrael (later changed to Aaron) Goeren, in PWA... Goeren was already on his way to PCW when the match happened, but Deville still takes great pride in the victory, despite Adrian Voltage's senseless inclusion to make it a triple-threat match. (Fuck you all, still; Goeren pwned.) Nerezza, in PWA... He and Deville had teamed the week before and won, having conspired brilliantly, so Hessian decided to make them fight... Exploiting the brilliance of FirePro, Deville won by making Rezz tap to his own signature hold. It was Deville's final match in PWA. Jason O'Neil, twice, in FSW, once on the second edition of the new era's show, and then again for the vacant Primtime Title, which Deville never lost. The first match set up the rivalry and the tone for the feud that would sporadically continue for the next two years. The title victory was the first title Deville had ever won as himself (he wrestled as Pump, "The Purple-Headed Warrior" in WWF and WEEF), and it was infinitely more important, intriguing, entertaining, and respected than the world title feuds/reign/itself. >=) Azala Zameer and Ming, in FSW, Primetime Title match. Heralded the formation of Mob Rule, with Deville, Tyler Nelson, Ming, and Chris Chase as the founding members. If Mike (Magnum Wolf) was allowed here, I'd ruthlessly mock his decision to refuse entry in favour of starting a different stable, which was like WCW's Revolution to the original nWo in terms of comparative pwn. >=) Magnum Wolf, in FSW. Even management was so disgusted by Wolf's decision that they fed him to Deville on the next show. Important because Deville later felt bad over how hard he slaughtered Wolf, leading to his agreement to feud with him in PWC. Kimbusa, semifinals of GTT3. Deville had just defeated Frankie Bones, while Kimbusa had just upset Violence Jack. This is the match I was most worried about throughout the entire tournament, infinitely moreso than the final, and I put way, way more thought and effort into it than any other round, even the final. Inphino Blitz, GTT3 Championship match. I was pretty contemptuous of Blitz even then, especially after he started posting his roleplays "against" me, so I just did enough to beat him, concentrating on tying up my storyline of pwn and counting on that to win it rather than going whole-hog... But it's still uber-important to me (and Deville) because it catapulted him into the spotlight and inspired Argyle and Justin to create the Universal Title, of which Deville was the inaugural lord. >=) Xavier Kannon, PCW Alliances, impromptu Universal Title match. When Mat approached me about helping to build and hype the belt by putting Deville over Kannon, albeit with gross interference, I was floored. Argyle was pissed off that we didn't clear it with him ("You went over my helmet?!" >=), but if you ask me, only good came from it, and it's the most honoured I've ever felt in the game. In-character, Deville turned it into an idolization and an obsession that we toyed with slightly in RPW, then finally explored, realized, and concluded in PRIME this past summer. Cassius Deamont, in FSW. Not only did Deville force him into retirement by submitting him with The Soprano, he took his bisexual valets too. Syko Arsonist, Universal Title tour to DWO. After months of bureaucratic red-tape and screwing around, Deville finally had his challenger from PTC named. Important for the fact that it was the first official defence of the title, it's when and how I met Reyes' monkeyass, and Callisto Bane(!) wrote the match. >=) Karina Wolfenden, in PWC. Karina was injured but refused to take time off, despite being booked against The Devil's Don. Deville came out and told her he'd understand if she wanted to skip out on the match, but since he was a sporting man, if she wanted to go through with it, he'd give her crippled ass five minutes of uninterrupted offence to try to put him away. The original version of it was cool, but Mat's rewrite for a roleplay in PRIME was absolutely brilliant. First meeting between Karina and Angelo. Ivan Stanislav, Universal Round Robin. After Deville lost to Kriegman and there was no one to step up to face the new Universal Champion, a 9-man round robin tournament was created to build a contender. The Legend of OSW, Ivan Stanislav, shocked the world by entering... Then got smacked the fuck down by Deville in the first round. >=) Skylar Green, Universal Round Robin. Green was the reigning PCW World Champion at the time, and Deville was eager to show him up as a further middle-finger to PCW for refusing to draft him out of the PWA. Plus, Sky pwned, even eyeless, so it was fun for me. I was a little too savage with my mockery, but Sky and I are still cool, so... >=) The only sad thing is that after the roleplaying for the next round, the game was called on account of Ron (sue me... I like the play on words). Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis, GTT4. I was just really impressed with the writing, moreso than with any of the others I faced in the tournament, and if I was going to lose before the finals against Doc, I figured this would be the place. Anyone in Face Off. Dem bitches f34r3d me. Period. >=) Baltasar, in PRIME. This was the first singles match I'd had in a fed since PWC, and the first roleplay match for a fed since PWA. More important because it's how I met Dean, who rules, and boy did we plan some shit. >=) Emilio Rage and Brandon Youngblood, in PRIME, Dual Halo Aftermath match. As the three runners-up in the Dual Halo, they were pitted against one another to see what would happen if it happened again without the pesky K-Wolf to monkey it up. ...You know what happened. >=) w/Justin Williamson (formerly The HIT) as Manifest Destiny, against Los Diablos, in PRIME, in a Mexican Prison. The feud ruled, and this is easily the most fun I've ever had planning and writing a match. Culminated with Deville executing the long-awaited backstab on Williamson as Manifest Destiny retained and the prisoners pulled an nWo ("We're... Taking... Over!"). Justin Williamson, in PRIME, following the backstab. It's not Almasy, but what the hell. >=) Brandon Youngblood, in PRIME. It was booked as a submission match since both men are specialists, and hyped through the roof since Deville has never submitted. Ever. A statement he can still make with a prideful sneer. >=) Xavier Kannon, in PRIME. The Million Dollar Match. ~wipes tear~ If you didn't pay attention, you don't deserve to know, and I'm not going to tell you. >=) Karina Wolfenden, at PRIME's Colossus II. That'll learn that uppity bitch to spoil Deville's big moment. >=) Huge turning point in what Mat and I had been liberally plotting and building since Deville first stepped through the PRIME curtain. Key Losses: pfft Fine. Fine. Master Fury, in WEEF, World Title match. This guy was hilarious. Sadly, when he got booked to face me for the title, he posted on the ooc board that he'd be gone and he'd have to no-show. I never no-show, so I put up a brief piece and left it at that... Then the morning after the deadline I logged onto the forum and he'd posted four roleplays overnight, all of which pwned. The fedhead made it a DQ victory because of the deceit, and I won the rematch, but I'll always remember this one. First and only time I lost because of sandbagging. "The Canadian Criminal" Chris Irving, in FSW. In the early re-opening of FSW, he was one of the many hapless incompetents who got a spot on the roster because they'd been there before, or, in the specific case of Irving, because he was related(!) to a "big name" from before. Dan told me I had to job to him because I was a relative unknown and this guy was the nephew/cousin/whatever of Duzza, or Doozer, or someone else I couldn't care less about, despite the fact he hadn't done shit and I was ruling... Important because it was Deville's first loss in WO/PTC, and it pissed me off so much that I made up my mind to definitely join GTT3. Who has the name value now, bitches? >=) Alan Kriegman, Universal Title tour to OSW. After a bunch of guys the administration had picked buggered off, it was decided that I'd get to select my opponent, so I talked to Dan, then called him out on the trashtalk board. I'll still argue that was some of the best trashtalk the board has ever seen, and the match itself ruled. First roleplay loss in WO/PTC. Clay Vendetta, in PWC. The Sleeps with the Fishes Match. Vendetta took exception to Deville "murdering" O'Neil by throwing him off Brooklyn Bridge, and told Deville to try that shit on him... So Deville proposed a match where the ring would be lined with aquariums full of electric eels. I got to book and write the match, and I wanted to play up Deville's mortality, so it was Deville who took the plunge. I really wish that Barney-Rubble-looking-motherfucker would've done more with it afterward. Jim Axtell, in PWC, World Championship match. Hey, someone with talent and character had to put over the champ, right? (Hi Penance.) >=) Magnum Wolf, in PWC, Hell in a Cell match. This feud kicked so much ass, and we'd built it up that it would be impossible for Wolf to win, and moreover that it was death for him to face The Deivlle in a cell, where he'd never before lost. Exceptional Dave Hurst, GTT4. Ended Deville's 15-match tournament winning streak. Second roleplay loss in WO/PTC. Brandon Youngblood, in PRIME, Tropical Turmoil number one contendership cluster match. Youngblood finally defeats The Deville, albeit by pinfall instead of submission. Deville hasn't been seen or heard from since. Key Feuds: This is taking longer than I thought it would... If I feel motivated later, I'll fill in details. Again, it's in order of occurrence (though some continued for years, and could be considered ongoing). Thunderhawk (first feud) Soulfire Jason O'Neil Xavier Kannon Magnum Wolf Los Diablos Karina Wolfenden Why Does He Wrestle: Beyond the requisite passion for wrestling, Deville got into the sport because of the stardom and celebrity by necessity denied in his regular life. Then, as with everything else in his life, it became a compulsion, a conquest for unchallenged dominance. Idols: Xavier Kannon Jason Snow Federations: WarWarFederation World Extreme E-Fed World Internet Wrestling Primetime Wrestling Academy Five Star Wrestling Pro Wrestling Circuit Old School Entertainment Rush-Pro Wrestling PRIME New Frontier Wrestling (Deville's also made guest appearances in Primetime Championship Wrestling, Old School Wrestling, Dominant Wrestling Organization, Universal Wrestling Federation, World Internet Wrestling [after he'd left the roster], a few other feds I'm forgetting, and several other feds I'm not even familiar with thanks to plagiarism. >=)
