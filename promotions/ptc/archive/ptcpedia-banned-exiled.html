Style | Quick Tags & Dirty Heel Tactics championships | GCW Tag Team Champion. handler | Bobby & Gerad Images | IMAGE CATEGORY NAME IF APPLICABLE Banned & Exiled~! is an American professional tag team in Global Championship Wrestling consisting of Chris Storm (real name, Scott Drake, born November 22 1977) and Markus Stone (born November 25 1980) best known for their anti-authority style and trouble making. The two returned shortly ago from serving an indefinite suspension following WorldWide 71 when the two pulled a prank on owner Steven Caldera. It was their second suspension from the company. They are currently the longest reigning GCW Tag Team Champions, still holding the titles after defending them against the Establishment (Steven Caldera, LeStatt Knight, & Peter Maxwell) on WorldWide 79 in a handicap match. As of now, Steven Caldera is hoping the ex-sumo wrestler Kimbusa can finally dethrone the longest reigning tag team champions, placing him in a match against the five hundred pounder at Rampage. After having passed the longest title reign in GCW, the two now are looking to hold onto the Tag Team titles for over a year straight. If they defeat Kimbusa, they will be guaranteed over one year of being the champions. Professional Wrestling Career The true origins of Banned & Exiled~! isn't clearly known, but it is believed they were formed as a joke backstage in WIW after both men were banned from any Primetime Central events. Others claim the two officially began their careers as Banned & Exiled~! during the first and only Primetime Central Tag Team tournament. The two defeated Jade & Lacuna Debris in the first round but Markus Stone had to pull out of the tournament before the start of the second round. Markus was replaced by a wrestler named Sin. Chris and Sin lost to the eventual winners of the tournament, The Phenomenons. After short stints in various promotions some years later, Markus Stone resurfaced in Global Championship Wrestling. His old partner, Scott Drake, was actively in Legacy of Champions. During the several down periods of LoC, Scott signed a pay-per-appearance contract with Global Championship Wrestling. The deal saw the reformation of a previous tag team, Banned & Exiled~! with his long-time friend from WIW, Markus Stone. The two are enjoying a lengthy title reign as the Tag Team Champions, winning the belt off the previous champions, UNITED, at the supershow High Stakes. Consequentially, the two have remained in their old ways and continue to get in trouble with authority. The duo were suspended indefinitely after WorldWide 71 after pulling a prank on owner Steven Caldera. This was their second suspension. They have also been fined almost $10,000 a piece in damages.
