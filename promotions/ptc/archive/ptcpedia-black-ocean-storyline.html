This is everything you need to know (w/o giving away spoilers) about the new Killer Pirates and Captain Jack Morgan storyline. This will be updated periodically. The story is currently taking place in various locations and can be seen weekly in GLOBAL federation promos written by that nefarious lunatic James von Doom who many claim to be "crazy" and "jobberific." When asked about this, he agreed with those assessments. :P Plot Synopsis: The pirate Captain Jack Morgan has found out about items known as Port Keys. These keys open portals at certain locations that can lead to anywhere in the universe, including alternate universe, mystical realms, and other planets. In a sense, you can end up anywhere. Each place you end up has a port key to go to the next portal and so and so forth. Jack and his pirate crew, which consist of Cave Hulk and the Killer Klown from Outer Space, have concocted a plan to sail the Black Ocean of the space and become the greatest pirates in history by reaching the end of the universe. However, other forces want to stop them. There are more sinister beings out there that want the portals to be used for other more darker purposes. Jack and his crew must survive them before they reach what lies at the end. Will it be treasure? Will they find God? Or will they find something darker? This will be a [b]LONG[/b] storyline, think Dark Tower or Harry Potter. (I know what it is and it's a surprise) [u]Good Guys:[/u] Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean - Captain Jack Morgan In the first chapter of the Black Ocean, Jack kills James Vaga on a small mountain on Illitharia in the first scene and obtains the Port Key to Hell. Then he and his crew decide that the best course of action is to find the Port Key located in Devonia that leads off world and take the Portal off planet. They want to do this to become the greatest pirates of all time. Once they leave Illitharia, they head to the Devonian port of Mammatus. The Mammatons are soon attacked by the Nephilim, causing the pirate and his crew to head south to avoid the battle. The only problem is Datth von Doom and his Ninja Army are waiting for them there. After killing all the Ninjas and evading Darth von Doom, the pirates escaped into the Devonian Northern Plains where they survived a Dire Wolf attack. They then ended up in the Hills of Satan where Wolfen met up with them and they ended up at the village of the Hulks. Shaman Hulk then told them of a prophecy about three men who would journey through the stars and reach the end of the universe. After this, the Pirates ended up in the Devonian Badlands where they survived a Giant Land Crab attack by killing the beast. They then end up in Goblin Valley where they fight off four Goblin warriors before witnessing the Knights of Rodhi attacking the Goblin army, thus giving them a better chance of sneaking into Rodhi at nightfall. The three pirates then are led by Jack into the Petrified Forest. There they witness a Hades Corporation employee surveying but decide to move on rather than investigate. After surviving walking through desert baking under the unforgiving sun, the pirates finally rest upon reaching the grassy coastal area only to have Hulk save Jack from being attacked by Zombie von Doom whom he kills. After killing Zombie von Doom, the Pirates waited until nighfall to sneak past the fortress of Omega Centauri and into the Kingdom of Rodhi. Upon sneaking past the Knightly guards, the pirate stole a small pouch of coins and the priates were safe until the clown broke wind. When the Knights heard this, they chased the pirates into the grasslands of Rodhi until they reached the Golden Throne. After the pirates escaped from the throne after a quick battle with the pirates, the Evil Ones circled the pirates. The clown used his white magic powers to help them escape to the Golden Stairs just as the Knights arrived and got into a skirmish with the Evil Ones. After escaping from the Evil Ones, the Pirates ended up running through the Valley of the Gods before finding the Port Key leading Off-World. Then they survived a quick skirmish with the Evil Ones before running to Oberon and leaving the planet through the portal. After exiting through the portal, they ended up in Metal City on the planet Ore. Once there, they were soon taken to the Mayor's office and he let them leave the domed city without incident. Upon leaving, they ended up in the dreaded Rusted Lands where they ended up in the ancient ruins of the planet's Great War. There they slayed a humanoid cult and the dragon they worshipped before finding the second Port Key and soon leaving the planet through the portal. Thier Allies: Wolfen, Wolfen was a college student in Slovakia before he was hiking one night and bitten by a wolf. The next day he woke up and was a werewolf. He was ostracized by his family and exiled from the mountain hamlet in which he lived. Now, he is a monster on the prowl in the dreaded Hills of Satan. His best friend is the Cave Hulk and they hate everybody. After trying to use his new stalking technique, he is caught by Hulk and Klown. Wolfen then joins the Pirates as they journey through the Hills of Satan and to the Hulk village. There, he leaves the Pirates and goes back into the wilderness from whence he came. Will appear on Future Earth. Knights of Lagoon, the Knights of Lagoon were forced to retreat from their home planet and go into hiding here after the Birds of Prey swarmed their kingdom at the behest of the Piscean Empire and plunged the once peaceful realm into chaos. After jettisoning from the planet in an escape pod, the Knights landed on Earth next to a wrestling show. There, they were followed inside by the Birds of Prey and survived only by pretending to be professional wrestlers. They did such a good job, however, that they got hired to do it full time. But only time will tell if the Birds of Prey and the Piscean Empire are done with their attacks or if this is just the beginning.
