GTT7 rules

What this is (written in 2026) The GTT7 tournament site’s own rules page, as the donor’s database holds it (lot 0159). The page names no date: it says the tournament was expected to run from early October to mid December 2009 and that 73 applications had been accepted. No capture of the page is held. The judging-panel heading is followed by its explanation only; the donor’s copy lists no names. The page’s navigation and the markup of its headings are not reproduced; its text is.
Rules What: GTT is a singles elimination roleplaying tournament. When: The tournament is expected to run from early October through to mid December, 2009. Who: GTT7 is an open invitation tournament. That means anyone can enter. The tournament director is Pete, PTC administrator. How: Registered entrants compete in the tournament by roleplaying against their nominated opponent. The roleplays are judged by peers of both entrants, and the entrant deemed to have posted superior work moves on to the next round. Why: GTT7 is provided by PTC for e-fedding enthusiasts to have fun competing against each other in matches they would not otherwise experience. There is no expectation on any of the Free Agent entrants to sign with a PTC-affilliated fed after playing in GTT. Cool… so how many people are going to be in this thing? 73 applicantions have been accepted. Damn, that's a lot of people… so how much work am I going to have to do each round? What's the RP Limit? That will vary each round. The first round will have a 1RP limit. Subsequent rounds will see increases in the RP limit, at the discretion of the tournament director. Did you just say it could be 5 RP's in the final round? Why so many? Why not three like usual? A RP limit is a maximum, not a minimum. Winning GTT is supposed to be difficult, so don't expect an easy ride all the way to the finals. How long is each Roleplay period? The duration of each Roleplay period will be announced by the tournament director at the start of each round. The field will be split into two halves, which will alternate between roleplay period and judging period. So, what are the RULES? One Character per Handler: Handling two characters in this tournament takes away a spot from someone else. Anyone found handling multiple characters will have each character expelled from the tournament. Plagiarism: Anyone found to be stealing the work of others and posting it in this event will be banned from participating in any future PTC events, for life. No Recycling: All Roleplays posted must be original for this event. This means that they are not to have already been used in your home fed. They may be archived in your home fed after you have finished participating in the tournament, not before. They are not to be used for competitive purposes in your home fed, period. If you are found doing this, you will be barred from future PTC events. Quoting: If you use snippets of other people's work, or quote song lyrics, or anything else that you didn't personally write, you MUST quote your reference. Linking: All Roleplays must be posted on the GTT7 website using a username and password determined during the application process. Roleplays linked and hosted elsewhere will not be considered during judging. Deadline Extensions: Are extremely unlikely to be granted, and definitely not when it is not something that is our fault. Time zone differences will not be accepted as an excuse. There will be countdown clocks posted on this website to determine the precise ending of the roleplay period relative to where the player is in the world. Login troubles will not be accepted as an excuse. It is the responsibility of the player to test the login and password combination given to them at the earliest possible time, even if they don't intend to roleplay at that point. The short of it is that the Tournament Director will be extremely unwilling to hold up the entire tournament for the benefit of one person, even if they are in fact being shafted by this not being done. Think Star Trek II - The needs of the many outweigh the needs of the few. GTT7 judging panel These people are required to make an attempt to reasonably justify their pick. Nothing more, nothing less. They are not required to give you detailed feedback or tips. They aren't your English teacher or your older brother or sister. Their job is to pick the winner of a match, and they should be able to do that without being molested afterward by people with sour grapes. They potentially have a lot of work to do to meet their judging commitments, so can't be expected to give hugely detailed comments. Comments are an added bonus, not a right. As the tournament goes on and people are eliminated, the judging panel changes. Eliminated people step up and help out, and people who judged previous rounds have to attend to other commitments, and therefore the judging panel is subject to change.
