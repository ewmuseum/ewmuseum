Wildlife Biography Biography Wildlife is an extreme recycler. He has his own stable known as Recyclatude. Other members include a lackey coming into his own known as Kidd, the super hero Captain Planet, and a man who has several personalities (Forest Ranger, Australian Pirate, and Avenging Mormon) named depending on his character at the time. He has held a few titles in XUW such as the IC, Tag Team, Hardcore, Submission and maybe others. He is about 6'0 and weighs 235, he is a heel unless you recycle then he's the biggest face you'll ever meet. He doesn't have any outstanding wrestling technique, it's a bit technical, with some risk moves such as a 180 legdrop from the top rope… actually that's the only risk move. The following is a move list: Set Up Move: Force of Nature (Reverse STO aka Kanyon's Flatliner ) Finisher: The Greenhouse Effect (Matt Hardy's 'Side Effect') Other Moves: Basic Moves Running Calf Kick Butterfly Suplex (In memory of that Butterfly Darkseid ate) Flash Magic/Shining Wizard Belly-to-Belly sideSuplex Swinging Neckbreaker Signature Moves: The Global Warning 180 Leg drop off the top turnbuckle. The Biodiversitator When Wildlife and opponent are back to back, WL reaches back and put them in a reverse headlock. He will then arch his opponents back and pick them up for a submission by holding his kneck and legs and arching their back. If they do not submit he drops to his knees for a back-to-back backbreaker. The Deforestator Spinning Reverse DDT The Canopy Snap Puts his opponent into an old school style Figure-four, but then rolls onto his stomach putting some of the pressure onto himself. He then grabs the ropes and forces himself to stand up, unfortunately it is very hard to maintain balance during the move. South American Legsweep Puts opponent in a fullnelson submission, steps beside of them to perform a modified Russian Legsweep. Other Feds XUW
