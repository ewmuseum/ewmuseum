Further information might be found on the talk page or at requests for expansion. About the SWF: We are an e-federation with several unique qualities. We use HeroMachine 2.5 to illustrate our characters as well as Macromedia Flash Pro 8 to produce our interactive website content. We have a large cast of NPCs you can roleplay against or join forces with. Player vs Player interaction by roleplaying and working out angles and storylines are also options. We have two shows that alternate on a monthly basis. They are the SWF Saturday Night Sizzler and the SWF Monday Night Riot. These shows are available to viewers to read via a posted online transcript at exactly 7pm Eastern Standard Time on the appropiate day of each event. We have creative writing team opportunities behind the scenes if anyone is interested. The SWF is fictionally based in Orlando Florida. SWF TV is affiliated with the Titan Entertainment Network which is a 24/7 pro wrestling channel in the SWF Universe. We are also proud to be apart of the ever evolving PTC Community. In the Superstar Wrestling Federation, players have the option to create a unique character (the more original the better) or manage an SWF Superstar from a wide variety of Non-Player Characters (NPCs). There are several rules/guidelines explained on how to go about doing this at the SWF website. We are currently developing a SWF e-Cash Money System to enhance gameplay beginning in January 2009.
