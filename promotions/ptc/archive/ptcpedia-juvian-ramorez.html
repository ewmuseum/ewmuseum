He is most well known for his run as a wrestler in Next Generation Wrestling, and The Former owner of HATE Wrestling. He currently uses the moniker of "The Last Orginal Saint". Early Life TBA Career Next Generation Wrestling Juvian Ramorez ended up in next generation wrestling after a failed attempt to gain world championship gold in Chris Harmons' Animosity Wrestling. Juvian Would stay here at NGW for only three months but would be considered one of the NGW's most talented performers. Furiously Addictive Wrestling Juvian Ramorez was a main player in FAW, he captured the Hardcore title on his second match and went on to hold the title for over three months, he then won the tag team titles on the first of many occasions with then world champion "Spawn". After fueding for the world title Juvian Ramorez went on to win the International Championship before leaving the company when it closed its doors. Domminent Wrestling Organization Juvian Ramorez was hired and then fired for failing a medical, due to suspicious drug use. He later re-applied after serving time in rehab, however Juvian got caught up in a love triangle with Rayne Lee which led to his ultimatium firing, leaving poor old Juvian fedless once more. Global Wrestling Juvian Ramorez was a breakout star in Global Wrestling, having claimed victorys over the likes of Jesse Jamester(who at the time was the PTC Champion), Seymour Almasy(also former PTC Champion) and His good friend John "The Prophet" Adams. Juvian Ramorez lasted in Global for the best part of six months where he enjoyed many fueds inclusing one with his future brother-in-law Kenji Lee. However leading up to a Pay Per View named Crossfire Juvian Ramorez Shocked the world leaving for Wrestling Midwest. Wrestling Midwest Juvian Ramorez joined Globals Rival promotion Wrestling Midwest for the chance to work with Noah Hanson, a man who Juvian had idolized for many months whilst working over at Global. And when Juvian Ramorez was able to make the jump he did just that. After months of working with Corvus, Juvian Ramorez was able to capture his first title, thus being the tag team championship with a un-realistic partner in Scottywood. However it was only a matter of time before Scottywood and Juvian would end up hatign each other, Juvian would turn his back on his partner and walk out of the company leaving Scottywood to defend the titles alone. Lights Out Wrestling When Juvian Ramorez joined the world of professional wrestling he was taught by one man named Digital Mortality, and though workign with his trainer in Global Wrestling, Juvian was able to get a contract in Lights Out Wrestling. LOW Was considered the prime company in PTC for many years and will also been known as one of the most established federations to be around. Juvian Ramorez was part of the new breed over at LOW, his most memorable moment being capturing the Extreme championship from Psymon, Juvian went on to retire from this company without losing the Extreme Championship. HATE Wrestling HATE wrestling was Juvian and Scottywood's own creation. Born in the dark streets of New York City, it was a place that Juvian could sit back and watch teh profits roll in from a stand point as a owner of the federation. He recruited an all star line up which featured some of the talent known as Aceldama, Jesse Jamester, Digitial Mortality, John Adams, Vampir Nostatfu, Dane Rennier, Durian David Orthane and Wolfen. HATE Closed its doors when Juvian walked out of the company following alligations of a framed murder attempt on Aceldama.
