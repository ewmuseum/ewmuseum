Find out exactly how bad they got for Justin Malice by clicking here === 25 November 2002 ][EWC][ Can CORINTHIAN take over? Can CORINTHIAN take over? By Ted Mathews In the recent months since EWC left WFWA there has been a revolution and many long time fans may not have liked it. When EWC left the alliance so did many top name superstars, gone were the Kevin Cages, Shaken Not Stirreds, Black Widows and Raynes. But the ones that stayed worked hard and for that hard work took the show into a totally different direction. Then on Commercially Crazy many months ago it was announced the parent company of Vortex Industries who had owned the company since Mr. Botherway had left the company to receive treatment for his alcohol addiction. The new owners were also a surprise when two heroes from EWC’s past Mr. Monday Night and Demented Dave. They quickly removed Bungle and Ryan Shaffer from their staff duties and suspended English Trilogy Champion Dark Wolf after he failed a steroid test and was sent straight to rehab. Then came CORINTHIAN, their self proclaimed ‘Promoter’ Mr. Dixon brought two top names from the world of professional wrestling, from Japan he brought a former shoot fighter turned wrestler Eriji Yamada and from Mexico he brought Gonzales Constallio. Then week by week he brought in new faces to his ‘gym’ which include former Olympic judo silver medallist Igor Yeltzin as well as bringing in former EWC Comissioner and Assistant Commissioner Ryan Shaffer and Bungle. CORINTHIAN did not like the direction that Demented Dave and Mr. Monday Night were taking and decided that they would take over. A cage match was signed between Eriji Yamada and Bungle taking on Demented Dave who brought EWC play by play announcer and former English Heavyweight Champion Big Daddy out from retirement to wrestle a brutal match which saw Yamada being powerbombed through a twenty foot cage onto thumbtacks. But that was not the end, the match which was originally signed because Yamada super kicked Mr. Monday Night out of his wheel chair was just the tip of the ice burg. In the weeks to come CORINTHIAN decided to run wild on EWC programming including stealing the Heavyweight Championship belt during a title defence between Sin and Kris Kollins. That saw a match being signed between CORINTHIAN and EWC in which the winning team would have control of the Heavyweight Championship, that match saw Sin win the match and enabled EWC Board of Directors Mr. Monday Night, Demented Dave and Big Daddy to keep control of the booking of the Heavyweight Championship. Now the match has been signed and EWC WAR GAMES match which will pit two teams of five inside a cage with a wrestler entering every five minutes, the winning team takes control of the company once and for all EWC or CORINTHIAN. Eriji Yamada, Bungle, Hell Razor and Ryan Shaffer taking on Dark Wolf, Ice, Sin, Big Daddy and Big Dogg Teddy Ted. But what would it be like if CORINTHIAN took over? Mr. Dixon is a big fan of brutal matches, not necessarily gimmick matches but ones in which no man will ever be the same, if he takes over we could see a much more rougher and violent EWC. Some would argue that would not be a bad thing and that EWC could with being spiced up. Others including the EWC Board of Directors believe that not only will a CORINTHIAN product turn off the fans but it will be hard to find athletes and wrestlers that would be willing to train hard and work the sort of matches that Dixon would want. Dixon is also known in the business as being extremely tight with his money, it is hard to believe that he could even bring over the International talent that he has, if he takes over EWC prices will go up and wages will go down. From looking at other products and projects that Dixon has been involved in other the years they have not all been very successful but he has always made money out of them. If CORINTHIAN take over, there will be dark days in store and many jobs and livelihoods will be at stake. Source: P1W === 30 November 2002 [NBW] Chris Collins Chris Collins has come into this federation from XCWF and has made a big impact as of late becoming the first No Boundaries Wrestling Heavyweight Champion when he beat Duff in an over the top battle royal at Truth or Consequences, NBW's first pay-per-view. Chris Collins feet touched the ground, but the officials didn't see it so on the Chaos following ToC, Duff came out and showed a video and it clearly showed that Collins' feet did touch the ground. The new President, Dave Stevens, made a match for that night that would be the main event. Chris Collins against Duff with special referee, Dave Stevens. The night progressed and it was finally time for the match. Chris Collins won after a brutal match as Dave Stevens made a quick 1,2,3 count in favor of Chris Collins and he retained his title. That was the beginning of an alliance that Dave Stevens and Chris Collins would have. Source: NBW
