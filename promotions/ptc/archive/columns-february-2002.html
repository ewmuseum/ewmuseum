The Bastard Speaks #1 1/28/02 The Bastard Speaks #1: Creating a Real Wrestler By “The Bastard” Brian Chapman “Screw you guys, I’m going home.” – Eric Cartman First of all, I expect many of you have heard this before. Shame I don’t care. I’ve seen hundreds if not thousands of e-wrestlers and they all seem to have one major thing in common. A lack of touch with reality. The problem starts with the gimmick itself. Who exactly is going to want a ex convict to hold a title? Even Vince had more sense than to give Nailz a belt, any belt in fact. So just how many people have got ex prison sentences in e-wrestling? More than you can imagine. It’s one of the more overused gimmicks on the web, and in reality it just wouldn’t happen. Think how “Sports Entertainment” would see it. Because frankly, any federation where a ex convict gets over is going to have to be trashyard wrestling, and look at how ECW ended up. No TV contract and no money. “Come partake, tattoo” – Ricardo Montalbon Next up is description. In particularly, tattoos. Let’s look at the WWF. How many people exactly have tattoos... three I can think of that have major tattoos... Lita, Taker, DDP. Others might have tattoos but it’s not nearly so obvious... I believe the Godfather has tattoos, but all are covered by his ring wear. So how many are there in e-wrestling? Well I did a quick count and it amounted to somewhere near the 45 to 48 percent mark. 48 eight percent with tattoos is ridiculous. Some range from offensive words tattooed on the chest, while others go down to little hearts on the arms. Well the demographics of the United States of America state that people think of people with tattoos as being thugs, and frankly, Mother’s don’t like their kids buying t-shirts of people they think of as thugs. Think about it. “HE COULDN'T EVEN SELL GIRL SCOUT COOKIES IF HE TRIED!” – Rent a Hero How many people in the real world no-sell moves? Kane. Undertaker. How many people expect their e-wrestling characters to no sell... about one in seven. Reason being that Taker no-sells is that he isn’t half the wrester he was five years ago and eleven years of wrestling is taking it’s toll on his body to the extent where his back will no longer let him sell a move properly. With Kane, it’s his damned gimmick. When Angle made him tap it looked really really stupid because he no sells the rest of the time. That’s another thing, at least if you’re going to do this, do it continually... it looks dumb no selling half the time. “Drugs are bad, m’kay?” – Mr. Mackey Drugs. Drugs are bad, m’kay? Seriously, drugs are bad for ratings. The moment a television show endorses drugs in an open manner, ratings go down because of the PTC, the real PTC that is, not the Primetime one. They phone your TV company and all of a sudden your television contract isn’t being renewed. What’s worse than drugs? Things like Murder of any form... a death on television is REALLY bad. It’s not to be taken lightly and have a fake death... and what’s worse than murder? Rape. Rape should not be used by anyone anywhere in any sort of e-wrestling. I saw an angle involving rape at the e-fed called Asylum and well, I will never visit their site again because of it. It is uncalled for, and something as mentally damaging as that should never ever be used in any context. So I ask all of you until next time. Get a flaming grip. The Bastard. NTICW Spotlight w/ Jim Smitty 1/26/02 With NTICW back open and the opening card announced, I figured it was time to leave the owner story alone and get onto more pressing issues, the NTICW roster itself. So I traveled to the state of Georgia where Damien Becket was getting ready to make the trip up to Vancouver and I was able to catch up with him in a local coffee shop. Smitty: So Damien tell all the NTICW fans a little about yourself. Beckett: Well, I grew up right smack-dab in the state of Georgia. I loved huntin' fishin' doin all that boy stuff. I was smart in school, and my father had big plans for me. You see, he was the governor. And he wanted me to follow in his footsteps. Smitty: So you became the governor? Beckett: Indeed I did, instead of following my dream of becoming a professional wrestler I ran for governor and won. I was happy with my job, until my father just happened to slip and fall, causing his death. I was ready to pursue my dream of becoming a wrestler Smitty: I'm sorry to here that Damien. What happened next? Beckett: Well, I got involved with the PWA. After a few short days in that I was promoted up to NTIC...WHATEVER.. And here I am now, living up my dream as a wrestler! Smitty: NTICW you mean of course. Beckett: Yes...Yes... Of course... Smitty: So what do you think of your first real road trip ahead? The trip that will send you to Vancouver on the 8th before trekking up to Anchorage, Alaska for the debut of Tuesday Mayhem Beckett: Well, I think of it as an adventure. Traveling with these little ragamuffins that I am working with wont exactly be a prize. And we have to go too such damn cold places. Why Canada and Alaska. First of all, Canada has nothing going for them and Alaska...who lives in Alaska? What is the attendance rate at this arena? 4? Smitty: Well actually a large turnout of Polar Bears and Seals are expected to fill out the majority of the crowd. Of course the TV ratings will be huge Beckett: Seals and Polar bears? What the hell is this NTIC...W? If you expect them to chant my name like the fans damn well should be doing you better get some humans to fill those damn seats! Smitty: Well I am a simple reporter, I'm merely stating the fact don't get mad at me Beckett: Was that a threat? Smitty: Why would a reporter threaten a wrestler? Let's just get on with the interview ok? Beckett: Ok. I was just checkin. Caus' if it was. You might just accidentally have something slipped into your drink...you know...accidentally... Smitty: Whatever, what do you think of your first opponent, Mason Creeper? Beckett: Mason Creeper, aka Mr. Creepy? He aint Creepy. You know what I think is creepy? I think it’s creepy that anyone took seriously that he could stand up to me. When I get with him in the ring, in my first NTICW match, he will regret ever taking up the sport. Smitty: And what do you want to accomplish in NTICW? Beckett: I want to become the one man everyone fears, and everyone hates. That’s what I want. I want people to think "SHIT! They put me up against Beckett!" y'know? Smitty: If you had your pick of OSW or PCW which would you choose? Beckett: I think I would choose OSW. I like the overall atmosphere in that federation. Also, I would not be the only newcomer there. Since the opening of OSW there would be more new guys I could relate to. Smitty: What other feds in PTC look impressive to you, or are you only interested in the big two? Beckett: well, in PTC not many of the federations are eye catchers. I have taken a look at some of the other federations' talent and there is a fair amount. I mean, we got Mr. Tony V. himself in EWA and Jeremiah Wilson in BMW. But those federations aren’t my type. I like to be in the spotlight all the time and each of the big two are a spotlight in themselves. Smitty: Have you thought about running for office again? Beckett: Well, since I became a wrestler they took me out of office that is true. I was moving out just as we shot my first promo. But as long as I have a career in wrestling I will not be involved in politics, but a little bit of it still runs through my blood. Smitty: Do you have any plans to make wrestling more politically correct now that you’re in NTICW? Beckett: Well, I seem to get this question a lot and here’s my most common response. If you really think about it, wrestling IS politically correct. Most everyone hates everyone else and there are some really bad guys who can never keep promises and some really good guys who always find a way out of things...that’s my view on it. Smitty: Interesting point there. One last question, will you be entering the 128-man tournament to crown the number one contender for Vampir Nosferatu's Global Title, and if so how do you think you'll fair? Beckett: I have in fact entered this tournament and I think I wont do too bad. There are some pretty big names chalked up for it. Some huge PCW and OSW stars along with some from smaller federations. I really don’t think I am that good enough to win, but I might get pretty far. And if I catch a lucky ride, who knows what'll happen. Smitty: A realistic answer from a man that seems pretty down to Earth. Thanks for your time Mr. Beckett, if there's any thing else you would like to add, now's the time. Beckett: I think I’m about done. Thank you for having me. Thank you Mr. Becket for your time and look for a promising career from this young and talented wrestler.
