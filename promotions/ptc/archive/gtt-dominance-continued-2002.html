GTT Dominance Continued!

2 Sep 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sep. 02, 2002 [UWF] GTT Dominance Continued! Chicago, Ill. (AP) -- Few residents of Chicago, Illinois can ever say they've truly witnessed the reported head of the UWF Board of Directors. Ever since his reported rise to power within the company, he's remained shroaded in mystery. ... mostly because he's a giant escaped gorilla who kidnapped the Board of Directors two months ago and has held the company at bay ever since. But let's not go there ... he's endeared himself to the company anyway, so it looks like he's going to stick around. Nevertheless, the head of the Board, known only as "The UWF Gorilla" to his friends and foes alike, made a rare appearance in front of the UWF Towers in Chicago Illinois tonight, on hand to celebrate with the victorious competitors in the latest round of the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. And really, what isn't there to celebrate for the mammoth spokesman and his golden boys? Since the first round of the GTT, the Universal Wrestling Federation has maintained a level of dominance unmatched by any other promotion. All four -- or maybe five, I really don't care enough to check -- representatives breezed through the first round, knocking off tournament favorites like Jordan X Shabazz, and ... well ... Jordan X Shabazz I say!! How many of YOU have beaten him? The victorious invaders -- Eric Cameron, Judgement, Digital Mortality, the Plague, and Kevin Starr -- were quickly joined by three more play-ins, with newcomer "The Final Fantasy" Seymour Almasy, Danny Jaye, Lance Rose, and ... um? Someone else. The UWF quickly jumped to a second place position in the standings after the first round, and immediately jumped out into first place with the ending of the second round. And now, as we head into the bracket finals, our hapless company stands in front of the bunch, leading OSW by two points and annihilating all other PTC feds. And in his press conference tonight, the UWF Gorilla was all smiles. As told through an interpreter: "We rule all." Asked to elaborate: "It's like this ... in the wild, you have the silver back gorilla. All of the other gorillas are his bitches. The UWF is the silverback ... the WT feds are our bitches, and the PTC feds are the children and chimpanzees we throw around when we're bored." Again: "The UWF would like to thank Seymour, Lance, and Digimon on their tremendous showing thus far. And you will be killed if you don't continue to win." After that, the press conference was quickly ended. The UWF Gorilla then ate his translater. We all fled in terror. Good luck to the UWFers!
