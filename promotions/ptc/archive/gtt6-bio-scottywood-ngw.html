Scottywood Biography Biography Owner of Next Generation Wrestling. Not you convential boss, and if you piss him off you will wish you were fired isntead of have to step into the ring with him. Known as one of the most sadistic wrestlers in NGW, he is willing to put his body though anything to entertain the fans and brutalize his opponents. Still wrestling in NGE, the company he created nearly 7 years ago he has seen the federation go through many changes from its start as XWF, into its changeover to NGW shortly after joining PTC in early 2002. A short merger with NTW in late 2002 say the birth of Animosity Wrestling which quickly died and NGW returned in January of 2003. April 19th, 2005 say the closure of NGW, but in July NGE opened up Hate Wrestling which had an almost one year run into April of 2006. July of 2006 say the return of NGW. Some would say NGW has been reborn more times then jesus…..or some say NGW just doesn't know how to die. While in NGW Scottywood has racked up one of the most impressive list of achievements of any superstar to ever step into its ring. He is a 4 time World Heavyweight champion and a 7 time NGW Xtreme champion. In total he has won 25 championships, including just about ever title the federation has ever seen. But won thing Scottywood has never done is had an impressive run in GTT. GTT3 was Scottywood's debut to the tournament, where he lost to Mike Meyhu in round 1. In GTT4 Scottywood advanced to round 2 with a bye where he was promptly defeated by WWA's Muppet Man. In GTT5 Scottywood was suppose to face Alec Lancer in the 1st round but was booted from the tournament due to his controversial and sometimes dangerous mouth. But with all that behind him Scottywood is ready to take on GTT6 and finnaly make that impact he has been looking for on a PTC tournament. Can The Hardcore Artist defeat Token Weed in the first round? Those pot heads are quite dangerous, but Scottywood plans to show him what a "hit" really is. Other Feds NGW
