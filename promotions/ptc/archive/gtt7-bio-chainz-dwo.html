Chainz Biography Biography Like many before him, Michael Sloan came from a broken home. Unlike most, it was broken because of him. His parents, were loving and caring folk who wanted the best for their son and daughter. Unfortunately for them it was not meant to be. Michael Sloan grew up and displayed strange signs at an early age, exhibiting signs of heightened intelligence coupled with heightened paranoia, anger, rage, and psychosis. At the age of 12 his parents were murdered. The case remained unsolved and is still being investigated, though many suspect Michael was responsible and he has done nothing to dissuade anyone. In fact, he has been seen smiling and boasting about it. His sister met a similar fate, though not intentionally. Michael became what many of his teachers feared; a sociopath and psychopath. His anti-social behavior heightened his lack of feelings for others. Having no sense of pity, regret, or remorse he has been known to do the most heinous of acts and caring less. The Police finally caught a break, when Michael was 18 he was finally caught half way through raping a prostitute or so she says; well the bruises said as well. Instead of going to trial he pleaded guilty and received 6 years in jail. He was not released early due to good behavior. He met his girlfriend and now wife, Tracy Stanton, before his prison sentence. She was lured in by his charm and was hooked and for the first time Michael could feel more than lust. For her, he is completely different hiding his wickedness as best as possible, but all it does is cause his outbursts when she's not around to be more violent and depraved. They've remained together, almost being unable to separate from one another as if their very existence depended on the other being there. Because of this, she's his prize, his trophy and to say he's protective of her would be an understatement. Of course, everything he says has to be taken with a grain of salt. Michael Sloan has been know to lie from time to time. Other Feds PRIME
