Bomb: Well it is a proud time for the NGW at the current time, everyone has been working their socks off to raise the standards within the NGW. Tournament and PTC Announcement Date: June 5th 2002 From the press office of the PTC Headquarters just the other day, a big announcement was scheduled. This to be on behalf of BOTH the XCWF and the PTC. Recent talks by Tom Mason involved that the R.E.B.E.L. Tournament was going to be huge. And some may know the tension surrounding this tournament by the PTC Front man, Mr. Batee. Batee disagreed with the decision for the XCWF to hold such an activity. Batee, of course, had his own plans and ideas for the upcoming PTC iPPV intitled: APEX. Promotional news for APEX began just days after Night of Champions. And Night Of Champions involved the naming of the APEX Main Event for the PTC Network Championship Title by the end of the night! Harbinger, Al Magz and the PTC Network Champion, Obsidian currently. That could of course always change in the next two weeks with Obsidians schedule to defend his title. Tom Mason was approached by Batee about this tournament on the very day of its original announcement. Condemming the tournament and its structure, Batee pulled any promotional support from the PTC and WeWa on the 64 Man R.E.B.E.L. Tournament. But recent talks in the past few days have led to a different mood between these two men. A brilliant idea on the half on Batee was brought to the attention on Tom Mason on June 3rd. The idea of introducing something new into the PTC Promotion and allowing the XCWF R.E.B.E.L. Tournament to play a special part in it. Batee had been planning and hoping to get some sort of big addition to the PTC in a prize sort of way. The idea of a new title, something with great value like the Network Title. Right now, the covetted title that Obsidian proudly wears for the WeWa promotion. Batee was rumored to have been constructing such and idea and here it is finally being drawn out. June 5th, the announcement came at the press release as Tom Mason and Batee shook hands with smiles on their faces and announced just this: "The XCWF and PTC have reached an agreement on the behalves of both myself [Tom Mason] and Mr. Batee. We have compromised on the idea of the XCWF R.E.B.E.L. Tournament and an activation of a great addition to the PTC franchise with the R.E.B.E.L. Tournament to host a very unique title to symbolize the hard work of the one dividual to win this tournament. A prize that without a doubt with always remind you of the XCWF." Tom Mason then handed Mr. Batee the podium allowing him to make the formal and out front speech: "The PTC is happy to give you... The PTC Extreme Championship Title! The blood, the sweat and the tears of every individual in the PTC. Something that will undoubtedly be cherished in the future. And the PTC is happy to allow the XCWF R.E.B.E.L. Tournament play a special part in that. The Winner of the R.E.B.E.L. Tournament will take home the PTC Extreme Championship Title from APEX on July 28th 2002!" With those words said, Batee and Tom Mason opened for questions. Many questions of course focused on the topic of the PTC/XCWF R.E.B.E.L. Tournament situation. Things like: Participant numbers, will they stay at 64 entrants or drop? Tom Mason and Batee said that at this point there are still going to be 64 competitors in this tournament. And they are hoping for this announcement to pull in a good deal of interest for them. Another reporter asked when they planned on begining this tournament and when the deadline for sign ups would be. As well as when and where matches would be held for this tournament? The rest of the tournament would take place on XCWF shows and events, house cards, Saturday Fear and even PPVs like Untitled and Unconstitutional. Tom Mason and Mr. Batee said that additional information would be provided in coming days. Some things were still being discussed and organized.
