NOW Power Rankings I won't list everyone who received votes. This will avoid too much drama from people not receiving many votes. If you're not on this list, don't worry. Just start working harder and do what you can. Remember, NOW is a fed full of talent. Making the top 5 is no easy task. Here's a breakdown of how it works: Each handler sends in their top 5. Their #1 receives 5 points, #2 gets 4 points, and so on. At the end, all points are totaled to come up with a base number. Now, any person who created a list gets an additional 2 points (since they created a list, they've given points to 5 people, some of which may not have made a list at all, which creates an unfair advantage for the list maker). After this is added to the base number, an additional 3 points is added to each score every time a wrestler makes a list. Here's an example: Johnny Wrestle created a list, but only one person put him on their list - at #2. Johnny would have a total of 4+2+3 = 9. Billy Pin didn't make a list, but he appeared on someone else's list at #1 (his only vote). Billy gets 5+0+3 = 8. With the list creation rule in effect, Billy can't overtake Johnny so easily. Basically, make a list if you want to be on an even playing field. Sitting there receiving votes without voting might hurt your overall ranking. Also, a list appearance point total has been added so that a person who appears on 4 lists at #5 won't be even with someone who only appears on one list at #2. That just isn't fair, either.
