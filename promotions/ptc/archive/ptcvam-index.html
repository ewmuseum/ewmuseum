Primetime Central presents: PTC vs Archibald MacGreggor! Home | Rules | Player Login | Roleplay Board | Discussion | PTC Website For the first time in PTC's long and windy history, a free agent holds a PTC singles title. The PTC Extreme Title. A title rich with history, a title held by some of the finest athletes ever to set foot in a wrestling ring. This should not be. The fact that it is is as a result of many things… luck, timing, arrogance and apathy. You draw the picture. Those with no small level of responsibility for allowing this travesty to happen - the PTC members who failed to prevent the free agent heathen from winning the right to challenge former champion Hoyt Williams in Infinite Gauntlet - have been charged with making things right. They have been charged with doing whatever it takes to return the Extreme Title to where it belongs - around the waist or over the shoulder of a proud PTC member. Primetime Central presents: PTC vs Archibald MacGregor Main Event PTC Extreme Champion Archibald MacGregor (Free Agent) vs The New Shit Bobino (FUSE) vs Karina Wolfenden (PRIME) vs Red Rock (AWC) vs Vivica J. Valentine (GCW) vs Cereal Killer (XUW) vs Damien Cruz (FUSE) vs Eryc Morgan (GLOBAL) Surely one of these people, by virtue of talent or pure luck, will walk out champion. (note, after the match, the rest are free to take out their frustrations on Macgregor, as PTC's insurance is fully paid up. The Uber Judge (XUW) (c) vs Shadow (GCW) Returning for only the second time in almost a year, the PTC Global Title takes a back seat to the priority of unseating MacGregor. The match is of no less important though, as immediately following PTCvAM, the Global Champion is going to be VERY busy. Shadow (GCW) won the right to challenge for the Global Title at last year's Super Series. Since then, Shadow has been quietly awaiting her opportunity. The Uber Judge also won the right to challenge for the Global Title at last year's Super Series. Although he fell to Paddy O'Shea in the first round of GTT5, he redeemed himself by defeating the reigning Global Champion and winner of GTT5, Rich "The Renegade" Rollins. This is a one fall match that will allow only one of these stars to walk out of PTCvAM with the Global Title. The other… well, there's always GTT6. What? PTC has tag team titles? Apparently so, and apparently management think that it is time to reinstate them. Miracles never cease! Four teams will vie to become the first PTC Tag Champs in nearly two years. Killer Pirates (GLOBAL) vs The Furious Fists of God (AWC) vs Team Baked (PRIME) vs UNITE (GCW) One team has the chance to put their names alongside such teams as Homo-Phobic, Windsor/Wolfenden, and… and… (someone help me here with some other teams that have held the PTC Tag Titles…) Time Remaining 0 days 0 hours 0 minutes ~ Promos ~ Site Header images by [name withheld].
