Source: PWA === 10 December 2003 PWA: In the eye's of Verlin. The following is a column from PWA Superstar, Payne. -------------- As anyone who keeps up with PWA would know, this last tuesday on Rampage tag teams competed against each other to win the right to go onto Christmas Carnage and compete for tag team gold. Team after team stepped into the ring and gave it their all. After the dust settled it was the tag team of Twizted Payne who came out on top. This earned them the right to go on and compete for the tag team title on the next pay per view. However, winning the gold will not be so easy. First they must beat a tag team that PWA officails have paired up. The big story behind this tag team is that no one knows who they are. That's right they are a complete mystery. This means that Payne and Twizted will have no idea who they are going to be facing. This makes preperation for the match almost impossible. Everyone in PWA have been speculating as to who this tag team could be. Some say it maybe returning PWA members. Others say that it maybe a tag team from another federation who are changing over to PWA. Or it could be the only team in PWA who did not compete in the tournament, the tag team of The Cat Pack. They of course would be the obvious tag team, but PWA officials have gone on the record to say that it in fact is not the cat pack and this is all they will say. Source: PWA === 12 December 2003 cW: Smash 35 Smash returns to the United States of America and close to Matthew Lanier's hometown of Orange, Texas, by traveling to Houston, Texas, for the thirty-fifth episode of Smash. Ringmaster marked the grand return of Matthew Lanier to power in Core Wrestling and there is said to be some massive changes once Core Wrestling hits the runaways at Houston International. Smash is rumored to get a redone look and new theme song as cW embarks on a new era of sports entertainment. Speaking of new era, joining The Alliance and Ed Gesumaria this week is New Era Wrestling owner, Bryan Chase. Fontaine and Fracas won the cW Tag Team Championships at Ringmaster and then plastered the cW gold with NEW paraphernalia. Could the actions of The Alliance at the end of their match lead to a confrontation between Matthew Lanier and Bryan Chase. Two new women are set to debut on Smash and participate in a Fatal Fourway Match with two other ladies from cW to see who will be the top contender for Artemis' Siren Championship. Artemis has held the title since it's creation on September 5, 2003, at Winner Takes All, but the talk in the back is that some of the newly hired ladies are looking to get their hands on that piece of gold. To make the match even more interesting, Lanier has named Artemis the Special Guest Referee for the match. Only two words can be used to describe this match: CAT FIGHT! A Classic Championship Contendership Match is also scheduled between Ringmaster runner-up, Skill Willie, Ted Masters and The Fenix. Fenix put his old tag team partner out of a job at Ringmaster and Skill Willie lasted a grueling battle for an hour against Jason Hughes. Skill Willie is the only man known to Core Wrestling Officials to stay locked in Hughes' No Man's Land for nearly a minute and not tap out. Skill Willie may use that fact against Hughes to try and get under his skin, but to get a title shot against Hughes, Skill Willie must first be victorious against Masters and the Fenix. And just to make it a little more interesting, Matthew Lanier through the Canadian Superstar, Ted Masters into the match allowing him the opportunity to a little revenge for being eliminated in the Gauntlet Match against Jason Hughes. But like Skill, to get his hands on Hughes, he'll have to go through the Fenix and Skill Willie. It is a known fact among internet fans of Core Wrestling that Matthew Lanier was bringing back Jack Diamond to Core. But what they were not expecting was a Jack Diamond versus Sidarian match up on his return Smash. Lanier made this match and was quoted to saying, "It's time to end this thing between them." The Icon Championship will not be on the line during the match, but you better believe both men's pride will be on the line. Sidarian is the one that put Jack Diamond on the shelf for nearly two months and Diamond is definitely looking for a little retribution. This and many more surprises are rumored to happen on Smash. Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling is proud to present its latest Pay Per View Supercard, December to Dismember Three, on December 14th 2003. The show, which was named two years ago by Henrik the King, is fittingly being headlined by a match designed by the very same wrestler, who is trying to regain the World Title he lost earlier in the year. This 'Torture Chamber' match will be a first in XUW, pitting six men against one another, to see who will end the year as XUW WOrld Champion. The match will take place in an enormous chained off chamber, which will house a ring, a moat of freezing cold water, and various torture implements outside the moat, should anyone be willing to try and get to them. Four competitors will start off behind a wooden drawbridge, each one being released at seven minute intervals, or whenever a pinfall or submission occurs. It is sure to be a sight for the ages, and is the first World Title match with such an extreme stipulation for almost a year, when Dave Kipfer fought The Ripper and Numark in a steel cage. Can Saij continue his run of form that led to him winning the Chris Williams Memorial Cup? Can The Anarchist retain the belt he won by pinning Saij just a few weeks ago? What about Dante, the crazy man who earned his title shot by winning the Halloween Battle Royal against everyone else on the roster? Could the winner be the Latch Key Kid, one half of the Lunchroom Patrol and the first homegrown XUDF star to get a World Title shot? You can't count out Ric Austin either, who has thrown his hat into the ring by relinquishing his Intercontinental Title belt for a shot at the number one prize in XUW... ...and then there is Henrik, the man so desperate for another shot at glory he invented a new match just for it. Also on the card, the Dark Alliance, part of the Ultra Violent stable, defend their tag titles against the Skeletons of Society. The DA have never lost a match together, but the Skeletons will be trying to wrench the power, and the titles, away from them. Another classic match is expected to be between Danyon and Bailey. These two men have been friends for what seems like forever, leading the New Royal Legion to be the most decorated tag team in XUW history. That's all behind them now as they meet in a Death Match that will surely finish them off once and for all. Before that we'll see battles for the Intercontinental Title, the Hardcore Title and the Submission Title, not to mention an opening tag team match and a match for the premier belt in the Xtreme Ultimo Development Federation, between XUW Hall of Fame member Woof Woof, and the current champion, Robert Cutter. Be sure to catch what all these stars have to say, and don't miss the show once it is on air this coming Sunday! Source: XUW 24 Hour Promo Channel === 16 December 2003 cW: Smash Episode 36 Smash returned to the United States this past week and did so with a bang. Alex Seeves was announced the new cW Commissioner as well as two contenders for a couple of Core Championships were named. Skill Willie defeated Fenix and Ted Masters in a Triple Threat Match to gain the next opportunity to face Jason Hughes yet again. This time, not for Ringmaster, but for the Classic Championship. Veronica Paige picked up the Siren Championship Contendership in a Fatal Fourway against friends and foes. Also on the show last week, Matthew Lanier announced that his birthday was the day after Smash Episode 36 and that he was in fact going to turn 36 years old. He went on to announce as a celebration of his birthday, he has named Smash 36 as Matthew's Birthday Bash. Lanier has booked a special night of matches he's always wanted to see. Lanier is in much anticipation for the night and also in high hopes that the NEW doesn't make it's presence known and ruin his celebration like they interrupted his returning speech this past week. For the second of the two part main event on Smash, we have two veterans of cW squaring off for the second time. The first time they fought was towards the beginning of both their illustrious cW careers. Their kinship with one another goes way back to the days of Blood Lust. The Icon Champion Sidarian and the Fenix meet one more time in the squared circle headlining Matthew's Birthday Bash. Veronica Paige also gets her grand opportunity at Artemis' Siren Championship. Artemis has held the title since winning the battle royal to obtain the title on the day of it's creation. Artemis has successfully defended it on several occasions and looks to defend it one more time. While Artemis looks to defend, Paige looks to obtain. Paige is new in Core Wrestling coming from the GCWF along with Jake Finale and Mercedes Styles. Could this be the beginning of a new reign for a Siren Champion or will Artemis continue to dominate the divas of cW. Lanier has also announced that the first of the two part main event of the night, Jantz Spalding will be defending his Legacy Championship. Matthew Lanier thought long and hard about who should have the greatest opportunity in Core Wrestling today. He considered past matches for both competitors and has deemed one man worthy of taking on Spalding for his Legacy Championship. This superstar fought through a rigorous battle in Ringmaster Round One. He overcame seven men being thrown amidst a Gauntlet Match. He even battled a long and hard sixty minute Iron Man Match. Lanier has graciously given Skill Willie a shot at stardom in Core Wrestling. Even though he holds the Contendership for the Classic Championship, his future battle with Jason Hughes may hold a much higher price. Can Skill Willie walk away with the Legacy Championship on Smash, or will Spalding continue his reign as a third time Legacy Champion. Magnum Wolf as shown in attendance early as the Wolf had bought a ticket to the show. Mason started the show off with a big announcement, the first ever RPW PPV Genesis. Just as Mason was about to make an announcement about the Tag Team division Peter Maxwell interrupts him. The business partner makes his way to the ring and makes a bigger announcement, the World title! Peter went on to say the fate of the belt will be decided by a Battle Royal were the final four will be facing each other at the PPV for the title. El Diablo shows up and announces that he plans to win the belt. The hatred was intense between El D and Mason and Maxwell made sure to help by showing his support for El Diablo! Two big PTC stars make their appearance as Jackson Rust goes and visits Chris Storm at his locker room. Jackson made sure to rub salt in the wound as he reminds Chris that he has now beat him three times following last weeks tag team win over Storm and Alyssa Sanders with Tessa Windsor’s help. Storm promises Rust and Tessa will get theirs in due time. The fans were able to get an update on Jason Marah as he was shown in his hotel room. Marah reminds the fans of the hate brewing between himself and Akiyama. Jason tells everyone the drug test will show he is not a steroid user and that he will face Akiyama. Sweet Illusion member Alyssa Sanders puts the moves on Jackson Rust but in true Rust fashion he shoots her down. Sweet Illusion will have the last laugh however as the rest of SI; Carys, Alotti, and Cruz attacks Tessa Windsor and leave her a bloody mess. Jackson is left to tend to his injured friend. In ring action finally is set as Brianna Washington faces Karina Wolfenden and El Diablo in a triple threat match. Though Magnum Wolf did try and put a damper on the mat with his own play-by-play the match was still the highlight of the night with amazing moves and high spots. There were near falls left and right and just when Karina was about to take the win Magnum made sure to distract her allowing El Diablo to hit his Sit-Out Liberation for the win. Jackson Rust tries his hat as a manager and inspirational speaker as he gives Tessa Windsor encouragement before her match. Tessa gets ready to face Carys later on the show. Backstage the normally light hearted Karina seemed a little angrier than usual. Her tag team partner for later and former opponent Brianna Washington tries to make sure they were still on the same page but Karina did not seem to want to communicate. Brianna is finally relieved to find out it was all just a joke by K-Wolf and the two are once more ready to give Civil D a match for next week. Once more we go back to the ring with Tessa Windsor vs. Carys. The action is dominated by Carys as she focuses on the ribs that were hurt in the earlier attack. Jackson Rust and Sam Alotti are at ring side for this one. It seems Tessa was out numbered as every time she took the advantage Alotti would cut it short. Tessa was finally able to beat the odds and win with her Final Vision, but the celebration was short lived as Carys and Alotti attack Rust and Tessa with a steel chair. Chris Storm shows up looking to help his former Schmoopies but only attacks Rust. El Diablo is seen after the match backstage were he tells of what is going through his head. The fans were disgusted as El D blames his deceased wife for his never reaching the top. He once more promises to be the first ever RPW World Champ. For the first time the Neo Void God appears with the Divine Prophetess this week. Akiyama and Cyclonia are met by Yakuza thugs. The disgruntled investors of the now closed X look to exact pay from Akiyama, who they blame for closing the X. The Divine Prophetess tricks the thugs into a room were Kaoru, a server of nVg beats them to a bloody mess. Once more Chris Storm appears as he goes to the SI locker room to thank them for the beat down on Tessa Windsor. Chris leaves with Alyssa as the next match starts. Kohana and Kaze take on Sweet Illusion as part of the Tag Team Tournament. Erika Cruz and Sam Alotti represent SI. The showcased great tag team action and SI looked to pick up a win when Sam Alotti tried to use hair spray to the opponent’s eye. There plan backfired when Kohana ducked causing Cruz to get the hair spray in the eyes allowing Kohana and Kaze to pick up the win after a Bridged German Suplex. The camera catches back up with Storm and Alyssa. Storm tells Sanders about his fear of Oompa Loompas only to be laughed at. Jackson Rust is nearby to over hear and runs off to form a plan. After leaving Sanders, Chris Storm ends up running into a look alike in Christian Crawford. After an exchange Chris makes a challenge for the newcomer Crawford for later. Before the match starts Chris Storm announces that if he loses the match that Joe Morris can name the type of match that will happen between the two at the RPW PPV Genesis. In the ring Crawford and Storm try every dirty trick in the book and some they made up. They suplex the life out of each other, and after several near falls Joe Morris appears from the crowd and levels Chris with a chair shot. The chair shot allows Crawford to pick up the win and Joe Morris getting revenge. The night does not get any easier for Chris as after the match Chris finds three very excited female fans. Sadly for Chris there is more than meets the eye to these woman and Chris is left wondering why he never noticed the Adam Apple. Joe Morris laughs it up revealing he set the entire thing up. The final match features Totakoxe Salvador taking on a mystery opponent. Peter Maxwell shows up first and takes a seat next to the play-by-play man. As everyone wonders who the mystery opponent is “Symphony of the Dead” played and a huge shock hit as Tristan Belmont shows up. The two fight an intense battle. No longer able to sit back and watch Peter Maxwell makes himself felt and brings a chair to Tota’s head. Tristan Belmont picks up the easy win after the interference. After the match everyone is surprised to see a wheel chair bound Gideon Poe. Poe announces he plans to try and wrestle for the number one contender spot. Peter laughs but the laughter dies when Poe kicks the wheel chair back showing he can walk despite Maxwell trying to take him out last week. Tota gets his revenge by attacking Maxwell and Belmont as the show comes to a close.
