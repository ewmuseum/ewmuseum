PTCpedia

the Primetime Central network's own encyclopedia

Placard

What   a MediaWiki encyclopedia of the Primetime Central network, written by its own members
Addresses   ptcpedia.primetimecentral.net, then community.primetimecentral.net/ptcpedia/
Captured   January 2008 – August 2010
Held   322 articles
Redactions   84 across 70 articles, per the privacy rule; each marked in place

An encyclopedia the network kept about itself: its member federations, their titles and tournaments, and the characters who worked them — written in-community, article by article, by the people whose characters they were.

What it holds

PTCpedia's articles are of two kinds and it is worth knowing which you are reading. A character article is written from inside the fiction: it gives a wrestler's billed birthplace, finisher and title history as the character's own biography, and says so — Seymour Almasy's own article warns in its second paragraph that his early history “has been subject to modification and outright falsification due to his RPG-oriented gimmick.” A federation, title or tournament article is closer to a record: a roster, a bracket, a lineage, a run of dates.

Both are secondary. PTCpedia is a community writing down what it remembered, years into a network that had already been running for years, and its claims stand as that — corroboration where a federation's own contemporary pages agree, a lead where they are silent, and never an override where they disagree.

Where an article carries an out-of-fiction attribution — a handler credit in an infobox, a staff listing, a contact address — the name or address is redacted in place and the marker says which rule took it out. Everything the fiction itself says stands exactly as written.
