Lights go out, they start to flash and fireworks go off. The crowd is seen and they go mad as the lights go back on and the camera's fly over the audience. Ladies, gentlemen and children are seated and ready to roll. The camera's zoom in on Felix and Johnny at ringside. Johnny: Welcome everyone! To EWA Extravaganza! We are live from Trenton, New Jersey and we are again... fully loaded! I am Johnny and this is Felix Felix: Yups. that's me... we have a great show here tonight, and we have an update on "Crippler" Evan Crowley! Johnny: Yes, Evan Crowley got his back injured last sunday at "Redemption"! He will not join us the upcoming weeks, we wish him all the best with his recovery and hope to see him back in the EWA in no time! Felix: Yeah, Tommy Starr already told us that he would love to take him on again in our squared circle! Johnny: Tonight, we have another great show with some great matches! What do you think of the main event, Felix? "Innovator" Damien Black versus "The #1 Stunna" Johnny Lambert! Felix: Lambert got screwed! Paul Valentine and Black got together and screwed him out of the win. Johnny: Well, after redemption we already have the name for the new pay per view.. and that is, EWA "Wrecked!" Next month, november 30! Felix: Great! Johnny: Felix, don't be so sarcastic! We have more news! The match of the month has been voted for and the match of the month is Tommy Starr versus Evan Crowley! Felix: Yeah, and what about the wrestler of the month? Damien Black? Amazing! Johnny: Well, i hope to receive word from him soon... Felix: What about the Paul Valentine and Damien Black situation? Johnny: Well, my guess is, Paul Valentine had enough of Evan Crowley and turned his back on him. I mean, Crowley injured for at least a month, and Damien Black the champion... easy pick for Valentine! Felix: Yeah Well i got to say tha.... Johnny: What the hell is this? Renegade and Kragias are planning something here?! The camera zooms in on Mayhem who is seen walking toward the ring readying himself for his match. The camera fades out to Brian Karagias & Renegade who coming down a corridor which intersects the one Mayhem is coming down. Renegade nods & picks up the chair he was holding Brian has a wooden baseball bat. Mayhem is seen walking by then Karagias holds up three fingers & points towards him. They rush out & Brian gets the first hit he continues to hit Mayhem as he moans in pain Renegade focuses on Mayhem's head. Brian breaks the bat over Mayhem & picks up the handle end. Brian: Alright thats enough. He leans over Mayhem & whispers just loud enough for the camera to hear. Brian: You wanted to play like a bitch. Now you look like a bitch. I done started playin your game now motherfucker. Johnny: ........... Well, the hot head Brian Karagias seemingly lead the attack on Mayhem. They stand up, Total Control whips The Reason into the ropes, he comes back and Total Control misses for the clothesline, Blasier runs after him, takes him into the waistlock, TC holds on to the ropes, Ryan Blasier falls back, Total control turns around, goes for a lariat and takes down Blasier. Johnny: Good technical moves here by both men... what do you think Felix? Total Control takes The Reason up, sends him into the ropes and takes him down with a back body drop! Total Control pins the Reason... 1.................2..........kickout by Ryan Blasier! Blasier stands up, Total control as well, TC for the hard right, blocked by Blasier, Blasier now with a sharp left, right into the kisser of Total Control! TC goes down by that knock and Blasier climbs the top turnbuckle. The fans go wild... Total Control is down and almost out... Blasier flies through the air..... and down with a legdrop off the top! He goes immediately for the pin.. 1............2..........kickout by TC! Ryan Blasier picks him up.... Whips him into the ropes.... Nails him with a kick to the gut, followed with a spike piledriver! He goes for the pin... 1...............2.............kickout by Total Control! Ryan Blasier takes him up..... bodyslams him down... Blasier climbs the turnbuckle again...... Splatterpoint executed!! The Shit and Tommy Starr the Intercontinental Champion! Then The Shit is seen walking from under the bigscreen towards the ring. The crowd boos at him as he looks at the fans in dispear. Ring announcer: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Making his way down the aisle, from Parts unknown.... The Shit! The Excorsist theme fades away and "Cocky" by Kid Rock takes over. Johnny: yesss! Felix: The intercontinental champion is coming out! Tommy Starr is seen coming from under the big screen. "Cocky" continues to play over the PA. Tommy Starr walks down the aisle with his IC Title over his shoulder. He looks at the Shit with a pissed off look on his face. He enters the ring with a roll and holds up his title to show the fans. Ring announcer: In the ring, from Hollywood, California weighing 202 pounds, the EWA Intercontinental Champion... Tommy Starr! The Shit is looking pissed off and goes for the lock-up with Tommy Starr, the smaller Starr just dodges it and kicks The Shit into his back. The Shit turns around and is furious, he goes for the grab, but again Tommy Starr slides away and gives him a hard right punch into the liver area. The Shit screams it out and again tries to lock up with the fast Tommy Starr, but again Tommy Starr dodges it and tries to take The Shit down with a drop toe hold, The Shit doesn't go down and instead he grabs the throat of Tommy Starr and executes a rough throat toss! Johnny: Damn! The Shit just threw Starr away and immediately the intercontinental champion is down! Felix: My guess is, the shit will capitalize here and he will finish Tommy Starr off, ruthless! The Shit kicks Tommy Starr in the midsection and takes him up, he sends him into the ropes as Starr comes back, The Shit takes him down with a thunderous sidewalk slam! He goes for the pin.. 1...........2..............kickout! The Shit takes him up, again into the ropes and down with a lariat! The Shit goes for the cover.. 1..........2........kickout by the champ! The Shit pushes the referee away as the crowd give him a boo reaction. The Shit grabs the hair of Tommy Starr, punch him into the midsection, kicks him in his gut and executes a release powerbomb! The Shit goes for ANOTHER cover! 1.............2...........foot on the rope by Tommy Starr!! The Shit has Tommy Starr on the turnbuckle, but Starr is resilient and reverses it into a hurrican-rana!! He goes for the pin.. 1...............2..............shoulder up by The Shit! The Shit is dazzled by the move that Starr executed and stands up, Starr bounces off the ropes and executes a roundhouse kick followed by a pin.. 1..............2.............kickout by The Shit! Tommy Starr is seemingly hurt as he takes up The Shit, he tries the Starr Struck, but he can't get the Shit on his shoulders... he drops him and rams a dropkick into his face! The Shit is able to get up as Tommy Starr punches him... The Shit is being taken back up by Tommy Starr, into the ropes, The Shit executes a Powerslam!! The cover! 1...........2.............3!!! *bell rings* Ring announcer: The winner of this match, Tommy Starr! As The Shit tries to get to his feet, Tommy Starr grabs the steel chair and swings it over the head of The Shit. As The Shit falls to the mat, Tommy continues to swing the chair over and over until he sees The Shit not moving.Tommy Starr drops the chair and picks up the barbed wire. The fans are going wild. Tommy Starr wraps the barbed wire around the neck of The Shit and chokes him with it. Soon after security hits the ring and pulls Tommy Starr off and take The Shit outside the ring. Tommy grabs the mic. Tommy Starr: Shit!!! You see what happens when you disrespect Tommy Starr??? I had to set a example, I can't let a nobody like you come into my house and try to make me look like a fool. Tommy signals for the ring crew guy to hand him his EWA IC title. Tommy Starr grabs the title off the guys hands. Tommy Starr: You see this? This a title that I will make famous! I will make this Championship just as important as The World Heavyweight Title. I plan to keep this Title for a long time. So I'll face who ever the Prez sees fit to challenge me! What happen to The Shit is just the start. It was a message to the other superstars of EWA. You want some of me, Fine, just ask! That asshole attacked me and hes laid out now. Now its time to have a real celebration for winning the IC title. I'm going to party. Well Until next week, See ya.. Cocky by Kid Rock plays as Tommy Starr poses in the ring. Tommy exits the ring with The EWA IC title on his shoulder. Johnny: Oh no... look at Tommy Starr walking away, he's sooo extreme! Felix: Lucas has to do something about this! We don't do this sh!t in here do we?! Johnny: Well, Tommy Starr has the power to do it to the 7'2 foot Giant! Felix: *Pfewh* what's next John? Johnny: It's Johnny Felix, damn... UP NEXT!
