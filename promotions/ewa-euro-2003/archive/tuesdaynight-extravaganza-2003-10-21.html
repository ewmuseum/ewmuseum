The crowd goes nuts and doesn't really have a clue what is happening. Then the camera's show signs created by the crowd. "Damien Black is god!", "Johnny Lambert the next EWA champion!" and "Paul Valentine SUCKS" are shown by the camera. Then the camera flies over the screaming people and right towards Felix and Johnny. Johnny: WELCOME ladies and gentlemen to another edition of EWA's Extravaganza, and it's a special night! Felix: Why is that?! do you know something that i don't?! Johnny: YES! Because tonight is the.... "The people are going nuts as the president of the EWA is shown. He stands under the bigscreen looking around and waiving to the crowd" Lucas: Ladies and gentlemen... tonight is a great night.. and in a second i will tell you why. Not only because we have some great matches, no no... it's my Birthday today!!! The crowd goes nuts as the president says that. Lucas: And because it's my birthday, i will.... "Nothing to lose" by 2Pac plays over the PA system as Titanium walks out from the curtains and almost knocks over the president of the EWA. Titanium looks at him and gives him the finger. The crowd start to boo as a bunch of maniacs as Titanium rolls into the ring and grabs a mic. Titanium: Cut my music! who the hell cares about your damn birthday!? It's my match the people want to see.. so get your ass out of here! Felix: What!? is Titanium telling his boss to get lost!? Johnny: Yes he is! my gawd! Lucas: Well.... you are ruiining my damn birthday you!! Titanium: Shut up and bring on the Rampage guys.... Brian Karagias over the speakers: The Name is Karagias...... BRIAN KARAGIAS! He shakes the hand of the president and he runs down the aisle and rolls into the ring. Brian Karagias vs Titanium Johnny: Well.. a good start here! *Bell rings* Karagias and Titanium lock up and they start to beat away on eachother. Johnny: This is a preview for what's going to happen at Redemption! Felix: Well, the president made this trip to his hometown Holland, Amsterdam and it's being ruiined by Titanium.. a NO NAME in the EWA! Felix: He's manhandling Brian Karagias! Johnny: Karagias went in and wanted to show Titanium that he should not mess with the president but the plan backfired on him! Titanium goes for the uppercut, blocked by BK, BK whips him into the ropes, reversed by Titanium and as BK comes back, Titanium takes him up and down with a rough atomic drop. Karagias is still on his feet, here comes Titanium, crossbody, catched by Brian Karagias..... backbreaker on Titanium! Karagias for the pin... 1.............2.....kickout by Titanium! Karagias takes him back up by his head, in the turnbuckle, nothing but steel as he barges in to execute a body splash! Titanium jumps up the turnbuckle..... Titanium Splash executed, but Brian Karagias moves aside and Titanium misses his finisher! Karagias spots him in pain and goes for another cover! 1....................2..............kickout! BK stands up and looks at the crowd.... he smiles and the crowd gives a mixed reaction... Johnny: Oh oh, Titanium is in trouble! Karagias gives the sign for the Sniper! He takes Titanium back up... Sniper executed! NO!? Brian Karagias takes him up, but Titanium reverses it into a small package.. 1..........................2..............kickout! Karagias kicks the head of Titanium and sends him into the ropes again, he comes back BK misses for the clothesline, again Titanium comes back Karagias goes for a powerslam and hits it! The Luv Machine comes down the aisle Felix: oh oh why the hell can't we just see a nice and clean finish here!? Karagias goes for the pin on Titanium and TLM climbs the apron... Johnny: The referee holds back TLM.... and Brian Karagias is going for the cover!! The referee is busy! Karagias stands back up and walks over to TLM and punches him in the face. From the back comes Titanium, but Brian Karagias catches him and executes the Sniper!! 1.................2.................3!! *The bell rings* Ring announcer: The winner of this bout... Brian Karagias! Johnny: TLM couldn't get the job done. Titanium lost the match and Brian Karagias walks away with the cake! Well ladies and gentlemen, we've just seen the first match end here and we are getting ready for the second match on this special night!! *Commercial break* The camera's show fans going crazy as the camera flies over them. Fireworks are again seen. The camera zooms in on Johnny and Felix. Johnny: Welcome back to the Amsterdam Arena ladies and gentlemen! We are ready for the next match!! Felix: What did we just see in the last match!? We saw Lucas being pushed away from under the big screen and we saw a failed attempt by TLM to make Brian Karagias lose the match against Titanium! And ladies and gentlemen, Titanium suffered another loss. Johnny: Unfortunately he did..... Mayhem (AkA The Luv Machine) vs Damien Omega Ring announcer: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. About to make his way down the aisle from Forest Hills,New York weighing 356 pounds, Damien Omega! "Stranger inside" by Shinedown start to rush over the sound system. The curtains move and Damien Omega start to swivel down the aisle and towards the ring. The crowd pops and Damien Omega laughs at the crowd. Johnny: A big man waiting to get his hands on Mahyem! They have a match next sunday at Redemption, ladies and gentlemen! Omega rolls into the ring and awaits the arrival of Mayhem. Ring announcer: His opponent from Weymouth, England weighing 327 pounds, Mayhem!! "I believe in a thing called love" by Darknes start to blare over the theme of Damien Omega as the crowd gives a mixed reaction to the appearance by Mayhem. He high fives the fans as he makes his way to the ring. Felix: My personal favorite in this match, Mayhem the man that changed his name from the Luv Machine!! Johnny: Well, if he wins the match he will still be undefeated here in the EWA. Felix: Is that a fact? Johnny: Yes it is, if he wins the match, he will come at 3 and 0! Mayhem walks into the ring and throws his hands up. The crowd again, gives a mixed reaction. Crowd: Mayhem sucks, Mayhem sucks! *The bell rings* Both men lock up and they start to exchange fists. A kick in the midsection by Damien Omega on Mayhem and gives him an irish whip, he comes back and Omega takes him down with a nice drop toe hold. He turns and locks in the hammer lock. Johnny: Well, Omega with a good hammer lock executed on him! Felix: he ain't going to submit in this match... can't be! Omega releases the hammer lock and takes him back up to whip him in the ropes, and again.. DOWN with a drop toe hold. Again he rolls over and grabs his head for a nice crossface. Referee: Do you give up!?!? Damien Omega releases the hold and bounces off the ropes, he comes back and executes a fine legdrop on the back of Mayhem's head. He stands back up and the crowd pops. Johnny: well, he's pretty popular here in Holland, Amsterdam! Damien Omega had the head of Mayhem locked and goes for a suplex.... executed! he takes him up again and takes him up for the ride again..... vertical suplex into a brainbuster! He goes for the pin.. 1.................2.......kickout by Mayhem! Mayhem is being taken up by Omega and whipped into the ropes, he comes back Spine buster slam by Omega, but Mayhem reverses it into a fine DDT! Mayhem gets up slowly and grabs the head of Omega, he kicks it and executes an irish whip, he returns.. Samoan Drop executed on the big Damien Omega! The pin... 1..............2.....kickout! Felix: Well, good match, good moves and i love the agility! Johnny: It's a little weird to call the Luv Machine mayhem now, so please forgive us ladies and gentlemen if we make a mistake in calling him by his right name. Mayhem grabs the arm of Omega and whips him into the ropes, no reversal by the big man, Mayhem comes back and executes a high knee on Damien Omega. Mayhem takes him back up, in the ropes and down with a drop toe hold. He takes the leg and goes for the sharpshooter, but his opponents kicks him away and gets back up just to be knocked down again by Mayhem with a big boot to the face. Johnny: Mayhem is now dominating the match and Omega has no chance at all! Mayhem now climbs the turnbuckle, he looks down and smiles as the crowd boos at him. He jumps up and down for an elbowdrop! Felix: WOW! He found nothing by canvas there as Omega rolled away and now he's just going for the pin on the hurt Mayhem!! 1......................2..................KICKOUT! Omega and Mayhem stand back up, but having a lot of trouble. the Lock-up.... hammerlock reversed by Omega into a hammerlock of himself, Mayhem rolls away from him and grabs his opponent into another hammerlock, Omega stomps him in the head with an elbow.... Omega turns, belly to belly, but Mayhem reverses it into a kick to the gut, followed by a LoveStruck!! Johnny: Oh nooo!! Lovestruck executed and he goes for a pin immediately!!! 1.................2................3!! Ring announcer: The winner of this match, Mayhem! Johnny: Unbelievable, Mayhem took him out with the lovestruck and Omega couldn't do anything about it!! Felix: Well, Mayhem took up the big man and the mat was just SHAKING like crazy! I thought they would go through the damn canvas! i'll be damned! Johnny: Well everybody as his theme is blaring over the speakers we are amazed by the power that Mayhem just showed us! This is a wrestler in form... a GREAT form! Felix: Well, let's just await the pay per view, Johnny! I just KNOW that Damien Omega and Brian Karagias will dominate the match and that Mayhem and Titanium will lose the match. Johnny: Well, i agree that Omega is a big and strong man, but he hasn't shown anything yet! Mayhem just left the ring and Damien Omega is rolling out of the ring as the crowd cheers for him. Johnny: Well, as the wrestlers are out of the ring, we can start talking about our next match, Felix. What do you think about The #1 Stunna versus the New F'N Diamond?! Felix: You wanna know what i think!? I think Simon Poole will win this and he will win it with flags and a lot of balloons! Johnny: What the hell are you talking about!? Felix: Never mind, i'll put my money on Poole! Johnny: Ok, Then The Stunna is my pick! The both commentators slide a ten dollar bill onto the announcers table. Johnny: Ehmm... you people did not see that... *smiles* "The New F'N Diamond" Simon Poole vs "The #1 Stunna" Johnny Lambert Ring announcer: The following contest is scheduled for one fall.... *commercials* Johnny: Welcome back ladies and gentlemen, we had to go to a fast break here... Simon Poole is already in the ring and we are awaiting Johnny Lambert! The man that has impressed us a lot lately! Ring announcer: From Romeo, Michigan weighing 275 pounds....... "The #1 Stunna" Johnny Lambert!! "#1 Stunna" by the big Tymers start to play over the sound system as the curtains slide and fireworks enlight the arena. Then The #1 Stunna is seen under the big screen. He stops and waives to the crowd who go insane. He walks down the ramp and to the ring. He walks slowly and with no stress at all. Johnny: Here comes a man that has the power to WIN here in the EWA! Felix: No he doesn't..... Johnny: .............. *caughs* Johnny Lambert walks into the ring and throws up his fist as he stands on the turnbuckle. The crowd pops. *The bell rings* Felix: Simon... take him down!! Poole executes a hiptoss and immediately Lambert jumps up and grabs the arm of Poole, Poole whips him into the ropes and as he comes back Lambert is being locked away by Poole with a headlock. Lambert slides his arm around the back of Poole and executes a belly to back suplex on him. Simon Poole is down and the arm is locked by Lambert. Johnny: Nice armlock takedown by Lambert. Poole is able to get back up, reverse the armlock and executes a twist into a belly to back hold, he tries to take Johnny Lambert down, but Lambert turns around with an elbow smash, Poole dodges it and executes a Tazzplex! Felix: That's it! Poole took him down with a nice suplex! Poole for the pin.. 1........kickout by The #1 Stunna. Lambert is managing to get back up, whips The New F'N Diamond in the ropes, ducks as Poole comes in with a clothesline, as Lambert stands back up Poole goes for a roundhouse kick, ducked by Lambert and Poole goes down and falls to the outside of the ring. Johnny: Now that's the way to do it! Lambert points to his brains and the crowd pops. He rolls out and grabs the head of Poole. He smashes it onto the side of the ring! Poole punches Lambert, but he's being countered with a dodge and immediately taken down with a suplex. Poole is being pulled back up and Stunna is executing a nice front face lock. He kicks him in the midsection and executes a thunderous underhook powerbomb! The #1 Stunna takes him back up kicks him in the midsection again, bounces of the ropes and as he comes back he kicks the back of Poole's head! Johnny: And DOWN goes Simon Poole! Johnny Lambert picks up Poole and sends him in the ropes with an irish whip, here comes Poole, Lambert misses for the clothesline... He looks down at the ring and Johnny Lambert spots him. The crowd sees Damien Black and start a massive "Damien Black" chant. Johnny: Oh......... here comes the champion! Felix: What's wrong with him?! Johnny: I don't know.... Damien Black: Now I'm sorry to disappoint you all, but I don't have much time tonight as I have some important business to attend to. Well if he beats me at Redemption then and only then will I believe that. So until Lambert beats me, until he pins me for the three, or as it must be at Redemption, makes me utter those words "I Quit" I will remain the number one guy in this company. Damien throws down the mic and storms of to the back Johnny: Well... did you see that? Felix: What? Johnny: I am sure and VERY positive that i saw him... hmm, i don't know... the champ was unsure and very tense when he said that... Felix: What are you saying? He's scared?
