GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

GWO Overdrive, 15 November 2004

15 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phoenix, AZ Backstage we see Joe Davidson entering the building which causes the fans to cheer. However, he rounds a corner and takes a chair shot to the head from a waiting Leech. Leech then tosses the chair aside, laughing to himself and leaves Joe lying there bleeding from the forehead. We then see the opening credits as "Rio Bravo" by CKY plays. In those opening credits we see images of Youthful Aggression, The Dying Breed, The CWA Legends, Myst, Leech, Lethal Weapon and Stevie James before we head to ringside where Henry Lesher has returned to commentate alongside Casey Braun.
Sal Clemenzo vs Ben Rhys "Remedy" by Cold starts to play and Ben Rhys emerges through the curtain to the cheers of some who have seen him before. His walk to the ring is interrupted as Sal Clemenzo attacks him from behind and chop blocks him to the floor. Sal then puts the boots to him as fans in that area cheer him for using tactics they booed him for not long ago. Sal stands Ben up, places him in a side headlock and brings him to ringside that way, then releases it and tries to slam Ben's head into the guard railing only to have him block it. Ben then elbows Sal in the gut and bounces his head off of the guard railing right after that. Ben then rolls him into the ring and shakes off the effects of the attack before rolling himself into the ring as well. The bell then finally sounds to put this match into the books.
Casey Braun - And with this violent start, we welcome you to another exciting edition of GWO's Overdrive.
Henry Lesher - I wouldn't call it exciting. Casey Braun - Just once I would like to sit beside someone who is in a good mood. Henry Lesher - Maybe they're not the problem. Besides, I hear Dante did superb work while I was away and that doesn't surprise me in the least.
Ben stands Sal up, whips him off the ropes and knees him in the gut, sending him head over heels through the air. Ben then places Sal into a headlock and gives him a bulldog to the mat and then tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Sal Clemenzo. Ben then stands Sal up again, whips him off the ropes and Sal leap frogs Ben and then gives Ben a rabbit punch to the back of the neck before giving him a reverse neckbreaker. Sal then heads to the ropes as the fans cheer him on still. Sal reaches the top and then leaps off for a flying leg drop only to have Ben move out of the way at the last possible moment. Ben then rolls himself out of the ring to regroup. Sal grabs at his spine, his tailbone having hit the mat hard, as he rolls himself out of the ring in pursuit. Ben turns and takes a swing at Sal which Sal ducks before giving Ben an atomic drop and then sending him face first into the steel corner post. Sal Clemenzo then rolls Ben back into the ring.
Henry Lesher - Even this wuss version of Sal is too smart to go outside of the ring with. Casey Braun - I don't see how he's a wuss. Henry Lesher - Well of course you wouldn't. You have a big grade five math test tomorrow. Your mind must be swimming in knowledge.
Sal stands Ben up, whips him off the ropes and clothesline him to the mat. Sal then stands Ben up in the corner, picks him up and gives him a power bomb onto the corner turnbuckle before Ben lands in a heap on the mat, a move people once called the Shock Treatment. Sal smiles and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Sal Clemenzo with a convincing win here tonight over a debuting star who will need to rethink his GWO career to this point.
Casey Braun - Nice finishing move. Henry Lesher - It would be even better if it was his own. Thief... Backstage we see Joe Davidson being attended to by EMTs as Steve Blake enters the picture. Steve Blake - I knew I should have been with him when he got here! Dammit! Dave Jalldoff enters the shot and taps Steve on the back. Dave Jalldoff - He'll be fine. Leech just started something he definitely cannot finish. Let's go.
Jalldoff and Blake leave together as Joe Davidson struggles to get to his feet, his face badly bloodied by this attack tonight.
Stevie James vs Leech "Enemies of Reality" by Nevermore plays as Leech comes out to the ring, the crowd booing him and even a couple of fans throwing their paper cups at him. He raises a fist in the air as he reaches ringside and looks around at the capacity crowd. He then spits in the direction of the camera before stepping up onto the apron and then into the ring. He tests the ropes as we hear "Rock Superstar" by Cypress Hill and we see Stevie James making his way to the ring, the fans on their feet giving him an ovation.
Henry Lesher - I don't know what these idiots are cheering about since their hero is about to suffer a loss.
Casey Braun - Those idiots buy the tickets that pay for your salary. Henry Lesher - Yes and they hate me which means by supporting me and my lifestyle they are complete idiots.
Stevie James stops at ringside and looks in at Leech and shakes his head. Stevie then makes his way up the ring steps onto the apron and ducks a clothesline from a charging Leech, then shoulder blocks him through the ropes before doing a sunset flip into the ring as the ref calls for the bell. Stevie rolls Leech up for the pin. 1...2... Leech kicks out, narrowly avoiding the quickest pinfall win in GWO history. Stevie then quickly gets to his feet and puts the boots to Leech on the mat. He boot chokes him in the ropes as the ref warns Stevie about such tactics. Stevie then releases the choke, grabs Leech by the hair and stands him up. He then whips Leech off the ropes and gives him a clothesline to the mat. Stevie then heads to the ropes, makes his way to the second rope and then lands a double axe handle smash across the back of Leech. He then goes for the pin attempt again. 1...2... kick out by Leech. Stevie James shakes his head as he stands Leech up, whips him into the corner and then charges into raised knees from Leech before Leech then spears Stevie to the mat.
Henry Lesher - That learned him. Casey Braun - Learned? Henry Lesher - It can be a word. Casey Braun - Maybe to you. Leech stands Stevie up and places his neck over the middle rope and chokes him with it as the ref counts towards a disqualification. Leech releases the choke before a DQ can be called for as we see Dave Jalldoff and Steve Blake coming out from the back, neither of them looking very happy right now.
Henry Lesher - What the hell are they doing out here? Casey Braun - Maybe they want to comment to Leech on his handiwork earlier against Joe. Leech whips Stevie off the ropes and then backdrops him to the mat. Leech then leg drops Stevie and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Stevie James. Leech then stands Stevie up again and whips him into the corner where he then lands a series of forearm smashes to the jaw of Stevie and then monkey flips him to the center of the ring. Leech then takes notice of Dave and Steve Blake standing at ringside and has a few words for them, referring to them as "old men" before grabbing Stevie James and pulling him to his feet. Leech then whips Stevie off the ropes and both men clothesline each other to the mat. The crowd starts a Stevie James chant which Dave Jalldoff and Steve Blake encourage from ringside. Leech shows signs of movement first as the ref reaches a four count. Leech grabs the bottom rope and starts pulling himself to his feet. Stevie grabs the ropes on the opposite side of the ring and starts pulling himself up. Leech gets to his feet and Dave Jalldoff calls out to him. Leech turns and has a few words for Dave. Stevie then takes advantage of the situation with a school boy roll up. 1...2...3! Stevie James wins the match!
Casey Braun - Stevie showing the mix of talent and a little luck that makes him a fine Pacific champion.
Henry Lesher - Stop praising the anti-Leech factions of the GWO. Stevie leaves the ring and is handed his Pacific title which he drapes over his shoulder before he heads to the back. Dave Jalldoff then slides into the ring with Steve Blake doing the same a moment after. They stand over Leech and look out at the crowd as Leech looks for a way out. Leech rolls out of the way of an elbow smash attempt by Steve Blake and then rolls himself out of the ring and takes off through the crowd with Dave Jalldoff in close pursuit.
Henry Lesher - Good luck catching him. Casey Braun - You could pretend to be impartial. Henry Lesher - Like you pretend to be innocent? The scene opens with the headshot of a giggling Kumquat Kid standing outside the arena, the sound of children giggling around him as we pan out to find he's wearing a full body Dawg Suit as he calls it. Basically, its a sumo suit with the word Dawg written in large Sharpie black writing. He begins stomping around, making fish faces with his mouth.
Kumquat Kid - Me Dawg, me eat small children! Me eat small children from Rhode Island, therefore, not show up on census. Yum!
Ryan is approached by Kelly Buchanan who giggles a bit herself at the sight of the Kumquat Kid.
Kelly Buchanan - Ryan? Kumquat Kid - Who be this Ryan you speak of? I'll eat this Ryan, and all his hot dawgs. Get it, hot dawgs?
Kelly Bucnahan - Not really. So how do you feel you'll fair against The Dawg tonight? Kumquat Kid - Well Kelly, ahem, I mean, Kelly, me thinks I fair well against Dawg! Sure, he big, stupid, fat, and slow, but that does not change fact that he could eat me. All he needs is a hotplate. But, never less, Kumquat Kid, after inspiration from sumo suit, will think fat, feel fat, just not walk fat. Because Kumquat Kid fast, like lightning, like Chevy Chase talk show. He go up escalator fast, while Big Dawg crashes through them, causing shoppers to curse in front of their children.
Kelly Buchanan - I...see. Kumquat Kid - I know you do Kelly, even though you look nothing like NFL star Ray Buchanan, you have his toughness. You cut in line at cafeteria today, didn't you Kelly?
Kelly Buchanan - Well, I- Kumquat Kid - It ok, KK respect that, Dawg love that. Dawg hungry, Dawg want eat! Before Kelly can react, Ryan takes a styrofoam ball from his pocket, the same kind used to make silk floral arrangements, and takes a big bite out of it. He actually swallows it!
Kumquat Kid - Hmmm, Quaker Oat cornballs not taste how they used to. No matter, Dawg cut down on carbs.
Ryan, still in body suit, waves to the camera with a grin before sauntering away, leaving Kelly a bit perplexed. We then head to commercial.
We see Frank Merritt sitting reading the paper. He lowers the paper, an uncharacteristic smile on his face. "Hi there. I am usually miserable. I know that and I decided it was time to fix that problem. My way of fixing it? I kicked Xtasy's ass and am going to write about it in my new tell all book titled 'I Handed X His Ass So You Can Kiss Mine.' It's a great read with big print for those fellow Flockers in the GWO audience. Pick up your copy today!" The camera then focusses on Xtasy who is hanging upside down from a flagpole as Frank hums to himself reading the paper.
Shengee Shmelky vs Kosmic Kitty "Rumors" by Lindsay Lohan plays as Kosmic Kitty makes her way towards the ring with a blanket on one arm and a picnic basket in the hand on that same arm. With her free hand, she blows kisses to an adoring crowd who are all huge Kosmic Kitty marks. Kitty steps onto the apron and then into the ring where she lays the blanket out and sets down her picnic basket.
Henry Lesher - I am going to puke. This is wrestling... Casey Braun - I think it's cute. Henry Lesher - Yeah, you would. As Kitty takes a seat on her blanket, we hear "Blue" by Eiffel 65 and Shengee Shmelky makes his way to the ring with a smile on his face. He has numerous gold chains on his neck and a baseball cap on his head tilted sideways. He has a Phoenix Coyotes jersey on, though it's far too large for his slight frame and he has the old red and blue Converse runners on. He is a fashion plate and close behind him walk Ingee and Adam West. Shengee steps onto the apron, suddenly looks nervous at seeing Kitty sitting on the blanket and looks out to father figure Adam who motions him to get into the ring.
Casey Braun - I am being told Adam West was supposed to drive the Batmobile in here tonight, but it is at the shop for repairs.
Henry Lesher - By the looks of Adam, he could use a trip to the body shop himself. Shengee steps into the ring and the ref present is confused and asks the two of them if they are going to fight. They both smile shyly and say no. Shengee then takes a seat on the blanket and accepts a Twinkie from Kitty who asks Shengee how he's doing. This brings on the standard white rapper response from Shengee who talks about the power of Shmelk and the power of the Twinkie. He then calls for the microphone from Shelley Morris who tosses it to him and he catches it awkwardly.
Shengee Shmelky - Yo yo yo, Phoenix! How be da peeps tonight? *loud pop* Yo yo yo, check it out, word yo! Tonight da Shmelk and da Twinkie unite and da wrestling world is gonna see why good always beats bad cause bad sucks!
Shengee is stopped as we hear "Vindicated" by Dashboard Confessional and Radd Matthews makes his way to the ring which draws boos from the crowd which has turned on him for his attitude in recent weeks. Radd makes his way onto the apron, then steps through the ropes and quickly grabs the microphone from a cowering Shengee.
Radd Matthews - You cannot be serious. Kitty, I have been away from you for two weeks and this is where you are now? You're hanging around losers like the Shmelkys? I am ashamed. In fact, I am shocked at what a cheap girl you really are.
Kitty gets up and slaps Radd across the face. Radd steps towards her, but Shengee stands between the two of them. The crowd explodes when they see Shengee standing up for Kitty. Radd gives Shengee a Rock Bottom onto the picnic and food flies everywhere. Radd then steps towards Kitty and grabs her by the hair. Ingee rolls into the ring and low blows him in a style even Triple H would envy. Radd crumples on the mat as Ingee helps his brother and Kitty out of the ring. Adam West shakes his head as he leads the boys and Kitty towards the back, asking her questions about how Catwoman is doing these days. Radd uses the ropes to get to his feet and looks out at the crowd which is booing and mocking him. They even start a "Radd sucks" chant which enrages him. Radd then slides out of the ring and races towards the back in search of someone or something to take out his frustrations on.
Henry Lesher - That was as awful as I thought it would be. Casey Braun - It was fine till Radd got in there. Backstage we see Laney Miller and Alexis Sykes, both wearing ACW T-shirts, entering the arena. The crowd sees them on the GWO Four Screens which hang above the ring and Laney and Alexis make their way towards their dressing room when they round a corner and are headed off by Lethal Weapon, Max Cameron, Slim Pickens, Shannon Skanes, Ricky Simms, Austin Fierro, Rated R, Sal Clemenzo, Boomshalkalaka, Rod Gordon, and Gerard Angelo who then part to allow the two girls through between them, glaring at them the entire way.
Shannon Skanes - Welcome to the backwoods. Enjoy your stay. The two ladies don't look back and the GWO roster representatives scatter after a few nasty words are tossed the visitors' way.
Casey Braun - That wasn't friendly. Henry Lesher - Putting it mildly. The Dawg vs Kumquat Kid "Who Let the Dawgs Out" by Baha Men plays as The Dawg comes out to a more mixed reaction than usual. Dawg shakes his head as he makes his way down the aisle, high fiving a few fans who are cheering for him. Dawg then steps up onto the apron and then into the ring. "Happy Together" by Simple Plan plays as The Kumquat Kid makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd as he is led by managers Dunk and Jenny Retsyn. He looks into the ring and frowns when he spots The Dawg, then grabs the ropes and jumps up onto the apron. He looks into the ring at Dawg again and shakes his head as Dawg can be seen cursing.
Casey Braun - Apparently Kumquat played some form of prank on Dawg in the back. Henry Lesher - Well we all saw that bit with Kelly Buchanan outside, so maybe he got his fat fingers to turn a TV on and watch as well.
Kumquat steps into the ring and the bell sounds as the two men circle each other. Kumquat offers to shake hands with The Dawg, then pulls back the moment Dawg accepts it and goes to shake the Kid's hand. Kumquat Kid laughs, but that stops when The Dawg starts landing knife edge chops on him that send him into the corner buckle. Slim then whips Kumquat Kid hard to the opposite corner and then gives him a belly to back suplex to the mat. The Dawg then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kumquat Kid. Dawg stands The Kumquat Kid up, whips him off the ropes and Kumquat ducks a clothesline and then dropkicks The Dawg in the back, sending him through the ropes to the floor. Kumquat then slides out of the ring and grabs Dawg by the hair and rams his head into the guard railing. Kumquat then jumps onto the guard railing and then jumps off of it, giving Dawg a kick in the side of the head from there. Kumquat Kid then rolls himself into the ring. The Dawg grabs at the apron and then pulls himself into the ring where Kumquat Kid leg drops him across the back of the neck. Kumquat then goes for the pin. 1...2... Dawg gets a foot on the bottom rope.
Henry Lesher - This Kid might do better if he had a brain. Casey Braun - You're a sad man. Henry Lesher - I have been subjected to this drivel painted as wrestling for over two years now. I am a result of my circumstances.
Kumquat Kid stands The Dawg up and whips him off the ropes and then dropkicks him backwards. Dawg stumbles into the ropes where his arms get tangled in the top and middle rope. Kumquat Kid then lands a series of knife edge chops on The Dawg as the referee tries to free him from the ropes. The ref does manage to free Dawg from the ropes and he grabs Kumquat Kid around the throat. Dawg then pulls the Kid to the middle of the ring and gives him a choke slam. The Dawg then makes his way up the ropes and starts climbing as Kid's managers look on, clearly worried. Dawg leaps off, Kid rolls out of the way and Dawg lands hard on his back. Kid then leaps to the top rope and raises his arms high before executing the Five Alive Frog Splash on the fallen Dawg and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! The Kumquat Kid garners a win for himself this week over The Dawg. Kumquat Kid then rolls himself out of the ring where Dunk throws him a Kumquat he then proceeds to eat as the fans cheer him on. He then starts for the back where he is headed off by Tony Parizi who is smiling.
Tony Parizi - You know, I had to come out personally and congratulate you on your win. In fact, you impressed me so much that next week you get a shot at a title. I'll tell you at the end of the night which title that will be. Enjoy the kumquats until then. You're about to be initiated into the hardcore portion of the roster.
Tony leaves as Kumquat Kid talks this over with his managers before they continue to head to the back with the victorious Kid. We head to commercial.
We see Pyro looking in the windows of a store as our commercial begins. He frowns, then looks at us. "You know, since leaving the GWO, I realized something." He then smiles and points to a McDonald's across the street. "I love McDonald's! See, as the new official spokesman for McDonald's I have been..." Ronald enters the picture, arms folded across his chest. "Pyro, we went over this. You're not our spokesman." "Aw, come on, Ron! I'll work for food! This could be great!" Large men in black suits then enter the shot and carry Pyro away as he continues to protest the interruption in what could have been a great money making venture.
Max Cameron vs Radd Matthews "Vindicated" by Dashboard Confessional plays as Radd Matthews comes out to the boos of the crowd. He has a few words for one fan in the front row as he makes his way to ringside. He steps onto the apron and into the ring without any posing or usual smiles from the one time fan favorite. Radd then paces inside the ring as "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays and Max Cameron, the GWO World Champion, makes his way to the ring. He is booed almost as loudly as Radd Matthews was.
Henry Lesher - I am noticing a trend in GWO. There aren't any fan favorites anymore. Casey Braun - That bothers you? Henry Lesher - No, just means the talent appear to be all taking short cuts. It shows me how crappy things really are around here.
Casey Braun - Then maybe you can leave with Alexis and Laney after the show. Henry Lesher - Why would I do that? If I want disease, I can grow some in the lab. Casey Braun - That's nasty. Max steps into the ring and the bell sounds as Max and Radd circle each other. They lock up and the fans start a chant for Lethal Weapon. Max whips Radd off the ropes and shoulder blocks him to the mat. Max goes for an elbow smash which misses when Radd rolls out of the way. Radd then kips up to his feet only to have Max sweep his feet out from under him and then places him in a hammerlock face down on the mat. Radd grabs the ropes with his free arm to force the break and Max quickly gets to his feet as radd again kips up to his. The two then start jawing at each other. They trade punches and Max then thumbs Radd in the eye before whipping him hard into the corner buckle. Max Cameron then picks Radd up and gives him an atomic drop. Radd stumbles and then gets caught in a headlock by Max who DDTs him to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Radd Matthews.
Casey Braun - Unusual to have a world title match this early in the show. Henry Lesher - Well it is Max, after all. Lethal Weapon chants continue from the crowd as Max screams at them to shut up. Max stands radd up, and goes to whip him off the ropes only to have Radd reverse it, sending Max to the ropes. Max then takes a dropkick from the challenger. Radd follows that up by heading to the ropes and making his way to the top. Radd then lands a dropkick to the chin on Max from there and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by the defending champion. Radd then stands Max up, whips him into the corner and spears him as he comes out of it. Radd again goes for the pin. 1...2... again Max manages to kick out. Radd then stands Max up while telling the ref why that should have been a three count. Max responds with a thumb to the eye on Radd and then gives him a clothesline to the mat. Max then looks to the crowd and appears set to deliver the finisher on Radd. He stands Radd up and then picks him up for a power bomb. Radd starts wailing away on Max as Max holds him up and Radd then grabs the top rope to prevent the move from being executed. Max thinks fast, dropping down and causing Radd to land with his throat on the top rope. Max then pulls his feet forward, sending Radd hard to the mat where he smashes the back of his head soundly.
Henry Lesher - That was vicious. Casey Braun - So you approved of it. Henry Lesher - Of course I did. Max Cameron stands Radd up, then gives him a Rock Bottom and then goes for the pin, his feet on the ropes. 1...2...3! Max retains as the ref never saw the feet on the ropes. Max Cameron then rolls out of the ring and grabs his title belt mere moments before Lethal Weapon is there with a chair in hand. Max catches sight of Lethal out of the corner of his eye and ducks the chair shot before disappearing into the crowd, Weapon in close pursuit.
Casey Braun - Max won the battle, but Weapon is looking to win the war. Henry Lesher - If Max runs from this guy every week, he'll be in the next Olympics and be favored to win a medal.
Backstage we see Stevie James walking into the locker room when he almost runs into Kosmic Kitty.
Stevie James - *smiles shyly* Excuse me. Kosmic Kitty - *in broken speech* It's fine. Stevie James - Kitty, have you been crying? Kosmic Kitty - I'm fine. *sniffs* Just having a bad night. Radd attacked me and... Stevie drops his bags and heads off in search of Radd Matthews as Kitty shakes her head and starts crying again.
GoBoom vs Youthful Aggression "Points of Authority" by Linkin Park plays as Boomshalkalaka and Rod Gordon come out to mostly cheers from the crowd. Boom hits the ring first and poses in the corner buckle for the cheering masses. They then talk together in the middle of the ring as we hear "I Hate Everything About You" and Rated R makes his way to the ring with Gerard Angelo close behind. As they reach ringside, we look out into the crowd and see BLPW's Simon again taking in a GWO event.
Henry Lesher - Who let him into the building? Casey Braun - I guess he bought a ticket. Henry Lesher - They shouldn't have even done that. That's one guy this place can do without... along with pretty near everyone else.
The bell sounds as rated R starts off against Boom. The two stand in the center of the ring and begin to engage in a shoving contest. Rated R then tries for a clothesline, but Boom ducks it. Boom then lands a series of rights and lefts to the chin of Rated R, driving Ryan into GoBoom's corner where Rod knees him in the back. Boom then tags Rod in. Rod leaps over the top rope and then gives Rated R a hip toss to the center of the ring where he applies an arm bar. Rated R then does a standing somersault to break that and then gives Rod a knife edge chop to the chest before tagging in Gerard Angelo. Gerard steps through the ropes and then grabs Rod by the hair and headbutts him backwards. Gerard then clotheslines Rod to the mat and has a few words for Boom on the apron. Gerard then turns to Rod Gordon, places him in a headlock and sets for a suplex. Rod blocks it and then gives Gerard a snap suplex to the mat. Rod follows that up by quickly getting to his feet and applying a Boston Crab on Gerard. The ref checks for a submission as Rated R screams to his partner from the apron. Rod tries to sit back further in the hold, but Gerard refuses to give up as he reaches for the ropes. Rated R gets tired of calling from the apron, enters the ring and plants a heel kick to the face of Rod Gordon. Rated R is then caught with a clothesline from Boom as he tries to leave the ring right away after.
Henry Lesher - Good work by Ryan, but you always have to watch for the partner. Casey Braun - Clearly you have a lot of experience inside the ring. Henry Lesher - Smart ass idiot. Boom and Rated R fight their way out of the ring as Rod stands up slowly. Gerard is seen reaching into his tights and pulling out what appear to be brass knuckles. He puts them on and takes a swing at Rod which Rod ducks. Rod then gives Gerard an atomic drop that sends Angelo through the ropes and crashing into the guard railing. Rod Gordon heads out after him as Boomshalkalaka takes a low blow from Rated R while the referee's back is turned. Rated R then picks up a chair and smashes Boom over the head with it. Rated R then steps back up onto the apron as Gerard is rolled into the ring by Rod. Gerard's brass knuckles are lying at ringside. Rod stands Gerard up and looks to his corner to make the tag when he finds out Boom is barely moving and nowhere near his corner. Gerard then gives Rod an inverted atomic drop and then tags in Rated R. Rated R enters the ring with a smile on his face.
Henry Lesher - See, Rated R is the clever one here and has turned this into a handicap match.
Rated R kicks Rod Gordon in the gut, then picks him up and gives him a power slam. Rated R goes for the pin and appears to be lazy about it. 1...2.... Rod manages to roll a shoulder out. Rated R curses and stands Rod up, looks to the crowd and gives Rod the Restricted. Rated R smiles and goes for another cocky pin. 1...2... Rod gets a foot on the bottom rope. Rated R starts screaming at the referee. He then heads to the corner and makes his way to the top. As he does, Boom slides into the ring and the ref starts to shoves him out. Myst then appears with a chair in hand and clubs Rated R across the back with it, knocking him off the top rope. Rod Gordon then rolls over onto Rated R as Boom gives up on getting into the ring and starts battling Gerard on the floor. The ref then counts the pin as Myst retreats into the crowd. 1...2...3! GoBoom with the win this week!
Henry Lesher - That was robbery by Myst! Casey Braun - I guess she figures payback is fair game. Her war with Youthful Aggression continues.
Backstage we see Hellfire Jack getting set for tonight's tag match when Shannon Skanes enters the picture.
Shannon Skanes - Just wanted to wish you luck out there tonight. Hellfire Jack - Against your wife? Shannon Skanes - Against outsiders, especially those who feel they are too good for us. Hellfire Jack - *laughs* Those two bitches are going down and fast. Shannon Skanes - You're in line for a world title shot soon at the rate you're going. Hellfire Jack - The fiery hand of righteousness always manages to get to the top, boss. Wrestling is in my blood.
Shannon Skanes - Well, best of luck. Shannon leaves as Halloween Jack comes in and does a double take. Halloween Jack - What was he doing in here? Hellfire Jack - Wishing us luck. Halloween Jack - Against his wife? Hellfire Jack - No, against those who don't show the GWO roster respect. Halloween Jack - So anyone who isn't in the GWO then. We head to commercial. We see Charley MacAdam in the GWO studio with the usual smile on his face. "Charley Mac here with a huge GWO update! Steve Stryker is back! Next week he will be inside a GWO ring for the first time in over a month! We are also proud to announce the signing of a new Canadian hero, Blake Young! He'll make his GWO debut next week! Now, with Bloodshed two weeks away, we are getting set for some big action! The world title match will be announced tonight! That's right, boys and girls! It will be announced tonight! We know Austin Fierro is facing Halloween Jack and that the tag titles will be up for grabs and tonight we'll see the card take shape even further! It should be great and with all these stars signing on, it should just keep getting better every week! I am Charley and thanks for your time!" Charley winks as the shot fades to black.
Thunder vs Jake Evans vs Joe Davidson "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays as Thunder is walked to the ring by Marla Storme. The crowd responds with a more mixed reaction to Thunder this week as he steps up onto the apron and then steps over the top rope. He then has a few words with the ref as we hear "Just Lose It" by Eminem and Jake Evans is walked to the ring by Matt Classic and Dizz. Dizz is high fives by fans who are overjoyed when he even takes the time to sign a couple of autographs. Jake steps onto the apron and then into the ring. He has a few words with Thunder who tries to intimidate Jake who responds by stomping on the big man's foot and then gives him an eye gouge.
