GWO Overdrive, 22 November 2004

22 November 2004

Tuscon, AZ Backstage we see Max Cameron entering the building with the world title over his shoulder. He is quickly headed off by Leech who has a chair in his hand, but doesn't swing it just yet.
Max Cameron - Well look what we have here. Leech - You think I've been running from you? Max Cameron - I know you have been running from me. Leech - *laughs* You're in for a long night, Max Cameron. Leech then turns and walks away without incident as Max looks on confused. He fully expected to be attacked before the show. With that near incident passed, we see the opening credits of the show, complete with images of Youthful Aggression, The Dying Breed, GoBoom, Myst, Sal Clemenzo and Lethal Weapon before we head to ringside where Ricky Simms is back at the side of Henry Lesher.
Henry Lesher - I knew you would be back. Ricky Simms - There's a reason and it's going to be dealt with. The Dawg vs Shengee Shmelky "Blue" by Eiffel 65 plays as Shengee Shmelky comes out with Adam West leading the way. He high fives fans on his way to the ring while showing off some new gold chains he got off Ebay. Shengee then steps onto the apron and poses for the crowd. It would, of course, be more impressive if he had muscles to show off. As Shengee enters the ring, we hear "Who Let the Dawgs Out?" by Baha Men and The Dawg makes his way to the ring. He gets a more mixed reaction as the crowd has chosen to side with underdog Shengee here tonight. Dawg shakes his head as he comes down to the ring. He steps up onto the apron and then into the ring. The bell sounds and Dawg clotheslines the rapping Shengee off his feet.
Henry Lesher - Our first match together in awhile and it has to be the same crap that sent you away from me. Are you going to defend the helpless Shengee?
Ricky Simms - Look, I am not in the mood tonight. Drop it. Henry Lesher - But we are all so very interested in why you are down here and word has it that Joe Davidson is out of action as well.
Ricky Simms - If Tony Parizi has a problem with the CWA legends in there, he could have gone another route!
Henry Lesher - So it's true he pulled your insurance for tonight? Ricky Simms - He said that the GWO's insurance company would not cover the four of us. Are ya happy?
Henry Lesher - I am, yeah. That's a good call by Parizi. Now let's watch the fat guy and the albino freak that they did insure.
The Dawg picks Shengee up, then gives him a sidewalk slam to the mat. Dawg then heads to the ropes and climbs onto the second rope and leaps off, landing a leg drop across the throat of the fallen Shmelky from there. He then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! The Dawg gets the win and then immediately grabs the microphone from ring announcer Shelley Morris.
The Dawg - I have earned a test! This was no test tonight! I am going to beat down every damned idiot jobber I have to twice as bad now because that dressing room fears me!
Dawg throws the mic aside and grabs Shengee by the hair. As he does, Rod Gordon hits the ring with a chair in hand and smashes Dawg across the back of the head with it. Gordon then slams the chair over the back of The Dawg before he slips back out of the ring, shaking his head. Adam West moves to thank Rod, but Rod hauls off as if he is going to drill Adam in the head as well so Adam backs off.
Henry Lesher - Why did he save that idiot? Ricky Simms - Sometimes a person is just ready for a fight and looking for a reason to enter one.
The scene opens backstage in the parking lot, a black porsche 911 pulls up into a parking spot and stepping out is Stevie James in a black leather jacket and Heartbreaker merchandise, he grabs his back from the passenger seat and takes a deep breath before looking into the arena area, the fans watching in the arena are going mad as he nods and heads in the arena area whispering the words, "Come on Stevie!"
Holmes vs Cordelia Parizi "Black Number One" by Tupe O Negative plays as Holmes comes out for his debut tonight. He gets a mixed reaction as most unknowns do when they start their GWO careers, though he sure doesn't appear friendly or upbeat tonight and views a few people in the crowd seemingly with deep hatred. He steps up onto the apron and surveys the packed house before entering the ring. Once inside the ring, we hear "Come With Me" by Puff Daddy and out comes Cordelia Parizi to a warm reaction as Big Guido leads the way for her. Cordelia steps onto the apron and immediately Holmes grabs her by the hair and pulls her over the top rope into the ring as the bell sounds to make this match official. He then grabs her by the throat, stands her up and whips her off the ropes before clotheslining her to the mat. He then picks her up and gives her a sidewalk slam and goes for an early pin. 1...2... kick out by Cordelia.
Henry Lesher - I always like it when they try to end the match right away, like they have somewhere to be.
Ricky Simms - According to his resume, Holmes has been inside the ring a few times and been successful in his attempts previous.
Henry Lesher - He should be a God here seeing as we have so many wanna bes on board. Holmes stands Cordelia up, whips her off the ropes and Cordelia ducks a clothesline and then comes back with a dropkick on Holmes that staggers him and sends him into the ropes. She then gives him a monkey flip to the center of the ring Cordelia then comes off the ropes and tries for a rolling thunder only to have Holmes move out of the way in time to avoid it and she lands hard on her back on the mat. Holmes then quickly gets to his feet, puts the boots to Cordelia and then grabs her by the hair, stands her up, gives her a headbutt and then picks her up and delivers a power slam on her. He goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out again by Cordelia. Holmes then stands her up, whips her into the corner and charges straight into raised knees from Parizi who then jumps to the second rope and clotheslines Holmes to the mat from there before she tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Holmes.
Ricky Simms - Back and forth action here and a few near pins. Henry Lesher - Spoken like a man meant to not wrestle. Ricky Simms - Keep it up, Doc. Cordelia stands Holmes up, whips him off the ropes and Holmes ducks a clothesline, then spears Cordelia to the mat and applies a Holmes Death Lock which sends Cordelia into unimaginable pain as she reaches for the rope with her free hand. She sees she is too far away and feels herself fading in the hold and she then taps out.
Ricky Simms - Holmes with the win as Cordelia Parizi taps out. Henry Lesher - This Holmes guy has potential. Holmes rolls out of the ring and heads towards the back with a smile on his face. We then head to commercial.
We see Shannon Skanes in a studio in front of a white screen. He shrugs. "Frank Merritt is here tonight as a special guest referee, so we are without our opening commercial, a touching tale of an old lady being led across the street by Frank before he power bombs her to the concrete. Oh man, the censors were gonna love that. Oh well, it's been as close to thirty damned seconds as I'll get you, so let's go back to the show.
Steve Stryker vs Rod Gordon "Destroy All" by Static X plays as Steve Stryker comes to the ring in his heavily anticipated GWO return. He is booed on his way to the ring as he has a few words with one fan in the front row before continuing on his way to the ring. He steps through the ropes and then stands on the second rope in the corner and poses once before jumping down into the middle of the ring again and awaiting the arrival of his opponent tonight. "Diamonds and Guns" by The Transplants plays as Rod Gordon comes out to a rare positice reaction. He even high fives that same fan who had been arguing with Steve Stryker moments earlier. Rod then mnakes his way to ringside and has a few words with Steve who motions Rod into the ring. Rod steps up onto the apron, into the ring and the bell sounds.
Henry Lesher - Yet another pointless GWO match. I guess we are trying to rob people of their will to live tonight.
Ricky Simms - I had hoped your sense of humor would have improved while I was gone. Henry Lesher - I had hoped a few knocks in the head would make you entertaining, too. Rod and Steve lock up. Steve Stryker quickly shoves Rod Gordon into the corner and then gives him a backdrop to the center of the ring. Rod kips up to his feet only to take a roundhouse kick to the side of the head from Stryker who then boot chokes Gordon in the ropes. Steve Stryker doesn't release the choke until the ref reaches a four count on it, one away from the fatal disqualification. Stryker then stands Gordon up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a clothesline to the mat. Stryker then takes to the ropes, heading to the second rope as Rod reaches his hands and knees. Steve comes off the ropes looking for a double axe handle smash across the back of Rod Gordon only to have Rod then sucker punch Steve in the breadbasket. Rod then headbutts Stryker backwards into the ropes and then gives him a snap suplex to the middle of the ring. Gordon then quickly gets to his feet and does a standing somersault onto the fallen Stryker and then hooks the leg for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Steve Stryker. Rod asks the ref if he knows how to count to three, then stands Steve Stryker up, whips him off the ropes and then backdrops him to the mat.
Henry Lesher - Why is this still going on? We have better matches on hold like Leech's and.... well, that's pretty much it. Let's get Leech out here.
Ricky Simms - Leech will be most welcome in the ring tonight. Henry Lesher - What? Why are you smiling like that? What's going on? Ricky Simms - Just shut up and watch the match. Rod picks Steve Stryker up and races towards the corner. Steve slides down the back of Rod Gordon, slams him head first into the corner buckle and then rolls him up for the pin. 1...2... Rod manages to roll out and rolls himself right out of the ring. Steve Stryker then follows Rod out, grabs him by the hair and slams his head into the guard railing. Rod then eye gouges Steve Stryker, then tries to whip him into the ring steps only to have Steve Stryker reverse it and send Rod into the steel ring steps shoulder first instead. Steve Stryker then grabs Rod by the hair again and slams his head off of the steel steps before tossing him over the guard railing into the aisle between rows of fans as security holds the fans back. The ref reaches a ten count and calls for the bell here.
Ricky Simms - Both men counted out here. Henry Lesher - Works for me since they are both losers. Steve heads over the guard railing and starts brawling with Rod in the aisle as security tries to both holds the fans back and pry these two off of each other. The scene once more opens in the back and Kosmic Kitty is seen entering the arena. She isn’t looking very happy and has her bag over her shoulder she heads down a lobby before seeing the canteen door open, Weapon walks out a coffee in hand and Kitty suddenly smiles wide and leaps at him nearly making him jump.
Lethal Weaponm - Oh Kitty, he shocked me, you ok? Kosmic Kitty - Yep, Yep! You ok? Lethal Weapon - I am fine, thank you for asking how are you after last week, I can’t say I’d heard from you this week?
Kosmic Kitty - No, I needed some time to myself…Erm…How’s Stevie? Lethal Weapon - He is very good, he arrived earlier, maybe you should go and see him. Kosmic Kitty - Oh, well I er…erm…Better not, I mean, I got a lot to do. Lethal Weapon - Ah I see…Ok, well if you need me you know where I’m going to be. Kitty just nods as Lethal walks off, more and more he was becoming like a farther to her, supportive, understanding and experienced, this was not the man she had come to know him as from the tails and rumours, Lethal was…Humane. She shrugs and skips away a little happier now.
Radd Matthews vs Leech "Vindicated" by Dashboard Confessional plays as Radd Matthews comes out to the loudest boos of the night. Radd grabs at a Kosmic Kitty poster someone has in the crowd, but he is unsuccessful at grabbing it. Radd then makes his way to ringside and smiles as he looks out at the booing throngs of fans. He steps up onto the apron, into the ring and tests the ropes as "Enemies of Reality" by Nevermore plays and Leech starts making his way to the ring. Leech gets booed just as loud as Radd and shakes his head as he makes his way to the ring. Leech then starts to run the rest of the way to the ring and slides undfer the bottom rope and then trips the legs out from under an unprepared Radd. Leech then lands a series of rights and lefts to the head of Radd Matthews before standing him up and then hip tossing him back down to the mat.
Henry Lesher - Now we have a perfect match here with Leech in it, showing us how wrestling is really done.
Ricky Simms - A match that Joe Davidson was supposed to be in. Henry Lesher - No more senior citizens in the ring. Leech applies an arm bar on Radd. Radd gets up to one knee and Leech kicks him in the face, then twists the arm again. Leech then stands Radd up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a knee to the gut that sends him through the air and somersaulting to the mat where he lands on his tailbone. Leech then applies a sleeperhold. The ref checks for a submission, but Radd refuses to give even as he starts to look very drowsy. The ref raises the arm of Radd once and it drops. He raises the arm of Radd a second time and again it drops. The ref raises Radd's arm a third time and this time Radd keeps it in the air to prevent a loss here. Radd then starts elbowing Leech in the ribs, then snap mares him to the mat thus breaking the hold. Radd then rolls himself out of the ring to regroup and rests his hands on the announce table. Ricky stands up and lands an upper cut on Radd, then slams his head into the table while the ref is having words with Leech.
Henry Lesher - What the hell was that? Ricky Simms - Hey, I'm not wrestling. Leech rolls himself out of the ring and goes to whip Radd into the steel corner post only to have Radd reverse it and Leech slams head first into the corner post. Radd rolls himself into the ring and has a few words with the ref as Ricky gets up from his chair, folds it and heads over to Leech, then slams the chair over his head. He then heads back to his seat, sits the chair out and sits back down as the fans cheer him on.
Henry Lesher - There's something wrong with your brain! That is Leech, a God among men! Ricky Simms - Both men are slime. Leech attacks kids and Radd has talked enough about Kosmic Kitty being a whore and whatever other lies he's spewing. I've seen enough of both of them.
Leech rolls himself into the ring where Radd grabs him by the hair. Radd headbutts Leech into the corner where he then lands a series of kicks to the gut on Leech. Radd then hip tosses Leech to the center of the ring and then goes for a springboard moonsault which misses when Leech rolls out of the way and then hooks the tights on Radd for the pin attempt. 1...2...3! Leech gains the win here tonight.
Henry Lesher - Despite your stupidity, Leech wins and furthers his legend. Leech grabs the microphone from Shelley Morris. Leech - Hey, Simms, asshole! You wanna go? You wanna do this? Get in here! Ricky Simms gets up from his position at the table and takes off his sport coat and unbuttons the cuffs on his shirt. He takes off his headset as the fans start a chant for him. Ricky steps up onto the bottom step of the ring steps and we then hear "Come With Me" by Puff Daddy and Tony Parizi comes out with Big Guido close behind.
Tony Parizi - Whoa there chief! Ricky, I gave you a directive this week! I told you to stay at your desk and stay out of it. Insurance companies being what they are, they will not insure your group. You're not insured, so you stay as an announcer. Leech, you did what you had to by beating Radd Matthews and you've earned yourself a world title shot next week in the fatal four way. Ricky, if you want to deal with Leech, do it on your own damned time not mine!
Ricky backs down as Leech gives him the finger and then turns back to say something to Parizi who is already gone. Leech then takes a spear from Radd who has been listening to and watching all of this. Radd then grabs the microphone and he decides it's his turn.
Radd Matthews - Next week I will take that Stevie James bastard down! Next week I will show you all what a whore Kitty was to me and why she deserved everything she got when she lost the tag titles and why she'll wish she never met me when I am done with her new boy!
Before Radd can say anything more, we see Myst on the four screens hanging above the ring which brings the crowd to their feet.
Myst - Oh look, it's Radd Matthews, the pathetic and feminine man who thinks he is winning back the Pacific title. I have been listening to everyone calling myself and Kosmic Kitty slut, whore, slut, whore, to the point where I expect Jerry Springer to host the damned show! See you next Monday, Radd.
Myst's image turns to snow before it goes back to showing us Radd inside the ring as he screams and yells. Radd then races out of the ring towards the back, not appearing to be very impressed with this turn of events as we then go to commercial.
We see an old man feeding the birds next to a quiet pond as the sun shines down on him. He smiles at the camera. "Hi there. Xtasy was supposed to be here, but he's wrestling. Then Frank was gonna be here, but he's reffing." He frowns. "So they sent Ingee Shmelky." The camera turns and we see Ingee down on the ground being attacked by geese and screaming "My eyes! My eyes! Dear Lord, it hurts!" The camera then turns back to the old man. "This was going to be a life insurance commercial, but nothing is worth this crap." He gets up and storms off as the camera settles back on Ingee before we see the Aflac logo. Mercifully, we return to the show.
Xtasy vs Kosmic Kitty "X Gonna Give It To Ya" by DMX plays as Xtasy makes his way to the ring. The crowd explodes as if he's a big star or legend or something as he high fives hyper fans all the way to the ring. He then poses once for the camera before he continues on his way into the ring.
