GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

GWO Genesis, 23 August 2004

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coquitlam, BC We see Hector Braun pacing backstage as the crowd files in for another packed show. Every week now the ratings are rising and the heat on Hector is rising along with it. He finally hears the squeal of tires and smiles as he sees Dan Jalldoff step out of his black Porsche, sunglasses on his young face. Hector heads out the nearby back door and looks around to make certain no one can see him.
Hector Braun - Dan, good to see you. Dan Jalldoff - You made an offer and here I am. We then leave them to discuss things that need to be discussed and see somewhere else in a locker room where Dizz is being dressed up by Crystal Braun. Max Cameron enters the shot.
Max Cameron - This is awful. Crystal Braun - No, this is ratings. Daddy thinks if we can find another wheelchair bound wrestler to put Dizz in the ring with we will get worldwide exposure and Daddy says there is no bad exposure.
Max Cameron - There's a place in hell reserved for people like you. Max leaves Crystal there, her jaw dropped in shock and we then see Shannon Skanes backstage with Wyatt Gibson.
Wyatt Gibson - I've got your back in case Max turns on you. Shannon Skanes - Good to know, but I shouldn't need it. None of these guys have proved themselves against me and none of them will.
Wyatt Gibson - Just be careful. Shannon Skanes - You're sounding like my wife. The crowd is filing in and outside stands Devin Blade and Bobby Banks signing autographs and having their pictures taken with fans. Corina has come out on the other side of the building to sign a few herself and allows for a few pictures to be taken before she heads back inside the building. The preliminary stuff out of the way, we are ready to begin our event this week, the last before Rumours of War.
The pyro goes off and the crowd is louder than last week's. The fact is that the GWO is growing in popularity every week and this week is no exception. We head to ringside and then we hear "All Over You" by AC/DC and the crowd gets to its feet, exploding with cheers as they anticipate someone coming out, but they boo when they see Hector Braun instead. He is laughing.
Hector Braun - You idiots should see your faces! You expected Vigilante to come out? Dear God, he is far too good for this place! Now, all joking aside, there is a reason for that. I wish to build the GWO up to the level we were at in the CWA all those ages ago... a month or two I believe. Anyways, I have consulted with some people and we agreed it was time to bring in someone to police the place since Wyatt Gibson failed miserably to do the job. From the moment Dirk Bailey's fat drumstick went through a plywood ramp in Penticton, I knew he was doomed. Now, I played the music of a legend because that legend had a fine manager before CWA went under and now I bring you his son.... your new Commissioner Dan Jalldoff!
"Ticks and Leeches" by Tool blares through the arena as Dan Jalldoff comes out in a new suit with his girl Sabrina on his arm. The crowd boos him and he shares a laugh with Hector before grabbing the microphone.
Dan Jalldoff - Damned straight my father's client is too good for this place. The GWO is no CWA! These fans have to see that! We are in Coquitlam! What is a Coquitlam and how can I cure it? My father will not be seen inside a GWO arena now or ever, but I figured that Hector here needed a real soldier to protect his best interests and that man is me. My girl Sabrina and me will do a fine job running the show, no matter what you idiots think!
"Ticks and Leeches" by Tool starts to play again as Dan walks to the back with his girl still on his arm and Hector close behind. That done, we head to our first commercial.
Frank Merritt is seen once again with a headset on in a Shaw call center. "Hello again. I am Frank, an employee of Shaw Cable. Last week we were just starting things off when I.... oh, wait, got a call. Hi there. My name if Frank. How may I help you? Yes you have reached Shaw. I'm sorry I didn't open the call by saying I was at Shaw, bitch, figured you'd know who you dialled. Yes I called you a bitch but not like you think. It's German for fucking cow whore. Did you know that? Great and thank you for calling Shaw." Frank sighs. "It's not easy being nice, but it's my job... oh got another call. Hi there. Thanks for calling Shaw. How may I help you? My name is Frank. Look asshole, I know that's supposed to be in my opening..." We fade to the Shaw logo.
Rated R is seen coming to the ring as "I Hate Everything About You" plays throughout the packed arena. Rated R steps through the ropes and waves to his fans who start chanting his name.
Rated R - Now allow me to introduce myself. I am Rated R, former CWA star and former member of Anarchy Inc. which I understand is done away with. Now my former ally Vesuvius has had plenty of chance to respond to me, but he hasn't and therefore I am calling him out right now. Come on, Vesuvius! Show me what you're made of!
Rated R throws the microphone down, but it's not Vesuvius' music that plays. It is "Ticks and Leeches" by Tool that plays instead. Dan Jalldoff comes out and looks around at the booing crowd and laughs again.
Dan Jalldoff - Ryan, Ryan, Ryan, you were the guy who had been at the top and then down you fell. Now you're here in the GWO hoping to get a shot at Vesuvius? He's busy. You want a match that damned bad? Tell you what. Rumours of War next week I'll get you a great match. Tigre Blanco has nothing to do so why don't you two fight it out and we'll see which of you gets a shot at the Canadian title the week following Rumours of War at our first ever Overdrive.
Rated R picks up the microphone. Rated R - So Vesuvius doesn't have the guts to come out and face me like a man? I have to go find him?
Rated R throws the microphone down again and storms out of the ring. Dan Jalldoff shakes his head.
Dan Jalldoff - Ryan, you're not scheduled to even be here tonight. You have no match. I am not going to have you harming the talent here tonight. Boys, walk him out.
Security guards come out and guide Rated R out of the building. Rated R kicks over a stack of boxes and shoves over a palette which had audio equipment sitting on top of it. Once he is in the parking lot, Rated R smiles and thanks the security guards for walking him out and heads out of the shot. Dan Jalldoff is then seen looking concerned at him taking this too well.
Devo vs Celia Air raid sirens sound off as Suende leads Devo towards the ring, the crowd immediately voicing their disapproval of this wrestler. He shouts at a few fans in the front row, then jumps up onto the apron and looks around at the crowd and spits on the floor before stepping into the ring, Suende taking her place at ringside. Devo tests the ropes as "Heads Explode" by Monster Magnet plays and out comes Celia. She is without a smile this week as Nikolus Flagg leads her down to ringside. Celia and Nick have a few unkind words before she steps through the ropes and the bell sounds as Devo lands a series of kicks to the ribs of Celia, then DDTs her to the mat and tries for the quick pin fall. 1...2... kick out by Celia.
Ricky Simms - The show gets underway and we are looking at a tough match right off the start.
Henry Lesher - I'll be the judge of that. Celia is nasty, but so is Devo. It should be interesting, but this place has let me down so many times already.
Devo stands Celia up and then two handed chokes her against the ropes. Celia responds by thumbing Devo in the eye, then raking her nails down his back with a sadistic smile on her face. Celia then comes off the ropes and lands a lariat on Devo that knocks him off his feet and out of the ring near where Nick is standing. Suende comes around the ring and makes eye contact with Nick. Neither back down as Celia rolls herself out of the ring and starts putting the boots to Devo before he has had a chance to get to his feet. Celia then whips Devo towards the steel corner post only to have Devo stop himself and then deliver a standing side kick to the chest of Celia before clotheslining Nick to the floor. Devo then rolls Celia into the ring and has a few words for a fan in the front row before he enters the ring himself, barely beating the ten count.
Ricky Simms - Very physical match up and apparently the managers are going to be involved here.
Henry Lesher - Clearly Nick has no place out here if he cannot protect Celia. My daughter did a much better job of protecting her.
Ricky Simms - Yeah, yeah, yeah, your daughter can do no wrong. Henry Lesher - This is true. She does no wrong. Devo boot chokes Celia in the ropes for four seconds, releasing it just before the ref is set to DQ him. Devo stands Celia up, whips her hard into the corner and then gives her a German suplex out of it. Devo then stands over her and looks to Suende who nods in approval of his work.
Ricky Simms - What is the deal between those two? Henry Lesher - Do you really want to know? Ricky Simms - No, no I don't. Devo stands Celia up, whips her into the corner and charges shoulder first at her, but Celia moves out of the way and Devo hits the corner hard. Celia then heads out of the ring onto the apron and does a sunset flip on Devo into a pin fall attempt. 1...2... kick out by Devo. Nick applauds at ringside, having got up after the shot from Devo and looking none the worse for wear. Celia stands Devo up, whips him off the ropes and Devo ducks a clothesline and then gives Celia an atomic drop to the mat followed by a reverse neck breaker. Devo then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Celia and Devo starts arguing with the ref about his ability to count to three and the crowd starts into a chant for Dizz?
Henry Lesher - Maybe the fans aren't total morons. They want Dizz to come out here! Ricky Simms - They are cheering for a legend, not for his exploitation. Devo stands Celia up. Celia eye gouges Devo, then rakes his eyes along the top rope. Celia then whips Devo off the ropes and backdrops him over the top rope and to the floor. Celia then climbs to the top as Devo gets to his feet only to find Suende standing between Celia and her target. Celia decides to launch herself onto Suende and lands a flying cross body on her to the floor. Devo then rolls himself into the ring, waits for Celia to slides under the bottom rope and then gives her a leg drop to the back of the neck. Devo then stands Celia up and looks to the crowd, spitting on the mat. He delivers the Hate Driver and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Devo gains the win here this week over Celia. His celebration is short lived as Scavenger enters the ring and the brawl is on between the two of them. Scavenger backs Devo into the corner with a series of rights and lefts and then snap mares him out of the corner and gives him a stiff forearm to the back of the neck.
Henry Lesher - Why does Scavenger have to ruin every event he is in? Suende enters the ring and smashes Scavenger across the back of the head with a chair. Devo then clotheslines Scavenger down to the mat before putting the boots to him. Devo grabs the chair and looks down on Scavenger with wild eyes and the crowd boos, but Austin Fierro enters the ring and dropkicks Devo from behind, driving him right through the ropes to the floor. Celia is to her feet by now, so she spears Suende to the mat and they roll out of the ring in a battle of their own. Austin Fierro then looks down at the chair Devo just had and at the fallen Scavenger. Austin picks up the chair, rears back as if he might use it and then decides to drop it and rolls himself out of the ring. Austin tells the camera there will be time for that next week and then heads to the back as security guards come out to deal with this mess.
Ricky Simms - What a mess this is leading into Rumours of War. Henry Lesher - You're right... I like it. We see a video feed through snow. It's Johnny K-9 in an orange jumpsuit and handcuffed. He looks crazed and is screaming.
Johnny K-9 - Let me out! You bastards cost me everything! Let me out now! Johnny kicks a chair over and then head butts the video camera we are watching him on, sending our feed to just snow.
Ricky Simms - What the hell was that? Henry Lesher - Looks like Johnny's incarceration isn't going well. Ricky Simms - Well, last week we all saw what happened when Gerard Angelo was busy wrestling, didn't we?
Footage is shown of Halloween Jack stealing the TV title belt from Gerard and strolling to the back. We then go backstage to Jack laughing next to Ray Dees. He has the TV title over his shoulder.
Ray Dees - Jack, last week we saw you stealing Gerard Angelo's belt. Halloween Jack - Ray, you are so wrong it's sad to hear you speak. Facts are as follows. Gerard did not beat Johnny K-9 for the title. Fact two is that Gerard Angelo was the CWA TV champ and this is not the CWA. What that means is I took a piece of gold attached to a strap of leather that no one owns until next week when I pin his sorry ass at Rumours of War.
Ray Dees - You sound very confident about your chances. Halloween Jack - Ray, it's Gerard Angelo. It's not like I'm facing the Flock or anything. Ray Dees - True enough. The Dawg vs Vesuvius "Schism" by Tool plays as Vesuvius makes his way to the ring, led by Leech. Vesuvius has an arrogant smile on his face as he steps onto the apron and takes a look around the arena before stepping over the top rope into the ring. We then hear "Who Let the Dawgs Out" by BAHA MEN and out comes The Dawg to a rousing ovation. He makes his way to the ring high fiving fans and shouting at Vesuvius the whole way to the ring where he is headed off by Leech. Dawg head butts Leech down to the floor and then rolls himself into the ring where Vesuvius lands a series of knee drops to his throat as the bell sounds. Vesuvius then starts two handed choking The Dawg. He follows that up by standing The Dawg up, whipping him off the ropes and clotheslining him to the mat.
Ricky Simms - There you see the man who refused to answer Rated R's challenge this week. Henry Lesher - Ryan is not worth his time and he knows it. Vesuvius stands The Dawg up, whips him into the corner and then charges shoulder first into the buckle as Dawg sidesteps him. Dawg then rakes the eyes of Vesuvius. The Dawg whips Vesuvius into the ropes but as he comes off he nails the Dawg with a vicious clothesline that sends The Dawg to the mat gasping for air. The Dawg crawls over to the ropes and pulls himself to his feet only to be nailed again by a flying drop kick that sends him over the top rope and crashing to the concrete below. Vesuvius climbs through the ropes and jumps down off the apron, only to be met by a huge fist to the groin that takes him to his knee’s grimacing in pain. The Dawg gets to his feet and grabs a hand full of hair, and slams Vesuvius face first into the guard rail and then kicks him in the guts. The Dawg rolls under the bottom rope, back into the ring, and as Vesuvius enters the ring, The Dawg puts him in a headlock and rakes his eyes half way down the top rope. Vesuvius covers his eyes with his hands, but as The Dawg comes up behind him, he spins around with a devastating back elbow that catches The Dawg along side of the head and sends him grabbing for nothing but air. Vesuvius then whips Dawg into the corner buckle and gives him a kick in the gut before snap maring him out of the corner.
Henry Lesher - Dawg is in over his head and it's nice of Vesuvius to show him the err of his ways.
Ricky Simms - I don't see anything nice about this. Vesuvius heads to the ropes, makes his way to the second rope and lands a back elbow from there to the chest of The Dawg before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by The Dawg. The fans start a chant for The Dawg which draws a demand to shut up from Vesuvius. Vesuvius then stands The Dawg up and snap mares him into the corner buckle. Vesuvius then takes a hold of The Dawg and whips him through the ropes to the floor. He heads outside the ring and eye gouges Dawg before slamming his head into the steel ring steps. Dawg responds with a series of elbows to the gut of Vesuvius before smashing his head into the steel corner post before The Dawg rolls himself into the ring. Vesuvius grabs the ropes to enter, but something on the screen over the ring catches his eye. There stands Rated R with a baseball bat in his hands and right next to a black SUV.
Rated R - Too good to come fight me, Vesuvius? Not tough enough to answer a challenge, mountain man? Let's see if you answer this!
Rated R starts destroying the mirrors and windshield of the SUV before moving on to the headlights as Vesuvius races out towards the exit to deal with Rated R. Inside the ring Dawg laughs as the ring reaches a ten count and Dawg's arm is raised in victory.
Henry Lesher - That's not a fair win! Ricky Simms - A win is a win, right doc? Vesuvius is seen racing into the parking lot and to where his vehicle stands broken. Vesuvius screams as he finds no sign of Rated R save the baseball bat lying through the shattered windshield. The crowd can be heard laughing as Vesuvius starts calling out for Rated R and races off in the direction he figures Ryan went with the baseball bat in hand.
Ricky Simms - If Ryan wanted the big man's attention, I am pretty sure he just got it. Henry Lesher - He'll wish he was never born. We head to commercial. Xtasy is seen in his study working on his computer. We see large numbers on his screen and he's laughing to himself. "I am so well off thanks to smart financial investments. You know where I would be without smart financial investments? Right about here." We see Frank Merritt at the front of the line up at a convenience store. "My card is not maxed out! Take it! Take the fucking thing now! All I wanted was a pack of damned smokes so you are taking my card!" Frank takes his credit card and starts trying to shove it into the clerk's mouth. "Here you bastard! Choke on it!" We then see Xtasy again, laughing to himself. "Oh, Frank, will you never learn? Invest wisely, people. You won't be as well off as me, but you'll still be better off than Frank."
Tex Murrphy vs Radd Matthews "Vindicated" by Dashboard Confessional plays as Radd Matthews comes out to a large pop from the crowd. He makes his way to the ring and high fives fans on his way down, wearing a Smooth Kriminals T-shirt as we all wonder if we will ever see these two in an actual tag team match up. Radd steps into the ring and is inside the ring a few moments when Radd sees Soul Reaver on the screens hanging over the ring with Tex Murrphy tied up in a chair behind him.
Soul Reaver - Radd, your opponent here stole something from me last week. *shows the camera the Pacific title belt* I stole it back. Tex here won't be hunting for any clues this coming week. He'll be trying to save his very life. Radd, next week you learn the meaning of the word pain. This week, you see what happens to people who cross me.
Soul Reaver turns and smashes Tex in the face with the title belt which drops his chair onto its back and Tex hits the floor hard, blood streaming down his face. Soul Reaver stands over him and smiles before turning to address Radd yet again.
Soul Reaver - Radd, next week the ladder match will end your career. It will show the world why no one messes with me.
The shot turns to snow for a moment before it again shows the action inside the ring. Radd looks hesitant to leave the ring for a moment, but he does so and races into the backstage area.
Ricky Simms - Looks like Radd's match this week is off. Henry Lesher - I like it. Radd wins by forfeit and Reaver stopped me from watching another bore fest. Let's elect that guy president.
We see Radd enter his locker room where Tex Murrphy lies in a heap with EMTs attending to him and the Pacific title belt is bent and broken hanging on a locker over the fallen detective. Radd shakes his head as he watches them place Tex on a stretcher. Kitty then enters the frame.
Kosmic Kitty - Thank God you're okay. That Soul Reaver is really mean. Radd Matthews - *embracing Kitty* I'm fine. Kosmic Kitty - *pouting* Why are people always trying to ruin our lives? Radd Matthews - *stroking her hair* I don't know, but it'll be all right, Kitty. The two leave the room in each other's arms. Henry Lesher - Gag me, please. Why is everyone in love with that blond airhead? Ricky Simms - She's genuine. Henry Lesher - Sure she is. They all are for awhile. John Empire vs Reverend Darius "American Jesus" by Bad Religion plays and we see Reverend Darius making his way to the ring with a sadistic smile on his face as we again see Leech tonight, leading Reverend Darius down the aisle to the ring. Darius closes his eye and appears to be praying. He draws a Twinkie out of his coat and smiles at it and it becomes ablaze with fire as the crowd is caught off guard. Darius then drops it to the ground and smiles at the camera before he enters the ring. "Empire" by The Bam Bam Francs plays as John Empire makes his way to the ring. John wastes no time in rushing into the ring and the bell sounds as the two go at it toe to toe in the middle of the ring. Darius gains the advantage with a thumb to the eye of Empire, then whips him into the corner buckle. Darius then gives Empire a reverse neck breaker which he follows up with a leg drop before he attempts the pin here. 1...2... kick out by John Empire.
Henry Lesher - Darius is in a hurry. I like that. Ricky Simms - Stop leering at Leech like that. It's disturbing. Henry Lesher - Nuts to you. Darius stands Empire up, grabs him by the tights and whips him through the ropes where he then crashes into the guard railing. The ref pushes Darius back, warning him about these actions as Leech heads around, helps Empire up, then gives him a knife edge chop to the throat and then picks him up and drops him on the guard railing before he backs away. The ref turns back, sees Empire bleeding now and has a few words for Leech. However, since the ref saw nothing he cannot end the match here. Leech laughs with his back to the ref as the fans boo. Darius heads outside the ring, starts to stand Empire up and takes a jawbreaker from John Empire. Empire then whips Darius into the steel ring steps. Empire then rolls Darius into the ring, rolling himself in just before the ten count. John Empire then pulls Darius to the center of the ring and places him in a sharpshooter. The crowd appears to be supporting Empire as the lesser of two evils here this week. Darius screams in pain as Leech slaps the apron and screams at Darius to get to the ropes.
Henry Lesher - Look at Leech all bossy and stuff. Ricky Simms - I am going to ask we no longer sit together. Henry Lesher - I have been asking for that for over a year. Leech jumps onto the apron which distracts the ref. Vesuvius enters from the crowd, reaches into the ring and lands a right hand to the head of Empire. It's not a hard shot, but Empire releases the sharpshooter and has a few words for Vesuvius. Darius, meanwhile, uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet and notices Empire has his back to him. Darius dropkicks Empire in the back which sends him flying out of the ring to the feet of a laughing Vesuvius.
Ricky Simms - Vesuvius is already snarly about the whole thing where Rated R smashed up his truck.
Henry Lesher - Snarly? He's not a cocker spaniel, Ricky. Vesuvius backs off as the ref orders him to. Darius climbs to the top rope and lands a flying cross body on John Empire from there. Darius then stands Empire up and rolls him into the ring. He stands Empire up, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. He appears to condemn Empire, but we hear him call Empire Kosmic!
Ricky Simms - Darius is snapping. He is seeing Kosmic Kitty in there with him. Henry Lesher - That idiot could make anyone snap. Darius steps onto the apron, appears to damn a few fans in the front row and then lands the Rebirth on John Empire and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Reverend Darius gets the win here this week.
Ricky Simms - Darius has plenty of momentum heading into next week's match at Rumours of War and I must say I am worried about Kitty.
Henry Lesher - All of you bleeding hearts are worried about that idiot. Darius is a real man and with Leech leading him, he's unstoppable. Looks like Kitty is in it deep.
Darius grabs a microphone from the ring announcer. Reverend Darius - Next week, Kosmic Kitty becomes one with the darkness. Next week, I will show every misguided fool in this crowd why I am to be feared. Next week, it's war and Kitty will be the casualty. I will defeat her, win her for myself and I will destroy the man who has been her boss. No friend of hers is safe. No ally of hers shall survive. Radd Matthews, get in my way and you shall perish as well. Don't be a fool!
Darius throws the microphone down as Vesuvius and Leech are seen at ringside giving their "amens" to that little speech by Darius. He leads the way as the other two trail behind him, all quite confident of The Parish's chances at Rumours of War. We head to commercial.
We see Myst looking at her nails. She smiles when she sees her commercial has started. "People have been asking me what I have been doing with my time off. There's a simple answer to that. You see, I have been training on this." We see a machine that resembles a torture device from the 12th century. "This is the Asskicker 5000. It might make you bleed, might make you stronger, but whatever it's doing it will make me the toughest bitch any of you have ever seen." We see her again and she smiles. "I would love to see that mouthy Merritt on there. Let's see if he is man enough for the Asskicker 5000." We see Frank Merritt choking the director with the machine in the background. "I... do... not... smile... on... command," Frank says through gritted teeth." Myst is then seen laughing. "Oh that Frank Merritt. Will he ever learn? The Asskicker 5000. Be a tough bitch like Myst and you, too can beat the crap out of losers like Max Cameron!"
Slim Pickens vs Halloween Jack "Back In Black" by AC/DC plays as Halloween Jack makes his way to the ring. He has his TV title belt over his shoulder and takes a few looks back as if he expects Gerard Angelo to charge out after him. That doesn't happen, so Jack hands the belt to the ref and steps through the ropes before posing for the fans. We then hear "Welcome To the Jungle" by Guns N Roses and out comes Slim Pickens with the Canadian title over his head and the fans cheer him on. Slim is even carrying a Canadian flag and gives the thumbs up sign to the camera as he passes by. Slim steps through the ropes and the bell sounds as Jack and Slim stand nose to nose in the center of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Heated verbal exchange here. Henry Lesher - Nothing heated here now that Leech is gone for the night. Slim and Jack start exchanging rights and lefts to the head. Jack eye gouges Slim. Slim responds with a head butt on Jack. Slim picks Jack up and body slams him to the mat. Jack rolls over and mule kicks Slim into the corner, then hip tosses him as he staggers out of it. Jack applies an arm bar, takes an eye gouges from Slim who then reverses the arm bar. Jack hooks the ropes with his leg to force the break. As Slim backs off, Jack lands a forearm to the chin of Slim Pickens and then arm drags him right through the ropes to the floor. Halloween Jack then climbs out of the ring to the floor. Jack whips Slim into the steel guard railing. Slim falls to his knees and grabs at his back as the fans start a chant for the Canadian champion. Jack stands Slim up, whips him towards the steel steps but Slim steps aside of them. Slim then turns and clotheslines Jack to the floor. Slim rolls into the ring to break the count and then rolls right back out.
Ricky Simms - Looks like this battle is being taken to the floor. Henry Lesher - At least they might bleed out here and do something dangerous. Slim reverse headlocks Jack and signals for a DDT, but Jack shoves Slim hard into the steel corner post. Again Slim is left on his knees grasping for his back. Jack rolls himself into the ring to break up the count before rolling back out of the ring as the ref threatens to call this match a no contest. Jack grabs Slim in a headlock and then bulldogs him to the floor. Jack then stands Slim up and rolls him inside the ring before pulling himself inside as well. Jack then goes for the pin on Slim. 1...2... kick out by Slim Pickens. Jack stands Slim up, whips him off the ropes and Slim ducks a clothesline and then gives Jack a spinning neck breaker and goes for the pin himself. 1...2... kick out by Jack at the last possible moment. Slim shakes his head as he stands Jack up, then whips him to the corner and gives him a sidewalk slam out of it. Slim goes for the pin again. 1...2... Jack gets his foot on the bottom rope to break it up. We then see Gerard Angelo making his way to the ring and the crowd boos. Slim Pickens stands Jack up and whips him into the corner buckle.
Ricky Simms - What is Gerard doing out here? Henry Lesher - Looking for revenge? It makes sense to me. Gerard reaches ringside and the lights go out in the arena. We hear a solid thud as the lights stay out. When the lights finally come back up, Jack is out cold inside the ring and the timekeeper at ringside is down and bleeding. The TV title belt is nowhere to be seen. At ringside lies a cinder block busted in half.
Henry Lesher - It was Dante! Dante's trademark is the cinder block! Ricky Simms - Sure it was and he knocked Jack out and took the belt and Gerard has disappeared.
Henry Lesher - Oh ye of little faith! Dante is back! Slim Pickens looks around and goes for the pin on Jack. Neither he nor the ref saw anything that just went on. The ref counts the pin. 1...2...3! Slim Pickens gets the win, but he looks around even as his arm is raised for any further intrusions. He leaves the ring shaking his head, with the Canadian flag held high and the crowd celebrating even as medics attend to Halloween Jack inside the ring.
Ricky Simms - Jack is a mess. Henry Lesher - It has to be Dante! Ricky Simms - Let it rest, doc. Backstage we see Crystal Braun with Dizz. Crystal Braun - Are you ready to referee tonight, Dizzy? *makes Dizz nod his head as the crowd becomes nauseous* Are you going to have fun tonight, Dizzy? *again makes Dizz nod his head* You're gonna call a fair match, right Dizzy? *makes him nod his head a third time*
Chastity enters the screen with a scowl on her face. Chastity - You know, I have been watching this from backstage and I must say it is the most distasteful treatment I have ever seen given to anyone. I find it repulsive.
Hector Braun - *as he enters the shot* Really, Miss Chastity?
