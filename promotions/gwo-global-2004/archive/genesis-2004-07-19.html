GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

CWO Genesis, 19 July 2004

19 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kelowna, BC plays host to the second CWO Genesis show. The lights are brighter and tonight there will be entrance music as the wrestlers prepare themselves for a long night with ten matches in total. At ringside sits Troy Marks at what appears to be a rather crudely set up announcer's table. At his side is backer Hector Braun and they appear ready to broadcast the audio for tonight's show over the internet. This is a major step forward for the CWO.
Troy Marks - Thanks for this chance, sir. Hector Braun - Please, kid, I am simply trying you out for our play by play guy is all. Troy Marks - Well, it's 7 PM and that means the show should be ready to start. Aladdin Sane vs Chester Firefiend "Grandma Got Run Over By A Reindeer" plays as Aladdin Sane makes his way to the ring and the crowd doesn't quite know what to make of him.
Troy Marks - Oh, goody, it's hump boy. You dudes need to learn what a bad gimmick is. Hector Braun - Gimmicks? CWO don't need gimmicks. CWO is laced with talent. Troy Marks - If this isn't a gimmick, it means he really is crazy. That frightens me. Aladdin poses in the corner and then we hear “Two Smart Guys Fight About Michelangelo” by The Dave Brockie Experience and Chester makes his way to the ring. He actually tries to take a seat close to the ring when a fan reminds him that he is actually the wrestler here, not a fan. He swears that isn't the case, but he agrees to enter the ring just because this Aladdin person is creeping him out. The bell sounds and Aladdin goes straight for the leg and in a bad way.
Troy Marks - See now this is wrong. Dude cannot be humping another dude's leg to start the match. That is just wrong.
Hector Braun - This kid makes me laugh. He is staying. Chester shoves Aladdin down, then starts putting the boots to him. Chester then stands Aladdin up, whips him hard into the corner buckle and charges in shoulder first, but Aladdin side steps it and then sunset flips Chester and rolls him up for the pin. 1...2... kick out by a surprised Chester. Aladdin then quickly gets to his feet, spots the nearby turnbuckle and starts bronco busting with no one in the corner to receive the humiliation. Chester gets to his feet, looks around confused and then gives Aladdin a diamond cutter off the second rope and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Chester gets a rather bewildering win here this week.
Troy Marks - So much for hump boy. Hector Braun - Yes, he is funny though. Chester leaves the ring and then someone slides into the ring with a Batman outfit on? He starts verbally berating Aladdin about his outfit and his unworthiness of wearing it.
Troy Marks - That looks like Adam West. Hector Braun - Right.... looks like. The man in the Batman costume drags Aladdin from the ring, still verbally berating him as he drags him to the back.
Troy Marks - Dude, noticed you ain't got a ramp this week. Hector Braun - Just trying things out and seeing what works at this point. That clearly didn't.
Troy Marks - Any attendance numbers, yet? Hector Braun - Presales were over four hundred. I imagine there will be some walk ups, too, so we're doing real good.
Reverend Darius vs Jack Quinn Generic music plays as Jack Quinn makes his way to the ring. Troy Marks - Dude? Is that the UFC music? Hector Braun - I am not going to get into whose music it is or isn't. Jack steps through the ropes and takes a few deep breaths as he looks around at the growing crowd. "Orchids" by Stone Sour plays as Reverend Darius makes his way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. They might simply be booing him because he is a priest and everyone knows they are rarely face wrestlers. Regardless, Darius enters the ring and appears to give last rights to his opponent. Jack is less than impressed and starts throwing punches to the head of the deranged preacher as the bell sounds and the crows sounds as if they are really into it.
Troy Marks - The priest dude looks cool and Jack looks good, too. Where the hell did an indy fed find so many wrestlers?
Hector Braun - I won't get into how we found these people. All I will say is that they were found.
Jack picks Darius up and power slams him to the mat and goes for an early pin. 1...2... kick out by Darius. Jack stands the Reverend up, whips him into the corner and then they both clothesline each other to the mat. Jack shows signs of motion first as he grabs the bottom rope, then the middle rope and pulls himself to his feet. Reverend Darius gets to his feet in the center of the ring and ducks another clothesline. Darius gives Jack a gutwrench suplex and then two handed chokes him until the ref is on the verge of DQing him. Darius then appears the bless Jack and then headbutts him to the mat.
Troy Marks - Seriosuly twisted reverend dude here. Hector Braun - Well, yes he is. It works for us and will make us big news. Darius then rolls Jack out of the ring and follows him out. He attempts to ram Jack's head into the railing. Jack blocks it, elbows Darius in the gut and then rams his head into the guard railing. Jack then picks Darius up and drops him on the guard railing.
Troy Marks - Dude! That looked sick! Hector Braun - Perhaps because there is a sickness among the CWO, a virus growing every day. This virus will take over the wrestling world. We are not stopping with backwater arenas in the middle of nowhere.
Troy Marks - Okay, dude, whatever. The two conbatants continue to battle on the floor and the bell is called for as they are both counted out. The crowd boos the result, but they cheer when Jack and Darius both pick up chairs to swing at each other. Darius ducks the chair shot and then clubs Jack with his chair, knocking Jack hard to the floor. Darius then stands over Jack and appears to bless him again before leaving ringside, bleeding from the head smash on the railing.
Troy Marks - Sick chair shots, man. Spotfesting tonight or what? Hector Braun - We believe in entertaining fans. That means money. Olivia Maine vs Sunshine Mathews "Clint Eastwood" by Gorillaz plays and Sunshine makes her way to the ring with a smile on her face. She high fives a few fans before rolling into the ring. We then hear "Rudy" by Shocore and out comes Olivia Maine which appears to impress the guys in the crowd, the prospect of two girls in a match against each other.
Olivia enters the ring and offers to shake hands with Sunshine. Sunshine looks to the crowd, then down at Olivia's outstretched hand. They shake hands and circle each other as the match starts. They lock up and the crowd cheers as Sunshine whips Olivia off the ropes and then shoulder blocks her to the mat. Sunshine then stands Olivia up, whips her into the corner and then cartwheels into the corner, wraps her legs around the neck of Olivia and flips her down to the mat. As Sunshine sets to stands Olivia up again, the lights in the gymnasium go out and blue stroke lights go off. We then hear Dio playing and out comes a gigantic man with a woman leading him to the ring.
Troy Marks - What the hell is that? Hector Braun - That is Evil Eye. Troy Marks - He's breaking up a match between these two chicks? He ain't winning me over here, dude.
Hector Braun - Must you speak like a teenage Hulk Hogan wanna be? I am seeking refinement in my play by play guy. You may not be the guy.
Troy Marks - Sorry, man, I'll try to stop it, man. Evil Eye steps over the top rope and Sunshine and Olivia roll themselves out of the ring. Evil Eye grabs a microphone from the ring announcer.
Evil Eye - All of this frill and sweet nothings shall end here. I shall curse everyone until I am allowed to rest.
Evil Eye then makes his way out of the ring and Sunshine and Olivia re-enter. Olivia is distracted with Evil Eye's exit with Diva Blue and Sunshine rolls her up for the pin. 1...2...3! Sunshine gets herself the win here mostly thanks to Evil Eye's distraction. Sunshine leaves the ring and shrugs when Olivia gets to her feet, not appearing to be impressed. Olivia then leaves the ring, chasing Sunshine to the back. This feud is just getting started.
Troy Marks - Now that sucks. I was looking forward to some indy chicks getting down and dirty in the ring!
Hector Braun - It is my opinion that we need to appeal to the lowest common denomenator which, in this case, would be you.
Troy Marks - And I thank you for that. Virgin Kitties vs Regulators "Girls Girls Girls" by Motley Crue plays as the Virgin Kitties make their way to the ring and the crowd likes the looks of them as they kiss each other in the middle of the ring.
Troy Marks - CWO may not have a TV contract, but they have lesbians. Hector Braun - Lowest common denomenator. As the Kitties celebrate their love in the ring, we hear "Regulator" by Warren G and Nate Dogg and out comes the Regulators. They get a more mixed reaction from the crowd. Devin Blade enters the ring while Banks takes his place on the apron. The bell sounds and Summer locks up with Devin. Devin quickly pushes her into the corner and Summer tags in Winter. Winter enters the ring and she delivers some kicks to the chest of Devin which drives him to a neutral corner. Winter then monkey flips Devin to the middle of the ring. Winter goes for a small package on Devin. 1...2... kick out by Devin Blade.
Troy Marks - Lesbian chicks can fight. Hector Braun - Yes, these girls can fight or they wouldn't be here. Winter stands Devin up, whips him off the ropes and Devin holds onto the ropes as Winter dropkicks air. Devin tags in his partner and Banks climbs to the top rope. Banks launches himself for a moonsault off the top and lands on Winter! The crowd cheers really loud for that amazing move from the big man who then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Regulators pick up the win!
Tony Marks - Wow! Way to crush that lesbian! From backstage we see Devo and Soul Reaver charging into the ring. The Regulators meet them in the middle and the two teams start brawling. Banks tosses Devo over the top rope. Soul Reaver throws Devin Blade over the top. All four men are then fighting outside the ring. The crowd cheers as the fight heads to the backstage area.
Tony Marks - When do you guys set up tag titles for these guys to beat each other with? Hector Braun - We are in the process of getting our belts. Tonight will give us some serious cash and then we'll be able to think about gaining ourselves some belts and other perks.
Inside the ring, Shelley Morris announces the attendance at 632 for tonight's event which more than doubles last week's event. This news brings a smile to Hector's face.
Hector Braun - Yes, we are making some money tonight. Tony Marks - Do I get paid tonight? Hector Braun - No, you don't. Tony Marks - Yeah, whatever... Tex Murrphy vs Tigre Blanco Standard Mexican music plays as Tigre Blanco makes his way to the ring. He receives a strong reception from the crowd as he makes his way into the ring. He steps through the ropes and looks around, nodding his head as if to say that he is ready for this match. "Pink Cigarette" by Mr. Bungle plays as Tex Murrphy makes his way to the ring, handing out pictures of the missing Sarah Creed. Tex then removes his trench coat and places it at ringside next to the timekeepers table. The CWO has a timekeeper? Apparently they do!
Tony Marks - Wwird detective guy here against the Mexican dude. Hector Braun - Truly expert analysis. The bell rings and Tigre opens with an insiguri on Tex and then goes for the very early pin. 1...2... kick out by Tex Murrphy. Tigre stands Tex up, whips him off the ropes and Tex ducks a clothesline, then goes behind Tigre and delivers a German suplex on Tigre Blanco. Tex then stands Tigre up and delivers a second German suplex and holds him there for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tigre Blanco.
Tony Marks - Well, this match looks fast so far. Hector Braun - Of course it is. This is the CWO, home of the stars. The GWO will dominate the wrestling world!
Tony Marks - Yo, dude, it's the CWO remember? What's the GWO? Hector Braun - *coughs* Of course. My mistake. Tex places Tigre Blanco into a sleeperhold. The crowd is starting a chant for Tex? We have to suspect at this point there are some in the crowd who witnessed the show last week in Penticton. Tigre's movements slow down somewhat and the ref starts lifting his arm. It drops once. He lifts the arm and it drops a second time. He lifts the arm, but Tigre Blanco manages to keep his arm up to keep the match going. Tigre Blanco then fights back to his feet and elbows Tex in the gut four times to break free of the hold. Tigre Blanco then shoves Tex into the corner and gives him a Russian leg sweep as he comes back out of it.
Tony Marks - Nice move. Hector Braun - Yes, all of my wrestlers have nice moves and such. That is why I pay for it.
Tigre heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He then delivers a missile dropkick on Tex from the top rope. Tigre Blanco then does a lionsault onto the chest of Tex and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tex Murrphy. Tigre Blanco climbs to the top rope, launches himself for a moonsault which misses when Tex rolls out of the way. Tex then quicklym pulls himself to his feet and spots someone in the crowd. His eyes light up and he races out through the ropes and into the crowd as a girl runs for the front door.
Tony Marks - Where the hell is he headed? Hector Braun - It would appear that Tex found his girl. Tigre Blanco gets to his feet and looks around for his opponent as the ref counts Tex out. Tigre Blanco's arm is raised, but Blanco shakes his head the whole time before letting himself out of the ring. Tigre Blanco heads into the back as the crowd murmurs about someone coming in and taking a seat in the back.
Tony Marks - Is that Max Cameron? Hector Braun - No telling who might show up at a CWO event. Tony Marks - No kidding, man. Johnny K-9 vs Xtasy "X Gonna Give It To Ya" by DMX plays and Xtasy makes his way to the ring as the fans cheer him on. Xtasy enters the ring and poses in each corner with his arms raised and tells the fans he will be the first CWO champion. He then jumps off the ropes just as Johnny K-9 enters the ring and attacks Xtasy from behind, quickly throwing him through the ropes to the outside of the ring as Shelley Morris announces that this is a no DQ match. The crowd cheers loudly as Johnny lands repeated double axe handle smashes to the back of Xtasy. Johnny then tries to whip Xtasy into the ring steps, but Xtasy stops his forward progress and runs Johnny head first into the corner post. Xtasy then dropkicks him into the guard railing.
Tony Banks - Hardcore match! This is a nice surprise! Hector Braun - Well, these two clearly hate each other so this makes sense. Xtasy eye rakes Johnny K-9, then rolls him into the ring before rolling himself into the ring as well.Xtasy then places Johnny K-9 into a Boston Crab. Johnny reaches for the ropes as the crowd appears to be somewhat divided on who to cheer for. Xtasy tries to lock the hold in tighter as Johnny continues to reach for the ropes and refuses to submit here. Johnny lunges, pushes himself up from the mat and manages to grab the rope on his next effort, forcing the break. Xtasy is reluctant to release the hold, but he does release it when told to by the ref. He then starts putting the boots to Johnny before he has a chance to get up. Johnny rolls himself out of the ring. Xtasy rolls out after him. Johnny starts running and Xtasy runs right after him. Johnny slides into the ring, quickly gets to his feet and then elbows Xtasy in the back of the neck as he slides into the ring as well. Johnny then places Xtasy into an STF.
Tony Marks - Xtasy was stupid to follow him out there. Hector Braun - Or Johnny was brilliant to bait him into it. It goes both ways here. Johnny holds the STF perfectly, but Xtasy manages to snag the ropes with his foot. The break is forced and Johnny comes off the ropes and knees Xtasy in the face which sends him awkwardly out of the ring. Johnny K-9 then gets to the top rope and waits for Xtasy to get up. Xtasy gets to his feet very slowly, favoring the leg he landed on outside the ring. Johnny leaps off and lands a flying body press on Xtasy on the floor. He then rolls him into the ring as Xtasy is grabbing at his knee. Johnny K-9 drags Xtasy to the middle of the ring and places him in a scorpion deathlock, knowing full well that Xtasy's knee is bothering him. Xtasy screams in pain as the ref asks him if he submits.
Tony Marks - Johnny is a smart wrestler. Hector Braun - I don't hire idiots. Xtasy reaches for the ropes, but is forced to tap out. The bell sounds and Johnny holds onto the legs of Xtasy for a few moments longer before he finally releases him. Johnny then gives Xtasy a couple of kicks in the head before he leaves the ring with some in the crowd loving what they just witnessed, others sounding quite upset with his treatment of their hero Xtasy.
Tony Marks - Are they done with each other? Hector Braun - Of course not. Feuds never end in this ring. They simply get put off for a few weeks due to injury.
Xtasy rolls out of the ring and limps his way to the back with the help of the ref. The crowd cheers him for his effort tonight, no matter what the result is.
Devo vs Kosmic Kitty "Scandalous" by Mis-Teeq plays as Kosmic Kitty makes her way to the ring. Everyone appears to be willing to cheer this blonde bombshell on tonight. She even signs an autograph for a fan at ringside before she makes her way up the steps and then enters the ring. Air Raid Sirens scream through the small gym as Devo then makes his way to the ring which draws the ire of the crowd. He gives one fan the finger and then looks into the ring, spots his prey and races the rest of the way into the ring as the bell sounds. Devo lands a series of knife edge chops on Kosmic which drives her back into the corner as the fans boo louder. Devo hip tosses her to the middle of the ring and then places her in a front chinlock and asks her how she likes it. He tells the fans that he hates them all as Kosmic struggles to free herself. She rakes her fingernails down the back of Devos calf and Devo lets her go in shock, then looks at the scratch marks.
Tony Marks - That's cool, a chick who uses her nails. Hector Braun - Against that guy, she had better use more than that. Yes, Devo is one of my personal favorites. Pure hate, no heart, no feelings. That is a man who has champion written all over him.
Tony Marks - You're the boss. Devo stands Kitty up, whips her off the ropes. Kitty ducks a clothesline and then gives Devo a spinning neckbreaker in desperation and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Devo. Kitty then leg drops Devo across the throat. From the mat, Devo grabs Kitty's neck and starts slowly getting to his feet. He picks her up and choke slams her to the mat. She is seen choking for air as Devo stands over her. He grabs her around the neck again. He stands her up with a wild look in his eyes, ready to choke slam her again. Before he has a chance to, Radd Matthews is in the ring and attacks Devo from behind. The ref calls for the bell to DQ Kitty.
Hector Braun - No, no, no. Radd is not playing the hero tonight. Hector gets up from his position at the makeshift announce table and heads to the ring and has a chat with the ref as Shelley Morris announces the DQ. The ref then relays Hector's message and passes it on to Shelley Morris who then announces this will now be a tag team match even as Soul Reaver appears from the back.
Tony Marks - Whoa, cool! Tag match! Radd whips Devo off the ropes as the tag match is now underway. Radd clotheslines Devo to the mat and then knee drops him to the forehead before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Devo. Radd stands Devo up as we see Kosmic on the apron trying to get her breath back. Radd whips Devo into the corner and then shoulder blocks him three times in the corner before Devo ends that with a knee lift to the face of Radd. Devo then headbitts Radd and pulls him to his corner where he tags in Soul Reaver.
Tony Marks - That dude is nasty. Hector Braun - I hope this lesson teaches Radd to not play hero. Kitty was losing fair and square. He should have stayed out of it.
Soul Reaver picks Radd up and power slams him to the mat. He then gives him an elbow smash and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Radd. Soul Reaver then stands Radd up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Soul Reaver then climbs the ropes in the corner and lands a flying elbow smash to the chest of Radd and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Radd Matthews as Kosmic applauds on the apron. Soul Reaver stands Radd up, whips him into the corner and charges in for the spear which Radd sidesteps and Soul Reaver then nails his shoulder into the corner buckle. Radd then tags in Kosmic. Kosmic quickly jumps to the top rope, waits for Soul Reaver to turns to her and lands a flying head scissors on Reaver from there which brings him down to the mat. Kitty then rolls him into a small package for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Soul Reaver which throws Kitty off with authority. She applies a sleeper, but Reaver thinks fast and snap mares her into his corner where Devo kicks Kitty in the face.
Tony Marks - These two dudes are sickos. Hector Braun - They are ministers of hatred. They live exactly as they claim to. Admire them.
Soul Reaver tags Devo in. Soul Reaver holds Kitty open for a series of punches to the ribs by Devo. Devo then places her in an abdominal stretch in the middle of the ring, but it doesn't last as Radd charges into the ring and clotheslines Devo to the mat. Soul Reaver enters the ring and starts brawling with Radd as it becomes more obvious that the ref has no control here. Devo picks Kitty up. Kitty shifts her weight, hits the top rope, headlocks Devo and then drops to the mat, delivering a bulldog on him. She goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Devo. Kitty stands Devo up. She whips him into the corner. She then takes a raised boot to the chin from Devo who then climbs to the second rope and clotheslines her to the mat from there as Radd and Soul Reaver grapple and both fall over the top rope to the floor. Devo goes for the pin on Kitty. 1...2...3! Devo and Soul Reaver win another one!
Tony Marks - These two were overmatched. Hector Braun - Like I said, they are learning not to play the hero. Devo stands Kitty up as Reaver rams Radd's head into the steel guard railing. Reaver enters the ring as Devo holds Kitty by the throat. Reaver has a few words for her and he grabs her by the throat as well, a double choke slam coming perhaps? Before they have a chance to execute the move and perhaps end the career of Kosmic Kitty, Wyatt Gibson and Devin Blade race into the ring. Outside the ring, Radd grabs a steel folding chair. Devo and Reaver leave the ring and Reaver takes a chair shot to the face from Radd as he round the corner of the ring. Radd then throws the chair down on Reaver before entering the ring. Devo pulls Reaver away while shouting at the men inside the ring that next week, they will all pay.
Tony Marks - I didn't see that coming. Hector Braun - Look at all those heroes in that ring, or misguided fools as I call them. Tony Marks - Nice start for a feud, though. Hector Braun - This is more than a simple feud. This is all about hate. This is about finding out who the men are and who the boys are here.
Wyatt Gibson vs Dirk Bailey "I'm Interested In Apathy" by TISM begins to play as Dirk Bailey makes his way to the ring. Radd Matthews and Devin Blade leave the ring and head to the back, leaving Wyatt there not looking very confortable.
Tony Marks - This match was supposed to be last. Hector Braun - I changed my mind. I do that sometimes. Dirk steps onto the apron as Wyatt charges at him, shouldering him in the side which knocks him off the apron to the floor. Wyatt then climbs to the top rope, then lands a scissor kick to the back of Dirk's neck from there and the fans cheer in appreciation. Wyatt then rams Dirk's head into the makeshift announce table. He follows this up by rolling Dirk into the ring and says something nasty Hector's way.
Hector Braun - He needs to keep his mouth shut. Tony Marks - Getting a little tense down here. Wyatt enters the ring, stands Dirk up, whips him off the ropes and Dirk shoulder blocks Wyatt to the mat. He then splashes him and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Wyatt Gibson. Dirk then stands Wyatt up and snap mares him into the corner. He stands Wyatt up again, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Dirk then leg drops Wyatt and again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Wyatt Gibson. Dirk stands Wyatt up again, whips him off the ropes and Wyatt ducks a bog boot from Dirk, then lands a springboard moonsault on Dirk finally bringing him down here. Wyatt then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top.
Tony Marks - This could be a mistake. Hector Braun - The man has made many mistakes tonight. Why stop now? Wyatt lanunches himself off the top and lands a flying headbutt on Dirk Bailey and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dirk Bailey. Wyatt shakes his head as he pulls Dirk to his feet and then whips him to the corner. Dirk knee lifts Wyatt to the chest from the corner, gives him an elbow to the forehead and then headbutts him to the mat. Dirk follows this up by standing Wyatt up, picking him up and delivering a tombstone piledriver on him as the crowd oohs and ahhs over that. He goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Dirk Bailey gets the win and Wyatt isn't moving.
Hector Braun - See, now Wyatt didn't do the right thing here tonight. He didn't do as I asked and thus I had Dirk punish him.
Tony Marks - What are you talking about? Hector Braun - What I am talking about is for me to know. Wyatt does roll out of the ring moments after Dirk has left apopearing quite satisfied with the job he did. On his way back, Dirk is met by a woman we haven't seen in the CWO before. He has a few words with her about busy schedules and what not. That will probably develop in the coming weeks. Wyatt limps his way to the back for the second week in a row and one has to wonder if this place is shortening his wrestling career by years at a time.
Slim Pickens vs Zack McCracken "Beautiful Stranger" by Madonna plays as Felicity leads Zack McCracken to the ring. Zack raises an eyebrow at someone in the crowd as we see Slim Pickens come out from the back shaking his head. He hasn't been impressed with Zack in the least. Slim clotheslines Zack to the floor from behind, then grabs him by the hair and rolls him into the ring.
Tony Marks - How did James Bond wind up in the main event? Hector Braun - There were issues backstage with him berating an audio guy. Turns out Zack thinks he stole the works of Mozart in Prague twenty years ago.
Tony Marks - It is just a gimmick, right? He doesn't really believe this stuff, does he? Hector Braun - I am not at liberty to discuss what one person might or might not be when the cameras are off and fans are not around.
Slim enters the ring and the bell sounds to get the match started. Slim picks Zack up and slams him face first into the turnbuckle. Slim then rolls Zack over and goes for the pin. 1...2... Zack gets his foot on the bottom rope. Slim stands Zack up and whips him into the corner buckle. Slim then gives him a sidewalk slam out of the corner and again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Zack McCracken. Slim again stands Zack up, whips him off the ropes and then spears him to the mat. Once again, Slim goes for the pin. 1...2... Zack again kicks out.
Tony Marks - Zack showing he's easy to beat up, hard to beat for three. Hector Braun - Zack's an interesting person. Slim again stands Zack up, then lands a series of knife edge chops to the chest of Zack. Zack responds with a knee to the gut of Slim, then thumbs him in the eye. Zack then places Slim in a comedic looking sleeperhold. Slim falls to the ropes and the break is forced which doesn't go over well with Zack. Slim then gives Zack a spinebuster to the mat and smiles. He has a few things to say to Felicity who shakes her head. Slim heads to the second rope and poses for the fans before dropping the Slim Proposition on Zack McCracken from there and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Slim Pickens is your winner!
Tony Marks - Nice belly flop. Tough dude! Hector Braun - Zack needs to work on his talent a little bit. Tony Marks - Well, I am gonna run out for autographs. Look forward to hearing from you on whether or not I get the job.
Hector Braun - My people will call you. Tony Marks - Sweet man! --------------------------------- Backstage notes: - Wyatt was hobbling badly backstage and rumor has it he may have hurt his ankle. - Word has it that Tex didn't catch the girl he was after. - Radd and Kosmic Kitty are quite chummy backstage. - Max Cameron came backstage to meet the wrestlers and wished them all well. - Rumor has it that Shelia Lavin was in the crowd in some disguise. - Thunder was backstage learning the trade and apparently ready to go next week. - Evil Eye is keeping to himself and not trying to fit in. - That was the real Adam West. - The Virgin Kitties were nowhere to be found after the show and word has it they are off contract.
- Wyatt Gibson, Devin Blade, and Radd Matthews are all anxious to work together next week as is Reverend Darius with Soul Reaver and Devo. This is being seen as a great match to highlight the level of talent in the CWO.
Tune in for more next week!!
