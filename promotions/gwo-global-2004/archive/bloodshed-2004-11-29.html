GWO Bloodshed, 29 November 2004

29 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens backstage with Shannon entering the arena. He is headed off by Noah, his son.
Noah Skanes - Where's Mom? Shannon Skanes - She's parking the car. We then hear the squeal of tires followed by a crashing noise and Noah and Shannon race out the door. We then hear "Are You Ready?" by AC/DC and we see the opening credits of tonight's huge GWO event. We see images of Youthful Aggression minus Slim Pickens, of Lethal Weapon with Stevie James, The Dying Breed, Myst, G2, Kumquat Kid, Leech and GoBoom before we head to ringside where Henry Lesher is tonight joined by Casey Braun. The crowd is already cheering as we are all set for action.
Casey Braun - That crashing noise didn't sound good. Henry Lesher - No, it looks bad since Myst was driving and we know how women are behind the wheel of a car.
Casey Braun - Ghoul. We then go backstage and see Crystal Braun with Max Cameron. Crystal Braun - Max, we have heard Slim Pickens left Youthful Aggression. Max Cameron - Slim left us, yeah. If it was someone important, I would be upset. Crystal Braun - And there has been speculation that Pyro would rejoin the group. Max Cameron - Don't put any stock in that Crystal. What matters tonight is that I am going to retain my world title. I won it fair and square and I am going to keep it in the same manner.
Crystal Braun - Any of the four men worry you the most? Max Cameron - No, but I look forward to pinning Leech's sorry ass to the mat. Max leaves as Crystal nods. We then head back to ringside. "Take That Nancy Kerrigan" by Lenore plays as Pyro comes out to a rather mixed reaction. There's never really any way to know whether or not Pyro is on the side of the good or the evil. He shows no emotion as he makes his way to the ring, looking like he means business tonight in our first match to lead off Bloodshed. He steps through the ropes and has a few words with the ref, nothing heated, as "Beautiful Atranger" by Madonna starts playing and the Victoria crowd gets to their feet to cheer on Zack McCracken. He appears taken back by the ovation as Felicity walks him to the ring and he high fives a few fans on his way to the ring.
Henry Lesher - What makes this worthy of a match people pay to watch? Casey Braun - Fans love Zack and there's never any way to know what Pyro might do! Henry Lesher - You were a cheerleader in high school. Casey Braun - How'd you know? Henry Lesher - Lucky guess. Zack steps through the ropes and the bell sounds. Zack offers to shake hands with Pyro who simple slaps Zack's hand away and then they circle each other and lock up. Pyro shoves Zack into the corner, then knees him in the gut and then follows that up with a vertical suplex back to the middle of the ring. Pyro then heads to the ropes, makes his way to the second rope and then lands a double axe handle to the back on Zack McCracken who has started slower than he intended to. Pyro then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Zack. Felicity applauds at ringside as Pyro stands Zack up, then whips him off the ropes. Zack ducks a clothesline and then kicks Pyro in the ribs on one side, then the other and then knife edge chops Pyro in the throat and then headbutts him back into the ropes. Zack then snaps mares Pyro to the center of the ring where he slaps on a sleeper hold as the fans cheer Zack on. After months of wrestling, he is finally starting to show some real skill inside the ring. Pyro elbows Zack in the ribs, then again and then a third time to force the break.
Casey Braun - Zack almost had him, surprise to us. Henry Lesher - Not to me. They both suck. Pyro almost had my support at one point, but he blew it as usual.
Pyro turns and gives Zack a jawbreaker. He then picks Zack up and power slams him to the mat. Pyro then heads out onto the apron and then slingshots himself over the top rope and legdrops Zack across the throat. Pyro then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top as Felicity pleads for Zack to get up. Pyro then lands The Burn Out on Zack and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Pyro defeats Zack to open the show tonight. The lights then start to flicker and we see Rated R and Gerard Angelo coming towards the ring. Pyro stands and looks back at them, not backing down even a bit. From the crowd comes Max Cameron with a chair in hand. He slides into the ring behind Pyro and then clubs him over the back of the head with it. Felicity, meanwhile, is trying to roll Zack out of the ring when Max turns and sees her there and smiles. He wields the chair and is ready to strike when Carl Simms pulls her and Zack both from the ring. Felicity then hugs Carl who stares into the ring at Max.
Henry Lesher - We have enough soap operas here. What the hell good does this do? Casey Braun - People have to explain their life choices to you? Henry Lesher - I expect everything done the way I like it. Casey Braun - Must be why you live alone. Backstage we see Blake Young with Kelly Buchanan. Kelly Buchanan - Blake, last week you had a setback against Sal Clemenzo. This week you have Scavenger, a man Sal has called out. How do you feel about tonight?
Blake Young - I am the best in the business, Kelly. Tonight I show you all what I learned from last week's setback. I learn, move on and get better. Scavenger doesn't have a chance.
Kelly Buchanan - Anyone in particular you're targetting? Blake Young - Cowards. Bring me the cowards that need to be taught a lesson or two. I can be a great motivator.
Blake then turns and leaves Kelly there to turn to the camera and shrug. We're set for our second match of the night.
"Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays as Thunder is led to the ring by Marla Storme and Devanshi. The crowd actually appears to be on Thunder's side tonight, cheering either him or the two ladies leading him to the ring. Thunder reaches ringside with his usual stone look and looks up at the ring and nods as he walks up the steps and then steps over the top rope into the ring. Thunder then tests the ropes as we hear "Black #1" and out comes Holmes fresh off a win in his GWO debut last week. Holmes doesn't get a very good reaction from the crowd, but booing doesn't faze this man as he approaches his opponent.
Henry Lesher - Holmes has potential. I will admit that, but so many potential Leeches turn into Pyros.
Casey Braun - Hey, Pyro did win tonight! Henry Lesher - Against the GWO's Brooklyn Brawler. Holmes steps into the ring and the bell sounds. The two men lock up in he middle of the ring. Thunder breaks it with a knee in the gut on Holmes before he hip tosses Holmes into the corner buckle. Thunder then picks Holmes up and body slams him to the mat and then uses the ropes for leverage as he boot chokes Holmes. The ref demands the break and Thunder obliges only on the four count just before the ref would have called for the DQ. Thunder then takes Holmes by the hair and pulls him up to his feet. Holmes thumbs Thunder in the eye, then eye gouges him along the top rope into the corner where he slams Thunder's head into the corner buckle. Holmes then lands a series of knife edge chops to the chest of Thunder that drive him to the center of the ring. He then manages a spine buster on him and goes for the pin. 1...2.. Thunder rolls a shoulder out in time to keep this match going. Holmes then stands Thunder up, whips him off the ropes and then both men clothesline each other to the mat. Outside the ring, Marla and Devanshi give Thunder support in the way of cheers and applause with some fans cheering along with them. Thunder shows signs of motion at the four count and grabs the ropes to start pulling himself to his feet. On the six count, we see motion from Holmes and he grabs the ropes on the same side of the ring as Thunder. Thunder knees Holmes in the gut, then places his throat over the middle rope and chokes him on it.
Henry Lesher - I hope guys in the back are watching this. Learn from the two brawlers out here trying to hurt each other. No pretty and dainty wrestling here.
Casey Braun - Then go announce UFC. Henry Lesher - I would, but having seen Tank Abbott once was enough to scar me for life. I am frightened of the ugly and the stupid that would compete there and having to speak to them.
Casey Braun - You just like to hear yourself talk. Henry Lesher - Everyone else likes to hear me talk, too. Thunder picks Holmes up over his shoulder and runs towards the opposite buckle, but Holmes slides off Thunder's shoulder, then shoves him into the corner buckle and then gives him a German suplex out of it and then holds him for the cradle pin attempt. 1...2... Thunder rolls out of it. Holmes then quickly gets to his feet and starts putting the boots to Thunder before he has a chance to get all the way to his feet. Thunder rolls himself out of the ring. Holmes follows and comes face to face with Devanshi. He has a few words with her. Holmes takes a swing at her which she ducks, then Thunder spears Holmes to the floor. Thunder then stands over Holmes and shakes his head once before grabbing Holmes by the hair and then rolling him into the ring. Thunder then rolls himself into the ring and looks to the cheering crowd. Holmes slowly gets to his feet and Thunder catches him with The Dugstown and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Thunder pulls off the win, giving Holmes his first setback in his GWO career.
Casey Braun - Tough break for Holmes, but impressive match for Thunder. Henry Lesher - Brawling is fun to watch. Backstage we see Kumquat Kid with Ray Dees. Ray Dees - Kumquat, last week you showed you could hang with the best of them against Austin Fierro. This week, if you beat Gerard, you get a shot at some gold in the GWO.
Kumquat Kid - I'm glad you mention that. See, I came here to be a champion and tonight I take a step towards that while showing everyone just how powerful the kumquat can be.
Ray Dees - And what do you say to people who compare your kumquats with Kitty's twinkies? Kumquat Kid - Yuck, Ray, kumquat flavored twinkies? You're sick! Ray Dees - No, I wasn't meaning that. Kumquat Kid - You've scarred me and the mighty kumquats. Hope you're happy. Kumquat walks away and Ray looks lost. We're certain a sleeze like Ray wanted another assignment, this not really being up his alley at all. Mercifully, we head back to the ring.
"Rumors" by Lindsay Lohan plays as Kosmic Kitty makes her way out to the ring, tossing the usual pastry treats to the fans who cheer her loudly as usual. Once she is out of her Twinkie supply, she hands her basket to a young girl in the front row and gives her a kiss on the cheek before she makes her way up the steps and into the ring. She then grabs a microphone from announcer Shelley Morris.
Kosmic Kitty - Hi there fans! Last week, Stevie came to the ring and declared his love for me. I told him how hard it was for me to deal with because of Radd. Well, I did a lot of thinking this week and I have decided that I love Stevie too! *crowd cheers* So Stevie, if you...
She doesn't have a chance to finish as "Destroy All" by Static X plays and out comes Steve Stryker with a scowl on his face as the fans boo him the whole way to the ring. He steps through the ropes and Kitty backs off a bit as Steve tells her the soap opera is over, that it's time to wrestle. The bell sounds and Steve lunges at Kitty only to have her shoulder roll out of the way. He turns towards her and again lunges at her. Again Kitty shoulder rolls out of the way as the fans start a chant for her.
Henry Lesher - Thank God Stryker put an end to that crap! Casey Braun - She was trying to be romantic and sweet. Henry Lesher - Thanks God Stryker put an end to it. Didn't you hear me the first time? Steve catches Kitty on the next shoulder roll, putting his foot out to break her roll and then Steve grabs her by the hair and pulls her to her feet. Steve then shoves her into the corner and kicks her in the ribs, then snap mares her to the middle of the ring where he dropkicks her in the back and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kosmic Kitty. Steve then stands her up, whips her off the ropes and misses with a clothesline as Kitty ducks it. She then does a hand stand into a headscissors lock on Steve Stryker, then takes him to the ground. Kitty then quickly does a springboard moonsault onto Steve Stryker and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Steve Stryker. Kosmic Kitty then stands Steve up, whips him into the corner and then tries for a monkey flip on Steve, but he shoves her off and then Steve boot chokes her, using the ropes for leverage here as the fans boo him. Steve has a few unkind words for them as he stands Kitty up, whips her off the ropes and then backdrops her to the mat. Steve then heads for the ropes and goes to the top. He leaps off for a frog splash only to find no one home as Kitty rolls out of the way.
Casey Braun - Steve was close to finishing this one. If he had hit that move, that probably would have done it.
Henry Lesher - Yes and I could have been happy. Kosmic Kitty rolls herself right out of the ring as she tries to get herself together here. Steve Stryker rolls out of the ring after her, grabs her by the hair and rams her face into the guard railing. Steve then has a few words with a fan at ringside before he whips Kitty shoulder first into the steel ring steps. Steve then rolls Kitty into the ring and then pulls himself onto the apron and into the ring, beating the count by the ref. Steve then goes for the pin on Kitty. 1...2... Kitty gets a foot on the bottom rope. Steve stands her up, then hip tosses her over the top rope to the floor. Stryker then heads to the top rope with a smile on his face. However, as he readies to fly down onto his prey, we see Stevie James rushing around the ring and he blocks Kitty from Steve Stryker's attack. Stryker shouts at Stevie to move and then decides to gives Stevie a flying body press. He lands on Stevie who catches Stryker and gives him a spinning power slam to the floor as the ref calls for the bell.
Casey Braun - Stevie got Kitty disqualified. Henry Lesher - Stevie saved that idiot from a beheading. Stevie stands Stryker up and the two start trading rights and lefts as we see security coming to ringside to try and break this fracus up. We then head backstage to Kate Redman set to interview Radd Matthews.
Kate Redman - Radd, your ex-girlfriend just lost thanks to Stevie James. Bring back any memories?
Radd Matthews - It reminds me of how much more successful I have been since getting rid of her. Tonight that culminates with me getting my title back.
Kate Redman - And if Kitty tries to get involved? Radd Matthews - Then she'll learn quickly why that's a very bad idea. I will do whatever it takes to get my title back, Kate.
Kate Redman - Impressive from this Dying Breed representative at Bllodshed. Back to Henry and Casey.
"Walk" by Pantera plays as Rod Gordon comes out from the back to a mixed raction. He does high five a few fans on his way to the ring where he slides under the bottom rope and then perches himself on the second rope and poses for the fans as flashbulbs go off. We then hear "Who Let the Dawgs Out?" by BAHA Men and The Dawg comes out to largly a positive reaction from this capacity crowd tonight. The Dawg looks into the ring at Rod Gordon and has a few words for him before he steps up onto the apron.
Henry Lesher - Epic battle here between the stupid and the ugly. Casey Braun - I wonder what would happen if you went into the ring and said that. Henry Lesher - Don't think too much there, Casey, or you'll need a nap. The Dawg steps through the ropes and the bell sounds. Rod steps towards Dawg and has a few words for him, poking him in the chest. Dawg looks down at the finger of Rod Gordon, then slowly looks up to look Rod in the eye, then grabs his hand and bends his wrist back, causing Rod great pain. Dawg has a few nasty words for Rod as Rod dances with his wrist twisted back, almost to the point of a break. Rod then thumbs Dawg in the eye to break the painful hold. Rod then shakes his wrist a few times to try and ease the pain and get the circulation going into it. Rod kicks Dawg in the gut a few times, then whips him off the ropes and takes a shoulder block from Dawg that sends him flying. Dawg then picks Rod up and body slams him to the mat. He leg drops Rod across the throat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rod Gordon. Dawg then pulls Rod Gordon into the corner, then climbs to the second rope and launches himself for a splash only to find Rod moved out of the way in time and Dawg winds himself when he lands on the mat. Rod Gordon then uses the ropes to quickly get to his feet, then places Dawg into a sleeperhold. The Dawg quickly gets to his feet and backs Rod into the corner buckle, avalanching him and breaking the hold at the same time. Dawg then smashes the back of his head into the forehead of Rod. Dawg steps out of the corner and Rod staggers out of it, then falls face first to the mat.
Casey Braun - Good strategy for Dawg. Henry Lesher - Yes, isn't it great he's so fat? I mean, it makes him a great wrestler. Thank God Leech will be out here later.
The Dawg stands Rod up, sets him for and gives him a vertical suplex to the mat. He then stands him up and then kicks him in the gut and delivers a gutwrench suplex to the mat and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rod Gordon. Dawg shakes his head, certain that should have been a three count, then stands Rod up and whips him off the ropes. Rod ducks a clothesline and then hip tosses Dawg over the top rope to the floor. Dawg lands hard on his knee on the floor. Rod heads out, smelling blood and places Dawg into a figure four leglock on the floor, even as the ref counts towards a possible count out. Rod releases the hold, rolls into the ring to break up the count and then rolls right back out. He stands Dawg up, then picks him up and atomic drop him onto the ground, one knee hitting the steel ring steps. Rod then rolls Dawg into the ring as he screams in pain and grabs at his knee. Rod then jumps to the top rope and then lands the Shock Drop on The Dawg and then hooks the leg for the pin. 1...2...3! Rod Gordon gets the win here tonight and holds his hands high as he rolls out of the ring.
Casey Braun - Good showing for Rod Gordon here. Henry Lesher - The guy finally looks like an enforcer. It's about bloody time. Good to see fat boy looks injured in there.
Casey Braun - Dawg's knee was hurt and Rod took advantage of it. Rod Gordon leaves with his arms raised as the crowd appears nearly won over by that performance tonight. As he leaves and EMTs come out to attend to The Dawg, we head backstage and see Austin Fierro's manager Deacon Raymore heading into the building. He is headed off by Rated R who slams a crow bar into the ribs of Deacon, then DDTs him on the floor. Rated R then looks down on the out cold and bloodied manager of Austin Fierro and smiles. He can even be heard laughing as he walks away from the carnage he just caused. That done, we look outside where a smashed up car is and an ambulance is nearby as Kelly Buchanan tries to find out what was going on.
Kelly Buchanan - Did anyone see what happened? Fan - Yeah Kelly! It was insane! Myst was parking the car when it was broadsided! Dude that was driving that car took off and fast!
Paramedics push past Kelly with Mys on a stretcher. She tries to get Shannon's attention as he and Noah attend to her while letting the paramedics to their job.
Kelly Buchanan - Is she awake? Shannon Skanes - Now isn't the time Kelly. Kelly Buchanan - Who was driving the car? Noah Skanes - Kelly, my father is being nice. Keep asking questions and I won't be as nice.
Kelly backs off as Myst is wheeled into the ambulance the doors are quickly shut behind her with Shannon and Noah in the ambulance as well. The siren wails as the ambulance speeds off for Victoria General Hospital.
Casey Braun - Our prayers are with Myst and her family. Henry Lesher - Don't assume things for me. With that family gone for the night, this should make for a great night.
Casey Braun - From what I hear, they had to be careful of Myst's neck when they removed her from the car. I certainly hope she's all right.
"Walk Over You" by AC/DC plays for a few moments. then stops. The crowd cheers a bit, then boos when they realize it was an audio screw up backstage. Moments later, we hear "My Girlfriend's Girlfriend" by Type O Negative and Scavenger starts making his way to the ring. He is cheered as Lauren Strong walks her husband out. He high fives fans on his way to the ring, tonight a man with no steel chair. Scavenger is on his own this week as he makes his way around ringside high fiving the crowd before he slides into the ring. Scavenger poses once for the crowd in the center of the ring. We then hear "Dr. Online" by Zeromancer and we see Blake Young on his way to the ring. He also receives a decent pop from the crowd, though their lack of familiarity with him causes it to be more muted. Blake Young reaches ringside and looks up at Scavenger who is waiting for him with a smile on his face. Blake steps up onto the apron and then steps through the ropes. The bell sounds and the two men circle each other, then lock up. Blake backs Scavenger into the ropes where the ref asks for and gets a clean break. At this point, they are playing by the rules. Scavenger and Young then lock up again. Scavenger breaks it and places Blake in a headlock. Blake backs Scavenger into the ropes and then whips him off the ropes and both men shoulder block each other, with neither of them moving an inch. Scavenger then ducks a right hook from Blake and then gives him a belly to back suplex to the mat.
Casey Braun - Good match, but I am still upset by the footage we just saw. Henry Lesher - Get over it. It happened. Myst is mercifully removed from tonight's event, the way it was meant to be.

Blake twists the ankle farther. Scavenger manager to roll over onto his back and uses his free foot to kick Blake into the corner. Blake then tries for an elbow smash on the fallen Scav only to have him roll out of the way. Scavenger then rolls to the ropes and uses them to start pulling himself to his feet. Blake gets up, takes a kick to the gut from Scavenger who then gives Blake a snap suplex to the mat and then goes for the pin. 1...2... Blake kicks out.

Blake then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He poses once there for the fans, then leaps off and lands a flying headbutt to the chest of Scavenger before again going for the pin. 1...2...3! Blake Young pulls off the win as Scavenger kicks out a moment too late.

Gerard then perches himself on the guard railing and launches himself onto Kumquat Kid with an elbow to the back. Gerard then slams Kumquat's head into the guard railing before he dumps him back over it.
Casey Braun - Gerard has really taken complete control here. Henry Lesher - No surprise to me. Gerard rolls Kumquat Kid into the ring, has a few words with his managers and then he rolls himself into the ring. Gerard Angelo with some steel post help defeats the Kumquat Kid.

Gerard then chokes Kumquat with it while screaming at him that he will never be equal to anyone within Youthful Aggression.

Gerard finally leaves the ring as Dunk and Jenny Retsyn head into the ring to attend to the fallen Kumquat Kid as the fans chant rude things in Gerard Angelo's direction. He then screams at the camera that next week he gets a world title shot.
Henry Lesher - That could be interesting... Casey Braun - You would find it interesting? Henry lesher - Yes, because Leech is going to be world champ, so that's his title Gerard just sent out a challenge for.

The Theme From Magnificent Seven plays as Hellfire Jack makes his way to the ring, led by Kerry Wylde. The crowd cheers him on as he holds his TV belt high, aware that every title has changed hands to this point tonight. Hellfire Jack steps up onto the apron and then into the ring. As he does so, we hear "Halls of Fear" by Nino Rota and Sal Clemenzo comes out to a more mixed reaction than Hellfire Jack got.
Casey Braun - Another big title match, our last before the main event. Henry Lesher - Hopefully Sal makes this like a wedding night with Kirstie Alley, get in and out quick and collect your paycheck.

Sal then wraps the knee around the middle rope and kicks away at it as Hellfire Jack winces in pain. Hellfire headbutts Sal backwards and then pulls his leg off the ropes and then lands a series of punches on Sal that drive him to the center of the ring and then to the opposite corner. Sal responds with a stiff kick to the sore knee of Hellfire Jack. Sal then chokes Jack over the top rope and tells him to squeal and give up. Sal then pulls the rope hard into the throat of Hellfire, sending him falling down to the mat. Sal then boot chokes him, using the ropes for added leverage.
Casey Braun - Vicious attack here by Sal. Henry Lesher - That's what he is all about. Sal releases the choke before the ref reaches a five count, then stands Hellfire Jack up, whips him off the ropes and then clotheslines him to the mat. Sal follows this up with a leg drop to the chest of Hellfire and then goes for the pin. 1...2... Jack manages to kick out. Sal stands Jack up, whips him into the corner and then tries for a monkey flip. Jack rolls and manages to land on his feet, then gives Sal a reverse neckbreaker to the mat and Jack tries for the surprise pinfall here. 1...2... kick out by Sal Clemenzo. Jack then pulls Sal off of there and then whips him into the steel ring steps shoulder first. Jack then rolls Sal into the ring as fans in the area start a chant for Hellfire Jack.

Sal again tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Hellfire Jack.
