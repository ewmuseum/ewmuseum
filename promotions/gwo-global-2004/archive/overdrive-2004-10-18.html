GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

GWO Overdrive, 18 October 2004

18 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
San Jose, CA It's a full house looking on as we get set for another edition of GWO Overdrive. Backstage we see Tony Parizi in his office when Boomshalkalaka bursts in rather rudely.
Tony Parizi - Do you realize how stupid you are? Boomshalkalaka - Rod and I want a shot at the tag titles next week. Tony Parizi - *laughs* Boy, people have been coming to me with all sorts of demands lately, but that takes the cake. What have you two done exactly?
Boomshalkalaka - Just watch us tonight, Parizi. We are ready for the tag titles. They are coming to us.
Tony Parizi - Watch yourself. Your boy is against my little girl. Boomshalkalaka - She signed the contract. Now she pays the price. Boom leaves Tony's office and we see the opening credits as "Overrated" by Three Days Grace plays and we see images of Stevie James, T-Rex, Myst, Scavenger, The Dying Breed, Leech and Youthful Aggression before we go to ringside where Ricky Simms and Henry Lesher are at their familiar announcing table yet again for another edition of the show.
Pyro vs Jon Shocker "The 20th Century Was Entirely Mine" by Fear Before The March Of Flames plays as Pyro makes his way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. He tells the camera to watch this entire match, all thirty seconds of it and then steps onto the apron and smiles as he looks out on the crowd. He finally steps into the ring and tests the ropes as we hear "Phenomenon" by LL Cool J and Jon Shocker comes out led by Chastity and the crowd is on their feet cheering the man on.
Henry Lesher - I'm not sure what's worse, watching him wrestle or listening to the idiot talk.
Ricky Simms - Shocker very popular with this Californian crowd. Henry Lesher - Proves American IQs are not higher than Canadian. Shocker steps onto the apron and starts arguing with Pyro from there, then steps into the ring where Pyro attacks him with a series of double axe handles across the back. The bell sounds as Pyro then snap mares Shocker to the mat and places him in a sleeper hold. Shocker doesn't wait long to starts elbowing Pyro in the gut to break the hold, then hip tosses him across the ring into the corner where Shocker then puts the boots to Pyro. The crowd cheers as Shocker then stands Pyro up and whips him hard into the opposite corner. Shocker then charges at Pyro who raises a knee into the ribs of the attacking Shocker before head butting him to the mat. Pyro then climbs to the second rope and lands a knee drop to the forehead of Shocker from there and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Shocker.
Ricky Simms - Shocker just gets out of that one. Henry Lesher - You know your career is in the crapper when this guy starts beating you. Pyro stands Shocker up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Pyro then heads for the ropes again and this time makes his way to the top as Shocker quickly gets to his feet behind him. Pyro turns to face the ring from the top and takes a shot to the stomach from Shocker who then climbs the ropes, stands on top and delivers a superplex on Pyro from there. Shocker then goes for the pin. 1...2... Pyro rolls a shoulder out. At ringside we see Chastity cheering Shocker on as he stands Pyro up, whips him into the corner buckle and then gives him an inverse atomic drop out of it before Pyro thumbs Shocker in the eye, drawing the ire of the crowd. Pyro then picks Shocker up and body slams him to the mat. He then boot chokes Shocker in the ropes, releasing the choke before the ref has a chance to reach the five count which would result in a disqualification.
Ricky Simms - Action going back and forth here. Henry Lesher - As the fans wonder why they are watching it. Pyro stands Shocker up and whips him off the ropes and then hip tosses him over the top rope and to the floor. Pyro quickly drops and rolls himself out of the ring to continue the punishment. He stands Jon up, walks him to the steel ring steps and slams his head into them. He then spots Chastity and heads after her. The crowd boos as the ref shouts at Pyro to back off from Chastity. Chastity doesn't back down and slaps Pyro across the face. Pyro grabs her arm and squeezes it, causing her to drop to her knees in pain. From the back we see Gerard Angelo charging into the picture. He picks Shocker up and runs him fact first into the steel corner post and then gives him a running power slam on the floor before rolling him back inside the ring. Pyro then releases Chastity and rolls himself inside the ring where he applies the Third Strike on Shocker who is out cold in it. The ref checks on Shocker and immediately calls on Shocker and signals to the back for medical attention. Pyro stands and smiles as he is announced as the winner and EMTs make their way to the ring with a stretcher for Pyro's victim this week.
Ricky Simms - Pyro outsmarts Shocker this week. Henry Lesher - That really isn't saying much, you know. Backstage we see Max Cameron entering th building when he is headed off by Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Max, tonight you square off against Don Mega for the UWA Xtreme title. Thoughts on this challenge?
Max Cameron - Yeah, the poor bastard doesn't have a hope. Joe Davidson - That's all you have to say on the matter? Max Cameron - You expected more? Why? He has said all week I'm beneath him. Tonight, the poor bastard will wish he was never born.
Joe Davidson - And after tonight, then what? Max Cameron - After tonight, Myst had better be ready for me. She'll pay for being champion right now.
Joe Davidson - But what if Rated R takes her title? Max Cameron - Then he moves to the top of my list. That title is my focus, Joe. Whoever has it will suffer. However, I have other tasks tonight.
Max moves on and we head back to the ring. Freddy Thrills vs Jack Spade "Thriller" by Michael Jackson plays as Freddy Thrills makes his way to the ring, led by Radd Matthews. Freddy high fives fans on his way to the ring and then steps up onto the apron and looks out at everyone. He smiles, nods his head and steps through the ropes into the ring as his music fades. We then hear "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne and Jack Spade comes out to a huge ovation from the capacity crowd.
Ricky Simms - I can say that on the surface here Jack appears to be giving up a lot in the size department to the much taller and heavier Freddy Thrills here.
Henry Lesher - Two debuts I could care less about for the price of one. How special is that?
The bell sounds as Jack steps through the ropes and the two men lock up. Freddy quickly shoves Jack to the mat. Jack then rolls himself out of the ring and paces for a bit as Freddy waits impatiently in the ring. Jack then steps onto the apron and through the ropes again. Before they go into another lock up, Jack dropkicks Freddy in the knee cap, then pulls the feet out from under him to negate the height advantage as he applies an ankle lock submission on Thrills. Thrills refuses to submit here as he reaches towards the ropes and Jack fights to keep him in the center of the ring. Thrills lunges again and manages to pull Spade close enough to the ropes to grab onto them and force the break of the hold. Thrills then uses the ropes to quickly get to his feet, though he looks wobbly on them.
Ricky Simms - Jack Spade showing here that he knows how to fight big men. Henry Lesher - If this fool was brought in to protect Kosmic Kitty from Rated R, who the hell is going to protect him?
Thrills elbows Spade in the head, then headbutts him once before landing a series of knife edge chops on Spade which drive him into the corner. Thrills then chokes Spade over the top rope for a few moments before releasing the choke as Radd applauds at ringside. Freddy then whips Spade off the ropes and gives him a giant boot to the face, knocking him hard to the mat where Thrills goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Jack Spade. Thrills then stands Spade up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Spade does a somersault, however, and lands on his feet before kicking Freddy Thrills in the back. He then rolls Freddy up from behind for the schoolboy pin attempt. 1...2...3! Jack Spade quickly exits the ring after a roll up win over Thrills in his debut match this week.
Ricky Simms - Hard fought victory for Spade. Henry Lesher - Great bodyguard. Ricky Simms - Hey, it was his first GWO match too. This doesn't diminish the guy as a body guard. His task is to protect Kosmic Kitty which is totally different than a standard wrestling match.
Jack Spade comes face to face with Radd Matthews at ringside. Radd offers his hand to congratulate a sceptical Spade. However, Spade takes the handshake and then heads to the back without incident. Radd then enters the ring to find Thrills is all right save a sore ankle. We then head to commercial.
We see Frank Merritt reading a map and we can hear him cursing under his breath. We then pull back from him and see Xtasy in the foreground. "Hello friends, do you find yourself much the same as my friend Frank here? Are you completely unable to read a bloody map? Pick up my new book *holds up before the camera* 'Please Don't Think, I'll Show You Where You Are.' In my new book, I detail how easy it can be for a man to read instructions and even tell you how to fold a map." He looks back over his shoulder at Frank who is battling to fold the map. "Poor fool. He tries so hard but is so stupid." We then hear Frank say, "Fuck you,X!" before we head back to the show.
Boomshalkalaka vs Zack McCracken "Beautiful Stranger" by Madonna plays as Zack McCracken makes his way to the ring. Felicity leads him to the ring and informs us through the camera following them that he is going to keep the wins coming. Zack reaches ringside and raises an eyebrow towards a fan. He then pulls a pad of paper from his jacket and signs his name and hands the paper to the very happy young fan. As he then turns to the ring he takes a clothesline from behind from Boomshalkalaka. Boom then puts the boots to the fallen Zack before standing him up, whipping him shoulder first into the corner post and then rolls him into the ring. Boom rolls himself into the ring and the bell sounds to start the match as he two handed chokes Zack McCracken. The crowd boos as he releases the choke and shouts at the crowd. He then follows that up by standing Zack up and whipping him into the corner. He then gives Zack a vertical suplex, holding it for a few seconds with him straight up in the air before dropping him hard to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Boom trying to send a message here. He believes that he and Rod deserve a shot at the tag team titles.
Henry Lesher - Clearly a record of no wins and one loss show he is justified in his belief in himself and his idiot partner.
Boom heads to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and drops an elbow to the throat of Zack from there and then goes for the pin. 1...2... Zack gets a foot on the bottom rope. Boomshalkalaka then stands Zack up, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him back to the mat. He then turns to Felicity and smiles. She backs off as Boom steps over the top rope and then drops to the floor. He follows her towards the back as she retreats, begging for him to leave her alone. Felicity then takes off one of her high heeled shoes and throws it at Boom, striking him in the chest. He steps back and then charges at her only to be attacked by The Moose who charges in and clotheslines Boom to the floor. The crowd cheers as Leech comes out soon after and the two of them start putting the boots to Boomshalkalaka as the bell is called for. Boom wins by DQ thanks to The Moose and Leech who are clearly sending a message here. They stands Boom up and then Leech 3-Ds him to the floor. They then head to the back as Felicity hurries into the ring to help Zack to his feet.
Henry Lesher - Leave it to Moose and Leech to save the show! Ricky Simms - Is that what they were doing? Henry Lesher - My hero stands triumphant and that masked idiot is down for the count. We then see The Moose and Leech headed backstage when they are headed off by Tony Parizi. Tony Parizi - Interfering again, are you? Leech - Just showing the loudmouth who is really in charge around here. Tony Parizi - That's my job. The Moose - Then do your job. Sal Clemenzo and Big Guido then step in behind Tony and glaring at The Moose and Leech. Sal Clemenzo - Is there a problem with our esteemed leader? Leech - Nothing Moose and I can't deal with. Stay out of it, Sal. Sal Clemenzo - Don't give me a reason to get into it, Leech. The Moose and Leech turn away and Sal exchanges a glance with Guido before they and Tony head in the opposite direction.
The Dawg vs Eddie Vines "Who Let the Dawgs Out?" by Baha Men plays as The Dawg comes out to an ovation from the crowd. He flexes his muscle once for the camera and tells us he is all set for a shot at Myst when she's ready to stop hiding from him. He then reaches ringside and high fives fans on his way around the ring. As he steps onto the apron and then into the ring, his music fades. "Eat You Alive" by Limp Bizkit plays as Eddie Vines comes out to a mixed reaction this week.
Henry Lesher - There's the fool who was dumb enough to attack Leech last week. Ricky Simms - Eddie is a sick and twisted man who is seeking Leech for his own purposes. He sees these opponents as people he will devour, his food on his way to the main course, GWO gold.
Henry Lesher - That was almost witty. Did you think that up by yourself? Eddie steps onto the apron and has a few words for The Dawg before he steps into the ring and the bell sounds to get this match underway.
Henry Lesher - Eddie will find Dawg is quite the super sized meal. Eddie and Dawg lock up in the center of the ring. Eddie Vines breaks that up and gouges the eyes of The Dawg and then lands a series of forearms to the jaw on Dawg that drive him into the ropes. Vines then whips Dawg into the corner face first and slams his face into the corner buckle. Dawg answers with an elbow to the jaw on Vines which he follows up with a headbutt on him. Dawg then clotheslines Vines to the mat. The Dawg then leg drops Vines across the throat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Eddie Vines. Dawg stands Vines up and whips him off the ropes. Vines leap frogs Dawg, jumps onto the ropes and then lands a hurricarana that pulls Dawg to the mat. Once Dawg is fallen, Eddie bites the forehead of The Dawg as the referee shouts at him to stop. Eddie then looks to the crowd and smiles.
Henry Lesher - First time I have seen a person bite a dog. Ricky Simms - But this match still doesn't entertain you? Henry Lesher - Oh, not in the slightest. Vines heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He screams and dives off for a five star frog splash that misses when The Dawg rolls out of the way. Dawg then rolls himself out of the ring to regroup, grabbing at his forehead. Eddie Vines rolls out of the ring after The Dawg. He chases The Dawg only to have Dawg turn and kick him in the stomach. Dawg then whips Eddie face first into the steel ring steps. The crowd cheers as Dawg then stands Eddie up again. Eddie spits blood in the face of The Dawg, though and then gives him a dropkick that sends him to the floor. Vines rolls himself into the ring to break up the referee's count and then rolls himself back out of the ring. Vines stands Dawg up and whips him face first into the steel corner post. Vines then bites the forehead of The Dawg again as the ref shouts he is going to disqualify him if this continues.
Ricky Simms - Brutal display here by Eddie Vines this week. Henry Lesher - It's a shame the referee has to stop him from doing the one interesting thing about this entire match.
Eddie rolls Dawg into the ring and then rolls himself inside as well. Eddie then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He launches himself from there and this time lands the five star frog splash on The Dawg and goes for the pin. 1...2... Dawg manages to roll a shoulder out to the shock of the fans and the dismay of Eddie Vines who immediately starts shouting at the referee. The ref shouts back and the two stand nose to nose in the center of the ring. Dawg takes advantage and rolls Eddie up for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Eddie Vines. Eddie then rolls himself out of the ring, tosses the timekeeper out of his chair, folds that chair up and rolls himself into the ring. The ref tells Eddie to drop the chair, so Eddie clubs the ref with the chair first and then turns it on The Dawg who he lands a series of chair shots on as security streams into the ring and a replacement ref comes in calling for the bell. Eddie greets many of the security officers with chair shots to the head and spits blood in the face of another. As he does, we see Tony Parizi coming out from the back with a microphone in hand.
Tony Parizi - Eddie Vines, they told me you were dangerous before I hired you. I had no idea. You thirst for Leech, do you? After all, that was your focus last week and clearly you are an enraged man seeking blood and pain of others. Maximum Impact, two weeks from now, you get what you want. You get a shot at Leech one on one in a match with hardcore rules. May God have mercy on your soul. However, you are done for tonight. You're headed off with the San Jose Police for tonight. Have a good night.
Tony leaves and Eddie is dragged in handcuffs from the ring by security and we see a police car, lights flashing, waiting outside the arena.
Henry Lesher - Vines gets himself disqualified and I must admit he endeared himself a little bit to me with that brutal display.
Ricky Simms - Even with him targeting your boy Leech? Henry Lesher - Oh, Leech will kill him, but it doesn't mean I can't admire Vines a little. As Vines is led to the police car, we head to commercial. We see Sal Clemenzo standing on an overpass with a rifle in hand. He appears to be targeting an unsuspecting motorist when he suddenly faces the camera and smiles. "If you're going to get life insurance, make sure to go with Met. I have found them to be most understanding of the risks I take every day to make America safer. I also find their people to be most helpful in my locating of people set to cash in on their life insurance policies. It takes very little to get them to tell me so much. You would listen to a doctor talking about your health. Listen to me, a self employed and made man, about life insurance. Go with Met because you could die tomorrow." Sal then looks through the sight on his gun again, humming to himself as he awaits his prey.
Rod Gordon vs Cordelia Parizi "Diamonds and Guns" by The Transplants plays as Rod Gordon makes his way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. He vows to the camera this match is dedicated to his partner.
Henry Lesher - I see Dweedledumber is in the house. Ricky Simms - There has to be a lot going on in his mind after the attack on Boomshalkalaka earlier.
Henry Lesher - Must confuse him to have songs from Sesame Street battling for his attention with that.
Rod steps into the ring and paces there as we hear "Power Trip" by Monster Magnet and Jamie and Jessica Parizi lead their cousin Cordelia to the ring. Rod has a few words for the twins as they take up positions on opposite sides at ringside as Cordelia steps up onto the apron and then inside the ring. The bell sounds, but Rod demands to the ref that the twins be barred from ringside. The ref refuses and states that they haven't done anything to warrant being thrown out.
Henry Lesher - Would he like some cheese with his whine? Rod finally gives up and locks up with Cordelia. He quickly shoves her to the mat and then puts the boots to her as she rolls herself out of the ring. Jamie Parizi helps her up and Rod shouts again to the ref about the managers for Cordelia here. Cordelia steps onto the apron and then back inside the ring. Rod looks for the lock up, but Cordelia stomps on his foot, then eye gouges him. She then headbutts him, sending him falling to the mat. Cordelia then knee drops Rod to the forehead and goes for the pin. 1...2... Rod manages to kick out. Cordelia stands Rod up, whips him into the corner and then does a handspring into a head scissors choke hold on Rod before she brings him back to the mat. Cordelia then heads to the top rope and then springs off, moonsaulting right onto the raised knees of Rod Gordon. Rod then uses the ropes to get to his feet and then puts the boots to Cordelia before he stands her up. Rod then whips her into the corner buckle and then gives her a spine buster as she staggers out of it. Rod then goes for the pin. 1...2... Cordelia manages to kick out. Rod then stands her up, whips her off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Cordelia really taking some nasty shots from Rod Gordon here. Henry Lesher - Yeah, like I care. Bring on the tag team title match! Rod stands Cordelia up, a grin on his face. That grin fades when Cordelia lands a jawbreaker on him. Cordelia then places Rod's throat over the second rope and chokes him with it. The ref then pushes her away and warns her about it. As he does that, Jessica Parizi chokes Rod with the rope and then punches him in the face before she backs off. Cordelia then goes back to him, stands him up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Cordelia then comes off the ropes and lands a rolling thunder on Rod and goes for the pin. 1...2... Rod manages to roll a shoulder out. Cordelia then rolls Rod out of the ring. She heads outside the ring and puts the boots to Rod there before rolling herself back inside. Again, the ref has a few words with her as Jessica Parizi makes her way around the ring and clubs Rod in the head with a chair before rolling him back inside the ring where Cordelia lands a lionsault on him and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Cordelia Parizi comes out with a win this week.
Ricky Simms - Cordelia with a win heavily aided by the twins outside the ring. Henry Lesher - Rod's not bright. He fought the Mob. He lost. This is a guy hoping to take down Leech and Moose?
Backstage we see Radd Matthews in the office of Tony Parizi. Tony Parizi - You understand what you are asking me for? Radd Matthews - I know exactly what I am asking you for. I believe I have earned it. Tony Parizi - And you want me to grant you what might be your death in the middle of the ring?
Radd Matthews - Yes. Tony Parizi - Fine, then, at Maximum Impact it will be you and Rated R for the Pacific Title in a steel cage ladder match.
Radd Matthews - If he wins tonight against Myst, that becomes a world title shot, right? Tony Parizi - Don't push your luck, kid. Radd leaves the office and Tony returns to his work as we head back to the ring. T-Rex vs Slim Pickens "Can't Get the Best of Me" by Cypress Hill plays as we await T-Rex. Instead, we find he is already fighting in the hallway leading to the entrance with Slim Pickens. The crowd cheers as they see the two of them trading rights and lefts to the jaw. T-Rex eye gouges Slim. Slim eye gouges T-Rex. Slim then rams T-Rex's head into the wall. T-Rex responds with a low blow kick on Slim. T-Rex then staggers through the entranceway and into the aisle leading to the ring. Slim staggers out after him, clearly in pain as he grabs T-Rex by the hair and slams his head into the guard railing. Slim then tosses T-Rex over the guard railing and into an aisle between the fans. Slim steps over the guard railing and then puts the boots to T-Rex out there. T-Rex then grabs the foot of Slim and trips him to the floor. T-Rex rains down punches on Slim Pickens, then eye gouges him before giving him a two handed choke as the nearby fans chant for Slim to make a comeback. T-Rex then stands Slim up, and then throws him head first into the guard railing before dumping him over it and back into the aisle which leads to the ring.
Ricky Simms - Brutal action here and the match hasn't even started yet. Henry Lesher - This is how every match should be. Well, that and more Leech. T-Rex pulls Slim to the ring in a headlock and then bulldogs him on the floor. T-Rex then has a few words with a fan at ringside before he rolls Slim into the ring. As T-Rex rolls himself into the ring, the bell finally sounds to make this match official. T-Rex stands Slim up, whips him off the ropes and Slim shoulder blocks T-Rex to the mat. The fans start a chant for Slim as he places T-Rex in a side headlock. T-Rex elbows Slim in the ribs a few times to break that lock up and then T-Rex gives Slim a fall away slam to the mat. T-Rex shakes his head and catches his breath as he pulls himself to his feet. T-Rex then places Slim in a hammerlock submission hold. Slim refuses to submit and lunges to the ropes which forces the break. T-Rex backs off as Slim gets to his feet and then they lock up again. Slim breaks the lock up, placing T-Rex in an arm bar. T-Rex eye rakes Slim, then arm drags Slim to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Both men doing well here. Good match. Henry Lesher - Matter of opinion on that, Rick. No Leech. No good match. Slim grabs the ropes and pulls T-Rex out through the ropes to the floor. T-Rex rolls into the guard railing as Slim rolls himself out of the ring. Slim boot chokes T-Rex against the guard railing as we see Stevie James coming out from the back. Slim stands T-Rex up and takes a thumb in the eye. T-Rex then spots Stevie and has a few words for him. T-Rex then rolls Slim Pickens into the ring while still having a few words for Stevie who laughs him off. T-Rex then rolls himself into the ring where Slim then elbows him in the gut. Slim picks T-Rex up and drops him on the top rope where his neck slingshots off of it. Slim then climbs the ropes as Stevie applauds from ringside. Slim Pickens launches himself from the top and drops the Slim Proposition on T-Rex and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Slim Pickens pulls off the win here over T-Rex!
Ricky Simms - A win for Slim here over T-Rex as he heads into a shot at the Canadian title next week.
Henry Lesher - Stevie James caused that. The guy really bugs me. He's an idiot. As Slim leaves the ring and heads to the back, we see Stevie James entering the ring with a smile on his face. He takes the microphone from ring announcer Shelley Morris.
Stevie James - Benn watching you since we last met, T. Your ladies aren't out here tonight to save your sorry ass. You just lost to redneck Pickens and now I am about to show you why I am the only ladies man in the GWO, the true heart breaker, the true...
Stevie's words are cut off when "Phenomenon" by LL Cool J starts to play and Jon Shocker comes out with a microphone in hand as well.
Jon Shocker - Whoa whoa whoa! This is what I was talkin' about! Da shockman had to come back to silence you and Rex! Who is the bigger ladies man between you? Who cares? The real man in GWO is da shockman, the true champion of the ladies! I propose that the three of us find out. Next week Stevie, Rex and Shocker in the ring to decide who the true man of the ladies is! This match will not be a standard drag out fight, Mister James. This will be with women suspended above the ring we are to free. That's right! Caged women hanging above the ring! First man to free his woman gets the title as GWO ladies' man! What do you say?
Stevie James - You have got yourself a match, Shocker. Jon Shocker - Ya damned right I got a match! Next week the three of us battle to free the ladies from the cages. Oh, Stevie, look out behind you.
Jon leaves and Stevie turns to find T-Rex on his feet. T-Rex kicks Stevie in the gut, then DDTs him to the mat before rolling out of the ring and heading to the back in search of Jon Shocker.
Ricky Simms - That should be quite the match next week! Henry Lesher - Caged women? Oh, yeah, that'll be fantastic. *rolls his eyes* Which woman would T-Rex choose and who the hell will Stevie have inside the cage for him? We know Shocker will have Chastity in her kennel where most dogs belong anyways.
We then head to commercial. We see Kosmic Kitty standing in front of a Hostess factory. She smiles and waves. "I am here fighting for the life of the Twinkies we all love. However, we need your help and that means writing to your local political leaders about Twinkies, the wonderful pastry capable of doing pretty much anything. If you like me, you'll buy some," she says and we then see a crush of young men charging at the factory door to buy their share of the snack treat Kitty loves so much. She winks and blows a kiss to the camera.
Thunder Storme vs Scavenger/Fixxxer "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays as Thunder and Marla Storme make their way to the ring, led tonight by Kate Redman. Kate waves to the fans as she reaches ringside first and Thunder and Storme step up onto the apron to their usual mixed reaction. As they step inside the ring, we hear "Mouth" by Bush and the lights darken as Fixxxer comes out with Scavenger, their older brother Troy trailing close behind them. Scavenger has his trusty chair in hand and has Beverly Hills 90210 stickers all over it. Fixxxer rolls himself into the ring as Scavenger hands his chair to brother Troy, there being no sign of the girls this week. The bell sounds as Thunder opts to start this match off with Fixxxer and the two lock up. Thunder opens things up by shoving Fixxxer down and then boot choking him in the ropes. Thunder then stands Fixxxer up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a knee to the gut, then a gut wrench suplex before going for the early pinfall attempt. 1...2... kick out by Fixxxer.
Ricky Simms - Good match between these two evenly matched teams thus far. Henry Lesher - How the hell has this been even so far? Thunder stands Fixxxer up and pulls him to his corner where he tags in Marla Storme. Marla climbs to the top rope as Thunder body slams Fixxxer to the mat and then Marla leaps off and lands a shooting star press on Fixxxer. Marla tries for the pin. 1...2... Fixxxer gets a foot on the bottom rope. Marla stands Fixxxer up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him back to the mat. Marla Storme then heads to the ropes again and launches herself from the top for a headbutt that misses when Fixxxer rolls out of the way. Fixxxer then goes to his corner and tags in Scavenger. Scavenger enters the ring and lands a series of knife edge chops on Marla which drives her to the ropes. He then whips her off the ropes and backdrops Marla to the mat. Radd then dives through the ropes to the floor and escapes through the crowd.
Carl Simms - Ryan is having a tough night. Henry Lesher - I know how he feels. Dante, why?? Why side with The Authority against such a delicious villain? What happened to you?
Myst is seen leaving as our shot fades to black, Dante accompanying her with no rhyme nor reason given for it.
