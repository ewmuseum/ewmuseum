GWO Overdrive, 13 December 2004

13 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Edmonton, AB We see backstage as Lethal Weapon comes into the arena with the world title over his shoulder. The Skyreach Center is full and the crowd cheers on sight of their champion. Lethal is headed off by Stevie James.
Stevie James - Champ, something's up. Lethal Weapon - What are you talking about? Stevie James - Sal Clemenzo got here hours ago to meet with Tony Parizi. I saw him with a title belt. Something is up, man.
Lethal Weapon pushes Stevie aside and storms off towards Tony Parizi's office. The camera follows him down the hall. He opens it and finds the office empty. Lethal then slams his fists against the wall and storms off as we see a concerned Stevie James looking on and shaking his head. We then see the opening credits and hear "Breathe" by Swollen Members featuring Nelly Furtado play in the background. We see images of Lethal Weapon, Kosmic Kitty, Stevie James, Youthful Aggression, The Dying Breed, Razorblade and Leech before we head to ringside where the announcers are ready to call the action.
Ricky Simms - Tonight should be quite the night. Henry Lesher - Well, I heard Leech won't be showing up tonight, so I highly doubt that. "Mine Teil" by Rammstein starts to play as the arena turns dark. The drums echo and then the lights slowly come up to reveal Vesuvius and Xandra standing in the middle of the GWO ring. Vesuvius has a microphone and smiles towards Ricky Simms and Henry Lesher.
Vesuvius - Sorry to interrupt the stupendous announce team, but I thought tonight I should make my presence known. You see, I have been watching the crap going on within the GWO. I have decided it is time to bring the instability back, the..... what is the word I seek?.... Anarchy. Yes, fans, Anarchy is back and we are hiring. This is a warning to some in the back and an invite to others. Time will tell which side each man stands on.
He throws the microphone down and Xandra follows him out, though she doesn't appear to be doing so willingly. He appears to be helping her along.
Ricky Simms - That was bizarre. Henry Lesher - I think the word you seek is ridculous. Anarchy is the past. Kosmic Kitty vs Radd Matthews "Vindicated" by Dashboard Confessional plays as Radd Matthews comes out with the Pacific title over his shoulder. Radd has words with a fan in the front row and then makes his way to the ring with a smile on his face. He looks at his reflection in the title belt and nods his head before he steps up onto the apron. He then looks out to the crowd and shows them the title belt and again shakes his head, then steps into the ring and poses on the second rope in the corner buckle. As he steps down, we hear "A Place For My Head" by Linkin Park and out comes Kosmic Kitty all dressed in black. She tosses the usual Twinkies to the crowd from her basket, but she doesn't take her eyes off of Radd all the way to the ring. She then hands her basket to a little boy in the front row and then looks into the ring, shaking her head.
Ricky Simms - This should be very interesting. Henry Lesher - Not really. The soap opera hopefully ends tonight. Ricky Simms - She is using Myst's theme tonight. I think that speaks volumes. Henry Lesher - She is hoping that using music of a champion makes her a champion. Kitty steps up onto the apron and Radd backs off, handing the Pacific title to the timekeeper through the ropes. Kitty steps into the ring and the bell is called for. Radd Matthews shakes his head and tells Kitty to give it up, that this can end before she gets hurt. Kitty shakes her head and the two lock up. Radd breaks that with an arm bar. Kitty does a standing somersault to break it and then gives Radd a hiptoss to the mat. Kitty then backs off as Radd quickly gets to his feet with a nasty look on his face. He tells the fans to shut up as they start a chant for Kitty. The two then lock up in the center of the ring. Radd breaks it with a headbutt on Kitty, then rakes her eyes and follows that up by placing her throat over the top rope and choking her with it. Radd then whips Kitty off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat before again throwing some nasty words towards the crowd.
Ricky Simms - Radd fighting dirty in this match tonight. Henry Lesher - Good for him. He might be earning my respect tonight, showing he wants to keep that title belt.
Radd stands Kitty up, whips her into the corner and then arm drags her to the center of the ring. She gets to her feet and catches Radd off guard with a dropkick that sends him into the corner. Kosmic Kitty then gives Radd a monkey flip to the center of the ring. Kitty follows that up with a leg drop across the throat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Radd Matthews. Radd then rolls himself out of the ring with a look of shock on his face. He has words with a fan in the front row, then grabs his belt from the timekeeper and heads around the ring, fingering Kosmic Kitty and signalling this is where he leaves. As he reaches the midway mark, he is headed off by Stevie James. Radd backs away from Stevie, telling him he has no beef with him. Stevie just nods and tells him to get back into the ring as the crowd starts nasty chants towards the defending Pacific champion. Radd backs up to ringside as Kitty rolls out of the ring. She grabs Radd by the hair and slams his head into the steel ring steps and then rolls him back into the ring. Radd's belt falls at ringside and Stevie picks it up with a smile on his face.
Henry Lesher - See, the feline needs her little boyfriend to come out here. Ricky Simms - Radd was leaving! Henry Lesher - I was about to leave, too. Kosmic Kitty whips Radd off the ropes, backdrops him to the mat and then gives him a knee drop to the forehead. Kitty then stands Radd up and goes to the corner. We see Devanshi coming through the crowd to make the save. She slides into the ring as Stevie James slides in from the other side. He crowd her with the title belt and rolls her out of the ring as Kosmic Kitty delivers the Boo Boo Kitty on Radd Matthews and rolls him over for the pin. 1...2...3! Kosmic Kitty is the new Pacific Champion! She rolls out of the ring, tears running down her cheeks as we see her sister Jamie coming out from the back to congratulate her on the win. Stevie James comes around the ring and embraces her. As they celebrate, the arena goes dark and the four big screens hanging above the ring fill with the image of Thunder.
Thunder - Enjoy that, Kitty. Enjoy the title. Dying Breed has made life a living hell for Rated R and now we will do the same to you.
Lightning explodes from the roof and appears to strike the corner post nearest to Kitty and she hides behind Stevie James as Jamie races out of the area. The lights then come up and Kitty kisses Stevie on the cheek and they head to the back together.
Ricky Simms - Great win for the new champion, but Thunder threw down a challenge and a warning to her and she would be a fool not to heed that.
Henry Lesher - She's marrying someone she hardly knows. She has already proven herself to be a fool.
Backstage we see Razorblade with Ray Dees. Ray Dees - Razor, we have received word that Leech is not here tonight. Your reaction? Razorblade - Leech is a royal pain in my ass. Leech has screwed me over for weeks, so him not being here bodes well for me. Tonight I get a chance to show the world what I am made of.
Ray Dees - Any words for Jack Spade? Razorblade - Yeah, just a few. I was made for this match, Jack. I am coming for that title. Shine it up nice for me.
Ray Dees - Confident man. Razorblade - That's because I am a damned good wrestler. Sabrina Davidson vs Slayer "Edge of Seventeen" by Stevie Nicks plays as Sabrina Davidson makes her way to the ring. She gets a strong reaction from the fans as she high fives some fans, set for her first ever wrestling match. Sabrina steps onto the apron, clearly nervous about her debut here tonight. We then hear "So Far Away" by Staind playing and out comes the monster Slayer. He smiles as he wrings his hands looking into the ring at Sabrina who has now backed into a corner and has got to be wondering if she made a wise decision coming out here to wrestle tonight.
Henry Lesher - This will be a slaughter. Ricky Simms - You thought Kitty would lose, too. Henry Lesher - By all logic she should have. Won't happen two matches in a row. Slayer steps onto the apron and then steps through the ropes and the bell sounds to get this match started tonight. Slayer appraoches Sabrina who rolls out of the way of his attack. She then stands in another corner as Slayer charges in and raises a leg for a kick to the chin that barely misses as Sabrina ducks it and then shoots a leg out from under Slayer. Sabrina applies an ankle lock submission on Slayer. He rolls over and kicks her away from him and then gives her a kick in the gut. Slayer gets to his feet as Sabrina is still doubled over. He then grabs her by the hair and has a few not too kind words for her before slamming her face into the corner buckle. He looks out to the booing crowd and shakes his head. Sabrina then catches him off guard with a clothesline that sends him over the top rope to the floor. Sabrina then retreats to the center of the ring as she gathers her composure.
Ricky Simms - Sabrina's showing a lot of guts tonight. Henry Lesher - Slayer is teaching her that wrestling is not her game. Trust me. She's gonna fail.
Slayer gets to his feet and pulls himself back into the ring. He gets to his feet and Sabrina greets him with a dropkick that sends him into the corner. Sabrina then attempts a monkey flip, but Slayer shoves her away. Sabrina falls on her back, then Slayer grabs her by the hair and looks out to the crowd with a sadistic smile on his face. He grabs ahold of Sabrina, has a few words for the crowd and then delivers the Black Hawk Down on her and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Slayer stands and raises his arms in victory and looks out at the booing crowd, shaking his head. He then grabs Sabrina by the hair and pulls her to her feet.
Ricky Simms - This isn't necessary. Henry Lesher - No, it's entertaining, though. Slayer whips Sabrina off the ropes and gives her a boot to the jaw. We then see Noah Skanes coming out from the back with a chair in hand. Slayer sees him coming and then drops down and slides out of the ring. Noah attends to Sabrina who appears to be out cold as Slayer heads to the back laughing. He has words with a fan in the front row and then heads to the back victorious and hated, just the way he planned it. We then look backstage and see Sal Clemenzo with Kelly Buchanan.
Kelly Buchanan - Sal, tonight you have a shot at the gold. Sal Clemenzo - No, tonight I win the gold. Tonight I become the world champion. Kelly Buchanan - It appears to be the night of confident challengers. Sal Clemenzo - Kelly, I am not the challenger. I am the champion. Kelly Buchanan - Okay, but you haven't won the title yet. Sal Clemenzo - Oh I won the title alright. It's up to me to show Lethal that it's mine. Spartacus Conway vs Stevie James "I am Spartacus" echoes through the arena. We then hear "Points of Authority" by Linkin Park and out comes Spartacus Conway. He is booed on his way to the ring, but he's too busy posing and telling the camera how he is the best wrestler in the damned world to pay attention. He also makes a point of telling us he had nothing to do with the hit and run on Myst two weeks ago. Spartacus steps up onto the apron and looks out at the booing crowd and shakes his head. He then steps through the ropes and has a few words with the ref about how tonight's match is to be called as his music fades and "Open Road" by Bryan Adams plays and out comes Stevie James to his second ovation of the night.
Henry Lesher - Let's see if loverboy can fight his own battles. Ricky Simms - He has been a champion in GWO before, so I don't see why he can't. Henry Lesher - That's because you're a fool. Stevie steps up onto the apron and then somersaults over the top rope into the ring, landing on his feet. The bell sounds and Spartacus offers to shake hands with Stevie. Stevie slaps his hand away and the two lock up. Spartacus quickly powers Stevie into the corner where he lands a series of knee lifts to the gut on Stevie James. Spartacus then follows that up with a hip toss to the center of the ring where he quickly applies a sleeperhold. The ref checks on Stevie who appears to be fading fast as the crowd starts a chant for him, much like they did for his fiancee earlier tonight. The ref checks for a submission, then lifts the arm of Stevie James once and it falls. He lifts it a second time and it falls. He lifts it a third time, but Stevie manages to prevent it from falling and looks to be gaining his strength back fast as he gets to his feet and starts delivering elbows to the gut on the larger man Spartacus. Stevie then snap mares Conway to the mat to break the hold altogether and Stevie then comes off the ropes and dropkicks Conway in the back before he has a chance to get to his feet.
Ricky Simms - Good comeback for Stevie James here. Henry Lesher - Ask me if it will last. Go on. Ask me. Ricky Simms - I would if I cared. Stevie stands Spartacus up, whips him into the corner and then takes a thundering clothesline out of the corner from Conway. Conway then boot chokes Stevie James in the ropes, releasing him before the ref has a chance to count to five and call for the disqualification. Spartacus then stands Stevie up, whips him off the ropes and ducks his head for a backdrop only to have Stevie land a double axe handle across the back of the neck on him. Stevie then places his throat on the middle rope, comes off the ropes and lands a Ring the Bell on Conway. He then pulls Conway to the corner, jumps to the top rope and poses once for the crowd before landing the Stevie Splash on Conway and going for the pin. 1...2...3! Stevie James pulls off the win tonight over Spartacus Conway.
Ricky Simms - Looks to me like Stevie James won this match. Henry Lesher - Boy, nothing gets past you, Rick. Stevie rolls out of the ring and makes his way to the back, his arms raised in victory. We then look backstage and see ThunderStorme and Kate hanging out by the craft service table, as Gerard Angelo passes and stares at Thunder. Kate Redman is holding her coffee.
Thunder - What? Gerard Angelo - Just looking at a nothing. Thunder - A nothing huh? Thunder gets in his face. Gerard Angelo - You heard me. Marla Storme - Don't do this now, if this asshole is here, his "bed pals" are not too far behind.
Thunder - I don't mind. Gerard Angelo - You should. Gerard pushes Thunder hitting Kate, knocking her coffee on her. Thunder turns his attention to Kate. Gerard shakes his head and walks away laughing.
Marla Storme - Come on Kate, let's get you cleaned up. Dan Jalldoff vs Relic "Bound To Violence" by Hatebreed plays as Relic makes his way to the ring, getting much the same reaction as his tag team partner Slayer got earlier tonight. He is slow and methodical in his approach to the ring and doesn't look sideways at the crowd, his focus on the ring only.
Henry Lesher - I hear this new stable calls themselves The Masters. First off, they suck and are masters of nothing and second two of the four are so damned ugly they have to wear masks.
Ricky Simms - Kelly didn't hit you hard enough when she sat here. Henry Lesher - Kelly? She hits like a girl. Relic steps into the ring and tests the ropes as "Shut Your Mouth" by Pain plays and Dan Jalldoff quickly makes his way to the ring. He slides under the bottom rope and Relic starts putting the boots to him as the bell sounds to get this match going. Relic stands Dan up, whips him off the ropes and goes for a clothesline that misses when Dan ducks it. Dan then comes off the ropes and lands a flying cross body on Relic, knocking him down to the mat where Dan lands a series of punches to the face on Relic. Jalldoff then stands Relic up, whips him into the corner buckle and then gives him a series of forearms to the jaw that he follows up with a whip to the opposite buckle. Dan charges into the opposite buckle at Relic only to get a raised boot to the jaw from Relic that knocks him back down to the mat. Relic then goes for the pin on the dazed Dan Jalldoff. 1...2... kick out by Dan Jalldoff.
Ricky Simms - Dan coming in with a head of steam tonight. Henry Lesher - That's what it's full of all right, just like his father. Relic stands Dan up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a clothesline to the mat. He then picks Dan up and delivers a sidewalk slam on him before again trying for the pin. 1...2... Dan manages to roll a shoulder out. Relic then stands Dan Jalldoff up again and this time whips him through the ropes to the floor. Relic slowly steps out of the ring as Dan gets to his feet, clearly not certain where he is at this point of the match. Relic jumps down to the floor, grabs Dan by the hair and slams his head into the guard railing. Relic then pulls Dan to the ring steps and tries to slam his head into them only to take a series of elbows to the gut from Dan who blocks the steps from his face. Dan Jalldoff then slams Relic's head into the steel steps and rolls him back into the ring. Dan then hops up onto the apron and starts making his way to the top rope. He leaps off and lands a flying elbow smash across the throat of Relic and goes for the pin. 1...2... Relic manages to get a foot on the rope. Dan stands Relic up, whips him off the ropes and the two collide in the center of the ring. Relic then shoves Dan Jalldoff down to the mat. Relic then looks to the crowd who are booing him more as the match goes on here.
Henry Lesher - That's the right thing to do, idiots. Boo the guy beating the hell out of your favorite. That won't make him madder or anything.
Relic stands Dan up, then sets him for and delivers a Relic Bomb on him. Relic then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Relic pulls off the win here tonight over Dan Jalldoff in impressive fashion.
Henry Lesher - Looks like Dave's kid is a loser, too. Ricky Simms - I'll let him know you said hi. Tremendous win tonight by Relic. It'll be interesting to see how his career progresses here in the GWO.
Henry Lesher - No it won't. Backstage we see The Kumquat Kid entering the building. Fans seeing him on the big screens over the ring cheer as Shawna Miller approaches him for an interview.
Shawna Miller - Kid, you've been gone a couple of weeks and people were getting worried. Why are you here tonight?
Kumquat Kid - The sales of my fruit dropped, so they wanted me back. Xtasy walks into the frame. He doesn't look happy, looks over at Kumquat Kid. Xtasy - What is this? Kumquat Kid - I'm The Kumquat Kid and I'm back. Xtasy - I didn't miss you. Anyone seen Tony Parizi? Shawna Miller - It's my understanding that it's hard to track him down tonight. Xtasy - I won a lucrative commercial contract by defeating Frank Merritt and here I am weeks later and no commercial. *looks at Kumquat's feet* Your shoes bite.
Kumquat Kid - What the?... Xtasy walks off and Kumquat and Shawna exchange an awkward glance before Kumquat suddenly heads out the door again, ending this bizarre chain of events.
