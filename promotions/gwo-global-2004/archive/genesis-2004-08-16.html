GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

GWO Genesis, 16 August 2004

16 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chilliwack, BC Backstage we see Pyro in his locker room stretching when Max enters the picture. He looks Pyro up and down and laughs.
Pyro - What's so funny exactly? Max Cameron - Your new attitude. It's a joke. You're not convincing anyone. You've always been a loser and you always will be.
Pyro - You're all in for a rude awakening tonight. Max Cameron - Oh, I don't think so. Outside the arena, Dizz is wheeled inside which has some fans in the area shouting slanders of all sorts at Crystal Braun and her nearby father Hector. Hector glares at the nearby fans, but holds back from saying anything. The GWO is set to experience record attendance numbers tonight in Chilliwack as they are hosted at Prospera Place, a newly opened arena certain to put this community on the map, since it's too small and flat to be noticed by anyone else. Hector knows they are two weeks away from Rumors of war when he feels the big payoff will happen.
Inside the arena, Myst is at her husband's side, tonight as a manager. She sighs as she massages Shannon's shoulders.
Myst - I don't have a good feeling about tonight. Shannon Skanes - Keep a good thought, honey. Myst - I can't. I never should have hired Pyro to protect me from Max. Now you're the one paying for that.
Shannon Skanes - No, tonight he is the one paying. Elsewhere, Henry Lesher enters the building and finds himself face to face with Ricky Simms, another night working together for these two.
Henry Lesher - The card tonight makes better toilet paper than reading material. Ricky Simms - Positivity radiates from you, Doc. Henry Lesher - They don't pay me enough to be positive. They spot Reverend Darius and Vesuvius together talking in the back parking lot. Ricky Simms - Evil meeting to be sure. Henry Lesher - It has better be. I demand to be entertained. The audience makes their way inside and we are looking at a full house. Over 3,000 people have turned out for what will no doubt be a rocking show for the GWO. Pyrotechnics are becoming more elaborate every week now as they surge towards the big time. The four large screens again hang above the ring, set to show the fans close ups of in ring action and of events transpiring backstage. Some in the crowd are apparently upset with Johnny K-9 being scratched off the card, though that will apparently be addressed to start this night off. There is a lot of press here tonight to cover this for the local paper and for Vancouver new stations as the buzz grows about the newest wrestling league which is growing by leaps and bounds.
The show itself is about to start as ring announcer Shelley Morris heads into the ring to warm up the crowd and she hears a few catcalls as she steps through the ropes and takes hold of the microphone.
Shelley Morris - Tonight's event will be televised on Vancouver's Channel M, home of multicultural TV. Chilliwack is our gracious host tonight as we have many matches scheduled and we are bringing you only top notch GWO action. Two weeks from tonight will be the first ever GWO pay per view, titled Rumors of War and it has been decided that this event will come to you live from two venues! Half of the event will be from General Motors Place while the other half will be hosted at Pacific Coliseum as GWO tries something not seen since WWF's Wrestlemania 2. Though on a smaller scale, this promises to be a big event and payment can be made directly to Shaw cable for a 24.95 pay per view fee. Also, they have worked out a deal where you can cash in your air miles to see it for free. Call Shaw for more information and enjoy the show!
Shelley steps out of the ring and pyro shoots off which signals to those in their seats that the show has started and it causes many to hurry to their seats who had been dawdling. Lesher and Simms take their place at ringside and mumble to each other about the coming show, Lesher usually shaking his head and appearing ready to gag.
Soul Reaver vs Tex Murrphy "Pink Cigarette" by Mr. Bungle plays as Tex Murrphy makes his way to the ring. He receives a positive reaction from the capacity crowd here this week as he steps onto the apron and then into the ring. Soul Reaver is then announced and he comes out to a strong negative reaction here. He holds the Pacific title over his shoulder, very proud of the Pac Man imaged belt he has held for two weeks now. Soul Reaver looks into the ring at Tex Murrphy and laughs before stepping onto the apron and then through the ropes as the bell sounds to get this title defense underway.
Ricky Simms - Big title shot here for Tex. Henry Lesher - Yeah he wins Atari games if he wins this match. What an honor. Give me a break.
Tex and Soul Reaver lock up in the center of the ring. Soul Reaver then breaks that, placing Tex into a headlock and then whips him into the corner buckle before kneeing him in the stomach as he stumbles out of it. Reaver then places Tex into a side headlock as Tex gets to his feet, then whips Reaver into the opposite corner before giving him an atomic drop. Reaver then picks Tex up and gives him a spinning power slam to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tex Murrphy and the crowd cheers, starting a chant for Tex as Soul Reaver stands him up and again whips him hard into the turnbuckle. Tex then ducks a clothesline and gives Reaver a reverse neckbreaker before going for a surprise pin. 1...2... kick out by Soul Reaver. Tex then stands Reaver up, lands a series of knife edge chops on him which drive him into the corner and then monkey flips Reaver out of it.
Ricky Simms - Tex fighting hard here this week. Henry Lesher - The idiot detective wasn't even on last week. On a side note, rumor has it he may be associated with Dante which might put him in my good books. Now, if only someone could track down Q we'll be all set.
Tex whips Reaver off the ropes and both men clothesline each other to the mat. The ref starts counting both men out. Reaver starts moving at four and grabs the ropes. Tex is moving at six and pulls himself to his feet. Reaver is up first at the nine count and kicks Tex in the jaw. Tex lands on his back and looks to be out cold. Reaver smiles and goes for the pin on Tex. 1...2... Tex reverses it with a small package roll up. 1...2...3! Tex Murrphy with an unlikely win here this week!
Ricky Simms - We have a new Pac Champion! Henry Lesher - Yay, Tex gets Atari games and a belt with Pac Man on it. Tex holds the belt high and then we hear "Money" by Pink Floyd and out comes Hector Braun which draws boos from the crowd.
Hector Braun - This makes situations for me, Mister Murrphy. Along with congratulating you on this week's win and title capturing and such, I must inform you that you that you will now join Mister Reaver and Mister Matthews in our little ladder match for that title belt at Rumors of War. Goos luck to you, Tex. You're going to need it.
Hector leaves and Tex continues his celebration as we look backstage. There is Dizz in his wheelchair with Crystal Braun playing with his cheeks and smiling. Max Cameron enters the picture.
Max Cameron - I am tired of this sick game. Give Dizz back his dignity. Crystal Braun - Dignity? Dizz is a legend, Max. You should know that and legends should not be hidden away.
Max Cameron - He's not well and should be in a hospital. Crystal Braun - I am capable of taking care of him. That's what matters. Max walks away, leaving her there and looking ready to be ill. We then head to ringside where we hear "Schism" by Tool playing and Leech leads Vesuvius and Reverend Darius to the ring which draws a very surprised and mixed reaction from the crowd. Leech enters the ring first, flanked on either side by Vesuvius and the evil reverend.
Henry Lesher - My night just got better. Leech - Hello GWO and welcome to hell. You see, a couple of weeks ago, Shannon Skanes retired me and it was one helluva match. However, I decided to visit some vengeance on him from beyond my career's grave. I have joined The Parish as an advisor and manager. It was I who decided these two belong together, the GWO monster who will crush Slim Pickens and a man who will put that frilly little package Kitty in her place. I'll be at Rumors of War and for me, I didn't burn out or fade away. I just changed my direction. My targets remain the same, men who have faced me before and now will pay the price while I watch and laugh.
The Parish poses together and the three of them leave as "Enemies of Reality" by Nevermore plays. The crowd is giving more boos than they did when these three came out together, so they must not have liked what Leech had to say much. We then head to our first commercial.
We see Frank Merritt in front of a weather map with a smile on his face. "Let's see, we are a couple of days removed from Hurricane Charley ruining Florida's shit, first non pussy named Charley I have ever seen. Anyways, it's gonna be fucking hot out there and..." Frank is interrupted as an audio guy walks through the shot by accident. Frank starts wailing on him. "Fucking retard glory hound! Mess with my weather forecast, will you?" We then see the Channel M logo before we head back to the ring.
Tigre Blanco vs Jack Quinn Generic Mexican music plays as Tigre Blanco makes his way out from the back. He is half way down the aisle when he is attacked from behind by Jack Quinn. The fans cheer the attack, but also start a chant for Tigre right away. Blanco is rolled into the ring by Quinn who then grabs a steel chair from ringside.
Ricky Simms - Something snapped in Quinn here before this match. Henry Lesher - Yeah, but what? I mean, I like it. Don't get me wrong there. I just wonder what could make this guy snap like this. I never knew he had it in him.
Quinn steps into the ring and the bell sounds as he appears ready to smash Tigre Blanco over the head with the chair. Quinn has a sadistic smile on his face as he rears back to drill Blanco with the chair. From the back we see Wyatt Gibson storming into the ring. He attacks Quinn before he has a chance to nail Tigre and the belkl is called for. This match is declared a no contest. Wyatt and Jack brawl their way out of the ring, through the ropes and to the floor. As they brawl out there, Tigre gets to his feet and leans on the ropes as we see Crystal Braun with a mic in hand near the entrance.
Crystal Braun - Daddy's not impressed with this! Daddy hereby places Wyatt Gibson back on the active roster and Jack Quinn is suspended until further notice. Some of you boys just don't get it.
Apparently oblivious to what has been said, Jack and Wyatt continue to brawl outside the ring. Security then comes out in an effort to break the fight up. Crystal retreats to the back and Tigre Blanco shakes his head as he exits the ring. We then head backstage and we see Casey Braun there in a three piece suit and a black hat and the crowd boos her when they see her there.
Casey Braun - I love that the fans know who I am. That's so cool. I just wanted to announce that in two weeks at Rumors of War we are going to have the coolest matches to decide who is the world champion. Well, the semis happen at Rumors of War and the final match happens the week after that. Dirk Bailey will face Pyro and Max Cameron. Austin Fierro will face Devo and it was gonna be Shannon Skanes, but Daddy says he doesn't deserve the shot so it's gonna be Scavenger instead. Daddy also wants people to know Johnny K-9 was arrested last week and Gerard Angelo and Halloween Jack will be fighting for the TV title in a triple threat match against Tigre Blanco. Daddy must really like threes because every title is involved in a triple threat match. I think that's pretty neat. Well, the Canadian title won't be a triple threat, but it will be a lumberjack match. Those are fun to watch. Well, that's it for now.
Casey blows a kiss at the camera, winks and then we head back to the ring. Bailey then stands Darius up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Darius. Bailey then stands Darius up again, whips him off the ropes and Darius ducks a clothesline. Darius then comes off the ropes and shoulder blocks Bailey who stumbles, but does not go down. Darius then bulldogs Bailey to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dirk Bailey.

Darius stands Bailey up, whips him hard into the corner buckle and then charges in for a shoulder block that misses when Bailey steps out of the way. Bailey then grabs Darius and whips him hard into the nearby buckle shoulder first twice before setting him up for and delivering a pump handle slam on him. Bailey then stands Darius up, whips him off the ropes and lands a big boot to the jaw of Darius which sends him hard to the mat. Bailey then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out as Vesuvius gets up on the apron. Bailey has a few words with Vesuvius as the ref orders Vesuvius off the apron. Vesuvius does drop off the apron, but by now Darius is on his feet and he dropkicks Bailey in the back which sends him through the ropes to the floor. Leech jumps onto the apron and the ref goes to deal with him as Vesuvius puts the boots to Dirk Bailey outside the ring. The crowd boos as Vesuvius stands Bailey up and whips him head first into the steel corner post. He then rolls Bailey into the ring. Darius goes for the pin as Leech finally drops off the apron on the other side of the ring. 1...2... kick out by Dirk Bailey as Vesuvius slams his hands down on the ring steps in anger.
Henry Lesher - We need a real ref like Dizz out here to call things fair and square, a man who knows how to count to three.
Ricky Simms - I find myself in a somewhat unusual position of agreeing with Max Cameron that the idea of Dizz being out here is a sick one at best.
Henry Lesher - You never respected Dizz as the true legend he is. Reverend Darius pulls Dirk Bailey to the corner and starts to the top rope. He then dives off, landing a flying headbutt on Bailey before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out again by Dirk Bailey. Darius then stands Bailey up, whips him off the ropes and Vesuvius looks ready to pull the ropes down. The ref warns him about that as Darius whips Bailey to the opposite ropes and Leech pulls down the top rope and Bailey tumbles outside the ring as the crowd boos again. Leech grabs the nearby steel ring steps and drills Bailey in the back with them before rolling him back inside the ring. Darius heads onto the apron, looks skyward and then lands the Rebirth on Dirk and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Reverend Darius with a win here this week!
Henry Lesher - What a great win that was. Ricky Simms - Yeah, three on one, really nice odds for Dirk Bailey. Vesuvius and Leech enter the ring and smile at the fallen Dirk Bailey. Vesuvius asks the crowd if that's their monster. He smiles as he stands Bailey up and then lands a Rock Bottom on him. Leech starts putting the boots to him as we see Slim Pickens rushing in from the back. The crowd cheers as Slim enters the ring and smashes Leech in the forehead with his belt, then does the same to Vesuvius before Reverend Darius retreats from the ring. Darius is still smiling and has a few damning words for Slim Pickens as he, Vesuvius and Leech make their way to the back.
Henry Lesher - You see that? The Parish works well together, a very cohesive unit as any congregation should be in this day and age. They are inspiring to watch.
Ricky Simms - Give me a break. Thank God Slim came out here when he did or Bailey's career might have been over.
Henry Lesher - Thank God? He's not in Slim's corner. Ricky Simms - Men of God don't kidnap people and blow up cars. Henry Lesher - They do when God says to. Man, you think the Mob collects a hefty fee? This God guy collects more than they do and for eternity as well.
We then head to commercial. We see Tamara Taggert in Stanley Park for her weekly look at Vancouver events. As she speaks, we see teens fighting in the background. "Hi there. Scavenger vs Radd Matthews "My Girlfriend's Girlfriend" by Type O Negative plays as Scavenger makes his way to the ring and that brings the crowd to their feet.
