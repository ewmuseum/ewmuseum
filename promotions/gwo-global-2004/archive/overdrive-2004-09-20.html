GWO Overdrive, 20 September 2004

20 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seattle, WA The first ever cross Canada broadcast of a GWO show is going to be well attended in Seattle. It's obvious from the Canadian flags that a lot of people made the drive down from the border to see this, but there are also many here who have seen the GWO via their satellite systems. There's no doubting the growing public profile of the GWO. Backstage, however, Hector Braun sees little to celebrate. Dan Jalldoff approaches, about to become the victim of the boss's wrath.
Hector Braun - What do you do here exactly? Dan Jalldoff - Hey, chief, before you wig out on me, you haven't asked me to do anything yet. I am simply here collecting a paycheck every week the same as everyone else is.
Hector Braun - Tonight you work, my friend. If you fail in your task, you join Max Cameron and Dizz in the special Olympics.
Dan Jalldoff - All right, Hec. Don't get your panties in a bunch. Dan pushes past Hector with his girlfriend Sabrina close behind. Hector Braun - Where the hell have you been? Sabrina - Watching, boss man. Hector then heads for his office to worry there for awhile. We then head to ringside where the action is set to begin.
TJ Mercer vs Jessica Parizi "Regulator" by Warren G and Nate Dogg plays as TJ Mercer makes his way to the ring. The crowd has a mixed reaction to him as he makes his way down the aisle to the ring. He looks around at the near sold out crowd and then steps onto the apron and into the ring. He then tests the ropes as we hear "Power Trip" by Monster Magnet and Jessica Parizi makes her way to the ring with her twin sister leading the way. They are both clad in all black and close behind them walks Tony Parizi, the proud uncle of the two.
Henry Lesher - So Tony goes from protecting them from the ring to being the one leading them into a loss?
Ricky Simms - These two have been impressive. Henry Lesher - There are two of them? I had hoped I was seeing things. The bell sounds to mark the start of the match and the two of them lock up. TJ breaks the lock up, placing Jessica in a headlock. TJ then releases that and kicks her in the gut, then gives her a double axe handle across the back follows by a leg drop across the back of the neck of the fallen Parizi girl. He rolls her over for the pin. 1...2.... kick out by Jessica Parizi. TJ stands Jessica up as we see Tony Parizi at ringside shaking his head. TJ whips Jessica off the ropes and tries for a clothesline that misses when she ducks it. Jessica then kicks TJ in the gut, and follows that up with a DDT to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Nice turnabout by Jessica here. Henry Lesher - It would impress me, but it's TJ Mercer, so it doesn't impress me. Jessica rolls TJ over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by TJ Mercer as Tony Parizi slaps the apron, certain that should have ended the match. Jessica stands TJ up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him back to the mat. Jessica then heads to the ropes and starts to make her way to the top. Tony protests her going up and they starts to have words as she stands perched on the top rope. She totally misses seeing TJ getting to his feet. He comes over and shoves Jessica off the top and to the floor. The ref then pulls TJ back and has a few words with him as Jamie springs into action. Tony pulls Jessica away and Jamie lays down where Jessica fell. Jamie staggers to her feet and rolls herself inside the ring. TJ grabs her by the hair and then Jamie rolls him up for a cradle pin. 1...2...3! Jamie pulls off the win for her sister who is just now getting to her feet outside the ring.
Henry Lesher - They're Parizis all right. Ricky Simms - TJ dropped the ball there in not seeing that the switch had been made. Henry Lesher - Failure again for Mercer. The guy should realize by now he just isn't any good.
Jamie and Jessica leave ringside with their uncle close behind as he taunts TJ who is now realizing what just happened here. TJ shakes his head, drops and rolls out of the ring as we take a look backstage where The Moose is seen entering the building. He is just inside the door when Devo and Vesuvius attack him, both armed with chairs. Vesuvius drills Moose in the gut as Devo smashes him over the head. They then nod towards each other.
Devo - One down, one to go. Vesuvius - Yeah and we know where he'll be later, so this is almost too easy. Vesuvius and Devo then smash Moose over the head with the chairs before dropping them on him and leaving the area.
Ricky Simms - I don't like the looks of this. Henry Lesher - I'm a Leech fan, but I agree that Moose and Leech had better expect some form of revenge against them this week.
Myst/Rayn vs Sephiroth/Scavenger "Episode 29" by Mushroomhead plays as Sephiroth makes his way to the ring. The crowd has a strong positive reaction to him as he steps through the ropes and poses once for the camera. We then hear "My Girlfriend's Girlfriend" by Type O Negative and Scavenger comes out to a loud pop from the crowd. Scavenger has a chair in his right hand and wife Lauren following close behind him. He quickly enters the ring and has a few words with Sephiroth about last week and not letting that happen again. As they argue back and forth, we hear "Enemy" by Sevendust and Myst comes out to the loudest pop yet for anyone in this match. Myst stands outside the ring with her arms folded across her chest as Rayn comes out as well to a nice reaction from the crowd. Myst and Rayn enter the ring and start getting into a verbal argument here. The four of them then start trading blows as the bell sounds to get this match underway.

Sephiroth head butts Myst into the corner, then hip tosses her out to the center of the ring. Sephiroth then grabs the arm of the fallen Myst and leg drops her across the shoulder. Sephiroth then elbow smashes the shoulder of Myst and then places her in a hammerlock for an attempt at getting her to submit. The ref checks on Myst as we see Scavenger whip Rayn into the steel ring steps at ringside. Scavenger then takes his place in his corner and awaits the tag from Sephiroth and Rayn very slowly gets to his feet and makes his way to his corner. As the ref checks for a submission, Myst starts to get to her feet. Sephiroth grabs her by the hair and pulls her back down to the mat. The ref has a few words with Sephiroth about the hair pull as Scavenger calls for the tag. Sephiroth tightens his hold on Myst, but she manages to reach out with her free hand and grab the ropes to force the break.
Henry Lesher - Poor ring sense there. Ricky Simms - Myst forces the break. Sephiroth pulls Myst by the hair to his corner and tags in Scavenger. Scavenger enters the ring and clotheslines Myst to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Myst. Scavenger stands Myst up, whips her off the ropes and tries for a clothesline which Myst ducks. She gives Scavenger a dropkick to the mat and rolls to her corner and tags in Rayn. Rayn enters the ring and clotheslines Scavenger to the mat, then backdrops a charging Sephiroth to the mat as well. Rayn then leg drops Scavenger and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Scavenger. Rayn then stands Scavenger up, whips him off the ropes and gives Scavenger a sidewalk slam to the mat. Rayn then goes for the pin once again on Scavenger. 1...2... again Scavenger rolls a shoulder out before the three count. Sephiroth returns to his corner and shouts at Scavenger to get up. Rayn stands Scavenger up, whips him off the ropes and Myst knees Scav in the back. Rayn then lands a spinning neck breaker on Scavenger before tagging in Myst. Myst quickly enters the ring and slaps Scavenger in the Mystifyer before he has a chance to get up. Sephiroth shakes his head and drops off the apron and starts making his way to the back even as his partner fights to reach the ropes. Scavenger rolls to the ropes and manages to force the break. Scavenger then rolls to his corner to make the tag to a partner who is not there.
Ricky Simms - Sephiroth gave up on Scavenger here! Henry Lesher - That makes him smarter than the fans are. Scavenger shouts out to Sephiroth to no avail. Myst then knees Scavenger in the back, kicks him in the head and then places him in the Mystifyer again. This time, Scavenger wastes little time in tapping out. The bell sounds and Myst and Rayn celebrate a win as Scavenger rolls himself out of the ring and heads after Sephiroth to resume a conversation with his so-called partner.
Ricky Simms - Sephiroth abandoned his partner there. Henry Lesher - He knew when to abandon a sinking ship. We head to commercial. We see Frank Merritt looking at his watch and frowning. He then heads inside a McDonald's and makes his way to the front of the line, shoving people aside along the way. "I want breakfast." "Sorry, sir, breakfast was served until eleven. It is now eleven fifteen," the clerk says with a smile. Frank jumps over the counter, punches the clock right off the wall and then shows her his watch. "Look here! It's ten thirty! I get a sausage McMuffin or I do bad things to that idiot dancing in the Grimace suit out front." The clerk smiles an uneasy smile. "Well, why didn't you say so, Mister Merritt? Right away sir!" She rushes about to make Frank what he demanded as Frank smiles at the camera. "At McDonald's you have it your way. You just have to fight for it."
The Dawg vs Mordecai Darius "Who Let The Dawgs Out?" by BAHA MEN plays as The Dawg makes his way to the ring. However, he comes out already fighting tooth and nail with Mordecai Darius. Dawg picks Mordecai up and body slams him to the floor before boot choking him on the floor. Dawg then stands Darius up, pulls him to ringside and then rolls him into the ring. Dawg rolls himself into the ring and then starts raining down a series of punches to the head of Darius as the bell sounds to get this match officially started.

Darius then puts the boots to Dawg outside the ring and smiles as he stands him up and then starts choking him. Darius head butts The Dawg as he releases the choke and then rolls himself inside the ring. Darius releases the choke before the ref reaches the five count for a disqualification. Darius then stands The Dawg up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Darius then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He dives off for a frog splash that misses when Dawg rolls out of the way. Dawg then uses the ropes to get to his feet as we see Gerard Angelo making his way to the ring which brings a mixed response from the crowd. Dawg stands Darius up and places him in a bear hug as Gerard takes his place at ringside with his Playboy TV title over his shoulder. Dawg spots Gerard there and has a few words for him. Gerard motions Dawg over to him. Instead of taking the bait, Dawg tightens his grip on Darius as the ref checks for a submission. Darius reaches for the ropes as the crowd chants for The Dawg. Darius then low blows Dawg to break the bear hug and the ref spots that and calls for the bell.
Ricky Simms - Darius couldn't beat him, so he got himself disqualified. Henry Lesher - Oh he ended a brutally boring snore fest. Leave the guy alone. Darius lays a beating on Dawg, but takes a shot to the head from Angelo as he charges into the ring with the TV belt in hand. Angelo then spears Darius through the ropes to the floor. As he does so, he drops the belt on the mat. Dawg gets to his feet and grabs the belt and smiles as Angelo and Darius battle outside the ring. Dawg places the TV title over his shoulder and rolls out of the ring as Angelo whips Darius over the railing and into the front row of the crowd. Angelo then spots Dawg with his belt, picks up a chair and charges after Dawg and clubs him in the back of the head with it before grabbing the belt off of him. Angelo then spits on The Dawg and retreats to the back as Darius gets to his feet and starts after the TV champion.
Ricky Simms - Angelo starting some chaos here tonight. Henry Lesher - I might have cared at one point, but I really don't care right now. Backstage we see Vesuvius and Devo in Hector Braun's office. Hector Braun - So you two idiots club my main event tonight. I don't like that idiot Moose anymore than you two. Him and Leech and prancing their asses around with titles I don't recognize here. I am sick of CWA guys being out of control. I don't want to add Vesuvius to my list.
Vesuvius - We felt we had to take our shot, so we took it. Hector Braun - Where the hell are we? Top gun? No, you work for me and this is how things are done. Now I want you two to...
Hector doesn't get to finish his thought as Moose enters the room swinging a chair and drills Devo in the head and Vesuvius in the back before laying a beating on them both.
Hector Braun - Security! Get in here! Get them all out of here! Security guards enter the office and start dragging Vesuvius, Devo and Moose from the office.
Hector Braun - You idiots want to kill each other? Fine with me! At Security Breach, Vesuvius meets The Moose! Devo, you get a match with that paraplegic idiot Max Cameron! Winner is the first ever GWO world champ! I hope the four of you kill each other!
