GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

CWO Genesis, 26 July 2004

26 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 26, 2004 Osoyoos, BC This week's event looks to be well attended as a local high school gym is quite full. There are no announcers tonight. No, tongiht Hecotr has opted to change things up and this is one of those changes. There are rumors backstage that there is a TV deal being struck with a local Vancouver TV station. There are rumors that the show will be broadcast in Cantonese, though most wrestlers backstage doubt that.
Hector Braun makes his way to the ring before the event has officially started as he fully intends to clear up the confusion. He announces something big.
"Tonight is a big night. The CWO is soon going to become the Global Wrestling Organization. I am going to be running a pretty large show because I have made a pretty large purchase. I am owning not only the CWO, but I will soon own the CWA, the same CWA that has been owned by Shannon Skanes and Myst. I am going to take what Q couldn't. I am going to take total control."
Hector is interrupted when "Deep" by Nine Inch Nails starts to play and the crowd rises to its feet as Shannon Skanes makes his way into the ring. He even steps up onto the second rope and looks out to the crowd, appearing to be in good shape and good spirits. He then jumps down and grabs the microphone from Hector.
"Hector, you plan to buy CWA from me? Take the damned thing! It has become a monster beyond my control and you are welcome to it. What I need to know is who is behind you in all of this. You're simply not a man of enough means to accomplish such a feat alone. I have run the CWA for over two decades and have repelled many outsiders along the way. What exactly is really going on here? The fans want to know and I want to know."
Hector grabs the microphone back. "Mister Skanes, what I am doing and who is helping me is for me to know. You will know answers when I am ready to provide them to you. Until then, you're in the dark with all of these fans here."
Shannon then drops Hector with a stiff right to the chin. Security guards rush into the ring and pull Shannon out as Hector gets to his feet rubbing his chin. Hector did manage not to drop the microphone.
"Shannon! You want to hit me? Next week you enter this ring and wrestle then! Next week you see how life is when you don't run the show!"
Shannon is pulled to the back and Hector makes his way to the back. They then announce that the event starts now and the first match to be viewed is Aladdin Sane against Tex Murrphy. Aladdin comes to the ring first and the crowd cheers him on. Aladdin straddles the guard rail and starts his hump dance. There are fans who pick up their chairs and start humping them. Yeah, this gimmick might need to die. Aladdin then enters the ring and smiles to the crowd as Tex Murrphy makes his way to the ring to a very mixed reaction.
The match itself is rather quick, thanks to the ever present refereeing of Adam West. Adam comes in wearing a Batman costume colored black and white to match a referee's outfit. Adam continuously is on Aladdin for his outfit and how wrong it truly is. Tex wins the match via submission with a Sharpshooter. Tex then leaves the ring and again sees a woman in the crowd that he believes is that woman he has been searching for. However, before he gets far into the crowd, he realizes that this is a false alarm and is left pacing for a bit before he heads to the back. Adam West drags Aladdin to the back by his earlobe chastising him and telling him how he is about to teach him a lesson.
What the fans don't see backstage is Hector offering Adam West a lot of money for a one time appearance against Aladdin at a later date. Adam West offers no answer right now, one of the few times he has ever been left speechless.
The next match is announced and it is Evil Eye against Sunshine Mathews. The bell sounds and Evil Eye choke slams Sunshine which impresses the crowd. The match doesn't go on for too long, though as Slim Pickens charges into the ring and makes the save and puts Evil Eye out of the ring with a vicious clothesline over the top rope. Slim then helps Sunshine out as Evil Eye appears to be livid at ringside over all of this. This might be the start of a feud here.
Backstage Evil Eye is berated by Wyatt Gibson for a stiff chokeslam on Sunshine and for not letting up a little and revealing that is why Slim was sent to the ring to put him out. Evil Eye is then fired by Hector Braun who then tells Wyatt to let him make the decisions.
Match number three is Chester Firefiend against Stalker who is still a mystery. Chester has a hard time getting into the ring for the fact that he doesn't believe it is actually his turn yet. Once the bell sounds, both men execute high flying moves and get near pinfalls. Chester grabs the win via pinfall with a sunset flip roll up and the crowd appears to be supporting this guy who has had little to say in the past couple of weeks.
Backstage both wrestlers are received well, but Hector reveals that next week there will be a special match involving Pyro and Gerard Angelo here so there's no telling who will or won't be booked in the coming week. This brings about a lot of angry looks and silence backstage and we wonder just how much worse things might get as this takeover progresses.
Tigre Blanco fights Johnny K-9 next. This match goes well for both, with Johnny using his raw power and Tigre showing us all why he is the Rey Mysterio of the CWO. A beautiful 619 by Tigre is followed by a kick out by Johnny who then power bombs Tigre Blanco. Johnny K-9 is all set to finish this when Xtasy appears from the back and distracts K-9 and Tigre scores the win with a roll up from behind. Tigre takes off from the ring as Xtasy enters the ring and brawls with Johnny K-9, much to the delight of the fans. Xtasy and Johnny battle their way out of the ring and to the backstage area yet again.
Hector Braun hails the feud between Xtasy and Johnny K-9 as being the best feud to this point and says the others in the back here can learn from them on the proper way to develop a feud.
At ringside, fans notice that Shelia Lavin is there. She signs a few autographs and appears to be rather warm considering her usual demeanor. We then move onto our next match which is Dirk Bailey against Olivia Maine against Jack Quinn. The match starts with Jack and Dirk going at it tooth and nail with Olivia hitting a hurricarana on Quinn. That costs her as Dirk gives her a pump handle slams and then splashes her on the mat. Dirk then goes to the top rope and Jack pulls him off of there, tossing him to the mat. Jack then lands a rolling thunder on Olivia and manages to pin her for the three count. Dirk Bailey isn't impressed with someone else getting the three count and he whips Jack over the top rope to the floor. Dirk then appears ready to exterminate the still fallen Olivia when Chastity and Corina emerge through the crowd and pull Olivia from the ring and escape before Dirk can get to them.
Dirk comes backstage and finds a happy Hector Braun who shakes hands with Dirk and praises him as the top heel in the company. Dirk responds by stating he is no heel and then the lady Dirk has been seen with has a heated exchange with Hector. Hector apparently refused to allow her to walk Dirk to the ring because he felt it weakened Dirk's status as a bad man.
The next match of the night features Kosmic Kitty against Zack McCracken and Xtasy which is natural since Xtasy just finished running into and ruining Johnny K-9's match. Xtasy is the first one out and gets a resounding ovation. Kosmic comes out next with Zack making his way to the ring last, all three of them getting a warm reception. The match itself is a high paced entertaining match between the three as Zack finally shows some skill inside the ring. Kosmic nearly scores the pin on Xtasy with a lionsault. Zack nearly scores a pin on Kosmic with a nice vertical suplex into a cradle pin. Xtasy gains himself a near win with a springboard bulldog that brings the fans to their feet. The ending winds up with Kosmic landing the boo boo kitty on Zack while Johnny K-9 comes out to renew pleasantries with Xtasy. Kosmic gains the win. Xtasy and Johnny brawl with chairs. The winners are the fans. Zack is the only clear failure in this match and Felicity chastizes him all the way to the back for his inability to win.
Backstage Shannon Skanes introduces himself to the roster and says he is looking forward to working with them. He then brings in Pyro and Gerard Angelo and says they will be wrestling against each other next week in a big match. Some of the guys ask where Chastity and Corina are, but Shannon doesn't know.
Slim Pickens is against Thunder next and the fans appears to be really backing Slim tonight. Marla Storme is pacing at ringside as the match gets started. Thunder and Slim exchange body slams and power moves. Thunder lands a leg drop to the throat of Slim for a two count. Slim then lands an avalanche in the corner on Thunder before DDTing him to the mat. Slim scores the pinfall win over Thunder with a second rope splash on him. Marla Storme isn't impressed as Slim makes his way out victorious yet again. It is announced that Slim will be in a title match next week, though the fans are not told for which title.
Backstage Marla is introduced to Shannon Skanes. Marla is told that Shannon might have some clues as to the whereabouts of her brother, but she doesn't have the courage to ask him straight out. Celia is seen backstage and appears to be quite out of it. Wyatt is close by, but doesn't appear to be exactly tending to her. It's a bizarre scene to be sure.
The main event is next as Radd Matthews, Devon Blade and Wyatt Gibson take on Devo, Reverend Darius and Soul Reaver in a six man tag team match up. The bell rings and the brawl is on. There's never really a time where the ref has complete control over the match. There is simply a time when there might only be two wrestlers inside the ring and then the ref watches them while the other four battle outside the ring. Radd and Soul Reaver have the most brutal exchanges in the center of the ring. Reaver repeatedly chokes Radd on the ropes before Radd manages to tag in Wyatt. Wyatt takes control for a few minutes, but Reaver tags in Devo and that reverses momentum again. Devo controls Wyatt until he winds up in the wrong corner and Devin Blade enters the action and takes down Devo with a spear. All the way along the fans are taken on a roller coaster ride of emotions as they watch the battle unfold within the squared circle. Devo tags in Darius and Darius takes it to Devin Blade. The match finally ends in a DQ when Radd Matthews enters the ring and takes a chair shot from Reaver who then tries to set fire to Radd Matthews before Wyatt and Devin make the save and the ring is cleared by security.
Backstage the roster is congratulated by Hector Braun who tells them that next week's event will be televised on Channel 9 in Vancouver. He also informs them that they will receive two hundred dollars per promo starting this week and two hundred dollars per match. He also informs them that CWA wrestlers will receive the exact same wage as CWO guys have and that concludes his meeting. Clearly, CWA guys won't be happy about their pay being cut down to a maximum of eight hundred dollars a week.
That's it for this week. Next week is the start of a new chapter for the CWO as it becomes the GWO.
