GWO Maximum Impact, 1 November 2004

1 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
San Francisco, CA We see Carl Simms in the middle of a crowd with a smile on his face. Judging by the costumed people around him, it would appear he is at a GWO halloween party.
Carl Simms - GWO fans in Sacramento were just witnesses to the first ever halloween event and what an event it was. GWO champion Myst teamed with hubby Shannon Skanes and defeated Leech and Moose with a little help from my good old dad. Halloween Jack defeated Austin Fierro in a non title match. Devo took out Jon Shocker. Shengee Shemlky and Ingee Shmelky were reunited and proved to be better than Mnoose for a win over Bob. Lethal Weapon was a surprising partner for Stevie James as they defeated Scavenger and Fixxxer. Here around me everyone is in costume except for Halloween Jack who has been trying to collect a cover chanrge on his holiday. As he gets more drunk, it apparently also becomes his birthday. All of this on the eve of the biggest GWO pay per view yet! Stay right there! You don't want to miss a thing.
Halloween Jack stumbles into the shot and puts his arm around Carl. Halloween Jack - On my birthday, won't you support us in our hour of need? Carl Simms - You're drunk. Halloween Jack - You think I'm bad? Lesher's been making out with what he calls the best looking drag queen he's ever seen. Joke's on him! Wait till he finds out it's a real woman!
We hear a man screaming off camera. Halloween Jack - Sounds like he solved the mystery. Now, I don't like the look of your cheap face.
Jack takes a swing at Carl and then face plants onto the floor. Carl simply shakes his head and walks off as we begin our show. We hear "Home" by Three Days Grace as we see images of The Authority, Moose, Leech, Stevie James, The Dying Breed, Youthful Aggression, Slim Pickens, Eddie Vines, Kosmic Kitty, Radd Matthews, Rated R, Jack Spade and Ricky Simms before we head to ringside where Henry Lesher and Carl Simms are set to call the action.
Henry Lesher - For the record, that was not me screaming you heard. Carl Simms - Apparently it wasn't. Word has it that people were charging their drinks at the party to Austin Fierro and that scream was him being presented his tab total for the night. What happened with your date?
Henry Lesher - I shudder recalling it, so let's just skip it. Carl Simms - Lesher got hot and heavy with a woman. Henry Lesher - I said to skip it. Boomshalkalaka vs Pyro "Hooch" by Sum 41 plays as we await Boomshalakalaka from the back. He comes out with a hold on Pyro by the hair. He starts pulling Pyro to the ring which appears to have the crowd cheering him on at this point. Pyro, however, starts punching Boom in the ribs and then gives him an atomic drop before bulldogging him to the floor. Pyro then turns and has a few words for the crowd before standing Boom up and rolling him into the ring. Pyro then rolls himself into the ring and the bell sounds to get the match officially underway.
Carl Simms - First match of the night here as Boom found Pyro in the back and didn't want to wait for their match.
Henry Lesher - These two got into a fight at the party last night over who could choke worse. It was a draw. It was the lowlight of the party and sent a lot of people home.
Pyro places Boom into a front facelock. Boom quickly grabs onto the ropes, but Pyro is hesitant about letting go of the hold. He finally does, and Boom rolls himself out of the ring to get his bearings early on here. Pyro doesn't wait for him, instead launching himself through the ropes and onto Boom, knocking him to the floor where Pyro then rains down punches to the head of Boom. Pyro then stands Boom up and rolls him into the ring again. Pyro quickly enters the ring as well and then stands Boom up, whips him off the ropes and then dropkicks him to the mat. Pyro then tries for a leg drop only to find no one home as Boom rolls out of the way. Boom then uses the ropes to quickly get to his feet and starts putting the boots to Pyro before he has got all the way to his feet. Boom then whips Pyro off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Boom then points to the ropes and starts making his way to the top. Once there, he poses once and executes a shooting star press which Pyro is able to roll out of the way of.
Carl Simms - If Boom had landed that, it would have been over. Henry Lesher - Which would have made me happy. Damn him for not making me happy. Pyro then opts to roll out of the ring to take a breather as Boom is winded inside the ring. Pyro then makes his way around the ring, pulls the timekeeper out of his chair and picks that chair up, folds it and slides it into the ring. Pyro then slides himself into the ring, gets to his feet and finds himself in an argument with the referee about the presence of the chair. Boom then chop blocks Pyro to his knees and follows that with a DDT. Boom rolls Pyro over for the pin. 1...2... Pyro manages to roll a shoulder out. Boom stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a clothesline to the mat where he strikes the back of his head on the chair. Boom then knee drops Pyro to the forehead, Pyro's head still resting on the chair. The ref then kicks the chair out of the ring, allowing the action to continue as it wasn't Boom who brought the chair into the ring. Boom than picks Pyro up and body slams him to the mat near the corner. Boom then takes to the ropes, makes his way to the top and lands a shooting star press that Pyro doesn't roll out of the way of this time. Boom then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Boomshalkalaka defeats Pyro!
Henry Lesher - Looks like Pyro wins the choking argument after all. I'm sure this is a proud moment for his parents from whichever correctional facilities they currently reside.
Carl Simms - You're awful. Big win for Boom and... he has the mic. Boomshalkalaka - Tonight I did what I said I would. Next week, I demand a shot at the hardcore title! If I don't get it, this will be the first of many men I cause to bleed in this ring.
Carl Simms - So Boom wants a shot at the hardcore title. Henry Lesher - I don't see how a match between him and Leech will solve anything. Carl Simms - No one has the hardcore title yet. Don't assume it will be Leech. Henry Lesher - I can assume all I like. Backstage we see Joe Davidson entering the arena. He is headed off by Leech. Leech - Well if it isn't GI Joe. Joe Davidson - Back off, Leech. Leech - *smiles* Or what? Dave Jalldoff walks in the door behind Joe. Dave Jalldoff - You heard him. Back off. Leech - Must be nice for you guys, getting ten percent discounts on everything. Just stay out of my business, Joe. Do your damned job.
Joe Davidson - *nods* Same as I do every week, Leech. Ricky Simms vs Rod Gordon "Overrated" by Three Days Grace plays as Ricky Simms makes his way to the ring and the crowd gets to their feet for this entrance. He high fives fans on his way to the ring, makes his way onto the apron and gives a thumbs up to his son at ringside who gives the same back to his father. Ricky then enters the ring, steps up onto the second rope and poses for the cheering crowd of people. Ricky then jumps down and tests the ropes as we hear "Diamonds and Guns" by The Transplants and Rod Gordon starts making his way to the ring. Rod hears a moxed reaction from the crowd and tell the cameraman he is about to make his team two for two tonight. Rod steps onto the apron and has a few words with Ricky who simply shakes his head and motions Rod into the ring. Rod steps through the ropes and the bell sounds. The two then lock up in the middle of the ring, a lock up broken when Rod knees Ricky in the gut. Rod then gives Ricky a series of knife edge chops which drive him into the corner buckle. Rod then attempts to whips Ricky into the opposite buckle, but Ricky reverses it and Rod hits the corner hard, then takes a German suplex from Ricky into the middle of the ring.
Carl Simms - Good start for Dad here. Henry Lesher - Yeah and they call me biased. Rick goes for the pin on Rod. 1...2... Rod manages to roll a shoulder out in time. Ricky stands Rod up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Ricky then lands an elbow smash to the throat on Rod and again goes for the pin. 1...2... Rod reverses the pin into a small package. 1...2... Ricky manages to kick out and then pulls himself to his feet quickly. Ricky then grabs Rod by the hair. Rod lands a jawbreaker on Ricky and then goes for a quick pin himself. 1...2... Ricky manages a kick out as neither man is quite able to put the other away here. Rid stands Ricky up and whips him through the ropes to the floor. He then smiles as he looks out at the mostly booing crowd and he gives them the finger. Rod then steps out through the ropes onto the apron, poses once and dives into a bearhug Ricky quickly turns into a spinebuster on the floor. The crowd starts a Ricky chant as Ricky stands Rod up and whips him into the steel corner post.
Carl Simms - Classic. My father never gets respect outside the ring, yet always managed to take care of himself out there.
Henry Lesher - I find it odd that your father is teaming with Joe Davidson, the man who ended his career.
Carl Simms - Ancient history. Henry Lesher - Yes, your father is that. Ricky rolls Rod into the ring and then quickly rolls himself in as well. Ricky stands Rod up, whips him off the ropes, Rod reverses, whips Ricky off the ropes and gives him a tilt a whirl backbreaker to the mat. Rod then stands Ricky up, whips him into the corner and charges in shoulder first only to find no one home as Ricky moves out of the way, then delivers a pump handle slam on Rod folloed by a standing somersault leg drop. Ricky then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Ricky Simms gains a victory here this week. Ricky then rolls himself out of the ring as Rod pulls himself to his feet. Rod then turns to the ref and has a few words with him about his ability to count to three properly. The ref states his case and Rod answers with a kick to the groin on the ref before he leaves the ring, the crowd booing loudly here.
Henry Lesher - Maybe this Rod guy isn't so bad. Carl Simms - Yeah, what a great guy he is. I wonder what punishment he'll get for that. Backstage we see Lethal Weapon entering the building. The crowd cheers when they see him and he comes across Kosmic Kitty's sister Jamie backstage.
Lethal Weapon - Jamie, just wanted you to know I have no hard feelings for you playing my music a couple of weeks ago.
Jamie - Well, that's good. Lethal Weapon - Maybe we can discuss it over dinner sometime. Jamie - Well, I don't know about that. Weapon strokes her chin and moves on as Jamie blushes. Jack Spade vs Thunder "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays as Marla Storme leads Thunder to the ring. Thunder receives a mixed response from this crowd tonight as he and Marla discuss matters on their way to the ring. Thunder steps onto the apron and then over the ropes. Thunder then poses on the second rope as we hear "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne and the light go out for a few moments before we see a sptlight on the entranceway and Alicia is then seen leading Jack Spade making his way to the ring. The crowd cheers as he and Alicia high five the crowd and play up their entrance. Jack then jumps onto the apron and poses there once before Thunder clotheslines him off of it to end the celebration. Thunder then reaches through the ropes and pulls Jack into the ring and the bell sounds to get this match started. Thunder stands Jack up, whips him off the ropes and delivers a huge double axe handle across Jack's chest, then headbutts him to the mat. Thunder follows this up by climbing to the second rope, then drops a scissor kick across the back of Jack's neck from there and goes for the quick pin attempt. 1...2... kick out by Jack Spade as the crowd cheers and Alicia applauds.
Henry Lesher - How does this qualify for a pay per view match? I mean, people are expected to pay for this garbage?
Carl Simms - The GWO is anticipating the strongest buy rate ever tonight. Henry Lesher - That is because people are awaiting Leech. It is all about Leech. Thunder stands Spade up, whips him off the ropes and then clotheslines him to the mat. Thunder then picks Spade up and gives him a running powerslam in the corner before he again takes to the ropes. He leaps from the second rope for a knee drop, but instead takes a boot to the chin from Jack Spade who then kips up to his feet. Jack then elbow drops Thunder to the back of the neck, then places him in a crossface chicken wing. The fans cheer as the ref checks for a submission that isn't coming here. Thunder reaches for the ropes and Jack Spade struggles to pull Thunder more towards the center of the ring. Thunder then lunges with all he has and manages to grab the bottom rope. The ref forces the break as Jack shakes his head. Jack then grabs Thunder by the hair, pulls him to his feet and takes a thumb to the eye from Thunder. Thunder then shoves Jack into the corner and gives him a series of kicks that knock him off his feet. Thunder follows that up with a two handed choke in the corner which he breaks before the ref has a chance to reach the five count and DQ Thunder.
Henry Lesher - Thunder needs to show more tenacity like this every week. Carl Simms - Thunder may be many things, but I know of few who have ever seen him as cautious in the ring.
Thunder pulls Jack Spade by the hair to the middle of the ring, then headbutts him to the mat. Thunder then comes off the ropes, measures Jack for and delivers a right hook to the skull before again choking Spade on the mat. He releases the choke with his hands, then steps one foot onto the throat of Spade as the ref shouts that he's had enough of that. Thunder raises his foot, kicks Jack in the face, then heads to the ropes. He makes his way to the second rope where he stays perched as Jack stands. Jack turns as Thunder launches from the ropes. Jack ducks a clothesline and raises a knee into the gut of Thunder. Jack then lands a standing Train Wreck dropkick on Thunder and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Jack Spade with what could be seen as a lucky win here as he rolls himself out of the ring. Jack receives adulation from the fans as he also gets a hug from Alicia and heads for the back. Thunder gets to his feet, looks out at the crowd and then turns to Marla and has a few words with her.
Carl Simms - Thunder is not impressed. Henry Lesher - And why should he be? Spade gets a lucky win here. Carl Simms - A little luck never hurt. Impressive appearance for both wrestlers tonight. Backstage we see Stevie James with a fan, signing an autograph. Stevie James - So I make this out to?... Fan - Rose. Stevie James - What a perfect name for a beautiful flower such as yourself. He winks at her, causing her to blush, signs his autograph and then watches her round the corner. His smile fades when Radd then rounds the same corner, coming towards him.
Stevie James - What do you want? Radd Matthews - Tonight I take Rated R's title. Tonight you had better hope Kitty is unharmed because I will be watching.
Stevie James - Keep your eyes on your match, Radd, and I'll keep my eyes on mine. Radd Matthews - *stands nose to nose with Stevie* Don't dismiss me, Stevie. Don't piss me off. Kitty is mine.
Stevie James - From what I understand, it's all fair game if you lose tonight. Radd shakes his head and leaves as Stevie laughs to himself. The crowd sounds disappointed at there being no physical confrontation here.
Kosmic Kitty/Stevie James vs Mordecai Darius/T-Rex "Can't Get the Best of Me" by Cypress Hill plays as T-Rex and Mordecai Darius make their way to the ring. They receive loud boos as Mercedes and Lexus lead the way. T-Rex tells the camera there ain't no damned way twinkies are gonna get the best of him this week. Darius holds a picture of Kitty in his hand and tears it to pieces half way to the ring, then wipes his feet on the remains of it before continuing his trek to the ring. They step into the ring and don't speak to each other, showing a severe lack of communication here this week as Darius appears to be in prayer and T-Rex is having words with his girls about their job tonight and doing it right.
Carl Simms - It'll be interesting to see how these two work together tonight. Henry Lesher - For you, maybe, not interesting to me at all. Get me to Leech. We then hear "Scandalous" and Kosmic Kitty starts making her way to the ring tossing Twinkies and Payday bars to the crowd with one hand and blowing kisses with the other as her basket rests on her arm. She empties the basket and then hands it to a little girl in the front row who has a blonde wig on to go with her Kosmic Kitty T-shirt. Kitty pinches the girl's cheek and then turns to face the two hostiles inside the ring. We then hear "Open Road" by Bryan Adams and Stevie James makes his way to the ring which brings more cheers from the crowd. Stevie and Kitty stand side by side as Darius and T-Rex have words for them. The ref holds them both back and Stevie tells Kitty he'll start the match. She nods and lowers her head and we wonder if she's blushing.
Henry Lesher - What is this? Young and the Restless? Get on with wrestling! Carl Simms - Why don't you leave like you did last week? Henry Lesher - Shut up. Stevie steps into the ring and the bell sounds as T-Rex nominates himself to start this match off. Stevie and Rex lock up in the middle of the ring. Rex breaks the lock up and places Stevie in a headlock. Stevie then backs Rex into the ropes and whips him off the ropes. Rex shoulder blocks Stevie down, then comes off the ropes, leap frogs Stevie and then comes off the ropes again and falls to a monkey flip from Stevie. Stevie then gets to his feet quickly and motions Rex to him as Rex nods. Rex charges and ends up on the floor as Stevie throws him through them. Stevie then lands a springboard body press over the top rope to the floor on Rex before he has even had a chance to get all the way to his feet. Stevie then grabs T-Rex by the hair and slams his head into the steel steps in the corner. Rex responds with a kick to the front of the knee on Stevie. Rex then rolls himself into the ring, bleeding from the forehead. Stevie then follows, clearly favoring the knee. Stevie wastes no time in tagging in Kosmic Kitty as T-Rex lunges and tags in Darius. Darius enters the ring and smiles when he sees Kitty standing there before him.
Henry Lesher - Maybe tonight Darius can finally end this Kitty's career once and for all. Carl Simms - Don't be so sure. She has proven herself to be resilient when she needs to be.
Darius and Kitty lock up in the center of the ring as the fans start a chant for Kitty. T-Rex orders his girls to flash the crowd to stop the chant, but they seem hesitant to do as he says. T-Rex shakes his head as we see Darius kicking Kitty in the gut in the ring and then Kitty responds with a knee of her own to the gut on Darius. Kitty then gives Darius a belly to belly suplex. Kitty follows that up by standing over Darius and then giving him a standing somersault that winds him. Kitty then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Mordecai Darius. Kitty stands Darius up, whips him off the ropes and then backdrops him to the mat as the crowd cheers. She tells Darius she isn't afraid of him anymore and tags in Stevie James who quickly makes his way to the top rope. Stevie then lands a flying elbow smash to the throat of the evil reverend and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Stevie James and Kosmic Kitty anre victorious here! Stevie gets to his feet to celebrate only to have T-Rex chop block him to the mat. Rex then takes a dropkick from Kitty that sends him rolling out of the ring.
Carl Simms - The teams of Stevie and Kitty win. Henry Lesher - And those of us who value wrestling over drama lose and lose big here. Kitty asks Stevie if he is okay as Stevie cradles his sore knee. We then see Radd Matthews making his way to the ring and not looking impressed. He rolls into the ring and tells Kitty she has done her job and now she should go. Kitty appears to hesitate, but backs away from Stevie. Kitty then follows Radd away as Stevie rolls himself out of the ring and starts to make his way out. Darius is then seen getting to his feet and appearing to be in shock as to what just occurred. On his way out of the ring, he takes the time to curse a few fans. It's the least he can do.
Carl Simms - Darius needs to lighten up. Henry Lesher - If you were just pinned by Joanie loves Chachi you'd be pissed too. Backstage we see Tony Parizi and Guido in Tony's office when Lethal Weapon enters with a smile on his face.
Lethal Weapon - Nice place you have heer, all nice and neat. You know, I have been away for awhile and I couldn't help passing by and thinking about how I was screwed out of title shots and screwed over by the very people running the show now. It means it's time to send a message.
Weapon draws a baseball bat from behind him and starts trashing the office as Tony shouts out for security and Guido backs off. Security guards race into the room and restrain Weapon and Tony shakes his head.
Tony Parizi - Next week, welcome back to the ring Weapon. You want a fight with management? You got it buddy. We'll make you wish you were dead.
Security drags Weapon out of the office and then we head back to the ring. Hellfire Jack vs The Moose vs Gerard Angelo "Hell Song" by Sum 41 plays and Gerard Angelo starts making his way out to the boos of the crowd. Moose then whips Angelo into the opposite corner and then clotheslines him to the mat as he comes out of it.
