GWO Overdrive, 6 December 2004

6 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Calgary, AB Our show begins with Zack McCracken backstage making his way to the curtain with Felicity close behind. They are both wearing tuxedos tonight as we fade from them into our opening credits. We hear "Breathe" by Swollen Members as images of Lethal Weapon, The Authority, Dying Breed, Youthful Aggression and Xtasy flash across the screen before we hear The Pink Panther Theme and out walks Zack McCracken. He has a half smile on his face as he walks confidently to the ring.
Henry Lesher - Last week's wonderful accident has been dashed with this idiot being put in charge of the investigation. I would find it funny if he wasn't such a pain in the ass.
Kelly Buchanan - Thus begins my week in purgatory, better known as the seat next to the foul doctor.
Zack steps into the ring and takes the microphone from Shelley Morris. Zack McCracken - Last week, someone tried to kill the woman known as Myst, the CWA and GWO legend, the queen of wrestling controversy. I am going to find out who and why. I am going to find out by ruling people out. At the end of tonight's show, I will give you a list of ten possibilities and why they are possibilities. Tonight, you shall see what a great sleuth Zack McCracken really is.
Henry Lesher - I hope he gets locked in a broom closet and left there. Zack leaves the ring and, as he heads to the back, we take a look backstage and find Noah Skanes entering the arena which brings the crowd to its feet. He is headed off by Sabrina Davidson.
Sabrina Davidson - Did you hear about Zack's plans for tonight? Noah Skanes - Of course, Dad and I told him we wanted ten names tonight. Sabrina Davidson - Are you okay? Noah Skanes - Yeah, just anxious to kick someone's sorry ass tonight. He makes his way passed her and she follows him down the hallway. Dizz vs Xtasy "X Gonna Give It To Ya" by DMX plays as Xtasy comes out to a decent ovation from the crowd, clad in and Xtasy T-shirt and carrying Xtasy posters and flags that he hands to lucky fans in the front row on his way to the ring. He high fives a few fans at ringside as he finishes emptying his arms and then steps up onto the apron, the official commercial spokeman for the GWO. As he steps into the ring, we hear "Just Lose It" by Eminem. Dizz comes out pushing the wheelchair he once rode in and waves to all of his adoring fans on his way to the ring. He then looks into the ring at Xtasy who hasn't taken his eyes off of Dizz once the whole way to the ring. Dizz then jumps up onto the apron, then steps through the ropes and the bell sounds as these two popular wrestlers square off. They lock up. Dizz breaks it up with an arm bar on Xtasy. Xtasy reverses it. Dizz lands a knife edge chop to the chest of Xtasy, whips him off the ropes and Xtasy shoulder blocks Dizz to the mat and the misses with a leg drop as Dizz rolls out of the way.
Kelly Buchanan - Impressive start to this match up. Henry Lesher - Oh goodie, Kelly is impressed. Kelly Buchanan - And Lesher is again thinking people give a damn what he thinks. Xtasy and Dizz get to their feet and lock up again. Dizz shoves Xtasy into the corner buckle and then monkey flips him back to the center of the ring where he then applies a sleeperhold. Xtasy thinks fast, elbowing Dizz in the ribs a few times before snap maring him to the center of the ring. Xtasy then stands Dizz up, whips him into the corner and then hip tosses him out of it to the corner of the ring. Xtasy then picks Dizz up and power slams him to the mat and then tries for the pin. 1...2... Dizz manages to kick out. Xtasy then stands Dizz up, whips him into the corner, knees him in the gut a few times and then gives him a snap suplex to the center of the ring. Xtasy then lands a leg drop to the throat on Dizz and again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dizz again. Xtasy then stands Dizz up, whips him to the ropes and Dizz grabs the ropes and pulls himself out of the ring under the bottom rope. Xtasy then slides out of the ring after him and the two start trading punches on the floor.
Kelly Buchanan - Xtasy trying to maintain control here. Henry Lesher - Dizz isn't nearly the man he was when he couldn't walk. Dizz rams Xtasy's head into the guard railing, then whips him into the apron, chest first. Dizz then rolls Xtasy into the ring and gives the crowd a knowing smile. He rolls himself into the ring and goes for the pin on Xtasy. 1...2... Xtasy reverses it into a small package 1...2...3! Xtasy grabs the win and rolls out of the ring to the floor and bows once to the crowd.
Kelly Buchanan - Big win for Xtasy over a former world champion! Henry Lesher - Dizz is not a man anymore. Sad to say it, but it has to be said. Sinners Inc. saw the champion Dizz. EOS saw the popular Dizz. Now we have loner, loser Dizz.
Kelly Buchanan - Give the guy a break! He was in a wheelchair! Henry Lesher - And was more entertaining there than he has been since he returned to the ring!
Backstage we see Leech lacing up his boots as he readies for action here tonight. As he does so, Razorblade enters the room. Leech spots him and then the two stand nose to nose.
Razorblade - I don't like being blindsided. Leech - This kind of thing just happens. Razorblade - Stay out of my hair and I'll stay out of yours. Leech - So that's a warning then? Razorblade - Hey, you got my point the first time I made it. Stay out of my way. Razor turns and leaves and Leech starts to smile, shaking his head. That leads us to believe this battle has just begun.
Shmelky Brothers vs Beyond Control "Blue" by Eiffel 65 plays as The Shmelky Brothers make their way to the ring. The crowd cheers them on. Adam West trails behind them, all three men high fiving fans as they continue on to the ring.
Henry Lesher - Why didn't I stay home tonight? Kelly Buchanan - We have all wondered that about you at one time or another. As the two Shmelkys try to negotiate the ropes, we hear "Just Lose It" by Eminem. The arena then goes dark. The music is abruptly turned off. We hear the opening guitar for "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC. The ring ropes suddenly burst into flames and the crowd cheers for a few moments, though no one is sure what is going to happen here tonight. When the lights do come back up, the music again abruptly stops. Inside the ring, lying motionless, are Jake Evans and Matt Classic. Shengee thinks fast, rushing over for the pin. The ref looks baffled, calls for the bell and then counts the pin. 1...2... The lights go out again. This time there is no music, only flaming ropes that ringside crews are trying to put out. A few moments later, the lights are back on and the Shmelky Brothers are nowhere to be found.
Henry Lesher - God must like me tonight, sparing us that awful match. Kelly Buchanan - This appears to have been the work of Thunder and perhaps Marla Storme. It'll be interesting to see how the tag champs respond.
Henry Lesher - You're assuming they're going to get up. Backstage we see Tony Parizi watching the action with Big Guido standing behind him. Tony Parizi - All this melodrama crap. If the tag champs wanted shots, they could have asked me. I would be hurt if I cared about the tag champs.
Big Guido - Whatcha gonna do boss? Tony Parizi - Only thing I can do, give Thunder Storme tag team title shots next week. Shelia Lavin then bursts into Tony's office. Shelia Lavin - Tony, someone attacked Hellfire Jack in his dressing room. Tony Parizi - Who? Shelia Lavin - Frank Merritt. Looks bad too. Tony and Guido follows Shelia out of the office and down the hallway to Hellfire Jack's locker room. The camera crew doesn't go inside, but we see a trail of blood from the door in. Tony looks inside and winces.
Tony Parizi - Okay, I have seen enough. Tony Parizi and Guido then turn from that room and continue down the hall as paramedics arrive with a gurney and rush past the two Mafia men down towards the fallen Hellfire Jack. Tony bursts into Frank's dressing room where they find Austin Fierro and Frank Merritt drinking beer and laughing.
Tony Parizi - So this is how it's gonna be with Frank on the show? Last week you got yourself disqualified. This week you destroy the TV champ!
Frank Merritt - Match was non title. That made it clear to me I had to make a statement. Tony Parizi - Well, it's clear I have got to make a statement as well then, isn't it? Frank Merritt - This should be good. *chuckles* Tony Parizi - Frank, you're suspended indefinitely. Austin, your little group here has destroyed two matches now.
Frank Merritt - I'm Flock, not Dying Breed. Tony Parizi - Like anyone can tell the difference. Austin, if anything dirty goes on in your match, you'll pay the consequences tonight.
Austin Fierro - *laughs* I'll take my chances. Tony and Guido shake their heads and leave the locker room. Big Guido - What are you gonna do with Hellfire in that state? Tony Parizi - I don't have much choice. He won't be competing for awhile, so I am gonna have to strip him of the TV Title and put it on the line next week in a battle royale.
The crowd cheers when they hear that news. Robbie Watson vs Zack McCracken "Beautiful Stranger" by Madonna plays as Felicity leads Zack McCracken to the ring. Zack steps up onto the apron and smiles out at the crowd. He then tells the camera this is his big moment for this week, the first of two. He has a little chat with the ref about the weather as "Vertigo" by U2 plays and out comes Robbie Watson. Zack smiles and waits for his opponent, leaning on the top rope as Felicity tells Zack to smarten up and take this seriously.
Kelly Buchanan - Felicity is giving good advice here, but Zack doesn't appear to be heeding it.
Henry Lesher - He probably needs help getting dressed in the mornings. Robbie Watson steps onto the apron, then through the ropes and offers to shake hands with Zack. The bell sounds and Zack shakes hands with Robbie. A moment later, as Zack smiles, Robbie clotheslines him to the mat. Robbie Watson then stands Zack up, whips him hard into the corner and then gives him a sidewalk slam out of it again before going for the pin. 1...2... Zack manages to roll a shoulder out in time. Robbie stands Zack up, whips him off the ropes and Zack ducks a clothesline, then gives Robbie a side Russian leg sweep to the mat followed by a Crippler Crossface. Zack seems to be in amazement at having locked this hold in as the fans cheer him on. Felicity applauds at ringside as the ref asks Watson if he submits. Robbie Watson refuses to submit here and reaches for the ropes. Wayson lunges and manages to grab the ropes on his second attempt.
Henry Lesher - So much for dopey's chances. Kelly Buchanan - I think he's doing a marvelous job of learning how to wrestle. Henry Lesher - He's been in the ring for six months now. He should have at least mastered one hold by now.
Zack stands Robbie Watson up, whips him into the corner and then charges straight into raised knees to the face from Watson. Watson then quickly climbs to the second rope, then leaps off with a double axe handle to the chest of Zack McCracken, sending him falling to the mat. Watson then places Zack into the Watson Bomb, a modofied Sharpshooter and Zack is forced to tap out immediately. Watson quickly gets to his feet, a smile on his face. He then exits the ring as Felicity enters the ring to attend to Zack. As she does, we see Rated R sliding into the ring. He has a microphone in hand.
Rated R - Another loss for the supersleuth. There's a shock! Listen, Zack and listen good! When you read those ten names tonight, if my name is on your little list, it's going to be you and me inside a GWO ring very soon.
He then drops the microphone, grabs Felicity by the hair and pulls her to her feet. He then starts shouting in her face as the fans boo his actions. The crowd reaction changes when they spot Carl Simms and Noah Skanes racing into the ring. Rated R bids a hasty retreat after shoving Felicity to the mat. Noah shouts at the retreating Rated R as Carl checks on Felicity to make sure she's all right as Zack comes to and realizes something has been said to him.
Henry Lesher - That kid always ruins a good show. Kelly Buchanan - I think it's good to see guys like Rated R are not able to get away with that kind of crap.
Backstage we see Crystal Braun walking towards her dressing room when Wyatt Gibson heads her off.
Crystal Braun - Hey, Wyatt, what's up? Wyatt Gibson - A lot apparently. How long have you and Casey known about your father screwing up the audio in the truck? How many people did he pay off to make the GWO look bad?
Crystal Braun - My father did what? Wyatt Gibson - Look, I don't know why he targetted Kitty's sister Jamie, but if I see him again, he'll be under arrest for tresspassing. Your family lost. Shannon got his company back and I still have a job. Deal with it.
Wyatt walks away as Crystal watches him go, her eyes narrowed. She then continues into her dressing room, shaking her head. We're pretty sure we see Hector Braun in there as the door closes soon after.
Sal Clemenzo vs Scavenger "Halls of Fear" by Nina Rota plays as Sal Clemenzo comes out to a solid ovation. The former world champion of the CWA has a decent following in old CWA territories and Calgary is no different. Sal steps onto the apron and looks out at the crowd and then steps into the ring. For a popular guy, Sal doesn't show any warmth or affection to the fans. It's business in there for him and always has been. Sal then looks up the aisle as "My Girlfriend's Girlfriend" by Type O Negative plays and out comes Scavenger, led by his wife Lauren Strong. They get the loudest ovation of any so far tonight. Scavenger has a few words with Sal as soon as he reaches ringside. Scavenger jumps onto the apron, enters the ring and starts throwing rights and lefts with Sal right away as the bell sounds to get this match underway.
Kelly Buchanan - They're not wasting any time tonight. Henry Lesher - Sal was better before he went soft. Kelly Buchanan - Scavenger is apparently missing his chair tonight. Henry Lesher - He's been missing a brain for years, never stopped him from coming out here to lose.
Scavenger starts gaining the ipper hand on Sal with a series of right hooks to the jaw, then lands a haymaker on Sal that sends him over the top rope and to the floor as the crowd cheers. Scavenger then heads out of the ring after Sal, grabs him by the hair and slams his head into the apron. Sal answers then with an elbow to the gut on Scavenger, then slams his head into the steel steps before rolling him back into the ring before they both get counted out. Sal rolls himself into the ring and starts raining down punches to the head of Scavenger, then two handed chokes him near the ropes. He breaks the choke on the ref's order, but darts a glare at him as a warning. Sal then stands Scavenger up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Sal then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He leaps off and tries for a legdrop from there, but Scavenger rolls out of the way at the last possible second. Scavenger then quickly gets to his feet, then starts putting the boots to Sal Clemenzo before he has a chance to get up. Scavenger then stands Sal up, whips him hard into the corner buckle and then shoulder blocks him in the corner.
Kelly Buchanan - Scavenger firmly in control here. Henry Lesher - Don't worry your stupid head, Kelly. He'll blow it here. Scavenger whips Sal into the opposite turnbuckle, then gives him a clothesline to the mat as he stumbles out of the corner. Scavenger then goes for the pin. 1...2... Sal manages to roll a shoulder out to keep the match going. Scavenger then stands Sal up, whips him off the ropes and takes a thumb to the eye from Sal who then DDTs Scavenger to the mat and quickly rolls him over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Scavenger. Sal turns and again glares at the ref and this time tells him he had better learn to count to three or learn to swim with cement on his feet. Sal stands Scavenger up, whips him into the corner and then kicks him in the chest repeatedly until he falls face down to the mat. Sal then pulls Scavenger towards the middle of the ring and rolls him over. He barks another warning to the ref as he climbs to the top rope. Sal then lands a flying elbow smash to the throat of Scavenger and then quickly places him into an Indossi di indossi Lock. The ref immediately calls for the bell, though it isn't clear that Scavenger actually gave up there.
Henry Lesher - Sal knew how to beat Scavenger. He showed up. Kelly Buchanan - Intimidating the ref didn't hurt his chances either. Henry Lesher - I didn't see that. Kelly Buchanan - Of course you didn't. Backstage we see Hector Braun emerging from Crystal's dressing room, looking both ways to make sure no one sees him. He then turns around, starts for the exit, opens the door and finds Shannon Skanes entering the building.
Hector Braun - Shannon.... I.... I.... Shannon Skanes - *cheerful* Hector, my man, good to see you! How's the CWO doing? Hector Braun - CWO? But you... Shannon Skanes - Yeah it sure is nice having some competition. Well, gonna come in and visit with the talent. Hope you don't mind.
Shannon pats Hector on the shoulder and whistles to himself as he enters the arena. Hector is only perplexed for a moment before he quickly exits the premises. It's clear something has snapped within Shannon's memory.
