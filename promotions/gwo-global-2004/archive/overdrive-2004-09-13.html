GWO Overdrive, 13 September 2004

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Edmonton, AB It's going to be close to capacity this week in Edmonton as the crowd files into the building. Backstage Hector Braun is pacing a hole in the floor. He has heard rumours all week about what Shannon Skanes might be doing and he needs to know what's going on. Dan Jalldoff enters the picture with a smile on his face.
Dan Jalldoff - How goes it, boss? Hector Braun - As well as can be expected. I need to know what Shannon is up to. Dan Jalldoff - Don't worry, Hec. I am sure it's nothing. Hector shakes his head and makes his way to his office still scowling and clearly concerned. We then take a look at the arena where the fans are getting seated and we see that Ricky Simms is back this week to take his spot at the side of hated Henry Lesher. The wrestlers backstage are anxious for the show to get started and it starts with a flash of fire from the stage and "Come With Me" by Puff Daddy playing which brings the crowd to its feet. Out comes Shannon Skanes led by Tony Parizi and Cordelia Parizi. Close behind are Wyatt Gibson, Myst and the Stryker Sisters. The rest take their places at ringside as Shannon steps into the ring.
Shannon Skanes - Now, here I am knowing full well that Hector Braun and his two whore daughters are sweating it out in the back. See, I have decided that I am going to run the show because that is what I am meant to do and Hector Braun is going to take a big fall. As you can see, I have called upon the Parizi family and we are in the midst of making things right. Soon, Hector, your little empire here will belong to me. I am going to do to you what you did to me. The difference is you killed what I had and I am going to make what you have much much bigger than it is right now. Have yourself a good day.
"Come With Me" by Puff Daddy plays again as the Parizis and company leave the ring to another ovation from the crowd. We then hear "Shut Your Mouth" by Pain and our show begins. Credits roll this week with images of Dizz in a wheelchair, Max Cameron, Pyro, The Moose and Slim Pickens before we head to ringside where Simms and Lesher have assumed their usual places at ringside.
Henry Lesher - Thank God that's over. I am very glad to announce the Brauns are far too smart to fall into any trap that idiot sets. He had his chance to run the show and he failed. It is time for someone new to run things.
"Walk Over You" by AC/DC plays and the crowd gets to its feet cheering. Hector Braun comes out laughing and behind him are Max Cameron and Dizz, both wheelchair bound.
Hector Braun - That will always be funny to me. You idiots think he's going to come back? Priceless, really priceless reaction. Now, regardless of what Mister Parizi and his bitches want to tell you, I run this show and our first match will illustrate that. Off to work with you, Max!
TJ Mercer vs Myst "Regulator" by Warren G and Nate Dogg plays as TJ Mercer comes out to the ring already shaking his head. He steps past Hector Braun, Dizz and Crystal Braun and heads into the ring after giving Casey Braun a dirty look as she wheels a still dazed Max Cameron, leaving us to wonder how much medication he might be on this week to leave him like this. "A Place For My Head" by Linkin Park plays and out comes Myst to a huge ovation before she steps by the Brauns and Dizz with a few unkind words for Dizz and Hector as she passes by. She then makes her way to the ring where she steps onto the apron and looks around at the cheering crowd. There's no telling why they are cheering her when she hasn't been doing anything to warrant that treatment recently. She steps through the ropes and the bell sounds.
Henry Lesher - With that bell, my night in heaven is assured. Violence and gore will fill the arena.
Ricky Simms - No, a week off didn't help. You're already started up. The two lock up in the center of the ring. TJ places Myst in a headlock. Myst then backs TJ into the ropes and whips him across the ring and then takes a shoulder block from TJ which knocks her down. TJ then leg drops Myst and goes for the early pin. 1...2... kick out by Myst. TJ then stands Myst up and whips her through the ropes to the floor where she then rolls and crashes her shoulder into the railing. The crowd appears to be behind Myst for this one as TJ steps out of the ring and jumps down to the floor. He starts putting the boots to her, kicking her now tenderized shoulder repeatedly before standing her up and whipping her shoulder first into the ring steps. TJ stands Myst up and rolls her into the ring before entering the ring himself. TJ then places Myst into a wrist lock submission hold. Myst reaches with her free arm and grabs the ropes.
Henry Lesher - TJ isn't thinking hardcore here. He needs to get a crow bar and take that to her shoulder.
Ricky Simms - The views of Henry Lesher do not reflect those of myself or anyone within the company. I apologize to mankind and God for this abomination.
Henry Lesher - People wish they were me. Myst gets to her feet grabbing at her shoulder as she tries to shake her arm to get feeling back into it. She looks to lock up with TJ, then kicks him below the belt and brings him to his knees. Myst then kicks TJ straight in the jaw be3fore rolling herself out of the ring, still favouring her shoulder. Myst lifts the apron and looks under the ring. She brings up a trash can as TJ Mercer rolls himself out of the ring to the floor. Myst turns and crowns TJ with the trash can and then dropkicks him in the chest which drives him into the steel corner post where it drill the back of his head. TJ falls in pain as Myst is still shaking that one arm he was working on.
Henry Lesher - At least she is thinking hardcore. Myst stands TJ up and whips him towards the ring steps, but stops when Casey wheels Max in between them. Myst then turns and starts to whip TJ towards the steel ring steps, but J reverses it and Myst's sore shoulder bounces off those steps yet again as she screams in pain. TJ then stands Myst up and rolls her into the ring. TJ enters the ring, places Myst's arm over the bottom rope and knee drops it. He then places her in a hammerlock, face down on the mat as a chant starts for her. Myst brings her foot up and low blows TJ with it. Myst then rolls over, throwing TJ off of her. She then slowly gets to her feet, again grabbing her arm in pain. Myst then rolls TJ over and locks in the Mystifyer even as she screams in pain from the strain the hold is putting on her sore shoulder. TJ starts tapping and Casey Braun tilts her head at ringside watching as if deciding whether or not to allow the match to end here.
Ricky Simms - Ring the bell! The match is over! He tapped! Henry Lesher - Max isn't seeing it that way and neither is Casey. You're outvoted. Myst releases the hold, aware TJ is tapping out and rolls herself out of the ring and gets into an argument with Casey Braun who keeps shrugging and saying she didn't see a tap. TJ rolls himself out of the ring and grabs a chair from ringside and folds it and walks up behind Myst with a sadistic smile on his face. He sets and fires, but Myst sidesteps it and TJ clubs wheelchair bound Max over the head with the chair which causes the fans to groan as the chair contorts around Max's head. J recoils in horror, dropping the chair and stepping back, right into a German suplex into a roll up pin fall by Myst. 1...2...3! Myst scores the win as Max's hand is slapped on the apron three times by Casey Braun.
Henry Lesher - Max is a better ref than he ever was as a wrestler. Ricky Simms - Nice grammar, Doc. That was sick. There's a place reserved in hell for people like Hector Braun for this garbage.
Henry Lesher - The throne. Backstage we see Celia in the dressing room tying her boots as she readies for her match with The Moose. Leech enters wearing a referee shirt.
Celia - Where the hell have you been? Leech - Don't fret Celia. Tonight The Moose learns a lesson from The Parish. I've got your back.
Leech pats her on the shoulder and whistles to himself as he leaves the locker room. Vesuvius enters the picture and shares an uncertain glance with Celia. We then head back to the ring.
Hellfire Jack vs Pyro The theme from Magnificent Seven starts to play and out comes Hellfire Jack to a decent pop from the crowd. As he makes his way to the ring and high fives a few fans, he is attacked from behind by Pyro who tosses him over the guard railing as the bell sounds to make this match official.
Henry Lesher - I like Pyro's new attitude. Ricky Simms - Yes, being a coward seems to be something you would appreciate. Pyro picks up a chair meaning to smash Hellfire Jack over the head with it, but Jack grabs Pyro around the throat and gives him a choke slam onto a row of chairs next to them. Jack then lands a series of forearm smashes to the chin of Pyro as the fans count them off. He then pulls Pyro off the chairs and whips him back down into the guard railing which Pyro strikes shoulder first. Hellfire Jack then stands Pyro up and then snap mares him over the guard railing back into the aisle. Hellfire Jack climbs onto the guard railing and then drills Pyro in the back with a double axe handle before he has a chance to get all the way to his feet. Jack then grabs a nearby audio cord and wraps it around Pyro's throat. Pyro chokes, grabs the audio cable, takes it and whips Jack in the face with it to break the choke. Pyro then rolls away from Jack towards ringside. Pyro crawls on his hands and knees to the ring, reaches under the apron and pulls out a fire extinguisher. Jack stumbles towards Pyro and gets a face full of fire retardant.
Henry Lesher - Did he put the fire out? Get it? Ricky Simms - You're not funny. Pyro stands and wields the fire extinguisher as a club, but Jack ducks it and kicks Pyro in the gut. Jack then head butts Pyro to his knees, then kicks him in the chest before grabbing the fire extinguisher away from him and clubbing him in the forehead with it. Jack then rolls Pyro over on the floor and goes for the pin. 1...2.... kick out by Pyro. Jack then stands Pyro up and rolls him inside the ring. Jack reaches under the ring and pulls out a ladder. He then slides it inside the ring before looking under the apron again and pulling out a kendo stick. Jack rolls into the ring and whips Pyro across the back with the kendo stick as the fans cheer.
Ricky Simms - Jack impressive in his debut here tonight. Henry Lesher - Pyro will win it, though. I can't see this hick taking him down. Jack puts the boots to Pyro, then stands the ladder up in the corner after dragging Pyro to the center of the ring. Hellfire Jack climbs the ladder to the top, then dives off aiming the kendo stick at the fallen Pyro. Pyro rolls out of the way and the kendo stick bounces off the mat and strikes Jack between the eyes, leaving him lying in a heap face down on the mat. Pyro uses the nearby ladder to get to his feet with a smile on his face. He grabs the kendo stick and starts striking Hellfire Jack across the back with it. He then heads to the ladder and starts making his way to the top. Hellfire Jack stirs. Pyro perches himself on top and Jack grabs the bottom of the ladder and Pyro topples off the ladder, over the ropes and even over the guard railing and into the quickly parting crowd blocked off by security.
Ricky Simms - Dear God, I think Jack broke Pyro in half! Henry Lesher - I may be a Pyro fan, but that looked pretty cool. Hellfire Jack leaves the ring and heads to the guard railing and steps over it. He picks up a chair and slams it across the forehead of a very groggy and bloody Pyro. Hellfire Jack then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! The ref raises Hellfire Jack's hand in victory as EMTs make their way out to attend to the fallen Pyro. Backstage we see Myst watching and smiling as they set up a stretcher and Pyro's face is covered in blood.
Ricky Simms - Pyro will be feeling the effects of this for a long time. Two matches in and I am sick already.
Henry Lesher - That is because you don't have the stomach to watch real men fight. We head to commercial. Frank Merritt is reading the newspaper with a frown on his face. He shakes his head as he faces us. "Today's finances get me down. Everyone seems to be fighting to get money that just isn't there. Allow me to introduce you to the Frank Merritt Retirement Plan." He gets up calmly from his booth in a coffee shop and grabs a man by the scruff of his neck. "Give me twenty dollars or your head will be tenderized." The man gives Frank twenty bucks, so Frank lets him go and the man runs off. Frank takes a seat and shows us the money. "You see, with the Frank Merritt Retirement Plan, I take care of me. Take care of yourself. Tell others to fuck themselves and then you can be happy like me." Outside the window we see Xtasy passing by with his head poking through the sunroof of a limo. "Fucking X," he growls as he shakes his head and starts reading his paper again.
The Moose vs Celia Heads Explode by Monster Magnet. Blasts in the arena as Celia makes her way out from the back.She's without any help from the Parish, and set to go one on one with The Moose.
Ricky Simms - Celia may not have the rest of the Parish around, but the one she does have could make quite an impact.
Henry Lesher - You mean Leech? He's only the ref Rick. Celia enters the ring as "H" by tool hits the PA system. Smoke fills the entrance, and Moose walks through slapping the hands of fans near the entranceway. He has a large black leather bag in his hands with him. Moose slaps more hands halfway down to the ring, and then sprints the rest. He slides under the bottom rope and spears Celia, dropping the bag in the corner of the ring. Celia hits hard as Moose stands up and laughs as though it was nothing.
Ricky Simms - Well the match should be underway, but Leech isn't here yet. Henry Lesher - Maybe he got my letter. Ricky Simms - What? Henry Lesher - It's a personal issue Rick. Celia gets to her feet, and jumps on the back of Moose trying to choke him out, as Moose starts to stagger around. Moose grabs onto Celia's head and flips her over his back, making a loud thud as her body hits the mat.
Ricky Simms - I wonder what's in the black bag Moose carried out here? Henry Lesher - Unless it's Leech all hacked up, I really don't care. Moose rolls out of the ring, and looks under it as Celia climbs to her feet. She bounces off the ropes on the far side of the ring and leaps over the ropes on the other side colliding with Moose who was reaching for a table. They both hit the ground hard.
Ricky Simms - This match didn't take long to get going, but it would be nice to have a ref out here.
Henry Lesher - Who cares about a ref, it's Leech I want to see. Celia gets to her feet first and finishes setting up the table that Moose grabbed from under the ring. Celia attempts to pick up Moose and put him on the table but he counters with a fireman's carry making Celia land on it herself. Moose gets to his feet and rolls into the ring.
Ricky Simms - Someone is going to go through that table. Henry Lesher - Nice observation Ricky, I thought it was tea time... Moose climbs the turnbuckles but Celia rolls off of the table, and underneath it. Moose just laughs and jumps down on the table anyway in hope of pinning Celia under it. Half-way through the air, Moose gets hit right in the face with a steel chair sending him backwards landing on the mat on the outside. Leech out of nowhere just nailed Moose with a chair.
Ricky Simms - It's a good thing Leech showed up, or Celia may have been crushed! Henry Lesher - .... Ricky Simms - Are you drooling Lesher? Henry Lesher - What's it to you?! Celia comes out from under the table to see Leech and smiles. Leech picks up Moose and rolls him into the ring. Celia follows them on in and climbs the turnbuckles. Leech stands next to the fallen Moose as Celia turns around to hit a moonsault. With her back turned Leech pulls Moose out of the way as Celia dives. Moose grabs a chair that was concealed under his stomach and leaves it in his place. Celia moonsaults nothing but the chair and the ring canvas.
Ricky Simms - Why did Leech do that?! Henry Lesher - Maybe he thought she needed to sit down? Celia looks up and Leech runs over and clotheslines Moose. He then returns and helps Celia to her feet. Leech and Celia have a few words with each other and don't even notice Moose approaching with another chair. Moose runs up to hit Leech, and Leech pulls the Celia in front of him, making Moose hit Celia right in the face with the Chair. Leech spears Moose to the ground again and helps Celia back up.
Ricky Simms - I really don't know what's going on here. It seems as if Leech is turning on the Parish, but he's hitting Moose, as well.
Henry Lesher - Rick, it's obvious who he's with.... me. Ricky Simms - Huh? Leech starts laying in punches on Moose as Celia staggers to her feet. She walks over to Leech and Moose and starts stomping Moose for good measure. Leech picks up Moose and holds him, as Celia delivers a dropkick to the stomach of Moose. Leech holds on again and has Celia repeat the process, making Moose fall to the canvas of the ring. Celia climbs the turnbuckles again as Leech rolls to the outside grabbing a baseball bat, and a cheese grater from under the ring.
Ricky Simms - What the hell is Leech going to do with a Cheese grater? Henry Lesher - Well I doubt he's going to use it for it's normal purpose. Celia leaps off the turnbuckles and hits a huge flying leg drop across the throat of The Moose, as Leech rolls into the ring with his toys. Leech signals for Celia to pick up Moose and she does so. Celia holds Moose as Leech drops the bat and holds the grater tightly. He swings his arm towards the Moose who ducks at the last second making the grater scrape across the face of Celia tearing her face open. Leech throws down the grater with his mouth open as he walks over to the fallen Celia. Moose takes Leech down with a Clothesline and starts stomping him repeatedly.
Ricky Simms - Leech just slashed open Celia's face, was that intentional or an accident? Henry Lesher - Well, we may never know, but did you see the blood?! That was great! Moose continues his assault on Leech, as Celia grabs the dropped grater, while she lays on the ground. Moose turns his attention to Celia, and goes for a falling head butt but Celia won't have any of it. She holds up the grater turning Moose's face into a bloody mess.
Ricky Simms - Oh, that was hideous! Henry Lesher - If they only made Polaroids for memories. Celia covers Moose but Leech is still on the mat. Celia yells to Leech but he doesn't move. She gets to her feet, and stomps Moose in the head, and places the cheese grater on his head. She walks over and picks up the bat that Leech brought into the ring and lines it up to the grater on Leech's face. She swings downward and crushes the grater on the face of Moose, and makes another cover, Leech still down to not make the count.
Ricky Simms - Celia might have had this match won, but Leech is down! Henry Lesher - He'll get back up again, after all I'm at ringside. Ricky Simms - Your humour never fails to disgust me Lesher. Celia walks over to Leech and helps him to his feet, and Leech staggers into the corner where Moose placed his black bag. Celia Irish whips Moose into the ropes, but Moose reverses it sending Celia to them instead. Leech quickly picks up the black bag and swings it, nailing Celia in the face with it as he approached the ropes. Celia falls to the mat completely un-conscious. Leech and Moose start laughing as Moose grabs the bag and removes a heavy duty towing chain from it, and throws it aside. He then produces two title belts.
Ricky Simms - I've seen those belts before... Henry Lesher - He had me at hello... Leech straps on one of the belts as Moose places on the other. They then turn their sites towards Celia. Moose picks her up as Leech walks to the far end of the ring where he stands waiting Moose's move. Moose Irish whips her to Leech and Leech hits a huge back body drop that sends Celia through the table on the outside of the ring, that was set up in the beginning of the match.
Ricky Simms - Oh my god! Henry Lesher - That was one of the best thing's I've ever seen. Leech and Moose laugh, and slide under the ropes of the ring as Vesuvius and Devo run out from the back. Devo and Vesuvius stand over the fallen Celia as Leech and Moose make haste through the crowd. Leech wearing the CWA Chaos World Title, and Moose wearing the Commonwealth.
Ricky Simms - So it was a setup! I wonder what's going on with the belts though? The CWA is done.
Henry Lesher - Yes, but Leech sure sent a message here tonight. I really hopes he likes my flowers.
Ricky Simms - I thought you a big fan of The Parish! Henry Lesher - Whatever. Backstage we see Shannon shaking his head as Ray Dees steps into the picture. Ray Dees - A moment of your time? Shannon Skanes - Make it quick. Ray Dees - What can you tell us of your efforts to take over the GWO? Shannon Skanes - Nothing, next. Ray Dees - Any reaction to Leech leaving the Parish and uniting with Moose? Shannon Skanes - Doesn't surprise me in the least. The man turns against everyone in the end.
Ray Dees - You weren't booked for a match tonight. Why is that? Shannon Skanes - Ask big boss Hector. Shannon leaves Ray standing there alone. Ray looks at the camera and shrugs before we head back to the ring.
Reverend Darius vs Wyatt Gibson "Somebody Told Me" by The Killers plays as Wyatt Gibson comes out, but he's not coming to the ring as he would intend to. Instead, he is unconscious and strapped to a block of wood. His forehead is bleeding as the reverend pushes him out on a gurney. The crowd boos as he takes Wyatt off the gurney and slides him inside the ring. He then steps onto the apron and shakes his head as he enters the ring. He grabs a microphone from the ring announcer and addresses the crowd.
Reverend Darius - Simpletons! Evil people are here tonight! I always knew Edmonton was evil! *loud boos "Asshole" chant starts* That's right. Chant your way into hell. Tonight I am sacrificing Wyatt Gibson. I am sacrificing him because television has brought me to this. Television is the root of all evil. This place has a television champion and tonight, with this public sacrifice, I give myself to the destruction of he who carries the television title. Tonight, this is on the heads of Gerard Angelo and the powers that be who hand out titles celebrating television.
Darius pulls a match out of his knee pad and lights it and looks down on Wyatt. He smiles as he readies to drop the match only to have Gerard Angelo hit the ring and spear Reverend Darius to the mat. Gerard then stands Darius up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a spine buster which brings the fans out of their seats. He then takes his new TV title to the camera before he slams it into the head of Reverend Darius. Angelo then rubs blood off the belt as he leaves the ring, spitting in the direction of the now fallen reverend as the already overworked EMTs arrive to assess the situation for both men lying in the ring.
Ricky Simms - That was bizarre. I guess that match isn't happening. Henry Lesher - First it was Myst and now it's Wyatt. Shannon has dropped the ball twice in defending his allies, leaving them to first Scavengidiot and now Gerard to deal with things.
We head to commercial. We see Xtasy sitting in the back of a limousine sipping champagne. "You see, style is all about being smart enough to afford it. I was very smart... still am actually. Thanks to my frugal spending and smart business sense, I am set for life in retirement. Other of us... one moment." He rolls down the window and looks out at Frank Merritt driving a banged up and old Austin Mini which doesn't have a muffler. "Got that on credit Frank?" "Fuck off, X," Frank says, giving Xtasy the finger. Xtasy shuts the window and sips champagne again. "Be smart and don't be a Frank." We then see an image of a book written by Xtasy titled, "The Xcesses of Being X." People are going to want to buy that.
Tex Murrphy vs Scavenger vs Halloween Jack "Sucked In" by Jerk plays which is meant to bring Scavenger to the ring. Instead, the four screens facing all four directions from the ring show Scavenger fighting with Halloween Jack and Tex Murrphy in the parking lot. The crowd cheers as they see the three trading blows and the bell rings at ringside.
Henry Lesher - This is the life. Matches start in parking lots. Rules are of no consequence. Perfect matches tonight, even with these three fools in it.

Halloween Jack then takes advantage by kicks Tex in the gut and giving him a DDT on the concrete before rolling him over for the pin. The problem is there's no ref there yet. Jack stands up shaking his head and goes over to Scavenger. Scavenger shoulder blocks Jack seemingly from out of nowhere and then goes for the pin. Again, the ref has yet to make it here while he is running as fast as he can. Scavenger stands Jack up, brings him over to Tex, picks him up and body slams him on the bloodied detective. The ref shows up, so Scavenger pulls Jack away from Tex and tries to pin Tex. 1...2... Tex gets a shoulder up. Jack is then seen crawling to the door into the building. He manages to get it open while Scavenger argues with the ref. Scavenger then sees the door shutting and follows behind.
Henry Lesher - Don't know where Jack thought he was going. Scavenger opens the door and takes a super kick to the side of the head as soon as he enters the building. Jack then rams his head into the wall and goes for the pin. The ref fights his way inside and starts the count. 1...2... Scavenger rolls a shoulder out just in time. Jack then stands Scavenger up, grabs him by the hair and pulls him down the hallway into a receiving area. He then grabs a palette and slams it into the side of Scavenger's head. Scavenger stumbles and falls over and through a stack of boxes. Jack throws the palette down and puts the boots to Scavenger. He then stands Scavenger up, pulls him to a table and lays him on it. He then goes to a nearby forklift, sits behind the controls and raises the lift up. The crowd cheers in anticipation of what Jack is intending to do. Jack steps out of the forklift, climbs up onto it and carefully makes his way onto the end of it where it is holding as palette in the air. Jack then dives off the forklift onto the table as Scavenger rolls himself off of it. Jack crashes through the table to the floor.
Ricky Simms - Bad miscalculation there by Jack. Henry Lesher - I liked it a lot. Scavenger gets to his feet and looks quite dazed. He picks up a nearby two by four and smashes it across the chest of Halloween Jack. He then picks up a palette and drops it on Jack. Scavenger pulls it off of Jack and pulls him to his feet and whips him into the side of the forklift. Scavenger then gives Halloween Jack a spine buster on the floor and goes for the pin. 1...2.... Scavenger is busted over the head with a chair. The camera pans up and it's Sephiroth with a sadistic smile on his face. Sephiroth stands Scavenger up, asks if he remembers him and then clotheslines him to the floor. He then places Jack on Scavenger for the pin and tells the ref to count it. 1...2...3! Sephiroth stands over Scavenger and we hear the fans giving him a mixed reaction for his efforts.
Ricky Simms - Scav and Seph haven't been in the same ring in years! Seph comes out of nowhere with this attack to let everyone know he has returned!
Henry Lesher - Great appearance. At least his beginning is promising. Ricky Simms - Anyone seen Tex? Henry Lesher - Anyone care? Beyond Control vs Parizi Twins "Headsprung" by LL Cool J plays as Beyond Control comes out to a huge ovation from the crowd. They slide into the ring and jump to the second rope and head bang to the music before switching corners and doing it again. They then jump back down and start to discuss strategy as their music fades and "Power Trip" by Monster Magnet plays and out come the newly renamed Parizi Twins. Jessica and Jamie high five fans on their way down as Beyond Control fixes their eyes on them. The two girls step onto the apron side by side and then into the ring. She shakes hands with him and then the four retreat to their corners.

They lock up in the center of the ring. Classic breaks the lock by placing Jamie in an arm bar, then gives her a knife edge chop to the throat. Evans goes for the pin. 1..2... Jamie gets her foot on the bottom rope. Evans stands Jamie up, whips her off the ropes and tries for a clothesline that Jamie ducks before she lands a reverse neck breaker on Jake Evans. They both lie on the mat as the fans cheer. Evans shows signs of motion first, rolling away from Jamie and towards the ropes. Jamie grabs the ropes and starts pulling her way to her corner. Jessica is reaching for the tag and screaming at her sister to try harder.

Matt Classic rolls himself out of the ring as Jessica rolls Jake over for the pin. 1...2... Jake gets his foot on the bottom rope. Jessica stands Jake up and attempts to whip him off the ropes, but Jake grabs the top rope and pulls himself out of the ring. Matt Classic slides into the ring behind Jessica with a chair in hand. He crowns her with it from behind and then slides out of the ring as Jake Evans enters the ring with a smile on his face.
