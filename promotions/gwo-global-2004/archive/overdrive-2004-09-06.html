GWO Overdrive, 6 September 2004

6 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Calgary, Alberta The crowds are anxious to see the first ever GWO show outside of British Columbia as rumours swirl that there are new talents coming into the GWO from the outside world. The excitement around today's event is something to behold among both the fans and the wrestlers backstage who are happy about the new TV deal with CTV Sportsnet Pacific which means they will have more fans watching them today than usual. Backstage we see Hector Braun pacing and looking quite worried. He turns and the camera turns to follow his field of view. We see Casey Braun wheeling a wheelchair bound Max Cameron into the shot. Hector applauds and seems quite happy about this.
Hector Braun - Darling Casey, you brought our esteemed guest here for tonight's festivities. I am so happy to see the great legend Max Cameron here tonight for this great show.
Hector laughs as he kneels before the wheelchair. Hector Braun - Hey there, Max! Can you hear me? Do you know what tonight is? It is the start of the humiliation of you and your pals! It is the start of the end of your career Max and I am going to drag this thing out and torture you with it!
We see Dan Jalldoff enter the scene, the GWO commissioner who the fans love to hate. Dan Jalldoff - Sorry to interrupt the fun. You wanted to see me? Hector's smile quickly fades as he stands and folds his arms across his chest while looking Dan right in the eye.
Hector Braun - You know, I have been waiting an hour for you. Dan Jalldoff - I only just got your message a few minutes ago, Hector. Hector Braun - Look, we have seen a remarkable amount of passivity from this roster lately. Tonight we shake things up and they find out what Hector Braun is all about.
Dan Jalldoff - Sounds promising. Hector and Dan leave together as Casey wheels Max down the hallway in the other direction. We then go to the dressing room where we see Rated R getting ready for action tonight when Shannon Skanes enters the shot.
Shannon Skanes - Ryan, I wanted to let you know that mountain man's been a little more unstable lately than usual. Watch yourself.
Rated R - Shannon, I appreciate that, but I know what I am doing. I lived underground with him and his so called minions. I'll deal with Vesuvius as only I could.
Shannon Skanes - I wanted you to know I got your back tonight if you need it. Rated R - I won't need it. The fans fill the arena as the action gets set to begin. There's more pyrotechnics set up than before and word has it that the mysterious man or woman who funded the takeover of the CWA will speak tonight. The crowd is treated to begin the night with a few matches not shown on TV. Celia and Wyatt Gibson fight a match that ends with Wyatt winning by DQ when Devo interferes and drops Wyatt with a crow bar. Austin Fierro and Soul Reaver battle next and this match is a hard fought double count out with them ending up fighting their way into the parking lot. Shannon Skanes then comes out and faces Thunder and they battle for a long match that ends with Thunder gaining a win by count out. The final dark match sees The Moose victorious with a Moosedriver on Gerard Angelo in a non title match up that brings the crowd to their feet. The arena then turns dark and we hear "Shut Your Mouth" by Pain which means that the first ever edition of Overdrive has started. Fire erupts from a new staging area. It's not elevated, but it is wide and clearly visible to the entire crowd here tonight. There are still the four large screens that hang over the ring facing all directions that reveal action in the ring close up or show the crowd action going on elsewhere in the arena. There are extra lights on the ceiling and there are more floor seats than we ever saw at a Genesis show.
Jon Shocker - Welcome to Overdrive!!! *huge pop* Tonight the GWO takes a large step forward as we come to you from CTV Sportsnet Pacific! This is a big night for all GWO fans and it's time for some new ones to be won over! I am Jon "Lord Have Mercy" Shocker and I am joined by Henry Lesher!
Henry Lesher - I thought that was just a character. I can see I will be taking the blue pill tonight.
Jon Shocker - Baby, I gotta tell you, this is the biggest night any wrestling anywhere has seen in a very long time!
Henry Lesher - Do you have any clue how much more important other place are than this one? Jon Shocker - Yes, people, we are here! The GWO is in da house! TJ Mercer vs Halloween Jack Regulator" by Warren G and Nate Dogg plays as TJ Mercer makes his way to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd. This group is seen as one that could go either way, so they are reserving their judgement for now. TJ steps onto the apron and then through the ropes and starts testing the ropes as we hear "Back In Black" by AC/DC and Halloween Jack starts making his way to the ring.
Shelley Morris - And now announcing the contestants in this match scheduled for one fall. In the ring to my right weighing in at 245 pounds and hailing from Mesa, Arizona, TJ Mercer! And on his way to the ring weighing in at 228 pounds and hailing from Newcastle England, Halloween Jack!
The crowd cheers Jack on as he steps onto the apron and then through the ropes as his music fades. The bell sounds and the two men lock up in the center of the ring. TJ starts with an arm bar on Jack, but it is reversed. Jack then thumbs TJ in the eye before whipping him off the ropes and then back dropping him to the mat. Jack then plants a knee drop to the forehead of TJ Mercer and goes for the early pin. 1...2... kick out by TJ Mercer. Halloween Jack then stands TJ Mercer up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a tilt a whirl back breaker. Jack again goes for the pin on TJ. 1...2... kick out again by TJ Mercer. Halloween Jack then rolls TJ out of the ring and quickly follows him out. Jack starts putting the boots to TJ on the floor, then stands him up and whips him head first into the steel ring steps.
Jon Shocker - Great to see two young guys fighting here tonight! Henry Lesher - You know what would be better, though? Anything. Anything would be better than this.
Jon Shocker - Yes, he words straight from Doctor Lesher, an argument for assisted suicide against the man's will!
Halloween Jack has a smirk on his face as he pulls TJ to his feet. The smile fades when TJ drops and gives Jack a jawbreaker outside the ring and then TJ follows that up by slamming Jack's head into the guard railing four times. TJ then rolls Jack back inside the ring and heads to the ropes. Halloween Jack slowly gets to his feet as TJ reaches the top rope. Jack turns and takes a flying dropkick to the chest and then TJ goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Halloween Jack. TJ then stands Jack up, whips him into the corner buckle and then gives him a hip toss out of it into a side headlock as the fans cheer the action. Jack fights to his feet, but TJ snaps him in the headlock back to the mat and then turns it into a sleeper hold.
Jon Shocker - I gotta tell ya, TJ is getting better at this wrestling stuff. I remember him in CWA in the day and he was lousy, but he is getting better.
Henry Lesher - You a firm believer that the Red Sox will win the World Series then or are you betting on the Cubs?
Jon Shocker - What's that got to do with anything? Henry Lesher - Never mind. Jack battles to free himself from the sleeper hold even as the ref asks him if he is ready to submit. Jack backs TJ into the ropes and then whips him to the opposite corner which breaks the hold. Halloween Jack then shoulder blocks TJ to the mat and uses the ropes to keep himself steady as he shakes off the effects of the sleeper. Jack then stands TJ up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a fireman's carry into a power slam to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... TJ gets his foot up on the ropes. Halloween Jack then stands J up, whips him off the ropes and drops his head to try a backdrop. TJ then delivers a DDT on Jack and rolls him into a cradle pin. 1...2...3! TJ Mercer wins a match that has to be seen as an upset here this week.
Jon Shocker - There you go, TJ! That was some great stuff there from a guy I like a lot! Henry Lesher - You're like a big dumb dog who speaks. TJ leaves the ring in celebration, but it doesn't last as we hear "Money" by Pink Floyd and out comes Hector Braun with a smile on his face. Behind him Crystal wheels Dizz out while Casey wheels Max Cameron out.
Hector Braun - Look, the jobber won a match. Hey, TJ, got anything to say to your partner here? Come on, you've gotta have something to say, right?
TJ has a few words for Hector, but he is nowhere near the microphone and thus the fans miss hearing those expletives that censors would have been battling to keep up with anyways.
Hector Braun - TJ, you and your little band of merry men want to know what's going on? Let's let the crippled man talk.
Dizz can be heard speaking through a voice synthesizer. Dizz - When I lost my career, I learnt to hate. I learnt to hate those who took my place. I hate everyone now in my hell. I am in hell, TJ. I cannot move and I hate those who can. I hate Max most because he stole something from me, something precious. He stole my thunder. He became this main event which was my place to be. I am stuck here and he gets the glory after EOS made him the hero he was! I was behind the buy out, TJ. I was behind the fall of the CWA and I hate everyone who kept going as if I never existed. I made the CWA great. I made the CWA what it was more than anyone else. Now I sit here and we are forced to sit through second rate wrestlers like you TJ and your leader Max. Max will not be coming back to you anytime soon, TJ. Max belongs to me. I bought it all, TJ. I own you.
"Nobody's Listening" by Linkin Park plays as Dizz and Max Cameron are both wheeled out and Hector Braun laughs as he follows behind.
Jon Shocker - I cannot believe what I just heard! Dizz was behind it all! That is truly, truly shocking as only the shockman could ever know!
Henry Lesher - You are entirely too excited to be here. We then head to commercial Frank Merritt is seen in line at McDonald's. He smiles at the camera, but it looks very strained. "Ever get tired of waiting in line at McDonald's?" he asks. "Here's how you deal with that." He stands straight up and shouts "If I don't get a Big Mac in five minutes, someone loses a fucking leg!" The line parts to allow him to the front. He then turns to us and smiles again. "Gotta love all that protein. Now go to hell." We then hear the McDonald's theme music. Apparently they are going for more in-your-face advertising.
Vesuvius vs Rated R We see Vesuvius on his way to the ring as "Mein Tiel" plays and we hear Shelley Morris announcing the next match up.
Shelley Morris - And now for our next match, on his way to the ring, weighing in at 330 pounds and hailing from Phoenix, Arizona, Vesuvius!
We hear "I Hate Everything About You" by Three Days Grace as Rated R starts on his way to the ring.
Shelley Morris - And his opponent, from Nova Scotia and weighing in at 241 pounds, Rated R!
Rated R gets a huge ovation from the crowd as he circles the ring and high fives fans along the way, even high fiving Jon Shocker at the announce table before stepping onto the apron and then locking eyes with Vesuvius. He steps through the ropes, never taking his eyes off of Vesuvius the entire way in.
Jon Shocker - Ryan being a true hero here in facing his demons this week, staring down the mountain man!
Henry Lesher - Proving he is a fool. The bell sounds and the two men lock up in the center of the ring. Rated R places Vesuvius into a headlock. Vesuvius fights back by shoving Rated R head first into the corner buckle and then giving him a tornado DDT to the mat and Vesuvius tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rated R. Vesuvius stands Rated R up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a sidewalk slam. Vesuvius then comes off the ropes and tries for a leg drop which misses when Rated R rolls out of the way. Rated R uses the ropes to get to his feet before Vesuvius has a chance to get up and kicks Vesuvius in the head repeatedly before giving him a body slam to the mat. Rated R then boot chokes Vesuvius in the ropes as the crowd cheers him on. Rated R then rolls Vesuvius out of the ring. Rated R heads out of the ring and whips Vesuvius, shoulder first, into the guard railing. Rated R then picks Vesuvius up and body slams him on the floor as the fans continue to cheer him on, even starting a chant for him.
Jon Shocker - Rated R taking out the trash here! Henry Lesher - Vesuvius will come back. Rated R is in for a world of hurt now. The big man is playing with him.
Rated R stands Vesuvius up, rolls him into the ring and then slides into the ring himself. Rated R comes off the ropes and leg drops Vesuvius and goes for the pin. 1...2... Vesuvius gets a shoulder up before the three count. Rated R then stands Vesuvius up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat before kneeling over Vesuvius and landing a series of punches to the head of the mountain man. Rated R then boot chokes Vesuvius again as the ref warns Rated R against this tactic.
Jon Shocker - Domination by Rated R over the big man! The big man has got to be scared now!
Henry Lesher - Hardly, Rated R is making a big mistake here this week. Rated R rolls Vesuvius out of the ring again and then rolls himself out of the ring and proceeds to slam Vesuvius' head into the ring steps. We then see Devo and Celia coming down the aisle, clad completely in red with Suende following closely behind. They make their way around the ring as Rated R again slams Vesuvius' face into the steel steps. Devo and Celia then double clothesline Rated R to the floor from behind as the referee calls for the bell. Devo puts the boots to Rated R as Celia places Rated R into an ankle lock. Vesuvius slowly gets to his feet, blood trickling down his cheek. Devo then rolls Rated R into the ring as Vesuvius makes his way onto the apron. Celia yanks the microphone away from Shelley Morris and rolls herself into the ring.
Celia - Tonight we have a major announcement and you people are going to hear it! Reverend Darius isn't here tonight due to the incompetent staffing in the GWO. Tonight Rated R pays the price for that. Look into this ring and see the new Parish!
Devo takes a length of rope from Suende and starts tying it to the top rope as Vesuvius and Celia lay a beating on Rated R. Devo then ties the ropes around Rated R's throat as they prepare to hang him when we see Slim Pickens and Tex Murrphy on their way into the ring. They slide into the ring. Tex clotheslines Celia over the top rope to the floor as Slim rams Devo and Vesuvius' heads together and then sends Devo over the top to the floor as Tex dropkicks Vesuvius through the ropes and he hits the floor as well. The crowd cheers wildly as Slim and Tex beckon The Parish back into the ring. Instead of accepting, Celia, Devo and Vesuvius retreat to the back, none of them looking too happy with the current situation.
Henry Lesher - This was the Parish's moment! They ruined it and they'll pay for that! Jon Shocker - Anyone see Leech with that little party? Henry Lesher - I'm sure he meant to be there but was very busy. Backstage we see The Moose putting on his coat and appearing ready to leave. He is headed off by Ray Dees.
Ray Dees - Moose, where are you headed? The Moose - I am going home. I've dropped from the main event to dark match? I think I made a mistake coming here.
Ray Dees - Word has it that you've been in touch with a former ally. The Moose - Word has it about a lot of things. Out of my way. Moose pushes past Ray and makes his way out of the dressing room as Ray looks around as if lost. He had planned a longer interview than that.
The Dawg vs Stryker Sisters "Who Let the Dawgs Out?" by Baha Men plays as The Dawg makes his way to the ring and the crowd cheers him on. Dawg steps onto the apron and waves to the crowd as he then steps through the ropes.

Jamie rolls herself into the ring and attacks Dawg with a chop block to the back of the knee. Dawg drops to the mat in pain as the ref tells the sisters to pick one and wrestle the match. After a brief discussion, Jessica rolls out onto the apron and Jamie starts putting the boots to The Dawg.

Dawg then uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet. Dawg grabs Jamie by the hair, head butts her once while she is down and then picks her up and body slams her to the mat before heading to the ropes and making his way to the second rope. Dawg drops to the mat as Jessica enters the ring and starts wailing on him with the chair. We then hear "Money" by Pink Floyd and Hector Braun comes out with his daughters pushing the men in the wheelchairs behind him. He shakes his head as he looks at what is happening inside the ring.
