GWO Overdrive, 11 October 2004

11 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sacramento, CA It is going to be a packed house, the first ever GWO show in California and one which promises to see some major announcement being made. "Come With Me" by Puff Daddy plays and the crowd cheers as Tony Parizi makes his way to the ring with Sal Clemenzo and Big Guido close behind acting as his bodyguards tonight. He steps through the ropes and begins to address the crowd.
Tony Parizi - It sure feels damned good to be back in California at a wrestling event! *cheap pop* Now, I have been hearing a lot of questions this week about how I will run things differently since I hate a lot of the same people Hector Braun did. Well, tonight we are looking at a situation where I shall answer those questions and set your minds at ease. First off, it appears that wrestlers on GWO are underpaid and that needs to change. From this day forward, five hundred dollars goes to each promo put up by a GWO wrestler and they will be paid five hundred dollars a match. That means wrestlers could now earn two grand a week, perhaps more with extra incentives.
Tony looks to the crowd, lowering his mic as a few start a chant for Crystal Braun. He laughs and continues on.
Tony Parizi - So, the redheaded Braun girl interests the crowd? I am not surprised. Crystal and Casey will have jobs of their own within this company and you will see them tonight. I have also been asked about the whereabouts of Dizz. I have no clue where he is and I don't care. He no longer has any part of the ownership of the GWO. Finally, I have been asked about whether we keep the name GWO or go back to CWA. My answer to that is simple. CWA failed and has passed on. I am here to make the GWO bigger than the CWA ever was. It all starts here tonight!
"Come With Me" plays again as Tony slips out of the ring with Sal and Guido in tow. They continue to the back and we are now ready for our first match of the night.
Kosmic Kitty vs Sephiroth "Episode 29" by Mushroomhead plays as Sephiroth comes to the ring to a rather mixed reaction. Sephiroth steps onto the apron and then through the ropes and starts talking to the ref about how this match should be called. "Scandalous" by Mis-Teeq plays as Kosmic Kitty makes her way to the ring with her Coca-Cola Tag belt on her one shoulder and a basket of Pay Day bars on her arm. She stops to hand out some Pay Day bars and promises a few fans once she has bailed out the dying company of Hostess. That she will indeed have more Twinkies to share with them, and all of her special fans. While scanning the crowd of faces, Kitty notices a familiar and un-expected face staring back from the front row. Kitty stops abruptly, and lingers for a moment, her timing with the music completely off now, as she looks on in shock. There amongst the crowd is her baby sister Jamie and Kitty is exuberantly happy to see her little sister. Kitty resumes her steps to the ring, and blows a kiss to her sister Jamie while she continues to the ring, skipping happily. Kitty climbs onto the apron and then inside the ring where the bell sounds and the action is all set to begin.
Henry Lesher - She has a sister in the crowd? Is that right? Ricky Simms - Geez, doc, you got facts right for once. That is her sister, yes. Henry Lesher - Which means that there's more than one of her. I can only handle one, you know.
Kitty and Sephiroth lock up in the center of the ring and Sephiroth quickly kicks Kitty in the gut and places her in an abdominal stretch, going for a quick submission here. Kitty cries out in pain, but refuses to give up here and reaches with her free arm towards the ropes as the crowd cheers her on. She does manage to reach the ropes on her second lunge for them and the ref forces the break of the hold. Sephiroth doesn't let up, choking Kitty across the top rope, clearly frustrated here by his lack of wins in recent weeks. He then whips Kitty off the ropes and tries for a clothesline only to have Kitty duck it before Kitty lands a dropkick on Sephiroth, knocking him down to the mat. Kosmic Kitty then quickly stands and leg drops Sephiroth across the throat before she tries for the pin here. 1...2... kick out by Sephiroth. Kitty stands Sephiroth up, whips him off the ropes and again dropkicks him to the mat. She then heads for the ropes and makes her way to the top. Once on top, she turns and sees Sephiroth already on his feet. He grabs her and body slams her from the top rope to the mat where she lands hard. Sephiroth then climbs the ropes himself and flies from the top for a flying headbutt that misses when Kitty rolls out of the way.
Ricky Simms - Miscalculation there by the veteran Sephiroth. Henry Lesher - Bad move all around. I am not going to be impressed if that flakey girl wins this match tonight.
Kitty rolls herself out of the ring clutching at her back. The camera shows Jamie in the crowd looking concerned for a moment before it shows Sephiroth heading right out of the ring after her, clearly having a hard time keeping his balance. Kitty rolls herself back inside the ring. Sephiroth pulls himself inside a ring a moment later and takes a leg drop across the back of the neck from Kitty. Kitty rolls Sephiroth over for the pin. 1...2... Sephiroth rolls a shoulder out. Kosmic Kitty stands Sephiroth up and starts to whip him towards the corner only to have Sephiroth reverse it. Kitty hits the corner hard, but moves out of the way when Sephiroth charges in for a shoulder block. Kitty then keeps Sephiroth in the corner, climbs to the second rope and delivers the Boo Boo Kitty from there. She again goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Kosmic Kitty jumps to her feet, a smile on her face as she blows kisses to the crowd, a winner again this week.
Henry Lesher - The nightmare continues, seemingly without an end. Ricky Simms - Hard fought win for Kitty there over a game Sephiroth who appears to have lost a step in recent weeks. Might be something bothering him or a matter of motivation, no way to know from here.
Henry Lesher - What, you're into psychoanalysis now? Leave the real work to me. Kitty heads to the back, greeting her sister Jamie warmly again as she heads to the back. In the middle of the ring, Sephiroth gets to his feet shaking his head. He exits the ring to a mixed response from the crowd as we head backstage. There we find Radd Matthews with Joe Davidson.
Joe Davidson - Radd, tonight you are in the ring with three other men vying for number one contender status in the GWO for the world title. One of the men you are set to face is your rival Rated R.
Radd Matthews - Yes and Rated R is in for a long night. His continued assaults on Kitty and myself end tonight. He won't leave the ring on his own two feet.
Joe Davidson - Some big threats there, Radd. Aren't you concerned in the slightest about Ryan wanting revenge for what you did to him last week.
Radd Matthews - Forget last week. This week will be far worse for him. Joe Davidson - Any comment on Kitty's win this week? Radd Matthews - Kitty is a winner. People write her and I off as the pretty people of GWO, but they are finding out we have a lot of talent, too.
Joe Davidson - Well put. Now let's head back to the ring. Scavenger vs The Dawg "My Girlfriend's Girlfriend" by Type O Negative plays as Scavenger makes his way to the ring. He is only half way to the ring when we see The Dawg charging out from the back and he attacks Scavenger with a series of double axe handle smashes across the back. Dawg then whips Scavenger into the guard railing before pulling him to the ring and rolling him inside. Dawg then enters the ring and boot chokes Scavenger as the bell sounds to get this match started.
Henry Lesher - Shame this guy is a major tool or I would be cheering him on for choking and beating Scavenger.
Ricky Simms - And, as always, Lesher finds it important to get his two cents in there. Henry Lesher - Not my fault he sucks. Dawg releases the choke, stands Scavenger up, whips him into the corner and backdrops him out of it to the mat. The Dawg then stands Scavenger up again, whips him off the ropes and gives him a sidewalk slam to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Scavenger. Dawg stands Scavenger up, whips him hard off the ropes again and this time kicks him in the gut, then gives him a DDT to the mat before again trying for the pin. 1...2... Scavenger kicks out again to keep this match going. Dawg rolls Scavenger to the corner and starts to climb the ropes. He reaches the second rope and dives off for a splash that misses when Scavenger rolls out of the way. Scavenger then uses the ropes to get to his feet quickly before putting the boots to The Dawg and then stands Dawg up and whips him into the corner where he then lands a series of kicks to the chest. Dawg drops off his feet and Scavenger then kicks him in the head once before pulling him from the corner to the middle of the ring where he picks Dawg up and quickly body slams him to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Both men showing good power thus far. Henry Lesher - And amazingly little talent. Scavenger comes off the ropes and then elbowsmashes Dawg across the throat and goes for the pin. 1...2... Dawg rolls a shoulder out. Scavenger then stands Dawg up, whips him off the ropes and both men clothesline each other to the mat. This brings the fans out of their seats as the ref counts both men down.
Henry Lesher - If there is any justice left in the GWO, they will both stay down. Scavenger grabs the ropes at the four count and starts to pull himself to his feet. Henry Lesher - No justice, none at all. Scavenger gets to his feet by the six count and kicks Dawg in the chest as he gets up. Scavenger then stands Dawg up, lands a series of knife-edge chops to the chest on him and drives him into the corner. Dawg eye gouges Scavenger, then gives him a whip to the opposite corner before spearing him to the mat in the corner. Dawg then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! The Dawg pulls off the win here over Scavenger.
Ricky Simms - Dawg won the match using Scavenger's own move against him. Henry Lesher - It's a spear. No decent wrestler uses the spear for a finisher or gets finished by it.
Dawg rolls himself out of the ring and heads towards the back with another victory under his belt. Dawg high fives fans on his way to the back and then we head to commercial.
We see Frank Merritt holding his mouth and looking to be quite upset. "Stupid American medical crap. You know, I spent some time in Canada and their medical is better than this crap. Oh and when a ride tells you that you're too tall to get onto it, don't laugh and try it anyway. You might lose a couple of teeth. Dammit!" We see Xtasy passing by with a newspaper in hand. "Keep up the good fight, Frank." "Fuck off, X," Frank says, still holding his mouth clearly in pain.
We see Charley MacAdam in the new GWO studio. "Charley MacAdam here on GWO programming and quite thankful for Frank's public service announcement. I personally think he was wrongly convicted of that. I mean, clearly he thought the cop was a prostitute and she was misrepresenting herself. Now, onto GWO action! Halloween is coming, but the bigger news is the coming Maximum Impact, an event that saw some big matches last year and promises to see the same this year. All of the best of the GWO will be on display there, so order now and then sit back and watch some great GWO action! Maximum Impact comes your way on October 25th!
Steve Stryker vs Stevie James "Destroy All" by Static X plays as Steve Stryker makes his way to the ring to many boos from the crowd. Stryker steps onto the apron, looks out at the crowd and shakes his head as he steps into the ring. He then poses on the second rope and gives one fan in the front row the finger as he jumps back down to the mat. He then turns to Stryker and says he'll win the battle of the Steves tonight. He then steps onto the apron and the ref holds Stryker back to Stevie has a chance to step through the ropes and the bell sounds. The two men circle each other and then lock up. James then hip tosses Stryker to the center of the ring and then places him in a sleeperhold.

Stryker resists the sleeper from Stevie as the ref checks for a submission. Steve doesn't submit, so the ref checks the arm of Stryker. It falls once. He lifts it and it nearly falls a second time, but Steve Stryker manages to keep it raised. Stryker starts to battle his way out of the hold, but Stevie James realizes this, releases it and knees Stryker in the ribs, then bulldogs him to the mat. Stevie then rolls Stryker over for the pin. 1...2.. kick out by Steve Stryker. Stevie James then stands Stryker up, whips him off the ropes and Stryker ducks a clothesline attempt and then gives him a gutwrench suplex to the mat and follows that up with a knee drop to the forehead. Stryker then stands James up, whips him into the corner and then gives him a body slam to the mat after that before trying for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Stevie James.
Ricky Simms - Stevie sees himself as quite the ladies man and has already banged heads with T-Rex over it.
Henry Lesher - Well Leech is the only one who qualifies in my book. Ricky Simms - Ladies' man, doc. Ladies. Steve Stryker stands Stevie James up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Stryker then measures Stevie for and delivers a fist to the forehead. Stryker stands Stevie up, whips him off the ropes and ducks his head to backdrop James only to have James knee Stryker in the face, then clothesline him to the mat. He then comes off the ropes and lands the rolling thunder on Stryker before heading to the ropes. Stevie quickly reaches the top and then delivers a flying elbowsmash to Stryker. Stevie James then slaps on his version of the figure four and the crowd cheers. Stevie grabs the ropes when the ref's back is turned and Stryker screams and taps the mat. Stevie releases the ropes as the ref calls for the bell. Stevie James then rolls out of the ring with his arms raised and the crowd cheers him on.
Henry Lesher - Why the hell do they cheer him cheating and boo Leech for it? Ricky Simms - Maybe they were paying attention in Stryker's previous fights and saw him doing the same thing. It's payback and that's all it is.
As Stevie heads out, we take a look backstage to Tony Parizi's office where he has Max Cameron in a meeting.
Tony Parizi - Max, you're failed one too many times for most, but I believe you can do something for me.
Max Cameron - Why should I do anything for you? You got your champion last week. Get Myst to do it.
Tony Parizi - *smiles* I love your bitterness, Max. See, the GWO is going head to head with other wrestling leagues and it starts next week. Next week you face off against Don Mega, a champion in the UWA.
Max Cameron - Why me? Tony Parizi - Because you won't fail again. Hell, we saw you in the past defeating big champs like Vigilante when you felt inspired. My advice is simple. Get inspired immediately. You're the man of the hour. Prove yourself.
Max Cameron - I am not doing this for you or your company. I'll do it for myself. Max leaves the office and heads down the corridor with Gerard Angelo and Pyro close behind. We look back into the office and find Tony smiling, though we don't know why yet.
Rod Gordon/Boomshalkalaka vs Zack McCracken/Cordelia Parizi "Down With the Sickness" by Disturbed plays as Rod Gordon and Boomshalkalaka makes their way to the ring, which gets a mixed reaction from the crowd. They quickly roll into the ring and pose in opposite corners, which brings some fans to their feet, but not all. We then hear "Beautiful Stranger" by Madonna and the crowd gets loud when they see Zack McCracken and Cordelia Parizi on their way to the ring. Zack raises an eyebrow as he lays his eyes on Boomshalkalaka inside the ring and shakes his head as he makes his way up the ring steps onto the apron. Rod Gordon doesn't wait for the bell, attacking Zack with a forearm raised to the throat that sends Zack to the floor. Zack reverses that and whips Rod into the steel steps instead.
Henry Lesher - Great, four matches and all of them are awful. Ricky Simms - Then why are you here? Henry Lesher - Because every match holds the promise of a surprise appearance by Dante, Q or Leech. I never lose faith in that.
Inside the ring, Boom stands Cordelia up, whips her off the ropes and misses with a clothesline. Cordelia then headlocks Boom and gives him a springboard bulldog to the mat as Zack rolls himself into the ring and then knees Boom in the back of the head. Zack then leaves the ring and Cordelia stands Boomshalkalaka up and gives him a series of kicks in the gut and then gives him a DDT to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Boomshalkalaka as Rod Gordon gets to his feet and slowly makes his way to their corner. Cordelia again stands Boom up, whips him to the corner and charges into a boot to the face. Boom then headbutts Cordelia to the mat and follows that up by dragging her to the corner where he tags in Rod Gordon. Rod stands her up, whips her off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat. Rod then tries for a lionsault, but Cordelia raises her knees and catches Rod in the chest with them. She then rolls towards her corner and tags in Zack which brings the crowd to their feet.
Henry Lesher - They cheer for morons. Ricky Simms - They must love you then. Henry Lesher - How sad of a comment was that? Zack enters the ring and lands a series of rights and lefts to the jaw of a reeling Rod Gordon, sending him into the corner where he then lands a series of elbows on Gordon before hip tossing him to the center of the ring. Zack then climbs to the second rope and clotheslines Rod from there, sending him to the mat where he goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rod Gordon.
