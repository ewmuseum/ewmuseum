GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

GWO Genesis, 9 August 2004

9 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Surrey, BC The crowd files in, but Hector Braun is shaking his head already. It turns out there was a petition the wrestlers took up to keep him off the show tonight. That's fine for Hector, but someone is going to pay for this outrage, even if it isn't on camera. This is his show and he's not going to be pushed off of it. He is distracted by his daughter Crystal who is once again clad in her nurse's outfit and wheeling Dizz around.
Hector Braun - Crystal, what are you doing? Fans can see you here! Crystal Braun - Dizz wanted to be out here. Hector Braun - Nice try. The vegetable doesn't speak, remember? We have to get him ready for his refereeing debut tonight.
Crystal Braun - I thoguht there was an injunction against him being on screen. Some activists or something, right?
Hector Braun - We are not discussing that right now, princess. Backstage Max Cameron is seen lacing up his boots. Shannon Skanes enters the frame. There's no cheering or booing as the fans have not been seated yet. The GWO appears to be set to try something completely different with its shows. Could it be a pre-game show?
Max Cameron - What do you want? Shannon Skanes - Tonight the war ends for good between you and me. Max Cameron - Okay, why come and tell me that? Shannon Skanes - I have bigger fish to fry. Max Cameron - Dirk Bailey? Shannon Skanes - Why him? Max Cameron - Pretty big fish. Shannon Skanes - I am going to find out who's behind the buyout of CWA. Max Cameron - That's pretty easy. Hector Braun and his dimwits. Shannon Skanes - No, too easy. Max Cameron - Huh? Shannon Skanes - Can't be him. Leech enters the frame smiling. He's busted up, but looks quite happy for a guy who was nearly killed in the parking lot.
Shannon Skanes - What the hell do you want? Leech - You didn't kill me, Skanes, but you'll wish you had. Shannon Skanes - I don't have the patience for your cryptic warnings tonight, Leech. I have work to do, unlike the retired community.
Leech - I am going to make your life a living hell. You'll wish you had killed me. Leech laughs as he leaves the frame and Shannon shakes his head. Shannon Skanes - *offering to shake hands with Max* It's been fun, but the fight ends tonight.
Max Cameron - *shakes his hand* All right, you've got it. Shannon leaves the locker room and Max can continue getting himself ready. Shannon doesn't make it far before he is headed off by Pyro who has a smile on his face.
Shannon Skanes - Not now, man. Pyro - Still think you're the man, huh? It's funny how the CWA crumbles and you still act like you're the king of the world.
Shannon Skanes - Piss off. Shannon blows past Pyro who just smiles. Everyone is on an even playing field now. Shannon had better get used to this. The fans file in and are treated to a dark match between Aladdin Sane and Tex Murrphy which ends with a win for Tex that brings the crowd to its feet, though they appear to enjoy the whole "humping" gimmick that Aladdin employs here. What they don't see backstage is that Celia feels she is all ready to go and is getting restless about not being booked yet. Shegoes to Hector about it, which is probably her best bet if she wants to get on TV next week. We are then set to begin the show. "Breathe" by Swollen Members featuring Nelly Furtado plays and the crowd knows that means they are on TV and they go wild.
Vesuvius vs Dirk Bailey "Du Hast" by Rammstein plays as Vesuvius makes his way to the ring. He receives a warm reception despite his heelish ways right now. He steps over the top rope and looks out to the crowd not appearing to be very affectionate towards those who paid decent money to see this regional show this week. Vesuvius stands in the corner and looks out to the back as "I'm Interested In Apathy" by TISM plays and Dirk Bailey makes his way to the ring to a mixed response. People know he's not on Hector's side, but they don't know what to make of him. Dirk quickle makes his way to the ring, steps onto the apron and has a few words for Vesuvius before he steps into the ring and the bell sounds to get the match officially underway.
Ricky Simms - We are back for another edition of the show. Casey Braun - Two big guys up first. Henry Lesher - How did I wind up sitting between you two dimwits? The two big men lock up. Dirk shoves Vesuvius into the corner. Vesuvius laughs, nods and tells Dirk he can play that game. They lock up again. Dirk once again shoves Vesuvius to the corner buckle. Vesuvius comes out and lands a forearm to the chin of Dirk Bailey and then headbutts him back into the ropes. Vesuvius two handed chokes Dirk there until the ref threatens to DQ and then Vesuvius lets go. Vesuvius motions Dirk to him, then they lock up again. They lock up again. Dirk headbutts Vesuvius backwards to break the lock up, then whips him into the corner buckle and then backdrops him to the mat. Dirk leg drops the throat of Vesuvius and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Vesuvius.
Casey Braun - Oooh, that was close. Henry Lesher - Oooh? That was not an oooh moment. Dirk Bailey stands Vesuvius up, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Dirk asks Vesuvius where the monster is now. He then grabs Vesuvius by the hair and pulls him to his feet, then headbutts him into the corner where he lands a series of knee lifts on Vesuvius. Dirk then hip tosses Vesuvius to the middle of the ring before kneeing him right in the back of the neck. Vesuvius is down on the mat and Dirk again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Vesuvius. Dirk stands Vesuvius up, whips him off the ropes and Vesuvius ducks a clothesline. Vesuvius then gives Dirk a jawbreaker. Vesuvius follows that up with a camel clutch which brings the fans to their feet. The ref asks Dirk if he submits, but Dirk refuses to do so. Dirk extends his foot out and manages to snag the bottom rope.
Ricky Simms - Good ring sense by Dirk Bailey there. Henry Lesher - Like you would know. Ricky Simms - I wrestled for ten years. I would know. Vesuvius releases the camel clutch and pulls Dirk to his feet as we see Reverend Darius coming out from the back. Vesuvius whips Dirk off the ropes. Dirk ducks a clothesline and knees Vesuvius in the back, then gives him a DDT to the mat. He then spots Darius at ringside and has a few words for him. Vesuvius gets to his feet and charges at Dirk. Dirk steps aside and trips Vesuvius to the mat, face first. Dirk then slaps on a crossface and Darius responds by throwing salt in the eyes of Dirk Bailey. The ref calls for the bell as Darius slides into the ring. Darius starts drilling punches and kicks in on Dirk Bailey as Vesuvius gets to his feet. Darius whips Dirk towards Vesuvius who spears Dirk to the mat as the crowd boos.
Ricky Simms - What are we witnessing here? Henry Lesher - I guess these two are a team. It's news to me. Casey Braun - What a waste of perfectly good salt, too. Henry Lesher - What a waste of a headset. Backstage Crystal Braun is seen wheeling Dizz around. She is stopped by Max Cameron. Max Cameron - You know, this isn't funny. Crystal Braun - No one said it was. Dizz is a legend. Max Cameron - Right and you're not giving him the respect he deserves. Send him somewhere for proper medical attention.
Crystal Braun - Dizz is refereeing tonight. Max Cameron - You just don't get it. Hector Braun - *entering picture* I don't like your tone, Max. It lacks respect. You'll learn to treat my little girl with respect.
Hector puts his arm around his daughter and leads her away as Max glares at them both. He cannot stand seeing a legend treated this way.
Radd Matthews vs Wyatt Gibson "All Downhill (From Here)" by New Found Glory plays as Radd Matthews makes his way to the ring. He receives loud cheers from the crowd. He quickly enters the ring and tests the ropes as we hear "Somebody Told Me" by The Killers play and Wyatt Gibson makes his way to the ring. The crowd has a warm reception for him as well as he steps onto the apron, then through the ropes and the bell sounds. The two lock up. Radd breaks the lock up and monkey flips Wyatt into the corner. He then whips Wyatt to the oppsite corner and dropkicks him as he staggers out of it. Radd then picks Wyatt up and power slams him to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Wyatt Gibson.
Ricky Simms - Fast paced match here. Casey Braun - GO WYATT! Henry Lesher - No, better yet, both go home. Radd stands Wyatt up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Radd then heads to the ropes as the crowd gets more and more into this match up. Radd leaps off the top for a moonsault which misses when Wyatt rolls out of the way. Wyatt then uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet. Wyatt takes Radd, places him in a headlock and then gives him a bulldog to the mat. Wyatt rolls Radd over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Radd Matthews.
Casey Braun - It sucks cause they're both really cute. Henry Lesher - It's like having a hormonal Baby Calaway with me. Casey Braun - Thanks! Ricky Simms - I don't think that was meant as a compliment. Wyatt pulls Radd to his feet and whips him to the ropes. Radd uses the ropes to pull himself out of the ring to get collected. Wyatt follows Radd outside the ring. The two start trading punches on the floor as the crowd cheers. Wyatt slams Radd's head into the guard railing. Radd eye rakes Wyatt. Radd then tries to whip Wyatt into the ring steps, but Wyatt stops his forward motion and whips Radd into the steel steps. The ref calls for the bell, having reached a ten count which means that both men are counted out. The match is a draw as Wyatt protests from ringside.
Henry Lesher - They are equally inept. "Money" by Pink Floyd plays and Crystal Braun comes out, pushing Dizz into the aisle before her. She has a microphone in hand.
Crystal Braun - My Daddy is not allowed on TV because bad people stopped him from coming out, but he wanted me to come out here and tell Wyatt his in ring performance has been lacking and he is off the active roster effective immediately!
The crowd boos as Crystal then heads out and Wyatt is left with his jaw hanging as he continues to protest the count out. Radd, meanwhile, is grabbing at his shoulder as he heads towards the back. This isn't over, not by a long shot.
Henry Lesher - The boss won't deal with mediocre wrestlers. Ricky Simms - Apparently he doesn't mind a mediocre color guy. Casey Braun - That's funny. We head to commercial. We see Frank Merritt fishing on a clear and sunny day. He gets a bite and starts reeling it in. He has a big smile on his face until the line breaks. He looks at the rod and frowns. "Stupid fucking rod!" He snaps it in half and jumps in the lake. "Get over here, fish! I am gonna make all you bastards pay for this outrage! Damn you, nature! Damn you to hell!" We then hear a voiceover. "Get back to nature and relax. A message from Today's Church of Latter Day Saints."
Halloween Jack vs Johnny K-9 "One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Halloween Jack makes his way to the ring and the fans give him a very warm reception indeed. He high fives fans on his way to the ring, greeted as if he was a native son to the place. He steps into the ring and looks to the back as "Angel of Death" by Pantera plays and out comes TV Champion Johnny K-9. He gets a mixed reaction as he carries the belt over his shoulder and looks quite arrogant as he does so. In the crowd we see men in orange jumpsuits same as we did last week. Johnny steps onto the apron and shows the belt to Jack and has a few unkind words for him. Jack doesn't take this well, charging at Johnny, landing a series of forearms to the chin on him before dropping and guillotining him off the top rope. Johnny falls off the apron to the floor in a heap as the crowd cheers.
Henry Lesher - See, now that's just wrong. If I did that, people would hate me for it. Ricky Simms - Yeah, like you could do that. Jack rolls out of the ring and gets into a fistfight with Johnny before we have even gotten underway here. Jack tries to whip Johnny into the steel corner post, but Johnny puts the brakes to that and clotheslines Jack to the floor. Johnny then puts the boots to Halloween Jack before rolling him back inside the ring. Johnny enters the ring right after, so the bell finally rings. Johnny starts off by placing Jack into a Boston crab. The ref asks Jack if he submits even as Jack reaches for the ropes. Jack pulls himself towards the ropes while shaking his head in response to whether or not he gives up. He then manages to grab the bottom rope to force the break. Johnny does release the hold, but places a knee square in the spine of Halloween Jack and then places him in a front face lock.
Ricky Simms - Johnny on a hot streak, now the current TV champion. Henry Lesher - Well, look at the competition though. He's not exactly facing the second coming of Ric Flair.
Casey Braun - That's not very nice. Jack struggles to get to his feet and elbows Johnny in the gut repeatedly to break the hold, then whips Johnny off the ropes before backdropping him to the mat. Jack knee drops Johnny to the forehead, then heads to the ropes. He makes his way to the second rope, then dives off, landing a double axe handle smash to the back of the neck of Johnny K-9 before he has a chance to get to his feet.
Ricky Simms - Jack really showing a lot of tenacity here and the crowd eating it all up. Henry Lesher - Well, they're morons. Halloween Jack stands Johnny up, whips him into the corner buckle and then gives him a gutwrench suplex after that and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Johnny K-9. From the back we see Gerard Angelo making his way to the ring. The crowd boos him immediately, knowing this won't be good news. Halloween Jack whips Johnny off the ropes. Johnny spears Jack to the mat as Gerard Angelo steps onto the apron and into the ring! Johnny K-9 spots Gerard with his CWA TV title over his shoulder and the two have words. Halloween Jack gets to his feet and rolls K-9 up from behind. 1...2...3! Halloween Jack gets the win with a heavy assist from Gerard Angelo!
Ricky Simms - Jack will take the win, even if he doesn't like how he got it. Henry Lesher - So much for K-9's roll. Jack spots Gerard Angelo in the ring and gives him a spinning heel kick to the chest before landing a series of knife edge chops to the chest of Angelo. He then whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Jack then leaves the ring with the crowd cheering him on.
Casey Braun - So many handsome men in the GWO. It hardly seems fair to only like one. Backstage we see Austin Christiasn Fierro with the GWO's new interviewer, Kelly Buchanan. Kelly Buchanan - Austin, next week you make your GWO debut. How excited are you to be here?
Austin Christian Fierro - It's always good to have a fresh start somewhere and I am looking forward to...
His words are cut off as he is laid out with a chair by Devo! Devo - We don't need any pretty boys taking valuable air time with me around. Devo leaves Austin who is crumpled in a heap. We then head back to the ring. Scavenger vs Soul Reaver "My Girlfriend's Girlfriend" by Type O Negative plays as Scavenger makes his way to the ring with the chair in hand. He is only halfway down the ramp when he is attacked from behind by Soul Reaver. Soul Reaver chop blocks Scavenger down, then grabs the chair and smiles a sadistic smile as he looks to the crowd.
Henry Lesher - Do it, dammit! Cripple the freak! Casey Braun - That's not even close to nice of you to say. Ricky Simms - You're talking to the wrong person if you want nice. Soul Reaver drags Scavenger to ringside and wraps him in a figure four as we see Devo coming out from the back with his own chair in hand. Devo holds the chair high, then brings it down hard to the knee of Scavenger. The crowd boos and starts throwing objects at Reaver and Devo who pay no attention to it. They're not here to win popularity contests. Before Devo has a chance to strike again, Austin Christian Fierro races out of the back and attacks Devo. Devo rolls into the ring to escape, but Fierro follows him into the ring, whips him off the ropes and delivers the Cashed on Devo. Soul Reaver sees this and decides to retreat to the back for now as medical staff come out to attend to the fallen Scavenger.
Henry Lesher - If Devo and Soul Reaver just ended Scavenger's career, they just became my new best friends.
Ricky Simms - You're such a petty man. You really are. We head to commercial. We see a young boy throwing a baseball through a tire swing. He throws and throws and every toss goes right through the middle. There's never any doubt with any of his throws. We then hear a voiceover. "Tire swing.... 50 dollars. Baseball.... eight dollars. Glove.... twenty dollars. Buying steroids for your son to make him a major leaguer.... priceless. There are things in life money just can't buy. For everything else, there's MasterCard."
The Dawg vs Tigre Blanco Generic Mexican style music plays as Tigre Blanco makes his way to the ring. The crowd cheers him on as he high fives them. He steps onto the apron and then steps through the ropes into the ring and poses on the second rope in each corner for the crowd. "Who Let the Dawgs Out" by Baha Men plays as The Dawg comes out to a very mixed reaction. The women in the crowd don't appear to like this guy in the least and, given his past track record, that makes sense. Dawg slides into the ring, then gets to his feet and the bell sounds. From the back we see Dizz being wheeled to the ring by Crystal. Dawg and Tigre look around and realize there's no ref. The parapalegic Dizz will be the ref for this contest. The crowd boos Crystal on her way to the ring. As usual, she is completely oblivious to their feelings towards her and she blows kisses to them before taking a place next to the ring with Dizz.
Ricky Simms - This is sick. Henry Lesher - No, it's classic! Dizz is out here, the hero same as he was before he retired.
Ricky Simms - He didn't retire. He almost died. He is barely living now. He should be in a rest home under twenty four hour care.
Henry Lesher - Lucky for us then that one of the Braun girls is a nurse. Casey Braun - Well, she's an intern, but that's close enough. She looks so cute out there! Dawg and Tigre lock up in the center of the ring. Dawg whips Tigre into the corner, then delivers a forearm to the back of the masked man before giving him an atomic drop. He then picks Tigre up over his shoulder and gives him a spinning powerslam to the mat. He then goes for the pin. Crystal picks up Dizz's hand and slaps the apron with it. 1...2... kick out by Tigre Blanco. Dawg then stands Blanco up, whips him into the corner and then gives him another atomic drop. Tigre grabs at his back as he falls to the mat again. Dawg takes a glance over at Crystal with Dizz and shakes his head. He then stands Tigre Blanco up, whips him off the ropes and is caught off guard as Tigre lariats him to the mat. Tigre Blanco then leaps to the top rope and does a split legged moonsault onto Dawg before going for the pin. Crystal again uses Dizz's hand to slap the apron. 1...2... kick out by Dawg.
Henry Lesher - Dizz is a great ref! Ricky Simms - This is just gross. The man is a living legend, a three time world champ and he is reduced to this sideshow appearance? Hector Braun should be ashamed of himself.
Casey Braun - Daddy says this is great for ratings. Ricky Simms - That doesn't make it right. Henry Lesher - Hector gets ratings, then we get money. You were never very good at figuring that out, Rick.
Tigre Blanco tries for a lionsault, but Dawg pulls his knees up, winding the masked Mexican. Dawg then uses the ropes to get to his feet as Tigre catches his breath. Tigre charges at Dawg and right into a spinebuster. Dawg then heads to the second rope and lands the Tenderizer on Tigre Blanco and goes for the pin as Crystal once again moves the hand of Dizz. 1...2...3! The Dawg pulls off the win and quickly vacates the ring, looking back over his shoulder at Dizz a couple of times. Clearly, few are comfortable with the crippled Dizz being here and being involved.
Henry Lesher - Dizz might be the best ref I have seen in my years watching this farce. Ricky Simms - Give it a rest, Doc. Backstage we see Soul Reaver with the Pacific title over his shoulder and a smile on his face. Next to him stands Kelly Buchanan.
Kelly Buchanan - Any explanation for what you and Devo did to Scavenger? Soul Reaver - That idiot had the nerve to try and cost Devo and me last week, so we made him pay this week. The united forces of hate in the GWO won't be stopped by some chair wielding idiot.
Kelly Buchanan - At Rumors of War in a couple of weeks, word has it that you and Devo will be facing Radd Matthews and a partner of his choosing. Any reservations about that?
Soul Reaver - Pretty boy has lost to me before. I don't see how losing to me again helps his cause any.
Kelly Buchanan - Thanks for your time. Let's head back to the ring. Pyro vs Jack Quinn "The 20th Century Was Entirely Mine" by Fear Before The March Of Flames plays as Pyro makes his way to the ring. The crowd boos him as he holds up a sign proclaiming that Shannon Skanes is a coward. He then steps onto the apron and steps through the ropes into the ring. Jack Quinn's music starts and he makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Pyro stands in the middle of the ring motioning Jack in. Jack pauses at ringside and looks into the ring, then looks around at the crowd. Jack then pulls himself onto the apron and into the ring so this match gets underway.
Henry Lesher - Who let Pyro in this week? Hasn't he lost enough? Casey Braun - He's hot. That's how he got in the door. The two men lock up in the center of the ring. Pyro eye gouges Jack Quinn right away and the ref has a few words for Pyro who then says it was an accident. Pyro then gives Jack a bulldog and goes for an early pin here. 1...2... kick out by Jack Quinn. Pyro asks the ref if he can count to three as well as the guy in the wheelchair did earlier. Pyro then stands Jack up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a tilt a whirl backbreaker. Pyro smiles as the crowd boos. He gives one fan in the front row the finger. He then stands Jack Quinn up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. He goes to the ropes and poses for the crowd as Jack gets to his feet and dropkicks Pyro in the back before rolling him up from behind for the pin. 1...2... Pyro manages to kick out.
Henry Lesher - See, the match should have ended there. Let's get a competent ref like Dizz out here to count this.
Ricky Simms - The GWO could be in a world of trouble when this show gets broadcast. Henry Lesher - Good thing we don't have any viewers. Jack stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes and both men clothesline each other to the mat. The ref starts counting them both down. Pyro uses the ropes to start pulling himself to his feet. Jack Quinn then kips up to his feet and gives a superkick to the jaw of Pyro which sends him over the top rope and to the floor outside the ring. Jack then steps out onto the apron and lands a scissor kick to the back of Pyro's neck from there. Pyro lands hard on the floor to the delight of the crowd. Jack then stands Pyro up and rolls him into the ring. Pyro is seen drawing out a set of brass knuckles from his tights. Jack grabs him by the hair and takes a punch to the heads with the brass knuckles out of view of the ref. Pyro then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Pyro gets the win here as he puts the brass knuckles back into his tights and he leaves the ring with his arms raised.
Henry Lesher - Pyro finally getting to the point where he discovers the secret to winning! Ricky Simms - Well, of course, he cheats and becomes your best friend. Backstage we see Shannon Skanes with Ray Dees. Ray Dees - Shannon, Pyro had some harsh words for you earlier. Shannon Skanes - Pyro isn't my concern tonight. Max is. Tonight Max and I end it right. Ray Dees - Fair enough, but are you concerned with Pyro's challenge at all? Shannon Skanes - No, I am not and I won't be. Pyro isn't in my league. We go to commercial as Shannon leaves Ray wanting to ask more. We see a man entering Tim Horton's coffee chop and ordering coffee as nice music plays. We then hear a voiceover. "Nothing beats Tim Horton's coffee. When you've had a long day walking polar bears, spearing whales and doing other things Canadians do, have yourself a Tim Horton's coffee and relax. You've always got time for Tim Horton's." We then see the coffee drinker getting into a fight with another customer which resembles a hockey fight as he pulls the guy's shirt over his head and then pounds on him.
Myst vs Gerard Angelo "A Place For My Head" by Linkin Park plays as Myst makes her way to the ring. She gets a warm reception from the crowd and she high fives some fans on her way to the ring. Her attidue from last week following her loss appears to be gone for now. She enters the ring and waves to the crowd as we hear "Hell Song" by Sum 41 and out comes Gerard Angelo with his TV title over his shoulder. The fans really don't like him tonight and they cheer as Myst wastes no time in baseball sliding out of the ring, delivering a dropkick to the chest of Angelo in the process. Nyst then whips him head first into the steel ring steps before rolling him into the ring where this match can get started.
Ricky Simms - Myst didn't take her first loss well. Henry Lesher - Well, without hubby running the show, she cannot get wins like she used to. She either deals with that or she leaves.
Casey Braun - That's not nice at all. She's pretty. Myst stands Gerard up, whips him into the corner and takes a clothesline from Gerard as he comes back out of it. Gerard then boot chokes Myst in the ropes as the fans boo him. He shouts at the fans, asking them how much they like this. He then releases Myst before the ref has a chance to DQ Gerard. Gerard then stands Myst up, whips her off the ropes and clotheslines her to the mat again. He flexes as he stands over her, arrogance written all over his face. He then stands her up, whips her off the ropes and Myst ducks this clothesline and then gives Gerard a reverse neckbreaker to the mat. Myst goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gerard Angelo. Myst stands Gerard up, whips him hard into the corner buckle and then chop blocks him and Gerard lands face first on the middle turnbuckle. Myst then shoves Gerard down on the mat and tries to slap on the Mystifyer. Gerard quickly grabs the ropes and pulls himself from the ring.
Henry Lesher - About time that move was exposed for how weak it is. Ricky Simms - That so called weak move ended a lot of matches. Henry Lesher - Against weak people. Johnny K-9 is seen coming down from the back. Gerard Angelo spots him and has a few words for him before he steps onto the apron. He has a few more words for Johnny there and Myst gets tired of waiting, dropkicking Gerard off the apron and his face bounces off the guard railing. Johnny laughs as Myst rolls out of the ring and throws Gerard inside. Myst pulls Gerard to the center of the ring and then slaps on the Mystifyer. The crowd cheers and Johnny K-9 applauds as he grabs the CWA TV title Gerard had been wearing off the floor at ringside. He dusts it off and then starts for the back as Gerard finally gives up and Myst wins by submission.
Henry Lesher - Nice revenge for Johnny. He took the belt, too. Ricky Simms - Nice flip flop there, Doc. Casey Braun - Well, Myst was gonna win anyways. Johnny K-9 holds both TV titles high before he disappears into the back whils Myst raises her arms outside the ring, victorious this week to stop her losing streak at one. Backstage we see Johnny's smile fade when he comes across Halloween Jack.
Halloween Jack - Enjoy that while it last, Johnny. It's gonna be mine at Rumors of War. The two start brawling as security comes in to break them up. We head to commercial. Frank Merritt is seen answering the door where a Girl Guide asks if he would like some cookies. Frank notices the mother of the Girl Guide and smiles at her and she smiles back at Frank. The mother is seen disappearing inside the house. We then fast forward to that night and the mother is seen leaving, the kid still on the porch and the mother's clothes disheveled. The mother then hands Frank four cases of cookies and he laughs as he closes the door. "Girl Guides of Canada, because it's good to give."
Reverend Darius vs Marla Storme Before the match can begin, Celia is announced as the special guest referee. She makes her way to the ring with an angered look on her face and has a few words for fans in the front row before going back to mostly talking to herself. As she enters the ring and starts to pace, "Orchids" by Stone Sour plays as Reverend Darius makes his way to the ring. He is booed the loudest we have heard so far tonight as he makes his way to ringside. He steps onto the apron and then into the ring where he has a few words with Celia.
Ricky Simms - Don't know what Celia is doing refereeing a match. Wyatt Gibson was put in charge of her and now she's out here without anyone to keep her in check.
Henry Lesher - She's fine. After all, she dated my daughter and no one who dated my daughter can be that bad.
Casey Braun - Oh, so she's batting for the other team! Ricky Simms - I hear she is a switch hitter. "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays as Thunder leads Marla Storme to the ring. They get a mixed reaction as Thunder takes his place at ringside to watch and Marla steps onto the apron and looks into the ring at the evil reverend. She steps through the ropes and the bell sounds. Reverend Darius charges at Marla who ducks, knees the reverend in the gut and then gives him a snap suplex to the middle of the ring. Thunder applauds from ringside as Marla gets to her feet and delivers a somersault leg drop to the throat of Darius. She then heads to the ropes and makes her way quickly to the top. Darius gets to his feet, turns and takes a hurricarana off the top rope from Marla who then goes for the pin. 1...2... Darius gets his foot on the bottom rope. Marla stands Darius up, whips him hard into the corner and then Darius shoulder blocks Marla to the mat and gives her an elbow smash to the throat.
