CWO Genesis, 12 July 2004

12 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yo, it's Troy Marks here with a report on the CWO's first ever show. Yeah so I'll keep notes during and before and after the show and stuff, so I hope this reads okay on the internet.
6:33 PM So I am in line and the doors were supposed to open at 6 PM. I wonder what the hold up is. 6:41 PM The doors finally opened and I have a seat at ringside. The ring looks pretty good for an indy federation. The ropes are red and the mat is white with black skirting on the apron. I wonder how they afforded that.
7:00 PM The show was supposed to start now, but instead we get Wyatt Gibson going in and trying to warm up the crowd. The guy is nervous and it shows. He makes some jokes about the Prime Minister of Canada which don't go over at all. I hope this isn't a sign of things to come.
7:13 PM The first match is announced. Olivia Maine is facing some Mexican dude Tiger Blank or something like that. Olivia is hot, but her outfit sucks. Adidas work out gear in the ring? That's stupid and looks cheap. The Mexican dude does some neat flips and kicks to start off. She does some defence which was okay. I noticed some older guy in a suit at the back with two gorgeous girls so maybe they're part of the show. They sure look interested in what's going on inside the ring.
7:24 PM The Mexican Tiger guy pinned Olivia with a West Coast Pop I was impressed with. She hit some decent moves, but he was clearly the better wrestler. Olivia might get better with more practice. I guess that match gets a 7 out of 10 from me. If that's the opening match, this place might have promise.
7:27 PM The attendance is announced as 243 for this event. That looks to be about right, I guess. 7:29 PM The second match is announced. Slim Pickens is anything but slim. The guy is a horse. Well, not literally or anything. Dudes, Jack Quinn looks solid as he comes to the ring. This should be good.
7:35 PM Slim executed a top rope splash, though he killed Jack. Jack kicked out! That looked sick! 7:37 PM Jack rolls Slim up for a two count. The ref gets knocked down after that and Jack had Slim pinned for five seconds after a sweet reverse neckbreaker. Ref's out though, so it means jack shit.
7:40 PM Slim wins via another top rope slpash on Jack. This match impressed the hell out of me. Cool wrestlers. I'd pay to watch for these guys alone actually. I am looking forward to the rest of the night now.
7:43 PM So this Wyatt Gibson guy comes out again to yap at the fans and gets attacked by some guy named Brian who says he is sending a message. I cheered because he shut Wyatt up.
7:45 PM Third match is announced and it's a tag match. So it starts with some guy in a trenchcoat asking questions and then he' joined by some James Bond type guy. Dude's gonna wrestle in a tux? Man, the guys they are against look like monsters. Devo is a nasty sonofabitch and Soul Reaver's eyes almost bored a hole in my chest. This could be messy.
7:52 PM The guy in the trenchcoat can wrestle. He came off with this handspring back elbow I was impressed with. He then ended up in the other team's corner and his partner starts grilling fans on the whereabouts of the Mona Lisa? When was that stolen?
7:57 PM The trenchcoat detective finally tags in the Bond dude and the Bond dude totally gets destroyed. Devo and Reaver wrap this thing up and the trenchcoat guy doesn't appear to be impressed with that at all. He gets in the ring and gives the Bond dude a kick in the head. Then this chick comes out to save the Bond dude and Devo and Reaver lay her out with a 3-D. It was pretty sweet. They impressed me.
8:00 PM The fourth match is announced and we have a triple threat match. Xtasy seems familiar to me. He looks good. Johnny K-9 looks good. Aladdin dude starts humping the ref's leg? That was very wrong.
8:06 PM Aladdin keeps finding things to hump, the ropes, the turnbuckles and locks in a sleeper on Xtasy and humps his back? This dude is in need of help!
8:09 PM Match ends with idiot hump boy getting pinned by Johnny K-9. K-9 and Xtasy then brawl their way to the back. Hump boy leaves the ring and mercifully exits without humping anything else. That might be the worst gimmick ever. Funny as shit though.
8:12 PM Johnny K-9 and Xtasy brawl back into the ring? Where were they for three minutes? The bell sounds and these two are now wrestling a singles match impromptu style. I like that idea. Great moves between these two with hump everything boy gone out of the picture. K-9 lands the best boot to the chest anyone has so far tonight.
8:19 PM Xtasy gets the pin with a cool finisher. The crowd were digging both guys. K-9 gets on the microphone and says it's not over between him and Xtasy. I guess this is the start of a feud. I agree with it. These guys will battle each other well. K-9 then came around and high fived some of the fans.
8:22 PM Wyatt Gibson is out again, this time to wrestle. The weird guy with the two hot girls then comes and sits at ringside? Maybe they are part of the show. Wyatt's opponent comes out and he looks decent. The dude who attacked Wyatt earlier is with his opponent, so now that bit makes sense. The match should be good from the looks of things.
8:26 PM Wyatt executes a tarantula that I liked a lot. Devin executes a West Coast Pop for a two count I liked a lot as well. The battled outside the ring, too after Devin landed a nice la plancha on Wyatt.
8:28 PM Okay, so they fought into the midst of tables and shit and busted each other open. Devin looks better than Wyatt. He's fucked up. Devin lands what must be his finisher and pins Wyatt, but he looks bad. The ref checks on Wyatt and signals to the back. Even Devin looks a bit worried and looks at the dude who came with him to the ring. Devin leaves and the guy at ringside with the two girls looks happy. Yeah, there's something going on here.
8:32 PM Next match is up now. Are we at seven or something? Man this is a big card for a new league! We're getting our money's worth, for sure. Kosmic Kitty against Radd Matthews? Chick's hot, guy looks like he can wrestle. This should be interesting.
8:37 PM Yeah, I don't know what's going on here. Radd and Kitty Kosmic or whoever keep helping each other up and shit. Is this wrestling or Dating Game?
8:42 PM Kitty appears to win with her finisher, but Radd reverses the pin and he gets a three count. He then stands her up after the match and they shake hands. Those monsters from earlier, Devo and Soul Reaver, come back out and attack these two. Maybe this will make sense later, but Devo and Soul Reaver again look impressive. They were chased off by what appeared to be a couple of hot lesbians. CWO has a lot of promise, it would appear.
8:45 PM So next up we have some redhead chick named Sunshine against some nutjob named Chester who appears to be having a hard time concantrating. I am having a hard time figuring out if it's a part of his act or if there is something mentally wrong with him. Dude has issues.
8:50 PM Okay, so Chester has three times started to leave the ring because he thought he won. He should be hump boy's tag team partner. That would be hilarious. When he's inside the ring, Chester does wrestle well. Sunshine is a high flyer and quite good actually. She isn't letting Chester's forgetfulness distract her which is cool.
8:54 PM Sunshine lands a moonsault for the win here. Chester then asks where she's going when he gets up. Most everyone here is laughing at him. I keep waiting for idiot hump boy to come out and hump his leg or something.
9:00 PM Damn, two hours of wrestling from a new promotion? This is cool. Last match is announced as a body slam match. First guy is some crazy religious dude and I like his look. I would go to church to see a priest that looks like him. His opponent is huge, so I know who's getting slammed or going over the top rope or whatever. Man... the fat dude's leg went through the ramp they are using. Who the hell decided a plywood ramp was a good idea. People are stupid!
9:05 PM The fat dude can wrestle. He lands some cool moves but the Reverend guy is quick and sneaky and shit. Good choice for main event.
9:07 PM Yeah I have been looking at these chicks all night. I thought it was cuz they are hot, but I swear that's Shelia and Gloria Lavin. Why would CWA be slummin' here? Maybe they're lookalikes.
9:12 PM Fat dude throws Rev over and Rev drags fat dude with him over the top to the floor. Match is called a draw and they fight to the back. Nice ending, leaves everything open for feuds and stuff. The Shelia and Gloria girls are first fans out.
9:20 PM I get signatures from some of the wrestlers and I am gonna be at their show next week, too. These guys are goin' somewhere.
9:25 PM I head home. Show's mark: 9 out of 10 Production Value: 3 out of 10 (no entrance music!) Angles: 5 out of 10 (first week so that'll go up and stuff) Best Match: The triple threat that turned to a singles match Favorite Wrestler: Too early to say yet. Dirk was cool, though he didn't come out for autographs or anything.
Best Wrestler: Devin Blade hit some sick moves on Wyatt who would be around the top himself.
Next Show: July 19th in Kelowna and I'll be there! Backstage stuff the kid didn't see: Dirk Bailey refused to sign autographs after the show on account of a bad back which matched his bad attitude. He was pissed over his foot going through the ramp and says he'll quit if it happens again.
The Virgin Kitties arrived late to the show and that didn't impress anyone. Aladdin Sane and Chester Firefiend had most backstage laughing all night. Shelia and Gloria Lavin gave a pep talk to the roster before the show, though no one is sure why they were there.
Kosmic Kitty and Radd Matthews appear to be quite close at this point. Olivia Maine was said to be the least prepared of the night, but her work inside the ring solidified her place on the roster for now.
Soul Reaver and Devo had the biggest egos of the night, clashing one with Johnny K-9 and once with Xtasy.
Tex Murrphy and Zack McCracken left separately before the show was done, both saying they had "work to do."
Jack Quinn is always practising new moves and Wyatt Gibson appears to be the same outside the ring, always looking for ways to improve.
Tigre Blanco is quite eloquent when you can figure out what he is saying. After the show, the backers thanked the wrestlers for their work and paid them in cash. The show apparently earned the CWO 3200$ which helps along the way to credibility.
July 19th you will read the next report from Kelowna BC.
