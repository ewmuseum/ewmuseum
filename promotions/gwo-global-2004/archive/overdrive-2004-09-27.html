GWO Overdrive, 27 September 2004

27 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Portland, OR It's a near capacity crowd tonight in Portland, many of which are wearing CWA T-shirts. Backstage we see Vesuvius entering the office of Hector Braun.
Hector Braun - Wow, the show hasn't started yet and you're already prepared to cause problems.
Vesuvius - I don't want Moose at Security Breach, Hector. I want Scavenger. I want to end his career. He's been running from me long enough.
Hector Braun - I wasn't aware he was the one running. Vesuvius - Keep being a smart ass, Hec. Look, give me Scavenger next week. Hector Braun - You got it, V-Man. Scavenger against Vesuvius for next week's pay per view. We then head to our opening credits where we see images of Slim Pickens, The Parish, Leech, Moose, The Authority and Smooth Kriminals as "Breaking the Habit" by Linkin Park plays before we head to ringside where Ricky Simms and Henry Lesher are once again all set to call the action tonight.
Mordecai Darius vs T-Rex "American Jesus" by Bad Religion plays as Mordecai Darius comes out to a chorus of boos from the crowd. He curses a few fans in the front row as he makes his way to the ring. He then steps into the ring and has a few words with the ref about how this match is supposed to go. As he does, we hear "Can't Get the Best of Me" by Cypress Hill and T-Rex is walked to the ring by Mercedes and Lexus which brings the fans to their feet. He says something to the camera about the money he has made and the money he will make. He has a few words for Darius inside the ring. As he steps through the ropes, Darius attacks him and the bell sounds to get the match underway.
Henry Lesher - Well, Darius is anxious to get things going and who could blame him? Ricky Simms - Darius hasn't been the same since the casket landed on him. T-Rex is whipped off the ropes by Darius and then T-Rex ducks a clothesline and gives Darius a reverse neck breaker to the mat. T-Rex goes for the quick pin as the girls at ringside applaud. 1...2... kick out by Darius. T-Rex then quickly stands Darius up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. T-Rex smiles and looks to the ropes. He makes his way to the second rope and lands a double axe handle smash to the back of Darius, knocking him face down to the mat. T-Rex then stands Darius up, whips him off the ropes and then uses that momentum to toss him through the ropes to the floor. T-Rex steps out onto the apron and then delivers a double axe handle smash to the back of Darius before letting out a scream to the rafters.
Ricky Simms - T-Rex is pumped for this match. Henry Lesher - I was backstage last week and met this guy. He had that funny Flockish smell to him.
Ricky Simms - I'm not surprised. T-Rex stands Darius up, whips him into the guard railing and then puts the boots to him there as if curbing him against the railing. He knee drops the side of Darius' head and then stands him up and rolls him into the ring, rolling himself in just before the ref reached the ten count here.
Ricky Simms - T-Rex toying with a count out in the match. Henry Lesher - Looks like he is sending a message that he's going places and the Parish is a good place to make your case.
T-Rex boot chokes Darius in the ropes as Mercedes and Lexus voice their approval from ringside. T-Rex releases the choke, stands Darius up and whips him into the corner buckle. Darius then comes out and clotheslines T-Rex to the mat and boot chokes him in the ropes. Darius releases the choke and stands T-Rex up, whips him into the corner and gives him a snap suplex from there to the middle of the ring. Darius then lands a leg drop across the throat of Rex and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Darius. Rex stands Darius up, whips him off the ropes and Darius ducks a clothesline, then the two start trading rights and lefts in the center of the ring. T-Rex thumbs Darius in the eye, then gives him a knee in the gut. T-Rex then places Darius into an abdominal stretch and the ref starts asking Mordecai about a submission here. Darius refuses to submit and reaches for the ropes with his free hand.
Ricky Simms - T-Rex showing some good skill here. Henry Lesher - I don't believe it will last, though. Darius gives up on reaching the ropes and instead starts working on battling out of the hold. T-Rex's hold weakens as Darius fights and winds up hip tossing T-Rex to the mat. Darius then places Rex into an arm bar. He twists Rex's arm as the ref starts asking Rex about a submission. Rex responds by standing up and landing a series of knife edge chops with his free arm on Darius to break the hold. He then head butts Rex into the corner and then sets and lands the Rex-Plex on Darius and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! T-Rex scores himself an impressive win here to remain undefeated in the GWO.
Ricky Simms - I must say that was a good showing for Rex, a man who appears to be going places in the GWO.
Henry Lesher - Yes, hopefully he'll be leaving. Backstage we see Slim Pickens entering the building. He is headed off by Shannon Skanes, already clad in a referee's shirt and black pants.
Shannon Skanes - All set, Slim? Slim Pickens - I was the first ever Canadian champion here in the GWO. Of course I am ready for tonight.
Shannon Skanes - Leech is more than just another match. He's the nastiest wrestler I have seen in any ring.
Slim Pickens - He is about to become a statistic. Slim then pushes past Shannon and heads to his locker room while Shannon shakes his head and goes the other way.
TJ Mercer/Pyro vs Halloween Jack/Devo "The 20th Century Was Entirely Mine" by Fear Before The March Of Flames plays as Pyro leads TJ Mercer towards the ring, the crowd cheering for the most part. Pyro rolls himself under the bottom rope into the ring as TJ leaps onto the apron and looks around at the capacity crowd, soaking it all in. We then hear "Back In Black" by AC/DC and Halloween Jack leads the way to the ring with Suende and Devo close behind. Suende starts telling Jack how this match is going to go tonight and Jack takes issue with a few things she says. He and Devo then get into it and it appears they are ready to fight each other in the aisles. The crowd cheers as they anticipate a fight, but they are disappointed as the reluctant tag team resumes the walk to the ring. Devo enters the ring and Jack heads around the ring and then hops onto the apron in the corner and the bell sounds with Devo and Pyro locking up in the middle.
Ricky Simms - Odd tag match here. Henry Lesher - Parish dragging Jack up from the gutter and J dragging Pyro down into it, from the looks of things here this week.
Pyro places Devo into an arm bar, breaking the lock up. Devo then head butts Pyro into the corner and gives him a fisherman's suplex out of it. Devo then stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a knee in the gut followed by a gut wrench suplex to the mat. Devo then tries for the early pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro. Devo stands Pyro up, then tags in Halloween Jack. Jack enters the ring and gives Pyro a kick in the ribs as Devo holds him open for it. Jack then places Pyro into a full nelson in the middle of the ring and the ref starts asking Pyro if he submits here. Pyro refuses to submit as TJ cheers on his partner from the apron. Pyro starts to power out of the hold, so Jack releases it. Jack then tries for a bulldog on Pyro, but Pyro shoves Jack hard into the corner buckle. Pyro then lunges for and makes the tag to TJ Mercer. TJ enters the ring and backdrops Jack to the mat and then backdrops a charging Devo to the mat as well. Both Jack and Devo then opt to roll themselves out of the ring to regroup.
Ricky Simms - A bit of housecleaning there by TJ Mercer. Henry Lesher - Fluke. He had offence and it confused Devo and Jack. It won't last. Devo steps onto the apron with Jack next to him. Jack steps through the ropes. He and TJ circle each other as Devo makes his way back to his corner. Jack locks up with TJ. Jack breaks it, placing TJ into a headlock. TJ elbows Jack in the ribs, then hip tosses him to the mat. TJ follows that up with a leg drop across the throat of Jack before going for the pin. 1...2... Jack rolls a shoulder out in time. TJ stands Jack up, whips him off the ropes and then both men clothesline each other to the mat. The ref starts counting them both down as the fans seem split on who to cheer for here to get up first.
Henry Lesher - Maybe they'll both stay down and end this here. They bore me anyways. Ricky Simms - Nothing meets with your approval unless it's Leech these days. At the four count, TJ shows signs of life, grabbing the ropes and starting to get to his feet. Jack does the same at the six count. On the eight count, TJ stands free of the ropes and the count is stopped. TJ then takes a clothesline over the top rope from a charging Devo. Devo then lets himself out of the ring as Jack steps out through the ropes and then down to the floor where he puts the boots to TJ Mercer. Jack then stands TJ up and whips him shoulder first into the steel ring steps. Jack then rolls TJ into the ring before rolling himself inside the ring just in time to beat the count out. Jack then goes for the pin on TJ. 1...2...3! Devo and Jack win the tag match between the unlikely tag teams. Jack rolls himself out of the ring, then high fives fans on his way to the back. Devo and Suende are following close behind and appear to be satisfied with this week's victory as Devo continues his trek towards the world title match with Max Cameron next week. we see Pyro standing over TJ as he does we see coming down the ramp Gerard Angelo. As he slides into the ring TJ gets to his feet. As the three men stand there it is like an old fashioned stand of as the three look at each other confused.
Ricky Simms - What is this all about? Henry Lesher - I don’t know but If I were TJ I would be very nervous right now. TJ looks at the two men as they all circle the ring. After a few moments the lights go out.
Ricky Simms - Now what? Henry Lesher - I have no idea but there go the dam lights again. Better see Q or Dante in the ring when they come back on or I'll be very disappointed.
After a few minutes we hear the sound of a bell. As this happens the spotlight comes on. Then another bell the crowd starts to cheer as they think they know whats coming. Then the guitar riff of "Hells Bells" kicks in as the spotlight shines at the entranceway. Out from behind the curtain we see Max coming down to the ring the crowd cheers but then it is short lived as they see he is still in a wheelchair. There curiosity is peaked though as they notice who is pushing the wheelchair.
Ricky Simms - Hey why is Maxine pushing Max’s wheelchair? Henry Lesher - I don’t know. Do I look like a damn psychic? I'm still ticked it wasn't Dante or Q.
As she gently places Max at ringside she slides into the ring carrying a bag. She then places herself directly in the middle of the three men.
Ricky Simms - What is she doing? They're going to kill her! She grabs a microphone as we see Max sitting at ringside looking out of it. Maxine - First of all Hector your time of control over my husband is up. *loud cheering* Your month is over now he will get the proper treatment he needs.
The crowd again cheers as the three men in the ring look on. Maxine - Now as for the three of you. As she says that Pyro steps in front of her and grabs her hair. Gerard just looks at him and smiles as the crowd looks on stunned.
Ricky Simms - What is this all about there not going to hurt her. Henry Lesher - Oh this is going to be good. As Gerard steps forward he looks at Pyro and raised up his hand. Pyro high fives him. Ricky Simms - What is that all about? Henry Lesher - Looks like they have formed something. Ricky Simms - So it was them who were behind all of this. As TJ looks on stunned he goes in to stop them but then he stops. He then smiles and he high fives the both of them.
Ricky Simms - what the hell? Henry Lesher - What is going on here? As the three men taunt Maxine in the ring. Pyro looks over at Max and smiles. Pyro - Max this one is for you. As he goes to pick up Maxine the lights go out again. Ricky Simms - Now what? As the lights come back up we see Maxine is now standing there and the other three guys are just looking around confused. As they do the crowd erupts.
Henry Lesher - What are these morons cheering about? Ricky Simms - Henry look Max’s wheelchair. Its empty. As the crowd looks on TJ notices this and points to it. Pyro and Gerard look around the ring confused. The lights go out again. As the do there is another sound of a bell as the lights come back up we see Max standing in the ring and Maxine standing behind him. The crowd erupts as they see this. Max grabs a Mic and smiles at the three across from them.
Max Cameron - I’m back. The crowd cheers him on as the three men stand there ready to fight. Max stands there seemingly unconcerned.
Max Cameron - So what were you guys going to do to my wife anyway? As Max looks on TJ smiles and steps forward. TJ Mercer - Max I am so sick of you. You always getting in the way. So I have reformed your most famous creation. Your looking at the new version of Youthful Aggression.
Max looks on and laughs as the crowd boos. Max Cameron - You reformed what? So I suppose you were behind all of this lights going out stuff and taking out Bobby and Devin.
TJ looks on confused. TJ Mercer - Well no but they were they told me they had it all under control. I trust these two they hate you as much as I do.
Max stands there smiling. As he does TJ is not amused. Max looks over at Pyro then at Gerard and nods as he does TJ looks at Max as Max smiles.
Max Cameron - Well TJ alls I can say is you better be careful who you trust. As Max says that Pyro and Gerard blind side TJ. They start taking him apart to the shock of the crowd. As they beat him down the hold him up for Max as TJ looks beating and battered as Maxine look on.
Max Cameron - You did nothing you ignorant Fool. It was me the whole time. Remember the Vision I told you and the other regulators about. Well this is it. You didn’t reform Youthful Aggression I did.
As Max says that the crowd is in shock. Max then takes TJ and throws him out of the ring. He then looks out at the crowd as the remaining four members of now YA look on at the now booing crowd.
Max Cameron - You should have known never to trust me. I told you before I cant be trusted yet you did. Man you people are stupid. You see YA is back together this time stronger then ever. This time its not just about taking them on this time its about taking them out. That’s right this time we are here to take what Shannon so desperately wants and what Hector so desperately wants to hold on to. This time we are taking control and were taking out everyone including the Regulators to do it. So Devo come to the PPV bring everything you have. Because I am coming to the PPV not as the drooling idiot you thought but as the man who is your next World Champion.
As Max says that "Youth of the Nation" by POD starts to play over the PA system as Gerard and Pyro stand next to Max with their hands raised in victory as the crowd looks on stunned.
Ricky Simms - I cannot believe what we just witnessed here. Henry Lesher - Neither can I. Max outsmarted someone? Last time that will happen. We head to commercial. We see Frank Merritt looking at shoes at Foot Locker. He looks at a pair of white Nike's and frowns. He then faces the camera. "Man, these shoes are screaming to get their asses kicked. I realized the need to decent shoes ages ago and now we have it." He tosses the Nike's aside and picks up black runners that bring a smile to his face. "Say hello to the Shit kickers 3000. These runners have steel toes, pepper spray built into the right toe with easy release mechanisms for those times when you want to blind people. With each purchase of Shit kickers, we will send five dollars to the fund to get Richard Simmons ass kicked just because he's a pussy. Frank Merritt ain't gonna make no pussy ass shoes. Buy them today or watch me kick your ass."
Zack McCracken vs Rod Gordon "Diamonds and Guns" by The Transplants plays as Rod Gordon makes his way to the ring to a mixed reaction from the Portland crowd. He quickly slides into the ring and poses on the second rope in the corner, clearly anxious to get his first match started here. As he steps down, we hear "Beautiful Stranger" by Madonna and Zack McCracken is walked to the ring by Felicity. The crowd catcalls her and cheers the lovable loser Zack as he steps onto the apron and waves to his fans. His smile is taken away as Rod clotheslines him off the apron to the floor. Rod then leaves the ring and starts putting the boots to Zack on the floor before rolling him into the ring. Rod rolls himself back into the ring and the bell sounds to make this match official.
Ricky Simms - Rod not wasting any time here this week in his debut. Henry Lesher - Hopefully this ends that idiot detective for good. Rod stands Zack up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Rod then heads to the ropes, makes his way to the second rope and scissor kicks Zack across the back of the neck just as he was getting to his feet. Rod then rolls him over for the attempted pin. 1...2... kick out by Zack McCracken. Rod argues with the ref about whether or not that should have been a three count. Rod stands Zack up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Rod then goes for an elbow smash which misses when Zack rolls out of the way. Zack then rolls himself out of the ring. Rod follows Zack out. Zack turns, knees Rod in the gut and then whips him shoulder first into the steel corner post before rolling himself back into the ring. Zack then goes for the pin on Rod Gordon. 1...2... Rod kicks out and reverses the pin into a small package on Zack. 1...2... Zack grabs the bottom rope to stop the count. Rod and Zack then both get to their feet and start trading punches. Rod eye gouges Zack, then knees him in the chest, driving him back to the corner before monkey flipping him back to the center of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Zack showing some fight this week. Henry Lesher - Yeah just to piss me off. Rod places Zack into a camel clutch in the center of the ring. Zack manages to raise his back which weakens the hold as Felicity cheers him on from ringside. Rod releases the camel clutch, stands Zack up and whips him off the ropes. The two shoulder block each other, neither moving. Rod then head butts Zack backwards and then gives him a body slam to the mat. Rod then stands Zack up and then drops him with a lightning DDT and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Rod Gordon grabs himself the win here this week over Zack. Rod then stands over Zack with a sadistic smile on his face. He boot chokes Zack in the ropes as the crowd boos. Felicity rolls herself into the ring and pleads with Rod to let Zack go. Tony clotheslines Rod down as Cordelia puts the boots to him as he rolls out of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Why would the Parizis come to the aid of Zack McCracken and Felicity? Henry Lesher - Because they've gone soft. That's why. Backstage we see Max Cameron with Ray Dees. Ray Dees - Max, this week you shocked the world by reforming Youthful Aggression once you were freed from your wheelchair. Next week you face Devo. What plans have you got for that match?
Max Cameron - If it wasn't for Myst, I would have been champion already. If it wasn't for Hector Braun's manipulations after that, I would have been champion already. Next week I cash in. Next week, I will finally be champ.
As Max walks away from Ray, Devo charges in with a chair in hand and attacks Max. This quickly escalated as Pyro and Gerard Angelo enter the scene and Celia and Vesuvius do the same soon after that with security trying in vain to split this brawl up. Ray wisely steps away from all of this.
