GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

GWO Overdrive, 14 February 2005

14 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Detroit, MI Joe Louis Arena is buzzing with anticipation of tonight's Valentine's edition of Overdrive. They erupt when, instead of the usual opening music, we instead hear "Rock Superstar" by Cypress Hill and the lights dim throughout the building. However, after a few moments, we see a laughing Hector Braun on his way to the ring instead of the man that the crowd anticipated. He steps onto the apron and into the ring and shakes his head before he begins to speak.
Hector Braun - You people crack me up. You know, I thought it was funny when I pretended Vig was coming out, but pretending Weapon is coming out is just as fun. Now, this is Valentine's Day and, as such, we are celebrating. We are...
"Somebody Told Me" by The Killers plays and Wyatt Gibson starts making his way to the ring as the crowd cheers him on. He has roses in one hand and a heart-shaped box of chocolates in the other. He steps up onto the apron and into the ring and then stands nose to nsoe with Hector for a few tense moments before he grabs the microphone.
Wyatt Gibson - Though standing inside the ring with a man who once tried to have me fired, I am still moved enough to state a fact. I haven't been on camera much these past few months and yet a drama has played itself out before our eyes as Kosmic Kitty, the darling of the GWO, has changed thanks to the incompetent, impotent, overrated and clearly overmatches Stevie James. Kitty was hurt and tonight, in honor of this occasion, I am here to tell her there is a man in the GWO who would treat her right and it's me.
"Rumors" by Lindsay Lohan plays as Kosmic Kitty comes out from the back to a standing ovation from this crowd, many of whom have T-shirts on that praise her. She wastes no time running down to the ring and sliding under the bottom rope, then jumping to her feet. She stands face to face with Wyatt. A tear runs down her cheek. She reaches out for the roses. He smiles as she takes them. She looks at them and, as she reaches out for the chocolates, she kicks him below the belt and then slaps him across the face with his roses before she gives him a roundhouse kick to the side of the head. She throws the roses down on the mat and then stomps on the box of chocolates as well. Kitty then leaves the ring with a more mixed reaction from the crowd now. We then head to the usual opening as "Sucked In" by Jerk plays and we see pictures of Myst, Shannon Skanes, Youthful Aggression, Dying Breed, Sal Clemenzo and the VWA wrestlers all posed together before we head to ringside where Henry Lesher is joined this week by Shawna Miller.
Beyond Control vs Glory Boys "Headsprung" by LL Cool J plays as Jake and Matt make their way to the ring wearing old CWA T-shirts that were Shannon Skanes and Bruce Miller ones as well. Of course, they crossed all of that out with a big red x in spray paint. They pull the shirts off, wipe their noses with them and then toss them into the crowd. They step up onto the apron and then into the ring where they then await their opponents for tonight. "Blaze of Glory" by Jon Bon Jovi plays as Shannon Skanes and Bruce Miller make their way out from the back. Neither man looks impressed as they stroll down the ramp to the ring where Beyond Control is already mocking them and asking which hip they want replaced after the match. Shannon jumps up onto the apron first and he has a few words with Jake Evans. Evans takes a swing at Shannon and Shannon jumps back off the apron to avoid it. Shannon and Bruce then talk strategy from outside the ring for a few moments as Beyond Control head to their corner and opt to have Matt Classic start this match. Bruce steps through the ropes a few moments later and the bell sounds to get things underway.
Shawna Miller - This is great, being at ringside to watch my man inside the ring. Henry Lesher - Usually I accept whoever they send out to sit with me, but I have heard you're pretty vacuous so I don't see why you would be out here unless it's those mammaries you possess. They should have given me Q or Shelia Lavin or Dante or maybe a furry kitten.
Bruce locks up with Matt. Matt backs Bruce into the ropes. The ref asks for a clean break, but doesn't get it from Bruce who raises a knee to the gut on Matt and then Bruce gives him a series of forearm smashes to the side of the head before setting him for and delivering a vertical suplex on the former GWO tag champion. Bruce then gives Matt a knee drop to the forehead and then goes for the quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Matt Classic. Bruce then pulls Matt to his side of the ring and then tags in Shannon. Shannon steps into the ring and gives Matt a series of kicks to the ribs as Bruce holds his arm up. Bruce then steps out of the ring as Shannon applies an arm bar. Matt lands a knife edge chop to the throat on Shannon, then whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Matt Classic then rolls to his corner and tags in Jake Evans. Evans jumps to the top rope and lands a flying headscissors takedown on Shannon from there. Jake then quickly gets to his feet and gives Shannon an elbow smash across the chest and then Jake goes for the pin, his feet on the ropes. The referee spots the feet on the ropes and refuses to count the pin. Jake denies he was doing that as he pulls Shannon to his feet, then whips him into the corner and gives him a German suplex out of it. Jake then goes for the pin without a foot on the ropes. 1...2... Shannon rolls a shoulder out.
Henry Lesher - This might be the longest stretch of time he has been inside the ring without getting injured in months. Of course, at his age, you have to expect that.
Shawna Miller - Just wait for it. They are going to show the world what they're made of. Jake stands Shannon up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a spinning heel kick to the chest. Jake then tags in Matt Classic who climbs to the top rope and goes for a five star frog splash from there only to find no one home when he hits the mat. Shannon rolls to his corner and tags in Bruce Miller. Bruce steps through the ropes and lays the boots to Matt Classic. Bruce then stands Matt up and whips him off the ropes and shoulder blocks him to the mat. Bruce then rolls Matt over and applies a Boston Crab on Matt Classic. Bruce leans back as Matt struggles not to submit here. His pain is short lives as Jake Evans enters the ring and kicks Bruce in the head twice to break the hold. The referee then sends Jake back to his corner as Bruce stands Matt up. Bruce whips Matt Classic into the corner and then gives him a belly to belly suplex out of it and goes for the pin. 1...2... Matt manages to kick out. On the apron, Shannon calls for the tag. As he does, a man emerges through the crowd, leaps over the guard railing and then pulls Shannon off the apron and then slams his head into the corner post. The mystery man then picks Shannon up and gives him a body slam to the floor. The camera then is able to capture a shot of his face and it's Halloween Jack.
Shawna Miller - What the hell is he doing out here? Henry Lesher - I would say he is sending a message about next week. Bruce goes to his corner to make the tag and finds no one there, then spots Shannon virtually motionless on the floor. He leans through the ropes and calls out to his partner as Jake smiles behind him. Jake Evans, freshly tagged in by Matt Classic, rolls Bruce up from behind, complete with a handful of tights. 1...2...3! Beyond Control are the winners. As Jake and Matt high five each other, we see Bruce exiting the ring and Kathy Smith racing in from the back. Shawna Miller scowls upon seeing Kathy and she leaves her broadcast position.
Henry Lesher - First match and my partner leaves, gotta be a new record. Shawna heads off Kathy and tells her to stay out of this. Kathy looks innocent to her charges. Shawna calls Kathy a homewrecker. Kathy slaps Shawna across the face and then the brawl is on. The two ladies fight as Beyond Control stays half way up the ramp to cheer them on, telling them to kill each other. Security starts to come in from the back to break things up as Bruce Miller checks on Shannon, unaware of what his wife is up to. We then head backstage where we see Boomshakalaka with Ray Dees.
Ray Dees - Boom, tonight you are in a Weapons of Choice match against some dangerous people. Any fear going into this one?
Boomshalkalaka - What would I have to be afraid of? Leech steps into the shot with a brown paper bag in his right hand. Leech - Be afraid of what I have in my hand here. This weapon will end the match quick. Ray takes a whiff of whatever is in the bag and proceeds to pass out. Boom steps back as Leech opens the bag and smiles at what's inside of it. We then head back to the ring.
Max Cameron vs Thor "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays as Max Cameron makes his way to the ring, drawing the ire of the fans. He has a few words with one fan in the front row once he reaches the bottom of the ramp. He then steps up onto the apron, into the ring and there he waits. "Hell's Bells" is replaced by "Back In Black" and Thor comes out to the ring, bringing the fans to their feet. Thor looks into the ring at Max and shakes his head, then steps up onto the apron and then into the ring. The bell sounds as Max charges at Thor and the two of them exchange rights and lefts to the head. Thor whips Max off the ropes, then gives him a backdrop to the mat. Thor then boot chokes Max in the ropes. Thor releases the choke before the ref can reach a disqualification count. Thor then stands Max up, whips him into the corner buckle and then gives him a big boot to the chest that sends him back to the mat. Thor then climbs to the second rope, waits for Max to stand and goes for the double axe handle. Max ducks that and gives Thor a knee to the gut and then a reverse neckbreaker. Max then gets to his feet and he takes his turn boot choking Thor in the ropes. Max then stands Thor up, whips him into the corner buckle and then backdrops him out of it to the mat.
Henry Lesher - Why must I sit through drivel like this before seeing Leech? There is, of course, no one next to Lesher to talk back. Max lands a leg drop across the throat of Thor and then stands him up, whips him off the ropes and then clotheslines him to the mat. Max then goes for the quick pin. 1...2... Thor manages to kick out. Max again stands Thor up, whips him into the corner and then gives him a sidewalk slam out of it to the mat. Max then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. Thor stands, turns and takes a flying body press to the mat from Max who again goes for the pin. 1...2... Thor manages to roll a shoulder out in time. Max then stands Thor up again, whips him off the ropes and Thor ducks a clothesline before kneeing Max in the gut and then giving him a DDT to the mat where Thor then goes for the pin. 1...2... Max gets a foot on the bottom rope. Thor then rolls Max out of the ring and follows him out. Thor stands Max up on the floor and whips him shoulder first into the steel corner post. Thor then gives him a German suplex on the floor.
Henry Lesher - If this match stayed outside the ring or involved a lot of cheating, it might get my interest.
Thor stands Max up and rolls him into the ring. Thor then rolls himself into the ring and goes for the pin on Max. 1...2... Max manages to roll a shoulder out. Thor then stands Max up, whips him off the ropes and Max ducks a big boot from Thor. Max then gives Thor a fallaway slam to the mat. Max then stands Thor up and delivers the Maximum Impact on him and then rolls him over for the pin. 1...2...3! Max Cameron gets the victory over Thor.
Henry Lesher - This is when Ricky or Shannon would blubber on about how important a win this week's was for Max. Of course, that is because they prattle on like idiots.
Max leaves the ring and nods as his music plays and the crowd boos more loudly now than they did when he came to the ring initially. We then look backstage where new signee The Barber is all smiles with Kelly Buchanan.
Kelly Buchanan - Barber, next week you say you're all set to make a GWO debut? The Barber - I gotta tell ya, it sure is great to be in the GWO, the big time. I am set to debut in that ring and remind people what wrestling is meant to be about.
Gerard Angelo - *as he walks through* Bad hair? The Barber - What the...? Kelly Buchanan - That's Gerard Angelo. He is going for the Canadian title next week. The Barber - Good for him. Maybe the week after he can make some snide remarks inside the ring with me.
Barber heads off shaking his head as we head back to the ring for more action. Bra and Panty Catfight "Closer" by Nine Inch Nails plays as Xandra comes to the ring in a black bra and black panties much to the delights of the male fans in the front rows. Xandra is accompanied to the ring by Vesuvius who has a few words with a few fans in the front row. Xandra steps into the ring as her music fades and "Halls of Fear" by Nino Rota plays and Serena Marcelli makes her way to the ring with Sal Clemenzo close behind her. She is clad in a pink bra and panty set for this match up tonight. She slides under the bottom rope into the ring and Vesuvius and Sal Clemenzo have a few words outside the ring. Inside the ring, Xandra picks Serena up and body slams her to the mat. Xandra then knee drops Serena in the stomach. We then see Tanya Whitmore running to the ring with a white bra and red thong on. She jumps onto the apron as Sentinel gets in between Sal and Vesuvius and the three of them start arguing. Whitemore rams Serena and Xandra's heads together, then whips Xandra into the corner buckle. Serena chop blocks Tanya down, then takes a boot to the face from Xandra. Xandra then lands a series of kicks to the back on Tanya, sending her out of the ring to the floor. Serena then gets to her feet and gives Xandra an inverted atomic drop. Serena then headbutts Xandra and then clotheslines her over the top rope to the floor.
Henry Lesher - I would love to make some form of comment, but I comment on wrestling and this isn't it.
Serena stands Xandra up, whips her into the corner and then charges in shoulder first. Xandra moves out of the way and then gives Serena a body slam out of it. Xandra then goes to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and then lands a leg drop across the chest of Serena. Xandra stands Serena up as Tanya Whitmore rolls herself into the ring. Xandra whips Serena off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat. Tanya gets to her feet and she lands a dropkick on Xandra that sends her through the ropes to the floor. Tanya then headlocks Serena and lands a bulldog on her and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Serena. Tanya stands Serena up, whips her off the ropes and hip tosses her to the mat. Xandra gets to her feet and snarls at Tanya Whitmore inside the ring. Xandra rolls herself into the ring and grabs the back of Tanya's bra and shakes her violently back and forward with it, then turns her around and thumbs her in the eye before giving her a DDT to the mat. Xandra then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Xandra pulls off the win here. Outside the ring, Sal Clemenzo jumps onto the apron and protests that call. Sentinel pulls Sal off the apron. Vesuvius drills Sentinel in the back of the head with a chair. Inside the ring, Xandra has her arms raised in victory when Serena dropkicks her in the back.
Henry Lesher - It was almost interesting in the end. Tanya gets to her feet, but Serena lands a jawbreaker on her. Serena then rolls herself out of the ring where Sal Clemenzo is waiting for her. Xandra gets to her feet, turns to Tanya inside the ring and knees her in the chest. Sentinel rolls himself into the ring and pulls Xandra off of Tanya by the hair. Vesuvius rolls himself into the ring, grabs Sentinel around the back of the throat, then knees him in the back as security fills the ring.
Henry Lesher - If we could lose the females in the ring, this would turn interesting. As they are pulled out of the ring, Xandra gets her hands on Tanya again. From within the throng of people, Xandra is suddenly holding Tanya's bra high as some nearby whistle and catcall. They are then rushed to the back as we look backstage and see Shawna Miller with Ray Dees.
Ray Dees - Shawna, you attacked Kathy tonight and we are all wondering why. Shawna Miller - It's not enough that Kathy ruined Shannon's marriage? It's not enough she is destroying Myst's career? She has to keep coming out here and sticking her nose where it doesn't belong!
Ray Dees - You have some strong feelings on the matter then. Shawna Miller - I have had issues with Kathy for years. This is nothing new. Next week at Malicious Intent, I challenge Kathy to a falls count anywhere match. Let's see what that bitch has to offer.
Ray stands speechless as Shawna walks away and we head to our next match. Nikki Alexander-Starr vs Madison Ali vs Kathy Smith "Gimme" by Alice Cooper plays as Nikki Alexander makes her way to the ring to a very mixed reaction from a crowd that isn't certain how to take her at this point. She reaches ringside and looks up at the bright lights and into the ring for a few moments before she pulls herself onto the apron. As she steps into the ring, we hear "Gold Dust Woman" by Hole and Kathy Smith makes her way to the ring. Kathy wastes no time running down the ramp and sliding into the ring where she then gets into a fistfight with Nikki. Madison shoulder blocks Kathy through the ropes and then Madison steps into the ring as Nikki pulls herself back inside under the bottom rope. Madison gives Kathy a side Russian leg sweep. Nikki stands Kathy up, whips her off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat. Madison then lands a superkick to the jaw on Nikki and then follows that up with a leg drop across Kathy's throat. Kathy has a few words with her, then Madison rolls her into a school boy pinfall attempt. 1...2...3! Madison Ali pulls off the win here this week, with a small aid by Shawna Miller who is seen smiling at ringside.
