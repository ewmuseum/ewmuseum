GWO (Global, 2004) — Card

CWO Genesis, 2 August 2004

2 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Abbotsford, BC The crowd files in. The cameras are all set to start filming the first ever televised CWO show. The CWO is on the verge of a name change and becoming something larger and backstage appears to be proving that as egos clash over developing angles and coming events. There are those who are from CWA and not impressed with a major pay cut and other news Hector Braun plans to reveal to the crowd here tonight. This crowd is expected to be above the 1,000 mark which will be a first in the CWO's brief history and a lot of fans are travelling a long way to see what happens with the company that bought out the CWA after it's twenty years of operation.
One big question coming into tonight's show revolves around who will be the announcers for this major event. Hector hasn't enlightened anyone on who is going to be calling the action and said it is for him to know and them to find out when it is their time to know. Hector is charming, as always.
The crowd filed in, the pyros and music all set for the night, the CWO is all set to go and it is none other than Henry Lesher and Casey Braun sent out to call the action tonight, which should make things interesting. The camera starts to roll and we hear "Walk" by Pantera and the lights in the cramped arena flash in sync with the music as Genesis begins yet again, this time with all the bells and whistles with one exception. There is no entrance ramp. Wrestlers will enter down aisles as they used to in days of old and the Titantron has been replaced with a set of four large screens hanging over the ring. This will save people craning their necks around to see what is happening backstage when that kind of thing transpires. "Walk" fades away and then we start the action as the show is ready to go.
Henry Lesher - The Doc is back and on minimum wage. I want answers as to why... Before Lesher has a chance to say anything further, "Money" by Pink Floyd plays and out comes Hector Braun to a very mixed reaction. CWO has been a word of mouth kind of thing, so he's not really well known yet. He makes his way to the ring, waving to his daughter in the broadcast position at ringside and shaking hands with one of many fans here in orange jumpsuits in support of Johnny K-9. Hector steps into the ring with a smile on his face not unlike the Cheshire Cat.
Hector Braun - Tonight is a big night. I have destroyed the CWA. I have made that worldwide conglomerate into a very manageable regional wrestling organzation and I have high hopes for this crew. I know you people came here to see the big names, so I am not going to disappoint.
"Nobody's Listening" by Linkin Park plays and the crowd cheers as they know that music belongs to Dizz.
Henry Lesher - I thought Dizz was a vegetable. Casey Braun - Daddy is smart. He gets the best people here and the fans will love him for that.
Dizz comes out all right. He is pushed out in a wheelchair hooked up to an IV and a respirator which appears to be keeping him alive. His head is slumped down and forward and he shows no reaction to the fans. Wheeling him out is Crystal Braun in a nurse's outfit. She waves to the crowd who are clueing in that this isn't a good thing and so the boos start.
Hector Braun - You are not booing my little girl. She is wheeling out here a wrestling legend, a man you people would die to be and he almost died being. Dizz is a hero. Dizz is a man among men. Dizz is a person who simply has to be here and my little girl there is in charge of him for this evening. She has an internship at Vancouver General and she's capable of doing what needs to be done tonight. Now, you may wonder why Dizz is here and I don't blame you for wondering. Tonight Leech faces Shannon Skanes in what is being called Leech's swan song. Who better than Dizz here to referee the match? Come on. Who else would you choose, really?
The crowd boos to an almost deafening pitch and it leaves Hector shaking his head and sighing.
Henry Lesher - This seems low... and I know low. Casey Braun - My family deals with heroes. These people are heroes and Crystal looks so cute up there.
Henry Lesher - Well, it has me interested. Better keep me damned interested as three hundred bucks for calling a show hardly seems worth it.
Hector Braun - Now, tonight you people will witness history as that overrated Slim Pickens has his shot at the Canadian title, our highest profile title at this point thanks to the good people at EA Sports. All titles will be handed out tonight and you people will see it as will the mighty Dizz here.
Henry Lesher - Dizz is like Weekend at Bernie's without the funny, just like the movie itself.
Casey Braun - You can't talk about a legend like that. Now let's get that cute Wyatt out here.
Henry Lesher - Let's get Wyatt out here when the boy has facial hair and can legally drink. What the hell is up with that anyway?
Casey Braun - I can't legally drink either. Henry Lesher - I died and went to Sesame Street. Dizz is then wheeled to the back by Crystal who keeps smiling and waving despite the negative crowd reaction. We then go to commercial.
We see a blonde on TV with a smile on her face. "Hi there, I am Tamara Taggert and this is around BC. Penticton will be holding its annual apple picking fair this Saturday from 1 PM till 4. Pick a hundred apples and you get one free. Also this Saturday, Vancouver will hold its annual Symphony of Fire fireworks show which promises to have a lot of drunken brawling and unintended pregnancies, so come out and get knocked up under pretty lights! Richmond RCMP will be holding their annual "Drink, Drive and Die" seminar where they will be stringing up guilty drivers by their testicles. That is promising to be fun and will be followed by apple bobbing in the police lobby. That's Around BC and I am Tamara Stanners."

Zack responds with a kick to the face of D4 and then gives him a clothesline to the mat, followed by an awkward looking elbow drop.

D4 then goes to the ropes as Zack staggers to the center of the ring. D4 climbs to the second rope, dives off and elbows Zack between the eyes. Zack fallsback to the mat and D4 goes for the pin. 1...2... Zack kicks out and the fans cheer.

D4 stands Zack up, whips him off the ropes and Zack lands on his feet after a backdrop. Zack looks surprised, ducks a clothesline attempt from D4 and then delivers a Rock Bottom on D4 and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Zack McCracken picks up his first ever CWO win here tonight! The crowd explodes.
Henry Lesher - Og goody, let's encourage him to keep wrestling. Move on to the next match please.
Casey Braun - Zack is a marketable wrestler, Daddy says. Daddy is super smart. Backstage we see Hector Braun making his way through some audio equipment section when he is headed off by Shannon Skanes.
Shannon Skanes - What kind of farce are you setting up here tonight, Braun? Hector Braun - Oh no farce to be sure. Dizz will be your ref tonight for this grand exit match for Leech.
Shannon Skanes - I won't stand for it. Hector Braun - Then you will lose your first ever CWO match tonight, Mister Skanes. Shannon Skanes - Pull rank on me, Hec. See how far it gets you. Shannon leaves and Hector is seen smiling as we head to commercial. Frank Merritt is seen playing Nintendo. Suddenly the TV turns itself off. He grabs the remote and tries to turn it on with no results.
Frank shrugs, gets up, grabs a golf club and starts beating the TV with it. "Fucking stupid piece of shit! Damn you to hell! I was gonna win and you're a piece of shit and I have had enough of you and this is what I think of you! Worthless piece of shit!"
We then hear a voiceover. "Have you remembered to pay your Hydro bill lately? BC Hyrdo, call us before you beat the shit out of your TV."

Reverend Darius - Miss Kitty, you shall not enjoy your victory long. I challenge you to a rematch at Rumors of War, an event that Hector Braun informed me will be coming soon.
