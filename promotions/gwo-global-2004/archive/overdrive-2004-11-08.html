GWO Overdrive, 8 November 2004

8 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Los Angeles, CA Backstage we see Shannon Skanes entering the arena for what should be another wild night in the GWO. He rounds a corner and finds Slim Pickens waiting for him.
Shannon Skanes - What the hell do you want? Slim Pickens - Just wanted to take a last look at the handsome Shannon before Youtful Aggression ruins your looks. You're gonna need a lot of luck tonight.
Shannon Skanes - I don't have time for you. Shannon moves past Slim who turns and starts to laugh as he slowly leaves. We then see the credits roll and we see images of Dizz, Moose, Leech, The Flock, Vigilante... and then the shot freezes and the film appears to burn for a moment before we see "Technical problems are temporary. Please stand by." A few moments later, we hear "Money" by Pink Floyd and see images of The Braun family and hear Hector Braun laughing. We then head to ringside where the announce table is currently unoccupied. The crowd is cheering as they see themselves on screen. Their cheers turn to boos as we hear "Money" by Pink Floyd and Casey Braun comes to the ring waving her hand to the crowd. She blows kisses to a choice few fans who are cheering her and she winds up taking a seat at the announce table. We then hear "Know" by System of a Down and Dante is seen coming out from the back to a very mixed reaction from the GWO audience. He also makes his way to the ring, minus the waves and blown kisses, and then takes a seat next to Casey Braun for a first time announcing team.
Casey Braun - It's great to be back in the GWO. Dante - This is only done as part of my community service and because people are sick of hearing Henry Lesher's voice, apparently. I have no problem with him as he is my fan, but nevertheless I am here tonight.
Casey Braun - I don't know why they picked you, though. Dante - I was about to tell you the same thing. Casey Braun - Apparently we had a video glitch at the start of the show. Dante - If rumours are true. I suspect we know who made that glaring error. In the back where the video and audio are set up, we see Kosmic Kitty's sister Jamie in tears as Wyatt Gibson enters the room.
Wyatt Gibson - Jamie, how did I know you would bring up the wrong intro? Assault looks like Overdrive to you?
Jamie - I didn't mean to. I... Wyatt Gibson - You never mean to. Look, you are done for tonight. I simply cannot deal with this right now.
Jamie hangs her head, puts on her jacket and leaves. The rest of the crew looks at Wyatt. Wyatt Gibson - What the hell are you looking at? I'm the boss and she messed up! Wyatt leaves the room and then a man at one of the mixing boards turns to face the camera and it's none other than Hector Braun himself.
Casey Braun - What a handsome gentleman he is... whoever he is. Dante - Something stinks about this. Kumquat Kid vs Shmelky Brothers "Blue" by Eiffel 65 plays as Ingee and Shengee are led to the ring by Adam West. The trio get a huge ovation as they seem scared as usual. They appear more likely to head for the exit than wrestle here tonight. They are in prime Shmelky form. Los Angeles appears to love that. Ingee and Shengee step through the ropes into the ring, Shengee actually tripping on it and faceplanting to the mat. We hope he doesn't have a nosebleed. As they stand in the middle of the ring and say a prayer to whatever God they believe in, Kumquat Kid's theme music plays and out he comes already laughing at what he sees inside the ring, the genetically enhanced twins who look nothing alike anymore. This just makes Kid laugh louder and louder as he gets closer to the ring. He steps up on the apron and has a few words for Adam West who is in his full Batman costume as usual. Ingee gets a "USA USA USA" chant started for no reason at all.
Dante - The fans are sheep. Casey Braun - Apparently so. I want to see some of the good looking guys in the GWO, though. Get Wyatt out here.
Dante - *clears his throat* I am right here, you know. Casey Braun - You're a six. I wanna look at a nine or a ten. Dante - I'll give you a nine or ten. *grumbles to himself* The bell sounds as Kumquat Kid steps through the ropes and his music fades. Ingee opts to start this match and locks up with the Kumquat Kid. Kid quickly shoves Ingee to the mat as Adam West shakes his head at ringside. Ingee quickly gets to his feet and again locks up with the Kumquat Kid only to be shoved down yet again. Ingee then tags in Shengee who looks lost. Shengee steps into the ring and poses once as if he were a gansta rapper. Kumquat laughs and Shengee dropkicks him to the mat. Shengee then stands and poses again as if he were a gansta rapper and Kumquat gets to his feet nodding. Kumquat charges and spears Shengee to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... Shengee gets a foot on the bottom rope. Kumquat stands Shengee up, whips him off the ropes and tries for a superkick that Shengee ducks before Shengee sweeps the legs out from under Kumquat, dropping him to the mat. Shengee looks shocked and looks to Adam West as if he doesn't know what to do. Adam motions for Shengee to attack. Shengee kicks at Kumquat Kid as Kumquat quickly regroups. Kumquat uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet, then kicks Shengee in the gut and gives him a body slam to the mat.
Casey Braun - The Shmelky Brothers sure are small. Dante - They're stupid and lack talent, too. Casey Braun - I wouldn't say that. Dante - I know you wouldn't, so I said it for you. Kumquat stands Shengee up, whips him off the ropes and Shengee ducks a clothesline, then comes off the ropes, leap frogs Kumquat and then arm drags him to the mat. He turns to Adam West again dumbfounded as he holds Kumquat Kid in an arm bar. Kumquat breaks the arm bar and then gives him a fireman's carry into a power slam to the mat. Shengee them scrambles to his corner and tags in Ingee. Ingee looks confused as he steps into the ring and into a hip toss to the mat from Kumquat Kid. Kid then picks Ingee up and body slams him to the mat and then follows that up with a lionsault onto the fallen Ingee whom Kumquat then goes for the pin on. 1...2...3! Kumquat Kid gets the win as Ingee is unable to mount any offence.
Casey Braun - The new guy won a match here. That has to impress people. Dante - Doesn't impress me. The guy still sucks donkey nuts. Kumquat leaves as Shmelk tries to figure things out inside the ring. We then look backstage and find Radd Matthews walking out of his dressing room and running into Kitty's sister Jamie who is still in tears.
Radd Matthews - What's wrong, Jamie? Jamie - Wyatt threw me out of the truck for screwing up the intro tonight. Radd Matthews - Really? I heard about the intro mess up. What I don't understand is how you have all of these audio and video tracks the GWO doesn't even use.
Jamie - *shrugs* I just do as I'm told. Radd Matthews - I'll take a look into this. *takes her chin in his hand* We'll get this fixed, no matter what ok?
Jamie - *nods* Okay. They embrace and we then head back to the ring. Joe Davidson vs The Dawg "Secret Separation" by The Fixx plays as Joe Davidson is led to the ring by Steve Blake, both wearing their yellow tights and black vests from yesteryear as the crowd who has seen them tag before cheers loudly. Blake and Joe discuss things as Joe reaches ringside and Joe nods a few times before he rolls into the ring and then poses for the cheering masses present tonight. As Joe stands on the second rope and tries to get the crowd to make more noise, we hear "Who Let the Dawgs Out?" by Baha Men and out comes The Dawg to a more mixed reaction than usual as he slowly heads to the ring. He starts jawing at Steve Blake at ringside and the crowd cheers the idea of them going at it, two strong men who could do some real damage to each other inside or outside the ring. Blake backs off and motions Dawg into the ring. Dawg laughs as he looks into the ring at Joe. He steps onto the apron and makes a sudden lunge towards Joe, but Joe doesn't flinch, still motioning The Dawg into the ring.
Dante - I am not sure what this match is going to prove. Casey Braun - I think it's cool. Joe is trying for a big comeback and Dawg is trying to stop him.
As Dawg steps through the ropes, we hear the bell sound and the two men then circle each other inside the ring. They lock up and Dawg breaks that with a boot to the gut on Joe, then drives him into the corner buckle with a series of forearm smashes to the head. Dawg then hip tosses Joe to the center of the ring and dropkicks him in the back before he has a chance to get all the way to his feet. Joe then rolls Dawg onto his back, pulls him to the center of the ring and sets up for the figure four as the fans go wild. It was Joe's finisher years ago, so he is looking to end this one quickly. Dawg kicks Joe in the back when he turns on the leg to try and cinch the hold in, sending Joe to the corner where his forehead slams off of the turnbuckle pad. Dawg then gets to his feet and places Joe in a reverse headlock and DDTs him to the mat and rolls him over for the pin. 1...2... Joe gets a foot on the bottom rope. The crowd starts a chant for Joe, led by Steve Blake. Dawg stands Joe up, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him down to the mat before slapping a sleeperhold on the fallen veteran.
Dante - Ring rust more apparent as each moment passes. Casey Braun - I am disappointed. I had hoped to see some really hot guys like Radd or Rated R by now.
Dante - I think you need to be hosed down. The crowd's chants for Joe get louder as the ref raises the arm of Joe Davidson and it drops once. It drops a second time and we hear Steve Blake slapping the apron and shouting at Joe not to fade here. The arm is raised a third time and Joe manages to keep it raised. Joe then starts to fight his way back to his feet as Dawg quickly look for something to prevent a change in momentum. Dawgb releases the sleeper and gives Joe an atomic drop. Dawg then comes off the ropes and misses with a clothesline that Joe manages to duck. Joe then kicks Dawg in the gut and then locks up and gives him a snap suplex to the mat, showing a shocking amount of strength for a man who has been out of the ring for a long time. Joe then signals to Blake outside the ring. Blake throws the timekeeper off of his chair and folds it and slides it into the ring to Joe. Joe picks up the chair and smashes it into the face of The Dawg, causing the referee to call for the bell.
Dante - Nice twist. Casey Braun - Good thing for Dawg he wasn't a pretty man to start with. That won't help. Joe grabs a microphone from Shelley Morris and shoves her into the corner. Joe Davidson - I was never a really nice guy inside the ring. Leech, you lowlife, I am not coming to beat you. I am coming to end you!
Steve Blake steps through the ropes and stands The Dawg up. Joe then gives The Dawg a stunner to the mat and then poses once for the crowd who have a more mixed reaction now to the returning legend. We go to commercial.
We see Frank Merritt woth a copy of Halo 2 in his hands. "This is Halo 2. Soon, many people will be playing this day and night. People will abandon friends, girlfriends and family to play this. You know why? It's because you all need a fucking life!" Frank tosses the game to the floor and the camera zooms in as he stomps on it. He can be heard cursing as he leaves the set and our commercial comes to a sad end.
Myst vs Gerard Angelo "Enemy" by Sevendust plays as Myst makes her way to the ring. She immediately heads around the ring and she has a few words for Dante before she steps onto the apron and looks out to the cheering crowd.
Casey Braun - She isn't a fan of yours. Dante - That actually went well. Myst steps onto the top rope and then leaps down into the ring as her music fades and "Hell Song" by Sum 41 plays and Gerard Angelo makes his way to the ring, yelling at the camera about him losing the TV title being far more important than any title Myst may have lost. He also shows off his Youthful Aggression shirt and poses at ringside as Myst stands in the middle of the ring and never taking her eyes of of Angelo. Gerard steps onto the apron and looks out to the crowd and has a few words with a kid in the front row. Myst takes advantage of his break in concentration and dropkicks him in the side, knocking him off the apron and to the floor. Myst then slides out of the ring and grabs Gerard by the hair and whips him head first into the ring steps as the fans cheer her on. Myst then rolls Gerard into the ring and slides back inside and starts raining down punches on Gerard as chants start for Myst. Myst then pulls Gerard to his feet, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Myst then points to the ropes and quickly makes her way to the top, then attempts a split legged moonsault which misses when Gerard rolls out of the way. Myst is winded as she lands face down on the mat.
Casey Braun - At least they're good looking. Dante - That would have been a nice move if her instincts didn't suck donkey nuts. She didn't think she was going to hit that, did she?
Casey Braun - I guess she did. Gerard gets to his feet and puts the boots to the fallen former world champion. He then grabs her by the hair and says a few not very nice things to her before whipping her into the corner buckle. He then charges in and shoulder tackles her in the corner. Gerard follows this up by monkey flipping her to the center of the ring. He then stands her up and executes a vertical suplex on her, holding her high in the air for a few moments to make a bigger impact. Gerard then goes for the pin on Myst. 1...2... kick out by Myst. Gerard stands Myst up, whips her off the ropes and backdrops her to the mat. As he does, we see Rated R coming out from the back. This causes a mixed reaction from a crowd who have no idea what to make of this turn of events. Gerard doesn't see him as he stands Myst up, whips her off the ropes and drops his head for a backdrop only to take a kick to the face from Myst who follows that up with a Rock Bottom on Gerard before she goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gerard Angelo. Myst shakes her head as she stands Gerard up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. She then spots Rated R at ringside and tells the ref to get him out og here. The ref shrugs and states that Rated R hasn't done anything to warrant his removal. Myst then turns back to Gerard and takes an eye gouge from him.
Dante - Both taking their eyes off of each other. That makes them stupid wrestlers. Casey Braun - Then why aren't you in there? Dante - I am far too important. Gerard lands a series of punches to the jaw on Myst, driving her into the corner and then hip tosses her out of it. Gerard then stands her up and whips her through the ropes to the floor. The ref has a few words with Gerard about those tactics as Rated R steps towards Myst. He grabs her by the hair and smiles. He headbutts her back into the guard railing and then he rolls her into the ring.
Casey Braun - That wasn't nice, even if he is cute. Dante - Why does cute matter? Gerard stands Myst up and looks to the crowd, set on delivering his finisher here. Myst then thumbs Gerard in the eye and gives him a side Russian leg sweep. Myst then heads to the ropes and jumps to the top where Rated R then shoves her off. The ref sees this and calls for the bell, the second disqualification in as many matches. The crowd boos as Rated R shrugs and leaves ringside. He is almost to the back when Shannon Skanes comes through the curtain and meets Rated R in the aisle, the two of them exchanging rights and lefts as security comes out to get a handle on the situation.
Dante - Gerard loses by disqualification as we all wonder why Rated R was out here in the first place.
We then look and see in the backstage area, the camera is focused on Stevie James who is preparing for his title match against Rated R later in the evening, his body is gleaming with sweat as he does some light training in the build up to the match. Suddenly, his door opens and he stops in his movements before turning to face the man who walks in. Radd Matthews just places his bag down on the sofa as he passes it and walks eye to eye with Stevie James.
Stevie James - Nice to see you again Radd, ever heard of knocking? Radd Matthews -That would be too polite for you… Stevie James - Charming…So what do you want in here? Can’t you see I’m preparing to become the new Pacific Champion?
There’s a brief silence. Stevie James - Oh…Sorry pal, I forgot wasn’t the match you lost last week and wasn’t that the match where you put the gold above your own girlfriend…Or should I say ex-girlfriend?
Radd Matthews - You son of a…Look buddy, you wanna taunt me go for it, I wanna punk you out as it is…But don’t you dare try anything with Kitty now I am not with her…I will get her back!
Stevie James - I don’t doubt that, but when will you learn Radd…I am just her friend, nothing els-
Radd Matthews - Bullshit! Suddenly, a light knock at the door comes as Lethal Weapon steps in, a smile appears of his face as both men look at him, Radd just steps back from Stevie and nods respectively at Weapon who just nods back at him. He then looks at Stevie the same smile on his face.
Lethal Weapon - Your match happening? Stevie James - Why don’t you ask Radd? I already made the offer… Weapon turns to Radd an inquisitive look on his face. Radd Matthews - Oh…I wouldn’t turn it down for the World James, but you do me one favour…If you win tonight, if you beat Rated R…I want the Pacific title on the line!
Stevie James - Gold before the woman again huh Radd? Whenever will you learn!?! Radd just grits his teeth as a smile appears of Stevie’s face… He grabs his bag and just turns from the room leaving to head back into the lobby Weapon just turns to Stevie.
Lethal Weapon - You got to concentrate on your match tonight kid, get him out your mind and start preparing yourself…
Stevie just nods and takes a second before continuing his preparation. Weapon can just be seen staring over his shoulder at the face of Stevie a look of care for the lad on his face.
Hellfire Jack vs The Moose "H" by Tool plays as The Moose makes his way to the ring which brings the crowd to their feet booing. Moose makes his way around the ring and has a few words with a couple of fans at ringside. He then holds his TV title high and places it over his shoulder as he makes his way up the ring steps onto the apron. He then steps into the ring and smiles arrogantly as he looks to the back waiting for Hellfire Jack. The Theme From Magnificent Seven plays as Hellfire Jack quickly makes his way to the ring with a sign stating that The 2 Jacks are going to both be winners here tonight. Jack hands the sign to a kid in the front row and then slides into the ring where The Moose starts putting the boots to him. Jack fights through it, pulling the legs out from under The Moose and then quickly slaps on a sharpshooter as the fans quickly get to their feet and cheer Jack on.
Dante - I haven't seen anyone knock Moose down and place him in a major hold like that so early.
Casey Braun - Maybe Jack is better than you are. Dante - Not bloody likely. Moose struggles to get free from the hold as Jack leans back and struggles to hold Moose in the center of the ring. Moose uses his forearms to try to drag himself to the ropes. Jack struggles to keep Moose from the ropes, but Moose uses his strength to lunge and manages to snag the ropes. The ref forces the break and Jack backs off to the middle of the ring. Moose quickly gets to his feet and smiles at Jack. Jack responds with a slap across the face of Moose. Moose takes a swing at Jack who ducks it and then gives Moose a German suplex to the mat before trying for a pin with s school boy. 1...2... Moose manages to roll a shoulder out. Jack then stands The Moose up, whips him off the ropes and both men clothesline each other to the mat. They both scramble to their feet and then stand nose to nose as the crowd starts chanting for Jack.
Dante - Quite the group of chanters we have tonight. What a bunch of sheep. Casey Braun - Hey, be nice to them. They paid to watch these people and have to sit through the less cute ones like Moose here.
Dante - Moose would be devastated that you do not find him cute. Moose shoves Jack. Jack eye gouges Moose. Jack headlocks Moose, but Moose manages to whip Jack off the ropes and shoulder blocks him down, then measures him for an elbowsmash that misses when Jack rolls out of the way. Jack then gets to his feet and gives Moose a standing dropkick. Hellfire Jack then stands Moose up and gives him a gutwrench suplex to the mat. He follows that up with a leg drop across the throat and goes for the pin. 1...2... Moose manages to kick out. Jack stands Moose up, whips him into the corner buckle and charges in for a shoulder tackle only to have Moose sidestep it. Jack hits the corner hard and Moose picks him up and gives him a shoulder breaker. Moose then slaps an arm bar on Jack while holding his face on the mat with his knee. The ref checks for a submission, but Jack refuses even as Moose twists the arm more and more. Jack reaches for the ropes with his free arm. Moose again tightens the hold. Jack uses his free arm to push himself off the mat and manages to rolls the hold over, pinning Moose's shoulders to the mat even as his arm is twisted more. 1...2... Moose rolls out and releases the hold. Jack rolls himself out of the ring. Moose quickly follows.
Dante - Moose is right at home outside the ring. This match is without rules, of course. Casey Braun - Yeah but it seems Jack wants to win this match fair and square. Jack rolls himself inside the ring again as Moose reaches under the apron and pulls out a table. Moose starts setting it up, but Jack baseball slides into a dropkick to the face on Moose. Moose falls back into the guard railing, the table falling from his grip. Jack kicks the table aside and rolls Moose into the ring. Jack then rolls himself inside and looks to the cheering crowd. He then picks Moose up and delivers The Spur on him and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Hellfire Jack has regained the TV title. He rolls out of the ring, favoring his shoulder as he holds his belt high once again. His theme music plays as he heads for the back.
Dante - He pinned Moose. Now that is impressive! Casey Braun - Big week for Jack here as he again become the TV title holder. We then head to commercial. We see Charley MacAdam in the GWO studio with his trademark smile. "Welcome fans to another GWO report! I am Charley MacAdam. Boy, it sure has been great seeing those CWA legends in the ring again and, as our California Dreamin' tour ends, we look forward to our trip to Arizona and beyond. Ricky Simms vs Lethal Weapon "Overrated" by Three Days Grace plays as Ricky Simms makes his way to the ring. The crowd cheers him on as he high fives fans down to ringside and then makes his way aqround the ring. He even stops to sign an autograph for a young fan before he rolls himself into the ring. He tells the camera this is all about unfinished business as he looks into the ring and nods his head as Ricky motions him to the ring.

Ricky stands, turns and takes a Golden Bullet Spear to the mat. He then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Lethal Weapon picks up the win here.

Stevie's just another Kosmic Kitty crush and history shows I have a way of crushing them. I'll crush this one, too.

Rated R - Time being? The match stipulation was clear and if I even see them holding hands I will know what a weasel Matthews is.
