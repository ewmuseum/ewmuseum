A fantasy federation of weekly Overdrive shows and monthly pay-per-views staged on its tenant directory from July 2004 to February 2005, which opened as the CWO and by its own account bought out the CWA, was renamed the Global Wrestling Organization in August 2004, and crowned its first World champion on 4 October 2004; the last of its cards in the mirror is a Valentine's edition of 14 February 2005. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The GWO begins as the CWO on 12 July 2004, a first show reported by a fan with a stopwatch, and by the card of 2 August its owner says he has bought out the CWA and cut it to a regional company. That night the Canadian, Pacific, tag and television belts are handed out as new ones, the company is renamed the Global Wrestling Organization by 9 August, a World belt is first won by Myst on 4 October, and a hardcore belt follows on 1 November.

The mirror holds twenty-six cards, weekly Overdrives and pay-per-views named Security Breach, Maximum Impact and Bloodshed, with Hector Braun, Tony Parizi and Shannon Skanes passing the booking among them; the last is a Valentine's edition of 14 February 2005, after a January card in which Shannon's controlling shares are sold to what the page calls a new venture, a company called the CWA. Anarchy Wrestling's cards of 2006 say it filled a void the GWO left when it closed its doors.

Affiliations

Championship Wrestling Association (CWA, Association, 2003)

Predecessor to GWO (Global, 2004)
The CWO's owner says on its card of 2 August 2004 that he bought the CWA out and cut it to a regional company; every title on its cards was handed out afresh that night and the CWA's belts are called titles nobody there recognises. Roster and owner carry over; the titles do not.

Anarchy Wrestling (AW, Anarchy, 2005)

Successor to GWO (Global, 2004)
Anarchy Wrestling's AW and Xtreme titles are awarded vacant on 21 December 2005, no GWO belt continues, and its card of 25 January 2006 says it fills a void that the GWO left behind when it closed its doors.
