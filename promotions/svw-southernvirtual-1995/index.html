A July 1995 WLS 5.1-simulated fed, self-declared "Southern Virtual Wrestling" by its own founding posts, crowning inaugural Heavyweight, Bunkhouse, Alabama Heavyweight, and Tag Team champions across four title tournaments run inside three weeks under a single self-chosen ring-persona Commissioner, "The Alabama Stud." Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Southern Virtual Wrestling opened 13 July 1995 under a commissioner who signed himself “The Alabama Stud,” announcing a WLS 5.1-simulated federation offering No Disqualification, I Quit, Texas Death, and Two-Out-of-Three-Falls matches, and five championships from the start: the Heavyweight, Bunkhouse, and Alabama Heavyweight titles, and the Tag Team and Alabama Tag Team titles.

The inaugural Heavyweight Tournament closed 20 July with “Marvel” Miguel Conover defeating Pain, of the tag team Torture. Pain's manager, The Master of Darkness, slipped him a pair of brass knuckles and distracted the referee, but Conover wrestled the weapon away and used it himself before finishing Pain with a five-minute Figure Four Leglock.

The Bunkhouse Tournament final followed on 24 July: “The Economist” Randy Blake, accompanied by his “executive assistant” Ms. Paula, beat an overconfident Snoop, managed by Dr. Dre, with a brainbuster-cradle-suplex combination Blake called The Recession. Three days later the Alabama Heavyweight Tournament crowned Ice Tre, also managed by Dr. Dre, who rolled up The Bushman for the pin after The Bushman climbed to the top rope instead of covering him.

The Tag Team Tournament closed the run of finals on 2 August: Canadian Carnage, Johnny Canuck and The Mountie II, managed by Sir John A. MacDonald IV, won the titles over The Privateers — Heavyweight Champion Conover and “Amazing” Rich Seroch — when MacDonald shoved Seroch off the top rope and The Mountie II rolled him up with a handful of tights.

The Alabama Tag Team Championship, announced alongside its four sister titles at the federation's founding, never held a tournament in the surviving record, and no document survives after that 2 August final.
