[SVW] New Federation!

13 July 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Get on the bandwagon! Southern Virtual Wrestling is a new federation which I have created and will run, with the help of all you e-wrestlers out there.
SVW will be run using WLS 5.1 to determine the matches. Because I am using the newest version of WLS 5.1, the following specialty matches are available:
No DQ I Quit match (Submission only.) Texas Death match (After pinfall, pinned wrestler must answer a 10 count.) 2 out of 3 Falls match Also, if you are just dying to have another type of specialty match, I will try to write it up to the best of my abilities.
Each person may have 3 wrestlers, one singles only wrestler and one tag team. Additionally, the tag team wrestlers may each wrestle singles matches as well.
SVW will start with 3 singles titles and 2 tag team titles. The singles titles are: 1) SVW Heavyweight Title 2) Bunkhouse Title (Champion must defend in No DQ matches.) 3) Alabama Heavyweight Title The tag titles are: 1) SVW Tag Team Titles 2) Alabama Tag Team Titles More titles may be added in the future, depending on the number of wrestlers in the federation.
As soon as I have enough wrestlers, I will hold tournaments for all of the titles. Cards will be held twice per week to begin with, with one card being on
Sunday and one card being on Wednesday.
My next post will contain the wrestler creation sheet for SVW. It contains three sections, one for each of your wrestlers.
Hurry and enter to make sure you are in the tournaments! The Alabama Stud [detail withheld] Commissioner of SVW
