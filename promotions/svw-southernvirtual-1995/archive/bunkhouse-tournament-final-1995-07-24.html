[SVW] Bunkhouse Tourn. Final

24 July 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and Gentleman, this match is for the SVW Bunkhouse Title, as it is the final match of the tournament. Introducing first, from Compton, California, led to the ring by his manager Dr. Dre, he stands 7' 0" and weighs in at an impressive 300 lbs., the master of The Gang Banger, Snoop!
And his opponent, from Ottawa, Ontario, Canada, he is 6' 2" and weighs in tonight at 246 lbs., accompanied to the ring by his executive assistant, the lovely Ms. Paula, here is "The Economist" Randy Blake!
Ding...Ding...Ding... This match was all Blake, as Snoop had grown overconfident with his Gang Banger. He tried to apply it too many times in bad situations, thinking that if he could just get it applied, he would own the title. Blake, however, dug deep into his pocket and pulled out some excellent moves. Blake won when he unveiled his finisher, a combination brainbuster/cradle suplex which he calls
"The Recession". Ding...Ding...Ding... Here is your winner, and NEW SVW Bunkhouse Champion, "The Economist" Randy Blake! Congratulations to Randy Blake and his handler, [name withheld]. Remember, this title will only be defended in specialty matches.
