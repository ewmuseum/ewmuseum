[SVW] Finals of Heavy. Tourn.

20 July 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall. It is the Final Match of the Inaugural SVW Heavyweight Title Tournament. Introducing first, he stands 6' 3" and weighs in at 276 lbs., from Parts Unknown, accompanied to the ring by his manager, The Master of Darkness, he is one half of Torture, Pain!
And his opponent, one half of The Privateers, standing 5' 11" and weighing in at 242 lbs., from Pomona, NJ, here is "Marvel" Miguel Conover!
Ding...Ding...Ding This match had it all. Excellent scientific moves were applied by both wrestlers. Many pure power moves were also executed by both men. Toward the end, The Master of Darkness slipped Pain a pair of brass knuckles.
The Master of Darkness then jumped up on the ring apron and distracted the referee so that Pain could make use of the international object.
Conover blocked Pain's punch, however, and took the knuckles away from him. Conover then hit Pain with the brass knuckles, which stunned Pain long enough for Conover to slap on The Figure Four Leglock. After 5 minutes in the Figure Four, Pain finally submitted.
Ding...Ding...Ding Here is your winner, and NEW SVW Heavyweight Champion, "Marvel" Miguel Conover! Congratulations to Miguel and his handler, [name withheld]. The first official card will take place as soon as all of the tournaments have been taken care of. Up next will be the tournament for the Bunkhouse Title.
Remember, all Bunkhouse Title matches will be no DQ or specialty matches. Once again, congratulations to "Marvel" Miguel Conover.
The Alabama Stud [detail withheld]
