LPW — Friday Frenzy

Friday Frenzy, 8 March 2002

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FRIDAY FRENZY - MARCH 8, 2002 (Brief LPW logo animation is shown, then fades to black. The words "3 days ago..." quickly fade in then fades out moments later to highlights of last Tuesday's legends match between Kobra Khan and
Hurricane Wild Child, ending with Terminator coming out to save HWC from a possible career ending injury.)
(Heavy metal version of the piano classical "Flight of the Bumblebee" plays while showing brief video animations of previous LPW matches 2-3 years ago. A red/white shaking Friday Frenzy logo smashes through the animations like it was glass. Scene fade to a long pan of inside the arena, the fans on their feet cheering, "LIVE - UTC Arena,
Chattanooga, TN" slides into view in the upper left corner.) (As the camera crew shows various shots of the crowd) Voice of Eric
Davidson: We are in the UTC Arena in Chattanooga, TN! Coming off the heels of our first show back in 2 years, tonight promises to be just as intense, despite not having any new players sign up lately. I'm
Eric Davidson and with me, as always, is Michael Hunter and we have 3 matches in store for you this evening with even more returning legends, including (scene changes into an animated matchup image) the mysterious Shadowlords.. Fire and Ice.. against Bull Beast and native
American, Iron Eagle.
Michael: (scene change to another matchup image) After what happened to Dead Skull at the hands of the Oriental Assassins on Tuesday, he's ready to unleash some frustration at the expense of "Maniac" Mike
Marsh.
Eric: Speaking of frustration... (another matchup screen is shown) ...can you imagine Kobra Khan's state of mind after Terminator stopped him from ending Hurricane Wild Child's career, then getting heart punched out of the ring?
Michael: They say lightning doesn't strike in the same place twice and
I believe Terminator won't be able to do that again when these two forces collide in the main event!
(as music fades to silence with a wide camera shot of the crowd...) Eric's voice: We will have a medical update on Hurricane Wild Child later in the show. Right now, let's go to our first match of the night!
(The sound of an indian dance and drum begins to play. Bull Beast and
Iron Eagle come out during their introduction and walk to the ring.)
Marty Jennings: Our opening contest is scheduled for 1 fall. Coming down the aisle... at a combined weight of 566 lbs... the team of...
Bull Beast and Iron Eagle! Eric: What do you think their chances are of beating the Shadowlords? (Beast and Eagle enter the ring.) Michael: The same chances as an ice cube staying frozen on a hot sunny day... no chance at all.
(The indian chants fade to the sound of wind blowing and fire burning. The lights slowly fade alternately from orange to blue, over and over as two hooded men make their way out.)
Marty Jennings: Their opponents... at a combined weight of 600 lbs... Fire and Ice... the Shadowlords! Eric: This is one of the most dominant tag teams LPW has ever known. They are relentless and show no mercy whatsoever. Michael: And that's just when they are in a *good* mood. *They enter the ring as the music fades away and the lights return to normal. They remove their hoods and robes. Bull Beast insists on starting for his team. Iron Eagle lets him. Ice starts with him. They lock up and Ice shoves Beast in the corner with authority. Beast pauses a moment and comes at Ice with his Gore finisher but bounces right off to no effect. Beast gets up, runs off the ropes and tries the Gore again. Still no effect. Beast tries yet again. This time, Ice catches Beast, lifts him upright, leaps in the air and plants him with a modified tombstone piledriver.*
Eric: WHOA! Now is that scary strength or what? Michael: You got that right... *Ice tags in Fire. They double team Beast sending him in the ropes and does a double press slam throw from inside the ring to the outside next to the aisle. Beast lands flat on his back.*
Michael: Correction... THAT was scary! Eric: And Iron Eagle is making no attempt to save his partner! Just standing there watching. (camera on Eagle for a moment as Ice steps outside to pick up Beast and throw him back inside.)
*Fire picks Beast up and slams him in the middle of the ring. He and Ice go up to the top turnbuckles in opposite corners. Fire leaps off and connects with a splash to Beast's head and chest. Ice does the same.*
Michael: Bull Beast should change his name to Human Punching Bag... Eric: As I stated earlier, they are relentless. *Beast slowly turns over and crawls to his corner. Both Fire and Ice make no attempt to stop him. Beast finally makes it there and extends a shaking hand. The camera can't hear him but it looked like Eagle called him pathetic. He steps off the apron and starts walking back up the aisle.*
Eric: What's he doing?? Michael: Well, um... I guess he wanted a partner who could take care of himself and Bull Beast obviously can't.
*Fire and Ice drag Beast back to the center of the ring and go back up to the top turnbuckles. Beast very slowly gets up and the Shadowlords leap at him and hit with their Arctic Inferno double clothesline.
Beast drops to the mat unconscious. Fire makes a cover. 1... 2... 3!* Eric: Mercifully, it's over. Marty Jennings: Here are your winners... the Shadowlords! *They pick up Beast and throw him out the ring.*
(Scene change to backstage, showing Dead Skull walking down the hall with the cameraman walking in front of him. He's getting psyched up for his match.) Eric: Coming up after the break, Dead Skull goes 1 on
1 with "Maniac" Mike Marsh and you got to believe the Assassins will be watching close by. (Goes around a corner and bumps into someone he never expected to see again...) Stay with us, we'll be right bac...
Michael: (interrupting) Wait, wait! Stay right here! Eric: (seeing who it is Skull ran into, in a very concerned tone) ...oh my god... it's Devastator! (The former "demon in black", now head of security, towers over Dead
Skull with a burning look in his eyes. There is a long pause before a single word is spoken between them.)
Devastator: You have a lot of nerve showing your face around here again, especially after what you and the Slayers did to my brother.
Skull: (after swallowing hard) ...Hey, that was an accident.... we didn't mean to kill anybody. It just... happened. I can't speak for the others but... I'm sorry... (Devastator quickly grabs him by the throat, lifts him up to eye level with ease and slams him against a wall, almost breaking it, holding him up there.)
Devastator: BEING SORRY WON'T BRING ANNIHILATOR BACK!! (pauses as
Skull struggles for air) But if you EVER mess with me or my family again... (pulls Skull right to his face, eye to eye) (in a cold hatred tone) ... you... will... join... him...
(Devastator throws Skull through the already fractured wall with authority. Security arrives to get Devastator away from him. As most of security make him leave, one of them radios for an EMT crew as another tries to help Skull out of the wall.)
(Fade to black.) ------------------------------ *Commercials* ------------------------------ (Fade in to backstage. Dead Skull is sitting down in his dressing room. A medic is using a small light to check his vision. As he continues the testing, the scene changes to the commentators.)
Eric: In case you're just now tuning in... before we went to commercial, Dead Skull ran into an old nemesis of his in the form of security chief Devastator. Words were exchanged and the big man threw
Skull right through a wall.
Michael: (pauses, looking at his show notes) Well, I'm assuming we will go straight to the main event match between Kobra Khan and
Terminator. Eric: It's the only match left and Dead Skull is injured so we have to. ("Jam On It" by Newcleus plays. Crowd cheers.) ...what's this?
("Mastermind" B.T. Anthony and senior referee Daniel Tappen makes their way out and to the ring. BT is wearing a long blue casual suit jacket with the sleeves pushed up to the elbows, no shirt, full length silver tights and silver boots.)
Eric: It's B.T. Anthony, former owner of Legend Pro Wrestling! Michael: What's he doing out here?? Eric: I noticed he's dressed for competition but... why? (BT and Daniel enters the ring. BT asks for a mic. He pauses to take in the cheers from the fans as the music fades away.) BT: Being Darren
Diamond's business advisor, I think it would be best to give Dead
Skull time to recover so he can compete tonight. (crowd cheers) I've been talking with another returning legend in the back and after seeing what Devastator did, we decided that, while Dead Skull is recovering, we will have ourselves a bonus legends match. Right here, right now. (crowd cheers louder)
Eric: All right! Any match with B.T. Anthony in it will definitely be exciting to see! Michael: This should be a great matchup, even though we don't know who he's facing... yet. (BT returns the mic to Marty Jennings, hands him notes on how to introduce them and removes his jacket. His physique has greatly improved since LPW last closed 2 years ago.)
Marty: The following contest is a special BONUS match, scheduled for 1 fall. In the ring... from Nashville, TN... weighing 252 lbs... former
8-time World champion... "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony! (crowd cheers)
Michael: (to Eric) Who you think it is? Eric: I have no idea. (The music of a slow oriental funeral song plays. It takes the crowd a few moments to recognize it but once they do, the crowd cheers. A very tall man in red/gold asian ceremonial robes, long white hair and a wide rim straw cone hat walks out and slowly down the aisle. The hat blocks his eyes from the camera.)
Eric: Oooooooooooooh yeah! Michael: This is gonna be GOOD! Marty: His opponent... from Beijing, China... weighing 339 lbs... the
"chinese undertaker", Shi'Noku! (he enters the ring)
Eric: Now this is what I call a bonus! Michael: I don't remember these two ever facing each other before. Do you? Eric: I think they did at Doomsday back in 1993 but I'm not sure. *Shi'Noku removes his hat and robes. His eyes are solid white. BT continues to stretch and prepare. Daniel calls them to the center of the ring. As they stare at each other, with BT having to look up a little, the crowd go crazy. In classic "Mastermind" style, BT smirks before trying to shove Shi'Noku back. Shi'Noku's feet never moved.
Shi'Noku envelopes BT's face with his huge hand and shoves him back in the corner with force.*
Eric: Does this remind you of a certain legendary confrontation years ago? Michael: Definitely! BT Anthony vs Devastator, one of the 3 greatest World title matches ever... with BT being the first and ONLY man to make Devastator submit!
*They go to lock up and BT drives a few hard knees in Shi'Noku's gut. BT goes to whip Shi'Noku off the ropes. Shi'Noku holds on to the top rope, preventing it. BT tries a couple more times with no success.
Shi'Noku grabs BT's arm that he's holding to and twists it slightly, forcing BT to let go.*
Eric: That's one way of getting out of it. Michael: No kidding.. *Shi'Noku repositions himself away from the ropes, still holding BT's arm, and connects with a short clothesline. He waits for BT to get up.* Michael: BT needs to turn on the heat or he's a goner. *BT slowly gets up. Shi'Noku positions him in the ropes, whips him off, misses a clothesline, BT on the rebound hits with a flying shoulderblock. Shi'Noku stumbles back and goes down to one knee.*
Eric: Uh oh, he's coming back! *BT sets up Shi'Noku for a suplex and raises him up. He hold him in the air for 5 seconds before completing the move. Covers him... 1... Shi'Noku kicks out.* Michael: I'm surprised he was able to hold him up that long. Eric: Me too, considering how long BT has been inactive as a wrestler. *BT picks him up and goes for a bodyslam. Shi'Noku slips behind him and applies a dragon sleeper.* Eric: A dragon sleeper on BT Anthony? He's usually the one using that, not being in one himself!
*But Shi'Noku had different plans. Still using a dragon sleeper, he lifts BT up and drops him over in an inverted suplex.* Michael: Daaaaaaaaaamn! Eric: Someone get BT some Advil or something! He's gonna need it after tonight! *Shi'Noku makes a cover. 1... 2... kickout.* Michael: Almost had him there. *Shi'Noku gets BT to his feet, lifts him up and places him on the top rope. Shi'Noku goes up to the second rope and sets BT up for a superplex.* Eric: Shi'Noku seems to be looking to score a knockout blow. This might be it! *BT punches Shi'Noku in the kidney area a few times, then pushes Shi'Noku away, making him land hard on the mat. BT gets on the top turnbuckle and flies off for a 360 splash. Shi'Noku barely moves out of the way just in time. BT is folded over in pain.*
Michael: Ooooooooooooo, he's gonna feel that in the morning. Eric: Longer than that, I believe... *Shi'Noku makes another cover. 1... 2... kickout, but barely.* Eric: Uh oh, I can tell BT is almost out of it. Can Shi'Noku score what could be considered a major upset? (Shi'Noku makes a "cut the throat" gesture, signaling the end may be near.)
Michael: We won't have to wait long to find out! *Shi'Noku picks him up and positions him for a tombstone, then executes it.* Eric: That's it... that has to be! Michael: I don't see him getting out of this one. *Shi'Noku makes yet another cover. 1... 2... shoulder up, but barely.* Eric: How the...??? Where is he getting the strength to do this?? Michael: ...I have no idea. *Shi'Noku picks BT up, sends him in the ropes, misses a clothesline and BT bounces off the other side for his famous Back Roll. (He leaps at Shi'Noku for a crossbody press but quickly spins his body so that his back is on Shi'Noku's chest instead. They both go down in a crossbody position, yet the momentum swings Shi'Noku's legs up, BT grabs one with all his weight on Shi'Noku's head and upper chest, using one of his legs to prevent being rolled over.) 1... 2... 3!*
Eric: He got him!! B.T. Anthony has won it! (BT rolls over, face down, to catch his breath.)
Marty Jennings: Here is your winner... "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony! *Shi'Noku sits on the mat in disbelief for a moment, then stands up. He helps BT get back to his feet and raises his hand to a standing ovation.* Michael: I told you he would win. Eric: Suuuuuuuuuuuure you did... I just recieved word that the EMTs requested more time with Dead Skull so we will move on with what was scheduled to be the main event. Before we do, let me inform everyone that Hurricane Wild Child only suffered a migraine headache and minor internal bleeding in his forehead. He will be back in action sometime next week. (scene change to animated matchup screen) Coming up after the break, Kobra Khan and Terminator do battle in the squared circle!
"Venom" vs "Broken Heart"... NEXT! ------------------------------ *Commercials* ------------------------------ (Fade in to a wide crowd shot as the theme from Terminator starts to play. Terminator steps out and stops at the top of the aisle.)
Marty Jennings: The following contest is scheduled for 1 fall. Making his way to the ring... from Phoenix, AZ... weighing 235 lbs...
Terminator! Eric: (to Michael) You really think he can't hit Khan with another heart punch, eh? Michael: He caught Khan when he wasn't looking on Tuesday and got in a cheap shot, namely the Broken Heart.
(As Terminator enters the ring...) Eric: Well, keep in mind... in years past, Terminator once defeated Khan with that very move.
Michael: But once Khan gets his Venom latched on your head, it's all over. (Slow Egyptian music plays as Kobra Khan walks out and down the aisle.) Marty Jennings: His opponent... from Cairo, Egypt... weighing 266 lbs... Kobra Khan! (loud boos from the crowd)
Michael: Terminator and Kobra Khan are currently among the top 3 contenders for the vacant World title at #1 and #3, respectively.
Eric: Yes they are, with Hurricane Wild Child between them at #2. (Khan enters the ring) Michael: He won't be after this match, I'll tell you that now... *Terminator and Khan charge each other and the punches start swinging.* Michael: Here we go! Not wasting any time! *Terminator thumbs Khan in the eye, sends him in the ropes and knocks him down with a clothesline. Khan gets right back up, Terminator clotheslines him again.* Eric: So far, Terminator has the upper hand. Michael: Why does Terminator keep insist on using cheap shots to get the advantage? Eric: Oh but it wouldn't be cheap if Khan does it, right? Michael: You are correct, sir! Eric: Whatever... *Terminator goes to pick him up and Khan jabs him in the throat.* Eric: Now what do you call that?? Michael: Call what? I didn't see what he did... Eric: You were looking right at it! *Khan grabs Terminator from behind and flips him over in a belly to back suplex.* Michael: Yes, that was an impressive belly to back suplex. Eric: *sigh* Damn, you're impossible sometimes... *Khan covers him. 1... kick out. He chokes Terminator with a hand on his throat. Ref starts a 5 count and Khan breaks it at 4. He repeats this 2 more times.* Eric: Is all that choking really necessary?? Michael: That's what a snake does to its prey... choke the life out of it. *Khan picks him off, goes to send him in the ropes, Terminator reverses and powerslams Khan into an immediate cover. 1... 2... kickout. Terminator gets up and drops a series of elbow drops to the heart. Another cover. 1... 2... kickout.*
Eric: Terminator is no ordinary prey, mind you. *Terminator picks Khan up and throws him hard in the corner. He signals for the Broken Heart and charges at full speed, preparing to strike. Khan side steps him and Terminator hits his fist on the ringpost. Khan gets behind Terminator as he holds his hand in agony and slaps on the Venom finisher.*
Michael: There it is! The most feared submission hold in LPW history! Eric: C'mon Terminator, fight out of it! *Still holding Venom on, he drags Terminator around the ring. Terminator eventually taps out. Surprisingly, Khan let go!* Eric: Damn it... Michael: That victory just made him the new #1 contender for the title! Marty Jennings: Here is your winner, by submission... Kobra Khan! *Khan gets a few more kicks in then leaves. Terminator is barely able to roll out the ring.* Eric: We're taking our last break and when we return, we will find out if Dead Skull will be able to face "Maniac" Mike Marsh or not. We'll be right back...
------------------------------------- *Commercials* ------------------------------------- (Fade in to crowd shot) #Let's do it...# ("Wild Thing" by Tone Loc begins to play.) Eric: We're back... but I'm not sure if Dead Skull will be. Marty Jennings: (as Marsh walks out) The following contest is scheduled for 1 fall. Coming down the aisle... from Henderson, TN...
weighing 215 lbs... "Maniac" Mike Marsh! Michael: We haven't heard anything from the medics yet so I say he won't. (Marsh enters the ring and gets the mic. The music fades away.)
Maniac: Hey, I've been waiting all night to fight this Dead Skull guy and he ain't been out here at all. (to the referee) Save yourself some time and raise my hand since he will not... come ... out...
("For Whom The Bell Tolls" by Metallica plays. Maniac turns with surprise... along with everybody else. Skull slowly walks out with a bandaged head. He has a long bag with him.)
Eric: He's actually going through with it? After being slammed through a wall earlier tonight??
Michael: Well DUH! Why else would he be out here? Marty Jennings: His opponent... from Naguabo, Puerto Rico... weighing
225 lbs... Dead Skull! (He enters the ring)
Michael: What could be in that bag he's holding? Eric: (mocking Michael) Well DUH, how should I know? Michael: What are you, a copycat? Quit stealing my lines! *Dead Skull sets the bag in a corner and quickly charges Maniac with a clothesline. Skull stomps his head a few times before having to stop due to pain in his own head.*
Michael: I still can't figure out why the EMTs would let him wrestle. *Skull climbs up to the top rope and waits for Maniac to get up. When he does, Skull goes for the Line Of Order (diving spear into a boston crab). The spear connects and rolls Maniac over into a boston crab.
Maniac struggles to stay in the match and pulls himself to a bottom rope to break it.* Eric: He's risking further injury by being in this match. Michael: But at least he's winning. *Skull picks him up, goes to whip him in the ropes but throws him in the referee instead.* Eric: What....... what did Skull do that for?? *Skull goes to his bag and pulls out a metal bat.* Michael: Does THAT answer your question? Eric: Aw c'mon, Skull! Maniac had nothing to do with the attacks you endured all week! *Skull violently hits Maniac in the head, back, arms and legs with the bat non-stop. The beatings become so severe that Maniac passes out from the pain. Skull tosses the bat aside as the ref regains his senses. Skull covers him... 1... 2... 3!*
Michael: It's a miracle! Not even Devastator could prevent him from scoring a hard fought victory!
Eric: What??? The only thing "hard fought" was that bat breaking
Maniac's body! *Skull rolls out of the ring and grabs a chair, comes back in, chases the ref out the ring and continues the onslaught, even though Maniac is out cold.* Eric: Why is he doing this?? (After the savage attack, Dead Skull drops the chair and signals for a mic, then snatches it from the ring announcer. He rolls Marsh out of the ring then begins to speak, the fans now booing him loudly for his actions.)
Skull: SCREW ALL OF YOU!!! (The fans' boos grow louder.) YOU ALL
TURNED YOUR BACKS ON ME!!! (The boos intensify with every word.) I don't care... (The fans just keep booing) I've been attacked in the back by an oversized freak!!! I've been three on oned by a bunch of half witted wannabe ninjas, who got kicked out their country for their stupidity because their country was ashamed of those jackasses!!! I'm sick and tired and I don't care how much you backstabbing morons boo me... I'm gonna get revenge on each and every one of these pieces of crap!!! Starting NOW!!!
(The fans boos intensify again as Dead Skull evil grin once again come accross his face.) Michael: He's lost it, Eric... Eric: I agree. This hasn't been a good week for Dead Skull. This isn't how he wanted to make his return at all...
Skull: 3 on 1... you piece of crap Assassins attacked me 3 on 1! Well guess what? You want a 3 on 1? I'll give you a 3 on 1. I'll take on all of you by myself if I have to!! So why don't you get the f*** down here and fight me... RIGHT NOW!!!
(Dead Skull throws the mic down and holds the chair in attack position. The fans boos change to sort of a mixed reaction as the oriental music of the Assassins begins playing tauntingly. All 3
Assassins slowly make their way out from the back to the ring. They surround the outside of the ring, leaving Dead Skull easy prey in the middle. Everytime one of them tries to come in Dead Skull swings at their heads with all his might in prevention. With Ninja Master and
Dragon Ninja distracting Skull, Samauri Master starts to get on the ring apron but at the same time, someone comes out from the crowd carrying a barbwired bat and nails Samauri from behind with it.)
Eric: Who....?? (recognizes him) It's Songi! ...of the Puerto Rican
Slayers!! Michael: Where did HE come from??? (As the other two notice Samauri Master's bad fortune, Dead Skull swiftly and silently knocks the hell out of Ninja Master with the chair. Dragon Ninja slips in and tackles Dead Skull from behind but
Songi knocks him out immediately with the barbedwire bat. Dead Skull grabs the mic again.)
Dead Skull: Odds are even... for now... THE SLAYERS... ARE... BACK!! (Dead Skull hands the mic over to Songi.) Songi: ....die slow.... (They drag Dragon Ninja down the aisle and dissapear into the locker room area. The camera follows and we see Dead Skull grabbing his own barbedwire bat from a locker and then begin to beat Dragon Ninja with the bats. They beat him on the legs and stomach mostly. Then Songi grabs the camera, gives it a big, evil smile and tosses it to the ground. Camera cuts back to ringside where the other Assassins are still knocked out cold. After a few minutes the bells begin to ring again but no song comes on this time. Just the bells repeating. We hear a voice.)
Dead Skull: This is what happens when you mess with the Slayers. (The cameras look all around the arena with no luck in finding The
Slayers.) Dead Skull: No you dumbasses.... up here. (The camera looks up and zooms in on the rafter where the Slayers have
Dragon Ninja laced with a noose apparently around his neck, still unconcious. They both carefully lift him up over the rail. Then they both let go and drop him. The noose tightens, not around his neck though, but around Dragon Ninja's knees as he hangs there upside down.
The other 2 Assassins wake up to see, on the big screen, their partner hanging upside, they look up and see him hanging from the rafters.
They then run to his rescue looking to be in a rather irate mood. We see them running up the stairs to the aid of their friend. The scene cuts to Dead Skull and Songi leaving the Assassins locker rooms with three bags, each one belonging to a different member of the Assassins.
They slip into a car and leave the arena as the show fades to black....)
