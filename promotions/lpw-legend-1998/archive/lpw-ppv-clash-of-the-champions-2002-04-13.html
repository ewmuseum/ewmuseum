LPW — Pay-Per-View

Clash Of The Champions

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CLASH OF THE CHAMPIONS - PPV ICE PALACE - TAMPA, FL **Small LPW initials logo fades into view in front of dark blue blurry moving clouds. Each letter flashes from left to right, then all 3 at once. The entire scene fades to black. The theme of tonight's event, "The Way You
Like It" by Adema, starts to play as the scene shows blurry images of previous matches, rapidly changing. The words "Clash of the Champions" slide into view from all 4 sides of the screen.**
Male voiceover: And now.... Legend Pro Wrestling presents.... Clash Of The
Champions! **After a couple moments, the scene fades to inside the Ice Palace as red, white and blue pyros explode at the videotron. The lights return to normal as the crowd is on their feet cheering. Cameramen pan various sections of them.**
Voice of Eric Davidson: The Ice Palace in Tampa, Florida has been sold out for weeks! This... is the 'Clash Of The Champions'! And tonight, the World championship tournament comes to an end as Jake MacKenna and Superfight champion Kobra Khan meet in the main event tonight. The winner will become the new LPW champion!
**The scene now changes to the commentator table at ringside.** Eric: Hello everyone, I'm Eric Davidson and the card has been changed from 4 matches to 3. The reason is because Andrew Dixon has failed to arrive. We don't know the details on why Dixon isn't here but he was scheduled to go one on one with Ultimate Warrior in a superfight. So now, that match won't take place. That leaves 3 title matches that WILL happen tonight. And with that said, let's go up to the ring for our first championship match!
**The event music fades to silence. A few moments pass before "Cemetery Gates" by Pantera starts to play. The fans begin to boo as Wesley Potter and his new manager make their way out. They stop at the top of the aisle and do a pose together before walking down the aisle. Referee Stephen Woods is holding the National title belt in his hands.**
FOR THE VACANT LPW NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP JOHNNY ZIRO -VS- WESLEY POTTER (W/ BRITTANY) Referee: Stephen Woods [DING, DING, DING] "Shorty" Marty Jennings: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and it is for the LPW National championship. Introducing first... being accompanied to the ring by Brittany Bankok... from Jonesville, VA...
weighing 250 lbs... Wesley Potter! (They enter the ring.) Eric: Last night on Friday Frenzy... (Scene changes to highlights of what
Eric is explaining)... the Ziro brothers and Jim "Hardass" Masters surprised everyone when they made this announcement after brutally assaulting the
Assassin...
Johnny Ziro: We are the guys you don't wanna mess with...... we are the guys that if you screw us, you'll get the same treatment as the Assassin here...... we are...... THE RAGIN' REDNECKS!!
**Scene changes back to in the ring as Wesley and Brittany was watching that video footage and talks strategy among themselves as the music fades to silence. After a few moments, "Renegade" by Tim McGraw starts to play. The fan cheer as Matt Ziro comes out to the ring instead of Johnny. He's holding the same bat he was carrying backstage earlier in the day.**
Eric: Wait a second... That's Matt Ziro coming out... it's supposed to be
Johnny Ziro in this match, not him.
Marty: His opponent... from Nashville, TN... weighing 320 lbs... he is a member of the Ragin' Rednecks... Johnny Ziro!
**Matt gets to the ring and rolls in. He starts taunting them with his bat, threatening to hit them with it. The music fades to silence... but the fans are a bit confused.**
Eric: Did something happen to Johnny that we don't know about? I don't see him out here anywhere.
**Johnny comes out of the crowd next to where Eric is sitting and sneaks in the ring behind Potter. The fans cheer his arrival.** Eric: There he is! He's sneaking in through the back door! **Johnny clotheslines Wesley from behind, knocking him down. Matt chases Brittany out of the ring and up the aisle until she is gone. He returns to the ring and stands guard, watching for her to try to come back out. The ref hands the title belt to the timekeeper and starts the match.**
[DING, DING] Eric: The Rednecks mean business tonight and we're underway! **Johnny picks up Wesley and lifts him overhead in a press slam.** Eric: Going up... **Johnny drops Wesley behind him.** Eric: ...and down! Johnny in full control at the moment. **As he raises a fist in the air and the fans cheer it, he turns around and picks up Wesley with the other hand, lowering the fist. Johnny whips him in the ropes and goes for a clothesline. Wesley runs under and comes off the other side with a crossbody press. Johnny catches him with ease.**
Eric: Caught him in mid air! That's 250 pounds! **Johnny takes a couple steps towards the ropes and throws Wesley over and behind him like Bradshaw does.** Eric: Potter is taking a beating so far. At this rate, the Rednecks will easily score their first championship. Here's a cover...
[1..... 2...... kickout] Eric: Potter is not out of it yet but he needs to find a way to turn the tide. **Johnny picks him up again and takes him to the nearest corner. He knees Wesley in the gut, hits him in the head with a forearm smash then rams his head in the top turnbuckle.**
Eric: Wow, Johnny is all over him, almost not giving him a chance to breathe. **Johnny hits him with a back elbow smash. He picks up Wesley on his shoulder and turns around to do a running powerslam. He starts to do it,
Wesley slides off his shoulder and pushes him in the opposite corner as hard as he can. After impact, Johnny staggers back and gets caught in a german suplex. Fans boo Wesley.**
Eric: Potter finally getting in some offense. Let's see if he can keep it up.... Potter going for his first cover....
[1..... 2..... kickout] **Wesley picks him up and sets the big man up for a fisherman suplex.** Eric: Potter-plex coming up..... **Wesley lifts him over and holds him down.** Eric: And he gets it... can he keep Johnny there? [1..... 2.... kickout] Eric: I guess not. **Wesley gets him to his feet and struggles to get him up on the top rope, but does. Wesley goes up with him and signals for a spinebuster.** Eric: He's going for the Superstar Maker... if he hits this, we could be looking at our first National champion!
**But the Redneck had other ideas. He punches at Wesley while holding him with the other hand to keep him from falling off. The fans start cheering lightly.** Eric: Johnny trying to fight his way out of it... **Johnny moves his legs around to behind the ropes and positions Wesley for a powerbomb. The cheers of the fans increase in anticipation...** Eric: ...No way... you got to be kidding! A powerbomb from the top rope?? **As Johnny starts to execute the top rope powerbomb, flashes from cameras in the crowd light up the ring. WHAM!!** Eric: Oh my.... god! That's it! That has to be! **Brittany runs back out to the ring. Matt sees her.** Eric: Uh oh, here comes Brittany! And there's Matt to greet her! **As Matt keeps her from getting to ringside, the Assassin jumps out from the crowd. He has his club with him. Neither the ref or even Johnny doesn't see him yet.** Eric: Hold on... there's Assassin! What's he doing out here? **Assassin drops the club for a moment as he reaches up to grab the ref by the face and chest, pulling him out of the ring and stuff him under the ring. Stephen never saw who it was as Johnny is making a cover, hooking the leg. There's no ref to count and the fans are really booing what's happening.**
Eric: Assassin just stuffed the referee under the ring! This is gonna get ugly at any moment!
**Johnny gets back up and looks around for the ref, wondering why a count was not made. Wesley is also starting to get up. By the time Johnny sees
Assassin, he is struck in the head with the club. Matt notices this and runs after him. Assassin quickly runs out of the ring and into the crowd, with
Matt in hot pursuit.**
Eric: Assassin getting a bit of revenge for last night, it seems! **Johnny staggers back while turning around. Wesley kicks him in the gut and sets him up for the Final Cut pedigree. He hits it as the ref finally makes his way back in the ring and a cover is made.**
Eric: Final Cut! Will that do it?? [1..... 2..... 3!] [DING, DING, DING] Eric: Johnny Ziro got a taste of his own medicine and we have a new National champion! **"Cemetery Gates" plays again. The fans boo very loudly as Brittany enters the ring and congratulates Wesley as he gets back to his feet. Wesley is handed the National title and he raises it overhead in a victory stance.**
Marty: The winner of this match..... and NEW *United States* National champion..... Wesley Potter!
**Matt runs back to the ring in shock but by the time he gets back inside, Wesley and Brittany had already left and are walking up the aisle. The title belt is still held overhead as Matt now turns his attention to his big brother. The ref is checking on him also.**
Eric: The Assassin practically snatched the National title away from Johnny
Ziro, who clearly had this match won. I can't imagine what the Rednecks are going to do about this!
**Scene changes to backstage as the music fades away. Jake MacKenna is looking for Hillary, calling her name. The fans cheer when seeing him. But
Hillary doesn't answer. He notices LPW owner Darren Diamond nearby, going over some new applicant contracts. Jake goes to him.**
Jake: Excuse me, Mr. Diamond? Darren: (looking over to him) Hey Jake, how are you tonight? (He shakes
Jake's hand) Jake: Just fine, thank you. Getting ready for my match tonight and all.... Darren: Good luck out there. Kobra Khan will not be easy to beat. Jake: I understand... but I wanted to ask you if you have seen my girlfriend, Hillary, around lately.
**Darren pauses to think it over...** Darren: Oh yes, I did. She had to leave to take care of "something important". Jake: By herself?? Darren: No... she wasn't alone... I noticed Sherry Sheryl went with her... **Fans cheer loudly after hearing that, suspecting more female bonding taking place somewhere. Jake looks surprised then walks away, not knowing what to think of this.**
**Scene changes back to Eric DAvidson.** Eric: Well, um... seems Hillary won't be in Jake's corner later tonight when he faces the ever dangerous Superfight champion Kobra Khan later tonight for the LPW title....
**Scene changes to a wide shot of the crowd as techno japanese music starts to play and the fans cheer who is coming out. The reigning tag team champions, the Oriental Assassins, make their way out and to the ring.**
WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH *FINAL CONFRONTATION* SUPERFIGHT (C) 'ORIENTAL ASSASSINS' -VS- 'PUERTO RICAN SLAYERS' Referee - Ted Hawking [DING, DING, DING] Marty: The following contest, scheduled for one fall... is the "final confrontation" superfight... for the LPW tag team championship! Introducing first... from Tokyo, Japan... at a combined weight of 722 lbs... they are the LPW tag team champions... Ninja Master, Samauri Master and Dragon
Ninja... the Oriental Assassins! Eric: (as they enter the ring) This will be a 3 on 2 war! All three Assassins against the 2 Slayers. And remember, if the Assassins lose, they will leave Legend Pro Wrestling permanently.
**Ninja and Samauri remove their title belts and give them to the ref. The Assassins stand in the center of the ring and pose in a variety of combat stances as the camera flashes light up all around them. Their music fades to silence. Moments later, "For Whom The Bell Tolls" by Metallica chimes in the arena to very loud boos.**
Marty: Their opponents and challengers.... at a combined weight of 449 lbs... Dead Skull and Songi... the Puerto Rican Slayers!
Eric: This fued was rekindled the day of LPW's return to national television back on March 5. And tonight, this superfight will be the last time these two factions ever meet again.
**No one comes out. The music fades to silence and the fans start to quiet down some.** Eric: Where are the Slayers? Were the injuries made by the Shadowlords more serious than we thought?
**As the champions continue to wait, alert for anything, Dragon Ninja goes outside to the ring to greet some ringside fans dressed in ninja outfits and fake title belts.**
Eric: Well, at this rate, the Assassins will end this rivalry without having to face their arch rivals.
**The masked fans suddenly grab Dragon and pull him over the railing, punching and kicking. The other Assassins sees what's going on and runs out to stop it.** Eric: Wha... those fans just yanked Dragon Ninja from ringside and are beating him further into the crowd! And here comes his partners!
**Ninja knocks down one masked fan, Samauri with the other. After a few punches, they rip off the masks...** Eric: It's the Slayers! They were out here the whole time and we didn't know it! **They fight through the cheering crowd with Dragon Ninja and the referee following behind and go to a backstage area.** Eric: All 5 men disappearing in this sea of humanity.... I can't make out where they are now...
**Scene on the videotron changes to the concession area. Samauri grabs a cup of beer, removes his mask, takes a big gulp and spits it in Songi's eyes.** Eric: This one's for you.... literally! **Ninja has Skull by a t-shirt stand and slams his head in the wooden stand a few times. He sees a Die Slow shirt close by and grabs it.** Ninja: 5 dollar for shirt! You buy shirt for 5 dollar? I give to you free! **Ninja wraps the shirt around Skull's neck and chokes him with it. Samauri starts to whip Songi in a popcorn stand. Songi reverses and Samauri hits it back first, almost knocking it to the ground.**
Eric: Ouch, Samauri's back slamming into metal... you know that won't feel good.... **Skull kicks Ninja with a low blow kick to escape. Songi reaches over and grabs a can of salt and shakes a lot of it in 2 bags of fresh buttered popcorn. He takes the bags, walks over to Samauri and dumps most of it in his face and eyes, then slings the rest everywhere.**
Songi: What's wrong? Don't you oriental bastards like... (stomps his head at the same time the next word is said) ... popcorn??
**Dragon runs at him from out of nowhere with a spear attempt but Songi sensed it and moves aside. Dragon hits the wall head first. Songi grabs an empty popcorn container and pours some hot butter in it. He goes to Dragon, forces one eye open with his fingers and splashes the butter right on the eye itself. Dragon screams in pain, holding his eye. Skull walks over to
Dragon.**
Skull: How you like that, you asian piece of s***?? Songi: Look out! **Songi quickly grabs another cup of beer and throws it at a charging Ninja Master, right in the face. Ice cubes scatter all over the floor as Ninja falls down.** Skull: I'll be back in a minute.... (he leaves out of camera range) Songi: What? Where the f*** you going?? **Skull doesn't answer or look back.** Eric: What in the world could Dead Skull be doing?? He's leaving Songi alone with 2 remaining Assassins, Dragon Ninja is apparently unable to continue.
**Just then, a man runs up from behind Ninja Master with a barbwire bat...** Eric: Who the...?? It's Michael Hunter, my former broadcast partner! He's come to the Slayers' aid!
**Michael slams the bat in Samauri's gut.** Songi: About time you showed up! **Without warning, Michael swings the bat right in Songi's face, knocking him down and out cold.** Eric: YES! Michael turned on the Slayers!! The Assassins are gonna win it!! **A small black dart hits Michael on the left side of the forehead. The effects of it are immediate and Michael drops to the floor unconscious.** Eric: The damn dart, again? From where?? **Camera turns around to see a ninja leap down from a nearby booth. He is defined and sleeveless, you can see the ripped muscles on his arms. Estimated to be around 6 feet tall. He walks over to where Songi is, looks down at him for a moment then looks right in the camera. You can see eerie gray eyes through the hood.**
Eric: OH MY..... GOD! IT'S SUICIDAL!! ONE OF THE ORIGINAL SLAYERS!! **Suicidal charges at Ninja and side kicks him over the t-shirt stand counter. He hops over it, grabs Ninja's head below the counter where the camera can't see and quickly pulls back his arms as if he twisted something.
Suicidal leaps back across and goes to Samauri, who is getting up.** Eric: ....Don't tell me he just...... no, he couldn't have.... **Suicidal charges and spears Samauri smack into the wall at full velocity, almost breaking it. Suicidal grabs him by the hair, forces him up and carries him back in the crowd.**
Eric: Suicidal is single handedly annihilating the champions! **As Suicidal and Samauri start to pass under the upper seating section, Dead Skull appears at the railing, looking down.** Eric: Wait a minute. There's Skull up there! **Skull waits for the right moment, climbs to the top of the railing and flies off at both Samauri and Suicidal with a shooting star press!** Eric: Oh my.... god! Did you see that??? **Suicidal appears to not be hurt much since he's already starting to get back up. He helps Skull up and takes him over the ringside barrier and puts him in the ring. Suicidal then goes back for Samauri...**
Eric: Skull in the ring now, almost out of it... Suicidal going back to bring Samauri in....
**The Assassin, from the Ziro-Potter match, runs out from the crowd and quickly slips in the ring.** Eric: Assassin?? What's he doing back out here? **Assassin rolls Skull on his back, rips Skull's shirt off then pulls out a small can of spray paint from his boot. He sprays a silver "X" on his chest. After Suicidal tosses Samauri over the barrier, he sees Assassins and quickly slides in. But Assassin rolls out and goes back in the crowd.**
Eric: Assassin strikes again! This time, he's made his mark on Dead Skull !
**Suicidal goes out the ring and throws Samauri inside. He rolls in and immediately spals on a dragon sleeper. He wraps his legs around Samauri in a body scissors, pulling back on his neck.**
Eric: Assisted Suicide! It's locked on! **The ref checks Samauri if he can continue. There is no response or reaction to the hold itself. Ref calls for the bell. MEGA loud boos from the fans.** [DING, DING, DING] Eric: Damn............ The Slayers have won it and now, the Oriental Assassins are history.... **The referere goes to get the title belts and hand them to Suicidal. He snatches them out of the ref's hands.** Marty: The winners of this match....... and -NEW- LPW tag team champions.... the Puerto Rican Slayers! **"The Four Horsemen" by Metallica starts to play as Eric is totally speechless over how this match ended. Suicidal rolls Skull out of the ring and assists him in walking up the aisle, still holding the tag titles in his hand. The fans throws small trash and drinks at them, booing them out of the arena. The referee goes to the back to check on Ninja Master.**
**Scene changes of video footage, showing parts of the tournament, and how it ended up being Jake MacKenna and Kobra Khan in the finals. After it ends, scene goes back to the ring. Referee Danial Tappan is holding the brand new
LPW championship belt in his hands. The leather strap is painted to look like dark marble. The metal plates on the front and sides are solid gold with silver lining around the edges.**
FOR THE VACANT LPW CHAMPIONSHIP JAKE MACKENNA -VS- KOBRA KHAN Referee: Daniel Tappan [DING, DING, DING] Marty: The following contest, scheduled for one fall... is the final match of the World title tournament, for the vacant LPW championship...
**The fans cheer over the announcement** Marty: And... it will be under Superfight rules! **The fans react in complete surprise and shock!** Eric: ...WHAT??? When did THAT get added to the match??? That easily tips the advantage to the reigning Superfight champion!
**"Wherever I May Roam" by Metallica begins to play. The sitar intro indicates who is on his way out and the boos continue to flood the arena. Superfight champion Kobra Khan makes his way out to his new entrance music.** Marty: Introducing first... from Cairo, Egypt... weighing 266 lbs... he is the LPW Superfight champion... Kobra Khan!
Eric: Only Darren Diamond could authorize a change like this... but why?? **Khan slides into the ring like the evil snake he is. He removes the Superfight title from his waist and holds it up high for all to see. About
10 seconds pass and the music fades to silence. "Shattered" by Adema starts to play. Fans cheer Jake as he walks out without Hillary, now even more nervous knowing this has been changed to a superfight. He walks down the aisle to the ring.**
Marty: His opponent... from Halifax, Nova Scotia... weighing 276 lbs... Jake
MacKenna! Eric: What could be going through his mind at this very moment? Besides survival... **He enters the ring and is greeted by Khan with hard lefts and rights to the head and chest. Music stops and the belt is given to the timekeeper.**
[DING, DING] Eric: Bell sounds and we're underway! When this is all over, one of them will be the first LPW champion in over 2 years!
**Khan takes Jake to the center of the ring and lifts him up in a suplex, holding him upside down.** Eric: Khan showing a little technical prowress... we don't see that often. **After 10 seconds, he drops him down. Jake is feeling light headed.** Eric: Awesome strength by the Superfight champion... **Khan goes out the ring and gets a chair as Jake stands up.** Eric: Oooooooooh man.... here comes the part he likes best.... using anything as a weapon! **Khan reels back to smack Jake on top of the head. Jake kicks him in the gut, the chair drops, Jake hooks Khan's head, positions over the chair and gives him a DDT.**
Eric: Jake foiled that idea quick! Here's a cover! [1..... 2..... kickout] Eric: Wow, I'm surprised he was able to get to 2 so early in the match. **Jake picks up Khan and gives him a german suplex on the chair.** Eric: Khan is being punished big time here... maybe Khan overestimated him when he took Devastator out of the tournament last night with a vile attack with poison... we'll have more on Devastator's condition on Tuesday Night
Clash. Another cover...
[1..... 2..... kickout] Eric: Another 2 count by Jake. Why would Hillary leave her man at a time like this? He's fighting for the LPW title!
**Jake picks him up again and goes immediately for the Untouchable pedigree.** Eric: He's going for it! Can he beat one of the most dangerous legends of all time? **Khan answers that by backdropping Jake over the top rope, then collapses to his hands and knees to catch his breath.** Eric: Jake landing hard on the floor almost right in front of me! **"Zero Signal" by the Mortal Kombat movie soundtrack, without the slow intro, erupts on the soundsystem and the fans explode in cheers.** Eric: He's here! It's Devastator and he's coming for Kobra Khan! **The demon in black runs out to the ring at full speed and full intensity. He slides in the ring, quickly picks up Khan and lifts him up for a devastating (no pun intended) chokeslam. The cheers are so loud that Eric can't speak and be heard. Devastator grabs a weakened Khan by the throat and sets him up in a powerbomb position. They know what's coming next.
Devastator hoists the Superfight champion up on his right shoulder, quickly grabs him by the neck and drives him to the mat on his head, slightly breaking the ring!**
Eric: (barely heard) ANNIHILATOR! Kobra Khan is down and OUT! **Devastator steps out of the ring, picks up Jake in a press slam and *throws* him over the top rope. Jake lands almost right on top of Khan. Daniel makes a count and the fans count with him.** [1.......................... 2.............................. 3!!! [DING, DING, DING] **Extreme mega pop from the fans as the count of 3 ends. Daniel helps Jake to his feet then goes over to get the title belt and hand it him. Jake holds it up high as "Shattered" plays again.**
Marty: The winner of the match.... and -NEW- LPW champion..... Jake MacKenna! **Devastator taps Jake on his side, getting his attention.** Devastator: You're welcome........ **Devastator leaves the ringside area, heading back as the celebration continues and the show fades to a brief promo for LPW's next pay per view,
Doomsday (July 13), is shown.**
