LPW — Friday Frenzy

Friday Frenzy, 15 March 2002

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FRIDAY FRENZY - MARCH 15, 2002 (Brief LPW logo animation is shown then fades to black. Scene fade in to backstage. The Slayers are standing over the luggage that they stole from The Oriental Assassins in the locker room area last week.
Dead Skull looks over at Songi.) Dead Skull: Ready? (Songi nods and they each open a bag. With the camera showing them from the back and above waist level, they unzip their pants and pee into the Assassins' bags.)
(Heavy metal version of the piano classical "Flight of the Bumblebee" plays while showing brief video animations of previous LPW matches 2-3 years ago. A red/white shaking Friday Frenzy logo smashes through the animations like it was glass. Scene fade to a long pan of inside the arena, the fans on their feet cheering, "LIVE / Georgia Dome -
Atlanta, GA" slides into view in the upper left corner.) (As the camera crew shows various shots of the crowd) Voice of Eric
Davidson: LIVE from Atlanta, GA... we welcome you to another edition of Friday Frenzy! I'm Eric Davidson... but as for my broadcast partner... (camera on Eric) Michael Hunter is not here tonight. He suffered a minor head injury at the hands of Dead Skull and is scheduled to be back on Tuesday night. So for now, I'll be calling the action by myself. (The lights lower to half brightness as the camera view changes to above the ring, where random multicolored strobe lights are shining on an enclosed steel cage.) And there you see the
15 foot steel cage... the very cage where Kobra Khan and Hurricane
Wild Child will have their final confrontation ever as they battle for the vacant Superfight title. And I've been informed before we went on the air that a new stipulation was added... this will now be a CAREER
ENDING match! The winner will become the new Superfight champion and the loser must leave Legend Pro Wrestling... forever! (All the lights return to normal as the music fades to silence.)
(Camera view in the ring, Bull Beast is already in it, looking up at the cage for a moment.)
"Shorty" Marty Jennings: Our opening contest is scheduled for 1 fall. In the ring... from Athens, Greece... weighing 289 lbs... Bull Beast! (No reaction from the fans) Eric: (showing video footage) This match came about during last week's tag team match between Bull Beast and Iron Eagle against the
Shadowlords. Bull Beast was getting his ass handed to him on a silver platter and Iron Eagle simply walked out on him. These two are about to face each other right now...
(Music: Indian warcrys and drums. Iron Eagle makes his way out and walks down to the ring to a decent pop.)
Marty: His opponent... from Kansas City, KS... weighing 277 lbs... Iron Eagle! Eric: Iron Eagle looks very focused on this matchup... but then again, he always looks that way. (Eagle enters the ring and Beast immediately goes to hit a few chops to the chest.) Bull Beast caught him off guard there...
*The chops appear to have no effect. Beast pauses, then gets a few more chops in. Still no effect. Eagle grabs him by the throat with both hands and gives him a hard headbutt, then lets go. Beast stands there for a moment, as if it had no effect, then slowly falls down on his butt, dazed.*
Eric: Have a seat, Bull Beast... (snickers) *Eagle picks him up, whips him in the ropes and knocks him down with a boot to the face.* Eric: Ouch... hope he has a good dental plan. *Eagle stands in the corner and waits for Beast to get back up. It takes a few moments but Beast gets on his feet. He turns around towards Eagle, still dazed, and Eagle charges at him with a running clothesline. Beast does a near 360° in mid air, landing on his head.*
Eric: Tomahawk clothesline by Iron Eagle. That almost turned Bull
Beast inside out.
*Eagle picks him up and positions him for a bearhug. But he pauses then lets him go, Beast falls to the mat. Eagle puts a boot on his chest.* 1.... 2.... 3! Eric: Easy victory for the navive American, Iron Eagle! Michael was right when he called Bull Beast a human punching bag.
Marty: Here is your winner... Iron Eagle! (Eagle leaves the ring and heads back up the aisle.)
Eric: Still to come tonight, (animated matchup screen is shown) the tag team champions, the Shadowlords, put their newly won titles on the line against Terminator and Shi'Noku! But up next, (matchup screen changes) we will see the debut of Johnny Ziro, as he goes against Dead
Skull of the Puerto Rican Slayers!
(Scene change to outside in the parking lot. A diesel rig is parked nearby.) Eric: What's going on here? The camera pans over to show Dead Skull paint spraying the words "Die
Slow" on the side in bold red letters.)
Eric: As if Skull didn't have enough to worry about with the Oriental
Assassins... when Johnny sees this, he will tear him apart. We'll be right back! (fade to black)
---------------------------------------- Commercials ---------------------------------------- (Fade in to backstage where the Slayers are walking to a dressing room carrying the Assassins' bags. They break down the door. Ninja Master and Samauri Master jump up from their chairs and assume attack positions. Skull gestures them to settle down.)
Dead Skull: Hold on! We just wanted to give you your bags back. We got no use for them anymore. (He and Songi toss them close to the
Assassins' feet.) Songi: Go on... take them... (Ninja and Samauri slowly reach down to get them, not taking their eyes off the Slayers. They smell something unusual with the bags and open them. They run to the restroom where you hear gagging sounds. Skull and Songi walk out the room laughing.)
Eric: Do the Slayers have a death wish?? Ugh! (Scene back in the arena. Music: "Hard Workin' Man" by Brooks and
Gunn.) (Johnny Ziro comes out and pauses at the top of the aisle. He raises his right fist into the air for a moment then makes his way to the ring. The fans cheer a little.)
Eric: Wow, look at the size of this guy! He's a giant! Marty: The following contest is scheduled for 1 fall. Coming down the aisle... making his LPW debut... from Nashville, TN... weighing 320 lbs... Johnny Ziro! (He steps over the top rope and raises his fist in the air again, then waits on the other side of the ring for Dead Skull to arrive.)
Eric: Talk about mismatches.... this will be one of those power versus speed matchups. (Ziro's music fades away.)
(Music: "For Whom The Bell Tolls" by Metallica. The camera is on
Johnny during the first few bell gongs of the music.)
Marty: His opponent... from Naguabo, Puerto Rico... weighing 225 lbs... Dead Skull! Eric: Ziro is almost 100 pounds heavier and 1 1/2 feet taller than
Dead Skull. (A few moments pass and Skull has not come out. The music fades away and the crowd is a little silent in confusion.) ....Where is he? (pauses as Ziro wonders why he's not coming out) We know he's here because we just saw him and Songi in the Oriental Assassins'
dressing room moments ago. ("For Whom The Bell Tolls" starts again.) Marty: His opponent... from Naguabo, Puerto Rico... weighing 225 lbs... Dead Skull! (10 seconds pass and still, no sign of Dead Skull.
Music fades again.) Eric: Where could he be? Has Skull finally 'learned his lesson' , so to speak? *Ziro talks with the referee on the reason for the delay. Ref shrugs his shoulders. But then, Songi sticks his head out into view behind the backstage curtain. Ziro sees him and steps out of the ring to find out what's going on. The moment he does, Songi leaves. Ziro turns to head back to the ring. The referee is going up to the top rope.*
Eric: Wait a minute... what is the referee doing???
*The referee comes off the top rope with a missile dropkick on Ziro's knee. Ziro falls to the floor. The fans boo loudly. The ref grabs a huge wrench from outside the ring and repeatedly beats the back of
Ziro's knee as hard as possible leaving him clutching his knee in pain. You can see the agony on his face as the referee pulls his ref clothes off and a hat and glasses that were covering his face.*
Eric: That's not a referee! It's Dead Skull! How did he get out here unnoticed?? Dead Skull (screaming at Ziro): "What do you think now!?!? Huh!?!? Does that hurt!?!? You think you bad cuz you big!?!? Huh!?!? You may be big but you will never be a bad ass like ME!!!"
*Dead Skull beats Ziro a few more times on the knee then throws the wrench under the ring again then goes back into the ring and waits for him as referee Ted Hawkings runs out to the ring. Ziro manages to get in the ring limping.*
Eric: The last thing Dead Skull needs is to make more enemies. He already got 4 of them as it is, the Oriental Assassins and Devastator.
*Skull dropkicks his knee and Ziro goes down again. Skull slaps on a figure 4 leglock.* Eric: Normally, a small guy taking away a big man's vertical base is good strategy but the way Dead Skull did it was just not right!
*Ziro slowly manages to pull himself over to the bottom rope, forcing the hold to be broken. Skull argues a few words with the ref as Ziro pulls himself up. Skull goes back over to him but Ziro reaches down and grabs Skull in a bearhug, lifting him off the canvas. Crowd cheers.*
Eric: Ziro with a bearhug... squeezing the life out of him! *Ziro shakes Skull around like a rag doll, still in the hold. He steps back and, without letting go, drops Skull hard on his back. Ziro holds his knee.* Eric: That one move took a lot out of Dead Skull. you can tell he's in a lot of pain now. *Ziro crawls over and makes a cover. 1.... 2.... kickout.* Eric: 2 count in the early going by Johnny. If he continues to use moves like that, he will have no problem scoring the win.
*Ziro stands, picks up Skull and gives him a backbreaker. Skull hollers out in pain. Again, he holds his knee, still hurting from the attack moments earlier but makes another cover. 1.... 2.... kickout.*
Eric: Another 2 count. Skull can't take much more of this.
*Ziro and Skull get up. Ziro sends Skull in the ropes, Skull ducks under a clothesline attempt and bounces off the other side with a spear. Skull just bounces off him to no effect, hurting his shoulder.
The crowd laughs.* Eric: Yeah right... like he can tackle someone that big with a move like that. *Ziro picks him up, grabs him by the throat and gives him a vicious chokeslam. He drops to his good knee, holding his hurt one. Skull appears to be knocked out. Crowd cheers.*
Eric: Oooo I could feel that one from over here! Cover him, Johnny! *Songi runs out to the ring, with a bat in hand.* Eric: Wait a minute! Songi's out here and he's armed! *Songi quickly rolls in, stands and cracks the bat across Ziro's head, knocking him down. Ref calls for the bell. Ziro manages to roll out of the ring moments later. Songi swings at him and misses, then goes over to check on Skull.*
Marty: The winner of the match... as a result of a disqualification... Johnny Ziro! (cheers) Eric: The Slayers tried to steal another one but this time, they failed! (Cheers as the Assassins run out to the ring) The Oriental
Assassins are coming for the Slayers! (They slide in and the fight is on between the 4 of them. Ziro limps away from the ring, taking a couple glances back at what's happening.)
(Camera still on Ziro) Eric: We will try to regain control and get them out of here! The tag team titles will be up for grabs when we return! (fade to black)
----------------------------------------- Commercials ----------------------------------------- (Fade in to outside in the parking lot. Dead Skull and Songi are running out and getting in their car, Skull holding the side of his head. The Assassins soon follow, Ninja Master with a trash can in hand, and throws it at their car as it speeds off. The camera focuses on the angry eyes of both Assassins for a moment.)
(Scene change to Eric Davidson) Eric: Folks, you got to believe that somewhere down the road at some point in time, the Slayers and the
Assassins have to settle their differences once and for all. The
Slayers thought they ended it almost 3 years ago in a 6 man superfight. But that's what they get for thinking...
(Music: theme from Terminator. Fans cheer as Terminator walks out to the ring.) Marty: The following contest, scheduled for 1 fall, is for the LPW tag team championship! Introducing first... from Phoenix, AZ... weighing
225 lbs... Terminator!
Eric: He and Shi'Noku made the challenge to the Shadowlords, it was accepted and here we are. (enters the ring)
(Music: oriental funeral music. Fans cheer a little more loud. Shi'Noku makes his way out) Marty: His partner... from Beijing, China... weighing 339 lbs... the chinese undertaker, Shi'Noku!
Eric: Shi'Noku is no stranger to tag team gold. He was teamed up with the WWF's Undertaker for a short time and became the champions.
(Music: sound of blowing wind and fire burning. The light change to orange and blue.) Marty: Their opponents... at a combined weight of 600 lbs... the LPW tag team champions... Fire and Ice, the Shadowlords!
*The Shadowlords run in from behind the commentator table already unrobed, carrying the tag belts, and sneak attack them. Fire throws
Shi'Noku out of the ring. Lights return to normal.*
Eric: Whoa, Shadowlords getting a little aggressive tonight! They have been like this since their return last week!
*Ice picks up Terminator, whips him in the ropes and connects with a powerslam. Shi'Noku attempts to get back in the ring but he gets double teamed with punches by both Shadowlords and is knocked back outside again as Terminator gets back up.*
Eric: I'm starting to sense a pattern here. They seem to be concentrating solely on who they feel is the weaker of the two. In this case, Terminator.
*Fire bodyslams Terminator, then he and Ice go up to the top for Arctic Inferno as Terminator slowly stands up. Shi'Noku knocks Ice off the rope and starts to roll in the ring as Fire hits with his part of the double flying clothesline.*
Eric: Shi'Noku saving his partner from the Arctic Inferno, the Shadowlords are really trying to end this one early! *Fire charges at Shi'Noku and hits him with a clothesline so hard, they both go over the top to the floor.* Eric: They are doing everything they can to keep Shi'Noku out of this match... and succeeding.
*Ice pulls himself over to Terminator and makes a cover. 1.... 2... 3!* Eric: Terminator and Shi'Noku didn't have a chance to get in a single move! Incredible. Marty: The winners of the match... and *still* LPW tag team champions... the Shadowlords! *Fire and Ice get their tag belts and make a hasty exit. Shi'Noku looks at them in disgust as the ref helps Terminator to his feet.*
Eric: After the break, one career will end and the other will reign as
the Superfight champion. Kobra Khan vs Hurricane Wild Child... in the cage... NEXT! (fade to black) --------------------------------------------- Commercials --------------------------------------------- (Fade in to the cage above the ring. The music of a low constant hum is heard as the lights are dimmed and the multicolored strobe lights flash against the cage as it is slowly lowered to the ring. The fans are cheering loud.)
Eric: Fasten your seat belts for this one! It's going to be a wild ride! (Lights return to normal. Music: slow Egyptian sitar music. Loud boos from the crowd as Khan makes his way out.)
Marty: The following contest, scheduled for 1 fall, is a career ending steel cage match for the LPW Superfight championship! (pauses)
Introducing first... from Cairo, Egypt... weighing 266 lbs... Kobra
Khan! Eric: This rivalry first started 9 years ago at Doomsday, where Hurricane got a surprising pinfall victory over Khan to eliminate him from the event. Ever since then, Khan feels he was cheated out of the
World title and is strongly determined to get it back... at any cost!
(Khan enters the cage) (Music: "Rock You Like A Hurricane" by the Scorpions. Loud cheers as
Hurricane walks out.) Marty: His opponent... from Panama City, FL... weighing 245 lbs... Hurricane Wild Child! Eric: He hasn't had much luck with Kobra Khan in the past 2 weeks. Maybe he will tonight. His carerer depends on it! (he enters the cage and the punches start flying) And here we go! Hurricane and Khan slugging it out in the middle of the ring!
*Khan drives a hard knee in Hurricane's gut, whips him in the ropes, Hurricane reverses and charges at Khan with a clothesline, knocking him down. Khan gets right back up and is clotheslined down again.
Hurricane chokes Khan down on the canvas.* Eric: Hurricane is going crazy on him! I've never seen him this intense before but I don't blame him for being like this.
*Hurricane lets go, picks up Khan and throws him head first on the commentator side of the cage. Hurricane quickly takes him and throws him in the other side of the cage.*
Eric: He's teaching Khan a whole new meaning to the word "pain"! *Hurricane covers him. 1... kickout.* Eric: Khan's not giving in that easily. *Hurricane picks him up, Khan thumbs him in the eyes. Khan sneaks around behind and slaps on his Venom finisher.* Eric: NO! Hurricane needs to get out of that right now or it's all over! *Hurricane grabs Khan's wrists and with all his strength, pushes back with authority. The back of Khan's head hits the cage, causing him to break the hold.* Eric: He broke it... but I think the longer this goes, the advantage goes to Kobra Khan. He has more endurance than Hurricane.
*As Khan staggers away from the cage wall, Hurricane makes some momentum by running in the opposite ropes and charges at Khan. Khan grabs him, lifts him up in a press slam and in one swift move, throws him in the cage wall head first. The force of the impact is so strong that the cage wall breaks off the hinges and Hurricane goes crashing to the outside. The broken wall almost lands on some ringside fans.*
Eric: Oh my... god! (Khan drops to his knees to catch his breath.) The cage is being destroyed!
*Camera on Hurricane, laying among twisted metal and bleeding from the head.* Eric: Oh no... we need someone out here... I mean it, send somebody out here NOW! *Devastator and the other referees, except Lynni, run out. While Devastator moves the section of the cage wall out of harm's way, the refs tend to Hurricane. Khan stands up and staggers in a daze to a corner. He himself is bleeding from the back of the head.*
Eric: Hurricane has not moved a single muscle since being thrown... literally... through the cage. (videotron starts showing replays of it from various angles) Hurricane had no time to block the impact but it was his body weight that most likely causes the cage to collapse.
*Senior referee Daniel Tappen, assigned to this match, makes a judgement call and signals for the bell.* Marty: (long pause) Referee Daniel Tappan has stopped this match..... the winner... and *NEW* LPW Superfight champion... Kobra Khan! (The crowd is more interested in Hurricane's condition than booing Khan out of the arena. Khan gets his belt and leaves. He pauses long enough to take one last look at his fallen opponent.)
Eric: Hurricane Wild Child must now leave Legend Pro Wrestling, never to return. We wish him the best in the future. Ladies and gentlemen, we are almost out of time... thank you for joining us and we will see you in Charlotte this Tuesday... (removes his headset and goes over to offer any assistance as the show fades to black)
