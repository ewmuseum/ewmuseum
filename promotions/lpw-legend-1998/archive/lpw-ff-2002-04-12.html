LPW — Friday Frenzy

Friday Frenzy, 12 April 2002

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 1 of 3, Apr 12, 2002, 11:57:37 PM] FRIDAY FRENZY - APRIL 12, 2002 **Small LPW initials logo amination fades into view in front of blurry dark blue moving clouds, moving from the horizon to the top of the screen. Each letter flashes for a moment, from left to right, then all 3 at once. Scene fades to black.**
**Friday Frenzy intro plays to a heavy metal version of the piano classical "Flight of the Bumblebee", showing brief moments of previous matches. The rapid shaking red and whit ewords "Friday Frenzy" shatters through the intro from behind like it was glass. Scene fades to a wide shot of the crowd, cheering as multicolor pyros explode at the videotron and the ring. After the pyros, the cameras pan different sections of the fans.**
Voice of Eric Davidson: We are LIVE from the Reunion Center in Dallas, Texas and we are now less than 24 hours away from LPW's first PPV event in over 2 years, 'Clash of the Champions'! Tonight, we will find out who Kobra Khan's opponent will be tomorrow night for the vacant LPW championship! Will it be rookie sensation Jake MacKenna or the legend "demon in black", Devastator?
**Frenzy's intro music fades to silence. Moments later, the scene changes to backstage where Jake MacKenna is stretching, preparing to meet Devastator. He looks nervous, knowing Devastator has almost god-like strength and is not afraid to show it. Hillary is sitting close by, watching with concern. The fans cheer when they appear on the videotron. Neither Jake or Hillary says a single word to each other.**
Eric: And there's Jake now, preparing for perhaps the most difficult challenge any LPW superstar could have... one on one with Devastator!
**Scene changes to the dressing room of the Puerto Rican Slayers. Songi is sitting in front of a tv monitor, watching footage from this past Tuesday. The fans boo him. Dead Skull comes walking up behind him and taps him on the shoulder. He has a serious look on his face.**
Dead Skull: It's time.... **Skull walks away as Songi gets up to follow him. As he leaves out of camera viewing range, he starts to form a smile.** kYd-X VS Matt Ziro Referee: Lynni Tappan **"Renegade" by Tim McGraw starts to play. The Ziro brothers, Johnny and Matt, make their way out. Matt starts working up the crowd and Johnny is seen holding a sledge hammer. They head to the ring, giving high-fives to the fans on the way. Once they're at the ring, Matt climbs on the apron, then jump into the ring over the top rope. Johnny stays on the outside. Matt then goes around the ring, working up the crowd.**
[DING, DING, DING] "Shorty" Marty Jennings: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. In the ring... making his LPW debut... being accompanied by his brother, Johnny
Ziro... from Nashville, TN... weighing 220 lbs... Matt Ziro!
Eric: Johnny got his game face on tonight, sledgehammer in hand... wonder what's on his mind...
**Music fades away and is replaced with "Break Stuff" by Kid Rock. kYd-X comes out on the entrance way with his arms going straight up in an X. kYd-X runs down to ring and quickly slides in. He goes to every turnbuckle to do the X symbol with his arms again. The fans cheer.**
Marty: His opponent... also making his LPW debut... from Charlotte, NC... weighing 221 lbs... kYd-X! Eric: The roster count has slowly been growing over the past couple weeks and even more applicants are being interviewed.
**kYd-X climbs down from the last turnbuckle as Matt goes for a spinning heel kick and the music fades to silence.** [DING, DING] Eric: Matt not wasting any time getting this one started... **kYd-X ducks under the kick attempt but Matt quickly hits with a roundhouse kick, knocking kYd-X down. Matt goes up to the top rope and flies off with a moonsault.** Eric: Matt with a moonsault into an early cover.... **1..... easy kickout.** Eric: Only a one count, says referee Lynni Tappan. **Matt attempts a boston crab. kYd-X twists his body to swing Matt off balance and to the mat. Matt quickly gets back up and climbs to the top rope. kYd-X follows right behind him, leaps up to the top and executes a headscissors, sending Matt crashing to the floor outside the ring.**
Eric: kYd-X showing incredible agility, Matt laying on the outside. Johnny: ...Whoa! **Lynni starts her 10 count. Matt is half standing at 5 and back in the ring at 8. kYd-X picks him up and takes him to the middle of the ring. He slams
Matt then runs to the ropes. kYd-X attempts a lionsault but Matt raises his knees, kYd-X's gut landing on them. He's rolling around on the mat in pain.**
Eric: Matt sensed that maneuver and blocked it well. **Matt gets up and goes up to the top yet again. He waits for kYd-X to stand then comes off with a missile dropkick. kYd-X pushes Matt's legs aside at the last moment and Matt lands hard on his side.**
Eric: kYd-X swiping him away, now in position to run in the ropes for something. **kYd-X gets in a position to run in the ropes, watching for Matt to get up. He makes a X symbol with his arms overhead to signal for his finisher, the
XDT. When Matt does stand, kYd-X runs off and bounces back, leaping in the air and at the same time, spins around to catch Matt in a ddt and drive his head in the mat.**
Eric: XDT by kYd-X! Will that be enough? **kYd-X makes a cover, hooking the leg..... 1..... 2..... kickout.** Eric: Nope, only a two count. I thought it was over right there. **kYd-X starts to pick him up for another XDT, Matt grabs him into a small package..... 1..... 2..... kickout.** Eric: Matt with a near 3 count on kYd-X... **They both get up to their feet. Matt kicks him in the gut and gets him in position for a piledriver. He hits it to perfection. Matt then gets up, goes to the top rope and leaps off, connecting with a shooting star press into a cover..... 1..... 2........ 3!**
[DING, DING, DING] Eric: Matt scores the victory over kYd-X! **Fans cheer as Johnny enters the ring, helps Matt up and congratulates him. "Renegade" plays again.** Marty: Here is your winner..... Matt Ziro! **kYd-X rolls out of the ring and walks back up the aisle as Matt goes around the ring working the crowd again. The fans cheer kYd-X for a good effort.** Eric: Great lightweight match to kick off Friday Frenzy... **Scene changes to backstage as the tag team champions, the Oriental Assassins, walk down a hall on their way to go into battle. The title belts are around their waists. Fans cheer for them.**
Eric: Ninja Master and Samauri Master... the Oriental Assassins.... won the tag team titles this past Tuesday and will defend them tonight against
Shi`Noku & Zula Bon Bimbay.... next! (fade to black)
======COMMERCIALS====== **Fade in to backstage where we see Matt and Johnny Ziro talking in the distance. The fans cheer as they appear on the videotron. Johnny walks away to a soda machine and the camera zooms in on a tired Matt.**
Male voice: Hey Matt... **Thinking it's Johnny, Matt turns around and Dead SKull appears out of nowhere, quickly spearing him to the ground. Fans boo as the attack begins. While he's on the floor, Dead Skull punches his face until Matt is busted open. Then he kicks him in the head repeatedly knocking him out. From behind, we hear a rustling coming closer then see Johnny spin Dead Skull around to face him but as he does, Dead Skull turns with a strong roudhouse kick to Johnny's head, knocking him down to the floor. As Johnny stands up enraged, Dead Skull puches him in the head, knocking him out and busting him open also. The camera zooms in on Dead Skull's hand and we see a pair of brass knuckles on him. Skull then turns to look down on Matt.**
Dead Skull: Dink just kicked your ass.... what does that make you, you f*n wuss? **Dead Skull leaves them laying, barely able to even get up to a sitting position. Scene changes back to the commentator table.** Eric: ...I'm not surprised by what Skull just did. Typical Slayers mentality... wait until someone has their back turned or weakened after a match, then brutally attack them. The Assassins have their work cut out for them tomorrow night as they face the Slayers for the very last time with the tag titles on the line.
**Scene changes to a wide shot of the crowd.** WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH (C) 'Oriental Assassins' VS Shi`Noku & Zula Bon Bimbay Referee: Stephen Woods **Theme from the tv series Shaka Zulu starts to play and Zula Bon Bimbay makes his way out to cheers. He's not wearing his war mask this time.**
[DING, DING, DING] Marty: The following contest, scheduled for one fall, is for the LPW tag team championship. Introducing first, the challengers... from Uganda,
Africa... weighing 321 lbs... Zula Bon Bimbay! Eric: Shi`Noku and Zula Bon Bimbay were originally to meet the Shadowlords for the titles at 'Clash of the Champions' but that all changed on Tuesday and the new champions offered to give them their match tonight.
**Music fades away and slow oriental funeral music starts to play to louder cheers. The scene on tv is extended vertically as if it's a classic kung fu movie. The lights change to a pale gold.**
Marty: His partner... from Beijing, China... weighing 339 lbs... the chinese undertaker, Shi`Noku!
Eric: Shi`Noku, a popular legend as of late, about to attempt to become a tag team champion...
**The fans start quieting down when they notice Shi`Noku not coming out.** Eric: ...Where is he? It's not like him to be late. **Another 10 seconds pass and still nothing. The music fades away, lights and tv image returned to normal. Fans are all wondering where he could be. Zula is speaking with the referee.** Eric: I got a bad feeling about this... **The videotron flashes on and shows the chinese undertaker laying face down on the floor. There is blood around his head.** Eric: What the hell?? **Camera pans back and up to reveal Dead Skull standing there with a bloody barbwire bat. The look on his face is cold and heartless. Fans boo loudly.**
Eric: Dammit, not him again! Can't we have a tag team title match without
Dead Skull ruining it??
**As Zula runs out of the ring and to the back, Dead Skull looks to the camera.** Dead Skull: No.... not here.... not tonight.... the next challengers for the titles.... is me.... and Songi..... no one else! You can have your fu..
freakin title match AFTER we win the belts tomorrow night! **The Oriental Assassins arrive just as Skull leaves. Ninja Master and Samauri Master check for possible other injuries.** Eric: I told you I had a bad feeling about it... so much for that matchup. Ladies and gentlemen, we're gonna take a commercial break and have another match between two newcomers. Stay with us... (fade to black)
======COMMERCIALS====== **Fade in to a shot of the fans as newcomer The Assassin comes down the aisle to no music. He is carrying a club in his hand and completely ignoring the fans, who are now booing him.**
THE ASSASSIN VS JIM "HARDASS" MASTERS Referee: Ted Hawking [DING, DING, DING] Marty: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle... making his LPW debut... weighing 230 lbs... The Assassin!
Eric: Folks, just so there is no confusion, he's not related with the Oriental Assassins. **Assassin climbs in the ring and waits for his opponent, swinging the club in the air a few times.** Eric: And last I checked, this was not a Superfight. **"It's Alright To Be A Redneck" by Alan Jackson begins to play as Jim Masters makes his way out. As he shakes a few fans' hands, he starts getting decent cheers.**
Marty: His opponent... also making his LPW debut... from Mobile, AL... weighing 320 lbs... Jim "The Hardass" Masters! **Halfway down the aisle, Jim runs to the ring and slides in. Assassin drops the club and dropkicks Jim down as he stands up. Music fades away quickly and the ref kicks the club out of the ring.**
[DING, DING] Eric: Assassin using the advantage of being in the ring first... **Assassin gets up and drives his knee in Jim's chest, stands again and drops an elbow.** Eric: He's really taking it to Masters tonight. It's been all Assassin so far. **Assassin picks him up, lifts him up for a piledriver and connects with it. Assassin makes an immediate cover..... 1..... kickout.** Eric: At this rate, the Assassin will have no problem scoring the victory. **Assassin picks him up and whips him in the ropes. Jim bounces off with a hard spear takedown.** Eric: Well, Masters finally gets some offense in. Let's see if he can keep it up. **Jim kneels over Assassin's chest and pummels him with lefts and rights. The ref has to order him to stop.** Eric: Masters now with the advantage, putting a hurtin' on the Assassin... **As Jim kicks away at Assassin's ribs, the injured and bandaged Ziro brothers make their way out. The fans cheer their arrival.** Eric: What are they doing back out? I thought Dead Skull put them out of commission earlier!
**They get on the apron as Jim is about to whip Assassin in the ropes. Johnny enters first as the whip is made. Johnny hits Assassin with a big boot to the face, knocking him down. Ref calls for the bell.**
[DING, DING, DING] Eric: Hey, what is he doing?? **While Assassin is down, Matt climbs to the top turnbuckle and hits Assassin with the shooting star press. He's holding his own ribs after the move and has to be helped up to his feet.**
Eric: What did Assassin do to deserve this brutal assault?? **Johnny pulls Assassin to his feet and chokeslam him. Then he pulls him back to his feet and irish whip him toward Jim. Jim hits him with another
Redneck Ram.** Eric: Assassin is being assassinated! But why??? **Johnny pulls Assassin back to his feet and execute a powerbomb. Afterward, Jim pulls a bloody Assassin up and hold him in a full nelson lock. Johnny gets a mic.** Eric: Maybe now we will get some explanation... Johnny: Listen up everyone and listen damn good! (pauses) This especially goes for you guys back there in the locker room. (points at Assassin) Do you all see what just happened to this poor fool?? He just got his ass kicked!
Thought he was so tough.... beating up guys in alley ways and dark rooms.... but just look at him now. (pauses) We don't have anything against him personally. He just happened to be the poor sap in our way. Now I'm sure you all noticed I've been using the word "we." Who are we? We are the guys you don't wanna mess with...... we are the guys that if you screw us, you'll get the same treatment as the Assassin here...... we are...... THE RAGIN'
REDNECKS!! Eric: What??? Oh my.... God! Johnny: Slayers..... if you win the tag team titles tomorrow... your sorry asses and those belts will belong... to... US!
**Johnny drops the microphone. Then he kicks Assassin in the stomach as Jim lets him go and Johnny powerbombs Assassin again. Matt and Jim stand on either side of Assassin with Johnny at his head. Johnny puts a boot on his chest and raises his fist in the air. Moments later, all 3 leave the ring to heavy cheers as "It's Good To Be A Redneck" plays again.**
Eric: We just witnessed the forming of a new stable, made their presence felt at Assassin's expense and issued the Slayers a sincere warning! What's gonna happen tomorrow night at 'Clash of the Champions'??? (fade to black)
======COMMERCIALS====== **Fade in to backstage in the dressing room area. The cameras show a closeup of the Slayers, smiling and looking down at something. The fans boo at them.** Eric: Now what are those two doing? **Camera pans over to what they are looking at. It's all three Oriental Assassins, unconscious and bleeding from the heads. They are almost piled on top of each other.**
Eric: What the... it's the Assassins! Skull and Songi have struck the champions again! **Songi starts to turn to go to the door as Skull continues to smirk at their opponents tomorrow night. Songi stops and his expression changes to frozen shock. He taps Skull on the shoulder, without taking his eyes off what's in front of him....**
Songi: (almost whispering) ........skull?........ **No response from Skull, as he's enjoying this.** Songi: .....skull ! ........ Dead Skull: (turning to Songi) Can't you see I'm savoring the moment.... (sees what has Songi spooked, now he has the same look) **The camera turns around and shows the former tag champions, the Shadowlords, standing there. For the first time in LPW history, they are being cheered.** Eric: The Shadowlords! It's about time someone stood up against the Slayers! Skull and Songi: (at the same time) Holy sh.... **Fire and Ice give them hard right hands to the face, knocking them down. The cheers grow even louder. Ice grabs a mirror from a nearby wall and smashes it on Songi's head as Fire picks Skull up. Glass flies everywhere.
Fire throws Skull head first in the concrete wall on the opposite side of the room.** Eric: Fire and Ice are manhandling them! **They stop, pause to survey the damage and start to leave. Fire stops and looks back as Ice leaves the room.** Fire: (in a deep tone) ....Die... slow..... (he leaves the room and the camera turns back to the fallen Slayers)
**The fans can't believe it! A Shadowlord speaks for the first time EVER! Scene changes to Eric at the commentating table.** Eric: I... I can't believe what just happened! After all the times that the
Slayers ambush others, they now know what it's like! From the most unlikely duo to get the job done... Fire and Ice, the Shadowlords! If Dead Skull and
Songi win the tag titles tomorrow, their reign promises to be a very short one! (pauses) Folks, I was informed during the break that the National title will be decided at 'Clash of the Champions'. LPW owner Darren Diamond has selected the top 2 players to be in that match. It will be.... Johnny Ziro versus Wesley Potter. One of them will become the first National champion tomorrow night.
**Scene changes to the top of the aisle. "Shattered" by Adema starts to play and the fans cheer the arrival of Jake MacKeena & Hillary. They walk down to the ring a little slower than usual, still nervous.**
SEMIFINALS OF THE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNAMENT Jake MacKenna VS Devastator Referee: Daniel Tappan [DING, DING, DING] Marty: The following contest is the semifinals of the World championship tournament, with the winner to face Kobra Khan tomorrow night, at 'Clash of the Champions', for the vacant LPW championship. Introducing first... coming down the aisle... being accompanied by Hillary... from Halifax, Nova
Scotia... weighing 276 lbs... Jake MacKeena! Eric: The Reunion Center is going to erupt in just a few moments! (Jake enters the ring, Hillary stays at ringside) No disrespect towards Jake's abilities but he's not the top favorite to win this tournament...
**Music fades to silence and "Zero Signal" by the Mortal Kombat movie soundtrack erupts on the soundsystem, without the slow pulsing beginning. Defeaning cheers from the fans fill the arena.** Eric: ...this man is! **Devastator makes his way out to white and blue strobe lights.** Marty: His opponent... weighing 380 lbs... Devastator! Eric: Here comes the "demon in black"! He's waited an entire week for this matchup! The fans still believe he will easily go all the...........
**Eric is interrupted by a figure running down the aisle towards Devastator.** Eric: Wait a minute..... who is that? **The person attacks him from behind to the head with what appears to be a chair.** Eric: Someone's ambushing Devastator! Who?? **The music stops and the lights return to normal. The fans boo very loudly** Eric: It's Kobra Khan!! He doesn't want to face Devastator in the main event on Saturday! **As Devastator starts to stand and shake off the blows, Khan pulls out a medical needle filled with a green colored liquid.** Eric: What the heck is THAT? **Khan hops on Devastator's back long enough to inject the liquid in his neck.** Eric: No! Someone get out here and stop him! **But it's too late. The needle is already removed and Khan watches the big man drop to the floor in near overwhelming pain. Jake and Hillary look on in horror, not daring to confront Khan at this moment.**
Khan: (to the camera) Let'ssss find out how tough he issss againsssst.... POISSSSON! Eric: Poison??? Security, get this lunatic out of here... NOW! **Khan picks up a very weakened Devastator and throws him in the ring. He leaves without looking back. Jake pauses, not knowing what to do. But the thought of being LPW champion made him do what had to be done. He asks the ref to start the match.**
[DING, DING] Eric: He wouldn't...... this is not fair at all ! **Jake struggles to pick him up to his knees but does so and sets him up for the Untouchable.** Eric: No, not like this! **Jake finishes the move and rolls him over into a cover...... 1...... 2....... 3!** [DING, DING, DING] **The fans are having a mixed reaction of unbelievable shock and anger.** Marty: (pause) Here is your.... winner..... Jake MacKenna..... **Eric removes his headset and tosses it aside in disgust. Devastator is now unconscious. The show ends with the ref, Jake and Hillary tending to the big man as a medic crew rushes to the ring.**
