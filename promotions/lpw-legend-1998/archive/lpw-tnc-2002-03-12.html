LPW — Tuesday Night Clash

Tuesday Night Clash, 12 March 2002

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TUESDAY NIGHT CLASH - MARCH 12, 2002 (Brief LPW logo animation is displayed then fades to black. Scene fades in to backstage where 2 more legends will be returning to action tonight. The crowd begins to cheer as the first shown is Zula Bon
Bimbay, LPW's first African competitor, applying a few lines of black paint above and below the eyes. "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony walks nearby and sees him.)
BT: ...Zula? Is that you? Zula: (stops and looks over to see who it is, then turns to him with a smile) Greetings, old friend. It has been a long time. (BT places his right hand on his heart and bows his head for a moment.) Ah, you still remember the custom of my people. I am honored. (He returns the gesture.)
BT: LPW is honored to have you back with us. I just wanted to stop by and see you for a moment before the show starts.
Zula: Thank you. (goes back to finishing the face paint) Voice: (to BT, from behind) Ready for some 'rock and roll'? (BT turns around to see Rock And Roll Rebel (Mike Patton of the band
Faith No More), greatest International champion in LPW history. He has a large duffel bag, containing his "Falling To Pieces" video attire and hat, over his shoulder. Crowd cheers loudly.)
BT: Rebel ! I didn't know you were back as well! (shakes hands with him) Rebel: Darren called me a few hours ago and asked me if I wanted to return to action for a while until you guys get more people to sign up.
BT: We never had this problem before so I don't know what we are doing wrong. Rebel: I'm sure you will have the roster filled soon... well, I better go get ready. Nice seeing you again.
BT: Same here. (Rebel proceeds to his dressing room. BT continues down the hall with the cameraman behind him. Moments later, the camera sees
Kobra Khan walking into view and standing in BT's way. Crowd boos as he does this. BT tries to go around but Khan again cuts him off.)
BT: There IS room in this hallway for both of us, in case you haven't noticed! (Khan continues to stare, the sound of a faint hissing can be heard). What do you want??
Khan: (always speaks in a loud whisper) ...Your title... BT: (confused) Title? What title? Khan: The title you carry asss being one of the greatessst championssss of all time. You have not earned that honor yet!
BT: (more confused) What do you mean I "haven't earned it"? Last I checked, I held 8 World titles and over 20 various others. I can count the total number of titles you've held on one hand. So I'd say the honor is definitely mine!
Khan: (pauses) How can you be one of the greatesssst... when you never defeated ME?? (BT's smirk turns into a concerned look) It'ssss true. I am 2-0 againsssst you... (holds up his hands in Venom formation) both timessss by ssssubmission! (Crowd has quieted down with concern as well upon hearing that statement.)
BT: You're forgetting one thing. You injured me before those two matches took place. Because deep down, we both know that's the only way you will ever be able to beat me. At 100% health, I'll take your boot and shove it so far up your ass, you have to open your mouth to tie the laces! (Loud cheers from the crowd, mixed with some
"Ooooooooooooooooo!"s) And to prove I'm truly a better champion than you will ever be, I will will make *you* submit.... TONIGHT! (Goes around Khan and walks out of camera view. Khan turns in somewhat surprise.)
(TNC intro plays to the tune of "We Have Explosive" by Future Sound Of
London. The words of the TNC logo slide into view moments later, then scene fades to inside the Mobile Civic Center, where a sold out crowd is on their feet cheering. Pyros go off under the videotron and above the ring.)
Eric: LIVE from the Mobile Civic Center in Mobile, Alabama... we welcome you to Tuesday Night Clash! I'm Eric Davidson, along with
Michael Hunter, and we already have the making of an incredible night!
Michael: The returning Zula Bon Bimbay and Rock And Roll Rebel (scene change to a matchup screen) will challenge the Shadowlords in tag team action!
Eric: (another matchup screen is shown) After being left to be destroyed by the Shadowlords last Friday, Bull Beast will square off against Iron Eagle.
Michael: (another matchup screen is shown) The Puerto Rican Slayers and 2 of the Oriental Assassins are all in the arena tonight! Dead
Skull against Ninja Master and Songi against Samauri Master. We don't know the medical condition of Dragon Ninja at the moment but as soon as we find out, we will let you know.
Eric: (another matchup screen is shown) And as we just heard moments ago, "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony is out to prove he is deserving to be named one of LPW's greatest champions as he goes into battle with the very dangerous Kobra Khan! (Music fades away...)
(Moments later, japanese music starts to play and Samauri Master makes his way out.) "Shorty" Marty Jennings: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle... from Tokyo, Japan... weighing 248 lbs... Samauri Master! (Mixed reaction from the fans)
Eric: Last Friday, the Slayers went one up on the Assassins in the form of Songi making his return and helping Dead Skull brutalize them.
Michael: I have a strong feeling the Assassins will find a way to get even with them very soon. It's only a question of how and when.
(The bass and drum beat of "Steam Will Rise" by Silverchair start to play. The crowd start booing. Songi steps from behind the curtain as the lights go out, the opening solo hits, and a blue laser flashes wildly thoughout the arena. He moves calmly but quickly, carrying a bag owned by Samuri Master.)
Marty: His opponent... from the deserts of southern California... weighing 224 lbs... Songi ! Eric: He gave Dragon Ninja a severe beating to the legs and gut with a barbwire bat, which is why he is not here tonight.
Michael: The war between the Slayers and the Assassins is far from over... *Songi tosses the bag over the top rope and in an instant slides right under the bottom rope and is on his feet. He starts taunting Samuri
Master, calling him an oriental freak. Samuri Master jumps foward just as Songi grabs the bag and slams him in the face with it. The sound of metal clanking metal and bone are heard as Songi drops the duffel bags and pummels Samuri Master on the mat.*
Eric: What in the world could be in that bag?? Michael: Most likely, the Slayers' preferred weapon of choice... bats. *Songi stands up and threatens to hit the referee, then stands on the inside second rope, hollering at the fans to shut up. The crowd boos as Samauri slowly gets back up. Songi hops back down and approaches him. Samauri attempts a right punch, Songi dodges it, but Samauri quickly turns it into a hard backhand fist to the jaw, knocking Songi down.*
Michael: He must have taken Cheap Shot 101 at Terminator University. Eric: It may be cheap to you but against one of the Slayers, I hope he gets more of them in!
*Samuari picks Songi up and whips him in the ropes. Songi reverses and Samuari grabs the top rope and using his martial arts training, he goes up over the top rope and stays there in a headstand.*
Eric: Wow, amazing balance! *Songi runs to him and Samuari comes back down with a vicious mule kick to the chest. The force of it almost sends Songi flying almost the entire length of the ring. Songi rolls under the bottom rope to the floor. The fans start to CHEER for an Assassin. Samauri looks around in amazement.*
Michael: ...Am I hearing things or are these people actually cheering for Samauri Master?? Eric: Sounds that way to me. This is something the Assassins have never experienced! *Samuari steps out of the ring and makes his way over to Songi. He picks the Slayer up and gets thumbed in the eyes. Songi grabs Samuari and throws him into the ring steps. Samuari is laying on the floor.
Crowd boos. Songi removes a section of the floor padding, exposing the concrete under it, then rolls back in.*
Eric: What do you suppose he's gonna do here? Michael: Whatever it is, it will hurt... and hurt big time! *Songi goes up to the top rope, waits for Samauri to stand then leaps off to deliver a hurricarana with Samauri's head landing on the concrete.* Michael: Now there's something the Assassins are used to... getting their heads pounded with hard objects.
Eric: Get it in the ring, will ya? This is not a superfight! *Songi picks up Samuari and throws him back inside. Before he rolls in too, he gives a cruel look towards Michael Hunter.* Michael: Wh.. what's he looking at ME for?? I'm not in this match! Eric: Because you're easy to hate, I guess. Even for a Slayer. *As Samuari stands up a little groggy, Songi grabs him and gives him a bodyslam. He goes to Samauri's legs and hooks him in a boston crab.*
Eric: Samauri Master needs to find a way out of this hold or he will have to give it up.
Michael: Against a Slayer, giving up might be the only way to walk out in one piece. *Samuari manages to pull himself close enough to the ropes and grab the bottom one. Ref starts his 5 count and Songi breaks the hold at 4. Crowd cheers slightly. Songi picks him up, brings him to the middle of the ring and puts Samauri in a gutwrench powerbomb, almost dropping
Samuari on his head instead of his back.*
Eric: Samauri is in trouble and looks to be almost out of it. *Songi goes to the nearest corner and starts to climb up. He gets to the middle rope, pauses and looks back at Michael Hunter again with another cruel look.* Michael: Eric, tell him to stop looking at me like that! Eric: Okay... (with sarcasm) Songi, would you please stop staring at
Michael before he craps in his pants?
Michael: Oh veeeeeeeery funny. *Songi goes up to the top rope, facing the audience who continues to boo him.* Eric: Possible moonsault coming up... *Songi starts to leap off for a moonsault but the turnbuckle he's standing on breaks off the ringpost. He falls straight down, his heels catch on to the middle rope and the back of his head lands very hard on the canvas. His feet are still on the ropes and the fans both laugh and cheer about it.*
Michael: What the heck?? (to Eric) Did you loosen that before the show started? I bet you did!
Eric: You're wrong... as always. Songi may have knocked himself out! Samauri rolling over and putting an arm on him... *1.... 2.... 3!* Michael: No fair! The match should have been stopped until the ring was fixed! Marty: Here is your winner... Samauri Master! (As the ref helps Samauri out of the ring) Eric: A little bit of revenge for the Assassins! Coming up after the break, it's tag team action as the Shadowlords take on Zula Bon Bimbay and Rock And Roll
Rebel. We'll be right back! (fade out)
---------------------------------------- ***Commercials*** ---------------------------------------- (Fade in to the ring crew making final adjustments in the repairs of the turnbuckle.) Eric: During the break, Songi had to be carried out on a stretcher because he... literally... knocked himself out.
Michael: If that hadn't happened, he would have beaten Samauri Master. What happened to him was totally not fair. Eric: Whatever... (ring crew leaving) Marty: The following contest... scheduled for one fall... is for the vacant LPW World tag team championship!
Michael: ....What?!? Eric: A title match?? When was THAT added??? (The sound of blowing wind and burning fire is heard as the lights fade to shades of orange and blue. The Shadowlords slowly walk to the ring.)
Marty: Introducing first... at a combined weight of 600 lbs... Fire and Ice... the Shadowlords!
Eric: I'm now being told on my headset that Darren Diamond made this match for the vacant tag titles earlier tonight.
Michael: That's a smart thing to do since we need to determine our new champions as soon as possible. (Shadowlords enter the ring as their music fades away and lights return to normal.)
(Theme from "Shaka Zulu" plays and Zula Bon Bimbay makes his way out to average cheers, wearing his trademark long face war mask.)
Marty: Their opponents... first, from Uganda, Africa... weighing 321 lbs... Zula Bon Bimbay! (Shadowlords remove their robes)
Eric: Zula and Rebel have a grand opportunity to become the new tag team champions. But they have to get past Fire and Ice to do just that.
("What Is It" by Faith No More plays. Rock And Roll Rebel comes out to loud cheers and walks down to the ring.)
Marty: His partner... from Eureka, California... weighing 228 lbs... Rock And Roll Rebel! Michael: I wonder if his right hand is still as powerful as it was in the past? Eric: We're about to find that out. Rebel was the greatest International champion ever and half of the, now defunct, International tag champions with Screamin' Delta Demon (Axl Rose of the band Guns N Roses) but he would love to be a World tag team champion. (music fades after he enters the ring and remove his hat)
*Zula and Ice start off. They lock up. Zula powers Ice into a corner and the ref asks for a break. Zula breaks and Ice punches him in the nose. Ice sends him in the ropes, attempts a shoulderblock but Zula knocks him down instead.*
Michael: Not many people can say they knocked down a Shadowlord with a move like that! *Zula comes down for an elbow drop, Ice moves aside. Zula gets up holding his elbow. Ice lifts him overhead and holds him up there for almost 10 seconds before depositing him over the top rope to the floor.*
Eric: The strength of the Shadowlords is truly remarkable! Michael: I'd like to see them match power for power with Devastator sometime. Eric: Now THAT would be awesome. *Fire goes to Zula and throws him back in the ring. Ice picks him up and goes for a slam. Zula slips behind and gives him a slam of his own. Tag to Rebel. Rebel catches Ice sitting up with a kick to the back. Rebel lowers Ice back down, comes off the ropes and hits with a legdrop. Makes a cover.... 1.... kickout.*
Michael: It takes a lot more than a legdrop to beat a team like the
Shadowlords. Eric: You're right on that one. *Rebel waits behind Ice for him to stand. He does and Rebel applies a sleeper. Ice almost immediately drops down, giving Rebel a jawbreaker. He reaches over and tags in Fire.* Michael: Shadowlords in control at the moment... *Fire stomps away at Rebel's head. He then picks him up, grabs him by the throat and hoists him up for a possible chokeslam. Rebel puts his arm around Fire's head and as he is dropped, Fire goes down with him and is on the receiving end of a DDT.*
Eric: Good counter by Rock And Roll Rebel! Michael: I don't recall Rebel facing anyone almost a hundred pounds heavier than him before.
*Rebel rolls over and gets a tag to Zula. Zula pulls Fire away from his corner and lands a few kneedrops. A cover... 1.... 2... kickout.* Eric: That was a close one! *Zula pulls Fire up and is jabbed in the throat. Zula staggers to the ropes to clear his throat. Fire gives a signal to Ice. Ice starts to enter the ring, distracting the referee. Rebel uses the opportunity to come in and help his partner. He punches away at the red Shadowlord with a series of rights. Fire kicks him in the gut, Rebel slowly stands back upright, Fire points his hand at Rebel's face and a massive fireball engulfs his eyes. Rebel stumbles around, holding his eyes in pain.*
Michael: Is it hot in here or is it just Rebel? (laughs) *Fire steps back as Zula goes to check on Rebel's condition. Zula puts his hand on his shoulder, Rebel thinks it is Fire and decks Zula with a Epic punch.* Eric: Oh NO! Rebel can't see what he's doing and just knocked out his own partner! *Fire throws Rebel out of the ring and tags in Ice. They go up to opposite top turnbuckles, wait for Rebel to get back up and fly off with the Arctic Inferno. Rebel goes down quick as Ice makes a cover...
1.... 2.... 3!* Michael: The Shadowlords rule the tag team division once more! Marty: The winners of the match... and *NEW* World tag team champions... the Shadowlords! Eric: That damn fireball was the beginning of the end for Zula and
Rebel! Michael: And it's now the beginning of the Shadowlords' 2nd title reign. I don't see anyone stopping them this time. (Ref checks on Zula as Rebel pulls himself up to the ring apron, still partially blinded.)
Eric: I have just gotten word that the Bull Beast vs Iron Eagle match has been postponed until Friday Frenzy. No word as to why... anyway, coming up, Dead Skull goes one on one with Ninja Master... next! (fade out)
--------------------------------------- ***Commercials*** --------------------------------------- (Fade in to backstage, where B.T. Anthony is dressed in silver leg length tights and silver boots. He is doing some pushups.)
Eric: There you see "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony, warming up for a epic confrontation with the one man he has never defeated... Kobra Khan.
Michael: And remember, Khan has 2 submission wins over him already. Eric: Will it become 3 wins or will B.T. shatter Khan's dreams of regaining the LPW World title? (Scene back to the ring as japanese music begins to play.)
Marty: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first... from Tokyo, Japan... weighing 254 lbs... Ninja Master! (He comes out and walks down the aisle.)
Michael: We need to have the ring checked so we don't have another incident like what Songi had earlier tonight.
Eric: "We" are staying right here, pal... *Halfway down the aisle, Dead Skull runs out from behind with a chair and levels Ninja with it.* Eric: Wait a minute! Dead Skull with a chair from behind! Michael: At least it's not a bat with barbwire on it. *Skull continues to pound Ninja with the chair in the aisle some more before dropping the chair, picking him up and throwing him in the ring. Skull makes his way to the commentators and grabs Michael by the shirt. Michael is now scared for his life.*
Skull: YOU THINK YOU CAN BEAT ME?? I DARE YOUR SORRY ASS TO TRY IT!! (He lets go and slaps the headset off Michael's head with authority. He looks over at Eric. Eric raises his hands up in a "I don't want involved with it" gesture.)
*Skull starts to head back in the ring and notices Michael going to put his headset on. Skull hops back down, goes over, yanks the headset off and clobbers him on the head with it. As Skull returns to the ring, Eric removes his headset to assist Michael, who is now bleeding above the forehead, back to the dressing room area. Crowd boos VERY loudly for his actions.*
*Ninja is getting back up as Skull rolls inside the ring. Skull delivers a swift series of kicks to Ninja's knees and legs. He picks
Ninja up, sets him on the ropes and punches like a madman. Ninja stumbles to a corner. Skull whips him to the opposite corner, charges at him and Ninja hits him with a Karate Kid-like kick to the jaw.
While Skull's back is to Ninja, he clotheslines him from behind, knocking him down.* *The crowd starts cheering for Ninja. He pulls Skull up by the hair and gives him two headbutts, the second one harder than the first, without letting go of his hair. Ninja goes for an irish whip, Skull reverses and dropkicks him in the leg. Ninja falls down. Skull makes a cover... 1... 2... kickout.*
*Skull goes up to the top rope as Ninja gets back to his feet. Ninja stumbles back in the ropes, causing Skull to fall down, groin hitting the turnbuckle. Ninja goes over and lifts him over in a press slam throw. Songi makes his way down to the ring, holding the back of his head. Ninja doesn't see him yet.*
*As Songi gets close to the ring, Samauri Master charges out and runs down Songi with a clothesline. Ninja kneels down and pummels Skull with hard punches. Songi gets up and a fistfight with him and Samauri breaks out. Their struggle spills in the ring. Moments later, both teams were at each others throats. The ref stops the match and signals for others to come out and break them up as the show goes to a commercial...*
--------------------------------- ***Commercials*** --------------------------------- (Fade in to wide crowd shot as slow Egyptian music plays. Kobra Khan makes his way out, flexing his hands, crowd booing. Neither Eric or
Michael has come back out yet.)
Marty: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle... from Cairo, Egypt... weighing 266 lbs... Kobra Khan!
*Khan gets to the ring and rolls in. Music fades away as "Jam On It" by Newcleus starts to play. B.T. Anthony makes his way out.* Marty: His opponent... from Nashville, TN... weighing 252 lbs... "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony! *BT charges to the rings and slides in while the music fades away. Khan meets him with forearm smashes to his upper back. Khan gets him to his feet and chops him swiftly against the chest, almost knocking him down. Khan sends BT in the ropes and floors him with a snap thrust kick. Makes a cover... 1.... 2.... shoulder up.*
*Khan goes to pull him up but BT grabs him and rolls him over in a small package. 1... 2... kickout. BT sends Khan in the ropes, Khan reverses and BT attempts the Back Roll for a quick win. Khan anticipated that, caught BT around the waist, swinging him downward and Khan drops him in a modified tombstone. Khan stands up and gets behind BT, preparing to use his Venom finisher.*
*"Relax" by Powerman 5000 begins to play. Darren Diamond walks out to a loud ovation. Hurricane Wild Child is with him. Khan turns in surprise as to why they are out here. Music stops.*
Darren: Kobra Khan, I just want to inform you of another match you will be involved in this Friday night. It will be you against
Hurricane Wild Child... (BT slowly gets back up) no hold barred... in a steel cage... for the vacant Superfight championship!
*As Khan says something back in disbelief, BT stands on the other side of the ring, waiting. Khan turns, BT runs at him and successfully gets the Back Roll on him. 1.... 2.... 3! At almost the same time,
Hurricane runs down and enters the ring, attacking Khan.* Marty: The winner of this match.... "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony! Hurricane and Khan swap punches as BT and the ref leave the ring. Show ends with Darren shown still standing at the top of the aisle, smiling....*
