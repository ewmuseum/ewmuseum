LPW — Tuesday Night Clash

Tuesday Night Clash, 9 April 2002

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TUESDAY NIGHT CLASH - APRIL 9, 2002 ((Personal note: Scheduled for tonight was kYd-X vs Matt Ziro. kYd-X failed to send in a match strategy so Matt is awarded the win by forfeit.))
**Brief LPW initials logo amination is displayed in front of blurry dark blue moving clouds. Each letter flashes for a moment, from left to right, then the scene fades to black. A new scene fades in in the form of previously recorded footage by LPW president Darren Diamond in an office environment. The sounds of the audience is muted to the television viewers.**
Darren: This past Friday, Dead Skull and Songi were scheduled to meet in the semi-finals of the World championship tournament. As we all know, Dragon
Ninja pretended to be Songi and scored a win over Dead Skull. Since Dragon Ninja was already eliminated, he's not eligible to advance to the finals. And because referee Lynni Tappan made a legit 3 count leading to Dead Skull's pinfall loss and the referee's decision is final, Skull is also eliminated. Due to Kobra Khan's current position in the lower half of the tournament, he is now automatically advanced to the finals and will face either Wesley
Potter, Jake MacKenna or Devastator for the vacant LPW championship in the main event at Clash of the Champions this Saturday. And finally, because of recent events between Andrew Dixon and former 16-time LPW champion Ultimate
Warrior, they will now face each other in the ring at Clash of the Champions. Thank you... **TNC intro instantly appears to the tune of "We Have Explosive" by Future Sound Of London, showing brief moments of previous matches. The words "Tuesday Night Clash" slide into view from different sides. Scene fades to a wide shot of the fans cheering as multicolor pyros explode around the videotron.**
Voice of Eric Davidson: Welcome everyone to another edition of Tuesday Night Clash and the countdown to Clash of the Champions continues. 4 days away and 4 competitors remaining, one of them will be crowned the new LPW champion this Saturday! Tonight is the beginning of "Championship Week" here in
Legend Pro Wrestling. **TNC music fades to silence as the scene changes to the parking lot where a sports car is pulling in a parking space. Ultimate Warrior steps out and gets his gear bag as the fans cheer for his arrival.**
Eric: There's Ultimate Warrior, now arriving at the arena. He and Andrew Dixon need to settle the issues between them and they will this Saturday night... **Suddenly, without warning, Dixon appears from out of nowhere and nails Warrior from behind with a forearm to the head, knocking him down.** Eric: ...Hey! That's Dixon! He's not waiting until Saturday! **Dixon stomps Warrior for a few moments, then picks him up and throws him head first through the passenger side window. Glass flies everywhere and setting off the alarm of a nearby car. Warrior slumps to the cold pavement unconscious.**
Dixon: (looking down at him, speaking in a near sinister tone) Greatest LPW champion ever..... what a f*n joke....
**Dixon stomps his head one more time for good measure then calmly leaves. Scene changes back to Eric at the commentator table, almost speechless. The fans are shocked, booing Dixon.**
Eric: ...What the hell is wrong with Dixon?? He has taken this way too far and personal ! Warrior is gonna tear him apart this Saturday and Dixon has no one to blame but himself!
LIGHTWEIGHT TITLE MATCH (C) Rock And Roll Rebel VS Dragon Ninja Referee: Lynni Tappan **Techno japanese music starts to play and the camera focus shifts to the top of the aisle. Dragon Ninja walks out, pauses to make a couple martial arts stances then walks down to the ring. The fans give him a mixed reaction because they are still surprised over the incident moments ago.**
[DING, DING, DING] "Shorty" Marty Jennings: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and it is for the LPW Lightweight championship. Introducing first, the challenger... from Tokyo, Japan... weighing 220 lbs... Dragon Ninja!
**(as Dragon enters the ring and does a few more stances)** Eric: Dragon's partners, Ninja Master and Samauri Master, will be in action later in the show as they challenge the Shadowlords for the tag team titles.
**Dragon's music fades to silence and "Epic" by Faith No More starts playing. The champion makes his way out and to the ring, shaking hands on the way. The fans are starting to cheer louder, putting the Warrior-Dixon scene aside.**
Marty: His opponent... from Eureka, CA... weighing 228 lbs... the LPW Lightweight champion... Rock And Roll Rebel ! Eric: Rebel has made an open challenge for his lightweight title at Clash of the Champions but no one has yet to accept it.
**Music fades to silence.** [DING, DING] Eric: Bell sounds and we're underway. **They shake hands, which gets cheers from the crowd. Long-time LPW fans don't see that very often. They circle around the ring then lock up. Rebel gets the initiative with a arm twist and yanks it a couple times. Dragon reverses the arm twist and does it to Rebel, then gives him a short kick to the gut. Rebel drops to his knees, holding his gut.**
Eric: Dragon with the early advantage, he needs to make full use of his martial arts to become the champion.
**Dragon goes for a kick to his head. Rebel blocks it, reaches back and pulls Dragon's other leg out from under him. Dragon falls down and leaps right back up as Rebel gets to his feet.**
Eric: Both men back to a vertical base... **Dragon goes for a right punch, Rebel dodges by leaning back and attempts his Epic punch. Dragon saw it coming and dodged the same way, then hops out of the ring.** Eric: Whoa! Rebel almost had this one over, good thing Dragon was able to dodge it. Dragon rolling back in the ring at 3...
**They circle around again and before they can lock up, Rebel kicks him in the gut and hits with a few European uppercuts, taking Dragon to a corner.** Eric: Rebel with a greater amount of experience than Dragon... taking control at this point.
**Rebel whips Dragon to the opposite corner, Dragon reverses and Rebel hits the other corner. Dragon charges at him, Rebel grabs the top rope and lifts himself up and above Dragon as he goes for a spear and lands in the ringpost. Rebel drops down on Dragon and they both roll over into a modified sunset flip position..... 1..... 2..... Dragon kicks out by rolling up and back.**
Eric: Wow, that was pretty original! At least, it is in LPW... **Rebel goes to stand and is met by stiff kicks, alternating from his left and right sides. Dragon goes to whip Rebel to the other corner, Rebel stops in mid-whip and floors him with a short clothesline.**
Eric: Rebel being the weaker of the two, knocks him down again. **Rebel goes up to the top rope as Dragon stands. Dragon staggers back into the ropes and Rebel lands groin first in the turnbuckle.** Eric: Oooooooo.... he's gonna feel that one in the morning.... **Dragon steps through the ropes to the outside, still on the apron. He climbs up to the top rope behind Rebel and carefully turns to face the audience.** Eric: ...What is he going to do here?? **In one swift motion, Dragon quickly leaps up just enough to wrap his lower legs around Rebel's head, leaning forward to catch the middle rope avoiding landing on the turnbuckle himself and spins Rebel backwards over the top and to the floor in a modified hurricarana. Still holding to the middle rope, he pulls himself up and over back in the ring.**
Eric: What a move! He might have knocked out the champion with that! But he can't win the title by countout!
**Lynni starts her 10 count. Rebel is back to his hands and knees at 6. He barely rolls back in at 9.** Eric: I'm surprised he's still able to get up! **Dragon picks him up and whips him in the ropes. Rebel ducks under a clothesline. They both had the same idea of going for a Epic punch and both hit each others head. Rebel and Dragon go down and possibly out.**
Eric: They thought the same thing and hit each other with the same move. **As Lynni is making another 10 count, Rebel slowly turns over and puts one arm over Dragon's chest for a cover..... 1..... 2...... 3!** [DING, DING, DING] **Loud cheers from the crowd as "Epic" plays again. Lynni goes to get the title belt and award it to Rebel.** Marty: The winner of this match... and *still* LPW Lightweight champion... Rock And Roll Rebel ! Eric: Rebel retains the lightweight championship and will go on to Clash of the Champions with his open challenge still intact.
**Lynni goes to help Dragon up to a sitting position as Rebel leaves. Scene changes to backstage, where interviewer Sherry Sheryl, Jake MacKenna and Hillary are standing by.** Sherry: Tonight, Jake, you go one-on-one with the "American Superstar" Wesley Potter in a first round match of the world title tournament. What are your thoughts on this?
Jake: Well, at least Potter had the guts to show up tonight... unlike my opponent last week... who not only was too scared to show up and face me but then left the LPW immedietely afterwards. But tonight is not about running my mouth, claiming I'm the best or making fun of Potter's wrestling ability or lack of it.
**Sherry begins noticeably checking Hillary out, which gets a loud cheerful response from the fans.** Jake: Tonight is about my goal. And that goal is very simple. So simple, that even Wesley Potter could comprehend it. To win this match and advance further in the tournament. Only one thing stands in the way of that goal.
What is that thing you may be asking yourself? Well, it's simple. Wesley Potter. And in order for me to achieve my goal, I have to go through this obstacle. Indeed, Potter is in a very bad situation tonight...
**Jake notices Sherry checking Hillary out and stops mid-sentence.** Jake: But I think there's something I forgot back in my dressing room. Come on, Hill. **He takes her hand and leads her away. Away from Sherry. The ponytailed beauty watches them leave with a sexy look in her eyes, smiling. Fade to black.** =====COMMERCIALS===== **Scene fades in to a large dressing room where a small birthday party is being held in Darren Diamond's honor, who turns 31 today [detail withheld]. Legends
Shi`Noku, Iron Eagle, "Maniac" Mike Marsh, Bull Beast, Ninja Master, Samauri
Master and Zula Bon Bimbay are there, preparing plates of cake and other snacks. The camera pans towards the door and walking inside is heavy metal singer and LPW legend Ozzy Osbourne. He was Rock And Roll Rebel's tag team partner and they were known as the "Metal Brigade". The fans cheer loudly, totally not expected.**
Ozzy: Is there room in this party for one more? Darren: ...Ozzy! You made it! **He goes over with a glass of punch in one hand and shakes Ozzy's hand with the other. The other legends notice his arrival and greet him.** Ozzy: I know this is supposed to be a happy occasion but I saw something happen in the parking lot that you should know about.
Darren: (a little confused) Like what? Ozzy: Ultimate Warrior was attacked by a big guy I never seen before... **Darren starts to frown, realizing who he's talking about.** Ozzy: He took Warrior by the hair and threw him head first in the side window of his car.....
Some of the legends: ...What?? **Darren pauses, turns around and throws his cup to the opposite wall, not believing what he heard. Scene goes back out to the crowd.** **"Hair of the Dog" plays as Andrew Dixon heads down to the ring with loud boos from the crowd on all sides. Dixon doesn't even look at them as he enters the ring and gets a mic from the announcer.**
Eric: Hasn't he caused enough trouble for one day?? Dixon: Diamond, you stupid bas**rd!.... You cost me my shot at the world title! (pauses, more boos) I should already be on my way to the finals right now but noooooooooo.... you're afraid of seeing me with the World title around my waist because you and everyone else here knows I am the best thing this federation has ever produced in a long time!
**"Relax" by Powerman 5000 starts to play and LPW president Darren Diamond walks down to the ring, without his glasses and a serious look on his face.** Eric: Uh oh! Here comes the birthday man himself, all 31 years old of him... and he's not happy! **Darren steps in the ring and pulls out another mic from inside his jacket.** Diamond: What the bloody hell are you blabbering about this time?? I did not cost you any titles!
Dixon: But you gave the Wrinkled-up Warrior permission to ref my match! And you knew that there wasn't anyway in hell Warrior would let me walk out the victor on Friday!
Diamond: Even if Warrior was NOT the ref, I doubt you would have won anyway because as arrogant as you are, you are like a toilet bowl...... big, white and full of crap!
[very loud cheers] Eric: ...Wow! **Dixon turns around for a moment, thinking about that comment, then turns back to Darren.** Dixon: (in an eerie calmed tone) You know what? (pauses) I'm a forgiving man.... and I'm willing to forgive and forget.
Eric: What? Dixon: But the fact of the matter is, as we Americans say, "what goes around comes around". And whether you had anything to do with it or not, there will come a time when you have to pay the piper. But until that day comes, then how about you put my name on that open challenge Kobra Khan made for tonight? (tone raises back to usual) I'm in the mood to kick someone's ass and I don't care whose it is!
**Dixon drops the mic and leaves the ring to no music. Darren turns around in Dixon's direction.** Darren: Then what I'm about to say next will really make you want to kick somebody's bum.... (Dixon stops and turns around) Your match this Saturday against Ultimate Warrior is now......... A SUPERFIGHT!
**Dixon looks a bit in shock.** Eric: Oh my.... god! Dixon and Warrior... superfight... Clash of the Champions... this Saturday! Darren: ...Who will pay the piper now?? **"Relax" plays again as Dixon leaves frustrated. Camera focuses on Darren.** Eric: Dixon may have bitten off more than he can chew! Darren laid down the law on him tonight! (fade to black)
=====COMMERCIALS===== **Fade in to a shot of the crowd.** QUARTERFINALS OF THE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNAMENT "American Superstar" Wesley Potter VS Jake MacKenna Referee: Stephen Woods **"Cemetery Gates" by Pantera begins to play and Wesley Potter makes his way out, insulting the fans on how much they wish they were as great as he is.** [DING, DING, DING] Marty: The following contest is a quarterfinals match of the World championship tournament, with the winner against Devastator in the semifinals this Friday. (The fans cheer over the mention of Devastator's name, the odds-on favorite to win the title this Saturday.) Introducing first... from Jonesville, VA... weighing 250 lbs... the "American
Superstar", Wesley Potter! (Potter enters the ring.) Eric: 3 more matches to go until we have a new LPW champion. It's been two years since we had one...
**Music fades away. "Shattered" by Adema hits as Jake MacKenna makes his way out with Hillary. The fans pop like mad for this young superstar and his stunning girlfriend. They enter the ring and Jake climbs the turnbuckle, raising his arms high. Flashes from hundreds of cameras go off as the cheering continues.**
Marty: His opponent... being accompanied by Hillary... from Halifax, Nova Scotia... weighing 276 lbs... Jake MacKenna! Eric: Hillary and backstage interviewer Sherry Sheryl has been a hot topic backstage as of late. Sherry obviously has personal feelings for her... but does Hillary feel the same way? We never seen them both "in the act" so it's hard to tell.
**Music fades to silence.** [DING, DING] Eric: Either Potter or MacKenna will advance and have the unfortunate opportunity to go one on one with the 'demon in black', Devastator this
Friday night. That's going to be very interesting to see. **As Jake helps Hillary out of the ring, Potter attacks him from behind with punches to the back of the head.** Eric: Potter with a cheap shot from behind... **Potter whips Jake in the ropes and catches him with a strong powerslam into an immediate cover..... 1..... Jake kicks out quickly.** Eric: Potter going for an early cover but only gets a 1. It's way too soon to beat someone like Jake after just one move.
**Potter stands up and picks up Jake's legs. He does a legdrop on his groin area.** Eric: Ow... I could feel that one from over here. **Potter picks up the legs again and goes for another legdrop. Jake catches it and slowly gets Potter's legs in position for a possible sharpshooter. He turns over and gets to his feet, definitely applying a sharpshooter.**
Eric: Sharpshooter on Potter! How long will he be able to stand it? **Potter struggles for a bit with Hillary cheering her man on. Potter manages to slowly pull himself close enough to the bottom rope and grabs it, forcing a break. Ref starts a 5 count. Jake lets go at 4.**
Eric: Potter got himself out of that hold but the damage is already done. **Jake picks him up and sets him up for a powerbomb. After he lifts him up, before he can drop him, Potter pushes himself off and away, landing on his feet. He then grabs Jake and slams him with a rock bottom.**
Eric: Star Killer! He planted it but will it be enough? **Potter makes a cover..... 1..... 2..... Jake gets the shoulder up. Potter pushes his arm back down..... 1..... 2..... Jake gets the other shoulder up. Potter now holds down both arms..... 1..... 2..... Jake kicks out this time. Potter gets up, frustrated.** Eric: Potter can't keep Jake down for a 3. He's wondering "What do I have to do to beat this man?".
**Potter picks Jake up and whips him in the ropes. Jake ducks a clothesline and bounces off the other side with a spear. He covers him..... 1..... 2..... Potter kicks out.** Eric: Whoa, almost had him there. The ref's hand was coming down for the 3. **The fans react to the presense of Andrew Dixon coming back to the ring.** Eric: Now WHAT is he doing out here again? Can't he wait until he takes on Kobra Khan?? **Dixon hops on the apron and distracts the ref, who is ordering Dixon to step down. As Jake picks up Potter, the "American Superstar" jabs him in the throat. Jake gasps for air.**
Eric: Dixon has no business being involved in this match. He really needs to leave! **Superfight champion Kobra Khan makes his way out. Because of Dixon being his opponent tonight, the fans are slightly *cheering* for Khan.** Eric: It's Kobra Khan! And he's coming for Dixon! **Potter lifts Jake up and places him on the top rope. He climbs up there too.** Eric: Superstar Maker coming up (spinebuster off the top rope)... if he hits this, it's all over!
**Dixon hops down and turns to leave. Khan catches him off guard with a running clothesline. Dixon is knocked back and hits the side of the ring. The impact sends both Potter and Jake landing groin first on the turnbuckle. Potter falls off.** Eric: The brawl on the outside is doing more damage to those on the inside! **As Khan and Dixon duke it out, Jake regains his senses and stands on the top rope. He flies off and hits with a splash into a cover..... 1..... 2...... 3! Hillary and the fans cheer.** [DING, DING, DING] **But before an announcement could be made, the battle with Khan and Dixon rolls its way in the ring. Potter rolls outside, unaware of what's happening as Jake escorts Hillary away from the ring, glancing back at what's going on.**
Eric: Looks like we don't have to wait on this one... we're seeing it right now! **Referee Stephen Woods makes a quick decision and calls for another starting bell.** [DING, DING] Eric: It's now official and here we go! **Dixon gets the advantage after an eye gouge. He whips Khan in the ropes and bodypresses the Superfight champion over his head before dropping him to the mat. Khan bends over in pain.**
Eric: Dixon showing us a little hidden strength there... **Dixon slaps on an iron claw to Khan's head.** Eric: Uh oh, Khan's in trouble! How will he get out of this? **Khan answers that question with a modified Venom hold to Dixon's arm. A couple seconds later, Dixon's arm is experiencing tremendous pain, forcing him to let go.**
Eric: ...That's one way of doing it! **As Dixon tries to shake off the effects of Venom, Khan stands up and prepares to use Venom on his head. But then, the fans erupt upon yet another arrival to the ring....**
Eric: Here comes the Warrior! Bandaged head and all! **Right before Khan slaps on Venom, Warrior quickly rolls in the ring and tackles Dixon down, followed with hard punches. Khan seems confused by his interference.** [DING, DING, DING] Eric: Warrior exploding on Dixon, who by the way will end up winning this match by disqualification!
**Khan makes a "You can have him" motion and leaves the ring, not looking back. Security runs out to separate the two gladiators in the ring.** Eric: This fued is going to get very nasty this Saturday! Someone is in for a trip to the local hospital after this one! (fade to black)
=====COMMERCIALS===== **Fade in to the crowd as the sound of blowing wind & burning fire starts to play. The lights shift to shades of orange and blue. The fans boo the champions as they make their way out.**
WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH (C) "Shadowlords" VS "Oriental Assassins" Referee: Daniel Tappan [DING, DING, DING] Marty: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and it is for the LPW tag team championship. Introducing first... at a combined weight of 600 lbs... the LPW tag team champions... Fire and Ice, the Shadowlords!
**Eric is now having problems getting his wireless headset to work and there are no extra ones close by. He makes a hasty exit to the back and try to get it repaired. The champions enter the ring and remove their robes & title belts. The lights return to normal as the music fades away. Techno japanese music starts to play and the fans cheer for the challengers, who now make their way out.**
Marty: Their opponents and challengers... from Tokyo, Japan... at a combined weight of 502 lbs... Ninja Master and Samauri Master, the Oriental
Assassins! **They slide in the ring and a brawlfest between both teams breaks out. Within moments, the much stronger Shadowlords are overpowering them and have the Assassins on the mat. Fire picks up Samauri and throws him out of the ring. Both Shadowlords double team Ninja with hard kicks. They both get him to his feet and execute a double suplex. They immediately go to opposite corners for a very early finish. Just then, both Fire and Ice jerk in pain as a small black dart hits each of them in the neck. At the same time,
Samauri Master is rolling back inside. The champions fall off the turnbuckles, Ice to the inside and Fire to the outside. The Assassins take position on either side of Ice, waits for him to stand and they hit their double roundhouse kicks to both sides of his head. Ninja and Samauri both cover him, each hooking one of his legs..... 1...... 2.......3!**
[DING, DING, DING] [very loud cheers form the fans] Marty: The winners of this match..... and *NEW* LPW tag team champions... the Oriental Assassins! **The new champs get their belts and quickly leave, holding the belts up high. The camera tries to catch a view of the mysterious shadowed figure but is nowhere to be found. But the camera does spot something else.... a 2nd masked dark figure. This one is allowing the camera to see him and now, the fans notice him as well. His attire is white from the head and shoulders & fades to black around the stomach area. He has white boots that go up to the knees and the color fades upward towards the beltline to black. The eyes cannot be seen at all but he is looking at the activity down below. His body is slightly shaking in extreme anger towards something. The scene changes to the party where Darren and the legends are sitting around, talking. The door is forced open and 4 men walk in. Andrew Dixon, Wesley Potter, Dead Skull and Songi. The Slayers are holding barbwire covered bats. Darren is on his feet outraged.**
Darren: Now what??? Dixon: The four of us are getting tired of being screwed over by you and your sorry ass legends! (They all get up, ready for combat.) It ends NOW!
**Dixon rushes over, grabs Darren and slams his head into what's left of the cake. Potter catches Maniac coming at him and drops him with a Star Killer rock bottom through a nearby table. The Slayers keep the others at bay, swinging their bats. Dixon gets close to the Slayers, preparing to leave.
Dead Skull and Songi give a gesture at each other then whacks Dixon with their bats, knocking him down and almost out. Potter dashes out the room without thinking twice.**
Dead Skull: (looking down at Dixon) ...Never trust the Slayers.... **He grabs a cup of punch that wasn't knocked over in the brawl and raises it in the air in the form of a toast.** Dead Skull: Happy birthday, Darren...... **He drinks the punch as the Slayers calmly leave the room and Darren is on his knees with a look that could kill as the show fades to black...........** Legend Pro Wrestling "Superstars of today, legends of tomorrow" http://mywebpages.comcast.net/legendpro/
