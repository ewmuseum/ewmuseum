Special Report: World Title Vacated

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following is from an email I received earlier tonight by the now former LPW champion, Andrew Dixon: "Hey guys... I'm not really sure where to start... with the stress of the finals and all that on me now [detail withheld] I'm gonna need to take some time off for several weeks and I do not feel it is fair for me to remain champion so it is my wish to vacate the title. I hope to get back in the LPW as soon as possible. I will keep in close touch with all of you, but most of all keep [name withheld] in your prayers. Thanks guys."
We're now right back to where we started when LPW reopened exactly 2 months ago today.... without a champion.
