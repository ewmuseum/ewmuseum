LPW — Tuesday Night Clash

Tuesday Night Clash, 5 March 2002

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TUESDAY NIGHT CLASH - MARCH 5, 2002 (Tuesday Night Clash intro begins to play to the tune of "We Have
Explosive" by Future Sound Of London. After the intro concludes, scene fades in to a wide angle shot of the arena where over 10,000 fans are on their feet cheering, trying to get their signs on camera and the videotron.
Voice of Eric Davidson: We are LIVE... from the Gaylord Entertainment
Center, formerly the Municipal Auditorium, in Nashville, Tennessee! On this day, 13 years ago, back before we were on the internet, Legend
Pro Wrestling had its very first show, where Hulk Hogan became the first LPW champion, defeating WWF rival Zeus!
Voice of Michael Hunter: ...Only to lose that belt the next night to
Ultimate Warrior, the first of Warrior's 16 World title reigns.
(Camera focus on commentators, sitting at their table) Eric: Hogan was never the same after that... oh well (quickly changes the subject)...
Welcome to Tuesday Night Clash! I'm Eric Davidson, along with my long time broadcast partner Michael Hunter, and (to Michael) I'm sure you would agree that it's great to be back here in Legend Pro Wrestling after a recent 2 year absense.
Michael: (breaking character for a moment) No doubt about it. It's an almost overwhelming feeling to be back again. The new talent backstage are excited, the legends that are here are excited and these fans know they are in for a great show.(back in character)
Eric: Speaking of which, this will be a rather short show tonight. The registration offices opened on March 1 but only two people have been hired since then. Fortunately, they have worked with us before so we could see either one or both of them tonight. So until more talent signs up, we only have 2 official matches for you, both involving the legends of LPW's past.
Michael: Not much but it's better than having no show at all. Eric: With that in mind, let's head up to the ring and our new announcer... "Shorty" Marty Jennings! (music fades away)
Marty: Ladies and gentlemen... (crowd settles down some) please welcome to the ring at this time... the president of Legend Pro
Wrestling... Mr. Darren Diamond! (Crowd cheers as "Relax" by Powerman 5000 begins to play. Darren steps out in the open as the lyrics start to be spoken. He walks down to the ring, shaking a few hands on the way. Darren enters the ring to a standing ovation. He shakes Marty's hand and is handed the mic. Marty leaves the ring. Before Darren can say a single word, the cheers becomes louder so he takes a moment to look around, seeing all the people in attendance.)
Darren: Thank you. (crowd a little more quiet) We... are... back! (cheers) And we are ready to deliver the best in pro wrestling entertainment as only Legend Pro Wrestling can deliver! (cheers) Many of our new generation stars probably have no idea we are back in business but hopefully, someday, we will bring the family back together again. (cheers) On behalf of all of us here in Legend Pro
Wrestling, we thank you for the honor and privilige of performing for all of you tonight, for without you, we would not even be here!
(The lights in the arena fade to black as a mystic church bell chimes through the arena. The first guitar riff to Metallica's "For Whom The
Bell Tolls" begins playing. The music rumbles through the arena for a little bit. The solo in the intro hits and Dead Skull walks through the curtain with a blue spotlight on him. He slowly walks towards the ring looking around the arena at the fans as if in awe. He rolls into the ring under the bottom rope and the lights come back on. Dead Skull walks around the ring a few times seemingly taking everything in. He goes up to the second rope and the crowd begins to cheer him. He gets down, gets another mic from the timekeeper and approaches Diamond.)
Skull: Sorry about the intrusion but they cut my music on early. Darren: That's okay, I was done anyway. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce to you, one of the first new generation competitors LPW signed back in 1998..... Dead Skull! (As the crowd starts to cheer, they shake hands, then Diamond leaves the ring and walks back up the aisle.)
Skull: (to the fans) It's great to be back! (Crowd cheers some more.) Thank you, thank you. (Crowd cheers. He has a big smile on his face. A rare occurance but it's happened today. Motions for the crowd to calm down but they just get louder.) It's nice to see I wasn't forgotten.
(The crowd cheers some more.) Ok, ok... now I gotta get on with this. I can't be here all night. Even though I wish I could. (They begin to go crazy for his return.) HEY DARREN!!! LET ME STAAAAAYYYYYY!!!! (The fans cheer some more.) Ok really now, if I don't get on with it, I'm gonna piss off the suits and then I'll be in some deep sh... uh, trouble. I gotta abide by the rules now. I got a reputation. (He laughs and the fans begin to cheer again.) Seriously, I found that out the hard way. You see, after LPW went out of buisness 2 years ago, no other "big name" feds would give me a chance. (pauses) They all thought I was too dangerous... too much of a loose cannon... they were too damn stuck up with their big heads up their ass to let a loser like me in to bring their feds down. (The crowd begins to boo.) Yea I know, they're jerks. So I went back down and I've been in the indies ever since down in that... I can't say cuz, like I said, I got to abide by the rules. Heh. Well, I got a message for you stuck up snobs in those feds... (pauses and his trademark evil grin comes across his face.) I'm BAAAA-AAAAAACK! So fff... excuse me... Screw you!!!
(pauses) I wanna thank "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony, my first boss from years ago, and of course, Darren Diamond for giving me another chance to truly shine in this great company. My promise to them and to you fans that whether you hate me or not, I will always do my best to put on the best show that I can. (The crowd begins to chant "Dead Skull!!!
Dead Skull!!! Dead Skull!!!") Feels good to be back, although they told me that I had to tone down my language. What do you guys think?
(The crowd cheers.) And I've been trying but right now, I'm gonna have to say F... my language may be toned down but you can do your best to tame me but that's never gonna happen! You bring an opponent to me and
I'm gonna destroy that person!!! While we're on the subject, I WANT A
MATCH TONIGHT!!! So I'm putting out an open challenge to any wrestler in the back, be he a legend or a new guy. A word of warning to whoever has the b... hmm, that one confuses me so I'll just say 'guts' for now... (starts pacing around the ring) This is my first match back and
I'm NOT gonna lose! No matter what I have to do to win, I'm gonna do it! I'm gonna kick somebody's ass tonight!!! (The crowd cheers in reaction to the statement.) Now it's time to say goodbye till later guys. (Crowd boos.)
(a little out of character) Skull: Rules are a bitch, huh? (The sound of an oriental gong fills the arena. Everyone looks around in confusion. Another gong is heard.)
Skull: ....What the hell?? (Japanese music starts to play as something catches the fans' attention towards the aisle. A cameraman turns and looks in that direction. Standing at the top is a hooded white ninja with red/blue trim. Skull sees him and looks surprised.)
Michael: That... that's Dragon Ninja of the legend trio, the Oriental
Assassins! Eric: Oooooh boy, this is going to get very interesting! (Dragon walks down the aisle) For those who didn't watch us in the past, Dead Skull once had a group called the Puerto Rican Slayers. They had a brief fued with the Assassins, which ended in a 6-man superfight. The
Slayers were victorious. (Dragon Ninja enters the ring and gets almost right in Skull's face as the music fades out. For a few moments, they stand there, eyes locked on each other. The crowd senses a fight about to break out at any moment and cheers in anticipation. You can tell Skull is becoming a little frustrated over his arrival.)
Skull: What are YOU doing here?? Last I remember, me and the Slayers left you and your kung fu friends on your back, looking up at the lights, in a superfight! After all this time, are you a glutton for punishment or do you just not know when to qui..... (Dragon snatches the mic out of Skull's hand, never breaking eye contact.)
Eric: Uh oh.... Dragon: (pauses) After all this time, you still not know when to shut up! (crowd goes "Oooooooooooo...")
Michael: Whoa, good rebound! Eric: This is gonna get ugly in a hurry. Michael: Not as ugly as Dead Skull looks... Eric: Hey, you better be glad he can't hear you! Michael: (snickers) I could beat him with my eyes closed. Eric: Yeah, sound asleep and dreaming of it. Dragon: Yes, you beat us in a superfight... but I was more than you could handle and I almost knocked you out cold.
Michael: He has a point there. (Skull grabs the mic back.) Skull: Yeah? (gets right in Ninja's face) Then let's see you try to knock me out right now! (As the crowd cheers, Ninja slowly turns away, pondering the temptation, then quickly attempts a backhand strike. Skull takes a short step back to avoid contact then takes Ninja down with a spear takedown.)
Eric: There they go! Michael: Oooh yeah! (They roll around, exchanging punches as the fans cheer them on. Referees run out to separate them but still have difficulty keeping them that way.) Michael: Looks like they just picked up where they left off 3 years ago! (Skull finds the mic laying on the canvas and picks it up.)
Skull: (to Ninja) You and me! Right here! TONIGHT! (Crowd cheers again as Ninja nods in acceptance. Ninja points at Skull, makes a "cut the throat" gesture and leaves the ring, heading back up the aisle.)
Eric: (as cameras show Skull being restrained from leaving the ring before Ninja gets backstage) What started as a welcoming return has turned into a lightweight match for later on! Dead Skull... Dragon
Ninja... Slayers vs Assassins, round 2! -------------------- Commercial for a Zen laxative is shown. Their motto: "All things must pass." -------------------- (Camera fade in to Bull Beast about to enter the ring. He has no entrance music.) Eric: We're back and getting ready for our first of 2 legends matches... Marty: Our opening contest is a legends match, scheduled for 1 fall. Making his way in the ring... from Athens, Greece... weighing 289 lbs... Bull Beast! (Bull Beast enters the ring and removes his bull's head as the theme from "Terminator" begins to play. The crowd cheers as Terminator makes his way out and starts down the aisle.)
Marty: His opponent... from Phoenix, AZ... weighing 235 lbs...Terminator! Michael: Here comes one of the best Intercontinental champions of all time. Of course, that belt has been changed into the National title since LPW's internet debut 4 years ago.
Eric: (as Terminator enters the ring) Bull Beast never had much success to begin with, especially against Terminator... (Terminator charges Beast with a spear takedown right away) ...whoa, not wasting any time and this match is underway!
*Terminator picks Beast back up and whips him hard in the corner. He does it again to the opposite corner.* Eric: He's not giving Bull Beast a chance to even breathe! Michael: I've never seen him like this before. Wonder why he turned up the intensity...? *Terminator whips Beast in the ropes and catches him in a hard impact powerslam near the ropes on the other side. He stands and raises his right fist above him, assuming a charging stance, waiting for Beast to get up. He flexes his fist open and close.*
Eric: Broken Heart already?? Michael: Good thing he don't get paid by the hour. *Beast staggers to his feet and slowly turns around. Terminator waits for the right moment and runs at him, pulling the fist back. He leaps forward and executes his famous flying heart punch. Beast falls down, almost through the ropes. Terminator pulls him back in the ring and covers him.*
1.... 2.... 3! Eric: Just like that, it's all over! Marty: Here is your winner... Terminator! (Ref raises his hand in victory) Michael: I don't think he realizes there were only 3 matches tonight. At this rate, the show will be over a lot sooner. (Terminator leaves the ring and heads back up the aisle, shaking a few hands)
Eric: After the break, the action will be more fast paced when Dead
Skull of the Puerto Rican Slayers locks up with Dragon Ninja of the
Oriental Assassins.... next!
-------------------- Commercial for the movie "Blade 2" is shown. It will be in theaters on
Friday, March 22. -------------------- (Camera fade in to the beautiful Lynni Tappan, LPW's first female referee, entering the ring for the next match. A lot of whistles and cheers for her. She smiles and waves.)
Michael: Now that's a showstopper for ya... (clears throat) Eric: Former legend womens' champion, Mistress Death... it's great to see her back. Michael: And her front... Eric: Hey, behave yourself. She IS married, you know. (Japanese music starts to play and Dragon Ninja makes his way to the ring.) Marty: The following contest is scheduled for 1 fall. Coming down the aisle... from Tokyo, Japan... weighing 220 lbs... Dragon Ninja!
Eric: This should be an exciting fast paced match, given he and Dead
Skull are almost similar in size, speed and brawling skill.
("For Whom The Bell Tolls" by Metallica plays as Dragon enters the ring. Crowd cheering.) Michael: Dragon Ninja has an opportunity to avenge their loss to the Slayers 9 years ago. Don't expect a lot a technical wrestling in this one.
(Dead Skull walks out to the ring.) Marty: His opponent... from Naguabo, Puerto Rico... weighing 225 lbs... Dead Skull! Eric: Given the hostile nature of these two, it would be best for
Lynni just to stand back and let them go at it.
(As the camera trails behind Skull as he approaches the ring, Skull is jumped from behind by 2 hooded men. They fiercely punch and kick him on the padded floor. The cheers turn into boos.)
Eric: What the...? My god, it's the Assassins! Ninja Master and Samauri Master! Where did they come from?? (Lynni looks surprised at what is happening. Dragon appears to be laughing under his mask.)
Michael: (in a sarcastic tone) Well, this show just got even shorter... *The Assassins pick up Skull and throw him in the ring. With Lynni leaving the ring for her safety, Dragon picks up Skull, whips him in the ropes and connects with a solid thrust kick to under the chin, almost causing a whiplash effect as Skull falls down.*
Eric: Oh come on! Why did they ruin this match for?? Michael: Why don't you go over there and ask them? Eric: Nooooooooo thank you... *Ninja and Samauri enter the ring and gesture to Dragon to pick Skull up. He proceeds to do so and moves Skull to the center of the ring, holding him up. Ninja and Samauri assume attacking stances on either side, Dragon quickly moves back and they hit Skull with spinning kicks to both sides of the head. Skull falls down again. This time, he appears to be out cold.*
Eric: (to Michael) You were not kidding about them wanting to avenge their superfight loss. I think they did just that!
Michael: But we would have preferred to have an actual match to settle thngs. (All 3 Assassins stand over Skull and assume combat poses.)
Eric: Unfortunately, the other Slayers are not even here. How will
Dead Skull be able to survive against this trio?? (fade out)
-------------------- Commercial for the movie "Resident Evil" is shown. Opens this Friday. -------------------- *Slow mysterious suspense music plays. Fade in to a black screen with the words "JUNE 18, 1993..." in red letters fading into view. They fade away and "DOOMSDAY" appears in its place. Still photo fades in:
wide shot of the crowd in the arena. Eric's voice (Michael Hunter was not there): This event... is a historical night for LPW, our very last show! (Still photo: an exhausted Hurricane Wild Child struggling to leave the ring) The entire roster will be paired up in elimination matches by random drawing until only one wrestler remains. That man will be the undisputed Supreme World champion! (Still photo: Kobra Khan walking down the aisle) ...You got to be kidding! Wild Child already wrestled the previous 2 matches! Now he has to face the current champion right now?? (Still photo: Khan press slamming Hurricane) Aw come on, Khan!
Just end it already! I don't see how Hurricane is hanging on. (Still photo: Khan using his Venom submission finisher) As if Hurricane wasn't suffering as it is! Now he has to endure that damn Venom hold!
(Still photo: Khan arguing with the referee) The referee is risking being Venom-ized himself by breaking it. (Still photo: Hurricane with a small package rollup on Khan) Wait a minute! Hurricane got him rolled over! (Sound of ref's 3 count made) HE DID IT! HE DID IT!
(Still photo: Khan is in total shock over what happened) Hurricane
Wild Child just won the LPW World title! (Still photo: Khan applying
Venom again, Hurricane's forehead is bleeding and referees are trying to make Khan let go) Thanks to Kobra Khan, Hurricane Wild Child is forced to drop out of the event. The title is now vacant until
Doomsday is over... (music fades to silence as the image fades too) -------------------- (Camera fade in to inside the arena as Egyptian music plays and Kobra
Khan slowly walks down the aisle, flexing his snake painted hands. The crowds' boos are the loudest of the night.)
Marty: The following contest... is the legends main event, scheduled for 1 fall. Coming down the aisle... from Cairo, Egypt... weighing 266 lbs... Kobra Khan!
Eric: (Khan entering the ring) This man, for a lack of a better word, is cold... cruel... relentless... and almost sadistic.
Michael: And those are his good points... ("Rock You Like A Hurricane" plays and Hurricane Wild Child comes out. He has a serious "game face" on and stops at the top of the aisle. Marty: His opponent... from Panama City, FL... weighing 245 lbs... Hurricane Wild Child! Eric: He's waited 9 years for this moment and he looks more than ready! (Hurricane runs to the ring at full speed. He slides in and Khan immediately locks Venom on.)
Eric: No! Not again! *Hurricane seems to be withstanding it for a moment, then squeezes his fingers into both the insides of Khan's wrists and with authority, slowly pulls Khan's hands off his head with a battle shout of sorts.
Khan can't believe what is happening as the crowd cheers* Michael: ...Oh my god, no one has ever powered out of it like that before! Not even Ultimate Warrior! (HWC quickly pulls Khan to him and delivers a few headbutts before releasing him. Khan drops to one knee, a little dizzy.)
Eric: Venom didn't seem to have any effect at all! *HWC picks him up, whips Khan in the ropes and hits with a clothesline that makes Khan do a 360 in mid air* Michael: Who-ho-ho-hoa! Hurricane is manhandling him! Eric: I can't believe what I'm seeing... but I like it! Hurricane: (to Khan) GET UP! (starts to pick him up, Khan low blows him. HWC stumbles to a corner, folded over in pain.)
Michael: Uh oh, the tide is turning! *Khan stands up, walks over to where HWC. He positions HWC's upper body through the ropes with the middle and bottom ropes in front and the top rope behind him. Khan stands HWC up and puts the Venom hold back on, pulling back so that HWC's feet are actually off the ring apron. Referee Daniel Tappan starts making a 5 count for him to let go.*
Eric: This doesn't look good for Hurricane! He needs to somehow get out of this! *Daniel counts to 5 but Khan has no intention of releasing the hold. Daniel calls for the bell, disqualifying Khan, but that still doesn't cause a break, so he tries to pull Khan off. Khan lets go and backhands Daniel out of the ring, then puts Venom back on yet again.*
Michael: (concerned) ...This is going a bit too far...
Eric: Someone needs to stop this lunatic! *Theme from "Terminator" plays as Terminator runs to the ring, crowd cheers loud* Michael: Ask and you shall receive! *Terminator slides in the ring and uses his arm from behind to choke Khan, forcing him to let HWC go. HWC falls out to the padded floor unconscious. Terminator lets Khan go, spins him around and hits him with a series of punches. He send Khan in the ropes, immediately bounces off the opposite ropes and gives Khan a Broken Heart, knocking
Khan out of the ring with authority. Crowd cheers loudly. Terminator then goes over to check on HWC*
Eric: What a chaotic situation this is! (Khan stumbling up the aisle, clutching his chest around the heart, looking back at Terminator with an evil look) The legends have torn the roof off the arena... and this is just our first show back! (Camera on Terminator helping HWC) I can't imagine what Friday Frenzy will be like! For Michael Hunter, I'm
Eric Davidson saying good night everybody! My god, whata night...
*Camera fades to black, still on Terminator and HWC* END OF SHOW -------------------- Legend Pro Wrestling "Superstars of today, legends of tomorrow" http://mywebpages.comcast.net/legendpro/
