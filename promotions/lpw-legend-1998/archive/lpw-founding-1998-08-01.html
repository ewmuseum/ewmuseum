LPW — Founding

Legend Pro Wrestling

1 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am "Mastermind" B.T.Anthony, founder and CEO of the LPW. I'm here in search of good roleplaying talent to join my league. The LPW motto is: 'Where the superstars of today become the legends of tomorrow'. I offer two weekly cards on Tuesdays and Fridays, a bi-weekly Sunday card, and a monthly PPV. It's somewhat booked, meaning I use special dice and charts from a real wrestling rpg gamebook to run matches. All results are completely fair (unlike most feds, WFW for example). The LPW is made to allow you to customize your character and your attacks/strategies, Take a look around the LPW site and check it out for yourself. Everything there should be self-explanatory, but feel free to email me with any questions you might have. Many players I've had, past and present, tell me that I run the best fed there is. So if you're looking for a great place to start your e-wrestling career, then the LPW is the place for you. "Mastermind" B.T.Anthony LPW president http://www.angelfire.com/ca2/LPW
