LPW — Accepting New Players

LPW accepting new players

16 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am "Mastermind" B.T.Anthony, president of Legend Pro Wrestling. Our motto is "Where the superstars of today become the legends of tomorrow". I use special dice and charts from an actual WWF rpg gamebook to run matches. Do roleplays affect the outcome of a match? Sort of. If one is of poor quality or is simply no good, the opponent gets a slight temporary increase in his attributes, giving him a better chance to win. Everything on the LPW site is self-explanatory. If you still have a question about any of it, email me. The site address is www.angelfire.com/ca2/LPW
