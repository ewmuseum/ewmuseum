LPW — Relaunch

The return of a 'Legend'

20 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wuzzup! No, I'm not the legend I mentioned in the subject. I'm referring to the fed itself. Legend Pro Wrestling.... a once very popular and highly competitive league. The motto was "Where the superstars of today become the legends of tomorrow". This was actually the 3rd reconstruction of the league. It went through a 4th one a few months later and became Dark Reality Wrestling. The previous owner, "Mastermind" B.T. Anthony, retired from e-wrestling almost 2 months ago right after closing DRW. My name is "Duke" Darren Diamond. I've been following BT's leagues since the beginning (January 1998). With his permission, Legend Pro Wrestling will soon be making a comeback with me as the new president. Here's a sample of what LPW will feature..... Booked matches - Quality of roleplays and match strategies determine who wins. I'm an excellent card writer too. Equal opportunity - In most feds, you have to be within a certain ranking range to challenge for a title. In LPW, anyone can get a shot at any title, regardless of rank. Of all the dozens and dozens of leagues I have visited, I never saw any of them make this kind of offer. If you're looking for the chance to compete for gold but never got it, LPW will give it to you. Two weekly cards (a house show on Tuesdays and a televised show on Fridays) and a monthly PPV (usually the last Sunday of each month). At the moment, there are 4 titles: World heavyweight, a unique National title that is renamed after the home country of the champion, Cruiserweight & World tag team. The rest will be explained in detail at the LPW site before its grand opening on June 1. So if Legend Pro Wrestling sounds like where you want to be, then be sure to sign up on the 1st. Openings are limited so get there early. If you have any questions about the league before then, feel free to email me at [detail withheld] "Duke" Darren Diamond Legend Pro Wrestling president http://www.angelfire.com/ca3/legendpro
