LPW — Friday Frenzy

Friday Frenzy, 3 May 2002

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FRIDAY FRENZY - MAY 3, 2002 The Legend Pro Wrestling slogan logo appears in front of blurry fast moving blue clouds. Small lightning flashes can be seen in the clouds. The letters
LPW flashes for a moment, from left to right, then the entire name flashes to the sound of thunder. As the thunder fades to silence, the scene fades to black.
Scene begins inside a large office. All the remaining legends, including
Jason, is in there. Darren Diamond walks into camera range to an average pop. He pauses to look at them all before speaking.
Darren: When we reopened 2 months ago, I made it clear to you guys that you are to "take it easy" on the new generation talent and put them over if needed. But lately, they consider you all a joke... not a serious threat...
not worthy competition... (pauses) That will change tonight. The legends look at each other like "What does that mean?". Darren: As of right now, that clause in your temporary contracts is waived... meaning, you can go after the new generation at full intensity as you once were. Show them who the true threat really is!
They cheer in appreciation, some high-fiving each other. Darren: If they want serious competition so bad, then GIVE IT TO THEM! The legends make their way out the office, still happy over that decision. Darren smiles as the scene changes to the Friday Frenzy intro, playing to the tune of the heavy metal version of "Flight of the Bumblebee", showing moments of previous matches. As the intro concludes, the scene fades to the crowd at Madison Square Garden. Multicolor pyros ignite at the videotron and within the ring for about 10 seconds, then the lights return to normal as the cameramen pan different sections of the fans. Some signs shown are:
"Beware the EVIL!", "Dixon Fears Devastator" and a picture of Adam Simon with a red circle and line over it.
Eric Davidson: LIVE from Madison Square Garden in New York City, welcome to
Friday Frenzy! The fans here are in great anticipation concerning tonight's main event... (videotron shows an animated matchup screen) ...Adam Simon defending his "Undisputed Universal" title against LPW legend Zodiac in a best of 3 falls SUPERFIGHT.
Michael Hunter: Adam is always bragging how he's this and how he's that... but what's he gonna do when Zodiac shuts him up and takes that belt from him? What will he say then? Nothing!
Eric: That remains to be seen but before we get to that, there is a slight change in tonight's card. (scene changes to the commentary table) Jeremy
Cross and newcomer Chad Chan are both not here. That will be a double forfeit. Also, the *United States* National champion Wesley Potter is not here as well. The Bard will win the non-title match by forfeit. If this had been an actual title match, Bard would be the new National champion right now.
Michael: And what a travesty that would be, to lose a championship to someone who's only been in one match and lost. He doesn't deserve a shot at the National title.
Eric: Maybe you're right... Michael: Maybe?? Eric: We're set for our first of 3 matches so with that in mind... Michael: MAYBE right??? Eric: ...Sorry I mentioned it... let's go to the ring! Scene changes to a wide shot of the videotron and entrance curtain. The
Frenzy intro music fades away and moments later, "Hard Working Man" by
Brooks and Dunn starts to play. The fans cheer as the Ziro brothers make their way out. They pause a few seconds before Johnny raises his right fist above him. Matt works up the crowd a bit. A few moments later, they walk down to the ring. Matt is shown to be holding a baseball bat.
============================= "Sadistic" Steve Liermann -VS- Johnny Ziro Referee: Ted Hawking ============================= [DING DING DING] Lynni Tappan: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first.... being led to the ring by Matt Ziro.... from Nashville, TN....
weighing 320 lbs.... Johnny Ziro! Eric: This match came about as a result of what happened last Friday. (video footage is shown) It was the Ziros against the Assassin in a handicap match.
Johnny and Matt were in full control until Steve Liermann got involved and knocked them both out. Now, they meet one on one tonight...
Footage ends and they enter the ring. Johnny climbs in by stepping over the top rope then raises his fist again. Matt stays on the outside, watching out for possible run-ins.
Michael: If Johnny is half the man he says he is, he will leave that runt brother of his in the back.
Music fades out. The lights dim out as a video plays on the videotron. The fans in the arena are at a lulled silence. The video envokes the words
"Hardcore Legend", "Pioneer of Pain", "Champion of Carnage", and "Extreme
Icon". Suddenly, eerie overtones play, and the pulsating sounds of
"Dragula" by Rob Zombie awaken the lights, which turn to reds, along with chaser lights, moving in response to the music.
# Dead I am the one # # Exterminating son # # Slipping through the trees # # Strangling the breeze # Fog shoots from the entryway, as "Sadistic" Steve Liermann emerges from the curtain, wearing a golden-colored leather trenchcoat. He is wearing a pair of black sunglasses, a black T-Shirt which reads "The Epitome of Extreme" in red scrawled type, with a white outline to it, and his normal black pants trimmed in gold at the waist, with gold and white tribal designs down the legs. The disgust for this crowd, booing him into next week, seems to ooze off of him. He stands at the apex of the rampway and turns to face the videotron. He too has a baseball bat with him.
# Dig through the ditches # # And burn through the witches # # Slam in the back of my Dragula # Liermann positions his arms into a crucifix and then spins around, as the coat flaps in the breeze. The shine from Liermann's gelled up hair and his sunglasses almost blinds the camera as he passes it. He enters the ring and places his bat in a corner as the music fades out.
Eric: Liermann and Johnny Ziro to kick things off tonight and still to come, the Puerto Rican Slayers defend their tag team titles against Jason and a mystery partner. Who do you think it is, Michael?
Michael: I have no idea. It could be anyone but the champs aren't worried. Any trio that can kill 2 legends is near impossible to beat. [DING DING] Johnny runs at Liermann and catches him off guard with a big boot to the face. Liermann drops to one knee. Johnny goes to pick him up and gets punched hard a few times in the gut. Liermann stands and buries a boot into
Johnny's midsection. He locks him up, and drives him into the mat with a devastating DDT.
Michael: He just SPIKED him! Eric: This guy has more marquee moves than I have ever seen in my life. Liermann picks him up and whips him in the ropes. He presses him overhead, struggling a little and quickly drops him down in a powerslam. He makes a cover...
1................. 2................. kickout. Michael: WOW! Liermann calls that move the "Millennium Special". Eric: I'm surprised he even got him up there! Liermann kneels over Johnny and smacks his head with hard lefts and rights. Dazed, Johnny struggles to sit up. Liermann gives him a hand and helps him up. Johnny grabs him by the throat with both hands and lifts him up, holding him there for a good 5 seconds or so. Johnny then throws him away and down, almost falling out of the ring. Johnny drags him to the center and makes a cover.
1....................... 2....................... kickout. Michael: Hello, ref! That was a choke, as in CHEATING! Disqualify him right now! Eric: Last time I checked, a throat toss is a legal move. Michael: Well, it shouldn't be! Johnny pulls Liermann up, whips him hard in the corner and immediately follows up with a clothesline. Liermann has nowhere to fall and just stands there. Johnny hits with a knee to the midsection, then a back elbow strike, then a couple forearm smashes. Ref is already in the middle of his 5 count.
Johnny stops at 3. Michael: Get him out of the corner, you cheater! Eric: He's wearing the man down, for crying out loud! What did you expect
Johnny to do, lay down willingly and be pinned??
Michael: (pause) If he was smart, he would and save himself a lot of punishment! Johnny whips him into the opposite corner and charges for another clothesline. Liermann somehow had the sense to barely move out of the way in time. Johnny hits the turnbuckles hard and is staggering in pain. Liermann climbs the turnbuckles behind him. He wraps his right arm around Johnny's throat.
Michael: Oh man. This is gonna be good! Eric: Impossible... he's 7 feet tall. There's no way. He leaps off... DIAMOND DUST! Liermann rolls over and hooks the leg. 1......................... 2.......................... 3! [DING DING DING] Michael: Yes way! Lynni: Here is your winner..... "Sadistic" Steve Liermann! Liermann grabs his baseball bat. Matt hurries in the ring but before he could fend off Liermann, he gets whacked in the side of the head with a bat.
Matt drops to the mat. Liermann then turns his attention to big Johnny. Eric: Hey! Is this really necessary?? Michael: Steve obviously thinks it is, otherwise he wouldn'r be doing it! Duh! Liermann rolls Johnny over on his back and stands above him. He takes his bat and parts the hair on Johnny's head with it.
Eric: That's enough! Where is Warrior when you need him?? Michael: Forget about Warrior. Steve singlehandedly put that has-been out of commission! The bell sounds again as Liermann stands alone amongst the carnage and bodies in the ring, again victorious, here at Madison Square Garden! The bat is held high as "Dragula" blares once more.
Eric: That's just sick..... Michael: No, not "sick"... sadistic! Liermann hops out of the ring as the scene fades to black. =====COMMERCIALS===== Scene fades in right to the backstage area where Songi is standing in front of The Slayers' dressing room door. The fans boo him moments after he appears on the videotron. His white/black hair is hanging behind his ears, while he's wearing a black "DIE JASON DIE" T-Shirt.
Songi: I've got a confession to make. That is why I've called for you right now. Ya see, I've been sending little gifts to this cute girl. But I've realized, she's not so cute anymore. Dixon. I mean, she admitted to Dixon being her daddy. Its sick, she probably has all sorts of STDs! I mean, c'mon, Dixon isn't any sort of ladies man.
Jake, Dixon is a "I'll-give-you-a-dollar-if-you-give-me-an-STD" kinda guy. And now that gremlin Hillary is stuck with the millions of STDs that she's sure to pass on to you. I've got to get ready for my match with that legendary freak Jason and whatever loser partner he's sure to have. But rest assured, your little gremlin will surely receive no more gifts from me.
He opens his locker room door and steps in mumbling obscene comments about
Hillary under his breath. Scene switches to a wide shot of the fans.
Eric: Can you believe what we just heard?? Michael: Songi.... you da man! All of the lights in the arena go out. The words Die Slow come on the videotron and we hear the fans begin to boo in anticipation. "Slither" by
Metallica begins playing.
# Don't go looking for snakes you might find them # # Don't send your eyes to the sun you might blind them # # Haven't I seen you here before # # No there aint no heroes here # Dead Skull and Suicidal come out through the curtain to the arena as the blue spotlight hits them. Dead Skull has his tag team title belt around his waist, the lightweight belt over his right shoulder, and the Superfight belt over his left shoulder. He walks out and taunts the crowd with his belts.

As Suicidal is looking very anxious, he's jumping around and also taunting the crowd. Suicidal enters the ring while Dead Skull walks around the side and grabs a microphone from the ring announcer, then rolls under the bottom rope into the ring. The music cuts out and Dead Skull begins to speak as
Suicidal seemingly does a 180 in attitude. He just stops and stares down the aisle with his arms crossed staring a void into the backstage area, looking almost stone like.
Dead Skull: Hello my loyal fans! The fans let out a roar of boos. DS: I love pissing you guys off, you're just so easy to piss off.... easy and simple.... just like Sherry Sheryl.
The boos erupt once again this time intensifying from before. DS: Thanks for proving my point, morons. Boos grow louder yet. DS: Yea, you throw about as good as your mother sucks!!! They start chanting "SUCK MINE!!!" DS: Nice to be back in the city I live. You guys definitely get points for an original chant. Anyway, now to the point at hand.
The chants get louder and he is cut off. DS: (sarcastically) I love this city. Anyway, back to the business at hand. (clears his throat) HEY MA, LOOK IT'S ME!!! Look at my belts!!! First time anyone has ever done it and probably the only time in a very, very long time. Because none of these pathetic new so called LPW wrestlers have what it takes to do what I've done. You all suck!!! I mean look at our champion.
First it was Jake Mackenna, what a loser. Now it's even worse, Andrew Dixon. Now Dixon, I seem to remember at the last TNC you saying something about those "ignorant ass slayers". If we're so ignorant, what does that make you?
Last time we saw you, we outsmarted your dumbass. Let's take a look back shall we? Videotron changes to footage of Darren's birthday bash on April 9. Andrew
Dixon, Wesley Potter, Dead Skull and Songi break in. After a few words from
Dixon, he attacks Darren while the Slayers hold everyone at bay with their bats. As all hell breaks out, the Slayers turn on Dixon and nearly knock him out with the bats. Wesley Potter sees this and runs out of the room. Dead
Skull looks down at Dixon and says "Never trust the Slayers". Then he grabs a cup of punch and says "Happy Birthday Darren." Then the Slayers leave with
Dixon on the floor. Video ends.
DS: Yea there you go. Ignorant ass Andrew Dixon down on his ass and his little girlfriend Wesley Potter terrified and running with his tail between his legs. So Dixon, I'M CALLING YOU OUT B****!!!
Eric: Skull is calling out the LPW champion?? Michael: Wouldn't a 4th title look good on him? Eric: Perhaps but owning 4 is not possible, given the rules on which ones any one person can have.
Michael: Gee, aren't we the technical analyst tonight... Dixon has not come out yet to the somewhat surprise of the fans. DS: Ok... if that's how it's gonna be... I'm coming back there and get you right now! Eric: He's gonna what?? The Slayers leave the ring, ignoring the fans, heading up the aisle. Michael: The Man better be careful! There are 2 Slayers looking for him and they mean business!
Eric: ...When have they ever NOT meant business? (fade to black) =====COMMERCIALS===== Fade into backstage, Dead Skull just told Suicidal to search one hallway as he goes down another.
Eric: The Slayers are continuing their search for Andrew Dixon. So far, no luck. Michael: He might not even be here. As the LPW champion, he can take the night off if he wishes to!
Skull turns left down another hall and sees Dixon far in the distance, talking on a cellphone.
Eric: Uh oh! DS: DIXON! GET YOUR ASS OVER HERE, YOU LITTLE B****! Skull runs at him just as Dixon had noticed what's going on. As Skull is about to run past an intersection where a hall goes to the right, a dark figure in a black trenchcoat and a masked hood (similar to a Halloween type mask, he can see out but you can't see his face) runs out from that hall, grabs a surprised Slayer by the throat and with incredible force, slams him against the wall. He appears to be around 6 feet tall and shaped like he's muscular. Skull is grabbing the strong arm of the unknown figure, gasping for air. Dixon stands in the background in surprise.
Michael: What the hell??? Eric: Who is THAT??? ???: (using a voice synthesizer under the hood to disguise his voice) After
I finish some other matters at Doomsday..... (he grabs the Superfight title off Skull's shoulder and repositions it in his hand to show the plating of it to him and holds it up to his face) .....I'm coming back for THIS!
(pauses) By the way.... it's good to see you again. Still holding Skull by the throat, he smashes the belt VERY hard in his face
3 times before letting him go. Skull collapses to the ground against the wall with a bloody "crimson mask". The dark figure delivers a few strong kicks to the chest.
???: But it's better to see you suffer! (leans down, looking at him) Just like the rest of LPW will suffer... at OUR hands!
He stands up, proud of what he did. Michael: ....day-amn! Eric: Is.... is that part of the "Great Evil" the legends keep referring to?? He then slowly turns his attention to Dixon's direction. Michael: Aw crap! Dixon, get out of here! NOW! The dark figure casually walks over to Dixon and gets right in his face. He pauses to see a slight hint of worry and possibly fear in Dixon's eyes.
???: Your challenge for Doomsday.... has been accepted. (pauses) Prepare.... to be.... ANNIHILATED! The man steps around and walks down the hall behind the champ. Dixon looks back at the same time the camera does... and he's nowhere to be seen. The commentators are too surprised to comment on what just happened. Scene fades to black.
=====COMMERCIALS===== Fade in to a replay of the incident before going to commercial. Eric: ...Everyone here is still in shock over what just happened minutes ago! A dark figure who we now believe is part of the "Great Evil" has viciously assaulted Dead Skull and answered Andrew Dixon's challenge made to
Devastator.

Michael: He sure did a number on him...... but what did he mean by "it's good to see you again"??
Eric: If what the mysterious person said is true, the entire Legend Pro
Wrestling roster is in big time trouble!
Scene changes back to the ring area. All of the lights in the arena go out. The words Die Slow come on the videotron and we hear the fans begin to boo in anticipation. "Slither" by Metallica begins playing again. Songi and
Suicidal walk out under a blue spot light with a blue strobe over the rest of the arena. They taunt the fans as always on their way out. They get to the ring and just drop their belts. They look all buisness as the lights return to normal.
