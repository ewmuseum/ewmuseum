LPW — Relaunch

In 4 days, the 'Legend' returns.

28 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Legend Pro Wrestling, a popular fed in the summer-fall of 1998, is slowly preparing to reopen its doors once again on June 1. "Where the superstars of today become the legends of tomorrow" was the famous slogan of the league. And it still does. I believe in equal opportunity. In the new LPW, you don't have to be ranked at a certain point to challenge for a title. You could lose the first 50 matches you have and still be able to challenge for the World title. There are 5 titles: World heavyweight, World tag team, Hardcore, Cruiserweight and an unique National title that is renamed after the nationality of the champion. Two weekly cards (1 house show and 1 televised show) and a monthly PPV. When it comes to roleplays, I look for how impressive and/or creative it looks, not how many lines it has. Roleplays and match strategies determine who wins. All this and much more can be found on the LPW website. The address is shown below. Become a part of e-wrestling history on June 1! "Duke" Darren Diamond Legend Pro Wrestling president www.angelfire.com/ca3/legendpro
