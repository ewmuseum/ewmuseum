LPW — Recruitment

Still Hiring New Talent And Free Agents

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm Darren Diamond, creator and president of Legend Pro Wrestling. This is not a new e-fed as I have ran this league a few times before in 1998-2000. LPW was once a popular fed and now that we have reopened since March 1 (after a two year absense), we need to get a new roster of players.
At the moment, there are only 2 players and I'm having to bring back some of LPW's past legends as temporary opponents. Many have visited the site but no new players have been officially signed. Each LPW player has various stats like Strength, Speed, etc. to make gameplay more realistic. There is also a salary system where you spend your hard earned money towards improving your wrestlers' stats. Further information on all that can be viewed on the LPW site.
There are 5 titles: World, National, Superfight, Lightweight and World tag. We will determine the champions of two of those titles this week.
LPW has 2 shows a week on Tuesday and Friday. PPV events are held once every 2 months on a Saturday.
Match results are based on roleplay quality (not quantity), player strategy and any angles that may be involved. Everything about how LPW works is on the site.
You will need the Flash 5 player to view the main LPW interface. Feel free to look around and see if LPW is the league for you. But if it's not of interest to you, let me know what I can do to improve it.
