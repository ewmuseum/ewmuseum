LPW — Recruitment

In Serious Need Of New Players

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 1 of 3, Mar 25, 2002, 4:21:11 PM] I'm Darren Diamond, president of Legend Pro Wrestling. Ever since our reopening on March 1, only 3 people have signed up and we really need a lot more. I'm having to use past legends as temporary opponents until more players join. All but 2 legends (staff members) will eventually leave when we get a roster of about 20-25 wrestlers.
LPW has two shows each week (Tuesdays and Fridays) and a PPV every two months on a Saturday. There are a couple point systems used to provide some realism among the characters. All shows are booked and a message board is used for roleplays. There are 5 titles to compete for: World heavyweight,
National, Lightweight, Superfight and World tag. The actual name of the
National title will vary depending on the champion's nationality. Because of the lack of new wrestlers lately, the World title tournament will now begin one week early (starts this Tuesday night).
LPW was once a very popular league a few years ago and I'm trying to make it popular again but having just 3 players won't accomplish that. My two returning players, Dead Skull and Songi, would both tell you that Legend Pro
Wrestling is more fun than any other league. They have been with me since I first started doing this back in 1998. Check us out and judge for yourself.
