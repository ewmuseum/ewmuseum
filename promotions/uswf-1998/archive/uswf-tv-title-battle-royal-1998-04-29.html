USWF — Card

TV Title Battle Royal

29 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
USWF broadcasters Tony DiMera and Steve Hendrix are at a ringside announcing table, sitting in front of enthusiastic fans. Some are wearing black "HC2000" shirts, others holding posters for their favorite USWF stars.)
TONY: Hello again, fans! Welcome to USWF Wednesday War! I'm Tony DiMera, and with me as always is "Stunning" Steve Hendrix. Tonight is going to be a night like no other!
STEVE: That's right, DiMera! This is going to be one of the biggest nights in USWF history, because we're going to have almost every USWF superstar in the ring at the same time!
TONY: A total of 27 men will be in that ring tonight, as we are ready for a big BATTLE ROYAL to determine who will walk away tonight with the USWF TV Title! Every wrestler who currently holds a USWF contract, with the exception of USWF Champion Mark Williams, will compete in this match tonight. Now as you know, this is going to put some very volatile personalities in a very volatile situation!
STEVE: That's an understatement! Some of these guys have some dangerous tension building between them, and I don't think they're as concerned about winning the TV Title as they are about tearing each other up!
TONY: Fans, not only will we see some of your old favorites, we're also going to be seeing some newcomers making their debut in this big Battle Royal tonight! And the interesting thing is, everyone has an equal chance of walking our of this arena tonight with the USWF TV Title around his waist!
STEVE: This is going to be some night, DiMera! TONY: You can say that again! We'll be ready to start the match right after a few commercial messages! Stay with us! ************************************************************************ (The thumping sound of a heartbeat is all that is heard, as the screen is dark. An announcer does a voice-over, as images of USWF superstars in their past matches appear on the screen.)
ANNOUNCER: The United States Wrestling Federation... where strength, endurance, skill, and fortitude combine to create the most intense professional wrestling atmosphere in the world today. The battle lines have been drawn.... enemies have answered the challenges of their foes... allies have pledged support to their friends... and soon they must all collide!
MAYhem: The Mother of All Battles! Live from the Pyramid in Memphis, Tennessee on May 10th. The USWF is proud to present this wrestling extravaganza LIVE on Pay Per View television. Call your local cable operator for availability. ************************************************************************** (The camera shows the ring, which is now overflowing with 27 USWF wrestlers, along with referees, security guards, and USWF officials. The fans are going wild.)
TONY: All right, we're back fans, and I'm telling you the intensity level here in this arena is unbelievable! I don't know how we've managed to go this long without a fight breaking out!
ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen! The following Battle Royal contest is for the USWF Television Championship! To be eliminated from this match, a wrestler must go over the top rope, and both feet must touch the floor! Once a wrestler has been eliminated, he must leave the ring area. When only one man remains in the ring, he will be declared the winner, and will be the NEW USWF TV Champion.
STEVE: Some of those refs and cops are starting to sneak out of the ring, but they know that any minute this ring could explode!
TONY: We haven't had a bell to start the match yet, and these men are ready to fight! Just look at that! Twenty-seven of the greatest athletes in the world today, all in one ring!
STEVE: I hope they reinforced the ring for this one! TONY: I'm sure they have... OH WAIT! LOOK OUT! Viper just charged across the ring and is attacking Steely Eyed Dan! The bell hasn't even rung yet, all of the officials are not out of the ring!
STEVE: Viper doesn't care, DiMera! You've heard how these two have been talking to each other, they are hungry for blood! There is all kinds of animosity between those two!
TONY: Hungry for blood is right! It's almost as if he other wrestlers have caught the scent of violence, and the ring has erupted into a virtual feeding frenzy! It looks like everybody is beating up on someone now... and finally there is the bell to officially start the match!
STEVE: Viper and Steely Eyed Dan are tearing each other up! I've never seen such a brawl! TONY: And here comes Remington Steele, Dan's manager and a former wrestler himself. He is ready to lend his man a hand! Some devastating blows to the back of Viper!
STEVE: Backwoods Bob Wheeler is joining in the fray, getting some licks in on Steely Eyed Dan as well. Wheeler has Dan occupied as Viper... LOOK OUT! Viper is picking up Remington Steele! Look at that ferocious look in Viper's eyes! He lifts Steele up over his head... and there he goes! Viper just tossed Steele out over the top and onto the floor for the first casualty of the match! ************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Remington Steele has been eliminated! ************************************************
TONY: Now Backwoods Bob and Viper are doing a little double team work on Steely Eyed Dan! Wheeler is choking Dan as Viper gets some vicious uppercuts Dan's midsection.
STEVE: WHOAH! Take a look at this, DiMera! I think that The Railroad Express, Freight and Diesel, are sensing an opportunity here! Viper, Dan, and Wheeler all are next to the ropes and have their back turned. Freight and Diesel are taking a running start, bouncing off the ropes and charging at the three unsuspecting victims!
TONY: Wait, Viper sees them coming! He sidesteps just in time as Freight and Diesel wit Dan and Wheeler with two big clotheslines! And... YES! The momentum carries Backwoods Bob and Steely Eyed Dan over the top rope and onto the floor! **************************************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Backwoods Bob Wheeler and Steely Eyed Dan have been eliminated! **************************************************************************
STEVE: And take a look at this! Viper, his eyes on fire with rage, steps right over the top rope and goes right for Steely Eyed Dan! I can't believe it! Viper just eliminated himself! **********************************************
ANNOUNCER: Viper has been eliminated! ********************************************** TONY: Oh my! Viper is pounding away at the right knee of Steely Eyed Dan! He is hammering away with vicious kicks right into the right knee! Now he's stomping away at it.... Remington Steele has ran back out from the locker area to try to assist his man, but Backwoods Bob is holding him back, while Viper continues his assault!
STEVE: And now Viper has Steely Eyed Dan locked in the figure four! It looks like he is trying to break the leg of his enemy!
TONY: Here come some officials, trying to establish some order! They finally get Viper off of Dan, but at what expense!
STEVE: There go Viper and Wheeler back to the locker room, as Steele goes to check on his man. He doesn't look to be in good shape! Steele is having to help him up, and I don't think he can stand on that right leg! I think he's been seriously hurt!
TONY: We'll have to try to update you on his condition later, but there is action all over the ring! It looks like the animosity between Death and HC-2000 has led to a big confrontation in the ring, as Death and Arc Angel are busy fighting Saturn, Spider, Crow, Sha Dog, and The Hitman. Curiously, Bill Vaughn, who has an alliance with HC-2000, is staying clear of the fight. He is busy hooking up with Justin Credible, another of his enemies!
STEVE: Death and Arc Angel are big guys, but they are outnumbered 2 to 5! This can't last very long! Wait a minute, it looks like they're getting some help. Who is that guy??
TONY: That is Eclipse, the partner of Viper! It looks like the has decided to enter the fray against HC2000! He has grabbed Sha Dog by the hair and is pulling him into the opposite corner! Eclipse is a big monster, standing over 7 feet tall!
STEVE: He's pounding Sha Dog furiously! He hooks up him for... YES, a big DDT. Sha Dog is wobbly, and Eclipse is picking him up, and throwing him out! ****************************************
ANNOUNCER: Sha Dog has been eliminated **************************************** TONY: Meanwhile, Spider and James The Hitman are doing a double team number on Arc Angel. The big Hitman is holding the arms of Arc Angel while Spider kicks his repeatedly in the midsection! It looks like they're wearing him down...
STEVE: Hey, DiMera! Get a load of "Hollywood" Steve Shine! TONY: Look at him! Everyone else is busy fighting with someone, but Steve Shine is not touching anyone! In fact, he's looking around shiftily.... making sure no one sees him.... OH MY GOODNESS!
STEVE: I love it! Shine just went under the ropes and onto the floor! TONY: Eliminated himself? STEVE: No, DiMera! You have to go over the top rope to be eliminated! Shine snuck out under the bottom rope! Now he's crawling under the ring!
TONY: I don't believe it! That coward! STEVE: I love it! TONY: Whoah! While we were busy watching Shine, it looks like the Hitman has Arc Angel up, trying to throw him out. Arc Angel is trying to hold on to the top rope, but here comes Spider! They just dumped him over! *********************************************
ANNOUNCER: Arc Angel has been eliminated! ********************************************* STEVE: Spider and The Hitman are celebrating... HOLD ON! It looks like the Rail Road Express are seeing another opportunity! They are behind Spider and The Hitman, they're running, a rebound from the ropes, charging at Spider and Hitman.... BIG CLOTHESLINES!
TONY: They just knocked Spider and James The Hitman over the top! HC2000 is furious! *************************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Spider and James The Hitman have been eliminated! *************************************************************
STEVE: The Rail Road Express are having an awesome night here at the battle royal! TONY: And it looks like a couple of newcomers are battling it out in a corner, Steve. John McKenzie, a brawler from Australia, is busy pounding Anthony Mislan's head into the turnbuckle. Mislan, a rookie from New Jersey, looks to be having some trouble with the rough Aussie! McKenzie hooks him in for a big suplex, grabs the hair, brings him back up... and McKenzie tosses Anthony Mislan over the top rope like a sack of potatoes! ************************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Anthony Mislan has been eliminated! ************************************************************
STEVE: And it looks like Death is still having some trouble from HC2000! Crow and Saturn are busy double teaming him, keeping the big man on the defensive. Death looks tired, and his partner Arc Angel is gone!
TONY: Wait! Here comes Ryan Taylor! Remember that as The Missing Link, Taylor was a member of HC2000. But he has since changed his ways and dropped out of the group. He comes up behind Crow and nails him with a big belly to back suplex!
STEVE: One on one with Saturn, Death is finally able to get back on the offensive! He nails Saturn in the face with several big right hands... now he's setting him up.... YES! The Apocalypse Drop! He nailed Saturn with it! Saturn looks like he's out of it!
TONY: Here comes Zarley Cavendish! Snuff takes his opportunity! He grabs the stunned Saturn, picks him up, and slams him right over the top rope! He just "snuffed" Saturn! **********************************************
ANNOUNCER: Saturn has been eliminated! ********************************************** STEVE: Ryan Taylor is still pounding away on Crow, hitting him with awesome bodyslams. But up from behind comes Justin Credible, he grabs Ryan Taylor and nails him with a devastating reverse neckbreaker! Credible then tosses Taylor over the top rope! **********************************************
ANNOUNCER: Ryan Taylor has been eliminated! **********************************************
TONY: Our big Australian newcomer, John McKenzie, continues the onslaught on Crow. He picks him up and hooks him in for.... an awesome DDT! I believe that is his finisher, and as I check my notes here, I see that he calls it "Detention!" After that, he has no trouble in throwing Crow out! ********************************************
ANNOUNCER: Crow has been eliminated! ******************************************** STEVE: Another USWF newcomer, Subway, is busy hammering away on Bolt, who just happens to be the nephew of "Hollywood" Steve Shine.
TONY: Who, I suppose, is still hiding out under the ring! STEVE: Subway has about a 70 pound size advantage over young Bolt, and he's using it to his advantage! He's taking it to bolt with suplexes and bodyslams.
TONY: And here he goes with another big suplex.... NO! Wait! He inverted that suplex! Instead of dropping Bolt on his back, he dropped him down on this stomach and face! That is an awesome maneuver! That's another new finishing maneuver we've seen demonstrated.... let me see what he calls it. Yes, "Pocket Full of Kryptonite." I suppose Subway is a comics fan.
STEVE: Whatever he is, he just tossed Bolt over the top. *****************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Bolt has been eliminated. *****************************************************
TONY: Here comes the Rail Road Express! They caught a glimpse of Subway executing his finisher, and it seems to have made them mad! I believe Subway, Freight, and Diesel are old enemies from regional federation action.
STEVE: Freight and Diesel are pummeling away at Subway with chops and axehandle blows. They've got him down on his knees! Subway is suffering! Diesel is setting Subway up for a Piledriver, Freight is going to the second turnbuckle.... BOOM! Freight jumped down off the turnbuckle, grabbed Subway's feed, and helped Diesel drive the big man down in a big piledriver. Awesome stuff!
TONY: It's fairy easy for them to throw Subway over the top! *******************************************
ANNOUNCER: Subway has been eliminated! ******************************************* TONY: Freight and Diesel are awesome tonight! They have been at each other's side all night, using their tag team skills to their advantage!
STEVE: Here comes the big Killer Whale! He looks like he wants to put a stop to Rail Road Express' domination here tonight. And it just might take a big 400 pounder like him to do it!
TONY: Killer Whale just charged across the ring and hit both of them with a big belly splash in the corner. He's got the two of them trapped against the turnbuckle, as he hits them with shoulder blocks. He's really knocking the wind out of Freight and Diesel.
STEVE: It looks like the Whale is going to get some help, here comes Manta Ray. TONY: Manta Ray is a martial artist coming to us from Ecuador. STEVE: Manta Ray and Killer Whale take Freight and send him across the ring with a double irish whip into the turnbuckle. Manta Ray then takes Killer Whale and Irish whips him into the corner, where the Whale hits Freight with a diving splash. Freight goes down!
TONY: And they're repeating the process with Diesel! The Rail Road Express is down! STEVE: And Storm, another relative newcomer to the USWF, is moving in on Diesel! He picks Diesel up and hits him with a side suplex, then tossses him out over the top rope. **************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Diesel has been eliminated! ************************************************** TONY: Killer Whale and Manta Ray are going over to finish off Freight. Whale picks up Freight, as Manta Ray his him with a big swinging kick to the face. The force of it sends Freight over the top rope! **************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Freight has been eliminated! **************************************************
TONY: Killer Whale and Manta Ray high five each other in celebration! STEVE: They don't get much time to celebrate, as Chris Williams moves in on Manta Ray and Eclipse steps up to Killer Whale. Eclipse is a good size match for the big Killer Whale, and just as strong! Those two lock up and fight it out, gradually working their way over to the ropes. The two big behemoths pound away at one another with chops, punches, and kicks. Eventually, they lock each other with choke holds.
TONY: Here comes Death! Death is moving in on the two big men! They don't see him coming! He bounds off the rope and nails them with a big dropkick when topples them over the rope! **********************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Killer Whale and Eclipse have been eliminated! **********************************************************
STEVE: Chris Williams and Manta Ray are still fighting, and it looks like Manta Ray has the upper hand! Manta Ray sends Williams running across the ring with an Irish whip, Williams rebounds off the ropes.... BUT HE'S CAUGHT ON THE REBOUND BY BILL VAUGHN!!! CRITICAL MASS! Vaughn just caught Chris Williams with the big powerslam! Whoah! That came out of nowhere!
TONY: The fans are going wild for Vaughn, who scoops Williams up for a body slam and dumps him over the top! **************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Chris Williams has been eliminated! **************************************************
TONY: And seeing his opportunity, Manta Ray jumps on Bill Vaughn, locking him in a tight sleeper! Vaughn may be in trouble! He's fading fast, as Manta Ray squeezes tighter and tighter!
STEVE: Uh oh! Here comes Death! Death grabs Manta Ray, who breaks the hold, and Death quickly sets him up for a big piledriver! Manta Ray is down, Vaughn is groggy from the sleeper! Death takes Vaughn and tosses him over the top! Vaughn is out! *****************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Critical Bill Vaughn has been eliminated! *****************************************************
TONY: Death is turning around, and scooping up Manta Ray. He sends him flying over the top! ******************************************
ANNOUNCER: Manta Ray has been eliminated! ****************************************** STEVE: The fans are going wild, and Death is working them for all they're worth! But he's got his back turned, and he's standing awfully close to the ropes!
TONY: Justin Credible sees his chance! He gets a running start and nails Death in the back with a big dropkick! Death's teetering over the top rope, his feet in the air...
STEVE: And Justin Credible gets up, and pushes him the rest of the way over! **************************************
ANNOUNCER: Death has been eliminated! ************************************** TONY: Death is furious! Absolutely furious! Justin Credible is at the ropes, pointing at Death, and laughing. WHOAH! Death just reached up and grabbed him by the hair!
STEVE: He's pulling him over the top! Yes! There he goes! Justin Credible's down! ***********************************************
ANNOUNCER: Justin Credible has been eliminated! ***********************************************
TONY: Death and Justin are fighting outside the ring! They are furious with one another! The officials are breaking it up, but I'm sure we'll see more from this one!
STEVE: Only three men left in the ring, DiMera! And one of them is going to be the new TV Champ!
TONY: And I have to tell you, Steve, these guys were not favorites coming into this match! All three are relative newcomers! Zarley "Snuff" Cavendish, Storm, and John McKenzie!
STEVE: These three all look tired, and they're sizing each other up. None of them look anxious to start a fight.
TONY: It looks like it's going to be the outback brawler, John McKenzie, who starts it off. He locks up with Cavendish, and sends him across the ring. On the rebound, McKenzie hits him with a dropkick. He then quickly locks up with Storm, and just as Cavendish is getting back to his feet, McKenzie breaks his hold on Storm, the two seem to have an idea. The join hands, run at Cavendish, and hit him with a big double clothesline that sends "Snuff" reeling.
STEVE: WHOAH! Storm just hit Snuff with a big diagonal super kick! Right to the face of Cavendish! That almost took his head off!
TONY: And here comes McKenzie, he hooks Cavendish up for.... BOOM! That big "Detention" DDT again! That is an awesome maneuver!
STEVE: Now Storm is picking Cavendish up, and sending him sailing over the top rope! *********************************************************
ANNOUNCER: Zarley "Snuff" Cavendish has been eliminated! *********************************************************
TONY: Storm and John McKenzie remain, both men USWF newcomers, and one of them will be the first TV Champion. They have worked hard here tonight!
STEVE: And in a show of sportsmanship, the two men shake hands before they lock up again! TONY: And it's McKenzie who locks Storm in a side headlock. He hold is for a while, then Storm uses leverage to send McKenzie running into the ropes. Storm catches him with a kick to the midsection, McKenzie doubles over. Storm hooks him up for a vertical suplex.
STEVE: McKenzie gets up, and Storm runs against the ropes. He's close to McKenzie, but McKenzie bends down... and catches Storm with a backdrop! Storm is up... and over the top rope! The backdrop sent Storm over the top!
TONY: Storm is down! ***************************************** ANNOUNCER: Storm has been eliminated! ***************************************** TONY: We have our new TV Champion, ladies and gentlemen! The newcomer from Melbourne, Australia is the last man standing! In his debut match, John McKenzie has surprised us all to win the TV Title! Amazing!
STEVE: McKenzie is going over to the corner, where the referee is handing him the TV title. The fans seem to like this rough brawler, as they are responding to him!
TONY: McKenzie is obviously exhausted, but he's all smiles as the holds the TV belt above his head.
STEVE: (starts to laugh) TONY: What are you laughing at? STEVE: (through the laughter) Just take a look.... TONY: Oh no! You've got to be kidding me! It's Steve Shine! "Hollywood" Steve Shine! STEVE: (laughing) I love it! TONY: Steve Shine, who went under the bottom rope and was never eliminated, has been hiding under the ring the entire time!
STEVE: McKenzie doesn't see him! TONY: Shine is going up to the top rope, the fans can't believe their eyes! STEVE: McKenzie's turning around..... TONY: But it's too late! BIG SHINE LINE! Steve Shine's trademark clothesline off the top rope!
STEVE: "Hollywood" takes a minute to pose for the crowd... TONY: Now he's picking McKenzie up by the hair... STEVE: And sends him sailing! TONY: Over the top rope... this is unbelievable.... STEVE: There's your new champion, DiMera! I love it! ANNOUNCER: John McKenzie has been eliminated! Ladies and gentlemen, your winner and new USWF Television Champion..... "Hollywood" Steve Shine!
TONY: This is totally unbelievable, fans. We will try to bring you some reaction from McKenzie, Shine, and everyone involved in tonight's match as soon as we can! But we're out of time here tonight. For Steve Hendrix, this is a speechless Tony DiMera saying good night!
